## Gift Strategy: How to Choose Toys That Actually Get Played With

Before we dive into the picks, let’s talk strategy. The best toys share a few common traits: they’re age-appropriate, they encourage open-ended play, and they’re built to last. Here’s our quick checklist:

- **Consider the child’s developmental stage.** A toy that’s too advanced frustrates; one that’s too simple bores.
- **Look for durability.** Reviews that mention “cheap plastic” or “broke after a week” are red flags.
- **Check the review count and rating.** A 4.5-star product with 50 reviews is less reliable than one with 5,000.
- **Think about storage and cleanup.** Parents will thank you for gifts that don’t require a dedicated room.
- **Budget wisely.** You don’t have to spend a lot to give a great gift—sometimes the simplest toys are the most beloved.
