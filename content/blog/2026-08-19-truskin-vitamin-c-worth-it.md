## What Reviewers Complain About (The Not-So-Good)

No product is perfect, and TruSkin has its share of complaints. Let's look at the most common issues raised in negative reviews.

TruSkin has a distinct, earthy, slightly herbaceous scent that comes from the botanical extracts (orange, lavender, and chamomile). Some people love it; others find it off-putting. If you're sensitive to fragrance, this could be a dealbreaker — though the scent does fade quickly after application.

### It Can Cause Breakouts (for Some)

A notable minority of reviewers report purging or breakouts when they first start using the serum. This is common with active ingredients like vitamin C, but it's still frustrating. If you have highly sensitive or acne-prone skin, you'll want to patch test first and start slowly (every other day).

The dropper is functional but not great — it can be messy, and the product can oxidize (turn orange) if you don't close it tightly. Some reviewers also mention that the bottle leaks during travel. Minor, but worth noting.

### Not a Miracle Worker for Deep Wrinkles

While TruSkin helps with brightness and fine lines, it's not a heavy-duty anti-aging serum. If you're expecting it to erase deep wrinkles or tighten sagging skin, you'll be disappointed. It's a brightener and antioxidant shield, not a facelift in a bottle.
