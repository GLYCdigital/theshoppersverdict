## Buying Tips: How to Get the Most Value Under $20

- **Prioritize versatility.** A product that can handle multiple tasks (like spray paint that works on wood, metal, and plastic) delivers more value than a single-use gadget.
- **Check the review count, not just the star rating.** A 4.8 average from 8,000 buyers is more trustworthy than a 5.0 from 50.
- **Look for pro-grade materials at budget prices.** Brands like Klein Tools and Rust-Oleum are used by professionals, so you're getting proven durability.
- **Factor in consumables.** For spray paint, calculate cost per square foot of coverage. For tools, consider whether you'll need to buy replacement parts (like cutting blades).
- **Don't overlook the flaw.** Every product has one. Decide if you can live with it before you buy.
