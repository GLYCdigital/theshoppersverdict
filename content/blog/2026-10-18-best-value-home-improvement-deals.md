## What to Skip (and Why)

We only listed three products, so there's no filler to pad the roundup. But there are a few deal patterns worth skipping this week:

- **Safety gear sold in huge multipacks at a “discount.”** Unless you're outfitting a crew, you don't need 12 pairs of glasses. One good pair with replaceable parts beats a box of disposables.
- **Coffee gear with a big percentage off but no burr specs.** A 40% discount on a blade grinder is still a bad grinder. The OXO's value comes from the burrs, not the badge.
- **Filter multipacks for systems you don't own.** This is the classic deal-alert trap. A filter “deal” only exists relative to a pitcher you already use.
