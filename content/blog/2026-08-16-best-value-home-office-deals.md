### How to Get the Most Out of Them

- **Clean the surface first:** Use rubbing alcohol on both the wall and the frame. Let it dry completely.
- **Press firmly:** Apply the strip to the frame, then press the frame against the wall for 30 seconds. Use the included level to make sure it's straight.
- **Wait before hanging:** Give the adhesive at least an hour to bond. Overnight is even better.
- **Remove carefully:** When it's time to take them down, pull the strip straight down slowly. It should release without residue.
