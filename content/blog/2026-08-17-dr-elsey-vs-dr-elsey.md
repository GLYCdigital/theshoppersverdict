## Where the Scented Ultra Wins

### 1. Odor Control That Actually Lasts

Dr. Elsey's **Ultra Cat Litter** comes with a 10-day odor control guarantee. In my testing and across thousands of reviews, that promise holds up — even in multi-cat households. The added fragrance masks ammonia better than the UnScented version, which relies purely on the clay's natural absorption.

Our full [Dr. Elsey's Ultra Cat Litter review](/pet-supplies/dr-elseys-ultra-cat-litter-review/) digs into the details, but the short story is: if you have more than one cat, the scented version gives you more breathing room between scoops.

### 2. Clumping That Saves You Money

Both litters form rock-hard clumps, but the scented Ultra tends to clump slightly faster and denser. That means less litter gets stuck to the bottom of the box, so you use less per scoop. Over a month, that's a real cost saving.

The clumps don't shatter during scooping, which is the #1 complaint with cheaper clays. You'll waste less litter and spend less time cleaning.

### 3. The 10-Day Guarantee

Only the scented version comes with the official 10-day odor control guarantee. If you're skeptical, that's a safety net. The UnScented doesn't advertise the same promise, which tells you where the brand's confidence lies.

## Where the UnScented Ultra Wins

### 1. No Fragrance for Sensitive Noses

Some cats are picky — or allergic — to even mild scents. The **UnScented Clumping** formula eliminates that variable entirely. If your cat has been avoiding the box or sneezing, switching to unscented is a smart move.

It's also a win for humans with fragrance sensitivities. The scented Ultra uses a clean, fresh scent, but it's still a perfume. The UnScented version is truly odor-free on its own.

### 2. Same Dependable Clumping Base

You're not sacrificing the core formula. The UnScented version uses the same fine-grained clay that clumps like concrete. You get the same low-dust, easy-scoop experience — just without the added smell.

Our [Dr. Elsey's Ultra UnScented Clumping review](/pet-supplies/dr-elseys-ultra-unscented-clumping-clay-cat-litter-40lb-review/) confirms it's a solid choice for minimalists.

### 3. Better for Multi-Cat Homes with Mixed Preferences

If you have one cat that loves the box and another that's finicky, the UnScented version is the neutral ground. No scent to complain about, just clean clay.

## Where Each Falls Short

### Scented Ultra's Weaknesses

- **The scent isn't for everyone.** Some humans find it too strong, and a few cats turn up their noses.
- **Slightly higher dust** compared to premium unscented brands, though still low.
- **The 10-day guarantee** only applies if you follow strict scooping rules (daily, in most cases).

### UnScented Ultra's Weaknesses

- **Odor control is weaker.** Without fragrance, ammonia can sneak through faster, especially in hot weather or with multiple cats.
- **No guarantee.** You're trusting the clay alone, and it's not as effective at masking litter box smells.
- **No scent masking for humans.** If your litter box is in a living area, you'll smell it more.

## FAQ

### Is Dr. Elsey's Ultra scented or unscented better for odor control?

For maximum odor control, the scented Ultra wins. Its 10-day guarantee and added fragrance mask ammonia far better than the unscented version, which relies solely on clay absorption.

### Can I use unscented litter if I have multiple cats?

Yes, but you'll need to scoop more frequently. The unscented formula still clumps well, but without fragrance, odors can build up faster in a multi-cat household.

### Are these litters dust-free?

Both are low-dust, but not 100% dust-free. The scented version may produce slightly more dust due to the added fragrance coating. If dust is a major concern, check our full reviews for more details.

### Which litter is more cost-effective long-term?

The scented Ultra is more cost-effective because its harder clumps and better odor control mean you use less litter per scoop and replace it less often. Plus, you get the 10-day guarantee.

## Final Thought

Don't overthink it. Unless you have a specific reason to avoid fragrance, the scented Ultra is the clear winner. Your cat will be happy, your home will smell fresh, and your wallet will thank you.
