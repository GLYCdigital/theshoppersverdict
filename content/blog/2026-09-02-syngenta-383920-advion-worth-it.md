## What Reviewers Complain About: The Honest Gripes

No product is perfect. Even with a 4.6/5 rating, there’s a vocal minority of dissatisfied customers. Here’s what they say:

### 1. It’s Not an Instant Kill

Some people expect to see dead roaches immediately. That’s not how Advion works. It takes time — often 1–2 weeks for full colony elimination. Impatient users sometimes declare it a failure after a few days, not realizing the slow-burn strategy.

### 2. Reapplication Is Often Necessary

If you have a severe infestation or a multi-unit building (apartments, duplexes), the gel can only do so much. New roaches can wander in from neighboring units. Many reviewers say you need to reapply every few weeks to maintain control.

### 3. Gel Can Dry Out or Get Contaminated

If you apply too much in one spot, the gel can dry out and lose effectiveness. Dust and debris can also contaminate it. Proper placement is key — you can’t just glob it anywhere.

### 4. Not a Magic Bullet for German Roaches

German cockroaches (the most common indoor species) are notoriously resilient. While Advion is effective against them, some reviewers report that it takes longer or that a second product (like a growth regulator) is needed to fully wipe out the population.

### 5. The Syringes Can Be Messy

The gel is sticky and can be difficult to clean off surfaces. If you accidentally smear it, it’s a pain. Also, the plunger can be stiff to push, especially in cooler temperatures.

### 6. Some Users Report Roaches Avoiding It

In rare cases, if you’ve previously used a different bait (like a spray or trap), roaches may be bait-shy. It’s not common, but it happens.

## How to Get the Best Results (Pro Tips)

If you decide to buy it, here’s how to make sure you actually get the 4.6/5 experience:

1. **Identify roach hotspots** — under the fridge, behind the stove, in cabinet hinges, near drains, under sinks. Roaches love moisture and warmth.
2. **Apply small dots, not smears** — a pea-sized dot every 12–18 inches in those areas. Too much gel can be avoided by roaches.
3. **Reapply every 2–3 weeks** — especially in the first month, to catch newly hatched roaches.
4. **Combine with a growth regulator** (like Gentrol) if you have German roaches — this is the professional combo.
5. **Clean up food crumbs and grease** — the gel is attractive, but if there’s a free buffet around, roaches may ignore it.
6. **Be patient** — give it at least 2 weeks before judging.
