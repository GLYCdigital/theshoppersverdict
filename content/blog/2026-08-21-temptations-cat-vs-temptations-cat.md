## Where the Classic Crunchy Wins

### 1. Texture Consistency

The biggest complaint in the shared review pool for the “Crunchy and Soft” variant is that the soft center is sometimes *too* soft—or, conversely, not soft at all. Owners report that the texture varies batch to batch. The pure [Classic Crunchy](https://www.theshoppersverdict.com/pet-supplies/temptations-cat-treats-classic-chicken-flavor-review/) formula, on the other hand, delivers the same firm crunch every time. That consistency is why we gave it a 4.8/5 verdict versus 4.6/5 for the soft version.

### 2. Cats Seem to Prefer It

We parsed hundreds of comments mentioning “favorite treat” and “goes crazy for these.” The crunchy version came up nearly twice as often. Cats are texture-driven creatures, and the uniform crunch seems to trigger a stronger feeding response. If you have a picky eater, the crunchy version is the safer bet.

### 3. Less Mess, Better Storage

With the soft-center treats, owners mention pieces sticking together in the bag or leaving residue on fingers. The fully crunchy version stays loose and clean. That’s a small but real quality-of-life win for daily feeding.

### 4. Value for Multi-Cat Households

Because the crunchy version is more universally accepted (even by older cats who still have teeth), you’re less likely to have one cat turn up their nose. That means less waste and a smarter buy if you’re feeding multiple felines.

---

## Where the Classic Crunchy Falls Short

### 1. Not for Cats with Dental Sensitivity

If your cat has dental issues or is a senior with sore gums, the hard crunch can be a problem. The soft-center version is gentler on teeth, and some owners specifically buy it for that reason. For those cats, the crunchy version is a no-go.

### 2. Less Variety in Mouthfeel

Some cats get bored with the same texture day after day. The crunchy and soft combo offers a two-stage experience that can keep things interesting. If your cat is easily bored, the soft version might hold their attention longer.

---

## Where the Crunchy & Soft Version Wins

### 1. Gentler on Gums

For kittens losing baby teeth or seniors with dental problems, the soft center is a genuine advantage. Many reviewers specifically mention switching to this version after dental cleanings or extractions.

### 2. A Treat for Treat-Bored Cats

If your cat has been on the same crunchy treat for years and has lost interest, the novelty of a soft center can rekindle their excitement. It’s a simple way to mix up their routine without changing brands.

### 3. Easier to Break into Smaller Pieces

The soft center makes the treat easier to snap in half, which is handy for training or portion control. You can break one treat into two or three smaller rewards without crumbling.

---

## Where the Crunchy & Soft Version Falls Short

### 1. Texture Inconsistency

This is the #1 complaint. Some bags have a gooey center, others are nearly solid. This inconsistency can lead to your cat rejecting treats they previously loved—and you wasting money on a bag they won’t touch.

### 2. Stickiness Issues

Owners report treats sticking together in warm weather, or leaving a greasy residue on fingers and bowls. It’s a minor nuisance, but it’s a recurring theme in the reviews.

### 3. Lower Our Verdict Score

We gave it a 4.6/5 versus 4.8/5 for the crunchy version. That 0.2 gap is driven entirely by the texture complaints and the slightly higher rate of “my cat won’t eat these” comments.

## Who Each Is For

### Choose the Classic Crunchy if:

- Your cat has no dental issues and loves a satisfying crunch.
- You want the most consistent texture bag after bag.
- You have multiple cats with different preferences—this is the crowd-pleaser.
- You’re buying for the first time and want the safest bet.

### Choose the Crunchy & Soft if:

- Your cat is a senior or has dental sensitivity.
- Your cat is bored with the same crunch and needs a novelty texture.
- You want to easily break treats into tiny training pieces.
- You’re okay with some batch-to-batch variance in texture.
