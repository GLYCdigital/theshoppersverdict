## What to Avoid When Buying Luxury Beauty Gifts

Luxury beauty gifting has a few common pitfalls. Avoid these and you will do better than 90% of gift givers.

- **Do not buy a full skincare routine for someone else.** Skin types vary. What works for you might break them out. Stick to universally tolerated products like lip masks.
- **Do not buy scented products unless you know their preferences.** A perfume or strongly scented cream is a gamble. Fragrance-free is safer.
- **Do not buy tools that require skill.** A high-end eyeliner brush is useless to someone who does not wear eyeliner. A lip mask works for everyone.
- **Do not ignore the packaging.** Luxury beauty is partly about presentation. A product that arrives in a damaged box feels less special. Check return policies.
- **Do not forget the card.** A handwritten note explaining why you chose the gift makes it feel personal, even if the product is a cult classic.
