## Where the Baker Signature Wins

### 1. Perforations actually improve cooking

This is the strongest argument for the Baker Signature. The perforated design lets hot air circulate while still catching grease, so food cooks evenly instead of sitting in its own drippings. If you cook bacon, chicken wings, or anything that renders fat, that drainage matters more than it sounds. Soggy bottoms are a real problem with solid liners.

### 2. The 8-inch square fits without curling

The Baker Signature is a true 8-inch square, sized for most 4–6 quart baskets. The specific win here is that it doesn't curl or ride up the walls the way cheaper liners do. If you've ever pulled a warped, half-collapsed liner out of a hot basket, you know exactly why this is worth paying a little more for.

### 3. It has the higher Amazon rating

4.6/5 across 24,510 reviews. That's a higher star average than the Air Fryer Paper Liners. It's a narrow edge, but it's the kind of edge that comes from people noticing the perforations doing their job.

## Where Each One Falls Short

No product wins everything. Here's the honest downside of each.

### Air Fryer Paper Liners — the weak spots

- **Solid base means grease pools.** Without perforations, rendered fat sits on the paper under your food. For greasy proteins, that can mean a softer crust than you'd get with a draining liner.
- **Round shape isn't universal.** If your basket is square or rectangular, a round liner leaves corners exposed. It's designed for round 4–6 quart baskets, and that's where it shines.
- **Lower Amazon star rating.** 4.5 versus 4.6 is close, but it's not the top of the pile.

### Baker Signature Air Fryer Paper — the weak spots

- **It costs more.** About $11.99 versus $9.99, and you get slightly fewer sheets. The value math favors the other guy.
- **Perforations can let small bits through.** Tiny crumbs and thin batters can slip through the holes, which means a slightly dirtier basket than a solid liner would leave.
- **Lower review volume.** 24,510 is a big number, but it's meaningfully behind the Air Fryer Paper Liners' 31,988. Less crowd-sourced evidence, even if the average is higher.
