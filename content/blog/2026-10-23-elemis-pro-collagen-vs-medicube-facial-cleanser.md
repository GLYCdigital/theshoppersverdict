## Where Each Falls Short

No product is perfect, and these two have their weaknesses.

### ELEMIS Pro Collagen Cleansing Balm

- **Price:** The biggest barrier. It’s a significant investment, and not everyone can or wants to spend that much on a cleanser.
- **Packaging:** Some reviewers mention the jar can be messy, and you need a spatula (often included) to avoid getting water inside.
- **Not for oily skin?** While it’s generally well-tolerated, those with very oily or acne-prone skin might find it too rich, especially if not rinsed thoroughly.

### medicube Facial Cleanser Zero Pore

- **Not a makeup remover:** It struggles with waterproof mascara and long-wear foundation. You’ll need a first cleanse.
- **Potential dryness:** A few reviewers with dry skin found it slightly drying if used twice daily, though most found it gentle.
- **Ingredient transparency:** The brand doesn’t highlight specific active ingredients as much as ELEMIS does, which makes it harder to know exactly what’s doing the work.
