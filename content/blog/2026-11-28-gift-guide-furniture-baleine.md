Furniture gifts get a bad rap because people imagine hauling a sofa up three flights of stairs. But not all furniture gifts are big-ticket items. The key is matching the gift to the recipient's needs and space. ## Gift Strategy: How to Choose Furniture Gifts

Before you click 'add to cart,' consider these three rules:

- **Know their space.** A bed frame is useless if they already love theirs. A rug pad is universally useful.
- **Size matters.** Furniture gifts should be easy to integrate—avoid anything that requires rearranging a room.
- **Think practical luxury.** The best furniture gifts solve a problem or add comfort they wouldn't buy themselves.

With that in mind, let's dive into the picks.

## What to Avoid When Gifting Furniture

- **Oversized pieces.** Unless you know their exact space, avoid sofas, large tables, or anything that requires assembly.
- **Style-specific items.** A bold patterned chair might clash with their decor. Stick to neutral or classic designs.
- **Mattresses.** Too personal and too hard to return.
