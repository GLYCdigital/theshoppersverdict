## Gift Strategy: How to Pick Fitness Gear Without Guessing

Before you click anything, run your giftee through these three filters. It'll save you a return.

### 1. Match the gift to their *environment*, not their goals

Someone who says "I want to get in shape" could be a garage lifter, a hotel-gym traveler, or a weekend hiker. Ask where they'll use the gift. A weighted vest is brilliant for the home trainer and useless for the person who only does studio classes. A beach blanket is a home run for the traveler and a weird gift for the homebody.

### 2. Size-agnostic beats size-specific

Apparel and shoes are the highest-return categories in fitness gifting. Unless you know their exact size in that exact brand, skip it. Gear like vests with adjustable straps, bottles, and blankets removes the fit risk entirely.

### 3. Buy into a review base, not a product photo

A 4.6 rating means very different things at 40 reviews versus 15,000. Every pick in this guide has thousands of ratings behind it, which means the score reflects real-world durability, not launch-week enthusiasm.
