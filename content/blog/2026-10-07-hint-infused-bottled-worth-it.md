## What Reviewers Complain About

No product is perfect, and Hint has a few consistent criticisms. We sifted through the negative reviews so you don't have to.

### The Flavor Is Subtle — Sometimes Too Subtle

The most common complaint is that the flavor is faint. If you're used to Crystal Light or LaCroix, Hint might taste like barely-flavored water. One reviewer wrote, "I wanted to love it, but it's like drinking water with a hint of a memory of fruit." That's the trade-off for using only natural flavors without sweeteners — your brain expects sweetness when it tastes fruit, and without it, the flavor feels muted.

### Price Per Bottle Adds Up

At around $1.49 per bottle, Hint is not cheap. If you drink three a day, that's $4.50 daily — over $130 a month. Several reviewers mention that while they love the product, they can't justify the cost for everyday drinking. It's more of a treat or a soda replacement than a hydration staple for budget-conscious buyers.

### Some Flavors Are Polarizing

In a variety pack, you're bound to get at least one flavor you don't like. Grapefruit and watermelon tend to be love-it-or-hate-it. If you're picky, you might feel like you're paying for bottles you won't drink. That's a minor risk, but worth noting.

### Bottles Can Arrive Damaged

A handful of reviews mention leaking or dented bottles during shipping. Amazon is generally good about replacements, but it's an occasional annoyance. If you order, check the package on arrival.
