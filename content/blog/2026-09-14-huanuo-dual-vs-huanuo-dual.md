## Where Each One Falls Short

No product is perfect, and pretending otherwise is how review sites lose trust. Here is what to watch for on both.

### Shared weaknesses

- **Desk compatibility matters.** Both use clamp or grommet mounting. Very thick desks, glass desks or desks with a deep lip can cause problems. Measure before you buy.
- **Cable management is basic.** Neither is a cable-management showcase. You will want velcro straps or a tray to keep things tidy.
- **Setup takes patience.** Dual-arm mounts are not a five-minute install. Budget 30–60 minutes, especially if you are aligning two screens precisely.
- **VESA pattern check required.** Confirm your monitors are VESA-compatible (usually 75x75 or 100x100mm). Most are, but not all.

### Standard version quirks

- May feel slightly less rigid with very heavy 32-inch panels compared to the gaming variant.
- Styling is neutral — which is a plus for some, a minus for nobody, honestly.

### Gaming version quirks

- The gaming styling is not for everyone, especially in a shared or client-facing space.
- The price advantage swings with promotions — it is not always cheaper.
