## How We Determine "Worth It"

We don't just look at the percentage off. We look at three more important factors:

1. **Price history:** Is this an actual drop from the typical price, or a fake markdown? We track prices over months, not just the "list price" games.
2. **Review consistency:** A product with 4.8/5 from 44,000 reviews is more trustworthy than one with 5/5 from 20 reviews. All three above have massive sample sizes.
3. **Value per use:** A $70 fan used every night for 5 years costs $0.04 per use. A $9 marker used twice is $4.50 per use. Think in terms of cost per use, not sticker shock.

## The Hidden Cost of "Deals"

Here's a rule we apply to every deal roundup: **if you weren't going to buy it at full price, a 15% discount shouldn't change your mind.** The DREO fan and Fancy Feast both pass this test — they're products we'd recommend at full price. The Sharpie markers fail it because the current price is *higher* than normal, so you're not getting a deal at all.

That's the trap with deal roundups. Some sites will list anything with a slash-through price. We only list things where the math actually works in your favor. That's why this roundup has two buys and one skip.
