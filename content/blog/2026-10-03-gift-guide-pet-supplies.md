## Gift Strategy: How to Choose the Right Pet Gift

Before you hit "add to cart," consider these factors:

- **Budget:** You can find excellent gifts under $25, but premium options exist if you want to splurge.
- **Pet type:** Cats and dogs have different needs. Know who you're buying for.
- **Dietary restrictions:** Some pets have allergies or special diets. Stick to well-known brands with clear ingredients.
- **Personality:** Is the pet parent practical? Sentimental? Busy? Match the gift to their lifestyle.
- **Reviews:** Don't just trust the marketing. Look for products with high ratings and lots of feedback.
