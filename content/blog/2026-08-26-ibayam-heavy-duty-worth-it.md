## What Reviewers Complain About

No product is perfect, and the iBayam set has its share of grumbles. Here’s what the negative reviews (and some mixed ones) actually say:

### Not a True “Heavy Duty” Replacement

Some users who tried to use these as a replacement for industrial-grade shears were disappointed. If you’re cutting through thick stacks of cardboard every day (like in a warehouse or shipping department), these may not hold up as well as a dedicated pair of heavy-duty shears. They’re heavy-duty for home use, not commercial use.

### The Smallest Pair Is Almost Too Small

The 5.5-inch scissors are great for snipping threads or opening small packages, but a handful of reviewers found them too small for tasks like cutting paper or fabric. One user wrote, “The tiny ones are basically for thread. Don’t expect to cut a full sheet of paper with them comfortably.”

### Blade Alignment Issues (Occasional)

A small percentage of buyers reported that one of the three pairs arrived with slightly misaligned blades, causing them to “chew” rather than cut cleanly. This seems to be a quality-control hiccup rather than a widespread flaw—most reviewers got all three pairs in perfect working order.

### Not Great for Lefties

The handles are asymmetrical and clearly designed for right-handed users. Left-handed reviewers noted that the grip felt awkward, though the blades are technically ambidextrous. If you’re a lefty, you might want to look elsewhere or try them first.
