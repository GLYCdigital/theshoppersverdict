## Gift Strategy: How to Choose Without Overthinking

Before we dive into the picks, here's a simple framework to match a gift to the person. It's not rocket science, but it helps.

- **Know the budget first.** Set a range before you browse. It's easy to get upsold by fancy packaging. Our picks span roughly $10 to $39, so there's something for every wallet.
- **Match the energy.** Is the recipient active and outdoorsy? Creative and crafty? Anxious and in need of calm? The right toy fits their personality, not just their age.
- **Check the reviews, but read between the lines.** Look for consistent themes in pros and cons. A 4.5-star average with complaints about durability tells you more than a 5-star average with ten reviews.
- **Consider the parents.** If you're buying for a child, think about cleanup and noise. A gift that parents resent is a gift that gets quietly donated.
- **When in doubt, go consumable or collectible.** Things that get used up or added to are safer than one-and-done toys.
