No machine is perfect, and it would be dishonest to pretend otherwise.

### Cuisinart Food Processor Cup — the trade-offs

- **Standard feed tube:** you'll do more pre-chopping before ingredients go in. For whole fruits and vegetables, that's real added prep time.
- **Not the most convenient layout:** if you're frequently processing whole produce, the narrower feed tube becomes a daily annoyance.
- **Price is the same as the upgraded version:** at ~$339.95, there's no cost savings to justify choosing it over the wider-feed-tube model unless you specifically prefer it.

### Cuisinart Food Processor Cup Review — the trade-offs

- **Same price, so no discount for choosing it:** if you were hoping the wider feed tube came with a lower price, it doesn't.

- **Learning curve on dough:** like most food processors, dense dough takes a little practice to get right — the motor won't stall, but you still need to follow the recipe.
