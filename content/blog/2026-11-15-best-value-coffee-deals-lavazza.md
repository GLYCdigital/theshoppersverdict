## How We Judge a "Deal"

Most deal roundups are just discount percentages in a trench coat. We take a different approach: a deal only counts if the product clears a quality bar first. That's why every product in this roundup already has a verdict score from our own review process — we don't feature products we wouldn't recommend at full price just because they're temporarily cheaper.

The second filter is whether the current price is actually better than normal. We don't have historical price-tracking data on every product, and we're not going to pretend we do. What we can tell you honestly is whether the current price is in line with the product's usual range, and whether the value proposition makes sense at that number. Sometimes that means saying "this is a fair price, not a great one." That's more useful than a fake countdown timer.
