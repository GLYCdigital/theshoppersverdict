If you're chewing for dental reasons, xylitol gum makes more sense than sugar-sweetened alternatives.

## What Reviewers Actually Complain About

No product is perfect, and PUR's 4.5/5 rating hides a few consistent gripes. Here's what comes up again and again:

### 1. Xylitol Can Cause Digestive Upset in Sensitive People

This is the big one. Xylitol is a sugar alcohol, and sugar alcohols can cause bloating, gas, or laxative effects in people who are sensitive — especially if you chew a lot of pieces in one sitting. If you have IBS or a sensitive gut, this is a real consideration. It's not a defect in the product, but it's a dealbreaker for some.

### 2. It's Toxic to Dogs

Xylitol is dangerous for dogs, even in small amounts. If you have a dog that gets into bags or purses, this is a serious safety consideration. It's worth repeating: keep PUR Gum well out of reach.

### 3. Flavor Fades Faster Than Mainstream Gum

Some reviewers note the flavor doesn't last as long as conventional brands. That's the trade-off for a natural formula — but if you're used to gum that keeps its taste for an hour, adjust your expectations.

### 4. The Price Adds Up

At roughly $4.99 per pack, heavy chewers will notice the cost. Some reviewers say they ration it or buy in bulk to soften the blow.

### 5. Texture Isn't for Everyone

A minority of reviewers find the texture too firm or the pieces too small. It's a personal preference thing, not a quality issue.

PUR Gum isn't for everyone, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest:

- **You have a dog that gets into bags.** Xylitol is toxic to dogs. If you can't guarantee it stays out of reach, pick a different gum.

- **You're on a tight budget and chew a lot.** At ~$4.99 a pack, heavy chewers will feel it.
