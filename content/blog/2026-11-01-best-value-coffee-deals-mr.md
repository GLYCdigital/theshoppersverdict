## Mr Coffee Cup Mini Brew — The Best Deal in This Roundup

**Current price:** ~$26.99. **Verdict: 4.4/5. Worth it — strongly.**

We reviewed the [Mr Coffee Cup Mini Brew](/coffee/mr-coffee-5-cup-mini-brew-review/) in full, and the short version is this: it's a no-nonsense 5-cup brewer that does the basics extremely well. Two things stand out from the 27,799 Amazon reviews and our own testing.

- **Grab-a-cup auto pause.** This is the killer feature at this price point. You can pull the carafe out mid-brew to pour a cup without coffee dripping all over the hot plate. Sounds minor until you've owned a cheap brewer that doesn't do it and spent a Saturday scrubbing burnt coffee off the base.
- **Compact footprint.** At roughly 10.8 x 7.4 x 10.2 inches, it slides under most upper cabinets. If your counter space is a war zone, this matters more than any spec sheet will tell you.

At ~$26.99, you're paying entry-level money for a brewer that doesn't behave like entry-level. The auto-pause alone is usually reserved for machines that cost $20–$40 more. There's no timer, no programming, no smart features — and that's precisely the point. Fewer parts, fewer failures.

**Who should skip it:** If you need a programmable timer so coffee is ready when you wake up, this isn't that machine. If you routinely brew for four or more people, 5 cups will feel cramped. But for one or two people who want a hot, reliable cup without a learning curve, this is the deal to grab.

## HP 910XL Black High Yield Ink — The Boring Deal That Saves Real Money

**Current price:** ~$73.43. **Verdict: 4.6/5. Worth it — if you actually print.**

Let's be honest: nobody gets excited about ink. But the [HP 910XL Black High Yield cartridge](/home-office/hp-910xl-black-high-yield-ink-cartridge-works-with-officejet-8010-8020-review/) is one of the highest-scoring products we've reviewed, sitting at 4.6/5 across 22,128 Amazon ratings — and that's a category where customers are usually furious.

Why the high score? The XL designation. You're buying a high-yield cartridge that prints substantially more pages than the standard version, which means fewer reorders, fewer "printer offline at 11pm" emergencies, and a lower cost per page over the life of the cartridge. At ~$73.43 it looks expensive next to a $30 standard cartridge — until you do the math on how often you'd be rebuying.

**The catch, and we'll be blunt:** if you print a couple of pages a month, skip this. High-yield ink sitting in a printer for a year is money evaporating — cartridges can dry out or clog, and you'll have paid a premium for ink you never used. This deal rewards households and home offices that burn through black ink on documents, schoolwork, and shipping labels.

**Compatibility check before you buy:** this cartridge is built for the HP OfficeJet 8010 and 8020 series. Confirm your model in our full [HP 910XL review](/home-office/hp-910xl-black-high-yield-ink-cartridge-works-with-officejet-8010-8020-review/) before checkout — buying the wrong cartridge is the most common and most avoidable mistake in this category.

## How We Judge a "Deal"

A price drop is only a deal if the product was worth buying at full price in the first place. Our process is deliberately boring:

- **We start with the review score, not the discount.** A 3.2/5 product at 40% off is still a bad purchase. All three products here score 4.4/5 or higher in our own testing and on Amazon.
- **We check the review volume.** 20,000+ ratings means the score has survived real-world use, not just launch-week enthusiasm. Every product in this roundup clears that bar.
- **We ask what the money actually buys.** Auto-pause on a $27 brewer, high-yield page counts on a $73 cartridge, a flat cable on $9 earbuds — each of these is a concrete feature you'd otherwise pay more for.
- **We name the skip conditions.** Every product has a buyer it doesn't suit. If we can't articulate who should walk away, we haven't done the work.
