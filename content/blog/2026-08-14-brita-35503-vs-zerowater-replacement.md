## Where BRITA Standard Falls Short

### 1. Not for High TDS Water

If your water has high dissolved solids (like hard water or well water), BRITA won't remove them effectively. It's not designed to eliminate TDS—only certain contaminants.

### 2. No TDS Meter

ZeroWater includes a TDS meter to show you when to change the filter. BRITA relies on a time-based indicator (which some users find inaccurate).

### 3. Fewer Contaminants Removed

BRITA focuses on taste and odor, plus some heavy metals like copper and mercury. It doesn't remove as many contaminants as ZeroWater's 5-stage system.

## Where ZeroWater Falls Short

### 1. High Cost Per Filter

At ~$22.50 per filter, ZeroWater is expensive. And because filters die faster (especially with high TDS water), the annual cost can be three to four times higher than BRITA.

ZeroWater filters last 20-25 gallons—about half of BRITA's 40 gallons. In areas with very hard water, you might change filters every 2-3 weeks. That's a hassle and a budget drain.

### 3. Slower Filtration

### 4. Not Certified for Health Claims

While ZeroWater touts contaminant removal, it's only NSF/ANSI 42 certified (aesthetics). BRITA has both 42 and 53 certifications (health-related). For some buyers, this matters.

## FAQ

### 1. Which filter removes more contaminants: BRITA or ZeroWater?

ZeroWater removes more dissolved solids (TDS) due to its 5-stage ion-exchange system, including heavy metals like lead and chromium. BRITA focuses on chlorine, taste, and some metals but doesn't reduce TDS as dramatically. If you need near-zero TDS, ZeroWater wins.

### 2. How often should I replace each filter?

BRITA recommends every 40 gallons (about 2 months). ZeroWater suggests 20-25 gallons or when the TDS meter reads above 006 ppm—which could be as often as every 2-4 weeks for hard water. Check your water quality to gauge replacement frequency.

### 3. Is ZeroWater worth the extra cost?

Only if you have high TDS water or specific health concerns. For typical municipal water, BRITA is more cost-effective. ZeroWater's filter cost is more than double, and shorter lifespan makes it up to 4x more expensive annually.

### 4. Can I use these filters in any pitcher?

No. BRITA filters are designed for BRITA pitchers, and ZeroWater filters fit ZeroWater pitchers. They're not cross-compatible. Always check your pitcher model before purchasing replacements.
