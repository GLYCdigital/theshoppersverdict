## Where Each Falls Short

### Holstein Housewares 5-Cup Drip Coffee Maker

- **Glass carafe loses heat**: The included glass carafe doesn't retain heat as well as a thermal mug, so coffee cools faster. You may need to reheat or drink quickly.
- **No single-serve option**: If you only want one cup, you'll be brewing more than you need (though you can always make less).
- **Basic features**: No programmable timer, auto-shutoff (check model), or fancy extras. It's a straightforward drip machine.

### Elite Gourmet EHC Personal Single-Serve Brewer

- **Higher upfront cost**: At ~$35, it's about $8 more than the Holstein. However, you save on pods in the long run.
- **Only brews one cup at a time**: Not suitable for serving multiple people simultaneously. You'll need to brew sequentially.
- **Small water reservoir**: Designed for single servings, so you'll refill often if you want more than 14 oz.
- **Less versatile for entertaining**: If you often have guests, a 5-cup pot is more practical.
