**Is this the quick fix your lawn needs, or just green paint in a bottle? Here's what 200+ buyers discovered.**

You've got two options when your lawn looks like a patchwork of brown and green: wait weeks for grass to recover, or make it green today. The Petra PetraMax Grass Paint belongs firmly in the second camp — and with a 4.1-star average across 204 ratings, it's clearly working for a lot of people. But "working" has limits, and knowing them before you buy separates smart shoppers from disappointed ones.

**What you actually get**

The PetraMax comes as a concentrated liquid grass colorant. You mix it with water — the ratio depends on whether you want a light tint or full coverage — and spray it on. As shown in the product image, the concentrate is a deep green liquid that turns your water into something that looks like lawn dye. It's not a fertilizer, not a growth stimulant. It's purely cosmetic, designed to restore that perfect green look without the wait.

**The real-world performance**

This stuff works best on grass that's dormant, drought-stressed, or showing patchy discoloration. Buyers consistently report that one application transforms brown lawns back to vibrant green. A reviewer named Mark put it plainly: *"My backyard looked dead after a hot summer. Two hours after spraying, it looked like I'd laid new sod."*

The color holds up reasonably well. Most users report three to six weeks of visible green before reapplication is needed. The formula is biodegradable and non-toxic, which matters if you've got dogs or kids running around. Once it dries — usually within two to four hours depending on humidity — it's safe for foot traffic and pets. The manufacturer claims it won't harm beneficial soil microbes, and multiple buyers confirmed their lawns looked healthier after use, likely because the dye encourages them to water more consistently.

**Where it stumbles**

Let's be honest about the downsides, because they're real.

First, drying time matters more than you'd think. If you spray and then let the dog out too soon, you'll see green paw prints on your patio. Several reviewers mentioned this exact scenario. Plan your application for a morning when you can keep foot traffic off the lawn for at least four hours.

Second, rain is the enemy. A heavy downpour within 24 hours of application will wash significant color off. The label warns about this, but buyers still get caught. Check the forecast before you spray.

Third, the coverage claim is optimistic. The label suggests one gallon covers roughly 2,000 square feet, but multiple buyers with thicker grass reported getting only 1,200 to 1,500 square feet per gallon. Thick, dense turf absorbs more product. Buy extra if your lawn is lush but discolored.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does the color last?**
A: Most buyers report three to six weeks of visible green, depending on rainfall, foot traffic, and mowing frequency. Mowing will gradually remove the colored tips, so expect faster fading if you cut weekly.

**Q: Is it safe for pets?**
A: Yes, once dry. The formula is non-toxic and biodegradable. The bigger risk is tracking wet pigment indoors, so keep pets off until fully dry.

**Q: How many coats do I need?**
A: One coat for light discoloration. Heavily damaged lawns often need two coats, spaced a few hours apart. The second coat dramatically improves uniformity.
