**FAQ: Real Questions Buyers Ask**

**Q: Can I use this with a saltwater chlorine generator?**
Yes. It neutralizes free chlorine regardless of source. Turn off the SWG for 4–6 hours after application so it doesn't regenerate chlorine while you're trying to lower it.

**Q: Will it affect my pH or alkalinity?**
No. Sodium thiosulfate is pH-neutral in pool water. Retest after 30 minutes, but you shouldn't need to adjust anything else.

**Q: How long do I wait after adding before swimming?**
Most users report safe levels within 20–30 minutes with the pump running. Test before entering — don't trust the clock, trust the test kit.
