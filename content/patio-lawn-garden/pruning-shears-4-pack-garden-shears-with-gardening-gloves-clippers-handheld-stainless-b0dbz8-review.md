Let's be honest: most budget garden tool sets are junk you'll replace by July. This 4-pack of pruning shears with included gloves is not that. It's the rare value bundle that earns its place in your shed.

**What you actually get**

Four identical bypass pruning shears with stainless steel blades, ergonomic orange handles, and a pair of gardening gloves. Each shear measures roughly 8 inches long and weighs about 8 ounces — light enough for extended use but substantial enough to feel solid in hand. The blades are SK5 carbon steel, which is a meaningful upgrade over the cheap stainless you find on dollar-store clippers.

The gloves are the surprise here. They're not the flimsy cotton things that come with most kits. These have a nitrile-coated palm and fingers that actually grip wet stems and resist punctures from rose thorns. I've worn them through a full afternoon of deadheading and came away with zero scratches.

**Real-world performance**

The bypass design means one blade slides past the other for a clean cut, which is what you want for live growth. I tested these on everything from soft annuals to quarter-inch hardwood branches. The clean cuts on green wood are satisfying — no crushed stems, no tearing. On thicker, dried-out branches past half an inch, they struggle and require significant hand pressure. That's expected at this price point, but worth knowing.

What impressed me most is edge retention. After a month of regular use, the blades still slice through material cleanly without the ragged cuts that signal a dull edge. The included locking mechanism keeps blades closed for safe storage, though it does require two hands to engage — a minor annoyance when you're balancing on a ladder with a branch in one hand.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews reflects genuine satisfaction. One buyer noted, "I bought these for my mother who gardens daily. She's replaced three different pairs of expensive shears this year, but these are still going strong." Another mentioned, "The gloves alone are worth the price — they fit perfectly and I can finally prune my roses without bleeding."

**Where it falls short**

The locking mechanism genuinely frustrates. You need both hands — one to squeeze the handles, another to slide the lock — which is impractical mid-task. Several reviewers echoed this. The gloves are also a gamble if you're outside average hand sizes. They fit my medium-large hands fine, but a buyer with smaller hands reported them sliding around.

There's also no included sharpening tool. You'll need to pick up a carbide sharpener to maintain the edge, which adds to the total cost. And the included storage pouch is functional but thin — don't expect it to survive multiple seasons.

**Who should buy this**

This set is ideal for casual gardeners, first-time homeowners, or anyone who keeps losing shears in the yard. The four-pack means you can stash one in the garage, one in the shed, one in the kitchen drawer for herbs, and still have a backup. It's also a practical gift for new gardeners — the gloves make it feel complete.

Skip it if you're a professional landscaper or manage heavy pruning of thick branches. You'll want a forged steel bypass pruner with replaceable blades, and you'll pay three times as much for it.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Are these shears suitable for cutting thick branches?**
They handle live wood up to about half an inch diameter comfortably. Anything thicker — dead wood, large branches — will strain the blades and your hands. Use loppers for those.

**Do the gloves fit true to size?**
They're one-size-fits-most, designed for medium to large hands. If you wear size S or smaller, expect a loose fit. If you're XXL+, they'll be snug.

**Can the blades be sharpened?**
Yes. Use a carbide sharpener or a fine file along the bevel edge. The stainless steel responds well to sharpening, but you'll need to supply your own tool.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, affordable set of pruning shears that'll survive a season of regular yard work without breaking the bank. The included gloves are a legitimate bonus, not a throwaway.

**Skip it if** you need heavy-duty performance for thick branches or have non-standard hand sizes.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A smart value buy that beats expectations at this price point. The locking mechanism and glove sizing keep it from being perfect, but for most home gardeners, this is the last set of shears you'll need to buy this year.
