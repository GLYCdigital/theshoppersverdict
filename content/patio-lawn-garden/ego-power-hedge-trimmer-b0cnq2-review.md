The EGO Power+ Hedge Trimmer sits in an interesting spot. It's not the cheapest option, and it's not the most powerful gas model on the market. But for homeowners who've already invested in EGO's 56V ARC battery platform—or who are ready to ditch gas entirely—this trimmer represents the sweet spot between professional-grade performance and cordless convenience.

I tested this against overgrown boxwoods and thick privet, and the results surprised me. Here's the full breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the trimmer itself, a scabbard-style blade cover, and a basic manual. No battery, no charger. That's the first thing to know—if you're not already in the EGO ecosystem, factor in the cost of a 56V battery (2.5Ah runs around $150, 5Ah closer to $250) plus a charger.

The trimmer itself measures 42 inches from end to end, with a 26-inch dual-action blade. At 7.5 pounds with a 5Ah battery attached, it's light enough for overhead work without your arms giving out halfway through the job.

## Performance That Justifies the Hype

The 26-inch blade is the star here. Dual-action design means both blades move, which translates to significantly less vibration than single-blade trimmers. After 30 minutes of continuous trimming, my hands weren't numb—that's rare in this category.

What impressed me most was the branch capacity. EGO rates this for 3/4-inch branches, and it handled them cleanly. The gear-driven cutting mechanism doesn't stall under load like many cordless trimmers do. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: "Cuts through anything I've thrown at it without bogging down."

The variable-speed trigger deserves special mention. Feather the trigger for delicate shaping work around flowers, or squeeze it fully for aggressive cuts through overgrowth. There's enough control to make precise topiary cuts without accidentally shearing off a branch you wanted to keep.

Battery life depends heavily on your battery size and cutting conditions. With a 5Ah battery, expect 45-60 minutes of continuous runtime under normal conditions. Thick, woody growth will drain it faster—one reviewer noted "the battery drains noticeably quicker when cutting thick branches, but that's physics, not a flaw."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across 235 ratings at 4.7 stars, certain themes emerge. The weather resistance gets frequent mentions—the IPX4 rating means it shrugs off morning dew and light rain without issue. One buyer wrote: "I've used this in light drizzle and after leaving it out overnight by accident. Works perfectly every time."

The low vibration and quiet operation (82 dB, roughly half the noise of a gas trimmer) are also consistent praise points. "My neighbors actually thanked me for switching to electric," one reviewer noted.

## Where It Falls Short

The blade cover is disappointingly thin. It protects the blade adequately during storage, but it feels like an afterthought compared to the tool's otherwise robust construction. Several reviewers mentioned the cover cracking or warping over time.

The bigger issue is cost of entry. If you're not already using EGO tools, the total investment approaches $400 with battery and charger. That's steep compared to corded electric trimmers at $60-80. You're paying for the convenience of cordless operation and the quality of the cutting system—but it's a real premium.

Also note: this is a dedicated hedge trimmer. It can't convert to a pole saw or reach tall hedges beyond its 42-inch reach. If you have tall hedges, you'll need a ladder or a different tool entirely.

## Who Should Buy This

- **EGO ecosystem owners**: You already have batteries charging. This is a no-brainer addition.
- **Homeowners with medium-to-large hedges**: The runtime and cutting power handle serious trimming sessions without cord hassles.
- **Anyone tired of pull-start gas trimmers**: The instant start, low noise, and zero maintenance make the switch worth it.
- **Skip it if**: You have a tiny yard with minimal hedging (a corded trimmer suffices), or you need pole-reach capability for tall hedges.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work with all EGO batteries?**
A: Yes, all EGO 56V ARC batteries fit and work, from the 2.0Ah up to the 12Ah. Smaller batteries give you roughly 20-30 minutes; larger ones push past an hour.

**Q: How loud is it compared to a gas trimmer?**
A: It's rated at 82 dB, which is noticeably quieter than gas models that typically run 95-100 dB. Normal conversation is about 60 dB for reference. You can use it early morning without annoying neighbors.

**Q: Can it handle overgrown, neglected hedges?**
A: It cuts branches up to 3/4 inch cleanly. For anything thicker, you'll want loppers first. But for neglected hedges with mixed growth, it handles the vast majority without stalling.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Buy it if you're already in the EGO ecosystem or ready to commit to quality cordless tools and want a hedge trimmer that performs like a professional unit. Skip it if you're on a tight budget or only trim hedges a few times per year—a corded trimmer covers that use case at a fraction of the cost.

For everyone else, this is the hedge trimmer that makes you wonder why you ever wrestled with extension cords or gas fumes in the first place.
