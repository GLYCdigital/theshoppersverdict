**Is the Greesum 3-piece patio set a genuine bargain or just another cheap wicker set that'll fade by August? I dug through 215 ratings, the spec sheet, and what actual owners are saying to give you the straight answer.**

Let's be honest about what you're getting here. At this price point, most "rattan" sets are actually plastic resin wrapped around hollow tubes. The Greesum is different — the listing specifies a powder-coated steel frame underneath a hand-woven PE rattan exterior. That's a meaningful distinction. Steel doesn't flex and crack the way aluminum tubing does on cheaper sets, and the powder coating resists rust better than painted frames.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get three pieces: a two-seater loveseat (about 47 inches wide) and two matching ottomans. The ottomans are the real value here — they're not just footrests. Push them against the loveseat and you've got a chaise lounge. Pull them away and you have two extra guest seats or makeshift side tables. The set also includes three seat cushions and two back cushions, all with removable, machine-washable covers.

Assembly is straightforward but not effortless. One reviewer noted, "Took me about an hour with a friend. The bolts are clearly labeled, but you'll want a decent screwdriver — the included Allen wrench works but your wrist will thank you for a power drill." Another mentioned that the pre-drilled holes on one armrest didn't line up perfectly, requiring a bit of persuasion. Plan for 45–60 minutes and have a second person help flip the frame.

## Real-World Performance

The 4.3-star average across 215 ratings holds up when you look at the detailed feedback. Owners consistently praise the sturdiness. One five-star review states: "I was skeptical about the weight capacity, but I'm 230 lbs and sat on the ottoman — no creaking, no wobble. Feels solid." The woven rattan is UV-treated, which matters if your patio gets direct afternoon sun. That said, several reviewers recommend a cover during winter months, especially in wet climates.

The cushions are where opinions split. They're firm — some buyers love that for back support, others find them too hard for long lounging sessions. The seat depth is roughly 21 inches, which is shallower than typical outdoor sectionals. If you're over 6 feet tall, your knees will sit higher and the backrest may hit you mid-shoulder blade rather than supporting your full back.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the cushion firmness and shallow depth, the lack of a table is the most common complaint. You'll need to buy a separate coffee table or side table — there's no flat surface built into this set. Also, the color in the product photos reads as a warm brown, but several buyers noted it's actually more of a grayish-taupe in person. Check the image carefully if color matching matters to you.

## Who Should Buy This

This set is ideal for:
- **Apartment balconies or small patios** — the footprint is compact (roughly 47" x 27" as a loveseat)
- **First-time patio furniture buyers** — solid construction without the $1,000+ price tag
- **People who entertain casually** — the modular ottomans adapt to different seating arrangements

Skip it if you're over 6'2", want a deep lounging seat for afternoon naps, or need an all-in-one set with a built-in table.

## FAQ

**Q: Will the rattan fade or crack after one season?**
A: The PE rattan is UV-stabilized, so it resists fading better than untreated wicker. However, it's not indestructible — use a cover in winter and avoid leaving it in standing water. Most owners report no visible wear after a full season.

**Q: Are the cushion covers really removable?**
A: Yes, all cushion covers have zippers and are machine-washable on a gentle cycle. Air dry them — putting them in the dryer can shrink the fabric.

**Q: Can I leave this out in rain?**
A: The frame is rust-resistant and the rattan is water-resistant, but the cushions will absorb moisture. Bring cushions inside during heavy rain or invest in a waterproof cover.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a sturdy, good-looking, modular seating set that won't break the bank and you're willing to spend an hour assembling it.

**Skip it if** you need deep, plush seating for tall family members or want a table included.
