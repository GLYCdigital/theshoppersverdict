Let me be direct: If you own a Ninja Woodfire Outdoor Grill, you already know the one thing it doesn't do well — sit somewhere convenient. It's a countertop unit that wants to be a standalone grill. This cart solves that problem, and for the most part, it solves it properly.

## What You Actually Get

The package arrives with the cart frame, two side shelves, four caster wheels (two locking), and all necessary hardware. Assembly is straightforward but not instant — plan for 30 to 45 minutes with a Phillips screwdriver. The steel frame has a matte black powder-coated finish that matches the Ninja's aesthetic well enough that it looks like an OEM accessory rather than an afterthought.

The cart is rated for grills up to roughly 60 pounds. The Ninja Woodfire sits on it with room to spare, and the side shelves fold down when not in use — a thoughtful touch for storage in tight garages or balconies.

## Real-World Performance

The headline feature is compatibility. This cart fits the Ninja Woodfire Outdoor Grill, Blackstone griddles, and Ooni pizza ovens. That's not marketing fluff — the mounting brackets are adjustable, and the platform is wide enough to accommodate different footprint sizes. If you're the type who swaps cooking gear seasonally, this is one cart that won't become obsolete when you upgrade your grill.

The locking caster wheels are the standout feature. They're not cheap plastic casters — they're rubber-coated and roll smoothly over pavers and deck boards. The locks hold firmly, which matters when you're sliding a loaded grill across an uneven surface. One buyer noted, *"The wheels lock solidly — my Ninja doesn't budge even when I'm scraping the grates with force."*

The side shelves handle moderate weight. You can park a cutting board, a platter of seasoned meat, or a sauce caddy without worrying about collapse. But don't expect to load them like a prep station — they're rated for lighter duty, and the frame will wobble slightly if you lean on them.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 225 ratings, the recurring themes are stability and fit. Multiple reviewers mention that the Ninja Woodfire sits level without shimming, which is more than some competing carts can claim. One verified buyer wrote, *"Finally my grill isn't wobbling on a flimsy folding table. This thing is rock solid."* Another praised the compact footprint: *"Fits perfectly on my small balcony — takes up less space than my old plastic cart and looks way better."*

The weather resistance also earns consistent mentions. The powder-coated finish has held up through rain and sun exposure without rusting for several long-term reviewers.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. Assembly is the biggest friction point — the instructions are adequate but not exceptional, and the bolt alignment requires some fiddling. If you're not handy, budget extra time.

The lack of built-in storage is a genuine miss. There are no hooks for tools, no rail for a towel, no shelf underneath the main platform. For a cart in this price range, that's a notable omission. You'll likely add a magnetic tool holder or a hanging hook system yourself.

The side shelves, while foldable, don't lock into place when extended. A firm bump can fold them down mid-cook. It's not a safety issue, but it's mildly annoying if you're working around the cart.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You own a Ninja Woodfire (or plan to) and want a permanent, stable home for it that doesn't require a table or counter. Also ideal for Blackstone griddle owners who want mobility without buying a dedicated, more expensive griddle stand.

**Skip it if:** You need integrated storage, or you're looking for a cart that doubles as a full prep station with drawers and hooks. This is a purpose-built stand, not a kitchen island.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit the Ninja Woodfire ProConnect or older models?**
A: Yes, the adjustable platform accommodates all Ninja Woodfire models, including the newer ProConnect series. Just verify your grill's base dimensions — if it's wider than 24 inches, double-check the platform width before ordering.

**Q: Can I leave the cart outside year-round?**
A: The powder-coated steel handles rain and sun well, but you'll extend its life by covering it during harsh winters or heavy storms. The wheels and locking mechanisms benefit from occasional lubrication if you live in a humid climate.

**Q: Does it work for the Ooni Karu 16 or only the smaller models?**
A: It fits Ooni pizza ovens up to the Karu 16, but keep in mind that the Karu 16's legs extend beyond the cart's platform. It works, but it's a tighter fit — the Blackstone and Ninja are the ideal matches.
