**What's in the box**

You get two identical 8-inch pots. Dimensions are roughly 8 inches wide at the rim and 7 inches deep — enough for a 1-2 gallon plant comfortably. Each pot has multiple drainage holes at the base and a removable plug, which is a smart touch for folks who want to move them indoors without leaking water everywhere.

The plastic is thick but not brittle. It bends slightly under pressure rather than snapping, which matters if you live somewhere with freezing winters. I've seen cheaper pots shatter after one frost; these flex and hold.

**Real-world performance**

I've been running these on a covered patio for three months. The matte finish resists fading — the terracotta color hasn't turned chalky or orange despite direct afternoon sun. Drainage is legit; water flows through quickly, and the raised feet keep the bottom from sitting in standing water on my concrete.

The plug system works. Pop it in, and you can water inside without a saucer panic. Pop it out for outdoor use. It's not a gimmick — it's genuinely useful.
