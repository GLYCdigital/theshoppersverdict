## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and the Dumpy Square has a few honest limitations.

**The reservoir capacity is modest.** For larger plants or during hot summer weeks, you might need to refill every 5-7 days rather than the 2+ weeks some competing planters advertise. If you're planning a month-long vacation, this isn't your answer.

**The wicking system requires the right setup.** If you pack the soil too densely or use a mix that doesn't wick well, the system won't work as intended. One reviewer noted: *"You have to follow the instructions about soil type carefully. I used regular potting soil and it worked fine, but a friend used a heavier mix and the water wasn't reaching the roots."*

**Drainage is minimal.** While this is by design (it's a self-watering planter), it means you can't easily flush excess salts or fertilizer from the soil. For heavy feeders, you'll need to occasionally water from the top to keep the soil healthy.

**The "dumpy" proportions aren't for everyone.** The squat, wide shape is efficient but not elegant. If you're looking for a tall, dramatic planter, this isn't it.

## FAQ

**Q: How often do I need to refill the reservoir?**
A: For most houseplants and herbs, plan on refilling every 5-7 days. During hot summer months, check it more frequently. The water level indicator takes the guesswork out of it.

**Q: Can I use this outdoors year-round?**
A: The UV-resistant plastic holds up well outdoors, but you'll want to bring it inside or empty the reservoir before freezing temperatures — expanding ice can crack the plastic.

**Q: What soil should I use?**
A: A standard, well-draining potting mix works best. Avoid heavy garden soil or mixes with large chunks of bark that can impede wicking.
