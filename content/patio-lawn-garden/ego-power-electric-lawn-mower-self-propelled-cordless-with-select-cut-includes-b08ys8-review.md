# EGO POWER+ Electric Lawn Mower: The Cordless That Finally Feels Like a Gas Replacement

Most cordless mowers compromise. You get convenience but lose cutting power. You get quiet operation but accept flimsy decks. The EGO POWER+ Self-Propelled with Select Cut is the rare exception — it's the mower that made me seriously consider retiring my gas Toro.

## What's Actually in the Box

The package includes the 21-inch steel deck mower, the 56V 10Ah ARC lithium battery, the rapid charger, a grass bag, and the Select Cut dual-blade system with a mulch plug. No assembly required beyond unfolding the handle — it clicks into place with two knobs. The bag holds 2.2 bushels, which is smaller than typical gas mowers, but the dual blades mulch finely enough that you'll empty it less often than you expect.

## Real-World Performance

The headline feature is Select Cut — EGO's dual-blade design that cuts grass twice per pass. On my fescue lawn, the difference is visible. Clippings are nearly dust-fine. I stopped raking after the second mow because there was nothing left to rake. The 21-inch cutting deck handles a 0.5-acre yard in about 45 minutes on a single charge, and the battery indicator still showed 2 bars of 5 remaining.

The self-propelled drive is where EGO separates from cheaper competitors. The variable-speed trigger lets you dial in walking pace, and the rear-wheel drive pulls you up slopes that stall most front-drive electrics. I tested it on a 12-degree incline — no bogging, no wheel spin.

**Cut quality** is genuinely good. The deck is stamped steel, not the thin aluminum you'll find on budget cordless models. It's 10-gauge steel and resists dents. The 5-position height adjustment ranges from 1.5 to 4 inches, controlled by a single lever on the deck.

## What 225 Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.5-star average holds up across the board. The most repeated praise centers on battery longevity. One verified buyer wrote: *"I have a third-acre lawn and can mow it twice on one charge. My old Ryobi died halfway through. This is a whole different class."* Another noted: *"The power is shocking for electric. It never bogs down in thick spring grass."*

Multiple reviewers mention the quiet operation — it's noticeably quieter than gas but still has enough rumble to feel substantial, not like a toy.

## Where It Falls Short

At 67 pounds, this is not a light mower. The 10Ah battery is heavy, and lifting the unit into a truck bed or up stairs is a workout. If you have a flat yard with a shed, you won't care. If you're hauling it weekly, you will.

The mulch plug is a common complaint. One reviewer put it plainly: *"If you don't slam the plug in hard, you get a trail of grass behind the deck."* It's a minor annoyance but worth knowing — seat it firmly.

The bag is smaller than gas equivalents. You'll empty it more often on large lawns, though the fine mulching partially compensates.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you have a quarter to half-acre lawn, want to ditch gas and oil changes, and care about cut quality. It's ideal for suburban homeowners who value quiet morning mowing without disturbing neighbors.

**Skip it if** you have over 0.75 acres, steep terrain beyond 15 degrees, or a tight budget — this is a premium-priced mower. Also skip if you need a self-propelled unit that folds vertically for minimal garage storage; the handle folds but the mower doesn't stand upright.

## FAQ

**How long does the battery really last?**
On a standard 0.25-acre lot, expect 60-75 minutes of continuous mowing. For 0.5 acres, you'll finish with 20-30% remaining. The 10Ah battery is the largest EGO offers.

**Does it work with other EGO batteries?**
Yes, it's compatible with all EGO 56V ARC batteries. If you already own EGO tools, you can buy the bare tool version and save money.

**Is the deck really steel?**
Yes, 10-gauge stamped steel. It's heavier than aluminum decks but far more durable for years of service.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The EGO POWER+ Select Cut is the closest thing to a gas mower without the gas. It's powerful, well-built, and the cut quality justifies the premium price. The weight and finicky mulch plug are the only real drawbacks. For most homeowners with average-sized yards, this is the last mower you'll need to buy for a decade.

**Buy it if** you want gas-level performance with zero maintenance headaches. **Skip it if** you have a huge lawn, steep terrain, or a tight budget — then look at the EGO 20-inch model or a gas alternative.
