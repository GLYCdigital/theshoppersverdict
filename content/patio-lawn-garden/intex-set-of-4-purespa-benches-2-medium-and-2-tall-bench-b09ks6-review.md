**Skip the soggy towel pile. These benches solve the "where do I sit in my inflatable spa?" problem properly.**

If you own an Intex PureSpa, you already know the built-in seats are basically inflatable ledges—fine for leaning, useless for actually sitting. This four-piece bench set is the aftermarket fix that 229 Amazon buyers have rated 4.6 stars. I've dug through the feedback, specs, and real-world photos to tell you whether they're worth the spend.

**What you get in the box**

Four separate benches: two medium and two tall. The mediums measure roughly 17 inches high, the talls about 21 inches—both with a 19.7-inch wide seat. Each bench is a welded steel tube frame with UV-resistant resin slats. The set ships flat-ish, and you assemble each one with eight bolts. No tools included, so have a Phillips screwdriver handy. Total assembly for all four: about 20 minutes if you're methodical, 15 if you've done this before.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The steel frames are the standout. At 250 pounds per bench, these aren't flimsy folding chairs. Buyers consistently note the stability—one reviewer wrote, *"I'm 230 lbs and these don't shift at all when I get in and out of the tub."* The rubber-sheathed feet are a thoughtful touch; they grip slick patio stone and won't scratch the inside of your spa liner if you place them inside the tub.

The resin slats are the right call over fabric or mesh—they dry fast, won't mildew, and don't absorb chlorine. But here's the honest tradeoff: they're smooth when wet. A few reviewers mentioned slipping when the benches were soaked. Not a dealbreaker, but if you have mobility issues, budget for adhesive grip strips.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The two-height system is the most-cited win. A reviewer noted, *"I'm 5'2\" and my husband is 6'1\"—the mediums are perfect for me, the talls for him. Finally we can both sit comfortably."* Multiple buyers confirmed the benches fit inside the PureSpa Plus and Saluspa models without crowding. The 4.6-star average holds up across both short and long-term ownership—no structural failure reports, no rust complaints after seasonal use.

**Where it falls short**

The tall benches are *tall*. If you're under 5'4", your feet will dangle. That's not a flaw for the intended audience, but it's worth knowing before you buy. Assembly requires a bit of torque—one reviewer called it *"fiddly but manageable."* And there's no carrying handle, so moving them from patio to storage is a two-hand job. Minor stuff, but real.

**Who should buy this**

- **Intex PureSpa or Saluspa owners** who want actual seating instead of inflatable ledges
- **Couples or households with height differences**—the two-size set is genuinely clever
- **Anyone who wants benches that double as poolside seating**—they're sturdy enough for dry use too

**Skip it if** you're under 5'3" and want the tall benches for yourself, or if you need a bench that's safe when soaking wet without modifications.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do these fit inside the Intex PureSpa Plus?**
A: Yes. The medium benches (17") fit comfortably inside most 4-person PureSpa models. The talls are better placed outside the tub for entry/exit, or inside if you're tall enough to use them without your feet floating.

**Q: Can I leave them outside year-round?**
A: The steel frame is powder-coated and the resin slats are UV-resistant, but winter freezing can weaken the welds over time. Bring them inside for storage if you live in a freeze-thaw climate.

**Q: Will they fit other brands of inflatable spas?**
A: Likely, but measure your tub's interior diameter first. The benches are 19.7" wide, so they fit most 6-8 person inflatable spas, but Intex's own models are the guaranteed fit.
