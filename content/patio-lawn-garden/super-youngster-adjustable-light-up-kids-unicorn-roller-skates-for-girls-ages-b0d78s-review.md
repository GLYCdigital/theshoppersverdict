If your kid only skates midday, the glow factor diminishes significantly.

**Who should buy this:** Parents with kids aged 3-7 who want a single pair of skates that won't be outgrown in six months. Families who skate on smooth sidewalks or indoor rinks. Anyone who wants the light-up effect without dealing with battery replacements.
