**The Andersons Professional DGL Dark Green Lawn™ 25-0-0 Fertilizer Review: High-Nitrogen Punch for a Quick Green-Up**

Most lawn fertilizers promise results. Few deliver them fast enough to notice. The Andersons Professional DGL Dark Green Lawn 25-0-0 is one of the exceptions — a 25% nitrogen formula that, when applied correctly, visibly darkens turf within days. But this isn't a complete fertilizer, and it's not for every lawn. Here's the honest breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

This is a 25-0-0 fertilizer — all nitrogen, no phosphorus, no potassium. That's a deliberate choice for one job: pushing rapid vegetative growth and chlorophyll production. The bag uses Andersons' DGL (Disperse Granule Technology), which means the particles are designed to dissolve quickly and spread evenly, unlike the cheap coated granules that bounce off your lawn and into the sidewalk. One bag covers roughly 5,000 square feet at the standard application rate of 4.3 lbs per 1,000 sq ft — enough for a quarter-acre lot with a bit to spare.

**Performance and Real-World Results**

The nitrogen source is a blend of fast-release urea and slow-release sulfur-coated urea. That split matters. The fast-release portion gives you the dramatic color change within 3-5 days — buyers repeatedly mention "dark green in under a week" — while the slow-release fraction feeds the lawn for another 4-6 weeks, reducing the chance of a sudden growth flush that leaves you mowing every other day.

Build quality is what you'd expect from a professional-grade product. The granules are uniform in size, which means your spreader settings stay consistent. One reviewer noted, "First time using DGL technology and I'm sold — no stripes, no missed patches, just even color across the whole yard." Another said, "Applied with a cheap Scotts spreader and got better coverage than the big-box store brands I've used for years."

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews reflects two recurring themes. First, speed: multiple reviewers report visible results within days, not weeks. Second, cost-effectiveness: because the nitrogen is concentrated, you use less product per application than a diluted blended fertilizer. One verified buyer wrote, "This is the greenest my lawn has been in 10 years. Used it in early spring and again in fall. Amazing." Another noted, "My lawn guy recommended this and I was skeptical — now I'm ordering a second bag."

**Where It Falls Short**

This is a specialized tool, not a complete lawn care solution. With zero potassium, your grass lacks the nutrient it needs for root development, drought tolerance, and disease resistance. If you apply this exclusively, you'll get a green lawn that struggles under stress. You'll need a balanced fertilizer (like a 20-0-5 or 10-10-10) in the offseason to maintain soil health.

The smell is another consideration. High-nitrogen fertilizers carry a distinct ammonia odor that lingers for a day or two after application. It's not overwhelming, but if you have pets that roam the yard or plan to host an outdoor gathering, time your application accordingly.

Finally, watering is non-negotiable. The fast-release nitrogen needs to be watered in within 24 hours — ideally within 12 — or you'll lose a significant portion to the atmosphere. That's not a product flaw; it's how nitrogen works. But it's a step you can't skip.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is ideal for two types of homeowners: those who've done a soil test and know their phosphorus and potassium levels are already adequate (and just need a nitrogen boost), and those who want a quick cosmetic green-up before a big event or for a lawn that's healthy but looking tired. It's also a strong pick for cool-season grasses like fescue and bluegrass, and works well on warm-season Bermuda and zoysia when applied during active growth.

Skip it if you're looking for a single all-in-one fertilizer, if your lawn has never been soil-tested, or if you live somewhere with water restrictions that might prevent you from watering it in promptly.

**FAQ**

**Q: How often can I apply this?**  
A: Every 6-8 weeks during the growing season. Don't exceed 4 pounds of actual nitrogen per 1,000 sq ft per year, so roughly 3-4 applications max.

**Q: Will this burn my lawn?**  
A: Only if you misapply. Follow the label rate of 4.3 lbs per 1,000 sq ft, water it in within 24 hours, and you're safe. Double the rate and you'll see browning within a week.

**Q: Can I use this on a new lawn?**  
A: No. Newly seeded or sodded lawns need a starter fertilizer with phosphorus (the middle number) for root establishment. Wait until the lawn is at least 6-8 weeks old.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want fast, dramatic greening, you've confirmed your lawn doesn't need phosphorus or potassium, and you're willing to follow the watering requirement.

**Skip it if** you want an all-in-one fertilizer, your soil is untested, or you can't commit to watering it in promptly.

The Andersons Professional DGL 25-0-0 earns its 4.6-star rating. It does exactly what it claims — quickly, evenly, and cost-effectively — as long as you understand it's a tool with a specific job, not a complete lawn care program.
