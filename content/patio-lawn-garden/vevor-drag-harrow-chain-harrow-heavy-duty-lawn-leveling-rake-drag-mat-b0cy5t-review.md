Let me be blunt: if you've been manually raking your lawn to level out bumps and divots, you're wasting hours you'll never get back. The VEVOR Drag Harrow is the kind of tool that makes you wonder why you didn't buy it sooner — but it's not without quirks. ## What You're Actually Getting

The VEVOR is a chain drag harrow — essentially a mat of interlinked steel chains with serrated teeth that drag behind your ATV, UTV, or tractor. As shown in the product image, it's a wide, flat mat that looks deceptively simple. That simplicity is the point. The steel links are welded, not just crimped, which matters when you're dragging it over rocky soil.

You get the harrow itself, a tow bar that attaches to your vehicle's hitch, and the hardware to connect everything. Don't expect a manual — assembly is straightforward but does require basic tools.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where this tool earns its keep. Owners consistently report that it shreds through lawn leveling tasks that would take days by hand. One buyer noted, *"Leveled my entire 1-acre yard in an afternoon behind my ATV. The soil was compacted and rocky — this thing chewed through it and left a smooth, seed-ready surface."*

Beyond leveling, the VEVOR pulls double duty. Users report using it to:
- Break up and spread topsoil over new lawns
- Work compost into existing turf
- Prep seedbeds by exposing fresh soil
- Drag behind a tractor for pasture maintenance

The weight — roughly 50 pounds depending on width — is a feature, not a bug. It keeps the teeth engaged with the ground so you're actually moving soil, not just skimming it. The chain links flex and conform to uneven terrain, which is why it handles bumpy yards better than rigid drag mats.

## What Owners Consistently Praise

The build quality gets the most love. Multiple reviewers mention the welded construction holding up after seasons of use. One wrote, *"I've dragged this over roots, rocks, and clay for two years. Still straight, still tight, no broken links."* That's the durability you're paying for.

Speed is the other big win. If you have a large property, this isn't a luxury — it's a necessity. Manual leveling on anything over a quarter-acre is a weekend-killer. The VEVOR turns it into a 30-minute chore.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. Assembly is the most common complaint. Several buyers report that the bolt holes on the tow bar don't align perfectly with the harrow frame, requiring a drill or file to make things fit. It's a 15-minute fix, but annoying when you're eager to get to work.

The weight cuts both ways. If you don't have an ATV, UTV, or tractor, this is the wrong tool. Dragging it by hand is miserable, and the instructions don't do enough to warn you about that. You need a vehicle with a hitch, period.

Also, be careful on thin or patchy lawns. The teeth are aggressive. One reviewer warned, *"If your grass is already struggling, this will tear it up more before it levels. Wait until you're ready to overseed."* That's solid advice — this is a renovation tool, not a maintenance tool.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You own an ATV or tractor, have a lawn over half an acre with noticeable bumps or uneven spots, and you're preparing to overseed or lay new sod. This is your tool.

**Skip it if:** You have a small yard, don't own a tow vehicle, or your lawn is mostly healthy with just a few low spots. You'd be better served by a manual leveling rake or hiring a pro.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this by hand without a vehicle?**
Technically, yes. Realistically, no. At 50+ pounds with aggressive teeth, you'll exhaust yourself in minutes. This is designed to be towed.

**Q: Will it damage my existing grass?**
It can, especially on thin turf. The teeth dig in to level soil, which means they'll tear up struggling grass. Use it when you're ready to renovate or overseed, not for routine maintenance.

**Q: How do I attach it to my ATV?**
The tow bar connects to your vehicle's standard trailer hitch. Most buyers use a clevis pin or hitch pin — check your hitch size before ordering to ensure compatibility.
