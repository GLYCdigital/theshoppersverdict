**What You’re Actually Getting**

The box contains the trimmer head, a metal blade attachment (that’s the “brush cutter” part), a wheeled guide for edging, a telescoping shaft, and the 6000mAh battery with charger. It’s a 4-in-1 system: string trimmer, brush cutter, edger, and wheeled trimmer. The shaft extends, which helps if you’re taller or have deep beds to reach.

The battery is the headline spec. In theory, 6000mAh should give you 45-60 minutes of run time. In practice, that number depends heavily on what you’re cutting. Light grass on a low speed? You’ll get close to the claim. Thick weeds or using the metal blade? Expect that time to drop by a third or more.

**FAQ: What Shoppers Ask**

**Q: Does the 6000mAh battery really last 60 minutes?**
A: Only on the lowest speed with light grass. Real-world use with normal cutting gets you 25-40 minutes. Thick weeds will cut that further.

**Q: Can I use this to clear blackberry vines or dense brush?**
A: The metal blade will handle small vines up to half an inch, but it’s not a brush hog. For dense growth, you’ll burn through the battery fast and the vibration is tiring.

**Q: Is the string easy to replace?**
A: It’s standard 0.065-inch line, but the feed mechanism is the weak point. Expect to open the head and manually re-feed more often than you’d like.
