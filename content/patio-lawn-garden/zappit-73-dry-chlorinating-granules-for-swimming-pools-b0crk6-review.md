## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the tradeoffs. Because this is calcium hypochlorite, every dose adds calcium to your pool water. Over months of regular use, calcium hardness can creep up, especially in areas with naturally hard tap water. When that happens, you'll see scaling on tiles and cloudy water that won't clear with algaecide. You can manage this with a hardness test kit and occasional partial drain/refill, but it's an extra chore you don't have with liquid chlorine or salt systems.

Second, this product is not compatible with saltwater chlorine generators. If you run a salt system, skip this entirely—the added calcium will cause scaling inside the cell. Similarly, don't mix it with other chemicals in the same treatment; always dissolve in a bucket first and add separately.

**Q: Will it turn my pool cloudy?**
