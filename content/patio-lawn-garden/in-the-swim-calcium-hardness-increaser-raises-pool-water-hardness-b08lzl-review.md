The critical step — and where most people mess this up — is pre-dissolving. You absolutely cannot broadcast this directly into the pool. ## FAQ

**How much do I add for my pool size?**
For every 10,000 gallons, add 1 pound to raise hardness by 10 ppm. Test first, subtract your current reading from your target (typically 200–400 ppm for plaster, 150–250 for vinyl/fiberglass), and dose accordingly. Never add more than 2 pounds per 10,000 gallons per day.

**Can I swim while the pump runs after adding?**
Wait at least 4–6 hours after adding, and retest before swimming. The calcium needs time to fully dissolve and distribute. Swimming earlier risks cloudy water and potential skin irritation from concentrated pockets.

**Does this work for saltwater pools?**
Yes, but monitor your salt levels. The sodium component will nudge salinity upward slightly. If you're already near your generator's upper salt threshold, dose in smaller increments over multiple days.
