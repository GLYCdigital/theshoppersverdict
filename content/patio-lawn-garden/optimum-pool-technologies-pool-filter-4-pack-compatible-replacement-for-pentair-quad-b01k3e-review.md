Let's cut to the chase: pool filters are boring, expensive, and nobody wants to think about them. But if you're running a Pentair Quad DE system, you already know the OEM cartridges cost an arm and a leg. The Optimum Pool Technologies 4-pack promises the same filtration at a fraction of the price. Does it deliver? After digging through 200 ratings and the technical specs, here's the real story.

**What's actually in the box**

You get four replacement cartridges designed to drop into Pentair Quad DE 60, 80, and 100 filter housings. Each cartridge measures roughly 9.5 inches in diameter with a standard 3-inch threaded riser tube — the same dimensions as the OEM part. The package includes no gaskets, O-rings, or instruction manual. It's just the four filters, wrapped tight.

One thing you'll notice immediately: these ship compressed. The pleats are vacuum-packed flat to save shipping costs. That means on installation day, you need to manually separate and fluff each pleat before loading them into the housing. Skip this step and you'll get uneven water flow and reduced DE coating distribution.

**Real-world performance**

Here's where this product earns its keep. The filtration media is a polyester-cotton blend with pleat spacing that closely mirrors the Pentair original. In practice, that means it catches the same particle sizes — down to roughly 2-3 microns with DE powder added. Multiple long-term reviewers report their pools staying crystal clear for the full season, with one noting, *"I was skeptical about off-brand filters, but after 4 months the water is just as clear as with the Pentair brand."*

The end caps are glued rather than injection-molded, which is the main structural difference from OEM. Under normal pressure (up to 50 PSI), this holds fine. But if you let your pressure gauge creep toward 60 PSI because you skipped a cleaning, you risk the pleats pulling away from the cap. That's a genuine weakness worth knowing.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The value proposition is the headline. A 4-pack of Pentair OEM cartridges will typically run you $150-$200 depending on the retailer. This set lands significantly below that, and the math gets even better when you factor in that DE filters typically need replacement every 1-2 years depending on usage.

Fitment gets consistent praise too. Reviewers across Pentair Quad models report no modification needed, no leaks at the riser threads, and easy installation. One verified buyer put it plainly: *"They fit perfectly. I was worried about the aftermarket horror stories, but these slid right in."*

**Where it falls short**

Be honest about the build quality difference. The outer mesh on these is noticeably thinner than OEM — maybe 15-20% less substantial. That's fine for most pool owners, but if you have a heavy debris load (lots of trees, frequent storms), you'll want to clean these more often to prevent premature wear.

The compressed shipping is also a minor annoyance. Fluffing 4 cartridges takes about 10 minutes and makes your hands feel like you've been handling fiberglass insulation. It's a one-time hassle, but it's worth knowing before you tear open the box expecting plug-and-play.

There's also no warranty to speak of. Pentair backs their filters for a year. Optimum doesn't offer anything comparable, so you're betting on your own care habits.

**Who should buy this**

If you're a DIY pool owner who maintains your own equipment and costs your filters annually, this is a smart buy. The savings are real, the fitment is proven, and the filtration performance matches OEM in normal conditions.

Skip it if you're running a commercial property, a rental pool, or you're the type who lets pressure gauges max out before cleaning. Those thinner end caps won't forgive neglect like the OEM version might.

**FAQ**

**Will these work in my Pentair Quad 60?** Yes. The 60, 80, and 100 models all use the same cartridge dimensions. Just verify your riser tube is 3 inches before ordering.

**How often should I clean them?** Same as OEM — when your pressure gauge reads 8-10 PSI above the clean baseline. For most residential pools, that's every 4-6 weeks.

**Can I use the same DE powder?** Absolutely. These work with standard diatomaceous earth exactly like the OEM filters.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want OEM-equivalent filtration at a meaningful discount, you're comfortable with basic maintenance, and you replace filters on a regular schedule.

**Skip it if:** You need maximum durability, abuse your equipment, or want the peace of mind of a manufacturer warranty.
