## What You Get in the Box

The unit arrives as a freestanding shelving system with integrated LED grow light bars mounted under each tier. ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs.

**The shelves are shallow.** If you have wide pots (8+ inches in diameter), they'll overhang the edges. This isn't a shelf for oversized ceramic planters—it's built for standard nursery pots and smaller decorative containers.

**No casters.** You can't wheel this around. Once it's in position, it stays. If you rotate plants to chase winter sun, you'll be lifting it (with plants on it, which is awkward) or moving plants individually.

**The lights are not dimmable.** There's no brightness adjustment—just on/off and the timer. For sensitive plants that scorch easily, you might need to position them on the lowest shelf or accept the fixed intensity.

**Cord management is minimal.** The power cord exits the frame at the bottom and there's no tidy channel to hide it. A minor aesthetic gripe, but worth noting if you're particular about cable clutter.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're an apartment dweller with a windowless room or north-facing light. You want to consolidate a scattered collection of small-to-medium houseplants onto one organized, self-lit unit. You appreciate a set-it-and-forget-it timer.

**Skip it if:** You grow heavy, large plants in decorative ceramic pots. You need adjustable light intensity for finicky species. You want portability to chase light patterns through the seasons.

## Real Shopper FAQs

**Q: Can I use this for seed starting?**
A: Yes, for most common vegetable and herb seeds. The light intensity is adequate for germination and early seedling growth. You'll want to keep the trays on the top shelf where light output is strongest. Once seedlings get their second set of true leaves, they may need more direct light to avoid legginess.

**Q: How many plants can it realistically hold?**
A: With standard 4-inch nursery pots, you can fit 6–8 per shelf comfortably. That's roughly 24–32 plants across four tiers. With 6-inch pots, drop that to 3–4 per shelf. Plan around pot diameter, not just shelf count.

**Q: Is the timer easy to set?**
A: Yes. It's a simple push-button cycle—press to toggle between 3, 6, and 12 hours, then set it and leave it. The light remembers your last setting when you power it back on. No app, no Bluetooth, which some users actually prefer.
