**What you actually get** — and why the RV crowd cares  

The Blackstone On The Go 22" Omnivore Griddle isn't just a smaller version of their big flat-tops. It's a purpose-built portable that ships with everything you need to cook at a campsite, tailgate, or beach parking lot: the griddle itself, a 1-lb propane cylinder, a regulator hose, and a padded carry bag. No separate purchases, no "oh wait, I need an adapter" moment. That's rare in this category, and it's the first thing buyers mention. As one verified purchaser put it, *"Took it out of the box, hooked up the tank, and was cooking in 10 minutes."*  

**Cooking performance: the Omnivore top earns its name**  

The 22-inch Omnivore cooking surface (330 square inches) uses rolled steel with a textured finish that holds seasoning better than the older plain steel tops. In practice, that means eggs slide off with a tiny pat of butter, and smash burgers develop a crust without tearing. Heat distribution is surprisingly even for a portable — the dual H-style burners put out 12,000 BTUs total, which is enough to sear but not blast. Preheating takes about 6 minutes to hit 350°F, and you can dial it down to a low simmer for pancakes.  

The folding legs are a genuine upgrade over older models. They lock into place with a positive click, and the griddle stays stable on packed dirt or a picnic table. One reviewer noted, *"Used it on a slightly sloped campsite — didn't wobble at all."* The whole unit weighs 31 pounds, which is manageable for one person, though you'll want the included carry bag if you're hiking it more than 50 feet.  

**Where it falls short — and it's not minor**  

The biggest complaint across 220 ratings is the lack of a side shelf. On a 22-inch griddle, you're cooking and plating in a tight space. You'll need a folding table or a cutting board balanced on the carry bag. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's a daily friction point.  

The grease management is also basic. The rear trough collects runoff, but the cup is small — about 8 ounces — and fills fast when cooking bacon or fatty burgers. You'll be swapping it mid-cook. Cleanup involves scraping into the trough and wiping down with a paper towel. No removable drip tray, so plan for that.  

**Who should buy this**  

This is for the RV owner who wants a real griddle without hauling a 36-inch model. It fits in a pass-through storage compartment, the propane tank is included so you don't have to hunt one down at a campground store, and the cooking surface is big enough for a family of four. It's also a solid pick for tailgaters who want smash burgers and breakfast without dragging a full-size gas grill.  

Skip it if you're cooking for more than six people — you'll be doing batches and waiting. And if you're expecting a multi-burner griddle with a built-in grease drawer, this isn't it.  

**FAQ**  

**Q: Can I use a standard 20-lb propane tank?**  
Yes. The included hose connects to the 1-lb cylinder, but you can swap in a standard tank adapter (sold separately) for longer cook sessions. The regulator handles both.  

**Q: How long does the 1-lb tank last?**  
At medium heat (around 350°F), expect 60–90 minutes of continuous cooking. For a full breakfast and lunch, bring a spare.  

**Q: Does the griddle top need seasoning out of the box?**  
Yes. It ships with a light protective coating. Wash, dry, and run a thin layer of oil at medium heat for 20 minutes before first use. Blackstone's seasoning instructions are in the manual — follow them and the surface improves with use.  

**The Verdict**  

**Buy it if:** You want a portable griddle that's genuinely ready to cook out of the box, you value a stable cooking platform, and you're okay with basic grease management. The Omnivore top performs above its price class.  

**Skip it if:** You need prep space, cook for large groups regularly, or want a built-in grease drawer. You'd be better served by a larger model with side shelves.  

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A well-built, thoughtfully packaged portable griddle held back by small but real ergonomic gaps. The 4.4-star Amazon average is earned; this is a solid buy for the RV or tailgate crowd.
