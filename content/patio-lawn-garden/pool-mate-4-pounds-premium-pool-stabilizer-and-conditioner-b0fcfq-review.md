## What You Actually Get

The Pool Mate ships as a 4-pound resealable bag of granular cyanuric acid (CYA). That's the active ingredient—nothing else, no fillers, no clarifiers mixed in. Per the label, one pound treats 5,000 gallons to raise CYA by 30 ppm. So the full bag covers a 10,000-gallon pool to the recommended 30-50 ppm range. The granules are fine, similar to coarse sand, and they dissolve slowly rather than clouding the water instantly.

## Real-World Performance

Here's the thing about cyanuric acid: it's not magic, it's math. CYA binds to free chlorine molecules, creating a protective shield against UV rays. At 30-50 ppm, you cut chlorine loss by roughly 50-70% on sunny days. Buyers consistently confirm this works. One reviewer noted, "I was adding chlorine every other day before this. ## Where It Falls Short

Honest talk: this product requires you to know what you're doing. If you're new to pool chemistry, you might overshoot. CYA above 80 ppm actually makes chlorine less effective—a condition called "chlorine lock." You can't just dump this in and hope. You need a test kit that reads cyanuric acid, and many basic 3-way kits skip it. That's an extra $15-20 purchase if you don't have one.

Also, the granules can clump if the bag sits partially open in a humid shed. A few reviewers mentioned wrestling with hardened chunks mid-season. It's fixable—break them up before use—but it's a minor annoyance. Finally, this only protects chlorine; it does nothing for pH, alkalinity, or algae. Buyers expecting a full conditioner might be disappointed, but that's not what this product claims to be.

## FAQ

**How do I add this without clouding the water?**
Fill a nylon sock with the granules, tie it off, and hang it in front of a return jet. Let it dissolve over 24-48 hours. Don't dump it directly into a skimmer—it can sit and cake.

**How often do I need to reapply?**
CYA doesn't evaporate; it only leaves through backwashing, splash-out, or partial drains. Most pools need one application per season, maybe two if you drain and refill.

**Can I use this with saltwater pools?**
Yes. Salt chlorine generators benefit from CYA too—it stabilizes the chlorine they produce and reduces cell wear from overworking.
