**FAQ**

**Q: Will these fit my camping stove?**
Check if your stove uses threaded canisters. If yes, the 7/16-inch valve will fit most major brands. If it's a pierce-style (puncture) canister, this won't work.

**Q: How long does one canister last?**
On a standard 10,000 BTU stove at medium heat, expect roughly 90–120 minutes of continuous burn. A torch at full blast will drain it in about 45 minutes.

**Q: Are these safe for indoor use?**
Butane stoves are technically indoor-safe for cooking, but always ensure proper ventilation. The self-sealing valve minimizes leak risk, but no fuel is truly leak-proof.
