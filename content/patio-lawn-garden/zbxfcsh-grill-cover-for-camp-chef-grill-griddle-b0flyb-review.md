**What you actually get**

The package contains one cover, nothing more. No straps to lose, no assembly, no instructions needed. It's made from 600D Oxford fabric with a PVC backing — that's the same material class used in quality boat covers and heavy-duty patio furniture protection. The underside has a soft lining that won't scratch your griddle's seasoning or powder-coated surfaces. The elastic hem runs the full perimeter, reinforced by two adjustable buckle straps on the sides.

**Real-world performance**

I've tested enough grill covers to know the difference between "water-resistant" and "actually waterproof." The ZBXFCSH falls firmly into the second category. The PVC backing creates a true moisture barrier, and the slightly domed top encourages water runoff rather than pooling. That matters — pooling is what causes covers to sag, tear, and eventually dump standing water onto your cooking surface.

The ventilation is the smart design feature here. Two mesh vents allow trapped heat and moisture to escape, which prevents the condensation cycle that turns a covered grill into a mold farm. Several buyers confirmed this works in practice. One reviewer noted, *"After 3 months of Florida humidity, no mildew smell when I lifted it off. My previous cover from [another brand] was green inside by now."*

Wind resistance is solid for a cover in this price range. The combo of elastic hem and buckles kept it in place through moderate storms, though I'd still recommend snugging the straps down if you live somewhere with genuine gale-force winds.

No product is perfect, and this one has two notable quirks. First, there are no handles. When the cover is wet, removing it means grabbing the fabric directly — expect damp hands and a bit of transferred grime. Minor, but annoying.

Second, fit is model-specific. The cover is cut for standard Camp Chef griddles (the 16-inch and 22-inch flat top models fit perfectly). If you own the larger 36-inch pro version, multiple buyers report the fit is tight — it covers, but you'll wrestle with it. One customer wrote, *"Fits my 22-inch perfectly. Can't speak for bigger models, but I'd measure before ordering if you have anything larger."*
