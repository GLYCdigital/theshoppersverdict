## Build Quality: Better Than Stock

Here's where QuliMetal earns its keep. Weber's factory grates are porcelain-coated cast iron — they work great for two years, then the coating chips and rust sets in. This kit uses **304 stainless steel throughout**, which is the same alloy used in commercial kitchens. It won't rust, won't chip, and won't flake into your food.

The 7mm rod thickness matters. Cheap aftermarket grates use 4-5mm wire that warps under high heat. These bars hold their shape even after a full sear session at 600°F. The flavorizer bars are stamped from 0.8mm stainless — thinner than OEM, but the material's corrosion resistance means they'll outlast the porcelain version anyway.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. **Stainless steel is harder to clean than porcelain.** Food residue bonds more aggressively to bare metal. You'll need a grill brush with stiff stainless bristles, and you should clean while the grates are still warm. Let it cool completely and you're scrubbing for twenty minutes.

Second, **no hardware is included**. If your grill is older than eight years, those screws holding the flavorizer bars are likely corroded. Plan on a quick trip to the hardware store for replacement stainless fasteners before install.

Finally, these aren't non-stick. If you're used to cooking delicate fish or vegetables on your Weber, you'll want to oil the grates before each session. It's a minor adjustment, but worth knowing.

## FAQ: Three Questions Buyers Actually Ask

**Will this fit my Weber Spirit?**
Most Spirit models share the same grate dimensions as Genesis. Measure your existing grate — if it's roughly 22" x 13", this kit will work. Check the product listing for the full compatibility list before ordering.

**Is stainless steel really better than porcelain?**
For longevity, yes. Porcelain chips when metal utensils scrape it, then moisture gets under the coating and rust spreads. Solid stainless has no coating to fail. The tradeoff is harder cleaning.

**How long will these last?**
With normal use and basic care, expect 5-7 years. The flavorizer bars will degrade first since they absorb the most heat. Replacing just those is cheaper than buying a complete kit again.
