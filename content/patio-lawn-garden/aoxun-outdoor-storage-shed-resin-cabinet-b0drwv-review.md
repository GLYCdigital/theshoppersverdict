**The $200 resin shed that won't warp in the sun — but read this before you buy**

You've seen the cheap plastic sheds at big-box stores. The ones with walls that ripple in the wind and doors that stop latching by August. The Aoxun Outdoor Storage Shed is not that. This is a serious step up in resin cabinet construction — but it's not without a few gotchas that could turn your weekend project into a headache.

**What you actually get**

The box contains 12 double-wall resin panels, a reinforced roof cap, two adjustable shelf boards, and a steel crossbar for the door frame. No floor. No included base. No lock. That last bit matters — I'll get to it.

The footprint is compact: roughly 43 inches wide, 23 inches deep, and 70 inches tall. As shown in the product image, it's a tall, narrow cabinet that hugs a wall or sits flush in a corner. Think of it as a vertical storage closet for your patio, not a walk-in shed.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The double-wall resin panels are the headline feature. They're not the hollow, single-layer plastic you see on cheaper units — they have an internal honeycomb structure that adds rigidity and insulation. In practical terms, this means the walls don't flex when you push on them, and they won't warp into a banana shape after a season of direct sun.

Assembly is genuinely tool-free. Panels click into place with a rubber mallet (included). Most buyers report 45 minutes to an hour for solo assembly. One reviewer noted: *"Took me about 50 minutes by myself. The instructions are mostly pictograms, but once you see how the first two panels lock, the rest is obvious."*

Weather resistance is solid for a resin cabinet. The roof has a slight pitch that sheds water, and the door has a small drip lip. The double-wall panels also handle temperature swings without cracking — a common failure point on cheaper sheds.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The adjustable shelving is the most-loved feature. Two track rails run the interior height, letting you position shelves wherever you need them. One customer wrote: *"I can fit a 40-inch tall weed trimmer vertically and still have a shelf above it for pots and fertilizer. The flexibility is fantastic."*

Durability gets consistent mentions too. Multiple reviews reference the cabinet surviving heavy rain and direct sun without fading or warping. Several note they bought a second unit after the first impressed them.

**Where it falls short**

The floor situation is the biggest catch. There's no floor panel included, and the base frame has gaps. If you set it directly on grass or dirt, the door will drag within weeks as the frame sinks unevenly. You need a flat, level surface — concrete, pavers, or a pressure-treated wood base. Budget an extra $30–50 for that if you don't have a suitable spot.

Security is disappointing. The padlock tab is a thin molded plastic loop. A determined person could snap it with a screwdriver. This is fine for keeping honest people out and storing garden tools, but don't store expensive power equipment in it.

The resin also scratches more easily than metal. If you slide a metal rake handle along the interior walls, you'll leave scuffs. Cosmetic, not structural, but worth knowing.

**Who should buy this**

- **Patio homeowners** with deck or balcony space who need to stash cushions, pots, and small tools without a full-size shed
- **Gardeners** who want vertical storage for long-handled tools and can provide a flat base
- **Renters** who need semi-portable storage that disassembles for a move

Skip it if you need real security, plan to store heavy items (the shelves hold about 40 pounds each), or can't provide a level foundation.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it hold up in heavy snow?**
Double-wall panels are sturdier than single-wall plastic, but the flat roof isn't rated for deep snow accumulation. In heavy snow zones, clear it after storms or add a simple A-frame cover. It's fine for light to moderate winter weather.

**Q: Can one person assemble it?**
Yes, but it's easier with two for the roof cap and door alignment. One reviewer did it solo in under an hour; expect 45–90 minutes alone.

**Q: Does the door lock?**
There's a padlock tab but no integrated lock. You'll need to supply your own small padlock. Just know it's a deterrent, not a real security feature.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a durable, warp-resistant resin cabinet for patio or garden storage, don't mind providing your own level base, and need adjustable shelving. **Skip it if** you require actual locking security, plan to store heavy items, or want a shed with a floor you can stand in.

The Aoxun Outdoor Storage Shed earns its 4.3-star rating. It's well-built for the price, assembles quickly, and looks decent against a house wall. Just budget for a base and a real lock, and you'll get years of service.
