The Greenworks 24V Brushless Hedge Trimmer sits in that awkward middle ground: not the cheapest corded option, not the brute-force gas machine. But after digging through 220 ratings and the spec sheet, this is the one I'd actually recommend to most homeowners. Here's why.

**What you actually get**

The trimmer itself features a 24-inch dual-action steel blade with a 5/8-inch cutting capacity. The brushless motor is the headline — it's the same tech powering premium tools, delivering more torque per watt while running cooler and lasting longer than brushed motors. The rear handle rotates 180 degrees for vertical cuts, and the front assist handle adjusts for awkward angles. Total weight lands at 6.2 pounds, which is noticeably lighter than comparable gas units that push 10-11 pounds.

Note: unless you buy the kit version, the 2Ah battery and charger are separate. Factor that into your total cost.

**Real-world performance**

The brushless motor matters most when you hit woody growth. Multiple buyers describe chewing through overgrown privet and boxwood without bogging down. One reviewer noted, *"My old corded trimmer would stall on thick branches — this just powers through them."* The dual-action blades (both edges cut) reduce vibration significantly, which translates to less hand fatigue during 30-minute trimming sessions.

Battery runtime with the 2Ah pack runs roughly 30-40 minutes of continuous cutting — enough for most suburban hedge lines, but tight if you've got serious acreage. The motor's efficiency means you won't see the dramatic power fade that plagues cheaper trimmers; it cuts strong until the battery quits.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The weight-to-power ratio is the recurring theme in positive reviews. Owners coming from gas trimmers specifically call out how much easier it is to maneuver overhead and at shoulder height. The rotating rear handle gets frequent mention for making vertical side cuts feel natural rather than awkward.

The brushless motor's reliability also earns trust. Multiple reviewers note this is their second or third Greenworks tool, and they're consolidating on the 24V platform specifically because the motors haven't failed them.

**Where it falls short**

The 24-inch blade is the most common complaint. For tall, dense hedges (think 6+ feet), you'll make more passes than with a 26-inch gas unit. One reviewer put it plainly: *"Great trimmer, but I wish I'd bought the 26-inch for my tall hedges."*

The battery situation is the bigger practical issue. The 2Ah battery is adequate but not generous — expect to buy a second pack (or a 4Ah upgrade) for any serious yard work. And there's no blade oiler, so you'll need to manually lubricate after heavy use to prevent sap buildup and blade sticking.

**Who should buy this**

- **The corded-cutter refugee**: If you're done dragging extension cords around the yard, this is a clean upgrade path.
- **The gas-averse homeowner**: You want gas-level power without the pull-start frustration, mixing fuel, or ear protection.
- **The Greenworks ecosystem owner**: If you already have 24V batteries from a leaf blower or string trimmer, the bare tool is a smart, cost-effective add.

**Skip it if**: You've got tall, sprawling hedges (go 26-inch), or you're starting fresh with zero Greenworks tools and want maximum value (a corded trimmer at half the price may serve you fine).

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this handle thick branches or just soft growth?**
A: It'll cut up to 5/8-inch diameter branches cleanly. Beyond that, switch to loppers — forcing it will strain the blades and battery.

**Q: Is the 2Ah battery enough?**
A: For typical suburban hedge maintenance, yes — roughly 30-40 minutes. For larger properties, budget for a 4Ah battery.

**Q: Does the brushless motor actually make a difference?**
A: Yes. More torque, less vibration, longer lifespan, and no carbon brushes to replace. It's the difference between a toy and a tool.

**The Verdict: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if** you want a lightweight, powerful cordless trimmer that handles most residential jobs without the gas hassle.

**Skip it if** you have oversized hedges or no existing investment in the Greenworks battery platform — the upfront cost may not justify it for occasional use.

For the majority of homeowners, though, this is the sweet spot: real cutting power, manageable weight, and a platform that grows with you.
