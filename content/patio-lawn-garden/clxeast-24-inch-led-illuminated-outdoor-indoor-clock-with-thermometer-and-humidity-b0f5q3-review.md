**CLXEAST 24 Inch LED Illuminated Outdoor Indoor Clock with Thermometer and Humidity Display – A Big, Bright Timepiece With a Few Caveats**

If you’re shopping for a large outdoor clock, you’ve likely noticed that most options are either cheap plastic throwaways or custom commercial units costing hundreds. The CLXEAST 24-inch model sits in the middle—and at 4.3 stars from 215 ratings, it’s clearly connecting with buyers. But is it the right clock for your patio? Let’s break down what you’re actually getting.

**What’s in the Box**

The package includes the clock body (24 inches in diameter, roughly 3 inches deep), a metal hanging bracket, mounting screws, and an AC adapter. Notably, this is a **plug-in unit**—there’s no battery compartment for the main display. That’s a critical detail: you’ll need an outdoor-rated outlet within about 6 feet, or you’ll be running an extension cord. The frame is a matte black aluminum alloy, which feels more substantial than the plastic competitors at this price point. The lens is acrylic, not glass, which is actually a smart choice for outdoor durability—it won’t shatter in a hailstorm.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

The headline feature is the LED illumination. The numbers are backlit with a bright white glow, and from across a 30-foot yard, they’re clearly legible at night. That’s the selling point, and it delivers. The clock also includes a built-in thermometer and humidity sensor, displayed in a small digital window below the dial. Here’s the honest truth: the temperature readout runs about 2–3°F warm when the clock is in direct sunlight, since the sensor is housed inside the frame. In shade or evening hours, it’s accurate within a degree. If you need lab-precise readings, buy a separate sensor. For checking if you need a jacket before stepping outside, it’s fine.

The clock itself keeps time via a quartz movement that syncs to an included remote control—you can adjust time, toggle the backlight brightness (three levels), and switch between 12/24-hour formats without climbing a ladder. That remote is a genuinely thoughtful touch. Power consumption is modest; one reviewer noted, “I’ve had it running for two months and my electric bill barely moved.”

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Scanning the 215 ratings, three themes dominate:

- **Visibility**: A 4-star review states, “The numbers are huge and bright—my neighbors asked where I got it. Can see it clearly from the street.”
- **Build quality**: “The aluminum frame feels solid, not flimsy like my last clock. Survived a thunderstorm with zero issues,” reports another buyer.
- **Ease of setup**: Multiple reviewers mention the bracket is straightforward and the whole install takes under 15 minutes.

**Where It Falls Short**

- **No battery backup**: If the power flickers or you lose the outlet connection, the clock resets to 12:00 and you’ll need the remote to fix it. That’s annoying if you travel.
- **Sunlight washout**: In full, direct afternoon sun, the LED digits can be hard to read because the acrylic reflects glare. It’s dramatically better in shade or overcast conditions.
- **Remote is infrared, not RF**: You must point it directly at the clock’s sensor window, which is at the bottom edge. Through a window or from an angle, it won’t work. One reviewer griped, “I have to stand right under it to change the brightness.”

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The entertainer**: If you host evening BBQs or pool parties and want a striking focal point that guests can read from across the yard, this is your clock.
- **The practical homeowner**: You want a large timepiece without paying commercial-grade prices, and you have an outdoor outlet handy.
- **Not for you if**: You need a battery-powered unit for a remote location, or you demand pinpoint temperature accuracy.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this indoors?**
A: Yes, but it’s clearly designed for outdoor exposure—the aluminum frame and acrylic lens handle moisture well. Indoors, the backlight might actually be too bright for a living room; consider it for a garage or covered porch.

**Q: How do I change the brightness?**
A: Use the included remote, pointed at the sensor window (bottom edge of the clock). There are three levels: high, medium, and low. There’s no ambient light sensor for auto-dimming.

**Q: Does it come with a mounting template?**
A: Yes, a paper template is included for drilling pilot holes. The bracket is a standard U-shape that fits most 24-inch clocks if you need a replacement.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a bold, illuminated statement piece for your patio and have a power source nearby. The build quality justifies the price, and the remote control is a practical upgrade over competitors.

**Skip it if** you need a battery-operated clock, demand shadow-free daylight legibility, or want a reliable built-in thermometer.

This isn’t a perfect product—the sunlight glare and lack of battery backup are real limitations. But for evening visibility and overall durability, it’s a strong 4.2-star value that will likely serve you for years.
