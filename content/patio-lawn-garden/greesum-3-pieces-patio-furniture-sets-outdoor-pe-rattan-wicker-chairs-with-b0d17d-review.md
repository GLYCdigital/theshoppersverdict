If you're shopping for a compact outdoor seating set under $300, the Greesum 3-piece patio set keeps showing up. With 215 ratings averaging 4.3 stars, it's clearly doing something right. But is it the right call for your deck, balcony, or patio? Here's what you actually need to know.

**What you get in the box**

The set includes two single chairs and a square side table with a tempered glass top. Each chair measures about 24.5"W x 29.5"D x 34"H with a seat height around 17.5 inches—standard for average-height adults. The table is roughly 19.5 inches square, perfect for drinks, a book, or a small plant. The set ships in three boxes (the glass top is separate), so be prepared for multiple deliveries over a day or two.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The frame is powder-coated steel—not aluminum—which makes it heavier and more stable in wind than many competing sets. That's a genuine advantage if you live in a breezy area; a buyer named Melissa noted, "It survived a thunderstorm without blowing over, which my previous set didn't." The PE rattan is hand-woven over the frame, and the weave density is solid for the price point. No sharp edges or loose strands out of the box.

The cushions are the standout. They're about 2.5 inches thick with a high-resilience foam core, and the covers zip off for washing—a feature usually reserved for sets costing twice as much. One reviewer said, "The cushions are way more comfortable than I expected for this price. I've napped in the chair twice already."

**Where it falls short**

Assembly is the biggest friction point. The instructions are mostly diagrams with minimal text, and the pre-drilled holes on the table base can be slightly misaligned. Plan on 45 minutes to an hour with a Phillips screwdriver. A few reviewers mention needing to "persuade" bolts into place—nothing a little patience won't fix, but don't expect a 10-minute setup.

The cushions arrive vacuum-sealed and take a full day or two to expand fully. Even after fluffing, some buyers report the seat foam is firmer than they'd like. If you prefer plush, sink-in seating, this set will feel more supportive than luxurious.

The other honest caveat: the rattan isn't aggressively UV-stabilized. In direct, all-day sun, expect some color fading by the end of season two. Covering the set when not in use will extend its life significantly.

**Who should buy this**

- **Balcony and small-patio dwellers**: The 3-piece footprint fits tight spaces without feeling cramped.
- **Budget-conscious buyers** who want a set that looks more expensive than it is.
- **Anyone who prioritizes easy cleaning**—the zip-off cushion covers are a genuine win.

**Skip it if** you need seating for more than two people, want plush, deep-cushion comfort, or live in an area with harsh, year-round sun exposure without shade or covers.

**FAQ**

*Can these stay outside in winter?*  
The frame and rattan can handle rain and cold, but snow load and freezing can stress the joints. Cover it or store indoors if your winters are severe.

*Are the cushions waterproof?*  
No—they're water-resistant at best. Bring them inside during heavy rain or use a set cover. Water pooling on the foam can cause mildew over time.

*What's the weight capacity per chair?*  
The steel frame supports roughly 300 pounds per chair, which is solid for this category.

**The Verdict**

The Greesum 3-piece set delivers real value: a stable, good-looking, comfortable-enough seating group that handles the elements better than its price suggests. It's not a premium set, and it doesn't pretend to be. For small spaces and modest budgets, it's a smart buy.

**Buy it if** you want a solid, compact bistro-style set that punches above its weight on comfort and stability.  
**Skip it if** you need a full-sized conversation set or demand cloud-like cushioning.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Recommended for most small-patio shoppers, with the caveat that assembly requires patience and direct sun will fade the rattan over time.
