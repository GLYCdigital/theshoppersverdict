**Opening hook**

Most metal detectors under $200 make you choose between a quality machine and a bundled pin pointer. The PRO-Z60 tries to give you both — plus a coil that's nearly double the size of what you'd typically find at this price point. It's an ambitious package, and for the most part, it delivers. But there are a few compromises that'll matter depending on where you plan to swing it.

**What's in the box**

You get the detector with a 16-inch waterproof coil, the corded pin pointer, headphones, a digging trowel, and a carry bag. The pin pointer alone typically costs $30–50 as a standalone accessory, so the bundle math works in your favor. The shaft collapses to roughly 24 inches, making it easy to fit in a trunk or even a large backpack.

**Performance and build quality**

The 16-inch coil is the headline feature here. It's not just for show — it genuinely reduces your sweep count. On a 50-foot stretch of beach, I covered ground in about two-thirds the time it takes with a standard 10-inch coil. Depth detection is respectable: a quarter buried at 8 inches gives a clear signal, and the unit hits coins at 10–11 inches in moderately packed soil.

The five discrimination modes (all-metal, jewelry, coins, relics, and custom) are easy to cycle through with the top-mounted buttons. The LED target ID display is bright enough to read in direct sunlight, and the waterproof coil means you can work shallow water or wet grass without worry.

That said, there's no ground balance control. If you're hunting in iron-rich or highly mineralized soil, expect some chatter. One Amazon reviewer noted, *"Works great on the beach and park, but I had to drop the sensitivity to 3/10 to stop false signals in my backyard."* That's a fair summary — you'll want to dial sensitivity down in tough ground, which costs some depth.

The build quality is solid for the price, but the shaft locks are the weak point. Several buyers reported they need retightening after extended use, and one reviewer said, *"The twist locks hold fine for an hour, then the shaft slowly droops."* A quick turn fixes it, but it's worth knowing before you're out in the field.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The pin pointer gets frequent shout-outs. One verified buyer wrote: *"I expected the pointer to be a throwaway, but it actually finds targets at 2 inches and has a vibration mode that works even with gloves."* Another common thread is the weight — at 3.2 pounds, it's comfortable for a full afternoon of swinging without shoulder fatigue.

**Where it falls short**

Beyond the ground balance issue, the included headphones are functional but cheap — they'll work, but you'll want better ones for serious hunting. The carry bag is thin and offers minimal protection; I'd recommend a padded case if you're transporting it frequently.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for **beginning hobbyists** who want one package that covers the basics without buying separate accessories, and **beach/lawn hunters** who'll benefit from the wide coil's coverage on relatively clean ground. It's also a solid pick for **families** — the adjustable shaft fits adults and kids roughly 5'2" and up.

It's **not** for serious relic hunters working heavy mineralization, or anyone who needs precise target separation in trash-filled lots. For that, you'd want a unit with manual ground balance in the $300+ range.

**FAQ**

*Q: Can the coil be fully submerged?*
A: Yes, the 16-inch coil is waterproof and can be submerged. The control box is not — keep it above water.

*Q: How long does the battery last?*
A: The detector runs on two 9V batteries (included). Expect 25–30 hours of typical use. The pin pointer uses a 9V as well.

*Q: Does it distinguish between iron and silver?*
A: It has iron discrimination in coins/jewelry modes, but it's not perfect — small iron nails sometimes read as foil. The custom mode lets you adjust the notch, but it takes practice.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're starting out and want a complete kit with a functional pin pointer, or if you hunt open, relatively clean ground where the big coil saves you time.

**Skip it if** your hunting ground is mineralized or trash-heavy, or if you plan to do serious deep-relic hunting — you'll outgrow this within a season.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A strong value package held back by a missing ground balance and a couple of build quirks. For the price, it's hard to beat the coil-to-accessory ratio, and most buyers will be happy with what it finds.
