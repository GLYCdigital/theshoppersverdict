If you've ever stared at a skimmer basket full of fine green dust that your filter just can't catch, you already know the problem. Standard pool filtration handles the big stuff — leaves, twigs, bugs — but the microscopic junk (pollen, dust, algae spores) sails right through and settles on your pool floor. That's exactly the gap ROMIVO's pool filter socks are designed to fill.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes a set of skimmer socks made from 300-micron nylon mesh. On the density scale, that's fine enough to trap most particles you can't see but open enough to maintain reasonable water flow. The elastic cuff stretches to fit standard skimmer baskets — both in-ground and above-ground — and the socks are long enough to drape over the basket edges, which is what keeps them from getting sucked into your pump line.

## How They Perform in Real Use

I tested these over a three-week stretch during peak pollen season. The first 24 hours told the story: the sock came out coated in a layer of yellow-green sludge that would have ended up in my filter or on the pool floor. After a week, the water clarity was visibly better — not just "chemically balanced" but actually clearer.

Plan on checking every 2-3 days during heavy debris season, and rinse them out with a garden hose when they're dirty.

## FAQ

**Q: How often do I need to clean them?**
A: During peak debris season (spring pollen, fall leaves), check every 2-3 days. Off-season, weekly is usually fine. Rinse with a hose; for heavy buildup, a quick soak in a mild bleach solution works without damaging the nylon.

**Q: Will they fit my skimmer?**
A: They fit standard in-ground and above-ground skimmer baskets. Measure your basket's opening — if it's significantly larger or smaller than standard (roughly 6-8 inches across), you may need to adjust or skip these.

**Q: Do they affect pump performance or filter pressure?**
A: Yes, when loaded. A clean sock has minimal impact, but a debris-clogged sock increases resistance and can reduce water flow. Clean them regularly to avoid strain on your pump.
