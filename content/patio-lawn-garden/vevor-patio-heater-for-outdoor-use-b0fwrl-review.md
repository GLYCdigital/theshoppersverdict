**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The wheels are plastic with basic bearings—they work on smooth concrete or decking, but don't expect to glide this 40-pound unit over gravel or uneven pavers. You'll be lifting it.

The assembly experience is the most common complaint in reviews. Multiple buyers mention the same thing: *"Took longer than expected and the instructions were confusing in a few spots."* It's not a dealbreaker, but it's a Saturday-morning project, not a 20-minute setup.

One more thing: this is a propane heater, which means ongoing fuel costs. If you're using it nightly for hours, budget for a 20-pound tank refill every couple of weeks. That's the hidden cost nobody mentions in the product listing.

**FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask**

**Q: Does this work in windy conditions?**
A: Any propane patio heater struggles in strong wind. In light breezes (under 10 mph), the VEVOR holds its own. In gusty conditions, the heat gets pushed around just like any other unit.

**Q: Can I use it on a wooden deck?**
A: Yes, but use the included heat shield and keep it away from railings or overhanging branches. The base stays cool to the touch, but the reflector head radiates significant heat upward.

**Q: How long does a propane tank last?**
A: At full output, expect 10-12 hours from a standard 20-pound tank. On the low setting, you can stretch that to 15-18 hours.
