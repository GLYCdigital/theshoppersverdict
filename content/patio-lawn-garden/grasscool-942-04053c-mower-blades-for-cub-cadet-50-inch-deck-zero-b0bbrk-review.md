In real-world cutting, these blades deliver a clean, even cut on standard turf. The moderate lift profile handles normal residential grass without issue — think weekly cuts on a half-acre lot. Users consistently report that the blades stay sharp for 8-10 mowings before needing a touch-up, which is respectable for the price point.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. The lift is moderate, not aggressive. If you regularly bag wet, thick grass, expect to make two passes or slow down. This is a standard-duty blade, not a mulching or high-lift performance blade.

The mounting bolts are included, which is nice, but they're standard-grade — not hardened. If you're replacing blades more than once a season, upgrade to hardened bolts separately.
