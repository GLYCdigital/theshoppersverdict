You don't buy a pool cover because it's fun. You buy it because you're tired of scooping leaves out of 4,000 gallons of water every October. The POOLHACKER 18FT round cover is aimed directly at that frustration — and for most shoppers, it delivers. But the 4.1-star average across 204 ratings hides some nuance worth understanding before you click buy.

**What's actually in the box**

The cover itself is a 20-foot diameter sheet designed to fit an 18-foot round above-ground pool, giving you that 6-inch overlap all the way around. It's triple-layer laminated polyethylene — not the flimsy blue tarp material you might be picturing. The underside is a dark, light-blocking layer that does a legitimate job of starving algae. You also get a perimeter cable, a winch-tightening mechanism, and grommets spaced roughly every 24 inches around the edge.

Missing from the box: any center support system. That's not a dealbreaker, but it's a cost you should factor in. A $15 inflatable pool pillow will save you the headache of a sagging middle collecting rainwater and debris.

**How it holds up in real use**

The material is the star here. It's thick enough that you won't accidentally poke a hole in it while dragging it across the top rail, and the UV stabilizers mean it won't turn brittle after one summer of sun exposure. One reviewer noted, "Survived a full Michigan winter with heavy snow and ice — no rips, no tears." That tracks with the construction quality.

The winch system is functional but not elegant. It tensions the cable around the perimeter, pulling the cover tight against the pool wall. The problem? Getting it evenly tensioned takes patience. Tighten one side, and the opposite side goes slack. A second pair of hands makes this dramatically easier. If you're installing solo, expect to walk the perimeter three or four times before it's snug.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The fit gets the most love. Multiple buyers with 18-foot pools report the overlap is generous and forgiving — it doesn't need to be perfectly centered to do its job. One verified purchase said, "Fits like it was made for my pool. No gaps, no flapping." The light-blocking capability is also a recurring theme, with several reviewers crediting the cover for keeping their pool crystal clear come spring.

**Where it falls short**

The grommets are the weak point. They're reinforced, but not indestructible. On particularly windy days, if the cable isn't cranked down hard, the cover can billow and put stress on those attachment points. A few reviewers reported grommets pulling through after a season of heavy wind exposure. The fix is simple — keep the cable properly tensioned — but it's worth knowing going in that this isn't a set-it-and-forget-it product.

The center sag is the other common complaint. Without a support pillow, water pools on top, which adds weight, stresses the seams, and eventually leads to stretching. This isn't a manufacturing flaw — every flat pool cover has this issue — but POOLHACKER could have included a basic support or made it a bundle option.

**Who should buy this**

- **The seasonal pool owner** who wants solid winter or shoulder-season protection without spending $150+ on a premium cover. This hits the sweet spot.
- **Anyone replacing a cheap tarp cover** that disintegrated after one season. The triple-layer construction is a clear upgrade.
- **Buyers who don't mind a 20-minute installation** with a helper. If you're strictly solo and impatient, look elsewhere.

Skip it if you need a cover with integrated water bags (this uses a cable system), or if you live in an area with extreme, sustained winds — you'd be better served by a heavier-duty cover with a track system.

**FAQ**

*Does this fit a 16-foot pool?*
No. It's sized specifically for 18-foot round pools. The overlap would be excessive on a 16-footer, and the cable wouldn't cinch properly.

*Can I use it as a solar cover?*
No. This is a winter/debris cover. It blocks sunlight, which is the opposite of what a solar cover does.

*Will it keep leaves out completely?*
Yes, if it's tensioned properly. The edge overlap and tight cable seal prevent debris from slipping under. Loose installation invites problems.

**The Verdict**

The POOLHACKER 18FT cover does its primary job — keeping debris out and sunlight off the water — without breaking the bank. The material quality punches above its price class, and the 5-year warranty suggests the manufacturer believes in it too. The grommet concerns and the missing center support are real tradeoffs, but both are manageable with proper installation and a $15 accessory.

**Buy it if** you want dependable, multi-season protection at a reasonable price and don't mind a bit of installation effort.

**Skip it if** you're installing solo in a high-wind zone or want a zero-maintenance cover that works without any additional purchases.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, honest cover that outperforms its price point, with a few corners cut that you should know about before buying.
