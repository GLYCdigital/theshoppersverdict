If you've ever opened your pool skimmer basket to find it half-empty while the water stays cloudy, you already know the problem: standard baskets catch the big stuff but let fines slip right through. The WXFANA 140-pack of skimmer socks attacks that gap directly — and at a price that makes the math almost suspicious.

**What you actually get**

This bundle combines a 100-pack of standard skimmer socks with a 40-pack of filter socks — 140 total. Each sock is a stretchy knit tube that slips over your existing skimmer basket. The material is fine-mesh but visibly porous, designed to catch what the basket misses while still letting water flow. The 100-pack socks are your everyday workhorses; the 40-pack filter socks are slightly denser, better for those post-storm or pollen-bomb weeks.

**Real-world performance**

I tested these across three weeks of typical summer conditions — cottonwood fluff, grass clippings, and the inevitable sunscreen film. The difference is immediate. Where a bare basket let fine green dust recirculate back into the pool, the WXFANA sock catches it. The knit stretches over both standard round baskets and the larger rectangular ones common on variable-speed pump systems. One buyer noted, *"I was skeptical but these caught stuff I didn't even know was in my pool. The water went from hazy to clear in three days."* That tracks with my experience — the fines are exactly what these catch best.

Fit is the standout feature. The socks have enough stretch to grip the basket rim securely without being a wrestling match to install. I never had one slip off mid-cycle, even with the pump running full tilt. The 140-count means you can burn through them liberally — one per basket change — and still have plenty for the season.

**Where it falls short**

Honesty requires the downsides. These are single-use only. You can rinse and reuse them a couple times if you're careful, but the knit weakens after the first wash and the elastic loses its grip. During peak leaf drop, you'll be swapping socks daily — the fine mesh clogs fast when the debris load is heavy. If your pool sits under a maple tree, a 100-pack might last you a month, not a season.

The material is also thinner than premium brands like Skimmit or PoolSkim. Overstuffing the basket or catching a sharp twig can tear the fabric. One reviewer complained, *"They work great but I went through them faster than expected because they ripped when I got too aggressive with the skimmer pole."* Fair point — handle with a lighter touch.

**Who should buy this**

- **The budget pool owner**: If you're tired of paying $15 for 12 socks, this bundle changes the economics entirely.
- **The allergy sufferer**: These catch pollen and fines that keep your pool hazy and your sinuses angry.

Skip it if you want a reusable solution or if your pool is surrounded by heavy foliage year-round. You'd be better off with a mesh leaf bag or a robotic cleaner.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will these fit my skimmer basket?**
A: They stretch significantly — roughly double their resting size. They fit standard round and rectangular baskets from Hayward, Pentair, and Jandy. If your basket is oversized or oddly shaped, you can still stretch the sock over the rim and secure it with a binder clip.

**Q: How often should I replace them?**
A: When the sock looks visibly dirty or water flow through the skimmer noticeably slows. In normal conditions, that's every 3-5 days. During heavy debris periods, daily. Each sock takes about 10 seconds to swap.

**Q: Can I wash and reuse them?**
A: Technically yes — rinse them with a hose and let them dry. But the elastic degrades after the first couple washes, and they lose their grip on the basket rim. At this price, treating them as disposable is the better play.
