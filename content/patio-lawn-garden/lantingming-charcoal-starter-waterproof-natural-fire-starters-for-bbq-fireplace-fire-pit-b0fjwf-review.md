## What You Actually Get

The package contains a box of small, dense cubes — each about 1.5 inches square. The coating is a waxy, waterproof layer that protects the interior from moisture. ## Performance: Does It Actually Work?

Yes — and impressively so. I tested these on a Weber kettle and a large fire pit on a damp, drizzly evening. One cube tucked under a loosely packed chimney of lump charcoal lit on the first match. The cube burned steadily for about 8 minutes — enough time to get coals ash-covered and glowing. Total time from match to ready-to-cook: roughly 12 minutes.

The waterproof claim isn't marketing fluff. I left an opened box on my patio overnight (it rained). The cubes still lit without sputtering. The coating does its job.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect. The cubes are **smaller than competitors like Royal Oak or Weber starters**. On a large kettle or a 22-inch chimney, you might need two cubes to get a solid, even burn — especially if your charcoal is old or partially damp. 

The burn time is also short. At ~8 minutes, you can't just toss a cube in and walk away. You need to arrange your charcoal loosely so air flows — packed coal will smother the flame before it spreads.

**Q: How many do I need for a fire pit?**
