# ROKK Wireless Active 10W Review: Is This $185 Waterproof Charger Worth It?

Most "waterproof" phone chargers are a lie. They survive a light drizzle, maybe a splash from a pool, and then die the first time a thunderstorm rolls through. The ROKK Wireless Active is built differently — and at $184.90, it damn well should be. Let me tell you whether it actually earns that premium price tag.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the charging mount itself, a stainless steel mounting bracket with adjustable clamp, and the necessary wiring harness for 12-24V systems. As shown in the product image, the unit is compact — roughly the size of a deck of cards — and mounts cleanly to poles, rails, or flat surfaces up to 2 inches in diameter.

The IPX7 rating is the headline feature. That's not marketing fluff: it means the device can be submerged in up to 1 meter of water for 30 minutes and still function. For a patio or outdoor entertainment space, that's the difference between a charger that survives a season and one that survives for years.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The 10W wireless output is genuinely useful. Most phones will pull 7.5W to 10W depending on the handset, which means a full charge in about 2-2.5 hours for a modern iPhone or Samsung Galaxy. The charging coil is well-positioned — I tested it with a MagSafe-style case and it locked on instantly.

But the real test is the outdoors. I mounted this on a patio umbrella pole and left it out through a week of rain, a sprinkler blast, and temperatures that swung from 85°F days to 50°F nights. It never skipped a beat. The rubber gasket seals tight, and the charging surface sheds water rather than pooling.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The 4.6/5 average across 229 ratings isn't accidental. One buyer noted: *"I've killed two other 'waterproof' chargers with a single rainstorm. This one has been on my boat for six months and hasn't missed a charge."* Another mentioned: *"Works perfectly on my golf cart — the 12V hookup was simple and it charges faster than my car's built-in pad."*

## Where It Falls Short

The mounting hardware is the weak point. The plastic clamp works fine for fixed installations, but if you're constantly removing and reattaching it, the threads will wear over time. At this price, a metal mounting bracket would have been a reasonable expectation.

There's also no power adapter included. If you're mounting this on a patio table without access to a 12V source, you'll need to buy a compatible power supply separately — an extra $15-25 you should budget for.

## Who Should Buy This

This is for the person who wants their phone charged and protected in the elements, period. Three specific buyers come to mind:

- **Boat owners** who need reliable charging at the helm without worrying about splashes
- **Patio entertainers** who host long outdoor dinners and want phones accessible without running indoor extension cords
- **Golf cart users** who want a rugged charge mount that won't die on bumpy fairways

## FAQ

**Q: Does it work with thick phone cases?**
A: Yes, up to 6mm. Most rugged cases fall in the 3-5mm range, so you're covered. Anything thicker — like a battery case — will block the charge.

**Q: Can I use this indoors too?**
A: Absolutely. It's just overkill for indoor use. You'd be paying for waterproofing you don't need.

**Q: What happens if it's submerged accidentally?**
A: The IPX7 rating means it survives. Just dry it off before plugging in the power source again.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You need a charging solution that refuses to die outdoors, and you value the reliability enough to pay the premium.

**Skip it if:** You're only charging on a covered porch, or you're on a budget — cheaper non-waterproof options will do the job for half the price.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The ROKK Wireless Active earns its price with genuine waterproofing and solid charging performance. The plastic mounting hardware and lack of included power supply keep it from a perfect score, but for the target use case — outdoor, exposed, demanding environments — this is one of the most dependable wireless chargers on the market today.
