If your spa filter just gave up the ghost, you're staring at a wall of lookalike cartridges online. The POOLPURE PLFPRB50-IN cuts through the noise with a deceptively simple promise: match the OEM specs, hold up under chemical abuse, and don't cost $80. It mostly delivers.

Let's talk fit first, because a filter that doesn't fit is a paperweight. POOLPURE engineered this cartridge to match the exact dimensions of the Unicel 5CH-950 and Filbur FC-0950: 9.88 inches tall with a 3.56-inch outside diameter. That means it drops into Bullfrog spas, Lifesmart models, and a broad range of 50-square-foot filter housings without fighting the lid. The 2-inch top hole and 1.5-inch bottom hole align correctly on standard pump unions. Buyers confirm it: "Fit my Bullfrog A7 perfectly. Threaded right on, no adapter needed."

**What's in the box:** one filter cartridge, period. No gasket, no O-ring, no instruction manual. That's fine if you're replacing an old cartridge and can reuse the O-ring. It's a minor annoyance if this is your first filter change on a new-to-you spa.

**Build quality and real-world performance.** The pleated polyester fabric is denser than what you'll find on budget no-name filters. It catches the fine suspended particles — sunscreen residue, dead skin, dust — that cloud spa water. The rigid center core and end caps are ultrasonically welded, not glued. That matters because spa water is a chemical cocktail of chlorine, bromine, and pH adjusters. Cheap filters delaminate in that environment within months. This one holds its pleat structure through shock treatments.

One buyer noted: "Three months in, still holding shape. The old filter I bought from a pool store was already collapsing at the pleats." That tracks with the 4.7-star average across 235 ratings.

**Where it comes up short.** The off-gassing issue is real. Several reviewers mentioned a noticeable plastic odor for the first day or two after installation. It dissipates, but if you're sensitive to chemical smells, run the spa with the cover off for a few hours before your first soak. Also, the fabric is slightly less rigid than OEM Bullfrog filters. It won't affect filtration, but if you're aggressive about pressure-washing your filters, be gentler with this one — high-pressure water can distort the pleats over time.

**Who should buy this:**

- **Bullfrog and Lifesmart owners** — this is the drop-in replacement you want without the OEM markup.
- **Anyone replacing filters every 6-12 months** — at this price point, you can stock two and swap them on a schedule.
- **Seasonal spa users** who need a reliable cartridge that survives winter storage.

**Skip it if** you need the absolute longest filter life possible (the OEM filter may last an extra month or two), or if you demand a gasket in the box.

**FAQ:**

**Q: How often should I replace this filter?**
A: Every 6-12 months depending on usage. Clean it monthly with a garden hose spray. If you use your spa daily, lean toward the 6-month mark.

**Q: Will this fit my Hot Spring spa?**
A: Only if your model uses a 50-square-foot cartridge with the dimensions listed above. Hot Spring uses several different filter sizes — measure your old cartridge before ordering. Don't trust model-name compatibility lists.

**Q: Can I clean this with muriatic acid?**
A: Yes, but dilute it properly (1 part acid to 20 parts water) and soak for no more than 15 minutes. The polyester pleats tolerate acid cleaning better than paper-based filters, but oversoaking will degrade the fabric.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5 — Buy it if you want OEM-equivalent performance at a reasonable price. Skip it if you need a gasket included or demand the absolute stiffest pleats on the market.**

This filter does the job without drama. It fits, filters, and lasts. For most spa owners, that's the entire game.
