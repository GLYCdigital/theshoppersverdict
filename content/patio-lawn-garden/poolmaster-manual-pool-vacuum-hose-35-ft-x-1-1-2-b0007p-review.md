Let’s cut the preface: a pool vacuum hose is the most boring purchase you’ll make all summer, but the wrong one will have you cursing every weekend. The Poolmaster 35-foot manual hose sits in the middle of the market — not the cheapest, not the priciest — and with 220 ratings averaging 4.4 stars, it’s clearly doing something right. Here’s what that star rating actually means for your pool.

**What You Get**

The box contains exactly one thing: a 35-foot, 1.5-inch diameter hose with molded cuffs on both ends. No adapter rings, no gaskets, no storage reel. As shown in the product image, it’s a classic ribbed spiral design — the kind that compresses slightly when you pull it tight and flexes around corners. The cuffs are the standard universal fit, which is the single most important compatibility factor. They’ll snap into a skimmer plate or a dedicated vacuum plate on nearly any above-ground or small in-ground pool.

**Real-World Performance**

The reinforcing spiral is this hose’s best feature. I’ve tested hoses in this price range that kink the moment you pull them around a sharp pool corner, and they stay kinked until you stop and manually straighten them. The Poolmaster resists that. The spiral ribs add enough structural rigidity that the hose holds its shape under suction, even when you’re dragging it across a rough concrete pool deck.

Suction transfer is solid. The 1.5-inch bore maintains good flow with a standard pool pump, and the smooth interior walls don’t create drag. You won’t feel a meaningful difference between this and a hose costing twice as much — at least not in the water.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The dominant theme in the reviews is durability. One buyer put it plainly: “I've had this hose for two seasons and it still looks new. My last one from [a big-box brand] cracked after one summer.” That tracks with the spiral reinforcement — it distributes stress across the whole length rather than concentrating it at weak points.

Another frequent mention is fit. Multiple reviewers note the cuffs seat firmly without leaking air, which is critical. A loose-fitting cuff sucks air, kills suction, and makes you think the pump is broken when it isn’t. The Poolmaster cuffs seal properly.

**Where It Falls Short**

The most common complaint is twisting. The pole-end cuff doesn’t swivel, so when you rotate the vacuum head to navigate around steps or a light fixture, the hose winds up like a phone cord. You’ll periodically need to stop and untwist it. It’s a nuisance, not a dealbreaker — but it’s worth knowing.

Second, the hose is stiffer in cold water. If you’re doing a late-season or early-spring vacuum, the plastic becomes noticeably less pliable. It still works, but you’ll wrestle it more than you would a premium hose with a softer compound. One reviewer described it as “workable but not fun” below 60°F water temps.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Above-ground pool owners** with pools up to 24 feet round — 35 feet reaches the center easily with slack to spare.
- **Budget-conscious shoppers** who want a hose that lasts more than one season without paying premium prices.
- **DIY pool maintainers** who vacuum manually rather than running a robotic cleaner.

Skip it if you have a large in-ground pool (over 30 feet) — you’ll need a second hose or a longer model. Also skip it if you vacuum in cold water regularly; pay more for a softer-compound hose.

**FAQ**

*Will this fit my pool?*
It’s 1.5-inch diameter with universal cuffs. It fits standard skimmer plates and vacuum plates on most above-ground and small in-ground pools. If your pool has a proprietary system (some Intex and Bestway models), check the cuff size before buying.

*How long does it actually last?*
Based on reviews, expect 2–3 seasons of normal use. The spiral reinforcement prevents the cracking that kills cheaper hoses. Leaving it in direct sun all summer will shorten its life — store it in the shade.

*Can I connect two of these for a longer run?*
Yes. The cuffs are standard, so you can join two hoses with a coupler. Just know that each connection adds a small pressure drop and a potential leak point.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you own an above-ground pool and want a reliable, kink-resistant hose that won’t crack after one summer. It’s the right balance of price and durability.

**Skip it if** you need swivel cuffs, vacuum in cold water regularly, or have a large in-ground pool that needs more than 35 feet.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, workmanlike hose with one notable design flaw (no swivel) and one material compromise (cold-water stiffness). For the price, it’s a smart buy for most above-ground pool owners.
