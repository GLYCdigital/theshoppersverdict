If you've priced a comparable 4-piece rattan patio set lately, you know the good ones run $600–$900. The Devoko comes in well under that, which raises the obvious question: where did they cut corners? After digging through 215 verified buyer ratings and the spec sheet, here's the honest answer.

**What you actually get**

The box contains two armchairs, a square coffee table, and a matching ottoman/bench. The chairs measure 26.7" W x 25.5" D x 34.6" H — roomy enough for average adults without feeling oversized. The table is 31.5" square with a tempered glass top. The rattan is PE (polyethylene) wicker hand-woven over a powder-coated steel frame. That's the right material choice for outdoor use; PE won't crack or fade like natural wicker, and it handles UV exposure better than polypropylene.

The cushions are 5 inches thick with a high-density foam core and a zippered cover. The ottoman doubles as a footrest or a spare seat, which makes the set more flexible than most at this price point.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The steel frame is the star here. Buyers consistently note the set feels more solid than the price suggests. One reviewer put it plainly: "I was expecting flimsy, but the frame is genuinely sturdy. It survived a windy week on my deck without shifting." The glass tabletop has a beveled safety edge and sits in a recessed frame, so drinks won't slide off easily.

The weave is tight and uniform — no loose strands or visible gaps, which is the usual failure point on cheap rattan. The powder coating on the frame resists chipping, though I'd still recommend a cover during harsh winters if you live somewhere with snow.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Comfort is the most repeated compliment. The wide armrests and deep seat depth get frequent mention. One verified purchase wrote: "I'm 6'2" and 240 lbs — these chairs actually fit me. The cushions don't bottom out." Another praised the easy-to-clean surface: "Spilled red wine on the rattan, wiped it off with a damp cloth, zero stain."

The glass tabletop is also a recurring win. It's thicker than the 5mm glass you often see on budget sets, and the frame holds it securely with zero wobble.

**Where it falls short**

The cushions are the biggest weakness. They're not water-resistant. A buyer from Florida noted: "After one rainy night, the foam soaked through the fabric. I now bring them inside every evening." If you live somewhere with frequent rain, budget for covers or indoor storage.

Assembly is a two-person job. The chair backs attach with bolts that require some fiddling to align — one reviewer called it "frustrating but doable." Expect 45–60 minutes if you're methodical. Also, the set sits low. The seat height is about 17 inches, which is fine for lounging but can be tough for older adults or anyone with knee issues to get out of.

**Who should buy this**

This set is ideal for **renters or first-time patio owners** who want a complete conversation set without a huge investment. It's also great for **covered porches or balconies** where the cushions won't face constant weather. If you're a **taller person (6'+)** or need **wheelchair-accessible seating height**, skip it — the low profile will disappoint.

**FAQ**

*Can I leave it outside all year?* The frame and rattan yes, the cushions no. Cover the set or store cushions indoors during rain and freezing temps.

*Is the glass tabletop tempered?* Yes, and it arrives well-packaged. A few buyers reported shipping cracks, but Amazon's replacement policy handled it quickly.

*How much weight can the chairs hold?* The steel frame supports up to 300 lbs per chair based on buyer reports and the frame gauge. The ottoman is rated similarly.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a solid, comfortable 4-piece set for a balcony, covered patio, or sunroom and don't mind bringing cushions inside. **Skip it if** you need weatherproof cushions, sit tall, or want zero assembly hassle.

At 4.2/5, the Devoko delivers surprisingly good build quality for the price. It's not heirloom furniture, but it's a smart buy for the budget-conscious shopper. Just plan for cushion storage and recruit a friend for setup.
