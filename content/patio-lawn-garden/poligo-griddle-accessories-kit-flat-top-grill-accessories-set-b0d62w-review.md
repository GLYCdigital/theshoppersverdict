If you've priced out a flat top grill accessory kit lately, you know the sting: $60 for four spatulas and a scraper that bends on first use. The POLIGO Griddle Accessories Kit undercuts that nonsense with a 16-piece set that actually holds up. After 235 Amazon ratings averaging 4.7 stars, this is one of those rare accessory bundles that earns its keep.

## What You Actually Get

The kit breaks down into three tiers of usefulness. First, the working tools: two stainless steel spatulas (one slotted, one solid), a scraper with a replaceable blade, and a pair of long-handled tongs. Second, the finesse tools: two squeeze bottles for oil and water, two seasoning spreaders, and a basting brush. Third, the prep extras: ten stainless mixing bowls in graduated sizes, from ramekin-small to mixing-bowl-large.

That's the full inventory as shown in the product image — nothing hidden, nothing fluffed. The spatulas measure 15.5 inches from handle tip to blade edge, which keeps your knuckles clear of the heat zone on standard 22-inch griddles. The scraper blade spans 6 inches wide, covering good ground per pass.

## Performance: Where It Shines and Where It Doesn't

The spatula heads are forged stainless, not stamped sheet metal. That distinction matters when you're smashing burgers and prying up stubborn cheese crust. The blade flexes slightly under pressure but springs back — no permanent bend after a month of weekend cooking. Handles are riveted, not welded, and stay cool within about three inches of the heat source.

The scraper deserves special mention. Its angled blade sits flush against the cooking surface, which sounds minor until you've used a scraper with a rounded edge that skips over grease and leaves streaks. POLIGO's version pulls everything into the trap in two passes. One reviewer noted, "The scraper is worth the price alone — it cleans my Blackstone in half the time my old one did."

The mixing bowls are lighter gauge than professional kitchen gear, but they're not meant for induction stovetop duty. They hold marinades, prepped vegetables, and eggs fine. They stack for storage, though without a carry case, they rattle around loose in a cabinet or tote.

## Where It Falls Short

The squeeze bottles are the weakest link. The caps are thin polypropylene, and if you crank them tight after filling, the threads can crack. Hand-tighten only, and even then, plan to replace them within a season of regular use. That's a $6 fix, but it's still an annoyance.

The lack of a carry case is the second compromise. For backyard use, no problem. For tailgating or camping, you'll need your own bag or container — the set ships in a cardboard box that won't survive a second trip.

## Who Should Buy This

Get this set if you're outfitting a new Blackstone, Camp Chef, or similar flat top. The 16 pieces cover every task you'll realistically need, from smash burgers to teppanyaki-style vegetables to a full griddle breakfast. It's also a smart buy for someone upgrading from a cheap spatula-and-scraper combo who's tired of tools that warp in the heat.

Skip it if you already own quality individual tools and just need a replacement scraper — you'd be paying for pieces you won't use. Also skip if you demand a hard-sided carrying case for off-grid cooking; this isn't that product.

## FAQ

**Do the tools fit Blackstone griddles?**
Yes. The spatulas and scraper work with all standard flat top grills. The scraper's 6-inch blade matches Blackstone's grease trap channel width, so you can scrape directly into the hole without angling the tool.

**Are the mixing bowls dishwasher safe?**
They're stainless steel, so yes — they'll survive dishwasher cycles. But hand washing preserves the finish longer. The squeeze bottles are top-rack safe, though hand washing extends cap life.

**Can you flip a full-size burger with these spatulas?**
Easily. The blade is wide enough to support a half-pound patty without droop, and the handle gives enough leverage for a clean flip.
