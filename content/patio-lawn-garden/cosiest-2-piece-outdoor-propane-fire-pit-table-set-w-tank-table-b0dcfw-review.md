If you've priced outdoor fire pit tables lately, you know they're either cheap junk that rusts in one season or $800+ designer pieces that make you wince at checkout. The COSIEST 2-piece set sits in that awkward middle ground — and for most buyers, it lands closer to the right side of that equation.

What makes this set different from the dozen other fire tables on Amazon is the layout. You get a 42-inch square fire pit table *plus* a matching 18-inch side table with a cabinet door. That side table is where the 20-pound propane tank lives. No visible hose, no tank sitting beside your chair like a garage-sale propane accessory. The fire table itself stays completely clear underneath, which means more legroom and a cleaner look.

**What's actually in the box**

The set arrives in two large boxes, and I want to be upfront: this is not a 20-minute assembly. The fire table has a steel frame, a tempered glass top, a 50,000 BTU burner, and a basket for the included lava rocks. The side table matches the same finish and has a hinged door with enough clearance for a standard 20-lb tank. You get the regulator hose, a protective cover for the fire table, and the ignition system pre-installed.

**Real-world performance**

Fire it up and you get what COSIEST claims: a 50,000 BTU output that puts out genuine warmth, not just flickering flames. On a 55-degree evening, sitting within four feet of the table keeps you comfortable. The flame adjuster gives you decent control from a low simmer to a roaring display. As shown in the product image, the flame pattern is a clean ring rather than a center column — that's actually better for heat distribution around the table.

The build quality is respectable for the price. The steel frame has a powder-coated finish that doesn't flake, and the glass top is a nice touch for setting drinks when the fire's off. The side table's cabinet door uses a magnetic latch, which holds up fine over multiple open-close cycles.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the 220 ratings (4.4 stars), the recurring theme is the layout. One reviewer put it plainly: *"The separate tank table is genius. No more tripping over the hose or having a propane tank sitting in the middle of the patio."* Another noted, *"This is the first fire table I've owned where the heat actually reaches you. The 50K BTU rating is real."*

**Where it falls short**

Assembly is the biggest complaint, and it's legitimate. Multiple buyers mention the tempered glass top being heavy and awkward — one said, *"You absolutely need two people. I cracked the glass trying to lift it alone."* That's a specific, painful warning worth heeding.

The piezo ignition is the second most common gripe. It works, but it's sensitive to wind. If you're in a breezy spot, expect to click several times before the pilot catches. That's annoying but not a dealbreaker — a long lighter solves it permanently.

The included lava rocks are underwhelming. They cover the burner but don't create the dramatic rock bed you see in marketing photos. Budget $15 for an extra bag of lava rock or glass beads if appearance matters to you.

**Who should buy this**

- **Patio entertainers** who host regularly and want the convenience of propane without the tank clutter.
- **First-time fire pit buyers** who want a complete set (table + side table + cover) without piecing together components.
- **Renters or homeowners** who want a solid, mid-range unit that won't break the bank but won't look cheap either.

**Skip it if** you're looking for a premium, zero-assembly piece, or if you have a strict weight limit on your deck — the fire table alone is substantial.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the side table fit a standard 20-lb propane tank?**
A: Yes, a standard 20-lb tank fits with room to spare. The door closes fully with the hose attached. Just leave enough slack in the regulator hose to avoid pinching.

**Q: How much heat does it actually put out?**
A: The 50,000 BTU burner is high for this category. On high, you'll feel warmth up to about 5 feet away. On low, it's more ambiance than heat.

**Q: Can I use it on a wooden deck?**
A: Yes, but use a fire pit pad or heat shield underneath. The base doesn't get dangerously hot, but the radiant heat from the burner can discolor wood over time.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a functional, good-looking propane fire pit set with smart tank storage and real heat output at a mid-range price. **Skip it if** you're unwilling to spend a couple hours assembling, or if you need a single-piece unit.
