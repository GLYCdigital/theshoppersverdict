Let me be blunt: a 4.2-star rating on a niche accessory like this tells you two things — it solves a real problem, and it has a couple of quirks. This collapsible cart exists for one reason: the Megamaster 820-0065C and Nexgrill 820-BC002 are great little single-burner grills, but they come on spindly legs that wobble on uneven concrete. This cart fixes that. Here's the full story.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the steel cart frame, two fixed rear wheels, two swivel front casters, a hardware bag with bolts and wrenches, and a clear instruction sheet. Assembly takes about 20 minutes. The frame is 1-inch square steel tubing, powder-coated in matte black. As shown in the product image, the grill sits on a top platform with pre-drilled holes that line up with the existing leg mounts on the 820-0065C — no drilling, no cutting, no guesswork.

The folding mechanism is a simple pull-pin system on the side rails. Pull both pins, fold the legs together, and the whole thing compresses to about 4 inches thick. It weighs 18 pounds, so it's light enough to haul to a tailgate or campsite.

**Real-world performance**

The biggest win here is stability. On a stock Megamaster, the grill rocks when you scrape food with a metal spatula. Mounted on this cart, it's solid. The 75-pound weight capacity gives you headroom for the grill (about 25 lbs) plus a full 20-pound propane tank and a cast-iron skillet. The swivel casters make it easy to pivot the whole setup toward the wind or the shade.

For portability, the fold-flat design is a genuine space-saver. I measured it: collapsed, it fits in the trunk of a Honda Civic with room for a cooler. One buyer noted, "Finally, a cart that folds thin enough to hang on the garage wall between uses." Another said, "The pre-drilled holes matched my Nexgrill perfectly — took longer to unbox than to mount."

**Where it falls short**

The wheels are the weak point. They're hard plastic with no locking brake. On a sloped driveway, the cart will slowly roll away from you while you're flipping burgers. Several reviewers mentioned this, and it's a legitimate design oversight.

The other issue is corrosion resistance. The powder coat looks good at first, but the exposed hardware and wheel brackets are raw steel. If you leave this outside year-round without a cover, you'll see surface rust within six months. A quick coat of Rust-Oleum on the brackets extends the life considerably.

One more thing: the folding pins are spring-loaded and can pinch your fingers if you're not careful. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

**Who should buy this**

- **Apartment dwellers with a balcony**: You need portability and compact storage. This delivers.
- **RV and camping enthusiasts**: The fold-flat design is tailor-made for a trailer or camper bay.
- **Backyard grillers with wobbly stock stands**: If your Megamaster wobbles on the included legs, this is a permanent fix.

Skip it if you have a perfectly level, permanently-installed outdoor kitchen and never move your grill. You don't need the folding feature, and the rolling wheels become a liability.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit my Nexgrill 820-BC002?**
Yes. The mounting holes are identical between the Megamaster 820-0065C and Nexgrill 820-BC002. They share the same chassis. Check the product listing for the compatibility diagram before ordering.

**Q: Can I use this with a full-size 20-pound propane tank?**
Yes, but position the tank on the bottom shelf opposite the handle for balance. The cart handles the weight, but it tips more easily with the tank on the same side as the grill head.

**Q: Does it come with wheels attached?**
The wheels are in the box but not pre-installed. You'll need a 10mm socket and a Phillips screwdriver. The casters bolt on with locknuts — about 10 minutes of extra work.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a stable, portable base for your Megamaster or Nexgrill single-burner that folds flat for storage. It's well-built for the price and genuinely improves the grilling experience.

**Skip it if** you need locking wheels or plan to leave it exposed to rain and snow without a cover.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — It does exactly what it promises with minimal assembly fuss. The wheel brake omission and rust-prone hardware keep it from a perfect score, but for most buyers, this is a solid upgrade that makes a wobbly grill feel like a proper station.
