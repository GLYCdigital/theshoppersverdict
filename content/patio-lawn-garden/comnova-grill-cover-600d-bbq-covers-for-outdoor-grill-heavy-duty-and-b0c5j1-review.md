Most grill covers are basically glorified tarps — too thin, too loose, and useless after one storm season. The Comnova 600D cover is not that. With 229 ratings holding a 4.6-star average, this is one of the better-reviewed heavy-duty covers in its price class. Here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The cover ships folded flat in a plastic bag — nothing fancy. Unfold it and you'll notice the difference immediately. The 600D polyester exterior has a tight weave, and the underside has a waterproof PVC coating that feels substantial, not sticky or cheap. ## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest part. There are no air vents. If you cover a completely cold grill after it's been rained on, trapped moisture can lead to mildew over time. The fix is simple — let the grill dry before covering — but it's a design gap that matters in humid climates.

Second, the black color fades to a dull gray after a few months of full sun. It doesn't affect performance, but if you care about aesthetics, pick the brown or green options instead. A few reviewers also noted the straps can be stiff when new, though they loosen up after a few uses.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have a mid-size gas grill exposed to wind, rain, or snow, and you're tired of replacing flimsy covers every season. It's also great for RVs or campsites where wind can be brutal.

**Skip it if:** You live in a humid area and want a breathable cover, or you need a custom fit for an oddly-shaped kettle or pellet smoker. This is designed for standard gas grills.
