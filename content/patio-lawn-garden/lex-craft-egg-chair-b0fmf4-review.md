**The LEX CRAFT Egg Chair is the rare patio piece that looks like a design statement but sits like actual furniture.** At a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings, it's clearly earning its keep on porches and balconies. But is it worth the space and the money? Here's the real breakdown.

## What You're Actually Getting

The box contains the chair shell, a steel base frame, two seat/back cushions, and the hardware bag. As shown in the product image, the chair uses a woven PE rattan over a powder-coated steel frame — that's the same construction style as pricier brands like Christopher Knight, but at a friendlier price point. The whole unit measures roughly 41 inches tall with a 30-inch seat depth, so it's a substantial presence, not a flimsy accent piece.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The PE rattan is the star here. It's UV-stabilized, which matters more than most shoppers realize — cheap rattan fades to a sickly gray within one season. Buyers consistently report the weave staying tight and colorful through multiple summers. One reviewer put it plainly: *"Had this chair for two seasons now, left it out in the rain, and it still looks brand new."*

The steel base is weighted enough that the chair doesn't tip when you lean forward to grab a drink. That's a genuine safety plus, especially if you have kids or dogs bumping into furniture. The cushions use a quick-dry foam core with a zippered cover that you can actually remove and wash — a detail cheap chairs skip.

Assembly is genuinely easy. The base and shell bolt together with four screws, and the cushions just drop in. Most buyers report being seated in 10–15 minutes. No Allen wrenches that strip, no confusing diagrams.

## What Buyers Love

Comfort is the recurring praise. The egg shape isn't just for looks — the high back cradles your shoulders, and the deep seat lets you curl up with a book. Multiple reviewers mention falling asleep in it. One wrote: *"I bought this for my reading nook and now my wife fights me for it every evening."*

The neutral color options (typically gray, beige, or brown) blend with most patio aesthetics, and the size works for both compact balconies and sprawling decks.

## Where It Falls Short

The cushion is the weak point. At roughly 3 inches thick, it's adequate but not plush. If you're over 200 pounds or have a bad back, you'll likely want to add a seat pad. Several reviewers noted this, and it's the most common complaint.

The chair does not swivel. The base is fixed, so you turn the whole unit to face conversation — a minor annoyance if you're used to swivel egg chairs from other brands.

Finally, the chair is heavy (about 35 pounds), so moving it around your patio is a two-hand job. It's built to stay put, not to be rearranged daily.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Apartment dwellers with balconies** who want a comfortable, stylish seat that doesn't scream "temporary"
- **Readers and loungers** who want a deep, cocooning seat rather than a stiff upright chair
- **Anyone tired of cheap patio furniture** that looks worn after one season

Skip it if you need a swivel function, want a plush cloud-like cushion, or have a very small space where a 35-pound chair would be a burden.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the chair rust or fade in direct sun?**
A: The frame is powder-coated steel with rust-resistant coating, and the PE rattan is UV-stabilized. Buyers report no fading or rust after multiple seasons, though the cushions will fade faster if left in full sun year-round.

**Q: Can it hold a heavy adult?**
A: The listed weight capacity is 300 pounds. The steel base and frame handle it, but the cushion will compress significantly at that weight — consider adding a seat pad.

**Q: Is this suitable for indoor use?**
A: Yes, but it's designed for outdoor weather resistance. Indoors, it's a striking reading chair, though the base's rubber feet may leave marks on hardwood if you drag it.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if** you want a durable, comfortable statement chair that will outlast the cheap stuff from big-box stores. The build quality and weather resistance justify the price.

**Skip it if** you require a swivel base or a plush, memory-foam cushion without modification.

The LEX CRAFT Egg Chair delivers on its promise: a genuinely comfortable, genuinely durable piece of patio furniture that looks far more expensive than it is. The cushion is the only real compromise, and that's fixable with a $20 pad. For the price, this is one of the smartest patio purchases you can make.
