Let's be honest: most garden hose storage looks like a plastic toadstool vomited in your yard. The EMSCO Group Hose Hiders take a different approach—they look like decorative stone planters until you lift the lid and find 125 feet of coiled hose hiding inside. That's the pitch, anyway. After digging through 204 ratings and the real-world feedback, here's what actually matters.

**What You Get**

This is a 2-pack of resin storage boxes, each measuring roughly 18 inches in diameter and 14 inches tall. Each unit holds up to 125 feet of standard 5/8-inch hose coiled neatly inside. The stone-look exterior comes in a natural gray-brown finish that genuinely passes for decorative garden rock from a few feet away. Inside, there's a center core that guides the hose into a coil as you feed it through the side opening.

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

The resin material is the standout feature here. It's thick enough to feel substantial when you lift it, and multiple buyers noted it survives full sun exposure without fading or becoming brittle. One reviewer put it plainly: "Had these for two seasons in Texas heat. No cracking, no warping, no color fade. My last cheap holder fell apart in one summer." That durability matters if you're leaving these out year-round.

The hose-feeding system works better than most. You thread the hose through the side port, and the internal guide wraps it around the center core as you push. It's not magical—you still need to feed it evenly or you'll get a tangled mess—but it's noticeably smoother than the free-coil-and-hope method most boxes use. Pulling hose out is equally straightforward: grab the end and pull; the coil spins freely without kinking.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The disguise factor is the most-loved feature. Multiple reviewers mentioned neighbors asking where they bought the decorative rocks. One wrote, "My HOA literally complimented the 'stone planters' before realizing they were hose storage." The capacity is also a recurring highlight—people with 100-foot hoses confirm they fit with room to spare.

**Where It Falls Short**

Assembly is the most common complaint. The two halves screw together, but the screw holes don't always align perfectly, forcing you to wrestle with the pieces. It's a one-time frustration, not a dealbreaker, but budget 15 minutes and have a decent screwdriver ready.

The lid is the bigger functional issue. It sits on top without any latch or locking mechanism. If you don't fully seat the hose and the lid doesn't close flush, a strong gust can flip it open. A few buyers reported finding their lids across the yard after storms. The fix is simple—make sure the hose is fully inside and the lid seats flat—but it's a design oversight worth knowing.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for homeowners who care about curb appeal but don't want to drag hoses around. It's ideal if you have a 75-125 foot hose, want something that survives harsh sun without looking like a lawn ornament casualty, and prefer a "set and forget" solution. Skip it if you have a short hose you can hang on a wall hook, need something you can wheel around, or want tool-free assembly.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

*Can these hold a hose with metal fittings?*
Yes, the side port accommodates standard brass or aluminum fittings. The opening is about 2 inches, so oversized commercial fittings might be tight, but standard household connectors pass through fine.

*Do I need to drain the hose before storing?*
Yes. The boxes have small drainage holes in the bottom, but leaving water in the hose invites mildew and makes the coil heavier than the lid can handle. Drain it, then feed it in.

*Can you paint or stain these to match your yard?*
Several buyers successfully used exterior spray paint on the resin. Light sanding first helps adhesion. The stone texture takes paint well, but you'll need a flexible exterior-grade paint to avoid cracking.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want hose storage that doesn't look like hose storage, need capacity for a long hose, and value UV-resistant build quality over bargain-bin pricing.

**Skip it if:** You're on a tight budget, want something you can assemble in 60 seconds, or live in an area with hurricane-force winds that will test the unlidded design.

The EMSCO Group Hose Hiders earn a solid 4.2/5. They're not perfect—the lid issue and assembly quirks are real—but for the core job of hiding and protecting a long hose, they deliver with durability that cheaper options can't match.
