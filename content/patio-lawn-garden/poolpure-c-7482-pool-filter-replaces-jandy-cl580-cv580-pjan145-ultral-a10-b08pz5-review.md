## Where it falls short

Two honest complaints surface repeatedly. First, the cartridge ships without a gasket or O-ring. If you're replacing an old filter that's been in service for a year or more, that old gasket may be compressed, cracked, or just plain gross. You'll need to reuse it or order a replacement separately — an extra step that's easy to miss.

Second, a handful of buyers note the end caps feel "slightly less rigid" than the OEM Jandy part. In practice, this means you should handle it gently during installation — don't torque it hard or drop it on concrete. Once seated in the housing, it performs fine, but the plastic isn't quite as beefy as the original.
