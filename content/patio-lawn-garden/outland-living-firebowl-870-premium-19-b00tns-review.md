If you've ever wrestled with wet firewood, smoky clothes, and the 20 minutes it takes to get a campfire going, the Outland Living Firebowl 870 is the antidote. This 19-inch propane fire pit delivers instant flame with zero smoke and zero ash — and with 240 Amazon ratings averaging 4.8 stars, it's clearly doing something right. But is it the right fire pit for your patio? Let's break it down.

**What You Actually Get**

Right out of the box, the Firebowl 870 is nearly ready to go. The burner bowl and base legs arrive fully assembled — the only step is connecting the attached 10-foot hose to a standard 20-pound propane tank. That's it. No wrenches, no screwdrivers, no deciphering cryptic instructions. The package includes the fire bowl, a 10-foot hose with regulator, and a bag of lava rocks that you spread over the burner to create that natural fire look.

The unit itself is compact: roughly 19 inches in diameter and about 12 inches tall. The stainless steel bowl has a brushed finish that looks more premium than the price point suggests. It's a portable unit at 17 pounds, so moving it from patio to deck to campsite is genuinely easy.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The headline number is 58,000 BTUs. In practical terms, that's enough heat to feel substantial warmth on a cool evening within a 6-foot radius. The stainless steel burner ring distributes flames evenly around the bowl's perimeter, and the included lava rocks diffuse the heat nicely. One buyer noted: "This thing puts out serious heat — we sat outside in 50-degree weather with jackets open." That matches the general consensus in the reviews.

The push-button ignition is reliable — no fumbling with lighters or matches. The flame adjustment dial gives you good control from a low, subtle flame to a roaring blaze. Safety features are solid: the unit is UL-listed, the regulator is CSA-certified, and it automatically shuts off the gas if the flame goes out. That's not a spec to gloss over — it's the difference between a safe product and a liability.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Reviewers across the board highlight three things. First, the lack of smoke and smell. As one verified purchaser put it: "No smoky clothes, no ash in your drink, no waiting for wood to catch." Second, the build quality — the stainless steel bowl and burner hold up well to regular use. Third, the convenience of the attached hose and regulator. You'll find multiple reviews comparing it favorably to more expensive brands like Outland's own higher-end models or Solo Stove's fire pits.

**Where It Falls Short**

Honest assessment: this isn't a perfect product. The most common complaint is the lack of a cover — the bowl will collect rain and debris if left outside, and stainless steel will eventually show water spots. Buy a compatible cover for about $20 extra; it's worth it.

Second, the stainless steel is a fingerprint magnet. The brushed finish looks great in the product image, but in practice, it smudges easily. You'll want a microfiber cloth handy.

Third, the legs are short — the fire bowl sits low, roughly knee-height. If you have tall patio chairs, you may find the flame sits below your sight line. It's fine for low-profile seating or ground-level chairs, but measure your furniture before committing.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is ideal for three types of shoppers: apartment dwellers or homeowners with HOA restrictions where wood fires are banned (propane is universally allowed), campers who want a packable heat source, and anyone who wants a fire feature without the maintenance of hauling wood and cleaning ash. It's not the right pick if you want a permanent, in-ground fire feature or if you need a flame height that competes with a full-size wood bonfire.

**FAQ**

**How long does a 20-pound propane tank last?** On medium heat, expect 10-12 hours of burn time. On high, closer to 6-8 hours. That's enough for several evenings of use per tank.

**Can you cook over it?** Not effectively. The lava rocks and burner design aren't meant for grilling. Consider it strictly for ambiance and heat.

**Does it work on uneven surfaces?** The legs are stable on flat ground but can wobble slightly on grass or gravel. Place it on a firm, level surface for best results.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want instant, smoke-free warmth with zero setup hassle, and you value safety certifications. This is one of the best-value propane fire pits at this size.

**Skip it if:** You need a tall fire feature for high seating, or you're unwilling to buy a separate cover for outdoor storage.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The Outland Living Firebowl 870 does exactly what it promises: reliable, safe, portable heat with no fuss. The minor flaws are easily worked around, and at this price point, it's tough to beat.
