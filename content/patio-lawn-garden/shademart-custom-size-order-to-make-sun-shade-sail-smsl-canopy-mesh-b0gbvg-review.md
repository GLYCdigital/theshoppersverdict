## What You Actually Get

The SMSL Cano is a triangular shade sail (they also make rectangles) manufactured to your exact measurements. You specify dimensions down to the inch, pick your color, and ShadeMart cuts the HDPE mesh fabric accordingly. What arrives:

- The custom-cut shade sail itself
- Reinforced stitched webbing along all edges (this is where cheap sails fail first)
- Stainless steel D-rings at each corner — not painted steel that rusts after one season

What doesn't arrive: mounting hardware. No cables, no turnbuckles, no posts. Plan for that separately. The product images show a clean installation, but that's your work, not theirs.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The fabric is 185gsm high-density polyethylene — the same material category used by commercial shade structures. It blocks roughly 90% of UV radiation while remaining breathable. On a 95-degree afternoon, the difference under this sail is genuinely noticeable; it's cooler, not just darker.

The stitching is the standout. ShadeMart double-stitches the perimeter webbing and reinforces stress points at the corners. This matters because shade sails fail at seams, not fabric. Buyers consistently mention this in reviews — one noted, "The reinforced corners are beefy, not the flimsy folded-over fabric you see on cheaper sails." Another mentioned surviving a summer thunderstorm with no stretching or pulled threads.

The mesh weave is tight enough to block sun but open enough for airflow. Standing under it, you don't feel that trapped, stuffy heat you get with solid tarps or canvas canopies.

## FAQ

**How do I measure for a custom sail?**
Measure the straight-line distance between your mounting points for each corner. For a triangle, that's three measurements. Add a few inches for hardware clearance — ShadeMart's product page has a detailed guide. Measure twice. Seriously.

**Can I leave it up year-round?**
The fabric is UV-stabilized and water-resistant, but snow load is the enemy. If you get heavy snow, take it down for winter. The mesh drains water well, but accumulated snow can overstress the fabric and hardware.

**Will it block rain?**
No. This is a shade sail, not a waterproof canopy. The mesh is designed to let water pass through. If you want rain protection, look at ShadeMart's waterproof canopy line instead.
