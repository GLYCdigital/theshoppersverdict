## FAQ: Three Questions Buyers Actually Ask

**Q: Can I use this on eggs I plan to hatch?**
Yes—and this is a key advantage. The enzyme formula doesn’t penetrate the shell or clog pores, so it won’t suffocate the embryo. Just rinse thoroughly with warm water (not cold) to avoid drawing bacteria inward.

**Q: What water temperature should I use?**
Warm water, 90–100°F. Hot water causes the egg’s contents to expand and can push bacteria through the shell. Cold water pulls bacteria in. Warm is the sweet spot.

**Q: How long does a bottle last?**
A 16-ounce bottle makes roughly 16 gallons of wash solution. For a 10-hen flock cleaning daily, expect 4–6 months. For heavier use (hatching or selling), closer to 2–3 months.
