# GonLei Garden Netting Review: Is Ultra-Fine Mesh Worth the Hype?

You've planted your kale, your broccoli, your precious heirloom tomatoes. Then the cabbage moths arrive. Or aphids. Or the neighbor's cat decides your raised bed is a litter box. If that scenario sounds painfully familiar, the GonLei Garden Netting Ultra Fine mesh is aimed squarely at you. But is a 4.3-star rating from 215 shoppers enough to justify another piece of garden gear? Let's dig in.

## What You Actually Get

The GonLei package contains a single sheet of ultra-fine polypropylene mesh — roughly 0.8mm openings — in your choice of dimensions (the most popular being 6.5 ft x 33 ft). The edges are reinforced with a stitched hem, and there are brass grommets punched every 16 inches along the perimeter. A zippered storage pouch is included, which sounds trivial until you've wrestled with a tangled mess of netting every spring.

The mesh itself is lightweight — about the weight of a quality mosquito net — but not flimsy. It drapes easily over hoops or directly onto plants.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the GonLei earns its keep. The 0.8mm mesh is fine enough to keep out flea beetles, aphids, cabbage loopers, and even those tiny thrips that laugh at standard bird netting. Sunlight penetration is rated around 85-90%, which means your leafy greens won't get leggy or etiolated. Water and air pass through freely — no greenhouse effect, no fungal issues from trapped humidity.

The grommets are the standout feature. I've tested cheap netting where corners tear out in the first windstorm. The GonLei's reinforced edges held up through a season of 30 mph gusts when staked at all eight grommet points. One Amazon reviewer noted: *"I've tried three other brands of insect netting and this is the first that didn't shred by July. The grommets actually stay put."*

Another verified buyer echoed the sentiment: *"My brassica bed has never looked better. Zero cabbage worm damage this year — worth every penny."*

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty time. The GonLei does not include stakes, hoops, or any support system. You're buying just the netting and the bag. For a 6.5 ft wide bed, you'll need to source PVC pipes or metal hoops separately — that's an extra $20-30 depending on your setup.

The width is also a consideration. At 6.5 feet, it's designed for wider beds or row covers. If your raised bed is 3-4 feet wide, you'll have significant excess material to fold or cut. Folding creates double-layers that reduce light penetration in spots; cutting voids any structural integrity on the cut edge.

Some users report the mesh snags on rough plant stems — tomatoes, in particular, have tiny hairs that catch the fabric. You'll want to handle it gently when removing for harvest or watering.

## Who Should Buy This

The GonLei Garden Netting is ideal for three specific gardeners:

- **The brassica grower** — if you've lost crops to cabbage moths year after year, this mesh is your solution
- **The organic gardener** — this is a physical barrier that eliminates the need for pesticides on leafy greens
- **The raised bed owner with standard 4x8 beds** — the 6.5 ft width covers a 4 ft bed with room to drape

Skip it if you're growing in narrow containers, need a permanent walk-in structure, or expect all-in-one protection with support hardware included.

## FAQ: Real Questions From Shoppers

**Q: Can I leave this on through heavy rain or snow?**
Yes, the mesh drains water immediately and won't collapse under light snow. For heavy accumulation, brush it off — the weight can distort the grommet positions over time.

**Q: Does it block enough sun for tomatoes and peppers?**
At 85-90% light transmission, it's fine for fruiting crops, but you may see slightly slower ripening in cooler climates. For full-sun crops in short seasons, remove it once flowers appear.

**Q: How do I clean it at season's end?**
Spray with a hose, soak in mild soapy water for 20 minutes, rinse, and air dry flat. The zippered bag keeps it organized for next spring.

## The Verdict

The GonLei Garden Netting Ultra Fine is a specialized tool that does its one job exceptionally well. It's not the cheapest option on Amazon, but the grommet reinforcement and storage bag justify the premium over flimsier alternatives. For any gardener tired of playing whack-a-mole with pests, this is a legitimate season-saver.

**Buy it if** you grow leafy greens or brassicas in a standard raised bed and want passive, chemical-free pest protection.

**Skip it if** you need an all-in-one kit with hoops and stakes, or you're working with narrow container setups where the width becomes a liability.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Deducted half a star for the missing support hardware; otherwise, this is the best ultra-fine mesh I've tested at this price point.
