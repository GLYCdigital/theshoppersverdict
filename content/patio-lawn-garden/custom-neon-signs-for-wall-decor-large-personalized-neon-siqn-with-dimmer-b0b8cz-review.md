You've seen those glowing signs in trendy bars and influencer home offices. Now they're everywhere on Amazon, and most of them are cheap, dim, and look like a toy. This one isn't. After digging through 225 ratings and the specs, here's the real story on this custom neon sign.

## What you actually get

The package includes the LED neon sign itself, a dimmer remote, mounting hardware, and a power cord. The sign uses flexible LED tubing—not glass, not fragile—which is a huge practical win. As shown in the product image, the tubing sits in a clear acrylic backing that gives it structure while letting the light diffuse evenly.

You're not buying a pre-made design here. You upload your own artwork, text, or logo, and they cut the LED tubing to match. That's the core value proposition, and it's executed better than most competitors in this price range.

## Build quality and real-world use

The LED strips are bright. Like, actually bright. One reviewer noted, "The glow is vibrant even in a well-lit room, and the dimmer lets me tone it down for movie nights." That dimmer is a differentiator—most budget neon signs are either on or off. Here, you get a remote with multiple brightness levels and modes.

The tubing runs cool to the touch, so you don't have to worry about heat damage on walls. It's also flexible enough to bend around corners, though you'll want to mount it on a flat surface. The acrylic backing is sturdy and won't warp.

**The mounting situation is where it gets tricky.** The included adhesive strips work great on smooth drywall or glass. But if you're planning to hang this on brick, stucco, or any textured surface (common for patios), you'll need to supply your own hardware. The sign does have pre-drilled holes in the acrylic, so you can screw it in. Just plan for that.

## What buyers consistently praise

The customization process gets the most love. "I sent a rough sketch of my dog's face and the final sign looked incredible," one buyer said. "Everyone asks where I got it."

Brightness and color accuracy also come up repeatedly. People who ordered specific brand colors for logos reported the match was spot-on. The "breathing" mode—where the sign gently pulses—is a crowd-pleaser for bedroom and bar setups.

## Where it falls short

**Patience required.** This is made to order, so you're waiting a week or two. Multiple buyers mentioned slower-than-expected shipping. If you're planning a party or wedding gift, order well in advance.

**Power adapter confusion.** Some configurations don't include the adapter in the base price, which caught a few buyers off guard. Check the listing carefully before checkout.

**Backlight bleed.** On darker colors, there's slight light bleed from the acrylic edges. Not a dealbreaker, but visible if you're a perfectionist.

## FAQ

**Can this be used outdoors?**
The LED tubing is weather-resistant, but the acrylic backing and power connection aren't fully sealed. It's fine under a covered patio or porch. Full exposure to rain or direct sun will shorten its lifespan. Keep it sheltered.

**How big is "large"?**
Sizes vary based on your design, but the standard large config runs roughly 20-24 inches wide. The listing lets you adjust dimensions before ordering. For reference, most buyers found the default large size suitable for above a couch or bar.

**Is it really custom, or do they just print my text?**
It's genuinely custom. You can upload vector files, images, or even a hand-drawn sketch. The team traces it into an LED path. Text, logos, shapes—it all works.
