## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this for actual rock climbing?**
No. It's a static rope with zero stretch. For climbing, you need a UIAA-certified dynamic rope that absorbs fall energy.

**Q: How do I stop the ends from fraying?**
Cut with a hot knife or sharp scissors, then carefully melt the cut ends with a lighter for 2-3 seconds. Don't hold the flame on too long — you'll melt the whole strand.

**Q: Will this hold a 200-pound adult on a swing?**
Yes. The 400+ pound rating gives you solid margin, and multiple reviewers confirm adult use without issues.
