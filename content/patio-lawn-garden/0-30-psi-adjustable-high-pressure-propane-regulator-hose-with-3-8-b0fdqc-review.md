You get the regulator body with an integrated gauge, a 3-foot rubber hose, and two brass adapters (3/8" and 1/4" NPT). **FAQs**

**Q: Can I use this on a standard 20-pound grill tank?**
Yes. It connects to any standard propane tank valve with the included 5/8" fitting. Just make sure your appliance is rated for high-pressure input.

**Q: Does it work with natural gas?**
No. This is propane-specific. Natural gas requires a different orifice and regulator configuration.

**Q: How do I know if I have a leak after installation?**
Mix dish soap with water, apply it to all connections, and open the tank valve. Bubbles indicate a leak — tighten and retest. Do this every time you reconnect.
