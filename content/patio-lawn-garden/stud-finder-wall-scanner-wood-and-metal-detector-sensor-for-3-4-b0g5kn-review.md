**FAQ**  
**Q: Does it work on concrete or brick?**  
A: No. It’s designed for drywall, wood, and light metal detection. For concrete, you need a dedicated concrete scanner with ground-penetrating radar.

**Q: How do I avoid false positives on textured walls?**  
A: Move the scanner very slowly—less than 1 inch per second—and press it flat. On heavy texture, do a first pass to identify the general area, then a second pass with slightly more pressure to confirm.

**Q: Does it detect live wires?**  
A: It detects metal, so it may pick up metal conduit or nails near wires, but it does not detect live AC current. Use a dedicated voltage detector for electrical safety.
