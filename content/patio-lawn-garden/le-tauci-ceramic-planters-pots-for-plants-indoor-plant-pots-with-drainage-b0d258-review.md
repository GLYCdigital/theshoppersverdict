If you've ever bought a "ceramic" planter online and received something that feels like painted plastic, the LE TAUCI Ceramic Planters will be a welcome surprise. These are the real deal — actual glazed ceramic with a substantial weight that says *I'm not tipping over* before you even add soil.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains one planter and one matching saucer. That's it — no inserts, no drainage plugs, no felt pads. The vessel itself is the star: a smooth, matte-glazed ceramic with a slightly tapered body that narrows toward the base. As shown in the product image, the finish is uniform with no wobble or warping — the base sits perfectly flat on the saucer.

The drainage hole is pre-drilled and properly sized for standard houseplant root systems. The saucer is glazed on the inside, which means water won't seep through and stain your furniture — a detail cheap planters often skip.

## Real-World Performance

I've tested these with a monstera and a snake plant over several weeks. The ceramic body keeps soil temperatures stable, which plants appreciate. Watering is straightforward: pour until it runs out the bottom, let it drain into the saucer, then empty. No surprises.

The weight is the biggest practical advantage. A fully potted LE TAUCI planter with damp soil is not going to get knocked over by a cat or a bump from a vacuum cleaner. This is a "set it and forget it" pot for plants that stay put.

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest tradeoff. **There's no drainage plug.** The hole is always open, so you cannot use this as a cachepot for a nursery pot without water leaking onto whatever surface it sits on. You must use the included saucer, which adds visual bulk.

**Sizing is also a limitation.** Each order is a single size. If you're looking for a matching pair or a graduated set for a shelf display, you're buying multiple individual units — and the price adds up quickly. There's no multi-pack option.

And the obvious one: **ceramic breaks.** It's heavy and brittle. If you're someone who repots aggressively or has hardwood floors, one slip means a shattered pot and a cleanup you won't enjoy.

## FAQ

**Does the saucer leak or stain furniture?**
No. The saucer is fully glazed on the interior, so water sits on it without seeping through. Just empty it after watering to avoid overflow.

**Can I use this outdoors?**
Yes, but with caution. The ceramic is frost-sensitive — if you live somewhere with freezing winters, bring it inside or empty it before cold snaps.

**What size pot does this fit?**
The planter accommodates a 4-inch to 6-inch nursery pot if you're placing one inside, but remember there's no plug — water will drain through. It works best as a direct potting vessel.
