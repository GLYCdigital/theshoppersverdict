**Real-world performance**

The planter measures about 5 inches tall with a 4-inch opening — roughly the size of a standard nursery pot. That's small, and you need to know that going in. A 4-inch succulent or a small snake plant cutting will fit comfortably. A full monstera? Not a chance. I'd also recommend adding a layer of gravel or a coffee filter over the drainage hole before adding soil, because the hole is wide enough that fine dirt will wash through on the first watering.

That drainage hole is a genuine plus — many decorative pots in this price range skip it entirely, which is a death sentence for succulents. The saucer catches runoff well, though it's shallow, so you'll want to empty it after heavy watering to avoid the pot sitting in standing water.
