Let me be straight with you: the budget patio furniture market is flooded with sets that look great in photos and fall apart by August. The Greesum 4-Piece Conversation Set sits at a 4.0 average across 200 ratings — which tells me it's neither a standout nor a disaster. But the details matter more than the aggregate score. Here's what you're actually getting.

**What's in the box**

This set includes two single chairs, a loveseat, and a tempered glass coffee table. The frame is powder-coated steel wrapped in all-weather PE rattan — the same material class you'd find on sets costing twice as much. Each chair measures roughly 25 inches wide with a seat height around 17 inches, which puts you at a comfortable dining-table height for conversation. The loveseat seats two adults at about 49 inches across. The glass tabletop is 35.4 by 17.7 inches, which is large enough for drinks, a board game, or a laptop without dominating the space.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The steel frame is the backbone here, and it's genuinely sturdy. The rattan weave is tight and uniform — no gaps or loose strands out of the box, which is the first thing I check on any wicker set. The cushions use high-density foam rather than the cheap polyfill you'll find on bargain sets. They're firm enough to support you for an afternoon of reading, but not so stiff that you feel like you're sitting on a park bench.

One buyer noted: *"The cushions took a full day to fluff out after unpacking, but after that they're surprisingly comfortable. We've had this set on our covered porch for six months and the color hasn't faded."* That tracks with what I'd expect from PE rattan — it's UV-stabilized and handles sun exposure far better than natural wicker.

The tempered glass table is a genuine upgrade over the mesh or plastic tables on comparable sets. It's thick enough to feel solid, and the smooth surface wipes down with a damp cloth. No worries about drinks leaving rings or crumbs getting trapped in weave.

**Where it falls short**

Assembly is the biggest friction point. The instructions are diagram-only, and the pieces are heavier than they look — especially the glass tabletop. Plan for 45 to 60 minutes with two people, and have a power drill with a hex bit handy. The included Allen wrench works, but your wrists will thank you for the upgrade.

The cushions arrive vacuum-sealed and compressed. They will look flat and sad for the first day or two. This is normal, but if you're hosting a party this weekend, set them out now.

Finally, there's no weather cover included. If you live somewhere with harsh winters, you'll want to buy a cover separately or store the cushions indoors. PE rattan is tough, but no outdoor furniture survives a Minnesota winter unprotected.

**Who should buy this**

This set is ideal for first-time patio buyers who want a cohesive conversation area without dropping $1,500 at a big-box store. It's also a solid pick for renters — the pieces are modular enough to move through standard doorways, and the neutral weave blends with most decor styles.

Skip it if you need a dining-height table, want deep lounge seating for napping, or require zero-assembly furniture. This is a conversation set first and foremost — the seats are upright, not reclined.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Is the glass table safe for outdoor use?**
Yes. The tempered glass is rated for outdoor conditions and resists thermal shock. It's also less likely to shatter into dangerous shards if something heavy drops on it.

**Can the cushions stay outside in the rain?**
The covers are water-repellent, but not waterproof. For heavy or prolonged rain, bring the cushions inside or cover them. The foam can absorb moisture if left soaked for days.

**How much weight can each chair hold?**
The steel frame is rated to approximately 300 pounds per seat. The woven rattan is decorative — the load-bearing structure is all metal underneath.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a good-looking, functional conversation set that won't break the bank, and you're willing to spend an hour assembling it with a helper. This is a solid 4-out-of-5 product that delivers on its promises.

**Skip it if** you need premium cushion comfort, want weatherproof everything-out-in-the-rain durability, or can't tolerate any assembly frustration. For those needs, budget another $300–500 for a higher-tier set.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A well-built budget set with a few practical compromises. The rattan quality and tempered glass elevate it above the typical Amazon fare. Just budget time for setup and plan for cushion fluffing.
