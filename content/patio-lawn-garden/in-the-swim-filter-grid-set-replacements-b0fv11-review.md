## What You Actually Get

The box contains the full set of grids for your specific filter model — not a universal "one size fits all" situation. You'll get the main grid assembly plus the smaller manifold grids, depending on which configuration you order. What you won't get is any hardware, gaskets, or O-rings. That's worth noting because many pool owners assume a "set" includes everything needed for the job. It doesn't. Inspect your existing seals before placing the order.

The grids themselves are constructed with a polyester weave over a rigid polypropylene frame. ## Where It Falls Short

Nothing's perfect, and this set has two honest drawbacks.

First, the frame tabs are slightly thinner than OEM parts on some models. This doesn't affect performance, but it means you should handle the grids gently during installation. Yanking on the tabs to seat the manifold can crack them. Take the extra 30 seconds to seat everything carefully.

Second, the lack of included gaskets is a genuine inconvenience. If your existing gaskets are compressed or cracked, you'll be making a second order. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's an avoidable friction point that In The Swim could easily fix.

## FAQ

**How often should I replace my D.E. filter grids?**
Most manufacturers recommend every 3-5 years, but it depends on usage. If you backwash frequently, run the pump year-round, or have heavy debris loads, inspect annually. Signs of replacement: rising pressure despite backwashing, cloudy water, or visible tears.

**Will these fit my filter?**
The listing specifies compatible models. Match your filter's model number exactly. Don't guess based on brand alone — Pentair and Hayward make multiple grid configurations.

**Can I clean these grids instead of replacing them?**
Yes, you can extend life with periodic chemical cleaning using a D.E. filter cleaner. But once the fabric tears or the frame cracks, cleaning won't help. Replacement is the only fix.
