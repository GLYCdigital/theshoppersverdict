## What You Actually Get

The kit includes the corner brackets themselves plus the mounting hardware. As shown in the product image, these are L-shaped steel brackets designed to sit flush against the inside corner of your 4x4 posts. They're not decorative — they're structural. The steel is substantial, and the powder-coated finish is meant to survive outdoor conditions without turning into a rusted mess by next spring.

One thing I appreciate: the pre-drilled holes actually line up with where you'd naturally want to place screws in a 4x4. That might sound basic, but you'd be surprised how many bracket kits on Amazon have holes that force you into awkward positions or require drilling your own. This one doesn't.

## Real-World Performance

The brackets do what they're supposed to: they hold corners tight. Multiple buyers noted how much easier this made their builds. One reviewer wrote, "Made my pergola build so much easier. ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about limitations. These are specifically for 4x4 lumber. If your project uses 6x6 posts — common for larger pergolas — these won't fit. Check your lumber dimensions before ordering.

The powder coat can also chip if you're aggressive with an impact driver. It's cosmetic rather than structural, but if you want a clean look, hand-tighten the final quarter-turn. No structural failures reported, but a few buyers mentioned scuffed finish from over-torquing.
