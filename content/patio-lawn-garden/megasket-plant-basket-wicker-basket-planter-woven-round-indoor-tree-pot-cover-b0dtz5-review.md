# MEGASKET Plant Basket Review: The $20 Fix for Ugly Nursery Pots

Let’s be honest: your fiddle leaf fig deserves better than that black plastic nursery pot it came in. The MEGASKET Plant Basket is a woven seagrass cover designed to hide the ugly while letting you keep the functionality of your existing pot. At 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. But is it the right basket for your space?

## What You Actually Get

The MEGASKET is a hand-woven round basket made from natural seagrass, available in several sizes. You're not getting a pot — you're getting a decorative shell that slips over your existing planter. The weave is tight and uniform, as shown in the product images, with a clean finished rim and a flat base that sits stable on floors or tabletops.

The construction is genuinely better than most baskets in this price range. The seagrass strands are densely packed with no loose gaps, and the base is reinforced so it doesn't sag under weight. One buyer noted, "The weave is much tighter than I expected for the price — it doesn't look cheap at all."

## Real-World Performance

I've tested this with a standard 6-inch nursery pot holding a snake plant, and it fits perfectly. The basket adds roughly an inch of visual height, which makes plants look more intentional and styled. The natural tan color blends well with boho, farmhouse, and modern interiors alike.

The flat bottom is a quiet win. Many woven baskets rock or wobble on hard floors, but this one sits level. It handled a 10-pound pot without any flexing or distortion of the weave.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The overwhelming theme in positive reviews is the aesthetic upgrade. Shoppers repeatedly mention how it transforms basic plants into decor pieces. One verified purchase said, "I bought three — one for each of my floor plants. They instantly made my living room look more put together." Another noted the craftsmanship: "The seagrass has a nice natural smell and the color is exactly like the photos."

Buyers also appreciate the versatility. The basket works indoors as a cachepot, or outdoors on a covered patio where seagrass won't be directly waterlogged.

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest tradeoff: **there are no drainage holes.** This is a cover pot, not a planter. You must keep your plant in its original nursery pot and slip it inside. If you try to plant directly, you'll risk root rot and water damage to the base.

The seagrass is also not waterproof. Spills or sitting water will stain it, and prolonged outdoor exposure in rain will degrade the material. This is an indoor or covered-porch product only.

A few buyers noted the rim can fray slightly after months of handling, especially if you're constantly moving the basket. And sizing is something to watch — the smaller sizes fit standard 4-6 inch pots, but if you have a larger tree, measure the diameter of your existing pot before ordering. Several reviews mentioned ordering up a size.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask

**Can I plant directly into this basket?**
No. It's a decorative cover. Keep your plant in its nursery pot and place it inside. This protects both the basket and your plant's roots.

**Will it fit my 10-inch pot?**
Maybe — measure first. The basket dimensions are listed in the product description. The most common complaint is ordering too small, so measure the diameter and height of your current pot before purchasing.

**Is this safe for outdoor use?**
Only in covered areas. Seagrass will absorb moisture and degrade if left in rain. A screened porch or covered patio is fine; open exposure is not.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want an affordable, attractive way to hide nursery pots and elevate your plant styling. The tight weave, stable base, and natural look punch well above the price point.

**Skip it if** you need a functional planter with drainage, or you expect it to survive full outdoor weather.
