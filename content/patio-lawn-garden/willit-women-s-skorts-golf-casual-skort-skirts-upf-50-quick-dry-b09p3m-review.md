**Opening Hook**

Most golf skorts fall into one of two camps: they look great but ride up on the seventh hole, or they're functional but make you look like you're wearing a diaper. The Willit Women's Skorts sit in a rare middle ground — they're genuinely comfortable, offer real sun protection, and don't look like athletic gear trying too hard to be business casual. At 4.3 stars from 215 buyers, this isn't a cult favorite, but it's a solid workhorse that's earned its rating through consistent performance rather than hype.

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes the skort itself — one piece, not two. That's the point: the outer skirt fabric (a polyester-spandex blend with a smooth, almost silky handfeel) is paired with built-in compression shorts underneath. The shorts have a silicone gripper at the hem to keep them from riding up, and there's a discreet pocket on the right thigh that fits a phone snugly. No separate liner to deal with, no extra pieces to lose.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The UPF 50+ rating is legit. The fabric is tightly woven enough that sunlight doesn't penetrate, and after several washes (cold water, hang dry, as recommended), the protection hasn't degraded. The quick-dry claim holds up too — a post-round dunk in the clubhouse sink and a wring-out leaves it wearable in about 20 minutes, though you'll still feel dampness if you sit in it immediately.

The waistband deserves specific mention. It's a wide, flat elastic band with a drawstring — not the flimsy cord that digs in, but a flat, ribbon-style tie that actually stays put. That said, multiple buyers note the waist runs snug. As one reviewer put it: "I'm normally a medium in everything, but the medium pinched my waist after an hour." If you're between sizes, size up.

The built-in shorts are where this product shines or fails depending on your body type. For most buyers, the compression fit prevents chafing during a full 18 holes. But the inseam is inconsistent — some colorways measure closer to 3.5 inches than the advertised 4 inches, which means thigh coverage is tighter than expected. Shorter women (under 5'4") may find this fine; taller women may want to size up for extra length.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The phone pocket is the most-cited feature across reviews. It's not a "just fits a card" pocket — it swallows an iPhone Pro Max without bulging. One reviewer noted: "Finally a skort where my phone doesn't fly out on the downswing." The fabric's wrinkle resistance also gets consistent shoutouts — it looks presentable straight out of a golf bag, which matters if you're heading to the clubhouse after a round.

**Where It Falls Short**

Beyond sizing and inseam inconsistencies, the color options are a real limitation. The standard size range offers about a dozen shades, but the plus-size range (1X-3X) cuts that down to six or seven, and several popular neutrals like olive and burgundy disappear. If you're shopping in larger sizes, you may feel like you're getting second-class color choices.

The fabric also shows sweat marks more than expected for a "performance" fabric. On darker colors it's fine, but the lighter shades — especially the white and light gray — will betray you on a hot day. This isn't a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you pick a color.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Golfers who walk the course** — the built-in shorts and gripper hem mean no adjustments mid-round.
- **Sun-sensitive players** — the UPF 50+ rating is real and doesn't wash out.
- **Travelers** — this packs flat, resists wrinkles, and can double as casual wear for dinner after the round.
- **Skippers:** Anyone between sizes (the waistband doesn't forgive), women over 5'7" looking for longer inseams, or plus-size shoppers who want the full color range.

Yes — the right thigh pocket fits most modern smartphones up to 6.7 inches, including an iPhone Pro Max, without visible bulging.

**Q: How does the sizing run?**

**Skip it if:** You're tall with long legs, you're between sizes and can't afford a return, or you need specific colors in plus sizes that aren't offered.
