The Gilmour Flexogen 5/8 x 50 ft is the hose you buy when you're done replacing hoses every spring. It's not the cheapest option on the shelf, and it's not the lightest, but it's built to survive the abuse that kills standard vinyl hoses within a season or two. With a 4.2-star average across 210 ratings, the consensus is clear: this is a workhorse, not a throwaway.

**What You Actually Get**

Out of the box, you get a 50-foot, 5/8-inch hose with a durable outer jacket and a full-flow brass coupling system. The fittings are the standout here — Gilmour uses crush-proof brass connectors that won't crack when you tighten them onto a spigot or drag the hose across a driveway. The hose itself weighs about 7.5 pounds empty, which is noticeable but manageable. It also comes with a limited lifetime warranty, which tells you the manufacturer expects this to last.

**Real-World Performance**

The Flexogen's claim to fame is its cold-weather flexibility. Unlike PVC hoses that turn into rigid, uncooperative snakes when temperatures drop, this hose stays supple. One Amazon reviewer noted, "I've left it outside overnight in 40-degree weather and it still coils up easily without kinking." That's a genuine advantage if you do any fall or early spring watering.

Water flow is solid. The 5/8-inch interior diameter with full-flow fittings means you won't see a dramatic pressure drop at the nozzle end of 50 feet. For typical residential use — filling a bucket, running a sprinkler, washing the car — it delivers consistent performance.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The most common theme in positive reviews is durability. One buyer wrote, "I've had this hose for three years and it still looks and works like new. My last hose lasted one summer." Another praised the fittings specifically: "The brass ends are solid — no leaks at the connection, which is rare in my experience."

The kink resistance also gets steady mentions. While no hose is completely kink-proof, the Flexogen's construction minimizes the frustrating mid-watering kinks that stop flow dead.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. The outer jacket scuffs easily. If you drag this hose across concrete, gravel, or brick, you'll see white marks and wear on the surface. It's cosmetic — it doesn't affect function — but if you care about appearance, this will bug you.

Also, this is a heavy hose when full of water. At 7.5 pounds empty, a filled 50-footer is a workout to drag around a large yard. If you have mobility issues or a massive property, you might prefer a lighter polymer hose, though you'll sacrifice durability.

A few reviewers mentioned the hose has a "chemical" smell when new. It dissipates after a few uses, but it's noticeable on day one.

**Who Should Buy This**

This hose is for the homeowner who waters a medium-to-large yard, washes vehicles, or runs sprinklers regularly — and who is tired of buying a new hose every year. It's also a solid pick for anyone in cooler climates who needs a hose that won't stiffen up in fall or early spring.

Skip it if you need a lightweight hose for occasional light duty on a small patio, or if you're on a tight budget and don't mind replacing cheap hoses every couple of seasons.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this hose kink?**
A: It kinks far less than standard vinyl hoses, but it's not 100% kink-proof. Sharp bends around corners can still cause flow restriction. The flexibility helps, but don't expect miracles.

**Q: Is the 5/8-inch size right for my needs?**
A: For most residential uses — sprinklers, nozzles, filling tanks — yes. The 5/8-inch diameter delivers good volume without the heaviness of a 3/4-inch hose. If you have very low water pressure, a 1/2-inch might actually perform better.

**Q: Will the brass fittings fit my standard spigot?**
A: Yes. The couplings are standard garden hose thread (GHT) and fit all standard outdoor spigots and attachments.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a durable, cold-weather-flexible hose that will outlast the cheap ones by years, and you don't mind the extra weight.

**Skip it if** you need something ultralight for small spaces or you're shopping on a strict budget.
