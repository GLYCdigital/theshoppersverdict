If you're tired of yanking a pull cord and mixing fuel for a pressure washer that weighs more than your lawnmower, the Greenworks Pro Brushless deserves a close look. This isn't another flimsy electric toy — it's a serious cleaning tool that takes on patio grime, driveway oil spots, and siding mildew without the gas-engine drama. With a 4.1-star average from 204 ratings, it's clear shoppers are finding real value here, but let's dig into whether it's the right pick for your specific cleaning needs.

**What You Actually Get**

The box includes the pressure washer unit, a spray gun, a 35-foot high-pressure hose, a 20-foot power cord, and a 5-in-1 quick-connect nozzle tip (0°, 15°, 25°, 40°, and soap application). The unit itself is compact — roughly the size of a small cooler — with a built-in handle and 10-inch wheels that roll smoothly over grass and gravel. The brushless motor is the headline feature here, and it's not just marketing fluff. Unlike brushed motors that lose power over time, this one maintains consistent pressure without carbon brushes wearing down.

**Real-World Performance**

Out on the driveway, this unit puts out up to 2,300 PSI and 1.1 GPM — enough to strip years of grime from concrete and blast through caked-on mud on lawn equipment. The brushless motor's advantage shows in the consistency: pressure stays steady even during extended use, something budget electric units struggle with. One Amazon reviewer noted, "I cleaned my entire two-car driveway and the power never wavered once. My old electric started fading after ten minutes." That's the brushless difference.

The trigger-controlled start/stop is a quality-of-life feature you'll appreciate immediately. Release the trigger, the motor shuts off. Squeeze it, and you're back at full power instantly. This saves electricity, extends motor life, and means you're not standing there with a running motor while repositioning your ladder. The 35-foot hose gives you solid reach without moving the unit constantly — I covered most of a two-story home's ground floor without relocating.

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

The quiet operation is a recurring theme in customer feedback. At roughly 62 decibels, this runs about half the volume of a typical gas pressure washer. One reviewer put it simply: "I can actually hear my neighbor talking to me while I'm cleaning. That never happened with my gas unit." The zero-maintenance aspect also gets consistent love — no oil changes, no fuel stabilizer, no carburetor cleaning at the start of each season.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about limitations. The 20-foot power cord is genuinely short for any real work. You'll need a 12-gauge extension cord (50 feet minimum) for most driveways, and that's an extra $40-50 you should budget for. The PSI is respectable but not gas-class — if you're trying to remove years of set-in oil stains from old concrete or strip multiple layers of paint, a 3,200 PSI gas unit will do it faster. And while the wheels are handy, the unit's center of gravity is high enough that pulling it over curbs takes a little effort.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is ideal for homeowners with a standard suburban lot: patios, driveways, siding, decks, fences, and vehicles. It's perfect if you hate gas-engine maintenance or live in a neighborhood with noise restrictions. It's also great for anyone who uses a pressure washer sporadically — the brushless motor doesn't degrade from sitting idle. Skip it if you're a contractor needing daily heavy-duty use, or if you have a large property (½ acre plus) where the cord length becomes a real constraint.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this to wash my car safely?**
A: Yes, with the 25° or 40° nozzle and the included soap tip. The lower pressure settings are gentle enough for automotive paint.

**Q: Is the 35-foot hose enough for most jobs?**
A: For most residential work, yes — you'll cover a standard driveway and two-story front of a house. For larger areas, you'll reposition the unit a couple times.

**Q: Does it come with everything to start cleaning, or do I need extra parts?**
A: Everything's included except an extension cord. The unit, wand, hose, and all five nozzle tips are in the box.

**The Verdict**

The Greenworks Pro Brushless earns its 4.2/5 rating. It genuinely delivers on the core promises: consistent power, quiet operation, and no maintenance headaches. The cord length is the biggest practical annoyance, but it's a solvable problem with a quality extension cord. For the typical homeowner who wants dependable cleaning power without the gas-engine ritual, this is a smart buy that'll serve you for years.

**Buy it if:** You want gas-washer performance without gas-washer maintenance, or you're replacing a dying electric unit and want one that'll actually last.

**Skip it if:** You need max PSI for commercial work, or your property is large enough that cord management becomes a constant battle.
