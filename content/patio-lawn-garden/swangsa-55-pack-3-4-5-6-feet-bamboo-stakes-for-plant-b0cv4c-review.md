**FAQ**

**Are these sturdy enough for 6-foot tomato cages?**
These replace cages, not supplement them. The 6-footers are sturdy enough for single-staking indeterminate varieties if you tie every 12 inches. For heavy fruiters, use two stakes per plant or expect some bowing.

**Do they need treatment before going in the ground?**
No. The bamboo is naturally pest-resistant and will last 1-2 seasons. If you want longer life, a quick coat of boiled linseed oil before planting adds a season, but it's not required.

**How do they compare to metal or fiberglass stakes?**
Bamboo is cheaper, lighter, and compostable, but it won't last as long as fiberglass (5+ years) or metal (indefinite). For most home gardens, the two-season lifespan is perfectly fine, especially at this price per stake.
