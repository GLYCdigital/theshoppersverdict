**What You're Actually Getting**

This is a liquid soil conditioner concentrate, not a fertilizer. It's designed to break the surface tension of compacted soil particles, allowing water, air, and nutrients to penetrate deeper. The product image shows a straightforward concentrate bottle — you'll need your own sprayer, whether a hose-end attachment for big lawns or a pump sprayer for targeted beds.

The key application method: mix it, saturate the soil, and let the biology do the work. It's meant to be used either solo as a liquid aeration treatment, or combined with mechanical aeration for maximum effect.

**Real-World Performance**

Here's where the honest picture comes in. This isn't a "pour and watch the soil crumble" product. It's a biological soil amendment that needs time and moisture to activate. Most users report visible improvement in 2-3 weeks — soil that accepts water instead of shedding it, grass roots that dig deeper, and less standing water after rain.
