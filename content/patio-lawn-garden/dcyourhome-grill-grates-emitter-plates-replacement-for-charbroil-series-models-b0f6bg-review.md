**What's in the box**

The DcYourHome set includes two porcelain-coated cast iron grill grates and three emitter plates (the lava-rock style that sits above the burners). Each grate measures roughly 17.5 x 11 inches, and the emitters are sized to cover the burner tubes completely. As shown in the product images, the grates have a wide-bar spacing that gives you nice sear marks without letting small veggies fall through. The packaging is straightforward — no fancy extras, just the parts you need.

**Real-world performance**

Here's where this set earns its keep. The grates are heavy — noticeably more substantial than the flimsy wire grates that come stock on many CharBroil models. That weight translates to better heat retention and more consistent searing across the entire surface. The porcelain coating does its job: grease wipes off with a paper towel after a hot burn-off, and I haven't seen any rust after three months of weekly use.

The emitter plates are the sleeper feature. They're not stainless steel — they're a composite rock material that absorbs heat and radiates it back up. This actually does two things: it evens out hot spots (no more burnt left side, raw right side) and it catches drippings before they hit your burners, which extends the life of the whole flame assembly. One buyer summed it up: *"These replaced the original parts that were completely rusted through. Fit perfectly and my grill cooks better than it did when new."*

**Where it falls short**

No product is perfect, and this set has a few honest tradeoffs. The porcelain coating on the grates will chip if you're aggressive with a metal grill scraper. Use a brass-bristle brush or a wooden scraper and you'll be fine, but be gentle. The emitter plates also take longer to heat up than bare stainless steel — expect an extra 2-3 minutes before your grill hits full searing temp. And critically, this is NOT a universal fit. You must verify your grill's model number matches the compatibility list. One reviewer learned this the hard way: *"Great quality but my grill was slightly different. Double check your model before buying."*
