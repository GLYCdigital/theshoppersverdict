**The UIRWAY 24 Ft Round Pool Cover: A Budget Cover That Asks You to Lower Your Expectations**

Let's be blunt: a 2.7-star average across 150 ratings is not a product that's quietly exceeding expectations. It's a product with a vocal group of frustrated owners. But it's also selling steadily, which means it's cheap and it shows up. The real question is whether "cheap and functional for one season" is enough for your pool.

**What You Actually Get**

Out of the box, you get the cover itself, a perimeter cable, a winch ratchet, and ground stakes. The material is a woven polyethylene that feels like a heavy-duty tarp — because that's essentially what it is. The reflective strips run in a cross-pattern across the surface, which is a genuinely thoughtful safety addition for anyone with kids or pets nearby at dusk.

Installation is the highlight. The cable threads through the reinforced hem, and the winch cinches it tight against the pool wall. Most owners report a 20- to 30-minute setup. The included stakes are basic but adequate for soft ground.

**Real-World Performance: Where It Works and Where It Doesn't**

The reflective strips deserve credit. Multiple reviewers specifically mention that the cover is visible at night in a way most budget covers aren't. One owner wrote: *"The strips caught my porch light and I could see the pool edge clearly — my old cover was invisible after dark."* That's a legit safety win.

The problems start with the material. At roughly 0.3mm thickness, this is a debris cover, not a winter cover. Owners in milder climates report it surviving a season of leaves and light snow. Owners in colder regions tell a different story. One reviewer summarized it: *"The grommets tore out by December. The cable just ripped straight through the fabric."* That's a structural failure, not cosmetic wear.

Water pooling is the second recurring complaint. The cover lacks sufficient dome or drainage design — water collects in low spots, adds weight, and accelerates the tearing issue. You'll want to manually pump off standing water after heavy rain, which defeats the purpose of a set-and-forget cover.

Sizing is the third wildcard. Several 24-ft buyers report the actual measurement coming up short by 2–4 inches. That might not sound like much, but on a round pool, it means the cable can't seat properly and the cover pulls unevenly.

**Who Should Buy This**

This cover makes sense for three specific buyers:
- Someone with a smaller budget who needs a one-season leaf barrier and will replace it annually
- Pool owners in mild climates (no snow load, minimal freezing) who just want to keep crud out during fall
- DIY installers who value the easy cable-and-winch setup over long-term durability

It's a poor fit for:
- Anyone expecting a multi-winter cover — this will not survive harsh conditions
- Owners with trees overhead who need a cover that sheds water aggressively
- Perfectionists who will be irritated by a 2-inch sizing shortfall

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Will this cover support snow?**
No. The fabric and grommets are not rated for snow load. Light dusting, maybe. Anything over a few inches, you're risking tears. Pump off standing water regularly.

**Q: Is it safe for pets or kids to walk on?**
No. This is not a safety cover. It will not support human or animal weight. The reflective strips help people *see* the pool, not walk across it.

**Q: Does the 24-ft size fit true?**
Measure your pool first. Several owners report the cover running short by 2–4 inches. If your pool is exactly 24 ft, consider ordering up a size if available.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a cheap, easy-to-install debris cover for a single mild season and you'll treat it as disposable. The reflective strips are a genuine safety plus, and the installation is painless.

**Skip it if** you want a cover that lasts multiple winters, sheds water without babysitting, or fits precisely. At this rating and with these consistent failure points, you'll likely be shopping for a replacement — or a better brand — by spring.

**Rating: 3.3/5** — It works, barely, for what it costs. Set your expectations accordingly and you won't be blindsided.
