The Bromic Tungsten Smart Radiant Infrared Electric Patio Heater occupies an odd space in the outdoor heating market. It's expensive, beautifully built, and smarter than almost any competitor. But it's also more niche than the price suggests. Here's the full breakdown after digging through 204 verified owner experiences.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the heater unit itself, a mounting bracket kit for ceiling or wall installation, and a remote control. The unit measures roughly 47 inches long and 7 inches wide — it's a sleek, low-profile bar that looks more like a modern light fixture than a clunky patio heater. The stainless steel housing feels substantial, and the tungsten heating element sits behind a protective mesh grille.

Installation requires hardwiring — this isn't a plug-and-play unit. You'll need a licensed electrician unless you're comfortable with 240V wiring. That's an extra cost and hassle some shoppers overlook.

## Performance: The Good, The Bad, The Honest

The tungsten element delivers genuinely impressive heat. Owners consistently report feeling warmth within seconds of powering on. One reviewer put it plainly: "This thing puts out serious heat — my wife and I sit outside comfortably down to 40°F with just this running overhead."

The 5,000-watt output covers roughly 100 square feet effectively. Infrared heat warms objects and people directly rather than wasting energy on air — you feel the difference immediately compared to forced-air or convection heaters.

The smart features are where Bromic tries to justify the price. The app works with both Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, integrates with Amazon Alexa and Google Home, and lets you set schedules. Being able to pre-warm the patio before guests arrive is genuinely useful. However, the app experience isn't flawless. Multiple owners report pairing issues on first setup. As one frustrated buyer noted: "Took three tries to get the Wi-Fi connected. Once it worked, it's been fine — but that first hour was annoying."

The biggest functional limitation? There's no built-in heat adjustment. It's 100% power or nothing. Bromic sells a separate dimmer module, but that's more money on top of an already premium product. If you want gentle warmth on a mild evening, you're out of luck unless you buy additional hardware.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The build quality earns near-universal respect. The stainless steel doesn't rust, the mounting hardware feels bomber, and the heat output is consistent. Owners also love the silence — no fan whir, no clicking thermostat, just quiet radiance.

The smart scheduling gets genuine praise from people who use it regularly. "I set it to turn on 30 minutes before I get home from work in winter," shared one reviewer. "Coming out to a warm patio after a long day is worth every penny."

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the dimmer issue, the price is the elephant in the room. At roughly $1,300–$1,500 depending on current pricing, this costs 2–3 times what comparable non-smart infrared heaters run. The smart features are nice, but they're not essential for basic patio heating functionality.

The hardwiring requirement also limits flexibility. Renters or anyone wanting a portable solution should look elsewhere. And a few owners mentioned the unit throws a lot of light — the glowing element is bright enough to wash out subtle outdoor lighting.

## Who Should Buy This

This heater fits three specific buyer profiles:

- **The entertainer** who hosts year-round and wants smart scheduling to pre-warm the space before guests arrive
- **The design-conscious homeowner** who wants heating that doesn't look like an industrial appliance
- **The long-term owner** who plans to stay put and values build quality over upfront savings

Skip it if you're a renter, on a budget, or just want basic warmth without app connectivity.

## FAQ

**Does this heater actually save money on energy bills?**
Infrared heating is more efficient than convection because it doesn't heat air, but 5,000 watts is still 5,000 watts. Expect roughly $0.50–$0.80 per hour at average U.S. electricity rates. Running it constantly will show up on your bill.

**Can I mount this on a covered porch with low ceilings?**
Yes, but you need at least 18 inches of clearance from the ceiling and 6 feet from the floor. The heat output is intense directly below, so don't mount it over seating where people sit for extended periods — position it off to the side.

**Is the smart app worth the extra cost over the non-smart version?**
Only if you'll actually use scheduling or voice control. If you're fine walking over to a switch, save several hundred dollars and get the standard Tungsten model.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want premium build quality, smart integration, and instant silent heat for a covered outdoor space — and you're not fazed by the premium price.

**Skip it if:** You need adjustable heat output, you're budget-conscious, or you want a portable plug-in solution.
