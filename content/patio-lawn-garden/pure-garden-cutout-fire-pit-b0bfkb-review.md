Let's be blunt: most fire pits under $100 are disposable junk that rusts out in a season. The Pure Garden Cutout Fire Pit, with its 4.4-star average across 220 ratings, claims to be different. Is it? Partially — with some caveats you should know before checkout.

**What You Actually Get**

This is a 22-inch diameter bowl sitting on four stable legs, bringing the burn surface to roughly hip height. The star-and-circle cutout pattern around the sides isn't just decorative — it throws warm, patterned light across your patio while containing stray embers. The package includes three things most competitors make you buy separately: a mesh spark screen with a lift handle, a 20-inch steel poker for adjusting logs, and a fitted weather cover. That's genuinely good value for the price point.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The steel is thin — about 1mm thick, which is standard at this price but worth flagging. On the first burn, you'll notice the paint curing and possibly smoking slightly; that's normal, burn it off before entertaining. The cutout design actually improves airflow compared to solid-wall pits, so you get a hotter, cleaner burn with less smoke. Multiple buyers noted this: *"The cutouts make it light up beautifully at night — my neighbors asked where I got it."* That's the main selling point, and it delivers.

Heat output is adequate for two to four people sitting close. It won't replace a proper 30-inch steel pit for a big gathering, but for a couple's evening on the patio, it's plenty. The legs have small foot pads that keep it from sinking into grass, though they're welded on — if one breaks, the whole pit is compromised.

**Where It Falls Short**

The 22-inch bowl sounds roomy but isn't. Standard 16-inch firewood logs won't fit without splitting them down. You'll either buy smaller bundles or invest in a maul. The thin steel also means heat discoloration is permanent — expect the bottom to turn a bronze/brown after three or four fires. That's cosmetic, not structural.

More concerning: rust. If you live in a humid climate or leave it uncovered, this pit will show surface rust within months. The included cover helps, but it's not waterproof — just a dust shield. Several reviewers mentioned this: *"Loved it for six months, then the bottom rusted through."* That's the reality of budget steel. Two-season lifespan is realistic for most owners.

The spark screen is functional but lightweight — it sits on the rim without clips, so a strong gust can knock it off. Not ideal if you're burning while a breeze picks up.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Patio decorators**: If the ambient light pattern is your priority, this is one of the best-looking options under $100.
- **Casual users**: Two to three fires a month during summer, stored in a garage when not in use — you'll get good value.
- **First-time buyers**: It's an affordable entry point to figure out if you actually use a fire pit before investing $300+.

**Skip it if** you burn weekly year-round, want to burn full-size logs, or live in a coastal/high-humidity area without covered storage.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Can I burn regular firewood in this pit?**
Split wood into 12-inch pieces or shorter. Standard 16-inch logs will hang over the edge.

**Q: Does the paint peel off?**
The high-heat paint will discolor and may chip near the rim after repeated burns. This is cosmetic and doesn't affect function.

**Q: How many people can sit around it comfortably?**
Realistically, two to four. The heat radius is tight, and the bowl is small enough that anyone beyond arm's reach won't feel much warmth.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you value the decorative light pattern, want an all-inclusive package (screen, poker, cover included), and don't mind a shorter lifespan for a lower price.

**Skip it if** you need a heavy-duty pit for frequent use, want to burn uncut logs, or can't store it out of the elements.

This is a solid 4.2/5 — a good-looking, functional pit that earns its price tag, provided you're honest about how often you'll actually use it. For weekend ambiance over a cold beer, it's hard to beat. For year-round workhorse duty, save your money and buy steel twice as thick.
