Let me be direct: if you're shopping for a cordless pool vacuum in this price range, you're weighing convenience against cleaning power. The VIVOHOME Cordless Pool Vacuum with 18.5 GPM suction and 60-minute runtime sits right in that tradeoff zone. Here's what 190 Amazon reviewers (averaging 3.8 stars) actually experienced — and what you should expect.

## What You Actually Get

The box includes the vacuum head, an extendable pole (which telescopes to reach most pool floors without you bending over), a filter basket, and the charging adapter. The unit weighs around 5 pounds, so it's manageable to maneuver even when the filter is full of wet debris. As shown in the product image, the head is wide — roughly 12 inches — which means fewer passes across your pool floor.

## Real-World Performance

The headline numbers matter: 18.5 gallons per minute of suction and a 60-minute battery. In practice, that suction translates to genuine pickup power. Multiple reviewers note it grabs fine sand and silt that cheaper cordless units just blow around. One buyer put it simply: "It picked up debris my old vacuum left behind." The wide mouth and rubber squeegee edge help it grab leaves and twigs without pushing them ahead of the head.

Battery life is honest — you'll get close to the full hour on a clean filter. But here's the catch: **the filter basket is small**. On a pool with heavy debris, you'll stop every 10-15 minutes to rinse it out. That's not a dealbreaker, but it's a rhythm you should expect. Several reviewers mentioned this exact frustration: "Great suction, but I emptied the basket four times in one cleaning session."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The cordless freedom is the top recurring theme. No hoses to prime, no connecting to your skimmer, no wrestling with a 40-foot hose that kinks. You charge it, drop it in, and push it around. Reviewers also consistently call out the lightweight design and easy assembly — most had it running within five minutes of unboxing.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about limitations. **This vacuum does not climb walls.** It's strictly a floor cleaner for above-ground pools. If you have an in-ground pool with vinyl liner steps or a gradual slope, it'll handle the floor but you're on your own for the walls.

The battery is the bigger long-term concern. A handful of reviewers report noticeable runtime drop after 6-8 months of weekly use. That's not unusual for cordless pool gear, but it's worth knowing the 60-minute figure is a "new battery" number.

Also, the included filter is fine for standard debris, but ultra-fine algae dust can pass through. You'll want to run your regular filtration system afterward for polishing.

## Who Should Buy This

This is the right tool for **above-ground pool owners** (up to about 24-foot round) who want a quick, no-hassle way to clean the floor without hooking up hoses. It's also a solid pick if you have mobility issues — the self-propelled push is light and the pole reduces bending.

Skip it if you have an in-ground pool with significant wall algae, a large pool over 30 feet, or you expect a hands-free robotic experience. This is a manual push vacuum, not a robot.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does the battery actually last?**
A: New, expect 50-60 minutes on a clean filter with normal debris. Heavier pickup loads drain it faster, and the filter clogging reduces runtime as suction drops.

**Q: Will it work on my in-ground pool?**
A: It'll clean the floor, but it won't climb walls and the pole length is optimized for above-ground depths. Stick to above-ground or very shallow in-ground pools.

**Q: How often do I need to clean the filter?**
A: On a typical weekly clean, 2-4 times per session. On heavy leaf/debris days, expect more frequent stops.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You own an above-ground pool, want cordless convenience, and don't mind stopping to rinse the filter a few times per clean.

**Skip it if:** You need wall climbing, have a large in-ground pool, or want a set-and-forget robotic cleaner.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — The VIVOHOME delivers strong suction and genuine cordless freedom at a fair price. The small filter and battery longevity concerns keep it from greatness, but for the right pool owner, it's a genuinely useful tool that beats wrestling with hoses every single time.
