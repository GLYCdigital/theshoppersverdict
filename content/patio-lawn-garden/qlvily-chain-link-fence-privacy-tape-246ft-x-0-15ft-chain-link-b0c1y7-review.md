The Qlvily Chain Link Fence Privacy Tape promises a cheap fix: 246 feet of woven polyethylene slats that thread through your diamond mesh and block prying eyes. ## What You Actually Get

One continuous roll of 246-foot tape, 1.8 inches wide (0.15ft). That's the headline number. For a standard 6-foot privacy fence with 1.75-inch diamond mesh, you'll cover roughly 35–40 linear feet of fence with a single roll — think one long side yard, not a full backyard. The material is high-density polyethylene, woven like a flat ribbon. It's flexible, UV-stabilized (on paper), and designed to weave over-under through the diamond links.

As shown in the product image, the tape comes in a tight spool. The weave pattern is uniform, and the edges are clean — no fraying out of the box. You'll need scissors or a utility knife to cut strips, plus about 90 minutes of patience for a standard install. No tools beyond that.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the rubber meets the road. The tape does its primary job: it blocks sightlines. Once woven through, you get roughly 90% opacity. Neighbors can't see your patio, and your dog stops barking at every passerby. Wind resistance is decent — the tape is flexible enough to bend without snapping, and the weave holds even in 20-30 mph gusts, based on multiple buyer reports.

Installation is the grind. The tape arrives tightly coiled, so it wants to curl back on itself. You'll fight it for the first 10–15 strips until it relaxes. One reviewer put it plainly: "Took me two afternoons to do a 50-foot fence. The tape itself is fine, but the coiling makes it a wrestling match." Plan for that. If you're doing a full 100-foot perimeter, budget a full weekend.

The bigger concern is longevity. Buyers consistently report color fade after one summer of direct sun. A reviewer noted, "By August, the green looked washed out — more sage than forest." That's not a dealbreaker for privacy, but if you care about curb appeal, factor in replacement every 2–3 seasons.

## Where It Falls Short

- **Fade is real**: UV stabilizers help, but direct southern exposure will lighten the color noticeably within 12 months. Not a functional failure, but a cosmetic one.
- **Installation friction**: The tight coiling is the #1 complaint in lower-rated reviews. It's manageable, but if you're not handy, this could test your patience.
- **Not fully opaque**: Up close, you'll see slivers of light between weaves. At 10 feet away, it's private. Right against the fence, not quite. Manage expectations.

**Q: Can I install this without removing the existing fence?**
