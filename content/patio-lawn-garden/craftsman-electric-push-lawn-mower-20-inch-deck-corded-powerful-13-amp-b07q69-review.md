**Bottom line up front:** If you've got a quarter-acre or less and don't want to deal with gas, oil changes, or battery charging anxiety, the CRAFTSMAN 20-inch corded mower is a solid workhorse that punches above its price class—provided you're honest about cord management.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the mower with a 13-amp motor, a 20-inch stamped steel deck, a 10-gallon hybrid grass bag, and a 50-foot 10-gauge power cord. Assembly takes about 15 minutes: fold out the handle, snap on the bag, plug in. No tools required beyond the included wrench for the handle knobs. The deck has three cutting options—rear bag, side discharge (chute included), and mulching (plug included)—so you're not locked into one mowing style.

## Real-World Performance

That 13-amp motor is the star here. In my testing on a mix of Bermuda and fescue, it chewed through 4-inch grass without the RPM drop you get on cheaper 11-amp units. The 20-inch deck is noticeably wider than the 14–17-inch decks on most corded mowers, which translates to roughly 20% fewer passes on a typical suburban lot. The single-lever height adjustment is genuinely tool-free: pull the lever, slide to one of seven positions between 1.5 and 4 inches, and you're done. No wrestling with individual wheel pins.

The 10-gallon bag fills evenly in practice and empties cleanly—the hybrid fabric/plastic construction doesn't clog like pure mesh bags. Buyers on Amazon consistently mention this. One verified reviewer wrote, *"Bag holds a lot more than I expected, and it doesn't clog even with slightly damp grass."* Another noted, *"The 20-inch deck is a huge upgrade over my old 14-inch mower. Cut my mowing time by almost half."*

## Where It Falls Short

Cord management is the elephant in the room. The 50-foot cord is heavy, and the rear-mounted cord retainer is just a hook—the cord drags along the ground behind you, which means you'll occasionally run over it if you're not careful. Plan on using a cord lock at the outlet and mowing in a pattern that keeps the cord behind you. It's manageable, but it's the #1 complaint in negative reviews. One buyer put it bluntly: *"The cord hook is useless. I ended up zip-tying the cord to the handle after the first mow."*

Mulching is serviceable but not impressive. On dry, short grass you'll get decent clipping distribution, but in anything taller than 3 inches, you'll see clumps. Use the bag or side discharge for those cuts. Also, this is a push mower—no self-propelled option. On flat, small-to-medium yards that's fine, but if your property has any real slope, you'll feel it in your shoulders.

## Who Should Buy This

This mower is ideal for the **corded-mower convert**—someone who wants electric's low maintenance and instant start but hates the runtime limits of batteries. It's also great for **budget-conscious homeowners** with lots under 0.25 acres who want a wide deck without paying $400+ for a battery model. If you have a perfectly flat yard and don't mind a cord, this is arguably the best value in its class. Skip it if you have a large or sloped yard, or if you despise cord management—you'll be better served by a self-propelled gas or battery mower.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this mower handle thick or wet grass?**
A: Yes, the 13-amp motor handles damp, thick grass well—better than most corded competitors. Just don't bag when it's soaking wet; the bag will still fill, but clumping increases. Use side discharge for the wettest cuts.

**Q: How long is the power cord, and can I extend it?**
A: It ships with a 50-foot 10-gauge cord. You can use a heavier-gauge extension cord (12-gauge minimum) if you need more reach, but avoid daisy-chaining thin cords—voltage drop will reduce cutting power.

**Q: Does the deck rust or corrode over time?**
A: The deck is stamped steel, not aluminum. It's painted and should hold up if you store it dry, but a few reviewers report surface rust after a season or two of outdoor storage. Keep it in a shed or garage, and wipe down after mowing wet grass.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a powerful, wide-deck corded mower for a small-to-medium flat yard and don't mind managing a cord. The 13-amp motor and 20-inch deck deliver genuine value at this price point.

**Skip it if:** You have a sloped property, a yard over 0.3 acres, or you'd rather pay more for the convenience of battery power or self-propulsion.
