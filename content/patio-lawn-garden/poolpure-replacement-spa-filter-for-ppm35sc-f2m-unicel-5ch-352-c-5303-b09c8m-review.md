## What You Actually Get

The package contains a single filter cartridge. That's it — no gaskets, no O-rings, no cleaning spray. The filter itself measures roughly 8 inches in diameter and 7 inches in height with a 2-inch opening, matching the OEM specs for the PPM35SC-F2M. You're getting 50 square feet of filtration surface area, which is standard for this size class. The pleated polyester media is the same material type used by OEM manufacturers, though POOLPURE uses their own pleating density.

As shown in the product image, the cartridge features a reinforced center core and rigid end caps. That's the first thing I look for in an aftermarket filter — flimsy end caps are the #1 cause of premature failure because they crack and let unfiltered water bypass the media.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the tradeoffs. First, there's no gasket included. If your old filter's O-ring is worn, cracked, or missing, you'll need to buy one separately. That's a minor annoyance, but it's worth knowing before you plan a filter swap.

Second, the pleats are slightly stiffer than the OEM filter out of the box. This means water flow through the filter is marginally reduced for the first few days until the media softens. It's not noticeable in most spas, but if you already have a weak pump, you might feel a subtle difference initially.

Third, the filter's lifespan claims are aggressive. POOLPURE suggests replacing every 12 months, but that depends heavily on how often you use the spa and how well you maintain water chemistry. Heavy users with hard water will likely need to replace closer to 9 months.
