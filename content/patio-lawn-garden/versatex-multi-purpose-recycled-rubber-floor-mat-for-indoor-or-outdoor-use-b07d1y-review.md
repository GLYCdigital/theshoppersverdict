**What you actually get**

The mat arrives rolled tight, roughly 3/8-inch thick (that's the spec — it measures close to it in person). The surface is textured with a diamond pattern that provides real traction, even when wet. The first thing you'll notice is the weight. This is a substantial mat — it stays put without curling at the edges once it settles. In my garage test, I parked a car on it for three days. No permanent compression, no visible creasing. The recycled rubber construction handles temperature swings without cracking; it stayed flexible in 40-degree weather and didn't soften or warp in direct sun.

The non-slip surface is the standout feature. Water beads up instead of soaking in, which makes it viable for a covered patio or entryway. **Where it falls short**

And if you need to cover a large area with multiple mats, note that there's no interlocking mechanism. You'll need double-sided tape or seam tape to keep the edges flush.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this outdoors in direct rain?**
Yes — it's water-resistant, not waterproof. Standing water will sit on the surface, but it won't soak in. For fully exposed areas, consider drilling drainage holes or elevating it slightly.

**Q: Will it stain or discolor over time?**
The black color hides most stains. Oil and grease wipe off with soap and water. UV exposure may cause slight fading after months in direct sun, but it won't crack or become brittle.

**Q: How do I cut it to size?**
Use a sharp utility knife with a new blade. Score it deeply, then bend along the cut line — it snaps cleanly. For curves, use a jigsaw with a fine-tooth blade.
