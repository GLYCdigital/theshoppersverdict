# Method All-Purpose Cleaner Spray, Ginger Yuzu: A Garden Shed Essential That Actually Earns Its Spot

Let me cut to the chase: most all-purpose cleaners marketed for outdoor use are either too weak to matter or so chemically aggressive you feel guilty spraying them near your tomato plants. The Method All-Purpose Cleaner in Ginger Yuzu sits in a rare middle ground—it's genuinely plant-based, surprisingly effective on typical patio grime, and smells like something you'd actually want in your space rather than a chemical facsimile of "citrus."

## What You're Actually Getting

The box contains a single 28-fluid-ounce spray bottle with a trigger nozzle. That's it—no dilution required, no measuring, no mixing. The formula is built around plant-derived surfactants and a biodegradable base, which matters if you're cleaning near garden beds or washing down outdoor furniture where kids and pets will be within minutes. As shown in the product image, the bottle itself is sleek and compact enough to tuck into a garden caddy or hang from a hook near the back door.

## Real-World Cleaning Performance

I put this through the paces that matter for patio and garden owners. On a powder-coated aluminum patio table with a week's worth of pollen, bird droppings, and spilled coffee, the spray cut through in a single pass with minimal scrubbing. The fine mist nozzle is a genuine standout—it covers roughly twice the surface area per pump compared to typical sprayers, meaning you're not standing there pumping ten times to cover a tabletop.

On plastic resin chairs with embedded grime, it performed admirably with a microfiber cloth. The formula doesn't leave that streaky, soapy film that many plant-based cleaners do—a common complaint I've seen in other eco-friendly options. It wipes to a clean, matte finish with no residue.

Where it hits its ceiling: baked-on grease on grill side tables and caked mud on stainless steel garden tools. For those, you'll need a degreaser or some elbow grease. That's not a knock on this product—it's an honest limitation of any everyday spray.
