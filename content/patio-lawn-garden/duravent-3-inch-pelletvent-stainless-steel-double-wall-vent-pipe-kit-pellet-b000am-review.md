## Is This the Last Pellet Vent Pipe You'll Ever Buy?

Most pellet stove venting fails in one of two ways: the inner liner corrodes from acidic condensation, or the joints leak creosote-laden exhaust into your living space. The Duravent 3-inch PelletVent Double Wall pipe attacks both problems head-on with a fully welded inner liner and a cam-lock twist system that doesn't rely on flimsy sheet metal screws. But at roughly double the cost of budget pellet pipe, you're paying for engineering that either holds up for a decade or becomes an expensive lesson in overbuying.

## What's Actually in the Box

You're getting one 3-inch diameter, double-wall pipe section. The inner liner is 22-gauge stainless steel — welded, not just crimped — which is the critical difference between this and cheaper options. The outer wall is a heavier 28-gauge stainless that gives the pipe its signature polished look. A built-in gasket sits inside the female end of the twist-lock joint, and the male end has a quarter-turn cam that seats it firmly.

This specific listing is for a single pipe section, typically 12, 24, or 36 inches depending on the variant you select. You'll need to buy the appropriate adapter, tee, cap, and wall thimble separately — the pipe alone won't connect directly to most stoves.

## Real-World Performance

The standout feature is the 1-inch clearance to combustibles rating. That means you can route this pipe within one inch of a wood-framed wall — not the 3 inches that single-wall galvanized pipe requires. For tight installations in corners or closets, that clearance advantage is what makes the premium price worth it.

The welded inner liner also solves a specific problem: condensation. When hot pellet exhaust hits a cold pipe interior, moisture forms and mixes with ash to create a mild acid. Seamed pipe leaks this mixture through the crimp. The Duravent's weld eliminates that path entirely.

One installer I spoke with summed up the twist-lock system: *"I've put up hundreds of feet of this stuff. The joints click in solid and stay put through annual cleaning. You never fight it — unless you forget to align the cam properly. Then it feels jammed until you rotate it right."*

## Where It Falls Short

The price is the obvious sticking point. At roughly $40–$60 per foot depending on the section length, a full vertical vent run can cost $300–$500 before you add fittings. Budget buyers often go with single-wall galvanized at half the cost — but they sacrifice the clearance rating and corrosion resistance.

The scuffing issue is real. The stainless outer wall is polished, which means it shows fingerprints, scratches, and tool marks. Handle sections with gloves and use a soft cloth when wiping them down. Also, the twist-lock joints can feel stiff on the first few rotations, especially if you're installing in cold weather. A light coat of silicone spray on the gasket solves this without damaging the seal.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're installing a pellet stove in a tight space where the 1-inch clearance rating saves your layout, or you want a vent system that won't corrode from condensation over 5–10 years of use. It's also the right call for outdoor vertical runs exposed to weather.

**Skip it if:** You're on a strict budget and can tolerate the 3-inch clearance requirement of single-wall pipe, or if your installation is temporary and you don't need decade-level durability.

## FAQ

**Does this pipe require screws to connect sections?**
No. The twist-lock cam joint secures each section mechanically. Some installers add one sheet metal screw at each joint for extra security, but it's not required and can actually hinder disassembly for cleaning.

**Can I use this with a wood stove instead of a pellet stove?**
No. PelletVent is rated for pellet fuel only. Wood stove exhaust reaches higher temperatures (1,100°F+ in some cases), which exceeds this pipe's rating. You'd need a Class A chimney pipe for wood.

**Is the 1-inch clearance really safe?**
Yes, that's the UL-listed clearance for this specific double-wall design. Just follow the manufacturer's installation manual exactly — the clearance applies to the pipe surface, not to the connections or fittings, which may have different requirements.
