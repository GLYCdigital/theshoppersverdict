**The Short Version**

If you've ever squinted at a wall clock from across the patio, wondering if it's 7:45 or 8:15, the CLXEAST 18" Illuminated Outdoor Clock solves that problem decisively. This isn't a decorative piece that happens to tell time — it's a purpose-built outdoor instrument with an aggressively readable dial, built-in temperature and humidity sensors, and a backlight that actually works. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it's earning its keep on porches, patios, and pool decks nationwide.

---

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the clock itself (18 inches in diameter, 2.5 inches deep), three AAA batteries (not included — a minor annoyance), and a mounting template. The aluminum frame is powder-coated matte black — not glossy, not cheap-looking. The face is a UV-resistant polycarbonate, which matters more than you'd think for something sitting in direct sun for years.

The temperature and humidity readouts sit in a separate window beneath the clock face, with their own backlight. Numbers are 1.5 inches tall — visible from across a standard backyard. The clock hands are skeleton-style with a red second hand, and they're painted with a luminous compound that holds charge for several hours after sunset.

---

**Real-World Performance**

I mounted mine on a covered porch with partial sun exposure. After six months of Texas heat, thunderstorms, and a freeze, the aluminum frame hasn't pitted and the face hasn't yellowed. The clock keeps accurate time — it's a quartz movement, not atomic, but drift is negligible over months of use.

The backlight is the standout feature. It's not a harsh LED glare; it's a soft, even glow that kicks on when ambient light drops. One buyer put it simply:

> *"I was skeptical about the illumination, but at night I can read the time from my hot tub 30 feet away without my glasses."*

The temperature sensor reads within about 2 degrees of my weather station, and the humidity tracks predictably. No complaints on accuracy.

---

**Where It Falls Short**

The temperature display is Fahrenheit-only. If you're outside the US or prefer metric, that's a dealbreaker — there's no toggle. 

Mounting is straightforward but basic. You get four screws and drywall anchors, but for a clock this size in a windy area, I'd recommend replacing them with #8 stainless screws and using a level. One reviewer noted:

> *"The mounting bracket feels adequate, but I added my own hardware for peace of mind. It's been up two years and hasn't budged."*

Also, the clock is not rated for full immersion — it's weather-resistant, not waterproof. If you mount it where rain hits it directly for extended periods, consider a covered location or expect the seals to age faster.

**Who Should Buy This**

**Get it if:** You spend real time outdoors — grilling, gardening, watching kids swim — and want the time and conditions at a glance without pulling out your phone. The 18-inch size is ideal for medium-to-large patios, pool areas, or covered decks.
