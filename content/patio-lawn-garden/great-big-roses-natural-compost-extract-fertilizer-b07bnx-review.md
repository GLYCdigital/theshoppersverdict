**Where It Falls Short**

Let me be direct about the downsides, because there are a few.

**The smell.** This is the #1 complaint in negative reviews. It smells like fresh manure for the first 24 hours after application. If you're applying near a patio or open windows, that's a real consideration. The odor does dissipate, but it's not pleasant while it lasts.

**No measuring cap.** The bottle has a simple cap, not a measuring one. You'll need your own measuring spoon. Minor, but annoying.

**It's not a complete fertilizer.** If your roses are already nutrient-deficient, this alone won't fix them. You still need a balanced rose food. Think of this as a soil amendment that *enhances* your feeding program, not a replacement for it.

**Bottle size is modest.** For larger gardens (20+ bushes), you'll need multiple bottles per season. Check the price per ounce before committing.
