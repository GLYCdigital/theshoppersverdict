**Q: Can I use this directly on indoor plants?**
Yes. It's odorless and won't attract fungus gnats if you don't overwater. Mix 20-30% into potting soil or top-dress lightly.

**Q: Will the worm eggs survive in my garden?**
They need moisture and organic matter. In established garden soil, yes — most buyers report worm activity within weeks. In bone-dry conditions or heavy clay, survival drops.

**Q: How much should I apply per square foot?**
For lawns, about 20 pounds per 100 square feet, raked in lightly. For garden beds, mix 1-2 inches into the top 6 inches before planting.
