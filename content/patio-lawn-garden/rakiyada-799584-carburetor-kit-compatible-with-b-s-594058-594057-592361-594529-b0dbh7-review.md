**What's Actually in the Box**

This isn't a rebuild kit with loose gaskets and a prayer. You get a complete, assembled carburetor — bowl, float, needle valve, and primer bulb already fitted. Also included are the mounting gaskets, an air filter, and a new fuel line. The unit is cast from zinc alloy with a plastic float bowl, matching the dimensions of the OEM B&S 594058 part exactly. As shown in the product images, the primer bulb is pre-installed, which saves you the fiddliest part of the job.

**Real-World Performance**

The fitment is the headline here. It bolts directly onto engines using the 594058, 594057, 592361, and 594529 carburetor mounts — that covers most push mowers with 140cc to 190cc Briggs engines from the last decade. Owners report it fires up on the first or second pull after installation. One reviewer noted, *"Bolted right on, primed it three times, and it started like it was new."* Another said, *"My 12-year-old mower runs smoother than it has in years."*

Fuel delivery is consistent once you set the idle screw. The included adjustment screws are pre-set from the factory, which works for most people. However, the soft metal on those screws is a known weak point — if you need to adjust the air-fuel mixture aggressively, swap in your original steel screws before you strip the heads.

**Where It Falls Short**

The gaskets are the weak link. They're functional but noticeably thinner than OEM. A handful of reviewers mentioned a slow fuel seep around the bowl after a few weeks of use. The fix is simple — use the old gasket if it's still pliable, or spend $4 on an OEM gasket set — but it's an extra step you shouldn't have to take. Also, the included air filter is basic; it'll work for a season, but plan to replace it with a quality paper filter next spring.

**Who Should Buy This**

If you're mechanically inclined and your mower is otherwise in good shape, this is a no-brainer. It's also perfect for anyone with a spare mower they're trying to resurrect without sinking serious money into it. If you're the type who wants to set it and forget it for five years without touching a wrench, spend the extra on OEM — but for everyone else, the savings are worth the minor gasket gamble.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit my Briggs & Stratton engine?**  
Check your engine's model number. If it's a 594058, 594057, 592361, or 594529, yes. It covers most 140cc–190cc push mower engines from the 2010s onward. If your model isn't listed, measure the bolt spacing — it's a standard 2-bolt pattern.

**Q: Do I need to adjust anything after installing?**  
Usually not. It comes set from the factory. If the idle is rough, turn the idle screw clockwise a quarter turn. Don't touch the mixture screws unless you know what you're doing — and if you do, use your original screws.

**Q: Is this a genuine Briggs & Stratton part?**  
No, it's aftermarket. It's built to OEM specs and works identically, but it won't carry the B&S warranty. At this price, you could buy two and still come out ahead of one dealership part.
