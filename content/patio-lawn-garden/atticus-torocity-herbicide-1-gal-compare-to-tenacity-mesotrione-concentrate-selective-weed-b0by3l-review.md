But is it actually a 1:1 replacement, or are you trading performance for savings? Here's what 210 buyers at 4.2 stars are really saying.

## What's in the jug

You're getting one gallon of concentrated mesotrione at 40% active ingredient — the exact same chemistry as Tenacity. The label claims this covers up to 4 acres at standard rates, which is a massive value if you've got a big yard or run a small lawn care side hustle. The jug itself is sturdy with a child-resistant cap, though there's no measuring device included. Buy a cheap oral syringe before this arrives.

## How it actually performs

Mesotrione works two ways: pre-emergent (stops crabgrass and foxtail seeds from germinating) and post-emergent (kills broadleaf weeds already growing). Torocity delivers both, and it's particularly effective on bentgrass, clover, and wild violet — weeds that laugh at standard 2,4-D products.

The catch is patience. One reviewer noted, "Took about 10 days to see anything happening, then another week for full kill. Don't panic if it looks like nothing's working." That's consistent with how mesotrione behaves — treated weeds turn white and bleached before collapsing. That whitening effect is actually the signal it's working, but it's alarming if you don't know what to expect.

## What buyers consistently praise

The value angle is the dominant theme. "Same active ingredient as the name brand at a fraction of the price per ounce," one verified buyer wrote. Another said, "Used it on a 2-acre fescue lawn for crabgrass prevention. Zero crabgrass this summer." Coverage estimates from the label check out — at 4-5 ounces per acre for pre-emergent, a gallon stretches a long way.

## Where it falls short

You need a non-ionic surfactant. Torocity doesn't include one, and mesotrione is notoriously finicky without it — droplets bead up and roll off waxy weed leaves, wasting product. Factor in another $10-15 for surfactant.

The other real complaint is measurement. For a small lawn (under 5,000 sq ft), you're measuring fractions of an ounce. Without a syringe or scale, you risk over- or under-applying. Several reviewers mentioned this frustration. Also, this is strictly for cool-season turf — if you have Bermuda or St. Augustine, skip it.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this on a new lawn seeding?**
Yes — mesotrione is one of the few herbicides safe for seeding. You can apply it at seeding time or just after, which is a huge advantage over other pre-emergents that block grass seed germination.

**Q: How long until I see results?**
Expect 5-14 days for visible whitening, and 2-3 weeks for full kill. Post-emergent control works faster on young weeds than mature ones.

**Q: Does it need a surfactant?**
Yes. The label requires it, and skipping it will give you streaky, uneven results. Any non-ionic surfactant at 2 teaspoons per gallon works.
