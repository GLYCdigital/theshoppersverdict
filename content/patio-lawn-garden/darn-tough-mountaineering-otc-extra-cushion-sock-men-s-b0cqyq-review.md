Let’s cut through the noise: the Darn Tough Mountaineering OTC Extra Cushion sock is the pair you’ll grab when the trail gets serious. With a 4.8-star average across 240 Amazon ratings, this isn’t a flash-in-the-pan novelty product. It’s a purpose-built hiking sock with a lifetime warranty, and for anyone logging real miles in boots, it earns its reputation.

## What You Actually Get

One pair of over-the-calf (OTC) socks, knit in Vermont from a 66% merino wool, 33% nylon, 1% spandex blend. The extra cushion runs from heel through the footbed — that’s where you need it when carrying weight. The OTC height (roughly 14–15 inches from heel) is the standout design choice here, protecting your shins and calves from boot tops and brush. The seam at the toe is flat and barely detectable, which matters more than most people realize on descents.

## Real-World Performance

I’ve tested these on rocky ridgelines and wet, cold spring mornings. The merino wool does its job: feet stayed dry when the temperature swung 20 degrees, and there was zero stink after a 10-mile day. The cushioning is substantial — think a plush running shoe, not a thin dress sock. Under a broken-in hiking boot, that extra layer absorbs shock and prevents hot spots on long downhills. The spandex content keeps the sock hugging your arch without bunching or sagging, even after hours of movement.

One thing to note: this is not a summer sock. The extra cushion traps heat. On a 75-degree day with a steep climb, my feet ran noticeably warmer than with a lighter-weight pair. If you’re a year-round hiker, you’ll want a second rotation for warm weather.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The Amazon reviews cluster around two themes. First, durability. One reviewer summed it up: *“I’ve put 200+ miles on these and they still look nearly new. My other socks are threadbare by comparison.”* Second, the warranty. Darn Tough replaces any pair that wears out — no receipt, no hassle. ## Where It Falls Short

Let’s be honest about tradeoffs. At roughly $30 per pair, this is an investment. If you’re a casual weekend hiker doing ten miles a season, you’ll get better value from a $15 pair. The extra cushion also means these are heavier and more substantial — not ideal for trail runners or ultralight packers who prioritize minimalism. And sizing runs slightly generous; if you’re between sizes, size down. A few reviewers noted the calf area felt snug if they had larger calves, so measure before buying.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these socks really last a lifetime?**
A: Darn Tough’s warranty is unconditional. If the sock wears out, they replace it. It’s not a marketing gimmick — the company has honored this for years. The socks themselves are built to withstand 1,000+ miles of use, which is why the warranty is rarely invoked.

**Q: Are these too hot for summer hiking?**
A: Yes, for most people. The extra cushion is designed for insulation and shock absorption. If you hike in 80+ degree heat, look at Darn Tough’s light or midweight options instead.

**Q: How do I wash them?**
A: Machine wash cold, tumble dry low. Avoid fabric softeners — they coat the wool and reduce its moisture-wicking ability. They dry faster than cotton, roughly overnight.
