The first thing you'll notice when you unbox these is the weight. At roughly 2.8 pounds per blade, they're heavier than the stamped OEM units on many residential Ferris decks. ## Where It Falls Short

The biggest trap here is fitment. This is not a universal 52" blade — it's specific to the 21227S/481711 and 482462 part numbers. If your Ferris or Scag deck uses a different blade, it won't bolt up. Double-check your parts diagram before ordering. Also, the finish is functional, not premium. After a month of wet grass cutting without cleaning, I saw surface rust along the sail edges. It doesn't affect cutting, but it looks rough and can accelerate edge dulling if left unchecked.

Another minor gripe: the center hole is 3.0 inches, which fits most commercial spindles, but a few older Ferris models use a 2.0-inch center hole with a separate bushing. Verify yours before you assume it fits.

## FAQ

**Will these fit my 52" Ferris with a 72-inch deck?**
No — they're strictly for 52-inch decks using part numbers 21227S or 481711. The 72-inch deck uses a longer blade entirely.

**How often should I replace these?**
For commercial use, rotate every 25-30 hours if you're cutting clean turf. If you're hitting dirt or sand, check the edge every 10 hours — a dull blade tears grass and invites disease.

**Can I sharpen these at home?**
Yes, they're standard steel. Use a bench grinder or angle grinder with a flap disc, keep the original 30-degree bevel, and balance them on a blade balancer afterward. Avoid overheating the edge — dip in water if it discolors.
