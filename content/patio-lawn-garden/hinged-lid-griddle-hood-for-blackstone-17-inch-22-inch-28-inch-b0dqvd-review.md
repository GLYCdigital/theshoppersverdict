Let’s be direct: if you own a Blackstone griddle, you’ve probably stared at that bare cooking surface and wondered why a hood wasn’t included. This hinged lid accessory aims to solve that, and for the most part, it works. But “works” and “works well” are two different things—and the 3.8-star average across 190 ratings tells you exactly where this lands.

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes the hood itself, two mounting rails, and the necessary hardware. The design is straightforward: the rails slide onto the sides of your griddle, and the hood hinges on the rear. It’s compatible with the 17-inch, 22-inch, and 28-inch Blackstone models—that’s the selling point. One product, three griddles. The installation takes about five minutes with no tools beyond what’s in the box, and it doesn’t require drilling or permanent changes to your griddle.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

Here’s where things get honest. The hood is made from thin-gauge steel with a matte black finish. It’s not heavy-duty—it’s serviceable. When you lift it, there’s a slight flex in the metal that makes you wonder about longevity. Several reviewers echo this. One buyer noted, *“After about a month of regular use, the edges started to warp near the hinge.”* That’s not universal, but it’s common enough to mention.

The hinge action is smooth when new, and the lid stays in the open position thanks to a simple stop. That’s the good news. The bad news: there’s no latch or lock. If a breeze kicks up or you accidentally bump the handle, the lid slams down. That’s not just annoying—it’s a safety hazard if you’re reaching over a hot surface.

Functionally, the hood does its job. It traps heat, which means faster cheese melting, better steaming for buns, and noticeably less grease splatter on your patio. That’s the main reason people buy this, and it delivers. The fold-flat design is a smart touch—when you need full access to the cooking surface, the lid rests behind the griddle instead of hovering overhead.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The fit across multiple Blackstone sizes is the standout feature. Reviewers with 17-inch tailgating models and 28-inch backyard setups both report a snug, secure fit. The splash control also gets consistent thumbs-up. One reviewer summed it up: *“No more wiping down the side table after every cook. The lid catches 90% of the grease.”*

**Where It Falls Short**

The lack of a locking mechanism is the biggest complaint, and it’s legitimate. The thin metal construction is the second. This isn’t a buy-it-for-life product—it’s a functional accessory that may need replacement in a couple of seasons. There’s also a small gap where the rails mount, which means it’s not a perfect seal. Some heat and smoke escape, so don’t expect it to act like a true oven lid.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The weekend griddle cook** who wants better heat retention for melting cheese or keeping food warm while cooking in batches.
- **The patio entertainer** who’s tired of grease spots on the table or deck near the griddle.
- **The multi-Blackstone owner** who wants one accessory that works across different sizes.

**Skip It If**

- You expect commercial-grade build quality. This is a budget accessory, and it feels like one.
- You cook in windy conditions regularly. Without a lock, the lid will annoy you.
- You already own a hard cover or dome lid and don’t need the hinged convenience.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit my older Blackstone model?**
A: It’s designed for the current generation of 17, 22, and 28-inch models. If your griddle is more than a few years old, measure the side rails before ordering—older models had slightly different dimensions.

**Q: Can I leave it on while using the griddle’s built-in cover?**
A: No. The hood sits on the rails and will interfere with the original cover. You’ll need to remove it when storing the griddle.

**Q: Does it help with wind?**
A: It blocks some breeze from hitting the cooking surface directly, but it won’t fully shield a flame in gusty conditions. And since it doesn’t lock, strong wind can slam it shut.

**The Verdict**

**Rating: 3.7/5**

**Buy it if** you want an affordable, multi-size splash guard and heat trap that installs in minutes and folds out of the way. **Skip it if** you need rugged construction or a locking lid—you’ll be replacing this sooner than you’d like.

It’s a solid concept with acceptable execution. For the price, it does what it promises, just not with premium finesse. If your Blackstone lives on a covered patio and you’re careful with wind, this is a worthwhile upgrade. If it sits exposed to the elements, spend a little more on something sturdier.
