## Where it falls short

Honest downsides exist. The bag lacks a resealable zipper, so moisture can get in if you don't transfer the pellets to an airtight container — and wet pellets are useless pellets. At 40 pounds, the bag is also heavy and awkward to lift into high-mounted hoppers. A few reviewers mentioned batch-to-batch variation in the hardwood blend, which can subtly shift smoke flavor between bags. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing if you're chasing a specific taste profile.
