## The Corner Problem, Solved

Every pressure washer owner knows the frustration: you're cleaning your patio, and there's that one spot behind the downspout, or the corner where the fence meets the house, that your straight wand just can't reach. You contort your body, angle the spray, and still end up with a dirty stripe. This kit exists to solve exactly that problem — and it mostly does.

## What's Actually in the Box

The kit arrives with the Corner Max attachment as the centerpiece, plus a set of standard accessories. You get:

- **Corner Max angled wand** with a pivoting head that rotates up to 45 degrees
- **Standard 15-inch extension wand** (1/4-inch NPT male/female threads)
- **Four spray tips**: 0° (red), 25° (green), 40° (white), and soap nozzle (black)
- **Thread seal tape** — a small touch, but it means you can assemble and use it immediately

The 1/4-inch NPT threading is the industry standard for most residential gas and electric pressure washers, so compatibility is broad. As shown in the product image, the fittings are solid brass, not the zinc alloy or plastic you see on cheaper kits.

## Performance and Build Quality

The Corner Max is the star here. The pivoting head locks into multiple positions, letting you direct water at awkward angles without bending your wrist into unnatural positions. In testing, it handled a 2,000 PSI gas unit without leaking or vibrating loose — a common failure point on cheaper angled attachments.

The brass fittings thread smoothly and seat properly. One buyer noted, *"I've bought three other angled attachments and all leaked at the swivel. This one has held up through two months of weekly use with zero drips."* That tracks with the 4.5-star average across 225 ratings.

The spray tips are color-coded and machined precisely. The 25° tip is the workhorse for most patio and siding jobs, while the 0° tip delivers serious cutting power for stubborn mildew and caked-on mud. The soap nozzle draws detergent effectively, though it's best used with a downstream injector if your washer has one.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be straight: this kit isn't perfect.

**The 15-inch wand is short.** If you're cleaning a driveway or ground-level concrete, you'll be bent over the entire time. A longer wand would make this far more ergonomic. The tradeoff is that shorter length gives you more control with the Corner Max in tight spaces — but for big flat surfaces, you'll want your original wand.

**No quick-connect fittings included.** If your pressure washer uses quick-connect couplers, you'll need to buy those separately (about $10 for a set). The threads are standard, so your existing gun should work, but it's an extra purchase you should budget for.

**The Corner Max head can't rotate a full 360 degrees.** It locks at intervals, which is fine for most applications, but a continuous swivel would be more versatile for complex angles.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Fence and deck owners**: The angled head makes cleaning between vertical boards and under railings dramatically easier
- **Siding cleaners**: Reach soffits and eaves without a ladder in many cases

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my pressure washer?**
A: If your washer uses standard 1/4-inch NPT threads — which most residential units from Sun Joe, Ryobi, Craftsman, and Simpson do — yes. Check your gun's connection point. If it's a proprietary quick-connect, you'll need an adapter.

**Q: Can the Corner Max handle high PSI?**
A: Yes. The brass construction is rated for up to 3,500 PSI, which covers virtually all residential gas pressure washers and most electric units.

**Q: Does the pivoting head leak?**
A: In our testing and based on buyer reports, no. The locking mechanism seals properly when fully engaged. Just make sure you tighten the collar fully before use.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're tired of missing spots in corners, reaching under eaves, or contorting around obstacles. This is a well-built, thoughtfully designed kit that solves a real problem.
