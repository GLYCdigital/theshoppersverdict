Lava rock's whole selling point is heat retention. Unlike fire glass, which lets heat pass through, these porous stones absorb warmth and radiate it outward. In practice, buyers consistently note the fire feels warmer and more enveloping — one reviewer put it plainly: *"I was surprised how much more heat these put out compared to the glass I had before."*

The sizing consistency deserves credit. Most pieces land in that sweet spot where they stack naturally but leave gaps for gas to escape. The 10-pound quantity covers roughly 1.5 to 2 square feet at a 2-inch depth. If you have an 18-inch round fire pit, you'll have enough to fill it properly without needing a second bag.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. **The dust is real.** Nearly every critical review mentions it. These arrive coated in volcanic grit, and if you dump them in dry and light up, you'll get a hazy cloud. Fix it: rinse them in a bucket or wheelbarrow, let them dry, then install. Takes 15 minutes and solves the problem entirely.

Second issue: size consistency isn't perfect. A handful of pieces come through smaller — closer to pea gravel. These can work their way down through the burner grate over time. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing if you have a particularly open burner design.
