Let's cut through the yellow-and-black branding. DEWALT slapped their name on plenty of homeowner-grade gear that doesn't live up to the jobsite reputation. The DEWALT Electric Pressure Washer, 2100 PSI, however, actually earns the logo. At 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, this machine has real traction with buyers who clean everything from siding to farm equipment. Here's what you need to know before handing over your money.

## What You Actually Get

Open the box and you'll find the pressure washer unit itself, a spray gun with wand, five quick-connect nozzles (0°, 15°, 25°, 40°, and soap), a 35-foot pressure hose, and the necessary fittings. The frame is a welded steel cart with 12-inch pneumatic tires — a serious upgrade over the flimsy plastic wheels on most electric units in this class. The unit weighs around 60 pounds, so the wheels aren't optional; they're essential.

## Performance and Build Quality

The brushless induction motor is the headline feature here. Unlike the universal motors found in cheaper electric washers, this one runs cooler, lasts longer, and delivers consistent pressure without bogging down under load. At 2100 PSI and 1.2 GPM, it's not the highest-flow unit on the market — you won't be stripping paint off a barn with this thing — but for residential work, it's the sweet spot. It'll blast mildew off siding, clean concrete driveways, and prep decks for staining without gouging wood if you keep the wand moving.

The 35-foot hose is generous but has a flaw. As one buyer noted, "The hose is stiff and wants to kink if you don't lay it out perfectly straight." That's a real annoyance mid-job. You'll spend time untwisting it. The lack of a hose reel compounds the problem — there's no built-in way to manage the hose when you're done, so you're coiling it by hand.

What buyers consistently praise is the pressure delivery. Multiple reviewers mention it outperforms their old gas units. One wrote: "I had a 2800 PSI gas washer that died after two seasons. This DEWALT cleans just as well and I don't have to fight with starting it." That's the real value proposition: gas-level cleaning power without the engine maintenance.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the kink-prone hose, the wand storage is awkward. The onboard clips hold the wand and gun, but they're positioned so the gun hangs at an angle that catches on things when you roll the cart. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

The soap nozzle is also underwhelming. It siphons detergent but doesn't create the thick foam you get from dedicated foam cannons. For $25 you can add a better one, but it's a gap in an otherwise well-thought-out package.

The power cord is 35 feet, which pairs decently with the hose, but you'll still need an extension cord for larger driveways. Make sure it's a 12-gauge cord rated for outdoor use — this motor draws 13 amps and a light gauge cord will starve it.

## Who Should Buy This

**The suburban homeowner** with a 2,000-square-foot house, a concrete driveway, and a deck that needs annual cleaning — this is your machine. It's lightweight enough to maneuver, powerful enough to do the job in an afternoon, and won't need a tune-up next spring.

**The light contractor** — handymen, pressure washing side hustlers, and rental property owners — will appreciate the brushless motor's longevity. This is built to run for hours, not just 20 minutes at a time like many electrics.

**Skip it if** you need to clean large commercial surfaces daily, if you're on a tight budget (there are capable units for $100 less, though without the DEWALT build quality), or if you absolutely require a hose reel.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this pressure washer use hot water?**
A: No. This is a cold-water-only unit. Feeding it hot water will damage the seals and void the warranty.

**Q: Does it come with a warranty?**
A: Yes, DEWALT includes a 3-year limited warranty and a 90-day money-back guarantee. Given the brushless motor, that's reasonable coverage for the price point.

**Q: Will 2100 PSI damage my car's paint?**
A: Yes, if you hold the nozzle too close or use the 0° nozzle. Use the 40° nozzle at a distance of at least 18 inches for vehicle washing, and keep the wand moving.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, powerful electric washer that won't need maintenance for years and you're willing to work around the hose management quirks.

**Skip it if** you're a casual user who washes the car twice a year — you'll overpay for capability you won't use.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The DEWALT 2100 PSI is a genuinely well-built machine that earns its jobsite branding. It's not perfect, but for the majority of buyers, it'll be the last pressure washer they need for a decade.
