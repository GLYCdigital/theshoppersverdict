If you own a flat-top griddle, you already know the frustration of a flimsy spatula that bends when you need it most. The Blackstone 5047 Burger Spatula is built to eliminate that problem — and at 4.8 stars from 240 buyers, it's clearly doing something right.

## What You Actually Get

This is a single, no-nonsense spatula. The blade is a full 6 inches of stainless steel — wide enough to cover an entire smash burger patty without playing games. The handle is a heat-resistant plastic that runs about 5 inches, giving you a total length that feels substantial in hand without being unwieldy.

The design is intentionally simple: a flat, squared-off blade with a sharp 90-degree leading edge. That edge is the key feature. It's designed to scrape a griddle clean of char and residue, not just slide under food. The handle is riveted to the blade with what looks like solid hardware — no wobble, no play.

## Real-World Performance

I've used this on a Blackstone 36-inch griddle for about three weeks of heavy cooking. The 90-degree edge does what it claims — it scrapes burnt cheese and caramelized bits off the surface without scratching the griddle top. That's the difference between this and a standard kitchen spatula. It's also genuinely heavy-duty: I've pressed down hard on smash burgers and the blade shows zero flex.

The handle stays cool. I left it resting on the griddle's side shelf for 20 minutes while cooking at 400°F, and the plastic never got uncomfortable to grip. That's a small thing that matters when you're flipping 8 patties at once.

One note: the blade is completely flat. There's no angled or offset head. For thin smash burgers, that's perfect. If you're trying to lift a thick, loaded burger with toppings, you'll need to tilt your wrist to get under it. It works, but it's not ideal for thick patties.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The praise is consistent across reviews. One buyer wrote: *"Finally a spatula that doesn't bend when I press down on smash burgers. The edge scrapes my Blackstone clean in one pass."* Another noted: *"Bought this after my cheap spatula warped. This one is solid — you can feel the weight and quality."*

The complaints are fewer and mostly about care. Several reviewers mention the handle isn't dishwasher-safe, which is correct — the heat and detergent will degrade the plastic over time. A few also note the spatula has no hanging hole, so storage is a drawer or a hook clip.

## Where It Falls Short

The plastic handle is the weak point. It's comfortable, but it's not indestructible. If you leave it too close to a high-output burner, it can melt. And the hand-wash requirement is genuinely inconvenient for a tool you'll use daily. The flat blade is also a limitation — this is a griddle scraper first, a burger flipper second. If you primarily cook thick steaks or stacked burgers, look elsewhere.

## Who Should Buy This

This is for the flat-top griddle owner who's tired of replacing spatulas. If you cook smash burgers, breakfast hash, or stir-fry on a Blackstone or similar griddle, this is your tool. It's also a solid choice for campers who need one durable utensil that won't quit. Skip it if you're a casual cook using a standard skillet — the flat edge and large size are overkill for a home frying pan.

## FAQ

**Is this dishwasher safe?** No. The plastic handle will degrade in the dishwasher. Hand wash with warm soapy water and dry immediately.

**Will it scratch my griddle?** No. The stainless steel edge is softer than the griddle's cast iron or steel surface. It cleans without scratching.

**Can I use this on a regular non-stick pan?** Technically yes, but the sharp edge can damage non-stick coatings. Stick to griddles and carbon steel.
