## The 4-Piece Set That Actually Looks Like the Photos

Most outdoor furniture in this price range shows up looking like a potato sack with legs. The LAUSAINT HOME set doesn't. The PE rattan weave is tight and uniform, the aluminum frame feels solid when you knock on it, and the tempered glass top is genuinely thick — not that cheap, flexy stuff you see on $200 sets. As shown in the product image, the dark brown wicker with cream cushions reads more expensive than it is.

## What You Actually Get in the Box

- 4 armchairs (fully assembled — just unfold and place)
- 1 square table with a 5mm tempered glass top
- 4 seat cushions and 4 back cushions
- All necessary hardware and an Allen wrench

The chairs arriving pre-assembled is the single biggest time-saver. You're looking at 10–15 minutes total to bolt the table base to the top. No wrestling with 47 screws at 9 PM in the driveway.

## Real-World Performance: The Good

**Weather resistance is the headline.** The PE rattan is UV-stabilized, and the aluminum frame won't rust. Multiple buyers on Amazon confirmed leaving it out through summer storms and full sun with zero fading or cracking. One reviewer wrote, *"Had this set for 3 months of Florida heat and daily rain. The wicker still looks brand new. My last set from [a big box store] was peeling by now."*

**Stability is impressive.** The table doesn't wobble, even on slightly uneven patio pavers. The chairs have a wide footprint and the arms give you leverage to push back without tipping. At roughly 20 pounds per chair, they're heavy enough to stay put in wind but light enough to drag around the yard.

**The glass top is a genuine upgrade.** It's rimmed so plates don't slide off, and it wipes clean in one pass. You don't need a tablecloth, which is a plus for anyone tired of scrubbing barbecue sauce out of wood grain.

## Where It Falls Short

**The cushions are the weak link.** At about 2 inches thick, they're more like firm mattresses than plush seating. Perfectly fine for a 30-minute meal, but don't plan on lingering over coffee for two hours. Several reviewers noted this — one said, *"Great set, but I bought separate 4-inch cushions for the chairs. The originals are too flat for my back."*

**Size reality check.** The table is 43 inches square. Four standard dining chairs fit, but if you're seating four adults with broad shoulders, it's tight. It's honestly better as a set for 2–3 people plus a serving tray.

**Assembly is easy, but the instructions are sparse.** The diagram is small and the text is minimal. If you're not handy, you'll figure it out — but it's not the step-by-step pamphlet you'd expect.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Does the glass top hold up to hot dishes?**
Yes. The tempered glass handles direct heat from serving dishes without cracking. Just don't drag cast iron across it — use trivets for anything heavy or rough-bottomed.

**Q: Can it stay outside in winter?**
The frame and wicker are fine. The cushions should be stored indoors or in a waterproof deck box — they're not rated for snow and freeze-thaw cycles. Cover the set if you're not using it for months at a time.

**Q: How much weight can the chairs hold?**
The aluminum frame is rated for roughly 250 pounds per chair, which is standard for this class. The arms are sturdy enough to lean on, but don't sit on the arms — they're not built for that kind of lateral stress.
