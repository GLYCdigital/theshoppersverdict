## Is this propane fire table a real buy or a backyard gamble?

At 4.2 stars across 210 ratings, this steel gas fire pit has earned a solid reputation — but it's not perfect. Here's what you need to know before you add it to your cart.

## What's in the box

You get the steel fire table frame, tabletop, burner assembly, lava rocks or glass stones (depending on variant), a wind guard, and the necessary hoses and connectors for a standard 20 lb propane tank. No tank included — you'll supply that yourself.

## Build quality and real-world performance

The steel construction is genuinely sturdy. The tabletop is large enough to hold drinks and plates while the fire runs — a functional two-in-one piece that earns its patio space. The wind guard is a thoughtful addition; several buyers noted it keeps flames from flickering out in moderate breezes.

Assembly is the biggest friction point. Multiple reviewers mention that holes don't align perfectly and you'll need a second pair of hands and some patience. One buyer put it plainly: "Took about an hour and a half to put together, and I had to re-drill one hole." That's not a dealbreaker, but plan for it.

Heat output is moderate. This is a vibe piece, not a space heater. On a cool evening, you'll feel warmth sitting close, but don't expect it to heat a large screened porch. If you want serious BTUs, look at dedicated fire pits with higher output.

## What buyers consistently praise

The aesthetic is the standout. Multiple reviewers describe it as "a beautiful centerpiece" and "much nicer looking than the price suggests." The flame appearance — especially with lava rocks — gets consistent compliments. Buyers also appreciate the clean propane setup with no smoke, ash, or sparks — a clear advantage over wood-burning pits.

Portability gets mentioned too. It's heavy enough to feel substantial but has wheels (on some variants) or is light enough to move around the patio as needed.

## Where it falls short

Beyond assembly, the ignition system draws mixed feedback. Some buyers report the electronic igniter is finicky; a few had to light it manually with a lighter. This seems to be a quality control variance — most work fine, but it's worth knowing.

The included lava rocks are fewer than expected by some buyers. If you want a fuller, more dramatic rock bed, budget for an extra bag.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** You want a stylish propane fire feature that doubles as a side table, you're okay with a bit of assembly effort, and you value no-smoke, no-ash convenience.

**Skip it if:** You need serious heat output for cold evenings, you expect tool-free assembly, or you want a large, dramatic fire feature — this reads as a medium-sized table, not a monumental pit.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use a standard 20 lb propane tank?**
Yes — it connects to the standard tank size used for gas grills. Check your specific variant, but most ship with the standard regulator and hose.

**Q: How long will a tank last?**
Expect 8–12 hours on medium flame, depending on tank size and output setting. Some buyers report closer to 10 hours on a full tank.

**Q: Is it safe on a wood deck?**
Yes, the steel body and elevated design keep the heat off the deck surface. Use the included heat shield if your variant has one, and keep the tank away from the flame per the manual.
