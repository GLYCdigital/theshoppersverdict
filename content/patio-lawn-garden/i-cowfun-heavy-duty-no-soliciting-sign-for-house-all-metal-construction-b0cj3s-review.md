## What You Actually Get

The package includes the sign itself—a solid 10x7 inch aluminum panel (roughly the size of a standard sheet of paper)—plus four stainless steel screws and wall anchors. The pre-drilled holes in each corner are spaced evenly, which matters more than you'd think. Cheap signs often have misaligned holes that force you to mount them crooked. That's not an issue here.

The metal is a genuine 1.5mm gauge, which is noticeably thicker than the flimsy .8mm signs most competitors sell. If you live somewhere with serious weather, this matters—thin metal flexes in wind and eventually cracks at the mounting points. This one won't.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The sign features a baked-on enamel finish with white lettering on a black background. The contrast is excellent—one buyer noted, "I can read it clearly from my driveway, and I have aging eyes." The lettering includes the standard "No Soliciting" headline plus a smaller line reading "No Exceptions, No Trespassing." It's direct, which is the point.

UV resistance is where this sign earns its keep. Several reviewers mention having previous plastic signs that turned chalky and unreadable within a year. The metal construction with enamel coating handles direct sun exposure without fading. One Florida reviewer specifically noted their sign has survived "two hurricane seasons and still looks brand new."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring theme in the 235 ratings is that this sign actually works. Multiple reviewers report a dramatic drop in door-knockers within the first week. One quoted: "I've had zero solicitors since installing this. The metal build and bold letters make it clear you mean business."

The mounting process gets consistent praise too. One buyer wrote, "Took me less than five minutes with a drill. The anchors grip tight—no wobble." The included hardware is decent quality, which is rare at this price point.

## Where It Falls Short

The permanent message is the biggest limitation. If you ever want to change what the sign says—maybe add "No Political Canvassing" or remove the trespassing warning—you're buying a new sign. Some competitors offer removable vinyl lettering for that flexibility.

A handful of reviewers also note the glossy finish shows fingerprints and water spots. If you're particular about appearances, you'll want to handle it by the edges during installation and wipe it down periodically. It's a minor aesthetic issue, not a functional one.
