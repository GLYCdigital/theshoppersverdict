**Tempera Black Patio Furniture Covers Review: Honest Look After 220 Ratings**

If you’ve priced replacement patio cushions lately, you already know a $40 cover is cheaper than a $300 cushion set. The Tempera Black Patio Furniture Covers sit in that sweet spot of affordability and protection — but they’re not flawless. Here’s what 220 Amazon reviewers and real-world use reveal.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the cover, four buckled straps, and hem cords at the base. No handles, no storage bag — just the cover itself. The fabric is 600D polyester with a water-resistant coating, which is the same material class as pricier brands like Classic Accessories. The black color is a solid, non-fading dye that looks sharp against most patio setups.

As shown in the product image, the cover drapes over standard sofas, loveseats, and dining sets. It’s not a custom-tailored fit — it’s a generic rectangle with an elastic hem. For a 3-seater sofa (around 70 inches), it covers completely with about 4 inches of overhang. That’s enough to tuck under the base.

**Performance: Where It Excels**

The 4.4-star rating isn’t accidental. Most buyers report the cover surviving full rainstorms without leakage. One reviewer noted: *“After a week of heavy rain, my cushions were bone dry. The water just beads off and rolls to the ground.”* That matches the coated polyester’s behavior — it repels water well, though it’s not fully waterproof under prolonged pooling.

Wind resistance is genuinely good. The hem cords cinch the cover tight, and the buckled straps anchor to table legs or sofa frames. A customer in a gusty coastal area wrote: *“We had 40 mph winds last month. The cover didn’t budge. My neighbor’s cheap tarp ended up in the yard next door.”* If you live in a windy region, this alone justifies the purchase.

The stitching is another strong point. Reinforced seams at the corners and along the hem held up through a full season of sun, rain, and frost — no fraying or splits reported in any of the detailed reviews.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let’s be direct: black fabric is a double-edged sword. It looks sleek, but it absorbs heat. On a 90°F day, the cover surface can reach 130°F+. If you’re pulling it off to use the furniture immediately, the cushions underneath will feel warm — not damaging, but uncomfortable. This isn’t a dealbreaker, but it’s worth knowing if you entertain often.

The bigger issue is ventilation. There are no air vents. In humid climates (think Gulf Coast or Pacific Northwest), moisture can get trapped underneath, leading to mildew on cushions over several weeks. One reviewer from Florida mentioned: *“After a month, I found mildew spots on my seat cushions. The cover itself was fine, but the lack of airflow is a problem.”* If you live in a wet, humid area, you’ll want to air out the furniture periodically or add a moisture-absorbing layer.

Another minor gripe: the elastic hem is snug but not adjustable. If your furniture has unusual dimensions (like a deep sectional), the cover may not reach the base fully. Measure your furniture before ordering — the listing provides dimensions, but they’re approximate.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Budget-conscious homeowners** who want solid protection without dropping $80+ on premium covers.
- **Windy-climate residents** — the strap system is genuinely robust.
- **Occasional users** who cover furniture for winter storage or seasonal gaps, not year-round in extreme weather.

**Skip it if** you live in a consistently humid, rainy region and plan to leave the cover on for months without checking underneath. The lack of vents makes that a mildew risk.

**FAQ**

*Q: Is it truly waterproof?*  
A: Water-resistant, not waterproof. It handles rain well, but standing water on top can eventually seep through. Slope the cover slightly or use a ball to encourage runoff.

*Q: Will it fit my 4-piece dining set?*  
A: Only if your set is within the listed dimensions. Measure the widest point and compare to the product specs. The cover is generous but not stretchy.

*Q: Does the black color fade?*  
A: Not noticeably. Multiple reviewers reported no fading after a season of direct sun. The dye quality is above average for this price point.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need dependable, affordable protection for standard patio furniture in most climates. The 600D fabric, solid stitching, and wind-resistant straps make it a smart value at this price.

**Skip it if** you live in a hot, humid region and plan to leave it on for months without ventilation, or if your furniture has non-standard dimensions.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — a strong, honest performer with two specific caveats. For the price, it’s hard to beat.
