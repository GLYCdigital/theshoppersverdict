# COBANA 7.5'x4' Half Rectangular Patio Umbrella: The Space-Smart Shade Solution Most Brands Overlook

Most patio umbrellas are round. That's fine if you've got a circular table floating in the middle of a wide-open deck. But what if your seating area sits flush against a wall, a railing, or a fence? That's where the COBANA 7.5'x4' half rectangular umbrella earns its keep. It's a niche product done well — and for the right setup, it's genuinely the best option you'll find.

## What You Actually Get

The package contains the umbrella canopy, a one-piece 1.5-inch aluminum pole, the crank mechanism, and a tilt adjuster. That's it. No base, no table, no mounting hardware. The pole is designed to drop through a standard 1.5-inch to 2-inch table hole. If you don't have a table with a pre-drilled hole, you'll need to buy a separate half-umbrella base — and those aren't as common as standard round bases, so check availability before you commit.

The canopy itself measures 7.5 feet across the straight edge and 4 feet deep. That's roughly 30 square feet of shade — enough to cover a 4-6 person rectangular table when positioned correctly. The fabric is a 300D polyester with a UV-protective coating. It's not solution-dyed, but the coating does the job.

## Real-World Performance

The crank mechanism is where this umbrella surprises. It's buttery smooth — no catching, no grinding. You can raise and lower it with one hand while holding a drink in the other. The tilt function is a simple push-button that lets you angle the canopy up to 45 degrees. It clicks into place and stays there, even with a mild breeze pushing against it.

The half-rectangular shape is the real selling point. As shown in the product image, the flat edge sits flush against a wall, meaning zero wasted shade. Every square inch of canopy is doing work. That's not true of a round umbrella, where a third of the shade is falling on your siding or into open space.

Buyers consistently highlight this efficiency. One reviewer noted, "Finally an umbrella that doesn't leave half my table in the sun just because it's against the house." Another praised the fabric quality: "The material is thicker than I expected — it's held up through a summer of afternoon sun with no visible fading."

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The aluminum ribs are functional but not premium. In a sudden gust, you'll want to close this umbrella — it's not built to ride out a storm. The pole is also a single piece, which makes storage awkward if you plan to take it down seasonally. It won't collapse down to a compact length.

The lack of an included base is the bigger frustration for some buyers. A standard round umbrella base won't work well here because the center of gravity is offset. You need a base designed for half umbrellas, or you need to bolt it through a permanent table. That's an extra cost and an extra step that some shoppers don't anticipate.

One more thing: the 4-foot depth is snug. If you have a deep table (over 36 inches), the shade won't reach the far edge when the sun is low. You'll need to angle the tilt aggressively, which trades vertical clearance.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Homeowners with wall-adjacent seating**: If your patio table hugs the house, this is the best shade-per-square-foot you'll get.
- **Balcony dwellers**: The half design is perfect for tight spaces where a full umbrella won't fit.
- **Commercial settings**: Cafés and restaurants with perimeter seating benefit from the flush-mount design.

Skip it if you want a freestanding umbrella, need a massive shade footprint, or live somewhere with frequent high winds.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this fit a standard patio table?**
A: Yes, if your table has a hole between 1.5 and 2 inches in diameter. The pole is 1.5 inches wide. Measure your table before ordering.

**Q: Can I use a regular umbrella base?**
A: Technically yes, but the offset weight distribution makes it unstable. COBANA recommends a base specifically designed for half umbrellas, or you risk tipping.

**Q: Will the fabric fade after a season?**
A: The UV coating is effective. Multiple buyers report no fading after full summer exposure. It's not solution-dyed like premium Sunbrella fabric, but it holds up well for the price.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you have wall-adjacent seating and want efficient, no-waste shade with a smooth crank mechanism. It's a well-built niche product that does exactly what it promises.

**Skip it if** you need a freestanding umbrella, have a deep table over 36 inches, or live in a wind-prone area where you won't be diligent about closing it during gusts.
