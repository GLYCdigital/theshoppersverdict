**The Short Version: A Surprisingly Solid Compact Set**

Most bistro sets at this price point feel like they'll blow away in a stiff breeze or shed their wicker within a season. The Bonnlo 3-piece set doesn't. It's not luxury patio furniture, but it punches well above its price class for small balconies, porches, or a quiet corner of the yard. If you need a functional, good-looking two-seater and don't expect heirloom quality, this is a genuinely smart buy.

---

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains two armchairs, one round table, and the necessary hardware. As shown in the product image, the set uses a dark brown PE rattan weave over a powder-coated steel frame — not the cheap, hollow plastic you sometimes see at this price. The table measures roughly 19.5 inches in diameter and stands about 21 inches tall, making it a proper coffee-height table rather than a flimsy accent piece. Chairs have a 29-inch height with a 17.5-inch seat width.

Assembly is genuinely easy. All bolts, an Allen wrench, and a small spanner are included. Most buyers report being done in 15–20 minutes per chair, and the table takes about five. No drilling, no guesswork.

---

**Real-World Performance and Build**

The PE rattan weave is the star here. It's tightly woven over the frame with no visible gaps or loose ends — a detail that matters because loose wicker is what kills cheap sets within a year. The steel frame underneath is adequately thick for the price, and the powder coating resists chipping better than you'd expect.

The tempered glass tabletop has a raised outer lip, which is a thoughtful touch that keeps cups from sliding off during a breeze. There's also a small bottle stopper built into the surface — a minor detail, but one that shows design consideration.

Weather resistance is decent but not unlimited. The wicker and frame handle sun and light rain fine. The cushions, however, are the weak point. They're about two inches thick — comfortable enough for a short coffee, not for an afternoon of lounging — and the fabric is water-resistant at best. One reviewer noted, "Cushions got damp after an overnight rain. They dried fine, but I wouldn't leave them out all season." That's fair. Bring them in when storms roll through.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be direct. The cushions are the weakest link — thin and not truly weatherproof. If you leave them out all season in a rainy climate, they'll degrade. Also, the set is compact by design. Buyers over 6 feet tall or heavier than 250 pounds have reported the chairs feel narrow. One reviewer put it plainly: "I'm 6'3\" and it works, but my knees are high and the arms dig in after 30 minutes." Know your body type before ordering.

Finally, there's no tilt or swivel mechanism on the chairs — they're fixed-position. That's fine for a bistro set, but if you want to recline, this isn't it.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Apartment and condo dwellers** with a balcony or small patio who want a real seating set without dominating the space.
- **First-time patio furniture buyers** who want something that looks good and holds up without a big investment.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is the wicker real or plastic?**
A: It's PE rattan — a synthetic, weather-resistant polymer designed to mimic natural wicker. It's more durable than real wicker outdoors and won't crack or splinter.

**Q: Can I leave it outside all year?**
A: The frame and wicker can handle sun and rain. The cushions cannot — they'll absorb moisture and degrade. Cover the set or store the cushions during winter or prolonged wet weather.

**Q: What's the weight capacity?**
A: The manufacturer lists 250 lbs per chair. That's the safe limit; exceeding it may stress the frame over time.
