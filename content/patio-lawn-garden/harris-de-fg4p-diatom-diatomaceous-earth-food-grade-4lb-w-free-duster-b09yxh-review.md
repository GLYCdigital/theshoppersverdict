## What You Actually Get

The 4lb container is a resealable jug — not a flimsy bag that splits open in your garage. The included duster is a bellows-style applicator that screws onto the top. It’s basic, but it works, delivering a fine, even puff of powder into cracks, along baseboards, and around garden beds. You also get a small measuring scoop inside the jug, which matters for mixing with water if you want to spray it on plants.

The product itself is 100% ground freshwater diatomaceous earth, OMRI-listed for organic use. That means it’s food grade — technically safe for human consumption (though you shouldn’t eat it), and far safer around pets than conventional insecticides.

## How It Performs

DE works mechanically, not chemically. The microscopic sharp edges in the powder cut through insects' exoskeletons, causing them to dehydrate and die. It’s not instant — expect results over 48 hours to a week, depending on the pest.

For crawling insects like ants, roaches, silverfish, and earwigs, this is genuinely effective. One buyer noted, *“I used this along the perimeter of my kitchen and in the bathroom. Within four days, the ant trail was gone and hasn’t come back in three months.”* Another mentioned using it in their chicken coop for mites, which is a common and smart application.

Outdoors, it works for slugs, beetles, and other garden pests, but here’s the catch: **it must stay dry**. Rain or even heavy dew renders it useless until reapplied. If you’re in a humid climate or get regular rain, plan on reapplication every week or two.

## Where It Falls Short

The mess is real. The powder is extremely fine, and if you’re not careful, it gets everywhere. Wear a mask — you don’t want to inhale this stuff even if it’s food grade. One reviewer warned, *“This is dust. Fine dust. It goes everywhere, and my wife wasn’t happy about the cleanup.”* Fair warning.

Also, don’t expect it to work on flying insects or pests that don’t crawl through the powder. It won’t help with mosquitoes, and it’s marginal for fleas unless you’re aggressively treating carpets and pet bedding.

## FAQ

**Is this safe for pets?**
Yes, when used as directed. Food grade DE is non-toxic, but keep it away from your pet’s face and food bowls. The dust can irritate lungs if inhaled heavily, so keep pets out of the room during application and let it settle.

**Can I use this indoors and outdoors?**
Both. Indoors, focus on cracks, baseboards, and under appliances. Outdoors, apply a thin ring around garden beds or the foundation of your house. Reapply after rain.

**Does it kill bed bugs?**
It can, but it’s slow — expect 7-10 days. It only works if the powder contacts the bugs directly. For a full infestation, combine with other treatments and be patient.
