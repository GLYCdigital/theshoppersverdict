# Dimension Granular Pre-Emergent Herbicide Review: The 50 LB Bag That Actually Pays Off

If you've waged war on crabgrass and lost, the problem might not be your tactics — it's your timing. Pre-emergent herbicides are the only real defense against crabgrass, and Dimension Granular Pre-Emergent is one of the most effective weapons you can spread. But at 50 pounds, this isn't a casual purchase. Here's what you need to know before you commit.

## What's In the Bag

You're getting 50 pounds of granular herbicide with **0.15% dithiopyr** as the active ingredient. That's the key differentiator here. Most pre-emergents (like prodiamine-based products) only prevent germination. Dithiopyr does that *plus* provides early post-emergent control — meaning if you're slightly late and crabgrass has already sprouted, Dimension can still knock it out as long as the weeds are young.

The bag covers roughly **16,000 square feet** at the standard 3.1 lb per 1,000 sq ft rate. If you're treating a typical quarter-acre lot (about 10,000 sq ft of turf), you'll get one full application plus a second application for late summer — or you can split it into spring and fall treatments.

## Real-World Performance

Applied correctly, Dimension delivers. The granular formulation spreads evenly through a broadcast spreader, and the product's ability to form a barrier in the soil is genuinely reliable. Most users report **90%+ crabgrass prevention** when applied before soil temperatures hit 55°F.

The 4-month residual control is accurate, not marketing fluff. One spring application typically carries you through the entire crabgrass germination window, which is the longest of any pre-emergent on the market. You'll also get suppression of foxtail, barnyardgrass, and other grassy weeds.

## Where It Falls Short

**The watering requirement is non-negotiable.** Dimension needs 0.5 inches of irrigation within 14 days of application to activate. Skip this, and you've wasted 50 pounds of product and a season of protection. If you don't have reliable irrigation or consistent spring rainfall, this is a real risk.

**Broadleaf weeds are unaffected.** Dandelions, clover, plantain — Dimension won't touch them. You'll need a separate broadleaf herbicide like 2,4-D or a combination product for those.

**Physical handling is a chore.** The 50 lb bag has no handle, and the granule consistency makes it prone to clumping if stored in humid conditions. Pouring into a spreader requires two hands and some care.

## FAQ

**When should I apply Dimension?**
Apply when soil temperatures reach 55°F consistently — typically 2-4 weeks before your area's last frost date. For most of the Midwest and Mid-Atlantic, that's mid-April. Southern lawns may need application in February-March.

**Can I overseed after using Dimension?**
No. Pre-emergents don't discriminate — they'll prevent grass seed germination too. Wait 8-12 weeks after application before overseeding, or 2-3 weeks after a fall application.

**How do I know if it's working?**
You won't see it working — that's the point. If you have zero crabgrass by July, it worked. If you see breakthrough, you either applied too late, didn't water it in, or had heavy rain that washed it away.
