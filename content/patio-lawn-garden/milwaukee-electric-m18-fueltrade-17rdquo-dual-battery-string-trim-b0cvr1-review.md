Let me get this out of the way first: Milwaukee's naming department needs help. But the M18 FUEL 17" dual battery string trimmer (model 2825-20) is a serious piece of lawn equipment that's earned its 4.9-star rating across 245 reviews. The question isn't whether it works — it's whether it's worth the premium over cheaper cordless options. Here's the real story.

**What you actually get**

The package includes the trimmer head, a straight shaft, the auxiliary handle, and a shoulder strap. Batteries and charger are sold separately — this is a bare tool. If you already own M18 batteries from other Milwaukee gear, you're set. If you're starting fresh, budget for at least two High Output batteries and a charger, which adds significantly to the total cost.

**Performance and real-world use**

The dual battery design isn't marketing theater. Running two M18 batteries in parallel provides consistent voltage under load, which means the POWERSTATE brushless motor doesn't sag when you hit thick grass or encroaching weeds. Buyers consistently report it chews through overgrown areas that stall lesser trimmers.

The 17-inch cutting swath covers ground efficiently — fewer passes, less time. The bump feed head is a genuine improvement over older designs. One reviewer put it simply: "Line reload takes seconds, not minutes. I don't dread the bump feed anymore."

That said, this thing is heavy. With two batteries mounted, you're looking at roughly 12-13 pounds. The shoulder strap helps, but your forearms will feel it after 30 minutes of continuous trimming. Multiple reviewers noted this — one called it "a beast that requires respect" — so if you have a small yard and weak arms, consider a lighter option.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The recurring themes in positive reviews are power and durability. Homeowners with acreage and pros doing commercial work both report it handles everything from regular grass to brush without complaint. The build quality is typical Milwaukee — tough plastic housings, metal guard, no flex in the shaft.

Runtime is the other standout. The dual battery setup extends run time considerably compared to single-battery competitors. With two 12.0 Ah High Output batteries, you can realistically trim a large property on one charge cycle.

**Where it falls short**

Beyond the weight, there are two honest criticisms. First, the price — this is a premium tool with premium tool pricing. If you're a casual homeowner trimming a postage-stamp lawn twice a month, you're overpaying for capability you'll never use.

Second, the balance point. With two batteries stacked vertically, the center of gravity sits higher than ideal. The auxiliary handle placement helps, but it's not as well-balanced as some single-battery competitors. It takes a few sessions to get comfortable with the feel.

**Who should buy this**

This trimmer fits three specific buyer profiles:

- **Existing Milwaukee M18 owners** — especially those with High Output batteries already in rotation. This slots into your ecosystem without new battery investment.
- **Property owners with serious trimming demands** — acreage, overgrown ditches, fence lines, or commercial landscaping work. The power and runtime justify the price.
- **Buyers who value durability over cost** — if you're tired of replacing cheap trimmers every 18 months, this will outlast several budget units.

**Skip it if** you have a small to medium suburban lot, don't own M18 batteries, and trim for less than 20 minutes per session. A lighter, cheaper trimmer will serve you fine.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this work with older M18 batteries?**
A: Yes, but performance scales with battery output. Standard M18 batteries work, but the High Output versions (XD or Forge) deliver the best sustained power and runtime.

**Q: How does it compare to gas trimmers?**
A: It matches or beats most gas trimmers in power for typical residential and light commercial use, with zero mixing fuel, no pull-start frustration, and far less noise and vibration.

**Q: Can I attach other heads (edger, pole saw)?**
A: Milwaukee offers a Quik-Lok attachment system for this trimmer that accepts edgers, pole saws, and other accessories, making it a versatile multi-tool platform.

**The Verdict**

**Rating: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if** you need professional-grade trimming power, already own M18 batteries, or have acreage that punishes lesser tools. **Skip it if** you're a light-duty user on a budget — you'll pay for capability you won't use.

The M18 FUEL dual battery trimmer is a heavy, expensive, genuinely excellent tool. It's not for everyone, but for the right buyer, it's the last string trimmer you'll need to buy.
