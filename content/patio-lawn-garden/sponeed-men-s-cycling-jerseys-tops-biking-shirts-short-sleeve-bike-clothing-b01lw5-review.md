**What You Actually Get**

**Where It Falls Short**

The sizing is the biggest complaint across reviews. This fits like a European race cut — snug through the chest and shoulders. If you're between sizes or carry any extra mass, go up a full size. The fabric is also thinner than premium options; it's fine for road and gravel, but if you're brushing against brush on singletrack, expect it to wear faster.

The sleeve length runs short. Cyclists with bigger arms or broader shoulders will find the cuffs ride up. It's a lean-build jersey, not a gym-build jersey.

**Who Should Buy This**
