If you've dropped $300+ on a Dolphin robotic pool cleaner, you already know the caddy is what keeps the unit, cable, and accessories organized between swims. But that caddy sits outside, baking in UV rays and getting hammered by rain — which is exactly where the Dolphin Genuine Accessory Caddys Cover comes in. At 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. Here's the real story after digging into what buyers actually say.

## What You Actually Get

The cover is a single-piece, fitted shell made from 600D polyester — the same material grade used in quality grill covers and boat seats. It's cut specifically for the Dolphin Caddys (the wheeled cart that comes with many Dolphin models), with a reinforced opening at the top for the handle and a slit that accommodates the caddy's upright pole. The underside has a water-resistant coating, and the seams are double-stitched rather than glued, which matters for longevity.

As shown in the product image, the cover drapes down to the caddy's wheel line, leaving the wheels exposed so you can still roll the whole unit around without removing the cover. That's a thoughtful design touch — you don't have to strip it off every time you move the caddy from the poolside to the garage.

## Performance and Build Quality

In practice, the fit is the headline feature. Multiple reviewers note it "fits like a glove" and "snaps on in seconds." One buyer wrote, "I was skeptical about the 'genuine accessory' premium, but the corners line up perfectly and it doesn't flap around." The handle opening has a Velcro closure that keeps rain from seeping down the pole — a detail cheap universal covers miss.

The 600D fabric feels substantial, not like the thin tarpaulin you get with generic covers. It's also UV-treated, which matters if your caddy sits in direct sun. Over a season of use, reviewers report no fading or cracking, and the waterproof backing holds up in heavy downpours without the cover becoming waterlogged.

## Where It Falls Short

The most consistent criticism is the lack of a bottom hem. There's no drawstring or elastic cord to cinch the cover against the caddy's frame. In gusty conditions, wind can lift the skirt and eventually flip the cover off. One reviewer noted, "Works great until a storm rolls through — then I'm chasing it across the yard." If your caddy lives in an exposed area, consider bungee-cording the bottom or moving it to a sheltered spot during severe weather.

Also, this is a genuine Dolphin accessory, which means it's priced at a premium over generic alternatives. And it only fits the official Dolphin Caddys — if you bought a third-party caddy or a DIY cart, the dimensions won't match, and you'll be dealing with a baggy or strained fit.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Dolphin Caddys owners who store their cleaner outdoors** — this is the best protection you'll find for the price of a few pool chemicals.
- **Anyone who's already replaced one caddy due to sun damage** — the UV protection alone justifies the cost.
- **Pool owners in windy regions** should buy it, but plan to secure the bottom with a strap or bungee.

Skip it if you have a non-Dolphin caddy, or if you keep your caddy indoors year-round — you're paying for weather protection you don't need.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my Dolphin Caddys if I have an older model?**
A: Dolphin has kept the Caddys frame dimensions consistent across recent models (S200, S300, S500 series). Most reviewers with these models report a snug, correct fit. If you have a very old or unusual model, measure the frame height and width against the cover's dimensions before ordering.

**Q: Can I leave the cover on while the caddy is folded or collapsed?**
A: Yes, but the cover is designed for the caddy in its upright, assembled position. If you fold the caddy flat for storage, the cover will be loose and offer less protection. It's best used with the caddy standing.

**Q: How do I clean the cover?**
A: Hose it down with water and mild soap, then let it air dry. Avoid machine washing — the waterproof backing can delaminate in a washer. Wipe off bird droppings or tree sap promptly to prevent staining.
