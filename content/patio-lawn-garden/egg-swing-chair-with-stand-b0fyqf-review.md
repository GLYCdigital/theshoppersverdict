If you've been eyeing one of those egg-shaped hanging chairs for your patio, you already know the market is flooded with flimsy options that buckle after one season. This one, hovering at 4.1 stars across 204 ratings, actually holds its own. Here's what you need to know before you click buy.

**What you actually get**

The box delivers the full package: the steel A-frame stand, the egg-shaped rattan-style seat, a hanging chain with a swivel mechanism, and a cushioned seat pad. The frame is powder-coated black steel, and the seat is a woven PE rattan — not natural wicker, which is good because PE won't crack or fade the way real wicker does. The stand footprint is roughly 4.5 feet wide and 6.5 feet tall, so measure your space before ordering. This is not a small piece of furniture.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The steel frame is genuinely sturdy. The A-frame design spreads the weight evenly, and multiple buyers noted it doesn't tip or wobble even on slightly uneven patio bricks. One reviewer put it plainly: "I'm 220 pounds and it holds me without any creaking or bending. The frame is solid." The 360-degree swivel is smooth and quiet, which matters more than you'd think — cheap chairs have a grinding feel when you rotate.

The woven seat is tightly constructed, not loosely wrapped like some budget options. The chain attachment points are reinforced, which addresses the most common failure point on these chairs. Assembly is the real hurdle. Plan for 45 to 60 minutes with two people. The instructions are diagram-heavy with small print, so set aside patience. Several buyers mentioned that the holes on the frame didn't perfectly align at first, requiring some gentle persuasion with a rubber mallet.

**What buyers consistently praise**

- **Stability:** The wide base and solid steel construction get repeated shout-outs. People trust this chair with kids and heavier adults.
- **Comfort once you adjust it:** The seat depth is generous, and the hanging height is adjustable via the chain links. Most buyers found the sweet spot after a few tries.
- **All-weather durability:** The powder coating resists rust, and the PE rattan holds up in sun and rain. Multiple reviewers reported leaving it outside for months with no visible wear.

**Where it falls short**

The cushion is the weakest link. It's thinner than the product photos suggest — more of a pad than a plush seat cushion. If you plan on spending hours reading or napping, budget for a separate thick seat cushion. The assembly frustration is real, too. If you're not handy, this could be a two-hour project. And while the chair itself is sturdy, the included cushion covers aren't removable for washing, which is a maintenance miss.

**Who should buy this**

- **Patio owners with covered or partially covered spaces** who want a statement piece that actually gets used
- **Couples or households with varied body sizes** — the 330-pound capacity covers most adults comfortably
- **Anyone who's previously bought a cheap hanging chair** and watched it rust or sag within a year

Skip it if you have a tiny balcony (the footprint is substantial) or if you're looking for a zero-assembly outdoor chair.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the chair swing or just rotate?**
A: Both. You get a gentle swinging motion from the chain, plus the 360-degree swivel. It's a soothing combo, but if you want a dramatic swing arc, this isn't a playground-style swing — it's a lounger.

**Q: Can I leave it outside in winter?**
A: The frame and rattan will survive, but the cushion won't. Bring the cushion indoors during harsh weather. The metal and woven seat can stay out, though covering it extends the finish life.

**Q: How tall is the stand, and will it fit under a low patio roof?**
A: The stand is about 6.5 feet tall at its highest point. You need clearance of at least 7 feet to mount the chain properly. Measure your covered patio height before ordering.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely sturdy egg chair that won't wobble, rust, or fall apart after one season. The build quality justifies the price, and the comfort is solid once you add a better cushion.

**Skip it if** you're not prepared for a moderately difficult assembly or you have limited patio floor space. This is a substantial piece of furniture, not a lightweight accent chair.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Good bones, honest materials, and a few fixable flaws. The cushion is the only real compromise, and that's an easy solve.
