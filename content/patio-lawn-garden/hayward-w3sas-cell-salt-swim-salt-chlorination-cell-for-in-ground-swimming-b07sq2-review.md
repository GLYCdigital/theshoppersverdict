If your in-ground pool's chlorinator has started flashing the dreaded "low salt" or "inspect cell" light despite a proper salt reading, the problem isn't your water—it's the cell. The Hayward W3SAS-CELL is the direct replacement for the T-15 style cell used in AquaRite and SwimPure systems. But is it worth the premium over generic knockoffs? Here's the honest breakdown after digging through the specs and 225+ owner experiences.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the cell itself, pre-wired with a 5-foot cable and the standard Tri-Clamp union fitting. The unit measures roughly 14 inches long and weighs about 8 pounds—solid, dense, and clearly built to handle a season of harsh pool chemistry. The titanium-coated copper alloy plates are the heart of this unit, and they're noticeably thicker than the plates on budget replacements.

What you don't get: an O-ring or gasket. If yours is worn out from the old cell, you'll need to order one separately. That's a minor annoyance, but worth knowing before you drain the cell housing and find yourself waiting on a $4 part.

## Real-World Performance

Installation is straightforward if you have basic plumbing skills. The universal union fits standard 2-inch PVC, and the electrical plug is keyed to match Hayward's control boards exactly. One buyer noted, *"Plugged right in, no wiring changes, and the system recognized it immediately. No reset needed."* That's the advantage of buying OEM—no compatibility headaches.

In terms of output, this cell generates approximately 1.45 pounds of chlorine per day, which is sufficient for pools up to 40,000 gallons. Owners consistently report consistent chlorine production, even in warmer climates where demand spikes. One long-time user shared, *"Replaced my original after 5 years. This one's been running 18 months and the plates still look clean. My old generic cell looked like a science experiment after one season."*

## What Buyers Love

The overwhelming theme in positive reviews is **reliability and longevity**. Unlike cheap cells that fail after 12-18 months, the Hayward unit is designed to last 5-7 years with proper care. The titanium coating resists the calcium scaling that plagues bare copper cells, especially if you're on hard water.

Another consistent praise point is the *exact fit*. Pool equipment isn't a place for "close enough," and the W3SAS-CELL's tolerances mean no leaks, no loose connections, and no electrical issues. One reviewer put it simply: *"It's the same part my pool builder installed originally. Why would I trust anything else with my pump system?"*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. **This cell requires maintenance.** If you let the plates scale over, chlorine output drops dramatically, and you'll see the "inspect cell" light more often. Cleaning with a diluted acid solution every 3-4 months is non-negotiable, especially in areas with hard water.

Second, the 5-foot cable is short. If your control box is mounted more than a few feet from the plumbing, you'll need to purchase the extension cord separately. That's another $20-30 on top of an already premium-priced part.

Finally, there's the cost. This isn't the cheapest T-15 replacement on the market. Generic cells run $150-200 less. But the tradeoff is clear: you're paying for better materials and a longer lifespan. For most pool owners, the math favors the Hayward if you plan to keep your pool for more than a couple of seasons.

## FAQ

**Q: How often do I actually need to clean this cell?**
A: In normal water conditions, every 3-4 months. If you have hard water (calcium above 400 ppm), plan on every 6-8 weeks. Look for white scale on the plates—that's your signal.

**Q: Will this work with my Hayward AquaRite if I have a different model number?**
A: The W3SAS-CELL replaces the T-15 cell, which is the most common size for in-ground pools. Check your control board's label—if it says T-15, this fits. For T-9 or T-5 systems, you need a different cell.

**Q: Is there a warranty?**
A: Yes, Hayward offers a 1-year limited warranty on this cell. Some retailers offer extended warranties, but the standard coverage is solid for a consumable part.
