Let's be blunt: squirrels are relentless. They chew through plastic feeders, bend aluminum poles, and treat your birdseed like a personal buffet. The OFFO 304 Stainless Steel Squirrel Spikes are designed to end that, and honestly, they mostly deliver.

## What You Actually Get

The package contains pre-assembled spike strips made from 304 stainless steel — not the cheaper 430 grade you'll find on budget options. That distinction matters. 304 is food-grade, marine-resistant stainless. It won't pit or rust after a season of rain, which is the #1 killer of cheaper squirrel deterrents.

Each strip comes pre-formed with sharp, angled prongs that wrap around poles between 1 and 2.5 inches in diameter. The "pre-assembled" claim is real — there's no tedious threading of individual spikes through wire mesh. You unroll, wrap, and secure. That's the whole installation.

## Performance: The Real-World Test

Here's where this product separates itself. The pre-assembled design means the spikes are evenly spaced and consistently angled. When squirrels try to climb past them, the points dig into their paws — not enough to injure, but enough to make the climb genuinely unpleasant. One reviewer put it simply: *"The squirrels tried once and never came back. I watched one jump from the fence, touch the spikes, and nope right off."*

The 304 stainless construction handles weather without complaint. Rain, snow, direct sun — the spikes don't corrode or weaken. The sharpness holds because stainless doesn't dull from moisture exposure like galvanized alternatives.

Installation takes about five minutes per pole. You wrap the strip around, use the integrated tension to hold it in place, and it grips. No tools required for most round poles.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations.

**The spikes are genuinely sharp.** You will cut yourself during installation if you're not careful. Wear gloves. This isn't a design flaw — it's what makes them work — but it's worth knowing before you open the box.

**No flat-surface mounting hardware included.** If you want to attach these to a fence rail, window sill, or flat ledge, you'll need to source your own screws or strong adhesive. The product is designed for poles, and it shows.

**Determined squirrels can sometimes bridge gaps.** If your feeder pole is near a tree or fence, a heavy squirrel can leap from above and bypass the spikes entirely. These work best as part of a multi-layered defense, not a standalone solution.
