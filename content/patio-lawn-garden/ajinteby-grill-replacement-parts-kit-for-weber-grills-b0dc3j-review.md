## What You Actually Get  

Open the box and you'll find the essentials to resurrect a 3-burner grill:  

- **2 cooking grates** — stainless steel, 7mm rod thickness, roughly 21.5" x 17.5" each  
- **4 flavorizer bars** — heavy-gauge stainless, shaped to sit over the burners

That's it. No screws, no manual, no gloves. If you expected hardware, buy a pack of #8-32 bolts separately. The kit is designed to drop into existing holes — as long as your grill's frame isn't bent, it's a 20-minute swap.  

## Fit and Build Quality  

The critical test for any aftermarket kit is fitment. Weber grills have slightly curved flavorizer bars and specific notches in the firebox. AJinTeby's bars match that geometry closely. One reviewer noted: *"Fit perfectly into my Spirit E-310. The bars sat flush, no rocking, and the grates aligned with the frame's ridges."*  

The stainless steel feels substantial — not the flimsy, shiny chrome that rusts after two beach weekends. The 7mm grate rods are a half-millimeter thinner than Weber's OEM 8mm, but buyers report they hold heat well and sear evenly. If you're a weekend warrior grilling 2–3 times a week, this is fine. If you're running a BBQ catering side hustle, spend the extra on OEM.

## FAQ: 3 Questions Shoppers Actually Ask  

**Q: Will this fit my Weber Genesis II E-310?**  
Yes. The kit is designed for the 3-burner Spirit and Genesis II line. Measure your existing grates — if they're roughly 21.5" x 17.5", it'll match.  

**Q: Are these parts porcelain-coated or bare stainless?**  
Bare stainless steel. That's actually better for flavorizer bars — porcelain chips, stainless doesn't. It'll discolor with heat, which is normal.  

**Q: Can I use this on a natural gas Weber?**  
Yes. The parts are ignition-agnostic. The burners, valves, and gas lines are separate — this kit only replaces grates and heat shields.
