If your fall routine involves raking leaves into piles, wrestling them into bags, and repeating until your back files a formal complaint — stop. The Agri-Fab 45-0546 is a 52-inch tow-behind sweeper that straps onto your lawn tractor and turns that whole miserable process into a few lazy loops around the yard.

I've spent time with this sweeper, and here's the honest story.

## What's Actually in the Box

This bundle includes the Agri-Fab 45-0546 sweeper plus its mounting hardware kit. You get the main sweeper frame, the 52-inch brush housing, the 200-pound-capacity hopper, the hitch assembly, and a bag of bolts, washers, and cotter pins that will test your patience. The brush is a full 52 inches wide, which is the headline feature — it's 10 inches wider than the popular 42-inch models, meaning one pass covers noticeably more ground.

The hopper holds up to 200 pounds of debris and dumps via a pull-cord release from the tractor seat. That's a genuinely useful feature — no dismounting every ten minutes.

## Real-World Performance

The sweeper does its best work on dry grass and fall leaves. The brush spins fast enough to flick debris into the hopper with authority, and the 52-inch width eats up lawn quickly. On a half-acre lot, I finished in about 20 minutes of driving time, versus a solid two hours of raking.

Build quality is solid where it counts: the steel frame is heavy, the brush bristles are stiff and durable, and the hopper fabric is a tear-resistant mesh that holds up to repeated dumping. Small parts like the height-adjustment lever feel flimsy, but they work.

The height adjustment is a bright spot — a single lever at the rear changes brush depth, so you can dial it in for bare ground, short grass, or thicker turf without tools. That matters, because getting the brush height right is the difference between "picks everything up" and "leaves a trail of debris."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 200 Amazon ratings average 4.0 stars, and the pattern in reviews is clear. Owners with dry lawns and moderate leaf loads love the time savings. One reviewer noted, "This thing picks up leaves, twigs, and grass clippings like a vacuum. My yard looks like a golf course after one pass." Another praised the wide brush: "The 52-inch width is the reason I bought this — I cut my sweep time almost in half compared to my old 40-inch."

The pull-cord dump mechanism gets consistent shout-outs. Dumping without leaving the tractor seat is a huge deal when you're doing 15 loads per session.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides. Wet leaves are this sweeper's kryptonite. If you try to pick up damp, matted leaves, the brush just pushes them across the lawn and packs them into a smear. You'll need to wait for a dry day — that's a real limitation for fall cleanup in rainy climates.

The frame flexes on uneven ground. On bumpy terrain, the outside edges of the brush lift off the turf, leaving a ragged strip. It's manageable, but if your lawn has dips and swales, you'll be making second passes.

Assembly is the other common complaint. The manual claims 30 minutes; plan for 90 with a second person. Some holes didn't align perfectly on my unit, and I had to coax bolts through with a rubber mallet.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Homeowners with ½ to 2-acre lawns** who mow with a tractor and want fall cleanup done in minutes, not weekends.
- **People with dry, mostly-level terrain** — this is where it shines.
- **Skip it if your lawn is perpetually damp**, heavily sloped, or if you're expecting it to double as a dethatcher for thick, wet debris.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this attach to any lawn tractor?**
A: It uses a standard 1-1/4-inch hitch pin. Most riding mowers and lawn tractors will accept it, but check your tractor's hitch clearance first. Universal hitch kits exist if yours doesn't match.

**Q: Will it pick up pine needles and acorns?**
A: Dry pine needles yes, but they can clog the hopper opening if you're hitting a heavy concentration. Acorns will bounce off — it's a sweeper, not a vacuum. For acorns, you'll want to rake first or accept they stay put.

**Q: How do I store this in winter?**
A: Fold the handle down and stand it upright against a wall. It takes up roughly 3×4 feet of floor space. Protect the brush from moisture — it'll last years if you keep it dry.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you have a dry, reasonably flat lawn, a tractor, and you're tired of raking. The 52-inch width and 200-pound hopper are genuinely effective time-savers.

**Skip it if** your property is wet, hilly, or heavily treed with debris that stays damp. You'll find yourself fighting the sweeper more than using it.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid, capable equipment with real limitations. It won't replace a leaf vacuum for tough jobs, but for routine fall cleanup, it's a workout-saver that pays for itself in saved weekends.
