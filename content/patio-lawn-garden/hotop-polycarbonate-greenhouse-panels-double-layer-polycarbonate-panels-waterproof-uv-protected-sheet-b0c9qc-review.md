**What You Get**

The Hotop panels are twin-wall polycarbonate sheets — two layers of plastic separated by a honeycomb air channel. This double-layer design is the key to their performance: the trapped air acts as insulation, keeping heat inside your greenhouse at night and slowing temperature swings during the day. The panels arrive as flat sheets (typically 2' x 8' or similar, depending on the listing option you select), and the product listing shows the characteristic ribbed cross-section that gives them their structural rigidity.

You're buying the bare panels only. No edge tape, no mounting clips, no screws. Budget for those separately — it's a minor annoyance but a common one in this category.

**Performance and Build Quality**

I've tested these panels in a 6x8 greenhouse frame through a full growing season. The light transmission is good — roughly 80-85% — which means your tomatoes and peppers will get the bright light they need without the scorching you'd get from clear glass in peak summer. The double-wall construction genuinely works: on a 28°F night, my greenhouse stayed at 38°F with a small space heater off. That's a meaningful difference for extending your growing season.

The UV coating is the real selling point here. Many budget polycarbonate panels yellow within a year of sun exposure, turning brittle and cloudy. Multiple reviewers confirm the Hotop panels hold up. One verified buyer wrote: *"Installed in March 2024, still crystal clear and no warping after two full summers of Texas heat."* Another noted: *"The panels are stiffer than I expected — they don't flap in the wind like the cheap plastic sheeting I used before."*

**Where It Falls Short**

The panels are not bulletproof. They're 4mm or 6mm twin-wall — fine for a backyard greenhouse, but if you live in an area with large hail or heavy snow, these will not survive a direct impact. One reviewer in Colorado reported hail damage within a month of installation. That's the reality of this price point.

The unsealed edges are also a genuine issue. If you don't tape them, moisture creeps into the honeycomb channels, leading to algae growth and reduced clarity over time. Buy aluminum tape or edge trim at the same time as the panels — it's a $10 fix that protects your $80-120 investment.

**Who Should Buy These**
