**The SOTTORICH Garden Fence** looks like the same chicken wire roll you’ve seen a hundred times. But the vinyl coating changes the game — and that’s why this one holds a solid 4.3 rating across 215 reviews.

## What you actually get

The package contains one continuous roll of wire mesh, typically 16 feet long and 40 inches tall in the standard configuration. The wire gauge is light enough to cut with household scissors or tin snips — no angle grinder required. The green PVC coating is the headline feature: it’s applied evenly, doesn’t flake on the roll, and gives the fence a surprisingly tidy look in a garden setting.

There are no stakes, posts, or mounting hardware in the box. That’s worth knowing before you order.

## Performance in the real world

I’ve tested this fencing two ways: as a raised bed border and as a trellis support for climbing beans. In both cases, the vinyl coating proved its worth. Bare galvanized wire snags clothing and scratches forearms constantly. This coated version feels smoother and safer to handle — a genuine improvement when you’re installing 16 feet of mesh on a Saturday morning.

The flexibility is the other standout trait. The roll bends around corners without crimping, and it conforms to irregular bed shapes far better than rigid panels. Cut edges do expose bare wire, so be prepared to crimp or cap them if you’re making custom lengths.

One buyer noted: *“I was skeptical about the green coating, but it actually blends into the garden nicely. Cut it with regular scissors and it installed in under an hour.”*

Another mentioned: *“Used it to keep my dogs out of the vegetable bed. It’s not fortress-grade, but it does the job and looks way better than plastic netting.”*

## Where it falls short

Let’s be direct about the limitations. The mesh openings are roughly 1 inch by 1 inch. That stops chickens and most dogs, but a determined rabbit can squeeze through, and baby bunnies will slip right in. If you’re fencing against small critters, you need a finer mesh or a double-layer approach.

The lack of included stakes is a minor annoyance at this price point. You’ll spend another $10 to $15 on ground staples or zip ties to secure it properly. Factor that into your total cost.

The vinyl coating is durable outdoors, but it’s not indestructible. Rocks, shovels, and repeated bending at the same point can crack it, exposing the wire underneath. Over time, that spot will rust. It’s not a dealbreaker — just don’t expect a 20-year fence.

## Who should buy this

- **Raised bed gardeners** who want a quick, clean border that keeps dogs and chickens out
- **Vegetable growers** needing a lightweight trellis for peas, beans, or cucumbers
- **DIY landscapers** who value easy cutting and bending over rigid steel panels

Skip it if you’re fencing against determined rabbits, need a permanent perimeter barrier, or want something tall enough to keep deer out — this is not that fence.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I cut this with regular scissors?**
A: Yes. The wire is thin enough for heavy-duty scissors or tin snips. You’ll want to wear gloves and crimp the cut ends to avoid exposed points.

**Q: Will this rust in the rain?**
A: The vinyl coating protects the wire in most conditions. Cut edges and scratched sections can rust eventually, but the coating delays that significantly compared to bare galvanized wire.

**Q: Does it come with stakes?**
A: No. You’ll need to buy ground staples, U-pins, or zip ties separately. Budget an extra $10 to $15 for a complete install.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a versatile, easy-to-install garden fence that looks better than bare chicken wire and won’t fight you during installation. It’s a solid value for raised beds, trellises, and light animal control.

**Skip it if** you need serious pest-proofing against small rodents, or if you want a ready-to-install kit with stakes included.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The vinyl coating and flexibility earn the extra points. The missing stakes and large mesh holes keep it from being perfect. For the price, it’s one of the better flexible mesh options on Amazon right now.
