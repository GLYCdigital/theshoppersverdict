## Is the Heyroll 0.8-Inch Turf Worth Your Money?

Most artificial grass under $1 per square foot looks like a green doormat — flat, shiny, and obviously fake. The Heyroll 0.8-inch turf sits a notch above that bargain-bin category, but it's not pretending to be the $4-per-foot contractor-grade stuff either. At 4.6 stars from 229 ratings, it's clearly satisfying most buyers. Here's who should care.

## What You Actually Get

The roll ships compressed and vacuum-packed. When you unroll it, expect it to need a few hours in the sun to relax and shed the crease lines. The 0.8-inch pile height is the headline spec — that's a third shorter than premium turf but noticeably plusher than the 0.5-inch mats. Each blade is a "C-shape" with a slight curl at the tip, which does a decent job mimicking real fescue rather than the straight, needle-like blades on cheap turf. The backing is a standard polyurethane-coated mesh with pre-drilled drainage holes spaced every few inches.

## Real-World Performance

The 37-ounce face weight is where this turf earns its keep. That's the density of the yarn per square yard, and it translates directly to how well the grass stands up. Set a grill on it, run a wheelbarrow over it, let a 70-pound dog sprint across it — the blades spring back. One reviewer noted, "My German Shepherd tears up real grass in a day. This stuff looks untouched after a month." That's the use case this product nails.

Drainage is genuinely functional. Water passes through the holes in seconds, not minutes. You can hose it down to rinse pet waste and it won't pool. For indoor use on a balcony or sunroom, that's a non-negotiable feature.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The appearance is the most-cited win. Multiple reviewers used the phrase "looks real from a distance" — and that's the honest bar for turf at this price. The dual-tone green (a mix of light and dark blades) breaks up the monotone look that gives cheap turf away. One five-star review said, "My neighbors asked what grass seed I used. I told them it's plastic and they didn't believe me until they touched it." Another mentioned it perfectly covered an ugly concrete patio for under $100.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest complaints surface repeatedly. First, the smell. Fresh out of the package, the rubber backing has a distinct chemical odor. In open air, it fades in 3-5 days. Inside a screened porch or enclosed balcony, budget for a week of ventilation. Second, cutting is fiddly. The mesh backing doesn't tear along a straight line — you need a sharp blade, a straightedge, and patience. Ragged cuts leave visible fraying at seams. Buy a fresh utility knife blade before you start.

One more thing: the turf arrives folded, not rolled. The fold lines can take a full day of sun to fully relax. If you're installing it the same day it arrives, plan accordingly.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Pet owners** with a muddy side yard or balcony — the drainage and cleanability are the top reasons buyers chose this over the cheaper mats.
- **Renters** covering an ugly concrete patio or deck without making permanent changes. It lays flat with double-sided tape and removes cleanly.
- **DIY balcony gardeners** who want a green base for potted plants without the upkeep.

Skip it if you need golf-green putting performance, if you're installing over large areas where seams will be highly visible, or if you're extremely sensitive to initial chemical odors.

## FAQ

**Does it feel like real grass?**
No — it's softer than real grass but has a springy, cushioned feel. Barefoot-friendly, but you'll know it's synthetic. The blades are not as soft as premium turf with sand infill.

**Can I use it indoors?**
Yes, but the smell is the caveat. In a well-ventilated room, it's fine after a few days. For a kids' playroom, consider airing it out in the garage first.

**How do I clean pet waste off it?**
Pick up solids, hose down the spot, and let the drainage holes do their job. A 50/50 vinegar-water spray kills odor without damaging the backing.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a realistic-looking, pet-proof ground cover for a patio, balcony, or small yard at a reasonable price. It punches above its weight class in density and drainage.

**Skip it if** you're laying more than 200 square feet where seams will be noticeable, or you need it usable the same day it arrives.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — This is the sweet spot for budget-conscious shoppers who want real curb appeal without contractor pricing. Handle the smell and the cutting with care, and it'll outlast your patience for maintaining real grass.
