# Intex 26645EG Krystal Clear 2100 GPH Sand Filter Pump: The Budget Workhorse That Actually Filters

Let's be blunt: most sand filter pumps under $300 are junk. They move water but don't filter, or they filter but leak like a sieve. The Intex 26645EG Krystal Clear sits in a crowded field of me-too pumps, yet with 210 ratings averaging 4.2 stars, it's clearly doing something right. After digging through owner feedback and spec sheets, here's the real story.

## What's Actually in the Box

The 26645EG ships as a complete system, not a bare pump. You get the pump motor housing, a 16-inch sand tank, the six-position control valve, two 1.25-inch hoses with clamps, and a bag of pool-grade #20 silica sand. That last part matters — many competitors make you buy sand separately. The unit stands about 30 inches tall assembled and weighs roughly 45 pounds when filled, so plan for a two-person carry to your pool deck.

The filter tank holds 50 pounds of sand, which is the sweet spot for above-ground pools up to 18,000 gallons. For reference, that covers most rectangular 15-foot by 48-inch pools and all 12-foot round models.

## Performance: Where It Earns the 4.2 Stars

The headline spec is 2,100 gallons per hour. That's not marketing fluff — the pump genuinely moves that volume at zero head pressure, which translates to a full turnover of a 15,000-gallon pool in about seven hours. Most owners report visibly clearer water within 24 to 48 hours of first run, with algae cloudiness gone by day three.

The six-position valve is the star of the show. Filter mode runs normal filtration; backwash mode reverses flow to flush trapped debris out the waste line; rinse settles the sand bed after backwashing; recirculate bypasses the sand for skimming; waste pumps water out without filtering (handy for lowering pool level); and closed shuts everything down. Owners consistently report the valve handle seats firmly and doesn't leak — a common failure point on cheaper Intex models.

One thing I appreciate: the pump primes quickly. Even after winterizing, it pulls water within 30 to 60 seconds, no manual priming needed. That's a practical convenience you'll notice every spring.

## What Owners Praise (and What They Grumble About)

Positive feedback centers on value and simplicity. One verified buyer put it well: *"I was skeptical at this price, but it cleared my 16-foot Intex pool in two days. No leaks, quiet enough to run at night."* Another noted, *"The instructions are actually clear, and the sand bag included means you don't need a second trip to the store."* The quiet operation deserves emphasis — it's noticeably quieter than comparable Hayward pumps, though not library-quiet.

Now the honest downsides. The hose barbs and plastic clamps are the weak link. Multiple reviewers report cracking the fittings when tightening clamps with pliers — hand-tighten only. Also, there's no timer. You'll need to buy an external outlet timer (about $15) to automate daily filtration cycles, which is an added cost and an extra point of failure.

One more consideration: the pump is rated for above-ground pools only. Don't even think about using it for an in-ground setup; the fittings and housing aren't built for that duty cycle.

## Who Should Buy This

This is the right pump if you own an above-ground pool between 8,000 and 18,000 gallons and want reliable filtration without spending $500+. It's also ideal for first-time pool owners who want straightforward setup — the entire install takes about 30 minutes with basic hand tools.

Skip it if you have a larger pool (over 18,000 gallons), need automated filtration scheduling out of the box, or plan to run it 24/7 for months on end. For continuous duty, step up to a Hayward or Pentair.

## FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: How often should I backwash?**
A: When the pressure gauge reads 8-10 PSI above clean baseline (usually every 1-2 weeks), backwash for 2-3 minutes, then rinse for 30 seconds. Add fresh sand every 3-5 years.

**Q: Can I use glass media instead of sand?**
A: Yes, but it's not necessary. The included sand works fine, and glass media costs 3x more for marginal improvement. Save your money.

**Q: Does it work with an Intex Easy Set pool?**
A: Yes, if your pool has a 1.25-inch hose connection. Older models with 1.5-inch hoses need an adapter, sold separately.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You need dependable sand filtration for an above-ground pool up to 18,000 gallons and want the complete kit with sand included. The 4.2-star rating reflects real-world reliability.
