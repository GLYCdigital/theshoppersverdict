Most hammocks follow the same formula: a strip of fabric, two ropes, and a prayer that the knots hold. The SONGMICS Padded Double Hammock tries something different — quilted padding and curved spreader bars that keep the bed open. After digging through 210 ratings and the spec sheet, here's the real story for shoppers considering this for their patio or backyard.

**What you actually get**

The box includes the quilted hammock body (about 78 inches long, 55 inches wide when open), two detachable curved spreader bars, two 9.8-foot tree straps, and two carabiners. The straps are the highlight — they wrap around trees up to about 19 inches in diameter and feature multiple loops for adjusting height and sag. No separate hardware purchase needed, which isn't always the case at this price point.

The fabric is a polyester-cotton blend with a quilted pattern stitched through. It's not just decorative — the padding gives real cushioning, roughly equivalent to a thin mattress pad. The curved spreader bars keep the hammock spread open at both ends, so you can sit down without the fabric bunching around your legs.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The stitching on the quilted pattern is consistent, and the fabric feels substantial rather than flimsy. The spreader bars are aluminum with a matte finish, and they lock into fabric sleeves at each end. Detaching them takes about 30 seconds — useful when you want to fold the hammock flat for storage.

Setup is straightforward: wrap the straps, clip the carabiners, adjust the length. The curved bars create a stable, open bed that's noticeably easier to get into than a traditional gathered-end hammock. Once you're in, the padding distributes pressure well across your back and shoulders. For two people, the 450-pound weight capacity is realistic — the fabric and bars handle the load without visible strain.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Owners frequently mention the comfort level relative to price. One reviewer noted, *"I was skeptical about the padding, but it's genuinely soft — I've fallen asleep in it three times this week."* Another called out the straps specifically: *"Finally a hammock that comes with everything. The straps are thick and the carabiners feel solid."* The detachable spreader bars also earn consistent appreciation — buyers like that the hammock stores flat without taking up half the garage.

**Where it falls short**

The quilted fabric is a double-edged sword. It's comfortable, but it retains heat. Multiple reviewers mention getting sweaty on warm days, especially without tree cover. If you're shopping for hot, humid climates, a breathable rope hammock will serve you better.

The spreader bars also add weight — roughly 3 pounds more than a comparable gathered-end hammock. It's not a dealbreaker for a backyard, but if you plan to pack this for camping or beach trips, you'll feel the difference. One buyer put it plainly: *"Great for the yard, but I wouldn't want to carry it far."*

**Who should buy this**

- **Backyard loungers** who want easy entry and actual padding over a traditional hammock
- **Older adults or anyone with mobility issues** — the open bed design is far easier to get in and out of than a rope hammock
- **First-time hammock buyers** who want everything included without researching straps and hardware separately

**Skip it if** you need a packable hammock for hiking, live somewhere intensely hot, or prefer the cocoon-like feel of a gathered-end design.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can two adults actually fit comfortably?**
Yes, but it's snug. The 55-inch width accommodates two average-sized adults; the curved bars keep the fabric from wrapping too tightly. For larger builds, consider it a generous single or a cozy double.

**Q: Does it come with tree straps?**
Yes — two 9.8-foot straps with multiple adjustment loops and carabiners are included. No additional purchase needed.

**Q: How do you clean it?**
The fabric is machine-washable on a gentle cycle. Detach the spreader bars first, then wash cold and air dry. Do not machine dry — the padding can clump.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a comfortable, easy-entry hammock for your yard or deck and don't want to piece together straps and hardware. **Skip it if** you prioritize breathability in hot weather or need something packable for travel.

The SONGMICS Padded Double Hammock delivers on its core promise: quilted comfort with a stable, open design. At 4.2/5, it's a solid pick for casual lounging, with tradeoffs that are clear before you buy. For the right buyer, this is a genuinely comfortable addition to any outdoor space.
