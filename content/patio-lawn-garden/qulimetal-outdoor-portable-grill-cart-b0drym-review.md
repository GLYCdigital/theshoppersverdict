Let’s be blunt: most portable grill carts under $100 are flimsy sheet-metal afterthoughts that wobble the moment you put a hot grill on them. The QuliMetal Outdoor Portable Grill Cart isn’t that — but it’s also not a permanent patio fixture. Here’s who should buy it, who should skip it, and what 220 Amazon buyers are saying.

## What You Actually Get

The cart ships flat in a 20-pound box. Inside: a welded steel frame, two folding side shelves, four caster wheels (two locking), and a small hardware bag. Assembly takes about 20 minutes with a Phillips screwdriver — no wrench needed, which is rare at this price point. The top grate measures 21.5” x 17.5”, so it fits most Kamado Joe, Weber, and Pit Boss portable grills up to 50 lbs. As shown in the product image, the side shelves fold down flat when not in use, and the whole unit collapses to roughly 5 inches thick — thin enough to slide behind a shed door or stand upright in a garage corner.

## Build Quality & Real-World Use

The frame is 1.2mm cold-rolled steel with a matte black powder coat. It’s not commercial-grade, but it’s noticeably sturdier than the mesh-and-tube carts you’ll find at big-box stores. The locking side shelves click into place with a simple pin mechanism — no tools needed once assembled. The wheels are 3-inch casters; they roll smoothly over pavers and deck boards, but the front wheels are smaller than the rear, which means the cart tracks slightly when you push it loaded. 

One thing that surprised me: the cart is genuinely stable with a heavy grill on top. The legs spread wider than the top surface (about 24” at the base), which keeps the center of gravity low. Multiple reviewers confirmed this — one wrote, *“I put my 40-lb Kamado Joe on it and it doesn’t wobble at all, even on my uneven patio.”* Another said, *“The folding shelves are actually useful — I can prep right next to the grill without a separate table.”*

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The dominant theme in positive reviews is **value for the price** and **portability**. People are using this for tailgating, camping, and as a secondary prep station next to a built-in grill. One reviewer summed it up: *“I’ve bought $150 carts that felt cheaper than this. The steel is thick, the welds are clean, and it folds up smaller than my camping chair.”* The folding mechanism gets specific praise — it locks open with a sliding collar that doesn’t require pinching your fingers.

## Where It Falls Short

You’ll find two consistent complaints in the 4.4-star rating breakdown:

1. **Wheels don’t lock reliably.** The two locking casters work, but they only lock the wheel rotation — not the swivel. On a sloped driveway or uneven grass, the cart can drift sideways. If your patio has any incline, plan to chock a wheel or position it against a wall.

2. **Hardware is fiddly.** The bolts are M6 metric, and the included Allen wrench is fine, but the screws are small enough that you’ll drop one or two during assembly. Order a spare set or use a magnetic tray. Also, the powder coat scratches if you drag the cart across concrete — lift it instead of sliding it.

A less common but notable gripe: the side shelves are rated for only 15 lbs each. They’re fine for a cutting board or a platter of raw meat, but don’t stack a full cooler on them.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Tailgaters and campers** who need a stable, foldable platform for a 20–40 lb grill.
- **Small-patio renters** who can’t install a permanent grill station and want something that disappears between cooks.
- **Kamado or pellet grill owners** who need a secondary cart for prep or serving.

**Skip it if** you have a permanently sloped yard (the wheel issue will annoy you daily) or if you plan to leave the cart outside year-round — the powder coat will fade and rust eventually, though that’s true of anything at this price.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit a Weber 22” kettle grill?**  
A: No. The top grate is 21.5” x 17.5”, and a 22” kettle’s legs spread wider than that. It fits 18” kettles and most kamado-style grills up to 50 lbs.

**Q: Can I leave it assembled outside?**  
A: You can, but the powder coat isn’t UV-stable long-term. Cover it or store it indoors if you want it to last more than two seasons.

**Q: Does it come with a grill, or just the cart?**  
A: Just the cart. You supply the grill. The box includes the frame, shelves, wheels, and hardware only.
