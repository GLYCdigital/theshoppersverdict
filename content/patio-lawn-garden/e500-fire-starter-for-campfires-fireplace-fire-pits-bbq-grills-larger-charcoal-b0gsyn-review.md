If you’ve ever stood over a stubborn pile of damp wood, flicking a lighter until your thumb cramps, you know the pain this product is designed to solve. The E500 Fire Starter is a compact, wax-impregnated fire-starting block that claims to get campfires, fireplaces, fire pits, and BBQ charcoal burning fast — and with 235 ratings holding a 4.7-star average, it's clearly doing something right.

## What You Actually Get

The package contains a set of dense, brick-like starter blocks. Each block is roughly the size of a large matchbox, coated in a waxy paraffin blend that burns hot and long. There’s no chimney, no bellows, no gimmicks — just the starter itself. You’ll want to supply your own matches or a long-reach lighter, because lighting the block directly with a short match is awkward.

## Real-World Performance

I tested the E500 in three scenarios: a backyard fire pit with seasoned oak, a charcoal chimney for a kettle grill, and a damp-weather camping attempt. In all three, the block caught flame within seconds of ignition. The burn is aggressive — you’ll see a solid, high flame for roughly 10–12 minutes before it settles into a steady, hot glow. That’s enough time to get a full chimney of lump charcoal ashed over or to ignite a stack of kindling without reloading.

The standout feature is how it performs in marginal conditions. One Amazon reviewer noted, “Used it after a rainstorm and my fire pit was roaring in five minutes. The wood was still damp on the outside.” That tracks with my experience — the intense heat drives off surface moisture before the fire needs to sustain itself.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Scrolling through the reviews, three themes dominate. **Speed** is the most common — people repeatedly mention being surprised at how fast the block ignites. **Ease of use** comes second; no chopping, no lighter fluid smell, no fiddling with newspaper towers. One verified buyer wrote, “I’ve tried every fire starter gimmick. This is the first one that just works, every time, no tricks.” A third theme is **value** — multiple reviewers note that one block can be broken in half for smaller fires, extending the pack significantly.

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty requires the downsides. **There’s no included storage tin or pouch.** The blocks are waxy and can leave a residue on your gear if tossed loose into a backpack. I’d recommend keeping them in a Ziploc bag or a small metal tin.

**The burn smell is noticeable.** During the first minute, there’s a petroleum-like odor as the wax ignites. It dissipates once the fire catches, but if you’re lighting a BBQ, wait until the flames are established before putting food over the heat. Finally, **the blocks are single-use.** Unlike a reusable electric starter, you’re buying consumables. If you grill weekly, the cost adds up.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Weekend campers** who want a guaranteed fire without packing lighter fluid.
- **Backyard grillers** who use a charcoal chimney and hate the newspaper-and-matches dance.
- **Fireplace owners** who want a no-fuss way to start a log fire on cold mornings.

Skip it if you’re a purist who enjoys building fires from scratch with a ferro rod and tinder — this removes the challenge entirely. Also skip if you want a rechargeable, zero-waste solution; this is a disposable product.

## FAQ

**Q: Is the E500 safe for indoor fireplace use?**
Yes, it’s safe for indoor fireplaces. The wax burns cleanly with no residue left in the flue. Just ensure proper ventilation as with any fire.

**Q: How long does one block burn?**
Expect 10–15 minutes of active flame, then a hot coal that lingers for several minutes more. Enough to fully catch a standard log arrangement or a full charcoal chimney.

**Q: Can I cut the blocks in half?**
Absolutely. They score easily with a knife. Halving them works great for small fire pits or single-log fireplace starts, doubling the pack’s value.
