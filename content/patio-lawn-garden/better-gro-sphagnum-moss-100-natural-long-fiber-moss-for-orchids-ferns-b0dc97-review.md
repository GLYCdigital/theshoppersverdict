## What you actually get

The bag contains 100% natural long-fiber sphagnum moss — no additives, no perlite, no mystery fillers. The fibers are visibly long and intact, ranging from roughly 3 to 8 inches in length. ## FAQ

**Q: Does it need to be soaked before use?**
Yes, absolutely. Submerge in warm water for 10-15 minutes, then squeeze out excess. Dry moss won't take up water properly and can damage roots.

**Q: Can I reuse it after repotting an orchid?**
Yes, unlike bark, sphagnum can be rinsed, squeezed, and reused once. After the second use, replace it — the fibers break down and water retention drops.

**Q: Is this safe for carnivorous plants?**
Yes. It's naturally low in nutrients and acidic, which is exactly what Venus flytraps and pitcher plants need.
