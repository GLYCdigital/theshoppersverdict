**Bottom line: The YEFU Fire Pit delivers 85% of the Solo Stove experience at a fraction of the price — if you can live with a smaller burn chamber.**

## First Impressions

The YEFU Fire Pit with Removable Ash Pan enters a crowded market of smokeless fire pits, and it's clearly aimed at budget-conscious buyers who want the double-wall airflow technology without the $300+ price tag of the big names. The 304 stainless steel construction is the headline feature here — that's the same marine-grade material used in premium grills, and it's a legit upgrade over the 201-grade steel found on cheaper knockoffs.

## What You Actually Get

The unit arrives mostly assembled. You're looking at the main burn chamber, a removable ash pan that slides out from the bottom, and a spark screen that sits on top. There's no stand included — the fire pit sits directly on the ground or your patio stones. The dimensions are compact: the fire bowl is roughly 18 inches in diameter, which is noticeably smaller than the 19.5-inch Solo Stove Bonfire. That difference matters more than you'd think.

## Real-World Performance

The smokeless technology works — but with caveats. The double-wall design pulls air through the bottom vents and recirculates it, burning off the smoke particles that would otherwise drift into your face. I tested this on a breezy evening, and once the fire established a good bed of coals (about 8-10 minutes in), the smoke reduction was dramatic. Several reviewers echo this: *"I was skeptical but the smoke really does go away once it gets hot enough."* The secondary burn ring at the top produces those satisfying blue-orange flames that look great.

The removable ash pan is the standout feature. After a fire, you pull the pan out, dump it, and you're done. No tipping the whole unit over, no scraping ash from the bottom. This is a genuine quality-of-life improvement that even some premium pits lack.

## Where It Falls Short

The small fire bowl is the biggest tradeoff. Standard 16-inch logs need to be broken or cut down to fit. You'll be adding wood every 20-30 minutes to maintain a good flame, which makes this more of a conversation pit than a long-burn fire source. If you're planning a 4-hour campfire session, bring a log splitter.

The lack of a carry handle is a head-scratcher. At roughly 25 pounds, it's not heavy, but it gets awkward once you're moving it with hot ash inside. And a few reviewers noted the paint on the spark screen started to discolor after repeated use — cosmetic, not functional, but worth knowing.

## Who Should Buy This

- **First-time fire pit owners** who want smokeless technology without the premium markup
- **Patio or small deck users** who want a compact, portable option for 2-4 people
- **Cleanup-conscious buyers** who hate dealing with ash — the removable pan is a game-changer

**Skip it if** you're hosting bigger gatherings, want a long-burn fire for hours, or need something heavy-duty for frequent, high-heat use. The compact size will frustrate you.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the ash pan really make cleanup easier?**
Yes. It slides out cleanly, holds a decent amount of ash, and you can dump it directly into a trash bag. No scraping required. Just make sure the fire is completely out and cool before removing it.

**Q: Can I cook over this fire pit?**
Technically, yes — the spark screen can hold a grill grate. But the small diameter means you're limited on cooking space, and the high heat output can scorch food if you're not careful. It's better for roasting marshmallows than searing steaks.

**Q: How does it handle wind?**
The double-wall design actually helps with wind resistance — the airflow channels keep the fire stable. That said, strong gusts can still blow smoke sideways before the secondary burn kicks in.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want genuinely smokeless performance, easy cleanup, and solid 304 stainless construction without spending premium money. It's an excellent value for patio users with modest fire needs.

**Skip it if** you need a large fire for entertaining, want to burn full-size logs without cutting them, or require a built-in stand and carry handle.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The YEFU Fire Pit earns its score through smart design and quality materials. The small burn chamber and missing handle keep it from being a perfect recommendation, but for the price point, it's a compelling choice that outperforms its budget category.
