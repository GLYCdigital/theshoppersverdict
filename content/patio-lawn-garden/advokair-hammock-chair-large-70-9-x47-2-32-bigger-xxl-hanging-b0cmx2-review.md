## The XXL Hammock Chair That Actually Delivers on "Big"

Most hammock chairs claim to be "large" and then arrive barely wide enough for a teenager. The ADVOKAIR XXL isn't that. At 70.9 inches long and 47.2 inches wide, this thing is genuinely oversized — 32% bigger than standard hanging chairs, and you feel it the moment you sit down. The fabric wraps around you instead of squeezing you.

This isn't a gimmick size. The dimensions translate to real comfort: a 6'2" adult can sit cross-legged, or you can fit two smaller kids side-by-side. That's the headline here, and it's why this chair holds a 4.6/5 rating across 229 reviews.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the chair itself (cotton canvas, not the cheap polyester blend many competitors use), a wooden spreader bar, and the hanging rope with a carabiner clip. That's it. No ceiling mount, no stand, no hardware kit.

This is the single biggest gotcha. You'll need to buy a mounting solution separately — either a ceiling hook rated for 300+ lbs or a freestanding hammock stand. Budget an extra $30–50 before you hit checkout. Several reviewers were caught off guard by this, so plan ahead.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The cotton canvas is thick — noticeably heavier than the $30 hammock chairs you'll find on Amazon. The double-stitched seams run the full length of the stress points, and the fabric loops where the rope attaches are reinforced with extra layers. This matters because that's where cheap chairs fail first.

The spreader bar is solid wood, not the hollow aluminum you'll see on budget options. It keeps the chair open and stable — no annoying spinning when you sit down, no wobble once you're settled. The rope is thick and feels secure when you clip in.

One thing to know: this chair is **heavy**. The cotton canvas alone weighs around 5–6 lbs, plus the wooden bar. That's fine for a sturdy stand or a properly installed ceiling mount, but don't hang it from a flimsy porch beam or a drywall anchor. You need a solid structural point.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The comfort factor comes up constantly. One reviewer wrote, *"I'm 240 lbs and was skeptical, but this holds me easily with room to spare. My kids fight over it now."* Another noted, *"The fabric is soft but sturdy — my cat's claws haven't damaged it after three months."*

The size is the recurring theme. Multiple reviewers mention using it for reading, napping, and even working on a laptop. The wide seat lets you curl up sideways, which is impossible in standard-width hammock chairs.

## Where It Falls Short

The cotton fabric is a double-edged sword. It's comfortable and breathable, but it wrinkles badly and shows dust and pet hair. If you plan to keep this outdoors uncovered, expect it to fade and collect grime within a season. This is a covered-patio or indoor chair, not a full-weather piece.

The lack of included hardware is the other real complaint. At this price point — and given that ADVOKAIR markets it as a complete setup — you'd expect at least a ceiling hook. You don't get one. Factor that into your total cost.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a taller or larger adult who's been squeezed out of standard hammock chairs. You want a chair that fits two kids comfortably. You have a covered patio, sunroom, or indoor space and want something that reads more "furniture" than "camping gear."

**Skip it if:** You need an all-weather chair that stays outside year-round — the cotton won't hold up. You're on a tight budget and don't want to spend extra on mounting hardware. You want a lightweight chair you can move around easily; this isn't that.
