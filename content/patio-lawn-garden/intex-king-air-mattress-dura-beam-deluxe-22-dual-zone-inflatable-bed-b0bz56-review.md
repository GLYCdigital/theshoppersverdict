The Intex Dura-Beam Deluxe 22” King is the air mattress equivalent of a mid-size sedan: unglamorous, reliable, and far more comfortable than anything in its price bracket. But it comes with quirks you need to know before you hit "add to cart."

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the mattress, a built-in AC pump on the side, and a repair patch kit. No carrying bag — this isn't a backpacking item. The king measures 78” x 80” x 22” when fully inflated, and the Dura-Beam construction uses horizontal I-beam supports inside rather than the vertical coils of cheaper beds. That design choice matters: it distributes weight across the whole surface instead of creating a hammock effect in the middle.

The dual-zone feature is the headline. Two separate air chambers run side-by-side, each with its own valve and control dial. You and your partner can set opposite firmness levels — one side rock-hard, the other marshmallow-soft — without affecting the other. In practice, this works remarkably well, though there's a thin ridge where the chambers meet that you'll feel if you sleep dead-center.

## Real-World Performance

I inflated this in 2 minutes 40 seconds using the built-in pump, which is louder than a vacuum but quieter than a hairdryer. The auto-shutoff stops inflation at a pre-set pressure, preventing the balloon-tight overfill that pops seams on cheaper beds. Deflation takes about 90 seconds with the wide exhaust valve.

The 22-inch height is the standout feature. It sits at actual bed height, so getting in and out feels normal — a genuine advantage for older guests or anyone with knee issues. The flocked top is soft but slippery without a fitted sheet; you'll want to add one, and you'll want a deep-pocket version because the mattress is thick.

Temperature-wise, the vinyl construction sleeps cold in winter. A mattress pad or thick blanket underneath you makes a noticeable difference. This isn't a flaw unique to Intex, but it's worth budgeting for.

## What Buyers Praise

The 4.2-star rating across 210 reviews reflects a pattern. Long-term owners consistently highlight the durability: "Had mine for three years of weekend guests and it still holds air overnight," one reviewer notes. Another echoes the comfort: "Better than my actual bed — I've replaced the guest room mattress with this."

The dual-zone feature earns frequent shout-outs from couples with different firmness preferences. Multiple reviewers mention the pump's reliability, noting it hasn't burned out after repeated use — a common failure point on budget air mattresses.

## Where It Falls Short

The built-in pump is AC-only. No battery option, no DC adapter. If you're buying this for camping or road trips, you'll need a power source or a separate pump — which defeats the purpose.

The flocked surface is a magnet for dust and crumbs, and it shows stains almost immediately. A fitted sheet is non-negotiable, but the 22-inch depth means standard sheets often pop off. Deep-pocket sheets are mandatory.

Some reviewers report a slow leak after several months of regular use, typically at the seam near the pump housing. The included patch kit works, but it's a temporary fix — the patch eventually loses adhesion on curved surfaces. If you're buying for daily use rather than occasional guests, budget for a 12-18 month lifespan.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You host overnight guests regularly and want something that feels like a real bed. You and your partner can't agree on mattress firmness. You want a spare bed that doesn't require a separate air pump purchase.

**Skip it if:** You need something portable for camping or travel. You're looking for a permanent mattress replacement — this is a guest bed, not a daily driver. You're on a tight budget and don't need the dual-zone feature; single-chamber models cost less.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this hold air all night?**
A: Yes, for most users. The Dura-Beam construction and quality valves hold pressure well for 8+ hours. You'll lose 1-2 PSI overnight, which is imperceptible. If you're a restless sleeper, you may need a small top-up before bed — that's normal for any air mattress.

**Q: How do I clean the flocked surface?**
A: Wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap, then air dry completely before deflating and storing. Skip harsh chemicals — they degrade the vinyl and weaken seams. Always store it dry to prevent mold.

**Q: Can two people sleep on this comfortably?**
A: Yes, but the king size means you're separated by the center seam. Each side supports up to 300 pounds independently. If you're both heavy sleepers who roll toward the middle, you'll feel the ridge between chambers.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The Intex Dura-Beam Deluxe 22” King is the best value in its category if your use case is "guest bed that feels like a real bed." The dual-zone feature is genuinely useful, not a gimmick, and the height makes it accessible for everyone. The AC-only pump and surface maintenance annoyances are real but manageable. Buy it for the guest room, not the campsite, and you'll likely get years of service.
