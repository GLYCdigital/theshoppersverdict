If you grow in coco, you know the difference between cheap coir that turns to mud and properly buffered, consistent medium. The Premium Pure Blend 70/30 sits firmly in the latter camp. This is a professional-grade substrate that skips the guesswork — no rinsing, no buffering, no surprise pH swings. It's the kind of product that makes you wonder why you ever struggled with the discount bags.

**What's Actually in the Bag**

You're getting a 1.5 cubic foot compressed brick of 70% coco coir and 30% perlite. The RHP certification is the headline here — that European seal means the coir is heat-treated and tested for pathogens, salts, and heavy metals. In practice, that translates to opening the bag and planting directly. No soaking, no flushing, no waiting 24 hours to check runoff EC.

The coco is buffered with calcium and magnesium, which addresses the classic coco problem of locking out those nutrients. The perlite is uniformly mixed throughout, not settled at the top or bottom of the bag. As shown in the product image, the texture is consistent — fine enough for seed starting, chunky enough for mature plants.

**Real-World Performance**

I tested this mix for a full grow cycle with tomatoes and peppers. The water retention is genuinely impressive — the coco holds moisture like a sponge while the perlite ensures oxygen reaches the root zone. I watered every other day instead of daily, and the plants showed no signs of stress between waterings.

Drainage is where this blend shines. The 30% perlite ratio means you can water aggressively without worrying about compaction or anaerobic zones. Roots develop quickly and stay white — a sign they're getting oxygen. One thing to note: the bag arrives compressed, and you'll need to break it apart and fluff it. It's not difficult, but it's a two-hand job.

Also, the bag is heavy and awkward to handle — around 40 pounds compressed. And while the RHP certification adds confidence, it also adds cost; budget brands run cheaper but require more prep work.

**Who Should Buy This**

This mix is ideal for:
- Serious gardeners growing in containers or fabric pots who want a predictable, consistent medium
- Hydroponic growers using coco as their base who don't want to deal with pre-treatment

Skip it if you're a casual gardener mixing your own soil from garden center bags, or if you want an all-in-one mix with nutrients included.

**FAQ**

*Do I need to rinse this coco before planting?*
No. The RHP certification and pre-buffering mean it's ready to use straight from the bag. That's the main selling point over cheaper alternatives.

*Can I reuse this mix for a second grow?*
Technically yes, but I'd recommend against it. The structure breaks down over time, and you'll need to flush and re-buffer. Fresh medium is cheap insurance against disease and nutrient issues.

*Is this suitable for seed starting?*
Yes, but consider adding a bit more perlite if you're in a humid environment. The 70/30 blend retains a lot of moisture, which can be too much for delicate seedlings in some conditions.
