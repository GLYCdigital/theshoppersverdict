**Performance and real-world use**

The most common thread across buyer feedback is that these blades cut clean and last. One reviewer noted, "I've mowed a full season on my MZ and the edges still look sharp — I normally replace OEM blades twice a year." Another said, "Balanced right out of the box, no vibration at full throttle." That's consistent with what I'd expect from a properly heat-treated blade: harder steel holds an edge longer, and the factory balance keeps your spindles happy.

That said, there's a tradeoff in lift. A few buyers mentioned that in damp, thick grass, these blades tend to leave clumps more than the factory set. The wing design is slightly less aggressive, so if you routinely mow wet lawns or let the grass get tall, you'll want to slow down or overlap your passes. On dry, maintained turf, the cut is indistinguishable from OEM — clean, even, and no striping issues.

You'll want a bench grinder or a professional sharpening service; a hand file will struggle.

**Who should buy this**
