If you're shopping for a metal detector that claims "professional" status without the $800 price tag, this waterproof unit with 215 Amazon ratings at 4.3 stars deserves a hard look. But "professional" is a loaded word — let's dig into what you're actually getting.

**What's in the box**

The package includes the detector with a 10-inch waterproof search coil, an adjustable shaft, a control box with backlit LCD, and an armrest. You get a basic instruction manual that covers the modes adequately, though not deeply. Notably absent: batteries (requires 2x 9V) and a carry bag. Budget for both if you plan to travel to hunting sites.

**Real-world performance**

This detector runs at 7kHz — a solid middle-ground frequency that handles coin shooting and relic hunting well, though it won't compete with multi-frequency units for gold prospecting in heavily mineralized soil. The 10-inch coil provides good ground coverage per sweep, and the five-tone audio system genuinely helps: iron gives a low growl, while silver and copper produce a clean, high-pitched signal. One reviewer put it plainly: *"Found a 1965 quarter at 8 inches on my second day out. My old detector missed it completely."*

The waterproof rating is the headline feature here. The coil and lower shaft are IP68-rated to 10 feet, meaning you can wade in lakes or hunt in heavy rain without the electronics failing. The control box itself is not submersible — keep that above water. For beach hunters, this is a genuine advantage over cheaper units that die on damp sand.

Detection depth is advertised at "higher accuracy" and in practice, it hits 8-10 inches on coin-sized targets in moderate soil. Deeper than that, signals get fuzzy. That's realistic for this price point.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The discrimination mode gets frequent shout-outs. It lets you filter out bottle caps and nails, which is the difference between a fun hobby and a frustrating one. Multiple reviewers noted the pinpoint mode works accurately for narrowing target location — one called it *"spot-on, saves so much digging."*

Build quality holds up. The shaft is aluminum alloy, not the flimsy plastic you see on budget detectors. The coil is sealed well; no moisture ingress complaints in the review history.

**Where it falls short**

The control box mounts with two screws — you'll need a Phillips head to assemble it. Not a dealbreaker, but annoying when you're eager to get outside. The included armrest is sized for average adults; users with larger forearms report it digs in during long sessions.

The 7kHz frequency means you'll miss very small gold nuggets and fine jewelry that higher-frequency detectors catch. If that's your primary target, look at a VLF unit in the 15-20kHz range. Battery life runs about 20 hours on fresh 9Vs, which is fine, but there's no low-battery indicator — it just dies mid-sweep.

**Who should buy this**

- **Weekend coin shooters** who want reliable discrimination without a steep learning curve
- **Lake and beach hunters** who need a waterproof coil for wading
- **Beginners** who want a step up from $50 toys but aren't ready for $500+ flagship units

**Skip it if** you're a serious gold prospector, or if you need a fully submersible control box for diving.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this in saltwater?**
A: Yes, but expect chatter in wet sand. The 7kHz frequency isn't ideal for saltwater mineralization; you'll want to use lower sensitivity settings. It works, but a multi-frequency unit handles beaches better.

**Q: How deep does it detect?**
A: Realistically, 8-10 inches on coin-sized targets in moderate soil. Larger objects like cans or horseshoes can be found at 12+ inches.

**Q: Does it come with batteries?**
A: No. You need two 9V batteries, which aren't included.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely waterproof detector with reliable discrimination and solid build quality for under $200. It's a legitimate step up from entry-level units and will serve you well for years of coin shooting and beach hunts.

**Skip it if** you're chasing gold nuggets or need a fully submersible setup — you'll outgrow this quickly.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid mid-tier detector that delivers on its waterproof promise, with minor ergonomic and frequency limitations that keep it from being truly "professional."
