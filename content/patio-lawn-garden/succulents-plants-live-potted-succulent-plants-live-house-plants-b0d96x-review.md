**FAQ**

**Q: How often do I water these?**
A: Wait until the soil is completely dry—usually every 1-2 weeks indoors. When you water, soak thoroughly until water drains from the bottom. When in doubt, don't water. Overwatering kills more succulents than underwatering.

**Q: Can I keep them in the pots they arrive in?**
A: Yes, for several months. The pots have drainage holes, which is essential. When roots emerge from the bottom, it's time to repot into something 1-2 inches wider.

**Q: How much light do they need?**
A: Bright, indirect light is ideal. A south or east-facing windowsill works. If they start stretching toward the light (etiolation), they need more sun.
