If you own a 40V SKIL tool and you're still using the charger that came in the box, you're leaving time on the table. The SKIL SC5364-00 PWRJump PWRCore 40 150W charger is the brand's answer to the "why is my battery still charging" problem. It pushes 150 watts into compatible PWRCore 40 batteries, which translates to noticeably faster turnarounds between cuts, trims, and blows.

Here's what you actually get: the charger unit itself, a standard AC power cord, and a wall-mount bracket. That's it—no battery included, no carrying case. As shown in the product image, the unit is a compact rectangular block with a single battery slot and a small LED status light. It's not flashy, but it's built to be hung on a wall and forgotten until you need it.

**Performance is where this charger earns its keep.** On a 2.5Ah PWRCore 40 battery, expect a full charge in roughly 30 to 45 minutes. That's a significant improvement over the standard 40V charger, which can take over an hour for the same pack. The 150W output makes the difference most noticeable when you're doing back-to-back yard work—trimming, then blowing, then swapping batteries mid-project. One buyer noted, *"I was skeptical about the speed difference, but it genuinely cut my charge time in half. I can rotate two batteries now instead of waiting around."* That sentiment echoes across the 4.3-star average from 215 ratings.

Build quality is solid. The housing feels durable, and the keyhole slots on the back make wall mounting genuinely easy—no need for a shelf or bench space. The LED indicator is straightforward: red while charging, green when full. It also flashes to signal a fault, which is helpful if a battery gets too hot or has a cell issue.

**Where it falls short:** This charger handles one battery at a time. If you own three or four PWRCore 40 packs, you'll still be swapping them out sequentially. The lack of a cooling fan also means that if you charge a hot battery immediately after heavy use, the charger will throttle down to protect the cells—slower than you might expect until things cool off. Neither issue is a dealbreaker, but they're worth knowing before you buy.

**Who should buy this?** Three specific types of shoppers:

1. **The multi-battery homeowner** — if you have two or more 40V batteries and you're tired of waiting for the second one to finish, this pays for itself in saved time within a season.

3. **The SKIL ecosystem loyalist** — if you're already invested in PWRCore 40 tools, this is the fastest way to keep them running.

**FAQ**

A: It's designed for PWRCore 40 batteries, including the 2.0Ah, 2.5Ah, and 4.0Ah packs. Older 40V SKIL batteries without PWRCore branding may not charge at full speed, though they generally still work.

**Q: Can I charge a battery while it's still warm from use?**
A: Yes, but the charger will reduce output to protect the cells. You'll see the LED flash or slow down until the battery cools. It's a safety feature, not a malfunction.

**Q: How long does a full charge actually take on a 4.0Ah battery?**
A: Expect roughly 60–75 minutes for the larger pack. That's still faster than the standard charger, which can push past 90 minutes.
