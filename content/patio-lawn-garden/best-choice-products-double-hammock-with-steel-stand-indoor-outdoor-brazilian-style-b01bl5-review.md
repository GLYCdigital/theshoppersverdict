The stand is the star here. It's a wide-based steel A-frame design that doesn't tip, even when you're swinging at full tilt. The 450-pound weight capacity is legit — one reviewer mentioned their family of three using it daily without any creaking or wobble. ## Where it falls short

The cotton-poly blend is stiff when new. Expect the first week or two of use to feel more like canvas than cushy cotton. It softens up with use, but you're paying the break-in tax. A few reviewers also noted the fabric pilling slightly after several months of heavy outdoor use — not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing if you're leaving it in direct sun all summer.

The bigger issue is spatial. The 9-foot stand needs a 10×10-foot clear area. If your patio is narrow or your apartment living room is compact, this will dominate the space. There's no compact mode. Measure your space before you order, not after.
