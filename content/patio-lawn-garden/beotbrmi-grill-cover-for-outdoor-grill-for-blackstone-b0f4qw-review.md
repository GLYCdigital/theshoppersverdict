**The Bottom Line Up Front**  

If you own a Blackstone griddle and you're tired of replacing cheap covers every season, the BEOTBRMI Grill Cover is worth a hard look. At 4.5 stars from 225 buyers, it's not perfect — but for the price, it punches well above its weight class. I've tested this against the OEM Blackstone cover, and honestly, the differences are minor.  

---

**What You Actually Get**  

The cover arrives folded, with zero assembly nonsense. Just slip it over your grill and cinch the straps. The material is 600D Oxford cloth with a PVC coating — that's the same spec you'd find on premium covers costing nearly double. The underside has a soft lining, which matters more than you'd think: it prevents scratching the griddle surface and stops condensation from dripping back onto your cooktop.  

The hem has an adjustable drawcord, and there are two side buckles near the bottom. Those aren't decorative — they're what keep this thing from becoming a kite during spring storms. The dimensions are listed for 36-inch Blackstone models, and the fit is tailored rather than baggy. It covers the side shelves completely, which is where most covers fail early.  

---

**Real-World Performance**  

I've had this on my 36-inch Blackstone for four months through rain, 90-degree heat, and one surprise hailstorm. The fabric repels water beautifully — it beads up and rolls off. The UV resistance is the real standout; the color hasn't faded noticeably, and the material hasn't gotten brittle or cracked like cheaper vinyl covers do.  

The wind straps work. I've had 30 mph gusts and the cover stayed put, even with the grill sitting in an exposed patio spot. That's a huge plus if you've ever chased a cover across your yard at 7 AM.  

One thing buyers consistently mention is the fit. As one verified reviewer put it: *"Fits my 36" Blackstone like a glove. The buckles are sturdy and the material feels thick enough to last at least a couple of seasons."* That sentiment lines up with my experience.  

---

**Where It Falls Short**  

Let's be honest about the flaws. The seams aren't taped or heat-sealed. In a normal rainstorm, you're fine. But if you get a torrential downpour with water pooling on top for hours, you might see dampness near the stitching. It's not a dealbreaker — just don't expect submarine-grade waterproofing.  

Second, the sizing is specific. This is built for 36-inch models. If you have a 28-inch griddle, it'll balloon at the sides and collect water in the folds. Measure your grill before you buy, not after.  

A few buyers also mentioned the material stiffens in freezing temps. It's not fragile, but it doesn't fold as easily in winter. Minor gripe, but worth noting if you live in a cold climate.  

---

**Who Should Buy This**  

**Buy it if:** You own a 36-inch Blackstone (or similar flat-top), you live in a windy area, and you want solid protection without paying the OEM premium. It's also a great pick for RV owners — the straps keep it secure during travel.  

**Skip it if:** You have a smaller griddle, you live somewhere with extreme prolonged downpours, or you expect it to last a decade. This is a 2-3 season cover at best, depending on your climate.
