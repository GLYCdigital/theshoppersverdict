**What You Actually Get**

The box contains one Moleskine Classic Large notebook, sized at 5" x 8.25" — the versatile middle ground between pocket and A4. The hard cover is a sturdy, rigid board wrapped in that iconic black leatherette. Inside, you'll find 192 pages of acid-free, ivory-colored paper with a squared (graph) grid pattern. A ribbon bookmark, an elastic closure band, and an expandable inner pocket in the back round out the package.

Note: The "Large" designation is Moleskine's own sizing — it's closer to a standard A5 than what most people call "large." Nothing wrong with that, but don't expect a massive notebook.

---

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

The hard cover is the standout feature. It provides a solid writing surface anywhere — on a train, in a coffee shop, standing in line. The elastic band keeps pages flat and secure, and the ribbon bookmark is a thoughtful touch for daily use.

The paper handles most pens well. Gel pens, rollerballs, and ballpoints all work without significant bleed-through. Fountain pen users will find the paper adequate but not exceptional — there's minor ghosting with wetter nibs, and the ivory tint is noticeably warm. If you're a heavy ink user, you'll want to test a page before committing to a full notebook.

The squared grid is crisp and consistent, with a subtle blue-gray color that doesn't compete with your writing. Page count is generous at 192 sheets, but remember: you're getting roughly half that in usable surface once you account for both sides.

The 4.8-star rating (240 reviews) reflects genuine satisfaction. Recurring praise centers on:

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be direct about the downsides:

1. **Price per page**: At this price point, you're paying for brand and design, not paper volume. Budget alternatives offer 2-3x the pages for half the cost.
2. **Paper isn't fountain-pen friendly**: Wet inks ghost and occasionally feather. This is a dealbreaker for serious fountain pen collectors.
3. **No lay-flat binding**: The hard cover resists bending, but the spine won't lie completely flat. You'll need to hold pages open for the first two weeks until the binding loosens.
4. **Elastic band tension**: It's strong — sometimes too strong. Closing the notebook with more than ~30 pages of loose sheets can crease the paper edges.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Bullet journalers**: The squared grid is ideal for rapid logging and habit tracking without the commitment to a pre-printed layout.
- **Students and professionals**: The hard cover protects against bag damage, and the size fits most messenger bags and laptop sleeves.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the hard cover bend easily?**
A: No. The cover is rigid and holds its shape well. It will survive years of bag abuse, but it will show scuffs and light scratches on the black leatherette finish.

**Q: Is the squared grid too faint?**
A: It's clearly visible but subtle — a light blue-gray that won't overpower your writing. If you need bold gridlines, this isn't for you.

**Q: Can I use this for fountain pens?**
A: You can, but expect ghosting (visible show-through on the back of pages) with wet nibs. Dry and fine nibs work acceptably. For heavy ink flow, look elsewhere.
