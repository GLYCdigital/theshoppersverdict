## Is This Swing Worth Your Patio Space?

Most porch swings under $300 are either flimsy metal skeletons or wooden benches that wobble after one summer. The PURPLE LEAF Palace Swing sits in a different category — it's a full-size, stand-alone swing with a 550-pound capacity that doesn't flex, creak, or tip when you actually use it. After digging through 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, the pattern is clear: this is one of the more substantial freestanding swings you can buy without stepping up to custom-built furniture.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the swing frame, seat, backrest, two 3.5-inch thick cushioned seat pads, two back cushions, and all mounting hardware. The frame is heavy-gauge steel with a powder-coated finish — available in black, brown, or white depending on your deck aesthetic. As shown in the product image, the canopy is not included; this is just the swing and stand. The seat itself measures roughly 53 inches wide, giving you genuine two-adult room without shoulder bumping.

## Real-World Performance

The first thing you notice when you sit down is the suspension system. The swing hangs from industrial-grade springs rather than bare chains, which absorbs the jerkiness you get on cheaper swings. The glide is smooth and controlled — no violent bouncing when you settle in. The base is wide enough that you don't feel any tipping risk, even if you sit off-center.

The cushions deserve special mention. They're upholstered in a solution-dyed polyester that resists fading, and the covers zip off for machine washing. Several reviewers specifically noted surviving surprise rainstorms without the cushions soaking through. One verified buyer wrote, "Left these out all season through rain and sun — they look brand new. The zippers haven't rusted either."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Reviewers repeatedly highlight three things. First, the weight capacity — multiple families report using this with two adults and a toddler without any strain. Second, the smooth glide from the spring suspension. Third, the included storage cover, which fits well and actually protects the swing through winter.

One five-star review summed it up: "I've bought three porch swings in the last five years. This one is the first that doesn't wobble, doesn't squeak, and doesn't rust after a season outside."

## Where It Falls Short

Assembly is the biggest complaint, and it's legitimate. The frame bolts need to be tightened firmly — one reviewer noted needing a breaker bar for the final turn on the main pivot bolts. Budget 60 to 90 minutes with two people. It's not hard, but it's not a 15-minute job either.

The cushions arrive vacuum-sealed and flat. They need a day or two to fully expand, which has led to some premature "these cushions are too thin" reviews from people who didn't wait. They do fluff up, but patience required.

The seat is also on the firmer side even after the cushions loft — you're not sinking into this like a hammock. If you want plush cloud-soft seating, this isn't it. It's supportive, not marshmallowy.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want a freestanding swing that doesn't require ceiling mounting, have a covered porch or patio, and want something that'll last more than two seasons. Families with kids will appreciate the weight rating and stable base.

**Skip it if:** You want a canopy included, need something you can assemble solo in under an hour, or prefer a soft, sink-in seat. Also skip if your space is smaller than about 8 feet by 5 feet — the footprint is substantial.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this be left outside year-round?**
A: The frame is powder-coated steel and resists rust, and the included cover helps. But if you live in heavy snow or freezing rain, the manual recommends storing cushions indoors. The frame can stay out, but covering it extends the finish's life significantly.

**Q: Does it sway or swing on its own in wind?**
A: The spring suspension will move slightly in strong gusts, but the base weight (roughly 60 pounds of steel) plus the wide footprint keep it planted. It won't tip or slide across the deck in normal weather.

**Q: Will the cushions fade in direct sun?**
A: The polyester fabric is UV-resistant, but no fabric is immune to prolonged full-sun exposure. Under a covered porch, expect years of color retention. In full direct sun all day, you'll see some fading eventually — the included cover helps if you use it.
