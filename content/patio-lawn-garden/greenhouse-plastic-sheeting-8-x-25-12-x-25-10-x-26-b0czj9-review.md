**Where It Falls Short**

No product is perfect, and this one has a few honest limitations.

First, there are no grommets or reinforced attachment points. If you plan to secure it with staples or nails, you'll risk tearing the film at the puncture points. Use greenhouse clips or a wiggle wire channel instead—those distribute stress properly.

Second, in humid regions, condensation forms on the inside surface and drips onto plants. This isn't a defect—it's physics—but it can promote fungal issues if you're growing tomatoes or peppers. A double-layer installation with an air gap solves this but requires more framing.

Third, the 24-foot width option is a single continuous sheet. If you need that width, great. If you need two 12-foot panels, you'll be cutting and wasting material. Measure your frame before ordering.

**Q: Will it yellow or crack after one summer?**
