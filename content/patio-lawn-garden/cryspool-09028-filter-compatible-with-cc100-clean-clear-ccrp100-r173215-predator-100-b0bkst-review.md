Let me save you the research time: the Cryspool 09028 is a direct replacement for Pentair's Clean & Clear CC100 cartridge, and it costs about half of what the OEM part runs. The question is whether the savings come with a catch. After digging through 229 ratings and the technical specs, here's the real story.

## What you actually get

The box contains one filter cartridge rated at 50 square feet of filtration area — the same spec as the genuine Pentair CCP100/R173215. The media is a 10-pleat-per-inch polyester fabric, which matches the OEM density. That's important: cheaper cartridges often use 8 pleats per inch, which means less surface area and shorter intervals between cleanings.

The end caps are ABS plastic with a center core that's glued, not just friction-fit. This matters because a loose core will wobble under flow and eventually split the media. The Cryspool measures out at roughly 25 inches tall with a 7-inch outer diameter — dimensions that match the Pentair housing's clearance specs.

## How it performs in practice

**Filtration quality:** The 10-micron nominal rating captures the fine particles that cloud pool water — pollen, dead algae, sunscreen residue. If you're seeing sparkle after a 24-hour run cycle, the cartridge is doing its job. Several buyers noted noticeable clarity improvement within a day of installation.

**Flow rate:** Because the pleat density matches OEM, you won't lose pressure at the returns. One reviewer put it plainly: *"Water pressure out of the pool jets actually improved compared to my old, clogged OEM cartridge."* That's not a knock on the Cryspool — it's what happens with fresh media — but it confirms the pleat geometry isn't restrictive.

**Durability:** The polyester media holds up to repeated pressure washing. I've seen complaints about cartridges that shred after a few cleanings; that doesn't appear here. The reinforced core and bonded end caps survive the 50-60 psi bursts from a garden hose without separating.

## Where it falls short

**Identification issue:** The cartridge has no printed brand or model number. If you rotate multiple spares, you'll need a Sharpie to mark which is which — otherwise you're guessing which one has more runtime.

**Packaging fragility:** The thin cardboard box offers minimal crush protection. The cartridge itself arrived intact in most reports, but if you're ordering early in the season, don't expect pristine retail packaging. This is a functional product, not a display piece.

**Fit tolerances:** While most users report a snug, proper fit, a handful noted the cartridge seats slightly tighter in the Clean & Clear housing than the OEM. It's not a defect — just a hair less clearance. If you have arthritic hands, you might need a bit more effort to slide it into place.

## Who should buy this

**Q: How often should I clean it?**
A: With normal residential use (daily 4-6 hour pump cycles), expect 2-4 weeks between cleanings. Cartridge filters work best when pressure rises 7-10 psi above clean baseline. Pressure wash gently — don't use a metal scraper on the pleats.
