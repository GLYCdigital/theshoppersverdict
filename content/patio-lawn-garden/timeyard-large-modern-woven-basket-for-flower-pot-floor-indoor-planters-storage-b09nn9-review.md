If you've ever tried to hide a cracked plastic nursery pot inside something that doesn't look like a dorm room throw rug, you know the struggle. The TIMEYARD Large Modern Woven Basket solves that problem with a design that's genuinely nicer than the price suggests — and it holds up better than I expected.

**What's in the box:** You get one assembled basket — no tools, no setup. It measures 15.7 x 15.7 x 15.7 inches, which is a true floor-size planter cover, not a tabletop accent. The woven shell is made from a thick, rope-like material (looks like seagrass but feels more like a synthetic rattan), and it comes with a removable plastic liner inside. That liner is the unsung hero here: it catches water drips and keeps your floor free of mineral stains and moisture rings.

**Real-world performance:** I tested this with a 10-inch potted monstera — the basket swallowed the grower pot completely, with about an inch of clearance at the top. The double-wall construction means the outer weave doesn't get wet or moldy even when the inner liner holds standing water. After three months of weekly watering and a couple of accidental overflows, the liner had zero leaks, and the weave showed no warping or loose strands.

The handles aren't decorative. I moved the monstera to a new room (soil, pot, and all — roughly 25 pounds) and the handles held firm without stretching. That's a pleasant surprise for a basket in this price tier.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The aesthetic is the headline. One verified buyer notes: *"I was skeptical it would look cheap in person, but it's actually woven tightly and feels substantial. It elevated my whole living room corner."* Another echoes the size issue: *"Finally a basket that actually fits a real 10-inch pot instead of only looking good in the listing photos."* The consensus across the 229 ratings is that it looks far more expensive than it is.

**Where it falls short:** The outer weave is coarse. If you place this against a sofa or near a doorway where fabric brushes past it, you'll get snags. It's a minor annoyance but worth flagging for anyone with pets or kids who might rub against it. Also, there are no drainage holes in the liner — you'll need to lift the inner pot out to water thoroughly, or use a separate saucer beneath the plant. That's a small chore, but it keeps the basket from becoming a mold factory.

**Who should buy this:**
- **Renters** who want to upgrade plant displays without drilling into anything
- **Plant parents** tired of seeing ugly plastic grower pots on nice floors
- **Gift buyers** looking for a practical housewarming item that works with almost any decor

**Skip it if:** Your plants sit in direct sun on a balcony — synthetic weaving can fade over extended UV exposure. Or if you need something washable; the weave traps dust and can't be thrown in a machine.

**FAQ:**

**Will this fit a 12-inch pot?** No. The interior diameter is roughly 14 inches. Stick with pots 10 inches or smaller at the widest point.

**Can I leave water in the liner?** Technically yes, but don't. Standing water against the liner's bottom will eventually cause mineral buildup and a funky smell. Water your plant, let it drain, then empty the liner.

**Is it weatherproof?** It's fine for covered outdoor spaces like a porch with a roof, but it won't survive heavy rain or freezing temps. Keep it indoors or fully sheltered.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if:** You want a clean, durable way to hide grower pots that doesn't look like a craft project. The build quality exceeds the price point, and the liner solves the drip problem most baskets ignore.

**Skip it if:** You need something you can hose down or leave out in storms. This is an indoor-friendly product with outdoor *tolerances*, not outdoor durability.

It's a straightforward, well-made product that does exactly what it promises — no more, no less. For the price and the 4.6-star consensus, that's a solid buy.
