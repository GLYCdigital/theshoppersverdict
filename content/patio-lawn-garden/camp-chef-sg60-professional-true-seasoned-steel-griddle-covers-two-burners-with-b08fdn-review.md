## What You Actually Get

The SG60 is a fitted cover, not a universal drape. It's built from heavy-duty 600D polyester with a waterproof PVC backing—this isn't the thin, shiny material you find on bargain covers. The underside is a soft fleece lining, which matters more than you'd think: it won't scratch your griddle's seasoned surface when you pull it on and off. The cover cinches down with buckled straps at the bottom, and it ships with a matching storage bag so you can stow it cleanly during summer cooking season.

Fit is the headline feature. This is designed specifically for Camp Chef's 2-burner griddles (SG24, SG30, and the SGX series). It's not a one-size-fits-all rectangle—it's shaped to accommodate the grease tray and side shelves without bunching.

## Real-World Performance

The waterproofing is the first thing buyers mention. One reviewer put it simply: "Living in the Pacific Northwest, this thing has seen months of straight rain and my griddle stays bone dry underneath." That's the PVC backing doing its job—dew and drizzle aren't getting through. The fleece liner also prevents condensation buildup, which is the silent killer of cast iron and steel cooking surfaces.

Wind is another test. Cheap covers balloon up and act like sails. The SG60's buckled straps and tailored cut keep it snug. A verified buyer noted, "Had 40 mph gusts last week and the cover didn't budge." The 600D fabric also resists UV fading—another reviewer mentioned theirs looked new after two full seasons outdoors.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The dominant theme across positive reviews is **fit and finish**. People who own Camp Chef griddles specifically appreciate that this isn't a generic Amazon special. One review stated, "Fits my SG30 like a glove, the straps keep it tight, and the storage bag is a nice touch." Another praised the material quality: "This is the thickest cover I've owned. You can tell it'll last years."

## Where It Falls Short

Honest tradeoffs exist. First, the price—this cover runs noticeably higher than a generic 48-inch cover. The material and fit justify most of that gap, but if you're on a tight budget, a universal cover will technically work. Second, the SG60 is **not** for larger setups. If you own the 3-burner Camp Chef Pro (SGX series with 36-inch surfaces), this won't fit—you need the SG90 or a larger model. Measure your griddle's width before ordering. Finally, the fleece liner, while protective, can trap moisture if you put the cover on while the griddle is still hot. Always let it cool completely first.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The dedicated outdoor cook**: You use your griddle weekly and want it to last a decade, not three seasons.
- **Anyone in a wet or snowy climate**: The waterproof backing is a genuine difference-maker versus breathable-but-leaky generic covers.
- **Camp Chef owners specifically**: If you've already invested in a quality griddle, the $40–50 premium for a perfect-fit cover is proportional insurance.

Skip it if you own a different brand's griddle with non-standard dimensions—a universal cover will be a gamble, and this one won't fit right.

## FAQ

**Will this fit my Camp Chef 3-burner griddle?**
No. The SG60 is sized for 2-burner models (SG24, SG30, SGX). For 3-burner units, look for the SG90 cover.

**Can I put this on a hot griddle?**
You shouldn't. The fleece lining and PVC backing can trap heat and moisture, which accelerates rust. Let the griddle cool fully before covering.

**Is it actually waterproof?**
Yes, the PVC backing is impermeable. It's one of the most consistent praise points in reviews—rain and snow don't penetrate.
