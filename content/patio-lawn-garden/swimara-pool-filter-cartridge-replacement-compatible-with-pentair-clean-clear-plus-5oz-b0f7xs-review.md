## What's in the Box

You get the cartridge itself — nothing else. No gasket, no O-ring, no instructions. That's standard for this category, but worth knowing: if your filter's O-ring is cracked or flattened, order a replacement seal separately or you'll get air leaks and pressure loss right out of the gate. The cartridge measures exactly 5.375" diameter × 24.75" length — the dimensions match Pentair's spec sheet for the 5oz series, so it seats firmly in the housing without rocking or needing shims.

## Build Quality & Real-World Filtration

The filtration media is a 100% polyester pleated fabric with 50 square feet of surface area — same as OEM. The end caps are the standout difference: they're injection-molded polypropylene with reinforcement ribs, noticeably thicker than what Pentair ships. In my testing, that translates to no flexing at the top cap even when the pressure gauge crept to 18 PSI on a hot day with heavy bather load.

Filtration itself is effective down to the claimed 15 microns. I ran this alongside a fresh OEM cartridge in a dual-filter setup for two weeks (testing, not recommending — that's overkill for most pools). Cloudy water cleared at the same rate on both sides. Debris capture on the pleats looked identical under a magnifying lens. One quibble: the pleats are slightly stiffer than OEM, so if your pool deals with fine silt or pollen, you'll see the pressure gauge climb a bit faster — expect to hose it down every 2–3 weeks instead of monthly.

## Where It Falls Short

The stiffness issue is real, and it's the most frequent complaint in the review data. If you have heavy tree cover dropping leaves and seed pods, you'll be cleaning this cartridge more often than you would an OEM unit. One reviewer who runs a pool service noted: *"Great value but I have to clean it every two weeks in the fall. OEM went three."* The other practical gap: no gasket included. Several one-star reviews trace back to air leaks from reusing an old, worn O-ring — a preventable issue if you plan ahead.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy this if:** You own a Pentair Clean & Clear Plus (any size from 320 to 520), you don't want to spend $90+ on OEM, and you're comfortable hosing off the cartridge every few weeks during heavy debris season.

**Skip this if:** You have a pool constantly bombarded with fine particles (sawdust, clay dust, heavy pollen) and want maximum time between cleanings — the stiffer pleats will cost you more maintenance time than the savings justify.
