**Performance and Real-World Use**

I put this pot through the paces with a mature hydrangea and a dwarf citrus tree. The full depth is immediately noticeable — you can plant deep without the rootball hitting the bottom prematurely. The thick walls do a decent job of insulating roots from temperature swings, though not as well as true terracotta.

**The critical issue: drainage.**

The pot comes with a single, pre-drilled drainage hole. It works, but it's not generous. If you're in a rainy climate or tend to overwater, you'll want to drill 2-3 additional holes in the base. The feet are integrated and provide about a half-inch of clearance, which is adequate for airflow but not enough to prevent water pooling underneath without a saucer.

**FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask**

**Q: Will this crack in freezing temperatures?**
The resin blend is frost-resistant to a degree, but no molded pot is indestructible. If you live in a zone that sees hard freezes, empty the pot and store it indoors or upside down. Water expanding inside is the killer, not the cold itself.

**Q: What size plant fits best?**
For the 17-inch diameter version, a 5-gallon nursery pot slides in perfectly, or you can plant directly with fresh soil. The full depth means you can go deeper than you'd expect — a 7-gallon rootball will have room.

**Q: Does the glossy finish scratch easily?**
It's scratch-resistant against normal wear, but dragging it across concrete will leave marks. Always lift it, and use a plastic pot saucer under it to prevent base scuffs.
