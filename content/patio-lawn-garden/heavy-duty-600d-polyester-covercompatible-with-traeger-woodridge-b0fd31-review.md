Let's be direct: most grill covers are glorified trash bags. They tear at the seams, fade within a season, and turn into soggy messes after the first thunderstorm. This 600D polyester cover for the Traeger Woodridge actually tries to be different. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it's clear owners have strong opinions — mostly positive. Here's what you need to know before spending your money.

## What You're Actually Getting

The cover is built from 600-denier polyester, which is a meaningful step up from the thin 420D or 300D materials you'll find on cheaper options. It has a water-resistant coating on the exterior, reinforced stitching at the seams, and a padded handle in the center for lifting. The hem includes adjustable straps on both sides to cinch it tight against wind.

As shown in the product image, this is a full-length cover designed specifically for the Traeger Woodridge series — not a generic "fits most" shape. It accommodates the side shelf and front folding table without that awkward tent-pole look you get when a rectangular cover is stretched over an L-shaped smoker.

## Real-World Performance

The 600D fabric does what it claims. Water beads up and rolls off rather than soaking through, and the material has enough weight that it doesn't flap around like a flag in moderate wind. The adjustable hem straps matter more than most people realize — they keep the cover from acting as a sail during storms.

One owner noted: *"The straps and velcro closures keep it tight even in 30 mph gusts. My last cover blew off twice a week."* That's the experience most buyers report: it stays put.

The fit is the standout feature. Because it's tailored for the Woodridge, it covers the entire unit down to the wheels with maybe an inch of clearance. This means rain can't splash up onto the smoker body from the ground, which is a common failure point on shorter covers.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. This cover has **no ventilation**. When warm, humid air gets trapped under the fabric and the temperature drops overnight, you can get condensation on the smoker's exterior. A few buyers mentioned this — one wrote: *"Found moisture under the cover after a hot day followed by a cold night. Wiped it off, no rust, but still annoying."*

The water resistance isn't the same as waterproofing. In prolonged, wind-driven rain, moisture can find its way through at stress points where the fabric stretches tight over corners and handles. For most users, this is a non-issue — the Woodridge's exterior is powder-coated steel designed to handle weather. But if you live somewhere with monsoon-level rain, you may want to add a secondary tarp or treat the cover with a waterproofing spray each season.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Traeger Woodridge owners who grill year-round** — this cover will survive sun, snow, and rain without degrading in a season.
- **Anyone tired of replacing covers annually** — the 600D material and reinforced seams give this a realistic 2-3 year lifespan with basic care.
- **People who store their smoker on an open patio or deck** — the wind straps and full-length coverage are worth the price alone.

Skip it if you own a different Traeger model — the tailored fit won't translate. And if you're looking for a $20 tarp-style cover, this isn't that. It's a mid-tier investment for people who treat their smoker like equipment, not a disposable appliance.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit the Traeger Woodridge with the front folding shelf extended?**
A: No — the cover is designed for the shelf in the folded position. Fold it down before covering.

**Q: Can I leave it on during summer heat?**
A: Yes, but wipe any condensation off the smoker before firing it up. The lack of vents means trapped moisture is the main tradeoff.

**Q: How does this compare to Traeger's official cover?**
A: It's heavier duty than Traeger's standard offering (600D vs. roughly 300D) at a similar price point. The tradeoff is slightly less flexibility in fit.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you own a Traeger Woodridge and want a cover that actually fits, stays on in wind, and won't disintegrate after one winter.

**Skip it if** you need a fully waterproof, vented cover for extreme weather conditions — this isn't that, and pretending otherwise will set you up for disappointment.

It's not perfect. The lack of ventilation is a real oversight, and the water resistance has limits. But for the price and build quality, this is one of the better tailored grill covers on the market. At 4.6 stars, the ratings back that up.
