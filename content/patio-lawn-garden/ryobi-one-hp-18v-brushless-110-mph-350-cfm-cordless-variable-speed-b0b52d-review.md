If you're already in the RYOBI ONE+ ecosystem, this blower is a no-brainer. If you're not, it's still worth a serious look — but you need to understand what you're buying before you click checkout.

**What's in the box**

The RYOBI ONE+ HP 18V Brushless blower (model PBLB01B) ships as a bare tool — no battery, no charger. Just the blower, a nozzle, and the manual. That's the first thing to know. If you don't already own RYOBI batteries, you're looking at another $100+ for a starter kit. The product image shows it with a battery for demonstration, but what arrives is the tool only.

**Performance and build**

Here's where this blower separates itself from the pack. The brushless motor delivers 110 MPH at 350 CFM — numbers that used to require a 40V or 60V platform. In real-world terms, that means it can actually move wet, matted leaves in the fall, which is the killer test. I've seen cheaper 18V blowers just push air around while wet leaves sit there mocking you. This one clears them.

The variable speed dial is a genuine feature, not a gimmick. At the low end, it's gentle enough to sweep a patio without scattering mulch. Crank it up and it'll clear a two-car driveway of light debris in minutes. The trigger lock keeps you from holding the button down for extended sessions.

Build quality feels solid for the price point. The housing is rigid, the nozzle locks in with a positive click, and the brushless motor should outlast the brushed units RYOBI still sells at the lower price point. One ergonomic note: with a 4Ah battery mounted, it's noticeably nose-heavy. You'll want to switch hands periodically during longer jobs.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.2/5 rating across 210 reviews is earned. The recurring theme in customer feedback is the power-to-weight ratio. As one verified buyer put it: "I was skeptical an 18V could replace my gas blower, but this thing is shockingly strong for how light it is." Another reviewer noted, "The variable speed is the reason I bought this over the cheaper model — it actually makes a difference when you're doing detail work around flower beds."

The ONE+ battery compatibility is the other big winner. If you've got a garage full of RYOBI tools, this slots right in. That's a real cost advantage over switching to a different platform.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the battery life. At full throttle, you're getting roughly 10-12 minutes from a 4Ah battery. That's enough for a small yard or quick cleanup, but if you have a large property, you'll be swapping batteries constantly. Plan on owning at least two packs, or step up to the 6Ah or 8Ah batteries (which add weight to that nose-heavy balance).

The other consideration is noise — it's not whisper-quiet. It's not the worst offender, but at high speed it's loud enough that ear protection is a reasonable choice for extended use.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for homeowners with small to medium yards who already own RYOBI ONE+ tools and want to consolidate their battery platform. It's also great for anyone who wants gas-blower-adjacent power without the maintenance headaches of mixing fuel, pulling cords, or dealing with a 2-stroke engine.

Skip it if you have two acres of oak trees, need it for commercial work, or you're starting from scratch with no RYOBI batteries — in that case, a 40V kit with a battery included is a smarter first purchase.

**FAQ**

**Does this come with a battery?**
No. It's a bare tool. You'll need a RYOBI ONE+ 18V battery (sold separately). The included battery in some product listings is for illustration only.

**Can I use this with my existing RYOBI 18V batteries?**
Yes — that's the whole point of the ONE+ system. Any RYOBI 18V battery works, from the old 1.5Ah packs to the new High Performance 4Ah and larger.

**How long does the battery actually last?**
Realistically, 10-15 minutes at full power with a 4Ah battery. On the low speed setting, you can stretch that to 25-30 minutes for light work.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You're in the RYOBI ecosystem, need real power in a lightweight package, and value variable speed control over raw battery life.

**Skip it if:** You need all-day runtime, you're starting fresh with no batteries, or you have a large property with heavy fall cleanup demands.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The RYOBI ONE+ HP 18V Brushless blower delivers genuine, gas-adjacent power in a package that costs a fraction of the big boys. Battery life and the bare-tool pricing are the tradeoffs, but for the right buyer, this is the sweet spot of the 18V blower market.
