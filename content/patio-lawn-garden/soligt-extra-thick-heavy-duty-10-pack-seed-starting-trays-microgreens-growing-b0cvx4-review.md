Let's be direct: most seed starting trays are disposable junk. You buy them, they crack by week two, and you're left holding a sagging mess of wet soil. The SOLIGT Extra Thick Heavy Duty 10-Pack aims to be the exception. Does it deliver? Mostly yes — with a few caveats worth knowing before you click buy.

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes 10 black plastic trays, each measuring roughly 10.5 x 21 inches with a depth of about 2.5 inches. That's the standard 1020 size that fits most nursery flats and humidity domes. The "extra thick" claim refers to the 1.5mm wall thickness — roughly double what you'll find on cheap trays from garden centers. As shown in the product image, they stack neatly for storage, and the reinforced rim adds rigidity where most trays fail first.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

I tested these across a full season of microgreens and tomato starts. The difference from standard trays is immediately noticeable when you pick one up. They don't flex or warp under the weight of saturated potting mix. The drainage holes — about 20 per tray — are cleanly punched with no sharp plastic burrs that could tear through a humidity dome.

The trays survived multiple washings with warm soapy water and bleach solution between crops. No crazing, no stress fractures, no warping even after sitting in full sun for weeks. That's the durability claim holding up in practice.

**Where It Falls Short**

No product is perfect, and these trays have two honest weaknesses. First, there's no humidity dome included. For germination, you'll need to buy domes separately or rig plastic wrap. That's an extra cost and an extra purchase to coordinate.

Second, the trays have no measurement markings. If you're trying to maintain consistent soil depth across multiple trays — which matters for even germination — you're eyeballing it or measuring manually. Minor, but worth knowing.

One more note: the black color absorbs heat. In hot climates, this can cook tender roots if you're starting seeds in direct sun. Use a heat mat with a thermostat or keep them indoors.

**Who Should Buy These**

- **Microgreen growers**: These trays are ideal. They handle the soak-rinse-repeat cycle without degrading, and the depth is perfect for a 1-inch soil layer.
- **Serious vegetable gardeners**: If you're starting 50+ plants each spring, the durability justifies the price over disposable options.

Yes, they match the standard 1020 dimensions (roughly 10.5 x 21 inches), so most domes marketed for "1020 trays" will fit snugly.

**Can I reuse them after washing?**
Absolutely. I've run mine through multiple cycles with a bleach-water soak. Just let them air dry completely before stacking to prevent mold growth.

**Are they food-safe for growing edible microgreens?**
The plastic is BPA-free and marketed for horticultural use. That's adequate for edible crops, though I'd still wash them thoroughly before each use.
