Here's where this product separates itself from the bargain-bin algaecides. In practice, that means you pour it in, run the pump for 24 hours, and the water clears noticeably.

Customer feedback backs this up. One verified buyer wrote: *"I was skeptical because my pool had been neglected for two weeks. Dumped in the initial dose, brushed the walls, and by the next morning the water was visibly clearer. Two days later it was swim-ready."*

---

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about limitations. This is a preventive and mild-treatment product, not a rescue weapon. If your pool is already dark green and you can't see the bottom step, you need to shock with liquid chlorine or calcium hypochlorite first. Algatec will help afterward, but it won't resurrect a swamp.

A few reviewers mentioned foaming. If you accidentally pour in double the recommended dose — easy to do with the open pour spout — you'll get surface foam that takes time to dissipate. Measure carefully.

One more note: this product works best in pools with a clean filter. If your cartridge or sand filter is clogged, the clarifier has nowhere to push the dead algae. Backwash first.
