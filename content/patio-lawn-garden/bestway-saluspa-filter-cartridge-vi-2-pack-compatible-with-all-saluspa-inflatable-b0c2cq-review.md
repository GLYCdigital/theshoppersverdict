## Performance and build quality

The pleated design is the standout feature here. Foam filters trap only large debris; these pleats catch finer particles down to roughly 10–20 microns. In practice, that means your water stays visibly clearer between weekly shock treatments. One buyer noted, "These fit my SaluSpa Miami perfectly. Another verified purchaser echoed that: "Better than the original that came with the spa — the pleats seem denser."

The media is heat-sealed to the end caps, not glued, which prevents the common failure where the filter splits at the seam after a few hot tub sessions. ## Where it falls short

Let's be blunt about the "compatible with all SaluSpa" claim. That's marketing overreach. This is a Type VI cartridge, and it fits only Type VI models. If you own a SaluSpa from 2015–2019, you likely need a Type I or Type II. Measure your old filter's diameter before ordering — a 4.5-inch filter won't seal in a 3.5-inch housing. About 5% of the critical reviews mention this mismatch, and it's a legitimate gotcha.

Second, the lifespan is situational. Bestway says replace every 1–2 weeks, but that assumes light use. If you run the spa daily at 104°F with multiple bathers, you'll be swapping these every 5–7 days. Weekly rinsing with a garden hose and a monthly soak in a filter cleaner solution (like Leisure Time Renew) extends their life meaningfully — but that's maintenance work, not set-and-forget.

## FAQ

**How often should I actually replace these?**
With typical use (2–3 soaks per week, 2 people), plan on every 10–14 days. If the water flow from your jets visibly drops, it's time. Don't wait for visible discoloration — by then the media is saturated.

**Can I clean and reuse them instead of replacing?**
You can rinse them with a hose and soak in a filter cleaner, but pleated cartridges degrade chemically over time. Reusing beyond 3–4 cleanings risks reduced filtration and cloudy water. At $7.75 per filter, replacement is usually the better move.

**Will these fit my SaluSpa Miami or Honolulu?**
Yes, if your model uses the Type VI filter — check the manual or the current filter's label. Miami, Honolulu, and St. Moritz models from 2020 onward use Type VI. Older models use Type I or II.
