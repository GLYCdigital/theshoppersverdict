That makes them a poor choice for temporary applications like seasonal row covers or tent stakes. They're designed to go in and stay in.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can these go through thick landscape fabric?**
A: Yes. The sharp taper and 1.6mm wire punch through heavy-duty woven fabric cleanly. For double-layered barrier, pre-punch with an awl or screwdriver to avoid fabric bunching.

**Q: How far apart should I space these?**
A: For weed barrier, 12-18 inches along edges and 24-36 inches in the field. For edging or drip lines, every 12 inches. The 6-inch length means fewer staples overall than shorter alternatives.

**Q: Will they rust in coastal/salt environments?**
A: The hot-dip galvanization is robust, but no steel staple is truly salt-proof. In coastal areas, expect surface oxidation over 2-3 years. Rinse with fresh water occasionally to extend life.
