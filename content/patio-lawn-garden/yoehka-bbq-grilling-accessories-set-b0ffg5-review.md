**The short version:** If you're tired of digging through a drawer for one missing spatula every time you grill, this Yoehka set solves that problem permanently. It's a complete 23-piece kit that covers cooking, flipping, cleaning, and safety — for about what you'd pay for two mid-range tools separately.

## What's actually in the box

As shown in the product image, you get a serious spread: two spatulas (solid and slotted), locking tongs, a basting brush, fork, skewers, grill brush, cleaning pads, and a digital meat thermometer. Everything nests into a hard-sided carry case with a foam insert that keeps tools from rattling.

The stainless steel construction is legitimate — not the flimsy stamped stuff you find at big-box stores. The handles are thick, with a nice textured grip that stays put even with greasy hands. At 18 inches long, they clear the heat zone on standard 22-inch kettles and most gas grills.

## How it performs in real use

I tested this set across a full summer of grilling — burgers, chicken thighs, salmon, vegetables, and a brisket that ran 14 hours. The locking tongs are the standout. They grip with real authority, and the lock mechanism stays engaged in the case, which means no snagging when you reach for them.

The meat thermometer is surprisingly accurate for a kit tool. I checked it against a $60 ThermoPro and it read within 2 degrees on every test. That's close enough for home barbecue.

The grill brush does its job on both cast iron grates and stainless steel. The scraper edge handles stuck-on residue, and the bristles didn't shed — a real concern with cheaper brushes.

## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The silicone basting brush works fine for thin sauces, but it flexes too much when you're slathering a thick BBQ rub or glaze. You'll want a firmer brush for serious saucing.

The case has a cutout that looks like it should hold a bottle opener — but no opener is included. It's a minor miss, but noticeable when you're reaching for one mid-cook.

The latch on the case is functional but not rugged. If you drop it loaded with tools, there's a real risk of it popping open. It's fine for patio storage and car trips, but don't treat it like a Pelican case.

## Who should buy this

This set is ideal for:

- **New grill owners** who are starting from zero and want one purchase to cover everything
- **Weekend home cooks** who grill 2-3 times per month and want quality without premium prices

Skip it if you're a competition pitmaster with specific tool preferences, or if you already own quality individual tools. You don't need another set.

## FAQ

**Is the case durable enough for travel?**
It's fine for car trips to a park or cabin, but it's not airline luggage. The latch is the weak point — pack it carefully if you're flying.

**Does the thermometer work for all meats?**
Yes, it covers the standard range for beef, pork, chicken, and fish. The probe reads from 32°F to 572°F, which handles everything from cold-smoking to searing.

**Will the tools rust if left outside?**
The stainless steel resists rust well, but the case keeps moisture in if you store it wet. Dry tools before packing away, and they'll last for years.
