If you've ever stood on a ladder trying to blast second-story gutters while balancing a pressure washer wand in one hand, you know the pain. The McKillans Pressure Washer Extension Wands set is designed to end that circus act. At 4.7 stars from 235 ratings, it's clearly doing something right — but let's dig into whether it's right for you.

**What You Actually Get**

The kit includes two aluminum extension wands (typically 24-inch and 40-inch sections) plus a 15-foot pressure hose with a swivel fitting. The wands feature M22-14mm threaded connections on both ends, which is the standard for most residential pressure washers from brands like Ryobi, Sun Joe, Craftsman, and Simpson. The aluminum construction with powder-coated finish keeps weight down while resisting the corrosion that plagues cheaper steel wands after a season of outdoor storage.

**Real-World Performance**

Attaching the wands takes about 30 seconds — thread the wand onto your trigger gun, add the second section if needed, and connect the hose. The swivel fitting on the hose is the standout feature here; it prevents the hose from twisting into a pretzel when you're rotating the wand to reach soffits or second-story windows. The combined reach easily handles two-story cleaning without moving the machine constantly.

The M22 fittings seat snugly with no leaks at standard residential PSI ranges (up to roughly 3,100 PSI). One buyer noted, *"I was skeptical about the quick-connect claims, but these threads are machined properly — no leaks even at full pressure on my Simpson 2,800 PSI unit."* That sentiment echoes across multiple reviews: fitment is consistent, not a lottery ticket.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Reviewers repeatedly highlight three things: build quality, leak-free connections, and the hose swivel. A verified purchase wrote, *"The aluminum wands feel solid — not like the flimsy plastic-coated steel ones that bend if you look at them wrong."* Another mentioned, *"Finally cleaned my gutters without a ladder. The reach combined with the swivel hose made the job 20 minutes instead of an afternoon."*

**Where It Falls Short**

The most common complaint is that the kit doesn't include a trigger gun. If you're buying this as a standalone accessory for a machine where you lost the original gun, you'll need to purchase one separately — that's an extra $20–40 depending on quality. The locking collar that connects the wand sections can also be stiff initially; a few buyers noted needing to work it back and forth a few times before it moved smoothly. Neither issue is a dealbreaker, but both are worth knowing before you order.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Two-story homeowners** who own a pressure washer and need gutter, soffit, or second-story siding cleaning without ladder acrobatics
- **Owners of M22-compatible machines** (the vast majority of residential units) who want a reliable extension without adapter roulette
- **Detailers or RV owners** who need extended reach for washing tall vehicles or campers

Skip it if you have a single-story home and only clean patio furniture, or if you're looking for a complete gun-and-wand kit — this is an extension system, not a full trigger assembly.

**FAQ**

**Will this fit my Sun Joe or Ryobi pressure washer?**
If your machine uses M22-14mm threads (the standard for nearly all residential units), yes. Check your owner's manual or the connection point on your current gun. If your machine uses a proprietary quick-connect, you'll need an adapter.

**Can I use both wands at once for maximum reach?**
Yes, the sections thread together. Combined, you'll get roughly 5.5 feet of extension added to your existing gun and hose length — enough for most two-story applications.

**Is the hose truly kink-resistant?**
The hose itself is reinforced and the swivel fitting prevents twist-kinks at the gun end. It's not indestructible, but it handles overhead work far better than standard straight-fitted hoses.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You own a compatible pressure washer and want safe, ladder-free reach for gutters, second-story siding, or tall vehicles. The build quality justifies the price, and the swivel hose is a genuine improvement over stock setups.

**Skip it if:** You need a complete trigger gun assembly, or your pressure washer uses non-standard connections and you don't want to deal with adapters.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A solid, well-machined extension system that solves a real problem, with minor gripes that don't undermine its value.
