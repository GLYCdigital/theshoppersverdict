You've seen those hanging chairs that look dreamy in the listing photos but arrive with a cushion as thin as a bath mat. The SONGMICS Hanging Chair with Large, Thick Cushion is not that chair. This is the rare Amazon patio purchase where the product photos actually undersell the real thing — the cushion is genuinely substantial, and the included stand makes it a turnkey setup rather than a DIY headache.

**What's actually in the box**

The package includes the steel stand (in three sections), the hammock chair itself, a separate seat cushion, and a matching backrest cushion. The chair fabric is a woven polyester-cotton blend that feels more like canvas than the scratchy nylon you find on cheaper swings. The stand assembles into a classic A-frame with a 9.8-foot crossbar. Total footprint is roughly 6.5 feet wide — you'll want a clear corner of the patio or a covered porch spot.

**Real-world performance**

This chair holds up to 265 pounds, and the weight rating isn't marketing fluff. The steel tubing is 1.5 inches in diameter with welded joints, not the bolted-together thin metal you see on budget stands. The hanging mechanism uses a smooth-swivel ball bearing joint, so you can rotate gently without the jerky twisting that plagues cheaper chairs.

The cushion is the star. It's 4 inches of high-resilience foam, and it holds its shape even after hours of sitting. One buyer noted: *"I was skeptical because most of these chairs have a pathetic excuse for a cushion. This one is actually comfortable enough to fall asleep in."* That tracks with the 4.6-star average across 229 ratings — comfort is the most cited positive.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Beyond the cushion, reviewers highlight the ease of the stand setup. No drilling into ceiling joists, no finding the perfect tree branch. One verified purchase wrote: *"Assembled in about 30 minutes. I'm not handy at all and had zero issues."* The removable cushion covers are another recurring win — they zip off and go in the washing machine, which matters when you're using this outdoors with kids or pets.

**Where it falls short**

The stand is heavy — roughly 35 pounds of steel — and the crossbar section is awkward to lift into place. Plan on needing a second person for assembly, or at least a sturdy step stool and patience. One reviewer warned: *"Do not attempt the crossbar alone. I almost dropped it on my foot."*

The bigger limitation is clearance. Because the chair hangs from a stand rather than a ceiling mount, the seat sits lower to the ground. If you're over 6 feet tall, your knees will be higher than your hips, which gets uncomfortable after 20 minutes. Shorter users — under 5'8" — won't notice this.

Also, the cushion covers are polyester, not outdoor fabric. They'll fade and absorb moisture if left in the elements. This is a covered-porch or indoor-sunroom chair, not a rain-exposed backyard piece.

**Who should buy this**

- **Apartment dwellers with balconies** — the stand means no permanent installation, and it breaks down for storage
- **People under 5'10"** who want a comfortable reading nook without mounting hardware
- **Families** — the 265-pound capacity and washable covers make it kid-friendly

**Skip it if** you're tall, want a fully weatherproof outdoor lounger, or need something that fits in a compact space (the stand's footprint is substantial).

**FAQ**

*Can I hang this from a ceiling instead of using the stand?*  
Yes, the chair itself attaches to any standard ceiling hook rated for at least 300 pounds. The stand is optional — but you're paying for it, so factor that into the value.

*Is the cushion actually removable for cleaning?*  
Yes, both cushions have zippered covers that come off and machine-wash on gentle cycle. Air dry only — the foam core shouldn't go in a dryer.

*Does the chair swing or just hang stationary?*  
It swings in a gentle arc and also rotates 360 degrees at the swivel point. It's not a porch swing motion, but it's enough for a calming sway.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a comfortable, stand-alone hanging chair without ceiling installation and value a genuinely thick cushion over cheap alternatives. **Skip it if** you're tall, need weatherproof materials, or have limited floor space.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Comfort and build quality justify the price; the height limitation and assembly hassle keep it from a perfect score.
