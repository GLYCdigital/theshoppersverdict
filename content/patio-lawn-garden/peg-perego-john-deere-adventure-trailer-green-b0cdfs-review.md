If your kid has a Peg Perego John Deere ride-on tractor, this trailer is less an accessory and more a missing piece. It converts a solo joyride into a hauling machine — and kids absolutely love having a job to do. But the Adventure Trailer isn't perfect, and knowing where it cuts corners will save you from disappointment.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the trailer body (already assembled as one piece), two wheel assemblies, the steel hitch tongue, and a handful of bolts. Assembly takes roughly 20 minutes with a single Phillips screwdriver. No special tools, no wrestling with confusing diagrams — the instructions are straightforward and the parts line up cleanly.

The trailer bed measures about 37 inches long and 16 inches wide — roomy enough for a 5-year-old passenger, a load of mulch, or an army of stuffed animals. The folding rear gate swings down on hinges and locks with a real latch mechanism. That's a thoughtful touch; kids can load and unload independently without pinched fingers.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Peg Perego builds this to match their tractor lineup, and it shows. The plastic bed is thick and UV-stabilized — it won't fade to pink after a summer in the sun. The steel frame underneath adds rigidity; this isn't a flimsy wagon that flexes under load. The green paint and John Deere decals are sharp and authentic-looking, not cheap stickers.

The trailer is rated to haul 55 pounds. That's generous for a kids' accessory, and it means two toddlers can ride in the bed without stressing the hitch. The hitch itself is designed to click onto Peg Perego John Deere tractors (models like the Ground Force and Gaucho series). If you own a different brand — Power Wheels, for example — you'll need a universal hitch adapter. Check compatibility before you buy.

## Where It Falls Short

The hard plastic wheels have no bearings or suspension. On smooth pavement, they roll quietly. On grass or gravel, the ride gets rattly and loud — your kid will feel every bump, and so will the trailer bed. It's not a dealbreaker, but don't expect a smooth carriage ride.

The hitch pin, which secures the trailer to the tractor, can work loose with heavy use. A few reviewers mention re-tightening it periodically. A dab of thread-locker will solve it permanently, but it's worth knowing.

Assembly is straightforward, but the bolts are small and easy to drop in grass. Lay down a towel before you start.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:**

## FAQ

**Will this fit a Power Wheels tractor?**
Not directly. The hitch is designed for Peg Perego John Deere models. A universal hitch adapter (sold separately) can make it work, but it won't be a perfect fit. Measure your tractor's hitch height before ordering.

**Can a child actually ride in the trailer?**
Yes. The 55-pound capacity covers one or two small children. The bed is deep enough that a 4-year-old can sit comfortably with the gate up. Just keep the ride slow on uneven terrain — the trailer has no suspension.

**Is the trailer weatherproof?**
The plastic body and steel frame handle rain and sun fine. The wheels and hitch are rust-resistant. No need to store it indoors, though it'll last longer if you do during winter.
