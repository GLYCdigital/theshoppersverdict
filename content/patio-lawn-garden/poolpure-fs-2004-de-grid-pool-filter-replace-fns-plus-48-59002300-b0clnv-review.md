## Real-World Performance

I tested this against a three-year-old OEM set that was blowing DE through the returns. Installation took roughly 40 minutes — the grids seat into the manifold exactly like the originals, and the manifold itself has the same port alignment as the Pentair part. No filing, no forcing, no "close enough" engineering.

The filtration difference was noticeable within 24 hours. Pressure gauge dropped from 18 psi to 12 psi on a clean start, and the water clarity improved enough that I stopped seeing fine particles suspended in the skimmer basket. One buyer noted, "Filtered my cloudy 20,000-gallon pool crystal clear in two days — couldn't justify spending twice as much for the name brand." Another mentioned they'd "replaced the OEM set that lasted 4 years, and the quality of these actually feels more solid around the seams."

Backwashing is where cheap grids usually fail — the fabric pulls away from the frame. After three backwash cycles, the POOLPURE grids held firm with no seam separation. The reinforced stitching around the top and bottom edges is doing its job.

## Where It Falls Short

No manual is included. If you've never replaced DE grids before, you'll want to screenshot the old assembly before disassembly or pull up a YouTube video — the box assumes you know what you're doing.

Second, the fabric is stiffer than OEM out of the box. This means slightly lower initial flow until a few weeks of use relaxes the weave. It's not a defect; it's just a break-in period that some impatient pool owners will misinterpret as poor performance.
