Most tow straps are an afterthought — something you grab when the ATV gets stuck in the mud or the lawn cart needs moving. The AIRHEAD Heavy Duty Tow Harness tries to be more than that. It's a purpose-built hitch attachment, not just a strap with loops sewn on the ends. And for the 229 shoppers who've rated it 4.6 stars, it's mostly delivering.

## What You Actually Get

Opening the box, you're looking at a complete towing kit, not a bare strap. The harness uses 3/4-inch heavy-duty webbing rated at 10,000 lbs break strength — overkill for a riding mower, but the right call if you're dragging logs with a UTV or pulling a disabled vehicle across the yard. The business end is a 2-inch receiver hitch, which means it slides directly into any standard trailer hitch receiver. No adapters, no jury-rigging.

The kit includes two clevis pins to secure the strap to the receiver, plus two safety chains with hooks. The chains are the detail that sets this apart from cheaper straps — they're not decorative. If the strap ever fails under load, the chains keep whatever you're towing attached instead of sending it rolling downhill toward your neighbor's fence.

## Real-World Performance

The harness's party trick is the 2-inch rise built into the design. This matters more than you'd think. Most tow straps attach at the receiver height, which on a lawn tractor sits lower than the tow point on a utility cart. That angle puts stress on both the strap and your hitch. The rise on the AIRHEAD keeps the pull flat and level, which translates to smoother towing and less strain on your equipment.

One reviewer put it plainly: "I use this on my Kubota BX to pull a 1,000 lb dump cart. The level pull makes a huge difference — no more jerking when I start moving." Another noted the versatility: "Fits my John Deere mower and my Polaris Ranger. Bought a second one for my dad's golf cart."

The safety chains are long enough to loop around standard trailer tongues without binding, and the hooks are coated to avoid scratching your equipment.

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty time. The clevis pins that hold the strap in the receiver can work loose over time, especially if you're doing constant stop-and-go towing. A few reviewers mentioned checking them before each use. It's a five-second fix, but it's worth knowing.

Second, this isn't a lightweight accessory. It's built heavy because it's rated for heavy work, but if you're swapping it between a mower and an ATV regularly, you'll notice the weight. The straps also don't fold up as compactly as a basic tow strap — it's more of a "leave it installed" piece than something you toss in a toolbox.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you tow regularly with a lawn tractor, ATV, UTV, or golf cart — especially if you're moving heavy loads like dump carts, trailers, or brush. The level-pull design and safety chains make it a legitimate upgrade over a $15 strap from the hardware store.

**Skip it if** you only need occasional emergency towing of a stuck vehicle. A basic recovery strap is cheaper, lighter, and packs smaller. This is built for repetitive use, not emergencies.

## Common Questions

**Q: Will this fit my riding mower?**
If your mower has a standard 2-inch receiver hitch (most do, especially newer models), yes. The harness slides right in and pins in place.

**Q: Can it tow a car?**
Physically, the strap is rated for it — 10,000 lbs break strength. But realistically, you shouldn't be towing a full-size car with a lawn tractor. The strap isn't the weak link; your vehicle's drivetrain is.

**Q: Does it come with everything needed for installation?**
Yes. Two clevis pins and two safety chains with hooks are included. You need no additional hardware.

## The Verdict

The AIRHEAD Heavy Duty Tow Harness earns its 4.6 stars. It's well-built, thoughtfully designed with the level-pull rise, and includes safety features most competitors skip. The loosening pins and bulk are minor tradeoffs for the capability you get.

**Buy it if** you tow heavy loads regularly and want a harness that won't disappoint. **Skip it if** you need a compact emergency strap. For everyone else, this is the right tool for the job.
