Let’s be real: sleeping in a truck bed without a mattress is a backache waiting to happen. The **Sokeface Truck Air Mattress 5.5ft Yellow** aims to fix that for weekend campers, tailgaters, and overlanders. With a 4.2-star average across 210 ratings, it’s clearly working for many — but it’s not without quirks. Here’s the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the mattress itself, a built-in 12V air pump (wired to your truck’s cigarette lighter port), and a repair kit. No separate pump, no carrying case — the mattress folds into its own integrated storage bag. The 5.5-foot designation refers to the intended bed length, but the inflated dimensions stretch to roughly 79 inches long and 54 inches wide when the tailgate is down. That’s enough for a 6-foot adult to stretch out diagonally.

The yellow color isn’t just cosmetic — it’s high-visibility, which helps you spot the mattress in a dark bed at night. The top surface is flocked (that soft, suede-like material), while the bottom is a thicker PVC that resists punctures from bed liners.

## Real-World Performance

Inflation is the standout feature. The built-in pump runs off your truck’s 12V outlet and takes about 2.5 to 3 minutes to fully inflate. No manual pumping, no battery anxiety. Deflation is just as quick — open the valve and roll it up.

Comfort-wise, the raised side bolsters do their job: they keep you centered and prevent that “rolling off the edge” feeling that plagues flat air mattresses. The flocked top reduces noise and sticking, so you’re not waking up to the sound of your sleeping bag crinkling against rubber.

One thing to note: this is a **truck bed mattress**, not a ground tent pad. It’s designed to sit flat in the bed with the tailgate down, relying on the truck’s structure for support. If you’re using it on the ground, the bolsters feel less stable.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring theme in positive reviews is **fit and ease of use**. One verified buyer wrote: *“Fits my F-150’s 5.5-foot bed perfectly with the tailgate down. Inflated in under three minutes and slept better than my camping cot.”* Another noted: *“The built-in pump is genius — I don’t have to carry a separate air pump anymore.”*

Durability also gets props. Multiple reviewers mention using it for 10+ nights across rough terrain without leaks. The PVC bottom holds up against bed liner ridges and stray debris.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and the Sokeface has two honest weaknesses.

**First, the flocked top stains.** If you’re climbing in with dusty boots or muddy gear, that soft surface shows it. Several reviewers noted discoloration after the first trip. It cleans up, but it requires effort — not ideal for rough-and-tumble use.

**Second, the pump is a double-edged sword.** The 12V pump is fast, but it’s useless if your truck battery dies or you’re parked somewhere without a cigarette lighter. There’s no manual inflation valve for quick touch-ups, so you’re locked into the electrical system. One reviewer wrote: *“Great mattress, but I wish there was a manual backup valve. My truck outlet stopped working and I was stuck with a flat pad.”*

Also note: this is a **5.5-foot** size. If your truck has a 6.5-foot bed, it’ll fit but leave a gap at the tailgate. And if you’re over 6-foot-2, you’ll feel the length limit.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Weekend campers** who want a quick, comfortable setup without hauling tents and cots
- **Tailgaters** who want a lounge pad for game-day parking lots
- **Overlanders** with a 5.5-6 foot bed who want a dedicated sleep system
- **Budget-conscious buyers** who want a sub-$100 mattress with a built-in pump

**Skip it if** you need a ground-use mattress, if you’re over 6-foot-2, or if you camp in areas without reliable truck power.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my truck?**
A: It’s made for 5.5 to 6-foot short beds. With the tailgate down, it expands to 79 inches. Check your bed measurements — if your bed is exactly 5.5 feet, it fits flush. Longer beds leave a gap.

**Q: Can I inflate it manually if the pump fails?**
A: No. There’s no manual inflation valve. You’re entirely dependent on the 12V pump. Carry a portable jump starter with a 12V outlet as a backup.

**Q: Is it comfortable for side sleepers?**
A: Yes, reasonably. The flocked top reduces friction, and the bolsters support your hips and shoulders. It’s firmer than a home mattress but softer than sleeping on the truck bed itself.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a fast, comfortable truck-bed sleep solution with zero setup hassle and a built-in pump. **Skip it if** you need manual inflation backup, a larger size for longer beds, or stain resistance for heavy-duty use.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid value for the price, with a few material and power-source compromises that keep it from being perfect.
