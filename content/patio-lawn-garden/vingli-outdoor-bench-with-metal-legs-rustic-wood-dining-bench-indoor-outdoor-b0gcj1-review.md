If you've been hunting for a farmhouse-style bench that doesn't cost a small fortune, the VINGLI Outdoor Bench with Metal Legs has likely crossed your screen. At 4.3 stars across 215 ratings, it's clearly earning its keep on patios and porches. But before you click "add to cart," let's talk about what this bench actually delivers — and where it cuts corners.

## What's actually in the box

You get two main components: a pre-assembled acacia wood seat and a welded steel frame. The wood slats are thicker than I expected for this price point — roughly 1.5 inches — and they're sanded smooth with no rough edges right out of the box. The metal legs arrive powder-coated in matte black, and the X-brace crossbars are already welded to the frame, not bolted on. That's a meaningful quality difference versus cheaper benches that rely on flimsy corner brackets.

Assembly is genuinely simple. Four bolts connect the seat to the frame, and the included hex wrench does the job. Most buyers report being done in under ten minutes. One reviewer noted, *"I put it together while my coffee was still hot. The holes lined up perfectly — no wrestling."*

## Real-world performance

The bench measures 48 inches wide, which seats two adults comfortably or three kids. The seat height lands around 18 inches — which is where you need to pay attention. That's perfect for a low-profile fire pit or a 28-inch counter-height table. But if your dining table is a standard 30 inches, this bench will feel noticeably low. Several reviewers flagged this exact issue, so measure your table before ordering.

The acacia wood is the star here. It's naturally dense and water-resistant, but it arrives untreated. Buyers who applied a sealant or teak oil within the first month report the wood stays golden-brown for years. Those who skipped it saw the surface gray out within a season. That's not a defect — it's the nature of untreated hardwood — but it's worth knowing going in.

The metal frame is the real workhorse. The X-brace design keeps the bench stable even on slightly uneven patio pavers. I didn't experience any rocking or twisting when shifting weight side-to-side. The powder coating resists chipping, though as with any outdoor metal, expect some surface rust if you leave it in standing water for weeks on end.

## What buyers consistently praise

The durability-to-price ratio comes up again and again. One long-term owner wrote, *"Had this bench for two years now, through rain and snow. The wood has a nice patina and the frame hasn't rusted through."* Another praised the weight — roughly 20 pounds — saying it's light enough to move around but heavy enough not to blow over in a gust.

## Where it falls short

Three honest complaints surface in the reviews. First, the untreated wood requires maintenance. If you want it to stay looking new, budget 30 minutes with a sealer within the first month. Second, the 18-inch seat height is a mismatch for many standard dining setups. Third, the seat slats sit flush with no drainage gap, so water pools after heavy rain. Standing water accelerates wood wear, so consider storing it or tipping it on its side during downpours.

## Who should buy this

- **Fire pit loungers** — the low profile is perfect for pulling up to a flame
- **Farmhouse-style decorators** — the rustic wood and black metal match that aesthetic without the custom-furniture price tag
- **Small patio dwellers** — 48 inches fits balconies and compact spaces without overwhelming them

Skip it if you need a bench for a standard 30-inch dining table, or if you want zero-maintenance outdoor furniture. This one needs a little seasonal attention.

## FAQ

**Q: Can it stay outside in winter?**
Yes, but cover it or move it under an awning. Prolonged snow and ice will accelerate wood checking. Sealing the wood before winter makes a significant difference.

**Q: Does the metal frame scratch?**
The powder coat is durable but not indestructible. Dragging it across concrete will scuff it. Lift, don't drag.

**Q: What weight capacity does it hold?**
Most reviewers report two average-sized adults (roughly 400-500 pounds combined) with no flex or strain. It's not rated for jumping or roughhousing.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a sturdy, good-looking bench for a fire pit, porch, or entryway, and you're willing to spend 30 minutes sealing the wood once a year.

**Skip it if** you need standard dining height, or you want an outdoor piece you can ignore entirely.

The VINGLI Outdoor Bench hits a sweet spot for casual, rustic outdoor seating. It's not perfect — the seat height and maintenance requirements demand attention — but for the price, it delivers solid construction and honest materials. With a bit of seasonal care, this bench will serve you for years.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A well-built, attractive bench with a couple of clear caveats that don't diminish its value for the right buyer.
