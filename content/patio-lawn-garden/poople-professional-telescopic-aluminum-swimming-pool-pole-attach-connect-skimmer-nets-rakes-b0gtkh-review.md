## The $30 Pool Pole That Actually Does the Job

Let's be honest: Most telescopic pool poles are flimsy aluminum tubes with connectors that strip after a season. The POOPLE Professional Telescopic Aluminum Pool Pole aims higher, and for the most part, it delivers. With 210 ratings averaging 4.2 stars, this is one of those rare budget tools that earns its place in your pool maintenance arsenal.

## What You Get

The box contains a single telescopic pole that extends from approximately 4 feet to 8 feet in length. The anodized aluminum finish gives it a noticeably more premium feel than the painted poles you'll find at big-box retailers. The universal threaded end fits standard pool skimmers, nets, brushes, and rakes — no proprietary locking system to worry about.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this pole with a standard 18-inch skimmer net and a wall brush. The extension mechanism works smoothly: twist the collar, slide to your desired length, and lock it down. At full extension, there's minimal flex — impressive for a pole in this price range. The anodized finish also does what it should: it shrugs off chlorine exposure and doesn't develop the chalky white corrosion that plagues cheaper poles.

The threaded connector grips attachments firmly. I didn't experience any of the dreaded "net falls off mid-scoop" moments that plague lesser poles. However, the locking collar does have a quirk: vigorous scrubbing against a pool wall can cause it to loosen slightly. It's a 5-second fix to re-tighten, but worth noting if you're doing a deep clean.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The consensus among verified purchasers centers on value and durability. One reviewer noted, "Bought this after my third cheap pole broke. This one feels solid — the aluminum is thicker than what you get at the hardware store." Another mentioned, "Works perfectly with my existing skimmer. No adapters needed." The telescopic range gets consistent praise, with several owners mentioning it eliminates the need for separate deep-end and shallow-end poles.

## Where It Falls Short

The lack of a foam grip is the most common complaint. When your hands are wet, the smooth aluminum handle gets slippery — not dangerous, but annoying during longer cleaning sessions. A few buyers also mentioned that the pole sections can separate if you over-extend them beyond the marked stop point. Pay attention to the visual indicator and you'll be fine.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Above-ground pool owners**: The 4-8 foot range covers most above-ground pool depths perfectly.
- **Budget-conscious buyers**: You're getting 80% of the performance of $60+ poles at roughly half the price.
- **Anyone replacing a broken pole**: The universal connector means your existing attachments will work immediately.
- **Skip it if** you need a pole longer than 8 feet for a deep in-ground pool, or if you have arthritis — the smooth handle won't do you favors.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my existing skimmer?**
A: Almost certainly. The pole uses the standard 1-1/4 inch threaded connection that's industry standard. If your skimmer or net came from a pool supply store in the last 10 years, it'll fit.

**Q: How much weight can it hold?**
A: It handles standard skimmers, nets, and brushes without issue. For heavy-duty algae brushes or leaf rakes, keep the pole at mid-length to avoid excessive flex.

**Q: Will it corrode in saltwater pools?**
A: The anodized aluminum resists saltwater better than painted poles, but no aluminum product is truly saltwater-proof. Rinse the pole with fresh water after use if you have a salt chlorine generator.
