I tested this across two scenarios: a mulched flower bed and a gravel side path. In the flower bed, I cut X-shaped slits for hostas and covered with 2 inches of cedar mulch. Eight weeks later, zero weeds pushed through the fabric itself — the only growth appeared at the edges where I'd skimped on overlap. That's on me, not the product.

The gravel path was the real test. River rock is heavy, and cheap fabric tears under that weight. The LIUKANGZE held firm. No rips, no shifting, even after a pressure washer pass to clean the stones. The 5 oz weight is the sweet spot: heavy enough to resist punctures, but still flexible enough to contour over small bumps in the soil.

Drainage is where this fabric separates from cheaper options. During a two-inch downpour, water passed through quickly — no runoff or pooling. That's critical.
