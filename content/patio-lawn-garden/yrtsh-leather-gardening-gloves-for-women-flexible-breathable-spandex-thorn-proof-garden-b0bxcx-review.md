**Where they fall short**

Here's the honest tradeoff list:

- **Leather stiffens when wet.** Soak these through, and the palm becomes stiff and uncomfortable until fully dry — which takes several hours. Don't plan on using them for back-to-back watering sessions.
- **Not for heavy-duty demolition work.** These are gardening gloves, not work gloves. Dragging lumber or moving rocks will wear the leather down faster than expected.
- **One-season durability for heavy users.** If you garden daily for hours, the stitching at the thumb crotch may start to separate by month six. For weekend gardeners, they'll last much longer.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I wash these gloves?**
A: Hand wash with mild soap, air dry flat. Don't machine wash — it'll ruin the leather. Spot cleaning works for most dirt.

**Q: Are they actually thorn-proof?**
A: They're thorn-resistant, not armor. Goatskin handles rose thorns, blackberry canes, and brambles well. But heavy hawthorn or thick woody thorns can eventually pierce through with enough force.

**Q: What sizes are available, and how do they fit?**
A: Small through X-Large. They run small, especially in the palm width. Measure your hand circumference and add one size if you're borderline.
