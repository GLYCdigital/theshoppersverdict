**Real-World Performance**

On 18V power, this thing isn't going to fell a tree. It's a limbing tool. With a charged 4Ah battery, expect 20–30 minutes of intermittent cutting — enough for a weekend's worth of dead branches, overgrown shrubs, and light pruning. The 8-inch bar handles branches up to roughly 6 inches in diameter. Push it past that and you'll stall the chain.

The cutting action is smooth for a cordless unit. The chain speed is adequate, not aggressive. If you're used to gas-powered equipment, you'll notice the difference — this is a precision tool, not a demolition tool. It excels at clean cuts on live wood and dry branches, but it will bog down on dense hardwood.

One thing buyers consistently mention: the pole is sturdy. No flimsy flexing when you're reaching overhead. The extension locks positively, and the grip stays comfortable even through a longer session.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be direct about the weaknesses.

**Battery drain is real.** The 18V platform struggles under sustained load. If you have a big job — say, multiple trees with heavy deadwood — you'll be swapping batteries frequently. A 2Ah battery will die in under 15 minutes of continuous cutting.

**Tool-only pricing is deceptive.** At the current price point, you're looking at $50–$80 minimum for a compatible battery and charger. That pushes the real cost closer to $150–$180. If you're not already in the RYOBI ecosystem, factor that in before you commit.

**The chain is proprietary.** The 8-inch bar and chain are RYOBI-specific. Replacement chains run around $15–$20, and you can't swap in a generic brand. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's a lock-in.

**No automatic tensioning.** You'll need to manually adjust chain tension with a wrench as the chain stretches. It's a minor annoyance, but worth knowing if you're used to tool-free tensioning systems.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Does this come with a battery and charger?**
A: No. The "Tool ONLY" designation means it's bare. You need a RYOBI 18V battery (any capacity) and a charger. If you have other RYOBI tools, you already have what you need.

**Q: Can I use it on thick branches?**
A: The 8-inch bar handles up to about 6 inches in diameter. Anything thicker will stall the motor. This is a pruning tool, not a felling saw.

**Q: Is the pole really oil-free?**
A: Yes. The chain runs sealed. You never add bar oil. However, you'll still need to periodically sharpen the chain — oil-free doesn't mean maintenance-free.
