Let’s be blunt: lawn chemicals are a crowded shelf, and most weed-and-feed combos are mediocre at both jobs. The **Pennington Full Season Weed & Feed + Crabgrass Preventer (25-0-8)** tries to do three things at once — kill broadleaf weeds, feed your turf, and stop crabgrass for four months. Does it pull it off? Mostly yes, with caveats that matter.

## What's Actually in the Bag

This is a granular product, and the label tells you what you're paying for. The **25-0-8 NPK** means heavy nitrogen (25%) for green-up, zero phosphorus (skip this if you're near waterways or have high-P soil already), and 8% potassium for root strength. The crabgrass control comes from **dithiopyr (Dimension)** — a pre-emergent that also catches early crabgrass that's already germinated. That's a meaningful difference from straight pre-emergents like dithiopyr-only products; it gives you a small window of post-emergent control.

The "4M" in the name is the promise: up to four months of crabgrass prevention with a single application. That's legit for dithiopyr at proper rates, but only if you apply before soil temps hit 55°F consistently.

## Performance: The Real-World Story

The fertilizer part works. Users consistently report darker, thicker grass within 7–10 days of application when they water it in properly. The nitrogen is slow-release (hence the "RF" — resin-coated), which reduces burn risk compared to quick-release urea products. One reviewer noted, *"Lawn greened up fast, no burn spots, and it held color for a solid month."* That tracks with the formulation.

The weed control is solid for dandelions, clover, and chickweed — the label's target weeds. But it's not a broad-spectrum herbicide. If you have creeping Charlie, wild violet, or other tough perennials, you'll need a spot treatment with something like triclopyr. Don't expect this to fix a mature weed infestation.

The crabgrass prevention is where the mixed reviews come from. When applied at the right time (early spring, before soil temps hit 55°F), it works. The 3.9/5 Amazon rating reflects the reality that many buyers miss that window. One verified purchase complained, *"Applied in late May and still got crabgrass by July. Waste of money."* That's not the product failing — that's a timing error. The label is clear, but if you're not monitoring soil temps, you're gambling.

## Where It Falls Short

**Watering requirements are non-negotiable.** This product must be watered in within 24 hours of application — ideally within a day. If you apply it and get a dry week, you've wasted your money. That's a real drawback for anyone without an irrigation system or who can't control rainfall timing.

**It's not for new lawns.** If you've seeded or laid sod in the past few months, skip this. The pre-emergent chemistry will prevent new grass seed from germinating. You need to wait until your new grass has been mowed at least twice. That's a hard constraint that isn't obvious to first-time lawn owners.

**Coverage confusion.** The bag covers 4,000 square feet at the standard rate, but if you're applying to a densely shaded or heavily weeded lawn, you may need a follow-up application for full season coverage. The "Full Season" name overpromises for high-pressure crabgrass zones.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The proactive lawn owner** who tracks soil temps and can water within 24 hours — you'll get genuine 4-month crabgrass control.
- **The busy homeowner** who wants one pass for feeding and broadleaf weed control without juggling multiple products.
- **The established lawn owner** with existing turf that's mostly healthy, just needs maintenance.

**Skip it if** you're seeding this spring, you're in the deep South with year-round crabgrass pressure, or you can't commit to the watering schedule.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I overseed after using this?**
A: No — not for at least 3–4 months. The dithiopyr prevents all seed germination, including grass seed. Plan your seeding for fall if you use this in spring.

**Q: When exactly should I apply it?**
A: When forsythia blooms or soil temps hit 55°F (usually early spring). Apply to dry grass, then water in within 24 hours. Don't wait for "it looks like it's time" — that's usually too late for crabgrass prevention.

**Q: Is it safe for pets and kids?**
A: Once watered in and dried, yes. Keep them off until the granules are gone and the lawn is dry — typically 24 hours after watering.
