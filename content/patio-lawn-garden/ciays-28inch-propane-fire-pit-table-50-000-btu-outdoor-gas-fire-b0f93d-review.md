At 50,000 BTU, this thing produces enough heat to actually extend your patio season into the cool months, and with 235 Amazon ratings averaging 4.7 stars, buyers consistently confirm it's the real deal.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the assembled table base and top, a 10-foot hose with regulator, a bag of lava rocks and glass stones, and the hardware kit. There's no tank included — that's standard for this category, so budget for a 20-pound propane tank if you don't already own one. The unit measures 28 inches in diameter and stands about 24 inches tall, which places it at standard coffee table height. As shown in the product image, the black tempered glass tabletop and stainless steel burner ring give it a clean, modern look that doesn't scream "cheap patio special."

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The steel body has a powder-coated finish that resists rust, and the burner ring is stainless steel — the two materials that matter most in outdoor heating equipment. In use, the flame adjusts from a low, lazy 12-inch flicker to a roaring column that's genuinely aggressive. Buyers consistently mention the heat output. One reviewer put it plainly: "This thing puts out serious heat. We sat around it in 40-degree weather and were comfortable." That tracks with the BTU rating; most competitors in this size range sit at 30,000–40,000 BTU.

The piezo ignition sparks on the first or second click in calm conditions. In wind, it's less reliable — a few buyers noted needing multiple attempts. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, but keep a grill lighter in the side table drawer.

The tabletop is a functional surface. You can set drinks and plates on it while the fire runs, and the heat doesn't warp or discolor the tempered glass. The tank hides neatly behind the hinged door, and the included cover for the tank opening keeps the propane connection protected when not in use.

## What Buyers Praise

The recurring themes in positive reviews are heat output, assembly ease, and value. Multiple buyers commented on how quickly it assembled. One wrote: "Took me 20 minutes with my wife holding the top. Everything lined up perfectly." Another noted the build quality: "Feels much heavier and more solid than the price suggests."

The look earns consistent compliments too — the clean lines and glass stones photograph well and look more expensive than the price point.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and three tradeoffs matter here. First, there's no included cover. This is an outdoor unit, and the powder-coated steel will fade and weather if left exposed. Budget an extra $25 for a proper cover. Second, the glass stones come in a single bag — you'll spend a few minutes spreading them evenly over the lava rock base. It's not hard, but it's an extra step. Third, the wind sensitivity on ignition, as mentioned. If your patio is exposed, keep a backup ignition method handy.

One more note: the unit is rated for propane only. If you have natural gas at your patio connection, this isn't the unit for you — you'd need a conversion kit that isn't sold by the manufacturer.

## Who Should Buy This

The Ciays 28-inch is ideal for apartment dwellers and renters who want a real fire feature without a permanent installation. It's also a strong fit for anyone hosting fall or spring gatherings who wants actual warmth, not just ambiance. If you're looking for a purely decorative piece with a tiny flame, this is overkill — buy something smaller and cheaper.
