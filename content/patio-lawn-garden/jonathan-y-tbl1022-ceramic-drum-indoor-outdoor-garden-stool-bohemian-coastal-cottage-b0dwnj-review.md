## Honest Shortcomings

The glossy glaze shows water spots if you leave it wet after rain. It's not damage — just mineral deposits that wipe off — but it's noticeable on darker colors. Buyers in humid climates mentioned this more than once.

The open bottom is a minor design tweak that matters: if you place it directly on a wood deck or tile, moisture can get trapped underneath. A cheap felt pad or rubber foot solves it, but it's worth knowing upfront.

Finally, available colors come and go. If you fall in love with a specific finish, grab it when it's in stock. JONATHAN Y runs popular colorways in batches, and restocks aren't always quick.
