## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my Briggs & Stratton engine?**
Check your engine's part number. If it uses the 593260, 798452, or 334404 air filter, yes. The housing and mounting are identical to OEM specs.

**Q: Is this a washable filter?**
No. It's a dry paper element that should be replaced, not cleaned with water or compressed air. Tapping out loose debris is fine, but the paper will degrade over time.

**Q: How often should I replace it?**
Every 25 hours of operation or annually, whichever comes first. If you mow in heavy dust, check it monthly and replace it when the paper looks dark or the foam is clogged.
