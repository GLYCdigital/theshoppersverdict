**What You Get**

The package contains the flag stand itself: a two-piece metal pole that threads together mid-shaft, a ground stake, and a ring-style flag attachment at the top. Assembly takes under a minute — screw the two sections together, push the stake into the ground, slide your flag onto the ring. No tools required.

The pole's straight design is the standout feature here. Most budget flag stands taper or bow slightly, which makes flags hang at awkward angles. ESTTOP's version stays perfectly vertical, which matters more than you'd think when you're looking at it from across the yard.

**Where It Falls Short**

A few reviewers recommend adding a small amount of concrete or using a heavier mallet to seat it deeper. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

Also note: this stand is designed for ground installation only. If you want to mount a flag holder on a deck, porch, or paver patio, you'll need a separate adapter — nothing in the box helps with hardscape mounting.
