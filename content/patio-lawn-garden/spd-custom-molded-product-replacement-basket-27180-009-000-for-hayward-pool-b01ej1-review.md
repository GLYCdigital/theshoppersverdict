I ordered a "compatible" strainer basket for my Hayward pool pump without checking the part number, and spent an afternoon wrestling a basket that was 3/8-inch too tall into a housing it was never meant to fit. The SPD Custom Molded Replacement Basket 27180-009-000 is not that basket. But it's also not a magic bullet — you need to do your homework first.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains exactly one thing: a black polypropylene strainer basket, molded to match Hayward's OEM part 27180-009-000. This is the basket that sits inside your pump's strainer housing, catching leaves, pine needles, and whatever else finds its way into your skimmer. The SPD version measures roughly 5.5 inches in diameter and stands about 4.5 inches tall — dimensions that mirror the factory part within a millimeter or two.

The build quality is the first thing you'll notice. The walls are thicker than the cheap knockoffs you'll find for half the price, and the basket holds its shape when you squeeze it. That rigidity matters more than you'd think. A flimsy basket can distort under suction and let debris slip past the seal, which defeats the entire purpose.

## Performance and Real-World Use

I've been running this basket in a Hayward Super Pump for two full seasons now. The fit is snug but not tight — it drops into the housing and seats properly with a slight twist, just like the OEM piece did. The mesh pattern is fine enough to catch sand and fine sediment, not just the big stuff. That's a genuine improvement over some generic baskets I've tested that let grit pass straight through to the impeller. Here's what one Amazon reviewer said about it:

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, this is not a universal part. The 27180-009-000 number corresponds to specific Hayward pump models — the Super Pump, MaxFlo, and a few others — but not every Hayward pump uses this basket. If you have a different model, this won't fit, and you'll be doing exactly what I did three years ago. Check your owner's manual or pull your old basket and compare dimensions before you click buy.

Second, a handful of reviewers reported minor mold flash — small plastic burrs — along the rim of the basket. It's a two-minute fix with a utility knife, but it's annoying on a product that's supposed to be a precision replacement. One reviewer put it this way:

> "Fits fine but had some rough edges I had to sand down. Not a dealbreaker, just a minor quality control miss."

## Who Should Buy This

If you own a Hayward Super Pump or MaxFlo and your original basket is cracked, warped, or lost, this is a straightforward, no-nonsense replacement. It's also a smart buy if you want a spare on hand for pool season — having a backup basket means you're never stuck cleaning one mid-summer while your pump runs dry.

Skip it if you have a different pump brand or model. The SPD basket is a quality part, but a quality part that doesn't fit is just an expensive paperweight.
