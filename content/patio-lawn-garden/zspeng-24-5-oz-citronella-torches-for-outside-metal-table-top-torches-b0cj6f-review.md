Priced as a mid-range set (typically $30–$40 for two), these aren't the flimsiest options on Amazon — and they're not the priciest either. ## What's Actually in the Box

The set ships with two torches, each standing about 19 inches tall with a 4.5-inch diameter metal base. The 24.5 oz fuel capacity per torch is the headline feature — that's roughly double what most tabletop torches hold. You also get two fiberglass wicks pre-installed and a metal snuffer cap for each. You'll need to supply your own citronella fuel (or a 50/50 mix of citronella oil and torch fuel), which is standard for this category.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The construction is where ZSPENG separates itself from the $15 junk sets. The base is weighted — one buyer noted, "These don't tip over even when my dog's leash catches them." The metal body is powder-coated, which holds up better against rust than the painted options in this price range. The flame adjustment knob is genuinely functional, letting you dial down to a small blue flame for longer burn time or crank it up for a bigger repellent zone.

Burn time math: at a moderate flame setting, you're looking at roughly 8–10 hours per fill. At full blast, expect closer to 6. That means a 24.5 oz fill covers a solid weekend of evening use. The fiberglass wick burns clean and doesn't mushroom out the way cotton wicks do.

The 4.6-star average holds up in the written reviews. Recurring themes:

## Where They Fall Short

No product is perfect, and the ZSPENG set has a few honest annoyances. The fuel tank isn't removable — refilling means either tilting the whole torch over a fuel can or using a funnel with a flexible spout. It's manageable but messier than it should be. A few reviewers also noted the wick degrades after a couple months of regular use, and ZSPENG doesn't include spares in the box. You'll need to order replacements (standard 1/8-inch fiberglass wicks work fine). Finally, the powder coating will chip if you bang the torches against concrete — treat them with reasonable care.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask

**How long does one fill last?**
At a medium flame, expect 8–10 hours per 24.5 oz fill. That's roughly three to four evenings of typical use.

**Do these actually repel mosquitoes?**
The citronella fuel does create a repellent zone, but it's not a force field. They work best on a patio or deck where the scent lingers — not in a large open yard with wind.

**Can I use regular torch fuel instead of citronella?**
Yes, but you'll lose the repellent benefit. A 50/50 mix of citronella oil and standard torch fuel is the sweet spot for both burn time and bug deterrence.
