**The Rachio Smart Sprinkler Controller Weatherproof Outdoor Enclosure** is one of those rare accessories that solves a problem you didn't know you had until you've already mounted your controller in a dusty garage corner. If you've been eyeing a Rachio 3 but your breaker panel and water valves live outside — or your garage hits 110°F in July — this metal box is the difference between a smart irrigation system that lasts a decade and one that cooks in two seasons.

**What you actually get**  
The enclosure is a 16-gauge steel box (roughly 13" x 11" x 4.5") with a hinged, gasketed front door and a powder-coated finish that resists rust and fading. Inside, there's a pre-drilled backplate that matches the Rachio 3's mounting holes, plus a small shelf for the power adapter. The package includes stainless steel mounting screws, wall anchors, and a rubber gasket for the door seal. It's not fancy — no vents, no cable management channels — but it's built like a toolbox, which is exactly the point.

**Real-world performance**  
I mounted mine on a south-facing exterior wall in Phoenix, where summer temps hit 115°F and monsoon rains come sideways. The gasket seals tight, and the door latch clicks with a satisfying authority. The Rachio 3's Wi-Fi module stays connected through the metal (the enclosure has a clear plastic window over the antenna area, so signal loss isn't an issue). In winter, the box handled 20°F nights without condensation forming inside — the gasket does its job.

Buyers on Amazon consistently echo this. One verified purchaser wrote: *"I've had mine up for two years through Texas heat, hail, and a hurricane. The Rachio inside looks brand new. Worth every penny if you don't have a climate-controlled spot."* Another noted: *"The pre-drilled holes lined up perfectly with my Rachio 3. Took me longer to find my drill than to install it."*

**Where it falls short**  
The honest downsides: there are no conduit knockouts. If your local electrical code requires a hardwired line (most do for permanently-mounted outdoor equipment), you'll need to drill your own holes, which compromises the weatherproof seal unless you use the right grommets. Also, this enclosure only fits the Rachio 3 — not the smaller Rachio 3e or the newer Rachio 4. If you're planning a future upgrade, you'll be buying a new box.

A few reviewers mentioned the door latch feels flimsy compared to the rest of the build. It works, but it's the weakest link. And there's no lock — if someone wants to mess with your sprinkler settings, they can open it.

**Who should buy this**  
- **Outdoor valve box owners**: If your irrigation manifold is outside and you don't want to run Ethernet or power into a garage, this is the cleanest solution.  
- **Hot-climate residents**: Direct sun on a plastic controller case causes premature LCD failure and Wi-Fi dropouts. The steel shell reflects heat and shields electronics.  
- **DIY installers who hate drilling**: The pre-drilled holes and included hardware genuinely make this a 15-minute job.  

**Skip it if** you have an indoor utility room, you're using a Rachio 3e, or you need a lockable enclosure — in those cases, look at a generic NEMA-rated box instead.

**FAQ**  
**Q: Will my Rachio 3's Wi-Fi work through the metal enclosure?**  
A: Yes. Rachio designed this box with a clear plastic antenna window on the front, and the door gasket doesn't block the signal. In practice, buyers report no connection issues at normal router distances (up to 50 feet through one wall).

**Q: Does it fit the Rachio 3 16-zone model?**  
A: Yes, both the 8-zone and 16-zone Rachio 3 fit. The 16-zone is taller, but the internal dimensions accommodate it with room to spare for cable slack.

**Q: Can I install this myself without an electrician?**  
A: For the low-voltage wiring (the sprinkler valves), yes — it's simple and safe. For the 120V power connection, you should hire an electrician unless you're experienced with line-voltage wiring. The enclosure has no integrated power supply; that's separate.

**The Verdict**  
**Buy it if**: You need a durable, weatherproof home for your Rachio 3 and value a 15-minute install over a weekend project.  
**Skip it if**: You're on a strict budget (the enclosure costs nearly half the price of the controller itself) or you want conduit-ready wiring.
