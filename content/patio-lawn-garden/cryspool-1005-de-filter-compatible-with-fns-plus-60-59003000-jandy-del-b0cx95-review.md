## What You're Actually Getting

The Cryspool 1005 is a complete DE filter grid assembly — not a single element. You get the full set of grids that slide into your FNS Plus 60 tank, built with what the manufacturer describes as heavy-duty poly mesh over rigid frames. The end caps are reinforced plastic, and the assembly includes the manifold connections that lock into your filter's header. It's designed as a drop-in replacement for the Jandy 59003, meaning the dimensions match the OEM part precisely — no trimming, no forcing, no creative engineering in your pump room.

The standout feature buyers consistently mention is the fit. One verified purchaser put it plainly: *"Bolted right in place of my old Jandy grids. ## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and the Cryspool has a couple of quirks worth knowing. The most common complaint involves the gaskets — several buyers noted they arrived stiff and required a soak in warm water before they'd seat properly against the manifold. It's a minor annoyance that adds 10 minutes to installation, but it's worth planning for.

The second issue is compatibility confusion. The Cryspool 1005 fits the FNS Plus 60 and the 59003 part number specifically. It does *not* fit the FNS Plus 48 or the Del 60, even though they look similar. A handful of negative reviews stem from buyers who ordered without verifying their model number. Check your filter's label before you order.

## FAQ

**Will this work with my Jandy FNS Plus 60 if I have the 59003 part number?**
Yes. The Cryspool 1005 is designed as a direct replacement for the Jandy 59003. Verify your filter's model number on the tank label first — it must be FNS Plus 60, not 48 or Del 60.

**How often should I clean these grids?**
Follow the same schedule as your OEM grids — typically every 3-6 months depending on pool usage and debris load. When your pressure gauge reads 10 PSI above clean baseline, it's time to hose them off.

**Does it include DE powder?**
No. This is just the filter grid assembly. You'll need to add fresh DE powder after installation, per your filter's manual.
