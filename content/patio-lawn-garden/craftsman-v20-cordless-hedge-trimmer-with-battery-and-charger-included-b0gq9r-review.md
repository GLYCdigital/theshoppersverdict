Let's be honest about hedge trimmers: most of them are either underpowered toys or corded beasts that tangle you in extension cords. The CRAFTSMAN V20 sits in the increasingly crowded middle ground, and after digging through 229 ratings and the spec sheet, here's what actually matters.

**What you get in the box**

This kit includes the trimmer, a 2.0Ah V20 lithium-ion battery, and a charger. That's the full story — no hidden "battery sold separately" trap, which is refreshing for a cordless tool at this price point. The trimmer itself measures 22 inches of dual-action blade, which is the sweet spot for residential hedges. Dual-action means both blades move, canceling out much of the vibration that makes cheaper single-blade trimmers feel like they're trying to shake your forearms off.

The pivoting handle offers three positions: straight for overhead work, angled for side trimming, and a downward position that saves your back when you're cleaning up under shrubs. It's not a revolutionary feature, but it's executed well enough that you'll actually use it.

**Real-world performance**

With a 2.0Ah battery, you're looking at roughly 25–35 minutes of actual cutting time. That's fine for a typical suburban hedge job — think 50 to 75 linear feet of established boxwood or privet. What you won't get is a full day of property maintenance. If your yard is more "estate" than "subdivision," budget for a second battery or look at the 4.0Ah kits.

The cutting power handles standard hedge growth without complaint. The 3/4-inch branch capacity is the spec to note — anything thicker and you'll bog down or stall. Multiple reviewers noted this isn't a brush cutter; it's a hedge groomer. One 4-star reviewer put it plainly: "Great for maintaining established hedges, but don't expect it to chew through overgrown juniper." Fair warning.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The vibration reduction gets the most love. Multiple reviewers mention being able to trim with one hand — something you rarely read about with cordless trimmers. One buyer wrote, "I have arthritis and this is the first trimmer I've used that doesn't leave my hands numb after 20 minutes." That's meaningful feedback, not marketing copy.

The included battery and charger also score points. For anyone not already invested in the V20 ecosystem, this kit removes the biggest friction point of cordless tools — the hidden cost of accessories. And since the V20 platform spans chainsaws, leaf blowers, and mowers, this trimmer can be a gateway into a broader system.

**Where it falls short**

The 2.0Ah battery is the obvious compromise. CRAFTSMAN clearly made a cost decision here, and it shows. You'll want a spare if your hedges run longer than a typical suburban lot. The battery also takes about an hour to fully charge, so you can't just swap and go — you need to plan your charging.

The blade gap is the other limitation. At 3/4-inch capacity, this is strictly a maintenance tool. If your hedges have been neglected for a season or two, you'll need loppers or a heavier-duty trimmer to establish a shape before this can maintain it.

**Who should buy this**

- **The V20 ecosystem starter** — if you're eyeing CRAFTSMAN's cordless lineup, this kit gets you a battery and charger you can use across their tools.
- **The casual homeowner** — established hedges, light annual trimming, and no desire to wrestle extension cords.
- **The comfort-focused buyer** — if vibration bothers you or you have hand/joint issues, the dual-action blade genuinely helps.

**Skip it if** you have overgrown, thick-branched hedges that need serious restoration, or if you need all-day battery life without stopping.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does the battery actually last?**
A: Expect 25–35 minutes of continuous trimming with the included 2.0Ah battery. Intermittent use stretches that further since the trigger isn't locked on.

**Q: Can this trimmer cut through thick branches?**
A: It handles up to 3/4 inch comfortably. Beyond that, it struggles and you risk stalling the motor. Use loppers for anything thicker.

**Q: Is the battery compatible with other CRAFTSMAN V20 tools?**
A: Yes. The V20 battery platform is shared across CRAFTSMAN's cordless lineup — chainsaws, blowers, string trimmers, and more.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, vibration-friendly cordless trimmer for regular hedge maintenance, especially if you're starting fresh with the V20 platform.

**Skip it if** your hedges are severely overgrown, or you need all-day runtime without stopping to recharge.
