If you've got an indoor cat that hacks up hairballs on your rug twice a week and has packed on a few extra pounds from a sedentary lifestyle, you're juggling two problems. Purina Cat Chow Naturals Indoor with Real Chicken & Turkey aims to solve both with one bag. At 4.8 stars from 240 ratings, it's clearly working for a lot of owners. Here's the full breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The bag contains a dry kibble formula where real chicken and turkey are the first two ingredients — that's a meaningful step up from many budget brands that lead with corn or by-product meal. The recipe includes natural fiber sources like dried beet pulp and whole grain corn, designed to help hair pass through the digestive system instead of accumulating in the stomach. It's also formulated with reduced fat content compared to standard adult formulas, targeting weight management for less-active indoor cats.

As shown in the product image, the kibble is small, round, and dark brown — roughly the size of a pea. This is intentional: smaller pieces encourage chewing, which slows down fast eaters and helps with portion control.

## Real-World Performance

The hairball control is the headline feature here, and it delivers. The natural fiber blend works by essentially "sweeping" ingested hair through the digestive tract. Multiple owners report a noticeable drop in hairball frequency within two to three weeks of switching. One verified buyer noted, *"My long-haired cat used to vomit hairballs every few days. We're now at one every couple of weeks, and they're smaller."* That's consistent with the fiber mechanism — it doesn't prevent shedding, but it prevents the hair from accumulating.

The weight management aspect is more passive. The formula is lower in fat and uses moderate protein levels to support lean muscle without excess calories. It's not a prescription weight-loss food, but for a cat that's mildly overweight and needs maintenance, it works. Owners consistently mention their cats maintain a stable weight on this food when fed according to the portion guide on the bag.

Palatability is a strong point. Cats that are picky about food generally accept this readily. The real chicken and turkey content provides natural flavor that most felines find appealing. One reviewer wrote, *"My cat is notoriously finicky and she cleaned her bowl the first time. That never happens."*

## Where It Falls Short

This is not a grain-free food. Corn and soybean meal are present, which will disqualify it for owners who've committed to grain-free diets for their cats. If that's you, this isn't the product.

The kibble size, while good for slowing down fast eaters, can be problematic for senior cats with dental issues or missing teeth. Several reviewers noted needing to soften the food with warm water for older cats. That's an extra step you should factor in if your cat is 10+ years old.

The bag design is a minor annoyance — no zipper seal. You'll need a clip or transfer the food to an airtight container to maintain freshness, especially if your cat takes a few weeks to go through a bag.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Multi-cat households with indoor cats** — one bag covers both hairball and weight concerns for all your cats
- **Owners of long-haired breeds** (Persians, Maine Coons) who struggle with frequent hairballs
- **Budget-conscious shoppers** wanting real meat as the first ingredient without paying premium "natural" brand prices
- **Cats that are picky eaters** — the chicken and turkey flavor has high acceptance rates

**Skip it if** you require grain-free food, your cat has severe dental problems, or you need a therapeutic weight-loss formula for significant obesity.

## FAQ

**Q: How quickly will I see a difference in hairballs?**
A: Most owners report improvement within 2–3 weeks. The fiber needs time to build up in the digestive tract. If you see no change after a month, your cat may need a veterinary hairball remedy.

**Q: Is this food appropriate for kittens?**
A: No. This is formulated for adult cats (1+ years). Kittens need higher protein and fat for growth. Use a kitten-specific formula until they're at least a year old.

**Q: Can I mix this with wet food?**
A: Yes, and many owners do. Just account for the calories in the wet food to avoid overfeeding. The kibble works well as a topper or mixed in.
