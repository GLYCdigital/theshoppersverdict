**Where it falls short**

The carrageenan content is the elephant in the room. This seaweed-derived thickener is what gives the gravy its luxurious texture, but some pet owners report digestive sensitivity to it. If your cat has a history of inflammatory bowel issues, you may want to test with a single can before committing to a case.

The 3-ounce can size is also worth noting. A single cat will eat this in one sitting (or two if you're portioning). For multi-cat households, you'll burn through a 24-pack in about two weeks. It's not the most economical option per ounce compared to larger 5.5-ounce cans from other brands.
