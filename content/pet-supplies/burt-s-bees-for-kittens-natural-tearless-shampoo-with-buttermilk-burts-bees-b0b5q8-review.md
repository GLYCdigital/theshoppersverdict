# Burt's Bees for Kittens Natural Tearless Shampoo with Butter: A Gentle Clean for Sensitive Skin

Bathing a kitten is a high-stakes operation. They're tiny, squirmy, and their skin is thinner than a human baby's. The last thing you want is a shampoo that stings their eyes or strips their natural oils. That's exactly where the Burt's Bees for Kittens Natural Tearless Shampoo steps in — and after looking at 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, it's clear this formula has earned its reputation.

## What You Actually Get

This is a 16-ounce pump bottle of tearless shampoo specifically formulated for kittens 6 weeks and older. The star ingredients are buttermilk and honey — both known for gentle conditioning — and the formula is completely free of sulfates, parabens, phthalates, and artificial fragrances. As shown in the product image, the pump dispenser is a practical touch for one-handed use while you're wrangling a wet, slippery kitten.

## Performance and Real-World Use

Here's the honest truth: this shampoo doesn't produce the thick, foamy lather you might expect from a dog shampoo. The formula is runnier and creates a lighter foam. That's actually a feature, not a bug — it means you're not stripping your kitten's delicate skin of its natural moisture barrier.

The tearless claim holds up. Multiple reviewers specifically mention washing their kitten's face without the frantic eye-rubbing that comes with harsher shampoos. One buyer noted, *"I was nervous about bathing my 8-week-old rescue, but this didn't irritate her eyes at all. She actually seemed to enjoy it."* Another reviewer praised how well it handled a flea bath: *"Used this after a flea treatment and it left her coat soft without that weird residue other shampoos leave behind."*

The honey and buttermilk combo does its job — coats come out soft and manageable, not stripped or brittle. And because there's no artificial fragrance, there's none of that overpowering "perfume" smell that can linger long after bath time. The scent is subtle and natural, which is preferable for cats who are sensitive to smells.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the limitations. First, the faint scent is a double-edged sword. If you were hoping your kitten would smell like a fresh bouquet after bath time, this isn't the product for you. The natural formula means minimal odor coverage.

Second, the thin consistency means you might use more product per bath than you'd expect. Several reviewers noted they needed extra pumps to work up enough lather for a full-body wash. At this price point, that's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

Finally, a few reviewers mentioned the pump can be finicky — it dispenses a bit unevenly at first. Nothing a quick prime doesn't fix, but worth knowing.

## Who Should Buy This

- **First-time kitten owners** who want a forgiving, gentle formula for their first bath experience
- **Owners of sensitive-skinned breeds** like Sphynx or Devon Rex who need sulfate-free options
- **Rescue adopters** who need a safe, non-irritating wash for a kitten that may have skin issues or fleas
- **Anyone who dislikes strong artificial fragrances** in pet products

## FAQ: What Shoppers Ask

**Q: Can I use this on an adult cat?**
A: Yes, but it's formulated for kittens. Adult cats with normal skin can use it, but you may want a stronger formula for heavy-duty cleaning or deodorizing.

**Q: Is it truly tearless?**
A: Yes. The formula is pH-balanced for kittens and designed specifically to not sting eyes during face washing. Multiple reviewers confirm this works as advertised.

**Q: Does it help with fleas?**
A: It cleans and rinses away debris, but it's not a flea treatment. It's best used after a flea treatment to soothe and condition the skin.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a safe, no-frills, tearless shampoo that respects your kitten's sensitive skin and doesn't load them down with artificial chemicals. It's a solid choice for first baths and regular gentle cleaning.

**Skip it if:** You need a strong deodorizer, expect a rich foamy lather, or want your kitten to smell heavily fragranced after bath time.

At 4.6 stars with 229 ratings, this is one of the most reliable kitten shampoos on the market. It does exactly what it claims — no more, no less — and for a product going on your pet's skin, that's exactly what you want.
