If you've ever wrestled a reluctant dog into submission just to get a heartworm pill down their throat, you already know the pain. Greenies Pill Pockets are the most recognized solution to that daily battle — but do they actually work, or are you just paying for overpriced dough?

I've tested these with multiple dogs, including a notorious pill-spitter who can detect a hidden tablet from three rooms away. Here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The Small Size package contains 30 pre-formed pockets with a slit down the middle. Each pocket is roughly the size of a large grape, designed for dogs weighing 10-25 pounds. As shown in the product image, the texture is soft and pliable — think Play-Doh with a meaty, savory scent that's distinctly appealing to dogs.

The design is simple: press the pill into the slit, pinch the edges closed, and you've got a seamless treat. No measuring, no mixing, no mess.

## Real-World Performance

The texture is where these shine. Unlike cheaper alternatives that crumble or dry out, Pill Pockets stay malleable straight from the package. You can mold them around tablets snugly, and the seal holds — no pill popping out mid-bite.

The flavor masking is legitimately impressive. The pockets have a strong chicken-liver aroma that overwhelms most medication smells. One reviewer noted, "My dog thinks these are just treats — she's never once suspected a pill inside. I've used them daily for two years of thyroid medication."

For dogs on daily meds, consistency matters. The dough holds up well even when refrigerated, and each pocket is individually sealed in the tray to prevent drying.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**Cost per treat is steep.** At roughly $0.30-0.40 per pocket, this adds up fast for daily medications. A month of daily use runs you $10-12 just for the delivery vehicle. DIY options like cream cheese or canned food cost pennies.

**Size limitations are real.** The Small formula works for standard tablets and most capsules, but larger pills require sizing up to the Large version. If your dog is between sizes, you'll waste pockets trying to stretch them.

**Some dogs get wise.** A minority of reviewers report their dogs eventually learned to chew carefully and spit out the pill. If you have a dedicated pill-detector, no treat will fool them forever.

**Digestive sensitivity.** The ingredient list includes wheat flour and glycerin. Dogs with grain sensitivities may experience loose stools if given multiple pockets daily.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy if:** You have a dog who needs daily medication and you're tired of the wrestling match. The convenience factor is worth the premium for most owners.

**Skip if:** Your dog is a grazer who eats slowly and inspects every bite, or if you're on a tight budget and have a dog who'll eat anything mixed into their regular food.

## FAQ

**Can I use these for cats?** Technically yes, the Small size is appropriate for cats, but Greenies doesn't market them for felines. Cats are pickier about texture changes, so results vary.

**Do they work for liquid medications?** No. These are designed for tablets and capsules only. Liquid meds will soak through the pocket and ruin the texture.

**How should I store them?** Keep the tray sealed in a cool, dry place. Refrigeration extends freshness but isn't required. Use within the expiration date printed on the package.
