**Bottom line:** If your dog's ears get that funky yeast smell or you're tired of wrestling with liquid ear cleaners that end up all over your kitchen floor, Vet's Best Ear Relief Finger Wipes are a practical upgrade. They're not a cure for infections, but for routine cleaning and maintenance, they're about as fuss-free as it gets.

## What you actually get

The package contains 75 pre-moistened wipes shaped to slip over your index finger like a thimble. Each wipe is saturated with a solution featuring aloe vera, chamomile, and eucalyptus — all chosen for soothing properties rather than just fragrance. The wipes themselves are textured, which gives you gentle friction to lift debris without needing to scrub.

As shown in the product image, the wipes come in a soft-sided pouch with a resealable flap. It's compact enough to toss in a travel bag, which matters if you board your dog or visit the groomer regularly.

## How it performs in real life

Here's the thing that surprised me: the finger design actually matters. With liquid cleaners, you're pouring solution in blindly, hoping it reaches the right spots, then dealing with your dog shaking solution all over your walls. With these wipes, you slip one on, lift the ear flap, and you can *feel* the surface you're cleaning. That tactile feedback means you can target the visible wax and debris without cramming a cotton swab too deep.

The wipes are well-saturated straight out of the pack. One wipe is enough for both ears on a medium-sized dog — I'd use a fresh one per ear for a larger breed or a particularly dirty situation.

Most owners report that their dogs tolerate these far better than liquid cleaners, which makes sense — no cold liquid being poured into a sensitive ear canal, no scary noise. One reviewer noted, *"My dog used to run when I brought out the ear cleaner bottle. Now she sits still for these wipes. The difference is night and day."* Another said, *"These work great on my golden retriever who swims weekly. His ears stay clean and dry between vet visits."*

## Where it falls short

The cost adds up. At roughly $12–15 for 75 wipes, you're looking at about 20 cents per use. If you're cleaning ears weekly on a single dog, one pack lasts about 18 months — that's fine. But if you have multiple pets or a dog prone to chronic ear issues, this becomes a recurring expense.

The resealable lid is also a weak point. If you don't press it down until you hear the click, the wipes will dry out within a couple of weeks. I'd recommend storing the pouch upside down to keep moisture pooled at the opening.

And to be crystal clear: these are maintenance wipes, not medication. If your dog has an active ear infection — redness, swelling, discharge, persistent scratching — skip these and see your vet. This product won't fix that, and delaying treatment makes things worse.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use these on my cat?**
A: Yes. The manufacturer states they're safe for cats over 12 weeks old. Just be more careful — cats are more sensitive to ear handling, so go slow.

**Q: How often should I clean my dog's ears with these?**
A: Once a week for most breeds. Dogs with floppy ears, like Labs or Cocker Spaniels, may benefit from twice weekly during allergy season or after swimming.

**Q: Will these help with the "yeasty" ear smell?**
A: Yes, for routine odor caused by wax and debris buildup. If the smell persists after cleaning or is accompanied by scratching, that's a vet visit.
