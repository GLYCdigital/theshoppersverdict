## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct: **this is not a magic solution for severely matted coats.** The smaller 5" shears feel lighter and less substantial — one reviewer warned, *"The little scissors bend if you push hard against a mat. Go slow or use a dematting tool first."* That's fair advice. These are maintenance tools, not rescue tools.

Also, **there's no storage case included**. The blades are exposed, and they're sharp enough to damage other items (and themselves) if tossed carelessly in a drawer. Plan to buy a blade guard or repurpose a pencil case.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these safe for trimming around a dog's eyes?**  
A: Yes, the round tips help, but you still need a steady hand and good lighting. Use the small 5" curved scissors and go slowly. Always brush and dry the area first so you can see what you're cutting.

**Q: Can I use these on cats?**  
A: Technically yes, but cats have thinner skin and move more unpredictably. The round tips help, but we'd recommend a dedicated small-animal setup and a second person to hold the cat.

**Q: Do the blades rust?**  
A: They're stainless steel, but no steel is rust-proof. Wipe them dry after use and apply a light coat of oil (like mineral oil) periodically. Store them in a dry place away from humidity.
