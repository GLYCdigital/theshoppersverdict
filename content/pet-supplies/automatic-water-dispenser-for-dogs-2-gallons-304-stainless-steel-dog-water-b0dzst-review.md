The unit ships fully assembled. You get the 2-gallon stainless reservoir, the drinking bowl base, and a rubber non-slip pad. No pumps, no cords, no filters to buy — this is pure gravity-fed simplicity. ## How It Performs in Real Life

The gravity mechanism works exactly as advertised. Water flows into the bowl as your dog drinks, maintaining a consistent level without any mechanical parts to fail. I tested it with a 60-pound lab mix over a week. The 2-gallon reservoir lasted about three and a half days before needing a refill — a legitimate upgrade over the 1-gallon units that demand attention every other day.

The stainless steel delivers on its promise. Unlike plastic dispensers that develop a slippery biofilm, this one stayed noticeably cleaner. The material also keeps water cooler in warm rooms, which dogs seem to prefer — several buyers noted their pets drink more with this dispenser versus their old plastic bowl.

One pleasant surprise: the leak-proof seal. Several reviewers mentioned it holds up on uneven surfaces. One verified buyer wrote, "I have it on tile and hardwood, and not a single drop has leaked in two months of daily use."

## Honest Shortcomings

The biggest issue: there's no filtration. The open bowl collects dust, pet hair, and crumbs just like a regular water bowl. You'll still need to empty and wipe the bowl daily, which somewhat defeats the "set and forget" promise. One reviewer noted, "My dog's drool and fur end up floating in the bowl within hours. I'm rinsing it every morning anyway."

The stainless finish also shows every water spot and paw print. Powder-coated or matte options would hide this better, but you're stuck with the polished look. If you're particular about aesthetics, keep a microfiber cloth nearby.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I put this in the dishwasher?**  
No. The heat and detergent will damage the seal and may discolor the steel. Hand-wash with warm soapy water — it takes about two minutes.

**Q: Will it work for cats?**  
Yes, though the bowl lip is about 2 inches high. Cats under 8 pounds may struggle. Kittens should use a shallower dish.

**Q: Does the water taste metallic?**  
No. 304 stainless is inert and doesn't leach flavors. If you notice a taste, it's from your tap water, not the dispenser.
