## What You Actually Get

These are crunchy, bite-sized biscuits made with real deboned chicken or beef as the first ingredient — no poultry by-product meal, no corn, wheat, or soy. The bag is resealable, which matters more than you'd think for keeping that crunch intact. You get a solid number of treats per bag, though portion size depends on your dog's weight; the feeding guide on the back is straightforward and actually useful.

The texture is dense and satisfyingly crunchy — this isn't a soft-baked treat. That crunch does double duty: it gives your dog something to work for and helps scrape plaque off teeth while they chew. The biscuits are small, roughly the size of a quarter, which is perfect for training sessions or small-to-medium breeds.

## Real-World Performance

I've seen these hold up well in everyday use. The crunch factor is legit — dogs have to actually chew, which slows down gulpers and gives the dental benefits time to work. The ingredient list is refreshingly clean: deboned chicken, oatmeal, barley, and flaxseed are the headliners. No artificial preservatives, no vague "animal digest" mystery meat.

One thing that stands out: these don't crumble into dust in your pocket or treat pouch. That might sound minor, but if you've ever reached into a bag of treats only to pull out a handful of crumbs, you know exactly why this matters.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The biscuits are small — if you have a Labrador or German Shepherd, these are two-bite treats at best. You'll go through a bag faster than you'd like with a large breed.

The dense crunch is also a double-edged sword. Senior dogs with dental issues or small breeds with delicate jaws might struggle with the hardness. If your dog has dental problems, you'll want to soften these or look at a softer alternative. And while the ingredient list is clean, the price per ounce runs higher than generic supermarket brands — you're paying for the quality, and that's a tradeoff some budgets won't love.

## FAQ

**Are these safe for puppies?**
Yes, but supervise chewing — the crunch is dense. For puppies under six months, break them into smaller pieces or soften with a little water.

**Do these actually clean teeth?**
They help mechanically — the crunch scrapes plaque as your dog chews. They won't replace brushing, but they're a solid complement for daily dental care.

**How many can I give my dog per day?**
The bag recommends portioning based on weight — roughly 2-4 treats for small dogs, up to 8-10 for larger breeds. Treats should stay under 10% of daily calories.
