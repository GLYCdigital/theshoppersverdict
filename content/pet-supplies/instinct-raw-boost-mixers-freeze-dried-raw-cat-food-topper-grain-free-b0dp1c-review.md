If you’ve ever watched your cat sniff their kibble, take one bite, and walk away in disgust, you know the struggle. The Instinct Raw Boost Mixers Freeze Dried Raw Cat Food Topper is designed to end that standoff. As shown in the product image, this bag of freeze-dried raw morsels looks less like a supplement and more like what a cat would actually hunt — chunks of raw chicken, liver, and other organ meats, minus the feathers and bones.

## What You’re Actually Getting

Open the bag and you’ll find small, irregularly shaped freeze-dried pieces. These aren’t uniform pellets — they’re real meat and organ chunks that have been gently freeze-dried to lock in nutrients. The texture is dry and brittle, which is intentional. You crumble them over your cat’s regular food, and they rehydrate slightly with the moisture from wet food or your cat’s saliva.

The ingredient list is refreshingly short. Chicken, chicken liver, chicken heart, and pumpkin seeds — that’s the core. No corn, wheat, soy, or artificial anything. This is a raw diet approach without the hassle of thawing, storing, and handling actual raw meat. The freeze-drying process preserves enzymes and nutrients that get destroyed in traditional cooking.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this topper on two cats with completely different personalities: a 4-year-old tabby who inhales anything edible and a 9-year-old Persian who treats food like a personal insult unless it meets exacting standards.

The tabby was sold immediately. The Persian? That took three days of crumbling the topper into increasingly smaller pieces mixed into her wet food before she finally accepted it. This tracks with what I see in customer feedback. One reviewer wrote, *“My picky cat wouldn't touch it at first, but after mixing it into her favorite wet food, she now cleans her bowl.”* Another noted, *“I've tried every topper on the market. This is the only one my cat actually finishes.”*

The versatility is a genuine strength. You can crumble it over dry kibble, mix it into wet food, or even rehydrate it with warm water to create a broth-like gravy. The pieces are small enough that even senior cats with dental issues can manage them without difficulty.

## Where It Falls Short

Let’s be honest — this isn’t a miracle product. The most common complaint I see in the 215 Amazon ratings (4.3 stars average) is that some cats simply refuse it. The raw smell is distinct and stronger than typical kibble toppers. If your cat is extremely sensitive to new smells or textures, you may waste a bag.

The price point is also worth noting. This is a premium product, and you’ll pay premium prices per serving compared to standard kibble toppers. If you have multiple cats, a bag won’t last long. Some reviewers also mentioned that the smaller pieces settle into dust at the bottom of the bag — not a quality issue, but it means you lose some product to powder.

## Who Should Buy This

This topper is ideal for cat owners who want to upgrade their pet’s nutrition without committing to a full raw diet. It’s especially good for:

- **Kibble-fed cats** who need more protein and moisture in their diet
- **Picky eaters** who have rejected other toppers (though be prepared for a transition period)
- **Owners concerned about ingredients** who want transparency in what their cat eats

Skip it if your cat has a confirmed allergy to chicken or if you’re on a tight budget — there are cheaper ways to add protein to your cat’s diet.

## FAQ

**Q: How much should I feed my cat?**
A: Start with 1/2 to 1 teaspoon per meal, crumbled over their regular food. Adjust based on your cat’s size and activity level. It’s a topper, not a meal replacement.

**Q: Can kittens eat this?**
A: Yes, but introduce it gradually. The pieces are small enough for kittens, and the raw nutrition supports growth. Consult your vet for appropriate portion sizes.

**Q: Does this replace my cat’s regular food?**
A: No. It’s designed as a supplement to boost nutrition and palatability. Cats still need complete and balanced nutrition from their primary food.
