# Frisco 72-Inch Cat Tree Review: Vertical Real Estate Your Cat Will Actually Use

Cats are vertical creatures. Give them a window perch and a scratching surface, and they'll leave your curtains and sofa alone. The **Frisco 72-Inch Cat Tree** is Amazon's best-selling large cat tower, and with 32,000+ reviews at 4.4 stars, it's clearly doing something right.

We analyzed the reviews to see if this $80 behemoth earns its perch at the top of the cat furniture category.

## Assembly: The 45-Minute Workout

Let's address the first thing every reviewer mentions: assembly. The box is heavy (50+ lbs), the instructions are diagram-based (no text), and you'll want a second pair of hands for a few steps. Expect 45-60 minutes from unboxing to finished tower.

The payoff is immediate. Multiple reviewers report their cats jumping on the tree *during assembly* — a strong endorsement from the target audience. Once up, the 72-inch height dominates a room, giving cats a commanding view that satisfies their instinct to survey territory from above.

## Build Quality at This Price

For $80, the materials won't compete with $300+ designer cat furniture, but they don't feel cheap either. The support poles are wrapped in natural sisal rope that holds up to aggressive scratching, and the platforms are covered in soft faux fur that cats find immediately appealing.

The base platform is the critical stability component — double-check it's perfectly level before tightening, and the entire structure becomes solid. Multiple reviewers with large cats (Maine Coons, 15+ lb domestic shorthairs) confirm the tree handles full-speed launching and landing without wobbling.

The anti-tip strap is included for a reason: use it. Even if the tree feels stable, a determined cat at full zoom can generate surprising leverage at the top.

## Multi-Cat Dynamics

This is where the Frisco shines. Three perches, two condos, and multiple intermediate platforms mean multiple cats can stake out territory without conflict. The varied heights let more timid cats stay low while confident cats claim the penthouse.

The scratching surfaces are strategically placed — cats naturally scratch on the poles while climbing, redirecting behavior from your furniture. Reviewers with multi-cat homes consistently report reduced furniture damage after introducing this tree.

## The Verdict

The Frisco 72-Inch Cat Tree delivers outsized value. It's not perfect — assembly is a project, the faux fur sheds at first, and the materials won't last a decade — but at $80 for a 6-foot tower, it makes vertical enrichment accessible for every cat owner. Your cats will use it daily, and your sofa will thank you.

**Verdict: 9/10** — The best large cat tree under $100, bar none.
