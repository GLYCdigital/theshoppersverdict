If your dog's scratching keeps you up at night or their digestion is a roll of the dice every meal, you've probably cycled through a dozen "sensitive" formulas already. Natural Balance Sensitiv takes a different approach than most: instead of throwing ten different proteins and a probiotic cocktail at the problem, it strips the recipe down to the essentials. One animal protein, one grain, and nothing else to trip over.

## What's actually in the bag

The Sensitiv line features salmon as the sole animal protein source (listed first on the ingredient panel), paired with oatmeal as the only grain. No chicken, no beef, no corn, wheat, or soy. That's the entire protein-and-carb backbone of the recipe — which is exactly what makes it work for dogs with unidentified food sensitivities.

The bag itself is standard for the category: a 24-pound resealable sack with clear feeding guidelines printed on the side. You get the kibble, the nutritional analysis, and nothing else. No sample packs, no measuring cup. Slightly annoying that a brand at this price point doesn't throw in a scoop, but it's not a dealbreaker.

## Real-world performance

The most telling feedback comes from owners whose dogs had chronic issues before switching. One buyer with a bulldog mix wrote: *"Three weeks in, the hot spots are gone and she stopped chewing her paws. I didn't change anything else — just the food."* That pattern repeats across the reviews: skin improvements typically show up in weeks two through four, while stool firmness often normalizes faster, sometimes within the first week.

The kibble texture deserves mention. It's a dry, crunchy nugget with a light fish aroma — not overwhelmingly fishy, which is worth noting if you've tried salmon-based foods that make your whole kitchen smell like a cannery. The pieces run on the small side, roughly dime-sized. That's fine for small and medium breeds, but if you've got a 70-pound lab that inhales food, you may notice more gulping than chewing. Several reviewers with large breeds flagged this specifically.

## Where it falls short

The small kibble size is the most consistent complaint. It's not a safety issue, but it's a real consideration for large-breed owners — especially if your dog is prone to bloat or eats too fast.

The lack of a grain-free variant also matters for a subset of shoppers. Natural Balance makes grain-free formulas under their L.I.D. line, but this specific Sensitiv recipe is grain-inclusive by design. If your vet has mandated zero grains, this isn't the bag for you.

And then there's the price. At roughly $2.10 per pound, it sits above comparable sensitive-stomach formulas from Purina Pro Plan and Hill's. The quality justifies much of the premium, but budget-conscious buyers should know they're paying for the limited-ingredient approach.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** Your dog has mild to moderate skin irritation or digestive upset and you haven't identified the specific trigger yet. The single-protein formula is an excellent elimination baseline. Also ideal for dogs that do well on fish-based diets and owners who prefer a simpler ingredient list without sacrificing complete nutrition.

**Skip it if:** Your dog needs a grain-free diet (vet-ordered), you have a giant breed that needs larger kibble for chewing, or you're on a tight budget and can't sustain $50+ per bag.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this food suitable for puppies?**
A: The formula meets AAFCO standards for all life stages, including growth. However, the small kibble size can be a choking hazard for very small puppies — supervise early feedings and consider moistening the kibble for the first few weeks.

**Q: My dog is allergic to chicken. Is salmon in this recipe safe?**
A: Yes, salmon is the only animal protein source. The manufacturing facility follows strict protocols to avoid cross-contamination, though no facility can guarantee zero trace exposure. If your dog has anaphylactic-level allergies, consult your vet first.

**Q: How quickly should I transition my dog to this food?**
A: Over 7-10 days. Start with 25% Sensitiv mixed with 75% current food, then gradually increase the ratio. Rushing the transition can cause digestive upset that mimics the very symptoms you're trying to fix — and then you won't know if the food is working.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Natural Balance Sensitiv delivers exactly what it promises: a clean, limited-ingredient diet that resolves sensitivity symptoms for the majority of dogs. The small kibble and premium price are legitimate tradeoffs, but for owners of food-sensitive dogs who've exhausted cheaper options, this is a formula that consistently works.

**Buy it if** your dog needs a simple, salmon-based diet with proven results for skin and stomach issues. **Skip it if** you need grain-free, giant-breed kibble, or a lower price point. Everyone else — this is the bag that finally stops the scratching.
