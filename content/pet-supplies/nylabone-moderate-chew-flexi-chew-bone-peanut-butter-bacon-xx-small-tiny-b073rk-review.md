If you’ve got a small dog with a chewing habit that’s wrecking your baseboards, the Nylabone Moderate Chew Flexi Chew Bone is worth a hard look. This isn’t the rock-hard nylon chew your vet warned you about — it’s a flexible, bone-shaped chew that bends under pressure, which is exactly what you want for a pup who chews hard enough to hurt themselves. The peanut butter and bacon flavor is the bait, but the real value is in the design.

**What you actually get**: A single bone-shaped chew, roughly 5 inches long, with textured nubs running along the surface. The Flexi Chew line uses a softer nylon blend that gives slightly when squeezed — think firm rubber, not brick. It’s designed for dogs under 25 pounds, and the XX-Small/Tiny size in this listing is specifically for dogs under 5 pounds. The flavor is baked in, not coated, so it doesn’t leave greasy residue on your carpet.

**Build and performance**: The flexible material is the standout feature. Unlike standard Nylabones that can chip or cause dental fractures in enthusiastic chewers, this one bends and flexes. It’s not indestructible — a determined chewer will eventually shred it into pieces, and you should replace it once it wears down to a nub. But for moderate chewers, it holds up for weeks. The nubs do a decent job scraping plaque off molars, though they’re not a substitute for brushing.

**What buyers consistently praise**: The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews reflects genuine satisfaction. One verified buyer wrote: *“My 8-month-old Yorkie goes crazy for this. It’s the only chew that keeps her busy for more than 10 minutes.”* Another noted: *“I love that it flexes — I was worried about her teeth, but this feels safe.”* Multiple reviewers mention the scent staying strong for days, which is key for picky dogs.

**Where it falls short**: Two honest downsides. First, the “moderate” label is misleading — this is still a nylon chew, and very aggressive chewers will either shred it or lose interest. Second, the flavor fades. After about a week of daily chewing, the peanut butter and bacon scent weakens, and some dogs lose interest entirely. A few reviewers specifically mentioned their dogs ignoring it after the novelty wore off. Also, the sizing is easy to get wrong — if your dog is over 5 pounds, this specific size is too small.

**Who should buy this**: 
- Small dog owners (under 15 pounds) with moderate chewers who need a safe, non-destructive outlet
- People tired of plush toys that last 20 minutes or rawhide that leaves stains on furniture
- Pet parents concerned about dental health who want a chew that cleans teeth without vet bills

**Skip it if**: 
- You have a power chewer — they’ll destroy this in under an hour
- Your dog is over 25 pounds — get the larger size
- Your dog is toy-destructive and needs something they can’t tear apart

**FAQ**:

*Is this safe for puppies?*  
Yes, for puppies over 12 weeks old. The flexible material is gentler on baby teeth than hard nylon. Just supervise first use and swap it out once it gets small enough to swallow.

*How long does the flavor last?*  
Expect 5-7 days of strong scent with daily use. After that, the flavor fades but the chew remains functional. Some buyers reapply a tiny dab of peanut butter to refresh interest.

*Can this replace dental chews?*  
It helps with plaque, but it’s not a substitute for professional cleaning or enzymatic dental chews. Think of it as a supplement, not a solution.

**The Verdict**:  
**Buy it if** you have a small, moderate-chewing dog who needs a safe, long-lasting chew that won’t wreck your furniture or their teeth. **Skip it if** you have a power chewer or a dog who loses interest in toys quickly — you’ll waste your money.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, well-designed chew with genuine safety benefits, held back only by its misleading “moderate” label and diminishing scent. For the right dog, it’s a winner.
