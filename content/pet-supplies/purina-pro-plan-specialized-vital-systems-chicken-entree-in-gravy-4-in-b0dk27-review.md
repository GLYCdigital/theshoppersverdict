Also, the "specialized vital systems" positioning means you're paying a premium for targeted nutrition. If your cat is already healthy, eating well, and has a normal coat, you may be overpaying for benefits you don't strictly need. One reviewer admitted, "It's great food, but I switched back to a cheaper brand once my cat's fur and digestion improved."
