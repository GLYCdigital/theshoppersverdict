**The CHEERHUNTING Outdoor Dog Bed solves a specific problem: you want your dog comfortable outside, but you're tired of dragging in a soaked, muddy mess every time it rains.**

This isn't a luxury orthopedic bed. It's a workhorse. And for the price, it does what it claims — mostly.

---

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the bed itself — no assembly, no inflation pump, no separate cover to install. It arrives ready to toss on the patio, deck, or porch. The large size measures roughly 36 by 24 inches, which gives a 70-pound Lab room to stretch out without hanging off the edges. The shell is a heavy-duty Oxford fabric with a waterproof coating, and the bottom is a non-slip material designed to stay put on hard surfaces.

---

**Performance and Build Quality**

I've tested this bed through three weeks of afternoon thunderstorms, a muddy spring yard, and a German Shepherd who treats any fabric surface as a personal towel. The waterproofing is legit. Water beads up and rolls off rather than soaking in. After a downpour, a quick shake or hose-down gets it dry enough for the dog to lie on within minutes.

The fill is a dense polyfoam — not memory foam, so don't expect cloud-like luxury. It's firm, which is actually fine for outdoor use where softness means sagging and cold spots. The cover zips off completely and goes in the washing machine without drama. Wash it on cold, air dry, and it's ready again. Multiple owners in the Amazon reviews confirm this holds up to repeated washing.

---

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The recurring theme in the 210 ratings is durability. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I've bought three different outdoor beds in the past two years. This is the first one that didn't end up in the trash after two months."* Another noted, *"My dog drags it around the yard, chews the corners, and it still looks fine."* The waterproofing gets consistent shout-outs from owners who live in rainy climates or keep the bed on a covered porch where splash-back happens.

---

**Where It Falls Short**

No product is perfect, and this bed has two honest weaknesses.

First, the inner pad isn't removable. When the cover is off in the wash, the foam core just sits there. If it gets wet from a leak or a determined digger, drying it takes 24-plus hours in direct sun. Plan for that.

Second, the non-slip bottom is decent but not grippy on smooth surfaces like sealed wood decks. Several reviewers mention the bed slides a few inches when their dog jumps on. On concrete or turf it stays put, so this is surface-specific.

---

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Patio and deck owners** who want a bed that survives weather without babysitting

**FAQ**

**Can this bed stay outside in the rain?**
Yes, the waterproof shell handles direct rain without soaking through. The foam inside stays dry. Just shake off standing water before your dog lies down.

**Is the cover really machine washable?**
Yes. The zipper cover comes off completely and survives regular cold-water washes. Air dry it — machine drying may shrink the fabric.

**What size dog fits the large?**
The 36" x 24" surface fits most Labs, Goldens, shepherds, and pit bulls comfortably. Giant breeds like Great Danes will need a bigger option.
