If you've ever deep-cleaned a plastic litter box and noticed that faint ammonia smell never quite goes away, you already know the problem. Plastic is porous — it traps odor and bacteria no matter how hard you scrub. The Booche High-Sided Stainless Steel Cat Litter Box with Lid attacks that issue head-on with a material change that makes immediate sense.

**What you actually get**

The box arrives with three pieces: the main stainless steel pan, a matching snap-on lid, and a metal scoop that clips to the side. The steel is brushed, not mirror-polished, which is smart — it hides the fine scratches that come from daily scooping. As shown in the product image, the lid isn't a full enclosure; it wraps around the top edge to block litter from flying out while keeping the entry open on one side. That design compromise matters: it cuts scatter dramatically without making your cat feel trapped in a covered box.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

This is not a flimsy sheet-metal pan. The steel gauge feels substantial, and the rolled rim adds rigidity — no flexing when you lift a half-full box. The high sides run about 8 inches, which handles the two biggest litter box complaints: urine overspray and litter kick-out. For cats that back up to the wall and let loose, this contains it.

Stainless steel's real advantage shows up in maintenance. Odors don't soak into the surface, so a rinse and wipe genuinely resets the box. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"I didn't realize how much smell my old plastic box was holding until I switched. This one rinses clean and doesn't hold onto anything."* That tracks with the 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews — the material is the story here.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the weight. Stainless steel is heavy, and once you add a few inches of litter, this becomes a two-handed carry. If you have back issues or limited grip strength, that's a real consideration. The lack of molded handles makes it slightly awkward to haul to the sink for weekly deep cleans.

The side-mounted scoop clip is a minor annoyance. It's a thoughtful add, but cats exiting the box sometimes bump it loose. You'll reattach it more often than you'd like. And while the open-side lid design is a winner for scatter control, it doesn't do much for odor containment — this box relies on regular scooping, not carbon filters, to manage smell.

**Who should buy this**

- **Multi-cat households** dealing with ammonia buildup in plastic boxes
- **Owners of large or tall cats** (Maine Coons, ragdolls) who need a bigger footprint than standard boxes offer
- **Anyone tired of replacing cracked plastic boxes** every 18 months — this will outlast several of those
- **Cleanliness-focused owners** willing to invest upfront for easier daily maintenance

**Skip it if** you need a lightweight box for a senior cat room, or if you're looking for an enclosed hood that fully traps odor. This is an open-top design with a scatter guard, not an odor-sealing cabinet.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Is the lid removable for cleaning?*
Yes. It snaps off with light pressure on the side tabs, and the steel pan rinses clean without the lid attached. No tools needed.

*Will a 15-pound cat fit comfortably?*
The extra-large footprint gives even big breeds room to turn around. Multiple reviewers with Maine Coons confirm their cats use it without crouching awkwardly.

*Does stainless steel really control odor, or is that marketing?*
It's physics, not marketing. Steel is non-porous, so bacteria don't colonize the surface the way they do in scratched plastic. The box itself won't smell; the litter still needs regular scooping.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5 — Buy it if you're tired of plastic boxes that hold onto odors; skip it if you need something light enough to carry one-handed.**

The Booche high-sided stainless steel box solves a genuine problem — permanent odor absorption in plastic — with a durable, easy-to-sanitize design. The weight and the flimsy scoop clip are minor tradeoffs for a box that will likely outlast your current one by years. At this price point, it's a solid investment in your cat's hygiene and your nose's peace of mind.
