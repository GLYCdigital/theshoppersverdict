**What you actually get**

The box contains 10 replacement pads designed specifically for the Breeze litter tray system. Each pad measures roughly 13.5 x 13 inches — close to the OEM dimensions but, as several reviewers note, "just a touch narrower." They're constructed with a three-layer design: a plastic backing, an absorbent core with what PETSWORLD claims is "ammonia neutralizer," and a quilted top layer that wicks moisture away from the surface.

I appreciate that they ship flat, not folded, which means no annoying creases that compromise the absorbent layer. Out of the box, they feel noticeably denser than the stock pads — roughly 30% thicker by my hand test.

**Real-world performance: two cats, one week**

I ran these through a full week with two adult cats using a Breeze XL system. The critical test: how long until ammonia smell breaks through.

Day one: flawless. Urine wicked down immediately, top surface stayed dry to the touch. Day two: still odor-free, pad hasn't sagged or developed pooling. Day three was where it got interesting — the pad was clearly saturated (both cats are heavy drinkers), but the plastic backing held. No wet spots on the tray itself. I changed it on day four, which is a full day longer than I typically get from the Breeze-brand pads.

The absorbent core uses a polymer blend that gels on contact with liquid. This matters because it prevents the "sloshing" problem you get with cheaper cellulose pads. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: "I forgot I had a litter box in the laundry room until the pad needed changing. That's the highest compliment."

**Where it falls short**

The narrower width is the most common complaint. It's not a dealbreaker — you just need to center it — but if you have a cat that likes to pee near the edge of the tray, you may get occasional seepage onto the rim. That's annoying to clean.

The crinkling noise is real. The plastic backing is stiffer than the OEM pad's fabric bottom. Most cats adapt within a day or two, but if you have a particularly anxious cat, expect a few days of hesitation before they use the box comfortably.

No corner adhesives means the pad can shift if your cat is an aggressive digger. You can solve this with a small piece of double-sided tape, but honestly, the OEM pads don't have this issue.

**Q: How often should I change these with two cats?**

**Q: Can I use these with clumping litter on top?**
A: No. These are designed for the Breeze system's non-clumping pellets. Clumping litter will clog the top layer and prevent urine from reaching the pad.
