You know the drill: cat carrier day means a hiding cat, scratched forearms, and a yowling soundtrack for the whole car ride. The Prutapet Large Cat Carrier aims to change that script. It's the rare soft-sided carrier that actually respects the size of a big cat—and it's earned a 4.8-star average across 240+ Amazon ratings. But is it built for the long haul or just a weekend trip?

## What You Actually Get

The Prutapet arrives fully assembled. Out of the box you're looking at the 24x16.5x16.5-inch crate, a removable fleece liner, and a shoulder strap. No tools, no cryptic instructions, no wrestling with screws—just unfold and it's ready. As shown in the product image, the design is clean: mesh windows on three sides, a top-loading zipper, and a front entry door secured with both zipper and buckle.

The frame is collapsible for storage, which is nice if you don't want a permanent cat cave dominating your hallway. One thing worth noting: the shoulder strap clips on with simple buckles, but there's no luggage pass-through to slide over a rolling suitcase handle. If you're flying, you'll want to double-check airline dimensions—this fits under most seats but it's not a hard-sided FAA-approved carrier.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The 600D Oxford fabric feels sturdier than most carriers at this price point. The mesh is fine-gauge—cats can't hook a claw through it easily—and the zippers are heavy-duty enough to survive repeated use. The base has a reinforced bottom board that doesn't sag under a 15-pound cat's weight. That matters.

The top-loading zipper is genuinely the standout feature. If you've ever tried to coax a stressed cat out of a front-opening carrier at the vet, you know the value of lifting them out from above. Multiple reviewers mention this specifically. One five-star review says: "My 18 lb Maine Coon fits comfortably and the top opening made the vet visit so much easier. He actually walked in on his own the second time."

The fleece liner is removable and machine washable—a real plus when motion sickness hits. It also means you can swap in a familiar-smelling blanket for anxious cats.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the weaknesses. Soft-sided carriers are not escape-proof. A determined cat with claws can eventually work at the zipper or mesh. If you have a known Houdini, this is a risk—hard-sided is safer for those escape artists.

The carrier also lacks a built-in seatbelt loop. You'll need to thread the seatbelt through the handles or bungee it down, which isn't ideal for safety. And while the shoulder strap helps, carrying a 20-pound cat any real distance gets uncomfortable fast—the strap padding is thin.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Maine Coon and large breed owners**: This is one of the few soft-sided carriers that actually fits big cats without squishing them.
- **Frequent vet visitors**: The top-loading design is a stress saver for both you and your cat.
- **Road trippers**: It folds flat for storage and the washable liner handles accidents gracefully.

Skip it if you need a hard-sided carrier for airline travel or have a cat that's a confirmed escape artist.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this carrier truly large enough for a 20-pound cat?**  
A: Yes. The interior is a genuine 24x16.5x16.5 inches. One reviewer with a 22-pound ragdoll confirms their cat can stand up and turn around. That's rare for soft-sided carriers.

**Q: Can this be used for airline cabin travel?**  
A: It fits most airline under-seat dimensions, but it's not hard-sided. Some airlines require hard-sided carriers for cabin pets. Check with your carrier before booking.

**Q: How do you clean the liner?**  
A: Unzip the base, pull out the fleece liner, and machine wash on cold. Let it air dry—the liner can shrink slightly if you toss it in the dryer.
