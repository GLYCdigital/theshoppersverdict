Let me be straight with you: hiding a litter box is one of the hardest problems in cat ownership. You want it accessible for your cat, concealed from guests, and not smelling like a public restroom. The HOOBRO Litter Box Enclosure attempts to solve all three. Here's my honest breakdown after digging through specs, customer feedback, and the realities of daily cat life.

**What You Actually Get**

This isn't a flimsy fabric tent. The HOOBRO is a solid piece of furniture-grade particle board with a laminated wood grain finish. The dimensions give you a footprint roughly 23.6" L x 15.7" W x 19.7" H — roomy enough for most standard and jumbo litter boxes (up to about 22 inches long). The standout feature is the **removable divider** inside. Install it and you've got two separate compartments — handy if you have two cats who refuse to share. Remove it and you get one open space for a single large cat or a bigger box.

The front opening is a cutout with a flap door, sized right for average adult cats. Set the enclosure on top of a mat, and you've got a functional side table or plant stand. The top is rated to hold up to 110 lbs, so you can actually put things on it — a lamp, books, a small plant. That's a genuinely useful feature most competitors don't offer.

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

The panels are 0.45-inch thick particle board with a laminated finish. It's not solid wood — don't expect heirloom quality — but it's substantially sturdier than the fabric-over-frame options flooding Amazon. The finish wipes clean easily, which matters when litter dust settles on surfaces. The hinge on the top lid operates smoothly, and the magnetic catch keeps it closed when your cat isn't inside.

Here's where the reality check comes in. Assembly is the biggest complaint across customer reviews — and it's justified. You're looking at 20+ screws, cam locks, and dowels. The instructions are mostly diagrams, and some buyers report alignment issues that require fiddling. Budget 30-45 minutes and have a Phillips screwdriver ready. If you're not handy, that's a real consideration.

The other tradeoff: **odor control is not built-in**. The enclosure limits airflow, which actually helps contain smells, but without a carbon filter or deodorizer, odors will still escape when your cat finishes business. Several reviewers mention adding their own odor absorbers inside. Plan for that.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.3-star rating across 215 reviews holds up. The most frequently mentioned positives: the furniture look (it genuinely passes as a cabinet, not a litter box), the sturdiness, and the size. One reviewer noted: *"I was skeptical about the particle board, but it feels solid and my 15-pound Maine Coon fits comfortably with the divider removed."* Another said: *"Finally, a litter box cover that doesn't look like an eyesore in my living room."* The removable top makes scooping and box changes much easier than front-access-only hideaways.

**Where It Falls Short**

Beyond assembly, there are two honest drawbacks. First, if your litter box is taller than 18 inches, you'll struggle to fit it — measure your existing box before ordering. Second, the interior space, even with the divider removed, is roughly 22" x 14" — fine for most cats, but if you have a large breed or a senior cat who needs extra room to maneuver, this might feel cramped. Also, the particle board doesn't love moisture — if your cat is a vigorous digger, litter will scatter and accumulate at the edges. A mat underneath is essential.

**Who Should Buy This**

This works best for: apartment dwellers with one or two cats who want a furniture piece that hides the litter box in a living space; owners of small-to-medium cats; anyone who values a solid, flat top surface for decor. Skip it if you have a large breed like a Maine Coon that needs a 25+ inch box, if you're not comfortable with moderate assembly, or if you need a fully sealed odor control system.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can two cats use this at the same time?**
A: With the divider installed, it's tight — roughly 11" x 14" per side. It works better for sequential use. Remove the divider for a single large cat or a cat who likes to sprawl.

**Q: Does it keep litter and odors contained?**
A: It contains litter scatter reasonably well thanks to the enclosed design, but odor control is passive. Add a charcoal filter or baking soda to manage smells effectively.

**Q: Is this easy to clean?**
A: The top opens fully for scooping and box changes. Wipe the interior with a damp cloth — the laminated surface resists staining.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely furniture-grade litter box hideaway, have a standard-size cat, and don't mind an afternoon of assembly. **Skip it if** you have a large breed, need active odor filtration, or prefer tool-free setup.

The HOOBRO earns a solid **4.2/5** — a smart, functional solution that hides the mess without hiding the convenience. Just measure first, plan for assembly time, and add your own odor management.
