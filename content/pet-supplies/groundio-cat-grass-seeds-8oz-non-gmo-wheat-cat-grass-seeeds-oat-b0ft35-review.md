The germination rate is the headline here. Across buyer feedback, sprouts consistently appear in 3-5 days, and the grass reaches the ideal 4-6 inch grazing height within 10 days. **FAQ**

*How long do the seeds stay viable after opening?*
Stored dry and sealed, expect 6-12 months. Refrigeration extends that, but let seeds warm to room temperature before soaking.

*Can I grow these outdoors or in a hydroponic setup?*
Yes, but they’re optimized for shallow trays with potting mix. Hydroponic sprouting works — just rinse twice daily to prevent rot.

*Is this safe if my cat eats a lot?*
Wheat grass is safe for cats and provides fiber that aids hairball passage. It’s not a meal replacement — monitor if your cat overindulges and vomits, which can happen with any grass.
