**The 4.7-Star Treat That Picky Dogs Actually Eat — But Read This First**

If your dog turns their nose up at every "premium" treat you've tried, the INABA Churu Bites might be the peace treaty you've been looking for. This is the dry, bite-sized version of the brand's famous Churu lickable purees — and it solves the biggest complaint about the original: messiness. No gooey tubes, no licking mats to wash. Just soft, chewy morsels you can toss in your pocket for walks.

**What You Actually Get**

The package is a 1.76-ounce pouch of small, cube-shaped treats. They're about the size of a lima bean — small enough for a Chihuahua, but substantial enough that a Lab won't feel cheated. The texture is what sets these apart: genuinely soft and moist, not the hard, crunchy kibble most "soft" treats turn into by the time they reach your door. As shown in the product image, each piece has a visible, meaty appearance — no suspicious gray coating or artificial sheen.

Ingredients are refreshingly straightforward: chicken is first, followed by chicken fat, tapioca starch, and vitamin E. That's it. No corn, wheat, soy, or artificial anything. For dogs with sensitive stomachs, this short list is a plus.

**Real-World Performance**

I tested these with a 9-year-old beagle mix who has missing molars and a 2-year-old golden retriever who inhales everything. The beagle chewed them without hesitation — the softness is legit. The golden, who usually swallows treats whole, actually paused to chew. That's the texture doing its job.

The vitamin E addition is a subtle but smart move — it supports skin and coat health, which matters for older dogs or breeds prone to dryness. You're not getting a supplement-level dose, but it's a nice touch for a treat.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Across the 235 ratings, three themes dominate. First, palatability: "My dog is the pickiest eater on the planet and she devours these." Second, freshness: "Every piece in the bag was soft, not a single hard one." Third, training utility: "Perfect size for reward-based training without overfeeding."

**Where It Falls Short**

Two honest complaints. The pouch is not resealable — once opened, the treats dry out within 3-4 days if not transferred to an airtight container. That's a real oversight for a product priced per ounce. Second, the 1.76-ounce size is small. For a large breed, that's maybe two training sessions. You'll be reordering frequently, which adds up.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Senior dogs and small breeds** with dental sensitivity — the softness is a genuine win.  
- **Owners of picky eaters** who've tried everything else.  
- **Trainers** who need a high-value, low-calorie reward (each piece is roughly 3 calories).  

**Skip it if** you have a large breed and want a cost-effective bulk option, or if you need something that survives in a treat pouch for weeks.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can these be broken into smaller pieces for training?**  
A: Yes, they crumble cleanly with light finger pressure. No knife needed.

**Q: Are they suitable for puppies?**  
A: Generally yes, but supervise — the pieces are soft enough to gulp. For pups under 4 months, break them in half.

**Q: How long does an open pouch stay fresh?**  
A: About 3-4 days in the original bag. Transfer to a sealed container immediately and they'll last 2-3 weeks.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** your dog is picky, senior, or small — or if you want a clean, soft treat with real ingredients. **Skip it if** you need resealable packaging or bulk value.
