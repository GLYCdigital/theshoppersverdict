## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the assembly. This is a 2-to-3-hour project, and the instructions are mostly exploded diagrams with minimal text. The bigger issue: there's no floor panel. That's not a dealbreaker if you're placing it on concrete, pavers, or decking, but on grass, a determined digger will tunnel out. A $10 tarp underneath solves this, but it's worth knowing before you buy.
