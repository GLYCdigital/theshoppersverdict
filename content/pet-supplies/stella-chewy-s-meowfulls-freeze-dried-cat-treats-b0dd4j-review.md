If your cat turns their nose up at everything in the treat drawer, you've probably cycled through a dozen brands already. Stella & Chewy's Meowfulls Freeze Dried Cat Treats are a different animal — literally. These are single-ingredient, raw-coated, freeze-dried morsels that skip the carb-heavy fillers most commercial treats rely on. At 4.4 stars from 220 ratings, they're not perfect for every feline. But for cats with sensitive stomachs or discerning palates, these might be the closest thing to a sure bet.

## What's actually in the bag

The Meowfulls line comes in several protein options — chicken, salmon, and duck are the most common. What you're getting is real meat, freeze-dried to lock in nutrients without cooking. No peas, no potatoes, no artificial colors or flavors. The ingredients list reads like a single line, which is exactly what you want when your cat has food sensitivities.

The treats themselves are small, irregularly shaped nuggets with a dry, crunchy exterior. Each bag is resealable, which matters more than you'd think — freeze-dried products go stale fast if exposed to air. Buyers consistently mention the packaging holding up well over weeks of use.

## How they perform in real life

Here's where the 4.4 rating starts to make sense. The crunch factor is a genuine hit with most cats. One reviewer noted, "My picky eater goes absolutely bonkers for the chicken flavor. She hears the bag crinkle and comes running from the other room." Another owner praised them as a solution for a senior cat with dental issues, saying, "I crumble them over her wet food and she eats every bite — it's the only way I get her to finish her meal."

The treats are also small enough to use for training without blowing your cat's daily calorie budget. You can break each piece into two or three smaller rewards, which stretches the bag further and helps with portion control.

## Where it falls short

Let's be straight: these are not cheap. Compared to standard baked treats from Friskies or Temptations, you're paying a noticeable premium per ounce. If your cat inhales treats without chewing, you'll go through a bag quickly.

The texture is also a double-edged sword. Some cats love the crunch; others want nothing to do with it. Multiple reviewers reported their cats walking away from the treats as-is, only to eat them when crumbled. If you have a senior cat with dental problems or a kitten with baby teeth, plan on breaking them up first.

One more thing: the strong meat smell. It's a sign of quality, but it's potent. If you're sensitive to fish or meat odors, the salmon variety in particular will let you know it's in the pantry.

## Who should buy these

- **Owners of picky eaters** — the single-ingredient formula and strong aroma win over cats that reject standard treats
- **Cats with food allergies or sensitive stomachs** — the limited ingredient list eliminates most common triggers
- **Training-focused households** — the small size and breakable texture make portion control easy

Skip them if your cat prefers soft, chewy treats or if you're on a tight budget — the cost per treat is higher than most mainstream options.

## Common questions shoppers ask

**Are these treats a complete meal replacement?**
No. They're formulated as treats, not a balanced diet. They work great as a topper or reward, but your cat still needs their regular food for complete nutrition.

**Can kittens eat these?**
Yes, but break them into smaller pieces. The hardness can be tough on baby teeth. The single-ingredient formula is generally gentle on developing digestive systems.

**How should I store them after opening?**
Keep the bag tightly sealed and store in a cool, dry place. The resealable closure helps, but if you live in a humid climate, consider transferring them to an airtight container. Freeze-dried products absorb moisture quickly.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** Your cat is a picky eater, has food sensitivities, or you want a clean-ingredient training treat that doesn't feel like junk food.

**Skip it if:** Your cat prefers soft treats, you're feeding multiple cats on a budget, or your cat has advanced dental issues that make crunching difficult.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The quality is genuinely excellent and the ingredient transparency is refreshing in a category full of vague "meat by-products." The price and texture limitations keep it from a perfect score, but for most cats, these deliver exactly what they promise: real food, freeze-dried, with nothing you don't need.
