## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this vest has two notable weaknesses. The hook-and-loop patch system works well initially, but it's a magnet for fur and lint. If you have a shedding breed, you'll need to clean the velcro periodically or the patches will start slipping. It's manageable, but it's maintenance you should expect.

The sizing is the bigger issue. This vest runs large, and it's genuinely unsuitable for small dogs. The smallest size still feels bulky on dogs under 25 pounds, and the handle placement becomes awkward when the dog's frame can't fill the vest properly. If you have a miniature poodle or a small terrier, this isn't your vest.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Will the patches come off in the wash?**
The patches stay secure through machine washing on a gentle cycle, but remove them before washing if you want to extend their lifespan. The velcro holds well, but repeated washing with the patches attached will eventually weaken the adhesive on the patch backing.

**Q: Does this vest work for dogs that pull on leash?**
Yes — the reinforced handle is specifically designed for control. It won't stop pulling by itself, but it gives you a solid grip point to redirect your dog. Pair it with proper training for best results.

**Q: How do I measure my dog for the right fit?**
Measure the girth (around the widest part of the chest) and the neck circumference. Compare those numbers to the size chart — don't rely on weight alone. If you're between sizes, size up, since the four-point adjustment lets you cinch it down.
