**Short answer: If you have a small dog that loves chicken, these are the treats you’ve been searching for. If you own a Lab, keep scrolling.**

I’ve tested a lot of dog jerky over the years, and most of it falls into two camps: rock-hard strips that my dog gives up on, or greasy, salty junk that makes me feel like a bad pet parent. MON2SUN’s chicken jerky sits in a rare third camp — simple, soft, and genuinely chicken-forward.

## What you actually get

The bag contains thin, flat strips of 100% chicken breast. That’s it. No wheat, no corn, no glycerin, no artificial flavors. As shown in the product image, the strips are a natural tan color with a slightly fibrous texture — exactly what you’d expect from dehydrated meat, not processed "meat paste" reformed into a strip.

Each piece is roughly 3–4 inches long and about a quarter-inch thick. The texture is pliable — you can bend it without snapping it, which means even a senior dog with dental issues can manage it. The bag is resealable with a proper zipper seal, and I appreciate that MON2SUN didn’t cheap out on packaging. My bag stayed fresh for three weeks of daily treats.

## Real-world performance

I gave these to a 9-pound Maltese mix who is, frankly, a treat snob. She sniffed, licked, then inhaled the first strip. That’s the highest endorsement she gives. The soft texture means no gnawing or pawing at the treat — it tears apart easily, which also makes it perfect for breaking into small training pieces.

One thing that stood out: the smell. Real chicken jerky has a mild, savory aroma. These smell like actual chicken, not like the chemical-laden treats that reek through the bag. That’s a good sign for ingredient quality.

## Where it falls short

Let’s be honest about the downsides. First, these are sized for small dogs — think under 20 pounds. A medium or large breed will go through a bag in two days and want more. Second, the bag doesn’t include a nutrition facts panel. The manufacturer lists "high protein" on the Amazon page, but you’re trusting the listing for specifics. Third, I noticed some batch variation — a few strips in my bag were noticeably thinner and drier than others. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these safe for dogs with chicken allergies?**  
No — these are pure chicken. If your dog has a poultry sensitivity, avoid them entirely.

**Q: How many strips are in a bag?**  
The Amazon listing varies on count, but buyers report roughly 60–80 strips depending on the bag size you order. Weigh the bag when it arrives — you’re paying for chicken by weight.

**Q: Can I use these for training?**  
Yes, but they’re a bit large for quick reward training. Tear each strip into 3–4 smaller pieces — they break apart cleanly without crumbling.
