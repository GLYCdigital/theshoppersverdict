**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the pate texture. Some cats — particularly those used to shreds or flaked food — will look at this dense block and walk away. If your cat is already eating a different texture successfully, don't assume they'll switch without protest. You may need a transition period mixing this with their current food.

There's also the carrageenan question. This ingredient is used as a thickener in many wet foods, but some owners actively avoid it due to anecdotal reports of digestive issues in sensitive cats. The FDA hasn't banned it, and most cats handle it fine, but if your cat has a known sensitive stomach, it's worth factoring in.
