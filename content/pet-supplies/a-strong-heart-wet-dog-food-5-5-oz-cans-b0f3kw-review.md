**A Strong Heart Wet Dog Food, 5.5 oz Cans Review: Real Talk for Picky Pups**

You've stood in the pet aisle staring at forty different cans, wondering if the $3 one actually tastes better than the $1.50 one. A Strong Heart sits somewhere in the middle — not budget, not boutique. The 4.2-star average across 210 Amazon ratings suggests most dogs agree on something. Here's what that something is, and where it falls short.

**What's actually in the can**

Each 5.5 oz can delivers a smooth pâté with visible shreds of meat throughout. The first ingredient is deboned meat (chicken or beef depending on the formula), followed by beef broth, chicken liver, and a short list of supplements. No corn, wheat, or soy — that's genuinely rare at this price point. As shown in the product image, the cans stack cleanly and the pull-tab opens without a can opener, which matters when you're fumbling at 6 AM.

The texture deserves specific mention: it's denser than gravy-style foods but softer than traditional pâtés. One Amazon reviewer described it as "mousse-like, not mush" — accurate. This consistency makes it the easiest food I've tested for hiding pills. You can press a tablet into the center and most dogs will inhale it without noticing.

**What 210 owners actually report**

The praise clusters around three things. First, digestion. Multiple reviewers with senior dogs noted improvement in stool quality within a week. Second, the moisture content — at roughly 78%, it's doing real hydration work for dogs who ignore their water bowl. Third, the "lick-the-bowl-clean" response from notoriously picky eaters. One verified purchase wrote, "My 14-year-old shih tzu who stopped eating after losing her companion finished a full can the first night." That's the emotional core of why this product gets bought.

**Where it stumbles**

The fish-forward aroma is the most common complaint — and it's legitimate. Even the chicken formula carries a noticeable ocean note that intensifies after sitting out. If your dog grazes over an hour, you'll smell it across the room. Some batches also show inconsistency in chunk size, with occasional larger liver pieces that smaller dogs will leave behind. Neither is a dealbreaker, but both are worth knowing.

**Who should buy this**

- **Senior dog owners** — The soft texture and hydration benefits align perfectly with aging teeth and kidneys.
- **Picky eater households** — If you've tried three brands this month, this one converts most dogs within two feedings.
- **Multi-dog homes** — The 5.5 oz size splits cleanly into two meals for 10-15 lb dogs without leftovers.

**Skip it if** your dog has a known fish sensitivity, or if you need the food to sit out for extended periods during free-feeding.

**FAQ**

**Q: How many cans should I feed my 20 lb dog?**
A: One can per day, split into two meals. For 30-40 lb dogs, one-and-a-half cans daily. Adjust based on activity level.

**Q: Is this suitable for dogs with sensitive stomachs?**
A: Generally yes — the limited ingredient list and single protein source make it a solid option. But introduce it over 4-5 days mixed with their current food.

**Q: Does it need refrigeration after opening?**
A: Yes. Covered in the fridge, it stays fresh for up to 3 days. Serve cold or warmed slightly — most dogs prefer it at room temperature.

**The Verdict**

A Strong Heart delivers on its core promise: quality wet food that picky dogs actually eat. It's not the cheapest option, but the clean ingredient list, hydration benefits, and consistent texture justify the premium over generic brands. The aroma issue is real but manageable — feed promptly and wash bowls immediately.

**Buy it if** you're fighting a picky eater or need a senior-friendly food that's easy on digestion.

**Skip it if** your dog is sensitive to strong fish odors, or if you're on a tight budget and your dog eats everything anyway.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid, dependable, and one of the better mid-tier wet foods for problem eaters.
