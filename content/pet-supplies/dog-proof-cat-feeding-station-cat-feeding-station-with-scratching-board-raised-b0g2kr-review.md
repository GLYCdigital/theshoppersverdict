## Does This Feeding Station Actually Keep the Dog Out?

If you've ever watched your dog inhale your cat's pricey grain-free kibble while your cat sits there judging you from the counter, you know the struggle. This Dog Proof Cat Feeding Station attempts to solve that with a raised platform, a barrier wall, and a built-in scratching board. The concept is sound. The execution? Mostly — with some caveats worth knowing before you buy.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the feeding station frame, a sisal-wrapped scratching board that mounts to the front, two stainless steel bowls (they're the standard 10-ounce size, not the giant ones), and the hardware bag. As shown in the product image, the design is a raised platform with a front-facing scratching panel that doubles as the dog-deterrent barrier. Assembly takes roughly 10–15 minutes. The wood is a medium-density composite with a smooth finish — not premium furniture grade, but sturdy enough for daily use.

## Real-World Performance: Does It Work?

The core question: does it stop the dog? Based on the 204 ratings at 4.1 stars, the answer is a qualified yes. One reviewer put it plainly: *"My 55-pound lab mix can't get his head over the barrier — he just stands there confused. The cat eats in peace for the first time in a year."* That sentiment echoes across multiple reviews. The raised height (roughly 12 inches off the ground) combined with the front panel creates a physical barrier that genuinely stumps most medium and large breeds.

But here's the honest catch: it's not foolproof. Smaller, athletic dogs — think terriers, dachshunds, or any dog that can jump a baby gate — will clear this. Several reviewers noted their smaller dogs simply jumped it. And one clever cat owner mentioned their cat figured out how to knock the bowls off the platform entirely. So, it's a deterrent, not a fortress.

The scratching board is a nice bonus. Your cat gets a legitimate scratching surface right where they eat, which reduces clawing on your couch. The sisal material holds up reasonably well, though heavy scratchers will wear it down faster than a dedicated floor-standing post. It's replaceable, but you'll need to source replacements separately.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

- **Peace at mealtime:** The #1 recurring theme. Multi-pet households report significantly less food stealing.
- **Cat acceptance:** Most cats adapt quickly. The elevated height seems to appeal to their natural preference for eating off the ground.
- **Dual functionality:** Buyers genuinely appreciate the scratching board integration — it's not just a gimmick.

## Where It Falls Short

The assembly hardware is the weakest link. The included hex wrench is cheap and prone to stripping. Have your own screwdriver handy. The composite wood is also prone to scratching if your cat uses the platform edges as a launch pad. Finally, the footprint is compact — if you have a larger breed cat (Maine Coon, for example), they might feel cramped eating from the bowls.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Multi-pet households** with a determined dog and a cat that eats too slowly — this is your solution.
- **Senior cat owners** — the elevated bowls genuinely help cats with arthritis or mobility issues.
- **Small-space dwellers** — the built-in scratcher means you skip buying a separate scratching post.

**Skip it if** you have a small, athletic dog that jumps fences, or if you're expecting a premium, heirloom-quality piece of furniture.

## FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Can a determined cat get past the barrier?**
A: Yes. The barrier is designed to deter dogs, not confine cats. Most cats can hop over it if they want. The design relies on your cat *wanting* to use it, which most do because their food is up there.

**Q: Are the bowls dishwasher safe?**
A: The stainless steel bowls are. The wooden frame is not. Wipe the frame down with a damp cloth — don't soak it.

**Q: What are the exact dimensions?**
A: The platform sits about 12 inches high. The overall footprint is roughly 20 inches wide and 15 inches deep. Check the listing for exact measurements before ordering to ensure it fits your space.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're tired of playing referee at mealtime and need a practical, reasonably priced solution that most dogs can't crack. **Skip it if** you have a jumper, a giant breed, or you're expecting flawless furniture-grade construction.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A functional, affordable solution for the classic dog-steals-cat-food problem, held back only by modest build quality and the reality that no station is truly dog-proof against every dog.
