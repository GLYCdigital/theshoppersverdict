If your cat turns up its nose at pâté and demands visible meat, Pure Cravings Chicken Recipe in Broth is worth a serious look. This shredded wet food comes in 3-ounce pouches and delivers exactly what the name promises: identifiable chicken shreds suspended in a light broth. For finicky felines who eat with their eyes first, that visual appeal matters — and it's the reason this product holds a solid 4.1-star average across 204 ratings.

**What you actually get**

Each package contains 12 individual 3-ounce pouches. The texture is the headline: real shredded chicken pieces rather than the uniform mush you find in most canned options. The broth is thin and clear, not gelatinous, which makes it easy to spoon over dry food as a topper or serve standalone. Ingredient-wise, it's a limited-ingredient formula — chicken, chicken broth, sunflower oil, and a few vitamins and minerals. No grains, no carrageenan, no artificial colors. That's a clean list that appeals to owners managing food sensitivities.

**Real-world performance**

Cats are the ultimate critics, and the feedback here is mostly positive. One reviewer noted, "My picky 14-year-old finally eats a full bowl without coaxing. She licks the broth clean and then works on the shreds." That pattern — broth first, meat second — shows up repeatedly in customer feedback. The high moisture content is a genuine health plus, especially for cats prone to urinary issues or those who don't drink enough water. The shreds are small enough for senior cats to manage but substantial enough that adult cats feel like they're getting real food.

**Where it stumbles**

No product is perfect, and Pure Cravings has a few quirks. The pouch design is the most common complaint. Multiple buyers mention tears and splatters: "Every single pouch splashes broth on my counter when I open it. I've learned to open over the bowl with a paper towel underneath." It's a minor annoyance but one you'll deal with daily. The broth-to-meat ratio also varies between pouches — some arrive broth-heavy, which frustrates cats who prefer the solids. And at roughly $1.50–$2.00 per 3-ounce pouch depending on the seller, it's pricier than Fancy Feast or Friskies. For a multi-cat household, that adds up quickly.

**Who should buy this**

This is the right food for owners of picky eaters, senior cats with dental issues who struggle with hard kibble, and cats with grain sensitivities. It also works well as a hydration booster — if your cat is a reluctant water drinker, the broth content helps compensate. It's less ideal for budget-conscious households with multiple cats or cats who prefer a thicker, more substantial texture.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Can I use this as a complete meal or just a topper?*
It's formulated as a complete and balanced meal per AAFCO guidelines for adult maintenance. You can serve it alone, though many owners use it as a topper to entice cats eating dry food.

*Is this suitable for kittens?*
The formula is labeled for adult cats. For kittens, consult your vet — they typically need higher calorie density and specific nutrient profiles during growth.

*How should I store opened pouches?*
Refrigerate after opening and use within 2–3 days. The broth can separate when chilled, so stir before serving. Some cats prefer it slightly warmed.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if:** Your cat demands visible meat, has a sensitive stomach, or needs extra hydration. The clean ingredient list and high moisture content justify the premium price for a single picky cat.

**Skip it if:** You're feeding multiple cats on a budget, or your cat prefers thicker gravies over thin broths. The pouch mess and per-ounce cost will wear on you.

Pure Cravings delivers exactly what it promises — quality shredded chicken in broth with a straightforward ingredient list. It's not the cheapest option, but for cats who've rejected everything else, it's often worth every penny. **4.2/5 stars.**
