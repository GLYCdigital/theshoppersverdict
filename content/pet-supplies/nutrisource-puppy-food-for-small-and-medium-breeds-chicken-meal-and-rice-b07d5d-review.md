Raising a puppy is a minefield of decisions, and food is the biggest one. You want something that fuels their zoomies without turning their stomach into a drum solo. NutriSource’s Puppy Food for Small and Medium Breeds (Chicken & Rice) is a contender that has built a solid reputation, holding a 4.6 out of 5 stars across 229 ratings. But is it just another bag of brown bits, or does it actually deliver? I dug into the ingredient panel, the customer feedback, and the real-world implications to give you the straight answer.

**What You’re Actually Paying For**

This is a dry kibble formula designed specifically for puppies up to 12 months old. The headline feature is the "good grain" philosophy—they use white rice as a digestible carbohydrate source rather than relying on fillers like corn or soy. The first ingredient is chicken, which is a high-quality protein source for muscle development. It’s also fortified with DHA for brain and eye development, a critical component for the early growth phase.

The bag includes added probiotics (specifically *Bacillus coagulans*) and prebiotics to support gut health. In plain terms, they are trying to minimize the "puppy diarrhea" phase that happens when you switch foods. It’s a solid, mid-tier recipe that isn't grain-free but is free from artificial colors and flavors.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The most noticeable physical trait is the kibble size. It is remarkably small—almost like a coarse sand. This is a genius move for toy breeds like Chihuahuas or Yorkies, but it also works well for medium breeds like Cocker Spaniels who are still teething. The texture is crunchy enough to help scrape off puppy plaque but not so hard that it hurts sensitive gums.

The real test is whether puppies actually eat it. Based on the feedback, the palatability is high. One buyer noted, *"My picky Shih Tzu puppy actually finishes his bowl now, and we haven't had any tummy issues since switching."* Another reviewer mentioned, *"The small pieces are perfect—no more choking hazards with my mini dachshund."* This suggests the formula is gentle on the stomach while still being tasty enough to entice finicky eaters.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Beyond the "no upset stomach" comments, the recurring theme in the 229 ratings is the coat condition. Multiple buyers mentioned that after a few weeks, their puppies' coats became noticeably shinier and softer. This is likely linked to the Omega-6 and Omega-3 fatty acids in the chicken fat and flaxseed. It’s a nice "bonus" effect that indicates the fat profile is well-balanced.

**Where It Falls Short (The Honest Truth)**

No product is perfect. The primary complaint isn't about quality, but about the protein source. This is a chicken and rice formula—if your puppy has a poultry sensitivity, this is a hard pass. You’ll need to look at their salmon or lamb varieties instead.

There is also the "gulper" problem. Because the kibble is so tiny, some medium-breed puppies (like Labradors or Golden Retrievers, though they are large breeds) will inhale it without chewing. If you have a voracious eater, you might need to use a slow-feeder bowl with this specific kibble size to prevent bloating or vomiting. Additionally, the price point is slightly higher than grocery-store brands like Purina Puppy Chow, though it sits below the ultra-premium freeze-dried options.

**Who Should Buy This?**

- **The Toy Breed Owner:** If you have a Maltese, Papillon, or French Bulldog, the kibble size is ideal. It prevents choking and is easy for their small jaws to manage.
- **The Sensitive Stomach Owner:** If you are transitioning from a breeder's food and want to avoid the "runny stool" phase, the probiotics here are a lifesaver.
- **The Value Seeker:** You want a grain-inclusive diet with real meat as the first ingredient but don't want to pay $90 for a 25lb bag.

**Skip It If:**

- Your vet has diagnosed a grain or chicken allergy.
- You have a giant breed puppy (Great Danes, Mastiffs) — this formula is not tailored for their specific growth rate needs.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this food suitable for a 12-week-old puppy?**
A: Yes. The kibble is small enough for weaning puppies, and the DHA content supports the rapid brain development happening at that age. Just ensure you are feeding the amount recommended on the bag based on their projected adult weight.

**Q: Can I mix this with wet food?**
A: Absolutely. The dry kibble holds its shape well in gravy without turning into a complete mush, which is a good sign of quality baking.

**Q: How long should I keep my puppy on this formula?**
A: Until they reach about 12-14 months of age, or when they reach their adult size. For small breeds, this might be sooner (around 10 months); for medium breeds, closer to a year.

**The Verdict**

NutriSource delivers a well-rounded, digestible puppy formula that checks the boxes for nutrition and palatability. It’s not a revolutionary product, but it is a reliable one. The 4.6-star rating is justified by the consistent results regarding digestion and coat health.

**Buy it if:** You want a grain-inclusive, high-protein food with probiotics that is easy for small mouths to handle.
