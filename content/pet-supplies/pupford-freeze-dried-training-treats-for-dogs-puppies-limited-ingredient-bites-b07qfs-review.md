**Who should buy these**

This is the sweet spot. If you're actively training a puppy, working on recall with a reactive dog, or have a pup with food sensitivities, these are excellent. The low calorie count means you can reward generously without ballooning your dog's daily intake. They're also small enough to be the perfect "jackpot" reward that doesn't interrupt training flow.

*Can these be used as a meal topper?*
Absolutely. Many buyers crush them over kibble to entice picky eaters. The freeze-dried texture rehydrates nicely when mixed with wet food.

*How long does an open bag stay fresh?*
The resealable pouch helps, but freeze-dried treats absorb moisture from the air. Use within 2-3 weeks of opening for best texture and freshness.
