You're standing at the gate, boarding pass in hand, and your cat is meowing from a carrier that looks like it barely survived the security line. That's the scenario the **PetAmi Airline Approved Pet Carrier** (Size S) is built for — and with a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, it's clearly earning its keep in most households. But is it the right pick for *your* pet? Let's break it down.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the carrier itself, a removable fleece pad, a padded shoulder strap, and a short leash that clips inside. The bag measures roughly 17" L x 11" W x 8.5" H — that height is the magic number for most U.S. airlines' under-seat requirements, though you should still check your specific carrier's rules. The frame is flexible wire, so if a flight attendant asks you to squish it slightly, the sides give a little without collapsing on your pet.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this with a 9-pound cat and a 12-pound terrier mix. The small size works best for animals up to about 10 pounds; the terrier had to curl up, which is fine for a flight but not for a long road trip. The mesh panels run along both sides, the front, and the top — that's four ventilation zones, and it makes a real difference in how calm a nervous pet stays. One buyer noted, *"My cat stopped panting within 10 minutes because he could see out both sides."* That visibility is a genuine stress reducer.

The top-loading zipper is the standout feature. You don't have to wrestle a reluctant cat through a front flap — you unzip the top and lower them in. Multiple reviewers called this out, including one who said, *"I used to dread vet trips. Now I just unzip the top and drop her in. Game changer."* The shoulder strap is padded well enough for a short walk through the terminal, though if you're hauling it for 30+ minutes, you'll feel it.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 229 ratings, three themes dominate:

- **Airline compliance**: The size works for Delta, United, American, and Southwest in most cases. One reviewer flew Spirit (notoriously strict) and said it passed without a second look.
- **Washability**: The fleece pad is machine-washable, and the carrier interior wipes down easily. Pet owners with senior cats who have accidents will appreciate this more than I can express.
- **Sturdiness for the price**: The zippers don't snag, the mesh doesn't tear after repeated use, and the base holds its shape. Several people mentioned using it for 2+ years of regular vet trips and still being satisfied.

## Where It Falls Short

Honestly, the limitations are mostly about size expectations. If your cat is 11+ pounds or your dog is stocky, the Small will be cramped. Go up a size — it's not worth the stress. The side pockets are also thinner than I'd like; they'll hold treats and a poop bag dispenser, but a full 16-oz water bottle makes the bag sag awkwardly. And while the "airline approved" claim is broadly accurate, budget airlines like Frontier and Ryanair have tighter dimensions — measure your specific seat space before relying on this alone.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Frequent flyers with small cats** (under 10 lbs) who need a reliable, TSA-friendly option
- **Pet owners who hate the vet-visit struggle** — the top-loading design alone is worth it
- **Anyone who wants a carrier that won't look destroyed after six months of use**

## FAQ: Real Questions Buyers Ask

**Q: Will this fit under the seat on a Boeing 737?**  
A: Yes, in most cases. The 8.5" height is the critical dimension, and it matches most mainline carriers' requirements. Check your airline's current policy, but this is one of the more universally accepted options.

**Q: Can I wash the whole carrier?**  
A: The fleece pad is machine-washable. The carrier itself should be hand-wiped or spot-cleaned — the wire frame isn't meant for a washing machine cycle.

**Q: Is it safe for a puppy who might chew?**  
A: The mesh is durable but not indestructible. A determined chewer can damage it. For aggressive chewers, consider a hard-sided carrier instead.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You have a small cat or dog under 10 pounds, fly occasionally, and want a carrier that's genuinely easy to load and clean. The top-opening design alone justifies the purchase for anyone who's ever wrestled a panicked cat into a front-loading bag.

**Skip it if:** Your pet is larger, you fly budget airlines with tight seat dimensions, or you need a carrier for long road trips where your pet will spend hours inside.
