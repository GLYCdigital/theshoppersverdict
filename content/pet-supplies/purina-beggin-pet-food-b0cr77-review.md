If your dog turns their nose up at everything you put in front of them, you've probably already heard of Purina Beggin'. The commercials make it look like bacon-obsessed dogs lose their minds over these strips. After digging through 240 Amazon ratings and the actual product specs, I can tell you the hype is mostly justified — with a few caveats worth knowing before you add to cart.

## What You're Actually Getting

The product image shows the familiar red and yellow bag with those bacon-strip-shaped pieces. Each piece is soft, moist, and roughly the size of a human thumb — small enough for training rewards but substantial enough to feel like a real treat. The ingredient list leads with meat by-products and soy flour, which isn't surprising at this price point.

You're not getting a premium, single-protein, grain-free treat here. You're getting a mass-market treat engineered for one thing: making dogs go absolutely nuts. And based on the reviews, that's exactly what it does.

## Real-World Performance

The 4.8-star average across 240 ratings isn't inflated. What stands out in the feedback is how consistently this product solves the picky-eater problem. One reviewer put it plainly: "My dog is the pickiest eater on the planet and she devours these like they're going out of style. I've tried every premium brand and nothing works like this."

Another common thread: the texture holds up. Several buyers mentioned using these for training sessions and noting the pieces don't crumble into a mess in your pocket or treat pouch — a real issue with softer treats from other brands.

The bacon scent is strong. Some reviewers noted it's almost too strong for human noses, but that's precisely what makes it effective for dogs. If you're using these for obedience training, that high-value appeal translates to faster responses and better focus.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

Three consistent themes in the positive reviews: effectiveness with picky eaters, freshness that lasts, and value. Multiple reviewers mentioned the bag staying soft for weeks after opening — something that can't be said for all soft treats, which tend to dry out.

One reviewer with a senior dog noted: "My 13-year-old lab has lost interest in most food but she'll still do tricks for Beggin'. It's the only thing that gets her excited anymore." That's a meaningful data point if you have an aging or ailing pet.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The ingredient list includes artificial colors (Red 40, Yellow 5) and BHA, a preservative that some pet owners actively avoid. If clean ingredients are non-negotiable for you, this isn't the product.

The calorie density is also worth watching. These aren't low-calorie training treats. For small dogs, a training session can quickly add up to noticeable calorie intake. Some reviewers mentioned needing to break pieces into smaller bits for portion control.

And the smell. It's strong. If you're sensitive to artificial bacon aroma, you'll want to store these in an airtight container. A few reviewers mentioned the scent lingering on their hands after feeding.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have a picky eater, need high-value training rewards, or want a treat that works for dogs of all ages — from puppies to seniors.

**Skip it if:** You're committed to clean-label ingredients, avoiding artificial additives, or need a low-calorie option for frequent training sessions.

This is also a solid choice for multi-dog households since the soft texture works well for dogs with dental issues — a point several reviewers with senior dogs raised.

## FAQ

**Is Purina Beggin' safe for puppies?**
Yes, it's formulated for all life stages. Just account for the calorie content — break pieces into smaller portions for puppies under six months.

**How long does a bag stay fresh after opening?**
Most reviewers reported the treats staying soft for 3-4 weeks when stored properly. The resealable packaging helps, but an airtight container extends freshness further.

**Can these be used as a complete meal replacement?**
No, these are treats. They're not nutritionally complete and shouldn't replace regular dog food. Limit to no more than 10% of daily caloric intake.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

Purina Beggin' delivers exactly what it promises: a treat that dogs genuinely love. It's not the cleanest option on the market, and the artificial ingredients will turn off some owners. But for effectiveness, consistency, and solving the picky-eater problem, it's hard to beat at this price.

**Buy it if** your dog needs convincing at mealtime or you want a reliable high-value training reward. **Skip it if** clean-label ingredients are your priority — you'll find better options, just don't expect the same enthusiastic response.
