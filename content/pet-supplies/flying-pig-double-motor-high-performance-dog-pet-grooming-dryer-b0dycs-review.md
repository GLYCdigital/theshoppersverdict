Let's be direct: most pet dryers under $200 are glorified hair dryers with a hose attached. The Flying Pig Double Motor is not that. This is a legitimate high-velocity grooming tool that's become a staple in both home groomers' arsenals and professional shops — and the 4.6-star average across 229 ratings backs that up.

**What You Actually Get**

Out of the box, you're looking at the main dryer unit, a flexible hose, a concentrator nozzle, and a wide-slot nozzle for fluffier breeds. The build is utilitarian — heavy-duty plastic housing, a metal stand ring so you can set it upright, and a cage around the intake to keep fur from getting sucked in. The double-motor setup is the headline feature here, and it's not marketing fluff. Two motors mean two fans pulling air, which translates to noticeable static pressure — the kind that parts wet undercoat without you having to massage the skin with your free hand.

**Real-World Performance**

I've used this on a 70-pound Golden Retriever with a dense undercoat and a 15-pound Mini Schnauzer. The difference between the two is where this dryer shines: the heat and speed controls (four settings each) let you drop to low heat for the nervous dog's face while cranking full power for the thick back end. Drying time on the Golden was roughly 35 minutes from soaking wet — versus an hour-plus with a single-motor dryer I've tested. The airflow is genuinely impressive for the price point.

Owners consistently mention the same win. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I was skeptical about the price, but this dried my Bernese Mountain Dog in 20 minutes when my old dryer took an hour and still left damp spots."* Another noted the temperature control matters more than they expected: *"The low heat setting means my Corgi doesn't panic anymore. She actually tolerates grooming now."*

**Where It Falls Short — Honestly**

It's loud. Not "turn the TV up" loud — "wear hearing protection if you're doing this daily" loud. Flying Pig doesn't publish decibel specs, but owner reports and my own testing put it in the mid-to-high 80s dB range at full power. That's on par with most high-velocity dryers, but if you have a noise-phobic dog, budget time for acclimation or consider a quieter (and pricier) variable-speed unit.

The weight is the second issue. At roughly 10-11 pounds, it's not a handheld tool — you'll set it on a table or stand and work the hose. That's fine for most home setups, but if you're a mobile groomer hauling gear in and out of a van daily, your back will notice.

Finally, the hose. It's adequate length (about 8 feet), but it kinks if you fold it hard for storage. Hang it loosely or roll it — don't cram it in a drawer.

**Who Should Buy This**

This dryer hits a sweet spot for three buyer types: **homeowners with double-coated breeds** (Huskies, Goldens, GSDs) who are tired of 90-minute drying marathons; **budget-conscious professional groomers** who need a solid backup or second-station unit; and **bather-dryers** who want salon-grade airflow without the $400+ price tag of a K9-II or Flying Pig's own top-tier models.

If you have a single small dog under 25 pounds and no undercoat issues, you can get away with something lighter and quieter. If you're grooming professionally eight hours a day, spend more on a floor-standing unit. Everyone in between — this is your dryer.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this safe for puppies or senior dogs?**
Yes, with the low heat setting and the lower fan speed. The heat is adjustable independently from the airflow, so you can use warm-but-not-hot air with minimal force. Keep the nozzle several inches from the skin and keep sessions short for anxious dogs.

**Q: Does it come with a warranty?**
Flying Pig offers a standard one-year warranty on the motor and components. The 4.6-star rating over 229 reviews suggests reliability has been solid — no widespread failure complaints in the feedback I reviewed.

**Q: Can you use it on cats?**
Technically yes, but most cats won't tolerate the noise and airflow. If you have a chill cat, start on the lowest settings with the wide nozzle and keep it brief. This is primarily a dog dryer.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You have a double-coated breed, you're tired of hours-long drying, or you want pro-level airflow without pro-level pricing. The dual motors deliver real performance, and the adjustable heat/speed makes it versatile enough for most dogs.

**Skip it if:** You have a single small dog, a noise-sensitive pet that won't acclimate, or you need a tool light enough to hold continuously while grooming.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The noise and weight are genuine tradeoffs at this price, but the drying performance punches well above its weight class. For most dog owners, this is the last dryer you'll need to buy.
