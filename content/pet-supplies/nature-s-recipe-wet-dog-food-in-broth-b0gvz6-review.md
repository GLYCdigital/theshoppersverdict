If your dog turns up their nose at dry kibble but you're not ready to commit to a raw diet, Nature's Recipe Wet Dog Food in Broth sits in a practical middle ground. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, this isn't an obscure product — it's a steady performer that dog owners keep repurchasing. Here's what you're actually getting.

**What's in the pouch**

Each 2.5-ounce pouch contains shredded chicken (or the protein variant you choose) suspended in a thin, savory broth with visible chunks of sweet potato, green beans, and carrots. The ingredient list is refreshingly short: chicken, chicken broth, vegetables, and a few supplements like taurine and vitamins. No corn, wheat, or soy. No artificial colors. For dogs with sensitive stomachs, that limited ingredient approach matters.

The texture is the headline feature. Unlike traditional canned pâté that requires scooping and mashing, this pours like a light stew. As shown in the product image, the meat is actual shreds, not formed loaf. That visual distinction makes a difference for picky eaters who inspect their food before committing.

**Real-world performance**

The moisture content runs around 78%, which means two things: it's excellent for hydration, and it's not a calorie-dense meal replacement on its own. Most owners use this as a topper over kibble — a generous pour adds both aroma and palatability that dry food lacks. A 40-pound dog eating this as a complete meal would need roughly three pouches per day, which gets expensive. As a topper, one pouch covers two meals comfortably.

Buyers consistently highlight the same win: dogs that ignore their bowl suddenly eat. One verified purchaser noted, "My 14-year-old beagle lost interest in food until I started mixing this in. She licks the bowl clean now." Another owner mentioned using it to hide medication — the strong broth smell masks pills effectively.

**Where it falls short**

The thin broth is the most common complaint. Several reviewers expected a thicker gravy based on the marketing images and found the actual consistency closer to chicken stock. It's not a dealbreaker, but manage expectations.

Portioning is the second issue. Once opened, pouches last 2–3 days refrigerated. If you have a small dog, you'll either toss unused portions or get creative with freezing. The pouches are also not resealable — you'll need a clip or transfer container.

**Who should buy this**

This works best for:
- **Picky eaters** — the aroma and texture win over most reluctant dogs
- **Senior dogs** — easier to chew than crunchy kibble, and the extra moisture supports kidney health
- **Dogs on medication** — the broth masks pills effectively
- **Owners transitioning dogs off canned pâté** — the shredded texture feels more like real food

Skip it if your dog has a strict prescription diet, you need a standalone complete meal on a budget, or your dog prefers thick gravies.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can this be fed as a complete meal?**
A: Yes, it's AAFCO-compliant for adult maintenance. But at roughly three pouches daily for a medium dog, it's pricey. Most owners use it as a topper.

**Q: Is it suitable for puppies?**
A: This formula targets adult dogs. Nature's Recipe makes a separate puppy line — use that instead.

**Q: How long does an open pouch last?**
A: Two to three days refrigerated. Don't leave it out longer than 30 minutes at room temperature.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** your dog is a picky eater, you want clean ingredients without the raw-food commitment, or you need a reliable way to boost hydration.

**Skip it if** you need a budget-friendly complete meal or your dog prefers thick, gravy-style wet food.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Solid, dependable, and effective at what it promises. The broth consistency and portion waste keep it from a perfect score, but for most dog owners, this is a kitchen staple worth reordering.
