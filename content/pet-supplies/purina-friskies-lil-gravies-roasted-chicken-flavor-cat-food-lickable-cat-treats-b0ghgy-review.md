Let’s be honest about what Purina Friskies Lil' Gravies Roasted Chicken Flavor actually is: a lickable wet treat, not a meal replacement. If you’ve got a cat that turns its nose up at dry kibble or needs medication hidden in something irresistible, this 0.85-ounce pouch is the kind of product that either becomes a daily ritual or collects dust in the pantry. With a 4.2-star average across 210 ratings, the consensus is clear — most cats love it, but the packaging and ingredient list give some owners pause.

## What’s actually in the pouch

Each box contains 12 single-serve pouches. The texture is a thin, smooth gravy — think thick broth rather than pâté. There are no meat chunks, which is either a plus (easy to lick up) or a downside (no satisfying bites), depending on your cat’s preference. The roasted chicken flavor comes through strongly in the smell, which is honestly the main selling point. As one reviewer put it: “My cat comes running from across the house the second she hears the pouch crinkle. It’s the only treat she’ll actually finish completely.”

## Real-world performance

The gravy consistency works well for several use cases. It pours cleanly without splattering, and the narrow pouch opening makes it easy to squeeze into a bowl or directly onto dry food as a topper. For senior cats with dental issues or kittens transitioning to solids, the lickable format is genuinely practical — no chewing required.

The calorie count is a definite win: each pouch comes in at under 2 kcal, meaning you can offer it multiple times a day without wrecking your cat’s daily intake. That’s a meaningful advantage over creamy tube treats that pack 10+ calories per serving.

## What buyers consistently praise

- **Picky-eater reliability** — Multiple reviewers mention cats that ignore other treats but go crazy for this. One owner noted: “My 14-year-old cat has kidney disease and lost her appetite. This is the only thing she’ll eat without coaxing.”
- **Clean application** — The liquid texture means no sticky paw prints or crumbs. It’s also easy to mix with water for extra hydration, which several owners do for cats prone to urinary issues.
- **Medication delivery** — The strong flavor masks pills surprisingly well, according to several reviews.

## Where it falls short

The serving size is the biggest complaint. At 0.85 ounces, a single pouch is barely a mouthful for a larger cat. You’ll likely need two pouches per sitting for a 12-pound cat, which makes it pricier than it first appears. 

The ingredient list is also a sticking point for some buyers. It contains carrageenan, a thickener that some pet owners avoid due to potential digestive sensitivity. While most cats tolerate it fine, if yours has a sensitive stomach, start with a single pouch before committing to a full box.

## Who should buy this

- **Multi-cat households** with at least one picky eater — the universal appeal saves you from buying three different treat brands
- **Owners of senior cats** with dental issues or reduced appetite
- **Anyone needing to administer pills** without a fight
- **Kitten owners** looking for a high-value training reward

## FAQ

**Is this a complete food or a treat?**  
It’s a treat. It doesn’t meet AAFCO nutritional standards for a complete diet, so use it as a supplement, not a meal.

**Can I use it as a dry food topper?**  
Yes, many owners squeeze it over kibble to encourage eating. It softens the dry food slightly and adds moisture.

**How should I store opened pouches?**  
Refrigerate any unused portion and use within 24 hours. The pouches are single-serving, so leftover amounts are minimal.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** Your cat is picky, senior, or needs medication hidden. The low-calorie formula and high palatability make it a solid daily treat.

**Skip it if:** You’re on a tight budget (the small servings add up), or you strictly avoid carrageenan in pet foods.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — It does exactly what it promises, with minor compromises on portion size and ingredients.
