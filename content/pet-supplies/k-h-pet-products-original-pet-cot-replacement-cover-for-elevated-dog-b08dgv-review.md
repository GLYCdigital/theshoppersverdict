If you've owned a K&H Original Pet Cot for more than a year, you already know the problem: the frame is bulletproof, but the mesh cover eventually gives out. A determined chewer or an overenthusiastic claw session leaves you with a sagging, torn surface and a frame that's perfectly fine. That's exactly where the K&H PET Products Original Pet Cot Replacement Cover comes in — and it solves that specific problem cleanly.

## What you're actually getting

This is a direct OEM replacement cover, not a generic "fits most" aftermarket gamble. It's made from the same 600D polyester mesh as the original, with a reinforced hem and corner pockets that slip over the cot's frame tubes. The package contains just the cover — no frame, no legs, no hardware. That's the point: you're replacing the worn part, not rebuying what you already own.

As shown in the product image, the cover has distinct corner pockets that create tension when pulled over the frame. That design matters. It's what keeps the sleeping surface taut rather than sagging in the middle like a hammock that's lost its stretch.

## Real-world performance

The mesh is what you'd expect from K&H's outdoor-grade line: dense enough to support a dog without pressure points, breathable enough for summer use. One buyer noted, *"My 70-pound lab has destroyed two cheaper cots. This cover has survived six months of daily use and still looks new."* That tracks with the materials — 600D polyester is the same weight class used in truck bed covers and heavy-duty luggage.

Washing is straightforward. Hose it off, or toss it in the washer on gentle and air dry. Multiple reviewers confirmed it comes out of the wash without shrinking or losing shape, which is a common failure point on cheaper replacement covers.

## Where it falls short

The biggest issue is compatibility. This cover only fits K&H Original Pet Cot frames — not the K&H Coolin' Cot, not the Coolin' Pet Cot, and not knockoff frames from other brands. The corner pocket design is specific to the Original's frame geometry. If you're not sure which model you have, measure the frame length and check against K&H's sizing chart before ordering. Several one-star reviews are people who bought it for the wrong frame.

The second issue: there's no waterproofing. If your dog has an accident or comes in soaked from outside, moisture passes straight through to the frame. That's fine for outdoor use, but if you're using it indoors, you'll want a waterproof liner underneath. It's not a dealbreaker — the original cot has the same limitation — but it's worth knowing.

Finally, the tension isn't permanent. With heavy dogs, the mesh relaxes over time and needs to be re-stretched by pulling the corner pockets tighter on the frame. It's a five-minute fix, not a structural flaw, but don't expect it to stay drum-tight forever.

## Who should buy this

- **Owners of K&H Original Pet Cots** whose cover has torn or sagged — this is the obvious, cost-effective fix
- **Outdoor dog owners** who need a breathable, quick-drying surface that won't hold heat
- **Multi-dog households** who want a spare cover on hand so they can wash one while the other is in use

Skip it if you have a different cot brand, need waterproofing, or have a dog that chews through everything — no mesh cover will survive a determined power chewer.

## FAQ

**Will this fit my K&H cot?**
Only if you have the Original Pet Cot. Measure the frame's interior length and compare with K&H's size chart. The Coolin' Cot and Coolin' Pet Cot use different frame geometry and won't work.

**Can I machine wash it?**
Yes. Use cold water, gentle cycle, and air dry. Avoid the dryer — high heat can shrink the mesh and compromise the fit.

**How long does it last?**
With normal use, expect 1–2 years depending on dog weight and activity level. Claw damage and chewing accelerate wear, but most buyers report the cover outlasting their previous full cot.
