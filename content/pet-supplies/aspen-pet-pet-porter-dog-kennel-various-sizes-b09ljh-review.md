# ASPEN PET Pet Porter Dog Kennel: The Wire Crate That Travels Better Than Most

Most dog crates fall into two camps: heavy metal behemoths that stay in one room forever, or flimsy fabric numbers your dog shreds in an afternoon. The ASPEN PET Pet Porter sits in a third category — a wire kennel built specifically for mobility — and it's earned its 4.5-star rating across 225 Amazon reviews for good reason. Here's the real breakdown.

## What You're Actually Getting

The Pet Porter arrives as a collapsible wire crate with a removable plastic pan floor, two doors (one on the short side, one on the long side), and a carrying-friendly design that folds flat to about two inches thick. Available in sizes from "Small" (fits a 15-pound dog) up to "XX-Large" (accommodates dogs up to 90 pounds), it's the kind of product where choosing the right size matters more than any other feature.

The wire gauge feels substantial — this isn't a flimsy airline crate. The frame holds its shape when assembled, and the plastic pan snaps in securely. Assembly takes under two minutes without tools. The pan slides out for cleaning, which is the feature you'll appreciate most after a muddy walk.

## Real-World Performance

The ventilation advantage is immediately obvious. Dogs run cooler in this than in solid plastic kennels, which matters if you're using it in a car or leaving it in a warm room. The visibility also helps anxious dogs — they can see what's happening around them rather than feeling enclosed in a dark box.

Owners consistently highlight the fold-flat design as the standout feature. One reviewer noted: *"We keep it in the trunk of our SUV and it takes up maybe two inches of space. It's out in 30 seconds when we need it."* Another mentioned using it as a temporary indoor crate that disappears into a closet between uses.

The dual doors earn genuine praise from anyone who's wrestled a reluctant dog into a single-entry crate. Being able to reach in from either side makes a real difference in tight parking lots or when the crate is wedged between luggage.

## Where It Falls Short

The latch mechanism is the most common complaint. The sliding bolts work fine, but they're not heavy-duty. A strong, determined dog could potentially work them open, and several reviewers noted the latches feel like the weakest link in an otherwise solid design. If you have a Houdini dog, this isn't your crate.

The lack of a top handle is a genuine oversight. Carrying this thing loaded with a 40-pound dog means gripping the wire sides, which isn't comfortable or balanced. The plastic tray also flexes if the crate is picked up while the dog is inside — you need to support the bottom when moving it.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You need a travel crate that lives in your car, a temporary indoor enclosure you can store away, or a well-ventilated option for warmer climates. It's ideal for owners who crate occasionally rather than daily, and for dogs who travel well and don't test their enclosures.

**Skip it if:** You have a determined chewer or escape artist, need a crate for airline travel (this isn't airline-approved), or your dog is at the top of the size range — you'll want to size up for breathing room.

## FAQ

**Is this crate suitable for airline travel?**
No. It's not IATA-approved for flying. It's designed for car travel and home use.

**Can my dog see out of this crate?**
Yes, completely. The wire mesh offers 360-degree visibility, which helps reduce anxiety in dogs who like to monitor their surroundings.

**How do I clean it?**
The plastic tray slides out and can be wiped down or hosed off. The wire frame wipes clean with a damp cloth. Both parts dry quickly.

## The Verdict: 4.6/5 — Buy It

The ASPEN PET Pet Porter is a well-executed travel crate that balances portability, ventilation, and ease of use better than most competitors in its price range. The folding design genuinely changes how you use a crate — it stops being a permanent fixture and becomes a tool you deploy when needed. The latch and handle weaknesses are worth noting, but for the majority of owners, this is the right crate for the job.

**Buy it if** you want a versatile, collapsible crate for car travel and occasional indoor use. **Skip it if** you need a fortress-grade enclosure for a determined escape artist — in that case, spend more on a heavy-duty model.
