You can buy a dog treat that's basically a shaped biscuit with food coloring, or you can buy one that actually does something. Dogswell's Skin & Coat treats fall firmly into the second camp — and at 4.5 stars across 225 Amazon ratings, owners are noticing the difference.

These soft chews are built around a single functional goal: improving your dog's skin and coat from the inside out. The formula leads with salmon, a quality animal protein that delivers natural omega-3 fatty acids, then backs it up with flaxseed for additional omega-6s. That's a legitimate nutritional approach, not marketing puffery. Omega fatty acids are the building blocks of healthy skin cell membranes and hair follicles — if your dog is scratching more than usual or shedding like a tumbleweed factory, this is the mechanism that addresses it.

**What you actually get**

Each bag contains soft, chewy treats about the size of a nickel, scored for easy breaking into smaller pieces. The texture is pliable — think fruit leather rather than hard kibble — which makes them ideal for older dogs with dental issues or puppies still teething. A 6-ounce bag runs roughly $10-12 depending on the retailer, and at 2-3 treats per day for a 30-pound dog, you're looking at about a three-week supply. The resealable bag is a small but welcome touch that keeps them from drying out.

**The real-world results**

Owners consistently report visible improvements within 2-4 weeks of daily use. One verified buyer noted, "My pit bull had dry, flaky patches on her back for months. Three weeks on these and her coat is noticeably glossier, and the flakes are gone." Another mentioned their vet commented on their dog's coat condition at an annual checkup — which is about as objective a third-party endorsement as you can get.

The fatty acid content works gradually, so don't expect overnight transformation. This is a maintenance product, not a miracle cure. Dogs with environmental allergies or food sensitivities may need veterinary intervention beyond what any treat can provide.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be direct: the fish smell is real. Opening the bag releases a distinct salmon aroma that some owners find off-putting. Your dog won't care — they'll likely go nuts for it — but if you're sensitive to fishy scents, keep the bag sealed in a pantry or container.

The price is also a consideration. Per-ounce, these cost roughly 30-40% more than standard training treats. You're paying for the functional ingredient quality, and for most owners that's justified. But if you just need a cheap reward for basic obedience training, this isn't the most economical choice.

**Who should buy these**

This product shines for specific situations:

- **Owners of dogs with dull, brittle coats or visible dandruff** — the omega blend addresses the root cause
- **Senior dogs with dental sensitivity** — the soft texture is genuinely easier to chew than hard biscuits
- **Breed owners prone to skin issues** — Golden retrievers, bulldogs, and Labradors with known skin sensitivities benefit from consistent omega supplementation

Skip it if your dog has a confirmed fish allergy, if you need a high-volume training treat on a budget, or if your dog's skin issues stem from environmental allergies that require prescription management.

**Common questions buyers ask**

**How many treats per day?** The feeding guide suggests 2-3 treats daily for dogs under 30 pounds, 3-5 for medium breeds, and up to 6-8 for large dogs. Start at the low end and adjust based on your dog's response and weight.

**Can puppies eat these?** Yes, the formula is safe for puppies over 8 weeks. The soft texture makes them particularly easy for young dogs to manage.

**How long until I see results?** Most owners report visible coat improvements within 2-4 weeks of consistent daily use. Skin flaking and itchiness typically resolve faster than full coat regrowth.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** your dog has dry, flaky skin, a dull coat, or you want a proactive daily supplement disguised as a reward. The salmon-first formula delivers legitimate nutritional value that most supermarket treats can't touch.

**Skip it if** you need a budget-friendly high-volume training treat or your dog has a confirmed fish allergy.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The fishy smell and premium price are the only real drawbacks. For the functional benefit delivered, this is one of the better value propositions in the skin-and-coat treat category. Your dog gets a daily reward; you get a shinier, healthier companion. That's a fair trade.
