If you've ever paid $8 for a single bully stick only to watch a determined Lab destroy it in eleven minutes, the Cadet Gourmet Bull Sticks 12-pack is the value play you've been hunting for. This is a 21-ounce bag of beef pizzle chews — the same muscle tissue that makes bully sticks so irresistible — but sold in a multi-pack that actually respects your budget.

## What's actually in the bag

You're getting 12 individual bull sticks weighing in at roughly 1.75 ounces each. They're made from free-range Brazilian beef, which sounds fancier than it is — what matters more is that the ingredient list stops at "beef pizzle." No rawhide, no artificial colors, no preservatives, no mystery binding agents. For dogs with poultry allergies or sensitive stomachs, this single-protein chew is about as clean as it gets.

The sticks are slow-roasted rather than smoked, which is worth noting if you've ever banned bully sticks from the house because the smell reminded you of a barn fire. These have a noticeably milder aroma — still present, because it's dried animal tissue, but your living room won't smell like a feedlot for three days.

## How they actually perform

The texture hits that sweet spot between tough and yielding. A moderate chewer will work through one of these in 30 to 45 minutes, which makes them ideal for crate time, movie nights, or keeping a hyper dog occupied while you take a conference call. The sticks soften slightly as they're chewed, so they're gentler on teeth than antlers or bones — a real consideration for senior dogs or breeds prone to dental fractures.

One thing buyers consistently mention is how long the pack lasts. As one reviewer put it, "My German Shepherd goes through these in about 20 minutes, but at this price I don't feel robbed like I do with single sticks." Another noted that the pack "saved my baseboards" — which is honestly the highest praise a chew product can receive.

## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the variability. Because these are natural animal products, no two sticks are identical. Some are thick and dense; others are thinner and get demolished quickly. If you have a power chewer, the thinner sticks won't last — you'll want to supervise and swap in a tougher chew when you notice the stick getting small enough to swallow whole.

There's also the calorie question. Bully sticks are protein-dense, and one of these runs roughly 120-150 calories. For a small breed or a dog on a strict diet, that's a meaningful chunk of their daily intake. This isn't a daily treat for every dog — it's a once-in-a-while chew session kind of product.

## Who should buy this pack

This is for owners of medium-to-large dogs who chew regularly and need a supply that doesn't require a second mortgage. It's also ideal for households with multiple dogs — 12 sticks means you won't be rationing or refereeing over the last chew. If you have a small breed, a puppy under six months, or a dog with pancreatitis or weight issues, you'll want to think carefully about portion control before committing.

## FAQs shoppers actually ask

**How long does one stick last?**
For a moderate chewer, expect 30-45 minutes. Aggressive chewers might finish in 15-20 minutes, especially with the thinner sticks in the pack.

**Are these safe for puppies?**
Not ideal for puppies under six months. The density can be tough on developing teeth, and the calorie load is significant for growing dogs. Stick to puppy-specific chews.

**Will these stain my carpet?**
They can leave a greasy spot if chewed on carpet. Give them to your dog on a hard floor or a washable mat — that's a universal bully stick truth, not a flaw specific to this brand.

## The verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a clean-ingredient, high-value chew that keeps a medium or large dog busy without breaking the bank. The 12-stick format is genuinely economical, and the odor control makes indoor use realistic.

**Skip it if:** You have a tiny dog, a puppy, or a dog with dietary restrictions that make high-protein treats a problem. Also skip if your dog needs a marathon chew — these are a solid 30-45 minute distraction, not an all-day occupation.
