If your dog is slowing down on stairs or hesitating before jumping on the couch, you've probably started googling joint supplements. The market is flooded with options, but Old Mother Hubbard by Wellness Mother's Solutions Hip & Joint stands out for one reason: it actually contains meaningful doses of the two ingredients veterinarians recommend most — glucosamine and chondroitin.

As shown in the product image, this is a straightforward powder supplement, not a chew. That's a deliberate choice. The powder format allows for higher active ingredient concentrations without the fillers and binders that soft chews require. Each scoop delivers 500mg of glucosamine HCl and 400mg of chondroitin sulfate, plus MSM for added joint support. For a 50-pound dog, that's a clinically relevant daily dose — not the token amounts you'll find in many budget chews.

## What You Actually Get

The bag contains a 5.3-ounce container of powder with a measuring scoop inside. The base is sweet potato and chickpeas, which explains why so many owners report their dogs eat it without fuss. You mix it into food once daily, and it dissolves quickly. No pill battles, no hidden-cheese-in-a-pill-pocket routine.

Wellness is a brand with real credibility in the pet food space, and this supplement is manufactured in their US facilities. The ingredient sourcing is transparent, and they include a money-back guarantee if your dog doesn't show improvement within 60 days.

## What Owners Consistently Praise

Across the 225 ratings (4.5 stars), the most common theme is **visible mobility improvement within 2–4 weeks**. One owner of a 9-year-old Labrador wrote: *"My girl was struggling to get in the car. After three weeks on this, she jumps in like she's a puppy again."* Another reviewer with a German Shepherd noted the powder was the only supplement their dog would actually eat consistently, saying *"I've tried three different chews and she spit them all out. This she gobbles down with her breakfast."*

The palatability advantage is real. The sweet potato base masks the typically bitter taste of glucosamine, and multiple reviewers noted their cats have tried stealing it too — which tells you the smell is appealing.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The scoop is small, and if you have a large breed dog (70+ pounds), the directions recommend two scoops daily. At that rate, one bag lasts about a month. That's not cheap for a long-term supplement. Several reviewers mentioned this cost factor directly: *"Works great, but I wish the bag was bigger. I'm ordering every month."*

Also, the formula contains chicken meal. If your dog has a poultry sensitivity, this is an immediate dealbreaker. There's no fish-based or vegan alternative in this line, so you'd need to look elsewhere.

Finally, this is a **maintenance supplement**, not a cure. If your dog has severe arthritis or hip dysplasia, this won't replace prescription NSAIDs or Adequan injections. It works best as a preventive measure or for mild-to-moderate stiffness.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Owners of aging dogs (7+ years)** who are seeing early signs of stiffness but don't need prescription pain meds yet
- **People whose dogs refuse joint chews** — the powder format solves this problem
- **Active breeds prone to joint issues** (Labs, Goldens, Shepherds) as a preventive measure starting at age 5–6

## FAQ

**How long until I see results?** Most owners report noticeable improvement in 2–4 weeks. Some see changes in as little as 10 days. Give it a full 60 days before judging — that's why Wellness offers the money-back guarantee.

**Can I give this with other medications?** Generally yes, but always tell your vet. Glucosamine can interact with blood thinners, so if your dog is on any medication, get a quick vet confirmation first.

**Is this safe for puppies?** No. This is formulated for adult and senior dogs. Puppies are still developing joint cartilage and need different nutritional support.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** Your dog has mild-to-moderate stiffness, you want a supplement with clinically relevant dosing, and your dog refuses pill-form joint chews.

**Skip it if:** Your dog has a poultry allergy, you have a large breed and a tight budget (monthly cost adds up), or your dog needs prescription-strength pain management.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The effective doses, high palatability, and trusted brand make this one of the better joint supplement values on Amazon. Just factor in the recurring cost if you own a big dog.
