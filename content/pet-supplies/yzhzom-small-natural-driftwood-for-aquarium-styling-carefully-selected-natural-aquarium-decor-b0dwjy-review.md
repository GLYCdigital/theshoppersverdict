**FAQ**

**Q: Do I need to boil this before adding to my tank?**  
Yes—or at minimum soak it for 3-5 days. Boiling for 10-15 minutes speeds up waterlogging and kills any hitchhikers. Skipping this step risks introducing unwanted organisms.

**Q: Will this lower my pH?**  
Yes, slightly. The tannins released are mildly acidic. That’s great for bettas, tetras, and dwarf cichlids. It’s not ideal for African cichlids or livebearers that prefer higher pH.

**Q: How many pieces are actually in the pack?**  
It varies by listing variant, but most buyers report receiving 4-6 pieces. The sizes range from about 2-6 inches. Check the specific variant you’re ordering for the piece count.
