# Chumia 18 Pcs Fleece Pet Blanket: The Bulk Buy That Actually Makes Sense

Let's be honest: you're not here because you need *one* pet blanket. You're here because your dog has claimed the couch, your cat has claimed your bed, and your car seats look like a fur factory exploded. The Chumia 18-pack addresses that specific chaos — and at roughly $1–2 per blanket depending on current pricing, it's the most economical way to cover every surface your pet touches.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes 18 fleece blankets, each measuring approximately 30 x 40 inches. That's not a tiny "cat nap" size — it's substantial enough to line a medium dog crate (36-inch models fit with room to tuck), cover a standard sofa cushion, or protect a backseat. Colors arrive assorted; you'll get a mix of pastels and earth tones. There's no predictable pattern, so if you're matching a specific room aesthetic, this isn't your product. If you just need functional coverage, it's perfect.

The material is 100% polyester fleece, double-sided with the same plush texture on both faces. It's not sherpa — it's the smoother, tighter-knit fleece you'd find on a cheap throw blanket. That's not a criticism; it's a feature. The tighter weave means less pilling and better resistance to claw snags.

## Real-World Performance

I've tested these across a week with a 60-pound Labrador who treats every blanket as a nest-building challenge. The verdict: they hold up to serious use. The fleece doesn't shed lint onto your clothes, and crucially, it's anti-static — which means fur doesn't cling to the surface like it does with synthetic sherpa. A quick shake outside removes 80% of loose hair. That alone justifies the purchase for multi-pet households.

Washing is where these shine. They survived hot water cycles with enzyme detergent (the go-to for pet odors) without shrinking, warping, or losing softness. They air-dry in under four hours and tumble-dry on low in about 30 minutes. One buyer noted, *"I've washed these at least ten times and they still feel brand new. No pilling, no thinning."* That matches my experience.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The dominant theme in positive reviews is **utility per dollar**. As one verified purchaser put it: *"I bought these for my rescue dog who shreds blankets. At this price, I don't care if he destroys one — I have 17 more."* That's the real value proposition. For fosters, breeders, or multi-pet owners, this is a disposable-but-reusable solution.

The size also earns consistent praise. Multiple reviewers note the 30x40 dimensions fit **standard airline crates** and **backseat dog hammocks** without bunching. Unlike smaller 20x20 mats, you're not constantly repositioning.

## Where It Falls Short

Be clear-eyed about the limitations. These are **not** luxurious bedding. The fleece is thinner than a sherpa-lined throw — maybe 3mm thick. For a short-haired dog in a cold garage, this alone won't cut it in winter. You'd need to layer it or pair it with a padded bed.

The edges are raw-cut. They **won't fray** — fleece doesn't unravel — but they *will* curl after repeated washes. It's cosmetic, not structural, but if you care about crisp edges, that's a minor annoyance.

Some buyers report the colors run slightly in the first wash. Wash with like colors initially. And if your pet is a dedicated chewer, these won't survive — the fleece is snag-resistant, not puncture-proof.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Multi-pet households** that need coverage on every surface
- **Foster/rescue owners** who cycle through dogs and need cheap, sanitizable bedding
- **Crate training** — having spares means you can wash one while another is in use
- **Car owners** who want disposable seat protection for muddy or shedding dogs

**Skip it if** you want a single premium orthopedic mat, or if your pet needs heavy winter insulation. This is a utility product, not a luxury one.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these blankets true to the 30x40 inch size?**
A: Yes, within about 1 inch. They're not pre-shrunk, but washing on cold and air-drying keeps them true to size.

**Q: Can I use these for cats or small dogs?**
A: Absolutely. The 30x40 size folds down easily for smaller pets, and the fleece is soft enough for sensitive paws. Many cat owners report their cats knead and sleep on them happily.

**Q: How many wash cycles before they wear out?**
A: Based on reviews and testing, expect 20–30 machine washes before noticeable thinning. The edges curl earlier (around wash 5–7), but the fabric integrity holds.
