**The Rywell PETMAT0007 is a solid mid-tier pet mat that solves the "my dog slides across the kitchen floor" problem — but it's not the plush cloud bed the marketing photos suggest.**

If you've ever watched your dog's legs splay out on laminate flooring, you already know why a grippy mat matters. The Rywell PETMAT0007 is aimed squarely at that scenario. With a 4.3-star average across 215 ratings, it's clearly doing something right for most owners. But as with any pet product, the real question is whether it holds up to your specific dog's chaos.

**What you actually get**

The package contains just the mat itself — no inserts, no carrying case, no extras. It arrives rolled up, which means you'll want to give it a day to flatten out completely. As shown in the product image, it's a single-piece mat with a textured top surface and a rubberized bottom layer. The dimensions are generous enough for medium breeds, though large dogs will find it snug rather than sprawling. There's no thickness spec printed on the box, but owners consistently describe it as "about a quarter-inch thick" — closer to a rug than a mattress.

**Real-world performance**

The standout feature is undeniably the non-slip backing. Multiple verified buyers mention their dogs sprinting across the mat without budging it an inch. One reviewer put it simply: "My greyhound used to slide into the wall when he turned too fast. This mat ended that entirely." That's meaningful feedback from a breed notoriously prone to flooring-related injuries.

The water-resistant top layer earns its keep too. Accidents, slobber, and post-walk muddy paws all wipe clean with a damp cloth. For the deep-clean crowd, owners report the mat survives machine washing without curling at the edges or losing its grip. There are a few reports of slight shrinkage after hot-water washes, so stick to cold cycles if you want it to stay true to size.

**What owners consistently praise**

Beyond the grip, the most recurring positive thread in the reviews is durability. Owners with puppies and destructive chewers note the mat doesn't shred or fray at the edges — a common failure point on cheaper mats. The material also holds up to regular claw contact without visible wear marks.

The low-profile design gets a lot of love too. It doesn't look like a dog bed, which means it blends into living rooms without announcing "pet owner lives here." If aesthetics matter to you, that's a genuine selling point.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. The most common complaint is the factory smell. Several reviewers describe a strong chemical odor that lingers for several days after unboxing. It does fade, but if you're sensitive to smells or have a dog that likes to sniff everything obsessively, plan to air it out before letting them claim it.

More importantly, the padding is thin. If you're shopping for a senior dog with arthritis or hip dysplasia, this isn't the product for you. It provides essentially zero cushioning against hard floors. It's a grip mat, not an orthopedic bed. One owner put it bluntly: "Great for stopping the slide, but my 12-year-old lab still prefers her actual bed for sleeping."

There are also scattered complaints about the size running smaller than expected. No official dimensions are listed on the product page, which is frustrating. Measure your space before ordering, and if you're between sizes, size up.

**Who should buy this**

This mat is ideal for active dogs who need traction during zoomies, for protecting floors from water bowls and food spills, or for renters who want to protect their security deposit from scratched floors. It's also a smart pick for pet owners who need something easy to clean and don't want the bulk of a traditional bed.

Skip it if your dog needs joint support, if you have a giant breed that needs serious floor coverage, or if you're sensitive to initial off-gassing smells.

**FAQ**

*Is the Rywell PETMAT0007 machine washable?*
Yes, but use cold water and air dry. Hot cycles risk minor shrinkage.

*Will this work on carpet?*
No. The non-slip backing is designed for hard surfaces. On carpet, it will slide just like any other mat.

*Is it safe for puppies who chew?*
The material resists shredding, but no mat is indestructible. Supervise heavy chewers until you know how they'll treat it.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need reliable non-slip traction for an active dog on hard floors, or you want a low-maintenance mat that's easy to wipe down and machine wash.

**Skip it if** you're looking for orthopedic cushioning or a plush bed — this is a functional mat, not a comfort product.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — It does exactly what it claims, with minor tradeoffs on smell and padding that keep it from being perfect.
