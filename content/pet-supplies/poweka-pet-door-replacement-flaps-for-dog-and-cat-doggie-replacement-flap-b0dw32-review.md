If your pet door flap has turned into a stiff, cracked, draft-letting slab of plastic, you don't need a whole new door. You need a replacement flap — and the Poweka Pet Door Replacement Flaps are the kind of product that makes you wonder why you didn't swap it out months ago.

**What You Actually Get**

The package contains a single replacement flap made from clear, flexible vinyl. It's designed to work with most standard pet doors, including popular models from PetSafe, Ideal Pet Products, and similar brands. The flap features a magnetic strip running down the center seam to help it close quietly and seal against drafts. As shown in the product image, the edges are reinforced — this isn't a flimsy sheet of plastic that'll curl after a week.

**Real-World Performance**

The vinyl has enough give that a determined 70-pound Labrador can push through without hesitation, yet it springs back to create a decent seal. That balance is the hardest part of any pet door flap, and Poweka gets it right. The magnetic closure is strong enough to block wind but not so aggressive that small cats or elderly dogs get stuck halfway through.

One thing worth noting: this is a replacement flap, not a full pet door kit. You're getting the flap itself — no frame, no mounting screws, no templates. If your existing door frame is in good shape, that's fine. If you're starting from scratch, this isn't the product for you.

**Where It Falls Short**

The biggest complaint is sizing. While the product claims universal compatibility, several reviewers found the flap ran slightly narrower than their existing frame. One customer wrote, "I measured twice and still had to trim the edges. It works, but it wasn't the drop-in replacement I expected." If your pet door is an older or less common model, measure the opening carefully before ordering.

There's also no hardware included. If you need new screws or a fresh frame seal, you'll have to source those separately. And while the vinyl is durable, it's not indestructible — a determined chewer could still damage it over time.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is ideal for anyone whose existing pet door is structurally sound but has a worn-out flap. It's also a smart choice for renters who installed a pet door and need to restore the original flap before moving out. If you have a standard brand door — think PetSafe or similar — this will likely save you 30-50% compared to buying the manufacturer's replacement part.

Skip it if your pet door frame itself is damaged, warped, or missing pieces. You need a full door replacement in that case.

**FAQ**

*How do I know if this fits my pet door?*
Measure the width and height of your existing flap opening. Compare those dimensions to the product listing's specifications. If you're between sizes, size up — you can trim the edges if needed.

*Is this flap chew-proof?*
No. No pet door flap is truly chew-proof. The reinforced edges help, but a determined chewer will eventually damage any vinyl flap.

*Does the magnetic seal really block drafts?*
Yes, noticeably better than non-magnetic flaps. The seal isn't airtight, but it stops the majority of cold air infiltration.
