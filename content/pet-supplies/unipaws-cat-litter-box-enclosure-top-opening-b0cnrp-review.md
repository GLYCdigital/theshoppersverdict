If you’ve ever winced when guests walk past your cat’s litter box — or tripped over scattered clay pellets for the hundredth time — the Unipaws Cat Litter Box Enclosure is designed to solve both problems at once. It’s a piece of furniture that happens to conceal a toilet, and at a 4.1-star average across 204 ratings, most owners agree it does the job. But is it the right enclosure for *your* cat and *your* home? Let’s get specific.

**What you actually get**  
The box arrives flat-packed, and you’ll need about 20–30 minutes with a Phillips screwdriver to assemble it. The main cabinet is made from laminated MDF — not solid wood, but sturdy enough that it won’t wobble when your cat jumps on top (a favorite perch, apparently). Dimensions are roughly 22” L x 20” W x 22” H, with a top opening that fits most standard medium litter boxes up to about 19” long. You also get a charcoal filter pad that slides into a vent on the side, plus a clear plastic privacy flap for the front entrance.

**Performance and real-world use**  
The top-opening design is the standout feature here. Instead of bending down to scoop from the front, you lift the lid and reach straight down — easier on your back, and it keeps litter from spraying out into the room. Owners consistently mention this in reviews: *“I don’t have litter all over my floor anymore.”* The charcoal filter genuinely helps with odor between scooping, though it’s not a miracle worker — if you’re gone 10 hours, you’ll still smell it when you walk in.

The front flap is a double-edged sword. Cats that are skittish or senior may hesitate to push through it. One reviewer noted their 12-year-old cat refused to use it for three days until they removed the flap entirely. It’s detachable, so that’s a fixable issue, but worth knowing before you buy.

**What buyers consistently praise**  
- **Aesthetics**: “It actually looks like a side table, not a litter box.” This is the #1 reason people choose it over open trays or cheap hooded boxes.  
- **Scatter control**: The high walls and top entry mean less tracking and kicking.  
- **Durability**: Multiple long-term reviewers say the finish holds up to scratching and cleaning with mild soap.

**Where it falls short**  
The biggest complaint is size. The interior is snug — a large or long-bodied cat (think Maine Coon or a chunky tabby) will have trouble turning around. If your cat is over 15 pounds, measure your existing litter box before buying. Also, the lid is not hinged; it lifts off completely. That means you need a flat surface nearby to rest it on while scooping, and the lid can trap odors if you don’t air it out. A few reviewers also mentioned the assembly instructions are mostly diagrams with minimal text — manageable, but not foolproof.

**Who should buy this**  
- **Apartment dwellers** who want to hide the litter box in a living room or hallway without sacrificing style.  
- **Owners of small-to-medium cats** (under 15 lbs) who want easier scooping and less litter mess.  
- **People with limited floor space** — this has a small footprint and can double as a plant stand or side table.

**Skip it if**  
- You have a large breed cat (over 15 lbs) — look for a larger, front-entry enclosure.  
- Your cat is elderly or has mobility issues — the front flap and step-up entry may be a barrier.  
- You need a fully odor-proof solution — this reduces smell, but doesn’t eliminate it.

**FAQ**

*Does it fit a standard 20” litter box?*  
Yes, but barely. The interior is about 19.5” wide and 16” deep. If your box is larger than that, it won’t fit. Measure your current box first.

*Is the charcoal filter replaceable?*  
Yes, it’s a standard 5” x 7” pad. You can buy generic replacements on Amazon for about $10 for a 4-pack.

*Can the front flap be removed?*  
Yes, it snaps off in seconds. Many owners remove it permanently for shy cats or kittens.

**The Verdict**  
The Unipaws Cat Litter Box Enclosure is a solid, attractive solution for average-sized cats in homes where appearance matters. It’s not perfect — the size limits and non-hinged lid are real tradeoffs — but for the price, it beats most flimsy plastic cabinets.

**Buy it if** you want a good-looking, functional enclosure for a small-to-medium cat and don’t mind an extra step when scooping.  
**Skip it if** you have a big cat, a senior cat, or need absolute odor containment.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A smart buy for the right household, with honest limitations that won’t surprise you if you read the specs.
