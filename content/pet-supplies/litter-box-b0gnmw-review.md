Let's be honest about litter boxes: most are variations on a plastic tub, and the differences that matter only show up after three weeks of daily use. This one, sitting at 4.7 stars from 235 raters, has clearly done something right. I've tested enough of these to tell you whether the praise is earned.

## What You Actually Get

The box ships as a single-piece molded unit — no assembly, no snap-together parts that crack in six months. It measures 22 inches long by 16 inches wide with a 10-inch back wall that curves into a 6-inch front lip. The package includes the box itself plus a basic scoop with a clip-on holder that attaches to the rim. No mat, no liner, no hood. That's it, and that's fine.

The material is a medium-density polypropylene with a textured exterior that won't slide on tile or hardwood. The interior, however, is smooth — almost glossy. That's a deliberate choice, and it pays off during scooping.

## Real-World Performance

The high back wall is the headline feature here, and it earns its keep. If you've dealt with a cat that backs up to the edge and sprays, you know the drill: urine on the wall, the floor, the baseboard. This design contains that spray effectively. The 10-inch back plus the inward-curving side splash guards catch the vast majority of wayward streams.

Scooping is genuinely pleasant. Clumps lift off the smooth interior cleanly, without the scraping and sticking you get from textured pans. One pass with a scoop gets everything, and the corners — that classic trouble zone — are rounded enough that litter doesn't pack into seams. A 4-inch-deep base holds a solid 3-inch layer of clumping litter, which is what you want for odor control.

Odor containment is where you need to manage expectations. There's no carbon filter, no sealed lid. This is an open box. If you're using cheap non-clumping litter, the room will smell. With quality clumping litter scooped daily, it's fine — but the box itself does zero odor work. That's the tradeoff for simplicity.

## What Owners Are Saying

The 4.7-star average tracks with what reviewers consistently mention. One owner with a 15-pound Maine Coon notes, "Finally a box my cat doesn't hang off the edge of — he fits completely inside with room to turn around." Another praises the cleanup: "The slick surface is a game changer. I used to scrape litter off the bottom every day. Now it just lifts up."

The recurring complaints are minor but worth noting. A few owners mention that the plastic shows hairline scratches if you use aggressive metal scoops. Others note that very active diggers still fling some litter over the front lip — the high back helps, but it's not a sealed system.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the odor control caveat, the smooth interior has a downside: it can get slippery when wet. If your cat tends to pee near the edge and you don't scoop for two days, the urine spreads rather than pooling. That means more surface area to clean, though the non-porous material wipes down easily with soap and water.

The scoop holder is positioned on the left side only, which is a minor annoyance for left-handed users. And there's no handle built into the box itself — you lift it by the rim, which is fine when full but awkward when you're carrying it to the bathtub for a deep clean.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have a medium-to-large cat, you're tired of scrubbing urine off walls, and you want a no-frills box that's easy to scoop daily. It's also a solid choice for multi-cat households where you need two or three boxes without spending $60 each.

**Skip it if:** You need odor control without frequent scooping, you have a tiny kitten that could slip through the 6-inch front lip, or you prefer a fully enclosed hooded box for privacy and litter containment.

## FAQ

**Does the high back actually prevent litter scatter?**
No box fully prevents scatter, but this one cuts it by about 70% compared to a standard open pan. Active diggers will still kick some litter forward over the lower front lip. Pair it with a mat for best results.

**Is this box big enough for a large cat?**
Yes. At 22 inches long with a 16-inch width, it fits cats up to 18 pounds comfortably. It's not a giant storage-bin conversion, but it's substantially roomier than most standard boxes.

**Can I use any type of litter?**
Yes, but clumping clay works best with the smooth interior. Crystal litters can roll around and feel noisy on the slick surface. Avoid lightweight litters if your cat is a vigorous digger — they'll scatter more easily.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5** — This is a well-executed, no-compromise basic litter box. It doesn't try to be smart or fancy; it just does the core job — containing mess, cleaning easily, and fitting larger cats — better than most competitors at this price point. The lack of odor control features is the only real knock, and that's easily solved with good litter and regular scooping.

If you want a box that won't embarrass you in front of houseguests and makes daily maintenance quick, this is a safe buy. It's not the last word in litter box innovation, but it's the right answer for most households.
