## Where It Falls Short

The wheat gluten and pork liver powder are dealbreakers for sensitive dogs. If your pup has grain allergies or protein intolerances, you need to look elsewhere — full stop. This isn't an occasional sensitivity issue; it's a core ingredient.

Some dogs get bored. One frustrated owner noted, "My golden retriever loved these for two weeks, then started ignoring them entirely." If your dog is picky about repetitive treats, rotate these with other dental options.

The "medium" sizing is narrow. Dogs at the 25-30 pound end might struggle with the size, while 60+ pound dogs finish too quickly. Measure your dog's size honestly before buying.

## Who Should Buy This

These are ideal for owners with dogs 25-50 pounds who need consistent plaque control, particularly breeds prone to dental disease like greyhounds, whippets, and small terriers. They're also excellent for dogs who've had dental cleanings and need maintenance. If your dog has existing dental disease, these complement — not replace — veterinary treatment.

## FAQ

**How often should I give these?**
Daily is the standard recommendation. The enzyme system works cumulatively, so consistency beats intensity. Missing a day occasionally won't undo progress, but daily use is the sweet spot.

**Can these replace professional dental cleanings?**
No. They reduce plaque and tartar buildup significantly, but established tartar requires veterinary scaling. Think of these as preventive maintenance, not treatment.

**Are the enzymes safe for daily consumption?**
Yes. Glucose oxidase and lactoperoxidase are naturally occurring enzymes in saliva and milk. They're recognized as safe for oral use and have been studied extensively in veterinary dental care.
