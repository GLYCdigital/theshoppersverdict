Two crate pads for the price of one usually means you're getting two flimsy pieces of nothing. The Magik & Kover Dog Crate Pad 2 Pack doesn't quite fall into that trap, but it also isn't the orthopedic luxury liner some listings imply. Here's what 225 buyers at a 4.5-star average are actually getting.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get two identical crate mats with a plush faux-shearling top, a water-resistant inner liner, and a non-slip PVC-dotted bottom. The covers zip off completely, which matters more than you'd think — you can toss the whole thing in the washing machine without wrestling a giant pillow into a pillowcase.

The fill is a recycled polyester fiber, not memory foam. That distinction matters for anyone shopping for joint support.

## Real-World Performance

I've tested crate pads across every price tier, and the Magik & Kover holds up better than its price point suggests. The non-slip backing genuinely works — one buyer noted, "My dog spins in circles before settling, and this pad doesn't budge an inch on the plastic tray." That's a real complaint with cheaper mats that bunch into a corner by morning.

The water-resistant liner is a practical touch for puppies or senior dogs with accidents. It keeps moisture from soaking into the fiber fill, which is the main reason most crate pads end up smelling like a gym bag after three weeks.

Washing is straightforward: zip off the cover, machine wash cold, air dry or tumble low. Multiple buyers confirmed the cover holds up — "Washed it six times already and the stitching hasn't pulled" — which is more than I can say for several premium brands I've tested.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct: the "soft" in the product name is doing heavy lifting. At roughly 2 inches of compressed fiber fill, this pad is closer to a cushioned mat than a supportive bed. Large-breed dogs over 60 pounds will bottom out against the crate tray. One reviewer with a 75-pound Lab wrote, "She seems comfortable, but I wouldn't call it plush. It's more like a thick blanket than a bed."

The bigger issue is chew resistance. The plush fabric is soft and inviting — to your dog's teeth too. Multiple reviews mention destruction within the first week. If your dog is even a casual chewer, this pad is a budget gamble, not a solution. The 2-pack softens the blow when one gets shredded, but don't expect this to survive a determined destroyer.

Sizing runs slightly small. If your crate is 30 inches, order the 36-inch pad. Several buyers flagged this, and it's an easy fix at checkout.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Puppy owners** crate training — the 2-pack means a clean pad is always ready while the other washes
- **Multi-dog households** with two crates on a budget
- **Calm adult dogs** who don't chew and just need a softer surface than bare plastic

**Skip it if** you have a heavy chewer, a dog over 60 pounds needing joint support, or you expect orthopedic-grade cushioning.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this pad actually waterproof?**
A: The inner liner is water-resistant, not waterproof. It handles minor accidents and wet paws, but prolonged soaking will eventually seep through. For heavy incontinence, pair it with a waterproof crate liner underneath.

**Q: Can I wash the whole pad or just the cover?**
A: The cover zips off for machine washing. The inner fiber fill is not machine washable — spot clean it and air dry. Washing the fill directly will clump the fibers.

**Q: What crate sizes does the 2-pack fit?**
A: The listing offers multiple sizes, but order one size up from your crate dimensions. The pads run slightly small, and a snug fit prevents bunching.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a budget-friendly 2-pack that handles normal wear, washing cycles, and daily crate life without falling apart.

**Skip it if** your dog chews, needs real orthopedic support, or you're looking for a long-term investment bed.
