**The Vertical Stretch Your Cat Actually Wants — But Watch the Stability**

Most cat scratchers fail for one simple reason: they're too short. Cats don't just scratch — they dig their claws in and stretch their entire spine. That's why the SMARTBEAN Cardboard Cat Scratcher stands out. At 23.6 inches tall, it hits that sweet spot where a cat can fully extend without hunching. And at 4.3 stars across 215 ratings, it's clearly working for a lot of households.

**What You Actually Get**

The package contains one vertical scratching board, the base support, and a few assembly pieces. No tools required. You slide the board into the base, flip it upright, and you're done — most buyers report under two minutes from box to scratching. The board itself is dense corrugated cardboard, roughly the thickness of a standard scratching pad but oriented vertically. The base footprint is modest, so it won't dominate your living room, but that compactness comes with tradeoffs we'll get to shortly.

**Real-World Performance**

The corrugated texture is what sells this product. Cats instinctively prefer cardboard's give-and-resistance over carpet or sisal rope, and the vertical orientation matches how they naturally scratch door frames and couch arms. As shown in the product image, the board sits upright like a small pillar, inviting that full-body stretch. One verified buyer noted, "My cat ignored every flat scratcher I bought him, but he went after this within five minutes. It's the height."

The double-sided design doubles the lifespan — when one side gets frayed and shredded, you flip the board and get a fresh surface. For heavy scratchers, that's roughly two to three months per side before replacement, depending on claw enthusiasm.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The height and the cardboard quality come up repeatedly in positive reviews. "Finally a vertical scratch pad that doesn't require mounting to a wall," one reviewer wrote. Another mentioned, "The cardboard is much denser than the cheap ones on Amazon — it doesn't disintegrate in a week." Multiple buyers also appreciated that their cats stopped clawing furniture within days of introducing this board.

**Where It Falls Short**

Here's the honest part. The base is lightweight — there's no weighted plate or non-slip grip that anchors it firmly. Aggressive scratchers, especially 12+ pound cats, can shove this thing across the floor or knock it over mid-scratch. Several reviewers mentioned this exact issue. One wrote, "My cat is a maniac and tips it over every time. I had to put a dumbbell against the base." That's a fixable problem, but you shouldn't have to MacGyver your cat's scratcher.

Also, expect cardboard debris. It's inherent to any cardboard scratcher, but the vertical design means shreds scatter around the base rather than staying contained. Plan on sweeping every few days.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Apartment dwellers** with limited floor space who need a tall scratcher that doesn't require drilling into walls
- **Owners of cats that prefer vertical scratching** on door frames, chair backs, or couch arms
- **Budget-conscious shoppers** who want a functional scratcher under the cost of a sisal post tower

**Skip it if** you have a large, heavy cat that scratches with serious force — you'll need to add your own weight to the base. Also skip if you hate sweeping up cardboard bits.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does it last before needing replacement?**
A: With a single cat, expect 2–4 months per side. Flip the board when the surface looks frayed and shredded. Two sides means roughly 4–8 months total before replacing the board.

**Q: Is it really 23.6 inches, or is that an exaggeration?**
A: It's accurate. Measured from base to top, the board stands just over 23 inches. Tall enough for most cats to stretch fully. Exceptionally large Maine Coons might want something taller.

**Q: Can I mount it to the wall for stability?**
A: Not directly — there are no wall-mount holes or brackets. Some buyers have used heavy-duty double-sided tape or placed it against a wall with furniture anchoring it. But it's designed as a freestanding unit.

**The Verdict**

At the price point, the double-sided cardboard and 23.6-inch height deliver real value. **Skip it if** your cat is a heavy, aggressive scratcher or you want a zero-maintenance option — this one requires occasional sweeping and possible base weighting.
