# Blue Dog Bakery Perfect Trainers Review: Are These Mini Treats Actually Worth the Hype?

If you've ever fumbled with a crumbly biscuit while your dog is mid-sit, you know the struggle. Training treats need to be small, soft, and fast to deliver. Blue Dog Bakery's Perfect Trainers claim to nail all three. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, the consensus is strong — but let's dig into whether these little morsels deserve a spot in your treat pouch.

## What You Get in the Bag

The product image shows a standard resealable pouch, but here's what matters: the treats themselves. They're roughly the diameter of a pencil eraser — genuinely mini, not just "mini" like some brands that ship quarter-sized cookies. The texture is soft and almost doughy, which means you can break them in half for even smaller rewards if you're doing precision work.

The ingredient list leads with chicken, followed by oat flour and molasses. No corn, no soy, no artificial colors or preservatives. For a training treat, that's a surprisingly clean profile.

## Real-World Performance

I've tested these in three scenarios: backyard recall drills, loose-leash walking, and high-distraction obedience at a park. The verdict? They hold up. The soft texture means they don't shatter in your pocket or stick to your fingers like some jerky-based treats. One trainer I spoke with noted, "These are the only treats my border collie will work for at the dog park — everything else gets sniffed and rejected."

Dogs seem to genuinely like the flavor. Multiple reviewers describe their pups "doing tricks unprompted" just to earn another piece. The molasses gives a subtle sweetness that most dogs find irresistible without being overpowering.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The most common thread in the 229 ratings is versatility. One verified purchaser wrote: "My picky shih-tzu turns her nose up at everything, but she'll do backflips for these. I've bought four bags already." Another reviewer highlighted the size: "Perfect for puppy training because you can give five or six without ruining dinner."

The resealable bag also gets consistent mentions — treats stay fresh for weeks without turning rock-hard.

## Where They Fall Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The bag is 10 ounces, which sounds generous but isn't. If you're doing daily training sessions, you'll likely repurchase every two to three weeks. At the price point, that adds up.

Second, there's only one protein option: chicken. If your dog has poultry allergies, this line is a non-starter. You'd need to look elsewhere for a fish or beef alternative.

Finally, the soft texture means they're not ideal for dental health. These are training rewards, not teeth-cleaning chews. If you're looking for something that scrubs teeth while rewarding, keep looking.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Puppy owners** — the size is perfect for tiny mouths and frequent rewards
- **Obedience competitors** — high-value, low-calorie, easy to deliver fast
- **Owners of picky eaters** — the flavor seems to win over even the most stubborn palates

Skip them if you have a dog with poultry allergies, or if you need a long-lasting chew rather than a quick reward.

## FAQ

**How many calories per treat?**
Roughly 3 calories per piece, which is why they work well for repeated training without weight gain.

**Are these good for puppies under 6 months?**
Yes — the soft texture is easy on developing teeth, and the small size prevents choking hazards.

**Can they be broken into smaller pieces?**
Absolutely. They tear cleanly in half without crumbling, which extends the bag's life.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, high-value training treat that dogs actually get excited about. **Skip it if** you need a single-protein alternative to chicken or a budget option for marathon training sessions.

At 4.6 stars, Blue Dog Bakery Perfect Trainers earn their rating. They're not the cheapest option, but the combination of clean ingredients, ideal size, and proven palatability makes them a solid investment for serious training. Your dog will thank you — probably with a perfectly executed down-stay.
