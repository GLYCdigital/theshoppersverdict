**Birdie & Louie Wet Cat Food Variety Pack Review: Is This Tuna Duo Worth the Hype?**

Let's cut to the chase: your cat is a picky dictator, and you're tired of wasting money on cases of food they sniff once and walk away from. The Birdie & Louie Tuna Tuesday/Seafood Sunday variety pack tries to solve that problem by giving you two flavors in one 12-can box. After digging through 204 ratings (4.1 stars), here's what actually matters.

**What You Get**

The box contains 12 cans — six Tuna Tuesday and six Seafood Sunday. Each can is 3 ounces, which is the standard single-serving size for most adult cats. The packaging is clean and modern, and the cans have easy-pull tabs that don't require a Herculean grip (a small detail, but arthritis-friendly).

**Texture and Ingredients: The Real Story**

As shown in the product image, this is a flaked pâté — not a smooth mousse, not shredded chunks in gravy. The first ingredient on both recipes is real tuna, followed by fish broth. That's a good sign; you're not paying for "meat by-products" or fillers like corn and wheat. It's also grain-free, which makes it a reasonable option if your cat has a sensitive stomach.

The moisture content sits around 78%, which is solid for urinary tract health. If your vet has ever told you to increase your cat's water intake, wet food like this is the easiest way to do it.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The most common thread in positive reviews is that *finicky cats* actually eat it. One reviewer wrote, "My cat is the pickiest eater I've ever owned — she turned her nose up at Fancy Feast and Tiki Cat, but she licks the bowl clean with this." Another mentioned, "I love that it's made with real tuna and no weird fillers. My cat's coat looks shinier after two weeks."

Owners of senior cats also reported success — the soft texture is easy to chew for cats with dental issues.

**Where It Falls Short**

The honesty part. The fishy smell is *strong*. If you're sensitive to seafood odors, this will linger in your kitchen long after your cat finishes eating. One reviewer noted, "My cat loves it, but I have to air out the kitchen afterward."

Texture is the second sticking point. The flaked pâté isn't for every cat. If yours prefers smooth pâté or chunky cuts in gravy, they may reject this. Several reviews mentioned their cats only ate one of the two flavors — which is exactly why the variety pack is the smart first purchase rather than committing to a full case of one flavor.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The picky-cat owner**: If you've cycled through five brands this month, this variety pack is a low-risk test.
- **The senior-cat caregiver**: Soft texture, high moisture, easy to chew.
- **The ingredient-conscious shopper**: First ingredient is real tuna, grain-free, no by-products.

**Skip it if** your cat refuses anything that isn't smooth mousse, or if you're extremely sensitive to fish smells.

**Q: Is this suitable for kittens?**
A: It's labeled for adult maintenance. For kittens, you'll want a growth formula. Check with your vet before switching.
