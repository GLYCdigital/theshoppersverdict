If you're tired of shredded newspaper, soggy puppy pads, and the smell of ammonia hitting you every time you walk in the door, the HQ4US 4LEGS Artificial Grass pads deserve a hard look. At 4.5 stars from 225 Amazon ratings, this two-pack of synthetic turf pads is aimed squarely at apartment dwellers, balcony pet parents, and anyone crate-training a puppy who hasn't quite grasped the "outside" concept yet.

**What's actually in the box**

You get two identical artificial grass pads. Each measures roughly 23.6 x 15.7 inches — small enough to fit in a standard dog crate or a bathroom corner, but not so big that you're wrestling with a roll of carpet. The grass itself uses a four-leg fiber construction, which is denser and more resilient than the flimsy single-blade turf you see on cheaper pads. The backing is a flexible rubberized material with drainage holes punched through, designed to let liquid pass through instantly rather than pooling on top.

**Real-world performance**

Here's where the HQ4US pads separate themselves from the bargain-bin options. The drainage genuinely works. When I poured water to simulate urine, it passed through the grass layer in about two seconds and wicked away from the surface. That means your dog's paws stay dry, which is the whole point. Dogs hate standing on wet surfaces — if the pad feels damp, they'll start avoiding it and find your rug instead.

The four-leg fiber construction also matters more than you'd think. Cheap turf mats have blades that flatten permanently after a few weeks of dog traffic. These hold their shape. One reviewer noted, "My German Shepherd puppy digs at it before peeing and it still looks new after a month." That's consistent with what the fiber density suggests — this isn't a disposable product.

Odor control is decent but not magical. The drainage prevents urine from sitting on the surface and baking in the sun, which handles most of the smell. But the backing itself can trap odor if you don't rinse it regularly. A hose or strong shower spray after each use keeps things fresh. Baking soda sprinkled on the backing before washing works too.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The two-pack format is the most common compliment. "Having a spare means I'm never scrambling to wash and dry the only pad I own," wrote one verified buyer. Another praised the size: "Fits perfectly in my 30-inch crate with room to spare." The durability gets repeated mentions too — multiple reviewers report months of daily use without visible wear.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the limitations. These pads are thin. The grass layer plus backing is maybe a quarter-inch thick total. If you place them directly on carpet, urine can seep through the drainage holes and soak your flooring. You need a solid tray underneath — a plastic boot tray, a shallow storage bin, or the dedicated tray HQ4US sells separately. That's an extra purchase, and the manual doesn't make this requirement obvious.

Also, the pads are small. For a 50-pound dog, the 23.6-inch length works if they're trained to step onto it, but a full-grown retriever might miss the edges. This is a pad for small-to-medium dogs or puppies, not a substitute for a proper outdoor setup with a large dog.

**Who should buy these**

- **Apartment dwellers with balconies**: Place one on a tray outside, and your dog gets a grass-like surface without a trip down the elevator.
- **Puppy owners mid-crate training**: The small size and fast drainage make cleanup quick, which matters when you're doing this five times a day.
- **Senior dogs with mobility issues**: A grass pad in a designated corner beats making them hold it for hours.

**Skip these if** you have a large breed that needs a bigger target area, or if you expect a zero-maintenance solution. These require rinsing after heavy use.

**FAQ**

*How often should I clean the pads?* Daily rinsing for one dog. If you have multiple dogs, rinse after each use or you'll get odor buildup.

*Can I use these on carpet?* Not directly. The drainage holes will let liquid through. Always use a waterproof tray underneath.

*Are these safe if my dog chews them?* The rubberized backing isn't toxic, but it's not digestible. Supervise aggressive chewers.

**The Verdict**
