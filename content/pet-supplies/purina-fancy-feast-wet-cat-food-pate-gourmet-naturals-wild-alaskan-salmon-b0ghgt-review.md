If your cat turns up its nose at the endless parade of "natural" foods that taste like cardboard, this Purina Fancy Feast Gourmet Naturals Pate in Wild Alaskan Salmon might be the peace treaty you've been negotiating. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, it's not just another shelf-filler — it's a consistent performer that earns its spot in the rotation.

**What's actually in the can**

The Gourmet Naturals line strips away the artificial stuff. The primary protein is real Wild Alaskan salmon — not fish meal, not "salmon by-product," but actual salmon. The ingredient list stays tight: salmon, fish broth, liver, and essential vitamins and minerals. No corn, wheat, soy, or artificial colors. As one reviewer put it, "My 14-year-old rescue who refuses everything except rotisserie chicken actually finishes this in one sitting."

You get the standard 3-ounce can, which works for most adult cats as a single meal or two smaller feedings. The pate is dense and smooth — it holds its shape when scooped but breaks apart easily with a fork. For cats that need extra hydration, this texture blends seamlessly with warm water to create a soupy consistency that encourages drinking.

**Real-world performance**

The moisture content sits around 78%, which is excellent for cats prone to urinary issues or those that simply don't visit the water bowl enough. The smell, however, is unmistakably fishy — stronger than some competing salmon recipes. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you're sensitive to pet food odors, you'll notice it lingering in the bowl area for a few minutes after feeding.

Texture consistency is generally reliable, though a handful of reviewers noted occasional variance between cans. One buyer mentioned, "Two cans in a case were noticeably drier and crumbly — my cat still ate them, but the fresh ones were clearly preferred." This isn't a quality-control crisis, but worth knowing if your cat is a texture snob.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The picky-eater success stories dominate the reviews. Cats that "won't eat anything" suddenly clean their bowls. Seniors with dental issues manage the pate easily. Multi-cat households report it works as a reliable base that can be topped with dry food or supplements. The lack of carrageenan — a common thickener that some owners avoid — also earns points among ingredient-conscious buyers.

**Where it falls short**

The salmon-only protein source means this won't work for cats with fish sensitivities. And while the price point is reasonable for the quality, it's not the cheapest option in the Fancy Feast lineup — you're paying a slight premium for the natural ingredients and single-source protein. Some reviewers also noted the cans arrive occasionally dented from shipping, though none reported spoiled product as a result.
