**What You’re Actually Getting**

**Where It Falls Short — Be Honest With Yourself**  

First, the size. If you have a Great Dane or a Rottweiler, these are essentially single-bite snacks. A 20-ounce bag lasted my lab five days. That’s a real cost consideration if you’re buying for a large breed.  

Second, there’s no grain-free version. The oat and barley base is fine for most dogs, but if your vet has confirmed a wheat or grain intolerance, skip these. Don’t gamble on “it might be okay” — it won’t be.  

Third, the price. These are mid-range to premium per ounce. You’re paying for the small-batch, USA-made process. If you’re on a strict budget, store-brand biscuits will cost half as much — but you’ll get half the ingredient quality.
