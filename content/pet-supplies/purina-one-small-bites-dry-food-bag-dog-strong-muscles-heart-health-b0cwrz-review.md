## What's Actually in the Bag

You're getting a dry kibble with real chicken listed as the first ingredient. That's a meaningful starting point. The formula delivers 26% protein, which supports lean muscle maintenance without blowing past what most adult dogs need. The "strong muscles" claim isn't just tagline fluff—it's backed by a clinically proven antioxidant blend that includes vitamin E and selenium.

For heart health, Purina One incorporates natural sources of taurine. That's important because taurine deficiency has been linked to dilated cardiomyopathy (DCM) in dogs, particularly in breeds like Golden Retrievers and Newfoundlands.

The small bite size is the differentiator here. At roughly 8mm, these kibbles are noticeably smaller than standard formulas. For a 12-pound Chihuahua or a 14-year-old Lab with worn teeth, that difference matters far more than most owners realize.

This appears to be a seller issue rather than a Purina manufacturing problem, but it's frustrating enough that several reviewers mentioned it directly.

## Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Is this good for puppies?**
Not specifically. This formula is designed for adult maintenance. Purina One has separate puppy formulas with different calcium-to-phosphorus ratios. Stick to those until your dog hits about 12 months.

**Q: My dog has a chicken allergy — is this safe?**
No. Chicken is the primary protein source throughout. Look for Purina One's salmon or lamb varieties instead.

**Q: How quickly should I transition?**
Mix 25% new food with 75% old for two days, then 50/50 for two days, then 75/25 for two days. Full switch by day seven. This reduces digestive upset significantly.
