# Icelandic+ Fish Chips: Cod Review — Are These Single-Ingredient Treats Worth It?

If your dog turns up their nose at processed chicken jerky loaded with glycerin and artificial smoke flavor, Icelandic+ Fish Chips might be the reset button their palate needs. These little squares are exactly one thing: wild-caught cod, freeze-dried into crunchy chips. That's it. No wheat, no corn, no soy, no preservatives, no vague "meat by-products." As shown in the product image, each 2.5oz bag contains small, uniform chips that look almost like pale golden crackers.

## What You Actually Get

The 2.5oz bag is compact — roughly the size of a standard chip bag you'd pack in a lunchbox. Inside are freeze-dried cod chips that measure about an inch across. The texture is dry and brittle, which is actually a feature: you can snap each chip into smaller pieces without any mess or crumbling dust. The bag itself is resealable, though with 229 Amazon ratings averaging 4.6 stars, most buyers report the resealable top holding up well through daily use.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The freeze-drying process preserves the cod's natural nutrients while creating a shelf-stable treat that doesn't require refrigeration. The chips deliver a satisfying crunch that most dogs seem to love — one reviewer noted, "My picky corgi who spits out most treats devoured these in seconds." The fish flavor is present but not overwhelming; multiple buyers specifically mention that these don't leave that lingering "fishy breath" that plagues many salmon-based treats.

The single-ingredient nature makes them an option for dogs with sensitive stomachs or food allergies. Since cod is a lean, easily digestible protein, these work well as a high-value training reward. The low calorie count per chip means you can reward generously without blowing your dog's daily caloric budget.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The most repeated theme in reviews is the ingredient transparency. One verified purchase put it simply: "Finally a treat I can read the label on and understand every word." Another owner of a dog with pancreatitis mentioned these were one of the few treats their vet approved. Dogs on elimination diets for allergies also pop up frequently in reviews, with owners grateful for a treat that won't trigger flare-ups.

The low odor is another consistent point of praise. If you've ever carried salmon treats in your pocket, you know the struggle — these chips reportedly stay neutral-smelling even after being handled.

## Where It Falls Short

Be honest with yourself about portion size: 2.5 ounces is not a lot. For a medium or large dog, you're looking at maybe a week and a half of daily training treats. The price per ounce runs higher than typical grocery-store jerky, which surprises some first-time buyers. If you have a large breed or a treat-motivated dog, budget accordingly.

The crunch is also a double-edged sword. While most dogs handle it fine, owners of puppies under six months or seniors with dental problems report needing to soak the chips in warm water to soften them first. That's a minor inconvenience, but worth knowing before you buy.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Owners of picky eaters** who've tried everything short of cooking fresh fish
- **Dogs with food allergies or sensitive stomachs** — this is about as clean as a treat gets
- **Training-focused owners** who need a high-value, low-calorie reward
- **Anyone who hates fishy-smelling treats** — these are remarkably neutral

Skip them if you have a dog with severe dental disease or if you need a budget-friendly bulk option for a large multi-dog household.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these be used as a meal topper?**  
A: Yes, though they're designed as treats. Crush them over kibble to add protein and encourage picky eaters. The freeze-dried texture rehydrates slightly when mixed with wet food.

**Q: Are these safe for puppies?**  
A: Generally yes, but the hard crunch may be tough on very young puppies. Soak in warm water for 30 seconds to soften before offering.

**Q: How long does one bag last?**  
A: For a 25-pound dog getting a few chips per day, expect 7-10 days. For training-heavy sessions, closer to 4-5 days.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you value single-ingredient transparency, need a low-odor fish treat, or have an allergy-prone dog that deserves a clean reward. The quality justifies the premium price.

**Skip it if** you need a budget bulk option, have a dog with dental problems, or prefer chewy treats over crunchy ones.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A genuinely clean, well-made treat that delivers on its promises. The only real drawback is the small bag size relative to price.
