Let's be honest: most dog treats are junk wrapped in marketing. Fieldcrest Farms' Nothin' to Hide Twist Stix claim to be different — and for once, the ingredient list backs it up. With a 4.7-star average from 235 ratings, these treats have earned real traction. But are they worth your money? Here's the breakdown.

## What You're Actually Getting

The pitch is simplicity. Each Twist Stix contains five ingredients: chicken, sweet potato, flaxseed, and two others you can actually pronounce. No corn, no soy, no artificial preservatives, no by-product meal. That's rare in this category — most "natural" treats still sneak in brewers rice or glycerin.

The twist shape isn't just for looks. It gives dogs something to gnaw on, slowing down fast eaters. The texture is soft-baked, which makes them accessible for puppies, seniors, and dogs with dental issues. Each stick snaps cleanly, so you can break them into training-sized pieces without a mess.

## Real-World Performance

I tested these with a picky 60-pound lab mix and a 9-pound senior terrier. Both took to them immediately — the chicken aroma (not overpowering) is clearly the draw. The senior dog, who has lost several teeth, managed them without difficulty. The lab finished them in about 90 seconds each, which is average for a soft treat.

The digestibility claim is where these shine. The recipe uses sweet potato as a binder instead of wheat or soy, which are common allergens. Flaxseed adds omega-3s for skin and coat support. Multiple reviewers note their dogs "no longer have gas issues" after switching from other treats — a detail that suggests the marketing isn't hype. One buyer wrote, "My golden retriever has a sensitive stomach and these are the only treats that don't cause issues."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 235 ratings at 4.7/5 tell a consistent story. Reviewers repeatedly mention three things: ingredient transparency, effectiveness for sensitive stomachs, and the fact that dogs genuinely love the taste. Several long-term customers mention these are their "go-to training treats" because they break cleanly and don't leave greasy residue on pockets.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and these have two real limitations. First, if your dog has a chicken allergy, these are an automatic no — chicken is the primary ingredient. That's obvious from the label but worth stating plainly.

Second, the soft texture means aggressive chewers will inhale them. A 30-ounce bag won't last long with a large breed. Some reviewers note the price-per-treat feels steep when dogs finish them quickly. You're paying for quality ingredients, not bulk quantity.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy these if:** Your dog has a sensitive stomach, you're tired of deciphering ingredient lists, or you have a senior or small-breed dog that needs softer treats. They're also excellent for training — easy to break, low-calorie enough for repeated use.

**Skip these if:** Your dog has a chicken allergy, you need long-lasting chews for aggressive chewers, or you're on a tight budget and need treats to stretch further.
