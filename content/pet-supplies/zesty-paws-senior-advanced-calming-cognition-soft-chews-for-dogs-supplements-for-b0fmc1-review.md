## What you actually get

The jar contains 90 soft chews (about a 45-day supply at the standard two-chew daily dose for dogs over 25 pounds). Each chew is a pumpkin-colored, bite-sized square with a palatable chicken-liver flavor that dogs generally take willingly — a big deal when you're medicating a picky senior.

As shown in the product image, the formula combines three active ingredients you don't see in typical calming treats: **Suntheanine** (a patented form of L-theanine, the same compound studied in human anxiety research), **Sensoril ashwagandha** (an adaptogen shown to lower cortisol), and **Cognitune** (a phosphatidylserine complex aimed at memory and cognitive function in aging brains). This is a cognition-focused formula, not just a sedative.

If your dog's anxiety is severe (separation anxiety with destructive behavior, panic-level storm phobia), a supplement alone likely won't fix it — you need behavioral work or veterinary medication.

The chews are still usable if you break them apart, but it's a quality-control issue Amazon reviewers flagged consistently enough to mention.

Finally, the price. This is not a budget product. You're paying roughly $1 per two-chew dose — more than generic calming treats. The senior-specific ingredients justify a premium, but if your goal is just "calm my dog during fireworks," cheaper options exist.

## FAQ

**How long until I see results?**
Most buyers report noticeable calming within 1–2 weeks, with cognitive benefits (less confusion, better sleep cycles) taking 3–4 weeks. The ingredients work cumulatively, not like a sedative.

**Can I give it with other medications?**
The formula contains no known major drug interactions, but ashwagandha can affect thyroid and blood pressure medications. If your senior is on prescription meds, run it past your vet first.

**What's the dosage for small dogs?**
The label recommends one chew daily for dogs under 25 pounds, two for larger dogs. Don't double up without veterinary guidance — the ashwagandha dose matters.
