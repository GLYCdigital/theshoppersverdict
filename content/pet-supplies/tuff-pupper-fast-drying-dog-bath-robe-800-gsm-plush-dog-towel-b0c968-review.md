## Where It Falls Short

The biggest practical issue is drying time between uses. At 800 GSM, this robe is thick — really thick. After wringing it out following a bath, it takes a full day to dry completely in normal indoor conditions. If you bathe your dog weekly, that's fine. If you have two water-loving dogs who need baths back-to-back, you'll need to either own two robes or accept a damp second round.

Sizing is the other trap. The size chart is based on back length, but the real constraint is chest girth. Deep-chested breeds — think Greyhounds, Danes, or athletic mixes — may find the chest panel snug even when the back length matches. Measure your dog's chest at the widest point and size up if you're between sizes.
