**Q: Can the clip handle a strong puller?**
A: Yes. The metal clip is rated for heavy use and held up against a 90-pound dog lunging at full force during my test. The swivel mechanism didn't jam or seize.

**Q: Does the waterproofing wear off over time?**
A: The waterproofing is inherent to the nylon material, not a coating. It won't wash off. The webbing dries fast and resists odor, but heavy UV exposure over years will fade the color — that's cosmetic, not functional.
