**Build quality and real-world performance**

The defining feature here is cardboard density. Cats that dig in with full force will still generate debris — that's physics — but the pieces are smaller and less prone to scattering across the room.

During testing with a 12-pound Maine Coon mix that goes through standard pads in under two weeks, these held structural integrity for nearly a month of daily use. If you're looking for a zero-debris solution, this isn't it — it's just less messy than the alternatives.
