Let me be blunt: most cages in this price range are flimsy wire boxes that bend during assembly and wobble once your ferret decides the third level is a launchpad. The HABUTWAY Metal Large Ferret & Chinchilla Cage isn't that — but it's also not a custom-built critter mansion. After digging through 200 ratings and the spec sheet, here's the real story.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the multi-level wire frame, two plastic ramps, a deep plastic base pan, and the hardware kit. Assembled, it measures roughly 30.7 inches long, 18.5 inches wide, and 35.8 inches tall. The 0.5-inch wire spacing is the key spec — tight enough that a determined baby chinchilla or rat can't squeeze through, but open enough for airflow. That's the sweet spot for small-to-medium critters.

You're getting three access points: two full-width front doors and one top door. This isn't a gimmick. When you're cleaning a ferret cage every few days, being able to reach the back corners without contorting your arm is a genuine quality-of-life win.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The frame is powder-coated metal that holds up reasonably well against the inevitable urine spray and chewing attempts. The base pan deserves specific praise — it's thick plastic with 4-inch walls, which means bedding stays inside instead of migrating across your floor. Multiple buyers noted this specifically. One reviewer put it plainly: "The deep tray is the reason I bought this over cheaper options. My ferret is a digger and this contains the mess."

The double doors are a standout for cleaning. You can open both front panels fully, giving you unobstructed access rather than the awkward one-hand-through-a-hole approach cheaper cages force.

**Where it falls short**

The included plastic ramps are the weak link. They work fine for young, lightweight animals, but heavier adult ferrets cause them to flex noticeably. If you're housing a senior ferret or a particularly chunky chinchilla, plan on replacing the ramps with solid wooden shelves or PVC pipe perches.

Assembly is a real project. Budget 45–60 minutes with a Phillips screwdriver. The instructions are illustrated but occasionally vague about which screw goes where — you'll likely re-do at least one panel. Not a dealbreaker, but don't plan on setting this up while your pet waits impatiently in the carrier.

The wheels are functional but small. On carpet or uneven floors, the cage can wobble when your pet is sprinting between levels. It's not dangerous, but it's noticeable.

**Who should buy this**

This cage suits owners of one or two ferrets, rats, or chinchillas who want a multi-level setup without paying boutique prices. It's particularly good if you're a first-time exotic pet owner — the deep tray and easy-access doors forgive the inevitable learning curve of cleaning routines.

Skip it if you have a large male ferret over two pounds (ramp flex becomes a real issue), or if you need a cage for a pregnant female who'll need a solid-floor maternity setup. For those cases, spend more on a cage with metal or wood platforms throughout.

**FAQ**

**Is the wire spacing safe for baby chinchillas?**
Yes. The 0.5-inch spacing is tight enough to prevent escapes from kits and young juveniles. Just check the tray seam periodically — that's the most common escape route if the pan shifts during cleaning.

**Can this cage hold two adult ferrets comfortably?**
It's workable for two, but tight. Each ferret needs roughly 2 square feet of floor space, and the multi-level design compensates for the modest footprint. Three would be overcrowded.

**How do I clean the base tray?**
The pan lifts out completely once you open the front doors. Slide it out, dump bedding, wash with mild soap and water, dry fully before replacing. The 4-inch walls prevent most spillage during removal.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a functional, accessible multi-level cage for small-to-medium critters without paying premium boutique prices. The deep tray and full-front-door access make daily maintenance genuinely easier than comparable cages.

**Skip it if:** You have heavy adult ferrets (ramp flex is a real concern), need a solid-floor setup for breeding, or want a cage that assembles in under 30 minutes.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, honest cage that gets the fundamentals right. The ramps and assembly friction hold it back from greatness, but for the price, it's a smart purchase for most small-pet owners.
