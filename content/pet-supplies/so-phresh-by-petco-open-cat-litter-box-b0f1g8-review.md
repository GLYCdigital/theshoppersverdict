The So Phresh Open Cat Litter Box from Petco doesn't look revolutionary. It's a rectangular plastic pan with a high back and a cut-out front. But in the pet supply world, boring often means *reliable* — and with 235 ratings averaging 4.7 stars, this box has clearly earned its place in thousands of homes. The question is whether it earns a spot in yours.

## What You're Actually Getting

This is a standard open-top litter box, not a hooded enclosure or automatic unit. The dimensions run roughly 20 inches long by 15 inches wide, with the back wall standing about 8 inches tall and the front lip sitting around 3 inches high. The plastic is noticeably thicker than the flimsy trays you get at dollar stores — think sturdy storage tote, not disposable takeout container.

There's no lid, no scoop, no liner included. You're paying for the box itself, and that's it.

## Real-World Performance

The high back wall is the standout feature here. As shown in the product image, that elevated rear panel catches most of the urine splash and litter spray that happens when cats dig with enthusiasm. If you've ever scraped dried pee off your baseboard, you understand the value of this design immediately.

The low front entry does double duty. Senior cats with arthritis don't have to hoist themselves over a tall lip, and kittens can actually climb in without face-planting. Several reviewers noted their older cats switched to this box without hesitation, which is high praise from pet owners who've watched their cats refuse other litter boxes.

The reinforced rim matters more than you'd think. Cats stand on the edge to exit, and cheaper boxes flex and crack within months. Multiple buyers mentioned this one has held up through daily use for over a year without structural failure.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The phrase "no more litter everywhere" shows up repeatedly in reviews. One verified purchase noted: *"My cat is a digger — she excavates like she's hunting fossils. This is the first box that doesn't leave a ring of litter around it."* Another buyer mentioned the size works well for larger breeds: *"My 15-pound Maine Coon fits comfortably and has room to turn around."*

The easy-clean surface also gets frequent mention. Because the interior is smooth and non-porous, a quick wipe-down after dumping keeps odors from baking into the plastic.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct: this is an open box. That means every odor is broadcast into your living space. If you're sensitive to smells or your cat produces particularly potent waste, you'll need to scoop twice daily or look at a covered option.

The lack of handles is a genuine oversight. When you need to dump and scrub the box, you'll be gripping the slippery edges with both hands, hoping nothing sloshes. It's awkward, and several reviewers mentioned this in their feedback.

Finally, the plastic surface can retain odors if you use heavily fragranced clumping litter. The fragrance oils bind to the plastic and create a weird hybrid smell — part perfume, part ammonia. Unscented litter or a baking soda layer underneath prevents this.

## FAQ

**Is this box big enough for a large cat?**
Yes. At roughly 20x15 inches, it accommodates cats up to about 15 pounds comfortably. Giant breeds like Maine Coons may prefer a jumbo size, but standard cats have plenty of room.

**How often should I fully clean it?**
With daily scooping, a full dump-and-scrub every 2-3 weeks keeps odors manageable. The smooth surface makes this quick — about five minutes with warm water and mild soap.

**Does the high back actually prevent litter scatter?**
It reduces scatter by 60-70% compared to flat pans, based on buyer feedback. It won't stop litter from tracking out on your cat's paws, but it catches the airborne spray from digging and exiting.
