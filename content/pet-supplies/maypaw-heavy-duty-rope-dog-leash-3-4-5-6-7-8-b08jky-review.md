## Where It Falls Short

Honest tradeoffs exist. The rope texture is rough — if your dog lunges unexpectedly and the leash slides through your hands, you'll feel it. Gloves help, but it's worth knowing going in.

This is also not a small-dog leash. The hardware and rope thickness are overkill for a 10-pound Chihuahua. You're paying for durability that a small dog simply won't need. And while the clasp is solid, it's not a quick-release — you're unscrewing and rethreading if you need to swap leashes mid-walk.
