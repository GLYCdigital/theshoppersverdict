If you own a pet water fountain, you already know the dirty secret: the replacement filters are where the real money goes. The driamor PF-S cartridges sit in that crowded space of "will fit most fountains" replacements, but with 225 ratings holding a 4.5-star average, they've clearly earned their keep with real pet owners. ## What You're Actually Getting

The PF-S is a standard cylindrical filter cartridge — roughly 2 inches tall — built around a three-stage filtration stack. The outer white mesh catches fur and larger debris, the activated carbon layer handles odor and chlorine taste, and the inner ion-exchange resin pulls heavy metals and ammonia byproducts from recirculating water. It's the same architecture you'd find in OEM filters from PetSafe or Veken, which is exactly why these work across so many fountains.

The packaging includes multiple cartridges (typically 4-6 depending on listing), each wrapped individually to keep carbon fresh. That's a small touch, but it matters — loose carbon absorbs ambient odors from your cabinet and dies faster.

## Real-World Performance

I've tested these in a PetSafe Drinkwell and a no-name Amazon fountain. Fit was snug in both — no water bypassing around the edges, which is the #1 complaint with generic filters. Flow rate stayed strong through week two, then tapered predictably in week three. That aligns with what reviewers consistently report.

One buyer noted: *"These fit my Catit fountain perfectly and my cat actually drinks more now that the water tastes cleaner."* Another long-term user mentioned: *"I've bought three packs over the year — no quality variance between batches, which is rare for third-party filters."* That consistency matters when you're trusting a filter to keep your pet hydrated.

The carbon does its job on tap water. If your municipal water has that classic chlorine edge, these knock it down noticeably. The ion-exchange resin is the unsung hero here — it's what keeps hard water scale from building up in the pump, which is the real killer of fountains.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct: you must rinse these thoroughly before installation. The first cartridge I pulled from the pack shed black carbon dust for a solid four minutes of running water. Skip this step and you'll see black specks in the bowl — harmless, but alarming, and several reviewers reported exactly this. Budget five minutes of prep per cartridge swap.

There's also no printed replacement date on the individual cartridges. You'll need to track that yourself, which is annoying given that most OEM filters have a little date wheel. Minor, but worth knowing.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy if:** You own a common fountain brand (PetSafe, Veken, Catit, Pioneer Pet) and want filters that cost notably less than OEM without sacrificing performance. Also buy if you have hard water — the resin layer genuinely extends your pump's life.

**Skip if:** You have an unusual fountain with a proprietary filter shape. Check your fountain's manual for compatible cartridge dimensions before ordering. Also skip if you're unwilling to do the rinse ritual — you'll be frustrated by carbon dust.

## FAQ

**How often should I replace these?**
Every 2-4 weeks depending on your pet count and water quality. Multi-pet households with hard water should lean toward 2 weeks. Single cat with soft water can stretch to 4.

**Do these fit the PetSafe Drinkwell?**
Yes — the PF-S is designed for the standard 2-inch cylindrical cartridge slot used by Drinkwell, Catit, and most Veken models. Double-check your fountain's manual for "PF-S compatible" or measure your current cartridge.

**Will these eliminate the need to clean the fountain?**
No — filters handle water quality, not biofilm. You still need to disassemble and scrub the fountain every 1-2 weeks. Filters reduce the frequency, not the need.
