If you've ever tried to wipe down a muddy golden retriever with a flimsy baby wipe, you know the struggle: the wipe shreds, the dirt spreads, and the smell lingers no matter what you do. Arm & Hammer's Odor Control Wipes for Dogs are engineered to solve both problems at once. After testing these on a wet, grass-stained border collie and a notoriously stinky senior hound, here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The package contains 100 thick, 9x9-inch wipes infused with Arm & Hammer's signature baking soda formula. That's the key differentiator here — baking soda doesn't just cover up odor with perfume, it chemically neutralizes the compounds causing the smell. The wipes are textured on one side for scrubbing dirt and smooth on the other for gentle finishing. You also get a resealable plastic tub-style package that holds up reasonably well to daily use, though the lid mechanism requires deliberate pressing to maintain a proper seal.

## Real-World Performance

I tested these on two very different scenarios. First, the standard muddy paws situation — a 10-minute walk in light rain left my dog's paws and belly coated in mud. One wipe per paw handled the job completely, and the textured side lifted dirt out of the paw pads without needing excessive pressure. The thick material held up without tearing, which is rare in this category.

Second, the true odor test: my senior hound has a natural "hound musk" that intensifies after exercise. A full-body pass with three wipes noticeably reduced the odor — not just covered it up. The light baking soda scent dissipates within minutes, leaving a neutral, clean smell rather than a lingering floral or citrus fragrance that conflicts with your dog's natural scent.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews reflects genuine satisfaction. One verified buyer noted, "These are the only wipes I've found that don't leave my dog smelling like a scented candle — the odor control actually works." Another long-term user mentioned they've repurchased four times for their French bulldog's daily face and paw cleaning routine, specifically calling out the durability of the wipes mid-use.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and these wipes have two notable weaknesses. First, the resealable tab is finicky — if you don't press it down firmly, the wipes dry out within a week. Several reviewers mentioned this exact issue, so it's not a one-off defect. Second, the packaging is a wide, flat tub that doesn't fit in most walking bags or car glove compartments. You'll likely need to decant a few wipes into a ziplock for on-the-go use.

One more limitation: these are not formulated for facial use near the eyes. The baking soda solution is gentle on paws and body, but you'll want a separate, eye-safe product for tear stains or eye area cleaning.

## Who Should Buy These

**The daily walker** — if your dog comes home with dirty paws every day, these will save your floors and your sanity. **The odor-sensitive owner** — if you're tired of masking smells with heavy fragrances, the baking soda approach is a smarter chemistry play. **Multi-dog households** — the 100-count pack is economical when you're wiping down multiple dogs regularly.

Skip these if you need a portable wipe for quick on-the-go fixes (the packaging is too bulky) or if you're specifically looking for a facial wipe formulation.

## FAQ

**Are these safe for puppies?**
Yes, the formula is mild enough for puppies over 8 weeks, but always test a small patch first and avoid the eye area.

**Can I use these on cats?**
The product is labeled for dogs only. Cats have different skin pH and grooming habits — stick to feline-specific wipes.

**Do they leave a residue?**
No greasy or sticky residue. The wipes dry quickly with a clean finish, which makes them suitable for between-bath touch-ups.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely effective odor-neutralizing wipe that's thick enough for real dirt and doesn't perfume your dog. **Skip it if** you need a portable solution or require an eye-safe facial wipe.
