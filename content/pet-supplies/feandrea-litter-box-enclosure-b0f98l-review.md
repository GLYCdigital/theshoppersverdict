Let's be honest about what a litter box enclosure actually does: it hides the ugly plastic bin, contains scattered litter, and gives your cat a den-like space. The Feandrea enclosure (model B0F98L75Y6) does all three with a furniture-grade look that most competitors miss. But is it worth the assembly headache and the tight squeeze for bigger cats? Here's the breakdown after digging through 215 verified ratings.

**What you actually get**

The package includes the enclosure itself (four panels, a top, and a base), a clear plastic entry flap, and all necessary hardware. You're not getting a litter box inside — this is purely the cabinet. The dimensions are roughly 24" x 18.5" x 19.5" externally, which means it'll fit in a bathroom corner or under a console table. The wood-grain laminate is convincing up close; it's not real wood, but it doesn't have that shiny plastic sheen that screams "pet product."

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The MDF panels are thicker than what you'd find in budget cat furniture — about 0.5" — and the whole thing weighs around 20 lbs, so it doesn't wobble when your cat hops in. The side-entry design is smart: cats enter through a flap on the left side, which keeps the front face clean and visually unbroken. The top is flat, so you can set a plant or a stack of books on it, which makes it blend into a living room better than most enclosures.

One thing buyers consistently praise is the odor containment. The enclosure traps smells reasonably well because the only opening is the small side flap. One verified buyer noted: *"I was skeptical, but the smell is way less noticeable than with an open box. The flap does a great job of keeping it in."* That tracks with the design — enclosed spaces naturally limit air circulation.

**Where it falls short**

Assembly is the biggest complaint. The cam locks and dowels require patience. Several reviewers mention stripped screws and misaligned holes. One customer wrote: *"Took me 45 minutes and I had to redo two panels because the cams were loose. Not hard, just annoying."* Take your time, don't overtighten, and you'll be fine.

The interior space is genuinely snug. A standard 20" x 16" litter box fits, but just barely. If you have a Maine Coon or a chunky tabby over 15 lbs, they'll have to crouch to turn around. Multiple reviewers with larger cats report their pets avoiding the enclosure entirely. Measure your cat before you buy.

There's also no carbon filter or deodorizing system built in. The enclosure relies on the flap and the enclosed space alone. If your cat's waste is particularly pungent, you'll want to add a charcoal pouch inside.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for apartment dwellers who want to hide the litter box in a visible room, or anyone who's tired of seeing litter tracked across the floor. It's also great for small-to-medium cats who appreciate a private, cave-like spot. If you have a large breed or a senior cat with mobility issues, skip it — the side entry and tight interior will frustrate them.

**FAQ**

*Will my cat actually use it?*
Most cats adapt within a few days, especially if you leave the flap open initially. If your cat is skittish, propping the flap open with a small object for the first week helps.

*Does it fit a top-entry litter box?*
No. The interior height is about 18", so a top-entry box won't work. Stick with standard or low-sided boxes.

*How do I clean it?*
The top lifts off completely, giving you full access to the interior. Wipe down the interior with a pet-safe cleaner weekly; the laminate wipes clean easily.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a furniture-grade enclosure for a small-to-medium cat and don't mind a 30-minute assembly session. **Skip it if** you have a large cat, need built-in odor filtration, or want something that's ready to use out of the box.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — It does what it claims, looks decent, and contains odors better than expected. The assembly quirks and size limitations keep it from being perfect, but for the price, it's a solid upgrade over an exposed litter box.
