If you've got a large-breed dog with a serious chewing habit, you already know the struggle. Bully sticks are pricey. Rawhide is risky. ## What You Actually Get

The package contains fully dried turkey tendons — one ingredient, nothing else. These are thick, dense strips (roughly 6–8 inches long) sourced from free-range turkeys and slow-dehydrated to preserve nutrients. As shown in the product image, they're a pale golden-brown color with a fibrous texture that looks like it means business. The resealable bag is a nice touch, because you won't go through all of these in one sitting unless your dog is a demolition machine.

## Real-World Performance

**Chew time:** This is where iPaw earns its keep. Most large-breed owners report 20–40 minutes of active chewing per tendon. That's significantly longer than standard jerky or even some bully sticks at similar price points. The dense collagen matrix means your dog has to work for it — which is exactly what you want when you're trying to redirect destructive chewing behavior.

**Digestibility:** This is the big one. Turkey tendons break down into soft, pliable pieces as your dog chews, making them far less likely to cause blockages than rawhide. One buyer noted: *"My lab has a sensitive stomach and these are the only chews that don't end up on my carpet at 3 AM."* That's the kind of real-world feedback that matters.

**Nutrition:** At roughly 80% protein and under 5% fat, these are a leaner option than pig ears (which can be 25%+ fat). For dogs on weight management plans, this is a solid choice.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**The smell.** Several buyers mention the natural turkey aroma is noticeable. It's not overpowering like some bully sticks, but it's definitely present. If your dog chews in your living room, you'll know about it.

**Texture concerns.** For senior dogs or those with dental sensitivity, these may be too hard. One reviewer noted: *"My 10-year-old lab loved the flavor but struggled to break through the density."* If your dog has dental issues, consider soaking these in warm water for a few minutes before serving.

**Inconsistency.** A handful of reviews mention size variation between bags. Not a dealbreaker, but worth noting if you're expecting uniform pieces.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Owners of large breeds** (60+ lbs) with strong jaw muscles — Labs, Shepherds, Pit Bulls, Mastiffs

## FAQ

**Q: How long does one tendon last?**
A: For a large breed, expect 20–40 minutes of active chewing. For power chewers, maybe 15 minutes. It varies based on your dog's determination.

**Q: Can puppies have these?**
A: Technically yes, but they're very dense. I'd wait until your puppy has adult teeth (around 6 months) and always supervise first-time chewing sessions.

**Q: Are these safe for dogs with poultry allergies?**
A: No. These are 100% turkey. If your dog reacts to chicken or turkey, look for a beef or fish alternative.
