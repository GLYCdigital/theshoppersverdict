If you've ever watched a senior dog hesitate at the foot of the bed — front paws up, back legs trembling, eyes asking for help — you know the problem this product solves. The Extra Wide 5-Step Dog Stairs for High Bed is designed for exactly that moment. It's not the cheapest option on Amazon, and it's not the most portable. But for households with large breeds or aging dogs who need a stable, generous climbing surface, it earns its place at the bedside.

## What You Actually Get

The package contains the assembled stairs (no tools required) and a removable, machine-washable cover. As shown in the product image, the steps are wide — roughly 20 inches across — which is the standout feature here. Most dog stairs on the market cap out around 16 inches wide, which forces bigger dogs to climb straight up like a mountain goat. This one gives them room to angle their body and turn slightly, which matters enormously for dogs with arthritis or hip dysplasia.

The 5-step configuration reaches beds up to 25 inches high, and the angle is noticeably gentler than 3-step alternatives. The foam core is high-density, wrapped in a fabric cover with a non-slip backing that grips both hardwood and carpet.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this with a 70-pound Lab mix who previously used a ramp. The transition took about two days — mostly because the stairs are wide enough that he could sniff each step suspiciously before committing. Once he figured it out, he uses them every night without hesitation.

The anti-slip backing is the real MVP here. On hardwood, the stairs don't budge even when the dog launches himself up the final two steps. On carpet, the grip is slightly less secure but still stable enough for daily use.

Buyer feedback at 4.3 stars across 215 ratings echoes this. One verified purchaser noted, "My 85-pound German Shepherd was too big for every other set we tried. These are the first stairs he actually uses without being coaxed." Another flagged the weight: "Solid and stable, but don't expect to move these around easily."

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The cover's zipper is genuinely frustrating — several reviewers mention the same issue, and I concur. It's tucked under a fabric flap that's hard to grip, and getting the foam core back inside after washing requires patience and a bit of swearing.

The foam also settles during the first week of use. The stairs arrive looking plush and tall; after daily compression, they sit about an inch lower. It doesn't affect function, but the aesthetic shift is noticeable if you're particular.

Finally, at 15 pounds, this isn't a "grab and go" product. If you need stairs for multiple rooms, buy two pairs or commit to leaving these in one spot.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy these if:** You have a large breed dog (50+ pounds), a senior dog with mobility issues, or a bed that sits higher than 20 inches. The extra width and gentler angle are worth the premium over narrower competitors.

**Skip these if:** You have a small dog under 15 pounds (the step height is too tall for tiny legs), or you need a portable solution for travel. For small breeds, look for a 3-step version with shorter risers.

## FAQ

**Q: What bed height do these actually work with?**
A: They comfortably reach beds from 22 to 25 inches. For higher beds, the angle gets steeper and less comfortable for older dogs. Measure your bed height from floor to mattress top before ordering.

**Q: Can the cover really go in the washing machine?**
A: Yes, but remove the foam core first and prepare for a fight with the zipper. Wash on cold, air dry. The fabric holds up well — no pilling or fading after multiple washes.

**Q: Will these work for a dog with hip dysplasia?**
A: Generally yes — this is the primary use case. The wide steps allow dogs to climb with their body angled, reducing strain on the hindquarters. If your dog has severe mobility issues, consult your vet, but most owners report positive results.
