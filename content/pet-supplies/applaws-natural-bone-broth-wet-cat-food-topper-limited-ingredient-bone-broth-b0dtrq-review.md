**If your cat turns up its nose at everything you put down, this broth topper might be the reset button you've been looking for. But it's not without tradeoffs.**

Applaws positions this as a "limited ingredient" bone broth topper, and for once the label isn't marketing fluff. The ingredient list on the chicken variety reads: chicken broth, chicken, natural flavor. That's it. No xanthan gum, no carrageenan, no "natural flavors" hiding a dozen mystery compounds. For cats with sensitive stomachs or owners who've been burned by vague pet food labels, that transparency alone carries weight.

**What you actually get**

Each pack contains 12 individual 1.4-ounce pouches. The broth is thin — think light stock, not gravy — with small shreds of real meat suspended in it. You can pour it directly over kibble, mix it into wet food, or serve it standalone as a hydration treat. The chicken and fish varieties are the most common; both follow the same stripped-down formula.

**Real-world performance**

The hydration angle is legitimate. Cats evolved with a low thirst drive, and many chronic kidney issues trace back to chronic dehydration. Pouring this over dry food adds meaningful moisture to every meal. One reviewer noted: *"My 14-year-old with early kidney disease went from ignoring his water bowl to lapping up every drop of this broth. I mix it with his renal diet and he finishes the bowl in minutes."* That's the use case this product nails.

The picky-eater turnaround is the other consistent win. Multiple reviews describe cats who "would rather starve than eat the same kibble twice" suddenly cleaning their bowls. The aroma is strong and meaty — noticeably more appealing than most commercial toppers that smell like wet cardboard.

**Where it stumbles**

The fat separation issue is real. Several pouches arrive with a congealed layer that requires aggressive shaking to redistribute. It's not spoiled — just unappetizing to look at. A few reviewers reported pouches that were mostly liquid with minimal meat shreds, which feels thin for the price point.

And let's be direct about cost: at roughly $0.50–0.75 per pouch depending on subscription pricing, you're paying for broth. This is a supplement, not a meal replacement. If you're looking for a complete wet food, this isn't it. Multi-cat households will burn through a box in under a week.

**Who should buy this**

- **The CKD cat owner**: If your vet has recommended increasing moisture intake, this is an easy, palatable way to do it without switching foods.
- **The finicky feline parent**: Cats who've rejected every topper or supplement you've tried. The single-protein formula removes variables.
- **The ingredient label reader**: If you refuse to feed your cat anything you can't pronounce, this is one of the cleanest options available.

**Skip it if** you're on a tight budget, have a cat that already drinks plenty of water, or you're expecting a meal-replacement product.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I feed this as a complete meal?**
No. It's a topper with roughly 10–15 calories per pouch. It complements food; it doesn't replace it.

**Q: Is the fish variety safe for cats with allergies?**
The fish version uses single-source fish, but if your cat has a known fish allergy, stick to the chicken. The limited-ingredient formula helps isolate triggers.

**Q: Does it need refrigeration after opening?**
Yes — any unused portion should be refrigerated and used within 24 hours. The pouches are single-serving for a reason.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** your cat needs more hydration, turns up its nose at current offerings, or has a sensitive stomach that reacts to fillers. **Skip it if** you need a complete meal or want maximum value per dollar.

At 4.2/5, this is a solid, honest product that does exactly what it claims — no more, no less. The premium price is the cost of clean ingredients and convenience. For the right cat, it's worth every penny.
