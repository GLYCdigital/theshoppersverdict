## Clumping Performance

Dr. Elsey's produces what reviewers describe as **"rock hard"** clumps. Urine clumps hold together firmly — you can scoop them cleanly without the clump disintegrating into a soupy mess.

One detailed review explained: *"It clumps EXTREMELY well. Honestly sometimes it's a bit like scooping a rock. If it's a larger clump sometimes I have to put some effort in to break it in half."*

This has a practical benefit: **less wasted litter**. When clumps don't break apart, you don't lose the clean litter around them. You can shake off excess clean litter from the scoop and return it to the box.

The only downside is that rock-hard clumps can make filling a **Litter Genie** more difficult — the bag fills up faster because the clumps take more volume than they would if they were softer and compressible.

## Dust and Tracking

**Dust:** Dr. Elsey's is genuinely low-dust. No clay litter is truly dust-free (that's the nature of clay), but the difference from brands like Tidy Cats or Fresh Step is noticeable. When pouring, you see a minimal dust puff rather than a cloud.

**Tracking:** Reviewers consistently rate the tracking as lower than other clay litters. The granules are medium-sized — not so small that they cling to paws and scatter everywhere, not so large that cats dislike the texture. A rug under the litter box catches most of what does escape.
