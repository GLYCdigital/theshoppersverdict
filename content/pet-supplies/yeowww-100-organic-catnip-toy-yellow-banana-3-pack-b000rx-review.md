The **Yeowww! 100% Organic Catnip Banana** takes a different approach: it's basically a cloth sausage *stuffed* with certified organic catnip. ## What You Actually Get

Three yellow banana-shaped toys, each roughly 7 inches long. The exterior is a durable cotton twill — similar to canvas work-bag material — with a stitched green tip and brown accent lines to mimic a ripe banana. There are no bells, no crinkle plastic, no feathers. Just fabric and the strongest organic catnip you've likely encountered.

The "100% organic" claim matters here. The catnip is grown in the USA without pesticides, and it's packed inside the toy rather than coating the surface. You'll notice the difference immediately when you squeeze the banana — it's firm and heavy with herb, not fluffy with polyester stuffing.

## Real-World Performance

My test panel of three cats produced different but equally enthusiastic responses. The senior cat rolled on it for twenty minutes, drooling and purring. The middle cat carried it around the house like prey for two days. The kitten went absolutely feral — bunny-kicking, tossing, and growling at it until it was soaked and slightly misshapen.

That's the durability test, and it passed. The triple-stitched seams held up against serious claw work. Multiple Amazon reviewers echo this: *"My cat is a shredder and this is the first toy she hasn't destroyed in a day. Three weeks in and it's still intact."* Another buyer noted, *"My cat ignores everything else now. She carries these bananas everywhere."*

The scent longevity is solid. Cats lose interest in most catnip toys within a few days as the oils evaporate. Because the Yeowww banana is densely packed, the potency lasts roughly 4-6 weeks of regular play — significantly longer than average. Rubbing the toy between your hands to crush the leaves inside can revive it temporarily.

## Honest Shortcomings

The lack of a refill option is the biggest frustration. Once the catnip is spent, the banana becomes a mildly interesting lump of fabric. You can't unzip it and recharge it. At roughly $5-7 per banana depending on the listing, that's acceptable, but it's a downside if you're budgeting.

The potency can be *too* effective for some cats. A few reviewers mentioned their cats became possessive and aggressive while under the influence — growling and nipping when approached. If your cat has a sensitive temperament, supervise their first session.

Also worth noting: dogs like these. The banana shape and dense fabric read as a fetch toy to many canines. If you have both species in your home, prepare for inter-species disputes.
