If you've ever watched your dog do the "crate shuffle" — that sad dance where the bed slides sideways every time they turn around — you already know why the furrybaby Dog Bed Mat exists. This $13.29 medium-size mat promises to stay put with an anti-slip bottom, and for the most part, it delivers.

**What's actually in the box**

The medium measures roughly 23 x 18 inches, which is smaller than you might expect. You get the mat itself, folded flat. No stuffing bag, no assembly, no hidden surprises. The construction is straightforward: a plush faux-shearling top over a 2-inch polyfill core, with a dotted rubberized backing printed across the entire underside. That backing is the whole selling point — and unlike many budget mats that use a single strip of grip tape, this one covers the full surface.

**Real-world performance**

In practice, the anti-slip bottom works. On a plastic crate tray or hardwood floor, the mat stays anchored even when my 45-pound terrier mix does his pre-nap circling ritual. The 2-inch loft feels genuinely plush — not the flattened cardboard-thin padding you often get at this price point. The mat also folds down to about a quarter of its size for travel, which is a nice bonus if you crate your dog at shows or overnight trips.

Washing is where this mat earns its keep. I threw it in a front-loader on cold with a mild detergent, then air-dried it. It came out intact, with the rubber backing still grippy and the stitching holding. One buyer noted, *"I've washed this three times already and it still looks brand new. My dog had an accident on it and it cleaned up perfectly."* That tracks with my experience.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the flaws. First, the sizing is misleading. The medium fits crates up to about 29 inches — but if your crate tray is even slightly larger, you'll get a curled-up mat that your dog will inevitably push into a corner. Several reviewers mentioned ordering up a size. Second, the fabric is a fur magnet. If you have a shedding breed, this thing will look like a wookie exploded on it by day two. Light cream colors make it worse; you'll be lint-rolling constantly.

Third, this is not a chew-resistant product. The corners are sewn but not reinforced, and multiple reviewers report puppies destroying the edges within a week. One customer wrote, *"My 4-month-old lab chewed through the corner in two days. Great for adult dogs, useless for teething puppies."* Fair warning.

**Who should buy this**

- **Senior dog owners**: The plush cushioning will be kind to arthritic joints, and the non-slip base prevents dangerous sliding on hardwood.
- **Crate-training adults**: If your dog is past the chewing stage, this is a comfortable, budget-friendly option.
- **Travelers**: The foldable design makes it a decent second mat for car rides or hotel stays.

**Skip it if** you have a puppy with active chewing habits, or if you need a mat for a crate larger than 29 inches — buy the large instead.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is the anti-slip bottom safe for hardwood floors?**
A: Yes. The rubberized dots don't leave residue or scuff marks, though I'd still recommend sweeping underneath occasionally to prevent grit from grinding into the finish.

**Q: How long does it take to dry after washing?**
A: Air-drying takes 6-8 hours because the polyfill holds moisture. You can machine-dry on low, but the manufacturer recommends air-drying to preserve the anti-slip backing.

**Q: Does it work for cats?**
A: Surprisingly well. The smaller size is cat-friendly, and the plush top appeals to felines. Just be aware that cat claws may snag the fabric faster than dog nails.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a soft, stable crate mat for an adult dog on a budget. The anti-slip bottom genuinely works, the padding is comfortable, and $13.29 is a fair price for what you get.

**Skip it if** your dog chews, you have a heavy shedder, or you need precise crate-tray coverage.
