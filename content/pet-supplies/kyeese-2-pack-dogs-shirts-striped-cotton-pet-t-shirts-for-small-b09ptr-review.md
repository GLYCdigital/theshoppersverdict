Honest tradeoffs exist. The white stripes are a magnet for mud and grass stains, and the fabric shows dirt more readily than darker solid shirts. You'll be washing these frequently if your dog is an outdoor enthusiast.

Second, there's no belly coverage. The shirt ends at the waistline, leaving the underbelly exposed. For female dogs in heat or male dogs who drag their bellies on grass, this is a limitation. You'd need a full-body onesie for that kind of coverage.

Finally, the size range tops out at Large (roughly 22-inch chest). If your dog is over 30 pounds, you're out of luck — this is strictly a small-breed product.

**Q: Are these good for dogs with sensitive skin?**
