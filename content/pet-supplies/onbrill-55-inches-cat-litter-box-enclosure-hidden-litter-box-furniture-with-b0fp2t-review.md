Let’s be honest: most “hidden” litter box cabinets are just plywood boxes with a hole cut in the side. The ONBRILL 55-inch enclosure tries to be something more — a piece of furniture that happens to hide your cat’s bathroom. After digging through 225 ratings and the spec sheet, here’s the real picture.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the 55" W x 23.6" H x 19.7" D cabinet, a removable side table top, a cat door flap, a charcoal filter, and the hardware kit. The MDF panels arrive with a laminated wood grain that looks like a mid-century modern sideboard from three feet away. It’s not solid wood — don’t fool yourself — but the finish is matte and consistent, not the cheap glossy print you see on budget cabinets.

The layout is the smart part. The main compartment is wide enough for a standard XL high-sided litter pan with room to spare. Most 40-pound bagged cats won't feel cramped. There’s a top access door for scooping and a front cat flap for entry, which solves the two biggest complaints with cheaper enclosures: having to slide the whole unit out to clean, and cats refusing to squeeze through a tiny hole.

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

The cam lock assembly is standard for this price tier, but the fit tolerances are better than average. Panels align without gaps. The hinges on the top door are metal, not plastic, which matters after daily opening. One buyer noted, *“I was skeptical about MDF, but it’s held up for 6 months with two 15-pound Maine Coons jumping on top of it to sleep.”* That’s a common theme in the reviews — the structure holds weight.

The charcoal filter does almost nothing for ammonia smell. That’s not a defect; it’s physics. The filter is a small pad near the flap. If you scoop daily, it’s fine. If you let it go two days, the smell leaks from the flap and the top door seams. This is an enclosure, not an air purifier. For odor control, you’ll want clumping litter with activated carbon and a regular scooping habit.

**Where It Falls Short**

Assembly is the biggest friction point. Expect 45-60 minutes. The cam locks require a screwdriver and some elbow grease; the door hinges need careful alignment or the top door will rub. Two people make it much easier — one to hold panels square while the other tightens.

The cat flap is a simple swinging vinyl door. Some cats hate it. If your cat is skittish, you may need to prop it open for a week before they commit. Also, the 23.6-inch height means larger breeds (think Ragdolls or chunky tabbies) have to duck slightly. It’s not a problem, but it’s not palace-like either.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Apartment dwellers with limited floor space** who want the litter box in a common area without it being the first thing guests see.
- **Owners of large cats or XL litter pans** — the 55-inch width is genuinely generous. Most competitors max out at 30-40 inches.
- **People who hate bending over to scoop** — the top door is a genuine back-saver.

Skip it if you have a cat that refuses enclosed spaces, or if you’re expecting odor-blocking magic. This is furniture with a function, not a sealed sanitation unit.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit a standard 20-inch x 15-inch litter pan?**
Yes, easily. It fits most XL pans up to about 24 inches long. For jumbo pans wider than 24 inches, measure first.

**Q: Does the top door lock?**
No, it just closes flush. If you have a determined cat that learns to paw it open, you may need a small latch. Most cats won’t bother.

**Q: Is the wood frame stable on carpet?**
Yes, the base is flat and wide. It doesn’t wobble, even with a cat jumping on top. On hardwood, consider felt pads to avoid scratches.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need a genuinely furniture-grade enclosure that fits an XL pan, you have a big cat, and you value top-down scooping access.

**Skip it if:** You want zero assembly hassle, or you expect the cabinet to neutralize odors without diligent scooping.

The ONBRILL earns a 4.6/5. It’s not perfect — assembly is tedious and the filter is more decorative than functional — but it’s one of the few enclosures on Amazon that doesn’t look like a cat box in a costume. At 225 ratings and a 4.5-star average, the consensus holds: this is a solid, livable solution for keeping the litter box out of sight without losing your living room to it.
