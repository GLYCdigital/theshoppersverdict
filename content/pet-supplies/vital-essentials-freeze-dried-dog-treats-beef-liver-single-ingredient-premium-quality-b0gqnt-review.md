**Frequently asked questions**

**Q: Are these safe for puppies?**
Yes, but introduce gradually. The pieces are crumbly and easy to chew, making them suitable for puppies over 8 weeks. Break into smaller bits for very young dogs.

**Q: How should I store them after opening?**
Keep the bag tightly sealed in a cool, dry place. The resealable pouch works well, but if you live in a humid climate, transferring to an airtight container extends freshness.

**Q: Can I rehydrate these for senior dogs?**
Absolutely. Add a teaspoon of warm water per treat and let it sit for 5 minutes. It softens to a paste-like consistency that's easier for older dogs with dental issues.
