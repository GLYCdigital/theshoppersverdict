## What's actually in the bottle

Nutramax is the same company behind Cosequin and Dasuquin, so the pedigree is solid. This liquid omega-3 supplement delivers EPA and DHA directly in a form that bypasses the digestive breakdown issues some dogs have with chewables. The unflavored formulation is the standout feature here — it's essentially odorless and tasteless, which matters enormously when you're dealing with a finicky eater.

The bottle comes with a measured pump dispenser that delivers a consistent dose. You'll want to shake it well before each use, and yes — it needs refrigeration once opened. That's a real consideration for travel, but it's also a sign there's no heavy preservative load in there.

The 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews isn't inflated by hype. ## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The pump mechanism is the most common complaint in reviews. Several buyers reported the pump getting stuck or delivering inconsistent doses after a few weeks of use. It's fixable (rinse the pump with warm water), but it's an annoyance at a premium price point.

The refrigeration requirement is genuinely inconvenient. If you board your dog or travel frequently, you'll need a mini-fridge or a cooler. And for owners of large breeds on the maximum dose, the bottle doesn't last as long as you'd hope — expect to reorder monthly.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** Your dog is a picky eater who refuses fish-flavored chews. Your dog has dry, itchy skin, hot spots, or dull coat. You want a supplement from a vet-founded brand with quality testing you can trust. You're willing to pay a bit more for faster absorption and visible results.

**Skip it if:** Your dog will only eat treats, not liquid mixed into food. You need something shelf-stable for frequent travel. You're on a tight budget and have a 70+ pound dog requiring the max dose — you'll go through a bottle in under three weeks.

## FAQ

**Q: How long until I see results?**
Most buyers report noticeable improvement in 2-4 weeks. Coat shine typically improves first, followed by reduced scratching around week 3-4. Give it a full 8 weeks before judging effectiveness.

**Q: Can I give this to a cat or puppy?**
The label is for dogs. Consult your vet for cats or puppies under 6 months — dosing differs significantly and this formula isn't calibrated for feline needs.

**Q: Does the unflavored version really have no smell?**
It has a very mild, neutral oil scent — nothing like the overpowering fish odor of salmon oil. Most dogs don't notice it in food. A few picky eaters still detect it, so start with a half dose mixed into wet food.
