## Where It Falls Short

Be honest about the sizing. This collar runs small — a 4.7-star average doesn't mean it fits every neck perfectly. Measure your dog's neck before ordering and add two inches to be safe. Owners of thick-coated breeds like Huskies and Goldens consistently report needing to size up.

The padding is also a sponge. If your dog swims or plays in the rain, the neoprene soaks up water and stays damp for hours. That's a hygiene consideration if your dog wears the collar 24/7. Remove it after water activities and let it air dry. Also, the quick-release buckle — while solid — is still plastic. Extreme chewers might eventually destroy it, so this isn't the collar for dogs that like to grab their own collar and tug.

**Q: Is this collar safe for dogs that pull on leash?**

**Q: How do I choose the right size?**
A: Measure your dog's neck circumference with a flexible tape measure. Add 2 inches for comfort and compare to the size chart. When in doubt, size up — the adjustable slide gives you room to tighten.
