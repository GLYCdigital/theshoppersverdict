Cats are masters of making you feel guilty, aren't they? You drop $60 on a bag of kibble they sniff and walk away from, yet they'll beg for a squeeze of something that comes in a tube like it's gourmet dining. The **Beefeaters Lickable Puree Variety Pack** understands this dynamic perfectly — it's the treat equivalent of fast food for felines, and that's exactly why it works.

## What You're Actually Getting

This variety pack delivers three flavors — salmon, tuna, and chicken — in individual squeeze tubes. Each tube is a smooth, broth-like puree that you squeeze directly onto a plate, into a bowl, or straight into your cat's mouth if they're cooperative. The packaging is smart: thin, flexible tubes that let you control portion size and keep the rest sealed for later.

As shown in the product image, the three flavors are color-coded, which matters more than you'd think when you're half-asleep at 6 AM trying to figure out which tube is which. The puree consistency is consistent across all three flavors — no watery separation, no weird chunky bits that defeat the "lickable" purpose.

## Real-World Performance

The standout feature here is versatility. I tested this with a senior cat who's missing several teeth and a picky two-year-old who turns her nose up at anything that isn't chicken-flavored. Both went after the puree with genuine enthusiasm. The salmon flavor had the strongest aroma — you'll smell it the moment you open the tube, so be prepared for your cat to materialize from nowhere.

The texture is genuinely smooth, which makes it an excellent carrier for crushed medications. One reviewer noted, "I've been hiding my cat's thyroid pill in the tuna flavor and she licks the tube clean every time. This is the only treat that's worked consistently." That's a real-world use case that justifies the price on its own.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

With 210 ratings at 4.2 stars, the consistent praise centers on palatability. Cats who "won't eat anything" apparently eat this. ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. Each tube is small — roughly two tablespoons of puree. For a multi-cat household, you're going through several tubes per day, and the cost adds up quickly. This is a treat, not a meal replacement, and some buyers miss that distinction.

There's also the fish-sensitivity issue. Cats who aren't used to fish-based treats can experience loose stools if you overdo it. Start with half a tube and monitor your cat's reaction before making this a daily ritual. This isn't a flaw in the product itself, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

## Who Should Buy This

This variety pack is ideal for:
- **Kitten owners** — the smooth texture is perfect for weaning or introducing treats

## FAQ

**Q: Can I feed these as a meal replacement?**
No. These are treats with a high moisture content but not complete nutrition. They're designed for supplemental feeding, not as a substitute for balanced cat food.

**Q: Do these need refrigeration after opening?**
Once opened, you should use the tube within a few hours. Unopened tubes are shelf-stable. The product image shows the packaging clearly — each tube is single-serving.

**Q: Are these safe for kittens?**
Yes, the smooth puree texture is appropriate for kittens, but consult your vet first if your kitten is under eight weeks old.
