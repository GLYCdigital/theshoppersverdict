**Is this the collar that finally makes your dog look as good as they think they are?** The Lionet Paws Dog Collar with Bowtie has racked up a 4.7-star average from 235 ratings, which tells you something: people aren't just buying this for the novelty. They're buying it because it holds up. But "cute" and "durable" rarely live in the same product. Here's where this one actually lands.

**What you get in the package**

The box contains exactly one collar with the bow tie pre-attached. No spare parts, no matching leash — just the collar. That's fine, because the construction is where the value sits. The bow tie is stitched directly onto the collar band rather than clipped on, which is a deliberate design choice. Clips break, snap off in dog parks, and get chewed. Stitching doesn't.

The collar itself is cotton webbing with a soft underside that sits against the coat. The D-ring for your leash is welded metal, and the buckle is a standard side-release plastic that feels dense, not flimsy. For a product in this price tier, the hardware is noticeably better than what you'd find on a $12 pet-store impulse buy.

**Real-world performance and build quality**

I've tested enough dog gear to know that the bow tie is usually the weak point. On this collar, it's the selling point. Because it's stitched rather than clipped, it doesn't flop forward or twist around the neck. One reviewer put it plainly: *"My dog is a maniac on walks, and the bow stays perfectly in place. I've washed it three times and it still looks brand new."* That's consistent with what I found — the stitching is tight, and the fabric doesn't fray at the edges even after repeated wet-dog shakes and brush encounters.

The cotton material breathes well, which matters for summer walks. It's not a heavy collar, so small dogs won't feel weighed down. For long-haired breeds, the soft backing is a genuine plus — it doesn't pull or mat the fur the way nylon webbing can.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The overwhelming theme in the feedback is that this collar looks *more expensive than it is*. Multiple buyers mention strangers stopping them on walks to compliment it. One owner of a French Bulldog wrote: *"I bought this for a wedding photo shoot and ended up using it as his everyday collar. It's that sturdy."* Another noted that the bow tie survived a full year of daily wear on a Golden Retriever puppy — which is essentially a torture test for any accessory.

**Where it falls short**

The sizing is the biggest complaint, and it's legitimate. The collar runs small. Several buyers with Corgis and Beagles report needing to size up. If your dog is between sizes, go up — a too-tight collar is a safety hazard, not just a comfort issue. The bow tie is also not removable, so if you want a plain collar for hiking or messy outdoor days, you'll need a second collar. And the bow itself is stiff when new; it softens after a few wears, but it's not floppy by design.

**Who should buy this**

This collar is ideal for:
- **Small-to-medium breed owners** who want a dressy look without the fragility of cheap costume collars
- **Photo shoots, weddings, or holidays** — it photographs exceptionally well
- **Owners of active dogs** who need a bow that won't spin or detach mid-walk
- **Gift buyers** — it comes in enough colors to match any dog's personality

Skip it if you have a giant breed (the largest size may still run snug on a 90-pound Lab), or if you need a collar that can go from muddy trail to formal event without a swap.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will the bow tie survive a dog that rolls in mud?**
A: Yes, but you'll need to hand-wash it. The bow is stitched in place, so machine washing can distort the fabric. Spot-clean with mild soap and air dry.

**Q: Is this safe for puppies?**
A: For puppies over 8 weeks, yes — as long as you size correctly. The cotton is gentle on growing fur, and the hardware isn't heavy enough to strain a young neck.

**Q: Does it come with a matching leash?**
A: No. You'll need to buy a separate leash. The D-ring is standard size, so any leash clip will fit.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a collar that looks polished for special occasions but can handle daily wear without falling apart. **Skip it if** you're between sizes — order up — or if you need a fully machine-washable everyday collar.
