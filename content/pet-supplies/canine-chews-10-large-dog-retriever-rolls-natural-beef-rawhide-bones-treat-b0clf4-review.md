**Where It Falls Short**

These aren't perfect, and you should know the downsides before buying.

First, the smell. That roasted beef aroma is strong. One reviewer put it bluntly: *"Great chew, but I had to move it to the garage. The whole living room smelled like a deli counter for a week."* If you're scent-sensitive, factor that in.

Second, supervision is non-negotiable. These are rawhide products, which means they soften as they're chewed. Dogs that gulp rather than gnaw can break off and swallow chunks. If your dog inhales food without chewing, skip these entirely.

Third, sizing. These are genuinely large — 10 inches long and thick. Small breeds and puppies will struggle to get a good grip. This is strictly for medium-to-large dogs.
