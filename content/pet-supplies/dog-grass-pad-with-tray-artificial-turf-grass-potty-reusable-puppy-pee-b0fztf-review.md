This is not a set-and-forget product. The biggest complaints cluster around three issues:

**Size confusion.** The dimensions listed are often the total footprint, but the usable turf area is smaller because of the tray lip. A 24-inch pad gives you maybe 21 inches of actual potty space. Measure your dog before ordering. A 40-pound dog will struggle on anything under 30 inches.

**Not for heavy chewers.** The plastic tray edges are vulnerable. If your dog is a power chewer, they will destroy the tray’s rim within weeks. This is a product for trained dogs, not puppies in the destruction phase.

## FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Can I use this with the tray filled with water or litter?**
No. The tray is designed to be empty. Adding water creates a bacteria soup. Use an enzyme spray after rinsing instead.

**Q: How often do I need to clean it?**
Daily rinse for the turf, full wash with soap weekly. The tray needs wiping every 2–3 days depending on your dog’s output.

**Q: Will my dog know to use this instead of a real lawn?**
Most dogs adapt within 3–5 days if you place it near the door they use to go outside and transfer a soiled puppy pad onto the turf initially. The grass texture feels familiar enough.
