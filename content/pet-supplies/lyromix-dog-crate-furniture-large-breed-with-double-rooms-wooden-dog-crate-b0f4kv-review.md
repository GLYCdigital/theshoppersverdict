**Is the Lyromix Dog Crate Furniture worth the premium over a wire crate?** For multi-dog households or owners who refuse to look at metal bars in their living room, the answer is a cautious yes — if you measure first.

## What You're Actually Getting

Unboxing reveals a substantial package: engineered wood panels with a walnut-toned water-resistant laminate, a slide-out plastic tray, and enough screws to populate a small hardware store. The double-room configuration means you get a center divider (removable), two side doors, and one front door — all with locking latches. As shown in the product image, the design mimics a real sideboard, so it won't scream "dog cage" in your dining area.

The internal dimensions matter more than the exterior. The large size accommodates dogs up to 70-80 lbs in single-room mode, but that's with the divider removed. With the divider in, each compartment fits a medium dog (30-50 lbs) comfortably. **Measure your dog from nose to tail and standing height before you order** — this is not a "guess and hope" purchase.

## Real-World Performance

The wood construction is the headline feature, and it delivers. The laminate finish resists scratches from claws and wipes clean with a damp cloth. The slide-out tray catches accidents and fur efficiently — you can remove it without opening any doors, which owners consistently mention as a favorite feature.

The three-door design offers genuine flexibility. One buyer noted: *"I use the side door for my older dog who hates being reached over, and the front door for my puppy. Both can access their spaces without fighting over one entrance."* That's the double-room benefit in practice.

## Where It Falls Short

**Assembly is the biggest complaint.** Multiple 4-star reviews mention the same issue: *"The instructions are mostly pictures, and some predrilled holes didn't line up perfectly. Took me almost two hours with a drill."* Bring patience and a power drill — a manual screwdriver will test your marriage.

Weight is the second issue. At roughly 75 lbs fully assembled, this isn't a crate you'll relocate weekly. If you need portability, stick with a wire crate.

Third, the internal height. Several owners of Great Danes and Irish Wolfhounds reported their dogs couldn't stand fully upright. The wood frame eats into usable vertical space. If your dog's shoulder height exceeds 28 inches, look elsewhere.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have two medium dogs who need separate spaces, you want furniture-grade aesthetics in a main living area, or you're willing to spend an evening assembling it properly.

**Skip it if:** You own a giant breed, need a crate you can collapse and store, or your dog is a confirmed chewer — the wood edges are sturdy but not indestructible against a determined power-chewer.

## FAQ

**Q: Can the divider be removed permanently?**
Yes, but you'll have exposed screw holes on the top panel. It's functional but not pretty if you ever switch back.

**Q: Does it hold up to puppy accidents?**
The laminate is water-resistant, and the tray catches most issues. Wipe up promptly; prolonged moisture on seams will cause swelling over time.

**Q: What's the weight capacity per compartment?**
Each side comfortably holds a 40-50 lb dog. With the divider out, a single 70-80 lb dog fits — again, height permitting.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The Lyromix double-room crate earns high marks for design, material quality, and genuine two-dog functionality. The assembly frustration and height limitations are real tradeoffs, but for the right household, this transforms a necessary evil into a piece of furniture.

**Buy it if** you want a crate that doesn't look like a crate and have two medium dogs. **Skip it if** you need quick assembly or own a giant breed. At 4.4 stars from 220 ratings, most owners agree — just budget your evening for assembly.
