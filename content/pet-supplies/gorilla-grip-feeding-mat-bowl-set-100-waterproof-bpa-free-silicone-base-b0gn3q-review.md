I've tested more feeding stations than I care to count, and most fall into one of two camps: they look good but slide across the floor when your dog gets excited, or they're functional but ugly enough to hide in the laundry room. The GORILLA GRIP Feeding Mat Bowl Set tries to bridge that gap. Here's the honest breakdown after living with it.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get two stainless steel bowls (roughly 2-cup capacity each) seated in a single rectangular silicone mat. The mat is the star here — it's thick, flexible, and features that distinctive GORILLA GRIP textured underside that genuinely locks onto hard floors. As shown in the product image, the raised lip runs the full perimeter, which is the feature that separates this from a $10 mat-and-bowl combo.

## Real-World Performance

The waterproof claim holds up in practice. The 1-inch raised edge contains splashes from drinking, kibble that ricochets off the bowl sides, and the inevitable drool trail from a thirsty dog. I tested it with a messy eater who flings water everywhere, and the floor around the mat stayed dry. That's the headline feature, and it works.

The non-slip base is equally legit. On tile and hardwood, the mat doesn't budge — even when my 60-pound dog shoves his face in with full enthusiasm. On carpet, it's less secure, but that's true of every feeding mat in this category.

The stainless steel bowls are solid. They're not heavy-gauge commercial grade, but they resist dents, don't stain, and clean up easily. The silicone mat, however, is hand-wash only. That's a real annoyance given the "100% waterproof" marketing — waterproof doesn't mean dishwasher-safe, and the manufacturer doesn't recommend machine washing.

The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews reflects genuine satisfaction. Repeated themes in customer feedback:

## Where It Falls Short

The bowls are smaller than they look in photos. At roughly 2 cups each, they're fine for cats, small dogs, and medium breeds. If you have a Labrador, German Shepherd, or anything larger, you'll be refilling mid-meal or using these as water-only bowls.

The silicone's hair-magnet problem is real. Between washes, dust, pet hair, and crumbs cling to the textured surface. A quick wipe handles it, but you'll be wiping more often than you'd like.

Also note: the mat is flexible, not rigid. If you're using it on an uneven surface or trying to carry it with full bowls, it will wobble.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have a dog or cat who splashes water, pushes bowls around, or makes a mess at mealtime. You have hard floors that you're tired of wiping down. You want a set that looks decent enough to leave out in a visible kitchen area.

**Skip it if:** You have a large-breed dog needing 4+ cups per meal. You prefer dishwasher-safe everything. You're on carpet and need max grip.

## FAQ

**Q: Is the mat truly 100% waterproof?**
A: Yes, the silicone itself is completely waterproof and won't absorb liquids. The raised lip contains spills. Just know the mat is hand-wash only.

**Q: Will this work for a large dog?**
A: The bowls hold about 2 cups each. It's workable for water, but you'll refill food bowls frequently for larger breeds.

**Q: Does the non-slip base work on all floors?**
A: Excellent on tile, hardwood, and laminate. Decent on vinyl. Less effective on carpet, where the weight of the bowls can shift the mat.
