**Bottom Line: A solid, affordable dog door that does the job without fancy extras — but it's not built for every home or every dog.**

Most dog doors fall into two camps: cheap junk that flaps in the wind and leaks air, or $300+ units with smart features you'll never use. This one sits in the practical middle, and for most owners, that's exactly where you want to be.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the frame, the flap, a locking panel, mounting screws, and a template for cutting. The flap opening measures roughly 6.5 x 8.25 inches — big enough for dogs up to about 60 pounds, but you'll want to measure your dog's chest width, not their weight. A bulky Lab will squeeze through; a 50-pound bully breed with a wide chest will struggle.

The frame is plastic, which keeps the price down. The flap itself uses a magnetic seal that snaps shut quietly and stays closed even in wind — a genuine upgrade over the loose vinyl flaps on cheaper models.

## Real-World Performance

Installation is the biggest hurdle. You're cutting a hole in your door. The included template helps, but you'll need a jigsaw and steady hands. Expect 45–60 minutes if you're reasonably handy. The instructions are adequate, not great — check a YouTube video first if you've never installed a dog door.

Once installed, the four locking modes are genuinely useful. Set it to "in-only" when you don't want the dog wandering out at night. Lock it fully when you're away. It's a simple sliding panel, but it works and adds real versatility.

One owner put it this way: *"The magnet is what sold me. My old door flapped every time the AC kicked on. This one stays shut unless the dog is actually pushing through it."* That's the experience most buyers report.

## What Buyers Praise

Across the 220 ratings, the recurring positives are:

- **The magnetic seal** — reduces drafts and noise noticeably
- **The locking mechanism** — simple, functional, not gimmicky
- **Value** — several reviewers compared it favorably to doors costing twice as much

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides.

The plastic frame is the weak point. If you have a 70+ pound dog that barrels through at full speed, the corners can stress and crack over time. This is a medium-dog door, not a large-dog door, regardless of what the box claims.

The single-flap design means zero insulation. In Minnesota winters or Phoenix summers, you'll feel temperature transfer around the opening. If energy efficiency is your priority, you need a double-flap or insulated model — and you'll pay more for it.

Installation is permanent. There's no rental-friendly option, no sliding-glass-door adapter. Once you cut that hole, it's there.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You own a small-to-medium dog (under 60 pounds), you're comfortable with basic DIY tools, and you want a reliable, affordable door with useful locking features.

**Skip it if:** You have a large or heavy-chested dog, you rent your home, or you live in extreme climates where insulation matters. Also skip it if you're not confident with a jigsaw — consider hiring a handyman or buying a door-threshold model instead.

## FAQ

**Will this fit my door?**
Check two things: door thickness (max 1.75 inches) and the cutout size (6.5 x 8.25 inches). Measure both before ordering. Wood doors are fine; steel doors require extra tools and patience.

**Can my cat use it too?**
Yes, if they're willing to push through. The magnetic seal requires a bit more force than a loose flap, so some cats refuse. There's no small pet setting — this is a dog-sized door.

**Is the locking panel secure?**
It's a sliding plastic panel that blocks the opening. It'll stop your dog and casual intruders, but it's not a security door. For a more secure option, look for a door with a metal lock plate — you'll pay more.

## The Verdict: 4.2/5

This dog door nails the essentials — strong magnetic seal, functional locking, easy-enough installation — at a price that undercuts premium competitors. The plastic frame and lack of insulation are real limitations, but for most dog owners in moderate climates, this is a smart purchase that'll last several years.

**Buy it if** you need a reliable, mid-range dog door for a small-to-medium dog. **Skip it if** you have a giant breed, extreme weather, or a rental lease. For everyone else, this is a solid, honest value that does exactly what it promises.
