**What You Actually Get**

The bottle ships with a pump top that meters out the dose — a small detail that matters more than you'd think when you're wrangling a squirming 60-pound dog or a cat that views medication as a personal insult. The most common reason owners buy this is skin and coat issues, and the feedback backs that up. One reviewer noted, "My 12-year-old lab had bald patches on her flanks for months. That's not a flaw in the product; it's just digestive reality with any concentrated fish oil.
