If you've ever tried to brush out a matted cat, you know the drill: the cat yowls, the brush snags, and you end up with fur everywhere except where you want it. The Freshly Bailey Cat Detangler Spray promises to change that equation. After digging through 204 ratings (averaging 4.1 stars), here's the real story on whether this bottle earns a spot in your grooming kit.

## What You Actually Get

The product is a straightforward 8-ounce spray bottle with a pump nozzle. As shown in the product image, it's a no-frills amber bottle — nothing fancy, which is fine. The formula is water-based with natural conditioning agents (aloe, glycerin, and a few botanical extracts). It's not a heavy silicone-laden detangler like some dog products on the market, which matters for cats who groom themselves obsessively.

## Performance: The Good, The Bad, The Tangled

Here's the honest breakdown. On **medium-length coats with loose mats or minor tangles**, this stuff genuinely works. Spray it on, wait 30 seconds for it to penetrate, and the brush glides through where it previously snagged. Multiple reviewers noted it "softens the fur instantly" and makes combing "much less stressful for both of us." One buyer with a Maine Coon mix specifically called it "the only detangler that doesn't turn my cat's fur into a greasy mess."

The non-greasy claim holds up. Unlike some detanglers that leave a film you can feel, this dries down clean. That's a real advantage for indoor cats who'll lick any residue off their coat.

**Where it falls short:** Severe mats — the kind that are tight against the skin — are not going to be saved by any spray. A few reviewers learned this the hard way, noting they still had to use clippers on the worst spots. Also, the spray nozzle is narrow. Covering a full-sized cat's back takes 6-8 pumps, and some users found their cat getting antsy by pump four. One reviewer described it as "annoying to apply evenly on a wiggly cat." Fair point.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Three themes dominate the positive reviews:

1. **Scent control.** The fragrance is mild and fades quickly. Several reviewers specifically compared it favorably to other detanglers that "smell like a perfume aisle exploded."
2. **Brushability improvement.** The core promise delivered — brushing goes from a wrestling match to a manageable task.
3. **No residue.** Cats with sensitive skin or allergies didn't show adverse reactions in the review sample, and the formula rinses out cleanly at the next bath.

## Where It Falls Short (Beyond the Nozzle)

The 8-ounce size is small for multi-cat households. If you have two long-haired cats, you'll go through a bottle in a month with regular brushing. At the current price point, that's a recurring cost worth factoring in.

Also, this is a maintenance tool, not a rescue tool. If you're buying it because your cat already has a full-body mat situation, you're going to be disappointed. It's designed to prevent tangles and ease routine dematting — not to undo months of neglect.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Long-haired cat owners** who brush regularly but hit snags in the armpits, belly, and behind the ears
- **Cats who hate brushing** — the spray genuinely reduces pulling, which calms nervous cats
- **First-time cat owners** who adopted a fluffy cat and are learning the grooming ropes

**Skip it if:** Your cat has severe, felted mats touching the skin (you need clippers, not spray), or you have a Sphynx (save your money — no fur to detangle).

## FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Is it safe if my cat licks it off?**
A: The formula uses pet-safe, food-grade ingredients like aloe and glycerin. It's not toxic, but it's not a supplement — try to brush it through rather than leaving heavy wet spots. If your cat aggressively grooms after application, wait until it dries before letting them clean up.

**Q: Does it work on dogs too?**
A: Technically yes, it'll detangle dog fur. But dog coats are denser, so you'll use more product per session. You're better off with a dog-specific detangler if that's your primary use.

**Q: How often should I use it?**
A: For maintenance, once or twice a week alongside brushing. For active mats, daily application on the matted spots until they loosen. Don't oversaturate — a light mist is enough.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if** you have a long-haired or medium-haired cat who fights the brush, or you want to prevent tangles before they become mats. It's a legitimately effective, low-residue detangler that makes grooming sessions more humane for everyone involved.

**Skip it if** you're dealing with severe, skin-level mats or you need a heavy-duty solution for a neglected coat. This is a preventive and light-treatment tool, not a miracle worker — and the small bottle size means frequent repurchases for multi-cat homes.

The Freshly Bailey Cat Detangler Spray does what it claims, does it without the greasy residue, and does it at a fair price. For most cat owners, that's a win. Just keep your expectations realistic and your clippers handy for the worst offenders.
