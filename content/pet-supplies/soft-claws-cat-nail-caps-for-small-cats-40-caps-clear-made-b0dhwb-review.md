If you've ever woken up to shredded curtains or a couch that looks like a scratching post exploded, you've probably Googled "cat nail caps" at 2 a.m. The Soft Claws brand is the original — the one veterinarians have recommended since the 1990s — and this 40-cap clear pack is their smallest size. For kittens and dainty adult cats, it's a legitimate alternative to declawing. For everyone else? Read the sizing section before you buy.

## What's in the box

You get 40 clear vinyl caps, two tubes of cyanoacrylate adhesive (the same type used in nail glue), and an applicator tube to help seat each cap. That's roughly 8–10 full applications, since each paw needs 5 caps (front paws only — you don't cap back claws). The clear finish is invisible from more than a foot away, which is why most buyers prefer it over colored options.

## Real-world performance

The caps work exactly as advertised: they blunt the claw tip, so scratching becomes harmless to furniture and skin. As shown in the product image, each cap slides over the claw and bonds with the adhesive. Once dry, they stay on for 4–6 weeks until the nail grows out and the cap naturally pops off.

The critical step is application. You need to trim the claw first, fill the cap about 1/3 full with adhesive, and hold it in place for at least 60 seconds. Then — this is the part people skip — the cat needs to stay calm for another 5–10 minutes while the glue fully cures. One Amazon reviewer put it bluntly: *"The caps are great once on, but getting them on a wiggly cat is a two-person job. I wrapped my kitten in a towel and it still took 20 minutes."*

## What buyers consistently praise

The 4.1-star average (204 ratings) reflects a product that works when applied correctly. Repeated themes in positive reviews:

## Where it falls short

The biggest complaint isn't the product — it's the sizing. This "Small" size fits kittens 8 weeks and up, and adult cats under 8 pounds. If your cat is a standard 10-pound tabby, these will be too tight and may split or pop off early. Several frustrated reviews mention ordering Small without measuring and having to return for the Medium.

Second, the adhesive is unforgiving. If you don't wait for full drying, the cap slides off sideways and you're back to square one. Also, the caps only cover front claws — if your cat scratches with rear feet (some do during play), this won't help.

## Who should buy this

- **Kitten owners** — get them used to caps early, and you'll avoid years of furniture destruction.
- **Small-breed cat owners** — Singapura, Munchkin, or any cat under 8 lbs.
- **Post-declawing cats** — some vets recommend caps to protect healing paws, though check with your vet first.
- **Renters** — if your lease has a "no claw damage" clause, these are your insurance policy.

**Skip it if** you have a 10+ pound cat (order Medium), a cat that chews at its paws, or you're unwilling to do a 20-minute two-person application session every 4–6 weeks.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these hurt the cat?**  
A: No. The caps are blunt and don't interfere with normal claw retraction. Cats may lick at them for the first day, but they adapt quickly. If a cap is applied too tightly, it can cause discomfort — always measure the claw width against the cap opening first.

**Q: How long does one application last?**  
A: 4–6 weeks, depending on how fast your cat's nails grow. You'll know it's time when caps start falling off — that means the new nail has pushed the old cap off naturally. Never try to peel them off; let them shed.

**Q: Can I reuse caps that fall off early?**  
A: No. Once a cap comes off, the adhesive bond is broken and the cap is deformed. This pack includes 40 caps, which covers about 2.5 months of continuous use.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you have a small cat (under 8 lbs) and want a humane, reversible solution to scratching damage. The 40-cap count gives you months of protection for under $15, and the clear finish is practically invisible.

**Skip it if** your cat is average-sized or larger — order the Medium size instead — or if you can't commit to the careful application process. This isn't a "slap on and go" product; it rewards patience.
