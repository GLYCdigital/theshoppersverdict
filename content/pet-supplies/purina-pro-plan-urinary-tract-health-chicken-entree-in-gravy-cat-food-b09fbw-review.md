## How It Actually Performs

The science here is legit. This formula is designed to maintain a urine pH between 6.0 and 6.4 — the sweet spot that discourages struvite crystal formation. It's also restricted in magnesium, which is the primary building block for those painful crystals. The gravy formula delivers roughly 78% moisture, which is crucial because cats are notoriously bad at drinking enough water. More moisture in food means more diluted urine, which means fewer opportunities for crystals to form.

In real-world use, the texture holds up well. It's not soupy, but the gravy coats the chicken pieces nicely, making it easy to portion and serve. One Amazon reviewer noted, "My vet recommended this after my cat had a blockage. She devours it — I've never seen her eat wet food this fast." ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the price. This is not a budget cat food — you're paying for the clinical formulation, and it shows. Expect to spend roughly $1.50–$2.00 per 3-ounce can, which adds up fast for multi-cat households. Second, some cats experience digestive upset during the first week of transition. Third, if your cat specifically needs to dissolve existing stones (not just prevent new ones), your vet may still recommend a prescription-only diet with higher efficacy.

## Who Should Buy This

This food is ideal for three specific groups: cats with a history of struvite crystals who need ongoing management, male cats who are at elevated risk for urinary blockage, and owners who want a proactive, moisture-rich diet without committing to a veterinary prescription. It's less ideal for cats with calcium oxalate stones (a different crystal type that requires an opposite pH strategy) or for budget-conscious owners with healthy cats who don't need the extra clinical support.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this the same as a prescription urinary diet?**
No. Prescription diets like Hill's c/d or Royal Canin Urinary SO are stronger and can dissolve existing stones. This Pro Plan formula is designed for maintenance and prevention — it manages pH but isn't typically strong enough to dissolve an active blockage.

**Q: Can I feed this alongside dry food?**
Yes, but be careful. If you mix it with a high-magnesium dry food, you're undermining the urinary benefits. If your cat needs urinary support, feed this as the primary diet or pair it with a urinary health dry formula.

**Q: How quickly will I see improvement?**
Most owners report noticeable changes in litter box behavior within 2–4 weeks. For existing crystals, your vet should re-check urine or imaging at 4–6 weeks to confirm progress.
