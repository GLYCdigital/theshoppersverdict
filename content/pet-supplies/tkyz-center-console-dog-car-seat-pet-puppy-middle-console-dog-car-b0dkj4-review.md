**What you actually get**  
The box contains the seat itself, a padded liner, a tether strap with a heavy-duty clip, and two adjustable straps that wrap around your console to keep everything anchored. The base measures roughly 12" x 10" and the seat sits about 7" high — enough elevation for a small dog to see over the dash without being in your face. The outer shell is a tough Oxford fabric with a plush, removable fleece pad on top. Assembly takes under two minutes; you just thread the straps through the loops and tighten.

**Build quality and real-world use**  
I've tested a lot of pet car seats, and the TKYZ is sturdier than its price suggests. The plastic base has a slight texture that grips the console, and the straps — once cinched — don't slip. The tether clip is the standout feature: it attaches to your dog's harness (not collar), which is exactly what safety experts recommend. In a sudden stop, that clip keeps your pup from becoming a projectile.

The elevated position genuinely calms anxious dogs. One verified buyer wrote, "My chihuahua used to cry the entire drive. Now she just sits and watches the road like a tiny cop." **Where it falls short**  
The biggest limitation is size. The manufacturer says up to 15 lbs, and that's accurate — a 12-lb terrier fits fine, but a 20-lb corgi will look like a circus act and likely tip the seat on sharp turns. The cover also isn't waterproof. If your dog comes back from a rainy walk and hops in, the fleece pad soaks through, and the foam underneath takes hours to dry. You'll want to keep a towel in the car.

A few owners also reported that on very wide consoles (over 10 inches), the straps don't reach around cleanly. Measure your console width before ordering. And while the seat works in most cars, it won't fit trucks or SUVs with extra-wide center storage compartments — that's the #1 complaint in the reviews.

**Who should buy this**  
- **Small dog owners** (under 15 lbs) who drive frequently and want their pup to ride comfortably up front.
