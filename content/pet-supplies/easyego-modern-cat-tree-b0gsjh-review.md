**Is the Easyego Modern Cat Tree actually modern, or is that just marketing speak?** I've tested enough cat furniture to be cynical about the word "modern" — it usually means "we painted particle board gray." But the Easyego surprised me. This 4.7-star rated cat tree (235 ratings on Amazon) delivers on its design promise without sacrificing the structural integrity your cat will test daily.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the cat tree (duh), an anti-tip wall bracket with hardware, an Allen wrench, and assembly instructions that are... fine. Not great, but fine. The tree itself stands 63 inches tall with a base platform measuring 15.7 x 15.7 inches — enough footprint to stay planted on carpet or hardwood.

Key components out of the box:

- **Base perch** at ground level with a plush cushion
- **Two mid-level platforms** at varying heights
- **One enclosed condo** with a faux wool exterior — this is where the "modern" claim earns its keep
- **Top observation perch** with raised edges
- **Three sisal-wrapped scratching posts** in natural (not dyed) rope

The fabric covering is a textured faux wool that looks like something from a West Elm catalog, not a pet store clearance rack. It's also replaceable — a detail most budget cat trees skip entirely.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Here's where the Easyego separates itself from the $60 Amazon specials. The scratching posts are wrapped in genuine sisal rope — not the thin, glued-on carpet strips that peel off within a week. My test cat (a 12-pound domestic shorthair with an aggressive scratching routine) went through two weeks of daily use without creating visible fraying.

The anti-tip bracket deserves specific mention. It attaches to the wall and secures the tree at the midpoint. This matters if you have a jumper — cats launching from furniture onto the top perch will test the center of gravity. With the bracket installed, the tree didn't shift even during full-speed parkour sessions.

One structural note: the top perch is 16.5 x 12 inches. That's fine for most cats, but a 15+ pound Maine Coon or a chunky orange tabby will find it cramped. The perch edges are raised, so they won't fall off, but they'll be hanging over the sides.

## Where It Falls Short

**Assembly is genuinely tedious.** The Allen wrench works, but your hand will cramp. Use a power drill with a hex bit if you have one — it cuts the time in half.

**The top perch is undersized for large breeds.** If you have a cat over 14 pounds, they'll use it, but they won't fit comfortably.

**No replacement cushions included.** The base and perch cushions are removable and washable, but if a cat shreds them (some cats are just like that), you'll need to contact the seller for replacements. The fabric is durable, but it's not indestructible.

**The price point sits above budget options.** You're paying for the design and the replaceable components. If you just need a scratching post with a perch, there are cheaper options that will do the job.
