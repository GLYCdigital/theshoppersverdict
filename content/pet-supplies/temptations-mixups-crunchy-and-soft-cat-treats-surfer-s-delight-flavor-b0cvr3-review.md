Let's be honest about cat treats: most are either crunchy kibble bites or soft, stinky morsels. Temptations MixUps Surfer's Del tries to have it both ways—and according to 240 Amazon reviewers averaging 4.8 stars, it mostly works.

**What's actually in the bag**

The "MixUps" concept is straightforward: one bag contains two different treat textures. You get the classic crunchy Temptations shell filled with a soft, creamy center, plus separate soft-baked bites that bend rather than snap. The Surfer's Del flavor profile is tuna and crab, which sounds gimmicky but lands closer to "fish-forward" than "synthetic seafood."

A standard 2.1-ounce bag runs around 120 treats, and each one clocks in under 1.5 calories. That's meaningful if you're using treats for training or have a cat on a weight management plan.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The crunchy-soft combo works better than I expected. The crunchy pieces hold their structure—they don't crumble into dust at the bottom of the bag like some competitors. The soft bites have a jerky-like chewiness that older cats or dental-sensitive felines often prefer.

One thing worth noting: the resealable zipper actually functions. That matters more with this product than with standard treats because the soft pieces will dry out and harden if exposed to air. A properly sealed bag kept in a cool cabinet should stay fresh for 3-4 weeks after opening.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The dual-texture appeal is the headline. One reviewer put it simply: "My cat is obsessed with the soft ones and my other cat only wants the crunchy ones. At least this bag makes both of them happy." Another noted the low calorie count as the deciding factor: "Finally a treat I can give multiple times a day without feeling guilty."

Pickiness is the other recurring theme. Multiple reviewers describe cats that turn their noses up at standard treats but go after these. The strong fish aroma is doing a lot of that heavy lifting.

**Where it falls short**

The fish smell is real. If you're sensitive to pet food odors, this isn't the bag for you. It's not overwhelming while sealed, but opening it releases a distinct seafood punch that lingers.

The soft centers also have a texture issue in warm environments. If your kitchen runs hot or you keep the bag near a sunny window, the soft pieces can soften further and stick together. You'll be wrestling with a clump of treats rather than pulling out clean individual pieces. Not a dealbreaker, but annoying.

**Who should buy this**

- **Multi-cat households** where one cat prefers crunchy and another likes soft textures—this bag solves the "two different treat bags" problem
- **Owners of senior cats** with dental issues who still want to give treats but need softer options
- **People using treats for training** who want a low-calorie option that cats actually respond to

Skip it if you have a single cat with a clear texture preference and no interest in the other style—you'll be throwing away half the bag. Also skip if anyone in the house is sensitive to fish-based pet food smells.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these treats nutritionally complete or just snacks?**
A: Just snacks. They're formulated as a treat, not a meal replacement. Under 1.5 calories per treat and roughly 30% protein, but don't substitute these for regular food.

**Q: Can kittens eat these?**
A: Yes, but monitor portion sizes. The soft texture is actually easier for young kittens than hard crunchy treats. Start with one or two pieces per day.

**Q: How long does an opened bag stay fresh?**
A: Properly sealed in a cool, dry place, expect 3-4 weeks. The soft pieces degrade first—if they turn hard or brittle, they've gone stale.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You have a picky cat, multiple cats with different preferences, or want a low-calorie training treat that actually motivates your feline.

**Skip it if:** You need a single-texture treat, dislike strong fish aromas, or live in a hot climate without cool storage space.

The 4.8-star rating holds up. Temptations nailed the dual-texture concept here, and the low-calorie formulation makes it an easy daily option. It's not perfect—the smell and the clumping issue are real—but for most cat owners, this is a solid addition to the treat rotation.
