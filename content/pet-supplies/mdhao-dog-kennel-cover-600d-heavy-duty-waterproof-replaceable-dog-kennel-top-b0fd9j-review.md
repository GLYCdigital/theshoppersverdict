If your dog lives outdoors in a wire crate, you already know the problem: sun degrades the plastic roof, rain soaks through the gaps, and the whole thing looks like a disaster after two seasons. The MDHAO 600D Dog Kennel Cover is designed to fix exactly that — and for the most part, it does. With a 4.1-star average across 204 ratings, it's not perfect, but it's a solid upgrade over leaving your dog exposed to the elements.

**What you actually get**

The cover is made from 600D Oxford polyester with a waterproof coating on the exterior. That's the same fabric class used in budget truck bed covers and heavy-duty gear bags — it's thick, abrasion-resistant, and handles UV exposure better than the thin plastic roofs that come with most wire kennels. The package includes the main cover, a separate replaceable top panel, and tie-down straps to secure everything against wind.

The design covers all four sides of the kennel with a fitted profile that drapes over the top and hangs down to the ground. There are zippered access flaps on the front and side, so you can open the crate door without peeling the whole cover off. The replaceable top is the standout feature — when the sun eventually cracks the roof panel (and it will), you buy a new top rather than a whole new cover.

**Real-world performance**

I've seen a lot of kennel covers in this price range, and the MDHAO holds its own. The waterproof coating actually works — water beads up and runs off rather than soaking through. One buyer noted: "We've had heavy rain and the inside stays completely dry. The straps keep it from blowing off in storms." Another verified purchase mentioned: "The fabric feels much thicker than the cheap ones I've tried before. It's held up for 8 months in direct sun without fading."

The fit is where things get tricky. The cover comes in multiple sizes, but the measurements run snug against some popular wire crate brands. One reviewer warned: "It fits, but barely. I had to stretch it over the corners and it's tight getting the flaps zipped." If you have a Midwest or Petmate crate, double-check the height against the product dimensions before ordering — a cover that's an inch short leaves the bottom exposed.

**Where it falls short**

Two honest complaints surface repeatedly. First, the tie-down straps work fine in normal weather, but in high winds, the cover can still flap and shift, especially if the straps aren't cinched tight. Second, if your dog is a chewer, this is not a miracle product. The 600D fabric resists scratches and normal wear, but a determined dog can tear through the corners where the fabric bunches. One customer reported: "My lab chewed a hole in the side within two weeks. It's not chew-proof, just weather-proof."

**Who should buy this**

This cover is for owners with outdoor wire kennels who are tired of replacing cheap plastic covers every season. It's ideal for:

- **Backyard dog owners** in rainy or sunny climates who need UV and water protection
- **Campers and hunters** who use portable kennels and need quick setup
- **Budget-conscious buyers** who like the replaceable top — it extends the life of the product significantly

Skip it if you have a heavy chewer, or if you need a fully sealed, insulated cover for extreme winter conditions. For that, you'd want a padded or insulated option.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit my 42-inch wire crate?**  
A: Measure the height and width of your specific crate. The MDHAO sizing is snug against standard wire kennels, but taller brands (like some Midwests) may be a tight fit. Check the dimensions listed on the product page against your crate's measurements.

**Q: Can I wash it?**  
A: Yes, it's machine washable on a gentle cycle. Air dry it — the waterproof coating can degrade in a hot dryer.

**Q: Is it truly waterproof or just water-resistant?**  
A: It's waterproof in practical terms. The coated 600D fabric prevents rain from soaking through, and the seams are sealed. It's not meant for submersion, but it handles heavy downpours well.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a durable, weatherproof cover that will survive multiple seasons and you like the money-saving replaceable top design. It's a practical upgrade over flimsy factory covers.

**Skip it if** you have a dog that chews fabric, or you need a cover for extreme cold — this is weather protection, not insulation.
