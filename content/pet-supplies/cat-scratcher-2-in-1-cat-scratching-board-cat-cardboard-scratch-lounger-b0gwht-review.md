**Where It Falls Short**

Being honest: this is cardboard. It will not last forever. If you have a large cat — think Maine Coon or a heavy Siamese — the dimensions may feel cramped, and aggressive scratching will wear through the material faster than you'd like. Expect a lifespan of 2-4 months with daily use before the surface becomes too shredded to be effective. When that happens, you'll need to replace it. That's the tradeoff with any cardboard scratcher at this price.

Also, expect some cardboard debris. The scratching action naturally dislodges small bits of material. It's not excessive, but if you're someone who hates vacuuming, this will add to your routine. Place it on a hard floor or a mat if that bothers you.

**Who Should Buy This**

*Can this replace a vertical scratching post?* Not entirely. Some cats prefer stretching vertically. This satisfies horizontal and angled scratching, but if your cat already uses a vertical post, keep both.
