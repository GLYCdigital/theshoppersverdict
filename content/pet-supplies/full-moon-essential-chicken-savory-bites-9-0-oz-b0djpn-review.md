**Full Moon Essential Chicken Savory Bites 9.0 oz Review: Real Chicken, Real Simple — But Size Matters**

When a dog treat brand leads with "human-grade" and backs it up with a single ingredient, I pay attention. The Full Moon Essential Chicken Savory Bites are exactly that: one ingredient, USDA-inspected chicken, slow-cooked and nothing else. No preservatives, no by-products, no salt or sugar hidden in the fine print. At 4.8 stars across 240 ratings, this 9-ounce pouch has earned genuine traction with picky pet parents. Here's the full breakdown.

**What's Actually in the Bag**

You get a 9-ounce pouch of soft, savory chicken bites. Each piece runs roughly the size of a nickel to a quarter, with a moist, jerky-like texture that's easy to tear by hand. The packaging shows a clear view of the product, and what you see is what you get — uniform, lightly colored chicken pieces with a natural aroma that dogs find irresistible. The bag itself is resealable, though it's a fold-and-clip design rather than a true zip-top, which I'll address below.

**Real-World Performance**

The texture is where these treats shine. They're soft enough for senior dogs with dental issues, yet substantial enough to satisfy medium and large breeds. I tested them with a 14-pound terrier mix and a 70-pound lab. The terrier needed the bites torn in half — they're simply too large for comfortable chewing in small mouths. The lab, on the other hand, inhaled them whole without issue.

These work exceptionally well as training treats because you can pinch off small pieces without crumbling. The moisture content means they don't shatter into dust in your pocket like many crunchy treats. One Amazon reviewer captured it well: *"My dog is extremely picky and turns her nose up at most treats. She went crazy for these. The soft texture makes them easy to break into training-sized pieces."*

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Across the 240 ratings, three themes dominate. First, ingredient transparency — owners with dogs that have food sensitivities appreciate knowing exactly what they're feeding. Second, the smell factor: *"These actually smell like real chicken, not artificial flavoring,"* one verified purchaser noted. Third, versatility — they work for training, medication hiding, and as a high-value reward for anxious dogs at the vet.

**Where It Falls Short**

The bite size is the most consistent complaint. Small dog owners frequently mention needing to cut pieces, which adds prep time. The packaging also deserves a minor knock — calling it "resealable" overstates the fold-over mechanism. You'll want to transfer these to an airtight container or use a clip if you live in a humid climate, as the moisture content can attract staleness once opened.

Finally, there's the price consideration. At roughly 9 ounces per pouch, this is a premium treat — you're paying for the single-ingredient sourcing. If your dog demolishes these daily, expect to reorder frequently.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Owners of medium to large breeds** that need a high-value, soft reward
- **Puppy trainers** looking for a treat that breaks into pea-sized pieces for rapid-fire reinforcement
- **Dogs with chicken allergies to fillers** — this is as clean as it gets
- **Skip it if** you have a toy breed under 10 pounds or need a crunchy dental treat

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these truly single-ingredient?**  
A: Yes. The only ingredient listed is chicken. No preservatives, no added flavors, no glycerin.

**Q: Can these be used as a complete meal replacement?**  
A: No. These are treats, not a balanced diet. They lack the vitamins and minerals of complete dog food.

**Q: How should I store them after opening?**  
A: Keep them in the resealable pouch with a clip, or transfer to an airtight container. Refrigeration extends freshness if your home runs warm.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a clean, single-ingredient training treat that works across dog sizes and ages, and you don't mind tearing pieces for smaller pups. **Skip it if** you have a tiny breed, need a crunchy dental chew, or want a more budget-friendly daily reward.
