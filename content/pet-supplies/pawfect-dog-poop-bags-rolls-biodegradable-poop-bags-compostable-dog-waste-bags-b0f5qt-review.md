**What you're actually getting**

The Pawfect rolls come in a standard 9x13-inch size, which fits most hands comfortably. The bags are made from a cornstarch-based biodegradable compound — not the typical polyethylene that sits in landfills for centuries. Each roll runs about 15 bags, and the perforation points are reinforced so you don't get the frustrating "half-tear" that leaves you fumbling with a shredded edge.

**How it performs in the real world**

The standout feature here is thickness. At roughly 1.5 mil, these bags are noticeably beefier than the generic rolls you grab at the dollar store. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"I've had bags split on me before, but these held up even when my dog decided to go in a bramble patch. No punctures, no leaks."* That tracks — the material has a slight matte texture that gives you grip even when your hands are sweaty or it's drizzling outside.

The biodegradable claim deserves a closer look. Pawfect uses an additive that accelerates decomposition in landfill conditions, not just industrial composting facilities. That means these bags won't sit intact for 500 years like standard plastic. They're not fully compostable for your backyard pile — don't toss them in with your vegetable scraps — but they're a legitimate step up from conventional bags.

It fades quickly and doesn't transfer to your hands, but if you're sensitive to odors, you'll notice it on the first few uses.
