## Arm & Hammer for Pets Tartar Control Dental Mints: The Honest Take

Let's get one thing straight right now: **these are breath fresheners with a dental assist, not a substitute for brushing your dog's teeth.** If you're expecting a miracle cure for years of accumulated tartar, you'll be disappointed. But if you're looking for a daily treat that fights bad breath and slows down plaque formation, Arm & Hammer's Dental Mints deliver — for most dogs, most of the time.

That's why this product sits at a solid 4.2 out of 5 across 210 Amazon ratings. It does what it claims, with a few caveats worth knowing before you buy.

---

## What You Actually Get

The product arrives as a resealable bag of mint-shaped, green chewy treats. The texture is noticeably softer than typical dental chews — think dense marshmallow rather than rawhide. Each piece is sized for medium to large dogs, though smaller breeds can still manage them with a bit of effort.

The key ingredient story here is **baking soda** — Arm & Hammer's signature move. It's incorporated into the chew matrix, so as your dog gnaws, the baking soda mixes with saliva and works on neutralizing the volatile sulfur compounds that cause dog breath. Smart chemistry, honestly.

---

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the picture gets nuanced. Owners consistently report one big win: **dramatically fresher breath.** One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"I've tried everything for my golden's breath — these are the first treats that actually made a difference. His breath smells like mint, not dog, for hours after."*

That's the core value proposition, and it holds up. The baking soda mechanism works.

The tartar control claims are weaker. The soft texture means these mints don't provide the mechanical scraping action you'd get from a hard dental chew like Greenies or a raw bone. You're getting **mild abrasive action at best.** Established tartar won't budge. However, for dogs with relatively clean teeth, regular use does appear to slow new plaque formation — likely because the baking soda disrupts the bacterial biofilm before it hardens.

One thing I appreciate: the ingredient list is clean. No artificial colors, no added sugars, and each treat runs under 15 calories, making them viable for daily use even for weight-conscious pups.

---

## The Catch: Palatability Is a Coin Flip

Here's the honest downside — **dogs are divided on the mint flavor.** About as many reviewers report their dogs devouring these eagerly as report them sniffing and walking away.

One frustrated owner wrote: *"My lab will eat literally anything. These? He spit it out and looked at me like I insulted his ancestors."*

If your dog is picky, buy the smallest bag first. Don't commit to a bulk order until you know they'll actually eat them.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Can these replace brushing my dog's teeth?**
No. They complement brushing, not replace it. Think of them as a daily maintenance layer. You still need mechanical brushing or professional cleanings for real dental health.

**Q: How many should I give per day?**
One to two treats daily is the sweet spot. They're low-calorie, but they're not a meal replacement — adjust your dog's food portions if you're giving them consistently.

**Q: Are they safe for puppies or senior dogs?**
The texture is soft enough for most adult dogs, but very young puppies or seniors with missing teeth may struggle. Check with your vet if you're uncertain — the mint flavor can be strong for sensitive stomachs.
