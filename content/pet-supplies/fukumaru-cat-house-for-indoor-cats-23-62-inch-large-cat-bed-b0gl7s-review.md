Let's be clear about something upfront: the pet furniture market is flooded with "large" cat houses that barely fit a kitten. The FUKUMARU actually delivers on its 23.62-inch claim. I measured it. That matters if you've got a big boy at home.

**What's actually in the box**

You get the cat house frame (PP board panels), a plush faux-fur cushion, and a washable cover. The whole thing assembles into a cube-style hideaway with a circular entry on the front. The 23.62-inch dimensions refer to the overall length and depth — the interior is genuinely roomy enough for a 15-pound cat to stretch out, not curl into a ball.

The cover zips off completely, which is the feature that separates this from cheaper options. You'll appreciate that the first time your cat coughs up a hairball inside it.

**How it holds up in real use**

The PP board frame is denser than the flimsy cardboard you find in budget cat houses. It holds its shape when a cat launches onto the top — that's a common failure point for this category, and the FUKUMARU handles it fine. The plush fabric has a soft, almost suede-like texture that cats seem to gravitate toward.

One thing worth noting: assembly takes a few minutes of firm pressure. The panels snap together with plastic connectors, and you need to seat them properly or the frame feels loose. Not difficult, but not zero-effort either.

**What buyers consistently mention**

The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews reflects genuine satisfaction. The most repeated praise centers on the size and the washable cover. One reviewer noted, "Finally a cat bed my 18-pound Maine Coon can actually use without hanging off the edge." Another appreciated the practical side: "Being able to unzip and machine wash the cover is a lifesaver — and I've washed it four times already with zero shrinkage."

**Where it falls short**

The cushion is the weakest component. It's functional but thinner than the plush look suggests. Heavy cats will compress it almost immediately, so you'll likely buy a supplementary pad — which feels like an unnecessary extra expense at this price point.

Assembly frustration is real for some buyers. If you're not handy or patient, wrestling the panels into place can test your nerves. And the single color option — a neutral beige/gray tone — won't appeal to everyone.

**Who should buy this**

This is for owners of larger breeds or simply big cats who've been squeezed out of standard-size beds. It's also ideal if you're tired of throwing away entire cat houses because the cover can't be cleaned. Multi-cat households will find the size accommodates two smaller cats cozying up together.

Skip it if your cat prefers open beds over enclosed spaces, or if you're looking for something with aesthetic variety.

**FAQ**

*Will my cat actually use it?* Most cats take to enclosed spaces — they feel secure. If your cat has never used a hideaway before, place it near a window or somewhere they already nap. Add a familiar blanket inside for the first few days.

*How hard is cleaning?* The cover zips off and goes in the washing machine on a gentle cycle. Air dry it — the dryer can shrink the fabric.

*Is it really 23.62 inches?* Yes, and that's the outer dimension. The interior floor space is roughly 20 inches square, which still beats most competitors.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you have a large cat that deserves real space, you value washable components, or you've been burned by flimsy frames before.

**Skip it if** you're on a tight budget, your cat refuses enclosed beds, or you expect a cloud-like cushion straight out of the box.
