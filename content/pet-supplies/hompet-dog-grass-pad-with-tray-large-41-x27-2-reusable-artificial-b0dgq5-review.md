If you’ve ever cleaned urine off a balcony floor at 6 a.m., you already know why the **Hompet Dog Grass Pad with Tray Large** exists. This is a 41”x27” plastic tray paired with **two reusable artificial grass mats** — essentially a washable, non-disposable alternative to puppy pads. It’s a solid concept, and at 4.5 stars from 225 ratings, most buyers agree. But is it right for *your* dog? Let’s dig in.

## What’s Actually in the Box
You get three physical pieces: the plastic tray (roughly 41 inches long, 27 inches wide), and **two separate grass mats** that snap into the tray. The mats are made of a soft, tufted plastic grass with a drainage layer underneath. The tray has a low rim — about 2 inches — designed to catch splashes and hold the mat in place. The “two reusable” part is the key feature: one mat can be in use while the other air-dries after a rinse, which means no waiting around for a wet pad.

## Real-World Performance
The grass itself is surprisingly comfortable underfoot — not scratchy like some cheaper turf. Dogs seem to accept it quickly, especially puppies who are already used to grass outdoors. The drainage works: urine filters through the mat into the tray below, so the top surface doesn’t stay soggy. You empty the tray (it’s shallow, so it’s easy to tilt into a toilet or drain), then rinse the mat with a hose or in a bathtub.

The snap-on design is the standout. The mat stays tight — no bunching, no sliding around when a dog scratches after going. That’s a real problem with cheaper flat pads, and Hompet solved it.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise
The two-mat system gets the most love. One reviewer put it plainly: *“Having a spare mat is a lifesaver. I wash one, put the other down, and my dog never has to wait.”* Another praised the size: *“Finally something wide enough that my 60-pound lab doesn’t miss. The rim catches everything.”* Multiple reviewers also noted the grass holds up to repeated washing — one said they’d had it for three months with daily use and *“it still looks new.”*

## Where It Falls Short
The tray is **lightweight plastic** — durable enough, but it slides on smooth floors. If your dog is a runner, you’ll want a rubber mat underneath. Several reviewers mentioned this exact issue. Also, at 41 inches, it’s not a small footprint. If you’re putting this in a tight laundry room or a narrow balcony, measure twice. Finally, the grass mats are *not* machine washable — you have to rinse them by hand or with a hose. That’s fine for most, but if you were hoping to toss it in the washer, this isn’t that product.

## Who Should Buy This
- **Apartment dwellers** with a balcony or a large bathroom who want a grass-feel option without the mess of real sod.
- **Puppy owners** who are house-training and want a consistent surface that’s easy to clean.
- **Owners of senior dogs** who can’t hold it as long and need a quick, accessible spot indoors.

Skip it if you have a tiny space, a dog that likes to chew on plastic, or you’re looking for a fully disposable, zero-cleanup solution.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I clean the mats?**  
Rinse each mat with a hose or in the shower, using a mild soap if needed. Let it air dry completely before putting it back in the tray. The tray itself just wipes out or rinses.

**Q: Will this work for a large dog?**  
Yes — the 41”x27” surface fits most medium and large breeds. But a Great Dane or similarly huge dog may still hang off the edges. Check your dog’s standing footprint.

**Q: Is there a smell if I don’t clean it daily?**  
The drainage layer helps, but urine will sit in the tray. If you don’t empty it every day, odor will build up. Daily rinsing is the best way to keep it fresh.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a reusable, grass-feel pad that won’t bunch or leak, and you have the space for a 41” tray. **Skip it if** you need something portable, ultra-compact, or fully disposable.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The two-mat system, sturdy snap-on design, and easy drainage make this one of the better reusable grass pads on Amazon. Just budget a few extra minutes for daily rinsing, and grab a non-slip mat underneath.
