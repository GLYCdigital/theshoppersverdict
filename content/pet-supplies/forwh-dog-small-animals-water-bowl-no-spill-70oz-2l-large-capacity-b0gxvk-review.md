## FAQ

**Q: Is this bowl safe for small animals like guinea pigs or rabbits?**  
A: Yes, the low profile and 2L capacity work well for small pets. The floating disc prevents them from getting their heads wet, though you may want a smaller version if you have just one small pet — 70oz is a lot of water for a guinea pig.

**Q: How often do I need to clean it?**  
A: Every 2-3 days. The gap between the floating disc and bowl wall traps debris and can develop a biofilm if neglected. Dishwasher-safe, but hand washing is quicker.

**Q: Will the floating disc break if my dog plays with it?**  
A: The disc is durable but not indestructible. If your dog is a chewer, this isn't the right bowl — they could dislodge or damage the disc.
