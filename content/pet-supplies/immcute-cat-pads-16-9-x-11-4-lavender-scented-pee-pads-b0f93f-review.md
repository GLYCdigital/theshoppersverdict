The IMMCUTE Cat Pads are different — not because the lavender is overpowering, but because the absorbency actually holds up. At 16.9" x 11.4", these are sized specifically for cats, small dogs, and confined spaces, not as a one-size-fits-all floor protector.

Here's what you're actually getting: a 5-layer pad with a rapid-absorbing core, a leak-proof polyethylene backing, and a lavender-infused top layer. The dimensions matter — they're compact enough to fit inside most standard litter boxes, crates, and carriers. No more trimming oversized puppy pads to fit your cat's space, which inevitably leaves fuzzy edges that shred.

**Real-world performance**

The absorbency is the headline. The core locks liquid away quickly, which keeps paws drier and prevents tracking wet paw prints across your floor. Multiple buyers noted the pads handle several urinations before needing replacement — one reviewer wrote, "My senior cat has accidents daily and these actually last a full day without leaking. The lavender smell is subtle and I don't smell ammonia at all." That's the key metric: no ammonia breakthrough.

The lavender scent is genuinely effective at masking odors, but it's worth noting it's not faint. It's noticeable immediately out of the package and lingers. For most people, that's the point. But if you or your pet are sensitive to fragrances, this could be a dealbreaker. A handful of reviewers mentioned their cats avoided the pads initially because of the scent — though most reported their pets acclimated within a few days.

**Where it falls short**

The size is the biggest limitation. At under 17 inches, these are not puppy pads. If you have a large dog, a rabbit enclosure, or a senior cat that needs a generous target zone, you'll be replacing these frequently. This is a targeted solution for small pets and tight spaces, not a general-purpose pad.

The scent intensity is the other honest tradeoff. One reviewer captured it well: "Great pads, but the lavender is strong right out of the bag. Air them out for a few minutes before using if your cat is picky." That's practical advice worth following.

**Q: How often should I change them?**

**Q: Is the lavender scent safe for cats?**
