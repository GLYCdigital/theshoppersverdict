Bathing a dog indoors is a messy logistical nightmare — unless you've got a dedicated tub. The Goderewild 7.4 Gal/28L Collapsible Pet Bathtub tries to solve that problem by giving you a portable, storable washing station that doesn't require a permanent fixture in your bathroom. At 4.3 stars from 215 ratings, it's clearly working for a lot of owners. But is it working for *your* dog? Let's dig in.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the collapsible tub itself, a carrying strap, and a drain hose attachment. The tub measures roughly 27.5" x 18" x 9.5" when fully expanded — large enough for small to medium breeds, but you're not fitting a Golden Retriever in here. The walls are made of a thick, reinforced PVC that holds its shape when filled, and the interior has a subtle textured pattern meant to give your dog traction so they don't panic-slide around.

The standout feature is the bottom drain plug. You attach the included hose, pull the plug, and water flows straight into a bucket or floor drain. No more wrestling a 40-pound wet animal while trying to tip a 7-gallon tub without soaking the bathroom floor.

## Performance and Real-World Use

I tested this tub in an apartment bathroom with a 15-pound terrier mix. Assembly is genuinely zero-effort — it unfolds and locks into shape in about five seconds. The walls feel sturdy when filled; there's a slight flex if you push hard, but no bowing or collapse under the water weight.

The non-slip texture does help, though it's not miraculous. My dog still scrambled a bit when the water hit, but she had better footing than she would on bare tile. The drain system works exactly as advertised — the plug seals tight during the bath, and water empties in about 30 seconds once you pull it.

Where it stumbles: the base is smooth PVC with no rubber feet. On wet tile, the whole tub can slide a couple inches when your dog shifts weight. It's not dangerous, but it's annoying — and it means you should dry the floor first or place a towel underneath.

## What Owners Consistently Praise

Scrolling through the ratings, the recurring theme is convenience. One reviewer put it plainly: *"This thing folds up so flat I forget I own it until bath day. Then it takes me 10 minutes total including drying the dog."* Another noted, *"Finally something that doesn't require me to kneel over a slippery tub edge for 20 minutes. The height is perfect for my back."* The portability angle gets repeated love — multiple owners mention using it for camping, grooming appointments, or as a DIY paw-washing station after muddy walks.

## Where It Falls Short

The capacity is the biggest caveat. At 7.4 gallons, this is a small-dog tub. Owners with breeds over 20-25 pounds report cramped baths and splash-heavy sessions. One reviewer said, *"My 30-pound beagle fit but looked miserable and water went everywhere. Wish I'd sized up."* If you have a medium or large dog, skip this and look for a 30+ gallon option instead.

The chemical smell out of the box is also a real thing. It's not overpowering, but it's noticeable — and it lingers on your dog's coat for the first couple of baths if you don't air it out beforehand.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Apartment dwellers** with small to medium dogs who need a bathing solution that doesn't require permanent plumbing
- **Outdoor enthusiasts** who want a portable washing station for camping, hiking, or beach trips
- **Owners with back issues** who need a raised tub that doesn't require kneeling on the floor
- **Multi-pet households** with dogs under 20 pounds who need a quick, contained rinse station

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this in the shower or bathtub?**
Yes, but note that the tub is 9.5 inches tall — it fits inside a standard shower stall, though you'll want to place it on a non-slip mat to prevent the base from sliding.

**Q: Does the drain hose work with a standard bathroom drain?**
The included hose is about 3 feet long with a standard 1-inch opening. It fits most floor drains and can be directed into a bucket or toilet. It won't thread onto a standard sink faucet, so plan accordingly.

**Q: How long does it take to fully dry before folding?**
Wipe it down with a towel, then let it air dry for 2-3 hours. Folding a damp tub can trap moisture and cause mildew, so don't rush it.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you have a dog under 25 pounds, want a genuinely portable bathing solution, and value storage efficiency. The fold-flat design, working drain, and sturdy build justify the price for small-dog owners who bathe at home.

**Skip it if** you have a larger breed, need something for multiple big dogs, or want a permanent tub replacement. This is a portable convenience tool, not a full bathing station.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Great for its intended niche, held back by slippery feet and a size limit that excludes medium and large breeds.
