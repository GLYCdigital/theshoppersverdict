**The Yuposl Automatic Cat Feeder: Does It Earn Its Keep on Your Counter?**

If you've ever been woken up at 5:45 AM by a cat who believes breakfast is a constitutional right, you already know why automatic feeders exist. The Yuposl Automatic Cat Feeder promises scheduled meals, portion control, and a full night of uninterrupted sleep. At 4.3 stars from 215 Amazon ratings, it's clearly doing something right — but the question is whether it'll work for *your* cat and *your* schedule.

**What's Actually in the Box**

The Yuposl arrives fully assembled. You get the feeder unit with a 4-liter hopper (that's roughly 3-4 pounds of standard kibble), a stainless steel bowl — a genuinely nice touch since plastic bowls can harbor bacteria and cause chin acne — and a user manual that's surprisingly readable. No phone app, no Wi-Fi setup, no subscription nonsense. This is a dumb device in the best way: it does one job and does it without requiring you to troubleshoot a network connection at 7 AM.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

Programming the Yuposl takes about two minutes. You set up to four meal times per day, and for each meal you select between 1 and 10 portions. The portion sizes aren't adjustable by weight — you're locked into the dispenser's preset serving size, which is roughly a tablespoon per portion. That's fine for most cats, but if your vet has prescribed a specific gram amount, you'll want to weigh what comes out and adjust accordingly.

The rotating drum mechanism is where this feeder shines or stumbles. On standard kibble — think Purina One, Hill's Science Diet, or Blue Buffalo — it dispenses reliably. The 4.3-star average reflects that most buyers have zero issues. But here's the honest truth: **irregular kibble shapes or anything over about 12mm in diameter can jam the mechanism.** I've seen this complaint echoed in multiple reviews. If your cat eats a chunky, freeze-dried raw kibble, measure the pieces before you commit.

The battery backup is a legitimate lifesaver. Three D-cell batteries keep the schedule running through power outages, and the unit will alert you when the batteries get low. That's a feature you don't appreciate until a storm knocks your power out and your cat is still fed on time.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The most repeated praise centers on durability and simplicity. One reviewer noted, *"I've had this for six months and it hasn't jammed once — my previous feeder died after three weeks."* Another mentioned, *"Setup was genuinely five minutes, and my cat figured out the sound of the dispenser within a day."* The stainless steel bowl is also a frequently cited plus — easy to clean, dishwasher-safe, and doesn't hold odors.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be direct about the limitations. **There's no app, no Wi-Fi, and no camera.** If you're looking for a feeder you can control from your phone while at work, this isn't it. The Yuposl is strictly a set-it-and-forget-it device.

The portion size lockout is another real constraint. You can't adjust the volume of a single portion — you're stuck with the preset amount, which means a 10-portion meal is the maximum you can dispense. For larger cats or multiple cats sharing one feeder, that could be a dealbreaker.

And the kibble compatibility issue bears repeating. If you feed a grain-free brand with large, oddly shaped pieces, budget for potential jams. The manual suggests using standard kibble, and it means it.

**Who Should Buy This**

This feeder is ideal for:
- **Busy professionals** who need consistent meal times during long workdays
- **Weekend travelers** who want a reliable backup for a pet sitter
- **Cat owners with standard kibble** who want portion control without smart-home complexity
- **Multi-cat households** that need a secondary feeding station to prevent one cat from hogging all the food

Skip it if:
- You need **app-based control or remote monitoring** — this feeder has neither
- Your cat eats **large or oddly shaped kibble** — measure your food first
- You need **precise gram-level portioning** — the preset portions aren't adjustable

**FAQ: Three Questions Every Shopper Asks**

**Q: Can this feeder dispense wet food?**
No. This is designed for dry kibble only. Wet food will clog the mechanism and create a bacteria risk.

**Q: How long does the battery backup actually last?**
Most buyers report 2-3 months on a fresh set of D-cell batteries, depending on how often the dispenser rotates. The feeder runs on AC power first and switches to battery only during outages.

**Q: Will my cat figure out how to break into it?**
The hopper lid locks securely, and the dispensing opening is too small for most adult cats to reach into. That said, determined cats can sometimes knock the unit over — it's lightweight at under 5 pounds, so secure it against a wall or cabinet if your cat is rambunctious.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, no-frills automatic feeder for standard dry kibble and you don't need smartphone connectivity. At this price point, the battery backup and stainless steel bowl are genuinely competitive features.

**Skip it if** you require app control, precise portion tuning, or your cat's food won't fit through the dispenser's drum.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, dependable workhorse with honest limitations. It won't blow your mind, but it'll feed your cat on time, every time.
