Here's the thing about dog treats: most of them are either boring kibble-like pellets or greasy jerky strips that leave orange residue on your fingers. The Thoughtfully Pets Dog Cookie Gift Set sits in a different category entirely — these are cookies that look like you'd serve them at a human baby shower, not something you'd toss in a crate for a training reward.

I've looked at the product images closely, and the hand-decorated aspect isn't marketing fluff. Each cookie shows visible piping work — colored icing borders, little decorative dots, actual effort. That matters if you're buying these as a gift rather than just daily training fuel.

The set arrives in a clear, gift-ready box that shows the cookies off before you even open it. Based on buyer photos and descriptions, you're getting roughly 12-16 cookies depending on the specific set variation, each individually decorated. The base is a crunchy biscuit — think shortbread density, not Milk-Bone hardness.

## Performance and Build Quality

The crunch factor is legit. These are baked, not dehydrated, so they snap cleanly rather than requiring your dog to gnaw through rubbery protein. That makes them suitable for medium and large breeds — a 40-pound Lab can crunch through one in about 30 seconds. Small breeds or senior dogs with dental issues may struggle with the density.

Ingredient-wise, the base is a standard wheat flour and peanut butter recipe. Nothing exotic, but nothing alarming either. The real consideration is the icing — it's a sugar-based royal frosting. It's fine for occasional treats, but if your dog has diabetes or you're strict about sugar intake, these aren't the daily option.

## FAQ

**Are these safe for puppies?**
Yes, but monitor them. The crunch is hard enough that puppies under six months with baby teeth may struggle. Break them into smaller pieces.

**How long do they stay fresh?**
Buyers report they stay fresh for 2-3 weeks if kept in the sealed box. The icing can absorb humidity, so don't leave them out in a damp environment.

**Can I freeze them?**
Yes. Multiple buyers freeze half the box to extend shelf life. Thaw at room temperature for 10 minutes before serving.
