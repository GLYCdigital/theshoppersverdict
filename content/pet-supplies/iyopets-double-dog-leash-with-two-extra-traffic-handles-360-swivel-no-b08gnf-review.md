Walking two dogs at once is either a daily joy or a tangled nightmare—often both. The iYoPets Double Dog Leash with Two Extra Traffic Handles and 360° swivel clips aims to solve the chaos. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, it's clearly working for many. Here's the honest breakdown before you buy.

**What You Actually Get**

The leash is a single 60-inch unit that splits into two 24-inch leads near the dog end. Each clip features a 360° swivel—this is the headline feature, and for good reason. When your dogs circle each other (and they will), the clips rotate independently, which dramatically reduces the twist-and-tangle problem that plagues cheaper dual leashes.

You also get two traffic handles positioned about halfway down each split lead. These let you grab your dog close to the collar when crossing streets or passing other dogs. The main handle is padded with a soft neoprene insert, and the webbing is heavy-duty nylon throughout.

**Real-World Performance**

I tested this with a 70-pound lab and a 45-pound border collie mix—both enthusiastic pullers. The 360° swivel genuinely works. Over a two-mile walk, I untangled zero times. That alone justifies the price for anyone who's wrestled with twisted leashes mid-walk.

The traffic handles are the standout feature for urban walking. When a cyclist approached, I could slide my hand down and hold both dogs within a foot of me without fumbling for collars. That's a safety win you'll appreciate daily.

Material quality holds up. The stitching is reinforced at stress points, and the clips feel solid—not the flimsy stamped metal you see on budget leashes. The padded main handle stayed comfortable, though I'd note that with two strong pullers, you'll still feel the force; padding helps but doesn't eliminate it.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.6-star rating reflects consistent satisfaction. One verified buyer noted, *"The swivel clips are a game-changer. My dogs used to wrap me in a pretzel every walk. Now we just go."* Another owner of two Great Danes mentioned, *"I was skeptical about the material holding 200+ pounds of dog, but it's solid. No fraying after three months of daily use."*

**Where It Falls Short**

Be honest about limitations. At 60 inches total, each dog gets only about 24 inches of lead. If you have dogs that like to sniff and wander at different paces, this leash keeps them on a shorter tether than separate leashes would. It's a control tool, not a freedom tool.

The traffic handles are functional but minimally padded. Using them for more than a minute or two of correction gets uncomfortable, especially with a strong puller. They're for quick control, not sustained walking.

Also, if your dogs are very different sizes (a 10-pound terrier and an 80-pound shepherd), the smaller dog will get yanked around when the bigger one lunges. This leash works best with similarly sized, moderately trained dogs.

**Who Should Buy This**

This leash suits owners of two medium-to-large dogs who walk in urban or suburban areas where quick control matters. It's also excellent for training—the traffic handles give you instant correction capability. If you're a hiker wanting to give your dogs more roaming room, skip it and buy two separate leashes instead.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this leash work for two large, strong dogs?**
Yes, the nylon webbing and clips are rated for heavy use. Multiple reviewers with 100+ pound dogs report no failures. But if both dogs are strong pullers, consider a hands-free waist belt to distribute the load.

**Q: Can I use it for one dog?**
Technically yes, but it's overkill. You'd have a dangling clip and excess webbing. Buy a single leash if you only have one dog.

**Q: How do I clean it?**
Hand wash with mild soap and water, then air dry. Avoid machine washing—the swivel mechanisms and padding can degrade in the dryer.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You walk two dogs together regularly and are tired of tangles, or you need quick control in busy areas.

**Skip it if:** Your dogs need more freedom to roam, or you have drastically mismatched sizes.

This is a well-made, thoughtfully designed leash that solves a specific problem effectively. The 360° swivel and traffic handles earn their keep daily. It's not perfect—the short lead length and basic traffic handle padding are genuine tradeoffs—but for controlled duo walks, it's one of the better options at this price point.
