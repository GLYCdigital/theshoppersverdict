This 5 oz bag has earned a solid 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, and after digging through the feedback, the consensus is clear: these soft-baked treats deliver exactly what they promise.

## What You Actually Get

The 5 oz bag contains soft, bite-sized treats that look like miniature golden discs — about the diameter of a quarter and roughly a quarter-inch thick. As shown in the product image, they're baked, not raw or freeze-dried, giving them a slightly crumbly exterior with a pliable, moist interior. The ingredient list is refreshingly short: chicken, oat flour, and a few other recognizable components. No corn, no wheat, no soy, no artificial preservatives — just straightforward soft-baked goodness.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The texture is the star here. These treats bend without breaking, tear easily for smaller portions, and don't shatter into a million crumbs like hard biscuits do. That makes them genuinely useful for training — you can break off a quarter of a treat without making a mess, which is something you can't say for most crunchy options.

For senior dogs or pups with dental sensitivities, the softness is a game-changer. One reviewer noted, "My 14-year-old beagle has lost most of her teeth and this is the only treat she can actually enjoy now." Another owner of a picky Shih Tzu reported, "She spits out anything crunchy but devours these."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 229 ratings, a few themes emerge. The soft texture is the number one reason people buy these, and it doesn't disappoint. The ingredient quality also gets consistent shout-outs — pet owners who scrutinize labels feel good about feeding these daily. The size is another plus: small enough for training treats without being so tiny that you lose them in your pocket.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be straight about the downsides. The 5 oz bag is small — a medium or large breed dog will power through this in a few days of regular treating. If you're using these for training multiple times a day, you'll be reordering within a week. That's not a quality issue, but it is a value consideration.

Some dogs also just prefer crunch. The soft texture is polarizing — if your dog is used to hard biscuits, they might sniff these and walk away. One reviewer mentioned, "My lab loves them, but my border collie refuses to touch anything that isn't crunchy." You won't know until you try.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy these if:** You have a senior dog, a puppy with a sensitive mouth, or a picky eater who prefers soft textures. You also want a treat with clean ingredients made in the USA. The 5 oz size is perfect for testing the waters before committing to a bigger bag.

**Skip these if:** You have a large breed that goes through treats fast and you're on a tight budget — you'll need to buy multiple bags at once. Also skip if your dog is a crunchy-biscuit loyalist.
