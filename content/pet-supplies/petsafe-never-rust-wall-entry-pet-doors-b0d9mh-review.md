If you're tired of replacing flimsy plastic pet doors every 18 months, the PetSafe Never Rust Wall Entry is the last one you'll likely buy. This is the heavy-duty option that actually lives up to its name, but it comes with an installation caveat you need to understand before committing.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the assembled pet door with an aluminum frame, a clear vinyl flap, a mounting frame for the exterior side, and the screws. The tunnel depth adjusts from 5.5 to 7.25 inches, which fits standard 2x4 and 2x6 wall construction. As shown in the product image, the design is clean and low-profile — no bulky plastic trim sticking out.

The critical spec is the aluminum frame. Unlike the PVC frames on cheaper doors, this won't warp in summer heat or crack in winter cold. The twin flaps overlap with a magnetic center seal, which is the difference between a drafty hole in your wall and a properly insulated entry.

## Real-World Performance

The magnetic seal is the standout feature. One buyer noted, "I installed this in my mudroom leading to the garage, and I can't feel any air coming through the seam. My previous plastic door let in cold air constantly." That's the experience most owners report — the flaps snap shut cleanly and stay closed even in windy conditions.

The vinyl flap material is thick enough that most dogs learn to push through it within a day or two. For smaller pets, the flap weight is noticeable but manageable. Cats under 10 pounds may struggle initially, so factor that in if you have a small feline.

The "Never Rust" promise is backed by the aluminum construction. Even in coastal environments with salt air, this frame won't corrode. That's a legitimate durability advantage over steel-framed alternatives.

## Where It Falls Short

The installation is the real hurdle. This is not a "cut a hole and slap it in" project. You need to:

- Cut a precise opening in your wall (template included)
- Build a wooden frame inside the wall cavity to support the door
- Secure the mounting frame properly to prevent moisture infiltration

A buyer put it plainly: "Took me four hours and a trip to the hardware store for lumber. If you're not handy, budget for a professional installer." That's fair warning. The instructions are clear, but they assume basic carpentry skills.

Also note: there's no built-in lock. The door comes with a simple slide latch for the flap, but for security when you're away, you'll want the separate PetSafe locking bolt accessory. That's an extra purchase most buyers don't anticipate.

The size range is another consideration. The small size fits pets up to 15 pounds, medium up to 30, large up to 45. Measure your pet's chest width, not their weight — a bulky dog can get stuck in the opening even if they're within the weight range.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Homeowners with permanent walls** — this door is meant to be a permanent fixture, not a rental-friendly solution
- **Owners in extreme climates** — the insulation and seal quality justify the higher price point
- **People who've replaced cheap doors before** — if you're on your second or third budget pet door, the math works in favor of buying once

Skip it if you're renting, if you need a dog door for a sliding glass door, or if you're not comfortable with basic construction work.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this door be installed in a door instead of a wall?**
A: Yes, if the door is at least 1.75 inches thick. For hollow-core interior doors, you'll need to reinforce the area around the cutout.

**Q: Does the flap keep out rain and insects?**
A: The magnetic seal does a solid job with both. The flaps seal tightly enough that mosquitoes and flies rarely get through. Heavy wind-driven rain can still sneak in around the edges, so proper exterior caulking is essential.

**Q: How do I choose the right size?**
A: Measure your pet's height at the shoulder and add 2 inches for the opening height. Then measure their widest chest point and add 2 inches for width. When in doubt, size up — a slightly large door is better than one your pet refuses to use.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you own your home, want a pet door that will outlast your dog, and you're willing to invest the installation effort. The aluminum frame, magnetic seal, and adjustable tunnel make this the most durable option in its class.

**Skip it if** you're renting, need a temporary solution, or want a quick weekend project. The installation complexity and lack of an included lock are genuine limitations.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — loses half a star for the installation difficulty and the separate lock accessory. Everything else about this door is genuinely excellent.
