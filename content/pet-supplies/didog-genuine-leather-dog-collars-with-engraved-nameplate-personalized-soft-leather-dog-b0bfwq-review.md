### The Quick Take

A leather collar is one of those purchases that feels like a "grown-up" decision for your dog. The Didog Genuine Leather Collar with Engraved Nameplate aims to deliver that premium feel without the boutique price tag. After digging through 220 ratings and analyzing the build, here's the honest breakdown: it's a solid mid-tier collar that nails the personalization aspect but has a couple of quirks you should know about before you click "buy."

### What You Actually Get

The package is straightforward: one leather collar with a pre-attached, laser-engraved stainless steel nameplate. The plate is riveted on—not just stitched—which is a meaningful durability upgrade. You're getting a solid brass buckle and a D-ring that's welded, not just bent into shape. The leather itself is full-grain, which means it will develop a patina over time rather than peel or flake like bonded leather.

One thing the product images don't fully convey: the leather is stiffer than you might expect out of the box. It's not uncomfortable, but it will take a few days of wear to break in and mold to your dog's neck shape.

### Real-World Performance

This is where the Didog collar earns its 4.4-star rating. The engraved nameplate is the standout feature—it's crisp, legible, and won't rub off. Multiple reviewers specifically noted this, with one saying, *"The engraving is so clear, I get compliments on it every walk."* Another owner of a heavy puller reported, *"The D-ring hasn't budged after six months of daily walks."*

The leather holds up well to water and mud, though it's not fully waterproof. If your dog swims regularly, you'll want to let it air dry rather than hit it with a hair dryer, which can crack the leather.

### Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides:

- **Sizing runs snug.** If your dog is between sizes, size up. The collar doesn't have much adjustment room—about 2 inches—so a "medium" for a dog with a 16-inch neck will be tight if your dog is closer to 17 inches.
- **The leather smell is strong initially.** It's a genuine leather scent, not chemical, but some sensitive noses (human and canine) might find it overpowering for the first week.
- **The buckle is polished brass, not antique.** If you're going for a rugged, distressed look, this collar reads more "classic" than "rough." The brass will tarnish slightly over time, which some owners love and others find annoying.

### Who Should Buy This

- **The personalization enthusiast:** If you want your dog's name and your phone number permanently etched in, this is the best-value option in this price range.
- **City dogs and casual walkers:** This is a great everyday collar for dogs who aren't pulling constantly or swimming daily.
- **Gift givers:** A personalized leather collar with a nameplate is a genuinely thoughtful gift for a new puppy owner or a friend who just adopted a rescue.

**Skip it if:** You have a heavy puller who needs a heavy-duty harness-style collar, or if you want a collar that can go swimming daily without special care.

### FAQ

**Q: Does the nameplate come with the collar, or do I buy it separately?**
A: It comes included and pre-installed. You just fill in the engraving text when you order. No assembly required.

**Q: How many lines of text can I engrave?**
A: Typically two lines—name on top, phone number below. The text is about 1/8 inch tall and very readable.

**Q: Is this collar safe for puppies?**
A: Yes, but only for puppies over 6 months. The leather is substantial and might be too heavy for a tiny puppy's neck. For a young puppy, wait until they're done growing to avoid buying twice.

**Rating: 4.2/5** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
