## What You Actually Get

The package includes the frame, mesh panels, and a super-large entry door — the headline feature here. This isn't the standard 12x12-inch cat flap you find on most enclosures. As one buyer noted, "I can actually get in there to clean it without contorting my body." That door is a game-changer for anyone who's wrestled with a cramped catio before.

The frame is powder-coated steel tubing, and the mesh is a thick PVC-coated wire that withstands scratching. Assembly is tool-free — panels snap together with connectors. The product images show a spacious multi-tier setup, but be aware: **the shelves, ramps, and hammocks shown in those pictures are sold separately.** Factor that into your budget before ordering.

## Real-World Performance

Owners consistently praise the build quality for the price point. The mesh holds up to determined claws, and the frame feels sturdier than the typical Amazon catio. The enclosure is genuinely roomy — most cats get plenty of vertical and horizontal space to move.

But there are honest tradeoffs. The included ground stakes are on the flimsy side. Multiple reviewers mention adding sandbags or bricks to the base after a windy day shifted the frame. If you live somewhere breezy, plan for that from day one.

The mesh floor is another consideration. On grass, it's fine — waste breaks down naturally. On concrete or a deck, you'll want a tarp or tray underneath, or cleaning becomes a hassle. One reviewer put it plainly: "Great for supervised play, but I'd never leave my cat out here unsupervised on a hot day — it heats up fast."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

- **The door size**: Repeatedly called out as the deciding factor. Being able to reach in and refresh water bowls or scoop litter without fully entering is a quality-of-life win.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the stakes and the floor issue, the biggest complaint is the extra cost. The base unit is functional but bare. Cats want vertical space, and the accessories to provide that aren't cheap. You're looking at the enclosure plus another $40–60 for shelves and ramps to make it truly enriching.

Also, this is a "supervised play" enclosure, not a permanent catio. The mesh is strong but not predator-proof against determined dogs or raccoons. Don't leave cats out overnight.

## Who Should Buy This
