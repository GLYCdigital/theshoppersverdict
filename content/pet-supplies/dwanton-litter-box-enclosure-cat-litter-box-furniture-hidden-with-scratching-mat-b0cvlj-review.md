**If you're tired of looking at a plastic litter box sitting in your living room, the DWANTON enclosure offers a genuinely decent solution — with a few caveats worth knowing before you buy.**

I've tested my share of "hidden litter box" furniture, and most of it falls into two camps: overpriced particleboard that wobbles, or flimsy fabric tents that your cat shreds in a week. The DWANTON sits somewhere in the middle — it's a real piece of furniture with real limitations.

## What You Actually Get

The box arrives flat-packed. Inside, you'll find the MDF panels with a laminated wood-grain finish (the walnut color looks surprisingly close to actual wood from a few feet away), a clear plastic flap for the side entry, hinges, and the star feature: a sisal scratching mat that mounts on top.

As shown in the product image, the enclosure measures roughly 23" L x 17" W x 20" H. That's compact enough to fit beside a couch or under a console table, but you need to measure your existing litter box before ordering. A standard 20-inch box slides in fine, but anything with higher walls or a hood won't fit through the side opening.

## Real-World Performance

The side-entry design is the smartest part of this unit. Cats enter through the flap, which means they can't launch litter straight out the front like they do with open-top boxes. The clear flap also lets your cat see inside before entering — a detail that matters more than you'd think, especially for nervous cats.

The scratching mat on top earns its keep. It's attached with screws, so it stays put, and it gives your cat a vertical scratching surface that's actually at a comfortable height. Multiple buyers mention their cats gravitate to it immediately. One reviewer noted: *"My cat used to scratch the corner of my sofa every morning. This mat has completely redirected that behavior."*

The wood-grain MDF is sturdier than I expected for the price point. It doesn't wobble, and the top can hold a small plant or a stack of books without flexing. The finish wipes clean easily — important when litter dust settles on every surface within three feet of the box.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**Assembly is fiddly.** The cam locks and dowels require patience, and a few reviewers report that pre-drilled holes don't line up perfectly. You'll need a screwdriver and maybe 30 minutes of your evening. It's not hard, but it's not fun either.

**Odor control is average.** There are no ventilation holes in the back panel. If you use clumping litter and scoop daily, you'll be fine. But if you're someone who lets it go a couple days, opening the enclosure will hit you with a concentrated blast of ammonia. This is an enclosure, not an air filtration system.

**The interior is tight.** If your cat is a digger who kicks litter everywhere inside the box, that litter will pile up against the interior walls. You'll need to pull the box out to clean — there's no slide-out tray or easy-access door on the side.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have a standard-size litter box, a cat who likes to scratch furniture, and you want something that doesn't scream "cat toilet" in your living space. It's also a solid pick for small apartments where you can't hide the litter box in a laundry room.

**Skip it if:** You have a Maine Coon or any large-breed cat, you use a jumbo litter box, or you're hoping this will solve odor problems. Those needs require a bigger enclosure with active ventilation.

## FAQ

**Will my cat actually use it?**
Most cats adapt within a few days, especially if you place the enclosure where their old box was. Leave the flap open for the first day or two so they can investigate. If your cat is elderly or has mobility issues, the 6-inch step-up into the side opening might be a hurdle — consider a ramp.

**Can I fit a top-entry litter box inside?**
No. The side opening is only about 8 inches tall, and the interior height is roughly 18 inches. Top-entry boxes with their lids won't fit. Stick with a standard open pan or a low-sided box.

**Is the scratching mat replaceable?**
Yes, it's a standard sisal mat screwed into the top. You can order a replacement sisal mat and cut it to size when the original wears out, which typically takes 8–12 months with regular use.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The DWANTON Litter Box Enclosure does exactly what it promises: it hides the litter box and gives your cat a place to scratch. It's not perfect — assembly requires patience, odor control is basic, and large cats won't fit — but for standard-size cats and standard-size boxes, it's one of the better-looking, more functional options in this price range.

**Buy it if** you want to reclaim your living space from the litter box and your cat fits the size constraints. **Skip it if** you need serious odor control or have a large-breed cat. For everyone else, this is a solid, honest purchase that should last a few years with normal use.
