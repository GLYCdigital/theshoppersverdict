If you've ever watched a cat turn its nose up at a $2.50 can of "premium" pâté, you know the struggle. Purina Fancy Feast Delights with Cheddar Grilled Chicken cuts through that drama with a simple formula: visible shredded meat in a glossy gravy, plus a cheese twist that seems to flip a switch in even the fussiest felines. With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, this isn't a fringe favorite — it's a proven crowd-pleaser.

**What's actually in the can**

Each 3-ounce can delivers what the name promises: grilled chicken in a savory gravy, finished with cheddar cheese. The texture is the headline here. Unlike the uniform pâté or flaked styles, this one has distinct, shreddable chicken pieces suspended in a medium-thick gravy. It pours out easily and doesn't require mashing or mixing. As one buyer put it, "My cat goes absolutely nuts for this — she licks the gravy first, then comes back for the chicken chunks." That two-phase eating pattern is a good sign; it means the gravy and the meat are both doing their job.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The ingredient list isn't going to win any awards for "human-grade" purity — chicken, liver, meat by-products, and cheddar cheese make up the core. But for a mainstream wet food at this price point, it delivers consistent quality batch after batch. The cans are reliably filled, the gravy has a consistent viscosity, and the aroma is strong but not off-putting (your cat will disagree — but that's a good thing).

In real-world testing, the biggest behavioral win is how this food handles the "gravy-first" cats. The liquid portion is substantial enough to keep cats hydrated, which is a genuine health benefit for cats that don't drink enough water. The 3-ounce size is also spot-on for a single feeding — no Tupperware Tetris with half-empty cans in the fridge.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The picky-eater redemption stories dominate the reviews. "I have a 14-year-old tortie who's refused every wet food for two years," one reviewer wrote. "She cleaned this bowl in under three minutes." Another noted, "The cheddar isn't overwhelming — it just adds a richness that makes the chicken taste better." Multiple reviewers also highlighted the value: "Cheaper than the vet-prescribed food my cat was on, and she actually eats this one."

**Where it falls short**

This isn't a universal solution. The cheddar cheese and by-product ingredients are potential triggers for cats with food sensitivities. If your cat has a history of digestive upset or a confirmed poultry allergy, this is a hard pass. The gravy-heavy consistency also means mess — several buyers mentioned splatter on the floor or walls when their cats ate with too much enthusiasm. And if you're looking for a grain-free or limited-ingredient diet, this won't fit the bill.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the owner of a picky eater who needs a reliable, affordable option that's more exciting than standard pâté. It's also a strong choice for multi-cat households where one cat is a slow eater and another is a vacuum — the 3-ounce cans make portion control simple. If you're transitioning a kitten to wet food, the shreds are easy to chew and the flavor profile is generally well-received.

No — it's subtle, more of a savory richness than an in-your-face cheese taste. Most cats don't seem to register it as "cheese" at all, just "more delicious."

*How many cans come in a case?*
