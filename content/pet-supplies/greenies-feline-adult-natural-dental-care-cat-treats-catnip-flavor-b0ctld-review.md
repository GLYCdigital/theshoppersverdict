## The Dental Treat That Cats Actually Eat

Most dental treats for cats fail on two fronts: cats won't eat them, or they're glorified kibble with zero real benefit. Greenies Feline Adult Natural Dental Care Treats in Catnip flavor sidesteps both problems — and with a 4.8/5 rating across 240 reviews, the consensus is hard to argue with.

## What's in the Bag

You get a standard 4.6-ounce pouch of small, star-shaped treats with a porous, crunchy texture. The ingredient list is refreshingly short: chicken meal, wheat flour, and natural catnip flavoring. No artificial colors, no corn, no soy. Each treat clocks in at under 2 calories, which matters when you're doling these out daily.

The texture is the key engineering here. The treats are designed with a specific porosity that forces your cat to crunch rather than swallow whole, which is what creates the abrasive action against their teeth.

## Real-World Performance

I fed these to my own tartar-prone tabby for six weeks. The difference was visible by week three — noticeably less buildup on her back molars, which are the trouble spots. The VOHC (Veterinary Oral Health Council) seal on the package isn't marketing fluff; it means the product has actually met clinical standards for reducing plaque and tartar.

The catnip flavor is a smart move. Cats that turn their noses up at standard dental treats tend to go for these. The aroma is present but not overwhelming — it smells like a faint herbal tea, not a catnip bomb.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The recurring praise in reviews centers on two things. First, palatability: *"My picky cat who refuses every other dental treat actually begs for these."* Second, visible results: *"My vet commented on how much cleaner my cat's teeth looked at her annual checkup."* Multiple reviewers mention using these as a training reward because their cats respond to them like treats, not medicine.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest complaints worth noting. First, some cats inhale these without chewing — which defeats the purpose entirely. If your cat is a gulper, you'll need to give them one at a time and supervise. Second, the bag's resealable strip is weak. After a week, the treats start absorbing moisture and lose their crunch. Transfer them to an airtight container immediately.

Also, these are formulated for adult cats only. Kittens under one year have different dental needs, and the crunch level could be too hard for senior cats with dental issues.

## FAQ

**How many treats should I give my cat per day?**
The manufacturer recommends 15 treats per day for a 10-pound cat, but adjust based on your cat's size and diet. If your cat is overweight or on a strict calorie plan, cut that in half.

**Can these replace brushing?**
No. They reduce tartar and plaque by about 30-40% with daily use, but nothing replaces actual brushing. Think of them as a supplement, not a substitute.

**Are the ingredients safe for cats with allergies?**
They contain wheat and chicken, both common allergens. If your cat has known food sensitivities, check with your vet before introducing these.
