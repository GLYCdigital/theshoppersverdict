Let's be honest about cat food: most of it looks like canned meatloaf and smells like regret. Nutrish Purrfect Broths Tasty Tuna Recipe takes a different approach — it's essentially a soup for cats, with flaked tuna suspended in a savory broth rather than mashed into a gelatinous loaf. That distinction matters more than you might think.

**What's actually in the pouch**

Each 2.5-ounce pouch contains flaked tuna as the primary ingredient, swimming in a broth that also includes carrots, peas, and sweet potatoes. The texture is noticeably different from standard wet food — it pours like a light gravy rather than plopping out as a solid block. For senior cats with dental issues or cats recovering from illness, this consistency is genuinely easier to eat.

The ingredient list is refreshingly short. Tuna, broth, vegetables, and a few thickeners. No caramel coloring, no "meat by-products," no artificial preservatives. Rachael Ray's Nutrish line has built a reputation on this transparency, and Purrfect Broths holds that standard.

**Real-world performance**

The hydration angle is the biggest selling point here. Cats are notoriously terrible at drinking enough water, and chronic dehydration is a leading contributor to kidney disease and urinary tract issues. At roughly 90% moisture content, each pouch delivers meaningful hydration in a form cats actually want. One reviewer put it plainly: *"My cat won't touch a water bowl but licks every drop of broth from this. I've never seen her drink this much."*

The feeding versatility deserves mention. You can serve it as a standalone meal, pour it over dry kibble as a topper for picky eaters, or warm it slightly to release the aroma and entice a cat whose appetite has waned. Multiple reviewers reported success using this to coax sick or senior cats into eating.

**Where it falls short**

The portion size is the most common complaint. At 2.5 ounces per pouch, a typical 10-pound cat needs two pouches per day to hit proper caloric intake — and that's before you factor in treats. Multi-cat households should expect to go through a 12-pack in under a week. The math works out to roughly double the cost per ounce of standard pate food.

The vegetable inclusion is another sticking point. While carrots and peas add nutritional value, some cats simply won't touch them. One frustrated buyer noted: *"My cat eats the broth and leaves every single pea on the plate. It makes a mess and I feel like I'm wasting half the pouch."* If your cat is texture-sensitive, this could be a dealbreaker.

**Who should buy this**

This product shines for three specific scenarios: cats who need extra hydration, picky eaters who refuse standard wet food, and owners who want a clean-ingredient topper to upgrade dry kibble. It's also excellent for senior cats with dental problems who struggle with chunky textures.

If you're on a tight budget or have multiple cats, you'll feel the cost. This is a premium product with a premium price tag — you're paying for the broth format and ingredient quality, not sheer caloric value.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Can this be served as a complete meal?*
Yes, it's nutritionally complete per AAFCO standards. But given the small portion size, most cats need two pouches daily. Many owners use it as a supplement or topper instead.

*Should I refrigerate unused portions?*
Yes. Once opened, refrigerate and use within 24 hours. Unopened pouches store fine at room temperature.

*Is this suitable for kittens?*
It's formulated for adult cats. For kittens under one year, stick with kitten-specific formulas that provide the higher protein and fat content growing cats need.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** your cat needs more moisture in their diet, you're dealing with a picky eater, or you want a clean-ingredient topper without artificial junk. **Skip it if** you're feeding multiple cats on a budget, your cat refuses vegetables, or you prefer traditional pate textures.

Nutrish Purrfect Broths Tasty Tuna Recipe earns a solid 4.2 out of 5. It's not the most economical option on the shelf, but for hydration, ingredient quality, and palatability, it delivers where standard wet food falls short. Your cat will notice the difference — and that's really what matters.
