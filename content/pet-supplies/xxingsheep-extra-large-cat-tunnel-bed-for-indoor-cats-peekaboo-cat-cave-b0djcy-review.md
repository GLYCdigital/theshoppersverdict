If you've bought a "cat tunnel bed" before, you know the problem: they're either small enough for a kitten, or they collapse into a sad fabric pancake within a week. The XxingSheep Extra Large version actually fixes both of those issues — and at 4.7 stars from 235 ratings, it's not just me saying that.

**What's actually in the box**

You get the tunnel bed, fully assembled. No tools, no stuffing to fluff, no "insert cardboard frame" gymnastics. The bed itself is a cylinder about 20 inches in diameter with a peekaboo opening cut into the side — your cat enters through the hole and curls up inside the enclosed cave. The outer shell is a plush coral fleece (the kind that feels almost velvety), and there's a PP board insert that holds the tunnel's shape. The bottom is padded with what feels like a 1.5-inch thick cushion.

**How it performs in real homes**

The "extra large" claim is the headline here, and it holds up. Owners of big breeds report their Maine Coons and Ragdolls fitting comfortably, which is rare for this product category. One verified buyer wrote: *"My 18-pound orange tabby refuses to sleep anywhere else now. He stretches out fully inside and I can still see his tail poking out the opening."* That matches the general sentiment — cats that ignore typical beds seem drawn to the cave-like security this provides.

The PP board frame is the real workhorse. It's rigid enough that the tunnel doesn't sag or fold when your cat launches into it, and the shape holds through repeated washing. The interior is lined with the same plush fabric, so it's cozy rather than cavernous-feeling.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Three things come up repeatedly in the positive reviews:

- **Washability.** The entire bed goes in the washing machine on gentle cycle. No zipper covers to fight with, no foam inserts to remove. Air dry and it's back in service in a day.
- **The peekaboo design.** Cats seem genuinely entertained by the side opening — they can see out while feeling hidden. Multiple reviewers noted their cats use it as a watchtower for birds outside windows.
- **Durability.** Several reviewers mention owning it for months with zero flattening, even with daily use. One wrote: *"My cat kneads it aggressively for 20 minutes before every nap. It still looks brand new after 4 months."*

**Where it falls short**

Honest downsides, because they exist:

- **The fabric is a fur magnet.** Light gray shows every single hair. If you have a white cat or a black cat, you'll be lint-rolling weekly. Darker color options would help, but they're limited.
- **No anti-slip base.** On hardwood or tile, a running cat pouncing into the opening will slide the whole bed across the floor. It's not a dealbreaker, but a rubberized bottom would fix it easily.
- **It's large.** Make sure you have floor space. This isn't a corner-hugging bed; it's a statement piece that needs about 2.5 square feet of clearance.

**Who should buy this**

- **Multi-cat households** — the size accommodates two smaller cats sharing, or a large cat plus a kitten.
- **Owners of big breeds** (Maine Coons, Ragdolls, Norwegian Forest Cats) who struggle to find beds their cats actually fit in.
- **People who wash pet beds frequently** — the all-in-one washability is genuinely convenient.

**Skip it if** you have a tiny apartment with zero floor space, or if you're expecting a "pretty" bed that matches your decor — the gray plush is functional, not fashionable.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can two cats fit inside at once?**
A: Two average-sized cats (10-12 lbs each) can share it comfortably. It's snug but they'll do it — cats love tight spaces. Bigger cats will want it to themselves.

**Q: How long does it take to dry after washing?**
A: Air drying takes 12-24 hours depending on humidity. The PP board holds water in the seams, so give it a full day. You could machine dry on low, but check the tag first — some units have a heat-sensitive adhesive.

**Q: Is this safe for kittens?**
A: Yes, but the opening is 20 inches in diameter, which is large for a 2-month-old kitten. They can enter fine, but they might feel exposed rather than hidden. It's better suited for cats 6 months and up.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you have a large cat that needs a cave-style bed with real structure, or you're tired of replacing flattened tunnel beds every few weeks. **Skip it if** you're short on floor space or have a cat that only sleeps in open beds.

At 4.6/5, this earns its rating. It's not perfect — the fur attraction and lack of anti-slip bottom are real annoyances — but for the core job of giving a big cat a durable, washable, enclosed sleeping spot, it delivers. At its price point, it's one of the better values in this category.
