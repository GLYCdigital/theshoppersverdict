With 95,521 Amazon reviews at 4.3 stars, Dr. Elsey's Ultra is the most-reviewed cat litter on the platform — for good reason. Cat owners are passionate about litter (seriously, read the reviews) and what they say about this stuff borders on evangelical.

## The Odor Control That Converts

The headline feature is simple: this litter controls odor without perfume. One cat owner with three cats and an open floor plan puts it to the test: "I had a friend visit whom I hadn't seen in over 10 years, and he said he'd never know I had cats had he not seen them." Two litter boxes, three cats, zero smell. That's the kind of real-world performance that builds a 95K-review reputation.

Another reviewer who tried everything before settling here writes: "I've honestly forgotten to scoop the box a couple times because I NEVER SMELL IT! Even when scooping, it hardly smells. Only towards the end of its life cycle does it tend to stink much, but again, that's only when scooping."

The unscented formula is a deliberate choice — it doesn't mask ammonia with floral perfume. It actually neutralizes the odor. For anyone who's tried scented litters and found the combination of fake lavender + cat smell somehow worse than just cat smell, this is the solution.

## The Dust Factor

Dust is the second biggest complaint across all clay litters, and Dr. Elsey's addresses it directly: 99.9% dust-free. "This has less dust than any other litter I can think of and I love that," one reviewer writes. Another calls it "lower dust than all the other litters I've tried."

Is it truly 100% dust-free? No — that's not physically possible with clay. But compared to Tidy Cats, Fresh Step, and Arm & Hammer, it's dramatically better. You won't see a cloud when pouring. You won't sneeze when scooping. Your cat won't leave dusty paw prints on the couch.

## The Clumping Reality

Dr. Elsey's clumps extremely hard — almost too hard sometimes. "It clumps EXTREMELY well. Honestly sometimes it's a bit like scooping a rock," notes one multi-cat household reviewer. The upside: clumps don't disintegrate when you scoop, so you waste less litter. The downside: if you don't fill the box deep enough (3-4 inches minimum), clumps can cement themselves to the bottom.

One practical tip from a pro: "If you are like me and you scoop the litter-box once a day, all you have to do is just run your scooper across the bottom, below the surface, to loosen everything up before you start scooping."

Dr. Elsey's Ultra is the cat litter that cat owners switch to and then never leave. The combination of genuine odor neutralization (not masking), low dust, hard clumping, and reasonable pricing makes it the standard-setter in its category.

The 40lb bag at $22.99 works out to about $0.57 per pound — excellent value for premium litter. Yes, it's heavy. Yes, the clumps can be stubborn. But for odor control that actually works without artificial scents, it's the best in the business.
