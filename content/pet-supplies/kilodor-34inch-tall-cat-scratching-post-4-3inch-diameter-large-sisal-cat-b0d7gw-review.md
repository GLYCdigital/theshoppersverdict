**FAQ**

**Q: Is the sisal safe if my cat ingests it?**  
A: Natural sisal is plant-based and generally safe if small amounts are swallowed. That said, monitor your cat — if they're aggressively chewing and pulling fibers, redirect them to a cardboard scratcher instead.

**Q: Will this hold up to a cat that sharpens claws on furniture?**  
A: Yes, but you need to train them. Place the post near their preferred scratching spot and reward use with treats. The thick sisal will hold up to the punishment, but the cat still needs to choose it.

**Q: Can I replace the sisal when it wears out?**  
A: Not easily — the sisal is wrapped and glued around the column. When it eventually wears, you'll need to rewrap with sisal rope and hot glue yourself, or replace the whole post.
