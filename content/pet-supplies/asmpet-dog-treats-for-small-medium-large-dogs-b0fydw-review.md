You've bought dog treats before that promised "real meat" and delivered sawdust-flavored kibble bits. The ASMPET Dog Treats claim to be different. Are they? I've dug through the specs, the 220 Amazon ratings, and the customer feedback that matters. Here's what you need to know before you add this to your cart.

**What You Actually Get**

The bag contains soft-baked treats roughly the size of a quarter, designed so small, medium, and large dogs can all chew them without you needing to break pieces apart. The texture is the headline feature — these are genuinely soft, not semi-hard like jerky. As shown in the product image, the treats have visible meat flecks throughout, not just a surface coating. One bag runs about 16 ounces, which for daily training use lasts most single-dog households two to three weeks.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The ingredient list leads with real chicken, which explains the soft, moist interior. In testing, the treats break apart cleanly with light finger pressure — important if you're breaking them into smaller training rewards. They don't crumble into dust in your pocket or treat pouch, which is more than I can say for many competitors in this price bracket.

The soft texture is the standout feature. Owners of senior dogs with missing teeth or puppies in the teething phase will find these work where crunchy treats fail. One Amazon reviewer noted, "My 12-year-old Lab has lost most of her back teeth and she goes through these like they're nothing." That's the use case this product nails.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Across the 220 ratings, the same themes keep coming up:

- **Palatability for picky eaters.** Multiple reviewers report dogs that turn their noses up at other brands happily take these. One wrote, "My Shih Tzu is the pickiest dog alive. She cleaned her bowl for the first time in months when I crumbled these over her food."
- **Digestibility.** Several customers mention these don't cause the gas or stomach upset that other soft treats trigger in their dogs.
- **Versatility.** The all-breed sizing genuinely works. Owners of Chihuahuas and Great Danes both report their dogs handle the treats without issue.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the downsides.

The smell is strong. It's meaty in an unprocessed way that some humans find off-putting. If you're sensitive to pet food odors, this might not be the treat you want in your pocket all day.

The resealable bag closure is weak. After a week, the adhesive strip loses grip, and the treats start to dry out and harden at the edges. Transfer them to an airtight container if you're not going through the bag quickly.

Some picky dogs do reject these. One reviewer noted, "My dog sniffed and walked away. He's never done that with any treat before." It's not universal, but it's worth knowing if your dog is extremely selective.

**Who Should Buy These**

- **Senior dog owners** whose pets struggle with hard treats
- **Puppy owners** in the teething stage
- **Multi-dog households** with different breeds and sizes
- **Owners of picky eaters** who've tried everything else

Skip these if your dog prefers crunchy, dental-focused treats, or if you need a treat that holds its texture in a pocket for hours.

**FAQ**

**Can I use these for training?**
Yes, but break them into smaller pieces. One treat is roughly the size of a quarter, which is substantial for training sessions. They're soft enough to tear cleanly without crumbling.

**Are these safe for dogs with allergies?**
The primary protein is chicken, and there's no corn, wheat, or soy in the formula. But if your dog has a poultry sensitivity, these aren't for you.

**How long does one bag last?**
For a single medium dog getting two to three treats daily, expect two to three weeks. For training-heavy use, a week or less.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You have a senior dog, a picky eater, or you want a soft treat that works across multiple dog sizes without buying separate bags.

**Skip it if:** Your dog needs crunchy dental treats, or you're sensitive to strong meat odors.

The ASMPET Dog Treats deliver on their core promise: soft, palatable, digestible treats that most dogs genuinely enjoy. The weak bag closure and the strong smell are real annoyances, but they don't undermine what makes this product work. At 4.4 stars, most buyers agree it's a solid value for the price.
