## New World Dog Crate: The 4.0-Star Cage That Divides Owners

Most crates in this price range are either flimsy wire boxes or overbuilt monsters that take 20 minutes to assemble. The New World Dog Crate sits in an awkward middle — and for many owners, that's exactly where they want it. With 200 Amazon ratings averaging 4.0 stars, it's not a runaway hit, but the pattern of feedback tells a clear story: this is a solid, no-drama crate for everyday use, with a couple of compromises you should know about before buying.

**What's in the box:** The crate arrives fully disassembled but goes together in under five minutes — no tools needed. You get the wire frame, a metal pan, and the standard two-door configuration (one on the side, one on the front). The folding mechanism is the standout feature: it collapses flat to about two inches thick, which makes it genuinely portable. If you travel with your dog or need to store the crate between uses, this is a real advantage over welded one-piece units.

**Build quality and real-world performance:** The wire gauge is average — not the thinnest I've seen, but not the thickest either. The finish is a matte black powder coat that resists scratches well; one reviewer noted it "held up to my German Shepherd's enthusiastic digging for six months with no rust." The rounded corners and smooth welds matter more than they sound: no sharp edges to catch on your dog's fur or your own forearms when you're reaching in to grab a toy.

The double-door design is the practical winner here. You can place the crate flush against a wall and still reach your dog from the side door — a small thing that makes a big difference in tight apartments or bedrooms. The front door swings fully open with a wide 180-degree arc, which helps when you're trying to coax a reluctant puppy inside.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The folding mechanism gets the most love. One verified purchase wrote, "I've owned three crates over the years and this is the first one I can actually pack in my trunk without disassembling panels." Another owner highlighted the assembly: "Took me four minutes from box to crate — my old one took a hex key and a lot of cursing."

**Where it falls short:** The metal pan is the weakest link. Multiple reviewers mention it denting under heavy dogs or from enthusiastic scratching. It's also not the easiest to slide in and out — the lip is low, so you'll want to lift the crate slightly to remove it for cleaning. The door latch is stiff out of the box; a few owners reported needing two hands to open it until it loosened up after a week or two of use. Neither issue is a dealbreaker, but both are worth knowing.

**Who should buy this:**  
- **Travelers** — the flat-fold design is the main reason to choose this over a cheaper fixed crate.  
- **Apartment dwellers** — the side door and compact footprint (when placed against a wall) make it work in tight spaces.  
- **First-time crate buyers** — easy assembly and no sharp edges make it forgiving for nervous pets.  

**Skip it if:** You have a determined escape artist (the latch is secure but not chew-proof), or you need a crate for a dog over 90 pounds — the wire gauge starts to feel light at that size, and the pan will likely sag.

**FAQ:**

*Is it safe for puppies?*  
Yes, but supervise the first few days. The wire spacing is standard, so a determined chewer could bend it, but the smooth welds mean no injury risk from sharp edges.

*Can I wash the pan in a dishwasher?*  
Technically yes, but it's large and awkward. Hand-washing with mild soap is faster and won't risk warping the thin metal.

*Does it come with a divider panel?*  
No. If you're crate-training a puppy who needs a smaller space, you'll need to buy a separate divider or use a box inside. Worth factoring into your total cost.

**The Verdict: 4.2/5 — A smart buy for most owners**

**Buy it if** you want a foldable, easy-to-clean crate that doesn't dominate your living space and you're willing to accept a thinner pan.

**Skip it if** you need a heavy-duty crate for a large, powerful dog — in that case, spend more on a reinforced model.

The New World Dog Crate nails the basics: it's safe, simple, and genuinely portable. The pan and latch are minor annoyances, not fatal flaws. At this price point, you're getting exactly what you pay for — and for most dogs and owners, that's more than enough.
