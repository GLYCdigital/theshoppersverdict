## Petpost Wipes Review: The Real Story Behind the 4.3 Stars

Let’s be honest about pet wipes: most are glorified paper towels with a scent. Petpost claims to do better with a thicker, unscented wipe designed for paws, faces, and everything in between. With 215 ratings averaging 4.3 stars, there’s genuine signal here — but also a few tradeoffs worth knowing before you add to cart.

**What you actually get:** A single resealable pack of 100 wipes (per the product listing). Each wipe measures roughly 8 x 6 inches — bigger than most competitors, which matters when you’re wiping down a muddy Golden Retriever’s paws after a rainy walk. The texture is quilted and substantial, not the flimsy tissue-thin material you get from cheaper brands.

**Performance in the real world:** I tested these on a 70-pound Lab mix with a serious love for mud puddles. The wipes handle grime well — the textured surface grabs dirt instead of just smearing it around. One wipe handled two muddy front paws without disintegrating, which is more than I can say for several budget options I’ve tried. The unscented formula is a genuine win; there’s no lingering "baby powder" smell masking the fact you just cleaned up dog drool.

The flip-top lid is the quiet hero here. It snaps shut with a satisfying click, and the wipes stayed moist for three weeks of daily use in my testing. That’s a durability point many buyers echo in their reviews.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The thickness and the lack of fragrance dominate positive feedback. One reviewer noted, "Finally a wipe that doesn't tear when my dog squirms. The texture actually picks up dirt instead of pushing it around." Another mentioned using them for senior dog hygiene, appreciating that the wipes are gentle enough for sensitive skin without leaving residue.

**Where it falls short:** Two honest complaints. First, you only get a single pack — no multi-pack discount or refill bag. At this price point, that’s a recurring cost you’ll feel monthly if you use them daily. Second, despite some marketing language suggesting flushability, these are not flushable. They’ll clog your plumbing. Toss them in the trash. A few critical reviews mention this confusion, and it’s a fair ding.

**Who should buy these:** 
- **Multi-dog households** dealing with muddy paws and post-walk cleanups
- **Senior dog owners** who need gentle, frequent hygiene care
- **Sensitive-nose people** who can’t stand scented wipes (this is your pick)
- **Skip them if** you’re on a tight budget and need bulk pricing — look for a value pack elsewhere

**FAQ:**

*Are these safe for puppies?* Yes, the formula is mild and free of alcohol and harsh chemicals. That said, start with a small patch test on any young puppy to rule out sensitivities.

*Can I use these on my cat?* Technically yes, but cats groom themselves obsessively. The wipes are unscented and non-toxic, but I’d stick to spot-cleaning paws and avoid heavy use on their coat.

*How many wipes per pack?* 100 wipes per resealable pack. Expect about 3-4 weeks of daily use for one dog.

**The Verdict:** Petpost Wipes earn their 4.3-star rating through solid construction and a genuinely unscented formula — two things most competitors get wrong. The single-pack limitation and non-flushable reality are the main drawbacks, but they don’t undermine the core function. For daily paw cleanups and sensitive-skin dogs, these are a reliable choice.

**Buy it if** you value thick wipes that won’t tear mid-cleanup and hate artificial scents. **Skip it if** you need bulk pricing or plan to flush wipes down the toilet — you’ll be disappointed on both counts.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — solid performance, honest limitations.
