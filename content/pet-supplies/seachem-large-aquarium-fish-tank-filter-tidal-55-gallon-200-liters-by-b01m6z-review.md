**The SeaChem Tidal 55 isn't just another hang-on-back filter — it's the one that fixes the annoyances every other HOB gets wrong.** With 225 Amazon ratings averaging 4.5 stars, this filter has built a loyal following among aquarists who got tired of priming pumps, scraping protein film off the surface, and replacing proprietary cartridges. Here's the honest breakdown after looking at what real owners report.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the filter body, an adjustable flow control knob, a surface skimmer attachment, a spray bar, and the media basket — but **no filter media whatsoever**. That's the single biggest gotcha. Plan to drop another $15–25 on Seachem Matrix, Purigen, or plain filter floss before you can run it. The basket is generously sized (roughly 5×4×3 inches), so you can stack mechanical, chemical, and biological media in whatever order your tank needs.

## Real-World Performance

The headline feature is the **self-priming pump**. When the power blips, the Tidal 55 restarts itself automatically — no manual priming, no refilling the chamber, no flooded carpet. One reviewer put it simply: *"Power went out for 6 hours. Filter just started right back up. My old AquaClear never did that."* For anyone who's woken up to a dead filter after a storm, that alone justifies the price.

The surface skimmer is the second standout. It sits at the water line and continuously pulls the oily biofilm that forms on top of unskimmed tanks. Owners consistently mention clearer water and less algae within a week. As one 5-star review notes: *"The film on top disappeared in 2 days. I didn't even know I had a film problem until it was gone."*

Flow rate is adjustable from a trickle to a strong current — useful for bettas that hate turbulence or for pushing water in a planted tank. The spray bar attachment distributes outflow evenly, so you won't blast a single spot.

## Where It Falls Short

The impeller housing is the weak point. Several reviewers report that after cleaning, the impeller doesn't seat perfectly, producing a rattle until you open it and reseat it. It's fixable, but it's a fiddly 10-minute job that shouldn't be necessary on a filter in this price range.

Also, the "55 gallon" rating assumes a **light to moderate bioload**. If you're keeping goldfish, large cichlids, or a heavily stocked community tank, this filter alone won't cut it. You'll want to run it alongside a sponge filter or a second unit. That's not a defect — it's just honest sizing.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Planted tank keepers** who want clear water without surface film and need adjustable flow for CO2 distribution
- **Anyone who's had a power outage kill a filter** — the self-priming feature is a genuine lifesaver
- **Hobbyists tired of buying proprietary cartridges** — the open basket lets you use whatever media you prefer

Skip it if you want an all-in-one cartridge system with zero setup thinking, or if you're stocking a 55-gallon tank with messy fish and expect one filter to handle it.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this filter work on a rimmed aquarium?**
Yes. The hanger accommodates rims up to 1 inch thick. It fits standard Aqueon and Marineland tanks without modification.

**Q: How often do I need to clean it?**
Every 2–4 weeks, depending on bioload. The media basket lifts out cleanly, and the impeller needs a quick rinse to prevent the rattle issue mentioned above.

**Q: Can I use it on a smaller tank, like a 20-gallon?**
Absolutely. The flow control dial lets you dial it way down. It's actually a popular choice for 20–40 gallon planted tanks because of the skimmer.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a filter that restarts itself, keeps your water surface clean, and lets you choose your own media without locking you into expensive cartridges. The 4.5-star rating holds up — this is a well-engineered piece of kit.

**Skip it if** you don't want to buy media separately or you have a heavily stocked tank that needs industrial-strength filtration.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — loses half a star for the impeller seating quirk and the no-media-included surprise. Otherwise, this is the best HOB filter I've seen for the money.
