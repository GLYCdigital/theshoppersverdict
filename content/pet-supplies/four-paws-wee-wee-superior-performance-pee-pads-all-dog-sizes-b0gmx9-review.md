**Where it falls short**  
Let’s be honest about the downsides. These pads are **thinner** than premium options like Amazon Basics or PetSafe’s heavy-duty line. Also, while the attractant works, it’s not as strong as some competing brands. If your dog has a history of ignoring pads entirely, you may need to pair this with a training spray.
