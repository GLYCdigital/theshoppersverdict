**What You Actually Get**

The 16-ounce bottle looks clinical — white, no-nonsense, with a flip-top cap that dispenses without mess. The formula is a thick, pearlized liquid with a light, clean scent (think mild oatmeal, not artificial "baby powder"). I tested this on a golden retriever with seasonal environmental allergies and a pit bull with chronic dry flaking. Two baths, one week apart. And while it's excellent for maintenance, severely allergic dogs may still need a prescription medicated wash — this is a moisturizer, not a treatment for active infections.
