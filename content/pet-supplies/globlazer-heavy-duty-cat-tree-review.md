# Globlazer Heavy Duty Cat Tree Review: Finally, a Tower Big Cats Can Trust

Most cat trees are built for average cats. If you own a Maine Coon, a Norwegian Forest Cat, or just an unusually hefty domestic shorthair, you've probably watched a "large" cat tree wobble precariously under your cat's weight. The **Globlazer Heavy Duty Cat Tree S72** was designed specifically to solve this problem — and with a 4.6-star rating, it appears to deliver.

At $139.99 for 72 inches, this is a premium cat tower aimed at a specific audience: owners of cats that overwhelm standard furniture. We analyzed the reviews to see if it earns its "heavy duty" label.

## Built Different

The construction is the defining feature. Where standard cat trees use 2.75-inch support posts and 0.5-inch particle board, the Globlazer uses thicker posts throughout and reinforced platform connections. The base is notably wider and heavier than competitors at this height — a critical design choice, since a 72-inch tower's stability lives or dies at the base.

The six scratching posts are wrapped in natural sisal rope, and reviewers with multiple large cats note they hold up significantly better than budget alternatives. The dark gray plush covering is low-profile and blends into most room aesthetics better than the beige or tan found on most cat furniture.

## Assembly: A Project, Not a Quick Setup

Two people, 60-90 minutes, diagram-only instructions. The box is heavy, and you'll want a power drill for faster assembly (the included Allen key works but tests patience). The wall anchor strap is included and should be used — even on a stable tree, a 20-pound cat launching at full speed generates serious momentum.

Reviewers consistently note the assembly is worth the effort once the tree is up and cats claim their territory — which, as with most cat furniture, happens almost immediately.

## Who This Is For

This is a specialized product. If your cat weighs under 12 pounds, you don't need a heavy-duty tree — standard 72-inch options at half the price will serve you fine. The Globlazer exists for the Maine Coon owners, the multi-cat households with large breeds, and anyone who's tired of watching their cat hesitate before jumping onto a wobbly platform.

The hammock is a standout feature — positioned at mid-height and generously sized, it gets more daily use than the top perches according to multiple reviewers. Cats that rarely use enclosed cat caves apparently make exceptions for this hammock.

## The Verdict

The Globlazer S72 isn't the best cat tree for everyone — it's the best cat tree for a specific, underserved group: owners of large cats who are tired of replacing budget towers every year. At $139.99, it's a premium product that delivers on its core promise of stability. If your cat breaks standard trees, this one breaks the cycle.

**Verdict: 8.5/10** — The heavy-duty cat tree that large breed owners have been waiting for.
