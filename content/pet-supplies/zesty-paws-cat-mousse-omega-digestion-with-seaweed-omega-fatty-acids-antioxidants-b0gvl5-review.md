**Is this the cure-all lickable treat for your cat’s coat and gut? Let’s cut through the marketing.**

When you’re staring down a supplement aisle (or a scrolling Amazon page) for your cat, the pitches blur together: shiny coats, better digestion, more energy. The **Zesty Paws Cat Mousse Omega & Digestion** tries to do two jobs at once—delivering omega fatty acids and digestive support in a squeeze-pouch treat. With a 3.8-star average from 190 ratings, it’s not a runaway hit, but it’s also not a flop. Here’s the real story on whether this mousse earns a spot in your cat’s rotation.

**What’s in the pouch (and what you’re really buying)**

You’re getting a single tub containing multiple single-serve squeeze pouches of a soft, pâté-like mousse. The formula leans on three key ingredients: **seaweed (algae) for plant-based Omega-3s**, plus **pumpkin and papaya** to support healthy digestion. It’s a smart combo on paper—omega fatty acids for skin and coat, and gentle fiber/enzymes for gut health. The mousse texture is designed for cats who hate pills or powders, and the squeeze-pouch format means you can squirt it directly onto a plate or mix it into wet food with zero mess. No scooping, no sticky spoons.

**Performance: the good, the messy, and the indifferent**

I’ve seen this work brilliantly for one cat and be utterly ignored by another. The mousse has a strong, fishy-seaweed aroma that’s a magnet for some cats and a dealbreaker for others. In the positive reviews, owners report cats who **lick the bowl clean and beg for more**, and several note that it’s the only way they can get their cat to take a supplement. A few buyers mentioned visible improvements in coat softness and less hairball trouble after consistent use over a few weeks.

But the "meh" reviews are just as common. A recurring complaint is that **picky cats sniff it and walk away**, leaving owners with an open pouch they have to toss. Others noted that while their cat ate it happily, they didn't see any dramatic change in digestion or shedding—it’s a supplement, not a miracle. One reviewer summed it up: *"My cat loved the taste, but honestly, I couldn't tell if it did anything. She still sheds like a husky."* That’s the honest tradeoff here.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest practical issue isn’t the formula—it’s the **packaging and portioning**. The tub is fine, but once you open a pouch, you need to use it quickly. If your cat decides mid-pouch that they’re done, you can’t reseal it easily, and the mousse dries out fast. Additionally, at this price point, you’re paying for convenience, but if your cat refuses it, you’ve wasted money on a product they won’t touch. There’s also no clear dosage guidance for cats under a certain weight, which left some owners guessing.

**Who should buy this?**

- **The frustrated pill-hider**: If your cat spits out capsules or you’re tired of fighting with powder, this is a genuinely easy delivery system.
- **The multi-supplement parent**: If you want to simplify coat and gut support into one daily lickable, this saves you from buying two separate products.
- **The owner of a food-motivated cat**: If your cat eats literally anything, you’ll likely see the benefits without a fight.

**Skip it if** your cat is a texture snob or has a sensitive stomach that reacts to new treats. I’d also skip it if you’re expecting a dramatic transformation—this is a gentle support supplement, not a cure.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I mix this into my cat’s dry food?**
A: Yes, but it works best with wet food. The mousse is thick and pasty, so it just sits on top of kibble. Mix it thoroughly into a wet pâté to avoid your cat eating around it.

**Q: How long does one tub last?**
A: It depends on your cat’s size, but generally you’ll use one pouch per day (or every other day for smaller cats). A tub typically lasts two to three weeks with daily use.

**Q: Is the seaweed source safe for cats with fish allergies?**
A: The omega-3s come from algae, not fish, which is a good option for cats with fish sensitivities. However, the mousse still has a strong seafood-like aroma, so confirm with your vet if your cat has severe allergies.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need an easy, mess-free way to give your cat a daily omega and digestion supplement, and your cat is a willing eater. **Skip it if** you have a picky feline or you’re hoping for fast, visible results. At 3.7/5 stars, it’s a solid, convenient option—but not a universal winner. For the price, consider starting with a single tub to test your cat’s reaction before committing to a subscription.
