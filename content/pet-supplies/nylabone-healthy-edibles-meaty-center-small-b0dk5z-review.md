Let's be honest about dog chews: most are either rock-hard synthetics your 8-pound Yorkie ignores or flimsy rawhide that ends up stuck to your ceiling. The Nylabone Healthy Edibles Meaty Center Small sits in a smarter middle ground—an edible chew with enough structure to occupy a small dog without requiring a chainsaw to break down.

**What you actually get**

The package contains a single chew shaped like a miniature bone, roughly 4 inches long and about the diameter of a Sharpie. The exterior is a firm, tan-colored edible material with textured ridges along the sides, and there's a darker "meaty center" running through the middle that's visible in the product photos. It's designed for dogs up to 25 pounds, so we're talking small-breed territory here, not something you hand to a lab.

The ingredient list is refreshingly short: plant-based proteins, glycerin, lecithin, and natural meat flavoring. No rawhide, no artificial preservatives. It's made in the USA, which matters to a lot of owners who've read too many recalls on imported chews.

**How it performs in the real world**

The texture is the standout feature. It's firm enough that a dog has to actually work at it—chewing, gnawing, repositioning—but soft enough that it softens and breaks down as saliva works in. The ridges serve a dual purpose: they give teeth something to scrape against (Nylabone's whole "dental cleaning" claim) and they give your dog a reason to keep going back to it.

One pattern that shows up repeatedly in the 229 ratings is that small dogs *finish* these. That's not typical for edible chews in this category. One reviewer noted, "My 12-pound shih tzu chews for about 20 minutes and finishes the whole thing. She's never done that with any other treat." Another owner of a picky chihuahua said, "The meaty center smell is strong enough to get her interested, but not so strong that it stinks up the room."

The 4.6-star average holds up because the product does what it claims: it keeps small dogs busy, it's digestible, and it doesn't leave behind slimy rawhide remnants.

**Where it falls short**

Two honest complaints surface consistently. First, the calorie count is higher than you'd expect for a small-dog treat—around 80 calories per chew. For a 5-pound dog, that's a fifth of their daily caloric needs. If you're handing these out daily, you need to adjust meals or your dog will pack on weight.

Second, the durability claims are optimistic. Nylabone markets this as a "long-lasting" chew, but for determined chewers—especially terriers and other breeds with strong jaws for their size—it's more like 10 to 15 minutes of entertainment. One frustrated buyer wrote, "My 15-pound jack russell finished it in 8 minutes. It's a treat, not a distraction." That's fair. If you need a chew to survive a work-from-home meeting, this isn't it.

**Who should buy this**

If you have a small dog that's a moderate chewer—a shih tzu, maltese, poodle mix, or similar—this is a solid daily treat that doubles as a dental aid. It's a great choice for puppies too, since the edible formula is gentler on developing teeth than nylon chews. It's also a smart pick if you want a chew that won't leave residue on your carpet or require you to pry a soggy rawhide knot off your dog's tongue.

Skip it if you have an aggressive chewer, a dog on a strict calorie budget, or a large-breed dog that will swallow it in three bites. This is a small-dog product, full stop.

**Three questions buyers actually ask**

*How long does one chew last?*
For most small dogs, 15 to 30 minutes of active chewing. Aggressive chewers will crush it faster. It's a treat with some staying power, not an hour-long distraction.

*Can puppies have these?*
Yes, and they're actually a good choice for teething puppies since the texture is firm but gives way under pressure. Just supervise and adjust meal portions.

*Are these safe if swallowed in chunks?*
The edible formula is digestible, which is a major safety advantage over rawhide. That said, no chew is completely risk-free—supervise your dog and remove any large pieces that break off.

**The verdict**

The Nylabone Healthy Edibles Meaty Center Small earns its 4.6-star rating honestly. It delivers on flavor, digestibility, and dental benefits, with a small-breed focus that the market actually needs. The calorie density and modest durability are real tradeoffs, but they're manageable if you're mindful about portion control.

**Buy it if:** You have a small dog under 25 pounds that needs a daily chew that's safe, digestible, and actually gets eaten.

**Skip it if:** You need a long-lasting distraction for a power chewer, or your dog is on a strict weight management plan.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a genuinely good product that does exactly what it promises for its target audience.
