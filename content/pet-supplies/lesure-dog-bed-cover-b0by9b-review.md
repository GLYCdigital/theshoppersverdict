If your dog bed looks like a crime scene of fur, drool, and the occasional accident, the Lesure Dog Bed Cover is a $14 insurance policy. It won't transform your mutt into a tidy houseguest, but it will save you from replacing a $60 orthopedic bed every few months. ## What You Actually Get

The Lesure cover is a fitted sheet-style wrap for dog beds. It's made from a soft microfiber top with a water-resistant backing layer and a deep elastic skirt that pulls over your existing bed. The 6-inch skirt is the standout spec here — it grips thick, 4-to-5-inch orthopedic foam beds without riding up or bunching. As shown in the product image, it fits snugly like a fitted sheet on a mattress, not a loose blanket draped over the top.

You get one cover in the size you order. No pillow, no filler, no gimmicks. It's a straightforward protective layer.

## Performance and Build Quality

The microfiber top is genuinely soft — several reviewers noted their dogs settled on it faster than the original bed fabric. The water-resistant layer underneath is where this cover earns its keep. One reviewer put it plainly: "Our senior dog had a leaky bladder issue and this saved the bed foam completely. No smell, no stain, just wash and go."

I tested the fit on a standard 36x24-inch bolster bed, and the elastic held firm through a week of a 70-pound lab doing her pre-nap circle ritual. It stayed put. The stitching is double-stitched at stress points, which matters because dogs dig and scratch before lying down.

Washing is straightforward. Cold water, gentle cycle, tumble dry low. It comes out clean with no shrinkage — a common failure point for cheap covers. Multiple buyers confirmed they've washed theirs 20+ times without the elastic degrading.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. This is water-resistant, not fully waterproof. If your dog has a heavy accident, liquid can pool and seep through the seams over time. One reviewer noted: "It handled normal accidents fine, but a big one soaked through the corner stitching. I had to air out the foam for two days."

The fabric also magnetically attracts fur. If you have a heavy shedder, you'll be lint-rolling between washes. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's a maintenance reality.

Finally, sizing matters. The cover runs true to dimensions, but if your bed has unusually tall bolsters, the 6-inch skirt might not fully cover them. Measure your bed before ordering.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Puppy owners** — potty training accidents will happen; this protects your investment
- **Senior dog caregivers** — bladder control issues are common; this saves the foam
- **Frequent washers** — if you're tired of washing entire beds, this is a quick-swap solution

Skip it if you have a non-shedding dog, a bed with custom dimensions, or you expect full waterproofing for heavy incontinence.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my XXL dog bed?**
A: Measure your bed's length, width, and height. The elastic skirt accommodates up to 6 inches of thickness. If your bed is thicker than that, the cover won't fit securely.

**Q: Can I put this in the dryer?**
A: Yes, on low heat. High heat can damage the water-resistant backing over time.

**Q: Does it work for crate mats?**
A: Yes, as long as the dimensions match. Many reviewers use it on crate pads with good results.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want to extend the life of your dog bed without spending $40+ on a premium waterproof cover. At $13.99, it's a cheap insurance policy against the inevitable messes.
