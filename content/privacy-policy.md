**Last updated:** May 11, 2026

The Shopper's Verdict respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what choices you have.

## Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected

When you visit our site, we may collect certain information automatically, including:

- **Log data** — your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, pages visited, and timestamps
- **Device information** — screen resolution, device type, and browser settings
- **Usage data** — how you interact with our pages (clicks, scrolls, time on page)

This data is anonymized and used to improve our site and understand what content our readers find valuable.

### Cookies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies for:

- **Analytics** — to understand how our site is used (via self-hosted Umami analytics, where applicable)
- **Affiliate links** — Amazon Associates uses cookies to track referrals. These are set when you click an affiliate link and allow Amazon to credit us for purchases you make.

You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Blocking cookies may affect your experience on affiliate-linked purchases but will not affect your ability to browse our content.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Operate and improve our website
- Understand reading patterns and popular content
- Track affiliate referrals for commission attribution
- Respond to inquiries (if you contact us)

## Third-Party Services

### Amazon Associates

Our site participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. When you click an affiliate link to Amazon, Amazon may set cookies on your browser for tracking and attribution purposes. Amazon's use of your data is governed by the [Amazon Privacy Notice](https://www.amazon.com/privacy).

### Cloudflare

Our site is hosted and distributed via Cloudflare. Cloudflare processes certain data (including IP addresses) to deliver our content securely and efficiently. See the [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for details.

### Analytics

We use privacy-respecting analytics (self-hosted where possible) that do not track individuals across sites. No personal data is sold or shared with advertisers.

## Data Retention

Log data and analytics are retained for a limited period (typically 90 days or less) and then anonymized or deleted.

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request deletion of your data
- Opt out of data collection (via browser settings or cookie controls)
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, [contact us](/contact/).

## Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
