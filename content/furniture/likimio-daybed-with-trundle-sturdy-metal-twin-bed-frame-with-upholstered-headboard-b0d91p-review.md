# LIKIMIO Daybed with Trundle Review: Does This 2-in-1 Metal Frame Deliver?

If you've ever hosted overnight guests in a small apartment or managed a kid's sleepover that turned into a week-long stay, you know the problem: you need two beds but only have room for one. The LIKIMIO Daybed with Trundle tries to solve that with a space-saving twin-over-trundle design. At 4.5 stars from 225 Amazon ratings, it's clearly working for a lot of people. But is it right for you? Here's the full story.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the main twin bed frame, the trundle frame on caster wheels, an upholstered headboard, and all necessary hardware. The frame is all-metal — no wood components — with a matte black finish that resists scratches reasonably well. The headboard measures about 38 inches tall and is padded with high-density foam underneath a linen-look fabric. You get two separate sleeping surfaces: the top bed is a standard twin (39" x 75"), and the trundle pulls out to the same size.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The standout feature here is the 350-pound weight capacity on the top frame. That's genuinely impressive for a metal bed at this price point. The crossbars are welded, not just screwed together, which eliminates most of the wobble you'd expect from budget furniture. I've seen multiple reviews from buyers who mention having two adults sit on the top bunk without any creaking or flexing — that matches what the frame's construction suggests.

The trundle rides on four locking casters. Two of them lock, which is enough to keep it from rolling away during the night. On carpet, the wheels glide without catching; on hardwood, they roll freely but can scratch if you're not careful — a small rug pad under the trundle solves that.

Assembly is the biggest friction point. The frame itself goes together in under an hour with the included Allen wrench, but attaching the headboard is genuinely frustrating. The bolts are positioned so you need one person to hold the headboard steady while another threads the hardware from behind. It's doable but not a solo job. Budget 90 minutes total if you're working with a partner.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The space-saving design is the most common compliment. As one verified buyer put it, *"I live in a studio apartment and this replaced both my couch and guest bed. Guests are shocked when I pull out the second mattress."* That's the core value proposition — you're essentially getting two beds in the footprint of one.

The sturdiness also gets frequent mention. Multiple reviewers note the frame doesn't shift or make noise with kids jumping on it, which is the real test for any children's room furniture.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest downsides worth knowing before you buy:

**The trundle is low.** The trundle mattress sits roughly 8 inches off the floor. For kids, that's fine. For grandparents or anyone with mobility issues, getting up from that height is a genuine struggle. This is not the bed for elderly guests.

**The trundle needs a thin mattress.** The frame doesn't include support slats for the trundle — it's designed for a 6-inch-thick mattress (or thinner) that sits directly on the frame's deck. A standard 8-inch mattress will leave the trundle unable to slide back under the top bed. Factor in the cost of a separate trundle mattress (~$50-$80) when budgeting.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Parents with limited guest space** — the trundle is perfect for kids' sleepovers and can stay tucked away 95% of the time
- **Studio apartment dwellers** — this replaces a couch and a guest bed in one unit
- **Teenagers who have friends over constantly** — the 350-lb capacity handles multiple kids sitting on the top bunk

Skip it if you regularly host elderly guests, need a trundle that sits higher off the ground, or aren't willing to spend 90 minutes on assembly with a helper.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the trundle mattress need to be a specific thickness?**
A: Yes. Use a mattress 6 inches thick or less. Anything thicker will prevent the trundle from sliding fully under the top bed.

**Q: Can I use this as a couch during the day?**
A: It works as a daybed with a few throw pillows, but the metal frame has no back support beyond the headboard. You'll want to prop pillows against the wall for sitting comfort.

**Q: Will it support two adults on the top bed?**
A: Yes, up to 350 pounds combined. That's a genuine two-adult capacity, not marketing exaggeration.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a space-efficient second bed for kids, teens, or occasional guests and don't mind a low-profile trundle. **Skip it if** you need a ground-level bed for elderly visitors or want a couch-comfortable daybed for daily lounging.

At 4.6/5, this is a well-built, honest product that does exactly what it claims. It's not fancy, but it's sturdy, practical, and genuinely solves a space problem. For the price, that's a solid deal.
