## The skinny-table solution your cramped room has been waiting for

Apartment dwellers know the struggle: you need a place for your coffee mug, reading lamp, and phone, but there's zero floor space. The Homedawn 3-Tier Narrow End Table attacks this problem with a footprint that's almost laughably small — just 11.8 inches wide. That's narrower than a standard laptop. It slides into the gap between your sofa and wall, beside a bed, or into that dead corner behind the armchair.

## What actually shows up at your door

The box contains five particle board panels, the metal frame, a bottom magazine rack, and hardware. The dimensions are honest: 11.8"D x 15.7"W x 23.6"H — roughly the height of a standard end table. The three tiers are spaced well: 8.7 inches between the lower shelves, which accommodates tall books and small appliances without cramping. The bottom rack is a genuine add, not a gimmick — it holds magazines, tablets, or a basket of remotes.

## Build quality and real-world performance

The frame is a mix of powder-coated metal and laminated particle board. The legs have adjustable levelers, which I appreciated on a slightly uneven apartment floor — no wobble, no shims needed. Each tier supports up to 22 pounds, so a loaded bookshelf or a sizable plant won't stress it. The laminate surface resists scratches but is not waterproof; a reviewer noted, "I put a sweaty glass on it overnight and it left a white ring — use a coaster." That's fair warning.

Assembly took me about 20 minutes. The instructions are diagram-only, but straightforward. The included Allen wrench is annoyingly short, so bring your own Phillips screwdriver — you'll thank me.

## Honest shortcomings

The particle board is the biggest tradeoff. It's fine for a side table, but if you need something that can take a beating or survive frequent moves, look elsewhere. The lack of cable management holes is a minor annoyance — if you're using this as a charging station, you'll have cords dangling down the side. And at 23.6 inches tall, it's slightly shorter than a standard 26-inch end table; if you're pairing it with a tall sofa, check the height match.
