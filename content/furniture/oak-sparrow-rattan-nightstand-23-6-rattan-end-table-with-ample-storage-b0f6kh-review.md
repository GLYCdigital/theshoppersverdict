If you've been scrolling past a hundred identical black-and-brown nightstands, the Oak&Sparrow rattan end table is the one that makes you stop. That woven cane front isn't printed on — it's actual hand-woven rattan wrapped around solid MDF drawer fronts. At 23.6 inches wide and roughly 23 inches tall, it's sized right for a queen bed without dominating the room. But at 4.2 stars from 210 ratings, the story is more nuanced than "pretty boho table." Here's what you actually need to know.

**What's in the box**

You get the nightstand body, two drawers, a lower open shelf, and the hardware. Assembly takes about 25 minutes with a Phillips head screwdriver — the predrilled holes line up well, and the included hardware is decent. One Amazon reviewer noted, "Took me maybe 20 minutes to put together alone. The instructions are mostly pictures, which is fine because it's pretty intuitive." The back panel is thin MDF, so don't overtighten those screws.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The frame is sturdy MDF with a warm natural finish that reads more expensive than the price suggests. The rattan weave is the star — it's tight, consistent, and has that organic texture that instantly warms up a bedroom. The top surface handles a lamp, a book, and a glass of water without flexing. The drawers glide smoothly on side rails and are deep enough for a Kindle, glasses, and charging cables. The open shelf below fits a basket or a stack of books.

The honest downside: the rattan is delicate. The weave at the corners is vulnerable — one reviewer warned, "My vacuum bumped the front corner and a small piece of cane split. It's not catastrophic but it's there." This isn't a piece for high-traffic kid rooms or clumsy pets. It's furniture for adults who treat their bedroom furniture with reasonable care.

**Where it falls short**

The drawers are the weakest link. They're not dovetailed, and the side rails are basic — overload them with heavy items and you'll feel the difference. One buyer put it plainly: "The drawers are fine for nightstand stuff, but I wouldn't pile them with heavy things." Also, there's no cable cutout in the back panel. If you want to run a lamp cord or charger through the back, you'll need to drill your own hole or route the cord awkwardly around the side.

**Who should buy this**

- **Boho/coastal decor lovers** — the natural rattan finish is the whole point, and it delivers
- **Smaller bedrooms** — the 23.6-inch width fits tighter spaces without sacrificing storage
- **Anyone tired of flat-pack, all-wood lookalikes** — this has actual texture and warmth

**Skip it if** you need a heavy-duty nightstand for a rough-and-tumble household. The rattan won't survive being treated like a $40 pressboard table.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the rattan scratch or snag easily?**
A: The flat-woven cane on the drawer fronts is fairly smooth and won't catch on clothing. The edges and corners are the vulnerable spots — avoid bumping them with vacuums or hard objects.

**Q: Is the finish consistent across pieces?**
A: Mostly yes, but natural rattan can vary slightly in tone from batch to batch. One reviewer mentioned their first unit had a lighter finish than expected; Amazon's replacement policy covers this easily if it bothers you.

**Q: Can this fit a lamp and a book on top?**
A: Yes. The top surface is roughly 23.6'' x 15.7'' — enough for a standard bedside lamp, a hardcover, and a phone charger without crowding.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want genuine rattan texture and two drawers plus a shelf in a compact footprint, and you'll treat it with reasonable care. **Skip it if** you need heavy-duty construction or have a high-traffic home.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — a beautiful, well-priced boho piece with one real fragility (the weave) and one design oversight (no cable hole). For the style and storage, it's a solid buy.
