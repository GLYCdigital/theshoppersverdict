# LITTLE TREE Farmhouse Coffee Table Review: Solid Wood Charm With a Few Rough Edges

You know the drill. You search "farmhouse coffee table" and get 40 pages of identical-looking tables with "rustic" slapped in the title, most of them made from glorified cardboard. The LITTLE TREE Farmhouse Coffee Table actually tries to be different — and for the most part, it delivers.

With a 4.4-star average across 220 ratings, this table has clearly found its audience. But is it the right pick for your living room? Here's the honest breakdown after digging through buyer feedback and the specs.

## What You Actually Get

The table arrives in three boxes (the top, base, and lower shelf ship separately to prevent damage). Inside, you get the solid acacia wood tabletop, a powder-coated black steel frame, the lower display shelf, and all necessary hardware.

As shown in the product image, the design is classic farmhouse: a chunky wood slab on top, clean black metal legs, and a lower shelf for storage or decor. The dimensions — roughly 47 inches long, 24 inches wide, and 18 inches tall — make it a solid fit for most standard three-seat sofas.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Here's where this table separates itself from the budget pack. The top is genuine acacia wood, not a photo-finish veneer. That means you get real grain variation, natural color differences, and a surface that will actually age gracefully. Buyers consistently mention the wood quality as the standout feature. One reviewer put it plainly: "The top is heavy and solid — this isn't one of those hollow-sounding tables."

The metal frame is 1.2mm thick steel, welded at the joints rather than just screwed together. That gives the table a reassuring stability. It doesn't wobble, even on carpet. The overall weight (around 35 pounds) means it stays put when you lean on it.

Assembly is straightforward. The pre-drilled holes align properly, and the included Allen wrench does the job. Most buyers report 20-30 minutes from box to fully assembled. One caveat: the instructions are mostly diagrams, so if you're not comfortable with basic furniture assembly, set aside a little extra time.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring themes in positive reviews are clear:

- **The wood top quality.** Multiple reviewers note it looks far more expensive than the price point suggests.
- **Stability.** No wobble, no creaking, even with kids leaning on it.
- **The farmhouse aesthetic.** It photographs well and matches both modern and traditional decor.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the weak points, because they matter.

**The lower shelf is particle board.** Not solid wood. If you plan to set drinks or plants on that shelf, you'll want coasters and trays. Liquid left sitting will cause swelling and bubbling. This is the most common complaint in negative reviews.

**The finish off-gasses.** Several buyers report a strong chemical smell for the first few days. Plan to assemble it in a ventilated area and let it air out before bringing it inside.

**The black frame scuffs easily.** It's powder-coated, which is good, but it's not scratch-proof. Moving the table or bumping it with vacuum cleaners will leave marks. Touch-up paint isn't included.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want a real wood tabletop at a reasonable price, you're furnishing a living room or great room with a standard-size sofa, and you appreciate the farmhouse look without wanting to spend boutique furniture store prices.

**Skip it if:** You need a table for heavy daily use where the lower shelf will take abuse, you're extremely sensitive to chemical odors, or you have light-colored flooring where scuff marks on the legs will drive you crazy.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Is the wood top real wood or veneer?**
A: It's solid acacia wood. You can see the grain on the edges, not just the top surface. It's not a wrapped veneer.

**Q: Will this fit in a small apartment?**
A: At 47 inches long, it's on the larger side of coffee table standards. Measure your space first. It works in smaller rooms but will dominate the center of the space.

**Q: Does the lower shelf hold much weight?**
A: The frame supports it fine, but the shelf itself is particle board. Keep it to lightweight decor, books, and baskets — not heavy electronics or potted plants with standing water.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The LITTLE TREE Farmhouse Coffee Table earns its rating through a genuinely solid wood top and sturdy construction at a price point where most competitors cut corners. The particle board lower shelf and off-gassing finish are real compromises, but they don't undermine the table's core purpose.

**Buy it if** you want the farmhouse look with real wood where it matters most. **Skip it if** you need a workhorse table that can take daily abuse on every surface. For most shoppers, this is a solid, honest purchase that will look good for years.
