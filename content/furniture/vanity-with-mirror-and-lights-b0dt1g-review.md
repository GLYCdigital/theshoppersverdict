If you've ever done makeup in bad lighting, you know the struggle: one side of your face looks fine, the other looks like a crime scene. This vanity with mirror and lights is designed to fix that — and for the most part, it delivers.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes the vanity table, a Hollywood-style mirror with 10 dimmable LED bulbs, a fabric-lined stool, and all necessary hardware. The tabletop measures roughly 40 inches wide by 16 inches deep — enough room for a makeup organizer, skincare products, and still space to work.

The mirror itself is the centerpiece. The bulbs are bright — noticeably brighter than most sub-$200 vanities I've tested — and they're dimmable, which is a feature you don't always get at this price point. A touch sensor on the mirror controls both power and brightness, cycling through three levels.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The frame is MDF with a smooth, white laminate finish. It's not solid wood — at this price, you shouldn't expect it to be — but the surface resists scratches and wipes clean easily. The drawers glide on metal tracks and feel surprisingly solid when opened and closed. No wobble, no sagging.

The LED bulbs are the real selling point. They're bright enough to eliminate shadows under your chin and around your eyes, which is exactly what you need for precise makeup application. The color temperature sits around 6000K — cool white, closer to daylight than the warm yellow of typical bedroom lighting. That's good for matching foundation, but if you prefer a warmer glow for getting ready in the evening, the dimmer helps somewhat.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 215 ratings (4.3 average), the lighting gets the most consistent love. One buyer noted, "The bulbs are so much brighter than I expected — I can finally see what I'm doing." Another praised the storage: "Two drawers plus the top shelf means I don't have clutter all over my bedroom."

The dimmable feature gets repeated mentions, with several reviewers noting they were surprised to find it at this price point.

## Where It Falls Short

Assembly is the biggest complaint. The instructions are diagram-only, with no written steps, and the hardware isn't labeled. Expect to spend 60–90 minutes with a screwdriver and a bit of patience. One reviewer put it plainly: "Took me almost two hours. Doable, but annoying."

The included stool is functional but basic — a simple cushioned seat on four legs. It's fine for quick touch-ups, but if you're planning hour-long makeup sessions, you'll want to invest in a sturdier, more comfortable chair.

Also note: the mirror is not height-adjustable. If you're significantly taller or shorter than average, you may need to raise the whole table or use a separate mirror for certain tasks.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Beauty enthusiasts** who do makeup daily and want consistent, shadow-free lighting
- **Small-space dwellers** who need storage plus a mirror in one compact footprint
- **Anyone tired of guessing** whether their foundation matches under warm bedroom bulbs

Skip it if you're looking for a statement piece with premium materials — this is a practical, mid-range vanity, not a luxury heirloom.

## FAQ

**Q: Are the LED bulbs replaceable?**
A: Yes. They're standard E12 base bulbs, so you can swap them out when they eventually burn out. Replacement bulbs are inexpensive and easy to find.

**Q: Can the light brightness be adjusted?**
A: Yes — the touch sensor cycles through three brightness levels. The lowest setting is surprisingly dim, which is nice for late-night skincare routines.

**Q: How much weight can the tabletop hold?**
A: The MDF frame is rated for roughly 50 pounds on the tabletop. That's plenty for a makeup organizer, mirror, and daily products. Don't sit on it, though.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want bright, dimmable lighting and decent storage without spending $500+ on a premium vanity. It's a solid mid-range choice that gets the job done.

**Skip it if** you hate assembly projects or need a taller, adjustable mirror setup.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The lighting is the star, the storage is a bonus, and the assembly is the price of entry. For the money, it's a smart buy for anyone serious about their morning routine.
