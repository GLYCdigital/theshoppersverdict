## Where It Falls Short

Honest tradeoffs to consider:

**No mattress retention system.** There are no velcro straps or grippy strips on the top surface. Memory foam mattresses, especially thinner ones, can shift slightly. A non-slip rug pad between mattress and frame solves this for about $8.

**Potential squeaking on uneven floors.** The metal-to-metal connection points can develop a squeak if your floor isn't level. Adding felt pads under the legs or a thin rubber washer at the joints eliminates this — but it's an extra step.

**No box spring height option.** This is strictly 9 inches. If you need 12+ inches to match your existing furniture, this won't work.
