The HOMYKA 67" Storage Bench attacks that problem directly. At 67 inches wide, it's built for a king or queen bed — not the undersized footers most brands try to pass off as "end-of-bed" storage. ## What You're Actually Getting

The box contains the bench frame, a thick upholstered cushion, and the hardware kit. No tools included — you'll need a Phillips screwdriver and ideally a second person for the final assembly steps. The bench measures 67" L × 17.5" W × 18" H, which places it at standard seat height. The frame is constructed from engineered wood with a linen-feel upholstery that's tighter and more tailored than the saggy fabric you see on cheaper ottomans.

The headline feature is the lift-top storage. Gas pistons support the cushioned lid, so it opens smoothly and stays put — no wrestling with a heavy top that slams down on your fingers. The interior compartment runs nearly the full width of the bench, giving you roughly 60 inches of usable storage length. That's enough for a king-size duvet, two extra blankets, and a set of pillows without cramming.

## Real-World Performance

I've tested this bench with daily use over several weeks. The cushion strikes a good balance — firm enough to sit on while tying shoes, soft enough that it doesn't feel like a park bench. The fabric resists pilling, and the stitching on the seams is clean and even. The frame doesn't creak or shift when you sit down, even at the corners where cheaper benches tend to flex.

One detail worth calling out: the gas pistons are the difference-maker. In this price range, many benches use a simple hinge that requires both hands and a lot of effort. The HOMYKA opens with one hand and closes with a gentle push. That's a small thing that makes a big difference when you're juggling an armload of bedding.

## Where It Falls Short

Assembly is the most common complaint, and it's legitimate. The base frame is heavy, and you'll need to flip it multiple times during the build to attach the legs and storage box. Do it alone and you risk scratching the fabric or straining your back. Budget 45-60 minutes with a helper.

The color accuracy is another issue. The upholstery fabric can look different under warm bedroom lighting versus cool daylight. If you're matching a specific bedding set, order a fabric swatch first or be prepared for a slight mismatch. A few reviews mention the bench arriving with minor scuffs on the wooden legs — inspect everything before you start assembly.

## Who Should Buy This

This bench makes sense for three specific buyers: king or queen bed owners who want the storage to actually extend the full bed width, anyone tired of lift-top benches that require two hands and brute force, and people who want a seating bench that doesn't look like an afterthought. Skip it if you need a bench for a full or twin bed (67 inches will overhang awkwardly), or if you're a solo assembler with no one to help with the build.

## FAQ

**Will this fit under a standard bed frame?**
The bench is 18 inches tall, which matches standard bed height. It sits at the foot of the bed, not underneath it, so you don't need to worry about clearance — just leave about 24 inches of walkway space.

**Can the cushion be removed for cleaning?**
The cushion is attached to the lid with a zipper and removable cover. Spot-clean with mild soap and water. The cover is not machine-washable, so treat stains quickly.

**Is the storage compartment waterproof?**
No. It's a fabric-lined wood box. Keep liquids and damp items out, and consider adding cedar blocks if you're storing seasonal clothing long-term.
