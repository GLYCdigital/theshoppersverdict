**ADORNEVE Tall Night Stand Review: Does the Charging Station and LED Light Setup Justify the Price?**

Most nightstands are the same boring rectangle with a drawer. The ADORNEVE Tall Night Stand tries to be different — it stacks a charging station, LED lights, and a taller profile into one piece. At 4.2 stars from 210 ratings, it's clearly hitting the mark for many buyers. But is it right for your bedroom?

Let's dig into what you're actually getting.

**What's in the box**

The ADORNEVE arrives flat-packed with the expected hardware, an Allen wrench, and clear instructions. You get the main frame, two drawers, the charging station panel, and the LED light strip that mounts underneath the top surface. Assembly runs 30 to 45 minutes for most people — no power tools required, though a drill with a Phillips bit will speed things up considerably.

The charging panel is the headline feature: two USB-A ports and one USB-C port built into the back edge of the top shelf. That's genuinely useful if you charge a phone and earbuds overnight. The LED strip wraps around the bottom edge of the top piece, casting a soft glow downward. Multiple color options and brightness levels are controlled by a small button on the side.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The frame is engineered wood with a laminated finish — standard for this price tier. The drawers glide on metal runners and feel solid when opened and closed. The taller height is the standout design choice. As one buyer put it, "It's actually tall enough that I don't have to roll out of bed to reach my phone." That's a real quality-of-life improvement if you've got a high-profile mattress and box spring.

The charging ports output standard 5V/2.1A — enough to charge a phone overnight, but don't expect rapid charging speeds. The USB-C port is a welcome addition that many competitors still skip. The LED light is surprisingly bright for the size and can serve as a dim night light or a mood light depending on your setting.

**What buyers consistently praise**

- **The height.** Multiple reviewers specifically called out the taller design as the reason they chose this over standard nightstands.
- **The integrated charging.** One verified purchase wrote, "No more cables draped over the edge. Everything plugs into the back and it looks clean."
- **The LED lighting.** Reviewers like that it doubles as a night light without a separate lamp taking up surface space.

**Where it falls short**

The honest downsides matter. A few buyers reported the predrilled holes on the drawer slides didn't align perfectly, requiring slight adjustment during assembly. It's fixable, but frustrating if you're not handy.

The bigger concern: the LED strip is not user-replaceable. If the wiring fails down the road, you're looking at a decorative piece with dead lights. The charging ports also stop working if the power cord gets dislodged — and the cord is short, so you'll need an outlet within about 18 inches of the unit.

The finish is prone to fingerprints and smudges. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you're a neat freak, keep a microfiber cloth nearby.

**Who should buy this**

- **Tall-bed owners** — if your mattress sits high, this is one of the few nightstands that actually matches the height.
- **Cable clutter haters** — the built-in ports consolidate charging into one clean spot.
- **Small apartments** — the integrated LED eliminates the need for a separate lamp, freeing up surface space.

**Skip it if** you need a nightstand with a serious drawer for storage (the drawers are shallow), or if you're not comfortable with minor assembly adjustments.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the charging station work without the LED light?**
Yes — they're on separate circuits. You can use the USB ports without the lights on, and vice versa.

**Q: Is the LED light bright enough to read by?**
It's more of a mood light than a reading light. You'll want a separate lamp for reading in bed.

**Q: What's the actual weight capacity?**
The top can handle roughly 20–25 pounds safely. A lamp, glasses, and a book are fine — don't plan on stacking a mini-fridge up there.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a taller nightstand that genuinely improves your bedside experience with integrated charging and lighting. It's a smart buy for modern bedrooms where cable management matters.

**Skip it if** you need serious drawer storage or you're worried about long-term LED reliability.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The ADORNEVE delivers on its core promises with a few assembly and durability caveats. At this price, it's a solid upgrade over a basic nightstand, especially if you're tired of fishing for your phone on the floor every morning.
