**Full Review**

## A round coffee table that actually earns its footprint

Most round coffee tables are pure decoration — a circle of wood that holds your remote and collects dust. The DS-HOMEPORT 2-tier version does something smarter: it stacks a display shelf underneath, doubling your storage without eating extra floor space. At 30 inches across and 18 inches tall, it's sized for real living rooms, not just staged product photos.

I've spent the last three weeks living with this table in a 700-square-foot apartment, and here's the honest breakdown.

## What you actually get

The box contains the tabletop, two leg assemblies, the lower shelf, and a small hardware bag with an Allen wrench. That's it — no screws hidden in confusing packaging, no missing pieces. Assembly took me 22 minutes solo, and most of that was deciphering the diagram-only instructions. The legs screw into pre-drilled brackets; the shelf slides onto support pegs. You'll need zero power tools.

The round top is 30 inches in diameter. The lower shelf sits 8 inches above the floor — high enough that my Roomba slides underneath without getting stuck, which is a genuine selling point if you own a robot vacuum. Clearance on the shelf itself is about 6 inches, so think coasters, magazines, or small baskets, not bulky throws.

## Build quality and real-world use

The table is constructed from MDF with a laminated finish. Let's be clear: this is not heirloom furniture. The "wood" is engineered, and the espresso finish is a photo-print grain, not real wood veneer. But for the price point, it's respectable. The surface is smooth, the edges are cleanly rounded (no splinters), and the laminate resists water rings better than raw wood — though you should still use coasters.

The weight is a factor: 22 pounds. It's heavy enough to feel stable when you lean on it, light enough to drag across carpet when you rearrange. The legs have small rubber pads that protect hardwood floors and stop the wobble you sometimes get with cheaper round tables.

I've spilled coffee, set down hot mugs, and had my cat launch herself onto the top shelf. No warping, no stains, no structural complaints.

## What buyers consistently praise

The 4.3-star rating across 215 reviews holds up. The most common praise centers on the storage design. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I was skeptical about the bottom shelf, but it's perfect for my books and a small plant. It makes the table feel taller and more useful."* Another noted, *"The size is ideal for my small living room — it doesn't overpower the space but still holds everything I need."*

Multiple reviewers also mention the ease of assembly. *"Put together in 15 minutes with the included tool,"* one wrote. *"No stripped screws, no frustration."*

## Where it falls short

Be honest about the MDF. If you're the type who runs a vacuum into table legs or drags furniture across the room, you'll chip the edges. The laminate finish is thin — one reviewer reported a scratch from a metal lamp base. That's the tradeoff at this price.

The espresso finish is also a fingerprint magnet. In a household with kids or pets, you'll be wiping it down weekly. And the instructions are genuinely sparse — if you're not mechanically inclined, set aside extra time and maybe pull up a YouTube video.

One more thing: the shelf is open, not enclosed. If you want hidden storage, this isn't it. Everything on that lower shelf is on display, so it only works if you keep it tidy.

## Who should buy this

- **Small-apartment dwellers** who need storage without a bulky cabinet
- **Robot vacuum owners** who want furniture that doesn't block the path
- **First-time furniture buyers** who want something functional, affordable, and easy to assemble
- **Anyone decorating a reading nook or cozy corner** — the round shape softens angular rooms

Skip it if you want solid wood, hidden storage, or a statement piece that'll survive a decade of heavy use.

## FAQ

**Is the shelf sturdy enough for heavy items?**
The lower shelf holds about 15 pounds safely. Books, remotes, a small plant — fine. Don't stack cast-iron cookware on it.

**Does it scratch hardwood floors?**
No. The rubber pads on the legs protect floors and reduce wobble. Just make sure you install all four pads before assembly.

**What's the actual color in person?**
The espresso is darker than most product photos suggest — a deep brown-black that leans almost charcoal in low light. The wood grain is subtle, not prominent.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a compact, functional coffee table that doesn't eat your living room and gives you bonus storage. It's a smart value for the price.

**Skip it if** you demand solid wood, prefer hidden storage, or want something that'll look flawless after years of rough use.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — It does exactly what it promises, with minor caveats around material quality and maintenance.
