**Meta Description:** Honest LOAKEKEL Night Stand Set 2 review: LED lighting, USB-C ports, assembly ease, and real buyer feedback. Is this 2-pack nightstand worth it?

Let's cut through the listing photos. A $100-ish two-pack of nightstands with built-in charging and LED lighting sounds like a gimmick waiting to disappoint. But after digging through 215 ratings and the actual specs, the LOAKEKEL set is more legit than the price suggests — with a few caveats you should know before checkout.

**What you actually get**

Two identical nightstands. Each measures roughly 15.7" wide, 11.8" deep, and 21.7" tall. That's compact — apartment-friendly, not bedroom-showroom. Each unit has:
- A top surface with a flip-top compartment hiding the charging ports
- Two AC outlets embedded in the side (not the back, which is smart)
- One USB-A and one USB-C port per side
- A fabric drawer with soft-close glides
- LED strip lighting along the bottom edge, controlled by touch

The melamine surface is a practical choice — it shrugs off water rings and scratches better than the veneer on comparably priced sets. The legs are solid rubberwood, which is rare at this price point and gives it real stability.

**Performance and real-world use**

The charging station is the headline feature and it works as advertised. The USB-C port delivers 15W, which will fast-charge most modern phones (though not laptops). The AC outlets are positioned on the left side of each unit — check your bed layout before ordering because you can't swap them. The 4.9-foot power cord reaches most outlets behind a standard bed frame, but if your outlet is more than five feet from the headboard, you'll need an extension cord, which kills the clean look.

The LED lighting is warm and adjustable — three brightness levels and a color temperature toggle from cool white to warm amber. It's not app-controlled or voice-activated; it's a simple touch button. That's fine for a nightstand. The light casts a soft glow downward, which is nice for middle-of-the-night bathroom trips without blinding your partner.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The drawer is a recurring highlight. One reviewer noted: "The soft-close mechanism actually works — no slamming at 2 AM." Another mentioned: "The drawer glides are smoother than my $400 dresser." The charging speeds get consistent approval too: "My iPhone hits 50% in about 30 minutes from the USB-C port. No joke."

**Where it falls short**

Assembly is the biggest complaint. The drawer alignment is the culprit — one buyer called it "the most frustrating 45 minutes of my weekend." The instructions are adequate but not great, and you'll need a Phillips screwdriver. Plan for an hour total for both units.

The LED strip is integrated — if it dies, you're not swapping a bulb. That's a real long-term durability question. At this price, you're rolling the dice on the LED lifespan.

The depth is tight. A phone in a bulky case (think OtterBox) plus a charging cable can make the top feel crowded. The flip-top compartment helps, but it's not a deep storage space.

**Who should buy this**

- **Apartment dwellers** with limited floor space who want nightstands that do double duty
- **Couples** who need bedside charging without visible cable clutter
- **Anyone** who wants the modern LED-nightstand look without paying boutique furniture prices

Skip it if you need substantial drawer storage (this drawer is shallow) or if you want a piece that'll survive a decade of heavy daily use — this is solidly mid-tier furniture, not heirloom quality.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do the AC outlets work without the LED lighting on?**
A: Yes, the outlets and USB ports are independent of the LED strip. You can have charging active with the lights off completely.

**Q: Can the LED color be changed or is it just brightness?**
A: It's a fixed color range — cool white to warm amber. No RGB cycling or custom colors. It's a two-setting toggle, not a color wheel.

**Q: How much weight can the top hold?**
A: The frame supports roughly 30 pounds per unit. A lamp, phone, and a book are fine. Don't sit on it or stack heavy decor.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want functional, modern nightstands with genuinely useful charging built in, and you're okay with a modest assembly project.

**Skip it if** you need deep storage, want replaceable LED components, or your bed layout requires the outlets on the right side.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The LOAKEKEL set delivers on its promises at a fair price. The charging ports are real, the LED lighting is a nice touch, and the build quality exceeds the price point. The assembly frustration and non-replaceable LED strip cost it a half-star. For the price, it's a smart purchase — just budget an hour for setup and don't expect heirloom craftsmanship.
