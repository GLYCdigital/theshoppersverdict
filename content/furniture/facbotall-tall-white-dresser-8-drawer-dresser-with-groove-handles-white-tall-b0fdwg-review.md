**FACBOTALL Tall White Dresser Review: 8 Drawers, 4 Square Feet of Floor Space**

If you've ever tried to fit a standard six-drawer dresser into a bedroom that's barely wider than the bed, you know the struggle. The FACBOTALL Tall White Dresser solves that problem by going vertical — 55 inches up instead of 4 feet wide. The tradeoff? A tall, narrow silhouette that's a bit tippy without proper anchoring. Here's the full story after digging through 204 ratings and the spec sheet.

**What You Actually Get**

Out of the box, you get eight drawers, a frame, and the hardware bag. The dimensions are the headline: 15.7" deep by 23.6" wide by 55.1" tall. That's roughly the footprint of a small nightstand, but with the capacity of a full dresser. The groove handles are actually routed channels in the drawer fronts — a nice touch that means no protruding knobs to catch on sleeves or snag in tight hallways.

The white laminate finish on particleboard is clean and uniform. It's not solid wood, and you shouldn't expect it to be at this price point. But the laminate is applied well, with no visible seams on the drawer fronts in the product images.

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

The construction is decent for the category. All eight drawers ride on metal slides with ball bearings — they open smoothly when aligned correctly. The drawer boxes themselves are dovetail-jointed particleboard with a fabric-lined bottom, which is standard at this tier.

Where this dresser shines is capacity per square foot. The bottom three drawers are deeper — roughly 9.8" — and will hold jeans, sweatshirts, and folded linens. The top five drawers are shallower, better for t-shirts, socks, and accessories. One buyer noted, "I fit my entire wardrobe in this thing — it replaced a 6-drawer dresser and a small armoire."

The vertical design does have a tradeoff you need to plan for: the top drawers sit at 45+ inches off the floor. If you're under 5'4", you'll be reaching up. If you're over 6 feet, the top drawer is at a comfortable height. Consider your household's height before committing.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Across the 204 ratings, three themes dominate positive reviews:

- **Space efficiency**: The tall, narrow footprint is the reason most people bought it. One verified purchase wrote, "Perfect for my tiny apartment bedroom — I couldn't fit a normal dresser, but this holds everything."
- **Clean aesthetic**: The white finish and groove handles give a modern, minimal look that buyers consistently say "looks more expensive than it is."
- **Sturdiness once assembled**: Multiple reviewers note that with the back panel secured and all screws tightened, the unit feels solid and doesn't wobble.

**Where It Falls Short**

The most common complaint is assembly. At roughly 60-90 minutes, it's not the worst furniture assembly I've seen, but the drawer slides are finicky. If you don't align them perfectly during installation, drawers stick or sit crooked. Several reviewers reported this — one wrote, "Third drawer has never aligned right no matter what I do." Take your time with this step, and have a second person available to hold panels steady.

The second issue is safety. This dresser is tall and narrow — a textbook tip-over hazard. FACBOTALL does not include an anti-tip bracket in the box. You need to purchase one separately and attach it to a stud. If you have kids or pets, this isn't optional. The 4.1-star average reflects these assembly and anchoring frustrations, with several 3-star reviews citing the missing anchor.

**Who Should Buy This**

Buy it if you live in an apartment, dorm, or small bedroom where floor space is premium. It's also a solid choice for a guest room or nursery where you need storage without a bulky footprint. Skip it if you're over 6'2" (the top drawers will feel low), if you want solid wood construction, or if you're not comfortable with a 90-minute IKEA-style assembly session.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the dresser come with an anti-tip anchor?**
A: No. You must supply your own wall anchor. This is a safety must, not an option, given the tall, narrow design.

**Q: Can I fit a queen-size comforter in the bottom drawers?**
A: Folded, yes — the bottom three drawers are about 22" wide, 13" deep, and 9.8" tall. A bulky comforter will need to be compressed, but it fits.

**Q: How long does assembly actually take?**
A: Plan for 60-90 minutes with a power drill. Hand-screwing everything will take closer to 2 hours. The drawer slide alignment is the tricky part — go slowly.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need serious storage in a tiny footprint and don't mind an evening of assembly. The FACBOTALL Tall White Dresser delivers exactly what it promises: eight drawers of clean, white storage in about four square feet of floor space.

**Skip it if** you want solid wood, you're very tall, or you won't anchor it to the wall. This is a well-executed budget dresser, not a heirloom piece.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The space efficiency and price win the day, docked slightly for assembly fussiness and the missing anti-tip hardware.
