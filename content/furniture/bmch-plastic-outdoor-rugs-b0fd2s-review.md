Plastic rugs get a bad rap. They're either flimsy tarps that blow across the yard or stiff mats that look like they belong in a car trunk. The BMCH Plastic Outdoor Rug sits somewhere more interesting: it's a flat-woven, PVC-based rug that actually behaves like a rug, not a tarp. At $30–$50 depending on size, it's competing with the budget end of the outdoor market — and for many shoppers, it's the right call.

**What you actually get**

The BMCH arrives rolled tight, wrapped in plastic. Unroll it, and it lies flat within minutes — no bricks on corners, no wrestling with curled edges. The weave is a tight, basket-style pattern with a low profile (about 3mm thick) and a slightly textured surface that grips shoes and bare feet. Sizes run from small 3'x5' doormats up to 8'x10' patio coverage. The underside is a smooth PVC backing that stays put on concrete, wood decking, and even synthetic turf.

**Real-world performance**

I tested the 6'x9' version on a covered porch for six weeks. Morning dew, afternoon sun, the occasional dropped glass of iced tea — it took everything without complaint. A quick rinse with the hose and a stiff broom brought it back to new. No mildew smell, no color bleeding onto the concrete beneath. The weave is tight enough that sand and pine needles don't get trapped in it, which is more than I can say for most indoor-outdoor rugs at this price.

The rug's weight is a mixed bag. At roughly 4–5 pounds for a 6'x9', it's light enough to shake out and reposition, but on windy days on an open deck, corners will lift. The included rubber backing helps, but if you're placing it in an exposed area, a few outdoor rug tape strips are worth the extra $8.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews is consistent with what I found. Buyers repeatedly mention the color accuracy and the flat-weave texture. One verified purchase put it simply: "Looks way more expensive than it is. The pattern is crisp and it doesn't look like a plastic mat." Another noted, "Bought for my RV patio. Survived a full summer in Arizona without fading." The ease of cleaning comes up again and again — "hose it off, hang it to dry, done."

**Where it falls short**

Be honest about the tradeoffs. The rug is thin. If your patio is uneven concrete or you're placing it over pavers with gaps, you'll feel every ridge through your shoes. There's zero padding, so if you plan to sit or kneel on it for long stretches, bring a cushion. The colors are vivid out of the box, but multiple reviewers note fading after a full summer in unshaded areas. If your patio gets six-plus hours of direct sun daily, expect the deep blues and reds to soften to pastel by season two. Finally, the largest sizes (8'x10') are heavy enough that one person will struggle to move them alone.

**Who should buy this**

- **Renters and apartment dwellers** — it's affordable, easy to clean, and you can leave it behind without guilt.
- **Campers and RV owners** — lightweight, folds or rolls compactly, and shrugs off mud and rain.
- **Parents with kids and pets** — spills and accidents are just a hose-down away.

**Skip it if** you need a cushioned rug for a seating area, or if your patio is fully exposed to harsh sun year-round and you want colors to stay true for more than a couple of seasons.

**FAQ**

*Will this rug blow away in strong wind?*  
On open decks, yes. The 6'x9' weighs about 4.5 pounds — enough for normal breezes, not enough for gusts. Use rug tape or place furniture on the corners for exposed areas.

*Can I use it indoors?*  
Technically yes, but it's not designed for living rooms. The texture is rough on bare feet, and there's no softness underfoot. It's better suited for mudrooms, garages, or covered patios.

*How do I clean it?*  
Hose it down, use a mild soap if needed, and let it air dry. A leaf blower works great for quick debris removal between washes.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want an affordable, genuinely weatherproof rug that looks sharp and cleans up in minutes. **Skip it if** you need cushioning or your patio bakes in unrelenting sun all day.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid value with honest limitations. At this price, the BMCH is one of the better budget outdoor rugs available, and it earns its place on patios, porches, and campsites across the country.
