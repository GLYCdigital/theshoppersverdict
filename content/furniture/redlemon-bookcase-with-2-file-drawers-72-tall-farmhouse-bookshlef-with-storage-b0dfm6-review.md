**The 72-inch bookcase that tries to do everything — and mostly pulls it off**

You know the problem: you need book storage, but you also have a pile of paperwork that can't live on a shelf. Most tall bookcases force you to choose. The RedLemon 72" Farmhouse Bookcase tries to give you both — five open shelves up top and two file drawers down below. After spending time with the specs and combing through 220 ratings, here's the real story.

**What you actually get**

Out of the box, you're looking at a 71.6" tall, 31.5" wide, 15.7" deep unit. That depth matters — it's shallow enough to fit a narrow hallway or behind a door, but deep enough for standard books and file folders. The two bottom drawers are letter-size compatible (they accept hanging file folders), and the top five shelves are adjustable, which is rare at this price point.

The farmhouse styling comes from the X-brace details on the sides and the warm wood-grain laminate. It's not real wood — it's engineered board with a printed grain — but the finish is convincing from three feet away, and the matte texture doesn't show fingerprints like glossy alternatives.

**Build quality and real-world use**

Here's where the 4.4-star average starts to make sense. The frame is solid — multiple buyers note the unit doesn't wobble once assembled, and the shelves hold full hardcover sets without visible bowing. The drawers glide on metal slides and have a safety stop, so they won't dump out if you pull too hard.

But assembly is the friction point. The instructions are mostly diagrams with minimal text, and the drawer alignment is the trickiest part. Plan for 90 minutes with two people. One reviewer put it plainly: *"The drawers took three attempts to get the slides parallel. Once they clicked, they've been perfect for six months."* That tracks.

The most common complaint across ratings is the back panel — it's thin fiberboard that can arrive with a slight curve. It's cosmetic, not structural, but you'll want to staple it carefully to avoid a visible gap.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The storage flexibility gets the most love. The adjustable shelves mean you can configure a 12-inch shelf for tall binders and a 7-inch shelf for paperbacks in the same unit. The file drawers are genuine hanging-file compatible, which is rare in furniture at this price. One verified buyer summed it up: *"Finally a bookcase that doesn't force me to choose between books and files. The drawers are smooth and the shelves are sturdy enough for my entire cookbook collection."*

**Where it falls short**

At 72 inches tall, this is a tip-over risk if you have kids or pets. The manufacturer doesn't include a wall-anchor kit — you'll need to buy one separately. That's a genuine safety miss.

The laminate finish is durable but not scratch-proof. A sharp object will leave a mark, and the edges are vulnerable to chipping if you bump them during assembly. Also, the drawers are full-extension but not soft-close — they'll thunk if you let them go.

**Who should buy this**

- **Home office workers** who need both book storage and file access in one footprint
- **Small-space dwellers** in apartments or condos where a lateral file cabinet plus a bookcase won't fit
- **Farmhouse-style decorators** who want the X-frame look without paying solid-wood prices

Skip it if you need a heavy-duty library shelf for oversized art books, or if you're not comfortable with multi-step assembly.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do the drawers hold standard hanging file folders?**
A: Yes — they're sized for letter-size hanging files. The interior drawer depth is about 12 inches, which fits both regular and oversized folders.

**Q: Can I adjust the shelf heights after assembly?**
A: Yes. The shelf pins are removable, and each shelf has multiple height positions. You'll need to empty the shelf to adjust it, but it's a simple process.

**Q: Does it come with a wall-anchor kit?**
A: No. The box includes assembly tools and hardware for the frame, but not wall anchors. Given the height, I strongly recommend buying a $10 anchor kit separately.

**The Verdict**

The RedLemon 72" Bookcase is a smart hybrid that solves a real problem — combining open display storage with concealed filing in a piece that looks more expensive than it is. The assembly headache and missing anchor kit keep it from a perfect score, but the build quality, adjustable shelves, and working file drawers deliver genuine value.

**Buy it if** you need book storage plus file storage in one vertical footprint and don't mind a 90-minute assembly session.

**Skip it if** you want soft-close drawers, solid wood construction, or a piece that's ready to go in under 30 minutes.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Recommended with the caveat to plan for assembly time and purchase wall anchors separately.
