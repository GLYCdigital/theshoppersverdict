**The Maihail 6 Drawer File Cabinet isn't trying to be the prettiest piece in your office. It's trying to be the most useful — and for the most part, it pulls it off.**

This rolling cabinet combines a six-drawer filing system with a built-in charging station and a flat top that doubles as a printer stand. At 4.2 stars from 210 ratings, it's solidly rated but not universally loved. Here's what you're actually getting.

**What's in the box**

You get the cabinet (disassembled), four locking casters, a charging station module, and all necessary hardware. The top surface measures roughly 23.6" deep by 15.7" wide — enough for a standard inkjet printer or a small monitor. The charging station includes two USB ports and one standard 3-prong outlet, all rated for typical household current.

The drawers split into four larger file drawers (letter/legal size) and two smaller half drawers on top. That's a smart layout — you get hanging-file capacity where you need it and a place for pens, cables, and miscellany without dedicating a full drawer.

**Performance and real-world use**

Build quality is mid-range. The steel frame feels solid once assembled, and the drawer slides are ball-bearing, which makes a noticeable difference over cheaper friction slides. Full-extension means you can actually see the back of each drawer — no more fishing for misfiled documents.

The casters are the standout feature. Each wheel locks independently, and the cabinet rolls smoothly on both carpet and hardwood. If you're the kind of person who rearranges their workspace seasonally or needs to move files between rooms, this is a genuine advantage over stationary cabinets.

The charging station works as advertised. It sits on the back panel, so keep that in mind — the cabinet needs to be a few inches from the wall to access it comfortably. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's a design choice worth knowing.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Reviewers repeatedly mention the storage capacity and the mobility. One buyer wrote, *“I was skeptical about the charging station being useful, but I use it every day for my phone and wireless earbuds. The drawers hold a ton — I moved everything from a two-drawer file cabinet plus a desk organizer into this one unit.”*

Another echoed the versatility: *“The half drawers on top are perfect for office supplies. I no longer have a drawer of chaos.”*

**Where it falls short**

Assembly is the biggest complaint. Multiple reviewers noted that the drawer slides are finicky to align, and if you don't get them perfect, the drawers bind. Budget 45–60 minutes and follow the instructions to the letter. A few also mentioned that the metal frame can scratch if you overtighten bolts with a drill — use a hand wrench.

The charging station's placement on the back is the second most-cited issue. If your cabinet is pushed flush against a wall, you'll need to pull it forward to plug things in. It's manageable, but it's a quirk.

**Who should buy this**

- **Home office workers** who need combined file storage and a charger for their growing collection of devices
- **Small business owners** who want a mobile filing solution they can roll between desks or rooms
- **Students** with a lot of paperwork and limited floor space — the vertical design earns its footprint

**Skip it if** you want a furniture-grade cabinet that looks like it belongs in a showroom. This is functional-first, and the metal finish is more utilitarian than elegant.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will it hold legal-size hanging files?**
Yes. The four main drawers accommodate both letter and legal hanging files. The two top half-drawers are sized for standard office supplies, not hanging files.

**Q: Can the casters handle carpet?**
They handle low-pile carpet fine. High-pile shag might catch the front casters when pushing — you'll want to lift slightly or accept some drag.

**Q: Is the charging station safe for a laptop?**
The USB ports are standard 5V/2.1A — fine for phones and tablets. For a laptop, use the AC outlet with your original charger, not the USB ports.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're drowning in paper and cables and need one piece of furniture to solve both. **Skip it if** you need a premium look or can't tolerate a 60-minute assembly session.

The Maihail 6 Drawer File Cabinet delivers exactly what it promises: serious storage, real mobility, and a charging station that isn't a gimmick. It's not flawless, but at 4.2 stars, it earns its rating.
