## What You Actually Get

The MIULEE Fluffy Rug is a machine-woven shaggy pile rug made from 100% polyester microplush. The pile height is substantial — roughly 1.2 inches of dense, silky fibers that feel significantly softer than the cheaper "fluffy" rugs you'll find at big box stores. The backing is a textured TPR (thermoplastic rubber) that grips floors surprisingly well.

You're getting a rectangle rug (sizes range from 2' x 3' up to 8' x 10'), and it arrives vacuum-sealed and folded. **Important:** it will look flat and lifeless when it arrives. Give it 24-48 hours to decompress, then give it a vigorous shake or vacuum with a brush attachment. The fluff factor returns fully.

I tested this in a bedroom (the classic use case) and here's what stood out: the softness is not just surface-level. The density of the fibers means your feet sink in slightly with each step. ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**Shedding is real.** For the first two to three weeks, you'll vacuum up a surprising amount of loose fibers. It's not defective — it's just how faux fur rugs behave. If you have allergies, budget for daily vacuuming initially.

**The texture requires maintenance.** In high-traffic paths, the fibers will mat down. You'll need to fluff them back up with your hand or a pet brush periodically. It's not set-and-forget.

**Sizing reality check.** The smaller sizes (2' x 3', 3' x 5') are true accent rugs. If you want a proper bedside or living room rug, expect to pay noticeably more for the larger cuts. The 8' x 10' isn't cheap.

**Not ideal for heavy furniture.** The pile compresses permanently under dresser legs or sofa feet. Use furniture coasters if you plan to place anything heavy on it.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want a cozy, indulgent texture for a bedroom, nursery, or reading nook. You're okay with a short break-in period of shedding. You appreciate a rug that makes a space feel instantly warmer and more inviting.

**Skip it if:** You need something for a high-traffic hallway or entryway — it'll mat down and look tired quickly. You have pets that dig or scratch at surfaces. You want a low-maintenance, set-it-and-forget-it rug.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can you really machine wash this rug?**
A: Yes, for sizes up to 5' x 8'. Use cold water on a gentle cycle, no bleach, and air dry (never machine dry). It comes out clean and fluffs back up. Larger sizes need spot cleaning or professional care.

**Q: Does it shed a lot?**
A: Initially, yes — expect visible loose fibers for 1-3 weeks. It diminishes significantly after that. Vacuuming with a brush attachment helps speed up the process.

**Q: Is the color true to the photos?**
A: Generally yes. Reviewers consistently note the colors match what's shown. Just be aware that the faux fur catches light differently than flat-weave rugs, so it may look slightly lighter or darker depending on your lighting.
