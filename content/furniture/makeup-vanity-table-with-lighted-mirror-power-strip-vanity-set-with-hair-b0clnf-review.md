**Is this vanity actually worth your money, or are you paying for a pretty product photo?** I've dug through 220 ratings, buyer photos, and the spec sheet to give you the straight story on the Vanit Makeup Vanity Table with Lighted Mirror & Power Strip.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the vanity table, a lighted mirror, one storage drawer, two open side shelves, and a matching stool. The table measures roughly 39.4" wide by 15.7" deep — compact enough for a bedroom corner but with enough surface area for a serious makeup collection. The drawer is deeper than most at this price point, and the side shelves hold your everyday products within arm's reach.

The headline feature is the mirror: a 30" x 20" rectangle with 72 LED bulbs around the frame. It's not just on/off — you get three color temperatures (warm, natural, cool) and dimming via touch controls. This matters more than you'd think. Natural light for daytime looks, warm for evening, cool for that harsh office fluorescent vibe. One buyer noted, *"I can finally see what my makeup looks like in actual office lighting before I leave the house."*

The integrated power strip sits on the desktop surface — two AC outlets plus two USB ports. It's a small thing, but if you've ever balanced a flat iron on the edge of a sink while getting ready, you understand why this is a genuine quality-of-life upgrade.

## Real-World Performance

The frame is engineered wood with a smooth, wipeable surface. It's not solid oak — and at this price, you shouldn't expect it to be — but multiple long-term reviewers confirm it holds up. One customer who's had it for 14 months says, *"No wobble, no sagging drawers, the lights still work perfectly."* The drawer slides are smooth, and the legs have adjustable levelers for uneven floors.

The mirror's touch controls are responsive — a quick tap cycles through light modes, and holding the button dims. The LED bulbs are rated for roughly 50,000 hours, meaning you'll replace the vanity before you replace the bulbs.

**Where it falls short:** Assembly. Expect to spend 45–60 minutes with the included Allen wrench (which is fine for the frame) but a Phillips screwdriver for the drawer hardware. Several buyers reported the drawer slide screw holes don't perfectly align, requiring some persuasion. It's fixable, but it's the most common complaint by far.

The stool is the other weak point. It's padded and functional, but the cushion is firm and the height is fixed. If you plan to sit for 30+ minute sessions, budget for a better chair eventually.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're setting up a dedicated makeup station in a bedroom or small apartment. You want versatile lighting without installing hardwired fixtures. You're tired of hunting for outlets while getting ready.

**Skip it if:** You're over 5'7" (the mirror height is fixed and you'll be looking down), you need serious drawer storage (this has one drawer — not a full dresser), or you're expecting heirloom furniture quality. This is a well-made functional piece, not a luxury investment.

## FAQ

**Q: Is the mirror bright enough for detailed work like eyeliner?**
Yes. At full brightness, it's comparable to a 10x magnifying mirror with a ring light. The dimming function helps avoid eye strain during longer sessions.

**Q: Can the power strip handle a hair dryer or curling iron?**
The AC outlets are standard 120V and handle typical styling tools fine. Just don't run a hair dryer and a space heater simultaneously — it's a standard 15-amp circuit, not a heavy-duty setup.

**Q: Does the mirror have a magnification option?**
No. It's a flat mirror only. If you need 5x or 10x magnification for precision work, you'll need to add a separate magnifying mirror.
