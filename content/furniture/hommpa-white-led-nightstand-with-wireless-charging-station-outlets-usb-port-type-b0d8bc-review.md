**Is the HOMMPA LED Nightstand a Smart Upgrade or Just a Gimmick?**

You're tired of the tangled mess of charging cables on your nightstand. You've knocked your phone onto the floor one too many times reaching for it in the dark. The HOMMPA White LED Nightstand with Wireless Charging Station promises to solve both problems in one piece of furniture. After digging through 204 ratings and the real customer experiences behind that 4.1-star average, here's what you actually need to know before buying.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The box contains the nightstand in flat-pack form, hardware, and a pre-wired power system. The unit measures roughly 20 inches wide by 16 inches deep and stands about 20 inches tall — compact enough for smaller bedrooms but substantial enough to hold a lamp, glasses, and a book. The tabletop features a marked wireless charging pad on one corner, while the side panel houses two USB-A ports and one standard three-prong outlet. The LED strip runs along the bottom edge of the frame and casts a warm glow downward.

**Real-World Performance**

The wireless charging pad is the headline feature, and it delivers. Most customers report their iPhones and Samsung Galaxy devices start charging immediately when placed on the marked spot. One reviewer noted, "I just set my phone down and it charges — no more hunting for the cable in the dark." The USB ports output standard 5V/2.1A, which tops up a phone reasonably fast overnight, though it won't match fast-charging wall adapters.

The LED lighting is controlled by a simple touch button near the charging pad. You get two brightness levels, and it's genuinely useful as a subtle night light. However — and this is worth noting — the LED system plugs into the built-in outlet. If you don't have a wall outlet within reach of where you want this nightstand, plan for an extension cord.

**Build Quality and Assembly**

The frame is engineered wood with a laminate white finish. It looks clean and modern in photos, and most buyers say it matches expectations. The drawer glides smoothly on metal tracks, and the overall construction feels stable once assembled. That said, assembly is the most common complaint. The legs attach with bolts that require aligning precisely, and the drawer needs adjustment to sit flush. Plan for 45 minutes to an hour if you're doing it solo.

**What Buyers Love**

The convenience factor dominates positive reviews. "This nightstand has been a game-changer," one customer wrote. "My phone, watch, and e-reader all charge from one spot, and the light is perfect for middle-of-the-night bathroom trips without waking my husband." Multiple reviewers mention the charging pad working reliably with cases on — no need to strip your phone down every night.

**Where It Falls Short**

The white finish is a magnet for dust and smudges. Several long-term owners mention needing to wipe it down more often than they'd like. The drawer is on the smaller side — roughly 12 inches deep — so don't expect to store large books or tablets in there. And if you're someone who prefers a totally dark bedroom, the LED light even at its lowest setting might be more than you want, though the touch button does turn it completely off.

**Who Should Buy This**

This nightstand is ideal for apartment dwellers with limited floor space who want to consolidate their charging setup. It's also great for older adults who struggle with fumbling cables in the dark — the glow and wireless charging remove both friction points. If you have a bed positioned away from wall outlets, though, skip this unless you're comfortable with an extension cord running along the baseboard.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Does the wireless charging work with iPhone MagSafe cases?**
Yes, most standard MagSafe and slim cases don't interfere. Thick OtterBox-style cases might slow charging but generally still work.

**Can the LED light stay on all night?**
It can, but it draws power through the outlet. Many users prefer the lower brightness setting for overnight use.

**Is the outlet grounded?**
Yes, it's a standard three-prong grounded outlet, so you can safely plug in lamps or other devices.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a clean, consolidated charging station that doubles as a functional nightstand and you have a wall outlet nearby. **Skip it if** you're not handy with assembly, prefer a darker bedroom, or need significant drawer storage.

The HOMMPA LED Nightstand earns a solid **4.2 out of 5 stars**. It's not perfect — the finish requires upkeep and assembly tests your patience — but for the convenience it delivers nightly, most buyers find it well worth the effort. At this price point, it's a smart upgrade over a basic nightstand plus separate charging accessories. Just budget time for setup and keep a microfiber cloth handy.
