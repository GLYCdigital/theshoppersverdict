One thing to understand upfront: this is not an inflatable mattress you blow up in five minutes. Second, the fill process is genuinely annoying. You need a garden hose connection near your bedroom, which means either running a long hose through your house or using a sink adapter. Finally, the 10-inch depth means you need a compatible frame. If you have an older waterbed frame, check the dimensions — some frames designed for thinner mattresses won't accommodate this one.

**FAQ: 3 Real Questions Shoppers Ask**

**Q: How long does filling actually take?**
A: Plan on 4–6 hours for a Queen size with average water pressure. The mattress needs to be filled to about 90% capacity — overfilling puts stress on the seams. After filling, give it 24 hours to stabilize before sleeping on it.

**Q: Does it fit a standard waterbed frame?**
A: Yes, if your frame accommodates a 10-inch mattress. Measure your frame's interior dimensions first. Older frames designed for 8-inch mattresses may be too shallow.

**Q: What does the conditioner actually do?**
A: It prevents algae and bacteria growth inside the mattress and conditions the vinyl to prevent cracking. It's not optional — water without conditioner will develop a musty odor within months.
