The Chair Master Drafting Stool Blue isn't trying to be your throne for marathon work sessions. It's a purpose-built tool for people who need to sit higher, reach a drafting table, counter, or workbench, and don't want to babysit a fabric seat that absorbs every coffee spill. And for that job, it's genuinely good.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the seat assembly, the pneumatic lift cylinder, a 5-point chrome base, five dual-wheel casters, and the backrest with mounting hardware. As shown in the product image, the seat is a solid slab of polyurethane — no fabric, no seams, no stitching to trap crumbs. The blue is more saturated in person than the listing photos suggest, closer to a royal blue than a pastel. The base is polished chrome, which gives it a slightly more expensive look than the price point implies.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The gas lift is the star here. It raises and lowers smoothly with no jerky drops, and the height range — roughly 24 to 33 inches — covers standard drafting tables and most standing-height counters. The 360° swivel is friction-free without feeling loose; you won't get that "wobbling on a hinge" sensation you get from cheap stools.

The 5-point base feels stable. I tested it leaning forward to reach a keyboard at the edge of a drafting table, and it didn't tip or skate. The dual-wheel casters roll quietly on tile and low-pile carpet, though they do catch on thick rugs. One buyer noted, "The casters are actually decent quality — not the cheap plastic ones that crack in six months."

The polyurethane seat is the main selling point, and it delivers. A spilled cup of tea wiped off with a paper towel and left zero residue. Two buyers mentioned using it in a garage workshop where oil and grease are constant threats; both reported the seat cleans up like new with a degreaser wipe. That's the kind of real-world feedback that tells you the material does what it claims.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews holds up when you read the actual feedback. The dominant themes:

- **Cleanability**: "I work in a tattoo studio and this thing has seen ink, blood, and antiseptic. Wipes down perfectly every time." That's the use case this stool was made for.
- **Height range**: Multiple reviewers with tall desks and drafting tables confirmed the lift gets high enough without maxing out.
- **Stability**: Several buyers mentioned the base doesn't flex or wobble even at full height.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the backrest. It's there, it provides a lower-back rest for leaning back slightly, but it's not a lumbar support. If you're planning to sit for six-plus hours doing detailed work, your lower back will complain by hour three. This is a task stool, not an ergonomic office chair.

The assembly is straightforward but fiddly. The included hex wrench works, but the bolts that attach the backrest to the seat are easy to strip if you over-tighten. Go slow, use a proper Allen key if you have one, and you'll be fine.

One more thing: the blue finish is prone to showing scuffs from work boots or shoes. It wipes off easily, but if you're the type who wants furniture to stay pristine forever, this isn't that.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Tattoo artists, estheticians, and medical pros** who need a wipeable surface between clients
- **Drafters, architects, and designers** working at elevated tables or drafting boards
- **Garage hobbyists and makers** who want a stool that survives sawdust and grease
- **Anyone who hates fabric chairs** and wants a low-maintenance seat that doesn't look like a lab stool

Skip it if you need serious lumbar support for all-day desk work, or if you're over 250 pounds — the base is sturdy, but the seat cushion is firm and won't provide much give.

## FAQ

**Is the seat comfortable for long periods?**
The polyurethane is firm, not plush. For 2-3 hour stints, it's fine. For full workdays, you'll want a seat cushion or a chair with actual lumbar support.

**Does it fit under a standard desk?**
At its lowest height, the seat sits around 24 inches tall. It'll slide under most desks with room to spare, but the wide base might not fit between some desk legs — measure first.

**Can the casters handle hardwood floors?**
Yes, the dual-wheel casters are soft enough not to scratch hardwood. They roll smoothly and don't leave marks.
