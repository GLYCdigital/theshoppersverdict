The Gizoon Sideboard is the furniture equivalent of that friend who's good at everything but doesn't brag about it. It works as a dining room buffet, a coffee bar, a TV console, or entryway storage. At 225 ratings with a 4.5-star average, it's clearly hitting a nerve with shoppers who want modern aesthetics without paying designer prices.

### What you actually get

The box contains the sideboard (roughly 47 inches wide, 15.5 inches deep, 31.5 inches tall), two cabinet doors, two interior shelves, a back panel, and all hardware. The manufacturer includes a hex wrench, which tells you assembly is DIY. The engineered wood panels arrive with cam locks pre-installed — you're not hunting for inserts or dealing with stripped screws.

The top surface is a matte finish that resists water rings better than I expected. As shown in the product image, the design is clean: two cabinet sections flank a slim center opening. That center gap is narrower than it looks in photos — roughly 11 inches — so it works for a coffee maker or a stack of plates, but not for a microwave or a large mixer.

### Real-world performance

The adjustable shelves inside each cabinet are the standout feature. Each side accepts items up to 13 inches tall, and the shelves move in 1.5-inch increments. One buyer noted, "I fit a full dinner service for eight on one side and my wine glasses and bottles on the other." That's realistic — the internal height and depth accommodate standard dinner plates stacked eight high and wine bottles lying flat or standing.

The cabinet doors use magnetic catches rather than soft-close hinges. They close securely, but they make a noticeable thud. If you're a light sleeper or have a baby napping near the dining area, expect to hear it.

Assembly is genuinely straightforward. The panels are labeled with letters that match the instruction sheet, and the cam locks tighten with the included wrench. Most buyers report 40-60 minutes solo. One reviewer summed it up: "Took me an hour with a beer and a podcast. Everything lined up perfectly."

### What buyers consistently praise

The most repeated compliments center on value and appearance. "Looks way more expensive than it is," appears in multiple reviews. The matte black finish photographs well and hides smudges — a real advantage in a dining area. Another frequent positive: the depth is shallow enough to fit against a wall without eating into walkway space, yet deep enough to store platters and small appliances.

### Where it falls short

The back panel is thin — roughly 3mm fiberboard. It's stapled into a groove, and if your floor is uneven, the cabinet will twist slightly, causing the doors to misalign. Level the unit before loading it. One buyer learned this the hard way: "After three months, one door started rubbing. I shimmed the front foot and it was fine, but you need to do this on day one."

The matte top scratches if you slide heavy objects across it. Use a mat or lift items. This isn't a defect — it's the nature of matte finishes — but it's worth knowing before you place a cast-iron serving tray directly on it.

The center open section has no shelf or drawer. It's purely decorative negative space, which limits its utility for some buyers. You can't add a shelf to it.

### Who should buy this

This sideboard suits three buyer types:

- **Small-space dwellers** who need a single piece that serves as dining storage, a coffee bar, and a TV stand in one
- **Entertainers** who want a dedicated buffet for dinner parties without building custom cabinetry
- **Renters** who want a piece that looks permanent but can be disassembled and moved — though disassembly is a two-person job

Skip it if you need solid wood construction, soft-close hardware, or a piece rated for daily heavy-duty use in a commercial setting.

### FAQ

**Is the Gizoon sideboard sturdy enough for a heavy TV?**
Yes, up to about 60 pounds distributed across the top. The cabinet frame is load-bearing, but the top panel itself is not solid wood — avoid placing a single heavy object like a large CRT or a heavy marble-top console on it.

**Will the cabinet doors stay aligned?**
Only if you level the unit. The adjustable feet have a wide range, but you must set them before loading. Once leveled, the doors stay true.

**Does it come in other colors?**
The most popular is matte black, but the manufacturer also offers a white version. Check the product listing for current availability — the black sells out periodically.

### The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a versatile, modern storage piece at a mid-range price that outperforms its price tag. **Skip it if** you require premium hardware like soft-close hinges or if your floors are uneven and you won't take the time to level the unit.

At 4.6/5, the Gizoon Sideboard earns its rating. It's not heirloom furniture — it's a smart, functional piece that solves real storage problems without breaking your budget. For the price, it's one of the better-balanced sideboards on Amazon right now.
