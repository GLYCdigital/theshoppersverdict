Here's the thing about most "washable" rugs: they're printed polyester mats with a rubber grip layer that peels, cracks, and ends up in a landfill within a year. The Dash & Albert Malta takes a different approach — it's a genuine flat-woven cotton rug, the kind of construction that's been used for centuries, just with a modern twist: you can throw it in the washing machine.

**What you actually get**

This is a true flat-weave rug, not a printed mat. The stripe pattern runs through the weave itself, which means the design won't fade or wear off over time. The cotton construction gives it a substantial feel — at roughly a quarter-inch thick, it's heavier and more tactile than the flimsy washable rugs that dominate Amazon's search results. The edges are finished with a clean, bound hem rather than the fraying fringe you often see at this price point.

**Real-world performance**

Out of the box, the Malta lies flat immediately. No curling corners, no need to stack books on it for three days to flatten the creases. That's a small thing that makes a big difference when you're setting up a room.

The washability is the headline feature, and it delivers. Multiple buyers report running it through standard home washers with no issues — the cotton weave holds up to repeated cycles without shrinking, pilling, or losing color intensity. One reviewer noted, "Washed it three times in the first month (two kids, a dog, and a spill incident) and it looks exactly like it did the day it arrived." That's the experience you're paying for.

As a flat weave, it's also forgiving in high-traffic areas. The pattern hides crumbs, pet hair, and general daily grime better than a solid-color rug would. And because there's no thick pile, dirt doesn't get trapped deep in the fibers — a quick vacuum or a shake outdoors handles most of the maintenance.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The color accuracy is a recurring theme. The stripes are crisp and saturated, matching the product photos closely — which is rare for online rug shopping. Multiple reviewers specifically mention that the rug looks more expensive than it is, with one noting, "Guest thought it was a $400 wool rug from a boutique."

The lack of shedding is another consistent win. Unlike many cotton rugs that leave lint balls on your socks for the first month, the Malta's weave is tight enough that shedding is minimal from day one.

**Where it falls short**

The flat-weave cotton construction means this rug isn't plush. If you want something soft underfoot for a bedroom or a nursery, this isn't it — it's a low-profile rug designed for durability, not comfort. Expect it to feel like a heavy canvas or a woven beach mat, not a carpet.

There's also no non-slip backing. On hardwood or tile, this rug will slide without a pad underneath. You can buy a separate rug pad for around $20-30, but that's an additional cost that pushes the total higher than the listed price suggests.

Cotton flat-weaves also show wear patterns more visibly than synthetic rugs. In high-traffic walkways, you'll notice the weave compressing and the colors slightly dulling over time. It's not a flaw — it's the nature of natural fiber — but it's worth knowing before you buy.

**Who should buy this**

The Malta is ideal for high-traffic family spaces — kitchens, entryways, playrooms, dining areas. It's also a strong choice for anyone with pets or kids who needs a rug they can actually clean rather than spot-treat and hope. If you rent and need a rug that can handle a shared laundry machine, this is a safe bet.

Skip it if you're looking for a plush, sink-your-toes-in carpet, or if you want a rug for a bedroom where you'll be walking barefoot. Also skip if you don't want to budget for a separate rug pad on hard floors.

**FAQ**

*Can this really go in a standard washing machine?*
Yes, but check your machine's capacity — this is a full-size rug, and it'll be heavy when wet. Use cold water, gentle cycle, and air dry. One buyer noted it took about six hours to fully dry on a drying rack.

*Does it shed?*
Minimal shedding compared to most cotton rugs. A few buyers reported slight lint in the first wash, but nothing persistent.

*Is it safe for a washer with an agitator?*
Yes, the flat weave handles agitation fine. Just avoid washing it with anything with zippers or hooks that could snag the weave.

**The Verdict**

The Dash & Albert Malta is a genuinely well-made washable rug that prioritizes durability and cleanability over plush comfort. It earns its 4.6-star rating through solid construction, accurate colors, and a washability feature that actually works.

**Buy it if:** You need a stylish, hard-working rug for a high-traffic area and actually plan to wash it regularly.

**Skip it if:** You want something soft underfoot or don't want to spend extra on a rug pad.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A quality flat-weave that does exactly what it promises, with the only real compromises being comfort and the lack of built-in grip.
