## The 7-Tier Over-Toilet Cabinet That Actually Earns Its Space

Most over-the-toilet storage units are flimsy wire racks that wobble every time you grab a towel. The Besiost 7-tier cabinet takes a different approach: it's a closed-back, multi-surface unit that turns the dead air above your toilet into genuine storage. At 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, it's clearly working for most buyers — but there are a few setup quirks you need to know before you commit.

## What You're Actually Getting

This isn't a minimal wire frame. The Besiost ships with a powder-coated steel frame, seven tiers of shelving, and two fabric drawers that slide into the lower section. As shown in the product image, the layout is: a top shelf for display items, three open shelves in the middle, then the two drawers flanked by two more open shelves at the bottom. The whole unit measures roughly 24 inches wide, 8 inches deep, and 59 inches tall — sized to straddle a standard toilet tank without touching it.

The closed-back design is the differentiator here. Most over-toilet racks are open on both sides, which means everything you store is visible from every angle. The Besiost gives you a cleaner look and keeps items from sliding off the back.

## Real-World Performance: Where It Shines and Where It Struggles

**The good:** Once assembled, this thing is solid. Multiple buyers note the steel frame handles daily use without the wobble you get from cheaper racks. The shelves are rated for moderate weight — I'd trust it with stacked towels, a hair dryer, and decorative baskets. The fabric drawers are a nice touch for hiding the ugly stuff (extra toothpaste, medicine bottles, hair ties) that would visually clutter open shelving.

**The not-so-good:** Assembly is the main complaint, and it's legitimate. The instructions are minimal, and the upper frame requires careful alignment to get the bolts threaded correctly. Budget a full hour and grab a helper for the final step. Several reviewers also flagged that the fabric drawers are thin — they'll sag if you overload them, and they're not moisture-resistant, so keep damp washcloths off them.

**The clearance catch:** This is the one that gets people. The unit sits *over* the toilet, meaning the bottom shelf needs to clear your tank. On standard round toilets, it fits fine. On elongated or tall-height toilets (especially comfort-height models), the bottom shelf can sit too low to store anything taller than a small shampoo bottle. Measure your tank height and depth before ordering — the product dimensions are listed, so do the math.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Will this fit my toilet?**
It fits standard round toilets with tanks up to about 22 inches tall and 20 inches deep. Measure your tank height and depth against the product dimensions (approximately 59" H × 24" W × 8" D). Elongated toilets may have clearance issues on the bottom shelves.

**Q: Is it easy to assemble?**
No, and the instructions don't help. Budget 45–60 minutes, and have a second person hold the frame steady while you tighten the bolts. Read the included manual fully before starting — skipping ahead causes alignment problems.

**Q: Can the drawers hold anything heavy?**
The fabric drawers are best for lightweight items — toiletries, first aid, hair accessories. Don't overload them with heavy bottles or they'll sag and eventually tear. Heavy items belong on the steel shelves.
