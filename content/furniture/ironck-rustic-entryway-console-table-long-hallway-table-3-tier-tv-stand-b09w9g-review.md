## What You're Actually Getting

The table measures 47.2 inches long, 15.7 inches deep, and 31.5 inches high, which makes it substantial enough to anchor a long wall without overwhelming a room.

The design pairs a rustic brown engineered wood top with a black metal frame. As shown in the product image, the three tiers are all full-length — not stubby little ledges. The bottom two shelves are open, which means you can actually use them for storage, not just display. The top tier has a subtle lip that keeps items from sliding off.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The metal frame is the standout here. It's welded, not just screwed together, and the legs have adjustable leveling feet — a small detail that matters more than you'd think on older floors. The engineered wood has a convincing wood-grain texture; it's not the glossy, obviously-fake finish you see on cheaper consoles.

Assembly is genuinely manageable. One reviewer noted, "Took me about 25 minutes by myself. The holes lined up perfectly, which is rare in my experience with flat-pack furniture." Another mentioned, "I was worried about stability with three open shelves, but once assembled, it doesn't wobble at all."

The weight capacity is respectable. You can comfortably place a 50-inch TV on top — IRONCK markets this as a TV stand alternative, and at 47 inches wide, it will hold most smaller flatscreens. The lower shelves handle stacked books, storage baskets, or a record player without sagging.

## Where It Falls Short

Be honest about your space before ordering. At 47 inches wide, this is a long table — it will dominate a small entryway. Several reviewers noted they had to return it because they underestimated the width. Measure your wall first.

The engineered wood top is durable but not indestructible. Dragging a heavy ceramic pot or metal decor across it will leave scratches. Use felt pads under anything with a hard base. Also, the rustic finish has a slightly distressed look in places — if you want a perfectly uniform color, this isn't it. The intentional wear pattern is part of the farmhouse aesthetic, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

## FAQ

**Is the wood real or veneer?**
It's engineered wood with a printed wood-grain texture. It looks and feels like real wood from a distance, but it's not solid lumber. This keeps the weight manageable and the price reasonable.

**Can this hold a 55-inch TV?**
The table is 47 inches wide, so a 55-inch TV would overhang the edges. Stick to 50 inches or smaller, or use it as a media console for a larger TV mounted on the wall.

**How long does assembly take?**
Most buyers report 20–40 minutes with the included Allen wrench. The holes are pre-drilled and aligned well. Two people make it easier, but one person can handle it.
