You don't buy a pink nightstand by accident. You buy it because you want your bedroom to feel like *yours* — and you're tired of the same espresso and whitewashed oak options everywhere. The Glory Furniture Daniel 3 Drawer Nightstand in Pink is a statement piece with actual storage credentials. After digging through 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, here's the real story.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the nightstand body, three drawer fronts, the drawer boxes, six metal handles, and the hardware kit. Assembly is required — expect 45 to 60 minutes with a Phillips head screwdriver. As shown in the product image, the piece measures roughly 24 inches wide, 22 inches deep, and stands about 24 inches tall. That's a substantial footprint; this isn't a skinny minimalist bedside table. The pink is a soft, chalky pastel — more millennial blush than bubblegum. The legs are solid wood with a natural finish, which gives it a slightly mid-century modern feel rather than a toy-like vibe.

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

The frame uses engineered wood with a laminated pink finish, but the legs and drawer glides are where Glory Furniture spent their money. The legs are solid — not hollow dowels — and they screw into metal brackets that are actually anchored into the frame, not just surface-mounted. Several buyers noted the sturdiness: *"I was worried it would feel cheap, but the drawer glides are metal and smooth. It doesn't wobble even on carpet."*

Drawer capacity is the standout feature. Each drawer is about 19 inches wide and 6 inches deep — deep enough for folded sweaters, not just socks. The full-extension glides mean you can pull the drawer completely out without it tipping, which is rare at this price point. The handles are brushed metal, not plastic painted to look metallic.

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

The color accuracy is the most repeated compliment. *"I was nervous the pink would look different in person, but it's exactly like the photos — soft and not overwhelming."* Multiple reviewers mentioned pairing it with white or gray bedding and getting compliments. The assembly instructions are also called out as genuinely clear — labeled parts, numbered steps, no mystery screws left over.

**Where It Falls Short**

The pink is the selling point and the limitation. If you ever repaint your bedroom in warm terracotta or deep forest tones, this nightstand will clash hard. It's not neutral, and you need to commit to that. The finish is also laminate, not painted wood — so if you scratch it, you'll see the underlying MDF. A few reviewers mentioned that the drawer fronts are heavy and can be tricky to align perfectly during assembly; misaligned fronts look slightly off until you loosen and re-tighten the screws. This is a two-person job, full stop.

**Who Should Buy This**

Buy it if you're furnishing a child's room, a teen's first "big girl" bedroom, or a guest room where you want a playful accent. It also works well in a home office or craft room as a storage piece. Skip it if you need a neutral nightstand that will survive multiple decor changes, or if you're not prepared for assembly with a helper.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the pink show fingerprints or smudges easily?**
A: The laminate finish is surprisingly resistant to smudges. A damp microfiber cloth wipes it clean. Just avoid abrasive cleaners — they'll dull the finish.

**Q: Can this fit a standard bed frame height?**
A: At 24 inches tall, it aligns well with most standard bed frames (which typically sit 22–26 inches high). If you have a platform bed that's lower, it'll still be usable but might feel slightly tall.

**Q: Is the pink more coral or pastel in person?**
A: It's a true pastel pink — think ballet slipper, not coral or salmon. It has a slight warm undertone but reads clearly as pink, not peach.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a functional nightstand that makes a deliberate style statement and you have a second person to help assemble it. **Skip it if** you need a neutral piece or you're assembling solo with limited patience.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The color accuracy, smooth glides, and solid construction earn the score. The laminate finish and assembly quirks keep it from a perfect five.
