If you're working with a cramped home office, the GYIIYUO 2 Drawer File Cabinet with 24'' Extended Desktop is trying to solve a very specific problem: how to get filing storage and a usable work surface without dedicating floor space to both. The 4.2-star average across 210 ratings suggests it mostly succeeds — but the extended desktop is the feature that makes or breaks this purchase.

## What You Actually Get

The unit ships as a mobile file cabinet with two drawers, topped by a 24-inch deep extended desktop that overhangs the cabinet body. The desktop adds roughly 10-12 inches of depth beyond the cabinet footprint, which is the entire point. You're getting a rolling file cabinet that doubles as a compact standing desk or side work surface.

The drawers are sized for letter documents (8.5 x 11 inches). Both drawers run on ball-bearing slides, and the top drawer includes a locking mechanism. Four caster wheels come pre-installed — two with locking brakes. The cabinet body is steel with a powder-coated finish; the desktop is a wood-grain laminate over MDF.

## Real-World Performance

The extended desktop is genuinely functional, not decorative. At 24 inches deep, it comfortably holds a 24-inch monitor plus a keyboard and mouse. Users who pair this with an adjustable chair report it works well as a dedicated work-from-home station for laptops or single-monitor setups. The overhang design means you can tuck the cabinet under a desk and still use the extended surface as a secondary workspace.

The drawer slides are the standout component. They're full-extension — meaning the drawer pulls completely out, giving you full access to the contents. At this price point, most competitors use partial-extension slides that leave you digging for files in the back. Customers consistently note the smooth operation, with one reviewer stating, "The drawers glide open and shut without sticking, even after six months of daily use."

The locking mechanism on the top drawer is simple but effective. It's a single-key lock that secures both the drawer and, by extension, the cabinet when engaged. If you're storing sensitive documents, this is a meaningful feature — many budget file cabinets skip locking entirely.

## Where It Falls Short

The assembly process is the most common complaint. The desktop attachment requires precise alignment, and several reviewers note that you'll need a second person to hold the desktop level while you drive the screws. Plan for 45-60 minutes of assembly, not the 20 minutes the instructions suggest.

The wheels are a weaker point. They're hard plastic casters, which means noticeable noise on hardwood or tile floors. If your home office is carpeted, this is a non-issue — but on hard surfaces, you'll hear every roll.

Also worth noting: the drawers are letter-size only. If you use legal-size hanging files, they will not fit without bending the folders. Measure your existing filing system before ordering.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You need a rolling file cabinet that doubles as a compact work surface. It's ideal for small apartments, dorm rooms, or home offices where floor space is at a premium. The extended desktop genuinely replaces a dedicated desk for light workloads.

**Skip it if:** You need legal-size filing, work primarily on hardwood floors without a mat, or expect a zero-assembly experience. Also skip if you need heavy-duty capacity — this handles standard office filing, not heavy binders or box files.

## FAQ

**Can this hold a 27-inch monitor on the extended desktop?**
Yes, but barely. A 27-inch monitor with a standard stand will fit within the 24-inch depth, but you'll have very little clearance behind it. If your monitor uses a wide V-shaped stand, measure the depth before committing.

**Does the extended desktop wobble during use?**
When properly assembled and with all four wheels on the ground, no. The wobble complaints in reviews almost always trace back to uneven floor surfaces or loose hardware from partial assembly. Tighten everything and use the wheel locks if you notice movement.

**Is the locking mechanism secure enough for sensitive documents?**
The lock is a basic cam lock — it deters casual access but isn't a security-grade solution. It's fine for keeping files away from children or houseguests, but don't use it as your primary security for irreplaceable documents.

## The Verdict

The GYIIYUO 2 Drawer File Cabinet with 24'' Extended Desktop earns its 4.2-star rating through a smart design that genuinely saves space. It's not perfect — the assembly is fiddly, the wheels are noisy on hard floors, and legal-size filing is a no-go. But for the price, you're getting a functional work surface and real filing capacity in a footprint that fits beside a bed or in a closet office.

**Buy it if** you're short on space and need a dual-purpose solution. **Skip it if** you need legal-size filing or want a furniture-grade piece that disappears into your decor — this looks and feels like what it is: a practical, budget-conscious workspace addition.
