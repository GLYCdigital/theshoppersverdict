If you've been scrolling past $300+ vanities from brands that charge extra for the chair, the BORNOON setup deserves a second look. This unit bundles the LED mirror, seven drawers, and a matching stool at a price point that undercuts most comparable sets. But is it a genuine daily driver or a weekend DIY headache? After digging through 220 Amazon ratings and the specs, here's the real story.

**What You Actually Get**

The box includes the vanity tabletop, a 7-drawer base (three on each side, plus one deep center drawer), the LED light strip mirror, and the upholstered stool. The tabletop measures roughly 39.4 inches wide, which fits a standard bedroom wall without dominating the room. The mirror is the centerpiece — a rectangular panel with a built-in LED strip that runs along all four edges. It's powered by a USB cord, which means you can plug it into a laptop, power bank, or wall adapter. No batteries to replace, no proprietary charging dock.

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

The frame is engineered wood (MDF), 0.9 inches thick, finished in a smooth white or black lacquer depending on your choice. Weight capacity is listed at 220 pounds for the tabletop, which is generous — you can lean on it while applying eyeliner without feeling the board flex. The drawers glide on metal tracks, not the cheap plastic slides you often see at this price. They're not soft-close, so they'll clatter if you slam them, but they open and close smoothly in normal use.

The LED mirror is the standout feature. It has three color temperature modes (warm, neutral, cool) and a dimmer. The light strip wraps the entire mirror perimeter, which means illumination comes from all sides — no harsh shadows under your chin or cheekbones. One reviewer put it simply: "I can finally see my pores and foundation lines before I leave the house. The lighting is better than my bathroom vanity."

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The storage capacity is the most repeated compliment. Seven drawers handle what most vanities with three or four can't. A verified buyer noted, "I fit all my palettes in the deep center drawer, brushes in one side, skincare in the other. Everything has a home now." The USB-powered mirror also gets consistent praise for flexibility — you're not tethered to a specific outlet location.

**Where It Falls Short**

Assembly is the biggest complaint, and it's legitimate. There are over 60 screws, and the instructions are diagram-based with no written steps. Expect 1.5 to 2 hours of work with a Phillips head screwdriver. A power drill with a hex adapter helps enormously, but it's still tedious.

The stool is functional but bare-bones. It's a wood frame with a thin foam seat covered in PU leather. It does the job for quick touch-ups, but for a 30-minute full-face routine, your backside will feel it. Several reviewers mentioned buying a separate cushioned stool or adding a seat pad. Budget for that if you plan long sessions.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is ideal for renters or students who want a full vanity setup without a big investment. The USB mirror is perfect for dorms where outlets are scarce. It's also a strong pick for anyone with a large makeup collection — the seven drawers genuinely hold more than the price suggests. If you're a minimalist with three products, you'll have empty drawers, and a smaller vanity might serve you better.

**FAQ**

**Is the LED mirror bright enough for detailed makeup application?**
Yes. The strip runs the full perimeter, and with three color modes plus dimming, you can match the lighting to your environment. It's notably better than a single overhead bulb.

**Can the stool support heavier users?**
The listed capacity is 220 lbs, but the stool's four legs are stable and the frame is solid. Several reviewers over 200 lbs reported no issues. Still, it's not built like a heavy-duty chair.

**Does the vanity require a wall outlet for the mirror?**
No. The mirror plugs into any USB port via the included cable. You can use a wall adapter, power strip, laptop, or even a power bank.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a complete vanity setup with serious storage and good lighting at a budget-friendly price, and you're willing to spend an evening assembling it.

**Skip it if** you hate DIY furniture assembly, need a plush seat, or prefer a smaller footprint for a compact space.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The BORNOON delivers where it matters (storage, lighting, stability) and stumbles only on assembly and the basic stool. For the price, it's a solid value that most buyers will be happy with for years.
