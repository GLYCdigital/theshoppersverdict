# GrandNoor Rattan Counter Height Stools Set of 2 with Solid W: The Natural-Fiber Upgrade Your Kitchen Actually Needs

Most counter stools under $200 a pair look the part for about three months. Then the veneer peels, the legs wobble, and you're back to scrolling Amazon. The GrandNoor Rattan Counter Height Stools aim to break that cycle — and with 240 ratings holding a 4.8-star average, they might just pull it off. Here's what you're actually getting for your money.

## What's in the Box

Two fully assembled stools (minus the legs), all hardware, and a hex wrench. That's it. No confusing multi-page manual — the four bolts per stool screw directly into pre-drilled holes on the base. One buyer noted, "I had both stools together in the time it took my coffee to cool." That's about right. Plan for 10–15 minutes total.

Each stool measures 16.5 inches deep by 16.5 inches wide, with a seat height of 24 inches — sized for standard 36-inch kitchen counters and bar-height islands. The seat itself is 13.5 inches across, which is snug but not cramped. The full backrest extends to 34 inches tall, giving you genuine lumbar support rather than that perched-on-a-barrel feeling.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Here's where GrandNoor earns its keep. The frame is solid rubberwood — not the "wood grain" printed MDF you'll find on comparable sets. The legs are 1.5 inches thick at the top, tapering slightly, and the crossbars are actually welded into the frame rather than glued. The result: zero wobble, even on uneven tile floors. One reviewer who's owned them for six months reported, "No squeaks, no loose joints, and my 220-pound husband sits on these daily."

The rattan back is hand-woven, not machine-pressed. That matters because machine-woven rattan tends to unravel at the edges. The GrandNoor weave is tight, uniform, and finished with a clear lacquer that resists the yellowing you see on cheaper natural-fiber furniture. The seat is a solid wood plank with a subtle curve — comfortable for a 30-minute meal, though you'll want a cushion for marathon homework sessions.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The aesthetic is the headline. Multiple reviewers describe these as looking "way more expensive than they are," with the natural rattan adding warmth that complements both modern farmhouse and boho-industrial kitchens. One buyer captured it well: "I was worried the rattan would look cheap and orange. It's actually a beautiful honey tone that matches my oak floors perfectly."

Sturdiness is the second most common compliment. At 14 pounds per stool, these have real heft. The rubberwood legs are screwed into the seat from underneath with metal brackets, not just driven into the wood — a detail that prevents the seat from splitting over time.

## Where They Fall Short

The fixed 24-inch seat height is the biggest limitation. If your counter is taller than 36 inches — common in older homes or custom builds — these will feel too low. Measure before you buy.

The footrest is a bare wood dowel. For taller users, that hard edge digs into the back of the calves. A few reviewers noted this, with one suggesting, "I added a foam pipe insulator wrap and it's perfect now — but I shouldn't have to."

Finally, because the rattan is a natural material, color varies slightly between stools. Most find it charming; a small minority found the variation noticeable enough to return one stool.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Renters** — they look custom, assemble without tools, and won't damage floors (the feet have felt pads included)
- **Small-space kitchens** — the open back keeps sightlines clear, making the room feel larger
- **Anyone tired of fake wood** — these are real materials that age gracefully

## FAQ

**Q: Do these fit under a standard kitchen counter?**
Yes, the 24-inch height tucks under a 36-inch counter with about 2.5 inches of clearance. They won't slide fully under a 30-inch desk-height table, though.

**Q: Is the rattan durable enough for daily use?**
Yes, the lacquer coating protects the weave, and the frame — the structural part — is solid wood. With 240 ratings at 4.8 stars, durability complaints are almost nonexistent.

**Q: Can I stain or paint them?**
The frame is pre-finished, but light sanding will let you refinish the wood. The rattan back is lacquered and should be left as-is — painting it will crack the weave.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want real wood construction, a natural woven look that photographs beautifully, and a set that won't self-destruct in a year. **Skip it if** your counter height is non-standard, you're over 6 feet tall (the 24-inch seat will feel low), or you need padded seating for hours-long use.

At 4.6/5, the GrandNoor stools deliver on the promise that solid materials and honest craftsmanship beat veneer and luck. For the price, there's nothing else in this category that offers the same combination of genuine rubberwood, hand-woven rattan, and under-15-minute assembly. Just measure your counter first.
