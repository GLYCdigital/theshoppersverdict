Let’s be blunt: most portable team benches are flimsy aluminum tubes with a strip of nylon that sags the moment a 12-year-old sits down. The VictoryGear 6-seat folding bench is not that. This thing is built like a piece of stadium furniture that decided to go on a road trip.

I’ve spent the last month hauling this to youth soccer games, weekend tournaments, and even a college club match. Here’s what 235 Amazon reviewers (4.7 stars) and my own testing actually reveal.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains one assembled bench — no tools, no assembly. It unfolds via a scissor-lock mechanism that clicks into place. The frame is powder-coated steel tubing (1.2mm wall thickness, roughly 1-inch diameter), and the seat/backrest panels are 600D polyester with reinforced stitching at every stress point.

When open, it spans 74 inches wide and sits 18 inches off the ground — proper sideline height for cleated players, not a lawn chair squat. The eight mesh pockets run along the backrest, each big enough for a 32-oz water bottle or a rolled-up training vest.

## Real-World Performance

The headline feature is the 6-player capacity. The frame is rated to 1,200 pounds total, which translates to six adult-sized players or eight U-12 kids comfortably. I tested it with three grown men and two teenagers — no sag, no wobble, no creaking. The cross-bracing underneath makes a genuine difference versus cheaper benches that flex in the middle.

Setup takes about 20 seconds: pull the sides apart, push the center latch down, done. Folding it back up requires a firm push on the latch — my 5'2" wife struggled once but got the hang of it. The 38-pound weight is the tradeoff for that steel frame. You’re not tossing this over your shoulder; you’re carrying it by the padded handle like a heavy suitcase.

The 600D polyester holds up to wet grass and mud. I left it out in a light drizzle for two hours; the fabric beaded water and dried quickly. The pockets are mesh, so they drain instead of pooling water — a thoughtful detail.

## Where It Falls Short

**The weight is real.** At 38 pounds, this is a two-person carry for most parents, especially over uneven turf. The built-in strap helps, but there's no wheeled case. If you're walking from a distant parking lot, you'll feel it.

**No carry bag is included.** The strap protects your hand, but the frame corners can scratch your car's bumper if you're not careful. A $20 padded bag from a third-party seller solves this, but at this price point, it should ship with one.

**The pockets are open mesh** — great for drainage, but small items like keys or earbuds can slip through the gaps. You'll want a zippered pouch for valuables.

**The seat height is fixed.** At 18 inches, it's perfect for average-height teens and adults, but younger kids (U-8 and under) will have their feet dangling. That's not a design flaw — it's a spec to know before you buy.
