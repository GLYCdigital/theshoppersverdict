Let's be blunt: most nightstands under $100 are wobbly, particleboard disasters that you'll hate by month three. The OAKHAM HOME Boho End Table is not that. With a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings, this piece punches far above its price class — but it's not without tradeoffs. Here's what you need to know before you click "Add to Cart."

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the nightstand in flat-pack form, plus the usual Allen wrench and hardware kit. As shown in the product image, you're getting a two-tier design: one smooth-gliding drawer on top and an open cubby shelf below. The dimensions are compact — roughly 19.7" wide by 15.7" deep and 23.6" tall — making it a fit for tight bedroom corners or beside a low-profile platform bed. The boho aesthetic comes through in the warm teak-like wood grain finish and the clean, rounded edges. It doesn't scream "temporary college furniture," which is a win at this price point.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The frame is engineered wood (MDF) with a laminated finish that resists water rings and scratches better than you'd expect. Here's the thing that surprised me reading through buyer feedback: multiple reviewers specifically mentioned the weight. One said, "I was shocked at how heavy this was for the price. It feels solid, not hollow." That's a good sign — it means the internal framing isn't just glorified cardboard.

The drawer rides on metal ball-bearing tracks and opens with a satisfying, quiet glide. It's deep enough for a tablet, reading glasses, chargers, and small sundries — but it's shallow, so don't plan on storing chunky knitwear or a tall bottle of lotion upright. The open shelf below is the real workhorse; it fits a medium basket, stacked books, or a small plant.

Assembly is the one place this thing loses points. It's not hard, but it's tedious. The cam-lock system requires precise alignment, and you'll want a second pair of hands for the final panel attachment. Budget 30–45 minutes and don't rush it. A few reviewers noted that over-tightening the cams can strip the plastic inserts, so use firm but gentle pressure.

## What Buyers Love

The consensus praise centers on three things: looks, stability, and versatility. One reviewer wrote, "It looks way more expensive than it is — friends have asked where I got it." Another noted, "I use it as a plant stand in my living room; the boho vibe fits perfectly." The finish seems to be the standout feature — it photographs well and looks even better in person, with a subtle grain texture that feels more natural than printed-on wood grain.

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty time. The drawer is the weakest link — not in function, but in capacity. If you're a "throw everything in the nightstand" person, you'll be frustrated. Also, the open shelf means dust collects visibly; if you hate dusting, this design will test you. Finally, the finish is matte, which looks great but shows fingerprints and smudges more readily than glossy alternatives. A quick wipe with a microfiber cloth handles it, but it's worth knowing.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Boho/farmhouse decor lovers** who want a warm, natural look without paying boutique prices
- **Small-space dwellers** needing a compact nightstand that doesn't dominate the room
- **Side-table multitaskers** who want drawer storage plus open display space for books or plants

**Skip it if** you need serious drawer capacity, want a glossy/white finish, or hate flat-pack assembly.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a handsome, sturdy boho nightstand that doesn't break the bank and you're okay with a 30-minute assembly session. **Skip it if** you need deep drawers or a zero-maintenance finish.
