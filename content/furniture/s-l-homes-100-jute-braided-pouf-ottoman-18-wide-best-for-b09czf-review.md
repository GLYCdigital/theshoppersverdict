Somewhere between a floor pillow and a real ottoman sits the S & L Homes 18-inch jute braided pouf—and honestly, that's exactly where it wants to be. At 4.3 stars across 215 ratings, this isn't a viral sensation, but it's a steady performer that earns its keep in the right room.

**What you actually get**

The box contains one circular pouf, 18 inches in diameter. That's it—no stuffing kit, no cover, no assembly. It arrives fully formed, weighing enough to feel substantial but light enough to kick around with your foot. The 100% jute braid is tight and uniform, spiraling from the center outward in a classic coiled pattern. As shown in the product image, the natural tan color is consistent throughout, with no dye splotches or uneven sections. It's about 7-8 inches tall when fully fluffed, which puts it at a usable height for most sofas and accent chairs.

**How it performs in real life**

The first thing you notice is the density. This isn't a hollow shell that caves under weight—it's packed firmly, giving you a stable surface whether you're resting your feet or using it as a spare seat. The jute fibers have a natural grip, so your feet don't slide off like they might on a slick leather ottoman. Sitting on it for short periods works fine, though it's not a substitute for a real chair for extended lounging.

The braid holds its shape well after months of daily footrest duty. One reviewer noted, "I've had this for six months and it still looks brand new—no flattening, no loose threads." Another mentioned using it as a plant stand between uses, which tells you the structure is solid enough for more than just feet.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The natural aesthetic is the star here. Multiple reviewers mention pairing it with rattan furniture or jute rugs, and the texture complements those materials perfectly. One buyer wrote, "It's the exact look I wanted for my boho living room—warm, organic, and doesn't scream 'Amazon purchase.'" The weight also earns compliments; at roughly 5-6 pounds, it stays put when you rest your legs on it but slides easily when you need to move it.

**Where it falls short**

The shedding is real. Jute is a natural fiber, and loose strands will come off on your floors, your pants, and your pets. Most reviewers say it settles down after a few weeks, but you'll want to vacuum around it regularly early on. Also, there's no removable cover—if you spill coffee or a dog tracks mud onto it, you're spot-cleaning with a damp cloth and hoping for the best. Water can darken the fibers permanently, so this isn't the piece for high-traffic, high-spill zones.

**Who should buy this**

This pouf is ideal for the person who wants functional boho style without paying boutique prices. It suits living rooms, reading nooks, and bedrooms where it can serve as a footrest, a low seat for guests, or a decorative anchor. If you have kids under five who will treat it as a landing pad or a snack table, skip it—jute doesn't forgive sticky fingers. Pet owners with heavy shedders should also think twice, as the fibers will trap fur.

**FAQ**

*Does it flatten over time?*  
The braid compresses slightly with heavy use but springs back when not in use. One reviewer reported it lost a bit of loft after a year of daily footrest duty, but it still functions fine.

*Can you sit on it comfortably?*  
For short periods, yes. It's firm, not plush, so it's better for occasional seating than marathon TV sessions.

*How do you clean it?*  
Spot-clean with a barely damp cloth and let it air dry completely. Avoid soaking it, and never machine wash it.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want an affordable, natural-textured accent piece that pulls double duty as a footrest and spare seat. It looks more expensive than it costs and holds up well in low-traffic living spaces.

**Skip it if** you need something you can wipe down, have pets that shed heavily, or want a plush surface for daily sitting.
