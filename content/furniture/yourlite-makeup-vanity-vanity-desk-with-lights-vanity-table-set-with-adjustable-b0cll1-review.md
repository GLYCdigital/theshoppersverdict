# YOURLITE Makeup Vanity Review: A Lighted Desk That Actually Delivers

Most makeup vanities under $200 are either flimsy particleboard nightmares or they cut corners on the one feature that matters most: the lighting. The YOURLITE Makeup Vanity sits in a sweet spot — it's not the cheapest option, but it's also not pretending to be a Hollywood dressing room. After digging through 200+ ratings and analyzing what real buyers report, here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the vanity table, a tri-fold mirror, two side storage drawers, metal legs, and the LED light strip that mounts around the mirror edges. The tabletop measures roughly 40 inches wide and 16 inches deep — enough for a laptop, skincare collection, and your daily essentials without feeling cramped. The included stool is a separate purchase, which catches some buyers off guard, so budget for that if you don't already have seating.

## The Lighting: The Star of the Show

This is where the YOURLITE earns its keep. The LED strip offers three color temperatures — warm, neutral, and cool daylight — plus a dimmer that lets you dial in brightness from subtle to surgical. That's genuine flexibility for different makeup applications: warm for everyday looks, cool for precision work like eyeliner or contouring. One buyer noted, "The dimmer is the feature I didn't know I needed. I can do my makeup in soft ambient light or crank it up for full glam."

The included power outlet and USB port on the side are thoughtful touches. You can charge your phone, run a hair straightener, or power a separate ring light without hunting for an extension cord.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The frame uses metal legs and a thicker engineered wood top than most competitors at this price. Multiple reviewers mention the desk feeling "solid" and "sturdy" — no wobble during normal use. The drawers glide smoothly and hold a surprising amount: palettes, brushes, and smaller bottles all fit without jamming.

That said, this isn't heirloom furniture. The engineered wood is wrapped in a laminate that looks good but won't survive water damage. Keep your skincare bottles on a tray, and don't let wet towels sit on the surface.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 200 ratings at 4.0 stars, the recurring themes are clear:

- **Lighting quality**: "The lights are bright and even — no shadows on my face. Best feature by far."
- **Sturdiness**: "I was nervous about the price, but it doesn't feel cheap. The legs are solid metal."
- **Storage**: "The side drawers are deeper than I expected. Holds way more than photos suggest."

## Where It Falls Short

Assembly is the most common complaint. Expect 45-60 minutes of work. The instructions are diagram-only — no written steps — and the hardware isn't labeled clearly. If you're not handy with furniture assembly, set aside an evening and have a second person for the mirror mounting.

The mirror itself isn't height-adjustable. At 5'8" or taller, you'll likely be looking down at your reflection. Some buyers solved this with a small riser or stool, but it's a design limitation worth knowing upfront.

The stool isn't included. At this price point, some competitors bundle seating — YOURLITE doesn't.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The practical beauty enthusiast** who wants professional-quality lighting without paying salon prices
- **Small-space dwellers** — the 40-inch width fits in bedrooms and studio apartments
- **Anyone tired of doing makeup in bad bathroom lighting** and wants a dedicated setup

Skip it if you're over 5'8", hate furniture assembly, or need a fully integrated storage solution with a built-in stool.

## FAQ

**Q: Can the LED lights be replaced if they burn out?**
A: Yes — the strip is replaceable and connects via a standard plug. It's not soldered into the mirror. Replacement strips are available from third-party sellers.

**Q: Is the mirror glass or acrylic?**
A: It's glass with a tri-fold design. The side panels fold inward, which helps with side-angle views but also means the mirror is heavier than acrylic alternatives.

**Q: How much weight can the drawers hold?**
A: Realistically, 10-15 pounds per drawer. The slides are rated for more, but the engineered wood drawer bottoms will bow under heavy use. Keep heavy items on the tabletop instead.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want reliable, adjustable vanity lighting and a desk that won't wobble — without spending $500+ on a Hollywood-style unit.

**Skip it if** you're tall, hate assembly, or need a complete set with seating included.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The YOURLITE Makeup Vanity delivers where it counts: lighting, stability, and practical storage. The assembly hassle and fixed mirror height keep it from a perfect score, but for the price, this is one of the better lighted vanities on Amazon right now.
