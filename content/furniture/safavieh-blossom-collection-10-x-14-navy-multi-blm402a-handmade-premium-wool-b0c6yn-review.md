## What You Actually Get

The box delivers the rug and nothing else. No pad, no fringe protectors, no samples. At 10' x 14', this is a room-defining piece that weighs roughly 40 pounds, so plan for two people to maneuver it into place. The wool construction is immediately apparent — this isn't a printed polypropylene throwaway. The hand-knotted pile sits at about half an inch, giving it a plush-but-not-sinking feel underfoot.

## Performance and Build Quality

The navy/multi colorway does double duty. From across the room, it reads as a sophisticated deep blue anchor. Up close, the multi-tonal floral pattern adds texture that hides crumbs, pet hair, and the general grime of daily life remarkably well. Buyers consistently note this: one reviewer said, "I have two dogs and a toddler, and this rug still looks presentable at the end of the week." That's the kind of real-world endorsement that matters.

The wool holds up to furniture indentations better than synthetic blends. Move a couch after a few months and you'll see marks that recover within a day or two. Stitching is tight, edges are finished cleanly, and the rug lays flat within 48 hours of unrolling — a common pain point with large rugs that this one handles well.

## Where It Falls Short

Be honest with yourself about the shedding. Hand-knotted wool rugs shed — it's physics, not a defect — but this one sheds noticeably for the first month or two. A reviewer warned, "Vacuum twice a week for six weeks and you'll still find wool fibers in the canister. It stops eventually, but it's real." If you have allergies or a robot vacuum that clogs easily, budget for that maintenance window.

The pattern is busier than the product images suggest. The "multi" in navy/multi means multiple floral tones that read almost traditional up close. If your aesthetic is Scandinavian minimalism or industrial modern, this will fight your furniture rather than complement it.

Finally, no pad included. On hardwood or tile, you'll want a rug gripper underneath — not just for safety but to protect the wool backing from premature wear.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Traditional-to-transitional homeowners** with a living room or formal dining space that needs an anchor piece

Skip it if you need a low-pile rug for under a dining table with chairs constantly sliding, or if your space demands a neutral, understated foundation.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this rug work on hardwood floors without a pad?**
A: It'll stay put initially, but over weeks it will shift, especially in high-traffic paths. Buy a separate gripper pad — it's worth the $40-60 investment for safety and to extend the rug's life.

**Q: How bad is the shedding really?**
A: Moderate to heavy for the first 4-6 weeks. Vacuum with a beater bar weekly (twice if you're fussy) and it tapers off. It's normal for wool, not a quality issue.

**Q: Will the navy fade in direct sunlight?**
A: The wool holds dye well, but no rug is sun-proof. If it's in a room with hours of direct afternoon light, rotate it seasonally to keep fading even.
