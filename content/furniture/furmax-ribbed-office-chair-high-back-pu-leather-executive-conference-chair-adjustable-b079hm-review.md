**The Furmax Ribbed Office Chair proves that a good-looking chair under $150 isn't a myth — but it's also not a forever chair. Here's who should buy it and who should walk away.**

You've seen this chair a hundred times scrolling Amazon: the sleek ribbed back, the PU leather sheen, the price that makes you double-check it's not a typo. The Furmax Ribbed Office Chair is one of the most visually striking budget executive chairs on the market, and for good reason. But after digging through 195 ratings and owner experiences, the real story is more complicated than the product photos suggest.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the chair disassembled: the five-star aluminum base, five caster wheels, the seat pan with pneumatic gas lift pre-installed, the high backrest with its signature ribbed design, and two fixed armrests. Assembly takes about 20-30 minutes with the included Allen wrench. The hardware is standard — nothing premium, but nothing flimsy either. The chair measures approximately 40 inches tall with a 20-inch-wide seat and supports up to 250 pounds.

**The good: comfort that punches above its weight**

The seat cushion is genuinely thick — about 4 inches of high-density foam that doesn't bottom out even after several hours of sitting. The high back (22 inches) provides real lumbar support, and the ribbed design isn't just aesthetic; it adds subtle structure that keeps the backrest from flexing too much under pressure. As one verified buyer put it: *"I was skeptical at this price, but I've worked 8-hour days for three months and my back feels better than it did with my old $400 chair."*

The pneumatic gas lift offers smooth height adjustment, and the tilt mechanism with tension control lets you rock back comfortably. The casters roll quietly on both carpet and hardwood — a detail many budget chairs get wrong.

**The bad: durability is the elephant in the room**

Here's where things get honest. The PU leather is the chair's biggest weakness. Multiple long-term reviewers report peeling and cracking starting around the 6-12 month mark, particularly on the seat edges and armrests. One reviewer wrote: *"Loved it for eight months, then the leather started flaking off and getting all over my clothes. For a daily driver, it didn't last a year."*

The armrests are also fixed — no height or angle adjustment — and they sit lower than ideal for taller users. If you're over 5'10", expect to hunch slightly to rest your arms, which can lead to shoulder fatigue during long typing sessions.

**Where it falls short**

- The 250-pound weight capacity is on the lower end; heavier users report the gas lift losing height over time
- The headrest is part of the backrest, not adjustable — it hits some users at the wrong spot
- Assembly instructions are sparse, and a few buyers report misaligned screw holes requiring extra patience

**Who should buy this chair**

- **Budget-conscious home office workers** who want executive looks without executive pricing
- **Moderate-use sitters** (4-6 hours daily) who don't need a 10-year chair
- **Shorter users** (under 5'10") who will benefit from the fixed armrest height
- **Guest chairs or secondary workstations** where daily wear-and-tear is minimal

**Skip it if** you're over 250 pounds, need adjustable armrests, or want a chair that will look new after two years of full-time use.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Q: Is the PU leather breathable? Will my back sweat?**
A: No, PU leather doesn't breathe like mesh. If you run hot or work in a warm environment, expect some back sweat during longer sessions. The ribbed design helps slightly with airflow, but it's still a leather-style chair.

**Q: Does the tilt lock into position?**
A: Yes, there's a lever under the seat that locks the recline at your preferred angle. The tension knob lets you adjust how much resistance you feel when leaning back.

**Q: How tall is the backrest from the seat?**
A: About 22 inches from the seat base to the top of the headrest. This provides good support for users up to around 6 feet tall.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a genuinely comfortable, sharp-looking chair for under $150 and understand it's a 2-3 year product, not a lifetime investment. For moderate use, it's one of the best values on Amazon.

**Skip it if:** You need a chair for 40+ hour weeks, you're over 250 pounds, or you're tall. The durability issues and fixed armrests will frustrate you within a year.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — A great chair for the price with real comfort and style, held back by PU leather that won't survive heavy daily use.
