If your living room has an awkward empty corner and a TV that needs a home, you already know the problem: most TV stands are rectangles. They eat floor space, block walkways, and force you to arrange furniture around them. The HOMISSUE Corner TV Stand takes a different approach—it's built to tuck into that dead zone where two walls meet, and at 4.7 stars from 235 Amazon ratings, it's clearly working for a lot of people. But is it the right call for your setup? Let's dig into the specifics.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the corner stand, hardware, and an instruction manual. The unit measures roughly 48 inches wide at the front, about 28 inches deep, and stands 24 inches tall—dimensions that let it hold TVs from 32 up to 60 inches, with a weight capacity of 110 pounds. That's enough for most modern LED and QLED sets, though you'll want to skip this if you're mounting a massive OLED. You get two open shelves flanking the center, plus two cabinets with doors below. The finish is a wood-grain laminate that reads convincingly in person—not luxury, but not cheap-looking either.

**Real-World Performance and Build**

The center column is where your TV sits, and it's reinforced to handle the weight without sagging. The 24-inch height puts your screen at a good viewing level for most sofas—you won't be craning your neck. One thing I appreciate: the open shelving is positioned so a soundbar can sit directly beneath the TV without blocking the remote sensor on the set itself. That's a detail many stands get wrong.

Assembly took me about 45 minutes. The cam-lock bolts are standard for this price class, but the diagrams are a bit sparse. A few Amazon reviewers mention the same—one wrote, "Took two of us an hour because the instructions weren't clear on which screws went where." Have a Phillips head driver handy and sort the hardware before you start. The particleboard construction is solid for the price, though the fabric backing on the lower cabinets is the weakest point—it's thin and can tear if you're careless when sliding things in.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The overwhelmingly common theme in the customer feedback is the space-saving design. As one verified buyer put it: "Finally a stand that fits our awkward corner without making the room feel cramped." Another noted, "The corner orientation completely changed our living room layout—we reclaimed about three feet of walking space." Durability also gets mentions; several long-term owners report no wobble or sag after months of daily use.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be direct about the tradeoffs. First, if you're not putting this in an actual corner, the angled shape wastes more floor space than a standard stand—it's designed for a specific configuration. Second, there are no cable management cutouts in the top panel. You'll need to route HDMI and power cables around the edges, which can look messy unless you use adhesive clips. Third, the two lower cabinets are shallow—about 14 inches deep—so bulky AV receivers likely won't fit. A slim soundbar or game console will, but measure before you buy.

**Who Should Buy This**

This stand is ideal for apartment dwellers, small living rooms, or anyone with an unused corner that makes their current layout feel off. It's also a strong pick if you want a TV stand that doesn't dominate the room visually. Skip it if you have a massive center-channel speaker, need deep storage for AV gear, or you're shopping for a corner that isn't a true 90-degree angle.

**FAQ**

*Does it fit a 65-inch TV?*  
Technically no—the spec says up to 60 inches and 110 pounds. A 65-inch set would exceed the width rating, making it unstable. Don't risk it.

*Can the shelves hold a game console and soundbar at once?*  
Yes. The open shelves are about 18 inches wide and support up to 30 pounds each, so a PS5 or Xbox Series X plus a soundbar will sit comfortably.

*Is the wood finish real or laminate?*  
It's a laminate over particleboard. It looks like wood from a few feet away, but it's not solid timber. That's expected at this price point.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You're fighting for floor space, need a stand for a 55-60 inch TV, and want something that disappears into a corner instead of shouting for attention. The 4.7-star rating holds up—this is a well-designed piece for its niche.

**Skip it if:** You need deep storage, prefer a wide traditional media console, or your TV is over 60 inches.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A smart, space-saving solution with minor assembly and cable-management quirks that are easy to work around. If the corner is your constraint, this is the stand to beat.
