If you've ever slept on a real tatami in a Japanese ryokan, you know the feeling — that clean, grassy scent and the firm-yet-giving support that foam mattresses just can't replicate. The FULI Japanese Tatami Mattress tries to bottle that experience for your home, and for the most part, it succeeds. But it's not without quirks, and whether it's right for you depends on how much you value authenticity over convenience.

## What You Actually Get

The FULI is a traditional square tatami mat — essentially a dense rush grass core wrapped in woven igusa (Japanese rush grass) with a cotton cloth border. The listing offers multiple sizes, from compact floor cushions up to full mattress dimensions. The square design (79x79 inches in the largest configuration) makes it versatile: you can use it as a floor bed, a meditation mat, a yoga surface, or a decorative rug that doubles as seating.

What's in the box is straightforward: the mat itself, rolled tightly. No tools, no assembly. Unroll it and give it 24-48 hours to flatten completely — the edges may curl initially, which is normal for compressed tatami.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The weave is tight and consistent. Run your hand across the surface and you'll feel the texture of the rush grass — slightly coarse but not scratchy. The cloth border is stitched cleanly, and the non-slip backing is genuinely functional. One buyer noted: *"I put this on hardwood and it doesn't budge even with my kids jumping on it."* That's a real plus for households with active children or slippery floors.

As a sleeping surface, it's firm. Very firm. If you're used to plush memory foam, this will feel like sleeping on a yoga mat over a hardwood floor. But that's the point — traditional tatami promotes spinal alignment, and many users report better back comfort after switching. The grass also naturally regulates humidity, which is a genuine benefit in humid climates.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The aroma is the standout feature. Nearly every positive review mentions the smell. One reviewer wrote: *"Opened the package and my whole bedroom smelled like a Japanese spa. It's been two months and the scent is still there, just subtler."* Another praised the craftsmanship: *"The weaving is beautiful — you can tell this isn't a cheap knockoff. It looks expensive."* The firmness and durability also earn consistent praise, with multiple long-term users reporting no sagging after months of daily use.

## Where It Falls Short

The initial odor is the biggest complaint — and it's real. The rush grass smell is strong, and while most find it pleasant, some describe it as "overwhelming" or even "like a barn." If you're sensitive to scents, plan to air it out in a garage or spare room for several days before bringing it inside.

The surface is also delicate. Furniture legs will leave permanent indentations, and dragging anything across it scuffs the weave. This isn't a set-and-forget rug; it requires care. And because it's not washable — spot-clean only — spills and pet accidents are a real problem. One frustrated buyer noted: *"My cat peed on it and I basically had to throw it away. There's no way to properly clean it."*

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You value authentic materials and traditional Japanese aesthetics, you sleep on the floor or a low bed frame, or you want a natural, humidity-regulating surface for yoga or meditation. It's also excellent for creating a minimalist, zen-inspired room.

**Skip it if:** You have pets prone to accidents, you prefer plush sleep surfaces, or you want a low-maintenance rug that survives kids and heavy traffic. Also skip if you're sensitive to strong natural scents.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the smell go away?**
A: The strongest odor fades within 3-7 days. A subtle grassy scent lingers for months — most find it pleasant, but it never fully disappears.

**Q: Can I use this directly on the floor?**
A: Yes, the non-slip backing grips hardwood, laminate, and tile. For concrete or carpet, use a thin underlay to prevent moisture buildup.

**Q: How do I clean it?**
A: Vacuum regularly with a soft brush attachment. For spills, blot immediately with a damp cloth — don't soak it. Never machine wash or use harsh chemicals.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The FULI Japanese Tatami Mattress delivers exactly what it promises: an authentic igusa rush grass mat that transforms a room's atmosphere.
