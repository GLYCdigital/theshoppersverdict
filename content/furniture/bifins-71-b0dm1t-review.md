## Where It Falls Short

The color match is the biggest inconsistency. The product images show a neutral tone, but multiple buyers report the actual finish leaning warmer or darker depending on their lighting. It's not a dealbreaker, but don't expect an exact match to the photos.

Surface durability is another consideration. This is engineered wood, not solid hardwood. It handles normal use fine, but sliding heavy objects across it will leave scratches. Use felt pads under decor or electronics.

And at 71 inches, measure your space before ordering. This is wider than a standard TV stand, which is great if you need the room — but it can overwhelm a small wall.
