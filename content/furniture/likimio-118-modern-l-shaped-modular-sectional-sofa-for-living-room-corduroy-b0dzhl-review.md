## A Modular Sofa That Actually Earns the "Modular" Label

Most sofas claiming to be "modular" are just a chaise bolted to a loveseat with a one-way configuration. The LIKIMIO 118" L-Shaped Modular Sectional is different — each of the four seats is an independent unit with interlocking brackets. You can build the classic L-shape shown in the product image, flip the chaise to the other side, or split it into two separate seating groups. That flexibility is the single biggest reason this sofa earns a spot on your shortlist.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains four modular seat units, a chaise extension, and two backrests per seat. Each piece has a solid pine frame underneath the corduroy upholstery, and the cushions are high-density foam wrapped in a soft polyfill layer. The fabric is a medium-weight corduroy with a subtle rib — not the heavy-duty stuff from your grandparent's couch, but a modern take that feels soft without being fragile.

Full dimensions: 118 inches across the longest side, 66 inches deep at the chaise, and 33 inches tall. Seat height sits at 18 inches, which is comfortable for most adults to get in and out of without feeling like you're sinking into a pit.

## Real-World Performance

The corduroy is the standout feature. It's noticeably softer than the flat-weave polyester most sub-$800 sectionals use, and it has enough texture to hide minor spills and pet hair between cleanings. The foam density in the seat cushions (claimed at 30D) holds up well — one reviewer noted, "After 8 months of daily use, the seats still bounce back. No sagging, no permanent dents."

The modular brackets are metal, not plastic, which is a good sign for longevity. Once locked together, the joints feel solid — no wobble or creaking when someone sits down hard. That said, the assembly process isn't a 10-minute job. Plan for 45 minutes with a second person. The brackets require firm pressure to seat properly, and the diagram-only instructions leave some room for guesswork on which bracket orientation goes where.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The modularity is the recurring theme in positive reviews. One buyer wrote, "I moved apartments twice since buying this, and being able to break it down into manageable pieces was a lifesaver. No dolly needed." Another praised the color accuracy: "The beige is exactly what the photos show — not too warm, not too gray."

The corduroy also earns consistent compliments. Multiple reviewers mention it feels more expensive than the price suggests, with one noting, "My guests always ask where I got it because they assume it cost double."

## Where It Falls Short

The back cushions are the weakest link. They ship vacuum-compressed and take a full day or two to fully expand — and even then, they're noticeably softer than the seats. If you prefer firm lumbar support, you'll want to add throw pillows or a backrest bolster. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

The corduroy nap is a maintenance quirk. Vacuum against the grain and you'll see visible streaking. You have to vacuum in consistent strokes, which is more fuss than a flat-weave fabric. Also, the included assembly tools are basic — a hex key and wrench — so a powered driver will make the job faster.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want a sectional that adapts to your space — apartment dwellers who might move, renters who need to fit awkward corners, or anyone who likes rearranging furniture on a whim. The corduroy texture is a bonus if you want a sofa that doesn't look like every other gray microfiber slab on Amazon.

**Skip it if:** You need firm back support out of the box, or you want a zero-assembly setup. This isn't the sofa for a formal living room where you'll never move the pieces — it's a functional, comfortable workhorse for everyday living.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I wash the cushion covers?**
A: The covers are not removable for machine washing. Spot-clean with a damp cloth and mild detergent. A handheld upholstery vacuum works well for crumbs and pet hair.

**Q: Will this fit through a standard doorway?**
A: Yes — each seat unit is roughly 30 inches wide and ships fully assembled. You won't need to disassemble frames to get it inside, which is a major advantage over one-piece sectionals.

**Q: Is the chaise reversible to the other side?**
A: Yes. Since each piece is independent, you can place the chaise on either end. You can also detach it entirely and use the sofa as a four-seat straight configuration.
