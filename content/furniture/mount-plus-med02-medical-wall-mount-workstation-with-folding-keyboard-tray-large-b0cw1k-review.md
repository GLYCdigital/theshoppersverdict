If you've ever watched a nurse balance a chart on a rolling cart while typing one-handed, you already know why wall-mounted workstations exist. The Mount Plus MED02 solves that exact problem — but it's not just for hospitals.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the wall plate, dual-arm assembly, folding keyboard tray, VESA mounting brackets, and all necessary hardware. The keyboard tray folds flat against the wall when not in use — a genuinely useful feature in tight exam rooms or home offices where floor space is at a premium. The arm extends and retracts smoothly, giving you about 16 inches of reach from the wall plate to the monitor face.

This isn't a flimsy TV mount. The steel construction is substantial — you feel the heft when you lift it out of the box. The included cable management channels run along the arm, which keeps IV lines and cords from becoming a tangled mess. In a medical setting, that's not cosmetic — it's a safety feature.

## Real-World Performance

The headline spec here is the 100-pound weight capacity on the keyboard tray. That's not marketing fluff. One Amazon buyer noted, "I mounted a 22-pound all-in-one medical PC and the arm holds position perfectly — no drift, no sag, even after months of daily use." Another reviewer mentioned they use it in a veterinary clinic with a heavier AIO unit and had zero issues.

The gas spring arm handles the monitor side with adjustable tension. Dialing it in takes a hex wrench and a few minutes of trial-and-error — you'll tighten, test, loosen, retest. It's not difficult, but it's not tool-free either. Once set, the arm stays put. The folding mechanism clicks into place securely, and the friction hinges on the keyboard tray hold their angle without creeping downward.

**VESA compatibility** covers both 75x75 and 100x100 patterns, which means it'll accept nearly any medical-grade monitor or compact all-in-one PC on the market. The included mounting hardware accommodates different thicknesses of VESA plates, so you won't be hunting for longer screws.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 235 ratings (4.7 stars), three themes dominate:

- **Build quality** — "Solid steel, powder-coated finish, no sharp edges. This feels like commercial-grade equipment, not consumer furniture."
- **Space savings** — The folding tray is the standout feature. Multiple reviewers mention reclaiming floor space in cramped treatment rooms.
- **Stability** — Even with heavier monitor-arm combinations, buyers report zero wobble during use. One reviewer specifically praised the lack of "that cheap TV-mount sway" when typing.

## Where It Falls Short

The installation is not a 10-minute job. The wall plate needs to be anchored into studs — and the included hardware assumes you have wood studs at standard 16-inch spacing. If you're mounting into metal studs, concrete, or tile, you'll need to source your own anchors. Several reviewers mentioned this as an unexpected hiccup.

The gas spring adjustment, as mentioned, requires patience. One buyer wrote, "Getting the tension right took about 20 minutes of fiddling. Once it's set, it's perfect, but don't expect plug-and-play." If you're not comfortable with basic tools, budget extra time.

Also note: the arm has a minimum depth when fully retracted. The monitor sits about 4 inches from the wall at its closest, so don't expect a flush-mounted look.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Medical professionals** — exam rooms, dental offices, veterinary clinics, or telehealth setups where hygiene and space matter
- **Home office users** with a heavy all-in-one PC who want to reclaim desk space
- **Workshop or garage users** who need a sturdy monitor station that folds out of the way

Skip it if you're mounting a lightweight consumer monitor to drywall without stud access, or if you want a tool-free, 5-minute install.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this hold a 27-inch monitor plus a keyboard?**
A: Yes. The VESA plate handles monitors up to about 20 pounds on the arm, and the tray supports up to 100 pounds. A 27-inch monitor plus keyboard and mouse is well within limits.

**Q: Can I mount this on drywall without studs?**
A: Technically, but you shouldn't. The included anchors are for light loads. With a monitor and keyboard tray, you want solid studs. If you must use drywall, buy heavy-duty toggle bolts rated for at least 150 pounds.

**Q: Does the keyboard tray lock in the folded position?**
A: It stays folded via friction hinges, not a mechanical latch. It won't swing open on its own, but it's not locked — a firm push will unfold it.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a commercial-grade wall mount that handles heavy equipment without sag, especially in medical or tight spaces. **Skip it if** you're looking for a cheap, tool-free TV mount for a light consumer monitor — this is overkill.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The Mount Plus MED02 is a well-engineered, durable workstation that earns its price through build quality and thoughtful design. The installation friction and gas spring tuning are minor tradeoffs for a mount that will outlast the equipment it holds.
