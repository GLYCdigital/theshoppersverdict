If you've ever tried to cram towels, toiletries, and cleaning supplies into a bathroom that has no built-in storage, you know the struggle. The Topeakmart Freestanding Floor Cabinet steps into that gap — literally. It's a narrow, four-drawer tower that claims to organize the chaos without taking over your floor plan. After digging through 220 ratings and the buyer feedback behind that 4.4-star average, here's the real story.

## What You're Actually Getting

The box contains the cabinet body, four drawer fronts, the necessary hardware, and a set of instructions that are mostly clear. The unit measures roughly 23.6 inches wide by 11.8 inches deep and stands about 31.5 inches tall — a footprint that fits neatly beside a toilet or in a tight corner. The construction is MDF with a melamine coating, which is the right call for a bathroom. This isn't solid wood, but you're not paying solid-wood prices either.

The top panel is a slightly darker wood-grain finish that gives it a bit of visual interest against the white drawers. As shown in the product image, it's a clean, minimal look that won't clash with most bathroom aesthetics.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Here's where the cabinet both shines and stumbles. The MDF core is dense and the melamine coating does resist water — a critical feature for anything living in a bathroom. Several buyers confirmed this, with one noting, "I've had it in a steamy bathroom for six months and there's zero swelling or warping." That's the good news.

The less-good news is the drawer slides. They're plastic, not ball-bearing metal. In practice, they slide smoothly enough when the drawers are moderately loaded, but load them with heavy bottles or full-size shampoo containers and you'll feel a bit of wobble. One reviewer put it plainly: "The drawers work, but they don't glide. It's functional, not fancy."

The cabinet also needs to be anchored if you have kids or pets. It's stable when empty, but top-heavy when the upper drawers are packed. The included anti-tip hardware works, but only if you actually install it.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The dominant theme in positive reviews is the storage capacity relative to the footprint. As one shopper put it, "I didn't think this little cabinet would hold half my bathroom stuff, but it swallowed everything I threw at it." The four drawers are deep — about five inches each — and the full-width design means you're not wasting space on dividers or frames.

Buyers also repeatedly mention the assembly. The pre-drilled holes align properly, the parts are labeled, and most people report finishing in 45 to 60 minutes with just a Phillips screwdriver. That's a genuine plus in a category where frustrating builds are common.

## Where It Falls Short

The finish is the weak point. The melamine coating resists moisture, but it's not scratch-proof. Setting a metal can or a ceramic soap dish directly on the top will eventually leave marks. A simple tray or mat solves this, but it's worth knowing before you unpack.

The back panel is also thin and needs careful handling during assembly — a few reviewers reported cracking it when they over-tightened screws. Go easy with the driver and it's fine, but the margin for error is slim.

## Who Should Buy This

This cabinet is ideal for renters or homeowners with small bathrooms, half baths, or laundry rooms that need vertical storage without a full remodel. It's also a solid pick for anyone who wants a narrow, closed-storage solution that hides clutter behind drawers rather than open shelves.

Skip it if you need heavy-duty, furniture-grade quality that will survive a decade of abuse. This is a practical, budget-conscious solution, not a heirloom piece.

## FAQ

**Will this fit in a small bathroom?**
Yes. At under 24 inches wide and 12 inches deep, it fits beside most toilets or in corners. Measure your space first — it's compact, but it's not tiny.

**Is it waterproof?**
The melamine coating resists moisture and splashes, but it's not fully waterproof. Standing water left on the surface or edges for extended periods can eventually cause damage. Wipe up spills promptly.

**Can I use it outside the bathroom?**
Absolutely. It works well as a bedroom nightstand alternative, a craft storage unit, or a garage organizer, though the moisture resistance makes it best suited for humid spaces.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need compact, closed storage for a small bathroom and value function over premium finishes. The space efficiency and moisture resistance make it a smart pick at this price point.

**Skip it if** you expect heirloom quality, silky-smooth drawer action, or a scratch-proof surface. This is a practical buy, not a luxury one.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A genuinely useful storage solution with a few honest compromises on hardware and finish. It does its job well, and at this price, that's what matters most.
