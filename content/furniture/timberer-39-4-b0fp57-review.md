## Where It Falls Short

Honest downsides, because they exist. First, the darker finish options show **fingerprints and smudges** easily. You'll be wiping it down more often than you'd like, especially if you have kids. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's a maintenance reality.

Second, the **back panel is thin hardboard**. It's fine for structural support, but if you plan to push heavy items against the back from inside the cabinet, you'll feel it flex. Keep heavier items toward the front of the shelves.

Third, **no wall-anchoring hardware is included**. For a piece this height, especially in homes with kids or pets, you should secure it to the wall. You'll need to buy anchors separately — a minor annoyance, but worth flagging.
