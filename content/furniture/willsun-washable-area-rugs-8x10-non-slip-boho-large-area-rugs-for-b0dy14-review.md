**FAQ**

**Can I really machine wash an 8x10 rug?**
Yes, but only in a front-loading or high-capacity top-loader. Fold it accordion-style, use cold water, gentle cycle, and no fabric softener. Air dry only — never machine dry.

**Will the non-slip backing damage my floors?**
No. The TPR backing is gentle on hardwood and tile. Just ensure the floor is clean and dry before laying it down for maximum grip.

**How long does it take to flatten?**
Unroll it immediately and reverse-roll it if needed. Heavy furniture helps speed up the process — most buyers report it's flat within 24-48 hours.
