Let me save you some scrolling: the HOOBRO 4-Tier AV Media Stand isn't trying to be a $900 piece of walnut furniture. It's a functional, open-concept media console that nails the essentials — including a built-in charging station — for a price that won't make you wince. Here's the breakdown after digging through 215 Amazon ratings and the specs.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the four-tier steel-frame stand, particleboard shelves with a laminated finish, the top panel with integrated charging ports (one USB-A, one Type-C), and all necessary hardware. As shown in the product image, the design is airy — no solid cabinet doors, just open tiers that let your receiver's remote signal pass through without a second thought. The dimensions are compact enough for a 55-inch TV setup but substantial enough to hold a soundbar, cable box, and a couple of consoles. The charging ports sit flush on the top shelf, drawing power from a single cord you run to the wall.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The steel frame is the star here. It's welded, not just bolted, and it gives the whole unit a rigidity that many particleboard-only stands lack. The laminate shelves wipe clean easily, though they show fingerprints more than a matte finish would. Adjustable leveling feet are a genuine plus — I've seen wobbly $200 stands that don't have these, and they make a real difference on older hardwood floors.

Assembly is the tradeoff. The cam-lock system works, but it's fiddly. One reviewer summed it up: "Took me almost an hour, but the instructions were clear and I didn't have any leftover screws." That tracks. Go in expecting to spend some time, and you'll be fine.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The charging station is the headline feature, and it delivers. Multiple reviewers note the convenience of plugging in phones and tablets without hunting for an outlet behind the stand. One wrote: "The USB-C port charges my phone fast, and I actually use it every single day." The open design also earns consistent love — "No more bending down to point a remote at a closed cabinet door" is a recurring sentiment.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. Open shelving means everything you own is on display — cables, dusty game cases, all of it. If you're a neat freak, this will test you. The weight capacity is also modest; one reviewer noted their heavy AV receiver made the shelf sag slightly over time. And the laminate isn't scratch-proof — drag a metal console across it and you'll leave a mark. Finally, the charging cable management is minimal; you'll want zip ties to keep the power cord tidy.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the practical shopper who wants a sturdy, functional media stand without paying designer markup. It's ideal for:
- Gamers with a console or two who want easy access and airflow
- Apartment dwellers needing a compact, open unit that doesn't dominate the room
- Anyone tired of bending behind closed cabinet doors to press buttons on their receiver

**FAQ: Three real questions**

*Does it fit a 65-inch TV?* The stand itself is rated for TVs up to 65 inches, but check the weight — the top shelf isn't meant to bear a massive old plasma. A modern LED is fine.

*Can I use the charging ports without plugging the stand in?* No, the ports need the power cord connected. It's a single cord, but it does have to reach an outlet.

*Is the shelf spacing adjustable?* No, the four tiers are fixed height. Measure your components before buying — a tall amplifier might not fit between shelves.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a dependable, open media stand with a genuinely useful charging station and don't mind spending an hour with an Allen wrench. **Skip it if** you need enclosed storage, plan to place a heavy amplifier on a shelf, or want a piece that looks like it cost ten times more.

At 4.2/5, the HOOBRO 4-Tier earns its rating through function over flash. It's not furniture you'll pass down to your kids — but it's a smart, honest purchase for what it is.
