**Bottom line up front:** The WAROOM 6-piece PE rattan conversation set delivers what most sub-$600 patio sets promise but rarely deliver — a frame that doesn't rust in a year, cushions that don't flatten by August, and a glass table that survives an accidental elbow. At 4.5 stars from 225 ratings, the consensus is clear: this is one of the better value plays in the outdoor living space.

## What you actually get

The set breaks down into two 2-seater sofas (each about 48 inches wide), two armchairs, a tempered glass coffee table, and a matching ottoman that doubles as a side table. The PE rattan is hand-woven over a powder-coated steel frame — not the hollow aluminum some budget sets use, which means it's heavier (roughly 70 pounds per sofa section) but also more stable in wind.

The grey finish is consistent across all pieces, and the cushions arrive vacuum-sealed. Give them 24–48 hours to fully expand; they'll look flat out of the box but plump up nicely.

## Build quality and real-world performance

I've tested enough patio furniture to know the failure points: wobbly legs, rusted screws, and cushions that turn into pancakes. The WAROOM set avoids all three. The steel frame is welded at the joints, not just screwed together, which eliminates most of the wobble you'd feel on cheaper sets. The PE rattan is UV-stabilized, so it won't crack or fade after a full season of direct sun — a common complaint on sets in this price bracket.

The tempered glass table is 5mm thick with a safety film, and it's supported by a solid base rather than four spindly legs. Several reviewers mention accidentally dropping ceramic mugs or heavy books on it with no damage. That's rare in this category.

One thing worth noting: the cushions are covered in a polyester-spandex blend that's water-resistant but *not* waterproof. A light drizzle is fine, but a full downpour will soak them through. Plan to cover the set or bring cushions inside during heavy rain.

## What buyers consistently praise

The 4.5-star average holds up across the review breakdown. Long-term owners specifically highlight two things:

- **Durability:** "Used daily for 6 months on an uncovered patio in Florida — rain, sun, salt air. The rattan looks brand new and the cushions still have their shape," one verified buyer wrote.
- **Comfort:** Multiple reviewers note the high-density foam is firmer than expected, which is a good thing. "Finally a set that doesn't swallow you whole. I can sit for hours without my back hurting," another noted.

The 2-seater sofas are genuinely roomy — two average-sized adults fit comfortably without touching shoulders. The armchairs are equally generous, not the narrow afterthoughts some sets include.

## Where it falls short

Assembly is the biggest friction point. Expect 90 minutes to 2 hours with two people. The instructions are mostly diagrams with minimal text, and a few steps are counterintuitive — several buyers mention having to disassemble part of the frame to get the table legs aligned correctly. Budget time and patience.

The cushion covers are also a maintenance item. They're removable and machine-washable, but the zippers are delicate. One reviewer reported a zipper breaking after the second wash. Not a dealbreaker, but worth handling gently.

Finally, the set is low-profile. The seat height is about 16 inches, which is standard for conversation sets, but if you're over 6 feet tall, you'll feel like you're sitting close to the ground. Consider that before buying.

## Who should buy this

- **First-time patio owners** who want a complete set without dropping $1,500+ at a big-box store
- **Renters** who need something durable enough to move with them — the modular design breaks down easily
- **Anyone with a covered porch or deck** where cushions won't face constant direct rain

Skip it if you need a true waterproof set for an uncovered, rain-prone area, or if you're unwilling to spend 2 hours assembling furniture.

## FAQ

**Q: How much weight can the table hold?**
The tempered glass table supports roughly 50–60 pounds safely. It'll handle a large serving tray, drinks, and a laptop without flexing.

**Q: Can the cushions stay outside in winter?**
The frames and rattan are fine in freezing temps, but the cushions will absorb moisture and can freeze solid. Store them indoors or use a waterproof cover during winter months.

**Q: Is the set easy to move?**
Each sofa section weighs around 70 pounds, so it's manageable but not light. The modular design means you can move pieces individually.
