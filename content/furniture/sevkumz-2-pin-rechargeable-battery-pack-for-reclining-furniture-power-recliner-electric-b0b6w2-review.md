**The sevkumz 2-Pin Rechargeable Battery Pack solves a problem you didn't realize you had — until you've tripped over your recliner's power cord one too many times.**

If you own a power recliner, you know the drill. The cord snakes across the floor, gets caught under the footrest, and dictates where the chair has to sit. This battery pack cuts that cord entirely, turning any 2-pin power recliner into a truly wireless piece of furniture. At 4.2 stars from 210 ratings, it's not perfect — but for most people, it's the fix they've been looking for.

## What You Actually Get

The package is straightforward: the battery pack itself, a wall charger, and a short cable that connects from the pack to your recliner's motor. The unit is compact — roughly the size of a thick paperback — and clips discreetly to the side or back of your chair frame. As shown in the product image, it's designed to be out of sight, not a decorative feature.

The key spec here is the 2-pin connector. This is the industry standard for most budget-to-mid-range power recliners from brands like Homall, Best Choice Products, and Flash Furniture. If your chair has a round 2-prong plug where the power cord meets the motor, this will likely fit. If it has a different connector (like a 3-pin or proprietary plug), you'll need to look elsewhere.

## Performance and Real-World Use

Installation takes about two minutes. Unplug your recliner from the wall, plug the battery pack into the motor's existing connector, and attach the pack to the frame with the included straps. That's it. No wiring, no adapters, no technical skill required.

In practice, the battery pack delivers what it promises. Your recliner works exactly as it did when plugged in — recline, lift, and headrest functions all operate normally. The freedom to place your chair anywhere without a nearby outlet is genuinely liberating, especially in open-concept rooms or if you like rearranging furniture.

That said, battery life is the tradeoff. Real buyers report anywhere from two weeks to two months per charge, depending heavily on how often you use the recline function. One verified buyer noted: *"I use my recliner maybe 3-4 times a week for an hour or two, and I get about 3 weeks per charge. It's not a big deal to plug it in overnight."*

Charging takes several hours, and there's no LED indicator showing charge level. This is the single biggest frustration — you won't know the battery is low until the recline motion slows or stops mid-recline. It's not dangerous, but it is annoying.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The overwhelming theme in positive reviews is the elimination of extension cords. One reviewer wrote: *"I was tired of the cord running across my living room. This battery pack completely solved it. My recliner sits in the middle of the room now."* Another praised the build: *"Feels solid, not cheap. ## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the battery life uncertainty, there are a few honest drawbacks. The pack adds a slight bulk to the chair's frame — it's not invisible, and you'll notice it if you're looking. It also doesn't work with every 2-pin recliner; some motors draw more power, which drains the battery faster and can make the recline sluggish as the charge depletes. If your recliner is a heavy-duty model with power headrest and lumbar, expect shorter battery life.

There's also no replacement battery option. When the internal cells eventually wear out (typically 2-3 years with regular use), you'll be buying the whole unit again.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have a 2-pin power recliner and want freedom from wall outlet placement. You're tired of tripping over cords or need to position your chair in a spot without easy outlet access. You're comfortable with a weekly-to-monthly charging routine.

**Skip it if:** You have a 3-pin or proprietary connector. You use your recliner heavily every single day and don't want to deal with recharge cycles. You need a battery level indicator — this doesn't have one.
