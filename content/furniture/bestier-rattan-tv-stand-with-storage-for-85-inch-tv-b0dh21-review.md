## What You Actually Get

The box contains the TV stand in flat-pack form, a hardware kit, and an instruction manual that's better than average for this category. No tools included — you'll need a Phillips screwdriver and maybe a rubber mallet for the dowels. The unit measures roughly 70 inches wide, which is the sweet spot for 85-inch TVs: wide enough to feel proportional without dominating the room.

Build materials are a mix of engineered wood (MDF) with a natural rattan weave on the cabinet doors. The legs are solid wood — a nice touch at this price point. The top surface is rated for the weight of an 85-inch TV, which typically runs 80–110 pounds depending on the model. That's a real spec, not marketing.

## Performance and Build Quality

The rattan doors are the star here. They're woven, not printed, which means you get actual texture and depth. As shown in the product image, the natural tone works well with both light and dark interiors. The doors conceal two cabinets with adjustable shelves inside, plus an open cubby in the center for media players or game consoles.

Buyers consistently praise the storage capacity. One reviewer noted, "I fit my soundbar on top, four streaming devices inside, and still had room for board games in the bottom cabinet." That tracks with the dimensions — the cabinets are deep enough for cable boxes and game consoles without cramming.

The cable management cutouts in the back panel are positioned correctly — high enough to route HDMI cables from a wall-mounted TV, low enough for floor-level outlets. Small detail, but it saves you from drilling holes or buying grommets.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The natural aesthetic is the #1 reason people choose this over a standard media console. "Finally a TV stand that doesn't look like it belongs in a dorm room," one verified buyer wrote. Another mentioned the color accuracy: "The rattan is lighter in person than the photos — more honey than brown. It brightened up my whole living room."

Sturdiness also gets consistent mentions. Multiple reviewers note that the stand doesn't wobble even with heavy TVs on top, thanks to the solid wood legs and cross-bracing underneath.

## Where It Falls Short

Assembly is the biggest friction point. It's not difficult, but it's tedious — expect 60 to 90 minutes if you're doing it solo. Door alignment is the tricky part. The hinges have adjustment screws, but several reviewers mention needing multiple attempts to get the doors to close flush. Budget extra time or recruit a second person.

The rattan weave is decorative, not durable. It will scratch if you have pets that like to rub against furniture. And it's not wipe-clean friendly — dust settles into the weave, requiring a soft brush or vacuum attachment. One reviewer warned, "Don't put a drink on the rattan doors. Water marks show immediately."

The center open shelf has no back panel, so anything stored there is visible and exposed. If you want a clean, hidden look for all your gear, you'll need to use the closed cabinets.

## FAQ

**Will this actually hold an 85-inch TV?**
Yes. The top is rated for the weight, and the solid wood legs provide real support. Just make sure your TV's feet fit within the 70-inch width — most do, but ultra-wide base stands might overhang slightly.

**Can I mount a soundbar on top?**
Yes, there's plenty of surface area. Just keep the soundbar in front of the TV feet to avoid blocking the IR receiver.

**Is the rattan real or printed?**
It's real woven rattan, not a printed pattern. That's why it looks better in person than most photos suggest.
