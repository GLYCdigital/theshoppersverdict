Let’s be upfront: a 3.9-star average across 195 ratings tells you this LED medicine cabinet isn’t perfect. But for the price, it does things mirrors twice its cost don’t. Here’s the breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The GUIQIYANG arrives fully assembled — just unpack, mount, and wire. You get the mirrored cabinet itself, a backplate for recessed mounting, a surface-mount bracket alternative, and basic hardware. The mirror measures roughly 24 x 20 inches, with an interior depth around 4.5 inches. That’s enough for daily toiletries but not a full medicine stash.

The headline feature is the three-color LED strip: warm white (3000K), natural (4500K), and cool daylight (6000K). The touch switch on the mirror face cycles through them and remembers your last setting. The anti-fog pad runs continuously while powered, which is a genuine convenience if your bathroom steams up fast.

**How It Performs in Real Use**

The LED lighting is the standout. One buyer noted, “The cool white setting is perfect for makeup application — I can finally see what I’m doing.” That’s accurate. The light is bright and evenly diffused across the mirror face, not a harsh strip. The memory function means you won’t be fumbling for your preferred setting every morning.

The anti-fog works — mostly. It clears the main mirror surface within a minute of a hot shower ending. But the edges, where the frame meets the glass, can stay fogged longer. Minor, but worth knowing.

Storage is adequate: two adjustable glass shelves and a bottom storage area. The catch? The shelves adjust in fixed increments, so a tall mouthwash bottle might not fit without removing a shelf entirely. Plan your layout carefully.

**Where It Falls Short**

The biggest complaint across multiple reviews is the installation. The included screws and wall anchors are cheap. As one reviewer put it, “The cabinet itself is solid, but the mounting hardware is trash. Buy your own anchors before you start.” This is a real issue — drywall installation without upgraded anchors risks a wobbly cabinet.

Wire management is also awkward. The power cord exits from the top, and with no junction box cover included, you’ll need to hide the connection yourself. If you’re not comfortable with basic electrical work, budget for an electrician.

Finally, the mirror has a slight blue tint at the edges, a common artifact in tempered glass. Most won’t notice, but if you’re obsessive about color accuracy, it’s there.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Renters with bathroom light switches nearby**: If you can wire it into an existing switch circuit, this upgrades a rental bathroom without a full remodel.
- **Anyone tired of foggy mirrors post-shower**: The anti-fog function genuinely works, and the three light temperatures make grooming easier.
- **DIYers comfortable with basic wiring**: The electrical hookup isn’t complex, but it’s non-negotiable.

**Skip It If**

- **You’re renting and can’t hardwire**: There’s no plug-in version. Battery or outlet-only options from other brands serve this crowd better.
- **You need tall storage**: The fixed shelf spacing and 4.5-inch depth won’t fit large bottles or hair tools.
- **You want a true “smart” mirror**: This has no Bluetooth, no defogger timer, no dimmer. It’s a light-up mirror, not a tech hub.

**FAQ**

**Is this mirror truly anti-fog, or just a coating?** It’s a powered heating pad, not a coating. It stays on while the cabinet is wired and actively clears condensation. You can’t turn the defogger off separately — it runs whenever the unit has power.

**Can I install this without an electrician?** If you’re replacing an existing light fixture or have a junction box nearby, yes. The cabinet needs a 110V connection. If you’re starting from scratch, hire someone. Several negative reviews trace back to wiring mistakes, not the product.

**What’s the actual interior storage space?** Roughly 19 x 14 x 4.5 inches of usable space. Two glass shelves split that into three tiers. Small bottles and daily-use items fit fine; bulk Costco-sized products won’t.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a functional, well-lit mirror cabinet with solid anti-fog performance and don’t mind handling the wiring yourself.

**Skip it if** you’re a renter, need flexible storage, or expect premium installation hardware at this price point.

The GUIQIYANG sits in a sweet spot: better lighting than big-box store alternatives, but it cuts corners on mounting hardware and shelf adjustability. At 3.7/5, it’s a solid value for the right buyer — just bring your own anchors and a patient attitude for the install.
