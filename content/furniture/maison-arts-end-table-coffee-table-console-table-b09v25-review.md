# MAISON ARTS End Table Coffee Table Console Table Review

Furniture shopping online is a gamble. You're betting on dimensions that look right, colors that might match, and build quality you can't touch. The MAISON ARTS 3-in-1 table tries to hedge that bet by giving you three placement options in one package. After digging through 215 ratings and the actual product specs, here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes the table itself — a rectangular top measuring roughly 40 inches long, 16 inches wide, and 18 inches tall — plus a steel frame, hardware, and a hex wrench. The top is a wood-grain laminate over MDF (medium-density fiberboard). The frame is powder-coated steel in black, which gives it a more substantial feel than the flimsy aluminum legs you see on budget furniture.

Assembly takes about 10 minutes. The parts come labeled, the holes line up properly, and you won't need anything beyond the included wrench. That's rare in this price bracket — most competing tables require a Phillips head screwdriver and a patience test.

## Real-World Performance

The steel frame is the standout feature here. It's welded at the joints, not just screwed together, which makes a noticeable difference in stability. Several buyers mention using this as a plant stand for heavy potted plants and a TV stand for smaller sets. One reviewer wrote, "I was skeptical about the weight capacity, but I have a 50-pound fish tank on mine and it hasn't budged in three months." That tracks with the 120-pound weight rating.

The laminate top is where you'll find the compromise. It looks like wood from a distance and resists water rings well — a coffee cup left overnight won't leave a mark. But it's not solid wood. Drag a ceramic vase across it and you'll see a scratch. Use coasters and lift things instead of sliding them, and it'll hold up fine.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 215 ratings, three themes keep coming up:

**Versatility.** This is the main selling point, and it delivers. As an end table beside a sofa, it's the right height. As a coffee table, it works if you're okay with a slightly lower profile. In a hallway or behind a couch, it functions as a console. One buyer noted, "I bought two — one for the living room, one for the entryway. Same table, two completely different looks."

**Sturdiness.** Multiple reviewers mention the lack of wobble, even on uneven floors. The adjustable feet pads help with that — you can twist them to level the table.

**Assembly.** "Took me longer to unbox it than to put it together," is a recurring sentiment. The instructions are clear, and the parts fit without forcing.

## Where It Falls Short

The height is the most common complaint. At 18 inches, it's on the shorter end for a coffee table. If you have a tall sofa (19+ inches seat height), this will feel low. Measure your furniture before ordering — don't assume it'll match.

The laminate finish is the second issue. It's durable against moisture but not against impact. A dropped book or a heavy vase can chip the edge. If you have kids who treat furniture like playground equipment, this isn't the table for you.

Finally, the color options are limited — mostly black and a few wood tones. There's no white or light oak option, which might rule this out for brighter rooms.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You need a versatile piece for a small apartment or rental. The 3-in-1 design means you can move it between rooms as your needs change. It's also great for first-time furniture buyers who want something functional without spending $300 per table.

**Skip it if:** You want a statement piece, need solid wood construction, or have a tall sofa that requires a 20+ inch coffee table. This is a practical, mid-range option — not a heirloom-quality item.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the top scratch easily?**  
A: The laminate resists water and normal wear, but it's not scratch-proof. Use felt pads under decor items and lift, don't drag, heavy objects.

**Q: Can I use this outdoors?**  
A: No. The MDF top and steel frame aren't weatherproof. Keep it indoors or on a covered porch at most.

**Q: How much weight can it really hold?**  
A: The frame is rated for 120+ pounds. Buyers report using it for fish tanks, large TVs, and heavy planters without issues. The top itself is the limiting factor — distribute weight evenly.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The MAISON ARTS 3-in-1 table earns its rating through honest versatility and solid construction. It's not the fanciest piece you'll own, but it's a workhorse that adapts to your space. For the price, you're getting three furniture pieces in one.

**Buy it if** you value function over form and need a table that can play multiple roles. **Skip it if** you're after premium materials or need a taller profile. This is a smart, practical buy for most homes — just measure first and use coasters.
