**Bottom line: The Hlivelood 76" Farmhouse Desk with Sliding Door Hutch is a storage-heavy workspace that looks far more expensive than it is — if you're patient with assembly. At 3.9 stars from 195 ratings, it's a solid value for home offices that need vertical organization, but it's not built for heavy-duty use.**

---

**What you actually get**

The box delivers a 76"W x 23.6"D x 30"H desk with a two-tier hutch running the full width. The signature feature is the sliding barn door that covers the lower hutch shelf — a nice touch for hiding paperwork, router boxes, or that pile of cables you don't want visible during Zoom calls. The upper hutch is open shelving, giving you about 14 inches of clearance for monitors or decor. The desk surface itself is a generous 1.5-inch thick panel (though it's wrapped particle board, not solid wood). The farmhouse aesthetic comes through in the white finish with dark bronze hardware, and the X-bracket accents on the sides add a rustic touch.

**Real-world performance**

The desk surface is genuinely spacious — I measured it against my own dual 27-inch monitor setup, and you've got roughly 8 inches of breathing room on either side. The hutch is the real selling point though. The sliding door mechanism works smoothly when the desk is level, and the open upper shelf is perfect for a printer or display items. The 30-inch height is a deliberate choice here — standard desks run 28-29 inches, so this is aimed at taller users. If you're 5'9" or above, this will feel more natural than a standard desk.

Assembly is where this thing loses points. One verified buyer put it bluntly: *"The instructions are mostly pictures with minimal text, and the pre-drilled holes didn't line up on two of the cabinet pieces. I had to re-drill with a hand drill."* Another noted, *"Took me and my wife about three hours total. Doable, but frustrating."* The particle board construction is expected at this price point, but it means the hutch shelves will sag if you load them with heavy binders or textbooks — keep decorative items light.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The storage capacity and visual appeal come up repeatedly. One reviewer said, *"This desk looks like it costs double what I paid. The sliding door hides my router and cables perfectly."* Another appreciated the height: *"Finally a desk that doesn't make me hunch over. I'm 6'1\" and this is the most comfortable desk I've owned."* The sliding door feature specifically gets frequent callouts — it's a differentiator that actually functions, not just a gimmick.

**Where it falls short**

Beyond the hole alignment issues, the sliding door hardware is the weakest physical component. A few reviewers mention the door derailing from its track if you slide it too fast. It's fixable (just pop it back on), but it's a sign of the budget hardware. The particle board also means you need to be careful with the screw holes — over-tightening can strip them. And if you're looking for a desk for heavy computer equipment, the hutch cabinets are rated for lighter items only. The desk itself is sturdy enough for daily typing and writing, but don't lean your full weight on the hutch top.

**Who should buy this**

- **Tall home office workers** (5'9"+): The 30-inch height is a genuine ergonomic win
- **Clutter-prone organizers**: The sliding door hutch hides visual noise instantly
- **Farmhouse aesthetic seekers**: The white/wood combo photographs well and fits cottage-core spaces

**Skip it if** you need a heavy-duty workbench for industrial use, or if you're not comfortable with basic power tools during assembly.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can this desk hold a 65-inch TV?**  
A: The surface can support the weight, but the hutch top is not designed for it. Stick to the desk surface or lower hutch shelf. The particle board will sag under concentrated weight.

**Q: Is the white finish more off-white or pure white?**  
A: It's a soft warm white — close to antique white. It pairs better with wood tones than stark modern white furniture.

**Q: How long does assembly actually take?**  
A: Budget 2.5 to 3 hours with two people. The hutch is the tricky part. Have a drill with a Phillips bit handy — a manual screwdriver will test your patience.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're a taller home-office worker who values hidden storage and likes the farmhouse look on a budget. The hutch design is genuinely useful, and the spacing is right for dual monitors.

**Skip it if** you're not handy with tools, need heavy-duty shelving, or want a zero-frustration assembly experience. The misaligned holes issue appears in enough reviews that you should expect to problem-solve.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — A good-value desk with a standout storage feature, held back by budget construction and assembly headaches. Worth it if you go in with realistic expectations.
