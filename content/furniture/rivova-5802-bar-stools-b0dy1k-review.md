**The Short Version: Solid Mid-Range Stools That Nail the Basics**

The RIVOVA 5802 bar stools sit in that crowded middle ground of Amazon bar seating — not budget junk, not designer-tier. At 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, they've clearly satisfied most buyers. But the real question is whether they work for *your* kitchen island, *your* height, and *your* tolerance for faux leather quirks. Here's the honest breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains two bar stools (the listing is for a pair), each with a pre-assembled seat and separate metal X-frame legs. You get the Allen wrench, bolts, and floor caps in the package. As shown in the product image, these have a classic pub-style silhouette: padded rectangular seat, curved backrest, and that industrial X-cross base that's become the default for modern kitchens.

The seat sits at roughly 30 inches from the floor — standard counter height, not bar height. Measure your counter overhang before ordering. If you've got a 42-inch tall bar, these will feel too low.

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

The frame is welded steel with a matte black powder coat. In practice, that means it doesn't scratch when you slide the stools around and the finish doesn't chip at the weld points — a common failure on cheaper sets. The floor caps are rubber but not adjustable; on uneven floors you might get a slight rock, but most buyers report a stable seat on flat surfaces.

The seat cushion is high-density foam wrapped in faux leather. Multiple verified reviews mention the same thing: it's firmer than it looks but comfortable for 45-minute meals. One buyer wrote, *"These are not plush living-room chairs, but for sitting at the counter for breakfast and dinner, they're perfectly comfortable."* Another noted, *"The stitching is clean and the material hasn't peeled after six months of daily use."*

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The most common thread in positive reviews is the assembly experience. Most buyers report finishing both chairs in under 15 minutes with zero alignment headaches. That's genuinely rare in this category — many competing stools require wrestling with misdrilled holes.

The second recurring theme is sturdiness. Multiple reviewers specifically call out the lack of wobble even on tile and hardwood. The X-frame design distributes weight well, and the 250-pound weight capacity (per the manufacturer) holds up in practice.

**Where It Falls Short**

The footrest height is the most common complaint. At 7 inches off the floor, it's positioned for someone around 5'8". Shorter users — under 5'4" — report their feet don't comfortably reach it, leaving legs to dangle. That gets uncomfortable fast.

The faux leather also doesn't breathe. In warmer months, buyers report bare legs sticking to the seat. If your kitchen runs hot or you live in a humid climate, consider seat pads or long pants.

Finally, the backrest is more decorative than supportive. It curves slightly, but there's no lumbar give. You're not leaning back in these — they're for perching, not lounging.

**Who Should Buy These**

- **Counter-height islands at 35-36 inches:** These are the sweet spot.
- **Renters or frequent movers:** Lightweight (around 12 lbs each) and easy to disassemble.
- **Busy households:** The faux leather wipes down fast and doesn't stain easily.
- **Anyone who hates complicated assembly:** The tool-free bolt alignment genuinely works.

**Skip Them If**

- You're under 5'4" and want foot support.
- You need bar-height (30-inch seat) seating for a 42-inch bar.
- You want a chair to recline in — these are upright perching stools.

**FAQ**

**Q: What's the actual seat height?**
A: The seat surface sits at about 30 inches. Pair with a 35-36 inch counter or table. Not suitable for 42-inch bar tops.

**Q: Can these support heavier users?**
A: The manufacturer rates them at 250 lbs per stool. Multiple larger-framed reviewers report no issues, but the X-frame design means weight concentrates on the four floor caps.

**Q: Do they scratch hardwood floors?**
A: The rubber caps protect against scratches during normal use, but if you drag them, the metal frame can catch. Lift, don't slide.

**The Verdict: 4.2/5 — Buy It If You Fit the Profile**

These are competent, honest stools that deliver exactly what the listing promises — no more, no less.

**Buy it if:** You have a standard counter-height island, want easy assembly, and need durable, low-maintenance seating under $150 for the pair.

**Skip it if:** You're shorter than 5'4", need bar-height seating, or want a chair that supports lounging.

The RIVOVA 5802s are a safe, solid purchase for the right space. Just measure your counter and your inseam before you click buy.
