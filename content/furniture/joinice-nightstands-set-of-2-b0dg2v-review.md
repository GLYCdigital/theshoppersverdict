**A bedside table that actually works — if you're willing to build it**

Most nightstands under $150 are either flimsy particleboard disasters or so plain they vanish into the wall. The JOINICE set of 2 sits in a smarter middle zone: it looks like a $200+ piece, includes charging ports that people actually use, and holds up better than the price suggests. But there are tradeoffs, and you should know them before you click buy.

**What you actually get**

Two identical nightstands, each measuring about 16 inches wide, 14 inches deep, and 20 inches tall. The package includes all hardware, a hex wrench, and an instruction booklet that's mostly clear — though a few steps benefit from squinting at the diagrams. Each unit has one soft-close drawer, an open lower shelf, and a charging station built into the back panel: two USB-A ports and one standard AC outlet. The surface is a matte engineered wood with a wood-grain texture that looks better in person than the photos suggest.

**Real-world performance**

I've had these in a guest bedroom for three weeks of steady use. The build quality is the headline: the panels are dense, the corners align cleanly, and the finish hasn't scratched or scuffed despite a lamp, a glass of water, and a phone being set down repeatedly. The charging ports work as advertised — my phone charges at a reasonable speed, and the AC outlet handles a small lamp without issue.

The drawer is where you'll notice the cost-cutting. It's a decent size — enough for a book, glasses, and miscellany — but the glides are basic. They slide smoothly when you push evenly, but pull from one corner and you'll feel resistance. Several Amazon reviewers mention this too. One wrote, "The drawer sticks a little if I don't close it straight," and that matches my experience. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's not a premium feel.

Assembly is the real hurdle. Plan on 45–60 minutes per nightstand, and have a Phillips screwdriver ready — the included hex wrench is fine for the bolts but slow for everything else. The instructions are decent, but a few holes required a bit of pressure to align. Reviewers consistently echo this: "Took about an hour each, but the result is sturdy." Another said, "Doable, but don't rush it."

**What buyers consistently praise**

The charging station is the most-cited reason people buy these. "I love that I don't need an extra plug for my phone," one reviewer noted. The look also gets consistent love — "These look way more expensive than they are" appears in multiple reviews, and I agree. The matte finish and tapered legs give them a modern, slightly Scandinavian vibe that punches above the price point.

**Where it falls short**

The drawer glides are the weakest link — they're functional but not smooth. The USB ports are USB-A only, so if you've switched to USB-C cables, you'll need adapters (or use the AC outlet). And the engineered wood, while solid, won't survive a move — disassemble carefully if you relocate. Also, the open lower shelf collects dust visibly; you'll want to wipe it weekly.

**Who should buy this**

- **Apartment dwellers** needing two solid nightstands without spending a fortune
- **Anyone who charges devices bedside** — the ports are genuinely useful
- **Renters** who want a stylish look without permanent installation

**Skip it if**

- You hate assembly — this is 2 hours of your Saturday, minimum
- You need a larger storage drawer — this holds the essentials, not a wardrobe
- You want a premium, soft-close drawer experience — the glides are basic

**FAQ**

**Q: Are the USB ports fast-charging?**
A: They're standard 5V/2A — fine for overnight charging, not for rapid top-ups. Use the AC outlet if you need speed.

**Q: What's the weight capacity per shelf?**
A: The top surface handles a lamp, phone, and book easily. The lower shelf is sturdy but keep it under 20 pounds — it's not built for heavy storage.

**Q: Can these be used as a console table in a hallway?**
A: Yes, the dimensions work well for narrow spaces, but the drawer and charging ports are bedside-focused, so you'd be paying for features you won't use.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a good-looking, functional nightstand set with useful charging ports and don't mind an hour of assembly per unit.

**Skip it if** you expect buttery-smooth drawers or a zero-assembly experience — this delivers solid value, not luxury.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A smart buy for the price, with honest limitations you can plan around.
