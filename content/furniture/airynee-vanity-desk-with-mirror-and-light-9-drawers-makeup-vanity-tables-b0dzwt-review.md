Let's be direct: a vanity with nine drawers and an integrated LED mirror sounds like the dream setup. The Airynee delivers on storage capacity, but the 3.9-star average across 195 ratings tells you there's nuance here. This isn't a "buy it and forget it" piece — it's a "buy it, assemble it carefully, and respect its limits" piece.

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes the vanity table itself, the illuminated mirror (which mounts to the tabletop), nine drawers — arranged as a mix of wide and narrow configurations — and a matching stool. As shown in the product image, the layout is designed for serious collectors: your everyday products in the top drawers, backup stock in the middle, and hair tools or bulkier items in the lower, deeper drawers. The surface is engineered wood with a smooth, wipeable finish. It's not solid hardwood at this price point, and you shouldn't expect it to be. The dimensions are roughly standard for a bedroom vanity — wide enough for a monitor or large mirror setup, but not so sprawling that it swallows a small room.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

The nine drawers are the headline feature, and they genuinely deliver. Multiple buyers note that the storage capacity eliminates the need for separate acrylic organizers — you can stand bottles upright in the taller drawers and lay flat palettes in the shallower ones. The LED mirror is bright and has adjustable brightness levels, which matters more than most people think. If you've ever done makeup under a dim overhead light, you know the struggle. This solves it.

But here's the honest tradeoff: assembly is the great equalizer. Several verified reviews describe the process as "frustrating" and "time-consuming," with one buyer writing, *"Took me almost three hours to put together. The instructions are mostly pictures and some steps aren't clear."* Drawer track alignment is the most common pain point — if you don't get the tracks perfectly level, drawers stick or sit crooked. Take your time, use a level, and don't rush this step. The reward is a genuinely stable piece of furniture. Buyers who assembled it carefully report no wobble, no sagging drawers, and a vanity that feels solid.

**What Buyers Praise**

The storage layout earns consistent praise. One reviewer put it simply: *"I was shocked by how much fits in this thing. All my makeup, brushes, and even my hair dryer have a home."* The LED mirror's brightness adjustment also gets mentioned repeatedly as a standout feature — it's not a gimmick, it's functional. And the overall aesthetic — clean lines, neutral finish — photographs well and fits most bedroom styles.

**Where It Falls Short**

Beyond assembly, there are two recurring complaints. First, the lighting is a single color temperature — bright, cool white. If you prefer warm, soft lighting for getting ready, this will feel harsh. Second, the included stool is essentially a padded box. Several buyers describe it as "uncomfortable after 20 minutes" and recommend budgeting for a separate cushioned vanity chair. Also worth noting: the mirror is not height-adjustable, so if you're significantly taller or shorter than average, you may find yourself hunching or craning.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The organized collector**: If you own a substantial makeup and skincare stash and want everything visible and accessible, the nine drawers are a genuine win.
- **The practical shopper**: You want an all-in-one solution (lighting + storage) without buying separate components.
- **The patient assembler**: You're comfortable with a multi-hour furniture build and have basic tools.

**Skip it if** you want a quick setup, need soft/warm lighting, or expect a plush, comfortable stool included.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can the LED mirror be used without the lights on?**
A: Yes, the mirror functions normally with the lights off. The lighting is on a separate switch, so you can use it as a plain mirror during the day and switch on the LEDs when you need them.

**Q: Is the vanity sturdy enough for daily use?**
A: When assembled correctly with all screws tightened, yes. The engineered wood is dense and the frame holds its shape. The weak point is the drawers — they're on basic slides, so don't overload them with heavy items.

**Q: Does it come with a warranty?**
A: Standard Amazon return policy applies (30 days). Some buyers report the seller offering replacement parts for damaged items, but this isn't guaranteed — inspect all pieces before starting assembly.

**The Verdict**

This is a solid, feature-rich vanity that rewards patience. The storage capacity and integrated lighting genuinely outperform many competitors at this price point. But the assembly difficulty, cool-only lighting, and subpar stool are real drawbacks.

**Buy it if** you value storage and integrated lighting and don't mind a Saturday afternoon build project.

**Skip it if** you want plug-and-play furniture or need warm lighting for your routine.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — A good value for organized beauty enthusiasts who can handle the assembly; a frustrating experience for everyone else.
