# The EnHomee Bed Frame with 3 Drawers: Storage That Actually Works, With a Couple of Caveats

If you're shopping for a bed frame that does double duty as a dresser, the EnHomee with three drawers is likely on your radar. The concept is straightforward: skip the box spring, gain three pull-out drawers, and get a padded headboard — all in one unit. After digging through 200 ratings and the real-world feedback that comes with them, here's the honest picture.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the platform frame, three pre-assembled drawer units, the upholstered headboard, and all necessary hardware. The frame itself is a metal construction with a fabric-upholstered headboard. As shown in the product image, the drawers slide out from beneath the platform — not from the foot or sides, which keeps your nightstands clear.

The setup eliminates the need for a box spring entirely. Your mattress sits directly on the platform slats. That's a significant cost saving — box springs typically run $80–$200 depending on size. The slats are spaced closely enough to support memory foam and hybrid mattresses without sagging, per multiple buyer reports.

## Real-World Performance

Assembly is where this frame earns its keep. The interlocking design means no tools are required for the main frame — pieces click together, then you tighten a few bolts. One verified buyer noted, *"I put it together by myself in about 30 minutes. The drawers were already assembled, which was the part I was dreading."* That's consistent with what I'm seeing across reviews — most people report 30–45 minutes, even solo.

The drawers are the headline feature, and they work well. They roll on metal tracks with a smooth action — no sticking or wobbling reported in the majority of reviews. Each drawer is roughly 22 inches wide and about 6 inches deep. That's perfect for t-shirts, folded jeans, or sweaters. One buyer summed it up: *"I replaced a clunky dresser with this bed and freed up a ton of floor space in my small apartment."*

The headboard is foam-padded and upholstered, which makes sitting up in bed genuinely comfortable. It's not just decorative — the padding has enough give to lean back against for reading or watching TV.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the limitations.

**The drawers are shallow.** At approximately 6 inches deep, they won't accommodate bulky items — no thick comforters, no stacks of heavy sweaters, definitely no shoes. If you're expecting dresser-level capacity, you'll be disappointed. This is storage for daily essentials, not a replacement for a full dresser.

**It sits low.** The platform design means the top of the mattress sits closer to the floor than a standard frame with a box spring. For most people, that's fine — even preferable if you're short. But if you have knee or back issues, getting in and out of bed requires more effort. Several older reviewers mentioned this specifically.

**The headboard is low too.** It's more of a styling element than a functional headboard. If you want something to lean against while sitting fully upright, you may find it a bit short.

## Who Should Buy This

**Small apartment dwellers** — the under-bed storage genuinely replaces a small dresser and frees up floor space. **Anyone furnishing a guest room** — it's a complete bed solution in one box, no box spring needed. **Renters** — it's easy to disassemble and move, and the neutral upholstery works with most decor.

**Skip it if** you need serious storage capacity, if you have significant mobility challenges, or if you prefer the higher profile of a traditional frame.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need a box spring with this frame?**
No. The platform slats support your mattress directly. Adding a box spring would make the bed too tall.

**Q: What mattress types work with this frame?**
Memory foam, hybrid, and innerspring mattresses all work. The slats are closely spaced (roughly 2.5 inches apart), so there's no sagging risk for foam mattresses.

**Q: Can I use this without a headboard?**
The headboard is integrated into the frame design — it's not removable. If you don't want a headboard, this isn't the frame for you.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a space-saving bed with genuinely functional under-bed storage, and you're comfortable with the low profile.

**Skip it if:** You need deep, dresser-level drawer capacity or you have mobility concerns with low beds.

The EnHomee bed frame delivers exactly what it promises — practical storage, a comfortable headboard, and tool-free assembly — at a price point that undercuts buying a frame and box spring separately. The shallow drawers and low height are real tradeoffs, but for the right buyer, this is a smart, space-efficient solution.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid value with honest limitations, well-suited for apartments and guest rooms.
