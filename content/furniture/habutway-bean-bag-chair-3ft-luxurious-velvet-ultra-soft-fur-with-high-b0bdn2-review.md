Let’s cut through the fluff — literally. The HABUTWAY 3Ft Bean Bag Chair is one of those Amazon finds that looks questionable in the listing photos but surprises you when it actually shows up. With a 4.1-star average across 204 ratings, it’s not perfect, but it’s earning its keep in a lot of living rooms. Here’s what you’re actually getting.

## What Shows Up at Your Door

The chair arrives vacuum-compressed in a surprisingly manageable box. You get the velvet cover (zippered and removable) and a hefty bag of shredded memory foam fill. The 3-foot dimensions translate to roughly 36 inches across — enough to swallow a 6-foot adult but compact enough for a dorm room or reading nook. Assembly takes about 10 minutes: fluff the fill, pour it in, zip it up. No tools, no swearing.

The fabric is the headline here. It’s a synthetic velvet-fur blend that feels genuinely luxurious — think plush teddy bear, not cheap costume fur. The stitching on the seams is reinforced and double-stitched at stress points, which matters because this thing will get thrown around.

## How It Performs in Real Life

I’ll be direct: this is a lounging chair, not a posture-correcting ergonomic marvel. You sink into it. That’s the point. The shredded foam fill conforms to your body shape and provides decent support for reading, gaming, or watching TV — but don’t expect to work from it for eight hours.

The cover is removable and machine-washable, which is a non-negotiable feature for anything that lives on a floor. The zipper is sturdy, not the flimsy plastic kind that snaps on the third wash.

## What Buyers Actually Say

The praise is consistent. One verified buyer describes it as “shockingly soft” and notes it “looks way more expensive than it cost.” Another mentions using it as a guest bed alternative and being pleasantly surprised at the support. The plush factor comes up repeatedly — people genuinely seem to love how this fabric feels.

## Where It Falls Short

Here’s the honest part. Multiple reviewers report the fill settles significantly within the first month. The chair goes from “firm and fluffy” to “slightly deflated” — you’ll want to add more filling, which costs extra. It’s not a dealbreaker, but budget for it.

The velvet fur also collects everything. Pet hair, lint, crumbs — it all finds its way into the fibers. A lint roller becomes part of your routine. And if you’re over 200 pounds, the 3ft size might feel snug rather than spacious. Some taller buyers wished they’d sized up.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Gamers** who want a casual chair for long sessions without the price tag of a gaming chair
- **Parents** looking for a soft, safe play area seat for kids (the fabric is genuinely kid-friendly)
- **Apartment dwellers** who need flexible seating for guests without committing to permanent furniture

## FAQ

**Does the cover actually come off for washing?**
Yes, the zipper runs the full circumference. Machine wash cold, air dry. The fill stays in a separate inner liner, so you don’t have to fish foam out.

**How much fill should I buy to top it up?**
Most people find 5-10 pounds of additional shredded memory foam restores the original firmness. Check the dimensions before ordering — the 3ft model needs roughly 10 cubic feet of fill.

**Is this suitable for a child’s room?**
Yes, with a caveat. The fabric is soft and safe, but the fill can be a choking hazard if the cover is unzipped by curious little hands. Supervise young kids and keep the zipper closed.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely plush, affordable lounging chair and don’t mind the occasional fill top-up. It’s a solid value that punches above its price point on comfort and aesthetics.

**Skip it if** you need serious back support, have a house full of shedding pets, or want a zero-maintenance piece of furniture.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A strong buy for casual lounging, docked slightly for the settling fill and fabric maintenance. For the price, it’s hard to beat the comfort-to-cost ratio.
