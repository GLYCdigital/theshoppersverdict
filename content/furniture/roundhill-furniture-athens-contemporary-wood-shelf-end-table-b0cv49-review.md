## What You Actually Get

The box contains the table top, two side panels, one lower shelf, four tapered rubberwood legs, and the hardware packet. That's it — no tools included, so have a Phillips screwdriver handy. The piece measures 23.6 inches tall, 15.7 inches deep, and 11.8 inches wide. That's compact enough for a narrow entryway or beside a twin bed, but it's smaller than it looks in the product photos.

The finish is a dark espresso laminate over engineered wood, with solid rubberwood legs. It's not solid oak — don't kid yourself — but the construction feels stable, not wobbly.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The top surface holds a lamp, a coffee mug, and a phone without feeling crowded. The open lower shelf is the standout feature: it fits a standard 13x13 inch storage basket perfectly, or stacks of hardcover books. Because it's open rather than enclosed, it doesn't visually weigh down a small room.

Buyers consistently note the stability. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I was worried about wobble with such thin legs, but it's actually rock solid on carpet."* Another mentioned, *"The finish matches my darker furniture perfectly — no color mismatch like I've had with other brands."*

The espresso color is consistent across the piece, and the laminate resists water rings if you use coasters. The legs have small plastic floor glides that prevent scratching hardwood — a thoughtful touch at this price.

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty time. The depth is the biggest limitation. At 15.7 inches, a standard 16-inch deep sofa won't align flush, and oversized drinkware will overhang. If you need a true sofa table, measure first.

Assembly is straightforward but not tool-free. The pre-drilled holes align well, but the instruction sheet is minimalist — expect to figure out the leg orientation yourself. Most buyers report 15–20 minutes from box to standing.

The laminate surface, while attractive, is not scratch-proof. Dragging a ceramic mug or setting down a metal key bowl will leave marks. Use a pad or tray for anything abrasive.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You need a compact end table for a small apartment, a narrow bedside table, or a corner accent piece. The open shelf makes it practical for storage without visual clutter. It's also a solid choice for a first apartment or rental where you don't want to invest in heirloom furniture.

**Skip it if:** You need a full-size sofa table, want solid wood throughout (the top is laminate over MDF), or plan to put heavy items like a printer on the lower shelf — it's rated for lighter loads.
