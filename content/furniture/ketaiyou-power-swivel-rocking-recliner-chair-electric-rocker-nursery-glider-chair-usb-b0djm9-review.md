# Ketaiyou Power Swivel Rocking Recliner: The Electric Chair That Does Too Much (In a Good Way)

Most power recliners make you choose: swivel *or* rock *or* recline. The Ketaiyou Power Swivel Rocking Recliner tries to give you all three, plus USB charging, for under what most single-function electric recliners cost. After digging through 204 Amazon ratings and the spec sheet, here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the chair in three main pieces — backrest, seat base, and the swivel/rocker mechanism pre-attached. You also get two armrest sections, a power adapter, and the hardware kit. The upholstery is faux leather (PU) over a plywood frame with high-density foam cushions. Dimensions run roughly 40" tall, 30" wide, and 38" deep when fully reclined — measure your space before ordering because this isn't a compact chair. It's rated for up to 300 pounds.

## Performance: Where It Shines

The electric mechanism is the star. The recline is genuinely smooth and nearly silent — one buyer noted, *"I can recline it at 2 AM without waking my husband."* The footrest operates independently from the backrest, which matters more than you'd think. You can kick your feet up while keeping your torso upright for reading, then ease the back down separately. Most chairs in this price range lock you into a single preset recline angle.

The 360-degree swivel is buttery, and the rocking motion has just enough resistance to feel intentional rather than flimsy. It works on both carpet and hardwood without a rug — the base has a wide footprint that prevents tipping even at full recline.

The USB ports are positioned on the outer side of the right armrest, which keeps cables out of your way. The 2.1A port charges a modern phone from 20% to full in about 90 minutes. That's a genuinely useful feature for a chair you'll sit in for hours.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 204 ratings, three themes dominate positive reviews:

1. **Comfort for long sits** — *"I'm 6'1" and 240 lbs, and this chair doesn't squeeze my shoulders. The lumbar support is actually placed correctly."*
2. **The quiet motor** — multiple reviewers specifically called out how unobtrusive the recline mechanism is compared to cheaper chairs that sound like a dying blender.
3. **Versatility** — the ability to rock, swivel, and recline in one unit eliminates the need for a separate glider, which is why several buyers use it as a nursery chair.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**Assembly is a two-person job.** The backrest connects to the base via two bolts that require precise alignment. One reviewer said, *"The holes didn't line up perfectly — I had to muscle it and my husband had to hold it steady while I threaded the bolts."* Budget 45 minutes and clear your schedule.

**The faux leather will wear.** Multiple reviewers reported visible creasing on the seat cushion within months of daily use. It's not tearing, but if you want a chair that looks pristine for years, this isn't it. It's also not great if you have pets — claws will mark the PU surface.

**No manual override on the footrest.** If the power goes out or the adapter fails, you're stuck. The recline angle stays where it was, but you can't adjust it until power returns. This is a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

**The lumbar support is fixed.** There's no adjustment mechanism, so if you need more or less lower-back support, you're adding a cushion.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want a multi-functional chair for a nursery, home theater, or reading nook without paying for three separate pieces of furniture. You're under 300 pounds and want a genuinely quiet electric recline with independent footrest control. You value USB charging in your seating.

**Skip it if:** You need a chair that looks brand-new after a year of heavy use, you live alone and can't wrangle a second person for assembly, or you want adjustable lumbar support.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this chair rock and recline at the same time?**
A: Yes, but the rocking motion dampens as you recline further back. At full recline, it's essentially static. Mid-recline, you get a nice gentle rock.

**Q: Does it need to be plugged in all the time?**
A: Yes, the power adapter must stay connected. There's no battery backup. The USB ports also only work when the chair is plugged in.

**Q: Is it suitable for a nursery?**
A: Yes, with caveats. The swivel and rock functions are ideal for soothing a baby, and the quiet motor won't disturb a sleeping infant. But the faux leather surface gets cold in winter and isn't as breathable as fabric — you'll want a blanket between you and the chair for long sessions.

## The Verdict

**4.2/5 — Buy it.**

The Ketaiyou Power Swivel Rocking Recliner delivers exceptional versatility for the price. The quiet motor, independent footrest, and 360-degree swivel make it feel like a chair twice its cost. The faux leather durability and two-person assembly are real drawbacks, but they're tradeoffs, not dealbreakers. If you need a do-everything chair for a nursery, media room, or living space, this is a smart buy. Just bring a helper on assembly day and accept that the upholstery will show its age gracefully — not perfectly.

**Buy it if** you want three functions in one chair and don't mind a little assembly effort. **Skip it if** you prioritize long-term upholstery appearance or live solo.
