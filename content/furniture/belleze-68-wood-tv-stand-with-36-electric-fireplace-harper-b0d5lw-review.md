# BELLEZE 68" Wood TV Stand with 36" Electric Fireplace: The Real Deal or Just Pretty Flames?

Most TV stands with electric fireplaces fall into one of two traps: they're either all looks and zero durability, or they're built like a tank but look like a hospital waiting room. The BELLEZE Harp 68-inch model tries to split the difference. After digging through 225 ratings and the spec sheet, here's the honest picture.

## What You Actually Get

The box delivers the stand in flat-pack form — you'll find the 68" wide body, a 36" electric fireplace insert, glass panels for the front, and all the hardware. The unit measures roughly 68" W x 20" D x 24" H, which puts it in the sweet spot for TVs up to 65 inches. The wood is engineered hardwood with a walnut finish that looks more expensive than the price point suggests.

The fireplace unit itself is the headline feature. It's a 36-inch wide insert with realistic flame effects — 12 different color options (from classic orange to cool blue), adjustable brightness, and a 5,100 BTU heater that can run with or without heat. You get a remote control that actually works from across the room, which is rarer than it should be at this price.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Let's be direct: this is not solid oak furniture. It's engineered wood, and you need to know that going in. But it's *good* engineered wood — the kind with a smooth laminate finish that doesn't look like a cheap sticker wrap. The claimed weight capacity is 200 pounds on top, and several long-term reviewers confirm they've loaded 65-inch TVs plus soundbars and center speakers without any sagging.

The cable management is where this stand quietly earns its keep. As shown in the product image, there are cutouts in the back panel and open shelving on both sides of the fireplace unit. One buyer put it simply: *"Everything hides cleanly. No more rats' nest of cables behind my TV."* The two open side shelves are deep enough for A/V receivers or game consoles, though they're not enclosed — so if you're allergic to visible gear, factor that in.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The flame effect is the star. Multiple reviewers specifically mention the 12-color LED options, with one noting: *"The blue flame setting at low brightness makes the room feel like a high-end lounge."* The heat output also gets consistent thumbs-up for its intended use — warming a 12x12 room or taking the chill off a larger space without cranking the central furnace.

The walnut finish and clean lines earn frequent compliments too. *"Looks way more expensive than it was,"* is a recurring theme in the reviews. The glass front panels on the fireplace compartment give it a modern, built-in look that punches above its weight class.

## Where It Falls Short

Assembly is the biggest complaint, and it's legitimate. Plan for 90 minutes minimum with two people. The instructions are dense, and several reviewers mention that some pre-drilled holes don't perfectly align — you'll need patience and possibly a rubber mallet. One reviewer bluntly stated: *"The assembly took my husband and I almost two hours. The directions skip steps and assume you know what you're doing."*

The heater's reach is the second limitation. At 5,100 BTU, it's designed for zone heating, not whole-house warmth. If your living room opens into a kitchen or dining area, it'll take the edge off but won't replace your furnace. Also note: the fireplace unit is fixed — the flame panel doesn't swivel, so it's best viewed head-on.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Apartment dwellers** who need supplemental heat without installing anything permanent
- **Home theater setups** with a 55–65 inch TV and multiple components that need organized storage
- **Anyone who wants the ambiance of a fireplace** without the cleaning, venting, or gas line costs
- **Renters** — this is a freestanding unit, no wall modification required

Skip it if you're expecting heirloom furniture quality, if you need to heat a large open floor plan, or if you hate furniture assembly with a burning passion.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I run the heater without the flames?**
A: Yes — heat and flame operate independently. You can have warm air with no visual, or flames with no heat for year-round ambiance.

**Q: Will this hold a 65-inch TV?**
A: Yes, the top supports up to 200 lbs. A 65-inch TV plus a soundbar and center channel will sit comfortably. Just make sure your TV's feet are within the 68" width.

**Q: Does the fireplace require venting or special wiring?**
A: No. It's plug-and-play with a standard 120V outlet. No venting, no chimney, no gas line — that's the entire point of an electric insert.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely attractive, functional media console with a fireplace that adds ambiance and supplemental heat without breaking the bank. The 4.5-star rating from 225 buyers holds up — this is a solid value for the price.

**Skip it if** you're not prepared for a lengthy assembly session or you need primary heating for a large space.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The BELLEZE Harp does what it promises, looks good doing it, and only stumbles on assembly difficulty and heater range. For the price, that's a winning formula.
