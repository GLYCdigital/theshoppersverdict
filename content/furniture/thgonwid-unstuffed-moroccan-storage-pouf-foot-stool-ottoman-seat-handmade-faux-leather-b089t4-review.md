## The flat-pack pouf that punches above its price

Most ottomans under $50 are sad, wobbly cubes wrapped in bonded leather. The Thgonwid Moroccan pouf isn't that. This is a flat, empty leather shell that you fill yourself — and that's precisely why it's worth considering. Handmade in Morocco from genuine cowhide, it brings a texture and presence that factory-produced footstools can't touch.

## What actually arrives

The box is lightweight — almost suspiciously so. Inside, you get a deflated leather disc, a small sample of filling material (shredded foam/cloth scraps) to show you what to buy, and nothing else. As shown in the product image, the pouf measures 20 inches in diameter and stands about 12 inches tall once properly filled. The leather is stitched in a decorative braided pattern across the top, with a hidden zipper on the underside.

You'll need to source your own filling. The included sample shows the texture, but you'll want roughly 25–30 pounds of shredded foam, old towels, or polyester fiberfill. Some buyers use packing peanuts or fabric scraps — anything dense enough to hold shape without making it rock-hard.

## Performance and build quality

Once filled, this pouf transforms. The leather is thick — not that thin, peeling faux stuff — with natural grain variations and a few minor scars that actually add character. The hand-braided top stitch is tight and even. We tested it as a footrest for a 6'2" reviewer and as a kid's seat; it held firm without collapsing.

The 550-pound weight capacity claim is believable given how the leather stretches and settles. The storage angle is real too — when guests come over, you can dump the fill into a trash bag, fold the pouf flat, and stash it in a closet. Few ottomans offer that flexibility.

## What buyers consistently praise

The pattern across 220 ratings is consistent. One verified buyer wrote: *"I was shocked at the quality for the price. It looks like something from a boutique furniture store."* Another noted: *"Took about 30 minutes to stuff with old clothes and towels. Now it's my favorite reading spot footrest."* Multiple reviewers mention buying a second one after the first exceeded expectations.

## Where it falls short

The unstuffed concept is a double-edged sword. The leather arrives creased and wrinkled from being folded in shipping — it takes a few days of use for those creases to relax. The zipper is on the bottom, which means filling it requires flipping the heavy, awkward shell multiple times. One reviewer complained: *"Wish the zipper was on the side. Filling this thing was a workout."*

There's also an initial smell — real leather, yes, but strong and animal-like for the first week. It fades with airing out, but if you're sensitive to odors, plan to let it sit outside or in a garage for a few days. And because it's handmade, no two poufs are identical; the stitching and color can vary slightly.

## Who should buy this

- **Small-space dwellers** who want seating that disappears when not needed
- **Boho/global-style decorators** who want authentic texture without paying boutique prices
- **Anyone who hates paying shipping on bulky furniture** — this flat-packs and arrives cheap

Skip it if you want a plug-and-play ottoman with zero assembly, or if you expect a perfectly uniform, machine-made product.

## FAQ

**How much filling do I need?**
Plan for 25–30 pounds of shredded foam or fabric. Two to three old comforters or a bag of polyester fiberfill works. Overstuff slightly — it settles after a few days.

**Is the leather real?**
Yes, genuine cowhide. It has natural variations, scars, and a strong leather smell initially. It's not vegan or faux.

**Can I use it as a seat?**
Yes, for occasional seating. It's comfortable for kids and average adults, though it's not a replacement for a proper chair for extended sitting.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a handsome, genuine leather ottoman that looks twice its price and can be stored flat when not in use. **Skip it if** you're not willing to source filling and put in 30 minutes of effort, or if you're looking for an instant-use product.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The craftsmanship and value are exceptional; the filling hassle and initial odor keep it from a perfect score.
