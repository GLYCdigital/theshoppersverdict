# LOAKEKEL Night Stand Set 2 Review: Do You Actually Need LED Nightstands?

Most nightstands are boring. They hold a lamp, a book, and whatever dead gadget you forgot to plug in. The LOAKEKEL set of two tries to fix that with built-in LED lighting and a charging station. After digging through 220 ratings and the spec sheet, here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

This is a two-pack — you're getting two identical nightstands, not one. Each unit measures roughly 15.7" W x 15.7" D x 19.7" H, which puts them on the compact side. They're designed for smaller bedrooms, guest rooms, or apartments where floor space is tight.

Each nightstand includes:
- One open shelf (bottom) and one enclosed cabinet (top) with a soft-close hinge
- A USB-A port and USB-C port on the front edge
- Two AC outlets (110V) on the side
- An LED strip mounted under the top edge, with remote control
- The remote, a CR2032 battery, and all cam-lock hardware

The melamine-coated particleboard body comes in a matte finish that resists scratches and water marks. It's not solid wood — don't expect that at this price point — but it's sturdier than the flimsy laminated MDF you often find in this category.

## Real-World Performance

The LED lighting is the headline feature, and honestly, it's better than I expected. The strip runs across the back underside of the top surface, casting a soft glow against the wall. You get roughly 16 color options and 4 brightness levels via the remote. It's not smart-home compatible — no Alexa or Google Home integration — but as a standalone ambient light, it works well. The "warm white" setting is genuinely useful as a middle-of-the-night light that won't blind you.

The charging station is where this earns its keep. Two AC outlets and two USB ports per unit mean you can charge a phone, a tablet, and a lamp simultaneously. One buyer noted, *"I have my CPAP on one side and my phone charger on the other — no more fighting over the one outlet behind the bed."* That's the exact use case this solves.

Assembly is the tradeoff. The cam-lock system isn't difficult, but it's tedious. Plan for 30–45 minutes per unit. Several reviewers mentioned that the instructions are mostly diagrams and the holes don't always align perfectly — you'll need to wiggle things into place. A Phillips screwdriver is required; a power drill with a clutch helps.

## What Buyers Praise

The consistent themes in positive reviews are the lighting quality and the charging convenience. One verified purchase wrote, *"The LED light is surprisingly bright and the remote works from across the room. The nightstand itself looks more expensive than it is."* Another praised the soft-close doors, saying, *"I didn't expect soft-close at this price — it's a nice touch that keeps my bedroom quiet at night."*

The waterproof coating also gets repeated mention from people with kids or pets — spills wipe off without staining.

## Where It Falls Short

The LED remote requires line-of-sight to the receiver on the front of the unit. If the nightstand is partially behind your bed frame, you'll be leaning over to change colors. Not a dealbreaker, but annoying.

The cable management gap at the back is roughly 1.5 inches wide. Standard phone chargers pass through fine, but chunky laptop power bricks won't. You'll either leave the back panel off or accept a messy cord situation.

The particleboard construction means it won't survive a move unscathed. If you're a renter who relocates every few years, expect some corner chipping. Also, the enclosed cabinet has a fixed shelf — you can't adjust it to fit taller items like a tissue box or a stack of hardcovers.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy them if** you want a clean, integrated charging solution beside your bed without visible cable clutter. The LED lighting is a bonus that genuinely improves a dark bedroom, and the two-pack price per unit is reasonable.

**Skip them if** you need heavy-duty furniture that survives multiple moves, or if you want a nightstand large enough for a 10" tablet plus a lamp, a book, and a glass of water — the top surface is just 15.7" square, so it fills up fast.

## FAQ

**Q: Do the LED lights stay on after you turn off the unit?**
A: The LED strip is independent of the AC outlets and USB ports. You control it entirely via the remote — there's no switch on the nightstand itself. The lights do not stay on automatically after a power outage; you'll need to use the remote to turn them back on.

**Q: Can I use the charging ports while the LED is off?**
A: Yes. The charging functionality is completely separate from the lighting. You can charge devices with the LED off, which is the default state anyway.

**Q: How much weight can the top surface hold?**
A: The manufacturer doesn't publish a load rating, but from the construction, keep it under 30 lbs on top. A lamp, a phone, and a book are fine. Don't put a heavy monitor or a stack of hardcover encyclopedias on it.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The LOAKEKEL nightstand set delivers on its core promise: a tidy, illuminated, charging-ready bedside table. The LED lighting and integrated outlets genuinely improve the bedroom experience, and the two-pack price makes it a solid value. The assembly hassle, line-of-sight remote, and particleboard fragility are the predictable tradeoffs at this price point.

**Buy it if** you want an affordable, feature-rich nightstand set for a bedroom, guest room, or Airbnb rental. **Skip it if** you need furniture-grade durability or oversized surface area.

For most shoppers, this is a smart buy — just budget time for assembly and don't plan on moving it cross-country.
