**The 10-inch C table that slides exactly where you need it — if your sofa cooperates**

You know that dead zone between your couch arm and the wall? The one where a regular end table sticks out like a sore thumb and turns your walkway into an obstacle course? The Simplihome Brook C Table exists for that spot. This is a narrow, space-saving side table designed to tuck under furniture and over your lap, but at 10 inches wide, it's not for everyone. Here's the honest breakdown.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the table, a small Allen key, and not much else. Assembly takes about three minutes — you attach the tabletop to the C-shaped frame with four bolts, hand-tighten, and you're done. No drilling, no confusing diagrams, no leftover hardware. The whole unit weighs roughly 8 pounds, so it's easy to move between rooms but solid enough not to topple when you set a drink down.

The frame is welded steel with a white powder-coated finish. As shown in the product image, the C-profile is clean and minimal — no visible seams or rough edges. The tabletop measures approximately 10 inches deep by 12 inches wide, with a subtle lip that keeps items from sliding off when you bump it.

**Real-world performance**

The genius of a C table is the base slides fully under your sofa or chair, so the surface hovers directly over your lap. No leaning forward to reach your coffee. The Brook does this job well — the clearance under the C is about 24 inches, which fits most standard sofas and recliners.

The white finish is practical. Powder coating holds up better than painted metal; I've seen these tables take years of coffee rings and pet hair without rusting or peeling. Wipe with a damp cloth and it looks new. The steel frame doesn't wobble on flat floors, though on thick carpet you may notice slight instability when you push down on the edge.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Early feedback on this model highlights the size. One shopper noted: *"Finally a table that fits between my couch and the wall — I don't have to climb over it to get to the balcony."* Others appreciate the build for the price: *"Surprisingly sturdy for the cost. Doesn't feel cheap like other metal tables I've tried."*

**Where it falls short**

The 10-inch width is the headline feature, but it's also the biggest limitation. You can fit a mug and a phone, maybe a small book, but not a laptop or a full dinner plate. If you want to work from the couch, this isn't your table.

More critically, there's no height adjustment. The tabletop sits at about 24 inches. If your sofa arms are lower or higher than that, the table won't sit flush over your lap — it'll either hover too high or press awkwardly against the seat cushion. Measure before you buy.

**Who should buy this**

- **Apartment dwellers with narrow living rooms** — this is the table that makes a tight space livable
- **Anyone with a sectional** — the C-shape slides over the chaise portion perfectly
- **Bedroom use** — works as a bedside table in small rooms where a nightstand won't fit

**Skip it if:**

- You need a surface for actual meals or laptop work
- Your sofa arms are unusually high or low (more than 2 inches off standard)
- You want a table that doubles as display space for decor

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this hold a 55-inch TV?**  
No. This is for drinks and small items. Weight limit is roughly 30 pounds, but the 10-inch surface makes a TV impractical.

**Q: Does it scratch wood floors?**  
The base has small plastic floor glides that protect hard surfaces. They stay in place during normal use, though they can pop out if you drag the table roughly.

**Q: Can I use it outdoors?**  
Not recommended for prolonged outdoor exposure. The powder coating resists moisture, but the steel frame can rust if left in rain or high humidity.

**The Verdict**

The Simplihome Brook C Table solves a specific problem well: fitting a side table into a space where nothing else works. It's well-constructed, easy to assemble, and the white finish is practical. But the compact size and fixed height make it a niche purchase, not a universal solution.

**Buy it if** you have a tight clearance next to your sofa and want a sturdy, unobtrusive surface for drinks and remotes.

**Skip it if** you need a multi-purpose table or your furniture dimensions don't match the 24-inch height.

**Rating: 4.0/5** — a solid, purpose-built product that does exactly what it claims, with limitations you should know before buying.
