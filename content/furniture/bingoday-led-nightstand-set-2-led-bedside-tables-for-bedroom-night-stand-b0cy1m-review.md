**The short version:** The Bingoday LED Nightstand Set gives you two functional, good-looking bedside tables with genuinely useful lighting and charging — at a price point that undercuts most "smart furniture." At 4.3 stars from 215 ratings, it's not perfect, but for most buyers, the tradeoffs are worth it.

**What you actually get**

The box contains two identical nightstands, each measuring roughly 15.7" D x 15.7" W x 19.7" H. Each unit has one soft-close drawer, one open cubby shelf below, and a top surface that fits a standard lamp, phone, and water glass comfortably. The LED strips are pre-installed along the bottom edge of each unit, and the remote control operates both nightstands simultaneously.

You also get a power adapter for each table, USB-A and USB-C ports built into the side panel, and the hardware kit with cam locks and dowels. No tools needed beyond the included Allen wrench — though you'll want a screwdriver for the drawer slides.

**How it performs in real use**

The LED lighting is the headline feature, and it delivers. Sixteen colors plus a few brightness levels and a color-cycle mode create a soft under-glow that works well for a nightlight without being blinding. The remote works from across a standard bedroom, and the memory function keeps your last color setting after you power off.

The charging ports are a practical addition. Two USB-A ports and one USB-C per nightstand means both partners can charge phones overnight without a wall wart. The ports push standard 5V/2.1A, which charges most phones at a reasonable rate — not fast charging, but fine for overnight.

The drawer glides smoothly and the soft-close mechanism is a nice touch at this price. The open shelf below fits books, a tablet, or a small basket. The MDF construction is sturdy enough for daily use — several reviewers note it holds up well with lamps, phones, and the occasional coffee mug.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The lighting feature gets the most love. One reviewer wrote, *"The LED lights are the reason I bought these — they make the whole room feel different at night. My kids think it's a spaceship."* Another noted, *"Assembly was straightforward, maybe 25 minutes each. The instructions were clear."* Multiple reviewers mention the charging ports as a deciding factor: *"Having USB ports on both sides means we don't fight over the outlet behind the bed anymore."*

**Where it falls short**

The remote is the biggest annoyance. It's IR, not RF, so you need a direct line of sight to the sensor on the front of the nightstand. If your bed's footboard or a tall comforter blocks the sensor, you'll be reaching over to change colors. One reviewer put it plainly: *"The remote works fine if you aim it right, but I wish it were Bluetooth."*

Assembly is doable but not trivial. The LED wiring must be routed through pre-drilled channels during assembly — if you rush it, the light strips can kink or detach. Budget 30–45 minutes per nightstand if you're methodical. And like any MDF furniture, these won't survive a move well. They're solid for stationary use, but disassembly will likely damage the cam locks.

**Who should buy this**

- **Renters or dorm dwellers** who want a modern look without a permanent installation
- **Gamers or anyone who wants ambient RGB lighting** in the bedroom — the color options are surprisingly versatile
- **Couples who need bedside charging** — the USB ports on both units solve the outlet shortage problem
- **Anyone furnishing a guest bedroom on a budget** who wants a cohesive, contemporary set

**Skip it if** you need furniture that survives multiple moves, or if you can't tolerate occasional finicky remote control behavior.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do the LED lights stay on when the nightstand is off?**
A: No, the lights only work when the unit is powered on. There's no battery backup, so you'll need the power adapter plugged in. The memory function remembers your last color setting when you power back on.

**Q: Can I control each nightstand independently?**
A: The included remote controls both units simultaneously. There's no app and no per-unit control. If you want different colors on each side, that's not possible with the stock remote.

**Q: How much weight can the top surface hold?**
A: The MDF top is rated for roughly 30 pounds. A lamp, phone, and a few books are fine. Avoid sitting on the top or placing heavy electronics like a large desktop monitor.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a functional, good-looking nightstand set with lighting and charging that punches above its price class. The LED ambience, USB ports, and decent storage make this a strong value for most bedrooms.

**Skip it if** you need furniture that survives a move, or if IR remote limitations would drive you crazy.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid value with two meaningful caveats (remote line-of-sight and assembly care). For the price, it's hard to beat.
