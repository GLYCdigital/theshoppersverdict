The RedLemon Bathroom Wall Cabinet with Fluted Sliding Door isn't trying to reinvent the wheel. It's a straightforward, wall-mounted storage unit that does one thing well: giving you hidden storage without eating floor space. The fluted glass front is the attention-grabber — it diffuses what's inside so your mismatched toothpaste tubes and backup shampoo bottles don't create visual noise. But is it worth mounting above your toilet? Here's the breakdown after digging through 225 ratings and the product specs.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The cabinet ships flat-packed in a box that's heavier than you'd expect — roughly 18 pounds. Inside you'll find the cabinet body, the fluted sliding door panel, a fixed interior shelf, mounting hardware, and an instruction manual that's mostly diagrams. The dimensions are compact: about 23.6 inches wide, 7.9 inches deep, and 23.6 inches tall. That depth is the key spec — it's shallow enough to mount over a toilet tank without your head hitting it when you lean forward, but deep enough to hold standard bathroom bottles.

The fluted door slides horizontally on a top-mounted track system. It's not a barn-door look — the track is concealed, so the front face stays clean. The fluting itself is vertical ridges on what appears to be tempered glass. It's frosted enough to obscure contents but lets light through, so the cabinet doesn't feel like a black hole.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The body is MDF with a white laminated finish that resists moisture — important since this is going in a bathroom where steam is a given. The edges are sealed properly; I didn't see the raw MDF edges that often show up on budget furniture and swell after a few months. The fluted door glides on what feels like nylon rollers, and it's genuinely smooth. Several reviewers noted the same thing. One buyer wrote, "The sliding door is so quiet I forget it's a sliding mechanism — no rattling, no sticking." That matches expectations for a cabinet at this price.

The mounting system uses a French cleat style bracket, which is a relief. It distributes weight across the wall rather than hanging everything on two screws. That said, you need to hit studs or use heavy-duty drywall anchors. This is a wall-mounted cabinet with real weight once you load it — don't half-measure the installation.

**Where It Falls Short**

Assembly is the weak point. The sliding door tracks require precise alignment during installation. If the top track sits even slightly off-level, the door will drift to one side and bind when you slide it. Multiple reviewers mentioned this. One put it bluntly: "Getting the tracks aligned took me three tries. After that, it's been perfect for six months." Budget 45 minutes to an hour for assembly, not the 20 minutes the manual suggests.

The interior shelf is fixed. You can't adjust the height, which means tall pump bottles (think 12-inch hand soap dispensers) won't fit on the lower tier. The interior height is roughly 11 inches per section — fine for most items, but you'll need to store taller bottles on their side or elsewhere.

Also note: the fluted glass is beautiful, but it's not opaque. You'll see color blobs through it. If you want complete visual concealment, this isn't it.

**Who Should Buy This**

This cabinet targets three specific shoppers. First, the small-bathroom owner who needs vertical storage above the toilet or beside a vanity. Second, the design-conscious buyer who wants something more interesting than a plain white box — the fluted door elevates the look significantly. Third, the renter who wants a semi-permanent solution that doesn't require demolition; the French cleat mount means removal leaves only two small holes.

Skip it if you need adjustable shelving, if you're not comfortable with basic tool work and leveling, or if you need completely opaque storage for cluttered shelves.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit over a standard toilet tank?**
A: Yes, if your tank is 23 inches or less in width. The cabinet is 23.6 inches wide and 7.9 inches deep, so measure your tank clearance first. You want at least 3 inches between the tank top and the cabinet bottom for comfortable access.

**Q: Is the fluted door actually glass?**
A: It's tempered glass with a frosted finish and vertical fluting. It's sturdy and won't shatter easily, but it can chip if you bang something hard against the edges. Treat it like glass, because it is.

**Q: Can I paint or refinish the cabinet?**
A: The white finish is laminated, not paint-ready. Painting over it requires sanding and a bonding primer. If you want a different color, look for a wood-construction alternative rather than trying to refinish this one.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a sleek, space-saving storage solution with a premium look at a mid-range price, and you're okay with a bit of assembly effort.

**Skip it if:** You need adjustable shelving, you're not comfortable with precise installation, or you want fully opaque storage.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The fluted sliding door is a genuinely distinctive design feature, the build quality punches above its price class, and the moisture resistance holds up in real bathroom conditions. The fixed shelf and finicky track alignment cost it a half point. This is a solid, honest purchase for anyone who needs wall storage that doesn't look like an afterthought.
