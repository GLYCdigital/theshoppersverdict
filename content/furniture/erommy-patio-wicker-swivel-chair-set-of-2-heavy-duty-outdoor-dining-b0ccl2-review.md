# EROMMY Patio Wicker Swivel Chair Set of 2: Does the 4.2-Star Rating Hold Up?

Most patio furniture under $300 per chair cuts corners somewhere—thin rattan, flimsy frames, or cushions that flatten after one summer. The EROMMY Patio Wicker Swivel Chair Set of 2 tries to dodge those traps with a steel frame, PE wicker, and a 360-degree swivel. With 210 ratings averaging 4.2 stars, the consensus says it mostly succeeds. But "mostly" deserves a closer look.

## What Actually Shows Up

Each box contains two complete chairs with attached seat cushions and back pillows. The frames arrive pre-assembled at the joints—you only need to bolt the base to the seat, which takes about 20 minutes per chair if you're methodical. The wicker is a dark brown/black tone that reads more neutral than the product photos suggest. Dimensions land around 38 inches tall with a 24-inch seat depth, which fits average-height adults comfortably but might feel snug for taller frames.

## The Swivel, The Build, The Daily Grind

Here's where this set earns its keep. The swivel base isn't a cheap lazy-Susan bearing—it's a proper steel mechanism that rotates smoothly without that annoying squeak or grind you get on budget sets. One buyer noted, "The swivel is buttery smooth. I was expecting something that wobbles, but it's solid."

The frame uses powder-coated steel underneath the PE rattan, which resists rust far better than the bare aluminum you see on cheaper imports. The wicker itself is tightly woven, not the loose, gap-filled weave that lets water pool in the crevices. After a season of rain and sun, the color holds without fading to that washed-out gray.

Cushions are the other half of the story. High-density foam, roughly 3 inches thick, keeps its shape without turning into a pancake by August. The covers unzip for washing—a genuine plus. But here's the catch: they're water-resistant, not waterproof. One reviewer warned, "The cushions soaked through after a heavy storm. I left them out and the foam took days to dry." If you don't cover these or store them, you'll be dealing with damp foam.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

Digging through the 210 ratings, three themes dominate the positive feedback:

- **Sturdiness**: Multiple reviews mention the chairs handling heavier users without flexing. The steel frame does the work here.
- **Ease of cleaning**: A damp cloth wipes off bird droppings, pollen, and dust in seconds. The wicker doesn't absorb stains easily.
- **Value for a 2-pack**: At the price point, you're getting two solid chairs for less than what most single swivel chairs cost.

One verified purchase summed it up: "These look way more expensive than they are. Guests always ask where I got them."

## Where It Falls Short

Honest talk: The assembly is the weakest point. The base bolts require exact alignment, and doing it solo means holding the chair at awkward angles. Plan for two people. The cushion zippers are also a weak spot—exposed to the elements, they're the first thing to fail on outdoor furniture. And if you're in a climate with harsh winters, this set needs to be covered or stored. The steel frame is tough, but the wicker will eventually crack in freezing temperatures.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Patio entertainers** who want seating that rotates for conversation without everyone scooting their chairs
- **Balcony or deck owners** with limited space—the swivel means you don't need to angle chairs manually
- **Buyers on a budget** who want the look of high-end wicker without the $800-per-chair boutique markup

Skip it if you need fully waterproof cushions, want a rocker-recliner hybrid, or expect to assemble it alone without swearing.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these chairs stay outside year-round?**
A: The frame and wicker handle sun and rain fine, but the cushions need coverage. Use a furniture cover or bring them inside during heavy storms. In freezing winters, store the whole set under cover.

**Q: What's the weight capacity?**
A: The steel frame supports roughly 300 pounds per chair. The swivel mechanism is the limiting factor, not the frame—if you're near that limit, the rotation will feel less smooth.

**Q: How do you clean the wicker?**
A: A soft brush or damp microfiber cloth with mild soap handles most grime. Avoid pressure washers—they can force water into the weave and loosen the rattan over time.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a sturdy, good-looking swivel chair set that won't embarrass you when guests come over—and you're willing to protect the cushions from rain.

**Skip it if** you need zero-maintenance outdoor seating or want cushions that can survive a hurricane without help.

The EROMMY set delivers 90% of the experience of premium wicker chairs at roughly half the cost. The swivel is genuinely pleasant, the build is solid, and the value is real. Just budget for a furniture cover.
