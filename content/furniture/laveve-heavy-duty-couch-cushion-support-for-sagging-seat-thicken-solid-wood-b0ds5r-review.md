**If your sofa has turned into a human hammock, the Laveve Heavy Duty Couch Cushion Support is the $30-ish fix you've been searching for. But it's not magic — here's what it actually does and doesn't do.**

You know the feeling. You sit down, and instead of a supportive seat, you sink six inches into what used to be a couch. The frame's fine, the cushions are fine — but the webbing or springs underneath have given up. Replacing the sofa costs hundreds. Replacing just the cushions costs almost as much. The Laveve support boards slide under your existing cushions and give them something solid to rest on. Simple concept, solid execution.

## What You Actually Get

The product is a set of solid wood boards — not the hollow particleboard you see in cheaper options. These are thick, honestly built planks designed to span the width of your seat base. You position them directly on the sagging webbing or springs, then put your cushions back on top. No tools, no disassembly, no wrestling with the sofa frame.

The "thicken" in the product name isn't marketing fluff. The boards have real heft to them, which matters because the entire point is to distribute your weight evenly across a flat, rigid surface instead of letting it concentrate where the webbing has stretched out.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the Laveve earns its 4.0 rating. On a standard 3-seat sofa with sagging center support, these boards eliminate the "bottoming out" feeling immediately. The difference is night and day — you go from sitting on a trampoline to sitting on a stable, level surface.

The key is measurement. The boards need to fit your seat width properly. Too narrow and they'll tilt. Too wide and they won't sit flat. Measure the interior width of your sofa frame before ordering, not the cushion width — that's the mistake most people make.

## Where It Falls Short

**It won't fix a broken frame.** If your sofa's structural frame itself is cracked or the springs have completely detached, these boards are a band-aid on a bullet wound. They only address sagging webbing or tired springs that still have some tension left.

**No non-slip backing.** On fabric upholstery, the boards stay put. On leather or vinyl, they can shift slightly when you sit down or stand up. A few strips of double-sided tape or a non-slip rug pad underneath solves this, but it's annoying that it's not included.

**Visible edges on deep cushions.** If your cushions are thick and your sofa is deep, the front edge of the board might be visible or create a slight ridge where the cushion meets the frame. Positioning them slightly back from the front edge helps, but it's a minor aesthetic compromise.

## Who Should Buy This

**Skip it if** your sofa frame is visibly cracked, if the springs are poking through, or if you're expecting these to make a 15-year-old sofa feel brand new. They won't.

## FAQ

**Q: Will these work on a sectional or L-shaped couch?**
A: Yes, as long as you measure the seat width of each section. You may need multiple sets for a large sectional.

**Q: Can I use these on a sofa with removable cushion covers?**
A: Yes — actually easier. Just remove the covers, place the boards, and replace. No pinching or bunching.

**Q: Do they work on recliners?**
A: Only on the stationary seat portion. They'll interfere with the reclining mechanism, so don't place them there.
