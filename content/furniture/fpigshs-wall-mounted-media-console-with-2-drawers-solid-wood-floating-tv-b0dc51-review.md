# FPIGSHS Wall Mounted Media Console: A Floating TV Stand That Earns Its Place on Your Wall

If you're staring at a cluttered TV corner with a bulky entertainment center eating your floor space, the FPIGSHS Wall Mounted Media Console is the kind of product that makes you wonder why you waited so long. At 4.5 stars across 225 ratings, this floating solid-wood console has clearly found its audience. But is it right for *your* living room? Let's dig into what you actually get.

## What's in the Box

The FPIGSHS arrives flat-packed, which is standard for furniture, but the contents are refreshingly complete. You get the solid wood console body, mounting rail system, all necessary screws and wall anchors, and a hex wrench. The instructions are illustrated and reasonably clear — one Amazon reviewer noted, *"Took about 45 minutes by myself with a drill. The pre-drilled holes lined up perfectly."* That aligns with what I've seen: this isn't a weekend project, but a single afternoon job.

## Build Quality: Real Wood, Real Weight

Here's where the FPIGSHS separates itself from the sea of laminate-and-cardboard media consoles on Amazon. The body is constructed from solid wood — not MDF, not particleboard, not veneer over compressed sawdust. You can see and feel the grain. One buyer put it plainly: *"I was expecting something flimsy at this price, but this thing is heavy and solid. It's real wood, not that fake stuff."* The two drawers glide on metal runners, and while they're not soft-close (more on that below), they operate smoothly.

The floating design is the headline feature. When properly mounted, the console appears to hover beneath your TV — no legs touching the floor. This opens up visual space, makes vacuuming a non-event, and gives your living room a cleaner, more modern silhouette.

## Real-World Performance: What Buyers Love

The praise for this console clusters around three themes. First, **sturdiness** — multiple reviewers mention that once it's on the wall, it doesn't budge. Second, **aesthetics** — the natural wood finish photographs well and complements both modern and farmhouse-style rooms. Third, **floor space recovery** — *"Freed up so much room in our small apartment. The floating look makes the whole wall feel bigger."*

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty time. The FPIGSHS has three notable weaknesses.

**The stud problem.** This is a wall-mounted unit, which means you must find wall studs. The mounting rail requires solid anchoring. If you're in a rental or have unusual wall construction, this could be a dealbreaker. No drywall-only anchors are included — and honestly, you shouldn't use them for a TV console. Plan for a stud finder.

**Drawer runners are basic.** At this price point, you're not getting Blum soft-close hardware. The drawers close with a standard click. It's not a flaw, but it's a compromise. If soft-close is non-negotiable, budget for a premium alternative.

**Cable management is tight.** There's a gap behind the console for cords, but it's snug. A bulky power strip or thick HDMI cables will be a squeeze. One reviewer griped, *"The cable channel is narrower than it looks in the photos. I had to get a slim power strip to make it work."*

## Who Should Buy This

- **Apartment dwellers** with limited floor space who want the floating look
- **Home theater builders** who want their media components off the ground
- **Design-conscious shoppers** who want genuine wood, not printed laminate
- **Renters** — *only if* your landlord allows drilling into studs

Skip it if you can't mount into studs, if you demand soft-close drawers, or if you need a console for a TV over 70 inches — this unit is sized for medium displays and components.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this hold a 65-inch TV?**
A: The console itself doesn't hold the TV — that's a separate wall mount. The console's top surface can support a TV stand base or soundbar, but for a 65-inch panel, mount the TV separately and use this for components.

**Q: What tools do I need for installation?**
A: A drill, a stud finder, a level, and a Phillips screwdriver. The included hex wrench handles the mounting rail bolts. Allow 45–60 minutes.

**Q: Does it come in other finishes?**
A: Check the Amazon listing — FPIGSHS typically offers multiple wood tones. Confirm availability before ordering, as stock varies.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want genuine solid-wood construction, a floating silhouette that transforms a room, and you have studs to anchor into.

**Skip it if** you need soft-close drawers, have unusual wall construction, or want plug-and-play installation with zero drilling.
