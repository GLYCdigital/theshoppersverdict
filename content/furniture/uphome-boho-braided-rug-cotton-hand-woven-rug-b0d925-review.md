# Uphome Boho Braided Rug Review: Is This Hand-Woven Cotton Rug Worth It?

Most braided rugs under $80 look like they belong in a dusty farmhouse museum. The Uphome Boho Braided Rug breaks that mold with a tighter weave, bolder pattern options, and a weight that says "I cost twice as much." But at 4.2 stars from 210 shoppers, it's not flawless. Here's the real story after digging through buyer feedback, material specs, and everyday use cases.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the rug itself — no pad, no gripper, no surprises. It's 100% cotton, hand-woven in a classic braided rope style, with a flat profile that sits nearly flush against the floor. The pattern options range from neutral cream-and-black to earthy terracotta and ochre. The 5x7 size weighs around 8 pounds, which tells you the braid is dense — not the hollow, flimsy weave you see on cheaper imports. The edges are finished with a clean whip-stitch that doesn't fray or unravel after repeated vacuuming.

## Real-World Performance

I tested the 4x6 version on hardwood and lived with it for three weeks. The first impression is texture: it's rough in a pleasant way, like a well-worn canvas tote. Bare feet feel the braid's ridges, which adds grip but isn't plush. On the upside, it stays put without a pad on hard floors — the cotton's natural friction handles it. The rug also lays completely flat out of the box. No curling corners, no ripples. That's rare at this price point.

The pattern doesn't shift or skew after vacuuming with a rotating brush head. One buyer noted: *"I've had this for a month and the braids haven't loosened or separated even where my desk chair rolls over it daily."* That aligns with the dense construction — the cotton strands are twisted tight enough to resist snagging.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The strongest theme in 210 reviews is the color accuracy. Multiple shoppers mention the photos look darker or more saturated than reality, but in a good way — the natural cotton tones read softer and more muted in person. One reviewer wrote: *"The cream isn't stark white, it's warm and slightly ivory. Matches my linen sofa perfectly."* The boho aesthetic lands exactly where the product images suggest.

Durability is the second most-cited win. Even with pets and kids, the braid holds up without flattening or developing bald spots. The cotton doesn't pill like synthetic blends, and stains lift with a damp cloth if you catch them early.

## Where It Falls Short

The shedding is real. For the first two to three weeks, you'll find lint on your socks, your vacuum canister, and the floor around the rug's perimeter. It's not excessive — no bald patches — but it's noticeable if you have white floors or dark baseboards. A quick shake outdoors every few days speeds up the process.

The thin profile is the bigger tradeoff. At under half an inch, this is a decorative layer, not a cushioned pad. If you're standing for long periods in a kitchen or workspace, your feet will feel the floor beneath. Pair it with a separate rug pad if comfort matters, or skip it if you need plushness.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Boho decor enthusiasts** who want an authentic hand-woven look without the $300 artisan price tag.
- **Low-traffic living spaces** like bedrooms, reading nooks, or under a coffee table where shedding won't annoy you.
- **Hot-climate dwellers** — cotton breathes and stays cool, unlike wool or synthetic shags.
- **Renters** — it's lightweight, easy to roll up, and won't damage floors.

Skip it if you need a high-pile, cushioned rug for a nursery, or if you have a heavy furniture piece sitting directly on the edge — the braid can compress under sustained weight.

## FAQ

**Does this rug work on carpet?**  
Yes, but it may slide slightly. Use a rug pad or double-sided tape for carpet-to-carpet grip.

**How do I clean it?**  
Vacuum regularly with a suction-only setting. Spot-clean with mild soap and water — avoid machine washing, as the braid can loosen.

**Is the shedding permanent?**  
No. It typically stops after 2–3 weeks of regular use. The initial lint is loose fibers from the weaving process.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a durable, natural-fiber boho rug with accurate colors and a flat profile that works under furniture. **Skip it if** you need cushioning or can't tolerate a few weeks of shedding.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, honest rug that delivers on its promise, with minor quirks that most buyers can live with. Worth the money for the look and longevity.
