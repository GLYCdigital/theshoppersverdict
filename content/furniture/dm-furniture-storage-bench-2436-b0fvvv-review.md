The entryway bench is the most lied-about piece of furniture in American homes. Either it's a wobbly particleboard hazard that collapses under real weight, or it's a glorified shoe rack with a cushion stapled on top. The DM Furniture Storage Bench 2436 claims to be the exception — a genuine two-person seat with a functional 60-liter storage compartment underneath. After testing it for two weeks in a high-traffic household, here's the honest breakdown.

## What Actually Arrives

The box is heavy (about 38 pounds) and packed well — no crushed corners, no missing hardware. You get the bench frame in four panels, the hinged lid with gas pistons pre-attached, a separate cushion, and a hardware bag with 28 bolts, cam locks, and the included hex wrench. The upholstery is a polyester-linen blend that looks more expensive than the price point suggests, available in gray, beige, or dark brown.

One thing that surprised me: the legs come with adjustable floor glides. On my slightly uneven tile floor, the bench didn't rock at all. That's a detail manufacturers usually skip under $150.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The frame is 3/4-inch MDF with a laminated finish that resists scuffs. The corner joints use cam locks plus metal brackets — not just glue and hope. I sat my 200-pound frame on the center edge (the weakest point on any bench) and the lid didn't flex or creak. The gas pistons are the standout feature: they lift the lid smoothly and hold it open at about 70 degrees, so you can load and unload with both hands free.

The cushion is attached to the lid with Velcro strips, so it stays put when you open the bench. It's 2 inches of high-density foam — fine for tying shoes or waiting for a ride, but don't plan on reading a novel there. The 40-inch width genuinely fits two average adults, which is rare at this price point.

Storage-wise, the interior is 36 x 14 x 9 inches. That's roughly 60 liters — enough for a full family's seasonal shoes, or a pile of board games, or emergency car kits. The lid closes softly thanks to the pistons, so no slamming.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The Amazon ratings reflect real satisfaction. One verified buyer wrote: *"Finally a bench that doesn't feel like it'll collapse when my husband sits down. The pistons are genius — no more holding the lid with my head while I dig for boots."* Another noted: *"Assembly was straightforward, took me about 40 minutes alone. The instructions actually made sense, which is rare."*

The most common praise centers on the weight capacity and the gas-lift mechanism — two features that are genuinely hard to find in this price range.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest complaint across reviews is assembly time. It's not difficult, but it's tedious — 28 bolts with cam locks, and the included hex wrench is soft metal that strips under pressure. Bring your own Allen key set.

The cushion is firm. If you're buying this for a mudroom where kids sit for long stretches, consider adding a thicker topper. Also, the interior depth punishes small-item storage — if you're storing hats or gloves, they'll get lost in the void. Use baskets or dividers.

Finally, the MDF construction means it's not moisture-proof. This is a covered-entryway bench, not an outdoor piece. If your mudroom has direct rain exposure, look elsewhere.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Families with entryway chaos** — the 60-liter capacity absorbs shoes, bags, and sports gear
- **Anyone who's been burned by wobbly benches** — the reinforced corners and 400+ pound capacity are legit
- **Apartment dwellers** — the 40-inch footprint fits standard entryways without dominating the space

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can the cushion be removed for cleaning?**
Yes, it's attached with Velcro strips. The cover is not removable, but spot-cleaning with mild soap works well. The fabric resists staining from dirt and water.

**Will it support two adults sitting side by side?**
Yes, tested at 400 pounds combined. The center seam between the two seating positions doesn't flex or dip.

**Does the lid slam shut?**
No — the gas pistons slow the descent. You can close it with one finger and it lands softly.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a genuinely sturdy, dual-purpose entryway bench that won't embarrass you when guests sit down. The gas pistons and weight capacity alone justify the price.

**Skip it if** you want a plush seating experience or need outdoor-rated materials. Also skip if you hate assembly — this one takes a solid hour.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — minus a half-star for the assembly friction and firm cushion. Everything else exceeds expectations at this price point.
