## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest part. **Jute sheds.** For the first month, you'll see fibers accumulating along baseboards and under furniture. It's not defective — it's the nature of natural fiber. Vacuuming regularly (without the beater bar, which can pull the weave) minimizes this, but it won't disappear entirely.

The texture is also rough. If you're planning to walk barefoot across this rug daily, reconsider. It's a "shoes-on" rug, not a "curl up and read" rug. Several reviewers mentioned this tradeoff explicitly, with one noting, "Beautiful but scratchy — I had to add a soft pad underneath for comfort."

Sizing runs slightly small. The 5x5 round measures closer to 4'10" in practice, so measure your space and size up if you're borderline.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this rug need a pad underneath?**
A: Not for staying put — the weight handles that. But a felt pad adds cushioning and reduces the rough texture if you're wearing socks or slippers.

**Q: How do I clean jute?**
A: Vacuum regularly with suction only. Spot-clean spills immediately with a damp cloth — jute stains easily and absorbs liquids fast. Never steam-clean or use wet extraction.

**Q: Will the color fade in sunlight?**
A: Natural fibers can lighten with prolonged direct sun. Rotate the rug every few months to keep fading even.
