**FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Ask**

**Can these hold a microwave or small appliance?**
Yes, if you hit studs. The bracket system supports roughly 30-40 pounds per shelf when properly anchored. A compact microwave around 25 pounds is fine. Heavier appliances over 40 pounds? Look elsewhere.

**Are they real wood or MDF?**
MDF with a laminated wood-grain finish. It's heavier and denser than particle board, but it's not solid hardwood. The finish looks convincing in person, but don't expect premium timber quality.

**What's the wall gap behind the shelf?**
About 0.4 inches. If you're mounting them on textured walls, the gap will be slightly more noticeable. On smooth drywall, it looks tight and professional.
