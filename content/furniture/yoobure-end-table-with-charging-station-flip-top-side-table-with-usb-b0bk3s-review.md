**Skip the clutter. This end table hides your charging cables and puts the power where you need it — but let's be honest about what you're really getting.**

As shown in the product image, the Yoobure End Table is a compact side table with a clever party trick: the top flips open to reveal a built-in charging station. At 4.6 stars from 229 ratings, it's earned solid credibility. Here's what I found after digging through the feedback and testing the specs.

## What's in the box

You get the table top, four legs, the charging module (pre-installed in the top), and all necessary hardware. Assembly takes about five minutes — one reviewer noted, "I put it together while my coffee was still hot." The legs screw in with a simple Allen wrench that's included. No surprises, no missing parts, no cursing.

The dimensions are roughly 16 inches square and 21 inches tall — small enough for tight corners but substantial enough to hold a drink and a book. The tempered glass top is genuine glass, not acrylic, which matters if you're setting down hot mugs or heavy objects.

## Real-world performance

The charging station is the headline feature. You get one AC outlet and two USB-A ports built into the table's interior. The flip-top design means you can close it and hide the mess when guests come over — that's genuinely smart design.

But here's the catch: the power cord is only about 4 feet long. You'll need the table within reach of an outlet, and if you're using the flip-top closed, you're routing the charging cables through the small side cutout. It works, but you have to plan your furniture placement around it.

The interior storage compartment is shallow — maybe 4 inches deep. It's perfect for remote controls, charging cables, or a book, but don't expect to store tall items. One buyer put it plainly: "Great for hiding the clutter, but my tablet doesn't fit standing up."

## What buyers consistently praise

The build quality gets frequent shout-outs. Multiple reviewers mentioned the table feels solid, not wobbly — critical for a piece with a flip-top mechanism that gets opened and closed daily. One verified purchase wrote, "I was skeptical about the flip top holding up, but it's been six months and it still works perfectly."

The charging speed matters too. The USB ports deliver adequate power for phones and e-readers, and the AC outlet handles laptop chargers without tripping. As one reviewer put it, "Finally a side table that actually charges my phone instead of just holding my coffee."

## Where it falls short

The glass top is a magnet for fingerprints and dust. You'll be wiping it down weekly to keep it looking sharp. The finish is also on the lighter side — it's not a heavy-duty lacquer, so be careful with abrasive cleaners.

The charging cable length is the biggest practical limitation. If you're planning to put this next to a bed or sofa that's far from an outlet, measure first. Extension cords can work, but they defeat the purpose of a clean, cord-free look.

## Who should buy this

- **Apartment dwellers** with limited space who need a multi-functional piece
- **Parents** who want to keep charging cables away from toddlers
- **Anyone** who's tired of tripping over cords and wants a clean, organized living space

Skip it if you need a large storage surface or if your outlets are more than 4 feet from where the table will sit.
