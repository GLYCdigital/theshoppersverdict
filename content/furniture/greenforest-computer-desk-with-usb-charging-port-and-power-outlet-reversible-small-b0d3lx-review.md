Let's be honest: most desks under $100 are flimsy pressboard nightmares that wobble the moment you rest your elbows. The GreenForest Computer Desk with USB Charging Port and Power Outlet tries to be different — and for the most part, it succeeds. With a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings, this compact workstation has clearly found its audience. Here's the real story after digging through customer feedback and scrutinizing the specs.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the desktop (a laminated particleboard panel), a steel frame, a reversible shelf, a monitor stand, and the star of the show: a power module with two USB ports, one USB-C port, and a standard 3-prong AC outlet. The unit measures 40 inches wide by 20 inches deep — small enough for a dorm room or apartment nook, but roomier than the typical 32-inch "compact" desks flooding the market. The included monitor stand raises your screen about 3 inches, which helps if you're working on a laptop and want it at eye level.

**Performance and build quality**

The frame is the standout here. It's welded steel with a matte finish, not the hollow tubing you see on cheaper options. The crossbars underneath add genuine rigidity — several buyers noted they could lean on it without the desk flexing. One reviewer put it plainly: "I was expecting it to wobble like my last desk, but this thing is solid. I've got a 27-inch monitor, a laptop, and a printer on it, and it doesn't budge."

The power module is genuinely useful. It's mounted on the right side (or left, depending on how you assemble it — more on that below), and the 6-foot cord reaches most wall outlets. The USB-C port delivers 15W, which charges a phone at a reasonable pace, though it won't fast-charge a modern flagship. The AC outlet is a standard 2-prong, so three-prong plugs need an adapter. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The reversible shelf is the feature people mention most. You can mount it on either side, and it's sized to hold a printer, a stack of books, or a desktop tower. One buyer wrote: "I built mine with the shelf on the left and the power on the right — it fits perfectly in my corner. Feels custom."

The adjustable leveling feet also get consistent shoutouts. If you've ever owned a desk that rocks because your floor is slightly uneven, you'll appreciate the twist-to-adjust feet. They're a small touch that makes a big difference.

**Where it falls short**

Assembly is the most common complaint. It's not difficult, but it's tedious — roughly 45 minutes with two people, or close to an hour solo. The instructions are mostly diagrams, and the included Allen wrench is serviceable but slow. If you've built IKEA furniture before, you'll manage. If you haven't, budget a full evening.

The cable management tray is my biggest gripe. It's a shallow metal channel that runs along the back, but it's only about 1.5 inches deep. Thick power adapters (like laptop chargers) won't fit underneath, so you'll end up resting them on top of the tray or zip-tying them to the frame. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's not the clean solution the marketing implies.

Finally, the desktop surface is a fingerprint magnet. On the darker finish options, smudges from hands and drinks show up almost immediately. A quick wipe with a microfiber cloth fixes it, but it's something to know before you buy.

**Who should buy this**

This desk is ideal for three specific buyers: students in dorms or small apartments who need a compact, functional workspace; remote workers with limited square footage who want a dedicated setup without a full-size desk; and anyone who's tired of crawling under furniture to plug in a charger. If you're a gamer with a triple-monitor setup or a heavy-duty desktop tower, you'll want something larger and sturdier — this isn't that desk.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Is the power outlet grounded?**
No, it's a 2-prong outlet, not a 3-prong grounded one. It's fine for phone chargers, lamps, and most small electronics, but don't plug in anything high-draw like a space heater or a gaming PC.

**Will it fit a 27-inch monitor on the stand?**
Yes, the monitor stand is 20 inches wide and supports up to 40 pounds. A 27-inch monitor fits comfortably, though a 32-inch ultrawide will overhang slightly.

**Can I mount the power module on the left side?**
Yes, the power module and shelf are both reversible. You can configure it either way during assembly, which is a thoughtful design touch.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need a solid, compact desk with integrated charging, you're working with limited space, and you don't mind spending 45 minutes with an Allen wrench.

**Skip it if:** You want a large work surface, you need a grounded outlet, or you're looking for a zero-assembly option.

The GreenForest Computer Desk earns its 4.5-star rating. It's not perfect — the cable tray is shallow, assembly takes patience, and the surface smudges easily — but for the price, the combination of a sturdy frame, reversible layout, and genuinely useful power module makes it one of the better value desks in its class. It's a smart buy for anyone who needs a functional workspace without sacrificing floor space.
