Most end tables lie to you. They call themselves "large" when they can barely hold a coffee mug and a remote. The Joaxswe Farmhouse End Table doesn't do that. At 24 inches across, this thing actually earns the description — and the built-in charging station makes it genuinely useful rather than just another place to stack magazines.

Here's what you're actually getting when the box arrives: the table top, two legs, a lower shelf, the hardware kit, and the pre-wired charging module. The construction is solid MDF with a laminated farmhouse finish — the kind of warm brown that photographs well and looks even better in person. As shown in the product image, the silhouette is classic farmhouse: clean lines, a lower display shelf, and a top surface that's genuinely usable. The dimensions matter here — it's not just taller than typical side tables; the 24-inch surface gives you room for a laptop, a snack plate, and your drink simultaneously. That's rare in this category.

**Real-world performance**

The charging station is the headline feature, and it works as advertised. You get one AC outlet and two USB ports built into the side of the table, which means your phone, tablet, and lamp can all live off the same piece of furniture. No more crawling behind the couch to find an outlet. One buyer noted: *"I was skeptical about the charging ports on furniture, but they've been flawless for three months now. My phone charges as fast as it does on the wall charger."* That tracks — the USB ports deliver standard 5V/2A output, which isn't fast-charge territory, but it's perfectly adequate for overnight or evening top-ups.

The lower shelf is a genuine bonus, not an afterthought. It's sized to hold baskets, books, or a stack of board games, and it adds storage without making the table feel bulky. The whole unit measures roughly 24 x 16 inches on top and stands at a comfortable height for standard sofas — you won't be reaching down or craning up to grab your drink.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the 229 ratings, a few themes keep coming up. The size is the most common compliment — people repeatedly mention it's *larger than expected* and that it finally replaced a bulky TV tray. The charging station gets consistent positive mentions, especially from people who use the table as a permanent work-from-home spot on the couch. Assembly difficulty is described as moderate — one reviewer said: *"Took me about half an hour with a screwdriver. The pieces fit together well, no stripped screws."*

**Where it falls short**

Honest tradeoffs exist here. The charging station needs to be within about six feet of a wall outlet — the cord is six feet long and it's not replaceable. If your sofa floats in the middle of the room, this table won't work for you. The straight-cut legs mean no leveling feet, so if your floor has any slope or unevenness, you'll feel a slight wobble. A furniture pad or felt coaster under one leg solves it, but it's worth knowing before you buy. And while the farmhouse finish is attractive, it's a laminate — so don't expect solid wood durability. It'll handle daily use fine, but it won't survive a heavy impact or years of rough treatment without showing wear.

**Who should buy this**

This table is ideal for three specific buyers: apartment dwellers who need a compact workstation next to the couch, families who want a kids-friendly surface with hidden cord management, and anyone whose sofa area has a wall outlet nearby. It's less ideal for people with heavily textured or uneven floors, or those who need a purely decorative piece without charging functionality.

**FAQ**

*Can the charging station be removed if I don't want it?*  
Technically yes, but it's built into the table's structure. Removing it leaves an unsightly hole in the side panel. If you don't need charging, buy a plain end table instead.

*Is the finish real wood?*  
No. It's a laminated MDF with a farmhouse-style wood grain pattern. It looks convincing from a distance and holds up to normal use, but it's not solid wood.

*Does the table require special assembly tools?*  
No. You need a Phillips screwdriver and about 30 minutes. The included Allen wrench handles the leg bolts.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely large side table with integrated charging and a style that works in modern farmhouse or transitional living rooms. **Skip it if** your outlet situation is tight or you need a wobble-free table on carpet or uneven flooring.

At 4.6 out of 5 stars from 229 ratings, the Joaxswe Farmhouse End Table earns its score. It's not perfect — the wobble issue on uneven floors is real, and the fixed cord length is limiting. But for the price, you're getting a functional, good-looking piece of furniture that solves the "where do I plug in my stuff" problem elegantly. That's a solid buy in my book.
