No product is perfect, and the Demi Shade has honest limitations. The 24-pound weight that provides stability makes it annoying to drag around if you like to reconfigure your patio weekly. It's not heavy enough to be immovable, but it's enough to be a two-hand lift. Second, the lighter color options show every bit of spring pollen and summer dust. If you're the type who notices surface grime, choose a darker shade or accept weekly wipe-downs. Finally, there's no lower shelf. This is a single-surface table, so if you need storage for books, planters, or beverage coolers, look elsewhere.
