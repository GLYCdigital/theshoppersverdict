## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The mesh shelves are dust magnets. If you're someone who hates dusting, these will test your patience — the open weave collects every particle within days. A solid shelf would have been easier to maintain.

The finish on the wood top is also thinner than premium brands. It resists scratches but isn't waterproof. Spill a drink and wipe it up immediately, or you'll see the mark. I'd recommend a clear coat if you plan to use this heavily.

Finally, the screws require a gentle touch. Several reviewers mentioned stripping screw heads by overtightening. Use the manual screwdriver for the final turns, not your drill.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want an affordable industrial piece that anchors a hallway, entryway, or living room without dominating the space. It's ideal for renters (easy to assemble/disassemble) and anyone who values stability over decorative flourish.

**Skip it if:** You need a piece for heavy daily use like a work desk, or if you want a wood table that can handle water rings without care. Also skip if you hate dusting mesh surfaces.
