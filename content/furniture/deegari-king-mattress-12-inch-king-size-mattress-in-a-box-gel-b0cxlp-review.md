**The Deegari King Mattress has a 4.2-star average from 210 Amazon ratings — solid, but not spectacular. Here's what those ratings actually mean for your back, your budget, and your sleep.**

## What You Actually Get

The box shows up compressed and rolled — roughly the size of a large cooler, weighing in around 70 pounds for the king. Inside is a 12-inch, three-layer construction: a gel-infused memory foam top (about 2 inches), a transition layer (roughly 2 inches), and a high-density support base (8 inches). The cover is a standard knit fabric — nothing premium, but not scratchy either. You get the mattress, a small patch kit, and nothing else. No box spring required; it's designed for slatted frames, platform beds, or even the floor during a transition period.

## Real-World Performance

The standout feature here is temperature regulation. Gel-infused memory foam is often marketing fluff, but multiple verified buyers specifically noted sleeping cooler than on their previous all-foam mattress. One reviewer wrote, *"I was expecting to wake up sweating like with my old memory foam. Three weeks in, that hasn't happened once."* The 12-inch profile also gives it a substantial feel — it's not one of those flimsy 8-inch mattresses that bottom out if you sit on the edge.

Firmness lands around a 6.5 out of 10 — medium-firm territory. Back and stomach sleepers in the 150–230 pound range consistently report good spinal alignment. The motion isolation is genuinely impressive; if you sleep with a partner who tosses, you'll feel minimal transfer. That's a direct benefit of the all-foam construction.

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest truth: this is not a plush mattress. Side sleepers under 150 pounds have repeatedly flagged the same issue — pressure points at the shoulder and hip. If that's you, budget for a 2-inch soft topper or look elsewhere. The lack of edge support is also notable. Sit on the corner and you'll sink more than you'd like; it's fine for sleeping, but not for tying shoes.

The other practical gripe: no handles. Once you unbox this thing, moving it even a few feet is awkward. Several reviewers mentioned this specific frustration. And while off-gassing is minimal — most report it's ready in a few hours, not days — there is still a faint chemical smell on day one.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you're a back or stomach sleeper on a budget who runs warm at night, or if you need a guest-room mattress that performs above its price tier. **Skip it if** you're a petite side sleeper, or if you need strong edge support for sitting on the bed regularly.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does it take to fully expand?**
A: Most buyers report full expansion within 24 hours, though it's sleepable after 4–6. Give it a full day before judging the feel.

**Q: Does it fit standard king sheets?**
A: Yes — it's a true 76" x 80" king. Deep-pocket sheets are recommended since the 12-inch profile is thicker than standard.

**Q: What's the return policy like?**
A: Standard 100-night trial with free returns, but read the fine print — you'll need to re-compress the mattress yourself for pickup, and that's a two-person job.

## The Verdict

The Deegari King Mattress delivers what most budget box mattresses promise but rarely achieve: genuine cooling, solid support for average-weight sleepers, and minimal headache during setup. The 4.2 rating reflects real strengths and real compromises.

**Buy it if** you sleep warm, prefer medium-firm support, and want a quality king without spending flagship money. **Skip it if** you're a slight side sleeper, need robust edge support, or expect luxury plushness at this price point.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A strong value with one notable caveat for lighter side sleepers.
