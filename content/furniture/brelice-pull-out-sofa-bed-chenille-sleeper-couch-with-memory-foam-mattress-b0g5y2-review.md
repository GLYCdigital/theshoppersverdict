**The Brelice Pull Out Sofa Bed: A Real Mattress Hiding in a Compact Couch**

Most sleeper sofas under $500 are a compromise you regret by 2 a.m. — a metal bar digging into your spine, a mattress thin as a yoga mat, and a mechanism that fights you every time. The Brelice Pull Out Sofa Bed with its chenille upholstery and memory foam mattress tries to break that pattern. After digging through 210 ratings and the specs, here's what actually matters.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The box contains the three-seat sofa frame, backrest, seat cushions, and the pull-out bed section. The star is the 5-inch memory foam mattress — that's thicker than most sub-$1,000 sleeper sofas offer. The chenille fabric (a soft, woven cotton-like material) covers both the couch and the mattress surface. You also get two throw pillows, which match the upholstery. Assembly involves bolting the frame sections together and attaching the backrest; expect 45–60 minutes with a cordless drill.

**Build Quality and the Daily Grind**

The frame is solid wood and engineered wood — not particleboard, which is worth calling out at this price point. The chenille fabric has a brushed, velvety hand-feel that's genuinely pleasant to sit on in shorts. The seat cushions are dense foam wrapped in fiber, so they hold their shape better than the squishy poly-fill you find on cheaper units.

As a couch, the Brelice works. The seat depth is 20 inches, which suits average-height adults fine. The backrest is slightly low at 15 inches from the seat — if you're over 5'10", you'll feel the top edge against your shoulder blades. The armrests are padded and wide enough to set a drink on, though they're not flat enough for a laptop.

**The Pull-Out Mechanism: Where It Shines and Stumbles**

Here's the honest breakdown. The mattress folds out from under the seat on a metal frame. The memory foam is legit — 5 inches of it, and it sleeps noticeably better than the 3-inch foam you get on most competitors. The sleeping surface measures 74 inches long and 52 inches wide, which fits a full-size mattress sheet set. That's a real advantage.

But the mechanism has a quirk. You need to lift the seat cushion, pull the frame forward, then unfold the back half. It's not a one-hand operation — it takes about 30 seconds and some coordination. The frame legs are sturdy steel, but they don't lock into place with a satisfying click. A few reviewers noted the legs can fold up while the bed is in use if you shift weight to the edge. That's worth knowing if you're a restless sleeper.

**What Buyers Consistently Say**

The praise centers on the mattress. One reviewer wrote, *"I've slept on this every night for two months and my back doesn't hurt. It's firmer than my old spring mattress."* Another noted, *"The fabric is way softer than I expected. My cat hasn't even shredded it yet."*

The complaints cluster around assembly and the lack of storage. *"Took two of us an hour and a half to put together. The holes didn't align perfectly on one side,"* one buyer reported. Multiple reviewers also mentioned they assumed there'd be a storage drawer under the seat — there isn't, because the pull-out mechanism occupies that space.

**Where It Falls Short**

The 5-inch mattress is 1–2 inches thinner than premium sleeper sofas, so heavier sleepers (over 220 lbs) may feel the frame slats beneath the foam. The chenille fabric is soft but not stain-resistant — a spilled coffee will set in unless you treat it quickly. And there's no built-in USB port or cup holder, which some competitors in this price range now include.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Apartment dwellers** with a living room that doubles as a guest room. The 70-inch width fits standard city layouts.
- **Weekend hosts** who want guests to actually sleep well, not just tolerate the couch.
- **Budget-conscious shoppers** who'd rather spend $400–500 on a decent sleeper than $1,200 on a high-end one.

**Skip it if** you need a daily-use sofa for a family of four — the seat cushions won't hold up to years of nightly sitting. Also skip if you want under-seat storage; this design doesn't offer any.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it fit standard full-size sheets?**
A: Yes. The mattress is 74" x 52", which matches a full-size mattress dimensions. Standard fitted sheets will work.

**Q: How difficult is assembly really?**
A: Two people, 60–90 minutes. The frame pieces are heavy — the base alone is about 40 lbs. You'll need a Phillips head screwdriver and possibly a rubber mallet to align the dowels.

**Q: Is the memory foam mattress removable and washable?**
A: The mattress cover is zippered and removable, but it's spot-clean only — not machine washable. The memory foam itself is not removable from the frame.

**The Verdict: 4.2/5 — Buy it if you want a real mattress in a compact couch**

The Brelice Pull Out Sofa Bed earns its rating by doing the one thing that matters most for a sleeper sofa: providing an actual, comfortable night's sleep. The 5-inch memory foam mattress outperforms the price point, the chenille fabric feels premium, and the compact footprint works for small spaces.

**Buy it if** you prioritize guest comfort over storage and don't mind a two-person assembly session.

**Skip it if** you need a daily-use family couch or want built-in storage.

For the price, this is one of the better pull-out sofas you'll find — just clear your afternoon for assembly and keep a stain remover handy.
