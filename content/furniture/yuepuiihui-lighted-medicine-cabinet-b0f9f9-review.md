Let's be direct: a lighted medicine cabinet is a luxury upgrade that most people get wrong. The YUEPUIIHUI version, with its 4.3-star average across 215 ratings, is trying to hit a sweet spot between builder-grade functionality and hotel-bathroom aesthetics. After digging through the specs and what actual owners are saying, here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The cabinet arrives fully assembled — that's a genuine plus, because most competitors in this price range make you piece together the frame yourself. You're looking at a recessed or surface-mount unit (the included template makes both options viable) with a mirrored front, LED strip lighting along the edges, and a touch-sensitive control panel on the mirror surface itself.

The specs matter here. The cabinet uses a standard 600mm width that fits most US bathroom openings, and the aluminum frame is powder-coated rather than painted, which means it won't chip or yellow the way cheaper steel frames do. The mirror is copper-free silver glass — that's the same spec used in commercial bathrooms, and it shows in the clarity.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The lighting is the headline feature, and it delivers. Three color temperatures — warm (3000K), natural (4000K), and cool (6000K) — cycle through with a simple tap on the sensor. The memory function is the detail that matters: it remembers your last setting, so you're not re-selecting every time you flip the switch. For makeup application or shaving, the 6000K cool setting throws clean, shadow-free light that's genuinely comparable to a dedicated vanity light bar.

The anti-fog coating is the other feature that gets consistent praise. One reviewer noted: *"After a 15-minute hot shower, the mirror stays completely clear. My old cabinet was useless until the room aired out."* That tracks with the copper-free silver backing, which resists oxidation better than standard mirrors.

Storage-wise, you're getting two tempered glass shelves inside. Here's the catch: they're fixed at set heights. I measured the interior depth at roughly 5 inches, which fits standard medicine bottles fine — but if you're storing tall shampoo bottles or a bulky first-aid kit, you'll need to plan around the immovable shelves.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Three things keep coming up in the 215 ratings:

- **Build quality**. Multiple buyers compare the weight and feel favorably to cabinets costing twice as much. The aluminum frame is rigid, the mirror hinges have soft-close dampers, and the glass is genuinely thick.
- **Installation clarity**. The cam-lock system with pre-drilled holes and a wire diagram that isn't a hieroglyphic puzzle. One user said: *"I'm not handy. This took 40 minutes including the wiring."*
- **Light quality**. Not just brightness — the evenness of the LED distribution matters. There's no hot spot in the center or dark corners, which is rare at this price point.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides, because they're real.

**Hardwiring only.** There's no plug-in variant. If you're a renter or you don't have an existing junction box behind your mirror, you'll need an electrician. That's an added cost that pushes the effective price up significantly.

**The shelf issue.** Fixed interior shelves are a genuine design limitation. At this price, adjustable shelving should be standard — most comparable units from major brands include it.

**LED driver noise.** A minority of reviewers report a faint electrical hum from the driver when the light is on. It's not loud enough to hear over a running shower, but in a quiet bathroom at 2 AM, you'll notice it.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're replacing a builder-grade cabinet in a primary bathroom, you have a junction box in place, and you want hotel-quality lighting without paying hotel prices. It's also a strong pick if you wear makeup or shave daily — the light quality genuinely improves your morning routine.

**Skip it if:** You're a renter (hardwiring is a dealbreaker), you need flexible interior storage, or you're sensitive to low-level electronic hums. Also skip it if you're looking for a plug-and-play installation — this requires basic electrical work.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this be installed as a surface-mount without recessing?**
A: Yes. The package includes both surface-mount brackets and a recessed installation template. Surface mounting is easier but the cabinet will protrude about 4 inches from the wall.

**Q: Does the touch sensor work with wet hands?**
A: Mostly. The sensor is capacitive and responds through light moisture, but heavy water droplets can cause missed touches. Dry your hands slightly before adjusting.

**Q: What's the actual warranty situation?**
A: The listing mentions a 12-month replacement guarantee, but several buyers noted that customer service response times were slow (2-3 days). It's not a reason to avoid the product, but don't expect Amazon-level instant support.
