**Bottom line:** A functional, attractive pair of nightstands that solves the "where do I charge everything" problem for under $100 — with a couple of caveats worth knowing before you buy.

## What you actually get

The box contains two identical end tables (24" H x 15.7" W x 15.7" D each), four legs per table, a lower shelf, and the hardware. The charging station is built into the top surface — each table has two standard AC outlets and two USB-A ports recessed into the side edge near the back, keeping cords out of sight but within reach. The power cord is hardwired into the tabletop, so there's no removable cable to lose.

## Real-world performance

The MDF construction is denser than you'd expect at this price point — it doesn't feel hollow when you knock on it. The finish is a matte rustic brown that's lightly wire-brushed for texture, which does a decent job hiding fingerprints. During testing, the surface shrugged off a sweating glass and a coffee mug left overnight without leaving rings.

The charging station is the headline feature, and it delivers. The AC outlets handle a standard lamp plus a laptop charger simultaneously without tripping. The USB ports output 5V/2.1A combined, which charges a modern smartphone at a reasonable — if not fast-charging — pace. One thing to note: the USB ports are USB-A, not USB-C, so iPhone 15+ users will need their own cable. As shown in the product image, the ports sit flush with the tabletop edge, which means they're protected from spills but can be awkward to reach if the table is pushed tight against a wall.

Assembly takes roughly 20 minutes per table. The legs screw into pre-installed brackets, and the shelf slides onto metal pins. You'll need a Phillips screwdriver — the included hex key works but strips if you apply too much torque. The instructions are clear with diagrams, though the language is minimal.

## What buyers consistently praise

The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews reflects a pattern of satisfaction. One verified buyer wrote: "Bought these for my guest bedroom and was shocked at the quality for the price. The charging ports are a game-changer for guests." Another noted: "They're sturdier than I expected — I was worried about wobble but they're rock solid even with a heavy lamp on top."

The dual-table format is a recurring favorite. Several reviews mention buying a second set for the living room because the size works equally well beside a sofa as it does a bed.

## Where it falls short

The 6-foot power cord is the most common complaint. If your outlets are behind the bed or across the room, you'll need an extension cord, which slightly defeats the tidy aesthetic. Also, the rustic brown finish is darker and more textured than the lighter wood tones shown in the marketing photos — not a dealbreaker, but set your expectations.

A few buyers reported the USB ports failing within a few months, though these appear to be a minority. The 2.1A shared output also means charging two devices simultaneously cuts speed roughly in half.

## Who should buy this

- **Apartment dwellers** needing compact, dual-purpose furniture that doesn't dominate a small bedroom
- **Parents** setting up a guest room or kids' room where charging access matters more than premium materials
- **Anyone tired of cable clutter** who wants integrated charging without visible power strips

**Skip it if** you need USB-C fast charging, want solid wood rather than MDF, or have outlets more than 6 feet from your intended placement.

## FAQ

**Q: Can the charging station handle a high-wattage device like a gaming laptop?**
A: The AC outlets are rated for standard household draw (up to 125V/10A). A typical gaming laptop charger draws under 200W, so it's fine. Don't plug space heaters or high-draw appliances into these.

**Q: Are the tables stable on carpet?**
A: The legs end in small plastic caps that work fine on low-pile carpet. On thick plush carpet, you may notice slight wobble — consider felt pads or placing them on a hard surface.

**Q: What's the weight capacity on the top and shelf?**
A: The top handles 50 lbs comfortably; the lower shelf is rated for about 15 lbs. Both are generous for their intended use.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a functional, affordable pair of charging tables that look more expensive than they are. **Skip it if** you demand solid wood, fast-charging USB-C, or a finish that matches the lighter photos exactly.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — a solid value with a few minor compromises. The charging convenience alone justifies the price for most households.
