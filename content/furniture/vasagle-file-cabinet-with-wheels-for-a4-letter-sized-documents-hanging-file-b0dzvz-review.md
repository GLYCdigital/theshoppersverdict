If your filing system has devolved into a teetering stack of manila folders on the floor, the VASAGLE File Cabinet with Wheels is a practical, space-conscious fix. This isn't a showpiece — it's a workhorse designed for A4 and US Letter hanging files, and at a 4.1-star average across 204 ratings, it's clearly earning its keep in home offices and studios. But before you click buy, let's talk about what it actually does well and where it cuts corners.

## What You're Actually Getting

The cabinet ships flat-packed, which is standard for this price tier. Out of the box you get the steel body panels, a set of four casters (two with locking brakes), full-extension drawer rails, and the hanging file frame. The drawer is sized to accept both A4 and Letter hanging files without any adapter bars — a genuine convenience if you mix paper sizes or print international documents.

Dimensions are compact enough for a desk alcove or closet floor, but the real story is the wheels. All four casters swivel, which makes this cabinet surprisingly maneuverable. Lock the front two and it stays put when you're yanking open a full drawer. Unlock them and you can roll it from desk to printer to archive corner without breaking a sweat.

## Real-World Performance

The full-extension slides are the standout feature here. You can pull the drawer completely out, giving you unobstructed access to the back files. That's a feature usually reserved for pricier units, and it makes a tangible difference when you're digging for a specific folder in the middle of tax season. The hanging bars are spaced correctly, and the frame sits flush so files slide without catching.

Build quality is decent for the price. The steel panels are sturdy enough that the cabinet doesn't wobble when the drawer is fully loaded, and the casters roll smoothly even on low-pile carpet. Assembly requires patience — several reviewers noted it took close to an hour with two people. The instructions are mostly diagrams, and a few steps require a bit of interpretation.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The mobility is the recurring theme in positive reviews. One buyer wrote, "Being able to roll this from my desk to my filing corner is a game-changer — I'm not walking back and forth 20 times a day anymore." Another noted the full-extension drawer was the deciding factor: "I can finally see every file without playing a game of file Jenga."

The dual-size compatibility also gets consistent shoutouts. For people dealing with mixed international and US paperwork, not needing separate cabinets for A4 and Letter is a real convenience.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The metal finish shows fingerprints and dust quickly — if you're particular about aesthetics, you'll be wiping this down more often than you'd like. The assembly process is the most common complaint in lower-rated reviews. One frustrated buyer said, "The holes didn't all line up perfectly on the first try, and I had to loosen and re-tighten everything." That's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing going in.

The cabinet itself is fairly lightweight when empty, which means it can shift slightly on hard floors when you open the drawer aggressively. The locking casters help, but they're not a substitute for a fixed base.

## Who Should Buy This

This is ideal for home office workers who need their files near their desk but don't want a bulky lateral cabinet eating floor space. It's also a strong pick for small business owners who process mixed paper sizes, crafters with pattern storage, or anyone renting who can't mount permanent shelving. If you have heavy-duty commercial filing needs or want a piece that looks like furniture rather than office equipment, skip it — this is functional, not decorative.

## FAQ

**Will it hold legal-size documents?**
No. This is specifically sized for A4 and US Letter. Legal-size hanging files will not fit without bending.

**Can the casters be removed for stationary use?**
Yes, but you'd be left with exposed mounting holes. If you don't want wheels, buy the non-mobile version of this cabinet instead.

**Does it work with standard Pendaflex hanging files?**
Yes, any standard hanging file frame will work. The rails accept common letter and A4 hanging folders without issue.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You need portable, dual-size filing that rolls where you work, and you're comfortable with a 45-minute assembly session.

**Skip it if:** You want a furniture-grade piece, need legal-size capacity, or have zero patience for aligning screw holes.
