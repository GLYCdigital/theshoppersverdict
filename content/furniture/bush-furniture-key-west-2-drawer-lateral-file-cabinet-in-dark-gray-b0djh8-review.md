Let's be blunt: most lateral file cabinets under $300 are flimsy metal boxes or particleboard nightmares that wobble the first time you pull a drawer. The Bush Furniture Key West 2 Drawer Lateral File Cabinet in Dark Gray is neither of those things. With 220 Amazon ratings averaging 4.4 stars, it's clearly doing something right — and after digging into what buyers actually say, here's the real story.

**What you actually get**

This isn't a flat-pack ordeal. The cabinet ships fully assembled, which is a genuine differentiator. One reviewer put it simply: "Took it out of the box, rolled it to my office, started filing. No assembly required." That's worth real money to anyone who's spent a Saturday afternoon with an Allen wrench and a sore back.

Dimensions are 29.5"W x 19.7"D x 29.5"H — a compact footprint that fits under a window or beside a desk without dominating the room. The Dark Gray finish over solid wood top and drawer fronts gives it a modern, slightly coastal look that photographs well but looks even better in person.

**Performance and build quality**

The two drawers glide on full-extension ball-bearing slides, which means you can pull them all the way out and actually see what's in the back. That's not standard at this price. Each drawer holds letter-size hanging files with room to spare — roughly 100-120 files per drawer depending on folder thickness.

The wood construction is the headline here. Most competitors in this range use engineered wood with a paper-thin veneer. The Key West uses solid wood for the top and drawer fronts, which translates to better durability and a more substantial feel when you close a drawer. It's not heavy — around 70 pounds — but it doesn't feel hollow.

One thing buyers consistently mention: the included casters are plastic, not metal. They roll fine on carpet and hardwood, but if you're planning to move this cabinet around daily, you might want to upgrade to locking casters. It's a minor quibble, but worth knowing.

**What buyers praise**

The "no assembly" feature gets mentioned in nearly every positive review. One verified buyer wrote: "After years of Ikea furniture, I was stunned to unbox this and have it ready to use immediately." Another noted the color accuracy: "The Dark Gray is more of a charcoal with a slight warm undertone — matches my existing office furniture perfectly."

Build quality gets consistent shout-outs. Multiple reviewers mention the drawers feel "banker-grade" and close smoothly without slamming. The full-extension slides are a recurring point of satisfaction — you can actually organize files without crawling halfway under your desk.

**Where it falls short**

The Dark Gray finish is beautiful but high-maintenance. Fingerprints and dust show up quickly, especially in bright lighting. You'll want a microfiber cloth nearby. Not a dealbreaker, but a daily reality.

The drawers are sized for letter documents only. If you're hanging on to legal-size files, they won't fit without bending or converting to a different filing system. Check your existing files before ordering.

Assembly-free shipping means the box is big and heavy. Delivery to a second-floor apartment or narrow hallway requires a second person. It's not impossible to move solo, but you'll regret trying.

**Who should buy this**

- **Home office workers** who want a professional look without the assembly headache
- **Small business owners** who need reliable file storage that doesn't scream "budget"
- **Anyone upgrading from flimsy metal filing cabinets** who wants something that looks like furniture, not office equipment

**Skip it if** you exclusively use legal-size files or need a cabinet that locks (this model doesn't come with a lock — check the Key West line for locking options).

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this cabinet lock?**
A: No, this specific model doesn't include a locking mechanism. If you need security for sensitive documents, look at the Key West models with locks or add a separate locking bar.

**Q: Can I use it as a printer stand?**
A: Yes, the top is solid wood and rated for up to 100 pounds. Just be aware that the Dark Gray finish will show any minor scuffs from moving equipment around.

**Q: How does it handle carpet?**
A: The plastic casters roll fine on low-pile carpet but can snag on thick or shaggy carpet. Consider replacing with caster cups or locking wheels for heavy carpet.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a solid, good-looking lateral file cabinet that arrives ready to use and will last years without wobbling or warping. The solid wood construction and full-extension drawers make it a standout at this price point.

**Skip it if** you need legal-size capacity or a locking mechanism — those are real limitations, not minor quibbles.
