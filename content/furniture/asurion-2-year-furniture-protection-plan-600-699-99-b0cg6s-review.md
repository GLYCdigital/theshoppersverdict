**What You're Actually Getting**

This isn't just a fabric shield. The plan covers mechanical and electrical failures on powered furniture — think recliners with motors, lift chairs, adjustable beds, and massage features. That's a significant differentiator from cheap fabric protection sprays. It also includes stain coverage, but the real value sits in the mechanical side. If your power recliner's motor dies in month 14, ASURION repairs or replaces it. That's a $200+ repair you won't pay out of pocket.

The plan activates from your purchase date, not delivery. So if your sofa sits in a warehouse for two weeks before shipping, you've already burned through part of your coverage window. Set a calendar reminder for month 21 to do a full inspection of every moving part before the plan lapses.

**What Buyers Actually Say**

The 4.0-star average tells a split story. The praise is consistent: "The claim process was surprisingly painless. Filed online, had a repair tech out in 5 days." That's the recurring theme — ASURION's digital claims system works. Customers who filed for mechanical failures report quick approvals and actual repairs, not runaround.

But the gripes cluster around what's not covered. One reviewer noted, "They denied my claim for a scratch that appeared on the armrest — said it was cosmetic wear." That's accurate per the policy, but it stings when you paid $60+ for the plan. Another frustration: stains. Multiple customers mention the claims process for pet accidents or mystery marks requires documentation and sometimes a home visit from an adjuster. It's not a simple "send a photo, get a check" situation.

**Where It Falls Short**

The exclusions are where shoppers get burned. Normal wear and tear? Not covered. Fading from sunlight? Not covered. Pre-existing damage? Obviously not. The plan is designed for catastrophic events — mechanical failure, structural damage, accidental stains — not the slow degradation of daily life. If you're buying this for a fabric sofa that'll see heavy family use, temper your expectations. It's not a maintenance plan; it's a failure plan.

Also, note the coverage window: 2 years from purchase. Most furniture issues from manufacturing defects surface in the first 12 months, so you're really paying for years 1–2 of peace of mind on the mechanical components.

- **Households with kids or pets**: Accidental stains are covered (with documentation), which is the most common furniture killer.

**Who Should Skip It**

- **Static furniture buyers**: If you're buying a solid wood dining table or a basic non-powered sofa, the mechanical coverage is wasted, and stain coverage alone is thin value.

**FAQ**

**Q: What happens if my item can't be repaired?**
A: You'll receive a replacement or reimbursement up to the original purchase price, capped at $699.99 for this tier. The exact method depends on the claim and availability of the item.

**Q: Can I file a claim online, or do I need to call?**
A: Online is the preferred route. You submit photos and a description through Amazon's claims portal, and ASURION typically responds within 24–48 hours. Phone support exists but expect longer hold times.
