**A 24-inch counter stool that actually respects your back — and your budget.**

Most bar stools under $100 are either wobbly metal skeletons or plywood torture devices. The DAGONHIL 24 Inch Counter Height Stool sits in a sweet spot: it's upholstered, properly proportioned, and has earned a 4.2-star average from 210 Amazon buyers. Here's the full breakdown before you click "add to cart."

**What you actually get**

The box contains two identical stools (this listing is for the pair), each with a pre-attached seat back, four legs that screw in with a provided hex wrench, and a footrest bar that bolts between the front legs. The upholstery is a linen-look fabric over a padded foam seat — roughly 2 inches thick based on buyer measurements. The frame is powder-coated metal in matte black, and the legs have small rubber floor caps to protect your tile or hardwood.

**How it holds up in real kitchens**

At 24 inches from floor to seat, these pair correctly with standard 36-inch kitchen counters and 42-inch bar-height tables. The seat width is about 17.5 inches — wide enough for average adult hips without feeling like a throne. The backrest curves slightly, and the padding runs the full height of the back, which matters more than you'd think when you're sitting through a long meal or working at the counter.

Buyers consistently mention the stability. One reviewer wrote: *"I'm 6'2\" and 230 lbs — I sat on these without any wobble or creaking. The legs feel solid."* Another noted the assembly: *"Took me 12 minutes for both stools. Holes lined up, no stripped screws."* The rubber caps do their job — no scratches reported on wood or tile floors.

The fabric is a genuine plus. Unlike faux leather that peels within a year or bare wood that bruises your tailbone, this padded cloth is grippy (kids won't slide off), breathable, and easy to wipe for most spills. But — and this is the main tradeoff — light-colored variants show coffee rings and pasta sauce. If you buy the beige/gray tone, treat the fabric with a stain repellent spray on day one.

**Where it falls short**

Three honest complaints surface repeatedly in buyer feedback.

First, the footrest bar position. It's mounted around 6-7 inches off the floor, which works fine for taller people but forces shorter users (under 5'4") to dangle or sit awkwardly. One buyer said: *"I'm 5'2\" and my feet barely reach the bar — I just rest them on the floor instead."*

Second, no swivel. If you're used to spinning around to grab something from the counter behind you, you'll have to physically scoot the stool. Not a dealbreaker for most, but worth knowing.

Third, the fabric on the seat cushion is not removable. When it eventually stains or wears through, there's no zip-off cover to toss in the washer. You'll need to spot-clean or reupholster.

**Who should buy these**

- **Kitchen island owners** with standard 36-inch counters who want padded seating for daily breakfast and homework sessions
- **Renters** who need durable, no-tools-required assembly and don't want to leave permanent marks
- **Taller households** (5'6" and up) where the footrest height won't be an issue
- **Anyone who hates cold metal or sticky vinyl seats** — the fabric is genuinely comfortable

**Skip them if** you're under 5'4" and need a footrest you can actually use, or if you want a swivel feature. Also skip if you have kids who eat spaghetti in white clothes — get the darker fabric version.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do these stools scratch hardwood floors?**
A: No — the rubber caps on the legs protect floors. But check the caps occasionally; they can wear down after a year of heavy use, and replacements are cheap at any hardware store.

**Q: Can these be used outdoors?**
A: Not recommended. The fabric will fade and mildew in moisture, and the metal frame isn't rust-resistant for outdoor exposure.

**Q: What's the weight limit?**
A: Amazon's listing doesn't specify, but buyer feedback and construction suggest a safe range of 250-300 lbs per stool. If you're closer to 300, sit gently — the legs are sturdy, but the seat frame has limits.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a comfortable, stable, budget-friendly counter stool for a standard kitchen island and you're of average height or taller. The 4.2-star rating reflects genuine value — this is a solid 4-star product with a few ergonomic caveats.
