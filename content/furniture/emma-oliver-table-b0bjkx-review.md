A 4.7-star average across 235 ratings tells you one thing: this table is doing something right. But a strong rating doesn't tell you whether it'll survive a household with toddlers, whether the finish holds up to hot coffee mugs, or whether assembly will test your patience. Here's what I found digging through the reviews and product specs.

**What you actually get**

The box includes the tabletop, four legs, a cross-brace frame, and all necessary hardware — bolts, washers, and an Allen wrench. No tools needed beyond what's in the box, though a Phillips screwdriver helps for a couple of steps. The tabletop arrives fully finished; there's no staining or sealing required. As shown in the product image, the design is clean and modern — nothing flashy, nothing cheap-looking.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The tabletop uses a medium-density fiberboard core with a laminated finish. That's not premium hardwood, but it's honest for the price. The finish resists water rings — several reviewers mentioned leaving sweaty glasses on it overnight with no marks. One buyer noted: *"I've had this table for six months and my kids have dropped everything from crayons to juice boxes on it. Wipes clean with zero staining."*

Stability is where this table surprises. The cross-brace frame locks everything together tightly. Once assembled, there's no wobble — even on slightly uneven floors. The legs have adjustable leveling feet, which helps in older homes with sloping floors.

**Where it falls short**

The darker finish options show fingerprints like crazy. If you have kids or just hate cleaning smudges, factor that in. One reviewer put it bluntly: *"Love the table, but I'm wiping it down every single day. It shows every handprint."*

Assembly has a minor but consistent issue: the pre-drilled holes on the legs sometimes don't line up perfectly with the frame brackets. It's not a dealbreaker — most buyers report finishing in 30-45 minutes — but a few reviewers mentioned needing to loosen all bolts and adjust before tightening everything down. Budget extra time if you're not handy.

**Who should buy this**

This table fits three buyer profiles well:

- **First-time apartment dwellers** who need functional furniture that won't break the bank
- **Small-space households** needing a compact table that doesn't dominate a room
- **Budget-conscious buyers** who want solid construction without paying designer prices

Skip it if you're looking for a statement piece or need a table that seats more than four comfortably. This is a practical, no-nonsense table — not a showpiece.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Is the table sturdy enough for daily use?*
Yes. The cross-brace frame and adjustable feet eliminate wobble. Multiple long-term reviewers confirm it stays solid after months of regular use.

*How long does assembly actually take?*
Most buyers report 30-45 minutes with two people. Solo assembly is possible but trickier — you'll need to hold the frame while attaching legs.

*Does the finish scratch easily?*
The laminate surface resists everyday scratches from plates and utensils. But it's not indestructible — dragging heavy objects across it will leave marks.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a solid, functional table that looks good without costing a fortune. **Skip it if** you need a large capacity table or can't tolerate visible fingerprints on dark finishes.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — the assembly quirk and smudge-prone finish cost it a perfect score, but for the price, this is one of the most dependable tables in its category.
