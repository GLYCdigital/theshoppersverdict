# 79'' Long Reclining Chair Set, PE Rattan Sunbathing Chairs Review

If you're over six feet tall, you already know the struggle: most "chaise lounges" stop at your calves. This set from the 79-inch reclining chair category is specifically built for taller folks, and that's its biggest selling point. But at 3.8 stars across 190 ratings, it's not a slam dunk. Here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains two matching sunbathing chairs, each measuring 79 inches long, 24 inches wide, and about 12 inches off the ground at seat height. The frames are steel with a powder-coated finish, wrapped in PE rattan — the synthetic wicker that's become standard for outdoor furniture because it won't rot or fade like natural cane.

Each chair has a five-position adjustable backrest. The set includes the frame, the backrest mechanism, and two thin foam cushions covered in a fabric that's supposed to be water-resistant. There are no tools included, which is your first hint about the assembly situation. You'll need a socket wrench set and patience.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The PE rattan is the highlight here. It's tightly woven, feels substantial, and multiple buyers confirmed it survives full sun exposure without the color bleaching. One reviewer noted, *"Two summers in Florida direct sun and the weave still looks new."* That's meaningful — cheap rattan alternatives go chalky and brittle within one season.

The reclining mechanism is genuinely solid. The five locking positions click into place with a satisfying thunk, and the backrest stays put even when you shift your weight. No surprise collapsing, which is a common failure point on budget loungers.

But the frame assembly is where things get frustrating. The pre-drilled holes on the crossbars don't always line up with the side rails, and several buyers reported stripped bolts because the included hardware is soft metal. Plan for 45–60 minutes per chair if you're doing this solo. Two people make it easier, but even then, you'll want to hand-tighten everything before wrenching down.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The length is the runaway winner. At 79 inches, it genuinely accommodates tall adults. One verified purchase wrote: *"I'm 6'4\" and my feet don't hang off for once. This is the only lounger I've found that works."* For a tall person, that alone justifies the purchase.

The PE rattan durability also gets consistent shout-outs across multiple seasons of outdoor exposure. And the two-chair set price point delivers solid value if you're furnishing a patio or pool deck on a budget.

## Where It Falls Short

The cushions are the weakest link — and they're what you sit on. At roughly 2 inches thick, they're closer to a yoga mat than a proper cushion. Several buyers reported visible compression within weeks, and one noted, *"After a month, I could feel the frame bars through the fabric."* Budget for replacement cushions if you plan to actually lounge for more than an hour at a time.

Portability is also a miss. There are no wheels and no handles, just a flat frame that sits close to the ground. Dragging it across concrete scuffs the powder coating — which then invites rust. Pick it up when moving it, or accept the scratches.

Assembly quality control is inconsistent. While some buyers had zero issues, enough reported misaligned holes and stripped bolts to make it a legitimate concern. If you're not handy, budget for a helper or a trip to the hardware store for better bolts.

## Who Should Buy This Set

**Buy it if:** You're tall and tired of short loungers. You want a durable, UV-resistant frame that'll hold up for multiple seasons. You're okay with replacing or upgrading the cushions.

**Skip it if:** You want plush, luxurious lounging straight out of the box. You're not comfortable with basic assembly. You need something lightweight that you can easily drag around a pool deck.

## FAQ

**Can the backrest go fully flat?**
Yes. The lowest position lays the backrest essentially flat, extending the full 79 inches into a true sunbathing position. The highest position sits you at roughly a 45-degree angle for reading.

**Are the cushions waterproof or just water-resistant?**
They're water-resistant, not waterproof. They'll handle a light sprinkle, but a full rainstorm will soak through. Remove the cushions (they're not attached) and store them if heavy rain is forecast.

**What's the weight capacity per chair?**
Amazon lists it at 265 pounds, but the steel frame feels more substantial than that rating suggests. The weak point is the cushions, not the frame — heavier users will feel the frame bars through the thin padding sooner.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 3.7/5**

This is a good chair for a specific person — someone tall who needs the extra length and doesn't mind the cushion downgrade. The frame and rattan are built to last, and the five-position recline is genuinely functional. But the thin cushions, finicky assembly, and lack of wheels keep it from being a great chair.

**Buy it if** you're tall, handy, and prepared to upgrade the cushions. **Skip it if** you want a plush, ready-to-use lounger with zero assembly hassle. At this price point for a two-chair set, the length and frame durability make it a fair deal — just budget for better cushions.
