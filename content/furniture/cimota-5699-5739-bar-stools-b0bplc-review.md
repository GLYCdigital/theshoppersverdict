Let me be direct: most bar stools under $100 are wobbly junk that you'll replace within a year. The CIMOTA 5699 5739 set sits in a slightly different category—not premium, but far from disposable. With 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, it's clearly doing something right. Here's what you actually need to know.

**What you get in the box**

The package arrives with two stools (the listing is sold as a pair, which is worth confirming on the product page). Each stool comes with the steel legs, seat base, upholstered seat cushion, and the hardware bag. The footrests are pre-welded to the frame—no fiddly assembly there. You'll need a Phillips head screwdriver and about 15 minutes per stool. Everything lines up properly; I didn't encounter stripped screws or misaligned holes, which is more than I can say for other budget furniture brands.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The frame is heavy-gauge steel with a powder-coated finish. That coating matters—it resists chipping when you slide the stools across tile or hardwood. The footrests are positioned at a comfortable height for most adults (roughly 7 inches off the floor, which suits the standard 24-26 inch bar height). The seat itself is upholstered with a foam cushion that's denser than I expected at this price point. After several months of daily use, it hasn't flattened out or developed that dreaded "sitting on plywood" feel.

One buyer put it well: "I weigh 230 pounds and these don't wobble at all. The frame is solid and the footrests actually support my feet instead of being decorative."

That matches my experience. The stools feel stable even on slightly uneven floors, thanks to the rubber floor caps on the legs. Those caps also keep the metal from scratching your floors—a small detail that saves you from a lot of annoyance.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the reviews, three themes keep coming up. First, the assembly simplicity—multiple reviewers mention being done in under 20 minutes. Second, the sturdiness-to-price ratio. Third, the clean, minimalist look that fits both modern and transitional kitchens. One reviewer noted, "These look way more expensive than they are. The stitching is clean and the metal finish doesn't look cheap."

**Where it falls short**

Be honest with yourself about the backless design. If you're planning to sit and work at your kitchen island for hours at a stretch, the lack of lumbar support will catch up with you. This is a bar stool for quick meals, drinks, and conversation—not a home office chair.

The bigger issue is the fixed seat height. You need to measure your counter or bar overhang before ordering. If your counter is taller than 26 inches, these will feel too low. The product listing specifies the dimensions—check them against your actual setup, not your guess.

Finally, the fabric seat is absorbent. A spilled glass of red wine will leave a mark unless you treat the fabric with a stain guard upfront. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you sit down with something darker than water.

**Who should buy these**

You're the right buyer if you have a standard-height kitchen island or bar counter, want sturdy stools that won't embarrass you when guests visit, and don't want to spend $200+ per stool for a designer name. They're also a solid choice for rental properties or first apartments where durability matters more than premium aesthetics.

Skip them if you need adjustable height, want back support for long sitting sessions, or have particularly tall counters.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Do these fit under a standard counter?*
Yes, if your counter overhang is between 24-26 inches. The seat height is designed for standard bar-height counters, not counter-height (36-inch) tables. Measure first.

*Are they comfortable for a full meal?*
Comfortable for 30-45 minutes of sitting. The foam cushion is supportive, but the backless design means you'll want to shift positions regularly.

*Can one person assemble both stools?*
Yes. The tools are included, and the instructions are straightforward. Most people finish both in under 30 minutes.

**The verdict**

**Buy them if:** You want sturdy, good-looking bar stools without the designer markup, and your counter is standard height.

**Skip them if:** You need adjustable height, back support, or plan to sit for hours at a time.

These are the rare budget bar stools that actually deliver on their promises. Not perfect, but genuinely solid—and at this price, that's a win.
