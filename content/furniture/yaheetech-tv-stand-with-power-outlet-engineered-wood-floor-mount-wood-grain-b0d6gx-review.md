If you're tired of crawling behind your TV stand to reach a power strip buried in a nest of cables, the Yaheetech TV Stand with Power Outlet is worth a serious look. At 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, this unit sits firmly in the "buy with confidence" category — but it's not without a few honest compromises.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the TV stand itself (obviously), a power outlet module pre-installed in the back panel, and all necessary hardware. The unit measures roughly 47 inches wide, 15.7 inches deep, and 20 inches tall — a standard media console footprint that fits most 50–65 inch TVs. The wood grain finish is a laminate over engineered wood, not solid timber. It looks convincing from three feet away, though up close you'll notice it's printed grain.

The power module is the headline feature: two AC outlets and two USB ports integrated into the back panel. This means your streaming device, soundbar, and phone charger all plug directly into the stand — no visible surge protector required.

## Build Quality and Daily Use

The engineered wood construction is what you'd expect at this price point — solid enough, but not heirloom furniture. The panels are dense and the laminate resists scratches well. One buyer noted, "Assembly took about an hour but the pieces fit perfectly. No stripped screws, no misaligned holes." That's consistent with what most reviewers report.

The open shelving handles the real-world test well. A 15-inch soundbar fits flush on the middle shelf, and the side compartments swallow a cable box, game console, and a stack of Blu-rays without crowding. The cable management holes in the back are generously sized — you can route thick HDMI cables through without fighting them.

The top surface holds up to 110 pounds, which covers most TVs in the 55–65 inch range. The legs have small adjustable levelers, which saved me on an uneven apartment floor.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The power outlets are the clear MVP. One verified purchase put it plainly: "No more bending behind the TV to find the plug. Everything connects to the stand itself. Game changer." Another reviewer highlighted the value: "Tried to find a stand with outlets for under $100 — this is the one."

Buyers also frequently mention the clean look. The wood grain, while printed, photographs well and doesn't scream "cheap laminate" in a living room setting.

## Where It Falls Short

The engineered wood surface is vulnerable to water damage. Set a cold drink directly on it and you'll likely see a white ring form. Use a coaster — this isn't negotiable.

Assembly is the other friction point. The instructions are purely diagrammatic with no written steps, and there are over 20 screws to manage. Budget a solid hour, and have a screwdriver with a magnetic tip handy. Several reviews mention that the included Allen wrench gets tedious after the tenth screw.

The open back panel means dust accumulates on your components faster than a closed-cabinet unit. If you're in a dusty environment, budget for regular cleaning.

## Who Should Buy This

This stand is ideal for **apartment dwellers and first-time TV owners** who want integrated power without paying premium furniture prices. It's also a smart pick for **renters** — the neutral wood grain works with most decor, and the compact footprint fits smaller spaces.

Skip it if you need **closed storage** to hide clutter, if you're furnishing a **formal living room** where solid wood matters, or if you're buying for a **TV over 65 inches** — the weight capacity and width won't comfortably support larger panels.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the power outlet work with the TV's power?**
A: No. The outlet module plugs directly into your wall socket. It's independent of the TV, so you can leave devices plugged in with the TV off.

**Q: Can I mount this on the wall?**
A: No. This is a floor-standing unit with fixed legs. There are no wall-mount brackets or keyhole slots.

**Q: What's the maximum TV size this supports?**
A: The top surface is rated for 110 pounds. For most modern flat screens, that covers 55–65 inches. Always check your TV's weight against the 110-pound limit.
