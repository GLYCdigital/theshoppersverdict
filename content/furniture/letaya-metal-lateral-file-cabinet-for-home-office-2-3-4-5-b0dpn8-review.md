If you're drowning in paper but your home office is the size of a closet, the Letaya Metal Lateral File Cabinet deserves a hard look. This isn't a flashy piece — it's a utilitarian metal box designed to hold files, and it does that job competently. With 210 ratings averaging 4.2 stars, it's clearly working for a lot of people. But before you click buy, here's what you need to know.

## What you actually get

Letaya ships this in four drawer configurations — 2, 3, 4, or 5 drawers — so you can scale to your paper problem. The lateral design means drawers open sideways (front-to-back depth is shorter than a vertical cabinet), which makes it a natural fit under a window or against a wall where depth is tight. It accommodates both letter and legal-size hanging files.

The box contains the cabinet body, drawers, ball-bearing slides, handles, and a locking mechanism with two keys. There's no assembly required for the main structure beyond attaching handles and sliding in drawers — but that's where some buyers hit friction.

## Build quality and real-world use

The metal isn't bargain-bin thin. It has a decent powder-coat finish that resists scratching from file folders and general office abuse. The full-extension slides are the standout feature here — you can pull a drawer completely out to access files in the back, which is something cheap cabinets skip. At this price point, that's genuinely impressive.

One Amazon reviewer noted: *"The drawers glide smoothly even when fully loaded with legal files. I've had this for six months and it still operates like day one."* Another echoed: *"Heavy duty for the price. I was expecting flimsy, but the metal gauge is thicker than what I've seen at big-box stores."*

The locking system works — one key turns and engages a central bar that secures all drawers. Fine for basic privacy, not a security feature.

## Where it falls short

The assembly complaints are consistent and worth flagging. Several buyers reported that screw holes didn't align perfectly on their first unit, requiring persuasion (or a drill) to seat screws. If you're not handy, this will frustrate you. One reviewer put it bluntly: *"Took me two hours to get the drawers aligned. The holes were off by a few millimeters."*

The lack of soft-close is a real miss. If you let a drawer go, it slams — hard. In a quiet home office, that's a noise that'll have you wincing every time. You can install aftermarket阻尼 pads, but you shouldn't have to.

The included key feels like a toy — light, thin metal that could bend if you torque it. The lock itself is fine, but the key won't survive years of daily use.

## Who should buy this

**The paper-piler**: If you've got stacks of documents and need a no-nonsense filing solution, this works. **The small-office operator**: The 5-drawer version replaces an entire filing stack in a corner. **The budget-conscious buyer**: You're getting metal construction and full-extension slides at a price where competitors give you particleboard and partial slides.

**Skip it if** you need a furniture-grade piece for a client-facing space — this looks utilitarian, not premium. Also skip if you're not comfortable with minor assembly finesse.

## FAQ

**Q: Does it fit legal-size files?**
A: Yes. The lateral design accepts both letter and legal hanging files. Letter files will sit loosely in the rails, so use adapters if you're mixing sizes.

**Q: How much weight can each drawer hold?**
A: The ball-bearing slides are rated for roughly 75-100 lbs per drawer, which is generous for paper. Your floor will give out before these do.

**Q: Is it really all metal?**
A: The frame, drawers, and slides are metal. The handles are metal too. There's no particleboard in the construction, which is why it holds up.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need dependable, affordable filing storage and don't mind a little assembly effort. The metal construction and full-extension drawers deliver real value.

**Skip it if** you expect soft-close drawers, flawless out-of-box assembly, or furniture-grade aesthetics.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid workhorse with honest limitations. It won't win design awards, but it'll hold your files for years.
