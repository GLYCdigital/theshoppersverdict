Let me be blunt: if you own a power recliner, you've probably cursed the wall wart transformer that forces you to position furniture within arm's reach of an outlet. The SWANPOW 2500mAh battery pack promises to cut that cord permanently. It mostly delivers — with some asterisks you need to know about before clicking buy.

**What's in the box:** The battery unit itself, a USB-A to barrel plug charging cable, and a short adapter cord. That's it. No wall charger is included, which is mildly annoying since you'll need a 5V/2A USB adapter you probably already own. The unit measures about 5.5 x 3 x 1 inches and weighs under a pound, so it tucks neatly behind most recliners or inside the base cavity.

**Performance and real-world use:** The install genuinely takes 30 seconds. Unplug your recliner's factory power adapter, plug in the SWANPOW's barrel connector, and zip-tie the battery somewhere out of sight. That's the entire process. The 2500mAh cell (which is 12V, not the 5V USB voltage — important distinction) outputs enough current to move even heavier three-position recliners without straining. In my testing, a standard dual-motor recliner got roughly 55 full reclines on a single charge. That's about a week of nightly use for most households.

The USB recharge is where the experience sours. Full charge takes 4-6 hours, and there's no LED indicator showing charge status beyond a simple red/green light on the unit. When that light goes green, you're done — but you'll be guessing at intermediate levels. If you drain it completely, you're looking at an evening without recliner power. Plan ahead.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The convenience factor dominates positive reviews. As one verified purchaser put it: "Finally moved my recliner away from the wall and it looks 10x better. Charging once a week is nothing." Multiple reviewers specifically mention how quiet the unit is compared to some competing packs, and the universal barrel plug fits nearly every major brand — Bestar, Southern Motion, and most import models included.

**Where it falls short:** The 3.6-star average isn't random noise. Roughly 15-20% of critical reviews mention units that arrived dead or died within the first month. That's a meaningful quality control failure. Another common complaint: the plastic housing feels thin, and one reviewer reported the barrel connector came loose from the PCB after three months of daily use. The lack of any over-discharge protection also means you can kill the battery permanently if you run it to zero and leave it that way for weeks.

**Who should buy this:** 
- Anyone with a power recliner positioned more than 6 feet from an outlet who's tired of extension cords.
- RV or camper owners who want recliner functionality off-grid.
- Renters who can't install outlets where furniture actually looks good.

**Skip it if:** You expect daily multi-hour use (the recharge cycle will frustrate you), or you're not comfortable with the possibility of a DOA unit — buy from a seller with easy returns.

**FAQ:**

*Q: Will this work with my Lazy Boy power recliner?*
A: If your chair uses a standard 12V DC barrel plug (most do), yes. If it uses a proprietary connector, you'll need an adapter.

*Q: How long does one charge last?*
A: Expect 50-60 full recline cycles, roughly 5-7 days for a typical household.

*Q: Can I use it while charging?*
A: Technically yes, but the charging current is low, so performance during simultaneous use is sluggish.

**The Verdict:**
**Buy it if** you want furniture placement freedom and can tolerate a weekly charging ritual. **Skip it if** you need reliability above all else — the QC gamble is real.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — A genuinely useful product undermined by inconsistent manufacturing and slow recharging. Worth it for the convenience if you win the quality lottery.
