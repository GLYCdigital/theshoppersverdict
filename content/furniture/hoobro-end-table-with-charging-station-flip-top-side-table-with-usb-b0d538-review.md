**The Hoobro Flip-Top End Table: A Smart Space-Saver With a Catch or Two**

Most end tables are furniture. This one is more of a *tool*. The Hoobro End Table with Charging Station earns its keep by doing two jobs at once — hiding your living room clutter and keeping your devices juiced up without a tangle of floor-level cords. But as with any multi-function piece, the execution has a few rough edges worth knowing about before you hit "add to cart."

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the table top, side panels, a bottom shelf, the flip-top mechanism, and a power module with two AC outlets and two USB ports. The unit measures roughly 19.7" square and stands about 23.6" tall — a compact footprint that fits beside a standard sofa or armchair without dominating the room. The finish is a smooth, matte composite wood that resists fingerprints, and the manufacturer offers it in a few colorways, though the classic black walnut look is the most versatile.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

The headline feature is the flip top. Press down on the front edge, and the entire surface tilts up to reveal a hidden compartment — deep enough for a laptop up to 15 inches, a stack of magazines, or the remote control graveyard that accumulates on every coffee table. In practice, the hinge mechanism works smoothly once assembled, and the top locks firmly in place when closed. You can also flip it fully backward to use the table as a standing-height laptop desk, which is genuinely useful for a quick work session on the couch.

The charging station sits on the side panel, not inside the compartment — a smart call, because you can plug in your phone without lifting the lid. The two AC outlets and two USB ports are rated for standard 5V/2A output. That's fine for overnight charging or older devices, but if you're expecting USB-C Power Delivery or Qualcomm Quick Charge, you'll be disappointed. A modern flagship phone plugged in here will charge at roughly half the speed of a wall adapter. It's adequate, not impressive.

**What Buyers Praise**

Early adopters consistently highlight the space-saving design. One reviewer noted, *"This table replaced both my nightstand and my charging station — the top lifts up and hides my Kindle and glasses, and my phone charges right on the side. Perfect for a small apartment."* Another echoed the sentiment: *"The flip top is the selling point. I can work from the couch with my laptop on it, then close it and hide everything. It really does double duty."*

The build quality also gets solid marks for the price point. The composite wood is dense, not hollow-sounding, and the feet have adjustable levelers to handle uneven floors.

**Where It Falls Short**

Assembly is the biggest friction point. The flip-top hinge requires precise alignment — several users report spending 45 minutes to an hour getting it to open and close smoothly. If you're not handy with a screwdriver, budget extra time or recruit a second person.

The lack of cable management is a genuine oversight. The power cord exits the side panel and just hangs down to the outlet. There's no groove or channel to tuck it along the back, so it's visible from certain angles. For a "charging station" product, that's a miss.

And as mentioned, the USB ports are dated. If you're buying this specifically to fast-charge a modern phone, you'll want to plug into the AC outlet instead and use your own adapter.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Small-space dwellers** — apartments and dorms where every square foot counts
- **Couch workers** — anyone who regularly uses a laptop from the sofa and needs a stable, hideaway surface
- **Cable-haters** — people tired of crawling under furniture to plug in devices

**Skip it if** you need fast charging, want a piece that looks high-end up close (the composite wood is fine, not furniture-grade), or you're not comfortable with a moderately involved assembly.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can the flip top hold a heavy laptop?**
A: Yes — the top is rated for about 22 pounds. A 15-inch laptop, a mug of coffee, and a phone will be fine. Don't sit on it.

**Q: Does the charging station work when the table is unplugged?**
A: No. The USB and AC ports require the included power cable to be plugged into a wall outlet. There's no battery backup.

**Q: Can I mount this to the wall?**
A: The Hoobro is designed as a freestanding table only. There are no wall-mount brackets or keyhole slots.

**The Verdict: 4.0/5 — Buy It If You Value Hidden Storage Over Fast Charging**

The Hoobro end table is a genuinely clever piece of furniture that solves a real problem: where to put your stuff and how to charge it without a cable disaster. The flip-top mechanism is the standout feature, and the side-mounted power module is well-positioned for daily use. But the dated USB ports and missing cable management keep it from being a slam dunk.

**Buy it if** you need a compact, multi-functional table that hides clutter and handles basic device charging. **Skip it if** fast charging is a must — in that case, just buy a basic end table and a separate GaN wall charger. At its price point, though, the Hoobro delivers solid value for the space-conscious shopper.
