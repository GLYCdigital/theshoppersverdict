You just dropped $1,200 on a power recliner. The last thing you want is the motor dying in month 14 with no recourse. That's exactly the gap ASURION's 3-Year Furniture Protection Plan tries to fill — and for the most part, it does.

This isn't a warranty that just covers manufacturing defects. It's an accident protection plan, which means spilled coffee, torn upholstery, and broken mechanisms fall under its umbrella. Priced between $100 and $125 depending on your furniture's value tier, it's the kind of insurance that either pays for itself with one claim or feels like a donation to corporate profits. Here's what you need to know before clicking add to cart.

**What You Actually Get**

The plan activates after your manufacturer's warranty expires, giving you three years of coverage from your purchase date. It covers mechanical and electrical failures on power furniture — think recliner motors, lift chair mechanisms, massage units — plus accidental damage from everyday use. Stains, rips, burns, and structural breaks from normal wear are included.

You register the plan through ASURION's portal using your order number. The coverage is tied to the specific piece of furniture, not the buyer, and it's transferable if you sell the item mid-plan. That's a genuine plus for anyone who resells furniture.

**How It Performs in Real Life**

The claims process is refreshingly modern. File online or through the app, upload photos of the damage, and get a decision typically within 48 hours. ASURION's network of technicians handles repairs; if it can't be fixed, they cut a reimbursement check for the current value or replace the item.

What buyers consistently praise is the speed. One Amazon reviewer noted, "Filed a claim on my power recliner's motor on a Tuesday, technician was out Thursday, fixed by Friday. Painless." Another mentioned, "They didn't fight me on the stain claim — just asked for photos and approved it."

But here's the less glossy side. The 4.1-star average (204 ratings) tells you not everyone walks away happy. The most common complaints center on the deductible structure. Some claim types carry a service fee that isn't clearly disclosed at purchase. Others report that "accidental damage" interpretations vary by adjuster — what one approves, another might deny.

**Where It Falls Short**

The exclusion list deserves your attention. Pet urine and ink stains are not covered. Neither is damage from improper assembly or misuse. If you've got a dog with a bladder control problem and a ballpoint pen habit, this plan won't save your sofa.

Also, this is reimbursement-based, not replacement-based. You won't get a brand-new identical couch. You'll get the depreciated value or a repair, whichever is cheaper for ASURION. That's standard for protection plans, but it's worth knowing going in.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Power furniture owners.** If your recliner or lift chair has motors, gears, or electrical components, this plan is a no-brainer. Repairs on those mechanisms routinely run $200-$400, so the math works.
- **Households with kids.** Crayons, juice boxes, and general chaos. The stain and tear coverage pays for itself quickly.
- **Anyone buying a floor model.** Floor models often have hidden wear. This plan gives you a safety net for issues that surface after you get it home.

**Who Should Skip It**

- **Static furniture buyers.** A basic wooden dining chair or bookcase doesn't need this. The mechanical and electrical coverage is the real value, and you won't use it.
- **Pet owners with accident-prone animals.** The exclusions on pet damage make this a weaker bet for you. Look for a plan with broader pet coverage.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this cover scratches on wooden furniture?**
A: Yes, surface damage from everyday use is covered, including scratches and scuffs. But it won't cover pre-existing damage or defects from improper assembly.

**Q: Can I cancel and get a refund?**
A: You can cancel within 30 days for a full refund. After that, you'll get a prorated refund based on time remaining in the coverage period.

**Q: How long does a claim take to process?**
A: Most claims are approved within 48 hours of submitting photos. Technician visits typically happen within a week, depending on your area.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you own power furniture or have an active household. The mechanical and electrical coverage alone justifies the cost for motorized pieces. **Skip it if** you're insuring a static bookshelf or you have pets prone to accidents — you'll be paying for coverage you can't use.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid coverage with a smooth claims process, docked slightly for the exclusion fine print and occasional adjuster inconsistency. Worth the money for the right furniture.
