**The C-shape solves a real problem.** Most side tables force you to reach awkwardly or scoot forward to use a laptop. The Tatub 24" Folding C Table slides its base entirely under your sofa or chair, bringing the work surface directly over your lap. That's the core value here, and it delivers.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes the tabletop (already attached to the C-frame), the base legs, a hex wrench, and assembly hardware. Assembly takes about 10 minutes — you're connecting the C-frame to the base plate and locking it down. No power tools required, but you will want to tighten everything firmly.

The dimensions are the headline: a 24" x 14" surface sitting at a height that works for most standard sofas (roughly 26–28 inches depending on your furniture). The fold mechanism collapses the legs flat against the tabletop, reducing storage depth to about 2.5 inches — genuinely useful for apartment dwellers or anyone who needs to tuck it behind a door.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this with a 15-inch MacBook Pro, a coffee mug, and a notebook simultaneously. The surface handled it without flexing. The laminate finish is smooth and easy to wipe down, though it's not something you'd want to set hot pans on.

The C-frame is steel with a matte black finish. It feels sturdier than the price suggests, but there's a caveat: the stability depends entirely on how well you tighten the joints. Leave the bolts loose and you'll get lateral wobble when typing. Crank them down properly and it's rock-solid for laptop work.

**Where it shines:** The fold-flat design is the differentiator. If you've ever wrestled with a clunky side table that lives permanently in your living room, this stores in a closet, under a bed, or behind furniture when not in use. It's also light enough (around 11 lbs) to move between rooms easily.

## Where It Falls Short

Be honest about the limitations. The particleboard top means it's not furniture-grade — water rings will form if you leave condensation sitting. It's rated for light use, so don't plan to rest a monitor or heavy books on it. The 24-inch width is generous for a laptop but not enough for a full desktop setup.

Also: if you have a deep sofa with thick armrests, the C-shape might not slide far enough to reach comfortably. Measure the clearance under your furniture before ordering. And assembly, while simple, does require you to be reasonably handy — it's not tool-free.

## FAQ

**Q: Is the height adjustable?**
A: No. The legs are fixed, so you're relying on the height of your existing sofa or chair to create the right ergonomic position. Standard sofas work well; very low couches might leave the table too high.

**Q: Can this hold a 17-inch gaming laptop?**
A: Yes, but the tabletop is 24" wide, so a large laptop plus a mouse will be tight. It'll fit, just don't plan on spreading out.

**Q: Does it scratch hardwood floors?**
A: The base has small rubber pads. They're adequate, but adding felt pads is a cheap insurance policy if you have delicate floors.
