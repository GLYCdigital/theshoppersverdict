I'll be straight with you: most "egg chairs" under $200 are flimsy, rattan-lookalike junk that wobbles the moment you sit down. The Yaheetech Egg Rattan Chair is not that. It's also not a $1,200 Crate & Barrel statement piece. It sits somewhere in the middle — and for most buyers, that's exactly the right spot.

Here's what you actually get: a deep, enclosed basket seat wrapped in all-weather PE rattan over a powder-coated steel frame. The weave is tight and uniform — no gaping holes or loose strands like you see on cheaper knockoffs. The basket measures roughly 43 inches tall, so it reads as a substantial piece of furniture, not a backyard afterthought. A matching cushion set (seat and backrest) comes in the box, along with all mounting hardware.

**Assembly is the real test of patience.** The frame arrives in multiple pieces, and aligning the bolt holes while holding the basket steady is a two-person job. One reviewer summed it up: *"Took my husband and I 40 minutes and some choice words, but once it's together, it's rock solid."* That's the consensus — frustrating to build, but the end result feels genuinely stable. The 265-pound weight capacity is accurate; I've seen multiple verified buyer photos of larger adults sitting comfortably without any frame flex.

**Where this chair shines is the lounging experience.** The high back and enclosed sides wrap around you in a way that a standard patio chair simply doesn't. It's a reading nook, a phone-scrolling pod, and a nap station all in one. The PE rattan handles sun, rain, and frost without deteriorating — several owners report two-plus years outdoors with zero cracking or fading. That's the single biggest reason to choose this over natural wicker.

**But let's talk about the downsides honestly.** First, the included cushions are thin — two inches of foam that won't win any comfort awards. Most long-term owners replace them within a month. Budget an extra $30–50 for decent replacement cushions. Second, there's no footrest, and the seat is deep enough that shorter users (under 5'4") will find their feet hovering. A small ottoman solves this, but it's an extra purchase. Third, the chair is heavy — around 25 pounds — so moving it around the patio isn't a one-finger job.

**Who should buy this?** Three specific buyer profiles:
- **Renters** who want a stylish reading nook without drilling into walls or committing to built-in furniture
- **Patio owners** in rainy or snowy climates who want the wicker look without wicker's fragility
- **Anyone under 5'8"** who wants a cocoon-style chair; taller folks may find the top rim hits at an awkward shoulder height

**Who should skip it?** If you're over 5'10" or need a chair for a small apartment balcony, the footprint (35" diameter) is bigger than it looks in photos. Also, if you want a chair that arrives ready to use in ten minutes, this isn't it.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this hold up in direct sun and heavy rain?**
Yes. The PE rattan is UV-stabilized and waterproof. Multiple verified reviews confirm it survives full seasons outdoors without fading or cracking. The steel frame is rust-resistant, though it's wise to keep the base off standing water.

**Q: Can one person assemble this alone?**
Technically yes, but you'll regret it. The basket needs to be held at a specific angle while bolts are threaded through the frame. Use a second person or heavy-duty zip ties to hold pieces in place temporarily.

**Q: Do the cushions stay in place?**
The backrest cushion stays put thanks to tie straps. The seat cushion slides slightly when you shift — a common complaint. A strip of non-slip shelf liner underneath fixes it permanently.
