If you've slept on a factory sofa bed mattress, you know the drill: a thin slab of foam over metal bars that leaves you feeling like you spent the night on a park bench. The ArtleTa-Zg 4.5-inch cool gel memory foam replacement is designed to fix that — and based on 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, it's largely succeeding. But is it the right upgrade for your specific sofa? Let's dig in.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains a single 4.5-inch thick memory foam slab, compressed and vacuum-sealed into a roll roughly the size of a large camping duffel. You're not getting a cover, a carrying case, or any accessories — just the foam core itself, which measures 75 inches long and 54 inches wide (standard full/sleeper dimensions, though you should measure your existing mattress to confirm compatibility before ordering).

The foam has two distinct layers visible in the product images: a top layer infused with cooling gel beads and a denser support layer underneath. The gel infusion isn't just marketing — it changes the feel. The surface stays noticeably cooler to the touch than standard memory foam, and several reviewers specifically mention this as the reason they bought it. One buyer noted, *"My husband runs hot and would wake up drenched on our old mattress. This one actually sleeps cool — first night he didn't complain once."*

## Real-World Performance

The 4.5-inch thickness is the key engineering decision here. Too thin (2-3 inches) and you're still feeling the metal bars underneath. Too thick (6+ inches) and the mattress won't fold properly into your sofa bed frame. At 4.5 inches, it hits a workable middle ground — dense enough to provide genuine pressure relief for side sleepers, but pliable enough to fold away when converting back to couch mode.

Firmness lands around a medium. It's not plush-soft like a hotel bed, but it's a significant upgrade from the firm, unyielding foam that comes standard with most pull-out sofas. Back sleepers will find adequate support; side sleepers may want a pillow between their knees to maintain hip alignment. If you prefer a truly soft bed, this isn't it — but for a guest bed that needs to serve double duty as a couch, it's the right balance.

The foam expands fully within 24 hours of unrolling, though you'll want to give it a full 48 hours to reach maximum loft. Several reviewers note the initial off-gassing smell — that chemical-plastic odor common to compressed foam products. It dissipates in 2-3 days with windows open, but if you're sensitive to smells or installing this in a guest room right before visitors arrive, plan ahead.

The dominant theme in positive reviews is the comfort upgrade. ## Where It Falls Short

The lack of a removable cover is the most significant drawback. The foam is bare, which means you're either sleeping directly on the gel-infused surface or you need to buy a separate mattress protector. That's an added cost most shoppers don't anticipate.

The off-gassing is real, though temporary. If you're installing this the same day guests arrive, the chemical smell will be noticeable. It's not toxic or dangerous, but it's unpleasant — give yourself a few days of airing out.

Finally, this isn't a universal fit. Full-size sofa beds vary in their frame dimensions, and if your existing mattress is an unusual size (some older sleeper sofas use non-standard measurements), you'll need to trim the foam with an electric knife — doable, but not ideal.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have a standard full-size sofa bed with a worn-out or uncomfortable mattress, and you want an affordable upgrade without buying a whole new sofa. It's also a good fit if you're a hot sleeper who avoids memory foam for that reason — the gel infusion genuinely helps.

**Skip it if:** You need a truly plush, hotel-soft sleeping surface, or if your sofa bed frame is older and uses non-standard mattress dimensions. Also skip if you're not prepared to buy a separate mattress cover, because the bare foam will need protection.

## FAQ

**Will this fit my IKEA sofa bed?**
Most IKEA sleeper sofas use standard full-size mattresses, so yes — but measure your existing mattress first. IKEA has used slightly different dimensions on some older models, and a 75x54-inch replacement won't fit a frame designed for 74x53.

**How long does it take to fully expand?**
The foam reaches usable thickness within a few hours, but full expansion takes 24-48 hours. Don't judge the comfort level on day one — it firms up as it expands.

**Can I cut this to fit a smaller frame?**
Yes, with an electric carving knife. Score the foam along your cut line, then slice through slowly. The gel layer cuts cleanly, but take your time to avoid jagged edges.
