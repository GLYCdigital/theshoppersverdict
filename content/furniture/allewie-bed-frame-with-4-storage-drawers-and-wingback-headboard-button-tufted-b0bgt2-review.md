**The short version:** If you're shopping for a bed frame that hides clutter and looks twice its price, the Allewie with four storage drawers delivers. But it's not a one-person, 20-minute build — plan for a Saturday morning and a helper.

## What you actually get

The box arrives heavy — roughly 90 pounds for a queen. Inside you'll find the assembled wingback headboard, the footboard, two side rails, and four pre-built drawers on metal roller tracks. The upholstery is a linen-look polyester that reads more expensive than it is, with button tufting across the headboard that gives it that boutique-hotel vibe shown in the product images.

No box spring required. The slats are included and sit in a metal frame, so your mattress goes directly on top. That's a genuine cost savings if you're upgrading from a frame-and-box-spring setup.

## Real-world performance

The storage is the headline feature, and it earns the hype. Each drawer measures roughly 20" x 15" x 6" — deep enough for folded sweaters, extra bedding, or off-season shoes. The metal tracks roll smoothly even when loaded. One Amazon reviewer noted, *"I was skeptical about the drawers holding weight, but I've got heavy blankets in two of them and they still glide like new."* That matches the 4.5-star consensus across 225 ratings.

The frame itself is sturdy. Steel support legs run down the center, and the headboard attaches with brackets that don't wobble once tightened. If you're someone who sits up in bed reading, the wingback design gives real back support — it's not purely decorative.

## Where it falls short

Assembly is the biggest hurdle. The instructions are picture-only, and the drawer alignment takes fiddling. Plan for 60–90 minutes with two people. One buyer put it plainly: *"Great bed, but my husband and I almost divorced putting it together."* The drawers also lack soft-close hardware — they roll fine, but they'll bang if you're not careful.

Two smaller gripes: the headboard is fixed at a set height, so if you have an unusually thick mattress (14"+), the proportions look slightly off. And the upholstery is stain-prone — a spill on the headboard needs immediate attention, and there's no removable cover.

## Who should buy this

- **Small-apartment dwellers** who need extra storage without adding a bulky dresser — this replaces both.
- **Guest-room outfitters** who want a furnished, polished look without dropping $1,500+.
- **Renters** who want a stylish frame that breaks down if they move — it disassembles into manageable pieces.

**Skip it if** you hate assembly, need a headboard that adjusts to different mattress heights, or expect commercial-grade drawer damping.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the frame require a box spring?**
No. Slats are included and support the mattress directly. Memory foam, hybrid, and innerspring mattresses all work.

**Q: Will the drawers clear area rugs?**
The drawers sit about 1.5" off the floor, so they'll catch on thick shag rugs. On low-pile or hardwood, they roll cleanly.

**Q: What's the weight capacity?**
The frame supports up to 800 pounds distributed — fine for a couple plus kids jumping on the bed occasionally.
