**What you're actually buying**

The Leesa mattress in a box isn't a mystery box. The 10-inch profile breaks down into three distinct layers: a 2-inch top comfort layer of proprietary foam designed for pressure relief, a 2-inch middle transition layer that adds support and bounce, and a 6-inch high-density base foam that does the heavy lifting. The cover is a soft, stretchy polyester blend that's removable and washable — a detail that matters more than you'd think.

It ships compressed, rolled, and wrapped in plastic. Unboxing takes about 10 minutes: cut the plastic, unroll, and give it 24 hours to fully expand. It will smell faintly of "new mattress" for the first couple of days — that dissipates, but it's worth knowing if you're sensitive to odors.

**How it actually performs**

The 4.1-star average across 204 ratings tells a story of general satisfaction with some caveats. What buyers consistently praise is the pressure relief. The top layer cradles hips and shoulders without turning into a hammock. Side sleepers — especially those in the 150-200 pound range — report waking up without the usual shoulder or hip ache. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I've had this for six months. My chronic lower back pain is gone. I was skeptical about a mattress in a box, but this is legit."*

The transition layer adds enough responsiveness that you're not fighting the mattress to change positions. It's not bouncy — don't expect innerspring spring. But it's not dead-sink either. That middle ground makes it a solid choice for combination sleepers who move around at night.

The CertiPUR-US certification is real, not marketing fluff. Verified labs test for heavy metals, formaldehyde, and ozone depleters. If you're chemically sensitive or buying for a kid's room, that's a meaningful box to check.

**Where it falls short**

Here's the honest part. Edge support is mediocre. Sit on the edge to tie your shoes and you'll slide toward the floor. If you're a couple who needs the full usable surface, that's a real tradeoff. Also, while the cover breathes better than most, this is still an all-foam mattress. Hot sleepers will want a cooling mattress pad in summer months. And there's only one firmness — a medium that leans slightly firm. Strict stomach sleepers, especially heavier folks, will likely want something firmer. The lack of options is a limitation, not a flaw.

**Who should buy this**

Buy it if: You're a side or combination sleeper, you're on a budget, you want a mattress you can bring into an apartment without a truck, and you value verified materials over marketing claims. It's also a strong pick for guest rooms — the medium firmness is the most universally tolerable setting, and the all-foam construction dampens motion transfer if a guest is a restless mover.

Skip it if: You're over 220 pounds and need deep support (the 6-inch base foam will feel too soft), you're a strict stomach sleeper, you hate any initial off-gassing, or you frequently sit on the edge of the bed.

**FAQ**

**How long does it take to fully expand?** Plan for 24 hours. It'll be usable after a few hours, but the edges and corners need a full day to reach their final shape. The box says "allow up to 24 hours to fully inflate" — that's accurate.

**Is it good for back pain?** Depends on your sleeping position. Side sleepers with lower back issues report real relief. Back sleepers generally do fine. If you're a stomach sleeper, the medium-firm feel might not give enough lumbar support — look at firmer options.

**Does it sleep hot?** Warmer than a hybrid or innerspring, cooler than most budget memory foam. The breathable cover helps, but if you're a heavy night-sweater, budget for a cooling topper.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a certified, well-constructed foam mattress that handles pressure relief and motion isolation without emptying your wallet. **Skip it if** you need firm support, strong edge hold, or run hot while sleeping.

At a 4.2/5, this is a solid, honest mattress that does what it claims — no more, no less. For the price point, you're getting legitimate materials, verified construction, and a comfort profile that suits the majority of sleepers. Just know its limits before you buy.

If you're on the fence between this and a hybrid, pay the extra for the hybrid if edge support and cooling are priorities. If you want a straightforward, reliable foam bed that ships to your door — this is it.
