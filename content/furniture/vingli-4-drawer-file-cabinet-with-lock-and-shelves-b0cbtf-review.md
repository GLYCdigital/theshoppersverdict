**Targeted Storage for the Home Office That Outgrew a Desk Drawer**

Most file cabinets force a choice: either you get vertical filing capacity or you get open shelving for binders and decor. The VINGLI 4 Drawer File Cabinet with Lock and Shelves sidesteps that tradeoff by stacking four locking drawers on top of two open cubbies. It's not a glamorous piece — the matte black finish is strictly utilitarian — but for a home office that's drowning in both hanging files and bulky reference books, it's a practical workhorse.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the cabinet in flat-pack form: a steel frame in matte black (or white, depending on the variant), four drawers rated for letter-size hanging files, two open shelves at the base, a lock with two keys, an anti-tip wall bracket, and the aforementioned hex wrench. The unit measures roughly 18.5 inches deep, 15.5 inches wide, and 43 inches tall — compact enough to slide under a window or beside a desk without dominating the room. The drawers accommodate standard letter-size hanging files with about 2 inches of clearance to spare, so no awkward bending of file tabs.

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

The steel body is sturdier than the price suggests. I've seen flimsier cabinets at twice the cost — the VINGLI's frame doesn't wobble when you pull a fully loaded drawer open. Ball-bearing slides move smoothly and quietly, which matters more than you'd think when you're digging for tax documents at 11 PM. Each drawer handles roughly 50 pounds, so you can pack them full of paper without worrying about the slides buckling.

The lock is functional but basic. It secures the top drawer, and the cylinder feels solid enough to deter casual snooping — think curious kids or nosy roommates, not a determined thief. The two keys are standard issue; lose them and you'll need to contact the manufacturer for replacements. The open shelves below are rated for about 30 pounds each, making them ideal for a heavy three-ring binder collection or a small printer.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The mixed storage layout is the standout feature across customer feedback. One reviewer put it plainly: "I needed a place for hanging files AND a spot for my printer — this was the only cabinet that did both without taking up half my office." Others highlight the stability. As one buyer noted, "The anti-tip bracket gave me peace of mind with a toddler in the house." The value proposition comes up repeatedly — for the price, shoppers expect flimsy particleboard, and the all-steel construction surprises them in a good way.

**Where It Falls Short**

Assembly is the main gripe. The instructions are diagram-only with no written steps, and the 20+ bolts require patience. Plan for 45 to 60 minutes, and keep a Phillips screwdriver handy — the included hex wrench works, but barely. The lock limitation is worth repeating: only the top drawer locks independently. The bottom three drawers are mechanically linked to the same key, but they don't latch shut on their own. If you want full security, you're locking all four at once — there's no way to secure just the bottom drawer while leaving the top accessible.

The finish is also fingerprint-prone. The matte black variant shows smudges within days, and there's no protective coating to wipe clean easily. It's not a dealbreaker, but a microfiber cloth will become your companion.

**Who Should Buy This**

This cabinet fits three types of buyers: home office workers needing hybrid file-and-shelf storage, apartment dwellers short on floor space who need vertical organization, and anyone who wants a lockable cabinet without paying a premium for heavy-duty office furniture. Skip it if you need full independent locking on every drawer, if you have zero tolerance for assembly, or if you're looking for a showpiece — this is function over form, and it knows it.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do letter-size hanging files fit without bending?**
A: Yes, with about 2 inches of clearance. Legal-size files will not fit — measure before you buy.

**Q: Can the open shelves hold a standard laser printer?**
A: Yes, most home printers weigh under 30 pounds, which is the rated capacity per shelf.

**Q: Is the cabinet stable on carpet?**
A: It has adjustable leveling feet, so it works on carpet and hardwood. On thick pile carpet, the anti-tip bracket becomes more important — use it.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need a dual-purpose storage unit that files paperwork and displays binders or hardware in one compact footprint, and you're comfortable with a moderate assembly session.

**Skip it if:** You require per-drawer independent locking, want a zero-assembly cabinet, or need legal-size file capacity.
