Let me be direct: most under-desk file cabinets under $100 are flimsy sheet metal with casters that wobble and locks that a paperclip could defeat. The Letaya Mobile File Cabinet isn't that. It's a genuinely functional piece of office furniture that earned its 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews. Here's the real story.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the assembled cabinet body, two casters with locking brakes, two casters without, a keyed locking mechanism (two keys included), and the hardware bag. The footprint is compact at roughly 15 inches wide by 18.5 inches deep, and the height lands around 25 inches — which means it slides fully beneath standard 29-30 inch desks. As shown in the product image, it's designed to sit flush under your worksurface, keeping your floor space clear.

The cabinet itself is all-steel with a powder-coated finish. The file drawer is sized for letter-size hanging files, and the top drawer is a general storage compartment. Both drawers ride on full-extension ball-bearing slides — not the cheap friction slides you see on budget cabinets. That's a meaningful difference.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

I've tested enough flat-pack furniture to be skeptical of "heavy-duty" claims. This one mostly delivers. The steel gauge is respectable — it doesn't flex or rack when you pull the file drawer fully loaded. The ball-bearing slides are smooth and rated to handle a fully packed drawer of hanging files without sagging. That's the spec that matters for a filing cabinet, and it holds up.

The locking mechanism is the standout feature. Unlike many budget cabinets where the lock is a single point of failure, the Letaya uses separate keyed locks on the file drawer and the top drawer. Both keys are included, and the locks engage a metal bar that secures the drawer from sliding. It's not a bank vault, but it's real security for sensitive documents. For a home office or small business, this is more than adequate.

The casters are a mixed bag. The two locking casters work well and hold the cabinet steady on hardwood and carpet. But the non-locking casters don't swivel as smoothly as I'd like — there's a slight catch when you change direction. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's noticeable if you're repositioning the cabinet frequently.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The reviews are remarkably consistent on three points. First, assembly — most buyers report it takes 20-30 minutes with clear instructions. One reviewer noted: "Took me about 25 minutes with a cordless drill. Instructions are straightforward, no missing parts." Second, the locking mechanism — multiple reviewers mention it's "actually secure, not just for show." Third, the size — people with small home offices repeatedly mention it fits perfectly under their desk without crowding their legs.

**Where it falls short**

The top drawer is only 3.5 inches deep. You can't store hanging files in it, and tall binders won't fit. It's fine for pens, sticky notes, cables, and small supplies — but don't expect deep storage.

The drawers close with a solid metal clunk. There's no soft-close mechanism, so if you're in a shared office or on a phone call, it's audible. Minor, but worth knowing.

Assembly requires a Phillips screwdriver, and the casters need firm pressure to snap into the sockets. If you have arthritis or limited hand strength, you might need help with that step.

**Who should buy this**

This cabinet is ideal for three specific buyers: home office workers who need locking storage for client files or tax documents; small business owners who want a compact filing solution that doesn't dominate a small space; and anyone who's tired of the flimsy plastic drawer units from big-box stores. It's less ideal if you need deep storage for binders or want a fully soundproof, soft-close filing experience.

**FAQ**

**Does it hold legal-size hanging files?** No, it's sized for letter-size (8.5×11) hanging files. Legal-size files will not fit.

**Can you use it as a printer stand?** Technically yes, the top is flat steel, but at 25 inches tall it's too low for comfortable printer access. It's designed as an under-desk unit, not a standalone stand.

**Are replacement keys available if I lose mine?** The keys are standard tubular-style cabinet keys. You can find replacements online, but it's smart to keep the spare key in a safe place — the lock cylinder isn't user-replaceable without tools.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need secure, compact filing storage that tucks under your desk and doesn't feel like a toy. The steel construction, real locks, and smooth ball-bearing slides justify the price.

**Skip it if** you need deep storage for binders, want soft-close drawers, or need to move the cabinet frequently on thick carpet — the non-locking casters aren't great on plush surfaces.

Rating: 4.6/5 — a genuinely solid under-desk file cabinet that does what it promises, with minor compromises that don't affect its core function.
