**The Smart Bedside Table That Actually Earns Its Plug-In**

Your nightstand has one job: hold your phone, glasses, and that half-empty water glass. The HOMMPA LED Nightstand tries to do more — wirelessly charge your devices, light up your bedroom, and organize your chaos. After digging through 215 ratings and the specs, here's the real story.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the nightstand (two drawer units, legs, top panel), a power adapter, assembly hardware, and an instruction booklet. The footprint is compact — roughly 19.5 inches wide, 15.5 inches deep, and 20 inches tall — which fits most standard bedroom spaces without overwhelming a small room.

The headline feature is the integrated charging station. A 15W wireless charging pad sits flush on the top surface, flanked by two USB-A ports and one USB-C port on the side. The LED strip runs along the bottom edge, offering multiple colors and brightness levels controlled by a touch button on the front panel.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The high-gloss finish looks premium in photos and better in person — it reflects light nicely and gives the piece a modern, almost lacquered appearance. The particleboard construction feels solid for the price point; drawers slide smoothly on metal rails, and the legs have adjustable levelers for uneven floors.

Wireless charging works reliably with Qi-compatible phones. I tested it with a recent iPhone — it locked onto the charging coil within seconds and held a steady charge. The USB ports push out standard 5V/2.1A, which is fine for overnight charging but won't fast-charge newer phones. That's a minor limitation, not a dealbreaker.

The LED lighting is genuinely useful. The touch control cycles through white, warm white, and several color options. At the lowest setting, it's a soft glow that works as a nightlight without blinding you at 2 AM. Higher settings are bright enough to read by, which surprised me for a nightstand feature.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The charging integration and lighting are the standouts. One reviewer noted: *"I've had this for three months and my phone is always charged when I wake up — no more hunting for cables in the dark."* Another called the LED feature *"a game changer for middle-of-the-night bathroom trips."*

The assembly process earned mixed but generally positive feedback. Most buyers said it took 20-30 minutes with a screwdriver, and the predrilled holes aligned properly.

**Where It Falls Short**

The high-gloss surface is the biggest practical issue. It shows fingerprints, dust, and smudges almost immediately. If you're bothered by visible marks on furniture, budget for regular wiping — this isn't a "set it and forget it" finish.

The LED strip brightness at minimum is still noticeable in a completely dark room. Light-sensitive sleepers might want to position the nightstand away from direct line of sight or use the "off" setting entirely.

Assembly instructions are minimal — mostly diagrams with sparse text. It's manageable, but not the friendliest experience for a first-time furniture builder.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Tech-forward sleepers** who want their phone, watch, and earbuds charged overnight without a cable jungle
- **Apartment dwellers** needing a compact, multi-functional piece that saves floor space
- **Night-light users** who want adjustable ambient lighting without a separate lamp

**Skip It If** — you're a stickler for solid wood construction (this is particleboard), or you demand fast-charging speeds from your bedside ports.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the wireless charging work with phone cases?**
Yes, most standard cases under 5mm thickness work fine. Thick rugged cases or ones with metal components may interfere — test yours when it arrives.

**Q: Can the LED lights be set to turn off automatically?**
No, there's no timer function. You'll need to tap the touch button to turn them off manually each night.

**Q: Is the power adapter included?**
Yes, a standard wall adapter is in the box. No hardwiring needed — just plug it in.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a functional, good-looking nightstand that consolidates charging and lighting into one piece. The 4.3-star rating holds up — this is a solid mid-tier option that delivers on its core promises.

**Skip it if** you need fast charging, prefer real wood, or can't tolerate a surface that shows every fingerprint.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Recommended for most buyers, with minor caveats on finishes and charging speed.
