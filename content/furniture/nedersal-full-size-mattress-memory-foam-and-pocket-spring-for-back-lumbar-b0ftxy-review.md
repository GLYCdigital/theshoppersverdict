Let me be direct about the Nedersal Full Size Mattress: at its price point, you're not getting a luxury hotel bed. But you're also not getting the mushy, sagging foam slab that plagues most budget hybrids. This is a legitimate dual-layer construction — two inches of gel memory foam on top of a seven-inch pocket spring core — and it shows in the sleep experience.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The box contains the mattress compressed and rolled, plus a basic care guide. No fancy unboxing tools, no free pillows. Unroll it, cut the plastic, and give it 24 hours to fully expand — though most buyers report it reaches usable shape in about four hours. The cover is a standard polyester knit; nothing premium, but it's removable and machine-washable, which matters more than thread count for most people.

**The Real-World Performance**

The pocket springs are the star here. Each coil is individually wrapped, meaning when your partner shifts at 3 AM, you don't feel the ripple effect. One reviewer put it plainly: "My husband gets up twice a night and I never feel it anymore. That alone was worth the switch from our old innerspring." That's the core value proposition — independent motion isolation without the heat retention that plagues all-foam mattresses.

The memory foam layer is gel-infused, which does help with temperature regulation, but don't expect miracles. It sleeps cooler than pure memory foam, not as cool as a true latex or coil-top mattress. If you run hot, this is a step in the right direction, not the final destination.

Edge support deserves specific praise. The perimeter coils are firmer gauge, and it shows. Sitting on the edge to put on socks doesn't create that dreaded roll-off feeling. For a full-size mattress — which is often used in guest rooms where people sit on the edge to read or chat — this is a practical win.

**Where It Falls Short**

The firmness is the biggest surprise. The listing suggests "medium-firm," but most buyers report it skews firmer than expected. If you're a side sleeper under 150 pounds, you'll likely feel pressure at the shoulder and hip. Back and stomach sleepers will find it supportive. One customer summarized it well: "It's a great mattress for back sleepers but my wife (side sleeper, 130 lbs) found it too hard and we had to add a topper."

The off-gassing is real. Expect a noticeable chemical smell for the first two to three days. It's not toxic — just the volatile organic compounds from the foam manufacturing process releasing — but it's strong enough that you shouldn't sleep on it immediately. Air it out in a ventilated room.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Back and stomach sleepers** who want firm support without the sink of all-foam beds
- **Couples** (or anyone sharing a full-size bed) who need motion isolation on a budget
- **Guest room setups** where a versatile, medium-firm feel accommodates most visitors
- **Anyone upgrading from a worn-out innerspring** who wants the hybrid feel without the $800+ price tag

Skip it if you're a lightweight side sleeper, if you need serious pressure relief for joint issues, or if you're sensitive to off-gassing smells during the first week.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it require a box spring?**
No. The pocket spring core provides its own support. Place it on a solid platform base or slatted frame with slats no more than three inches apart. A box spring is unnecessary and can actually reduce the firmness benefits.

**Q: How long does it take to fully expand?**
Most buyers report usable shape within four to six hours, but full expansion takes 24 hours. The mattress reaches its true firmness profile after 48-72 hours, so don't judge the feel on night one.

**Q: Is the cover really removable and washable?**
Yes, the outer cover zips off. Machine wash on cold and air dry. Keep in mind the internal layers aren't washable — the cover is the only protection, so consider a mattress protector as well.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a firm-to-medium-firm hybrid with strong edge support and motion isolation, and you're okay with a few days of off-gassing.

**Skip it if:** You're a petite side sleeper needing plush pressure relief, or you can't tolerate any chemical smell during the break-in period.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — It's not perfect, but at this price, the Nedersal delivers exactly what it promises: a supportive, durable-feeling hybrid that outperforms its price class. The firmness bias and off-gassing are honest tradeoffs for the value.
