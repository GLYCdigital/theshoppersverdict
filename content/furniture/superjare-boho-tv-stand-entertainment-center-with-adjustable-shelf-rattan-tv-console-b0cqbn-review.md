## Is this the boho centerpiece your living room is missing?

The boho trend has flooded Amazon with lookalike TV stands—most of them just MDF boards with a rattan-print sticker slapped on the front. The SUPERJARE stands apart because it commits to the aesthetic with actual woven rattan door fronts. At 4.3 stars from 215 ratings, it's not perfect, but it's a genuine step up from the cheap imitators.

## What you actually get

The box contains the TV stand itself, four solid acacia wood legs, an adjustable interior shelf, and the hardware kit. No tools included, so have a Phillips screwdriver handy. The dimensions are a practical 55 inches wide, 15.7 inches deep, and 21.7 inches high—sized for TVs up to 65 inches, though I'd keep it to 55–60 inches for visual balance.

The two cabinet doors are the star feature. Each has a woven rattan panel set into a solid wood frame. The middle shelf slides to three different height positions, which is more flexible than most stands in this price range.

## Build quality and real-world performance

The frame is engineered wood with a walnut finish that reads convincingly as real wood from a few feet away. The acacia legs are the real deal—solid, substantial, and they screw in securely. One reviewer noted, "The legs feel sturdier than my previous stand that cost twice as much."

The rattan doors open smoothly on soft-close hinges. The adjustable shelf handles the height difference between a soundbar and a PlayStation 5 without issue. Weight capacity is rated at 110 pounds across the top surface, which is adequate for most modern TVs.

## What buyers consistently praise

The aesthetic is the overwhelming favorite. Multiple reviewers describe it as "exactly the boho look I wanted" and "a conversation starter." The natural rattan texture photographs beautifully and holds up better than expected in dry climates.

Assembly feedback is mostly positive, though the doors require patience. One detailed review mentioned: "The hinges took 20 minutes to align perfectly, but once set, the doors close flush every time."

## Where it falls short

The open back is the biggest practical complaint. Wires are visible from the side, and there's no built-in cable management. Plan on zip ties and a power strip with a long cord.

The rattan is delicate. One buyer reported, "A cleaning spray with bleach discolored the door panel—stick to a dry microfiber cloth." This isn't a stand for high-traffic homes with toddlers who might poke at the weave.

The walnut finish scratches more easily than expected. Use felt pads under heavy components.

## Who should buy this

- **Boho/eclectic decorators** who want authentic texture, not printed patterns
- **Apartment dwellers** needing a compact footprint that still holds a 55-inch TV
- **Gamers** who need adjustable shelf height for consoles and soundbars

**Skip it if** you need serious cable management, have aggressive cleaners in the house, or want a solid wood stand (this is engineered wood with real wood legs).

## FAQ

**Q: Does the TV stand work with a 65-inch TV?**
A: Technically yes, but the 55-inch width means a 65-inch TV will overhang the sides. For stability, stick to 60 inches and under.

**Q: Can the rattan doors be removed for an open-shelf look?**
A: Yes, the doors lift off the hinges. You'll lose the boho focal point, but gain open shelving flexibility.

**Q: How sturdy is it on carpet?**
A: The legs have adjustable levelers. One reviewer on plush carpet reported "zero wobble" after leveling.
