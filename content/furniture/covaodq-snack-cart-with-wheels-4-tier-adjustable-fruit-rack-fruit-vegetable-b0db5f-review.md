## Where It Falls Short

The wire shelves have a real flaw: no side rails. Small items — cherry tomatoes, loose granola bars, bags of nuts — can slip through the gaps or off the edges when you're wheeling the cart around. You'll want to use baskets or keep smaller items in containers.

The 2-inch casters are fine for hard floors but struggle on plush carpet. If your kitchen has thick pile, expect the cart to drag and catch. It's a minor issue for most, but worth knowing.
