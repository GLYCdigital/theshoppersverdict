**How it performs in the trenches**

**Where it falls short**

Assembly is straightforward but not tool-free. The four seat bolts need even tightening to avoid a slight wobble. One reviewer noted: *"Took me about 20 minutes to put together. The bolts are a little finicky, but once it's set, it's rock solid."* That matches the general consensus.

Also, this is a stool — there's no backrest. If you're planning marathon sessions that stretch past two hours, your lower back will feel it. That's not a flaw in the product; it's just physics. This is built for quick sessions and standing breaks, not eight-hour grinds.
