If you sleep on a split king, you already know the enemy: that cold, dark canyon running down the middle of your bed. The ENITYA Split King Gap Filler is a foam wedge designed to bridge that crevice. At 4.3 stars from 215 ratings, it's one of the more popular solutions — but does it actually stay put? I dug through the feedback and the specs to find out.

**What you actually get**

The box contains a single wedge of high-density foam (roughly 28 inches long) plus a removable cover made from a bamboo-blend fabric. The foam measures about 2 inches thick at its base, tapering to a thin edge — the idea being you slide the thick side under one mattress and let the thin side fill the gap. It's designed to accommodate splits ranging from 2 to 4 inches wide, which covers most adjustable base setups. No tools, no straps, no installation. You just position it and drop your mattress back down.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The foam density is the standout feature here. Multiple reviewers note it doesn't compress into a useless pancake after a few nights. One buyer put it simply: *"The foam is solid, not the cheap squishy stuff. It actually holds my weight when I roll to the middle."* That's the core job — bridging the gap so you don't sink into the crevice — and it does that well for most users.

The cover deserves a mention too. It's got a subtle texture that grips the mattress fabric reasonably well, and it zips off for cleaning. That's a practical touch, because the crevice between split mattresses collects dust and crumbs like a magnet.

**Where it falls short**

The honest tradeoff: this is a top-of-mattress solution, not an under-mattress one. Because the wedge sits on top of both mattresses, you'll feel a slight ridge along the seam. If you're a back sleeper who lands right on the center line, you'll notice the transition. Side sleepers who hug the edge of one mattress generally won't.

The other gripe is movement. Without any adhesive backing or strap system, the wedge can shift if you're an aggressive tosser or if you use a slick mattress protector. A few reviewers solved this by layering a fitted sheet tightly over everything, which holds the wedge in place. If you're on an adjustable base that you raise and lower frequently, expect to reposition the filler occasionally — it's not a set-and-forget solution.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for couples with a split king where one partner uses an adjustable base and the other doesn't — that's the most common cause of the gap. It's also a solid pick if you hate the "falling into the crack" sensation but don't want to drop money on a full mattress bridge or custom foam insert. If you're a stomach sleeper who lands dead-center on the seam, though, you might be better off with a full-length mattress gap filler that tucks under both mattresses for a flusher surface.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this work with a standard non-adjustable split king?**  
Yes. It doesn't require an adjustable base. As long as you have two twin XL mattresses with a 2-4 inch gap, it'll fill it.

**Q: Does it make noise when you roll over it?**  
No. The foam is solid and the cover is quiet — no crinkling or squeaking. The only sound you'll hear is the mattress itself.

**Q: Can I cut it to fit a smaller gap?**  
Technically yes, but the foam density makes it tough to cut cleanly with scissors. You'd need a serrated knife, and you'd lose the sewn cover's clean edge. Better to measure your gap first and buy accordingly.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You have a 2-4 inch split king gap, want a solution that arrives ready to use, and don't mind a slight ridge when you sleep directly on the seam.

**Skip it if:** You need a completely flush sleeping surface, use a very slick mattress protector, or raise your adjustable base every night and don't want to reposition the wedge.

The ENITYIA Split King Gap Filler does its primary job well — killing the crevice and holding its shape night after night. It's not a perfect flush system, but for most split king owners, it's a practical, affordable fix that removes the worst part of the setup.
