You've seen a hundred cross-back dining chairs online. Most are particle board with a printed wood grain. The East West Furniture Clarksville set is not that. Here's the honest breakdown after digging through specs and 225 buyer ratings.

**What ships to your door**

You order the "Double" and you get two identical chairs, each measuring 17.5" W x 21.5" D x 38.5" H. The seat sits at 18.5" — which matters more than most people realize. If your dining table is the standard 30" height, you'll have roughly 11.5" of leg clearance. That's comfortable for average-height adults, but if you're over 6'2", your knees might brush the underside of the table.

The frame is solid rubberwood. Not engineered wood. Not "wood composite." Actual hardwood that's kiln-dried and stained in a dark walnut tone. The cross-back design isn't decorative — it's structural, and it's what gives these chairs their rigidity. Each chair weighs about 15 pounds, which tells you there's real material here.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The joinery is mortise-and-tenon in the critical stress points, with dowel reinforcement where the legs meet the seat. That's the difference between a chair that wobbles after six months and one that stays solid for years. Multiple Amazon reviewers noted the stability specifically. One buyer wrote: *"I'm 230 pounds and these don't creak when I lean back. The cross-back actually flexes slightly, which feels intentional, not cheap."*

The wood seat is contoured — barely, but enough to notice. It's a subtle scoop that keeps you from sliding forward. That said, there's zero padding. For a quick breakfast or a 45-minute dinner, you're fine. For a Thanksgiving marathon or a board game night that stretches past two hours, you'll feel every minute. Buy a seat cushion if that's your scenario.

Assembly took me about 20 minutes per chair. The legs attach to the pre-drilled seat base with four bolts each. The included Allen wrench is serviceable but cheap — swap in your own hex key and you'll avoid stripping the bolt heads. You'll also need a Phillips screwdriver for the cross-back supports.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The finish is the star. The dark walnut stain is applied evenly with a matte clear coat — no orange-peel texture, no brush marks. It matches well with mid-century modern and farmhouse tables alike. Buyers repeatedly mention the color accuracy: *"The photos don't do it justice. It's darker and richer in person."*

The double-pack value is also a recurring theme. At roughly $85-100 per chair when bought as a pair, you're paying about 15-20% less than buying two singles. For solid hardwood with this build quality, that's genuinely competitive.

**Where it falls short**

The matte finish is a double-edged sword. It looks premium, but it's not sealed enough to shrug off moisture. Water rings appear if you leave a sweaty glass on the seat. Use coasters or placemats.

The lack of floor protectors on the leg bottoms is another oversight. On hardwood or tile, you'll want to add felt pads — the bare wood will scuff your floors over time. That's a $5 fix, but it's annoying to have to do it yourself.

**Who should buy this**

Buy the Clarksville chairs if you're furnishing a dining room with a wood table, you want something that'll hold up for a decade, and you don't mind adding cushions. They're also a solid pick for a breakfast bar — the 18.5" seat height works with 36" counter-height tables.

Skip them if you need upholstered comfort for long dinner parties, if you have a modern or industrial aesthetic that calls for metal or fabric, or if you're on a tight budget — there are cheaper chairs out there, but they won't be solid wood.

**FAQ**

*Do these chairs fit under a standard dining table?*  
Yes, the 21.5" depth tucks neatly under a 30" table with about 8" of clearance between the seat and table apron.

*Are they stackable?*  
No. The cross-back design prevents stacking. Plan for storage space.

*Will the wood crack in a humid environment?*  
The rubberwood is kiln-dried to 8% moisture content, which is standard for indoor furniture. As long as you don't leave them in direct sunlight or a damp garage, they'll hold up.

**The Verdict**

The East West Furniture Clarksville double pack delivers solid hardwood construction, an attractive finish, and real structural integrity at a fair price. It's not a luxury chair, but it's an honest, well-made piece of furniture that will outlive cheaper alternatives.

**Buy it if:** You want solid wood dining chairs that look expensive without the price tag, and you're okay adding your own cushioning.

**Skip it if:** You need all-day sitting comfort or you want a chair that requires zero assembly.
