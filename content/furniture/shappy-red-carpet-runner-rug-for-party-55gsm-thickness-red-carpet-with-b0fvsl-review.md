**What's in the box (and what isn't)**

You get the runner itself — that's it. No tape, no weights, no carrying case. **Real-world performance**

I've seen these used for wedding aisles, graduation parties, and church events. The 55gsm weight matters more than you'd think. At this thickness, the carpet lays flat without rippling, and it absorbs foot traffic without bunching up under people's shoes. The non-slip backing does its job on concrete and outdoor surfaces. **Where it falls short**

The creasing issue is real. These runners ship folded, and the creases don't disappear on their own. Plan to unroll it and let it sit flat for a day or two before your event, or you'll have visible lines running across your aisle. If you're setting up the morning of, you'll need to work the folds out by hand or pin them down — not ideal for last-minute setups.

Also, don't buy this thinking it's a permanent flooring solution. At 55gsm, it's designed for temporary use. One long-term reviewer reported visible wear after six weeks of daily hallway use — the material isn't meant to withstand that kind of constant traffic. For a weekend event or a month-long display, it's perfect. For a permanent runner, look at something in the 100gsm+ range.
