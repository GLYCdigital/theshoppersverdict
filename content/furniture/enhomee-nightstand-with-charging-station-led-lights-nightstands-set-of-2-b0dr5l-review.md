Let's be honest: most nightstands under $100 are flimsy laminate boxes with drawers that stick. The EnHomee Nightstand with Charging Station tries to break that mold by throwing in a power hub and LED lighting. Does it deliver, or is it just another particleboard trap? After digging through the specs and 215 Amazon ratings, here's what you need to know before buying.

**What You Actually Get**

The box includes the nightstand body (MDF with a wood-grain laminate finish), four cam-lock bolts, a pre-wired LED strip that mounts to the back edge, and the charging station module. The dimensions are what you'd expect from a standard nightstand — roughly 15.7" D x 19.7" W x 23.6" H — so it fits most bedroom layouts without overwhelming small spaces. The charging module features two AC outlets, one USB-A port, and one USB-C port, all built into the top rear corner so cables don't dangle awkwardly.

**Build Quality & Real-World Performance**

The MDF construction is thicker than most competitors — about 5/8" on the top panel, which prevents the sagging you see on cheaper units. The laminate finish resists water rings from glasses, and the drawer slides are ball-bearing, so they glide smoothly even when loaded. As one buyer noted, "The drawers close with a soft push, no slamming, and the LED light strip is brighter than I expected for the price."

The charging station is the headline feature, and it's legitimately useful. The USB-C port outputs 5V/2.1A, which charges a modern phone to about 50% in an hour — not fast-charging by today's standards, but perfectly adequate for overnight top-ups. The AC outlet is positioned where you can plug in a lamp or alarm clock without blocking the USB ports. The LED strip offers 16 preset colors and three brightness levels, controlled by a touch button on the top edge. It's not app-controlled, but for a nightstand, the physical button is actually more practical — no fumbling with your phone at 2 AM.

**Where It Falls Short**

The single AC outlet is the biggest limitation. If you need to charge a phone, a tablet, and run a white noise machine simultaneously, you'll need a power strip — which defeats some of the purpose. Assembly is also more involved than advertised. The instructions claim 20 minutes; realistically, budget 35-40 minutes, especially when aligning the drawer rails. A few reviewers mentioned stripped cam lock threads, so use a hand screwdriver, not a drill. The LED strip is also permanently attached — if it fails after the warranty, replacing it requires disassembly.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is ideal for renters or dorm dwellers who want the convenience of built-in charging without installing anything permanent. It's also great for guest bedrooms — the LED lighting doubles as a dim nightlight that doesn't require a separate lamp. If you're a light sleeper who needs both a phone charger and a lamp on the same surface, this earns its keep. Skip it if you need multiple AC outlets or prefer a classic, furniture-grade piece that will last 15+ years — this is a 5-7 year product at best.

**FAQ**

*Q: Can I use the USB-C port to fast-charge a laptop?*
A: No. It outputs 5V/2.1A (10W), which is fine for phones and tablets, but laptops require 30W+. You'll need to use the AC outlet with your laptop's charger.

*Q: Does the LED light stay on all night?*
A: There's a motion sensor mode that activates the light when you approach — it shuts off after 60 seconds. You can also set it to stay on continuously, but that will drain the light's lifespan faster.

*Q: Is the top surface scratch-resistant?*
A: The laminate is decent, but it's not scratch-proof. Use coasters for sharp objects; the finish holds up well against normal use but can mark if you drag heavy items across it.

**The Verdict**

The EnHomee Nightstand with Charging Station & LED Lights earns a solid 4.2 out of 5. It's not high-end furniture, but it's a smartly designed, functional piece that solves a real problem: cluttered nightstand cords. The charging hub works as advertised, the LED lighting is a genuinely nice touch, and the build quality exceeds what the price point suggests.

**Buy it if** you want a practical, modern nightstand with integrated charging and ambient lighting without paying premium furniture store prices.

**Skip it if** you need multiple AC outlets, want app-controlled smart lighting, or expect heirloom-quality wood construction. This is a smart buy for your first apartment, a dorm, or a guest room — just don't expect it to survive three moves.
