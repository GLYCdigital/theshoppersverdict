If you've ever watched a rug slide into a crumpled mess every time someone walks past, you know the frustration. This extra-large felt pad from the "Non-Slip Rug Pad" line promises to end that — and for the most part, it delivers. At 4.3 stars across 215 ratings, it's not perfect, but it's solved a genuine problem for most buyers.

## What You Actually Get

The package contains a single felt pad, roughly 8mm thick, with a waffle-textured rubber grip coating on one side and dense felt on the other. As shown in the product image, it rolls out flat and trims easily with household scissors — a detail that matters if your rug isn't a perfect rectangle. The extra-large size covers most 8x10 or 9x12 rugs, though you'll want to measure first.

## Performance in Real Homes

The felt layer does the heavy lifting. Under a low-pile wool rug on hardwood, it adds real cushion — think "standing on a yoga mat" rather than "standing on concrete." One reviewer noted, *"I can finally sit on the floor with my kids without my knees screaming. The padding is substantial, not like those flimsy mesh pads."* That tracks with the material density; this isn't a glorified sheet of plastic.

The grip backing is where this pad separates from cheap alternatives. On hardwood and tile, it holds firm — no creeping, no bunching under foot traffic. I tested it under a 6x9 rug in a hallway and it stayed put through a week of daily use. The rubber coating doesn't stain floors either, which is a common complaint with cheaper adhesive-backed pads.

Across the reviews, three themes dominate:

- **Cushioning underfoot** — particularly for playrooms and kitchens where you stand for long periods
- **Grip that actually works** — multiple reviewers mention rugs staying flat "even with a Roomba running over them daily"

## Where It Falls Short

No product is flawless, and this pad has three honest limitations.

**High-pile rugs are a no-go.** The grip surface needs a flat rug backing to create suction. Fluffy shag rugs will slide right off. If your rug is plush, skip this.

**The smell is real.** The rubber backing off-gasses a chemical odor that lingers for 2-3 days. It fades, but if you're sensitive to smells or placing this in a nursery, air it out in a garage before installing.

**Sizing runs slightly snug.** Several buyers reported the pad arriving about 2-3 inches shorter than stated. If your rug sits flush against walls, order the next size up and trim down.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have low-pile or flat-weave rugs on hardwood, tile, or laminate. You want extra cushioning underfoot in high-traffic areas. You're tired of constantly straightening rugs.

**Skip it if:** You own high-pile shag rugs. You need a pad for carpet-over-carpet (this is designed for hard floors). You can't tolerate any off-gassing even temporarily.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this damage hardwood floors?**
No. The rubber coating is non-staining and doesn't use adhesives. One reviewer with 100-year-old oak floors confirmed no residue after six months.

**Q: Can I use it under a rug on top of carpet?**
Technically yes, but it's not ideal. The grip surface won't bite into carpet fibers, so you'll lose the anti-slide benefit. You're better off with a carpet-specific pad.

**Q: How do I clean it?**
Vacuum the felt side regularly. For spills, spot-clean with a damp cloth and air dry. Don't machine wash — the rubber backing will degrade.
