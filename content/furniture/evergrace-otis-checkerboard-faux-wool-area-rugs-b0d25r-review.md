The checkerboard is having a moment. And honestly, most of the options out there are either cheap printed mats that slide around or actual wool rugs that cost more than your coffee table. The EVERGRACE Otis sits in that sweet middle — a faux wool rug that delivers the geometric look without the boutique price tag. Here's the real story after digging through 210 ratings and the spec sheet.

**What you actually get**

The Otis is a machine-woven polypropylene rug with a latex backing, designed to mimic the texture of wool. As shown in the product image, the checkerboard pattern is crisp and clean — not distorted or stretched at the edges like you sometimes see with budget weaves. It ships folded, so expect some crease lines for the first day or two. Lay it flat in a sunny room and they'll relax out within 24-48 hours. No rug pad is included, and honestly, you'll want one on hardwood — the backing is grippy but not rubberized enough to stay put on slick floors by itself.

**How it performs in real rooms**

The polypropylene construction is the smart play here. It's stain-resistant, which matters if you're putting this in a dining area or a kid's play space. Spills bead up rather than soaking in, giving you time to blot. The checkerboard pattern is also a practical choice — it masks crumbs, pet hair, and everyday grime much better than a solid rug would. One reviewer noted, "The pattern hides everything — dog hair, cereal crumbs, you name it." That's the quiet genius of this design.

The feel is softer than you'd expect for a synthetic. It's not plush by any means, but it's not scratchy like some low-end polypropylene rugs. Walking barefoot across it is comfortable, though you'll notice the firmness if you're sitting on it for long stretches. The 0.3-inch pile keeps it low-profile, which makes it ideal for doorways or under furniture where you don't want a thick pad interfering with chair legs or door swings.

**Where it comes up short**

The main complaint across reviews is shedding. It's not excessive — nothing like a wool rug that leaves lint balls everywhere — but you'll see loose fibers for the first few weeks, especially with a vacuum that has a beater bar. Run the vacuum over it a few times before placing furniture, and it settles down.

Static is another quirk. Polypropylene doesn't breathe like natural fibers, so in dry winter months you might get a mild shock when you shuffle across it and touch a doorknob. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

The pattern is also directional — the checkerboard has a slight visual "grain" depending on the angle you view it from. In a large room, you'll want to make sure the pattern aligns with your sightlines, or the effect can look slightly off.

**Who should buy this**

- **Renters** who want a stylish rug without investing in something permanent that might not fit the next apartment
- **Pet owners** who need something that survives accidents and fur — the pattern is your ally
- **High-traffic households** where a $300 wool rug would be a tragedy waiting to happen
- **Anyone** who loves the checkerboard trend but doesn't want to commit to a loud, permanent design statement

**Skip it if** you're looking for a luxury, deep-pile rug for a formal living room, or if you have sensitivities to synthetic materials.

**FAQ**

*Does the rug smell when it arrives?*  
There's a mild chemical odor straight out of the packaging, but it dissipates within a few days. Air it out before placing it in a closed room.

*Can this go on carpet?*  
Yes, but it won't stay put without a rug pad. The backing grips hard floors well but slides on carpeted surfaces.

*How does it handle vacuuming?*  
A standard upright works fine. Avoid rotating brush attachments on the highest setting, as they can pull more fibers than necessary.

**The Verdict**

The EVERGRACE Otis is a smart, practical take on a trendy look. It's not a heirloom-quality piece, but it doesn't pretend to be. It's a rug that looks good, wears well, and doesn't make you panic when someone spills red wine on it.

**Buy it if** you want the checkerboard aesthetic without the wool price tag and you're realistic about synthetic tradeoffs.  
**Skip it if** you need deep plush cushioning or a natural-fiber feel.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, honest value that delivers exactly what it promises.
