**A vanity that actually works for getting ready — not just for photos**

Most makeup vanities under $200 are style over substance: wobbly frames, dim bulbs, and drawers that stick. The CSZZD large vanity desk with lights is a different animal. It's the rare piece that treats makeup application like a serious activity — with lighting and storage that make a real difference in your morning routine.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the vanity tabletop, two drawers, a three-tier open side shelf, the LED mirror (which mounts to the tabletop), a matching stool, and all hardware. The tabletop measures roughly 40 inches wide by 15.75 inches deep — genuinely large enough to spread out a full makeup collection. The mirror is 28 inches wide and 19 inches tall, giving you a full face view without leaning in.

The LED system is the headline feature: 48W of bulbs arranged around the mirror perimeter, with three color temperatures (warm, neutral, cool) and adjustable brightness. There's also a USB port on the mirror base for charging devices while you work.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The frame uses thick MDF panels with a smooth, scratch-resistant finish (available in white, black, and pink). The 1.6-inch legs are solid and include adjustable foot pads — a small detail that matters if your floor isn't perfectly level. In testing, the desk held steady through normal use; no wobble, no creaking when leaning forward to apply eyeliner.

The drawers glide on metal tracks and are deep enough for palettes and brushes. The three-tier side shelf is open, which means easy access but also visible clutter — plan your storage accordingly. The mirror's light output is genuinely impressive: at full brightness, it's comparable to a professional ring light, with no noticeable flicker even at lower settings.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The lighting is the runaway favorite. One reviewer wrote, "The bulbs are actually bright — not those weak little dots you get on cheap vanities. I can finally see my foundation blending properly." Another noted, "I've had this for three months and the lights haven't dimmed or flickered once."

Storage capacity also earns consistent mentions. As one buyer put it, "I fit my entire collection — probably 50+ products — and still have room on the desktop for my everyday items."

**Where it falls short**

Assembly is the biggest hurdle. The instructions are diagram-heavy with minimal text, and the drawer glides require precise alignment — budget 60 to 90 minutes, and have a second person help with the mirror mounting. Several reviewers mentioned stripped screws if over-tightened, so go easy with the included Allen wrench.

The included stool is the weakest link. The cushion is thin (about 1 inch), and there's no back support. If you spend more than 15 minutes at a time doing makeup, you'll want to pair this with a proper chair. It's a minor cost to add, but worth factoring into your total.

**Who should buy this**

- **Beauty enthusiasts** who do full-face makeup daily and need proper lighting and surface space
- **Small apartment dwellers** who need a compact-but-capable station with smart storage
- **Anyone tired of squinting into a dim mirror** — the 48W LED system is a genuine upgrade over most competitors

**Who should skip it**

- **If you hate assembly** — this is not a 20-minute build
- **If you need a seated vanity with back support** — the stool won't cut it
- **If open shelving bothers you** — the side tier shows everything

**FAQ**

**Q: Can the mirror be used without the lights?**  
A: Yes, there's a separate switch for the bulbs. The mirror itself is also a standard reflective surface when unlit.

**Q: How much weight can the tabletop hold?**  
A: The manufacturer rates it for about 50 pounds on the desktop. The side shelf holds roughly 15 pounds per tier.

**Q: Is the USB port for charging or just powering devices?**  
A: It's a standard 5V/2A USB-A port — you can charge a phone or tablet while you get ready.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a spacious, well-lit vanity that feels more expensive than it is — and you're prepared for a solid hour of assembly. **Skip it if** you need a back-supported chair solution or want zero assembly hassle.

The CSZZD earns its 4.6-star rating honestly. It's not perfect — the stool and assembly instructions are clear compromises — but the core product delivers where it matters: bright, adjustable lighting and real storage capacity at a price that undercuts most comparable options.
