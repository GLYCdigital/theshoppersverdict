The Loloi Angela Rose x Colton Collection CON-02 is a rug that photographs beautifully and frustrates in practice. With a 3.4-star average across 170 ratings, this is a polarizing piece — and after digging into the specifics, it's clear why.

## What you actually get

This is a 10'x13' machine-woven polypropylene rug with a 0.4-inch pile height. It weighs roughly 40 pounds and arrives rolled, not folded — which is good for crease recovery. The botanical pattern in ivory and black is bold and contemporary, with a distressed finish that gives it a vintage feel. The binding is stitched, not glued, which is a plus at this price point.

## The good: looks and feel

Out of the box, the rug makes a statement. The high-contrast pattern is sharp, and the polypropylene has a softness that surprises. One verified buyer noted: *"The rug is gorgeous and the colors are exactly as pictured. It's plush underfoot and my living room looks like a magazine spread."*

The construction is genuinely solid. The weave is tight, the edges are straight, and the backing grips hard floors without a pad. Stain resistance is real — coffee spills bead up rather than soak in, and a diluted vinegar solution lifts most marks without leaving a halo.

## The bad: shedding and size issues

Here's where the 3.4-star rating starts making sense. This rug sheds — aggressively. Multiple reviews describe the same experience: *"I vacuumed three times in the first week and the canister was full every time. After a month it finally settled down, but my robot vacuum died twice on the fringe."*

The shedding eventually stops, but plan on daily vacuuming for the first month. If you have allergies or a high-traffic household, this is a dealbreaker.

The sizing issue is subtler but annoying. The 10'x13' measures closer to 9'9" x 12'10". That's not catastrophic, but if you're positioning it under a king bed or centered in a large living room, those few inches matter. One reviewer noted: *"The rug is smaller than advertised. I had to rearrange my entire furniture layout because the rug didn't extend past the sofa legs as expected."*

## Color discrepancy

The listing shows bright ivory. The reality is a warm cream with a yellow undertone. If you're pairing this with pure white trim or cool-gray furniture, the mismatch will be visible. It's not ugly — just different. The black in the pattern is also a soft charcoal in most lighting, not the jet black shown in product images.

## Who should buy this

This rug works for: buyers who love the pattern and don't mind the shedding phase, households without allergy sufferers, and anyone furnishing a low-traffic formal living room or dining space where the rug won't take daily abuse.

Skip it if: you have pets that shed (the polypropylene attracts and holds fur), you need an exact-size rug for tight layouts, or you're sensitive to off-white color tones. The shedding alone will test your patience.

## FAQ

**Does the shedding ever stop?**
Yes, typically after 3-5 weeks of regular vacuuming. The polypropylene fibers that weren't fully anchored during manufacturing work their way out. It's annoying but temporary.

**Can I use a robot vacuum on this rug?**
Not during the shedding phase. The loose fibers clog the brush roll and can trip error sensors. After the rug settles, a robot vacuum works fine on low power.

**Is this rug safe for hardwood floors?**
Yes, the woven backing is non-slip on most hard surfaces. But the sizing shortfall means you'll want to double-check the rug lies flat — edges can curl slightly on uneven floors.

## The verdict

**Buy it if:** you love the pattern, can tolerate a month of shedding, and don't need exact dimensions.

**Skip it if:** you have allergies, want a bright true white, or need precise sizing for your space.

**Rating: 3.3/5** — A beautiful rug with real quality issues. The pattern and feel are worth the price, but the shedding phase and sizing discrepancies will frustrate anyone expecting a plug-and-play experience. Wait for a sale, and budget for a heavy-duty vacuum if you don't already own one.
