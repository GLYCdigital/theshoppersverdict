Most budget standing desks force you to choose between stability and storage. The COMHOMA 48x24 Electric Standing Desk with Drawers tries to give you both at a price that undercuts the big names. After digging through 220 ratings and the spec sheet, here's what actually matters before you click buy.

## What you're actually getting

The box contains the pre-drilled top, a dual-motor steel frame, two drawers (already assembled — a nice touch), the control pad, and all mounting hardware. The 48x24 inch surface is solid for a compact home office. The drawers are mounted on the right side, which is worth noting if you're left-handed and want mouse space on that side.

The dual-motor system is the headline feature. It lifts up to 155 pounds, which covers most dual-monitor setups plus a laptop and peripherals. The speed is 1.5 inches per second — not record-breaking, but consistent even when loaded. It runs quiet enough that you won't wake anyone in the next room during a 6 AM stand-up session.

## Real-world performance

Here's where this desk earns its 4.4-star rating. The anti-collision system actually works — it stops and reverses if something's in the way, which matters when you've got a dog crate or a plant underneath. The four memory presets are genuinely useful if you share the desk or have specific sitting and standing heights you cycle between.

The stability is good but not exceptional. At standing height (around 39 inches), you'll see slight wobble if you're aggressively typing or leaning on the desk. It's not a dealbreaker — it's less shake than single-motor desks in this price range — but if you're a heavy gamer who slams a mechanical keyboard, you'll notice it.

The drawers are the differentiator. They're deep enough for a tablet, notebooks, and cables. One reviewer noted, "I bought this specifically for the drawers — they're not just decorative, they actually hold a surprising amount." That's accurate. They slide smoothly but aren't ball-bearing silky; expect a slight friction at the end of the track.

## What buyers consistently praise

The value proposition is the through-line in positive reviews. As one verified buyer put it, "This is the first standing desk I've owned where I didn't need a separate drawer unit — it's a space saver." The dual-motor performance also gets consistent shout-outs for its smoothness at load, with another reviewer writing, "I was skeptical of a sub-$400 desk with drawers, but the lift is stable even with a heavy monitor arm clamped on."

## Where it falls short

The 48x24 surface is the biggest limitation. If you're running a 27-inch monitor plus a 24-inch secondary, you'll have maybe two inches of clearance on each side. Add speakers and you're in Tetris territory. The drawer placement also eats into legroom slightly — if you're 6'2"+ and like to stretch your right leg, you'll feel the drawer housing.

Assembly isn't terrible, but it's a two-person job. The frame bolts to the top with about 12 screws, and the drawers attach to the underside. One reviewer reported misaligned pre-drilled holes, though that seems to be an outlier. Budget an hour and have a second person hold the top steady while you torque the frame.

## Who should buy this

- **Small home office workers** who need storage but don't have floor space for a separate filing cabinet
- **Standing desk first-timers** who want dual-motor features without the $600+ price tag
- **Renters** — the drawers are built-in, so you don't need to buy extra furniture you'll have to move

## FAQ

**Q: Can I clamp a monitor arm to this desk?**
A: Yes, the top is solid wood composite, and the frame has a channel for cable management. Clamp-style arms work fine, but avoid overtightening — the underside isn't reinforced where the drawers attach.

**Q: Is the 155 lb capacity realistic?**
A: That's the max load across the full surface. If you're using a heavy monitor arm, factor that into the center of gravity. It'll handle a standard dual-monitor setup without issue.

**Q: Does the desk remember heights after power loss?**
A: Yes, the controller has a battery backup for the memory presets. You won't lose your settings if you unplug or lose power.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a compact standing desk with integrated storage and want dual-motor reliability without paying premium prices. The drawers genuinely save floor space, and the lift system performs like desks costing twice as much.

**Skip it if** you're running a triple-monitor setup or need a 60-inch surface. The 48x24 top is the ceiling here, and the drawer placement won't suit every body type.
