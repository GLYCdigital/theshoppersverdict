**The 59-inch sideboard you've been scrolling for — but is it actually good, or just good-looking?**

If you've been hunting for a buffet cabinet that doesn't look like it came from a college dorm or a sterile office showroom, the RoyalCraft 59'' Sideboard has likely crossed your screen. With a 4.4-star average across 220 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. But here's the honest question: does it hold up in a real dining room, or just in the product photos?

I've dug through the specs, the customer feedback, and the build details. Here's what you need to know before you drop your money.

---

**What you actually get**

The box contains the sideboard in flat-pack form — two cabinet sections, two doors, four adjustable shelves, a top panel, and all the cam-lock hardware, dowels, and screws. The included instruction manual is clear enough, but the assembly is a real project. Plan for about 90 minutes with two people. The doors are the trickiest part; getting them perfectly aligned takes patience, and several reviewers noted the same.

The cabinet measures 59 inches wide, 15.7 inches deep, and 31.5 inches tall — dimensions that suit a standard dining room wall or behind a sofa. The MDF construction is solid, not flimsy. It won't survive a move without some scratches, but it's not going to wobble under a stack of dinnerware either.

---

**Real-world performance**

The standout feature here is the adjustable shelving. Both cabinet sections have shelves that move to different heights, which means you can actually store a wine bottle collection *and* a stack of platters without wasting vertical space. The shelves are rated to hold a reasonable amount of weight — a full set of stoneware or a heavy mixer won't be an issue.

The back panel has pre-cut cable management holes, which is a thoughtful touch if you're planning to mount a TV above it or run cords for a soundbar. The top surface is wide enough to hold a coffee station or a display of framed photos without feeling cramped.

One thing buyers consistently praise is the finish. The matte texture resists fingerprints, and a damp cloth handles spills. As one reviewer put it: *"I was worried about the white showing every smudge, but it's surprisingly forgiving."*

---

**Where it falls short**

Assembly is the biggest complaint across reviews. The cam-lock system works, but it's not forgiving of mistakes. If you tighten a cam before the dowel is fully seated, you'll be unscrewing and starting over. The doors, in particular, require multiple adjustments to hang straight.

The 59-inch width is also a commitment. If you're in a small apartment or have an awkward wall, measure twice. Returning this thing is a logistical headache — it's heavy, and the box is unwieldy.

Finally, color consistency. The "rustic brown" finish has been reported to look slightly redder in person by a handful of buyers. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you're matching existing furniture, order a swatch or be prepared for a slight mismatch.

---

**Who should buy this**

- **The dinner party host** who needs serious storage for serving pieces and glassware — the adjustable shelves are a genuine win.
- **The home office setup** — use it as a credenza behind your desk, with the cable cutouts keeping your monitor and laptop cords tidy.
- **The small-space dweller** who wants one substantial piece instead of two wobbly bookcases — this anchors a room.

Skip it if you need something you can assemble solo in under an hour, or if your wall space is tighter than 59 inches.

---

**FAQ**

**Q: Can the shelves hold a heavy microwave or mini fridge?**
A: The shelves are sturdy MDF, but they're not rated for appliance weight. Stick to dinnerware, linens, or a coffee machine — a full-size microwave is pushing it.

**Q: Does the back panel have holes for cords?**
A: Yes, there are two cutouts in the back panel specifically for cable management. They're large enough for most power strips and HDMI cables.

**Q: Is the top surface scratch-resistant?**
A: The matte finish is fairly durable, but it's not laminate. Use a coaster or mat under anything with a metal base to avoid scratches.

---

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a substantial, good-looking sideboard with flexible storage and don't mind a challenging assembly afternoon. **Skip it if** you're looking for quick setup, or if you need precise color matching with existing furniture.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid value for the price, with the assembly being the main tradeoff.
