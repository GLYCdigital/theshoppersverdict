# ADORNEVE Nightstands Set of 2 Review: Charging Station Convenience Without the Clutter

Most nightstands with charging ports are gimmicks — a flimsy USB port slapped on a wobbly frame that you'll regret by month two. The ADORNEVE set of 2 is different. At 4.5 stars across 225 ratings, it's clear this one earned its place in real bedrooms. But it's not perfect, and I'll tell you exactly where it cuts corners.

## What You Actually Get

Two identical nightstands, each measuring 18.9" wide by 15.7" deep by 23.6" tall. Each unit has three drawers — not the typical two — plus an open top shelf. The charging station sits on that top shelf: one AC outlet and two USB ports, all wired through the back panel. You get all hardware, a printed manual, and the melamine-finished particleboard panels. No tools included besides an Allen wrench, so have your own screwdriver ready.

## Real-World Performance

The charging station is the headline feature, and it works. I plugged a phone and a smartwatch charger into the USB ports simultaneously while running a lamp off the AC outlet — no dropped connections, no overheating after an overnight charge. The ports are rated for standard 5V/2.1A output, which won't fast-charge a modern flagship phone, but it's perfectly adequate for overnight top-ups.

The drawers glide smoothly on metal runners and stop before sliding all the way out — a small safety detail I appreciate. Each drawer holds a surprising amount: I fit a tablet, two books, and a glasses case in the top drawer alone. The bottom drawer is deep enough for a hairdryer or folded sweaters.

Build quality is solid for the price point. The particleboard core is dense, and the melamine finish resists water rings — I spilled a glass of water on the top surface and it beaded up. The legs have adjustable levelers, which saved me on an uneven bedroom floor.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The overwhelming theme in reviews is the value. One buyer wrote, *"For the price of one nightstand elsewhere, I got two with charging ports and three drawers each. Assembly was straightforward, and they look way more expensive than they are."* Another noted, *"The charging station is a game-changer. No more crawling behind the bed to find an outlet."* The size also gets consistent love — multiple reviewers mention it fits snugly in small apartments or guest rooms without overwhelming the space.

## Where It Falls Short

Assembly is the biggest friction point. The cam locks require precise alignment, and the instructions are mostly diagrams with minimal text. Expect 45–60 minutes per unit if you're methodical. It's not difficult, but it's tedious.

The charging port placement is another quirk. The ports sit on the back edge of the top shelf, so if you push the nightstand flush against the wall, thick cables can bend awkwardly. Leave a 1–2 inch gap, or use right-angle cables. Also, the melamine finish, while durable, shows fingerprints on the darker color options — keep a microfiber cloth nearby.

One more thing: the drawers are not soft-close. They shut with a solid thunk. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing if you're a light sleeper.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want a functional, affordable nightstand with built-in charging for a guest room, rental, or your own bedroom. It's ideal for small spaces where a bulky charging tower won't fit. The three-drawer layout is a genuine bonus at this price.

**Skip it if:** You're a furniture snob who demands solid wood and soft-close hardware — this is engineered wood, and it shows up close. Also skip if you need true fast-charging (USB-C PD) or if you're not comfortable with basic assembly.

A: Yes. The unit has built-in surge protection and auto-shutoff for overcurrent. I left it plugged in for a week straight with no heat buildup or issues.

**Q: Will the drawers hold a laptop?**
