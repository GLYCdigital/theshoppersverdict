If you've got a dead zone behind your sofa or a hallway that's too narrow for a real console, the HOOBRO Skinny Console Table is trying to solve a very specific problem. The twist? It brings power outlets to a spot where you'd normally have to run extension cords.

Let's start with what you're actually getting. This is a narrow sofa table measuring roughly 11.8 inches deep — slim enough to tuck behind a couch or against a tight entryway wall. The frame is particle board with a laminated finish, which is common at this price point. Assembly takes about 20-30 minutes with the included hex wrench and instructions. Nothing about the build feels premium, but it doesn't feel flimsy either.

The headline feature is the charging station. Two AC outlets and two USB ports sit on the side panel, all powered by a single cord you route along the back. This is genuinely useful. You can plug in a lamp, charge your phone, and keep a tablet topped off without crawling behind furniture. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: *"I love that I can charge my phone and tablet without having to reach behind the couch. The cord management is decent too."* That sentiment shows up repeatedly in the feedback.

Storage-wise, you get two open shelves. They're not deep — this is a skinny table — but they're tall enough for stacked books, small baskets, or a couple of decorative bins. The bottom shelf sits close to the floor, which makes it better for bulkier items you don't mind bending for. There are no drawers, so if you need hidden storage, this isn't your piece.

What buyers consistently praise:

- **The footprint.** Multiple reviewers mention it fits where nothing else would. One wrote: *"Perfect for my narrow hallway. I didn't think I could fit a console table here at all."*
- **The power feature.** People use it for everything from phone charging to powering a small desk lamp. It's the differentiator that justifies the price.
- **Ease of assembly.** Most report straightforward setup, with one buyer noting: *"Took me about 25 minutes by myself. The holes lined up well."*

Where it falls short:

- **Particle board construction.** This is not heirloom furniture. It's laminated particle board, which means scratches, water rings, and sagging are real risks over time. Don't put a heavy aquarium on it.
- **Cord dependency.** The charging station has one power cord that needs to reach a wall outlet. If you're placing this in the middle of a room, you'll see the cord or need an extension cable.
- **No cable management channel for the shelves.** The power cord runs along the back, but the shelves themselves have no grommets or channels. You'll need zip ties or clips to keep things tidy.

Who should buy this:

- **Apartment dwellers** with narrow entryways or small living rooms who need a slim surface and charging access.
- **People with sofas against a wall** who want a behind-the-couch table for drinks, phones, and remotes without a bulky piece.
- **Anyone needing a hallway table** that doubles as a charging hub for a family's worth of devices.

Skip it if you're expecting solid wood, if you need drawers for concealed storage, or if your space demands a piece that can handle heavy daily abuse.

**FAQ**

**Does the charging station work without plugging in the main cord?**
No. The outlets and USB ports only function when the main power cord is connected to a wall outlet. There's no battery backup.

**Can the shelves hold a heavy item like a printer?**
The shelves are rated for light-to-moderate use. A standard printer (around 15-20 lbs) should be fine, but don't push it with heavy equipment or large books stacked high.

**Is the finish easy to clean?**
Yes, the laminated surface wipes down with a damp cloth. Avoid abrasive cleaners and standing water, which can damage the particle board edges over time.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a slim, functional console that solves the "no outlet near the couch" problem and fits in tight spaces.

**Skip it if** you want solid wood, hidden storage, or a piece that can take heavy use.

At 4.6 stars from 229 ratings, the HOOBRO Skinny Console Table delivers exactly what it promises: narrow, powered, practical. It's not fancy, but it's smartly designed for a real problem — and that's worth your money if the dimensions match your space.
