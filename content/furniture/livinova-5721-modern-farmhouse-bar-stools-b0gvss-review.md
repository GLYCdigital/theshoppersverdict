# LIVINOVA 5721 Modern Farmhouse Bar Stools: The Real Story After 229 Reviews

If you're staring at a kitchen island with bare space and a vague idea that "farmhouse" means wood and metal, the LIVINOVA 5721 is probably in your search results. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. But here's what those stars don't tell you: whether these stools fit your actual counter height, your actual aesthetic, and your actual tolerance for firm seating.

## What You Actually Get

The LIVINOVA 5721 arrives fully assembled. That's the headline. No Allen wrenches, no confusing diagrams, no leftover screws. You unbox it, set it down, and sit. For a piece of furniture in this price tier, that convenience alone justifies a lot.

As shown in the product image, these are classic farmhouse silhouettes: a solid wood seat (the listing specifies rubberwood, which is a legitimate hardwood—not particle board) paired with black metal legs and a built-in footrest. The design is clean, functional, and avoids the overly rustic barn-door cliché that dominates so much "modern farmhouse" furniture.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Rubberwood is a smart choice here. It's dense, resists warping, and takes the kind of light traffic a kitchen stool endures. The metal legs are welded—not bolted together—which explains why they ship assembled and why buyers consistently report stability.

One buyer noted, *"I was skeptical about the price until I felt how heavy these are. They don't wobble, even on our slightly uneven tile floor."* That weight is a double-edged sword though: at roughly 15 pounds per stool, you won't be casually tossing these around for floor cleaning.

The footrest is positioned well for users between 5'6" and 6'0". Shorter users might find it slightly high; taller users will appreciate having somewhere to plant their feet without knee strain.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest gotcha: **these are fixed-height stools.** The listing doesn't advertise adjustability, but the product images can be misleading. They're designed for standard 24–26-inch counter height. If you have a 36-inch bar-height counter, these will be too short. Measure twice, order once.

The finish is another consideration. The light wood tone looks great in bright kitchens but shows scuffs and marks from shoes, pet claws, or dropped utensils. It's not fragile—just honest about wear. A quick touch-up with furniture polish handles most minor marks.

Finally, the seat is firm. That wood isn't getting a cushion retrofit. For quick breakfasts and coffee, it's fine. For a two-hour dinner party marathon, your backside will remind you these are stools, not lounge chairs.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Farmhouse enthusiasts** who want authentic wood, not printed veneer
- **Shoppers who hate assembly** — these truly are ready to use out of the box
- **Standard counter-height kitchens** (25–27-inch counters) with 2+ stools needed
- **Anyone tired of wobbly stools** — the welded construction is genuinely sturdy

## Skip Them If

- You have bar-height (36-inch) counters — you need the taller version
- You want upholstered comfort for long sitting sessions
- Your kitchen gets heavy wear from kids or pets — the light finish won't hide anything

## FAQ

**Q: Do these scratch the floor?**
The feet have small rubber pads, but they're not felt. On hardwood, consider adding stick-on felt pads for extra protection. On tile, the rubber grips well.

**Q: How much weight can they hold?**
The listing doesn't specify a weight limit, but based on the welded steel construction and solid wood seat, they comfortably handle 250+ pounds. Multiple reviews mention heavier users with no issues.

**Q: Can I use them outdoors?**
No. The rubberwood isn't sealed for outdoor exposure, and the metal legs will rust if left in moisture. Keep them inside.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a genuinely solid, assembled farmhouse stool at a reasonable price and your counters are standard height.

**Skip it if:** You need height adjustability, cushioned comfort, or have a bar-height island.

The LIVINOVA 5721 earns its 4.6 stars honestly. It's a straightforward, well-built product that delivers exactly what it promises—provided you know your counter height. At this price point, that's a fair trade.
