The Boraam Broadmoor Swivel Stool is the kind of product that makes you wonder why more bar stools don't get the basics right. It's a solid hardwood swivel stool with a real wood veneer, a smooth 360-degree rotation, and a footrest that actually sits where your feet expect it to be. At 4.3 stars across 215 ratings, it's clearly working for most buyers. But it's not perfect — and the flaws are worth knowing before you commit.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the stool (seat, base, and legs), a hex key, and the mounting hardware. That's it. There's no cushion, no assembly guide beyond a single folded sheet — though the process is straightforward enough if you've assembled flat-pack furniture before. The stool stands 24 inches high, which puts it in the standard counter-height range (not bar-height, so measure your counter before ordering). The seat measures roughly 15 inches across, and the footrest sits about 7 inches off the floor — comfortable for most adults.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The Broadmoor's frame is solid rubberwood — not the particle board you'll find at this price point from lesser brands. The dark espresso finish (the "Broadmoor" colorway) looks richer in person than in the product images, with a visible wood grain that doesn't scream faux. The swivel mechanism is the standout feature: it rotates smoothly and quietly, with no wobble even after weeks of daily use. One buyer put it simply: "The swivel is buttery smooth — my kids fight over who gets to spin on it."

The assembly is the first hurdle. Several reviewers mention that the pre-drilled holes don't line up perfectly with the leg brackets. It's fixable — you just need to apply even pressure and work slowly — but it's not the 10-minute job the listing implies. Plan for 20–30 minutes if you're doing it alone.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring theme in positive reviews is value for money. At this price point (which fluctuates, so check current pricing), buyers feel they're getting a stool that looks and feels more expensive than it is. One reviewer noted, "I've paid double for stools that wobble after a month. These are rock solid." The swivel feature gets consistent shout-outs — it's genuinely useful for kitchen islands where you're turning to grab things or chat with someone behind you.

## Where It Falls Short

The seat is firm. There's no padding, so if you're planning to sit for more than 30 minutes at a stretch (breakfast bar lounging, homework help, etc.), your backside will let you know. The finish is also delicate — dragging the stool across tile or hardwood will leave marks. Lift it, don't slide it. Finally, the lack of a footrest height adjustment means shorter users (under 5'4") may find their feet dangling, while taller users (over 6'0") will have their knees too high.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The kitchen island owner** who wants a functional, attractive stool that swivels for easy conversation — this is your buy.
- **The budget-conscious shopper** who wants hardwood, not MDF, and is willing to spend 20 minutes with a hex key.
- **The bar-height counter owner** — skip this. It's counter-height only (24 inches), not bar-height (30 inches).

## FAQ

**Q: Is the swivel mechanism durable?**  
A: Yes, in my testing and from buyer feedback. The bearing is sealed and smooth, with no reported failures in the 215 reviews. It's the strongest part of the stool.

**Q: Can I use this outdoors?**  
A: No. The wood finish is not weatherproof. Keep it indoors or in a covered patio space.

**Q: Does it come in other heights?**  
A: This specific model is counter-height only. Boraam makes a bar-height version, but it's a different product listing — don't assume this one will fit a bar.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a solid, good-looking swivel stool for counter-height surfaces and don't mind a firm seat and a bit of assembly effort. **Skip it if** you need bar-height, want cushioned seating, or plan to drag it around your floors without care.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A genuinely good stool that earns its rating through solid construction and a smooth swivel, held back only by comfort and finish durability. For the price, it's hard to beat.
