A round coffee table is one of those pieces that either anchors a living room or becomes a constant annoyance — too big, too flimsy, too awkward. The Condemo Round Coffee Table sits firmly in the first camp, and with a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, it's clearly earning its keep in real homes. Here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes the tabletop, two shelf panels, four metal legs, and all necessary hardware. The table measures 31.5 inches in diameter — a sweet spot that accommodates drinks and remotes for a sofa seating four without overwhelming a compact apartment. The two-tier design gives you a lower shelf for magazines, baskets, or whatever you want within arm's reach but out of the way.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The tabletop is constructed from MDF with a smooth laminate finish, supported by a powder-coated metal frame. In practice, this combination delivers exactly what you'd expect: a stable, sturdy table that doesn't flex or creak under normal use. The metal legs have adjustable floor glides, which is a thoughtful touch for uneven floors or protecting hardwood from scratches.

One buyer noted, "I was skeptical about the weight capacity on the bottom shelf, but I've got three large books and a plant on it, and it's rock solid." That tracks with the construction — the legs bolt directly into pre-drilled plates, creating a rigid connection that doesn't loosen over time.

Assembly is straightforward but not tool-free. You'll need a Phillips screwdriver and roughly 20-25 minutes. The instructions are clear, and all holes align properly — no fighting with misaligned brackets or stripped screws.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 229 ratings, a few themes repeat. The size is the most common compliment, with shoppers noting it fits perfectly in smaller spaces. One reviewer wrote, "I was worried a round table would make my living room feel cramped, but it actually opens up the space more than my old rectangular one did."

The stability also gets consistent shout-outs. Multiple buyers mention using it daily for meals, work, and kids' activities without any wobble or sagging. The easy-to-clean surface is another recurring positive — spills wipe up with a damp cloth, and the finish doesn't stain easily.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and the Condemo has a few honest drawbacks. The white finish, while clean and modern, shows scuffs and marks more readily than darker wood tones. Several buyers noted that shoes or pet claws can leave visible marks that require a gentle cleaner to remove.

Assembly complaints are minor but present. A handful of reviewers mention that the instructions could be clearer on the shelf spacer placement, and one noted the legs arrived with slight scuffs from shipping. These aren't dealbreakers, but they're worth knowing before you buy.

The round shape, while space-efficient, means you lose the corner storage that rectangular tables offer. If you're used to tucking items at the ends of a table, you'll need to adjust your storage habits.

## Who Should Buy This

This table is ideal for apartment dwellers, first-time furniture buyers, or anyone working with a compact living space. It's equally suited to a reading nook, a small home office, or a bedroom seating area. If you have a large sectional or entertain frequently with multiple drinks and plates, you might want a larger, rectangular option instead.

## FAQ

**How much weight can the Condemo coffee table hold?**
The top can comfortably support 50-60 pounds — enough for a large TV tray, a stack of books, or a heavy decorative piece. The bottom shelf handles 30-40 pounds without issue.

**Does the white finish yellow over time?**
Based on buyer feedback over several months of use, the laminate finish maintains its color well. Direct sunlight for extended periods may cause slight warming, but this hasn't been a widespread complaint.

**Can I use it outdoors on a covered patio?**
The MDF construction isn't designed for outdoor exposure. Even covered, humidity can cause swelling. Keep this one indoors.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You need a space-efficient, stable round table that looks more expensive than it costs, and you don't mind a simple assembly session.

**Skip it if:** You prefer a darker finish that hides daily wear, or you need a large surface for regular entertaining.

The Condemo Round Coffee Table delivers solid performance at a fair price point. It's not a heirloom piece, but it's a smart, dependable choice for everyday living.
