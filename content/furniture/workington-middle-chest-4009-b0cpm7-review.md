The Workington Middle Chest 4009 sits in that crowded middle ground of Amazon furniture: not bargain-basement, not heirloom quality. With 204 ratings averaging 4.1 stars, it's clearly doing something right — but the real question is whether it earns a spot in your bedroom. After digging through buyer feedback and the spec sheet, here's the honest breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the chest disassembled: three drawer fronts, the carcass panels, hardware, and a basic Allen wrench. As shown in the product image, this is a three-drawer vertical chest measuring roughly 30 inches tall and 24 inches wide — compact enough for a guest room or apartment bedroom without dominating the wall. The drawer fronts use a wood-grain laminate over MDF, but the frame structure is solid wood, which matters for longevity.

**Real-World Performance**

Assembly runs 45 to 60 minutes for most people. The cam-lock fittings are standard, but the drawer slide alignment is where patience pays off — several buyers noted that rushing this step leads to drawers that catch. Once assembled, the chest feels planted. At roughly 40 pounds empty, it doesn't wobble when you pull drawers open, and the dovetail joints on the drawer boxes are a genuine step up from the butt-joint construction you see on cheaper competitors.

The drawers glide smoothly on ball-bearing slides and accommodate folded sweaters or jeans easily — each drawer is about 5 inches deep, 20 inches wide. One buyer put it plainly: "I've bought three of these for different rooms. The drawers don't stick, the finish matches the photos, and it's held up for two years without sagging."

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The recurring theme in positive reviews is value-per-dollar. The solid wood frame, dovetailed drawers, and smooth slides are features typically reserved for pieces costing twice as much. Multiple reviewers specifically called out the "premium feel" of the drawer action. Another common compliment: the chest's proportions work in tight spaces without looking cramped.

**Where It Falls Short**

Be honest about the tradeoffs. The matte finish on darker colorways shows fingerprints and dust quickly — you'll be wiping it down weekly if it's in a high-traffic area. The instructions have a known weak spot: the hinge alignment step is under-illustrated, and several reviewers mention needing to backtrack. Assembly is genuinely a two-person job for the carcass; attempting it solo is frustrating.

There's also the durability ceiling. This is not a piece you'll pass down. The MDF drawer fronts can chip if you're rough with them, and the laminate can peel if exposed to direct sunlight over years. It's a solid 5-to-7-year piece, not a lifetime investment.

**Who Should Buy This**

The Workington Middle Chest is ideal for three buyer types: first-time apartment dwellers needing functional storage without breaking the bank, guest bedroom furnishers who want something presentable but not precious, and anyone adding a secondary storage piece to a home office or entryway. If you're furnishing a primary bedroom and want something with character that will age gracefully, look at solid-wood pieces from dedicated furniture brands instead.

**FAQ**

*Does it come in multiple colors?*
Yes — the 4009 is available in several finishes, including black, white, and a walnut-toned option. The walnut shows fingerprints less than the black.

*Can one person assemble it?*
Technically yes, but the carcass is awkward to hold while securing cam locks. Budget an extra 20 minutes if you're solo, and use a blanket to protect the finish while working.

*Are the drawers removable for cleaning?*
The slides have a release lever, so yes — they pull out fully. This also makes moving the chest easier since you can lighten it first.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a functional, well-built three-drawer chest that outperforms its price class on drawer quality and frame sturdiness. **Skip it if** you need a showpiece, hate assembly, or expect heirloom durability.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The Workington Middle Chest 4009 delivers exactly what it promises: dependable storage with better-than-expected construction. Just budget time for assembly and keep a microfiber cloth handy.
