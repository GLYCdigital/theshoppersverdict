# COLAMY Night Stand with Charging Station, Mid Century Modern

**The Short Version:** A $70-ish nightstand that looks like it costs twice as much, with built-in charging that actually works. The 4.8-star rating is earned — but there are a couple of things you should know before you buy.

---

## What You're Actually Getting

The COLAMY nightstand ships flat-packed (yes, you'll need a Phillips head screwdriver and about 30–45 minutes of patience). Inside the box: the main body, two drawers, a fabric drawer liner, and the charging station components pre-wired into the top panel. Assembly is straightforward — the pre-drilled holes line up correctly, and the instructions are mostly visual, which is fine since the build is simple enough that you likely won't need to read them closely.

The dimensions are 15.7" W x 15.7" D x 19.7" H — compact enough for tight bedroom corners but substantial enough to hold a lamp, phone, glasses, and a book without feeling cluttered. The mid-century look comes through in the angled legs (included, screw-on) and the clean, rounded edges of the top panel.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

Here's where the COLAMY surprises. The engineered wood construction is solid — not "cheap furniture store" hollow-sounding when you knock on it. The drawer slides feel smooth and hold up to daily use without sticking. The finish (available in several colors including walnut and white) is applied evenly with no rough spots or peeling at the edges.

The headline feature — the charging station — is genuinely useful. The top panel has two AC outlets and two USB ports (one USB-A, one USB-C). The USB-C port delivers 20W, which will fast-charge most modern phones. The outlets are positioned so a plugged-in charger doesn't block drawer access. One reviewer put it well:

> "I was skeptical about the charging station being a gimmick, but it's become the reason I bought a second one. Phone charges overnight without a separate adapter cluttering the top."

Another noted:

> "The drawers are deeper than they look in photos. I fit a tablet, e-reader, and all my bedside odds and ends with room to spare."

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs:

**The cord situation.** The power cord is about 6 feet long and comes out the back. If your outlet is more than 6 feet from where the nightstand sits, you'll need an extension cord — which somewhat defeats the "clean look" purpose. Plan your placement accordingly.

**The drawer liner.** It's a thin felt material that's glued in. It does its job, but it's not removable or washable. If you spill something in the drawer, you're cleaning the liner in place or replacing it yourself.

**Assembly quibbles.** The legs screw into pre-installed threaded inserts, which is good — but the instructions don't emphasize that you should hand-tighten them first before using the included Allen wrench. Over-tightening can strip the insert. Go slow.

**No soft-close drawers.** At this price point, that's expected — but if you're used to soft-close hardware, the standard drawer slides will feel a bit abrupt when closed quickly.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:**
- You're furnishing a guest room, rental, or small apartment and want a stylish, functional piece without dropping $200+
- You're tired of tripping over phone chargers and want a clean, integrated solution
- You want a nightstand that looks like it cost more than it did — several reviewers mention guests asking where they got it

**Skip it if:**
- You need a large, full-size nightstand with deep storage (this is compact — measure your space)
- You're a heavy drawer user who needs soft-close or ball-bearing slides
- Your outlet situation won't reach the 6-foot cord without an extension

## FAQ

**Q: Does the charging station work if the nightstand is unplugged?**
A: No. The USB ports and outlets only work when the unit is plugged into a wall outlet. There's no battery backup.

**Q: Can I use the charging station for devices other than phones?**
A: Yes. The AC outlets are standard 120V and will handle lamps, alarm clocks, or small appliances. The USB-C port at 20W can charge tablets and most laptops that support USB-C charging.

**Q: How sturdy is it? Will it wobble on carpet?**
A: On hard floors, it's rock-solid. On thick carpet, the legs can sink unevenly — reviewers recommend felt pads under the legs if you have plush carpet.

## The Verdict: 4.6/5 — Buy It

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely attractive, functional nightstand that solves the bedside charging problem without breaking the bank. It's well-built for the price, the charging station is a real feature (not a gimmick), and the mid-century styling punches well above its weight class.

**Skip it if** you need serious drawer capacity, can't reach an outlet within 6 feet, or demand premium hardware like soft-close slides. This is a smart, budget-conscious choice — not a luxury piece.

At 4.8 stars from 240+ ratings, the COLAMY is one of those rare finds where the reviews match the reality. Just budget 45 minutes for assembly and keep an eye on the cord length.
