# FORTNIGHT BEDDING Best 2 Rest Memory Foam Mattress: Does the Cooling Gel Actually Work?

Most memory foam mattresses under $500 make one of two promises: they'll sleep cool, or they'll support your back. The FORTNIGHT BEDDING Best 2 Rest tries to deliver both with a gel-infused top layer and a medium-firm feel. After digging through 215 ratings and buyer experiences, here's the real story — including where this mattress cuts corners.

## What You Actually Get

The Best 2 Rest arrives compressed in a box — roughly 40 inches long and 15 inches wide, manageable for one person to carry. Inside you get the mattress (available in standard sizes from Twin to King), a removable and washable knit cover, and zero assembly tools. The cover is a genuine plus; most mattresses in this price range ship with a non-removable polyester blend.

The construction layers are straightforward: a gel-infused top layer for temperature regulation, a transition layer for pressure relief, and a high-density base foam for support. Total thickness lands around 10 inches, which is standard for this category.

## Real-World Performance

**Firmness:** The medium-firm feel sits around a 6 out of 10. Back sleepers generally find it supportive without excessive sinkage. Side sleepers report it works best with a pillow top or mattress pad — the foam doesn't contour deeply enough on its own for significant shoulder pressure relief. Stomach sleepers, proceed with caution; several buyers noted hip dip over the first month.

**Cooling:** This is the headline feature, and it mostly delivers. The gel-infused layer does pull heat away more effectively than traditional memory foam. Multiple reviewers noted they "no longer wake up sweating" compared to their previous foam mattress. That said, it's not a miracle worker — if you run hot, you'll still want breathable sheets and possibly a cooling pad during summer months.

**Motion Isolation:** Exceptional. One reviewer described it perfectly: "My husband gets up at 5 AM for work and I don't feel a thing." If you share a bed with someone on a different schedule, this is a genuine strength.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The dominant theme in positive reviews is value. At this price point, buyers expect compromises — and most feel the Best 2 Rest exceeds expectations for the cost. Specific praise centers on the cooling performance, the removable cover, and the ease of setup. One verified buyer wrote: "Unboxed it at noon, slept on it that night. No back pain the next morning, which is more than I can say for my old spring mattress."

## Where It Falls Short

**Off-gassing:** The chemical smell is real. Despite claims of low VOC materials, multiple reviews mention a strong odor lasting 2-4 days. Plan to air it out in a well-ventilated room before your first night.

**Edge support:** This is the weakest aspect. Sitting on the edge causes significant sinkage — you'll slide toward the floor if you sit near the border. If you regularly sit on the edge of your bed to put on shoes or read, this will frustrate you.

**Durability concerns:** A handful of long-term reviewers (6+ months) report visible sagging in the center sleeping area. It's not universal, but it's worth noting for heavier users (over 220 lbs).

## Who Should Buy This

- **Budget-conscious back sleepers** wanting a medium-firm feel without spending $800+
- **Couples** needing motion isolation for different schedules
- **Apartment dwellers** who need a mattress that fits through tight hallways and stairs

**Skip it if** you're over 220 lbs, need strong edge support, or require a true plush feel for side sleeping.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does the off-gassing smell last?**
A: Plan for 2-4 days. Open a window, keep the room ventilated, and consider sleeping elsewhere the first night. The smell fades but isn't instant.

**Q: Is this mattress good for heavy people?**
A: The 10-inch profile and high-density base foam support up to roughly 250 lbs per the manufacturer. Real-world reviews suggest lighter sleepers have fewer durability issues. If you're over 220 lbs, consider a thicker mattress with reinforced edge support.

**Q: Does the cooling gel really work?**
A: Yes, for most people. It's noticeably cooler than standard memory foam, but it won't compete with active cooling mattresses that use phase-change materials or water circulation. It's a meaningful improvement, not a game-changer.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if** you want a reasonably cool, motion-isolating memory foam mattress at a budget-friendly price and can tolerate the off-gassing period.

**Skip it if** you need firm edge support, weigh over 220 lbs, or expect premium durability from a budget mattress.

The FORTNIGHT BEDDING Best 2 Rest is a solid value play — not a luxury product, but a competent one that delivers on its core promises. Just go in with realistic expectations about the smell and the edges.
