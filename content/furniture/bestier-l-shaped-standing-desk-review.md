## Bestier L Shaped Standing Desk: Big Corner Workspace with a Clever Lift-Top

Corner desks are a classic solution for maximizing workspace, and the **Bestier L Shaped Standing Desk with Lift Top** adds a modern twist: a lifting desktop section that lets you stand without buying an expensive motorized frame.

We analyzed 159 reviews to see how this $146 desk holds up in real home offices.

### Design and First Impressions

The Bestier desk arrives as a flat-pack in a heavy box. The grey wash finish has a modern, slightly rustic look that works well in both home offices and dorm rooms. The 55x55 inch L-shape provides a genuinely generous surface area — enough for a dual monitor setup, laptop, notepad, and then some.

The standout feature is the lift-top section on the main desk surface. A gas-spring mechanism allows you to raise the rear portion of the desk to standing height while the front edge stays put. This gives you the health benefits of standing without needing a full sit-stand frame.

One reviewer captured the general sentiment: *"Pictures simply do not do it justice! This desk is truly amazing."*

### Assembly Experience

Assembly is where the experience varies most. Some reviewers found it manageable: *"The only slightly challenging part was assembling the stand, as it can be a little tricky. However, with the screwdriver and tools provided, I was able to assemble it all by myself."*

Others had a harder time. One owner noted: *"Cool, solidly built but the movable part was difficult to assemble."* The lift-top mechanism has more moving parts than a standard desk, and the instructions can be unclear for this section.

The desk is heavy. Plan for assembly to take 1-2 hours, ideally with a second person to help align large panels.

### Standing vs Sitting — The Lift-Top in Practice

The lift-top works as advertised. It raises to a comfortable standing height for most users, and the mechanism holds position securely. One owner shared their experience: *"I am pushing the weight limit of the lift-top and have to use two hands in opposite corners to lift it but once up it stays up. I also guide it down, so it does not slam."*

However, the fixed desk height when sitting is worth noting. The same reviewer mentioned: *"The only drawback is not having a keyboard tray as the height when sitting is a bit too high for me even with my chair fully extended."*

If you are tall (over 5'10"), the sitting height is likely fine. Shorter users may want to invest in an under-desk keyboard tray.

### Storage and Organization

The reversible file drawer is a thoughtful addition. It mounts on either the left or right side depending on your room layout and includes an adjustable metal bar for hanging letter, legal, or A4 files.

The included monitor stand is simple but effective. It raises your monitor to a more ergonomic height, and being movable, you can position it where it works best for your workflow.

### Quality Control and Customer Service

Most units arrive in perfect condition, but the heavy panels are susceptible to shipping damage. One reviewer noted: *"Only one negative I will mention is one of the side boards was cracked. Did not affect the stability, nor could the crack be seen once it was assembled. But it was way too heavy to try to send it back."*

Another customer had a more positive resolution: *"I had a crack on the earlier shipment. The seller promptly shipped a replacement. It looks great, is spacious and of good quality."*

### Safety Note on Edges

One reviewer raised a real safety concern: *"The edges are SHARP. I have had this desk for almost 4 months now and I have taken two chunks of skin off my knees from moving too quickly near the edge of the desk!"*

If you have a layout where your legs will contact the desk edges frequently, consider adding edge guards or rounding the corners with sandpaper.

### Verdict

The **Bestier L Shaped Standing Desk** earns a **4.3 out of 5**. It delivers a generous L-shaped workspace with the added benefit of a lift-top standing option at a price well below motorized alternatives. The build quality is solid, the design is attractive, and the storage options are practical.

The assembly difficulty and sharp edge concerns are real but manageable. For the price, this is one of the best value L-shaped standing desks available.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the weight capacity of the lift-top section?**  
The lift-top holds a monitor and accessories easily. The gas spring mechanism is rated for typical desk loads. Pushing down on both corners is recommended to test the mechanism.

**Can the desk be used without the lift-top feature?**  
Yes. You simply do not engage the lift mechanism and use it as a standard height desk. The lift-top does not get in the way when lowered.

**What tools are needed for assembly?**  
All necessary tools are included. A Phillips head screwdriver is helpful but not strictly required.

**Does the desk wobble?**  
No. Multiple reviewers confirm the desk is sturdy and stable, even with the lift-top raised. The heavy construction and steel frame prevent wobbling.

**Is the drawer large enough for hanging files?**  
Yes. The file drawer includes an adjustable metal bar that accommodates letter, legal, and A4 hanging file folders.
