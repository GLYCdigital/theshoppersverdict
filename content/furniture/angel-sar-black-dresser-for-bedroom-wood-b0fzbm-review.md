The Angel Sar Black Dresser sits in a crowded field of budget-friendly bedroom storage. At 3.9 stars from 195 ratings, it's neither a standout nor a disaster — but the details matter. Here's what you actually need to know before you click buy.

**What you're getting**

The box contains the dresser body in six flat-pack panels, six drawers (fronts pre-attached to the boxes), metal glide rails, cam locks, and a hardware kit. As shown in the product image, the design is straightforward: a low-profile silhouette, matte black engineered wood, and slim metal bar pulls. The piece measures roughly 47 inches wide and 31 inches tall — compact enough for a small bedroom, but the full-width bottom drawer adds real capacity for bulkier items.

The engineered wood construction is what I'd expect at this price point. It's dense and surprisingly heavy — the assembled unit has a reassuring heft that prevents that "cheap furniture" wobble. The matte finish also handles daily use better than glossy alternatives; fingerprints and dust don't visibly accumulate the way they do on lacquered surfaces.

**The assembly reality check**

Here's where the Angel Sar tests your patience. Most buyers report 2–3 hours of assembly with two people. The cam lock system requires precision — and several reviewers note misaligned pre-drilled holes. One Amazon customer wrote, "The holes for the cams were slightly off on two panels. I had to re-drill them myself. Took a total of three hours." That's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you commit.

The drawer glides are metal (good), but the tracks need careful alignment during install. If you rush, drawers will stick or sit crooked. Take your time, use the included Allen wrench, and test each drawer before moving to the next.

**Where it shines**

Once assembled correctly, the storage capacity is genuinely impressive for the footprint. The six drawers are deep — you can stand folded jeans upright without cramming. The bottom drawer easily swallows a king-size comforter or a stack of sweaters. The metal glide rails are smooth when aligned properly, and the reinforced drawer bottoms don't bow under weight — a common failure point on cheaper dressers.

Buyers consistently praise the look. "Looks way more expensive than it is," one reviewer noted. "The matte black is sleek and modern. I get compliments from guests." Another said, "Durable for the price. I've had mine for 8 months and it still looks new."

**Where it falls short**

The finish is matte, not glossy. If you're expecting the high-shine black you see in some stock photos, you'll be disappointed. It's flat, almost chalky in certain lighting.

Drawer alignment out of the box is inconsistent. Even with careful assembly, a few owners report drawers that sit unevenly or scrape the frame. This is fixable with minor adjustments, but it's an annoyance at this price point.

The back panel is thin fiberboard and must be nailed into place. It's not structural, but it means the piece can feel slightly less rigid than solid-wood alternatives.

**Who should buy this**

- **First-time apartment dwellers** who need functional storage without spending $800 on a solid-wood dresser
- **Small bedroom owners** who need vertical capacity in a compact footprint
- **DIY-competent buyers** who don't mind a weekend assembly project

**Skip it if** you have zero patience for furniture assembly or need a true high-gloss finish.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does assembly actually take?**
A: Budget 2–3 hours with two people. One person can do it, but it's frustrating. The cam locks need simultaneous pressure from both sides.

**Q: Does the matte black finish scratch easily?**
A: It's resistant to fingerprints and minor scuffs, but it's not scratch-proof. Sharp objects and careless furniture moving will leave marks. Use felt pads under decor items.

**Q: Can I use this in a nursery?**
A: Yes, if you anchor it to the wall. The dresser is heavy enough to be stable, but any tall furniture in a child's room needs anti-tip hardware. The included strap kit covers that.

**The Verdict — 3.7/5 stars**

**Buy it if** you want solid budget storage and don't mind a lengthy assembly session. **Skip it if** you want plug-and-play furniture or a glossier aesthetic.

The Angel Sar Black Dresser delivers on storage, looks, and durability — but only after you've earned your assembly stripes. At this price, that tradeoff is fair. Real shoppers should go in with realistic expectations: this is a good dresser, not a great one.
