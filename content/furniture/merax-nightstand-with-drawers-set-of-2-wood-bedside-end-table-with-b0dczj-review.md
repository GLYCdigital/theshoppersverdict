# Merax Nightstand with Drawers Set of 2: The $100 Bedside Table That Beats the Big Box Stores

Let's be direct: finding a decent set of nightstands under $150 that doesn't wobble, arrive damaged, or look like cheap dorm furniture is genuinely hard. The Merax Nightstand with Drawers Set of 2 sits in that sweet spot — and after digging through 204 ratings and the build specs, here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

Two identical bedside tables, each measuring roughly 19.7" wide by 15.7" deep by 23.6" high. Each unit has two drawers — not one drawer and an open cubby, which is what many competitors at this price point try to pass off. The top surface is 1.5" thick engineered wood with a smooth laminate finish that mimics oak grain convincingly.

The set includes all hardware: cam locks, drawer slides, handles, and a hex wrench. No tools needed beyond what's in the box. Assembly takes about 20 minutes per unit if you follow the illustrated instructions — the pre-drilled holes on the side panels align correctly, which isn't always a given in this price range.

## Real-World Performance

The drawers are the standout feature here. They ride on metal ball-bearing slides — not the plastic tracks you'll find on cheaper sets — and they handle daily use without sticking. Each drawer holds a reasonable amount: a tablet, glasses, chargers, a book, and still closes flush.

The 4.1-star average across 204 ratings reflects genuine buyer satisfaction. One verified purchaser noted: *"I was skeptical at this price point, but the drawers slide smoothly and the finish matches the photos exactly. They've held up for six months now."* Another mentioned: *"Sturdier than I expected — the back panel is real wood, not cardboard."*

The top surface handles a lamp, phone, and water glass without flexing. That's the practical test that matters.

## Where It Falls Short

**Particleboard reality check.** The main body is engineered wood with a laminate veneer. If you spill water and leave it, the surface will swell and warp permanently. Use coasters. This is the tradeoff for the price.

**The film frustration.** Drawer fronts arrive with a protective plastic film that multiple reviewers described as "a pain to peel off completely." Budget 10 extra minutes to deal with it.

**No cable management.** The back panel is solid, so charging cords have to drape over the sides or hang down the front. Not a dealbreaker, but annoying if you're particular about cord organization.

**Assembly alignment quirk.** Some reviewers noted the drawer front screws need adjusting after installation — the fronts sit slightly uneven until you loosen, align, and retighten. It's a 5-minute fix, not a structural issue.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want two functional nightstands with real drawer storage and don't want to spend $300+ per unit. It's ideal for guest bedrooms, rental properties, or furnishing a new place on a budget. The neutral finish works in both modern and farmhouse-style rooms.

**Skip it if:** You need furniture that will survive moving multiple times — particleboard doesn't handle disassembly/reassembly well. Also skip if you want a statement piece; these are clean and functional, not decorative.

## FAQ

**Can the drawers hold a laptop?**
Each drawer interior is roughly 17" wide by 12" deep — a 13" laptop fits, but a 15" one won't close properly. Keep laptops on the top surface.

**How much weight can the top hold?**
The top surface handles 30-40 lbs without issue. A TV, lamp, and decor are fine. Don't sit on it — this isn't a step stool.

**Do the nightstands match in color between the two units?**
Yes, the laminate finish is consistent across the set. One reviewer confirmed: *"Both units look identical side by side — no color mismatch."*

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a functional, good-looking nightstand set under $150 and understand the particleboard tradeoff. **Skip it if** you're a heavy water-spiller or plan to move within a year.
