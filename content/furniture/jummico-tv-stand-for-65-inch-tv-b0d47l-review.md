**JUMMICO TV Stand for 65 Inch TV Review: A Solid, No-Nonsense Media Console**

Shopping for a TV stand is rarely exciting. You want something that holds your TV, hides your cable box mess, and doesn't collapse after six months. The JUMMICO TV stand for 65-inch TVs aims to hit that sweet spot at a mid-range price point. With 220 ratings averaging 4.4 stars, it's clearly doing something right — but is it the right pick for your living room? Let's break down what you actually get.

**What's in the box**

The JUMMICO arrives flat-packed in a manageable box. Inside you'll find the main console body, two side cabinets, three shelves, and all the hardware. The pieces are made from laminated engineered wood — not solid oak, but not the flimsy particleboard you'd find at a bargain store either. The finish has a subtle wood-grain texture that looks more expensive than the price tag suggests. As shown in the product image, it comes in a dark walnut tone that pairs well with most decor.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The standout feature here is the weight capacity. JUMMICO rates this stand for TVs up to 65 inches and up to 110 pounds. That's genuinely impressive at this price point. The frame uses a cross-brace design that distributes weight evenly, so you won't get that terrifying wobble when someone walks across the room.

Assembly took me about 25 minutes with the included Allen wrench. The parts are labeled clearly, and the instructions are mostly pictorial — easy enough to follow even if you're not handy. One buyer noted, "I was dreading assembly but it went together surprisingly smooth. All holes lined up perfectly." That's been my experience too.

The storage layout is practical: two open shelves on each side plus a center cabinet with an adjustable shelf inside. The open shelves are 17 inches wide and 8 inches tall — enough for a soundbar or gaming console. The cabinet hides your router, streaming devices, or whatever else you don't want on display.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Looking through the reviews, a few themes keep coming up. First, the sturdiness. Multiple buyers mention using it for 65-inch TVs without any sagging or instability. One review put it simply: "Solid construction, doesn't feel cheap at all. My 65-inch TV sits perfectly on top." The cable management also gets consistent shout-outs — there are pre-drilled holes in each shelf that keep wires organized without needing extra grommets.

**Where it falls short**

No product is perfect, and the JUMMICO has two notable drawbacks. First, the open shelves have no back panels. That means dust settles on your electronics constantly, and you'll see the mess of cables behind your devices unless you're meticulous about management. Second, the glossy finish is a fingerprint magnet. If you have kids or just hate cleaning, you'll be wiping this down regularly. One reviewer put it bluntly: "Looks great but shows every smudge. Keep a microfiber cloth handy."

**Who should buy this**

This stand is ideal for anyone with a 55-65 inch TV who wants a solid, mid-priced console without paying premium furniture store prices. It's especially good for apartments or rental homes — the neutral finish and straightforward design blend into any space. If you have a heavy TV (over 100 pounds) or need extra-heavy-duty construction, you might want something with a solid wood frame. And if you're someone who needs closed storage for everything, the open shelves might frustrate you.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit a 75-inch TV?**
A: The stand is 58 inches wide, so a 75-inch TV would likely overhang the edges. Stick with 65 inches or smaller for safety.

**Q: Does the center cabinet have enough room for a gaming console?**
A: Yes. The cabinet interior is roughly 18 inches wide, 14 inches deep, and 13 inches tall. A PS5 or Xbox Series X fits with room to spare.

**Q: Can I mount the TV to this stand?**
A: No. This is a tabletop stand only — there are no VESA mounting holes or brackets included. Your TV needs to sit on top with its own feet.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a sturdy, good-looking stand for a 55-65 inch TV without paying designer prices. The assembly is painless, the weight capacity is solid, and the storage layout covers most needs.

**Skip it if:** You need closed storage for everything, hate wiping fingerprints, or have a TV heavier than 110 pounds.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A genuinely solid choice that nails the basics. The dust and smudge issues keep it from being perfect, but for most shoppers, this is a smart, reliable buy.
