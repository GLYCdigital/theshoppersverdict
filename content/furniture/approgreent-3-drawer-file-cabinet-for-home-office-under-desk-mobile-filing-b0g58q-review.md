**The under-desk file cabinet that actually fits under your desk — and doesn't wobble.**

Most compact file cabinets under $80 are flimsy plastic trays on wheels that tip when you pull a drawer open. The Approgreent 3-drawer unit is different. It's a welded steel frame with real ball-bearing slides, and at 15.7 inches wide and 23.6 inches deep, it slides completely beneath a standard 30-inch-deep desk. If you're drowning in loose paperwork and your "filing system" is a pile on the floor, this is the fix.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the cabinet body, three drawer fronts, a set of casters (two locking, two non-locking), a hex wrench, and a small parts bag. The drawers arrive fully assembled — you only attach the four wheels and the drawer fronts. The instructions are simple line drawings, not confusing exploded diagrams. Most shoppers report being done in under 20 minutes.

The dimensions matter here: each drawer interior is roughly 13 inches wide by 16 inches deep. That's enough for letter-size hanging files (the rails are included) or a stack of manila folders side by side. The top drawer is slightly shallower — good for pens, notepads, and daily-use items — while the bottom two hold full hanging files.

**Real-world performance**

This is where the 4.3-star rating makes sense. The full-extension slides are the standout feature. You can pull a drawer all the way out and see the back of it — no fumbling for files that slide back. The locking mechanism uses a single key that locks all three drawers simultaneously, which is a nice security touch for tax documents or sensitive client files.

The casters are the weak point. They're functional but small. On hardwood, laminate, or tile, the cabinet glides smoothly. On medium-pile carpet, it rolls but drags. Several reviewers noted it catches on thick carpet thresholds. If your home office is in a carpeted bedroom, factor that in.

Weight capacity is about 30 pounds per drawer — enough for a full load of hanging files plus a ream of paper. The cabinet doesn't tip when you fully extend a loaded drawer, which is a safety win.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Reviewers repeatedly mention two things: the assembly ease and the solid feel. One shopper wrote, "I was expecting something flimsy for this price but the steel is sturdier than I thought. It doesn't rock when I pull the bottom drawer out." Another noted, "The key actually locks — my last cabinet's lock broke after a week." The black finish is matte and doesn't show fingerprints badly, which matters if you're constantly reaching under your desk.

**Where it falls short**

Be honest about this: the steel is thin. It's a light-gauge metal, so a hard bump from your office chair leaves a dent. This is not a commercial-grade filing cabinet — it's a home office piece. The key is also a basic cam lock, not a high-security mechanism. It deters casual snooping, not determined thieves.

The assembly, though quick, requires you to attach the drawer fronts with screws that can strip if you overtighten. Use the hex wrench, not a power drill, and you'll be fine.

**Who should buy this**

- **The home office organizer** who needs letter-size filing within arm's reach without sacrificing floor space
- **The remote worker** with a small apartment or studio who needs a mobile unit that can roll from desk to closet
- **The student** with a dorm room or rental who wants lockable storage for important documents

Skip it if you need legal-size filing (it won't fit), or if you have thick shag carpet and a heavy load of files — you'll want a four-wheeled unit with larger casters.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it hold letter-size hanging files?**
Yes. The rails are pre-installed and accommodate Pendaflex-style letter-size folders without bending.

**Q: Can I lock the drawers individually?**
No. One key locks all three drawers simultaneously. You can't lock just the bottom one.

**Q: Will it fit under a standing desk?**
Only if your desk's lowest height clears 24 inches. The cabinet is 23.6 inches tall without the casters, so measure before you buy.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a compact, lockable, mobile filing solution that doesn't squeak, wobble, or tip — and you're okay with light-gauge steel that requires gentle handling.

**Skip it if** you're a heavy user who opens drawers dozens of times daily, or you need legal-size capacity. For the price, the Approgreent delivers honest value: real ball-bearing slides, pre-installed hanging rails, and a footprint that disappears under your desk.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — a solid, functional file cabinet with minor compromises on caster quality and steel gauge. The right choice for most home offices.
