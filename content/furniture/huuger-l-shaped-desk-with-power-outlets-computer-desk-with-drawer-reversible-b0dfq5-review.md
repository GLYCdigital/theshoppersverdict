A sweaty glass of iced coffee will leave a white ring if you don't use a coaster. The finish also scratches if you slide heavy equipment across it — pick up your monitor, don't drag it.

**Q: Can the power outlets handle my gaming PC and monitor simultaneously?**
A: The module is rated for standard household load (typically 15A total). A gaming PC pulling 500W plus a monitor is fine. But don't daisy-chain a space heater or high-draw appliance. Use a dedicated surge protector for expensive electronics if you're paranoid — the built-in module is a convenience feature, not a full surge solution.

**Q: Does the reversible feature require disassembling the whole desk?**
A: No. You loosen the bolts connecting the main surface to the return wing, flip the wing, and retighten. The drawer and power module stay in place. It's a 15-minute job, not a rebuild.

**Q: Will it fit in a small apartment?**
A: The L-shape needs roughly 55 inches along each wall. Measure your room first. The return wing can be positioned to fit tighter corners, but you need clearance for the chair on the inward-facing side.
