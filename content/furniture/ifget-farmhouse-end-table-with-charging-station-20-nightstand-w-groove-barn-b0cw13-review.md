**The IFGET Farmhouse End Table doesn't try to be the fanciest nightstand you've ever owned — it tries to be the most practical one, and honestly, that's a smarter play.**

At 20 inches tall with a 19.7" by 15.7" footprint, this end table slots neatly beside a queen bed without overwhelming a small room. The sliding barn door is the headline feature — it glides open to reveal a shelf compartment that hides your clutter while keeping your phone charger, Kindle, and reading glasses within reach.

---

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the table (in two main body sections), the barn door, a back panel with the pre-mounted charging station, hardware, and a clearly illustrated instruction sheet. The charging station is the standout: **two standard AC outlets and two USB-A ports** mounted on the interior back panel. The 6-foot power cord is generous enough to reach most wall outlets behind a nightstand.

The finish is a medium-toned "wood" that's actually a textured particleboard and MDF combo. Up close, the groove pattern on the door mimics a plank look that fits the farmhouse aesthetic. It's not real wood, but at this price point, expecting solid oak would be unrealistic — and the laminate resists water rings better than raw timber ever would.

---

## Real-World Performance

**The charging station works as advertised.** Plug in your phone and lamp simultaneously; the USB ports push standard 5V/2.1A, which charges a modern smartphone at a reasonable pace — not warp-speed, but overnight is fine. One buyer noted: *"The charging ports are on the back, which is actually perfect — I don't want cables dangling in front. Plugged in my phone and a small fan, no issues."*

**The barn door is the weak point, but only during assembly.** The track system requires patience. If you rush the alignment, the door will stick or wobble. Multiple reviewers mention this: *"Took me 40 minutes to get the door sliding smooth, but once it's on, it works great."* Take your time with the screws — don't overtighten.

**Stability is solid.** The table doesn't wobble on carpet, and the feet have small plastic glides to protect hardwood floors. The top surface holds a 55" TV comfortably (some reviewers use it as a media console), though I'd keep anything heavier than a table lamp off the top shelf area.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

- **The hidden storage:** "I love that I can close the door and my clutter disappears. Guests think it's just a decorative cabinet."
- **Assembly clarity:** Despite the door track, most users report the instructions are straightforward. One reviewer said: *"Took me 20 minutes, and I'm not handy at all."*
- **Value:** The combination of charging ports + farmhouse styling at this price is hard to beat. Multiple reviews call it "a steal" or "looks way more expensive than it is."

## Where It Falls Short

**No drawer.** If you need to stash medications, glasses, or remotes in an enclosed space, the open shelf behind the barn door doesn't keep dust out. A small tray or basket solves this, but it's an extra purchase.

**The cable management hole is on the back panel only.** If your outlet is on the opposite side of the room, the cord becomes a trip hazard. Measure your outlet location before buying.

**The USB ports are USB-A, not USB-C.** If you've gone all-in on USB-C cables, you'll need an adapter.

## FAQ

**Q: Can the charging station handle a tablet and phone simultaneously?**
Yes — the two USB ports and two AC outlets operate independently. Charging a tablet and phone at once works fine, though both will charge at standard (not fast-charge) speeds.

**Q: Is the barn door actually functional or just decorative?**
It's fully functional. It slides along a top-mounted track and a bottom guide. It's the most fragile part of the build — be gentle when opening and closing.

**Q: What are the exact dimensions?**
19.7" wide x 15.7" deep x 20" high. The shelf opening behind the door is roughly 14" wide x 10" tall — enough for a few books, a tablet, and a small bin.
