# YaFiti Swivel Bar Stools with Backs: The $100-A-Pair Reality Check

Let's cut through the noise. At this price point, most swivel bar stools are either cheaply constructed with wobbly bases or look like they belong in a waiting room. The YaFiti 24" counter height stools sit squarely in the middle — and for most buyers, that's exactly the right spot.

## What You Actually Get

The set ships as two stools, each measuring roughly 24" from floor to seat — that's counter height, not bar height. If you're pairing these with a standard 36" kitchen counter or island, the math works. If you've got a taller bar setup, you'll need to look elsewhere; these won't clear a 42" bar.

Each stool features a padded faux leather seat and backrest over what the manufacturer claims is a heavy-duty steel frame. The base is a four-leg design with a footrest ring that sits about 7" off the floor. As shown in the product image, the look is clean and modern — slightly mid-century, but neutral enough to blend with most kitchens.

## Real-World Performance

The 360° swivel is the headline feature here, and it doesn't disappoint. The ball-bearing mechanism is smooth and silent — no grinding, no catching. One Amazon reviewer noted, "The swivel is buttery smooth, my kids spin on these constantly and they haven't loosened up at all." That's consistent with what we see in the majority of the 210 ratings.

The seat height is fixed at 24", so there's no pneumatic adjustment. That's a tradeoff — you're getting a simpler mechanism that's less likely to fail, but you lose the flexibility of a gas-lift model. If multiple people of different heights will use these regularly, measure your counter and your tallest family member's leg length before committing.

The steel frame feels solid. The powder-coated finish resists scratches well, though it's not entirely immune to scuffs from shoes or barstool drag. Weighing in at roughly 14 pounds per stool, they're heavy enough to stay put when you swivel but light enough to move when cleaning.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The comfort level punches above the price tag. Multiple reviewers specifically called out the high-density foam padding:
- "The seat cushion is actually thick — not like those cardboard-thin stools you see everywhere."
- "I sat on these for a 3-hour dinner party and didn't need to stand up once."

Assembly is straightforward but not tool-free. The steel frame bolts together with the included hardware, and the seat attaches to the frame with four bolts. Plan for 25-30 minutes per stool if you're doing it solo. One reviewer warned, "Use your own screwdriver, the included allen wrench strips easily."

## Where It Falls Short

The faux leather is the biggest compromise. It's not breathable. In a warm kitchen or during summer months, you'll feel the heat buildup. For a breakfast-nook stool where you sit for 15 minutes at a time, it's fine. For a home office setup where you'll be planted for hours, look elsewhere.

The 24" height is also a limiting factor. This is counter height, not bar height. If you have a standard 36" counter, you're good. If you're pairing these with a pub-height table or a raised bar, the 24" seat height won't clear the surface properly. The product title is honest about this, but it's worth repeating — measure twice, order once.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Kitchen island setups** — the swivel makes it easy to turn toward the counter or the conversation
- **Small dining areas** — the footprint is compact, and two stools fit snugly under a 36" counter
- **Budget-conscious buyers** — you're getting decent construction and genuine comfort for the money

**Skip them if:** you need bar height (30"), want adjustable seats, or expect genuine leather. This is a faux leather product at a faux leather price point.

## FAQ

**Q: What's the weight capacity?**  
The manufacturer lists 300 pounds per stool. The steel frame and four-leg design handle that fine, but the swivel mechanism is the weak point — if you're near the top of that range, expect some resistance in the spin over time.

**Q: Do these scratch hardwood floors?**  
The base has plastic floor glides, not felt. They protect against scratching but can leave faint marks on soft wood. Adding felt pads is a cheap insurance policy.

**Q: Can the backrest be removed?**  
No, it's fixed. If you want a backless stool, this isn't the model.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** you need a pair of solid, comfortable counter-height stools under $150 total and don't mind the non-breathable upholstery.

**Skip it if:** you need bar height, want adjustable seats, or plan to sit for hours at a stretch.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The YaFiti stools deliver what they promise: smooth swivel, decent comfort, and honest construction. The height limitation and faux-leather heat retention keep them from perfection, but for the price, they're a solid value that most buyers will be happy with.
