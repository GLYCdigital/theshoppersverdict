Let's be blunt: buying a bunk mattress online is a gamble. Standard sizes rarely fit those weird built-in bunk frames, and you're stuck guessing whether the foam will feel like a brick or a marshmallow. The FoamTouch 6-inch bunk mattress takes a swing at both problems — and mostly connects.

## What you're actually getting

The headline feature here is the sizing. FoamTouch builds this mattress in any dimension between 36" x 72" (a short twin) and 48" x 80" (a full long). That's not just marketing — you punch in your exact measurements at checkout, and they cut the foam to spec. For anyone with a custom-built loft bed or an RV bunk with weird proportions, this is the whole ballgame.

The mattress itself is a 6-inch stack of high-density polyurethane foam with a 1-inch quilted, breathable cover on top. You're not getting memory foam contouring here — this is a firmer, more traditional feel. The cover is removable and washable, which matters for kids' bunks where juice spills and bedwetting are inevitable.

## Real-world performance

The medium-firm density is the right call for a bunk. It's supportive enough that a 200-pound adult won't bottom out, but soft enough that a 40-pound kid isn't sleeping on a board. Multiple buyers describe it as "hotel firm" — not plush, not hard. If your kid likes sinking into a mattress, this won't be it. But for back support and for stacking two bunks without the top mattress sagging into the lower one, it's solid.

One thing that surprised reviewers: the edge support. Even at 6 inches, the foam holds its shape along the perimeter. Kids rolling to the edge of a top bunk won't feel like they're about to tip over. That's a legit safety consideration that many cheaper foam mattresses get wrong.

## What owners consistently praise

The custom sizing is the runaway winner in the feedback. One buyer wrote, *"Our bunk frame is 37.5" wide — nothing standard fits. This came in at exactly 37.5" and sits flush with zero gaps."* Another parent noted, *"We ordered the 6x36x72 for a camper bunk and it fit like it was made by the same factory."* When the measurements are right, it's a perfect fit.

The price is the second most-cited plus. FoamTouch undercuts most national brands by 30-40% on comparable custom sizes, and buyers repeatedly mention the value for a kids' room where the mattress will get jumped on for five years.

## Where it falls short

The off-gassing is real. Despite the "CertiPUR-US" and low-VOC claims, the foam arrives with a noticeable chemical smell that takes a day or two to dissipate. Plan to air it out in a well-ventilated room before the first sleep.

The 6-inch height is also a tradeoff for heavier adults. If you're over 220 pounds, you'll feel the support layer compress noticeably. This is a kids' bunk mattress — don't buy it as a primary adult bed and expect luxury.

Finally, the cover zips off for washing, but the fire sock underneath is loose. One reviewer warned, *"I washed the cover and the white fire barrier slipped sideways. Had to wrestle it back into place."* If you're not handy, that's a frustrating 20 minutes.

## Who should buy this

- **Parents with custom bunk frames** — this is the only practical option if standard sizes leave gaps
- **RV and camper owners** — the 36" x 72" size fits most narrow bunks perfectly
- **Budget-conscious families** — you're paying half of what a brand-name mattress costs

**Skip it if** you need a plush, sink-in feel, or if the sleeper is a heavy adult. Also skip if you can't tolerate any initial odor — this one needs airing out.

## FAQ

**Is this mattress actually 6 inches thick?**
Yes, the foam core is a full 6 inches. Some buyers measured 5.75" due to the cover compression, but it's within normal tolerance. The height works well for standard bunk rails.

**Can I use it on a regular bed frame?**
Technically yes, but it's designed for tight spaces. On a standard twin frame, it'll feel firm and low-profile. It's fine for guest rooms, just don't expect plush comfort.

**How long does the smell last?**
Most buyers report 24-48 hours with the cover off in a ventilated room. It's not the worst off-gassing I've seen, but it's definitely present.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're fighting a non-standard bunk or RV frame and need a custom fit without the custom price tag.

**Skip it if** you want plush comfort or a zero-odor unboxing experience.

The FoamTouch 6-inch bunk mattress earns its 4.6/5 score through sheer practicality. It's not fancy, but it solves a real problem — fitting oddly-sized bunks with a firm, safe, affordable mattress. For its intended purpose, it's the right purchase.
