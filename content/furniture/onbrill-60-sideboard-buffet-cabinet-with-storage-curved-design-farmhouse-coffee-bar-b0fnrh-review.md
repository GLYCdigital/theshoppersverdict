Let me save you some scrolling: the ONBRILL 60-inch sideboard is a genuinely attractive piece of farmhouse-style furniture that most buyers are happy with, but it's not a weekend- warrior's dream to assemble. Here's what 200 Amazon ratings at 4.0 stars actually tell you — and what they don't.

**What's in the box**

You get the expected flat-pack experience: two side cabinets, one center open shelf, four adjustable shelves total, and a top panel that spans the full 60 inches. The curved door fronts are the design centerpiece — as shown in the product image, they're not flat slabs but have a subtle arch that catches light nicely. Hardware is included, but the included hex wrench is the kind that makes your palm sore after ten minutes. Plan on having your own Phillips screwdriver and a rubber mallet on hand.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The MDF construction is denser than what you typically find at this price tier. The manufacturer applied a moisture-resistant coating that buyers specifically call out as holding up well against condensation from coffee cups and occasional wine spills. The adjustable shelves sit on metal pegs, which is a step up from plastic clips.

The 60-inch width is the sweet spot. It fits a standard dining room wall without overwhelming it, and the center open shelf gives you display space for a coffee station or a few stacked cookbooks. The two closed cabinets each hold roughly six dinner plates, a stack of bowls, and serving pieces without crowding. One buyer noted: *"I fit my entire everyday china set plus my grandmother's gravy boat in here. The adjustable shelves made the difference."*

**Where it falls short**

Let's be direct about the assembly. Multiple reviewers use phrases like "took forever" and "bring a friend." The hinge plates are the weak point — pre-drilled holes on some units arrive slightly off, forcing you to either re-drill or flex the plate. This is fixable, but it's the kind of thing that turns a 90-minute job into three hours.

The open center shelf has no rear panel. If this cabinet backs against a wall, nobody sees it. If it's a room divider or backs to a hallway, anyone walking behind can see whatever you've stacked on that middle shelf. That's a design shortcut that feels cheap.

Also worth noting: the curved doors don't have soft-close hinges. They close fine, but they'll clack if you let them go. At this price, that's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing.

**Who should buy this**

This is for you if you're furnishing a dining room or large kitchen and want a piece that looks like you spent more than you did. It's also a strong fit for a coffee bar setup — the center shelf is exactly the right height for a 12-cup machine and a grinder. It's not for you if you're renting an apartment with narrow doorways — the assembled top panel is unwieldy, and you'll likely need to build it in the room where it's staying.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can one person assemble this alone?**
A: Technically yes, but you'll regret it. The top panel is heavy and awkward to align without a second set of hands. Budget three hours solo; two hours with help.

**Q: Does the finish match the photos?**
A: The wood grain is slightly more muted in person than in the product images, but the curved doors and overall silhouette look as advertised. No buyer complaints about color mismatch.

**Q: Will it hold a heavy microwave?**
A: The top is rated for roughly 80 pounds of distributed weight. A standard microwave (30–40 lbs) is fine. Don't park a full-size mixer and a microwave on the same corner.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a substantial, good-looking sideboard that delivers on storage and style without breaking the bank, and you're willing to invest an afternoon in assembly.

**Skip it if** you have zero patience for misaligned pilot holes, need soft-close hardware, or plan to use it as a room divider where the open back would be visible.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A strong value with real design presence, docked slightly for assembly fiddliness and the open-back center shelf. For the price, it's hard to beat.
