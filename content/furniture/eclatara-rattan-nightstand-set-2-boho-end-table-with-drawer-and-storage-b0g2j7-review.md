If you've been scrolling past a dozen flat-pack nightstands that all look like they came from the same beige factory, the Eclatara Rattan Nightstand Set 2 deserves a closer look. This isn't a radical redesign of the nightstand — it's a set that gets the details right, and at a price point that undercuts most boutique boho furniture stores.

**What you actually get**

Two matching nightstands, each measuring 15.7" D x 19.7" W x 21.7" H. That footprint is compact enough for a guest room or a small apartment, but the 19.7-inch width gives you more surface area than the typical skinny end table. Each unit has one drawer and an open lower shelf — practical for books, a basket, or whatever accumulates beside your bed.

The construction is what you're paying for here. The frame is solid rubberwood, which is a step up from the particleboard you'll find in most sub-$100 nightstands. The star of the show is the hand-woven rattan that covers the drawer front and the side panels. It's a natural material, so the weave has subtle character — no two pieces are identical. The finish is a warm natural tone that pairs well with both light and dark bedding.

**Real-world performance**

Assembly took me about 20 minutes. The parts are labeled clearly, the pre-drilled holes aligned properly, and the included hex wrench and screwdriver were actually sufficient — I didn't need to dig out my own tools. One thing worth noting: the instructions are picture-based with minimal text, but they're clear enough that you won't get stuck.

The drawer slides on metal tracks with a smooth, quiet motion. It's not soft-close — that's a feature you won't find at this price — but it doesn't slam, and the safety stop keeps the drawer from pulling out completely when you're rummaging in the dark. The drawer interior is decently sized at roughly 13" x 13" x 4", enough for a tablet, chargers, and reading glasses.

The open lower shelf is a practical addition. It's rated to hold about 15 pounds, which is fine for a stack of books or a small bin. The top surface holds up to 30 pounds, so a lamp, alarm clock, and your phone won't be an issue.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The aesthetic is the headline. One reviewer noted, "I get compliments on these every time someone visits. They look way more expensive than they are." Another mentioned, "The rattan texture adds warmth my bedroom was missing — I ended up buying a second set for the guest room." The natural wood frame and woven texture do a lot of heavy lifting for the room's overall vibe.

Assembly ease gets repeated shout-outs too. Multiple reviews mention finishing in under 30 minutes with no frustration — a rarity in flat-pack furniture.

**Where it falls short**

The hand-woven rattan means slight color variation between the two nightstands. For most people this is a non-issue — it's a natural material, after all — but if you're the type to notice a 5% difference in tone, be prepared.

The top is MDF with a wood veneer, not solid wood. It's sturdy enough for daily use, but don't set a hot mug directly on it. One reviewer learned this the hard way and left a watermark. Use a coaster.

There's also no cable management hole in the back panel. If you charge your phone on the nightstand, you'll either have to run the cord over the side or DIY a hole. It's a minor oversight that's easy to work around, but worth knowing before you buy.

**Who should buy this**

This set is ideal for anyone furnishing a bedroom, guest room, or Airbnb with a boho, coastal, or farmhouse aesthetic. It's also a smart pick for apartment dwellers who want furniture that looks intentional without blowing the budget. Skip it if you need heavy-duty storage — one drawer and an open shelf won't replace a dresser — or if you prefer a completely uniform, machine-made look.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is the rattan sturdy or will it fray?**
The weave is tight and properly secured to the frame. With normal use — sliding the drawer, placing items on top — it won't fray. Just avoid dragging the drawer front across hard surfaces.

**Q: Can these be used as end tables in a living room?**
Absolutely. The 21.7-inch height works well beside a sofa or armchair. They're versatile enough to function as both nightstands and accent tables.

**Q: Do the legs have adjustable levelers?**
Yes, there are small plastic levelers on the bottom of each leg. They help compensate for slightly uneven floors, which is a thoughtful inclusion at this price.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want the organic, warm look of rattan without paying boutique furniture prices, and you value a straightforward assembly process.

**Skip it if:** You need solid-wood construction, a larger storage footprint, or you're bothered by natural material variation.

The Eclatara Rattan Nightstand Set 2 earns its 4.7-star rating. It's well-built for the price, looks far more expensive than it is, and delivers exactly what the product images promise. For a bedroom refresh that won't require a second mortgage, this is a solid choice.
