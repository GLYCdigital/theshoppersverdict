## What You Actually Get

Unboxing reveals the table itself, a carrying bag, and the hardware. The top is a 23.6-inch round MDF surface with a matte black finish that resists scratches better than you'd expect at this price point. The steel frame is powder-coated—no bare metal edges to snag your tablecloth.

Here's the kicker: the legs telescope. You can set it from 29.5 inches (standard cocktail table height) up to 41.7 inches, which turns it into a standing-height podium. That's a genuinely useful range. A single locking knob per leg adjusts the height, and once tightened, the legs stay put—no creeping down mid-event.

Folded, it's about 4 inches thick. It fits behind a car seat, in a closet, or under a bed. The whole package weighs around 22 pounds, which is manageable for one person to carry.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides.

First, the carrying bag is minimal. It's a thin nylon sleeve with a single shoulder strap. It protects the table from scratches during a quick car ride, but it won't survive checked luggage or rain. If you're a frequent vendor, budget for a sturdier bag or a padded case.

Second, the locking knobs require attention. If you don't tighten them fully, the legs can shift slightly during use—especially on uneven outdoor ground. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth checking them before you load anything heavy.

Third, the MDF top is durable but not waterproof. Spills left to sit will stain. For indoor events or covered outdoor setups, this is a non-issue. For rain-exposed use, bring a waterproof tablecloth.
