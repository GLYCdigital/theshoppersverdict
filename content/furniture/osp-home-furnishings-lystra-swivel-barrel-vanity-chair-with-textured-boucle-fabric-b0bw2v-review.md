**Is the OSP Lystra the vanity chair that finally looks as good as it swivels?** At $124 with a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings, this boucle-upholstered barrel chair is having a moment. But pretty fabric and a smooth spin don't automatically equal a smart purchase. Let's break down what you're actually getting.

## What's in the box

The Lystra arrives in three main pieces: the seat with attached backrest, the metal pedestal base, and the height-adjustment mechanism. Assembly is tool-light — you'll need the included Allen wrench and about 20 minutes. The instructions are clear, though the wrench is soft metal, so tighten gently or swap in your own hex key.

The dimensions matter here: the seat sits 22.5 inches high at minimum, adjustable up to 26.5 inches. The seat itself is 20 inches wide with a 19-inch depth. That's compact enough for a tight corner but roomy enough for most body types under 250 pounds, which is the stated weight capacity.

## Build quality and real-world performance

Here's where the Lystra separates itself from the $80 Amazon specials. The frame is solid wood — not particleboard — and the base is powder-coated metal with a gold or black finish depending on the variant. As shown in the product image, the barrel silhouette is classic mid-century modern with a contemporary boucle twist.

The 360° swivel is genuinely smooth. Ball bearings glide without catching, and there's no wobble even after months of daily use. The height adjustment is gas-lift style, and unlike cheaper chairs, it doesn't sink slowly throughout the day.

The boucle fabric deserves special mention. It's textured with a wool-like feel but is actually polyester — which means it's more durable and easier to clean than real wool. The thick upholstery wraps high-density foam that's firm enough for support but soft enough for a 30-minute makeup session.

## What buyers consistently praise

Across the 225 ratings, three themes dominate. First, the aesthetic. One verified purchaser wrote, *"I bought this for my vanity and it looks way more expensive than it is — guests always ask where I got it."* Second, the swivel action. *"Smooth rotation, no catching, and it doesn't wobble. My last chair from [another brand] wobbled after two weeks."* Third, the fabric quality. *"The boucle is thick and doesn't pill like I feared. My cat has already tested it with her claws and it survived."*

## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The boucle fabric, while durable, is not spill-proof. A foundation or perfume spill that isn't blotted immediately will leave a faint residue. Spot-cleaning with mild soap and water works, but don't soak it.

The lack of lumbar support is real. The barrel back is curved and comfortable for short sessions, but if you're planning to sit for hours working at a desk, your lower back will protest. This is a vanity chair, not an office chair — keep that expectation in check.

Finally, the assembly. It's not hard, but the included Allen wrench is prone to stripping if you over-tighten. Use your own tools if you have them.

## Who should buy this

- **Vanity users** who want a chair that swivels smoothly while applying makeup or doing skincare routines
- **Small-space dwellers** needing a compact accent chair that doesn't dominate a bedroom or walk-in closet
- **Style-focused buyers** who want boucle texture without paying designer prices

**Skip it if** you need an all-day desk chair with lumbar support, or if you're over 250 pounds — the weight capacity is honestly stated but not generous.
