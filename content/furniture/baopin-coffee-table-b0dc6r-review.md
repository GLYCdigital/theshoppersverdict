**Where It Falls Short**

The matte finish has a real weakness: water rings. If you set a cold, sweating glass directly on the surface and leave it for a few minutes, you'll see a mark. It fades after an hour or so, but repeated exposure will eventually dull the finish. Use coasters — that's not a suggestion, it's a requirement with this table.

There's also no cable management. If you plan to run charging cords from the shelf to the top, they'll hang visibly down the front. A small cable grommet in the top would have solved this; its absence feels like a missed opportunity.
