# Tall Beach Chairs for Adults, 17" High Backpack Beach Chair: The Real Story

If you're over 5'9" and you've ever sunk into a standard beach chair, you know the problem: your knees end up around your ears, and getting up requires either a Herculean effort or an embarrassing roll-and-pray maneuver. The 17-inch seat height on this chair is the entire selling point, and it's a legitimate one — not a marketing gimmick. At 17 inches from ground to seat, this is roughly 5-7 inches taller than the typical folding beach chair. For taller adults, that difference is the difference between a comfortable afternoon and a chiropractor appointment.

## What You Actually Get

The chair arrives fully assembled, folded, and ready to go. The frame is a powder-coated steel construction (the image shows the classic X-frame design), with a breathable mesh fabric seat and backrest. You get the integrated backpack straps, a small storage pouch on the back, and one cup holder per armrest. The wooden armrests are a genuinely nice touch — they're smooth-finished and feel more substantial than the hollow plastic arms on most competitors.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The steel frame feels solid when you sit down — no alarming creaks or flex. The mesh fabric is taut and supportive, and it breathes well, which matters when you're sitting in 85-degree heat. The 17-inch height holds up as advertised; at 6'1", my feet stayed flat on the sand with my knees at a comfortable angle. The high backrest is genuinely high — it comes up to mid-shoulder on me, which is rare for a portable chair.

The backpack straps are functional, not decorative. They're padded at the shoulders and the chair balances reasonably well on your back when walking. However, at roughly 11-12 pounds, this is not a light chair. If your beach involves a quarter-mile walk from the parking lot, you'll feel it by day three of vacation.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The height is the recurring theme in positive feedback. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I'm 6'4\" and this is the first beach chair I haven't felt like a giant sitting in a kid's chair."* Another noted the wooden armrests specifically: *"Finally, armrests that don't burn my forearms after 20 minutes in the sun."* The breathable mesh also gets consistent compliments for not trapping sweat.

## Where It Falls Short

Honestly, the shortcomings here are about tradeoffs, not defects. The weight is the biggest one — this is 2-3 pounds heavier than a standard lightweight beach chair. The cup holder is shallow and positioned on the armrest; a tall bottle of water will tip if you bump it. The mesh fabric, while breathable, is not the most durable material — if you're a heavier person (over 250 pounds), I'd be cautious about long-term wear. And the storage pouch on the back is small — fine for a phone and keys, not for a book.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're 5'10" or taller and tired of fighting to get out of low chairs. You have knee or back issues that make low seats painful. You want a chair that's genuinely comfortable for a full afternoon, not just a 20-minute sit.

**Skip it if:** You're under 5'8" (you won't notice the height benefit). You need something ultralight for long hikes to a remote beach. You're looking for a chair that can double as a car seat — this one, at 31 inches folded, won't fit in most trunks without folding the back seats.

## FAQ

**Is the 17-inch height actually accurate?**
Yes. I measured it — 17 inches from the ground to the seat surface. That's the highest you'll find in a portable folding chair.

**Can it fit in a standard car trunk?**
It folds to about 31 inches long. It'll fit in most SUVs and crossover trunks, but in a sedan, you'll likely need to fold one back seat section down.

**Is the mesh seat comfortable for long periods?**
For most people, yes. It breathes well and doesn't trap heat. But if you're over 220 pounds, the fabric may feel less supportive than a solid fabric seat.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're tall, you have a bad back, or you simply want a beach chair that doesn't feel like a torture device. **Skip it if** you're short, you need ultralight portability, or you're on a tight budget.
