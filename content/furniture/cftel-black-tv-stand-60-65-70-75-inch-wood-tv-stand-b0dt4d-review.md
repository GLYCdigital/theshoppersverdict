## What You Actually Get

The box contains the main console top, two side panels, four adjustable shelves, a back panel with pre-cut cable ports, and all the hardware — plus a hex key and a printed manual that’s actually legible. The dimensions are generous: roughly 60 inches wide, 15.7 inches deep, and 23.6 inches tall. That depth is critical — it accommodates most soundbars without hanging off the front edge.

The top surface is rated for 150 pounds. That’s enough for a 75-inch Sony or Samsung without worrying about sagging. You also get two open compartments and two enclosed cabinets behind the adjustable shelves, giving you flexible storage for a receiver, game consoles, or a media collection.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The CFTEL uses a combination of engineered wood and a high-gloss black laminate finish. It looks more expensive than it is — the matte black surface has a subtle texture that hides minor scratches. The legs are steel, which is a welcome upgrade over the hollow plastic feet you see on cheaper stands.

Assembly takes about 40 minutes with two people. The pre-drilled inserts line up correctly, and the labeled hardware eliminates guesswork. One buyer noted: *“I’ve assembled a dozen TV stands. The adjustable shelves are a genuine highlight. You can position them to fit a tall AV receiver or space them out for games and books. The soft-close hinges on the cabinet doors are a small touch that makes a big difference in daily use.

## Where It Falls Short

The back panel is thin — about 1/8-inch fiberboard. It does its job, but it won’t survive heavy impact. If you have toddlers or rambunctious pets, secure the stand to the wall with the included anti-tip strap. This isn’t a flaw unique to CFTEL, but it’s worth flagging.

The black finish shows dust and fingerprints. It’s not a dealbreaker, but if you have a dog that sheds or kids who touch everything, you’ll be wiping it down weekly. Also, the included handles are basic brushed metal — functional but not premium.

One more thing: the stand is designed for TVs with a center-mounted base. If your TV has widely spaced legs (common on 75-inch models), confirm the footprint fits within the 60-inch top before ordering.
