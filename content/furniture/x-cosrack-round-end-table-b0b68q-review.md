## The Little Table That Punches Above Its Weight

Most end tables under $50 are wobbly, cheap-feeling disasters that you regret within a month. The X-cosrack Round End Table isn't that. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, this compact side table has quietly become a favorite for small apartments, dorm rooms, and anyone tired of furniture that falls apart.

Here's the real story after digging through buyer feedback and the specs.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the tabletop, three metal legs, an X-brace crossbar, and the hardware. That's it — no tools required beyond the included Allen wrench. The round top measures 15.7 inches in diameter, and the table stands 20.5 inches tall. That's right in the sweet spot for sitting next to a standard sofa or armchair.

The frame is powder-coated black steel, and the top has a wood-grain laminate finish. As shown in the product image, it's a clean, minimalist look that works with industrial, farmhouse, or modern decor. It weighs about 8 pounds, so it's light enough to move around but heavy enough to feel planted.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

Here's where this table surprises. The X-brace design isn't just decorative — it locks the three legs together rigidly. Multiple buyers noted the stability. One reviewer wrote, *"I was skeptical at this price, but it doesn't wobble at all. My cat jumps on it and it stays put."* Another mentioned placing it on thick carpet without the dreaded lean.

Assembly genuinely takes under 10 minutes. The legs screw into pre-welded brackets, and the X-brace bolts across the middle. One buyer said, *"Took me longer to find my glasses than to put this together."* That's the experience most people report.

The 15.7-inch surface fits a coffee mug, a book, and a TV remote comfortably. It's not a dining surface — don't expect to balance dinner plates on it — but for its intended job as a drink-and-remote holder, it's sized right.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The laminate top scratches. One reviewer noted, *"A ceramic mug left a faint ring after a few weeks."* Use coasters, and don't drag anything metal across the surface. It's not a defect — it's the reality of a budget-friendly laminate finish.

The bigger limitation is the lack of storage. There's no lower shelf, no drawer, no hidden compartment. If you need a place to stash magazines, chargers, or coasters, this table won't do it. It's purely a surface. Buyers who wanted storage consistently mentioned this as their only real complaint.

Also, the wood grain is printed. From across the room it looks like oak. Up close, you'll see it's a repeating pattern. If you're a wood snob, this will bother you. If you just want a table that looks good and holds your coffee, it won't.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Apartment dwellers** with narrow gaps between furniture — this fits where rectangular tables won't

Skip it if you need storage, want real wood, or expect furniture-grade durability at this price point.

## FAQ

**Is the X-cosrack table stable on uneven floors?**
Mostly yes. The X-brace provides solid rigidity, but like any three-legged table, it can rock slightly on a truly uneven surface. A small furniture pad under one leg fixes this.

**Can two people assemble it?**
One person can do it alone. The legs are lightweight and the screw points are easy to reach. No second set of hands needed.

**What's the weight capacity?**
The frame supports roughly 50 pounds without issue. A lamp, books, and drinks are no problem. Don't sit on it — it's not built for that.

## The Verdict

For the price, the X-cosrack Round End Table delivers exactly what it promises: a sturdy, good-looking surface that assembles in minutes and doesn't wobble. It's not premium furniture, but it's honest furniture — and at 4.6 stars, most buyers agree.
