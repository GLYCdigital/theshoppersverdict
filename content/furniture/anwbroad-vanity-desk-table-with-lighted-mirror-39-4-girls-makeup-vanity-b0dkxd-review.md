Let's cut through the pink-tinted marketing. The ANWBROAD vanity desk with lighted mirror is a solid mid-range pick for the tween/teen bedroom set, but it's not the luxurious Hollywood setup the photos suggest. At 39.4 inches wide, it's positioned squarely between a compact jewelry armoire and a full dressing table — and for most families, that's the sweet spot. Here's what 215 ratings at 4.3 stars actually tell you.

**What's in the box**

You get the tabletop, two side drawers, a hutch with the LED mirror, a matching stool, and hardware. The engineered wood panels arrive with pre-drilled holes and labeled parts — nothing cryptic. The included stool is a bonus, though it's the weakest component. The mirror itself is the centerpiece: a 30-inch wide rectangular LED panel with three color temperatures (warm, neutral, cool) and a touch sensor. Dimmable, with memory function, so it returns to your last setting. That's genuinely useful, not a toy feature.

**Real-world performance**

Assembly is the first hurdle. Budget 45 minutes to an hour with two people — the drawer slides are the fiddly part. One reviewer noted, "The instructions are mostly pictures, and the drawer alignment took me three tries before they slid smoothly." That's consistent. Once together, the desk is sturdier than the price suggests. The frame uses 0.6-inch thick engineered wood, and the anti-tip bracket (which secures the hutch to the wall) is a thoughtful safety touch. The surface holds a 24-inch monitor, a makeup organizer, and a lamp simultaneously without sagging.

The LED mirror is the reason you're buying this. It's bright enough for makeup application — 6500K cool white for precision work, 3000K warm for evening touch-ups. The touch sensor responds instantly, no lag. However, the light strip is adhesive-mounted around the mirror's perimeter. Several long-term reviewers mentioned that after 6–8 months of daily use, the corners can start peeling. It's fixable with double-sided tape, but it's a cheap manufacturing shortcut.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The aesthetic is the headline. "My daughter actually keeps her room clean now because she loves how this looks," one parent wrote. The white finish with gold accents photographs beautifully and doesn't look cheap in person. The drawer space is another winner — each side drawer fits a full makeup bag plus hair accessories. Multiple reviewers specifically called out the mirror's brightness range, with one saying, "The dimmest setting is perfect for early mornings without blinding everyone."

**Where it falls short**

The stool cushion is thin particle board wrapped in faux leather. At 15.7 inches tall, it's fine for quick touch-ups, but don't plan on studying there for hours. The drawers are also shallow — 3.5 inches deep — so tall bottles need to lie flat. And while the LED mirror is dimmable, there's no USB port or outlet built in, which is a miss for a vanity in 2026. The cord management is basic; you'll want a cable clip to keep the adapter from dangling visibly.

**Who should buy this**

This is for parents of girls aged 8–14 who want a dedicated space that grows with them. It's also solid for a small apartment bedroom where a full dresser won't fit. The 39.4-inch width means it clears most standard bedroom layouts without dominating. Skip it if you need a serious workspace — the 15.7-inch depth is tight for a laptop plus textbooks — or if you're looking for heirloom-quality wood. This is good furniture, not great furniture.

**FAQ**

*Is the mirror bright enough for makeup?* Yes. The 6500K setting at full brightness is comparable to a professional ring light. The dimmer lets you adjust from subtle to intense.

*Can adults use this?* The 15.7-inch seat height suits average-height adults, but the drawers are sized for smaller items. It's a youth vanity, not a full dressing table.

*Does it come in other colors?* The current listing shows white and pink options. The white is more versatile if your daughter's style changes.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a well-lit vanity with real storage that won't break the bank, and you're comfortable with 45 minutes of assembly. **Skip it if** you need a study desk hybrid or expect premium materials at this price point.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The LED mirror and sturdy frame earn the score; the thin stool and adhesive light strip hold it back from a 4.5. For the typical tween bedroom, it's a reliable, attractive choice that delivers on its core promise.
