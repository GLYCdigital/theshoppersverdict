Some outdoor side tables feel like an afterthought — flimsy aluminum tubes, a plastic top that fades in one season, and a wobble that gets worse every time you bump it. The WILOUNGE cast aluminum side table is not that. It's a 20-pound chunk of solid metal that feels like it was carved, not assembled. ## What you actually get

This is a small accent table — 16 inches in diameter and roughly 18 inches tall. It arrives fully assembled in a single piece, which means zero Allen wrenches, zero confusing diagrams, zero "is this part supposed to be loose?" moments. Just take it out of the box and set it down.

The construction is the headline here. It's cast aluminum, not stamped sheet metal or hollow tubing. The fluted design (those vertical ridges you see in the product image) adds visual texture and structural rigidity. The powder-coated finish is applied evenly, including the underside — a detail that tells you the manufacturer wasn't cutting corners.

## Real-world performance

The weight is the first thing you'll notice. At roughly 20 pounds, this table stays put in moderate wind. I've seen buyer reports of it surviving stormy coastal weather without tipping. One reviewer noted, *"This thing is heavy. The wind knocked over my other furniture but this didn't budge."* That's the cast aluminum advantage — solid mass you can't get from tubular construction.

The top surface is smooth and slightly recessed. It holds a standard coffee mug, a wine glass, or a book without issue. The 16-inch diameter is workable but tight. If you're planning to set down a pitcher and two glasses, you'll be juggling. This is a "one person's drink and phone" table, not a serving station.

Heat resistance is solid — the metal won't warp or melt if you set down a hot grill pan, though the finish can discolor with extreme direct heat. Cold drinks will produce condensation, which beads up on the powder coat rather than soaking in. Wipe it down and it's fine.

## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the limitations.

**Size is the biggest tradeoff.** At 16 inches across, this is a small accent table, not a functional side table for entertaining. If you're picturing a spot for your coffee and a plate of snacks, think again. One reviewer put it plainly: *"It's smaller than I expected. Fine for a drink but don't plan on using it for much else."* Measure your space before buying.

**No drainage holes.** Rainwater pools on the recessed top surface. It doesn't damage the aluminum, but it means wiping it dry after storms or dealing with mineral spots if you have hard water. A quick tilt drains most of it, but it's an oversight.

**The dark finish shows everything.** Pollen, bird droppings, and dust are instantly visible on the dark powder coat. If your patio is under trees, you'll be wiping this down regularly.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this rust?**
No. Cast aluminum doesn't rust. The powder coat protects the surface, and even if it gets scratched, the underlying metal won't corrode like steel would.

**Q: Can I leave it outside year-round?**
Yes, with caveats. It handles sun, rain, and snow fine. But standing water will leave spots, and in freezing climates, ice can form in the recessed top. Either tip it upside down or wipe it dry before hard freezes.

**Q: How much weight can it hold?**
The construction is very solid — it can handle a heavy potted plant or a stack of books without flexing. Realistically, 50+ pounds is no problem. The concern isn't strength; it's the small surface area.
