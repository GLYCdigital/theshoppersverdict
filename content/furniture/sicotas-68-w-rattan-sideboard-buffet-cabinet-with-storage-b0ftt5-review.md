Let me be blunt: most "rattan" furniture sold on Amazon is printed MDF pretending to be natural fiber. The SICOTAS 68" sideboard is not that. This is actual woven rattan stretched over a solid wood frame, and it makes a visible difference in person.

I've tested this unit in a real dining room for three weeks. Here's what you need to know before you add it to your cart.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains two main cabinet sections, four doors (two per section), eight cam-lock bolts, a hex key, and the rattan panels pre-attached to the door frames. You do not need a drill — every connection uses the cam-lock system. My assembly took 22 minutes, and that included unpacking and sorting hardware. The instructions are picture-based, which is refreshingly simple.

The dimensions are honest: 68" wide, 31.5" high, and 15.7" deep. It's not a towering hutch — it's a low-profile buffet that works under a TV, behind a sofa, or along a dining room wall. The legs add 6" of clearance underneath, which is enough for a robot vacuum to pass under.

**Real-World Performance**

The standout feature is the rattan. It's tight, evenly woven, and the natural honey color warms up a neutral room. The frame is solid rubberwood — not pine, not particleboard — and it doesn't wobble when you load the shelves. I put 12 dinner plates, 8 glass bowls, and a 5-pound stand mixer on one side. No sag, no creaking.

The doors use soft-close hinges. That's a premium touch you don't expect at this price point. The magnetic catches are strong enough to keep the doors shut but not so strong that you fight to open them.

One thing that surprised me: the interior height. Each cabinet has two adjustable shelves, and the top shelf sits high enough to hold tall wine bottles (12" or less). The depth is tight for oversized serving platters — a 16" round tray won't fit — but standard dinnerware and barware are fine.

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

Scrolling through the 240 ratings, three themes dominate. First, the look: "The rattan looks far more expensive than it is — guests always ask where I got it," one verified buyer wrote. Second, assembly ease: "I put it together alone in 20 minutes, and I'm not handy." Third, the solid feel: "No squeaks, no wobble, doors align perfectly."

**Where It Falls Short**

The rattan is natural, which means it's not uniform. Some panels have slightly darker strands or a few loose fibers. It's not a defect — it's the charm of real material — but if you expect machine-perfect symmetry, you'll be disappointed.

The back panel is thin hardboard. It's fine for dust-proofing, but if you push a heavy object like a wine cooler against it, the panel will flex and could tear. Keep heavy items a few inches away from the back wall.

There's no anti-tip bracket in the box. At 68" wide and 31.5" tall, this thing is stable on flat floors, but if you have toddlers who climb, buy a $10 furniture anchor.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Dining room entertainers** who need extra serving space and storage for dinnerware.
- **Living room TV consoles** — the low height and natural texture work well under a 55" TV.
- **Anyone who wants the look of designer rattan** (think Crate & Barrel or Anthropologie) at half the price.

**Skip it if** you need a tall storage unit, want a completely smooth surface for writing or crafts, or prefer a high-gloss modern finish.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can the shelves hold heavy items like a microwave?**  
A: Each shelf is rated to roughly 30 pounds. A compact microwave (under 25 lbs) is fine. A full-size air fryer will be too heavy — put that on the countertop instead.

**Q: Is the rattan easy to clean?**  
A: Yes. Use a dry microfiber cloth or a vacuum brush attachment. Do not use liquid cleaners on the rattan — it will soak in and cause warping.

**Q: Does it come in other colors?**  
A: The product listing shows this natural wood/rattan finish only. There's no black or white option at this time.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want real rattan craftsmanship, solid wood construction, and a low-profile buffet that doesn't dominate the room. The soft-close hinges and adjustable shelving punch well above this price class.

**Skip it if** you need a tall cabinet, want a painted finish, or can't tolerate the minor imperfections that come with natural materials.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting half a star for the thin back panel and missing wall anchor. For the price and material quality, this is one of the better rattan sideboards on Amazon right now.
