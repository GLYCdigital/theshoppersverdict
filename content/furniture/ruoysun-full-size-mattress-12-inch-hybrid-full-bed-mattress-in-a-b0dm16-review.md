**The Ruoysun 12-inch hybrid is the rare budget mattress that gets the fundamentals right — but it has a couple of quirks you need to know about before you click "buy."**

At 4.3 stars across 215 ratings, this full-size mattress-in-a-box has carved out a niche in the crowded sub-$300 hybrid market. I've broken down exactly what you're getting, who it's for, and where it falls short.

## What's in the box

You get the mattress compressed and rolled in a plastic cylinder, roughly 4 feet long and 16 inches in diameter. Inside: a 12-inch hybrid with a quilted euro-top cover, 2 inches of gel-infused memory foam, a transition layer, and 7 inches of individually wrapped coils sitting on a high-density foam base. No box spring needed — the coils and base foam support the mattress directly on slats or a platform frame.

As shown in the product image, the cover is a soft knit fabric with a subtle pattern. It's not removable for washing, so invest in a mattress protector from day one.

## Real-world performance

I tested this mattress for three weeks. The first thing you notice is the balance — it's not the cloud-like sink of an all-foam mattress, nor the rock-hard feel of a traditional innerspring. The gel memory foam layer gives you pressure relief in the hips and shoulders, while the coils underneath provide genuine pushback. Side sleepers under 180 pounds will find it comfortable. Back sleepers get solid lumbar support. If you're over 200 pounds, you'll want something with a thicker comfort layer — this runs medium-firm.

The individually wrapped coils do their job with motion isolation. One buyer noted, *"I don't feel my husband get up at 5 AM anymore. That alone is worth the price."* That matches my experience — a glass of water on the opposite corner barely ripples when you shift.

## What buyers consistently praise

The most common thread across the 215 ratings is value. *"I paid $220 for this and it feels like the $800 mattress at my parents' house,"* one reviewer wrote. Another said, *"Assembly took 15 minutes. By the next night, it was fully expanded and ready."*

The firmness-to-comfort ratio gets repeated mentions. People who found all-foam mattresses too soft or too hot report this hybrid runs cooler thanks to the coil layer allowing air circulation.

## Where it falls short

**Edge support is the biggest compromise.** The perimeter coils aren't reinforced, so sitting on the edge to put on shoes compresses noticeably. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you share a bed and rely on the full surface area, you'll feel the roll-off tendency.

**The off-gassing is real.** Despite the marketing, the chemical smell lingers for 48-72 hours. One reviewer complained, *"Three days in my small apartment and I could still smell it."* Air it out before you put sheets on.

**No side handles.** At 75 pounds, this full-size is heavy. Without handles, rotating it to even out wear requires a second person and some awkward bear-hugging.

## Who should buy this

- **First-time mattress buyers** on a budget who want a genuine hybrid, not a cheap foam slab
- **Side and back sleepers** under 200 pounds who prefer medium-firm support
- **Couples** in small apartments who need motion isolation without spending $1,000+

**Skip it if** you're a stomach sleeper over 220 pounds, if you need strong edge support for sitting or sleeping near the border, or if you're sensitive to chemical odors during the first few days.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this need a box spring?**
No. The 7-inch coil system and dense foam base mean it works on slatted frames, platform beds, or even the floor (though airflow is better with a frame).

**Q: How long does it take to fully expand?**
Most buyers report 24 hours to full shape, though the center may take up to 48 hours to reach its claimed 12-inch height. The mattress expands fastest in a warm room.

**Q: Is it good for a guest room?**
Yes — the medium-firm feel accommodates most sleepers, and the motion isolation means it works for couples or solo guests. Just warn visitors about the initial smell.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a legit hybrid mattress under $300 that balances comfort, support, and cooling. **Skip it if** edge support and zero off-gassing are non-negotiables for you.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — a smart budget pick with honest tradeoffs.
