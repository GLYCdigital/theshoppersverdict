If you've been scrolling past "Hollywood mirror" vanities because they look like overpriced dorm-room decor, the ANWBROAD Makeup Vanity Set deserves a second look. This isn't a flimsy prop piece — it's a functional workstation that actually solves the two biggest vanity problems: poor lighting and zero storage. With 215 ratings averaging 4.3 stars, the consensus is clear: this is a solid mid-range option that undercuts the big names without feeling cheap.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the vanity desk, the 10-bulb LED mirror (which mounts to the desk surface), two side storage drawers, and a matching cushioned stool. The desktop measures roughly 39.4 inches wide — enough room for your everyday products without sprawling across a full room. The stool is padded and upholstered in a soft fabric that matches the desk's finish, which is a nice touch you don't always get at this price point.

The mirror itself is the centerpiece, and it delivers. The 10 bulbs are bright — not just for show. You get three color modes (warm, cool, and daylight) and adjustable brightness via a touch sensor on the mirror. The touch controls are responsive, a common complaint with cheaper LED mirrors, and buyers generally report the lighting is even without harsh hotspots.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

This is where the ANWBROAD earns its keep. The desk is made from engineered wood with a smooth laminate finish that wipes clean easily — important when you're dealing with powder fallout and liquid foundation spills. Buyers consistently note the surface doesn't scratch or stain easily. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I've had this for three months and it still looks brand new, even after my daily routine."*

The storage drawers are deeper than you'd expect for a vanity this size. They slide smoothly on metal runners and can hold palettes, brushes, and smaller skincare bottles without jamming. The stool is sturdy — it supports up to 250 pounds without wobbling, which is reassuring given how many cheap vanity stools fold under pressure.

**The catch:** assembly. This is not a 20-minute Ikea-style build. Expect 45–60 minutes of work, mostly dealing with small screws and aligning the drawer tracks. The instructions are diagram-heavy with minimal written guidance, which frustrated several buyers. If you're not handy with a screwdriver, budget extra time or have a friend help.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The lighting is the runaway favorite. Multiple reviews highlight that the 10 bulbs make a tangible difference in makeup application — no more leaning into a dim bathroom mirror to check your blending. The included stool and drawers are also repeatedly mentioned as adding real value, since comparable vanities from brands like Homary or Baxton Studio often sell for $50–$100 more without those extras.

## Where It Falls Short

The mirror is not height-adjustable. At 30 inches high, it's fine for average-height users, but if you're over 5'8" or prefer a taller seat, you'll be looking down at the mirror. That's a genuine ergonomic issue for daily use.

The LED bulbs are soldered into the light strip — they're not individually replaceable. If one burns out (which happens eventually), you're looking at replacing the whole strip, which costs about $20–$30. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

A few buyers also noted the desk surface can show fingerprints and smudges easily, though a quick wipe fixes it.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a makeup enthusiast who needs reliable, even lighting and actual storage, and you want a complete set without paying boutique prices. It's also great for renters — the footprint is compact enough for a small bedroom or apartment corner.

**Skip it if:** You're tall (over 5'8") and need an adjustable mirror height, or if you have zero patience for assembly. Also skip if you want real wood — this is engineered particleboard, not solid hardwood, so it won't survive a move unscathed.
