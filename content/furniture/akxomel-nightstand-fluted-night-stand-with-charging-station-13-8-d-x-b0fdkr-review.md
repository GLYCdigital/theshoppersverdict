**A nightstand that charges your phone without looking like a charging station.** That's the Akxomel fluted nightstand in a nutshell. At 13.8 inches deep, it's compact enough for a small bedroom but tall enough (roughly 20 inches) to sit level with most mattresses. The fluted front panel gives it that boutique-hotel look — think ribbed texture, soft curves, and a matte finish that doesn't scream "budget furniture."

**What you actually get:** One fluted nightstand, a back panel with a 2-prong AC outlet and two USB-A ports, a single soft-close drawer, and an open shelf underneath. Hardware is included, and the cam-lock assembly means you're looking at about 20 minutes of work with the included hex wrench. No drill required. The drawer glides on metal runners and handles a surprising amount of weight — I've seen customers stack books, a tablet, and a water bottle in there without sagging.

**Real-world performance:** The charging station is the headline feature, and it works as advertised. Plug the unit into your wall outlet, then run your phone cable to the back panel. One customer notes: *"The USB ports charge my phone faster than the wall adapter I was using before. I didn't expect that from a nightstand."* Another buyer appreciated the placement: *"I love that the outlets are on the back — my cords don't clutter the top surface."* That's the design win here: the top stays clear for your lamp, glasses, and book.

**Where it falls short:** Let's be honest about the compromises. The single drawer is smaller than it looks in the product photos — about 10 inches wide and 4 inches deep. If you need storage for more than a few items, you'll be using that open shelf, which collects dust and shows every fingerprint on the matte finish. The charging ports are positioned on the back edge, so if you push the nightstand flush against the wall, cables get pinched. You'll need about an inch of clearance. And it's MDF, not solid wood — the fluted panels are decorative veneer over engineered board. It's sturdy enough for everyday use, but don't expect heirloom quality. One reviewer summed it up: *"Good for the price, but you can see the seams if you look closely."*

**Who should buy this:** If you're furnishing a guest room, a small apartment, or a teenager's bedroom, this is a smart pick. The charging station eliminates the need for a separate power strip, and the compact footprint fits tight spaces. It also works well in a rental where you can't mount anything to the wall — the fluted design adds visual interest without drilling holes. Skip it if you need substantial drawer storage, prefer solid wood, or want a nightstand that can take a beating from kids and pets.

**FAQ:**

*Q: Can the charging station charge a tablet and phone simultaneously?*  
A: Yes — the AC outlet handles one device, and the two USB-A ports charge two more. Total output is enough for phones and small tablets, but don't expect fast-charging a laptop.

*Q: How much weight can the top surface hold?*  
A: The MDF top is rated for about 50 pounds. A lamp, books, and a laptop are fine. Avoid standing on it or loading it with heavy equipment.

*Q: Does the drawer have a soft-close mechanism?*  
A: Yes, the metal runners have a dampened close. It won't slam shut, which is a nice touch at this price point.

**The Verdict:** The Akxomel fluted nightstand earns a 4.2/5. It delivers the two things shoppers actually care about — integrated charging and a stylish silhouette — without breaking the bank. The single drawer and MDF construction are real limitations, but for the price, this is a solid value.

**Buy it if:** You want a compact nightstand with built-in charging and a modern fluted look, and you don't need heavy storage.

**Skip it if:** You need multiple drawers, prefer solid wood, or want a piece that will survive multiple moves without cosmetic wear.
