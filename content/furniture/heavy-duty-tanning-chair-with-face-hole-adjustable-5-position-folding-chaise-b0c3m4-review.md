# Heavy Duty Tanning Chair with Face Hole: Is the $80–100 Price Tag Worth It?

You've seen the cheap resin loungers crack after one summer. You've also seen the $400 spa-grade chairs that require a second mortgage. This heavy-duty tanning chair with face hole sits somewhere in that awkward middle — and honestly, it might be the sweet spot. Here's what 220 buyers at 4.4 stars are actually saying, and what they're not.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the chair frame (steel, powder-coated), the mesh webbing seat, a padded face-hole cushion, and the hex wrench for assembly. The whole thing measures 75 inches long fully extended, 24 inches wide, and stands 12 inches off the ground at the lowest position. That's a full-length lounger, not a stubby pool chair.

The face hole is 6 inches across with a soft foam rim — big enough for most adults to breathe comfortably face-down. The 5-position backrest adjusts from near-flat to roughly 45 degrees, locking with a metal pin mechanism that clicks into place.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The steel frame is the star here. Multiple reviewers note it doesn't creak or flex under heavier weights — one buyer wrote, "I'm 240 pounds and this thing doesn't even flinch when I shift around." The powder coating resists chipping, and the X-frame folding design means it collapses flat for storage.

The mesh webbing is where things get interesting. It's taut and supportive, but it's also *just* mesh. There's no cushioning layer. For a 30-minute tanning session, fine. For an hour of reading face-down, expect some serious pressure marks on your hip bones and ribs. Several long-session users mention having to flip over every 15 minutes just for comfort.

The 5-position adjustment system works smoothly — the backrest slides into each notch with a satisfying click. No fiddly knobs, no loose locking pins. Just lift, adjust, lock.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

- **Stability:** "Finally a tanning chair that doesn't tip over when I lean to grab my water bottle."
- **Face hole design:** "The face opening is actually positioned correctly — I can breathe without twisting my neck."
- **Easy storage:** "Folds flat enough to slide behind my door. Takes up almost no space."

## Where It Falls Short

The lack of padding is the #1 complaint across reviews. If you're buying this primarily for comfortable prone tanning, budget for a separate foam pad or towel layer. The mesh also stretches slightly after a few weeks of heavy use — not sagging, but noticeably looser.

Assembly is straightforward but requires patience. The bolts are soft metal and can strip if you crank them down with too much force. Use the included wrench, not a power drill.

One more thing: at 69 inches folded, this isn't a "throw it in the trunk" chair for most sedans. It fits, but it'll dominate your cargo space.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Backyard tanners** who want a durable lounger that lasts more than one season
- **Heavier users** (250+ lbs) who've broken cheaper resin chairs
- **People who actually use the face hole** — it's genuinely well-designed
- **Pool-side readers** who want adjustable positions without paying spa-chair prices

**Skip it if** you want plush cushioning, need a chair that fits in a small car trunk, or plan on marathon tanning sessions longer than 45 minutes without a padding add-on.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the face hole work for stomach sleepers?**
A: Yes, but the foam rim adds about an inch of height. If you're petite or have a short torso, your chin might not reach comfortably. Most average-height adults report no issues.

**Q: Can I leave it outside in the rain?**
A: The powder-coated frame resists rust, but the mesh will degrade faster with constant UV and moisture exposure. Bring it inside or cover it when not in use.

**Q: What's the actual weight capacity?**
A: The frame is rated for 400+ lbs, and reviewers confirm it holds up. The mesh is the weak point — it won't tear, but it will stretch more under heavier sustained weight.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a sturdy, adjustable tanning chair that won't fall apart after one summer and you don't mind adding your own padding.

**Skip it if** you need a plush, cushioned lounger or have limited trunk space for transport.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Durable frame, honest design flaws, and a face hole that actually works. At this price point, it's the best value in its category, provided you know what you're getting into.
