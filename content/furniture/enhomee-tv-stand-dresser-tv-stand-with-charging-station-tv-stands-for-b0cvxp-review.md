# EnHomee TV Stand Dresser Review: The 2-in-1 Media Console That Actually Earns Its Space

Most TV stands are either all looks or all function. The EnHomee TV Stand Dresser tries to be both — a four-drawer dresser on one side, an open media console on the other, with a built-in charging station tucked into the back. After digging through 215 ratings and the spec sheet, here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the TV stand, four drawers, an open cubby shelf, and the hardware kit. The dimensions are worth noting before you order: it's **47.2 inches wide, 15.7 inches deep, and 27.5 inches tall** — sized for TVs up to 55 inches. The top surface is a laminated particleboard with a wood-grain finish that looks more expensive than the price tag suggests.

The charging station is the headline feature: **two AC outlets and two USB ports** built into the back panel, so you can plug in your TV, soundbar, and phone chargers without a visible power strip. It's a small touch, but it makes a real difference in how clean your setup looks.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The frame is **0.6-inch thick particleboard** with a waterproof, scratch-resistant laminate. That's not heirloom furniture, but it's solid for the price point — several reviewers noted the drawers glide smoothly and close softly, even after months of daily use. Each drawer holds up to 15 pounds, which is plenty for controllers, remotes, and streaming devices.

The open cubby on the left (or right, depending on how you orient it) fits a cable box, game console, or a small collection of books. The top surface handles a 50-inch TV without any noticeable flex, though a few reviewers mentioned they'd feel safer with a 43-inch or smaller.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The charging station is the runaway favorite. One reviewer put it plainly: *"No more crawling behind the TV to find an outlet. This thing has saved my back and my sanity."* Another noted the drawers are *"surprisingly roomy — I fit all my streaming sticks, extra HDMI cables, and a full game controller collection in one drawer."*

The dual-purpose design also gets love from small-space dwellers. As one buyer said: *"I live in a studio apartment. This replaced both my TV stand and my dresser. It's not perfect, but it does double duty without looking like a compromise."*

## Where It Falls Short

The assembly is the biggest complaint — and it's legitimate. Multiple reviewers report **45 to 60 minutes of work** with two people, and the cam locks require patience. One reviewer warned: *"If you've never assembled flat-pack furniture before, budget extra time. The instructions are decent but not great."*

The other notable miss: **no cable management holes** in the back panel. For a stand with a built-in charging station, you'd expect a way to route cords cleanly. Instead, you'll be tucking cables around the edges or adding your own clips.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Studio apartment dwellers** who need furniture that does double duty
- **Cord-haters** who want charging built in without a visible power strip
- **Budget-conscious shoppers** who want a decent-looking media console under $150

**Skip it if** you need a statement piece or you hate assembling furniture with more than four screws.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Ask

**Q: What's the maximum TV size it can hold?**
A: EnHomee rates it for up to 55 inches, but the surface is 47.2 inches wide. A 55-inch TV will overhang slightly on most models. For safety and aesthetics, stick with 50 inches or smaller.

**Q: Are the charging ports always on, or do they shut off?**
A: They're always on as long as the stand is plugged in. There's no switch, so unplug the unit if you're leaving for extended periods.

**Q: Does the drawer hardware hold up over time?**
A: Based on reviewer feedback, yes — soft-close glides have held up well for months of regular use. The 15-lb weight limit per drawer is realistic; don't overload them.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a functional, decent-looking TV stand that solves the outlet problem and gives you extra storage without taking up more floor space.

**Skip it if** you want premium build quality, easy assembly, or a piece that will impress design-sensitive guests.

The EnHomee TV Stand Dresser earns **4.2/5 stars**. It's not perfect — assembly is a chore and cable management could be better — but for the price, it delivers solid value for small spaces and practical buyers.
