# HIFIT Bathroom Storage Cabinet, 67″: The Tall, Narrow Solution That Actually Works

If your bathroom has that awkward dead zone next to the toilet — too narrow for a standard cabinet, too wide to ignore — the HIFIT 67-inch storage cabinet is built for exactly that spot. With 235 ratings averaging 4.7 stars, this isn't a flashy piece. It's a workhorse. Here's what you need to know before you buy.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the cabinet panels, cam locks, dowels, a back panel, and hardware. No tools included, so have a Phillips screwdriver and a rubber mallet (or a hammer with a cloth) ready. The finished unit measures roughly 15.5 inches deep, 11.8 inches wide, and 67 inches tall — narrow enough to slot beside a pedestal sink or toilet tank.

You get three fixed shelves and one adjustable shelf behind a solid door. The wood-grain laminate finish looks more expensive than the price point suggests, but be clear-eyed: this is engineered wood, not solid lumber.

## Real-World Performance

Let's talk about what 67 inches of vertical storage actually does. It holds a surprising amount — think a stack of bath towels, a hair dryer, cleaning supplies under the sink, and toilet paper reserves. The adjustable shelf is the star; you can reconfigure it to stand tall bottles upright instead of stacking them sideways.

Assembly is where this cabinet asks for patience. The 67-inch height means long panels that are awkward to align alone. Multiple buyers note the cam-lock system works fine but takes time. One reviewer put it plainly: "Took about an hour and a half, but the instructions were clear and everything lined up perfectly." Another said, "Do yourself a favor and get a second person for the door alignment — it's fiddly."

Once assembled, the cabinet feels stable. The back panel is thin but functional, and the door closes with a satisfying, solid click. The floor-level feet are adjustable, which matters more than you'd think — bathroom floors are rarely perfectly level.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The recurring theme in positive reviews is the space-saving design. One customer wrote, "I have a tiny half-bath and this fits perfectly between the toilet and the wall. It holds everything without looking bulky." Another noted, "The finish looks much nicer in person — it's a matte wood grain, not shiny plastic."

The closed-door design also gets consistent love. If you're tired of looking at a shelf full of toilet paper and shampoo bottles, this hides it all behind one clean panel. That's a genuine upgrade over open shelving units.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The particleboard construction means moisture is the enemy. In a steamy bathroom, you'll want to wipe down any splashes quickly. Several reviewers mentioned water damage near the base after months of use — avoid placing it directly in a splash zone.

The lack of wall-mount hardware is a miss at this height. A 67-inch freestanding cabinet is top-heavy, especially when loaded. If you have young children or large dogs, anchor it to the wall yourself — the manufacturer doesn't provide the bracket.

Also, the 11.8-inch width is a double-edged sword. It fits tight spaces, but it won't hold bulky items like a stack of queen-sized comforters. Know your storage needs before committing.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Small bathroom owners** with narrow gaps that need vertical storage — this is the best fit
- **Renters** who want storage without drilling into walls (though anchoring is recommended)
- **Anyone tired of cluttered open shelving** who wants a clean, closed look
- **Skip it** if you need a heavy-duty cabinet for a damp, poorly ventilated bathroom or if you're unwilling to spend over an hour assembling

## FAQ

**Q: Can the shelves hold heavy items like a stack of towels?**
A: Yes, the fixed shelves handle folded towels and standard bathroom supplies without sagging. The adjustable shelf is slightly less sturdy — keep heavier items on the fixed shelves.

**Q: Is this cabinet waterproof?**
A: No. It's water-resistant at best. The particleboard will swell if exposed to standing water or constant high humidity. Use it in a well-ventilated bathroom and wipe up spills promptly.

**Q: Does the door open fully?**
A: Yes, the hinge allows the door to open a full 180 degrees, which is helpful in tight spaces. Just make sure you have clearance on the hinge side — the door needs about 15 inches of swing space.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a narrow, tall storage solution that hides clutter and fits in awkward bathroom corners. The 67-inch height maximizes vertical space without eating floor area, and the closed design keeps your bathroom looking tidy.

**Skip it if** you're in a high-humidity environment, you hate assembly, or you need something wider and more heavy-duty.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A solid, space-saving cabinet with real-world limitations. It earns its high rating by doing exactly what it promises, provided you respect its particleboard construction and budget time for assembly.
