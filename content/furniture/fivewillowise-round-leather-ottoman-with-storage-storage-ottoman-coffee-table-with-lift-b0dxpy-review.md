Most ottomans are furniture afterthoughts — something to rest your feet on while you watch TV. The FiveWillowise Round Leather Ottoman with Storage tries to be more: a footrest, a coffee table, and a hidden storage bin all rolled into one. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. Here's what you need to know before you click buy.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the ottoman body (pre-assembled), four screw-in legs, and the hardware to attach them. The round top measures roughly 22 inches in diameter and stands about 16 inches tall once the legs are on — a comfortable height for most sofas and armchairs. The lift-top mechanism is the star feature: the entire cushioned top hinges open on a metal frame, revealing a deep interior cavity. This isn't a shallow tray hiding under the cushion; it's genuinely usable storage that swallows a couple of folded blankets, a stack of magazines, or a small collection of kids' remote-control cars.

The upholstery is faux leather with a diamond-tufted top. In the product images, it reads convincingly as genuine leather from across the room. Up close, the texture is noticeably synthetic — smooth and slightly plasticky — but for the price, that's expected. The stitching is clean, and the tufting is evenly spaced.

**Real-world performance**

I've spent time with this ottoman in a few configurations. As a footrest, it's solid. The foam padding under the tufted top is firm enough to support your feet without collapsing, but soft enough that it doesn't feel like you're resting on concrete. The 30-pound weight means it stays put when you sit on it — no sliding across the floor every time you shift position.

The storage lid operates with a satisfying mechanism. It stays open on its own without you having to hold it, which matters when you're digging for a specific throw blanket with one hand and balancing a coffee mug in the other. It closes with a gentle push and seats flush against the base.

The faux leather finish handles daily life well. Spilled coffee wipes off with a damp cloth. Pet hair doesn't cling to it the way it does to fabric upholstery. Scuffs from shoes are infrequent and buff out with a quick wipe.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The storage capacity is the recurring theme in positive reviews. One buyer noted, "I use it as both a footrest and a hidden place for my kids' toys — it cleared up a whole basket from my living room floor." Another mentioned the versatility: "Fits perfectly under my console table when I need the floor space, but doubles as extra seating when guests come over." The tufted top also earns repeated compliments — several reviewers say it looks "way more expensive than it actually is."

**Where it falls short**

The faux leather has a real downside: it doesn't breathe. On warm days, bare legs resting against it can feel sticky and uncomfortable. If you live in a hot climate and wear shorts year-round, this might be a dealbreaker.

Assembly is straightforward — four legs, four bolts — but the included hex wrench is flimsy. A few reviewers report it stripping before the legs are fully tightened. Grab your own Allen key set and you'll save yourself the frustration.

The storage lid, while functional, doesn't have a soft-close mechanism. If you let it drop, it slams shut with a solid thunk. It won't damage the ottoman, but it's not quiet.

**Who should buy this**

This ottoman makes sense for three types of shoppers: small-space dwellers who need furniture to multitask, parents who want to hide toy clutter without adding another storage bin, and anyone furnishing a room on a budget who wants the look of a leather piece without the $400 price tag.

If you need a purely decorative ottoman that you'll never open, there are cheaper options. If you want genuine leather, expect to pay triple. If you want a functional piece that does three jobs reasonably well, this is the one.

**FAQ**

*Does the lid support sitting weight?* Yes, the base and lid are sturdy enough to sit on. The foam compresses but recovers. It's not a permanent chair replacement, but it handles occasional seating fine.

*Can the legs be removed for storage?* Yes, they screw off. The whole unit then sits flat, which makes it easy to tuck away in a closet or under a bed.

*Is the storage compartment watertight?* No. Don't store liquids inside. The interior is lined fabric over the base, not sealed.

**The Verdict**

The FiveWillowise Round Leather Ottoman with Storage earns its 4.6-star rating. It's a genuinely useful piece of furniture that combines three functions without compromising on any of them. The storage is the differentiator — it's deep enough to actually declutter a room, not just hide a remote.

**Buy it if** you need a compact, multi-functional piece that doubles as a footrest, coffee table, and hidden storage solution. **Skip it if** you require genuine leather, live in a hot climate with bare-leg contact, or need a silent-close lid.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a strong value pick that outperforms its price point on both function and looks.
