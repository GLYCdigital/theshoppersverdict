**A compact nightstand that actually solves the bedside clutter problem — if you've got the outlet placement for it.**

Most nightstands in this price range are either flimsy particleboard with fake wood grain or bare-bones cubes that offer zero organization. The RWYHG Farmhouse Night Stand with Charging Station sits in a smarter middle ground: it's a genuinely sturdy, 18-inch-wide side table with real storage and integrated power — and at 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it's clearly working for most buyers.

---

**What you actually get**

The box contains the table top, two side panels, the back panel, a drawer assembly, the X-frame cross brace, and hardware. The drawer is pre-assembled, which saves the most frustrating part of furniture assembly. The charging station — one AC outlet and two USB ports — is pre-wired into the back panel, so there's no electrical work on your end.

The dimensions are roughly 18"W x 15"D x 24"H. That's compact enough for a twin bed or a tight master bedroom corner, but the drawer is deeper than you'd expect — it holds a tablet, reading glasses, a book, and still has room for chargers and odds and ends.

---

**Build quality and real-world use**

The table uses a combination of engineered wood with a laminated farmhouse-style finish in a warm wood tone. It's not solid oak, and it doesn't pretend to be. But the X-frame side design gives it a visual heft that flat-pack furniture usually lacks — it reads as "intentional" rather than "temporary."

The charging station is the headline feature. The AC outlet handles a standard lamp or phone charger, and the two USB ports put out enough juice to fast-charge a modern smartphone. One buyer put it plainly: *"The charging ports work great — I plug in my phone and my watch at night, and the lamp still has its own outlet. No more fighting over the wall socket."* Another noted the cord management: *"The wire comes out the back and I tucked it behind the frame. You don't see it unless you look for it."*

The drawer glides are metal, not the cheap plastic tracks you find on budget nightstands, and they hold up to daily use without wobble. As one reviewer observed, *"I was surprised how solid it feels once assembled. No wobble, drawer slides smoothly."*

---

**Where it falls short — honestly**

The assembly is the weakest point. Several buyers flagged that the drawer face requires careful alignment, and the instructions are mostly diagrams with minimal text. If you're not handy, budget 40 minutes and have a drill with a Phillips bit ready — the screw holes aren't pre-drilled on the drawer front, which is a notable oversight at this price.

The power cord is fixed at roughly 6 feet. If your wall outlet is behind the bed or across the room, you'll need an extension cord, which somewhat defeats the "clean charging" purpose. Place this nightstand next to an accessible outlet, not in a corner.

Finally, the finish is a laminate, not real wood. It looks good from a distance, but if you press a fingernail in, it will dent. Treat it like furniture with a finish, not a heirloom piece.

**Who should buy this**

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the charging station work with fast charging?**
A: The USB ports support standard fast charging for most phones (5V/2A). They won't trigger proprietary fast-charge protocols like Qualcomm Quick Charge or USB-PD, but they'll charge an iPhone or Android at a reasonable speed overnight.

**Q: Can I remove the drawer for easier assembly?**
A: The drawer is pre-assembled and slides in on metal rails after the frame is built. You don't need to disassemble it — just slide it in once the frame is square and the X-brace is secured.

**Q: How much weight can the top hold?**
A: The top is rated for roughly 30–40 pounds. A lamp, a stack of books, and a phone charger are well within range. Avoid sitting or standing on it.
