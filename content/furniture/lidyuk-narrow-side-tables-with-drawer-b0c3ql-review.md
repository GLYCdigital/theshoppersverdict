# LIDYUK Narrow Side Tables with Drawer Review: The Slim Space-Saver That Actually Works

You know that awkward gap next to your sofa — too narrow for a normal end table, too wide to ignore? The LIDYUK Narrow Side Table is engineered for exactly that dead zone. At just 11.8 inches deep, this 17.7-inch-wide, 23.6-inch-tall table slides into spaces where bulkier furniture simply won't fit. And here's the kicker: it pulls its weight with a hidden drawer, not just a flat surface for your coffee mug.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the table top, two side panels, one drawer assembly, a back panel, and the hardware kit. There's no fancy tool set included — just the screws and cam locks. You'll need your own Phillips screwdriver. The composite wood panels come with a smooth, matte finish that looks more expensive than the $52.99 price tag suggests. The drawer glides on metal rails, which surprised me at this price point.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Here's where this table punches above its weight class. The composite wood frame feels solid once assembled, with no wobble during normal use. The protective coating handles condensation from cold drinks without leaving those ugly white rings. The drawer — the standout feature — runs smoothly on its metal tracks and is deep enough for a Kindle, reading glasses, and a TV remote stacked together.

Assembly is the one area where you'll earn your keep. Plan for 20-30 minutes of focused work. The cam locks are straightforward, but the drawer alignment requires some fiddling. One reviewer noted, "The drawer took three tries to line up right, but once it clicked, it's been perfect for six months." That tracks with my experience — patience during assembly pays off with a table that feels stable and functions well.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The narrow footprint dominates the feedback across 225 ratings. One customer wrote: "Finally! A table that fits between my recliner and the wall without blocking the walkway." Another highlighted the storage: "The drawer hides all the clutter my living room used to show. Remotes, chargers, reading glasses — all out of sight."

The finish also earns repeated compliments. Multiple reviewers mention the wood grain look passing for something twice the price. The clean, minimalist design blends with both modern and traditional decor without screaming for attention.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. The 17.7-inch top is genuinely small. A standard 16-inch lamp base plus a coffee mug? That's a tight squeeze. You'll likely choose one or the other unless you're just parking a phone and a drink. The 11-pound weight means it can shift if bumped hard — not ideal if you have toddlers who grab furniture to steady themselves. Anchoring it to the wall is a smart precaution.

The drawer, while smooth, doesn't have a soft-close mechanism. Let it go and it'll clack shut. Minor, but worth knowing. And the exposed screws on the back panel are visible if you position the table where the back is in plain view — face the finished side toward the room.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you're furnishing a small apartment, working with a narrow living room layout, or need a bedside table for a tight bedroom corner. It's also ideal for dorm rooms, home offices, or as a slim console behind a loveseat. The drawer makes it genuinely useful, not just decorative.

**Skip it if** you need a large tabletop for a substantial lamp collection, want a piece that can double as a plant stand with a big pot, or prefer furniture with a heavier, more substantial feel. This is a functional space-saver, not a statement piece.

## FAQ

**Q: Can the drawer hold a laptop?**
A: No. The drawer interior is roughly 14 inches wide and 11 inches deep — fine for tablets, Kindles, remotes, and small electronics, but a 15-inch laptop won't fit.

**Q: How much weight can the top hold?**
A: The manufacturer doesn't specify a formal limit, but buyers report using it for lamps, books, and drinks without issues. Keep heavy items like full printer stacks on sturdier furniture.

**Q: Is the table easy to move around?**
A: Yes. At 11 pounds, it's light enough to reposition easily. That's a double-edged sword — convenient for rearranging, but light enough to slide if you bump it hard.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The LIDYUK Narrow Side Table earns its place in tight spaces. It's well-constructed for the price, the drawer adds genuine utility, and the slim profile solves a problem most furniture ignores. The small top and assembly quirks are real tradeoffs, but they don't outweigh the value.

**Buy it if** you have a narrow gap crying out for a functional table. **Skip it if** you need serious surface area or a furniture piece with substantial heft. For under $53, this is a smart, honest solution that delivers exactly what it promises — a slim, storage-equipped side table that fits where others can't.
