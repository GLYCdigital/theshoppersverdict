## Where It Falls Short

Let’s be honest about the tradeoffs.

**Assembly is a project.** Plan for 60 to 90 minutes if you’re going solo. The instructions are mostly exploded diagrams with minimal text, and a few screws are easy to cross-thread if you’re not careful. One reviewer said, “Took me two hours and a glass of wine, but the result was worth it.” Have a power drill with a hex bit handy — the included Allen wrench will make your hand cramp.

**The surface smudges.** The matte laminate looks great out of the box, but it shows fingerprints, skin oils, and water rings quickly. You’ll want a desk pad or mouse mat covering the main work area. This isn’t a dealbreaker, but it’s worth knowing before you set your coffee mug down directly on it.

**The open shelf is tight.** The cubby next to the drawer unit fits most standard PC towers, but larger full-tower cases with aggressive cooling fans won’t fit. Measure your tower before you buy if that matters to you.
