The Loeway is a full metal frame system that replaces both your box spring and your bed frame. The headboard is upholstered, which softens the look considerably compared to bare metal rails. ## Performance and Build Quality

The structural core is steel with center support legs. That center leg is non-negotiable for a frame this size — without it, you'd get sagging in the middle where you sleep. The presence of multiple support points suggests the manufacturer understands load distribution.

For assembly, you're looking at a straightforward bolt-together job. Most metal frames in this category take 30 to 45 minutes with basic tools. The tradeoff to know upfront: metal frames develop squeaks over time. The fix is simple (retighten the bolts every few months), but if you're someone who never touches a wrench after assembly, this will eventually annoy you.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need a box spring with this frame?**
No. The slat system supports your mattress directly. Using a box spring would actually be redundant and could make the bed sit too high.

**Q: Will this frame support a memory foam mattress?**
Yes. Memory foam needs a solid, flat surface, and slats spaced properly provide that. The key is ensuring the slats aren't spaced too wide — check the product dimensions for slat spacing before ordering.

**Q: How long will assembly take?**
Plan for 30 to 45 minutes. Bolt-together construction means you'll need a wrench and possibly a screwdriver, but there's no complicated mechanism to figure out.
