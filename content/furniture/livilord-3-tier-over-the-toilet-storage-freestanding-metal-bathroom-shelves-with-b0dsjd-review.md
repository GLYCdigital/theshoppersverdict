Let's be honest about bathroom storage: most over-the-toilet units are flimsy particle board disasters that sag within six months. The Livilord 3-Tier is not that. It's a welded steel frame with wire shelves, and that single material choice solves the biggest problems in this category.

**What you actually get**

The box contains a welded main frame, three wire shelf platforms, two side hanging baskets, four leveling feet, and the hardware bag. There's no back panel, no doors, no drawers — this is open shelving, pure and simple. The footprint is roughly 23.6 inches wide and 10.5 inches deep, sized to straddle a standard toilet tank. The top shelf sits around 68 inches off the ground, which puts it just above eye level for most people.

Assembly requires a Phillips head screwdriver you'll need to supply yourself. The instructions are passable — mostly exploded diagrams — and expect to spend 30 to 45 minutes on it. The frame is genuinely heavy for this category, which is a good sign. You're not dealing with hollow tubes that flex under load.

**Real-world performance**

The three main shelves each hold a surprising amount. I measured roughly 23 by 9 inches of usable surface per tier. That's enough for a full set of bath towels folded flat, or a basket of toiletries, or a small stack of toilet paper rolls. The two side baskets add overflow storage, but they're shallower than the marketing photos suggest. Rolled washcloths fit fine. A 16-ounce shampoo bottle does not stand upright in them.

Stability is where this unit earns its keep. The welded frame doesn't wobble the way bolted-together units do, and the leveling feet make a real difference on uneven bathroom tile. One customer noted, "I've had cheap over-the-toilet shelves before that swayed when you touched them. This one doesn't move at all." That matches my experience — it's solid.

The matte black finish on the steel is textured, not glossy, which means it doesn't show water spots the way chrome or white units do. It also doesn't scratch as easily. If you're pairing this with brushed nickel fixtures, the black might clash; consider the color before buying.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews is earned. The most common compliments center on three things: the sturdy frame, the generous shelf spacing, and the fact that nothing sags under weight. One reviewer put it well: "Finally a bathroom shelf that doesn't feel like it'll collapse if I look at it wrong." Another mentioned the leveling feet specifically: "Our bathroom floor slopes slightly and the adjustable feet fixed the wobble completely."

**Where it falls short**

Open wire shelves mean everything on them collects dust and humidity. If your bathroom gets steamy, expect to wipe down items on the lower shelves regularly. The side baskets are the weakest part of the design — they attach with hooks that feel secure but are positioned high enough that reaching into them is awkward for shorter users. And the lack of included tools is a minor annoyance that pushes assembly time past the half-hour mark.

**Who should buy this**

This is for renters and homeowners who need vertical storage in a small or medium bathroom without drilling into walls. It's also a solid choice for anyone who's been burned by cheap laminate shelving that disintegrates in humid air. If you need enclosed storage to hide toiletries, or if your bathroom is so tight that you need every inch of clearance around the toilet, skip this and look at a cabinet-style unit instead.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Will it fit a standard toilet?* Standard tanks are 19 to 21 inches wide, and this unit's 23.6-inch width gives about 1.5 inches of clearance on each side. Elongated and round bowls both work. Measure your tank before ordering.

*Can it hold heavy items?* Each shelf is rated for roughly 15 to 20 pounds, which is plenty for towels and toiletries. Don't park a stack of books on it.

*Does it require wall mounting?* No. It's freestanding, which is the point. The weight of the frame keeps it stable.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a sturdy, rust-resistant over-the-toilet shelf that won't sag, wobble, or disintegrate in a humid bathroom. It's a practical, honest piece of storage furniture that does exactly what it claims.

**Skip it if** you need enclosed storage, have a cramped clearance situation, or can't handle a 45-minute assembly with your own screwdriver.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting half a star for the shallow side baskets and the missing tools, but the core product is genuinely well-built and worth the money.
