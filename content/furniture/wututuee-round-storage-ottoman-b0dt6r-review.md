**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. The assembly is straightforward but not foolproof — the hinge alignment issue mentioned above is real, and a few reviewers found the included instructions sparse. Budget 20 minutes, not 10.

The bigger issue is the fabric. It's a lint and pet hair magnet. If you have a shedding dog or cat, you'll be breaking out the lint roller constantly. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you commit.

Also, the round shape means it doesn't tuck neatly into corners the way a square ottoman would. If you're tight on floor space, measure your intended spot first.
