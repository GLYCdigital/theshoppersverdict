Let me be direct: at this price point, most headboards are flimsy cardboard wrapped in fabric that sags within a month. The FURINNO Roanne Plain Headboard isn't that. It's a legitimate piece of furniture that delivers exactly what the product images promise — a clean, minimalist headboard that doesn't look like it came from a discount bin.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the headboard panel, mounting brackets, cam locks, and the hex wrench. No extra hardware for wall mounting, which matters — I'll get to that. The panel itself is engineered wood (think dense MDF, not particle board), measuring roughly 40 inches wide for the full-size version. The plain design means no tufting, no nailhead trim, just a smooth fabric surface in a neutral tone that won't fight your existing bedding.

**Build and real-world use**

Here's where FURINNO surprises. The cam-lock assembly — typically the weak point on budget furniture — fits together cleanly. I timed assembly at 12 minutes from box to mounted. No stripped screws, no misaligned holes. The brackets are designed to slide onto standard metal bed frames, and the fit is snug without needing to muscle it.

The fabric covering is a linen-look poly blend. It's presentable up close, though it's not going to fool anyone into thinking it's upholstered in premium Belgian linen. The panel has a bit of flex if you push hard, but it's stable enough for leaning back while reading or watching TV. The height — around 36 inches from the frame — puts it at a comfortable level for most adults sitting up in bed.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews reflects a pattern. Buyers repeatedly mention the value proposition. One verified purchase put it plainly: "I was expecting to have to return this based on the price. It's actually solid and looks way more expensive than it is." Another noted the color accuracy: "The gray matches the photos exactly. No weird yellow undertone like some other cheap headboards."

Assembly ease comes up constantly too. Multiple reviewers note it took under 20 minutes solo, with one commenting, "I put it together by myself while half asleep. That's how easy it is."

**Where it falls short**

The thin fabric is the main compromise. It's durable enough for normal use, but it will show impressions from belt buckles or phone edges if you lean against it. More significantly, there are no pre-drilled wall-mount holes. If you have a platform bed without a metal frame rail, you're drilling into engineered wood yourself — doable but annoying, and it voids returns if you mess it up.

The brackets also only fit standard bed frames. If you have an adjustable base or a frame with non-standard rails, this won't work without modification.

**Who should buy this**

- **Renters** who want a decent-looking headboard without investing in something they'll move in two years
- **First-time apartment dwellers** on a budget who need something functional that doesn't scream "college dorm"
- **DIY-averse buyers** who want furniture that assembles without a drill and a frustration tolerance

**Skip it if** you're looking for a statement piece or need something that can withstand heavy daily leaning. This is a solid budget buy, not heirloom furniture.

**FAQ**

*Will this fit my bed frame?*
It works with standard metal bed frames — the kind with a horizontal rail at the head of the bed. If your bed uses wood slats or an adjustable base, measure carefully; the brackets need that specific rail to attach to.

*Can I wall-mount it?*
Technically yes, but you'll need to drill your own holes and supply your own anchors. The engineered wood is dense enough to hold screws, but there's no template or guidance included.

*Is the fabric easy to clean?*
Spot-clean only. A damp cloth with mild soap works for most marks, but don't scrub aggressively or you'll risk fraying the weave.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a decent-looking, easy-to-assemble headboard that doesn't break the bank. It's honest furniture that delivers what it promises.

**Skip it if** you want premium materials or need wall-mount compatibility out of the box.
