## Performance and Build Quality

The height adjustment works exactly as advertised. Each of the four legs adjusts independently, so you can fine-tune it on uneven floors—a small but genuinely useful detail. The pin system feels positive and locks securely; you won't accidentally collapse this table when leaning on it.

Stability is the trade-off you need to understand. At full extension (35.4 inches), there's noticeable sway if you bump it. This isn't a defect—it's physics. A 23.6-inch footprint supporting a 35-inch column is inherently less stable than a wider, shorter table. At the lower settings, it's rock solid.

The laminate surface handles condensation rings and minor spills fine, but it's not scratch-proof. Use coasters and don't drag metal objects across it.

## Honest Shortcomings

The stability issue at full height is the most common complaint. Several reviewers mention it wobbles when bumped at bar height. It's manageable, but if you're planning to use it exclusively as a tall bar table with people leaning on it, this isn't your table.

The particleboard top is the other limitation. It's fine for a home office or casual entertaining, but it won't survive heavy commercial use or enthusiastic parties. One reviewer wrote, *"The top scratches easier than I'd like. Use placemats."*

The 23.6-inch diameter also means no room for food and drinks plus laptops simultaneously. This is a drinks-and-conversation table, not a dining surface.
