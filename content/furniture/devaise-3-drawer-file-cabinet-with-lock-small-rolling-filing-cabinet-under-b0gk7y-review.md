Let's be blunt: a 3.9-star average across 195 ratings tells you this cabinet has a split personality. Some buyers call it the best $80 they've spent on their home office. Others describe a wobbly metal box that arrived dented. Both are right — and the truth depends entirely on what you need it for.

**What you actually get**

The DEVAISE 3 Drawer File Cabinet ships fully assembled. Pull it out of the box, slap on the four casters (the only "assembly" required), and you're filing within five minutes. The cabinet measures 15.4 inches deep, 15.7 inches wide, and 25.6 inches tall — small enough to slide under most standard desks, as shown in the product images. Each drawer is rated for 33 pounds and accepts both letter and legal-size hanging files.

**Performance and build quality**

The ball-bearing drawer slides deserve genuine praise. They're smooth, quiet, and don't bind even when loaded near capacity. The drawers pull out fully, giving you complete access to the back of the file — something budget cabinets often skip. The metal body is finished with a textured powder coat that resists fingerprints.

Here's where the wheels come off: the locking mechanism. The key lock secures **only the top drawer**. The two lower drawers slide freely even when the lock is engaged. If you need actual security — even from curious kids, let alone anyone with bad intentions — this cabinet is a locked box with an open bottom. One Amazon buyer put it plainly: *"I assumed the lock secured all three drawers. It doesn't. The bottom two open right up."* For a filing cabinet whose entire premise is protecting documents, that's a significant flaw.

The casters are the second compromise. At 2 inches in diameter, they're designed for hard floors. On carpet — especially anything with a medium or high pile — the cabinet drags and catches. Several reviewers noted it's effectively immobile once placed on carpeted home office floors.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The price-to-value ratio dominates positive reviews. One verified purchase wrote: *"For the money, this is unbeatable. It looks much more expensive than it is."* The fully-assembled delivery earns repeated shout-outs — nobody misses building a file cabinet with a hex wrench. And the compact dimensions are a recurring theme: it fits in tight corners, beside desks, and in apartment closets where full-size cabinets won't go.

**Where it falls short**

Beyond the lock and caster issues, the lightweight construction (the cabinet weighs roughly 25 pounds empty) means it can feel insubstantial. It's not tip-prone when loaded, but it's not built like a tank either. Shipping damage is a recurring complaint — thin sheet metal dents and scratches in transit. One reviewer reported receiving a cabinet with a bent drawer track that wouldn't close properly.

**Who should buy this**

- **The small-office organizer** with a hard-floor workspace who needs letter/legal filing and doesn't require real security
- **The budget-conscious buyer** who wants a decent-looking file cabinet without paying $200+ for steel construction
- **The apartment dweller** with limited floor space who needs a compact, mobile file solution

**Skip it if** you need genuine locking security across all drawers, have thick carpet, or expect commercial-grade durability.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the lock secure all three drawers?**
No. Only the top drawer locks. The bottom two drawers remain accessible when locked. If document security matters, look elsewhere.

**Q: Will it fit under a standard desk?**
Yes. At 25.6 inches tall, it slides under most standard 29-30 inch desks — but check your desk's clearance first, as some have support bars that reduce height.

**Q: Can it hold legal-size hanging files?**
Yes. The drawers accommodate both letter and legal-size hanging file folders, though legal files fit more snugly.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need an affordable, ready-to-use file cabinet for a hard-floor home office and locking security isn't a priority. The smooth drawer slides and zero-assembly delivery make it an easy recommendation at its price point.

**Skip it if** you need real security, have carpeted floors, or expect heavy-duty construction. The misleading lock and weak casters are honest limitations — and knowing them before you buy beats discovering them after.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — A genuinely useful cabinet for the right buyer, undermined by a lock that doesn't do its job.
