If your bathroom has more toiletries than counter space, the Hzuaneri Freestanding Cabinet is the kind of practical addition that quietly solves a daily annoyance. At $79.99 with a 4.4-star average across 220 ratings, it's not a premium piece — but it doesn't pretend to be. Here's what you're actually getting.

**What's in the box**

You get a tall, narrow cabinet (about 12" deep by 12" wide, standing roughly 50" tall) with one solid door, two adjustable shelves, and a fixed bottom shelf. The hardware kit includes cam locks, dowels, a hex wrench, and a non-slip foot pad. There's also a simple magnetic door catch — nothing fancy, but it holds the door flush.

**Performance and build quality**

The selling point here is the adjustable shelving. Each shelf sits on four metal pegs that slot into pre-drilled holes spaced about an inch apart. You can reposition them without tools — just pop the pegs out and move them. That's genuinely useful for fitting a tall shampoo bottle or stacking bath towels vertically. One buyer noted, *"I moved the shelves to fit my hairdryer and a stack of washcloths — took me two minutes."*

The cabinet body is laminated MDF with a smooth white finish. It looks clean and modern in person, roughly matching the product photos. The door hinges are metal and operate smoothly, though they're not soft-close — expect a gentle click when you close it.

Assembly is genuinely manageable. The cam lock system is forgiving, and most buyers report 20–30 minutes to full assembly. One reviewer summed it up: *"Went together without cursing — that's a win in my book."*

**What buyers consistently praise**

The compact footprint is the recurring theme. Multiple reviewers mention fitting it into awkward spaces — beside a toilet, in a narrow hallway, or under a window. One wrote, *"I was shocked how much fits in there. Two shelves hold all my skincare, and the bottom holds extra toilet paper."* The weight capacity is solid for the price; the shelves handle typical bathroom items without sagging.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. The back panel is thin MDF that can warp if exposed to direct water splashes. Keep it away from the shower spray, or you'll see the panel bow over time. The glossy finish is a fingerprint magnet — in a bathroom, that means constant wiping if you want it to look pristine.

The door alignment also needs a small adjustment out of the box. The hinges have play, and several reviewers mention fiddling with the screws to get the door to sit perfectly flush. It's a five-minute fix, but it's worth knowing before you assemble.

**Who should buy this**

This cabinet fits three specific scenarios: small bathrooms where every inch counts, renters who need movable storage without drilling into walls, and anyone who needs a dedicated spot for toiletries or cleaning supplies. It's also a reasonable pick for a laundry room or hallway if you want extra closed storage.

Skip it if you need something heavy-duty, water-resistant, or large enough to hold a full linen collection. This is a light-to-medium duty piece, and it's priced accordingly.

**FAQ**

**Can the shelves support heavy items like a stack of towels?**
Yes, up to about 15–20 pounds per shelf. The pegs are metal and the shelf boards are solid enough for typical bathroom loads. Just don't overload the top shelf — it's not designed for heavy appliances.

**Does it require wall mounting?**
No, it's freestanding. The base is stable enough for normal use, but if you have small children or pets that might pull on it, I'd recommend a basic wall anchor — the thin back panel makes it vulnerable to tipping if pushed hard.

**What's the actual width?**
The cabinet is about 12 inches wide and 12 inches deep. Measure your space before ordering — it's narrower than a standard 15-inch cabinet, which is both its strength (fits tight spots) and its limitation (won't hold wide items like large baskets).

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need affordable, space-efficient storage for a small bathroom and don't mind wiping down a glossy finish occasionally. **Skip it if** you want a statement piece, need water-resistant construction, or expect heirloom quality at $80.

The Hzuaneri delivers exactly what the listing promises: a compact, adjustable-shelf cabinet that assembles easily and holds the daily bathroom overflow. It's not flashy, but for the price, it's a solid 4.2 out of 5 — practical, functional, and honest about its limitations.
