The package arrives as a bulk set of mini magnetic drawing boards, each with a tethered magnetic stylus. ## Build Quality and Real-World Use

Here's where this product punches above its price point. The ABS plastic frame has real thickness to it — this isn't the flimsy, thin-walled plastic you see on dollar-store versions. I've seen these survive being dropped from a table, thrown across a room, and stepped on during the post-party frenzy. ## Where It Falls Short

Let's talk tradeoffs, because there are a few.

The drawing surface is genuinely small. At 4x6 inches, kids who want to draw detailed scenes will run out of room fast. This is a doodle pad, not an art tablet. Older kids in the 4-8 range may lose interest quickly if they're used to bigger drawing surfaces.

The stylus, while tethered, has no storage clip or slot. When not in use, it just dangles. Minor annoyance, but worth knowing.

And the faint dot residue I mentioned earlier — it's a known quirk of this style of magnetic board. It doesn't affect functionality, but if you're picky about screen clarity, it might bother you.
