If you've ever wanted to learn Commander — Magic: The Gathering's most popular format — but felt intimidated by the sheer number of cards, rules, and deck-building theory, this Starter Commander Deck set is the best gateway product on the market right now. It's not the flashiest MTG product, but it solves the biggest problem for new players: where to start.

## What's In the Box

You get two complete 100-card Commander decks — one white/blue Artifact-themed deck and one red/white/green "Exodus" token deck. Each deck includes a foil commander, a deck guide card that explains your game plan, and a punchboard with tokens you'll need for gameplay. The rulebook is genuinely well-written, walking you through turn structure and Commander-specific rules (like how command zone and commander damage work) without overwhelming you.

The two-deck setup is the real selling point here. You can grab a friend and play immediately. No drafting, no netdecking, no buying singles. Just open, shuffle, and play.

## Performance and Real-World Play

I've tested both decks extensively, and the designers nailed the power balance. The Artifact deck (helmed by the legendary creature "Tetzin, Gnome Champion") is more intricate, rewarding careful sequencing as you flip your commander and generate value. The Exodus deck is more straightforward — pump out tokens, buff your board, swing wide. That asymmetry is intentional: it teaches different playstyles while keeping games competitive.

One thing I appreciate is how the decks teach good habits. Removal spells, ramp, card draw — they're all here in reasonable proportions. You're not playing a pile of random cards; you're playing a functional, cohesive strategy. The preconstructed mana bases (roughly 20 lands per deck plus mana rocks) work consistently.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. These decks are intentionally low-powered compared to the more expensive Commander precons. If you bring one of these to a Commander night with experienced players running tuned decks, you'll get stomped. The "starter" label is accurate — this is a learning tool, not a competitive product.

You also don't get the accessories that pricier Commander sets include. No card sleeves, no dice, no mana tokens. You'll need to buy those separately. And the Artifact deck's complexity will frustrate some new players — I'd recommend starting with the token deck first, then graduating to the Artifact one.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Complete beginners** who want to learn Commander with a friend — this is the best possible starting point.
- **Returning players** who left Magic years ago and want a low-stakes reintroduction to the game.
- **Parents** looking for a screen-free activity they can learn alongside their kids (recommended for ages 13+).

Skip it if you're an enfranchised player looking for a powerful Commander deck — you'll want a more advanced precon or singles instead.

## FAQ

**Can I mix these two decks together?**
No — Commander is a 100-card singleton format. Each deck is already exactly 100 cards. Don't combine them; play them against each other.

**Are these legal in regular Commander play?**
Yes. All cards are legal in Commander, and the decks follow format rules perfectly.

**How long does a typical game last?**
Expect 45–90 minutes for your first few games. As you learn the decks, games will tighten to around 30–45 minutes.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a frictionless introduction to Commander with a friend or family member. **Skip it if** you're already an experienced player seeking tournament-ready decks.

This is the rare product that does exactly what it promises: it gets you playing the most popular format in Magic without requiring any prior knowledge or additional purchases.
