If you just dropped $200 on a PlayStation Portal, the last thing you want is to scratch that 8-inch LCD in your backpack. The Foluck 3-in-1 Accessories Set is designed to solve exactly that problem — and for the most part, it does it well. With a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, this kit has clearly found its audience. But is it the right protection for *your* setup? Let's break down what you're actually getting.

**What's in the Box**

The set includes three items: a hard-shell EVA carrying case, two pairs of thumb stick grips (one textured, one smooth), and a tempered glass screen protector with a wet-wipe, dry-wipe, and dust removal sticker. The case is the headline piece — it's rigid, not a flimsy sleeve. The interior has a molded cradle that holds the Portal snugly, with a mesh pocket on the lid for cables or a charging brick. The thumb grips are silicone, and they fit over the Portal's sticks with a satisfying click. The screen protector is 9H tempered glass, sized specifically for the Portal's display.

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

The case is where Foluck earns its keep. The EVA shell is thick enough to absorb real impacts — think dropped backpack, not just pocket lint. The zipper is smooth and doesn't catch on the fabric edge. As one verified buyer put it: *"The case fits the Portal like a glove. I was worried about the triggers getting pressed in transit, but the molded slots hold everything perfectly."* That's a common refrain in the reviews — the cutouts align precisely with the L1/R1 triggers and analog sticks, so there's no pressure on the delicate parts.

The thumb grips are a genuine upgrade over the stock sticks. They add just enough height and texture to improve control during long sessions. A reviewer noted: *"I didn't think I needed grips, but after two hours of Call of Duty, my thumbs don't slip anymore."* They're easy to pull off if you want to swap back, and they don't interfere with the Portal's charging dock (if you use one).

The screen protector is the weakest link — not because it's bad, but because it's fiddly. The included alignment frame helps, but you still need patience. One user said: *"The frame makes it easy, but I still got one dust speck. Used the sticker to remove it and it came out perfect on the second try."* Expect to spend 10 minutes on the application if you're meticulous.

**Where It Falls Short**

The case is bulky. It's not a slim slip-on — it's a rugged protective shell that roughly doubles the Portal's thickness. If you're looking for something to slide into a jacket pocket or a small crossbody bag, this isn't it. It's a dedicated "toss it in a backpack" solution.

The cleaning cloth included with the screen protector is almost an afterthought — it's small and not particularly absorbent. You'll want your own microfiber cloth for a streak-free install. Minor, but worth knowing.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The commuter** — if your Portal lives in a backpack or messenger bag, this case is essential. It's the difference between a pristine screen and a scratched one.
- **The long-session player** — the thumb grips genuinely improve comfort and control over stock sticks.
- **The first-time Portal owner** — this is a cheap insurance policy against the most common damage points: screen scratches and stick drift from travel pressure.

**Skip it if** you only play at home and never transport your Portal. The case is overkill for a couch-only device, and the screen protector alone can be bought cheaper elsewhere.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will the Foluck case fit the Portal with the official charging cable connected?**
A: No. The mesh pocket fits a folded cable, but the Portal must be unplugged to sit in the molded cradle. The case is designed for storage, not charging.

**Q: Are the thumb grips compatible with the Portal's touch-sensitive areas?**
A: Yes. The grips cover the analog sticks only — the touchpad and touch zones on the sticks remain exposed and functional.

**Q: Does the screen protector affect touch sensitivity?**
A: No. It's a standard 0.3mm tempered glass layer. Multiple reviewers confirm no lag or responsiveness loss.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you transport your Portal regularly and want one-stop protection without hunting for separate accessories. The case alone justifies the price.

**Skip it if** your Portal never leaves your living room, or if you already own a compatible case and just need a screen protector.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The Foluck 3-in-1 kit nails the essentials with quality materials and precise fit. The bulk of the case and the lackluster cleaning cloth keep it from a perfect score, but for the price, it's the smartest first purchase you can make for your Portal.
