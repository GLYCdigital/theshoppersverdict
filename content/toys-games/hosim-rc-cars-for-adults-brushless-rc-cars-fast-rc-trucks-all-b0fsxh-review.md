# Hosim Brushless RC Truck Review: Real Speed, Real Value

Most RC trucks in this price range are either slow toy-grade junk or fast but fragile. The Hosim brushless truck for adults sits in a rare middle ground — it's genuinely fast, reasonably tough, and priced under $200. After digging through 200 ratings and owner feedback, here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The box includes the 1/10-scale truck, two 7.4V Li-ion batteries, a USB charger, and a 2.4GHz transmitter. The truck measures roughly 18 inches long — big enough to feel substantial but small enough to toss in a trunk. The body is a rugged monster truck shell with LED light bars front and rear, which look sharp at dusk.

The important stuff is underneath. You get a 3650 brushless motor, metal differential gears, oil-filled shocks, and aluminum shock towers. That's not marketing fluff — those are the components that determine whether this survives its first hard landing.

## Performance That Surprises

The brushless motor pushes this truck to a claimed 45+ mph. Owners consistently confirm it's quick — one reviewer noted, "I've owned Traxxas before, and this holds its own for a fraction of the price." Acceleration is punchy, and the 2.4GHz radio gives responsive, lag-free steering up to 300 feet.

The two included batteries each deliver roughly 20-25 minutes of hard running. That's decent, but the 3-hour charge time per battery means you'll want to rotate them strategically. The low-voltage cutoff prevents over-discharge damage — a thoughtful touch at this price.

## Where It Earns Its Keep

The metal drivetrain is the standout. Owners who've crashed hard — and they will crash — report the shocks and chassis hold up. One long-term owner wrote, "I've flipped it, hit curbs, and jumped it off ramps. Still runs perfect." That durability matters because replacement parts are available but not as ubiquitous as Traxxas or Arrma.

Bashability is where this truck shines. The aluminum braces and metal gears absorb abuse that would strip plastic drivetrains on cheaper models.

## Honest Shortcomings

The stock tires are the weakest link. On loose dirt or gravel, they spin out and lack bite. Several owners swapped in aftermarket tires within the first month. Budget an extra $25-40 if you plan serious off-road driving.

The manual is frustratingly sparse — no exploded diagram, minimal torque specs. If you need to rebuild the diff or replace the steering servo, you'll be watching YouTube videos. It's a learning curve, not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

Battery charging is slow. At 3+ hours per pack, you can't just top up between runs. Plan charging overnight or buy a faster external charger.

## Who Should Buy This

**The weekend basher** — if you want a fast truck to thrash in a park or empty lot without dropping $400+ on a Traxxas, this is your sweet spot.

**The RC newcomer with some mechanical patience** — you'll learn to wrench, but the metal components forgive beginner mistakes.

**Skip it if** you're a competitive racer needing precise tuning, or if you want a ready-to-run truck with zero upgrades. The tire issue alone will push you to aftermarket parts.

## FAQ

**Is this suitable for kids?**
No. At 45+ mph, this is an adult toy. A child could seriously injure themselves or damage property. Consider a slower, toy-grade model for under-14s.

**How hard is assembly out of the box?**
Minimal — the truck arrives mostly assembled. You attach the wheels, charge the batteries, and bind the transmitter. Expect 15 minutes to first run.

**Can I upgrade parts later?**
Yes, and many owners do. The chassis accepts standard 1/10-scale aftermarket tires, shocks, and motors. The metal drivetrain means you can push more power without immediate breakage.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want genuine brushless speed and metal durability without the premium price tag. It's the rare sub-$200 truck that doesn't feel like a compromise.

**Skip it if** you're unwilling to budget for better tires or lack the patience for a thin manual. This truck rewards owners who tinker.

At $169.99, the Hosim brushless truck delivers 80% of the experience of trucks costing twice as much. It's fast, tough, and honest about its limitations. For the right buyer, that's a solid 4.2-star value.
