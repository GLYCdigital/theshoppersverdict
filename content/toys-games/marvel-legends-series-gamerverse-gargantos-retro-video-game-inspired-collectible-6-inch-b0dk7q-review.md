If you've been hunting for a Marvel Legends figure that actually looks like it jumped off a 16-bit screen, the Gamerverse Gargantos nails that brief. This is the interdimensional beast from *Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness*, but reimagined through a retro video game lens — and the result is one of the most visually striking Legends releases this year.

## What's in the Box

You get the Gargantos figure itself (roughly 6 inches tall, which feels bigger given the tentacle spread), a pair of translucent orange energy effect pieces that attach to the tentacle tips, and the retro-style cardback packaging. The packaging is the real showstopper here — it's designed to mimic a classic NES/SNES game box, complete with pixel art and a faux cartridge aesthetic. If you're an in-box collector, this one's a keeper.

## Build Quality and Articulation

The figure uses the standard Marvel Legends articulation scheme — ball-jointed head, hinged shoulders, double-jointed elbows, swivel wrists, and decent hip and knee movement. What's surprising is how well it all works given the sculpt. Gargantos has a chunky, cyclopean design with multiple tentacles sprouting from his body, and Hasbro managed to keep the articulation functional without compromising the silhouette.

The tentacles themselves are individually articulated at the base, which lets you spread them out for a dynamic display or pull them in for a more compact look. The translucent orange energy effects fit securely onto the tentacle tips and glow nicely under direct light. The paint application is clean — the deep purple body with lighter purple highlights matches the in-game aesthetic well, and the single large eye has a menacing glossy finish.

## What Buyers Are Saying

With 229 ratings and a 4.6 average, the feedback is overwhelmingly positive. One buyer put it simply: "This is a great piece for the collection. The retro packaging is a nice touch and the figure itself is solid." Another mentioned, "I wasn't sure about the Gamerverse line but this one surprised me. The tentacles look awesome on display."

The main criticism revolves around the tentacle joints. A few reviewers noted that after extended posing, the joints can loosen and the tentacles won't stay in position as firmly. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you plan to aggressively re-pose this figure frequently, keep it in mind.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the joint looseness, there's a lack of accessories. You get the two energy effects and nothing else — no alternate hands, no swappable eye piece, no extra tentacle configurations. For a figure at this price point, that's a bit thin. Also, the retro aesthetic won't appeal to everyone. If you're strictly a movie-accurate collector, the pixel-art style and slightly exaggerated proportions might not fit your display.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Retro gaming fans**: If you grew up with 16-bit Marvel games, this is a nostalgia hit with modern articulation.
- **Marvel Legends completists**: The Gamerverse line is niche, but this is one of the stronger entries.
- **Display-focused collectors**: The energy effects and dynamic tentacle posing make it a standout on any shelf.

## FAQ

**Is this figure worth the price?**
Yes, if you value the retro packaging and unique aesthetic. The build quality is solid, and the energy effects add enough visual flair to justify the cost for collectors.

**Does it scale with other Marvel Legends figures?**
Yes. It's a 6-inch figure, so it scales perfectly with the rest of the Marvel Legends line. It's bulkier than most, but that's accurate to the character.

**Can the tentacles be removed?**
No, they're permanently attached. You can only adjust their position at the base joints.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a distinctive, eye-catching piece that blends Marvel Legends quality with retro gaming charm. The packaging alone is worth the display space, and the figure itself is well-built.

**Skip it if** you're a stickler for movie accuracy or you need lots of accessories to justify the purchase.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A strong, unique addition to any Marvel Legends collection, with minor joint concerns keeping it from perfection.
