You just dropped $200 on a PlayStation Portal. The last thing you need is a cracked screen because you tossed it in a backpack next to your keys. The CoBak Hard Carrying Shell Case exists to prevent exactly that — and with 235 ratings averaging 4.7 stars, it's clearly doing something right. **What you actually get**

The package is straightforward: one hard-shell case, a wrist strap, and that's it. No screen protector, no carabiner — just the shell. That's fine, because the case itself is the product. The exterior is a rigid EVA material with a textured finish that resists scratches and minor impacts. The interior is where CoBak earned its keep: a custom-molded cradle that matches the Portal's contours exactly. Your device snaps in with a satisfying click and stays put.

The case measures roughly 12.5 x 5.5 x 2.5 inches when closed — compact enough for a daypack, too bulky for a coat pocket. The zipper is dual-directional with rubberized pulls, which sounds minor until you're fumbling one-handed on a crowded train.

**Real-world performance**

The molded interior is the star. Multiple buyers noted the Portal fits "like a glove" — one reviewer wrote: "The Portal doesn't move at all inside. I shook the case hard and there was zero rattling." That precision fit matters because the Portal's analog sticks are vulnerable to pressure when packed tightly. The CoBak's cradle suspends the sticks in open space, so they're not compressed against anything.

The mesh pocket on the top flap adds practical storage. It swallows the official USB-C cable easily, and a compact 30W charger fits without distorting the shell's shape. Just don't expect to stuff earbuds in there too — they'll rattle around.

**Where it falls short**

Honestly, this case has few genuine flaws, but they're worth knowing. The hard shell means zero flexibility — you can't squish it into a tight space. If you're the type to shove your Portal into a full messenger bag, this case will demand its own corner. Also, the zipper pulls are metal-coated plastic. They feel solid now, but heavy users may see the coating wear after a year. One reviewer mentioned the case "adds noticeable bulk," which is fair — protection has a weight cost.

**Who should buy this**

- **Commuters**: If your Portal travels in a backpack or tote, this case is non-negotiable. The hard shell absorbs the bumps that soft pouches can't.
- **Parents**: The precision cradle means kids can't jostle the device around inside. One parent noted it "survived a drop from a bunk bed" with zero damage.
- **Skippers**: If you only play on the couch at home, you don't need this. Save the money.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit the Portal with a grip case attached?**  
No. The molded interior is sized for a naked Portal. Any grip case or silicone cover will prevent it from seating properly.

**Q: Does the case protect the analog sticks?**  
Yes — the cradle is recessed around the sticks, so they don't make contact with the lid. This is a major advantage over generic tablet sleeves.

**Q: Can I charge the Portal while it's in the case?**  
No. The case seals completely. You'll need to remove the Portal to charge, which is standard for hard shells.
