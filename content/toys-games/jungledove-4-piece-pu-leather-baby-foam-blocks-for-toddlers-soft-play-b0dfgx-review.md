## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. Four blocks is not a lot. Your toddler will build a two-block tower and call it a day. For actual construction play, you'll need to buy multiple sets — and at this price point, that adds up fast. Some reviewers also noted the PU leather feels slick and slightly cold against bare skin in winter months. It's a sensory thing; some kids love the smooth texture, others prefer the coziness of fabric.

## Who Should Buy These

This set is ideal for parents with babies aged 6 to 18 months who are in the sit-up, crawl, and first-steps phase. The blocks work as tumbling cushions, leaning supports, and early stacking toys. They're also a smart pick for grandparents or daycare providers who need durable, sanitizable play equipment.

Skip them if your child is already 2+ and building complex structures — you'll want a larger block set with more pieces. And if your toddler insists on chewing everything, the PU leather can show tooth marks over time, though it won't peel or flake like cheaper covers.

## FAQ

**Are these blocks safe for teething babies?**
The foam core is firm enough not to be a choking hazard, and the PU leather won't shed pieces. However, tooth marks will appear on the surface. If your child is an aggressive chewer, consider covering them with a muslin cloth during play.

**Can these be used outside?**
They're fine for a covered patio, but direct sunlight can fade the PU leather over time. Keep them indoors or shaded for longevity.

**How do I clean them properly?**
Wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid bleach or harsh chemicals, which can damage the leather coating. For stubborn stains, a magic eraser works well.
