Let's be honest: most "12 in 1" accessory kits are 90% filler and 10% useful. The Auarte set for the PlayStation Portal is the rare exception where almost every piece earns its place in the bundle. With a 4.6 average across 229 ratings, this is clearly doing something right. After testing it for a few weeks, here's the real breakdown.

## What's Actually in the Box

The kit includes a hard shell travel case, tempered glass screen protector, two pairs of analog stick grips, a charging cable, a carabiner, and a handful of cleaning tools. The star is the case — it's molded specifically for the Portal's distinctive shape, with a snug interior that holds the device securely without putting pressure on the analog sticks. There's also a small mesh pocket for the cable and grips.

The screen protector comes with an alignment frame, which is a godsend. If you've ever tried to line up a glass protector on a 8-inch screen freehand, you know the pain. The frame clicks onto the Portal, you drop the glass in, press, and done. No bubbles, no dust trapped underneath.

## Performance and Build Quality

The thumb grips deserve special mention. They come in two textures — smooth and knurled. The knurled pair adds real traction for sweaty-handed sessions, and they fit tightly enough that they won't slide off mid-game. One reviewer noted, "The grips alone are worth the price — they make the Portal feel like a proper controller instead of a slippery tablet."

The case's hard shell is genuinely protective. It's not a flimsy sleeve — it's rigid polycarbonate that can take a drop. The interior is soft-touch material that won't scratch the Portal's screen. As one buyer put it, "I toss this in my backpack daily and my Portal still looks brand new."

## Where It Falls Short

The cleaning tools are the weak link. The included microfiber cloth is thin and leaves lint behind, and the brush is stiff and barely usable. You'll want to use your own cloth for the screen protector installation. It's a minor annoyance, but it's the kind of thing that makes you question the "premium" positioning.

The charging cable is also short at 3 feet. Fine for overnight charging on a nightstand, but you're not playing while plugged in from across the room. The carabiner is functional but nothing special — it's a metal clip, not a heavy-duty climbing hook.

## Who Should Buy This

This kit is for the Portal owner who wants to actually take their device somewhere. If you're a commuter, frequent traveler, or parent who needs to stash the Portal safely when kids are around, the case alone justifies the purchase. It's also great for new Portal owners who want everything in one order instead of piecing together a case, screen protector, and grips separately.

If you're a strictly-at-home player who never moves the Portal off your coffee table, you can skip this. You'll only use maybe half the kit.

## FAQ

**Is the screen protector actually tempered glass?**
Yes, it's 9H hardness tempered glass with an oleophobic coating. It resists fingerprints and smudges noticeably better than the bare screen.

**Will the case fit the Portal with the screen protector installed?**
Yes, and this is a common concern. The case's interior has enough clearance for the glass to sit flush without pressure on the edges.

**Do the thumb grips interfere with the Portal's touch screen?**
No. They only attach to the analog sticks, which sit below the touchscreen area. The sticks' touch sensitivity for swiping gestures still works fine.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a one-stop protection kit for your Portal, especially if you travel with it or have kids who might drop it. The case and screen protector alone are worth the money, and the thumb grips are a genuine upgrade.

**Skip it if:** You're a couch-only player who never moves the Portal and already have a case you like. You'd be paying for accessories you won't use.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A well-designed kit where the core pieces (case, screen protector, grips) are excellent, dragged down only by weak cleaning tools and a short cable. For the price, it's the best all-in-one option for Portal owners right now.
