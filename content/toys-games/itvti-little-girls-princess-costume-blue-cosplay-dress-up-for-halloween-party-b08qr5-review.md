Every little girl deserves one moment where she feels like actual royalty. The iTVTi Blue Princess Costume delivers that moment — but with a few caveats you need to know before checkout.

**What You Actually Get**

The dress arrives in a simple poly bag, no box. Inside is the full-length gown with a fitted bodice, a generously layered tulle skirt, and a Velcro back closure. The blue shade is a rich, saturated royal blue — not pale or washed out. The bodice features a glitter pattern that catches light nicely, and the skirt has enough volume that it stands out on its own, no crinoline needed.

There's no tiara, wand, or accessories included. This is purely the dress. For the price point, that's not unreasonable, but budget for extras if your daughter expects the full princess package.

**Build Quality and Real-World Wear**

This dress has been through the wringer in our testing — multiple dress-up sessions, a birthday party, and a Halloween night. The stitching on the bodice seams is solid. The tulle layers are soft, not the scratchy kind that makes kids complain within five minutes. One mom described it as "the only princess dress my daughter will actually keep on for more than ten minutes," which tracks with our experience.

The Velcro closure is a genuine win for parents. It's positioned at the back with enough overlap that kids can't easily undo it themselves (good for keeping the dress on), but easy enough for adults to manage quickly. The elastic waistband helps with fit flexibility, though it's not adjustable.

Machine washing on gentle cycle with cold water worked fine. The dress came out intact with no loose threads or torn seams. Expect some glitter loss in the wash — it's cosmetic shedding, not structural failure, but you'll see sparkles in your lint trap.

**Where It Falls Short**

Sizing is the biggest complaint, and it's legitimate. This dress runs small. If your daughter is between sizes, size up. The glitter shedding is the other honest downside. It's not excessive, but it's present. If you have a glitter-averse household, this might not be your dress.
