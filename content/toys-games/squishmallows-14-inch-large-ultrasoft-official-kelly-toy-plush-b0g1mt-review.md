Let's be honest: the plush toy market is crowded with cheap, sad-looking stuffed animals that lose their shape in a week. The Squishmallows 14-inch Kelly stands apart — and with a 4.9-star average across 245 ratings, I'm not the only one who noticed. But is it genuinely worth your money, or just another overhyped TikTok trend? I spent two weeks with this oversized plush to find out.

**What You Actually Get**

The package contains the single 14-inch Kelly plush — no accessories, no extras. Kelly is a pastel-colored character from the Squishmallows lineup, featuring the signature marshmallow-like shape that's made the brand famous. The exterior is a soft, stretchy polyester that gives that satisfying squish when you press it. Inside, the stuffing is a blend that rebounds quickly — it doesn't stay flat after you've been hugging it for hours.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

Here's where Squishmallows earns its reputation. The seams are double-stitched and reinforced at stress points — the ears, the arms, the base. I've seen cheaper plush toys tear at these exact spots within days of aggressive toddler play. The embroidered face is a thoughtful touch; printed faces on cheaper alternatives crack and peel within months. Kelly's eyes and smile are stitched cleanly and show no loose threads after two weeks of daily use.

The 14-inch size is genuinely versatile. It's large enough to serve as a legitimate pillow for adults — I used it for afternoon naps — but light enough that a 5-year-old can carry it around without dragging it on the floor. The stuffing density is balanced: firm enough to hold its shape on a shelf, but pliable enough to squish satisfyingly.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Scanning through the 245 ratings, a few themes emerge. One buyer noted, "My daughter sleeps with this every night — after 6 months it still looks brand new." Another mentioned, "The softness is unreal. I bought one for myself and one for my niece." The durability claims are consistent — multiple reviewers mention months of daily use without flattening or pilling, which is impressive for a plush in this price range.

**Where It Falls Short**

No product is perfect, and this one has real drawbacks. The material is a lint magnet — if you have pets, expect to be picking fur off Kelly regularly. There's no removable cover or zipper, so when it does get dirty, you're limited to spot cleaning. Machine washing is technically possible but risks damaging the stuffing's rebound quality. Also, if you're buying for a baby or toddler who mouths toys, the embroidered details are safe, but the surface fabric isn't as easy to sanitize as a wipe-clean material.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is a solid choice for three specific shoppers: parents of kids aged 3-10 who want a durable, huggable companion; adults who want a genuinely comfortable nap pillow that doubles as decor; and Squishmallows collectors adding to their stash. It's less ideal if you need a machine-washable toy for a baby, or if you're looking for a small, portable size — the 14-inch is definitely a lap-sized, not pocket-sized, companion.

A: Yes, the embroidered face is safe, and the stuffing is soft enough to be a comfort item. Just be aware it's not machine-washable, so keep it away from mealtime.

**Q: Will it flatten over time?**

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a genuinely soft, durable plush that will survive daily hugging without turning into a flat pancake. The quality justifies the price.

**Skip it if:** You need a machine-washable toy for a baby, or you're buying for a child who's rough enough to eventually tear even reinforced seams.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting half a point for the lint magnet issue and lack of washability. Otherwise, this is the best large plush I've tested in this price range.
