**The Orks are back, and Games Workshop actually listened this time.**

If you've been eyeing the green tide but hesitated because older Combat Patrol boxes felt like a grab bag of random units, the 2024 Orks set fixes that. This isn't just a reboxing of last edition's contents — it's a complete redesign built around the 10th Edition meta. And at 4.8 stars from 240 Amazon ratings, the community is clearly on board.

---

**What's in the box**

You're getting 25 plastic multi-part miniatures plus a 28-page Combat Patrol rulebook and a transfer sheet. The army breakdown:

- **1x Warboss in Mega Armour** — the centerpiece, and honestly one of the best character kits GW has produced in this scale. The pose is dynamic, the power klaw is chunky, and the head options give you real personality choices.
- **1x Deffkoptas (3 models)** — fast attack with decent firepower. These are the only shooting units in the box, and they're genuinely useful for grabbing objectives.
- **1x Nobz (5 models)** — multi-option kit with power klaws, big choppas, and slugga/choppa combos. You'll want to magnetize these if you plan to run them differently across games.
- **1x Boyz (20 models)** — the classic infantry core. The 2024 sculpts are a massive upgrade over the previous kit; they're more animated, with better proportions and far less "crouching goblin" energy.

As shown in the product image, the sprues are packed — expect around 3-4 hours of assembly time if you're experienced, longer if this is your first kit.

---

**Build quality and real-world use**

The plastic quality is standard GW — hard, crisp, and forgiving of mistakes. But here's the honest part: these are NOT push-fit models. Every single one requires glue, and there's a fair amount of flash on the Boyz sprues. One Amazon reviewer put it bluntly: "The mold lines are everywhere. You'll need a hobby knife and some patience before you even think about priming."

That's not a dealbreaker — it's par for the course with multi-part kits — but go in with realistic expectations.

On the tabletop, the Combat Patrol format (500-point games) is where this box shines. The Warboss in Mega Armour is a blender in melee — I've seen him chew through entire squad units in a single combat phase. The Boyz give you the board presence you need, and the Deffkoptas provide the mobility to actually score points.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The value proposition is the runaway theme in the reviews. One verified purchase wrote: "This is the first Combat Patrol where I didn't feel like I was paying for filler. Every model is usable and the rules included are actually fun." Another said: "Perfect starting point for Orks. The Warboss alone is worth the price."

The 10th Edition rules inclusion is a big deal too. You get a full playable army list, deployment maps, and secondary objectives — no codex needed. That's a genuine improvement over previous editions where you'd need multiple books just to field the box.

---

**Where it falls short**

Three honest complaints:

1. **Zero vehicles.** No Trukk, no Battlewagon, no Deff Dread. The Orks identity is partially about ramshackle vehicles, and their absence means this list is painfully slow footslogging. The Deffkoptas help, but they're fragile.

2. **The Nobz kit is old.** While the Boyz got new sculpts, the Nobz are the same kit from 2018. They're fine, but the quality difference is noticeable side-by-side.

3. **No upgrade sprues.** If you want to build any of the special weapon options (rokkits, big shootas, etc.), you're out of luck — the sprues are basic loadouts only.

---

**Who should buy this**

- **New Ork players** — this is the best starting point. The rules make it playable day one, and every model is useful in larger armies later.
- **Existing Ork collectors** — the Warboss and new Boyz are worth the price alone if you need them.
- **Competitive players** — skip it. This list is mid-tier at best; you'll want a Trukk-filled list with Beast Snaggas instead.

---

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I play this out of the box or do I need additional purchases?**
A: Out of the box, yes. The included rulebook has everything for Combat Patrol games. You'll need dice, a ruler, and glue — but no codex or extra models.

**Q: Is this the same as the 9th Edition Combat Patrol?**
A: No. The 2024 version has a new Warboss in Mega Armour, new Boyz sculpts, and updated rules. The 9th Edition box had different units entirely.

**Q: How long does assembly take?**
A: Expect 3-5 hours for an experienced builder. The Boyz are the time sink — 20 models with multiple parts each.

---

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You're starting Orks fresh, want the best dollar-to-points value in the Combat Patrol line, or need the new Warboss sculpt.

**Skip it if:** You're a competitive player, already own the 9th Edition box, or expect vehicle support.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The lack of vehicles and older Nobz sculpts cost it half a star, but for a starter army, this is the best Combat Patrol GW has produced this edition.
