# Beilunt 117 Pack Party Favors Toy Easter Egg Filler: The Bulk Buy That Actually Delivers

You've seen the drill: buy a 100-pack of "toys" that turns out to be 85 pieces of plastic junk and 15 barely-functional erasers. The Beilunt 117-piece set doesn't fall into that trap — but it's not perfect either. Here's what you're actually getting.

## What's In the Bag

The 117 pieces break down into roughly a dozen different toy types. You get fidget spinners, mini puzzles, finger skateboards, yo-yos, spinning tops, and various small games. As shown in the product image, the assortment leans heavily toward fidget-style toys rather than traditional Easter trinkets like bunnies or eggs — which is actually a strength. These are toys kids will play with in June, not just on Easter Sunday.

The pieces themselves are small — most fit in the palm of your hand — which makes them perfect for standard plastic Easter eggs. Plan on 8-10 pieces per egg for a satisfying fill, meaning this bag covers 12-15 eggs comfortably.

## Real-World Performance

Build quality is mixed but mostly positive. The fidget spinners and puzzles are solid — they'll survive being thrown, dropped, and shoved into backpacks. I've seen kids put these through daily use for weeks without breaking them. One Amazon reviewer noted, "My kids loved these, they played with them all week after Easter." That tracks with my experience.

The weaker links are the finger skateboards and yo-yos. These are thin plastic and will snap under enthusiastic play. A 7-year-old will probably break one within a day. That's not a dealbreaker at this price point, but set expectations.

Parents and teachers love the value. With 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, the consensus is clear: this is one of the better bulk toy packs available. Common praise points include:

## Where It Falls Short

The honest downsides:

1. **Choking hazard for young kids.** The fidget spinner bearings and small puzzle pieces are dangerous for under-3s. This is not a toddler toy — if you have kids under 3 in the house, skip it or keep it strictly supervised.

2. **No sorting.** Everything arrives loose in one bag. You'll spend 10-15 minutes sorting pieces if you want an even distribution across eggs. Not a huge deal, but worth knowing.

3. **The flimsy items.** Figure 15-20% of the pieces are cheap enough to break quickly. For the price, that's acceptable — but don't expect every piece to last.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Parents planning an Easter egg hunt** — this is the core use case and it nails it
- **Teachers** — perfect for classroom reward bins or end-of-year party favors
- **Crafters and event planners** — good for goodie bags, carnival prizes, or church events

Skip it if you need toys for kids under 3, or if you want premium-quality individual pieces rather than bulk variety.
