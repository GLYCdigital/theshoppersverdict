**The Crayola Easter Light Ups kit is the rare Easter craft that actually delivers on its promise.** The concept is simple: decorated eggs that glow from within. The execution, as it turns out, is surprisingly good.

## What's in the Box

You get three plastic egg-shaped light bases, each with a clear shell that opens for decorating. The kit includes six washable markers in bright, Easter-appropriate colors (pink, yellow, blue, green, purple, and orange). That's it — no dye, no vinegar, no boiling water. That's the point.

As shown in the product image, the eggs sit upright on their bases like little glowing trophies. Each base has a small switch and is designed to hold either the included plastic eggs or real hard-boiled ones up to standard large size.

## How It Actually Performs

Here's where this kit stands apart from every dye-based kit on the shelf. You decorate the clear plastic eggs directly with the markers, then snap them onto the light bases. The LED inside illuminates the entire shell, making your marker art glow like a neon sign. It's genuinely striking — kids see their scribbles transform into something that looks like a professional light-up decoration.

The markers are surprisingly good. They're washable (tested — comes off skin and most surfaces with water), and they dry quickly enough that the 5-second wait before placing the egg on the base isn't annoying. The fine tips allow for real detail work, which older kids and adults will appreciate. Younger kids (under 5) may struggle with the small tips and prefer chunky markers, but the glow effect keeps them engaged regardless.

The light bases are sturdy. They're not flimsy plastic — they feel like they'll survive being dropped on a hardwood floor, which is the real test for any kids' product. Battery life is solid; one set of button cells lasted through a full afternoon of decorating and several hours of display before dimming.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.8-star rating across 240 reviews holds up. Parents repeatedly mention two things: the "wow factor" and the reusability. One reviewer put it simply: "My kids decorated eggs three times in one week — they wouldn't stop. The glow makes everything look amazing." Another noted, "Unlike dye kits, there's zero mess and zero smell. We did this on the kitchen table in 20 minutes."

Multiple reviewers also highlighted the year-after-year value. Since the plastic eggs wipe clean with a damp cloth, the kit isn't a one-and-done Easter morning activity — it's a reusable craft staple.

## Where It Falls Short

The battery situation is the biggest annoyance. Each base needs three AG13/LR44 button batteries, and they're not included. That's nine batteries for the full set — you'll spend nearly as much on batteries as the kit itself costs. Buy them in bulk online rather than at a drugstore.

The marker selection is also thin. Six colors are fine, but there's no metallic or glow-in-the-dark option, which feels like a missed opportunity for a product literally about glowing. Some reviewers wished for thicker markers for younger kids and a white marker for contrast on the clear shells.

## Who Should Buy This

This is perfect for:
- **Parents of kids 4-10** who want an Easter activity that doesn't involve boiling water and vinegar fumes
- **Classroom teachers** looking for a low-mess holiday craft (the light bases make a great classroom display)
- **Families with egg allergies** — this kit completely sidesteps the real-egg question
- **Anyone tired of dye-stained fingers** and half-used dye tablets cluttering the pantry

Skip it if your kids are under 3 (small parts, button batteries) or if you're specifically looking for a traditional egg-dyeing experience. This is a modern alternative, not a replacement.

## FAQ

**Q: Can you use real eggs with this?**
A: Yes, the bases hold standard large hard-boiled eggs. Just note that the markers are designed for the plastic shells — drawing directly on a real egg works, but the glow effect is less dramatic since the shell isn't transparent.

**Q: How long do the batteries last?**
A: With moderate use (a few hours of decorating plus display time), expect 8-12 hours of continuous light. The bases have an on/off switch, so turning them off when not in use extends battery life significantly.

**Q: Are the markers actually washable?**
A: Yes. They come off skin and most fabrics with water. On the plastic eggs, they wipe off completely with a damp cloth, so you can redecorate as many times as you want.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely fun, reusable Easter craft with a real "wow" factor and zero mess. **Skip it if** you're looking for a traditional dye kit or don't want to deal with buying button batteries.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The battery hassle and limited marker selection keep it from a perfect score, but this is one of the most creative and well-executed Easter crafts to hit the market in years. It earns its place on the table every spring.
