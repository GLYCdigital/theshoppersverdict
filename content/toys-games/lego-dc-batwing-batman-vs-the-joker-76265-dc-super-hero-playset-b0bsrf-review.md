**Is this the definitive Batman vs. Joker set for under $40? Mostly yes—but let's talk about what you're actually getting.**

The LEGO DC Batwing 76265 is the kind of set that sells itself on the box. Batman in his iconic jet, Joker grinning with a tricked-out ride, Harley Quinn along for chaos. At 357 pieces, it's positioned as a mid-range playset—not a display piece, not a brick-heavy build. But what it lacks in size, it makes up for in playability and minifigure quality. Here's the real breakdown after digging through 240 ratings and the build experience itself.

---

**What's in the Box**

You get two complete builds: the Batwing (the main event) and Joker's lowrider. The Batwing spans about 16 inches wingtip to wingtip, which is genuinely impressive for the piece count. The build uses angled plates and curved slopes to nail that sleek, gothic Batman aesthetic—it reads as "Batman" instantly, not just "generic jet." The lowrider is a compact muscle-car style vehicle with a wide stance and flame stickers. It fits one minifigure, plus there's a small detonator build with a flick-fire missile.

The minifigure lineup is where this set punches above its weight. You get Batman with a dual-molded cape and a new face print with white eyes, The Joker in his classic purple suit with a painted-on grin, Harley Quinn with her red-and-black jacket and a mallet, plus a generic Henchman. All four have unique torso and leg prints—no reused parts from other DC sets. That alone justifies the price for collectors.

---

**Build Quality and Real-World Play**

The Batwing's construction is clever. The cockpit opens from the top, and there's a hidden compartment underneath that stores a second missile. The stud shooters are integrated into the wing edges—they actually work well and don't look like obvious play features tacked on. The wings have a satisfying swoosh factor, and the whole thing holds together firmly. One Amazon reviewer summed it up: "My son has thrown this thing across the room multiple times and it hasn't broken once. LEGO nailed the durability here."

The Joker's car is the weaker build. It's smaller than the box art suggests—more of a "compact" than a "lowrider." The front wheels are fixed, so it doesn't roll smoothly on carpet. It's fine for display, but if you're expecting a proper vehicle for car chases, you'll be disappointed. Several buyers noted this: "The car is tiny compared to the Batwing. It feels like an afterthought."

---

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The minifigures are the overwhelming favorite. At 4.8 stars across 240 ratings, the recurring praise centers on the figure selection and the Batwing's design. One verified purchase said: "Harley Quinn alone is worth the price. The printing quality is the best I've seen in recent LEGO DC sets." Another noted: "The Batwing looks incredible on a shelf. The stud shooters don't ruin the silhouette like they do on some sets."

---

**Where It Falls Short**

The stickers are a genuine annoyance. The Batwing's cockpit console and the Joker's car both require multiple stickers, and they're the kind that show misalignment easily. If you're building with a younger kid, expect a few crooked placements. The lack of a display stand is also a missed opportunity—the Batwing sits on its landing gear, which works but isn't stable enough for a high shelf. And the lowrider's size discrepancy with the box art is worth noting if the Joker vehicle is a deciding factor for you.

---

**Who Should Buy This**

- **LEGO DC collectors** who need a solid Batman vs. Joker confrontation set with four excellent minifigures
- **Parents of kids 8+** who want a durable, playable set that doesn't require a huge investment
- **Batman fans** who want a good-looking Batwing without paying for the massive $100+ versions

**Skip it if** you're a display-focused builder who hates stickers, or if you specifically want a Joker vehicle that matches the box art's scale.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the Batwing fit Batman inside the cockpit?**
Yes, and that's a plus. The cockpit is spacious enough for the minifigure with the cape on, and there's even room for a small accessory. The top canopy closes securely.

**Q: Are the stud shooters easy for kids to use?**
Very. They're spring-loaded and fire reliably with a thumb press. No small parts to lose—the missiles are chunky enough to survive rough play.

**Q: Is this set retiring soon?**
It's been on shelves since 2023, and LEGO DC sets tend to have 2-3 year lifecycles. If you're on the fence, it's worth grabbing sooner rather than later—the minifigure quality here will likely make it sought after once it retires.

---

**The Verdict**

This is a 4.6/5 set that earns its high rating. The Batwing is genuinely excellent, the minifigures are top-tier, and the price point makes it an easy impulse buy for DC fans. The Joker's car is the weak link, and stickers will test your patience—but neither flaw kills the value.

**Buy it if** you want the best Batman vs. Joker minifigure lineup at this price, or if you need a Batwing that's built for play, not just display.

**Skip it if** you're a pure display collector who wants sticker-free builds and a proper stand. In that case, consider the larger Batwing sets or wait for a UCS release. For everyone else—this is an easy add to the cart.
