If you're shopping for a Series X and Forza Horizon 5 is on your radar, this bundle is the smartest way to buy both. Let's be clear about what you're getting: a $499 console plus a game that normally costs $99 for the Premium Edition. You're effectively getting the game's full package at a discount, and the console itself remains the most powerful mainstream option on the market.

**What's actually in the box**

The bundle includes the Xbox Series X console, one wireless controller (the standard black model – no fancy Forza-themed paint job, unfortunately), and a download code for Forza Horizon 5 Premium Edition. You won't find a disc inside. That's worth knowing if you're the type who trades games or buys used. The Premium Edition is the complete package: the base game, both expansions (Hot Wheels and Rally Adventure), the VIP membership (double credits, weekly cars), and the Car Pass that adds 42 cars over time. That's a $99 value folded into the console price.

**Performance: where this bundle earns its keep**

The Series X outputs native 4K at a locked 60fps in Forza's Performance mode, and it's genuinely transformative. Mexico's open world loads in about four seconds from the dashboard – no splash screens, no staring at a loading bar. The console's 12 teraflop GPU handles the draw distances and weather effects without a stutter. If you have a 120Hz display, you can drop to 1440p and get double the frame rate, which makes a visible difference in high-speed races.

The 1TB NVMe SSD is fast, but it's not spacious. Forza Horizon 5 takes about 130GB with all expansions installed. Add Call of Duty or a couple of Game Pass titles and you're looking at your storage breathing hard. Microsoft sells a 1TB expansion card for $199 – a price that still stings. Factor that into your budget if you know you'll go deep on multiple games.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The Amazon reviews at 4.7 stars over 235 ratings keep circling the same themes. One buyer put it plainly: *"Came with the code for the full premium version, not just the base game. I thought it was a typo but no – everything unlocked."* Another noted the value angle: *"Bought this instead of a PS5 because of the Game Pass ultimate deal, but the Forza bundle made it a no-brainer."* Several reviewers mentioned the console running cool and quiet even during extended sessions – that's a real advantage over the previous generation.

**Where it falls short**

The lack of a physical disc is the biggest sticking point for a segment of buyers. If you're a collector or reseller, this bundle forces you to go all-digital. The download code also means you can't lend the game to a friend. Quick Resume – the feature that suspends multiple games – occasionally conflicts with Forza's online connectivity, kicking you back to the title screen. It's minor, but it happens.

There's also the controller situation: you get one, and it's the standard version. No rechargeable battery pack included; you'll burn through AA batteries or buy a play-and-charge kit separately.

**Who should buy this**

- **Xbox newcomers** who want the best console and a flagship racing game in one purchase
- **Racing fans** who haven't played Forza Horizon 5 and want the complete edition without a la carte pricing
- **Game Pass subscribers** – this console pairs perfectly with the service, and the Forza code is a bonus on top

**Skip it if** you're a physical media loyalist, already own Forza Horizon 5, or your gaming diet is mostly shooters and RPGs where the storage limit will bite faster.

**Three questions shoppers ask**

**Q: Is the Forza code for the base game or Premium Edition?**
A: Premium Edition. You get both expansions, VIP, and the Car Pass. Several reviewers confirmed this wasn't a bait-and-switch.

**Q: Can I play this bundle on a 1080p TV?**
A: Yes. The console scales down resolution automatically, and you'll still get 60fps. You just won't see the full 4K benefit until you upgrade your display.

**Q: Does this come with a disc for the game?**
A: No. It's a download code printed on a card inside the box. You'll need a Microsoft account and internet connection to redeem it.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want the most powerful console available and the full Forza Horizon 5 experience in one box. The bundle effectively discounts the game's premium version, and the Series X hardware remains a beast for its price.

**Skip it if** you already own the game or you're committed to physical media – the all-digital approach won't serve you well.

**Rating: 4.6/5** – A top-tier console bundled with the definitive version of a fantastic game. The storage limitations and digital-only format keep it from a perfect score, but for most buyers, this is the best way to enter the current Xbox generation.
