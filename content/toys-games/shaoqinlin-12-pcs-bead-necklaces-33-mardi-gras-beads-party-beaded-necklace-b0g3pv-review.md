Let's talk tradeoffs. These are lightweight beads. If you're used to the heavy, parade-grade strands with thick cords and dense bead packing, these will feel noticeably lighter. They're not going to survive being yanked, and the thinner cord means they won't handle rough play from toddlers.

The colors are also pre-mixed. You get what you get — there's no option to order all-purple or all-gold. For most party use this is fine, but if you have a specific color scheme, look elsewhere.
