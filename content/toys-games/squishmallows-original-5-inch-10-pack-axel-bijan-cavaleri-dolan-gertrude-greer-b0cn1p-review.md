Second, the size. These are genuinely small. For toddlers or young children who might put things in their mouths, the 5-inch size is actually a choking hazard consideration. The standard 8-inch Squishmallows are safer for that age group. Parents should weigh this before purchasing.

Third, the packaging is utilitarian. You're getting a plain brown shipping box with the plush inside. If this is a gift, budget for wrapping paper or a gift bag.
