If you've ever watched a preschooler lose interest in a sticker book after ten minutes because the stickers are flimsy, single-use, and stick to everything they shouldn't — the Melissa & Doug Puffy Sticker Play Set in the Princess theme is the antidote. It's a simple concept executed well: 67 chunky puffy stickers plus two glossy fold-out scenes that let kids create and recreate castle life endlessly. At $8.70, it's a solid value for the playtime it delivers.

**What you actually get**

The box contains 67 puffy stickers (princesses, princes, castles, animals, flowers, and accessories) and two double-sided scene boards. One side shows an interior castle ballroom with a throne, stairs, and windows; flip it over for an outdoor kingdom with a drawbridge and garden. The second scene offers additional play space with different backdrops. The stickers are roughly 1-3 inches tall with thick foam backing — these are not the paper-thin stickers from your childhood. The scenes measure about 10 x 7 inches when unfolded, compact enough to toss in a diaper bag.

**Performance and real-world use**

The puffy construction is the star here. The foam backing gives toddlers something to grip, and the adhesive is strong enough to hold through enthusiastic play but not so aggressive that it damages surfaces. In testing, stickers peeled off the glossy scenes cleanly after being pressed down repeatedly — no residue, no tearing. One parent noted, "My 3-year-old has had this for three months and the stickers still look brand new." That's the durability you're paying for.

The two scenes are a thoughtful touch. Kids can stage a ballroom scene, then flip to the garden for a completely different setup. Some buyers report their kids use the stickers on bathtub walls, windows, and even the fridge — the puffy material holds up to moisture and smooth surfaces alike.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.6-star average across 229 ratings reflects genuine satisfaction. Parents repeatedly highlight three things: the sticker quality ("thick and sturdy, not flimsy at all"), the reusability factor ("my daughter rearranges the castle scene every single day"), and the open-ended play value. Multiple reviewers mention this as a go-to gift for 3-6 year olds, especially for travel or restaurant outings. One grandmother wrote, "Bought this for my granddaughter's birthday — she played with it for an hour straight. Quiet, contained, and no mess."

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, there's no storage solution — the 67 stickers live loose in the box, and without a baggie or small container, you'll be hunting for stray princesses under couch cushions. Second, puffy stickers don't adhere to standard paper, so if your kid wants to decorate a notebook or card, these won't cooperate. Third, the scenes are glossy cardstock, not laminated — they'll show wear if a toddler gets too enthusiastic with bending or scribbling on them. These are minor issues, but worth knowing before you buy.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for parents of 3-6 year olds who want a screen-free, mess-free activity that travels well. It's also a strong choice for grandparents looking for a gift that won't require batteries or assembly. If your child is a princess-obsessed creative type who loves storytelling, this will hit hard. Skip it if your kid is over 7 (they'll likely want more complexity) or if you're looking for something that works on regular paper.

**FAQ**

**Are the stickers actually reusable?** Yes — the adhesive holds up through dozens of repositioning cycles on the glossy scenes. They'll eventually lose stickiness after heavy use, but you'll get months of play before that happens.

**Is this safe for younger toddlers?** The stickers are large enough (1+ inches) that they don't pose a choking hazard for most kids over 3, but the box recommends ages 4+. Supervision is wise for kids who still mouth objects.

**Do the scenes have a storage slot?** No — the scenes fold flat and the stickers go back in the box, but there's no compartment or pouch. Buy a small zip bag if you want to keep the set organized.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a durable, reusable sticker set that will survive a preschooler's daily play for months. It's a genuinely good value at $8.70.

**Skip it if:** You need something that works on paper, or you're tired of managing loose pieces.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The lack of storage and paper incompatibility keep it from a perfect score, but for the target age range, this is one of the best puffy sticker sets on the market.
