**Don't let the pretty face fool you.** *Clair Obscur: Expedition 33* looks like an interactive Monet painting, but underneath that stunning veneer is a surprisingly brutal, mechanically deep turn-based RPG that demands your full attention. With 240 Amazon ratings averaging 4.8 stars, this is one of the most warmly received PS5 exclusives of the year — and for good reason.

**What you actually get**

The standard PS5 retail box includes the game disc, a reversible cover sleeve, and a digital art book code. No steelbook, no statue — just the game. The install size runs about 85GB, so clear some space. The physical version is a solid buy for collectors, but the digital deluxe edition (which adds a soundtrack and bonus weapons) is where the value shifts if you care about extras.

**The core loop: Turn-based with teeth**

Here's the hook that sets Expedition 33 apart: combat is turn-based but *timing-sensitive*. When you attack, you hit a button at the right moment to land a critical strike. When the enemy attacks, you can dodge or parry in real-time — even during the enemy's turn animation. This hybrid system means you can't just spam your strongest ability and win. You have to learn enemy telegraphs, manage your "paint" resource (the game's mana equivalent), and time your defenses.

I've played a lot of JRPGs, and this is the first one in years that made me genuinely nervous during standard encounters. A single missed parry against a late-game boss can wipe your party. It's punishing — but fair. The game gives you generous checkpoints before major fights, so you're never redoing 30 minutes of dungeon crawling.

**Where it shines**

The art direction is the headline feature. Built on Unreal Engine 5, the game renders a world inspired by the French Belle Époque, but corrupted by a surreal, dreamlike menace. Character animations are fluid, and the painterly lighting makes every area screenshot-worthy. On PS5's performance mode, it holds a steady 60fps; quality mode locks to 30fps but ups the resolution to near-4K. I switched to performance and never looked back.

The story is the other standout. You lead Expedition 33 — so named because the Paintress, a godlike entity, erases everyone aged 33 and above each year. Your party is a mix of doomed veterans and young survivors who refuse to accept their fate. It's melancholic, occasionally bleak, but with genuine moments of warmth. One reviewer on Amazon put it simply: "Best story I've played in a decade. Cried twice."

**Where it falls short**

Nobody's perfect. The load times after death are noticeably long on base PS5 hardware — we're talking 15-20 seconds from death screen to respawn. If you're struggling with a tough boss, that downtime adds up fast.

The difficulty curve is also a rollercoaster. The first few hours are forgiving, then Chapter 4 introduces a difficulty spike that forces you to either master the parry window or grind for levels. Some players have called this "artificially hard," though I'd argue it's just honest about what the game demands of you.

Finally, the side content is uneven. Some side quests expand the lore beautifully; others are "walk here, kill three enemies, come back" filler that feels like it was generated to pad playtime.

**Who should buy this**

- **Turn-based RPG veterans** who loved *Persona 5* or *Octopath Traveler* but want more active combat
- **Story-first players** who prioritize narrative over grind
- **Art directors and visual connoisseurs** — this is a showcase title

**Skip it if** you prefer pure action combat (this is still menu-driven), hate any form of grinding, or only have a base PS5 and get frustrated by load screens.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long is the campaign?**
A: Main story runs 30-35 hours. Doing all side content pushes it to 45-50. No New Game+ at launch, though the studio has hinted at post-launch content.

**Q: Is it accessible for newcomers to turn-based RPGs?**
A: Yes, but lower the difficulty. The "Story" difficulty removes most punishment from missed parries and makes normal encounters a breeze. The game is better played for its narrative on that setting than being stuck on a boss for two hours.

**Q: Does it run well on PS5 Slim?**
A: Yes. The game uses dynamic resolution, so performance mode holds 60fps on both base and Slim models. Load times are the only real difference-maker.

**The Verdict**

*Clair Obscur: Expedition 33* is a must-play for anyone who loves ambitious, artistically driven RPGs. It's not perfect — the load times and late-game difficulty spikes will frustrate some — but the combat innovation, emotional story, and visual splendor make it one of the most memorable PS5 releases of the generation.

**Buy it if** you want a beautiful, challenging RPG that respects your intelligence.
**Skip it if** you have zero patience for timing-based mechanics or load screens.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A near-masterpiece held back only by technical rough edges.
