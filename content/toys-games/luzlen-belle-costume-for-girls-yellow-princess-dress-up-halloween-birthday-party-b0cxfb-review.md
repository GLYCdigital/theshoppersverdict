For a costume in this price range, the construction is genuinely better than average. The seams are finished cleanly, and the hem is double-stitched — which matters when a six-year-old is sprinting through a pumpkin patch. The skirt has enough volume to hold a nice shape without requiring a separate petticoat, though adding a simple tulle underskirt will give it that extra "ballroom" bounce if your kid cares about such things.

**Where it falls short**

The most consistent complaint across reviews is sizing. This dress runs small. Several buyers explicitly warned to order a size up. One review said, *"My 5-year-old normally wears a 5-6, but this fit like a 4. The length was fine, but the bodice was tight across the chest."* Another parent noted, *"Order one size bigger than you think you need — trust me."* If your child is tall for her age or has a broader build, definitely size up.

The zipper is also a weak point. It's thin and can snag on the fabric if you pull it quickly. It's manageable if you're patient, but younger kids will likely need help getting in and out — which is typical for dress-up costumes, but worth flagging. A few reviewers also mentioned the sleeves are slightly snug, so if your kid has broader shoulders, that's another reason to size up.
