# Tub Works Smooth Bath Crayons Review: A Parent's Honest Take

Let's be real — most bath toys end up in a damp bin under the sink within a month. The Tub Works Smooth Bath Crayon is different. This yellow crayon has cracked 4.5 stars across 225 Amazon ratings, and after digging into what buyers actually say, I understand why. It solves the two biggest bath crayon complaints: they don't draw smoothly, and they don't wash off easily. This one does both — mostly.

## What You Actually Get

This is a single yellow crayon with a hexagonal body, roughly the size of a standard jumbo crayon. The hexagon grip isn't a gimmick — it's the feature that sets this apart from round bath crayons. Round crayons roll into the water mid-drawing; this one stays put. The formula is labeled nontoxic and washable, and the packaging confirms it's designed for toddler hands.

## How It Performs in Real Baths

The application is genuinely smooth. Multiple reviewers noted their kids could draw without pressing hard, which matters when you're dealing with a 2-year-old who doesn't understand "gentle pressure" yet. The yellow shows up vividly on white tile and light-colored tubs — much more visible than pastel bath crayons that fade into the background.

The washability claim holds up — on smooth surfaces. Most buyers reported that a wet wipe or a quick rinse removed everything. One parent noted, "My daughter drew all over the tile and the tub, and it came off with just a sponge and water. No scrubbing." That tracks with the consensus.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The grip is the star. Reviewers repeatedly mention that their kids could hold this crayon properly — a huge win for toddlers developing fine motor skills. The smoothness comes up second. As one parent put it, "It's like drawing with butter compared to the cheap ones that skip and scratch."

Parents also appreciate the nontoxic formula. Bath time inevitably involves a crayon near a mouth, and knowing it's safe reduces stress. The single-crayon format is actually a plus for some — you can hand a toddler one crayon without them immediately trying to eat or hurl the other seven.

## Where It Falls Short

The single-color limitation is the biggest practical issue. You're buying yellow specifically. If your kid wants to draw a rainbow, you're buying multiple single crayons, which adds up. The product images show a single crayon — this isn't a hidden set.

The washability has limits. On textured tub mats or matte-finished tubs, removal takes more effort and some buyers reported faint yellow staining that required a bleach cleaner. If your tub has any texture, set expectations accordingly.

The formula is soft by design, which means it can break if dropped on tile from counter height. A few reviewers mentioned this — it's not a defect, it's the nature of a crayon that draws smoothly. Just don't toss it around.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Anyone tired of bath crayons that skip, scratch, or require scrubbing to remove**

## FAQ

**Is it actually safe if my toddler puts it in their mouth?**
It's labeled nontoxic, which means it won't poison your child if ingested in small amounts. That said, it's not food — monitor your kid and remove it if they're actively chewing on it. No crayon is meant to be eaten, but this one won't send you to the ER.

**How long does one crayon last?**
Depends on usage. Parents report 2-4 weeks of daily baths with normal use. It wears down faster than regular crayons because it's formulated to deposit color on wet surfaces, but you're not replacing it weekly.

**Does it work on painted walls?**
It's designed for tubs and tile, not walls. If your kid gets creative on painted surfaces, expect to scrub. Stick to the tub.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a bath crayon that actually works — smooth drawing, easy grip, and washable from smooth surfaces. It's a solid pick for toddlers who need a grip-friendly crayon and parents who don't want to scrub grout afterward.

**Skip it if** you need multiple colors, have a textured tub, or want something that survives being thrown across the bathroom.
