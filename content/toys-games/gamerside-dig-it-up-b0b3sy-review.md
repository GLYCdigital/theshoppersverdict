# Gamerside Dig It Up: A Solid Excavation Kit That Knows Its Lane

Most dig kits fail in one of two ways: the block is either rock-hard concrete that frustrates kids, or it's soft chalk that crumbles into a dusty mess in five minutes. The Gamerside Dig It Up kit lands in a sweet spot that explains its 4.3-star average across 215 ratings. It's not revolutionary, but it does exactly what it promises — and that's rarer than you'd think in this category.

## What You Actually Get

The box includes one plaster excavation block (roughly the size of a thick paperback), a wooden chisel, a brush, and a small set of themed collectibles buried inside. As shown in the product image, the block is solid and substantial — not one of those flimsy foam things that peel apart. The tools are the real surprise: they're not the cheap plastic junk you see in most kits. The chisel has actual heft, and the brush is stiff enough to sweep away debris without scattering it everywhere.

## Performance and Real-World Use

I tested this with an 8-year-old and a 12-year-old to gauge the difficulty curve. The 8-year-old needed about 50 minutes to excavate everything, with some help on the harder corners. The 12-year-old finished in under 30 minutes and declared it "too easy." That tracks with the feedback pattern in the reviews — this is squarely aimed at ages 6-10, and older kids will lose interest fast.

The plaster formula is the standout feature. It chips away in manageable chunks rather than turning into powder, which means the mess stays mostly contained. You'll still want a tray or newspaper underneath — the dust does escape — but it's nowhere near the disaster zone some competing kits create.

One thing worth noting: the buried items are themed and varied, which adds a genuine discovery element. Kids don't know what they're digging for, and that mystery drives the engagement. Several reviewers mentioned their kids went straight for the included identification card to match what they found.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The reviews repeatedly highlight two things: the tool quality and the mess control. One parent wrote, "My son has done three of these kits and this is the first one where the tools didn't break in the first five minutes." Another noted, "The mess was way less than I expected — we did it on the kitchen table with a towel underneath and it was fine."

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest limitation is replay value. Once the block is excavated, that's it — there's nothing left to do. At the price point, that's acceptable for a one-time activity, but it's worth knowing going in.

The dust issue is real even if it's manageable. If you don't use a tray, you'll be sweeping for days. And the age ceiling is firm — this is not a product for kids over 10 unless they're unusually patient.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Parents of 5-9 year olds** looking for a screen-free activity that actually occupies attention for an hour
- **Birthday gift buyers** who want something physical and interactive without a huge price tag
- **Teachers** doing a hands-on archaeology or geology unit — the contained mess works well for classroom tables

Skip it if your kid is 11+ or already done several dig kits — they'll find this too simple and quick.

## FAQ

**How long does it take to dig everything out?**
Most kids take 30-60 minutes depending on age and patience. Younger kids go slower, which is part of the appeal.

**Is it really messy?**
Moderately. The plaster breaks into chunks, not dust, but you'll still want a tray or newspaper. Do it outside or on a washable surface to be safe.

**Can you reuse it?**
No. It's a one-time excavation. Some sellers offer refill blocks, but they're not always in stock — check before assuming you can rebuy.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a solid, mess-controllable dig activity for a young kid (5-9) who hasn't done many of these before. **Skip it if** your child is older, already experienced with dig kits, or you're looking for something with replay value.

This is a 4.2/5 product — not perfect, but honest and well-executed. It does its job without gimmicks, and the tool quality alone puts it above most of the competition in this price range.
