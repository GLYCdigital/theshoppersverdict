Let me be direct: if you're chasing the $200+ Umbreon ex from Prismatic Evolutions, this mini tin is a lottery ticket with extra steps. But that's not necessarily a bad thing — it depends on what you actually want.

## What You're Actually Getting

The Prismatic Evolutions Mini Tin comes with **two booster packs** of Scarlet & Violet: Prismatic Evolutions, plus a collectible tin featuring Eeveelution artwork. The tins come in a case of eight with different Eeveelutions on the lid (Flareon, Vaporeon, Jolteon, Espeon, Umbreon, Glaceon, Leafeon, Sylveon), though buying a single listing usually means you get one random design.

The tin itself is sturdy — roughly the size of a deck box, with a hinged lid that snaps shut securely. It's genuinely useful for storing cards after you've ripped the packs. The artwork is vibrant and the print quality is solid, which matters if you're displaying these on a shelf.

## The Pull Rate Reality

Here's where things get honest. Prismatic Evolutions has infamously brutal pull rates. The set's special illustration rares are notoriously scarce, and with only two packs per tin, your odds of hitting something significant are low. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"Got two packs, got two regular holos. This set is brutal for pulls. Buy it if you like the tins, not for the cards."*

That said, the reprint wave in late 2025 did improve availability. If you're buying the 8-count display (which is what this listing appears to be based on the B0DP5J1T8B ASIN), you're getting 16 packs total — better odds, but still a gamble. At current street pricing, the per-pack cost is roughly comparable to buying loose packs, so you're not overpaying for the privilege.

## Where It Falls Short

**Dented tins are a recurring complaint.** When shipped individually, these tins arrive loose in poly mailers or lightly padded boxes. The metal bends easily, and sealed collectors rightfully get annoyed. One reviewer showed photos of a crushed corner that ruined the display value.

**Zero-hit frustration is real.** With two packs, you're rolling the dice and the house usually wins. If you're buying for pulls, expect disappointment roughly 60-70% of the time based on typical pull rates for this set.

**No promo card.** Unlike some other Pokémon tins that include an exclusive promo, these don't come with one. You're paying for the tin and two packs — nothing more.
