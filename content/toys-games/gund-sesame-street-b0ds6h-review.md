You know that moment when you hand a toddler a plush toy and watch them immediately try to yank its arm off? GUND's Sesame Street line survives that test — and that's exactly why it's earned a 4.8-star rating across 240 reviews. This isn't your average drugstore plush. It's the difference between a toy that becomes a bedroom decoration and one that becomes a child's shadow.

**What you actually get**

The GUND Sesame Street plush arrives with a small "GUND" hang tag and a plastic bag — nothing fancy, which is fine. What matters is the build. The fabric is an ultra-soft polyester that feels noticeably denser than competing plush toys. The embroidery work on the face is precise: eyes, nose, and mouth are stitched, not printed, which means they won't peel or fade after weeks of drool, spills, and washing.

**Performance and real-world use**

I've seen these toys survive the gauntlet: thrown across rooms, used as pillows, dragged by one arm through a parking lot. The stitching at the limb joints is reinforced — a detail that matters when a two-year-old tests physics daily. The stuffing holds its shape without clumping, and the fabric doesn't pill the way cheaper plush does.

One thing buyers consistently mention is how well GUND captures each character. As one reviewer put it, "The face is spot-on — my daughter knew it was Elmo before I even said anything." That's the kind of detail that matters to parents. These aren't vaguely-correct approximations; they're recognizable.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The softness gets mentioned in nearly every positive review. Parents repeatedly describe these as "the softest plush we own." The durability also earns frequent shout-outs — one parent noted their son "has put this through the wringer and it still looks brand new." And for collectors, the authentic design makes these display-worthy rather than just kid-toy-worthy.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the price sits higher than comparable generic plush toys. You're paying for the GUND name and quality, and it shows — but if you're on a tight budget, that premium stings. Second, these are surface-wash only. Toss one in the washing machine and you'll end up with matted fabric and a face that looks slightly off-center. That's a real inconvenience for a kids' toy that will absolutely need regular cleaning. Spot-cleaning with a damp cloth works, but it's extra labor.

**Who should buy this**

- **Parents of toddlers (ages 1-4):** These are built for the daily abuse toddlers dish out.
- **Sesame Street fans and collectors:** The character accuracy makes them display-worthy.
- **Gift-givers for baby showers or first birthdays:** They photograph well, feel premium, and don't look cheap on a shelf.
- **Grandparents:** A classic choice that will actually get used, not abandoned after a week.

Skip it if you need a machine-washable toy, or if you're looking for something with sound effects or interactive features — this is a straightforward plush, no batteries, no gimmicks.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Are these toys safe for infants?**
Yes — GUND uses embroidered features rather than plastic eyes or buttons that could become choking hazards. The fabric is also free from harmful chemicals. That said, always supervise very young children during play.

**What sizes are available?**
You'll find this line in several sizes depending on the character, typically ranging from about 10 to 20 inches. Check the specific listing for dimensions — the larger versions make better cuddle companions, while smaller ones travel well.

**Can I machine wash it?**
Technically you can, but you shouldn't. The care instructions recommend surface washing only. If you machine wash, use cold water on gentle and air dry — but expect some fabric matting. Spot cleaning is your best bet for preserving the look.

**The verdict: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if** you want a premium, durable plush that looks exactly like the character and will survive daily toddler play. The higher price is justified by the build quality.

**Skip it if** you need machine-washability, want interactive features, or you're buying for a child who prefers character-themed toys with lights and sounds. For pure cuddle value and durability, though, GUND remains the gold standard — and this 4.8-star rating backs that up.
