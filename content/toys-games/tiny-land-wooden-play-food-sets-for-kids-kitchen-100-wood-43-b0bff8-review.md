Most wooden play food sets are either overpriced junk or so lightweight they slide off the play kitchen counter the moment a toddler breathes near them. The set's real-world durability checks out against the 4.8-star rating across 240 reviews. **Who should buy this?** If you have a toddler or preschooler (18 months to 5 years) who has a play kitchen, this is the set that turns a static playset into an actual cooking experience.
