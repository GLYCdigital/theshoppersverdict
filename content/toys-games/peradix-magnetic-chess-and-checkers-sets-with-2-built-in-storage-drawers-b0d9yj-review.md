**Opening Hook: The travel chess board that actually solves the “lost pawn” problem**

Every chess set eventually loses a piece. It’s practically a law of physics. The Peradix Magnetic Chess and Checkers Set sidesteps that entirely with two sliding storage drawers built right into the board frame — a genuinely useful design choice, not a gimmick. With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, this is one of the better-reviewed dual-game travel sets on Amazon right now. Here’s the breakdown after digging through real buyer feedback and the specs.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the foldable 15-inch board (opens to full regulation size), 32 chess pieces, 24 checkers, and — critically — the two built-in drawers. The drawers slide out from the board’s side edges and are segmented, so chess pieces and checkers stay separated. The board folds in half via a central hinge, and the pieces stay in the drawers during transport. No separate pouch, no loose pieces rattling around. That’s the core value proposition, and it works.

**Build Quality & Real-World Performance**

The board surface is a smooth, printed material with clearly delineated squares. The chess pieces are standard Staunton-style with magnetic bases. Here’s the key spec: the magnets are rated as “strong” and multiple verified reviews confirm the board can be held vertically without pieces sliding. One buyer wrote: *“Tilted it 90 degrees to test — not a single piece moved. My 7-year-old can play in the car without losing pieces in the seat crack.”* That’s the use case this set nails.

The checkers are flat discs with the same magnetic base, and they stack cleanly. The drawers have a small lip that keeps them from sliding out accidentally during play, though they do require a firm push to seat fully. The hinge is solid metal, not plastic, which matters for longevity.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

- **The storage drawers** (mentioned in nearly every positive review). “Finally, a set where my kids don’t lose the knight before the first game ends.”
- **Magnet strength** — consistent across all feedback. Pieces hold firm, even on bumpy car rides.
- **The 2-in-1 value** — both chess and checkers playable on the same board, useful for families with mixed-age kids.

**Where It Falls Short**

The magnetic pull is strong — almost *too* strong for some players. A few reviewers noted that moving pieces quickly can cause them to “snap” to adjacent squares if you’re not lifting straight up. It’s a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, but tournament-speed players will notice it.

The board surface is printed vinyl over a rigid backing, not wood. It looks fine and plays fine, but if you’re expecting a heirloom-quality wooden set, this isn’t it. It’s a durable travel tool, not a display piece. Also, the drawers hold pieces snugly, but if you overstuff them, they can jam — keep the pieces organized and you’re fine.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Parents of kids 6-12** who want a game that survives car trips, camping, and picnic tables.
- **Commuter players** who want a board that fits in a backpack and doesn’t require a separate piece bag.
- **Checkers fans** who want a single game that covers both classics without buying two sets.

Skip it if you’re a chess purist who wants a wood board with weighted pieces for serious play at home. This is purpose-built for portability and durability, not aesthetic indulgence.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can you play on it while it’s folded?**  
No — the hinge creates a gap in the center. You must open it fully. That’s standard for foldable boards.

**Q: Are the pieces standard tournament size?**  
The king is about 2.75 inches tall, slightly smaller than a full-size tournament set (typically 3.5+ inches). Fine for casual play, not for formal matches.

**Q: Do the drawers hold all pieces with the board folded?**  
Yes. That’s the design intent. Both drawers close fully and the board folds flat with everything inside.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a rugged, magnetic, storage-integrated chess/checkers combo that survives real-world abuse. The drawer design is the standout feature — it eliminates the #1 problem with travel chess sets. **Skip it if** you need a premium wood board for serious play, or if you hate any magnetic “stickiness” when moving pieces.

For a 2-in-1 travel set under the $40 mark (check current pricing), this is the best storage solution I’ve seen in this category. The 4.7 rating is earned.
