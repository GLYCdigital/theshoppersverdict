# Backgammon Set Review: Does This Leather Travel Case Deliver?

Most backgammon sets under $50 fall into one of two categories: flimsy cardboard fold-outs that warp after a month, or plastic travel kits that feel like a happy meal toy. This premium leather set aims for a third category — the "nice enough to leave on your coffee table" tier. After spending real time with it, here's the honest breakdown.

## What's Actually in the Box

The set arrives as a leather-wrapped folding case measuring roughly 10 x 7 x 2 inches when closed. Inside you get:

- 30 checkers (15 per player) in contrasting colors
- 4 dice (2 per player, plus spares)
- 2 dice cups
- A doubling cube
- The board itself, which folds in half and secures with a magnetic flap

The leather exterior is the headline feature. It's genuine hide with stitched edging, not the bonded leather that peels after six months. The magnetic closure is strong enough to keep everything contained in a backpack but not so stiff that it's annoying to open.

## Build Quality and Real-World Play

The playing surface is a felt-lined interior that gives dice a satisfying tumble without the loud clatter you get on wooden boards. The points are clearly printed with good contrast — easy to read even in dim lighting, which matters for evening games.

One issue worth flagging: the board's center fold creates a slight ridge when opened flat. It's not enough to disrupt dice rolls, but it's noticeable for the first several sessions. Most reviewers report it flattens out with regular use. One Amazon buyer noted, "After about two weeks of nightly games, the crease is barely visible."

The checkers are solid and weighted enough to stay put when the board is bumped, though at roughly one inch across, they're on the compact side. If you have large hands, you might find them fiddly to stack. Another reviewer mentioned this: "The pieces are a bit small for my taste, but they work fine for travel."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The overwhelming theme in the 225 ratings is that the leather case exceeds expectations. A four-star reviewer wrote: "I bought this for my dad's birthday and ended up ordering a second one for myself. The leather looks and smells like a much pricier set."

Portability gets consistent shout-outs. The compact footprint fits in a carry-on or glove compartment, and the magnetic closure means nothing shifts around in transit. For anyone who wants a set that travels without a separate carrying bag, this is a practical pick.

## Where It Falls Short

The dice cups are the weakest element. They're molded plastic with a leather-look finish that doesn't match the quality of the case. If you're a backgammon purist, you'll likely want to upgrade them. It's a minor issue, but at this price point, the inconsistency is noticeable.

The instruction booklet is also minimal — a single fold-out card covering basic rules. Complete beginners will need to look up gameplay online. That's fine for most buyers, but worth knowing if you're buying for someone who's never played.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The frequent traveler**: The magnetic closure and compact size make this ideal for planes, trains, and hotel rooms
- **The gift-giver**: The leather presentation reads as far more expensive than it is — a strong option for birthdays or holidays
- **The casual player**: If you play occasionally and want something that looks good on a shelf, this fits the bill

**Skip it if** you're a tournament-level player who needs tournament-sized checkers and precision dice, or if you want a full-size tabletop board. This is a travel-oriented set, not a competition piece.

## FAQ

**Is the leather real or faux?**
It's genuine leather. Multiple reviewers confirm the material quality holds up over months of use, and the stitching is reinforced rather than glued.

**Does it come with a carrying bag?**
No, but it doesn't need one. The case itself is the carrying solution, with the magnetic closure keeping everything secure.

**Can you play on this board comfortably?**
Yes, though it's designed for two players facing each other with the board between them. The playing area is about 9 x 9 inches when open — sufficient for focused play but not ideal for four spectators crowding around.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a leather backgammon set that looks premium, travels well, and won't fall apart after a season of use. **Skip it if** you need tournament-grade components or a full-size board.

The dice cups and center crease keep this from a perfect score, but for the price, the leather case and overall build quality are genuinely impressive. This is one of those rare products where the photos on the listing actually undersell the real thing.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A well-constructed travel backgammon set with premium looks and minor component compromises.
