## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and the JOYIN coil has honest limitations.

**The tangling problem is real.** If a child twists the coil aggressively or folds it in half repeatedly, it can develop permanent kinks that ruin the walking action. This isn't a JOYIN-specific flaw — it's inherent to coil spring toys — but it's worth knowing before purchase. One reviewer noted, "Two of the ten we ordered got tangled beyond repair within a day."

**No storage solution.** These come loose in a simple package. There's no pouch or container, so if you're buying these for a classroom or party, you'll need your own storage system or they'll scatter across your house.

**Not for the under-3 crowd.** The small size and plastic construction present a choking hazard for toddlers. The product page doesn't make this warning prominent enough for my taste.
