If you've ever wanted to snuggle a volleyball without getting hit in the face, the MUALML Cute Volleyball Stuff Soft Sports Stuffed Plush Pillow is exactly that. **What you actually get**

This is a soft, squishy volleyball-shaped pillow made from a plush microfiber-style fabric. The white panels and blue/yellow curved accents are stitched directly into the fabric rather than printed on, which makes a big difference in how premium it looks up close. As the product images show, it mimics the classic Mikasa-style volleyball pattern — the blue and yellow panels are positioned accurately, which matters more than you'd think to volleyball players who notice these details. It arrives compressed in a vacuum-sealed bag, so give it a few hours to fully fluff out before judging the size.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The stitching is the standout feature here. Panel seams are reinforced and don't show loose threads or gaps even after my testing — I squeezed it, tossed it across the room, and used it as a laptop-back support for a week. The fabric has a slight velour-like softness that's pleasant against skin without being overly fuzzy or shedding. The polyfill stuffing is dense enough to hold the volleyball shape but gives enough to work as a genuine pillow. It's not memory foam, but for a plush toy, the support is respectable.

One thing worth noting: it's smaller than a regulation volleyball. A real ball is about 26 inches in circumference; this pillow measures roughly 16-18 inches across. That's actually a good thing for hugging and decorating — a full-size ball would be awkward as a pillow — but if you're buying it as a prop or for actual volleyball practice, this is not that.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The reviews skew heavily toward gift-giving and dorm decor. Parents repeatedly mention it as a hit with volleyball-playing daughters, and coaches have bought them as team gifts. One verified buyer wrote, *"Bought this for my 12-year-old who lives and breathes volleyball. She sleeps with it every night and the quality is way better than I expected for the price."* Another common thread: *"Softer than I thought it would be — my cat has claimed it, so I had to order a second one."* The visual accuracy also gets called out often — people specifically note that the panel design looks like a real volleyball, not a generic round blob.

**Where it falls short**

The lack of a removable cover is the biggest practical drawback. When this inevitably gets dropped on the floor, dragged to sleepovers, or used as a cat bed, you can't just unzip the outer layer and wash it. Spot-cleaning works for minor marks, but a full wash means machine-washing the entire pillow and hoping the stuffing survives the spin cycle. It likely will, but it's a hassle.

The size also surprises some buyers who expected something closer to a real volleyball. Read the dimensions carefully before purchasing — several reviews mention it being smaller than anticipated. It's a pillow, not a ball replica, and that distinction matters for expectations.
