**Q: Do they have a strong smell?**
A: Some batches have a slight chemical odor straight from the package. Air them out for a day before use — most reviewers say the smell dissipates quickly. If you're sensitive to synthetic scents, this might bother you initially.

**Q: Can these be washed?**
