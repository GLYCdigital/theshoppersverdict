Most RC cars in this price range make one promise and break it. The MaxTronic Amphibious RC Car makes three — it drives on land, floats on water, and flips 360 degrees — and somehow delivers on all of them. After 225 Amazon ratings averaging 4.5 stars, the consensus is clear: this is the rare budget toy that actually does what the box claims.

## What You Get

The box includes the car itself, a 2.4GHz remote control, a USB charging cable, and the manual. The car measures roughly 12 inches long and weighs about 2.5 pounds — substantial enough to feel solid, light enough for kids to carry. The chassis uses a sealed ABS shell with rubber gaskets around the electronics bay. That's the critical detail for the water feature: it's not just "water-resistant," it's actually sealed.

## Real-World Performance

On land, the MaxTronic hits about 8-10 mph on flat concrete. That's not hobby-grade speed, but for backyard bashing and driveway racing, it's plenty. The 390 motor provides decent torque for grass and gravel, though steep inclines will slow it noticeably. Steering response is tight at low speeds, slightly mushy at full throttle — typical for this class.

The amphibious claim is where this car separates itself. Drop it in a pool, lake, or puddle, and it keeps moving. The wheels act as paddle wheels, and the sealed electronics bay keeps the motor and battery dry. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: *"Took it to the lake with my kids and it didn't skip a beat. Drove right off the dock into the water."* Another noted, *"Works great in the tub for my 6-year-old. He's run it through puddles, mud, and the pool without any issues."*

The 360° flip feature is the headline act. One button on the remote triggers it, and the car uses its momentum to complete a full rotation mid-air. It works on flat ground, off ramps, and even in water. Is it a necessary feature? No. Is it fun? Absolutely. The mechanism is robust enough to survive repeated use — a common failure point in cheaper flip-capable RC cars.

## Battery Life and Charging

The dual 18650 battery setup delivers 25-30 minutes of continuous runtime. That's solid for this category, where 15 minutes is common. Charging takes about 2.5 hours via the included USB cable. The battery is replaceable, which extends the car's lifespan significantly — you're not throwing away the whole toy when the battery eventually degrades.

## Where It Falls Short

The remote eats 2 AA batteries that aren't included. It's a small annoyance, but if you're gifting this, grab AAs at the same time or the kid will be watching the car sit idle on day one.

Speed enthusiasts will be disappointed. This is not a fast RC car. If you're looking for 30+ mph performance, look elsewhere — this is a fun toy, not a racing machine.

The water performance is good, not great. It moves through water at maybe 2-3 mph, which is enough to be entertaining but not thrilling. Also, after water use, you should dry the car thoroughly before storing to prevent corrosion on the exposed screws and axles.

## Who Should Buy This

**Get it for:** Kids aged 6-12 who want a toy that works on land and water. Parents who want a durable RC car that survives the inevitable puddle encounter. Anyone who wants a genuinely fun backyard toy without spending $100+.

**Skip it if:** You're a serious RC hobbyist looking for speed. You want a waterproof car for actual underwater use — this floats, it does not submerge. You're buying for a child under 5 who might put small parts in their mouth.

## FAQ

**Q: Can it really go in saltwater?**
A: Technically yes, but you shouldn't. Saltwater will corrode the metal parts and seals quickly. Stick to fresh water.

**Q: How long does the battery last?**
A: Expect 25-30 minutes of mixed driving. Water driving drains it slightly faster. Full recharge takes about 2.5 hours.

**Q: Is it suitable for indoor use?**
A: Yes, but it's better outdoors. The flip feature needs space, and the wheels are designed for traction on rougher surfaces — they'll mark hardwood floors.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a versatile, durable RC car that works on land and water without breaking the bank. **Skip it if** speed is your priority or you're looking for something more serious.

The MaxTronic Amphibious RC Car earns its 4.6/5 score. It's not perfect — the AA-powered remote and moderate speed hold it back — but for the price, it delivers exceptional value and genuine fun. The amphibious capability isn't a gimmick; it's a well-executed feature that makes this car stand out in a crowded field. For parents who've watched cheaper RC cars die after one puddle encounter, this is the reliable choice.
