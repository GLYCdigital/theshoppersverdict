If you're shopping for a graduation gift that won't end up in a donation bin by August, this Squishmallow has a serious advantage: it's a genuinely good plush that just happens to wear a cap and gown. The 12-inch size hits the sweet spot — big enough to feel like a real gift, small enough to fit on a dorm bed or office chair. As shown in the product image, the graduate design features the classic Squishmallow face with a removable black cap and gown, which is smarter than it sounds.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the plush itself with the graduation gown already dressed, plus the cap. The gown slips off easily — a detail that matters more than you'd think. Once the ceremony passes, the recipient can remove the graduation gear and have a standard Squishmallow that doesn't scream "class of 2026" forever. That's the kind of design foresight that justifies the price.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

Squishmallows has built its reputation on that distinctive marshmallow-soft polyester fill, and this one delivers the same feel. It's squishy but holds its shape — it won't flatten into a pancake after a week of hugs. The stitching is clean, the embroidered face is well-executed, and the fabric has that slightly cool, silky touch that makes these so addictive to hold.

The gown is the weak point. It's made from a thinner polyester that feels noticeably less premium than the plush body. It's not bad — it looks fine on display — but if you run your hand over it, you'll feel the difference immediately. The cap sits securely but can shift if the plush is tossed around. One buyer noted, "The gown material is a bit scratchy compared to the body, but my daughter didn't care — she just wanted the graduation Squishmallow."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.8-star average across 240 ratings reflects genuine enthusiasm. Recurring themes in customer feedback:

- **Giftability**: Multiple reviewers mention buying it for kindergarten graduations, high school seniors, and even nursing school grads. It reads as thoughtful, not generic.
- **Size satisfaction**: "Perfect size — bigger than I expected from the photos," is a common refrain. At 12 inches, it's substantial without being overwhelming.
- **Durability**: Several parents report it surviving repeated machine washes (cold, gentle cycle) without losing softness or shape.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the gown material, there are two honest limitations. First, there's no sound chip — if you want a plush that plays "Pomp and Circumstance," this isn't it. Second, there's no personalization. You can't add a name or graduation year, so it's a generic design. For a milestone gift, some shoppers want that extra touch.

The cap also doesn't fit snugly on all heads — it's designed for display, not active play. If a toddler immediately rips it off, you'll be reattaching it constantly.

## FAQ

**Is the cap and gown removable?**
Yes. Both come off cleanly, leaving a standard Squishmallow. The gown slips over the head, and the cap rests on top.

**Can this be machine washed?**
The plush body can be machine washed on a cold, gentle cycle and air-dried. The gown and cap are better hand-washed to avoid warping the cap's shape.

**Is this a limited edition?**
Squishmallows graduation designs typically release seasonally (spring) and sell out. If you're buying for a spring 2027 graduation, don't wait until May.
