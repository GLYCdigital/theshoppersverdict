Let’s be honest: most 100-piece puzzles are an afterthought purchase. You grab one for a rainy day, the kid finishes it in 20 minutes, and it lives in a drawer forever. The Buffalo Games Squishmallows Buddies puzzle is different — not because it’s harder, but because it’s *better made* and genuinely enjoyable to complete. Here’s the breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains 100 jigsaw pieces that assemble into a **finished puzzle measuring 8x8 inches**. That’s a full foot of colorful Squishmallows characters — Cam the Cat, Fifi the Fox, and friends — rendered in the brand’s signature soft, chunky art style. The pieces are cut from **recycled puzzle board**, a nice touch for eco-conscious buyers, and they have a matte finish that reduces glare under bright lights. The box itself is compact and sturdy, easy to store or toss in a bag for travel.

**Build Quality & Real-World Performance**

The standout feature here is the **piece thickness**. Buffalo Games uses a denser board than most budget puzzle brands, and it shows. Pieces snap together with a satisfying click and hold firm — you can lift the completed puzzle by the edges without it falling apart. That’s a big deal if you’ve ever wrestled with flimsy pieces that bend or separate mid-assembly.

The print quality is sharp, with vibrant colors that pop. The Squishmallows characters are distinct enough that even younger kids can identify where pieces go without constant adult intervention. The cut pattern is varied — no two pieces are identical shapes — which adds a slight challenge without becoming frustrating.
