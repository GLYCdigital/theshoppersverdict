**The Myth of the 24/7 Pool Party vs. Reality**

Every pool owner has that fantasy: stringing up a volleyball net, flipping a switch, and playing until midnight under glowing lights. The Myodal Pool Volleyball Net Set with Solar Powered LED Lights promises exactly that. At 3.9 stars from 195 ratings, it's not a home run—but it's not a strikeout either. Here's what you're actually getting.

**What's in the Box**

The kit arrives with two adjustable poles (extending to fit both 15ft and 32ft pools), the net itself, a volleyball, a manual pump, ground stakes, and a carry bag. The solar panels sit on top of each pole, charging small LED strips integrated into the net's top edge. Assembly takes roughly 15-20 minutes—the poles screw together, and the net clips on. No tools required.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The net is regulation-ish for casual play: 32 feet wide when fully extended, with a 3-foot drop that sits properly above water level. The poles are aluminum with a powder-coated finish, which handles chlorine exposure better than bare metal. The included ball is inflatable and decent, though serious players will swap it out.

Now the LEDs. Here's the honest truth: they're accent lighting, not floodlights. In full darkness, you'll see the net's outline and maybe a foot of water on either side. If you're expecting to run a full night game with clear ball visibility, you'll be disappointed. But for ambiance—stringing the net up for a dusk swim and keeping the court visible enough to avoid face-planting into the net—they work fine. Solar charging takes 4-6 hours of direct sun for roughly 3-4 hours of LED use. Cloudy days cut that significantly.

The biggest structural complaint from buyers centers on the pole bases. The kit includes both ground stakes and weighted base plates, but on above-ground pools with curved walls, the poles can wobble under aggressive play. One reviewer noted, "The poles are sturdy but the base doesn't grip above-ground pool edges well—my kids knocked it loose twice during a single game." This is a design limitation, not a defect. In-ground pools with flat decks have far fewer issues.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The solar lighting gets genuine love. As one verified purchaser wrote, "I was skeptical about the lights, but they actually work. No wires near the pool is a huge safety win with kids around." The versatility of fitting both 15ft and 32ft pools also earns repeated mention—it's rare to find one kit that handles both sizes without adapters. And the carrying bag is sturdier than most in this price range, surviving multiple seasons of storage.

**Where It Falls Short**

Net tension is the recurring complaint. The net sags noticeably after a few hours of play, requiring you to re-tighten the ratchet straps. It's a 30-second fix, but it interrupts momentum. The ball pump included is cheap—expect it to fail after a few uses. And if you have a pool wider than 32 feet, this simply won't work. The poles max out at 32 feet, and overextending them compromises stability.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for the casual backyard pool owner who wants to add volleyball without a permanent installation. It's perfect for families with kids aged 8-14 who play for fun, not competition. It also suits anyone who wants evening pool ambience without running electrical lines near water. If you're a competitive player who expects tournament-grade tension and visibility, skip it.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do the solar lights work on cloudy days?**
A: Partially. They'll still charge, but expect only 1-2 hours of dimmer light instead of the full 3-4 hours. On overcast stretches, treat the LEDs as decorative.

**Q: Will this work on a 24-foot round above-ground pool?**
A: Yes, the adjustable poles compress down to fit 15-foot pools, so a 24-foot round is well within range. Just ensure the pole bases sit flat on your pool's top rail.

**Q: Can I leave it up all summer?**
A: You can, but the net fabric will fade faster, and the solar panels collect debris. Most buyers take it down weekly, which takes about 5 minutes.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a versatile, cord-free pool volleyball setup for casual family fun and don't expect floodlight-level illumination. The solar LEDs are a genuine safety bonus, and the dual-size fit adds real value.

**Skip it if** you're a serious player, have a pool wider than 32 feet, or need bright lights for competitive night games. The tension issues and decorative LED output will frustrate you.

At 3.7/5, the Myodal set delivers solid mid-range performance for its price point. It's not premium gear, but it's honest gear—and for most backyard pools, that's enough.
