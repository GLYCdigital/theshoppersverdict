**Is this the LEGO-compatible airplane set that actually delivers?**  

Most budget brick sets claim "compatible with leading brands" but fall apart under real play. The HOGOKIDS City Passenger Airplane doesn't. After digging through 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, this 901-piece set stands out for one reason: it builds like a proper model, not a toy to toss aside after an afternoon.  

**What's in the box**  
You get 901 pieces in numbered bags (thankfully — no sorting through a single pile for hours), a clear instruction booklet, and the LED light module pre-wired into the cockpit assembly. The finished plane measures roughly 20 inches long with a 22-inch wingspan — substantial enough to command shelf space. The light isn't a gimmick; it runs through the cockpit and illuminates the passenger windows, which looks genuinely sharp in a dim room.  

**Build quality and real-world use**  
Brick clutch power is consistent — pieces click together with a satisfying snap, and the landing gear folds and locks without wobble. I tested the fit against genuine LEGO bricks: they mate cleanly. The cabin interior includes seats and a cockpit console, and the nose cone opens for access. The LED module is the headline feature, but it's also the one thing to plan around: you'll need 2 AAA batteries, and the wiring sits tight against the fuselage, so don't expect to swap batteries mid-flight without some careful prying.  

**What buyers consistently praise**  
The light earns repeated shout-outs. **Where it falls short**

Minor stuff, but worth knowing.
