The Switch 2 is bigger, heavier, and more expensive than its predecessor. That means your old carrying case probably doesn't fit — and the one you're eyeing needs to earn its place in your bag. The MoKo carrying case for the 2025 Switch 2 has 240 Amazon ratings averaging 4.8 stars, which is impressive for a third-party accessory this early in the console's life. After handling one, here's the real story.

**What you actually get**

The package includes the hard-shell case itself — nothing else, no screen protector or cleaning cloth. That's fine, because the case is the product. It measures roughly 11 x 5 x 1.5 inches and weighs under half a pound. The exterior is a textured EVA hard shell with a smooth, water-resistant finish. Inside, you get a molded foam cradle shaped specifically for the Switch 2, a soft microfiber lining over the screen area, 10 game card slots, and a mesh pocket on the top flap for cables, earbuds, or a small power bank.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The molded interior is the standout feature. The Switch 2 clicks into place with an audible, satisfying snap — no velcro straps, no elastic bands, just a precision fit that holds the console firmly. I shook the case aggressively (the "will my kid's backpack survive this" test) and the console didn't budge. The hard shell flexes slightly under pressure but springs back without cracking, which is exactly what you want from EVA material.

I dropped the case from about four feet onto concrete. The corner took the impact, and the Switch 2 inside was completely unharmed. The zipper is smooth but not buttery — it's a standard coil zipper, not a waterproof YKK, so don't expect it to survive a dunk in a pool. It'll handle rain and splashes fine, but this isn't a submersion case.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The consensus across reviews is the fit. One buyer put it plainly: *"Finally a case that actually fits the Switch 2 properly. My old Switch case was too narrow and the new one slid around. This one locks it in place."* Another echoed the durability angle: *"The hard shell is no joke. My son's backpack gets thrown around daily and the console is flawless every time."* Multiple reviewers also called out the game card storage, noting that 10 slots covers a physical library for most people without adding bulk.

**Where it falls short**

The most common complaint is the lack of a dedicated slot for the Joy-Con wrist straps. They're too bulky for the mesh pocket to be convenient, and they rattle around. If you're someone who actually uses the straps for motion games, you'll need to find another home for them.

The zipper pull is also small — about a half-inch tab. Adults with larger hands or anyone wearing gloves will find it fiddly. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's a minor annoyance on a case that otherwise nails the details.

**Who should buy this**

This case is for the person who wants one bag for everything: console, 10 games, and a charging cable. It's ideal for commuters, parents packing for road trips, and anyone who's ever watched a Switch bounce around in a backpack unprotected. It's also a smart buy for new Switch 2 owners who skipped the official Nintendo case (which costs significantly more and holds fewer games).

Skip it if you need to carry the dock, a pro controller, or the charging grip — this case is console-only. And if you're a collector who wants a case with character, the two available colors (black and a muted gray) are purely functional.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit the Switch 2 with a grip case or thumbstick covers on?**
A: No. This is designed for the bare console with Joy-Con attached. Adding any aftermarket grip or thicker thumbsticks will prevent the lid from closing properly. Use it stock or not at all.

**Q: Does the case have room for the AC adapter?**
A: The mesh pocket fits the official USB-C cable, but the brick adapter is too thick. It'll stretch the pocket and bulge the case. Plan to carry the adapter separately.

**Q: Is the exterior scratch-resistant?**
A: The EVA shell resists minor scuffs, but it's not armor. Sharp objects like keys will leave marks on the textured finish. It'll still protect the console inside, but the case itself may show wear.

**The Verdict**

The MoKo carrying case for the Switch 2 delivers exactly what it promises: a precise, protective home for your new console at a price that undercuts Nintendo's official option. The build quality is solid, the fit is perfect, and the game storage covers most libraries. The wrist strap issue and small zipper pull are minor quibbles on an otherwise excellent product.

**Buy it if** you want a durable, everyday case that fits the Switch 2 like a glove and stores your games in one slim package.

**Skip it if** you need to carry accessories beyond the console and a few games, or if you use Joy-Con straps regularly.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The best third-party Switch 2 case I've tested so far, with only minor ergonomic quibbles holding it back from a perfect score.
