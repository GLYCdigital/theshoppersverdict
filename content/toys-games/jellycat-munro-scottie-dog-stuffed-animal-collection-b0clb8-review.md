## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**Size vs. price.** At roughly 12 inches, this is not a big plush. For the premium price, some buyers felt it should be larger. *"I was surprised at how small it was when it arrived,"* one reviewer admitted. *"Still cute, but I expected bigger."* Check the dimensions. If you want a lap-sized cuddle buddy, this isn't it.

**Not for rough play.** This is a quality plush, but it's not indestructible. Toddlers who throw, drag, and aggressively hug their toys may eventually cause wear. The fur stays put, but the ears can get bent out of shape if crushed repeatedly.

**The price.** There's no way around it: you're paying a premium for the Jellycat name. You can find cheaper Scottie plush toys for half the price. What you're getting for the extra money is superior materials, better construction, and that signature softness. Whether that's worth it is a personal call.
