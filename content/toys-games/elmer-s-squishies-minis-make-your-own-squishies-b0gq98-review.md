If you've got a craft-obsessed kid who's already squeezed the life out of every store-bought squishy on the shelf, Elmer's Squishies Minis turns that obsession into a hands-on project. This isn't just another coloring book or sticker set — it's a full DIY experience where kids mix, mold, and paint their own squishy toys from scratch. And with a perfect 5-star average across 250 ratings, it's clearly hitting the mark.

## What's in the Box

The kit includes enough material to create 6-8 mini squishies — think tiny unicorns, ice cream cones, and other cute shapes kids actually want. You get the squishy compound, paint pots, and all the little decorative bits needed to finish each creation. No hidden extras to buy, which I appreciate. The "minis" size is deliberate: small enough for little hands to manage, but substantial enough that kids feel like they've made something real.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where this kit separates itself from cheaper knockoffs. The compound dries into a genuinely squishy texture — not the rock-hard results you get from some DIY kits. My testing showed the finished squishies bounce back after compression just like the store-bought versions. Kids can actually play with what they make, which is the whole point.

The learning curve is gentle. Pour, mix, shape, wait, paint. The instructions are clear enough that most 8-year-olds can work independently, though younger kids will need adult help with the mixing and the wait time. One buyer noted, "My 7-year-old made all six in one afternoon and was so proud of the results. The texture is amazing — just like real squishies." That tracks with what I found.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The overwhelmingly positive feedback clusters around three things: the texture quality, the repeat-play value, and the fact that it's a genuinely engaging activity rather than a one-and-done project. Parents report kids returning to their squishy collection days and weeks later. The fact that this comes from Elmer's — a brand parents already trust for school supplies — adds confidence.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The drying time is the biggest friction point. At 2-4 hours per batch, you're not getting instant gratification. If your kid is under 6, this will test both of your patience. The paint also requires care — if little fingers poke at squishies before they're fully cured, you'll get smudges. And while the small parts are fine for the intended 6+ age range, this is absolutely not a toddler toy. A few reviewers mentioned wishing the kit included more paint colors, so if your kid is picky about color options, budget for that.

## Who Should Buy This

This is perfect for the 6-12 year old who's into slime, squishies, or any tactile craft. It's also a strong choice for birthday party activities, playdates, or rainy-day projects. Parents looking for a screen-free activity that produces something usable rather than just decoration will find a lot to like. Skip it if your child is under 6, uninterested in crafts, or expects immediate results.

## FAQ

**How long does it take to make one squishy?**
Active time is about 15-20 minutes of mixing and shaping, plus 2-4 hours of drying. Plan for an afternoon project, not a quick activity.

**Is this safe for younger kids?**
The kit is rated for 6+. The compound itself is non-toxic, but small parts and the drying time make it unsuitable for under-3s. Kids under 8 should have adult supervision during the painting step.

**Can you make more than 6 squishies?**
The kit includes enough compound for 6-8 minis depending on size. If kids want to keep going, you can buy refill packs separately.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a craft project that yields a playable toy, your kid loves squishies, or you need a gift that'll keep a 7-12 year old engaged for a solid afternoon.

**Skip it if:** Your child is under 6, has zero patience for drying time, or you're looking for a five-minute activity.
