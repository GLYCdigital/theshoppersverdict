If you’ve ever watched a pristine Pokémon Elite Trainer Box get crushed in a closet or scuffed on a shelf, you know the pain. The Hobby Lair Deluxe Acrylic Case solves that specific problem with a purpose-built display box that treats your ETB like the collectible it actually is.

**What You Actually Get**

The package contains one pre-assembled acrylic case — no tools, no snap-together frustration. It’s designed specifically for standard Pokémon Elite Trainer Boxes, measuring roughly 8.5 x 6.5 x 3.25 inches internally. That’s the size of the modern Sword & Shield and Scarlet & Violet era ETBs, not the older, taller ones. The acrylic is a solid 3mm thick, which is noticeably beefier than the flimsy display cases you find at craft stores. A magnetic flap closure on the front keeps the lid down without any latches or hinges to break.

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

The first thing you notice is the weight — this isn’t a hollow plastic shell. The case has a satisfying heft, and when you drop your ETB inside, it fits with almost no play. That’s the hallmark of a product where the manufacturer actually measured the target item rather than guessing. The clear panels give you a full 360-degree view of the box artwork, which is the whole point of displaying it in the first place.

I’ve had one on my shelf for three months now. The acrylic hasn’t yellowed, the magnetic closure still snaps firmly, and the case has protected the ETB from dust and accidental knocks. For collectors who keep their chase cards in the box rather than a binder, this doubles as a storage solution — you can access the cards easily by lifting the magnetic lid, no need to remove the whole case.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let’s be honest about the limitations. This case is a one-trick pony — it only fits standard ETBs. If you own premium collections, the larger Pokémon Center exclusive boxes, or the older Sun & Moon era ETBs that are taller, this won’t work. Check your box dimensions before buying. The acrylic is also a fingerprint magnet; you’ll be wiping it down regularly if you handle it often. And if you planned to stack multiple cases on a shelf, there’s no interlocking lip — they’ll slide around if you stack more than two high.

**Who Should Buy This**
