## The 20-inch statement piece your party actually needs

Most party balloons are an afterthought — something you blow up in five minutes and forget. The 20 Inch Bobo Balloons are different. These oversized clear bubbles (roughly the size of a beach ball when fully inflated) demand attention. They're the kind of balloon that makes guests ask, "Wait, where did you get those?"

As shown in the product image, these aren't your standard latex balloons. They're thick, transparent PVC-style bubbles that you fill with whatever fits your theme — confetti, curled ribbon, faux flowers, or even small LED lights for evening events. The visual effect is closer to a floating terrarium than a party decoration.

## What's in the package

You get 10 clear balloons, each measuring 20 inches when inflated, plus a roll of ribbon for hanging. That's enough for a full statement cluster above a dessert table or a small balloon garland. The balloons arrive flat and folded, so don't panic when you open the package — they need a moment to relax before filling.

## Build quality and real-world performance

Here's where these earn their keep. The material is noticeably thicker than the dollar-store alternatives. One Amazon reviewer noted, "I accidentally overfilled two of them and they didn't pop — they just got tight and I backed off." That's the kind of durability you want when you're setting up the day before an event.

The 20-inch size means they need a real helium tank or a steady hand with an air pump. Helium gives them that floating effect, but you can also hang them from ceilings or stands — the included ribbon is sturdy enough for that.

## Where they fall short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the clear surface is a fingerprint magnet. If you're filling them with confetti, you'll need to handle them carefully or wear gloves, or you'll have smudges that show up in photos. Second, the neck opening is narrow — getting larger filler items like silk flowers inside takes patience. Several reviewers mentioned this: "Loved the result but filling all 10 took me about an hour."

And while they're thicker than standard balloons, they're still single-use. Once popped, they're trash. If you're looking for reusable decor, this isn't it.

## Who should buy these

**Buy them if:** You're planning a birthday party, baby shower, gender reveal, wedding, or any event where you want Instagram-worthy decor without hiring a professional. They're also great for businesses — a bakery, boutique, or restaurant wanting eye-catching window displays.

**Skip them if:** You need something reusable, you're on a tight timeline (filling takes time), or you're looking for a quick 5-minute decoration solution.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these be inflated with regular air instead of helium?**
A: Yes. They'll hang or sit in clusters rather than float, but they work fine with an air pump. Just keep them anchored or hung.

**Q: How long do they stay inflated?**
A: With helium, expect 12–24 hours of good float. With air, they'll hold for days if not punctured.

**Q: What can I put inside them?**
A: Confetti, curled ribbon, fake flowers, feathers, small LED lights, or even photos. Avoid anything sharp or heavy that could puncture the material.
