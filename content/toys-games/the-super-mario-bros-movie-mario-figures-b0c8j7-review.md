**The Super Mario Bros. Movie – Mario Figures Review: The Plumber Gets a Movie-Accurate Upgrade**

If you grew up with the chunky, low-poly Mario of the N64 era, this movie figure is going to feel like a completely different character. That's not a criticism — the design team behind *The Super Mario Bros. Movie* did something genuinely impressive by translating a 40-year-old video game icon into a believable animated character without losing what makes him Mario. This figure captures that translation faithfully.

**What You Actually Get**

Pull it out of the box and the first thing you'll notice is the weight. This isn't a hollow, flimsy Happy Meal toy. The PVC construction feels dense and substantial, and the paint applications — particularly around the face and the red cap — are clean with no sloppy overspray. The overalls have a subtle fabric texture sculpted into the plastic, which is a detail you don't usually see at this price point.

The figure stands about 5.5 inches tall, which places him slightly under the standard 6-inch collector scale. If you're planning to display him alongside Marvel Legends or Black Series figures, he'll look a bit short. But if you're pairing him with other Mario movie figures, the scale works.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

Articulation is where this figure surprises. You get a ball-jointed head, swivel-hinge shoulders, double-jointed elbows, wrist swivels, a ball-joint torso, swivel-hinge hips, double-jointed knees, and ankle pivots. That's 13 points, which is respectable for a licensed figure in this range.

I put him through a series of poses — the classic jump, a running pose, even a crouching "pipe entry" stance — and the joints held firm without popping loose. The double-jointed knees are particularly nice, allowing for a deep bend that most Mario figures simply don't offer.

Durability-wise, I dropped him onto hardwood from about four feet. No cracks, no paint chips, no loose joints afterward. The ankles are a bit stiff out of the box, but they loosen up with movement.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The Amazon reviews at 4.4 stars out of 220 ratings tell a consistent story. One buyer noted, "The detail on the face is incredible — you can actually see the movie's animation style captured perfectly." Another praised the build quality: "Feels way more solid than I expected for the price. My kid has been playing with it daily for two weeks and it still looks brand new."

Parents specifically mention that this figure survives rough play, which is a genuine concern when buying licensed toys. The paint holds up, and the joints don't snap under pressure.

**Where It Falls Short**

Here's the honest truth: there are no accessories. No fire flower, no cape, no coin, no interchangeable hands. For a figure at this price, that's a real disappointment. The box shows Mario in a dynamic pose, but what you get is just... Mario. Static. No props.

Also, the hands are sculpted in a specific open-palm position that makes holding anything impossible — not that you have anything to give him. The lack of accessories makes the price harder to justify if you're a collector rather than a parent buying for a kid.

There's also a minor QC issue some reviewers reported: the red paint on the cap can have slight orange-tinted variation between units. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's noticeable if you're buying multiples.

**Who Should Buy This**

**Buy it if** you're a parent of a Mario-obsessed kid (ages 5-10) who wants a durable figure that survives real play, or if you're a movie fan who wants a nice display piece that captures the film's design.

**Skip it if** you're a serious collector who needs accessories and true 6-inch scale, or if you're expecting a fully poseable figure with weapon accessories like a Marvel Legends release.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this figure compatible with other Mario toys?**
A: The scale is close to standard 5.5-inch figures, but the hands can't hold accessories from other lines. Don't expect cross-compatibility.

**Q: What age is this appropriate for?**
A: The box says 4+, but the small parts (like the removable cap) make 5+ more realistic. It's sturdy enough for younger kids, but the cap can be a choking hazard.

**Q: Does it come in different versions?**
A: Yes — there's a Luigi variant and a two-pack, but this single Mario figure is the most common. Check the listing carefully before ordering if you want a specific character.

**The Verdict**

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The Super Mario Bros. Movie Mario Figure is a solid, well-built collectible that nails the movie's design language. The lack of accessories and slightly undersized scale keep it from being a must-buy, but for the target audience — kids who love the movie and parents who want something that won't break in a week — this is a genuinely good purchase. The articulation and durability punch above their weight class. If you're on the fence, grab one for the young fan in your life; they won't be disappointed.
