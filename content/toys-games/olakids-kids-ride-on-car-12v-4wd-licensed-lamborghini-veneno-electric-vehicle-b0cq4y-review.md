You've seen the real Lamborghini Veneno — the $4 million hypercar with scissor doors that looks like it escaped from a sci-fi set. This OLAKIDS version won't cost you a house payment, but it does bring the same head-turning design down to a 12V package your 3-to-8-year-old can actually drive. With 220 ratings averaging 4.4 stars, it's clearly getting attention. The question is whether it delivers on the hype or just the looks.

## What's Actually in the Box

When the box arrives, you're getting a disassembled ride-on that needs real work. The package includes the main body shell, four wheels with hubcaps, the 12V battery and charger, a parent remote control, and hardware. OLAKIDS also throws in a couple of welcome touches: a USB port and aux input so your kid can rock their playlist, and an LED display on the dashboard.

The licensed Lamborghini details are where this thing earns its keep. The scissor doors are functional, the body lines mirror the real Veneno's aggressive wedge shape, and the LED headlights and taillights actually illuminate. As shown in the product images, this is one of the better-looking licensed ride-ons on the market — most brands just slap a badge on a generic Jeep. This one genuinely looks the part.

## Real-World Performance

The 4WD system is the headline feature, and it's not just marketing. Two 12V motors drive all four wheels, and that makes a real difference on grass and gravel. One verified buyer put it plainly: "My son drives it on our lawn every day, and it doesn't stall like his old single-motor car." The 2.4G parent remote is a solid safety net — you can take full control of steering and braking from up to 30 feet away, which is essential for younger drivers.

Top speed is around 3.7 mph, which feels appropriately quick for a kid's first car without being dangerous. The suspension is basic — spring-based on the rear — so don't expect a Cadillac ride. On bumpy sidewalks, you'll hear the body rattle a bit, but nothing's broken in normal use.

## What Buyers Love and Loathe

The praise centers on three things: the looks, the power, and the remote. Parents consistently call it "the coolest car on the block" and note that the Lamborghini styling makes other ride-ons look toy-like in comparison. Multiple reviewers mention that their kids refuse to get out of it.

The honest critiques are about assembly and durability. One reviewer said, "Took me two hours to put together, and the door on the passenger side still doesn't line up perfectly." The scissor doors are a frequent complaint point — they're cool when they work, but they need careful alignment during installation. The battery is another common gripe; 45–60 minutes of heavy use is realistic, and if you've got siblings fighting over turns, you'll want a spare.

## Who Should Buy This

This is for parents who want their kid to have the coolest ride-on in the neighborhood without spending $500+. It's ideal for kids ages 3–7 who are ready to go beyond a push-car. The 4WD makes it a smart pick if your yard has grass or you live somewhere with gravel paths — that's where cheap 2WD cars die. It's also a great gift for car-obsessed kids who will appreciate the authentic details.

Skip it if you're not handy with tools. Assembly is not a 10-minute job, and the door alignment issues will frustrate you if you're not patient. Also skip if your kid is over 65 pounds — the weight limit is real, and the car will struggle.

## FAQ

**How long does the battery take to charge?**
About 8–10 hours for a full charge, which gives you roughly 45–60 minutes of driving. Plan overnight charging for the next day's play.

**Is the parent remote actually useful?**
Yes, and it's one of the best features. You can steer, brake, and stop the car from up to 30 feet away. It's a lifesaver when your kid is heading toward the street.

**Can my 3-year-old operate it?**
The car has a high-low speed switch, so yes. Put it on low speed (around 2 mph) for younger kids and let the parent remote handle the rest. The included seatbelt adds another layer of safety, though we recommend supervising at all times.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a head-turning, genuinely fun ride-on with real off-road capability and a safety remote. The licensed design and 4WD make it a standout at the price point.

**Skip it if:** You hate assembly, have a kid near the 65-pound limit, or expect showroom durability after a season of outdoor abuse. This is a toy, not a collector's item — but it's a damn good one.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — the looks and performance carry it; the assembly headache and battery life hold it back from perfection.
