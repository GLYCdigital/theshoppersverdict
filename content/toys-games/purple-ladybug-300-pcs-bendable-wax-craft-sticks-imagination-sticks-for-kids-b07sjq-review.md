## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and these have a few quirks worth knowing before you buy.

**Wax flakes.** When you cut the sticks (scissors work fine), the wax coating can chip slightly, especially in a warm room. It's not a mess disaster, but you'll want a tray or table covering if your kid is doing detailed cutting work.

**Glue doesn't stick.** If your project plan involves attaching these to cardboard or paper, standard school glue won't hold. The wax repels it. You'll need hot glue (for older kids) or rely on the twist-to-connect method. This caught several reviewers off guard — one said, "I assumed we could glue them, but they just slide off. Once we figured out twisting, it was fine."

**Shipping bends.** A handful of sticks arrive with a permanent bend from being crammed in the box. A few seconds of warmth (rub between hands or hold over a hairdryer) fixes it, but it's a minor annoyance out of the box.

## Who Should Buy These
