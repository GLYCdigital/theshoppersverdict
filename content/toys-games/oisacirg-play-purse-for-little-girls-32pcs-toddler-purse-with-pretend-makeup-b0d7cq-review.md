Let’s be honest — most toddler play purses are landfill fodder within a month. Thin plastic, zippers that jam, and pieces that snap the first time a three-year-old sits on them. The Oisacirg 32-Piece Play Purse is not that toy. It’s not perfect, but for under $20, it punches well above its price point.

**What’s actually in the box**

You get 32 pieces total: a pink faux-leather purse with a working zipper and shoulder strap, plus a full pretend makeup kit — lipsticks, eyeshadow palettes, a compact mirror, a hairbrush, a phone, keys, a wallet, and assorted accessories. The pieces are made from a soft-touch ABS plastic that’s smooth-edged and safe for small hands. The purse itself measures roughly 8 x 6 x 2 inches — big enough to feel like a "real" purse to a toddler, but small enough for them to carry comfortably.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The purse is the star here. The stitching is reinforced at the strap attachment points, and the zipper glides smoothly — two things that are shockingly rare in this category. I’ve seen multiple reviews mention the purse surviving being used as a step stool, a weapon, and a sleep aid. One verified buyer wrote: *"My daughter has thrown this thing across the room more times than I can count, and it still looks brand new."* That tracks with what I found.

The makeup pieces are decent, not great. The lipsticks twist open and closed, which delights toddlers but means they’ll end up scattered. The eyeshadow palettes have actual "shadow" in them that smears — washable, but expect a mess. The compact mirror is the weak link: it’s a cheap reflective sticker that scratches and smudges within days. Plan to keep it out of the rotation if you care about realism.

**What buyers consistently praise**

- **The value.** At 32 pieces, parents repeatedly note this costs less than a single trip to the dollar store for the same volume of stuff.
- **The variety.** Kids love having "everything mommy has" — phone, keys, wallet, makeup. It’s a complete pretend-play kit, not a sad collection of three lipsticks.
- **The durability.** The purse itself is the standout. Multiple reviewers mention it outlasting more expensive brands.

**Where it falls short**

The pieces are smaller than the product photos suggest. The lipsticks are about the size of a pinky finger, and the phone is roughly 3 inches long — fine for a toddler, but easy to lose under couch cushions. One reviewer noted: *"We've already lost three pieces in two weeks. They're just small enough to vanish."* Also, once everything is removed from the purse, it’s nearly impossible to fit all 32 pieces back in without a Tetris session. You’ll likely end up keeping a small baggie for overflow.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for:
- **Parents of 2–4 year olds** who want an affordable, safe introduction to pretend play.
- **Grandparents** looking for a gift that won’t get returned — it’s substantial enough to feel like a "real" present.
- **Daycare or preschool teachers** who need a durable, communal pretend-play set.

Skip it if you’re buying for a child over 5 — they’ll likely outgrow the novelty quickly, and the small pieces will frustrate them.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is the makeup washable?**
Yes. The eyeshadow and lipstick smear marks come off skin and most fabrics with soap and water. It’s not permanent — just be prepared for some cleanup during play.

**Q: Are there any choking hazards?**
The pieces are all larger than a standard choking tube, but the smaller accessories (lipstick caps, etc.) could be a risk for children under 2. Recommended for ages 3+.

**Q: Does the purse come with a strap?**
Yes, it includes a detachable shoulder strap. It’s adjustable but designed for toddler shoulder length, not adult.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a complete pretend-play set that survives real toddler abuse without breaking the bank. The 32-piece count, durable purse, and safe materials make this a solid 4.6/5.

**Skip it if:** You hate picking up tiny pieces, or your child is too old for pretend makeup.

For the price, this is one of the better toddler play purse options on Amazon right now. Just budget for a small storage container.
