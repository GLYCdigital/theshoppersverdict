**Real-World Performance**

I've tested enough party kits to know the difference between "looks good in the Amazon photos" and "survives a room full of sugared-up five-year-olds." This one sits firmly in the reliable camp.

The banner is the standout piece. It's printed on cardstock-weight material with a reinforced fold, and the pre-punched holes align properly — no wrestling with uneven spacing to get it hung. The string included is basic but functional; I'd swap in stronger ribbon if you're hanging it in a drafty location.

Plates are the right density — sturdy enough for cake and a pile of chips without flexing, but not so thick they're wasteful. The napkins are standard 2-ply party quality. Fine for the job, nothing more.

The tablecloth is where the kit shows its budget nature. It's the thin, crinkly plastic variety. On a perfectly smooth table it works fine. On a slightly rough surface or if you stretch it taut to cover an oversized table, it will tear. Plan accordingly — buy a separate heavier-duty cloth if you're using a rented or outdoor table.

**The Sticker Situation:** The included stickers are smaller die-cut shapes (pups, badges, "Happy Birthday" text) rather than full scenes. They're perfect for decorating goodie bags or name tags. If your kid expects wall-sized character posters, this isn't that.
