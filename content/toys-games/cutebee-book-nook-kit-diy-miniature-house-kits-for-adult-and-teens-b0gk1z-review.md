## Build Quality and Real-World Assembly

The pre-cut wood pieces are the standout feature. Buyers consistently mention that parts punch out cleanly and fit together without needing to sand edges or force joints. One reviewer put it plainly: "Every piece clicked into place — I didn't touch a single tool except the included sandpaper for two spots." The LED lighting system is genuinely functional. The kit includes a small battery box that hides in the base, with wires running through pre-drilled channels. ## Where It Falls Short

The small parts are genuinely small. If you have large hands or shaky fingers, the furniture assembly will frustrate you. Several buyers recommend having a magnifying lamp on hand.

The LED wiring is the most common failure point. A handful of reviewers report that wires disconnected during assembly, requiring soldering to fix. It's not widespread — maybe 5-8% of the feedback mentions it — but it's worth knowing before you start. Handle the wire runs gently, and test the lights before you glue everything shut.

The dust. MDF cutting produces a fine dust that gets everywhere during assembly. Plan to work on a surface you can wipe down.
