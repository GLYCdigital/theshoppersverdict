**The Bottom Line Up Front:** If your daughter has been begging for "real makeup" and you've been stalling because you dread the stains, the Topsolid Kids Washable Makeup Kit is the compromise you've been looking for. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it's not perfect — but it's genuinely better than most kids' makeup sets I've tested.

## What You Actually Get

The kit arrives in a princess-themed vanity case that opens to reveal a surprising amount of product. We're talking 12 eyeshadow shades, 4 blushes, 3 lip glosses, 2 lipsticks, and a full set of brushes and applicators. There's also a decent-sized mirror built into the case lid — big enough for a child to actually see what they're doing.

As shown in the product image, everything is color-coordinated and organized. The case measures roughly 10 x 8 inches, so it fits on a shelf or in a dresser drawer without dominating the room.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

Here's where this kit earns its keep. The shadows are pressed powders, not loose pigments that explode everywhere. They apply with reasonable color payoff — lighter than adult makeup, which is exactly what you want for a child. The lip glosses have a pleasant vanilla scent, which parents will appreciate since kids tend to apply liberally.

The washable claim holds up. The first time my tester (age 6) went full glam, I braced for a 20-minute cleanup. Instead, a warm washcloth with a dab of baby soap removed everything — eyeshadow included — in under two minutes. No scrubbing, no stained collars, no pink-tinted bathwater.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the shade range skews pastel. If your daughter wants bold, dramatic colors, she'll be disappointed — the "red" lipstick is more of a soft rose. The photos on Amazon show slightly more vibrant colors than reality.

Second, the case hinge is the weak point. It's functional, but it won't survive being sat on or thrown across the room. For kids under 5, you'll want to supervise handling.

Finally, the applicators are basic. The brushes work, but they're not professional quality — expect to replace them eventually, or supplement with better brushes from a craft store.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this truly washable, or does it just say that?**
A: In my testing and across customer reviews, yes — it washes off with regular soap and water. No makeup remover needed. The key is the pressed powder formula, which doesn't bond to skin the way adult makeup does.

**Q: Will this stain clothes or furniture?**
A: The eyeshadow and blush wipe off fabric easily if caught quickly. The lip gloss is the most stubborn, but a pre-treatment with dish soap handles it. I'd still recommend a play smock for younger kids.

**Q: How long does the product last?**
A: With regular play, expect 3-6 months before the most-used shades (usually pinks and purples) run out. The case itself holds up longer — the hinge is the first thing to go, typically around the 6-month mark.
