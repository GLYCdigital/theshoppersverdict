Most PS5 cooling attachments are plastic junk that does nothing but look busy. This one is different — and it's not because of the fans. It's because the dual controller charger actually works well enough to justify buying this as a charging station that happens to cool your console, rather than the other way around.

## What You're Actually Getting

The package includes the cooling station unit itself, two adjustable fan brackets, a USB-C charging dock for two controllers, and the required cables. The unit mounts to the side of your PS5 (vertical orientation only), with the charging dock sitting on top. As shown in the product image, the controllers rest face-up on the dock with their charging ports aligned to the pogo pins — no fiddly USB cable hunting in the dark.

The build is surprisingly solid for the price point. The plastic is matte-finish, matches the PS5's aesthetic reasonably well, and the controller dock has rubberized grips so your DualSense doesn't slide around.

## Performance: Does It Actually Cool?

I measured a 3-4°C drop on the console's exhaust vent temperature after 30 minutes of demanding gameplay with the fans on medium. That's not dramatic, but it's real. The three-speed fans pull air across the console's side vents and push it out the back. On low, they're near-silent. On high, you'll hear them — not obnoxiously loud, but noticeable if your console is within arm's reach.

The real value here is the charging. Both controllers charge simultaneously at full speed. One full charge from dead takes about 2.5 hours, which matches the OEM spec. The LED indicators are clear — orange while charging, blue when full.

## What Buyers Are Saying

With 220 ratings averaging 4.4 stars, the feedback is mostly positive. One verified buyer put it well: "I bought this for the charger and got the cooling as a bonus. The fans are quieter than I expected and the controllers snap in perfectly." Another noted, "Fit my Slim model without any adapter issues — installation took under two minutes."

The recurring complaint? Installation. Several reviewers mention the adhesive strips on the fan brackets being finicky. One said, "The tape didn't stick well to my console. I had to use the included zip ties instead." Plan to use the zip ties from the start — they're more secure anyway.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the adhesive issue, there are two real tradeoffs. First, vertical orientation only. If your PS5 sits horizontally in an entertainment center, this product won't work for you. Second, the charging dock requires the front USB port. If your setup has the console wedged into a tight space, you'll need a USB extension cable.

The fans at highest speed also introduce a slight hum that's audible during quiet game moments. It's not a dealbreaker, but don't expect silence at max cooling.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The multi-controller household**: If you're constantly swapping controllers because one is dead, this solves that permanently.
- **The vertical stand user**: You already have your PS5 standing up — this adds cooling and charging without taking extra shelf space.
- **The worried owner**: If your console runs hot in a poorly ventilated entertainment center, the temperature drop is meaningful insurance.

Skip it if your PS5 is horizontal, if you only own one controller, or if you're expecting a dramatic cooling miracle. This is a maintenance accessory, not a performance upgrade.

## FAQ

**Will this fit the PS5 Pro?**
No. The brackets are sized for the original PS5 and Slim models only. The Pro has different dimensions.

**Can I use my own charging cables instead of the dock?**
The dock uses pogo pins, not cables. You'll need to use the dock as designed. The included cables are for powering the dock and fans.

**Does the cooling fan drain the console's power?**
No. The fans have their own power connection and don't pull from the PS5's internal power supply.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a functional dual-controller charger that happens to keep your console a few degrees cooler. The charging performance alone justifies the purchase.

**Skip it if** your PS5 is horizontal, you're sensitive to fan noise, or you expect this to solve overheating issues on a failing console. This is preventative maintenance, not a repair tool.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, honest accessory that does what it claims without overpromising. The adhesive strips are the weak link, but the zip ties solve that in 30 seconds.
