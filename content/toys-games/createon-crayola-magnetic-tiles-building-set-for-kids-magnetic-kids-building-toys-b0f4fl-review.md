**The short version:** This isn't just another magnetic tile set. The CreateOn Crayola collaboration nails two things most competitors miss — color quality and magnet strength — while staying fully compatible with the tiles you probably already own. At 4.8 stars from 240 ratings, the consensus is clear. But there are a couple of gaps worth knowing before you click buy.

**What's actually in the box**

You get 64 pieces: 60 standard 3-inch square magnetic tiles plus 4 decorative Crayola-branded "crayon" pieces that snap into the tile frames. The tiles are translucent ABS plastic with embedded magnets along all four edges. As shown in the product image, the color range includes the classic Crayola eight — red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, plus pink and teal. Each tile measures 3 inches square, which is the industry standard.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The magnets are the story here. I've tested a dozen tile sets over the years, and weak magnets are the #1 frustration — structures collapse when a toddler sneezes near them. These are noticeably stronger. A 3-year-old can build a five-story tower and it holds. A 7-year-old can construct a castle with a working drawbridge and it survives sibling inspection. The ABS plastic is thick enough that tiles don't crack when dropped on hardwood, and the edges are smooth with no sharp seams.

The Crayola branding isn't just a logo slap. The colors are more saturated than the generic rainbow sets — the red is actually red, not orange-red, and the blue is deep rather than washed out. Parents consistently mention this. One reviewer noted, "The colors are so much prettier than our other set. My daughter specifically asks for these tiles." Another said, "I bought these to supplement our Picasso Tiles and they fit perfectly together."

**Where it falls short**

No triangles. That's the biggest limitation. The base set is all squares, so you can't build roofs, ramps, or angled structures without buying an expansion pack. For younger kids (2-4) this is fine — squares are actually easier for small hands. But if you have a kid who loves building cars or houses with pitched roofs, you'll want the expansion set too.

Storage is also on you. There's no bag, box, or bucket included. With 64 loose pieces, that's a problem if you have a "tiles go everywhere" household. Budget for a $10 storage bin.

**Who should buy this**

- **Parents of 3-6 year olds** — this is the sweet spot. The strong magnets mean less frustration for little hands, and the colors make clean-up almost fun.
- **Families with existing magnetic tiles** — the compatibility is seamless. You can mix these with Picasso Tiles, Magna-Tiles, or Playmags without issue.
- **Gift-givers** — the Crayola brand recognition plus the visual pop of the colors makes this a safe, impressive present. The box itself is sturdy enough for wrapping without a gift bag.

**Skip it if** your kid is 7+ and already building complex structures — they'll need the shape variety of a full set. Or if you're on a tight budget and just need maximum pieces per dollar; generic sets offer more tiles for less, albeit with weaker magnets and duller colors.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these compatible with Magna-Tiles?**
Yes. The 3-inch size and edge magnet placement match the standard. They click together fine with Magna-Tiles, Picasso Tiles, and most generic brands.

**Q: What age is this appropriate for?**
The box says 3+, which is accurate. The magnets are strong enough that a determined 2-year-old could pinch fingers, so supervise toddlers. No small parts, so choking isn't a concern.

**Q: How many pieces do I need to build something impressive?**
The 64-piece set is enough for a decent castle or a two-story house. For anything elaborate — a full village, a multi-level parking garage — you'll want a second set or an expansion pack.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a magnetic tile set that looks great, holds strong, and integrates with whatever you already own. The Crayola color quality and magnet strength justify the premium over generic sets.

**Skip it if** you need shape variety out of the box, or you're buying for an older kid who's already outgrown square-only building.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting half a star for the missing triangles and the lack of storage. Everything else is genuinely excellent.
