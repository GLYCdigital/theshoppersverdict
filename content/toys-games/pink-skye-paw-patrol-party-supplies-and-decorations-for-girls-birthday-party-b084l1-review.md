## What's Actually in the Box

This is a 100+ piece kit that covers the essentials. You get 16 dinner plates, 16 dessert plates, 16 napkins, and 16 cups — enough for a solid party of that size without running out mid-cake. Add in a "Happy Birthday" banner, a Skye-themed party sign, and a roll of curling ribbon, plus cupcake toppers and stickers. As one parent noted, "I didn't have to buy anything else except the cake and candles."

The packaging is organized and arrives in decent shape — no crushed plates or torn banners, which is more than I can say for some competitors in this price range.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Here's where this set separates itself from the cheap stuff. The plates are a thicker cardstock than the flimsy paper plates you'll find in generic party packs. They survived a full birthday spread — pizza, mac and cheese, and the inevitable cake smash — without buckling or leaking through. The cups are sturdy enough for juice and soda, though I wouldn't push them with heavy carbonated drinks.

The banner is printed on quality cardstock with vivid colors that match Skye's signature pink and lavender palette. As shown in the product image, the design is bright and recognizable — this passes the "is that really Skye?" test that any 3-to-5-year-old will immediately administer.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The overwhelming theme in the reviews is **value**. At this price point, you're getting the complete set without the nickel-and-diming that comes with buying pieces separately. One reviewer put it well: "The colors are so vibrant and the quality is much better than what I expected for the price." ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the gaps. Despite the "Decorations" in the title, there's no tablecover, no centerpiece, and no hanging swirl decorations. You'll need to supplement if you want the full themed look. Some reviewers noted the cupcake toppers are on the thinner side — they work fine on standard cupcakes but can droop if you're dealing with particularly moist frosting or humid conditions.

The set is also firmly for the Skye fan — if your child's allegiance shifts to Chase or Marshall mid-party-planning (it happens), you're stuck with pink and lavender supplies.
