I've tested more plush toys than I care to admit, and most of them blur together into a pile of polyester fluff. Celie the grasshopper isn't one of them. This 14-inch Squishmallow from the brand's original line brings something genuinely different: a bug that's actually adorable.

**What you're actually getting**

Out of the box, Celie arrives neatly compressed and vacuum-sealed — standard for Squishmallows. Give her a few minutes to fluff up fully. She's 14 inches from antenna to base, which puts her in the sweet spot between "desk companion" and "pillow substitute." The teal body with black spots and the little pink bow on her head (visible in the product images) give her a whimsical personality that photos don't quite capture. The embroidered eyes and stitched smile are clean and precise — no loose threads or misaligned features on the unit I examined.

**The feel factor**

This is where Squishmallows earns its reputation. Celie uses the brand's signature marshmallow-soft polyester fill that compresses easily but springs back. It's not a dense, overly firm plush — it's the kind of soft that makes you instinctively squeeze it while watching TV. One buyer noted, "I bought this for my daughter who collects insects and she hasn't put it down in two weeks." That tracks with the 4.8-star average across 240 ratings. The quality control on the stitching is consistent, which matters when a toy is going to endure daily hugging.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Looking through the feedback, three themes emerge. First, the design originality — parents and collectors repeatedly mention that Celie breaks the monotony of typical plush animals. Second, the durability; multiple reviewers mention washing her (gentle cycle, cold water, low dryer heat) with zero pilling or seam issues. Third, the giftability — one reviewer wrote, "Bought as a birthday gift for a 7-year-old who screamed with joy. The bow really makes it." The 14-inch size hits a practical balance: large enough to be meaningful, small enough to carry on road trips.

**Where she falls short**

The sewn-on bow is the biggest sticking point. It's cute, but it's permanent — if you or your child want to remove or swap it, you'll need a seam ripper and some patience. Also, at 14 inches, she's shorter than the 16-inch and 20-inch options in the Squishmallow lineup. For adults who want a full-body cuddle companion, the smaller size might feel more like a decorative piece than a sleep partner. And the teal-and-black color scheme, while striking, isn't a neutral that works in every bedroom — check the vibe of the space before committing.

**Who should buy this**

- **Insect-loving kids (ages 4-10)** who will appreciate a friendly bug character that isn't scary
- **Squishmallow collectors** filling out the less-common animal categories in their lineup
- **Gift-givers** looking for something unique for a birthday or holiday — the bow adds a touch of charm that photographs well
- **Teachers or therapists** who use stuffed animals as comfort objects or classroom rewards

**Skip it if** you need a larger cuddle partner, if removable accessories are a must, or if you're strictly collecting neutral-toned plushies.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can the bow be removed?**
A: No. It's securely sewn into the head seam. Removing it would require cutting stitches and could damage the fabric or leave visible holes.

**Q: Is this the same softness as other Squishmallows?**
A: Yes. Celie uses the standard marshmallow-soft fill across the brand's lineup. If you've felt any other Squishmallow, you know exactly what to expect.

**Q: How does she hold up to washing?**
A: Machine wash on gentle with cold water, then tumble dry on low. Multiple buyers report she comes out clean with no clumping or fabric damage. Avoid high heat — it can affect the polyester fill's loft.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5 — Buy it if you want a soft, unique, well-made plush that stands out from the crowd. Skip it if you need a bigger size or removable accessories.**

Celie is a solid addition to the Squishmallow family. She's soft, well-constructed, and genuinely charming. The bow limitation and the smaller size are the only real tradeoffs, but for most buyers — especially kids and collectors — those won't be dealbreakers. At this price point with this quality, she's an easy recommendation for anyone looking for a distinctive plush gift.
