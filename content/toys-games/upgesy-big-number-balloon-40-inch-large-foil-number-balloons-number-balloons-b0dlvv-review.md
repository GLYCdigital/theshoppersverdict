You've seen those giant number balloons at every milestone birthday, graduation party, and baby shower. The Upgesy 40-inch version is the one that makes people stop and say "whoa" when they walk in the room. But is it actually worth the hype, or is it just another party decoration that deflates your expectations? I've dug through the specs and the feedback from real buyers to give you the straight answer before you hit "add to cart."

**What's in the box**

The package contains the 40-inch foil number balloon (you pick the number you need), a straw for inflation, and a ribbon for hanging. That's it — no pump included. The balloon arrives flat, folded into a compact package. If you're expecting it to come pre-inflated or with any mounting hardware, adjust your expectations now. The self-sealing valve at the bottom is the standard foil balloon design: push the straw in, inflate, pull the straw out, and the valve seals itself.

**Performance and build quality**

The headline feature here is the size. At 40 inches tall, this thing is massive — about the height of a toddler. As shown in the product images, it dominates a standard backdrop and creates that "wow" factor you're paying for. The foil material is double-sided with a glossy finish, and the seams are reinforced, which addresses the most common failure point on cheaper foil balloons.

Inflation is the real workout. Multiple buyers note this takes genuine effort. The included straw works, but don't expect to blow this up in 30 seconds. It took one reviewer "a solid five minutes and a lot of deep breaths" to get it fully firm. If you're inflating multiple balloons for a party, budget time for this or buy a hand pump — the valve accepts standard balloon pump nozzles.

Once inflated, the balloon holds air well. Several reviewers mentioned it stayed fully inflated for 3-4 days when they sealed it properly. The trick is making sure you pull the straw out quickly and firmly so the valve closes completely — if you do it slowly, you'll lose air immediately.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The size and visual impact get the most love. One reviewer said, "This was the centerpiece of my daughter's sweet 16 — everyone asked where we got it." Another noted it "looked way more expensive than what we paid." The durability also earned positive mentions, with multiple people saying it survived being moved around the party and even got reused for a second celebration.

**Where it falls short**

The creasing issue is real. Since the balloon ships folded, it arrives with sharp crease lines that become visible once inflated. Most reviewers said these softened over time or weren't noticeable from a distance, but if you're photographing close-up, you'll see them. Some buyers also reported the balloon arriving with a tiny pinhole, though this seems to be the minority experience — and the return process handled those cases.

The lack of a pump is the other frustration point. For a balloon this size, including only a straw feels like an oversight. You'll either need strong lungs or a separate pump purchase.

**Who should buy this**

This is for anyone hosting a milestone celebration — 30th, 40th, 50th birthdays, graduations, or gender reveals — where you want a statement piece without hiring a decorator. It's also solid for event planners who need reliable, reusable decorations. Skip it if you're doing a quick, small gathering where a standard balloon will do, or if you don't have a pump and aren't willing to work for the inflation.

**FAQ**

**How long does it stay inflated?** With proper sealing, expect 3-5 days. Keep it away from direct sunlight and heat sources — foil balloons expand and can burst in the heat.

**Can I refill it?** Yes, the valve is reusable. Just reinsert the straw and add air as needed. Many buyers reported successfully reinflating for a second day of celebration.

**Does it come with a pump?** No. The package includes a straw and ribbon only. Buy a hand pump separately if you're inflating multiple balloons.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a high-impact, oversized decoration that photographs beautifully and lasts for days. The 40-inch size genuinely sets it apart from standard party store options.

**Skip it if** you're on a tight budget, don't want to deal with manual inflation, or need something that packs flat for storage without visible creases.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The size and durability earn the high marks, but the creasing and lack of a pump keep it from being a perfect score. For the visual payoff, it's a solid buy for your next big milestone.
