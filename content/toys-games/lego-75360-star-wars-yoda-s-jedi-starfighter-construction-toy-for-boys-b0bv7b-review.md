If you've been eyeing this set on the shelf, here's the honest breakdown: the LEGO 75360 Yoda's Jedi Starfighter is a solid mid-range build that nails the look of the iconic green starfighter from *Attack of the Clones* and *Revenge of the Sith*. With 253 pieces, it hits a sweet spot — substantial enough to feel like a real project, compact enough to finish in an evening.

**What's in the box:** You get the starfighter itself, two minifigures (Yoda with a cloth cape and R2-D2), a display stand, and two spring-loaded shooters. The instruction booklet is clear, and the pieces come in numbered bags — standard modern LEGO practice. Nothing surprising here, but the starfighter's design is where this set earns its keep.

**Build quality and real-world use:** The wing mechanism is the standout feature. Each wing folds from flight mode into the vertical landing position, and the action is smooth — no friction issues or loose joints. The green and dark green color blocking matches the on-screen ship accurately, and the printed cockpit details (no stickers for the main canopy) keep things crisp. The spring-loaded shooters tuck neatly under the nose and fire with satisfying force.

As shown in the product image, the ship sits on a clear display stand that lets you pose it mid-flight without balancing it on the landing gear. That's a thoughtful inclusion for adult collectors who want it on a shelf but still gives kids the freedom to swoosh it around. The build itself takes about 30-45 minutes at a relaxed pace — manageable for a 7-year-old with some experience, though the wing mechanism requires careful alignment.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The minifigures are the headline. Yoda's new molded head with the pointed ears and wrinkled features is a significant upgrade from older prints. As one verified purchaser put it: "The Yoda figure alone is worth the price — it's the best version LEGO has made." R2-D2 is the standard dome-headed droid, but having both characters in one set at this price point is rare. Multiple reviewers also noted the display stand as a "surprisingly nice touch" that elevates the whole presentation.

**Where it falls short:** Let's talk price-per-piece. At roughly $35, you're paying about 14 cents per brick — higher than the 10-cent average for LEGO sets in this range. You're paying for the licensing and the exclusive minifigures, which is fair, but budget-conscious buyers should know that upfront. The 4+ age rating on the box is also misleading; the wing folding mechanism and small pieces mean most 4-5 year olds will need significant adult help. This is realistically a 7+ build.

Another minor gripe: no landing gear. Once the wings fold up, the ship rests on its nose unless you use the stand. For kids who want to "land" it on the floor during play, this is a small frustration.

**Who should buy this:** The LEGO 75360 is perfect for three groups: Star Wars collectors who want the definitive Yoda minifigure, parents of kids aged 7-12 who want a build that's challenging but not overwhelming, and adult fans building a Jedi starfighter collection. If you're purely after piece count or want a complex build, look at the larger 75333 Obi-Wan's Jedi Starfighter instead.

**FAQ:**

**Q: Is this set compatible with other LEGO Star Wars sets?**
A: Yes — it uses standard LEGO connectors, so you can attach it to the 75333 Obi-Wan starfighter or display it alongside any other Jedi ships from the same era.

**Q: Are there any stickers, or are all details printed?**
A: Most details are printed, including the cockpit canopy and the Republic insignia. There are a couple of small stickers for the side panels, but they're optional — the ship looks fine without them.

**Q: How many minifigures are included?**
A: Two: Yoda with a cloth cape and R2-D2. No clone troopers or other figures in this set.

**The Verdict:** The LEGO 75360 Yoda's Jedi Starfighter earns its 4.8-star rating. It's a well-designed, accurate replica with two excellent exclusive minifigures and genuine play features. The high price-per-piece and misleading age rating are real considerations, but they don't outweigh the quality of what you're getting.

**Buy it if:** You want the best Yoda minifigure LEGO has produced, you're building a Jedi starfighter collection, or you need a satisfying weekend build for a young Star Wars fan.

**Skip it if:** You're on a tight budget and prioritize piece count over minifigures, or you're looking for a complex, multi-hour building challenge.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a strong set with minor pricing caveats, but one that delivers on both display and play value.
