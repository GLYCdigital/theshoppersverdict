You've seen the photos: a chunky blue hauler with two little cars perched on its decks. Cute, right? But here's what the product images don't tell you — the cars are sold separately. That's the single biggest point of confusion among the 240 Amazon ratings, and it's worth knowing before you click buy.

That said, once you understand what you're actually getting, the Green Toys Car Carrier is one of the most durable, thoughtfully designed toddler toys I've reviewed. Let's dig into the details.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the carrier itself — a two-level hauler made from 100% recycled plastic milk jugs. It measures roughly 13 inches long and 7 inches tall, which is a solid size for small hands. The top deck holds two cars; the bottom holds two more. There's a fold-down ramp on the back for loading, and the whole thing weighs almost nothing.

No cars. No batteries. No assembly. No tiny parts that'll end up in your vacuum. The packaging is minimal and recyclable, which fits the brand's whole ethos.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Here's where Green Toys earns its reputation. The plastic is thick, rigid, and completely free of BPA, phthalates, and external coatings. It's not glossy or "premium" feeling — it's more like a sturdy playground slide — but that's a feature, not a bug. This thing is built to be thrown, dropped, kicked, and left in the backyard overnight.

The ramp folds down and stays put, making it easy for toddlers to roll cars up and down. The wheel wells on each deck keep cars in place during play, though they're shallow enough that a vigorous push will send vehicles flying — which, honestly, is part of the fun for most kids.

One design note: the ramp doesn't lock into the upright position. It just rests against the back. That's fine for play, but if your kid expects a spring-loaded mechanism, they'll be disappointed. It's a simple, functional design, not an engineered one.

## What Buyers Actually Say

The 4.8-star average holds up under scrutiny. The praise is consistent and specific:

> "My 2-year-old is rough on toys and this has survived being thrown down the stairs multiple times."

Another parent noted:

> "Great quality, easy to clean, and my son loves loading and unloading his cars. Just wish they'd included at least one car."

That last point is the recurring theme. Roughly a quarter of the negative reviews mention surprise that cars aren't included. If you're buying this as a gift, pair it with a set of small cars or prepare for disappointment.

## Where It Falls Short

The missing cars are the obvious con, but there's another issue worth flagging: the carrier's size. It's compact enough for toddlers, but older kids (5+) will likely find it too small for their Hot Wheels collections. The decks fit standard-size cars (about 3 inches long), but anything larger won't fit.

Also, the ramp's lack of a locking mechanism means it can't be used as a vertical loading dock — a feature found on pricier competitors like the Melissa & Doug carrier. For most toddlers, this won't matter. For kids who love precise, structured play, it might.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Parents of 18-month to 4-year-olds**: This is the sweet spot. It's safe for mouthing, easy to grip, and simple enough for young toddlers.
- **Eco-conscious shoppers**: If you care about materials, this is the greenest toy on the market. Recycled plastic, minimal packaging, and it's recyclable at end of life.
- **Families who need an indestructible toy**: If your kid is rough on everything, this will outlast them.

**Skip it if** your child is 5+ and expects a more complex playset, or if you don't want to buy cars separately.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely indestructible, eco-friendly toy for a toddler who loves vehicles. **Skip it if** you're buying for an older kid or you don't want to buy cars separately.
