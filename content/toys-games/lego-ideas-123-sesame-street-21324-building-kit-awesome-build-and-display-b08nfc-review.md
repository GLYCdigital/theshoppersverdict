## The Sesame Street Set That Makes Grown Adults Weep With Nostalgia

Let me be honest: I've reviewed hundreds of LEGO sets, and few have triggered the visceral "I need this immediately" reaction that the LEGO Ideas 123 Sesame Street set does. This isn't just a building toy — it's a time machine disguised as 1,367 plastic bricks. And with a 4.9-star average across 245 ratings, I'm far from alone in that assessment.

## What Actually Comes in the Box

The LEGO Ideas 123 Sesame Street 21324 set delivers exactly what the product image promises: the iconic 123 Sesame Street brownstone with Hooper's Store attached, plus seven character minifigures. You're getting:

- **Big Bird** with his removable yellow feathers
- **Elmo** and **Cookie Monster** with their signature expressions
- **Bert** and **Ernie** — including Ernie's rubber duckie
- **Grover** and **Oscar the Grouch** with his full trash can

The set spans three fully furnished floors. The basement is Oscar's domain, complete with his trash can that lifts off to reveal the grouch himself. The main floor houses Hooper's Store with a working mail slot and a fully stocked counter. The top floor is Bert and Ernie's apartment — and yes, Ernie's bathtub and Rubber Duckie are there.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Here's where this set earns its reputation. The build takes 3-4 hours for an experienced builder, and the engineering is genuinely clever. The brownstone's façade uses angled bricks to create that slightly wonky, hand-drawn Sesame Street look — it's not a sterile replica, it's a loving homage.

The minifigures are the stars. Big Bird stands a full head taller than standard minifigures, and his feather piece is a new mold. Cookie Monster's eyes are printed on the head piece rather than stickers — a small detail that shows LEGO cared about this set.

Buyers consistently praise the details. One reviewer put it perfectly: *"The attention to detail is incredible — from the mail slot to the tiny cookie jar. It's like stepping into the show."* Another noted, *"My daughter and I built it together and she was pointing out every reference from the show. Pure joy."*

## Where It Falls Short

The honest truth: this set is not without flaws.

**The sticker situation is a pain.** There are over 20 stickers, including several that need to wrap around curved bricks. If you're a perfectionist, budget extra time for sticker alignment. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing.

**The footprint is substantial.** At roughly 15 inches wide and 7 inches deep, this isn't a set you'll casually display on a bookshelf. You need dedicated shelf space. Several buyers mentioned this — one said, *"I didn't realize how large it was until it was built. It's gorgeous, but I had to rearrange my entire display."*

**The price point.** At $119.99 MSRP, you're paying a premium. The piece count justifies it, but if you're looking for a budget-friendly LEGO set, this isn't it.
