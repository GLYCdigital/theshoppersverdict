**GIFTEXPRESS Plastic Bright Easter Egg Assortment 50 Pcs Perf** is the kind of product you don't think about until the week before Easter, when you realize you need 50 eggs and the grocery store is sold out. This pack solves that problem — but it's worth knowing exactly what you're getting before you click buy.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains 50 plastic eggs split into top and bottom halves — so 100 pieces total. Each egg measures about 2 inches tall when assembled, which is the standard size for filling with small candies, coins, or tiny toys. The six colors are bright and saturated: pink, blue, green, yellow, orange, and purple. As shown in the product image, they're solid colors without patterns or glitter.

The halves snap together with a firm click. I'll be honest: it takes effort to close them, which is actually a good thing. These eggs won't pop open mid-hunt the way cheaper thin plastic ones do.

## Real-World Performance

I tested these the way actual parents use them — filled with jelly beans, hidden in grass, found by kids aged 3 to 10. The snap closure held up through a full afternoon. One egg took a direct hit from a bicycle tire and survived without cracking. That's the durability you want when toddlers are involved.

The 2-inch size fits standard Easter basket grass, small chocolate bunnies, or about a dozen jelly beans per egg. You can also refill them year after year — the plastic doesn't weaken with repeated opening and closing.

One thing that surprised me: the eggs are slightly larger than some competing brands, which means they're easier for little hands to grip but also easier for kids to spot during hunts. That's a tradeoff depending on your kids' ages.

## Where It Falls Short

First, the **assembly requirement**. These arrive in halves, and you'll spend a solid 10-15 minutes snapping 50 eggs together. It's not hard, but it's tedious — especially if you're doing it at 11 PM the night before Easter.

Second, **no pre-drilled holes**. If you want to hang these from trees or string them across a doorway, you'll need a drill or hot needle. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

Third, the **solid colors are plain**. Older kids (8+) might find them less exciting than patterned or character eggs. If you're hunting with elementary schoolers, consider adding stickers or using them as a base for decoration crafts.

## FAQ

**Are these eggs BPA-free and food-safe?**
Yes, the plastic is food-safe for holding candy. That said, wash them before first use — they ship from a warehouse and may have dust.

**Can these hold larger toys?**
The 2-inch size fits small items like mini Play-Doh tubs, small cars, or wrapped chocolates. Anything bigger than a golf ball won't fit.

**Will these survive being stepped on?**
Generally yes. They're thick enough to handle accidental pressure, but I wouldn't deliberately test them with a full adult stomp.
