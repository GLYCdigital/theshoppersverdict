**The 3DS is a dead console walking — and that's exactly why you need this charger.**

Nintendo discontinued the 3DS family in 2020. The eShop shut down in 2023. But your console still works, and if you're reading this, you probably still play it. The problem? The official charger was discontinued too. Third-party replacements are your only option, and most of them are junk.

The VOYEE 3DS Charger is one of the better options out there. With a 4.6/5 average across 229 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. Here's what you actually need to know before you buy.

**What you get in the package**

This is a USB-to-barrel cable, not a complete charging solution. The box contains the 5-foot cable and nothing else. No wall adapter, no USB-C conversion tip — just the proprietary Nintendo connector on one end and a standard USB-A plug on the other.

That's worth noting. If you don't have a USB wall adapter lying around, factor that into your purchase. Most people have one from an old phone, but it's not included.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The connector is where most 3DS chargers fail. Cheap ones feel loose, wiggle in the port, and eventually stop making contact. The VOYEE cable has a snug, precise fit — it clicks in firmly and holds position. That's the most important thing a 3DS charger can do right, and this one does.

The cable itself is a mixed bag. At 5 feet, it's longer than the official 3-foot Nintendo charger, which makes it more practical for playing while plugged in on the couch or charging from a bedside outlet. But the cable jacket is standard PVC, not braided. It's not fragile, but it's not premium either. One buyer noted, "The cable is thin but has lasted me over a year of daily use," which matches my assessment.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the 229 ratings, a few themes dominate. Fit and compatibility comes up constantly. "Works perfectly with my New 3DS XL and my old DSi — no issues with either," one verified buyer wrote. Another said, "Finally a charger that doesn't fall out of the port. The connection is solid."

Charging speed is also consistently mentioned as adequate. The 3DS family charges at roughly 4.6V/900mA, and this cable delivers that without overheating or slow-charge errors. You'll get a full charge in about 3.5 hours for a standard 3DS XL and slightly less for the smaller models.

**Where it falls short**

The missing wall adapter is the biggest annoyance. The listing images show the cable plugged into a console, but there's no power brick in the package. If you're buying this as a replacement for a lost official charger, you might not have a spare USB adapter handy.

The LED indicator is also underwhelming. It's a tiny light on the USB plug end of the cable, not on the console connector. You'll barely see it unless you're looking right at the adapter. The console's own charging light is more useful anyway, so this is minor — but it's worth knowing.

**Who should buy this**

This charger is for three specific types of people:

- **Retro gamers** who kept their 3DS or DSi and need a working replacement cable without paying scalper prices for official ones
- **Parents** replacing a lost or chewed-through charger for a kid's console
- **Collectors** who want a backup charger stored in a drawer for future use

It's also fully compatible with the entire 3DS family — original 3DS, 3DS XL, New 3DS, New 3DS XL, 2DS, and 2DS XL — plus the DSi and DSi XL. If you own multiple devices, one cable covers them all.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Will this charge my Nintendo 2DS?*

Yes. The 2DS uses the same charging port as the 3DS and DSi. This cable works across the entire family.

*Can I use it with a fast-charging phone brick?*

Yes, but the console only draws what it needs. A 20W or 30W USB-C adapter will work fine with a USB-A adapter, but charge speed stays at the 3DS's standard rate. You won't damage anything.

*How long does a full charge take?*

Roughly 3 to 3.5 hours for a 3DS XL, slightly less for smaller models. This matches the original Nintendo charger's performance.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need a reliable replacement charger for any 3DS or DSi family console and already own a USB wall adapter.

**Skip it if:** You want a complete plug-and-play solution with a wall brick included — buy a kit that includes one instead.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The VOYEE 3DS Charger does exactly what it needs to do with a solid fit and dependable performance. The missing adapter and average cable quality keep it from being perfect, but for the price, it's the best 3DS charger replacement you're likely to find.
