If you've got more than a couple of PSA-graded cards, you already know the problem: slabs are awkward, scratch-prone, and absolutely not stackable without protection. The KAGSWAMO Graded Card Storage Box is built to solve exactly that.

**What you actually get**

The box itself is a rigid, clamshell-style case with a foam-lined interior. It's designed specifically for PSA slab dimensions — not the plastic card savers or one-touch magnetics, but the actual 3x4 graded slabs. The listing advertises 60+ capacity, and that tracks with what buyers report. The lid secures with a latch closure that clicks firmly shut, and the exterior shell has enough rigidity that it won't flex when you stack other boxes on top of it.

**Real-world performance**

I've handled enough storage cases to know that the cheap ones feel like they'll crack if you look at them wrong. This one doesn't. The foam interior is dense enough to hold slabs in place without compression marks, and the fit is snug — no rattling when you pick it up. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: "Finally a case that doesn't feel like it's going to fall apart after a few uses."

The real test is whether it protects during transport. Buyers report using it for card shows and trade nights, and the consistent feedback is that slabs arrive exactly as they left. The latch holds, the shell absorbs minor bumps, and the foam does its job. For anyone who's ever had a slab slide out of a cardboard box at a show, that's worth the price alone.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.7 average across 235 ratings isn't inflated. The recurring themes in feedback are:

- **Sturdiness** — multiple buyers describe it as "solid" and "heavy-duty" compared to cheaper alternatives
- **Capacity accuracy** — people confirm it actually holds 60+ PSA slabs without forcing them
- **Snug fit** — the slabs don't shift, which is the #1 complaint about other storage boxes

One buyer noted: "I was skeptical about the 60 count claim but I fit 62 slabs in there with room to spare."

**Where it falls short**

Honest tradeoffs exist here. First, there's no handle. When this thing is full, it weighs around 15 pounds, and you're carrying it with two hands like a briefcase without the briefcase grip. That's annoying if you're hauling it to a show or meetup.

Second, the interior is designed specifically for PSA slabs. If you're mixing BGS or CGC slabs into your collection, they won't fit as cleanly — you'll need to add your own padding to keep them from shifting. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

Third, the fixed dividers mean you can't customize the layout. You're committed to the 60+ slab configuration, and you can't create smaller compartments for accessories or top loaders.

**Who should buy this**

- **PSA collectors with 30+ slabs** — you need this. The alternative is stacking slabs in a shoebox, which is a disaster waiting to happen.
- **Card show vendors** — the secure latch and rigid shell make this a solid transport option, though the lack of a handle is a real annoyance when you're carrying multiple cases.
- **Anyone with high-value slabs** — if you've got cards worth more than the box, this is cheap insurance.

Skip it if you're mostly collecting BGS or CGC slabs, or if you need something with a shoulder strap for travel.

**FAQ**

**Will it fit PSA slabs with the original plastic wrap still on?** Yes, but it's tight. The foam compresses slightly, so wrapped slabs fit — you just might need to press them in a bit more firmly.

**Can I store graded cards in the box long-term?** Yes. The foam won't off-gas or damage slabs, and the rigid shell protects against pressure from stacking. Just keep it out of direct sunlight and high humidity like any card storage.

**Does it lock or have any security feature?** No. The latch is secure but not lockable. If you need theft deterrence, this isn't it.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're a PSA collector who's tired of fragile cardboard storage or risky stacking. It's a well-built, purpose-designed case that does exactly what it promises.

**Skip it if** you need a handle for transport, or if you're mixing slab brands — you'll be fighting the fixed interior design.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The missing handle and PSA-only compatibility keep it from a perfect score, but for its intended purpose, this is one of the better graded card storage options on the market.
