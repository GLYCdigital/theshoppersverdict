If you're a Star Wars collector who's been waiting for a Plo Koon minifigure without paying aftermarket prices, this Microfighter is your cheapest ticket. At 89 pieces, this isn't a build that will challenge anyone — but that's not really the point. The point is getting one of the most underrated Jedi into your collection, and LEGO knows exactly what they're doing here.

**What's in the box**

You get the Jedi starfighter in orange-and-gray配色, a Plo Koon minifigure with his distinctive head sculpt, and one lightsaber. That's it. No extra accessories, no display stand, no stickers — all the detailing is printed. The build itself is straightforward: a wedge-shaped cockpit, two engines at the rear, and a couple of flick-fire missiles mounted underneath. The whole thing measures about 4 inches long, which puts it right in line with other Microfighters in the series.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The pieces fit together with the usual LEGO precision — no loose connections, no frustrating gaps. The cockpit canopy opens cleanly, and Plo Koon sits snugly inside. The flick-fire missiles actually work, which matters more for kids than collectors. For display purposes, the ship looks sharp from the top and front angles, but here's the catch: there's no landing gear. The ship rests flat on its belly, which means you can't get that classic "parked on a landing pad" look. It's a minor gripe, but one that collectors should know about going in.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The minifigure is the star here, and buyers know it. One reviewer put it simply: "Bought this for the Plo Koon figure alone. The ship is a bonus." Another noted, "Great value for a unique character. The print quality on the torso is excellent." The exclusive nature of the figure is a recurring theme — this is currently the only affordable way to get Plo Koon in LEGO form, and that alone justifies the purchase for many.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the limitations. At 89 pieces, this is a 10-minute build for anyone past age 8. The scale is small, so it won't satisfy anyone looking for a detailed starfighter display piece. And while the orange color scheme is accurate to the on-screen ship, it's a bit loud for some display setups. One buyer noted, "Wish it came with a stand or at least some transparent supports." Fair point — the lack of landing gear does limit how you can show it off.

**Who should buy this**

- **Collectors missing Plo Koon** — this is your cheapest, most reliable option right now
- **Kids who love Clone Wars** — the flick-fire missiles and compact size make it a fun, portable toy
- **Gift-givers** — at this price point, it's an easy win for a Star Wars fan of any age
- **Skip it if** you're looking for a substantial building challenge or a display piece with presence

**FAQ**

**Is the Plo Koon minifigure exclusive to this set?**
Yes, in this specific form. Plo Koon hasn't appeared in a widely available LEGO set in years, so this is currently the most accessible way to add him to your collection.

**How long does the build take?**
For an experienced builder, roughly 8–10 minutes. For a younger child, maybe 20–30 minutes. It's a quick, satisfying build rather than a lengthy project.

**Does it include stickers?**
No. All the detailing on the ship is printed, which is a nice touch at this price point.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want an affordable, exclusive Plo Koon minifigure or a quick, fun build for a young Star Wars fan.

**Skip it if** you're expecting a substantial building experience or a ship large enough to dominate a shelf.

The LEGO Star Wars Plo Koon Jedi Starfighter Microfighter earns its 4.8-star rating honestly. It's not the most impressive set in the Microfighter line, but it delivers exactly what it promises: a solid little ship and a highly desirable minifigure at a friendly price. For collectors and kids alike, that's a winning combination.
