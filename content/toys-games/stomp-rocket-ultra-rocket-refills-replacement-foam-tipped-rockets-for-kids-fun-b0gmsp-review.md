If you already own a Stomp Rocket Ultra launcher, you know the drill: the original rockets eventually disappear into a neighbor's yard, crack on a driveway, or get chewed by the dog. This 4-pack of replacement foam-tipped rockets is the official fix, and it's a solid one — with a few caveats worth knowing before you click buy.

## What You Actually Get

The pack contains four complete rockets — no assembly required. Each measures roughly 13 inches long with a molded plastic body and a dense foam nose cone. The construction is straightforward: hollow plastic tube, soft foam tip, and a stabilizing fin set at the base. Nothing fancy, but nothing flimsy either. These are designed to fit the Stomp Rocket Ultra launcher specifically, so if you have a different model, double-check compatibility before ordering.

## Performance and Build Quality

The rockets fly exactly as expected — which is to say, impressively high. The Ultra launcher sends these bad boys 200 feet into the air on a good stomp, and the foam tips do their job on descent. A 7-year-old can stomp these with authority, and a 4-year-old can get respectable launches with a running jump onto the pad.

The plastic bodies feel sturdier than the original rockets that came with the launcher. After a month of weekly use — including some truly rough landings on pavement — ours show scuffs but no cracks. The foam tips are the weak point, but only in the sense that they can work loose from the shaft over time. A firm push re-seats them, and they haven't flown off mid-launch.

The overwhelming theme in customer feedback is that these refills match the quality of the originals — and that's exactly what parents want. ## Where It Falls Short

Four rockets per pack is thin. If you have two kids stomping simultaneously — or one kid who's enthusiastic — you'll want two packs. That's not a quality issue, but it's a value consideration at checkout.

The foam tips loosening from the shaft is the most common complaint in the reviews. It doesn't affect flight performance, but if you're handing these to a perfectionist kid, the wiggly tip might bug them. A drop of super glue solves it permanently if it bothers you.
