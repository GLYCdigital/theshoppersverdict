**The Hammond Collection line has been the gold standard for Jurassic collectors since 2022. This Jurassic Park III two-pack — featuring Alan Grant and a male raptor — continues that streak, but it's not without its quirks.**

Let me be direct: if you're a Jurassic collector, you already know the Hammond Collection is where Mattel's A-game lives. The question isn't *whether* this set is good — it's *how* good, and whether the tradeoffs matter to you. With a 4.7 average across 235 ratings, the community consensus is strong. Here's the real breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains two figures: Alan Grant in his JP3 field gear (that iconic sleeveless vest, cargo pants, and beat-up hat) and a male Velociraptor in the classic JP3 tiger-stripe pattern. Grant stands about 6 inches tall; the raptor is roughly 9 inches from nose to tail tip. Both come on collector-friendly window boxes with nice artwork — display-ready without needing to crack the plastic if that's your thing.

Grant includes three sets of swap hands (fists, relaxed, and gripping), plus his removable hat. The raptor gets a ball-jointed head with a surprisingly expressive range and a wired tail that holds poses. A clear display stand for Grant is included, which is a nice touch at this price point.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The articulation is the headline here. Grant has over 30 points of movement, and Mattel has hidden most of the joints inside the sculpt — the knees, elbows, and wrists all blend into the clothing folds. It's the kind of engineering that makes this figure look like a statue until you start posing it. The raptor's legs are double-jointed at the knee, and the toe claws are individually articulated, which is frankly overkill for a toy and perfect for a collector.

Paint work is where this set earns its premium positioning. The raptor's skin has a subtle dry-brush effect that picks up the scales, and the stripes aren't just slapped on — they follow the muscle contours. Grant's face has realistic stubble shading and his vest shows genuine wear marks. As one reviewer put it, *"Mattel finally figured out that collectors will pay for quality paint if you actually deliver it."*

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The overwhelmingly positive feedback centers on three things. First, the screen accuracy — *"This is the definitive Grant figure. The likeness is uncanny for a 6-inch toy."* Second, the raptor's poseability: *"You can finally get that classic JP3 stalking pose without fighting the joints."* Third, the value proposition: *"Two figures, swap parts, a stand — this is what $30 should look like."*

**Where It Falls Short**

The raptor's tail joint is notoriously stiff out of the box. Multiple buyers report needing to run it under hot water to loosen it before it'll hold a curve without snapping back. It's not a defect — it's a tight tolerance issue — but it's an annoyance.

Grant's vest is sculpted as one piece with the torso. That gives a clean look but restricts arm movement at the shoulder. You can't get a true "arms crossed" pose, and reaching movements are limited to about 45 degrees laterally. If you're an action-pose person, this will frustrate you.

And while the extras are nice, they're minimal. No JP3-specific accessories — no eggs, no plane debris, no alternate head sculpt. For a set this premium, that feels like a missed opportunity.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The diorama builder** who needs a solid Grant and a raptor that can hold dynamic stalking poses.
- **The completionist collector** filling out the Hammond Collection roster — this is essential.
- **The gift-giver** shopping for a Jurassic fan who's aged past "crash and bash" toys but still wants something displayable.

**Skip it if** you're buying for a young kid who'll play rough — the paint apps and joints deserve gentle handling, and the stiff tail will likely frustrate a child.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the raptor fit with other Hammond Collection figures?**
Yes. Scale is consistent across the line — it pairs perfectly with the JP3 Grant or any other human figures in the series.

**Q: Are the swap hands easy to change?**
They're standard peg-style. Firm but not fragile. A quick soak in warm water makes them easier if you're having trouble.

**Q: Is the hat removable?**
Yes, and it fits snugly. It won't fall off during posing, but you can take it off for a different look.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if** you value screen-accurate sculpting, premium paint, and a raptor that can actually hold a terrifying pose. This is collector-grade quality at a reasonable price.

**Skip it if** you need maximum arm range or you're looking for a rugged kids' toy. For the collector's shelf or the careful display, this is one of the best two-packs Mattel has released.
