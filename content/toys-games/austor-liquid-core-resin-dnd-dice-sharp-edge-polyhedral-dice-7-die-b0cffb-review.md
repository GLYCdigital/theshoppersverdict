## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs:

**The sharp edges are genuinely sharp.** If you're a dice-tower user or have young kids at the table, these will scratch surfaces and can be uncomfortable to roll bare-handed. You'll want a rolling tray.

**Some sets have inconsistent liquid flow.** A handful of reviewers mentioned the liquid in certain dice (particularly the d4 and d%) moves more slowly than others in the same set. It's not a defect — just variation in manufacturing.

**The storage box is basic.** It's a simple cardboard-style container, not a premium dice vault. If presentation matters, you'll want to upgrade your storage.

**The sharp-edge style isn't for everyone.** If you prefer soft rounded dice that feel like river stones, this geometric profile will feel aggressive.
