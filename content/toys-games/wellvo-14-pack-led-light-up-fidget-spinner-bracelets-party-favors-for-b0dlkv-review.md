## Build and Performance: Surprisingly Functional

The spinner mechanism is the standout feature. It's mounted on a small bearing that actually rotates smoothly, not the stiff, useless spinners you see on cheap knockoffs. Kids can spin it with a flick of the wrist, and it keeps momentum for a solid 8-10 seconds. The bracelet band itself is flexible silicone-type plastic, comfortable enough for a few hours of wear.
