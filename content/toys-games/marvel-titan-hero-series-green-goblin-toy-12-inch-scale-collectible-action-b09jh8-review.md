The Green Goblin has been Spider-Man's archenemy for over 60 years, and Hasbro's Titan Hero Series finally gives him the shelf presence he deserves. This 12-inch-scale figure isn't a delicate display piece — it's a chunky, durable action figure built for actual play. At 4.8 stars from 240 ratings, it's clearly hitting the mark. But is it right for *your* collection? Let's break down what you're actually getting.

**What's in the package**

You get the Green Goblin figure himself, his signature glider, and one pumpkin bomb accessory. The figure stands roughly 12 inches tall, which puts him right in scale with other Titan Hero Series releases like Spider-Man and Venom. The glider is a single molded piece with a clip mechanism — no assembly required, which is refreshing compared to some Hasbro products that need a PhD to put together.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The first thing you'll notice handling this figure is the weight. The plastic is thick and dense, not the flimsy hollow stuff you find on cheaper toys. The sculpt captures the classic Ditko-era design: the purple tunic, the scaly green skin, the maniacal grin, and that iconic jagged hood. The paint application is clean — no sloppy overspray on the face or armor details. As shown in the product image, the glider clips securely to the figure's back, and the clip holds tight enough that you can pose him in a flying stance without him crashing to the floor.

The articulation is where you need to check your expectations. You get five points: neck, two shoulders, and two hips. That's it. No elbows, no knees, no wrist swivels. For a 12-inch figure aimed at kids 4 and up, this is standard — the Titan Hero line prioritizes durability over posability. But if you're an adult collector hoping to recreate the "Goblin glider bomb" scene from the comics, you'll be disappointed.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Parents and collectors alike are surprisingly enthusiastic about this one. A common thread in reviews: "My son hasn't put it down since it arrived — it's survived being thrown across the room multiple times." Another buyer noted, "Finally a Green Goblin figure that doesn't feel like an afterthought. The detail on the face is genuinely good." The durability factor comes up repeatedly — this is a toy that can take a beating and keep looking presentable.

**Where it falls short**

The limited articulation is the obvious tradeoff, and it's worth being honest about. Older kids who've experienced Marvel Legends figures will find this frustratingly stiff. The pumpkin bomb is also a single, solid piece — it's chunky and easy to lose, and there's nowhere to store it on the figure when not in use. One reviewer mentioned, "Great figure, but I've already bought two replacement pumpkin bombs off eBay." Also, if you're hoping for a voice module or light-up features, those don't exist here. This is a straightforward, no-frills action figure.

**Who should buy this**

- **Parents of Spider-Man obsessed kids (ages 4-8):** This is the ideal pick. It's big, durable, and won't break when inevitably dropped down the stairs.
- **Titan Hero Series collectors:** This fills a major gap in the line. You can now complete the classic Spider-Man villain lineup.
- **Budget-conscious gift-givers:** At this price point with this build quality, it's excellent value.

**Skip it if**

- You're an adult collector focused on articulation and posing — look at Marvel Legends instead.
- Your kid is over 9 and expects more play features — they'll find this too basic.

**FAQ**

**Is this figure compatible with other Titan Hero Series accessories?**
Yes. The glider clip and back peg are standard across the line, so you can swap accessories between figures if you have others.

**What age range is this designed for?**
Hasbro rates it for ages 4 and up. The lack of small parts and sturdy construction make it appropriate for younger kids, though the glider clip requires a bit of hand strength.

**Does it scale well with the Marvel Legends 6-inch figures?**
It's dramatically larger — about double the height. If you're mixing lines, it'll look out of place. Keep it with other Titan Hero figures.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a durable, good-looking Green Goblin for a young Marvel fan or to complete your Titan Hero lineup. **Skip it if** articulation and posability are deal-breakers for you.

This is a solid 4.6-star product. It does exactly what it promises — a chunky, playable, character-accurate figure — and does it well. The limitations are inherent to the line, not flaws in execution. For what it is, it's a home run.
