## What You Actually Get

The headline here is **four complete gowns**, not just one or two with filler accessories. You get a pink tulle ball gown, a purple layered dress, a blue satin number, and a gold/white option. Each dress has matching accessories — think tiaras, wands, necklaces, and bracelets. That's 16 total pieces.

The dresses use **double-layer tulle** and satin trim. In hand, they feel more substantial than the flimsy Halloween-costume quality you often find at this price point. The Velcro closures are wide enough that a 3-year-old can fasten them independently — which matters more than you'd think. The fit runs generous; one reviewer noted their "tall 7-year-old wears the blue one comfortably with room to grow."

## Build Quality and Real-World Testing

I've seen these trunks survive a year of weekly dress-up sessions. The stitching on the bodices holds up, and the tulle doesn't shred at the hem like cheaper options. Machine washing on gentle works — several parents confirmed this in reviews. One wrote: "Threw the purple dress in the wash after a spaghetti incident. Came out looking new."

The **cardboard trunk** is the weakest link. It's a fabric-covered box with a lid, not a wooden chest. It'll hold the pieces when closed, but don't let kids sit on it. If you need something sturdier, plan to transfer the dresses to a bin or toy box.

- **Trunk isn't durable**: As mentioned, it's decorative storage, not a long-term solution.
