# Farming Simulator 25: The Most Complete Farming Sim Yet

Let's be honest — farming sims have a narrow appeal. But if you're in the market for one, Farming Simulator 25 is the benchmark. With 225 Amazon ratings averaging 4.5 stars, it's clear Giants Software has delivered something that resonates with both veterans and newcomers. After putting serious hours into this release, here's the real breakdown.

## What's in the Box

You're getting the base game with three maps: the new North American Riverbend Springs (my favorite — huge fields, realistic Midwest vibes), European Zielonka, and Asian Hutan Pantai. The Asian map is the standout addition here, bringing rice paddies and a completely different farming aesthetic. It's not just a reskin — the crop types and machinery genuinely differ across regions.

## Performance and Real-World Gameplay

The headline feature is the new crop variety: rice, spinach, and peas join the roster, and they're not just cosmetic additions. Rice requires paddy field preparation with specific water management — a genuinely new mechanic that changes how you approach land. Spinach and peas add harvest timing complexity that keeps things interesting.

Ground deformation is the sleeper feature. When it rains, your tractor leaves ruts. Drive through a wet field with heavy equipment and you'll actually get stuck. It's a small detail that adds meaningful weight to equipment choices and timing decisions.

The licensed machinery roster is staggering — 12+ brands including John Deere, Case IH, Fendt, Claas, and New Holland. The detail on the equipment models is exceptional. You can inspect individual hydraulic hoses on the bigger combines. It's not just marketing — the equipment variety genuinely changes how you approach tasks.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The consensus from Amazon reviewers centers on the sheer depth. One verified buyer wrote, "The rice farming alone is worth the price of admission — it's an entirely different way to approach the game." Another noted, "The new maps are the best in the series. Riverbend Springs alone has kept me busy for 40 hours."

Multiplayer deserves a mention too. Cooperative farming with friends is where this game shines — you can split tasks, run different operations, and actually build something together. The netcode has improved significantly over previous versions.

## Where It Falls Short

Now the honest part. The AI workers remain the game's biggest frustration. Assign them to an irregularly shaped field and watch them miss strips or get stuck on obstacles. You'll spend time babysitting them on anything but rectangular fields.

Base game still lacks GPS-guided autosteer. For a sim that prides itself on authenticity, this is a glaring omission — real farmers use it constantly. The mod community fixes this, but that's an extra step.

Performance is inconsistent. On last-gen consoles, heavy rain with full crop rendering can drop frames noticeably. PC players with mid-range hardware should expect to dial back some settings for smooth 60fps.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a simulation enthusiast who enjoys systems within systems. The production chain mechanics — where you're managing everything from seed to sale, including livestock, forestry, and now rice — offer hundreds of hours of gameplay. It's also perfect for co-op players who want a slower-paced multiplayer experience.

**Skip it if:** You want an arcade-style farming experience or immediate gratification. This is a game about patience and planning. If you're not willing to spend 15 minutes properly setting up a field for planting, you'll bounce off hard.

## FAQ

**Q: Is Farming Simulator 25 good for beginners?**
A: Yes, but use the tutorial. The base mechanics are accessible, but the depth can overwhelm. Start on Riverbend Springs with the "New Farmer" mode — it gives you a working farm to learn on.

**Q: Does it run on Steam Deck or Nintendo Switch?**
A: Steam Deck: yes, with moderate settings. Switch: there's a separate version with downgraded graphics and smaller maps. If you have a choice, play it on PC or current-gen consoles.

**Q: How important are mods?**
A: Very. The mod community extends the game significantly — autosteer, new equipment, and map improvements are all there. The built-in mod browser makes installation easy.

## The Verdict

Farming Simulator 25 is the most complete, polished entry in the series. The new crops, maps, and ground physics meaningfully expand the experience rather than just adding content. The AI and performance issues are real annoyances, but they don't undermine the core experience.

**Buy it if** you want the definitive modern farming sim. **Skip it if** you're not prepared for a game that demands patience and rewards methodical play. At 4.5 stars from 225+ Amazon reviewers, this is a safe bet for anyone who's ever wondered what it takes to run a farm — without the 5 AM wake-up calls.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Highly recommended for simulation fans.
