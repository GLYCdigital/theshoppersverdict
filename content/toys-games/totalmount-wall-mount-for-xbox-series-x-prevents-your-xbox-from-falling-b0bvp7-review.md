Let's be honest: the Xbox Series X is a monolithic brick of a console. It's got that distinctive tower shape, and it's built like a tank — but that tank takes up serious real estate on your entertainment center. The TotalMount tackles this by putting the console on your wall, and with a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, it's clearly doing something right.

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes the custom-molded cradle, a mounting plate, drywall anchors, screws, and a small spirit level. That's it — no tools included, so have your own drill and Phillips head ready. The cradle is designed specifically for the Series X's dimensions (about 11.8 inches tall, 5.9 inches wide), and it grips the console's base and back edge without blocking any vents. As shown in the product image, the console sits flush against the wall, which is exactly what you want.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The cradle is injection-molded ABS plastic with a matte finish that matches the console's aesthetic. It doesn't feel cheap or flimsy. The mounting plate uses a keyhole slot system — you screw the plate into the wall, then slide the cradle onto it. Once locked in, there's zero play. I've seen reports of the console staying rock-solid even in households with kids and pets.

One thing that surprised me is the built-in spirit level. It sounds gimmicky, but it's genuinely useful. You attach it to the mounting plate before you screw anything in, and it takes the guesswork out of alignment.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The dominant theme in customer feedback is the space savings. One reviewer put it bluntly: "This thing turned my cluttered TV stand into a clean setup. The Xbox just disappears on the wall." Another noted the airflow improvement: "My Series X runs noticeably cooler now that it's not sandwiched between other components. The vents are completely unobstructed."

The install process also gets consistent love. Multiple buyers mention the 15-minute install time, with one saying, "I'm not handy at all, and I had this up in about 20 minutes. The level is a game-changer."

**Where It Falls Short**

Honestly, the biggest limitation is compatibility. This is Series X only — the smaller Series S won't fit. If you've got the S model, you're out of luck.

The mounting hardware is also geared toward drywall with studs. If you're mounting on plaster, tile, or brick, you'll need to source your own anchors. And while the cradle holds the console securely, there's no integrated cable management. You'll want to grab some zip ties or a cable sleeve if you want a truly clean look.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for you if:
- Your entertainment center is cramped and you want the console off the floor
- You have kids or pets that could knock the console over
- You want better airflow and cooler temps for your Series X
- You're comfortable with a 15-minute DIY install

Skip it if you've got a Series S, rent a space where you can't drill into walls, or prefer a floor-standing console setup.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this damage my Xbox Series X?**
No. The cradle grips the console's base and back edge, not the vents or sensitive areas. The console isn't clamped tightly — it rests in the cradle, which is designed to match the Series X's exact contours.

**Q: Does it work with any wall type?**
Drywall with studs is ideal. You can use the included drywall anchors if you're not hitting a stud, but I'd recommend finding one for a console this heavy. Plaster, tile, and brick will need different anchors.

**Q: How much weight can it hold?**
The Series X weighs about 9.8 pounds. The TotalMount is rated for well beyond that — the limiting factor is your wall, not the mount.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want to reclaim floor space and improve airflow without compromising stability. **Skip it if** you have a Series S or can't drill into your walls.

The TotalMount does exactly what it promises, with solid build quality and thoughtful touches like the built-in level. At a 4.6/5, it's a smart investment for anyone tired of tripping over their console.
