You're looking at a 3.1-star average across 155 ratings, and that's probably fair. Here's the thing about the Glitter Dumpling Squishy box: it's not a bad product, but it's a product that sets expectations it can't quite meet.

**What's actually in the box**

You get 10 individually wrapped dumpling-shaped squishies. Each one measures about 2 inches across — smaller than the product images suggest, so check those dimensions before buying. The set is advertised as containing 10 colors, and it does, but here's the catch: the colors are random. You might get a nice spread of pastels, or you might get three pink ones, two purple, and only one of each other color. One reviewer noted, "I bought these for my daughter's birthday party favors and got four orange ones in a single box. Cute, but frustrating."

The dumplings come in that classic polyurethane squishy foam. The texture is soft with a slow rise — squeeze one flat and it takes a few seconds to puff back to its dumpling shape. That's exactly what you want from a squishy.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The foam quality is genuinely solid for the price point. These aren't the crumbling, sticky squishies you find in dollar store bins. They hold their shape after repeated squishing, and the glitter is mixed into the foam rather than painted on top, so it doesn't flake off within the first week. That's a real plus.

The scent is a light, fruity mix — think generic candy, not specifically peach or strawberry. It's pleasant enough, but it does fade. After two weeks of daily handling, one buyer reported, "The smell is basically gone. They still squish fine, but the scent was half the appeal for my kid."

The biggest durability issue isn't the foam — it's the surface. The dumplings have a slightly tacky finish that attracts lint and pet hair. Keep them off carpet and out of pockets.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The slow-rise quality gets consistent positive mentions. "My son has destroyed every squishy we've bought in a week. These are still intact after a month," one parent wrote. The individual wrapping also gets credit — it makes them feel more substantial as gifts, and the reveal moment works well for kids.

**Where it falls short**

The random assortment is the main complaint, and it's a legitimate one. There's no way to request specific colors, and duplicates are common. For a product marketed as "10 colors," getting three of the same shade feels like a bait-and-switch.

The size is the second issue. At 2 inches, these are smaller than the plump dumplings shown in the product photos. They're pocket-sized, not palm-sized.

**Who should buy this**

- **Parents of kids 5-10**: These are sturdy enough for rough handling, and the mystery aspect makes them fun to open.
- **Party favor planners**: Individually wrapped and cheap enough to distribute, just be ready for duplicate colors.
- **Squishy collectors**: Good value for the foam quality, but don't expect a curated color set.

**Skip it if** you want specific colors, need larger squishies, or the scent is the main selling point for you — it won't last.

**FAQ**

**Are these safe for young children?** They're recommended for ages 3+. The foam is soft and won't hurt if thrown, but the glitter can come loose if bitten. Supervise toddlers.

**Do they have a strong smell?** Initially, yes — a light fruity scent. It fades within 2-3 weeks. If you're sensitive to synthetic fragrances, air them out for a day first.

**Can you return them if you get duplicate colors?** Amazon's standard return policy applies, but since the assortment is random, duplicates aren't considered a defect. Don't expect a replacement for color repeats.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a solid, budget-friendly squishy that survives actual play and you're okay with randomized colors.
**Skip it if** color variety matters, you want larger squishies, or scent longevity is a dealbreaker.

**Rating: 3.3/5** — Good foam quality and durability, undermined by the random color system and smaller-than-expected size. Worth it as a cheap stress-relief toy or party favor, not as a collectible set.
