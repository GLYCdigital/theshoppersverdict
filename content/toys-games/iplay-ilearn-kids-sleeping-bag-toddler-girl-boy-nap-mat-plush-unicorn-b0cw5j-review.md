## Where It Falls Short

Honest tradeoffs, because every product has them:

- **No carry handle.** For a product designed to shuttle between home and school, this is a miss. You'll need to buy a separate tote or bag. The folded size is roughly 20" x 14" x 6" — it fits in a standard backpack but won't clip to one.
- **Size is for little kids.** The mat measures about 50" long when open. That fits most 2–5 year olds comfortably, but a tall kindergartner or a 6-year-old will have feet hanging off. If your child is on the older end of preschool, measure before buying.
- **The blanket is thin.** It's fine for a climate-controlled room, but if your daycare keeps the thermostat low, you may want to layer a separate blanket on top.
