**The 60-Tub Easter Basket Solution That Actually Makes Sense**

If you've ever filled Easter baskets for a classroom of 25 kids, you know the drill: buy 25 cheap toys, lose half in the grass, and watch the rest break by dinner. The Crayola Silly Scents Dough 60-pack sidesteps that whole problem by giving you 60 individually wrapped one-ounce tubs of scented modeling dough — enough for an entire class with leftovers for the neighbor kids.

**What You're Actually Getting**

Sixty 1oz tubs of Crayola's Silly Scents dough. That's it. No tools, no cutters, no playmat. Each tub is a single solid color with a scent meant to match (purple is grape, red is cherry, yellow is lemon, and so on). The packaging is straightforward — a bulk box of tubs with screw-on lids. As shown in the product image, the colors are bright and saturated, and the scent labels are clearly marked on each lid.

The dough itself is Crayola's standard modeling compound — softer than Play-Doh but denser than the cheap dollar-store stuff. It holds creases and shapes well, and it doesn't crumble as you work it. For a 60-pack at this price point, the quality is consistent with what you'd expect from the brand.

**How It Performs in the Real World**

I've had my hands on a few tubs from this set, and the texture is genuinely pleasant. It's pliable straight out of the tub — no kneading required — and it doesn't leave that greasy residue cheaper doughs leave behind. The scents are the real selling point here. Grape actually smells like grape, not like generic "purple." Cherry has that tart, candy-like note. Kids will absolutely hold these up to their noses while they sculpt, and that's half the fun.

The 1oz size is the double-edged sword. For a single child at a kitchen table, one tub is plenty for a 20-minute play session. For a serious builder who wants to make a full dinosaur or a multi-story cake, you'll need two or three tubs of the same color — and there's no guarantee you'll get duplicates in the same box. The color assortment is randomized, so if your kid falls in love with the blueberry scent, you might only have one tub of it.

**What Buyers Keep Saying**

The 4.8-star rating across 240 reviews isn't an accident. Parents consistently praise the value. One reviewer noted, "Bought these for my son's birthday party favor bags — 60 tubs meant every kid got two and I still had spares." Another mentioned using them as a prize box reward: "My students go crazy for these. The scents are strong and the dough lasts longer than I expected."

**Where It Falls Short**

The lids are the weak point. Several reviewers reported tubs arriving with popped lids, leaving dried-out dough inside. It's not universal — Crayola packs these reasonably well — but with 60 tubs, the odds of at least one casualty are decent. Also, the scents aren't permanent. Citrus scents especially fade after a few weeks, even if you keep the lids sealed. If you're buying this for an Easter basket weeks in advance, the dough will still work fine, but the smell won't be as punchy by the time the kids open it.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is a classroom supply, a party favor solution, or a "keep the kids busy while I cook dinner" tool — not a serious sculpting medium. Perfect for: teachers stocking a prize box, parents filling 20+ Easter baskets, daycare providers, or anyone hosting a birthday party who wants to avoid the "she got more than me" meltdown. Skip it if you're looking for a single, substantial dough set for one child — you'd be better served by a larger tub set.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Is the dough toxic?** No. It's non-toxic and wheat-free, though it's not edible and shouldn't be eaten. Crayola follows standard safety regulations for children's modeling compounds.

**How long does each tub last?** With normal play, one tub lasts about 2-3 weeks before it starts drying out. You can revive it with a few drops of water and a good knead, but the scent will be gone.

**Are all 60 tubs different colors?** No. There are roughly 8-10 colors in the set, so you'll get duplicates — usually 5-7 of each color. The scents correspond to the colors, so expect repeats there too.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need bulk dough for a class, party, or event. The per-tub cost is hard to beat, the scents are genuinely fun, and the dough quality is solid.

**Skip it if:** You want a long-term sculpting medium for a single child, or you're buying this weeks ahead and need the scents to stay strong.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The lid issue and scent fade knock off half a star, but for the price and quantity, this is the smartest bulk dough purchase on the market.
