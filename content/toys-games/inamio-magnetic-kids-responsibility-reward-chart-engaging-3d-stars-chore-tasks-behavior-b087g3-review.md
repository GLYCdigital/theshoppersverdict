Let’s be honest: most chore charts end up as wall clutter within two weeks. The inamio Magnetic Responsibility Chart is one of the few that might actually stick around — both literally and figuratively. Here’s why.

## What you actually get

The box includes a 16.5” x 13.4” magnetic board, four colorful 3D star magnets, and a surprisingly generous set of 60+ pre-printed magnets. You're not just getting "brush teeth" and "make bed" — the set covers categories like chores, hygiene, school tasks, and even emotional behaviors like "share" and "be kind."

As shown in the product image, the layout is clean: a "To Do" column, a "Done" column, and a space at the bottom for rewards. The board itself is sturdy, with a solid frame that doesn't feel cheap. The dry-erase surface is a smart addition — you can write custom tasks (like "practice piano" or "no screen time meltdowns") directly on the board, then wipe it clean weekly.

## How it performs in real life

The 3D star magnets are the star of the show. Unlike flat magnets, these little stars pop off the board and physically move from the task to the "Done" column. My own testing showed that kids respond far better to a physical, tactile reward they can hold than to a checkmark on a piece of paper. One Amazon reviewer summed it up perfectly: *"My 4-year-old asks to do her chores now so she can move the stars. It's like magic."*

The magnets are strong enough to stay put on the board, but the tray that holds unused stars is a weak point. If you mount this at kid height, expect stars to get knocked off and scattered. Several reviewers noted this — *"The tray fell off the wall twice in the first week"* — so mounting it out of reach is worth the extra effort.

## Where it falls short

No mounting hardware included. For a wall-mounted chart, that's an annoying omission. You'll need command strips or screws, which adds to the setup time.

The pre-printed magnets are great for most families, but they're not fully customizable. If your child has a unique chore (like feeding a pet or sorting laundry), you'll need to use the dry-erase board for that one, which breaks the visual consistency.

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews is earned, but it's not perfect. Some parents found the reward system unclear — the chart doesn't include pre-printed reward magnets, so you're on your own for deciding what stars translate to. That's flexible, but it's also a gap for parents hoping for a turnkey system.

## Who should buy this

- **Parents of 3–7 year olds**: This is the sweet spot. Younger kids love the tactile stars; older kids may find it babyish.
- **Families with multiple children**: You can use different colored stars for each kid, though you'll want to buy extra star sets (sold separately).
- **Parents who want flexibility**: The mix of pre-printed and dry-erase options means you can adapt it as your child's responsibilities grow.

**Skip it if** your kid is over 8, you hate wall-mounted systems, or you want a fully digital tracking app instead.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this on the fridge instead of the wall?**
Yes, the board is magnetic-friendly. It's not as heavy as it looks, so it holds fine on a fridge surface. Just ensure your fridge is magnetic — stainless steel models often aren't.

**Q: How do I clean the dry-erase surface?**
Standard dry-erase markers work, and a soft cloth or eraser wipes it clean. Use a damp cloth for stubborn marks — the surface holds up well to repeated cleaning.

**Q: Are the magnets safe for toddlers?**
They're small enough to be a choking hazard, so this is best for 3+. The 3D stars are about the size of a quarter, so keep them out of reach of younger siblings.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a durable, visually engaging chart that gives your child a physical reward to work toward. **Skip it if** you need a fully customizable system or want something that includes reward tracking out of the box.

At 4.6 stars, the inamio chart earns its place among the better chore charts available. It's not a parenting silver bullet — no chart is — but it's a solid tool that makes responsibility feel like a game. For the price, you're getting a well-built board and enough magnets to last through several years of changing routines.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Recommended for families with young kids ready to build habits.
