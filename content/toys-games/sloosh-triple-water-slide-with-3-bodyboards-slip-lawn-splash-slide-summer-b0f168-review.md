**Opening: Why this one's different**  

Most slip-and-slides are a single sad strip of plastic that bunches up after two runs. The **Sloosh Triple Water Slide** solves that with three 30-foot lanes and three bodyboards included—so kids can actually race side-by-side instead of taking turns. At 4.2/5 stars from 210 ratings, it's not perfect, but it's one of the few backyard slides that delivers genuine repeat-play value rather than a one-afternoon novelty.  

**What's in the box**  

You get three separate PVC slide lanes (each roughly 30 feet long), three foam bodyboards, six ground stakes, and a hose connector that splits water evenly across all three lanes. No pump, no inflation, no assembly beyond staking down the corners and attaching the hose. The material is a heavy-duty PVC—noticeably thicker than the cheap vinyl you'll find on $20 slides from big-box stores. As shown in the product image, the three lanes are color-coded, which makes organizing races with multiple kids dead simple.  

**Performance and real-world use**  

The key to this slide's performance is water distribution. The built-in sprinkler system runs the length of each lane, and when your hose has decent pressure (at least 40-50 PSI), all three lanes stay slick consistently. On flat, well-watered grass, kids hit serious speed—think belly-flop acceleration, not just a gentle glide. The bodyboards help younger kids (4-8) get going faster and protect their chests from grass rash.  

But here's the honest tradeoff: this slide *demands* a flat, smooth lawn. Hills, bumps, or even mildly uneven ground will slow riders to a crawl and create dry spots. And if your hose pressure is weak, the middle lane often gets less water than the outer two—a common complaint in the reviews. One parent noted, *"Works great on our level backyard, but my sister's sloped yard was a total bust—kids just slid 5 feet and stopped."*  

**What buyers consistently praise**  

The three-lane design is the star. Multiple reviewers mention that it ended sibling arguments because everyone slides at once. The durability also gets consistent shout-outs—the PVC holds up to sharp grass, repeated summer use, and kids who treat it like a wrestling mat. One verified purchase said, *"Survived a 6-year-old's birthday party with 11 kids going nonstop for three hours. No rips, no blown seams."* Setup is another winner: stakes are sturdy, the hose connector twists on securely, and you're ready in under 10 minutes.  

**Where it falls short**  

The included bodyboards are the weakest link. They're thin foam that bends under adults and feels cheap even for kids over 10. You're better off letting older kids slide directly on the plastic—it's faster anyway. The stakes are also a bit short for soft or sandy soil; on loose ground, the lanes can shift mid-run. And cleanup is mildly annoying—draining all three lanes takes a few minutes, and they need to dry fully before folding to prevent mildew.  

**Who should buy this**  

- **Families with multiple kids ages 4-12**: The triple-lane racing is a genuine winner for parties or summer afternoons.  
- **Anyone with a flat, decent-sized lawn**: If your yard is level and your hose pressure is solid, this is the best-value triple slide under $100.  
- **Skip it if** you have a sloped yard, weak water pressure, or only one child who'd use it—a single-lane slide is cheaper and sufficient.  

**FAQ**  

**Q: Does it need a pump or inflation?**  
A: No. It's a flat PVC slide that uses water from your hose. Just stake it down, attach the hose, and turn on the water.  

**Q: Can adults use it?**  
A: Yes, but the bodyboards are too flimsy for adult weight. Slide directly on the plastic—it holds up fine, just expect less padding on impact.  

**Q: How long does it take to set up and take down?**  
A: Setup is about 8-10 minutes including staking. Take-down takes longer because you need to drain each lane fully before folding—plan for 15 minutes.  

**The Verdict**  

**Buy it if** you want a durable, multi-kid water slide that ends turn-taking arguments and holds up to a full summer of abuse. **Skip it if** your yard is uneven or your hose pressure is weak—you'll be frustrated by slow, patchy slides.  

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A genuinely fun, well-built product held back only by flimsy bodyboards and a pickiness about lawn conditions. For flat-yard families with two or more kids, it's an easy recommendation.
