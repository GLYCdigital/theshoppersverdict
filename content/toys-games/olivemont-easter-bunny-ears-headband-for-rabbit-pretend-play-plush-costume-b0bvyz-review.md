If you've ever watched a toddler try to keep flimsy costume ears upright for more than 30 seconds, you know the struggle. The Olivemont Easter bunny ears headband solves that specific problem with wire-reinforced plush that actually stands at attention. After 225 Amazon ratings averaging 4.5 stars, this isn't a random impulse buy — it's the rabbit ear headband that keeps showing up in Easter baskets and dress-up bins.

**What you actually get**

The package includes one headband with two generously sized ears — roughly 9 inches tall from base to tip. The ears are covered in a soft, flocked polyester that feels closer to a stuffed animal than typical costume fabric. Inside each ear runs a flexible wire, which is the single most important design decision here. You can bend the ears upright, floppy, or at a jaunty angle, and they stay put.

The headband itself is a 0.5-inch wide plastic band wrapped in the same plush material. It's adjustable via a sliding mechanism rather than notches, which means you can fine-tune the fit. The product image shows the ears in a classic upright position on a child model, but real-world users report bending them into floppy bunny mode for younger kids — that flexibility is a genuine feature, not a gimmick.

**How it holds up in real use**

I tested this with a 4-year-old who wore it daily for three weeks of Easter-themed preschool activities. The wire inside the ears survived repeated bending without creasing or snapping — a common failure point on cheaper alternatives. The stitching where the ears meet the headband held firm, which matters because that's where most costume ears separate after a few tugs.

The plush surface is genuinely soft against skin. No scratchy seams or rough edges around the forehead contact points. One parent in the Amazon reviews noted, "My daughter forgot she was wearing it after 10 minutes," and I'd agree — comfort is this product's quiet strength.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the 225 ratings, three themes dominate. First, durability: multiple reviewers mention surviving "rough toddler play" and "daily wear for a month" without damage. Second, the adjustable fit — parents report using it on both a 2-year-old and a 7-year-old with the same headband. **Where it falls short**

Honest tradeoffs exist. The white ear tips pick up dirt and scuffs quickly — one reviewer noted their child's ears looked "gray after a week of outdoor play." Cleaning is hand-wash only; tossing it in the machine will likely warp the wire. Also, on very smooth or fine hair, the headband can slide forward. The package includes a small silicone grip strip, but you'll need to position it correctly or the ears end up tilting over the forehead mid-play. Finally, it's not a full costume — this is strictly an ear accessory, so manage expectations for complete rabbit ensembles.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for parents of toddlers and young children (2–8) who need a durable dress-up accessory for Easter, school plays, or pretend play. It also works well for adults needing a quick, comfortable bunny ear option for events — the adjustable band fits most head sizes up to about 22 inches. It's a poor fit for anyone seeking a full costume or a disposable single-use prop; this is built to last, and the price reflects that quality.
