Here’s where this purse earns its keep. The accessories are designed with toddler logic in mind — they’re chunky, rounded, and sized for small hands. ## Where It Falls Short

The mirror scratches easily. It’s not a dealbreaker, but if your kid is rough with toys, it’ll look worn within weeks. One reviewer noted: *“The mirror got scratched up pretty fast, but my daughter doesn’t care — she still uses it constantly.”*

The smaller pieces — lipstick, compact, and the cards — are easy to lose. They’re not tiny enough to be a choking hazard for kids 3 and up, but for younger toddlers, you’ll want to supervise. Several reviews mention losing pieces within a month. That’s the nature of sets like this, but it’s worth knowing.

There’s also no sound on the phone beyond the light-up screen. Some kids expect a ringtone or buttons that beep. This isn’t a dealbreaker for most buyers, but if your child is used to electronic toys, the phone may disappoint.
