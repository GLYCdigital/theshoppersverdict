If you've ever handed out slime at a birthday party, you know the drill: sticky fingers, stained carpet, and a dozen tiny containers with questionable lids. The Mermaid Slime Kit Party Favors aims to fix that with pre-sealed cups in a bulk pack. I tested these with three kids (ages 4, 7, and 9) over two weeks of regular play. Here's the real story.

**What's in the Box**

You're getting a set of individual slime cups — the exact count depends on which pack size you order, but the bulk configuration is the draw here. Each cup comes sealed with a mermaid-themed lid. The slime itself is the classic clear/translucent base with glitter and color swirls in teal, pink, and purple. No tools, no charms, no add-ins. It's just slime in cups, which is refreshingly straightforward.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The texture is where this kit earns its keep. The slime is genuinely stretchy — it pulls into long ribbons without snapping, and it doesn't immediately glue itself to every surface it touches. Compared to dollar-store slime that turns into a puddle of goo within ten minutes, this holds its consistency much better. One reviewer noted, "My kids played with these for over an hour and the slime didn't dry out or get sticky like other brands we've tried." That matches my testing — the 7-year-old stretched hers into a rope that she then wrapped around her wrist like a bracelet. It held shape.

The glitter is mixed throughout rather than just sprinkled on top, which means it doesn't shed everywhere. That's a small but significant detail for parents who've dealt with glitter-bomb aftermath.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The party-favor angle is the top compliment across reviews. Parents love that the cups are individually sealed — no one's fighting over who gets the "good" one. One Amazon customer wrote, "Perfect for my daughter's mermaid-themed birthday party. Each kid got one on the way out and there were zero arguments." Another mentioned using them as classroom rewards, which makes sense given the bulk pricing.

**Where It Falls Short**

Be honest about the quantity. Each cup holds about 2 ounces of slime. For a 4-year-old, that's plenty. For a 9-year-old who likes to make elaborate creations, it's gone in a single session. The scent is the other issue — it's a sweet, candy-like fragrance that some kids love but several parents found overpowering. One review said, "The smell is strong. It's not unpleasant, but it definitely lingers on hands." That's accurate.

The lids are also not airtight. If a cup is left open — which will happen — the slime will dry out within a day or two. Store them upright and sealed when not in use.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is a party favor or classroom prize product first, a toy second. If you're hosting a mermaid-themed birthday party, a school event, or you're a teacher building a reward bin, this is a strong pick. If you're looking for a single high-quality slime for one kid's extended play, you'd be better served by a larger individual container from a specialty slime shop.

**FAQ**

*Is this slime sticky?*
It's moderately non-sticky out of the cup. After a few minutes of play, it gets slightly tacky but not unmanageable. Wash hands after — the scent needs soap to come off.

*Can you mix the colors?*
Yes, and kids will. They blend into a murky purple-brown that's less appealing but still playable. If you want to keep colors separate, set that expectation upfront.

*Is the slime safe for young kids?*
It's non-toxic and safe for ages 3+, but it's not edible — supervise any child who still mouths objects. The glitter can also get in eyes, so keep it away from toddlers.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need a bulk supply of decent-quality slime for party favors, classroom rewards, or a group activity where individual portions matter more than sheer quantity.

**Skip it if:** You want a single, generous slime for one kid to play with repeatedly, or if artificial scents are a dealbreaker in your house.

The Mermaid Slime Kit Party Favors delivers exactly what it promises: stretchy, colorful, sealed slime cups at a fair per-unit price. It's not premium craft slime, but it's not priced like it either. For the party circuit, this is a solid 4.2-star product that does the job without drama.
