POP MART has built a cult following for its designer toys, and the PUCKY Beanie Bubble UP Series continues that tradition. This blind box plush pendant sits in a sweet spot: collectible enough for hardcore fans, functional enough for casual buyers who just want something cute on their bag. ## What's Actually in the Box

You get one sealed blind box containing a plush PUCKY character wearing a beanie, plus two attachment options: a ball chain (great for keychains) and a lobster clasp (better for clipping onto bags or belt loops). The plush itself measures roughly 10cm/4 inches — compact but not pocket-lint tiny. The packaging is typical POP MART quality: sturdy cardstock with the signature mystery-box aesthetic.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The plush material is genuinely soft — not the scratchy synthetic stuff you find on cheap keychains. The embroidery on the face and beanie details is clean and precise, which matters when you're paying premium blind-box prices. I've clipped mine to a daily tote for two weeks; no loose threads, no fading, and the clasp hasn't bent out of shape. The beanies come in different colors and patterns across the six designs in this series, so there's real variety if you're collecting.

One thing that surprised me: the plush has a bit of weight to it — not flimsy. That's a plus for durability, though it might dangle a bit heavy on a delicate lanyard.

If you're buying specifically to get one particular PUCKY design, you might be disappointed — duplicates are a real risk with six possible variants.
