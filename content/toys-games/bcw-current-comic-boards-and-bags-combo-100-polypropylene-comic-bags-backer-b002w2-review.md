If you've been stacking your recent pulls in a shoebox or—worse—rubber-banding them together, stop what you're doing and pay attention. The BCW Current Comic Boards and Bags Combo is the unglamorous workhorse that keeps $4.99 floppies from becoming $0.50 dollar-bin fodder. At 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. Here's what that something is.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get 100 polypropylene bags and 100 white backing boards, both sized for "Current" comics—that's the 6.625" x 10.375" standard that most modern titles ship in. The bags are 2 mil thick, which is the sweet spot: thick enough to resist tears at the spine, thin enough that you won't struggle to get the comic in without creasing the cover. The boards are 24pt, which is substantial enough to keep a comic rigid in a long box but won't add so much bulk that you run out of space halfway through your collection.

## Real-World Performance

I've bagged everything from $2 back-issue bin finds to a signed first printing of a current run, and the BCW combo handles both without complaint. The bags slide on easily—no fighting the film, no static cling making you wrestle with the opening. The flap folds over cleanly and takes tape well without the adhesive pulling the polypropylene when you eventually rebag.

The boards do exactly what they're supposed to do: provide rigid backing that prevents spine rolls and keeps your books flat. I tested one by stacking about 15 pounds of books on top of a bagged comic for a week—no warping, no indentations.

One thing worth noting: these are white boards, not the buffered acid-free archival grade. For the average collector who's storing comics for a few years, that's fine. If you're a serious long-term preservationist with books you plan to sell graded in a decade, you might want to pair these bags with higher-end buffered boards instead.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The praise is remarkably consistent. One reviewer noted, "These are the only bags I trust for my monthly pulls—they fit perfectly and the clarity is excellent for displaying covers." Another mentioned, "I've bought cheaper brands and they always seem too tight or too loose. BCW just fits right."

The most common complaint across reviews is availability—this combo routinely sells out, and when it does, third-party prices spike. A few reviewers also mentioned receiving bags with a slight factory odor, though most said it dissipated quickly.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The 100-count is a "starter" size—if you're bagging a full long box, you'll need two of these. And while the price per unit is fair, you can save money buying the 500-count bags and boards separately if you're a high-volume collector.

The white boards also show handling marks easily. Fingerprints and scuffs are visible, so if you're the type who reuses boards, they'll look ragged after a few rebagging cycles.

Also worth flagging: BCW changed their bag dimensions slightly in recent years. Older BCW bags were a touch larger. If you have comics from the 90s or earlier, these "Current" bags will fit them, but you'll have more fold-over at the top. For modern books? Perfect fit.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a regular comic reader who wants to keep your collection in solid shape without obsessing over archival perfection. You want a reliable, consistent bag-and-board system that won't fight you. You're restocking for a convention season and need a quick, dependable bulk option.

**Skip it if:** You're storing ultra-valuable keys you plan to sell graded in 10+ years (get Mylar and buffered boards). You're on a tight budget and can buy in larger quantities. You exclusively collect Golden or Silver Age books—you need the larger "Silver/Golden" size, not this.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these bags fit Marvel, DC, and Image comics the same way?**
Yes. All modern publishers use the same standard dimensions for floppy comics. Some thicker anniversary issues or cardstock covers will be snugger, but they fit.

**Q: Can I use these for magazine-size comics or graphic novels?**
No. This is specifically for standard floppy comics. Graphic novels need magazine bags, and oversized issues need "Silver" size bags.

**Q: How long will these protect my comics?**
Polypropylene bags are archival-safe and won't off-gas like PVC. For storage in a dark, cool, dry box, expect 10-15 years of solid protection before you'll want to rebag. The boards will last as long as you don't bend or wet them.
