Let’s cut through the marketing. The SteelSeries Arctis Nova 3X is a wireless headset that wants to be the only headset you own. It juggles PC, PlayStation, Switch, and mobile through a single USB-C dongle, and it mostly pulls that off. But “mostly” is doing a lot of work in that sentence.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the headset, a USB-C wireless dongle, a USB-C to USB-A charging cable, and a 3.5mm auxiliary cable for wired fallback. No carrying case, no replacement ear cushions. At this price point that’s a minor gripe, but worth knowing before you order.

The build is classic Arctis: a steel-reinforced headband wrapped in a flexible suspension strap, aluminum yoke, and memory foam ear cushions wrapped in a breathable fabric that SteelSeries calls AirWeave. The whole thing weighs about 9.5 ounces. You’ll notice it less than cheaper plastic headsets, but it’s not featherweight.

## Performance in the Real World

The 3X uses 40mm Neodymium drivers tuned with a flat-ish response curve. Out of the box, the sound leans neutral with a slight bass bump. That’s good for positional audio in shooters—footsteps and reloads land clearly without being muddy. Explosions lack the chest-thumping rumble you’d get from a bass-heavy gaming headset, but you can dial that in through the SteelSeries GG software on PC.

Wireless range is solid through one wall—roughly 30 feet before audio starts stuttering. The 2.4GHz connection held steady during a four-hour session with zero dropouts, which counts for something.

Battery life lands around 40 hours with the RGB lighting off. With the lighting on, expect closer to 25. The USB-C quick charge is the real winner here: 15 minutes plugged in gives you about 6 hours of play, which is exactly what you want when you forget to charge overnight.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The dual wireless feature gets consistent love. One reviewer put it plainly: *“Being able to play on PS5 while hearing a Discord call from my phone is a game-changer. No more choosing between teammates and friends.”* That’s the headline feature and it works as advertised.

Comfort is the second most-cited positive. The suspension headband does its job—multiple reviewers mention forgetting they’re wearing it after a few hours. The ear cushions are plush enough for glasses wearers, though not as forgiving as some competitors.

## Where It Falls Short

Let’s be honest about the microphone. The retractable boom mic is clear for voice chat, but the AI noise cancellation is a step behind what you’d get from a dedicated USB mic or even the newer Arctis Nova Pro. One buyer noted: *“My friends can hear my mechanical keyboard clicks even with noise cancellation enabled. It’s not terrible, but it’s not the 'studio quality' the box claims.”* That tracks with my testing.

The earcup swivel is another compromise. Unlike the wired Nova 3, these don’t lay completely flat. You can fold them for travel, but they won’t sit flush on a desk or in a bag easily.

Also, the 3X doesn’t support simultaneous Bluetooth and 2.4GHz on Xbox specifically—that’s a platform limitation, not a product flaw, but if you’re an Xbox primary, check whether the dual-audio feature matters to you before buying.

## Who Should Buy This

Grab the Arctis Nova 3X if you play across multiple platforms and want one headset that follows you. The dual wireless mixing is genuinely useful if you’re on Discord while gaming or want game audio plus a podcast on your phone.

Skip it if you’re a competitive audio purist—the bass response is tame and the mic won’t impress your streaming audience. Also skip if you’re on Xbox only, since you’re paying for a multi-platform feature you won’t fully use.

## FAQ

**Does this work with Xbox?**
The 3X is designed for PC, PlayStation, Switch, and mobile. It connects to Xbox via 3.5mm cable, but you lose wireless and the dual-audio feature. If Xbox is your primary, look at the Nova 3X’s Xbox-specific sibling.

**Can you use the mic while charging?**
Yes, USB-C charging works while you play. The cable is long enough for a couch setup, though it anchors you to a wall outlet.

**Are the ear cushions replaceable?**
Yes, they’re magnetic and pop off easily. SteelSeries sells replacements, and third-party options exist on Amazon.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you game on multiple platforms and want wireless without dongle-swapping. The dual-audio mixing, comfortable fit, and fast charging justify the price.

**Skip it if** you’re an Xbox-only player or want broadcast-quality mic audio. You’ll pay for features you can’t use or be disappointed by the mic performance.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A versatile, comfortable wireless headset with smart features and honest limitations.
