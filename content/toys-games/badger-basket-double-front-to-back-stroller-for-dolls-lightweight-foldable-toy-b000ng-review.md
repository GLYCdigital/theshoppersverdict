**Is this the double doll stroller that finally justifies the hype?** With 235 ratings averaging 4.7 stars, the Badger Basket Double Front to Back Stroller clearly has a fanbase. But before you click buy, let's dig into whether it's the right fit for your specific kid, doll size, and floor surfaces.

**What's actually in the box:** You get the steel frame (already partially assembled), two fabric seats with five-point harness straps, two canopies, four rubberized wheels, a small under-seat storage basket, and the necessary hardware. There's no assembly manual printed on recycled paper — it's a simple fold-out diagram. The frame is powder-coated steel, not flimsy plastic, which explains the 12-pound weight. That's heavier than a single doll stroller but reasonable for a double.

**Real-world performance:** The front-to-back (tandem) configuration is the standout feature. Unlike side-by-side double strollers that get wide and awkward for small arms, this one keeps the width narrow — about 15 inches across. Kids aged 3 to 6 can push it comfortably without bumping doorframes. The front wheels swivel 360 degrees, which helps with turning in tight playroom corners. On carpet, the rubberized wheels grip well; on concrete, they roll smoothly without catching. One Amazon reviewer noted, *"My daughter pushes both her 18-inch dolls around the park for an hour without getting tired — the wheels are surprisingly sturdy."* Another praised the harnesses: *"The straps actually keep the dolls in place, unlike cheaper strollers where they flop out."*

**What buyers consistently praise:** The folding mechanism gets frequent shout-outs. You press a button on the side, and the whole thing collapses to about 7 inches thick — small enough to slide under a bed or into a car trunk. Parents also love that it fits standard 18-inch dolls (American Girl, My Life As, etc.) without modification. The front seat reclines slightly, which adds a bit of realism for "naptime" play.

**Where it falls short:** The canopy is the biggest weak point. It's decorative more than functional — it doesn't extend far enough to shade a doll's face from direct sun. If your kid plays outdoors in bright weather, expect to hear complaints about "sun in dolly's eyes." Assembly is another hurdle. It's not hard, but it's not instant. You'll need a screwdriver and patience to attach the wheels and canopy brackets. One customer griped, *"Took me 25 minutes and a few choice words, but it's solid once together."* Finally, the storage basket is undersized — a 10-inch doll diaper bag fits, but anything larger won't.

**Who should buy this:**  
- **The sibling duo:** If you have two kids sharing toys, this tandem design prevents fighting over who pushes.  
- **The American Girl owner:** Fits 18-inch dolls perfectly, including the heavier hard-bodied ones.  
- **The traveling family:** The fold-flat design makes it easy to toss in the trunk for grandma's house or a park trip.  

**Skip it if:** Your kid is under 3 and still rough with toys — the steel frame is sturdy, but the canopy clips can snap if yanked hard. Or if you need a stroller for dolls smaller than 16 inches — they'll slide around in the seats.

**FAQ:**  
**Q: Does it fit Bitty Baby or other 15-inch dolls?**  
A: Not well — the harness straps sit too high for smaller dolls, and they'll look lost in the seat. Stick to 18-inch dolls.

**Q: Can a 2-year-old push it?**  
A: Maybe, but the handlebar is set at about 20 inches from the ground. A 2-year-old might struggle with steering due to the weight. Best for ages 3+.

**Q: Does the front seat recline fully?**  
A: No, it reclines about 30 degrees from vertical — enough for a "nap" but not a full lie-down position.

**The Verdict:**  
**Buy it if** you want a durable, foldable double stroller that handles real outdoor use and fits standard 18-inch dolls without wobbling. The 4.7-star rating is earned — this is a well-built toy that will survive multiple kids.  
**Skip it if** you need a deep canopy or a tool-free assembly experience.  

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Knock off half a star for the shallow canopy and fiddly assembly. Everything else is genuinely impressive for the price.
