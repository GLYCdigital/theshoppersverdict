## Where It Falls Short

Two honest complaints stand out. First, the sleeves run snug through the upper arm. If your daughter has broader shoulders or muscular arms, size up. Multiple reviewers mentioned this, with one saying, *"My daughter is average for her age but the sleeves were tight. We exchanged for a size up and it was perfect."*

Second, the black dye isn't fully set on first wear. If your child wears a white undershirt beneath it, expect some transfer. Wash it separately in cold water before the big night — that solves it completely, but it's worth knowing ahead of time.
