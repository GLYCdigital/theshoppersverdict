Let's be honest: you're not buying a Smiski figure for its utility. You're buying it because it's absurdly charming, and the Exercising Series Ra figure delivers exactly that.

**What You Actually Get**

The box is small — roughly the size of a matchbox on steroids. Inside, you'll find the figure (about 2 inches tall), a tiny instruction sheet, and a little collector card. The blind-box packaging means you won't know which of the six Exercising Series characters you've got until you tear it open. If you get Ra, you're looking at a little green guy mid-workout, caught in a pose that's equal parts determined and ridiculous.

The sculpt quality surprised me. For a figure at this price point, the detailing is sharp — you can clearly make out the straining posture and the little sweat details. The material is a soft PVC that feels solid, not cheap or flimsy.

**Glow Performance: The Real Test**

Here's where Smiski earns its reputation. Charge it under direct light for 30-60 seconds, and you get a genuinely bright, even green glow that lasts for hours. It's not a gimmick — the phosphorescent material is quality stuff. The glow is bright enough to read by in a dark room, though that's about the extent of its practical use.

**Where It Falls Short**

The random style is the biggest frustration point. If you're a completionist, you'll need to buy multiples and hope for the best. I've seen multiple reviews mention getting duplicates — that's the nature of blind boxes, but it's worth knowing going in.

The size is also a double-edged sword. At 2 inches, it's perfect for a desk or shelf, but it's also a genuine choking hazard. This is not a toy for young kids. The instruction sheet even says it's for ages 15 and up, which feels about right given the small parts.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does the glow actually last?**
A: After a 60-second charge under a desk lamp, expect 2-4 hours of visible glow. It fades gradually — you'll notice it dimming after the first hour, but it stays visible in complete darkness for several more.

**Q: Can you tell which figure is inside before opening?**
A: No. The packaging is completely opaque, and there's no way to weigh or feel your way to a specific figure. It's a true blind box.

**Q: Is it safe for kids?**
A: The manufacturer recommends 15+. The small size and detachable base make it a choking hazard for younger children. Keep it on a high shelf if you have curious little ones around.
