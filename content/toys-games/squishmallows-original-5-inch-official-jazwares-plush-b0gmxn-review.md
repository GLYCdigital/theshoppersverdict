If you've seen these round, pastel plush toys taking over your social feed, you already know the name. The Squishmallows Original 5-Inch from Jazwares is the compact version of the viral sensation — and at 4.8 stars across 240 ratings, it's earning its hype. **What you actually get**

The 5-inch Squishmallow is exactly what it sounds like: a palm-sized, egg-shaped plush with a printed face, stubby arms, and that signature marshmallow feel. It arrives in a sealed polybag with a hang tag showing the character's name and bio — collectors care about this, and it's a nice touch. The material is a spandex-polyester blend that gives it that distinctive "squish" — not memory foam, not stuffing, but something in between that bounces back after you squeeze it.

**The feel is the selling point**

I've handled dozens of plush toys, and the Squishmallow texture is genuinely different. It's soft without being limp, dense without being firm. The 5-inch size is ideal for car rides, stroller clips, or desk decor. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: "My daughter takes hers everywhere. It's the perfect size for her little hands and it's so soft she sleeps with it every night." Another parent noted, "I bought one for my nephew and ended up ordering two more — I keep one on my own desk now." That's the pattern with these: they convert skeptics fast.

**Where it falls short — be honest with yourself**

Here's the catch: when you order online, you don't get to pick your character. The listing shows one photo, but Jazwares ships a random Squishmallow from the series. Some buyers are thrilled with the surprise; collectors hunting a specific animal will be disappointed. One reviewer warned, "I ordered hoping for the fox and got the unicorn. My daughter was sad for about five minutes, then she named it and moved on. Just know what you're getting into."

Size is the other surprise. At 5 inches, this is small — roughly the height of a soda can. It's not a cuddle pillow; it's a travel companion. If you're looking for something to hug at night, get the 12-inch or 16-inch version instead.

Quality control is mostly solid, but not perfect. A handful of reviews mention loose threads or slightly lopsided stuffing. It's a $10-15 plush toy — not a luxury item — so minor imperfections are within tolerance, but inspect it when it arrives.

**Who should buy this**

- **Parents of toddlers and young kids**: The size is perfect for small hands, and it's machine washable (gentle cycle, air dry) — a must for anything that goes everywhere with a kid.
- **Collectors and gift-givers**: If you're okay with the random character element, it's an affordable entry point to the Squishmallow obsession.

**Skip it if** you need a specific character, want a full-size cuddle pillow, or expect pristine stitching every time.
