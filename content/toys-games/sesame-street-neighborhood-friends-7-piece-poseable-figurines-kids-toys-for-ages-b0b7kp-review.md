If your toddler has reached the stage where they're narrating Cookie Monster's snack habits at 7 AM, you already know the appeal of this set. The Sesame Street Neighborhood Friends 7-piece collection brings together the core cast — Elmo, Cookie Monster, Big Bird, Bert, Ernie, Abby Cadabby, and Zoe — in poseable vinyl form. With a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. Here's what that rating actually means for your money.

**What's in the box**

You get seven figures, each with articulation at the neck, shoulders, and hips. The construction is a dense vinyl that feels surprisingly premium for the price point — there's real heft here, not the hollow, cheap plastic you often find in multi-pack toys. Heights range from roughly 4 inches for Zoe up to about 7 inches for Big Bird, which gives you a nice visual hierarchy when they're lined up on a shelf.

**How they hold up in real use**

I tested these with a particularly energetic three-year-old who treats toys like personal wrestling opponents. After a week of aggressive play, including several launches across the room, every figure remained intact. The joints are tight enough to hold a pose but not so stiff that little hands struggle to bend them. That's a harder balance to strike than most manufacturers manage.

The paint application is clean — no sloppy edges around eyes or mouths, and the facial expressions capture each character's personality well. Elmo's perpetual enthusiasm and Bert's unimpressed stare are both spot-on. As shown in the product image, the figures stand independently without bases, which is convenient for play but means they'll topple if knocked. That's not a flaw, just a reality of poseable toys.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The recurring theme in customer feedback is durability. One parent noted, "My 2-year-old is rough on everything, and these have survived months of daily play." Another reviewer highlighted how the articulation encouraged imaginative play: "My son spends more time posing them than actually playing — he loves making Cookie Monster sit down to eat." The set's value proposition is strong when you calculate the per-figure cost against the build quality.

**Where it falls short**

The articulation is inconsistent across characters. Big Bird's wings are static vinyl — only his neck, arms, and legs move. For the tallest, most visually striking figure in the set, that's a missed opportunity. Zoe and Abby Cadabby are also on the smaller side, making them easier to lose in playroom chaos.

The character selection is solid but incomplete. No Oscar, no Count von Count, no Grover. If your kid has a specific favorite outside the core seven, you're buying a separate set or individual figure. Also worth noting: the joints on Bert and Ernie are slightly looser than the others out of the box, though they still hold poses adequately.

**Who should buy this**

This set is ideal for toddlers and preschoolers (ages 2–5) who are already invested in Sesame Street. The figures are sized appropriately for small hands, and the poseability adds a dimension that static figures lack. It's also a legitimate option for adult collectors — the quality is significantly better than typical kids' toy fare, and the character likenesses are accurate enough to display.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Are these figures safe for children under 3?* The manufacturer recommends ages 3 and up, primarily due to small parts. The figures themselves are large enough to be safe, but the joints can be pulled apart with effort, creating small pieces. If you have a child who mouth-tests everything, wait until they're past that phase.

*Can the figures be washed?* Yes, warm water with mild soap works fine. The paint is sealed and won't run or fade with gentle cleaning. Just air-dry them completely before storage.

*Do the figures come with any accessories?* No — this is a figures-only set. No furniture, food pieces, or play scenes are included. That's consistent with the price point, but worth knowing if you're expecting a playset.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want durable, well-made Sesame Street figures that can handle rough toddler play and still look good displayed on a shelf afterward. The articulation adds real play value, and the character accuracy is the best you'll find at this price.

**Skip it if** your child's favorite character isn't in this lineup, or if you need accessories to keep play engaging. The missing characters and lack of add-ons are the only genuine reasons to look elsewhere.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A strong set that earns its high ratings through solid construction and good design. The articulation gaps and incomplete roster keep it from perfection, but for most families, this is the Sesame Street figure set to get.
