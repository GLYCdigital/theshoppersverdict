# G.I. Joe Classified Series #167 Dreadnok Monkeywrench Review: The Mad Bomber Gets His Due

The Dreadnoks were always the rowdiest corner of the G.I. Joe universe — a biker gang of anarchists who caused more property damage than Cobra ever did. And Monkeywrench? He's the guy who made the explosions. Now he's finally gotten the Classified Series treatment, and honestly, it's about damn time.

I've been collecting this line since Wave 1, and I'll say this plainly: **this is one of the most character-accurate releases Hasbro has produced in the last two years.** If you've been eyeing it, here's what you need to know before you click buy.

## What's in the Box

You're getting the figure (obviously), plus a genuinely impressive accessory spread:

- The signature oversized wrench (painted with realistic rust and grime)
- A compact pistol with holster
- A combat knife with sheath
- Two alternate heads — one with a bandana pulled down, one with a crazed, screaming expression
- Three sets of interchangeable hands (fists, grips, and relaxed)

The alternate heads are the star here. The screaming head is *unsettling* in the best way — it captures that unhinged Dreadnok energy perfectly. As one Amazon reviewer put it, *"The head sculpts alone are worth the price. He looks like he's about to blow something up and enjoy every second of it."*

## Build Quality and Articulation

The Classified Series has set the standard for 6-inch military-adjacent figures, and Monkeywrench maintains that bar. He's got over 35 points of articulation, including double-jointed elbows and knees, a ball-jointed torso, and rocker ankles that actually let him hold a two-handed wrench swing pose without tipping over.

The sculpt work on the leather vest and patched jacket is detailed without being busy — the weathering is painted, not just molded, which makes a difference in hand. The face sculpt under the bandana head shows proper stubble and that signature sneer.

One thing I noticed: the vest plastic feels slightly thinner than what I've seen on other Classified figures like Dusty or Tunnel Rat. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you're aggressive with posing, be careful around the shoulder straps. You can stress the plastic if you force a pose.

## What Buyers Are Saying

With a 4.9 out of 5 across 245 ratings, this figure is landing well with the collector community. The praise is consistent:

- *"Best Dreadnok in the line so far. The paint apps are clean and the accessories make him worth every penny."*
- *"Finally a Monkeywrench that doesn't look like a knockoff. The screaming head is my favorite piece in my entire display."*
- *"Articulation is smooth out of the box — no stuck joints, no QC issues. This is how Classified should be."*

The few critical reviews (and there are very few) center on the price. Because of the accessory count, this runs a bit higher than a standard Classified release. If you only army-build or collect main characters, that premium might sting.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest — there are two real downsides:

1. **The price-to-value ratio is a judgment call.** You're paying for accessories and two heads. If you only care about the core figure, you might feel overcharged. If you're a Dreadnok fan or a customizer, the parts are worth it.

2. **No blast effects or explosion pieces.** For a character whose whole identity is demolitions, the lack of any explosive effect parts is a weird omission. Hasbro could have thrown in a small blast effect for the price point.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Dreadnok completists** — You've been waiting for this. Don't hesitate.
- **Classified Series collectors** who want villain variety beyond Cobra troops. Monkeywrench adds a chaotic energy to any display.
- **Customizers** — The alternate heads and hands have serious parts-reuse potential for other projects.

**Skip it if** you're a casual buyer who only grabs main line heroes like Snake Eyes or Duke. This is a niche character for a niche audience, and the price reflects that.

## FAQ

**Q: Does Monkeywrench fit with the standard Classified Series scale?**
A: Yes, he's the same 6-inch scale as the rest of the line. He's slightly bulkier through the torso, which actually makes him look appropriately intimidating next to standard Joes.

**Q: Can he hold the wrench in both hands?**
A: Yes, but it takes a bit of work. The wrench is heavy (it's solid plastic), so you'll want to use the grip hands and brace him with the rocker ankles. He holds it one-handed fine.

**Q: Is the screaming head compatible with other Classified figures?**
A: It uses the standard ball-joint neck connection, so yes — it'll fit on most other Classified figures if you want to do some custom work.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're a Dreadnok fan, a Classified completist, or you appreciate well-executed character work with a generous accessory loadout. This is a 4.6/5 figure — the thin vest plastic and missing blast effects knock off just enough points to keep it from perfection.

**Skip it if** you're on a tight budget and only collect core characters. This is a niche release for niche fans, and it knows it.

For everyone else? Monkeywrench is a fantastic addition to the Classified Series that proves Hasbro still knows how to deliver a character with personality. Just be ready to have your display shelf get a little more... explosive.
