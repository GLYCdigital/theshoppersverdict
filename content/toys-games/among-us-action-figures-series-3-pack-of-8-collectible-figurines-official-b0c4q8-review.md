If your kid (or honestly, you) can't get enough of the red crewmate getting tossed out of the airlock, this Series 3 8-pack hits that sweet spot. But at $49.93, you need to know exactly what you're paying for before you click buy.

## What You Actually Get

This is the full Series 3 wave in one box — eight individually styled crewmates, each roughly 3 inches tall. The lineup includes fan favorites like the Cyan crewmate with the security visor, a Pink holding a task card, and a Lime with the classic hard hat. Two figures in this pack are Series 3 exclusives, meaning you can't find them in single blind boxes.

Each figure has the signature Among Us bean shape, but the paint work separates these from cheaper knockoffs. The visor reflections are crisp, the task card details are printed cleanly, and the color saturation matches the game's palette faithfully.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The ABS plastic feels dense — these aren't hollow, flimsy shells. I dropped one from desk height onto hardwood and it bounced without cracking or scuffing. The connection points between the body halves are tight with no visible seam gaps.

That said, these are display figures first. The small accessories — mini task cards, hats, and the little scan station piece — attach via friction pegs that pop off with minimal force. If a toddler gets these, expect to play a daily game of "find the missing task card."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.8-star rating across 240 reviews isn't accidental. Parents repeatedly mention the durability factor. One review noted: "My 7-year-old has thrown these across the room more times than I care to admit, and they still look brand new." Another collector appreciated the completeness: "Finally a set where the visor paint isn't crooked on every single figure."

The two exclusive figures are the most common reason buyers cite for choosing this pack over buying individual blind boxes.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be straight about the math. At $49.93, you're paying about $6.25 per figure. Single blind boxes typically run $8-10 each, so there's a small value edge here — but only if you want all eight designs. If you're only chasing one or two specific crewmates, you're better off buying those individually.

The packaging is also a minor gripe. Figures come in a sealed plastic tray that's a genuine pain to open without scissors, and the tray isn't reusable for storage. You'll need to find your own display solution.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a completionist collector who needs the full Series 3 wave, or you're buying for a kid aged 6+ who plays with figures roughly but won't swallow small parts.

**Skip it if:** You already have several Among Us figures and just want to fill gaps — buy singles instead. Also skip if the recipient is under 5; those small accessories are a choking hazard.
