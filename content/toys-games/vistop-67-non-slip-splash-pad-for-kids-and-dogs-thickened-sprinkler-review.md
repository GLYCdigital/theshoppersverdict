**Where It Falls Short**

No product is perfect, and this one has three real issues:

**No drain plug.** This is the biggest annoyance. When playtime ends, you're flipping a 5.5-foot, water-filled PVC mat to empty it. At 67 inches, that's awkward. Plan to let it dry flat or have a second person help you drain it.

**Center-heavy spray pattern.** The fountain is concentrated in the middle. If your toddler prefers the edges, they'll only get wet if they crawl toward the center. Some kids love this — they can stay dry while watching the fountain. Others will be frustrated.

**Air valve placement.** It's located near the edge, which makes sense, but inflating the rim evenly takes patience. You'll need to roll the pad slightly to distribute air properly. Takes about 3 minutes, not a dealbreaker, just mildly annoying.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I leave it out in the sun?**
A: You can, but you shouldn't. Like all PVC products, prolonged UV exposure will eventually make it brittle. Store it in the shade or bring it in after use. It folds flat, so this is easy.

**Q: Does it work with a low-pressure hose?**
A: Yes, but the fountain height will be lower. It needs about 30 PSI for a nice spray. If your water pressure is weak, the center fountain may just trickle.

**Q: Is it safe for babies under 1 year?**
A: The water is shallow and the surface is soft, but always supervise. The fountain spray is gentle, not harsh. Just never leave infants unattended — even an inch of water is a drowning risk.
