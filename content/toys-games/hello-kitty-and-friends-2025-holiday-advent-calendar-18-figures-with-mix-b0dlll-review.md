## Is This Advent Calendar Worth Your Holiday Money?

Here's the thing about licensed advent calendars: most are overpriced cardboard with a few disappointingly small trinkets. The Hello Kitty and Friends 2025 Holiday Advent Calendar breaks that pattern — mostly. With 235 ratings averaging 4.7 stars, this is one of the better-regarded pop culture advent calendars on Amazon right now. But it's not without quirks, and I'll get to those.

## What's Actually Inside

You get 18 doors (not 24 — this is a shorter countdown, which matters if you're expecting a full month). Behind each door is an individually wrapped PVC figure from the Sanrio universe. The roster includes Hello Kitty, My Melody, Cinnamoroll, Pompompurin, Kuromi, and others. As one buyer put it: *"My daughter was thrilled to get different characters — not just five variations of Hello Kitty like last year's calendar."*

The figures range from about 1.5 to 3 inches tall. Larger characters like Pompompurin get bigger doors, which tells me the packaging was actually thought through rather than just stuffed.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

These aren't flimsy gacha toys. The PVC is dense, the paint lines are clean, and the figures stand on their own — a surprisingly rare feature in this price tier. One reviewer noted: *"The quality is honestly better than what I expected from a $25 calendar. My Cinnamoroll figure has held up to my 6-year-old dropping it on tile floors multiple times."*

The figures are solid enough for play, not just display. They're also sized compatibly with other standard mini-figure collections, so they don't look out of place next to similar products.

## Where It Falls Short

**The box is not a keeper.** There's no tray to hold the figures after you open them, and the door flaps tear easily if you're not careful. Several buyers mentioned this: *"I wish the doors were perforated better — I ripped two of them just trying to open them normally."* If you're a collector who wants the calendar intact after December 25th, budget for a separate storage solution.

**It's an 18-day countdown, not 24.** At this price point, some shoppers expect a full December calendar. Know what you're getting. That said, the per-figure cost is actually reasonable compared to buying individual Sanrio figures.

**No exclusives.** All the characters in this calendar are available elsewhere in various forms. If you're a completionist collector, there's nothing here you can't get another way — this is about convenience and the advent experience, not rare finds.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it for:** Parents of Sanrio-obsessed kids (roughly ages 4-10), collectors who want a compact holiday display piece, or anyone who wants a daily dose of cute without accumulating plastic junk they'll toss in January. The figures themselves are genuinely reusable as toys or desk decor.

**Skip it if:** You're expecting a 24-day countdown, you want exclusive/rare figures, or you need the packaging to double as storage. Also skip if the child is very young — some small parts (like My Melody's bow) are removable and could be a choking hazard for kids under 3.

## FAQ

**Q: Are the figures blind boxes or do you know what's behind each door?**
A: They're blind — that's the surprise element. The doors are numbered, but there's no way to tell which character is behind which number. If you're buying for a child with a specific favorite, manage expectations.

**Q: How big are the figures compared to other Sanrio merchandise?**
A: They're roughly 1.5 to 3 inches tall, which puts them in the "mini figure" category. They're larger than standard gacha capsules but smaller than the full-size plush line. They pair well with dollhouse setups or as desk decorations.

**Q: Is this a 2025-specific design or will it be the same every year?**
A: This is the 2025 edition, and Sanrio typically rotates the character lineup and designs each year. If you're buying for a repeat celebration, don't assume next year's will be identical — that's actually a plus for collectors who want variety.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely well-made Sanrio advent calendar with solid figures that survive actual play. The quality-to-price ratio is strong, and the character variety is a clear improvement over single-character calendars.

**Skip it if** the shorter 18-day countdown feels like a rip-off to you, or if you need the box to serve as permanent storage. Those are the only real strikes against this product.

For most Hello Kitty fans and parents of Sanrio-loving kids, this is one of the better licensed advent calendars you'll find this year. It delivers what it promises: a daily dose of cute, well-made figures that don't feel like landfill fodder.
