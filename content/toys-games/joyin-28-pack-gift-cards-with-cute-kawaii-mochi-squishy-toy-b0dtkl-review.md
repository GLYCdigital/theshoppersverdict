The JOYIN 28 Pack Gift Cards with Cute Kawaii Mochi Squishy Toy is not that. For under $20 (price varies by listing), you're getting 28 complete gift-ready combos. That's the hook.

**What you actually get**

Open the box and you'll find 28 individually wrapped squishies, each about 2.5 inches across, paired with a 4x6-inch card featuring a kawaii character design. The toys are foam-filled, not water-based, which is a plus — no leaky bags in your kid's backpack. The designs cover the usual kawaii suspects: smiling food items, animals, and fruit. Each squishy is a different shape and color, so no two favors are identical.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The mochi material is dense and slow-rising. When you squeeze one flat, it takes a few seconds to puff back to full shape. That's the satisfying part kids love. I've seen complaints about cheap squishies tearing at seams within days — that's not happening here. The outer skin is thick enough to handle repeated crushing from elementary-age hands. One Amazon reviewer put it bluntly: "My 6-year-old has squeezed these daily for a month and they still bounce back."

The cards are the weak link. They're printed on standard cardstock weight — fine for handing out at a party, but they'll crease if shoved in a pocket. The designs are genuinely cute, with clean linework and bright colors that photograph well.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Reading through the 235 ratings (4.7 stars), three things come up repeatedly:

- **The matching card-toy combo.** One parent wrote, "My daughter loved that the card matched the squishy — it felt like opening a tiny present, not just getting a toy." That's the differentiator here versus generic bulk packs.

**Where it falls short**

Be honest about the smell. Multiple reviewers flagged a strong chemical/plastic odor straight out of the box. It's not overpowering, but it's noticeable. Air them out for a day or two before the party if you're scent-sensitive.

Also, check the faces. A few buyers reported minor paint smudges on some squishies — uneven eyes, slightly off mouth placement. Cosmetic only, but if you're handing these to perfectionist kids, inspect them first. And the stickiness: a handful of units arrived with a slightly tacky surface. Wiping with a damp cloth fixes it.

The squishies themselves are soft foam, but the cards and packaging include small parts. JOYIN lists these for ages 3+. For under-3s, skip the cards and supervise play with the toys.

**Q: Do the squishies leave residue on surfaces?**
No, the material is dry to the touch after the initial tackiness wears off. They won't stain tables or clothes.

**Q: Can you write on the cards?**
Yes, but use ballpoint pen — marker bleeds through the thin cardstock.
