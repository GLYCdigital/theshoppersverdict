**Do all 32 animals look the same?**
No. The assortment includes multiple designs in varied colors. You won't get 32 identical pieces, though the exact mix varies by batch.

**Can these be washed?**
Spot-clean only. They're not machine-washable — the stuffing and stitching won't survive a cycle.
