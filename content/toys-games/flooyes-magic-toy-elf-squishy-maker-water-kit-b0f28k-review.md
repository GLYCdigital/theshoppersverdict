**The honest truth about this squishy maker:** Most craft toys promise endless fun and deliver a sticky mess. The Flooyes Magic Toy Elf Squishy Maker Water Kit actually delivers on the fun part — with a few caveats worth knowing before you hand it to your kid.

## What's actually in the box

You get the water injection tool, a set of elf-themed squishy shells (the kit includes multiple character designs — elves, animals, and fantasy creatures), and the filling mechanism. The concept is simple: fill the hollow shells with water using the included syringe-style tool, seal them up, and you've got squishies.

As shown in the product image, the finished toys have a satisfying jelly-like squish that appeals to kids who love fidget toys and stress balls. The elf designs are genuinely cute — not the cheap, off-model look you sometimes get with these kits.

## How it performs in real use

The filling process takes about 2–3 minutes per squishy once you get the hang of it. The syringe tool works well but requires a steady hand — you need to fill each shell to the right level without overdoing it. Get it right and you have a durable squishy that survives aggressive squeezing sessions.

Build quality is solid for the price point. The shells are made of a flexible material that holds up to repeated use, and the seal mechanism (once properly closed) doesn't leak in normal play. Kids who've had these for months report they're still going strong — provided they weren't overfilled during creation.

## What buyers consistently praise

The 4.4/5 rating across 220 reviews reflects genuine satisfaction. Parents repeatedly mention the "wow factor" of watching the squishy inflate with water. One reviewer noted: *"My 7-year-old made all six in one afternoon and they've survived two weeks of daily squeezing. The elf one is her favorite."*

Another common theme: the kit occupies kids for a solid hour-plus of focused activity. Unlike slime or kinetic sand, there's no mess during the making process — just water and the filling tool.

## Where it falls short

**The filling is genuinely fiddly.** The syringe-style injector takes practice, and most kids under 8 will need adult help. Several reviewers mention that overfilled squishies developed weak seams and eventually split — you need to leave a small air gap when filling.

**The finished toys are cold and damp.** This isn't a dealbreaker, but kids with sensory sensitivities may not enjoy the wet, chilly texture. Some reviewers noted their kids lost interest after the novelty wore off because the squishies feel like water balloons, not premium stress balls.

**No refill packs included.** Once you've made all the shells in the kit, you'll need to buy additional sets to keep going. Check if the brand sells compatible refills before committing.

**Q: Is this safe for a 5-year-old?**
