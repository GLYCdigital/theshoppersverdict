**Skip the candy meltdown and grab a bag of these instead.** The 134 Pcs Pop Party Favors set is exactly what it claims to be: a bulk box of fidget toys and novelties designed to fill Easter eggs, stock goodie bags, or rescue a classroom party. At 4.4 stars from 220 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. But this is a "you get what you pay for" situation in the best and worst ways.

**What's actually in the box**

The "134 pieces" figure is honest but padded. You're getting pop tubes (the classic accordion-style crackle tubes), mini squishies, puzzle cubes, stretchy animals, and a handful of slap bracelets. As shown in the product image, everything is bright, colorful, and sized for small hands — each item measures roughly 2-4 inches, which means they fit standard plastic Easter eggs without forcing the lid shut. There's no storage bag or bin, so plan to dump them into a bowl or Ziploc before the event.

**Real-world performance**

For the price per unit, these are genuinely impressive. The pop tubes are the stars — kids love the crackle sound and the way they extend and collapse. The squishies are dense enough to hold their shape after aggressive squeezing. The puzzle cubes are basic but functional, and they don't fall apart mid-twist like some budget versions do.

That said, the build quality is what you'd expect from a bulk novelty pack. The plastic is thin, and the paint on the squishy faces starts flaking after a few days of play. One Amazon reviewer noted, "They're cute and my kids loved them immediately, but three of the pop tubes cracked within a week." That tracks — these are designed for short bursts of fun, not long-term durability. If your kid is a dedicated fidgeter who will carry one in every pocket, expect replacements.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The value and variety come up repeatedly in positive reviews. One parent wrote, "Perfect for my son's birthday party goodie bags — I didn't have to buy anything else." Another mentioned, "My kindergartener took them to school and the whole class wanted one." The sheer quantity means you can afford to let kids trade, share, or lose a few without stress. For group events, this is the practical choice.

**Where it falls short**

Two honest complaints. First, the pop tubes are on the small side — about 3 inches collapsed. Sensory seekers who want a deep, resonant pop sound will be disappointed; these produce a tinny crackle instead. Second, the packaging is a single poly bag. There's no compartmentalization, so you'll dig through 134 mixed items looking for specific ones. Minor, but worth knowing before you dump them into an egg-hunting assembly line.

**Who should buy this**

- **Party parents**: Stocking 20-30 goodie bags? This covers you in one order.
- **Easter egg stuffers**: The small size fits perfectly, and you'll have leftovers for next year.
- **Classroom teachers**: Great for prize boxes or reward systems — cheap enough to give freely.
- **Skip it if**: You need durable fidgets for daily sensory use, or if you're looking for high-quality, individually wrapped items.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do these fit in standard plastic Easter eggs?**  
Yes, the largest items (about 4 inches) fit in regular eggs. The pop tubes and squishies slide in easily. Oversized novelty eggs aren't needed.

**Q: Are these safe for toddlers?**  
The pieces are small enough to be a choking hazard for kids under 3. The recommended age is 3+, but supervise younger children closely.

**Q: How long do they last?**  
With normal play, expect 1-2 weeks before some pieces crack or lose their pop. They're designed for short-term fun, not heirloom quality.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a bulk, low-cost solution for Easter baskets, party favors, or classroom prizes and you're okay with disposable quality. **Skip it if** you're looking for durable fidgets or premium materials.

At roughly 30 cents per piece, the 134 Pcs Pop Party Favors set delivers exactly what it promises: a mountain of cheap, fun, colorful toys that kids will fight over. Just don't expect them to survive the summer.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid value buy with a clear tradeoff between price and durability.
