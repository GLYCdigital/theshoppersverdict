The PlayStation 5 and Call of Duty have been synonymous with console generations since the PS3 era. This bundle pairs the PS5 Digital Edition with Modern Warfare III, and at 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. But let me tell you what the marketing doesn't.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the PS5 Digital Edition console (the slimmer 2023 model, not the original launch unit), one DualSense wireless controller, a USB-C charging cable, an HDMI 2.1 cable, and a digital voucher for Call of Duty: Modern Warfare III. That's it. No disc drive, no vertical stand (it's included in the box but requires assembly), and no physical game disc.

The console itself measures 15.4 x 4.3 x 10.2 inches and weighs about 6.8 pounds. It's noticeably slimmer than the original PS5, which matters if you're trying to fit it into an entertainment center.

## Real-World Performance

Let's talk about what actually matters when you plug this thing in. The custom AMD Zen 2 processor with 10.3 teraflops of GPU power handles Modern Warfare III at 4K resolution with ray tracing enabled. In practice, this means buttery-smooth 60fps gameplay in multiplayer and the campaign's more cinematic moments.

The star here is the DualSense controller. The adaptive triggers give real resistance when you're firing — you feel the weapon kick back. The haptic feedback is subtle enough to distinguish between footsteps on concrete versus grass in Warzone. One reviewer put it plainly: "The controller alone makes this worth it — playing CoD without it feels wrong afterward."

The SSD is the other game-changer. The 825GB internal storage (about 667GB usable after system software) delivers load times that make loading screens feel like a thing of the past. Warzone matches load in under five seconds from menu to drop. That's not marketing speak — that's the real experience.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

Looking through the 235 ratings, a clear pattern emerges. The vast majority of buyers mention three things: the value of getting a full-priced game bundled with the console, the significantly quieter cooling fans compared to the launch PS5, and how straightforward the setup process is.

One verified buyer noted: "Got this for my son's birthday. He was playing Warzone within 30 minutes of opening the box. No issues, no headaches."

Another mentioned: "The game voucher redemption was painless — took about 10 minutes to download the full game."

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest part. The Digital Edition means you're locked into the PlayStation Store ecosystem. If you already own a library of physical PS4 games, this console won't play them. That's a dealbreaker for some folks, and it's worth acknowledging.

The bundle also only includes the base game. Modern Warfare III's Vault Edition content — the Nemesis Operator pack, two weapon vaults, and the BlackCell season pass — isn't included. If you want that, you're looking at another $30-$40.

And despite the fast SSD, the initial game download is substantial. Modern Warfare III requires roughly 100GB of storage, and you'll need to download the multiplayer and campaign separately. On a typical 100Mbps connection, budget 30-45 minutes before you're actually playing.

## Who Should Buy This

This bundle is ideal for two specific types of buyers:

**The newcomer** — If you haven't owned a PlayStation since the PS4 era (or never had one), this is your entry point. You get the hardware and a flagship title in one purchase.

**The digital-first gamer** — If you've already moved to all-digital purchases or subscribe to PlayStation Plus, the lack of a disc drive won't bother you at all.

**Skip it if** you have a substantial physical game collection, want to use your PS5 as a 4K Blu-ray player, or you're only interested in single-player exclusives — the bundled CoD game adds no value for you.

## FAQ

**Q: Is the game a physical disc or digital code?**
A: Digital code. You'll receive a voucher in the box that you redeem on the PlayStation Store. The full game downloads to your console.

**Q: Can I play PS4 discs on this console?**
A: No. This is the Digital Edition — it has no disc drive whatsoever. You're limited to digital downloads only.

**Q: Does it come with a second controller?**
A: No, just one DualSense. If you're planning on split-screen or online multiplayer with friends locally, budget for an extra controller at around $70.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want the best console gaming experience paired with a flagship shooter at a reasonable entry point. The DualSense integration with Modern Warfare III is genuinely transformative, and the SSD performance will spoil you.

**Skip it if** you're committed to physical media or already own a PS5 and just want the game — you're better off buying the game separately.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It's a fantastic value bundle, but the digital-only limitation and lack of Vault Edition content keep it from perfection.
