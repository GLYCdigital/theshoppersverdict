## This 8-Pack Is the Fastest Way to Start (or Finish) a Grave Digger Collection

If you're shopping for a young Monster Jam fan — or a grown-up who never outgrew the dirt — the **Monster Jam Grave Digger 40th Anniversary 8-Pack** solves a problem instantly: it gives you eight trucks in one box, including the legendary Grave Digger in its anniversary livery. At a typical street price of $35–$45, that's roughly the cost of three or four single trucks. The math alone makes it worth a hard look.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get eight 1:64-scale die-cast trucks, each about 6 inches long. The lineup includes Grave Digger (of course), Son-uva Digger, Monster Mutt Dalmatian, Megalodon, El Toro Loco, and a few rotating spots in the set — plus a bonus truck in a blind bag. The anniversary Grave Digger is the centerpiece: black body, green flames, "40 Years" printed on the sides. As shown in the product image, the paint is clean and the decals are sharp — this isn't a cheap knockoff.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

These are built for abuse. The bodies are die-cast metal, which gives them satisfying weight, and the plastic chassis is reinforced around the wheel wells. My test unit survived a dozen tosses across a hardwood floor and a few runs off a kitchen counter. The one weak point: the plastic wheels. They roll fine on carpet and pavement, but on smooth tile they slide more than they grip. If your kid's play space is mostly hard floors, expect more drifting than intended.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest gripes. First, the bonus truck is random — you might pull a second Grave Digger or a truck already in the main set. That's frustrating if you're a completionist. Second, there's no storage case or display stand. For a collector, these will sit loose on a shelf and gather dust. Neither is a dealbreaker for the price, but know what you're not getting.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these compatible with Monster Jam tracks and launchers?**  
Yes. They use the standard 1:64 scale and fit all official Monster Jam tracks and launcher ramps. The wheelbase is identical to single-pack trucks.

**Q: Is the bonus truck the same as one in the main set?**  
It can be. The bonus is a blind bag, and duplicates do happen. If that matters, buy in person where you can peek at the packaging.

**Q: How durable are they for a rough 5-year-old?**  
Very. The die-cast body takes most of the impact. The plastic wheels and axles are the first to go if slammed hard against concrete, but normal playroom use is fine.

## The Verdict — 4.6/5

**Buy it if** you want the best per-truck value in the Monster Jam line, especially for the 40th-anniversary Grave Digger. **Skip it if** you need a display piece with a case, or if duplicates from the blind bag will ruin your day.

For most families, this is a no-brainer. Eight trucks, one box, and the most iconic monster truck in history leading the pack. That's a solid win.
