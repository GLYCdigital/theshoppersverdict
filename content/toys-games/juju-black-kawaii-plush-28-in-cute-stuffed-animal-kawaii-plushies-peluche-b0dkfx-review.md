## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, this is a black plush, and it will show everything. Lint, pet hair, dust — it all collects on that dark surface. You'll be lint-rolling it weekly if you keep it on a bed. Second, the firm stuffing means it's not a floppy, huggable plush in the classic sense. If you want something you can squish into a ball and throw around, this isn't it. It's more of a display piece that also happens to be cuddly.

A few buyers noted the fur can mat slightly in high-friction areas after extended use — the arms and head, mostly. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing if this is going to be a daily cuddle companion rather than a shelf ornament.
