You get one sealed blind box containing a single vinyl-plush doll from the Fall in Wild series. **Build Quality and Materials**

This is where POP MART differentiates itself from cheaper blind box brands. The hybrid construction uses a soft vinyl exterior with plush filling — it's squeezable but holds its shape. The autumn theme comes through in the paintwork: warm oranges, deep browns, and subtle leaf motifs that are crisp and clean. No sloppy paint bleeds or visible seam lines, which is consistent with the high ratings.

The hybrid material is genuinely clever. It's more durable than pure plush (won't flatten or lose shape), but more tactile than standard vinyl figures. **What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Across the 240 ratings, three themes dominate positive feedback:

The blind box format is the biggest frustration. You can't choose your character, and with multiple designs in the series, collectors chasing a specific one face duplication risk. One 4-star review summed it up: "Love the quality, hate that I got two of the same when ordering a set."

The hybrid material, while clever, isn't a full plush. If you're expecting something you can genuinely cuddle, this is stiffer than a traditional plush toy. It's a display piece first, a squeezable stress-reliever second.
