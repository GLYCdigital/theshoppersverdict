The set serves 16 guests and includes 16 paper plates, 16 lunch napkins, 16 cups, and 16 forks. **Real-world performance**

I tested these plates with the heaviest party offenders: pasta salad, watermelon slices, and a stack of chicken nuggets. The plates are thicker than the dollar-store variety — they didn't bow or soak through after 20 minutes of use. **Who should buy this**
