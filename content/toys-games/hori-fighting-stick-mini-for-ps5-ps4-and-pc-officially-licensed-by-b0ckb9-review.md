Let's be blunt: most "mini" arcade sticks are toys. The HORI Fighting Stick Mini for PS5, PS4, and PC is the exception that proves the rule. At roughly 13 inches wide and weighing just over 3 pounds, this officially licensed stick packs most of what you'd want from a full-size fight stick into something that actually fits in a backpack. But "most" is the operative word — and whether that's enough depends entirely on who you are.

**What's in the box**

You get the stick itself, a 10-foot USB cable, and not much else. No carrying case, no spare parts, no ball-top wrench. That's fine at this price point — HORI put the money into the hardware, not the packaging. The stick connects via USB-A and works natively on PS5, PS4, and PC. Notably absent is Switch support, so if you're a Smash player, keep looking.

**Build quality and performance**

The first thing you notice is the weight. The metal bottom plate gives it a heft that belies its size — it won't slide around your lap or desk mid-match. The lever is a proper HORI HAYABUSA stick, the same one used in their full-size Fighting Stick Alpha. It's responsive, with a satisfying click that registers cleanly on every input. The face buttons are HAYABUSA-style as well — quick to press, with a short throw that helps with fast double-taps.

I tested it on Street Fighter 6 and Tekken 8 on PS5, plus a few rounds of Guilty Gear Strive on PC. Input latency felt identical to my full-size stick. Quarter-circles, dragon punches, and even the more intricate Tekken wavedash inputs all came through without dropped inputs. The stick is genuinely competitive — not a compromise.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The Amazon reviews (210 ratings, 4.2 stars) repeatedly highlight two things: the official license and the portability. One reviewer put it simply: *"No adapter, no lag, just works on my PS5. I take it to my friend's place every week."* Another noted, *"I was skeptical about the size but it feels way more solid than the price suggests."* The plug-and-play nature is a recurring theme — no driver installation on PC, no compatibility mode toggles. It just works.

**Where it falls short**

Here's the honest tradeoff. The "mini" in the name means the palm rest is minimal. If you have larger hands or prefer to rest your wrist on the stick during play, you'll find yourself hovering rather than resting. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's noticeable on longer sessions. One reviewer hit it exactly: *"Great stick, but my hands are too big for hours of play. Fine for quick sessions."*

Also worth noting: the buttons are audible. They're not obnoxiously loud, but they're not silent either. If you play late at night with others sleeping, this might be a problem. And you won't find a headphone jack, touchpad button, or the extra L3/R3 buttons that full-size sticks offer — you'll need to map those in-game.

**Who should buy this**

- **The traveling player**: If you go to locals, weekly tournaments, or friend's houses, the size and weight make this the easy choice.
- **The budget-conscious beginner**: Want to try an arcade stick without dropping $150+? This is the smart entry point.
- **The PS5 owner who wants zero hassle**: Official license means no adapters, no workarounds, no firmware updates.

**Skip it if** you have large hands, play for 4+ hour sessions regularly, or need Switch compatibility.

**FAQ**

**Is this good for fighting games on PS5?** Yes — it's officially licensed, so it works natively with zero latency issues. Street Fighter 6, Tekken 8, and MK1 all work perfectly.

**Can I use this on PC?** Yes, plug-and-play via USB. It's recognized as a standard controller, so it works with Steam, Fightcade, and most PC fighting games.

**Is it worth upgrading from a pad?** If you're curious about arcade sticks, yes. It's a low-risk way to test whether stick play works for you before committing to a $200+ premium stick.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely portable, officially licensed arcade stick that performs like a proper fight stick, not a toy. **Skip it if** you have large hands or need a full-size form factor.

The HORI Fighting Stick Mini is the best compact arcade stick available for PS5, PS4, and PC. It won't replace a premium full-size stick for hardcore players, but for everyone else — travelers, beginners, or anyone who wants a reliable stick without the bulk — it's the right call. 4.2/5 stars.
