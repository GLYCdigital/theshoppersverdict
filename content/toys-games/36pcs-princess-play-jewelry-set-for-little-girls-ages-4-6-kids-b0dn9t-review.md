### What You Actually Get

The set includes 36 pieces: roughly 6-8 rings, 4-5 necklaces, 4 bracelets, a couple of tiaras or crowns, and a mix of clip-on earrings and hair clips. The pieces come in the standard pastel palette—pink, lavender, mint, and aqua—with plastic "gems" that catch the light well.

The tiaras are the standout. They’re flexible enough to survive being bent backward by an enthusiastic 5-year-old, which is more than I can say for similar sets from lesser-known brands. The necklaces use a breakaway-style clasp (a small magnetic or slot-in closure), which is a deliberate safety choice for this age group. That’s a thoughtful detail.

### FAQ

**Q: Are the earrings safe for a 4-year-old?**
A: They’re clip-ons, so no piercing required. But they pinch after 20 minutes. Use them for short play sessions or skip them entirely.

**Q: Will the gems fall out?**
A: Yes, eventually. They’re glued in, not set. You’ll lose one or two within a month. It’s not a defect—it’s the nature of inexpensive costume jewelry. A dab of clear craft glue fixes it instantly.

**Q: Is this a choking hazard?**
A: The pieces are small, but the set is marketed for 4+. The necklaces have a breakaway clasp specifically to prevent strangulation. That said, don’t let your child sleep in it, and supervise a 3-year-old if they’re playing near a younger sibling.

### The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a high-smile, low-stakes gift for a 4-6 year old. **Skip it if** you need heirloom quality or gold-toned pieces that don’t look like a happy meal toy.

This is a solid, honest 4.2/5. It does what it promises, the tiaras are genuinely cute, and the kids love it. Just buy a small craft box for storage and expect to replace a gem or two.
