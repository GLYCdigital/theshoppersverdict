This is where the iLAND set separates itself from cheap alternatives. The MDF is dense, not that flimsy particleboard that crumbles at the corners. The assembly requirement is a genuine hurdle for some. A few reviewers report stripped screws or pieces that needed gentle persuasion to fit. Nothing catastrophic, but if you're buying for a child who wants instant play, this isn't that. It's a project, not a grab-and-go toy.

Also, the set doesn't include any decorative accessories — no dishes, no plants, no wall art. You're getting furniture frames only. If you want a fully styled room, budget for add-ons.

It's true 1/12 scale, which is the standard for most dollhouses. Measure your room openings first — the furniture itself is proportional, but very old or non-standard houses might have tight doorways.

**Can I paint or customize the pieces?**
Yes. The MDF surface takes acrylic paint well. Lightly sand first, and use a primer for best adhesion.
