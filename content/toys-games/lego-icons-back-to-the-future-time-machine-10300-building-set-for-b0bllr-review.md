**The DeLorean you actually want on your shelf — if you have the patience for it.**

Most LEGO movie-licensed sets are either too small to feel real or too gimmicky to display. The LEGO Icons Back to the Future Time Machine 10300 is neither. This is a 1,872-piece tribute to the iconic DMC-12 that doesn't just sit there — it transforms. As shown in the product image, the wheels tuck under, the gull-wing doors open, and the Mr. Fusion reactor lights up (with the included light brick) when you flip the switch. It's the rare set that captures both the *car* and the *time machine* in one package.

**What you actually get**

The box contains 1,872 pieces, a light brick, and three minifigures: Marty McFly in his orange vest, Doc Brown in his radiation suit, and Einstein the dog. There's also a hoverboard, a Walkman, and a pizza box — small touches that fans will appreciate. The instruction manual is thick and includes background on the film's production. No stickers are pre-applied; you'll place them yourself, and they're plentiful.

**The build experience: rewarding but demanding**

This is not a set for casual builders. The chassis is built first, then the body panels, then the interior. The gull-wing doors are connected via a clever hinge mechanism that works — but they're the weakest point. If you grab the car by the roof, they'll pop off. One Amazon reviewer noted: *"The doors are beautiful but they don't stay shut unless they're perfectly aligned. I've had to reattach them three times."* That said, the transformation between flight mode (wheels tucked), hover mode (wheels halfway), and time-track mode (wheels down) is smooth and genuinely fun. No parts need to be removed — you just rotate the wheels and adjust the undercarriage.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The interior detail is the standout. The flux capacitor is fully visible through the rear window, the dashboard folds down to reveal the time circuits, and the Mr. Fusion reactor is a separate build that sits on the back. Another reviewer wrote: *"I've built 30+ LEGO sets and this is the most satisfying interior I've ever seen. The dashboard mechanism alone is worth the price."* The scale is also impressive — at 12 inches long, it's larger than most LEGO vehicles and looks great next to the UCS Star Wars ships.

**Where it falls short**

The build time is real. Expect 5-6 hours if you're methodical. The sticker sheet is also a frustration point — the "OUTATIME" license plate is a sticker, not a printed piece, and if you misalign it, you live with it. And while the light brick is a nice touch, it's a single small light behind the flux capacitor; it doesn't illuminate the whole car. The minifigures are good but not exceptional — Marty's face is the standard "determined" expression, and Doc's hair piece is a reused mold.

**Who should buy this**

- **BTTF superfans** — this is the definitive LEGO version of the DeLorean. No other set comes close.
- **Adult builders** who enjoy intricate, display-oriented sets with mechanical features.
- **Gift-givers** shopping for someone 16+ who loves both LEGO and 80s cinema.

**Skip it if** you want a quick build for a younger kid, or if you're a purist who hates stickers on expensive sets.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this set worth $170?**
A: For the piece count and the mechanical complexity, yes — but only if you value the build experience. It's not a play set; it's a display piece.

**Q: Can the DeLorean actually roll?**
A: In time-track mode, yes. The wheels are brick-built and roll smoothly on flat surfaces. In flight mode, the car sits on a stand.

**Q: Are there any missing pieces?**
A: A few reviewers reported one or two missing pieces, but LEGO's customer service replaces them free within days. It's not a systemic issue.

**The Verdict: Buy it if you're a fan. Skip it if you're not.**

This is a 4.6/5 set. It's the best LEGO DeLorean ever made, with clever engineering and genuine display presence. The fragile doors and sticker sheet keep it from a perfect score, but the transformation mechanism and interior detail are worth the build time. If you love *Back to the Future*, this belongs on your shelf. If you're lukewarm on the film, you're paying a premium for nostalgia you don't have.

**Buy it if** you're a BTTF fan or you want a challenging, rewarding LEGO build.
**Skip it if** you want a quick, child-friendly set or you hate stickers.
