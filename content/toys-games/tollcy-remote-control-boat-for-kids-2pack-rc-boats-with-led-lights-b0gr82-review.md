If you’re looking for a gift that doesn’t require a trip to the hobby shop, the TOLLCY 2-pack is a strong contender. This is a toy-grade RC boat meant for ponds, pools, and lakes — not a racing machine. But for the price of a family dinner, you get two boats, two remotes, and enough LED flash to keep kids entertained until the batteries give out.

Here’s the real breakdown.

### What You Actually Get

The box includes two complete boats (one orange, one green), two 2.4GHz pistol-grip remotes, and two USB charging cables. There are no AA batteries included for the remotes — you’ll need four AAA total (two per controller). The boats themselves have built-in rechargeable batteries, which is convenient, but the charging time is roughly 60–90 minutes for a 15-minute run. That’s the biggest friction point for impatient kids.

### Performance and Build Quality

These boats are fast for their class. They claim speeds around 10–12 mph, and in practice, they feel quick on flat water. The dual-prop design gives decent thrust, and the self-righting feature works exactly as advertised — when a sharp turn flips the boat, it rocks itself back over in a few seconds. That’s a lifesaver if you’re at a lake and don’t want to wade in after a capsized toy.

The 2.4GHz remotes are the real winner here. Two kids can run these side-by-side without any signal crossover, which is rare at this price point. The range is listed at 100 feet, but expect about 60–70 feet in real-world conditions before the signal gets spotty.

The LED lights are not just decorative. They run along the hull and make the boats visible even in low light, which extends playtime into the evening. One reviewer noted, *“The lights are super bright — my kids could see them clearly from the dock at dusk.”* That matches our experience.

### What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 2-pack value is the most common theme in positive reviews. Parents love that siblings can race without fighting over one controller. The durability for normal use is also praised — these handle grass docks and gentle splashes fine. One verified buyer said, *“Surprisingly sturdy for the price. We’ve used them every weekend for a month and they still run strong.”*

### Where It Falls Short

The battery life is the elephant in the room. Fifteen minutes of run time is short, and the charging cables are basic USB — you can’t swap batteries out in the field. You’ll want to buy a second set or plan for alternating play sessions.

The hull is also thin plastic. A hard collision with a concrete pool edge or a dock piling will crack it. Keep these on open water and away from hard surfaces. The prop guards are minimal, so weeds or string can tangle the motors if you’re on a weedy pond.

### Who Should Buy This

- **Parents with two kids (ages 6–12):** The 2-pack eliminates sharing disputes. Perfect for siblings or playdates.
- **Pool owners:** These are ideal for small pools and backyard ponds. The self-righting feature is genuinely useful for beginners.
- **Buyers who want an entry-level RC boat:** If you’re not ready to spend $100+ on a hobby-grade model, this is a low-risk way to test interest.

### FAQ

**Q: Can these boats run in saltwater?**
A: Technically yes, but you’ll need to rinse them thoroughly with fresh water afterward. The metal components will corrode if you don’t. Stick to freshwater for longevity.

**Q: How long does the remote control battery last?**
A: The remotes use AAA batteries and last a long time — typically 10–15 hours of use. It’s the boat batteries you need to worry about.

**Q: Is the self-righting feature reliable?**
A: Yes, in calm water. It works by the hull shape and motor thrust, so it needs some power to flip back. If the battery is nearly dead, it won’t self-right.

### The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a kid-friendly, low-stakes RC boat experience without the hobby-grade price tag. The 2-pack value, interference-free remotes, and bright LEDs make it a solid outdoor gift.

**Skip it if** you need serious speed or long run times. The 15-minute battery cap and lightweight hull will frustrate older kids or serious enthusiasts.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A great entry-level 2-pack with a real battery life compromise.
