## Where It Falls Short

The shaped pieces are a double-edged sword. For kids under 3, the irregular contours can be genuinely confusing — they may try to force pieces into wrong spots because the shapes don't give the same visual cues as traditional jigsaw tabs. You'll likely need to supervise and guide younger children through the first few assemblies.

Space is the other real constraint. These puzzles need floor room, full stop. If you're in a small apartment or have a cluttered play area, setup and takedown becomes a chore — and these aren't puzzles you can casually work on a coffee table.
