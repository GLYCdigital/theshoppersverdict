Let me be direct: if you've been collecting Marvel Legends for any length of time, you know the Human Torch has gotten the short end of the stick more than once. Prior releases leaned heavily on reused bodies and generic flame pieces that looked more like orange cotton candy than fire. The Marvel Legends Series Johnny Storm from *The Fantastic Four: First Steps* changes that calculus in a meaningful way.

This isn't just another repaint. Hasbro built a brand-new body for this figure, and it shows. The proportions are lean and athletic — Johnny should look like a stuntman, not a bodybuilder, and this sculpt finally gets that right. The jumpsuit detailing includes realistic fabric wrinkles at the elbows and knees, with the classic "4" logo tampographed cleanly on the chest. At roughly 6 inches tall, it scales properly with the rest of your Legends collection.

**What's in the box:** You get the Johnny Storm figure, four sculpted flame attachments (two for the arms, two for the legs), a larger flame piece that fits over the torso, and a clear display stand. No Build-A-Figure piece here, which is worth noting — this is a standalone release, so you're not committing to a full wave to complete a BAF.

The flame effects deserve specific praise. They're cast in a translucent orange that catches light well, with darker red tips that give them a layered, dimensional look. As shown in the product image, they attach via pegs that fit snugly into ports on the forearms, shins, and back. I've handled figures where these attachments feel like an afterthought — floppy, prone to popping off. Not here. The pegs seat firmly, and the flames stay in place through aggressive posing.

Articulation is where this figure genuinely surprises. The double-jointed knees and elbows offer a full range of motion, and the torso crunch allows for dynamic flying poses. The head sculpt captures Joseph Quinn's likeness with startling accuracy — cheekbones, jawline, that slightly quizzical brow. It's one of the best celebrity likenesses Hasbro has produced in the last two years, full stop.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The 4.8-star average across 240 ratings reflects real satisfaction. One reviewer noted, "The flame effects finally look like actual fire instead of translucent plastic chunks. Best Torch figure I own, and I have them all." Another called the likeness "scarily accurate" and praised the paint application on the eyes and hair. Multiple reviewers specifically mentioned the articulation holding poses without sagging, which is a legitimate concern with figures carrying this much add-on weight.

**Where it falls short:** The flame pieces are cast in soft PVC, which means they can warp if you display this figure near a heat source or leave it in a hot car. It's a minor issue, but worth knowing. More frustrating is the lack of alternate hands — you get one pair of open flame-hands and nothing else. No fists, no gripping hands, no additional flame blast effect. For a figure at this price point, that feels stingy.

**Who should buy this:**
- **Fantastic Four completionists** — this is the definitive Johnny Storm for your Legends shelf
- **Movie-accurate collectors** — if you want the First Steps lineup, this is the anchor figure
- **Customizers** — the new body is a great base for future flame-themed customs

**Skip it if:** you already own the retro-card Human Torch and don't care about movie accuracy, or if you're strictly a MIB (mint-in-box) collector — the flame pieces can warp if stored long-term in packaging.

**FAQ:**
- **Does it come with a BAF piece?** No. This is a standalone release.
- **Will the flames work on other figures?** The peg sizes are specific to this body, so don't plan on swapping them.
- **Is the face removable?** Yes, it's a standard head swap, compatible with other Legends figures.

**The Verdict:** At 4.6/5, this is the best Human Torch Hasbro has ever produced. The likeness, articulation, and flame effects all exceed expectations, and the minor negatives — soft plastic risk, no alternate hands — don't undermine what is a genuinely excellent action figure.

**Buy it if** you want the definitive Johnny Storm for your collection. **Skip it if** you're happy with your existing Torch and don't need movie-specific accuracy. For everyone else, this is an easy recommendation.
