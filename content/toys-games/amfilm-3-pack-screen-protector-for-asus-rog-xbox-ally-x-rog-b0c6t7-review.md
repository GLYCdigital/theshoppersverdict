## The $13 Insurance Policy Your ROG Ally X Desperately Needs

Here's the thing about the ASUS ROG Ally X: it's a $700 handheld you'll toss into backpacks, rest on kitchen counters, and hand to friends who don't treat electronics with the reverence they deserve. The stock screen is glossy Gorilla Glass, but it scratches. Mine did within two weeks. That's why amFilm's 3-pack exists — and it's why this is the first accessory Ally X owners should buy, not the fifth.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get three tempered glass protectors, each rated at 9H hardness — the same spec as most premium phone screen protectors. Also included: alcohol wipes, microfiber cloths, and dust removal stickers. It's a complete install kit. No alignment frame though — that's the one thing missing that would make this a 5-star product. You're eyeballing the alignment, which is doable but nerve-wracking on the first try.

The glass itself is 0.33mm thick, which is thin enough that the Ally X's screen still looks punchy and the touch response feels completely unaffected. The cutouts for the camera and ambient light sensor are precisely positioned — I didn't need to fiddle or adjust after install.

## Real-World Performance: The Good and the Gritty

The oleophobic coating is the standout feature here. After a 90-minute session of *Cyberpunk 2077* with greasy fingers, a single wipe with a cloth had the screen looking factory-fresh. Fingerprints don't smear — they bead up and lift off. That's rare at this price point.

Clarity is excellent. The glass adds no noticeable color shift or brightness loss. The Ally X's 1080p VRR display still looks crisp, and the anti-reflective properties are... fine. Not dramatically better than bare glass, but not worse either.

Here's where it gets honest: **the edges don't fully adhere**. The Ally X has a slightly rounded 2.5D display edge, and this flat glass protector leaves a thin air gap along the perimeter. You can see it as a faint silver line when the screen is off. It doesn't affect touch response or visibility in normal use, but if you're the type who notices imperfections, you'll see it. Multiple Amazon reviewers confirm this — one user wrote, "Fits well but edges don't lay flat," and another noted "the edges are slightly raised but not enough to bother me." That's the tradeoff you accept with a flat glass protector on a curved-edge screen.

## Who Should Buy This

- **New ROG Ally X owners**: Buy this before your first scratch. The stock screen is vulnerable, and this is cheap insurance.
- **People who actually travel with their handheld**: The 3-pack means you're covered when the first one gets a hairline crack from a drop.

**Skip it if** you're a perfectionist who will obsess over the edge air gap — you'll want a wet-apply film or a curved-edge-specific protector that conforms better.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this fit the original ROG Ally or just the Ally X?**
A: amFilm markets this for both models. The Ally X has a slightly different screen assembly, but the protective glass dimensions work for both. That said, the cutout alignment is optimized for the Ally X — original Ally owners may see slightly off-center camera cutouts.

**Q: Will the screen protector interfere with the Ally X's touchscreen or stylus?**
A: No. Touch response is identical to bare glass. The 0.33mm thickness doesn't create enough distance to affect capacitive touch.

**Q: How hard is the install without an alignment frame?**
A: Harder than it should be, honestly. Use the included dust stickers, line up the top edge first (where the camera cutout is), then gently lower the rest. The glass is rigid enough that you can't peel it up and reapply — if you mess up, open the second pack. That's the advantage of buying a 3-pack.

## The Verdict: Buy It

**Buy it if** you own an ROG Ally X and value your screen's longevity. The 3-pack pricing means you're covered for months of use with spares for the inevitable bad install or future scratch.

**Skip it if** you demand edge-to-edge adhesion on curved displays — this flat glass won't deliver that, and you'll be annoyed every time you see the air gap.

For $13, this is the best screen protection value for the Ally X on Amazon. The edge issue is a compromise, not a defect. Your screen will survive the drops, the scratches, and the greasy fingers. That's worth the price of admission.
