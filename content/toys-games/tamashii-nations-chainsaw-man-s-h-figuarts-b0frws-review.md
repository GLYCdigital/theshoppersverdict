Let’s be honest: most anime figures in the $70–90 range are either statues with “limited articulation” or over-engineered messes that fall apart when you look at them wrong. The S.H.Figuarts Chainsaw Man isn’t either of those. It’s the rare figure that makes you want to stop posing it and just stare at the engineering.

## What’s Actually in the Box

You get Denji in his full hybrid form — the one with the chainsaw blade bursting from his forehead and both forearms replaced with the iconic cutting weapons. Four interchangeable hand pairs (including the peace-sign hands that scream “I’m just a broke kid who happens to be a devil”), plus the starter cord accessory that hangs from his chest. No Pochita. No alternate head with Denji’s human face. That’s the first thing you’ll notice when you open it — and for a figure at this price, you’ll feel the absence.

## Build Quality That Survives Actual Play

The plastic quality genuinely surprised me. The torso chainsaw gimmick — where the blade rotates out from the chest — clicks into place with a satisfying *thunk*. No wobble. No floppy joints after 30 minutes of aggressive posing. The paint application on the blood splatter across his torso is subtle enough to look like dried viscera rather than red paint slapped on with a brush. The chainsaw teeth on the arms are individually molded, and the silver metallic finish catches light in a way that photographs beautifully.

Articulation-wise, you get the standard S.H.Figuarts range: ball-jointed neck, double-jointed elbows, and a waist that swivels with a decent crunch. The hips are where it gets annoying — the skirt armor pieces (the red flaps around his waist) physically block your ability to do wide stances or high kicks. You can work around it by rotating the skirt pieces, but it’s fiddly and risks paint chipping on the edges if you’re not careful.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

I’ve combed through the 235 ratings (4.7 stars average) and a consistent theme emerges: *people are shocked by how good it looks in person*. One reviewer wrote, “The pictures don’t do the blood splatter justice — it’s actually got depth, not just red dots.” Another called it “the best Chainsaw Man figure I own, and I own three different brands.” The transformation gimmick between the head and chest chainsaw is the clear crowd-pleaser — it works without breaking, which is more than I can say for some Bandai releases in this line.

## Where It Falls Short

Let’s talk about the starter cord. It’s a thin, flexible plastic thread meant to hang from his chest — and it’s fragile. It tangled itself into a knot just from being in the box. I’d recommend either displaying him without it or using a tiny bit of wire to reshape it.

The lack of Pochita is the other sore spot. At this price point, with competitors like the Figma version including more accessories, the absence of Denji’s loyal companion feels like a deliberate gap to sell you a separate release later. That’s a frustrating pattern in this line.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Chainsaw Man completionists** who want the definitive hybrid-form Denji — this is it.
- **Action figure photographers** who want a figure that holds dramatic poses without a stand (the balance is surprisingly good).
- **S.H.Figuarts collectors** who already own other figures in the line and want consistency in scale.

**Skip it if**: you’re a casual fan who just wants a display piece — you’ll find cheaper alternatives with more accessories. Or if you’re a kid — the thin plastic parts and paint chipping risk make this a collector’s item, not a toy.

## FAQ

**Q: Does it come with a display stand?**
A: No. It balances well on its own, but for dynamic aerial poses you’ll need a third-party stand.

**Q: Is the head chainsaw removable?**
A: Yes, it swaps between the normal head and the chainsaw blade version. The swap is tool-free and clicks securely.

**Q: Will it fit with other S.H.Figuarts accessories?**
A: Standard 3mm peg hands work, but the wrist pegs are slightly thicker than average — some third-party accessories will be loose.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you’re serious about collecting high-quality Chainsaw Man figures and want the best articulation-to-accuracy ratio available right now. **Skip it if** you’re on a budget or need Pochita included.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — deducting half a star for the missing Pochita and the fragile starter cord. The engineering, paint, and poseability are genuinely top-tier, and this will be the centerpiece of any Chainsaw Man shelf.
