Let’s be honest about what happens to a Meta Quest 3S after a few months of use. The glossy plastic front picks up every fingerprint. The controllers get slick when your palms heat up mid-game. And if you’ve handed the headset to a kid or a guest, you’ve already seen the micro-scratches that appear no matter how careful everyone claims to be. That’s exactly the problem this silicone cover set from VR Protect is designed to solve — and for the most part, it does it well.

## What’s in the Box

The kit includes two full-body silicone covers for the Quest 3S headset, two controller grips (one per Touch controller), and a padded face cover that snaps over the existing facial interface. The headset covers come in a few color options; the standard black version looks nearly OEM, which is a plus if you don’t want your VR rig to look like a toy. The silicone is roughly 2mm thick — enough to absorb minor bumps without adding noticeable bulk or weight to the headset.

## Real-World Performance

Installation takes about five minutes. The headset cover stretches over the device and aligns with the sensor cutouts — and this is where cheaper covers fail. I’ve tested knockoffs that block the IR sensors or muffle the speakers. This one has precise cutouts for the four tracking cameras, the vent grille, and the speaker slits. During a 45-minute session of *Batman: Arkham Shadow*, I noticed zero tracking hiccups and no overheating, which tells me the thermal vents aren’t being choked.

The controller grips are the sleeper hit here. They wrap around the handle and the ring, adding a rubberized texture that genuinely improves grip during fast movements. One reviewer put it plainly: *“I was constantly readjusting my grip on the controllers before. Now they stay put even when I'm flailing around in Beat Saber.”* That matches my experience — the added traction is subtle but noticeable.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Scrolling through the 225 ratings, three themes dominate. First, fit. Multiple buyers noted the covers “snap on perfectly” and don’t shift during play. Second, protection — several reviewers said the cover saved their headset from scuffs after dropping it or knocking it against a wall. Third, the matte finish. As one verified purchase noted: *“The original Quest 3S is a fingerprint magnet. This cover makes it look clean even after my kids use it.”*

## Where It Falls Short

The face cover is the weakest piece. It’s a thin silicone overlay that adds minimal padding. If you’re buying this hoping it will fix the Quest 3S’s pressure-on-the-forehead feeling, it won’t. You’d be better served by a dedicated padded facial interface replacement. Also, the silicone has a slight “new rubber” smell out of the box — it fades after a day or two, but it’s there.

One more honest note: the silicone picks up lint and pet hair like a magnet for the first week. It wipes clean easily, but if you have a shedding dog, you’ll be doing a quick dust-off before every session.

## Who Should Buy This

This set is ideal for three types of people: households with kids (the protection is worth the price alone), players who demo their headset to guests regularly, and anyone who hates the greasy fingerprint look. If you’re strictly a solo player who keeps your headset on a stand and never moves it, you can probably skip this — the Quest 3S is already durable enough for gentle use.

## FAQ

**Q: Will the cover interfere with the Quest 3S’s tracking or charging?**
A: No. The sensor cutouts are precisely aligned, and the charging port remains accessible. You can charge with the cover on — no need to remove it.

**Q: Does this fit the Quest 3 as well?**
A: No. This is specifically molded for the Quest 3S, which has a different shape and sensor layout. Don’t buy it for a standard Quest 3.

**Q: Can you still use the headset with the face cover attached?**
A: Yes, and it’s comfortable enough for short sessions. But if you wear glasses or play for more than an hour, pair it with a thicker padded facial interface instead.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want cheap, effective protection against scratches, drops, and fingerprints, or if you need better controller grip during sweaty sessions. **Skip it if** you’re looking for a comfort upgrade — this is a protection product, not an ergonomics fix.

At a 4.6/5, this is one of the better accessory values for the Quest 3S. It does exactly what it promises, fits perfectly, and costs less than a single replacement controller. That’s a solid deal.
