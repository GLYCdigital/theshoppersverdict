If you’re chasing Miraidon ex, Iron Hands ex, or any of the futuristic Paradox Pokemon, the Scarlet & Violet 10.5 Black Bolt Booster Bundle is the most efficient way to crack packs from this set. It’s not flashy — no promo, no accessories — but it delivers exactly what it promises: six packs of a solid expansion at a slight per-pack discount.

Let me be clear about what this isn’t first. This is not the mainline Scarlet & Violet base set, and it’s not a premium collection box. This is a supplemental mini-set, and the Black Bolt version specifically focuses on the future Paradox Pokemon from Area Zero. The bundle itself is a plain cardboard box — no frills, no extras. You get six booster packs, and that’s the whole transaction.

**What’s actually in the box**

Six booster packs from the SV 10.5 Black Bolt subset. Each pack contains 10 cards (one basic Energy, five common/uncommon, three reverse holos or better, and one rare slot). Weighing in at roughly 60 cards total, you’re looking at a full deck’s worth of cards for around the price of five individual packs at retail. That’s the math that makes this bundle attractive.

**Performance and pull rates**

Here’s the honest truth about this set: the pull rates are decent but not generous. The chase cards — Miraidon ex, Iron Hands ex, Iron Valiant ex — appear in roughly 1 in 12 to 1 in 15 packs based on community data. That means your six-pack bundle gives you about a 40-45% chance of pulling one of the big ex cards. You’ll almost certainly get at least one holo or better, but don’t count on a guaranteed double-hit.

The card quality itself is solid. The artwork on the Paradox Pokemon is genuinely striking — the futuristic designs pop against the dark background treatment. The texture on the ex cards and full arts is on par with recent Scarlet & Violet era sets, which is to say, excellent. These cards feel premium in hand.

Third, the packaging is aggressively minimal — no divider, no promo card, nothing. It’s a pure booster bundle, take it or leave it.
