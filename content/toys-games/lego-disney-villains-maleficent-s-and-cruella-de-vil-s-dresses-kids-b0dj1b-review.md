**LEGO Disney Villains Maleficent’s and Cruella De Vil’s Dresses Review: Two Iconic Villains, One Clever Set**

Let’s be honest—LEGO has made a lot of Disney sets over the years, and most of them are variations on the same castle-and-princess formula. This one breaks the mold. Instead of another palace, you get two buildable dress forms that turn your favorite villains into wearable-style display pieces. At 4.9 stars from 245 ratings, buyers are clearly impressed. But is it worth your money? Let's break it down.

**What’s Actually in the Box**

You get two separate dress builds: Maleficent’s purple-and-black gown with her signature horned headpiece, and Cruella De Vil’s black-and-white fur coat with red accents. Each dress sits on a display stand, and both come with their respective minifigures—Maleficent with her staff and dragon form, Cruella with her iconic car. The box also includes a small assortment of accessories like a cup of tea and a cigarette holder (in typical LEGO-safe form, it's a plain black stick).

The piece count is substantial—roughly 700 pieces across both builds—so this isn't a quick 10-minute set. Expect a solid hour or more of assembly time, especially if you're building with a child.

**Build Quality and Real-World Play**

The dresses themselves are cleverly constructed. The skirts use a combination of curved slopes and angled bricks to create that flowing silhouette, and the color blocking is true to the characters. Maleficent's gown has that deep purple with black trim, and the horns attach cleanly to the headpiece. Cruella's coat uses white and black plates in a checkerboard pattern that actually looks like fur from a distance—nice touch.

The minifigures are the highlight. Maleficent's headpiece is a single molded piece with the horns integrated, which is a step up from older versions that used separate clip-on horns. Cruella's coat is a new torso print with the fur collar wrapping around the neck. Both have that slightly menacing smirk you'd expect from villains.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.9-star rating isn't inflated. Reviewers repeatedly mention the display value—these look great on a shelf, especially next to the hero counterparts. One buyer noted, "The dresses are more detailed than I expected from the photos. The color contrast really pops." Another praised the build experience: "My 8-year-old and I built both in one afternoon. The instructions were clear, and the pieces fit perfectly as always."

Parents also appreciate that this isn't a fragile display-only set. The dresses are sturdy enough for play, and the minifigures can be removed and used with other LEGO sets.

**Where It Falls Short**

The symmetrical nature of dress builds gets tedious. You'll repeat the same skirt section four times per dress, which can bore younger kids. If you're buying this for a child under 7, expect to step in for the repetitive parts.

The lack of printed instructions is a real annoyance. Only the digital PDF is included, so you'll need a phone or tablet during assembly. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing before you buy.

**Who Should Buy This**

This set is perfect for three types of shoppers: Disney adult collectors who want display pieces, parents of kids who love villains (yes, they exist and they're often more passionate than princess fans), and LEGO enthusiasts who want a build that's different from the usual castle or car. If you're looking for a gift for a Disney fan who already has the standard princess sets, this is a refreshing change.

Skip it if you're strictly a minifigure collector—you can get Maleficent and Cruella in cheaper sets. And if you want a quick build, this isn't it.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these dresses wearable?**
A: No. They're buildable display models on stands, not actual clothing. The name "dresses" refers to the dress-shaped builds.

**Q: How tall is each dress?**
A: Each dress stands about 8 inches tall on its display stand. They're compact enough for a shelf but substantial enough to notice.

**Q: Is the dragon form a separate build?**
A: Yes, Maleficent's dragon is a small separate build that attaches to her dress base. It's not a full-scale dragon, but it's a nice bonus.

**The Verdict**

This is one of the better Disney villain sets LEGO has produced. The build is engaging, the minifigures are top-tier, and the display value is excellent. The repetitive sections and digital-only instructions keep it from perfection, but for the price and play value, it's a strong addition to any LEGO collection.

**Buy it if** you want a unique Disney display piece or a gift for a villain-loving kid. **Skip it if** you're on a tight budget and just want the minifigures—there are cheaper ways to get them.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A stylish, well-executed set that earns its high ratings with solid construction and genuine charm.
