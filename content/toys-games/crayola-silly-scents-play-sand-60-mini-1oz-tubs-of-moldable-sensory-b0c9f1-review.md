If you've ever bought scented play sand before, you know the disappointment: it arrives dry, crumbly, and smells like a candle factory explosion. The Crayola Silly Scents Play Sand 60-pack sidesteps most of those problems, but it brings its own quirks. With 220 Amazon ratings averaging 4.4 stars, this is a solid sensory toy — but "solid" doesn't mean "perfect for everyone."

**What's actually in the box**

You get 60 individual 1oz tubs of moldable sand. That's it — no tools, no trays, no storage bin. Each tub is a twist-off lid over a small plastic cup. The colors are vibrant: pinks, blues, greens, purples, oranges. Scents include bubble gum, grape, lemon, and a few others across the range. The packaging is compact — about the size of a shoebox — so it's easy to store on a shelf or toss in a closet between uses.

**Real-world performance**

The sand itself is where Crayola earns its keep. The texture is closer to wet beach sand than the crumbly kinetic sand you've tried before. It holds a shape when you press it into a mold, and it doesn't dry out mid-play. My test: I packed a small castle mold, let it sit for 30 minutes, and it held its form without crumbling. That's genuinely impressive for a product in this price range.

The scent is the other headline feature. It's noticeable but not overwhelming — you'll smell it when you open the tub, but it won't fill the room. One buyer noted, "My 4-year-old loved the scents but they faded after about three weeks of daily play." That tracks with my experience. The scent lives on the surface of the sand, and as kids dig and mix colors, the fragrance dissipates.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.4-star rating isn't an accident. The most common positive thread in customer reviews is the quantity. One verified purchase said, "Perfect for my daughter's birthday party — each kid got 3 tubs and we still had leftovers." Another praised the moldability: "It's the closest thing to actual sandcastle sand I've found for indoor use." Parents also appreciate that it's non-toxic and doesn't stain carpets — a real concern with colored sensory materials.

**Where it falls short**

The 1oz tub size is the biggest tradeoff. It's great for portioning out to multiple kids, but terrible if you want to build anything substantial. You'll need at least 4-6 tubs of the same color to make a small castle, and the colors don't always match across the pack. One reviewer put it plainly: "The tubs are cute but tiny. My son went through 8 tubs in one sitting trying to make a 'volcano.'"

The lids are another weak point. They're easy for toddlers to pop off, and if a tub gets left open overnight, the sand dries into a crumbly mess that won't mold anymore. You can revive it with a damp paper towel, but that's a hassle you shouldn't need.

**Who should buy this**

This is a party favor, classroom reward, or travel toy — not a serious sandcastle kit. Buy it if you're hosting a birthday party and want a take-home gift that won't break the bank. Buy it if you're a teacher stocking a sensory bin for a small group. Buy it if your kid is more interested in scooping and pouring than building structures. The 60-tub count means you can distribute generously and still have a stash for later.

Skip it if you want a single-color, large-format sand experience. For that, you're better off with a 2-3 pound bucket of traditional kinetic sand. Skip it if your child is under 3 — the small tubs are a choking hazard risk if the lid comes off.

**FAQ**

*Can you mix the colors together?*
Yes, but the scents will blend too. Mixing pink bubble gum with green apple creates a brownish color with a muddled smell. It's fine for open-ended play, but don't expect to keep colors pristine.

*Does it stain hands or surfaces?*
No. It's formulated to be non-staining on skin and most fabrics. I tested it on a white cotton t-shirt and a light-colored rug — both cleaned up with a quick wipe.

*How long does it stay moldable once opened?*
With the lid sealed properly, it stays usable for weeks. If left open, it dries out within a day. Store the tubs upright in a cool, dry place.

**The Verdict**

Buy it if: You need a versatile, portion-controlled sensory toy for group settings or want to test if your kid likes scented sand before committing to a larger bucket.

Skip it if: You want a single-color sand for serious building, or you're tired of managing dozens of tiny containers.

The Crayola Silly Scents Play Sand 60-pack earns its 4.2/5 rating through sheer versatility and quality texture. The small tubs are a blessing for distribution and a curse for ambitious projects. For the price per tub, it's a fair value — just know what you're signing up for before you open that first lid.
