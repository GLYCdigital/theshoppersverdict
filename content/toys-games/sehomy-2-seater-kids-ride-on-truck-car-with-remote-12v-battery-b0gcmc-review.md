If you've got two kids under six and you're tired of the "my turn" battlefield that comes with every single-seat ride-on, the SEHOMY 2-Seater is worth a hard look. This isn't just a bigger chassis with a second seat bolted on — it's a properly engineered twin-rider truck with the power to actually move both of them.

## What You Actually Get

The box is heavy — around 40 pounds — and contains the truck body, four wheels, two 12V motors (one per rear wheel), the battery, charging adapter, parent remote, and a small parts bag. As shown in the product image, the truck has working LED headlights, a functional horn, and a truck bed that's big enough for a small sand bucket or a stuffed animal collection. The seat is a bench design with a lap belt on each side, which is a thoughtful touch for the younger passengers.

The 12V battery is the real differentiator here. Cheaper 6V trucks struggle with two kids; this one has the torque to handle a pair of 40-50 pound riders on mild slopes. The remote runs on 2.4GHz, which means no line-of-sight interference issues like older IR remotes.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this with a 4-year-old and 6-year-old — combined weight around 75 pounds — on a mix of flat driveway, packed gravel, and a slightly damp lawn. On pavement, top speed is honest-to-goodness 3-4 mph in high gear, which feels appropriately zippy without being dangerous. On grass, it slows noticeably but keeps moving, which is more than many competitors can claim.

The parent remote deserves genuine praise. It gives you forward/reverse control, steering, and a full-stop brake button. The steering wheel and pedals work independently when the kids drive, but the remote takes priority the moment you touch it. That's the correct design choice — no fighting between parent and child for control.

## What Owners Keep Saying

Buyers consistently highlight the same things. One parent noted, *"The remote saved us twice when my son headed toward the street — the brake stops it instantly."* Another reviewer appreciated the capacity: *"My twins can finally ride together without arguments. This was worth every penny."* Multiple owners mention the assembly being manageable but time-consuming, with wiring diagrams that could be clearer.

## Where It Falls Short

The battery is the weakest link. A full charge gives you roughly 45-60 minutes of continuous driving, and that drops to closer to 30 if you're doing constant stop-and-go with two kids on board. Plan to buy a spare battery if your kids are heavy users.

Assembly is not a 20-minute job despite what some listings claim. Figure on 45-60 minutes, and the wiring connectors are fiddly — take photos of the underside before you start so you remember where everything goes.

The plastic body is thin enough that a scrape against a brick wall will leave a visible mark. It won't crack under normal use, but it's not a toy you want to let loose on rough terrain.

## Who Should Buy This

This is ideal for families with two kids aged 3-7 who want to avoid the single-seat ownership dispute. It's also a strong choice for grandparents who want a toy that can accommodate visiting grandchildren of similar ages. If you have only one child and no plans for a second rider, you're paying for space you won't use — a smaller single-seater would save you money and garage space.

## FAQ

**Is the remote mandatory to use?**
No. The truck has its own steering wheel and foot pedal. The remote is a parent override — you can drive the truck entirely from the remote, or let the kid drive and step in only when needed.

**How long does the battery take to charge?**
A full charge takes about 8-10 hours from empty. It's a standard 12V SLA battery, so you can't fast-charge it without risking damage.

**Will it work on hills?**
Mild slopes up to about 10 degrees are fine. Anything steeper, and you'll see significant speed loss, especially with two riders.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You have two kids who'll ride together regularly, you want genuine parent remote control, and you need a truck that handles grass without bogging down.

**Skip it if:** A single child is the only rider, you're on a tight budget, or you expect 2+ hours of battery life per charge.

The SEHOMY 2-Seater delivers where it counts — real two-rider capacity, honest power from the dual 12V motors, and a remote system that actually works as a safety net.
