The Losi Super Baja Rey 2.0 isn't just another RC truck. At 1/6 scale, it's a monster — nearly two feet long and over 30 pounds of steel, aluminum, and brushless fury. This is the truck you buy when you're done with toys and want something that genuinely terrifies your neighbors when it rips down the street.

## What You Actually Get

Open the box and you're staring at a fully assembled truck — no build required unless you want to break it down for maintenance. The Rey 2.0 ships with the Spektrum Firma 130A ESC and 4074 2000Kv brushless motor, a 6-channel DSMR receiver, and the Spektrum DX3 transmitter. You'll also get the manual and decals, but no battery or charger. That's an extra $150+ out of pocket for a 3S or 4S LiPo setup.

The construction is what sells this truck. It's built on a tube-frame chassis with a Ford F-150 Raptor-style body, fully independent front suspension with a solid rear axle on 4-link geometry. The aluminum shocks are pre-filled and ready to run. Everything feels premium — this isn't hollow plastic pretending to be tough.

## Performance and Real-World Use

Out of the box on 3S, you're looking at 40-45 mph. Drop a 4S pack in and it'll push past 50 mph — fast enough that you'll need a large open area just to use it responsibly. The brushless system is smooth and controllable, with no cogging at low speeds. Throttle response is instant.

The suspension is the star. I've launched this truck off 6-foot dirt mounds and it lands flat, soaking up the impact without bottoming out. The solid rear axle gives it that real trailing-arm feel — it hooks up and pulls hard. On loose dirt, it slides predictably. On pavement, the tires wear fast and the truck gets twitchy at speed. It's a desert truck; keep it on dirt.

One buyer on Amazon put it simply: *"This thing is an absolute beast. I've had Traxxas and Arrma trucks, and nothing compares to the build quality here."* Another said: *"The suspension is unreal. It eats bumps that would flip my other trucks."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be real about the downsides. First, the range. The stock receiver gives you maybe 300 feet before signal gets sketchy. At 50 mph, that's about four seconds of full throttle before you're out of range. I've seen videos of guys adding external antennas or swapping receivers entirely. That's a frustrating limitation on a truck at this price point.

Second, when you crash a 30-pound truck at speed, things break — and parts aren't cheap. The plastic A-arms are the weak point, and a bad hit at full throttle can cost you $30-$50 in parts plus shipping. The steel gears hold up, but the axles and hubs will bend before the drivetrain gives.

Third, the battery tray is annoyingly tight. The included specs say it fits standard 3S packs, but taller 5200mAh+ packs are a squeeze. You'll need to check dimensions before buying batteries. One reviewer noted: *"Had to shave foam and reposition the strap to fit my Gens Ace packs. Design flaw for sure."*

## Who Should Buy This

This truck is for the serious RC enthusiast who wants scale realism with legitimate speed. If you're a parent buying a first RC for a kid, stop — this is too fast, too heavy, and too expensive to repair. If you're coming from a Traxxas Slash or Arrma Senton and want the next step up, the Rey 2.0 delivers.

It's also great for owners of large properties or those with access to open dirt lots. You need room to let this thing stretch its legs. Suburban yards are too small.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I run this on 4S without upgrading anything?**
A: Yes. The Firma 130A ESC and motor are rated for 4S out of the box. The drivetrain handles it fine. Just be aware that speeds above 50 mph require excellent driving skill and open space.

**Q: How long does a battery last?**
A: With a 5000mAh 3S pack, expect 20-25 minutes of mixed driving. Hard throttle runs drain it in about 15 minutes. Buy two batteries minimum.

**Q: Is the body durable?**
A: The Raptor-style shell is thick polycarbonate, but it's also a big target. It'll take light tumbles, but a full-speed rollover will crack it. Apply the included body protection film if you plan to bash hard.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You're a seasoned RC owner ready for a high-maintenance, high-reward big-scale truck. You have open space, a healthy parts budget, and you appreciate the engineering that goes into a true desert racer.

**Skip it if:** You're on a tight budget, want a grab-and-go basher for the backyard, or you're new to RC and will likely crash hard within the first week.

The Losi Super Baja Rey 2.0 earns a 4.2/5. It's not perfect — range and battery fit are legitimate gripes — but the build quality, suspension performance, and sheer presence make it one of the most impressive RTR trucks on the market. It's a serious investment, but it delivers a seriously fun experience.
