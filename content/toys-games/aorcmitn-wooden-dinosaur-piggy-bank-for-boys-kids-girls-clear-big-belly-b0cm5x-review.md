This bank takes repeated abuse. The wood is genuinely solid — not that thin craft-store plywood that splits if you look at it wrong. The bottom has a removable plug for emptying — a feature that separates this from banks where you're stuck shaking coins out through the slot. It's snug enough that coins won't spill during normal handling, though I'll get to the catch in a moment.

## Where It Falls Short

Also, the coin slot is tight — folded dollar bills need careful insertion, and some larger foreign coins won't fit at all. Finally, the paint, while durable, can chip if the bank is dropped on hard tile repeatedly. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing.
