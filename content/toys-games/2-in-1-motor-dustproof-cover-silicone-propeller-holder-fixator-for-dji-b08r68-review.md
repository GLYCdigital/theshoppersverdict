If you've ever pulled a drone out of a backpack to find the motor bells coated in trail dust or the prop blades bent from rattling around loose, this little silicone kit is the $20 fix you didn't know you needed. The (2-in-1) Motor Dustproof Cover + Silicone Propeller Holder packs both protection jobs into one stretchy piece of molded rubber.

**What's Actually in the Package**

You get two identical silicone assemblies — one for each arm pair. Each piece combines a hollow cap that slides over the motor bell with a flat, slotted tab that traps the folded prop blades against the arm. The whole thing is one continuous mold, so there's no separate strap to lose or clip to break. It's about the size of a deck of cards when installed, and weighs almost nothing. For DJI Air 2S and Mavic 3 series owners, this is the correct fit; older Mavic Air or Mini models need a different variant.

**How It Performs in the Real World**

The silicone is the right durometer — soft enough to stretch over the motor without fighting you, firm enough to stay put once seated. The prop holder slot grips folded blades snugly, preventing the annoying "prop flop" that happens when you open a case and find blades at odd angles. The dust cover seals around the motor bell's base, which is where grit actually gets in. One reviewer noted, *"I crashed in sandy dirt, and after washing the drone, this kept the motors from grinding on the next flight. Worth it just for that."* That's the real-world scenario that matters — this isn't cosmetic.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit my DJI Mini 3 Pro?**
A: No. This is sized for the Air 2S and Mavic 3 series. The Mini lineup uses a different prop geometry and motor spacing. Check the listing's compatibility table for your exact model.

**Q: Does the dust cover block the motor vents?**
A: It covers the bell but leaves the cooling vents at the base partially exposed. It's designed to block debris ingress during storage, not during flight — you remove it before flying anyway.

**Q: Can I leave it installed while charging or storing in a hard case?**
A: Yes, that's the intended use. It fits within most hard case foam cutouts designed for the drone, though you may need to compress the foam slightly on the first install.
