If your kid has been begging for “real makeup” and you’ve been dodging it because you don’t want glitter bombs and stained carpets, the GORISPI Kids Makeup Set is the compromise that actually works. This is not a plastic playset with pretend compacts. It’s a 66-piece kit of genuine, washable cosmetics sized for small hands and young faces — and at a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, it’s clearly hitting the mark for most families.

## What’s Actually in the Box

The set is generous. You get 12 eyeshadow palettes, 6 lip glosses, 4 blushes, 2 compact powders, lip balms, makeup brushes, a sponge applicator set, and a few nail polishes — all tucked into a sturdy pink carrying case with a mirror. The case measures roughly 10 x 8 inches when closed, so it fits on a shelf but won’t disappear into a toy bin. Each item has a designated slot, which matters more than you’d think — it keeps the kit from becoming a chaotic pile of loose compacts within a week.

## Real-World Performance and Build Quality

The formulas are where this set earns its keep. The eyeshadows are pressed powders with decent pigmentation for a kids’ product — not chalky, but not professional-grade either. They blend easily and, crucially, wash off with warm water and mild soap. One parent noted, “My 7-year-old went full glam before a sleepover and it came off with just a wipe. No scrubbing, no tears.” The lip glosses are non-sticky and taste neutral, which is a relief — no one wants artificial strawberry flavoring on a 6-year-old’s mouth.

The brushes are soft enough for sensitive skin, and the handles are chunky, making them easy for little fingers to grip. The case itself feels reasonably durable — the hinge is solid, and the latch closes securely. That said, it’s not indestructible. If a toddler treats it like a step stool, the case will crack. It’s built for a 5-to-10-year-old’s use, not a demolition test.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The overwhelming theme in reviews is the “real” factor. Kids love that it’s actual makeup, not pretend. Parents love that it’s safe and washable. A verified buyer wrote: “My daughter feels like a big kid now, but I don’t worry about her using harsh chemicals. This is the best middle ground.” Another reviewer highlighted the organization: “The case keeps everything neat. My daughter actually puts things back because everything has a spot.”

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect. The pressed powders — especially the blushes and eyeshadows — are dusty. If your child applies with a heavy hand, expect fallout on cheeks and clothing. It washes out, but you’ll want to supervise the first few uses to establish a light-touch habit.

The lip gloss tubes are on the small side (roughly 2 inches tall), and the caps can loosen over time. If the set gets tossed in a backpack, you may find gloss on the inside of the case. It’s minor, but worth noting if you’re buying for a kid who isn’t gentle with belongings.

Also, the nail polishes are water-based and chip quickly. They’re fine for a few hours of play, but don’t expect salon durability.

## Who Should Buy This

This set is ideal for:
- **Parents of 5-to-10-year-olds** who want a safe first makeup experience
- **Gift-givers** looking for a birthday or holiday present that feels “big kid” without the risk
- **Families with multiple kids** — the 66 pieces means siblings can share without fighting over the same palette

Skip it if you’re buying for a child under 4 (small compacts are a choking hazard) or if you want a purely pretend playset — this is real cosmetics, so it requires some supervision.

## FAQ

**Is this makeup safe for sensitive skin?**
The formulas are fragrance-free and dermatologist-tested, but every kid is different. Do a patch test on the inner arm before the first full application.

**How do you remove it?**
Warm water and mild soap or a makeup wipe. It’s not waterproof, which is a feature — it comes off easily.

**Is the case refillable?**
The compartments are fixed, but the items are standard sizes. You can replace individual compacts with similar-sized products when they run out.
