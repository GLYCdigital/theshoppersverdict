## FAQ

**Is the paint safe if my toddler puts the pieces in her mouth?**
Yes. The paint is non-toxic and water-based, and the wood is sealed. That said, you should still supervise — the pieces aren't designed to be chewed on, and small parts pose a choking hazard.

**How do you clean it?**
Wipe with a damp cloth. The sealed wood finish means you can use mild soap and water without damaging the paint. Avoid soaking or dishwasher use.

**Is this suitable for a 2-year-old?**
Yes, but with supervision. The larger pieces like the lipstick and compact are fine for 2-year-olds, but the smaller accessories like the nail polish brush should be kept away from kids who still mouth objects.
