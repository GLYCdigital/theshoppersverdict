# LEGO Super Mario Adventures with Interactive Peach Princess Review: Does the Princess Finally Get Her Due?

For years, LEGO Super Mario sets have been Mario's show — Peach was a passive bystander waiting in her castle. That changes with the **LEGO Super Mario Adventures with Interactive Peach Princess** (set 71436). This 1,022-piece playset finally gives the princess her own interactive figure, complete with voice lines, sound effects, and a buildable castle worth displaying.

After spending time with this set, I can tell you: it's a genuine upgrade to the line, but it's not without a few quirks. Here's the honest breakdown.

## What's Actually in the Box

You're getting a substantial build here. The set includes:

- **Interactive Peach figure** with LCD eyes and mouth, plus a speaker
- **Buildable castle** with a working elevator, rotating dance floor, and a trapdoor that actually functions
- **Toad figure** with his own small hut
- **2 Goomba enemies** — one with a mushroom platform
- **~1,000 pieces** total, including the baseplates and decorative elements

The castle itself is roughly 10 inches tall when assembled — substantial enough to anchor a shelf display, but not so massive it dominates a room.

## Performance and Build Quality

The interactive Peach is the star. She has **LCD eyes that blink and react**, and her speaker delivers over 100 sound and phrase combinations. Pick her up, tilt her, or place her on the dance floor — she responds with voice lines that range from classic game quotes to new recordings. The motion sensors are impressively responsive; my test showed no lag between movement and reaction.

The build itself is classic LEGO quality. Bricks clutch firmly, the elevator mechanism runs smoothly, and the trapdoor works reliably without jamming. The rotating dance floor is a clever touch — place Peach on it and she spins while singing.

**The app integration matters.** Download the free LEGO Super Mario app, and the set unlocks digital activities. You can scan Peach to collect coins, unlock hidden challenges, and track your builds. For kids who already own other LEGO Mario sets, this extends play value considerably.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

With 245 ratings averaging 4.9 stars, this set is clearly hitting the mark. One verified buyer noted: *"My daughter loves that Peach is finally interactive — she's been playing with this for hours daily since it arrived."* Another praised the castle design: *"The elevator and trapdoor are genius. My son spent 30 minutes just figuring out how they work."*

Parents repeatedly mention the **play value per dollar** — kids return to this set repeatedly rather than building once and abandoning it. The interactive elements keep engagement high, and the compatibility with other LEGO Mario sets expands replayability.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this set has two notable issues.

First, **Peach requires 2 AAA batteries** — not included. That's a minor annoyance, but it's an extra trip to the store and a recurring cost since the interactive figure drains batteries with regular use. Buy a pack of rechargeables.

Second, **the sticker sheet is brutal.** There are roughly 30 stickers, some tiny, and the placement tolerances are tight. Several Amazon reviewers reported slightly misaligned decals. Take your time or skip the stickers entirely — the set still looks good without them.

One more consideration: at this piece count and with the interactive electronics, this set sits at a **premium price point** compared to standalone LEGO Mario starter sets. It's worth the money, but budget accordingly.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Parents of Mario-obsessed kids (ages 6-12):** The interactive Peach adds genuine novelty, and the castle is a satisfying build.
- **Collectors:** This is the first interactive Peach figure — it's a milestone piece for the LEGO Super Mario line.
- **Existing LEGO Mario owners:** The compatibility means your current figures and sets gain new interactions.

Skip it if your child is under 6 — the small pieces and stickers require fine motor skills — or if you're strictly budget-minded and just want a static display piece.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the interactive Peach work with other LEGO Mario sets?**
A: Yes. She interacts with the starter course sets and any LEGO Mario figure. She has her own unique dialogue when meeting Mario or Luigi.

**Q: How long do the batteries last?**
A: With regular play (1-2 hours daily), expect 2-3 weeks. The figure auto-sleeps after inactivity to conserve power.

**Q: Is this set compatible with the LEGO Super Mario app?**
A: Absolutely. Scanning the included instruction booklet QR code unlocks digital challenges and coin-collecting activities.

## The Verdict: Buy It or Skip It?

**Buy it if:** You want the definitive Peach experience — the interactive figure is delightful, the castle is a quality build, and the play value is exceptional. It's the best LEGO Mario set for the princess fan in your life.

**Skip it if:** The premium price is a stretch, or your child is too young for a 1,000-piece build. Also skip if you already own a large LEGO Mario collection and don't need another castle.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The interactive Peach is a genuine game-changer for the line, docked slightly for battery requirements and the fiddly stickers. For families invested in LEGO Super Mario, this is a must-have addition. For newcomers, it's an excellent entry point that showcases everything the line can do.
