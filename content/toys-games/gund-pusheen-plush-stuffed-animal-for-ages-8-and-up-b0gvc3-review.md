**What You Actually Get**

The GUND Pusheen Plush is a 9-inch tall, bean-bag weighted plush that captures the character's signature chubby proportions perfectly. The fabric is a soft, high-quality polyester that feels substantially better than the scratchy material you find on lower-tier plushies. The details are spot-on — the tiny ears, the striped tail, the little feet, and that perpetually sleepy expression. It's not a huge statement piece, but it's the ideal desk companion or shelf decoration.

**Real-World Performance and Build Quality**

This is where GUND separates itself from the pack. The stitching is tight and reinforced, particularly around the limbs and tail — areas where cheaper plush toys tend to fail after a few weeks of roughhousing. The bean bag weighting in the bottom gives it a satisfying heft and allows it to sit upright on flat surfaces, which is a small detail that makes a big difference for display purposes. The fabric is also surprisingly resilient; it holds up well to repeated squishing and cuddling without pilling or losing its shape.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, this is a collector-grade item, not a toy for aggressive play. The manufacturer recommends it for ages 8 and up, which is accurate — while the stitching is solid, the bean bag filling can shift if it's thrown around or washed carelessly. Second, and this is worth noting: it's not machine washable. GUND recommends spot cleaning, which is a real inconvenience if you're buying this for a younger child. Finally, at roughly $20-25 depending on the retailer, it's priced at a premium compared to generic plush toys — you're paying for the licensed design and GUND's quality control, and that's a fair trade for most buyers, but it's worth knowing going in.

**FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Ask**

**Q: Is this the official Pusheen plush?**
A: Yes. It's manufactured by GUND under a licensing agreement with the Pusheen brand. This matters — official plushies have accurate colors, proportions, and higher-quality fabric than unlicensed knockoffs.

**Q: How big is it really?**
A: It's 9 inches tall from head to bottom. To visualize, it's roughly the size of a standard laptop stand or a large coffee mug. It's not a pillow-sized plush.

**Q: Can this go in the washing machine?**
A: No. GUND specifically recommends spot-cleaning with a damp cloth. Machine washing will likely damage the bean bag filling and distort the shape.
