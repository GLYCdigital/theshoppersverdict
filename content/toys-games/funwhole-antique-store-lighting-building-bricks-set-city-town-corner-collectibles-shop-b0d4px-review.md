## What You Actually Get

The box contains 1,003 numbered pieces, a full-color instruction booklet, and the standout feature: a pre-wired LED lighting system. The lighting isn't an afterthought add-on. It's integrated into the build — two hanging lamps inside the shop, a warm glow through the front windows, and a street lamp outside. As shown in the product image, the finished model measures roughly 9.5 inches tall and 7 inches wide — a compact footprint that fits on a shelf or desk without dominating the space. The build includes a hinged front door, a detailed interior with display counters and shelving, and a brick-textured facade that reads convincingly as a turn-of-the-century shop.

## Build Experience and Real-World Use

Assembly took me about four hours spread across two evenings. The numbered bags are logical — you're not hunting through a pile for a single 1x2 brick. The pieces themselves have good clutch power; connections feel solid, and the finished structure has a satisfying heft. The instructions are clear, though a few steps involving the wiring require attention. You're routing thin cables through gaps in the interior walls, and it's easy to pinch one if you're rushing.

Here's where the lighting shines: once assembled, the effect is genuinely impressive. The warm LEDs transform the set from "nice brick model" to "miniature diorama." One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: "The lights make this set look 10x more expensive than it is. Photos don't do it justice." Another noted, "The attention to detail on the interior is unexpected at this price point — there's a working hanging sign and the shelves are stocked."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The lighting is the headline. Multiple reviews call it the reason they chose FUNWHOLE over competing sets. ## Where It Falls Short

The wiring is the weak point. During assembly, one of the LED connections came loose, and I had to disassemble part of the second floor to re-seat it. A few Amazon reviewers reported the same. It's fixable, but it's frustrating when you're mid-build. Also set expectations: this is a display piece. No moving parts beyond the door hinge, no minifigures included, no play features. If you're buying for a child who wants to "play" with it, look elsewhere.
