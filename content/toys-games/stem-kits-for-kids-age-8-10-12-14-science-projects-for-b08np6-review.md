## Where It Falls Short

Be honest about these before buying:

- **The crystal project is slow.** It takes 24-72 hours for meaningful growth. For kids who want instant results, this is a test of patience, not science.
- **Adult involvement is required** for the rocket and volcano. The instructions don't always clearly flag this — you'll need to be present for those two experiments. This isn't a "set them loose" kit.
- **Materials are single-use** for the chemical components. Once the pre-measured packets are gone, you're sourcing your own baking soda, vinegar, and similar household items.
