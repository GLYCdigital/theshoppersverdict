Crazy Aaron's has built a cult following for one reason: their putty feels unlike anything else in the sensory toy aisle. The Strawberry Shortcake™ SCENTsory® Putty carries that reputation forward with a sweet twist — but is it worth your money, or just another gimmick in a tin? After digging through 235 ratings and the product specs, here's the real story.

## What's in the Tin

You get 3.2 ounces of putty in a sturdy, screw-top tin. That's roughly the size of a hockey puck — enough for two adult hands or one kid's serious play session. The putty itself is a soft pink that matches the Strawberry Shortcake branding, and the tin lid features the character's familiar face. No tools, no accessories, no filler. Just the putty and the packaging, which is exactly how Crazy Aaron's does it.

## The Texture: Where Crazy Aaron's Earns Its Reputation

This is the star of the show. The putty has that signature Crazy Aaron's feel — smooth, buttery, and satisfyingly stretchy. It pulls apart cleanly without the stringy mess you get from cheaper putties. It bounces if you roll it into a ball, holds shapes briefly if you mold it, and snaps back when you tear it fast. It's the kind of tactile feedback that keeps fidgeters engaged for 20-minute stretches.

The texture holds up over time, too. Unlike dollar-store putties that dry out in a week, this one stays pliable for months if you keep it in the tin when you're done. Multiple reviewers noted their kids have been playing with it daily for weeks with no degradation.

## The Scent: More Than a Gimmick

The strawberry scent is genuine — not that fake plastic-strawberry smell you get from cheap erasers. It's closer to a fresh strawberry candy, sweet but not cloying. The scent is noticeable when you open the tin and when you're actively playing with it, but it doesn't linger on your hands or fill the room. That's a good thing for parents who don't want their living room smelling like a candy shop.

Here's the honest caveat: the scent fades. After three to four weeks of daily handling, it becomes much subtler. Some reviewers were disappointed by this; others said it just becomes a nice background note. If you're buying this purely for the aroma, manage your expectations. If you're buying it for the sensory experience, the scent is a bonus, not the main event.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The overwhelming theme in the reviews is texture and quality. One buyer wrote, "My daughter has sensory processing issues and this is the only putty that keeps her calm. She loves the smell and the way it feels." Another noted, "I've bought cheaper putties and they all end up in the trash. This one actually lasts." The durability and the resealable tin come up repeatedly as reasons people keep coming back to Crazy Aaron's.

## Where It Falls Short

The pink color does transfer slightly to hands during extended play. It's not staining — it washes off easily — but if you're handing this to a kid in their best clothes, know that it can leave faint traces. The scent fading is the bigger issue for some buyers, especially those who expected the fragrance to last as long as the putty itself. And if you're comparing this to other SCENTsory varieties, note that this one is straightforward — no color-changing properties, no glitter, no embedded charms. It's simple by design.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Parents of sensory-seeking kids** who need a durable, calming fidget that won't disintegrate
- **Adults who fidget** and want something more satisfying than a stress ball
- **Strawberry Shortcake fans** who want a functional collectible rather than a display piece
- **Teachers and therapists** looking for a reliable classroom or office tool

Skip it if you're looking for a strong, long-lasting fragrance or if you want a putty with visual gimmicks beyond the color.

## FAQ

**Is this putty safe for young children?**
It's non-toxic and made in the USA, but Crazy Aaron's recommends it for ages 3 and up. It's not edible, so supervision is wise for kids who still mouth objects.

**Will the putty dry out?**
Not if you store it in the included tin. The seal is tight, and with proper storage, this putty stays fresh for months. Leaving it out overnight is fine; leaving it out for days is not.

**Does the strawberry scent stain or irritate skin?**
No reported irritation issues, and the scent doesn't stain. The pink color can transfer faintly with heavy play but washes off with soap and water.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a premium sensory toy that delivers on texture, durability, and a pleasant scent bonus. It's a step above anything you'll find at a big-box store, and the quality justifies the price for regular fidgeters.

**Skip it if** you're primarily after a strong fragrance or want a putty with more visual features. This one is about the feel first, everything else second.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It's not perfect (the fading scent and slight color transfer are real issues), but for a sensory toy that actually holds up, Crazy Aaron's remains the gold standard, and this Strawberry Shortcake edition is a worthy addition to the lineup.
