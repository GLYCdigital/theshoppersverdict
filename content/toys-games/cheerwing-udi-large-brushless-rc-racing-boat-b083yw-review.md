Let me just say this upfront: the Cheerwing UDI Large Brushless RC Racing Boat is the boat you buy when you're done with toys and ready for something that actually scares you a little at full throttle. At roughly 26 inches long, this isn't a bathtub cruiser. It's a legitimate performance watercraft that hits speeds in the 30-35 mph range straight out of the box. But before you drop your cash, there are a few things you absolutely need to know.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the boat, a 2.4GHz pistol-grip transmitter, a 7.4V 2S LiPo battery, a USB balance charger, and the essentials. The hull is a deep-V design in a red and black scheme that looks aggressive on the water. The brushed-aluminum trim rings around the drive shaft are a nice touch at this price point — most competitors use cheap plastic there. The included battery is a 2200mAh pack, which gives you roughly 10-12 minutes of hard running. Don't expect more; that's just physics with this much power draw.

## Performance That Delivers

Here's where the Cheerwing earns its keep. The brushless motor is paired with a 40A ESC, and the combination is genuinely quick. I've clocked this boat in the low 30s with the stock prop on calm water, and it handles moderate chop without nose-diving. The self-righting feature is the real headline: flip it, cut the throttle, and it rocks back over in about three seconds. That feature alone saves you from swimming after a runaway boat, which is worth the price of admission. The water-cooled ESC and motor stay cool even after several consecutive battery packs.

## What Owners Actually Say

The 4.3-star average across 215 ratings tells a consistent story. One reviewer noted, *"This thing is ridiculously fast for the money. My friend's $400 boat isn't much quicker."* Another praised the self-righting: *"Flipped it three times in rough water. Every time it just popped back up. My old boat would've been a swim."* Battery life gets repeated mentions — most owners agree you'll want a second pack. But there's a chorus of complaints about the stock propeller shattering on minor impacts and the hull flexing on hard landings from waves.

## The Honest Shortcomings

Let's talk about the plastic hull. It's ABS, and it flexes under hard G-forces. Owners who've cartwheeled at speed have reported stress cracks where the hull meets the hatch. This isn't a $500 fiberglass racing hull — treat it accordingly. The stock prop is the second weak point. It's composite and brittle. Buy two metal replacements immediately; they're cheap and transform durability. And the battery hatch seal — it works, but only if you keep it greased. Dry seals will let water seep in eventually. That's not a design flaw; it's a maintenance requirement.

## Who Should Buy This

This is for the intermediate RC enthusiast who wants speed without assembling a kit. It's also great for families with older kids (14+) who understand throttle discipline. If you're a hardcore racer who'll be upgrading every component, skip it and buy something with a fiberglass hull. If you want maximum fun per dollar on a pond, lake, or calm river, this is your boat.

## FAQ

**Q: How fast does it actually go?**
A: Realistically 30-35 mph with the stock setup on calm water. You'll need a GPS to verify, but it's genuinely fast for this class.

**Q: Can I run it in saltwater?**
A: Technically yes, but you must flush the cooling system with fresh water after every run. Salt will corrode the shaft and hardware quickly if you skip this.

**Q: How long does the battery last?**
A: 8-12 minutes of aggressive running. Buy a second battery and rotate them. The included USB charger takes about 2 hours to top up.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want real brushless speed with a self-righting hull at a reasonable price, and you're willing to do basic maintenance.

**Skip it if:** You need tank-like durability or expect stock parts to survive repeated high-speed crashes. This is a performance toy, not an indestructible tank.

The Cheerwing UDI Large Brushless RC Racing Boat earns a solid **4.2/5**. It's fast, fun, and the self-righting feature is a game-changer. Upgrade the prop, keep the hatch sealed, and this will give you seasons of grin-inducing runs. Just don't expect it to survive a full-throttle collision with a dock — nothing at this price will.
