If you've ever watched a $200 Commander deck slide off a table because a flimsy cardboard box gave out, you know the pain. The Arkeiliy 110+ Storage MTG Deck Box aims to end that anxiety. With 240 ratings averaging 4.8 stars, it's clearly striking a chord with players. But is it the last deck box you'll need to buy? Let's get into it.

## What You Actually Get

The box itself is a rigid, hard-shell case with a magnetic flap closure. No straps, no buckles, no zippers — just a clean, satisfying snap when you press the lid down. The exterior has a subtle textured finish that resists fingerprints, and the interior is lined with a soft fabric that won't scuff card sleeves. It's available in several colorways, so you can match it to your deck's identity or just pick the one that doesn't get lost in your bag.

The "110+" in the name isn't marketing fluff. The box is designed to hold a full Commander deck (100 cards) plus up to 10 tokens or a handful of spares. If you run double-sleeved cards, you'll fit closer to 100-105 comfortably — the extra room matters when you're using thicker Dragon Shield sleeves.

## Real-World Performance

I've been using this box for about three weeks of weekly game nights, and it's held up exactly as advertised. The magnetic closure is strong enough that the box survives a jostle in a backpack without popping open, but not so strong that you'll struggle to open it mid-game. The hinge is sturdy — no wobble, no creaking.

Card protection is the real selling point. The rigid shell absorbs impacts that would crush a standard cardboard deck box, and the interior lining prevents sleeve scuffing. Drop it from table height and your cards stay perfectly aligned inside. That's the kind of peace of mind that matters when your deck contains cards worth more than the box itself.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

Scrolling through the reviews, a few themes repeat constantly. One verified buyer noted, "The magnet is way stronger than I expected — I can shake the box and nothing moves." Another praised the capacity: "Finally a box that fits my 100-card deck AND my tokens without bulging." The build quality gets consistent shoutouts, with multiple reviewers comparing it favorably to boxes that cost twice as much.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has two notable gaps. First, it's not waterproof. The seam where the lid meets the base is snug but not sealed — a knocked-over drink will find its way in. If you play at a table where beverages flow freely, keep this box away from the danger zone.

Second, there's no internal divider. You get one large compartment, so if you want to separate your sideboard from your main deck or store dice, you'll need to add your own organizer. For a box at this price point, that's a minor miss.

## FAQ

**Will this fit double-sleeved cards?**
Yes, but expect closer to 100-105 cards rather than the full 110. Double-sleeving adds significant thickness, and while the box is roomy, it's not infinite.

**Does the magnet affect card data?**
No. The magnet is in the closure, nowhere near the card storage area, and it's not strong enough to interfere with RFID or any card-based tech.

**Can I use it for other card games?**
Absolutely. It fits standard-size cards — Magic, Pokémon, Flesh and Blood, Lorcana — anything in the 63x88mm format. Just check your card count against the 110 limit.
