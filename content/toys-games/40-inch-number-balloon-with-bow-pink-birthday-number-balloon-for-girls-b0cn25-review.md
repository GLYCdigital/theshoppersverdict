Some birthday decorations whisper. This one announces itself from across the room. At 40 inches tall — that's about three and a half feet — the Number Balloon with Bow in Pink is the kind of centerpiece that makes a milestone birthday feel like an event. And with a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, it's clearly delivering for most buyers. But before you add it to your cart, here's what you should actually know.

## What's in the package

You get the giant foil number balloon in your chosen digit (0-9), pre-attached with a decorative bow and a coordinating tassel hanging from the bottom. The foil material is thick and glossy — the pink has a soft, slightly metallic sheen that photographs well. It arrives flat, folded into a surprisingly small package, and requires inflation on your end.

## The real-world performance

Here's where things get interesting. That 40-inch footprint is both the product's greatest strength and its biggest practical hurdle. Inflated, this balloon is massive. It dominates a wall, fills a photo backdrop, and makes standard 12-inch balloons look like afterthoughts. Multiple reviewers noted it became the focal point of their party photos.

But that size demands helium — and lots of it. A 40-inch foil balloon needs roughly 14-16 cubic feet of helium to reach full float. Standard retail helium tanks (1.4 cubic feet) won't cut it. You're looking at either a commercial-size tank rental or a party store fill. Several buyers mentioned this surprise, with one noting, "The balloon itself was great but I had to go to two stores to find enough helium to fill it."

Buyers consistently praise the visual quality. The foil is durable, the print is sharp, and the bow adds an elegant touch you don't typically see on number balloons. One reviewer captured the consensus: "It looked way more expensive than it was. Everyone asked where I got it."

## Where it falls short

The helium requirement is the biggest consideration — budget for it. If you're not prepared for that logistics dance, this could feel like a hassle rather than a win.

The included bow is decorative, not structural. It doesn't help with hanging. You'll need to figure out your own mounting solution — fishing line, command hooks, or a balloon stand. The balloon itself has a standard inflation tab, but no pre-attached string or clip.

And while the pink is a beautiful shade, it's committed to a specific aesthetic. If you're planning a gender-neutral party or want to reuse it for another occasion, this colorway limits that flexibility.

## Who should buy this

This is for the parent or party planner who wants a statement piece without assembling a full backdrop setup. It's ideal for milestone birthdays — sweet 16s, 18ths, 21sts, quinceañeras — where the number itself is the theme. It also works well for gender-reveal parties, baby showers, or any event where pink is already part of the palette.

Skip it if you're working with a tight budget that can't absorb helium tank costs, or if you need something you can hang in five minutes with zero extra purchases.

## FAQ

**Can I inflate this with a hand pump or straw?**
Technically yes, but it won't float. Foil balloons need helium to rise. Without it, you'd have a 40-inch pink blob sitting on the floor — not the look you want.

**How long does it stay inflated?**
Foil balloons are the marathon runners of the balloon world. When properly sealed, buyers report them lasting 1-2 weeks floating, and much longer if you deflate and store them flat for reuse.

**Does it come with the bow attached?**
Yes. The bow and tassel are pre-attached when it arrives. You just inflate and hang.
