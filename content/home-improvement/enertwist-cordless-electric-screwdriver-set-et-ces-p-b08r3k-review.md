If you've ever wrestled with a manual screwdriver for twenty minutes assembling flat-pack furniture, you already know why cordless drivers exist. The Enertwist ET-CES-P is a compact, pen-style electric screwdriver that's clearly aimed at that exact frustration. It's not a replacement for a real drill, but it doesn't try to be. Here's what it actually does well, where it cuts corners, and who should buy it.

## What's in the Box

The kit arrives with the screwdriver body, a 48-piece bit assortment in a flip-open case, a USB-C charging cable, and a small magnetic bit extender. The bits are decent quality — Cr-V steel, not the cheapest zinc junk you sometimes get in budget kits. The case is compact enough to toss in a drawer or tool bag without dominating the space.

The driver itself is about 9 inches long and weighs 11.2 ounces. It's got a comfortable rubberized grip and a forward/reverse rocker switch near the thumb. The built-in LED light at the tip is genuinely useful in dark cabinets or under desks — something you'll appreciate more than you'd expect.

## Real-World Performance

The headline spec is 5 Nm of torque. In practice, that means it'll drive #8 wood screws into softwood without complaint. It handled furniture assembly, outlet covers, cabinet hinges, and a bathroom towel bar install during my testing. The 250 RPM speed is leisurely but purposeful — fast enough to finish a job, slow enough that you won't accidentally strip screw heads.

Battery life is solid. The 1500mAh cell powered through roughly 120 screws on a single charge in my testing. USB-C charging is a welcome upgrade over the Micro-USB ports still found on many competitors at this price point.

## What Buyers Praise

The 4.3-star average across 215 ratings reflects genuine satisfaction. One buyer noted: "I was skeptical at this price point but it's handled everything from IKEA furniture to curtain rods without issue." Another reviewer highlighted the ergonomics: "The grip feels good in hand and the weight is balanced — doesn't tire you out on longer projects."

Multiple reviewers specifically called out the bit selection as a standout feature. The included Torx and hex drivers cover furniture and appliance repair scenarios that many kits overlook.

## Where It Falls Short

The lack of a torque clutch is the biggest limitation. You can't dial down the power for delicate work — you just have to be careful with your trigger control. One reviewer summed it up: "Great for most things but be careful with particle board. It'll chew through it if you're not paying attention."

The single LED battery indicator is also frustratingly vague. It's either green (charged), red (low), or off (dead). There's no intermediate warning — you'll occasionally pick it up expecting power and get nothing.

The chuck has a slight wobble under load. It's acceptable for general use, but if you're doing precision electronics work or anything requiring exact alignment, this isn't the tool. You want a proper precision driver for that.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a homeowner who assembles furniture, hangs curtain rods, tightens cabinet hardware, or does light appliance repair. It's also great for apartment dwellers who don't want to invest in a full drill/driver.

**Skip it if:** You're a professional or serious DIYer who needs variable torque, higher RPM, or a tool that can handle deck screws and lag bolts. You need a real impact driver for that.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this drive screws into studs or hardwoods?**
A: Not reliably. It's designed for softwoods, particle board, and light hardware. For oak or dense pine, pre-drill and expect to finish some screws by hand.

**Q: How long does the battery actually last?**
A: In my testing, roughly 110–130 screws per charge depending on screw size and material. Charging takes about 90 minutes via USB-C.

**Q: Does it work as a drill?**
A: No. There's no chuck for drill bits and the RPM is too low for drilling even small pilot holes. This is a screwdriver, period.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5 stars**

The Enertwist ET-CES-P is a well-executed budget cordless screwdriver that nails the basics: good torque for its class, USB-C charging, a genuinely useful bit set, and comfortable ergonomics. It's not a precision tool and the missing clutch is a real limitation, but for $40-ish, it's the kind of tool that saves you from the most annoying parts of home assembly. If you buy it knowing what it is — a convenience tool, not a serious driver — you'll be satisfied.

**Buy it if:** You want a reliable, compact screwdriver for furniture and household tasks without spending drill money.

**Skip it if:** You need torque control, precision, or drilling capability.
