## What you actually get

The package contains a single clear-glass bulb with a standard E26 medium screw base—the same base as most household lamps, so it’s compatible with virtually every North American oven, range, and broiler. It’s rated at 40 watts (equivalent output to a standard appliance bulb), and the clear glass is a deliberate choice: frosted appliance bulbs diffuse light, but clear glass throws a more direct, brighter beam into the cavity. As shown in the product image, it’s a compact A15-shaped bulb, roughly 2.4 inches long, designed to fit behind the small protective glass cover inside your oven.

## Performance and build quality

The headline feature here is heat tolerance. Oven bulbs aren’t just lamps—they’re operating inside a furnace. The SouLight is engineered for temperatures up to 300°C (572°F) during self-cleaning cycles, which is the spec that matters most. The filament and glass are reinforced to survive repeated thermal cycling—heating up to 450°F while baking, then cooling to room temperature, over and over. That’s what kills cheap bulbs, and it’s where this one earns its keep.

In real-world use, the clear glass delivers a noticeable upgrade in visibility. Multiple reviewers note they replaced a dim, yellowing frosted bulb and immediately saw their food clearly for the first time. One buyer wrote, *“I can actually see my pizza browning now—no more opening the door and losing heat to check.”* That’s the practical payoff: fewer peek-throughs, more consistent bakes.

**Q: Is it safe for self-cleaning cycles?**
