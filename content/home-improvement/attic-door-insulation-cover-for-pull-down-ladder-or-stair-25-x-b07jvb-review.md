If you're going up and down multiple times during a project, this gets tedious. One frustrated buyer wrote, *"I wish there was a zippered panel so I didn't have to fully unstuck the whole thing every time."*

The fabric shell is also thinner than expected. It's not fragile, but it will show scuffs if you store items on top of it. It's a cover, not a storage platform — don't stack boxes on it.

Also note: the 25" width is specific. If your attic stair frame is wider than 25 inches or your opening is non-standard, measure before ordering. This is not a one-size-fits-all solution.
