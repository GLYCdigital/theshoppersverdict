If your bathroom vanity currently has a cheap plastic bar light with yellowed diffusers, the Amico Farmhouse Rustic Painted Wood fixture is the upgrade you've been eyeing. With 229 ratings and a 4.6-star average, this is one of the more popular budget-friendly farmhouse lights on Amazon. But is it actually worth the install effort, or is it just another Amazon lighting gamble?

**What's actually in the box**

The Amico arrives as a single fixture, roughly 20 inches wide — sized for a standard 24- to 30-inch vanity mirror. The body is real pine wood with a hand-rubbed painted finish that leans warm white with visible grain texture. As shown in the product image, it uses three E26 base sockets with industrial-style exposed bulbs — the kind of look that pairs well with Edison bulbs if you want that vintage cafe vibe. No bulbs are included, which matters for your total cost.

You also get the mounting plate, screws, and wire nuts. The whole thing weighs about 4 pounds, so it's light enough to mount to a standard junction box without needing extra support.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

This is where the Amico earns its rating. The wood frame is genuinely solid — not the particleboard-and-sticker fake wood you see at this price point. The painted finish has a slight texture that hides fingerprints and water spots, which is smart for a bathroom environment. The wiring is straightforward: black to black, white to white, ground to ground. If you've swapped a light fixture before, you'll manage this in under 30 minutes.

One detail buyers consistently mention is the integrated bubble level on the mounting plate. It sounds like a gimmick, but anyone who's fought with a crooked vanity light knows how much of a difference this makes. Level it once, tighten, done.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The recurring theme in reviews is the look-to-price ratio. One reviewer wrote, "Everyone who walks into our bathroom asks where we got the light. It looks like it costs triple what we paid." Another mentioned the wood quality specifically: "I expected something flimsy for the price, but the pine is actually substantial."

The three-socket design is also a plus — you can mix bulb styles or use dimmable LEDs if your switch supports them. The open design means no glass shades to clean, which is a real advantage over drum-style fixtures.

**Where it falls short**

The painted finish is hand-applied, and that means variation. One buyer noted, "The paint had a few thin spots near the edges, but a quick touch-up with craft paint fixed it." If you're the type who demands factory-perfect consistency, this may frustrate you.

Also, the fixture is rated for damp locations but not wet locations. Use it over a bathroom sink or vanity, not directly inside a shower enclosure. And since there are no included bulbs, factor in $10–$20 extra for three decent LED Edison-style bulbs.

**Who should buy this**

- **DIY homeowners** upgrading a builder-grade bathroom vanity light
- **Farmhouse or rustic decor fans** who want authentic wood, not plastic
- **Renters** (with permission) who want a high-impact change without a huge investment
- **Anyone replacing an old fluorescent bar light** — this will transform the space

Skip it if you need a wet-rated fixture, want integrated LEDs, or expect identical wood grain across multiple fixtures.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this light dimmable?**
A: The fixture itself doesn't have a dimmer, but it uses standard E26 bulbs. If you install dimmable LED bulbs and have a compatible dimmer switch, yes, it will dim fine.

**Q: What's the actual width?**
A: The fixture measures about 20 inches across. That fits most standard 24–36 inch vanity mirrors. Measure your space before ordering.

**Q: Can I install it over a mirror or must it go on the wall?**
A: It mounts to the wall via a standard junction box. If you have a framed mirror, the light typically installs above it. If your mirror is wall-to-wall, consider a sconce-style alternative.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a genuine wood farmhouse fixture that looks custom, doesn't cost custom prices, and you're comfortable with minor finish variations.

**Skip it if:** You need wet-rated, want plug-and-play integrated LEDs, or can't handle the idea of touching up a paint spot here and there.
