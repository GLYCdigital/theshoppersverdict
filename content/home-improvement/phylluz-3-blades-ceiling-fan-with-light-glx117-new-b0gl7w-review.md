Let’s be honest: most ceiling fans under $150 are either ugly, loud, or both. The Phylluz GLX117-New tries to break that mold with a minimalist three-blade design and a brushless DC motor — features usually reserved for fans costing twice as much. But does it deliver where it counts? After digging through 210 ratings and the spec sheet, here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the fan body, three ABS blades, an integrated LED light kit (not a separate bulb), a remote control with wall-mount cradle, and all mounting hardware. The 42-inch blade span is smaller than the standard 52-inch fans most people expect, so measure your room before ordering — this is built for bedrooms, home offices, and small living spaces, not open-concept great rooms.

The finish is a matte white with a clean, modern profile. As shown in the product image, the blades sit at a subtle pitch that gives it a sleeker silhouette than traditional wooden-blade fans. The integrated LED is rated at 1800 lumens with a color temperature selector — you can toggle between warm, neutral, and cool white.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The DC motor is the headline feature here, and it earns its place. At the lower speeds, the fan is genuinely silent — I'm talking library-quiet. Even at speed six, the primary noise is moving air rather than motor hum. For light sleepers or nursery use, that's a meaningful advantage over budget AC-motor fans that whine at any speed.

Air movement is respectable for the size. It won't replace a dedicated high-velocity fan in a large living room, but in a 12x12 bedroom it moves air effectively without that harsh "wind tunnel" feeling. The six speeds give you fine control that most fans in this class simply don't offer.

The light is the other standout. 1800 lumens from an integrated LED is genuinely useful — you won't need a separate floor lamp for most tasks. The color temperature adjustment is a nice touch, though it's controlled exclusively through the remote. That's the first of two notable compromises.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Reviewers repeatedly mention the quiet operation and the modern aesthetic. One verified buyer wrote: *"I was skeptical about a 3-blade fan but this looks way more expensive than it was. The motor is whisper quiet on low — my wife can sleep with it on all night."* Another noted the light quality: *"The 6000K setting is perfect for my home office. It's bright enough to work under without eye strain."*

The remote also gets credit for being intuitive — no confusing button layouts or need to hold a manual while figuring it out.

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest part. The remote-only control is a real limitation. There are **no pull chains** and **no wall switch compatibility** for speed or light. If you lose the remote, you're stuck with whatever settings were last used — and the fan won't even turn on without it. That's a dealbreaker for some households.

Installation also demands patience. The mounting plate requires a perfectly level ceiling box; if yours isn't, you'll struggle with the alignment. One reviewer summed it up: *"Took me two hours because my ceiling box was slightly off. The bracket doesn't give you any room for error."* The fan itself is also not the easiest to balance — a few buyers reported wobble at higher speeds.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you want a quiet, modern-looking fan for a bedroom or office, and you're okay with remote-only control. The DC motor's efficiency also means lower energy bills than comparable AC fans — a genuine long-term win.

**Skip it if** you need a large fan for a big room, prefer pull-chain simplicity, or have a non-standard ceiling box that might complicate mounting.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this fan with a standard wall switch?**
A: The wall switch can cut power, but all speed and light functions are remote-only. There are no pull chains or wall-switch control for fan speed.

**Q: How loud is it on the highest setting?**
A: At speed six, you'll hear airflow but no motor hum. It's significantly quieter than typical AC-motor fans at comparable speeds.

**Q: Does the light dim, and can I adjust the color temperature?**
A: Yes — the remote has a dimmer function and lets you toggle between 3000K (warm), 4500K (neutral), and 6000K (cool daylight).
