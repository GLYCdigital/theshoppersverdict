Most homeowners grab whatever switch is on the shelf at the big-box store. That's fine for a 15-amp bedroom circuit. But when you're wiring a shop, a commercial space, or a heavy-duty appliance circuit, you need something with real guts. The Leviton 20 Amp Decora Plus Rocker Single-Pole is that switch. And with a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, it's not just me saying so.

## What you're actually getting

Inside the box: one switch, two mounting screws, and that's it. No wire nuts, no instructions worth reading — if you need a manual for a single-pole switch, call an electrician. The body is the familiar Decora Plus form factor: a 1.2-inch wide rocker face that fits standard wall plates. What sets it apart is the 20-amp rating at both 120V and 277V. That 277-volt spec matters if you're working with commercial lighting circuits — most residential switches top out at 120V.

The terminals accept #14 to #10 AWG wire, and you get both side screws and back-wiring holes. For solid copper, that back-wire option speeds up rough-in work considerably. Stranded wire? Use the screws.

## Build quality and real-world handling

The rocker action is where this switch earns its keep. It's a smooth, positive toggle — you feel the contact click, not a vague wobble. The internal mechanism uses a snap-action design that reduces arcing at the contacts, which is a genuine durability factor at 20 amps. One buyer noted, "I've replaced cheap switches every few years in my workshop. This one has been in service for 18 months with a dust collector and table saw on the same circuit — no heat, no flicker, no issues."

The body is the usual Leviton thermoplastic — tough, doesn't crack when you overtighten the mounting screws, and the rocker doesn't yellow over time. The face is glossy enough to wipe clean but not so slick it looks cheap.

## Where it falls short

Honest assessment: this isn't a perfect switch. The side-mounted grounding screw sits low on the body, and in a shallow single-gang box, getting a screwdriver on it is a finger-twisting exercise. Plan for that if you're doing a retrofit.

The body is also slightly bulkier than a standard 15-amp Decora switch. In older boxes packed with wires, you might need to trim back conductors to get a clean fit. One reviewer mentioned, "I had to do some careful wire folding to get it seated in a 1960s box. Not a dealbreaker, but be prepared."

And if you're a homeowner on a standard 15-amp lighting circuit — you don't need this. The extra capacity costs more for zero functional benefit.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this on a standard 15-amp residential circuit?**
Yes, a 20-amp-rated switch is safe on a 15-amp circuit. But you're paying for capacity you won't use. Save the money unless you plan to upgrade the circuit.

**Q: Does it work with standard Decora wall plates?**
Yes. It fits any standard Decora-style wall plate. Just match the color — white, ivory, and light almond are common options.

**Q: Is this switch rated for aluminum wiring?**
No. It's rated for copper and copper-clad wire only. If you have aluminum branch circuits, you need CO/ALR-rated devices.
