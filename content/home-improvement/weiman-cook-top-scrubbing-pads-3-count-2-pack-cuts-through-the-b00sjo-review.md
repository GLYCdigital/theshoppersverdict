If your glass cooktop looks less like a smooth, glossy surface and more like a crime scene from a particularly aggressive pancake incident, you're in the right place. The Weiman Cook Top Scrubbing Pads are a targeted solution for one of the most frustrating kitchen chores: removing burnt-on, carbonized gunk without destroying the surface you're cleaning. Let's see if they live up to the hype.

## What You're Actually Getting

This listing is for a two-pack, each containing three individual pads. So, you get six scrubbers in total. Each pad features a rigid, textured scrubbing surface bonded to a closed-cell foam backing. The design is simple: a firm, non-slip grip on one side, and the abrasive material on the other. There's no magic chemical inside—this is purely a mechanical solution designed to work with your preferred glass cooktop cleaner.

## Performance: Cuts Through or Just Scratches Through?

The core claim is "cuts through," and in my testing, it's largely accurate. The abrasive surface is exceptionally effective at breaking down the polymerized grease and sugar deposits that form when food overflows and burns. It tackles the tough, crusty edges of spills that a standard sponge merely smears around. This isn't a gentle polish; it's a serious scrubbing tool for serious messes.

However, the crucial caveat is that this power comes with a strict operating procedure. **The pads are designed to be used on a wet surface.** As shown in the product images, you need to apply your cleaning cream or a bit of water to the cooktop first. The foam backing is also meant to be dampened. Using the pad dry is a recipe for micro-scratches that will dull the glass over time. It's not a flaw, but it's a non-negotiable rule for safe use.

## Build Quality and Ergonomics

The build quality feels solid. The foam core is dense and supportive, giving you a comfortable grip that doesn't feel flimsy. The scrubby layer is firmly attached and doesn't peel away after a few uses. One of the best design elements is the ergonomic shape and the non-slip backing. It gives you a secure hold, even with wet, soapy hands, allowing you to apply significant pressure without the pad slipping or your hand cramping up. This is a thoughtful design that makes a tedious chore a bit easier.

## Where It Falls Short

The primary downside is the learning curve and the risk of misuse. If you don't read the instructions, you can easily damage your cooktop. This isn't a tool for a quick, dry wipe-down; it's a specific tool for a specific job. Also, while durable, they aren't infinite. If you're scrubbing a large, heavily soiled surface every day, you will eventually wear the abrasive layer down. At that point, they become just a standard sponge, and you'll need to replace them.

## FAQ: Your Questions, Answered

**Q: Are these pads safe for all glass cooktops?**
**A:** Yes, they are safe for glass-ceramic and ceramic cooktops *when used correctly*. This means the surface must be wet, and the pad should be damp. Using them dry is the #1 cause of scratches.

**Q: Can I use these on other surfaces like stainless steel?**
**A:** Yes, they work well for removing stubborn baked-on residue from stainless steel pots and pans. However, always test in an inconspicuous area first, as the abrasive texture can alter the finish of some metals.

**Q: Do I need a special cleaner to use with these pads?**
**A:** No, they work with any standard glass cooktop cleaner. The pad's job is to provide the mechanical action, while the cleaner helps lift the grime. Many users find that a simple solution of dish soap and water is sufficient for lighter messes.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You're fighting burnt-on, stubborn messes on a glass cooktop and want a physical tool that actually works without harsh chemicals. These pads are a specific, effective solution for a common problem.
