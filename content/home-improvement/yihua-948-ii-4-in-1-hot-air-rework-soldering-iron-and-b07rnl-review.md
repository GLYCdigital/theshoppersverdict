If you're a hobbyist who's been juggling three separate tools for through-hole, SMD, and rework, the YIHUA 948-II is the kind of consolidation that makes you wonder why you waited so long. This 4-in-1 station packs a hot air pencil, a soldering iron, a desoldering gun, and a ring light into one compact base unit. At a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings, it's clearly earning its keep on hobby benches — but let's be clear about what it is and isn't before you click buy.

**What's actually in the box**

The unit ships with the main station, a hot air pencil with two nozzle sizes, a soldering iron with a standard tip, a desoldering gun, a 10W LED ring light, a spare heating element, and a small stand for the iron. The base unit is roughly the size of a large textbook and weighs enough to stay put on your bench. The two digital displays show set and actual temperatures for the iron and hot air independently — a genuinely useful feature at this price point.

**Performance and real-world use**

The hot air pencil heats up to 480°C in about 30 seconds. For removing a 20-pin SOIC chip, it held steady temperature without the airflow spiking or sagging — something cheaper units often struggle with. The soldering iron hits 350°C in roughly 20 seconds and maintains it within a few degrees. The desoldering gun pulls vacuum through a hand pump that's controlled by a separate switch on the front panel; it cleared through-hole solder joints on an old PCB cleanly, though it's not as fast as a dedicated $200+ desoldering station.

The ring light is a nice touch — not a gimmick. It's bright enough to illuminate cramped boards without shadows, which helps if you're doing fine-pitch work under magnification.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The recurring theme in user feedback is value. One reviewer put it plainly: "Replaced three separate tools on my bench. Works as well as my friend's Hakko for half the price." Another noted, "The hot air is stable, the iron is quick, and the desoldering gun actually works — I wasn't expecting that at this price." Several long-term owners mention the sleep mode that kicks in after 30 minutes of inactivity, which is a thoughtful safety feature for anyone who's walked away from a hot iron.

**Where it falls short**

The stock soldering iron tip is blunt — fine for through-hole, but frustrating for fine-pitch legs on a QFP package. Budget for a set of finer tips (they're standard 900M-series, so cheap and easy to find). The desoldering gun's handpiece gets hot near the nozzle, and after ten minutes of continuous use, it's uncomfortable against bare skin. And the vacuum pump makes a constant whirr — not loud, but it's there. None of these are dealbreakers, but they're worth knowing.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the electronics hobbyist, the repair tinkerer, or the maker who works on mixed through-hole/SMD boards and doesn't want to spend $400+ on separate dedicated stations. It's also a solid choice for a beginner who wants one unit to learn soldering, desoldering, and hot air rework without buying three tools. Skip it if you do production-level rework daily — you'll want a higher-end station with better thermal recovery and a quieter pump.

**FAQ**

**Does it work for phone repair?**
Yes, the hot air pencil handles small component removal and the iron is fine for board-level work. For battery connectors and flex cables, the desoldering gun is useful, but you'll want fine tips for the iron.

**Is the desoldering gun strong enough for multi-layer boards?**
It cleared standard through-hole joints on a 2-layer board without issue. For heavy ground planes on 4+ layer boards, expect to add fresh solder and re-pull a couple of times.

**Does it require any special voltage or adapter?**
It's 110V for the US market with a grounded three-prong cord. No transformer needed.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want one compact station that handles soldering, desoldering, and SMD rework without breaking the bank, and you're okay upgrading the iron tip.

**Skip it if:** You're a daily production user who needs industrial-grade thermal stability and a near-silent pump.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The YIHUA 948-II delivers genuine 4-in-1 capability with stable performance and thoughtful safety features. The minor gripes (basic tip, hot handpiece, audible pump) are tradeoffs you'd expect at this price. For the hobbyist or repair tinkerer, it's a smart buy that consolidates your bench without compromising on results.
