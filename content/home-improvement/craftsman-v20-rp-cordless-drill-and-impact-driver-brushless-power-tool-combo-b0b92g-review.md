**The CRAFTSMAN V20 RP combo isn't trying to reinvent the cordless drill. It's trying to give you genuinely useful brushless performance without the premium price tag. And for the most part, it succeeds.**

If you've been eyeing the red or yellow brushless combos and flinching at the price, this Craftsman kit deserves a hard look. It's the kind of set that makes you question why you'd spend twice as much elsewhere.

## What's Actually in the Box

The kit includes the V20 RP brushless drill/driver, the V20 RP brushless impact driver, two 2.0Ah lithium-ion batteries, a charger, and a hard carry case. The "RP" in the name stands for "Rapid Performance"—Craftsman's brushless platform, and it's a meaningful step up from the older brushed V20 tools.

Both tools have a compact footprint. The drill measures about 7.5 inches and weighs 3.4 pounds with the battery attached. The impact driver is slightly shorter and lighter. Neither feels bulky in tight spaces like stud bays or cabinet interiors.

## Performance and Build Quality

The drill delivers 340 unit watts out (UWO), which translates to enough torque for 1/2-inch spade bits through dimensional lumber and 1-inch auger bits without bogging down. I tested it driving 3-inch deck screws into pressure-treated pine—it handled them with authority, though the 2.0Ah battery drains noticeably faster under sustained load.

The impact driver is the stronger of the two. Its 1,800 in-lbs of torque is respectable for the class, and the three-speed selector makes it genuinely versatile. Speed 1 is perfect for driving small screws into softwood without overtightening. Speed 2 handles most general fastening. Speed 3 is for lag bolts and heavy-duty work. The precision mode—a light touch trigger setting—is a real feature, not a gimmick. One Amazon reviewer put it well: "The precision mode on the impact driver is a game-changer for cabinet hinges and door hardware. I stopped stripping screws entirely."

The 21-position clutch on the drill is responsive, and the all-metal chuck grips bits without slipping. Both tools have LED lights that illuminate the work area, though they're positioned at the base of the tools rather than around the chuck—a minor annoyance when working in tight, dark spaces.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews isn't inflated. Recurring themes in customer feedback include:

- **Battery compatibility**: These use the same V20 batteries as the rest of the Craftsman line, so if you already own V20 tools, you're set.
- **Value proposition**: Multiple reviewers noted this kit performs comparably to brushless tools costing $100–$150 more.
- **The case**: While not as rugged as some, it's well-organized with dedicated slots for both tools, both batteries, and the charger.

One verified buyer wrote: "I've used DeWalt and Milwaukee on job sites for years. This Craftsman kit surprised me—for home projects and light professional use, it's honestly 90% of the performance at half the price."

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides.

The 2.0Ah batteries are the weakest link. They're fine for light to moderate use, but if you're doing a full day of deck building or drilling dozens of holes in thick wood, you'll be swapping batteries frequently. Plan on buying at least one 4.0Ah pack.

The lack of belt clips is genuinely annoying. For a kit clearly aimed at people who work on ladders and in attics, having to set tools down constantly is a minor but real workflow interruption.

The chuck is 1/2-inch, which is standard, but it's not a metal ratcheting chuck like on higher-end tools. It works fine, but it doesn't feel as premium.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if you're a serious DIYer or a light professional**—someone who needs reliable brushless performance for weekend projects, furniture assembly, shelving, and occasional renovation work. If you're just starting your tool collection, this combo gives you the two most-used tools in any shop for a reasonable investment.

**Skip it if you're a daily-driver professional** who needs maximum runtime and durability on job sites. The battery capacity and plastic case will frustrate you. Look at the premium brushless kits from DeWalt or Milwaukee instead.

## Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Are these tools compatible with all CRAFTSMAN V20 batteries?**
A: Yes. The RP tools work with every V20 battery, old or new. If you have a 4.0Ah or 5.0Ah pack from another V20 tool, it will fit and work here.

**Q: How does this compare to the brushed CRAFTSMAN V20 combo?**
A: The brushless RP motors provide roughly 30% more runtime per charge and better torque, especially at lower speeds. The brushed version is cheaper but noticeably weaker under sustained load. The price difference is worth it if you do more than occasional light drilling.

**Q: Can the impact driver remove lug nuts?**
A: No. This is a 1/4-inch hex impact driver, not an impact wrench. It will struggle with automotive lug nuts torqued to spec. You need a dedicated impact wrench for that.
