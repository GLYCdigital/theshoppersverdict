The 5-inch-deep air filter is the unsung hero of home HVAC. While most shoppers grab whatever 1-inch filter is on sale, the deeper pleated media in a 16x25x5 filter like the Ultravation 91-005 traps significantly more dust, pollen, and pet dander while keeping airflow resistance low. The catch? Not all 5-inch filters are built the same — and this one from Ultravation has earned its 4.7-star rating with 235 verified reviews for reasons that matter.

**What's in the box**

You get a single 16x25x5 MERV 11 pleated air filter. That's it — no gaskets, no installation hardware, no nonsense. The frame is constructed from reinforced beverage board with a rigid corrugated backing. The media itself is the standout: dense, evenly spaced pleats packed into the full 5-inch depth. As shown in the product image, the pleating is noticeably tighter than what you'll find on budget brands. For MERV 11 filtration, that density translates to better capture efficiency on particles in the 1.0–3.0 micron range — think mold spores, pet dander, and fine dust.

**Performance and real-world use**

The 5-inch depth is the key engineering advantage here. Compared to a 1-inch filter with the same MERV rating, the Ultravation's deeper media bed offers roughly four times the surface area. That means it pulls more contaminants out of the air without creating the pressure drop that would force your blower motor to work overtime. Homeowners running high-efficiency furnaces or heat pumps will notice the difference — airflow stays strong, and static pressure remains manageable.

Replacement intervals matter too. Ultravation recommends swapping this filter every 6 to 12 months depending on usage, which is a realistic window for a 5-inch filter in a typical single-family home. One reviewer noted, "I've run this for eight months with two Golden Retrievers and it still wasn't fully loaded." That aligns with what the media depth should deliver.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The dominant theme across the 235 ratings is durability and fit. Several reviewers specifically mention that the frame doesn't bend or flex during installation — a common complaint with thinner, cheaper filters that warp when you push them into a tight rack. One verified buyer wrote: "The rigidity is what sold me. My old filter would bow in the middle and let air sneak around the edges. This one sits flat and seals well."

Others highlight the filtration performance in allergy-heavy households. **Where it falls short**

Here's the honest tradeoff: the Ultravation 91-005 has no built-in side gaskets. If your filter rack has gaps or irregular edges, air will bypass the media entirely. That's not a problem if your system's filter slot is tight and well-sealed — and most reviewers report it is — but it's worth checking before you order. For a few dollars more, some competitors add foam gaskets; Ultravation skips them to keep the price down.

Availability is the second issue. Amazon stock fluctuates, and several recent reviews mention delivery delays or Subscribe & Save interruptions. If you rely on autoship, you may need a backup plan.

**FAQ**

*How often should I replace this filter?*
For a typical home with one or two occupants, plan on 6 months. Households with pets, smokers, or heavy dust generation should swap at 3–4 months. The 5-inch depth extends life versus 1-inch filters, but don't push past 12 months.

*Is MERV 11 enough for allergies?*
Yes for most people. It captures mold spores, pet dander, pollen, and dust mites. If you have severe respiratory issues, consider MERV 13, but verify your HVAC system can handle the higher airflow restriction.

*Will this fit any 16x25x5 system?*
The frame dimensions are standard, but measure your existing filter's exact depth before ordering. Some systems accept only 4-inch or 5.5-inch depths, and buying the wrong size wastes money.
