**The Moen Idora in Mediterranean Bronze is the rare faucet that looks custom-installed but costs like a stock part.** At 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, it's not just popular—it's consistent. But here's what the star rating doesn't tell you: whether this centerset actually fits your sink, and whether the bronze finish will hold up in a real bathroom.

## What's in the box

Moen packs the Idora with the faucet body, two lever handles, a matching pop-up drain assembly, and all mounting hardware. The centerset design means the spout and handles sit on a single 4-inch base—perfect for standard 3-hole sinks with 4-inch centers. You won't need to buy a drain separately, which is a real value add since most comparable faucets in this price range make you pay extra for that.

The faucet itself is solid brass, not the zinc alloy you'll find on cheaper options. It has some heft—about 6 pounds in the box—which tells you the internals aren't flimsy.

## Performance and build quality

The two-handle operation is smooth and precise. Moen's 1255 cartridge (the same one used across their entire line) gives you a quarter-turn from off to full flow, and it's replaceable without draining the sink. The aerator delivers a consistent stream that doesn't splash, even at full pressure.

The Mediterranean Bronze finish is the real story here. It's a dark, warm brown with subtle copper undertones—not the flat matte black you see everywhere now. In real-world use, it hides water spots far better than chrome. One buyer noted: *"I've had this for eight months and it still looks like day one. My chrome faucet in the guest bath needs wiping every other day."* Another mentioned the finish has a slight brushed texture that makes it forgiving with hard water.

The pop-up drain works smoothly—the lift rod connects easily and the stopper seats properly without sticking, which is a common failure point on cheaper faucets.

## Where it falls short

The centerset format is the biggest limitation. If you have a sink with 8-inch spread holes, this faucet won't work—you'd need the widespread version, which Moen also makes but costs more. Also, the handles are set close together, which can feel cramped if you have large hands. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth measuring before you order.

A few buyers flagged finish consistency issues when pairing the Idora with other Moen Bronze fixtures. One wrote: *"The finish on this is slightly darker than my Moen shower valve in the same color. It's close, but noticeable if you're looking for it."* If you're matching multiple fixtures, buy them all at once from the same batch if possible.

The drain assembly is functional but basic—it's a standard pop-up, not a trip-lever or a designer piece. If you want a more premium drain, you'll be replacing something that's perfectly fine.

## Who should buy this

- **The remodeler on a budget** who wants a premium look without paying premium prices  
- **Anyone with a standard 4-inch centerset sink** who wants a reliable, low-maintenance faucet  
- **House flippers or landlords** who need something durable that tenants won't break easily  

Skip it if you have a wide-spread sink, want a modern matte black finish, or need a faucet with a side sprayer—this is strictly a two-handle centerset.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this faucet work with a 3-hole sink?**  
A: Yes, but only if the holes are on 4-inch centers. If your sink has 8-inch centers, you need the widespread version of the Idora.

**Q: Is the Mediterranean Bronze finish durable?**  
A: Yes—it's a PVD (physical vapor deposition) finish, which is more scratch-resistant than standard painting. It will hold up better than brushed nickel in high-humidity bathrooms.

**Q: Can I use this with a vessel sink?**  
A: Technically yes, but the spout height is only 5.5 inches, so it's better suited to standard drop-in or undermount sinks. For a vessel sink, you'd want a taller gooseneck model.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a solid, good-looking faucet with a unique finish that won't show every water spot, and you have a standard 4-inch centerset sink. **Skip it if** you need a wide-spread layout or want a modern matte look.

The Moen Idora earns its 4.6/5 score—it's not flashy, but it's dependable, well-made, and the included drain makes it a genuinely good deal. For the price, you're getting a faucet that will outlast the cabinet it's mounted on.
