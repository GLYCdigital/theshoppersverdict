## The cordless lamp that actually earns its name

Most "cordless" lamps are a lie. They hide a cable, or they die after two hours, or they feel like a cheap plastic toy that'll snap if you look at it wrong. The ONEWISH Cordless Lamp with 3-Way Dimmer isn't any of those things. After 225 ratings and a solid 4.5-star average, this is the rare product that delivers exactly what it promises — a genuinely portable light source that doesn't require you to hunt for an outlet or sacrifice brightness for convenience.

## What you actually get

Out of the box: the lamp itself, a USB-C charging cable, and a user manual. No wall adapter included, so factor that in if you don't have a spare USB-C brick lying around.

The lamp stands about 14 inches tall with a fabric shade that diffuses light nicely. The base is metal and weighted — a detail that matters more than you'd think, because a cordless lamp that tips over is useless. The twist-knob dimmer sits on the base, and it cycles through off → low → medium → high with a simple clockwise turn. That's it. No apps, no remotes, no pairing. It just works.

## Performance and real-world use

Here's where this lamp surprises. The battery genuinely lasts. On the medium setting, you'll get a full evening of use — six to eight hours — before it needs a recharge. On low, you can stretch it to a couple of days if you're using it sporadically. A full charge takes about three hours via USB-C.

The light quality is warm (2700K–3000K range) and pleasant for reading or ambient lighting. The highest setting throws enough light to read comfortably from a chair nearby. The fabric shade does a good job of diffusing the LED source — no harsh hotspots or glare.

I tested it on a patio table, a nightstand, and a workshop bench. The weighted base kept it stable on all three. It's not weatherproof, so don't leave it out in the rain, but it's fine for covered outdoor spaces.

## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**The battery isn't replaceable.** When it finally wears out — and it will, after a few years of daily use — you're buying a new lamp, not a new battery. That's a real environmental and cost concern.

**The low setting is still fairly bright.** If you're looking for a nightlight that won't wake your partner, this isn't it. The lowest dim is more like a "reading lamp at reduced power" than a soft glow.

**No wall adapter included.** Minor, but annoying if you don't have a spare USB-C charger.

**The fabric shade collects dust.** It's removable and wipeable, but it's not machine-washable, and white fabric shows grime over time.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does the battery actually last on a full charge?**
On low, expect 20+ hours. On medium, 6–8 hours. On high, roughly 3–4 hours. Real-world results vary based on age of battery and usage patterns.

**Q: Can you use it while it's charging?**
Yes. The USB-C port allows pass-through charging, so you can keep using the lamp while it tops up. It won't damage the battery.

**Q: Is it bright enough to read by?**
On the highest setting, yes. It won't replace a dedicated task lamp for serious reading sessions, but it's plenty for casual reading or a book in bed.
