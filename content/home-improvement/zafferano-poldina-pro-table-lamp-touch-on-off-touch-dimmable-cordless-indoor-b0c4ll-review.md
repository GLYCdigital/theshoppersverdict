The Zafferano Poldina Pro isn't just another nice-looking table lamp. It's the rare piece that actually solves the problem it sets out to solve: bringing warm, controllable light to exactly where you need it — no cord, no outlet hunt, no compromises. After 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, the buzz is real. But is this $150+ Italian-designed lamp worth the premium? Let's dig into the details.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the lamp itself (the Poldina Pro measures roughly 12.6 inches tall with a 6.7-inch diameter base), a wireless charging puck, and the AC adapter that powers the puck. That's it. No wall mount, no carry strap, no extra diffusers. The lamp body is aluminum with a polycarbonate diffuser — the matte finish resists fingerprints well, which matters for something you'll be touching constantly.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The headline feature is the touch dimmer. Tap anywhere on the metal shade to turn it on, then hold to cycle through brightness levels. It's responsive — no lag, no double-tap confusion. The memory function is the quiet hero here: turn the lamp off, come back hours later, and it returns to your last brightness setting rather than blasting you at 100%. Small touch, huge quality-of-life improvement.

Battery life is genuinely impressive. On the lowest setting, you'll get over 20 hours. At full brightness, you're looking at 9–10 hours of steady light. The 2700K color temperature is warm and comfortable for evening reading, and at 300 lumens max output, it's enough to read by but won't flood a room. This is a task-and-accent lamp, not a primary light source.

The IP65 rating is the sleeper feature. Most cordless lamps in this class are strictly indoor. The Poldina Pro shrugs off rain, dust, and the occasional splashing from a poolside drink. That's rare and genuinely useful.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The consistency of positive feedback is striking. One verified buyer noted, *"I've bought three of these — one for the bedroom, one for the patio, one for my office. The battery genuinely lasts through a full evening of use, and the touch dimmer is buttery smooth."* Another reviewer highlighted the design: *"It looks far more expensive than it is. I've had guests ask where I got it."* The portability theme repeats constantly — people aren't just buying a lamp; they're buying the freedom to move light around their space.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The proprietary charging puck is the biggest annoyance. You can't just plug in a USB-C cable when you're traveling — you need that specific puck, and if you lose it, replacements run $30+. Some users have reported the puck's connection being finicky; you need to seat the lamp perfectly or it won't charge.

The base also feels lighter than the premium price suggests. It's stable enough on flat surfaces, but if you're carrying it by the shade (the natural grip point), the whole assembly flexes slightly. It's not fragile, but it doesn't have the solid heft of a $200+ fixture.

Finally, there's no handle or hook. For something marketed as portable, that's an oversight. You'll get used to the awkward grip, but it's not elegant.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The patio entertainer**: You want warm, weatherproof light without running extension cords across your deck.
- **The bedside reader**: You like changing brightness without fumbling for a switch or app.
- **The design-conscious renter**: You want premium aesthetics without hardwiring anything.

Skip it if you need a primary room light or if you'll only use it in one fixed spot — a corded lamp at half the price would serve you better.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does the battery actually last?**
A: Real-world use lands at 9–10 hours at full brightness, stretching to 20+ hours at the lowest setting. Recharge time is about 6 hours via the puck.

**Q: Can I leave the lamp on the charging puck all the time?**
A: Yes. The lamp uses lithium-ion cells with overcharge protection. It'll trickle-charge and stop when full — no battery memory issues.

**Q: Does the touch control work with wet hands?**
A: Generally yes, but the IP65 rating covers environmental moisture, not deliberate water exposure. Dry your hands first to avoid erratic behavior.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you value cordless freedom, appreciate a warm 2700K light that flatters any room, and want a lamp that transitions effortlessly from bedside table to patio side table. **Skip it if** you're on a tight budget, need a bright work light, or can't live with the proprietary charging system.

For a premium cordless lamp that actually delivers on its promises, the Poldina Pro earns its 4.6-star rating. It's not perfect — the charging puck and light base are real compromises — but the combination of design, battery life, and IP65 durability is something you won't find elsewhere at this price.
