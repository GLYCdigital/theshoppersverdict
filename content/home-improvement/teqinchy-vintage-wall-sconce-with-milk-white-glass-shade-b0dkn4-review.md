If you've been scrolling through hundreds of "vintage" sconces that all look like they were spray-painted in someone's garage, the Teqinchy Vintage Wall Sconce is a breath of fresh air. This isn't a flimsy piece of plastic pretending to be brass. It's a proper die-cast aluminum fixture with a milk glass shade that genuinely softens light the way old-school fixtures did. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it's clearly doing something right — but it's not without quirks.

**What's in the box**

You get the sconce body with the glass shade pre-attached, a metal mounting plate, wire connectors, screws, and a small Allen wrench for the set screws. The shade is not removable — I'll come back to that. The hardware feels substantial, not the hollow, lightweight stuff you often find at this price point. The antique brass finish is applied evenly with a slight patina variation that makes it look like it's been in a charming old house for decades, not fresh out of a box.

**Real-world performance**

Installation took me about 25 minutes. The mounting plate screws to a standard junction box, and the fixture body slides over it with two set screws holding it in place. The instructions are minimal but adequate for anyone who's wired a light before. If you're a first-timer, you'll want to watch a YouTube video on basic wiring first — this isn't a plug-and-play battery fixture.

The milk glass shade does exactly what it should: it diffuses light into a warm, even glow. With a 60-watt equivalent LED bulb, the light fills a hallway or reading nook without any harsh shadows. One buyer noted, "The glass is thick and feels expensive, not like the cheap thin stuff on other sconces." That matches my experience — this shade has heft to it.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the reviews, three themes keep coming up. First, the aesthetic — people repeatedly mention how much more expensive it looks than it is. Second, the light quality. As one reviewer put it: "The milk glass gives off such a soft, warm glow. My bedroom feels like a boutique hotel now." Third, the build quality. Multiple buyers commented on the weight and solidity, with one noting it "feels like it will outlast the house."

**Where it falls short**

Here's the honest part. The shade being permanently fixed means you're locked into this one look. If you crack the glass during installation, you're buying a whole new fixture, not just a replacement shade. That's a real consideration.

Also, the open bottom design means the bulb is partially visible from below. You absolutely need a small globe or candelabra-style bulb — a standard A19 bulb will look awkward and ruin the vintage silhouette. Budget for bulbs that fit the aesthetic.

Finally, at full brightness with a standard switch, the light can feel a bit stark for a "vintage" vibe. A dimmer switch transforms this fixture entirely. If you're not prepared to add one, you're leaving a lot of the ambiance potential on the table.

**Who should buy this**

- **Renters and homeowners** wanting a quick hallway or bedroom upgrade without rewiring
- **Farmhouse and traditional-style decorators** who need an authentic-looking fixture without paying antique-store prices
- **Anyone with a tight space** — the 7-inch depth keeps it flush against the wall, perfect for narrow hallways

Skip it if you need a fully enclosed fixture (this one has an open bottom), or if you're not comfortable with basic electrical work.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it come with a bulb?**
A: No. You'll need to supply your own E26-base bulb. A small globe or vintage-style filament bulb under 4 inches in diameter works best.

**Q: Is it dimmable?**
A: The fixture itself is — but only if you have a dimmer switch and a dimmable LED bulb. Standard bulbs will just flicker or stay at full brightness.

**Q: Can you replace the glass shade if it breaks?**
A: No. The shade is bonded to the frame during manufacturing. If it breaks, the entire fixture needs replacing. Handle it carefully during installation.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want an authentic-looking vintage sconce with quality construction and soft, diffused lighting that won't break the bank. **Skip it if** you need a fully enclosed fixture, want interchangeable shades, or aren't prepared to pair it with the right bulb and dimmer setup.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — This is a well-made fixture that delivers on its promise, with a couple of design limitations you should know about before you buy. For the price, it's a solid upgrade over the cheap alternatives, and the quality shows in the details that matter.
