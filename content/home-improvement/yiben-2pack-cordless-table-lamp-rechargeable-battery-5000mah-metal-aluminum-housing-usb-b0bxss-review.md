If you've ever tripped over a lamp cord at 2 a.m. or wrestled with an extension cord just to read on a patio, you already know why the YIBEN 2Pack Cordless Table Lamp exists. It's a rechargeable, portable light source that cuts the cord entirely. And at 4.6 stars from 229 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. But is it worth your money? Let's break down what you actually get.

**What's in the box**

Two complete lamps, two USB-A charging cables, and a basic user manual. That's it. No wall adapters, which is mildly annoying — you'll need your own phone charger block or a laptop USB port. Each lamp measures roughly 4.3 inches in diameter at the base and stands about 11 inches tall. The aluminum housing is brushed and anodized, giving it a premium feel that's rare at this price point. The shade is a frosted acrylic diffuser that softens the light nicely.

**Performance and real-world use**

The headline feature is the 5000mAh battery per lamp. On the lowest setting, you'll get well over 20 hours. On medium — which is honestly the sweet spot for most tasks — expect 8 to 12 hours. Crank it to max and you're looking at around 5 hours. That's legitimate, verified performance from multiple owners. One reviewer noted, "Used it for 3 nights straight on medium for reading, still had charge left." That tracks with my expectations.

The touch sensor on the base controls power and brightness in a single continuous dimming range. It's intuitive after about 30 seconds of fiddling. There's also a 3-hour auto-off timer, which is a thoughtful touch for bedside use. Light output is warm white at roughly 3000K — comfortable for ambient lighting, cozy for dinner, but not ideal if you need bright task illumination for detailed work.

The weighted base is a genuine highlight. These lamps don't tip over easily, and the aluminum body dissipates heat well. After 4 hours on medium, the base was barely warm. Build quality is the strongest argument for this product.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The portability and build quality dominate the positive reviews. One buyer said, "These feel way more expensive than they are. Solid aluminum, no wobble." Another praised the battery life specifically: "Took these camping, used them every night for a week on low-medium, never charged once." The two-pack value proposition also gets frequent mentions — for roughly the price of one premium cordless lamp, you get two.

**Where it falls short**

The charging situation is the biggest gripe. USB-A only, no USB-C, and no adapter included. At 5V/2A input, a full charge takes 4 to 5 hours. That's not a dealbreaker, but it's dated. The touch sensor's sensitivity is a minor annoyance — if you mount this on a headboard and brush it while sleeping, you'll wake up to a lit lamp. And the fixed warm white temperature means you can't switch to daylight for reading or tasks. If you need adjustable color temperature, look elsewhere.

**Who should buy this**

- **Campers and outdoor enthusiasts**: Durable, portable, long battery life. These are genuinely great for tents, picnic tables, or RV use.
- **Bedroom readers**: The warm light and auto-off timer make these excellent nightstand lamps.
- **Apartment dwellers without overhead lighting**: Place them on shelves, desks, or side tables without worrying about outlet proximity.
- **Anyone who hates cords**: If you're tired of extension cables and outlet juggling, this solves that problem cleanly.

**Skip it if** you need cool white task lighting, if you're strictly using them at a desk for detailed work, or if you expect USB-C charging at this price.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does the battery actually last?**
A: Realistically, 8–12 hours on medium, 20+ on low, around 5 on max. That's per lamp, and it matches what owners report.

**Q: Can you charge and use it simultaneously?**
A: Yes, but it'll charge slower than when off. It works fine as a wired lamp while charging, though the cable is short (about 3 feet).

**Q: Are these bright enough for reading?**
A: For a book or e-reader, yes. For fine detail work like soldering or sewing, no — you'll want a task lamp with higher output and cooler light.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want durable, portable, well-built lamps that look far more expensive than they are. The battery life is legitimate, the aluminum housing is a class above plastic competitors, and the two-pack pricing delivers real value.

**Skip it if** you need bright task lighting or USB-C convenience. This is an ambient/reading lamp, not a work light.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Solidly recommended, with minor caveats about charging speed and fixed color temperature.
