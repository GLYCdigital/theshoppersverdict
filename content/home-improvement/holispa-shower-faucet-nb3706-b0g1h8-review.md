Let me be direct: the shower faucet market is drowning in cheap, chrome-plated plastic that looks good in photos and corrodes within a year. The HoliSpa NB3706 isn't that. With a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings, this thermostatic system has earned genuine traction — but it's not a universal fit for every bathroom. Here's what you need to know before you buy.

## What's Actually in the Box

This is a complete shower system, not just a faucet. You get the thermostatic rough-in valve, the trim plate with control handles, a 10-inch round rainfall showerhead, a handheld sprayer with a 59-inch stainless steel hose, and a diverter that lets you run both simultaneously. All mounting hardware, gaskets, and the Allen wrench for the set screws are included — one buyer noted, "Everything I needed was in the box. I didn't make a single extra trip to the hardware store."

The valve body is solid brass with a ceramic cartridge. The trim is stainless steel with a brushed nickel finish (also available in matte black and chrome). It feels substantial in the hand — no thin-metal rattling, no cheap plastic knobs.

## Performance and Build Quality

The headline feature is the thermostatic valve. Unlike a standard pressure-balance valve, this one self-adjusts to maintain your set temperature. If someone flushes a toilet or runs the washing machine, the temperature holds steady rather than blasting you with a scalding spike. One verified buyer described it exactly as you'd hope: "Set it to 102°F and it stays there. My wife can run the dishwasher and I don't feel a thing."

The ceramic cartridge is a smart choice — it's more durable than rubber washers and less prone to dripping over time. Several reviewers with hard water reported no mineral buildup after 6+ months of use, which is a meaningful data point. The brushed finish hides water spots better than polished chrome, too.

Installation is the DIY-friendly surprise here. The rough-in valve connects to standard 1/2-inch copper or PEX, and the trim plate covers a 6-inch stud-to-stud gap without needing an oversized escutcheon. Most buyers with basic plumbing experience completed the install in an afternoon. A few noted the set screws are small — keep the Allen wrench handy, because you'll need it to adjust the handle position after mounting.

## Where It Falls Short

The rainfall head is the weakest link. It delivers what I'd call a "gentle soak" rather than a pressurized spray. If you're the type who wants a pounding massage shower, you'll be disappointed — the handheld sprayer is noticeably stronger, and you'll likely find yourself using it more. One reviewer was blunt: "The rainfall head is pretty but weak. I use the handheld 90% of the time."

Second, this is a complete system replacement, not a drop-in upgrade. If you're replacing an existing single-handle faucet, you'll need to open the wall to swap the rough-in valve. It won't fit a standard 3-hole tub/shower configuration without adapter parts. Measure your existing plumbing before you commit.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you're doing a full bathroom remodel or new construction, want temperature stability that protects kids or elderly family members from scalding, and prefer a modern, minimalist look.

**Skip it if** you're on a tight budget, want a high-pressure rainfall experience, or need a quick swap without opening up the wall.

## Three Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Will this work with my existing shower arm?**
A: No. It's a complete thermostatic system with its own valve. The rainfall head connects to the included diverter, not a standard shower arm. Plan for wall access.

**Q: Is it compatible with tankless water heaters?**
A: Yes. The thermostatic valve doesn't restrict flow excessively, and buyers report it works fine with tankless and standard tank systems. The ceramic cartridge handles the higher incoming temperatures without issue.

**Q: How does the brushed nickel finish hold up?**
A: Well, based on reviews. Multiple buyers report no corrosion, peeling, or water spotting after extended use. The stainless steel trim is a step above the zinc alloy you'll find at big-box stores.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The HoliSpa NB3706 delivers genuine value at its price point. The thermostatic temperature control is a genuine safety feature, the build quality exceeds expectations for this category, and the complete kit means no surprise purchases. It's not perfect — the rainfall head's gentle flow won't satisfy everyone, and it requires real installation work — but for a bathroom remodel where temperature stability matters, this is a solid, well-reviewed choice that should serve you for years.
