**What You Get**

The box contains the remote itself — a compact, black 3-button fob measuring roughly 3 inches long and 1.5 inches wide. It ships with a visor clip pre-attached (you can pop it off if you prefer carrying it on a keychain), plus a CR2032 battery already installed. That’s it. No wiring, no programming tools, no manual thicker than a receipt. The programming sequence takes about 30 seconds: press the learn button on your operator, hold the remote button for two seconds, done.

The 893Max uses the older Security+ 2.0 rolling-code protocol, not the newer myQ-only system. That means it works with LiftMaster and Chamberlain openers manufactured roughly between 2005 and 2018, plus many current models that still support Security+ 2.0. It won’t pair with the newest myQ-only units that dropped the physical learn button entirely — check your opener’s vintage before buying.

---

**Build and Real-World Performance**

The casing is hard plastic, not rubberized, so it won’t get gummy in your car’s cupholder after a few summers. The buttons are raised and tactile — you can find the right one by feel without looking, which matters when you’re trying to open a gate while your hands are full of groceries. The 3-button layout is genuinely useful if you have two gates and a garage door, or a gate plus a second entrance. Each button programs independently, so you can assign them to different operators.

Range is solid but not spectacular. In open driveways, expect 100 to 150 feet of reliable signal. Behind a brick wall or through a metal gate control box, that drops to 50–75 feet. That’s typical for this class of remote and shouldn’t surprise anyone who’s used a garage door opener before. The CR2032 battery lasts roughly two years with daily use, and replacing it takes about 15 seconds with a flathead screwdriver.

**Where It Falls Short**

The biggest complaint in the reviews is the lack of a keyring hole on the body itself. The visor clip is removable, but once it’s off, there’s nowhere to attach a lanyard or key ring unless you thread it through the clip. That’s a minor annoyance, but it’s worth knowing if you planned to carry this on your keys.

Also, the 3-button version is overkill if you only have one gate. The single-button 891LM is cheaper and does the same job. You’re paying a small premium here for the extra buttons, not for better range or build quality. And if you have a newer myQ-only operator (2019 or later), this remote simply won’t pair — you’ll need the 892LT or a myQ-enabled solution instead.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this work with my Chamberlain garage door opener?**
Yes, if your opener has a purple or yellow learn button (Security+ 2.0). If it has a green learn button or is a myQ-only model, it won’t work.

**Q: How many remotes can I program to one operator?**
Most LiftMaster and Chamberlain operators support up to 20 remotes. The 893Max doesn’t count against any special limit.

**Q: Can I program all three buttons to the same operator?**
Yes, but there’s no reason to. Program each button to a different operator, or leave the extras unprogrammed — they won’t interfere with anything.
