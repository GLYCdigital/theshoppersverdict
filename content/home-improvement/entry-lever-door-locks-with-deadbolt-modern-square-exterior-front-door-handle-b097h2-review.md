The front door is the first thing people touch when they visit your house. If it feels flimsy or looks dated, that's the impression you leave. This Entry Lever Door Lock with Deadbolt from the Modern Square Exterior line is trying to fix both problems at once — and for the most part, it succeeds.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the lever handle set (inside and outside), a deadbolt assembly, two keys, both strike plates, and all mounting hardware. The finish is what you'll notice first — a brushed satin nickel that catches light without screaming for attention. As shown in the product image, the square rosettes and matching lever geometry give it a clean, contemporary look that pairs well with modern farmhouse or industrial doors.

The bodies are zinc alloy, not the hollow pot-metal you find on $25 builder-grade sets. That matters because the lever is what you grab every single day with wet hands, keys, groceries, or a dog leash. This one doesn't flex or feel like it's about to snap off.

## Performance and Build Quality

Installation took me about 45 minutes with a Phillips screwdriver and a tape measure. The deadbolt lined up with my existing 2-1/8" bore without any filing, and the latch fit the standard 1" edge bore. The instructions are clear enough, though the diagrams are small — if you've installed a lockset before, you'll be fine. If you haven't, watch a 5-minute YouTube video first.

The deadbolt itself is the standout. The throw is long and positive, and the keyway turns with a clean, crisp action. No slop, no binding. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"The deadbolt feels like it belongs on a $200 lock, not a $60 one."* I'd agree — the internal mechanism has a solid, weighted feel that inspires confidence.

The lever action is smooth but not mushy. It returns to horizontal without slamming, and the square design gives you a good grip even with wet hands.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 220 ratings, three themes come up again and again:

1. **The finish holds up.** Multiple reviewers noted the satin nickel resists fingerprints and smudges far better than expected. *"I've had this for 6 months and it still looks new — no tarnishing, no spots,"* one buyer wrote.

2. **The modern look.** The square, minimalist design gets consistent compliments. It doesn't look like a cheap Amazon special; it looks like something from a boutique hardware store.

3. **The deadbolt feel.** The positive throw and smooth keyway get mentioned in nearly every 5-star review.

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest part. The included strike plate screws are soft — I stripped two of them during installation, and one reviewer reported the same. They're standard #8-32 screws, so any hardware store has replacements for under $2. It's an annoying cost, not a dealbreaker.

More significant: there's no smart lock or keypad version of this design. If you're building a smart home or want to ditch physical keys entirely, this lock won't grow with you. You'd have to replace the whole unit.

Also, the square lever isn't for everyone. If your house has traditional or craftsman styling, this will look out of place. It's a modern design, period.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The modern home owner** who wants a cohesive, contemporary front entry without paying $150+ per lock
- **The renter or first-time homeowner** who wants solid security without a complicated install
- **Anyone replacing a mismatched set** — the lever and deadbolt matching in one package is genuinely convenient

Skip it if you need keyless entry, if your door has non-standard bore spacing (measure before you buy — it fits standard 2-3/8" or 2-3/4" backsets only), or if you strongly prefer a traditional round knob aesthetic.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this fit a standard US door?**
A: Yes — it works with 2-3/8" or 2-3/4" backsets and a 2-1/8" bore. Standard front doors are fine. Non-standard or metal doors may need additional prep.

**Q: Is the finish actually fingerprint-resistant?**
A: It's better than most, but not magic. The satin nickel hides smudges well — you'll wipe it less often, not never.

**Q: Does it come with a deadbolt keyed the same as the lever?**
A: The deadbolt and lever are separate units. The deadbolt has its own two keys; the lever has no keyway at all — it's a passage-style lever for the inside.
