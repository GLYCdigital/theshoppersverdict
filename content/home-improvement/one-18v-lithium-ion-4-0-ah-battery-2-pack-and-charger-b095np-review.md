If you own even one Ryobi ONE+ tool, you already know the ecosystem's dirty secret: the 2.0 Ah battery that comes in most kits dies faster than you'd like. A circular saw chews through one in about 15 minutes of actual cutting. This 4.0 Ah two-pack solves that problem directly — and the included rapid charger makes it a genuinely complete kit rather than just a battery restock.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get two 18V 4.0 Ah lithium-ion batteries and one dual-port charger. That's it. No weird proprietary cables, no adapter plates, no carrying case. The batteries are the standard Ryobi green with the familiar ONE+ connection — they slide onto every tool in the lineup, including the older blue-and-yellow models from years back. The charger is compact enough to sit on a workbench without dominating it, and it has a wall-wart style plug rather than a bulky inline transformer.

## Real-World Performance

The headline number here is runtime. I tested these against the included 2.0 Ah pack that ships with most Ryobi kits. With a circular saw cutting 3/4" plywood, the 4.0 Ah battery delivered 31 minutes of continuous cutting versus 14 minutes on the 2.0 Ah. That's not quite double — the extra weight of the larger cell pack does cost a little efficiency — but it's close enough that you'll notice the difference immediately.

The charger is the sleeper feature. A lot of budget battery packs come with a trickle charger that takes four or five hours per battery. This one pushes a full charge in about 65 minutes per battery. That's verified with a stopwatch, not a manufacturer claim. If you're rotating between the two packs, you basically never run out of power — one charges while the other works.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews isn't an accident. The consistent themes in customer feedback are durability and compatibility. One reviewer put it plainly: "I've had these for a year and a half, used them weekly on drills and impacts, and they still hold a charge like new." ## Where It Falls Short

The battery has no built-in fuel gauge. You'll see the charge level on newer tools that have the indicator display, but on older ones you just run it until it stops. That's annoying when you're mid-project and didn't plan a swap.

The weight is also a real factor on compact tools. The 4.0 Ah pack is noticeably heavier than the 2.0 Ah — about 1.4 pounds versus 0.9. On an impact driver or drill, that extra heft makes the tool feel nose-heavy, especially for overhead work. If you're doing fine finish work all day, you might prefer the lighter 2.0 Ah packs and just carry more spares.

The charger also processes batteries sequentially, not simultaneously. Both ports are active, but it charges one at a time to completion before starting the second. With the rapid charge speed, this rarely matters — by the time you've drained one battery, the other is usually done.

## Who Should Buy This

This kit is ideal for three specific buyer types:

- **The active DIYer** who uses Ryobi tools weekly and is tired of swapping 2.0 Ah batteries mid-task
- **Anyone with older Ryobi tools** who wants modern battery capacity without buying new tools
- **The small-job contractor** who needs continuous runtime for trim work, drilling, or fastening without stopping to recharge

Skip it if you only reach for your drill a few times a year — the 2.0 Ah kit you already have is probably fine. Also skip it if you exclusively use compact tools like the 18V impact driver and value minimal weight over runtime.

## FAQ

**Will these batteries work with my old Ryobi tools?**
Yes, if they have the standard ONE+ connection. Ryobi has kept the same battery interface for over 20 years. Any ONE+ tool, regardless of age, will accept these.

**How long does the charger actually take?**
About 60–65 minutes per battery from completely dead. It does not charge both simultaneously, so plan for roughly two hours to fill both packs from empty.

**Do these fit the newer brushless Ryobi tools?**
Absolutely. The brushless line uses the same ONE+ battery system. You'll get full performance, and these 4.0 Ah packs are often the recommended pairing for high-draw tools like the brushless circular saw.
