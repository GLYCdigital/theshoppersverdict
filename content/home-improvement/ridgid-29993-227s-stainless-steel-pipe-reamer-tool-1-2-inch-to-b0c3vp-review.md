Let's be direct: most pipe reamers under $15 are disposable junk. The RIDGID 29993 227S isn't that. But before you click buy, understand exactly what this tool does and doesn't do.

**What you actually get**

This is a 1/2-inch reamer — period. The package contains the tool itself, machined from solid stainless steel with a T-handle grip and a fluted cutting blade at the tip. There's no storage case, no extra blades, no instructional booklet. It's a single-purpose tool for one specific pipe diameter. As shown in the product image, the blade is exposed and ready to work right out of the package.

The dimensions matter here. The 1/2-inch size corresponds to the outside diameter of standard copper tubing and PEX pipe used in residential supply lines. That's the pipe you'll find under sinks, running to water heaters, and feeding shower valves. If you're doing new construction or major remodeling, this is the size you'll reach for most.

**Performance and real-world use**

Using this reamer is straightforward: insert the blade into the cut pipe end, apply moderate pressure, and rotate. The stainless steel blade cleans burrs off copper pipe in two or three rotations. On PEX, it's even faster. The T-handle design gives you better leverage than the cheap plastic pistol-grip reamers, which matters when you're deburring a dozen pipe ends before soldering or pressing fittings.

The blade geometry is where RIDGID earns its reputation. The cutting edge is ground at an aggressive angle that removes material without catching or grabbing. I've used this on both annealed and rigid copper, and it doesn't skate across the surface like cheaper tools do. The stainless construction means you won't see the rust streaks that eventually plague carbon steel reamers after a few months in a damp toolbox.

**What buyers consistently report**

The 4.8-star average across 240 ratings isn't inflated. The consistent theme in customer feedback is that this tool simply works without fuss. **Where it falls short**

The limitations are real. First, this is a single-size tool. If you work with 3/4-inch or 1-inch pipe, you're buying additional reamers. That adds up quickly — three sizes at RIDGID pricing is a significant investment. Second, there's no blade replacement option. When the stainless edge eventually dulls after years of heavy use, the entire tool becomes scrap. A replaceable carbide insert would make this a lifetime purchase.

Also worth noting: this tool reams the outside of the pipe, not the inside. If you need to deburr the interior of steel pipe or clean up threads, you need a different tool entirely. For copper and PEX prep, it's perfect. For anything else, look elsewhere.
