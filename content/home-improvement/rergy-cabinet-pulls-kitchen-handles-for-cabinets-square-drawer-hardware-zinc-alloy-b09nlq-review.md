## What You're Actually Getting

The set ships with the pulls, mounting screws, and a template strip — that template is genuinely useful, not the throwaway junk most brands include. Each pull measures 5 inches on center (128mm hole spacing), making them compatible with the overwhelming majority of pre-drilled cabinet fronts. The profile itself is a clean square bar, roughly 0.5 inches thick, with a matte or brushed finish depending on which variant you order.

The key spec that separates these from the competition: one-piece zinc alloy construction. That's not marketing fluff. Many pulls at this price point are two-piece press-fit assemblies that loosen over time. The Rergy units are solid cast, weighing noticeably more than budget alternatives.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Installation is straightforward — mark your holes, drill pilot holes, drive the screws. The concealed screw design means no visible hardware on the face, which is the entire point of this aesthetic. The square profile gives a modern, slightly industrial look that pairs well with shaker cabinets or flat-panel doors.

One thing buyers consistently mention is the weight. As one reviewer put it, "These feel like $8 pulls, not $2 pulls." That density translates to durability — no flex when you pull a heavy drawer loaded with cast iron cookware.

The finish holds up better than expected. Multiple reviewers noted the brushed nickel variant resists fingerprints well, which matters in a kitchen environment. The matte black option has a subtle texture that hides smudges even better.

## Where It Falls Short

The square edges are the double-edged sword here. They create that sharp, architectural look — but they're not comfortable to grip for extended periods, and they'll snag on loose sleeves or dish towels. If you have kids who hang on drawer handles, this is a genuine concern.

The included screws are standard 1-inch length. If your drawer fronts are thicker than 3/4 inch — common with overlay doors or shaker-style builds — you'll need to buy longer screws separately. That's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy them if:** You're doing a full kitchen or bathroom remodel with modern or transitional styling, you want hardware that doesn't scream "cheap," and you're okay with a square profile that prioritizes look over ergonomic comfort.

**Skip them if:** You have young children, you prefer rounded bar pulls for comfort, or your drawer fronts exceed 3/4 inch thickness and you don't want the extra hardware trip.

**Are these available in different sizes?**
