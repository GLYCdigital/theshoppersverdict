## What You Actually Get

The box contains a single A19 bulb with a standard E26 medium base — the most common screw-in base in North American homes. The bulb measures 4.3 inches long and 2.36 inches in diameter, so it fits standard lamps, ceiling fixtures, and recessed cans without clearance issues. The frosted glass shell is a deliberate design choice, not just aesthetics — it scatters light to eliminate the harsh glare you get from clear-glass LED bulbs that expose their diode arrays.

This model runs at 2700K color temperature. If you don't know what that means, here's the practical translation: it's the same warm, slightly yellow glow you got from old incandescent bulbs. No clinical blue-white light. Your living room won't look like a hospital corridor.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has two genuine limitations.

**No dimmer compatibility.** This is the big one. If your dining room or bedroom runs on a dimmer switch, these bulbs will flicker or hum. Amazon sells a dimmable version of the same bulb for about a dollar more — buy that one instead if you need dimming.

**Enclosed fixtures reduce output.** In fully enclosed ceiling fixtures with no airflow, the heat buildup causes the bulb to throttle its light output. It won't fail or become dangerous, but you'll notice it's dimmer than in an open lamp. Skip these for sealed outdoor fixtures or fully enclosed flush-mount ceiling lights.
