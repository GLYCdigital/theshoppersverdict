## What you actually get

When you order, you're not buying a stock sign. You upload your logo, business name, or artwork — ideally a vector file (SVG, AI, or EPS) — and the manufacturer converts it to a cut acrylic shape with flexible LED tubing embedded along the outline. The package includes:

- The LED sign itself (dimensions vary based on your design, but expect roughly 1–3 feet across for most logos)
- Clear acrylic backing panel with pre-drilled mounting holes
- Acrylic standoffs and screws for wall mounting
- 12V power adapter with a plug-in dimmer
- ~6.5 feet of clear lead wire from sign to power source

One thing buyers consistently note: the sign arrives well-packaged. Multiple reviewers mention double-boxing and foam corner protection. One customer wrote, *"I was nervous about shipping damage, but it came in perfect shape — no cracks, no bent acrylic."*

## Build quality and real-world performance

The LED tubing is flexible silicone, not glass — which is exactly what you want for both durability and shipping safety. The light output is surprisingly uniform. I've seen cheaper custom signs where the tubing has visible dark spots at bends or corners; that's not the case here. The 12V system runs cool — you can leave it on 24/7 without worrying about heat buildup or fire risk.

The acrylic backing is 5mm thick, which feels substantial, not flimsy. The pre-drilled holes are positioned to work with the included standoffs, so you're not improvising hardware placement. Wall mounting takes about 15 minutes with a drill and a level.

Brightness is a common point in the reviews. The sign is genuinely visible in daylight — not washed out like some cheaper LED signs. One barber shop owner noted, *"Customers can see my logo from across the parking lot during the day. It's bright enough to matter."* For indoor use, you'll likely want the dimmer set to 50–70%; full brightness is intense up close.

## What buyers consistently praise

- **Accuracy of the design conversion:** Multiple reviewers note the manufacturer nailed their logo, including small text and complex shapes. One reviewer said, *"I sent a low-res JPEG and they still got the details right — I was expecting it to look rough but it's crisp."*
- **Customer service during the proofing process:** You get a digital proof before they cut anything. If something's off, they fix it. Several reviewers mention quick responses and willingness to adjust.
- **Real neon look:** The diffused LED light has that soft, continuous glow that reads as "neon" — not the harsh dot-matrix look of cheaper LED rope signs.

## Where it falls short

The lead time is the biggest friction point. 5–7 business days for production, plus shipping, means you're looking at 1.5–2 weeks from order to delivery. If you need a sign for an event this weekend, this isn't your option.

The included dimmer is basic — a knob you turn, not a smart controller. No app control, no scheduling, no remote. For a business that wants the sign on at sunset and off at close automatically, you'll need to add a smart plug (which works fine, but it's an extra purchase).

The power adapter is a wall wart — it's not the sleekest form factor, and the 6.5-foot lead wire means your nearest outlet needs to be reasonably close, or you'll need an extension cord.

Also worth noting: the sign is bright. If you're installing it in a small, dark space — like a home office or a bedroom — full brightness can be overwhelming. The dimmer helps, but even at low settings it's not subtle.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I upload a photo of my logo, or does it need to be a vector file?**
A: You can upload a JPEG or PNG, but vector files (SVG, AI, EPS) produce the cleanest results. If you only have a low-res image, the company will do their best, but fine text may lose sharpness. They'll send a proof before manufacturing, so you can catch issues then.

**Q: How big will my sign actually be?**
A: It depends on your design's aspect ratio. A simple text sign might be 2–3 feet wide; a complex logo might be smaller. The manufacturer scales your design to fit within the size you select (typically up to ~3 feet on the longest side). The proof will show exact dimensions.

**Q: Is it safe to leave on all day?**
A: Yes. The 12V LED system runs cool and is rated for continuous use. Many business owners report leaving theirs on for 10+ hours daily without issues.
