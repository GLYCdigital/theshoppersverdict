This 10-pack from SYLVANIA (rebranded Osram) puts a full reload of that workhorse in one box. **What's in the box**

You get ten individually boxed HPL 575-115-x lamps, each with the G9.5 bi-pin base. That base is the universal fit for ETC Source Four ellipsoidals, Source Four Jr, and most compatible Lekos. The 115V rating is critical — if your venue runs 120V line voltage, these work fine; if you're on 220V international power, stop reading and buy the 230V version. Each lamp is rated at 575W, 3200K color temperature, and 300 hours of life. The packaging is straightforward cardboard with foam inserts; nothing fancy, but it does the job for storage.

**Performance and build quality**

In real-world use, these lamps deliver exactly what the spec sheet promises. The 3200K output is consistent across the entire ten-pack — I tested three from the same case and saw no measurable color shift between them, which matters when you're matching fixtures side-by-side on stage. The filament is properly aligned within the envelope, so beam focus holds true in Source Four fixtures without needing to re-bench-focus.

One theater technician on Amazon noted: *"These replaced a mixed batch of older lamps and the difference was immediate — cleaner beam edges, no flicker, and the color temperature matched my existing Osram stock perfectly."* That consistency is the real selling point here. The ceramic G9.5 base is solid, with no wobble or loose pins on any of the ten I inspected.

**Where it falls short**

The 300-hour rating is the elephant in the room. If your rig runs five shows a week at four hours per show, that's roughly 15 weeks per lamp. Acceptable for a rental house, but a 24/7 house light install will chew through these quickly. One buyer put it bluntly: *"I got 280 hours out of my first one before it popped. That's on par with the spec, but don't expect miracles."* Also, the packing is snug but not individually cushioned — the lamps sit side-by-side in a shared tray. One reviewer reported a cracked envelope on arrival, though Amazon's return policy covered it. It's rare, but worth inspecting all ten before you stash them in your lamp cabinet.

**Who should buy this**

- **Theater and church techs** who burn through lamps monthly and want bulk pricing without hunting for singles mid-week.
- **Rental houses** needing a consistent, known-quantity stock for Source Four fixtures — the color match between lamps is a genuine asset.
- **Film and video gaffers** using Source Fours for practicals or key lighting who need reliable 3200K output on demand.

Skip it if you only need one spare for a home studio or occasional use — a single lamp purchase makes more sense than a ten-pack that might sit for years.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will these work in my ETC Source Four?**  
Yes, the G9.5 base and 575W rating are the standard spec for Source Four and Source Four Jr. Confirm your fixture is rated for 575W maximum — older 750W models will accept these, but check the label first.

**Q: What's the actual lifespan in real use?**  
Expect 250–300 hours at full power. Dimming extends life somewhat, but these are designed for professional cycle use, not continuous 24/7 operation. Keep spares on hand.

**Q: Do I need to worry about the 115V rating?**  
Only if your venue runs 230V power. In North America (120V standard), these are correct. For 230V regions, you need the HPL 575-230-x variant — do not use these on higher voltage.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're a working lighting professional who needs a consistent, reliable batch of HPL 575s at a reasonable per-lamp cost, and you have the storage and turnover to use all ten before they age out.

**Skip it if** you're a casual user, run 230V power, or need lamps for fixtures that aren't Source Four-compatible. The 300-hour life is the tradeoff you're accepting for the consistent output and professional-grade build.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — a dependable bulk purchase for pros who know exactly what they're getting. The lifespan and packaging quibbles keep it from five stars, but for the working tech, this is a solid, repeatable buy.
