The Panasonic WhisperGreen Select isn't just another bathroom fan. It's a solution for the specific problem of "one fan, two different rooms" — or one room that needs different airflow at different times. With 220 Amazon ratings averaging 4.4 stars, this ceiling-mount unit has clearly impressed most buyers. But let's dig into whether it's worth the premium over a basic exhaust fan.

## What You Actually Get

Out of the box, you're looking at the fan body, a dual-speed selector switch, mounting brackets, and a 4-inch duct connector. The unit measures 11-3/4 inches square — a standard new-construction or remodel size that fits most ceiling openings. The standout feature is that adjustable airflow: you can set it to 80, 90, 100, or 110 CFM depending on your room size and needs. That's rare in this category, where most fans ship with one fixed speed.

The SmartAction motion sensor is built in, not an add-on. It detects when someone enters the room and runs the fan for a preset period (adjustable from 1 to 20 minutes). That's genuinely useful for kids who forget to flip the switch.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the WhisperGreen Select shines. At 0.3 sones on low speed, this thing is whisper-quiet — you'll likely forget it's running. The 4-way pickup design pulls air from all directions, which makes a noticeable difference in clearing steam from a shower. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"I can't hear it running, and the mirror stays fog-free. My old fan sounded like a jet engine and did nothing."* That matches the consensus across the ratings.

The dual-speed selector is the real reason to choose this over the standard WhisperGreen. Set it low for a powder room, high for a master bath — same unit, no extra hardware. The motor is rated for continuous operation, so if you want to run it on low 24/7 for ventilation, it'll handle that without issue.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the gaps. First, there's no built-in humidity sensor. For a fan in the "Select" line, that feels like an oversight — you'd expect automatic moisture detection at this price point. You can add the WHFSHC1 kit, but that's another $50 or so.

Installation is more involved than a basic fan. The dual-speed switch and motion sensor wiring add steps, and some buyers have noted the mounting bracket alignment takes patience. If you're not comfortable with basic electrical work, budget for a pro.

Also, at 80 CFM the low setting is fine for bathrooms up to about 50 square feet, but if you're venting a larger space, you'll want to run it on high — which brings the noise up to a still-quiet 1.0 sone but reduces the efficiency advantage.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The "one fan, two rooms" buyer:** If you're installing in a jack-and-jill bath or a space that serves different needs, the adjustable airflow is a genuinely practical feature.
- **The noise-sensitive homeowner:** At 0.3 sones, this is one of the quietest fans at this CFM range. If you hate the buzz of a cheap fan, this is worth every penny.
- **The remodeler who wants a sensor:** The motion detection is reliable and adjustable, which is a solid upgrade over a plain wall switch.

Skip it if you want a simple, cheap fan with a humidity sensor — look at the WhisperWarm or a basic Broan instead.

## FAQ

**Is the motion sensor adjustable?**
Yes. You can set the run time from 1 to 20 minutes after detecting motion, and the sensor has a detection range of about 15 feet.

**Can this replace an existing 4-inch duct fan?**
Yes, the 4-inch duct connector matches standard residential ducting. Just check your ceiling opening — this needs 11-3/4 inches square, which is standard.

**Does the dual-speed selector require special wiring?**
The selector switch is wired inline with the fan motor, but it works with a standard single-pole wall switch. No special controller needed.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a quiet, versatile fan with adjustable airflow and a motion sensor — and you're willing to pay for build quality that lasts.

**Skip it if:** You need humidity-sensing automation out of the box, or you're on a tight budget and don't need the dual-speed flexibility.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A well-engineered fan that nails quiet operation and flexibility, with the missing humidity sensor keeping it from a perfect score.
