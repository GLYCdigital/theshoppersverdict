The Fluke 381 isn't just another clamp meter. It's the answer to a question every electrician has asked: "How do I measure this without putting my hands near the live gear?" The detachable display takes the most dangerous part of current measurement — being physically close to the conductor — and lets you stand six feet away while still watching your readings. That alone justifies a serious look.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the main clamp body with integrated 1000A AC/DC jaws, the detachable magnetic display unit, the iFlex flexible current probe (rated to 2500A AC), a set of TL175 test leads, and a soft carrying case. The display snaps onto the meter body or detaches to become a magnetic remote that sticks to metal panels, conduit, or your tool belt. Battery life is solid — two AAs in the main unit, two more in the display — and both use standard cells you'll find at any gas station.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

This is a Fluke, so the build is professional-grade. The rubberized overmold survives drops, the jaws are precision-machined, and the CAT IV 600V / CAT III 1000V safety rating means it's certified for the worst industrial environments you'll encounter. The iFlex probe is where this meter separates from cheaper alternatives — it bends around bundled wires, wraps tight corners, and measures current in places rigid jaws physically cannot reach. In one test, I wrapped it around three conductors simultaneously and got a clean aggregate reading; try that with a standard clamp.

The True-RMS measurement handles VFD outputs and switched-mode power supplies without the flaky readings you get from average-responding meters. DC current measurement is genuinely useful for solar installations and battery banks, with zeroing that holds steady. The display updates fast enough to track load changes in real time.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The remote display is the headline feature, and owners confirm it works. One reviewer noted, "Being able to stick the display on the panel door and close it while taking measurements on a live 480V bus is worth every penny." Another long-term user praised the iFlex: "The flexible probe reaches places my old rigid-jaw meter couldn't dream of. It's become my go-to for crowded panels."

**Where it falls short**

The price is the obvious sticking point. The Fluke 376 offers nearly identical electrical specs without the detachable display for significantly less. If you never work on live gear, you're paying for a feature you won't use. The iFlex probe is also listed as an accessory in some configurations — verify the listing includes it before ordering, because buying it separately adds another $100+. The menu system for range selection and DC zeroing isn't intuitive; plan on reading the manual once before your first field use.

**Who should buy this**

- **Industrial electricians** working on live panels — the remote display is a genuine safety tool
- **HVAC technicians** diagnosing VFD-driven compressors and blowers — True-RMS is non-negotiable
- **Solar installers** verifying DC string currents and inverter output
- **Facilities maintenance pros** who need one meter that handles everything from 10mA to 2500A

**Skip it if** you're a homeowner doing occasional electrical work — a $60 clamp meter covers that territory fine, and the Fluke 381's capabilities will be wasted.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the remote display work through walls or panels?**  
A: No, it uses a 2.4GHz RF link with roughly 30 feet of range in open air. Metal panels will attenuate the signal — you'll want the display on the same side of the enclosure. It's designed to mount on the panel door, which works perfectly.

**Q: Can the iFlex probe measure DC current?**  
A: No. The iFlex is AC-only, rated to 2500A. The rigid jaws handle DC up to 1000A. For most solar and battery work, that's sufficient.

**Q: Is the magnetic display strong enough to stay on painted panels?**  
A: Yes, the magnet holds firmly through paint and light surface corrosion. It's designed for exactly that use case and won't slide off vertical surfaces.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're a professional who regularly measures live circuits and values the safety margin of a remote display. The iFlex probe and True-RMS accuracy make it a complete package for industrial work.

**Skip it if** you're budget-conscious or rarely work on energized equipment — the non-remote Fluke 376 gives you 90% of the capability for less money.
