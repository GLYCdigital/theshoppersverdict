**FAQ**

**Q: Will this work on a screw with a completely rounded-out head?**
A: Not reliably. It works best on screws with intact heads that are merely seized. For fully rounded heads, you’ll need a screw extractor kit first.

**Q: Can I use this with a regular socket?**
A: Yes. The included 1/2-inch drive adapter lets you use standard sockets, making it a manual impact wrench for bolts and nuts.

**Q: What size hammer should I use?**
A: A 12- to 16-ounce hammer is ideal. Heavier hammers deliver more force but risk damaging the tool or the fastener.
