The build quality is typical Dremel: a hard plastic shell with a tight, click-in fit. No wobble when seated in the tool, which matters more than you'd think — a loose battery causes intermittent power loss that you'll blame on the tool itself.

## Performance and Real-World Use

I ran this battery through a typical weekend project: cutting through 1/8-inch aluminum sheet, sanding a wooden edge, and doing some detailed engraving on glass. The 2.0Ah rating translates to roughly 25-30 minutes of continuous heavy use, which aligns with what Dremel claims. For intermittent work — a few cuts here, some sanding there — you'll get a solid hour or more before the final LED blinks.

The voltage holds steady until the end. I didn't notice the tool slowing down as the battery drained, which is a common issue with cheaper cells. The charge time from dead to full is about 40 minutes with the standard Dremel charger, which is reasonable for this class.
