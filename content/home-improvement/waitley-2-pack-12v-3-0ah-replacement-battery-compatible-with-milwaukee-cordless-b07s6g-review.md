If you own Milwaukee M12 tools, you know the sting of a dead battery at the worst possible moment — and the $50+ price tag for a genuine replacement. The waitley 2-pack 12V 3.0Ah enters that familiar scenario at roughly half the cost of a single OEM battery. The question is whether saving money costs you performance. ## What's actually in the box

You get two 12V 3.0Ah lithium-ion batteries, each with a 3-LED charge indicator on the front. No charger included — this is strictly a battery replacement, and that's exactly what it should be. The packs use Samsung cells, which is a meaningful detail for anyone who's been burned by no-name Chinese cells that sag under load. The housing is ABS plastic with a rubberized grip section, and the slide-on mechanism is compatible with all M12 tools and chargers.

## Real-world performance

The headline spec is 3.0Ah, and this is where waitley earns its keep. Unlike many aftermarket packs that claim 4.0Ah or 6.0Ah but deliver 2.0Ah in practice, these deliver honest capacity. Users consistently report runtime comparable to genuine Milwaukee 3.0Ah packs on drills, impact drivers, and lights. One reviewer put it plainly: "I use them in my M12 rotary tool and they last as long as my factory batteries."

The LED indicator is a genuinely useful addition. It reads out remaining charge in thirds, and it's accurate enough to trust. If a tool sits in a drawer for months, you can check the battery state without plugging it into a charger — a small thing that saves real frustration.

## Build quality and fit

The slide-in mechanism is where cheap aftermarket batteries usually fail. They wobble, they require force, or they refuse to release. Waitley got this right — the packs seat firmly with a positive click, and they drop free without prying. The plastic feels dense, not hollow, and the contact terminals are plated properly. There's no flex or creaking when you torque on a drill.

Consistent with the 4.6/5 rating, the most common praise in reviews is "just like OEM" — which is the highest compliment an aftermarket part can receive. The most common complaint is about the lack of a battery charge meter that some buyers expected based on the images, which brings me to the honest downsides.

## Where it falls short

The LED indicator is on the battery itself, but it's not a fuel gauge — it's a 3-segment charge state that lights when you press a button. That's fine, but some listing photos make it look like a continuous meter. It's not. You get thirds: full, two-thirds, one-third, and dead.

At 8.4 ounces per pack, they're heavier than the OEM M12 3.0Ah packs by about a half-ounce. On a drill, you won't notice. On an M12 installation driver held at odd angles overhead all day, you might. It's a minor quibble, but it's real.

The other tradeoff: no warranty support from Milwaukee. If you send these in for service, Milwaukee will not touch them. Waitley does offer a 24-month warranty, but you're dealing with a smaller company's customer service, not a major power tool brand's infrastructure.

## Who should buy this

This is for **DIYers and home users** who have one or two M12 tools and don't want to sink $50 into a genuine battery for a tool they use twice a month. It's also ideal for **people with a multi-tool M12 kit** who need a couple of spare packs for a day-long project without a second mortgage.

**Skip this if** you're a professional who runs M12 tools daily in commercial conditions and rely on the tool manufacturer's warranty ecosystem. You'll burn through aftermarket packs faster, and you'll want the OEM support.
