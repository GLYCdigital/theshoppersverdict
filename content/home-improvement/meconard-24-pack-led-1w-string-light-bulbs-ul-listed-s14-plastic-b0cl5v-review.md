**What's in the Box**  
You get 24 bulbs. That's it. No string wire, no sockets—just the bulbs, packed in individual protective slots inside a sturdy cardboard box. Each bulb is the classic S14 shape: roughly 1.75 inches in diameter and 3.15 inches tall, with an E26 medium base that fits standard string light sockets (think commercial-grade patio lights, not cheap novelty strands). At 1W per bulb, these aren't going to light up a football field. The output is a warm 2700K glow that feels like classic Edison lighting without the heat or fragility.
