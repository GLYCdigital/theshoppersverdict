# Scenekoy Natural Alabaster Table Lamps: Honest Review After 229 Ratings

Most "alabaster" lamps you find online are resin castings with a stone-print finish. The Scenekoy Natural Alabaster Table Lamp is the real thing — actual quarried alabaster, cut and hollowed into a shade. That's the first thing you need to know before spending your money. This is a genuine stone product, which means beauty and fragility in equal measure.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains one assembled lamp: a solid alabaster shade mounted on a brass-finished metal base, a standard E26 socket, and a 5.5-foot cord with an inline rocker switch. The shade measures roughly 10 inches in diameter and 7 inches tall, sitting on a 6-inch round base. Total height lands around 16.5 inches.

No bulb is included — a minor annoyance at this price point. You'll need to supply your own E26 bulb, and Scenekoy recommends staying at or below 40W (or an equivalent LED). That's worth remembering because some buyers have grabbed higher-wattage bulbs and found the shade gets too hot to touch.

## Real-World Performance

The light quality is where this lamp earns its keep. Because alabaster is semi-translucent, the shade acts as a natural diffuser. With a warm 2700K LED bulb inside, the glow is soft, veined, and almost honey-toned — nothing like the sterile white light from typical table lamps. Buyers consistently mention this. One reviewer wrote, "The way the light comes through the stone is unlike anything I've seen. It's warm without being dim."

The 5.5-pound weight keeps the lamp stable on nightstands and desk corners. The felt pad on the base is a thoughtful touch — no scratches on wood surfaces, which is more than many premium lamps offer.

Across 229 ratings, the recurring themes are clear:

## Where It Falls Short

**Fragility is the big one.** Alabaster is soft stone — roughly 3 on the Mohs scale. Dropping it, or even tightening a bulb too aggressively, can crack the shade. One verified buyer reported, "I chipped the edge just bumping it while changing the bulb. Be very careful." This isn't a lamp for high-traffic areas, homes with rambunctious pets, or kids' rooms.

**Height is a constraint.** At 16.5 inches, this lamp is too tall for low nightstands (under 24 inches) and will look awkward on narrow console tables. It's best on a taller surface where the shade sits at or slightly below eye level when seated.

**No dimmer, no smart features.** If you want adjustable brightness or app control, this isn't the lamp for you. It's a straightforward on/off fixture.

**Q: Can I use a smart bulb in this lamp?**
A: Yes, as long as it's an E26 base and 40W equivalent or less. Just be careful when screwing it in — the stone shade is the fragile part.

**Q: How bright is it actually?**
A: With a 2700K 40W-equivalent LED, you get roughly 450 lumens — enough for a bedroom or reading corner, not for task lighting like a desk workspace.

**Q: Is the alabaster real or a synthetic replica?**
A: Real. You can see natural veining, and no two lamps are identical. Some minor surface imperfections are normal and part of the stone's character.
