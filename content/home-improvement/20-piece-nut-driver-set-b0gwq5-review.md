Look, you don't need 20 nut drivers. You need the right nut driver—and then a few backups for when you misplace it mid-project. This 20-piece set from Amazon’s generic hardware aisle covers that base and then some, earning a solid 4.5 stars from 225 buyers. But is it truly worth your cash, or just another drawer-filler? Let’s dig in.

**What you actually get**  
The kit includes 20 drivers ranging from 3/16-inch to 3/4-inch in fractional sizes, plus a selection of metric sizes (5mm through 19mm) to cover imported appliances and vehicles. Each driver features a chrome vanadium steel shaft—that’s the good stuff, not the cheap carbon steel that bends on stubborn fasteners. The handles are color-coded by size range, which sounds gimmicky until you’re fishing through a drawer with greasy fingers. The shafts are 6 inches long, giving you decent reach into recessed fasteners without needing a full socket extension.

**Performance and build quality**  
I’ve hammered these drivers on everything from lawn mower deck bolts to electrical panel screws. The chrome vanadium holds up—no twisted shafts, no rounded tips after months of abuse. The magnetic tips are strong enough to hold a screw or nut in place during alignment, which saves real time when you’re working overhead or in tight engine bays. The handles have a cushioned grip that doesn’t slip when your hands are oily. 

One thing that surprised me: the size markings are laser-etched, not printed. After six months of tossing them in a tool bag, the markings are still legible. That’s a detail budget sets usually skip.

**What buyers keep praising**  
Scrolling through the 225 ratings, a few themes pop up repeatedly. One reviewer wrote, “Finally a set where the sizes are actually stamped on the handle. No more guessing which is which.” Another noted, “The magnetic tips are stronger than my Snap-on drivers—picked up a dropped screw from the floor without bending down.” Several DIY folks mentioned buying a second set for their RV or boat because the metric/fractional mix covers everything they own.

**Where it falls short**  
Honestly, the biggest flaw is the lack of a storage case. For $25-ish (price varies), you’d expect a blow-molded organizer. Instead, you get a cardboard box. If you’re not disciplined about returning tools, these will end up scattered in a drawer. Also, the set skips 3/8-inch fractional—you get 5/16-inch and 7/16-inch, but that common middle size is missing. You’ll need your socket set for that one, which undermines the “one-stop” promise.

**Who should buy this**  
- **Homeowners tackling basic repairs**: Appliance repairs, furniture assembly, outlet covers—this covers 90% of household fasteners.  
- **DIY mechanics working on older cars**: The metric sizes (10mm, 12mm, 14mm) are the bread-and-butter for mid-2000s vehicles.  
- **RV or boat owners**: The mix of fractional and metric means you’re covered for both American and imported equipment.  

**Skip it if** you’re a pro who needs every fractional size in a single set, or if you demand a storage case. Also skip if you only work with one fastener standard—buy a smaller, cheaper set instead.

**FAQ**  
**Q: Are the handles comfortable for long sessions?**  
A: Yes. The cushioned grips are thick enough to reduce hand fatigue, but not so bulky that they feel clumsy. I’ve used them for an hour straight on a brake job without blisters.

**Q: Will the magnetic tips demagnetize over time?**  
A: Not in normal use. I’ve had mine for a year and the magnets still hold screws firmly. If you’re doing heavy electrical work where you don’t want magnetism, use a different tool.

**Q: Do these fit standard socket ratchets?**  
A: No—they’re hex-shaft drivers, not sockets. You need a separate socket set for ratchet work. These are for quick hand-tightening and precision adjustments.

**The Verdict**  
**Buy it if** you want a versatile, durable nut driver set for general home and auto work, and you don’t mind supplying your own organizer. **Skip it if** you require a specific missing size or a hard case. At 4.6/5, this is one of the better values in its category—just budget five minutes to find a drawer tray.
