# DIKAIDA Bathroom Light Fixtures Review: Does a $40 Vanity Light Deliver?

At $39.99, the DIKAIDA bathroom vanity light is priced like a budget afterthought. But with a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, it’s clearly punching above its weight. The question is whether this modern fixture belongs above your sink or back in the cart.

## What’s Actually in the Box

You get the main mounting bar, the backplate, glass shades, and all mounting hardware. The fixture ships in three length options within each light count — so the 3-light version spans roughly 19.5 inches, while the 4-light runs about 24 inches. That sizing flexibility matters more than most shoppers realize, because a vanity light that’s too long looks worse than no light at all.

The construction is aluminum and steel, not plastic, which surprised me at this price point. The matte black finish is uniform and doesn’t have that cheap, powdery feel you get with low-end spray paint. Glass shades are clear, cylindrical, and open at the bottom — which means you’ll see the bulb, so choose your bulbs accordingly.

## Real-World Performance and Install

Installation is genuinely easy. The mounting bracket screws into a standard junction box, then the fixture body snaps on. The glass shades twist into place with a friction fit — no tools, no tiny screws to drop behind the toilet. Most buyers report a 15-minute install. One reviewer noted, *“I’m not handy at all and had this up in 20 minutes. The wiring was clearly labeled.”*

The light output depends entirely on what bulbs you screw in. Since the glass is clear, I’d recommend frosted or filament-style LED bulbs to avoid harsh glare. With 60-watt equivalent LEDs, this fixture throws even, shadow-free light across a 36-inch vanity — exactly what you want for makeup or shaving.

The one catch: this is a surface-mounted fixture only. If you have a shallow junction box or want a flush-mount look, this isn’t it. The bar extends about 5 inches from the wall, which is standard for vanity lights but worth noting for tighter bathrooms.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The consistent theme in positive reviews is the value-to-looks ratio. One 4-light owner wrote, *“Looks far more expensive than it is. Guests have asked where I got it.”* Another highlighted the size options: *“The 8-light version across a double vanity looks custom — no one believes it cost under $100.”*

The finish holds up well in humid bathroom conditions too, with multiple reviewers confirming no rust or flaking after six months of daily use.

## Where It Falls Short

The bulbs-not-included bit stings, especially at this price point. You’re looking at another $10–$20 for decent LEDs, which pushes the real cost closer to $55–$60. That’s still reasonable, but it’s not the “$40 all-in” the listing implies.

The black finish is also fragile during installation. If you torque the mounting screws too hard, the paint can chip around the holes. Use a hand screwdriver, not a drill, and you’ll be fine.

A few reviewers mentioned the glass shades arrived with minor scuffs from shipping. Not a functional issue, but inspect them before install so you can request replacements early.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you want a modern, minimalist vanity light that looks custom without the custom price tag. It’s ideal for anyone redoing a powder room or rental bathroom on a budget. Choose the 3-light for a single 24–30-inch vanity, the 4-light for a 36–48-inch vanity, and the 6 or 8-light for double vanities.

**Skip it if** you need a fixture with built-in bulbs, want a brass or bronze finish (this is black only), or have a very shallow electrical box that can’t accommodate the 5-inch projection.

## FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Can I use this on a sloped or vaulted ceiling?**  
A: No. This is designed for vertical wall mounting only. The bar won’t angle, and the shades would hang incorrectly on a slope.

**Q: What bulb type does it take?**  
A: Standard E26 medium-base bulbs. Use LED or CFL — the fixture is rated for up to 60 watts per socket.

**Q: Does it come with a dimmer?**  
A: No dimmer included. The fixture itself is dimmable if you install a compatible dimmer switch and use dimmable bulbs.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The DIKAIDA vanity light is a rare budget win: genuinely good design, solid aluminum build, and installation so simple you’ll question why other fixtures cost three times as much. The bulb exclusion and fragile finish are minor annoyances, not deal-breakers. For $40, this is the best-looking vanity light you can buy without stepping up to the $100+ tier.

**Buy it if** you want modern style on a tight budget. **Skip it if** you need integrated bulbs or a finish other than matte black.
