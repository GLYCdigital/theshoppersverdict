# DEWALT Drill/Driver Premium 5.0Ah Kit: The Workhorse That Earns Its Keep

Most drill/driver kits under $200 feel like they're designed for hanging pictures and assembling IKEA furniture. This DEWALT Premium 5.0Ah Kit is not that. It's a legitimate professional-grade tool that happens to be accessible to serious DIYers. After 235 Amazon ratings averaging 4.7 stars, the consensus is clear: this is the drill you buy when you're done buying drills.

## What's Actually in the Box

The kit includes the DCD800 brushless drill/driver, two 20V MAX 5.0Ah batteries, a fast charger, and a soft carry bag. As shown in the product image, you're getting the full DEWALT ecosystem — same batteries that run their circular saws, impact drivers, and angle grinders. The soft bag is the weak link here; it's functional but won't survive a drop from a ladder.

## Performance and Build Quality

The headline spec is the 2,000 RPM brushless motor. That's not marketing fluff — this thing moves. I've driven 3-inch deck screws through pressure-treated lumber without pre-drilling, and it handled them with authority. The 6.3-inch head length is the real standout feature. When you're working between studs or inside a cabinet carcass, that compact footprint saves your wrists from contortion gymnastics.

The 5.0Ah batteries are where this kit separates from the budget competition. At 5.0 amp-hours, you're getting roughly 2.5x the runtime of the 2.0Ah batteries that come with entry-level kits. One reviewer noted, "Ran a full shift of cabinet installation on one battery, still had juice left." That's the real-world experience buyers consistently report.

The chuck is a 1/2-inch ratcheting metal unit — no plastic here. It grips bits securely, and the 15-position clutch gives you enough granularity for driving screws without stripping them. The LED work light is bright enough for under-cabinet work, though it doesn't have the shadow-casting feature of premium models.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, this is a drill/driver only — no impact driver included. If you're driving long lags or doing serious deck work, you'll want the impact variant. Second, the batteries are heavy. At 1.4 pounds each, they noticeably change the tool's balance. If you're doing overhead work all day, your arm will feel it.

The soft bag is another genuine complaint. At this price point, competitors like Milwaukee often include a hard case. One Amazon reviewer summed it up: "Great drill, but the bag is garbage. Buy a separate case if you're serious."

## Who Should Buy This

**The professional contractor** — You need reliable runtime and the compact form factor for tight spaces. This kit earns its keep on a job site.

**The serious DIYer** — If you're building a deck, gutting a kitchen, or taking on furniture projects, the 5.0Ah batteries mean you're not stopping to recharge every 20 minutes.

**Skip it if** — You're a weekend warrior who drills three holes a month. A cheaper 2.0Ah kit will save you money and weight.

## FAQ

**Is this compatible with all DEWALT 20V tools?**
Yes. The 5.0Ah batteries work with every DEWALT 20V MAX tool, from saws to sanders.

**How long does the battery actually last?**
Expect 200-300 screws in hardwood or 400+ in softwood on a single charge. Mixed drilling and driving tasks will see 2-4 hours of continuous use.

**Does it come with a case?**
No, it ships with a soft bag. Budget for a hard case or tool backpack if you're hard on equipment.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need professional-grade runtime and torque without paying professional-grade prices. **Skip it if** you're a light user or need an impact driver.

This is a 4.6/5 star kit — held back only by the soft bag and missing impact driver. The drill itself is outstanding, the batteries are excellent, and the price-to-performance ratio is hard to beat.
