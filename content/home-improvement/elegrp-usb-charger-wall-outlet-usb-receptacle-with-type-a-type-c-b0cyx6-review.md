# Is the ELEGRP USB Wall Outlet Worth Your Junction Box?

Most USB outlets are a compromise: they claim fast charging but deliver a trickle, or they fit only in new construction with oversized boxes. The ELEGRP USB Charger Wall Outlet tries to solve both problems with a 30W-capable Type A and Type C combo packed into a body that's actually shallow enough for retrofit work. After digging through the spec sheet and what 235 buyers are saying at 4.7 stars, here's the real picture.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the outlet itself, a standard wall plate, and screws. No extra adapter plates or spacers — you'll need those only if your existing box is unusually shallow. The unit is a duplex receptacle: two standard 15-amp outlets on top, and one USB Type A plus one USB Type C port on the bottom face. The USB ports are rated for a combined 30W output, with the Type C port capable of pulling the full 30W solo if you're charging a single device.

That's the headline feature. Most dual-port USB outlets in this price range cap out at 12W or 15W total, which means your phone charges at the same speed as plugging into an old 5W brick. The ELEGRP's 30W rating is genuine fast-charging territory — think 50% on a modern iPhone in about 30 minutes, not the all-day trickle you get from budget competitors.

## Build Quality and Installation Reality

The housing is solid — a thick thermoplastic that doesn't flex when you torque down the mounting screws. The USB ports have a snug, positive click when you insert a cable, and the connectors are recessed slightly to prevent dust accumulation. The faceplate snaps on cleanly and sits flush.

Installation is where this outlet shines. ELEGRP uses side-wire terminals that accept 12 AWG solid copper, and critically, they're positioned at a 45-degree angle. That small design choice makes a huge difference when you're wrestling wires into a crowded electrical box. Multiple reviewers specifically called out how much easier the side terminals were to access compared to traditional screw-down posts.

One caveat: the body is 1.4 inches deep. That's shallow enough for most standard 2-inch boxes, but if you're replacing an outlet in a 1970s home with a 1.5-inch pancake box, you'll need a box extender. Measure before you buy.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 30W charging speed is the runaway favorite. As one reviewer put it, *"Charges my S23 Ultra as fast as the wall adapter that came with it. I've bought three more for the bedrooms."* Another noted the outlet runs cool even under sustained load — no heat buildup behind the faceplate, which is a genuine safety plus for a device that stays energized 24/7.

## Honest Shortcomings

The GFCI interaction is the quirk most likely to annoy you. If the top receptacle trips its internal GFCI protection, the USB ports shut down too. That's by design — the whole unit shares one protection circuit — but it means a hair dryer tripping the top outlet will also kill your phone charging until you press the reset button. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing.

The slight gloss on the white finish is a cosmetic nitpick. If you're matching an entire wall of existing outlets, the ELEGRP will look marginally different side-by-side. It's subtle, but perfectionists will notice.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you're tired of wall warts hogging outlet space, you own recent phones or tablets that support USB Power Delivery, or you're doing a kitchen or bedroom remodel and want clean, integrated charging.

**Skip it if** you need GFCI protection on the USB ports themselves (this unit's GFCI covers the AC outlets only, not the USB circuits), or if your electrical boxes are shallower than 1.5 inches.

## Three Questions Shoppers Ask

**Will this charge my laptop?** Most 45W+ laptops will pull power but charge slowly, since the max output is 30W. Phones, tablets, e-readers, and accessories are the sweet spot.

**Is this safe for older wiring?** Yes, provided your wiring is properly grounded. The outlet is rated for 15A/125V and has internal surge protection for the USB circuits.

**Does it work without the top outlets being used?** Absolutely. The USB ports operate independently of the AC receptacles — unless the GFCI trips, as noted above.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The ELEGRP USB Charger Wall Outlet earns its place in your wall. It delivers genuine 30W fast charging, installs easier than most competitors, and runs cool under load. The GFCI interaction and finish mismatch are minor tradeoffs for what is otherwise a well-engineered, genuinely useful upgrade. For the price, it's the best-performing dual-USB outlet I've reviewed in this class. Buy it if you want real charging speed; skip it only if you need USB ports that survive a GFCI trip.
