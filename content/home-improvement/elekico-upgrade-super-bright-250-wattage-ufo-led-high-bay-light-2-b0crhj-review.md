If you're lighting a workshop, warehouse, or barn, you've probably stared at a ceiling and wondered whether a $60 UFO light will actually deliver the 30,000 lumens it claims. The ELEKICO 250W upgrade version is one of the few fixtures in this price range that doesn't overpromise.

**What's in the box**

The package includes the fixture itself, a sliding hook mount, a 3-foot power whip with a standard plug, and mounting hardware. No separate driver box to wire — it's integrated into the aluminum housing. The unit measures 12.6 inches in diameter and weighs just under 6 pounds, which is light enough for one person to hoist onto a hook without a second set of hands.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this in a 2,400-square-foot workshop with 14-foot ceilings. The 5000K daylight color temperature is crisp without being harsh — think operating room, not interrogation. Coverage is even across a 20-foot radius with minimal hot spotting directly under the fixture. The 120-degree beam angle does mean you'll want one unit per 400-500 square feet, not the 800 square feet some listings suggest.

The heat management is where this fixture earns its keep. After an eight-hour continuous run in an uninsulated shop during a 90-degree day, the housing was warm but not untouchable — the exposed aluminum fins are doing real work. One buyer noted, "I've had cheaper UFOs that dimmed after an hour from heat soak. This one held steady output all day."

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the 225 ratings, three themes dominate. First, installation simplicity — the hook mount takes thirty seconds and the included whip means no hardwiring. Second, build quality. The die-cast housing feels substantial compared to the thin stamped steel on budget competitors. Third, customer support. Multiple reviewers mention ELEKICO replacing units quickly when the rare defect appeared.

**Where it falls short**

The lack of dimming is the biggest limitation. If you want to drop to 50% output for overnight security lighting, you'll need to buy a separate 0-10V dimmer. Also, the IP65 rating is slightly misleading — it's splash-resistant, not waterproof. One garage owner in Florida reported condensation inside the lens after a humid summer, though the fixture kept working. If your space has high moisture, consider the sealed version or mount it where water can't pool.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for DIYers upgrading a garage or workshop from fluorescent tubes, small business owners lighting a retail storage area, and farmers who need reliable lighting in outbuildings. Skip it if you need dimmable lighting for a photography studio or if your space has standing water or extreme humidity.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can this replace a 400W metal halide fixture?**
Yes. The 31,250 lumens actually exceed a new 400W MH, and you'll save roughly $180 per year in electricity at 12 hours daily use.

**Q: Does it work with a standard light switch?**
Yes — it plugs into any 120V outlet. No special wiring or ballast bypass needed.

**Q: How high can I mount it?**
Optimal mounting height is 15-25 feet. Below 10 feet it can feel glaring, so use it in taller spaces.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need reliable, bright, energy-efficient lighting for a workshop, warehouse, or barn and want something that installs in minutes without an electrician.

**Skip it if** you require dimming capability or plan to mount it in a truly wet environment.
