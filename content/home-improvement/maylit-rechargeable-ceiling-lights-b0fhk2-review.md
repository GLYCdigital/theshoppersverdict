# maylit Rechargeable Ceiling Lights: A Handy Fix With a Catch

Let's be direct: if you've got a closet, hallway, pantry, or rental apartment where wiring a ceiling light isn't an option, the **maylit Rechargeable Ceiling Lights** are a genuinely clever workaround. But "workaround" is the key word — this isn't a permanent replacement for a hardwired fixture. With a 3.7-star average across 185 ratings, buyers are split between "brilliant for my rental" and "battery life killed it for me." Here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes the LED ceiling light panel, a mounting plate, adhesive tape, screws, and a charging cable. The unit itself is slim — roughly 12 inches in diameter for the round model — and sits close to the ceiling, so it doesn't look like a temporary hack. As shown in the product image, the flush-mount design is clean and unobtrusive.

Installation is genuinely simple: peel, stick, or screw the plate to the ceiling, then magnetically attach the light. No wiring, no breaker flipping, no permits. That's the core appeal, and it delivers.

## Performance: Bright Enough, But Watch the Battery

On full brightness, the light outputs a solid 600–800 lumens depending on the model — enough to illuminate a small bedroom or walk-in closet without feeling dim. The adjustable color temperature (warm to cool white) is a nice touch that adds versatility.

The elephant in the room is battery life. On maximum brightness, expect **2–4 hours of continuous use before the light dims**. That's rough for a primary room light. On the lower settings, you can stretch it to 8–10 hours, but that means living in a dimmer space. Buyers consistently note this tradeoff, with one reviewer writing, *"Great light in my pantry, but I'm recharging it every other day."*

Recharging is also clunkier than it should be. You have to take the fixture off the ceiling, plug it in, and wait 4–6 hours. If it's mounted high, that's a step stool situation every time.

## What Buyers Praise

When the battery isn't the issue, owners genuinely love these. The wireless install is the headline — *"Installed in 5 minutes in my rental without asking my landlord. Works perfectly for my entryway."* Multiple reviews echo that sentiment. The magnetic mount makes it easy to remove for charging or repositioning (when it works — see below).

## Honest Shortcomings

Three issues keep this from being a no-brainer:

1. **Battery degradation over time** — a few long-term reviewers report capacity dropping noticeably after 6 months of regular use.
2. **Mounting reliability on textured ceilings** — the adhesive can struggle with popcorn or heavily textured surfaces. One buyer reported their light "fell off at 2 AM." Screw mounting is the safer bet where allowed.
3. **No remote or motion sensor on the base model** — you're getting up to toggle it, which undercuts the convenience factor.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Renters** who can't hardwire but need light in a dark hallway, closet, or laundry room
- **Homeowners** looking for a quick, non-permanent solution for a pantry or utility room
- **Campers or emergency preparedness** — a rechargeable, portable light that works off-grid

**Skip it if** you need a main living space light for hours each evening, or if you hate the idea of recharging a ceiling fixture like it's a phone.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does the battery last on a single charge?**
A: 2–4 hours on full brightness, up to 8–10 hours on the dimmest setting. Recharge time is 4–6 hours.

**Q: Will the adhesive hold on textured ceilings?**
A: It's risky. Use the included screws if your ceiling is textured or if you're at all unsure. The adhesive is best on smooth, flat surfaces.

**Q: Can I leave it plugged in while mounted?**
A: Not with the standard setup — the charging port is on the unit itself, which must be removed to charge. No pass-through charging.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a quick, wireless light fix for a small space and don't mind the recharge routine. **Skip it if** you expect consistent, long-lasting illumination or a set-it-and-forget-it installation.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — A genuinely useful product with a real battery-life compromise. For the right use case, it's a solid purchase. For everything else, keep looking.
