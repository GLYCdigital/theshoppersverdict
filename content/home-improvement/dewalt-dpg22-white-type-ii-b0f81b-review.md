Let me be blunt: most safety glasses under $15 are disposable junk that fog up the second you start sweating. The DEWALT DPG22 White Type II sits in a strange middle ground — better than the bargain-bin stuff, but not quite premium. Here's the real breakdown after digging through 200 ratings and the spec sheet.

**What you actually get**

The DPG22 is a wrap-around goggle-style glass with a clear polycarbonate lens, integrated side shields, and a rubberized nose bridge. They weigh in around 1.1 ounces — noticeably lighter than the 3M Virtua series. The frame is a single-piece design, which means fewer crevices for dust to collect. Anti-scratch coating on the outside, anti-fog on the inside. Nothing fancy in the box — just the glasses. No case, no microfiber bag. For the price point, that's expected, but worth noting if you're tossing them in a tool bag.

**Performance in the real world**

The coverage is the standout feature here. The wrap-around shape and molded side shields create a seal that catches sawdust, grinding sparks, and wind-blown debris that flat-lens glasses let through. On a framing job or under a miter saw, that's the difference between finishing your cut and blinking sawdust out of your eye for ten minutes.

The lens clarity is solid. Optical distortion is minimal at the edges — something that plagues cheaper glasses. Colors stay true, which matters if you're doing electrical work and need to read wire colors accurately.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The recurring theme in positive reviews is durability. One buyer noted: *"Dropped these from a 12-foot ladder onto concrete. Scratched but didn't crack. Still wear them every day."* Another mentioned the fit: *"Finally a pair that doesn't slide down my nose when I lean over a workbench."* The rubberized nose bridge does its job — these stay put during overhead work and when you're bending down to grab stock.

**Where they fall short**

The anti-fog coating is the biggest complaint, and it's a fair one. Multiple reviewers report the coating starts peeling or loses effectiveness after a few months of daily use. If you work in hot, humid environments or go between air-conditioned spaces and the outdoors, you'll be wiping them constantly by month three.

The temple arms are also on the narrow side. If you've got a wider head — say, hat size 7¾ or larger — expect pressure points behind your ears during extended wear. A few reviewers with larger heads reported headaches after half-day use. There's no adjustment mechanism, so what you get is what you get.

Also worth noting: the lens is clear, not tinted. If you work outdoors in full sun, you'll want to pick up a shaded pair for those jobs or swap to sunglasses when you step outside.

**Who should buy these**

- **Carpenters and framers**: The side impact protection and lightweight frame make these a no-brainer for all-day wear.
- **DIY weekend warriors**: If you're tired of buying $5 glasses every other project because they scratch or fog instantly, the DPG22 will outlast three or four pairs of the cheap stuff.
- **Anyone wearing glasses under a face shield**: The low-profile frame sits close to the face without digging in.

**Who should skip them**

- **Wide-headed folks**: Try them on first if you can. The narrow temples are a real issue.
- **High-humidity environments**: If you're in the Gulf South or working in a steamy plant, the anti-fog fade is going to frustrate you fast.
- **Outdoor-only workers**: Clear lens means squinting in direct sun. Get a tinted model instead.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do these fog up when you wear a mask or respirator?**
A: Yes, more than you'd like. The anti-fog coating helps initially, but exhaled air from a mask still gets behind the lens. In cold weather, they'll fog on startup regardless of the coating.

**Q: Are these actually ANSI Z87.1 certified?**
A: Yes. They meet the Z87.1+ high-impact standard, which means they're rated for 150 fps impact resistance. That's real protection, not marketing theater.

**Q: Can you wear these over prescription glasses?**
A: No. They're designed to sit close to the face. Your prescription frames won't fit underneath comfortably. You'd need the DEWALT DPG24 (OTG style) for that.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want solid, durable eye protection that covers more angles than flat-lens glasses, and you're not in a high-humidity environment or working under direct sun all day.

**Skip it if** you've got a wide head, work in consistently humid conditions, or need tinted lenses for outdoor work.

At roughly the price of a decent lunch, the DPG22 delivers legitimate protection with real durability. The anti-fog coating and narrow fit are genuine flaws, but for the majority of users who need all-day coverage at a reasonable price, these are a solid 4-star pick. They won't be the last pair you buy, but they'll last long enough that you won't mind replacing them.
