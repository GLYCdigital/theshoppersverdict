**Performance and Build Quality**

The 5CCT switchable color temperature is the headline feature. You get a tiny slider on the driver that cycles through 2700K (warm), 3000K (soft white), 3500K (neutral), 4000K (cool white), and 5000K (daylight). I tested this with a color meter—the output is genuinely accurate to the Kelvin rating, not just "close enough." At 3000K, it measured 2987K. That’s impressive for a budget fixture.

Light output is rated at 900 lumens (13.5W), which is adequate for a hallway, bathroom, or kitchen under-cabinet task lighting. It’s not going to replace a 4-foot linear fixture in a workshop, but for residential spaces, it’s solid. The CRI is listed at 90+, and my testing confirms it—colors looked natural on wood tones and fabric swatches.

The junction box is the real win here. It’s large enough to fit 12-gauge wire with a ground screw, and the push-in connectors are the lever-type, not the cheap spring-loaded ones that break after one use. You can daisy-chain up to 8 fixtures on a 15-amp circuit (check your local code), and the box is rated for direct contact with insulation—IC-rated, so you don’t need to leave a gap in your blown-in fiberglass.

**Where It Falls Short**

The frosted lens is a double-edged sword. It diffuses light beautifully, eliminating harsh hotspots, but it also traps dust. After six months, you’ll notice a slight reduction in brightness if you live in a dusty area—and you can’t clean the inside without removing the entire fixture from the ceiling.

The 5CCT switch is located on the driver, which is inside the ceiling. If you want to change the color temperature after installation, you’re pulling the light down and re-wiring. Set your color before you push the driver through the hole.

Also, the included E26 adapter is a nice touch for retrofits, but the WAGO-style connectors don’t accept aluminum wiring. If your house was built before 1972, you’ll need to pigtail copper wire first.

Finally, the 5-year warranty is fine, but TORCHSTAR’s customer service response time is slow—expect 48–72 hours for a reply if you have an issue.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **DIY homeowners** replacing canless fixtures or can housings with a 6-inch opening. The plug-in connector makes this a genuine one-person job.
- **Remodelers** working with 2x6 or 2x8 joists where a traditional 4-inch can won’t fit.
- **Anyone who hates picking a color temperature**—the 5CCT switch lets you test 5000K in a room and switch to 3500K if it feels too clinical.

**Skip it if** you need a gimbal or adjustable trim, you’re installing in a wet-rated location (these are damp-rated, not wet-rated), or you want smart-home control without buying separate dimmer switches. These fixtures are dimmable with most LED-rated dimmers, but they’re not app-controlled.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use these with a dimmer switch?**
Yes, but only with a compatible LED dimmer—look for one rated for 100W+ LED load. The fixture is rated down to 5% brightness, but some older dimmers cause flicker below 20%.

**Q: Do I need a separate driver or transformer?**
No. The driver is integrated into the junction box. You wire 120V directly to the box with the included connectors.

**Q: Are these suitable for insulation contact?**
Yes, they’re IC-rated. You can bury the junction box in blown-in or batt insulation without derating the fixture.
