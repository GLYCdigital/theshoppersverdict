**Performance and Real-World Use**

I've used this jig on both frameless (European-style) and face-frame cabinets, and it handles both without modification. The key advantage is speed: once you set the arms to match your hinge spacing, you can drill hinge cup holes in under two minutes per door. The drill guides are hardened steel, so they won't wallow out after drilling dozens of holes. The built-in depth stop is a genuine time-saver — it prevents you from accidentally drilling through the door face, which is the most common rookie mistake with concealed hinges.

The centering tool deserves special mention. It automatically centers the hinge cup on the door edge, eliminating the need to measure and mark each door individually. If you're installing 20 doors in a kitchen remodel, this alone justifies the purchase price.

**Where It Falls Short**

Honest downsides exist. First, the jig only accommodates doors up to about 24 inches wide. Finally, the price point sits higher than basic drill templates, though the precision and repeatability justify the premium for anyone doing more than a single door.

**FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask**

**Q: Does this work with face-frame cabinets or only frameless?**
A: Both. The arms adjust to accommodate face-frame styles, and the 3mm spacer works for either configuration. Just follow the manual's setup steps for each type.

**Q: What hinge brand does it work with?**
A: It's designed for standard 35mm concealed hinges — Blum, Salice, Grass, and similar. If your hinges use a different cup diameter, it won't fit.

**Q: Can I use a standard drill with this jig?**
A: Yes. A 3/8" or 1/2" cordless drill with the included 35mm bit works fine. A drill guide attachment helps keep holes perpendicular, but the jig's hardened guides already ensure accuracy.
