**The Short Version**

If you're remodeling a powder room or replacing a builder-grade faucet that's seen better days, the Moen 6610 Brantford is the kind of purchase you stop thinking about after installation — and that's exactly what you want. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it's not flashy, but it's honest. This is a classic two-handle centerset faucet that does the job without drama, backed by one of the most reliable names in plumbing.

**What You Actually Get**

The box includes the faucet body, two lever handles, a matching pop-up drain assembly, and the mounting hardware. As shown in the product image, it's a low-arc design with a clean chrome finish — nothing ornate, nothing modern. It fits the standard 4-inch centerset configuration, meaning it drops into most existing 3-hole bathroom sinks without needing a deck plate or extra adapters. The unit measures about 7.5 inches from base to spout tip, with a spout height of roughly 4.5 inches above the deck.

The Brantford is part of Moen's value-oriented line, but don't mistake "value" for "cheap." The body is solid brass, and the handles are metal — no thin chrome-over-plastic here. The lever handles require about a quarter-turn to go from cold to hot, and they move with a satisfying, precise click rather than a mushy rotation.

**Real-World Performance**

Installation is straightforward for anyone who's replaced a faucet before. The supply lines are pre-attached, which saves a trip under the sink with a wrench. The mounting nuts are accessible, though tight — expect to spend 45 minutes to an hour if you're working in a confined vanity space. One buyer noted, "Took me longer to remove the old faucet than install this one. Everything lined up perfectly."

Water flow is consistent at 1.2 gallons per minute — enough for handwashing and toothbrushing, and it meets WaterSense criteria for efficiency. The cartridge design is Moen's standard 1222 model, which is widely available at hardware stores if you ever need a replacement. That's a small detail that matters years down the road.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The dominant theme in reviews is reliability. At this price point, buyers expect the faucet to work without fuss, and the Brantford delivers. One verified purchaser wrote: "Second Moen faucet I've installed in this house. Both work flawlessly. The finish has held up with hard water, no spots." Multiple reviewers mention the solid feel — the weight of the brass body reassures you this isn't a disposable fixture.

The chrome finish is another recurring point of praise. Several buyers with well water or mineral-heavy municipal water report the finish resists spotting better than other brands they've tried. That's a practical advantage if you're tired of wiping down fixtures every other day.

**Where It Falls Short**

The low-arc spout is the most common complaint — and it's a legitimate one. At 4.5 inches tall, you're not going to fill a water bottle or wash your face comfortably. This faucet is strictly for handwashing. If your sink is shallow or you have tall fixtures nearby, consider the high-arc version instead.

The included pop-up drain is functional but plastic. It works, but it feels out of place next to the brass faucet body. Several reviewers mention replacing it with a metal drain within the first year. Budget for that if you care about consistency in finish quality.

Also note: the handles are on the smaller side. If you have arthritic hands or limited grip strength, the levers require a bit of pinch — not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **DIY homeowners** replacing a dated faucet in a guest bath or half bath — the installation is manageable and the warranty covers your mistakes.
- **Landlords or property managers** who want a reliable, no-callbacks faucet at a reasonable price.
- **Anyone matching an existing chrome fixture set** — the Brantford's classic profile blends with almost any traditional bathroom.

**Skip It If**

- You need a tall spout for filling pitchers or washing pets.
- Your sink has a single-hole configuration — you'd need a deck plate, and this faucet doesn't include one.
- You're doing a modern or industrial bathroom; this is a traditional design that will look out of place.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this faucet come with supply lines?**
A: Yes, 12-inch braided stainless steel supply lines are pre-attached. You'll connect them directly to your shut-off valves with a 1/2-inch connection.

**Q: Is the finish resistant to hard water spots?**
A: Chrome is inherently more forgiving than brushed nickel or matte black. Buyers with hard water report minimal spotting, but no finish is completely spot-free — wipe it down occasionally.

**Q: Can I use this on a vessel sink?**
A: No. The low-arc spout is too short to clear a vessel sink rim. This is designed for drop-in or undermount sinks with standard clearance.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a dependable, traditional-looking faucet that installs easily and won't leak for a decade. **Skip it if** you need a taller spout or want a matching metal drain right out of the box.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The Moen 6610 Brantford earns its stars through solid construction, a trusted warranty, and consistent performance. It's not exciting, but it's exactly what a bathroom faucet should be: forgettable in the best possible way.
