If you've ever wrestled with a desk lamp whose cord barely reaches the outlet, or tried to read in bed without blinding your partner, you already know why the cordless category exists. This small desk lamp with a 3600mAh battery is a solid answer to that problem — but it comes with a few compromises worth understanding before you buy.

## What You Actually Get

Unboxing reveals the lamp itself, a USB-C charging cable, and a slim user guide. That's it — no wall adapter, no mounting hardware. The lamp measures roughly 14 inches tall when fully extended, with a flexible gooseneck that bends about 270 degrees. The base is a flat disc around 4 inches in diameter, and the whole unit weighs just over a pound.

The headline feature is that 3600mAh battery. For context, that's roughly double what most budget cordless lamps pack. In real-world use, that translates to about 10 hours on the lowest brightness setting and 5-6 hours at full output. If you're using it as a nightly reading lamp for 2-3 hours, you're charging roughly twice a week.

## Performance and Build Quality

The light itself is the strong suit here. You get three color temperatures — warm white around 3000K, neutral around 4500K, and cool white near 6000K — and each one dims smoothly via a touch sensor on the base. The stepless dimming is genuinely useful; you can dial in exactly the right brightness for a dark room versus a bright office.

The LED panel produces about 250 lumens at full tilt. That's respectable for close work like reading, soldering, or detail crafts. It won't wash a whole room in light, but that's not what this is designed for.

Build quality is decent for the price point. The gooseneck holds its position well and doesn't sag after repeated adjustments. The touch controls are responsive — one tap for power, a hold to dim, and a short tap to cycle color temperatures. The base is the weak link: it's lightweight and can tip if you bend the neck aggressively sideways. One Amazon reviewer noted, "Great lamp overall, but I had to set it against a book to keep it stable at extreme angles."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.5-star average across 225 ratings reflects genuine satisfaction. The most common praise centers on battery life and portability. A verified buyer wrote: "Took this camping and used it for three nights of reading without charging. That's the real deal." Others highlight the versatility — it works equally well on a nightstand, a desk, or clipped into a kitchen counter setup.

The color temperature control earns frequent mentions. Multiple reviewers specifically call out the warm setting for not being harsh on the eyes at night, which matters if you're reading before sleep.

## Where It Falls Short

The charging situation is the biggest friction point. With no included wall adapter and a 4-5 hour charge time, you're either using a spare phone charger or buying one separately. If you drain the battery completely and forget to charge it, you're in the dark for the better part of an evening.

The lightweight base is the second issue. It's fine for straight-up or slightly angled positions, but aggressive gooseneck bends can make it unstable. If your desk is subject to vibration or you bump it often, this could be a daily annoyance.

Finally, the 250-lumen maximum means this is strictly a task lamp. Don't buy it expecting to light a home office or a bedroom corner.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Campers and travelers** who need a reliable, packable light source that doesn't rely on wall outlets
- **Night readers** who want adjustable warmth and brightness without disturbing a sleeping partner
- **Students in dorms** with limited outlet access near their desks
- **Anyone who hates cord clutter** and wants a lamp that can move from desk to nightstand to kitchen without unplugging

Skip it if you need room-filling brightness, or if you want a lamp with a weighted anti-tip base for heavy repositioning.

## FAQ

**How long does the battery actually last?**
Real-world runtime is 5-6 hours at full brightness and 9-10 hours at the lowest setting with warm light. Cold temperatures reduce battery performance by roughly 15-20%.

**Can you use it while charging?**
Yes, it works while plugged in, which is handy if you're near an outlet. It just defeats the cordless purpose.

**Is the light bright enough for detailed work?**
At close range, yes — think 12-18 inches from the work surface. Beyond that, brightness drops off noticeably. Good for reading, journaling, or small repairs. Not ideal for large drafting surfaces.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a dependable, portable task light with genuine battery stamina and don't mind the slow charge time.

**Skip it if** you need to light a larger area, require a rock-solid base for aggressive positioning, or want a lamp that includes everything needed to charge out of the box.

At 4.6/5, this is one of the better cordless desk lamps in its class. The battery capacity is honest, the light quality is solid, and the flexibility covers most use cases. Just budget for a USB-C wall adapter and keep it on the charger when you're not using it.
