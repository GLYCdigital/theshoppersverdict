The WILIT LED Night Light solves a problem most bedside lamps don't even acknowledge: your phone doesn't need to be on the floor, and your eyes don't need to be assaulted by a 3,000-lumen flashlight when you stumble to the bathroom at 2 AM. This is a purpose-built nightstand tool, not a decorative afterthought.

**What you actually get in the box**

The lamp ships with the main unit, a USB-C power cable (no wall adapter included — you'll use your phone's charger or buy one), and a basic instruction sheet. The lamp itself measures roughly 6.5 inches tall with a 4.5-inch diameter base. The wireless charging pad sits on the front lip of the base, angled slightly upward. That's a design choice worth noting: you can glance at incoming notifications without lifting the phone. As shown in the product image, the phone rests at a natural viewing angle rather than lying flat.

**Performance and build quality in real use**

The headline feature is the three color temperature presets: warm white (about 3000K), neutral (4000K), and daylight (5000K+). Each has its own dedicated touch button on the base, so you're not tapping through five modes to get from "reading" to "nightlight." That's a small thing that matters when you're half-asleep. Brightness is stepless — touch and hold any button to dim or brighten.

The "eye-caring" claim holds up. The light panel uses a diffusion layer that eliminates hot spots, and at minimum brightness it's comparable to a dimmed phone screen. It's genuinely flicker-free at all levels — a rarity at this price point. One buyer noted, *"I have migraines triggered by fluorescent flicker, and this lamp is the first one I've owned that doesn't trigger them."* That tracks with the specs.

Build quality is acceptable but not premium. The body is matte ABS plastic with a soft-touch finish. It feels solid for what it is, but the base is hollow and lighter than you'd expect — roughly 1.2 pounds. The wireless charging pad is Qi-certified and works with any phone that supports wireless charging, including recent iPhones and Samsung Galaxy devices. However, it's capped at 10W output.

**Where it genuinely falls short**

The 10W charging limit is the biggest compromise. If you're used to 25W Samsung Super Fast Charging or 15W MagSafe, this will feel slow. Overnight, that's fine. But if you're topping up before heading out, you'll notice the difference. One reviewer summed it up: *"It charges my iPhone 14 overnight no problem, but it's not a fast charger. It's a maintainer."*

The touch controls are also more sensitive than they should be. A light brush from a sleeve or a charging cable can toggle the lamp on or change brightness. There's no lock mode, which is an oversight for a product designed for dark rooms. And the light base means a heavy braided cable can pull the lamp over if you're not careful about routing it.

**Who should buy this**

- **Light sleepers who need a dim, warm light** — the 3000K setting at minimum brightness is perfect for not waking your partner while you check on a kid or grab water.
- **Anyone with a cluttered nightstand** — combining lamp and charger frees up real estate.
- **Back-of-the-bedroom users who don't need fast charging overnight** — if you charge while you sleep, the 10W cap is irrelevant.
- **Skip it if you need rapid top-ups** or if you're a fidgety sleeper who might knock over a lightweight lamp.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the wireless charger work with a phone case?**
A: Yes, up to about 5mm of case thickness. Thick rugged cases with metal kickstands or magnetic accessories will interfere — test before relying on it.

**Q: Can you use the lamp while the phone is charging?**
A: Yes. The light functions independently of the charging pad. You can adjust brightness and color while the phone charges.

**Q: Is the USB-C port for power only, or can it charge other devices?**
A: Power input only. The lamp draws power from the USB-C connection; there's no passthrough charging port.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a dedicated nightstand lamp that doubles as a slow-and-steady phone charger, and you value adjustable color temperature over raw charging speed. The eye-caring diffusion and independent color controls are genuinely better than most competitors at this price.

**Skip it if** you need fast wireless charging, have a heavy-handed approach to your nightstand, or expect premium build materials.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A well-designed bedside tool with one major compromise (charging speed) and a minor annoyance (sensitive touch controls). The core function — comfortable, adjustable light — is excellent.
