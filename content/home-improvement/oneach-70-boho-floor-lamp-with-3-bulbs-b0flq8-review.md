## The Oneach 70" Boho Floor Lamp: The 3-Bulb Trick That Actually Works

Let's be blunt: most floor lamps under $100 are boring. A pole, a shade, one bulb, done. The Oneach 70" Boho Floor Lamp is different — not because it's flashy, but because it solves a real problem. That 70-inch frame with three separate bulb sockets lets you stack light sources vertically, which means you can light a dark corner without washing out the whole room.

Is it worth your money? Here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the floor lamp, three bulb sockets on a central pole, a rattan-wrapped shade structure, and a weighted base. You'll need to provide your own bulbs — it takes three standard E26 bulbs, so any LED or vintage Edison bulb works. Assembly is straightforward; the pole sections thread together, and the base screws on. Expect 10-15 minutes of setup, no tools required beyond your hands.

The boho look comes from the natural rattan weave wrapping the three-tier shade frame. As shown in the product image, it's a warm, textured piece that leans into the organic-modern aesthetic — think Anthropologie but at a fraction of the price.

## Build Quality & Real-World Performance

The metal pole is sturdy — it's not flimsy hollow tubing. The base has enough heft that the lamp doesn't wobble when you bump it, which is more than I can say for several lamps in this price range. The rattan is tightly woven and feels durable, not like it'll unravel after a month.

The three-bulb design is the standout feature. You can put warm Edison bulbs in the top and cooler LEDs in the bottom, creating a layered wash of light that's genuinely flattering. Or use all three for full room illumination. The 70-inch height puts the top bulb around eye level, which works well for reading if you position it right.

One note: the rattan has small gaps between the weaves. This creates a lovely dappled light pattern on walls, but it's not a fully diffused shade. If you want glare-free, soft light, this isn't quite it.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews isn't an accident. Recurring themes in feedback:

- **The aesthetic.** Buyers repeatedly mention it looks far more expensive than it is. One reviewer noted, "I bought this for my living room and everyone thinks I spent $200+ on it."
- **The stability.** Multiple customers with pets or kids confirmed it doesn't tip easily. "My cat runs into everything — this lamp hasn't budged," one wrote.
- **The light output.** The three bulbs make a real difference. "Finally a lamp that actually lights my whole corner instead of just a tiny circle," said a verified purchase.

## Where It Falls Short

Be honest with yourself before buying:

**No dimmer switch.** This is the biggest miss. You're at the mercy of your bulb choice. Buy dimmable bulbs and use a smart plug if you want variable brightness — a $10 workaround that fixes this.

**The rattan gaps.** As mentioned, light spills through the weave. If you're sensitive to glare or want a clean, even glow, look elsewhere.

**The price fluctuates.** It's not cheap-cheap — but for the construction quality and the three-bulb versatility, it's fair. If it drops below $80, it's a steal.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Boho or organic-modern decor lovers** who want a statement piece without the designer markup
- **Anyone with a dark corner** that needs actual light, not just accent lighting
- **Renters** who want a tall, stable lamp that doesn't require wall mounting

**Skip it if** you need fully diffused, soft lighting or you refuse to buy a separate dimmer solution.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use smart bulbs in all three sockets?**
A: Yes, all three sockets are standard E26. Just make sure your smart bulbs aren't physically too large to fit within the rattan frame — standard A19 bulbs fit fine.

**Q: Does the rattan shed or smell?**
A: No significant shedding reported. A few buyers noted a mild natural-fiber smell for the first day or two; it fades quickly.

**Q: Is it easy to clean?**
A: The rattan can be dusted with a microfiber cloth or vacuumed with a brush attachment. Avoid wet cleaners — they can discolor the natural fibers.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**
