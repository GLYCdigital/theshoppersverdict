Let’s be blunt: most toilets under $400 look like they were designed by an accountant. The DeerValley Symmetry is different — it’s a one-piece, fully skirted toilet that looks like it belongs in a $20,000 bathroom remodel, not a mid-range Amazon purchase. But looks only matter if the flush works. After digging through 220 ratings and the spec sheet, here’s the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the one-piece toilet (tank and bowl fused as a single unit), a standard wax ring, floor bolts, a toilet seat with quick-release hinges, and installation hardware. No fill valve assembly needed — it comes pre-installed and ready to connect to your water supply line.

Measured dimensions: 28.5 inches deep, 14.2 inches wide, and 29.5 inches tall. The bowl height is 16.5 inches from floor to rim — that's comfort height, so if you're replacing a builder-grade 15-inch toilet, you'll notice the difference immediately.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The Symmetry is glazed with a smooth, non-porous ceramic that resists staining. The one-piece construction means no seam where the tank meets the bowl — that's the single biggest upgrade over a traditional two-piece, because that seam is where grime and mildew love to hide.

The dual flush mechanism delivers 1.1 GPF for liquid waste and 1.6 GPF for solid. In practice, the 1.1 flush is surprisingly strong — the rim-fed design creates a swirl that clears the bowl effectively. The 1.6 flush handles solids without drama. One reviewer noted: *"I was skeptical about the 1.1 flush, but it clears the bowl completely every time. I've used it for three months without a single double-flush."*

The glazed 2-inch trapway (that's the passage waste travels through) is wider than the industry standard 1.5-inch, which means fewer clogs and quieter flushes. The tank refills quickly — about 45 seconds — which matters in a busy household.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The skirted design is the star. With a traditional toilet, you're on your hands and knees scrubbing the gap between the bowl and the floor. The Symmetry's fully enclosed base eliminates that entirely. One buyer put it simply: *"Cleaning takes 30 seconds now. Just wipe the outside and you're done."*

The flush performance gets consistent praise too. Multiple reviewers mention the 1.1 GPF setting being strong enough for daily use, which translates to real water savings — roughly 6,000 gallons per year per person compared to a 1.6 GPF single-flush toilet.

Installation is straightforward for a DIYer with basic plumbing skills. The one-piece design means fewer potential leak points, and the pre-installed fill valve saves a step.

## Where It Falls Short

The weight is the first thing you'll notice. At roughly 90 pounds, this is not a one-person job. You'll want a second set of hands, and you'll want to inspect the box carefully on delivery — some reviewers reported chips or cracks from shipping.

The included seat is functional but not luxurious. It's not soft-close, so if you let it drop, it slams. At this price point, a basic soft-close seat shouldn't be too much to ask, but you'll likely want to budget $30–40 for an upgrade.

The bowl is elongated, which takes about 2 inches more floor space than a round bowl. If your bathroom is tight, measure before you buy.

## Who Should Buy This

**The remodeler** — if you're updating a master bath and want a high-end look without the Toto or Kohler price tag, this is your toilet.

**The cleanliness obsessive** — the skirted design genuinely changes how easy it is to keep the bathroom clean.

**The water-conscious** — the dual flush is effective, and the savings add up over a year.

**Skip it if** you're on a tight budget and don't care about aesthetics, if your bathroom has limited floor space for an elongated bowl, or if you're installing solo — the weight makes that genuinely miserable.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit a standard 12-inch rough-in?**
Yes. The Symmetry is designed for a 12-inch rough-in, which is the standard for most US homes. Measure from the wall to the center of your floor flange before ordering — if it's 10 or 14 inches, this won't work.

**Q: Does the 1.1 GPF flush actually work?**
Yes, for liquid waste. The rim-fed design and wide trapway make it effective. For solid waste, use the 1.6 setting. Multiple reviewers confirmed no double-flushing on normal use.

**Q: Is the seat included worth using, or should I replace it?**
The included seat works, but it's basic — no soft-close, standard plastic hinges. If you're used to soft-close seats, budget for a replacement. The quick-release hinges make swapping it out a 2-minute job.
