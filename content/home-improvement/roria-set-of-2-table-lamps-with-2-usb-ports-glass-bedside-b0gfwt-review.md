**Bottom line up front:** The RORIA 2-pack bedside lamps solve a problem most lamp sets ignore — where do you plug in your phone? With two USB ports built into each lamp's base, this set eliminates the need for separate wall chargers on both sides of the bed. At 4.5 stars across 225 ratings, buyers are mostly happy, but there are some specifics worth knowing before you click buy.

## What's in the box

You get two identical lamps, each with a tempered glass base, a fabric drum shade, and a built-in USB hub. The shades measure roughly 14 inches tall overall with a 10-inch diameter shade — proportionate for standard nightstands, not oversized for king beds, but adequate.

The glass base is the standout material choice. It's heavy enough that the lamp feels planted. One buyer noted, "The glass base makes these feel way more expensive than they are — they don't wobble or tip when my cat jumps on the nightstand." That weight matters if you have kids or pets.

## Real-world performance

The 3-way touch dimming is the feature you'll actually use daily. Tap once for low, twice for medium, three times for full brightness. It's intuitive and doesn't require fumbling for a switch in the dark. The touch sensor sits on the metal neck, which means you don't have to find a tiny button — just reach for the lamp.

The USB ports deliver 5W/1A each. That's enough to trickle-charge a phone overnight, but it won't fast-charge anything modern. If you're expecting USB-C Power Delivery, look elsewhere. For bedside use where you plug in before sleep and unplug in the morning, it's fine.

Light output depends entirely on the bulb you choose since bulbs aren't included. The shade diffuses light reasonably well, but it's not a full room illuminator — this is task and ambient lighting, not a reading lamp replacement.

## What buyers consistently praise

The two-port-per-lamp design is the most-cited benefit. As one verified purchaser put it: "Finally, both my wife and I can charge our phones without an ugly power strip on the floor." The set covers both nightstands evenly, which is the entire point of buying a pair.

Build quality gets consistent mentions. The glass base, metal accents, and fabric shade all feel substantial — several reviewers compared the look to lamps costing twice as much.

## Where it falls short

The glass base's aesthetics come with a maintenance cost. Fingerprints, dust, and smudges show up almost immediately. If you're someone who notices these things, you'll be wiping the bases down weekly.

The 5W USB output is the other real limitation. A phone plugged in from 15% battery will take several hours to reach full — fine for overnight, frustrating if you need a quick boost before heading out.

The missing bulbs also sting a bit. You're paying for a complete lighting solution; having to buy two more bulbs adds hidden cost and another trip to the store.

## Who should buy this

- **Couples sharing a nightstand setup** — the two-port design solves the charger competition problem
- **Anyone who wants lamps that look more expensive than they are** — the glass base delivers on that
- **People who prefer touch controls** over fumbling for switches in the dark

Skip it if you need fast charging, want a single statement lamp rather than a matching pair, or hate cleaning glass surfaces.

## FAQ

**Do I need to buy special bulbs?**
No — standard E26 base bulbs work. Any LED bulb up to 60W equivalent will fit. Just note they're not included.

**Can I use the USB ports without the light on?**
Yes, the USB ports are independent of the light switch. You can charge devices with the lamp off.

**Is the touch control sensitive enough to accidentally trigger?**
A few reviewers mentioned brushing the neck while reaching across the nightstand can toggle the light. It's not a dealbreaker, but it happens.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a matching pair of solid, good-looking bedside lamps with built-in charging that won't tip over or look cheap. The 4.5-star rating holds up.

**Skip it if:** You need fast charging, hate cleaning glass, or expect bulbs at this price point.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The RORIA 2-pack delivers exactly what it promises: functional, attractive bedside lighting with practical USB ports. The minor frustrations (slow charging, fingerprint-prone glass) are tradeoffs, not dealbreakers. For most couples, this set is a solid buy.
