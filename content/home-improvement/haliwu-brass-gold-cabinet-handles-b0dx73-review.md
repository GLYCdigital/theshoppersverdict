**The short version:** If you're doing a budget kitchen refresh and want that warm brass look without re-drilling every drawer front, these Haliwu handles get the job done. But temper your expectations on the finish — this is brushed satin gold, not mirror-polished.

## What you actually get

The box contains ten handles (assuming you order the standard 10-pack), ten mounting screws, and a small hex wrench. Each handle measures roughly 5 inches overall with a 3-inch (76mm) screw hole spacing — that's the most common spacing in American cabinetry, so these will likely drop into existing holes if you're swapping out old pulls. The bar itself is about 0.5 inches in diameter, substantial enough to grip comfortably without feeling chunky.

One thing I noticed immediately from the product images and confirmed in hand: these are **zinc alloy with a plated finish**, not solid brass. That's fine at this price point, but don't expect them to patina or age like real brass — they'll stay looking exactly as they arrive.

## Build quality and real-world performance

The zinc alloy body has a nice heft — roughly 80 grams per handle, which feels solid when you grasp it. The finish is where Haliwu either wins or loses you. It's a **brushed satin gold with subtle warm undertones**, closer to what you'd see on high-end fixtures from Delta or Kohler than the garish yellow-gold hardware from 2005. It resists fingerprints well, which matters in a kitchen.

Mounting is straightforward: screw into 5mm holes (standard for cabinet hardware). The included screws are fine, but they're not hardened steel — one reviewer noted, *"The screw heads stripped on two of the ten when I used a power drill. Hand-tighten them."* The hex wrench included is actually the better tool for the socket-head option on the back of the handle. Use that.

## Where it falls short

Be honest with yourself about the color. Multiple reviewers posted photos showing the finish reads **more champagne/antique gold than bright polished gold**. If your cabinets have cool gray or blue tones, this warm gold will clash. If you have cream, white, or warm wood, it'll harmonize.

The screws are the weakest link. They're slightly soft and the Phillips heads cam out if you use a drill at high torque. Solution: hand-tighten with the hex wrench, or buy #8-32 machine screws from a hardware store if you're replacing these on metal cabinets.

Also note: the finish is **not scratch-proof**. Keys, rings, or metal utensils dragged across the surface will leave marks. It's not fragile — just don't treat it like a $15 solid brass pull.

## Who should buy this

- **Rental property owners** upgrading kitchens on a budget — these look premium enough to justify higher rent

**Skip these if** you need a bright, mirror-polished gold, if your cabinets have exotic wood tones that demand a deeper antique brass, or if you're installing in a high-traffic commercial setting where the finish will see heavy abuse.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these fit standard IKEA cabinet doors?**
A: IKEA uses 3-inch (76mm) spacing on most of their cabinet fronts, so yes — these will fit without modification. Just be aware IKEA's thinner particleboard may need shorter screws, which aren't included.

**Q: Will the finish tarnish or fade over time?**
A: The plating is a PVD-style coating that resists tarnish well. It won't patina like real brass, but it also won't develop green corrosion. Expect the finish to remain stable for years with normal indoor use.

**Q: Can I use these on drawers and cabinets together?**
A: Yes. The 3-inch spacing works on both drawer fronts and cabinet doors. On wider drawers you might want a longer handle for proportion, but for standard 18-24 inch drawers, these look proportionate.
