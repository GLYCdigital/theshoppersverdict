Let me be direct: most wall-mounted hose reels are plastic junk that cracks by year two. The Liberty Garden Products 708 is not that. It's a welded steel frame with a powder-coated finish that actually looks like a decorative fixture rather than a utility afterthought. At 4.0 stars across 200 ratings, it's not perfect — but it's one of the few reels in this category that will outlast your current hose.

**What you're actually getting**

Out of the box, you get the fully assembled steel reel frame, a mounting bracket set, hardware, and a lead-in hose connector. The unit measures about 18 inches square and sticks out roughly 6 inches from the wall. The "carriage" styling — curved steel arms and a classic spoked wheel design — is genuinely attractive. This is the reel you mount on the side of a garage or shed when you don't want to look at a bright orange plastic drum.

The steel is thick — not stamped sheet metal, but real formed steel with a textured powder coat. It's rated for 125 feet of 5/8-inch hose, which covers most residential needs. The geared crank mechanism is the standout feature: it winds smoothly and evenly, preventing the hose from bunching on one side, which is the primary failure mode of cheaper reels.

**Where it shines and where it stumbles**

Buyers consistently praise the build quality. One reviewer noted, *"This thing is built like a tank. My last plastic reel lasted one season; this has been up for three years and still looks new."* Another highlighted the aesthetics: *"Finally, a hose reel my wife doesn't hate looking at."* The rust resistance is real — the powder coat holds up well even in coastal or high-humidity environments.

But here's the honest tradeoff. The pre-attached hose connector uses a non-standard thread pattern. Multiple reviewers reported needing to buy a 3/4-inch to 3/4-inch adapter (about $6 at any hardware store) to connect their existing hoses. That's a manufacturing oversight, not a dealbreaker, but it's annoying to discover at 8 AM when you're trying to water the tomatoes.

The included mounting hardware is also underwhelming — small lag bolts that are fine for wood siding but will pull out of masonry or stucco. Plan to pick up proper masonry anchors if you're not mounting into wood studs. The crank handle is functional but compact; with heavy work gloves, you'll find yourself fumbling a bit.

**Who should buy this**

- **The aesthetics-conscious homeowner:** If you care about curb appeal, this is the best-looking steel reel under $100. Period.
- **The "buy once" shopper:** This is a 10+ year product if you tighten bolts annually and drain it before hard freezes.
- **Anyone with a 100-foot+ hose:** The 125-foot capacity and smooth crank make heavy use tolerable.

**Skip it if** you're on a tight budget (there are functional plastic reels for $30 less), you need a mobile/portable solution, or you can't be bothered to buy a $6 adapter for the hose threads.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this reel fit on a standard 4x4 wooden post?**
A: The mounting bracket pattern is 16 inches on center, designed for wall studs or flat surfaces. For a post, you'll need to fabricate a mounting plate or use a post-mount adapter — it's not designed for direct post installation.

**Q: Can I use this with a soaker or drip hose?**
A: Yes, as long as you use the thread adapter mentioned above. The reel handles lighter-weight soaker hoses fine, but avoid wrapping heavy commercial-grade hoses that exceed the 125-foot rating.

**Q: Does the hose stay attached when the reel is mounted high on a wall?**
A: Yes, the lead-in connector swivels, so the hose remains connected to your spigot while the reel is mounted at any height. Just make sure the lead-in hose is long enough to reach your water source.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a steel reel that looks good, lasts a decade, and handles a full-size hose without kinking. **Skip it if** you're a renter who needs portability, or you're unwilling to deal with the thread adapter hassle.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Points deducted for the thread issue and weak mounting hardware, but the core product is excellent. For a permanent wall-mounted solution, this is the right call.
