Let's cut through the farmhouse-chic marketing for a second. The PORTRES ceramic table lamp is a solid, no-nonsense piece of lighting that earns its 4.4-star rating through honest construction rather than flashy features. Here's what you need to know before you add it to your cart.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the lamp base (one solid piece of glazed ceramic), a fabric shade, and the harp/finial assembly. You'll need to screw the harp into the base and seat the shade yourself—takes about two minutes and requires no tools. The base is what you're paying for here. It's heavy, roughly 6-7 pounds, with a reactive glaze finish that creates subtle color variations across the surface. As one buyer put it, "The ceramic looks way more expensive than the price tag suggests." That's accurate. The distressed, matte finish fits squarely into that modern-farmhouse aesthetic without looking like a cheap replica from a big-box store.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The base is genuinely sturdy. The weight distribution is excellent—I'd trust this lamp on a nightstand next to a sleeping toddler's reach or in a high-traffic hallway. The rotary switch on the socket is a three-way (off/low/high), which is a thoughtful touch for bedside use. You can dial in a dim reading glow or crank it for full-room illumination.

Here's the catch on light quality: the included shade is a basic drum style in a light linen fabric. It's thin. With a standard A19 bulb, you'll see the bulb's silhouette glowing through the shade—a look some people genuinely dislike. The fix is simple (use a frosted or LED filament bulb), but it's worth knowing before you buy. One reviewer noted, "I had to swap the bulb to get the soft glow I wanted. Not a dealbreaker, but a minor annoyance."

The lamp stands roughly 22 inches tall with the shade, which is standard for a bedside table lamp. But that's also a limitation—there's no smaller version if your end table is compact. Measure first.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The feedback across those 220 ratings clusters around three things: the ceramic quality, the visual weight/style, and the value. A verified purchase review said, "This is the third farmhouse lamp I've tried and the first one that doesn't look cheap. The glaze is gorgeous and it feels like it'll last." Another praised the stability: "My cat knocks things over constantly. This one has survived three weeks. That's a record."

**Where It Falls Short**

The thin shade is the main aesthetic gripe, and I'll add another: there's no USB port or integrated outlet on the base. For a lamp squarely aimed at bedside use in 2026, that feels like a miss. You'll need to keep your charger separate. Also, the ceramic is durable but not indestructible—a hard drop onto tile will chip the glaze. Treat it like the decorative piece it is.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The farmhouse decorator:** The reactive glaze and neutral tones blend seamlessly with barnwood, wrought iron, and neutral palettes.
- **The bedside reader:** The three-way switch and warm light potential make it a functional nightstand lamp.
- **The gift-giver:** It arrives well-packaged and looks more expensive than it costs—a solid housewarming gift.

**Skip it if** you need a small-scale lamp for a narrow shelf, if you require integrated charging, or if you want a shade that completely diffuses the bulb.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is the lamp heavy enough to be stable on a nightstand?**
Yes. The ceramic base is substantial—around 6-7 pounds—and won't tip with normal bumping.

**Q: What bulb type does it take?**
Standard E26 medium base. A frosted LED or filament bulb works best to avoid the silhouette showing through the shade.

**Q: Is the shade replaceable?**
Yes, it's a standard harp-and-finial setup. You can swap in any drum shade with a 14-16 inch fitter if you want a different look.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a well-made, genuinely attractive ceramic lamp that anchors a bedside table or console without breaking the bank. The construction quality exceeds the price point.

**Skip it if:** You need built-in charging, a smaller footprint, or a shade that fully diffuses light. Those are fixable issues, but they're real tradeoffs.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid, honest lighting with a few minor compromises. The PORTRES delivers where it matters: looks and build quality.
