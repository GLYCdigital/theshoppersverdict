**The Roymelo Preassembled Clear Glass French Interior Door: A Rare Find That Actually Saves You a Weekend**

Most “preassembled” doors in this price range are a polite fiction—you still need a degree in cabinetmaking to get them square. The Roymelo clear glass French interior door is the exception. It arrives with the slab, frame, and barn door hardware all in one unit. You unpack it, bolt the track to the header, and hang the door. That’s it. If you can operate a stud finder and a drill, you can install this in under two hours.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the fully assembled door unit—two clear tempered glass panels set in a white hollow-core frame—plus the complete barn door hardware kit: a 6-foot steel track, two roller hangers, floor guides, and all mounting bolts. As shown in the product image, the door is 30 inches wide and 80 inches tall. The frame is 1.4 inches thick, which aligns with standard interior door jambs. The glass is genuine tempered safety glass, not acrylic—buyers confirm it handles daily abuse without crazing or yellowing.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The selling point here is the pre-hung design. Traditional French doors require you to shim the frame, mortise hinges, and pray the slab swings true. This unit skips all of that. The rollers use sealed ball bearings, and the track is rated for up to 150 pounds, so even with the weight of the glass, the door glides smoothly without the jerky motion you get from cheap hardware. One reviewer noted, *“I was skeptical about the track holding up, but it’s rock solid. Six months in, no sag, no noise.”*

The glass is genuinely clear, not the green-tinted stuff you see on budget doors. Light transmission is excellent—you can brighten a hallway or split a home office while keeping sightlines open. The white frame is primed and ready to paint if you want to match trim, though the factory finish is reasonably durable.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let’s be direct about the limitations. First, it’s only available in 30-inch width. If your opening is standard 32 or 36 inches, this door won’t fit without modifying the framing—which defeats the “preassembled” convenience. Second, the hollow-core construction means this is a light-diffuser, not a sound barrier. A reviewer put it plainly: *“You can hear everything. It’s fine for a closet, but don’t expect privacy.”* Third, the glass is a smudge magnet. Lower panels will need frequent wiping, especially in high-traffic areas or homes with small children.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Homeowners converting a den or office into a glass-wall partition** – the light transmission and barn door style are perfect for open-plan layouts.
- **DIYers with basic tool skills** – the preassembled design eliminates the most frustrating parts of door installation.
- **Anyone with a 30-inch opening who wants a stylish, functional door without hiring a carpenter.**

Skip it if you need soundproofing, have a non-standard opening, or want a solid-core door for a bedroom or bathroom.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the door come with the frame, or just the slab?**  
A: It comes fully assembled—slab, frame, and barn hardware. There’s no separate jamb to build.

**Q: Can this be installed as a swinging door instead of a barn door?**  
A: No. The hardware is exclusively for a sliding barn door installation. If you want a hinged swing, look elsewhere.

**Q: How heavy is the door, and can one person install it?**  
A: It’s roughly 70–80 pounds. One person can manage with a helper for lifting the track into place, but a second set of hands is recommended for aligning the rollers.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a stylish, functional glass partition that installs in an afternoon and dramatically improves natural light flow. It’s an excellent value for the price.

**Skip it if** you need sound dampening, have a non-30-inch opening, or want a solid wood door. This is a hollow-core product, and that’s a tradeoff you need to accept.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — a genuinely useful product with a narrow fit window and predictable acoustic limitations. For the right opening, it’s a winner.
