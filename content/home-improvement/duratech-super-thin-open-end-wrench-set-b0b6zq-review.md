If you've ever contorted your arm behind a dishwasher supply line or fought to reach a bolt tucked between engine components, you know the frustration: the wrench fits, but the handle hits something solid before you can turn it. The DURATECH Super-Thin Open End Wrench Set attacks exactly that problem. These wrenches are dramatically slimmer than standard fare — think half the thickness — which means they slide into gaps that would stop a typical Craftsman or Husky cold. For anyone who works on tight spaces, this set earns its place in the drawer before your standard wrenches do.

**What You Actually Get**

The set ships with the essential metric sizes: 8, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15, 17, and 19mm. That's eight wrenches covering the most common fasteners on modern cars, bicycles, appliances, and furniture assembly. Each wrench is forged from chrome vanadium steel and finished with a mirror polish that looks almost decorative — until you get grease on them and realize how easy they are to wipe down. The open ends are angled at 15 degrees, a small but critical detail that lets you flip the wrench to increase your swing arc without repositioning your hand.

**Real-World Performance**

Here's the honest tradeoff with super-thin wrenches: they're not torque monsters. The slim profile that lets you reach into tight spots also means less material to resist flexing. For snugging up bolts, adjusting fittings, and general assembly work, they perform beautifully. But if you're trying to break loose a corroded exhaust bolt or torque down suspension components, these aren't the tools for the job — you'll feel the wrench twist under heavy force, and you risk rounding the fastener.

The 15-degree offset is the standout feature in practice. In a cramped engine bay, that offset often makes the difference between a usable swing and a dead end. Multiple reviewers echo this sentiment, with one noting, "These fit where my standard wrenches couldn't even get close. Used them on a water heater install and saved me an hour of frustration." Another buyer highlighted the build quality: "The finish is excellent and they feel solid despite being so thin. No sharp edges, comfortable to grip."

**Where It Falls Short**

Beyond the torque limitations, there's no box-end side on these wrenches. A standard combination wrench gives you a closed end for final tightening and stubborn bolts; this set is open-end only. That means you lose the security of a full wrap-around grip when you need maximum bite on a fastener. Also, the 19mm is the largest size included — if you work on larger equipment, you'll need to supplement this set with bigger wrenches.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **DIY home repair folks** tackling appliance installs, furniture assembly, and basic plumbing  
- **Bicycle mechanics** who constantly work in confined frame clearances  
- **Auto enthusiasts** doing engine bay work where clearance is measured in millimeters  
- **Anyone who's ever cursed at an inaccessible bolt** and wants a purpose-built solution

Skip it if your primary work involves heavy torque, rusted fasteners, or large hardware above 19mm.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Will these wrenches break under normal use?**  
No, for standard tightening and adjustment work they're plenty strong. The chrome vanadium steel is heat-treated and holds up well. Just don't use them for breaking loose seized bolts — that's what a breaker bar and six-point socket are for.

**Q: Are these metric or SAE?**  
This particular set is metric only (8-19mm). If you work on older American vehicles or standard hardware, you'll need to look for the SAE version separately.

**Q: How thin is "super-thin" exactly?**  
Approximately 5mm thick at the head — roughly half the thickness of a standard wrench. That's what allows them to slip into tight gaps where regular wrenches bind.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you regularly battle tight spaces and want a dedicated set that fits where standard wrenches won't. The DURATECH Super-Thin set delivers exactly what it promises: slim, well-finished wrenches that solve a specific, frustrating problem.

**Skip it if** you need a do-everything wrench set for heavy-duty work — these are specialized tools, not a replacement for your standard set.
