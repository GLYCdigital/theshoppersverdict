Let me be blunt: this is a hasp, not a lock. If you buy it expecting the Master Lock logo to mean it secures itself, you'll be disappointed. But if you pair it with a solid padlock, this is one of the most thoughtfully designed hasps I've tested in this price range.  

## What You Actually Get  

The box contains the hasp assembly (staple plate and hinge plate), four Phillips mounting screws, and a wrench for tightening the included nuts. The body is die-cast zinc alloy with a polished chrome finish—it looks more expensive than it is. The staple plate accepts shackles up to 3/8-inch diameter, which covers the vast majority of standard padlocks.  

## Build Quality and Real-World Use  

The standout feature here is the **hidden screw design**. When the hasp is closed and locked, the mounting screws are completely covered by the hinge plate. A would-be thief can't simply unscrew the hasp from your shed door—they'd have to cut through the steel or pry the whole assembly off. That's a genuine security upgrade over the cheap stamped-steel hasps you'll find for half the price.  

The chrome finish isn't just cosmetic. It's a proper electroplated layer that holds up to outdoor exposure. One reviewer noted, "I've had mine on a gate for two winters and there's zero rust." That aligns with my experience—the zinc alloy base won't corrode like plain steel.  

Installation took me about 10 minutes with a drill and a 3/16-inch bit for pilot holes. The included screws are Phillips, which is my main gripe—they strip easily if you overtighten with a power driver. Swap them for Torx or hex-head screws and you'll have a tamper-resistant setup that's actually hard to defeat.

## Where It Falls Short  

Honest tradeoffs:  

1. **No padlock included** — This is a hasp only. Budget for a separate padlock, which adds $10–$20 to your total cost.  
2. **Phillips screws** — They work, but they're the weak point. Upgrade to security fasteners if you're serious about deterring theft.  
3. **Not heavy-duty** — This is a medium-duty hasp. For industrial or high-security applications (e.g., commercial storage), look at a heavier gauge product.
