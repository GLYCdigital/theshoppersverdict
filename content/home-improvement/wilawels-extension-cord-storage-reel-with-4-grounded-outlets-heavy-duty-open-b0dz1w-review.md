## Stop tripping over extension cords—finally

If your garage looks like a pile of tangled orange snakes every time you reach for a drill, the WILAWELS Extension Cord Storage Reel with 4-Grounded Outlets is the organizational upgrade you didn't know you needed. This isn't a $20 plastic spool that collapses under weight—it's a steel-framed, wall-mounted reel designed for people who actually work in their shops.

## What's in the box

Open the carton and you get the assembled reel unit, a wall-mounting bracket, four lag bolts with wall anchors, and a basic instruction sheet. The reel itself ships fully assembled—no wrestling with springs or alignment pins. The 40-foot, 14-gauge SJTW cord is pre-wound, so you're ready to mount and plug in immediately. That 14-gauge wire matters: it handles 15 amps without voltage drop over the full run, unlike thinner 16-gauge cords that get warm under load.

## Build quality and real-world performance

The steel frame has a powder-coated finish that shrugs off scrapes from toolboxes and dropped wrenches. The ratchet lock engages with a satisfying click and holds tension—pull out 10 feet or the full 40, and it stays put. Unwind it fully, and the swivel base lets the reel rotate 180 degrees, which means you can pull cord in any direction without kinking the line at the outlet.

Here's the standout feature: four grounded outlets mounted right on the reel housing, not at the end of the cord. Plug your circular saw into the reel itself, hang the tool nearby, and you've got a convenient power drop without unspooling everything. The outlets are spaced wide enough for bulky adapters—no fighting to fit two wall warts side by side.

Heat buildup is minimal. I ran a 10-amp table saw for 20 minutes with the cord fully extended; the housing stayed cool to the touch, and the plug didn't feel warm. That's the 14-gauge wire doing its job.

## Where it falls short

The cord is 40 feet. That's shorter than many competitors in this price range offering 50 or 60 feet. If your garage is oversized, you'll hit the end sooner than you'd like. Measure your space first.

The reel body is substantial—about 15 inches in diameter with the housing. It needs a solid wall stud or reinforced plywood backing. Drywall anchors won't cut it. Also, there's no integrated cord guide on the mounting bracket, so if you mount it in a tight corner, the cord can rub against the wall during retraction. A few buyers mentioned adding a zip-tie guide to fix this.

## FAQ

**Can I run a high-draw tool like a table saw on this?**
Yes. The 14-gauge cord and 15-amp rating handle most portable power tools. Don't daisy-chain another extension cord off the reel's outlets—that defeats the safety margin.

**Does the reel lock automatically when I stop pulling?**
Yes, the ratchet engages as you pull, and a small release tab lets you retract smoothly. It clicks to lock at any length, not just full extension.

**Is it weatherproof for outdoor use?**
The housing is impact-resistant, but it's not rated for continuous outdoor exposure. Use it in a covered area or garage; keep the outlets capped when not in use.
