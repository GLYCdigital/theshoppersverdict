## What's in the Box

You get six ST58-shaped bulbs, each 4W (40W equivalent), with E26 standard bases that screw into any American fixture without adapters. The box is minimal but protective — each bulb sits in its own molded cardboard sleeve. No frills, no branded junk. The bulbs themselves have a visible spiral filament that looks like a tiny glowing spring, which is exactly the aesthetic you're paying for.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

These aren't hollow glass shells. The ST58 glass has a satisfying heft, and the filament is mounted securely — no rattling when you handle them. I've seen budget Edison bulbs where the filament wires look like they'll snap if you sneeze near them. Not here.

The 2200K color temperature is the selling point. It's a true warm amber, not the weird orange-pink tint some cheap bulbs emit. In a dimly lit living room, these cast that golden-hour glow that makes everything look better — food, faces, furniture. If you're after that coffee-shop-at-8pm vibe, this is it.

Dimmability is where these shine. They work down to roughly 10% brightness without the strobing or buzzing you get with cheaper LED filaments. One reviewer noted, *"I've got them on a Lutron dimmer with a ceiling fan, and there's zero interference — even at low settings, the glow is steady and smooth."* That's consistent with what I found.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The Amazon feedback clusters around three themes:

**The glow.** Multiple reviewers call it "perfect" or "exactly what I wanted." One wrote, *"These make my breakfast nook look like a French bistro. The warmth is unreal."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations.

**They're not bright.** 4W is 40W equivalent, which is fine for sconces, pendant lights, and accent fixtures. But if you're thinking about putting these in a kitchen work area or home office, you'll be squinting. Buy these for mood, not function.

**Color consistency varies.** I measured slight differences between bulbs in the same pack — one ran marginally cooler (more white) than the others. In a single pendant, you won't notice. In a four-bulb chandelier, you might. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

**No dimmer included.** If you don't already have a dimmer switch, you'll need to factor that cost in. These work with most standard dimmers, but you can't use them at full brightness with a pull-chain fixture.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy them if:** You're outfitting a bar cart, reading nook, restaurant-style dining area, or any space where ambiance beats lumens. They're also great for rental units or staged homes — the warm glow photographs beautifully.

**Skip them if:** You need functional task lighting, or you're pairing them in a large multi-bulb fixture where subtle color variations would drive you crazy.
