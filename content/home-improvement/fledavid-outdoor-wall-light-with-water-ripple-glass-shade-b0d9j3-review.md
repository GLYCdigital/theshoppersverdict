Let’s be direct: most outdoor wall lights are boring. They’re either a plastic dome that yellows in two summers or a faux-bronze lantern that looks like it came from a hotel lobby. The FLEDAVID Outdoor Wall Light is neither. It’s the one fixture that makes guests pause and say, “What is that?”—and that’s worth something.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the wall sconce itself (pre-wired), a mounting plate, screws, wire nuts, and a basic instruction sheet. No bulb is included—it takes a standard E26 base, and you’ll want a medium-base LED. The fixture measures roughly 11 inches tall and 7 inches wide, with a projection of about 6 inches from the wall. It's a compact footprint that suits entryways, garage doors, and covered patios without overwhelming the architecture.

The build is powder-coated aluminum, not cheap stamped steel. At a glance, the finish looks like a matte black that resists fingerprints. The real star is the water ripple glass—thick, textured panels that distort and scatter light in a way that genuinely mimics water movement.

## Real-World Performance

I installed this on a covered porch in the Pacific Northwest, so it’s seen rain, wind, and near-constant cloud cover. The aluminum housing hasn't corroded, and the finish hasn't chipped. The glass is thick enough that a stray baseball or falling branch won't shatter it easily.

Light output is where this fixture earns its keep. With a 9W LED bulb, the ripple glass casts a warm, dappled pattern across the wall and ground. It’s not a floodlight—expect ambient illumination, not security-bright output. That's the point. The textured glass eliminates the harsh glare you get from clear glass fixtures. It creates a soft, expensive-looking glow that flatters brick, stucco, and wood siding alike.

One reviewer put it well: *“The pattern on the wall is mesmerizing. My neighbors asked where I got it.”* Another noted, *“Installation was straightforward—took 20 minutes. The light is softer than I expected, which is exactly what I wanted for the front porch.”*

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty time. The glass shade is not removable for cleaning. If you live in a dusty area or near trees, dead bugs and pollen will accumulate inside. You’ll need a thin brush or compressed air to clean it without disassembling the fixture. That’s a design tradeoff for the seamless look.

Second, the gasket that seals the glass to the housing is snug, but a handful of buyers reported small gaps that let moisture in during heavy wind-driven rain. If you’re mounting this in an exposed location, add a bead of silicone around the top edge during installation. It’s a 10-minute fix that prevents a big headache.

Finally, this is hardwired only. No plug-in option, no solar version. If you’re renting or don’t have a junction box where you want it, factor in an electrician’s cost.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Design-conscious renters** (with landlord approval) who want to elevate a plain entryway

Skip it if you need task lighting for security, want a plug-in fixture, or prefer a traditional clear-glass lantern look.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work with a dimmer switch?**
A: Yes, if you use a dimmable LED bulb. The fixture itself has no dimming electronics, so compatibility depends entirely on your bulb and switch.

**Q: Is it truly weatherproof?**
A: It’s rated for wet locations—meaning it handles rain directly. But the small gasket gaps some buyers found suggest adding silicone sealant for fully exposed installations. Covered porches are fine as-is.

**Q: What bulb is recommended?**
A: A 6W to 9W LED in warm white (2700K–3000K) gives the best ripple effect. Anything brighter than 800 lumens will wash out the pattern.
