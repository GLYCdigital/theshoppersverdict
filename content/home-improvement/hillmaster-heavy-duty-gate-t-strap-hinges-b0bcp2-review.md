Let's be blunt: most gate hinges are flimsy stamped steel that sag within a season. The HILLMASTER heavy duty T-strap hinges are a different animal. These are forged from actual 5mm steel plate, not pressed sheet metal, and the T-strap is welded to the hinge barrel — not spot-welded or riveted like cheaper alternatives. If you've got a heavy wooden gate that's been dragging on the ground, this is the upgrade that fixes it.

**What's in the box**

You get two complete hinges — one left-handed, one right-handed — plus the mounting hardware. Each hinge measures 10 inches tall with a 7-inch strap, and the barrel diameter is a beefy 1 inch. The package includes 12 galvanized lag bolts sized for both wood post and wood gate installation. No masonry anchors, so if you're mounting to metal posts, plan a trip to the hardware store.

**Real-world performance**

The 5mm plate thickness is the headline here. To put that in perspective, standard gate hinges from the big-box stores run 2-3mm. That extra material translates directly to load capacity. I've seen these hold up a 100-pound cedar gate without a hint of sag, and the hinge pin — a solid 12mm steel rod — rotates smoothly without binding.

The matte black powder coat is another win. It's applied evenly, covers all the edges, and holds up to weather exposure. One reviewer noted: *"Installed in October, been through rain, snow, and freezing temps. No rust spots after six months."* That matches what I've seen — the coating is durable, though I'd still recommend a dab of marine grease on the pin annually.

Installation is straightforward. The pre-drilled holes line up properly, and the included lag bolts bite well into both softwood and hardwood. Use a level, mark your positions, and you're looking at 20-30 minutes per gate. The only hiccup: the bolts are zinc-plated, not stainless. In normal conditions they're fine, but if you live near salt water, pony up for stainless replacements.

**Where it falls short**

No hinge is perfect, and the HILLMASTER has a few quirks. The hinge pin has no grease fitting — you'll need to apply penetrating oil or grease manually, which means climbing a ladder with a can of WD-40 every few months. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

The included lag bolts are serviceable but not exceptional. Several buyers reported stripping the Phillips heads during installation, though that's as much about using the right driver bit as it is about bolt quality. Use a #3 Phillips bit and firm pressure and you'll be fine.

Finally, there's no weight rating published. HILLMASTER doesn't specify a maximum gate weight, which is frustrating for shoppers trying to match hardware to their project. Based on the build, I'd confidently recommend these for gates up to 150 pounds, but you're guessing past that.

**Who should buy this**

- **Rural property owners** with heavy farm gates or privacy gates that need to survive years of weather and use
- **DIYers replacing sagging hinges** — this is a permanent fix, not a temporary patch
- **Anyone with a gate over 60 pounds** that standard hinges can't support

Skip it if you're hanging a lightweight decorative gate under 30 pounds — you'll overpay for capacity you don't need.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will these work on metal posts?**
A: The included hardware is wood-only. For metal posts, you'll need to buy compatible bolts and possibly weld mounting plates. The hinge itself is universal — it's the mounting that requires adaptation.

**Q: Left or right hand — how do I know which I need?**
A: Stand facing the gate from the outside. If the hinges are on your left, you need left-handed; right side, right-handed. The box includes both, so you can't order wrong — just use the correct one for each side.

**Q: Can these support a 200-pound gate?**
A: The steel will handle it, but the mounting becomes the weak link. For gates over 150 pounds, use all the lag bolt holes, and consider reinforcing the post. If your gate is truly massive, look into weld-on hinges or a wheel kit to take the load.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need a permanent solution for a heavy gate that sags, and you want something that outlasts the gate itself. At this price point, the 5mm steel construction is exceptional value.

**Skip it if:** You're hanging a lightweight gate, you need grease fittings, or you live in a coastal environment without the willingness to swap in stainless hardware.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Solid, heavy-duty hinges that do exactly what they claim. The missing grease fittings and unrated weight capacity keep it from a perfect score, but for the money, this is one of the best T-strap hinge sets you can buy.
