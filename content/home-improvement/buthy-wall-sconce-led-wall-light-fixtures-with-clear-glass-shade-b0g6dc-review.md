## The Bottom Line Up Front

Most wall sconces under $50 feel like they belong in a hotel lobby circa 1995. The BUTHY Wall Sconce breaks that mold with a genuinely modern silhouette — a slim cylindrical body, clear glass shade, and a warm 3000K glow that doesn't scream "builder grade." It's the kind of fixture you'd expect to pay double for. But there's a catch, and it's a big one if you're a renter. Let's dig in.

## What's Actually in the Box

The package arrives with two sconces (yes, it's a pair — check the listing carefully), mounting brackets, wire nuts, screws, and a small instruction sheet. The glass shades come pre-attached, which is good because the retention screws are tiny and easy to lose. The backplate is 4.7 inches in diameter and the overall projection is just under 5 inches, so it won't stick out awkwardly in narrow hallways.

One thing I appreciate: the mounting bracket is a standard 4-inch octagonal pattern, which means it'll line up with most existing junction boxes. No drilling new holes unless you're mounting it on masonry. For drywall, you're looking at a 20-minute install per fixture if you're comfortable with basic wiring.

## Performance and Build Quality

The integrated LED produces 800 lumens at 3000K — a warm, neutral white that flatters wood tones and doesn't wash out artwork. The clear glass shade does something smart: it diffuses the light just enough to kill the harsh diode hotspot while keeping the fixture visually light and airy. You won't get the shadow-free wash of a frosted globe, but you also won't feel like you're staring into a flashlight.

Build quality is better than the price suggests. The aluminum body is powder-coated, not painted, so it resists chipping. The glass is actual glass, not acrylic — it's heavier and won't yellow over time. One buyer noted: *"I was skeptical about the clear glass creating glare, but it's surprisingly soft on the eyes. The install took me 15 minutes."* Another mentioned: *"These look like $100 fixtures. My electrician asked where I got them."*

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest tradeoff: the LED is non-replaceable. When the diode eventually dies (rated at 50,000 hours, so roughly 10-12 years of normal use), you're replacing the whole fixture, not just a bulb. That's common in modern LED fixtures, but it's worth knowing.

The bigger issue? **These are hardwired only.** There's no plug-in cord option, no battery backup. If you're renting or don't have a junction box where you want the light, this isn't the product for you. You'll need a licensed electrician if you're not comfortable with basic wiring. That's an extra $100-150 on top of the fixture price.

Also, the clear glass shows dust and fingerprints more readily than frosted options. You'll be wiping these down every couple of weeks if they're at eye level.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a homeowner updating a hallway, staircase, or reading nook. You want modern, understated lighting that doesn't scream for attention. You're okay with a one-time installation and don't mind that the LED is sealed.

**Skip it if:** You're renting, you don't have existing wiring at the install location, or you prefer the flexibility of swapping bulbs. Also skip if you need something brighter than 800 lumens — this is accent and task lighting, not a primary room light.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these be installed upside down?**
A: Yes, the fixture works in any orientation. The glass shade is symmetric and the light distribution doesn't change. Just make sure the wiring is properly capped regardless of angle.

**Q: Do I need a dimmer switch?**
A: Only if you want dimming. The fixture works fine on a standard switch, but it's not dimmable unless you pair it with a compatible LED dimmer. Check the listing for the specific dimmer compatibility list.

**Q: What color temperature is this?**
A: 3000K warm white — not the yellow-tinted 2700K of old incandescents, not the sterile 4000K of offices. It's the sweet spot for living spaces.

The sealed LED and hardwire-only design are real limitations, but for homeowners who want a clean, modern fixture that'll run for a decade without maintenance, this is a smart buy. It's not perfect, but at this price point, it's tough to beat.
