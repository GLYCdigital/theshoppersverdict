Let's cut through the marketing. If you're here, you're either tired of paying $20 per wheel for stick-on weights that fall off, or you run a shop that balances a lot of tires. The magnum+ 17.6 lb bulk tub is aimed squarely at the second crowd — but it might still make sense for the first, if you think ahead.

## What you actually get

The 17.6 lb (8 kg) pail is exactly what it says: a heavy-duty plastic tub filled with ceramic micro-beads. It ships with a measuring cup and a laminated application chart that tells you exactly how many ounces to pour into each tire based on size — from a 155/80R13 up to a 315/75R16. The chart alone is worth a nod; it's printed clearly and covers passenger cars, light trucks, and Class A motorhomes.

As shown in the product image, the tub has a wide mouth, which is good for scooping but bad for controlled pouring. You'll want a funnel with a flexible hose to get the beads into the valve stem without making a mess. Plan for that.

## Real-world performance

The beads work on a simple principle: they move freely inside the tire and dynamically counterbalance the heavy spot as you drive. In practice, this means you get a smoother ride at highway speeds, and the balance stays consistent as the tire wears — something traditional lead weights can't do.

Customer feedback lines up with the physics. One verified buyer noted, *"Put these in my F-250 after two shops couldn't get the 35s balanced properly. Vibrations gone at 75 mph — three months and 8,000 miles later, still smooth."* Another reviewer used them on a travel trailer: *"No more death wobble at 65. Took 20 minutes per tire and I'll never go back to weights."*

The TPMS compatibility is legit. The ceramic beads are non-conductive and won't foul sensors. The 17.6 lb quantity handles a lot of tires — roughly 40 to 60 passenger car tires at the typical 2–4 oz per tire, or about 12–16 large truck tires at 6–8 oz each.

## Where it falls short

The biggest issue isn't the product — it's the packaging. The pail has no resealable liner. Once you crack the lid, moisture can get in, and these beads will clump if they sit in a humid garage. Buy a desiccant pack or transfer what you don't use to a sealed container.

Second, this is overkill for a single vehicle. If you own one sedan, you'll use maybe 8 ounces total. That leaves 17 pounds of beads sitting around, and they don't last forever once exposed to air. The per-ounce price is excellent, but only if you actually use the volume.

Finally, installation requires a valve core removal tool and a bead injector or a creative funnel setup. This isn't a five-minute job for beginners. Budget 30–45 minutes per tire the first time.

## Who should buy this

## FAQ

**Q: Will these beads damage my TPMS sensors?**
A: No. The ceramic beads are non-conductive and soft enough that they don't damage sensors. Multiple reviewers with TPMS-equipped vehicles reported zero issues. Just ensure you install them with the valve stem at the bottom so the beads enter the tire, not the sensor.

**Q: How many tires will this tub balance?**
A: Depends on size. A typical passenger tire needs 2–4 oz, so this tub handles 40–60 tires. A 35-inch truck tire needs 6–8 oz, so roughly 12–16 tires. The included chart is accurate — trust it.

**Q: Do the beads settle or clump inside the tire over time?**
A: No. Once inside a spinning tire, the beads stay loose and mobile. Clumping only happens in the storage tub if moisture gets in — keep the pail sealed with a desiccant pack.
