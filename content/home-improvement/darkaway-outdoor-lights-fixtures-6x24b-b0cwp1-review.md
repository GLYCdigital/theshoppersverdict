Some outdoor lights look great in the box and die within a season. The Darkaway 6X24B is not one of them. With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, this fixture has clearly earned its reputation among homeowners who actually installed it. I dug through the specs, the customer feedback, and the build quality to give you the straight story.

**What You Actually Get**

The box includes two complete light fixtures, each with a die-cast aluminum housing, a frosted glass lens, and a pre-installed integrated LED board. You also get the mounting plate, wire nuts, and screws. The finish is a matte black that resists fingerprints and doesn't glare in direct sunlight. The units measure roughly 11 inches wide by 7 inches tall — substantial enough to anchor a front entryway without overwhelming a standard door frame.

Each fixture delivers 1600 lumens at 5000K, which drops to 1400 at the warmer 3000K setting. The integrated LEDs are rated for 50,000 hours, so you won't be swapping bulbs. If an LED fails before then, that's a fixture replacement — not a bulb change. Worth knowing before you buy.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The housing is genuinely weatherproof, not just "weather-resistant." The gaskets sit flush, and the glass is sealed properly — no fogging, no moisture ingress. One buyer noted, *"Been through two Nor'easters and a hurricane. Bone dry inside, no corrosion on the screws."* That matches the aluminum construction, which handles coastal environments far better than stamped steel.

The three-color temperature switch is a practical touch. It lives behind the mounting plate, so you set it once during installation. Most buyers settle on 4000K — bright enough for security without the clinical blue of 5000K. As one reviewer put it, *"I tried all three. 4000K is the sweet spot for my driveway."*

Light distribution is even, with no harsh hotspots or dark edges. The frosted lens diffuses well, and the 120-degree beam angle covers a typical two-car garage front without leaving corners in shadow. At night, the output is genuinely useful — not just decorative.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The installation process gets frequent shoutouts. The mounting plate uses standard 4-inch spacing, and the wire nuts are generously sized. Multiple reviewers mention finishing in under 20 minutes with basic tools. One wrote, *"Easiest outdoor light swap I've done. My old fixture took an hour to fight with. This one clicked right in."*

Durability is the other recurring theme. Buyers in Florida, Texas, and the Pacific Northwest all report no rust, fading, or seal failures after a year or more. That's the kind of track record that separates a $40 fixture from a $100 one that behaves the same.

**Where It Falls Short**

The motion sensor — if you opt for the sensor version — has a rated range of 16-20 feet. That's conservative, and some buyers found it shorter than expected when mounted high on a wall. If you need detection at 30 feet, this isn't the fixture for you.

The included screws are the other weak point. They're adequate for wood siding, but if you're mounting into brick or stucco, replace them with stainless steel anchors. A few reviewers stripped them during installation. Minor, but annoying.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Homeowners replacing builder-grade porch lights** — immediate upgrade in both output and aesthetics.
- **Anyone in humid or coastal climates** — the aluminum housing is worth the premium.
- **DIYers who want a 20-minute install** — no electrician required if you're comfortable with basic wiring.

**Skip It If**

- You need wide-area motion detection beyond 20 feet.
- You prefer a warm 2700K glow — this fixture starts at 3000K.
- You want replaceable bulbs — this is integrated LED only.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do these work with a dimmer switch?**
A: No. They're designed for standard on/off switching. Using a dimmer can cause flickering and damage the LED driver.

**Q: How bright are they really?**
A: 1600 lumens per fixture at 5000K. That's equivalent to about a 100-watt incandescent. Plenty for a front door or garage.

**Q: Can I install them upside down?**
A: No. The gasket orientation is designed for downward or horizontal mounting. Installing upside down voids the weatherproofing.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a durable, well-lit, good-looking outdoor fixture that installs in minutes and lasts years. **Skip it if** you need long-range motion detection or insist on replaceable bulbs.

At 4.6/5, this is one of the most reliable outdoor light fixtures in its price range. The build quality justifies the cost, and the 235 reviews back it up. For most homeowners, this is the last porch light you'll buy for a decade.
