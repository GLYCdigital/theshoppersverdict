**The Delta T5797-RBWL isn't a faucet you buy on a whim.** It's a statement piece that demands you commit to the plumbing before you even unbox it. If you're remodeling a bathroom from studs out, this wall-mount filler rewards that effort with a clean, spa-like aesthetic and genuinely smart engineering. If you're hoping to swap out an existing deck-mounted faucet, stop reading now — this isn't that product.

**What's actually in the box:** The wall-mount tub filler body, dual-function spout with integrated diverter, hand shower with 60-inch hose, two cross handles, and mounting hardware. What's *not* included is the rough-in valve (Delta R22000 or equivalent) — that's a separate purchase, and it's non-negotiable. You'll also need to confirm your studs are spaced for the 6-inch center-to-center connections before you cut into your tile. The Venetian Bronze finish looks richer in person than in photos — it's a deep, warm brown with subtle copper undertones that shift in natural light.

**Performance is where Delta's engineering shows.** The Diamond Seal Technology isn't marketing fluff — it's a ceramic disc cartridge that eliminates the rubber washers that degrade and cause drips in cheaper faucets. The integrated diverter is the standout feature: a simple pull on the spout's knurled ring switches flow from the tub spout to the hand shower. No separate valve, no reaching behind the spout. It's intuitive enough that guests figure it out without instructions.

Water flow is strong at the standard 2.0 GPM, and the spout extends 7 inches from the wall — enough clearance for most freestanding tubs, though you'll want to measure your tub's deck depth carefully. The hand shower's spray pattern is wide but not aggressive; it's better suited for rinsing the tub and washing kids than for a massage shower.

**What buyers consistently praise:** "The finish is flawless and matches my other Delta fixtures perfectly," one reviewer notes. Another highlights the install quality: "Took my plumber two hours total. The rough-in was straightforward and the trim mounted flush with zero gaps." Multiple reviewers mention the diverter mechanism feeling solid — no cheap plastic click, just a firm, satisfying detent.

**Where it falls short:** The 60-inch hose is the most common complaint. On a standard 60-inch tub, you're fine. On deeper soaking tubs or for taller users, you'll be stretching. The cross handles, while visually elegant, have a stiff action — one reviewer describes it as "requiring two hands when your hands are wet and soapy." And that rough-in valve requirement? It's a dealbreaker for anyone not doing a full remodel. Several one-star reviews trace back to buyers who didn't realize the valve was separate.

**Who should buy this:** The homeowner doing a primary bathroom renovation with wall access from behind. The design-conscious buyer who wants a freestanding tub without the visual clutter of a floor-mounted faucet. The person who's already invested in Delta's Venetian Bronze line for matching fixtures.

**Skip it if:** You're retrofitting an existing tub, you have non-standard stud spacing, or you're on a tight timeline — sourcing the rough-in valve and scheduling a plumber adds a week minimum to any project.

**FAQ:**

**Q: Can I install this myself?**  
A: Only if you're comfortable with soldering copper or using PEX adapters behind a finished wall. The rough-in valve must be installed before tile or backer board goes up. Budget for a licensed plumber unless you've done wall-mount faucet installs before.

**Q: Does the hand shower have a pause button?**  
A: No. The spray head has a slide switch for full spray or a narrower rinse stream, but no water-saving pause feature. The diverter is your on/off.

**Q: Will this fit a freestanding tub?**  
A: Yes, if the tub is placed within the spout's 7-inch reach and you've positioned the rough-in valve at the correct height (typically 52-56 inches from the floor to the spout outlet). Measure twice, cut once.

**The Verdict:**  
**Buy it if** you're doing a full remodel, value leak-proof engineering, and want a high-end look without paying high-end boutique prices.  
**Skip it if** you're not prepared for the rough-in valve dance or you need a longer hand shower hose.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Deducting half a star for the hose length and the separate valve requirement. The hardware itself is excellent; the project around it is the real commitment.
