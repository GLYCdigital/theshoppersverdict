Let’s be honest: a towel bar is not a glamorous purchase. But the Delta 79735 Cassidy Double Towel is the rare home improvement item that earns its 4.8-star rating through pure execution. If you’re remodeling a bathroom or just tired of towels piling on the floor, this is the bar to beat in its category.

## What You Actually Get

Open the box and you’ll find the assembled double bar, two wall flanges, the mounting template, and a hardware pack with screws and anchors. The bar measures 24 inches across with two parallel rods spaced about 6 inches apart. As shown in the product image, the design is clean and minimal — no ornate details, just a straight-forward brushed look that leans modern-traditional.

The construction is where Delta separates itself from the $20 Amazon specials. This is zinc die-cast, not hollow tube steel. Pick it up and you’ll immediately feel the heft difference. The chrome finish is applied properly — it resists water spotting and fingerprints, which matters more than you think in a daily-use bathroom.

## Performance and Installation

The double-bar configuration is the entire point here. Two rods mean two towels hung flat and separate, so both dry faster without the damp-on-damp sandwich you get from folding towels over a single bar. For a family bathroom shared by two people, this solves a real problem.

Installation is straightforward if you follow the included template. Mark your holes, drill, insert the anchors (provided), and mount the flanges. The bars slide into place with a set screw on each side. Most buyers report 15-20 minutes total. One word of caution: use a manual screwdriver for the final tightening. The included screws are soft zinc, and several Amazon reviewers report stripping them with a power drill. It’s a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker — but worth knowing before you grab the impact driver.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

Scrolling through the 240 ratings, a few themes dominate. One verified buyer put it simply: "Installed in 10 minutes, looks like it cost three times more than it did." Another praised the drying improvement: "Two towels hang without touching each other — they actually dry now instead of staying damp."

The most common praise centers on the finish holding up over time. Multiple long-term reviewers note the chrome hasn't spotted or tarnished after a year of daily use, which is the complaint that plagues budget bars.

## Where It Falls Short

The fixed 6-inch spacing between bars is my main gripe. Standard bath towels fit fine, but if you use thick, plush Turkish towels or oversized bath sheets, they’ll drag on the wall or overlap at the edges. There’s no adjustability here — what you see is what you get.

Also, the mounting screws are underwhelming. They work fine for drywall with the included anchors, but if you’re mounting into studs, you’ll want to swap in your own screws for a more secure hold. It’s not a structural issue — the bar itself is rock solid once mounted — just a hardware quality nitpick.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The remodeler** building out a guest bath who wants a premium look without paying Kohler or Brizo prices

## FAQ

**Q: Will this hold two heavy bath towels?**
A: Yes. With the included anchors in drywall, it holds two standard cotton bath towels without sagging. If you’re mounting into tile, use a tile bit and the included anchors — just go slow.

**Q: Does the finish easily show water spots?**
A: No. The chrome is the standout feature. It’s a spot-resistant coating that wipes clean easily. This is the main reason buyers pick it over cheaper alternatives.

**Q: Can I install this on tile?**
A: Yes, but you’ll need a carbide-tipped tile bit and a hammer drill. The included anchors work in tile if you drill clean holes. Take your time — cracking tile is the only real risk with this install.
