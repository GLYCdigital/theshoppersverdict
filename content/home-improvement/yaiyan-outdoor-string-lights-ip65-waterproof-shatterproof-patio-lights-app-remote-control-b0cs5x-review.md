# YAIYAN Outdoor String Lights Review: Are These IP65 Waterproof Lights Worth It?

Most string lights under $50 are a gamble. You either get something that looks great but dies in the first storm, or something rugged that throws ugly, harsh light. The YAIYAN Outdoor String Lights try to hit both marks — smart features and true weather resistance — at a price that won't make you wince. After digging through 200 ratings and the specs, here's the honest picture.

## What You Actually Get

The box includes 48 feet of string lights (16 bulbs spaced 3 feet apart, plus a 10-foot lead cord), a remote control, and the controller hub. The bulbs are S14-style, which means they're the classic round patio light shape, not the elongated cafe bulbs. They're made of shatterproof plastic with a matte finish, and each one screws into the socket with a rubber gasket for a snug seal. The line is daisy-chainable, so you can connect up to three sets if one run isn't enough.

## How It Performs in Real Use

**The IP65 rating matters here.** That's not marketing fluff — it means the housing is fully dust-tight and protected against low-pressure water jets from any direction. Rain, sprinklers, snowmelt: this thing shrugs it off. The plastic bulbs are genuinely impact-resistant too. If a branch falls on them or you knock one with a ladder, you're not sweeping up glass. One buyer noted, "Left them out all winter in Minnesota, including a blizzard. Not a single bulb failed." That tracks with the build quality.

**Light output is solid but not blinding.** Each bulb puts out a warm, even glow — roughly equivalent to a 7-watt LED. It's enough to light a dining table or conversation area, but don't expect floodlight brightness. The dimmer helps: you can drop it to a soft 10% for mood lighting or crank it for dinner parties.

**The smart controls are the standout feature.** The app gives you 10 solid colors, three white temperature presets, and 16 dynamic modes like fade, strobe, and chase. You can set timers for sunrise/sunset and dim on a schedule. The remote works within about 30 feet, which covers most patios. It's genuinely useful, not a gimmick.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Reviewers keep coming back to two things: durability and the color options. As one verified purchase put it, "These are the first lights that survived my coastal porch — salt air ate through two other brands in a season." Another said, "The app is actually intuitive, unlike most smart home junk. My wife figured it out without asking me." The shatterproof build gets mentioned repeatedly by families with kids and pets who've had glass bulbs break before.

## Where It Falls Short

**The plastic bulbs are the tradeoff.** At close range, they don't have the crystal clarity of glass — they look slightly frosted, which softens the light but also dulls the "premium cafe" aesthetic. If you're trying to recreate an upscale restaurant patio, you'll notice. The controller hub is also chunky — about the size of a deck of cards — and it's not weatherproof itself, so you'll need to mount it under an eave or in a covered outlet box.

**The app pairing can be finicky.** Several reviews mention Bluetooth dropping during initial setup, requiring a reset or two before it connects. It's a one-time annoyance, not a recurring issue, but it's worth knowing before you spend 10 minutes cursing at your phone.

**No dimming via the physical switch.** If you lose the remote and don't have the app handy, you're stuck with full brightness. The inline switch is only on/off.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy if:** You want smart features (colors, schedules, dimming) without paying smart-home prices. You live somewhere with real weather — rain, snow, humidity — and need lights that'll survive seasons, not just weekends. You have kids or pets who might knock lights around. You're covering a large area and want to daisy-chain.

**Skip if:** You're a purist who insists on glass bulbs for aesthetic reasons. You hate dealing with apps and just want a dumb, reliable switch. You need truly bright task lighting — these are ambiance lights, not work lights.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I leave these out in winter?**
Yes. The IP65 rating handles snow and freezing rain. Just make sure the controller hub is protected from direct precipitation.

**Q: How long is the cord between bulbs?**
Three feet between bulbs, with a 10-foot lead cord from the plug to the first bulb. Total length is 48 feet.

**Q: Do I need a smart home hub?**
No. The app connects via your phone's Bluetooth/WiFi directly. It works with Alexa and Google Home for voice control, but that's optional.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The YAIYAN string lights deliver exactly what they promise: weatherproof, shatterproof, feature-rich lighting at a fair price. They're not the prettiest option on the market — glass purists will want to look elsewhere — but for function-first shoppers who want smart controls and genuine durability, these are a strong buy. The setup quirk and plastic bulbs knock off a fraction of a point, but the value proposition is hard to beat. **Buy it if you want smart, weatherproof lighting without the premium markup. Skip it if glass aesthetics are non-negotiable.**
