# Yolsunes 24 Inch Extra Large Outdoor Wall Light: A Statement Piece That Actually Delivers

Most "extra large" outdoor lights are a modest 16 or 18 inches. The Yolsunes 24-inch wall light is not that. It's a full two feet of matte black aluminum and clear glass that demands attention the moment you pull it out of the box. If your front entry has been looking undersized next to a tall door or double-height garage, this is the fixture that finally fills the space.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the assembled light fixture, a mounting plate, screws, wire nuts, and a template for drilling. No bulbs — that's a deliberate choice, and one I'll address below. The housing is die-cast aluminum with a powder-coated finish that resists chipping, and the glass is tempered, not the thin acrylic you often find at this price point.

As shown in the product image, the design is a clean, rectangular lantern silhouette — nothing fussy. The glass panels are clear, which means you see the actual bulbs inside. With a frosted or filament-style bulb, it looks sharp; with a standard utility LED, it looks like a warehouse light. Choose your bulbs accordingly.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

This thing is heavy — around 12 pounds. That's a good sign. Cheap fixtures feel hollow; this one feels like it will survive a hailstorm. The mounting plate is sturdy, and the included hardware is adequate, though I'd recommend using your own anchors if mounting into stucco or brick. The gasket on the back plate seals well against the wall, and the fixture is wet-rated, so direct rain exposure isn't a concern.

Light output depends entirely on your bulbs. With two 100-watt equivalent LEDs, it floods a standard porch area with even, shadow-free light. The clear glass means no diffusion — you get full brightness, but you also get a clear view of the bulb filaments. Choose warm white (2700K-3000K) for a residential feel; daylight bulbs will look clinical.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The dominant theme in the 225 ratings is scale. One reviewer wrote, *"I've bought three 'large' lights from big box stores and they all looked small. This one finally looks right on my 10-foot door."* Another noted, *"The aluminum housing surprised me — I expected plastic at this price. It's solid."*

Multiple buyers also mentioned the installation being straightforward for a two-person job — one to hold the fixture, one to wire it. The clear instructions and pre-drilled mounting holes eliminate guesswork.

## Where It Falls Short

First, no bulbs included. At this price point, that's mildly annoying, but it also gives you control over color temperature and dimmability. Budget an extra $15-20 for decent LED bulbs.

Second, the size is a double-edged sword. If your wall is narrower than 30 inches, this light will dominate it — and not in a good way. Measure your mounting surface before buying. Several reviewers mentioned returning it after realizing it was too large for their space.

Third, the clear glass means dust and bugs are visible. You'll want to wipe the interior occasionally, and clear glass shows every smudge on the exterior.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Homeowners with tall entryways or double-height garages** — this is the fix for undersized lighting.
- **Anyone replacing builder-grade fixtures** — the aluminum construction is a clear upgrade.
- **Coastal or rainy climates** — the wet rating means you won't worry about corrosion or water ingress.

Skip it if you have a standard 36-inch-wide porch area with a single door — this will visually overwhelm the space.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this with a dimmer switch?**  
A: Yes, but only if you install dimmable bulbs. The fixture itself is dimmer-compatible; the bulbs are the limiting factor.

**Q: Is it really 24 inches wide?**  
A: Yes — 24 inches across the widest point, about 13 inches tall, and 8 inches deep. It's the largest standard outdoor wall light you'll find without going custom.

**Q: Does it come with a dusk-to-dawn sensor?**  
A: No. You'll need a smart bulb or an inline photocell if you want automatic operation.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you have a large wall that needs a proportional fixture and you want metal construction, not plastic. **Skip it if** your mounting area is under 30 inches wide or you're not prepared to buy bulbs separately.

At 4.5 stars from 225 ratings, this is one of the better oversized outdoor lights in its class. It's not perfect — the bulbless packaging and imposing scale are real caveats — but for the right installation, it's a five-star product.
