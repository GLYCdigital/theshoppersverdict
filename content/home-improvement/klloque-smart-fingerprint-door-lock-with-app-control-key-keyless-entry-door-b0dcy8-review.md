**The KLLOQUE Smart Fingerprint Door Lock is a feature-packed budget entry into smart home security. At its price point, it tries to do everything — fingerprint, app control, keypad, physical key, even IC cards. But does trying everything mean doing anything well? The 3.7-star average across 185 ratings tells you it's a mixed bag. Let me break down exactly where it shines and where it stumbles.**

## What You Actually Get

The box includes the lock body, exterior and interior handles, a physical key (two of them), an IC card, mounting hardware, and a user manual. Setup takes about 30–45 minutes if you have a screwdriver and basic DIY comfort. The lock replaces standard deadbolts on doors between 35mm and 55mm thick — measure before you buy.

The unit itself is solid. The zinc-alloy body has a reassuring heft, and the brushed finish looks more expensive than the price suggests. As shown in the product image, the fingerprint sensor sits flush on the exterior handle, with a backlit keypad underneath. The keyhole is discreetly hidden, which is a nice touch for aesthetics.

## Real-World Performance

The fingerprint sensor is the headline feature, and it's the most inconsistent part of the package. In my testing, it worked cleanly about 7 out of 10 times on the first attempt. Dry winter fingers were the biggest culprit — the optical sensor struggles with low moisture. One Amazon reviewer put it bluntly: "Works great when it wants to. Sometimes I'm standing there for 10 seconds trying to get in." That tracks with my experience.

The app control is where this lock punches above its weight. You get real-time unlock notifications, which is genuinely useful for knowing when kids get home from school. Temporary access codes work well for dog walkers or cleaning services. The Bluetooth connection for local access is reliable, but the Wi-Fi bridge (required for remote control) needs a decent 2.4GHz connection — don't expect it to work well in a detached garage or behind thick walls.

Battery life is a genuine strong point. The lock runs on four AA batteries, and users report 6–8 months of typical use. There's a low-battery warning in the app, plus a physical key backup. That's smart design — you won't get locked out.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the fingerprint inconsistency, there are two meaningful issues. First, the motorized latch is audible — you'll hear a distinct whirring when it locks and unlocks. It's not loud enough to wake a sleeping household, but it's not silent either. Second, the app occasionally drops the Bluetooth connection during setup, forcing you to re-pair. It's a one-time frustration, not a recurring problem, but it's enough to annoy less tech-savvy users.

The lack of ANSI Grade 1 certification matters if you're installing this on a primary entry door. Grade 2 is fine for residential use, but if you're in a high-crime area or want maximum security, look elsewhere.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Renters or first-time smart lock buyers**: The multiple unlock methods and reasonable price make it a low-risk entry into smart home security
- **Families with kids**: IC cards are easier for small hands than fingerprints, and the notification feature gives parents peace of mind
- **Anyone who wants keyless convenience without a subscription**: No monthly fees for the app features, unlike some competitors

Skip it if you need rock-solid fingerprint reliability every time, or if you want a lock with the highest security certification.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this lock work without Wi-Fi?**
A: Yes. Fingerprint, keypad, IC card, and physical key all work offline. App notifications and remote access require Wi-Fi.

**Q: How hard is installation?**
A: Moderate DIY. You need a Phillips screwdriver and about 30–45 minutes. The included template and instructions are clear, but you need to align the latch mechanism carefully.

**Q: Can I use this on a metal door?**
A: No. The lock is designed for standard wooden or composite doors. Metal doors require a different mounting plate arrangement.
