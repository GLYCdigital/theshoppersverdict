## What's Actually in the Box

This is a big kit — 299 pieces organized in a blow-molded case that keeps everything visible at a glance. You're getting:

- **Sockets**: Deep and shallow in both 1/4-inch and 3/8-inch drive. SAE sizes run from 3/16-inch through 9/16-inch on 1/4 drive, and 3/8-inch through 3/4-inch on 3/8 drive. Metric coverage spans 4mm to 19mm, which handles most modern vehicles.
- **Drivers**: Hex bits (Allen), Torx bits, and slotted/flathead drivers — the stuff you need for brake calipers, interior trim, and appliance repair.
- **Ratchets**: Two quick-release ratchets (one per drive size) with 72-tooth mechanisms. That's 5-degree swing arc — enough to work in tight spaces without re-positioning constantly.
- **Extensions and adapters**: Standard and universal joint extensions, plus drive adapters to bridge between the two sizes.

What's missing? There's no 1/2-inch drive anywhere in this set. That's the most significant gap for anyone working on suspension components, axle nuts, or larger fasteners.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

CRAFTSMAN's chrome vanadium sockets have a polished finish that resists rust and cleans up easily with a rag. The laser-etched size markings are a genuine upgrade over stamped ones — they don't wear off after a few months of use. One Amazon reviewer noted: *"The size markings are actually readable, which is rare for the price point."*

## Where It Falls Short

Be honest about three tradeoffs:

- **The case is the weak point**. It's functional but not rugged. One reviewer reported: *"The hinge cracked after about six months of being tossed in a truck bed."* Treat it as a storage tray, not a tool box.
- **No 1/2-inch drive**. This caps the set's ceiling. You will need a separate set for serious automotive work.
- **Some 3/8-inch sockets lack the chrome-vanadium strength of pro-grade sets** like GearWrench or Proto. Fine for weekend work; not for daily professional use.
