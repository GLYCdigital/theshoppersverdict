If your bathroom is tight on square footage, you've probably stared at bulky vanity cabinets and wished they'd just… disappear. The QISHKA wall-hung vessel sink does exactly that. This 12.6-inch semi-oval ceramic basin mounts directly to the wall, freeing up the floor completely. At 4.5 stars across 225 ratings, it's clearly working for a lot of people. ## What's Actually in the Box

QISHKA keeps it simple. You get the ceramic basin, a mounting bracket, and the necessary screws and anchors for standard drywall or tile installation. That's it. No faucet, no drain assembly, no P-trap. If you're a seasoned DIYer, this is fine — you'll pick up a faucet and drain that match your style. If you expected a plug-and-play kit, budget for those extras and a trip to the hardware store.

The basin itself is the star. It's genuine ceramic with a high-temperature glaze, not the resin or acrylic you'll find at similar price points. The semi-oval shape is sleek and modern, with a smooth, glossy finish that's easy to wipe down. As shown in the product image, the profile is slim and elegant — it genuinely looks like a designer piece when mounted.

## Real-World Performance: The Good and the Splashy

Let's talk about what it's like to actually use this thing. The ceramic glaze is excellent — buyers consistently note that it cleans up easily and doesn't show water spots or soap scum the way cheaper finishes do. One reviewer put it plainly: *"The finish is beautiful and has held up perfectly for six months. No scratches, no dulling."* Another praised the build: *"This is a solid, heavy piece. It feels like quality, not a flimsy plastic knockoff."*

The wall-mount design delivers the floating look that's so popular right now. With the concealed bracket, you get a clean gap between the sink and the floor, which makes mopping and cleaning underneath effortless. Multiple reviewers mention this as a deciding factor — it makes a small bathroom feel significantly more open.

But here's the tradeoff you need to hear: this is a *small* sink. The 12.6" x 11.4" basin is roughly the size of a large dinner plate. Hand-washing is fine, but you're not washing your face or brushing your teeth with the same abandon you would in a full-size basin. Several buyers noted splashback as a real issue. One wrote: *"Water hits the back wall every time. I had to install a glass backsplash to protect the paint."* That's not a flaw in the product — it's physics. Shallow vessel sinks splash. Plan for it.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the splash factor, the most common complaint is the faucet limitation. The sink has a single 1.5-inch hole, which means you're restricted to single-hole faucets or wall-mounted options. If you had your heart set on a widespread two-handle setup, this won't work. Also worth noting: there's no overflow hole. That's standard for vessel sinks, but it means you need to be careful not to leave the water running unattended.

Installation is manageable but not trivial. The mounting bracket needs to be level and securely anchored into studs or heavy-duty wall anchors. If you're tiling, you'll want to install the bracket before the tile goes up, or use a diamond drill bit afterward. Several reviewers mentioned hiring a plumber for the rough-in, which is a smart move if you're not confident in your skills.

## Who Should Buy This

This sink is perfect for three specific scenarios:

## FAQ: Three Questions Buyers Actually Ask

**Q: What size faucet fits this sink?**
A: You need a single-hole faucet with a 1.5-inch shank, or a wall-mounted faucet. Standard single-hole bathroom faucets fit fine. Widespread two-handle units won't work without drilling, which voids the warranty.

**Q: Can I mount this on drywall alone?**
A: No. You must anchor the bracket into wall studs or use heavy-duty toggle bolts rated for the sink's weight (roughly 15-20 lbs plus water). Drywall anchors alone won't hold it securely.

**Q: Does it come with a drain?**
A: No. You'll need to purchase a vessel sink drain with a 1.5-inch tailpiece separately. Most standard vessel sink drains work, but check the thread type before buying.
