Let's cut through the lighting aisle noise. The Faymart Outdoor Wall Lantern is exactly what it looks like in the product image: a straightforward, black, die-cast aluminum sconce that doesn't try to be clever. And that's largely why it works.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the lantern body, a mounting plate, screws, wire nuts, and a rubber gasket. No bulb — plan for that. The fixture measures roughly 12 inches tall with a 7-inch width and extends about 8 inches from the wall, which puts it in the sweet spot for standard porch or garage lighting. The black finish is a matte powder coat, not glossy, which helps it blend into most exterior trim without looking like a cheap plastic replica.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The die-cast aluminum construction is the headline here. It's heavy — noticeably heavier than the hollow sheet-metal fixtures at big-box stores. That weight translates to wind resistance and longevity. One reviewer noted, "We live a block from the ocean and this thing has been up for eight months with zero corrosion." That tracks with what the materials suggest: the powder coating is applied evenly, and the seams where the glass meets the frame are gasketed properly.

The seeded glass is a double-edged sword. It looks fantastic when lit — the texture breaks up the bulb's glow into that classic, warm tavern-light pattern. But it's glass, and it's thin. Two separate reviewers mentioned chips near the screw holes while tightening the frame. Handle it like you would fine china during installation, and use the included rubber gasket exactly as shown in the instructions. Tighten evenly, not sequentially.

Light output depends entirely on your bulb choice. The fixture accepts a standard E26 base, but here's the catch: the glass shade is shallow. A standard A19 LED bulb works fine, but anything longer — like a filament-style globe or a candelabra adapter — will poke out past the glass and look awkward. Stick with a short, frosted LED in the 800-lumen range for a balanced output. One buyer suggested, "Use a 60W equivalent LED and it's perfect — bright enough for security, warm enough for ambiance."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

With 235 ratings averaging 4.7 stars, the feedback clusters around three themes. First, the install — most buyers report a 20-minute job with basic tools. The included gasket seals flush against the wall, which prevents the bug infiltration that plagues cheaper fixtures. Second, the aesthetics. Multiple reviewers describe it as looking "way more expensive than it is," specifically calling out the clean lines and the quality of the black finish. Third, durability — the aluminum body and sealed construction mean it holds up in rain, snow, and sun without fading or warping.

## Where It Falls Short

The glass fragility is the most consistent negative, and it's worth taking seriously. If you're installing this on a second-story wall where dropping a pane means a trip to the hardware store for custom glass, be extra careful. The lack of a built-in photocell is also a miss for the price — you'll either flip it manually or buy a smart bulb separately. And if you have unusually deep wall sconce boxes, the included mounting plate might not cover the gap cleanly; you'll need a slightly larger canopy or a trim ring.

## Who Should Buy This

This fixture is for the homeowner who wants solid, dependable exterior lighting without paying designer markup. It's ideal for front porches, garage side doors, and back patios in climates with real weather — the aluminum body shrugs off what kills cheaper steel fixtures. Skip it if you need integrated smart features, or if you're installing in a spot where a longer bulb is non-negotiable.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this fixture truly waterproof, or just weather-resistant?**
A: It's rated for wet locations — meaning it can handle direct rain and snow exposure. The gasket and sealed glass do their job, but the bottom is open, so don't submerge it or aim a pressure washer at it.

**Q: What size bulb fits without sticking out?**
A: Keep it under 4.5 inches in length. A standard A19 LED (the common 60W replacement) fits flush. Anything longer, like a vintage Edison bulb, will protrude past the glass.

**Q: Can I install this over an existing junction box?**
A: Yes, it's designed for standard 3.5-inch round or octagonal boxes. The included mounting plate covers both configurations. If your box is recessed deeper than 1 inch, you'll want a box extender.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a durable, good-looking sconce that installs cleanly and won't corrode in a few seasons. **Skip it if** you need smart features, or you're not comfortable handling fragile glass during installation.
