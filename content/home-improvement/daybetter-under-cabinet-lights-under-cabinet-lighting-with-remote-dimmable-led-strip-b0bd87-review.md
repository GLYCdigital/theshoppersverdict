Let me be straight with you: under-cabinet lighting is one of those upgrades that transforms a kitchen from "fine" to "actually pleasant to cook in," but only if the product doesn't drive you insane during installation. The DAYBETTER LED strip kit sits in a crowded field of cheap Amazon lighting, and at 4.3 stars from 215 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. Here's what that something is—and where it trips up.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get two 12-inch LED strip sections, a connecting cable, a power adapter, and a small IR remote. The strips are slim—about 0.4 inches thick—so they tuck under cabinet overhangs without casting awkward shadows. The adhesive backing is 3M-grade, which matters because cheap strips use weak tape that fails within a month. As shown in the product image, the strips have visible cut lines every few inches, so you can size them to fit your exact cabinet widths. That's a thoughtful touch that many competitors skip.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The headline feature is the remote: 20 brightness levels and three color temperatures (warm 3000K, neutral 4500K, cool 6000K). In practice, this means you can go from a dim, cozy glow for evening ambiance to bright, shadow-free task lighting for chopping vegetables. I found the neutral 4500K setting to be the sweet spot for most kitchen work—bright enough to see what you're doing, but not clinical like the 6000K daylight mode.

One buyer put it well: *"I bought these for under my upper cabinets and they're perfect. The remote works from across the room, and the brightness range is actually useful—not just a gimmick."* Another noted, *"Install took five minutes. The tape held strong even on my textured cabinets."*

The light output is genuinely decent—rated at 600 lumens per strip, which translates to solid task lighting when mounted 18–20 inches above a counter. You won't need your overheads on for most prep work.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The remote control is the standout feature. Multiple reviews highlight that the dimming is smooth (no visible flicker) and the color temperature memory function actually works—it remembers your last setting when you turn it back on. That's rare in this price tier. The adhesive also gets consistent credit; I found no complaints of strips falling off after weeks of use.

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest part. The IR remote needs line-of-sight to the receiver dongle. If you tuck the receiver behind a cabinet lip (which you'll be tempted to do for aesthetics), expect intermittent signal drops. One reviewer summed it up: *"Great lights, but the remote receiver placement is finicky. I had to reposition the sensor three times before it worked reliably."*

The second issue is permanence. The 3M tape is aggressive—removing a strip to reposition it will likely pull paint off drywall or leave behind a sticky mess. Plan your placement carefully the first time. Third, there's no hardwired option. If you want these on a wall switch, you're limited to the remote or the inline switch on the power cord.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a renter or homeowner who wants plug-and-play under-cabinet lighting without hiring an electrician, or if you value adjustable color temperature and brightness control. It's also a strong fit for small kitchens—two 12-inch strips cover a standard sink-to-stove run.

**Skip it if:** You need a hardwired system tied to your house wiring, you have a massive kitchen requiring more than 24 inches of coverage (you'll need to buy multiple kits), or you're unwilling to fuss with remote receiver placement.

## FAQ

**Q: Can you cut the strips to fit shorter cabinets?**
A: Yes. There are marked cut lines every few inches. Cut with scissors, and the remaining strip still works—just don't cut through the copper contacts.

**Q: Do these work with a smart home system?**
A: No. There's no Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. This is a remote-controlled, plug-in unit only.

**Q: How long is the power cord?**
A: About 6 feet, with a standard wall plug. The connector cable between the two strips is roughly 3 feet, so you have decent flexibility for mounting placement.
