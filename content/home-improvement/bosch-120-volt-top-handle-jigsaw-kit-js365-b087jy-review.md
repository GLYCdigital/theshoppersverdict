The Bosch JS365 isn't the flashiest jigsaw on the shelf. No LED light. No fancy dust blower. But after 235 Amazon reviewers gave it 4.7 stars, there's clearly something right here. The short version: this is a professional-grade cutting tool that prioritizes performance over gimmicks. If you need clean, accurate curves in wood, laminate, or metal, this deserves a hard look.

## What's in the Box

The kit includes the JS365 jigsaw, a T-shank blade (one wood blade to get you started), a hex wrench, anti-splinter insert, and a hard-shell carrying case. The case has custom cutouts for the tool and accessories — it's not the most compact storage solution, but it protects the saw well on jobsites. You'll need to buy additional blades immediately; one blade won't last through a serious project.

## Performance: Where the JS365 Earns Its Keep

The 7.5-amp motor with Bosch's Constant Response circuitry is the headline feature. What this means in practice: when you're pushing through 3/4-inch oak or pressure-treated lumber, the blade speed doesn't drop. At 3,100 SPM max, it cuts aggressively without bogging down. Several reviewers noted this specifically — one said, "Cuts through hardwood like butter where my old jigsaw would stall."

The 4-stage orbital action is genuinely useful. For rough fast cuts in softwood, crank it to stage 3. For clean, splinter-free cuts in plywood or veneer, drop it to 0 or 1. Combined with the anti-splinter insert, you get remarkably clean edges — one reviewer called it "the cleanest cuts I've ever gotten from a jigsaw."

The tool-free blade change works exactly as advertised. The lever-action chuck releases T-shank blades in seconds — swap a wood blade for a metal blade without hunting for an Allen wrench. It's a small thing, but it's the kind of detail that separates this from budget jigsaws.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Reviewers across the board mention three things: power, precision, and vibration control. The top-handle design keeps your hand directly over the blade, giving excellent control for intricate cuts. The variable-speed trigger has a nice range — one reviewer noted, "The speed control is smooth, not jumpy like cheaper saws."

The footplate tilts up to 45 degrees for bevel cuts, with positive stops at 0, 15, 30, and 45 degrees. It's a feature you'll use more than you think, especially for trim work.

## Where It Falls Short

The most common complaint: no LED work light. At street prices around $120-140, competitors in this range often include one. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's noticeable when working in dim conditions.

The transparent blade guard collects sawdust quickly, obscuring your view of the cut line. Several reviewers mentioned wiping it constantly. A simple dust blower would have solved this — and it's odd that Bosch left it off.

The cord is 8 feet, which is adequate but not generous. You'll want an extension cord for most jobs.

## Who Should Buy This

**DIYers upgrading from a budget jigsaw**: If you've been fighting a $40 saw that wanders off line and vibrates like a paint mixer, the JS365 is a massive upgrade. The control and cut quality justify the price.

**Finish carpenters and trim installers**: The clean cuts and orbital control make this genuinely useful for visible work.

**Skip it if** you need a light for dark workspaces, or if you rarely need to cut curves — a circular saw will handle straight cuts faster.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this jigsaw work for cutting metal?**
A: Yes, with the right blade. The variable speed control lets you slow down to around 500 SPM for metal, which is essential. Use a T-shank metal-cutting blade and cutting fluid for best results.

**Q: Is the JS365 compatible with all T-shank blades?**
A: Yes. It uses standard T-shank blades, which covers the vast majority of blades sold today. You're not locked into Bosch-brand blades.

**Q: How does this compare to the barrel-grip version (JS470E)?**
A: The barrel grip gives you more control for handheld work but is less comfortable for extended overhead cutting. The JS365's top handle is more versatile for general use. Same motor, same performance.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a jigsaw that cuts clean, doesn't bog down, and will last years without issues. The JS365 is a proven workhorse with professional-level performance at a DIY-friendly price.

**Skip it if** you need a light for dark spaces, or you're primarily cutting straight lines and don't need orbital action.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A genuinely excellent jigsaw held back from perfection by a missing work light and mediocre blade visibility. But for cutting performance, it's hard to beat at this price.
