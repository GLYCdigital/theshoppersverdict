If you're renovating a bathroom, upgrading a kitchen backsplash, or just tired of the same ivory plastic plates that came with your home, the Rio Salto Modern Metal Gold Outlet Cover catches your eye immediately. And for the most part, it delivers on that first impression.

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes a single metal wall plate with the screws you'll need for installation. That's it — no gasket, no mounting hardware beyond the basics, no instructions. For someone who's swapped a light switch cover before, that's fine. For a first-timer, you'll want to watch a quick YouTube video first.

The plate itself is made from zinc alloy with a brushed gold finish. It measures a standard single-gang size, so it'll fit any typical US outlet or switch box. As shown in the product image, the design is clean and minimal — a smooth face with slightly rounded edges, no ornate detailing. If you're going for a modern or transitional look, this fits the bill.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

Here's where the Rio Salto earns its keep. The zinc alloy construction gives it a satisfying heft — this isn't a flimsy stamped-steel plate that bends when you tighten the screws. The brushed gold finish is applied evenly, with no bare spots or drips along the edges. I've seen cheaper gold plates that look orange or brassy in certain light; this one holds a consistent, muted gold tone that pairs well with brass fixtures and warm wood tones.

Installation takes about two minutes. The screws thread cleanly into the mounting holes, and the plate sits flush against a flat wall. The screw holes are recessed, so the included screws sit nearly flush with the face — a nice touch that keeps the minimalist look intact.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. The included screws are silver-toned, not gold. On a dark wall or up close, they're noticeable against the gold faceplate. You'll likely want to source matching gold screws from a hardware store — a minor annoyance, but worth knowing before you buy.

The polished surface is also a fingerprint magnet. In high-traffic areas or if you have kids, you'll be wiping these down regularly. The brushed finish helps, but it doesn't eliminate the issue.

One more thing: because there's no backing gasket, the plate relies entirely on the screws to sit flat. If your outlet box is slightly recessed or your drywall isn't perfectly smooth, you might get a subtle rock when you press on a corner. Not a dealbreaker, but a consideration for older homes with imperfect walls.

**Who Should Buy This**

**Buy it if:** You're upgrading a bathroom or kitchen with gold or brass fixtures and want wall plates that match. You appreciate solid metal construction and a clean, modern aesthetic. You don't mind spending a few extra minutes sourcing matching screws.

**Skip it if:** You're on a strict budget — there are cheaper gold plates available, though you'll sacrifice finish quality and heft. You need a plate that's truly fingerprint-proof. You're covering an imperfect wall and want something with a gasket to hide flaws.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit a standard US outlet or switch?**
Yes. It's a standard single-gang size — 2.75" x 4.5" — and fits any typical residential outlet or toggle switch box.

**Q: Does the gold finish chip or scratch easily?**
In normal use, no. The finish is baked on and holds up well. But like any painted metal, it can scratch if you drop it on tile or drag a screwdriver across it during installation.

**Q: Are the screws really silver-colored?**
Yes, the included screws have a silver tone. If that bothers you, pick up gold screws at any hardware store for about a dollar — worth the extra trip.
