**The 19-inch bathroom sink that punches above its price tag — if you know what you're getting into.**

Most drop-in sinks in this size range are flimsy resin or thin steel that flexes when you lean on it. The PROFLO PF19164 Rockaway is different. It's actual vitreous china — fired clay with a glass-like glaze — and you feel the difference the moment you lift it out of the box. At roughly 19 inches long and 16.5 inches wide, it's built for tight spaces: powder rooms, small guest baths, or that awkward vanity where a standard 24-incher won't fit.

**What's in the box:** The sink itself, a mounting template, and basic installation hardware. No faucet, no drain assembly, no pop-up stopper. That's normal for this category, but factor in those extra costs when budgeting. The basin depth is 5.5 inches, and the center drain with a 1.5-inch overflow matches most standard plumbing — a direct swap if you're replacing an older oval sink.

**Real-world performance:** The glaze is where this sink earns its keep. Water beads off it rather than soaking in, and it wipes clean with a damp cloth — no scrubbing for hard water spots. The 19-inch width gives you enough splash room for everyday washing without dominating a small counter. **Where it falls short — be honest with yourself:** The biggest gotcha is the lack of faucet holes. If you're replacing a sink that had a 4-inch centerset faucet, you'll need to drill through the counter or rethink your setup entirely. Also, the shallow basin means splashing if you're heavy-handed with the tap — a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker. A few reviewers mentioned shipping damage: *"Arrived with a small chip on the rim. Amazon replaced it quickly, but the first box wasn't padded well."* Inspect the box before you cut into it.

**Who should buy this:** If you're remodeling a small bathroom, need a clean white oval that won't yellow, and have a wall-mount or counter-mount faucet ready, this is a smart buy. It's also ideal for DIYers replacing a builder-grade sink — the template and straightforward drop-in design make it a weekend project. Skip it if you need a self-rimming sink with faucet holes, or if you want a deep basin for washing pets or kids.
