**Bottom line up front:** The Amerdeco ZH0002 pulls are the rare hardware upgrade that delivers exactly what it promises — clean lines, solid weight, and a finish that doesn't show every fingerprint. At 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, buyers agree. But there's a measurement gotcha you need to know before you drill.

## What you actually get

The box contains the pulls, stainless mounting screws, and a paper template. That's it. No rubber washers, no Allen wrench (you'll need a Phillips driver), no fancy packaging. The template is genuinely useful — it snaps into place over your existing holes or marks fresh ones accurately.

The pulls themselves are machined from 304 stainless steel with a brushed finish. At roughly 1/2-inch in diameter, they have a substantial feel in the hand — not flimsy hollow tubes like some budget options. The ZH0002 is a straight bar design, which gives it a modern-to-transitional look that works on shaker cabinets, slab doors, and even some traditional kitchens.

## Real-world performance

I installed a set on a kitchen with 12 upper and 9 lower cabinets. The 3-inch and 4-inch pulls went on uppers; 6-inch and 8-inch on lowers. The included template made alignment foolproof — a real time-saver when you're drilling 42 holes.

The brushed finish is the standout feature. After two weeks of daily use — including kids with sticky hands — the pulls show no visible smudging. That's rare at this price point. The screws thread cleanly into the pulls without stripping, which tells me the threading is properly cut.

One thing buyers consistently mention: the weight. As one reviewer put it, "These feel like $5 pulls, not $1 pulls." The solid construction means no wobble once tightened.

## Where it falls short

The biggest gotcha: **hole spacing isn't standard on all sizes.** The 3-inch pull uses 64mm center-to-center, but the 4-inch uses 96mm and the 5-inch uses 128mm. If you're replacing existing hardware, measure your current hole spacing before ordering. Returning mismatched hardware is a hassle you can avoid with a tape measure.

Second, there are no rubber backing pads. On high-gloss or painted cabinets, the metal bases can scuff the surface over time. A $3 pack of adhesive felt pads solves this, but it's an extra step Amerdeco should've handled.

Finally, the 12-inch pulls are heavy — if you're putting them on soft-close drawers, make sure your hinges/drawer slides can handle the added weight. Most can, but it's worth checking.

## Who should buy this

**Buy if:** You're doing a full kitchen or bathroom refresh and want modern, durable pulls without paying $8-10 per piece. The mix-and-match sizing across the ZH0002 line means you can use one style throughout your whole home.

**Skip if:** You have non-standard pre-drilled holes (check that spacing!), or you're looking for a vintage/ornate style. This is a minimalist bar pull — it won't dress up a traditional kitchen.
