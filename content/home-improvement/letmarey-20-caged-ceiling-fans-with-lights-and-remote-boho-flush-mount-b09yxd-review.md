**The short version:** The Letmarey 20" caged ceiling fan is proof that "small space" doesn't have to mean "ugly ceiling fixture." It's a flush-mount boho statement piece that actually moves air — not just a decorative light with a motor bolted on. At 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, buyers mostly love it, but there are two tradeoffs you should know about before you commit.

## What you actually get

The box contains the fan assembly, a remote control (battery included), mounting hardware, and a surprisingly clear instruction manual. The cage is pre-assembled around the blades, which saves you the single most frustrating part of ceiling fan installation. You're looking at a 20-inch blade sweep with a 14-inch tall cage, finished in a matte black with woven rattan-look spokes.

As shown in the product image, this is a flush-mount design — no downrod, no hanging chain. It sits about 10 inches from the ceiling, which makes it viable for rooms with 8-foot ceilings or even lower. The integrated LED light is dimmable via the remote, and the whole unit weighs around 11 pounds, so standard electrical boxes handle it fine.

## Real-world performance

Let's be honest about physics: a 20-inch fan is not going to cool a living room. But in a bedroom, office, or 10x10 den, this thing moves more air than you'd expect. The six-speed DC motor is quiet — one buyer called it "silent on low, barely noticeable on high" — and the reverse function works properly for winter air circulation.

The remote is where this fan earns its keep. It's not one of those cheap IR remotes that only works if you point it directly at the unit. This is RF, so it works from across the room, through furniture. You get six speeds, a light dimmer, a 1/2/4/8-hour sleep timer, and a "natural wind" mode that cycles speeds randomly. The sleep timer alone is worth it — you can set the fan to shut off after you've fallen asleep without fumbling for a switch.

## What buyers consistently praise

The design is the headline. Multiple reviews mention getting compliments from guests. One verified purchase wrote: *"I was skeptical the boho look would feel cheap in person, but the rattan detailing looks genuinely woven, not printed. It's the first thing people notice when they walk into my office."*

Another buyer highlighted the installation: *"Took me 35 minutes by myself. If you've installed one ceiling fan before, this is easier because the cage comes pre-assembled. No balancing blades, no wobble."*

Durability also gets mentioned — several reviewers have had theirs running nearly continuously for over a year without motor noise or remote issues.

## Where it falls short

The cage is a dust magnet. The woven spokes create dozens of tiny crevices, and every one of them collects fuzz. You'll need a soft brush attachment on your vacuum or a microfiber duster — and patience — to keep it looking clean. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's a weekly chore if you're in a dusty environment.

The light is the other compromise. At 1800 lumens, it's roughly equivalent to a single 100-watt incandescent bulb. That's fine for ambient lighting, but if you're hoping to use this as your primary light source in a home office, you'll be squinting. Plan on a desk lamp or floor lamp to supplement.

Also worth noting: the 20-inch size is genuinely small. If your room is larger than 100 square feet, this fan won't cut it. Check your square footage before ordering.

## Who should buy this

- **Renters and apartment dwellers** who want a stylish fan they can install without major modifications
- **Low-ceiling spaces** — basements, bonus rooms, lofts with 7-foot ceilings where a downrod fan is dangerous
- **Boho or farmhouse decor enthusiasts** who are tired of builder-grade white fans
- **Anyone who wants a fan with a real sleep timer** and remote that works through walls

**Skip it if** you need a fan for a master bedroom (you want a 52-inch unit), or if you require bright task lighting from your ceiling fixture.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this fan safe for outdoor or covered porch use?**
A: No. This is rated for indoor dry locations only. Moisture will damage the motor and the rattan-style cage.

**Q: Does the remote need to be mounted on the wall?**
A: No, it's a handheld remote that comes with a small wall-mount bracket if you want to hang it. You can also just leave it on your nightstand.

**Q: Can you install this on a sloped ceiling?**
A: The manufacturer says no — it's designed for flat ceilings only. The flush mount won't accommodate an angled ceiling without an adapter kit that isn't included.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you have a small room (under 100 sq ft), want a statement piece that doesn't scream "cheap Amazon fan," and don't mind a little dusting duty. **Skip it if** you need serious airflow for a large space or bright primary lighting.

The Letmarey 20" caged fan isn't the most powerful or the brightest — but it's the most stylish flush-mount fan under $150 I've seen, and the remote functionality punches above its weight class. At 4.4 stars with 220 ratings, the consensus holds: this is a solid buy for the right room.
