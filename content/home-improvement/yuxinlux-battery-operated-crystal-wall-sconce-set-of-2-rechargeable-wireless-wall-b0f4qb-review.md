Let's be blunt: most battery-operated "crystal" sconces on Amazon are plastic junk with fake gems and a light so dim you'll wonder if it's on. The YUXINLUX set isn't that. It's not perfect, but it's the rare wireless sconce that actually looks intentional on a wall — and with 240 ratings averaging 4.8 stars, buyers agree more often than not.

**Here's what's in the box:** two complete sconces, two rechargeable batteries (already installed), a USB-C charging cable, mounting screws and anchors, and adhesive strips for renters who can't drill. The fixtures themselves measure roughly 11 inches tall with a 5-inch projection from the wall. The frame is metal — not the hollow plastic you'll find at half the price — and the crystal drops are faceted glass, not acrylic. They catch light properly when the sconce is on, which is the entire point of buying crystal in the first place.

**The real-world test:** Installation took me under ten minutes per sconce. The adhesive strips held fine on smooth drywall, but I switched to the screws for a more permanent mount — the included anchors are decent quality, not the flimsy drywall plugs that strip out on insertion. Each sconce has a touch switch on the frame itself; tap once for on, again for off, and a third tap cycles through brightness levels. The remote works from across the room, which is handy if you mount these high on a stairwell wall.

The light itself is warm (3000K) and genuinely pleasant. It throws a soft, ambient glow that's perfect for a hallway, reading nook, or above a console table. But here's the honest ceiling: this is an accent light, not a task light. At maximum brightness, it illuminates a comfortable seating area but won't give you enough lumens to read a book comfortably. If you need functional lighting, look elsewhere — this is for atmosphere, and it does atmosphere very well.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The aesthetic is the headline. One reviewer noted, "I was skeptical about battery-powered sconces looking cheap, but these look like they cost three times as much." Another mentioned the installation specifically: "Took me five minutes each, no electrician, no mess — my wife thought I hired someone." The rechargeable battery is a recurring theme in positive reviews, with multiple buyers noting they appreciate not buying replacement batteries every few months.

**Where it falls short:** Battery life is the tradeoff. At full brightness, you'll get roughly 8-10 hours before the light dims noticeably. On the lowest setting, that stretches to 20+ hours, but I found the lowest setting too dim for most rooms. Plan on charging weekly if you use these nightly for a few hours. The USB-C cable is included, but there's no wall adapter — you'll need your own. Also, the touch switch location on the back of the frame is slightly awkward if you have the sconce mounted high; you'll be reaching and fumbling. The remote mitigates this, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

**Who should buy this:** Renters who want sconce lighting without drilling or permanent wiring. Homeowners who want a quick refresh in a hallway or entryway without calling an electrician. Anyone who's bought cheap wireless sconces before and been disappointed — the build quality here genuinely justifies the higher price point.

**Skip it if:** You need bright, functional reading light. You want a fixture that runs months between charges. You're looking for something with a dimmer that goes truly low for nighttime ambiance — the lowest setting is still noticeable.

**FAQ:**

**How long does the battery actually last?** At medium brightness, expect 12-14 hours per charge. Charging takes about 3 hours via USB-C. If you use them two hours a night, that's roughly a week between charges.

**Can these be mounted outdoors?** No. They're rated for indoor use only. Moisture will damage the electronics and the metal frame isn't weather-sealed.

**Do the crystals come pre-installed?** Yes, each crystal drop is already attached to the frame. Nothing to assemble — which is refreshing, because most competitors ship these as a bag of loose crystals and a diagram that might as well be hieroglyphics.

**The Verdict:** 4.6/5 stars.

**Buy it if:** You want the look of hardwired sconces without the cost, mess, or electrician. The build quality and crystal accents justify the price.

**Skip it if:** You need actual task lighting or can't tolerate weekly charging. This is a mood-setter, not a workhorse — and it's honest about that. For accent lighting in a living space, it's one of the best wireless options at this price point.
