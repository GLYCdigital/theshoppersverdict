## What You Actually Get

The set includes Phillips #2 bits in extended lengths, built from a dual-steel construction: D2 tool steel for the shank and S2 for the driving tip. This matters because S2 is the industry standard for impact-rated bits — it's tough enough to handle the concussive torque of an impact driver without shattering. D2 adds wear resistance where the bit meets the chuck. As shown in the product image, these are long-format bits, roughly 3 to 6 inches depending on the variant, designed specifically for recessed fasteners.
