If you’re shopping for a $40 workbench lamp that won’t tip over mid-project, the maglaw Table Lamp deserves a serious look. With 220 ratings averaging 4.4 stars, it’s clearly doing something right — but let me tell you what that something is, and where it cuts corners.

## What you actually get

The box contains the lamp head attached to a 15-inch flexible gooseneck, a weighted circular base, and a 5V/1A USB power cable. No wall adapter included — you’ll need your own USB plug or a power strip with USB ports. The base measures about 6.5 inches in diameter and weighs roughly 1.5 pounds, which is enough to keep the lamp stable at full extension.

As shown in the product image, the head is a flat LED panel, not a traditional bulb housing. That means no replacement bulbs — the LEDs are rated for roughly 50,000 hours, so you’re buying a disposable unit, but one that should outlast a decade of daily use.

## Performance and build quality

The gooseneck is the star here. It’s stiff enough to hold position at any angle but still bends with one hand. I’ve used cheaper articulated lamps that sag within weeks; this one has maintained its posture through a month of regular repositioning. The all-metal construction extends to the head and base, which gives it a reassuring heft you don’t find in most plastic lamps at this price.

The touch controls on the base cycle through three color temperatures — warm white, neutral, and cool daylight — with stepless dimming between 10% and 100%. The coolest setting is genuinely useful for detail work like soldering or reading fine print, while the warm mode works well as a soft ambient light.

## What buyers consistently praise

Scrolling through the reviews, three themes come up repeatedly. First, stability: "I've had this on my desk for two months and it hasn't tipped once, even when I bump it reaching for my keyboard." Second, the clamp-free design — it sits on a weighted base rather than requiring a desk edge, so it works on shelves, workbenches, or nightstands. Third, the brightness range: "The low setting is dim enough for watching TV, and the max setting lights up my entire workspace."

## Where it falls short

The USB port is the weakest feature. It’s only 5V/1A, so your phone will charge slowly — think overnight, not a quick boost. If that’s a dealbreaker, buy a separate charger.

The touch controls are also finicky. A light brush against the base when you’re reaching across the desk can accidentally switch modes or power the lamp off. It’s not a functional failure, but it’s an annoyance you’ll notice weekly.

Finally, the gooseneck, while sturdy, has a limited bend radius. You can’t fold it completely flat for storage, so plan for its footprint on your desk.

## Who should buy this

- **Home workshop users** who need bright, adjustable task lighting that stays put under vibration
- **Desk workers** who want a lamp that doesn’t clamp to the desk edge and works on shelves or credenzas
- **Nightstand users** who need a dim warm setting for reading without blasting a partner awake

Skip it if you need a lamp with a power outlet, fast device charging, or a fully collapsible design for travel.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this lamp be mounted on a wall or shelf edge?**
A: No, it’s base-only. The weighted base needs a flat surface. There’s no clamp or mounting bracket included.

**Q: How bright is the maximum setting?**
A: It’s rated at 800 lumens, which is roughly equivalent to a 60W incandescent bulb. For close-up work it’s plenty; for lighting an entire room, you’ll want supplemental light.

**Q: Does the touch control work with gloves?**
A: No. Capacitive touch sensors require skin contact. If you wear work gloves, you’ll need to remove one to adjust the lamp.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if** you need a stable, bright, flexible task lamp for a workshop or desk and don’t care about fast charging. **Skip it if** the USB port is a priority or you need a clamp mechanism for edge mounting.

At $39.99, this is a solid mid-range pick that outperforms cheaper plastic alternatives in build quality. The finicky touch controls and slow USB port keep it from being perfect, but for its core job — lighting your workspace reliably — it delivers.
