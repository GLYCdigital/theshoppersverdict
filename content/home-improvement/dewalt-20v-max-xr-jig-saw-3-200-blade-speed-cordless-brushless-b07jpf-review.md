Let me be direct: cordless jigsaws are usually the weak link in a contractor's kit. They bog down in thick stock, eat batteries, and vibrate so bad your hand goes numb. The DEWALT 20V MAX XR brushed aside most of those complaints—and at 4.8 stars across 240 ratings, buyers agree. But it's not perfect. Here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The bare tool ships with just the saw and a blade wrench (stored in the base). No battery, no charger, no case—so factor that into your price if you're not already invested in the 20V MAX system. The saw itself weighs 6.2 pounds with a 5Ah battery attached, which is middle-of-the-pack for this class.

Key specs you'll care about:
- **3,200 SPM max** brushless motor
- **4-position orbital action** dial plus variable speed trigger
- **Keyless lever-action** blade change
- **Bevel capacity**: 45 degrees (both directions) with positive stops at 0°, 15°, 30°, 45°
- **Stroke length**: 1 inch—longer than budget models, which means faster cuts

## Performance: Where It Shines

This is the first cordless jigsaw I've used that feels genuinely close to a corded tool. The brushless motor holds speed under load. In 3/4-inch maple plywood, it ripped through with zero bogging. In 2x4 pine, the 3,200 SPM was honestly overkill—I dialed it back to the 2,400 SPM range for cleaner cuts.

The orbital action dial is the standout feature. Set it to position 0 for tight curves in thin material, or crank it to 3 for aggressive ripping. Combined with the variable speed trigger, you get real control. One reviewer put it well: *"I've used DeWalt tools for 20 years. This is the first jigsaw that cuts as fast as my old corded Bosch. The orbital dial makes a real difference on thick stock."*

The keyless blade clamp is another win. Lever pops up, insert blade, snap down—done in under five seconds. No fumbling with Allen keys mid-project.

## Where It Falls Short

**No work light.** For a tool in this price class, that's embarrassing. If you're cutting in a dark attic or under a cabinet, you're holding a flashlight in your teeth. Multiple buyers flagged this in reviews.

**Blade guide needs a hex wrench.** The blade clamp is tool-free, but adjusting the guide roller (to minimize blade deflection on curves) requires the included wrench. Not a dealbreaker, but inconsistent.

**Dust management is mediocre.** The integrated blower clears the cut line decently, but there's no hose adapter included. You'll pay another $15–20 for the dust port accessory if you want shop vac collection. The clear plastic guard also scratches quickly and fogs up with sawdust.

**Battery hog.** Expect about 30–40 minutes of continuous cutting with a 5Ah battery. The brushless motor helps, but it's still thirsty at high speeds.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Contractors and serious DIYers** already in the 20V MAX ecosystem—this earns its place in the bag
- **Anyone cutting thick hardwood or plywood regularly** who's tired of cordless jigsaws stalling
- **Fine woodworkers** who want orbital control for clean splinter-free cuts with the right blade

**Skip it if** you're a weekend warrior cutting mostly 1/2-inch plywood and occasional 2x4s—the cheaper DEWALT 20V brushed model (around $99) will handle that fine, and you won't miss the orbital dial or brushless power.

## FAQ

**Is this jigsaw compatible with all DEWALT 20V MAX batteries?**
Yes. It runs on any 20V MAX battery from 1.5Ah to 12Ah. Use a 5Ah or larger for best performance—smaller batteries cause more voltage sag under load.

**Can it cut metal?**
Yes, with the right blade. The variable speed trigger lets you slow it down to around 500–800 SPM for metal cutting. Use a bi-metal blade and cutting fluid for best results.

**Does it come with a case?**
No. The bare tool ships without a bag or case. DEWALT sells a separate kit version with a case and battery, but it costs roughly $100 more.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You're a serious woodworker or contractor who needs cordless cutting power that doesn't compromise. The orbital action, brushless motor, and fast blade changes justify the premium.

**Skip it if:** You're a casual DIYer, you're not already in the DEWALT 20V battery system, or the lack of a work light genuinely bothers you. In that case, look at the Milwaukee M18 Fuel jigsaw—it has an integrated LED and similar performance.

At 4.6/5, this is one of the best cordless jigsaws on the market. It's not flawless, but for clean cuts, real power, and day-in-day-out reliability, it earns its place in any serious toolkit.
