**The Verdict at a Glance**

Most freestanding tubs in this price range are either flimsy acrylic shells that flex under weight or so heavy you need a structural engineer. The WOODBRIDGE sits in a sweet spot: thick-walled, deep enough for actual soaking, and designed with installation realities in mind. After digging through 235 ratings and the specs, this is a legitimate contender if you're remodeling a primary bath — with a few caveats worth knowing before you commit.

---

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the tub itself, a pre-attached drain assembly, adjustable floor-mounting feet, and a leveling kit. That's it. No faucet, no overflow cover, no trim kit. The tub measures 59 inches long, 29.5 inches wide, and stands 23.5 inches high — a compact footprint that fits most standard alcoves but still offers a 60-gallon soaking capacity. The 22.5-inch interior depth is the standout spec here; most comparably priced tubs top out around 18-19 inches.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The construction is where WOODBRIDGE earns its keep. It's a three-layer acrylic sandwich with fiberglass reinforcement — the same approach used by brands charging twice as much. In practice, that means the walls don't flex when you lean against them, and the material retains heat noticeably longer than thin builder-grade tubs. Multiple buyers noted they could soak for 30-40 minutes without adding hot water.

The white high-gloss finish is smooth and easy to clean with mild soap and water. But here's the honest downside: it's not scratch-proof, and hard water spots show up quickly. One reviewer summed it up: *"Beautiful tub, but you'll be wiping it down after every soak if you have mineral-heavy water."*

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The overwhelming theme in positive reviews is the depth. Over and over, buyers mention this is the first tub they've owned where their shoulders are actually submerged. One verified purchase wrote: *"I'm 5'10" and I can fully recline with water covering my chest. My old tub barely covered my hips."*

The installation story is also surprisingly positive for a freestanding tub. The pre-leveled base with adjustable feet earned repeated shout-outs. *"I didn't need a contractor. Two of us carried it in, leveled the feet with a wrench, and hooked up the drain in an afternoon,"* reported one buyer. The integrated floor-mount design means no exposed plumbing underneath — a cleaner look than many competitors.

**Where It Falls Short**

The overflow cover omission is the most common complaint. The tub has an overflow hole, but WOODBRIDGE doesn't include a matching cover plate. You'll need to source one separately, and finding a perfect color match for the white finish takes some hunting. It's a small part, but it's an annoying oversight at this price point.

Weight is another consideration. At about 90 pounds, it's not impossibly heavy, but it's awkward. Plan your delivery route through doorways and around corners before it arrives. And if you're installing on a second floor, verify your subfloor can handle the weight of the tub, water, and a person — that's roughly 700 pounds fully loaded.

The surface requires maintenance. Abrasive cleaners will dull the finish, and the white gloss shows every water spot. This isn't a tub for someone who wants zero upkeep.

**Who Should Buy This**

This tub fits three types of buyers: the DIY remodeler who wants a freestanding look without hiring a plumber; the tall person (5'9" or above) who's tired of shallow tubs; and anyone upgrading a guest bath or primary suite on a mid-range budget. Skip it if you need a tub with built-in jets, want a colored or matte finish, or have narrow doorways that a 29.5-inch-wide tub won't clear.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this tub require a special floor, or can it go on standard subfloor?**
It works on standard subfloor with the adjustable feet, but for second-floor installations, reinforce the joists if you're concerned about load. The feet spread weight reasonably well, but 700 pounds concentrated in a small area is worth planning for.

**Q: What faucet should I buy with this?**
Any freestanding floor-mount tub filler works. Measure the distance from the floor to the tub's rim (23.5 inches) and ensure the faucet's spout height clears that. You'll also need to position the faucet within reach of the drain connection.

**Q: Is it easy to clean the outside and underneath?**
The exterior is smooth and accessible since it's freestanding — you can wipe all sides. The underside is enclosed with the base plate, so you don't have to crawl under to clean. Just keep the surrounding floor dry to avoid water marks.
