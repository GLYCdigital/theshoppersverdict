Let me be blunt: most bathroom light fixtures under $60 are flimsy stamped metal with plastic shades that yellow within a year. The HAHZT Bathroom Light Fixture isn't that. It's a genuinely solid piece of hardware that punches above its price class, and the 4.7-star average across 235 ratings backs that up.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the assembled three-light fixture, a mounting plate, screws, wire nuts, and the decorative cover plate. As shown in the product image, it's a classic vanity bar design—three upward-facing clear glass shades on a brushed nickel or matte black finish (both options are available). The backplate measures roughly 24 inches wide, and the fixture extends about 7 inches from the wall. You'll need three E26 base bulbs, which are not included—a point worth noting before checkout.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

Here's where this fixture separates itself from the budget pack. The backplate and arms are aluminum, not that thin stamped steel that bends when you look at it wrong. During installation, the arms didn't flex when I tightened the mounting screws—a small thing that tells you the metal is actually thick enough to hold its shape. The glass shades are clear, not frosted, which matters for light output. With 60W equivalent LED bulbs, you get bright, even illumination across a double vanity. One reviewer noted, "The glass shades are heavier than I expected—feels like quality, not cheap." I'd agree.

The finish held up well during installation too. No scratches from the wrench, no chipping where the shades seat into the socket rings. For a bathroom environment, the finish is rated for damp locations, not wet—so install it above the vanity mirror, not directly in the shower zone.

**Where it falls short**

The included mounting hardware is adequate but not generous. The screws are on the shorter side, and if you're mounting over tile or a thick backsplash, you may need longer ones from your local hardware store. Several buyers mentioned this: "Wish they included longer screws—I had to make a second trip to Home Depot." Also, the fixture is hardwired only—there's no plug-in option, so if you're renting or don't want to cut into your drywall, this isn't the fixture for you.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the homeowner doing a bathroom refresh who wants a fixture that looks custom without the custom price. It's ideal for a 24-to-30-inch vanity where you need three bulbs of task lighting. It also works well in a powder room or hallway if you want the same style but don't need the full width—just be aware the three-light configuration is the only size available here.

**FAQ**

*Is it compatible with dimmer switches?*
Yes. It works with standard dimmers, but you'll need dimmable LED bulbs to avoid flickering.

*Can I install it myself?*
If you're comfortable with basic electrical work—turning off the breaker, connecting three wires—yes. It took me about 30 minutes. If wiring isn't your thing, budget for an electrician.

*What's the maximum bulb wattage?*
The fixture accepts up to 60W per socket, but modern LED bulbs at 8-10W will give you equivalent brightness with far less heat.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a well-built, good-looking vanity light that won't embarrass you when guests use the bathroom. The aluminum construction and clear glass shades deliver genuine quality at a budget price.

**Skip it if** you need a plug-in fixture, want lighting that points downward (some prefer that for vanity mirrors), or you're not prepared to buy bulbs and possibly longer screws separately.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The HAHZT bathroom light fixture delivers where it counts: solid metal, clean light, and easy installation. The short screws and missing bulbs are minor annoyances, not dealbreakers. At this price, it's an easy recommendation for anyone upgrading a bathroom without hiring a contractor.
