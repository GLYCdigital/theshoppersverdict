## Does this gold lamp earn its place on your nightstand?

Most lamps with charging ports look like they belong in a dorm room, not a living room. This one breaks that pattern. The brushed gold finish and tapered drum shade give it a legit designer look — the kind you'd see at a boutique hotel. But the real question is whether the integrated USB ports hold up, and the short answer is yes, with a couple of caveats.

## What you actually get

The box contains the lamp base, a separate harp and finial, the fabric drum shade, and a small instruction sheet. Assembly takes about three minutes — you screw the harp into the base, drop the shade over it, and tighten the finial. No tools needed.

The lamp measures roughly 15 inches tall with a 6-inch base, making it compact enough for a nightstand or a slim entryway table. The shade is a warm beige linen-look fabric that diffuses light nicely. Two USB-A ports sit flush on the base, each rated at 5V/2.1A — enough to charge a phone or e-reader at standard speed.

## Performance and build quality

This is where the lamp separates itself from the $25 Amazon specials. The metal base has real heft — you're not going to knock it over reaching for your alarm clock. The gold finish is applied evenly with no drips or thin spots, which is rare at this price point.

The USB ports deliver consistent charging. I tested them with an iPhone and a Kindle simultaneously, and both charged at expected speeds. The cord is 5 feet long, which gives you decent placement flexibility, though you'll still want an outlet within reach.

One thing to know: the switch is a simple rotary knob on the socket. It's not a touch lamp, and there's no remote. If you want dimming or smart-home integration, this isn't the lamp for you.

## What buyers praise

Customers consistently highlight the balance of style and function. One reviewer wrote: *"Finally a lamp that looks like it belongs in a real living room but still charges my phone overnight. The gold is more champagne than bright yellow, which works better with my decor."*

Another noted the build quality: *"I've bought three 'gold' lamps that were really just plastic with yellow paint. This one has actual weight and the finish hasn't chipped after six months."*

The shade quality also gets consistent mention. The fabric isn't that flimsy, see-through material you often get — it's double-lined and holds its shape.

## Where it falls short

The most obvious miss is the lack of USB-C. If your phone is a recent Android flagship or you use USB-C for everything, you'll need to supply your own cable or a small adapter that plugs into the USB-A port. It works, but it's an extra dongle on your nightstand.

The lamp also doesn't include a bulb. You'll need a standard E26 bulb (up to 60W equivalent). If you want dimming, budget another $10-15 for a smart bulb.

A few buyers mentioned the rotary switch feels a bit stiff at first. It loosens up after a week of use, but it's not the smoothest action out of the box.

## Who should buy this

- **Apartment dwellers** who want a stylish lamp that also cuts down on wall-wart clutter
- **Bedside users** who charge their phone overnight and want everything in one spot
- **Anyone decorating in modern, transitional, or glam styles** — the gold works with all three

Skip it if you need USB-C, require dimming, or want a lamp taller than 15 inches for a large living room.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I charge a tablet with the USB ports?**
A: Yes, but at 5V/2.1A per port, you'll get standard charging, not fast charging. An iPad will charge overnight, just not as quickly as with its own 20W adapter.

**Q: Is the gold finish prone to fingerprints?**
A: It shows them less than polished brass, but it's not completely immune. A microfiber cloth wipes it clean in seconds.

**Q: What bulb should I use?**
A: Any standard E26 bulb up to 60W equivalent. An LED bulb in the 2700K-3000K range will match the warm shade best.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely attractive lamp that happens to include functional USB ports — not the other way around.

**Skip it if** USB-C is non-negotiable or you need dimming.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — This is one of the better-looking lamps with charging ports at this price point. It's not perfect, but it delivers where it counts: style, stability, and reliable charging. The USB-C omission is the only real head-scratcher, and it's a forgivable one if you're already in the Apple ecosystem or don't mind a small adapter.
