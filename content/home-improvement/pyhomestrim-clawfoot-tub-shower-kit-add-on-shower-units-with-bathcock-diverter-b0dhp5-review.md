If you own a clawfoot tub, you already know the struggle: you love the vintage look, but you're tired of taking sponge baths or kneeling awkwardly to wash your hair. The Pyhomestrim Clawfoot Tub Shower Kit with bathcock diverter promises to fix that without ripping out your beloved tub. At 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, it's clearly working for many people — but let's dig into whether it's the right solution for your specific setup.

## What You Actually Get

The kit includes the bathcock diverter assembly, a handheld shower head with a 60-inch stainless steel hose, an overhead shower head, a wall-mount bracket, and the necessary mounting hardware. The diverter threads directly onto your existing tub spout — no plumber required for the basic install, assuming you have standard 1/2-inch NPT threads. The chrome finish over brass construction is what you'd expect at this price point: not showroom quality, but solidly built with no rough edges or flimsy plastic parts.

## Real-World Performance

The diverter mechanism is the heart of this kit. You pull up to send water to the shower heads, push down to fill the tub. The internal brass valve feels substantial, and the water flow to the shower head is strong — noticeably better than some cheaper plastic diverters I've tested that choke flow to a trickle. The handheld shower head has multiple spray settings that actually make a difference, not the gimmicky "everything feels the same" modes you see on budget units.

One thing that surprised me: the overhead shower arm attaches to the wall, not the tub. This is actually a smart design because it keeps the shower head at a usable height without drilling into your tub's porcelain. The included bracket is straightforward to mount, though you'll want to locate a stud or use heavy-duty drywall anchors.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The install simplicity is the most common thread in positive reviews. One verified buyer wrote, "Installed in 15 minutes, no leaks, and my clawfoot tub finally feels complete." Another reviewer highlighted the versatility: "I use the handheld 90% of the time for bathing kids and rinsing the tub. The overhead shower is a bonus for actual showers."

The finish quality also gets consistent love. Multiple reviewers mention it matches the look of much more expensive fixtures — one called it "the perfect finishing touch for our farmhouse bathroom renovation."

## Where It Falls Short

The most frequent complaint is the stiff diverter lever. Several reviewers note it takes more force than expected to switch between tub and shower modes. It does loosen up with use, but if you have arthritis or limited hand strength, this could be genuinely frustrating daily.

The 60-inch hose is adequate but not generous. If you're tall or have a particularly wide tub, the handheld won't reach all corners comfortably. One reviewer noted, "I'm 6'1\" and the hose is fine for showering but I have to stretch to reach the far end of the tub for cleaning."

A minor nitpick: the hose connections can develop slow drips if you don't use plumber's tape generously during install. This isn't a defect — it's standard for threaded connections — but the included tape is low quality. Buy a good roll of PTFE tape before you start.

## Who Should Buy This

This kit is ideal for DIY homeowners who want a functional shower solution for a clawfoot tub without replacing the entire fixture. It's perfect if your tub already has a standard spout with exposed threads, and you're comfortable with basic hand tools and a half-hour of your Saturday.

Skip it if you need a permanent, integrated shower system with a sliding rail or if your tub spout has an unusual thread size. Also skip it if you want a truly premium, designer-grade fixture — this is solidly mid-range, not high-end.

## FAQ

**Will this fit my tub?**
If your tub spout has standard 1/2-inch male threads (most clawfoot tubs do), it will fit. Measure the thread diameter before ordering to be certain.

**Can I use the handheld and overhead shower simultaneously?**
No, the diverter sends water to one or the other — not both at once. This is a minor limitation but standard for this type of kit.

**Does it come with everything I need for installation?**
Yes, including mounting bracket, hardware, and plumber's tape. You'll need your own adjustable wrench or pliers.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a reasonably priced, functional shower conversion for your clawfoot tub and don't mind a firm pull on the diverter lever. **Skip it if** you need a longer hose, want a premium fixture, or have non-standard thread sizes.

At 4.2/5, the Pyhomestrim kit delivers exactly what it promises: a straightforward, reliable way to shower in your vintage tub. It won't win design awards, but it will get you clean without a renovation. That's a win in my book.
