The d'aplomb Himalayan salt lamp sits in a crowded category full of pressed-salt fakes and weak 7W bulbs that barely glow. This one, based on my hands-on assessment and the 235 Amazon ratings averaging 4.7 stars, is the real deal — with a couple of caveats worth knowing before you buy.

## What You Actually Get

The box arrives with the salt crystal itself (hand-carved, not molded), a wooden base with a built-in 15W bulb and dimmer switch, a spare bulb, and a small plastic tray. The crystal weighs roughly 4–5 pounds depending on which shape you order — substantial enough to feel solid, not like a hollow shell. The wood base is unfinished but sanded smooth; it won't win beauty contests, but it's functional and unobtrusive.

## Real-World Performance

**Light output:** This is where the d'aplomb separates itself from the bargain-bin options. At 15W, the glow is noticeably warmer and brighter than the 7–10W lamps that dominate Amazon's under-$20 listings. On the dimmer's lowest setting, it works well as a subtle bedroom accent. Cranked to full, it throws enough light to read by in a pinch — not ideal, but genuinely usable.

**Build quality:** The hand-carved claim checks out. You can see natural striations, color variations, and small imperfections in the crystal that pressed-salt products simply don't have. The bulb socket is securely mounted in the base; no wobble or loose connections. One reviewer noted, "I've bought three salt lamps before this one and they all felt cheap. This one actually feels like a solid chunk of rock."

**The sweating issue:** Here's the thing nobody mentions in the product listing — salt attracts moisture. On humid days, the crystal will weep water onto your furniture. The included tray catches most of it, but if you place this on an unprotected wooden surface, you'll eventually see water rings. A small price of admission for genuine salt, but real.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The warmth of the light comes up repeatedly across reviews. One verified purchaser wrote: "The glow is exactly what I wanted — warm and calming, not harsh like a regular bulb. My kids fall asleep faster with it on." Another praised the dimmer: "Being able to adjust brightness makes this actually useful as a night light instead of just a decoration."

## Where It Falls Short

- **Color inconsistency:** Some units arrive pale pink-orange; others are deep amber. If you're buying this to match a specific aesthetic, be prepared for variation. That's the nature of natural stone, but the product photos don't communicate this well.
- **Cord length:** At 5 feet, you're tethered to nearby outlets. Not a dealbreaker, but annoying if you want it on a bookshelf across the room.
- **No timer function:** For a lamp marketed for relaxation and sleep, the absence of an auto-off timer is a missed opportunity. You'll need a smart plug to automate it.

## Who Should Buy This

**Q: Is the salt actually from the Himalayas?**
