## The Flush Pull That Actually Does Something

Most pocket door pulls are dumb metal bars. You grab them, you slide the door. That's it. The HOMOTEK Privacy Pocket Door Pull without Switch Plate is different — it packs a genuine privacy lock into a flush mount that barely breaks the plane of the door. If you're finishing a bathroom, bedroom, or home office with a pocket door, this is the hardware that makes the setup feel intentional rather than improvised.

## What's Actually in the Box

HOMOTEK ships the pull with the mounting hardware, a mortising template, and the privacy mechanism pre-assembled. You're getting the flush pull body, the locking spindle, and the strike plate. What you're *not* getting — and the name says it plainly — is a switch plate. If you planned to run a light switch through this same opening, that's a separate purchase. The packaging is straightforward, no surprises, and the template is genuinely usable, not the flimsy paper kind that tears on the first trace.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The pull is machined from zinc alloy with a satin finish that looks closer to brushed nickel than the cheap chrome you see on builder-grade hardware. In hand, it feels dense and substantial — no hollow rattle. The flush profile is the standout feature: once installed, the pull sits almost perfectly coplanar with the door face. That means no snagging on sleeves, no bruised hips in tight hallways, and a cleaner sightline than any protruding handle.

The privacy lock is where this pull separates from the pack. It uses a thumb-turn on the interior side and a small emergency release on the exterior — standard for privacy applications, but executed with a smooth, positive action. One buyer noted, *"The lock engages with a satisfying click, and the emergency release works even when the door is under pressure."* That's the kind of detail that matters when you're locked in a bathroom and the door has settled into the frame.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, the mortising. This is not a surface-mount pull. You need to route out a recess in the door edge and face to set the mechanism flush. The template helps, but if you've never used a router on a hollow-core door, you're in for a tense afternoon. Practice on scrap first.

Second, the lack of a switch plate is a real consideration. On a pocket door, running electrical for a light or outlet through the same cavity is common. HOMOTEK makes a version *with* the switch plate, but this one doesn't include it. Check your wiring plan before you buy, or you'll be making a second order.

Third, the finish, while attractive, is not scratch-proof. Satin surfaces show scuffs more readily than textured ones. It's a minor issue, but if you have kids who treat doors like playground equipment, expect to see wear.

## Who Should Buy This

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit a standard 1-3/8" hollow-core door?**
Yes, but you'll need to reinforce the cavity with blocking where the lock mechanism sits. The included template accounts for this, but don't skip the blocking step.

**Q: Is the emergency release accessible from the outside?**
Yes — there's a small slot for a flathead screwdriver or coin on the exterior face. It's discreet but functional.

**Q: Does the privacy lock work from both sides?**
The thumb-turn locks from the interior. The exterior has the emergency release only — it doesn't lock from outside, which is correct for privacy applications.
