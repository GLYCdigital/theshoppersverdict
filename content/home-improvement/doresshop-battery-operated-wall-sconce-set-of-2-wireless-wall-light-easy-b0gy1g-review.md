If you’ve been staring at a dark hallway, a windowless bathroom, or a rental wall you can’t drill into, the DORESshop Battery Operated Wall Sconce Set of 2 is the kind of product that makes you wonder why you didn’t buy it months ago. At 4.3 stars from 215 ratings, it’s not perfect — but for the price of a pizza dinner, it solves a genuinely annoying problem.

## What’s Actually in the Box

You get two complete sconces, two adhesive mounting plates, the screws and plastic anchors, a remote control (batteries not included for the remote), and the mounting template. Each sconce is roughly 8.5 inches tall by 4.5 inches wide — compact enough for a tight hallway but substantial enough to look intentional, not like a nightlight.

The fixtures themselves are metal with a matte black finish that reads more expensive than the price tag suggests. The "glass" shade is actually a frosted plastic diffuser, which is fine at this price point but don't expect crystal clarity.

## Performance: Real Brightness, Real Flexibility

Here's where the DORESshop sconces surprise. Many battery sconces max out at a faint glow. These put out a legitimate 300 lumens at full brightness — enough to read by, to light a bathroom vanity, or to keep a staircase visible at night. The color temperature adjustability is the standout feature: three presets from warm 2700K to cool 6000K, plus stepless dimming. I set mine to warm white at 40% brightness for the bedroom and it looks like a hardwired fixture.

The remote works from across the room, which is essential once you realize the sconces don't have side-mounted switches. Each unit has a small push button on the front — fine for turning on, but you'll use the remote for dimming and color changes.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The no-drill install is the headline. One reviewer wrote: *"I'm a renter and I've been wanting sconces for two years. These went up in 10 minutes with the adhesive strips and they haven't budged in four months."* Another noted the battery life is real: *"I've had them on over 2 hours a night for six weeks and the battery indicator still shows full."*

The included remote is genuinely useful — you can sync both sconces to one remote, which means one button press controls both sides of your bed.

## Where It Falls Short

The adhesive strips are the weak point. They're great on smooth, flat paint, but several reviewers report failures on textured walls or wallpaper. The screws are included, but they're short — if your drywall needs anchoring, you'll want longer ones from a hardware store. Also, the battery compartment door has a design flaw on some units: it's stiff and requires a tool to pry open. One reviewer said, *"I nearly scratched the paint off trying to get the battery door open. Once I figured out the trick, it was fine, but it was frustrating."*

## Who Should Buy This

**Renters** who want the look of hardwired sconces without deposit-damaging holes. **Anyone with a windowless hallway or closet** in need of practical light. **DIY-averse homeowners** who want a quick weekend upgrade. Skip it if you need a primary light source for a large room — these are accent and task lights, not room-filling fixtures.

## FAQ

**How long do the batteries actually last?**
At 2-3 hours daily use on medium brightness, expect 4-6 months from quality AA batteries. Continuous use at full brightness will drain them in about 3 weeks.

**Can I mount these outdoors?**
No. They're not weather-rated. Indoor use only — a covered porch might work if they never get wet, but I wouldn't risk it.

**Do I need the remote for every function?**
Yes for dimming and color changes. The front button only toggles on/off at your last-used setting.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want hardwired-looking lighting without the hardwiring, and you have smooth, flat walls. **Skip it if** your walls are textured, you need a primary light source, or you're unwilling to manage AA batteries.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — a genuinely useful product with a minor battery-door annoyance and adhesive limitations. The value is undeniable.
