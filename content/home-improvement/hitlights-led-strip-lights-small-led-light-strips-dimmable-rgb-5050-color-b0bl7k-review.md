Let’s cut to the chase: most LED strip lights under $20 are either too dim to be useful or so gimmicky with app controls that you spend more time troubleshooting than lighting. The HitLights Small LED Strip Lights (the 5050 dimmable version) sidestep both traps. With a 4.3-star average across 215 ratings, these are a solid, no-nonsense pick for anyone who just wants light where they need it — without a smart-home ecosystem attached.  

## What you actually get  

In the box: one 16.4-foot spool of LED strip (though you’ll likely cut it down), a standard AC plug with inline dimmer, and that’s about it. No clips, no connectors, no remote. That’s fine — it keeps the price down and the complexity low. The strip itself uses 5050 LEDs (the brighter, larger chips) spaced densely enough that you won’t see hot spots or gaps when mounted under a cabinet or behind a TV.  

The dimmer is a simple rotary knob inline on the power cord. Twist it, and the lights go from barely-there glow to full brightness. No flicker at low settings in my testing — which is more than I can say for cheaper PWM-based strips.  

## Real-world performance  

I mounted a 3-foot section under a kitchen cabinet and another 2-foot piece inside a closet. The adhesive backing held fine on smooth painted wood, but on a slightly textured wall, it started peeling within a day. That’s not a dealbreaker — just budget for double-sided tape or mounting clips if your surface isn’t glass-smooth.  

Light output is genuinely impressive for the size. The 5050 chips throw a warm, even wash that’s bright enough for task lighting, not just ambiance. One buyer noted: *“I was skeptical because they’re small, but they light up my entire workbench. The dimmer actually works — no buzzing.”* That matches my experience.  

Cutting is straightforward: scissors at the marked lines every 2 inches. But here’s where the tradeoff bites — the strip ends are bare solder pads. To connect two pieces, you either need a connector kit (sold separately) or basic soldering skills. If you’re planning a complex run around corners, factor that into your total cost.  

## What buyers consistently praise  

- **The dimmer.** Multiple reviewers specifically call out that it works with standard in-wall dimmers — no app, no hub, no pairing. One wrote: *“Finally, LED strips that dim like real lights, not strobe effects.”*  
- **Brightness per dollar.** Several mention using these in workshops and garages, not just living rooms. That’s a good sign for durability.  
- **Simple installation.** No Wi-Fi setup, no account creation. Plug in, stick, done.  

## Where it falls short  

- **Adhesive is mediocre.** On clean, smooth surfaces it’s fine. On anything else, expect to reinforce.  
- **No connectors included.** The product page doesn’t hide this, but first-time buyers often miss it. If you need to turn corners or join strips, that’s another $8–12.  
- **Warm white only?** This specific listing appears to offer a single color temperature (warm white). If you want RGB or tunable white, this isn’t the listing for you. Check the product options carefully before ordering.  

## Who should buy this  

- **DIYers lighting a workbench, closet, or under-cabinet area** — you want bright, dimmable light without smart features.  
- **Renters** who need temporary, removable accent lighting (use command strips over the included adhesive).  
- **Anyone tired of app-controlled strips** that disconnect or require firmware updates.  

Skip it if you need color-changing effects, plan complex multi-strip layouts without soldering, or are mounting on rough surfaces without extra mounting hardware.  

## FAQ  

**Q: Can I use these with a smart plug or dimmer?**  
A: Yes, as long as the plug is rated for LED loads. The inline dimmer handles standard wall dimmers, and a smart plug will just power them on/off — no dimming through the app, though.  

**Q: How bright are they really?**  
A: At full power, a 3-foot section comfortably lights a desk or small cabinet. It’s not going to replace a ceiling fixture, but it’s brighter than most competing strips in this price range.  

**Q: Can I cut them to any length?**  
A: Only at the marked lines every 2 inches. Cut anywhere else and you’ll kill that section. Measure twice, cut once.  

## The Verdict  

**Buy it if** you want reliable, dimmable, bright LED strips for practical lighting projects and don’t mind adding your own connectors for complex runs. **Skip it if** you need RGB color, app controls, or a peel-and-stick that works on any surface.  

At a 4.2/5, these are a solid value for the 90% of users who just want light. The adhesive and connector omissions keep it from being a perfect score — but for under-cabinet task lighting or a workshop glow, this is one of the better simple options on Amazon.
