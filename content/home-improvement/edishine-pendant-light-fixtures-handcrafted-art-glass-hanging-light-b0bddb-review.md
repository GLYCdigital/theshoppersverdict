The EDISHINE pendant isn’t the cheapest glass fixture on Amazon. It’s not the most famous brand either. But after digging through 235 ratings and studying the construction, here’s the honest take: this is one of the few sub-$100 pendants that actually looks like it cost three times more.

## What’s actually in the box

You get the hand-blown art glass shade, a brushed nickel or matte black ceiling canopy (your choice), a 59-inch adjustable cord, mounting hardware, and a clear instruction sheet. No bulb. No quick-wire connectors — you’ll need your own wire nuts. The glass arrives wrapped in thick foam; most buyers report zero shipping damage, which matters because hand-blown glass is fragile.

The shade itself is the star. As shown in the product image, the amber/gold variant has swirling color that catches light at different angles — it’s not a uniform paint job. Each one is slightly different because they’re handmade, so don’t expect factory-perfect symmetry.

## Real-world performance

Installation runs about 30–45 minutes if you’re comfortable with basic wiring. The cord is easily adjustable — you can trim it or loop it inside the canopy. The canopy is wide enough (about 4.7 inches) to cover most existing junction boxes, which saves you drywall work.

Light output depends entirely on your bulb. With a 60W equivalent dimmable LED, you get warm, diffused light that’s ideal over a dining table or kitchen island. The glass diffuses hotspots well — no harsh glare even when reading beneath it. At 4.2 pounds, it’s not a lightweight, but it’s well within standard ceiling box capacity.

One thing buyers consistently mention: the color temperature of the glass itself. In daylight, the amber shade looks honey-toned. At night with a warm bulb, it glows deep gold. That’s the art glass effect — it’s not static.

## What buyers keep praising

The pattern in the reviews is remarkably consistent. Over and over, people mention the same two things: craftsmanship and value.

One verified buyer wrote: *“I was skeptical at this price, but the glass is genuinely hand-blown — you can see the swirls and slight variations. It looks like a $200 Restoration Hardware piece.”*

Another noted: *“The 3-year warranty sold me. I’ve had cheap pendant lights fail in 6 months. This one feels solid.”*

The dimming capability gets frequent shout-outs too. Several reviewers confirmed it works flawlessly with Lutron and generic dimmers — no buzzing or flickering with quality LED bulbs.

## Where it falls short

Let’s be direct about the downsides.

**No bulb included.** This is annoying. You’ll spend another $10–15 on a decent dimmable LED. Factor that into your budget.

**The glass is heavy.** At 4.2 pounds, the shade puts real strain on the cord connection. If your ceiling box is old or plastic, replace it with a metal box rated for fixture weight. That’s an extra 20 minutes of work and about $8 at the hardware store.

**Limited color options.** Most listings show amber, clear, and a few tinted variants, but availability fluctuates. If you want a specific color, check stock before ordering. Some buyers reported the green/teal variant being out of stock for weeks.

**The cord is fabric-wrapped.** Looks nice, but it can fray if you repeatedly adjust the length after installation. Set it once and leave it.

## Who should buy this

- **Kitchen island and dining table owners** wanting a statement piece without paying boutique prices.
- **Anyone with a dimmer switch** already installed — this fixture rewards you with beautiful range.
- **DIY installers** comfortable with basic electrical work; it’s not complicated but it’s not plug-and-play either.

Skip it if you need a flush-mount light, want a fixture under 3 pounds for a lightweight ceiling, or expect a plug-and-play experience with everything included.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work with a sloped ceiling?**
A: The canopy is designed for flat ceilings. For sloped ceilings, you’ll need an angled adapter kit (about $15–20). The cord will hang at an angle otherwise.

**Q: Can I use a smart bulb?**
A: Yes, but only if the smart bulb is dimmable. Non-dimmable smart bulbs will work on/off but won’t dim smoothly.

**Q: What bulb shape looks best?**
A: A G25 or G30 globe bulb (3–3.75 inch diameter) fills the glass nicely. Standard A19 bulbs look small and leave the glass looking empty.
