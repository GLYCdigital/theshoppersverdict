## What’s in the box

You get ten pulls, each with two mounting screws. The screws come in two lengths — 1-inch and 1.25-inch — which covers most cabinet door thicknesses (standard is ¾-inch, but the extra length helps if you have shaker-style doors or added paneling). You’ll need a Phillips head screwdriver or a drill with a #2 bit. No template is included, so grab a ruler and a pencil. Each pull measures 5 inches on center, with an overall length of about 5.5 inches from end to end.

## Build quality and real-world use

The body is stainless steel with a matte black coating — and it feels denser than the hollow aluminum pulls you’ll find at big-box stores. When you tighten the screws, the bar doesn’t flex or twist, which tells you the metal is thick enough to handle daily use without warping. The grip area is comfortable; fingers slide naturally, and the rounded ends prevent snagging on clothing or dish towels.

Installation took about 15 minutes for ten doors. The screws thread cleanly into the pulls — no cross-threading, no stripped heads. The one thing to watch: the matte finish, while scratch-resistant, does show fingerprints and light grease smudges. In a kitchen that sees heavy cooking, you’ll want to wipe these down weekly. It’s not a dealbreaker, but it’s worth knowing before you commit.
