If you've ever mixed a 60-pound bag of thinset with a standard drill, you know the feeling: the bit grabs, the handle twists, and your wrist pays the price. DEWALT's FLEXVOLT 60V MAX drill with the E-Clutch system exists specifically to end that pain. At 4.8 stars from 240 ratings, it's become the go-to for contractors and serious DIYers who mix mud, mortar, and grout regularly. But it's not for everyone — and the price of admission is steep if you're not already in the FLEXVOLT ecosystem.

## What's Actually in the Box

This is a bare tool listing. You get the drill, a side handle, and a depth stop rod. No battery, no charger, no mixing paddle. If you're new to FLEXVOLT, factor in another $200-$250 for a 60V battery and charger. If you already own DEWALT 20V tools, the FLEXVOLT battery cross-compatibly slides into those tools too — which softens the sting considerably.

## Performance: Where This Drill Earns Its Keep

The headline feature is the E-Clutch system. Unlike a mechanical clutch that disengages gears, this uses an electronic brake that stops the motor the instant it detects a bind. In practice, it's remarkably fast — fast enough that when the paddle catches a rock or hits the bottom of a mixing bucket, the drill just stops. No violent twist, no dropped tool, no sprained wrist. One reviewer put it simply: *"The E-clutch is a game-changer. I was skeptical until the first time it kicked in. It saved my arm and probably a trip to urgent care."*

The motor delivers genuine 60V power. At 0-550 RPM, it's not about speed — it's about torque. The drill churns through 80-pound bags of concrete mix without bogging down, and the variable speed trigger lets you feather the RPMs for thinner materials like self-leveling compound where you want less splatter.

The all-metal chuck grips mixing paddles up to 1/2-inch shank securely. The side handle threads into two positions, giving you leverage options depending on whether you're mixing on the ground or on a workbench.

## What Buyers Praise (and What They Grumble About)

The consensus across the 240 ratings is clear: this drill handles mixing chores that would smoke a standard 20V drill. Multiple reviewers noted they'd previously burned through two or three corded drills before switching. One contractor wrote: *"I've mixed over 200 bags of thinset with this. It's still going strong. My old corded drill died after 50."*

Battery life is the other big win. With a 6Ah FLEXVOLT battery, most users report getting through 4-6 bags of mortar per charge — enough for a solid day of tiling work on one battery.

The complaints are mostly physical, not mechanical. At over 8 pounds with battery and paddle, it's heavy. Overhead mixing — like stirring bucket mud while working on a ceiling — gets exhausting quickly. A few reviewers also noted the paddle that DEWALT recommends (the 1-1/2-inch mixing paddle) isn't included, which adds another $40 to the total cost.

## Who Should Buy This

This drill makes sense for a specific buyer: someone who mixes mortar, thinset, grout, or concrete regularly — at least a few times a month. Tile setters, masons, and serious DIYers tackling large renovation projects will get their money's worth. The E-Clutch alone justifies the price if you've ever had a drill wrench your wrist.

Skip it if you're a casual DIYer who mixes a bag of thinset twice a year. A 20V drill with a mixing paddle will handle light-duty work, and you'll save $200+. Also skip it if you need an all-purpose drill — this is a specialty tool, not a daily driver.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this drill also work as a regular drill?**
Technically yes, but it's not practical. At 0-550 RPM, it's far too slow for drilling holes in wood or metal. Keep a standard drill for those tasks. This is a dedicated mixer.

**Q: Will a 20V DEWALT battery work?**
No. This requires FLEXVOLT 60V batteries. However, those 60V batteries will power your 20V DEWALT tools, so the investment carries over.

**Q: How many bags of mortar can I mix per charge?**
With a 6Ah FLEXVOLT battery, most users report 4-6 bags of thinset or mortar per charge. Heavier mixes like concrete will get fewer.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You mix mortar, thinset, or concrete professionally or on major renovation projects. The E-Clutch is a genuine safety feature that will save your wrist, and the power is undeniably real.

**Skip it if:** You're a light-duty DIYer, or if you need a versatile drill for multiple tasks. This is a one-trick pony — but it does that trick exceptionally well.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Loses half a star for the weight and the bare-tool pricing, but for its intended purpose, this is the best cordless mixer on the market.
