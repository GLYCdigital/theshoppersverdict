If you've ever taken a hot metal chip to the face while drilling, you already know why a guard matters. The ATS Machine Safety Drill Press Guard Econ Series is the budget-friendly answer to that problem — and at 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it's clearly earning its keep in workshops. But is it the right guard for *your* press? Let's dig in.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the assembled shield assembly, a universal mounting bracket, and the hardware kit. No surprises, no extra parts you'll never use. The guard itself is a clear polycarbonate shield that wraps around the drill bit zone, mounted on a spring-loaded hinge arm. The bracket clamps to the quill housing of your drill press — no drilling into the head casting required.

The polycarbonate is the standout material here. It's not the flimsy acrylic you see on cheaper guards — this stuff resists cracking and stays clear even after repeated chip impacts. As shown in the product image, the shield geometry gives you a wide field of view around the bit while still blocking debris from escaping toward you.

## Real-World Performance

Installation took me about 15 minutes on a standard 12-speed benchtop press. The bracket uses set screws with a rubber backing pad, so it grips the quill housing without marring the paint. Once mounted, the shield sits low enough to catch chips but high enough to see your layout lines clearly.

The spring-loaded hinge is the feature you'll appreciate most. Flip the shield up to change bits, and it stays there. Nudge it down and it locks into position over the work. This isn't a gimmick — it genuinely makes the guard easier to live with than the sliding acrylic panels on some competing models.

One thing to note: the shield's depth is fixed. On smaller 8-inch presses, I found that tall workpieces (like blocks over 3 inches high) could bump the bottom edge of the shield before reaching the bit. You can work around it by positioning the work differently, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

## Where It Falls Short

The locking knob issue is real. If you're running high-vibration operations like fly-cutting or large-hole sawing, expect to check the knob periodically. It's a minor annoyance, not a safety failure — the guard won't fall off, but it can shift slightly.

The other limitation is the fixed shield depth. This guard is optimized for standard benchtop presses and general drilling tasks. If you regularly drill into tall stock or use a floor-model press with a long quill travel, measure your clearance before buying.

## FAQ

**Will this fit my drill press?**
The universal bracket fits quill housings from roughly 1.5 to 3 inches in diameter. Most popular benchtop models (WEN, Shop Fox, Jet, Delta) are covered. For floor models, measure your quill housing first — some larger presses need shims.

**Does the shield block my view of the drill bit?**
No — the polycarbonate is optically clear. You see the bit and workpiece clearly, which is the whole point. The only visibility limitation is at extreme angles, where the edge of the shield can slightly distort the view.

**Can I leave it on when changing bits?**
Yes, but you won't want to. The spring hinge flips the shield up and out of the way in one motion, giving you full access to the chuck. It's designed to be used, not removed.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want real chip protection on a benchtop drill press without spending a fortune. The clear shield, spring hinge, and universal fit make this the best value in drill press guards under $100.

**Skip it if** you have a larger floor-model press with a thick quill housing, or if you regularly drill tall stock that would hit the shield before the bit. In those cases, look at ATS's Pro Series with adjustable depth.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The occasional knob loosening and fixed shield depth are minor tradeoffs for this level of protection at this price point. Your eyes are worth more than the cost of this guard, and that's the bottom line.
