## FAQ

**Q: Can I bury this directly in the ground without conduit?**
A: Yes, UF-B is rated for direct burial at 24 inches deep per NEC. However, local codes can require more depth or conduit — verify with your building department before you dig.

**Q: Is this the same as Romex (NM-B)?**
A: No. NM-B is for indoor, dry locations. UF-B has a solid, moisture-resistant jacket and is rated for underground and wet locations. Don't use them interchangeably.

**Q: What size breaker do I need for 12/2 UF?**
A: A 20-amp breaker is the maximum for 12-gauge copper. If you're feeding a sub-panel, size the breaker to the panel's rating and your load calculation.
