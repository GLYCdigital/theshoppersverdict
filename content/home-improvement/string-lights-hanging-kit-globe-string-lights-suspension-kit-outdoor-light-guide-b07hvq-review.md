## FAQ

**Q: Can this kit support heavy commercial globe string lights?**
A: Yes. The 1/16-inch galvanized cable has a working load limit well above what any residential string light setup will put on it. Even double strands of heavier patio lights are fine.

**Q: Do I need to pre-drill holes for the screw eyes?**
A: For softwoods like pine or cedar, you can drive them directly. For hardwoods or composite materials, pre-drill a pilot hole slightly smaller than the screw eye shaft to prevent splitting.

**Q: How do I keep the cable from sagging in the middle?**
A: Use the turnbuckles. Start loose, hang the lights, then tighten both turnbuckles gradually until the cable is straight. For runs over 25 feet, consider adding a center support post.
