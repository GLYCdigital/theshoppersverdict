If you've been scrolling past a dozen brass sconces that all look like they came off the same factory line, this Alabaster Gold Long Wall Sconce is the one that'll make you stop. It's not the cheapest option, and it's not trying to be. What it is: a genuinely well-made fixture that solves a specific problem—long empty wall space above a console, a king bed, or a hallway that swallows standard-size sconces whole.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains one complete sconce: the antique brass backplate and arm, the alabaster stone shade, mounting hardware, and clear instructions. The fixture measures roughly 14 inches in length and extends about 7 inches from the wall, so it reads as a deliberate design statement rather than an afterthought. The alabaster is real stone—you can see natural veining and subtle color variation when the light's on. It's not a plastic imitation, and that matters when you're paying for this aesthetic.

The brass finish deserves specific mention. It's a brushed antique tone, not the shiny gold that screams "builder grade." It has depth—darker in the crevices, lighter on the edges. In the product photos, you'll notice the warm glow against the stone; in person, the finish has a slightly aged patina that photographs well but looks even better in low evening light.

**Real-World Performance**

This sconce takes a standard E12 candelabra bulb, and the difference between a cheap bulb and a quality one is night and day. With a 60-watt equivalent LED, the alabaster glows evenly—no hot spots, no harsh shadow lines. It's genuinely ambient lighting, not task lighting. If you're installing above a reading nook, keep that in mind; you'll want a separate floor lamp for actual reading.

One thing buyers consistently mention is the weight. This isn't a hollow, rattly fixture. One reviewer put it simply: "I've installed a lot of sconces, and this one felt solid in my hands. The stone is heavy, the arm is sturdy, and it doesn't wobble." That tracks with the build quality. The backplate sits flush against the wall, and the included mounting bracket is substantial enough to handle the weight without sagging.

**What Buyers Love**

The praise consistently centers on three things: the quality of the alabaster, the finish depth, and how it anchors a room. One reviewer noted, "I was nervous about the gold being too shiny, but it's muted and elegant. The stone is the star." Another said, "It looks like a $400 sconce. I paid half that." The long profile gets its own shoutouts—people using it above queen beds or in stairwells appreciate not having to buy two fixtures to fill the same space.

**Where It Falls Short**

Be honest about the installation. This is hardwired only—no cord-and-plug version exists. You need an existing junction box or a licensed electrician if you don't have one. That adds cost and planning time.

The alabaster is porous. In a bathroom with steam and humidity, you'll want to apply a light mineral oil treatment periodically to keep water spots from etching the stone. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's maintenance you should know about going in.

And while most buyers report fine dimmer performance, a few noted buzzing with certain LED bulbs. If you plan to dim, match it with a compatible LED dimmer and quality bulbs—don't cheap out there.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The console-table stylist**: You've got a long entryway table and a blank wall. This fills it perfectly.
- **The bedroom upgrade seeker**: Above a king headboard, the 14-inch length balances the scale without overpowering.
- **The hallway renovator**: One fixture per hallway segment beats two undersized sconces every time.

**Skip it if** you need plug-in lighting, want a pure white light (this is warm, cozy light only), or you're on a strict budget—there are cheaper brass sconces, but they won't have the stone quality or finish depth.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use LED bulbs with this sconce?**
A: Yes, and you should. Standard E12 candelabra base LED bulbs work fine. Stick to 2700K warm white for the best glow through the alabaster.

**Q: Is the brass finish going to tarnish over time?**
A: It's sealed, so it won't tarnish like raw brass. Wipe with a dry cloth occasionally; avoid harsh cleaners.

**Q: Can this be installed upside down or sideways?**
A: It's designed for vertical installation with the shade facing down. Sideways mounting would look odd and risk the stone shade shifting.
