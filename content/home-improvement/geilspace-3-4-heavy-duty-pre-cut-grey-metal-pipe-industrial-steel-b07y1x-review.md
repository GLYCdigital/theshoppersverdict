**Q: Is this pipe suitable for gas or water lines?**
A: No. This is structural/furniture-grade pipe, not certified for pressurized plumbing or gas. Use it for shelving, racks, and decorative builds.

**Q: What's the actual outside diameter?**
A: 3/4" is the nominal trade size. The actual outside diameter is approximately 1.05 inches, and the threading is standard NPT, so standard 3/4" fittings will mate correctly.

**Q: Can I cut these to shorter lengths?**
A: Technically yes, but you'll lose the threading on the cut end and need a die to re-thread. Better to order the closest pre-cut length or plan your design around the available sizes.
