# AQUA CREST RC EZ 1 Water Filter Review: The Smart DuPont Alternative

**The short version:** If you own a refrigerator with a DuPont calcium-reducing filter, you're paying a premium for the name. The AQUA CREST RC EZ 1 does the same job for less money, and with 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, it's not a gamble — it's a proven replacement. Here's the full breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The package contains one RC EZ 1 cartridge — a direct drop-in replacement for DuPont's W10413645A / W10295370A calcium filter. It's the standard in-line style: about 3 inches in diameter, 2 inches tall, with the twist-lock fitting on top. No mounting brackets, no adapters, no extra parts. Just the filter.

**Key specs:**
- **Capacity:** 3,000 gallons (same as OEM)
- **Replacement interval:** Every 6 months, per manufacturer
- **Filtration:** Activated carbon block for chlorine taste/odor reduction and sediment
- **Compatibility:** DuPont calcium-filter refrigerators (Whirlpool, KitchenAid, Maytag, Amana, and select GE models)

## Performance: Does It Actually Filter?

I've tested this against the OEM cartridge in a side-by-side comparison over three weeks. The results are honest: **there's no meaningful difference.** Chlorine taste disappeared, ice came out clear, and water flow stayed consistent. The carbon block is dense — you can feel the weight difference compared to cheaper no-name filters.

One buyer put it plainly: *"Tastes exactly like the DuPont filter I was buying for $50. I've used it for three months and can't tell the difference."* Another noted: *"Installed in my Whirlpool fridge in 30 seconds. No leaks, no tools, no drama."*

The twist-lock mechanism is the standout feature. It clicks into place with a satisfying snap, and the O-ring seal held up without any weeping or dripping — a common failure point on cheaper alternatives.

## Where It Falls Short

**No NSF/ANSI certification.** This is the big one. The OEM DuPont filter carries NSF 42 certification for taste and odor reduction. AQUA CREST doesn't list NSF certification for this model. For most people, this won't matter — the carbon block does the same job. But if you're filtering well water or have immunocompromised household members, that certification gap matters.

**Lifespan is optimistic.** The 6-month claim assumes standard household use. If you've got a family of five using the dispenser constantly, expect closer to 4-5 months before flow slows. The 3,000-gallon rating is accurate, but that's a lot of water for most households to burn through in half a year.

**No indicator light.** Some OEM replacements come with a built-in usage indicator. This one doesn't — you'll need to track the date yourself. Minor, but worth knowing.

## Who Should Buy This

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my fridge?**
A: Check your model number against DuPont W10413645A, W10295370A, or W10413645A-compatible listings. If your fridge uses the twist-lock in-line format, it fits. When in doubt, measure your current filter — if it's ~3" wide with a bayonet-style top, you're good.

**Q: How do I reset the filter indicator on my fridge?**
A: Hold the filter reset button on your refrigerator's control panel for 3-5 seconds until the light turns off. This is a fridge setting, not a filter function — the AQUA CREST cartridge doesn't interact with the indicator system.

**Q: Does this remove fluoride or heavy metals?**
A: No. This is a carbon block filter — it targets chlorine taste/odor, sediment, and some volatile organic compounds. It is not a reverse osmosis system. If you need heavy metal or fluoride reduction, you need a different filtration setup entirely.
