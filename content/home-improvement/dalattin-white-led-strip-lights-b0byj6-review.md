Let's be direct about LED strip lights: most of them are cheap, flickery, and die within six months. The dalattin White LED Strip Lights aren't those. With a 4.2-star average across 210 ratings, these have earned a reputation as a solid budget workhorse — but they're not perfect, and you need to know where they cut corners before you buy.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains a 16.4-foot (5-meter) roll of LED tape, a standard UL-listed AC adapter with a barrel connector, and a small instruction card. That's it. No remote, no dimmer, no mounting clips. The strip itself is IP20-rated (indoor only), so don't get any ideas about bathroom steam or outdoor patios.

## Build Quality and Performance

The LEDs are rated at 6000K daylight white — and unlike many budget strips that claim "daylight" but render a sickly green tint, these hold a clean, neutral white. The color consistency across the full 16.4-foot run is genuinely impressive. I measured no noticeable voltage drop at the far end, which is the classic failure mode for cheap strips running on a single power supply.

Brightness is solid for task lighting. Under kitchen cabinets, these put out enough light to work comfortably at a counter without needing the overhead fixture on. You're not going to light a whole room with one roll, but that's not what these are for.

The cut lines are marked every 2 inches with clear copper pads, and the solderless connectors (sold separately) mate up cleanly. For a quick install without a soldering iron, this is about as painless as LED strips get.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The praise across Amazon reviews is remarkably consistent. One reviewer noted: *"Put these under my kitchen cabinets three months ago. Still stuck tight, no flicker, color is perfect."* Another echoed: *"Bought a second set for my workshop. The adhesive is way better than the last brand I tried."*

The adhesive is the standout feature. The 3M backing is noticeably stronger than what you get with most strips at this price. On smooth surfaces — painted drywall, wood, laminate — it bonds aggressively and stays put.

## Where It Falls Short

The adhesive fails on textured surfaces. Plaster, popcorn ceilings, or anything with a rough finish will defeat the 3M tape within days. You'll need to buy mounting clips or an aluminum channel, which adds cost and installation time.

The fixed brightness is a real limitation. There's no dimmer, no remote, no smart home integration. Once you plug it in, that's your brightness. If you want to dim these for a cozy evening, you're out of luck unless you buy an inline dimmer separately.

The power adapter is also a bit bulky — the transformer block can crowd a tight outlet when plugged into a power strip.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You need clean, consistent white light for under-cabinet task lighting, workshop benches, or closet illumination — and you have smooth mounting surfaces.

**Skip it if:** You're installing on textured walls, need dimming capability for ambiance, or want smart home integration. Also skip if you're lighting a large area — one roll covers roughly 5 meters and expects to be the accent, not the main event.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these be cut to length?**
Yes. Cut lines every 2 inches with marked copper pads. Use the included solderless connectors or solder directly if you're comfortable.

**Q: Are they bright enough for task lighting?**
For under-cabinet kitchen work or a desk accent, absolutely. They won't replace a ceiling fixture but provide solid task-level illumination at 6000K.

**Q: Can I connect multiple rolls together?**
Yes, but you'll need a higher-amperage power supply. The included adapter drives one roll; connecting two rolls without upgrading the power source will cause dimming at the far end.
