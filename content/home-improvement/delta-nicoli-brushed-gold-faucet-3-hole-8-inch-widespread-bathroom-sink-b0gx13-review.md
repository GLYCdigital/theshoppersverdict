Let's cut through the catalog copy. The Delta Nicoli in brushed gold is getting a lot of attention, and with 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, it's not just hype. But before you click "add to cart," you need to know what you're actually getting — and what you're not.

## What's Actually in the Box

Delta ships the Nicoli as a three-piece widespread set: two lever handles and a central spout. You get the supply lines (standard 3/8-inch compression fittings) and mounting hardware. What you **don't** get is a pop-up drain assembly — that's a separate purchase, typically $20–40 depending on finish matching. Factor that into your total cost.

The faucet measures 8 inches between the handle centers, which is the standard widespread dimension for U.S. bathroom sinks. The spout reaches 5.5 inches forward and stands about 6.5 inches tall — enough clearance for washing hands without splashing your backsplash.

## Build Quality and the Finish Question

Here's where the Nicoli earns its keep. The brushed gold isn't a spray-on coating; it's applied via PVD (physical vapor deposition). That matters. PVD bonds at the molecular level, which means it won't chip, peel, or fade the way cheaper electroplated finishes do. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"I was nervous about gold looking cheap, but this has a soft, warm tone that looks expensive. Six months in, zero water spots or tarnish."*

The body is solid brass — not zinc alloy, not plastic. Pick it up and you'll feel the heft. The lever handles operate with a smooth, damped action, and the ceramic disc cartridges are the same internals Delta uses in faucets twice this price.

## Real-World Performance

The Nicoli delivers 1.2 gallons per minute, which meets WaterSense standards without feeling weak. The aerator produces a consistent, non-splashing stream — useful when your sink basin is on the shallower side.

Installation is straightforward if you've done a faucet before. The three-piece design means you can set each component independently, which is actually easier than wrestling a one-piece unit into place. The supply lines are pre-attached, saving you a trip under the sink with a wrench. One buyer noted: *"Took me about 45 minutes including the drain I bought separately. The instructions were clear, and the mounting nuts are brass, not plastic."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**The drain situation is annoying.** At this price point, Delta should include a matching pop-up drain. Instead, you're hunting for a brushed gold drain that matches — and if you buy a cheap one, the finish won't match, and it'll stand out like a sore thumb.

**The 8-inch spread is non-negotiable.** If your vanity has a 4-inch centerset faucet or a single-hole configuration, this won't work. You'll need to drill new holes in your countertop or granite, which is a job for a pro. Measure your sink **before** ordering.

**Gold is polarizing.** Brushed gold has a warm, almost champagne undertone. If your fixtures are polished brass or antique bronze, this won't match. It's specifically designed to complement warm neutrals, white marble, and dark wood vanities.

## Who Should Buy This

The Nicoli is perfect for three specific buyers:

1. **The renovator** doing a full bathroom remodel in a warm-neutral palette — white shaker cabinets, marble tile, brushed gold accessories.
2. **The upgrade seeker** who wants a premium look without paying $400+ for a designer brand. This delivers 90% of the aesthetic at half the price.
3. **The practical homeowner** who values a lifetime warranty. Delta's Diamond Seal technology means you'll never replace a washer, and the limited lifetime warranty covers parts and finish.

Skip it if you need a centerset faucet, if your sink has non-standard hole spacing, or if you're working with a cool-toned palette (chrome, nickel, or matte black fixtures).

## FAQ

**Q: Does this faucet fit a standard 3-hole sink?**
A: Yes, but the holes must be 8 inches apart (center-to-center). Standard U.S. widespread faucets use 8-inch spacing. If your sink has 6-inch or 4-inch spacing, it won't fit.

**Q: Is the brushed gold finish durable?**
A: Yes. The PVD finish is the same technology used in luxury hotel fixtures. It resists corrosion, scratching, and fading. Multiple reviewers report no wear after 6+ months of daily use.

**Q: Do I need to buy anything else?**
A: Yes — a pop-up drain assembly (about $25–35 for a matching brushed gold unit) and possibly a drain flange wrench if you don't have one. The faucet itself includes supply lines and mounting hardware.
