If you've ever fumbled for a spice jar in the dark recesses under your upper cabinets, you already know why the EZVALO Under Cabinet 3Pack exists. This isn't a fancy hardwired installation — it's a magnetic, rechargeable puck light that sticks where you need it and charges via USB-C when it dies. The question is whether three of them solve your lighting problem or just tease you with what's possible.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains three light pucks, three magnetic mounting plates, three USB-C charging cables, and adhesive tape for the plates. Each puck is roughly 3 inches in diameter and about 0.5 inches thick — slim enough that you won't notice it under most cabinetry, though the exposed metal housing does have a slight industrial look that won't match every kitchen aesthetic.

The magnetic mount is the standout feature here. The plate adheres to your cabinet's underside, and the light snaps on and off with satisfying click. When the battery drains, you pull the puck off, charge it at a desk or counter, and snap it back. No tools, no ladder gymnastics, no re-peeling adhesive every week.

## Performance in Real Kitchens

The three color temperatures are genuinely useful. The 3000K warm setting makes food look appetizing at dinner prep; the 6000K daylight mode is clinical enough for reading labels or checking whether that leftover is actually moldy. Switching between them is a simple tap on the touch sensor, and the brightness adjusts in steps rather than smoothly — a minor annoyance if you're particular about levels.

Motion detection works, but with a caveat. The sensor triggers when your hand enters its roughly 3-foot range, which is fine when you're reaching directly under the fixture. What it won't do is light up the whole counter when you walk into the kitchen. That's expected behavior for a puck light, but shoppers expecting room-scale motion sensing will be disappointed.

Battery life is the bigger practical consideration. At full brightness, expect around 4-5 hours per charge. That's fine for a few evenings of cooking, but if you use these as primary task lighting for hours daily, you'll be pulling them off to charge every other day. The USB-C port is a welcome modern touch — no proprietary cables to lose.

## What Buyers Are Saying

The 4.4-star average across 220 ratings tracks with my assessment. One reviewer noted, *"These are brighter than I expected for the size — I use them under my coffee bar and they light the whole surface, not just a spotlight effect."* Another mentioned the magnetic mount as the deciding factor: *"I bought cheaper ones before that fell off after a month. These stay put and the magnet makes recharging actually convenient."*

The consistent complaints center on the motion sensor's limited range and the battery drain at high brightness. A few buyers also mentioned that the adhesive on the mounting plates struggled with textured cabinet surfaces — worth noting if your cabinets are anything but smooth.

## Where It Falls Short

Three lights is fewer than it sounds. A standard 30-inch sink area eats all three just to eliminate shadows. If you're hoping to light a full galley kitchen or a pantry wall, budget for two packs.

The touch sensor is also sensitive — brushing against the light while grabbing a dish will cycle the color temperature accidentally. It's a minor nuisance, but it happens more often than you'd think.

## Who Should Buy These

This 3-pack is ideal for renters who can't hardwire lights, apartment dwellers with one problem counter, or anyone with a coffee bar, desk nook, or closet that needs targeted illumination without an electrician. It's also a solid choice for under-shelf lighting in a pantry or workshop cabinet.

Skip it if you need whole-room lighting, have rough-textured cabinets that resist adhesive, or want a system that charges without you ever touching it. For those scenarios, a plug-in LED bar or hardwired fixture is worth the extra effort.

## FAQ

**How do these mount?** The plate sticks to your cabinet with included adhesive tape, and the light attaches magnetically. You can also screw the plate in if you own the space and want a permanent mount.

**Can I leave them plugged in while using?** Yes, but the USB-C port is on the side, so you'd see a cable trailing down. It's designed as a recharge-and-reattach system, not a wired fixture.

**Do they work in closets or shelves?** Absolutely — the motion sensor and color modes work anywhere, not just under cabinets. Just remember the 3-foot sensor range.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want instant, tool-free under-cabinet lighting with flexible color temps and you're okay with occasional recharging. **Skip it if** you need wide-area coverage or permanent installation.

At 4.2/5 stars, the EZVALO Under Cabinet 3Pack earns its rating — it's a well-built, genuinely useful product with clear limitations. For the price of a decent pizza delivery, it solves a real daily annoyance. That's a fair trade.
