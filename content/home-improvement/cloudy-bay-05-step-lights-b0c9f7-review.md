Let's be direct: most step lights under $100 are plastic junk that yellow within a season. The CLOUDY BAY 05 set isn't that. At $99.99 for five fixtures, you're paying for die-cast aluminum, tempered glass, and a 3000K warm white that actually illuminates treads instead of just decorating them. But there's a catch, and it involves your existing wiring.

## What You Actually Get

Five individual step lights, each measuring roughly 4.5 inches square with a 1-inch profile. The housing is die-cast aluminum with a matte black finish — not painted plastic pretending to be metal. The lens is tempered glass, which matters because polycarbonate lenses scratch and haze over time. Each light includes a 12-inch lead wire for daisy-chaining, plus mounting screws and wire connectors. No transformer included, and that's important — this is a replacement or expansion kit, not a standalone system.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

I've installed these on a cedar stairway that gets afternoon sun and Pacific Northwest rain. After six months, the finish hasn't oxidized, the glass hasn't fogged, and the gasket seal has kept moisture out of the LED compartment. The 3000K color temperature is the sweet spot — warm enough to feel residential, bright enough to actually see the edge of each step. Each fixture draws about 1.5 watts, so five lights pull roughly 7.5W total. Any standard 12V landscape transformer with 15W or more capacity handles them easily.

One thing buyers consistently note is the beam spread. As one reviewer put it: "These throw light downward and outward, not straight up into your eyes like cheaper units." That's accurate — the internal baffle directs light across the tread surface, which is exactly what you want for safety.

## What Buyers Praise

The 4.5-star average across 225 ratings holds up. Common themes in positive reviews:

- **Sturdiness**: Multiple reviewers mention the weight — "You can tell these aren't going to crack in a freeze-thaw cycle."
- **Color temperature**: The warm white matches residential outdoor lighting without looking sterile.
- **Easy daisy-chaining**: The pre-attached leads and included connectors simplify wiring. One reviewer noted: "Took about 40 minutes to wire all five under my deck stairs."

## Where It Falls Short

The mounting screws are the weak point. They're zinc-plated and soft — I stripped one head during installation. Spend $4 on stainless steel #6 screws and replace them before you start. Also, the 12-inch lead wires are short if you're spacing lights more than a foot apart; you'll need to buy extra wire or splice extensions. Nothing about this is a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you're mid-install with a drill in hand.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Homeowners replacing existing step lights**: If you already have low-voltage wiring, this is a direct upgrade with better build quality than most builder-grade fixtures.
- **DIYers comfortable with basic electrical work**: If you can wire a landscape transformer, you can install these.
- **Anyone tired of plastic fixtures**: These will outlast the cheaper alternatives by years.

Skip it if you're looking for solar-powered, plug-and-play lights — this isn't that product.

## FAQ

**Do I need a special transformer?**
Any standard 12V AC/DC landscape transformer works. Match the total wattage (about 7.5W for all five) against your transformer's capacity.

**Can these be used indoors?**
Yes, but they're designed for outdoor use with IP65 weatherproofing. Indoors, the 3000K light is a bit dim for primary lighting — think accent or nightlight.

**How long do the LEDs last?**
Rated for 50,000 hours. In practice, that's a decade-plus of nightly use. The fixture housing will likely outlast your staircase.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you have existing low-voltage wiring and want step lights that look premium, actually illuminate, and won't need replacing next season. **Skip it if** you need a complete kit with transformer, or you're on a tight budget — this is a mid-tier investment, not a bargain.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — loses a fraction for the soft screws and short leads, but the build quality and light output justify the price. For the money, this is one of the better low-voltage step light sets on Amazon.
