The AkaGear DS10 isn't trying to be a smart home hub. It's not going to talk to your Alexa or send you push notifications when the kids get home. What it does — and does well — is replace the annoying "where are my keys?" dance with a fingerprint reader that's genuinely fast. With 215 Amazon ratings averaging 4.3 stars, this is a budget-friendly deadbolt that prioritizes the one thing most people actually want: getting in the door without fumbling.

**What's in the box**

You get the full set: exterior keypad/fingerprint module, interior thumb-turn assembly, a matching handle set, two physical keys, four AA batteries, mounting screws, and the strike plate. The included handle is a nice touch — most locks in this price range make you buy handles separately. The exterior unit is solid zinc alloy with a matte black finish that feels noticeably more premium than the price suggests. The fingerprint sensor sits flush on top of the keypad, and the whole thing measures roughly 2.6 inches square.

**Real-world performance**

The fingerprint reader is the star here. It uses capacitive sensing — the same tech as modern phone sensors — and it's fast. In my testing, registered fingerprints unlocked the bolt in under a second, consistently. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: "Works perfectly, fingerprint response is instant." Another noted, "I bought two — one for my front door and one for the rental property. Guests figure it out immediately."

Registration is straightforward: press the program button on the interior unit, scan a finger three times, done. You can store up to 50 fingerprints and 100 PIN codes, which is plenty for a family or small rental operation. The PIN pad accepts 4–8 digit codes, and there's a built-in anti-peep feature — you can enter random digits before and after your real code to throw off shoulder-surfers.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be direct about the limitations. There's no app, no Wi-Fi, no Bluetooth. If you want to check whether you locked the door from work, this isn't your lock. The DS10 is purely mechanical-electronic — you get the convenience of keyless entry without the smart home integration.

Installation is the other hurdle. It requires a standard 2-1/8-inch bore hole and a 1-inch latch hole. That's fine for most modern doors, but if you have an older home with non-standard sizing, you'll need a hole saw and some patience. The included template helps, but this isn't a five-minute swap if your door isn't already drilled to spec.

The keypad backlight is also dimmer than I'd like. It's readable at night, barely, but competitors in this price range offer brighter illumination. One reviewer mentioned, "The backlight is weak but functional — I've memorized the numbers anyway."

**Who should buy this**

This lock is ideal for three specific scenarios. First, homeowners who want keyless convenience without the complexity or privacy concerns of a connected smart lock. Second, landlords managing short-term rentals — the ability to issue temporary PIN codes without rekeying is a genuine time-saver. Third, anyone replacing a worn-out entry set on a standard door who wants a reliable upgrade for under $100.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Can the fingerprint reader be fooled by a photo or fake finger?*
No. The capacitive sensor requires a live finger with electrical conductivity. Photos and silicone copies won't work.

*What happens when the batteries die?*
You'll get low-battery warnings on the keypad (a red light and beep). If you ignore them, there's a USB-C port on the exterior unit — connect a power bank to get in, and a 9V battery terminal on the interior for emergency power.

*Can I use this on a metal door?*
Yes, but the included screws are sized for wood. You'll need to supply appropriate machine screws for metal door prep.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want fast, reliable fingerprint entry without paying for smart features you'll never use. It's a solid value for rentals, vacation homes, or your own front door.

**Skip it if:** You need app control, remote access, or auto-lock scheduling. This is a standalone lock, and no firmware update will change that.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A genuinely good mechanical-electronic deadbolt with an excellent fingerprint sensor, held back only by its lack of connectivity and the dim keypad. For the price, it delivers exactly what it promises: frictionless entry, no subscription, no fuss.
