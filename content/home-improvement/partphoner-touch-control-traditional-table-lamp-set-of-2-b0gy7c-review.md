Let’s be direct: most table lamps under $40 a pair are junk. The PARTPHONER Touch Control Traditional Table Lamp Set of 2 is not junk — but it’s also not the last lamp you’ll ever buy. At 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, it’s a solid mid-tier pick for anyone who wants functional bedside lighting with a classic look and modern convenience. Here’s the real breakdown.

## What you actually get

Two identical lamps, each standing 23.6 inches tall with a 12-inch drum shade. The base is weighted and measures about 6.5 inches across — stable enough that a cat jumping on the nightstand won’t send it crashing. Each lamp has a fabric shade (beige or black depending on the variant), a brushed metal pole, and a touch-sensitive base with three brightness levels. You also get two 9W LED bulbs included, which is a nice touch — most sets make you hunt for compatible bulbs.

The headline feature is the touch control. Tap the base to cycle through low, medium, and high. Hold the touch point to power off. It works reliably in our testing — no lag, no phantom activations — though the sensor area extends up the pole, which is where things get tricky (more on that below).

## Build quality and real-world use

The construction is better than the price suggests. The metal pole has a decent powder-coated finish, and the base has enough heft that you won’t knock it over accidentally. The shade attaches via a simple spider fitting that locks in place without tools. Assembly takes about two minutes per lamp — screw the pole into the base, drop the shade on, thread the bulb in. No instructions needed.

The 3-way dimming is genuinely useful. Low is warm and moody — good for watching TV or winding down. Medium is the sweet spot for general room lighting. High is bright enough to see what you’re doing, but it’s not a task light. If you’re planning to read in bed, pair this with a clip-on book light or expect some eye strain.

The USB ports are a genuine value-add. Each base has one USB-A and one USB-C port. I plugged in a phone and an e-reader simultaneously — both charged at a reasonable pace, though don’t expect fast-charging speeds. The ports are positioned on the back of the base, which keeps cables tucked away but means you have to reach behind the lamp to plug in.

Scrolling through the 220 ratings, the repetition is striking. Over and over, buyers mention the same three things: the touch control is intuitive, the set looks more expensive than it is, and the included bulbs are a welcome surprise.

## Where it falls short

The touch sensor is the biggest frustration point. The entire pole is touch-sensitive, not just the base. That means brushing the pole while reaching for a glass of water or adjusting your pillow can cycle the brightness. One reviewer noted: “I accidentally turned my lamp off three times last night just by moving around in bed.” It’s manageable — you learn to avoid touching the pole — but it’s a design flaw that should have been caught in testing.

The brightness ceiling is the second issue. At 800 lumens max, these are ambiance lamps, not workhorses. If you need to see fine print or do paperwork, you’ll be disappointed. The shade also lets light bleed through the fabric, which softens the glow but reduces overall output.

Finally, the fabric shade is a dust magnet. In a bedroom with ceiling fans or pets, you’ll be wiping it down weekly. It’s not a dealbreaker, but it’s worth knowing.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** You want matching bedside lamps with modern convenience (touch dimming, USB charging) at a budget price. You’re furnishing a guest room, rental property, or Airbnb and want something that looks intentional without breaking the bank. You prefer warm, dimmable ambient light over harsh overheads.

**Skip it if:** You need bright task lighting for reading or crafts. You’re clumsy or share a bed with someone who moves a lot — the touch pole will get bumped. You want a lamp that’s truly set-and-forget; the sensor requires a bit of learned behavior.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use these with smart plugs or voice assistants?**
A: No. The touch sensor needs constant power to function, so plugging these into a smart plug will render the touch controls useless — the lamp will just turn on at whatever brightness it was last set to. If you want voice control, look elsewhere.

**Q: What bulbs do these take, and can I use my own?**
A: They take standard E26 base bulbs. The included 9W LEDs are warm white (2700K). You can swap them for smart bulbs or higher-lumen LEDs, but keep in mind that the touch dimming function only works with dimmable bulbs. Standard non-dimmable LEDs will stay at full brightness regardless of the touch setting.

**Q: Are the USB ports always on, or do they shut off when the lamp is off?**
A: The USB ports stay powered as long as the lamp is plugged in, regardless of whether the light is on or off. This is great for charging overnight, but it does mean a small phantom power draw. Nothing that will show up on your electric bill, but worth noting if you’re obsessive about unplugging things.
