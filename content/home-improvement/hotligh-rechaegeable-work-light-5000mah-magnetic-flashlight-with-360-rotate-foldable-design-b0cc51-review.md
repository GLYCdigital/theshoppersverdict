# HOTLIGH Rechargeable Work Light Review: A 5000mAh Magnetic Beast That Actually Delivers

Let's cut through the noise. The HOTLIGH Rechargeable Work Light isn't trying to be the cheapest light on Amazon. It's trying to be the one you reach for every single time you crawl under a sink, pop a hood, or wire a panel — and based on 235 ratings averaging 4.7 stars, it's winning that battle.

## What You Actually Get

Inside the box: the work light itself, a USB-C charging cable, and a user manual. No wall adapter, no car charger, no mounting accessories. That's fine — you likely have a USB-C brick already. The light measures roughly 7 inches long when unfolded, weighs about 12 ounces, and features a machined aluminum housing with a rubberized grip section. The magnetic base is the star here — rated at 60lbs of pull strength, it snaps onto ferrous surfaces with authority.

The head rotates a full 360 degrees and the body folds 180 degrees, which sounds gimmicky until you're contorted under a dashboard trying to light a bolt that's facing away from every available surface. As shown in the product image, the locking hinge holds its position firmly — no sagging, no drooping mid-task.

## Performance: Real-World Light Output

The HOTLIGH pushes a claimed 1000 lumens on high, and while I don't have a lab-grade lux meter, the beam pattern is genuinely useful. It's a wide flood rather than a tight spot, which is exactly what you want for work lighting. The COB LED array covers a broad area without harsh hotspots.

Battery life is where this thing earns its keep. The 5000mAh cell delivers roughly 6-8 hours on high and up to 20+ hours on the lower settings. That's a full workday and then some. One Amazon reviewer noted, "Used it for a 10-hour garage session on medium and it never blinked." Another said, "Charged it once, used it every night for a week of HVAC calls."

Charging takes about 3-4 hours via USB-C, and there's a battery indicator light that shifts from red to green when full. No surprises.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Scrolling through the review data, three themes dominate:

- **The 360° rotation**: "Best feature hands down. The head twists any direction and stays put." This is the differentiator versus cheaper magnetic lights that only tilt one axis.
- **Magnet strength**: Multiple buyers report sticking it to truck frames, garage door tracks, and steel shelving without it falling. One mechanic mentioned it held onto an engine block at a steep angle without slipping.
- **Build quality**: "Feels way more expensive than it is. Solid aluminum, no creaking plastic." The IP-rated waterproofing is absent, but build quality itself earns consistent praise.

## Where It Falls Short

Nothing's perfect. Here's what you need to know:

- **No IP rating**: This is a genuine gap. If you work outdoors in rain, you'll want to keep it under cover. It'll handle a splash, but it's not rated for wet conditions.
- **Short charging cable**: The included USB-C cord is about 2 feet long. It works, but you'll likely swap in your own.
- **No dimming memory**: The light doesn't remember your last brightness setting when you power it off and on. Minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.
- **Price**: It's not the cheapest 1000-lumen magnetic light out there. You're paying for the battery capacity and the rotation mechanism.

## Who Should Buy This

## FAQ: Real Questions, Straight Answers

**Q: How long does it actually last on high?**  
Realistically, 5-7 hours on full brightness. On medium, you'll comfortably get a 10-hour workday. The 5000mAh cell is legit.

**Q: Will the magnet scratch my car?**  
The base has a rubberized coating, so on painted surfaces it's generally safe. That said, if you slide it around aggressively, you can mar clear coat. Place it, don't drag it.

**Q: Can I use it while charging?**  
Yes, it runs on pass-through power, but the charging port is on the rear, which can be awkward for positioning. Best to charge it fully before a job.
