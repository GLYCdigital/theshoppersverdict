# Cismmy Black Peel and Stick Wallpaper — Embossed Crocodile W Review

**Skip this if you want a flat, forgettable wall covering. Buy this if you want a statement wall that makes people reach out and touch it.**

The Cismmy Black Peel and Stick Wallpaper with its embossed crocodile texture is not subtle. That's the point. This is a textured, tactile product designed to bring a dark, moody, almost brutalist-luxe vibe to an accent wall, furniture piece, or backsplash. At 4.1 stars from 204 ratings, it's a solid performer — but it's also a product that demands the right surface and the right expectations. Here's the real story.

## What's in the roll

You get a standard-width roll of peel-and-stick vinyl with a raised, embossed crocodile scale pattern. The black is deep and matte — no shine, no plastic sheen. The texture is the headline: the scales are physically raised, giving it a leather-like feel that reads as far more expensive than the price point suggests. The backing is a standard release liner, and the adhesive is a typical peel-and-stick formulation — repositionable at first, but it grabs firmly once pressed down.

## Performance and real-world use

The texture is the double-edged sword here. It looks incredible on a smooth, freshly-painted wall. The raised pattern catches light and shadow, creating a dimensional effect that flat wallpaper simply can't match. One reviewer put it well: *"It looks like actual leather panels. People ask if it's real embossed leather."*

But that same texture punishes imperfect surfaces. This wallpaper does not hide flaws — it amplifies them. Every bump, every patch, every slightly textured drywall spot shows through the raised pattern. If your wall isn't smooth to the touch, you'll see it. Prep is not optional.

The adhesive itself is workable. It's repositionable for the first few minutes, which is a lifesaver for aligning patterns. But the material is on the thinner side — one buyer noted, *"It tears if you pull too hard while smoothing it out. Go slow."* That's fair. You need a plastic squeegee, not your hands, and you need to work systematically from top to bottom.

The pattern repeat is the other consideration. The crocodile scales don't align perfectly edge-to-edge. You need to plan your cuts carefully, especially if you're covering a large area. Visible seams are the #1 complaint in the reviews — *"The pattern doesn't match at the seams, you can see the line."* This is a real limitation, not a user error issue. For a small accent wall or furniture piece, it's manageable. For a full room, you'll need patience and sharp scissors.

## Where it falls short

Beyond the seam issue and surface sensitivity, there are a few honest drawbacks. The black color shows fingerprints and dust easily — you'll be wiping it down more than you'd expect. It's also not repositionable indefinitely; once it's been down for more than a few minutes, pulling it up risks stretching the vinyl. And while it's marketed as removable, the adhesive on the thinner material can leave a slight residue on very porous walls — test a corner first.

## Who should buy this

- **DIY renters** who want a dramatic accent wall without the commitment of paint or traditional wallpaper
- **Furniture flippers** — this stuff transforms dressers, nightstands, and cabinet fronts into high-end-looking pieces
- **Anyone with perfectly smooth walls** who wants a bold, textured statement without calling a professional

Skip it if your walls are textured, if you're covering a large area with multiple rolls that need seamless pattern matching, or if you want something forgiving of imperfect prep.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this wallpaper really removable without damaging walls?**
A: On smooth, well-primed walls, yes — it peels off cleanly. On porous or flat-painted walls, you may get slight residue. Test a small corner before committing.

**Q: How much area does one roll cover?**
A: Check the product listing for exact dimensions — the standard is roughly 15.7" x 118" per roll, covering about 12-13 square feet. Buy extra for pattern matching; you'll waste some on alignment.

**Q: Can I use this in a bathroom or kitchen?**
A: It's vinyl and moisture-resistant, so it holds up to humidity, but it's not meant for direct water contact. Keep it away from splash zones and wipe it dry.
