**Where It Falls Short**

The sharp corners are the honest tradeoff here. This design is all straight lines and right angles—which looks great but isn't forgiving. If you have small children who like to grab at cabinet fronts, or if you regularly bump into lower cabinets while cooking, you'll feel these corners. One reviewer mentioned, *"I caught my hip on the island drawer knob. It left a mark. On me, not the knob."* That's the reality of geometric hardware.

The included screws are functional but basic. They'll work fine for particleboard or MDF cabinet doors. If you're installing into solid oak or maple, spend a few dollars on better-quality screws to avoid any stripping risk during installation.
