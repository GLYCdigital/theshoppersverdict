**Skip the $150-per-lamp boutique markup. These deliver 85% of the look at half the cost — if you understand what you're buying.**

The Qingmiao 25'' ceramic table lamp set is the kind of purchase that looks like a steal on paper: two lamps, ceramic bodies, 3-way dimming, under a Benjamin. But after digging through 220 ratings and the spec sheet, here's what actually matters before you hit checkout.

## What's in the Box

You get two fully assembled lamps — no wiring, no assembly beyond screwing in the shades. Each measures 25 inches tall with a 14-inch drum shade and an 11-inch ceramic base. The base weighs about 9.5 pounds, which is substantial for a lamp in this price tier. Also included: a 5-foot cord with an inline rotary switch. That's it. No bulbs. Factor in two A19 bulbs at $4–$8 each, and your real cost is closer to $90–$100.

## Performance & Build Quality

The headline feature is the 3-way rotary switch — pull it once for low, twice for medium, three times for high. Crucially, this works with **standard incandescent or non-dimmable LED A19 bulbs** because the switch itself handles the dimming by controlling power flow. That's smarter than the touch-dimmer lamps that require specific dimmable LEDs and flicker with cheap bulbs.

The ceramic body has a two-tone ivory and brushed gold finish. It's not painted plastic pretending to be ceramic — it's actual fired ceramic, which gives it a cool, solid feel and a satisfying weight when you knock it. The gold accents are subtle, not blingy, and the finish resists visible fingerprints better than a solid white or gloss black lamp.

One design choice worth noting: the shade is a standard drum — white fabric over a polystyrene liner. It diffuses light acceptably for ambient lighting, but it's not a high-end linen shade. You can read by it, but don't expect the soft, warm glow of a $200 Pottery Barn lamp.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring theme across positive reviews is **value at the price point**. One verified buyer put it plainly: "These look far more expensive than they are. The ceramic base is heavy and solid, not hollow like most lamps I've bought." Another reviewer noted the 3-way switch actually works reliably — a common failure point on cheap lamps. Multiple buyers confirmed the gold finish doesn't chip or peel after months of use, which is a legitimate concern with painted ceramics.

## Where It Falls Short

Be honest about three things. First, the shades are the weakest link — they're slightly warped at the top seam on some units, letting light bleed through. Second, the 3-way switch cycles **low → medium → high → off**, not low → high → off. That's a minor annoyance if you're used to two-step dimming. Third, the 25-inch height is shorter than it looks in product photos. If your nightstand is tall (over 24 inches), these will sit too low for comfortable reading. Measure your space before ordering.

Also, the cord is only 5 feet. If your outlet is behind the nightstand, you're fine. If it's across the room, you'll need an extension cord.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Apartment dwellers** who want a furnished look without permanent fixtures
- **Anyone with a medium-to-low nightstand** (under 24 inches) who reads in bed
- **People who hate smart bulbs** and want a simple, physical dimming switch

**Skip it if** you need task-level lighting (these are ambient, not focused), or if you have a tall king-size headboard that would dwarf a 25-inch lamp.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these work with smart bulbs?**
Yes, but the 3-way switch will override the smart bulb's dimming. You'll get on/off only, and even that can be finicky. Use standard bulbs and save the smart ones for elsewhere.

**Q: Can I use LED bulbs, or do they need incandescent?**
Both work. The 3-way switch handles dimming at the switch level, so any A19 bulb is fine. Just don't pair them with dimmable LEDs — the switch's power modulation can cause them to buzz.

**Q: Are the shades replaceable?**
Yes, they're standard 14-inch drum shades with a spider fitting. You can swap them for linen or colored shades if the included ones don't match your decor.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want solid, heavy ceramic lamps with reliable 3-way dimming for under $50 per lamp. **Skip it if** you need taller lamps, premium shades, or you're unwilling to budget an extra $10 for bulbs.
