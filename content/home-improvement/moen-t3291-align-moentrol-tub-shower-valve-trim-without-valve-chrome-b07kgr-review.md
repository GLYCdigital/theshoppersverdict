# Moen T3291 Align Moentrol Tub/Shower Valve Trim: The Trim That Confuses People at Checkout

Here's the thing about the Moen T3291 that most shoppers discover too late: it's called "without valve" for a reason. This is the trim only — the pretty face that goes over your rough-in valve. If you're replacing an existing Moentrol system, this is a straightforward upgrade. If you're doing a full remodel and thought this was a complete valve kit, you're about to make a very common mistake.

The Align collection has Moen's signature lever handle design — clean, modern, with a slight curve that reads more contemporary than the builder-grade square plates you see everywhere. As shown in the product image, you're getting the lever handle, the volume control knob, the escutcheon plate, and the tub spout diverter hardware. What you're not getting is the actual valve body, the pressure-balancing cartridge, or the tub spout itself.

## What's Actually in the Box

Let's be precise here, because the box contents matter:

- One lever handle with the Align design
- One volume control knob (the larger round piece)
- One 7-inch diameter trim plate
- Mounting hardware and screws
- The diverter knob for the tub spout

That's it. No valve body, no cartridge, no spout. The rough-in valve (Moen's Moentrol line) is sold separately and has to be installed in the wall before this trim goes on. Multiple buyers flagged this — one reviewer put it plainly: "I didn't read carefully enough and thought this was a full valve setup. It's not. You need the Moentrol rough-in valve that's already in the wall."

## Real-World Performance and Build Quality

Once installed over the correct valve, the T3291 performs like you'd expect from Moen's mid-tier line. The lever handle moves through its arc smoothly — no grinding, no sticky spots. The volume control knob has a positive detent at the fully closed position, which sounds minor but matters when you're reaching into the shower with wet hands and don't want to fumble.

The chrome finish is worth calling out. It's a consistent, bright mirror finish that doesn't show water spots as aggressively as brushed nickel tends to. It matches other Align-series fixtures well, so if you're pairing this with a matching showerhead and spout from the same collection, the aesthetic stays unified.

The lever handle itself is comfortable. The shape gives you a good grip surface — better than the thin metal levers on cheaper trims. One reviewer who installed this in a rental property noted, "Guests figure out the lever immediately. The volume knob takes a second but it's intuitive."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 210 ratings, the recurring themes are:

- **Ease of installation** when you have the right rough-in valve. The trim mounts with two screws and the handle slides on with a set screw. Straightforward DIY work.
- **Aesthetics** — the Align design is genuinely attractive and looks more expensive than the price point suggests.
- **Moen's warranty** — the lifetime limited warranty covers finish defects and parts, which several reviewers mentioned as their reason for choosing Moen over cheaper alternatives.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest issue is the fitment confusion. This trim only works with Moentrol rough-in valves. It does NOT fit the standard Posi-Temp valve that Moen uses in most new construction. That's a hard incompatibility, not a "maybe with an adapter" situation. If you have a Posi-Temp valve, you need the T2191 or similar Posi-Temp trim instead.

Second, the 7-inch trim plate is average width, but if your old valve had a larger escutcheon or you had tile work that left a bigger gap, you'll need to buy a separate cover plate. That's an extra expense and an extra trip to the hardware store.

Third — and this is a minor nit but worth mentioning — the volume control knob doesn't have temperature markings. If you want to know exactly where "hot" is before you step in, you'll be guessing. Not a dealbreaker, but a design omission.

## Who Should Buy This

- **DIY remodelers** replacing an existing Moentrol trim — this is a clean, straightforward upgrade.
- **Homeowners matching an Align-series bathroom** — the finish consistency is excellent.
- **Anyone who prefers lever handles** over cross or knob handles for accessibility reasons.

Skip it if you're building new and don't have a Moentrol valve roughed in yet, or if you're looking for a complete valve-and-trim kit.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this fit my existing Moen valve?**
Only if it's a Moentrol valve. The box says "without valve" but the compatibility is specific. Check the rough-in valve model before ordering. If it says Posi-Temp, this won't fit.

**Q: Does the trim include the tub spout?**
No. You're getting the handle, volume knob, escutcheon plate, and diverter knob only. The spout and showerhead are separate purchases.

**Q: How hard is installation?**
If the rough-in valve is already installed and the tile work is done, this is a 20-minute job. Two screws for the plate, one set screw for the handle. If you're doing the valve too, budget for a full afternoon.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you have a Moentrol rough-in valve in the wall and want a clean, modern chrome trim with a comfortable lever handle and solid build quality.

**Skip it if** you have a Posi-Temp valve, need a complete valve kit, or want temperature markings on the volume control.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — It does exactly what it's supposed to do, looks good doing it, and Moen's warranty backs it. The only real sin here is the fitment confusion, and that's on the buyer to read the specs before clicking.
