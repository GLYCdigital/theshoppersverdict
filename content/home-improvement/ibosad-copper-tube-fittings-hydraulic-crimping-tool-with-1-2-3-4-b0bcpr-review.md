## What's Actually in the Box

Notably, IBOSAD includes both copper tube fittings and PEX crimp rings compatibility — the same dies work for both, which is a smart design choice. ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. There's no pressure gauge, so you're trusting the ratchet mechanism to tell you when the crimp is complete. It works — but if you're the type who wants visual confirmation of PSI, you won't get it here.

The weight is also a factor. At about 9 pounds, it's manageable for bench work but tiring for overhead crimping. You'll want a second hand or a support strap for ceiling work.

And here's the practical gotcha: replacement dies are not readily available as aftermarket parts. If you need a 1" die or a specific PEX brand's proprietary size, verify IBOSAD sells it before you commit.
