# Moen S112BG Collection 10-Inch Showerhead Review: Does Bigger Actually Mean Better?

Most "rain shower" heads are marketing gimmicks — a wide face that dribbles pathetic trickles unless you've got commercial-grade pressure. The Moen S112BG Collection 10-Inch Single Function Showerhead claims to fix that with something called Immersion Rain technology. After digging through 225 ratings and the specs, here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

Out of the box, you're looking at a substantial 10-inch round face — that's genuinely large, not a 7-inch head with a wide trim ring. The unit comes with a standard 1/2-inch NPT connection, so it threads onto any existing shower arm without adapters. The brushed gold (BG) finish is the standout here; it's a muted, warm tone that doesn't scream "look at me" like polished brass.

The head itself is solid metal with a silicone nozzle face. Those silicone tips matter — they're the difference between cleaning mineral buildup with a wipe versus soaking the head in vinegar for hours.

## Performance: The Immersion Rain Difference

Here's where Moen separates itself from the pack. Most wide showerheads spread water evenly across the entire face, which sounds good until you realize the outer edges get almost no pressure. The S112BG's Immersion Rain technology concentrates roughly 70% of the flow toward the center of the head. The result? You get that wide, drenching coverage across your shoulders and torso, but with enough concentrated force to actually feel like a shower instead of a gentle mist.

At standard residential pressure (around 45-55 PSI), this head performs admirably. The 1.8 GPM flow rate is compliant with water-saving regulations, but the engineering means it doesn't feel restricted. Multiple buyers describe it as feeling like "standing under a waterfall" — and that's not hyperbole when you're in the center stream.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The Amazon reviews at 4.5 stars tell a consistent story. One buyer notes: *"I was skeptical about another 'rain' head, but the water actually hits you with force. It's the first showerhead that made my wife stop complaining about pressure."* Another reviewer highlights the finish: *"The brushed gold doesn't show water spots like our old polished one did. Three months in, still looks new."*

The silicone nozzles get frequent shoutouts for easy cleaning, and the 10-inch face earns repeated praise for full-body coverage — especially for taller users who usually get cold shoulders with smaller heads.

## Where It Falls Short

The single-function design is the most obvious limitation. No pause button means you're stepping out of the stream to soap up, and there's no toggle between spray patterns. For a head in this price range, that's a deliberate simplification — not a flaw, but a tradeoff.

The stiff ball joint deserves mention. It's adjustable, but the friction is high enough that precise angle changes require two hands. You'll get it positioned eventually, but don't expect smooth, one-handed adjustments mid-shower.

Finally, the 1.8 GPM cap is firm. If you have a high-pressure system and want that aggressive, needle-point massage feel, this isn't the head for you. It's built for drenching, not exfoliating.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're remodeling a master bath and want a spa-like rain experience without installing a ceiling-mounted system. It's ideal for anyone with standard 45-60 PSI water pressure who wants maximum coverage. The brushed gold finish pairs beautifully with matte black or oil-rubbed bronze fixtures.

**Skip it if:** You need multiple spray modes, want a hand-held companion, or you're dealing with low water pressure (under 40 PSI) — no head can fix weak supply. Also skip if you prefer aggressive, high-velocity spray over wide coverage.

## FAQ

**Will this fit my existing shower arm?**
Yes, it uses a standard 1/2-inch NPT connection. Unscrew your old head, wrap the threads with plumber's tape, and thread this on. Five-minute install, no tools beyond a wrench for tightening.

**Is the brushed gold finish durable?**
Yes. Moen's BG finish is applied via physical vapor deposition (PVD), which bonds to the metal at a molecular level. It resists corrosion, tarnishing, and water spotting significantly better than electroplated finishes.

**Does it have a flow restrictor I can remove?**
It's set at 1.8 GPM and isn't designed for restrictor removal. If water efficiency isn't your concern and you want more output, look for a head without this cap.

## The Verdict
