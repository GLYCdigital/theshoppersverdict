**Review Body:**

You're installing heated tile floors, and you've already committed to the Schluter Ditra-Heat system — the orange uncoupling membrane with the embedded heating cables. That's the expensive, labor-intensive part. The thermostat is the brain, and the Schluter Ditra-Heat-E-WiFi is engineered specifically for that system. If you're outside the Schluter ecosystem, stop reading — this isn't for you. If you're in it, this is arguably the cleanest way to control your floors.

## What's Actually in the Box

The package includes the thermostat unit itself, a floor sensor probe with 10 feet of lead wire, and the mounting hardware. That floor sensor is the critical piece — it reads the actual tile surface temperature rather than just room air, which is how you avoid that "warm ceiling, cold feet" problem. The touchscreen display is bright, responsive, and shows both floor and ambient temperature simultaneously. It's a 120V unit rated for 15 amps, handling up to 1,800 watts — enough for roughly 150 square feet of heating cable.

## Performance and Real-World Use

Setup takes about 20 minutes if you're comfortable with basic electrical work. The wiring terminals are clearly labeled, and the sensor wire tucks into the same conduit as your heating cable leads. The WiFi pairing is straightforward — you connect to the Schluter app, which walks you through network setup. One note: it only works on 2.4GHz networks, so if your router broadcasts 5GHz only, you'll need to adjust settings.

The app itself is functional but not flashy. You get a 7-day programmable schedule with four time periods per day — morning, daytime, evening, night — each with its own target floor temperature. This is genuinely useful. You can set 68°F for 6 AM when you're stepping out of bed, drop it to 60°F during work hours, and have it back up before you walk in the door. The floor sensor keeps temperatures accurate within about a degree, which is noticeably better than air-sensing thermostats that leave tile cold.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews reflects strong owner satisfaction. A recurring theme in positive reviews is the precise temperature control. One reviewer noted, "The floor sensor makes a huge difference — it actually maintains the tile at the set temp instead of guessing from air temperature." Another common compliment is the app's reliability: "I've had other smart thermostats that drop connection constantly. This one has been rock solid for 8 months."

Installers also appreciate the straightforward wiring and the fact that the sensor ships in the box — no separate $40 purchase to track down.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the limitations. First, this thermostat is exclusively compatible with Schluter's Ditra-Heat system. If you have WarmlyYours, SunTouch, or any other heating mat, this won't work — the resistance values don't match, and you risk damaging both thermostat and cable. Second, it's not a "learning" thermostat. There's no geofencing, no occupancy detection, no auto-away. It follows your schedule until you change it. Third, the app interface feels dated compared to Ecobee or Nest — it works, but it's not elegant.

One more practical gripe: the touchscreen is a fingerprint magnet, and the white bezel shows smudges prominently. Minor, but noticeable in a bathroom setting.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy this if:** You're installing Ditra-Heat and want WiFi control with precise floor temperature management. It's especially good for bathrooms, kitchens, and basements where tile temperature matters more than air temperature. The 7-day scheduling is ideal for consistent daily routines.

**Skip this if:** You have a different heating system, want learning/geofencing features, or need to control more than 150 square feet of cable with a single thermostat.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need a separate transformer or relay for this thermostat?**
A: No. It's a line-voltage thermostat (120V, 15A max) that directly switches the heating circuit. No transformer needed.

**Q: Can I control multiple Ditra-Heat zones with one thermostat?**
A: No. One thermostat controls one heating circuit. For multiple rooms, you'll need separate thermostats, though all can be managed through the same Schluter app.

**Q: Does it work with Google Home or Alexa?**
A: No. Voice assistant integration isn't supported. You control it via the Schluter app or the touchscreen only.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The Schluter Ditra-Heat-E-WiFi is a purpose-built component for a premium system, and it delivers exactly what it promises: accurate, schedule-based control of your heated floors with dependable WiFi access. It's not the smartest thermostat on the market, but it's the right one for Schluter installations. If you're already paying for Ditra-Heat, this is the thermostat to pair with it — just don't expect it to work with anything else.

**Buy it if** you're committed to the Schluter system and want precise floor temperature control with app access. **Skip it if** you need multi-zone control, voice assistant support, or smart learning features.
