**If you're tiling a curbless shower or just want a modern look, the AVSIILE linear drain is one of those rare products that balances price, build quality, and installation flexibility.** But before you add it to your cart, understand what you're getting — and what it takes to install it right.

**What's in the box**

The AVSIILE arrives with the stainless steel drain body, the grate, a hair catcher basket, and the height-adjustment legs already attached. There's no membrane or bonding flange included, so this is strictly the drain assembly. You supply the waterproofing. The unit measures roughly 24 inches long (they offer several lengths) and the body is about 2.75 inches deep, which means it'll fit between standard 2x4 framing without modification. The 40mm outlet connects to standard PVC or ABS plumbing with a simple slip joint.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The body and grate are 304 stainless steel — the good stuff. It won't develop those rust spots you see on cheaper chrome-plated drains after a few months of hard water. As one buyer noted: *"The finish is flawless and the weight tells you it's solid metal, not the flimsy thin stuff from the hardware store."* That weight matters. A heavier drain body stays put during the tile install instead of floating up and creating lippage.

The grate sits flush and the design includes a small hair catcher basket that lifts out for cleaning. That's a genuinely useful feature — you'll spend less time snaking the drain. Water flow is rated for standard shower volume, and multiple reviewers confirmed it keeps up with a rainfall shower head without pooling. *"No standing water at all. My tile guy said it was the easiest linear drain he's installed,"* reported another verified purchase.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest catch: this is a *drain*, not a waterproofing system. For a true curbless shower with a linear drain, you need proper slope toward the drain — typically a foam pan or a mortar bed pitched at 1/4-inch per foot. If you're not tiling a custom shower, this is the wrong product. It's also not compatible with pre-sloped pan systems that require a specific bonding flange. You'll need to connect it to your waterproofing membrane (like Schluter-KERDI or a liquid membrane) yourself, and the instruction sheet is minimal. If you're a first-time DIYer, budget for a YouTube tutorial or two.

The grate can feel slippery underfoot with soap residue, though the brushed finish helps. Some users also noted the included screws are small — swap them for stainless ones if you have a big tub of hardware.

**Who should buy this**

- **DIY tile installers** doing a curbless shower or linear-drain upgrade who have done their homework on slope and waterproofing
- **Contractors** who want a reliable, code-compliant drain without paying Schluter prices
- **Any homeowner** replacing a center drain in a remodel where they're redoing the shower floor anyway

**Skip it if** you're a plumber swapping a drain without touching the tile, or if you need a complete pre-sloped shower pan system — this won't integrate with those.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit my existing shower plumbing?**
A: The 40mm outlet connects to standard 1.5-inch (40mm) drain pipe. If you have older 2-inch plumbing, you'll need a reducer. Measure your pipe before ordering.

**Q: How hard is the installation?**
A: If you're comfortable with tile work and waterproofing, moderately hard. The drain itself is simple — level the legs, connect the pipe, tile around it. The hard part is getting the slope right. Newbies should expect a learning curve.

**Q: Does it come with a warranty?**
A: AVSIILE offers a standard 1-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Several reviewers mentioned the seller responded quickly when a grate arrived scratched — customer service seems solid.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if** you're building or remodeling a shower and want a modern, high-capacity linear drain without paying premium brand prices. It's well-built, easy to clean, and performs exactly as advertised.

**Skip it if** you expected a drop-in solution or need a complete waterproofing kit. This is a component, not a system — but for the price, it's a genuinely good one.
