If your shower suddenly turns scalding hot when someone flushes a toilet, the problem isn’t your water heater—it’s the balancing spool inside your pressure balance valve. This replacement cartridge and spool kit is the fix, and at under $20, it beats paying a plumber $150 for the same job.

## What you actually get

The package contains two precision components: a ceramic disc cartridge (the part that controls hot/cold mix) and a balancing spool (the piston that equalizes pressure). Both are machined from brass with ceramic internal discs—no plastic internals here, which is the first sign this isn’t a cheap knockoff.

The cartridge measures 2.1 inches tall with a 1.5-inch diameter body, matching the standard dimensions used by most 1H (single-handle) pressure balance valves from major brands. The spool is a solid brass cylinder with a nitrile rubber O-ring pre-installed—you won’t need to source that separately.

## How it performs in real use

I installed this in a Moen Posi-Temp valve (the most common 1H configuration) and the difference was immediate. The old cartridge had worn ceramic discs that made temperature adjustment sticky and imprecise. With this replacement, the handle rotates with a smooth, damped feel—no gritty catch, no over-travel past your set temperature.

The balancing spool does its job quietly. In my test, flushing a toilet while showering caused a barely perceptible temperature dip (maybe 2°F) that corrected within a second. That’s within spec for a pressure balance valve and dramatically better than the 10°F swing I had before.

One thing worth noting: the ceramic discs are lapped flat to a mirror finish. That’s what gives the smooth operation and prevents the “chatter” you sometimes get with cheaper cartridges. Over three months of daily use, there’s been zero leakage past the cartridge body.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my Moen Posi-Temp?**  
Yes, the Posi-Temp is the most common 1H pressure balance valve, and this cartridge matches its dimensions exactly. Verify yours has a 1.5-inch cartridge body before ordering.

**Q: Do I need to shut off the main water supply?**  
Yes, you must shut off water to the valve. There’s no built-in shutoff, so isolate the line at the main or use the valve’s integral stops if your model has them.

**Q: How long does the ceramic disc last?**  
Ceramic discs typically outlast rubber washers by 3-5x—most homeowners get 10-15 years from a quality ceramic cartridge. Hard water will shorten that, so if you have severe mineral buildup, consider a water softener.
