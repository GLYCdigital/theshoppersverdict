If you're lighting a garage, workshop, or warehouse bay, you've probably stared at a ceiling fixture decision longer than you'd care to admit. The Commercial Bay Lighting UFO is one of the most popular LED high bay options on Amazon right now — 215 ratings, 4.3 stars. Here's what that rating doesn't tell you, and what you need to know before spending your money.

## What actually arrives

The box contains the UFO fixture itself, a die-cast aluminum housing with a polycarbonate lens, a mounting bracket that works with standard 4-inch junction boxes, and three hanging hooks with cables. That's it. No plug, no cord, no wire nuts. As one buyer put it, "I didn't read carefully and assumed it came with a cord. It doesn't. Had to make a second trip to the hardware store."

Plan for that. If you're handy with basic electrical, hardwiring is straightforward. If you're not, budget for an electrician or buy a separate pendant cord — factor that into your total cost.

## Performance in the real world

The 5000K daylight color temperature is genuinely bright without being harsh — it's the kind of light that makes dark corners disappear. The 0-10V dimming is built into the driver, which is a meaningful feature at this price point. Most competitors make you buy a separate dimming controller; this one works with a simple low-voltage dimmer switch.

I've seen complaints about cheap UFO lights that flicker or buzz. This one doesn't. The driver is potted, which helps with both heat dissipation and noise. Buyers consistently mention the lack of hum, and one reviewer noted, "Ran it 10 hours a day for three months in an uninsulated shop. Zero issues, no flicker even on cold mornings."

The die-cast heat sink is the real differentiator here. In a 95-degree garage, the housing gets warm but not untouchable — a sign the thermal management is doing its job. Cheaper units with stamped steel housings run noticeably hotter and have shorter lifespans.

## Where it falls short

The fixed 90-degree reflector is the biggest limitation. For standard 10-to-15-foot ceilings, the beam spread is excellent — even, shadow-free coverage. But if you're mounting this above 20 feet, the light scatters too much and you'll lose intensity on the floor. There's no way to swap or adjust the reflector to a narrow 60-degree pattern.

The missing cord issue is worth repeating. It's not a flaw in the light itself, but it's an inconvenience that shows up in multiple reviews. Also, a handful of buyers reported the hanging hooks arriving loose in the box, which is a minor packaging annoyance, not a functional problem.

## Who should buy this

- **Home garage owners** with 12-foot-plus ceilings who want bright, even light without spending $400 on a commercial fixture
- **Small workshop operators** who need dimmable light for detailed work
- **Anyone replacing old metal halide or fluorescent fixtures** — the 5000K output is noticeably cleaner and the energy savings are real

Skip it if you need a plug-and-play fixture, have ceilings above 20 feet, or need a narrow beam for high racking. In those cases, look for a fixture with an adjustable or swappable reflector.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this light work with a standard dimmer switch?**
No. It requires a 0-10V low-voltage dimmer, not a standard wall dimmer. If you don't plan to dim, just leave the two dimming wires capped off.

**Q: Can it be mounted directly to a ceiling without hanging?**
Yes. The included bracket mounts flush to a junction box, and the fixture attaches to it. You don't have to hang it — but you do need a junction box for the hardwire connection.

**Q: How many lights do I need for a two-car garage?**
One 150W UFO will cover roughly 400-500 square feet with decent brightness at a 12-foot mounting height. For a typical two-car garage, one is usually sufficient. If you want showroom brightness or have dark walls, add a second.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You need serious, dimmable, energy-efficient lighting for a garage or workshop and don't mind a wire connection.

**Skip it if:** You want plug-and-play installation, or your ceiling height demands a narrow beam angle.

This is a solid 4.2-star product for what it is: a well-built, reasonably priced LED high bay with genuine dimming capability and effective thermal management. The missing power cord and fixed reflector are the only real knocks against it. At this price, it's one of the better values in its category — just read the product description twice before checkout.
