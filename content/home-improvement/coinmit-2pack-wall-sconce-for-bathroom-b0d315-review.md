If your bathroom vanity is stuck with one overhead light casting shadows across your face, the **Coinmit 2PACK Wall Sconce** is a $40-ish fix that solves the problem with two matching fixtures. At 4.5 stars from 225 ratings, it’s not revolutionary — but it’s a solid, no-nonsense upgrade that gets the job done without breaking your budget.

## What’s in the box

You get **two complete sconces**, each with a matte black metal backplate, a clear seeded-glass shade, and all mounting hardware (screws, anchors, wire nuts). The shade is a standard 5-inch globe that twists off for bulb access. No bulbs included — you’ll need **E26-base LED bulbs** (40W equivalent max). The fixture is rated for damp locations, so it’s safe for bathrooms with ventilation, but not for direct shower spray.

## Build quality and real-world use

The metal body is surprisingly sturdy for the price point — no thin, flimsy feel. The matte black finish is consistent and doesn’t show fingerprints. The seeded glass gives a soft, warm light that flatters skin tones, which is exactly what you want around a mirror. One buyer noted: *“The glass texture hides dust and water spots really well — I don’t have to wipe it down every week like my old chrome fixture.”*

Installation is straightforward if you’ve done basic wiring before. The backplate has standard 4-inch mounting screws, and the included template helps mark holes. Expect **30–45 minutes per fixture** if you’re comfortable with a voltage tester and wire nuts. If you’re not, budget for an electrician — this is hardwired only, no plug-in version.

## What buyers consistently praise

- **Even light distribution** — the glass shade spreads light instead of throwing a hot spot. A verified purchase says: *“I swapped out a single vanity light for these two and my makeup application is way more accurate now.”*
- **Value for a two-pack** — most sconces sell individually. Getting two for roughly the price of one competitor unit is the main draw.
- **Compact footprint** — at 5.1 inches deep and 6.3 inches tall, they don’t overwhelm a small half-bath or hallway.

## Where it falls short

- **No dimmer compatibility out of the box** — you’ll need dimmable LED bulbs and a compatible dimmer switch. One reviewer was frustrated: *“I assumed these would dim with my existing switch — they flickered until I swapped to dimmable bulbs.”*
- **Fragile glass** — a handful of reports mention cracked shades on arrival. It’s not common, but inspect both shades before mounting. Amazon’s return policy covers this, but it’s a hassle.
- **Limited finish option** — only matte black is available. If your bathroom is brushed nickel or oil-rubbed bronze, this won’t match.

## Who should buy this

- **DIY homeowners** replacing a single overhead light with two side sconces for task lighting.
- **Rental landlords** upgrading dated bathrooms on a budget — two matching fixtures for under $50 is a strong value.
- **Small-space dwellers** with narrow vanities or hallways where a full-size sconce would overwhelm.

Skip it if you need a plug-in fixture, want a brass or chrome finish, or don’t have existing junction boxes near your mirror.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use these in a steam shower area?**  
No. They’re damp-rated, not wet-rated. Keep them at least 3 feet from direct spray.

**Q: What bulb type works best?**  
Use LED A19 bulbs, 2700K–3000K for a warm glow. Avoid CFLs — they can buzz with the glass shade.

**Q: Do they include mounting screws for brick or tile?**  
Yes, plastic anchors are included, but for tile you’ll want a masonry bit and possibly toggle bolts for heavier support.
