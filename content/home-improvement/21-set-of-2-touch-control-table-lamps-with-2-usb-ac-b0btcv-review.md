## A lamp that earns its spot on your nightstand

Most "smart" lamps are gimmicks. This one isn't. The 21-inch set of two touch control table lamps solves a genuinely annoying problem: you're in bed, your phone's at 3%, and the nearest outlet is behind the headboard. This lamp puts power where you actually need it.

These are two matching lamps, each standing 21 inches tall with a fabric drum shade. The base is a flat disc that holds a 2-prong AC outlet and two USB-A ports — a 5V/2.1A output that'll charge a phone or tablet at a reasonable clip, though not at warp speed.

## What you actually get

Two complete lamps, two fabric shades, two weighted bases, and two LED bulbs. The bulbs are included, which is rarer than it should be at this price point. Assembly is straightforward: screw the shade onto the base, drop in the bulb, plug it in. You'll be done in under two minutes per lamp.

The touch control is on the metal pole — tap once for low, twice for medium, three times for high, and a fourth tap kills the light. It's responsive enough, though the sensor is capacitive, so dry hands work better than sweaty ones. One Amazon buyer put it plainly: "The touch feature works great for me, but my husband has to tap it a few times sometimes. Not a dealbreaker."

## Build quality and real-world use

The base is substantial enough that the lamp doesn't feel tippy, but it's not a heavy steel block. If you're yanking a plug out of the AC outlet with force, the lamp can rock. Mount the outlet on the side rather than the bottom and this would be a non-issue, but as designed, you have to be a little careful.

The light itself is warm-white — think 2700K-ish. It's pleasant for reading or winding down, but if you want a daylight/cool white for task work, this isn't your lamp. The three brightness levels span from a dim nightlight glow to enough for comfortable reading, though the highest setting won't flood a large room.

## What buyers consistently praise

The charging capability is the runaway favorite. "I replaced two lamps with these and now have 4 USB ports and 2 outlets at my bedside. My phone, watch, and Kindle all charge without a power strip hanging off the nightstand," one verified buyer wrote. Another noted: "The lamps look more expensive than they are. The fabric shade is nice, not cheap plastic."

The set is the value proposition. Buying two matching lamps separately usually costs more, and these are designed to look like a cohesive pair.

## Where it falls short

The AC outlet placement is the biggest miss. It's on the underside of the base, which means you have to lift the lamp slightly to plug something in. It's workable but awkward, and it's the most common complaint in the reviews. A few buyers also mentioned the touch sensor being location-sensitive — you have to tap the pole itself, not the base or shade.

Also, there's no memory function. If you turn the lamp off at high brightness and turn it back on, it resets to low. Minor, but worth knowing.

## Who should buy this

- **Nightstand users** who charge devices overnight and want a single power source
- **Apartment dwellers** who can't install hardwired lighting and want a clean, matched pair
- **Anyone tired of power strips** cluttering their bedside table

**Skip it if** you need adjustable color temperature, want a lamp with a dimmer switch you can find in the dark, or plan to plug in heavy, bulky adapters.

## FAQ

**Q: Are the LED bulbs replaceable?**
A: Yes, they're standard E26 bulbs. The included ones are fine, but you can swap in your own if you want a different color temperature.

**Q: Can the USB ports charge a tablet?**
A: Yes, at 5V/2.1A. That's enough for a phone or tablet, but not for fast charging on newer devices that support USB-C PD.

**Q: Do the lamps need to be wired into the wall?**
A: No, these are plug-in lamps. The AC outlet on the base is a passthrough, not a hardwired connection.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a functional, good-looking pair of bedside lamps that double as charging stations. The touch control is a nice-to-have, but the integrated outlets and USB ports are the real draw.

**Skip it if** you need adjustable color temperature, want to plug in heavy adapters, or prefer a traditional switch you can locate without fumbling.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Solid, practical, and reasonably priced. The outlet placement and lack of color temperature options keep it from a perfect score.
