### The Nightstand Upgrade That Actually Earns Its Keep

Most "smart" lamps are overpriced gadgets that require an app, a Wi-Fi connection, and a degree in troubleshooting. This touch-control bedside lamp takes the opposite approach: it does three things—lights your room, charges your devices, and looks decent doing it—without a single menu to navigate. At 4.5 stars across 225 Amazon ratings, it's clearly hitting a nerve with buyers who just want a lamp that works at 2 AM.

### What's Actually in the Box

You get the lamp assembly (base, pole, and fabric shade), a detachable power adapter, and the bulbs—yes, bulbs included, which is rare at this price point. The lamp measures roughly 15.7 inches tall with a 7.9-inch diameter base, which is compact enough for a standard nightstand without overcrowding it. The fabric shade is a warm neutral tone that filters light nicely, and the base has a textured finish that resists fingerprints.

### Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The construction is solid for the category. The base has enough weight that you won't knock it over reaching for your glasses. The touch sensor is located on the base, not the pole, which is a smart design choice—you can tap it without looking. Three brightness levels cycle through: full bright for reading, medium for general use, and a dim nightlight setting that's actually dim enough to not blind you at 3 AM when you're stumbling to the bathroom.

The USB charging is where this lamp earns its keep. Both the USB-A and USB-C ports output 5V/2A, which is enough to charge a modern smartphone overnight, though it won't fast-charge anything. I tested it with an iPhone 15 and a pair of wireless earbuds simultaneously—both charged fine while the lamp was on. One note: the ports are on the rear of the base, so you'll want a short, right-angle cable to keep things tidy.

### What Buyers Consistently Praise

The consensus in the reviews centers on the touch control and the dimming range. One verified buyer wrote, *"I was skeptical about touch controls, but this is so much easier than fumbling for a switch when I'm half-asleep. The nightlight setting is perfect for when the baby wakes up."* Multiple reviewers echo this sentiment—the three-step dimming is intuitive and the lowest setting is genuinely useful, not just a marketing checkbox.

### Where It Falls Short

The touch sensor has a learning curve. It's sensitive enough that brushing the base accidentally can cycle through brightness settings, which is annoying if you've got a sleeping partner. Several reviewers mention this exact issue. The fabric shade also collects dust visibly, so you'll be dusting more frequently than with a smooth plastic or glass shade.

### Who Should Buy This

- **New parents** — the nightlight setting and USB charging make middle-of-the-night feedings infinitely easier
- **Dorm dwellers** — compact footprint, built-in charging, no app required
- **Anyone with limited nightstand space** — eliminates a separate charging brick and cable clutter

**Skip it if** you need a reading lamp with focused task lighting or if you prefer a physical switch you can operate without looking.

### FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Does the lamp work with smart home systems?**
No. This is a manual touch-control lamp—no app, no Alexa/Google integration. That's a feature for some, a downside for others.

**Q: Can the USB ports charge a tablet?**
Technically yes, but slowly. The 5V/2A output is fine for phones and earbuds. A tablet will charge, just not quickly.

**Q: What bulb does it use?**
It ships with an included LED bulb, and it accepts standard E26 base bulbs if you want to swap in your own.

### The Verdict

This lamp nails the basics that actually matter for a nightstand: intuitive controls, genuinely useful dimming, and integrated charging. It's not flashy, but it doesn't need to be. The touch sensitivity quirk and cable management are minor tradeoffs for the convenience.

**Buy it if** you want a functional, no-fuss lamp with built-in charging that won't require a manual to operate. **Skip it if** you're looking for smart home integration or task lighting for serious reading.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — it earns the high marks, with a slight deduction for the overly sensitive touch sensor.
