If you've sanded with a cheap orbital, you know the frustration: dust everywhere, discs flying off, and your arms vibrating numb after ten minutes. The BOSCH GEX33-6N is the antidote. This 6-inch random orbit sander is built for the DIYer who's tired of fighting their tools — and for pros who need a reliable workhorse without the Festool price tag.

**What's in the Box**

You get the sander itself, a microfilter dust canister, one paper dust bag, and a pad wrench. No carrying case, no extra discs — just the essentials. The 6-inch pad is the headline here, offering significantly more surface area than the 5-inch models most homeowners own. That translates to faster stock removal on tabletops, doors, and paneling.

**Performance and Real-World Feel**

The 2.5-amp motor spins the pad at a fixed 12,000 OPM. That's not variable speed — a limitation if you want to switch from aggressive sanding to fine polishing without changing tools. But for pure sanding duty, the power delivery is smooth and consistent. The sander doesn't bog down when you lean into it, and the random orbit action leaves a swirl-free finish that's ready for stain or paint.

Vibration control is genuinely impressive. Bosch's "low-vibration" design isn't marketing fluff — the grip stays comfortable even after extended use. At 4.2 pounds, it's light enough for overhead work without turning into a shoulder workout.

**The Dust Collection Story**

Here's where the GEX33-6N separates itself. The 12-hole pattern matches standard multi-hole abrasives — you can buy discs anywhere without hunting for proprietary Bosch-only paper. The microfilter canister captures the fine dust that typically escapes into your lungs or workshop air. Buyers consistently note this works far better than the single-hole designs on cheaper sanders.

One buyer put it plainly: *"The dust collection is night and day compared to my old sander. I barely need a respirator now when sanding indoors."* Another praised the hook-and-loop pad: *"Discs stay put — no more stopping to re-seat the paper every thirty seconds."*

**Where It Falls Short**

The dust canister fills up fast. On a full sheet of plywood or a solid-core door, you'll be emptying it several times. Running a shop vac hose through the included adapter is the practical fix, but it adds setup time. If you're doing a big project, budget for that.

The fixed speed is the other limitation. Polishing compounds and finishing passes benefit from lower speeds, and the GEX33-6N doesn't offer that adjustment. If your work is 90% sanding, this is a non-issue. If you need a do-everything tool for buffing and polishing, look at a variable-speed model instead.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **DIY furniture builders** who sand tabletops, cabinets, and shelving — the 6-inch pad makes quick work of flat surfaces
- **Woodworkers** who value dust control and want to work indoors without coating everything in a fine layer of sawdust
- **Pros who need a backup sander** or a lighter option for trim work and finish sanding

Skip it if you need variable speed for polishing, or if you're strictly doing small, intricate pieces where a 5-inch sander is more maneuverable.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the 12-hole pattern work with standard sanding discs?**
A: Yes — that's the point. Any multi-hole disc from major brands like 3M, Diablo, or Mirka will fit. You're not locked into Bosch's paper.

**Q: Can I use this for polishing or just sanding?**
A: Technically yes, but the fixed 12,000 OPM speed makes it less ideal for polishing compounds. It's built for sanding; keep a variable-speed tool for buffing.

**Q: Is the dust canister enough, or do I need a shop vac?**
A: For small jobs, the canister works well. For larger projects, you'll want a shop vac connected — the canister fills quickly and the vac also helps keep the pad cool.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a powerful, low-vibration 6-inch sander with genuinely effective dust collection and standard abrasive compatibility. **Skip it if** you need variable speed for polishing or prefer the maneuverability of a 5-inch pad.
