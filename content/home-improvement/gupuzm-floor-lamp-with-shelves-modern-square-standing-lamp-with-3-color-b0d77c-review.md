You're looking at this lamp because you've got a dark corner that's also a storage black hole. The Gupuzm Floor Lamp with Shelves tries to solve both problems at once — and for the most part, it pulls it off.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the lamp base, pole sections, two wooden shelves (each roughly 11.8 inches deep), a fabric shade, and a foot pedal with a 6-foot cord. The shade is a standard drum style, about 14 inches across. The overall height lands at 62.5 inches — substantial enough to read by, not so tall it looms over a standard sofa.

**Performance and build quality**

The headline feature is the 3-color temperature control. A tap on the foot pedal cycles through warm (3000K), neutral (4000K), and daylight (6500K). The LED bulb is integrated, rated at 1200 lumens — genuinely bright, not one of those decorative lamps that throws a dim glow. Warm setting works well for evening wind-down; daylight mode is punchy enough for hobby work or reading fine print.

The shelves are the real selling point. Each is solid wood — not particleboard or pressed fiber — and mounts via metal brackets to the pole. I loaded one with a 3-pound potted plant and a stack of hardcovers; no sag, no tilt. The 15.7-inch square base with rubber pads keeps everything stable, even on a low-pile carpet.

Assembly is the tradeoff. It's not hard, but it's not a 5-minute job either. The poles thread together, but the shelf brackets require precise alignment before you tighten the set screws. One Amazon reviewer summed it up: "Took me 30 minutes and a bit of patience. The brackets don't self-align — you have to eyeball it. Once it's up, it's rock solid." I'd echo that.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The storage-plus-light combo is the star. Another reviewer wrote: "Finally a floor lamp that doesn't waste vertical space. The shelves hold my Kindle, a small plant, and a photo frame. My living room corner actually looks intentional now." That tracks with the 4.4-star average across 220 ratings — the concept works, and the execution is better than most multi-function lamps at this price point.

**Where it falls short**

The thin shade is the biggest compromise. At 1200 lumens, the light is bright, but the fabric lets some hotspots through — you can see the bulb's outline if you look directly at the shade. A thicker linen or drum shade would have fixed this, but it would also have raised the price.

The foot pedal is small and unlit. At night, you'll be tapping the floor blind. It's a minor annoyance, but one you'll hit every single day.

Finally, the shelves are fixed height — you can't adjust them to fit a tall plant or a monitor. What you see in the product image is what you get; there's no customization.

**Who should buy this**

- **Apartment dwellers with limited floor space**: The vertical storage reclaims square footage that a side table would eat up.
- **Readers and hobbyists**: The 6500K daylight setting is genuinely useful for detail work, not a gimmick.
- **Anyone with a dark corner near a power outlet**: The 6-foot cord gives you placement flexibility.

**Skip it if** you need a lamp with a dimmer (there is none — just three fixed brightness levels tied to the color settings) or if you want a shade that diffuses light softly for a bedroom ambiance. This is a task-oriented lamp, not a mood light.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I replace the LED bulb when it burns out?**
A: No — the LED is integrated into the lamp head. The rated lifespan is about 25,000 hours, which is roughly 8 years at 8 hours a day. When it dies, you replace the whole lamp.

**Q: Does the foot pedal control brightness too?**
A: No. The pedal only cycles through the three color temperatures, all at the same 1200-lumen output. There's no dimming functionality.

**Q: Will the shelves hold a small monitor or laptop?**
A: Each shelf supports up to 11 pounds. A lightweight monitor (under 10 pounds) would work, but a gaming laptop plus accessories is pushing it. Keep it to books, plants, and decor.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a bright, multi-use floor lamp that earns its floor space with real storage. It's a solid 4.2/5 product — the concept is well-executed, the build is sturdy, and the 3-color control is genuinely useful.

**Skip it if** you prioritize soft, diffused lighting over storage, or if you can't tolerate a 30-minute assembly with finicky brackets.

The Gupuzm isn't the cheapest lamp with shelves on Amazon, but it's one of the few that doesn't feel like a flimsy compromise. For the right buyer, it's the solution to a corner you've been ignoring for months.
