**The Luvkczc Table Lamp Set of 2: A Charging Station Disguised as a Nightstand Light**

Most "lamp with USB ports" products are gimmicks — a cheap 5W port tacked onto a wobbly plastic base. The Luvkczc set of 2 is not that. After reviewing the specs, customer feedback, and real-world usability, this pair earns its 4.5-star rating. But it's not perfect, and you deserve to know where it cuts corners before you buy.

**What You Actually Get**

Two identical lamps, each measuring roughly 15.7 inches tall with a 7.9-inch fabric shade. The base is a solid metal disc (about 5.5 inches in diameter) that gives it surprising stability — one reviewer noted, "I have a cat that knocks things over daily, and these haven't budged." Each lamp includes a 9W LED bulb (replaceable, not integrated), so you're not locked into a proprietary system when the bulb eventually dies.

The headline feature: each base houses both a USB-C and USB-A port. That's four charging ports total across the set. The USB-C outputs 5V/2.1A — enough to fast-charge a modern smartphone overnight, though not a tablet at full speed. The USB-A is 5V/1A, fine for earbuds, smartwatches, or older devices.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The lighting itself is genuinely versatile. Three color temperatures — warm 3000K, neutral 4500K, and cool 6000K — cover everything from evening reading to task lighting. Stepless dimming means you can dial from a soft glow to full brightness (800 lumens max) by holding the touch base. That's a feature set typically found in lamps twice the price.

The fabric shade is a linen-look polyester that diffuses light nicely without harsh hotspots. The metal base has a matte finish that resists fingerprints. For a bedroom or living room side table, this looks more expensive than its price point.

**What Buyers Praise**

Across the 225 ratings, consistent themes emerge. The charging capability is the standout: "Finally a lamp that actually charges my iPhone fast — I ditched my nightstand charger entirely." Another buyer mentioned, "The USB-C port is the reason I bought these, and it delivers. No more fighting for wall outlets."

The light quality also gets consistent love. One reviewer wrote, "The warm setting is perfect for winding down, and the cool setting is bright enough for paperwork." Multiple buyers praised the sturdy base and the fact that the bulb is replaceable — a genuine differentiator in a category full of disposable integrated-LED lamps.

**Where It Falls Short**

The touch controls are the biggest complaint. They're capacitive, which means any accidental contact cycles through brightness levels. One reviewer said, "I bumped the base getting into bed and the lamp went from dim to full blast — woke up my partner." It's not a dealbreaker, but it requires muscle memory to avoid.

The USB ports are on the back of the base, which means cords snake behind the lamp. If your nightstand is against a wall, you'll need to route cables carefully to keep things tidy. There's no cable management channel built in.

No smart-home integration is a miss for some buyers. If you want voice control or app scheduling, look elsewhere — this is intentionally a simple, manual lamp.

**Who Should Buy This**

The Luvkczc set is ideal for three buyer types: **nightstand organizers** who want to eliminate wall warts and charge phones, earbuds, and watches in one spot; **bedroom minimalists** who want two matching, attractive lamps that don't scream "tech gadget"; and **renters** who want replaceable bulbs and sturdy construction that survives a move.

Skip it if you need smart-home compatibility, if your nightstand is so cramped that rear-mounted USB ports would be inaccessible, or if you're charging a tablet or laptop that requires more than 10W.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do the USB ports work when the lamp is off?**
A: Yes. The ports remain powered regardless of the light state. You can leave devices charging even with the lamp switched off.

**Q: Are the bulbs included and replaceable?**
A: Yes, each lamp ships with a 9W E26-base LED bulb. It's a standard size, so any equivalent bulb will work when it eventually needs replacing.

**Q: How bright is the maximum setting?**
A: The lamp outputs up to 800 lumens at 6000K — equivalent to a 60W incandescent. That's bright enough for reading or tasks, but not a room's primary overhead light.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want two attractive, sturdy lamps that double as practical charging stations without cluttering your nightstand with adapters. The build quality exceeds the price point, and the replaceable bulb extends its lifespan significantly.

**Skip it if** you need smart-home integration, have a cat or toddler that will trigger the sensitive touch controls repeatedly, or prefer a lamp with front-facing ports for easy access.
