The Kalium Wall Sconces Set of 2 is the kind of fixture that makes you wonder why more vanity lights aren't designed this way. Instead of the standard single globe pointing down or up, each sconce uses two stacked clear glass globes on a compact backplate. The result is light that washes both up and down the wall, which does wonders for eliminating the unflattering shadows that single-direction fixtures cast across your face.

This is a smart design choice for bathroom vanities, but it's not the whole story. Here's what 229 buyers (averaging 4.6 stars) are actually saying, plus the honest downsides the product photos don't show.

## What You Actually Get

Two complete sconces, each with the double-globe assembly already mounted to the backplate. The glass shades are clear, not frosted, which means you'll see the bare bulb inside — so buy decorative Edison-style or filament LED bulbs, not plain white ones. Each fixture takes two E12 candelabra-base bulbs (not included), giving you four bulbs total across the set.

The finish options include brushed nickel and matte black, both with a clean, industrial-modern look that pairs well with farmhouse, transitional, or contemporary bathrooms. The backplate measures roughly 4.7 inches in diameter, and the fixture extends about 11 inches from the wall.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The metal construction feels solid — not the thin, hollow stamping you get with budget fixtures. The glass globes are substantial, and buyers consistently note the packaging deserves credit. One reviewer put it plainly: "I've ordered similar sconces before and at least one globe always arrived cracked. These came perfectly intact with thick foam padding around every piece."

Installation is straightforward if you've wired a light fixture before. The included mounting hardware is standard, and the wires are color-coded. But here's the catch several buyers flagged: if your electrical box is flush with the drywall, you're fine. If it sits slightly proud, the backplate won't sit flat against the wall, leaving an ugly gap. A $10 box extender solves it, but it's an extra trip to the hardware store you should plan for.

Light output depends entirely on the bulbs you choose. With 60-watt equivalent LED filament bulbs, the dual-direction wash is bright enough for makeup application or shaving. The fixture is dimmer-compatible, which buyers love for evening ambiance.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

- **Light distribution**: Reviewers repeatedly mention how evenly the light spreads. One wrote, "No more shadows under my chin when doing my makeup. The up-and-down design makes a huge difference."
- **Aesthetic appeal**: The double-globe look gets consistent compliments. "These look way more expensive than they are. Guests always ask where I got them."
- **Packaging and quality control**: Multiple reviews note the globes arrived intact and the finish was flawless — no scratches or blemishes.

## Where It Falls Short

The visible backplate issue is the most common complaint. If your junction box isn't recessed properly, the fixture won't sit flush, and the gap is noticeable. Check your existing box depth before ordering.

The lack of included bulbs is also a minor annoyance at this price point. You're spending an extra $15–$25 on four decent LED filament bulbs. And while the small backplate looks sleek, it won't cover larger, older junction boxes — another reason to measure before you buy.

## FAQ

**Are these fixtures damp-rated for bathroom use?**
The listing doesn't specify a damp-rating, so treat them as dry-location only. If you're installing directly above a shower or in a steam-heavy bathroom, choose a fixture explicitly rated for damp locations.

**Can I use LED bulbs with these?**
Yes, and you should. They're dimmer-compatible with standard E12 LED bulbs. Avoid incandescent — the heat buildup inside the enclosed globes shortens their lifespan.

**What's the actual spacing requirement?**
Each sconce extends about 11 inches from the wall. For a 36-inch vanity, mount the pair 24–30 inches apart, centered on the mirror or medicine cabinet.
