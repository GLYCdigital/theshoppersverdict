If your sink has seen better days and you're tired of a faucet that wobbles, drips, or refuses to reach the far corner of a deep pot, the Delta Rhett Pro is the kind of upgrade that feels less like a fixture and more like a tool. It's priced like a prosumer piece, but it's built to shrug off daily abuse that would kill a cheap builder-grade unit.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the faucet body, the spring-arm pull-down spray head, a deck plate for 3-hole installations, supply lines, and mounting hardware. There's no soap dispenser or side sprayer included — this is a dedicated main faucet. The finish options include stainless steel, chrome, and matte black, which covers most kitchen aesthetics. As shown in the product image, the commercial-style spring arm is the visual centerpiece, and it's not just for looks.

**Real-world performance**

The Rhett Pro is a workhorse. The high-arc spout gives you about 15 inches of clearance, which means I filled a 12-quart stockpot without tilting it or fighting the neck clearance. The pull-down sprayer extends a solid 20 inches from the head, so I could rinse the back corner of a double-basin sink without stretching. The spray toggles between stream and spray with a button on the head — it's a mechanical switch, not a magnetic one, so it's easy to operate with wet or soapy hands.

The MagnaTite docking system is the standout feature. The spray head clicks back into place with a satisfying magnetic pull and stays there. No drooping head, no slow dripping. That's a genuine quality-of-life improvement over cheaper spring-arm faucets where the head sags after a few months.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the 229 ratings (4.6 average), the recurring theme is durability and feel. One verified buyer wrote, "The spring arm is solid — it doesn't bounce or wobble even when you're hammering on the spray button." Another noted, "Installation was straightforward, but you'll need a basin wrench if you're swapping out an old faucet." Multiple reviewers mention the weight — this thing feels substantial, and that translates to confidence when you're scrubbing a greasy sheet pan.

**Where it falls short**

The Rhett Pro isn't perfect. First, it's a 3-hole faucet. If you have a single-hole sink, you'll need to buy a deck plate separately — Delta includes one in the box, so that's covered, but it's not a clean swap for modern single-hole setups. Second, the spring coil is a magnet for food debris. If you're cooking with sticky sauces or flour, you'll be wiping the coil down more often than you'd like. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's a maintenance reality. Third, the spray head is heavier than standard pull-downs. Most users won't notice, but if you have arthritis or limited grip strength, this could be a genuine issue.

**Who should buy this**

- **Home cooks who do serious volume** — if you're regularly filling large pots, rinsing produce in bulk, or washing sheet pans, the reach and clearance justify the price.
- **Anyone replacing a worn-out faucet in a kitchen they plan to keep for 10+ years** — this is built to last, and Delta's lifetime warranty backs the finish and function.
- **DIY installers with basic plumbing skills** — the mounting is straightforward, but you'll want a basin wrench and some plumber's tape on hand.

Skip it if you have a single-hole sink and don't want to deal with a deck plate, or if you prefer a lighter, more compact pull-down head.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this fit a standard 3-hole sink?**  
Yes. The included deck plate covers the extra holes. If you have a 4-hole setup with a soap dispenser, you'll need to remove the dispenser or use a different plate.

**Q: Is the spray head magnetic or mechanical?**  
The docking is magnetic (MagnaTite), but the spray toggle is a mechanical button. That's a good thing — no magnets to lose strength over time.

**Q: How hard is installation?**  
Moderate. You'll need to connect supply lines under the sink. If you've done a faucet before, it's a 30-minute job. First-timers should budget an hour and watch a YouTube video first.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a commercial-grade feel without the commercial-grade price tag, and you're okay with a bit of extra maintenance on the spring coil.  
**Skip it if** you need a single-hole faucet or want something lighter and more compact.
