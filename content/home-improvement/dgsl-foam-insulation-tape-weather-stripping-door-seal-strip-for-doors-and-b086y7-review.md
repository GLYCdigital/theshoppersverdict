# DGSL Foam Insulation Tape, Weather Stripping Door Seal Strip Review

Drafty doors waste energy, spike utility bills, and make rooms uncomfortable. The DGSL Foam Insulation Tape promises an inexpensive fix—but does it actually hold up? After digging through 215 ratings averaging 4.3 stars, here's what real buyers need to know before clicking add-to-cart.

## What You Actually Get

The DGSL weather stripping is a self-adhesive EPDM foam strip — not the cheap polyurethane stuff that crumbles in a year. It comes in a single roll with a 3M adhesive backing already applied. You get a flexible, closed-cell foam strip that compresses when the door closes and springs back when it opens. As shown in the product image, it's designed to mount on the door jamb, not the door edge itself.

## Real-World Performance

The adhesive is the standout feature here. Multiple buyers noted it sticks firmly to painted wood, metal frames, and even slightly textured surfaces. One reviewer put it plainly: *"Stuck well, no issues with adhesion after months of use."* That's consistent with the 3M backing — it's the same adhesive tech used on automotive trim, so it handles temperature swings without turning into a gooey mess.

Compression recovery matters for a door seal. A strip that stays flattened after a week is worthless. The EPDM foam in this DGSL tape demonstrates legitimate bounce-back, maintaining its seal even on doors used daily. The closed-cell structure also blocks air and moisture — not just noise. Expect measurable draft reduction around standard interior and exterior doors.

## What Buyers Repeatedly Praise

- **Easy application** — peel, stick, trim with scissors. No tools required.
- **Effective draft blocking** — several reviewers reported noticeable temperature differences in adjacent rooms.
- **Versatile** — beyond doors, people use it on windows, garage entryways, RV hatches, and even cooler lids.

One reviewer captured the general sentiment: *"Works great, product as described. Keeps the cold air out."*

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty requires flagging the limitations.

**Thickness selection is critical.** The tape comes in multiple thicknesses. If you overestimate the gap and buy the thickest option, your door may not latch correctly. Measure your gap first — if it's under 1/8 inch, don't buy the 1/2-inch version.

**UV durability is questionable.** For a north-facing door, this tape will last years. For a south-facing door baking in direct afternoon sun, the foam will eventually dry out and crack. This is an interior-grade product that happens to work outside in sheltered conditions.

**Coverage math.** A single roll covers roughly 20-33 feet depending on thickness. Standard exterior doors need about 17 feet for a complete seal (both sides and top). That's cutting it close — buy two rolls if you're sealing multiple doors or want a second layer.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Renters** — it applies cleanly and removes without damaging paint (adhesive residue wipes off with rubbing alcohol).
- **DIY beginners** — genuinely foolproof installation in under 10 minutes.
- **Anyone sealing interior doors** — for noise reduction or keeping heat in specific rooms.

**Skip it if** you need a permanent, UV-resistant exterior seal, or if your door gap exceeds 1/2 inch (you need a different product, like a door sweep).

## FAQ

**Q: How long does the adhesive last before it fails?**
A: With clean, dry surfaces, expect 6-12 months of solid adhesion indoors. Outdoor exposure shortens that significantly — plan for replacement every season.

**Q: Can I remove it without damaging my door frame?**
A: Yes. Peel it off slowly at a 45-degree angle, then remove any residue with isopropyl alcohol. It won't pull paint off properly prepared surfaces.

**Q: Will it work on sliding windows?**
A: It works, but measure first — sliding windows need a thinner strip so the sash still moves smoothly.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a fast, budget-friendly draft stopper that actually performs and won't wreck your door frame when removed.

**Skip it if** you need a permanent exterior solution facing direct sun, or if you don't want to measure your gap before ordering.

The DGSL Foam Insulation Tape earns a solid **4.2 out of 5** — a genuine value at its price point, with the caveat that thickness selection and UV exposure matter more than the marketing suggests.
