## What You’re Actually Getting

The box contains the ceramic sink basin itself — nothing else. No drain, no mounting clips, no template. You’ll need to source a rectangular or round drain separately, plus your own silicone adhesive for the undermount installation. The sink measures 16.5 inches long, 13 inches wide, and 5.5 inches deep, with a 1.5-inch drain opening that matches standard plumbing.

The ceramic body is the star here. It’s a dense, high-fired porcelain with a glossy white glaze that’s thick enough to resist the micro-crazing you see on cheap imports. The edges are square but slightly softened — not sharp enough to chip during handling, yet crisp enough to look architectural rather than blobby.

## Performance and Real-World Use

For a small sink, the basin depth is surprisingly functional. At 5.5 inches deep, you can actually wash your hands without splashing halfway up the mirror — something many compact vessel sinks fail at because they’re too shallow. The rectangular shape gives you more usable washing area than a round bowl of similar footprint.

Installation is straightforward but not beginner-friendly. You’ll need to cut an oval or rectangular opening in your countertop, apply a bead of silicone around the cut edge, and press the sink into place. It’s critical to support the sink from below until the adhesive cures — the ceramic is heavy enough that gravity will win if you don’t. Buyers consistently mention that the sink sits flush and level when installed properly, which tells you the bottom rim is well-finished rather than warped.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The overwhelming theme in positive reviews is the glaze quality. One buyer put it plainly: “The white is pure white, not grayish like some ceramic sinks. Water beads up and runs off instead of leaving spots.” Another noted, “I’ve had mine for eight months and there’s zero staining from coffee or toothpaste. Wipes clean with a damp cloth.”

Multiple reviewers also call out the size as the sweet spot for half baths and powder rooms. It’s large enough to feel like a real sink but compact enough for a 24-inch vanity. The undermount design earns consistent praise for making countertop cleanup easier — no raised rim to trap grime.

## Where It Falls Short

No overflow hole. This is the biggest functional flaw. If you leave the water running while you step away, you’ll flood your vanity. You can mitigate this by installing a drain with an integrated overflow, but that’s an extra purchase and the drain will be visible inside the basin.

Splashing is a secondary issue. The basin is compact, so a wide faucet running at full pressure will send water over the back edge. Pair it with a mid-arc faucet and keep pressure moderate.

Installation tolerance is tight. The undermount design requires a precise countertop cut — you can’t hide a sloppy opening behind the sink rim. If you’re not comfortable with a jigsaw and template work, budget for a pro.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You’re remodeling a half bath or powder room with a small vanity, you want a modern rectangular silhouette, and you value a sink that stays white without scrubbing. It’s also a strong pick for rental properties or guest baths where durability matters more than showpiece looks.

**Skip it if:** You need an overflow hole, you want a vessel sink that sits *on top* of the counter rather than underneath it, or you’re working with a non-standard countertop material that’s tricky to cut cleanly.

**Q: Does this sink work with a standard 1.5-inch drain?**
A: Yes. The drain opening is 1.5 inches, so any standard pop-up or grid drain fits. You’ll need to supply your own — the sink doesn’t include one.

**Q: Can I mount this in a stone or quartz countertop?**
A: Yes, but the cut must be precise. Stone cuts require a diamond blade and steady hands. If you’re unsure, have a fabricator pre-cut the opening.

**Q: Does the ceramic chip easily?**
A: The glaze is tough, but no ceramic is unbreakable. Dropping a heavy glass bottle or metal tool into the basin can chip it. Handle installation carefully to avoid impact damage.
