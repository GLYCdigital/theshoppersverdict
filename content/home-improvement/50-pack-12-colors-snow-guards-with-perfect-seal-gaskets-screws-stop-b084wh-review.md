## Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Will these work on a standing seam roof without drilling?**
No. These require pre-drilling and screwing into the metal panel. They're not clamp-on style. If you want to avoid penetrating your roof, look for clamp-style guards instead.

**Q: How many do I need for a typical roof?**
Most metal roof manufacturers recommend two rows of guards — one near the eave and one halfway up. A 50-pack covers roughly 25-30 linear feet of roof edge with proper spacing.

**Q: Can I paint these to match my roof?**
Yes, but lightly scuff the powder coat first and use a metal-compatible spray paint. The factory color options cover most common roof colors, so painting should be a last resort.
