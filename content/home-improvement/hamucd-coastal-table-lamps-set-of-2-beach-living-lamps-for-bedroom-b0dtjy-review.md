# Hamucd Coastal Table Lamps Set of 2: Beach Style That Actually Holds Up

Let me be straight with you: most "coastal" decor pieces under fifty bucks look like they were fished out of a discount bin. The Hamucd Coastal Table Lamps are not that. These have a specific look—textured glass bases that mimic sea glass, a weathered driftwood finish on the stem, and soft off-white shades—that reads as intentional, not accidental. If you're after that breezy Cape Cod or Florida Gulf vibe without spending $150 per lamp, this set deserves a hard look.

## What's in the Box

You get two fully assembled lamps. No tools, no "some assembly required" frustration. Each lamp measures roughly 16 inches tall with a 9-inch diameter shade—sized right for nightstands, console tables, or a sideboard in a hallway. The base is resin with that sea-glass texture, and the stem has a faux driftwood wrap. It's not real wood, but it's convincing enough that guests won't inspect it with a magnifying glass. The shades are fabric (cotton-poly blend) and attach with a simple spinner finial.

## Build Quality and Performance

Here's where these lamps surprise you. The resin base has real heft to it—this isn't hollow plastic that'll crumble when you bump it. The textured glass look is actually molded into the resin, so it catches light from the bulb and throws a warm, rippled glow across the room. That's the standout feature. As one buyer put it: "I bought these for our beach house rental and guests keep asking where we got them. The light pattern on the walls is gorgeous at sunset." That's not marketing fluff—that's the actual effect.

The touch control on the base is straightforward: tap for low, tap again for medium, tap again for high, tap once more to turn off. It works with standard A19 LED bulbs (not included), and there's no annoying delay or phantom activation. A few reviewers noted the touch sensor can be oversensitive if you brush against it while making the bed—worth knowing if you're placing these in tight quarters.

## Where They Fall Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The shades are thin. If you use a higher-wattage bulb (over 60W equivalent), you'll see the bulb silhouette through the fabric, which kills the soft-focus effect. Stick to 40–60W equivalent LEDs. Also, there's no true dimmer—the three-step touch control is your only option, and it's not stepless, so you can't fine-tune brightness. And the top-heaviness is real. The base has weight, but it's concentrated at the bottom; a solid bump from a dog's tail will send one of these over. If you have pets or toddlers, plan to place them somewhere stable.

A: No. You'll need two standard A19 LED bulbs (E26 base). The touch control works with dimmable and non-dimmable LEDs, but I'd recommend dimmable ones anyway for smoother operation.

**Q: Can I use these outdoors on a covered porch?**
A: Technically yes—they're not rated for outdoor use, but the resin and fabric shade could handle a covered, dry location. Don't expose them to rain or humidity.

**Q: How tall are they exactly?**
A: About 16 inches from base to top of shade. That's standard nightstand height—enough to cast useful light over a bed without blinding you when you're lying down.
