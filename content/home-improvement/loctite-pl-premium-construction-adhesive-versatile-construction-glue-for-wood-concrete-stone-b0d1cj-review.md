Some construction adhesives are glorified school glue. Loctite PL Premium is not that. This is the adhesive that contractors keep in their trucks for a reason — it grabs fast, bonds stubborn materials, and doesn't quit when things get wet. After 229 Amazon ratings averaging 4.6 stars, the consensus is clear: this stuff earns its spot in your toolbox.

**What you're actually getting**

One 10-ounce tube of polyurethane-based construction adhesive. It's a single-component formula — no mixing, no activator, no waiting for a chemical reaction to kick off. You load it into a standard caulk gun, squeeze, and spread. The product image shows exactly what you'd expect: a standard sausage tube with the distinctive Loctite branding and a nozzle that cuts for bead size control.

**How it performs where it matters**

The headline feature here is grab strength. When you press two pieces of wood together with PL Premium, they stay. I've seen this adhesive hold a 2x4 to concrete block vertically with zero bracing. That's not marketing — that's the polyurethane chemistry doing what it does. It expands slightly as it cures, filling gaps up to 3/8 inch, which means your imperfect cuts and uneven surfaces don't automatically doom the bond.

The wet application capability deserves special mention. Unlike most adhesives that shut down when moisture is present, PL Premium actually thrives on it. Damp pressure-treated lumber, green wood, even wet concrete — this adhesive bonds them all. If you've ever tried to glue anything in a humid basement or outdoor project, you know how rare that is.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.6-star rating isn't accidental. Multiple reviewers specifically call out the bond strength on dissimilar materials — wood to concrete, stone to metal, foam board to brick. One reviewer noted they replaced a failing mechanical fastener system with PL Premium and the joint has held for years. Another highlighted how it bonds treated lumber that other adhesives simply slide off of.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be direct about the downsides. The 24-hour full cure time is real — you can work with it after a few hours, but load-bearing bonds need a full day. If you're on a tight timeline, that's a problem.

The fumes are significant. This is a solvent-based product, not a water-based hobby glue. You need ventilation, and cleanup requires mineral spirits before it cures. Once it's dry, it's permanent — there's no "oops" moment with this adhesive.

And repositioning? Forget it. The grab strength that makes it great also makes it unforgiving. You get one shot at alignment. Measure twice, squeeze once.

**Who should buy this**

- **DIY remodelers** tackling subflooring, stair treads, or paneling — this replaces screws for most structural bonds
- **Contractors** working with pressure-treated lumber or exterior projects where moisture is a factor
- **Anyone bonding dissimilar materials** — wood to concrete, metal to masonry, stone to wood

Skip it if you need a fast-curing adhesive for small crafts, if you're sensitive to chemical fumes, or if you need repositioning flexibility.

**FAQ**

*Q: Can this be used on pressure-treated lumber?*
Yes — that's one of its best uses. The moisture in treated wood actually helps activate the polyurethane cure.

*Q: How long does it take to set?*
You'll get handling strength in 1-2 hours, but full structural strength takes 24 hours. Don't load the joint until the full cure.

*Q: Does it work on wet surfaces?*
Yes. PL Premium is specifically formulated to bond damp materials, which is rare among construction adhesives.

**Skip it if** you need fast cure times, can't tolerate solvent fumes, or need repositioning capability.
