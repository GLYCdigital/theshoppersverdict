If you've been staring at dangling wires, patched drywall holes, or the sad gap where a hardwired sconce used to be, this rechargeable pair from Amazon's B0F1MYD8QT listing is aimed squarely at you. At $79.95 for two lights with a remote, it's not the cheapest cordless option out there — but after digging through 225 ratings and the 4.5-star average, the real question is whether it earns that premium. Short answer: mostly yes, with a couple of caveats worth knowing before you click buy.

**What actually arrives**

The box contains two sconce fixtures, a compact remote control, mounting plates, adhesive strips, screws with anchors, and a USB-C charging cable. The fixtures themselves are metal with a matte finish — not the plasticky feel you sometimes get at this price point. Each light measures roughly 12 inches tall with a 4.5-inch backplate, which puts them in the sweet spot for flanking a bedroom mirror or framing a headboard. The shade diffuses light nicely; there's no harsh LED hotspot even at full brightness.

**Real-world performance and battery life**

Here's where this set separates itself from the $30 Amazon specials. The built-in rechargeable battery runs roughly 20–30 hours on a full charge at medium brightness, depending on which of the three color temperature modes you select. The warm 2700K setting is genuinely cozy — closer to an incandescent glow than the sterile LED light you often get from battery fixtures. The neutral and daylight modes are serviceable for task lighting but won't win any awards.

The remote offers six brightness levels and a dimming function that actually steps down smoothly rather than jumping between preset jumps. One buyer noted, "I was skeptical about the remote, but it works from across the room and remembers the last setting after I turn the lights off." That memory function is a small detail that makes daily use feel less fiddly.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Scanning the feedback, three themes dominate. First, installation speed — most people report being done in under 10 minutes with the adhesive strips. Second, the aesthetic — "These look way more expensive than they are," is a recurring sentiment, with multiple reviewers mentioning guests assuming they were hardwired. Third, the absence of ongoing battery costs; one verified purchaser wrote, "I've had mine for three months, charge them once every couple weeks, and haven't touched a AA battery since."

**Where it falls short**

The remote is infrared, not RF. That means you need a clear line of sight to the sensor on the fixture. Mount one behind a tall headboard or inside a shelf cubby, and you'll be pointing the remote at awkward angles or walking over to press the manual button. Several reviewers specifically flagged this as their only frustration.

The adhesive strips are strong on smooth painted drywall and wood, but textured wallpaper or knock-down plaster will defeat them. The included screws and anchors work fine, but if you rent, drilling might be a dealbreaker. Also worth noting: the lights auto-shut-off after four hours if left on, which is great for bedside use but annoying if you want continuous ambient lighting for a dinner party.

**Who should buy this**

- **Renters** who can't hardwire and don't want to patch holes
- **Anyone with awkward corners** where running electrical is impractical — hallways, alcoves, stairwell landings
- **Bedrooms** where you want reading light without bedside table clutter
- **DIY-averse shoppers** who want a 10-minute install with no permits

Skip it if you need a single, larger statement fixture or if your walls are heavily textured — you'll be drilling regardless, and at that point, hardwired might be the smarter investment.

**Three questions shoppers actually ask**

*How long does a full charge take?* About 3–4 hours via USB-C. You can use the lights while charging, though it's not ideal to leave them plugged in long-term.

*Do the lights dim without the remote?* Yes — the manual button cycles through brightness levels, but you'll need the remote for color temperature changes. If you lose the remote, you're locked into the last setting.

*Can these work outside?* No. They're rated for indoor use only. Damp locations will void the warranty and likely corrode the internals.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want the look of hardwired sconces without the electrical work, and you value warm, dimmable light over raw brightness. **Skip it if** you demand RF remote reliability, have heavily textured walls, or need lights that run all night continuously.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — the best battery-powered sconce pair I've reviewed at this price, held back only by the IR remote and adhesive limitations. For most bedrooms, hallways, and rental situations, it's a genuinely smart buy.
