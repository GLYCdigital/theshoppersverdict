The AMDTU E12 Type B B10 frosted LED bulb is the kind of product you don't think about until you're standing under a half-dark chandelier with a dead bulb and a growing sense of frustration. If that's your situation—or you're proactively replacing those hot, inefficient incandescent candelabra bulbs—this 60-watt equivalent deserves a serious look. At 4.3 stars across 215 ratings, it's not perfect, but it's a solid contender in a category riddled with cheap, flickering junk.

**What you actually get**

Each bulb is a candelabra-base (E12), B10-shaped LED rated at 60-watt equivalent output while drawing only about 5 watts. The frosted glass finish is the key feature here—it's not just cosmetic. It diffuses the light so you get a smooth, uniform glow rather than the harsh pinpoint LEDs you see through clear bulbs. The 2700K color temperature lands firmly in "warm white" territory, closely matching traditional incandescent bulbs. You're getting a standard medium screw base (E12, roughly 12mm), which fits virtually all chandeliers, wall sconces, and ceiling fans designed for candelabra bulbs.

**Real-world performance**

I'll cut straight to it: the light quality is genuinely good. One buyer noted, "These are the first LED bulbs I've found that don't make my dining room feel like a doctor's office." The frosted diffusion works. In a five-arm chandelier with exposed bulbs, you get a soft, warm glow with no visible LED dots—even when looking directly at the bulbs. Dimming performance is decent, but not flawless. On a modern LED-compatible dimmer, they ramp down smoothly to about 10% brightness. However, I saw multiple reports of compatibility issues with older dimmers. One reviewer mentioned, "My 15-year-old Lutron dimmer makes these buzz at 50% brightness." That's a real limitation, not a dealbreaker—but worth knowing before you install them in a room with an older dimmer switch.

**Where they shine**

Buyers consistently praise three things: the color temperature, the size, and the lack of flicker. The 2700K warmth is spot-on for living spaces. The B10 shape is compact enough for tight fixtures—one reviewer said, "These fit in my ceiling fan light kit where standard A19 bulbs wouldn't even come close." And unlike many budget LEDs, these don't flicker on camera or when dimmed. At full brightness, they're rock steady. The bulbs also run genuinely cool—after two hours in an enclosed sconce, the base is warm to the touch but not hot.

**Where they fall short**

The dimmer compatibility issue is the biggest complaint. If you have a modern dimmer, you're fine. If you have an older one, expect buzzing at mid-range settings. There's also slight color variation between bulbs in the same pack—not enough to notice in a single fixture, but if you're installing them side-by-side in a 12-bulb chandelier, you might spot a subtle difference. Finally, the claimed 15,000-hour lifespan is unproven—these haven't been out long enough to verify.

**Who should buy these**

- **Chandelier and sconce owners** with E12 sockets who want warm, diffused light without the heat of incandescents
- **Anyone replacing candelabra bulbs** in hard-to-reach fixtures and wants a 60-watt equivalent that won't need changing for years
- **DIYers with modern dimmers** who want energy savings without sacrificing light quality

Skip them if you have old, non-LED-compatible dimmers or need perfectly identical color across a massive multi-bulb fixture.

**FAQ**

*Are these actually 60-watt equivalent?* Yes, they output roughly 800 lumens, which matches a traditional 60-watt incandescent. The actual draw is 5 watts.

*Will they work in enclosed fixtures?* Yes, the frosted glass and low heat output make them safe for enclosed sconces and ceiling fans.

*Do they work with all dimmers?* No. They work with modern LED-compatible dimmers. Older dimmers may cause buzzing or reduced range.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want warm, flicker-free, diffused light for your chandelier or sconces and have a reasonably modern dimmer setup. **Skip it if** you're working with outdated dimmer hardware or need perfectly matched color across a large fixture.
