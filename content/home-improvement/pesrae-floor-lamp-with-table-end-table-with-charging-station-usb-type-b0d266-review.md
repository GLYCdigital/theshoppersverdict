**The PESRAE Floor Lamp with Table: One Piece of Furniture, Three Jobs — Does It Actually Deliver?**

The PESRAE Floor Lamp with Table sits squarely in that "smart space-saving" category of furniture that promises to replace two things you own with one thing you'll actually use. And after digging through 225 ratings and the buyer feedback underneath them, here's the honest picture: this is a genuinely well-built piece that solves a real problem, but it comes with a couple of compromises you should know about before you hit "add to cart."

**What You're Actually Getting**

Out of the box, you get the floor lamp with an integrated side table surface at roughly waist height, a fabric shade, and a built-in charging station with one USB-A and one USB-C port. The tabletop is the headline feature — it's a wide, flat surface that sits on sturdy metal legs, not a flimsy shelf bolted onto a lamp pole. The base is weighted and broad enough that the whole unit feels planted.

One buyer put it plainly: *"I was skeptical about a lamp with a table attached, but this thing is solid. No wobble, even with my cat jumping on the table part."* That's the build quality story across the reviews — people consistently describe it as sturdier than they expected.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

The lighting does three color temperatures — warm, natural, and cool — controlled by a simple switch. It's not a dimmer, so you get three fixed brightness levels tied to each color. For reading, the cool white at full brightness works well. For evening ambiance, the warm setting is genuinely pleasant, not that harsh yellow you get from cheap lamps.

The charging station is the practical differentiator. Both ports sit in a recessed area on the tabletop, which means your phone stands up while charging rather than lying flat. That's a small design touch that makes a real difference — you can actually see notifications without picking the phone up. The USB-C port delivers enough juice for modern phones, though it's not a fast-charging spec, so expect standard charging speeds.

Here's the catch: the power cord is hardwired into the lamp. You can't detach it, so you're limited by the length of that cord (roughly 6 feet) to wherever your outlet is. Plan your placement accordingly.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Beyond the stability, the recurring praise centers on the space-saving design. Multiple reviewers in small apartments noted this replaced both a nightstand and a lamp. One wrote: *"I live in a studio and this freed up a ton of floor space. Two functions, one footprint."* Another common thread: assembly takes under 10 minutes, and all tools are included.

**Where It Falls Short**

The tabletop is about 15 inches in diameter. That's fine for a drink, a phone, and a book. It's not fine for a laptop and a lamp plus everything else. If you're expecting a full side-table experience, adjust your expectations — this is a lamp with a generous shelf, not a real end table.

The shade is fabric and not fully opaque, so light bleeds through slightly. Some buyers wanted a more focused downward beam. If you're sensitive to light diffusion, this matters.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Apartment dwellers** who need to consolidate furniture — this genuinely replaces two pieces
- **Bedroom readers** who want adjustable light and a place to set a book and phone
- **Anyone tired of cable clutter** — the integrated charging ports are a legit convenience

Skip it if you need a true side table surface for a laptop or if your outlet situation requires a detachable cord.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can the lamp be used with a smart plug or dimmer switch?**
A: No. The switch is built into the lamp body, and the three color modes are controlled there. A smart plug would just turn the whole lamp on/off — you can't control color temperature remotely.

**Q: Is the charging station fast-charging?**
A: No. It delivers standard charging speeds — enough to charge overnight or slowly during use. If you need rapid charging, use your own wall adapter instead.

**Q: How tall is the lamp, and does the table height work for a standard couch?**
A: The lamp stands about 55 inches tall, and the table surface sits at roughly 24 inches — comparable to a standard end table height. It works comfortably next to most sofas and beds.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're short on space and need to consolidate a lamp and a side table into one footprint. The build quality is genuinely better than the price point suggests, and the charging ports are a practical bonus.

**Skip it if** you need a full-size table surface or a detachable power cord.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A well-executed space-saver with minor compromises that most buyers will happily accept.
