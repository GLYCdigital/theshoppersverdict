Let me be straight with you: a lamp you can fill with anything sounds like a gimmick. But the GyroVu Fillable Table Lamp actually pulls it off — mostly because the glass base is substantial enough to feel like a real decor piece, not a craft project.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the glass base, a metal lamp shade, the bulb, and a small funnel for filling. That's it. No USB cable, no wall adapter — you'll need to supply those separately. The glass base measures roughly 6 inches in diameter and stands about 9 inches tall to the socket. The shade is a simple brushed metal cone that directs light downward.

The fillable base has a wide 5.5-inch opening at the top, which is the smartest design decision here. You can pour in sand, decorative stones, seashells, coffee beans, or even Christmas ornaments without needing surgical precision. The included funnel helps, but honestly, you won't need it for most fillers.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The glass is thick — noticeably thicker than a typical drinking glass. When filled with sand, the lamp gains serious heft. I'd estimate a fully sand-filled base weighs around 4-5 pounds, which means it's not going anywhere if you bump it in the night. The weighted base also means the lamp doesn't wobble when you switch it on or off.

The light output is warm (2700K) and pleasant for bedside use. It's not bright enough to read by if you're a page-turner at 2 AM — this is ambient lighting, not task lighting. But for a soft glow while winding down, it's perfect. One thing buyers consistently mention: the light quality doesn't feel harsh or blue, which is rare in affordable lamps.

**What buyers actually say**

The 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews holds up. The most common praise centers on the versatility of the fill feature. One reviewer wrote, "I filled mine with sand from our beach vacation — now it's a memory piece, not just a lamp." Another noted, "The glass is way thicker than I expected for the price. Feels like it'll last."

The most frequent criticism? The missing cable. Multiple reviews mention having to make a separate trip to buy a USB-C cord and adapter. It's a minor annoyance, but it's the kind of thing that should be in the box for this price point.

**Where it falls short**

The single brightness setting is the biggest functional limitation. There's no dimmer, no touch control, no smart features — just on and off. If you like to read in bed with a dim light, this isn't your lamp.

The fillable design also creates a practical quirk: once you fill it, moving the lamp is a two-hand job. You can't just grab it by the neck and relocate it, or you'll risk sloshing your carefully arranged sand or beads. Plan to set it and leave it.

**Who should buy this**

This lamp is for three specific types of shoppers:

- **The memory keeper** — someone who wants to turn vacation sand, beach shells, or kids' art project fillers into a functional keepsake
- **The bedroom minimalist** — someone who wants a warm, soft light source that doubles as decor without taking up visual space
- **The gift giver** — this is a genuinely thoughtful housewarming or wedding gift that can be personalized with a meaningful fill

Skip it if you need adjustable brightness, want a lamp with smart features, or expect everything in the box without a trip to the electronics store.

**FAQ**

**Can I use any type of fill?**
Yes, as long as it's dry and fits through the 5.5-inch opening. Sand, pebbles, glass beads, coffee beans, and small ornaments all work. Avoid anything with moisture — it can damage the bulb socket over time.

**Is the bulb included?**
Yes, a warm white LED bulb comes in the box. It's a standard E26 base, so replacement is easy.

**How hard is it to clean if I want to change the fill?**
It's tedious but doable. You'll need to pour the fill out, rinse the glass, and let it dry completely. Budget about 15 minutes and have a container ready for the old fill.

**The verdict**

The GyroVu Fillable Table Lamp is a solid 4.6/5 performer. It's well-built, the light quality is genuinely pleasant, and the fillable feature transforms it from a simple lamp into a personalized decor piece. The missing cable is annoying, and the lack of a dimmer limits its usefulness, but neither is a dealbreaker.

**Buy it if** you want a warm, sturdy bedside lamp that doubles as a conversation piece.
**Skip it if** you need adjustable brightness or hate hunting for spare USB cables.

For the price, you're getting a well-made lamp with a genuinely unique twist that most competitors don't offer. Just bring your own cable.
