The Moen 84346SRN Karis isn't the flashiest faucet on the shelf, and that's exactly the point. This is a workhorse bathroom fixture that skips the gimmicks and delivers what actually matters: reliable operation, a finish that survives real life, and an install that won't eat your entire Saturday. With a 4.7 average across 235 Amazon ratings, it's clearly earning its keep in bathrooms nationwide.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the single-handle faucet assembly, a Duralock quick-connect supply lines kit, and mounting hardware. Notably absent: a drain assembly. That's an extra purchase, and it's the most common complaint in buyer feedback. The faucet body measures roughly 5.5 inches tall with a 4.5-inch spout reach — compact proportions that suit a standard bathroom vanity without overwhelming a pedestal sink.

The brushed nickel finish is the real selling point here. Unlike chrome's relentless fingerprint magnet behavior, this finish shrugs off water spots and daily grime. One buyer put it simply: "Looks great, matches my other Moen fixtures perfectly, and doesn't show water spots like my old chrome faucet did."

## Performance and Build Quality

Moen's Duralock system deserves the praise it gets. The supply lines click into place with a satisfying snap, no soldering or pipe dope required. Homeowners consistently report install times between 15 and 30 minutes, even on first attempts. The single-lever handle moves with a smooth, dampened action — no cheap wobble or gritty resistance.

The cartridge inside is Moen's 1255 model, a proven design that's been in production for decades. That means replacement parts are cheap and available at virtually any hardware store. When something eventually wears out — and it will, eventually — you're looking at a $15 fix, not a $150 replacement.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Looking across the 235 ratings, three themes dominate positive feedback:

- **Finish durability**: Multiple reviewers mention the brushed nickel holding up after years of daily use, including in hard-water areas. "Two years in, still looks brand new," one long-term owner reported.
- **Ease of installation**: The Duralock system gets frequent shout-outs. "I'm not handy at all and had this installed in 20 minutes," one reviewer admitted.
- **Moen warranty reliability**: The limited lifetime warranty is genuine, and Moen's customer service actually responds. Several buyers noted successful replacement parts shipped free when issues arose.

## Where It Falls Short

The missing drain is the biggest annoyance. At this price point, most buyers expect a complete kit. Factor in $20–40 for a matching brushed nickel pop-up drain, and the total cost creeps higher than the sticker suggests.

The spout height also limits practicality. At 5.5 inches, you're not filling a large pitcher or washing a water bottle comfortably. The water stream hits the sink basin closer than taller faucets, which can cause splashing if you run it full blast.

A handful of reviewers mention the handle needing occasional re-tightening after months of use. It's a minor nuisance, not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing before you buy.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you're outfitting a guest bathroom, powder room, or rental property where reliability and easy install matter more than designer flair. It's also an excellent choice if you already own Moen fixtures — everything matches, and you'll only need one spare cartridge type on hand.

**Skip it if** you need a tall spout for utility sinks or if the missing drain assembly is a dealbreaker. Also consider stepping up if you want a more distinctive look — the Karis is clean and classic, but it won't turn heads.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this faucet work with a single-hole sink?**
A: Yes, it's designed for single-hole installation. If you're replacing a 4-inch centerset faucet, you'll need a deck plate (sold separately) to cover the extra holes.

**Q: Is the brushed nickel finish prone to scratching?**
A: It's more scratch-resistant than chrome, but no finish is indestructible. Use non-abrasive cleaners and avoid scouring pads. Buyers report the finish holding up well with normal cleaning.

**Q: Will this faucet fit an 8-inch widespread sink?**
A: No — this is strictly a single-hole or centerset configuration. For widespread installations, you'd need a different model.
