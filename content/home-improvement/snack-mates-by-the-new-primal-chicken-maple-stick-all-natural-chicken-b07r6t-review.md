# Snack Mates Chicken & Maple Stick: The All-Natural Meat Snack That Actually Delivers

Let's be honest — most meat snacks at the gas station are a chemistry experiment wrapped in plastic. So when a brand claims "all-natural" and backs it with a single-ingredient chicken stick, I'm skeptical. The New Primal's Snack Mates Chicken & Maple Stick earned its 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews for a reason: it's one of the few shelf-stable protein snacks that taste like real food.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains individually wrapped sticks — roughly 1.5 ounces each, about the size of a slim cigar. The packaging is straightforward: a resealable pouch that keeps the remaining sticks from drying out. As shown in the product image, these aren't your typical thick meat sticks; they're thinner, which means they cook up faster in terms of shelf life but also means you'll grab two or three in a sitting.

The ingredient list is refreshingly short: chicken, maple syrup, sea salt, and natural spices. No nitrates, no MSG, no soy protein isolate, no "natural flavors" hiding who-knows-what. For anyone tracking macros, each stick packs roughly 60 calories and 9 grams of protein — though the exact numbers vary slightly by batch.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Here's where Snack Mates separates itself from the pack. The texture is dry — genuinely dry, like a lean jerky rather than a moist sausage. Some reviewers called it "crumbly," and that's accurate. It doesn't snap like a pepperoni stick; it flakes and breaks apart as you bite. That's a feature if you want a clean, non-greasy snack, but it's a dealbreaker if you're expecting the soft, chewy texture of a Slim Jim.

The maple flavor is where the product shines. It's subtle — more like a whisper of sweetness than a syrup-drenched coating. One reviewer put it well: *"It tastes like real chicken that someone gently brushed with maple, not like candy pretending to be meat."* That balance is hard to achieve, and The New Primal nails it.

**What buyers consistently praise:**

- **Clean ingredients:** Multiple reviewers specifically mentioned feeling good about giving these to kids. One wrote: *"Finally a snack I don't feel guilty putting in my son's lunchbox."*
- **Portability:** The resealable bag and individual wrappers make these ideal for hiking, road trips, or office drawers. No refrigeration needed until opened.
- **Protein-to-calorie ratio:** For a snack, getting 9g of protein for 60 calories is genuinely impressive — better than most protein bars.

## Where It Falls Short

I won't sugarcoat the downsides:

**1. Price per ounce.** These aren't cheap. At typical retail, you're paying a premium for the clean ingredient list. If you're on a budget, you'll find cheaper (though less clean) options elsewhere.

**2. Dryness.** This is the most common complaint in the reviews. If you like moist, tender meat snacks, this will disappoint. The texture is closer to a dehydrated chicken breast than a sausage.

**3. Portion size.** The sticks are small. One is a snack, not a meal replacement. You'll likely eat two or three, which doubles the cost per sitting.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:**

- You're a parent who wants a clean, portable protein snack for kids' lunchboxes
- You're following keto, paleo, or Whole30 (this fits all three)
- You're a hiker or camper who needs shelf-stable protein that won't melt or spoil
- You actually like jerky-style texture and prefer subtle flavors over artificial ones

**Skip it if:**

- You want a soft, juicy meat snack
- You're on a tight budget — this premium costs more per serving
- You want a strong maple punch — the sweetness is gentle, not assertive

## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually gluten-free and paleo-friendly?**  
A: Yes. The ingredient list is just chicken, maple syrup, sea salt, and spices. No gluten, no grains, no dairy. It's compliant with paleo, keto, and Whole30 protocols.

**Q: How long do these stay fresh after opening the bag?**  
A: The resealable bag helps, but once opened, plan to finish within 1-2 weeks for best texture. Unopened, they have a long shelf life — check the "best by" date on the package.

**Q: Is the maple flavor artificial?**  
A: No. It comes from real maple syrup, which is why it's subtle. If you're used to artificial maple-flavored snacks, this will taste noticeably different — in a good way.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You value clean ingredients and don't mind a dry, jerky-like texture. This is a genuinely honest snack — no fillers, no fake flavors, just real chicken with a touch of maple.

**Skip it if:** You want a moist, indulgent meat snack or need maximum bang for your buck.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The New Primal delivers exactly what it promises, and the minor drawbacks (dryness, cost) are tradeoffs that come with choosing real ingredients over processed ones. For a cleaner protein snack, this is hard to beat.
