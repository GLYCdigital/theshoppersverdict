If you've spent any time shopping for wall sconces, you know the category is full of two types: cheap plastic junk that looks like it belongs in a dorm, and designer pieces that require an electrician and a second mortgage. This Long Double S Plug in Wall Sconce Set of 2 sits in a rare middle ground—it looks legitimately expensive, installs without touching your breaker box, and at 24 inches of reach, it actually functions as task lighting rather than just decoration.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains two complete sconces, each with a 24-inch double swing arm, two fabric drum shades, two 8-foot cords with inline rocker switches, and all mounting hardware. The mounting plate is a standard 4-inch round that covers most existing junction boxes, but since this is a plug-in model, you can mount it anywhere—no wiring required. The included template is genuinely useful; it's a paper cutout that shows you exactly where to drill.

Each arm is constructed from matte black steel with a slightly textured powder coat. The shades are a linen-look fabric stretched over a metal frame, and at 6 inches in diameter, they're proportioned well for the arm length. You're getting two separate plugs, which means you can mount these on opposite walls or far apart without daisy-chaining cords.

## Performance and Build Quality

The swing arms use a friction-hinge system with hex bolts. When properly tightened, they hold position firmly—no drooping. The full extension of 24 inches is the standout feature here. Most plug-in sconces max out at 12-14 inches of reach, which forces you to mount them directly next to your reading chair. This one reaches across a nightstand or over a headboard with room to spare.

Each socket accepts standard E26 bulbs up to 60 watts. I'd recommend LED filament bulbs in 2700K for bedrooms—the fabric shades diffuse the light nicely and eliminate harsh shadows. The inline rocker switch is on the cord, about 12 inches from the plug, which is convenient if you're mounting these above a headboard but less ideal if they're at desk height.

The build isn't perfect. The shades are riveted to the arm assembly, so you can't swap them for different colors or styles. And the friction hinges will loosen with heavy use—several reviewers mention retightening the hex bolts every few months. One verified buyer noted, "The arms are sturdy but need a quarter-turn on the Allen wrench every couple of months to keep them from sagging."

## Where It Falls Short

The cord is 8 feet, which sounds generous, but if you're mounting these on a tall wall, you may need a cord cover or an extension. There's no cord-hiding channel built into the arm, so the wire runs visibly along the metal. It's not ugly—the cord is black and blends with the finish—but it's not invisible either.

The shades are also a fingerprint magnet. The fabric picks up dust quickly, and cleaning them requires a lint roller or a gentle vacuum attachment. You can't wipe them down with a damp cloth without risking water spots.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you're a renter who wants permanent-looking lighting without drilling into wiring, or a homeowner who wants bedside reading lights without paying an electrician $200 to run new circuits. The 24-inch reach makes it ideal for king-size beds where standard sconces won't reach the middle of the mattress.

**Skip it if** you need adjustable color temperature or smart-home integration—this is a dumb fixture that requires smart bulbs to get those features. Also skip if you're mounting them outdoors; this is strictly an indoor piece.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I hardwire these if I change my mind later?**  
A: Yes. The mounting plate is a standard junction box size, and the wires are color-coded. You'd need to cut the plug off and wire it directly, which voids the warranty, but it's doable.

**Q: What bulb type is recommended?**  
A: Standard E26 base, up to 60 watts. LED filament bulbs look best through the fabric shades. Avoid CFLs—they cast weird shadows through the linen.

**Q: How far apart should I mount the two sconces?**  
A: For a king bed, mount them about 60 inches apart, centered over each nightstand. The 24-inch reach gives you plenty of overlap in the middle if needed.

## The Verdict

**4.6/5 stars.**

This is one of the few plug-in sconces that doesn't look like a compromise. The long arms, quality finish, and straightforward installation make it a smart buy for renters and homeowners alike. The shade replacement limitation and periodic hinge tightening are minor annoyances, not dealbreakers.

**Buy it if** you want functional, adjustable lighting without an electrician. **Skip it if** you require permanent hardwired fixtures with interchangeable shades. For the price of a single designer sconce, you're getting two solid units that will serve you for years.
