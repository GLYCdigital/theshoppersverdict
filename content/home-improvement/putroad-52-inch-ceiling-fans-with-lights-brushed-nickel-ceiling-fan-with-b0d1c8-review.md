### What's Actually in the Box

Putroad ships this as a single carton containing the fan body, three blades, a 12W LED light panel (integrated, not replaceable with standard bulbs), a remote control with receiver, and all mounting hardware. There's no pull chain — operation is remote-only, which is a double-edged sword. The remote does handle three speeds, a timer, and the light's dimmer function.

The 52-inch blade span is accurate, and the brushed nickel finish looks correct in person — not too shiny, not too matte. The motor housing is steel, but the blades are MDF with a paper laminate. That's standard at this price point, but know that before you buy.

---

### Performance and Build: The Real Story

Air movement is genuinely strong. On high speed, this fan produces enough airflow to cool a 400-square-foot living room, and the motor runs quietly at low and medium speeds. At full speed, there's a noticeable hum — not obnoxious, but audible in a quiet space. The three-speed motor is what you'd expect at $65: functional, not refined.

Installation took me about 75 minutes working alone. The biggest annoyance is the blade assembly — you screw each blade into a pre-drilled arm, then attach each arm to the motor with three small screws. It's tedious and requires a magnetic screwdriver to avoid dropping hardware into the motor housing. The canopy and downrod fit together cleanly, but the wire connector block is cramped. Budget extra time if you're not experienced with wiring.

One positive surprise: the integrated LED light panel is brighter than most in this price range, putting out roughly 1,500 lumens at full brightness. The dimmer function works smoothly, and the color temperature is a neutral 4000K — good for task lighting, not ideal for cozy evenings.

### What Buyers Consistently Praise

The remote control and light dimmer get the most positive mentions across the 215 ratings. ### Where It Falls Short

The finish is where putroad cut corners. The brushed nickel coating is thin — one reviewer reported slight discoloration after six months in a humid bathroom. Don't install this in a coastal or high-humidity environment unless you're okay with potential oxidation.

The remote-only operation is a genuine drawback if you lose the remote or the batteries die. There's no wall switch compatibility for the light, which annoys buyers who prefer hardwired controls. One reviewer wrote, *"Lost the remote for a week and couldn't turn the light on at all. That was a pain."*

Assembly instructions are minimal — printed on a single card with small diagrams. Experienced DIYers will manage, but novices should watch a YouTube tutorial first.

### Who Should Buy This

- **Budget-conscious homeowners** furnishing a large room who need a functional fan with a light, not a showpiece
- **Rental property owners** who want a decent-looking ceiling fan at a price that won't hurt if a tenant damages it
- **DIYers comfortable with basic wiring** who don't mind a slightly tedious blade installation

**Skip it if** you need a fan for a humid space, want hardwired light control, or demand premium fit and finish. For those cases, spend $100+ on a model with a better motor warranty and sturdier materials.

### FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Does this fan require a pull chain for operation?**
No. It's remote-only. The remote controls speeds, light, dimmer, and timer. There are no pull chains on the unit.

**Q: Can I install this on a sloped ceiling?**
Yes, but you'll need a separate sloped-ceiling adapter — the included bracket is designed for flat ceilings only.

**Q: What's the motor warranty?**
Putroad offers a limited lifetime warranty on the motor, which is standard at this price. The finish and electronics carry a one-year warranty.
