If you've ever dumped a drawer of mixed screws, anchors, and random fittings onto a bench just to find one 10-24 nut, you already know the pain this organizer solves. The CRAFTSMAN 14-compartment organizer 2-pack is a straightforward fix for that chaos — and at roughly $20 for two boxes, it's the kind of purchase you'll wonder why you didn't make years ago.

**What actually shows up**

Two identical organizers, each 14.5 inches long, 8.5 inches wide, and about 1.5 inches deep. Each has 14 compartments — six full-width slots running front to back, plus eight smaller squares along the sides. The clear polycarbonate lid is the headline feature: you can inventory your contents at a glance without unclipping anything. The latch is a single central clip that snaps closed with a satisfying, positive click. There's also a molded-in handle on top, though these are really designed to sit flat in a toolbox drawer or on a shelf, not to be carried around like a briefcase.

**How it holds up in real use**

I've had a set on my workbench for about three months now — one dedicated to drywall anchors and screws, the other to electrical fittings and wire nuts. The compartments are sized well for the jobs most homeowners and DIYers actually do. Screws up to about 2 inches fit fine; 3-inch deck screws will crowd the smaller compartments. The dividers are molded into the base, not removable, so you're locked into this layout — no customizing. That's fine for most people, but worth knowing if you need configurable space.

The lid seal is decent but not airtight. Fine for a garage or workshop; I wouldn't trust it for long-term storage of anything that corrodes easily. The latch has held up through dozens of opens and closes without loosening, and the hinge hasn't cracked under normal use. One buyer put it plainly: *"These are sturdy enough for my garage. I've dropped one off a ladder and it didn't break — the latch held."* That matches my experience. The plastic feels dense, not flimsy, and CRAFTSMAN's red-and-black branding is subtle enough not to look cheap.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews isn't an accident. The most repeated compliments center on three things: the clear lid, the two-pack value, and the secure latch. One reviewer wrote, *"Finally, I can see what's inside without opening every box. This is how all organizers should be made."* Another said, *"Two for the price of one elsewhere — I bought two sets and organized my entire fastener drawer."* The latch gets consistent shout-outs too — people trust it not to pop open mid-transport, which is more than you can say for cheaper Harbor Freight-style boxes.

**Where it falls short**

Nothing's perfect, and this organizer has two honest limitations. First, the living hinge — that thin strip of plastic connecting lid to base — will eventually fatigue. With daily use, expect two to three years before it cracks. It's not replaceable, so when the hinge goes, the box goes. Second, the compartments are fixed-size. If you store large items like spade bits or bulky connectors, you'll find the smaller squares too tight. And the latch, while secure, is a single point of failure — if it breaks, the lid won't stay shut.

**Who should buy this**

- **Homeowners with a junk drawer** — this is the single best $20 you'll spend to make that drawer functional.
- **DIYers with a small fastener collection** — if you've got less than a few hundred screws and anchors, two of these will hold everything.
- **Gift-givers** — this is a genuinely useful, low-cost housewarming or Father's Day item for someone who owns a drill.

Skip it if you need heavy-duty, portable storage (look at a metal cantilever box instead) or if you need configurable compartments (DeWalt's adjustable system is worth the extra money).

**FAQ**

**Q: Will these fit in a standard toolbox drawer?**
A: Yes. At 14.5 x 8.5 inches, they slide into most standard rolling tool chest drawers without issue. Measure your drawer first if it's shallow — the depth is about 1.5 inches.

**Q: Can I remove the dividers to make bigger compartments?**
A: No. The dividers are molded into the base. If you need adjustable layouts, look elsewhere.

**Q: Are these airtight or waterproof?**
A: No. The lid seals reasonably well against dust but isn't watertight. Don't store items that rust easily in a damp environment.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want an inexpensive, genuinely useful way to organize small parts and hardware. For the price of a single organizer from most brands, you get two solid, see-through boxes with a latch you can trust. **Skip it if** you need configurable compartments or heavy-duty portability — this is a drawer organizer, not a job-site toolbox.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a well-executed, no-nonsense product that does exactly what it promises, with minor longevity caveats that are fair at this price point.
