If you're shopping for that classic industrial warehouse look, you've probably seen a dozen RLM shades that all claim to be "heavy duty." Most of them aren't. The HTM Lighting Solutions 17-inch RLM Warehouse Shade with Gooseneck is one of the few that actually backs that claim up. ## What You Actually Get

This isn't a full light fixture — it's the shade and gooseneck arm assembly. The 17-inch dome is stamped from heavy-gauge steel with a powder-coated finish that feels substantial in hand. The gooseneck arm measures roughly 10 inches from base to shade and pivots 360 degrees at the mounting base, which is the feature most buyers rave about.

You'll need to supply the bulb (medium-base E26) and a way to mount it. It threads onto a standard 3/4-inch pipe, so it works with most ceiling-mount pendant kits, wall sconce arms, or a simple threaded ceiling adapter. Some buyers pair it with a cord kit and plug it into a wall outlet — it's flexible that way.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

This is where HTM separates itself from the cheap import options. The steel is thick enough that you won't see it flex or dent during installation. The powder coat is evenly applied and holds up well in kitchens and covered outdoor porches. Multiple buyers mention having theirs installed for over a year with zero rust or fading.

The gooseneck is the standout feature. It holds its position firmly — no sagging, no drooping over time. One reviewer noted: *"I aimed mine at a specific piece of artwork and it hasn't moved in eight months."* That's the kind of real-world feedback that matters.

Light output depends entirely on your bulb choice. The open-bottom design creates a focused downward cone of light with minimal spill. A 60-watt equivalent LED gives solid task lighting over a kitchen island or workbench. But don't expect this to wash a room in ambient light — it's directional by design.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. **The bulb is not included**, and it must be rated for enclosed fixtures — some buyers miss this and end up with a bulb that burns out early. The shade's open bottom also means it collects dust inside, and the inner surface is white-painted, so it shows grime over time. A quick wipe handles it, but it's worth knowing.

Also, the mounting hardware is minimal. You get the shade and arm assembly — nothing else. If you don't already have a ceiling box or pendant kit, factor in an extra $15–25 for the mounting components.
