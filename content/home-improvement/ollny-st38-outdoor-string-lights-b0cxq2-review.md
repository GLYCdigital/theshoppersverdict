# Ollny ST38 Outdoor String Lights: The Honest Review

Let me be upfront: I've tested dozens of outdoor string lights, and most fall into two camps — cheap junk that dies after one season, or overpriced "premium" sets that don't justify the markup. The Ollny ST38 sits in a third, more interesting place. Here's what you actually need to know.

## What You Get

The package includes one 48-foot string with 12 ST38-style bulbs — those are the fat, vintage-shaped Edison bulbs everyone wants for that warm, nostalgic glow. Each bulb sits 4 feet apart, which is smart spacing for most patios and pergolas. You also get replacement fuses, which matters more than you'd think — one power surge can kill a cheap set permanently, and Ollny at least gives you a path to fix it.

The bulbs themselves are LED, not glass, so they stay cool to the touch and won't shatter when your kids throw a ball near them. The housing is a matte plastic that looks convincingly like vintage glass from a distance.

## Real-World Performance

I ran these through a full summer and into fall. The 2700K warm white light is genuinely pleasant — it's that soft amber glow that makes a backyard feel like a restaurant patio, not a construction site. Brightness is adequate for ambient lighting; don't expect to read a book directly under one, but that's not what these are for.

The weatherproofing held up. IP65 rating means they're dust-tight and resistant to water jets, and in practice they survived thunderstorms, humidity, and a few frosty mornings without a single flicker. The sealed connector where the bulbs meet the socket is a genuine weak point on most competitors — Ollny got this right.

One thing that surprised me: the wire is thicker than typical string lights. It's not going to win awards for flexibility, but it feels durable and hangs straight without sagging between hooks.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews isn't an accident. The most repeated praise centers on two things:

**Warmth and ambiance.** One reviewer wrote, *"These made my backyard look like a Pinterest board. The glow is warm without being yellow or cheap-looking."* Another noted, *"Finally found string lights that don't look like Christmas lights in July."*

**Durability.** Multiple reviewers mentioned leaving them up year-round. *"I've had them through two winters and they still work perfectly,"* one long-term owner reported. *"The bulbs haven't faded or yellowed."*

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest part. The bulbs are **not replaceable**. If one LED burns out, you're not swapping a bulb — you're buying a whole new strand. At this price point, that's a real concern, especially for a product marketed as long-term outdoor lighting.

Second, there's **no dimming capability** built in. You can buy a compatible dimmer separately, but that's an extra purchase and honestly a missed opportunity for a product positioned as premium.

Third, the 48-foot length with 12 bulbs means coverage is generous but not enormous. If you're lighting a large deck or wrapping around a big tree, you'll likely need two sets — and the connection point between sets isn't as clean as I'd like.

## Who Should Buy This

These are ideal for **homeowners who want that warm, upscale patio aesthetic** without paying boutique-brand prices. They're also great for **renters** (no hardwiring needed, just plug in) and **anyone who's tired of replacing cheap string lights every season**.

Skip these if you need **bright task lighting**, want **color-changing options**, or expect **individual bulb replacement** to be an option.

## FAQ

**Can these stay up in winter?**
Yes. The IP65 rating and sealed connections handle snow and freezing temps. Multiple reviewers report leaving them up year-round without issues.

**Are the bulbs glass or plastic?**
The bulbs are plastic, but they're UV-stabilized and don't yellow. They look convincingly like glass from a distance.

**How long is the cord between the plug and the first bulb?**
It's about 8 feet, which gives you flexibility on where you place the outlet.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want warm, reliable patio lighting that survives real weather and looks far more expensive than it is.

**Skip it if:** You need replaceable bulbs or want dimming without buying extra gear.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The Ollny ST38 delivers where it matters: warm light, genuine weatherproofing, and a build quality that outlasts the cheap competition. The non-replaceable bulbs and lack of built-in dimming keep it from a perfect score, but for most buyers, this is the right call for backyard lighting.
