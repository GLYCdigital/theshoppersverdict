The Delta Albion in brushed gold is the faucet you buy when you want the warm, luxury look of gold without the maintenance nightmare of polished finishes. At 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, it's clearly doing something right—but let's dig into whether it's the right pick for your bathroom remodel.

## What's Actually in the Box

Delta ships the complete faucet assembly, a matching pop-up drain with lift rod, and the optional deck plate for converting to a 3-hole setup. You also get the supply lines pre-attached (standard 3/8-inch compression fittings) and the installation hardware. Notably absent: a drain flange wrench. You'll need your own basin wrench or a pair of channel locks for the under-sink work.

The faucet body itself is solid brass—you can feel the heft when you lift it out of the box. The brushed gold finish is applied consistently across all surfaces, including the underside of the spout where cheaper faucets often show raw metal.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the Albion earns its keep. The **Diamond Seal Technology** is the headline feature, and it's not marketing fluff. Instead of a rubber washer that degrades over time, Delta uses a diamond-coated ceramic valve. What that means for you: fewer leaks, smoother handle operation, and a valve that should outlast the finish on the faucet. One buyer noted, "Three years in, the handle still moves like day one—no drips, no stiffness."

The spout reaches 5.5 inches from the center of the base, which clears most standard bathroom sinks. Flow rate is capped at a sensible 1.2 gallons per minute—enough for handwashing and teeth brushing without splashing all over the countertop.

The brushed gold finish is the real star. Unlike polished gold that shows every fingerprint and water spot, the brushed texture scatters light and hides smudges. Multiple reviewers specifically called this out: "I was worried about maintenance with gold, but this finish looks clean even after a week of kids using it."

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The **pop-up drain assembly feels noticeably lighter** than the faucet body itself. It works fine, but if you're comparing side-by-side with a Kohler or a high-end Delta, you'll feel the difference. Several reviewers swapped it out for a brass drain from a third-party manufacturer.

The **handle has a shorter throw** than traditional faucets—about 100 degrees of rotation versus the 120+ you might expect. For most people this is a non-issue, but if you're used to fine-tuning water temperature, the adjustment feels more sensitive than it should. You'll find yourself making micro-adjustments to hit that perfect warm setting.

Finish matching is the biggest practical concern. Brushed gold has a muted, satin appearance that looks distinctly different from polished gold. If your bathroom has polished gold lighting fixtures or cabinet hardware, the Albion will clash. This is the #1 complaint in the review section: "Loved the faucet, but it didn't match my polished gold mirrors. Had to return it."

## Who Should Buy This

The Albion is ideal for:

- **Bathroom remodels aiming for a warm, modern aesthetic**—brushed gold pairs beautifully with white, cream, or dark green vanities
- **Households with kids or high traffic**—the fingerprint-resistant finish saves daily wiping
- **DIY installers**—the single-hole design and pre-attached supply lines make this a 30-minute job for most people

Skip it if your bathroom hardware is polished gold, or if you're on a tight budget—there are solid brushed gold options from Moen and Pfister for $30-40 less, though you'll sacrifice the Diamond Seal valve.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this fit a standard 1-hole sink?**
A: Yes. It's designed for a single-hole configuration, and the included deck plate covers 3-hole sinks if you're replacing an older faucet with separate handles.

**Q: Is the brushed gold finish durable?**
A: Delta uses a PVD (physical vapor deposition) coating process. It's the same technology used in high-end fixtures and should hold up for years with normal cleaning. Avoid abrasive cleaners—use mild soap and water.

**Q: What's the warranty situation?**
A: Delta offers a limited lifetime warranty on this faucet. It covers parts and finish, but not labor or installation costs.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if** you want a durable, low-maintenance gold faucet with a premium valve system and a finish that actually looks good after a week of real use.

**Skip it if** you need exact finish matching with polished gold hardware, or you want a heavier, more substantial drain assembly included in the box.

The Delta Albion is a well-engineered faucet with a finish that solves the classic gold-faucet problem. It's not perfect—the drain and handle throw are minor compromises—but for the money, it delivers where it counts: reliable operation and a finish that keeps its looks.
