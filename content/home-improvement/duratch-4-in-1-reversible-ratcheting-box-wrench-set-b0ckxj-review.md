If you've ever dropped a 10mm socket into the dark recesses of an engine bay, you already know why the DURATCH 4-in-1 Reversible Ratcheting Box Wrench Set deserves a hard look. This isn't a gimmick — it's a genuine space-saver that consolidates 16 metric sizes into four wrenches. Each tool features a sliding ratcheting head with both box-end and open-end profiles, meaning one wrench handles 10, 12, 14, and 17mm fasteners depending on how you position the head.

**What's actually in the box:** Four wrenches covering 8-19mm (skipping 9, 11, 15, 16, and 18mm — the sizes most DIYers rarely touch). The sliding heads lock into position with a detent, and each wrench has a 72-tooth ratchet mechanism that delivers a tight 5-degree arc. That's the number that matters in confined spaces where a standard combination wrench can't swing. The Cr-Mo alloy steel construction with a matte finish isn't just for looks — it sheds oil and grime better than polished chrome, and the grip is noticeably more secure when your hands are greasy.

**Real-world performance:** I've used these on brake caliper brackets, suspension links, and belt tensioners. The reversing lever is easy to flip with one thumb, even wearing work gloves. The sliding head is the standout feature: you can flip it 180 degrees to approach a fastener from either side without removing the wrench, which genuinely saves time on repetitive jobs.

**Frequently asked questions:**

**Q: Are these strong enough for breaker-bar torque?**
A: No. These are ratcheting wrenches, not impact tools. They're rated for hand torque only. Use a six-point socket and breaker bar for seized fasteners, then finish with these.

**Q: Can I use them with a cheater pipe for extra leverage?**
A: I wouldn't. The sliding head introduces a potential failure point under extreme torque. If you need more leverage, use a standard combination wrench.

**Q: How do the sizes work — does each wrench really do four sizes?**
A: Yes. Each wrench has a sliding head with a box-end on one side and an open-end on the other. Slide it to one of two positions, and you get a different size on each end. Flip the head 180 degrees for two more sizes. It's clever and functional.
