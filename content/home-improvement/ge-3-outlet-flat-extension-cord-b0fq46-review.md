## The $10 Fix for Your Worst Outlet Problem

You know the frustration: you've got a lamp, a phone charger, and a laptop brick, but only one wall outlet — and it's half-hidden behind your nightstand. The GE 3-Outlet Flat Extension Cord solves that specific problem for about the price of a fast-food meal. At 4.8 stars across 240 ratings, it's quietly become one of the most practical little gadgets in the home improvement aisle.

## What You Actually Get

This is a compact outlet tap, not a traditional extension cord. The package includes one unit with three grounded outlets arranged in a triangular pattern, a flat plug that sits nearly flush against your wall plate, and a short cord (roughly 12 inches) that exits at a right angle. The body is white, standard UL-rated plastic, and it's rated for 15 amps / 125 volts — plenty for typical household loads like lamps, phone chargers, and small electronics.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where this little device shines. The flat plug design means your furniture can sit tight against the wall — no more gap behind your dresser or nightstand. The right-angle cord exit routes the wire downward along the baseboard, keeping things tidy and out of sight.

The triangle outlet layout is actually smarter than it looks. It gives you three connections in a compact footprint, and because the outlets are rotated, most standard plugs won't block each other. One buyer noted, *"I've bought three of these now. They fit behind my TV stand perfectly and I can still plug in my soundbar and streaming stick without fighting for space."*

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The consistency of positive feedback is striking. Across 240 ratings, the recurring themes are:

- **Space savings**: *"Finally, a plug that doesn't stick out three inches from the wall."*
- **Build quality**: *"Feels solid, not cheap. The plug fits snugly in the outlet without wobbling."*
- **Versatility**: *"Use it for my router, modem, and smart home hub — all in one spot."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. First, there's no USB port — if you want to charge a phone directly, you'll need to bring your own adapter, which somewhat defeats the space-saving purpose. Second, the cord is only about a foot long. This isn't a reach extender; it's strictly for expanding a single outlet's capacity. Third, if you're using two bulky wall warts (those oversized chargers), the triangle layout can occasionally interfere — one adjacent outlet may become partially blocked.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Apartment dwellers** with limited outlets in older buildings
- **Home theater enthusiasts** cramming devices behind a console
- **Parents** needing to safely expand a hard-to-reach outlet behind a crib or dresser
- **Anyone** who's frustrated by plugs sticking out from the wall

Skip it if you need to run power across a room — get a proper extension cord instead. Also skip it if you want USB-C fast charging built in.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I plug a power strip into this?**  
A: Technically yes, but don't daisy-chain high-draw appliances. Keep total load under 15 amps.

**Q: Does it work with three-prong grounded plugs?**  
A: Yes. All three outlets are grounded, and the plug itself is a standard three-prong grounded design.

**Q: Is it safe for outdoor use?**  
A: No. This is an indoor-rated device only.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need to reclaim wall space behind furniture or expand a single outlet without adding a bulky power strip. It's well-built, thoughtfully designed, and delivers exactly what it promises.

**Skip it if** you need USB ports, long reach, or heavy-duty appliance power.
