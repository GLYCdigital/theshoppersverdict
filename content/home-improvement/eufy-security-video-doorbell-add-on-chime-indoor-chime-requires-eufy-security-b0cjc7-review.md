You've got a eufy doorbell, and you've already missed three packages because you didn't hear the chime from the kitchen. This add-on chime exists for exactly that problem — but is it the fix you need, or just another gadget that'll gather dust? Let's get into it.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the chime unit itself, a micro-USB cable, a wall adapter, and a small mounting bracket. No screws, no drill template — this thing is designed to sit on a counter or plug directly into an outlet. The unit measures roughly 3.5 inches square and about an inch thick, so it's compact enough to tuck behind a bookshelf or on a hallway table.

As shown in the product image, the front face is a clean white panel with a subtle speaker grille. The branding is minimal, which helps it blend in. There's no display, no camera, no smart speaker features — this is a pure, single-purpose chime.

**Setup and Performance**

Pairing took me 90 seconds. You plug it in, open the eufy Security app, hit "Add Device," and the app finds it instantly over your Wi-Fi. No QR codes, no Bluetooth handshake drama. It connects to your existing eufy doorbell through the app, and you're live.

Volume is the headline feature. The unit pushes up to 90dB at maximum — that's about as loud as a lawnmower from six feet away. I tested it from the garage with the door closed, and I heard it clearly. You can dial it down through the app if you don't want to alarm the neighbors, but the range of adjustment is genuinely useful.

You get eight chime tones, from classic "ding-dong" to a few more modern electronic sounds. If you have two doorbells, you can assign different tones to each — a practical touch that saves you guessing which entrance someone's at.

**What Buyers Are Saying**

The 4.1-star average (204 ratings) reflects a product that works as advertised for most people. **Where It Falls Short**

Be clear-eyed about this: without a eufy doorbell, this is a paperweight. It's an add-on, not a standalone product. If you're shopping for a chime before committing to a doorbell system, hold off.

The plastic housing feels a bit hollow and cheap — nothing premium about it. It won't look out of place in a utility room or garage, but if you want something that blends into a designer living room, keep looking.

The Wi-Fi dependency is a real weakness. If your router goes down, so does your chime. A hardwired doorbell chime keeps working during an internet outage; this doesn't.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Two-story homeowners** who can't hear the standard doorbell from upstairs — this is the exact use case it solves.
- **Garage or workshop users** who need an audible alert over power tools.
- **eufy ecosystem owners** who already have doorbells and want multi-room coverage without running wires.

**Who Should Skip It**

- **Renters with no eufy doorbell** — you're buying into a system you don't have.
- **Tech minimalists** who'd rather get a smart speaker that can announce doorbell presses and do a dozen other things.
- **Anyone with unreliable Wi-Fi** — you'll be re-pairing more often than you'd like.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the eufy chime work with non-eufy doorbells?**
A: No. It only pairs with eufy Security doorbells through the eufy Security app. Don't buy it expecting compatibility with Ring, Nest, or other brands.

**Q: Can I add more than one chime to my doorbell?**
A: Yes. You can pair multiple chimes to a single doorbell, which is useful if you want coverage in a basement and a second floor. Each chime connects independently to your Wi-Fi.

**Q: Does it work without the eufy HomeBase or Sync Module?**
A: The chime connects directly to your Wi-Fi network and pairs with the doorbell through the app. You don't need a separate hub, but the doorbell itself must be a eufy model that supports add-on chimes.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You already own a eufy doorbell and need louder, multi-room alerts. Setup is painless, volume is genuinely loud, and the 8-tone selection adds practical flexibility.

**Skip it if:** You're doorbell-shopping from scratch, have flaky Wi-Fi, or care about aesthetics. The plastic build and outlet tether will grate on you.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — It does one thing and does it well, but the hard Wi-Fi requirement and eufy-only compatibility keep it from being a universal solution. For eufy owners who've missed one too many deliveries, this is a solid, inexpensive fix.
