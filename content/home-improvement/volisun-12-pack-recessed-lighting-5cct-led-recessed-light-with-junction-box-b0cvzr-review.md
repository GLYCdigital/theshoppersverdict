If you're remodeling a basement or finishing a bonus room, the VOLISUN 12-pack of 5CCT recessed lights deserves a hard look. Most LED wafer lights force you to commit to a color temperature at checkout. This one lets you flip a switch on the housing to choose anywhere from warm 2700K to daylight 5000K — after it's mounted. That's genuinely useful if you're indecisive or if your spouse keeps changing their mind.

## What you actually get

Open the box and you'll find 12 complete fixtures — each with an integrated LED board, a junction box pre-wired for direct connection, and a slim trim ring that sits nearly flush against the ceiling. The housings are IC-rated, meaning you can bury them in insulation without fire risk. They're rated for both new construction and remodel applications, and the included junction box means you don't need a separate can or transformer. You're looking at roughly 4.5 inches of ceiling cutout per fixture — standard for retrofit wafers.

As shown in the product image, the driver and wiring are pre-assembled, so the installation is mostly about cutting holes, running Romex, and clipping the connectors together. A competent DIYer can knock out all 12 in a weekend.

## Real-world performance

The 5CCT selector is the headline feature. One fixture covers warm, soft white, bright white, and daylight. The switch is on the housing side, so you'll want to set it before you fully seat the trim — or pull the light down later if you need to adjust. The 2700K setting is genuinely warm, not that sickly yellow you get from cheap LEDs. The 5000K is crisp and clean, good for garages or workspaces. Middle settings (3000K/3500K/4000K) are subtle shifts, which is fine for most rooms.

Brightness sits around 900 lumens per fixture — roughly equivalent to a 60-watt incandescent — which is adequate for general lighting in hallways, bedrooms, and kitchens. If you're lighting a large open living area, you'll want them on a dimmer or spaced closer than the typical 4-foot grid.

## Where it falls short

No product is flawless, and this one has honest tradeoffs.

**Dimmer compatibility is picky.** The built-in driver doesn't play nice with all dimmers. Some buyers report a faint hum at low settings, and a few mention flicker at the bottom of the dimming range. If you plan to dim these, use a quality ELV or LED-rated dimmer — don't cheap out here.

**The fixed trim limits you.** Unlike can lights with interchangeable trims, these are all-in-one units. If you later want a baffle trim or a different aesthetic, you're replacing the entire fixture, not just the trim ring.

**The color switch is awkward after install.** If you realize the 5000K is too clinical after the trim is seated, you're pulling the light back down to flip the switch. Plan ahead and set your temperatures before finalizing each fixture.

Yes. The housings are IC-rated, so you can install them in insulated ceilings without clearance requirements.

**Q: Can I use these on a dimmer?**
Yes, but choose a quality LED-compatible dimmer. Avoid old magnetic or standard incandescent dimmers, which can cause flicker or hum.
