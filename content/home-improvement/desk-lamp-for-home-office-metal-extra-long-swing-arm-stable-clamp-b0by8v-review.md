**Skip the flimsy LED strips. This is a real lamp.**  

If you’re reading this because you’re tired of craning your neck toward a tiny pool of light, you’re in the right place. The **Extra Long Swing Arm Desk Lamp** isn’t a novelty — it’s a tool. With a 4.6-star average across 229 Amazon ratings, it’s clearly earning its keep on a lot of desks. But is it the right one for yours? Let’s dig past the marketing photos.

---

### What’s actually in the box

You get the lamp head, the two-piece swing arm assembly, a heavy steel C-clamp base, and the AC adapter. Assembly takes about four minutes — you slide the arm segments together and tighten two Allen bolts (wrench included). The clamp opens to about 2.2 inches, so it fits standard desks, standing desks, and even thicker workbench tops.

The head is a 12-inch LED bar that pivots 180 degrees vertically and rotates 350 degrees horizontally. Arm joints are tension-adjustable via a hex key, so you can set the stiffness to your liking. That’s a detail most sub-$50 lamps skip, and it matters.

---

### Performance and real-world feel

The headline feature is the reach. At full extension, the head sits roughly 30 inches from the clamp pivot. That means you can mount it on the back corner of a 60-inch desk and still light the front edge. I measured it — that’s a lot of coverage.

Light quality is solid, not spectacular. Three color temperatures (3000K warm, 4500K neutral, 6000K cool) and three brightness levels (roughly 200, 400, and 600 lumens at the head). The 6000K setting is crisp for detail work; the 3000K is pleasant for evening reading. There’s no flicker at any setting, and the diffuser panel does a decent job cutting harsh shadows. One buyer noted:

> *“I’ve had this for three months. The arm stays exactly where I put it — no drooping. My old lamp drove me nuts.”*

That’s the common thread in the reviews: stability. The clamp base is genuinely heavy (about 4.5 pounds of steel), and the arm joints hold position even with the head angled down over a soldering iron or schematic.

---

### What buyers consistently praise

- **The reach.** Multiple reviewers mention lighting a whole 48-inch workbench from one corner mount.
- **No wobble.** The clamp bites hard, and the arm doesn’t flex under the head’s weight.
- **Easy adjust.** Tension screws mean you can make it stiff for precise positioning or loose for quick swings.

---

### Where it falls short

Two honest gripes. First, the clamp pad is bare metal on the underside. If you tighten it hard on a walnut or oak desk, you *will* leave a mark. A $1 felt furniture pad solves it, but it shouldn’t be your job. Second, there’s no USB port — a feature creeping into lamps at this price point. If you want to charge your phone or power a fan, you’ll need a separate outlet.

Also note: the power adapter is a brick, not a slim plug. It won’t hide behind a power strip easily.

---

### Who should buy this

- **Drafters, engineers, and hobbyists** who need light to reach over deep work areas.
- **Home office workers** with wide L-shaped desks who want one light source instead of two.
- **Anyone tired of flimsy plastic arms** that sag after three months.

Skip it if you have a delicate antique desk (the clamp pressure is real) or if you absolutely need USB charging built in.

---

### FAQ

**Q: Will the clamp damage my desk?**  
It can leave marks if over-tightened on soft wood. Use a thin cloth or felt pad between the clamp and desk surface. On metal or laminate, you’re fine.

**Q: How long is the power cord?**  
About 6 feet. If your outlet is far from the desk, grab a short extension cord.

**Q: Can I replace the LED bar if it burns out?**  
The LED board is not user-replaceable — it’s integrated. The expected lifespan is rated at 50,000 hours, so that’s years of daily use.

### The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a stable, long-reach lamp that won’t droop or wobble. It’s a workhorse for serious desk setups.

**Skip it if** you want a compact accent light or need USB ports. This is a functional tool, not a lifestyle gadget.
