# Moen T2470BL Genta Posi-Temp with Built-in 3-Function Transfer Valve Trim Review

If you're remodeling a bathroom and want a shower system that actually works the way modern plumbing should, the Moen Genta T2470BL deserves a close look. This isn't just a pretty faceplate — it's the control center for a three-way shower setup, and it solves a problem most homeowners don't realize they have until they're standing in a suddenly freezing stream of water.

## What's Actually in the Box

Let's be clear about what you're buying. This is the **trim kit only** — the visible hardware that mounts on your wall. You're getting the handle, escutcheon plate, the built-in transfer valve mechanism, and the mounting hardware. The actual rough-in valve body (the part inside your wall that does the pressure balancing) is not included.

You'll need a **Moen Posi-Temp valve body** — either the 2510 (standard) or 2520 (with integrated stops) — which typically runs $60–$90 on its own. That's not a hidden fee; it's standard practice for Moen's two-piece system, but budget for it before you start.

## How It Performs in Real Life

The headline feature here is the **built-in 3-function transfer valve**. Instead of a separate diverter knob that requires twisting and hoping, this trim lets you flip between three outlets — showerhead, handheld wand, and tub spout — with a single lever. The mechanism is smooth and positive; you feel a distinct click at each stop, so there's no guessing whether you've actually engaged the right outlet.

The **Posi-Temp pressure-balancing technology** is the real workhorse. When someone flushes a toilet or turns on a sink while you're showering, the internal cartridge adjusts hot and cold water flow to keep your temperature steady. It's not instant — there's a half-second lag — but it's dramatically better than a standard valve that swings 10 degrees.

One thing worth noting: the **handle is plastic**. It's a dense, well-made plastic with a metal insert, and it looks fine, but if you run your hand over it, you'll know it's not solid brass. Moen's warranty covers the cartridge for life, but the finish is only covered for five years. Multiple Amazon reviewers noted the finish held up well after a year of daily use, though matte black is unforgiving with hard water — expect to wipe it down weekly.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, the consensus is strong. One verified buyer wrote: *"Installed this with the 2520 valve and it took about an hour. The transfer valve is genius — no more reaching over the tub spout to twist a diverter."* Another noted: *"Temperature stays rock solid. My kids can flush the toilet and I don't even feel it."*

The installation process gets consistent thumbs-up from DIYers. The trim mounts with a single screw behind the handle, and the escutcheon plate covers a standard 6-inch rough-in opening. If you're replacing an older Moen Posi-Temp trim, this is nearly a drop-in swap.

## Where It Falls Short

The **plastic handle** is the most common complaint. It's not a durability issue — multiple reviewers confirmed it hasn't cracked or loosened — but at this price point, you might expect all-metal construction. If you're the type who notices these things, it'll bug you.

The **matte black finish** is a double-edged sword. It looks premium and modern, but it shows every water spot, fingerprint, and toothpaste splash. In a shared bathroom, you'll be wiping it down constantly. Buyers with hard water specifically flagged this — one reviewer called it "a magnet for calcium deposits."

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're remodeling a tub/shower combo and want three-way functionality without a clunky diverter. The built-in transfer valve is genuinely useful for families — kids can use the handheld wand without you having to reconfigure the plumbing. If you're already using a Posi-Temp valve, this is a straightforward upgrade.

**Skip it if:** You're on a tight budget — the trim plus valve body will run you $200+ total. Or if you have extremely hard water and don't want to babysit a matte black finish. Chrome or brushed nickel versions of the same trim (T2470 and T2470BN) are more forgiving.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work with any shower valve?**
A: No. It's specifically designed for Moen Posi-Temp valve bodies (2510 or 2520). It won't fit standard Moen or other brand valves.

**Q: Can I use this with a rainfall showerhead?**
A: Yes, but only as one of the three outlets. You'd use the built-in diverters to select between your rainfall head, hand shower, and tub spout. You can't run two outlets at once.

**Q: Is the transfer valve reliable long-term?**
A: Generally yes. The cartridge is replaceable (Moen part 1423) and covered by Moen's limited lifetime warranty. Multiple reviewers reported smooth operation after 1–2 years of daily use.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The Moen T2470BL Genta is a well-engineered trim that solves a real problem — stable water temperature and convenient outlet switching — without overcomplicating things. The plastic handle and finish maintenance are legitimate gripes, but the core functionality is rock solid. For a family bathroom or a master shower where you want flexibility, this is a smart, reliable choice.

**Buy it if** you want a three-function shower system with proven pressure-balancing tech. **Skip it if** you're price-sensitive or hate wiping down matte black fixtures. Otherwise, this is a quality investment that'll last a decade or more.
