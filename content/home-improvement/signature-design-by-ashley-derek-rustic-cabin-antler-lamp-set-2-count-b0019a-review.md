# Two Lamps, One Strong Cabin Statement: The Ashley Derek Antler Set

Here's the thing about rustic cabin decor: it's easy to go overboard, but lighting is where you can anchor the whole aesthetic without committing to a taxidermy collection. This two-piece antler lamp set from Signature Design by Ashley Derek does exactly that — and does it well enough to earn a 4.6-star average from 229 buyers.

As shown in the product image, these aren't dainty little accent lights. Each lamp stands 24 inches tall with a base that spans roughly 11 inches. The cast-resin antler structure mimics the organic branching of a real rack, finished in a mottled brown-bronze that catches light nicely. From across a room, you'll likely get a double-take from guests who assume they're looking at the real thing.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get two complete lamp sets: two antler bases, two burlap-textured drum shades, and two harp-and-finial assemblies. That's it — no bulbs included, so factor that into your purchase. Each lamp takes two standard medium-base bulbs (up to 60W, or use LEDs for lower heat).

The shade cloth has a subtle weave pattern that glows warmly when lit. It's a natural fiber look that pairs well with leather furniture, log walls, or even a more modern farmhouse setup.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The resin construction is the right call here. Real antler would crack, warp, and cost five times as much. The cast texture includes the small ridges and bumps you'd expect on actual bone, and the finish has depth — darker in the crevices, lighter on the high points. You can tell this isn't a flat spray-paint job.

One thing buyers consistently mention: the weight. Each base has heft to it, so the lamps don't tip when you bump a nightstand. That's not nothing with a top-heavy design like this.

Assembly is minimal but real. The shade sits on a harp that screws into the base, and you'll attach the finial on top. Ten minutes per lamp with a standard screwdriver. Some buyers noted the harp screws can be finicky to align on the first try — just patience, not a defect.

Light output is genuinely useful. With two 60W-equivalent bulbs per lamp, a pair on either side of a sofa throws enough light for reading or conversation. The shades direct light downward and outward rather than harshly upward, which is what you want in a living space.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The dominant theme in reviews is value. As one buyer put it: "I expected to pay double for this look at a furniture store. These look like they cost $150 each, easy." Another noted: "The detail on the antlers is what sold me — they're not smooth plastic-looking. They actually have texture."

The set format also gets consistent love. Buyers placing them on bedroom nightstands or flanking a fireplace mantle appreciate the matched-pair symmetry without hunting for a second matching lamp.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides.

**The shades are the weakest link.** They're serviceable, but the burlap-style fabric is thin. In a bright room with direct window light, the shade can look slightly translucent and washed out. Several reviewers swapped in custom shades within a month or two. Budget for that if you're picky about shade quality.

**Assembly is a minor annoyance.** It's not hard, but if you're buying these as a gift or expecting plug-and-play, set expectations. You'll need a screwdriver and a few minutes of patience.

**The faux finish won't fool a purist up close.** From three feet, it's convincing. From six inches, the resin texture is clearly not bone. If you specifically want real antler, this isn't it.

## Who Should Buy This Set

This is for the **cabin-or-rustic-décor buyer** who wants the lodge look without the lodge price tag. It's also for anyone who needs two matching lamps for a guest bedroom, lake house, or Airbnb and doesn't want to spend $300+ per lamp at a specialty store.

Skip it if you're a stickler for real materials, or if your space leans more modern-industrial — the natural fiber and antler aesthetic is firmly rustic.

## FAQ

**Are these lamps heavy enough to stay put?**
Yes. The resin bases have real mass — reviewers consistently note they're sturdier than expected. They won't slide around or tip from a light bump.

**Can I use LED bulbs in these?**
Absolutely, and you probably should. The shades are fabric, so cooler-running LEDs will keep them from baking over time. Standard A19 LED bulbs in 60W equivalent work great.

**Do the shades come attached?**
No. Each shade arrives separate and requires the harp-and-finial assembly. It's straightforward — screw the harp into the base, drop the shade over it, thread the finial on top.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want affordable, high-impact rustic lighting with convincing faux-antler detail, and you don't mind 20 minutes of assembly.

**Skip it if:** You need premium shade quality out of the box, or you insist on genuine materials.

This is a solid 4.6/5. The set delivers exactly what the product images promise — and at this price, you'd be hard-pressed to find a better-matched pair for the cabin look. Just budget for bulbs and a possible future shade upgrade if you're particular.
