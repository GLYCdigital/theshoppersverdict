## What's Actually in the Box

## Real-World Performance: The Good and the Grindy

**Where these shine:** Moderate-draw tools. On a 6.5" circular saw doing framing cuts, buyers report getting 80-90% of OEM runtime. One verified purchase noted: *"Used them in my impact driver all week — charged Monday, still had two bars Friday. For the price, I'm not complaining."* Drills, impacts, and LED work lights are exactly what these are built for.

**Where they fall short:** Heavy continuous load. Under a grinder or worm-drive saw, voltage sags noticeably. One contractor wrote: *"My saw cuts slower with these than with my Makita batteries. Fine for occasional cuts, but I wouldn't run a demo job on them."* The cells are lower-discharge-rate than genuine Makita packs, so torque-heavy tools lose responsiveness.

**Build quality** is acceptable for homeowner use but not pro-grade. The shell has some flex around the mounting rails — one buyer reported a cracked rail after dropping a pack from waist height. The LED gauges are accurate and consistent across all four packs, which is better than some pricier aftermarket brands.

## Honest Shortcomings

**Longevity is the real concern.** Multiple 6-month update reviews report capacity drop-off. One detailed review noted: *"After 7 months of daily use on a framing crew, two packs hold maybe 70% of original charge. The other two are fine."* If you're a weekend DIYer, these will last years. If you're a full-time pro, expect to replace them annually.

**No brand-name cells** means performance varies between batches. Some buyers report fully meeting expectations; others get packs that feel like 4.0Ah batteries in disguise. It's a quality-control lottery.

**Safety certifications** aren't clearly documented. UL or UN38.3 certifications aren't visibly listed, which matters for shipping and peace of mind.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy them if:** You're a homeowner or serious DIYer with 2-3 Makita tools who needs spare batteries without spending $200+. You mostly use drills, impacts, and lights. You understand these are consumables, not lifetime investments.

**Skip them if:** You're a professional running grinders or saws all day, need guaranteed performance for client work, or require tools to perform identically to OEM under load. Stick with genuine Makita or premium aftermarket like DeWalt's FlexVolt-equivalent options.
