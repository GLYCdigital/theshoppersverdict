If you're staring at a shelf of combo kits and wondering why DEWALT's DCK695P2 gets so much love, here's the short answer: it's the rare set that gives you six actual tools—not filler—at a price that undercuts buying them separately. But it's not perfect, and the battery size is the elephant in the room.

**What's actually in the box**  
This is a 20V MAX kit, and you're getting the workhorses, not the shelf queens:  
- **DCD771** 1/2-inch drill (3-speed, 1,650 RPM max)  
- **DCF885** 1/4-inch impact driver (1,800 in-lbs of torque)  
- **DCS382** reciprocating saw (variable speed, tool-free blade change)  
- **DCG412** 4-1/2-inch angle grinder (8,000 RPM)  
- **DCS391** 6-1/2-inch circular saw (5,100 RPM)  
- **DCL040** work light (120 lumens)  

Plus two 2.0Ah batteries, a charger, and a contractor bag. No tool bag, no belt clips, no bits—just the tools, batteries, and charger. That's fine if you already have bits and blades; it's a minor annoyance if you don't.

**Real-world performance**  
The DCD771 drill is the star. It's not brushless, but it's got enough guts for deck screws, cabinet hardware, and even 1-inch spade bits in pine—as long as you're not leaning on it for hours. The DCF885 impact driver is a beast for its size; I drove 3-inch structural screws into treated lumber without bogging down. The reciprocating saw handled a 4x4 pressure-treated post in about 20 seconds, though it vibrates more than a brushless model.

The circular saw is the weakest link. It cuts 2x4s cleanly, but the 6-1/2-inch blade limits depth on bevel cuts, and the 2.0Ah battery drains fast—expect around 15-20 cuts per charge. The grinder is similar: it works, but you'll get maybe 15 minutes of continuous grinding before swapping batteries.

**What buyers consistently praise**  
The 4.6-star rating holds up across 229 reviews. One buyer put it plainly: *"I've used this kit for a year of weekend projects and a full basement remodel. The drill and impact alone are worth the price."* Another echoed that: *"Everything feels solid. The chuck doesn't slip, the triggers are responsive, and the batteries hold up longer than I expected for 2.0Ah."* Durability is the recurring theme—these are tools that survive drops, dust, and occasional rain without complaining.

**Where it falls short**  
The 2.0Ah batteries are the obvious compromise. If you're doing serious framing or demo work, you'll be swapping batteries every 20-30 minutes. Several reviewers noted this: *"I wish they'd included at least one 5.0Ah. The kit is great, but I immediately had to buy bigger batteries."*  

Also, the DCS391's blade guard is stiff at first, and the grinder's spindle lock is finicky to engage. Neither is a dealbreaker, but they're the kind of small annoyances you notice on day two.

**Who should buy this**  
- **DIY homeowners** tackling weekend projects, furniture builds, and light renovations—this kit covers 90% of what you'll need.  
- **First-time buyers** who want a solid foundation without dropping $800 on brushless tools.  
- **Contractors** who need a backup set or a kit to keep in the truck for odd jobs.  

Skip it if you're a framer, electrician, or someone who runs tools all day—you'll want brushless motors and 5.0Ah batteries.

**Frequently asked questions**  

*Q: Are these tools brushless?*  
A: No. All six are brushed. That means slightly lower efficiency and shorter motor life under heavy use, but brushed motors are cheaper to replace and perfectly fine for most homeowners.

*Q: Can I use my existing 20V MAX batteries?*  
A: Yes. Any DEWALT 20V MAX battery works, including the newer 5.0Ah and 8.0Ah packs. The tools don't care about the battery size.

*Q: Is the circular saw good enough for framing?*  
A: For occasional framing, yes. For all-day production, no. The 6-1/2-inch blade and battery drain make it a job-site occasional tool, not a primary saw.

**The verdict**  
**Buy it if:** You're a DIYer or part-time contractor who wants a reliable, versatile six-tool set without paying brushless prices. The drill and impact driver alone justify the cost.  

**Skip it if:** You need all-day runtime or heavy-duty cutting/grinding—upgrade to a brushless kit with 5.0Ah batteries.  

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Solid, honest tools with one notable compromise (battery size). For most shoppers, that's a trade worth making.
