**The long, horizontal wall light that actually flatters your house — if you measure twice first.**

Most outdoor fixtures fall into two camps: the cheapo big-box special that rusts in two winters, or the designer piece that costs more than your patio furniture. The ZSMAIKU long LED wall light sits in that sweet spot between them — a modern, linear sconce with real metal construction and warm, even illumination. At 4.5 stars across 225 ratings, it's clearly working for most buyers. Here's the full story.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the fixture (roughly 21 inches wide and 4 inches tall, with a 3-inch projection from the wall), a pre-wired LED board, mounting screws, wire nuts, and a silicone gasket. The die-cast aluminum housing has a matte black powder coat — the kind of finish that doesn't show water spots or fade unevenly in sun. You'll need a standard 4-inch round or octagonal junction box already in your wall; this fixture mounts flush against it with the gasket creating the weather seal. No caulk gun required, which is a genuine relief.

**Performance and build**

The integrated 12-watt LED emits 3000K warm white light — think classic incandescent warmth, not the sterile blue-white of cheap security lights. At roughly 800 lumens, it's bright enough to light a front door or garage entry without feeling like a stadium floodlight. The acrylic diffuser is frosted and wraps the light source, so there's no harsh hot spot or glare when you walk past it.

One buyer noted: *"I installed two flanking my garage door — the light spread is smooth and even, exactly what I wanted for a modern farmhouse look."* Another mentioned the weight: *"This thing is substantial. You can feel the metal thickness when you unbox it. Feels like it'll outlast the house."*

The 3000K LED is rated for 50,000 hours — about 17 years at typical evening use. The board is non-replaceable, though. When it dies, you're buying a new fixture, not a new bulb. That's the tradeoff for the slim profile.

**Where it falls short**

The single color temperature is the biggest limitation. If you're the type who wants 5000K daylight for security visibility, this isn't it. You're locked into warm white. The 3-inch projection also means it won't work under a shallow eave overhang or on a narrow porch column — measure your mounting surface before ordering. A few reviewers noted the screws included are slightly soft; use your own #8 stainless screws for a more confident mount.

**Who should buy this**

- **Modern farmhouse or contemporary homeowners** wanting a clean horizontal line on the front facade
- **Anyone replacing dated brass or plastic sconces** — this is a direct swap if you have a standard junction box
- **Renters or owners in wet climates** — the gasket and sealed driver handle rain and humidity well

**Skip it if** you need switchable color temps, want a bulb you can replace yourself, or have a mounting surface shallower than 3 inches.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it come with a motion sensor or dusk-to-dawn photocell?**
A: No — it's a manual on/off fixture. Wire it to a switch or add a smart bulb adapter (though the LED is integrated, so you'd need a smart switch instead).

**Q: Can I install it without an electrician?**
A: If you're comfortable wiring a standard junction box (black to black, white to white, ground to ground), it's a 20-minute job. If not, hire a pro — don't wing it with exterior voltage.

**Q: Is it truly weatherproof?**
A: It's rated IP65, meaning dust-tight and protected against water jets. That's solid for exterior wall use, including coastal areas.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a well-built, good-looking, energy-efficient fixture that installs cleanly and lights your entryway with warm, even light. **Skip it if** you need color temperature options or hate the idea of a sealed LED unit.

At 4.6/5, this is a genuinely solid pick for the modern exterior — just bring your own screws.
