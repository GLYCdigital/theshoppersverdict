If you're remodeling a master bath and want a Roman tub faucet that looks like it belongs in a boutique hotel without paying boutique-hotel prices, the Delta T2738-RB Lahara is worth a serious look. This is the trim kit — the part you see and touch — for Delta's R1000 series rough-in valve, and at 4.6 stars across 229 Amazon ratings, it's clearly doing something right. But here's the catch: it's not a complete faucet. That distinction matters, so let's dig into what you're actually buying.

## What's in the Box

The T2738-RB ships with the Roman tub spout, two lever handles in that rich Venetian Bronze finish, the hand shower wand, a 6-foot hose, and the diverter mechanism. The spout itself measures 8-1/8 inches long with a 6-inch deck clearance, which gives you enough room to fill the tub without the water hammering against the back wall. The valve body is not included — you'll need Delta's R1000-UNBX or similar rough-in kit, which runs about $80–$120 depending on where you shop. That's the single biggest gotcha with this product, and you should budget for it before you start.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

This is where Delta earns its reputation. The body is solid brass, not that cheap zinc alloy you'll find at big-box stores for half the price. The Venetian Bronze finish is a PVD (physical vapor deposition) coating, which means it's bonded at the molecular level rather than painted on. That translates to real durability — it won't flake off in six months like some electroplated finishes I've seen.

The lever handles operate with a smooth, precise action, and the diverter valve (the knob that switches between tub spout and shower wand) has a positive, tactile click. As one verified buyer put it: *"Installation took about 45 minutes. The instructions were clear, and the handles feel solid — no wobble, no cheap feel."* Another reviewer noted, *"The hand shower is actually useful, not just decorative. The 6-foot hose reaches across the tub easily."*

One feature that gets less attention but deserves it: the hand shower uses Delta's In2ition technology. That means you can switch between a concentrated spray and a wider coverage pattern, which is genuinely handy for rinsing the tub walls or washing a dog.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the reviews, three themes keep coming up. First, the finish — people love the Venetian Bronze. It's darker and richer than oil-rubbed bronze, with a subtle warmth that pairs beautifully with white or gray tile. Second, the value. For the money, you're getting a trim kit that includes the hand shower, which many competitors sell as an a la carte add-on. Third, the install. Multiple reviewers commented on how straightforward the process was, especially if you're replacing an existing Delta R1000 setup.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The finish, while gorgeous, is a fingerprint magnet. If you have hard water, prepare to wipe down the fixtures regularly or you'll see mineral spots. One reviewer complained, *"The bronze finish shows every water droplet. I have to dry it after every bath."* That's a maintenance consideration, not a dealbreaker, but you should know it going in.

Also, the spout is a bit long for smaller alcove tubs. If your tub is under 54 inches, the 8-1/8 inch spout might extend past the tub's inside edge, which can cause splashing. Measure your tub deck before committing.

## Who Should Buy This

This trim is ideal for three specific buyers: the DIY remodeler who already has a Delta R1000 rough-in valve in place (this is a direct swap), the homeowner who wants a hand shower included without paying extra, and anyone designing a warm, traditional bathroom palette where bronze works. Skip it if you have hard water and hate cleaning fixtures, or if you're on a strict budget — remember, the valve body is extra.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this fit my existing Delta valve?**
A: It fits Delta's R1000 series rough-in valves. If you have an older MultiChoice valve, check compatibility — Delta's website has a cross-reference chart. If in doubt, measure your valve's center-to-center distance and compare to the 8-inch standard.

**Q: Is the hand shower functional or just a sprayer?**
A: Fully functional with a 6-foot hose. It has Delta's In2ition dual-spray technology, so you can toggle between a concentrated stream and a wide rinse pattern.

**Q: Can I install this myself?**
A: Yes, if you're replacing an existing rough-in valve. The trim installs with two mounting screws and the handles thread on. DIYers with basic plumbing experience report 30–60 minute installs. If you're installing the valve from scratch, hire a pro unless you're comfortable with soldering or PEX work.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a premium-feeling Roman tub faucet with a hand shower, you have (or plan to buy) a Delta R1000 rough-in valve, and you don't mind wiping down bronze fixtures occasionally.

**Skip it if:** You're on a tight budget (the valve adds significant cost), you have aggressive hard water and hate maintenance, or your tub deck is too narrow for the 8-inch spout.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The Delta T2738-RB Lahara delivers exactly what it promises: solid brass, a beautiful finish, and a functional hand shower. It's not the cheapest option, and the separate valve purchase stings, but the quality justifies the price. For a master bath upgrade that'll last 20 years, this is money well spent.
