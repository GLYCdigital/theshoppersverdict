**What you actually get**

As shown in the product image, the kit includes the drain body, overflow elbow, tee fitting, and all necessary gaskets and washers. The key selling point is the universal design — the adjustable extension range accommodates most freestanding tub configurations without custom cutting. For a kit that retails in the budget-friendly range, you're getting everything needed to complete the rough-in, minus the trap arm and tubular pipe that runs to your wall stub-out.

**Performance and build quality**

The all-PVC construction is the smartest thing Oatey does here. Unlike chrome-plated brass kits that corrode or react with harsh chemical drain cleaners over time, PVC shrugs off everything you throw at it. The slip-joint connections are straightforward, and the gaskets seat well when you hand-tighten them properly.

One thing to note from my research: buyers consistently mention the importance of dry-fitting everything before gluing. As one verified purchaser put it, "I test-fitted the whole assembly twice before cementing anything — saved myself from a crooked drain." That's solid advice. The PVC threads are functional but unforgiving if you cross-thread them, and once you commit with primer and cement, there's no going back.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The universal fit is the star here. Multiple reviewers noted it worked for tubs they assumed would need a custom solution. One said, "I have a cheap Amazon freestanding tub and this drained perfectly — zero leaks on the first test." Another appreciated that it included the overflow plate, which many competing kits omit.

The value proposition is also strong — this kit runs significantly less than buying components separately, and the quality is consistent with what you'd expect from a brand that's been in the plumbing game since 1916.

**Where it falls short**

Honest assessment: the instructions are minimal. If you're not familiar with basic drain rough-in, you'll want to watch a YouTube tutorial before starting. The slip-joint nuts are also easy to over-tighten, which can crack the PVC or deform the gasket — hand-tight plus a quarter turn is the sweet spot.

Also, while it's called "universal," it's not a magic bullet. If your freestanding tub has an unusually deep base or a non-standard overflow position, you may still need an extension piece. Measure your tub's dimensions before ordering.

**Who should buy this**
