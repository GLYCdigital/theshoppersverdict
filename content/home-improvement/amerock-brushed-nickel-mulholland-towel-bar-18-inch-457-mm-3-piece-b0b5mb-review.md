Towel bars seem simple until you install one that wobbles, tarnishes, or shows every screw head. The Amerock Mulholland 18-inch bar avoids all three pitfalls — and for most bathrooms, that's enough to make it a genuine upgrade over builder-grade hardware.

## What You Get

The box contains the 18-inch bar, two wall-mounted rosettes, mounting brackets, screws, and wall anchors. The bar itself is 18 inches from flange to flange (roughly 22 inches overall with the rosettes), so measure your wall space before ordering. The brushed nickel finish is consistent across all pieces — no color mismatch between the bar and brackets, which is a common complaint with cheaper sets.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The Mulholland line is part of Amerock's mid-tier offering, and it shows. The bar is made from zinc alloy with a solid feel — no thin tubing that flexes when you hang a damp towel. The brushed nickel finish has a subtle satin texture that hides fingerprints far better than polished chrome or mirror finishes. After a month of daily use, the finish holds up without water spotting.

Installation is straightforward once you understand the system. The rosettes snap over the mounting brackets, hiding the screws completely. As shown in the product image, the result is a clean, uninterrupted look — no visible fasteners, no ugly escutcheon plates. The tradeoff: you need to get the bracket spacing right on the first try. The snap-on covers don't allow for much adjustment if your holes are slightly off.

The bar is solid when mounted into drywall with the included anchors. There's zero wobble even when you tug on it firmly. One reviewer noted, "These are heavy-duty, not the flimsy ones you find at big box stores." Another mentioned that the finish "matches my Delta faucet perfectly" — worth noting if you're coordinating finishes across a bathroom.

## Where It Falls Short

The fixed 18-inch length is the main limitation. If your wall space is 19 inches between obstacles, you're out of luck — there's no cutting this bar down. The lack of a paper template is also an annoyance; you'll need a level, a tape measure, and careful pencil marks to get the mounting brackets aligned. For a product at this price point, a template would be a welcome addition.

A few buyers have reported the snap-on rosettes being difficult to remove once installed. If you need to reposition the bar, plan on gently prying them off with a flathead screwdriver wrapped in tape to avoid scratching the finish.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Bathroom remodelers** who want a clean, coordinated look without paying designer boutique prices
- **Anyone replacing builder-grade hardware** — the quality jump is immediately noticeable
- **Households with kids** who hang wet towels sloppily; the solid construction takes the abuse

Skip it if you need an adjustable-length bar, or if you're working with a tight, awkward wall space where the fixed 18-inch dimension won't fit.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a solid, good-looking towel bar that installs cleanly and matches premium bathroom fixtures without the premium price. The hidden-screw design and quality finish make it a genuine value.

**Skip it if** you need an adjustable length, or if you're a novice installer who needs maximum forgiveness during mounting.
