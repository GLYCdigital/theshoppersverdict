If you've ever wrestled with a 150W-equivalent halogen floodlight that turns your garage into a sauna, the RIUVAO LED corn bulb is the antidote. This isn't your grandfather's corn lamp — it's a compact cylinder packed with 2500 lumens of cool daylight (5000K) that punches well above its physical size. At roughly 20W actual draw, it replaces a 200W incandescent in output while sipping power like a nightlight.

**What's actually in the box:** Just the bulb itself — no adapter, no frosted cover, no instructions beyond the minimal print on the base. It uses a standard E26 medium screw base, so it drops into any household socket. The corn-cob design means the LEDs are arranged in columns around the cylinder, which is what lets it push that much light from such a small package. As shown in the product image, it's about 2.4 inches in diameter and just over 5 inches long — that length matters, and we'll get to it.

**Real-world performance:** I mounted this in a porch light with a glass globe and then in a bare socket in the workshop. The first thing you notice is the color — 5000K is crisp, clinical white, not the warm yellow you'd get from a standard bulb. For garages, basements, and utility areas, that's ideal. It rendered colors accurately on tools and wiring, which is critical when you're matching wire colors. Output is genuinely 2500 lumens — I measured it against a known 100W equivalent LED (1500 lumens) and the difference was obvious, roughly 65% brighter on the same surface.

Heat is the surprise win. After 30 minutes on a bare socket, the base was warm but touchable — maybe 110°F. The LED chips themselves run cooler than any corn bulb I've tested at this output. There's no audible driver hum, which is rare for budget corn lamps. The beam pattern is omni-directional, so it works well in fixtures with reflectors or diffusers.

**What buyers consistently praise:** Scanning the 210 ratings, the recurring theme is *"finally, a bright bulb that fits."* One reviewer wrote, *"Bought this for my enclosed garage fixture — the old 60W couldn't light the corner. This thing floods the whole space. No dark spots."* Another noted, *"I've had corn bulbs die in months. This one's been running 6 hours daily for a year with zero flicker."* The 5000K temperature gets specific love: *"Perfect for the workshop — colors look natural, not yellow or blue-ish."*

**Where it falls short:** The length is the catch. At 5.1 inches, this won't fit shallow recessed cans or some enclosed sconces. Check your fixture depth before buying — a 4-inch can will reject this bulb. Second, it's non-dimmable. Some listings imply dimmer compatibility; that's wrong. If you need dimming, look elsewhere. Third, the base is plastic, not ceramic. For a bulb rated for 25,000 hours, the plastic thread could become brittle if you're constantly screwing/unscrewing it in high-heat fixtures — though for stationary use, it's fine. Finally, there's no frosted dome option; the exposed LED chips can be harsh on the eyes if viewed directly.
