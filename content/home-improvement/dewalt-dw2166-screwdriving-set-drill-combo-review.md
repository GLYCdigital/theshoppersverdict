### Frequently Asked Questions

**Can this drill drive large-diameter hole saws?**  
Yes, within reason. The 300 UWO motor handles hole saws up to about 1.5 inches in wood. For larger holes, step up to DEWALT's higher-end models or use a corded drill.

**Do the bits fit in the tough case securely?**  
Yes. The tough case uses a bit retention system that holds each bit in place. Bits do not fall out even when the case is shaken or dropped.

**Is this kit compatible with other DEWALT 20V MAX tools?**  
Yes. The batteries and charger are compatible with the entire DEWALT 20V MAX system of over 200 tools.

**What is the warranty on the drill and batteries?**  
DEWALT offers a 3-year limited warranty on the drill and a 2-year warranty on the batteries. Register your product on DEWALT's website for warranty coverage.

**Can you use impact-rated bits in this drill?**  
Yes. Impact-rated bits work fine in a standard drill chuck and will last longer than standard bits, especially when driving screws into dense materials.
