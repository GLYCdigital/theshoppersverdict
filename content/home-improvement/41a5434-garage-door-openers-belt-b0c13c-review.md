## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest truth: this is not a plug-and-play swap. You'll need to detach the opener from the ceiling, slide the rail apart, and thread the new belt through before reassembling. Plan for 60–90 minutes if you're mechanically inclined, longer if you're not. Several reviewers mention this in their feedback — one said, *"Took me two hours but saved $150 in labor."* That's the tradeoff.

Also, belt length matters. The 41a5434 fits a specific range of rail lengths. If your opener has an unusually long or short rail, double-check compatibility before ordering. Returning a partially installed belt kit is a hassle nobody wants.
