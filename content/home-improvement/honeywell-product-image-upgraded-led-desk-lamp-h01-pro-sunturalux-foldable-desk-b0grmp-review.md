# Honeywell H01 Pro Sun LED Desk Lamp Review: Is This the Last Task Light You'll Buy?

If you've ever squinted at a soldering joint or struggled to match paint colors under a dim ceiling fixture, you know the difference between "a lamp" and "task lighting." The Honeywell H01 Pro Sun sits firmly in the second category. With 235 Amazon ratings averaging 4.7 stars, it's clearly earning its keep on workbenches and drafting tables. But is it the right light for *your* specific work? Let's dig into the details.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the lamp head, the folding spring arm, a weighted base, and a 24W AC adapter. Assembly takes under 60 seconds — the head clicks onto the arm, the arm slots into the base, done. There's no wall-mount option here, so plan for desk space. The arm extends to roughly 22 inches from base to light head, giving you solid reach over a standard 24-inch-deep workbench without the head hovering in your face.

## Performance: Where This Lamp Earns Its Keep

The headline feature is the full-spectrum LED array. At 93 CRI, this isn't marketing fluff — I compared it side-by-side against a $40 "high CRI" lamp from another brand, and the difference in reds and skin tones is immediately visible. For anyone doing electronics repair, model painting, or jewelry work, this matters. The three color temperatures cover the realistic range: warm 3000K for reading, neutral 4500K for general work, and cool 6000K for detail tasks where you need maximum contrast.

Dimming is stepless from 5% to 100%. At minimum brightness, it's genuinely dim — not a flickering 40% like some budget units. At 100%, the 12-inch light head throws a wide, even pool of light across a full 2x3-foot work area with no visible hot spots or dead zones. The spring-arm mechanism holds its position well; I've had mine positioned at a 45-degree angle for six weeks without any droop.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The consistency of feedback is telling. Multiple reviewers highlight the color accuracy — one electronics repairer wrote: *"I can finally see the color bands on resistors without guessing. This is the first lamp I've owned where 100% brightness doesn't feel harsh."* Another buyer mentioned: *"The arm stays exactly where I put it. My previous lamp needed re-tightening every week."* The build quality of the folding mechanism appears to be the standout feature for long-term owners.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the tradeoffs. The base weighs about 2.5 pounds, which is light for this class of lamp. If you're clamping heavy workpieces or using it on a vibrating surface, the whole assembly can tip. A clamp mount isn't included — you'll need to improvise with a C-clamp or place weight on the base's rear edge. The touch controls are also hypersensitive; a single accidental brush against the panel cycles through modes, which is annoying mid-task. And notably, there's no USB charging port. At this price point, competitors like the BenQ e-Reading LED Desk Lamp include that convenience.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Electronics hobbyists and repair techs** who need accurate color rendering for component identification
- **Artists and model painters** working with color-critical materials
- **Anyone replacing a cheap LED lamp** that flickers or can't hold a position

**Skip it if** you need a portable light, want USB pass-through charging, or work primarily on a laptop (the 6000K mode can feel clinical for screen work).

## FAQ

**Q: Does the lamp flicker at low brightness settings?**  
A: No. The PWM dimming is implemented cleanly — even at 5%, there's no visible flicker on camera or in person.

**Q: Can you replace the LED module if it burns out?**  
A: The LED array is integrated into the head. It's rated for 50,000 hours, which is roughly 17 years at 8 hours daily, but it's not user-serviceable.

**Q: Is the base heavy enough for an articulating arm at full extension?**  
A: Only if you're placing light objects. For serious work, you'll want to secure the base with a clamp or add weight.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you do detail work that demands accurate colors and stable, flicker-free illumination. The build quality and light quality justify the price for serious users.  
**Skip it if** you need a lightweight, portable lamp or want USB charging built in — there are better options for those specific needs.

At 4.6/5, the Honeywell H01 Pro Sun earns its rating. It's not perfect, but for its core purpose — giving you a clear, accurate view of your work — it's genuinely excellent.
