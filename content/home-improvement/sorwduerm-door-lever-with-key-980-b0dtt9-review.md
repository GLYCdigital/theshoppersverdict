**Opening:** Most keyed door levers under $40 feel like they belong on a shed, not your front door. The SORWDUERM Door Lever with Key 980 is the exception — it's a genuine entry lock with a solid feel that punches above its price point. After 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, this is one of the better budget keyed levers on Amazon. But "better" doesn't mean perfect. Here's the real story.

**What you actually get:** The box contains two complete lever sets (interior and exterior), a standard deadlatch bolt, two keyed cylinders, three keys, and both 2.3-inch and 2.75-inch strike plates so it fits standard and pre-drilled doors. The lever itself is zinc alloy with a satin nickel finish that resists fingerprints better than polished chrome. You also get the mounting screws and a straightforward instruction sheet — no Allen wrenches or specialty tools required.

**Performance and build quality:** The first thing you notice is the weight. This isn't hollow die-cast metal; the lever has a solid heft that inspires confidence. The interior thumb turn operates smoothly with a positive click, and the exterior key cylinder turns without the grainy, gritty feel common on cheap locks. Installation is genuinely tool-free — slide the latch in, mount the two levers, tighten the screws. Most buyers report 15–20 minutes per door.

The finish is the standout feature. Multiple reviewers mention installing this in coastal or high-humidity areas without corrosion after months of use. One verified buyer noted: *"Been on my door for 6 months in Florida humidity. No rust, no sticking, still operates like day one."* That's meaningful for anyone who's watched cheap hardware tarnish within weeks.

**What buyers consistently praise:**
- Smooth key operation right out of the box (no graphite needed)
- The satin finish resists smudges and cleaning products
- Quiet, solid latching — no rattling or loose handle feel
- Universal fit for both left- and right-handed doors

**Where it falls short:** The biggest limitation is the non-rekeyable cylinder. If you need multiple doors keyed alike, you'll need to buy several sets and accept they won't match — or pay a locksmith. The latch bolt travel is also slightly shorter than Schlage or Kwikset premium models; on a thick exterior door with a wide weatherstripping gap, you may notice the bolt barely catches the strike plate. A few reviewers also noted the included screws are soft — use the existing screws from your old lock if they're longer. Finally, this is *only* a keyed lever. If you want a matching passage (non-locking) set for interior doors, it doesn't exist in this lineup.

**Who should buy this:**
- **DIY homeowners** replacing outdated levers — the tool-free install is genuinely simple
- **Landlords** needing durable, inexpensive keyed entry for rental units
- **Anyone in humid climates** who wants a finish that won't pit or corrode
- **Not for** anyone needing master keying, smart lock compatibility, or ADA-compliant lever length (this is a standard 4.5-inch lever, not extended)

**FAQ:**

**Q: Can I rekey this lock to match my existing house key?**  
A: No. The cylinder is a fixed pin design, not a rekeyable smart cylinder. You'll use the included keys, or a locksmith can repin it for roughly $20–30.

**Q: Will this fit a standard 2-3/8" backset door?**  
A: Yes. The included latch is adjustable between 2-3/8" and 2-3/4" backsets, and the two strike plates cover both standard pre-drilled depths.

**Q: Is the lever reversible for left- and right-handed doors?**  
A: Yes, the lever orientation flips with a simple screw adjustment. No disassembly needed.
