**The 1-Gallon Solution: When a Full Tank Is Overkill**

If you've ever stood at a bathroom sink for 45 seconds waiting for hot water while brushing your teeth, you already know the problem this Ecosmart solves. The 1-gallon 120V mini tank heater sits right under your sink and delivers hot water almost instantly — no more running the tap for a minute straight while the main tank's supply makes its way through cold pipes.

But here's the thing: this isn't a whole-house solution. It's a targeted fix for a specific annoyance. Let's break down whether it's the right fix for you.

**What You Actually Get**

The unit measures 12.5 inches tall, 12.75 inches wide, and 10.5 inches deep — roughly the size of a small carry-on bag folded in half. It comes with a 6-foot power cord, a pressure relief valve, and mounting brackets. You'll need to supply your own water connections (1/2-inch NPT fittings standard) and the mounting hardware for your specific wall studs is included.

One thing shoppers consistently note: the installation isn't hard, but it's not plug-and-play either. You're adding a hardwired appliance. The unit draws 12 amps at full load, which means it **must** be on a dedicated 15-amp circuit. Several reviewers mention this after the fact. One wrote:

> "Wish I'd known about the dedicated circuit before I blew the breaker three times. Once I ran a separate line, it's been flawless."

**Real-World Performance**

The 1440W heating element brings the 1-gallon tank from cold to 125°F in about 15-20 minutes. Once hot, it maintains temperature. The key number to understand: **you get about 1-2 minutes of continuous hot water** before the tank empties and needs to reheat. For washing hands, shaving, or rinsing a brush — perfect. For filling a pot or a long shower — absolutely not.

Where this unit shines is in scenarios like:
- A half-bath or guest bathroom far from the main water heater
- A workshop sink where you need hot water for cleaning tools
- An RV or tiny home setup (users confirm it runs fine on residential 120V)

The copper heating element is a thoughtful touch. In hard-water areas, copper sheds scale more effectively than stainless steel, meaning less maintenance and longer life. Multiple long-term reviewers (2+ years of use) report no element failures.

**Where It Falls Short**

The capacity limitation is the elephant in the room. If you have multiple people using the same sink in quick succession, you'll wait for reheats. Expect a 15-minute recovery time between uses.

The lack of a user-accessible temperature control is another minor gripe. The thermostat is behind the front panel, which is fine for a set-and-forget installation, but if you want to adjust from 120°F to 140°F, you're unscrewing the cover. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

A few buyers also mention the unit hums slightly during heating cycles. It's not loud — more of a low electrical buzz — but if you're mounting it directly under a sink in a quiet bathroom, you'll notice it.

**Who Should Buy This**

Buy it if:
- You have a sink that takes 60-90 seconds to get hot water from the main heater
- You need a compact, reliable hot water source for a workshop, garage, or laundry room
- You're handy enough to run a dedicated circuit or can budget for an electrician

Skip it if:
- You need hot water for anything beyond hand-washing or small tasks
- You can't add a dedicated circuit to the location
- You're expecting this to replace a traditional water heater

**FAQ**

**Q: Can this run on a standard 15-amp outlet?**
A: Yes, but only if that outlet is on a dedicated circuit. If other appliances share the circuit, you'll trip breakers. Most installations require a new circuit run.

**Q: How long does it take to reheat after running out?**
A: From fully cold to 125°F takes 15-20 minutes. If you only used half the tank, you're looking at 7-10 minutes for usable hot water.

**Q: Does it come with the pressure relief valve installed?**
A: It ships with the valve included but not pre-installed — you'll mount it during setup. The manual covers this clearly, and it's a 10-minute job.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you have a specific sink with slow hot water delivery and you can manage the electrical requirements. It's a reliable, well-built fix at a reasonable price point. **Skip it if** you can't run a dedicated circuit or you're expecting more than a gallon of hot water at a time.

At 4.2/5 stars, this is a solid, honest performer — just make sure you understand exactly what a 1-gallon tank can and can't do before you buy. For its intended purpose, it does the job admirably.
