## Real-world performance

The hook-and-loop backing is the make-or-break feature on any sander pad, and this is where LotFancy either wins or loses. In testing, the loops are dense enough to grab firmly — sheets stay put even when you're bearing down on edge grain. One reviewer noted, "I've had cheap pads fly off mid-stroke; these stay locked in until I peel them off." That matches my experience. The aluminum oxide grain holds its edge reasonably well too. On a pine shelf, an 80-grit sheet lasted through two full passes before showing noticeable wear; the 120-grit held up even better.

Here's the catch: these pads have no dust extraction holes. The Mouse sander's dust canister is effectively dead weight with these. You'll be sanding dry with dust flying — wear a mask and plan to wipe down between grits. This is a significant tradeoff for anyone who values a clean workspace.

## What buyers consistently praise

Across the reviews, three themes keep surfacing. First, the value is undeniable — fifty sheets for roughly what you'd pay for eight to ten at a hardware store. Second, the fit is exact; several users with older Mouse models confirmed the sheets align perfectly with the pad. Third, the variety covers most projects. ## Where it falls short

Beyond the missing dust holes, the grit labels are printed on the backing in ink that rubs off after a few minutes of use. If you don't sort them immediately, you'll be playing a guessing game. Also, the 60-grit is aggressive but short-lived — it's fine for stripping paint, but don't expect it to shape hardwood. And if you rely on the Mouse's dust collection for allergy control, these pads will disappoint you.

## Who should buy this

This pack is perfect for the DIYer refinishing furniture, sanding drywall patches, or prepping trim for paint. It's also great for hobbyists who use their Mouse sparingly and don't want to overpay for brand-name consumables. Skip it if you're a professional finisher who needs dust extraction, or if you prefer single-grit bulk purchases for production work.
