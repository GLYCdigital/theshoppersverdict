The Mr.Go Rechargeable LED Cube Light is one of those tools you don't realize you need until you're contorted under a sink with a flashlight in your teeth. This little 3x3-inch cube solves that problem with a magnetic base, a tripod mount, and 16 colors that go far beyond basic white work lighting.

**What's actually in the box:** You get the cube light itself, a compact remote control (about the size of a car key fob), a USB-C charging cable, and a small manual. No wall adapter, no batteries — the light has a built-in 2,000mAh cell that charges in roughly 2.5 hours. The cube weighs about 8 ounces, which is light enough to stick on a fridge or a metal toolbox without worrying about it dropping.

**Real-world performance:** The 16 RGB modes aren't a gimmick. In a dark garage, the warm white setting (around 3,000K) is genuinely pleasant for close work — not that harsh blue-white you get from cheap work lights. The red mode is a lifesaver for preserving night vision if you're camping or working in dark server rooms. Brightness adjusts across 10 levels via the remote, so you can dim it to a subtle ambient glow rather than blasting the whole room.

The magnetic base is strong — it held firmly to a steel workbench and a car hood during testing. The 1/4-inch thread on the bottom means you can mount it on any standard camera tripod, which makes it surprisingly versatile for under-car work or photography lighting. Battery life checks out: roughly 3 hours at full brightness, closer to 8-10 hours on the lower settings.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The portability and mounting flexibility are the stars here. One reviewer noted, *"I use this under my desk for cable management work — the magnet holds it perfectly and the red light doesn't wake my wife."* Another said, *"The remote is the reason I bought this over cheaper options. Being able to change colors without touching the light is huge when it's mounted somewhere awkward."* Several DIYers mentioned using it as a temporary under-cabinet light in kitchens while renovating.

**Where it falls short:** The remote is infrared, not RF. That means it needs direct line-of-sight to the light's sensor. If the cube is facing away from you or buried in a tool bag, you're walking over to press the button manually. There's also no IP waterproof rating, so this is strictly an indoor or dry-weather tool. One customer mentioned, *"I left it on a damp concrete floor overnight and the charging port corroded."* That's a fair warning — treat it like a precision tool, not a rugged job-site light.

The biggest limitation is output. At roughly 300 lumens on high, this isn't going to light up a full workshop. It's a task light, not a floodlight. For detailed work within 3-4 feet, it's great. For lighting an entire room, you'll need something bigger.

**Who should buy this:**
- **DIYers and hobbyists** who need hands-free lighting in tight spaces
- **Campers and truck campers** who want a compact, color-adjustable light that doesn't eat AA batteries
- **Photographers** needing a small, color-tunable fill light with tripod mounting
- **Mechanics** who work under hoods or dashboards and need a magnetically-mounted work light

**Who should skip it:** If you need serious lumen output for whole-room lighting, or if you work in wet, dusty environments, this isn't your tool. Also skip it if you're the type who loses remotes — the manual buttons work fine, but you'll lose the convenience factor.

**FAQ:**

**Q: Can it be used while charging?**
A: Yes, but it's not ideal. The USB-C port is on the bottom, so it's awkward to position while plugged in. Best to charge fully and go cordless.

**Q: How do the 16 RGB colors actually work?**
A: You cycle through 16 preset colors via the remote or the button on top. There's no custom color mixing — it's preset hues, not a full RGB slider. If you need precise color matching, this won't do it.

**Q: Does the remote work through walls or obstacles?**
A: No. It's infrared, so it requires a direct line of sight to the cube's sensor. It works up to about 15 feet in open space, but any obstruction kills the signal.

**The Verdict:**

**Buy it if** you need a compact, versatile, magnetically-mounted task light with color options and remote convenience for indoor DIY, camping, or photography work.

**Skip it if** you need high-lumen output, waterproofing, or plan to use it in harsh environments.

**Rating:** 4.2/5 — A well-executed niche tool that does exactly what it promises, with minor limitations that won't matter for most buyers.
