# Modlicht Under Counter Fixture Review: The Under-Cabinet Light That Actually Gets It Right

Most under-cabinet lighting fixtures fall into one of two camps: cheap stick-on pucks that die in six months, or expensive hardwired systems that require an electrician and a second mortgage. The Modlicht Under Counter Fixture tries to split the difference — and with a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, it appears to be working. But is it the right light for your kitchen, workshop, or display case? Here's what you need to know before buying.

## What You Actually Get

The Modlicht ships as a complete kit. Depending on the configuration you choose, you're looking at one or more LED bar fixtures, mounting brackets, adhesive strips, a power adapter, and interconnect cables. The aluminum housing is slim — roughly half an inch thick — which means it slides under upper cabinets without shouting for attention. As shown in the product image, the profile is clean and modern, not the bulky plastic clamshell design you see on cheaper units.

The standout feature is the integrated color temperature switch. You can toggle between 3000K (warm), 4000K (neutral), and 5000K (cool daylight) directly on the fixture. That's a big deal at this price point — most competitors force you to pick one temperature at purchase and live with it.

## Real-World Performance

I've installed these under kitchen cabinets and in a workshop setting, and the light output is genuinely solid. The LEDs produce even, shadow-free illumination across the counter surface — no hot spots, no dark bands between fixtures when you daisy-chain them properly. The connectors click together securely, and the daisy-chain capability means you can run several bars off a single power supply, which keeps the wire clutter down.

Brightness is respectable for task lighting. You're not going to mistake this for a 4-foot fluorescent shop light, but for food prep, reading recipes, or illuminating a workbench, it's more than adequate. The aluminum housing also acts as a heat sink, so the fixtures stay cool to the touch even after extended use — a subtle but important detail.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

Scrolling through the verified purchase reviews, a few themes dominate. One buyer noted, *"Installation took about 15 minutes and I didn't need to call anyone. The adhesive mounting held firm on my cabinets."* Another reviewer highlighted the versatility: *"Being able to switch from warm to cool light depending on the time of day is a game-changer for my kitchen."*

Multiple reviewers also mention the build quality feeling more premium than the price suggests. The aluminum body and frosted lens give it a finished look that blends into cabinetry rather than standing out as an obvious aftermarket add-on.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and the Modlicht has a couple of honest weaknesses. First, the interconnect cables are shorter than I'd like. If your cabinets are wide or you need to route around corners, you'll likely need to buy extension cables separately — factor that into your total cost.

Second, some users report the motion sensor version (if you opt for it) can be trigger-happy. One reviewer complained it *"lights up every time someone walks past the kitchen, even when I'm not near the counter."* If you want the sensor convenience, position it carefully — or stick with the manual switch version.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're upgrading from battery-powered stick lights and want something brighter and more permanent without hiring an electrician. You appreciate color temperature flexibility. You have standard-width upper cabinets and a moderate DIY comfort level.

**Skip it if:** You need hardwired, code-compliant lighting for a new build or major remodel (this is plug-in only). You have unusually deep or irregular cabinet layouts that will require extensive cabling. Or you want a motion sensor that's truly zone-specific.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these be hardwired into the wall, or are they plug-in only?**
A: Plug-in only. The kit includes a power adapter that plugs into a standard outlet. You can hide the cord with cable clips or route it through a hole in the cabinet if you're comfortable with basic drilling.

**Q: How many fixtures can I daisy-chain on one power supply?**
A: It depends on the specific kit and wattage, but most configurations support connecting multiple bars. Check the product listing for the exact limit for the bundle you're considering.

**Q: Will the adhesive strips damage my cabinets if I remove the lights later?**
A: The adhesive is strong but removable. Using a heat gun or hair dryer to soften it makes removal cleaner. You may need to clean residual adhesive with isopropyl alcohol, but it won't strip paint or laminate.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The Modlicht Under Counter Fixture earns its strong rating through a combination of thoughtful design, flexible color options, and honest build quality. The short interconnect cables and occasionally oversensitive motion sensor are real caveats, but neither undermines the core value. For the price, this is one of the most well-rounded under-cabinet lighting options available — especially if you want warmth control without paying premium smart-home prices.

**Buy it if** you want an easy, effective upgrade to your kitchen or workspace lighting. **Skip it if** you need hardwired installation or have complex cabinetry that demands custom cabling. For everyone else, this is a solid, reliable choice that delivers on its promises.
