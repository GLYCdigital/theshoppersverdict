**What You Actually Get**

The package includes two matching sconces, all mounting hardware, and a template for placement. Each fixture measures roughly 11 inches tall with a 5-inch backplate — compact enough for hallways, flanking a bathroom mirror, or framing a bed.

Key features from the product listing:

- **Dimmable compatibility** (when paired with a dimmer switch)
- **Standard E12 socket** — accepts small candelabra bulbs (not included)
- **Metal body** with a matte black finish
- **Uplight/downlight design** — light shines both upward and downward through the glass shade

The shade is a clear glass cylinder, which gives off that clean, contemporary look popular in modern farmhouse and industrial decor.

---

**Real-World Performance**

Installation is genuinely straightforward. The mount plate attaches with two screws, and the fixture wires with standard black/white/ground. If you've swapped a light fixture before, this will take 20 minutes per sconce. If you haven't, the included guide is clear enough for a confident beginner.

The lighting output depends entirely on the bulbs you choose. With a 60W equivalent LED in the E12 base, you get a warm, even wash that works well for ambient lighting. The open top and bottom design means no harsh shadows — a plus for bathroom mirrors where even light matters.

One note: the glass shade is thin. It arrived intact for most buyers, but a few reported chips during shipping. That's the tradeoff for a lightweight, budget-friendly design.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be direct about the downsides:

1. **Bulbs not included** — You'll spend another $10–15 on two candelabra bulbs. Budget for that.

2. **Hardwired only** — No plug-in option. If you're renting and can't run wiring, this isn't for you. You'll need an existing junction box.

3. **Quality control is variable** — Some customers received fixtures with minor paint imperfections or slightly misaligned mounting holes. Not a dealbreaker, but inspect yours upon arrival.

4. **The glass shade is fragile** — Handle carefully during installation. Replacement shades aren't sold separately, so a crack means contacting the seller.

**FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask**

**Q: Can these be mounted upside down?**
A: Yes. The design is symmetrical, so you can flip the direction of the light. Just ensure the wiring reaches the socket before securing the fixture.

**Q: What's the recommended bulb wattage?**
A: Stick with 60W equivalent or lower. The fixture is rated for standard E12 bulbs, but higher wattage generates heat that can stress the glass shade.

**Q: Do they work with smart home systems?**
A: The sconces themselves are dumb fixtures. If you install a smart dimmer switch, they'll respond to voice control and app scheduling. The fixture doesn't have any built-in smart features.
