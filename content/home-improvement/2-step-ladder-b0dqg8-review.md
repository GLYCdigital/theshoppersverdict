## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this ladder has two honest limitations. First, there's no handrail. At the top step, you're standing at roughly 20 inches off the ground — manageable, but if you have balance issues or need to reach something heavy, you'll want a wall or counter nearby. The second issue is the rubber feet. They're excellent on hardwood, tile, and concrete, but on soft vinyl or linoleum, dragging the ladder can leave scuff marks. Pick it up, don't slide it.

A few reviewers also mentioned that the locking mechanism requires a firm press to fully engage. It's not a flaw, but if you're arthritic or have limited hand strength, test the mechanism before committing.
