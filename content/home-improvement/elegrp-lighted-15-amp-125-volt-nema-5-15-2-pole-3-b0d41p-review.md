**ELEGRP Lighted 15 Amp Receptacle Review: The Outlet That Tells You the Truth**

You know that moment when you plug something in, nothing happens, and you spend ten minutes checking circuit breakers, flipping switches, and wondering if the outlet died? The ELEGRP Lighted 15 Amp receptacle eliminates that entire ritual. As shown in the product image, a small green LED glows through the faceplate whenever power is present. It's a simple idea executed well — and at 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, buyers clearly agree.

**What's in the Box**

You get one duplex receptacle, pre-wired with the green LED indicator already attached to the side terminals. The package includes the outlet itself but no faceplate — you'll need to supply your own standard duplex wall plate. The unit is rated 15 Amp, 125 Volt, NEMA 5-15, 2-pole, 3-wire grounded, which covers standard residential circuits. Construction is typical contractor-grade: impact-resistant thermoplastic body, brass terminals, and a steel mounting strap with rust-resistant plating.

**Installation and Real-World Performance**

Wiring this outlet is straightforward if you've done basic electrical work. It offers both back-wire (push-in) and side-screw terminals, accepting 14 AWG solid copper and up to 12 AWG. The back-wire holes are spring-loaded and grip firmly — one reviewer noted, "The push-in connection held tight when I tugged on it, which is more than I can say for some cheap outlets." The side screws are color-coded (brass for hot, silver for neutral, green for ground), so mistakes are harder to make.

The tamper-resistant shutters are a genuine plus. They meet 2023 NEC requirements for residential installations, meaning if a child tries to jam a paperclip in, the shutters block it. These aren't the flimsy kind that stick — they release smoothly when a two-prong plug is inserted properly.

The LED is the headline feature. It's bright enough to see across a room during the day, which is exactly what you want for troubleshooting. One customer put it simply: "I replaced every outlet in my garage with these. Now I can see at a glance which circuit is live before I start working." For anyone who's ever flipped a breaker and wondered if it actually tripped, this is genuinely useful.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Across the reviews, three themes dominate. First, the indicator light's visibility — buyers repeatedly mention it's "much brighter than expected." Second, build quality relative to price; people describe it as feeling "solid, not cheap." Third, the tamper-resistant mechanism, which multiple reviewers confirmed "works smoothly without being stiff."

**Where It Falls Short**

The permanent indicator light is a double-edged sword. In a bedroom, home theater, or anywhere you want darkness, that green glow is always on. There's no switch to disable it. If that bothers you, look for a model with a photocell or skip the lighted feature entirely.

The body is also slightly larger than standard duplex receptacles. In a shallow 1.5-inch box with existing wires, it can be a squeeze. One reviewer noted, "I had to push harder than I'd like to get it seated in an old work box." Plan for that if you're retrofitting.

Finally, this is a residential-grade device. The strap is steel, not the thicker metal found on commercial spec-grade outlets. For a home workshop or occasional use, it's fine. For a shop running power tools all day every day, spend more on a spec-grade unit.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Homeowners replacing outlets in garages, basements, or unfinished spaces** — the indicator light is invaluable for identifying live circuits quickly
- **Anyone who's ever struggled with tripped breakers or dead outlets** — you'll see power status instantly
- **Parents or landlords** — the tamper-resistant shutters provide genuine safety compliance
- **DIYers comfortable with basic wiring** — installation is straightforward with clear terminal markings

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this in a bathroom or kitchen near water?**
A: No. This outlet is not GFCI-protected. For wet locations, you need a GFCI receptacle or a GFCI breaker protecting the circuit.

**Q: Does the LED draw power and increase my electric bill?**
A: Negligibly. The indicator draws roughly 0.1 watt — less than a dollar per year in most areas. Not a practical concern.

**Q: Will the back-wire terminals accept stranded wire?**
A: No. The push-in terminals are rated for solid copper wire only. Use the side screws for stranded wire, or use a crimp ferrule.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, code-compliant outlet that tells you at a glance whether power is live — especially for garages, basements, or any spot where you've ever cursed a dead circuit.

**Skip it if** the constant green glow would annoy you in a bedroom or media room, or if you need commercial-grade durability for heavy daily use.

At roughly the price of a standard tamper-resistant receptacle, the ELEGRP adds a genuinely useful feature for almost no premium. It's a smart upgrade for any homeowner. Just bring your own faceplate and be ready for a snug fit in older boxes.
