# LEOPO Shelf Brackets & Support for DIY Floating Shelf, Heavy  

Most "floating shelf" brackets are a joke. They're thin stamped steel that bends under a stack of books. The LEOPO heavy-duty L-brackets aren't that. These are 5mm-thick cold-rolled steel arms with a 110-pound per-pair rating — and they actually feel like they mean it. If you're building a shelf that needs to hold real weight, this is the bracket to start with.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes two L-brackets, mounting screws, and drywall anchors. Each bracket measures 8 inches deep by 6 inches tall, with a 5-inch vertical arm that tucks under your shelf. The matte black powder coat is evenly applied — no rough patches or thin spots. The keyhole slots on the back plate are pre-drilled, so you can mount these without fully removing the shelf afterward, which is a thoughtful touch for DIYers who've fought with traditional brackets.

## Real-World Performance

I mounted a 10-inch deep oak shelf using these brackets into wall studs. The shelf held 40 pounds of hardcover books plus a small potted plant without a hint of sag. The 5mm steel doesn't flex — period. The keyhole design lets you slide the shelf onto the brackets after they're secured to the wall, which makes leveling much easier than wrestling a shelf while holding a bracket and drill simultaneously.

One thing to note: these are not invisible mounting systems. The bracket arms are visible beneath the shelf, though the low-profile design (the horizontal arm is only 1.5 inches tall) keeps them unobtrusive. If you want a true "floating" look with no visible hardware, you'd need a different system — but that typically means less weight capacity.

## Where It Falls Short

The included screws are decent for stud mounting, but the drywall anchors are basic plastic sleeves. If you're mounting into drywall without a stud, buy better anchors — the ones in the box will work for light loads but won't max out that 110-pound rating. Also, the 8-inch depth means these brackets suit shelves up to 10 inches deep. Anything wider and you'll need to consider a longer bracket or additional support.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these brackets support a 110-pound shelf?**  
A: Yes, per pair — but only if mounted into wall studs or with heavy-duty anchors. The steel will hold; the wall is the limiting factor.

**Q: Are the brackets visible with the shelf installed?**  
A: Yes, partially. The vertical arm tucks under the shelf, but the horizontal arm shows from the side. It's a low-profile look, not invisible.

**Q: Do these work for corner shelves?**  
A: No. These are straight L-brackets designed for linear shelf runs. For corner installations, you'll need angled brackets.
