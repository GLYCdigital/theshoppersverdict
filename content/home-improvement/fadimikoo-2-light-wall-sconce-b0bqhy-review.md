The FadimiKoo 2-Light Wall Sconce sits in that crowded middle tier of Amazon vanity lighting — not the cheapest, not the priciest, but carrying a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings. The question isn't whether it's a decent fixture. It's whether it's *your* fixture. Here's what 225 buyers and I have to say.

## What Actually Shows Up

The box contains two complete sconces — one left-facing, one right-facing — each with its own glass shade. The matte black finish is a baked-on powder coat, not spray paint, so it won't chip when you're tightening the mounting screws. Each fixture measures roughly 12 inches wide and 7 inches tall, with a 4.5-inch backplate.

The shades are the headline feature: clear glass with an open bottom, designed to show off your bulbs. That means you're not hiding anything — if you buy ugly bulbs, you'll see ugly bulbs. Buyers who used Edison-style filament bulbs got the warm, industrial look the product photos promise. If you want diffused light, this isn't the sconce for you.

## Installation: The Good and the Finicky

The mounting bracket is standard-issue — two screws, a ground wire, and you're done. The backplate is sized to cover a standard 4-inch junction box, which matters more than most shoppers realize. Several reviewers noted they swapped these in for older fixtures with zero drywall work.

One recurring complaint: the wire is fabric-wrapped and stiff. One buyer said, "The wire doesn't bend easily, so getting everything tucked into the backplate takes patience." It's a 10-minute annoyance, not a dealbreaker. If you've wired a fixture before, you'll manage. If you haven't, budget an extra 15 minutes and watch a YouTube video first.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The dimming capability comes up repeatedly. One review notes, "I paired these with dimmable LED bulbs and a Lutron dimmer — works flawlessly, no flicker." That's not universal for budget fixtures, so it's worth calling out.

The finish also gets consistent love. Multiple buyers describe the matte black as "sleek" and "modern" — one person said it "looks way more expensive than it is." The clear glass shades don't yellow or fog, which is a problem with cheaper plastic or coated-glass options.

## Where It Falls Short

The glass shades ship loose in the box, and a handful of reviewers reported cracks on arrival. Amazon's return process handles it, but if you're on a deadline for a bathroom remodel, a damaged shade means a wait.

The 60-watt maximum per socket is fine for most bathrooms but limiting if you wanted something brighter for a hallway or large powder room. You'll get plenty of light for grooming, but don't expect to light a 200-square-foot room with these alone.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Bathroom remodelers** who want a clean, modern look without paying boutique prices
- **Anyone replacing a Hollywood-style bar light** — this gives you that up/down dual-light effect in half the footprint
- **Renters or first-time homeowners** who want something that installs with basic tools and no electrician

Skip it if you need diffused or shaded light, or if you're installing over anything but a standard junction box.

## FAQ from Real Shoppers

**Q: What bulbs look best in these?**
A: Edison-style filament bulbs (ST19 or ST64 shape) in warm white — that's what appears in the product photos and what most satisfied buyers used. Clear standard A19 bulbs work too, but they look more utilitarian.

**Q: Can these be installed pointing down only?**
A: Yes. The socket swivels, so you can angle both shades downward if that suits your space. Just don't expect to rotate them 180 degrees — the wire limits the range.

**Q: Do I need an electrician?**
A: If you're comfortable wiring a standard light fixture (hot, neutral, ground), no. If you've never touched a wire nut, hire someone — it's a 30-minute job for a pro.

## The Verdict: 4.6/5

**Buy it if** you want a clean, modern vanity light that shows off your bulbs, dims properly, and installs without drama. The 4.5-star average across 225 ratings is earned — this is a solid, honest fixture.

**Skip it if** you need diffused lighting, don't want to deal with a stiff wire during install, or expect a premium unboxing experience. The glass shades are the weak point in shipping, but most arrive intact.

For the price, you're getting a fixture that looks custom but installs like a standard swap. That's a good trade.
