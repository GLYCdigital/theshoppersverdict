**The PARTPHONER Table Lamp Set of 2: A solid buy for bedside duty, with one nagging catch**

Let’s cut the chase: if you need two matching lamps for nightstands, a desk, or a living room side table, this set gets the job done without breaking the bank. But "gets the job done" isn't the same as "wows you." After digging through 215 ratings and the specs, here’s what you’re actually getting.

**What’s in the box**  
Two identical lamps, each with a metal base, an adjustable gooseneck arm, and a fabric drum shade. The base is roughly 6 inches in diameter — enough to stay planted but not so big it swallows a small nightstand. The shade measures about 10 inches across, and the whole lamp stands just under 16 inches tall. That’s a compact footprint, good for tight spaces like dorm rooms or apartment nightstands.

Each lamp comes with a 5-foot cord, which is longer than most in this price range. The cord has an inline rocker switch. That matters, so hold that thought — I’ll come back to it.

**Performance and build quality**  
The metal base and arm feel reassuringly solid. One reviewer noted, *"I was expecting cheap plastic, but the base is heavy enough that the lamp doesn’t tip when my cat bumps it."* That tracks. The gooseneck holds its position once bent — no sagging after weeks of use.

The fabric shade is the real differentiator here. It diffuses light evenly, so you don’t get that harsh, squint-inducing hotspot you get with bare-bulb lamps. It works with any standard E26 bulb, so you can dial in warmth or brightness. A couple of buyers mentioned using smart bulbs with these — no issues with clearance or heat build-up.

The finish is matte and fingerprint-resistant, which is a small thing until you realize how often you touch a lamp to turn it on.

**What buyers consistently praise**  
The recurring theme in positive reviews is value-per-dollar. As one shopper put it: *"Two lamps for the price of one cheap one at a big-box store. They look more expensive than they are."* Another noted the packaging — *"Both arrived without a scratch, and the shades weren’t crushed like my last Amazon lamp order."*

The adjustable arm also gets consistent love. It’s not just a gimmick — you can angle light exactly where you need it, whether that’s a book or a keyboard.

**Where it falls short**  
Here’s the catch I mentioned: the switch is on the cord, not the base. If you push the lamp against a wall or behind a headboard, you’ll be fishing for that switch in the dark. It’s a minor annoyance, but it’s exactly the kind of thing that grates on you nightly. One reviewer put it bluntly: *"I wish the switch was on the lamp itself. It’s fine, but it’s not where my hand naturally goes."*

Second, bulbs aren’t included. That’s not unusual, but at this price point, some shoppers expect a ready-to-go solution. Budget an extra $10–15 for two decent LED bulbs.

Finally, the shade is fabric, which means it’s not ideal for rooms with high humidity (bathrooms) or dusty environments — it’ll trap particles over time. Not a dealbreaker, just something to know.

**Who should buy this**  
- **Dorm dwellers or first-apartment renters** who need functional lighting without a big spend.  
- **Anyone furnishing a guest bedroom** — two matching lamps instantly make the space feel intentional.  
- **People with narrow nightstands** — the compact footprint fits where bulkier lamps won’t.  

**Skip it if** you need a lamp with a touch switch, want integrated LED bulbs, or prefer a classic push-button on the base.
