Let’s be honest about dining room lighting: most fixtures are either cheap-looking or wildly overpriced. The AMZASA Dining Room Chandelier sits in the middle, and after digging through 229 ratings and the specs, it’s clear why this one holds a 4.6-star average. It’s a solid, no-nonsense fixture that does exactly what it promises — no more, no less.

## What you actually get

The box contains the chandelier body, six clear glass globe shades, mounting hardware, and a clear instruction sheet. The fixture measures about 24 inches in diameter and 18 inches tall, with an adjustable chain that lets you hang it anywhere from 24 to 48 inches above your table. That range covers most standard 8-to-10-foot ceilings without needing extra parts.

The build is all metal — no plastic pretending to be brushed steel. The matte black finish is evenly applied and feels durable, not like it’ll flake after a season. The glass globes are the standout feature; they’re thick enough to feel substantial but not so heavy that they strain the mounting plate.

## Performance in the real world

The six E12 sockets are positioned to distribute light evenly across a dining surface. In practice, that means no dark corners and no blinding glare when you’re seated. Buyers consistently note the light quality: “The warm bulbs I put in make the whole room feel cozy without being dim,” one reviewer wrote. Another said, “It brightens our 8-seater table perfectly — no shadows on the far end.”

Installation is straightforward if you’ve done a ceiling fixture before. The mounting bracket is standard, and the wiring is color-coded. Expect to spend 30–45 minutes if you’re careful. If you’re not comfortable with electrical work, budget for an electrician — that’s not a knock on the product, just reality.

## What buyers consistently praise

The most repeated compliments in the reviews center on three things: the look, the light quality, and the price-to-value ratio. One shopper put it simply: “This looks like the $400 fixtures I was eyeing at the lighting store.” Another mentioned the finish specifically: “The matte black is exactly as pictured — not glossy, not gray, just right.”

The glass globes get their own shoutouts. They diffuse light well and don’t yellow over time, which is a common problem with cheaper plastic shades. Buyers also appreciate that the fixture works with LED bulbs, keeping energy costs low.

## Where it falls short

No product is perfect, and this one has two real issues. First, the glass globes are fragile in transit. A handful of reviewers reported receiving cracked shades. AMZASA’s customer service replaces them, but it’s an annoying delay if you’re mid-renovation. Second, the fixture doesn’t include bulbs or a dimmer. You’ll need six E12 candelabra bulbs — figure another $15–$25 depending on your choice — and if you want dimming, that’s an additional wall switch purchase and install.

Also note: the chain is exposed, not covered by a canopy sleeve. That’s a deliberate design choice that fits industrial and modern aesthetics, but if you prefer a clean, flush-looking rod, this isn’t it.

## Who should buy this

This chandelier is ideal for anyone furnishing a dining room, eat-in kitchen, or open-concept living area with a modern, farmhouse, or industrial vibe. It works especially well over rectangular tables (60–84 inches) because the six lights span the length evenly. If you have a round table, it’ll still work, but you’ll want to center it precisely.

Skip it if you need a flush-mount fixture for low ceilings — it needs at least 24 inches of clearance. Also skip it if you’re not prepared to source bulbs and possibly a dimmer separately.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use LED bulbs in this chandelier?**
A: Yes. It takes standard E12 candelabra-base bulbs, and LED options work fine. Many buyers use warm-white LEDs (2700K–3000K) for a cozy dining atmosphere.

**Q: Is this hardwired or plug-in?**
A: Hardwired. There’s no plug-in option, so you’ll connect it to your ceiling junction box. If you don’t have existing wiring, you’ll need an electrician.

**Q: What if a glass shade arrives cracked?**
A: Contact AMZASA customer service directly. Multiple reviewers reported getting replacements quickly and without hassle, though the wait time varies.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a well-made, good-looking chandelier at a fair price without hunting for a bargain-bin special. The build quality is genuinely above average for this price bracket, and the light output is flattering for dining spaces.

**Skip it if** you need a fixture with included bulbs, can’t tolerate the risk of shipping damage, or want a fully concealed hanging mechanism.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A strong, reliable choice that earns its stars. Just budget for bulbs and a bit of patience with shipping.
