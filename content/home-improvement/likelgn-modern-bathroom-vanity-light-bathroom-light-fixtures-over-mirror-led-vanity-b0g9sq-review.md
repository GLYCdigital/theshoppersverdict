The bathroom vanity light is the most ignored workhorse in home improvement. You notice it only when it's wrong — casting shadows across your face, buzzing at 60Hz, or throwing harsh glare off the mirror. The Likelgn Modern Vanity Light aims to fix all three. After digging through 215 ratings and the spec sheet, here's what actually matters.

**What you're actually getting**

This is a 30-inch integrated LED bar, not a traditional fixture with replaceable bulbs. The box contains the light itself, mounting hardware, wire connectors, and a template for positioning the junction box. The aluminum housing is CNC-machined with a matte black finish — it's substantially heavier than the hollow steel shells you find at big-box stores. The diffuser is frosted acrylic, which is what turns the individual LED diodes into one continuous wash of light instead of a dotted line.

Installation is straightforward if you're comfortable with basic wiring. The fixture mounts to a standard 4-inch junction box, and the included template marks the screw holes so you don't measure wrong and drill into tile you can't patch. The wire leads are pre-stripped and long enough to work with. Expect 30-45 minutes for a DIY-er with a voltage tester and wire strippers.

**Performance where it counts**

The 3000K color temperature is the sweet spot for bathrooms — warm enough to flatter skin tones, neutral enough to apply makeup without looking like you're in a candlelit cave. Color rendering sits at 90+ CRI, which means the reds in your face don't look muddy. For anyone who's ever done a Zoom call from a bathroom, this matters more than you think.

The integrated LED strips run the full length of the bar, so light distribution is even. Multiple buyers noted the same thing: "No shadows when I'm putting on makeup" and "The light is so even, it's like daylight." The fixture runs cool even after an hour — the aluminum body acts as a heat sink, which is good for longevity but also means you won't burn yourself reaching over it.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The design is the headline. The slim profile and clean lines get consistent mention. One reviewer called it "the perfect modern touch" while another said it "looks like it cost three times as much." The matte black finish resists smudges — a small thing until you realize how much you touch bathroom fixtures with damp hands.

Installation gets props too. A recurring theme in the reviews: "Came with everything needed" and "Mounting template made it easy." For a fixture in this price range, buyers expect something to be missing — a bracket, a screw, clear instructions. That's rarely the case here.

**Where it falls short**

The elephant in the room: the LEDs are integrated and non-replaceable. When they die — typically 25,000 to 50,000 hours, so 6-10 years of normal use — you're not swapping a bulb. You're replacing the entire fixture. That's a real environmental and cost consideration. Most buyers won't care, but if you're the type to keep fixtures for two decades, this isn't that.

The hardwired-only design also limits flexibility. There's no plug-in version, so if your bathroom lacks a junction box in the right spot, you're looking at an electrician visit that could double your total cost. Check your existing wiring before you commit.

A few reviewers mentioned the 3000K warmth as a con — they wanted cooler, crisper daylight. If you prefer 4000K or 5000K, this isn't the fixture for you. There's no color temperature switching.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the homeowner doing a bathroom refresh who wants a modern, integrated look without hiring an electrician for a full rewire. It's also right for anyone who's tired of the harsh, shadow-casting light from a single globe fixture and wants even illumination for grooming. Skip it if you need color temperature options, prefer replaceable bulbs, or have a junction box in an awkward spot.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can this be dimmed?**
A: Only if you have a compatible LED dimmer switch and the fixture is wired to it. It's not dimmable out of the box. Check your dimmer's compatibility list — standard trailing-edge dimmers usually work, but older leading-edge models may flicker.

**Q: How bright is it really?**
A: The fixture outputs roughly 2400 lumens, which is equivalent to two 60-watt incandescent bulbs. That's plenty for a standard 4-6 foot vanity. If your bathroom is larger or you have dark countertops, you may want supplemental lighting.

**Q: Will it hold up in a humid bathroom?**
A: Yes, it's rated for damp locations, which covers steamy showers. The aluminum housing and sealed acrylic diffuser prevent moisture ingress. Just don't install it directly inside a shower stall — that requires wet-rated fixtures.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want an even, modern light bar that installs cleanly and looks far more expensive than it is. The 90+ CRI and 3000K warmth make it a solid choice for grooming tasks.

**Skip it if:** You need replaceable bulbs, want color temperature flexibility, or have a non-standard wiring situation that would require an electrician.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The design, brightness, and ease of installation earn the score. The non-replaceable LEDs and fixed color temperature knock off the last points. It's a strong value for most bathrooms, with caveats that matter mostly to long-term owners.
