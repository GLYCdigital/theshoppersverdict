Let's be direct: these gloves are not durable in the "buy once" sense. The nitrile foam coating will wear through at the fingertips with steady use—expect 3–6 weeks of daily work before they start showing holes. That's the tradeoff for the price and dexterity.

The sizing is also worth scrutinizing. These run small. If you're between sizes, size up. Several reviewers noted that the "Large" fit like a medium in other brands. Also, they offer zero insulation and zero cut protection—do not use these near rotating blades, sheet metal edges, or in cold weather. They're gloves, not armor.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I wash these gloves?**
A: Yes, machine wash cold and air dry. Washing extends their life, but it won't restore worn coating. Expect them to shrink slightly after the first wash.

**Q: Are these good for handling chemicals?**
A: No. The nitrile foam coating is not chemical-resistant. They'll protect against dirt and mild oils, but for solvents or acids, get a proper chemical-resistant nitrile glove.
