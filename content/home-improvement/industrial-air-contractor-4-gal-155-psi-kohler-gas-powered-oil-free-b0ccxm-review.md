**Bottom line up front:** The Industrial Air Contractor 4 Gal 155 PSI Kohler gas-powered compressor is a niche tool done right. It's not for everyone — but if you're a roofer, framer, or fence installer working where extension cords don't reach, this little machine punches above its weight. The Kohler engine is the headline feature, and for good reason.

## What You're Actually Getting

This is a compact gas-powered compressor with a 4-gallon tank and a 155 PSI maximum pressure rating. The Kohler engine is a genuine selling point — Kohler has decades of reputation in small engines, and they're known for starting reliably and lasting. The oil-free pump design eliminates the messy oil-change routine that plagues traditional compressors.

As shown in the product image, the unit sits on a sturdy frame with a roll cage protecting the tank and engine. The overall footprint is small enough to toss in the back of a truck — this is clearly designed for mobility, not stationary shop use.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where this compressor earns its keep. The 155 PSI max pressure gives you headroom for most pneumatic tools. The 4-gallon tank means you're not listening to the engine cycle every 30 seconds when using a framing nailer. In practice, you'll get a comfortable burst of 40-60 nails before the engine kicks back in.

The gas engine is the differentiator. No cords, no generators, no extension cable math. If you're working on a roof or a fence line a hundred yards from the nearest outlet, this thing just works. Cold starts are reliable — Kohler's choke system is intuitive, and the engine idles smoothly once warm.

**The tradeoff:** The CFM output is modest. This is a nailer compressor, not a shop workhorse. If you're planning to run a DA sander or HVLP spray gun continuously, you'll be disappointed. The duty cycle simply isn't there for sustained high-demand tools.

## FAQ

**Q: How loud is this compressor in decibels?**
A: Expect roughly 85-90 dB at full load — that's comparable to a lawn mower. You need hearing protection, and your neighbors will know you're working.

**Q: Will this run a paint sprayer?**
A: Only small touch-up guns with low CFM requirements. For full painting projects, you need a compressor with at least double the CFM output this unit provides.

**Q: How often does the oil-free pump need maintenance?**
A: That's the beauty of oil-free — no pump oil changes ever. You still need to check the engine oil level periodically and keep the air filter clean.
