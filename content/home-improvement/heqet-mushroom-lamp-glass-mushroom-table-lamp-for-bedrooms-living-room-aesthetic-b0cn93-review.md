# HEQET Mushroom Lamp Glass Mushroom Table Lamp for Bedrooms

If you've scrolled past one too many "aesthetic" mushroom lamps that turn out to be cheap resin or plastic knockoffs, the HEQET glass mushroom lamp is the real deal. This one actually delivers the warm, whimsical glow that the Pinterest photos promise — and it's got the 4.6-star rating (229 reviews) to back it up.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the lamp base, the glass mushroom shade, and a standard AC adapter. Assembly takes about 10 seconds: set the glass dome on the base, plug it in, done. The base is a weighted wood-grain plastic (don't be fooled — it's not real wood, but it doesn't look cheap), and the shade is genuine hand-blown glass with visible air bubbles and slight color variations.

As shown in the product image, the mushroom cap sits at roughly 10.6 inches tall — compact enough for a nightstand, bookshelf, or that empty corner of your desk that needs a vibe upgrade. The glass has an amber-tinted warmth that shifts depending on the light source behind it.

## Performance & Build Quality

The light output is where the HEQET shines (pun intended). It runs a warm 2700K LED — closer to candlelight than the sterile daylight you get from most budget lamps. There are three brightness levels controlled by touching the base: low, medium, and high. On the lowest setting, it genuinely looks like a glowing mushroom in a fairy tale. On high, it's bright enough to read by, though it's not going to light up a whole room.

The LED bulb is integrated — you can't swap it out, but rated lifespan is around 25,000 hours, so you'll get years of nightly use before it dims. The touch control is responsive, and the base has a nice heft to it, so the lamp doesn't tip over when you bump the nightstand.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The dominant theme in the reviews is the aesthetic payoff. One buyer wrote: "It looks way more expensive than it is. The glass is thick and the light is so warm and cozy — I bought a second one for my office." Another noted: "Perfect for a cozy bedroom vibe. ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. There's no USB charging port, no smart-home compatibility, no remote — you get a touch dimmer and that's it. For a lamp in this price range, that's acceptable, but if you're expecting modern features, you'll be disappointed.

Also, because the shade is hand-blown, there's unit-to-unit variation. Some buyers reported their glass cap had more noticeable bubbles or a slightly different amber tint than the product photos. It's part of the charm, but if you're a perfectionist, buy from a seller with a good return policy.

Finally, the "wood" base is definitely plastic. It's convincingly textured, but it's not hardwood — so if you're paying a premium for materials, this isn't it.

## FAQ

**Can I use this as a reading lamp?**
On the highest brightness, yes — it's adequate for a paperback but not for detailed work. The glow is warm and soft, not focused, so consider it ambient lighting with a reading bonus.

**Is the glass shade fragile?**
It's standard glass thickness, which means it can break if dropped. The base is weighted, so it's stable, but don't let kids play with it. Replacement shades aren't sold separately, so treat it carefully.

**Does it work with a dimmer switch?**
No. The touch base controls brightness — it won't respond to a wall dimmer. Plug it directly into a standard outlet.

## The Verdict
