# Waterspecialist Water Filter Replacement for Pur: The Aftermarket Option That Actually Holds Up

Pur's proprietary filter cartridges are notorious for their price — typically $15-20 each, and they need replacing every three months. That's $60-80 a year just to keep your tap water tasting decent. So when a compatible aftermarket option shows up at roughly half the cost, it's worth a hard look. The Waterspecialist replacement filter for Pur systems has earned a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, and after digging into what buyers actually report, the praise is largely justified — with a few caveats worth knowing before you buy.

## What You're Actually Getting

The package contains a single replacement filter cartridge designed to fit Pur faucet-mount filtration systems, including the FM-3700, FM-9400, FM-9800, and similar models. Each filter uses a coconut-shell carbon block core — the same filtration media category Pur uses — and is rated for 100 gallons or three months of typical household use. The unit measures about 5 inches tall and 2.5 inches in diameter, so it fits the standard Pur housing without any tools or adapters.

## Real-World Performance

The filtration claims are specific: 99% lead reduction, 96% mercury reduction, and significant chlorine taste and odor removal. Independent testing certification backs these numbers, which is rare at this price point. Multiple buyers confirmed the filter performs as advertised, with one noting it "removed the heavy chlorine taste from our well water completely within the first few days."

The flow rate is where things get interesting. The carbon block is denser than Pur's standard filter, which means water flows slightly slower through the faucet mount. Most users report this is barely noticeable — a few seconds longer to fill a glass. One reviewer mentioned it "pours a bit slower than the Pur brand filter but the water tastes cleaner, so it's a fair trade."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Cost savings dominate the feedback. At roughly half the price of OEM Pur filters, the math is simple. One buyer put it plainly: "Why pay $18 for a Pur filter when this one costs $9 and does the same job? I've bought three of these now."

Compatibility also gets consistent praise. Users report the cartridge clicks into place with the same firm rotation as the original, with no leaks around the housing seal. The filter's slightly larger carbon block actually extends contact time with water, which several reviewers said improved taste compared to the OEM cartridge.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest issue: this aftermarket filter doesn't include the RFID chip that Pur builds into their OEM cartridges. On some Pur faucet mounts, this means the "replace filter" light won't reset automatically after you swap in the Waterspecialist. You'll need to manually reset the indicator or simply track the date yourself. It's a minor annoyance, but if you rely on that light to remember when to change filters, it's worth noting.

The initial priming process also takes longer than OEM filters. The dense carbon block needs a solid 2-3 minutes of running water to fully saturate and purge trapped air. Some buyers reported sputtering for the first glass or two. It's not a defect — just physics — but it can catch people off guard.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Budget-conscious households** that replace filters every 3 months and want to cut annual filtration costs roughly in half
- **Renters** with a Pur faucet mount already installed who don't want to be locked into Pur's premium cartridge pricing
- **Anyone who doesn't rely on the electronic filter indicator** and is comfortable tracking replacement dates manually

Skip it if you need the RFID chip functionality or you're prone to forgetting filter changes without an automatic reminder.

## FAQ

**Will this filter fit my Pur faucet mount?**
It fits FM-3700, FM-9400, FM-9800, and most other Pur faucet-mount models. Check your unit's model number before ordering — if it uses the standard twist-lock cartridge, this will work.

**How long does one filter last?**
Rated for 100 gallons or 3 months, whichever comes first. Heavier water usage means more frequent changes — the same as OEM Pur filters.

**Does it remove PFAS or other emerging contaminants?**
No. Like the standard Pur filter, this targets lead, mercury, chlorine, and sediment. It does not remove PFAS, pharmaceuticals, or fluoride. For those, you'd need a reverse osmosis system.
