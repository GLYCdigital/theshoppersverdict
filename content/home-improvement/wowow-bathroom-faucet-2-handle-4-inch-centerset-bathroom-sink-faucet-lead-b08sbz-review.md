Let’s be blunt: most faucets under $50 are zinc alloy with plastic guts that strip out the first time you tighten a supply line. The WOWOW 2-handle centerset isn’t that. For roughly the price of a nice dinner, you get a solid brass body, ceramic disc cartridges, and a metal pop-up drain — components you’d expect on a $150 Delta. That’s why 225 Amazon buyers have pushed it to a 4.5-star average.

## What’s Actually in the Box

The package includes the faucet body, two handle levers, a metal pop-up drain assembly, and pre-attached 3/8-inch compression supply lines. Notably, the drain is **all metal** — including the stopper and tailpiece. That’s a genuine differentiator at this price point, because most budget faucets ship with a plastic drain that warps or cracks within a year. The supply lines are already connected to the valve body, which cuts install time significantly.

## Real-World Installation and Performance

The 4-inch centerset configuration is the standard for most existing three-hole bathroom sinks, so this is largely a drop-in replacement. The pre-attached supply lines are the standout feature here — you don’t need to reach into a dark cabinet to thread nuts onto a valve body. One reviewer summed it up: “Took me 20 minutes to replace my old faucet. The lines being pre-attached saved my back.”

The ceramic disc cartridges give a smooth quarter-turn action — no grinding, no sticking, and a positive shutoff. That means no drip, drip, drip at 2 a.m. Water flow is solid at 1.2 GPM, which clears the current federal limit and is fine for a bathroom sink. The metal pop-up drain operates smoothly with the lift rod, though the linkage is a touch fiddly to adjust on first install — give yourself an extra five minutes to get the stopper height right.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 225 ratings, three themes dominate the positive feedback:

1. **Ease of installation** — The pre-attached lines and standard centerset spacing make this a true DIY project. One buyer noted: “I’m not handy at all and had this installed in under an hour with just a basin wrench and adjustable pliers.”
2. **Build quality for the price** — Multiple reviewers specifically call out the weight of the faucet. “It feels substantial, not hollow like the cheap ones at the big box store,” reads one verified purchase review.
3. **Finish durability** — The brushed nickel finish has held up well for most users beyond the 6-month mark, with no peeling or flaking reported.

## Where It Falls Short

The spout reach is the most common complaint. At roughly 4.5 inches from the deck to the aerator and a reach of about 5.5 inches, it’s compact. If you have a deep vessel sink or prefer to fill a large pitcher, you’ll find the clearance tight. One reviewer put it plainly: “Great faucet, but the spout is short. You can’t really wash your face without your forehead hitting the top of it.”

The finish is also more of a **satin** than a true brushed nickel. It looks good out of the box, but it’s not as resistant to water spots as higher-end finishes. Plan to wipe it down occasionally if hard water is an issue.

## Who Should Buy This

- **DIY homeowners** replacing a builder-grade faucet on a standard 4-inch centerset sink — this installs in under an hour with basic tools
- **Rental property owners** who want a decent-looking, functional faucet that won’t leak or need a plumber call for years

**Skip it** if you have a vessel sink or need a high-arc spout for filling tall containers — the compact profile will frustrate you.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my existing 4-inch centerset sink?**
A: Yes, if your sink has three holes with 4 inches between the center of the outer holes. If you have a single-hole or 8-inch spread, this won’t work without a baseplate or replacement.

**Q: Are the supply lines long enough?**
A: The pre-attached lines are 16 inches. For most standard under-sink cabinets, that’s sufficient. If your shutoff valves are further away, you’ll need extension lines.

**Q: Does the pop-up drain work well?**
A: Yes, the metal stopper seals properly and the lift rod mechanism is reliable once adjusted. The linkage does require a bit of fiddling on initial setup, but it’s a one-time task.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a solid brass, ceramic-cartridge faucet with a metal drain at a budget price, and your sink uses a standard 4-inch centerset configuration.

**Skip it if** you need a tall spout or a true brushed nickel finish that hides water spots.

At 4.6/5, the WOWOW delivers 90% of what a $120 faucet does for roughly half the price. The short spout is the only real compromise, and for most standard bathroom sinks, it’s a non-issue. This is one of the smarter budget buys in the category.
