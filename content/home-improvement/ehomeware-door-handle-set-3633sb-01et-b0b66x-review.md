## What You Actually Get

The box contains two lever handles (interior and exterior), a standard latch mechanism, a strike plate, and all the mounting screws you'll need. The set is designed as a passage function — meaning no lock, just a push-down lever that opens the door. It's built around a zinc alloy core with a brushed nickel finish. As shown in the product image, the design is clean and minimalist — no ornate scrollwork or fussy details. This is a "blend into the hallway" handle, not a statement piece.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The zinc alloy construction gives this handle a reassuring heft. It doesn't feel hollow or cheap in the hand — a common complaint with budget lever sets. The brushed nickel finish is applied evenly across all surfaces, including the edges of the levers where cheaper sets often show bare metal. Multiple buyers noted the finish holds up well against daily use without showing fingerprints or water spots.

Installation is straightforward for anyone who's swapped a door handle before. The latch has an adjustable backset that works with both 2-3/8" and 2-3/4" pre-drilled holes. No special tools beyond a Phillips screwdriver are required. The levers attach via a simple set screw mechanism that locks them firmly in place — no wobble after tightening.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The most common complaint involves the strike plate. On non-standard door frames, the screw holes didn't align perfectly with existing holes, forcing a bit of filing or drilling. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing if you're working with an older home or a door that's been previously modified.

The passage-only function is also worth flagging. If you need privacy for a bathroom or bedroom, this isn't the set for you. There's no keyed or privacy version of this specific model — you'd need to look at EHOMEWARE's other offerings for that functionality.

A few reviewers also mentioned that the included screws are a bit soft — they can strip if you overtighten. Use a light touch and a properly sized bit.
