Look, step lights are one of those products where you get what you pay for — cheap ones buzz, flicker, and die within a year. The LEONLITE 5CCT 120V LED Step Light sits at a different tier, and with 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, it's worth understanding why before you hand over your money.

## What You're Actually Getting

Each fixture is a compact aluminum rectangle measuring roughly 4.5 inches long and 2 inches tall. The housing is die-cast aluminum with a frosted polycarbonate lens — this isn't the flimsy plastic you'll find at half the price. The 3.5W LED output is spec'd at 240 lumens, which is modest but appropriate for step lighting. You get the fixture itself plus mounting screws; you don't get a junction box, which is worth noting before you start.

## The Feature That Actually Matters: 5CCT Selectable Color

Here's the standout: a physical dial on the back cycles through five color temperatures — 2700K, 3000K, 3500K, 4000K, and 5000K. That means you can match your existing trim or set a mood without buying multiple SKUs. One buyer captured it well: *"Being able to dial in 2700K to match my hallway fixtures saved me from buying three different products."*

But here's the honest caveat: the dial is on the back of the fixture. You're making that selection before mounting, which means if you change your mind after installation, you're unscrewing the light and pulling it out again. Plan ahead.

## Real-World Performance

The dimming range genuinely impressed me. Most LED step lights in this class only dim to 20% or 30% before flickering. The LEONLITE handles 5% to 100% smoothly with standard ELV and MLV dimmers — no special smart dimmer required. One reviewer noted: *"Wired these to my Lutron dimmer and they go down to a subtle glow with zero flicker — exactly what I wanted for nighttime hallway navigation."*

The 120V direct connection simplifies installation compared to low-voltage systems that need transformers. If you're comfortable with basic electrical work — turning off the breaker, matching wires — this is a straightforward install. If you're not, budget for an electrician because hardwiring isn't a beginner project.

## Where It Falls Short

The 3.5W output is genuinely subtle. At 240 lumens, these are accent lights, not primary illumination. If your staircase is wide or you want bold lighting, you'll need one every 3-4 feet, and that adds up fast. Also, the lack of an included junction box caught several buyers off guard — one commented, *"Wish they'd been upfront about needing a box; ended up costing me an extra trip to the hardware store."*

Outdoor use is fine — the fixture is wet-rated — but the 120V wiring means you need proper outdoor-rated connections. Don't cut corners there.

## Who Should Buy These

- **DIYers comfortable with basic electrical** — this is a one-hour project if you know what you're doing

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use these with a standard dimmer switch?**
A: Yes, but they need ELV or MLV dimmers. Standard leading-edge TRIAC dimmers may cause flicker at low levels. Lutron and Leviton both make compatible options.

**Q: Are they truly outdoor-safe?**
A: The housing is wet-rated, so rain and humidity won't kill them. Just ensure your wiring connections are in a weatherproof box if installing outside.

**Q: How many do I need for a typical staircase?**
A: Plan on one light every 3-4 feet. A standard 12-step staircase usually needs 4-6 fixtures depending on width.
