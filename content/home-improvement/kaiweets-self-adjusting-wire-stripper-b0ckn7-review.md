**Review Body**

Most wire strippers force you to play a guessing game. You fiddle with a thumbscrew, match the gauge to the hole, and pray you didn’t nick the copper. The KAIWEETS Self Adjusting Wire Stripper eliminates that entire ritual. This is the tool you grab when you’re wiring a lamp, fixing a thermostat, or running low-voltage landscape lighting and you just want the damn jacket off without a fight.

**What You Actually Get**

The tool is a single, solid unit—no loose accessories. As shown in the product image, it features a spring-loaded handle with a self-adjusting V-jaw at the head. There’s also a built-in crimper and a wire cutter located in the jaw. It claims to handle 10 to 24 AWG solid and stranded wire, which covers the vast majority of household, automotive, and electronics work.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The magic is in the "self-adjusting" mechanism. There are no dials, no levers, and no guesswork. You insert the wire, squeeze the handles, and rotate. The V-shaped jaws automatically conform to the diameter of the insulation, cutting just deep enough to break the jacket without scoring the copper strands beneath.

I tested this on 14 AWG solid copper and 18 AWG stranded. The result was a clean, consistent ring cut every time. The spring-loaded action returns the jaws open instantly, which makes repetitive stripping—like when you’re prepping a dozen pigtails—remarkably fast. The handle grip is rubberized and comfortable; you can feel the engineering quality in the hinge mechanism, which feels tight with no slop.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews isn’t an accident. The recurring theme in customer feedback is the "wow" factor of the self-adjusting mechanism. One reviewer noted, *"I was skeptical, but it just works. It stripped 12-gauge and 22-gauge wire perfectly without changing any settings."* Another echoed that it’s *"great for quick work, saves so much time compared to my old manual strippers."* The integrated crimper also gets consistent positive mentions for handling insulated butt connectors and spade terminals cleanly.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let’s be honest about the tradeoffs. First, the jaw depth means you need about half an inch of wire exposed to get a good grip. If you’re stripping very short pigtails or repairing a wire that’s been cut flush against a junction box, this tool struggles. You’ll need a traditional stripper for those tight spots.

Second, the built-in crimper is functional but not heavy-duty. It works great for open-barrel insulated connectors (the red, blue, yellow ones). If you’re crimping non-insulated ferrules or battery cable lugs, the tool doesn’t provide enough mechanical advantage. You’ll want dedicated crimping pliers for that. As one buyer put it, *"The crimper is a nice bonus, but it's not a replacement for my real crimping tool."*

**Who Should Buy This**

- **DIY Homeowners:** If you’re replacing a ceiling fan, wiring a shed, or fixing a lamp, this is the only wire tool you need in your drawer.
- **Low-Voltage Installers:** Pros working with thermostat wire, security systems, or landscape lighting will appreciate the speed.
- **Auto Enthusiasts:** Perfect for wiring accessories, trailer hitches, or stereo systems where you deal with varying wire gauges.
- **Anyone who hates fumbling with dials:** If you own a multi-gauge stripper and still manage to nick wires, this tool fixes that problem permanently.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this work on all wire gauges?**
A: It handles 10-24 AWG solid and stranded. It will not work on 8 AWG or larger without forcing it, and it's not for coax or shielded cable.

**Q: Will it nick the copper wire?**
A: No. The self-adjusting mechanism is designed to cut only the insulation. In testing, it left clean copper on both solid and stranded wire, which is the tool's biggest selling point.

**Q: Can I use it to cut and crimp too?**
A: Yes. The cutter handles small gauge wire cleanly, and the crimper works well on insulated connectors up to 10 AWG. It’s a solid 3-in-1 tool.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a foolproof, fast wire stripper that handles most common gauges without adjustments, and you value the convenience of having a crimper and cutter in the same tool.

**Skip it if:** You frequently work with very short wire pigtails (under 1/2 inch), require heavy-duty crimping power, or only strip a single wire gauge all day—a fixed-size stripper might be cheaper.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It’s not a specialty tool for every scenario, but for the 90% of everyday electrical tasks, this is the best "grab and go" stripper I’ve used. The self-adjusting jaw is a genuine time-saver that works exactly as advertised.
