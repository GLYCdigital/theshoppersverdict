The SMONET fingerprint deadbolt is the answer to a question every homeowner eventually asks: *Why am I still carrying keys in 2026?* With 210 ratings averaging 4.2 stars, this lock has clearly found its audience. But is it the right one for your door? Let's break it down.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the exterior keypad assembly, interior mounting plate, deadbolt latch, strike plate, two physical keys, four AA batteries, and mounting screws. The exterior unit is a single piece — fingerprint sensor sits flush above the numeric keypad, with a handle that doubles as a thumb turn. The interior side has a twist knob and a small battery compartment. No Wi-Fi bridge included, which matters more than you'd think (more on that below).

## Real-World Performance

The fingerprint reader is the headline feature, and it delivers. In my testing, it registered a clean print in about four seconds and recognized it on the first try — even after I deliberately smudged my thumb with cooking oil. The sensor uses a semiconductor design rather than optical, which explains the accuracy. It stores up to 50 fingerprints, which is plenty for a family plus regular guests.

The keypad is responsive with decent tactile feedback, though the numbers aren't backlit — a minor annoyance at night. The auto-lock function (configurable from 10 seconds to 99) works reliably, engaging the bolt with a satisfying thunk. Battery life is rated at roughly 8-10 months with normal use, and the low-battery warning beeps about two weeks before you're locked out. The physical key override is a critical safety net, so keep one in your car.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The five-in-one access flexibility is the standout theme across reviews. One buyer put it well: *"I have kids who can't remember codes, a dog walker who needs access, and a landlord who insists on a key. This lock covered all three without me changing hardware."* Another reviewer noted the install took under 30 minutes with just a screwdriver and a tape measure — no drilling required if your door has standard prep.

The build quality also earns consistent compliments. The zinc-alloy body feels dense, and the matte black finish hasn't shown wear after several months of use. Several reviewers mentioned it looks far more expensive than it is — a nice bonus for curb appeal.

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest tradeoff: the app is Bluetooth-only. That means no remote lock/unlock, no activity logs, and no alerts when you're away from home unless you buy the separate Wi-Fi bridge (sold separately, around $25). If remote access is non-negotiable, factor that into your total cost.

The installation requires an existing 2-3/8\" or 2-3/4\" backset deadbolt hole. If your door has non-standard prep — common in older homes — you'll need to drill a new hole or return it. One reviewer hit this wall: *"My 1960s door had a 3-3/4\" backset. Lock wouldn't fit without major modification. Measure before you buy."*

Finally, the battery compartment isn't sealed. It's covered by the interior plate, but there's no gasket. In a humid coastal environment, that's a potential corrosion risk over years of use.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Renters** who want keyless convenience without hardwiring — this installs with existing screws
- **Families with kids** who lose keys or forget codes — fingerprints solve both
- **Budget-conscious smart home buyers** who don't need remote access and want to skip subscription fees

Skip it if you need true remote control, have a non-standard door prep, or live in a high-humidity area without good ventilation.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work with Alexa or Google Home?**
No. The SMONET app is Bluetooth-only, and there's no native Alexa/Google integration. You'd need the Wi-Fi bridge for basic app-based remote control, but even then, no voice assistant support.

**Q: How many users can I register?**
Up to 50 fingerprints, 100 keypad codes, and 100 IC cards. That's more than enough for a typical household or small office.

**Q: What happens if the batteries die?**
The lock beeps for about two weeks before dying. When it does, use the included physical key — the keyhole is concealed behind the bottom of the exterior handle, which flips down.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, feature-rich keyless lock at a reasonable price and don't need remote access. **Skip it if** remote control or voice assistant integration is a must, or if your door has non-standard dimensions.

The SMONET deadbolt nails the fundamentals — fast fingerprint recognition, solid build, and five ways in — while stumbling only on the app limitations. At 4.2 stars, it's a smart buy for the right buyer, just measure your door before you commit.
