If you've ever bought a "women's" safety vest that drapes like a tent, you already know the frustration. The Amylove 4-pack of long-sleeve hi-vis shirts actually addresses that problem — with a cut that follows a female silhouette rather than borrowing from the men's rack. At 4.5 stars across 225 ratings, it's clearly resonating with women who spend their shifts on roadsides, warehouse floors, and loading docks.

## What's in the pack

You get four long-sleeve shirts in high-visibility yellow. Each has two horizontal reflective stripes — one across the chest, one below the waist — plus vertical stripes over the shoulders. The fabric is 100% polyester mesh with a moisture-wicking finish. Sizes run from S to 3XL, though the fit runs snug (more on that in a moment).

## Real-world performance

The mesh construction is the standout feature. Unlike the stiff, plastic-feeling fabric on budget hi-vis gear, this polyester knit has some give and breathes noticeably. During a 10-hour shift directing traffic in August, you'll still sweat — but you won't feel like you're wearing a trash bag. The long sleeves provide actual sun protection, which is a bonus if you're outside all day.

The reflective striping is positioned correctly for ANSI visibility: two inches wide, wrapping fully around the torso. It's not 3M Scotchlite, so the retro-reflective brightness drops off in heavy rain or fog, but under headlights at night it catches light competently. Multiple Amazon reviewers confirm durability through repeated washing — one noted, "I've washed these every week for three months and the stripes haven't peeled or cracked."

The women's cut is genuine. The waist nips in slightly, the shoulders are narrower, and the torso is shorter than unisex equivalents. One buyer put it plainly: "Finally a safety shirt that doesn't make me look like I'm swimming in fabric." The side seams sit properly, so the shirt doesn't bunch under a tool belt or harness.

## Where it falls short

Sizing is the biggest complaint, and it's consistent enough to take seriously. The shirts run small — multiple reviewers recommend ordering one size up, especially if you're a C-cup or larger. The fabric has minimal stretch, so a too-tight fit won't give you much forgiveness.

The collar is a simple ribbed knit that can feel scratchy against the neck for the first few wears. It softens after a wash cycle, but if you have sensitive skin, plan for that break-in period.

And while the mesh is breathable, it's not wind-resistant. In cold weather, you'll need a base layer or jacket underneath — this is a warm-weather shirt, not a year-round option.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these ANSI-rated for job site requirements?**
A: They meet Class 2 visibility standards for most non-highway applications, but they're not ANSI-certified to the same level as dedicated surveyor gear. Check your employer's specific requirements before relying on these as your sole PPE.

**Q: How do they hold up in the washing machine?**
A: Well, based on buyer feedback. The polyester mesh resists shrinking, and the reflective tape is sewn down firmly. Wash in cold water and hang dry to maximize lifespan — tumble drying is fine but will eventually break down the elastic.

**Q: Do the shirts run true to size?**
A: No. Most buyers recommend sizing up at least one size. If you're between sizes, definitely go with the larger option — the fabric doesn't stretch.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're a woman who needs functional hi-vis shirts that fit properly and don't cost a fortune per piece. The 4-pack price point makes this a practical choice for anyone who wears safety gear daily.
