# TUNNY 10-Pack Square Cabinet Handles: The Unpretentious Upgrade Your Kitchen Actually Needs

Let’s be honest — cabinet hardware is the jewelry of a kitchen. You can spend $15 a pull on designer brass, or you can buy a 10-pack of these TUNNY square handles for less than the cost of a single fancy knob. The question is: do they hold up, or do they just look good in the product photos? After digging through 235 ratings and the specs, here’s the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains ten square bar pulls, each measuring roughly 5 inches long with a 3-inch (76.2mm) screw hole spacing — the industry standard for most pre-drilled cabinet doors. You also get mounting screws, though you'll want to check the length against your door thickness. The handles are made of zinc alloy with a brushed finish. As shown in the product image, they have a clean, minimalist profile that sits flush against the door without awkward overhang.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Zinc alloy is the right call at this price point. It’s denser and more substantial than the hollow aluminum you often find at big-box stores. One buyer noted, "These feel like solid metal, not the cheap hollow ones from the hardware store." That weight matters — it means the handle won't flex when you pull a heavy drawer loaded with cast iron.

The brushed finish is consistent across all ten pieces, which is rarer than it should be in multi-pack hardware. No mismatched sheen between pulls. The square profile gives you a comfortable grip without the sharp edges you'd expect from the geometry. It's a thoughtful design touch.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The value proposition is the recurring theme. With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, the feedback is overwhelmingly positive. "Installed on all my kitchen cabinets and they look like they cost three times as much," one reviewer wrote. ## Where It Falls Short

The included screws are the weak point. At roughly 15mm, they're adequate for standard 3/4-inch cabinet doors but will come up short on thicker drawer fronts or if you're adding a backplate spacer. Plan to pick up longer screws at the hardware store if your doors are anything but standard.

The brushed finish, while fingerprint-resistant, does show water spots. If you're the type to spray cleaner directly on hardware and air-dry, you'll see streaks. A quick wipe with a dry cloth solves it, but it's worth knowing.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy these if:** You're redoing a kitchen or bathroom on a budget, you want a clean modern look without paying boutique prices, or you need to match hardware across a full run of cabinets without ordering multiple packs.

**Skip these if:** You're installing on extra-thick custom doors without checking screw length first, or you want a polished chrome look — this is brushed finish only.

## FAQ

**Q: Will these fit standard pre-drilled cabinet doors?**
A: Yes, they use the common 3-inch (76.2mm) hole spacing. If your doors are pre-drilled at that distance, these drop right in.

**Q: Are the handles actually square or just slightly tapered?**
A: They're square-profile with slightly eased edges on the grip surface. Not sharp, but definitively squared-off — not rounded.

**Q: Do the screws strip easily?**
A: Zinc screws at this price point can be soft. Use a hand screwdriver rather than a power drill to avoid cam-out, and pre-drill pilot holes for best results.
