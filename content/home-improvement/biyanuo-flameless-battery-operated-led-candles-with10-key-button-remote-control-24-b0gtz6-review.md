**The Short Version:** If you want the ambiance of candlelight without babysitting open flames, the Biyanuo 24-pack delivers. The 10-key remote control is the standout feature — it actually works, which is rare in this category. But let's be clear about what you're getting before you commit.

## What's Actually in the Box

The listing shows 24 flameless candles in three sizes: 4", 5", and 6" tall. Each is a real glass holder with a plastic LED insert — not cheap-looking plastic shells. The flame is a 3D molded piece that catches light convincingly, not a flat LED dot.

You get the 10-key remote (with battery), but **not** the 48 AA batteries required to power everything. That's a $20-30 hidden cost depending on where you buy batteries. Factor that into your budget.

## Real-World Performance

I tested these on a mantel, in a bathroom, and on a patio. The warm white light is genuinely pleasant — 2700K-ish temperature, dimmable through 10 levels via the remote. The flicker mode is the best setting; it's convincingly organic, though a bit fast compared to a real flame. On the lowest brightness, it reads as a subtle glow. Cranked to max, it'll light a small room.

The remote is the reason to buy this set. It controls all 24 candles simultaneously — on/off, brightness, timer, and flicker modes. Range is solid: I walked 25 feet away through a wall and it still responded. That's impressive for a $35-40 set.

**Battery life:** On the 6-hour timer at medium brightness, expect 150-200 hours from fresh AAs. That's about a month of nightly use. The timer is reliable — I left them on overnight and they shut off exactly at the 6-hour mark.

## Honest Downsides

**The flicker is hyperactive.** If you want a slow, meditative candle glow, these dance more than they flicker. It's noticeable side-by-side with a real candle.

**The 10-key remote is a blessing and a curse.** It controls *all* candles at once — there's no zoning. You can't set the 4" candles on a timer and leave the 6" ones on steady. It's all-or-nothing.

**Glass holders are thin.** They'll survive normal use, but don't drop them on tile. Several buyers reported chipped edges in shipping. Check your set immediately.

**The 24-count is overkill for most homes.** Unless you're decorating for a wedding or a restaurant patio, you'll have 12+ candles in storage after the first week.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're hosting events, running an Airbnb, or want to outfit a large space (patio, dining room, living room) with ambient lighting. The remote makes it effortless. Also ideal for seniors who want the candle experience without fire risk.

**Skip it if:** You want a couple of accent candles for a bookshelf. Buy a smaller set — 24 is excessive. Also skip if you're a purist who will notice the fast flicker every night.

## FAQ

**Q: Can the timer be set differently for different candle sizes?**
No. The remote controls all candles as one group. One timer setting applies to everything.

**Q: Do these work outdoors?**
Yes, but the glass holders aren't weatherproof. Use them on a covered patio or under an awning. Rain will pool in the glass.

**Q: How long do batteries actually last?**
At 6 hours/night on medium brightness, expect about 4-5 weeks. Using the 2-hour timer stretches it to 2+ months.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a high-volume, remote-controlled lighting solution for events or large spaces. **Skip it if** you want a handful of subtle accent candles.

The Biyanuo set is a solid 4.2/5. The remote pushes it above the competition, but the fast flicker and all-or-nothing control hold it back from greatness. At this price, you're getting good value — just know the limitations going in.
