If your garage or workshop still runs on flickering fluorescent tubes that hum louder than your table saw, the hykolity SLA-42W 4FT is the replacement you've been putting off. This is a 42-watt LED shop light that cranks out a claimed 5,000 lumens of 5000K daylight — and based on the 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, it's delivering on that promise for most buyers.

Here's the real story.

## What you actually get

The box contains one 4-foot LED fixture with an integrated 6-foot power cord, two V-hooks for hanging, and a quick-start guide. That's it — no mounting screws, no brackets for flush ceiling installation. The unit itself is linkable, meaning you can connect up to six fixtures together using the included connectors on each end, then power the whole chain from a single outlet.

Physically, it's a slim profile — about 4 feet long, roughly 2 inches thick — with a frosted diffuser that softens the light and cuts glare. The housing is lightweight plastic rather than metal, which keeps it around 3 pounds. That matters if you're hanging it from drywall without a stud finder handy.

## Real-world performance

The 5000K color temperature is what you want for task lighting — it's crisp, white, and makes small screws and dark oil stains easy to see. One fixture effectively lights a two-car garage bay when mounted at standard 8-9 foot ceiling height. Two fixtures eliminates shadows entirely.

Installation genuinely takes five minutes. Screw the V-hooks into the ceiling, drop the fixture's keyhole slots over them, plug it in. The linkable feature works as advertised — daisy-chaining three units from a single switch is clean and avoids running new wiring.

## What buyers consistently praise

The recurring theme in positive reviews is the brightness-to-price ratio. One verified buyer put it plainly: *"Replaced my old fluorescent shop lights and it's not even close — this thing is BRIGHT."* Another noted the linkable design specifically: *"Hooked three of these together in my workshop. One switch, no electrician needed."*

The lack of flicker also comes up repeatedly — common with cheap LEDs, but not here. The drivers are decent quality, which shows in stable output and no visible strobe in peripheral vision.

## Where it falls short

The plastic housing is the main compromise. It's not fragile, but it doesn't dissipate heat as efficiently as an aluminum fixture. That shortens the lifespan of the LED chips under continuous heavy use — expect maybe 30,000 hours instead of the 50,000 you'd get from a pricier metal unit.

The non-dimmable driver is a limitation if you wanted to use these in a living space. And the lack of included mounting hardware for flush installs is a minor annoyance — you'll need to buy screws or toggle bolts separately if you don't want the hanging look.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** You need bright, reliable lighting for a garage, basement, workshop, or utility room on a budget. You're comfortable with a 5-minute hanging install. You want to replace multiple fluorescent fixtures without rewiring.

**Skip it if:** You need dimmable lighting for a living area. You demand aluminum construction for maximum lifespan. You want a flush-mount ceiling fixture with included hardware.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I plug these into a regular wall outlet?**
A: Yes. The included cord terminates in a standard three-prong plug. No hardwiring required unless you want it on a wall switch — then you'd need to replace the cord or add an in-line switch.

**Q: How many can I link together?**
A: Up to six fixtures per chain, per the manufacturer. That's 252 watts total on one circuit — well within a standard 15-amp breaker's capacity (which handles about 1,800 watts).

**Q: Are these waterproof for outdoor use?**
A: No. They're rated for indoor dry locations only. For a covered patio or damp garage, you'd need a sealed fixture with a higher IP rating.

## The Verdict
