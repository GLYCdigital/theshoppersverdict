**A 4.3-star average across 215 ratings tells you this fixture isn't a dud — but it also isn't a "hang it in 20 minutes" project. The Upfelw Modern Crystal Tree Branch Chandelier delivers serious Art Deco drama if you're willing to invest assembly time and have the right ceiling height. Here's what you're actually getting.**

## What's in the Box

The package includes the main body with six pre-wired branch arms, 60+ individual K9 crystal prisms in the "raindrop" cut, a mounting canopy, and all necessary hardware — down to wire nuts and a grounding screw. What's **not** included: bulbs (E12 candelabra base), which catches some buyers off guard. The arms arrive folded flat for shipping; you'll articulate them into position yourself.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The frame is solid brass with a brushed gold finish — not painted plastic, which matters at this price point. The K9 crystals are the star here. K9 is optical-grade glass, and it shows: in direct sunlight or strong overhead light, you get genuine rainbow refraction, not just sparkle. One reviewer noted, *"The crystals throw tiny rainbows across the ceiling in the afternoon — my wife actually gasped when she saw it."*

The six arms are fully adjustable at the joints, which is more flexible than most chandeliers in this category. You can create a tight, upward-sweeping silhouette for an 8-foot ceiling or spread the branches wide for a dramatic 12-foot foyer statement. The fixture itself is listed at 26 inches in diameter and 18 inches tall — verify your ceiling clearance before ordering, because this is not a small piece.

Light output depends entirely on your bulb choice. With six E12 sockets, standard 40-watt equivalent LED candelabra bulbs give you adequate dining-room illumination (roughly 2,400–3,000 lumens total). Dimmability works fine with standard TRIAC dimmers — no proprietary driver required, which is a genuine plus for retrofits.

## Where It Falls Short

**Assembly is the real cost here.** Attaching 60+ crystals one at a time is meditative for some, maddening for others. The crystals use small threaded studs that require hand-tightening — and you'll want to handle each one carefully, because K9 glass is genuinely fragile if dropped on tile or hardwood. Budget a solid hour.

**Hardwired only.** This is a permanent fixture requiring a ceiling junction box. Renters or anyone without existing wiring will need an electrician, adding $150–$300 to the total cost. There's no plug-in conversion kit available.

**Bulb compatibility quirks.** The E12 candelabra base limits you to smaller bulbs. Standard A19 LEDs won't fit. You'll need T3 or candelabra-style LEDs — and some buyers reported that larger decorative bulbs interfere with the crystal placement on the lower branches. Read the dimensions carefully.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Design-focused homeowners** redecorating a dining room, entryway, or stairwell with Art Deco or Hollywood Regency styling
- **Anyone with ceilings 9 feet or taller** — the fixture's vertical drop and branch spread need room to breathe
- **DIYers comfortable with basic electrical work** — if you've replaced a ceiling fan, you can handle this install

**Skip it if** you're a renter, you have an 8-foot ceiling in a small room, or you want a "hang and done" fixture without crystal-by-crystal assembly.

**Q: Does this chandelier require a specific dimmer switch?**
A: No — it works with standard TRIAC dimmers when paired with dimmable LED bulbs. No proprietary dimmer or transformer needed.

**Q: Can I install this on a sloped or vaulted ceiling?**
A: Yes, but you'll need to order a sloped-ceiling adapter kit separately. The included canopy is designed for flat ceilings only.

**Q: How many crystals are there, and are replacements available?**
A: Approximately 60–65 prisms depending on arm positioning. Upfelw does sell replacement crystal sets, but contact customer service directly — they're not always listed on the product page.
