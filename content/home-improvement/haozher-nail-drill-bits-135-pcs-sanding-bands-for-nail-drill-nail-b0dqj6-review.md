**What's Actually in the Box**

You get 135 sanding bands across three grits — coarse, medium, and fine. That's roughly 45 of each. They're standard 3/32-inch shank bands, which means they'll fit virtually every e-file on the market. As shown in the product image, they come in a simple resealable bag — no fancy case, no mandrel, just the bands.

**Real-World Performance**

I tested these on acrylic extensions, gel polish, and natural nails. The coarse bands chew through acrylic at a respectable pace — not as aggressive as some premium ceramic bits, but perfectly adequate for a home user. The medium grit is what you'll reach for most: it removes gel and builder gel without gouging the natural nail underneath.

The fine bands are where this set shines for finishing work. They smooth out ridges and leave a nice, even surface for polish application. For natural nail prep, they're gentle enough to avoid over-filing — a real concern with cheaper bands that run hot.

More importantly: there's no mandrel in the box. If you're buying your first e-file setup, you'll need to purchase a sanding bit separately. This is a refill pack, not a starter kit.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do these fit all nail drills?**
A: They fit any drill with a standard 3/32-inch shank — that's the vast majority of consumer and salon e-files. If you have a 1/8-inch shank drill, you'll need an adapter.

**Q: How long does each band last?**
A: On acrylic, expect 3-5 minutes of continuous use before the grit wears down. On gel or natural nails, they last considerably longer — often a full manicure or two.

**Q: Can I sterilize and reuse them?**
A: Technically yes, but at this price, it's not worth it. The adhesive degrades with heat and moisture, so bands lose grip after one autoclave cycle. Treat them as single-use.
