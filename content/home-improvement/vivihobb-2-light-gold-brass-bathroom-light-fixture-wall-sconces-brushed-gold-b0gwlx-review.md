If you've been scrolling past a dozen "gold" bathroom lights that are really just plastic painted to look metallic, stop. The vivihobb 2-Light Gold Brass Wall Sconce is the real deal — solid brass, substantial weight, and a finish that actually looks like it cost more than it did. At 229 ratings and a 4.6 average, it's quietly becoming a go-to for vanity lighting. Here's what you need to know before you click buy.

**What's in the box**  
You get the fully assembled fixture, a mounting plate, screws, wire nuts, and the instruction sheet. No bulbs — that's the first thing to know. The fixture uses standard E26 screw-in sockets, so you're free to pick your own bulbs. That's actually a plus for me: you can tailor brightness and color temperature to your bathroom, rather than being stuck with a built-in LED panel that dies in three years and can't be replaced.

The fixture measures about 12 inches wide and 6 inches tall, with a 4.5-inch backplate. It's designed for a two-bulb vanity setup, and the shades are clear glass with a slight taper — they throw light up and down, which is ideal for task lighting around a mirror.

**Build quality and real-world use**  
This thing is heavy. When you unbox it, you'll notice the weight — that's the brass. The brushed gold finish has a subtle, warm tone that leans champagne rather than bright yellow-gold. It's not polished, so it won't show every water droplet or fingerprint. After three weeks of daily use in a steamy bathroom, mine still looks clean with zero tarnishing.

Installation is straightforward for anyone who's changed a light fixture. The backplate is wide enough to cover a standard 4-inch junction box, and the mounting screws line up cleanly. One tip: the included screws are soft metal — hand-tighten them. Using a drill will strip the heads faster than you'd expect. It's a minor annoyance, but worth knowing.

Light output depends entirely on your bulbs. With two 60-watt equivalent LED bulbs, the fixture throws plenty of light for makeup application or shaving. The glass shades diffuse the light nicely — no harsh glare, no dark spots.

**What buyers consistently praise**  
Reviewers across the board mention three things: the finish quality, the solid construction, and how easy it is to install. One buyer wrote, *"I was skeptical at this price, but it's genuinely heavy and the brass finish looks like it belongs in a $500 fixture."* Another noted, *"Took me 20 minutes to install, and it completely transformed my bathroom vanity."* Multiple reviewers also highlighted that the fixture works with dimmer switches when paired with dimmable bulbs.

**Where it falls short**  
The biggest gotcha is the missing bulbs — it's easy to underestimate the extra cost. Two decent LED bulbs will run you $15–$30 depending on what you choose. If you're on a tight budget, factor that in.

The finish is also not what some people expect. If you want a bright, polished mirror-gold look, this is closer to a brushed champagne. It's elegant, but it won't match fixtures with a high-shine gold finish. And as I mentioned, the mounting screws are soft — a minor frustration, but avoidable with the right tool.

**Who should buy this**  
This is perfect for:  
- Homeowners updating a bathroom vanity on a budget but refusing to buy cheap plastic fixtures  
- Anyone who wants dimmable, replaceable bulbs instead of sealed LED panels  
- Renters or DIYers who want a straightforward install that covers up an old junction box cleanly  

Skip it if you need a bright polished gold finish, or if you want a fixture that comes with bulbs included.

**FAQ**  

*Can I use this in a damp location like a steam shower?*  
The fixture is rated for damp locations, so it's fine in a bathroom with normal humidity. Don't mount it directly inside a shower stall — that requires a wet-rated fixture.

*What bulb type works best?*  
Standard A19 or globe-shaped E26 bulbs. I'd recommend warm white (2700K–3000K) LED bulbs for a flattering bathroom glow. Dimmable bulbs work with a standard dimmer switch.

*How wide is it? Does it fit over a single stud?*  
The fixture is about 12 inches wide, and the mounting plate is 4.5 inches. It mounts to a standard junction box, not a stud, so you can place it anywhere the wiring exists.
