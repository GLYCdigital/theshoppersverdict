Let me be blunt: most battery wall sconces are garbage. They look like cheap plastic pucks with a cold blue glow that belongs in a dentist's office. The Powfloven Battery Wall Sconce is not that. At 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, it's earned its place — but it's not perfect either. Here's what you need to know before you pull the trigger.

**What you actually get**

The box includes two sconce bodies (the listing sells them in pairs), two magnetic mounting plates, wall anchors, screws, and two USB-C charging cables. No hardwiring, no electrician, no junction box required. Each fixture measures roughly 4 inches wide by 7 inches tall — compact enough for hallways, flanking a bed, or framing a bathroom mirror. The matte black aluminum housing looks far more expensive than the price point suggests, and the fabric-wrapped cord is a nice touch if you ever want to use it plugged in via the included USB-C adapter (sold separately, mind you — they only give you the cable).

**Real-world performance**

The 3000K color temperature is the standout feature. It's a true warm white — think old-school incandescent, not the sterile LED tone that dominates this category. On full brightness, it's genuinely useful for reading or task lighting, not just ambient glow. On low, it dims enough to function as a nightlight without blasting you awake at 2 AM.

Battery life is honest: expect 8-10 hours on medium, less on high. That's roughly a week of nightly use (2-3 hours per evening) between charges. The magnetic mount is the quiet hero here — the light clicks firmly into place, and popping it off to charge is a one-handed operation. No fumbling with screws or clips.

One smart design choice: the touch sensor activates on the back of the unit. That means no accidental triggers when you brush past it, and it's genuinely convenient if mounted at shoulder height. But if you're mounting it tight against a headboard or in a corner, you'll be reaching around the fixture every time. Plan your placement carefully.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The warm light quality comes up repeatedly in reviews. One verified buyer wrote, *"I've bought three different battery sconces from Amazon and returned all of them — the light was too cold or too dim. These are finally warm enough that they don't look like a security light in my bedroom."* Another noted, *"The magnetic mount is genius. I charge them during the day and pop them back on at night — takes ten seconds."* The build quality also gets consistent shout-outs: multiple reviewers describe the aluminum housing as feeling "premium" and note the fixtures don't look battery-powered from across the room.

**Where it falls short**

The lack of dimmer memory is genuinely annoying. Every time you power the light off and back on, it jumps to full brightness. If you prefer a dim, moody setting, you'll be tapping the sensor to cycle down every single time. Minor, but it adds friction to daily use.

The touch sensor placement is a legitimate ergonomic issue for certain mounting positions. As one reviewer put it, *"If you mount these close to a wall corner, you'll be contorting your hand to hit the sensor."* Also worth noting: the motion sensor version (if you opt for that variant) has a shorter detection range than advertised — about 8-10 feet, not the 15 feet listed.

**Who should buy this**

- **Renters** who can't hardwire light fixtures and want something that looks intentional, not temporary
- **Anyone with a dark hallway or reading nook** that lacks an outlet — this solves that problem cleanly
- **People who hate cold, blue LED light** and want a warm ambient glow without replacing their entire lighting setup

**Skip it if** you need a light that stays on for 12+ hours daily, want smart-home integration (no Wi-Fi, no app, no Alexa here), or require a fixture that can be dimmed and stay dimmed.

**Three questions shoppers actually ask**

**Q: How long does the battery really last?**
On medium brightness, expect 8-10 hours. High brightness drops that to 5-6 hours; low stretches toward 12. Charge time is about 3 hours via USB-C.

**Q: Can these be hardwired?**
No — they're battery-only. The USB-C port is for charging, not permanent power. If you want hardwired sconces, look elsewhere.

**Q: Do they come with a remote?**
Standard version: no, touch sensor only. Some variants include a remote, but you'll pay extra and the remote is basic — on/off and brightness cycling, nothing fancy.

**The Verdict: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if** you want warm, cordless lighting that actually looks like a real light fixture — not a compromise. The build quality, magnetic mount, and 3000K LEDs justify the price.

**Skip it if** the no-dimmer-memory quirk will drive you insane, or you need smart-home integration. For everyone else, this is one of the better battery sconce options on Amazon right now — and the 220 ratings at 4.4 stars back that up.
