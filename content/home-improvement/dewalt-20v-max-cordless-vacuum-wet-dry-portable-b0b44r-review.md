If you're already holding a DEWALT 20V battery, this vacuum is a no-brainer addition to your kit. If you're not, it's still worth a hard look — but with one eye on battery life.

The DEWALT 20V MAX Cordless Wet/Dry Vacuum (model DCV580, the B0B44RLQ5S listing) is a compact, dual-purpose cleaner aimed at job sites, garages, and anyone tired of dragging a cord across a wet floor. It's rated 4.5 stars across 225 Amazon reviews, and after digging through what owners actually say, here's the real story.

## What's in the box

You get the vacuum body, a 4-foot flexible hose, a crevice nozzle, a wet/dry filter, and a shoulder strap. No battery or charger — this is a bare tool. If you don't own DEWALT 20V gear, factor $100–$150 for a battery and charger into your total. The unit itself weighs about 3.5 pounds without a battery, which makes it genuinely portable.

## Performance: The good, the wet, and the ugly

This thing earns its keep on wet pickup. Owners consistently report it handles spilled coolant, rain puddles in a truck bed, and even a flooded basement corner without choking. The wet/dry filter design means you don't swap parts between jobs — just vacuum the dust, then suck up the water. That's a real time-saver.

Dry suction is solid for a cordless unit, but it's not a shop-vac replacement. One reviewer noted, "It's great for sawdust and crumbs, but don't expect to clean a whole garage floor on one charge." That tracks. The motor pulls enough for quick cleanups, not marathon sessions.

**Battery life is the honest tradeoff.** On max speed with a 5Ah battery, you're looking at roughly 10–12 minutes of continuous use. That's fine for a car interior or a spilled bucket, but it's not a full-shop tool. Buyers with 6Ah or 9Ah batteries report more usable time, so if you have bigger packs, you'll be happier.

## Build quality and ergonomics

This is DEWALT, so the housing is tough ABS plastic that shrugs off drops. The latch mechanism for the canister is secure — no accidental pops mid-vacuum. The shoulder strap distributes weight well, and the whole thing fits in a 5-gallon bucket or behind a truck seat.

The 4-foot hose is the weak point. It's flexible and doesn't kink, but it's short. Reaching a ceiling corner or the far side of a car seat means moving the whole unit. A 6-foot hose would've made this near-perfect.

## What buyers consistently praise

The 4.5-star rating holds up across the board. Common themes in positive reviews:

- **Versatility**: "Use it in the house, the car, and the shop. It's the most-used tool in my garage."
- **Build quality**: "Feels like a real tool, not a toy. Dropped it off a ladder and it kept working."
- **Ecosystem value**: "Already had 5 batteries. This cost me less than a corded shop vac and takes less space."

## Where it falls short

Beyond battery life and hose length, there's the cleanup factor. After wet pickup, you need to rinse the canister and the filter. The filter is washable, but it takes time to dry before the next dry use. If you're switching between wet and dry jobs rapidly, that's a real friction point.

Also, the crevice nozzle is basic. It works, but it's not as narrow as some competitors' offerings, so tight spaces like between car seats can be a fight.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:**
- You're already in the DEWALT 20V ecosystem (this is a no-brainer addition)
- You need a portable vac for car interiors, RVs, boats, or job-site touch-ups
- You deal with both wet and dry messes and hate switching tools
- You have at least one 5Ah+ battery

**Skip it if:**
- You need a primary shop vacuum for heavy use — get a corded unit
- You're starting fresh with no DEWALT batteries (factor in the full cost)
- You need extended run time without stopping to swap batteries

## FAQ

**Q: How long does the battery last on a full charge?**
A: With a 5Ah battery, expect 10–12 minutes on max, up to 25–30 minutes on low. A 9Ah battery roughly doubles that.

**Q: Can it really pick up standing water?**
A: Yes, up to the filter line. It handles spills and shallow puddles well. Don't submerge the nozzle or pull water into the motor.

**Q: Does it work with all DEWALT 20V batteries?**
A: Yes, all DEWALT 20V MAX batteries fit and work, from the compact 1.5Ah to the 9Ah FlexVolt packs.
