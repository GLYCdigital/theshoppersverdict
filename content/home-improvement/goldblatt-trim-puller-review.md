## The Design: Simple and Effective

The Goldblatt Trim Puller is a cleverly shaped piece of stamped steel with a molded rubber grip. The business end has a thin, flat edge that slides behind trim, with a curved fulcrum that converts your pulling motion into controlled prying force.

There are two working edges:

## Performance in Real Use

The reviews paint a remarkably consistent picture. The tool works exactly as advertised. Here's what real users report:

**On painted and caulked trim:** Score the caulk line first with a utility knife. Then slide the Goldblatt behind the trim. One firm pull and the trim releases with minimal resistance. The caulk seal breaks cleanly rather than tearing.
