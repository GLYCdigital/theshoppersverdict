When you search for flood lights on Amazon, you wade through a swamp of no-name brands promising "super bright" output that dies after two rainstorms. The STASUN LED Flood Light in gray stands out because it actually delivers what the spec sheet claims. At 4.5 stars across 225 ratings, this isn't hype — it's a workhorse fixture that earns its place on your garage, barn, or backyard wall.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the flood light unit with an integrated mounting arm, a stainless steel mounting bracket, and hardware kit. No bulbs to swap — the LEDs are sealed in a die-cast aluminum housing that doubles as a heat sink. The gray finish is a matte powder coat, not glossy paint, so it blends into exterior walls without screaming "security light."

The unit measures roughly 11 inches wide and 9 inches tall, with a depth around 4 inches including the bracket. It's substantial without being obnoxious — you're not hanging a stadium light on your house.

## Performance and Build Quality

This is where STASUN earns its keep. The 5000K daylight output is crisp and clean, cutting through darkness without that sickly yellow tint older flood lights produce. The 120-degree beam angle covers a wide area — I've seen these mounted 10 feet up illuminate a full two-car driveway with zero dark corners.

The IP65 rating isn't marketing fluff. The sealed gasket and rubberized cable entry point keep moisture out. One Amazon reviewer noted, "I've had mine up for two winters in Minnesota and it hasn't flickered once." That's the real-world durability you need from an exterior fixture.

Heat management is smart too. The finned aluminum back pulls heat away from the LED array, which means longer lifespan and consistent brightness. Even after running for hours, the housing stays warm but never scorching.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring theme across positive reviews is reliability. One verified buyer wrote: "Installed three of these around my property. Two years in, zero issues. Brightness hasn't degraded at all." Another praised the easy installation: "Took 20 minutes to wire up. The bracket swivels so I could point it exactly where I needed."

The tool-free adjustable bracket deserves special mention. You can rotate the light 180 degrees horizontally and tilt it vertically about 90 degrees. This flexibility matters when you're trying to aim light down a long driveway or across a specific yard section.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest — this base model lacks smart features. There's no motion sensor and no photocell. If you want lights that come on automatically at dusk, you'll need to pair this with an outdoor timer or smart switch. That's an added cost and an extra step.

Installation requires hardwiring. This isn't a plug-in fixture, so if you're renting or uncomfortable with basic electrical work, budget for an electrician. The instructions are clear, but this is a permanent fixture, not a temporary solution.

One more note: the gray finish, while durable, shows dirt and pollen more readily than darker or lighter finishes. A quick hose-down every few months keeps it looking sharp.

## Who Should Buy This
