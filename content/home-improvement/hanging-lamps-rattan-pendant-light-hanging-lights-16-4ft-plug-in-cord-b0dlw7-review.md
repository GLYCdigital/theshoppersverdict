Let’s cut through the noise. If you’ve been scrolling through pendant lights for weeks, you already know the drill: hardwiring costs money, most cords are laughably short, and anything with “boho” in the name costs triple what it should. This rattan pendant from Hanging Lamps sidesteps most of that nonsense — but it’s not perfect.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the rattan shade, a ceiling canopy, and that headline feature: a 16.4-foot plug-in cord. No hardwiring, no junction box, no electrician. The shade itself is hand-woven natural rattan — expect slight variations in weave and color between units, which is part of the charm. As shown in the product image, it’s a classic dome silhouette that reads warm and organic rather than cheap.

The cord is cloth-covered, which matters more than you’d think. It drapes nicely instead of kinking like PVC, and it’s long enough to route along crown molding or behind furniture to reach an outlet. For renters, this is the entire selling point.

## Performance and Build Quality

Here’s where the 4.6 rating starts to make sense. The weave density is consistent — light filters through in a pleasant starburst pattern without harsh hotspots. You’ll want a warm-white LED (2700K) to get the full effect. A 60-watt equivalent E26 bulb is the max; the socket is standard, which is good.

The cord is genuinely 16.4 feet, not “about 15 feet” like some listings stretch the truth. The plug is a standard two-prong US plug. The ceiling canopy is plastic, not metal — a minor cost-cutting tell, but it’s hidden against the ceiling.

One caution: the rattan is natural fiber, not synthetic. It’s not rated for damp locations, so keep it away from bathrooms with steamy showers or open windows where rain could hit it. Direct sun will also fade the straw color over time.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

Scrolling through the 229 ratings, two themes dominate. First is installation simplicity. One reviewer wrote, “I hung this in my rental in 15 minutes and took it down just as fast when I moved. Landlord never knew.” Another noted, “The long cord saved me — my ceiling is 9 feet and I still had slack to spare.”

The second is the visual payoff. Multiple reviewers mention the light pattern on walls and ceilings — “the shadow play is gorgeous at night” and “makes my dining nook look like a $300 fixture.” That’s the honest appeal here: you’re paying for ambiance, not engineering.

## Where It Falls Short

The bulb isn’t included. That’s annoying at this price point, and it’s the most common complaint in the reviews. Budget an extra $6-10 for a good LED bulb.

Shipping damage is the second issue. Rattan is brittle, and a few reviews mention cracked or splintered shades on arrival. The packaging is adequate but not armored — inspect the shade the day it arrives and file a claim immediately if it’s broken. Amazon’s return policy covers it, but it’s a hassle.

Finally, the plastic canopy. It’s white and unobtrusive, but if you’re a perfectionist about hardware, you’ll notice it doesn’t match the organic vibe of the shade. Not a dealbreaker, just a tell.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Renters** who want statement lighting without drilling into a ceiling
- **DIY-averse buyers** who want a working light in under 20 minutes
- **Small-space dwellers** with high ceilings who need that long cord to drop the shade to table level

Skip it if you need a damp-rated fixture, want a metal shade for durability, or expect a premium hardware finish.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I cut the cord shorter?**
A: Yes, but you’ll void the simplicity. The cord can be shortened by an electrician, or you can coil the excess and tuck it behind furniture. Most users just route the slack — it’s cloth-covered, so it doesn’t look half-bad coiled.

**Q: What bulb size fits?**
A: Standard E26 base. Use a 60-watt equivalent LED max. A 2700K warm-white gives the best rattan glow; anything higher looks clinical.

**Q: Is it safe to use with a dimmer switch?**
A: Only if you install a smart bulb and control it via app. The cord is plug-in, so no wall dimmer will work without an inline dimmer adapter.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want the look of a designer rattan pendant without the electrician bill or the deposit risk. The 16.4-foot cord is genuinely useful, the shade delivers on ambiance, and installation is embarrassingly easy.

**Skip it if** you need a damp-rated fixture, want a sturdier synthetic shade, or can’t tolerate buying a bulb separately. It’s a light fixture, not a heirloom — but for the price, it earns its space in a rental or a cozy corner.
