If you’ve ever yanked a towel off a flimsy bar and watched the whole bracket pull out of drywall, you know the value of a properly built piece of bathroom hardware. The Delta Faucet 79724 Cassidy 24-inch Wall Mount Towel Bar is the antidote to that frustration — a straightforward, well-machined bar that does exactly what it should, with minimal fuss.

**What you actually get**  
Unboxing reveals the bar itself, two wall brackets, and the mounting screws. That’s it. No anchors, no template, no Allen wrench. Delta assumes you own a drill and a level, which is fair — but if you’re a first-time installer, budget for a separate drywall anchor kit. The bar is solid zinc die-cast, not the hollow aluminum tubing you’ll find at half the price. At 24 inches, it clears standard 20-22 inch bath towels with room to spare. The brushed nickel finish (there’s also chrome and oil-rubbed bronze) has a matte, low-glare look that hides water spots better than polished finishes.

**Real-world performance**  
Installation is the make-or-break moment for any wall-mounted bar. The concealed mounting screws are a thoughtful touch — once the brackets are up, the bar slides over them and locks into place, so you don’t see exposed fasteners. That said, the brackets have a bit of play before you fully seat the bar. Take your time to align them perfectly; a slight tilt becomes obvious with a long bar. Once mounted, the bar feels rock-solid. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *“I’ve installed a lot of towel bars and this is the first one that didn’t feel like it would rip out of the wall if my kid hung off it.”* Another noted, *“The finish is flawless — no rough edges, no cheap plating. Matches my Delta faucet perfectly.”*

**Where it falls short**  
Honest gripes are few but worth noting. First, the lack of included anchors is annoying at this price point — a $3 pack of toggle bolts would have saved a trip to the hardware store. Second, the bar is fixed. No swivel, no tilt. If you want to angle towels toward a heat vent or keep them off a shower splash zone, this isn’t the bar for you. Finally, the Cassidy line’s design is clean but unremarkable — it’s a classic rounded profile, not a statement piece. If you’re after a modern, angular look, look elsewhere.

**Who should buy this**  
- **The practical renovator**: You want a dependable bar that matches Delta’s other Cassidy fixtures (faucets, robe hooks, toilet paper holders) for a cohesive bathroom set.  
- **The high-traffic household**: Kids, guests, and heavy wet towels won’t compromise this bar’s integrity.  
- **The finish-matching perfectionist**: Delta’s brushed nickel is consistent across their lines, so pairing this with a Delta faucet is seamless.

Skip it if you’re on a tight budget and don’t care about longevity — there are serviceable $15 bars that’ll last a few years. Or if you need a pivoting bar for a tight space.

**FAQ**  
**Q: Does this bar fit standard 16-inch or 24-inch stud spacing?**  
A: The 24-inch bar’s mounting brackets are designed to align with standard 16-inch or 24-inch stud centers. But you should still verify your wall layout — if your studs are off-center, you’ll need anchors.

**Q: Is the brushed nickel finish prone to tarnishing?**  
A: No. Delta uses a physical vapor deposition (PVD) finish on this line, which is significantly more durable than electroplating. It’s resistant to corrosion, scratches, and everyday cleaning chemicals.

**Q: Can I mount this on tile?**  
A: Yes, but you’ll need a carbide-tipped drill bit and proper masonry anchors. The concealed screw design actually helps here — once mounted, you won’t see any gaps between the bracket and tile.

**The Verdict**  
**Buy it if** you want a sturdy, well-finished towel bar that will outlast your renter’s insurance policy. It’s a buy-once, cry-once piece of hardware.  
**Skip it if** you’re looking for a bargain, need a pivoting design, or expect included mounting hardware.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Loses a half-star for the missing anchors and the fixed design, but earns full marks for build quality and finish. This is the towel bar you install and forget about — in the best way.
