## What You're Actually Getting

The box contains the seat, lid, hinges, and mounting hardware — no tools needed for installation, which is refreshing. The 346 Affinity is a molded plastic seat in a standard elongated shape. The key features are the slow-close hinge mechanism and the quick-release tabs that let you lift the entire seat off the base for cleaning.

The plastic is high-gloss white, not matte, which means it wipes clean easily but will show water spots if you're not diligent. The hinge mechanism is the real selling point here — it uses a dampened cam system rather than a cheap spring, which is why it doesn't degrade into a slammer after six months like budget seats do.

## Real-World Performance

I've tested dozens of toilet seats over the years, and the slow-close on this MAYFAIR is genuinely good. Push it down hard and it still catches itself at the last 30 degrees, easing shut without the jarring clack that wakes up the whole house at 2 AM. The mechanism is quiet — not silent, but a soft pneumatic hiss rather than a bang.

The quick-release feature is where this seat earns its keep. Two tabs on the hinge posts release the seat body so you can carry it to the tub for a proper scrub. That's not a gimmick — it's the difference between a seat you actually clean and one you just spray and wipe around. The tabs are stiff at first but loosen up after a few uses.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest limitation is shape. This only comes in elongated — if you have a round bowl, this seat simply won't fit, no matter what the Amazon Q&A says. Measure your bowl before ordering.

Several long-term reviewers noted the hinge posts can loosen from the bowl over months of use. One wrote, "After 8 months the whole seat shifted about a quarter inch. Had to tighten the bolts underneath." It's a 30-second fix with a screwdriver, but it's annoying.

The plastic construction also means it feels lighter and less substantial than a wood-core seat. It won't crack — the material is thick and rigid — but it doesn't have that premium heft.

## FAQ

**Will this fit a round toilet?**
No. This is elongated-only. Measure the distance from the mounting bolts to the front of your bowl — if it's about 18.5 inches, you're elongated. If it's closer to 16.5, this won't work.

**Can I remove the seat without tools?**
Yes. The quick-release tabs on the hinge posts let you lift the seat off the base in seconds. The base stays bolted to the bowl. To fully remove the base, you'll need a screwdriver or wrench.

**Does the slow-close ever wear out?**
The cam mechanism is rated for tens of thousands of cycles, but the plastic bushings can eventually wear. Most buyers report the mechanism still working after 1-2 years of daily use. If it does fail, the entire hinge assembly is replaceable.
