## FAQ

**Q: Will this work with LED replacement tubes?**
A: Only if the LED tubes are specifically labeled "ballast-compatible" or "type A." Many LED tubes require you to bypass the ballast entirely, so check your tube's specs before ordering. If you're using direct-wire LED tubes, this ballast isn't needed.

**Q: How do I know if my ballast is actually the problem?**
A: If the lamps are new and still flicker or stay dark, the ballast is the likely culprit. Use a multimeter to check for 120V at the ballast input. No voltage means a wiring or breaker issue; voltage present but no output means the ballast is dead.

**Q: Does it matter if I have a 2-lamp fixture but only use 1 lamp?**
A: No — this ballast is rated for both 1 and 2 lamps. Just ensure you connect the unused lamp leads with wire nuts, and it will operate fine on a single tube.
