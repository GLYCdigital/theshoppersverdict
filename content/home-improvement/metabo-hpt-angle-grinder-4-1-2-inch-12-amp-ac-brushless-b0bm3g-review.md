# Metabo HPT Angle Grinder Review: Is the 12-Amp Brushless Worth It?

Most angle grinders under $100 are disposable. You burn through them on a big project, toss them in a drawer, and buy another when the brushes die. The Metabo HPT 4-1/2-inch with its 12-amp brushless motor is trying to break that cycle — and for the most part, it succeeds.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the grinder itself, a side handle that mounts on either side, a 4-1/2-inch guard, a 7/8-inch arbor wrench, and a locking flange nut. No grinding wheel included — budget for one if you don't have spares. The tool measures about 11 inches long with a body diameter that fits medium-to-large hands comfortably.

## Performance and Build Quality

The headline here is the brushless motor. Metabo HPT's 12-amp brushless design delivers consistent power through sustained use — something brushed motors can't claim. On a 30-foot run of 1/4-inch steel flat bar, this grinder held speed without the telltale whine-and-fade of a brushed tool overheating. That's the difference you're paying for.

The AC brushless motor also runs cooler and cleaner. The tool's body stays manageable even after 20 minutes of continuous cutting, which matters when you're working in tight spaces and need to grip the barrel.

The spindle lock is solid and engages cleanly, and the tool-free guard adjustment uses a lever that's stiff enough to stay put but easy enough to rotate one-handed. The 5,000 to 11,000 RPM range covers everything from heavy grinding to fine polishing work.

## What Buyers Praise

The 4.3-star rating across 215 reviews reflects consistent satisfaction. One reviewer noted, "I've owned Makita and DeWalt grinders and this one feels smoother and runs cooler under load." Another called the weight "a huge selling point — I can work overhead without my arm cramping."

The durability gets repeated mentions. Multiple buyers report using it professionally on job sites for months without issue. The rubberized grip zones — visible in the product image — get specific credit for staying grippy even with oily hands.

## Where It Falls Short

The slide switch is the biggest compromise. It locks on, which is convenient for long cuts but means you can't quickly kill power if something goes wrong. If you're used to paddle-switch grinders, this will feel like a downgrade in safety.

Vibration is present at high RPM. It's not the worst I've felt, but after 45 minutes of continuous grinding, your hands will know you've been working. Quality gloves are non-negotiable.

The dust guard is decent but not sealed. If you're cutting concrete or masonry regularly, you'll want a vacuum attachment. And while the 12-amp motor is strong, it's not a 15-amp beast — this is a professional-grade light-to-medium duty tool, not a demolition grinder.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Fabricators and metalworkers** doing daily cutting and grinding — the brushless motor pays for itself in longevity
- **HVAC and plumbing pros** who need a lighter grinder for overhead and confined-space work
- **Serious DIYers** who are tired of replacing $40 grinders every year

Skip it if you need a paddle switch for safety compliance or you're doing heavy demolition work that demands a 15-amp motor.

## FAQs

**Is this grinder actually brushless?**
Yes. The AC brushless motor is the main selling point. It eliminates brush replacement and runs cooler under sustained load.

**What wheel size does it use?**
Standard 4-1/2-inch wheels with a 7/8-inch arbor. It's also compatible with 5-inch wheels if you remove the guard, but 4-1/2 is the intended size.

**Does it come with a grinding wheel?**
No. It ships bare — you'll need to buy wheels separately.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want brushless reliability and lighter weight without paying premium brand prices. It's a workhorse that holds up professionally.

**Skip it if** you require a paddle switch or need a sealed tool for heavy masonry work.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The brushless motor and weight savings make this a strong value. The slide switch and vibration keep it from being perfect.
