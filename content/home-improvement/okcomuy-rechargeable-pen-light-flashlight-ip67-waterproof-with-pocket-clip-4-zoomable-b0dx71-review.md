The package is minimal — the pen light itself, a USB-C charging cable, and a user manual. No wall adapter, but you likely have a dozen USB-C bricks already. The light measures about 6 inches long and weighs 2.1 ounces, so it disappears in a shirt pocket. **Performance and build quality**

The headline feature is the 4-stage zoom. Twist the head to cycle through spot, medium, wide, and flood settings. The spot beam is genuinely tight — useful for reading circuit board traces or inspecting a cylinder wall. The flood mode covers a workbench-sized area without harsh hotspots. Brightness hits around 200 lumens on high, which is modest compared to full-size flashlights, but appropriate here — this is a close-quarters tool, not a searchlight.

The IP67 waterproofing is the standout spec. I've tested cheaper "waterproof" lights that fog up after a splash. This one has a sealed body with a rubber O-ring at the lens and a threaded tail cap. One Amazon reviewer noted, "Dropped it in a bucket of coolant at work, fished it out, kept working." That's the kind of real-world validation that matters.

The dominant theme in positive reviews is reliability for trade work. Electricians and HVAC techs specifically mention using it daily for months without issues. It's replaceable, but you shouldn't have to mod a new light. The zoom ring also requires a deliberate quarter-turn to lock. If you're adjusting it one-handed while holding a probe, it's a minor annoyance. And at 200 lumens max, this is not a replacement for a full-size work light — it's a supplement.

**Who should buy this**

This is built for tradespeople — electricians, mechanics, plumbers, HVAC techs — who need a dependable, pocketable light for inspections and tight spaces. **FAQ**

*Is the zoom actually useful, or a gimmick?*
It's functional. The four detents are distinct — you'll use spot for detail work and flood for general illumination. The lock ring prevents accidental shifts.

*How does it handle extreme cold or heat?*
The aluminum body dissipates heat well. In freezing conditions, the battery drains faster (typical for lithium cells), but the light itself works. One reviewer used it in a -10°F freezer walk-in without issues.

*Can you replace the battery?*
No, it's a sealed unit. The built-in cell is rated for 500 charge cycles — roughly 2-3 years of daily use. When it eventually dies, you're replacing the whole light.
