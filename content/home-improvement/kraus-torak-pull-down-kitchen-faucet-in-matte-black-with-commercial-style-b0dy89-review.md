The Kraus Torak looks like it was ripped out of a commercial kitchen and refined for your countertop. That spring coil, the industrial silhouette, the matte black finish — it's a statement piece. But statement pieces need to perform, and with 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, the Torak has receipts. Here's what I found digging through the specs, the customer feedback, and the real-world tradeoffs.

## What's in the box

Kraus ships the Torak with the faucet body, a 20-inch stainless steel braided supply lines, a deck plate (handy if you're covering a three-hole setup), mounting hardware, and a hex wrench for the set screw. The pull-down spray head is attached to a 68-inch nylon hose — enough reach to fill a stockpot sitting on the counter corner. Weights are included to keep the hose retracting smoothly.

## Build quality and real-world performance

This is a full metal faucet. No plastic base, no cheap chrome accents under the sink. The body is solid brass, and the matte black finish is applied via a physical vapor deposition process — that's the same method used on high-end fixtures, and it shows. One reviewer put it bluntly: *"I've had it for 8 months and it still looks like day one. My old faucet had peeling finish by now."*

The commercial-style spring coil isn't just for looks. It protects the hose from kinking at the pivot point, which is where most pull-down faucets fail. The dual-function spray head toggles between a steady stream and a pressurized spray with a simple push. The spray mode is genuinely aggressive — it blasts stuck-on food off plates without needing to soak everything first.

Flow rate is 1.8 gallons per minute. That's standard for modern faucets, but the Torak feels stronger because the nozzle design focuses the water. You won't be waiting around for pots to fill.

The matte black finish does have one quirk: it's a fingerprint magnet in theory, but in practice, the texture hides smudges better than glossy black. What it doesn't hide is dust. That spring coil has a lot of surface area, and it collects lint and kitchen dust like a filter. Plan on wiping it down weekly.

## What buyers consistently praise

The most repeated praise in the reviews centers on two things: the finish and the spray power. Beyond that, customers call out the smooth retraction mechanism. One buyer wrote: *"The hose glides back into place every time. No tugging, no wrestling. My last faucet required two hands to get the head back."* Another noted the installation was straightforward if you have basic plumbing skills — about 45 minutes including the water shutoff.

## Where it falls short

The spring coil's dust collection is the main aesthetic gripe. It's not a functional failure, but it's a maintenance reality you should know before buying.

Installation is also not for absolute beginners. The faucet requires a minimum 1-3/8-inch mounting hole, and the underside nut is tight to reach. If you have a shallow sink base cabinet, you'll be working blind. Several reviewers mention needing a basin wrench — don't skip it. Also, the spray head is slightly heavier than average due to the metal construction. It's not a problem, but if you have arthritis or grip issues, consider that weight.

One more thing: the matte black finish is durable, but it's not indestructible. Abrasive cleaners will dull it. Stick to soap and water or a non-abrasive cleaner.

## Who should buy this

- **The home cook who wants pro-kitchen aesthetics** — this faucet makes your sink the focal point of the kitchen.
- **Anyone replacing a faucet every 3 years** — the brass body and quality finish are built to last a decade or more.
- **Buyers who actually use the spray mode** — this isn't a decorative feature; it's genuinely useful for produce, dishes, and cleaning the sink itself.

Skip it if you have a super shallow cabinet, if you're set on a low-maintenance finish (go stainless steel instead), or if you're on a strict budget — this is a mid-range investment, not a bargain bin pick.

## FAQ

**Q: Will the matte black finish scratch or chip easily?**
A: No. The electroplated finish is far more durable than painted black faucets. It resists scratches from normal use. Just avoid abrasive cleaning pads.

**Q: Does the spray head stay in place while in use?**
A: Yes. The pull-down mechanism locks securely, and the hose retracts fully when you're done. The included counterweight keeps tension consistent.

**Q: Can I install this over an existing three-hole sink?**
A: Yes, the included deck plate covers the extra holes. Just make sure the center hole is at least 1-3/8 inches.
