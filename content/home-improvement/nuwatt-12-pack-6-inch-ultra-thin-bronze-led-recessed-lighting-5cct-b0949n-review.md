If you're rewiring a basement ceiling or swapping out old can lights, the NUWATT 6-inch ultra-thin LED kit gets one thing immediately right: it removes the single biggest headache in recessed lighting retrofits. There's no bulky can housing to wrestle into a joist bay, no insulation clearance dance, no fighting with existing framing. These drop into a cutout and lock in with spring clips. At 4.8 stars across 240 ratings, buyers clearly appreciate that simplicity. But let's get into specifics before you drop money on a 12-pack.

**What's actually in the box**

You get 12 complete fixtures, each with an integrated LED panel, a separate driver junction box, and a 6-inch round bronze trim ring. The driver box has a 12-inch input cable plus an 18-inch output lead for daisy-chaining. The whole fixture is rated for damp locations, which matters if any of these are going in a bathroom or covered porch. The housing measures roughly 7.5 inches in diameter and sits just 0.5 inches thick — that's the number that makes this work in shallow ceiling cavities.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The integrated panel pushes 900 lumens at 3000K, which is roughly equivalent to a 60-watt incandescent. In a standard 10x10 room, four of these spaced about 6 feet apart will give you solid general lighting without dark corners. The dimming range is respectable — down to about 10 percent — and the fixtures work with most leading-edge and trailing-edge dimmers, though NUWATT recommends their own or a compatible ELV dimmer for best results.

The color temperature switch is the standout feature. Each fixture has a small slide switch on the back of the driver box that lets you choose between 2700K, 3000K, 3500K, 4000K, and 5000K before you mount it. That's genuinely useful for matching existing fixtures in adjacent rooms or deciding between warm and cool tones without buying different SKUs. One Amazon reviewer noted, "I set half my basement to 4000K and the other half to 3000K for different zones — took two minutes per light."

Heat management is handled by the aluminum heat sink backing, and the driver is separated from the LED panel, which helps longevity. At 50,000 hours rated life, you're looking at roughly 17 years at 8 hours per day. The tradeoff: the LED is integrated, so when it dies, you replace the entire fixture. That's standard for this product category, but worth knowing.

**Where this kit falls short**

The biggest practical gap is wiring. The fixtures have push-in terminals for line-in and line-out, but NUWATT doesn't include the small jumper cables you need to connect multiple lights in a series. You'll either buy their separate connector kit or use your own 14/2 Romex and wire nuts. Several reviewers flagged this as an annoyance, especially when they'd already cut all their holes and were ready to install.

The bronze trim is painted, not anodized or plated. It looks good out of the box — a warm, matte bronze that pairs well with oil-rubbed bronze switch plates — but it's not armor. If you drag a screwdriver across it during install, you'll see a scratch. Handle with care.

Also, these are IC-rated but not airtight. In an unconditioned attic space, you'll want to seal around the fixture with fire-rated caulk to prevent air leakage. That's extra work some buyers didn't expect.

**Who should buy this**

This kit is ideal for three types of people: DIYers doing a whole-room or whole-floor retrofit where 12 lights is a realistic count; contractors who need consistent output across multiple job sites and want one SKU that covers five color temperatures; and anyone with shallow ceiling plenums — think mobile homes or dropped ceilings — where standard cans won't fit.

Skip it if you only need two or three lights, if you're dead set on a gimbal or adjustable trim (these are fixed, downward-only), or if you're working in a space with high humidity like a steam shower (damp-rated, not wet-rated).

**Frequently asked questions**

*Can these be installed in new construction without existing cans?*
Yes. Trace the 6.25-inch cutout template, cut the hole, run your wiring to the driver box, connect, push the fixture up, and tighten the spring clips. No framing or can housing needed.

*Do they work with smart dimmers?*
Yes, with caveats. They work with most leading-edge/trailing-edge dimmers, including Lutron and Leviton models. For smart dimmers, you may need an ELV-rated model for flicker-free performance. Test with your specific dimmer before finishing the ceiling.

*Are the color temperatures adjustable after installation?*
No. The switch is on the driver box, which sits in the ceiling cavity. Once the fixture is mounted and the ceiling is closed up, you can't change it. Set your color temps before you secure everything.

**The Verdict**

The NUWATT 12-pack solves the biggest pain point in recessed lighting — installation complexity — with a genuinely well-built, slim fixture that delivers consistent, clean light. The five-color tuning is a real differentiator, and the daisy-chain capability keeps wiring clean if you plan ahead with connectors. It's not perfect: the missing jumper wires are an annoying oversight, and the painted trim requires careful handling.

**Buy it if** you're doing a multi-room retrofit, want the flexibility of color temperature selection, or need lights that fit tight ceiling spaces.

**Skip it if** you need adjustable gimbal fixtures, want a physical metal trim, or only need a handful of lights and don't want to buy a 12-pack.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A strong value for whole-home installations, docked slightly for missing wiring components and the scratch-prone finish.
