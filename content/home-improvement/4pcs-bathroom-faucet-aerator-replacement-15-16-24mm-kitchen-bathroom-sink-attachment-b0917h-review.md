**What you actually get**

**The real-world fit test**

Here's the honest truth about aerator sizing: it's a guessing game until you measure. The 15/16" male thread fits most Delta, Moen, and Kohler bathroom faucets. The 24mm version is your friend if you've got a Glacier Bay or a cheap builder-grade fixture. The good news? This kit gives you both, so you've got a 50/50 shot on your first try. The bad news? If your faucet uses a 55/64" thread (common on some Pfister and American Standard models), none of these will fit.

Installing is genuinely tool-free — hand-tighten until snug, then a quarter-turn more with a rag for grip. **FAQ**

**Q: How do I know if I need 15/16" or 24mm?**
Remove your old aerator and measure the outer diameter. 15/16" is about 23.8mm — so close that you need a precise ruler. Better: check the faucet brand online. Delta/Moen/Kohler use 15/16"; Glacier Bay and some imports use 24mm.

**Q: Will these work on kitchen faucets too?**
Yes, if the thread size matches. Many kitchen faucets use the same 15/16" standard, though some use larger 13/16" or specialty sizes. This kit is bathroom-focused but works on compatible kitchen models.

**Q: Do they reduce water flow significantly?**
They include a standard flow restrictor (around 1.5 GPM). If your current aerator has no restrictor, expect a noticeable but not drastic reduction — enough to cut splashing without feeling like a trickle.
