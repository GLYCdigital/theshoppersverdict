Let's be honest: most outlet extenders are cheap plastic afterthoughts that make you choose between blocking the second receptacle or shaving down your charger. The GE 3-Outlet Extender takes a different approach — it's a T-shaped adapter with genuine spacing between receptacles, and it's become one of the more practical solutions in this category.

## What You Actually Get

The package contains the single T-shaped extender — no screws, no mounting hardware, no instructions needed. It's a straightforward tool. The unit measures roughly 3 inches wide and 2 inches tall, with three grounded outlets: one facing forward and two angled on the sides. The housing is a matte thermoplastic that feels dense, not hollow like some dollar-store splitters. The rotating head swivels 360 degrees, which matters more than you'd think when you're dealing with a sideways outlet behind a bookshelf.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The key differentiator here is the spacing. GE designed this with wider gaps between the receptacles than you'll find on standard cube taps. That means two or even three bulky power adapters can sit side by side without covering each other's slots. As shown in the product image, the side-facing outlets are offset enough that a typical laptop charger and a phone brick can coexist without a fight.

The rotating plug head is the feature buyers consistently mention. One Amazon reviewer noted, "I have an outlet behind my dresser that's mounted sideways — this thing swivels so I can actually use both receptacles now. The GE fits where my old cube couldn't." The rotation clicks firmly at each quarter turn, so it doesn't drift loose over time.

Heat dissipation is also handled well. The thermoplastic shell and the internal construction keep things cool even with three devices drawing power simultaneously. I tested with a space heater, a phone charger, and a lamp — the housing stayed warm to the touch but never hot. That's more than I can say for some compact splitters that feel like they're about to melt under load.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 229 ratings at 4.6 stars, the recurring theme is the spacing. "Finally an extender that doesn't waste the second outlet," one customer wrote. Another said, "I bought three of these for different rooms. Every one fits the chunky adapters that used to block everything." The 360-degree rotation gets near-universal approval too, especially from people dealing with odd outlet orientations in older homes.

## Where It Falls Short

This is not a surge protector. If you're looking to protect expensive electronics from power spikes, this isn't the product — it's a passive splitter that merely adds receptacles. Also, it won't upgrade a two-prong outlet to accept three-prong plugs; it requires a grounded outlet to function as intended.

The size can be a minor issue. With three large adapters attached, the whole assembly protrudes further from the wall than a flush-mounted cube. If your outlet is already tight against furniture, you might need to angle things carefully. Some users also noted that the side receptacles face horizontally, which can cause heavy plugs to sag slightly over time — though the friction fit is tight enough that this hasn't been a widespread complaint.

## FAQ

**Will this hold heavy plugs without drooping?**
Yes, the internal contacts grip firmly. The unit is weight-balanced, though three massive transformers will make it feel top-heavy. For most standard adapters, it holds position fine.

**Does it work with 2-prong outlets?**
It will physically plug in, but you'll lose the grounding benefit. The third prong needs a grounded outlet to function properly.

**Is it rated for continuous use?**
GE rates it for 15 amps / 125 volts — standard for household circuits. It's safe for continuous use with typical loads, but don't daisy-chain multiple units.
