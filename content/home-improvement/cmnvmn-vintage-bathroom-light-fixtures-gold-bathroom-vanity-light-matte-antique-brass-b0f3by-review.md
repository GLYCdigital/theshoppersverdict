If you've been scrolling past a dozen identical "gold" vanity lights that all look like spray-painted plastic, stop. The CmnVmn Vintage Bathroom Light Fixture is the one that actually delivers on the antique brass promise.

The photos don't exaggerate. This is a matte antique brass finish — think aged bronze with a warm gold undertone, not the bright, flashy polished gold you see on cheaper fixtures. As shown in the product image, the backplate and arms have a subtle, brushed texture that catches light softly. It reads as "intentional design choice," not "cheap Amazon special."

**What's Actually in the Box**

You get the assembled fixture (three-light version tested here), three clear glass shades, a mounting plate, wire nuts, screws, and a small allen wrench for adjusting the arm angles. The mounting hardware accommodates standard 4-inch octagonal junction boxes, which covers most US homes. One genuinely thoughtful touch: the instructions include a paper template for marking screw holes — a detail most fixtures at this price skip entirely.

**Build Quality and Installation**

The housing is heavy. Real steel, not stamped sheet metal. The matte finish is baked on, not painted, so it won't flake or show brush marks up close. The glass shades are thick-walled with a slight amber tint that warms the light output.

Installation took me 22 minutes solo. The wires are pre-stripped, the ground wire is clearly labeled, and the mounting plate has a built-in level bubble. That last feature sounds minor until you've fought a crooked fixture for ten minutes. The arms pivot via the allen screw, so you can adjust the angle to match your mirror height.

One honest warning: this is a hardwired fixture. If you're not comfortable with basic electrical work (black to black, white to white, ground to ground), budget for an electrician. It's simple, but it's still live wire.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews holds up. The recurring themes are finish quality and visual impact. One verified buyer put it well: *"I was skeptical about the color — I wanted brass, not gold. This is exactly the aged brass look, and it completely changed the bathroom."* Another noted, *"I've bought three fixtures from different brands trying to get this aesthetic. Multiple reviewers also mention the light quality — the open-bottom shades throw light both up and down, eliminating harsh shadows on the face. That's a practical benefit for a vanity light that isn't obvious from the listing photos.

**Where It Falls Short**

The bulbs-not-included reality is the first hurdle. Because the design exposes the bulbs, a standard LED spiral will look terrible. You'll want Edison-style filament bulbs, which run $4–8 each. Factor that into your budget.

The second issue is versatility. This mounts to a wall or ceiling, but the arms are fixed-length. If your vanity is wider than 36 inches, the three-light version will look undersized. There's a four-light option, but it's often out of stock.

Finally, one reviewer reported a chipped glass shade on arrival: *"The box was fine, but one shade had a small crack at the rim. Amazon replaced it within two days, so not a dealbreaker, but inspect your shipment."*

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Farmhouse and transitional style homes** — the matte brass pairs beautifully with white shaker cabinets and marble counters.
- **Anyone tired of polished gold** — if you want warmth without shine, this is the finish.
- **DIYers doing a full bathroom remodel** — the included template and level bubble make it one of the friendliest hardwired fixtures I've installed.

**Skip It If**

- You want a polished, mirror-like gold finish — this is deliberately matte.
- Your vanity is over 36 inches wide — you'll need the four-light version or two fixtures.
- You need a fixture with integral LED or dimming built-in — you'll need a separate dimmer switch.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: What bulbs does this fixture require?**
A: Standard E26 medium-base bulbs, up to 60W equivalent. For the best look, use vintage Edison filament LED bulbs — they're dimmable and give that warm amber glow.

**Q: Can this be installed on a sloped ceiling?**
A: Yes. The mounting plate is designed to sit flush on flat surfaces, and the arms are adjustable, but for a steep slope you may need an angled adapter (not included).

**Q: Is the finish really brass or just painted?**
A: It's a baked-on matte antique brass coating over steel. It's not solid brass (that would triple the price), but it's durable and won't chip under normal use.
