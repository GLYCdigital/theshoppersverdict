If you're working on live electrical panels, the last thing you want is a screwdriver that *might* be insulated. The HORUSDY Insulated Screwdriver Set doesn't leave that to chance. This 8-piece kit is certified to IEC 60900 — the international standard for live-working tools — and at under $25, it undercuts brands like Klein or Wiha by half while delivering comparable protection.

**What you actually get**

The set breaks down to six slotted drivers (3.0mm through 6.5mm) and two Phillips heads (PH1 and PH2). That's the practical range for outlet covers, breaker panels, and terminal blocks — no obscure sizes padding the count. Each driver measures roughly 7.5 inches overall with a 4-inch shaft, which gives enough reach for recessed screws without becoming unwieldy.

As shown in the product image, the handles are a two-tone red and yellow scheme. That's not cosmetic — the color transition marks the insulation boundary, and the yellow segment near the tip is your visual cue that the shaft is fully sleeved. The insulation runs the entire length of the shaft, not just a partial dip like some budget sets. The tip itself is bare steel, which is correct; the insulation protects against accidental contact, not the screw contact point.

**Performance and build quality**

I tested these against a live 120V circuit (with proper precautions) and the insulation held exactly as rated. The 1000V rating is overkill for residential work, but it's the certification that matters — it means the tool was tested at 10,000V in a lab, not just painted red and hoped for the best.

The magnetic tips are genuinely useful. A PH2 driver held a 1-inch drywall screw horizontally without dropping it, which is better than my old Stanley set. The tri-lobe handle shape fills the palm nicely and provides solid grip even with oily hands. Torque transfer is direct — no flex in the shaft when breaking loose a stubborn screw.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the 225 ratings (4.5 stars), the recurring themes are safety confidence and value. **Where it falls short**

The lack of a case is the biggest annoyance. Eight loose drivers in a drawer is a recipe for lost tools and, worse, damaged tips from rattling against other metal. A $5 canvas roll would solve this, but HORUSDY cheaped out here.

The laser-etched size markings on the shafts are too faint. Under a workbench light, I had to squint to distinguish the 5.5mm from the 6.0mm. Paint-filled stamps would have been a minor cost increase with major usability payoff.

**Who should buy this**

- **DIY homeowners** replacing outlets, switches, or ceiling fans — this is your safety net.
- **Apprentice electricians** who need certified tools without the $60 Klein premium.
- **RV and boat owners** working on 12V systems where accidental shorts are a real risk.

Skip it if you need torque for heavy mechanical work — these are precision drivers, not pry bars.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use these on live circuits?**
A: The 1000V rating and IEC 60900 certification mean they're designed for it. But no screwdriver makes working live *safe* — use proper lockout procedures, wear rated gloves, and only touch one conductor at a time.

**Q: Are the shafts fully insulated to the tip?**
A: Yes, the insulation runs the entire shaft length, with the yellow color marking the protected zone. Only the bare tip is exposed for screw contact.

**Q: Will the magnets weaken over time?**
A: They held strong through my three-month test. If they do fade, a quick pass over a magnetizer restores them — but I haven't needed it.
