## The 3-in-1 G9 Bulb That Actually Works

Most "switchable color" LED bulbs are gimmicks. This G9 is not. It's a genuinely useful upgrade for anyone stuck with halogen bi-pin fixtures — under-cabinet lighting, vanity mirrors, pendant lamps, or those tiny track heads that refuse to accept standard bulbs.

**What's in the box:** You get the bulb itself — that's it. One 3W G9 LED with a bi-pin base, rated for 350 lumens. No adapters, no remote, no manual worth reading. The color-switching is done by toggling your wall switch off and on within 2 seconds, cycling through 2700K (warm white), 4000K (neutral), and 6000K (daylight). It's simple and, once you get the rhythm, second nature.

## Build and Performance

The bulb is physically small — about the size of a large grape — which is exactly what you need for shallow fixtures. The ceramic base feels solid, and the frosted diffuser does a good job of eliminating harsh point-source glare. At 3W, it runs cool enough to touch after hours of operation, a welcome change from the finger-burning halogen G9s it replaces.

**Light quality is where this shines.** The 350-lumen output is honest — it's noticeably brighter than most 2W G9 LEDs I've tested, and the color rendering is above average for a budget bulb. Skin tones look natural at 2700K, and the 6000K setting is crisp without going clinical blue. Dimming works smoothly down to about 10% on a modern trailing-edge dimmer; on older leading-edge dimmers, you may notice a faint buzz at low levels.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limits. **350 lumens is not bright.** If you're replacing a 60W halogen G9 in a task-light application — say, reading lamps or kitchen under-cabinet work areas — you'll notice the drop. This is a mood/ambient light, not a workhorse.

The color-switch mechanism is also a double-edged sword. Every time you turn the light off and back on, it remembers the last setting — but if you want to change colors, you're doing the rapid-toggle dance. Some buyers find this annoying if they're used to a smart-home integration where everything is app-controlled. This is a dumb bulb; it does not connect to Wi-Fi, Zigbee, or anything else.

**Dimming compatibility is hit-or-miss.** While most modern dimmers work fine, there's a compatibility list for a reason. If you have a very old dimmer, expect buzzing or a higher minimum brightness.

## FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Does this work with any dimmer?**
A: No. It works with most trailing-edge LED dimmers and many leading-edge models, but older or cheap dimmers may cause buzzing or a minimum brightness around 20-30%. Test with your existing dimmer before buying multiples.

**Q: How do I switch between 2700K, 4000K, and 6000K?**
A: Turn the wall switch off, then on within 2 seconds. It cycles through the three temperatures in order. The bulb remembers the last setting when you power cycle normally.

**Q: Is 3W bright enough for a bathroom vanity?**
A: For a single vanity fixture, it's adequate but not bright. You'll want two of these per fixture or a higher-wattage bulb if you're doing close-up tasks like makeup application.
