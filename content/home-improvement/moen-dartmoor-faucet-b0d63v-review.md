**The Moen Dartmoor isn't the flashiest kitchen faucet on the market. It won't win design awards, and it doesn't have touchless sensors or voice controls. What it does — reliably, day after day — is deliver solid performance at a fair price, and that's exactly why 225 Amazon shoppers have pushed it to a 4.5-star average.**

## What you actually get

The box contains the faucet body, pull-down spray head, 36-inch nylon hose, mounting hardware, and a detailed installation guide. There's no soap dispenser or side sprayer — this is a single-handle, pull-down design that keeps the deck clean. The unit measures 15.2 inches from base to spout tip, with a 7.8-inch reach that clears standard 10-inch-deep sinks without splashing the backsplash.

The construction is where Moen didn't cut corners. The body is solid brass with a chrome or brushed nickel finish (both available). As one verified buyer put it: *"You can feel the weight when you pick it up — this isn't hollow metal. The handle glides smoothly with zero wobble."*

## Real-world performance

The pull-down spray head delivers two functions: a steady stream and a focused spray for rinsing. The toggle switch on the head is tactile and positive — no guessing whether you've actually switched modes. The 36-inch hose gives you full sink coverage, and the head retracts smoothly when you release it.

Duralock — Moen's quick-connect system — makes installation genuinely straightforward. If you're replacing an existing faucet, you're looking at about 45 minutes of work with basic tools. The supply lines are pre-attached and use standard 3/8-inch compression fittings, so no soldering required.

The lever handle is positioned high enough to clear tall pots, and the 360-degree swivel spout gives you full range without hitting the backsplash. Water pressure stays consistent even with the spray mode engaged — a complaint you'll see with cheaper pull-downs.

## What buyers consistently praise

The spot-resistant finish is the standout feature in customer feedback. One reviewer noted: *"I've had chrome faucets my whole life and hated wiping them down. This brushed nickel barely shows anything — I wipe it once a week and it looks new."* Multiple buyers also cited the quiet operation; the aerator doesn't produce that high-pitched whine you get from budget faucets.

The warranty deserves mention too. Moen backs the Dartmoor with a **limited lifetime warranty** — if it leaks, drips, or fails structurally, they'll replace it. That's not marketing fluff; Moen's customer service actually answers the phone and ships replacement parts without a fight.

## Where it falls short

No product is perfect, and the Dartmoor has three honest limitations.

First, the spray head uses a friction-fit docking system rather than a magnetic one. Over time — think two to three years — the head can sag slightly when docked. It won't fall out, but it won't sit perfectly flush either. The spray function still works fine; it's purely cosmetic.

Second, the hose has a "memory" when new. It wants to curl in the direction it was coiled in the box. Expect two weeks of mild annoyance before it hangs straight.

Third, the 1-3/8-inch mounting hole requirement is larger than some older countertops have. If your current faucet mounts through a 1-1/4-inch hole, you'll need a drill adapter or a deck plate to bridge the gap.

## Who should buy this

**Buy the Moen Dartmoor if:**
- You want a reliable, no-nonsense kitchen faucet without paying for smart features you won't use
- You're replacing a leaky faucet and want something that installs in under an hour
- You hate cleaning water spots off chrome — the brushed nickel finish is a genuine upgrade

**Skip it if:**
- You need a magnetic docking system — look at Moen's Align or Adler series instead
- You have a narrow countertop hole that can't be widened easily
- You want touchless operation or a built-in soap dispenser

## FAQ

**Q: Does this faucet fit a standard 3-hole sink?**
A: The Dartmoor is designed for single-hole mounting. If you have a 3-hole setup, you'll need a deck plate (sold separately) to cover the extra holes. Measure your sink's hole spacing before ordering.

**Q: Is the spray head easy to remove for cleaning?**
A: Yes. The spray head unscrews from the hose with a quarter-turn. The aerator is also removable with a standard key — soak it in vinegar monthly to prevent mineral buildup.

**Q: How does this compare to the Moen Arbor?**
A: The Arbor is roughly $50-80 more expensive and adds a magnetic docking system and a slightly taller profile. The Dartmoor is the better value if you don't need the magnetic hold. Both share the same Duralock installation and lifetime warranty.
