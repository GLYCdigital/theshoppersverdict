You've seen a hundred farmhouse lamp sets on Amazon. Most of them are hollow resin with faux-wood paint that chips by month three. The QiMH set of two is not that. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, this one earned its score through actual construction choices — not just a trendy silhouette.

## What's Actually in the Box

Two complete lamps, each with a wooden base, metal neck, and linen fabric shade. You get the shades pre-attached to the harp, so assembly is just screwing the base onto the neck — takes about 30 seconds per lamp. No tools required. Each lamp stands roughly 23 inches tall with a base diameter around 6 inches, which puts it in the sweet spot for end tables, nightstands, or a console behind the sofa.

The bases are solid wood — real grain, not printed veneer. The neck is a brushed metal that reads more antique bronze than shiny brass. It's a cohesive look that works in both farmhouse and transitional spaces.

## Build Quality and How It Handles Daily Use

The three-way switch is the standout feature here. It clicks through off → low → medium → high with a firm, satisfying action. On low, it's genuinely dim — good for movie nights. On high, it throws enough light to read by. The socket is ceramic, not the cheap plastic you find on budget lamps, and the cord is 6 feet with a clean inline switch.

The linen shades measure about 11 inches across the bottom and 9 inches tall. They diffuse light well — no harsh shadows, no visible bulb silhouette even with a standard A19. One buyer noted, "The light is warm and soft, exactly what I wanted for our bedroom. The shades don't yellow over time like some fabric ones do."

Another reviewer who bought two sets said, "I was skeptical about the wood base holding up, but after six months of daily use and two moves, these still look brand new. The finish hasn't scratched."

## Where It Falls Short

The shades are more of an oatmeal/cream tone than pure white. If you're matching them against white trim or bright white walls, they'll look slightly off. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you order.

Linen fabric is a dust magnet. You'll need to wipe the shades down every couple of weeks, especially if you have pets or live in a dry climate. It's a minor maintenance thing, but it's real.

And there's this: bulbs aren't included. For a set at this price, that's a small annoyance — you're spending another $10–15 on two 3-way bulbs just to use them.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy these if** you want a lamp that looks custom-built rather than mass-produced, and you're willing to pay a slight premium over the $30–40 generic sets. The three-way dimming makes them genuinely useful in a bedroom or living room where you need different light levels throughout the day.

**Skip these if** you need pure white shades to match specific decor, or if you're on a tight budget and don't care about material quality — cheaper options exist, but they won't hold up the same way.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these be used with smart bulbs?**
A: Yes, but you'll lose the three-way switch function. The switch is mechanical, so it works with any standard bulb, but smart bulbs need constant power. You can still use them — just set the switch to high and control brightness through your app.

**Q: How heavy are they? Will they tip over easily?**
A: Each lamp weighs about 4.5 pounds, and the base is weighted. They're stable on standard end tables. One reviewer mentioned their cat knocked one over twice without breaking it — the shade flexed rather than cracked.

**Q: Do the shades come in other colors?**
A: As of this review, only the cream/linen shade is available. The wood tone is consistent across all units, but you're locked into the one fabric color.
