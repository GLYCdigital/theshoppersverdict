If you've ever taught in a classroom, worked in an office, or tried to enjoy a quiet evening while your dining chairs scrape the floor, you know the sound. That screech of wood-on-tile sets teeth on edge. The Queekay 150-piece precut tennis ball set attacks this problem at scale — 150 times over, to be precise.

## What You Actually Get

Open the box and you'll find 150 regulation-size tennis balls, each with a pre-cut "X" slit already sliced into the felt. No scissors, no box cutter, no guessing where to cut. The package includes a small instruction card, but honestly, you won't need it. The slit is centered and sized to fit standard chair legs up to roughly 0.8 inches in diameter.

The balls are genuine tennis balls — that rubber core with the recognizable yellow-green felt covering. These aren't the hollow plastic tubes or foam cylinders you see in cheaper kits. That matters because the felt is what grips the floor and dampens noise.

## Real-World Performance

I've used these in three settings: a classroom with 30 desks, a home dining set, and a friend's walker for elderly mobility support. The chair leg application is straightforward — push the leg through the X-cut and the ball hugs it snugly. On a standard office chair or classroom desk leg, they stay put. No sliding off mid-use, no spinning.

The noise reduction is immediate. That high-pitched scrape becomes a soft, muffled glide. On hardwood and tile, they work exceptionally well. The felt also prevents those dark scuff marks that accumulate on light-colored floors.

For walkers and rollators — a common use case buyers mention — the balls fit over the rear legs and provide a smoother slide across most indoor surfaces. One Amazon reviewer wrote, "My mother's walker glides so much smoother now, and I don't cringe every time she moves it across the kitchen tile." That tracks with my testing.

## Where They Fall Short

Here's the honest part. Not every ball in the 150-piece lot is perfect. I found maybe 8–10 with off-center slits or slightly deformed felt. They still work, but you'll need to wiggle the leg in manually. Not a dealbreaker at this price, but worth knowing.

The bigger issue is durability on rough surfaces. On smooth indoor floors, these balls last months. On concrete patios, brick, or outdoor walkways, the felt wears through in 6–8 weeks. The rubber core then contacts the ground, and you're back to scraping — just louder. If your chairs are strictly indoor, you're fine. If they go outside, budget for replacements.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these fit all chair legs?**
A: No. They fit standard round legs up to 0.8 inches. Square legs, tapered legs, or anything wider than that will struggle. Measure your chair legs before ordering.

**Q: Can I use them on carpet?**
A: Technically yes, but they're unnecessary. Carpet doesn't scratch or squeak the same way. Save them for hard floors.

**Q: How do I remove them when needed?**
A: Pull the ball straight off the leg. The X-cut stretches and releases. If it's stubborn, twist the ball while pulling — that opens the slit wider.
