If you've ever stood in a bathroom staring at a builder-grade boob light with a cracked plastic globe and a bulb that's somehow both too dim and too harsh, you already know why a fixture like the Lepro 8.7-inch flush mount makes sense. This is a no-nonsense, sealed LED unit designed for one job: putting even, glare-free light over your vanity or shower without requiring an electrician. At 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, it's clearly doing that job well for most buyers.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the fixture body, a mounting bracket, wire nuts, and screws. The unit itself is a round, frosted acrylic diffuser measuring 8.7 inches in diameter with a depth of just 1.2 inches — it hugs the ceiling like a hockey puck. Inside is an integrated 20W LED board rated at 1600 lumens, which is roughly equivalent to a 100W incandescent. The aluminum heat sink backplate is a nice touch; it keeps the driver cool and extends the lifespan beyond the cheap plastic-bodied alternatives in this price bracket.

Installation is straightforward if you're comfortable with basic wiring. The bracket mounts to a standard 4-inch junction box with two screws, then you connect three wires (line, neutral, ground) with the included wire nuts. The fixture twists into place with a locking ring. Most homeowners should finish in under 30 minutes.

## Real-World Performance

The key differentiator here is the IP44 water resistance rating. That means the sealed diffuser and gasketed base are protected against splashing water from any direction. I've seen plenty of "damp rated" fixtures that are really just open-backed housings with a glass shade — this one is genuinely sealed. The acrylic lens is frosted enough to eliminate LED hot spots while still transmitting light efficiently.

The color temperature switching is where this fixture gets interesting. Instead of a physical toggle on the unit, you cycle through 3000K (warm), 4000K (neutral), and 5000K (daylight) by flipping your wall switch off and on within three seconds. It's a clever hack that avoids crawling into the attic to adjust a dip switch. But it has a quirk: every time you cut power for more than three seconds, it defaults back to 3000K. If you're a daylight-only person, you'll be doing the off-on dance every single time you enter the room.

## Where It Falls Short

The integrated LED is non-replaceable. When the diodes degrade — typically after 25,000-30,000 hours, or about 8-10 years of normal use — you're replacing the entire fixture, not just a bulb. That's an environmental and cost tradeoff you accept with any modern LED unit.

Also, the 1600 lumens might be overkill for a small powder room. At 3000K, it's warm and welcoming, but at 5000K in a tiny 5x5 space, it can feel like an operating room. Consider the square footage before committing.

## Who Should Buy This

This is for the practical homeowner renovating a bathroom or laundry room who wants a clean, modern look without paying for a designer fixture. It's also ideal for rental property owners — the neutral white diffuser works with any decor, and the IP44 rating means fewer callbacks about moisture damage. Skip it if you're a lighting purist who wants a dimmable fixture or color-tunable smart control; this is a fixed-output, non-dimmable unit.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually waterproof, or just damp-rated?**
A: It's IP44-rated, which covers splashing water — safe for directly above a shower or tub. It is not IP67, so don't submerge it or mount it inside a steam room.

**Q: Can I use it with a dimmer switch?**
A: No. This is a fixed-output fixture. Using a dimmer can cause flickering or damage the driver.

**Q: How do I change the color temperature?**
A: Turn the wall switch off, wait two seconds, turn it back on. Repeat to cycle through 3000K → 4000K → 5000K. It remembers your choice only as long as power stays on.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a bright, sealed, low-profile ceiling light for a bathroom, laundry room, or hallway and don't want to pay electrician rates for installation. **Skip it if** you require dimming, a replaceable bulb, or a consistent daylight color temperature without the switch-cycling ritual.

At 4.2/5, this is a solid, dependable fixture that outperforms its price point in build quality and light output. Just be prepared for the fact that when it dies, it dies — and you'll be buying another one in about a decade.
