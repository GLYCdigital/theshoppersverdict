The QLXTeltd Black Wagon Wheel Chandelier is the kind of statement piece that makes you rework an entire room's decor around it. If you're shopping for a farmhouse or rustic-industrial fixture that doesn't scream "builder grade," this one earns its place in your cart — but only if your ceiling height cooperates.

## What's in the box

You get the wagon wheel frame (roughly 23 inches in diameter), six glass shades, mounting hardware, wire connectors, and a 12-inch downrod. As shown in the product image, the black matte finish is uniform and well-applied with no drips or thin spots. The frame itself is welded steel, not cheap cast aluminum — pick it up and you can feel the weight difference.

One thing to know upfront: this is not a five-minute install. The arms attach to the central hub, each shade has its own collar, and the wiring requires patience. Set aside an hour if you're handy, two if you're not.

## Real-world performance

The six arms radiate evenly, and with candelabra bulbs rated at 60 watts equivalent each, you're looking at solid coverage for a 12x14 dining room or a two-story foyer. The glass shades diffuse light nicely — no harsh hotspots, no glare when you're sitting beneath it.

The matte black finish is the quiet winner here. It doesn't show the dust that plagues oil-rubbed bronze, and it won't develop that greenish patina over time. A quick wipe with a dry microfiber cloth and it looks new again.

## What buyers consistently praise

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews is backed by genuine satisfaction. Multiple reviewers specifically call out the visual impact:

> "Everyone who walks in asks where we got it. It's the centerpiece of our entryway."

> "Installation was straightforward and the quality is way better than I expected for the price."

The enclosed glass shades get repeated mentions — no bugs collecting inside, no constant dusting of individual bulbs.

## Where it falls short

The 12-inch downrod is the biggest gotcha. On a standard 9-foot ceiling, that puts the fixture roughly at eye level for tall family members. You'll likely need to order a longer downrod separately — factor that into your budget.

Assembly is genuinely tedious. The arms thread into the hub, and getting all six aligned while holding the frame is fiddly work. If you're not comfortable with basic electrical wiring, budget for a licensed electrician. This isn't a plug-and-play fixture.

Also note: no bulbs included. Six candelabra-base LEDs will run you another $15–$25 depending on quality.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** you have a vaulted ceiling, stairwell, or dining room with 10+ feet of clearance; you're committed to a farmhouse or rustic aesthetic; you're comfortable with basic wiring or willing to hire someone who is.

**Skip it if:** you have standard 8–9 foot ceilings and don't want to buy extra downrods; you're looking for a quick, tool-free install; you prefer modern or minimalist fixtures.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this be installed on a sloped or vaulted ceiling?**
Yes, but you'll need an angled ceiling adapter (sold separately). The included mounting plate is flat-only.

**Q: What bulb type does it take?**
Six E12 candelabra-base bulbs. LED recommended — they run cooler and you won't be changing bulbs on a ladder as often.

**Q: Is the finish durable?**
Yes. The matte black is baked-on powder coat. Reviewers report no chipping or fading after months of use.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The QLXTeltd wagon wheel chandelier delivers serious visual punch for the money. It's not a beginner install, and the downrod situation needs planning, but for anyone with the ceiling height, it's a legitimately impressive centerpiece that outperforms its price point.

**Buy it if** you have the vertical space and want a farmhouse showpiece without paying boutique lighting prices. **Skip it if** you're on standard ceilings or want something you can install in 20 minutes flat.
