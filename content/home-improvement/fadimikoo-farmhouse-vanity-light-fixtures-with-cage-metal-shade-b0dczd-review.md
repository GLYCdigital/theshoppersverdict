Let's be straight about this FadimiKoo farmhouse vanity light: it's not trying to be a statement piece. It's trying to be the *right* light for your bathroom or hallway — and based on 235 ratings averaging 4.7 stars, it's succeeding. Here's what you actually need to know before you order.

**What's in the box**

You get the main mounting bar with three (or four, depending on the configuration you choose) adjustable light arms, the cage shades with clear seeded glass, mounting hardware, and wire nuts. The matte black finish looks consistent across all metal parts — no mismatched tones. The individual heads rotate a full 360 degrees and tilt at the joint, which matters more than you'd think for vanity lighting. As shown in the product image, it's designed to mount horizontally above a mirror, but people have used it vertically on either side of a medicine cabinet too, since the arms adjust.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The metal is thick enough to feel solid — not flimsy like some budget fixtures that bend when you tighten the screws. The matte black finish is baked on, not painted, so it holds up to bathroom humidity and cleans easily with a damp cloth. The seeded glass cages diffuse light nicely, which means you get even illumination without that harsh squint-inducing glare you get from bare bulbs.

One thing worth noting: this is a hardwired fixture. There's no plug-in option, so you need existing wiring and a junction box. If you're comfortable with basic electrical work, installation takes about 30-45 minutes. Several buyers who installed it themselves mentioned the included mounting screws felt a bit short for thicker drywall — grab longer screws from the hardware store before you start to save yourself a trip.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The adjustable heads are the standout feature. One reviewer put it well: "I angled one light toward my face and the others to light the whole room — perfect for makeup." Another noted, "The quality surprised me for the price. The glass is thick, the black finish looks expensive, and it's been up for six months without any issues."

**Where it falls short**

Bulbs aren't included. You need A19 bulbs, max 60W, and many buyers are pairing this with vintage Edison bulbs to complete the farmhouse look — but that's an extra $20-30 you should budget for. The fixture is also designed for 120V hardwiring only, so if you're renting or don't have a junction box in place, this isn't your light. A few reviewers mentioned the glass shades arrive well-packed but are fragile, so handle them carefully during installation.

**Who should buy this**

- **The farmhouse-style renovator**: If you're doing a Joanna Gaines-inspired bathroom, this is period-correct without being kitschy.
- **The makeup or grooming enthusiast**: The adjustable heads give you task lighting exactly where you need it — this is a huge advantage over fixed fixtures.
- **The practicality-first shopper**: It's durable, easy to clean, and the finish will look good for years.

**Skip it if** you want plug-in convenience, have no existing wiring, or prefer a single-piece fixture without articulated joints (some people find the adjustability visually busy).

**FAQ**

*Q: Can this be installed vertically beside a mirror?*
A: Yes. The rotating heads and adjustable joints allow for vertical mounting on either side of a mirror. Just ensure your junction box is positioned correctly.

*Q: What kind of bulbs does it take?*
A: Standard E26 base A19 bulbs, max 60W. LED bulbs work fine and are recommended for energy efficiency and cooler operation.

*Q: Is this dimmable?*
A: The fixture itself is dimmable, but you'll need compatible dimmable bulbs and a dimmer switch already installed.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a solid, adjustable farmhouse vanity light that won't fall apart in a year and gives you real control over your lighting direction. The 4.7-star average is earned — this is a well-made fixture at a fair price.

**Skip it if** you're not prepared for hardwiring or you want a fixture that includes bulbs.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Docked half a star for the bulb-not-included requirement and the slightly short mounting screws. Everything else delivers.
