**The LEONLITE COB Security Light: Three Heads, Zero Excuses**

Most flood lights are one-trick ponies. You get a single beam, a cheap plastic housing, and a motion sensor that either misses your driveway or triggers every time a cat sneezes three houses down. The LEONLITE COB LED takes a different approach — three independently adjustable heads, a wide 240° detection sweep, and an aluminum body that won't turn brittle after one summer of UV exposure. At 3000 lumens total (roughly 1000 per head), it's genuinely bright enough to light a two-car driveway or a backyard patio without leaving dark corners.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the assembled three-head fixture, a mounting bracket, hardware, and a wire connector set. The unit measures about 11 inches wide and 6 inches tall — substantial, but not the comically oversized barn-light look. Each COB head pivots vertically and horizontally, so you can point one down the walkway, another across the yard, and a third at the garage door. The integrated PIR sensor sits centered between the middle and left heads, with sensitivity and timer dials (10 seconds to 5 minutes) tucked underneath. It's 120V hardwired — no plug-in option here, so plan for a junction box and basic electrical work.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The die-cast aluminum housing is the standout feature at this price point. It dissipates heat effectively, which extends LED lifespan, and the IP65 rating means it shrugs off rain and snow. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"Two years outside in Texas heat and thunderstorms, and it still looks new. The aluminum doesn't yellow like the plastic ones."* That durability matters — cheap flood lights often fail within 18 months due to moisture ingress or overheating.

The motion sensor is genuinely capable. The 240° detection angle picks up movement from the sides, not just directly in front, and the adjustable range (5 to 30 feet) lets you tune out street traffic. Set it to minimum near a busy sidewalk, max it out for a long driveway. The 3000K-5000K color temperature is a neutral white — bright enough for security, not so blue it feels like a hospital corridor.

**Where It Falls Short**

The biggest limitation: **no photocell mode.** This light is motion-only. If you want it on all night, you'll need to either leave the wall switch on permanently (which defeats security purposes) or add a separate dusk-to-dawn sensor. Several buyers flagged this, with one noting: *"I wanted it to stay on for evening entertaining, but it keeps shutting off after the timer. Wish I'd bought the version with a photocell."*

The mounting hardware also drew complaints. The included screws are smaller than standard junction box screws, and the bracket holes don't always align perfectly with existing boxes. Plan on 20-30 minutes of extra fiddling if you're replacing an older fixture with non-standard spacing. And while 3000 lumens is solid, it's not blinding — if you need to light a large commercial yard, you'll want two units.

**Who Should Buy This**

**Buy it if:** You need dependable, weatherproof lighting for a standard residential driveway, backyard, or entryway — and you want adjustable heads to aim light precisely without buying multiple fixtures. It's ideal for homeowners who can handle basic wiring and want something that will outlast the cheap plastic alternatives.

**Skip it if:** You require dusk-to-dawn continuous operation, or you're covering a large commercial area needing 5000+ lumens. Also skip if you're not comfortable with hardwired installation — there's no plug-and-play version.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this light dimmable?**
No. It's full-on or off via motion detection. No dimmer switch compatibility.

**Q: How long do the LEDs last?**
LEONLITE rates them at 50,000 hours — roughly 17 years at 8 hours nightly use. The aluminum housing helps achieve that by keeping temperatures low.

**Q: Can I use it in freezing temperatures?**
Yes. The operating range is -4°F to 104°F. Cold weather actually helps LED efficiency.

**The Verdict**

At 4.2/5, the LEONLITE COB security light earns its rating through solid construction, genuinely useful adjustability, and a motion sensor that works as advertised. The missing photocell function keeps it from a perfect score, but for motion-activated security lighting, this is a reliable, well-built pick that won't leave you replacing it in two years.

**Buy it if** you want durable, adjustable, motion-activated lighting without paying premium brand prices.
**Skip it if** you need continuous overnight illumination or can't handle a basic hardwire install.
