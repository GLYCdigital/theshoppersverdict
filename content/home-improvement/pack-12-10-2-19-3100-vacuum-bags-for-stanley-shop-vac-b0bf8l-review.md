If you own a Stanley Shop Vac in the 5-6 gallon range, you already know the struggle: the OEM 19-3100 bags cost roughly $10 each, and they fill up faster than you'd like when you're doing drywall work. This 12-pack from a third-party manufacturer attacks that problem directly — you're getting a dozen bags for what you'd normally pay for two or three name-brand ones. The question is whether the cost savings come with a tradeoff in fit or durability.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get 12 bags total: 10 standard bags plus 2 bonus bags, which explains the "10+2" in the product title. Each bag measures about 15 inches long with a 5.5-inch diameter, which matches the Stanley 19-3100 spec. The collar is a rigid cardboard ring, similar to what you'd find on the OEM version. The bag material is a multi-layer felt — noticeably thicker than the paper-thin bags that come with some budget wet/dry vacs.

## Performance and Real-World Use

I tested these on a Stanley SL18116 (5-gallon model) and a Stanley SL18117 (6-gallon model). The collar seats properly on both without any adapter. The felt material handles fine drywall dust without any blow-through, which is the most common failure point on cheap bags. One user noted, "I used one for a full basement drywall sanding project and it didn't tear or leak dust into the filter." That tracks with my experience — the seams are heat-sealed and reinforced, not just glued.

Where these bags fall short: there's no rubber gasket around the collar. If your vac's lid isn't clamped down tight, you'll get fine dust escaping around the top edge. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing — make sure those two side clamps are fully engaged before you start.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 229 ratings (4.6 average), the recurring theme is value without sacrificing function. ## Where It Falls Short

The main complaints are minor but real. The bonus bags are individually wrapped, which makes it easy to forget you have them — not a big deal, but slightly annoying. The fit is designed for Stanley's 5-6 gallon line, and it will *not* work on 8-gallon or larger models. If you're between sizes, measure your current bag before ordering. A couple of reviewers also noted the collar can bend if you overstuff the bag past the fill line, which causes the bag to pull away from the vacuum port.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these fit all Stanley 5-6 gallon models?**
A: They fit the vast majority, but not all. Check that your model uses the 19-3100 bag style. Stanley has used a couple of different collar designs over the years.

**Q: Can I use these for wet pickup?**
A: No. These are dry-only bags. For wet pickup, remove the bag entirely and use the filter without it.

**Q: How full can I fill them before changing?**
A: Stop at about 3/4 full. Overfilling risks the collar separating from the bag, which defeats the purpose.
