If you're staring at a cracked, discolored outlet and thinking "I should just replace it," the ELEGRP Decorator Receptacle is the kind of product that makes that job take 15 minutes instead of an hour. At $11.99 for a single unit, it's priced at the higher end of the basic range — but you're paying for something that actually feels solid in the hand.

## What you actually get

One standard 15A 125V duplex receptacle, rated 2-pole 3-wire grounding. The package includes the outlet itself and a matching decorator wall plate — that's worth noting because some competitors make you buy the plate separately. The body is a compact 2.64 inches deep, which gives you decent room in a standard junction box without cramping the wires. The break-off tabs for split-circuit wiring are clearly indicated and score cleanly, so you won't need a utility knife to separate them.

## The real question: how does it install?

Here's where this outlet separates itself from the $2 specials. The side terminals accept both 14 AWG and 12 AWG solid copper wire, and they grip tight — no push-in backstab connections that loosen over time. I've replaced enough outlets to know that backstabbed connections are responsible for a surprising number of "my outlet stopped working" calls. The ELEGRP's screw-down terminals are the right call for longevity.

The tamper-resistant shutters deserve a mention. Many TR outlets feel like you're fighting the mechanism every time you plug something in. These are smooth — one reviewer noted, "I was worried the shutters would be annoying, but they open easily with a standard plug and I don't feel any resistance." That matches my experience. The shutter only opens when both prongs insert simultaneously, which is exactly the code requirement.

## What buyers consistently praise

Across the 229 ratings, the recurring theme is reliability and fit. One homeowner wrote: "Replaced all the outlets in my 1970s house with these. The side screws grabbed the old wiring without issue, and the faceplate sits flush against the wall — no gaps." Another contractor-type reviewer flagged the build quality: "The housing feels denser than the cheap Leviton or Eaton units I've used. No flex when you insert a heavy plug."

The 4.6 average rating is earned mostly on consistency — no reports of units arriving cracked, missing screws, or with misaligned shutters, which is more than I can say for other budget brands.

## Where it falls short

The biggest annoyance is the non-captive screws. On the terminal screws, you need to back them out fully to get the wire under the plate, and if you drop one into the bottom of a crowded electrical box, you're fishing for it with a magnet. It's a minor inconvenience, but it's the kind of thing you notice mid-job.

Also, this is a strictly basic outlet. No USB-C ports, no night light, no smart home integration. If you're already opening up the wall, you might want to consider a combo unit instead — this is purely a replacement, not an upgrade. The single-pack price of $11.99 is also steep compared to multi-packs from bigger brands; you're paying a premium for the quality, but it adds up if you're swapping out the whole house.

## Who should buy this

- **DIY homeowners** replacing worn or outdated outlets — the installation is straightforward and the instructions are clear.
- **Landlords or property managers** needing code-compliant TR outlets without the premium price of commercial-grade units.
- **Anyone with aluminum wiring** — the side terminals work well with the copper pigtails you'll need to use.

Skip it if you need USB ports, want smart features, or are outfitting an entire new build where bulk pricing matters more than per-unit quality.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this outlet support split-circuit wiring for a switched top and always-on bottom?**
Yes. The break-off tabs on both sides are scored and easily removed with pliers. Just be sure to break off only the tab for the hot side if you're wiring a half-hot outlet.

**Q: Is this compatible with standard decorator wall plates, or do I have to use the included one?**
It uses the standard decorator/GFCI-size opening, so any brand's decorator plate will fit. The included plate is fine quality, but you can swap it without issue.

**Q: Can I use this with 12 AWG wire, or is it only for 14 AWG?**
Both. The side terminals accept 14 AWG and 12 AWG solid copper. For stranded wire, you'll want to use the back-wire option, but the clamp is designed to hold it firmly.
