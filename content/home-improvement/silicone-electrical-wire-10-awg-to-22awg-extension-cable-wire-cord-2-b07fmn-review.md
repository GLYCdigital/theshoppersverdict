## Where It Falls Short

Nothing's perfect, and this wire has two honest drawbacks.

**Soft jacket = less abrasion resistance.** Silicone is inherently softer than PVC or cross-linked polyethylene. If you're pulling wire through metal conduit or sharp-edged cable glands, the jacket can tear. Use grommets or split loom tubing in high-abrasion areas. One reviewer mentioned: *"The jacket nicked when I pulled it through a sharp hole in a steel enclosure — my fault for not deburring, but PVC would've survived."*

**No printed gauge markings.** Unlike many hookup wires that print "14 AWG" every six inches, this silicone wire has zero markings. If you buy multiple gauges, label the spools immediately. I learned this the hard way when I grabbed 18 AWG thinking it was 16 AWG for a 5-amp circuit.
