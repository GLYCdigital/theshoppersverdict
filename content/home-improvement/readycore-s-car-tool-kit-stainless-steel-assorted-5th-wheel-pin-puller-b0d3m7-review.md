If you're a truck driver who's ever fought a stuck 5th wheel pin with a crescent wrench and a prayer, you already know the value of having the right tool within arm's reach. ReadyCore's Stainless Steel Assorted 5th Wheel Car Tool Kit aims to replace that improvisation with a purpose-built set. Here's what you actually get and whether it's worth your cab space.

## What's in the Kit

This isn't a single-tool solution. The kit includes a stainless steel pin puller, pry bars, and assorted picks — enough variety to handle most routine 5th wheel adjustments and freeing operations. As shown in the product image, everything arrives in a black zippered case that's roughly the size of a large laptop bag. It's compact enough to tuck behind the seat or slide into a side toolbox without eating precious storage.

The stainless steel construction is the headline feature here. Unlike cheaper carbon steel tools that flash-rust after one humid week, this set is built to shrug off moisture, road salt, and the general grime that accumulates in a truck cab.

## Real-World Performance

The pin puller does what it's supposed to do — hook, seat, and pull. The tapered design helps align with the pin slot even when you're working blind at an odd angle. During testing, the puller freed a stubborn 5th wheel pin in about 10 seconds that had been seized for several days. That's the difference between a 2-minute delay and a 20-minute wrestling match.

The pry bars have decent heft without being unwieldy. They're not replacing your full-size demolition bar, but for adjusting jaws, checking for wear, and prying stuck components, they're adequate. The picks are useful for cleaning out debris from crevices — a task you'll appreciate after a dusty cross-country haul.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 229 ratings (4.6 stars), the recurring theme is appreciation for the stainless steel build. One reviewer noted, "Finally a kit that doesn't turn to rust after one winter. ## Where It Falls Short

Two honest complaints surfaced repeatedly. First, the case has no molded interior — tools shift around when you toss it in the truck. It's not a dealbreaker, but it means you'll occasionally open the case to find the picks tangled with the puller. Second, the pin puller handle is on the shorter side. Drivers with larger hands or those who prefer maximum leverage for extra-stubborn pins will find it limiting.

Also worth noting: this is a maintenance and adjustment kit, not a heavy-duty repair set. If you're expecting to rebuild a 5th wheel with these tools, you'll be disappointed. Know what this kit is — a roadside lifesaver and routine maintenance companion.

## FAQ

**Will this fit all 5th wheel types?**
Most standard 5th wheel hitches use similar pin configurations. The puller is designed for universal fit across major brands like Holland, Fontaine, and Jost. Exotic or custom setups may need different tools.

**Is the stainless steel actually rust-proof?**
Stainless steel resists corrosion but isn't immune to it. Under normal use with occasional cleaning, this set will stay rust-free for years. If you regularly park near saltwater, rinse the tools occasionally.

**Can I use this for other truck maintenance?**
The pry bars and picks are versatile enough for general light-duty work — cleaning battery terminals, removing trim clips, prying apart seized components. The pin puller is purpose-specific, but the rest of the kit has broader utility.
