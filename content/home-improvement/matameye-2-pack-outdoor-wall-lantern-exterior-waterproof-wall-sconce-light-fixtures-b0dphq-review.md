**What’s actually in the box**

You get two complete lantern fixtures, each with a clear glass shade, a die-cast aluminum body, and a pre-wired E26 socket. The mounting plate is attached, which saves you a step. There are no bulbs included — that’s the first gotcha. You’ll need to budget for two standard A19 or Edison-style bulbs. The fixtures are sized at roughly 10 inches tall and 6.5 inches wide, which makes them substantial enough for a front porch or garage side door without overwhelming a standard 8-foot wall.

**Real-world build and performance**

The housing is genuinely heavy for the price point. The die-cast aluminum has a powder-coated matte black finish that feels durable, not like painted plastic. As shown in the product image, the glass is framed with a foam gasket and the door seals with a positive latch. I’ve seen similar fixtures fail within months due to condensation; the MATAMEYE’s design addresses that head-on.

Light output depends entirely on the bulb you choose. With a standard 60-watt equivalent LED, you get clean, even illumination that casts a nice wash down the wall. The open-bottom design means minimal glare — it’s a lantern, not a floodlight, and it knows it.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The recurring theme in customer feedback is the value-per-unit math. One verified buyer noted, *“I priced out two matching bronze lanterns at the big-box store and it was nearly triple this cost. **Where it falls short**

The included mounting hardware is also on the basic side — if you’re mounting to brick or stucco, plan on picking up proper masonry anchors. Finally, the lack of bulbs is a minor annoyance, and some buyers reported that standard A19 bulbs sit slightly proud of the shade. A shorter globe-style bulb fits flush and looks better.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for homeowners updating front entryways, garage side doors, or porch areas where you want a cohesive look without paying boutique pricing. Skip it if you need a specific finish like oil-rubbed bronze or brass; the MATAMEYE comes in black only.

**FAQ**

**Do I need special bulbs for outdoor use?**
Yes. Use LED bulbs rated for damp or wet locations. Standard indoor LEDs can fail prematurely in the sealed, heated environment of an enclosed lantern.

**Can these be installed on stucco or brick?**
Yes, but you’ll need to swap the included screws for masonry anchors or toggle bolts. The mounting plate has standard keyhole slots that work with most anchors.

**Are these truly waterproof?**
The housing is IP65-rated, meaning it's dust-tight and protected against water jets. Rain, snow, and direct hose spray won’t get inside. Just ensure the door is fully latched after bulb changes.
