### Frequently Asked Questions

**Are the cutting edges replaceable?**  
No. The pliers are a single-piece forged construction. When the cutting edges dull, you replace the tool. At this price point, that is an acceptable trade-off.

**Can these pliers cut hardened wire or small nails?**  
The diagonal and linesman pliers can cut soft to medium wire, screws, and small nails. Do not attempt to cut hardened steel, spring steel, or piano wire — this will damage the cutting edges.

**How do you prevent rust on these tools?**  
Keep them in a dry environment. The manufacturer applies slushing oil for rust protection during shipping. Wipe them clean before first use, and apply a light oil if you store them in a humid environment.

**Do the grips slip off over time?**  
No. Multiple reviewers confirm the double-dipped rubber grips are tightly bonded to the handles. They do not slip or rotate.

**Is there a warranty on this set?**  
WORKPRO offers standard warranty coverage on their tools. Check the current listing for warranty terms, which can vary by seller.
