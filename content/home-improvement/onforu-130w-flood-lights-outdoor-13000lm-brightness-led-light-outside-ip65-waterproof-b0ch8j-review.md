**The Short Version:** If you need to turn night into day in your backyard, the Onforu 130W flood light delivers serious output at a price that undercuts most big-box store options. But it's not a set-and-forget fixture — you'll wire it once and control it manually.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the flood light fixture, a mounting bracket with three adjustable axes, and a wire nut kit for hardwiring. No bulbs, no sensors, no remote. The unit itself is compact for the output — roughly 11 inches long and 7 inches wide — with a flat panel design that sits close to the wall. The 130W rating translates to 13,000 lumens, which is roughly equivalent to eight 60W incandescent bulbs. That's not marketing math; that's real brightness.

## Build and Performance

The housing is die-cast aluminum with a tempered glass lens. It feels substantial in hand, not like the lightweight plastic fixtures you'll find at the discount bin. The IP65 rating means it's fully protected against dust and low-pressure water jets — in plain terms, rain and snow won't kill it. One detail worth noting: the gasket around the lens seats properly, which isn't always the case at this price point.

The 120° beam angle produces a wide, even wash of light rather than a narrow spotlight. I found the 5000K daylight color temperature crisp and clean — good for security purposes, less cozy for ambiance. Color rendering is decent at 80+ CRI, so you're not looking at everything in washed-out blue tones.

The adjustable bracket is genuinely useful. You can angle the fixture left, right, up, or down independently, which matters when you're trying to cover a specific corner or avoid lighting up a neighbor's bedroom window. The included wire nuts are fine, though I'd recommend upgrading to silicone-filled connectors if you're in a humid climate.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest friction point: this is hardwired only. If you're expecting to plug it into an exterior outlet, you'll be disappointed. You need a standard junction box and basic wiring knowledge, or an electrician's phone number. That said, the wiring is straightforward — black to black, white to white, green to ground — and the included instructions are clear enough for a competent DIYer.

There's no motion sensor or photocell on this model. You're controlling it with a wall switch or adding an external timer. If you want auto-on at dusk, look at Onforu's motion sensor version — but expect to pay more.

Heat dissipation is adequate but not exceptional. The aluminum housing does its job, but the fixture runs warm to the touch after extended use. That's normal for 130W draw, but worth knowing if you're mounting it somewhere you'll brush against it.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You need serious, reliable outdoor lighting for a driveway, backyard, barn, or commercial space and you're comfortable with basic hardwiring. You want maximum lumens per dollar without sacrificing weatherproofing.

**Skip it if:** You need plug-and-play installation, want motion-sensing automation, or prefer a warmer color temperature for ambient outdoor living spaces.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this replace an existing floodlight?**
A: Yes, if you have a standard junction box. The mounting holes align with most existing fixtures, and the included bracket covers the old footprint. You'll need to patch any screw holes that don't line up.

**Q: How bright is 13000 lumens really?**
A: It's roughly equivalent to eight 60W incandescent bulbs or two 500W halogen work lights. It will comfortably illuminate a standard 30x30 foot driveway or backyard. It's bright enough that you'll notice it from down the street.

**Q: Does it work in extreme cold or heat?**
A: The operating range is -4°F to 140°F. The LED driver handles temperature swings well, though you'll see slightly reduced output in extreme cold — that's true of all LED fixtures.

## The Verdict

The Onforu 130W flood light is a straightforward, well-built tool that does exactly what it claims: flood an outdoor area with bright, even light. It's not smart, it's not fancy, and it requires basic wiring skills. But at this price point with this output and IP65 weatherproofing, it's one of the best value flood lights you'll find. If you need light and you're okay with a switch, this is the one.
