If you’ve been scrolling through dozens of wall sconces that all look like cheap plastic knockoffs, the XSDeTu Set of 2 probably caught your eye for one reason: the price-to-build ratio. At first glance, these look like they belong in a boutique hotel lobby, not a budget Amazon order. But does the reality match the listing photos? After digging through 200 ratings and studying the specs, here’s the honest breakdown.

## What’s Actually in the Box

You get two complete sconces, mounting brackets, screws, and wire connectors. That’s it. No bulbs, no template, no instruction booklet beyond a single folded sheet. The fixtures themselves are made of stamped steel with a matte black powder coat — nothing fancy, but nothing flimsy either. Each sconce measures roughly 12 inches tall with a 5-inch projection from the wall, which puts it in the “medium profile” category. It won’t dominate a narrow hallway, but it’s not a flush mount either.

## Real-World Performance and Build Quality

The first thing you’ll notice when you unbox these is the weight. They’re heavier than the price suggests, which usually means the metal isn’t tin foil thin. The finish is consistent across both units — no drips, no rough edges, and the seams where the shade meets the backplate are tight. That’s more than I can say for other sconces in this price tier.

The light output is where things get interesting. These use E12 candelabra sockets, and the listing recommends 40W equivalent bulbs. With standard LED candelabra bulbs, you’re looking at a warm 3000K glow that’s perfect for ambient lighting. One buyer noted, “They throw just enough light to read by without blinding you when you walk past.” That tracks with the design — the open-bottom shade directs light downward and outward, not straight into your eyes.

Installation is straightforward if you have basic DIY skills. The backplate is 12 inches across, which covers most sloppy drywall cuts from previous fixtures. The screws align with standard US junction boxes. However, this is hardwired only. There’s no cord, no switch, no plug-in option. If you’re renting or don’t want to cut into drywall, these aren’t for you.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the ratings, three themes keep coming up. First, the aesthetic. Multiple reviewers called them “modern farmhouse” or “industrial chic” without feeling gimmicky. Second, the light quality — warm, diffused, and flattering in bedrooms and living rooms. Third, the value for a two-pack. As one verified buyer put it, “I priced out similar sconces at big-box stores and these were half the cost for the same look.”

## Where They Fall Short

Let’s be honest about the downsides. The E12 socket requirement is the biggest annoyance. Candelabra bulbs cost more and have fewer options than standard A19 bulbs. You’ll also need to buy bulbs separately, which adds $10–15 to your total. A few reviewers mentioned the shade collects dust quickly, and because it’s open at the bottom, you’ll see it. Nothing a quick wipe won’t fix, but it’s worth knowing.

The bigger issue is the wiring. If you’re not comfortable with basic electrical work, budget for an electrician. This isn’t a plug-and-play product, and a handful of negative reviews stem from installation mistakes — not the fixture itself. One reviewer noted their sconce flickered, but it turned out to be a loose wire nut on their end, not a defect.

## Who Should Buy These

Buy these if you’re updating a hallway, flanking a bathroom mirror, or adding reading lights beside a bed. They work best in pairs, which is why the set of two makes sense. Skip them if you need a plug-in sconce, want to use standard bulbs, or prefer a brighter, task-oriented light. These are ambient fixtures, not work lights.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these sconces need a dimmer switch?**  
A: No, but if you install one, make sure you use dimmable LED bulbs. Non-dimmable bulbs will flicker or buzz with a dimmer.

**Q: Can these be mounted upside down?**  
A: Technically yes, but the shade is designed to point down. Mounting them upside down would expose the socket and defeat the light direction.

**Q: What size bulbs fit?**  
A: E12 candelabra base, max 40W equivalent. LED bulbs run cool and are recommended since the fixture is enclosed at the top.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a solid, good-looking hardwired sconce at a fair price and don’t mind buying bulbs separately. **Skip it if** you need plug-in operation or want to use standard A19 bulbs.
