**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the limitations:

- **Bulb clearance** — If your fixture has a tight glass globe or shade, this adapter won't fit. It's roughly the size of a large bulb, so measure your fixture's depth before ordering. This is the #1 complaint in negative reviews.
- **No switch** — The socket's existing pull-chain or wall switch controls the outlet. That's fine, but if you wanted a separate on/off, this isn't it.
- **Aesthetic** — It's utilitarian. In a visible living-room fixture, it looks like what it is: a plastic adapter. Fine for garages, closets, and utility spaces. Not a design statement.
- **Weight limit** — Don't hang anything heavy from the outlet itself. The screw-in base supports the adapter's weight, but a dangling power strip or heavy plug could stress the socket over time.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this with a dimmer switch?**
A: No. Dimmer switches control voltage, which can damage the adapter and whatever you plug in. Use only with standard on/off switches.

**Q: Will it work in a pendant light fixture?**
A: Usually, but check the socket orientation. If the pendant hangs down, the adapter and plug will dangle awkwardly. Better suited for ceiling or wall fixtures.

**Q: Is it safe to leave plugged in 24/7?**
A: Yes, as long as you're not exceeding the 15A rating and the socket is in good condition. It's no different than leaving a lamp plugged in.
