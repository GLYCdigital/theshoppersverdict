The LAOPAO LED Desk Lamp with Wireless Charger tries to solve a real problem: too many cords snaking across your desk. A lamp, a phone charger, and a tablet cable all competing for one outlet. This lamp consolidates all three into a single footprint. But does consolidation come at the cost of actual task lighting? I dug through 220 ratings and the specs to find out.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the lamp base (which houses the wireless charging pad), the articulated arm, the LED light bar, and a power adapter. Assembly takes under two minutes — the arm slots into the base with a firm twist, no tools required. The lamp itself is lightweight at roughly 2.5 pounds, so it won't tip easily, but it's not a heavy-duty metal workhorse either. The base has a rubberized ring underneath that keeps it planted on wood and laminate desks.

As shown in the product image, the charging pad sits flush on the base, with two USB-A ports tucked behind it. The light bar spans about 14 inches and articulates at three points: the base joint, the mid-arm, and the head. That gives you decent range, though the head only tilts about 90 degrees vertically, so you can't point it straight up at a ceiling.

## Performance: Light Quality and Charging

Here's where the LAOPAO earns its keep. The light bar produces 5 color temperature modes — from a warm 3000K to a cool 6000K — and 10 brightness levels. At max brightness, it's genuinely sufficient for detailed work like soldering or reading fine print. The stepless dimming is controlled by a touch-sensitive strip on the base, which is responsive and doesn't require hunting for buttons in the dark.

The wireless charging pad delivers a standard 10W to compatible Qi devices. It works reliably with iPhone and Samsung flagships, but don't expect MagSafe-level speeds. The non-slip silicone surface is a thoughtful touch — phones don't slide off when you bump the desk.

The two USB-A ports output 5V/2.1A each, which is fine for topping off a tablet or earbuds case, but you won't fast-charge anything modern. One reviewer noted, "The charging pad is a lifesaver for my nightstand setup — no more fumbling for cables in the dark." Another praised the light quality: "I've had $100 lamps that didn't light my workspace this evenly."

## Where It Falls Short

The most significant flaw is the charging pad's position. Because it's built into the base, you can't angle the lamp head low over your work area without the base sitting too far away for the phone to reach. If your desk is narrow or you work close to the wall, the phone placement becomes awkward.

Also, there's no USB-C PD support. If you're running a modern laptop or a phone that supports 30W+ charging, this lamp won't keep up — you'll still need a separate brick for that. The touch controls also lack a memory function; every time you switch the lamp off and on, it resets to the default brightness and color temperature. That's a minor annoyance, but one you'll notice daily.

## Who Should Buy This

This lamp is ideal for a student dorm, a nightstand, or a compact home office where every square inch of desk space matters. It's also a solid choice for a bedroom reading lamp that doubles as a phone charger — you're not trying to light a full workshop, just your immediate area.

Skip it if you need serious task lighting for precision work all day, or if you've already invested in a fast-charging ecosystem. The charging capabilities here are supplementary, not primary.

## FAQ

**Does the wireless charger work through thick phone cases?**
Most cases under 5mm work fine. Anything rugged or with a built-in battery pack will likely block the charge — you'll need to remove the case.

**Can you use the lamp while it's charging a phone?**
Yes, the lamp and charging pad operate independently. The USB ports also work simultaneously, so you can charge three devices at once.

**Is the light flicker-free for video calls or sensitive eyes?**
Yes, the LED driver produces a steady output with no visible flicker at any brightness level. It's comfortable for extended reading sessions.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want to consolidate your nightstand or compact desk into a single device with genuinely good lighting and convenient phone charging. **Skip it if** you need fast charging or heavy-duty task lighting.

At 4.4 stars from 220 ratings, the LAOPAO delivers on its core promise: it's a competent lamp with a useful bonus, not a gimmick. For the price, you're getting solid build quality and real utility — just keep your expectations realistic about the charging speeds.
