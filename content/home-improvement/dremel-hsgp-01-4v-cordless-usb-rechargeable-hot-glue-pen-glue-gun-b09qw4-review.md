If you've ever tried to glue small craft pieces or fix a delicate repair with a full-size hot glue gun, you know the struggle: thick globs, burned fingers, and glue where you didn't want it. The Dremel HSGP-01 4V Cordless Hot Glue Pen takes a fundamentally different approach. Instead of a bulky trigger-fed gun, you get a pen-shaped tool with a fine metal tip that deposits a precise 2mm bead of glue. It's a niche tool, but for the right projects, it's genuinely transformative.

**What you actually get in the box**

The package includes the glue pen itself, a USB-C charging cable (no wall adapter, so plan to use your phone charger), a stand that doubles as a storage base, and a starter pack of 10 mini glue sticks. The pen weighs just 5.6 ounces — light enough for extended one-handed use without fatigue. Dremel also includes a spare needle tip, which is a thoughtful touch since the fine nozzle is the most vulnerable part.

**Real-world performance**

The HSGP-01 heats up in roughly 35-40 seconds, which is notably faster than most corded guns that take several minutes. The 4V lithium battery delivers about 25 minutes of continuous run time — enough for a solid craft session or a handful of small repairs, but not for all-day projects. The trigger mechanism is responsive, and the fine tip allows you to place glue exactly where you need it, whether you're attaching rhinestones to fabric, securing model parts, or sealing small gaps.

The cordless design is the real differentiator here. As one Amazon reviewer put it: *"Being able to move around my project without dragging a cord is a game-changer. I used it to fix a ceramic figurine and the precision tip let me apply glue without making a mess."* Another noted: *"Warms up fast and the pen grip is comfortable. Much easier to control than my old glue gun."*

**Where it falls short**

The most significant limitation is the glue stick size. This pen only accepts 7mm mini sticks, not the standard 11mm sticks most households already own. Mini sticks are more expensive per ounce and less commonly available at big-box stores — you'll likely be ordering refills online. The battery life, while adequate for most tasks, means you can't start a large project and finish it without a charging break. And while the precision tip is excellent for detail work, it's too narrow for tasks like adhering large surfaces or spreading glue across wide areas.

A few reviewers also mentioned that the trigger requires a firm squeeze, and some found the initial glue flow a bit slow until the stick melts fully. That's a minor quibble, but worth knowing before you buy.

**Who should buy this**

- **Crafters and DIYers** who do detailed projects — cardmaking, jewelry, model building, or fabric embellishment
- **Anyone who hates cord management** and works in tight spaces or on the go
- **Repair-conscious homeowners** who need a precise tool for small fixes on ceramics, plastics, or electronics

**Skip it if** you primarily do large-scale projects like upholstery, heavy woodworking, or anything requiring broad glue coverage — a standard full-size gun is faster and cheaper per use.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use regular hot glue sticks in this Dremel pen?**  
No. It only accepts 7mm mini sticks. Standard 11mm sticks won't fit the chamber.

**Q: How long does the battery last on a full charge?**  
Expect roughly 25 minutes of continuous use. Charging via USB-C takes about 90 minutes from empty.

**Q: Is the tip hot enough to burn me?**  
Yes. The metal tip reaches operating temperature and stays hot for a few minutes after power-off. Use the included stand and let it cool fully before handling.

**The Verdict**

The Dremel HSGP-01 earns a solid 4.2/5. It's not a replacement for your standard glue gun — it's a specialized precision tool that excels at detailed work. The mini-stick requirement and modest battery life are real tradeoffs, but the control, portability, and fast heat-up time make it worth the compromise for crafters and detail-oriented repair work.

**Buy it if** you value precision over volume and want a cordless tool for fine projects.  
**Skip it if** you need maximum glue output for large tasks or don't want to commit to mini sticks.
