## FAQ

**Q: How often should I replace this filter?**  
A: For most homes, every 3-6 months. If you have pets or allergies, lean toward 3 months. If it's just you and a clean house, 6 months is fine. Check it monthly — if it looks dirty, replace it.

**Q: Will this fit my Lennox system?**  
A: If your current filter measures exactly 20x25x5 inches, yes. Measure the slot itself, not the old filter — some brands run slightly small. This is an OEM part, so it's designed to fit Lennox cabinets perfectly.

**Q: Is MERV 16 overkill for a home?**  
A: It's not overkill if you have allergies, asthma, or pets. For everyone else, MERV 11-13 is sufficient. The tradeoff is cost — you're paying for the higher rating.
