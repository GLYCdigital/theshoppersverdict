## What You Actually Get

Each box contains the wall light fixture, a mounting plate, screws, wire connectors, and a template for drilling. No bulbs — that's important to know upfront. The unit itself measures roughly 11.8 inches tall and 4.7 inches wide, with a depth of about 5.5 inches. It's a compact footprint that works well on porches, garages, or flanking a front door.

The housing is die-cast aluminum with a matte black finish. The glass panels are clear and slightly recessed on both ends, which is what creates that signature up-and-down lighting effect.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

I'll be direct: for the price point, the construction is solid. The aluminum body has some heft to it, and the powder-coated finish resists chipping. Multiple buyers in humid climates confirm this — one reviewer from Florida noted, "Six months of salt air and rain, no rust or fading whatsoever." That's a meaningful data point for coastal shoppers.

The light output depends entirely on what bulbs you install. The fixture takes two standard E26 base bulbs — one on top, one on bottom. With 9W LED bulbs (roughly 800 lumens each), you get a warm 3000K wash that creates a soft, inviting glow across your exterior walls. It's not a security floodlight; it's architectural accent lighting. Set expectations accordingly.

Installation is straightforward for anyone comfortable with basic wiring. The included template is a nice touch, and the wire connectors are pre-installed. Most buyers report completing the job in 20–30 minutes per fixture. One reviewer mentioned, "Installed in 15 minutes replacing my old porch lights. The hardest part was deciding which bulbs to buy."

## Where It Falls Short

Three issues surface consistently in feedback:

**Bulbs not included.** This is the most common complaint. It's not a dealbreaker, but it adds $10–15 per fixture if you buy decent LEDs. The instructions recommend specific wattage, and you'll want to follow that — too-high wattage can cause heat issues in the sealed housing.

**The mounting plate is thin.** It's functional, but several buyers noted it flexes if you overtighten the screws. Use a hand screwdriver, not a drill, when securing it to the junction box.

**Motion-sensor version quirks.** If you opt for the sensor model, know that it resets to factory defaults after a power outage. You'll need to re-adjust the time and sensitivity settings. It's a minor annoyance, not a failure.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these be used in covered outdoor areas only?**
A: They're rated for damp locations (IP44), meaning covered porches, eaves, and overhangs. Fully exposed to rain is not recommended unless you have significant overhang protection.

**Q: What's the best bulb choice?**
A: Two 9W LED bulbs with E26 base, 2700K–3000K color temperature. Avoid anything over 100W equivalent — the sealed housing traps heat.

**Q: Do they work with dimmer switches?**
A: Yes, but only if you use dimmable LED bulbs. Standard LEDs will flicker or hum on a dimmer.
