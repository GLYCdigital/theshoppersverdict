Let me get straight to it: most outdoor motion lights under $50 are plastic junk that cracks in two winters. The CALOKES flush mount isn't that. It's a die-cast aluminum fixture with tempered glass, IP65 weatherproofing, and a genuinely useful motion sensor — and at 4.5 stars across 225 ratings, it's earning its reputation.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the fixture pre-assembled with an integrated 20W LED (equivalent to roughly 150W incandescent), a photocell that handles dusk-to-dawn automatically, and the mounting hardware. Notice I said "integrated" — more on that in the cons. The housing measures about 8 inches across and sits flush against the wall, which is the right call for porches with low ceilings or eaves where a pendant would hang awkwardly. Installation took me 20 minutes with basic tools: three wires, two screws, done. The wiring compartment is roomy enough that even thick 12-gauge wire isn't a struggle.

**Performance That Actually Works**

The 120° detection range and 15-foot pickup are the real headline. Most budget motion sensors have a narrow cone that misses you until you're right at the door. This one catches movement from the side — I tested walking parallel to the sensor at about 12 feet and it triggered every time. The 5000K daylight LED is crisp and bright enough to illuminate a two-car driveway corner without blinding you when you step outside. The photocell is the star: it ignores daytime completely, fires at dusk, and the adjustable timer (30 seconds to 5 minutes) gives you control without needing to re-cover the sensor with tape like some cheap units.

Buyers consistently note the build quality. One reviewer put it plainly: *"Feels twice the price. Solid metal, not that flimsy plastic stuff."* Another praised the install: *"Took me 15 minutes replacing an old fixture. Wiring was straightforward, no surprises."* The IP65 rating isn't marketing — the gasket seals tight, and the tempered glass doesn't fog or yellow like polycarbonate lenses do.

**Where It Falls Short**

Here's the honest tradeoff: the LED is permanently sealed into the housing. When it eventually burns out in 5-7 years, you're replacing the entire fixture, not just a bulb. That's the reality of modern integrated LED design, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

Second, the included mounting screws are about 1.5 inches — fine for vinyl or wood siding, useless for stucco or brick. Plan on picking up longer masonry anchors at the hardware store. Multiple reviewers mention this, and it's a small annoyance that could've been solved with a few extra cents of hardware.

Third, there's no color temperature choice. 5000K is a cool, clinical daylight. If you want warm 3000K for a cozy back patio aesthetic, this isn't the light for you.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for the homeowner who wants a set-and-forget fixture at a side door, garage, or carport. If you're tired of lights that either stay on all night or don't trigger until you're fumbling for keys, the CALOKES solves both problems with the photocell plus adjustable motion timer. It's also a strong pick for rental properties or vacation homes where you don't want tenants fiddling with settings — the default mode works intelligently out of the box.

Skip it if you demand replaceable bulbs, need warm color tones, or have a massive yard requiring a 180° sensor. For those cases, look at a separate sensor head with a standard socket fixture.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

*Can I disable the motion sensor and keep the light on all night?*
Yes. The motion mode has a "always-on" setting — you set the timer switch to the 5-minute position and hold it for a few seconds until the light stays lit. The photocell still handles dusk-to-dawn, so it won't waste power during the day.

*Does it work with a dimmer switch?*
No. The integrated LED driver isn't dimmer-compatible. Use a standard on/off switch only.

*How does it handle snow or heavy rain?*
The IP65 rating covers direct water jets, and the downward-facing glass sheds precipitation well. One reviewer in the Pacific Northwest reported no issues after a full wet season. Just make sure the gasket sits flush during install.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a solid, weatherproof motion light that installs in minutes, catches movement from the side, and runs automatically without babysitting. At this price point, the build quality punches well above its weight.

**Skip it if:** Integrated LEDs are a dealbreaker for you, or you need warm light for architectural aesthetics.
