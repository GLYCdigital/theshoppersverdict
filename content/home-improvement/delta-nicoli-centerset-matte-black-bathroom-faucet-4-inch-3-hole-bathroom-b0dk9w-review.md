**The Delta Nicoli Centerset Matte Black Bathroom Faucet, 4 Inch 3** is the kind of faucet that makes you wonder why anyone still buys chrome. At a glance, it's a clean, low-profile centerset with a matte black finish that looks far more expensive than its price point suggests. But as with any hardware purchase, the real story is in the details — and the installation, not the glamour shots.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the faucet body with pre-attached water supply lines, two lever handles, a metal pop-up drain assembly, and mounting hardware. That's it — no surprises, no missing pieces, no trip to the hardware store mid-install. The supply lines are 14 inches long, which covers most standard vanity setups. The included drain is a genuine upgrade: it's all metal, not the flimsy plastic you often find bundled with faucets in this range.

The package specs are straightforward: 4-inch centerset, meaning the handle holes are spaced exactly 4 inches apart, and it requires a 3-hole sink configuration. The spout reaches 5.28 inches from the base with a 3.94-inch height. It's compact, purposeful, and fits standard bathroom vanities without issue.

## Performance and Build Quality

Delta's Diamond Seal Technology is the headline feature here. Instead of a rubber washer that degrades over time, the valve uses a diamond-coated ceramic disc that's rated for 5 million cycles. In practice, that means the lever handles should stay smooth and drip-free for well over a decade. One buyer noted, "The handles are buttery smooth and the water flow is strong. No drips after six months of daily use." That sentiment echoes across most of the 220 ratings.

The matte black finish is applied through a multi-step electroplating process. It resists fingerprints and water spots far better than expected — a genuine concern with dark finishes. However, it's not impervious to hard water. In areas with heavy mineral content, you'll see white deposits forming on the spout base that require regular wiping. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

The lever handles are solid metal, not hollow-feeling zinc alloy. They move with a satisfying, weighted precision. The spout aerator produces a crisp, non-splashing stream that fills a glass cleanly.

## Where It Falls Short

The most common complaint among the 4.4-star aggregate is the low spout clearance. At under 4 inches of height, this faucet won't comfortably accommodate tall items. One reviewer wrote, "I can't fill my water bottle without tilting it at a 45-degree angle. Love the look, but wish the spout was taller." If you routinely wash your face or fill large containers, this is a genuine limitation.

The 4-inch centerset design is also a commitment. If your sink has a single hole or an 8-inch spread, this faucet won't work without an adapter plate — which always looks like a compromise. Measure your sink before ordering.

Finally, the installation, while straightforward for a DIYer, requires access under the sink. The mounting nuts are standard, but the pre-attached supply lines make the process slightly more finicky if your vanity has a shallow drawer directly below. Budget 45-60 minutes for a first-time install.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The design-conscious renter** who wants a modern matte black look without a $300 price tag
- **The DIY homeowner** replacing an existing 4-inch centerset faucet with a more durable option
- **The guest bathroom** — the compact footprint and low-maintenance finish make it ideal for low-traffic spaces

Skip it if you have a single-hole sink, need tall-spout clearance, or live with extreme hard water and refuse to wipe down fixtures regularly.

## FAQ

**Is the matte black finish prone to chipping?**
No. The electroplated finish is baked on and holds up well against normal wear. It will scratch if you're careless with metal tools during installation, but day-to-day use is fine.

**Will this faucet work with a tankless water heater?**
Yes. The Diamond Seal valve handles temperature fluctuations well, and the brass construction handles standard residential water pressure without issue.

**Does the included pop-up drain fit all sinks?**
It fits standard 1.25-inch drain openings, which covers the vast majority of bathroom sinks. Non-standard or vessel sinks may require an aftermarket drain.

## The Verdict
