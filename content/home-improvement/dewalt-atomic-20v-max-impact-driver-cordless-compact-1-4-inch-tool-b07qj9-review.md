## The compact impact driver that actually delivers full-size torque

Most "compact" tools make a trade: smaller body, weaker performance. The DEWALT ATOMIC 20V MAX impact driver (model DCF809) refuses that deal. It squeezes 1,700 in-lbs of torque into a 5.4-inch chassis — that's more power than DEWALT's full-size DCF887 from just a few years ago, in a package that's over an inch shorter. For anyone who's ever wedged a drill between wall studs or fought with a tool in a cramped corner cabinet, that difference is the whole game.

## What's in the box

This is a bare tool — just the impact driver itself and a manual. No battery, no charger, no case. If you're already in the 20V MAX ecosystem, that's fine. If you're starting fresh, budget for a starter kit or pick up a compatible battery and charger separately. The 1/4-inch hex chuck accepts all standard impact-rated bits, and the tool weighs in at a feathery 2.1 pounds with a compact 2Ah battery attached.

## Performance and real-world use

The 3-speed selector is where this driver earns its keep. Speed 1 caps at 500 RPM with precision drive mode — this is your finish work setting. I drove dozens of 2-inch cabinet screws into oak without a single stripped head. Speed 2 handles general fastening at 1,500 RPM. Speed 3 unleashes the full 1,700 in-lbs at 3,250 RPM for lag bolts and structural work.

One buyer summed it up: *"I've used this daily for 8 months framing, decks, and furniture. It's never stalled on a 3-inch screw, and the size means I don't need a second tool for tight spots."* Another noted: *"The precision mode saved me on a walnut desk project. Full torque would've been a disaster."*

The variable trigger gives genuine feathering control at low speeds — not the on/off jumpiness you get on cheaper impacts. The 20V MAX battery platform means you can swap cells from a dozen other DEWALT tools if you're already invested.

## Where it falls short

The LED light placement is the most common complaint. It's mounted at the base of the chuck, which means the chuck itself casts a shadow directly on your work area. In dark crawlspaces, you'll be angling the tool to see what you're driving. Not a dealbreaker — but annoying when a $30 drill from Harbor Freight gets this right.

The missing belt hook is a head-scratcher. DEWALT includes one on nearly every other driver in their lineup, but here it's a separate purchase. It's a $10 part, but it's the principle.

And while the compact size is the selling point, it does mean a slightly thicker grip than the sub-compact DCF850. If you have smaller hands or do precision work all day, test the ergonomics first.

## Who should buy this

This driver is ideal for three groups: **electricians and cabinet installers** who live in tight spaces, **DIYers** who want one tool that does everything from furniture assembly to deck building, and **existing DEWALT 20V owners** who want a lighter, shorter driver for their bag without learning a new battery system.

Skip it if you need a full kit with batteries and charger, if you do zero finish work (the precision mode would be wasted), or if you're already happy with a sub-compact like the DCF850.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this come with a battery and charger?**
No — this is the bare tool. You'll need a 20V MAX battery and charger. Compatible with all DEWALT 20V MAX batteries, including the compact 2Ah and the high-capacity 5Ah.

**Q: Will this drive 3-inch deck screws?**
Yes. The 1,700 in-lbs of torque handles them easily, especially on speed 3. You'll want a 2Ah or larger battery for sustained work.

**Q: What's the difference between this and the DCF850 sub-compact?**
The DCF850 is shorter (4.2 inches vs 5.4) and slightly more powerful (1,825 in-lbs), but it's also more expensive. The ATOMIC DCF809 is the better value for most users — you lose a fraction of an inch and gain a lower price.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The DEWALT ATOMIC 20V MAX impact driver delivers class-leading power in a genuinely compact package. The precision drive mode alone justifies the purchase for anyone who's ever stripped a screw in expensive wood. It's not perfect — the LED placement and missing belt hook are frustrating cost-cutting — but the performance-to-size ratio is unmatched at this price point.

**Buy it if** you need a compact driver that doesn't compromise on torque, or you're already in the 20V MAX ecosystem.
