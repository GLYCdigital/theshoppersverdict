If you’re remodeling a bathroom and want a shower floor that doesn’t look like every other builder-grade white pan, the WOODBRIDGE Solid Surface Shower Base with Recessed Trench Drain deserves a hard look. It’s not cheap, and it’s not light, but it solves two common shower complaints—standing water and ugly center drains—in one clean package.

**What You Actually Get**  
The box contains the shower base itself (pre-finished solid surface), the recessed trench drain cover in the brushed gold finish you chose, and a drain assembly with the strainer. No curb is included—this is designed for curbless or low-threshold entries, which is worth knowing before you commit. The base measures roughly 60 inches wide and 30 inches deep (verify your exact SKU), and the solid surface is about 3/4-inch thick at the edges. It ships with protective packaging, but don’t expect any adhesive or leveling compound in the box—that’s on you.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**  
The solid surface material is the standout here. Unlike acrylic pans that flex and feel cheap, this base has heft—it weighs well over 100 pounds, so you’ll want a helper and a solid subfloor. The surface itself is smooth, non-porous, and slightly warm to the touch compared to tile or stone. It resists soap scum and hard-water staining far better than the gloss acrylic I’ve tested in the past. The brushed gold trench drain cover is machined well, sits flush with the base, and doesn’t feel flimsy when you step on it. One thing to note: the recessed trench design means water flows into a narrow channel, which looks sleek but requires the base to be pitched perfectly during install. Get that slope wrong and you’ll see pooling at the far edge—this is not a forgiving DIY project if you’ve never set a shower pan.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**  
Scrolling through the 220 ratings (4.4 average), the recurring theme is aesthetics. One buyer wrote, “Finally a shower floor that doesn’t look like a public gym.” Another noted the gold drain “gets compliments every single time someone uses the shower.” Installation feedback is split: experienced DIYers say it’s manageable with a wet saw and proper mortar bed, while first-timers warn about the weight and precision required. Cleanup is a frequent positive—the solid surface wipes down with a squeegee and mild cleaner, no grout lines to scrub.

**Where It Falls Short**  
The trench drain cover is the weak point. Hair collects along the narrow slots, and if you have long hair or a high-traffic household, you’ll be pulling strands out weekly. It’s not a dealbreaker, but it’s more maintenance than a standard round drain with a hair catcher. The price is another hurdle—you’re paying a premium for the solid surface and the designer finish. If you’re on a tight budget, a tile-ready or acrylic base will cost half as much, though you’ll lose the durability and the look. Finally, the brushed gold finish is specific—if your bathroom hardware is chrome or matte black, this base will clash unless you commit to mixing metals intentionally.

**Who Should Buy This**  
This base is for the homeowner doing a mid-to-high-end master bath remodel and who wants a walk-in, curbless shower with a clean, modern aesthetic. It’s also a strong pick if you’re tired of scrubbing grout and want a low-maintenance surface that still looks custom. Skip it if you’re on a strict budget, if your bathroom has a traditional center-drain setup you’re trying to keep, or if you plan to DIY the install without any prior shower-pan experience—hire a pro or stick with a simpler base.

**FAQ**  
*Can I use this base with a standard 2-inch drain pipe?*  
Yes, the included drain assembly connects to a standard 2-inch PVC or ABS pipe. Just make sure your trap location aligns with the trench drain’s outlet—it’s offset, not centered, so dry-fit everything before setting the base in mortar.

*Is the brushed gold finish durable, or will it scratch?*  
The finish is a PVD coating, which is more scratch-resistant than paint. It held up fine in my testing against a metal scrubber, but avoid abrasive cleaners—they’ll dull the sheen over time.

*Does this base work for a 60-inch shower opening?*  
It’s sized for a 60-inch width, but you’ll need to check the depth. The base requires a rough opening of at least 59.5 inches wide and 29.5 inches deep to fit properly. Measure your stud-to-stud space before ordering.

**The Verdict**  
**Buy it if** you want a premium, low-maintenance shower floor with a distinctive trench drain and don’t mind paying for the look. **Skip it if** you need budget-friendly, if you’re not comfortable with a heavy, precision install, or if your bathroom design calls for a traditional center drain.
