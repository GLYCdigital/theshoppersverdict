# PoKat 23" Table Lamp, Farmhouse Bedside Lamps for Bedroom Se

## The Short Version

The PoKat 23" farmhouse table lamp set is the kind of purchase that makes you wonder why anyone overspends on bedroom lighting. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it's clearly doing something right — but it's not perfect. Here's what you actually need to know before clicking buy.

## What's in the Box

You get two complete lamps, each with a 23-inch tall body, a fabric-look drum shade, and the hardware to assemble everything. The base is a solid, slightly rustic wooden block with a dark walnut finish — not a cheap hollow plastic imitation. The metal pole is powder-coated black, which gives it that modern-farmhouse contrast that's been dominating interior design catalogs for years.

Assembly takes about five minutes per lamp. You're screwing the pole into the base, sliding the shade over the socket, and tightening a finial on top. The instruction sheet is basic but clear. You'll need a Phillips screwdriver for the base plate.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The lamp feels sturdier than its price point suggests. The base is heavy enough that knocking it over isn't a real concern — even with cats or kids in the house. The fabric shade is actually a polyester blend, but from a distance it reads convincingly as linen. It diffuses light well, casting a warm, even glow rather than a harsh spotlight.

The 23-inch height is the design's best feature. Most nightstands run 22-26 inches tall, and this lamp puts the bulb right at seated eye level — bright enough to read by, low enough to not blind you in bed. It takes a standard E26 bulb (not included), and the pull chain switch is responsive with a satisfying click.

One thing buyers consistently mention in reviews: the lamps match the photos. The walnut base has real grain variation, and the black pole is evenly finished. No factory defects, no wobbling sockets.

As one verified buyer put it: *"These look way more expensive than they are. My wife thought I spent triple what I actually did."*

Another noted: *"The light is soft and warm — perfect for reading without giving me a headache."*

## Where It Falls Short

The pull chain switch is the most common complaint. It hangs down about six inches, which means it can dangle below the shade — a bit awkward if your nightstand is tight against a wall. A touch switch would've been nicer, but that would push the price up.

The polyester shade is another compromise. Up close, it lacks the texture of real linen. If you're the kind of person who inspects fabric under bright light, you'll notice. Most people won't.

Assembly, while simple, does require a screwdriver. Some users found the base plate screws finicky to align. It's a five-minute annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

One reviewer noted: *"The chain gets a little tangled with the shade if you're not careful. Not a huge deal, but worth knowing."*

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're furnishing a bedroom, guest room, or reading nook on a budget and want that warm farmhouse aesthetic without spending boutique prices. The set of two is ideal for matching nightstands or flanking a king bed.

**Skip it if:** You hate pull chains, want a lamp with USB ports, or need something with a more modern/industrial look. This is firmly farmhouse-casual.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these lamps come with bulbs?**
A: No. You'll need two standard E26 base bulbs — a 60-watt equivalent LED works best. The warm white (2700K) LEDs pair perfectly with the shade's diffusion.

**Q: How tall are these lamps exactly?**
A: 23 inches from base to the top of the shade. The shade itself is roughly 10 inches wide and 8 inches tall. They're not oversized — they're proportionate for standard nightstands.

**Q: Can I use smart bulbs in these?**
A: Yes. Any standard E26 smart bulb fits, and the pull chain can be left in the "on" position if you want to control everything via app or voice assistant.
