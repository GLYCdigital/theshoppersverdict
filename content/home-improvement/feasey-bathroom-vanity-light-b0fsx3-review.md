If you're staring at a dated boob light above your bathroom mirror, the Feasey Bathroom Vanity Light is a legitimate upgrade — but it's not for everyone. With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, this three-globe LED fixture has earned its reputation. Here's the unfiltered breakdown.

## What's Actually in the Box

Feasey ships the fixture with three integrated LED globes already attached — no separate bulb shopping. You also get a brushed nickel mounting plate, screws, anchors, and wire nuts. What's notably absent: a light bulb. That's intentional, because the globes aren't replaceable. The whole unit is rated for 30,000 hours, which works out to roughly 10–12 years of normal daily use. As shown in the product image, the globes are cylindrical and frosted, casting a diffused glow rather than harsh spots.

## Real-World Performance

The 4000K color temperature is the sweet spot for task lighting — bright enough for makeup application and shaving, but not the sterile blue-white of 5000K work lights. At full brightness, the three globes output roughly 2,400 lumens combined, which is ample for a standard 24–36 inch vanity. Dimming works smoothly if you have a compatible LED dimmer switch; standard incandescent dimmers will cause flicker.

Installation is genuinely straightforward. The mounting plate uses a slotted keyhole design, so you can hang the fixture while wiring — a small touch that saves real frustration. Multiple buyers mentioned completing the swap solo in under 20 minutes. One reviewer noted, "I'm not handy at all, and I had this up in half an hour. The wire connectors they include are actually decent quality."

The wet rating is the standout feature. Unlike many vanity lights that are damp-rated at best, this one is UL-listed for wet locations. That means it's safe directly above a shower or in a steam-heavy bathroom — a genuine differentiator in this price range.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Three themes dominate the positive reviews: appearance, brightness, and ease of install. The frosted globes hide the LED diodes completely, giving a clean, seamless look that reads as more expensive than the price tag suggests. One verified buyer put it plainly: "This looks like a $200 fixture. I paid less than half that." Another highlighted the light quality: "Finally a vanity light that doesn't cast shadows on my face — the diffused globes make a real difference."

## Where It Falls Short

The integrated LED globes are the double-edged sword. When one fails, the entire fixture is a paperweight. At 30,000 hours that's likely 10+ years out, but it's still a valid concern for buyers who prefer repairable fixtures. The 4000K temperature also isn't adjustable — if you want warm, cozy bathroom lighting for evening soaks, this will feel clinical. And the finish is strictly brushed nickel; no black, bronze, or chrome options exist, which limits matching in mixed-metal bathrooms.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want bright, even task lighting over a vanity or in a shower alcove, you're comfortable with a non-replaceable LED system, and your bathroom hardware is brushed nickel or chrome.

**Skip it if:** You insist on replaceable bulbs, need a warm 2700K glow, or your fixtures are matte black or oil-rubbed bronze.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this light be installed vertically next to a mirror?**
A: Yes. The mounting plate slots allow horizontal or vertical orientation, though most buyers use it horizontally above a mirror.

**Q: Does it work with a standard dimmer switch?**
A: Only if the dimmer is rated for LED loads. A standard incandescent dimmer will cause flickering or buzzing. Check your switch before buying.

**Q: Is the glass globe easy to clean?**
A: The frosted glass wipes down easily, but dust can build up inside the open top of the globes. A quick blast with compressed air every few months handles it.

## The Verdict

The Feasey Bathroom Vanity Light delivers exactly what most shoppers want: bright, even, flattering light with a premium look and a painless install. The integrated LED design is a long-term commitment, but at this price point, the 10-year lifespan makes it a reasonable trade. It's a solid 4.6-star product that earns its rating.

**Buy it if** you need reliable, wet-rated vanity lighting and don't mind the fixed color temperature. **Skip it if** you want replaceable bulbs or a warm glow for a spa-like bathroom.
