**FAQ (Real Questions Shoppers Ask)**

**Q: Will these work with an impact driver?**
A: Yes. The 1/4" hex shank is designed for impact drivers and drill/drivers with a hex chuck. The stop collar stays put under the vibration of an impact driver, which is a common failure point on cheaper sets.

**Q: Can I use them on metal?**
A: Soft metals like aluminum and brass, yes, with cutting oil and slow speed. Steel, no—the HSS edges will dull quickly. Use a dedicated metal countersink for that.

**Q: How do I adjust the stop collar?**
A: Loosen the set screw with the included hex key, slide the collar to the desired depth (measure from the tip of the pilot drill), then tighten the screw. Test on a scrap piece before you start your project.
