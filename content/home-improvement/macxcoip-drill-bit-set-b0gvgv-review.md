**Build quality and real-world performance**

The twist bits are the stars here. The included hole saws are serviceable for cutting clean circles in wood up to about 3/4" thick — just don't expect them to survive a career in metal.

That magnetic driver holder is worth calling out. It holds bits firmly with minimal wobble, which is rare in budget sets. You'll actually use the included Torx and hex bits, which is more than I can say for some kits where those sizes are just case filler.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the weak points. The spade bits are the first thing to blunt, especially if you're boring through nail-embedded lumber or OSB. If you do heavy framing work, budget for a separate quality spade set eventually.

It won't fail immediately, but if you're throwing this in a truck bed or tool bag daily, treat the case with some care — or transfer the bits to a tougher organizer.
