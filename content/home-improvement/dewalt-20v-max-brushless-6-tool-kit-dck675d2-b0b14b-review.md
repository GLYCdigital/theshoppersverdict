If you're shopping for your first serious cordless tool kit—or replacing a mismatched collection of hand-me-downs—the DEWALT 20V MAX Brushless 6-Tool Kit (DCK675D2) is the benchmark. It's not the cheapest combo kit on the shelf, and it's not the most complete. But it hits a sweet spot of power, runtime, and tool variety that most homeowners and serious DIYers will appreciate for years.

**What's actually in the box**

Six tools, two batteries, a charger, and a soft bag. Specifically: the DCD791 brushless compact hammer drill, DCF887 brushless impact driver, DCS565 brushless 6½-inch circular saw, DCS382 brushless reciprocating saw, DCG412B 4½-inch angle grinder, and DCL040 work light. The two 5.0Ah batteries are the workhorses here—that's the capacity you need for the saws, not the weak 1.5Ah packs some kits ship with.

**Performance and real-world use**

The drill and impact driver are the stars. The DCD791 is compact—about the same footprint as DEWALT's 12V line—but delivers 460 unit watts out (UWO) and a 0-2,000 RPM range. That's enough to drive 3-inch deck screws without pre-drilling and handle 1-inch spade bits through studs. The DCF887 impact driver puts out 1,825 in-lbs of torque, which is genuinely strong; it'll snap a ¼-inch hex adapter if you lean on it too hard.

The circular saw is where the brushless motor earns its keep. With a 5.0Ah battery, you'll get roughly 80-100 linear feet of cutting through 2x4 pressure-treated lumber before swapping packs. That's respectable. It's also got a nice 51-degree bevel capacity and a magnesium shoe that won't warp. The reciprocating saw is a solid all-rounder—not the most aggressive in DEWALT's line, but it'll chew through a 4-inch branch or an old stud with a decent blade.

The angle grinder is the odd one out. It's a brushed motor (DCG412B), which is fine for occasional grinding, but it's the one tool that feels dated next to the brushless siblings. It's also the most battery-hungry—plan for about 10-12 minutes of continuous grinding per 5.0Ah charge. The work light is bright but narrow; it's fine for lighting up a dark crawlspace, not for illuminating a wide workbench.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.5-star average across 225 ratings reflects consistent themes. One reviewer noted: *"The drill and driver alone are worth the price of the kit—I've used them daily for six months and they haven't missed a beat."* Another said: *"Battery life surprised me. I cut a full sheet of plywood and still had juice left for the drill."* Build quality gets repeated mentions—the tools feel solid, the chucks are true, and the LED lights on the drill and driver actually help in dark cabinets.

**Where it falls short**

The bag is flimsy. It's fine for storing tools in the garage, but if you're hauling this to jobsites daily, the zippers will eventually give out. The grinder being brushed is a real inconsistency—if you plan to use it heavily, buy the separate brushless version. And there's no battery gauge on the 5.0Ah packs, which means you'll occasionally get caught mid-cut with a dead battery. Finally, the kit doesn't include a charger that handles two batteries simultaneously, so recharging two depleted packs takes time.

**Who should buy this**

This kit is ideal for: the homeowner tackling deck builds, drywall, furniture assembly, and light demolition; the DIY enthusiast who wants one set that covers most projects without buying separate tools; and the new homeowner starting their tool collection from scratch. Skip it if you're a professional framer or electrician who needs daily-driver tools with specific features—you'll want individual purchases with higher specs.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these tools compatible with other DEWALT 20V batteries?**  
A: Yes. All DEWALT 20V MAX batteries and chargers are cross-compatible within the 20V lineup. If you already own DEWALT 20V tools, these batteries will work in them and vice versa.

**Q: How long do the batteries last on a full charge?**  
A: Realistically, the 5.0Ah packs give about 2-3 hours of intermittent use with the drill and driver, 80-100 feet of cutting with the circular saw, and roughly 10-12 minutes of constant grinding with the angle grinder. Expect less if you're running the saws continuously.

**Q: Is this kit worth it compared to buying tools separately?**  
A: Yes, if you need at least four of the six tools. The kit price typically comes in 30-40% below buying the drill, driver, circular saw, and reciprocating saw individually. The grinder and light are effectively freebies.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, powerful cordless toolkit that covers 90% of home and DIY projects without breaking the bank. The brushless drill and driver are genuinely excellent, the battery capacity is substantial, and you'll likely never outgrow this set.

**Skip it if** you're a pro who needs a tool-specific setup, or if you rarely use more than a drill and driver—in that case, buy those two tools individually and save the money.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A strong, well-rounded kit with minor flaws that don't undermine its value. The brushed grinder and flimsy bag keep it from a perfect score, but the core tools are outstanding.
