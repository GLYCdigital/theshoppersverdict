Delta is a name every homeowner recognizes. The question isn't whether Delta makes good faucets — they do. The question is whether this specific model, sitting at 4.6 stars across 229 Amazon ratings, deserves your money over the dozens of other options filling that same search page. ## What You Actually Get

The box contains the faucet assembly, a pull-down sprayer head, the hose, mounting hardware, a deck plate for 3-hole installations, and the water supply lines. No plumber's putty, no Teflon tape — you'll need those separately. The metal construction is substantial; this isn't a flimsy builder-grade piece. The sprayer head has a toggle switch for stream versus spray modes, and Delta's DIAMOND Seal technology is the headline feature here — ceramic valves that replace the rubber washers that fail in cheap faucets.

## Performance and Build Quality

In daily use, the pull-down sprayer is the star. When you're done with it, the magnetic dock pulls it back into place with a satisfying click — no more drooping spray head that slowly sags into your sink. The hose extends about 20 inches, which covers a standard kitchen sink without issue. Water flow is consistent at 1.8 GPM, which is the federal maximum and sufficient for filling pots and rinsing dishes.

The finish holds up well. Multiple reviewers mention the chrome surface staying spotless through months of hard water and frequent cleaning. One buyer noted, "Installed in July, and it still looks brand new in December — no water spots etching into the finish." That's the kind of durability you expect from Delta, and this model delivers.

## What Buyers Praise

The recurring themes in the 229 ratings are installation ease and reliability. A verified purchaser wrote: "Replaced my 15-year-old Delta with this one. Same mounting holes, same supply lines, done in 30 minutes. Why did I wait so long?" Another reviewer highlighted the sprayer docking: "The magnetic holder is genius. My old faucet's sprayer always fell down, and this one stays put no matter what."

The lifetime warranty gets mentioned too. Delta backs the DIAMOND Seal cartridge against leaks forever, and the finish against tarnishing for life. That's meaningful peace of mind when you're installing something you'll touch hundreds of times a year.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. First, the finish options are basic — chrome and stainless steel, period. If you're after a modern matte black or brushed gold look, this isn't your faucet. Second, several buyers note the hose is stiffer than competing models. One reviewer said, "The pull-down works fine, but it takes more effort to extend than my old Moen. Not a dealbreaker, just noticeable." Third, this is a straightforward mechanical faucet — no touchless sensor, no voice activation, no Delta's Touch2O feature. You're paying for durability, not smart-home integration.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The DIY homeowner** replacing a worn-out faucet: The straightforward installation and standard 3-hole compatibility make this an accessible weekend project.
- **The practical buyer** who wants a faucet that will outlast their kitchen: DIAMOND Seal tech and the lifetime warranty make this a buy-once, forget-it purchase.
- **The landlord or property manager** who needs reliable fixtures that won't generate service calls: This is workhorse territory.

## FAQ

**Will this fit my existing sink?**
It works with 1-hole and 3-hole setups — the included deck plate covers the extra holes. Standard 1-1/2 inch sink holes fit without modification. Measure your sink spacing before ordering; if your holes are 6 inches apart, you're good.

**Is the pull-down sprayer going to fail like my last one?**
The magnetic docking is the key difference. It prevents the hose from kinking at the base, which is what kills most pull-down faucets. Combined with the DIAMOND Seal cartridge, the failure points are dramatically reduced.

**Can I install this myself, or do I need a plumber?**
If you've replaced a faucet before, you can handle this in under an hour. The supply lines are pre-attached and the mounting hardware is straightforward. If you've never touched a wrench, budget for a plumber — the $150–200 service call is cheaper than a water-damage repair.
