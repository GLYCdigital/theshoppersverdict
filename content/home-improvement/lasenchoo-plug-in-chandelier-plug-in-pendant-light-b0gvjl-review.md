**The LASENCHOO Plug in Chandelier solves a problem every renter knows** — you want statement lighting, but you can't rewire your apartment. This fixture hangs from your ceiling and plugs into any standard wall outlet. No junction boxes, no electrician, no landlord approval. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it's clearly working for a lot of people. The real question is whether it works for your space. Here's the honest breakdown.

**What you actually get**

The package includes the chandelier itself, a ceiling mount, and the plug-in cord with a standard three-prong plug. The fixture comes fully assembled — no snapping together dozens of tiny arms like some budget chandeliers. The cord length is generous, giving you flexibility on hanging height. As shown in the product images, the design is modern-farmhouse leaning: matte black finish with clear glass shades that let the bulbs (not included) take center stage. You'll need to supply your own bulbs — standard E12 candelabra base, so pick up LED Edison-style bulbs for the best look.

**Performance and build quality**

The metal construction feels sturdier than the price point suggests. The canopy mounts flush to the ceiling and holds the weight securely. One thing buyers consistently mention is how bright the fixture is with the right bulbs — several noted they were surprised a plug-in light could illuminate a dining nook or kitchen island this well. As one reviewer put it, "I was skeptical about a plug-in chandelier, but this thing puts out serious light. My whole dining area is bright now."

The adjustable cord is the standout feature. You can trim or hide the excess above the canopy, so it doesn't dangle awkwardly. That flexibility makes it work in rooms with standard 8-foot ceilings or higher.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The most common theme in positive reviews is the ease of installation. Over and over, customers describe hanging it in under 15 minutes with zero tools. One buyer wrote, "I'm not handy at all and I had this up in 10 minutes. Just screw in the mount, hang it, plug it in." That's the core value proposition — accessible lighting for people who don't own a drill.

The aesthetic gets consistent love too. Multiple reviewers mention that guests assume it's hardwired, which tells you the cord doesn't scream "renter hack." The matte black finish pairs well with both industrial and farmhouse decor.

**Where it falls short**

The cord is the double-edged sword. While it's the feature that makes installation possible, it's also the biggest visual compromise. If your outlet isn't near the ceiling or the wall behind the fixture, you'll have visible wire running down your wall. Some buyers solved this with cord covers, but that's an extra purchase and an extra step. Be realistic about where your outlets are before you commit.

The on/off control is also worth noting. Because it plugs into a wall outlet, you're controlling it with the switch on the cord or the outlet itself — not your existing wall switch. That's a minor annoyance if you're used to flipping a switch by the door. You can add a smart plug to make it voice-controllable, but that's another expense.

**Who should buy this**

This is perfect for:
- **Renters** who want statement lighting without permanent changes
- **First-time apartment dwellers** who don't own tools or have never wired a fixture
- **Anyone** who wants a chandelier in a room without overhead wiring — think breakfast nooks, reading corners, or home offices
- **Design-focused shoppers** who want a specific look without the electrician bill

Skip it if you need dimmer compatibility or if your room layout forces the cord to run across a highly visible wall.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this with a dimmer switch?**
A: Not with your standard wall dimmer. You'd need a plug-in dimmer that sits between the wall outlet and the fixture's plug. It works, but it's an extra device to manage.

**Q: How many bulbs does it take, and what size?**
A: It uses standard E12 candelabra base bulbs. The number depends on the specific model you order, but most configurations in this line take 4-6 bulbs. LED bulbs are recommended — they stay cool and last longer.

**Q: Is the cord long enough for high ceilings?**
A: There's enough length for most standard rooms. For vaulted or very tall ceilings, you may need to contact the seller about extension options, but for typical 8-10 foot ceilings, you're covered.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want the look of a chandelier without the hassle, cost, or permanence of hardwiring. It's a genuinely well-built fixture that delivers on its core promise.

**Skip it if** you can't live with a visible cord, or you need dimming capability built in.

The LASENCHOO plug-in chandelier earns its 4.6 rating. It's not perfect — the cord management requires thought, and the plug-in control is a compromise. But for renters and DIY-avoiders, it's the best way to get high-end lighting without high-end installation costs. For the price, it delivers disproportionate style value.
