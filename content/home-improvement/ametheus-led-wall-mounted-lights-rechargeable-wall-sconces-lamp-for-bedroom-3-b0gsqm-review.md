If you've been staring at a dark hallway or bedside corner knowing the cost and hassle of hiring an electrician just to add a sconce, the AMETHEUS LED Wall Mounted Lights are worth your attention. These rechargeable fixtures skip the wiring entirely. No junction boxes, no voltage testers, no drywall patching. As shown in the product image, they're slim, modern fixtures that look permanently installed — until you pull them off the wall to recharge.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes two sconces, two USB-C charging cables, a metal mounting plate set, adhesive strips, screws with anchors, and a small remote control. The fixtures themselves are aluminum with a matte finish, measuring roughly 15.7 inches long and 4.3 inches tall. Each one weighs under two pounds, so the adhesive option is viable on smooth surfaces. The remote handles on/off, brightness, and color temperature changes from across the room — a genuinely useful addition at this price point.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The headline feature is the 2000mAh battery per fixture. At medium brightness (around 400 lumens), you'll get a solid 8–10 hours before needing to recharge. That's realistic for nightly use — charge once a week and you're fine. The three color temperatures let you switch from warm 3000K for bedtime to cool 6000K for reading or task lighting. Stepless dimming works smoothly via the touch sensor on the fixture itself or the remote.

Build quality is better than the price suggests. The aluminum housing dissipates heat well, and the diffuser panel spreads light evenly without harsh hotspots. The touch sensor is responsive — when it works. Mount the light too high and you'll be reaching awkwardly. Damp hands occasionally trigger it, which is worth knowing before you install one in a bathroom.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The overwhelmingly common thread in positive reviews is the installation simplicity. One buyer put it plainly: *"I'm renting and can't drill into the walls. These went up with the sticky pads and look like they were professionally installed."* Another noted the versatility: *"Moved them from the bedroom to the hallway when I redecorated — took two minutes to relocate."* The remote control gets frequent mentions too, particularly for use in bedrooms where you don't want to get out of bed to adjust lighting.

## Where It Falls Short

The adhesive backing is the weak point. Multiple reviewers report the pads failing within a few weeks on textured walls, wallpaper, or surfaces with even slight moisture. If your walls aren't perfectly smooth and clean, use the included screws from day one. The touch sensor placement is also slightly awkward — it's on the side of the fixture, which means you sometimes hit the wrong spot before finding it. And while 400 lumens is fine for ambient light, don't expect to read fine print comfortably with these at full brightness in a large room.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Renters** who want permanent-looking lighting without drilling or permanent modifications
- **Anyone avoiding electrician costs** — these cost a fraction of what hardwired sconces run after labor
- **Bedrooms and hallways** where you want adjustable ambient light, not task lighting
- **People who rearrange furniture often** — the portability is a genuine advantage

Skip them if you need bright task lighting for detailed work, or if your walls are heavily textured and you refuse to use screws.

## FAQ

**How long does the battery actually last?**
At medium brightness, expect 8–10 hours. At maximum brightness, that drops to about 5 hours. The standby time between charges is excellent — the light won't drain the battery when off.

**Can these be hardwired?**
No, they're strictly battery-powered. The whole point is wireless operation. If you want hardwired sconces, look elsewhere.

**Do they come with a remote?**
Yes, one remote controls both fixtures. It requires the included CR2032 battery and works from about 20 feet away.
