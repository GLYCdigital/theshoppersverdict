**Review Body**

You're staring at a kitchen full of dated brass pulls and a $40 budget. The BUKOBYHE 10-pack of 3-inch matte black knurled drawer pulls promises a full cabinet refresh without re-mortgaging your house. At 4.4 stars from 220 ratings, the consensus is clear: these are solid, but let's dig into whether they're *your* solid.

**What's actually in the box**

Ten pulls, each measuring 3 inches center-to-center between screw holes. The overall length runs about 4.5 inches tip to tip, with a 0.5-inch diameter bar. You get 20 mounting screws — ten 1-inch and ten 1.25-inch options — plus a small Allen wrench. The wrench matters because these aren't standard Phillips-head mounts; they use a hidden Allen set screw system that keeps the exterior completely clean. No visible hardware, which is a genuinely premium touch at this price point.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The bar itself is zinc alloy with a powder-coated matte black finish. The knurling — that diamond-pattern texture running along the grip area — is hand-polished before coating, which is why the finish adheres so well. One buyer noted, *"I've had these on my bathroom vanity for five months and they still look brand new. No chipping, no fading, even with daily water exposure."* That tracks with the construction; the coating is baked on, not painted, so it resists the usual flaking you see on cheap hardware.

Installation is genuinely straightforward. The Allen wrench fits snugly, the set screws bite into the bolt threads cleanly, and the included screws work with standard cabinet construction. A buyer with 1950s cabinets warned, *"The 1-inch screws were too short for my thick drawer fronts, but the 1.25-inch ones worked perfectly."* Good thing both lengths are included.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The finish consistency gets the most love. Multiple reviewers mention ordering these to match existing matte black fixtures and finding the shade nearly identical. The grip is the second most-cited win — the knurling provides real traction, which matters when you're opening drawers with damp hands after washing up. One reviewer summed it up: *"These look like they cost three times what I paid. My contractor asked where I got them."*

**Where it falls short**

The 3-inch center-to-center spacing is the elephant in the room. It's not the standard 3.5-inch or 4-inch spacing most cabinets use. If your existing pulls have different hole spacing, you're drilling new holes — and these pulls won't hide the old ones. Several reviewers hit this exact wall. Measure twice, order once.

The knurling is also subtler than product photos suggest. It's a gentle texture, not an aggressive grip pattern. If you're expecting a heavy tactile feel like on high-end machined pulls, you'll be slightly underwhelmed. It's decorative with functional benefits, not the reverse.

**Who should buy these**

You're the target buyer if you're doing a full-kitchen or bathroom refresh and want a consistent matte black look across 10 or more drawers. The 10-pack price per pull is excellent value. You're also a good fit if you're handy enough to drill new holes if needed and want a clean, screw-free exterior look. Skip these if you're replacing existing hardware without measuring first, or if you need heavy-duty industrial-grade pulls for a workshop environment — these are residential-grade, not commercial.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will these fit standard cabinet drawers?**
A: Yes, if your drawer fronts are between 0.5 and 0.75 inches thick. You get two screw lengths to cover both. For thicker custom fronts, you'll need longer screws from a hardware store.

**Q: How do I clean them without damaging the finish?**
A: Mild soap and water with a soft cloth. Avoid abrasive cleaners or scouring pads — the powder coating is durable but not indestructible. One buyer used a magic eraser and noticed slight dulling, so stick to gentle cleaning.

**Q: Can I use these on cabinets with existing 3-inch hole spacing?**
A: Yes, and that's the ideal scenario. They'll be a direct swap. If your current spacing is 3.5 inches or another size, you'll drill new holes and likely need to fill the old ones.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a cohesive matte black upgrade for under $4 per pull, appreciate hidden-screw aesthetics, and either have 3-inch spacing or are comfortable drilling new holes.

**Skip it if:** You need a standard 3.5-inch or 4-inch spacing, want aggressive machined knurling, or expect a commercial-grade product for residential prices.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The BUKOBYHE pulls deliver exceptional finish quality and value for the price. The non-standard spacing and softer-than-expected knurling keep it from perfection, but for a budget-friendly full-cabinet refresh, these are hard to beat. Just measure before you buy.
