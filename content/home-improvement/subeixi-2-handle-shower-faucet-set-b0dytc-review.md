The SUBEIXI 2 Handle Shower Faucet Set sits in that tricky middle ground: cheap enough to tempt you, substantial enough to make you wonder if it's actually decent. After digging through 210 ratings and the spec sheet, here's the real story.

**What's actually in the box**

You get the two-handle valve body, trim plates, handle levers, a pressure-balanced cartridge assembly, and the shower head with arm and flange. The rough-in valve uses standard 1/2-inch NPT connections on 8-inch centers, which matches typical US plumbing rough-in. As shown in the product image, the dual levers operate independently — one for volume, one for temperature — which is simpler than most people expect and easier for kids or elderly users to master than a single-lever joystick.

What's notably absent: a tub spout with diverter. If you're replacing a tub/shower combo, budget another $25–40 for a compatible spout. The shower head itself is a basic 1.8 GPM unit — functional, not luxurious.

**Build quality and performance**

Here's where SUBEIXI earns its keep. The valve body is solid brass, not the zinc alloy you'll find at this price point from lesser-known brands. The ceramic disc cartridges are rated for 500,000 cycles — that's roughly 15 years of daily use. The pressure-balanced valve maintains temperature within a couple degrees when someone flushes a toilet, which is the kind of thing you don't appreciate until you've been scalded.

The 1.8 GPM flow rate is the federal maximum and feels adequate for a shower, though if you have low water pressure (under 40 PSI), you'll notice the shower head's spray pattern thins out. The included head has rubber nozzles that are easy to wipe clean of mineral buildup — a small detail that matters in hard-water areas.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The recurring theme in positive reviews is the value proposition. One buyer noted, "Replaced a 20-year-old Moen with this and honestly can't tell the difference in water feel. The brass body is heavy and solid." Another mentioned the install process: "Took about two hours including removing the old valve. Threads were clean, no leaks on first test."

Multiple reviewers specifically called out the pressure balance as working better than expected at this price. That's the feature that typically gets skimped on in budget fixtures.

**Where it falls short**

The handle action is the most common complaint. Several reviewers describe it as "stiff" or "notchy" initially. It does break in, but if you're accustomed to a high-end brand's buttery-smooth levers, this will feel less refined. The finish is the other tradeoff — it's marketed as brushed nickel, but it reads more like a satin chrome with a warm tint. If you're matching an existing brushed nickel fixture set, hold them side by side before committing.

There's also the missing diverter spout issue. It's a minor annoyance, but it means the "set" isn't actually complete for a standard tub/shower setup. Factor that into your total cost.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for a rental property, a guest bathroom, or a DIY homeowner replacing a failing valve on a budget. It's also a solid choice if you're doing a full bathroom remodel and need to stretch the budget — the savings over a Delta or Kohler equivalent (typically $150–200 more) can fund a better shower head elsewhere. Skip it if you're a finish-matching perfectionist or if you want the buttery-smooth operation of a premium brand.

**FAQ**

**Does this fit standard US plumbing?** Yes — 8-inch center-to-center rough-in with 1/2-inch NPT threads is the US standard. No adapters needed for most homes.

**Is the pressure balance actually effective?** Yes. Multiple buyers confirmed temperature stability during toilet flushes. The ceramic cartridge does real work here.

**Can I install this myself?** If you're comfortable with soldering or using sharkbite fittings on copper, yes. You'll need access behind the wall. Budget 2–3 hours for a first-timer.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a solid brass valve with ceramic cartridges and pressure balancing at a genuinely fair price, and you don't mind the minor finish and handle stiffness quirks.

**Skip it if:** You're matching premium fixtures exactly, or you need a complete tub/shower package without buying additional parts.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A legitimately good value that undercuts the big brands without the cheap-plastic compromises you'd expect at this price. Just budget for a diverter spout and a little patience during the break-in period.
