Somewhere between "my doorbell got stolen" and "my doorbell sees nothing but the neighbor's mailbox" is where this mount lives. The Blink Doorbell Mount with anti-theft hardware tries to solve two problems at once, and for the most part, it delivers.

**What you actually get**

The package includes the aluminum mounting bracket, a wedge spacer for angle adjustment, security screws, and the proprietary wrench needed to tighten them. The bracket is CNC-machined rather than stamped, which shows in the fit — no sharp edges, no flex when you torque the screws down. It's designed specifically for the Blink Video Doorbell and the Plus model, so you're not dealing with a universal-fit compromise.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The aluminum construction is the right call here. Plastic mounts warp in direct sun and get brittle in freezing temps; this won't. The anti-theft mechanism relies on a custom screw head that requires the included wrench. A thief with a standard Phillips or flathead isn't getting this off without significant effort and noise.

The wedge design deserves more attention than it gets. Most people mount their doorbell flat against the wall, which means the motion sensor's field of view cuts off about six feet out. With the adjustable angle on this bracket, you can tilt the doorbell downward to actually catch someone approaching — not just their torso as they're already at your door. That's the difference between a clip of a package thief's face and a clip of their shoulder.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.4 average across 220 ratings reflects genuine satisfaction. One reviewer noted, "Installed in five minutes. The security screws give me peace of mind since my last doorbell got ripped off the wall." Another mentioned the angle adjustment fixing their blind spot: "Couldn't see the porch steps before. Tilted it down and now I get full coverage."

The security aspect is the headline feature, and it works. The screws require the specialty bit, which means your average opportunistic thief isn't carrying it.

**Where it falls short**

Losing the included wrench is a real risk. These aren't screws you can remove with a standard tool, so if the wrench goes missing during a move or a reinstall, you're ordering a replacement or drilling the screws out. Keep the wrench with your other doorbell tools, not in a drawer somewhere.

The lack of a weatherproof gasket is an oversight. The bracket mounts flush against your siding or brick, but water can seep behind it over time. A thin bead of exterior silicone around the edges solves this, but you shouldn't have to add that yourself.

Also worth noting: the wedge angle is fixed at set positions, not infinitely adjustable. You get a few preset tilt options, which covers most scenarios but won't be perfect for every porch layout.

**Who should buy this**

If your doorbell is ground-level accessible from the street or a sidewalk, this is worth the purchase. Theft prevention matters most when your front door is visible from public areas. If you're in a low-traffic area or your doorbell is already out of easy reach, the anti-theft feature is less critical — but the angle adjustment still improves motion detection.

Renters will appreciate that this installs with just the included screws and doesn't require hardwiring changes, so you're not patching holes when you move.

**FAQ**

**Will this fit my Blink Video Doorbell?**  
It fits the standard Blink Video Doorbell and the Plus model. It doesn't fit the Blink Mini or other camera models, so verify your device before ordering.

**Can I use my existing wiring with this mount?**  
Yes, there's a pass-through channel for the wiring. Just route the wires through the bracket before securing it to the wall.

**Does it work with the Blink chime?**  
The mount doesn't interfere with the chime at all — it's purely a physical bracket, so all your wireless and chime functions work as normal.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want theft protection for a ground-level doorbell, need better motion detection angles, or want a solid metal mount that'll outlast plastic alternatives.

**Skip it if:** You lose small tools easily (that wrench is critical), you're installing on an uneven surface that needs a gasket, or your doorbell is already in a hard-to-reach spot where theft isn't a concern.

This mount does what it claims, and the machining quality justifies the price. The security screws and angle adjustment are genuinely useful upgrades over the stock mounting setup. Just don't lose the wrench.
