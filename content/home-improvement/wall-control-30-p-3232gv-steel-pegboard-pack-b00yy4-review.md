## What You Actually Get

The pack includes one 32" x 32" steel pegboard and a starter kit of 30 accessories — hooks, straight and curved, plus a few small shelves and brackets. As shown in the product image, the board features Wall Control's signature slotted hole pattern (not round holes), which is the core of why this system outperforms standard pegboards. The galvanized finish gives it a brushed silver look that reads "industrial" without being ugly.

You'll also need to supply your own mounting hardware — the board doesn't include screws or wall anchors. That's a minor gripe, but worth knowing before checkout.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The defining difference here is the steel. At roughly 13 pounds, this board has serious heft. It won't bow in the middle when you load it with power tools. The slotted design locks hook heads in place — once seated, they don't pivot or rattle. That's a massive upgrade over round-hole boards where hooks spin freely and drop your tools at 2 a.m.

Mounting is straightforward but demands effort: you're driving through steel, so pre-drilling pilot holes is non-negotiable. And because it's heavy, you need to hit studs or use quality drywall anchors. Skip that, and you'll have a 13-pound steel slab on your floor.

The galvanized surface is durable and rust-resistant, which makes it a strong candidate for garages or basements with humidity swings. The tradeoff: it shows fingerprints and smudges easily. If you're obsessive about a clean-looking wall, you'll be wiping it down more than you'd like.
