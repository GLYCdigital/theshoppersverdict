# DEWALT 20V Max Cordless Drill Review: The People's Champion

When a power tool racks up 49,000+ reviews and holds a 4.8-star rating, you pay attention. The **DEWALT DCD771C2 20V Max Cordless Drill Driver Set** isn't the newest model in DEWALT's lineup, but it might be the best value. At $99 for a drill, two batteries, a charger, and a bag — it's the drill your neighbor, your dad, and probably your contractor all own.

We analyzed thousands of Amazon reviews to separate the hype from the reality.

## What Makes It Great

The overwhelming consensus: this drill punches above its weight class. Users consistently praise the build quality, with many noting their DCD771 has survived years of drops, dust, and daily abuse. "I've had mine for 5 years and it still runs like new," is a recurring theme across hundreds of reviews.

The lightweight design is another highlight. At just 3.6 lbs with the compact battery, it's comfortable for overhead work and doesn't fatigue your arm during long projects. The LED light above the trigger is a small touch that reviewers consistently mention as surprisingly useful in dim garages and under sinks.

At $99 for the full kit, the value proposition is hard to argue with. Several reviewers compared it favorably to drills costing twice as much, particularly for typical homeowner tasks like assembling furniture, hanging shelves, and light construction.

## What Could Be Better

The included 1.3Ah batteries are compact and light, but they won't last all day on demanding jobs. Professional users and serious DIYers often supplement with DEWALT's larger 4Ah or 5Ah packs. The brushed motor — while proven and durable — isn't as efficient as the brushless motors in DEWALT's premium XR line.

The key limitation: there's no hammer function. If you're drilling into concrete, brick, or masonry, you'll need a hammer drill. For wood, drywall, metal, and plastic, however, this tool handles everything with ease.

## The Verdict

The DEWALT DCD771C2 is the archetypal "buy once, cry once" tool — except at $99, you won't even cry. It's the drill driver that turned millions of homeowners into weekend warriors and earned its 4.8-star reputation honestly. If you need a reliable, well-built cordless drill and you're not drilling into concrete, your search ends here.

**Verdict: 9/10** — The knockout value champion of cordless drills.
