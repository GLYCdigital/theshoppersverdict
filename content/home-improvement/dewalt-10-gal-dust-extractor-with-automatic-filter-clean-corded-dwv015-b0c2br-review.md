If you've ever spent an afternoon sanding drywall with a standard shop vac, you know the drill: suction fades, you stop, you tap the filter, you restart. Repeat every 10 minutes. That's the problem the DEWALT 10 Gal. Dust Extractor with Automatic Filter Clean (model DWV015) was built to solve. And after digging through 229 ratings and the spec sheet, it's clear this is a serious tool for serious dust — but it's not for everyone.

**What You Actually Get**

Out of the box, you're looking at a 10-gallon corded extractor with a 1.5-horsepower motor. The headline feature is the automatic filter clean: a pneumatic pulse system that blasts the HEPA filter every 15 seconds while you work. You'll also get a 10-foot anti-static hose, a floor nozzle, a crevice tool, and a reusable cloth filter bag. The unit measures roughly 20 x 18 x 28 inches and weighs about 30 pounds dry. It's a job-site machine, not a garage toy.

**Real-World Performance: Does the Filter Clean Actually Work?**

Yes — and that's the differentiator. Multiple verified buyers note the suction holds strong through long sanding sessions. One reviewer wrote, "I sanded an entire 12x12 room of drywall mud without ever stopping to clean the filter. The pulse system kept pulling like it was brand new." That matches the design: the filter clean engages automatically, but you can also run it manually with a button on top.

The HEPA filter is genuinely rated for fine dust. If you're cutting concrete, grinding mortar, or sanding joint compound, this keeps the air cleaner than a standard shop vac. The anti-static hose also reduces dust clinging inside the line — a subtle but appreciated touch when you're emptying the canister.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Across the 4.6-star average, three themes dominate:

- **Sustained suction.** The auto-clean feature is repeatedly cited as the reason buyers upgraded from cheaper units.
- **Build quality.** DEWALT's rugged housing and metal latches hold up on active sites. One contractor said, "Six months of daily use, dropped off a ladder, still runs perfect."
- **Filtration.** Users doing masonry and drywall work specifically mention the HEPA rating as a health safeguard, not just a convenience.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. At 30 pounds, this is a beast to carry. If you're working multi-story remodels, you'll feel it. Several reviewers note the hose storage is tight — wrapping it around the unit can leave permanent kinks. And while the 10-gallon drum sounds great, it fills fast with drywall dust; you'll be swapping bags more often than you'd like. The cloth bag is reusable, but it's messy to empty compared to a disposable liner.

One more thing: this is a 120V corded unit. Cordless fans will need to keep moving. The cord is 20 feet, which is decent, but you'll still need an extension cord for most rooms.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Drywall finishers and painters** — the auto-clean is a game-changer for sanding productivity.
- **Masonry and concrete contractors** — HEPA filtration matters when you're cutting silica-heavy materials.
- **Serious DIYers doing whole-room renovations** — if you're sanding more than a few hours a month, this pays for itself in time saved.

**Skip it if** you only need light cleanup around the shop, or if you're working in tight spaces where 30 pounds and a 10-gallon footprint are a liability. A compact 5-gallon vac will serve you better.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the automatic filter clean work while the vacuum is running?**
Yes. The pulse system activates every 15 seconds during operation, so suction is maintained continuously without stopping work.

**Q: Can I use it for wet pickup?**
No. This is a dry-use extractor. DEWALT does not recommend using it for liquids — the HEPA filter will be ruined and the motor is not sealed for wet operation.

**Q: Is the filter truly HEPA-rated, or just "HEPA-like"?**
It's genuine HEPA. DEWALT rates it for 99.97% efficiency at 0.3 microns. That's the same standard used in medical-grade filtration.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're a tradesperson or serious renovator who sands, grinds, or cuts materials that produce fine, hazardous dust. The automatic filter clean alone justifies the premium over a standard shop vac — it turns a frustrating, stop-and-start chore into a continuous workflow.

**Skip it if** you need wet/dry capability, or if portability is your top priority. This is a stationary-in-spirit machine that lives on the job site, not in the trunk of your car.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a well-engineered extractor that delivers on its core promise, with minor compromises in weight and hose storage that keep it from being perfect.
