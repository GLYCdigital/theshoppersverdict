## Performance and Build Quality

Here's where the double-head design earns its keep. On a recent 150-foot pull through 1.5-inch EMT with three 90s, I used one end attached to the fish tape and the other to the pulling rope. When the rope twisted and bound mid-pull, I swapped ends without cutting anything loose. That's the hidden value: you're never fighting the direction of your pull.

The grip tension is aggressive without being destructive. It bit into a 4/0 aluminum feeder cable without leaving the telltale spiral scoring marks you get from cheaper grips with wider mesh gaps. One thing worth noting: this is a grip for light-to-medium pulls. It's not a come-along replacement for heavy overhead pulls. The mesh will handle the tension, but the loop eyes are sized for hand-pulling and capstan work, not winch-rated loads. Know your limits.
