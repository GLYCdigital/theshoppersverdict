If you've ever stood in a bathroom where the vanity light cast shadows across your face, you know the struggle. The Possini Euro Design Midtown bath light attacks that problem head-on with downward-angled glass shades that put illumination where it belongs. At 23.5 inches wide and 7 inches tall, this two-light fixture is a compact workhorse that's earned a 4.5-star average across 225 Amazon ratings. But is it the right fixture for your remodel? Let's dig in.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the assembled fixture, mounting hardware, and installation instructions — but no bulbs. The fixture uses two standard A19 bulbs (max 60W each) with medium E26 bases, so you won't need any special adapters. The backplate measures roughly 5 inches wide and 4.5 inches tall, which covers most standard junction boxes cleanly. The overall projection from the wall is about 6.75 inches, so it won't feel like it's jutting into your walkway in a tight bathroom.

## Build Quality and Installation

This is where the Midtown earns its keep. The frame is solid steel — not the flimsy stamped aluminum you find on budget fixtures — and the chrome plating is genuinely polished, not just sprayed on. Several reviewers specifically noted the weight of the unit, with one commenting, "The quality is excellent for the price, heavy and well made." That heft translates to a fixture that feels anchored to the wall, not decorative plastic.

Installation runs 30-45 minutes for a competent DIYer. The mounting bracket attaches to a standard 4-inch round or 4-inch octagonal junction box. Wires connect via wire nuts (included), and the glass shades slip over the bulb holders and secure with small screws. The one hiccup multiple buyers mentioned: the screws that hold the shades are tiny and easy to drop behind the vanity. Go slow, use a magnetic screwdriver, and you'll be fine.

## Real-World Performance

The downward-angled shades make a noticeable difference. Instead of washing light across the ceiling, the Midtown throws illumination onto the sink area and your face. For a 24-30 inch mirror, this fixture provides even, glare-free light — especially when paired with 2700K-3000K LED bulbs. One reviewer captured it well: "Great light, looks much more expensive than it is. The chrome is beautiful and the glass shades give off a nice warm glow."

The compact footprint is genuinely useful for powder rooms or half baths where you don't want a 36-inch light bar dominating the space. It also pairs well with brushed nickel or chrome faucets because the polished finish picks up and reflects the surrounding fixtures.

## Where It Falls Short

No fixture is perfect, and the Midtown has two legitimate drawbacks. First, the chrome finish is a fingerprint magnet. If you have kids or hard water, you'll be wiping it down regularly to keep it looking showroom-clean. Second, the glass shades are fixed at a set angle — you can't tilt them independently. If your mirror is mounted unusually high or low, you're stuck with the factory angle.

Also worth noting: the fixture pulls only 120V and is rated for damp locations, not wet. That means it's fine right above a bathroom sink but not inside a shower stall or directly over a tub where water splashes directly on the glass.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're remodeling a powder room or guest bath and want an understated, quality fixture that won't break the budget. You appreciate solid steel over lightweight alloy, and you're pairing it with a 24-30 inch mirror. You're comfortable with basic wiring.

**Skip it if:** You have hard water that leaves mineral deposits on chrome quickly, or you need a fixture with adjustable shade angles. If you're lighting a 48-inch double vanity, step up to a three-light version.

## FAQ

**Q: What bulbs should I use?**
A: Use LED A19 bulbs rated 60W equivalent or less. Stick with 2700K-3000K for a warm bathroom tone. Avoid oversized bulbs — the shades are only about 5 inches deep, and bigger bulbs will crowd the glass.

**Q: Is this fixture dimmable?**
A: The fixture itself is dimmable, but only if you use dimmable LED bulbs and a compatible dimmer switch. Standard non-dimmable LEDs will flicker or hum.

**Q: Can I install this vertically?**
A: No. The shades are designed for horizontal mounting with downward light throw. Installing it vertically would leave the shades pointing sideways and waste the light output.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The Possini Euro Midtown delivers exactly what it promises: a solid, attractive bath light with smart light direction and quality construction. The fingerprint-prone chrome and fixed shade angles are minor annoyances, not dealbreakers. At this price point, you'd have to spend nearly double to get noticeably better build quality from a big-box brand.

**Buy it if** you want a no-nonsense, well-built fixture that flatters your face and your bathroom. **Skip it if** you need adjustability or hate maintaining polished chrome. For everyone else, this is a reliable, good-looking choice that's earned its 4.5-star reputation.
