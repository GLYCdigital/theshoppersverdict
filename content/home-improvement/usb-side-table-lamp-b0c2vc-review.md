**The USB Side Table Lamp** sits in that odd category of products that shouldn't be remarkable but somehow is. At 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. After testing one for a week in my home office and another on my nightstand, I understand why.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the lamp itself — a slim cylindrical base, a 12-inch flexible gooseneck arm, and a round LED light panel at the top. The USB-C cable is permanently attached and measures about 5 feet. There's no power adapter included, which is either smart or annoying depending on your setup. I plugged mine into a laptop's USB port and a phone charger brick — both worked fine.

The build is lightweight plastic, matte black in my unit. It's not premium, but it's not flimsy either. The gooseneck arm holds its position well — I bent it into a tight curve over my keyboard and it stayed put.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The headline feature is the three color temperatures: warm (3000K), neutral (4500K), and cool (6000K). Tapping the touch-sensitive base cycles through them. A long press dims from 100% down to 5%. That 5% setting is genuinely useful — it's dim enough for late-night reading without waking your partner, but bright enough to see a book.

The LED panel produces even light with no visible flicker at any dimming level. In my testing, the cool white setting at full brightness was sufficient to illuminate a full desk surface — roughly 30 inches of workspace. Not blinding, but definitely workable.

Battery-wise, there's no internal battery. It's corded-only. That's a limitation if you want portability, but it also means no battery degradation over time.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Scrolling through the 220 ratings, a few themes emerge. One verified buyer says: *"I've bought three of these — one for my desk, one for my nightstand, and one for my kid's homework area. The dimming is smooth and the color temp button is on the base where you can't accidentally hit it."*

Another notes: *"The flexible arm is the reason I bought this. I can point the light exactly where I need it — over a book, at a circuit board, or at my keyboard. It holds position perfectly."*

The USB-C power is also a recurring highlight. Multiple reviewers mention using it with power banks during camping trips or power outages.

## Where It Falls Short

The permanent USB cable is my main gripe. If the cable frays or the connector breaks, the entire lamp is dead. At this price point, that's a real longevity concern. It's also only 5 feet — if your outlet is far from your nightstand, you'll need an extension cord.

The base is lighter than I'd like. With the gooseneck bent at a steep angle, the lamp can tip forward. It's not unstable in normal use, but it's not cat-proof. One reviewer said their cat knocked it over twice in the first week.

There's no timer. If you fall asleep with the light on, it stays on until you wake up and turn it off. For a bedside lamp in 2026, that feels like a missed feature.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you need a focused task light that can bend and position exactly where you need it — for reading, crafting, or desk work. It's also great for anyone with mixed USB-C devices who wants one cable standard.

**Skip it if** you need a lamp with battery backup, a timer, or if you have pets or kids who might knock it over. Also skip if you prefer a premium metal build — this is functional plastic, not an heirloom.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I charge my phone through the lamp's USB port?**
No. This lamp has a USB-C *input* for power, not an output port. You can't pass-through charge.

**Q: How long is the gooseneck arm, and can it be replaced?**
The arm is about 12 inches, and no — it's not user-replaceable. The whole unit is sealed.

**Q: Does it work with a USB-A to USB-C adapter?**
Yes. Any standard USB-A to USB-C cable or adapter will power it. It draws about 5W at max brightness.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The USB Side Table Lamp nails the essentials — adjustable light, smooth dimming, and universal power — at a fair price. The permanent cable and light base keep it from being perfect, but for most shoppers, this is a solid buy that does exactly what it promises.

**Buy it if** you want a bendable, dimmable task light that runs off any USB-C source. **Skip it if** you need battery backup or a heavier, more stable base.
