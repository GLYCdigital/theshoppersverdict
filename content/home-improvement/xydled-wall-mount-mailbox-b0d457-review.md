This is where the xydled separates itself from the $25 plastic specials. **Where it falls short**

Also, the included mounting screws are zinc-plated, not stainless. In coastal or high-humidity areas, replace them with marine-grade bolts. That’s a $3 fix, but worth knowing before you mount it.
