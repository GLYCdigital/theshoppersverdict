If you've ever tried to mount a Logitech Rally or MeetUp camera system to a TV that's already on a wall mount, you've hit the same wall I did: there's nowhere to put it. The Logitech Mounting Bar solves that specific, frustrating problem—and it does so with an overengineered simplicity that's rare in the AV accessory world.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains a single steel mounting bar, roughly 24 inches across, with pre-drilled VESA hole patterns at 400x200 and 400x300 spacing. It's designed to bolt between your TV and its existing wall mount, extending outward to create a platform for your conferencing system. The bar weighs about 3 pounds and has a matte black finish that won't look out of place behind any display. There's a built-in cable channel running along the top edge, which is a thoughtful touch for keeping your camera's USB and power cables organized.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

This is where the Logitech Mounting Bar earns its keep. The steel is thick—not stamped tin like some budget alternatives—and the load rating of 44 pounds gives you serious headroom. A Rally system with its speaker and mic pod will sit comfortably without any sag. The VESA holes line up precisely with standard TV mount brackets, and once you've threaded the included bolts through, the whole assembly feels rock solid.

One thing to note: this bar doesn't work with every TV mount. It requires a mount with exposed VESA holes on the backplate. If your wall mount has a solid back panel without accessible holes, you'll need a different solution. That's a real limitation worth checking before you order.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the compatibility issue, the bar only ships with hardware for Logitech's own camera systems. If you're attaching a third-party conferencing bar or a surveillance camera, you'll need to supply your own bolts. That's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you start the install. Also, the bar is designed for horizontal mounting only—if you need vertical orientation, this isn't your product.

## Who Should Buy This

This bar is for anyone with a Logitech Rally, MeetUp, or similar conferencing system mounted to a wall-mounted TV. It's also a solid option for surveillance camera installers who need a stable platform above or below a display. If you're using a cart or floor stand instead of a wall mount, skip this—you don't need it.

## FAQ

**Will this fit my Samsung TV mount?**
It depends on your mount's VESA pattern. If your mount has exposed holes at 400x200 or 400x300 spacing, yes. Check your mount's backplate before ordering.

**Can I mount a non-Logitech camera on it?**
Yes, but you'll need your own bolts. The bar's holes are standard VESA spacing, so any camera with compatible mounting holes will work.

**Does it interfere with TV tilt or swivel?**
No, the bar sits between the mount and TV, so all mount articulation remains fully functional.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You need a sturdy, clean-looking way to mount a Logitech conferencing system to a wall-mounted TV, and your mount has compatible VESA holes.

**Skip it if:** Your TV mount lacks exposed VESA holes, or you're mounting to a cart or stand.
