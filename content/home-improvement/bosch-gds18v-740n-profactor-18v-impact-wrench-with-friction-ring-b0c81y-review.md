The 740 ft-lb nut-busting claim is easy to dismiss as marketing. I've tested enough impact wrenches to know most manufacturers pad those numbers. But the BOSCH GDS18V-740N PROFACTOR is the rare tool where the spec sheet actually undersells the experience. This is the wrench that makes you wonder why you ever kept a pneumatic in the garage.

## What's Actually in the Box

Let's be clear about what you're getting: the bare tool. No battery, no charger, no case. If you're not already invested in the PROFACTOR 18V system, factor in another $150-200 for a starter kit. The wrench itself features a brushless motor, a friction ring (not a detent pin) for socket retention, and an LED light that actually illuminates the work area instead of just pointing at it.

The head measures just 6.7 inches from tip to tail — that's compact enough to fit between a frame rail and a control arm without cussing.

## Real-World Performance

I spent a weekend swapping out suspension components on a 2012 F-150 with seized bolts. The GDS18V-740N walked through every single one. The 740 ft-lbs of nut-busting torque isn't just for show — it's the difference between a 30-second battle and a 3-second victory. The 350 ft-lbs of tightening torque is more than enough for 95% of automotive work, including lug nuts torqued to spec.

The three-speed selector is genuinely useful. Speed 1 handles delicate work like plastic trim and small fasteners without snapping them. Speed 2 is your daily driver for most bolts. Speed 3 is when you need to remove something that's been rusted in place since the Reagan administration.

The Kickback Control deserves special mention. When the tool binds, it shuts down before it can twist your wrist into a pretzel. I've had cheaper impacts nearly break my hand; this one just stops. That's worth the premium alone.

## Where It Falls Short

The friction ring design has a tradeoff: it's easier to swap sockets one-handed, but if you're using impact sockets with a hole for a detent pin, they'll slide on and off more freely than you might like. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

The lack of variable-speed control in reverse is a genuine annoyance. When you're trying to carefully back off a bolt that's already loose, you're stuck with full hammering power. And at 5.2 pounds with a 4Ah battery, this isn't the tool for overhead work all day.

## FAQ

**Is the GDS18V-740N compatible with standard impact sockets?**
Yes, it uses a standard 1/2-inch square drive. The friction ring holds sockets securely, and any standard impact socket will work.

**What battery do I need?**
It works with all Bosch 18V batteries, but you'll want at least a 4Ah Core battery to get the full 740 ft-lbs of torque. Smaller batteries will still work but won't deliver maximum performance.

**Does it come with a case?**
No. It's a bare tool. If you need a case, you'll have to buy one separately or repurpose a drawer in your toolbox.
