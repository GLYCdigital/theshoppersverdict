Some exterior lights scream "builder grade." Others whisper "I cost more than I actually did." The DEWENWILS Black Roman wall light sits firmly in the second camp — and at this price point, that's a rare thing. I've installed enough fixtures to know the difference between a $30 light that looks like $30 and one that looks like $80. This is the latter.

## What's actually in the box

You get two complete fixtures — the listing is for a pair, which matters for symmetry on either side of a garage or front door. Each fixture is a two-light, up-down design: one bulb socket facing up, one facing down, both tucked under clean Roman arches. The housing is die-cast aluminum with a matte black finish, and the glass is clear, not seeded or frosted. That's important because it means you see the actual bulbs — so pick accordingly.

The mounting hardware is included and standard enough that you won't need a trip to the hardware store. The fixture measures 17.7 inches high by about 7.5 inches wide, with a 5.5-inch projection from the wall. That projection is worth noting — it's shallow enough to clear most door casings but deep enough to cast real light pools on the wall.

## Build quality and real-world performance

The aluminum housing is the headline here. DEWENWILS didn't cut corners with thin stamped steel or plastic. The die-cast body has actual heft, and the black finish is baked on, not sprayed. One reviewer put it plainly: "These look and feel like fixtures three times the price. The metal is thick and the finish is smooth with no runs or drips." That matches what I found — no sharp edges, no misaligned seams, and gaskets that actually seal.

The E26 sockets are a quiet win. They're ceramic, not the cheap plastic ones that crack after a season of heat cycling. And because they're standard size, you're not locked into proprietary bulbs. Pop in two warm LED filament bulbs and you get that vintage gas-lamp glow. Want security brightness? Screw in two 100-watt-equivalent LEDs and light up the whole driveway. One buyer noted: "I used smart bulbs and now they integrate with my Alexa routine. The fixture itself is just a shell — the possibilities are endless."

Installation runs about 20-30 minutes per fixture if you're reasonably handy. The mounting plate is pre-attached, the wires are color-coded, and the screws align without fighting you. The one gripe I'll echo from the reviews: the clear glass means any debris inside is on display. If you install during pollen season, you'll see every speck. Plan to clean the inside before you mount it, or you'll be taking it back down.

## Where it falls short

Honest tradeoffs exist. The clear glass is the biggest one — it's beautiful when it's clean, but it shows every water spot and bug corpse. If you're in a dusty area or a bug-heavy climate, budget for occasional cleaning. Also, there's no dusk-to-dawn sensor built in. That's not a flaw at this price, but if you wanted automation, you'll need smart bulbs or an exterior timer. And while the finish is durable, it's matte — so it will show chalky residue if you live near the ocean. A quick wipe with a damp cloth handles it, but it's worth knowing.

## FAQ

**Q: What bulbs should I use?**
A: Any standard E26 bulb. For curb appeal, use vintage-style LED filament bulbs in warm white (2700K). For security, use 800+ lumen LEDs. Avoid bulbs over 60W actual draw — the fixture is rated for that.

**Q: Is this fixture wet-rated for direct rain exposure?**
A: Yes, it's wet-rated with sealed gaskets. Multiple reviewers report it surviving heavy storms and coastal weather without water intrusion.

**Q: Can I install it on stucco or brick?**
A: Yes, but you'll need a masonry drill bit and anchors. The included hardware is for wood studs. Use proper masonry anchors and it'll be rock solid.
