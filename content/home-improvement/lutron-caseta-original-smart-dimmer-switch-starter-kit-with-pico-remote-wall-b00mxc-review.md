Some smart home products feel like they were designed by software engineers who've never touched a wall box. The Lutron Caseta Original Starter Kit isn't one of them. After 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, this is the dimmer switch that keeps showing up in "just buy this one" threads — and for good reason.

**What's actually in the box**

You get the Caseta dimmer switch itself (the standard PD-6WCL model), a Pico remote control, a wall plate bracket for the Pico, and the required screw mounts. The dimmer is rated for 150W of LED/CFL load or 600W incandescent — enough for most residential circuits. The Pico remote is the unsung hero here: it's a battery-powered RF remote that can mount anywhere, no wiring needed. The kit also includes the essential LUT-MLC (minimum load capacitor) for older homes with problematic neutral wiring, which tells you Lutron actually thought about real-world installs.

**Performance and build quality**

The Caseta system uses Lutron's proprietary Clear Connect RF protocol rather than Wi-Fi. That's a deliberate choice — it operates on a dedicated frequency band, so it doesn't fight your router for bandwidth. In practice, this means the switch responds instantly when you tap the Pico remote or the app, and it stays responsive even when your internet drops. That's something the Wi-Fi-only smart switches on the market can't claim.

Dimming performance is where this switch separates from the budget pack. The leading-edge dimming engine handles low-quality LED bulbs without the annoying buzz or visible flicker that plagues cheaper dimmers. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: "I've tried three other smart dimmers and returned all of them. This one just works. No flicker, no buzzing, no nonsense." Another noted, "The Pico remote is genius. I mounted one by my bed and one in the hallway — no need to run wires."

The build quality is typical Lutron: solid, tactile toggle feel, and the switch has a clean, low-profile face that doesn't look out of place in a modern home. The app is functional but not flashy — you can set schedules, create scenes, and control from anywhere. It's not the prettiest app in the smart home space, but it's reliable, and that matters more.

**Where it falls short**

The neutral wire requirement is the big one. The Caseta dimmer needs a neutral wire in your wall box. If your home is from the 1960s or earlier, you might not have one, and that means an electrician visit. The included LUT-MLC helps in some cases, but it's not a universal fix. Also, the kit includes just one switch. If you're outfitting a whole house, you're looking at $50+ per additional switch, which gets expensive fast.

Another gap: there's no onboard scheduling. Everything lives in the app or requires the separate Smart Bridge hub (which is not included — this kit is switch + remote only, no hub). If you want voice control via Alexa or Google Home, you'll need to buy the hub version of the kit or add the hub separately. That's an extra cost that surprises some buyers.

**Who should buy this**

- **The "I just want it to work" homeowner:** If you're tired of fiddling with finicky smart switches, this is your product. It's set-and-forget reliable.
- **Anyone with a multi-gang setup:** The Pico remote replaces the need to run traveler wires for three-way configurations. It's a massive time-saver for renovations.
- **LED bulb users with old wiring:** The flicker-free dimming is a genuine lifesaver if you have problematic LED bulbs.

**Skip it if** you need a hubless system with voice control out of the box, or if you're on a strict budget for multiple switches.

**FAQ**

*Does the Pico remote need a hub?* No. It pairs directly with the dimmer switch via RF. It works out of the box without a Smart Bridge. You only need the hub for app access and voice control.

*Will this work with a 3-way setup?* Yes, but differently than a traditional switch. You replace one switch with the Caseta dimmer and use the Pico remote (mounted in the other gang box) as the second control point. No traveler wire needed.

*Is it compatible with Apple HomeKit?* Yes, but only if you have the Smart Bridge hub (sold separately). The switch itself is HomeKit-compatible via the bridge.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a smart dimmer that never drops off your network, dims LEDs without flicker, and comes with a remote that works even during an internet outage. **Skip it if** you don't have a neutral wire, need voice control without buying a hub, or only want one smart switch and can't justify the premium.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — This is the dimmer that made me stop recommending the cheaper alternatives. The price stings a little, but the reliability is worth every penny.
