**The Short Version**

If you've got wooden fence posts, deck posts, or mailbox posts that vanish into the darkness at night, the Greluna Solar Post Lights are a solid, low-commitment upgrade. They're not the brightest or the most feature-packed option on the market, but they nail the basics: easy install, decent build quality, and reliable warm lighting that doesn't scream "cheap solar gadget." At 4.4 stars from 220 ratings, buyers mostly agree.

---

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains two complete post light units (assuming you grab the standard 2-pack), each with an integrated solar panel, rechargeable battery, and the lamp head. The mounting hardware is where Greluna earns points — instead of forcing you to drill into the post top, the bracket slides over the wood and tightens with screws. It's a genuinely tool-free setup that takes under five minutes per light. As shown in the product image, the design is clean and low-profile, sitting flush on the post rather than looking like a bolted-on afterthought.

The housing is die-cast aluminum with a tempered glass lens, not the flimsy plastic you see on budget competitors. That matters for anyone leaving these outdoors year-round.

---

**Performance: Real-World Light Output**

The 3000K warm white LEDs put out a pleasant, amber-toned glow — think classic porch light, not stadium floodlight. It's enough to mark your property line, illuminate a walkway, or prevent guests from tripping over that uneven patio stone. What it won't do is light up a large backyard. These are accent lights, and that's fine if you set expectations accordingly.

Battery performance is the predictable weak spot. On a full summer day of direct sun, you'll get 8-10 hours of runtime. Come winter, or if your posts sit in partial shade, that drops to 4-6 hours. One reviewer noted, *"They work great from May through September, but by December I'm lucky to get three hours before they dim out."* That's the nature of solar, but worth knowing before you buy.

---

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

The installation ease gets consistent shout-outs. Multiple reviewers mention having them up in under ten minutes with zero wiring knowledge. The build quality also earns repeated compliments — several owners report these surviving hail, snow, and 90-degree summers without cracking or discoloring. As one buyer put it, *"I've been through three sets of cheap solar lights from big box stores. These actually look like they'll last."*

---

**Where It Falls Short**

The light sensor is trigger-happy. If your post sits near a porch light or under a tree canopy, the lights may click on earlier than they should — sometimes a full hour before sunset. There's no way to adjust sensitivity, so you're stuck with the factory setting.

Also, the mounting screws are small and easy to strip if you over-tighten. Not a dealbreaker, but go easy with the screwdriver.

---

**Who Should Buy These**

- **Fence-line homeowners** who want subtle perimeter lighting without running conduit.
- **Deck owners** who want post caps that double as functional lights.
- **Mailbox post owners** who want visibility for delivery drivers without harsh glare.

Skip them if you need serious security lighting or live in a region with heavy overcast winters — you'll be frustrated by the reduced output.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Do these fit 4x4 posts only, or can they handle 6x6?**
A: The adjustable bracket accommodates both 4x4 and 6x6 wooden posts. Just loosen the screws, slide it over, and re-tighten.

**Q: How long do the batteries last before replacement?**
A: Most users report 1-2 seasons before noticeable capacity loss. The batteries are replaceable, though you'll need to open the housing.

**Q: Can these be mounted on vinyl or metal posts?**
A: No — the bracket is designed specifically for wooden posts. The screws won't bite into vinyl or metal.

**Skip it if** you need dependable year-round illumination or your posts are in consistently shady spots. These are seasonal performers, not all-weather workhorses.
