Let me be direct: when a faucet costs less than $150 and promises a brushed gold finish that won't peel in two years, I'm skeptical. The Delta Albion 3-hole bathroom faucet has been on the market long enough to gather 210 ratings and hold a 4.2-star average. Here's what that rating actually means in practice.

## What's Actually in the Box

You're getting the faucet body with integrated handles (the 3-hole spread is 8 inches, not the more common 4-inch centerset), a matching metal pop-up drain assembly, and supply lines. The drain inclusion matters — Delta could have shipped a plastic stopper and called it a day, but the metal unit matches the gold finish and feels solid in hand. The faucet itself is all-metal construction; no hollow-feeling zinc alloy here.

## Build Quality and the Finish Question

The Albion's party trick is the brushed gold PVD finish. PVD (physical vapor deposition) is the same process used on high-end fixtures — it bonds the color to the metal rather than painting it on top. In practice, this means the finish handles daily wiping, toothpaste splatter, and cleaning products without dulling.

One buyer noted: *"I've had this for 8 months and it still looks brand new. My kids are rough on it and I haven't seen any scratching."* That tracks with the PVD claim.

The Diamond Seal technology is worth mentioning too — instead of a rubber washer that degrades over time, Delta uses a diamond-like carbon coating on the valve. It's the kind of feature you won't appreciate until five years from now when the faucet still doesn't drip.

## Where It Falls Short

The 8-inch centerset requirement is the biggest gotcha. If your vanity has a standard 4-inch centerset configuration, this faucet will not fit without a deck plate (which Delta doesn't include). Measure your sink hole spacing before ordering — this is the #1 reason for returns based on the review patterns.

Second, the "brushed gold" color has a warm, slightly champagne undertone. In bright daylight it reads as gold; under warm LED bulbs it can look closer to brass. If you're matching existing gold fixtures, order a sample or check the color match carefully.

A few buyers also mentioned the handles feel slightly loose on initial install until you tighten the set screws properly — not a defect, but worth knowing so you don't panic during setup.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The style-conscious renovator**: If you're doing a modern farmhouse or transitional bathroom, the Albion's rounded silhouette and gold finish photograph beautifully
- **The low-maintenance buyer**: PVD finish means you're not hand-polishing gold-toned metal every week
- **Budget realists**: You're getting Delta's warranty and reliability without paying Kohler or Brizo prices

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my existing single-hole faucet setup?**
No. This requires three separate holes spaced 8 inches apart. If you have a single-hole sink, you'll need a deck plate (not included) or a different faucet.

**Q: Does the finish really not scratch?**
PVD is durable, but it's not diamond. Normal use won't scratch it, but abrasive cleaners or metal scrubbers will. Use mild soap and water.

**Q: What's the warranty situation?**
Delta offers a limited lifetime warranty that covers finish defects and parts — but not labor or installation costs. Keep your receipt.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you have an 8-inch centerset sink and want a gold-toned faucet that will actually hold up to daily use without looking worn in a year.

**Skip it if** you're working with a 4-inch centerset, or if you need a true, bright yellow-gold finish — the Albion leans warm/champagne.

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The Delta Albion is a well-engineered faucet that solves the two biggest complaints in the gold-faucet category: finish durability and leak-prone internals. The 8-inch spacing requirement is a genuine limitation, but for the right installation, this is a solid value that should serve you for years.
