If you've ever replaced a boob light or hung a pendant only to find the canopy won't cover the mess of drywall patches and paint lines left behind, you know the struggle. The MOYOMAJO 5-5/16 Inch Pendant Light Canopy Kit is aimed squarely at that problem. It's a simple piece of hardware — but it does its one job well enough to earn a 4.6-star average from 229 buyers.

**What you actually get**

The kit ships with the canopy itself, a mounting strap (crossbar), a threaded nipple, locknut, and the set screws you'll need to secure the shade or pendant rod. The canopy measures 5-5/16 inches in diameter — that's roughly 135mm — which is noticeably wider than the standard 4-inch canopies most budget fixtures use. That extra inch and a third is the whole selling point here.

The body is machined aluminum with a powder-coated finish, available in several colors depending on the listing variant you choose. As shown in the product image, it's a low-profile dome shape with a clean, modern lip — not the sharp-edged metal disc you sometimes get at this price point.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

Install is straightforward if you've wired a fixture before. The mounting strap spans standard 3.5-inch and 4-inch octagon junction boxes, and the threaded nipple gives you a solid attachment point. A buyer named Tom noted, "Fits perfectly over the old hole left by a flush mount. The finish matches my matte black pendants exactly." That's the core use case: covering imperfections while looking intentional.

The set-screw retention system holds the canopy tight against the ceiling. But here's the honest tradeoff: for heavier pendants — anything over 8 pounds — I'd apply a dab of thread-locker to the set screws. A few reviewers mentioned needing to retighten after a few weeks. That's not a dealbreaker; it's just the nature of set-screw designs at this price point.

The one thing I'll flag that the listing doesn't emphasize: there's no rubber gasket between the canopy rim and the ceiling. On a flat, finished ceiling you won't notice. On textured or slightly uneven ceilings, you may see a hairline gap. Not a functional problem, but worth knowing if you're a perfectionist.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Scrolling through the 229 ratings, three themes dominate:

- **Coverage**: "Finally a canopy that covers my 6-inch patched hole from an old ceiling fan," wrote one verified buyer. This is the #1 reason people buy it, and it delivers.
- **Finish quality**: Multiple reviewers compared the powder coat favorably to fixtures costing 3x more. The color match across black, white, and bronze variants is consistent.
- **Simple hardware**: The strap and nipple arrangement is compatible with most existing wiring setups without modification.

**Where it falls short**

Beyond the set-screw and gasket notes above, the set screws are small — 4mm or so — and easy to strip if you overtighten with a cheap Allen key. Go gently. Also, the kit does not include wire nuts or a ground screw, which is standard for a canopy-only kit but worth remembering if you're starting from scratch.

**Who should buy this**

- **DIYers replacing a builder-grade flush mount** — you'll cover the old footprint cleanly.
- **Anyone installing a pendant with a long rod** — the wide canopy gives you room to work and looks proportional.
- **Renters or homeowners with patched ceilings** — this hides sins that a standard 4-inch canopy won't.

Skip it if you need a full pendant kit with wiring included, or if you're mounting something over 10 pounds without a separate support structure.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit a standard ceiling junction box?**
A: Yes. The mounting strap spans both 3.5-inch and 4-inch octagon boxes — the two most common residential sizes.

**Q: Does it come with the cord and socket for a pendant?**
A: No. This is the canopy and mounting hardware only. You supply the cord, socket, and shade.

**Q: Can I paint it to match my ceiling?**
A: You can, but the powder coat is durable and adheres well to most paints. Light scuff with fine sandpaper first for best results.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're tired of undersized canopies that expose patched drywall or old mounting holes. It's a well-made, wide-coverage solution at a fair price.

**Skip it if** you need a full pendant kit or are mounting a heavy fixture without independent support.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — the coverage and finish earn the score; the set-screw and gasket quirks keep it from a perfect five.
