# KICHLER Aviv 2-Light Wall Sconce: The $200 Look Without the $400 Price Tag

Most wall sconces in this price range are hollow stamped metal with a cheap spray-on finish. The KICHLER Aviv is not that. Hold it before installation and you'll feel the difference — this is solid brass, machined with real weight, and the brushed finish has a subtle warmth that photographs well but looks even better in person.

As shown in the product image, the Aviv features two clear glass globes stacked vertically on a slim backplate. It's a compact footprint — roughly 5 inches wide and 12 inches tall — so it works in hallways, flanking a bathroom mirror, or as a pair in a bedroom. This isn't a statement piece; it's the kind of lighting that elevates everything around it without shouting.

## What You Actually Get

The box includes the sconce body, two clear glass shades, mounting hardware, and a template. The glass ships separately inside the carton with foam sleeves — good thing, because one Amazon reviewer noted theirs arrived with "a tiny chip on the bottom edge of one shade" and KICHLER shipped a replacement within days. Check both shades before you start drilling.

The fixture is damp-rated (UL listed for wet locations), so it's safe in bathrooms with steam or covered porches. The backplate is 5 inches in diameter and covers standard 4-inch junction boxes cleanly. One detail buyers consistently mention: the mounting screws are captured, which means no hunting for lost hardware mid-install.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The brass finish is the star here. It's not polished to a mirror shine — it's brushed, with a slight satin quality that hides fingerprints. One buyer put it this way: "I was skeptical about paying this much for a sconce, but the weight and finish are noticeably better than the big-box store options I almost bought."

The glass shades are thick, not the thin blown-glass you get with budget fixtures. They're mounted with a simple push-and-twist mechanism that seats securely — no rattling, no risk of them falling out. The fixture accepts two candelabra-base (E12) bulbs up to 60W each. Most buyers pair it with LED filament bulbs for that exposed-bulb look, which suits the clear glass perfectly.

Wiring is straightforward: three wires (hot, neutral, ground) with clearly labeled screws. A typical install runs 30–45 minutes if you're replacing an existing fixture. If you're wiring fresh, add time for running the circuit.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and the Aviv has two honest tradeoffs.

First, the bulbs. Candelabra-base bulbs cost more than standard A19s, and if you want LED filament bulbs with that warm vintage glow, you'll spend $10–$15 per bulb. Factor that into your total cost.

Second, the mounting plate sits flush with zero give. On perfectly flat drywall, it's fine. On older plaster walls or uneven tile, you might need to shim the plate or use drywall anchors. One reviewer noted they needed "a bit of patience getting the plate level on textured drywall."

## Who Should Buy This

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use dimmable bulbs with this sconce?**
Yes, the fixture itself has no dimmer — it's fully compatible with any candelabra-base dimmable LED or incandescent bulb, provided you have a dimmer switch on the circuit.

**Q: Is the glass easy to clean?**
The shades twist off in seconds, so you can wash them in the sink. The clear glass does show dust, but it's a two-minute job.

**Q: Does it work in a shower or right above a tub?**
It's damp-rated, which means it handles steam and humidity. For direct water spray (inside a shower stall), you need a wet-rated fixture — this isn't that.
