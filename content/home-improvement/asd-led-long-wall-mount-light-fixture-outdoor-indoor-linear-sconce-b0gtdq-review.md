If you're looking at the ASD LED Long Wall Mount Light Fixture, you already know what you want: a clean, modern linear sconce that throws even light across a stretch of wall without breaking the bank. At 4.2 stars from 210 buyers, it's not perfect — but for the money, it does a lot right. Here's what you need to know before you click buy.

**What's in the Box**

The package includes the fixture itself (pre-wired with leads), a universal mounting plate, wire nuts, and the hardware kit. What you *don't* get: a gasket for the wall plate. That's worth noting if you're mounting on stucco or rough brick — grab a small tube of exterior caulk while you're shopping. The fixture measures roughly 24 inches long, making it a solid choice above garage doors, along covered patios, or flanking a modern entryway.

**Real-World Performance**

The light output is where this sconce earns its keep. The LED board runs at 3000K — a warm-neutral white that feels residential rather than clinical. Output lands around 1200 lumens, which is enough to wash a 10-foot section of wall comfortably. The diffuser does its job: no visible diode dots, no glare banding. Light spreads evenly across the full length, which is the whole point of a linear sconce.

Build quality punches above the price point. The housing is die-cast aluminum with a powder-coated finish that resists fading. The IP65 rating is real — buyers in coastal Florida and the Pacific Northwest both report no moisture ingress after a year of exposure. One reviewer put it plainly: *"Installed under the eave of my porch in Seattle. We had three straight weeks of rain. Dry inside, no rust spots."*

Installation runs about 30–45 minutes for a competent DIYer. The mounting plate has slotted holes that give you wiggle room if your junction box isn't perfectly square — a small detail that saves real frustration. It's a hard-wired fixture, so budget for a licensed electrician if you're not comfortable with line voltage.

**Where It Falls Short**

The honest tradeoffs: the LED board is integrated, not replaceable. A quality LED driver should push this past 50,000 hours, but when the diodes eventually fade, you're buying a new fixture — not a new bulb. That's standard for this price tier, but it's worth knowing going in.

The included screws are zinc-plated and adequate for covered installation. If you're mounting fully exposed in a coastal or high-humidity environment, spend $3 on stainless steel replacements. The fixture also lacks built-in motion sensing or dusk-to-dawn capability; you'll need to pair it with an exterior-rated smart switch or timer.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Modern-home renovators** — this reads as a $200+ architectural fixture, not a budget special
- **Garage and carport owners** — the long form factor lights up vehicle fronts without glare
- **Rental property owners** — durable enough for tenant turnover, affordable enough to replace if needed

**Skip it if** you need adjustable color temperature, want a plug-in model, or expect replaceable bulbs.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can this be mounted horizontally and vertically?**
Yes. The bracket is universal — mount it flush along the wall or rotated 90 degrees for a vertical accent.

**Q: Does it work on a dimmer?**
Most standard LED dimmers work, but compatibility varies. If dimming is critical, confirm your dimmer is rated for LED loads. At full brightness, you won't need dimming anyway — 1200 lumens is comfortable, not blinding.

**Q: What's the actual color temperature?**
It's 3000K, a soft warm white. It's not the blue-white of daylight fixtures, and it's not the yellow glow of old incandescents. It's the modern neutral that most designers spec.

**Skip it if** you're a stickler for replaceable components or need smart-home integration out of the box.
