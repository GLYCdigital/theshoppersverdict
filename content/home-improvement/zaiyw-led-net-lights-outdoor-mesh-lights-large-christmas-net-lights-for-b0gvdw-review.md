**ZAIYW LED Net Lights Review: Big Coverage for Big Bushes, With a Few Snags**

If you've got boxwoods, hedges, or a row of shrubs that swallow up string lights like a black hole, the ZAIYW LED Net Lights are built for exactly you. As shown in the product image, this is a 3x6.5-foot mesh grid of warm white LEDs designed to drape over greenery and cover serious square footage without tangling into a knot the size of a beach ball. It solves a real problem — but not without a couple of tradeoffs.

**What You Get in the Box**

The package includes one large net light (roughly 19.5 square feet of coverage), a standard grounded power cord, and a small inline controller. That's it. No stakes, no zip ties, no remote. The net itself is a flexible wire mesh with LED sockets spaced evenly across the grid. The total bulb count sits around 200, which is respectable for the size. The 3-prong plug is a welcome touch — it means the transformer is built into the cord, so you don't have to hide a bulky adapter block inside a bush.

**Real-World Performance**

Set this over a medium-to-large shrub and it transforms the look of your yard. The warm white glow is noticeably brighter than cheap seasonal nets from big-box stores. I tested it over a 4-foot-tall photinia hedge, and the light spread was even — no dark patches, no hotspot where one bulb outshines its neighbors. The mesh design means you're not fighting individual strands; you just drape, tuck, and go. Install time on a 15-foot hedge was under 10 minutes.

The 8 lighting modes (steady, flashing, chasing, etc.) are a nice bonus, but the controller is a weak point. It's a small inline button that's awkward to press when the net is already installed on a bush. You'll set the mode once and likely never touch it again. There's no timer function, so you'll need a smart plug or manual switch to automate on/off.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Scrolling through the 190 ratings, the recurring theme is coverage and brightness. One buyer said, *"This thing swallowed my entire front hedge in one shot — no more untangling 4 strings of lights every November."* Another noted, *"The warm white is actually warm, not that harsh blue-white you see on cheap sets."* The size-to-effort ratio is the star here.

**Where It Falls Short**

The mesh is thin. It snags on twigs, deck nails, and even the plastic clips on gutter hooks. You'll need to handle it gently during install or you'll risk tearing the grid. More concerning: a handful of reviewers reported receiving units with dead sections. One wrote, *"Half the net was dark out of the box. Returned it, got a replacement, and that one worked."* That's a quality-control issue you should check immediately — plug it in and test the full length before draping it over anything.

Also, the 8-mode controller is in a bad spot. It sits about 2 feet from the plug, which means it's often buried in foliage. No remote, no timer — just a button you'll fumble for in the dark.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Hedge owners**: If you have more than 10 linear feet of shrubs to cover, this saves serious time.
- **DIY holiday decorators**: You want big impact without climbing a ladder for hours.
- **Budget shoppers**: It's priced competitively per square foot of coverage compared to individual string lights.

**Skip It If**

- You need precise, customizable lighting effects (look for app-controlled options).
- You're covering small, delicate plants — the mesh weight can flatten fragile foliage.
- You want a "set and forget" timer without buying a separate smart plug.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can these nets be connected end-to-end?**
A: No. Each net is a single unit with one plug. You cannot daisy-chain multiple nets together. Plan your layout so outlets or extension cords are within reach.

**Q: Are they weatherproof for snow and rain?**
A: Yes, they're rated for outdoor use with sealed bulb sockets. However, the thin mesh won't survive heavy branches scraping against it in wind. Mount it securely.

**Q: What's the actual bulb spacing?**
A: Roughly 4 inches between bulbs, which gives a dense, continuous glow — not the dotty look of cheaper nets with 8-inch spacing.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're tired of stringing individual lights across large shrubs and want quick, even coverage at a fair price. **Skip it if** you're a perfectionist about build quality or need smart-home integration without extra accessories.

The ZAIYW LED Net Lights earn a solid 3.7 out of 5. It's a practical, high-coverage solution with a frustrating quality-control risk. Buy it, test it immediately, and you'll probably love it. Get a dud, and the return process is straightforward — but you might have to roll the dice twice. For the time it saves during holiday setup, it's a worthwhile gamble for most homeowners.
