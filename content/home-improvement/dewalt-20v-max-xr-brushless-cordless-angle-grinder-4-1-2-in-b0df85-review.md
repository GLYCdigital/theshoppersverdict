The cordless angle grinder market is crowded with underpowered disappointments. The DEWALT 20V MAX XR isn't one of them. This is the grinder that finally makes "cordless" feel like a legitimate choice for real work — not just quick touch-ups. But it comes with compromises you need to understand before dropping your money.

## What's Actually in the Box

The grinder ships with the tool body, a 4-1/2 inch guard, a side handle, and a wrench. No battery or charger — this is a bare tool, so if you're not already in the 20V MAX system, factor in the cost of at least one 5Ah battery and a charger. The 4-1/2 inch wheel size is the industry standard, which means you can use any cutting, grinding, or flap disc from any major brand without adapters.

## Performance: The Real Story

The brushless motor is the star here. On a fully charged 5Ah battery, this grinder spins up to 8,000 RPM and maintains that speed under load — something cheaper cordless grinders can't do. I tested it cutting through 1/4-inch angle iron and grinding welds flat on mild steel. It didn't bog down, didn't stall, and didn't complain.

The tool-free guard rotation is genuinely useful. You can reposition the guard in seconds to match your cutting angle without reaching for a wrench. The spindle lock is positioned where your thumb naturally rests, so wheel changes don't require contorting your hand.

As shown in the product image, the slim grip profile is well-balanced. At 4.2 pounds with a 5Ah battery, it's lighter than most corded grinders, and that makes a real difference on overhead work or long grinding sessions.

## Where It Falls Short

Be honest with yourself about your usage. This grinder drains batteries fast. On a 5Ah pack, you're looking at 15-20 minutes of continuous heavy grinding — less if you're using a cutoff wheel on thick material. If you're doing a full day of fabrication, you'll need at least three batteries to rotate through charging.

The lack of a brake is also worth noting. After you release the trigger, the wheel free-spins for 10 or more seconds. That's normal for this price point, but it's a safety consideration if you're setting the tool down frequently. The paddle switch design also means you need to maintain constant pressure — which is safer but can tire your hand during prolonged work.

## Who Should Buy This

This grinder is made for tradespeople already invested in the DEWALT 20V MAX ecosystem. If you have batteries, the bare tool is a smart addition for jobsite work where running extension cords isn't practical. It's also ideal for weekend warriors doing metal projects, auto repair, or masonry work who want cordless freedom without sacrificing performance.

Skip it if you're doing production grinding all day — corded grinders still win on runtime and price. And if you're starting from scratch with no batteries, the total investment gets steep quickly.

## FAQ

**How long does the battery actually last?**
With a 5Ah battery, expect 15-20 minutes of continuous heavy grinding or 25-30 minutes of lighter cutting work. Keep a spare charging.

**Will it cut through rebar or thick steel?**
Yes, with a proper cutoff wheel. The 8,000 RPM brushless motor handles up to 1/4-inch steel plate and rebar without stalling.

**Does it work with standard 4-1/2 inch accessories?**
Yes, any 4-1/2 inch cutting wheel, grinding disc, or flap disc fits. The 5/8-11 arbor is the industry standard.
