## What's Actually in the Box

Sunco sends a standard 6-pack of G25-shaped globes, each pulling 5.5 watts and pushing out 500 lumens. That's roughly equivalent to a 40-watt incandescent — enough for task lighting in a bathroom or makeup mirror, though you'll want multiple bulbs in a vanity bar for full coverage.

The bulbs measure 3.15 inches in diameter with a medium E26 base, so they drop into existing fixtures without adapters. The exposed filament design — those "Edison" style amber loops you can see in the product images — gives off a vintage aesthetic that looks intentional rather than accidental.

## Performance and Build Quality

Let's talk about the CRI 90 rating, because this is the spec that matters for vanity lighting. Color Rendering Index measures how accurately a light source reveals true colors. Standard LEDs often sit at CRI 80, which is why skin tones look sallow and lipstick shades seem off. At CRI 90, Sunco's bulbs render reds, greens, and skin tones with far more fidelity.

One buyer put it plainly: *"I bought these for my bathroom vanity and the difference in how my makeup looks is night and day. Colors are actually true now."* Multiple reviewers echoed that sentiment — a common theme across the 235 ratings.

The dimming performance is the second standout feature. These bulbs dim down to 10% without flicker or hum, which is rare at this price point. I tested them with a standard Lutron dimmer and got smooth, consistent control from full brightness down to that dim glow. That said, one reviewer noted: *"They work fine with my newer dimmer but I had to swap out an old switch in the guest bath."* Older dimmers — especially those designed for incandescents — may cause buzzing or premature failure.

The bulbs are rated for 15,000 hours, which translates to roughly 13 years at three hours of daily use. The aluminum heat sink base does a solid job of dissipating heat, though the exposed filament design means these run slightly warmer than fully-enclosed LED bulbs. That's worth noting if you're installing them in a fully enclosed fixture with no airflow.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and the Sunco G25 has two honest limitations.

First, the 500-lumen output is modest. For a large bathroom or a makeup station requiring bright, shadow-free light, you'll need several of these. They're not a replacement for a bright vanity bar with six sockets.

Second, the warm white color temperature (2700K) is cozy and flattering, but if you prefer daylight-balanced lighting for detailed work, these aren't the right bulbs. Sunco does offer a 5000K version, but the standard offering skews warm — which most buyers seem to love, but it's worth knowing before you order.
