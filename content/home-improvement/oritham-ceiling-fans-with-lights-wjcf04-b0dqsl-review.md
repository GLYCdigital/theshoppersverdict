# ORITHAM Ceiling Fans with Lights WJCF04: The Quiet Overachiever or Just Another Ceiling Fan?

Most ceiling fans in the sub-$150 range are loud, ugly, or both. The ORITHAM WJCF04 tries to break that mold with a brushless DC motor, integrated dimmable lighting, and a sleek low-profile design. After digging through 210 ratings and multiple verified purchase reports, here's the honest picture: it's a genuinely good fan with a few quirks that matter.

## What's Actually in the Box

The package includes the fan body with pre-attached blades (a nice touch — no blade alignment hassle), the LED light panel, a remote control with wall-mount cradle, mounting bracket, and hardware. The unit spans 42 inches, making it best suited for bedrooms, home offices, and smaller living spaces up to roughly 200 square feet. The low-profile design sits flush against the ceiling, which is a plus for rooms with 8-foot ceilings.

## Real-World Performance

The DC motor is the headline feature, and it delivers. Multiple reviewers specifically mention the quiet operation — one wrote, *"I can't hear it on speed 2 even in a dead-silent bedroom. My old Hampton Bay sounded like a helicopter compared to this."* At higher speeds there's detectable whoosh from blade movement, but no motor whine or hum.

The integrated 24W LED panel puts out respectable light — equivalent to roughly a 150W incandescent. The color temperature adjustability (3000K warm to 5000K daylight) is genuinely useful, letting you shift from cozy evenings to task lighting with the remote. Dimming is smooth with no visible flicker, which is rare at this price point.

Airflow is adequate, not exceptional. On the highest speed, you'll feel it across a standard bedroom, but don't expect hurricane-force circulation in a large open-concept space. The six speed settings give you granular control, and the reverse function for winter works as advertised.

## Build Quality and Installation

The housing is powder-coated steel that feels solid, and the blades are a mid-grade MDF with a matte finish. It won't win design awards up close, but it's clean and unobtrusive. The included remote has a magnetic back that sticks to the included metal cradle — a small detail that buyers consistently appreciate.

Installation is where things get slightly sticky. The canopy is compact, and several reviewers noted that holding the 15-pound unit while connecting wires requires either a helper or serious forearm stamina. One review stated plainly: *"Do not attempt this alone unless you're comfortable balancing a fan overhead with one hand while wiring with the other."* Budget 60-90 minutes if you're moderately handy, longer if you're not.

## Where It Falls Short

The remote dependency is the biggest complaint. The pull chain only turns the fan on/off and toggles the light — no speed adjustment, no dimming, no color temperature changes. Lose the remote and you lose most functionality. Amazon doesn't sell a replacement remote separately as of this review, so treat that little plastic clicker like it's made of gold.

A few buyers also reported the receiver module failing within the first year, requiring a warranty claim. The 2-year warranty covers this, but the hassle is real. And while the fan is rated for sloped ceilings up to 30 degrees, installation gets noticeably more awkward on an angle.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want a quiet, energy-efficient fan with integrated dimmable lighting for a bedroom or home office. The DC motor's energy savings (claimed 70% less than AC motors) add up over time, and the adjustable color temperature genuinely improves room versatility.

**Skip it if:** You're installing in a large open space needing serious airflow, you hate relying on remotes, or you're a solo installer without help.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this fan work with Alexa or Google Home?**
A: No. The WJCF04 is remote-only. No smart home integration exists for this model.

**Q: Can I use the light without the fan running?**
A: Yes, the remote lets you control the light independently. You can run the fan with no light, light with no fan, or both.

**Q: What's the actual noise level at high speed?**
A: Expect around 45-50dB at max speed — audible but not obtrusive. It's the low and medium speeds where this fan truly shines.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The ORITHAM WJCF04 delivers on its core promises: quiet operation, flexible lighting, and solid build quality at a reasonable price. The remote-only control scheme and slightly fiddly install keep it from being perfect, but for a bedroom or office fan, this is a smart buy. Just don't lose the remote.

**Buy it if** you want a quiet, feature-packed fan with good light output. **Skip it if** you need smart home integration or prefer a simple pull-chain experience.
