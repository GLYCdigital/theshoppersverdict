If you've ever watched a monstera stretch toward a dim window like it's begging for sunlight, you know the problem. Aumtrly's full spectrum grow light for large and tall plants directly addresses that — without turning your living room into a cannabis lab. At 4.5 stars from 225 ratings, it's not perfect, but it's a serious upgrade from the cheap blurple panels that make your plants look like they're in a rave.

**What's actually in the box**  
The unit measures 12.2 x 4.5 x 1.3 inches — a slim bar, not a clunky square panel. You get the light itself, a 5.9-foot power cord (which is longer than most competitors at this price), and a mounting kit with four screws and two wire hooks. The hooks are a nice touch for hanging under shelves, but the screws are short — if your shelf is thicker than 1 inch, you'll need a trip to the hardware store. No remote, no timer, no app. That's fine if you already own an outlet timer, but worth knowing.

**Performance: does it actually grow things?**  
The 60W equivalent (actual draw is around 35W) puts out 3200 lumens at 18 inches above the canopy. The full spectrum includes 380–780nm with extra IR and UV diodes — that's not marketing fluff. In real-world use, that IR/UV combo matters for fruiting plants like peppers or herbs going to seed. One reviewer noted: *"My basil that was leggy under a south window thickened up within two weeks. The stems are sturdy enough to support the leaves now."* Another mentioned: *"I run it 12 hours a day for my indoor lemon tree. It's pushing new growth even in February."*

Heat is the silent killer of cheap grow lights, but the aluminum heat sink works. After a 10-hour run, the surface hit 92°F — warm to the touch, but not scorching. Small leaves within 6 inches won't burn, which is rare at this price point.

**Where it falls short**  
No timer is the biggest annoyance. If you're growing seedlings, you're going to forget to turn it off at night — and that messes with photoperiod-sensitive plants. Also, the included screws are the weak point. One buyer wrote: *"The light is great, but I had to buy longer bolts to mount it under my IKEA shelving. The stock ones stripped after the second use."*

Coverage is another consideration. The light spreads well over a 2x2 foot area, but the edges drop off noticeably. For a 4-foot wide shelf, you'll want two units — and the daisy-chain capability (up to 4 lights) makes that painless, but it's an extra cost.

**Who should buy this**  
- **Apartment dwellers** with a collection of tropicals (monstera, pothos, philodendron) that need supplemental light through winter.  
- **Herb gardeners** growing basil, mint, or cilantro on a kitchen counter — the compact size fits under upper cabinets.  
- **Seed starters** who need consistent light for trays without paying for a 4-foot T5 fixture.  

**Skip it if** you're growing a full hydro setup with multiple shelves — you'll need more wattage per square foot — or if you want plug-and-play automation. This is a manual light for hands-on growers.

**FAQ**  
**Q: Will this burn my plants if I put it too close?**  
A: At 6 inches, the surface heat is around 95°F. Keep it at least 8-10 inches for tender seedlings. If leaves start curling, raise it.

**Q: Can I use this for succulents?**  
A: Yes, but succulents need more intense light — run it 14–16 hours a day, and keep it within 10 inches. You'll see better coloration than with a window alone.

**Q: Does the daisy-chain work with different Aumtrly sizes?**  
A: Yes, as long as they're the same wattage. Mixing 35W and 60W units can cause the lower-watt light to dim slightly.

**The Verdict**  
**Buy it if** you want a solid, low-heat full spectrum light for a 2x2 foot area and don't mind setting your own timer.  
**Skip it if** you need automation, thicker mounting hardware, or broad coverage on a single unit.

At $35–$40 (check current pricing), it's a fair deal for the build quality. It won't replace the sun, but it'll keep your plants from becoming sad, leggy shadows of themselves. **4.6/5 stars.**
