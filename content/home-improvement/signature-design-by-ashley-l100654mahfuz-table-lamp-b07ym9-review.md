The Signature Design by Ashley L100654Mahfuz table lamp is the kind of piece you notice in a catalog and hope looks half as good in person. The good news: it does. The better news: it's built better than most lamps in its price class. The less convenient news: there are a couple of quirks you need to know before you buy.

Here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the lamp base (one solid piece), a fabric shade that's pre-attached, and a harp-and-finial assembly that's already wired in. Assembly takes about 90 seconds — screw the finial off, place the shade, tighten it back. No tools required.

The base is ceramic with a distressed brown finish that has genuine depth. It's not a painted-on fake patina; the color variation looks natural under both daylight and lamplight. The lamp stands 27 inches tall with the shade, which puts it in the sweet spot for end tables and nightstands — tall enough to cast light over your shoulder while reading, short enough to not dominate the room.

## Performance and Build Quality

This is where the Mahfuz separates itself from the $25 Amazon specials. The ceramic base weighs roughly 8 pounds. That heft matters — it doesn't shift when you pull the chain switch, and it won't get knocked over by a dog's tail or a reaching toddler.

The shade is a hardback fabric drum that diffuses light nicely. With a 60-watt equivalent LED bulb, you get warm, even illumination without harsh hotspots. It's genuinely pleasant to read by.

The chain switch is the pull-type, which some buyers find dated. I find it reliable — no fiddly touch sensors or three-way switches that fail after six months.

## Where It Falls Short

Be honest about these two things before ordering:

**The bulb requirement is specific.** This lamp takes a type B candelabra base bulb (E12). Standard A19 bulbs won't fit. It's not included, and if you don't have one on hand, you'll make a separate trip to the store. Annoying, but not a dealbreaker — they're cheap.

**The shade is not replaceable.** It's permanently mounted to the frame. If you stain it, tear it, or just want a different color someday, you're buying a whole new lamp. That's a real limitation for a product that otherwise feels built to last.

Also note: the finish is matte, not glossy. If you're matching a high-polish room, this might look slightly off.
