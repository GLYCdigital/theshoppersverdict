If you've ever installed a budget bathroom faucet, you know the drill: flimsy plastic parts, wobbly handles, and an instruction manual that reads like it was translated by a vending machine. The NS-SF01-V-Father-20241123 — let's just call it the NS-SF01 — is not that faucet. It's not a luxury fixture either, but it sits comfortably in the "genuinely decent" zone that's harder to find than you'd think at this price.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the faucet body, a single-lever handle, a pop-up drain assembly with a stainless steel strainer, and two braided stainless supply lines with 3/8-inch compression fittings already attached. That last detail matters more than it sounds — most budget faucets make you fish for supply lines separately, and the pre-attached ones here are legitimately good quality, not the kink-prone vinyl tubes you sometimes see.

The unit itself is solid brass, which you notice immediately when you lift it out of the box. It has that heft that tells you it won't flex or crack during installation. The chrome finish on the model shown in the product image is bright and even, with no visible tool marks or thin spots.

## Performance and Installation

Installation is where this faucet earns its keep. The mounting hardware is standard — a single hole with a deck plate that covers a 3-hole setup if you're replacing an older fixture. The quick-connect supply lines snap on with a satisfying click, and the locking clips are metal rather than the brittle plastic that fails on cheaper units. Most buyers report completing the install in under an hour with basic tools.

The ceramic cartridge delivers smooth, progressive water flow — no sudden temperature spikes when you nudge the lever. One Amazon reviewer put it this way: *"Handle moves buttery smooth, and the water pressure is actually better than my old name-brand faucet."* Another noted, *"Replaced a 15-year-old Delta with this and I'm honestly surprised at the quality for the money."*

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The pattern in the 220 ratings is clear. People who've installed it themselves love the straightforward mounting system and the fact that everything you need is in the box. The solid brass construction gets frequent shout-outs, with one reviewer noting *"It's heavy — you can tell it's not going to crack like the cheap plastic ones."*

The pop-up drain mechanism also earns consistent praise for actually working smoothly without jamming, which is rare in this price bracket.

## Where It Falls Short

The chrome finish is a fingerprint magnet. If you have hard water, you'll be wiping this thing down weekly to keep it looking clean. Matte black or brushed nickel versions would hide this better — if you're choosing, factor that in.

The spout reach is 4.5 inches, which is on the shorter side. If you have a deep vessel sink or a basin that sits close to the wall, your hands may brush the back of the bowl when washing. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, but worth measuring your sink before you buy.

A few buyers reported the included drain assembly didn't perfectly match their existing sink hole size — standard 1.25-inch drains fit fine, but if you have a non-standard basin, plan for a trip to the hardware store.

## Who Should Buy This

- **DIY homeowners** replacing a dated faucet who want solid quality without paying plumber-grade prices
- **Rental property owners** who need reliability and easy installation, not luxury features
- **Anyone with a standard 1- or 3-hole sink** looking for a clean, modern aesthetic without the designer markup

Skip it if you need an extra-tall spout for a vessel sink, or if you want a brushed or matte finish — the chrome-only option limits you here.

## FAQ

Yes. The supply lines use 3/8-inch compression fittings that connect to standard shut-off valves. The drain is 1.25 inches, which fits virtually all residential sinks.

**Does it include a pop-up drain?**

**How does the finish hold up over time?**
Chrome is durable and won't tarnish, but it does show water spots and fingerprints. Buyers with hard water report needing to wipe it down more frequently.
