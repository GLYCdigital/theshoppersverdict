If you've ever squinted at a workbench or under-cabinet task area lit by a single-row LED strip, you know the problem: standard strips look bright in the product photo but deliver a disappointing, uneven glow in real life. The LEDENET 16.4Ft Triple Row strip attacks that issue head-on — literally stacking three parallel rows of 600 LEDs per 5-meter reel. The result is a light output that approaches what you'd expect from a hardwired fluorescent tube, but with the flexibility of a tape you can cut and bend around corners.

## What's Actually in the Box

The package includes the 16.4-foot triple-row strip (cuttable at marked intervals every 1.57 inches), a 12V power adapter, and a connector lead. That's it — no controller, no remote, no mounting clips. The strip itself is IP20-rated, meaning it's fine for dry indoor locations like workshops, garages, shelving, and under-cabinet use, but keep it away from moisture-prone areas like bathrooms or outdoor covered patios.

The daylight white color temperature sits in the 6000K-6500K range. That's a clinical, blue-leaning white — not warm, not cozy. It's the kind of light that makes small text crisp and colors read true, which is exactly what you want for detail work or photography. If you're looking for mood lighting, this isn't it.

## Performance and Build Quality

The triple-row architecture is the real differentiator here. Each of the three parallel LED rows runs the full 16.4-foot length, which means you're getting 600 LEDs total — roughly triple the density of a typical 300-LED strip. In practical terms, this thing is *bright*. We're talking enough light to illuminate a 6-foot workbench evenly without shadows, or to eliminate the dark zones that plague standard under-cabinet installations.

The strip runs on 12V DC power, which keeps it safe for DIY installation and compatible with standard LED dimmers if you want to add one. Heat management is solid — even after extended runs, the strip stays warm but not hot, which speaks to reasonable current regulation and good thermal design.

Cutting is straightforward: snip at the marked copper pads, and the remaining segments continue to work. Just make sure you cut straight across the pad to avoid shorting adjacent traces.

## Where It Falls Short

The adhesive backing is the most common complaint — and it's legitimate. The 3M tape holds fine on smooth, clean surfaces like painted drywall or laminate, but on textured walls, bare wood, or surfaces with dust or grease, it will peel over time. Plan on using mounting clips or supplemental adhesive for permanent installations.

The lack of a dimmer or controller in the base package is also worth noting. At full brightness, this strip is *intense* — almost uncomfortably so in a small room. You'll likely want to add a $10 PWM dimmer unless you're using it strictly for task lighting.

Finally, the connector between the strip and power supply is a bit fiddly. It works, but the fit is snug and polarity isn't clearly marked, so pay attention during initial setup to avoid reversing the connection.

## Who Should Buy This

This strip is purpose-built for three buyer types: **workshop users** needing bright, shadow-free task lighting; **photographers/videographers** who need consistent, flicker-free daylight color temperature; and **kitchen remodelers** who want under-cabinet lighting that actually lights the counter, not just a decorative glow.

Skip it if you're after ambient or accent lighting — the brightness and cool color temperature would be overwhelming in a living room or bedroom setting.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I cut this strip and still use the remaining portion?**
A: Yes. Cut lines are marked every 1.57 inches at the copper pads. Cut straight across the pad and the remaining segment works normally. Just don't cut through the middle of a pad.

**Q: Does this work with a smart home system or dimmer?**
A: Not out of the box. It's a basic 12V DC strip, so it needs an inline PWM dimmer for brightness control. It won't connect to Alexa or Google Home unless you add a smart controller yourself.

**Q: Is the adhesive strong enough for permanent installation?**
A: On smooth, clean, dry surfaces, yes — for now. On textured or porous surfaces, no. Use clips or extra adhesive for a permanent hold, especially in areas with temperature swings.
