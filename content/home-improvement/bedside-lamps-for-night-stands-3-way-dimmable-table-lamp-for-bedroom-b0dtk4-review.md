Let's cut to the chase: a bedside lamp needs to do three things — provide light without blinding you, fit on a night stand without dominating it, and survive being knocked around in the dark. This 3-way dimmable model from the generic Amazon home-goods space mostly delivers on all three, and that's why it's sitting at 4.4 stars from 220 buyers. But "mostly" is doing some heavy lifting here.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the lamp base, a fabric drum shade, and the harp/finial assembly. Assembly takes under two minutes — screw the shade onto the base, drop in an E26 bulb (not included, more on that below), and you're done. The dimensions are roughly 15 inches tall with a 10-inch diameter shade, which makes it a solid fit for standard night stands without overwhelming smaller surfaces.

The base is weighted, which is the single most important design decision here. Bedside lamps get bumped. This one stays put. Multiple buyers confirm this — one reviewer noted, "I've knocked it twice getting up in the night and it hasn't budged." That's worth more than any aesthetic feature.

**How it performs in real use**

The 3-way switch is a push-button on the base that cycles through low → medium → high → off. It's mechanical, not touch-sensitive, which is actually a plus — no accidental activation when you brush against it, and no frustrating "why won't it respond" moments that plague touch lamps.

Low is genuinely dim — good for watching TV or scrolling your phone without harsh light. Medium works for conversation or ambient lighting. High is bright enough for reading, though you'll want a warm-toned bulb (2700K-3000K) to avoid that clinical, office-building feel. The fabric shade diffuses light well, eliminating hotspots and harsh shadows.

One thing buyers consistently praise is the light quality. As one reviewer put it: "The shade makes the light soft and warm — doesn't hurt my eyes at night." That's the kind of feedback that tells you this isn't just a pretty object; it functions correctly.

**Where it falls short**

The bulb requirement is the biggest gotcha. This lamp does not include a bulb — it takes a standard E26 base. If you don't already own one, that's an additional $8-15 depending on what you choose. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's an unexpected friction point for a product that should arguably ship ready to use.

Build quality is decent but not exceptional. The fabric shade is stretched over a lightweight frame — it looks good from across the room, but up close you can see some slight unevenness in the fabric tension. One buyer noted, "The shade feels a bit thin compared to lamps I've bought at Target." It's not cheap-looking, but it's clearly a value-tier product.

The cord is also on the shorter side at around 5 feet. If your night stand isn't positioned near an outlet, you'll be shopping for an extension cord.

**Who should buy this**

- **Apartment dwellers** who want a functional, decent-looking lamp without dropping $80+ at a boutique store
- **People who read in bed** and need adjustable brightness without a smart-home setup
- **Anyone frustrated by touch-sensitive lamps** that turn on when you don't want them to

Skip it if you're a design purist who'll notice the fabric shade's imperfections, or if you want a lamp that works with smart bulbs for voice control — the mechanical switch makes that clunky.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use a smart bulb with this lamp?**
A: Yes, but the 3-way switch will override smart bulb settings. You'll be stuck using the lamp's mechanical switch, which limits smart features. If smart control matters, get a standard on/off lamp instead.

**Q: Is the lamp heavy enough to stay put?**
A: Yes. The weighted base is one of the most consistently praised features. Buyers report it withstanding bumps and even minor falls without tipping.

**Q: What bulb wattage should I use?**
A: The lamp accepts standard A19 E26 bulbs up to 60W equivalent. A 9W LED (60W equivalent) in warm white is the sweet spot — bright enough for reading on high, soft enough for ambient on low.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a dependable, functional bedside lamp with genuine 3-way dimming, solid stability, and even light diffusion at a reasonable price point.

**Skip it if:** You need premium build quality, smart-home integration, or a lamp that ships ready to use without buying a separate bulb.

This lamp earns its 4.2/5 score through reliability and function over flair. It won't win design awards, but it will light your bedroom consistently, survive midnight bumps, and do it all without annoying you. For most shoppers, that's exactly what a bedside lamp needs to be.
