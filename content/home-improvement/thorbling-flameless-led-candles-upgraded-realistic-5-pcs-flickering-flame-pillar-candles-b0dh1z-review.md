# Thorbling Flameless LED Candles Review: The 5-Piece Set That Fools the Eye

Most flameless candles look like what they are: plastic tubes with a glowing orange dot on top. The Thorbling 5-piece set tries something harder — and mostly pulls it off. These aren't just LED pillars; they're designed to genuinely mimic the chaotic, unpredictable dance of a real flame. If you've been burned by cheap flicker candles before, this review covers what actually matters before you buy.

## What's in the Box

You get five pillar candles in two sizes: three smaller ones at 3 x 3 inches and two larger at 4 x 4 inches. All are made from a real-wax outer shell (not plastic), which makes a huge difference in how they look on a mantle or dining table. Each candle requires 2 AA batteries — that's 10 batteries total, none included. There's no remote control in this set, so you're managing each candle individually at first setup.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where Thorbling earns its keep. The "flame" is a 3D sculpted tip with an LED underneath that creates a genuine flickering effect — the light dances, dims, and shifts like a real candle catching a draft. It's not the cheap strobe effect you see on $10 candles. The warm white light temperature sits around 2700K, matching the cozy glow of actual wax candles.

The timer function is the standout feature. Press it once, and the candle runs for 4 hours then shuts off. Do that again tomorrow at the same time, and it remembers — it'll turn on and off at the same time daily. Set it once on Monday, and it runs your evening routine all week without touching it.

Battery life is solid, though not spectacular. Expect roughly 100-150 hours of use per candle with the timer in play. That's about a month of daily evening use before swapping batteries.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across 215 ratings at 4.3 stars, the recurring theme is surprise at how realistic these look. One reviewer noted, "I put these next to real candles on my dining table and guests couldn't tell which was which until they touched them." Another praised the timer: "Set it once before bed and it's been turning on at 6 PM every day since. Zero maintenance."

The wax texture gets frequent mentions too — it's not glossy plastic, it's matte and slightly textured, which sells the illusion hard. Multiple buyers confirmed they're using them in place of real candles for safety with kids and pets, which tells you the tradeoff is worth it for them.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the flaws. The lack of a remote control is the biggest miss — with five candles, you're walking around pressing buttons on each one to sync them. The 4-hour timer helps, but if you want them all on at once, you're setting them individually.

The flame tip gets warm after about 30 minutes of continuous use. Not hot enough to burn, but noticeable if you touch it — worth knowing if you have curious toddlers. The faux-wax coating is also slightly tacky, which means dust sticks to it more than you'd expect. A microfiber cloth handles it, but it's an extra maintenance step.

Sizing is the other thing to verify. These run smaller than many product photos suggest. The 3-inch candles are fine for tabletops, but if you're planning to put them in large lanterns or tall holders, measure first.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want realistic ambient lighting without fire risk, you're tired of replacing batteries in cheap candles monthly, or you're decorating for a rental where real candles are prohibited. The timer feature makes this a set-and-forget solution for daily evening ambiance.

**Skip it if:** You need remote control operation for hard-to-reach spots, you're expecting large statement candles (these are medium at best), or you want a set that works with a dimmer switch — these don't dim.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I leave these on overnight?**  
The timer caps runtime at 4 hours, so they'll shut off automatically. You can override it by pressing the button again, but the timer is designed for daily repeat use.

**Q: Do the batteries last long enough to matter?**  
Yes — roughly 100-150 hours per candle. With the 4-hour daily timer, that's about a month of daily use per battery set. Alkaline batteries perform best.

**Q: Are these safe around kids and pets?**  
Yes, and that's the main selling point. No open flame, no smoke, no hot wax. The flame tip gets warm but not hot enough to cause burns.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5** — These are genuinely good flameless candles that look better than their price point suggests. The realistic flicker and smart timer make them a practical upgrade over both real candles and cheaper LED alternatives. The missing remote and smaller-than-expected sizing keep it from being perfect, but for most buyers — especially parents, pet owners, and apartment dwellers — this set delivers real value.

**Buy it if** you want candle ambiance without the fire hazard. **Skip it if** remote operation or larger sizes are non-negotiable for your space.
