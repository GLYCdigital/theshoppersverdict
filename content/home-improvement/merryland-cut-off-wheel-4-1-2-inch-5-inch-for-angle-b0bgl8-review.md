## What You Actually Get

Merryland sends a straightforward package: 4.5-inch and 5-inch abrasive cut-off wheels with a 7/8" arbor hole, the standard fit for most angle grinders including DeWalt, Makita, and Milwaukee models. The 4.5" wheels are 1.0mm thick—thin enough for fast cuts but not so fragile they shatter on contact. The 5" versions step up slightly in thickness for added rigidity. Each disc features a double-layer fiberglass reinforcement mesh, which is the critical safety feature that keeps the wheel from disintegrating at 13,000 RPM.

## Real-World Performance

The first thing you'll notice is the cutting speed. These wheels chew through 1/4" mild steel plate with noticeably less pressure than generic hardware store discs. The aluminum oxide abrasive grain is dense and uniform, so you're not fighting the wheel to make progress. On 1/8" angle iron, a single disc handled roughly 40 cuts before showing significant wear—solid performance at this price point.

One buyer noted: "These cut faster than the name-brand wheels I was using, and they don't load up on aluminum either." That's a fair assessment. The open-grain structure clears debris well, which means less heat buildup and fewer glazed wheels.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. These discs cut aggressively, which means they leave a slightly rougher edge than premium thin-kerf wheels. If you're doing finish work where cut quality matters—like fabricating visible welds—you'll want to deburr afterward. On stainless steel, wear accelerates noticeably; expect roughly 60% of the lifespan you'd get on mild steel. And while the fiberglass reinforcement is solid, these aren't meant for heavy side-grinding. Use them for cutting only, and they'll last.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy them if:** You're a DIYer or light fabricator who goes through cut-off wheels regularly and wants dependable performance without the premium markup. The value pack makes sense for auto repair, metalworking, or general construction projects.

**Skip them if:** You need surgical-precision cuts for fine metalwork, or you primarily cut stainless and need maximum wheel life. In those cases, spend more on specialty discs.

## FAQ

**Will these fit my grinder?**
If your grinder takes standard 4.5" or 5" wheels with a 7/8" arbor—which covers virtually all mainstream models—yes. No adapters needed.

**Are they safe for cutting aluminum?**
Yes, but use a light touch and let the wheel do the work. The open grain prevents loading, though you'll get better results with a dedicated non-ferrous blade for heavy aluminum work.

**How do these compare to DeWalt or Diablo wheels?**
The Merryland wheels cut at a comparable speed but wear slightly faster and leave a rougher finish. You're trading a bit of refinement for roughly half the cost per disc.
