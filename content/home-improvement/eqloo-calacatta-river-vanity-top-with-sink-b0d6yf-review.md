If you're replacing a dated vanity, the EQLOO Calacatta River top with integrated sink sits in a sweet spot: it looks like a $2,000 stone slab but costs a fraction. The question is whether engineered quartz at this price point actually delivers. After digging through 225 ratings and the specifics of the product itself, here's the real story.

**What arrives at your door**

The box contains the one-piece quartz top with the sink bowl molded in, a polished front edge, and a factory-drilled 3-hole faucet pattern (8-inch widespread spacing). No faucet, drain, or mounting hardware comes with it — this is just the top. The dimensions are generous: the standard 37-inch width fits most 36-inch vanity cabinets, and the depth runs about 22 inches, giving you a bit of overhang for a comfortable stance. The stone is roughly 0.75 inches thick at the edges, which feels substantial when you set it down.

**Build quality that justifies the price**

The Calacatta River pattern is a white base with bold, grey-charcoal veining running diagonally — busier than Carrara, but not so loud that it fights brass fixtures. The polished surface is what you'd expect from 7,000-series quartz: it resists the coffee rings and toothpaste splatter that would etch a natural marble top. Buyers consistently note the finish holds up. One reviewer put it simply: *"Looks exactly like the photos. The veining is gorgeous and it's been six months with zero stains."* Another praised the weight: *"You can tell it's not a flimsy cultured marble top — this thing is solid."*

The integrated sink is the standout feature. Because the bowl is molded into the slab, there's no seam where water can sneak under the rim. That eliminates the mildew problem you get with drop-in sinks and makes wiping down the basin genuinely easy. The bowl is shallow enough to keep a clean profile but deep enough to wash your face without splashing the counter.

**Where it gets real**

Two things will test your patience. First, the weight. At roughly 60 to 70 pounds depending on the size you choose, this is not a one-person job. You will need a second set of hands to lift it onto the cabinet without chipping a corner. Second, the pre-drilled faucet holes are non-negotiable. If you've already bought a single-handle or wall-mount faucet, you'll be staring at three empty holes and a return label. A widespread faucet or a deck plate is your only option.

The lack of a backsplash is another gap. The top sits flush against the wall, so you'll need to run a bead of silicone or a thin backsplash tile to keep water from running down the back edge. It's a minor addition, but it's an extra step that some buyers didn't anticipate.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the remodeler who wants the high-end look of natural stone without the maintenance. It's ideal if you're using a widespread faucet, have a helper for install day, and don't mind caulking the back edge. It's a poor fit if you're installing solo, need a single-hole faucet, or expect a full backsplash included in the box.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a durable, low-maintenance stone top with a seamless integrated sink and you're prepared for a two-person lift.

**Skip it if:** You're set on a specific faucet style that doesn't match a 3-hole pattern, or you're working alone without any way to brace the weight.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A strong value for the price, with minor tradeoffs that are easy to plan around.

**FAQ**

**Does the top scratch easily?** Quartz is harder than granite, but not scratch-proof. Use a cutting board for anything sharp, and clean with a non-abrasive sponge. Normal bathroom use won't mark it.

**Will the veining match the product photos?** Each slab is cut from the same pattern block, so the veining will be similar but not identical. The photos show a representative sample; your unit will vary slightly in veining density.

**Does it fit a standard 36-inch vanity?** Yes, the 37-inch width gives you a 0.5-inch overhang on each side, which looks intentional. Just measure your cabinet depth first — most are 21-22 inches deep, and this top matches that.
