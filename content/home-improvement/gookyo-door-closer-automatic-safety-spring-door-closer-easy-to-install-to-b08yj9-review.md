## What You Actually Get

The box contains the closer body, a steel mounting arm, both mounting brackets, screws, and a paper template for positioning. The unit itself is a standard hydraulic piston design — no fancy electro-mechanical guts here, just reliable fluid dynamics. The body is finished in a silver/gray tone that blends reasonably well with most aluminum and painted steel doors. As shown in the product image, the mounting arm folds flat against the door frame when closed, which keeps it out of the way visually.

## Real-World Performance

Installing this closer is genuinely straightforward. The included template marks your screw holes, and the hardware kit covers both wood and metal door frames. Most buyers report completing installation in 15–20 minutes with a drill and a Phillips head screwdriver. One reviewer noted, "Took me longer to find my drill than to actually mount the thing."

The standout feature is the dual adjustment valves. One controls the main closing speed; the other controls the final latch speed. This matters more than you'd think — you can dial in a slow, gentle swing that speeds up just before the door latches, ensuring it clicks shut every time without slamming. That's the difference between a $25 closer and a $60 closer, and GOOKYO delivers it at the budget end.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. This is not an exterior-grade closer. The hydraulic fluid will thicken in extreme cold and thin out in heat, changing your adjustment settings seasonally. If you need something for an exposed front door, spend more for a unit rated for outdoor use.

The 85-pound door weight limit is also real. If you're mounting this on a solid oak door or a heavy metal security door, you'll find the closer struggling to pull it shut — the arm will bend slightly and the closure will be sluggish at best. One buyer reported, "Works great on my hollow-core interior door, but I tried it on my shop's metal door and it just doesn't have the muscle."

## Who Should Buy This

- **Homeowners with interior doors** that need to close automatically — pantries, laundry rooms, kids' bedrooms
- **Renters or landlords** wanting to add fire-safety door closure without spending $80+ per unit

Skip it if you need exterior weather resistance, have doors over 85 pounds, or want a low-profile surface-mounted closer that's nearly invisible.

## FAQ

**Will this fit both left-hinged and right-hinged doors?**
Yes. The mounting arm and bracket flip to accommodate either swing direction. The template includes orientation marks.

**How do I adjust the closing speed?**
Two hex-head valves on the side of the body. Turn clockwise to slow, counterclockwise to speed up. Make quarter-turn adjustments and test between each change.

**Does it come with all the mounting hardware?**
Yes — screws for wood and metal frames, plus the template. You'll need your own drill and Phillips screwdriver, and a hex key is included for the adjustment valves.
