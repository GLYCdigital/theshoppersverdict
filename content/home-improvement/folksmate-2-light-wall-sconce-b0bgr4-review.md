### The Bottom Line First

Most wall sconces under $60 are flimsy, finicky, or flat-out ugly. The FOLKSMATE 2-Light Wall Sconce isn't any of those things—and at 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, it's clearly earning its keep in real homes. But it's not without caveats. Here's the honest breakdown before you click "add to cart."

---

### What You Actually Get

The box contains two sconce bodies with pre-attached gooseneck arms, two backplates, mounting screws, wire nuts, and a small Allen wrench for adjusting the arm tension. The sconces measure roughly 12 inches tall with arms that extend about 10.5 inches from the wall—compact enough for hallways and flanking a mirror, but substantial enough to make a statement over a console table.

Each sconce takes one standard E26 bulb (not included), so you can use LED, Edison, or smart bulbs. The matte black finish is genuinely well-applied—it's a powder coat that doesn't chip easily and shrugs off fingerprints, which matters if you're installing these in a high-traffic hallway.

---

### Performance and Build Quality

The standout feature is the fully adjustable gooseneck arms. You can angle each light independently—point one down for task lighting over a desk and the other up for ambient wash on a wall. The tension joint holds position firmly; one buyer noted, *"I've had mine for six months and they haven't drooped or shifted, even after my kids yanked on them."*

The metal housing is solid—no thin-gauge rattling or hollow feel. Heat dissipation is adequate for LED bulbs; even after four hours of continuous use, the fixture stays cool to the touch. Dimmable bulbs work without flicker, according to multiple reviewers, which is more than you get from many budget fixtures.

Installation is straightforward if you're comfortable with basic wiring: three wires (hot, neutral, ground), and the backplate slots over a standard junction box. The included hardware covers 4-inch round boxes cleanly. Plan for 30-45 minutes per sconce if you're replacing existing fixtures.

---

### What Buyers Consistently Praise

The flexibility is the recurring theme in positive reviews. One customer wrote: *"I use one pointed at a reading chair and the other at artwork on the opposite wall. It's like having two fixtures for the price of one."* Another mentioned the finish specifically: *"I was skeptical about matte black—thought it'd look cheap. It looks custom."* Buyers also repeatedly mention how well the sconces hide minor wall imperfections behind the backplate, a small but appreciated detail.

---

### Where It Falls Short

The gooseneck arms are adjustable, but they're not designed for frequent repositioning. The tension screws hold firm, but repeated adjustments can loosen them—you'll need to re-tighten with the included Allen wrench occasionally. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

The mounting screws are your standard drywall anchors, which are fine for light fixtures but not ideal if you're mounting into plaster or tile. Several reviewers recommended swapping in toggle bolts for heavier-duty installations. Also, the fixture is hardwired only—no plug-in variant exists, so renters or anyone avoiding electrical work will need to budget for an electrician.

---

### Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're outfitting a hallway, flanking a bathroom mirror, or adding reading lights above a bed and want adjustable, directional lighting without paying designer prices. It's also a strong choice for anyone replacing outdated sconces with a modern-industrial look.

**Skip it if:** You're a renter needing plug-in fixtures, you're mounting into tile or plaster without planning to upgrade hardware, or you prefer a fixed, sculptural sconce that doesn't move.

### FAQ

**Q: Can I use these with a dimmer switch?**
Yes—provided you use dimmable LED bulbs. Multiple reviewers confirm smooth dimming with standard Lutron dimmers.

**Q: What's the recommended bulb wattage?**
Max 60W per socket, but LED bulbs at 9-12W are plenty bright. The fixture is rated for standard A19 bulbs up to 6 inches long; larger bulbs may look awkward given the gooseneck curve.

**Q: Do the arms come pre-assembled?**
Yes, the arms arrive attached to the backplate. You only need to adjust the angle with the Allen wrench after mounting.
