## Where It Falls Short

Honest tradeoffs exist. The lack of rubber or vinyl coating means painted tool handles can get scratched. If you're storing premium tools, consider wrapping the hook tips or buying a smaller coated set for those specific items.

The single-prong design is also limiting. You won't hang a framing hammer, a circular saw, or bundled cords on these — those need double-prong or loop hooks. This set is best for organizing, not heavy-duty storage. If your pegboard holds mostly power tools, you'll need to supplement with specialty hooks.

**Will these work with metal pegboard?**
Yes. The 1/8-inch wire fits standard metal pegboard openings. Just ensure your metal board uses the standard 1-inch hole spacing — most do.

**How much weight can each hook hold?**
Realistically, 5-10 pounds per hook for the 4-inch size. The steel won't bend, but the hook can pull out if you overload it. Keep heavier items on lower rows.

**Are they easy to remove and reposition?**
Yes, they pull straight out with light upward pressure. No tools needed. The tradeoff is they can fall out if you drag a tool across them — place heavier items on lower hooks.
