Bathroom lighting is the most underrated renovation upgrade you can make. Bad vanity lighting ruins your morning routine — harsh shadows, yellow casts, and that unflattering “public restroom” glow. The XPEHK Bathroom Vanity Light aims to fix that with a dimmable, color-adjustable LED bar that looks far more expensive than its price point. After digging through 235 verified reviews and the spec sheet, here’s what actually matters.

**What You Get**

The box contains the main light bar (about 32 inches for the standard model), a tempered glass diffuser, mounting hardware, and a wiring kit. You get both hardwire capability and a plug-in cord option, which is rare at this price. The housing is extruded aluminum with a clean, matte white finish — no visible screws on the face, which keeps the minimalist look intact. As shown in the product image, the slim profile sits close to the wall, so it won’t protrude awkwardly over a medicine cabinet.

**Performance and Build**

The headline feature is the color temperature range: 3000K (warm) to 5000K (cool daylight), adjustable via a small switch on the side or a wall dimmer if you wire it that way. That’s genuinely useful — warm for relaxing baths, cool for applying makeup or shaving. The memory function is a small but appreciated touch; it remembers your last setting when you flip the switch off and on.

Brightness tops out around 2400 lumens for the three-light version. That’s respectable for a single vanity, but if you have a 60-inch double sink, you’ll want two of these or a higher-output fixture. The light distribution is even across the diffuser — no hot spots or flicker, which multiple reviewers confirmed. One buyer noted, *“No hum, no flicker, even on a dimmer. My old fixture buzzed like a bee.”*

Build quality is where this punches above its weight. The aluminum housing feels solid, and the tempered glass diffuser is a step up from the acrylic panels you see on cheaper units. It’s also IP44 rated, meaning it’s splash-proof enough for bathroom zones near a sink — but don’t mount it directly inside a shower stall.

**Where It Falls Short**

The mounting bracket is the weak point. Several reviewers mention that aligning the bracket perfectly level before attaching the light bar takes trial and error. One said, *“The bracket holes didn’t quite line up with my existing junction box — I had to oblong them slightly with a file.”* This isn’t a dealbreaker, but budget 20 extra minutes for installation if you’re not handy.

Also, the color switch is on the fixture itself, not on the wall. If you hardwire it without a smart dimmer, changing from warm to cool means reaching up behind the light. It’s fine at install time, but annoying if you like to toggle throughout the day.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **DIY homeowners** replacing an outdated 1980s fluorescent bar — this is a direct swap with standard wiring.
- **Renters** who want plug-in lighting without permanent modifications — the cord option works well.
- **Anyone with a single-sink vanity** (24–36 inches) who wants adjustable color temps at a mid-range price.

Skip it if you have a massive double vanity needing 4000+ lumens, or if you want full smart-home integration without buying a separate dimmer switch.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is the light dimmable with a standard wall dimmer?**  
A: Yes, but only if hardwired. The plug-in version works with the included inline switch, not a wall dimmer. Use a compatible LED dimmer (most modern ones work) for flicker-free control.

**Q: Can I install this myself if I’ve never wired a light fixture?**  
A: If you’re comfortable connecting three wires (black, white, ground), yes. If you’ve never touched a junction box, hire an electrician — it’s a 15-minute job for them and safer than guessing.

**Q: Does the glass diffuser get hot after hours of use?**  
A: No. The LED strips run cool, and the aluminum housing dissipates heat well. One reviewer ran it 10 hours daily for months with zero heat issues.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a stylish, adjustable vanity light without paying designer-brand prices. The build quality and color-tuning flexibility justify the cost, and the plug-in option makes it accessible for non-electricians.

**Skip it if** you need serious brightness for a large bathroom or you’re unwilling to fuss with bracket alignment during install.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A solid, well-made fixture with minor installation quirks that don’t outweigh its performance.
