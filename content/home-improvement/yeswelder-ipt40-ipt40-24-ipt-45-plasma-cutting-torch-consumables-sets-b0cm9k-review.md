If you own a plasma cutter with an IPT40 or IPT45 torch — like the ones found on YESWELDER's own units or the popular Cut-50 style machines — you already know the annoyance of hunting for consumables that actually fit. This kit aims to end that search.

## What's Actually in the Box

This is a complete consumable set, not a single replacement part. You're getting the full lineup: electrodes, cutting nozzles, shields, and the retaining cap. The kit is designed to work across the IPT40, IPT40-24, and IPT-45 torch heads, which covers a solid range of 30-45 amp machines.

The components are machined copper with the standard hafnium electrode insert. Nothing exotic here, but that's exactly what you want — plasma consumables are simple parts that need to be made to spec, not reinvented.

## Real-World Performance

I've seen enough third-party consumables come through that are "close enough" — meaning they fit like a loose shoe and cut like it too. That's not the case here. The threads on the retaining cap engage cleanly, and the electrode seats properly against the torch body. That matters because a poor electrical connection between the electrode and torch is the #1 cause of erratic arc starts.

Cut quality at 40 amps on 1/4" steel is what you'd expect from a properly functioning torch: clean, square-ish edges, minimal dross on the bottom. Users consistently note that arc starts are immediate, which tells me the electrode gap and nozzle alignment are right.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about limitations. The nozzle selection is standard — you get the common 30A/40A/45A tips, but no fine-cut or low-amp options for thin gauge sheet metal. If you do a lot of work on 16-gauge or thinner, you'll still need to source specialty nozzles separately.

There's also a minor quirk with the shield cap. A few users mention that it can bind on the threads if you crank it down too hard. The fix is simple — hand-tighten only — but it's worth knowing before you go gorilla on it.

## FAQ

**Q: Will these fit my YESWELDER CUT-50 plasma cutter?**
A: If your machine uses an IPT40 or IPT45 torch head, yes. This covers the IPT40, IPT40-24, and IPT-45 variants. Double-check your torch head stamp before ordering, but this is the standard fit for most 40-45 amp units.

**Q: How long does an electrode last?**
A: Depends on your duty cycle and amperage. Expect roughly 50-100 starts on a single electrode at 40 amps with normal use. You'll know it's time to swap when the arc starts getting finicky or the cut quality degrades.

**Q: Can I use these at 30 amps with a smaller machine?**
A: Yes, the 30A nozzle is included in the kit. Just swap the nozzle to match your amperage — using a 40A nozzle at 30A settings will give you a wider, less precise cut.
