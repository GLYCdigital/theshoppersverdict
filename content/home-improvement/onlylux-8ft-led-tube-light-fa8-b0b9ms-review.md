Some products overpromise. This one just tells you what it is — an 8-foot LED tube that replaces old fluorescent fixtures in garages, workshops, and utility spaces — and then delivers exactly that. The ONLYLUX FA8 has pulled in 220 ratings at a solid 4.4 stars, and after digging through the specs and what real buyers are saying, here's the honest breakdown.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the 8-foot tube itself (216W of LED power), a set of mounting clips, and connectors that let you link up to four tubes together. As shown in the product image, the tube has an FA8 double-pin base on each end — that's the slightly wider pin spacing compared to standard T8/T12 single-pin tubes, so make sure your existing tombstones accept FA8 before you commit. The 5000K daylight color temperature means you're getting crisp, white light, not the sickly yellow of old fluorescents.

**Installation: the real story**

The ONLYLUX requires a ballast-bypass install. That means you're either removing the ballast entirely or wiring around it. If you're comfortable with basic electrical work — we're talking "I've changed a light fixture before" level, not electrician level — this is a 20-minute job with the included wiring diagram. One buyer put it simply: *"Took me longer to dig out my ladder than to wire this in. Direct wire, no ballast issues, and it's been running daily for six months."*

The catch: if you're not comfortable with wiring, or your fixture still has a functioning ballast you'd rather not touch, this isn't your light. There's no plug-and-play version of this tube. That's the single biggest barrier to entry, and it's worth being honest about.

**Performance and build quality**

This thing is bright. At 216W, it's throwing out roughly 25,000 lumens (the exact figure varies slightly by batch, but it's in that ballpark). For context, that's about double what a typical 4-foot, 40W LED tube produces. In a two-car garage, one of these tubes mounted lengthwise down the center is genuinely enough to work under without squinting.

The aluminum housing does double duty as a heat sink, which matters because these tubes run warm — not hot, but warm — and the passive cooling extends the LED lifespan. The light output is even across the full 8 feet with no dark spots at the ends, which is a common complaint with cheaper long tubes.

Buyers consistently praise the brightness-to-cost ratio. One reviewer noted, *"Replaced three 4-foot fluorescent fixtures with two of these. My garage looks like an operating room now."* Another said, *"The linkable feature is the sleeper hit — I ran four down the length of my workshop and it's like daylight at noon."*

**Where it falls short**

Beyond the ballast-bypass requirement, there are two things to know. First, no dimming. If you want to knock the brightness down for evening work, you're out of luck — it's on or off. Second, the matching connectors for linking tubes are included, but the wire between connectors is short. If your fixture spacing is wider than about 6 inches between tube mounts, you'll need to source your own jumper wire. A minor annoyance, but worth planning for.

**Who should buy this**

- **Garage and workshop owners** with existing fluorescent fixtures who want a significant brightness upgrade without replacing the whole housing
- **DIYers comfortable with basic wiring** — this is not your first electrical project
- **Anyone covering long runs** like basements, warehouses, or retail back rooms where linking 3–4 tubes from one switch saves real money on installation

Skip it if you need a plug-and-play replacement for a fixture with a working ballast, or if you want dimming control. Also skip if you're renting and can't modify the fixture wiring.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this work with a standard T8/T12 fixture?**
A: Only if your fixture has FA8 tombstones (double-pin, 8mm spacing). Standard T8 single-pin tombstones won't accept this tube without changing the sockets.

**Q: Is the ballast-bypass installation dangerous?**
A: Not if you kill the power at the breaker first and follow the wiring diagram. If you're unsure, pay an electrician — it's a 30-minute job and shouldn't cost much.

**Q: How long do these tubes actually last?**
A: Rated for 50,000 hours. With typical garage use (4 hours a day), that's 34 years. Realistically, the LEDs will outlast the fixture housing.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you have a fluorescent fixture with FA8 tombstones, you're comfortable with basic wiring, and you want serious light output for less than the cost of a comparable 4-foot LED setup. **Skip it if** you need plug-and-play or dimming.

4.2/5 stars. The ONLYLUX 8ft LED Tube Light FA8 earns its rating with raw brightness, easy linking, and solid heat management — just make sure you know what you're signing up for on the electrical side.
