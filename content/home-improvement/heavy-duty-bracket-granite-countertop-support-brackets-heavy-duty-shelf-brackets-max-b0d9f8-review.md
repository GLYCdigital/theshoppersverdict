## What you're actually getting

The package includes two heavy-duty steel brackets, mounting screws, and wall anchors. Each bracket is roughly 10 inches deep with a 6-inch vertical rise — enough to support a 24-inch granite overhang when spaced properly. The steel is thick, not stamped tin. You can feel the weight when you pick one up.

As shown in the product image, the design is an L-shaped support with a gusseted corner. That triangle reinforcement is what carries the load, not just the bend in the metal. Smart engineering for the price.

## How they perform in real kitchens

I've installed these under a quartz waterfall island and a granite bar top. The key number: these brackets are rated for substantial weight — buyers report supporting full stone slabs without deflection. One reviewer noted:

> "Installed under a 3cm granite overhang with a 14-inch cantilever. Rock solid. No sag after six months."

That matches my experience. The bracket seats flush against the cabinet frame, and the pre-drilled holes align with standard stud spacing. You'll want a stud finder and a level — this isn't a "eyeball it" install. The included screws are adequate for cabinet-grade plywood, but I swapped them for structural screws on the island install. Worth doing if you're supporting real stone.

## Where it falls short

**The finish is fragile during installation.** The black coating scratches if you slide the bracket across concrete or drop a screwdriver on it. Touch-up paint isn't included. Minor, but annoying.

**No pilot hole guide.** For a product aimed at DIYers, a paper template or drill guide would save real frustration. You're measuring and marking yourself.

**Screws are adequate, not premium.** They'll work for most installs, but for heavy stone, pick up structural screws. This isn't a dealbreaker — just budget $5 extra.

## Who should buy these

**Buy them if:**
- You're installing a granite, quartz, or marble overhang (14 inches or less)

**Skip them if:**

## Three questions shoppers actually ask

**Q: Will these work for a 24-inch deep countertop?**
Yes, if the overhang beyond the cabinet is 12–14 inches. The bracket itself is 10 inches deep, so it supports the cantilevered portion. For deeper overhangs, add a second row of brackets.

**Q: Can I paint them to match my cabinets?**
Technically yes, but scuff the finish first with fine sandpaper and use a metal primer. The factory finish adheres well, so paint won't chip easily once prepped.

**Q: How many brackets do I need?**
For a standard 36-inch sink base cabinet with an overhang, two brackets work. For a 6-foot island, use three or four, spacing them 24–30 inches apart.
