## Where It Falls Short

The square trim is a double-edged sword. Unlike round trims that have a slight flange to cover minor drywall imperfections, the NUWATT's square bezel sits tight against the ceiling with minimal overlap. If your existing cutout is slightly oversized or jagged, you'll see gaps. Measure your opening carefully — the trim covers roughly a 6.25-inch square, and anything larger will show edges.

Dimming is the second caveat. These work with standard TRIAC dimmers, but below 15% brightness, some buyers report a subtle flicker on older dimmer models. One reviewer noted, *"Works perfect on my newer Lutron dimmer, but flickered on the old rotary dimmer in the hallway."* If you have older dimmers, budget for a compatible upgrade.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these work with all 6-inch recessed cans?**
A: They fit standard 6-inch housings with torsion spring slots. If your can is an older model without spring slots, you may need an adapter kit. Measure the inner diameter first — it should be roughly 6.25 inches.

**Q: Can I use these on a dimmer switch?**
A: Yes, they're dimmable, but performance depends on your dimmer. Modern LED-compatible dimmers (like Lutron or Leviton) work cleanly. Older leading-edge dimmers may cause flicker below 15% brightness.

**Q: How do I change the color temperature after installation?**
A: You'll need to pull the panel down slightly to access the small switch on the housing. It's easy to reach but requires removing the fixture from the can — plan your color choice before final installation.
