Cabinet hardware is the jewelry of a kitchen — and like jewelry, you notice it immediately when it's wrong. The Asidrama 3768 knobs have earned a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, which puts them in rare territory for a product category where most buyers are just hoping the finish doesn't flake off in six months. **What you actually get**

The package contains 10 knobs with mounting screws. Each knob measures approximately 1.1 inches in diameter with a 0.9-inch projection — compact enough for standard cabinet doors and drawer fronts without looking dainty or overwhelming. The zinc alloy body gives them a satisfying heft that you don't find in cheaper aluminum or plastic alternatives. As shown in the product image, the brushed finish is uniform across the set, and buyers consistently confirm the color matches what's pictured — no surprise copper-tinted "brushed nickel" arriving at your doorstep.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The mounting hardware is where cheap knobs usually fall apart, and the Asidrama set handles this correctly. The screws thread cleanly into the knob base without stripping, and the included wrench (yes, there's one in the box) makes tightening a two-minute job per knob. One buyer noted: "I've installed hundreds of cabinet knobs in flips, and these are the first ones where I didn't have to fight with the screws." That's meaningful feedback from someone who's done this repeatedly.

The finish has held up in kitchens with daily use — no tarnishing reported in the feedback, even from buyers who've had them installed for over a year. The brushed texture does a reasonable job hiding water spots and smudges, though it's not as forgiving as a dark oil-rubbed bronze finish. If your household has kids who grab cabinet handles with sticky hands, you'll be wiping these down more often than you would darker hardware.

**Where it falls short**

The included screws are adequate for standard 3/4-inch cabinet doors, but if you're mounting these on thicker drawer fronts or adding a backplate, you'll need to buy longer screws. Several reviewers mentioned this, and it's worth planning for upfront rather than discovering mid-installation. Additionally, the rounded profile means there's less finger clearance than a longer T-bar pull — fine for most hands, but if you have large fingers or arthritis, test the grip before committing to a full set.

**Q: How do they hold up against moisture and cleaning chemicals?**
