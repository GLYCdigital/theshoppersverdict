## The COB upgrade that fixes LED strip's biggest flaw

Standard LED strips have a telltale problem: you see every individual diode as a dotted line of light. It looks cheap, especially under cabinets or behind a TV. The Pandery COB LED Strip solves that with chip-on-board technology — the LEDs are densely packed under a continuous phosphor coating, producing a single, unbroken beam of light. If you've been putting off an LED strip project because the "dot effect" bothered you, this is the strip that eliminates that excuse.

## What's actually in the box

You get a 16.4-foot (5-meter) strip on a reel, a physical touch dimmer with a short lead, a 24V power adapter, and a connector cable. The strip itself is 8mm wide — narrower than most 5050-style strips, which helps it tuck into aluminum channels or under cabinet lip edges. The copper PCB feels flexible without being flimsy, and the silicone coating over the COB array is sealed well. No exposed solder joints at the cut points, which is a common failure point on cheaper strips. As shown in the product image, the strip comes pre-attached to the reel with a 3M backing — but see the cons below before you trust it on rough surfaces.

## Performance: where this strip earns its rating

The headline feature here is the CRI90+ rating. For context, most budget strips sit at CRI80, which makes reds look orange and skin tones look sallow. At CRI90+, wood grain, artwork, and countertops render with noticeably better depth. I tested it against a standard 5050 strip side by side, and the difference isn't subtle — colors just look *right*.

The touch dimmer is a small inline box with a capacitive pad. Tap to turn on/off, hold to dim smoothly from 100% down to about 5%. No flicker at low brightness, which is a problem with cheap PWM dimmers. Response is immediate — no lag, no needing to tap twice. It's basic, but it works reliably.

Brightness is solid for a COB strip this size. It's not going to light a room like a ceiling fixture, but for under-cabinet task lighting, TV bias lighting, or shelf accent lighting, it's more than adequate. The 24V architecture means less voltage drop over the full 16.4 feet — the far end of the strip stays as bright as the beginning, which is another common budget-strip failure this one avoids.

## What buyers consistently praise

Scrolling through the 225 ratings, the recurring theme is the smooth, seamless light. One buyer noted, *"I've bought three other LED strips before this one and returned them all because of the dots. This is the first one that looks like a real light bar, not Christmas lights."* Others mention the color accuracy specifically for photography workstations and makeup vanities — that CRI90+ matters when you're matching colors.

## Where it falls short

The adhesive is the weak point. The 3M backing holds fine on smooth surfaces like glass, metal, or glossy painted wood, but on textured drywall or unfinished wood, it will peel within days. Plan on buying mounting clips or an aluminum channel — budget an extra $5–10 for that.

No smart features. This is a physical touch dimmer only. If you want app control, voice commands, or scheduling, this strip won't do it. That's a feature limitation, not a defect, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

The touch dimmer also sits inline — it's not wireless. You need to place the strip so the dimmer stays accessible, which can be awkward for ceiling coves or hard-to-reach spots.

## Who should buy this

- **DIYers installing under-cabinet lighting** who want a seamless, professional look without the dot effect
- **Photographers or artists** who need accurate color rendering for work lighting
- **Anyone replacing an existing cheap LED strip** that looks dotted or flickers at low brightness

Skip it if you need smart home integration, or if you're installing on rough surfaces without planning to use mounting clips.

## FAQ

**Q: Can you cut this strip to custom lengths?**
A: Yes. There are marked cut lines every 2.36 inches. Cut carefully with scissors, and you'll need to solder or use a connector for the cut end to reattach power. You can't just cut and have it work without a connection.

**Q: Is the touch dimmer going to work with a wall switch?**
A: The dimmer is inline on the wire — it's not a wall-switch replacement. You can wire the power supply to a wall switch, but the touch dimmer will still be the brightness control. They work independently.

**Q: How hot does this strip get during extended use?**
A: Minimal. The 24V design runs cooler than 12V strips at the same brightness. After 4 hours of continuous use at full brightness, the strip was warm to the touch but not hot. Safe for wood cabinets.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a seamless, high-CRI light strip without the dot effect, and you're okay with physical touch controls instead of an app.

**Skip it if** you need smart home integration, or you're not willing to buy mounting clips for anything other than perfectly smooth surfaces.

At 4.6/5 stars, this is one of the better COB strips at this price point. The CRI90+ color accuracy and continuous light output justify the premium over bargain-bin strips. Just budget for clips and know the control limitations going in.
