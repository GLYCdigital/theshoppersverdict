**What you actually get**

The package contains a single bulb with a standard E26 medium base — the same screw-in size as most household lamps. That's the smart move here: it drops into virtually any refrigerator, oven, range hood, or microwave light socket without an adapter. The glass is frosted, which matters more than you'd think. Bare filament bulbs create a harsh spotlight in a fridge, making the back corners look shadowed. The frosted envelope scatters light evenly, so you can actually see the jar of pickles hiding in the back. As one buyer put it: "Brighter than the OEM bulb but not blinding — perfect for seeing into my deep freezer."

**Performance and build quality**

The Maelsrlg runs on standard 120V and draws minimal wattage — typically 25W equivalent output with far less heat than the old incandescents it replaces. That's a meaningful upgrade for fridge use. A cooler bulb means your compressor doesn't work as hard to maintain temperature, which is both an energy savings and a food-safety win. Multiple reviewers noted their fridge temperature stayed more stable after switching.

The filament and base construction feel solid. I've seen cheap appliance bulbs where the glass loosens from the base after a few thermal cycles — the Maelsrlg uses a firmer adhesive bond, and no buyers report rattling or separation issues. One long-term reviewer confirmed: "Installed six months ago in my oven and it's still going strong despite daily 400°F heat."

**Where it falls short**

Two honest caveats. First, this bulb is **not dimmable**. If you're putting it in a range hood with a dimmer switch, skip it — you'll get flicker and potential buzzing. Second, the glass envelope is slightly larger than some OEM bulbs. In a cramped oven cavity or an older refrigerator with a deeply recessed socket, you might struggle to seat it fully. One reviewer noted: "Fits my 2021 Whirlpool fridge fine, but I had to angle it carefully in my 2009 GE oven." Measure your socket clearance before ordering if you're working with older appliances.
