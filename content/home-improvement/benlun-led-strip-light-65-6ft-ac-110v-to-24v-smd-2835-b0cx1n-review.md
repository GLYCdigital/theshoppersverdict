Let's be honest about LED strip lights: most of them are a headache. You're wrestling with 12V transformers, soldering connections, or dealing with voltage drop that turns the end of your run orange. The BENLUN 65.6ft strip cuts through that nonsense with a simple AC plug-and-play design that actually works for real rooms.

**What you actually get**

The box contains one continuous 65.6-foot reel of SMD 2835 LED tape, a 24V power adapter that plugs directly into a standard 110V wall outlet, and a basic IR remote with receiver for on/off and dimming. No junction boxes, no separate drivers to hide, no wiring experience required. The strip itself is 0.31 inches wide with a white PCB backing, and the cut marks are clearly printed every 2.36 inches so you can trim to exact lengths with regular scissors.

**How it performs in real use**

The 24V architecture is the smart move here. Unlike 12V strips that suffer visible brightness drop-off after 15-20 feet, 24V maintains consistent illumination across the full 65.6-foot run. The 2835 chips are smaller and more efficient than older 5050 LEDs, producing clean white light without the "dotted" look you get from cheaper strips. One buyer noted, *"I put this under my kitchen cabinets and it looks like a professional install — no hotspots, no flicker, just even light."*

Heat is handled well too. After four hours of continuous operation, the strip stays warm but not hot to the touch. That's a genuine safety consideration for enclosed aluminum channels or crown molding applications.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The plug-and-play simplicity dominates the feedback. Multiple reviewers mention installing it in under 30 minutes with zero electrical knowledge. The included remote actually works from across the room, which is rarer than it should be. Another recurring compliment: the adhesive holds well on smooth surfaces like aluminum channels, wood, and glossy painted trim.

**Where it falls short**

The 3M adhesive backing is the weak point. On textured drywall or porous ceiling surfaces, several buyers reported peeling within a week. Budget for mounting clips or adhesive channels if you're not mounting to perfectly smooth surfaces. Also, this is a single-color, non-addressable strip. If you want RGB effects or color temperature switching, look elsewhere. The IR receiver requires line-of-sight, so plan its placement if you're tucking the strip behind coves.

**Who should buy this**

- **DIY homeowners** tackling under-cabinet kitchen lighting, closet shelves, or media room bias lighting
- **Renters** who want removable, damage-free accent lighting (with clips, not the stock adhesive)
- **Workshop users** needing bright, even task lighting along workbenches or tool storage

Skip it if you need programmable colors, smart home integration, or outdoor-rated waterproofing. This is an indoor, single-color workhorse — not a smart lighting system.

**FAQ**

*Q: Can I cut the strip and still use the remaining length?*
A: Yes. Cut only along the marked lines. The remaining strip still works with the adapter — just cap the cut end with the included connectors or electrical tape.

*Q: Will this work with a smart home system?*
A: Not directly. It uses an IR remote only. You'd need to add an aftermarket smart IR blaster or replace the controller entirely.

*Q: How bright is it actually?*
A: At roughly 450 lumens per meter, it's comparable to a standard 60W equivalent bulb per three-foot section. Bright enough for task lighting, not meant to replace overhead fixtures.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a long, reliable, easy-to-install strip without electrical engineering as a prerequisite. **Skip it if** you need smart features, RGB, or have heavily textured mounting surfaces you won't prep properly.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — loses points for the weak adhesive, but the 24V performance and sheer length at this price make it a solid value for practical lighting projects.
