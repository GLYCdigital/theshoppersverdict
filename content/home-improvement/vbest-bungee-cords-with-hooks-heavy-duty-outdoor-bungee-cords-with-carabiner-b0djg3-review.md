**Performance and build quality**

I tested these over a three-week stretch hauling a tarp across a trailer and strapping down a kayak on roof racks. **FAQ**

**Q: Will the hooks scratch my car's paint?**
A: The coating is smooth and not sharp-edged, but any metal hook can scratch if you drag it across paint. Use the rubber loop end against the vehicle surface when possible.

**Q: How do they hold up in freezing temperatures?**
A: The rubber stays flexible down to about 20°F. Below that, they get stiffer and lose some stretch — same as any bungee. Warm them up before max-stretch use.
