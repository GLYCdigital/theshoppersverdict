Some plumbing repairs feel like they require a degree in pipefitting. The Artiwell Pop-Up Drain Stopper Assembly isn't one of them. This is a drop-in replacement that solves the most common bathroom sink failure—a broken stopper mechanism—without the headache of custom fitting or calling a plumber.

**What's Actually in the Box**

The kit is refreshingly complete. You get the pop-up stopper body, the drain flange, a lift rod with knob, the clevis strap, the pivot rod, and the overflow plate with connecting rod. All the mounting hardware—locknuts, washers, and gaskets—is included. As shown in the product image, every piece is machined from solid brass with a brushed nickel finish that looks far more expensive than the asking price suggests.

**Real-World Performance**

Installation runs 20 to 40 minutes for most DIYers, and I found the tolerances genuinely impressive. The stopper seats flush against the flange with no wobble, and the lift rod action is smooth—no grinding, no sticking, just a clean up-and-down motion. The brass body threads cut cleanly into standard PVC drain pipes, and the gaskets seal without needing excessive torque.

The stopper itself has a nice positive seal when closed. Water stays put, and when you lift the rod, it drains fully without leaving a puddle. The overflow connection is properly sized, which matters more than most people realize—an undersized overflow tube causes gurgling and slow drainage.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews isn't accidental. Buyers repeatedly mention the build quality and complete kit. One reviewer noted, "Everything fit perfectly. I didn't need to make a trip to the hardware store for extra parts, which never happens." Another praised the finish: "Looks like a $60 faucet part, not a budget replacement."

The universal fit is the standout feature. It handles both 1-1/4-inch and 1-1/2-inch drain openings, which covers virtually every residential bathroom sink. That flexibility alone saves the frustration of buying the wrong size and starting over.

**Where It Falls Short**

Be honest about the linkage. The rod assembly is standard length, which works for typical drop-in sinks but can bind in deep vessel bowls or undermount basins with unusual geometry. If your sink has a tall profile, measure the distance from the overflow opening to the faucet deck before ordering. Some buyers report needing to bend the clevis strap slightly for a smooth action.

The finish is also a shade lighter than major brands like Delta or Moen. Against a fresh fixture it's a near-match, but if you're pairing it with an existing brushed nickel faucet that's a few years old, the slight difference may be visible. It's a minor aesthetic issue, not a functional one.

**Who Should Buy This**

The Artiwell is ideal for three types of shoppers: DIYers replacing a failed stopper who want a complete kit without extra trips to the store; landlords or property managers who need a reliable, universal replacement that works across multiple sinks; and anyone with a standard bathroom vanity who wants solid brass construction without paying premium brand prices.

Skip it if you have a non-standard sink configuration—deep vessel bowls, custom undermounts, or unusual drain angles—or if exact color matching with existing fixtures is critical to you.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Will this fit my 1-1/4-inch drain?**
Yes. The kit includes adapters that accommodate both 1-1/4-inch and 1-1/2-inch drain openings. The threaded body size matches standard US plumbing.

**Q: Do I need plumber's putty or Teflon tape?**
Plumber's putty is recommended for the flange seal, though the included rubber gasket works if you prefer a cleaner installation. The threaded connections benefit from a light wrap of Teflon tape.

**Q: How do I adjust the lift rod travel?**
The clevis strap has multiple holes. Loosen the screw, move the pivot rod to a different hole to change the lift amount, then retighten. It takes about 30 seconds.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a complete, well-made pop-up drain assembly that installs cleanly and works reliably for years. The brass construction and universal fit make this a genuinely smart purchase for the price.

**Skip it if** you have a non-standard sink setup that requires custom-length rods, or if exact finish matching with existing fixtures matters more than function.
