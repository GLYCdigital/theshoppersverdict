If you're lighting a warehouse, workshop, or garage and you're still running old metal halide fixtures, you know the pain: the hum, the warm-up delay, the electric bill that makes you wince. The 350W UFO LED high bay light is the modern replacement that solves all of that. And at 4.6 stars from 229 ratings, it's clearly doing something right.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the fixture itself — a circular UFO-style housing with an integrated LED array — plus a mounting bracket and hook. The unit measures roughly 13 inches in diameter and stands about 6 inches tall, with a weight that tells you it's built with real aluminum, not cheap plastic. You get a standard 3-wire pigtail for hardwiring. No remote, no smart features, no dimmer — just a straightforward light that does one thing well.

## Performance: The Numbers Match the Hype

This thing is bright. We're talking 45,000+ lumens from a 350W draw. That's the equivalent of replacing a 1000W metal halide fixture while cutting energy consumption by roughly 65%. The beam angle sits around 90 degrees, which gives you a focused, concentrated pool of light — ideal for mounting heights of 15 to 25 feet. If you're installing it lower than that, you'll want to prepare for some serious glare.

The color temperature options run from 3000K warm white to 5000K daylight. Most buyers opt for the 5000K for workshops and garages because it makes details pop and reduces eye strain during detailed work. The CRI is respectable at 80+, which means colors look natural rather than washed out.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The aluminum heat sink is the star here. High bay lights generate serious heat, and cheap units die fast because their drivers cook themselves. This one uses a proper finned heat sink that dissipates heat effectively. One reviewer noted, "I've run these 10-12 hours a day in an uninsulated shop through a Texas summer. They get warm to the touch but never hot, and no flicker or failure after six months." That matches what I've seen from similar units in this price class.

The IP65 rating is another point in its favor. That means it's fully sealed against dust and resistant to water jets — so it'll survive in a dusty workshop, a barn, or a covered outdoor area without issue.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring theme in the reviews is the "wow" factor when you first flip the switch. One verified purchase wrote: "Walked into my shop and thought I left the door open — it's that bright. My old 400W metal halide looks like a candle next to this." Another mentioned the easy installation: "Hardwired in 20 minutes. The bracket design makes it simple to position exactly where you need it."

Energy savings also come up repeatedly. Several buyers reported their electric bills dropping noticeably after swapping out multiple old fixtures for these.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has two notable issues. First, there's no dimming option on the standard model. If you're lighting a smaller space or want to adjust brightness based on the time of day, you're out of luck. You'll need to look for a dimmable version or add a separate dimmer switch if your setup supports it.

Second, the included mounting hook is underwhelming. It works, but it's thin and doesn't inspire confidence for a fixture this heavy. The bracket mount is much more secure — use that instead. A few reviewers mentioned they'd have preferred a stronger hanging kit out of the box.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Workshop owners** replacing old metal halide or fluorescent fixtures — this is your upgrade.
- **Warehouse managers** with mounting heights of 15-25 feet who need consistent, bright light.
- **Garage enthusiasts** who want showroom-level illumination for detailing or fabrication work.

**Skip it if** you need dimming capabilities, or if your ceiling height is under 12 feet — you'll find the output overwhelming.
