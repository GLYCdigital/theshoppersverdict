Let's be blunt about bathroom storage: most medicine cabinets are flimsy aluminum boxes with mirrors that distort your face. The Maxstow isn't that — but it's not perfect either. Here's the real breakdown after digging through 215 ratings and the specs that matter.

**What you actually get**

The box includes the cabinet body, mirrored door, two adjustable tempered-glass shelves, mounting hardware, and a paper template for marking studs. No tools included, so budget for a drill and level if you don't own them. The cabinet itself is steel with a baked-on white enamel finish — not the thin aluminum you'll find at big-box stores. The door is a standard 1/4-inch mirror with beveled edges.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The hinges are the standout feature. They're full-extension with soft-close dampening, which means the door doesn't slam even if you let it go. That's rare at this price. The shelves lock into place with metal clips — no wobble when you grab a bottle. One reviewer noted, "The soft-close mechanism makes me feel like I spent way more than I did." That tracks with the 4.3-star average.

The mirror itself is decent quality — clear reflection with minimal distortion. But it's a fingerprint magnet. Multiple buyers mention wiping it down weekly. That's cosmetic, not functional, but worth knowing if you have kids.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest issue is the recessed installation. The cabinet body is 4 inches deep, requiring a wall cavity that depth. Standard interior walls with 2x4 studs are only 3.5 inches deep. You'll need furring strips or a surface-mount kit, which adds cost and complexity. One buyer put it plainly: "I didn't measure the wall depth before ordering. Now I have a cabinet sitting on my bathroom floor waiting for a contractor." Measure your wall cavity before you buy.

The included template is helpful, but the screw holes aren't slotted — so if you're a few millimeters off, you'll fight to align the cabinet flush. Take your time marking.

**Who should buy this**

- **DIY homeowners** with a bathroom remodel underway — the clean lines work with modern or transitional fixtures
- **Anyone replacing an old recessed cabinet** in a custom-built wall — you already have the depth
- **Renters** who need surface-mount storage — just grab a mounting kit separately

**Skip it if** you're installing into a standard 2x4 wall without the ability to add depth, or if you want a frameless, fully seamless look — the white frame is visible around the door edge.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can this be installed as a surface-mount cabinet?**
A: Yes, but you'll need to buy a separate mounting kit or build a frame. The included hardware is designed for recessed installation only.

**Q: How much weight can the shelves hold?**
A: Each of the two glass shelves supports up to 15 pounds. That's enough for standard toiletries, but don't stack heavy shampoo bottles on one shelf.

**Q: Does the mirror fog up after hot showers?**
A: It's a standard mirror with no anti-fog coating. Expect condensation if your bathroom lacks ventilation. A quick wipe handles it.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you have the wall depth for recessed mounting and want soft-close convenience without paying boutique prices. **Skip it if** you're working with standard 2x4 walls or need surface-mount simplicity.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid value with one significant installation caveat. Measure your wall before you commit.
