A book light is one of those purchases that seems simple until you've tried five of them. The cheap ones flicker. The expensive ones have arms that sag after a month. The "rechargeable" ones die at 9 PM when you're two chapters from finishing. So when a light shows up with 225 ratings, a 4.5 average, and a 4.6 verdict score, it earns a hard look. Here's the real story after hands-on testing.

**What's in the box, exactly**

The package includes the light itself, a USB-C charging cable (about 2 feet, no wall adapter), and a basic instruction sheet. The build is matte plastic — nothing premium, but nothing flimsy either. The gooseneck arm measures roughly 12 inches fully extended, and the head swivels about 180 degrees. The clip underneath has a rubber pad to protect your book cover. Total weight is 3.2 ounces, which is light enough that you won't notice it on a hardcover.

**Performance where it matters**

The headline feature is the three color temperatures: warm white (3000K), neutral (4500K), and cool (6000K). That's genuinely useful. Warm for bedtime reading when you don't want to wreck your sleep cycle, cool for detailed work like cross-stitching or reading fine print in a manual. Each mode has stepless dimming via a touch-sensitive strip on the arm — hold it to ramp brightness up or down. It responds quickly, no lag.

Battery life is the spec people actually care about. On the lowest warm setting, I got 22 hours of continuous use. On max brightness with cool white, it dropped to about 6 hours. Realistic mixed use lands around 12-14 hours, which is solid for a $20-ish light. The USB-C charging port is on the clip base, and a full charge takes about 2.5 hours.

One thing I tested specifically: the gooseneck. This is where cheap lights fail. After a week of daily bending and repositioning, the arm still holds its angle without sagging. That's rare at this price.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The most common thread in reviews is the adjustable color temperature. One reviewer wrote, *"I didn't think I'd use the warm setting, but now it's all I use — my wife can sleep next to me with the light on and she never wakes up."* Another said, *"The dimming is what sold me. My old light had two settings: blinding or useless."* Multiple reviewers mention the clip holding firm on books without damaging pages — the rubber pad does its job.

**Where it falls short**

The clip has a practical ceiling. It opens to about 0.8 inches, which grips standard paperbacks and most hardcovers fine. But if you're reading a thick textbook, a spiral-bound manual, or a tablet in a folio case, you're out of luck. You'll be clipping it to a table edge instead, which changes the light angle.

The charging indicator is a small LED that stays lit the entire time the device is plugged in. In a dark bedroom, that's a glowing distraction. It's a minor thing, but for a product marketed specifically for bedtime reading, it's an oversight.

Finally, the cool white setting has a noticeable blue tint. It's fine for task lighting but not pleasant for extended reading. Stick with warm or neutral for that.

**Who should buy this**

- **Bedside readers** who need a light that won't wake a partner — the warm setting and dimming make this ideal.
- **Travelers** who want one light for planes, hotels, and camping. The compact size and USB-C charging are traveler-friendly.
- **Crafters and hobbyists** who need adjustable brightness for detail work — the cool setting and gooseneck position help here.

**Skip it if** you need a clip for thick textbooks, or you want a light with a built-in timer (this one doesn't have one).

**FAQ**

**Q: Can this light be used hands-free around the neck?**
A: No. It's clip-mount only. There's no neckband option.

**Q: How long does the battery last on a single charge?**
A: 6 hours at max brightness, up to 22 hours on the lowest warm setting. Realistic mixed use is roughly 12-14 hours.

**Q: Does it work while charging?**
A: Yes, you can use it plugged in. The charging light stays on, which may be distracting in a dark room.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a versatile, well-built book light with genuine color temperature control and reliable battery life. It outperforms lights twice its price in the gooseneck durability department.

**Skip it if** you need a wide clip for thick books, or you're sensitive to the always-on charging indicator.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A solid, honest performer that does the core job well and gets the details right, with only minor tradeoffs.
