You've already dropped serious money on a reverse osmosis system. Now you're staring at the replacement filter schedule and wondering if the alkaline stage is worth the extra cost — or if you can just skip it and save twenty bucks. Here's the straight answer: if you care about water taste and pH, the alkaline cartridge is the one filter you shouldn't skip. Here's why.

This listing is for the **Express Water 1-Year Alkaline Replacement Filter Cartridge**, part of the ALK filter set for their under-sink RO systems. It's the final stage in a multi-stage filtration process, designed to remineralize water after RO strips out everything — including the good stuff like calcium, magnesium, and potassium.

**What you actually get:** One sealed alkaline filter cartridge. No wrench, no tubing, no extra fittings. The cartridge itself is a standard-size 10-inch filter with quick-connect fittings on both ends, so installation is genuinely straightforward. As shown in the product image, it's a white cylindrical cartridge with clear labeling of the filtration media inside — a blend of natural minerals that dissolve slowly over the filter's lifespan.

**Performance and build quality:** The alkaline stage works by passing the RO water through a bed of mineral media that raises pH and adds back trace minerals. In real-world use, this translates to water that tastes noticeably smoother — less flat than straight RO water. The mineral blend is designed for a 12-month service life, which is longer than the 6-month cycle some brands use. That's a genuine advantage: fewer changeouts, less hassle, and roughly the same annual cost as replacing two cheaper cartridges.

The cartridge body is well-constructed, with a sturdy outer shell that doesn't feel flimsy when you handle it. The quick-connect fittings seat firmly without leaking — a common failure point on cheaper filters. Multiple buyers confirm this: one reviewer noted, "Installed in minutes, no leaks, and the water tastes great. My family actually drinks more water now." Another mentioned, "The mineral taste is noticeable but not overwhelming. It's a clean, crisp flavor."

**Where it falls short:** Compatibility is the big one. This cartridge only works with Express Water RO systems that use their standard filter housing configuration. If you own a different brand — APEC, iSpring, Home Master — this won't fit without adapters. Also, the 1/4-inch quick-connect fittings require you to have a filter wrench for your housing (included with your original system, but easy to misplace). Some users also report that the alkaline stage doesn't dramatically raise pH — it typically brings water to around 8.5-9.5 pH, which is fine for drinking but won't turn your tap into a high-alkaline wellness brand.

**Who should buy this:** If you own an Express Water RO system and your current alkaline filter is due for replacement, this is the exact OEM replacement you need. It's also a smart buy for anyone who's noticed their RO water tasting flat or lifeless — the mineral addition makes a real difference. If you're on a budget and only care about contaminant removal, you can skip the alkaline stage, but you'll notice the taste difference.

**FAQ:**

**Q: How often do I need to replace this filter?**  
A: Every 12 months or 1,200 gallons, whichever comes first. If you have a large family or hard water, you may need to change it closer to the 10-month mark.

**Q: Will this fit my non-Express Water RO system?**  
A: Probably not without adapters. The quick-connect fittings are standard 1/4-inch, but the housing size and connections are proprietary to Express Water systems.

**Q: Does the alkaline filter actually change the pH?**  
A: Yes, but modestly — expect water in the 8.5–9.5 pH range. It's a noticeable improvement over pure RO water (around 7.0), but not a miracle cure.
