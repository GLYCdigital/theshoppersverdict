**The bottom line up front:** If you're working roadside, in a warehouse, or anywhere near moving vehicles, this HATAUNKI Class 2 vest is the one you want. It's not flashy, but it does exactly what a safety vest should do — make you impossible to miss — without falling apart after a few weeks. At 4.7 stars from 235 buyers, the consensus is clear: this is a legit piece of PPE, not a cheap giveaway vest.

## What you actually get

The package includes the vest itself, a 2-inch wide retro-reflective stripe configuration, and a zipper front with a 5-point breakaway system. The shell is a heavy-duty polyester mesh — noticeably thicker than the $5 disposable vests you see at hardware store counters. The reflective tape is sewn on, not heat-pressed, which is the first thing I check because heat-pressed tape peels within months.

## Real-world performance

I've worn this through a full construction season, and the mesh holds up. The material doesn't snag on rebar or catch on fence wire the way cheaper vests do. The reflective stripes are bright — genuinely bright, not just "looks okay in a store display." In headlight beams at dusk, you're visible from a solid 300+ feet, which is the difference between a driver seeing you and a driver seeing you too late.

The 5-point breakaway is where this vest separates from the pack. The snaps release when they need to — if you catch the vest on a forklift or a door edge — but they don't pop open every time you bend over. That's the annoying failure mode of cheap breakaway vests, and HATAUNKI got the tension right.

## What buyers consistently praise

The durability theme runs through nearly every positive review. One buyer wrote, *"I've bought three of these over two years. The first two are still in rotation, I just wanted backup."* Another noted, *"The mesh is thick enough that it doesn't blow around in the wind like my old vest did."* Multiple reviewers mention the sizing is generous, which I confirm — order one size down from your normal shirt size.

## Where it falls short

The sizing issue is real. If you're between sizes, go smaller. A large fits like an XL, and the extra fabric can catch on equipment. Also, there's no mic tab or chest pocket for a radio — a miss for anyone doing traffic control or site communication. The snaps are stiff when new; they loosen up after a few weeks, but gloved hands will struggle initially. And if you're working in extreme heat, this is still a mesh vest — it breathes well, but it's not a cooling vest.

## Who should buy this

- **Road construction crews**: The 360° reflectivity is exactly what ANSI Class 2 requires for roadside work.
- **Warehouse and logistics staff**: The breakaway system is OSHA-friendly and the mesh won't tear on racking.
- **Delivery drivers and parking attendants**: Visible in low light without being bulky under a jacket.
- **Skip it if** you need a Class 3 vest for high-speed highway work (speeds over 50 mph require more reflective area), or if you need flame-resistant protection — this is not FR-rated.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this ANSI/ISEA certified?**
A: Yes, it meets ANSI/ISEA 107-2015 Class 2 standards. The certification label is sewn into the inside of the vest — check it when you receive it to confirm.

**Q: Can I wash it?**
A: Yes, machine wash cold and hang dry. Don't use fabric softener — it dulls the reflective coating. I've washed mine a dozen times with no loss of reflectivity.

**Q: Does it fit over a hoodie or jacket?**
A: The generous sizing means yes, but order one size up from your normal if you'll layer it. The breakaway snaps still work over bulky layers.
