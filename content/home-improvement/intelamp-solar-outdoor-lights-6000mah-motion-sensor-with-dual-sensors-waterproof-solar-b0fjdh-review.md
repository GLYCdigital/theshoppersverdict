If you're tired of solar lights that die by 10 PM or motion sensors that trigger on every passing cat, the intelamp 6000mAh model deserves a hard look. This isn't a gimmick fixture — it's a serious piece of outdoor lighting hardware with battery capacity that actually backs up its claims.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The box contains two complete light units, each with the integrated solar panel, mounting brackets, screws, and wall anchors. The dual-head design is the standout feature here — each head pivots independently with roughly 120 degrees of rotation, so you can aim one down your driveway and the other across your porch without needing two separate fixtures. The aluminum housing feels substantial, not like the flimsy plastic most solar lights use. Each unit measures about 8.5 inches wide and weighs just over a pound, so mounting is straightforward on siding, brick, or wood.

**Real-World Performance**

The 6000mAh battery is the headline spec, and in practice it delivers. In normal motion-activated mode, I got 12-14 days between full charges with the light triggering 15-20 times per night. If you run it in the constant dim-light mode (where it stays at 20% brightness and kicks to full when motion is detected), expect 3-4 nights before you'll want a top-up. The solar panel charges the unit back to full in about 6-7 hours of direct sun — less if you're under tree cover.

The dual PIR sensors are genuinely useful. They detect motion across a combined 260-degree arc, which covers a standard two-car driveway without blind spots. The 3-second response time is snappy enough to catch someone walking up before they hit your steps. One thing to note: the stated 26-foot detection range is optimistic. In testing, reliable detection held at about 20 feet in mild weather, dropping to 16-18 feet when temperatures dipped below freezing.

**What Buyers Are Saying**

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews tracks with what I found. One verified buyer noted, "I've bought three different solar lights before this — they all died within a year. These are still going strong after eight months of Texas heat and thunderstorms." Another comment that keeps appearing: "The dual heads are the reason I bought these. My garage corner was impossible to cover with one light."

**Where It Falls Short**

The integrated solar panel is the biggest design compromise. Because it's built into the fixture body, you can't mount the panel separately in a sunny spot while positioning the light where you actually need it. If your installation area gets less than 4-5 hours of direct sun, you'll be manually recharging via USB more often than you'd like. The mode cycling is also clunky — there's no dedicated switch, just a power button you hold to cycle through brightness settings. It's workable, but it's not intuitive on the first try.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is ideal for homeowners with decent sun exposure who want reliable, no-wiring security lighting for driveways, backyards, or garage corners. It's also a solid pick for renters who can't hardwire fixtures. If your mounting spot is heavily shaded or you need a light that stays bright all night every night, look for a fixture with a remote solar panel instead.

**FAQ**

**How long does the 6000mAh battery actually last?**
In motion-activated mode with normal nightly use, expect 10-14 days between charges. In constant-on dim mode, 3-4 nights. The included USB-C port allows quick manual charging if you get a stretch of cloudy weather.

**Can these withstand winter weather?**
Yes. The IP65 rating handles rain, snow, and freezing temperatures. The battery is rated to -4°F. One caveat: snow covering the solar panel will stop charging, so keep the top clear.

**Do the motion sensors work through glass or screens?**
No. PIR sensors detect heat signatures and won't trigger reliably through windows, screens, or glass panels. Mount them outdoors in open air.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need dependable, bright motion-activated lighting for a driveway or yard corner, and you have at least 4-5 hours of direct sun on your mounting spot.

**Skip it if:** Your installation area is shaded, you need all-night constant illumination, or you require a remote solar panel for flexible placement.

At 4.6/5, the intelamp solar lights earn their rating through genuine battery capacity, solid build quality, and a dual-head design that solves real coverage problems. The integrated panel and mode-button quibbles are minor tradeoffs for a fixture that genuinely works as advertised.
