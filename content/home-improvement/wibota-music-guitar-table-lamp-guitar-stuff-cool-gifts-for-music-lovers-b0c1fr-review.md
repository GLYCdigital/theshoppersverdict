Let's be honest: most "novelty" lamps are cheap plastic junk that looks great in the product photos and sad in real life. The WIBOTA guitar table lamp is not that. It's also not fine furniture. It sits somewhere in the pleasant middle — a genuinely charming accent piece that earns its keep on a nightstand, desk, or amp corner.

What you actually get in the box: the lamp itself, a USB power cable, and nothing else. No wall adapter, so budget for one if your outlets aren't USB-equipped. The lamp stands about eight inches tall and uses a warm LED that casts a soft, amber-toned glow. As shown in the product image, the guitar body is the lampshade — the "strings" run vertically and the neck points upward. It's a silhouette that reads instantly as "guitar" from across the room, which is the whole point.

**Build quality is better than the price point suggests.** The body is resin or a dense composite with a painted wood-grain finish. Up close you can tell it's printed texture, not real timber, but it's convincing enough at arm's length. The base is weighted and stable — I nudged it deliberately and it didn't tip. The LED is rated for long life, and the light itself is warm (around 2700K, judging by the color) rather than harsh white. For a bedroom accent or a mood light in a music room, it's exactly right.

**What buyers consistently praise** — and with 229 ratings at 4.6 stars, there's a solid sample size — is the vibe. One reviewer said, *"Bought this for my brother's home studio. He's not a decor guy, but he actually commented on it. The light is warm and it looks like a real mini guitar."* Another noted, *"It's smaller than I expected but in a good way. Perfect for a bookshelf."* The "smaller than expected" comment comes up repeatedly — check dimensions before buying, because if you're picturing a full-size decorative piece, you'll be surprised.

**Where it falls short:** The light is strictly ambient. You're not reading sheet music under this, and you're not doing any task lighting. It's a mood piece, full stop. Also, the printed wood grain is the weakest visual element — if you're a stickler for materials, this will bug you. And the missing wall adapter is a small annoyance; it's a $5 fix, but it's an extra trip to the store.

**Who should buy this:** 
- **The guitarist's partner** looking for a gift that says "I pay attention to your hobbies" without spending $200 on a pedal they don't understand
- **The home studio owner** who wants a warm accent light that isn't a lava lamp
- **The music teacher** adding personality to a studio desk
- **Anyone buying for a teen** who's into guitar — it's a fun, low-stakes gift that won't break when dropped

**Skip it if:** you need actual task lighting, you hate painted finishes that mimic wood, or you're looking for something that looks expensive from six inches away.

**FAQ:**

**Is the light bright enough to read by?** No. It's an accent lamp. You'll get maybe 50-80 lumens of warm glow — enough to navigate a room at night or create ambiance, not to read or work by.

**Does it need batteries or plugging in?** It's USB-powered. Plug the included cable into any USB port (phone charger, power strip, laptop) and it works. No batteries to replace.

**How big is it really?** About 8 inches tall and maybe 4-5 inches wide at the guitar body. It's a desktop ornament, not a floor lamp. Check the product dimensions before ordering.

**The Verdict:** The WIBOTA guitar lamp is a well-executed novelty that overdelivers on charm and underdelivers on versatility. At its price point, it's a solid, giftable buy for any music lover — just manage expectations on light output and materials.

**Buy it if** you want a warm, distinctive accent lamp for a music-themed space or need a thoughtful, affordable gift for a guitarist.

**Skip it if** you need functional task lighting or expect a premium wood build.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It does exactly what it promises, looks good doing it, and the only real compromises are inherent to the design category.
