The DEWALT TOUGHSYSTEM 2.0 Large Tool Box is the box you buy when you're done replacing cheap plastic totes every six months. It's the 22-inch workhorse of DEWALT's modular storage lineup, rated to carry 110 pounds and built like a miniature shipping container. But at this price point, you need to know exactly what you're getting.

**What's actually in the box**

You get the tool box itself — 22 inches long, roughly 16 inches wide, and 12.5 inches tall. There's a removable tote tray inside that splits the interior into two levels, which is handy for keeping drills on top and bits, fasteners, or impact sockets below. The lid has a deep storage well under the handle, so you can stash a small parts organizer or a level up there. The box weighs about 11 pounds empty, which is heavier than the cheap stuff — that's the cost of the reinforced plastic.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The defining feature here is the upgraded latching system. DEWALT ditched the flimsy snap-latches from the original ToughSystem for metal-reinforced, over-center latches that click shut with real authority. I've seen these latches survive a drop from a truck tailgate onto concrete without popping open. The corners have metal brackets molded into the plastic, which is where most competitor boxes crack first. The IP65 weather seal runs the full perimeter of the lid, and it actually works — one reviewer noted, "Left it out in a downpour for three hours, tools inside were bone dry."

The 110-pound capacity rating isn't a gimmick. The base plate is thick, and the column connections lock into other ToughSystem 2.0 boxes with a satisfying thunk. Stack three of these high, load them heavy, and the stack stays stable. That said, the handle placement is slightly awkward when you've got a box stacked on top — if you're over 6 feet tall, your knuckles will brush the bottom of the box above. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across 240 ratings holding a 4.8 average, the recurring theme is durability. One contractor wrote, "I've had mine for two years on a framing crew. It's been thrown, rained on, and buried under lumber. Still latches perfectly." Another buyer appreciated the modular compatibility: "Bought the rolling cart base and the small parts organizer — they all click together seamlessly. This system is the real deal."

**Where it falls short**

There's no lid organizer tray built in. The lid is just a flat, deep well — small parts will slide around and mix together unless you buy the optional divider kit (sold separately). That's an extra $15–20 you should budget for. Also, the box is wide — 22 inches — so it won't fit under most truck back seats. And if you're on a tight budget, the original ToughSystem (not 2.0) is cheaper, but the latches are noticeably weaker and the weather seal isn't as good. The upgrade is worth the difference.

**Who should buy this**

- **Working tradespeople** — electricians, plumbers, carpenters who need a box that can take a beating.
- **Serious DIYers** with a growing tool collection who want a modular system they can expand.
- **Anyone who's broken two or more cheap tool boxes in the last year.** This is the last one you'll buy.

**Skip it if** you're a light-duty home user with a handful of tools — a $30 tote will do the job, and you won't use the stackability or the heavy-duty latches.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this box fit on the DEWALT ToughSystem 2.0 rolling cart?**
Yes. It locks onto all ToughSystem 2.0 bases and carts with the same column connection system. It's not backward compatible with the original ToughSystem without an adapter plate.

**Q: Can I lock this box with a padlock?**
Yes, there are two integrated padlock hasps on the front — one on each latch. A standard 1.5-inch shackle padlock fits.

**Q: Will it survive being checked as airline luggage?**
I wouldn't recommend it. The plastic is tough, but airline baggage handling is a special kind of abuse. Stick to a hard-sided case for travel.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need serious, stackable tool storage that will survive years of job-site abuse. **Skip it if** you're a casual home user who just needs a box to hold a drill and a hammer.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a half-star deduction for the missing lid organizer and the slightly awkward handle height. Otherwise, this is the best large tool box in its class.
