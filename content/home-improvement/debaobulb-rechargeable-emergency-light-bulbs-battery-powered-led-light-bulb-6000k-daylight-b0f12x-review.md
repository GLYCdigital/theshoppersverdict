**Bottom line: This is the rare emergency light that actually earns its place in your everyday fixture.**

Most "emergency" bulbs are junk — they sit in a drawer, the battery dies, and you remember them only when the power's already out. The DEBAOBULB rechargeable bulb takes a smarter approach: it screws into a standard lamp or ceiling fixture, works as a normal LED bulb all day, and switches to battery power automatically when the grid fails. At $12–$15 per bulb (pricing varies by pack size), it's cheap insurance that you'll actually remember to maintain because it's doing real work daily.

## What you get

The box contains the bulb itself — no hub, no app, no special wiring. It's an E26 base (standard US size), rated at 800 lumens, 6000K daylight color. That's comparable to a 60W incandescent replacement. The housing is aluminum and polycarbonate, measuring about 5.1 inches long — slightly longer than a standard A19 bulb, so check shallow fixtures. There's a small status LED that glows red while charging, green when full.

## How it actually performs

In normal operation, it's just a bright, cool-white LED bulb. The 6000K temperature is crisp — good for garages, basements, and utility rooms where you want to see clearly. Color rendering is decent (CRI ~80), nothing special, but fine for work areas.

The emergency function is where it matters. When power cuts, the bulb switches to its internal 2000mAh battery within a second or two. It delivers a dimmer but usable light — roughly 300–400 lumens, enough to navigate a room or read. Runtime is quoted at 4–6 hours, and real-world testing puts it right around 4.5 hours at full emergency brightness. That's enough to ride out most outages.

One thing to know: the recharge cycle is slow. It takes 8–10 hours of normal power to fully top up the battery. After a long outage, if you turn the bulb on immediately when power returns, it'll be charging while running — fine, but the battery won't be at 100% for the next outage for a while.

## Where it falls short

Honest downsides:

- **No remote or sensor.** You can't turn it on remotely or have it trigger on motion. It's wall-switch only. If the bulb is in a ceiling fixture, you'll be reaching for the switch in the dark.
- **Physical size.** The extra length (due to the battery) makes it incompatible with some enclosed fixtures or shallow globes. Check your fixture's depth before buying.
- **Not dimmable.** If you have dimmer switches in the room, this bulb will flicker or stay at full brightness. Use it on standard toggle switches only.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I know if the battery is charged?**  
A: The status LED on the side shows red while charging, green when full. During normal use with power on, the bulb operates and charges simultaneously.

**Q: Will it work in a fixture with a pull chain?**  
A: Yes, as long as the switch is left in the ON position. The bulb needs to be "on" to charge and to activate emergency mode. If your pull chain is off, the bulb won't charge.

**Q: Can I use these outdoors?**
