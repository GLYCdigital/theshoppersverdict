# Haidms Black Interior Door Knobs Sets MS 2093 NEW: The Real Deal?

You're replacing builder-grade brass knobs with matte black hardware, and suddenly every set claims "premium quality" at a bargain price. The Haidms MS 2093 is one of the more popular budget options — 200 ratings, 4.0 stars — but is it genuinely good, or just cheap enough to feel okay? I've dug through the specs, the buyer feedback, and the actual build to give you a straight answer.

## What's Actually in the Box

Haidms sends a complete set: two passage knobs (no lock — for closets or hallways), two privacy knobs (twist-lock for bathrooms), and two keyed entry knobs (for exterior-facing interior doors like a home office). Each knob comes with the matching latch, strike plate, and screws. The packaging is utilitarian but protective — no crushed boxes, which matters more than you'd think.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The knobs use a zinc alloy body with a true powder-coated matte black finish. As shown in the product image, the finish is uniform — no drips or uneven texture. In practice, this means fingerprints don't show up like they do on glossy black or oil-rubbed bronze. That's a real win for high-traffic doors.

The turn action is genuinely smooth. Several reviewers noted the handle rotates a full 180 degrees with no catch or grind — that's better than some Kwikset and Schlage models at twice the price. The latch mechanism is magnetic rather than spring-loaded, which makes closing quieter, but it's also the weakest point (more on that below).

Installation is straightforward if you're swapping out standard US residential doors. The backset works with both 2-3/8" and 2-3/4" holes, and the spindle fits standard 1/4" bore holes. Most buyers had them installed in under 30 minutes per door with just a Phillips screwdriver.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The finish and feel dominate positive reviews. One buyer wrote: *"Replaced all 8 interior knobs in my house — the matte black looks way more expensive than what I paid. Zero fingerprints after a week of heavy use."* Another noted: *"The twist-lock on the bathroom actually engages smoothly — my old lock always jammed. This one is buttery."*

Multiple reviewers also highlighted the included keyed entry option — rare at this price point. You get real keys, not a cheap hex key override, which is a genuine convenience.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct: the magnetic latch is a gamble. Several reviews mention the latch losing holding strength after 12-18 months, causing doors to pop open on their own. This isn't a "maybe" issue — it's a design tradeoff. If you have kids who slam doors, expect to replace latches sooner.

The screws are another frustration. They're borderline soft — one reviewer said: *"Stripped the head on the second screw during install. Had to use pliers to get it out."* Pre-drill pilot holes, and you'll likely be fine, but this shouldn't be necessary on a product marketed as DIY-friendly.

Also, the latch is a fixed 60mm length. If you have thicker interior doors (common in older homes or European builds), you'll need to buy a separate longer latch — Haidms doesn't include one.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're updating a standard 2-3/4" backset American home, want matte black on a budget, and don't mind doing a little prep work during installation. It's ideal for landlords or flippers doing a quick refresh across multiple doors — the price per set is hard to beat.

**Skip it if:** You need heavy-duty durability for rental properties with rough tenants, or you have non-standard door thicknesses. Also skip if you hate the idea of replacing latches down the road — spend more on a solid brass latch mechanism instead.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these fit hollow-core interior doors?**
A: Yes, the standard 2-3/8" backset works fine with hollow-core doors. Just don't overtighten the screws — the soft metal can strip in the thin wood.

**Q: Are the keys real or decorative?**
A: Real. Two keys come with each keyed entry knob, and they work smoothly. You can have copies made at any hardware store.

**Q: Will the black finish scratch off over time?**
A: The powder coat is durable against normal wear — keys, rings, and light impacts. But if you're rough with tools near the knob, it will chip like any painted finish.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The Haidms MS 2093 is a solid budget pick with an impressive finish and smooth operation. It's not built for a lifetime — the magnetic latch will likely need replacement, and the screws test your patience — but for the price, you're getting a genuinely good-looking knob that performs well in everyday use.

**Buy it if** you want matte black hardware without paying premium prices, and you're handy enough to pre-drill screw holes.

**Skip it if** you need something bulletproof or have non-standard door dimensions. For everyone else, this is a smart value buy that looks far more expensive than it is.
