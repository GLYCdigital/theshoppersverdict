**The $90 faucet that doesn't embarrass you in front of guests — mostly**  

Let's be blunt: most kitchen faucets under $100 are chrome-plated plastic junk that drips by month three. The KirlystonE 2-handle with sprayer sits in that price bracket but throws a few surprises — some good, some that'll make you tweak the handles a second time. After 215 ratings averaging 4.3 stars, here's the unfiltered breakdown for anyone staring at their sink and hating what they see.

---

**What's in the box**  
You get the faucet body, two lever handles, a pull-down sprayer head, 23.6-inch nylon hose, mounting hardware, and a stainless deck plate (covers 3-hole setups). The box also includes Teflon tape and an Allen wrench — small touches that save a trip to the hardware store. Notably, the sprayer head is **metal**, not the usual ABS plastic, and the body is solid brass under the finish. For the price, that's rare.

---

**Real-world performance**  
The dual-function sprayer (stream or spray) has a positive detent — you feel the click. Water pressure stays consistent even with the pause button engaged, and the 1.8 GPM flow rate meets federal standards without feeling like a trickle. The 360-degree swivel base is smooth, and the spout reach (8.3 inches) clears a standard 10-inch sink basin.

But here's the catch: the **handles**. They use a quarter-turn ceramic cartridge, but the action isn't buttery. Multiple buyers describe it as "gritty" or "notchy" — one reviewer wrote, *"The handles have a slight catch right before full hot. Not a dealbreaker, but you notice it every time."* It's a tolerance issue, not a functional failure. After a few weeks, it loosens slightly, but it never gets Delta-smooth.

**Installation reality check**  
If you're replacing an existing 3-hole faucet, this is a 30-minute job with a basin wrench. The supply lines are pre-attached 3/8-inch compression fittings. The headache is the **sprayer hose** — it arrives coiled tight and fights you during installation. One buyer noted: *"Getting the hose through the sink hole took 20 minutes of wrestling. Once it settled, it's been fine."* Plan for that.

---

**What buyers consistently praise**  
- **Finish durability**: The brushed nickel doesn't show fingerprints or water spots as badly as chrome. Multiple owners confirm no peeling after 6+ months.  
- **Sprayer seal**: A common failure point on cheap faucets — the diverter valve leaks into the stream mode. Here, it holds. One reviewer: *"Six months in, the sprayer still switches cleanly. My last $60 faucet failed at 4 months."*  
- **Weight**: At 7.2 pounds, it feels substantial. No wobble when you yank the sprayer.

---

**Where it falls short**  
Besides the handle feel, the **sprayer hose stiffness** is the top complaint. It relaxes after heavy use, but if you do a lot of pot-filling, the first week is annoying. Also, the **sprayer head's rubber nozzles** are easy to clean but slightly recessed — debris can hide if you have hard water. And the **installation instructions** are minimal: a single sheet with small diagrams. If you're not handy, watch a YouTube video first.

---

**Who should buy this**  
- **First-time homeowners** on a budget who want a decent-looking faucet without the $250 Moen markup.  
- **DIY renters** who need a functional unit for a rental property — it's cheap enough to replace without tears.  
- **Anyone with a 3-hole sink** who wants the deck plate option (it also covers 1-hole setups with the included plate).

**Skip it if** you demand silky-smooth handle action, or if your sink has a non-standard 4-hole configuration — this won't fit without an extra plate.

---

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it fit a 1-hole sink?**  
A: Yes, the included deck plate covers 1-hole and 3-hole setups. For 1-hole, skip the plate and use the base ring.

**Q: Are the handles reversible for hot/cold sides?**  
A: No. The cartridge is fixed — left is hot, right is cold. You can't swap them without modifying the valve.

**Q: What's the warranty?**  
A: KirlystonE offers a 5-year limited warranty on the cartridge and finish, but you'll pay return shipping. Amazon's return window is your better bet.

---

**The Verdict: 4.2/5 — Buy it if you want function over polish**  

**Buy it if** you're replacing a leaking faucet and want brass internals + a reliable sprayer under $100.  
**Skip it if** handle smoothness is your priority, or you're installing in a high-end kitchen where $50 more buys a Delta.

For the price, this faucet earns its keep. Just budget 45 minutes for installation and don't expect luxury-grade touchpoints. It's a workhorse, not a show pony.
