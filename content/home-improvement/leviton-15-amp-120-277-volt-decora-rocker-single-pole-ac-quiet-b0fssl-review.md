The Leviton Decora rocker switch, though, is a different animal — it's the switch electricians spec when they want something that won't come back to bite them. ## What You Actually Get

This is a single-pole rocker switch — one location controls one light or fixture. No three-way capability, no dimmer, no smart features. It's rated 15 amps at 120V for standard residential use, but here's the kicker: it's also rated for 277V, which is common in commercial lighting. That dual rating makes it a legit choice for workshops, garages, or outbuildings wired for higher voltage.

The switch ships with the Decora-style rectangular paddle — the modern flat look that's become the de facto standard since Leviton introduced it decades ago. You get the switch body, a rocker paddle, and a metal mounting strap with a grounded screw. Ground wire included? No, you supply that. The unit accepts both side-wiring (screw terminals) and back-wiring (insertion holes) for up to #12 AWG.

## Build Quality and Real-World Feel

Here's where this switch earns its keep. The rocker action is noticeably smoother than the cheap contractor-grade switches you'll find in bulk packs. There's no sharp *CLACK*; instead you get a soft, positive detent that feels substantial. Internally, Leviton uses a "quiet" mechanism that dampens the snap — one reviewer put it simply: *"Replaced all the switches in my hallway. The difference in sound is dramatic. No more waking up the house at 2 AM."*

The body is constructed from a durable thermoplastic that resists cracking and discoloration. The terminals are brass, and the mounting strap is galvanized steel. It's a solid, dense unit — you can feel the difference in weight compared to a builder-grade switch. The paddle sits flush with the wall plate (not included), and the rocker has a subtle tactile ridge so you can find it in the dark.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. This is a bare-bones switch — no illuminated paddle, no motion sensing, no Wi-Fi. If you're looking for a smart switch or something you can find in the dark, this isn't it. You're paying for quality and reliability, not features.

The side screw terminals are also a bit snug with 12 AWG solid wire. It's workable, but if you're a DIYer with thick fingers, you might find the back-wiring slots easier. One reviewer warned: *"The terminals are decent, but don't overtighten the side screws — the plate can strip if you're aggressive with a power driver."*

One more thing: this is single-pole only. If you're replacing a three-way switch (two switches controlling one light), you need the three-way version of this same switch. Double-check your configuration before ordering.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this switch compatible with LED bulbs?**
A: Yes, but with a caveat. This is a standard switch, not a dimmer, so it will work fine with LEDs at full brightness. If you want dimming, you need a compatible LED dimmer, not this switch.

**Q: Does it fit standard Decora wall plates?**
A: Yes. It's the standard Decora form factor, so any Decora-style wall plate will fit — Leviton, Lutron, or generic. Pick up a screwless plate if you want the cleanest look.

**Q: Can I use this for a 3-way setup?**
A: No. This is single-pole only — one switch controls one light. For two switches controlling one light, you need the Leviton 15A Decora 3-way switch, which is a different SKU.
