## What’s in the box

You get the sconce pre-assembled, a mounting plate, wire nuts, and the screws you’ll need for standard US junction boxes. No bulb is included — it takes a standard E26 medium base, so grab two LED bulbs (the manual recommends 60W equivalent max). The unit is 9.5 inches tall and 5.5 inches wide, with a 6-inch projection from the wall. That depth matters: it clears most door casings and doesn’t poke into walkways.

## Build quality and real-world performance

The selling point here is the aluminum construction with an IP65 rating. That means it’s fully sealed against dust and low-pressure water jets — not just “splash resistant” like many cheap zinc fixtures. One buyer wrote: *“I live 200 yards from the ocean. After two years, no pitting, no green crust, still looks like the day I installed it.”* That tracks with the powder-coated finish, which resists UV fading better than painted plastic.

Heat is handled well too. The open-top design lets warm air rise out, so LED bulbs run cooler and last longer. The glass is real tempered glass, not thin acrylic that yellows. I’d trust this through a Midwest winter or a Gulf Coast hurricane season.

## What buyers consistently praise

- **Installation ease:** The mounting plate slots into the housing with a keyhole design — you can hang the fixture while wiring instead of holding it overhead. One reviewer noted: *“Took 15 minutes as a solo install, no extra hands needed.”*  
- **Light quality:** The up/down distribution creates layered light without glare. Several people mentioned it makes their entryway feel taller.  
- **Build weight:** At 3.8 lbs, it feels solid. No hollow rattle when you tap the housing.

## Where it falls short

No fixture is perfect, and here are the honest tradeoffs:

- **No integrated sensor.** If you want lights that auto-on at dusk, you’ll need to pair this with a smart switch or buy the brand’s motion-sensor version.  
- **Hardwired only.** Renters or anyone avoiding electrical work will need an electrician — there’s no battery or plug-in variant.  
- **Bulb choice matters.** Clear or frosted bulbs change the look dramatically. A clear vintage filament bulb looks great; a cheap frosted utility bulb looks institutional. Budget for decent LEDs.

## Who should buy this

- **Homeowners with modern or transitional exteriors** — this pairs well with black windows, metal roofs, or board-and-batten siding.

**Q: Is it safe for covered outdoor areas?**
