**The DeWalt 20V MAX Brushless 15-Degree Coil Roofing Nailer (model B088Y5) is the cordless tool that makes you question why you ever dragged an air compressor up a roof.** After 220 Amazon ratings averaging 4.4 stars, the consensus is clear: this is a serious workhorse, but it isn't for everyone. Here's the unvarnished truth.

## What's in the Box

You get the nailer itself, a belt hook, a rafter hook, and the manual. **No battery or charger included**—this is a bare tool, so you're already invested in the DeWalt 20V MAX platform. If you're starting from scratch, factor in the cost of at least one 5Ah battery and a charger. The tool accepts DeWalt's full range of 20V batteries, but I'd advise against anything smaller than a 4Ah for real work.

## Performance and Build Quality

The brushless motor is the star here. It drives 2-3/8" collated coil nails through OSB and into pressure-treated framing without the weak-sauce stall that plagued earlier cordless nailers. **The dry-fire lockout is a genuinely useful feature**—it stops the driver blade from slamming into your work when the magazine empties, which saves you from ugly divots and wasted nails.

The tool-free jam release lives up to its name. One roofer in the Amazon reviews put it simply: *"I jammed it twice on my first roof and cleared both in under a minute without tools. That sold me."* The depth adjustment is equally accessible—dial it in for shingle thickness or sheathing without breaking out an Allen wrench.

One thing to note: this is a 15-degree coil nailer, not a 30-degree or 34-degree stick nailer. **You're locked into coil collation**, which is fine for roofing and fencing, but it's a different nail format than what many framers carry. Check your existing nail inventory before committing.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring themes in positive reviews are battery life and power. Multiple buyers report driving 1,500+ nails on a single 5Ah battery, which is impressive for a cordless tool. One reviewer summed it up: *"I did a 30-square roof on one charge and had plenty left. My back thanks me for not dragging hoses around."*

The brushless motor's consistency at low battery is also a standout. Unlike some cordless nailers that slow down as the charge drops, this one drives nails at full depth until the battery is genuinely dead.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the weight. At 8.3 pounds with a 5Ah battery, this is a heavy tool. **On a full day of overhead work or steep-pitch roofing, your forearms will feel it.** Multiple reviewers noted this, with one saying, *"Great nailer, but I wish I'd bought the lighter 18-gauge version for trim work. This is a brute."*

The rafter hook is my second complaint. It's positioned in a way that catches on harness straps and ladder rungs. You'll likely end up removing it or just carrying the tool by its handle.

The magazine capacity is 120 nails, which is decent but not class-leading. Some competitive models hold 150+. It's a minor quibble, but when you're on a ladder, every reload matters.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if you're a professional roofer, siding installer, or fencing contractor** who needs cordless convenience without sacrificing power. It's also a solid choice for serious DIYers tackling a full roof replacement—just know you're committing to the DeWalt battery platform.

**Skip it if you're a trim carpenter or finish worker.** This nailer is too heavy and too specialized for interior work. You'd be better served by a lighter 18-gauge cordless nailer.

**Skip it if you're already invested in a different battery platform** (Milwaukee, Makita, etc.). Buying into DeWalt just for this tool is a steep entry cost.

## FAQ

**Does it work with all DeWalt 20V batteries?**
Yes, but I'd recommend at least a 4Ah battery. Smaller batteries like the 2Ah will work but drain fast and add to the weight imbalance.

**What nail sizes does it accept?**
It drives 15-degree wire-collated nails from 3/4" to 2-3/8". That covers most roofing and fencing applications.

**Is it weather-resistant for job site use?**
It handles light rain and dust reasonably well, but it's not waterproof. Keep it covered during downpours and store it dry.

## The Verdict: 4.2/5

**Buy it if** you need cordless coil-nailer power for roofing, decking, or fencing and you're already in the DeWalt ecosystem. The brushless motor, dry-fire lockout, and tool-free jam release make this a genuinely productive tool.

**Skip it if** you're a weekend warrior doing occasional trim work, or if you're not committed to the DeWalt battery platform. The weight and coil-nail format limit its versatility.

For the working professional who wants to ditch the compressor, this is a solid 4-star tool with a few ergonomic compromises. The power is there, the battery life is there, and the reliability is there. Just budget for a good harness if you're working overhead.
