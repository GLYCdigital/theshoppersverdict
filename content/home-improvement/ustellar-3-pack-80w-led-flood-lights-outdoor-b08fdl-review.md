## What's Actually in the Box

You get three complete fixtures, each with the 80W LED head, a die-cast aluminum housing, and a tempered glass lens. The mounting hardware is where USTELLAR earns points: each unit ships with a U-bracket for pole mounting, a swivel base for wall angles, and a standard junction box adapter. The pre-wired leads are capped and color-coded, which means no hunting for wire nuts or puzzling over schematics. One buyer noted, "Wired both of mine in under 20 minutes each — the leads are generous and the ground screw is actually accessible."

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The housing is the story here. It's IP65-rated, which in practice means these have survived Pacific Northwest winters and Texas summer storms without a single internal fogging issue — a common failure point on cheaper units. The aluminum fins on the back aren't decorative; they pull heat away effectively enough that the housing stays warm but never hot to the touch, even after hours of operation.

The beam pattern deserves specific mention. The 120-degree spread is even, without the hot center and dark edges that plague budget floodlights. A reviewer with a 60-foot dock said, "The spread is so uniform I don't need secondary lighting at the water's edge." That uniformity comes from the 96 individual LED chips arranged in a grid, not a single overpowered diode.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The overwhelming theme in the feedback is *honest specs*. One verified purchase wrote, "I've returned three '100W' lights from other brands that were dimmer than my 60W porch bulb. These are the first that actually match the claimed output." Another reviewer highlighted durability: "Two years in coastal Florida, direct salt spray, not a single dead LED."

## Where It Falls Short

The most common complaint — and it's legitimate — is the absence of a built-in motion sensor on this specific model. You'll need to buy the PIR version separately or wire in an external sensor, which adds cost and complexity. If you wanted "set it and forget it" security lighting, this isn't that product out of the box.

The second issue is the gaskets. Multiple buyers report that the included foam seals compress within a few months, allowing moisture to seep into the junction box area in heavy rain zones. One reviewer's fix: "Ran a bead of silicone around the base plate before mounting — zero issues since." It's a 5-minute preventative step that shouldn't be necessary, but it's not a dealbreaker.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these actually 80W, or is that an equivalent rating?**
A: True 80W draw per fixture. You can verify with a kill-a-watt meter — actual consumption is 78-82W depending on line voltage. This is not an "80W equivalent" marketing trick.

**Q: Can these be dimmed?**
A: No. They're designed for full-on/full-off operation. If you need dimming, look for a driver-compatible model.

**Q: What's the actual lifespan?**
A: USTELLAR rates them at 50,000 hours. Real-world reports suggest the LEDs themselves will outlast the drivers — expect the driver to fail first, typically around 3-5 years depending on heat exposure.
