If you're building a sliding gate and you've priced out individual rollers, track, and brackets, you already know the sting. The Pylex 11052 11070 Sliding Gate Kit bundles the critical hardware into one box, and at 4.4 stars from 220 buyers, it's clearly doing something right. The question is whether it's the right kit for *your* gate.

## What's Actually in the Box

This is a complete rolling hardware set for cantilever-style sliding gates. You get two 5-inch wheel assemblies with sealed ball bearings, a steel mounting track, heavy-duty brackets, and the ground-mounted guide roller. The black powder coat finish isn't just cosmetic — it's a genuine rust barrier that holds up better than the raw zinc you see on cheaper kits.

The wheels are the headline component. They're rated for gates up to 1,100 pounds, which covers everything from a lightweight aluminum driveway gate to a beefy 16-foot welded steel panel. The sealed bearings are a meaningful upgrade over unsealed options — they keep dust and moisture out of the critical moving parts.

## Performance and Real-World Installation

Let's be direct about installation: this kit demands precision. The track has multiple mounting holes, but they're not slotted for adjustment. You need your concrete anchors dead-on, or the wheels will bind. One reviewer noted, "Had to drill new holes in the track because my post spacing was off by half an inch — plan your layout carefully."

Once it's mounted correctly, though, the operation is smooth. The 5-inch wheels roll effortlessly, and the ground guide keeps the gate tracking straight without wobble. Buyers consistently praise the smooth glide — one wrote, "My 12-foot gate rolls like it's on a rail, even after a year of daily use."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Beyond the smooth rolling, the recurring positives in buyer feedback are:
- **Complete kit** — no separate trips for brackets or rollers
- **Load capacity** — handles heavy gates without flex or sag
- **Finish quality** — the black powder coat holds up well outdoors

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest complaint is the fasteners. The included bolts and nuts are grade 5 mild steel — fine for dry climates, but marginal for coastal or snowy regions where road salt is a factor. Several reviewers mentioned replacing them with stainless hardware within the first year. At this price point, it's a minor cost, but it's worth budgeting for.

The track is also not pre-drilled at exact industry-standard spacing, so if you're retrofitting an existing gate with different hole patterns, expect to drill new holes. This is a kit best suited for new construction or a gate you're building from scratch.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy this if:** You're building a new sliding gate — wood, aluminum, or steel — and want a proven, complete hardware package. It's also a solid choice for gates in the 8-to-14-foot range where you need reliable daily operation without premium pricing.

**Skip it if:** You're in a high-corrosion coastal environment (plan on aftermarket stainless fasteners), or if you're retrofitting an existing gate with non-standard mounting holes. Also, if your gate exceeds 1,100 pounds, step up to a heavier-duty industrial kit.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this kit work for a wooden gate?**
A: Yes, and it's a popular choice for wood gates. The brackets mount to the top rail, and the wheels support the weight. Just make sure your top rail is square and level — any twist will cause binding.

**Q: What's the maximum gate length this can handle?**
A: The 1,100-pound rating is the real constraint. A 12-foot cedar gate will be fine; a 16-foot steel gate may exceed the limit. Weigh your gate material and size before you buy.

**Q: Is this kit compatible with automatic gate openers?**
A: Generally yes, as long as the opener provides its own drive mechanism (chain or gear). The low-friction wheels actually help openers work more efficiently.
