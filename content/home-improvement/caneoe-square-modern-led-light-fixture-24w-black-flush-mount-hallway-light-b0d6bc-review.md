The CANEOE Square LED flush mount is the kind of fixture that makes you wonder why you ever bothered with bulky ceiling lights. At 24 watts pushing roughly 2400 lumens, this thing throws serious light — enough to illuminate a 10x10 room without breaking a sweat. And the matte black square design? It's modern without trying too hard, which is exactly what most homeowners want.

**What's in the box**

You get the fixture itself, a sliding mounting bracket, wire nuts, and mounting screws. Nothing fancy, but everything you need. The housing is aluminum with a polycarbonate diffuser — lightweight but solid. As shown in the product image, the black finish is uniform and doesn't have that cheap, glossy look you see on budget fixtures. The whole unit measures about 11.8 inches square and sits just 1.2 inches off the ceiling.

**Installation: genuinely easy**

The sliding bracket is the standout feature here. You screw the bracket to the junction box, then slide the fixture onto it and secure with two screws. No holding a heavy fixture overhead while you fiddle with wires — that's the worst part of any ceiling light install, and CANEOE eliminated it. One reviewer noted, *"Took me 15 minutes total, and I'm not handy at all. The bracket slides right in."* Wiring is standard black-to-black, white-to-white, ground-to-ground. If you've changed a light fixture before, you'll be fine.

**Performance and light quality**

The 4000K color temperature is a neutral white — not the sterile blue of 5000K, not the yellow warmth of 3000K. It's a good middle ground for hallways, closets, laundry rooms, and kitchens. The diffuser spreads light evenly with no hot spots or flicker. A buyer mentioned, *"I put this in my hallway and it's brighter than the two sconces I replaced. No buzz, no flicker, even on a dimmer switch"* — though note that last part is likely a mistake, because this fixture is **not** dimmer-compatible. Running it on a dimmer may cause flickering or damage.

**Where it falls short**

The integrated LED board is the elephant in the room. When these diodes burn out — rated at around 25,000 hours, roughly 6-8 years of typical use — you can't just swap a bulb. The whole fixture goes in the trash. At this price point, that's an acceptable tradeoff for most people, but it's worth knowing going in.

Also, there's no color temperature adjustment. You're locked into 4000K. If you prefer warm 2700K light in bedrooms, this isn't the fixture for you.

**Who should buy this**

- **Hallway and corridor lighting**: The slim profile and even light distribution make it ideal for narrow spaces.
- **Closets and laundry rooms**: Bright, functional, and doesn't collect dust like glass globes.
- **Modern home renovations**: The black square design pairs well with matte black hardware and contemporary trim.
- **DIY installers**: The sliding bracket makes this one of the easier ceiling lights to mount solo.

Skip it if you need dimmable lighting, want warm color tones, or prefer fixtures with replaceable bulbs for long-term sustainability.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Q: Does this work with a dimmer switch?**
No. It's strictly on/off. Using a dimmer can cause flickering and may damage the LED driver.

**Q: How bright is 24W really?**
Roughly 2400 lumens, which is comparable to a 150-watt incandescent. Plenty for a 10x10 room or a long hallway.

**Q: Is the light cool or warm white?**
It's 4000K — a neutral white. Not yellow, not blue. Good for task areas but not cozy enough for some living spaces.

**The Verdict**

The CANEOE Square LED flush mount delivers exactly what it promises: bright, even light in a clean, modern package that installs in minutes. The non-replaceable LED and fixed color temperature are real limitations, but at this price, the value is hard to beat. It's earned that 4.6-star rating.

**Buy it if** you need a reliable, bright flush mount for a hallway, closet, or utility space and want something that looks more expensive than it is.

**Skip it if** you require dimming capability or prefer warm, adjustable lighting for living areas.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a solid, honest performer that nails the basics.
