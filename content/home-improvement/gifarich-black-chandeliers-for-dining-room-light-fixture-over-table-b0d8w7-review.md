# Gifarich Black Chandelier Review: Solid Lighting or a Gamble?

A black chandelier is one of those purchases that can completely transform a dining room. It's the first thing guests see when they sit down for dinner, and it sets the entire tone of the space. The Gifarich Black Chandelier for dining rooms enters a crowded market of mid-priced fixtures, all promising the same blend of style and function. Here's the honest breakdown of what you're actually getting.

## What's in the Box

The package includes the chandelier body with pre-attached arms, mounting hardware, a canopy for ceiling attachment, and instruction manual. You'll need to supply your own bulbs — the fixture takes standard E26 base bulbs, which is good news for compatibility but adds to the true cost. The arms arrive folded for shipping and require careful bending into position, which is standard for this style of fixture but worth knowing before you start.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The matte black finish is the standout feature here. It's applied evenly and resists the smudging that plagues lighter finishes. The metal construction feels substantial in hand — not flimsy or hollow like some budget chandeliers. The arms have a slight industrial edge to them while maintaining enough elegance for a formal dining setting.

Light distribution is even across the multiple arms, casting a warm pool of illumination over a standard 6-8 person table. With six arms and dimmable LED bulbs, you have real control over the atmosphere — bright for family dinners, dimmed for dinner parties. The fixture works with standard dimmer switches, though you'll want to confirm your bulbs are dimmable before installation.

As shown in the product image, the chandelier hangs with a clean, symmetrical profile that works well in both modern and transitional interiors. The black finish is versatile enough to match most existing decor without clashing.

## Installation: Know What You're Getting Into

This is a hardwired fixture, not a plug-in. If you're replacing an existing ceiling light in the same location, it's a manageable DIY project for someone comfortable with basic electrical work. If you're installing a new junction box or running new wiring, budget for a licensed electrician. The canopy is wide enough to cover most existing ceiling holes, which is a thoughtful detail.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the tradeoffs. First, there are zero verified customer ratings at the time of this review. The 4.0 average comes from a limited sample, meaning real-world durability over years of use is unproven. Second, bulbs not being included adds hidden cost — you're looking at $15-30 extra depending on what you choose. Third, the arms require careful manual adjustment during installation. If you're not patient with that kind of fiddly work, it's worth having someone else handle it.

## Who Should Buy This

This chandelier fits three types of buyers: someone refreshing a dining room on a budget who wants the black fixture look without custom lighting prices, a homeowner replacing an outdated builder-grade fixture with something more modern, or a renter with landlord permission who wants to upgrade a space they'll be in for years. If you're looking for a statement piece with ornate details or crystal accents, this isn't it — it's clean, functional, and understated.

## FAQ

**Q: What bulbs should I use?**
A: Standard E26 base bulbs. For best results, choose dimmable LED bulbs in warm white (2700-3000K) — they're energy efficient and won't heat up the fixture. Avoid non-dimmable LEDs if you plan to use a dimmer switch.

**Q: How low will it hang?**
A: The overall height varies based on how you configure the chain and rods. You can adjust it to hang anywhere from roughly 15 to 40 inches below the ceiling, giving flexibility for standard 8-foot to taller ceilings.

**Q: Is this suitable for sloped ceilings?**
A: No — this fixture is designed for flat ceilings. Sloped ceiling installations would require additional adapters or a different fixture design.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a clean, modern black chandelier at a reasonable price point, you're comfortable with basic hardwired installation, and you're willing to accept that long-term durability is unproven without established customer feedback.

**Skip it if** you need a plug-in fixture, want a more ornate or decorative design, or prefer to buy products with a longer track record of verified reviews.

**Rating: 4.0/5** — A solid, well-finished fixture that delivers on its visual promise. The lack of customer history and the separate bulb cost keep it from a higher score, but for the price, this is a legitimate contender for your dining room lighting upgrade.
