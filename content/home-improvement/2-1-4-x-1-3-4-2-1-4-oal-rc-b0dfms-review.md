## What You're Actually Getting

The package contains one strike plate measuring 2-1/4" x 1-3/4" with a 2-1/4" overall length. The extended lip is the star here — it protrudes further than a standard strike, giving your latch bolt more surface area to catch. That's the difference between a door that closes properly and one that bounces back open every time the wind shifts.

The plate is stamped zinc with a plated finish. It's not decorative-grade hardware, but it's not meant to be. This is a functional replacement part, and it looks like one — clean, square, and unobtrusive on the jamb.

## Where This Strike Earns Its Keep

The extended lip solves a specific problem: doors that have settled, hinges that have loosened, or latch bolts that no longer align with the original strike hole. Instead of shimming the hinges or re-hanging the door, you swap in this plate and the extra lip surface catches the bolt that would otherwise miss.

Installation is straightforward if you've done basic door hardware before. The screw holes match standard spacing on most residential jambs. One thing to note: the plate is slightly thicker than some OEM strikes, so on older frames with tight mortises, you might need to chisel out an extra millimeter or two. It's a minor adjustment, but worth knowing before you start.
