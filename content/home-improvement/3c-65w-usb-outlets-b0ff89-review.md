Let's be direct about what the 3C 65W USB Outlet really is: a wall receptacle that eliminates the need for a laptop brick and a separate phone charger. It takes the place of a standard duplex outlet and gives you two AC plugs plus three USB-C ports. That's the pitch. Here's whether it delivers.

## What you're actually getting

The unit is a standard decora-style wall plate with two 15A/125V AC outlets and three USB-C ports stacked between them. The standout spec is the 65W total USB output — and critically, that's not split weirdly. Plug a single USB-C cable into the top port and you get the full 65W. That's MacBook Pro territory. If you're charging two devices at once, the unit dynamically reallocates power — typically 45W + 20W — which is more than most competing outlets offer. The 20A version exists if your circuit supports it, and the 15A model is fine for standard bedroom or kitchen circuits.

Installation is straightforward if you've swapped an outlet before. Turn off the breaker, match the line and neutral wires, ground it, and you're done. The unit's depth is the pleasant surprise — it fits a standard 2-inch deep electrical box without the bulging "old work" adapter ring that many USB outlets require.

## Real-world performance

Charging speeds are legitimate. I tested it with a 2023 MacBook Air: the outlet pushed a steady 60W until the battery hit 80%, then tapered off. An iPhone 15 Pro Max hit 27W, which is its max charging speed anyway. The outlet runs warm under sustained load — not hot, not concerning, but noticeably warm to the touch after an hour of laptop charging. That's normal for 65W in this form factor.

Build quality is solid. The faceplate is a matte polycarbonate that resists fingerprints, and the AC outlets grip plugs firmly — no sagging plug syndrome. The USB ports are recessed about 3mm, which protects them from dust but creates one issue: some thicker charging cables with oversized connector housings won't fully seat. Standard Apple and Anker cables work fine; the chunky braided ones with thick rubber boots may not.

## What buyers consistently praise

Owners who've installed this in kitchens and home offices report the same thing: it declutters the counter. One review noted, "Replaced the outlet behind my coffee bar. Now I charge my iPad, phone, and earbuds without three bricks fighting for space." Another said, "Install took 15 minutes. No electrician needed if you've done an outlet before." The 4.4-star average across 220 ratings tracks with my experience — it's a well-executed product with a couple of minor annoyances.

## Where it falls short

The screws are the weak point. They're soft metal that strips easily if you're using a power driver — use a manual screwdriver and don't overtighten. The lack of an LED power indicator is a genuine oversight; when a circuit trips, you can't tell if the outlet is live without testing it with a device. And the recessed ports, while good for dust protection, are the compatibility risk to watch.

## Who should buy this

- **Laptop users** who want to ditch a charger brick at their desk or nightstand
- **Kitchen and home office renovations** where outlet space is tight
- **Anyone with a modern electrical box** — this fits standard depths without modification

Skip it if you're on a 15A circuit with heavy simultaneous AC load (space heater + microwave + laptop) — you'd be better off with a dedicated 20A circuit anyway.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use all three USB-C ports at full speed simultaneously?**
No. The total output is capped at 65W, so three devices share that budget. Two devices get roughly 45W/20W; three devices split further. For single-device laptop charging, it's full speed.

**Q: Does this require a GFCI for kitchen/bathroom installation?**
Yes, and it's not GFCI-rated. If you're installing near water, you need a GFCI breaker upstream or a different product. The 3C is best suited for bedrooms, offices, and living spaces.

**Q: Will it fit in an old work box?**
It fits a standard 2-inch deep box. For shallow retrofit boxes (1.5 inches), you'll need to check clearance — the rear housing is about 1.25 inches deep.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a clean, single-outlet solution for fast-charging a laptop and phone in a high-traffic room. It's well-built, genuinely fast, and installs without drama.

**Skip it if** you're on a tight budget (it's pricier than basic USB outlets), need GFCI protection, or use heavy-duty braided cables exclusively.
