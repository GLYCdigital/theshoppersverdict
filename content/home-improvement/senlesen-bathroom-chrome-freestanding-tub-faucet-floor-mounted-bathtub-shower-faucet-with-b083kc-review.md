If you're remodeling a master bath and want that freestanding tub moment without paying $800 for a name brand, the Senlesen Chrome Floor Mount Faucet is the reasonable middle ground. At roughly half the price of Kohler or Delta equivalents, it delivers the look — but you need to understand exactly what you're getting before you commit to cutting holes in your floor.

## What's Actually in the Box

The package includes the floor-mounted faucet body, a handheld showerhead with a 59-inch stainless steel hose, a wall-mount bracket for the hand shower, and the mounting hardware. The deck plate is a generous 8 inches in diameter, which covers most rough-in holes. Everything arrives fully assembled except the hand shower connection — that's a 2-minute job.

One thing buyers consistently note: the chrome finish is genuinely good. It's triple-plated, and multiple reviewers mention it looks far more expensive than it is. One buyer put it simply: "The finish matches my $3,000 tub perfectly."

## Real-World Performance

The faucet puts out solid flow — the spout extends 7.5 inches, which properly centers over most freestanding tubs. The single-handle lever operates smoothly, with a 360-degree rotation range that makes temperature control easy from either side of the tub.

Here's where it gets interesting: the handheld showerhead is functional but not fancy. It has one spray mode, and the flow is controlled by a valve at the spout base — not a diverter on the hand shower itself. That means you can't switch between spout and hand shower without walking around the tub. It works, but it's a design compromise you should know about.

## What Buyers Are Saying

The 4.3-star rating across 215 reviews reflects genuine satisfaction with the value proposition. Recurring praise centers on the build quality. As one verified buyer noted: "This is a heavy, solid piece. I was expecting hollow metal — this is real brass." Another reviewer highlighted the installation: "If your plumbing is already stubbed out at the floor, this took me 25 minutes solo."

## Where It Falls Short

The supply lines are the biggest headache. They're only 12 inches long, and unless your rough-in plumbing is nearly perfectly positioned, you'll need to buy extensions. That's an extra trip to the hardware store and potentially a trip under the house.

Also, be honest about the hand shower valve situation. At this price, you'd expect a proper diverter — you don't get one. If you plan to use the hand shower regularly for rinsing the tub or washing pets, this will annoy you.

## Who Should Buy This

- **DIY remodelers** with existing floor rough-in plumbing who want the freestanding look without the premium price tag
- **Bathroom flippers** who need a visually impressive fixture that photographs well
- **Anyone replacing an old floor-mount tub filler** — the 8-inch base plate covers most existing holes

Skip it if you need a true thermostatic valve, want a proper diverter system, or don't have floor access for installation.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this faucet work with a freestanding tub that has no pre-drilled holes?**
Yes — that's the point. The faucet mounts to the floor, not the tub. The tub sits separately, which is why this style works with any freestanding tub.

**Q: Can I install this myself?**
If you have basic plumbing skills and your water lines are already stubbed at the floor, yes. If you're running new lines, hire a plumber — you'll need to access the subfloor.

**Q: Is the chrome finish durable?**
Multiple long-term reviewers report no peeling or spotting after 6+ months of daily use. The brass body is the reason — cheaper steel faucets corrode under chrome.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want the freestanding tub aesthetic at a reasonable price, have floor-mounted rough-in plumbing, and can live with the basic hand shower setup.

**Skip it if** you need a proper diverter system, want a thermostatic valve, or your plumbing situation requires more than simple extensions.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid value that nails the look and build quality, with functional compromises that are acceptable at this price point.
