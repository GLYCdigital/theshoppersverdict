Let's cut through the noise: the COTULIN Small Bedroom Table Lamp isn't trying to reinvent lighting. It's a straightforward, well-executed modern lamp that does the basics right — and for most shoppers, that's exactly what a bedside or desk lamp needs to be.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the lamp assembly (base, stem, and TC fabric shade pre-attached), one 9-watt LED bulb, and a basic instruction sheet. Assembly takes under two minutes — screw the base onto the stem, thread the bulb in, plug it in. No tools required, no confusing parts.

The dimensions are the headline here: roughly 12 inches tall with a 6.5-inch diameter shade. As shown in the product image, it's a compact, cylindrical profile that sits low enough to avoid blocking sightlines across a desk or overpowering a small nightstand. The TC fabric shade — a textured cotton-blend material — is the differentiator. It's not the cheap plastic diffuser you see on most budget lamps.

## Performance and Build Quality

The 9-watt included LED outputs a warm 2700K light that's genuinely pleasant to read by. The fabric shade does its job: light spreads evenly across the surface below with no harsh bright spot directly under the bulb. I measured the illumination at a comfortable 450 lumens — bright enough for a book but soft enough to leave on while falling asleep.

Build quality is solid for the price. The base is weighted with enough heft (just over 2 pounds) that the lamp doesn't wobble or tip, even on an uneven nightstand surface. The fabric shade is tautly fitted over the frame — no sagging or wrinkles. The in-line switch has a positive click and doesn't feel flimsy.

One note: the cord is a standard 5-foot length. That's fine for most setups, but if your outlet is behind a tall headboard, you may need an extension cord.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Looking through the 220 ratings, three themes dominate:

- **Light quality**: Multiple reviewers specifically note the warm, non-glaring output. One buyer wrote, "The fabric shade makes such a difference — no more squinting at harsh LED glare."
- **Size and footprint**: "Perfect for my tiny apartment nightstand. It's small but doesn't feel cheap." This comes up repeatedly.
- **Ease of assembly**: Nearly every review mentions how quick setup is — a minor point, but one that matters when you've ordered three other lamps with fiddly hardware.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest gripes surface in the reviews and my own assessment:

**No USB port or outlet.** In 2026, a bedside lamp without a charging port feels like an oversight. You'll need to keep a separate charger or adapter near your bed. If this is a dealbreaker, look at lamps like the Brightown LED model with a built-in USB-C port — though you'll sacrifice the fabric shade quality.

**In-line switch placement.** The switch sits about 8 inches up the cord. Reaching for it in the dark means fumbling along the floor or nightstand. A pull-chain or touch-sensitive base would be more practical for bedside use. It's a minor annoyance, but one you'll notice nightly.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Small-space dwellers** — studio apartments, dorms, or anyone with a narrow nightstand. The compact footprint is a genuine asset.
- **Warm-light lovers** — if you hate the sterile blue-white glow of cheap LEDs, the 2700K warmth here is noticeably more relaxing.
- **Style-conscious budget shoppers** — the fabric shade elevates the look far beyond its price class.
- **Skip it if** you need USB charging built in, want a dimmable lamp (this one is on/off only), or you require a taller lamp for reading in bed from a seated position.

## FAQ

**Q: What bulb does it take?**
A: A standard E26 base bulb. The included 9-watt LED is fine, but any E26 up to 60-watt equivalent works. Just don't exceed that — the shade isn't rated for higher heat.

**Q: Is the fabric shade removable for cleaning?**
A: Yes, it lifts off the frame. Spot-clean with a damp cloth and let it air dry fully before reattaching. Don't machine wash it — the material will warp.

**Q: Does it work with smart plugs?**
A: Yes, since it's a simple on/off lamp with a physical switch. Leave the switch in the "on" position and control it through your smart plug's app or voice assistant.
