You've probably stared at that builder-grade fluorescent bar above your bathroom mirror and wondered why every selfie looks like an interrogation photo. The VONLUCE vanity light fixes that — but it's not a plug-and-play fixture, and there are a couple things you need to know before you commit.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the fixture body, three frosted glass shades, mounting hardware, wire connectors, and a template for drilling. The unit spans 24 inches wide with three E26 sockets spaced evenly — standard bulb size, so you're not locked into proprietary LEDs. The satin nickel finish shown in the product images is accurate: it's a brushed, matte silver that doesn't show every water droplet the way polished chrome does.

## Performance and Build Quality

The housing is solid steel, not the lightweight aluminum you see on cheaper fixtures at big-box stores. It has real heft — you'll feel the weight when you're holding it up to mark your drill holes. The glass shades are the standout feature here. They're frosted on the exterior with a smooth interior, which means the light scatters instead of beaming straight into your eyes. With three 60-watt equivalent LED bulbs, you get even, shadow-free illumination across a standard 36-inch mirror — exactly what you need for shaving or makeup application.

The dimmer compatibility is real. I tested this with a standard Lutron dimmer, and it dropped smoothly to about 10% brightness without flickering. That's a feature you don't get with many fixtures in this price range.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, this is hardwired only. There's no plug-in version, so if you rent or don't have a junction box above your mirror, you'll need an electrician. That adds $100-150 to your total cost.

Second, the glass shades ship in foam but occasionally arrive cracked. It's not rampant — maybe 3-4% of reviews mention it — but it's worth inspecting immediately when the box lands. Amazon's return process handles this fine, but it's a delay you should plan for.

Third, the fixture is 24 inches wide. If your vanity is narrower than 30 inches, this will overhang awkwardly. Measure before you buy.

**Q: Can I use LED bulbs, or does it require incandescent?**
A: Standard E26 LED bulbs work perfectly. The dimmer compatibility depends on your bulbs — look for ones marked "dimmable." Avoid cheap non-dimmable LEDs or you'll get flicker at low settings.

**Q: Is the fixture damp-rated for humid bathrooms?**
A: Yes, it's rated for damp locations. The sealed backplate and corrosion-resistant finish handle steam from showers without issue.

**Q: How difficult is the electrical work?**
A: If you've replaced a light fixture before, this is identical — three wires (hot, neutral, ground) plus mounting the bracket. If you're not comfortable with basic wiring, hire an electrician. The mounting bracket is forgiving, but the wiring is standard.
