The FT-015 isn't perfect, and you should know the tradeoffs before buying.

**No storage case.** For a tool at this price point, the lack of a protective case feels like an oversight. The cutting edge is sharp — that's a feature — but it also means you need to supply your own protection for drawer or toolbox storage.

**The weight.** At 14.2 ounces, it's on the heavier side. For most tasks, that heft helps. But if you're doing overhead work or precision tasks for hours at a stretch, you'll feel it. One reviewer noted: "Great tool, but I had to take breaks during a long ceiling job."

**Initial stiffness.** A few users mention the action feels stiff out of the box. It loosens up with use, but don't expect buttery-smooth operation on day one.
