## What's Actually in the Box

This is a genuine OEM Kohler part, not a generic aftermarket substitute. As shown in the product image, you get the conversion kit with braided stainless steel supply hoses, quick-connect fittings, and the mounting hardware needed to swap out the old supply lines on a compatible faucet. The "No Finish" designation simply means the parts are bare metal — they're meant to live under your sink, so there's no need for chrome or brushed nickel plating.

The kit is sold as "One Size" because Kohler designed it to work across a specific range of their faucet models rather than being truly universal. That's both the strength and the limitation here.

## Performance and Build Quality

Kohler's OEM parts are consistently well-made, and this kit is no exception. The braided hoses feel substantial, the quick-connect fittings click into place with satisfying precision, and the threads are cleanly machined — no cross-threading frustration. Buyers who confirmed compatibility report the install takes about 15-20 minutes if you're reasonably handy with a basin wrench.

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest tradeoff. The biggest complaint across reviews is compatibility confusion. This kit does **not** work with every Kohler faucet. If you order it without verifying your model number, you may end up with a return on your hands. Kohler's customer service can confirm fitment, but that's an extra step.

Second, there's no instruction sheet in the box. You'll need the original faucet manual or a YouTube tutorial to complete the install. Not a dealbreaker for experienced DIYers, but worth knowing.

Finally, pricing sits near what you'd expect for OEM parts. If your faucet is still under Kohler's warranty, you may get this kit free through a warranty claim — check that before paying out of pocket.
