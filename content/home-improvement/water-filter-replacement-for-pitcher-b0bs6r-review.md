**Does this fit my Brita pitcher?**
Most likely yes. It's designed for standard-size pitchers (classic Brita, Pur, and generic models). Check the dimensions against your current filter — if it looks like a standard cylinder, it'll work. Slim-line pitchers may have compatibility issues.

**How often should I replace it?**
The manufacturer says 2 months or 150 gallons. In practice, replace it when you notice slower flow or a return of chlorine taste — usually 6-8 weeks for average households, sooner in hard-water areas.

**Is the carbon dust dangerous?**
No. It's harmless activated carbon particulate. Rinse the filter under cold water for 2-3 minutes before installing, then run one full pitcher through and discard it. That clears most of the loose dust.
