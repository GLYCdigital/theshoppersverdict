**Build Quality: Where It Wins and Where It Doesn't**

I've installed hundreds of cabinet pulls over the years. The JOY DECOR knobs feel denser than their price point suggests. The zinc alloy construction gives them a reassuring heft that cheap aluminum knobs lack, and the finish has held up through six months of daily kitchen use without chipping or flaking.

**Where It Falls Short (Being Honest)**

- The included screws are standard Phillips-head. If you're installing on frameless cabinets with European-style hinges, you'll want to swap in your own machine screws — the included ones are better suited to face-frame construction.
- The set-screw style on some models can loosen over time if you overtighten or undertighten at installation. Use the included wrench, snug it up, and you're fine — but don't expect zero-maintenance.
- No template or guide included. Not a dealbreaker for experienced DIYers, but if this is your first hardware swap, measure twice and use a level.
