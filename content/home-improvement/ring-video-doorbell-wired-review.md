# Ring Video Doorbell Wired Review: Smart Security on a Budget

Smart doorbells used to mean spending $200+. The **Ring Video Doorbell Wired** flipped that equation — $59 gets you 1080p HD video, two-way talk, motion alerts, and Alexa integration. With 28,000+ reviews and a 4.5-star average, it's the best-selling doorbell camera in America.

But does "budget" mean "compromised"? We dug through thousands of reviews to find out.

## Setup: The Wiring Requirement

Let's get the elephant out of the room: this doorbell needs existing wiring. If your home has a standard doorbell chime with 10-24 VAC wiring, installation takes about 15 minutes with the included tools. If not, you'll need to run new wiring or buy the battery-powered version.

Reviewers consistently praise the installation process when wiring exists. " Took me 15 minutes from unboxing to live feed," is a common refrain. The Ring app walks you through setup with clear instructions, and the physical mounting plate is well-designed for most door frames.

## Video Quality and Features

The 1080p camera delivers sharp, clear footage during daylight and impressive clarity at night thanks to infrared night vision. The 155° horizontal field of view and 90° vertical means you see packages on the ground, not just faces at eye level.

Motion detection is reliable and customizable — you can set motion zones through the app to reduce false alerts from passing cars. The two-way audio with noise cancellation is clear enough that delivery drivers can understand instructions through the speaker.

Alexa integration is a standout: Echo devices announce "Someone's at the front door" and Echo Show screens automatically display the live feed when the doorbell is pressed.

## The Subscription Question

The base functionality (live view, notifications, two-way talk) is free. But recording and reviewing footage requires Ring Protect ($4.99/month). Most reviewers consider this a fair trade-off given the $59 hardware price — even with two years of subscription, you're still under the cost of a Pro model.

## The Verdict

At $59, the Ring Video Doorbell Wired delivers 90% of the premium doorbell experience at 30% of the price. The wiring requirement is the only real gatekeeper. If your home qualifies, this is the simplest smart home upgrade you can make.

**Verdict: 9/10** — The budget smart doorbell that makes you wonder why anyone pays more.
