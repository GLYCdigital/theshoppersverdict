## What You're Actually Getting

This is a standard 15-amp duplex wall outlet — the same size and shape as any residential receptacle — with a twist. The right-side socket is replaced by two USB ports: one Type C, one Type A. Total output is 24 watts across both ports, with each port capable of delivering up to 2.4A at 5V.

The faceplate uses a modern, flat design with rounded corners. It's compatible with standard junction boxes, and the terminals accept both back-wiring and side-wiring. All the mounting hardware — screws, mounting straps, and wire connectors — is included in the box. You'll need a screwdriver and basic electrical knowledge; if you're not comfortable working with live wires, call an electrician.

## Performance and Build Quality

The 24W total output is the headline feature, and it delivers. One reviewer noted, "Charges my iPhone 15 and iPad simultaneously with no slowdown — the old outlet I replaced could barely handle one device." The Type C port alone pulls the full 2.4A, which is meaningful if you've got a newer phone that draws more current than older USB-A devices.

Build quality is solid. The receptacle has a durable thermoplastic face, and the USB module sits flush with the wall plate — no ugly bulge. The tamper-resistant shutters on the AC sockets work smoothly, and the USB ports have a firm insertion feel that won't loosen over time. One buyer mentioned: "Installation took 10 minutes. The terminals are clearly marked, and the outlet sits flush in the box."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

**The dual-port speed.** Multiple reviewers highlight that both ports deliver full power simultaneously — a rare feature at this price point. One customer wrote, "My wife and I charge our phones at the same time and both hit 100% by morning. The old dual USB outlet I had before split the power and took forever."

**The Type C inclusion.** Several reviewers specifically called out that most competing outlets still offer only USB-A ports. Having a native Type C port without needing a dongle or adapter was a deciding factor for many.

**Clean look.** The flat faceplate design and white finish blend into walls without looking like an aftermarket add-on.

## Where It Falls Short

**No Power Delivery (PD).** This is a standard 5V/2.4A USB port — not USB-C PD. If you're hoping to charge a MacBook or modern Android phone at full speed, this won't deliver. It's designed for phones, tablets, e-readers, and accessories, not laptops. One reviewer noted, "Great for phones, but my Steam Deck charges at a crawl on this."

**Wall plate not included.** The product ships with the receptacle and mounting screws, but not a screwless wall plate. You'll reuse your existing one, which can look mismatched if you're upgrading a room with newer plates.

**No power indicator light.** Some competing outlets have a small LED to show power status. This one doesn't, so if a breaker trips, you won't know until you plug something in.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you're upgrading a bedside table, kitchen counter, or home office and want to ditch charging bricks permanently. It's ideal for households with multiple phones, tablets, or e-readers — and anyone who wants a native Type C port without buying a new charger.

**Skip it if** you need to charge laptops or high-power USB-C devices. This is a 24W outlet, not a 65W or 100W PD outlet. Also skip if you're doing a full renovation and want a matching screwless wall plate — factor in that extra cost.

Yes. It's rated for 15A/125V, which covers standard residential circuits in the US and Canada.

**Q: Can I use the USB ports and the AC socket at the same time?**
Absolutely. The USB module runs independently of the AC circuit. Both AC outlets work while the USB ports charge simultaneously.

**Q: Does it require a neutral wire?**
Yes, like most standard outlets, it needs a neutral wire. If you're replacing an older two-wire outlet, you'll need to run a neutral or call an electrician.
