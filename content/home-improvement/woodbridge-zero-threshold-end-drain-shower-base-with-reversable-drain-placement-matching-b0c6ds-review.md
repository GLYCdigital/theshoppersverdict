## What You Actually Get

The box contains the shower base itself (available in 48", 60", and 66" widths), a reversible drain collar assembly, and the necessary hardware for the drain connection. Notably absent from the box: the drain strainer and plumbing trap. You'll need to source those separately, and the instructions recommend a specific tile-in drain flange for a truly flush finish.

The base is compression-molded acrylic, not the thinner vacuum-formed stuff you'll find at big-box stores. That matters — it's stiffer, less prone to flexing underfoot, and the surface has a molded texture pattern that provides real traction. As shown in the product images, the floor slopes uniformly toward the end drain, and the zero-threshold edge has a subtle bevel that helps water shed away from the bathroom floor.

## The Reversa Feature: Not a Gimmick

The standout engineering here is WOODBRIDGE's Reversa drain collar. Instead of rotating the entire base to get the drain on the left or right side of the shower (which throws off your wall alignments), the drain collar itself rotates independently. This means you can position the drain exactly where your existing plumbing sits without compromising the base's alignment with your walls.

One installer noted, "The Reversa system saved me from having to re-run the drain line. I rotated the collar, matched it to my existing P-trap location, and had the base set in under an hour." That's the kind of practical feedback that matters when you're paying for labor by the hour.

## Real-World Performance and Build Quality

The zero-threshold entry is genuinely flush with the finished floor line. At the front edge, the base is roughly 1/8-inch tall — low enough to meet ADA standards without a separate ramp piece. For wheelchair transfers or walk-in access, this is the feature that makes or breaks the product, and it delivers.

Water pooling is a non-issue. The pre-sloped pan channels water toward the drain efficiently, and the textured surface keeps wet feet planted. Multiple reviewers confirmed this, with one noting, "I was skeptical about the texture until I showered with soapy feet — it grips noticeably better than the tile floor outside the shower."

The 4.5-star average holds up because the base is genuinely well-made. The acrylic finish is glossy and white, resists staining from soap scum, and cleans up with standard bathroom products.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs.

**Installation is not a solo project.** The base ships in a large wooden crate, and even the 48-inch model weighs around 80 pounds. The packaging is protective but bulky — plan for a two-person unload and a clear path to the bathroom. You'll also need to set the base in a mortar bed regardless of what the "drop-in" language suggests; without mortar support, you'll feel flex and risk cracking the acrylic over time.

**Acrylic scratches.** If you drop a wrench or a screwdriver during installation, you'll leave a mark. The textured surface hides minor scuffs, but deep gouges are permanent. Tile or stone-resin bases are more forgiving in this regard, though they cost 2-3x more.

**Drain compatibility quirks.** The Reversa collar accepts standard 3/4-inch and 1-1/2-inch drain connections, but you'll need to purchase the WOODBRIDGE-specific tile-in drain kit if you want a truly flush, modern look. Some buyers found this an unexpected extra cost.
