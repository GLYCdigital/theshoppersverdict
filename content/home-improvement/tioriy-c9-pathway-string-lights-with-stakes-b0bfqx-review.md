Let's be direct: most pathway lights at this price point are disposable junk that dies in a season. The TIORIY C9 set with stakes is not that. Is it perfect? No. But for the money, it earns its 4.3-star rating with 215 reviews.

**What's actually in the box**

You get 12 C9 sockets on a 24-foot cord, 12 ground stakes, and 12 screw-in bulbs. The bulbs are genuine C9 size — about 2 inches tall — not the puny mini-lights that manufacturers sometimes pass off as "C9 style." The cord is 22 AWG, which is standard for this class. The stakes are powder-coated metal, roughly 8 inches long, with a loop at the top that holds the socket securely.

As shown in the product image, the assembly is straightforward: stake into the ground, push the socket into the loop, screw in the bulb. No tools needed. Took me about 15 minutes to do the full run.

**Real-world performance**

The light output is the headline. These throw a genuinely warm, amber glow that reads as classic C9 incandescent — but they're LED, so you get that look at about 10% of the power draw. At night, they light up a 3-foot radius around each stake clearly enough to define a walkway without blasting your neighbors' windows.

Build quality is mixed but acceptable. The bulbs are glass with a plastic base, and they seat firmly when screwed in. The stake's socket loop is a solid design — the socket doesn't twist or sag, which is a problem with cheaper sets that use friction clips. Multiple buyers in the Amazon reviews note this: "The stakes are actually sturdy, not the flimsy wire ones you get with most sets." Another reviewer wrote, "Three months in, still working, and I've hit one with the mower twice."

**Where it falls short**

The wire gauge is the weakest point. At 22 AWG, it's fine for a straight run, but if you're looping around curves or pulling taut across a driveway, you'll feel the flex. You need to stake at every socket — no skipping — or the line sags noticeably.

The stakes themselves are a mixed bag in hard soil. In dry, compacted clay (common in the South and Southwest), you'll need to pre-drill with a screwdriver or use a mallet. One reviewer put it plainly: "Great lights, but I broke two stakes trying to push them into dry ground. Soak the area first or drill pilot holes."

No dimmer or timer is included. If you want auto-on at dusk, that's an extra purchase.

**Who should buy this**

- **Homeowners with established, soft soil** — these will sink in easily and look great.
- **Anyone who wants classic C9 ambiance without the power bill** — the LED efficiency is a real win.
- **People who hate throwing away whole strings** — screw-in bulbs mean a dead socket is a 10-second fix, not a full replacement.

**Skip it if** your yard is rock-hard or you need a timer built in. And if you're running more than 50 feet, buy two sets and keep them separate — daisy-chaining these on one circuit will dim the far end.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can these stay out in winter?**
A: The LEDs are rated for temperature extremes, and the stakes won't rust quickly thanks to the powder coating. But the plastic bulb bases get brittle in sustained sub-zero temps. If you live where it stays below 20°F for weeks, bring the bulbs inside.

**Q: How bright are they actually?**
A: Each bulb is roughly equivalent to a 7-watt incandescent C9. Enough to define a path and add ambiance, but not enough to read by or light up a full driveway. That's the aesthetic they're going for — warm and inviting, not security lighting.

**Q: Can I replace just the bulbs if one dies?**
A: Yes. They're standard E17-base C9 LED bulbs. Any brand that fits the base works, though you'll want to match the color temperature (these are 2700K warm white).

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a classic C9 glow without the energy cost, and your soil isn't concrete. The stakes are genuinely better than the competition, and the screw-in bulb design means this set can last years with minimal upkeep.

**Skip it if** you need auto-on/off, have brutal hardpan soil, or plan to run these as your primary driveway lighting — they're accent lights, not floodlights.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid value, honest construction, a few compromises. For the price, this is one of the better C9 pathway setups on Amazon right now.
