If you’ve been scrolling through waterfall faucets and seeing nothing but zinc alloy and plastic internals, the HOIGY Brass 4-Inch is the first one that made me stop and read the fine print. It’s a genuine brass body with a wide, 4-inch spout that lays water out like a glass ribbon. At a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, buyers are clearly happy — but as with any waterfall design, there are a few installation realities you need to know before you commit.

## What You Actually Get

The box includes the faucet body, a single-handle lever, a brushed brass finish base cover, mounting hardware, and two 24-inch stainless steel supply lines with standard 3/8-inch compression fittings. That last part matters — many faucets in this price range ship with nylon hoses or make you buy lines separately. HOIGY includes solid steel hoses, which is a genuine value.

The faucet measures 4 inches tall from base to spout lip and the spout reaches about 5.5 inches forward. That low profile is intentional — it’s designed for vessel sinks or shallow countertop basins where a tall arch faucet would look awkward. The finish is a brushed brass that reads more muted gold than shiny, which photographs well and hides water spots better than polished brass.

## Build Quality and Daily Use

The body is solid brass, not a brass-colored coating over zinc. You can feel the weight difference when you lift it — this thing has heft. The lever action is smooth with a quarter-turn ceramic cartridge, which is the good kind that doesn’t drip after a year. One buyer noted, “The brass finish is uniform and the construction feels premium — way better than the $30 faucets I’ve tried before.” I’ll second that from the specs alone.

The waterfall flow itself is the star. It produces a wide, clear sheet of water — roughly 3 inches wide — that looks genuinely high-end. But here’s the catch: that wide, flat stream hits the basin with less vertical force than a standard aerated stream. On a shallow vessel sink (under 4 inches deep), you’ll get noticeable splashing on your countertop. Deep undermount sinks handle it beautifully. Plan your basin depth before you buy.

## Where It Falls Short

The single-handle lever is compact — about 2.5 inches — and it’s not the easiest to grip with wet hands. Multiple reviewers mention that fine-tuning temperature takes a delicate touch, especially if you have larger fingers. It’s not a dealbreaker, but it’s worth knowing if you’re used to paddle-style handles.

Also, the 4-inch spout height means this won’t work for hand-washing under the stream comfortably if you have a standard 6-inch-deep drop-in sink. It’s optimized for vessel or semi-recessed basins. If you have a deep standard sink, measure your clearance first.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Vessel sink owners** with a 4-inch or shallower basin — this is your match.
- **Anyone replacing a worn faucet on a 1-hole or 3-hole sink** — the included deck plate covers up to 3 holes cleanly.
- **Buyers who want a solid brass faucet under $80** without hunting for a deal.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the 1-hole version really cover a 3-hole sink?**
Yes. The base cover plate is included and has a 4-inch diameter, which covers standard 3-hole configurations with 4-inch centers. Just use the cover and mount it as a single-hole setup.

**Q: Are the supply lines long enough?**
The included 24-inch stainless steel lines are standard length and fit most vanity setups. If your shutoff valves are more than 24 inches from the faucet base, you’ll need extensions.

**Q: Will this fit a 3/8-inch or 1/2-inch supply line?**
It uses 3/8-inch compression fittings, which match standard shutoff valves. If you have 1/2-inch lines, you’ll need an adapter.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if** you have a vessel sink, want real brass construction without paying premium prices, and appreciate a wide, dramatic waterfall stream that’s genuinely functional — not just decorative.

**Skip it if** you have a deep standard drop-in sink, want a tall spout for maximum clearance, or need a handle that’s easy to grip with wet, soapy hands. Also skip if your shutoff valves are unusually far from the faucet base.

The HOIGY brass waterfall faucet is one of the few budget-friendly options that doesn’t cut corners on material. It’s not perfect for every sink, but for the right setup, it’s a knockout.
