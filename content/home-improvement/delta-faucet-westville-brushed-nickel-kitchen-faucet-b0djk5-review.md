The Delta Faucet Westville in brushed nickel is the kitchen faucet equivalent of a solid mid-size sedan. It won't make your neighbors jealous, but it will start reliably every single day for years without drama. That's exactly what most people actually need from a kitchen faucet — and it's why this one sits at a respectable 4.2 stars across 210 ratings.

## What You're Actually Getting

Out of the box, you get the faucet body, pull-down spray head, 59-inch nylon hose, mounting hardware, and supply lines. The brushed nickel finish has that subtle warm-gray tone that reads more expensive than it is — it doesn't scream "showroom" but photographs well and hides smudges better than polished finishes.

The headline feature here is Delta's DIAMOND Seal technology. Instead of a rubber washer that wears out and causes drips, this uses a ceramic disc valve. That's not marketing fluff — it's a genuine engineering improvement that addresses the #1 failure point on cheap faucets. The valve is rated for 5 million cycles, which translates to roughly a decade of normal family use.

## Real-World Performance

Installation is straightforward if you've swapped a faucet before — plan for 45 minutes to an hour. The supply lines are pre-attached, which saves a trip under the sink with a wrench. The magnetic docking for the spray head is satisfying; it clicks back into place with a solid thunk rather than flopping around like cheaper models.

Water flow is strong at 1.8 GPM, and the spray toggle on the head switches between stream and spray modes with one thumb press. The hose gives you solid coverage across a standard 33-inch sink, though the 59-inch reach means you'll stretch a bit for the far corners of extra-deep farmhouse models.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The finish gets near-universal love. One reviewer noted, "I've had this for six months and the brushed nickel still looks brand new — no spots, no fingerprints, nothing." Another long-term owner highlighted the durability: "Had a Delta with the same valve tech for 11 years. Bought this for the new house without hesitation."

The magnetic dock also earns repeated shout-outs. Multiple buyers mentioned how much they appreciate the spray head staying put — a small thing that you notice every single day.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the weak spots. The 59-inch hose is shorter than some competitors pushing 68 inches. If you have a deep prep sink or a wide double-basin, you'll feel the difference. One buyer put it plainly: "Great faucet, but I wish the hose was six inches longer. I have to lean in to reach the back of my sink."

A few reviewers also noted the spray head has a slight wobble when docked if you bump it. It's cosmetic, not functional — the head stays put — but it's the kind of detail that bugs certain people. And there's no soap dispenser or side sprayer included. Delta sells them separately, but factor that into your total cost.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want a reliable, low-maintenance faucet with a premium finish that won't demand attention. It's ideal for busy family kitchens, rental properties, or anyone who's tired of replacing cheap faucets every few years. The DIAMOND Seal technology alone justifies the price for long-term owners.

**Skip it if:** You need maximum hose reach for an oversized sink, or you're looking for a statement piece with designer aesthetics. Also skip if you want an integrated soap dispenser — you'll pay extra to add one.

## Three Questions Shoppers Ask

**Is the brushed nickel finish hard to keep clean?**
No — this is one of its strengths. The finish resists fingerprints and water spots noticeably better than chrome. A quick wipe with a damp cloth handles everything.

**Will this fit my existing sink holes?**
Yes, for most setups. It uses a standard 1-3/8 inch hole, and the deck plate covers configurations with three holes. If you're replacing a faucet from the last 20 years, you're almost certainly fine.

**How hard is installation for a DIYer?**
Moderate. If you can use a basin wrench and follow YouTube instructions, you'll manage. The pre-attached supply lines save the most frustrating step. Budget an hour for your first time.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The Delta Westville is the definition of a smart, boring purchase. It delivers exactly what it promises — reliable performance, a durable finish, and genuine engineering where it matters — without flashy gimmicks. The shorter hose and missing accessories keep it from a perfect score, but for the vast majority of kitchens, this faucet will serve you faithfully for a decade or more. That's a trade worth making.
