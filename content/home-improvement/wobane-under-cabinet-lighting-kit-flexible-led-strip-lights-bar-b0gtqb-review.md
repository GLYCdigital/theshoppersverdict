If you've ever fumbled for a light switch while holding a hot pan, you already know the problem this WOBANE kit solves. Under-cabinet lighting is one of those upgrades that feels almost frivolous until you have it — then you wonder how you cooked without it. The WOBANE Flexible LED Strip Light Kit (model B0GTQBPD2F) aims to deliver that upgrade without an electrician, a junction box, or a single wire nut. Based on 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, it's largely succeeding. Here's the real breakdown.

## What's Actually in the Box

The kit ships with two 19.7-inch flexible LED strips (about 40 inches total coverage), a 6-foot power cord with a USB adapter, adhesive backing pre-applied, and a set of metal mounting clips. The strips themselves are thin — roughly 0.2 inches — so they tuck neatly into that gap between your upper cabinets and the countertop. The motion sensor sits on the cord, not the strip, which matters: you can position the sensor where it sees you approach while the light itself stays hidden under the cabinet. That's a small design choice that makes a big difference in real kitchens.

## Performance and Build Quality

The LEDs output a warm white light (3000K) that lands somewhere between cozy and functional. It's bright enough to illuminate a standard 30-inch countertop workspace without being clinical. The motion sensor is the headline feature, and it's genuinely good — it triggers reliably within about a 3-foot range and a 100-degree angle, then shuts off 30 seconds after you step away. That's the "hands-free" experience the listing promises, and it delivers.

Build quality is respectable for the price point. The strip has a silicone coating that resists kitchen grease and moisture splashes, and the adhesive holds firmly on smooth surfaces. The included clips are your backup plan — screw them in if you're dealing with anything less than perfectly flat wood.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The ease of installation comes up in nearly every positive review. As one buyer put it, "Stuck them under the cabinet and plugged them in. Took longer to unbox than to install." Another noted the motion sensor was the deciding factor: "I don't have to touch anything with raw chicken hands. Wave and it's on."

The cut-to-fit flexibility also gets frequent mention. The strip has marked cut points every few inches, so you can trim it to match your exact cabinet width. One reviewer said, "I cut mine down to 14 inches for a narrow cabinet and it worked perfectly — no dark spots, no dead segments."

## Where It Falls Short

The adhesive is the weak link. On textured cabinets, particle board, or anything with a rough finish, the tape won't hold long-term. Several reviewers reported strips sagging after a few weeks. The fix is simple — use the included clips — but it's worth knowing before you install.

Also, there's no dimmer. The light runs at one fixed brightness. For a kitchen with a lot of ambient light, that's fine. For a dim pantry or a media nook, it might feel harsh. And the 6-foot cord means you need a nearby outlet; if your cabinets lack one, you'll need an extension or a battery-powered alternative.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Renters** who can't hardwire anything and want lighting without permanent changes
- **DIY beginners** who want a functional upgrade in under 15 minutes
- **Anyone with smooth cabinet undersides** who wants motion-activated task lighting for cooking or reading

**Skip it if** you need dimming control, have heavily textured cabinets (unless you're okay with screwing in clips), or need to cover more than 40 inches of continuous space without an additional kit.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I connect multiple kits together?**
No — each kit operates independently with its own power cord. You can't daisy-chain them. If you need longer coverage, buy two kits and place them near the same outlet.

**Q: How long do the LEDs last?**
The listing claims 50,000 hours. Realistically, you'll get several years of daily use before any noticeable dimming. The adhesive will likely fail before the LEDs do.

**Q: Does the motion sensor work through cabinet doors?**
No. It's infrared-based and needs a clear line of sight. The sensor is on the cord, so position it where it can "see" you approaching — typically hanging just below the cabinet front.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want motion-activated under-cabinet lighting without hiring an electrician and don't mind the fixed brightness. It's a solid, well-reviewed kit that does exactly what it promises.

**Skip it if** you need dimming, have rough cabinet surfaces you won't screw clips into, or require more than 40 inches of coverage per outlet.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The adhesive flaw and lack of dimming keep it from perfection, but for the price and ease of use, this is one of the better plug-and-play options on Amazon right now.
