A bathroom vanity light is one of those purchases you don't think about until you're trying to put on makeup in a shadow-filled cave. The A1A9 5-Light fixture promises to fix that. After digging through 235 ratings and the technical specs, here's the real story.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the 30-inch mounting bar, five adjustable light heads with clear glass shades, mounting hardware, and wire connectors. The fixture is designed for a 24- to 36-inch vanity — measure your mirror first, because this thing spans a solid 30 inches. The build is matte black metal with a modern, almost industrial look. It's lighter than it appears in photos, which is good for mounting but means the metal is on the thinner side. Not flimsy, just not heavy-duty commercial grade.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

The standout feature here is the adjustable heads. Each of the five lights pivots independently, so you can angle them to eliminate shadows on your face — a huge deal for anyone who's ever done makeup or shaving under a single overhead light. The clear glass shades mean maximum light output; with five 60-watt equivalent bulbs (E12 base, not included), you're getting serious illumination. One buyer noted, "I can finally see my face without leaning into the mirror like a gargoyle."

The damp rating is legit. Bathrooms with steamy showers won't wreck the finish or cause the sockets to corrode. The matte black paint holds up well to humidity, though it does show water spots if you don't wipe it down occasionally.

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

The adjustable heads come up constantly in positive reviews. One verified purchase wrote: "Each light moves independently so I can point them exactly where I need. My wife does her makeup at the vanity and she's thrilled." Another buyer mentioned the installation was straightforward — about 45 minutes for someone comfortable with basic wiring. The brightness is another consistent theme: "These are BRIGHT. Like, dentist office bright, which is exactly what I wanted."

**Where It Falls Short**

The bulb situation is the biggest gotcha. This fixture uses E12 candelabra base bulbs, not the standard medium base most people have in their homes. You'll need to buy five bulbs separately, and LED versions with good color rendering (90+ CRI for accurate makeup application) run $4–8 each. That's an extra $20–40 you should budget for.

Installation is a two-person job. The bar is heavy enough that holding it against the wall while connecting wires is genuinely awkward. One reviewer said, "I managed solo but dropped it twice and scuffed the paint. Get help." Also, the mounting screws are a bit short for thicker drywall — you might need longer ones from your local hardware store.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The makeup/skincare enthusiast** — the adjustable heads and bright output are worth the price alone
- **Anyone with a 24–36 inch vanity** — this fills the space properly without looking tiny or oversized
- **DIYers comfortable with basic wiring** — if you've replaced a light fixture before, you'll manage this one

**Skip it if** you want a fixture with included bulbs, prefer a traditional fixed-shade look, or have a single-gang junction box that doesn't align with the 30-inch mounting holes (you'll need a junction box extender).

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this fixture need special bulbs?**
A: Yes, E12 candelabra base bulbs. Five are needed. LED bulbs with 2700K–3000K color temperature look best with the matte black finish.

**Q: Can I install this over an existing junction box?**
A: Yes, but the mounting bar spans 30 inches. If your existing box isn't centered, you'll need to adjust the bar position — the slots allow some play, but not much.

**Q: Is it truly damp-rated?**
A: Yes, it's rated for damp locations, meaning it's safe in bathrooms with steam. It's not rated for wet locations, so don't put it in a shower enclosure.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want bright, adjustable vanity lighting with a modern look and don't mind buying bulbs separately. **Skip it if** you're on a tight budget (the bulb cost will sting) or want a fixture that's a true one-person install.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The adjustable heads and bright output are genuinely excellent, and the damp rating handles bathroom humidity well. The bulb situation and awkward installation keep it from a perfect score, but for the price, this is a solid upgrade over builder-grade fixtures.
