## The Bathroom Vanity Light That Actually Gets the Details Right

Most vanity lights fall into one of two traps: they're either cheaply built with plastic shades that yellow within a year, or they're overpriced designer pieces that don't justify the markup. The Indoi Bathroom Vanity Light with Clear Glass Shades sits in the sweet spot between those extremes — and after digging through 229 ratings and the spec sheet, here's what you need to know before clicking buy.

## What's Actually in the Box

This is a hardwired, wall-mounted fixture designed for bathrooms, and it comes fully assembled. That's a bigger deal than it sounds. Many competitors ship individual glass shades and sockets that you have to wire together yourself — Indoi arrives ready to mount. The package includes the fixture body, mounting bracket, and hardware. You'll need your own E26 base bulbs (more on that below), a screwdriver, and a wire stripper. No drill required if you're replacing an existing light in the same location.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The housing is constructed from rust-resistant steel with a matte black finish — not the cheap spray-paint coating you see on budget fixtures. The clear glass shades are the standout feature here. They're genuinely clear, not that slightly green-tinted glass you get on bargain options, and they diffuse light well enough to eliminate harsh shadows on your face.

For a bathroom, this matters. Whether you're shaving or applying makeup, side-mounted or above-mirror lighting needs to be shadow-free. The Indoi's open-bottom design allows light to bounce off the wall behind the fixture, creating the kind of ambient fill that flatters rather than flattens.

One caveat: this fixture requires E26 bulbs, and they're not included. For best results, pair it with 60-watt equivalent LED bulbs with a color temperature around 3000K (soft white) for a warm, flattering glow. Avoid daylight bulbs in the 5000K range — they'll make your bathroom feel like a hospital examination room.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the reviews, three themes keep surfacing. First, installation ease. Multiple buyers mention finishing the job in under 20 minutes. One reviewer wrote, "Took longer to unbox it than to wire it up. The bracket is solid and the connections were straightforward." Second, the quality of the glass. Another commenter noted, "These shades look expensive — like something from a boutique hotel, not a $60 fixture." Third, the even light distribution. As one verified purchaser put it, "No more shadows under my chin when I'm doing my skincare routine. Huge difference from my old light bar."

## Where It Falls Short

The bulb situation is the most common frustration. At this price point, buyers expect bulbs included — they're not. That's an extra $15–$25 depending on what you choose, and it's not disclosed clearly in the product photos.

Second, this is hardwired only. If you're a renter or you don't have an existing junction box in the right spot, you'll need an electrician. That could double your total cost. The fixture is also on the smaller side — measure your vanity width before ordering to make sure it's proportional.

## Who Should Buy This

- **DIY homeowners replacing an outdated builder-grade fixture:** This is a straightforward weekend upgrade that doesn't require special tools.
- **Anyone frustrated by plastic shades:** The glass here is the selling point. It won't yellow, crack, or look cheap.
- **Renters with existing hardwired lighting:** If you have a junction box, this is a landlord-friendly upgrade you can swap back when you move.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use dimmable bulbs with this fixture?**
Yes, as long as you buy dimmable LED bulbs. The fixture itself has no dimmer built in, so you'll also need a compatible dimmer switch installed.

**Q: Does it come with a warranty?**
Indoi offers a standard one-year warranty against manufacturer defects. It's not exceptional, but it's consistent with the category.

**Q: What's the maximum wattage per bulb?**
The fixture supports up to 60-watt equivalent bulbs per socket. Stick with LED to stay well within that range.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a polished, glass-shaded vanity light that installs easily and looks far more expensive than it is. This is a legitimate upgrade that will change how your bathroom feels every time you flip the switch.

**Skip it if** you're hardwired-out, want bulbs included, or need a larger multi-socket fixture for a wide double vanity.
