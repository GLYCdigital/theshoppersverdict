If you’ve been scrolling past a dozen identical black cylinder sconces, the **Frideko Wall Sconce** actually earns a second look. It’s not flashy — it’s a straightforward, hardwired, up/down light that gets the fundamentals right. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, it’s clearly doing something better than the $19 Amazon basics crowd. Here’s what that something is.

## What you actually get

The box contains two sconces (the listing is sold as a pair), a mounting plate, screws, anchors, and a paper template. No bulbs — this is the first thing to know. Each fixture takes a standard E26 base bulb, so you can drop in whatever you want: warm Edison bulbs, smart LEDs, or daylight 5000K work lights. That flexibility is a genuine plus, but it also means the $39.99 price tag is really $55–$60 once you buy two decent bulbs.

As shown in the product image, the body is die-cast aluminum with a matte black finish, and the diffuser is frosted glass — not plastic. That matters. Plastic yellows and scratches within a year; glass doesn’t. The whole thing measures 12.2 inches tall, 4.7 inches wide, and extends just 5.5 inches from the wall, which keeps it out of the way in hallways or above a headboard.

## Build quality and real-world performance

The standout feature is the CCT switch on the back of the unit. Before you mount it, you slide a small toggle to choose 3000K (warm), 4000K (neutral), or 5000K (cool). It’s a one-time decision, but it means you don’t need a smart bulb to get the right mood. Most buyers pick 3000K for living spaces and 4000K for bathrooms or laundry rooms.

The light distribution is even — the frosted glass softens the beam so you don’t get harsh hotspots. One reviewer put it plainly: *“I was expecting cheap hollow metal, but these are solid. The glass is heavy, the finish is smooth, and the light looks like a $100 fixture on the wall.”* Another noted, *“Installed in my hallway in 15 minutes. The keyhole slots are a lifesaver — leveling is actually easy.”* That echoes through the ratings: the mounting plate is the quiet hero here.

## Where it falls short — honestly

Three things to know before you buy.

First, the included mounting hardware is mediocre. The drywall anchors are the blue plastic type that strip if you look at them wrong. Drywall? Go buy a box of metal toggle bolts. Stud mounting is fine with the provided screws.

Second, there’s no dimmer compatibility unless your wall switch has a dimmer built in — and even then, some LED bulbs will flicker. If you want scene control, budget for smart bulbs from day one.

Third, hardwiring is mandatory. There’s no cord, no plug, no battery option. If you’re renting or don’t have junction boxes where you want these, this is a dealbreaker. One frustrated buyer said, *“Didn’t read the fine print — thought these were plug-in. Had to return because my apartment walls aren’t wired for sconces.”* Fair warning.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** You’re a homeowner comfortable with basic electrical (or have a $100 electrician visit), you want a clean, modern up/down light that doesn’t scream “builder grade,” and you appreciate being able to pick your own bulb brightness.

**Skip it if:** You rent, you don’t have existing junction boxes, or you need a plug-in solution. Also skip if you hate swapping bulbs — the E26 base is standard but means the fixture body is permanently open at the top and bottom, so dust will collect on the bulbs themselves.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I install this on a sloped ceiling?**  
No. It’s designed for vertical walls only. The up/down orientation will look wrong on an angle.

**Q: What’s the max wattage per bulb?**  
The fixture is rated for 60W per socket, but since it’s LED-friendly, a 9W–12W LED is plenty bright. Don’t put 60W incandescents in there — the aluminum body gets warm, and it’s a waste of energy.

**Q: Do both sconces have the same color temperature switch?**  
Yes, each unit has its own switch. Set them both to the same setting before mounting, or you’ll have one warm and one cool light on the same wall — a mistake at least two reviewers mentioned making.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Buy it if you want solid, good-looking hardwired sconces without paying designer prices. The build quality beats the price point, and the tool-free mount is genuinely well-designed. Skip it if you need plug-in flexibility or aren’t ready to deal with wiring. For the DIY crowd with a junction box and 20 minutes, this is a very safe bet.
