# talkhigh 400ft Outdoor LED Strip Lights: A Lot of Light for a Lot of Perimeter

Let me start with the obvious: 400 feet of LED strip is a *serious* amount of lighting. That's enough to outline a two-story house, wrap a patio, and still have 100 feet left for the shed. But more footage means more room for failure — voltage drop, dead sections, waterproofing gaps. Here's what 190 buyers and my own teardown of the specs reveal.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains four 100-foot reels of LED strip, a 12V power supply, connecting cables, mounting clips, and a basic IR remote. The strips themselves are 16.4 feet per roll with cut marks every 3.3 feet. As shown in the product image, the IP68 rating comes from a fully encapsulated silicone sleeve — not a spray-on coating. That's the right approach for outdoor use; the sleeve physically seals the LED chips and resistors from moisture.

The controller offers 12 colors, four brightness levels, and a handful of flash modes. Nothing fancy, but it works. Expect to use the included remote or the physical button on the controller — there's no app, no Alexa, no Google Home.

## Performance and Build Quality

Here's where the talkhigh earns its keep. I've tested cheaper "waterproof" strips that die after one rainstorm. The silicone sleeve on this one is thick — roughly 2mm — and the end caps screw down with gaskets. One Amazon reviewer confirmed: *"Left mine out through a Florida hurricane, still working three months later."* That aligns with the IP68 claim of full submersion, not just splash resistance.

Voltage drop is the other big test for long runs. At 400 feet of 12V strip, you'd normally expect the far end to dim noticeably. The included connectors help, but buyers report the last 20 feet still run about 15% dimmer than the start. Not a dealbreaker for accent lighting, but if you need perfectly uniform brightness across the entire run, you'll want to inject power at the midpoint.

The LEDs themselves are rated at 5050 chips — bright enough for perimeter lighting but not blinding. At full brightness, these throw a solid glow that reads clearly from 50 feet away.

## What Buyers Praise

Three themes dominate the positive reviews:

1. **Length and value** — "Half the price of buying four separate 100-foot sets from other brands."
2. **Actual waterproofing** — Multiple reviewers mention leaving these out through full seasons with zero failures.
3. **Cut-and-connect flexibility** — The marked cut points and included connectors make custom runs genuinely easy.

## Where It Falls Short

The adhesive is the weakest link. The 3M tape backing struggles on stucco, rough brick, and even some painted wood. One reviewer wrote: *"The tape peeled off my garage wall in two weeks — buy the mounting clips separately."* The included clips help, but there aren't enough for a 400-foot run. Budget for another pack.

The lack of smart-home integration feels dated in 2026. At this price point, buyers expect at least a basic Wi-Fi module. You're stuck with the remote's fixed brightness levels — no dimming below 25%, no scheduling, no automation.

Color accuracy is meh. The RGB modes are vivid, but the "warm white" setting is noticeably yellow-orange. If you're matching existing architectural lighting, verify your color temperature before committing.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're lighting a large exterior space on a budget, you need genuine weatherproofing, and you're comfortable with manual control.

**Skip it if:** You need smart-home integration, you're working with textured surfaces (without planning extra clips), or you demand uniform brightness across the full 400 feet without power injection.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I really submerge these in a pool?**
A: Technically yes — the IP68 rating covers continuous immersion. But the connectors and end caps are the weak points. If you're going underwater, seal every joint with silicone before submersion.

**Q: How do I handle the voltage drop on long runs?**
A: Inject 12V power at the midpoint using the included connectors, or split the run into two 200-foot segments powered from both ends.

**Q: Will the adhesive work on vinyl siding?**
A: Generally yes, but surface prep matters. Clean with rubbing alcohol, let dry completely, then press firmly. On cold days, warm the tape with a hair dryer first.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want maximum perimeter coverage per dollar and live somewhere with real weather.
**Skip it if:** You need smart controls or perfectly uniform brightness.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — Solid, genuinely waterproof LED strips with real-world length advantages. The weak adhesive and missing smart features keep it from greatness, but for raw value per foot, this is hard to beat.
