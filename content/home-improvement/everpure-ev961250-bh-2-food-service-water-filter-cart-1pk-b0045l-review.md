## FAQ

**How often should I replace this filter?**
Every 12 months or 12,000 gallons, whichever comes first. If your water is particularly hard or you run high volume, replace it at 10 months to be safe. The water flow rate will drop noticeably when it's exhausted.

**Will this fit my existing Everpure system?**
If your system uses a BH2 cartridge, yes. Check the model number on your current cartridge — if it says EV961250 or BH2, this is the direct replacement. If you have an older head assembly, measure the cartridge length to confirm it's the 20-inch version.

**Do I need to flush the new cartridge before using the water?**
Yes, absolutely. Run cold water through it for at least five minutes or 10 gallons before brewing anything. This removes carbon fines from the manufacturing process. Skipping this step will give you black water and a bad first cup.
