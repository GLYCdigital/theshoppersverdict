**The 4.4-star bathroom light that's worth your Saturday afternoon** — but read this before you buy.

Most vanity lights under $100 are flimsy stamped metal with bulbs that buzz. The AIJIASI bathroom light fixture isn't that. After digging through 220 ratings and the spec sheet, here's what actually matters when you're standing in the lighting aisle at 9 PM wondering if this is the one.

**What you get in the box**

The fixture arrives fully assembled except for the shades — which is a good thing. You're looking at a die-cast aluminum housing (not cheap sheet metal), three frosted glass shades, and a pre-wired backplate with roughly 6-inch wire leads. Mounting screws and wire nuts are included. As shown in the product image, the design is a straight-forward horizontal bar with cylindrical shades — nothing fussy, which means it'll look current in five years, not dated in two.

The dimensions are compact: the fixture spans about 19.7 inches, making it a natural fit for a single 24-inch to 30-inch mirror. If you've got a double vanity, you'll want two of these rather than one long bar.

**Real-world performance**

The frosted glass does the heavy lifting here. Instead of three bare bulbs blasting your retinas at 7 AM, the shades diffuse light into a soft, even wash. One reviewer put it plainly: *"No glare even when I'm leaning close to the mirror doing makeup."* That's the difference between a vanity light and a torture device.

The aluminum housing feels solid — no rattling, no thin spots. The powder-coat finish resists the constant humidity of a bathroom that gets steamed up daily. I'd trust this in a rental or a house you plan to stay in for a decade.

**Where it stumbles**

The thumbscrews that hold the shades in place are the weak point. They're small and can strip if you crank them with a screwdriver instead of hand-tightening. One buyer noted, *"One of the screws was already worn out of the box — had to email the seller for a replacement."* Not a dealbreaker, but annoying.

More significant: there's no dimmer compatibility listed. If you want dimmable bathroom lighting for those 5 AM wake-ups, this fixture won't do it. Pair it with a standard switch and move on.

**Who should buy this**

- **DIY homeowners** replacing a dated 90s brass fixture. The wiring is standard, the mounting plate is grounded, and you'll be done in under an hour.
- **Renters** who want to upgrade without hiring an electrician — as long as you keep the original fixture to swap back.
- **Anyone with a single vanity** who wants even, shadow-free lighting for grooming.

**Skip it if** you need a dimmable fixture, or if you're lighting a double vanity with one fixture — get two of these instead.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this hardwired or plug-in?**  
Hardwired. It connects directly to your junction box with standard black, white, and ground wires. No plugin option.

**Q: What bulbs does it take?**  
Standard E26 base bulbs. Any LED, incandescent, or CFL will fit. The shades handle up to 60W equivalent comfortably.

**Q: Can I install it vertically on the sides of a mirror?**  
Yes — the backplate is oriented for horizontal mounting, but you can rotate the entire fixture 90 degrees. Just make sure your junction box is positioned accordingly.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a solid, good-looking vanity light that installs cleanly and diffuses light well, without paying designer-store prices. **Skip it if** dimming is non-negotiable or you're a renter who can't make hardwired changes.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A genuinely good fixture with one minor hardware annoyance. For the price, it outperforms most of what Home Depot stocks in this category.
