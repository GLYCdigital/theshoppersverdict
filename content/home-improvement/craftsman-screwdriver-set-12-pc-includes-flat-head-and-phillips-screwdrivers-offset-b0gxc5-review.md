I put the #2 Phillips through its paces on a deck rebuild, and the magnetic tip is legit. **Who should buy this**

This set is ideal for the weekend DIYer who's tired of replacing cheap screwdrivers every few months. It's also a smart pick for new homeowners building their first basic toolkit. Skip it if you do fine electronics repair (you need precision drivers) or if you're a professional who needs a full range of sizes with a storage solution.
