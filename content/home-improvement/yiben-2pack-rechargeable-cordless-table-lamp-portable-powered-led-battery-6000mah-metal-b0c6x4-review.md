**The YIBEN 2Pack Cordless Table Lamp: A Genuine Workhorse That Earns Its Spot**

Most cordless lamps under $40 feel like disposable gadgets — hollow plastic, weak light, and batteries that die after six months of use. The YIBEN 2Pack is not that product. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, this lamp has quietly become a favorite among campers, power-outage preppers, and anyone who's tired of tripping over extension cords. Here's the honest breakdown after digging through the specs and user feedback.

**What You Actually Get**

Two complete lamps, each with a 7.5-inch aluminum alloy body and a weighted base that keeps it planted. The 6000mAh battery is the headline — that's roughly double what most competitors pack in this form factor. Charging is via USB-C (cable included, wall adapter not), and there's a USB-A output port so the lamp can charge your phone in a pinch. Each lamp measures about 4.3 inches in diameter at the base and stands 13 inches tall, with a gooseneck neck that holds position without drooping.

**Real-World Performance**

The light quality is where this lamp separates itself from cheap alternatives. Three color temperatures — warm (3000K), neutral (4500K), and cool daylight (6500K) — cover everything from cozy evening reading to task lighting. Stepless dimming lets you dial from a 10% night-light glow to the full 500-lumen output. That's roughly equivalent to a 40-watt incandescent bulb: fine for reading, eating, or working on a laptop. Just don't expect it to illuminate a garage workbench.

One buyer put it well: *"Used these during a two-day power outage. Ran them on medium brightness for 6 hours each night and never saw the battery dip below half."* That tracks with the math — at 50% brightness, you're looking at roughly 20 hours of continuous use. Crank it to max and you'll get closer to 8 hours, which is still respectable.

**Where Buyers Rave**

The build quality gets consistent praise. The aluminum body feels substantial, not tinfoil-thin, and the weighted base means the lamp doesn't tip over when you bump the table. Multiple reviewers noted using these for years without battery degradation — one mentioned *"still holds a charge like day one after 18 months of weekly use."* The two-pack value is also a recurring theme; at roughly $20-25 per lamp depending on current pricing, you're getting legitimate utility for the money.

**Honest Shortcomings**

The touch controls are the weak point. The panel has separate buttons for power, color temperature, and dimming, but they're close together and sensitive. A mis-tap switches modes instead of turning the light off — annoying in the dark. The lack of a memory function compounds this: every power-on defaults to warm white at 100% brightness, so you'll be re-dialing your settings constantly if you prefer cooler light or lower output.

Also worth noting: the 500-lumen max is fine for ambient and reading light, but it's not a task lamp. If you need focused illumination for fine detail work, look elsewhere.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Campers and RV owners** — the USB-C charging and long runtime make this a no-brainer for off-grid lighting
- **Emergency preparedness folks** — a reliable light source that also charges your phone is worth its weight in a blackout
- **Renters who hate overhead lighting** — the warm setting mimics incandescent bulbs nicely for living rooms and bedrooms
- **Students in dorms** — no outlet required means you can light up a bunk bed or study nook anywhere

**Skip It If:**

- You need task-level brightness for hobbies or detailed work
- You're looking for smart-home integration or app control — this is a manual lamp, and that's fine
- You prefer a lamp with a built-in timer or auto-off features

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does it take to fully charge?**
A: About 4-5 hours via the included USB-C cable using a standard 5V/2A adapter. A fast charger won't speed it up significantly since the lamp's charging circuit caps at around 10W.

**Q: Can I use it while it's charging?**
A: Yes, but with a caveat. The lamp will work plugged in, but you can't use the USB-A output to charge a phone simultaneously — it's one or the other.

**Q: Does the gooseneck hold position reliably?**
A: Generally yes. It's rigid enough to stay put once bent, but it's not a locking joint. If you constantly adjust the angle, it may drift slightly over time. Most users report no issues.
