**The $50 question: Can a budget keypad deadbolt deliver real security without the smart-home premium?**

Most shoppers land on this Amazon Basics keypad deadbolt because it hits that sweet spot — keyless entry without the $200+ price tag of a Wi-Fi smart lock. But "budget" doesn't automatically mean "good value." Here's what 210 buyers at a 4.2-star average are actually saying, and what you need to know before committing.

---

**What's in the box**

You get the full exterior keypad assembly, the interior turnpiece unit, a standard deadbolt latch, two keys, mounting screws, and the required strike plate. The finish is a matte black that looks more expensive than the price suggests. Importantly, it fits standard 2-1/8-inch bore holes — the universal size on most US doors — so you likely won't need to drill new holes. Install is genuinely straightforward: remove your old deadbolt, slide in the new latch, mount both sides, and connect the cable. Most buyers report 20-30 minutes start to finish.

---

**How it actually performs**

The touch keypad uses a capacitive surface rather than physical buttons. It lights up when you touch it and responds to gloved or slightly damp fingers better than most budget keypads I've tested. Entry codes can be 4-8 digits, and you can store up to 10 unique codes — enough for family, a house cleaner, and a dog walker without sharing your main code.

One standout feature at this price: a 9V battery backup. If the four AA batteries die, you can touch the backup terminals with a 9V battery to power the lock temporarily. That's a life-saver for the inevitable "I forgot to change the batteries" moment. The lock also beeps and flashes a low-battery warning for about two weeks before it quits.

Battery life lands around 8-10 months with normal use, which is respectable. Auto-lock is available but requires a specific programming sequence through the interior unit. Multiple reviewers note this is the trickiest part of setup — one wrote, "The auto-lock took me three tries to program, and it still occasionally unlocks a second late."

---

**What buyers consistently praise**

The reliability of the keypad draws the most positive feedback. A verified buyer noted: "I've had this for six months in Michigan weather — freezing temps, rain, snow — and the keypad has never failed me." Another praised the install: "Replaced my old deadbolt in 20 minutes. The screws lined up perfectly with my existing holes."

The mechanical key override is another consistent win. Unlike some electronic locks where the keyhole is an afterthought, this one uses a proper deadbolt cylinder that works smoothly even if the electronics completely die.

---

**Where it falls short**

The biggest limitation is obvious: **no Wi-Fi or Bluetooth**. This is a keypad-only lock. You cannot check lock status remotely, get push notifications, or unlock it from your phone. If you're coming from a smart lock, this will feel like a step backward. If you just want to stop carrying keys, it's fine.

The glossy keypad surface shows fingerprints and smudges almost immediately. It's cosmetic, but if you're particular about appearances, you'll be wiping it down weekly.

Some users report the interior turnpiece feels slightly plasticky compared to the exterior's substantial weight. It works fine, but you can tell where the cost savings went.

---

**Who should buy this**

- **Renters** who can't hardwire a smart lock but want keyless entry without drilling new holes
- **Anyone who loses keys constantly** and just needs a reliable code pad
- **Landlords** managing multiple properties who want to change codes between tenants without rekeying
- **Skip it** if you need remote access, integration with Alexa/Google Home, or activity logs with timestamps

---

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this work with Alexa or Google Home?**
No. It's a standalone keypad deadbolt with no smart home integration. You'll need a separate smart lock for voice control or app access.

**Q: Can I use it on a metal or steel door?**
Yes, but you may need to verify your door's bore hole size. Steel doors sometimes use non-standard dimensions, so measure before ordering.

**Q: How do I change the batteries?**
The battery compartment is on the interior unit. Remove the cover, swap four AA batteries, and replace the cover. The lock retains your programmed codes during the swap.

---

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want dependable keyless entry at a fair price and don't need remote access. **Skip it if** you're looking for a true smart lock with app control and notifications — you'll be frustrated within a week.

At 4.2 stars, this is one of the more reliable budget keypad deadbolts available. It does one thing — keyless entry — and does it well, with solid battery backup and genuine deadbolt security. Just know what you're not getting before you order.
