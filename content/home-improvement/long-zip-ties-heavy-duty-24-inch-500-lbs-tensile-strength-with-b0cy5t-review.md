## FAQ

**Q: Are these reusable?**  
No. Like all zip ties, they're one-time use. The locking mechanism is designed to ratchet one direction only. If you need reusable options, look for releasable cable ties with a thumb-release tab.

**Q: What's the actual operating temperature range?**  
The nylon handles roughly -40°F to 185°F. They'll hold in extreme cold, but as noted, they get stiff and harder to tighten. At high heat, they won't melt in normal use but avoid direct exhaust contact.

**Q: How do these compare to metal hose clamps for automotive use?**  
Zip ties are faster and lighter, but metal clamps are more permanent and heat-resistant. These ties work great for temporary fixes or non-critical applications. For exhaust systems or anything near high heat, stick with metal.
