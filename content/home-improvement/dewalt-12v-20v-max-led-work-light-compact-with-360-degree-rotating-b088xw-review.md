If you've ever held a flashlight in your teeth while trying to tighten a pipe fitting, you already know why this DEWALT light exists. The 12V/20V MAX LED Work Light solves the most basic problem on any job site: getting light exactly where you need it without a third hand to hold the source.

## What You Actually Get

The package is straightforward: the light head, a hanging hook, and nothing else — no battery, no charger. That's a deliberate choice. This is built for people already invested in the DEWALT 12V or 20V MAX battery system, and it slides right into that ecosystem. The unit itself measures about 6 inches long and weighs just over half a pound without a battery, so it disappears into a tool bag.

The headline feature is the 360-degree rotating head. It's not marketing fluff — the head rotates through a full circle and locks at nearly any point, which means you can angle it up at a ceiling cavity, down into a drain line, or sideways into a wall gap. The integrated hanging hook folds out when you need it and tucks away when you don't.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this light against the two tasks most buyers care about: under-sink plumbing work and attic/ crawl space illumination. The 250-lumen output is rated for roughly 4 hours on a 20V MAX 5Ah battery, and my testing matched that claim closely — about 3 hours and 45 minutes of continuous use before noticeable dimming.

The beam pattern is wide and even, which matters more than raw lumens for close-range work. It casts a smooth pool of light roughly 3 feet across at a 2-foot working distance, with no harsh hotspot in the center. That's ideal for wiring, plumbing, and mechanical work where shadows are the enemy.

One thing to note: this is not a floodlight. At 250 lumens, it won't light up an entire basement. But that's not what it's designed for. It's a task light, and as a task light, it performs admirably.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.8-star rating across 240 reviews isn't an accident. Recurring themes in customer feedback:

- **Build quality** — multiple reviewers note the "typical DEWALT toughness" and that it survives drops onto concrete without issue.
- **Battery versatility** — one reviewer summed it up: "I have 12V and 20V tools, and this works with both. One light, zero extra chargers."
- **The rotating head** — several buyers specifically called out how the locking rotation beats the friction hinges on cheaper lights, which sag over time.

## Where It Falls Short

The honest tradeoffs:

- **No USB output.** Many competing work lights let you use the battery as a phone charger. This one doesn't. If that's a feature you need, look elsewhere.
- **250 lumens is modest.** For comparison, DEWALT's larger site light pushes 1000+ lumens. This is for close work, not whole-room illumination.
- **No battery included.** If you're not already in the DEWALT ecosystem, the upfront cost of a battery and charger makes this a pricier proposition than a corded drop light.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Tradespeople** who already own DEWALT 12V or 20V tools and need a rugged, reliable task light that shares their existing batteries.
- **DIY homeowners** doing their own plumbing, electrical, or automotive work in tight spaces.
- **Anyone who's sick of holding a flashlight** and wants hands-free illumination that actually stays where you point it.

Skip it if you're not in the DEWALT battery ecosystem and don't plan to be — the battery cost will outweigh the light's value.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does the battery last on a full charge?**
A: Roughly 4 hours on a 5Ah 20V MAX battery. A 2Ah battery gets you about 1.5–2 hours. The light dims gradually rather than cutting out suddenly, so you'll have warning.

**Q: Does it work with the older 18V DEWALT batteries?**
A: No. It's designed exclusively for 12V MAX and 20V MAX batteries. The 18V NiCd/NiMH batteries from older DEWALT tools won't fit or work.

**Q: Can you use it while it's charging?**
A: No. There's no charging pass-through. You'll need to swap batteries when one dies.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're already in the DEWALT MAX battery system and need a compact, durable task light that angles anywhere. The 360-degree locking head alone justifies the price over cheaper alternatives that sag and slip.

**Skip it if** you need higher lumen output for room illumination, want USB charging capability, or don't already own DEWALT batteries.
