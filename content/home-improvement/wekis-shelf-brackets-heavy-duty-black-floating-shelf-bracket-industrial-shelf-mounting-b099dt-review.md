**Where it falls short**

The installation demands accuracy. You'll also need shelves at least 0.75 inches thick, or the set screws will push through the top surface. And if you're mounting into drywall without hitting a stud, you'll need to use the included anchors and accept a significantly lower weight capacity — probably 30-40 pounds max.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do these fit standard 2x4 lumber shelves?**
Yes, but you'll need to route a 0.5-inch groove along the bottom. A 2x4 is thick enough (actually 1.5 inches) to handle the bracket without issues.

**Q: Can I mount these to drywall without studs?**
Technically yes, using the included anchors, but the load rating drops dramatically. For heavy loads, find the studs — it's worth the extra effort.

**Q: What's the maximum shelf length supported?**
With a pair of brackets, keep the shelf under 36 inches for heavy loads. Longer shelves will flex regardless of bracket strength.
