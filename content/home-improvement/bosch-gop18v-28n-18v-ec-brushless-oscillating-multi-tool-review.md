The BOSCH GOP18V-28N is the cordless oscillating multi-tool that finally convinced a 40-year woodworking veteran that these tools aren't gimmicks. With 654 reviews and a 4.7-star average, it has a smaller sample size than some contenders, but the reviewers who own it tend to love it unreservedly.

## Converting the Skeptics

The most telling review comes from a professional woodworker with four decades of experience. "I'm a bit of an old dog not too keen on new tricks. I've been a professional woodworker for over 40 years. When I first saw these oscillating tools, I thought they were gimmicky and a bit silly." Then a crew member brought one to a job site. "I saw him use it a half dozen times, and finally I was in a position where my available tools weren't quite 'right' so I said 'Darn you! Let me borrow your oscillating tool.' I was awakened to its versatility."

That's the story of the GOP18V-28N: a tool that doesn't seem necessary until you have it, and then you can't imagine working without it.

## StarlockPlus System

The StarlockPlus blade interface is Bosch's proprietary quick-change system — and it's genuinely better than the competition. "Takes 5 seconds to remove and replace a blade. I've used other oscillating tools and replacing blades without StarLock is tedious," writes one reviewer. The snap-in lever releases the blade without touching the hot metal, eliminating burned fingers.

The downside? You're locked into Starlock blades, which cost more than universal-fit options. And that's not a minor consideration for heavy users who go through blades quickly.

## The Battery Concern

A detailed review from careful users flags a specific issue: "My only gripe with this tool is that newest generation Bosch battery packs do not seat perfectly in this tool. They click in and power the tool just fine, but they rattle loudly when the tool is in use. I reserve a couple of my older generation 4AH packs for use with it."

This is worth noting if you're already in the Bosch 18V system with newer batteries. The older packs fit perfectly, so if you have a mix, you're fine.

## The Verdict

The BOSCH GOP18V-28N is a premium cordless oscillating tool that rewards you every time you need to make a flush cut, plunge cut, or sand in a tight space. The brushless motor provides corded-like performance, the Starlock system genuinely saves time and frustration, and the build quality is unmistakably Bosch. The battery rattle on newer packs and the premium blade cost are real considerations, but for anyone doing renovation, trim work, or professional finishing, this tool pays for itself the first time it saves you from cutting a notch by hand.
