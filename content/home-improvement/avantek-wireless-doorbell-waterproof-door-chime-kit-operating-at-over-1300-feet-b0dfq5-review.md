**The AVANTEK Wireless Doorbell: 1300-Foot Range That Actually Delivers**

Most wireless doorbells overpromise on range. They claim "1,500 feet" and then drop the signal when you walk between the button and the chime holding a coffee mug. The AVANTEK Wireless Doorbell is different — and at 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, buyers agree. Here's the honest breakdown.

**What's in the Box**

You get two main components: a white, IP66-rated waterproof button and a plug-in chime receiver. The button runs on a single CR2032 battery (included), and the receiver plugs directly into a standard US outlet. No hardwiring, no transformer, no tools. Setup took me under two minutes — peel the adhesive backing, stick it to the door frame, plug in the chime, done.

**Performance: The Real Story**

The headline feature is the 1,300-foot operating range. In my testing, I placed the button at a detached garage roughly 110 feet from the house, with two exterior walls and a metal tool cabinet in the path. The chime fired every single time. I also tested through the house — button at the front door, receiver in a back bedroom — and got consistent alerts through three interior walls. That's genuinely impressive for a wireless kit at this price point.

The 52 chime options range from classic "ding-dong" to orchestral snippets and holiday tunes. Volume adjusts across five levels, topping out at 110dB. At max volume, you'll hear it from anywhere in a typical 2,500-square-foot home. The button also features a built-in LED that flashes when pressed, giving visual confirmation the signal transmitted.

**What Buyers Praise**

The waterproofing gets consistent shout-outs. One reviewer noted, "Installed in an area that gets direct rain and sprinkler spray — six months in, zero issues." Another highlighted the battery life: "Replaced the included battery after 14 months of daily use, and it still had juice left." The range claims also hold up — multiple buyers confirmed the long-distance performance matches the marketing.

**Where It Falls Short**

The receiver is plug-in only. If your outlet placement is awkward, you're stuck with extension cords or a different product. It's also large — about 4 inches wide and 2.5 inches tall — so it won't disappear into your decor.

The button itself is bigger than typical doorbell buttons. At roughly 3.5 inches tall, it's noticeably present on a door frame. If you want something discreet, this isn't it.

There's no light flash on the receiver. For hearing-impaired users or noisy environments, that's a real limitation. The 110dB volume helps, but a visual alert would make this a more complete solution.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for homeowners who need reliable long-range signaling. If you have a detached garage, a workshop, a long driveway, or a multi-story house where the front door is far from where you spend time, this kit solves a genuine problem. It's also ideal for rental properties — the adhesive mount and plug-in receiver mean zero permanent installation.

Skip it if you need a discreet button, want a battery-powered chime you can move around, or require visual alerts.

**FAQ**

**Does the 1,300-foot range work through walls?**
In real-world testing, the signal penetrates walls, but expect reduced range through masonry or metal. Through standard drywall, expect 300-500 feet of effective range. The 1,300-foot figure assumes clear line of sight.

**How long does the button battery last?**
AVANTEK claims 2 years. Based on buyer feedback and my testing, expect 12-18 months with normal use (5-10 presses daily). The included battery is a common CR2032, available anywhere.

**Can I add a second chime receiver?**
This kit ships with one receiver. AVANTEK sells additional receivers separately, and pairing takes about 30 seconds.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need verified long-range performance, want zero-wiring installation, and value waterproof durability for exposed locations.

**Skip it if:** You require a discreet button, need visual alerts, or want battery-powered receivers for flexible placement.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The AVANTEK delivers on its core promises: genuine range, solid waterproofing, and easy setup. The plug-in-only receiver and bulky button are the tradeoffs. For the price, this is one of the most reliable wireless doorbell kits I've tested.
