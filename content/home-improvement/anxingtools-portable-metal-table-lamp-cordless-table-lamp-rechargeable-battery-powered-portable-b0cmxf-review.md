# Anxingtools Portable Metal Table Lamp, Cordless Table Lamp Review: A $28 Light That Earns Its Spot

Most cordless lamps under $30 feel like cheap plastic toys with a battery shoved inside. The Anxingtools Portable Metal Table Lamp isn't that. It's got actual heft — a full metal body that doesn't flex when you pick it up — and it's earned a solid 4.2-star average across 210 Amazon ratings. Here's the honest breakdown before you spend $27.99.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the lamp itself, a USB-C charging cable, and a basic manual. No wall adapter included — you'll use your phone charger or a USB port. The lamp measures roughly 7 inches tall with a 4-inch base, making it compact enough for nightstands, workshop benches, or patio tables. The metal shade pivots about 90 degrees, so you can aim light where you need it.

## Performance and Build Quality

The metal construction is the headline. It weighs about 1.5 pounds, which gives it stability — it won't tip over when you bump the table. The finish is matte and resists fingerprints decently. As shown in the product image, it's a clean, industrial-minimalist look that fits both garage and living room settings.

Light output: three brightness levels via touch controls on the base, plus a color temperature toggle that shifts between warm (around 3000K) and cool (around 5000K) white. At medium brightness, it's genuinely useful for reading or detailed hobby work. Max brightness is bright enough to light a small desk, though it won't replace a proper work light.

Battery: USB-C charging is a smart choice. Full charge takes about 3 hours and delivers roughly 4-5 hours at max brightness, pushing closer to 10-12 hours at the lowest setting. That's adequate for most uses, but if you need an all-night light source, this isn't it.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Reviewers repeatedly mention the build quality relative to price. One buyer wrote, "Feels way more expensive than it is — solid metal, no creaking." Another noted, "Perfect for camping trips. Charged it once and used it three nights." The portability factor gets real love — it's genuinely easy to grab and move room to room without hunting for an outlet.

## Where It Falls Short

The touch controls are the most common complaint. They're responsive to the point of being finicky — a light brush while reaching for your coffee can cycle through brightness modes or shut the lamp off entirely. There's no physical switch, so you're stuck with the sensitivity.

The lack of brightness memory is annoying. Every power-on defaults to medium, so if you prefer low or high, you're tapping through modes every single time. Minor, but it adds up over months of use.

Battery life at max brightness is the real limitation. If you want this as a primary lamp for long work sessions, you'll be charging it daily. It's better suited as a supplemental or portable light.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Campers and outdoor enthusiasts**: The cordless design and metal build handle rough conditions well.
- **Apartment dwellers with awkward layouts**: No outlet nearby? This solves that problem cleanly.
- **Anyone needing a bedside or accent light**: The warm mode is pleasant and dims low enough for nighttime use.
- **Skip it if** you need all-night operation at full brightness, or if you hate touch controls on principle.

## FAQ

**How long does the battery actually last?**
At max brightness, expect 4-5 hours. Medium runs 7-8. Low stretches to 10-12. Real-world results vary with age and temperature.

**Can you use it while charging?**
Yes — it works plugged in. The USB-C port handles pass-through power fine.

**Is the head adjustable?**
The shade pivots roughly 90 degrees vertically. It doesn't swivel side to side, so you're limited to up/down aiming.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a well-built, genuinely portable lamp that doesn't feel like a disposable gadget. The metal body, USB-C charging, and adjustable color temperature make it a versatile $28 purchase.

**Skip it if** you need max brightness for extended periods or you'll be annoyed by touch controls that respond to accidental brushes.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, honest product with real tradeoffs. Worth the money for its intended use case, with the caveat that battery life and control sensitivity are genuine limitations.
