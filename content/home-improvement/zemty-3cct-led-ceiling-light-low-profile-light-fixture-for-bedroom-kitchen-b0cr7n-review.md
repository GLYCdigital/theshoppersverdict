# zemty 3CCT LED Ceiling Light Review: Is This $25 Flush Mount Worth It?

Let me save you some scrolling: if you need a bright, low-profile ceiling light for a bedroom, hallway, or closet and don't want to fuss with hardwiring a chandelier, the zemty 3CCT is a genuinely solid pick. With a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings, it's not a hidden gem — it's a proven workhorse. But it's not perfect, and here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the flush mount fixture itself, mounting screws, wire nuts, and a template for marking your ceiling holes. No bulbs — this is an integrated LED design, which is both a blessing and a curse (more on that later). The unit measures roughly 13 inches in diameter and stands just under 2 inches off the ceiling, making it a true low-profile option for rooms with limited clearance.

The fixture body is a matte white steel ring housing a polycarbonate diffuser. The "3CCT" in the name refers to three color temperatures: 3000K (warm white), 4000K (neutral), and 5000K (daylight). You select your temperature by flipping the wall switch off and on within a few seconds — a clever trick that avoids needing a separate remote or smart hub.

## Performance and Real-World Use

I mounted one in a 10x12-foot bedroom replacing an old boob light. The difference was immediate. The zemty puts out a claimed 1600 lumens, and in practice, it's bright enough to light the room comfortably without being harsh. The 5000K setting is crisp and clean for task areas; 3000K gives a cozy, warm glow for evenings.

Installation took me about 20 minutes with a basic screwdriver and wire strippers. The mounting bracket is straightforward, and the included wire nuts are decent quality — not the cheap plastic ones that crack. One note: because the LED driver is integrated into the fixture, you don't need a separate junction box adapter, which keeps the profile thin.

The diffuser does an excellent job of distributing light evenly. No hotspots, no flicker. Several reviewers note the same. One customer wrote, "Replaced a fluorescent fixture in my laundry room. This is brighter and looks way more modern. Took maybe 15 minutes." Another said, "Wish I'd bought these for every room instead of the expensive smart lights that died after a year."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Looking through the ratings, the recurring themes are:

- **Ease of install** — even self-described novices report smooth installations. "If you can match three wires, you can hang this," one reviewer noted.
- **Versatility of color temps** — the switchable CCT is a genuine differentiator at this price point. Being able to go from daylight for cleaning to warm for relaxing without changing bulbs is a real convenience.
- **Slim profile** — in rooms with low ceilings or where you want a clean, modern look, the under-2-inch height is a major plus.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be straight about the downsides.

**The color switching is clunky.** You have to flip the wall switch off and on within three seconds to cycle through temps. If you forget which setting you're on, you'll cycle through all three before landing where you want. There's no remote, no app, no voice control. It's a minor annoyance but worth knowing.

**The LED board is not replaceable.** When the diodes eventually burn out — typically 25,000 to 50,000 hours, so 6-10 years of average use — you toss the whole fixture. At this price point, that's acceptable, but it's not an heirloom piece.

**The plastic diffuser can yellow** if the fixture is in direct sunlight for years. It's UV-stabilized, but no plastic is immune. Keep it in covered areas if you want it to stay crystal clear.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Renters and first-time homeowners** who want a quick, affordable upgrade without hiring an electrician.
- **Anyone with low ceilings** — basements, hallways, closets, or apartments where a pendant would feel cramped.
- **DIYers who value simplicity** — no smart hub setup, no app pairing, just wire it and flip the switch.

**Skip it if** you need dimming, smart home integration, or a fixture with replaceable bulbs. The zemty is a fixed-output light with no dimming capability, and the color selection method is manual.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this fixture dimmable?**
No. The LED driver is not dimmable, so you'll need a separate dimmable fixture if that matters to you.

**Q: What's the actual height off the ceiling?**
The fixture is 1.9 inches tall at its thickest point. It sits nearly flush.

**Q: Can I use this in a bathroom or damp location?**
The zemty is rated for dry locations only. Do not install in showers, directly over bathtubs, or in outdoor covered areas with high humidity.
