**If you've priced hardwired motion lights lately, you know the sting.** Electrician fees alone can run $150–$300 per fixture. That's why battery-powered motion sensor lights keep showing up in carts. The FULEN 4-pack undercuts that cost dramatically, but the real question is whether they actually perform. After digging into the specs, 215 Amazon ratings, and what owners say, here's the straight story.

**What's in the box:** You get four complete light units, each with the fixture, mounting bracket, and hardware. The dual mounting approach is smart — each light has a magnetic base that snaps onto the included metal plate, plus traditional screw holes if you want something permanent. No wiring, no transformer, no hub required. Just slide in batteries (not included) and stick or screw them up. Each unit measures roughly 4.5 inches square and about 2 inches deep, so they're compact enough for tight corners without looking like an afterthought.

**Real-world performance:** The 3-mode brightness settings are genuinely useful. Low mode gives off a soft ambient glow that works well for a hallway or closet. Medium is the sweet spot for most outdoor entries. High mode throws roughly 800 lumens of warm white light — enough to light up a standard door landing or back patio clearly. The motion sensor has a 120-degree detection angle with about 10 feet of range. That's fine for doorways, garage man-doors, and stairwells, but it won't cover a wide driveway.

**Battery life is the tradeoff you need to respect.** On medium brightness with standard motion settings, owners report getting 3–4 months from a set of alkaline AAAs. Crank it to high with maximum sensitivity, and that drops to 6–8 weeks. Rechargeable batteries work fine, and if you already own NiMH AAAs, the operating cost effectively disappears. The IP65 waterproof rating is legit for outdoor use — several reviewers reported leaving them through rainstorms and snow without moisture getting inside the lens.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The installation flexibility gets the most love. One reviewer put it plainly: *"Rented apartment, didn't want to drill. The magnetic mount meant I had them up in five minutes and took them down when I moved."* Another noted the light quality: *"Not the harsh blue-white glow you get from cheap units. Warm and even, lights up my whole back step."* The 4-pack value proposition also comes up repeatedly — buyers use one or two outdoors and repurpose the rest for closets, laundry rooms, or garage workbenches.

**Where it falls short:** The motion sensor's 10-foot range is the most common complaint. It's adequate for a standard entryway but frustrating if you expected yard coverage. The sensor also has about a 3-second delay before triggering — noticeable if you walk fast. Battery life at high brightness is unimpressive, and the included adhesive strips are worthless on textured surfaces. Plan on using the screws or buying stronger outdoor tape. There's no dusk-to-dawn mode, so the sensor stays armed during daylight unless you manually switch it off.

**Who should buy these:** Renters who can't hardwire lights but want entryway illumination. Homeowners looking for a quick, no-electrician solution for a garage side door, shed, or porch. Anyone who wants to test motion light placement before committing to hardwired fixtures. Skip them if you need long-range detection (15+ feet), want a permanent hardwired solution, or expect months of battery life on high brightness.

**Frequently asked questions:**

**Q: Do these work with rechargeable batteries?**
A: Yes. Owners report good results with 1.2V NiMH rechargeables. Just note that battery indicator lights may show lower voltage, so don't be startled by a "low" reading that still works fine for weeks.

**Q: Can they withstand winter weather?**
A: The IP65 rating means they handle rain, snow, and freezing temps. The main concern is battery performance in extreme cold — alkaline batteries lose capacity below 32°F. Use lithium batteries if you live somewhere that regularly drops below freezing.

**Q: How do I adjust the sensitivity and duration?**
A: Both are controlled by small switches on the back of the unit. You can adjust detection range (short/long) and light duration (20 seconds / 60 seconds / 90 seconds). Set them before mounting — it's fiddly to adjust after installation.

For the price of a single professional installation visit, you get four functional, weatherproof lights that cover most entry-point scenarios. Just be honest about your detection range needs and plan for battery replacements every couple of months on standard settings. If you can live with those two caveats, this is one of the better value packs in the battery-powered motion light category.
