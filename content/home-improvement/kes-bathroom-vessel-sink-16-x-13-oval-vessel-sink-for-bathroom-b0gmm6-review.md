**The KES 16" x 13" Oval Vessel Sink is a compact statement piece that punches above its weight class — if you understand what you're buying before checkout.**

Most vessel sinks under $100 feel like they were pressed from recycled plastic in a rushed afternoon. The KES is different. It's genuine ceramic, feels solid when you knock on it, and the glaze is applied evenly with no chips, bubbles, or weird drips along the rim. The oval shape is clean and contemporary without veering into trendy territory that'll look dated in three years.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the sink basin itself — nothing else. No drain assembly, no mounting ring, no template. That's standard for vessel sinks, but worth noting so you're not caught off guard. You'll need to source your own drain kit (about $10-15) and a vessel faucet designed for above-counter installation.

The dimensions are genuinely useful: 16 inches long, 13 inches wide, and about 5.5 inches tall. That footprint is ideal for a 24-inch-wide vanity or a powder room where every inch counts. The basin depth is roughly 4 inches, which is shallower than a traditional drop-in sink but typical for vessel designs. The interior curve is smooth and modern, so water drains thoroughly toward the center without pooling.

## Performance and Build Quality

This is where the KES earns its 4.7-star rating. The ceramic is thick — noticeably heavier than the flimsy resin sinks you'll find at big-box stores. It feels substantial when you set it on the counter, and the glossy finish wipes clean with a damp cloth. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: *"I was honestly shocked at the quality for the price. It looks like the $300 sinks I was looking at in the showroom."*

The 16x13 oval shape handles daily use well. Splashback is minimal with a properly positioned faucet, and the smooth interior means soap scum doesn't cling stubbornly. The underside is unglazed, which actually helps the sink sit flush against your countertop with silicone adhesive.

## Where It Falls Short

The lack of an overflow hole is the biggest practical drawback. If you're washing your face or filling the basin for hand-washing clothes, you need to keep an eye on the water level. That's a safety consideration if you have kids or forgetful housemates. One reviewer noted: *"I almost flooded my bathroom the first week because I wasn't used to no overflow. Now I'm just careful."*

Also, the finish is smooth enough that water beads and runs, but it's not stain-proof. Dark liquids like coffee or hair dye should be rinsed off promptly. And while the white finish is classic, KES doesn't offer the matte black or brushed gold options that some competing sinks provide at similar price points.

## Who Should Buy This

This sink is perfect for small bathroom renovations, powder room upgrades, or guest bath remodels where you want a modern aesthetic without the designer markup. It's also a smart choice for DIYers — installation is straightforward enough for a weekend project, and the compact size means you won't have to wrestle with a heavy basin.

Skip it if you're outfitting a master bathroom where you need a deep soaking basin, or if you're a stickler for overflow safety features. Also, if your vanity is less than 18 inches wide, measure carefully — the 16-inch length leaves tight clearance on both sides.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this sink work with a standard 3-hole faucet?**
A: No. Vessel sinks require a vessel faucet designed for above-counter mounting. Standard deck-mounted faucets are too short. Plan for a dedicated vessel faucet with a tall, curved spout.

**Q: Can I install this on any countertop?**
A: Yes, as long as the surface is flat and solid. Granite, quartz, butcher block, and tile all work. You'll secure it with clear silicone adhesive around the base — no mechanical fasteners needed.

**Q: How do I clean this sink without damaging the glaze?**
A: Use mild dish soap and a soft sponge for daily cleaning. Avoid abrasive scrubbers and harsh chemical drain cleaners. The glaze is durable, but it's not bulletproof against steel wool or bleach-heavy products.
