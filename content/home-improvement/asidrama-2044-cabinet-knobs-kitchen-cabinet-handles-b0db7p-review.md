## Where They Fall Short

No product is perfect, and these have two honest limitations:

**Screw length.** The included screws are about 0.6 inches long. This works perfectly for standard 0.75-inch cabinet doors, but if you have overlay doors, shaker-style doors with extra thickness, or you're installing into drawer fronts with backing plates, you'll need to buy longer screws. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

**Matte black is high-maintenance.** If you choose the matte black finish, be aware it shows fingerprints and water splatter more visibly than brushed or satin options. Multiple reviewers mention needing to wipe them down regularly. The brushed nickel and oil-rubbed bronze finishes are more forgiving.
