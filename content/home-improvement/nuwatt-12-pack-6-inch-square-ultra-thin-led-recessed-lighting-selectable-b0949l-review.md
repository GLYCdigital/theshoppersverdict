**NUWATT 12-Pack 6-Inch Square Ultra-Thin LED Recessed Lights: The Honest Breakdown**

If you're swapping out old recessed cans or starting from scratch, the NUWATT 6-inch square kit is the kind of purchase that makes you wonder why anyone still buys round fixtures. With a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. But let's dig past the star count and talk about what you're actually getting — and where you might hit friction.

**What's in the Box**

Twelve complete fixtures, each with the ultra-thin LED panel, a separate driver box (the transformer), and mounting springs. There's no housing can to install — the whole thing is roughly the thickness of a drywall panel, which is the point. You cut a 6.1-inch square hole, push the driver through, wire it to your circuit, and clip the light in via the spring clips. The driver box is IC-rated, meaning you can lay insulation directly against it without fire risk, and it's rated for direct contact with ceiling materials.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The light output is genuinely solid. At 900 lumens per fixture, these punch well above what you'd expect from a 12-watt driver. The square aperture produces a wider, more even spread than round equivalents — the beam pattern feels intentional, not like a circle crammed into a square hole. Color rendering sits at 90 CRI, which means skin tones and paint colors look natural, not washed out.

The selectable color temperature switch is the standout feature. It's a tiny dial on the driver box that lets you choose 2700K (warm), 3000K, 3500K, 4000K, or 5000K (daylight) per fixture. That's huge if you're doing a whole house — you can use warm light in bedrooms and daylight in a garage, all from the same box of twelve. No guesswork, no ordering separate SKUs.

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

The consistency of feedback centers on ease of install and the clean look. One reviewer noted, "I was dreading cutting holes in my ceiling, but the template and springs made it a 20-minute job per light." Another said, "These are brighter than the 6-inch round cans I replaced, and the square look is what makes it. My contractor asked where I got them." The low profile is a recurring theme — in basements with low ceilings, the lack of a can saves precious inches.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the driver box. It's a rectangular unit roughly 4 inches long, and you have to maneuver it through that 6.1-inch hole before clipping the light in place. If your ceiling has thick drywall or you're working in a tight joist bay, this gets fiddly. A few buyers mentioned needing an extra pair of hands or a fish tape to pull the driver through.

Also, there's no quick-connect wiring harness in the box. You're using wire nuts and standard 14/2 or 12/2 cable. That's fine for experienced DIYers, but if you're new to electrical work, budget extra time for the wiring runs, especially if you're daisy-chaining multiple lights on one switch.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The whole-house renovator:** Twelve lights cover a typical 3-bedroom main floor or a large open-concept basement. The selectable color temps mean you can tune each room without buying different kits.
- **The low-ceiling sufferer:** If you've got 7-foot ceilings, these save you the 4-6 inches a can fixture eats.
- **The modern aesthetic hunter:** Square fixtures look intentional. They read as architectural, not builder-grade.

**Skip it if** you need dimmable lights with a sleek flush trim ring (these use a standard trim, not a flush bezel) or if you want a fixture that's truly "set and forget" — the driver box adds a step over all-in-one wafer lights.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these actually dimmable?**
A: Yes, they work with most standard LED-compatible dimmers. Some buyers reported slight flicker at the lowest dim setting with older dimmers — use a modern LED-rated dimmer to be safe.

**Q: Can I install these in a shower or outdoor covered area?**
A: No. They're rated for dry locations only. For damp-rated, look at NUWATT's specific bathroom-rated line.

**Q: Do I need a junction box for each light?**
A: The built-in driver box is the junction box — it's IC-rated and code-compliant, so no separate box is needed.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a modern, bright, energy-efficient lighting upgrade that's DIY-friendly and gives you color temperature flexibility without locking you into one vibe.

**Skip it if** you're looking for a zero-friction install with integrated connectors, or you need damp-rated fixtures.
