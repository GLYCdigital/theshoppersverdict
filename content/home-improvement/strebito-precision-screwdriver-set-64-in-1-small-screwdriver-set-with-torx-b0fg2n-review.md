The smaller Torx bits (T2, T3) are thin by necessity. They work fine for light duty, but apply heavy torque and you risk snapping them. For eyeglass screws and electronics, you're fine. For stubborn, corroded screws, step up to a dedicated Torx driver.

One more thing: there's no bit storage in the handle. Everything lives in the case, so if you lose the case, you lose the bits. Keep it in one place.
