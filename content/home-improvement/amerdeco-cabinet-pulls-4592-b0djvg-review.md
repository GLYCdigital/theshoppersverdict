**The $20 Fix That Makes Your Kitchen Look Custom**

Here's the thing about cabinet hardware: nobody notices it when it's done right, and everybody notices it when it's wrong. The Amerdeco Cabinet Pulls 4592 sits squarely in the "done right" category — a solid, no-drama upgrade that transforms tired cabinets without requiring a contractor or a second mortgage.

**What's Actually in the Box**

You're getting ten pulls, each measuring 5 inches overall length with the standard 3-inch (76mm) center-to-center hole spacing. That's the universal fit that works on 90% of pre-drilled cabinet fronts. Each pull is machined from zinc alloy — not the hollow aluminum you'll find at the big-box store for the same price — and finished in a brushed nickel that reads more matte than shiny. The box includes mounting screws and a paper drilling template. The template's actually useful, which surprised me. Most manufacturers toss in a piece of cardboard with holes in it and call it a day. This one has alignment marks and a straight edge.

**Build Quality: Heavier Than It Looks**

Pick one up and you'll notice it immediately — these have heft. The zinc alloy construction gives them a solid, substantial feel that cheap pulls lack. The brushed finish is consistent across all ten pieces, no blotching or uneven texture. The mounting posts are machine-threaded cleanly; screws thread in smoothly without that gritty, binding feel you get with poorly tapped holes.

Installation is a 15-minute job with a screwdriver and painter's tape. The template lines up correctly, the screws bite into wood without splitting, and the pulls sit flush against the cabinet face. No wobble, no play.

**Where It Falls Short**

The lack of size options is the biggest limitation. You get the 3-inch spacing or nothing. If your cabinets have 4-inch or 5-inch spacing, you're out of luck with this listing. The Phillips screws are functional but unremarkable — swap them for stainless if you're in a humid environment. And while the brushed finish is durable, it's not indestructible. Harsh, abrasive cleaners will leave micro-scratches, so stick to mild soap and water.

**Who Should Buy This**

Skip it if you need non-standard spacing, want a high-polish mirror finish, or expect commercial-grade durability from a $20 ten-pack.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these compatible with soft-close drawers?**
A: Yes — the pull attaches to the drawer front, not the slide mechanism. Any drawer works.

**Q: Will the finish chip or peel over time?**
A: The brushed nickel is applied via a PVD process, which bonds at the molecular level. It won't peel like painted finishes. It can scratch with abrasive cleaners, but normal use is fine.

**Q: How many pulls come in the package?**
A: Ten pulls with ten sets of screws. That covers a standard kitchen with upper and lower cabinets, or two bathroom vanities.
