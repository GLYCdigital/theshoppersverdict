If your hallway currently has a boob light — you know, that dated, frumpy glass dome that every builder installed in the 90s — the FadimiKoo 4-Light Flush Mount is the grown-up replacement you're looking for. At 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, it's clearly hitting the mark for most buyers. But is it right for your space? Here's what you need to know before clicking buy.

## What You're Actually Getting

The box contains the flush mount fixture, a mounting bracket, four glass shades, and hardware. That's it. No bulbs, which is worth noting upfront. The fixture measures roughly 16 inches in diameter with a 4.5-inch drop — slim enough for 8-foot ceilings while still making a visual statement. The matte black finish pairs well with both farmhouse and modern industrial interiors, as shown in the product images.

The standout feature here is the four individual E26 sockets. That means you're not locked into proprietary LED panels that fail as a unit. When a bulb dies in three years, you swap it for $3 instead of replacing the whole fixture. This is smarter engineering than most lights in this price tier.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The metal body feels substantial — not flimsy stamped steel you'd expect at this price point. The matte coating is evenly applied with no drips or thin spots. Buyers consistently mention the weight of the unit as a pleasant surprise. One reviewer noted, *"I expected something cheap but this has real heft to it. The glass shades are thick, not that thin stuff you see at big box stores."*

With four 60-watt equivalent LED bulbs, you're getting solid ambient light — roughly equivalent to a 12-light chandelier in most rooms. The shades diffuse light well, eliminating harsh hotspots while still providing functional brightness for tasks. In a 12x12 bedroom, you won't need additional lamps for everyday use.

Installation is straightforward if you've swapped a ceiling fixture before. The mounting bracket attaches with standard screws, and the fixture wires with conventional color-coded connections. Expect 30-45 minutes if you're reasonably handy. The minor gripe: aligning the glass shades into the mounting tabs takes a few extra minutes on the first go. They seat properly, but there's no obvious "click" telling you they're locked in.

## Where It Falls Short

The bulb situation is the most common complaint — not that bulbs are excluded, but that the listing doesn't make it obvious enough. Read the description carefully. If you're coming from a fixture with integrated LEDs, budget an extra $15 for quality bulbs. Go with 2700K warm white for living spaces; 3000K if you want a slightly crisper feel.

The glass shade alignment is the second gripe. It's not a dealbreaker, but expect to fiddle with three of the four shades to get them seated flush. Once they're in, they stay put.

One more consideration: this fixture isn't dimmer-compatible unless you install dimmable LED bulbs. If you plan to use a dimmer switch, pay attention to the bulb packaging — non-dimmable LEDs will flicker.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I install this on a sloped or vaulted ceiling?**
A: No. The flush mount design requires a flat, level ceiling. For sloped installations, you'd need an adapter or a different fixture.

**Q: What bulb wattage is safe?**
A: The sockets are rated for 60W maximum per bulb. With four sockets, that's 240W total. LED bulbs draw far less, so you'll never approach the limit with standard LEDs.

**Q: Does it work with a dimmer switch?**
A: Yes, but only if you install dimmable LED bulbs. The fixture itself doesn't include dimming technology — that's handled by the bulbs and your switch.
