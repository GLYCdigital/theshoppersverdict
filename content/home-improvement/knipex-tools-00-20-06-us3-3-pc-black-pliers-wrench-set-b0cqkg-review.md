The magic is in the parallel jaw design. Unlike a crescent wrench that applies force at two points, the KNIPEX grips the entire flat surface of the fastener. The push-button adjustment slides smoothly through 15+ detent positions, letting you dial in the exact fit one-handed.

I've used these for everything from brake line fittings to plumbing compression nuts. The 10-inch handles most automotive work; the 6-inch is perfect for tight engine bays and under-sink plumbing. The mechanism feels bank-vault solid — zero play when locked onto a fastener.
