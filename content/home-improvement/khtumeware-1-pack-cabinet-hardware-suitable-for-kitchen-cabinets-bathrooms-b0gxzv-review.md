# The Single-Pull Problem: Is This $6 Cabinet Handle Actually Worth It?

Most cabinet hardware reviews assume you're buying in bulk. This khtumeware listing sells **one pull**. That's either a head-scratcher or a smart way to test the waters before committing to a full kitchen redo. After digging through 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The package contains one cabinet pull, two mounting screws, and nothing else. The handle itself measures approximately 5 inches in total length with a **3-inch (76mm) hole center spacing** — the industry standard. The zinc alloy body has a brushed finish that mimics the look of much pricier hardware from brands like Amerock or Richelieu.

## Build Quality: Where It Surprises

The first thing you notice is the weight. At roughly 3.5 ounces, this isn't a hollow stamped-metal piece that flexes when you grip it. The zinc alloy casting feels dense, and the finish is applied evenly — no drips, no thin spots around the edges where cheaper electroplating often wears through.

One buyer noted: *"I bought one to see if the finish would match my existing fixtures. It matched perfectly and felt more expensive than the $6 price tag."* That's the exact use case this single-pack serves well.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 225 ratings, three themes dominate the positive feedback:

**Finish durability** — Multiple reviewers mention the brushed surface doesn't show fingerprints, a common complaint with polished chrome or brass finishes. The matte texture hides daily smudges well.

**Installation ease** — The 3-inch spacing means you can swap out existing pulls in under two minutes with just a screwdriver. No template needed, no new drilling.

**Value for testing** — Several reviewers specifically bought this to check color matching before ordering larger quantities. One wrote: *"Smart way to test the finish against your cabinets without committing $50."*

## Where It Falls Short

The single-pack format is the obvious limitation. If you're outfitting a kitchen with 12 cabinet fronts, you're looking at $70+ just for pulls — and you'll receive 12 separate Amazon packages, each with its own wasteful packaging. That's both a cost and environmental downside.

The included screws are also standard 1-inch length. If you have thicker shaker-style doors or add a backplate, you'll need to source longer hardware at a hardware store.
