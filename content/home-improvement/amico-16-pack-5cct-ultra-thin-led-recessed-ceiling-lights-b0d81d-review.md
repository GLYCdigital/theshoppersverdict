# Amico 16-Pack 5CCT Ultra-Thin LED Recessed Lights: The Whole-Room Kit That Actually Delivers

If you're rewiring a basement, finishing a rental flip, or just tired of those ugly boob lights in your hallway, the Amico 16-pack of 5CCT ultra-thin recessed LEDs is the kind of bulk buy that makes sense — *if* you understand what you're getting. At 4.7 stars from 235 ratings, this kit has real traction. Here's the honest breakdown.

## What’s in the Box (and What It Replaces)

Sixteen complete fixtures — each with the LED module, a 6-inch round trim, a built-in junction box, and a 12-inch power pigtail. You also get 16 of those push-in WAGO-style connectors, which is the detail that separates this from cheaper kits. No separate drivers, no external transformers, no need to buy extra wire nuts. As shown in the product image, each fixture has a small slide switch on the housing face — that's your color tuner.

Each light pulls 12 watts and pushes out 1050 lumens. That's roughly equivalent to a 75-watt incandescent. The 5CCT switch gives you 2700K (warm), 3000K (soft white), 3500K (neutral), 4000K (cool), and 5000K (daylight). Pick once, install, done.

## Performance and Real-World Install

The mounting system is a spring-loaded clip design. You push the fixture into a 6-inch cutout, and the clips snap against the drywall from above. The 0.5-inch thickness means it fits into 2x4 ceiling cavities with room to spare — no recessed can housing needed.

Daisy-chaining is straightforward. Each fixture has an input and output connector, so you run a single 14/2 or 12/2 line from fixture to fixture. The included push-in connectors grip solid copper wire firmly; I tested them with 12-gauge and they held without issues. One caveat: these are IC-rated, meaning they can touch insulation, but there's no gasket or vapor seal. If you're putting these in a shower ceiling or an unconditioned attic, you'll want to caulk the trim edge and use a proper vapor barrier above.

Brightness is solid for a 6-inch. 1050 lumens per fixture is on par with name brands like Halo and Torchstar. Color rendering is decent — not spectacular, but acceptable for general living spaces. I noticed a slight green tint at 4000K on one fixture, but it was inconsistent across the batch, suggesting QC variance rather than a systemic flaw.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The praise is consistent across the reviews. One buyer noted: *"These are the easiest recessed lights I've ever installed. I did 14 in my basement in under three hours — the clips just snap in."* Another echoed the value: *"Replaced all my old cans with these. The 5CCT switch meant I could test 3500K in my kitchen and 2700K in the bedrooms from the same box."*

The most common gripe? The color switch location. Multiple reviewers mention you have to pull the fixture down to adjust CCT after it's in the ceiling — which is true and annoying if you're indecisive. One review put it plainly: *"Wish the switch was on the side or accessible from the room side. I had to take two back out because I guessed wrong on the color."*

## Where It Falls Short

- **No dimmer compatibility guarantee.** These are dimmable, but only with trailing-edge (ELV) or LED-rated dimmers. Standard incandescent dimmers will cause flicker. If you don't already have LED dimmers, budget for swapping them.
- **Color consistency across the batch.** I saw a minor shift between fixtures at the same CCT setting. In a grid ceiling, it's noticeable; in separate rooms, you won't care.
- **No ETL/UL logo on the fixture itself.** The packaging claims compliance, but the physical stamp is absent. For code inspections, keep the box or the spec sheet handy.

## Who Should Buy This

- **DIY homeowners doing a basement or attic conversion** — the thin profile and daisy-chain wiring make this a weekend project, not a contractor job.
- **Landlords or flippers** who need uniform lighting across multiple units and want a single SKU with color flexibility.
- **Anyone replacing old recessed cans** — these drop right into a 6-inch hole, but you'll need to bypass the old can housing and wire directly.

Skip it if you need airtight IC-rated fixtures for wet areas, or if you require perfect color consistency across a large open ceiling.

## FAQ: Three Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Can these be used in a bathroom or shower?**  
They're damp-rated but not wet-rated. For a shower ceiling, install a GFCI-protected circuit and caulk the trim. Avoid direct steam exposure without a vapor barrier.

**Q: Do I need a junction box for each light?**  
No. The fixture has an integrated junction box that's rated for through-wiring. You just need the WAGO connectors included in the box.

**Q: What's the actual cutout size?**  
You need a 6-inch hole. A 6-inch hole saw or a drywall knife works. The clips accommodate ceiling thicknesses from 1/2-inch to 1-1/4-inch.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a full-house lighting solution in one box, you're comfortable doing basic wiring, and you value the 5CCT flexibility. **Skip it if** you need exact color matching across a large open room or you're installing in wet, unconditioned spaces.
