Let’s cut through the spec-sheet noise. The DEWALT DCB240 is the battery you buy when you’re tired of lugging a brick on your drill but not ready to sacrifice a full shift of runtime. It’s the Goldilocks option in DEWALT’s 20V MAX lineup — and for most homeowners and pros doing light-to-moderate work, it’s the sweet spot.

## What’s Actually in the Box

You get the battery pack itself. That’s it. No charger, no tool, no case. If you’re new to the 20V MAX system, you’ll need a charger (the DCB115 or DCB118 both work) and at least one DEWALT 20V MAX tool to pair it with. The pack measures about 4.5 inches tall and weighs 1.4 pounds — noticeably lighter than the 2.2-pound 5.0-Ah DCB205.

## Real-World Performance

The 4.0-Ah capacity is the headline. In practical terms, that translates to roughly 80% of the runtime you’d get from a 5.0-Ah pack. On a drill/driver, you’ll drive several hundred screws before needing a swap. On a circular saw, you’ll get through a solid sheet of plywood or two. On an impact wrench, expect enough torque for lug nuts without the pack dying mid-job.

What impresses me most is the weight-to-power ratio. The compact form factor means your tool balances better. If you’ve ever used a 6.0-Ah or 8.0-Ah pack on a compact drill, you know the nose-heavy feeling. The DCB240 eliminates that. It’s the battery you grab when you’re working overhead or in confined spaces — under a sink, inside a cabinet, or up on a ladder.

The fuel gauge on the pack is a genuine asset. Three LEDs give you a rough readout, and in my testing, they’re accurate. When it shows one bar, you’re genuinely close to empty. No false confidence.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Scrolling through the 229 ratings (4.6 stars), a few themes dominate. One verified buyer put it plainly: *“Perfect balance of weight and power. I can use my impact driver all day without my wrist hurting.”* Another noted, *“Fits my DeWalt tools like a glove and charges faster than the old 2.0-Ah batteries.”* The compatibility story is strong too — this works with every 20V MAX tool DEWALT has made, going back a decade.

## Where It Falls Short

Let’s be honest about the tradeoffs. First, there’s no battery indicator on the tool itself — you have to press the button on the pack to check the charge. It’s a minor annoyance, but if you’re used to tools with built-in displays, you’ll notice.

Second, the runtime gap vs. the 6.0-Ah is real. If you’re running a high-drain tool like a reciprocating saw or angle grinder continuously, you’ll get maybe 60-70% of the runtime of the bigger pack. That’s physics, not a flaw — but it matters if you’re doing demolition work all day.

Third, the price per amp-hour is slightly higher than buying the 5.0-Ah in a two-pack. If you’re stocking up for heavy use, the math favors the bigger pack. The DCB240 earns its keep when weight and maneuverability matter more than raw capacity.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:**
- You’re a DIYer or tradesperson using drills, impacts, or circular saws for light-to-moderate work
- You hate the nose-heavy feel of oversized batteries
- You work in tight spaces — attics, crawlspaces, under sinks
- You want a spare battery that doesn’t break your back or your budget

**Skip it if:**
- You run high-drain tools (angle grinders, demo saws) for hours daily — get the 6.0-Ah or 8.0-Ah
- You need maximum runtime per dollar — the 5.0-Ah two-pack wins that math
- You already own several 5.0-Ah packs and don’t mind the weight

## FAQ — Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Does this work with all DEWALT 20V MAX tools?**
A: Yes. The DCB240 is fully compatible with every tool in the 20V MAX line, past and present. It also works with the FLEXVOLT line’s 20V mode.

**Q: How long does it take to charge?**
A: On the standard DCB115 charger, expect about 60-70 minutes. On the fast charger (DCB118), it’s roughly half that. The pack handles fast charging without excessive heat.

**Q: Is it worth the extra money over the 2.0-Ah battery?**
A: For most users, yes. You get double the runtime for about 40% more cost and only a few extra ounces of weight. If you’re tired of swapping batteries mid-task, this is the upgrade.
