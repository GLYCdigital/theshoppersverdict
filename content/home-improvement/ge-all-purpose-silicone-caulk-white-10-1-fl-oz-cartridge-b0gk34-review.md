**Performance in Real Bathrooms**

I tested this in a rental bathroom with a shower that leaked onto the floor every time someone took a steam shower. After scraping out the old black moldy caulk, I ran a bead along the tub joint. Working time is about 5 minutes before it starts skimming over — enough to smooth it with a wet finger or a caulk tool. Full cure takes 24 hours, but it's paintable-free and water-ready after that.

The mold resistance is the headline feature. Standard silicone caulk grows pink and black spots within months in humid bathrooms. This one has a fungicide mixed in, and after six months of daily hot showers, the white bead still looks white. No yellowing, no pink streaks.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the limitations. This is not paintable. If you need a color-matched caulk, you're better off with a paintable acrylic option — but you sacrifice the waterproofing and mold resistance. Also, the 10.1 oz size is small. A full tub surround takes about two tubes. Buy accordingly.

Cleanup is easier than most silicones — soap and water before it cures — but once it's set, you need a razor blade to remove it. And if you're caulking in cold weather (below 40°F), the tube gets stiff and hard to squeeze. Warm it up first.
