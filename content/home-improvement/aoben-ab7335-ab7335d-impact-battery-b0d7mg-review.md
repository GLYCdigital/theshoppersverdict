Let me be blunt: the AOBEN AB7335 & AB7335D combo isn't a Milwaukee or DeWalt, and it doesn't pretend to be. But at this price point, it punches well above its weight class for homeowners, DIYers, and weekend warriors. Here's what 220 Amazon reviewers at 4.4 stars are actually saying — and what they're glossing over.

**What's in the box**

You're getting the AB7335 impact driver, the AB7335D battery (a 2.0Ah unit), a second battery, a fast charger, and a canvas storage bag. The kit includes two 1/4-inch hex bits — a Phillips #2 and a flathead — which is enough to get you started but not enough to finish most jobs. The tool itself weighs in at a manageable 2.8 pounds with the battery attached, which matters when you're driving screws overhead or into studs all afternoon.

**Real-world performance**

The brushless motor is the headline here. It delivers up to 2,900 RPM and 3,200 IPM (impacts per minute), which translates to genuine torque when you need it. I've seen reviewers mention driving 3-inch deck screws into pressure-treated lumber without pre-drilling — that's the kind of real-world test that separates a decent impact driver from a toy.

One reviewer put it simply: *"This thing has way more power than I expected for the price. It drove 3-inch screws into old oak without flinching."* Another noted the variable-speed trigger gives you enough control for delicate work like cabinet hinges, then full send when you need it.

The two-battery setup is genuinely useful. Each 2.0Ah battery gives you roughly 45-60 minutes of continuous driving, depending on the material. The included charger tops one up in about 90 minutes. If you're doing a weekend project — building a deck, assembling furniture, hanging drywall — you won't be waiting on power.

**What buyers consistently praise**

- **Power-to-price ratio.** Over and over, reviewers mention being surprised by the torque.
- **Ergonomics.** The rubberized grip and balanced weight distribution get consistent mentions. One reviewer said, *"Comfortable to hold for hours. My hand doesn't cramp like it did with my old drill."*
- **LED light placement.** It's positioned near the chuck and actually illuminates the work area. Small thing, but it matters when you're working under a sink or inside a cabinet.

**Where it falls short**

No tool is perfect, and the AOBEN has a few honest weaknesses:

- **1/4-inch hex only.** This is strictly an impact driver, not a drill/driver combo. If you need to drill holes with twist bits or hole saws, you'll need a separate drill. Several reviewers noted this disappointment after assuming it was a full drill kit.
- **Battery gauge is basic.** The 3-LED indicator gives you a rough idea — full, half, low — but there's no precise percentage readout. You'll get caught out occasionally if you're not paying attention.
- **Bag quality.** The included canvas bag is serviceable but thin. It won't survive heavy job-site abuse. Fine for home storage, not for daily commercial use.
- **No belt clip included.** Minor, but notable if you're used to clipping your driver on a tool belt.

**Who should buy this**

This kit is ideal for:
- **Homeowners** tackling furniture assembly, curtain rods, shelving, and light repair work
- **DIY enthusiasts** building decks, fences, or backyard projects
- **First-time tool buyers** who want a capable impact driver without dropping $200+
- **Apartment dwellers** who need something compact they can store easily

Skip it if you're a professional contractor hammering on it 40 hours a week, or if you need drill/driver versatility in one tool.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this come with a drill chuck for standard drill bits?**
A: No. This is an impact driver only, accepting 1/4-inch hex bits. For drilling holes, you'll need a separate drill or a hex-to-round shank adapter (which you'd have to buy separately).

**Q: How long do the batteries actually last?**
A: Real-world usage suggests 45-60 minutes of continuous driving per battery. Light work like screws into drywall or softwood will stretch that; heavy deck screws into hardwood will cut it down.

**Q: Is this compatible with other brands' batteries?**
A: No. The AB7335D batteries are proprietary to AOBEN's system. You're locked into their ecosystem for replacements, though the two included batteries should last years for typical home use.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need a reliable, powerful impact driver for home projects and want a two-battery kit at a budget price. The brushless motor, comfortable ergonomics, and genuine torque make this an outstanding value.

**Skip it if:** You're a professional needing daily job-site reliability, you need drill capability in the same tool, or you're invested in another battery platform and want tool-battery interchangeability.
