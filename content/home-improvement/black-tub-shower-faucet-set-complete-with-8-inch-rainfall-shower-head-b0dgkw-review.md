# Black Tub Shower Faucet Set Complete with 8-Inch Rainfall Shower Head — Is It Worth the Upgrade?

A full tub-and-shower faucet replacement is one of those projects that feels intimidating until you realize it's mostly about choosing the right valve. This black tub shower faucet set with an 8-inch rainfall head has been sitting at 4.2 stars across 210 ratings, which tells me it's solid but not flawless. Here's what you need to know before you commit to tearing out your old fixture.

## What's Actually in the Box

This is a complete setup, not a partial kit. You get the pressure-balanced valve body, the tub spout, the diverter, the 8-inch round rainfall shower head, a handheld sprayer with a 59-inch hose, and all mounting hardware. The trim pieces are all matte black — and I mean all of them, including the escutcheon plates and the shower arm flange. No mismatched chrome accents to ruin the look.

The valve is a standard 1/2-inch NPT connection, so it'll mate with most existing copper or PEX supply lines. The tub spout is 6 inches long with a slip-fit connection, which is the industry standard for tub spouts that thread onto a 1/2-inch pipe stub.

## Real-World Performance and Build Quality

The standout feature here is the dual-function setup. The 8-inch rainfall head delivers a wide, gentle spray that covers your shoulders without that needle-like sting you get from cheaper fixed heads. The handheld sprayer — which pulls out from the diverter, not the tub spout — is genuinely useful for rinsing the tub walls or washing pets. Most budget sets force you to choose between rainfall OR handheld; this one gives you both for roughly the same price as a single-function competitor.

The pressure-balanced valve is the hidden MVP. If you've ever been scalded when someone flushes a toilet while you're showering, you'll appreciate this feature. It holds temperature within a reasonable range during pressure fluctuations. That's not marketing fluff — that's a safety feature that matters in older homes with undersized supply lines.

Homeowners who've installed this unit consistently mention the finish. One buyer noted, "The matte black doesn't show hard water spots like my old chrome fixture did — six months in and it still looks new." Another mentioned the weight: "The shower head feels substantial, not like the flimsy plastic ones at the big box store."

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the installation constraints. This set requires a **3-hole, 6-inch center-to-center configuration**. If you're replacing an existing fixture that uses an 8-inch or 12-inch rough-in spacing — common in older homes — you'll need to buy an adapter plate or do drywall work. That's an extra $15–$30 and some fiddly alignment work that the product photos don't advertise clearly.

A handful of reviewers also flagged the diverter knob as stiff out of the box. It loosens up after a few dozen uses, but if you're installing this for elderly family members with limited grip strength, budget for some initial frustration. One reviewer put it plainly: "The knob took about two weeks of daily use before it turned smoothly. Not a dealbreaker, but unexpected."

The handheld sprayer's hose is 59 inches, which is adequate but not generous. If your tub is extra deep, you might find yourself stretching to reach the far end.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're doing a full bathroom remodel and want a cohesive matte black look, you need both rainfall and handheld functions without paying premium brand prices, or you're replacing a fixture with matching 6-inch center spacing.

**Skip it if:** You're working with non-standard rough-in spacing and don't want to deal with adapters, or you need a super-smooth diverter for someone with hand strength issues.

## FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Does this come with a rough-in valve or just trim?**
A: It includes the complete pressure-balanced valve body, not just trim. You get everything needed for a full replacement, minus the supply lines.

**Q: Will the matte black finish scratch during installation?**
A: Use a strap wrench (not pliers) on the chrome parts during install. The finish is baked-on and durable, but no finish survives metal-on-metal tool contact.

**Q: Can I use this with a tankless water heater?**
A: Yes — the valve handles both tank and tankless systems. Just verify your flow rate since rainfall heads need adequate GPM to feel satisfying.
