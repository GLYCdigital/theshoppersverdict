Most landscape lighting kits under $100 are plastic junk that cracks by the second winter. The EDISHINE HGSL35AB isn't that. It's a six-pack of die-cast aluminum well lights that actually looks like someone thought about the installation process — and after 235 ratings averaging 4.7 stars, buyers agree more often than not.

**What's in the box**

You get six complete fixtures with 12-inch ground stakes pre-attached, each with a 3.3-foot lead wire. The full set also includes a 200W low-voltage transformer, a 100-foot 16-2 gauge cable, and connectors. That's a complete system — no hidden trips to the hardware store for wire nuts or a timer you forgot existed.

As shown in the product image, these are compact well lights with a low-profile dome that sits close to the ground. The housing is powder-coated die-cast aluminum, not stamped sheet metal. That matters. Stamped steel corrodes where the coating scratches; die-cast holds up.

**Real-world performance**

Let's talk about the light itself. Each fixture houses a 3W warm-white LED rated at 2700K. That's the color temperature you want for landscaping — it makes greenery look richer without the sterile blue tone you get from cheaper 5000K units. The beam spread covers roughly a 6-foot diameter circle from ground level, which suits pathways, flower beds, and uplighting small trees.

The 15-degree adjustable angle is the underrated feature here. On many well lights, "adjustable" means you need a screwdriver and patience. EDISHINE uses a tool-free swivel that clicks into position. Angle the beam toward a focal point or straight up for a wash effect — it takes seconds.

Buyers consistently highlight the build quality. One reviewer noted, *"These feel like they cost double the price. Heavy, solid, and the powder coat is thick — not that flaky paint you see on budget sets."* Another mentioned the transformer: *"Finally a kit where the transformer is actually rated high enough to run all six lights without tripping."*

**Where it falls short**

Two honest tradeoffs. First, voltage drop is real if you push the system. The 100-foot cable is generous, but if you run all six fixtures off one long line past 80 feet, the last two lights will run noticeably dimmer. Keep runs short and balanced.

Second, there's no photocell or timer. The included transformer has a manual on/off switch only. For dusk-to-dawn automation, you'll need a smart plug rated for outdoor use or an inline timer. That's an extra $15–20 and a minor annoyance for a kit at this price point.

A few buyers also mentioned the stakes could be longer for soft, sandy soil — 12 inches holds fine in packed earth but might wobble in loose mulch.

**Who should buy this**

- **First-time landscape lighters** who want a complete system without researching transformers, gauges, and connectors separately.
- **Homeowners with medium yards** (up to ~1,500 square feet of frontage) who need 4–6 fixtures for pathways or focal points.
- **Anyone who's replaced cheap plastic lights twice** and wants something that survives actual weather.

Skip it if you're lighting a massive property needing 20+ fixtures — you'll want commercial-grade gear and a bigger transformer.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I connect these to my existing low-voltage system?**
Yes. The fixtures use standard two-wire connections. Cut the included cable, strip, and connect to your existing run. Just verify total wattage stays under your transformer's rating.

**Q: Do the LEDs burn out or can they be replaced?**
The LED boards are integrated, not replaceable. However, at 3W each, they're rated for roughly 25,000 hours — that's 10+ years of nightly use in most climates.

**Q: How bright are these compared to halogen?**
Each fixture outputs about 300 lumens — comparable to a 20W halogen but with a much more even spread. You'll get clean pools of light rather than hot spots.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a weatherproof, complete six-light system that installs in an afternoon and looks like it cost three times the price.

**Skip it if:** You need automation out of the box or plan to run fixtures more than 80 feet from the transformer.

The EDISHINE HGSL35AB earns its 4.7-star rating. It's not perfect — the missing photocell and voltage drop on long runs keep it from a perfect score — but for the money, it's the best-balanced low-voltage kit most homeowners will find.
