If you're remodeling a tub and shower, the Moen Posi-Temp Pressure Balancing Eco-Performance trim kit is the kind of purchase that doesn't feel exciting but saves your skin — literally. This is the part that keeps you from getting scalded when someone flushes a toilet mid-shower. With 220 ratings averaging 4.4 stars, it's not perfect, but it's consistently solid. Here's what you're actually getting before you hand over your card.

## What's in the box (and what's not)

This is a **trim kit only**. You get the chrome handle, the trim plate/escutcheon, the showerhead, and the shower arm with flange. What you do NOT get is the valve body — the actual pressure-balancing mechanism that mounts inside your wall. You'll need the Moen Posi-Temp valve (model R2200 or equivalent) separately.

This is the #1 source of confusion among buyers. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I didn't realize this was just the trim. The valve is sold separately. Read the description carefully before ordering."* The listing does state it, but it's buried. Budget an extra $40–60 for the valve, or check if your contractor already has one in the wall.

## Performance and build quality

The Posi-Temp mechanism is the star here. It's a pressure-balancing valve that maintains water temperature within about ±3°F when incoming water pressure fluctuates. Flush a toilet, start the washing machine, or have someone run the kitchen sink — the water doesn't jump to scalding or freezing. That's not a luxury feature; that's a safety feature, especially if you have kids or elderly family members.

The **Eco-Performance** designation means the showerhead is flow-limited to 1.75 gallons per minute, down from the 2.5 GPM standard. That's a 30% reduction in hot water usage, which matters for both your water heater's capacity and your utility bill. The spray pattern is decent — 16 jets with a decent spread — but it's not a rainfall experience. You'll feel the difference if you're coming from an old 2.5 GPM head.

Build quality is typical Moen: solid metal handle, chrome finish that holds up, and a trim plate that covers rough openings up to 6 inches. The handle operates smoothly with a positive detent at full hot. One buyer noted: *"Installation was straightforward, took about 20 minutes once the valve was in place. The chrome still looks new after a year."*

## What buyers consistently praise

- **Temperature stability** is the most-cited win. *"Our old valve would turn ice cold when the washer filled. This one holds steady. Worth every penny."*
- **Water savings** without a dramatic drop in pressure. *"I expected a wimpy shower, but honestly it's fine. Can't tell a huge difference from our old head."*
- **Moen warranty** — limited lifetime against leaks and finish defects. You won't need it often, but it's there.

## Where it falls short

The flow limiter is the most common complaint. If you have low water pressure at your home already, 1.75 GPM can feel like a drizzle. There's no easy way to remove the limiter — it's built into the showerhead — so you're stuck with it unless you swap heads.

The short handle travel is a minor annoyance. The range between "comfortable warm" and "too hot" is a small arc, so dialing in your ideal temperature takes a few showers of adjustment. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

And again — the separate valve purchase. It's not a flaw in the product itself, but it's a cost surprise that trips up first-time buyers.

## Who should buy this

- **DIY remodelers** replacing an old tub/shower setup — you'll appreciate the straightforward installation and the safety valve.
- **Families with kids** — the pressure balance feature is non-negotiable when little ones are in the tub.
- **Anyone on a water heater** who wants to stretch hot water during back-to-back showers.

**Skip it** if you're on a well pump with naturally low pressure, or if you're a "high-flow showerhead" person who needs a drenching spray.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this include the valve that goes inside the wall?**
No. You need the Moen Posi-Temp valve body (R2200) separately. Confirm what your contractor is installing before you buy this trim.

**Q: Will this work with my existing Moen valve?**
Only if it's a Posi-Temp (model numbers starting with R22 or T22). It won't fit older Moentrol or Standard valves. Check the cartridge inside your current valve before ordering.

**Q: Is the showerhead really limited to 1.75 GPM?**
Yes, that's the Eco-Performance spec. It's compliant with California and other low-flow regulations. If you want higher flow, look for Moen's non-Eco models.
