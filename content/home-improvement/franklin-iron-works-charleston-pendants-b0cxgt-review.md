## The Real Story on These Industrial Pendants

Most pendant lights in this price range are either flimsy stamped metal or overpriced designer knockoffs. The Franklin Iron Works Charleston Pendants sit somewhere in that sweet spot—substantial enough to feel permanent, but not so expensive that you'll wince when you look at your receipt.

**What actually arrives at your door:** Three complete pendant fixtures, each with a steel canopy, adjustable cord, and clear glass shade. The hardware kit includes mounting brackets and screws. You'll need to supply your own bulbs—these are E26 base, so standard Edison bulbs work perfectly. The adjustable cords let you trim them to your preferred hanging length, though you'll need wire cutters and basic electrical knowledge for installation.

---

## Build Quality That Justifies the Price

The first thing you notice pulling one of these out of the box is the weight. This isn't hollow, thin-gauge metal. The Charleston pendants use solid steel with a matte black finish that has a slight texture to it—fingerprints don't show, which matters more than you'd think in a kitchen setting.

The glass shades attach with a simple friction fit and a small set screw. They're not sealed, which means they're easy to remove for cleaning. That's a design choice with tradeoffs: you get effortless bulb access, but you also get an open bottom that collects dust and the occasional dead fly. One buyer noted, *"Love the look, hate that I can see dust building up inside the glass after a few weeks. Easy to clean, just annoying."*

**Installation notes:** If you're replacing existing fixtures, budget about 45 minutes per pendant. The mounting bracket is standard and fits most junction boxes. The cord adjustment mechanism is straightforward—loosen the set screw, pull the cord to length, tighten. No proprietary tools required.

---

## Light Quality and Real-World Performance

With three pendants spaced evenly over a kitchen island, you get solid, even illumination. The clear glass shades mean the bulb is fully visible, so your choice of bulb dramatically affects the look. Warm Edison bulbs at 2700K look fantastic. Daylight-balanced LEDs look harsh and industrial in a bad way.

A few buyers mentioned these are brighter than expected with standard bulbs. One review said, *"I was worried three of these wouldn't be enough light for a 6-foot island. Turns out I can cook comfortably with just these and the under-cabinet lights off."*

The finish has held up well in real-world use—no chipping or peeling reported even in humid kitchen environments. The cord is substantial, not that flimsy plastic-coated stuff on cheaper fixtures.

---

## Where These Fall Short

**The open-bottom design is the biggest practical issue.** You'll see every speck of dust on the bulbs and inside the glass. If you're a "set it and forget it" person, this will bother you.

**Height requirements are real.** The instructions specify a 36-inch minimum ceiling height. That's obvious for any pendant, but the fixed 12-inch rod on the canopy means you can't install these flush against the ceiling if your junction box is positioned oddly.

**No dimmer included.** These are basic fixtures—you'll need to add your own dimmer switch and compatible dimmable bulbs if you want adjustable brightness.

---

## Who Should Buy These Pendants

- **Kitchen renovators** who want industrial style without paying boutique lighting prices
- **Homeowners with 8-foot or taller ceilings** who can properly space these over islands or bars
- **DIY installers** comfortable with basic electrical work—these are straightforward to mount

**Skip these if** you have a low ceiling, want sealed fixtures to minimize dust, or need a plug-in option (these are hardwired only).

## FAQ

**Q: Can these be installed on a sloped ceiling?**
A: The canopy is designed for flat ceilings. On a sloped ceiling, the fixture won't sit flush and will look noticeably off. Use a sloped-ceiling adapter kit if you must.

**Q: What's the maximum hanging length?**
A: The cord extends to roughly 12 feet, though realistically you'll trim it to your space. The canopy has an adjustable cord lock that lets you fine-tune the length.

**Q: Do I need to buy special bulbs?**
A: No—standard E26 base bulbs work. Edison-style filament bulbs look best with the clear glass shades. Just make sure they're dimmable if you plan to add a dimmer switch.
