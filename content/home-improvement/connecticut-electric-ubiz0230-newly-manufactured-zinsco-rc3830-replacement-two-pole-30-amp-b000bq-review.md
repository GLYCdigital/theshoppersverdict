## Where It Falls Short

Honestly? Price. This costs significantly more than a comparable Murray or Square D breaker for a modern panel. You're paying a premium because there's no competition — Connecticut Electric holds the tooling. That's the reality of keeping an obsolete system alive.

Second, this is a band-aid, not a cure. The notorious Zinsco failure mode — bus bar arcing and scorching — lives in the panel itself, not the breaker. If your panel shows any signs of heat damage, melted insulation, or burnt bus stabs, no replacement breaker fixes that. You need a panel upgrade, and this breaker is just delaying the inevitable.
