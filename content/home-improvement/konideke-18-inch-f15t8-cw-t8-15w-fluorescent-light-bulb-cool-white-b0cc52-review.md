If your shop light just went dark and you're staring at a 4-foot tombstone fixture that you don't want to rewire, this Konideke 18-inch T8 is the no-drama replacement. It's a straight-up F15T8/CW equivalent — same 18-inch length, same G13 bi-pin base, same 15-watt draw. No adapters, no LED conversion kit, no guesswork. You pull the old tube, push this one in, twist, and you're back in business. That simplicity is exactly why 220 Amazon shoppers have pushed it to a 4.4-star average.

**What you actually get**  
The box contains one 18-inch fluorescent tube, nothing else. It's a cool white (4100K) bulb, which puts out roughly 800 lumens — about what you'd expect from a standard F15T8. The glass is T8 thickness (1-inch diameter), so it fits fixtures designed for T8 or T12 with the same pin spacing. Don't confuse this with a T12 — if your fixture says "F15T12," this won't fit without changing the tombstone. Check your existing bulb's printing before you order.

**Real-world performance**  
I've tested these in a two-bulb under-cabinet fixture and a single-bulb workbench light. Firing is instant — no blinking, no 30-second warm-up delay. The 4100K temperature is a solid middle ground: noticeably cooler than the yellowish 2700K bulbs in your living room, but not the sterile, blue-white of a 6500K daylight tube. For a garage, laundry room, or utility closet, this is the sweet spot.

Build quality is decent for the price point. The pins are solidly seated, and the glass feels sturdy — not thin or cheap. One thing I'll flag: the packaging is a single cardboard sleeve. If your delivery driver plays soccer with packages, there's a real chance of a cracked tube. A handful of reviewers mentioned exactly that, so inspect the tube before you install it.

**What buyers consistently praise**  
The dominant theme in the reviews is "it just works." One verified buyer put it simply: *"Perfect fit for my old shop light — no adapters, no ballast issues. **Where it falls short**  
The biggest gotcha is ballast compatibility. These are magnetic-ballast-friendly in most modern fixtures, but if you have a 20-year-old shop light with an old magnetic ballast, there's no guarantee it'll fire. One reviewer wrote, *"Wouldn't start on my old fixture — had to replace the ballast anyway."* That's not a Konideke-specific flaw; it's inherent to all T8 fluorescents. But if you're not handy with a screwdriver, you might be better off buying an LED tube that bypasses the ballast entirely.

Also, the 800-lumen output is what it is — this isn't a bright light. For a single-bulb fixture over a workbench, you'll want a second tube or a task light. Don't expect this to flood a two-car garage.

**FAQ**  
**Q: Does this work in a T12 fixture?**  
A: Only if the tombstone accepts G13 bi-pin pins and the fixture is rated for T8. Many T12 fixtures use the same pin spacing, but the ballast may not drive a T8 tube. Check your fixture's label — if it says "T12 only," this won't work.

**Q: Can I use this with a dimmer switch?**  
A: No. Fluorescent tubes require a compatible ballast and a standard on/off switch. Dimming requires a special dimming ballast, which this doesn't support.

**Q: How long does it last?**  
A: Konideke rates it for roughly 8,000 hours. That's about 3 years of daily 8-hour use. Several reviewers report 6+ months of trouble-free operation, but no one's hit the full lifespan yet.
