Let's cut through the lighting aisle noise. The E ENERGETIC LIGHTING G25 is a compact work light that's been quietly racking up 4.7 stars from 235 buyers — and after looking at what it actually does, the praise isn't hype. This is a seriously capable little light that punches well above its size class.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the G25 light head, a sturdy foldable tripod that extends to about 5 feet, a USB-C charging cable, and a basic carry pouch. No batteries included — it's got a built-in 5,000mAh pack, so you're charging it like your phone. The light head itself measures roughly 4 inches across and weighs about a pound, which matters when you're hauling gear up a ladder.

**Performance: the real story**

Crank it to high and you're getting 2,000 lumens from a COB LED array that throws a wide, even flood — no harsh hotspot in the middle, which is exactly what you want for under-cabinet work or engine bays. The color temperature sits at a neutral 5000K, so colors look true and your eyes don't fatigue after hours of detail work.

The swivel base is the standout feature. It tilts 180 degrees and locks with a firm detent. I've seen cheaper lights sag mid-project; this one holds position even when pointed straight down. The tripod legs spread wide enough that a bump won't topple it, and the rubber feet grip concrete and plywood alike.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The durability story keeps coming up in reviews. One buyer wrote, "Dropped it off a 6-foot ladder onto concrete — didn't even flicker." Another noted, "Used it in a light rain to fix a fence at night. No issues at all." The IP65 rating backs that up — it's sealed against dust and can handle water spray from any direction.

Battery life is the other recurring highlight. At medium brightness (around 800 lumens), you're getting 4-5 hours, which covers most evening projects. The USB-C charging pulls a full charge in about 2.5 hours, and you can run it while plugged into a power bank if you're in a pinch.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be straight: at max output, you're looking at roughly 90 minutes of runtime. That's fine for quick jobs but not for all-nighters. Bring a spare power source or drop to medium.

The lack of a magnetic base is a genuine miss. For a light this portable, slapping it on a metal door or tool chest would be natural — instead you're limited to the tripod or finding a flat surface. It's not a dealbreaker, but it limits some positioning options.

**Who should buy this**

- **DIY homeowners** doing garage projects, attic work, or under-sink plumbing — the compact size and flood pattern are ideal for tight spaces
- **Electricians and HVAC techs** who need a reliable, water-resistant light that survives being thrown in a service van
- **Campers and emergency kit builders** who want a bright, rechargeable light that doesn't rely on disposable batteries

Skip it if you need a magnetic mount or require all-day runtime at full brightness — look at larger 10,000-lumen units with swappable batteries instead.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can this replace a corded work light?**
For most home and light professional use, yes. The 2,000-lumen output rivals many plug-in units. For large open spaces like full-room painting, you'll want something bigger.

**Q: How long does the battery last on a single charge?**
Roughly 90 minutes at max, 4-5 hours at medium, and up to 10 hours at low. The built-in battery indicator gives you a clear read on remaining life.

**Q: Does it come with a wall charger?**
No — just a USB-C cable. You'll need your own wall adapter, which is a minor but annoying omission at this price point.

**The Verdict**

The E ENERGETIC LIGHTING G25 earns its 4.7-star rating through brute-force usefulness. It's bright, tough, and portable in a way that makes it the light you actually grab — which is the whole point.

**Buy it if** you want a rugged, pocketable work light that outperforms its size and shrugs off water and drops.

**Skip it if** you need magnetic mounting or all-day max brightness. Otherwise, this is one of the best compact work lights you can buy right now.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a small deduction for the missing magnetic base and short max-runtime, but otherwise a near-flawless performer.
