# Kwikset CP Security Tylo Knob Combo Pack Review: A Budget Deadbolt That Doesn't Cut Corners

You're standing in the door hardware aisle wondering if you can trust a $40 lockset to keep your family safe. Here's the honest answer: the Kwikset CP Security Tylo Knob Combo Pack punches well above its price point, but it's not without quirks. With 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, this is one of the most consistently recommended entry-level security sets on Amazon — and for good reason.

## What's Actually in the Box

This combo pack includes two keyed entry knobs (for exterior doors) and one single-cylinder deadbolt. You get all the mounting hardware, two keys per lock, and the strike plates. The finish is a smooth satin nickel that resists fingerprints reasonably well, though it's not the premium brushed texture you'll find on Kwikset's higher-end Signature series.

**Key specs:** The deadbolt is ANSI/BHMA Grade 2 rated — that's the sweet spot for residential security. The knobs are Grade 3, which is standard for entry-level sets. The latch has a 2-3/8" or 2-3/4" adjustable backset, so it fits most standard doors without modification.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Kwikset's SmartKey technology is the headline feature here. If you've ever paid a locksmith $80 to re-key a lock after moving into a new home, this feature alone justifies the purchase. You insert the current key, turn it 90 degrees, slide in the re-key tool, and swap the pins. It takes about 30 seconds per lock, and you can re-key all three to match one key — meaning you'll carry a single key for both doors.

The deadbolt throws cleanly with a satisfying, solid feel. The knob turns smoothly when the door is properly aligned. Installation is straightforward if you've done basic door hardware before — the included template and instructions are clear, and the whole set takes about 45 minutes for a DIYer.

One thing buyers consistently mention is the fit on standard pre-drilled doors. One reviewer noted: "Replaced three old locks in about an hour. The SmartKey feature meant I didn't have to call a locksmith when I moved in." Another said the latch "needed a bit of filing on the strike plate to align perfectly" on a 1970s door.

## Where This Set Falls Short

**The latch adjustment is real.** Older homes with sagging door frames will test your patience. The latch mechanism is unforgiving if the door doesn't sit perfectly square. Budget for a metal file or some shims if you're installing on a house built before 1990.

**The aesthetics are plain.** The Tylo knob is functional but unremarkable. If you're upgrading for curb appeal, this isn't the set that makes neighbors ask where you bought your hardware. It looks like what it is: a reliable, no-frills security upgrade.

**The finish wears over time.** Multiple reviewers noted the satin nickel shows wear on the knob edges after 12-18 months of heavy use. It's cosmetic, not functional, but worth knowing.

## Who Should Buy This

- **First-time homeowners** on a budget who need to secure two doors without spending $200 on premium sets
- **Renters** who want to re-key when they move in (and re-key back before they leave — SmartKey makes this practical)
- **DIYers** replacing old, worn-out hardware who want a one-and-done solution

Skip it if you're restoring a historic home, want a designer look, or need a high-security deadbolt with bump-resistant pins — this is a solid standard lock, not a fortress.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Can I re-key this to match my existing Kwikset keys?**
A: Yes — that's the whole point of SmartKey. You don't need the original keys. As long as you have a Kwikset key you want to match, you can re-key all three locks to that key in about five minutes total.

**Q: Will this fit a standard US door?**
A: Yes, for pre-drilled doors with 2-3/8" or 2-3/4" backset holes. It fits both 1-3/8" and 1-3/4" thick doors. If you have a non-standard door, measure before ordering.

**Q: Does the deadbolt come with a thumb turn on the inside?**
A: Yes, it's a single-cylinder deadbolt with an interior thumb turn — no key required to exit from inside, which is important for fire safety.
