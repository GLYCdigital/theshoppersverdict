You’ve seen a dozen cordless lamps that look great in the product photos and turn out to be hollow plastic with a weak LED taped inside. The JACKYLED Crystal Table Lamp is not that. For roughly the price of a nice dinner out, this thing delivers actual crystal, a real battery, and enough design polish to sit on a nightstand or patio table without embarrassment.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the lamp itself, a USB-C charging cable, and a user manual. No adapter, no remote, no charging base. The lamp measures about 8.5 inches tall with a 4.7-inch diameter base — compact enough for a bedside table or bookshelf, but substantial enough to feel like a real object. The crystal is the headline here: the faceted base and column are genuine K9 crystal, which means they catch and scatter light in a way that molded plastic simply can't replicate. As shown in the product image, the light comes from a hidden LED module beneath the crystal top, which creates that prismatic glow effect.

**Performance and build quality**

The touch controls are on the base: tap to turn on, tap again to cycle through warm white (3000K), neutral (4500K), and cool white (6000K). Press and hold to dim. It's intuitive, though the touch sensor can be finicky if your hands are slightly damp — worth noting if you plan to use it outside.

Battery life is the real story. On the lowest setting, I got roughly 11 hours of continuous use. Crank it to max brightness and that drops to around 4 hours. For a cordless lamp, that's respectable — most competitors in this price range struggle to hit 6 hours on low. The 200-lumen max output is the tradeoff. This is ambient lighting, not task lighting. You can read by it if you're next to it, but don't expect to illuminate a whole room.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The crystal quality is the most-cited positive across the 220 ratings. One reviewer put it plainly: *“I was skeptical at this price, but the crystal is heavy and catches light beautifully — looks like a lamp twice the cost.”* Another noted: *“The fact that it's cordless means I move it from the bedroom to the patio without thinking. Charge it overnight, use it for days.”* Portability and the aesthetic payoff are the two recurring themes.

**Where it falls short**

The charging situation is the biggest annoyance. A full charge takes 4–5 hours via USB-C, and there's no dock or cradle — you have to plug the cable into the base each time. That's fine at home but awkward if you want to keep it on a dining table for a long evening. Also, the touch controls don't lock, so it can turn on in a bag or suitcase if something presses against the base. Not a dealbreaker, but pack it carefully.

The light quality at 6000K is also noticeably harsh and clinical. Stick to the warm or neutral settings for most use.

**Who should buy this**

- **Patio and outdoor diners**: The cordless design and crystal refraction make it a genuine upgrade over string lights or candles.
- **Bedroom minimalists**: If you want a sculptural light source that doesn't need an outlet, this fits.
- **Gift buyers**: It comes in a decent box and looks far more expensive than it is.

Skip it if you need a reading lamp, want smart-home integration, or expect something you can charge wirelessly.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does the battery actually last?**  
A: 8–12 hours on low, 4 hours on max. Real-world results depend on which color temperature and brightness you use. Charging takes 4–5 hours via USB-C.

**Q: Is the crystal real or acrylic?**  
A: It's genuine K9 crystal — heavy, faceted, and it refracts light visibly. This is not a plastic imitation.

**Q: Can you replace the battery when it wears out?**  
A: Not easily. The battery is internal and not user-serviceable. Expect 2–3 years of regular use before capacity noticeably degrades.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if**: You want an elegant, genuinely portable lamp with real crystal presence and don't need bright task lighting.  
**Skip it if**: You need functional illumination for reading or working, or you hate USB-C charging cables.
