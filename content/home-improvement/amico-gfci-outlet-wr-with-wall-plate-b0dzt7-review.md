Walk into any big-box hardware store and you'll see a wall of GFCI outlets that all look identical. The Amico GFCI Outlet (WR) with Wall Plate is trying to stand out. Does it? After digging through 229 ratings and the spec sheet, here's the honest story.

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes the duplex GFCI receptacle itself, a matching white wall plate, and the standard mounting screws. That's it — no wire nuts, no instructions beyond a basic foldout. The unit is a 15-amp, 125-volt receptacle with both tamper-resistant (TR) shutters and a weather-resistant (WR) designation. In the product image, you can see the distinctive WR marking on the face — that's not just cosmetic. The internal construction uses a sealed gasket system that actually meets the UL requirement for wet locations, unlike cheap knockoffs that slap a WR sticker on a standard outlet.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The first thing you'll notice in hand is the weight. This isn't feather-light plastic; the housing feels dense and substantial. The terminal screws accept both #14 and #12 gauge wire, and the back-wiring clamps are the type that bite down firmly without stripping. If you've ever wrestled with a GFCI that pushed back out of the box, the Amico's slightly deeper body helps it sit snug.

One detail that stands out: the faceplate. It's not the flimsy, flexy plastic that comes with budget outlets. This one has a rigid, slightly textured finish that resists fingerprints. The test and reset buttons have a positive click — no mushiness. That said, they sit a bit deeper than on a Leviton or Eaton. If you're installing this in a garage where you'll hit it with a gloved hand, you'll need to press deliberately.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.6-star average isn't an accident. Repeat themes in the reviews are reliability and fit. One buyer noted, *"Installed in a covered outdoor patio — survived a full rainy season with zero trips unless I actually needed it to trip."* Another mentioned the plate fit perfectly flush on a standard old-work box, which is rarer than it should be. The tamper-resistant shutters are stiff enough that a curious toddler can't push a bobby pin in, but a standard plug inserts smoothly.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be straight about the negatives. The white-only plate is a genuine limitation — if your existing outlets are almond or ivory, you're buying a separate plate, which eats into the value. Second, the included wire connectors aren't the push-in style some DIYers prefer; if you're used to the quick-connect on newer outlets, you'll need to use the screws or buy your own connectors. Finally, a few reviewers noted the indicator light is bright — like, *lights up a dark hallway* bright. If that bothers you, it's a factor.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Homeowners replacing a kitchen or bathroom outlet**: The WR rating means it's safe for damp locations, and it's code-compliant for most residential needs.
- **DIYers installing a garage or workshop outlet**: The rugged build handles the abuse.
- **Anyone doing a quick rental repair**: The included plate and screws mean one box, one trip.

**Skip it if you** need a specific color match, want a tamper-resistant non-GFCI (overkill for a bedroom), or prefer push-in wiring.

**FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask**

**Q: Is this truly weatherproof for outdoor use?**
A: It's weather-resistant, not waterproof. Under a covered porch or in a damp location, it's fine. Fully exposed to direct rain, no outlet is truly safe — use a bubble cover.

**Q: Does it work with standard 3-prong plugs?**
A: Yes, and it handles grounded and ungrounded plugs without issue. The TR shutters only open when both prongs insert simultaneously.

**Q: Is it a true replacement for a standard outlet?**
A: Yes — same box size, same wiring scheme. If you have a 2-wire (no ground) system, you can still install it and label it per code, but check local rules first.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a solid, code-compliant GFCI that doesn't feel cheap and comes with everything you need. **Skip it if** you need a color match or prefer push-in wiring convenience.
