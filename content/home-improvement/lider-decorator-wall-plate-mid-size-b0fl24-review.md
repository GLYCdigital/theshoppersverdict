## What You're Actually Getting

The package contains the wall plate itself — a screwless, snap-on cover designed for decorator-style (rectangular) outlets and switches. Installation is straightforward: attach the mounting bracket to your device, snap the cover on, and you're done. The fit is the standout feature here. ## Where It Falls Short

The mid-size designation is the biggest hurdle. It's not a universal standard, so if you're replacing an existing plate, measure your current opening before ordering. A few reviewers mentioned ordering the wrong size on their first attempt — that's a return trip nobody enjoys.

The snap-on cover is also a bit stubborn when you need to remove it. If you're someone who likes to adjust wiring or repaint around outlets frequently, you'll find yourself wrestling with the cover more than you'd like. It's a minor annoyance, but worth knowing going in.
