**Performance and Build Quality**

The Ronan's real strength is its construction. The brass body feels substantial — no thin metal or plastic internals. The lever handles operate with a smooth, precise action that snaps into place without wobble. The ceramic disc cartridge (Moen's standard) should deliver decades of drip-free service.

The spout height is a practical 5.5 inches from the deck, and the reach extends 6.5 inches. That's enough clearance to wash your hands without knuckle-bumping the bowl, but not so tall that it looks out of proportion on a compact vanity.

The pop-up drain is metal, not plastic — a small detail that matters. The lift rod connects smoothly, and the stopper seals properly with minimal fiddling.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be direct about the downsides:

**Installation is not for novices.** Widespread faucets are more complex than centerset models. You're connecting three separate pieces with supply lines, and you'll need to reach under the sink to tighten nuts in tight spaces. Expect 1.5–2 hours if you're handy, or budget for a plumber.

**The finish needs care.** Brushed Bronze is a living finish — it will develop a natural patina over time, which some love. But it's also prone to water spots and fingerprints. You'll need to wipe it down regularly with a soft cloth. Harsh cleaners will strip the finish. If you hate maintenance, choose chrome or stainless instead.

**The spout is on the shorter side.** At 5.5 inches, it's fine for standard sinks, but if you have a deep vessel sink or a tall vanity, you might find the clearance tight.

**No matching accessories included.** The drain assembly matches, but there's no matching towel bar or tissue holder in the Ronan line unless you hunt for them separately.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit my existing sink?**
A: Only if your sink has three separate holes spaced 8–16 inches apart. Measure the distance between the center of the left hole and the center of the right hole. If it's not in that range, this faucet won't fit without modifications.

**Q: Do I need to buy the valves separately?**
A: No — the valves are included in the box. That's a major plus versus many competitors that charge extra.

**Q: How does the Brushed Bronze finish hold up?**
A: It's durable, but it will show water spots and fingerprints. Use a soft, damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid abrasive cleaners and acidic products. Over years, it will develop a natural patina — that's by design.
