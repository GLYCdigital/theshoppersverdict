**The cordless table lamp that actually earns its spot on the patio table**

Most "portable" lamps are portable the way a toaster is—technically movable, practically anchored to a wall outlet. The HAPYGI Portable Metal Table Lamp isn't that. It's a genuinely cordless light you can carry from the dining table to the deck railing to the tent vestibule without ever thinking about where the next plug is. After 210 Amazon ratings averaging 4.2 stars, it's clear this lamp has found its audience. Here's who that audience is—and isn't.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the lamp itself (roughly 7 inches tall with a 4-inch diameter base), a USB-C charging cable, and a manual that's mercifully short. No wall adapter included—you're expected to have a USB-C brick lying around, and most people do. The metal body is powder-coated, not painted, which matters for outdoor use because it resists chipping when you inevitably knock it over. The light head pivots about 90 degrees, so you can aim it at a book, a grill, or a card game without moving the whole unit.

**Real-world performance**

The headline feature is the rechargeable battery. On the low setting (which is still plenty bright for a dinner table), you'll get roughly 20 hours. Crank it to max brightness and that drops closer to 8 hours—still enough for a full evening plus breakfast cleanup. The three color temperatures are the standout feature here: warm 2700K for cozy dinners, neutral 4000K for reading, and cool 5000K for task lighting. Stepless dimming lets you dial anywhere in between, which sounds gimmicky until you're trying to read without blinding your dining partner.

The IP54 rating is honest. It shrugs off light rain and splashes from a pool or hose, but don't leave it out in a downpour or submerge it. One buyer noted, *"Used it on our covered patio through a humid summer and it never flickered or corroded."* That tracks with the sealed charging port cover.

**Where it falls short**

The metal build gives it premium heft, but that's also its weakness. At about 1.2 pounds, it's too heavy for backpacking and will feel bulky in a carry-on. The base has no rubber grip ring—on a smooth glass patio table, a solid bump can send it sliding. And if you drain it nightly, the 4-5 hour recharge time means you'll want to plug it in during the day, not right before guests arrive.

**Who should buy this**

- **Patio and porch owners** who want ambient light without running extension cords
- **Campers** who car-camp or glamp and want something sturdier than a plastic lantern
- **Anyone prepping for power outages** who wants a light that lasts multiple evenings
- **Renters** who can't hardwire outdoor lighting and want a temporary solution

**Skip it if** you're a ultralight backpacker (look at a headlamp), you need a reading light with a focused beam (this is ambient, not spot), or you expect it to be a standalone lantern for a tent (the directional head doesn't throw 360-degree light).

**Frequently asked questions**

**Q: How long does the battery actually last on one charge?**
A: Realistically, 8-20 hours depending on brightness. Low warmth gives you the full 20; max brightness and cool color cuts that to 8. The LED indicator turns red when you're under 20%.

**Q: Can I use it while it's charging?**
A: Yes, it works plugged in, but the cable port is on the base, so it's less stable while tethered. Fine for desk use, awkward on a table edge.

**Q: Is it bright enough to read by?**
A: At the highest setting and 5000K color, yes—for a single reader. It's not going to light up a whole room, but it's brighter than most camping lanterns at this size.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a durable, cordless lamp that moves between indoor and outdoor spaces without compromise. It's priced right for what it is, and the adjustable color temperature alone justifies the purchase for anyone who cares about ambient lighting.

**Skip it if** you need something ultralight, you're on a tight budget and can live with a wired lamp, or you expect a 360-degree lantern glow.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, well-built cordless lamp with real versatility. The lack of base grip and slower recharge keep it from a perfect score, but for most patio, camping, and emergency-use scenarios, it's a genuine upgrade over the flimsy plastic alternatives at similar prices.
