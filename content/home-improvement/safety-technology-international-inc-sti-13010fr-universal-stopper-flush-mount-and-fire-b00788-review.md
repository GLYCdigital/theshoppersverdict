**The STI-13010FR Universal Stopper isn't glamorous. It's a clear plastic box that goes over your fire alarm pull station. But it's the kind of product that pays for itself the first time a kid slaps the handle "just to see what happens" — or a disgruntled employee decides to make a point.**

The concept is simple: cover the pull station so it can't be accidentally or maliciously activated, but make it so opening the cover takes less than two seconds in a real emergency. This is the "flush mount" version, designed for pull stations that sit flat against the wall, which covers most commercial, school, and institutional installations.

## What you actually get

The box contains the clear polycarbonate cover (roughly 6" x 5" x 2.5"), a lock with two keys, self-tapping screws, and an instruction sheet. The polycarbonate is the real selling point — it's thick enough that you genuinely can't flex it with your hands. UV stabilizers in the plastic mean it won't turn that ugly yellow-brown color after a few years of direct sun, which is a common failure on cheaper covers.

Installation is genuinely straightforward. You hold the cover over your existing pull station, mark the screw holes, and drive in the self-tapping screws. The cover swings open on a hinge, and when closed, it locks via a small cam lock. The alarm pull handle sits flush inside, so you'd have to deliberately break through the cover to reach it without unlocking.

## What buyers consistently report

With 235 ratings averaging 4.7 stars, the feedback pattern is consistent. One reviewer noted, "I've had these in a school hallway for three years now. The universal sizing claim holds up. The cover accommodates pull stations up to 4.5" x 4", and the mounting holes are slotted, giving you about a quarter-inch of adjustment in each direction. That's enough to handle most major brands — Notifier, Wheelock, Simplex, Edwards — without issue.

## Where it falls short

Be honest about the lock. The included cam lock is a deterrent, not a barrier. A determined person with a screwdriver can pry it open in under a minute. For most applications — schools, offices, retail — that's fine. The goal is stopping accidental activation and casual vandalism, not defeating a deliberate attack. If you need actual tamper resistance, look at the STI-13010 with a keyed-alike cylinder upgrade.

The other limitation: this is strictly for **flush-mount** pull stations. If your station sits on a surface-mount electrical box that protrudes from the wall, the cover won't seat properly. You'd need the surface-mount variant (STI-13010SM), which has a deeper backplate. Measure first — this is the most common return reason.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my [brand] pull station?**
If your pull station is flush-mounted and the handle area is roughly 4.5" x 4" or smaller, yes. The slotted mounting holes give you flexibility. If it's a surface-mount box, get the SM version.

**Q: Can I use my own lock with this?**
No. The cam lock is integrated into the cover design. STI sells keyed-alike versions if you need multiple covers with the same key — worth it if you're installing more than two or three.

**Q: Does opening the cover trigger an alarm?**
No, this is a passive cover. It doesn't have a tamper switch. If you need an alarm on the cover itself, you'd need the STI-13010 with an optional tamper kit.
