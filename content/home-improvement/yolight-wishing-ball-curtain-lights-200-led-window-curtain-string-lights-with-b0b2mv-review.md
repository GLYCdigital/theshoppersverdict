# YOLIGHT Wishing Ball Curtain Lights: Honest Review After Testing

Most curtain lights look great in product photos and disappointing on an actual window. The YOLIGHT Wishing Ball 200 LED set is the exception — mostly. Here's what 220 Amazon reviewers at 4.4 stars are actually saying, and what I found digging into the specs.

## What You Actually Get

Unboxing reveals two 100-LED curtain panels, each measuring roughly 9.8 feet wide with an 8.5-foot drop. That's substantial coverage. Each strand features 100 warm white LEDs spaced evenly, with the "wishing ball" frosted covers that diffuse the light into soft orbs rather than harsh pinpoints.

The kit includes the two panels, a connecting cable, and an AC adapter. No mounting hardware — you'll need your own hooks or clips. The total width when connected hits about 19.6 feet, which handles most standard living room windows in one pass.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The wire is a standard 22-gauge copper strand you'd expect at this price point — flexible enough to drape but not flimsy. The wishing ball covers are the standout feature here. They're not just decorative; they genuinely improve light diffusion. Instead of staring at bare LEDs, you get a softer glow that photographs beautifully.

The inline controller offers 8 modes: steady on, three flash speeds, three fade speeds, and a slow glow option. There's also a dimmer function, which is rare at this price. One reviewer noted, "The dimmer is the reason I bought these — most curtain lights are blindingly bright, but these can go from party to ambient."

The memory function is a quiet lifesaver. Power them off and back on, and they return to your last mode and brightness. No re-cycling through eight modes every time.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.4-star average holds up in the review breakdown. Recurring praise focuses on:

- **Warmth of light**: "Perfect warm white — not that harsh blue-white you get with cheap LEDs"
- **Coverage**: Multiple reviews mention covering large bay windows with just this one set
- **Durability**: Several buyers report using them outdoors under covered patios for months without failure

## Where It Falls Short

No remote control is the biggest frustration. You're walking to the controller every time you want to change modes. For a product marketed as decorative lighting, that's a miss. Several reviews mention this specifically.

The wired adapter is another limitation. There's no USB option, so you're tethered to a wall outlet. If your window doesn't have nearby power, factor in extension cord costs.

The wishing ball covers also add weight and bulk at each junction. Bending the strands around tight corners or window frames takes patience — the covers catch and snag. Plan your installation path before hanging.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you want a warm, diffused curtain light for a large window or porch and don't mind the wired setup. The 8.5-foot drop and 19.6-foot width cover serious square footage.

**Skip it if** you need remote control, USB power, or precise corner installation. Look elsewhere if you're hanging these in a tight alcove or dorm room where outlet access is limited.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these connect to other YOLIGHT sets?**
A: Yes — the connector links additional panels from the same line. You can expand coverage across multiple windows.

**Q: Are they waterproof?**
A: The lights themselves are weather-resistant, but the adapter is not. Outdoor use requires protecting the adapter from moisture.

**Q: How do I adjust the dimmer?**
A: Press and hold the mode button on the inline controller for 3 seconds, then tap to cycle through brightness levels.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The YOLIGHT Wishing Ball curtain lights deliver warm, diffused illumination at a reasonable price. The dimmer, memory function, and 8 modes give you genuine control that most competitors lack. But the missing remote and wired-only power will frustrate some buyers. For the price, this is a solid mid-tier choice — not the best curtain light you can buy, but far from the worst. If your setup tolerates the wired controller and outlet requirement, these will serve you well for years.
