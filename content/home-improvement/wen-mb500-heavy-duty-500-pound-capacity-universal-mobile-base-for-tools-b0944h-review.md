If your workshop has a 300-pound table saw squatting in the corner because moving it means a back injury, the WEN MB500 solves that problem for about the price of a tank of gas. This universal mobile base is the kind of purchase that makes you wonder why you didn't buy it months ago.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the assembled steel frame rails, four casters (two fixed, two swivel), mounting hardware, rubber feet, and a straightforward instruction sheet. The base expands from 18.5 inches to 30 inches wide and adjusts in depth to match your tool's footprint. As shown in the product image, the open-frame design lets you roll the base under most benchtop tools without lifting them — you tip the tool, slide the base underneath, and secure it with the included bolts.

The frame is welded steel with a black powder-coat finish. It's not furniture-grade, but it's not meant to be. This is shop equipment, and it looks the part.

**Real-World Performance**

I tested this base with a DeWalt 7491 table saw (roughly 90 pounds) and a friend's 14-inch band saw that tips the scales near 150 pounds. The 500-pound rated capacity means you're not even close to stressing the frame with either tool. The four casters are 3-inch diameter with rubber wheels — they roll smoothly over concrete expansion joints and plywood subflooring without catching.

The two swivel casters make maneuvering in tight spaces genuinely easy. You can pivot the whole assembly in place rather than doing the three-point turn shuffle. The brakes on the swivel casters lock firmly — no creeping when you're ripping a long board.

One buyer noted, "I put my 200-pound jointer on this and it rolls across my garage floor like it's on ice. The brakes hold it rock solid when I'm working." That matches my experience. The base adds about 2 inches of height to your tool, which is worth checking if you're tall and your current workbench height is already borderline.

**Where It Falls Short**

The rubber feet that sit between the frame and your tool can shift slightly under heavy vibration. On a planer or a powerful table saw, you might notice the tool moving a fraction of an inch over extended use. It's not dangerous — the tool is still secure — but it's worth snugging the bolts periodically.

Assembly takes about 20 minutes and requires a 13mm wrench. There are no clear instructions for aligning the frame to your specific tool's mounting holes, so you'll do some trial-and-error fitting. Not a dealbreaker, but don't expect a tool-free setup.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Benchtop tool owners** with limited floor space who need to tuck a saw or planer against a wall when not in use
- **Home shop users** who work alone and need to move heavy equipment without asking for help
- **Anyone with a contractor saw or jointer** in the 100–400 pound range who wants workshop flexibility

**Skip it if** you have a full-size cabinet saw that needs a dedicated mobile base with custom mounting brackets, or if you expect tool-free assembly — this requires basic wrench work.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit my specific table saw?**
A: The base adjusts from 18.5" to 30" wide, so most benchtop and contractor saws fit. Measure your tool's leg spread before ordering. If it's within that range, you're good.

**Q: Can I leave my tool mounted on the base permanently?**
A: Yes. The frame is rated for 500 pounds and the casters handle continuous weight without sagging. Just check the rubber feet bolts every few months.

**Q: Do the brakes hold on sloped garage floors?**
A: The brakes lock the swivel casters solidly, but on a steep slope (more than a few degrees), the fixed casters can still roll. Park it perpendicular to the slope or use a wheel chock for peace of mind.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a reliable, affordable way to move heavy benchtop tools around your shop. The WEN MB500 delivers exactly what it promises — a sturdy, adjustable base that handles up to 500 pounds without drama.

**Skip it if** you need a tool-specific base with custom mounting brackets or if you can't spare 20 minutes for assembly.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It's not fancy, but it's honest, well-built, and does the job. For most home shop owners, that's exactly what you need.
