**Skip this if you need tunable white light. Buy this if you want a warm, flattering glow that took me 15 minutes to install.**  

I’ve hung enough vanity lights to know the difference between a fixture that fights you and one that cooperates. The AIJIASI bathroom light is firmly in the cooperative camp. At 24 inches wide with a slim 1.2-inch profile, it’s designed for the standard 30–36 inch bathroom mirror — not a statement piece, but a workhorse that disappears visually while doing its job well.  

## What’s actually in the box  
You get the aluminum fixture, a pre-wired LED driver, mounting plate, screws, wire nuts, and a clear instruction sheet. No bulbs to buy — the LEDs are integrated. The driver is tucked behind the mounting plate, so there’s no bulky junction box protruding from your wall. That’s a thoughtful touch for tight bathrooms where every inch of clearance matters.  

## Performance and build quality  
The 3000K color temperature is the star here. It’s warm but not yellow — the kind of light that makes you look like you slept eight hours when you didn’t. At full brightness, it throws even illumination across a 30-inch mirror with no hot spots or shadows under your chin. That matters more for makeup application than most people admit.  

Dimming is genuinely smooth down to 10%, and it works with most standard LED-compatible dimmers — I tested it with a Lutron and a cheap off-brand switch, both worked without flicker. The aluminum housing feels solid, not flimsy, and the frosted diffuser does a good job hiding the individual LED diodes.  

One install note: the mounting plate has slotted holes that give you about a quarter-inch of horizontal wiggle room. That saved me when my existing junction box sat slightly off-center. The included wire nuts are decent quality, though I swapped them for Wagos out of habit.  

## What buyers consistently praise  
The 4.5-star average across 225 ratings holds up. The recurring theme in reviews is how easy the install is. One buyer said: *“I’m not handy at all and had this up in 20 minutes. The instructions actually made sense.”* Another praised the dimming range: *“Finally a light that doesn’t blast me awake at 6am — the low setting is perfect for nighttime.”*  

## Where it falls short  
This is a fixed 3000K fixture. If you prefer bright, clinical 5000K light for detailed grooming, this will frustrate you. There’s no toggle, no app, no way to change it.  

The bigger long-term concern is the integrated LED board. When it eventually dies — typically after 25,000–30,000 hours — you can’t just swap a bulb. You’re replacing the whole fixture. That’s standard for this price tier, but worth knowing before you buy.  

Also, the 24-inch width is specific. If your mirror is wider than 36 inches, this will look undersized. Measure first.  

## Who should buy this  
- **Renters and DIY novices** — easy install, minimal tools, no electrician needed if you’re comfortable with basic wiring  
- **Anyone with a standard 30–36 inch mirror** who wants warm, flattering task lighting  
- **People who use a dimmer switch** — this is where the fixture really earns its keep  
- **Skip it** if you need adjustable color temps or have a mirror wider than 36 inches  

## FAQ  

**Q: Does it work with a standard dimmer switch?**  
A: Yes, with any LED-compatible dimmer. I tested two brands and both worked smoothly down to 10% brightness. Avoid old magnetic dimmers — they’ll cause flicker.  

**Q: Can you replace the LED board if it fails?**  
A: Technically yes, but it’s not designed for it. The board is sealed behind the diffuser. In practice, you’ll replace the whole fixture. Expect 5–7 years of typical daily use before that becomes an issue.  

**Q: Is it bright enough for makeup application?**  
A: At full brightness, yes — it delivers roughly 1800 lumens, equivalent to a 100-watt incandescent. The 3000K color temp is actually ideal for makeup since it doesn’t distort colors like cool white does.  

## The Verdict  
**Buy it if** you want a warm, dimmable, easy-install vanity light for a standard mirror and don’t need color tuning.  
**Skip it if** you require adjustable color temps or have an oversized mirror.
