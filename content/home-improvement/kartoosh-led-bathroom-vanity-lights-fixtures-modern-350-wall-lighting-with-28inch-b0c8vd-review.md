**The Short Version:** The KARTOOSH LED vanity light is a genuinely useful fixture for anyone tired of shadows across their face while getting ready. The 350° rotating heads are the headline feature, and they deliver. But it's hardwired, fixed at 4000K, and not for everyone. Here's the full breakdown.

---

**What You Actually Get**

Out of the box, you get the 28-inch aluminum light bar, a mounting bracket, screws, wire nuts, and a plastic template for marking your junction box holes. The fixture is hardwired — there's no plug, no battery, no smart-home module. If you're replacing an existing vanity light, the swap is straightforward: shut off the breaker, match the black/white/ground wires, mount the bracket, and slide the bar on. If you're installing fresh, you'll need a junction box already in place.

The bar itself is 28 inches wide and about 2.5 inches deep, which is the right footprint for a standard 24-inch bathroom mirror. The integrated LEDs are sealed behind a frosted acrylic diffuser. It's clean-looking, but you can't replace individual diodes — the whole unit is the light source.

---

**Performance and Build Quality**

The 350° rotation is the real selling point. Each head swivels independently, so you can point one down at the sink for toothbrushing, another at your face for makeup, and a third toward the shower niche if you want ambient light. The pivots have a firm, ratcheting feel — they hold position without drooping, which is rare at this price point. Multiple Amazon reviewers noted the same thing. One buyer wrote: *"I was skeptical the arms would stay where I put them, but after three months they haven't moved an inch."*

The light output is bright — roughly 3000 lumens across the bar, which is more than enough for a standard 5x7 bathroom. The 4000K neutral white is a solid middle ground: not sterile blue like 5000K, not dim and yellow like 2700K. It's the color temperature most contractors default to for task lighting. The aluminum housing stays cool even after 30 minutes of continuous use, which is a nice touch given how many budget fixtures run hot.

Build quality is respectable. The matte black finish resists fingerprints and doesn't chip easily. The acrylic diffuser is scratch-resistant in normal use. The mounting bracket is thick steel, not the flimsy stamped tin you see on cheaper bars. This feels like a $60–$70 fixture, not a $30 impulse buy.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Three themes dominate the 220 ratings at 4.4 stars:

**Where It Falls Short**

The fixed 4000K color temperature is the biggest limitation. If your bathroom has warm brass fixtures or cream tiles, 4000K can look slightly clinical. You can't dim it without a compatible dimmer switch, and even then, the color doesn't shift warmer — it just gets dimmer. One reviewer complained: *"I wanted a warm glow for evening baths, but this is strictly task lighting. It's fine for mornings, not for ambiance."*

The hardwired requirement is another hurdle. Renters or anyone without a junction box will need an electrician, which adds $100–$150 to the effective cost. There's no plug-in option, no battery backup, no motion sensor. It's a straightforward fixture with no smart features.

Finally, the 28-inch width is a bit long for smaller mirrors. If your vanity mirror is 18 inches, this bar will overhang significantly. Measure before you buy.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Homeowners replacing an existing vanity light** — this is a direct, clean swap with no surprises.
- **Anyone with a shadow problem** — the 350° rotation genuinely fixes uneven lighting on your face.
- **DIYers comfortable with basic wiring** — if you've changed a ceiling fan, you can install this.

**Skip it if:**

- You rent and can't hardwire.
- You want a warm, dimmable glow for evening relaxation.
- Your mirror is smaller than 24 inches.

---

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this with a dimmer switch?**
A: Yes, but only with a compatible LED dimmer (look for ELV or TRIAC type). The color temperature stays at 4000K — dimming doesn't warm the light. If you want warm dimming, this isn't the fixture for you.

**Q: Does it come with bulbs?**
A: No — the LEDs are integrated into the bar. You can't replace them. If a diode fails, you replace the whole fixture. That's a tradeoff for the slim profile.

**Q: Is it waterproof?**
A: No. It's rated for damp locations (IP44), meaning it handles bathroom humidity fine, but don't install it directly in a shower spray zone or outdoors.
