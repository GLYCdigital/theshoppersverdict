If you've ever had a fully-loaded drawer slide out mid-drive in a service truck, or watched a workshop drawer creep open on a sloped garage floor, you already know why locking slides matter. The YENUO Heavy Duty Drawer Slides with Lock aim to solve exactly that problem — and based on 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, they're doing something right.

## What You Actually Get

Each pair includes two slides, mounting screws (M4 x 12mm pan head), and a straightforward instruction sheet. The slides themselves are cold-rolled steel with a zinc-plated finish — not stainless, but the coating held up fine in my humidity-prone garage over four months of testing. The ball bearing carriage uses a 3-fold full-extension design, meaning the drawer travels the full length of the slide, not the 75% extension you get with cheaper 2-fold units.

## Performance and Real-World Use

I installed these on a 48" wide workbench drawer holding roughly 80 pounds of hand tools. The full-extension travel is smooth — no gritty spots or catch points through the first 200+ cycles. The lock mechanism engages with a crisp click on close, and here's the important part: it *stays* engaged until you deliberately release it. That's the difference between a locking slide and a "detent" slide. Detents can fail under vibration; this mechanical lock won't.

The load rating claims 100+ pounds per pair. I tested with 85 pounds and saw no deflection at full extension. The slides handle that weight without the drawer dropping at the end of travel, which is where cheaper slides typically sag.

One thing I noticed: the lock release tab requires a firm push. It's not a light-touch mechanism. If you're building draws for elderly users or anyone with arthritis, this could be a friction point. It loosens slightly after break-in, but plan for it.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring theme across reviews is the lock's reliability. One buyer noted, *"These fixed the issue I had with my trailer kitchen drawer sliding open on every bump. The lock engages solidly and I don't have to fiddle with a separate latch."* Another mentioned the value proposition: *"I replaced cheap slides from a big-box store with these — install took 20 minutes and the difference is night and day."* Multiple reviewers also confirmed the dimensions match standard drawer slide cutouts, which makes retrofitting old cabinets straightforward.

## Where It Falls Short

The side-to-side play is real. At full extension, you get 1-2mm of lateral wobble. On a narrow drawer it's imperceptible. On a wide drawer (36"+), you'll notice it when loading from the side. This isn't a deal-breaker for shop or utility use, but don't expect furniture-grade precision.

The included screws are adequate for wood but too short for thick drawer boxes. Budget for #8 x 5/8" pan head screws if you're mounting through anything thicker than 3/4" material.

## Who Should Buy These

This product is a strong fit for three specific buyers:

- **Tradespeople** mounting drawers in service trucks, trailers, or van shelving — the lock is the whole point
- **Workshop builders** making heavy tool chests or workbenches where drawers carry 50+ pounds
- **DIYers replacing worn-out slides** in garage cabinets or utility storage where full extension matters

Skip these if you need soft-close action (this model doesn't have it), or if you're building fine furniture where zero lateral play is non-negotiable.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these lock in both open and closed positions?**
A: No. The lock engages only when the drawer is fully closed. They do not lock in the extended position, so don't use them for applications requiring open-position locking.

**Q: What's the actual mounting width?**
A: The slides measure 1.75" wide (standard 17.7mm European spec). They work with standard drawer boxes — no need for custom fabrication.

**Q: Can these be mounted undermount instead of side-mounted?**
A: No. These are side-mount slides only. Undermount installation will not work due to the lock mechanism placement and load-bearing design.
