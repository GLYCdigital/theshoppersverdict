**FAQ**

*Will these work on painted drywall?*
Yes, if the paint is smooth and clean. Wipe with rubbing alcohol first, wait 24 hours before loading, and they'll hold. Matte or textured paint will cause failure.

*Can I reposition the hook after sticking it?*
No. Once the adhesive makes contact, it's permanent. Measure carefully before pressing.

*How do I remove them without damaging the wall?*
Use a hairdryer to warm the adhesive, then stretch the release tab slowly at a 45-degree angle. This breaks the bond cleanly.
