**Where it falls short — read this before buying**

Here's the honest tradeoff: this is drafting/masking tape, not true painter's tape. It lacks the low-tack, UV-resistant adhesive of branded blue tape (3M, FrogTape, etc.). That means two real-world consequences. First, on delicate surfaces — wallpaper, recently stained wood, or any paint that cured less than 7 days — removal can pull up material or leave adhesive residue. Masking a standard 10-foot wall section requires about 40 linear feet of tape just for the top and bottom edges — you'll use two full rolls on a single room. If you're masking large areas, buy the 1.88-inch version of a different product; this one is best for trim, window frames, and detail work.

**FAQ**

**Is this tape safe for freshly painted walls?**
Only if the paint has cured for at least 7 days, ideally 30. On fresh paint (under a week), the medium-tack adhesive can pull up the paint film on removal. Use a true low-tack blue tape for that scenario.

**Does it leave adhesive residue?**
On smooth surfaces like glass, metal, or plastic, expect some residue if left on for more than 48 hours. On painted drywall, removal within 24 hours leaves no visible residue. Remove at a 45-degree angle, pulling back on itself.

**Can I write on this tape?**
Yes — the crepe paper accepts ballpoint pen, pencil, and most markers without bleeding through. That makes it useful for labeling storage boxes and workshop organization.
