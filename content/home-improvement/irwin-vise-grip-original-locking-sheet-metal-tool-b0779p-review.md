**Q: Does this work on aluminum sheet metal?**
A: Yes, but dial the clamping pressure down with the adjustment screw. Aluminum is softer, and the serrated jaws can leave marks if you over-tighten.

**Q: Can I use these for welding hold-downs?**
A: Absolutely. The steel handles and locking grip make them ideal for holding pieces in place while you tack weld. Just be aware the jaws will conduct heat.

**Q: How do I adjust the clamping force?**
