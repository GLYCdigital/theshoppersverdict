**What's actually in the box**

The set ships with a compact aluminum-alloy handle, 32 interchangeable bits, and a clear storage case that doubles as a bit holder. **Performance and build quality**

The handle feels solid in hand — machined aluminum rather than the hollow plastic you often get at this price point. The magnetic bit holder is strong enough to keep screws attached during extraction, which is the difference between a smooth repair and a catastrophic one when you're working over a carpet. The bits themselves are hardened S2 steel, and in testing, they held up well against moderate torque without stripping.

That said, this is a precision set, not a demolition tool. You're not breaking loose rusted lag bolts with this. But for eyeglasses, watches, laptop backplates, drone repairs, and RC hobby work, the 32-bit variety covers nearly every fastener you'll encounter. A couple of reviewers mentioned the case latch feels a bit flimsy, though none reported it breaking in normal use.
