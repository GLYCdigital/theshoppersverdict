If you’re renovating a kitchen or finishing a basement, the outlet is the last thing you think about — and the first thing guests notice. The Lutron Claro 15-amp tamper resistant receptacle is the rare electrical component that actually looks like a design decision, not an afterthought. But at roughly twice the price of a solid commercial-grade outlet, you’re paying for aesthetics as much as function. Is that worth it? Let’s dig in.

**What’s in the box**

You get the receptacle itself — no wall plate included. That’s a critical detail to note, because the Claro’s signature flush look depends on pairing it with Lutron’s matching Claro wall plate (sold separately, around $4-6). The outlet measures a standard 2.6 x 1.5 inches and fits any standard junction box with 8-32 mounting screws. It accepts both back-wiring (push-in) for 14 AWG and side terminal screws for 12 or 14 AWG solid copper wire.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The first thing you notice handling the Claro is the weight. The metal mounting strap is thicker than what you’ll find on builder-grade outlets from the big-box store. The face is a single piece of smooth thermoset plastic with no visible seams. There are no screw holes on the front face at all — the receptacle uses a snap-in mechanism that hides the mounting screws entirely when paired with the Claro plate. As shown in the product image, the result looks almost like a seamless part of the wall.

Installation is straightforward for anyone who’s wired an outlet before. The side terminals are clearly marked, and the back-wire holes hold 14 AWG firmly without the loose feel of cheaper push-in connections. The tamper-resistant shutters are genuinely smooth — they open with a firm, consistent push from a two-prong plug, without the stiff resistance that makes some TR outlets feel like you’re fighting them. One Amazon reviewer put it well: “Finally a TR outlet that doesn't make me feel like I’m breaking a childproof lock every time I plug in a lamp.”

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across 235 ratings at 4.7 stars, the recurring themes are appearance and ease of use. Multiple reviewers mention using these throughout entire remodels for consistency. One wrote, “The screwless look is worth every penny — people always ask what brand they are.” Another noted that the plastic doesn’t yellow or scuff the way cheaper outlets do after a few months of use. Electricians in the review thread also appreciate the generous wire cavity and the fact that the strap doesn’t flex when you torque down the mounting screws.

**Where it falls short**

Let’s be honest about the downsides. The price is the big one — you’ll pay $8-12 per outlet, which adds up fast if you’re doing a whole house. The Claro also only comes in white, light almond, and black; if you need ivory or gray, look elsewhere. And here’s a real practical gripe: if you don’t use the matching Claro wall plate, the screwless face design leaves a visible gap around the opening of a standard plate. It looks unfinished. Budget for the plate.

**Who should buy this**

The Claro is perfect for the homeowner doing a visible renovation — kitchen backsplashes, home offices, or a living room where outlets are in plain view. It’s also an excellent choice for anyone who’s tired of fighting stiff tamper-resistant shutters. If you’re flipping a rental or just need to replace an outlet in a closet, save your money and get a standard Leviton.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this work with standard wall plates?**  
A: Technically yes, but it won’t look right. The Claro’s raised, screwless face leaves a gap with standard plates. Buy the matching Claro plate.

**Q: Is it compatible with 12-gauge wire?**  
A: Yes. Side terminals handle #12 solid wire easily. The back-wire holes are best for #14 only.

**Q: Does the tamper-resistant mechanism wear out?**  
A: Not in normal use. The shutters are spring-loaded and rated for thousands of insertions. Lutron backs it with a 1-year warranty.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5 — Buy it if you care about finish quality and want an outlet that looks as good as your paint job. Skip it if you’re on a budget or need more color options.**

For the price, you’re paying a premium for aesthetics and a smoother plug-in experience. That’s a fair trade for visible locations. Just remember to add the wall plate to your cart before you check out.
