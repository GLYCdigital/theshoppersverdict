Non-treated pins bend under repeated stress — especially if you're hauling anything with real tongue weight. The heat treatment hardens the steel through and through, not just on the surface, so it holds its shape over years of use.

The swivel handle is the standout feature. It rotates a full 360 degrees, which sounds gimmicky until you're crouched behind a truck trying to align a pin in a tight coupler. That rotation lets you position the handle out of the way while you guide the shank home. It's a small thing that saves real frustration.

The cotter pin retainer is simple and effective. No fancy locking mechanisms to fail, no keys to lose. You push it through the hole at the end of the shank and bend the legs. Done.

**Q: Is the swivel handle sturdy or will it break?**
