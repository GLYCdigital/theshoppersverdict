## Performance and Build Quality

The MERV 11 rating sits in the sweet spot for residential HVAC. It captures pollen, dust mites, mold spores, and pet dander — the stuff that actually triggers allergies — while maintaining enough airflow that your furnace blower doesn't work overtime. Compared to a MERV 8, you're getting noticeably better filtration. Compared to a MERV 13, you're sacrificing some capture efficiency but gaining lower static pressure, which keeps your system running efficiently.

The frame rigidity deserves specific mention. Five-inch filters face more static pressure than their 1-inch cousins, and cheap filters collapse under that load. ## FAQ

**How often should I replace this filter?**
Every 3 months is the safe default. If you have no pets and live in a low-dust area, you can stretch to 6 months. Check monthly by holding it up to light — if you can't see through the pleats, replace it.

**Can I wash and reuse this filter?**
No. This is a disposable filter. Washing damages the media and destroys the MERV rating. If you want reusable, you'd need an electrostatic washable filter, which is a different product category entirely.

**Will this work in a standard 1-inch filter slot?**
No. This is 5 inches deep. It will not fit a standard 1-inch return grille or filter rack.
