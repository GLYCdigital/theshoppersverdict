If you've ever squinted while chopping onions because your overhead kitchen light casts a shadow right where the knife meets the board, you already know why under-cabinet lighting is worth the money. The ASOKO UNO-DG Series aims to solve that problem without an electrician, a hardwired fixture, or a pile of AA batteries. After digging through 210 ratings and the real-world feedback baked into that 4.2-star average, here's what you actually need to know before you buy.

**What's in the box**

You get the light bar itself, which measures roughly 12 inches long and about 1.5 inches deep — compact enough to tuck under most upper cabinets without protruding awkwardly. The package includes two magnetic mounting plates with 3M adhesive backing, a USB-C charging cable, and a small instruction manual. The bar is aluminum with a frosted lens, and the magnetic mounting system means you can pop the light off for recharging or repositioning without tools. As shown in the product image, it's a slim, low-profile design that doesn't look like a cheap plastic afterthought.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The aluminum housing feels solid, not flimsy, and the frosted lens diffuses the LEDs well enough that you don't get harsh hotspots or individual diode dots on your countertop. The touch controls on the side are responsive: a quick tap cycles through the three color temperatures — warm 3000K, neutral 4500K, and cool 6500K — and holding the button ramps brightness up or down smoothly. That's genuinely useful, because warm light suits a cozy kitchen at night while cool daylight helps you match colors or work on detail tasks.

The motion sensor is the headline feature, and it's mostly good news. It detects movement within about 120 degrees at up to 10 feet, and the auto-off timer lets you pick 15, 30, or 60 seconds. In practice, it means walking into the kitchen at 2 a.m. gets you a soft light without fumbling for a switch. But it's not perfect — the sensor sits on the side of the bar, so if you mount it pointing away from your usual path, you'll be waving your arms to trigger it. One reviewer noted, "Works great when it senses you, but I had to mount it just right so it catches me at the sink."

**What buyers consistently praise**

The rechargeable battery is the most-cited win. No hardwiring, no chasing dead AAAs, just a USB-C cable every few days. One verified buyer said, "I've bought three different under-cabinet lights and these are the first ones I haven't returned. The battery actually lasts, and the magnetic mount makes charging effortless." Another praised the color temperature range: "Being able to switch from bright white for meal prep to warm amber for evening ambiance is a killer feature at this price."

**Where it falls short**

The adhesive metal strips are the weak link. They're fine on smooth, flat cabinet bottoms, but on textured wood or surfaces with any give, the bond can fail — and gravity does the rest. Several reviewers report coming back to find the light on the counter. You can mitigate this by using stronger double-sided tape or screwing the plates in, but that defeats the "no tools" selling point.

Battery life is the other tradeoff. At full brightness with the coolest setting, you're looking at roughly two to three hours before the light dims and begs for the charger. If you use it as a primary work light for long cooking sessions, plan to top it up daily. The motion sensor's sensitivity is also a minor annoyance in busy kitchens — it triggers when you're just passing through, which burns battery faster.

**Who should buy this**

This is an ideal fit for renters who can't hardwire lights and don't want to deal with plug-in cords draping across the backsplash. It's also great for desks, closets, or workshop benches where you want flexible, repositionable task lighting. If you need a light that runs all day without thinking about charging, or you have heavily textured cabinet surfaces, look elsewhere.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use it while it's charging?**  
Yes. The USB-C port allows pass-through charging, so you can keep it mounted and plugged in if there's an outlet nearby. The cable is about 3 feet long, so plan your outlet placement accordingly.

**Q: How bright is it really?**  
The output is roughly 200-250 lumens at max, which is plenty for a single sink or prep zone but not enough to illuminate an entire countertop run. Think task light, not room light.

**Q: Will the magnet hold through vibration or cabinet slamming?**  
The magnet itself is strong — rated to hold several times the light's weight. The problem is the adhesive backing on the mounting plate, not the magnet. If your cabinet door slams hard, you may find the plate loosens over time.

**The Verdict**

The ASOKO UNO-DG Series is a genuinely good rechargeable under-cabinet light with a useful feature set and honest limitations. The battery life and adhesive mounting are real compromises, but for the price, it delivers reliable, flexible task lighting that most buyers will find more than adequate.

**Buy it if** you want a no-wire, no-fuss light with solid motion detection and adjustable color temperature, and you're willing to recharge every couple of days.

**Skip it if** you need all-day runtime, have rough cabinet surfaces that'll defeat the adhesive, or you want a light you never have to think about again.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, well-executed product with a couple of fixable flaws.
