If you've ever worn a cheap hi-vis shirt that disintegrates after three washes, you know the drill. The Uno Mejor Class 3 work shirt isn't that shirt. At 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, it's carving out a reputation among road crews, warehouse workers, and anyone who needs to be seen at dawn or dusk. But is it worth your money? Let's break it down.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains one long-sleeve Class 3 shirt in the standard safety yellow or orange. The fabric is 100% polyester mesh — that familiar breathable weave that keeps air moving when you're sweating under a summer sun. The reflective striping is 2-inch wide, ANSI-compliant tape running vertically down the shoulders, horizontally across the torso, and completely around the arms. As shown in the product image, the pattern gives you 360-degree visibility whether you're facing traffic or working with your back turned.

You also get two flap-closure chest pockets. These aren't decorative — the snaps hold securely. One buyer noted, "I carry my phone and a small multi-tool in the left pocket daily. Nothing's fallen out in four months of use." That's the kind of detail that matters when you're on a ladder or leaning over equipment.

**Real-World Performance**

The mesh fabric does exactly what it's supposed to: it breathes. On an 85-degree day, you won't feel like you're wearing a plastic bag. But here's the tradeoff — the reflective tape is stiffer than the fabric. Multiple reviewers mention the elbows feeling stiff for the first few wears. It breaks in, but don't expect buttery flexibility right out of the package.

The cut runs generous. Most buyers report that ordering your usual size gives you a relaxed, slightly boxy fit. If you're between sizes or plan to wear this over a thermal base layer, go with your normal size. If you want a trimmer silhouette, size down.

Durability is where this shirt earns its keep. The stitching on the shoulder seams and hem holds up to industrial laundering. One reviewer who works in a steel fabrication shop wrote, "I've washed this twice a week for three months. The reflective tape hasn't peeled, and the color hasn't faded to that washed-out yellow you see on cheap shirts." That's consistent with what we'd expect from a Class 3 garment at this price point.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The visibility factor is the headline. "Drivers see me now — I've had people actually stop and wave me across construction zones instead of rolling through," noted one highway maintenance worker. The breathability gets consistent shout-outs, especially from guys who wore cotton hi-vis before and swore they'd never go back.

The price-to-value ratio also earns frequent mentions. Buyers repeatedly note that comparable Carhartt or Dickies Class 3 shirts cost significantly more, and many say they've bought multiple Uno Mejor shirts to rotate through the workweek.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. The reflective tape stiffness at the elbows is the most common complaint — it's not a dealbreaker, but it's noticeable. The lack of a mic tab or radio loop is a genuine miss for police, security, and EMS personnel who need to clip equipment to their shoulders. And while the mesh is great for warm weather, it's not a cold-weather shirt. You'll need a thermal layer underneath once temperatures drop below 50 degrees.

**Who Should Buy This**

This shirt is ideal for:
- Road construction crews and flaggers who need ANSI-compliant visibility
- Warehouse workers in facilities with forklift traffic
- Utility workers doing roadside repairs
- Anyone who wants solid Class 3 protection without spending $50+ per shirt

Skip it if you need a winter-weight hi-vis garment, if you require a mic tab for radio communication, or if you want a fitted athletic cut.

**FAQ**

**Is this shirt actually ANSI Class 3 certified?**
Yes. It meets ANSI/ISEA 107-2015 Class 3 requirements with the correct background material coverage and 2-inch reflective trim. The certification tags are sewn into the side seam.

**How does the sizing run?**
Generous. The fit is relaxed through the chest and shoulders. Most buyers recommend ordering your normal size for layering or sizing down for a more tailored fit. Check the size chart — it's accurate.

**Can I machine wash and dry this?**
Yes. The fabric and reflective tape hold up to regular machine washing. Wash cold, tumble dry low. Avoid fabric softener — it can degrade the reflective coating over time.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need dependable Class 3 visibility for a fraction of what premium brands charge, and you work in warm-to-moderate conditions.

**Skip it if** you need a mic tab, require a winter-weight fabric, or want a slim fit right out of the package.

The Uno Mejor Class 3 is a strong value that performs where it counts — visibility, breathability, and durability. It's not the most feature-rich hi-vis shirt on the market, but for the price, it's hard to beat.
