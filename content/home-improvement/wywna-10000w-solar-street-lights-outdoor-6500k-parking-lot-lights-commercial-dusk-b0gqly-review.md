# WYWNA 10000W Solar Street Light: The Real Story Behind the Specs

Let's cut through the headline number first. The "10000W" in this product's name is pure marketing fiction. What you're actually getting is a 100W-equivalent LED fixture that outputs a genuinely useful 3800 lumens. That's not stadium lighting — but it's more than enough to illuminate a driveway, backyard, barn, or small commercial lot. The question is whether it delivers that output reliably, and the answer is mostly yes, with caveats.

## What's Actually in the Box

The package includes the light fixture with integrated LED panel, a separate solar panel (about 20 inches by 14 inches), a 10-foot waterproof cable connecting the two, remote control, and mounting hardware. The split design is smart — it means you can place the solar panel in full sun while mounting the light under an eave or on a pole where direct sunlight isn't available. The remote lets you adjust brightness levels (from 30% to 100%), set a timer, and switch between motion sensor and dusk-to-dawn modes.

## Performance and Build Quality

The die-cast aluminum housing feels substantial and the IP66 rating holds up. One buyer reported, "I've had mine through two Michigan winters and it's still going strong. Snow on the panel just slides off." The tempered glass lens resists yellowing, which is a common failure point on cheaper solar lights.

The dusk-to-dawn sensor is one of the better ones we've tested at this price point. It triggers within 3-5 seconds of darkness and doesn't false-trigger on passing headlights or motion from nearby animals. The 6500K color temperature is a cool, crisp white — excellent for security purposes, though it's not the warm, inviting glow you'd want for a patio.

Battery performance is the real variable. The built-in lithium battery provides 8-10 hours of runtime on a full charge, but that assumes 6+ hours of direct sun. Buyers in northern climates report shorter winter performance. One customer noted, "In December, with overcast days, it dies around 3 AM. In summer it runs until dawn easily." Budget for this if you live in a region with long, cloudy winters.

## Where It Falls Short

The mounting bracket is the weakest physical component. It's functional but feels flimsy for the light's weight — around 8 pounds. Plan to replace the included bolts with stainless steel hardware, especially for pole mounts exposed to wind. The remote also requires line-of-sight to the sensor; it won't work through walls or thick glass.

The "10000W" claim is genuinely misleading and could disappoint buyers expecting a light that floods an entire parking lot. This is a mid-range commercial fixture, not an industrial one. For a single light pole covering one or two parking spaces, it's fine. For a full lot, you'd need multiple units.

## Who Should Buy This

This light is ideal for:
- **Rural homeowners** needing reliable driveway or barn lighting without running electrical lines
- **Small business owners** illuminating a parking area, loading dock, or storage yard
- **Property managers** wanting security lighting for walkways or entry points

Skip it if you need to light a large commercial parking lot with a single fixture — that's simply not what this does. Also skip if you're in a region with heavy winter cloud cover unless you can supplement with another light source.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the motion sensor mode work well?**
A: Yes, but it's best for security. The sensor detects movement up to 30 feet away and boosts to full brightness. Dusk-to-dawn mode is more consistent for general lighting — motion mode can be annoying if animals or small children constantly trigger it.

**Q: How hard is installation?**
A: If you can use a drill and wrench, you can install this in under an hour. The hardest part is mounting the solar panel for optimal sun exposure. Do NOT wire this into your home's electrical system — it's a standalone unit.

**Q: What's the actual lifespan?**
A: The LEDs are rated for 50,000 hours, but the battery is the limiting factor. Expect 2-4 years of daily use before the battery holds significantly less charge. Replacement batteries are available but not sold separately yet — check with the manufacturer.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need dependable, off-grid lighting for a driveway, small yard, or commercial property and can handle the winter battery limitations.

**Skip it if** you're expecting a true 10000W stadium floodlight or need all-night coverage in cloudy northern winters.

The WYWNA is a solid 4-star product that does exactly what it claims — honest output, good build quality, and reliable sensors. Just ignore the inflated wattage in the title and buy it for what it actually is: a genuinely useful 100W solar light with commercial-grade construction.
