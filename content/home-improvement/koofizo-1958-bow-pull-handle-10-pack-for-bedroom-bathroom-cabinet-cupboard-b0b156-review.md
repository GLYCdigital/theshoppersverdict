## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has a few honest limitations. First, the finish options are limited to brushed nickel and matte black. If your kitchen is rocking oil-rubbed bronze or polished chrome, you're out of luck. Second, the bow shape is lovely until it snags — the curved profile can catch on apron strings, loose cardigan sleeves, or the drawstring of a hoodie in tight galley kitchens. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing if you're installing these in a high-traffic cooking zone. Third, the included screws are Phillips-head only. For most people, that's fine; if you prefer Torx for better torque, bring your own.
