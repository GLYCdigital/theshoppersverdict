## Real-World Performance: Where It Works and Where It Doesn't

Here's the thing about adhesive hooks: surface preparation is 90% of the battle. This hook uses a 3M-grade VHB-style adhesive strip, which is the good stuff — the same material that holds automotive trim to cars. On smooth, clean surfaces like ceramic tile, glass, or painted drywall, it genuinely holds. I tested it with a 4-pound cast iron skillet in a rental kitchen (yes, I risked my security deposit). ## Installation Tips That Actually Matter

- **Alcohol wipe first.** Even "clean" surfaces have oils. Use isopropyl alcohol, let it dry completely, *then* apply.
- **Press for 30 seconds.** Not 5. The adhesive needs pressure to bond at a molecular level.
- **Wait 24 hours before hanging anything.** The bond strengthens over time. Hang a lightweight item immediately, and you risk the hook pulling off.
- **Removal is easy.** Stretch the adhesive tab downward slowly — it releases cleanly without residue. This is a major advantage over permanent mounting options.
