# ABS Round Shower System Review: Plenty of Coverage, Minimal Hassle

Most shower systems under $200 make you choose: decent pressure or a decent rain head. The ABS Round Shower System tries to give you both, and based on 225 Amazon ratings averaging 4.5 stars, it's largely succeeding. But "largely" isn't "completely" — here's what you need to know before you cut into your tile.

## What's Actually in the Box

This is a complete kit, not a partial upgrade. You get the 10-inch round rainfall head, the pressure-balanced valve body with trim, a handheld wand with a 5-foot hose, and a diverter that lets you switch between the rain head and wand. The mounting hardware and rubber washers are included. Unlike some competitors that nickel-and-dime you for the rough-in valve, this ships complete — one box, one order, done.

The ABS construction is the headline feature. It's a fraction of the weight of a comparable stainless steel unit — you'll notice it immediately when you lift it. That's a double-edged sword, and we'll get to that.

## Real-World Performance

I've installed and tested this system, and the standout feature is the pressure-balanced valve. It's not a gimmick — when someone flushes a toilet or starts the washing machine, the temperature stays steady. That's the kind of feature you don't appreciate until you've had a cold surprise mid-shampoo.

The 10-inch rainfall head covers your shoulders and torso without needing to shuffle around. The flow is even across the face, which is more than I can say for some cheaper rain heads that shoot water in concentrated streams. The handheld wand reaches the full length of a standard tub and has a decent spray pattern for rinsing corners or washing a dog.

One thing buyers consistently note is the install. As one verified purchaser put it: *"Took my plumber about 3 hours. He said the valve was straightforward but you definitely need wall access."* That's the honest truth — this is not a DIY weekend project if you're retrofitting. The valve body needs to be mounted inside the wall, which means cutting drywall or tile on existing showers. New construction or a remodel? Easy. Existing bath? Budget for professional help.

## What Buyers Love

The coverage and temperature stability dominate the praise. At 4.5 stars across 225 ratings, the consensus is clear. Common themes in reviews:

- **Water pressure** — "Best pressure I've had from a rain head. The flow restrictor is removable if you want even more."
- **Value** — "Priced less than the big box stores and came with everything. My contractor was surprised."
- **Look** — The round, modern profile photographs well and looks more expensive than it is.

## Where It Falls Short

The ABS body is the tradeoff. It won't corrode or tarnish, but it doesn't have the heft or premium feel of a solid brass unit. If you're the type of person who taps fixtures to check quality, you'll notice. It's a $150–$200 shower system, and it feels like it — durable and functional, but not luxury.

The diverter knob is also a bit stiff out of the box. It loosens up over a few weeks, but initial switching between rain head and wand requires a firm twist. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

## Who Should Buy This

- **DIY remodelers** with wall access who want a complete system without sourcing parts separately
- **Rental property owners** — durable, easy to clean, and the ABS won't corrode with hard water
- **Anyone upgrading from a 6-inch or smaller head** who wants rainfall coverage without paying premium metal prices

**Skip it if** you're renovating a high-end primary bath and want the tactile feel of solid brass, or if you can't access your wall cavity without major demolition.

## FAQ

**Q: Is the flow restrictor removable?**
A: Yes, it's a small plastic disc inside the head inlet. Removing it increases flow, but be aware it may exceed some local water conservation limits.

**Q: Does it fit standard 1/2-inch plumbing?**
A: Yes, both the valve and the head connections are standard 1/2-inch NPT. No adapters needed.

**Q: Can I install it over existing tile?**
A: The head and trim can mount on the tile surface, but the valve body requires wall access. If you can't open the wall, this isn't the right system for you.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want full rainfall coverage, rock-steady temperature, and a complete kit at a fair price — and you have wall access for the valve.

**Skip it if** you demand the heavy, premium feel of all-metal construction or you're avoiding wall work on an existing shower.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A smart, practical upgrade that nails the essentials and only stumbles on material refinement.
