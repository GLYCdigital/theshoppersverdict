**The $38 Fix That Ends a Flushmate Nightmare**

If you own a toilet with a Flushmate pressure-assist system, you already know the telltale signs: the handle gets stiff, then sticky, then eventually snaps or refuses to actuate the flush. The FLUSHMATE AP300503 Universal Handle Replacement Kit is the OEM answer for the 503, 503H, and 503UH series. At 37.92, it's not the cheapest fix, but it's the one that actually works.

**What's Actually in the Box**

The kit includes the replacement handle assembly, the mounting nut, and the rubber gasket that seals against the tank. It's a complete package — you won't discover mid-install that you need a separate part. The handle itself is reinforced plastic with a chrome-like finish, and it matches the factory profile of the original. As shown in the product image, the design is straightforward: a single lever arm with the threaded shank that passes through the tank wall.

**Installation: Manageable, With One Caveat**

This is a doable DIY job in about 15 minutes if you're comfortable with basic plumbing. You'll shut off the water supply, drain the tank, remove the old handle from inside the tank (the retaining nut is on the interior), and swap in the new assembly. The threads are standard, and the gasket seats cleanly.

The catch: there are no instructions in the box. FLUSHMATE assumes you know what you're doing. If you've never opened a toilet tank, watch a quick video first. Several reviewers noted this — one buyer said, "Took a bit to figure out the orientation of the spring, but once I did, it fit perfectly." Another said, "Works like the original. The old one lasted 8 years, so this should be the last handle I buy."

**Performance and Build Quality**

Once installed, the handle actuates the flush valve with a satisfying, positive click. The throw is shorter than a standard gravity-flush toilet, which is normal for pressure-assist systems. The plastic construction feels dense and durable — not the flimsy, hollow feel of cheap aftermarket knobs. The mounting nut threads smoothly and holds the handle firmly against the tank with no wobble.

One thing buyers consistently praise: this is a genuine FLUSHMATE part, not a third-party approximation. That matters for a system that runs at 30-50 PSI. A poorly toleranced aftermarket handle can bind or leak. This one seals correctly and operates reliably.

**Where It Falls Short**

The price is the main gripe. Generic handles for Flushmate systems run 10-15 dollars, and this costs more than double. The OEM reliability is worth it for most, but if you're on a tight budget, you might gamble on the cheaper option.

Also, this is strictly a left-hand handle — the lever mounts on the left side of the tank. If your flush handle is on the right, you need the AP300504 instead. Double-check your orientation before ordering, or you'll be doing a return.

A: It fits Flushmate 503, 503H, and 503UH series toilets with left-hand handles. Check your tank for a model number sticker — if it's one of these, this is your part. Right-hand setups need the AP300504.
