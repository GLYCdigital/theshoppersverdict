Here’s the thing about cabinet hardware: it’s the jewelry of your kitchen. You can spend thousands on quartz countertops and custom cabinetry, but if the pulls look cheap, the whole room reads cheap. The KOMOTIA KO1805 pulls aim to fix that without the boutique price tag. After digging through 235 buyer ratings and the specs, here’s the real story.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the pulls themselves, mounting screws, and a paper drilling template. The KO1805 is a straight bar pull measuring 5 inches overall with a 3-inch (76.2mm) center-to-center screw spacing — the industry standard for most cabinet doors and drawer fronts. Each pull is machined from zinc alloy, not the hollow aluminum you often find at this price point. The brushed finish is uniform across the surface, with no visible weld seams or rough edges along the bar.

**Build Quality and Daily Use**

In practice, these feel denser than their price suggests. The zinc alloy gives them a satisfying weight when you grip them — no flex or wobble. The brushed texture does what it's supposed to: it hides the inevitable fingerprints and water spots that plague polished chrome and stainless pulls. One buyer noted, “I replaced all 24 pulls in my kitchen and they look like they cost three times more. The finish has held up through six months of daily cooking without tarnishing.” Another mentioned the screw alignment was spot-on: “Every single one lined up with the existing holes from my old pulls. Took me 20 minutes to swap the whole kitchen.”

The mounting screws are standard #8-32 thread, so if you lose one, any hardware store has replacements. The included template is a nice touch for new installations — it prevents the classic mistake of drilling one hole slightly higher than the other, which ruins the whole look.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let’s be honest about the downsides. The brushed finish is not coated with a clear lacquer. That means harsh chemical cleaners — especially anything with citrus or bleach — can dull the surface if you spray it directly and let it sit. Wipe it off promptly and you’ll be fine, but it’s worth knowing. One reviewer flagged this: “I used a degreaser on my cabinets and didn’t wipe the pulls dry right away. They developed a slight haze that I couldn’t fully buff out.” That’s a real tradeoff at this price point.

Also, these are simple mechanical pulls. No soft-close mechanism, no dampening. If you let your drawers slam, they’ll slam. That’s true of most bar pulls, but worth stating plainly.

**Who Should Buy These**

You’re the right buyer if you’re doing a full kitchen refresh and need 10, 20, or 30 pulls without blowing your budget. The uniform finish across multiple units is consistent — no mismatched batches. They’re also ideal if you’re replacing old hardware and the existing holes are at the standard 3-inch spacing. If you’re building new cabinets and want a modern, minimalist look that won’t date itself in five years, this is a safe choice.

Skip them if you need a specific vintage or ornate style, or if you insist on a sealed, lacquered finish for maximum chemical resistance. Also skip if you need a longer reach — the 3-inch spacing is compact, so large drawers might benefit from a 5-inch or 6-inch pull instead.

**FAQ**

*Q: Are these compatible with standard US cabinet holes?*  
Yes. They use 3-inch center-to-center spacing with #8-32 screws, which matches the vast majority of pre-drilled cabinet doors and drawer fronts.

*Q: How do they hold up in a humid environment like a bathroom?*  
The zinc alloy resists rust well. The main risk is the finish dulling from cleaning products, not humidity itself. Wipe them dry occasionally and they’ll hold up.

*Q: Do they come with all the screws needed?*  
Yes, each pull includes the mounting screws. No need to buy extra hardware unless you lose one.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a clean, modern look with solid metal construction at a reasonable price, and your cabinets use standard 3-inch spacing.

**Skip it if:** You need a sealed finish that withstands aggressive chemical cleaners, or you’re looking for a decorative, ornate style.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — These pulls deliver 90% of the look and feel of premium hardware at a fraction of the cost. The finish sensitivity to harsh cleaners is the only real mark against them, but for most kitchens, that’s a minor caveat.
