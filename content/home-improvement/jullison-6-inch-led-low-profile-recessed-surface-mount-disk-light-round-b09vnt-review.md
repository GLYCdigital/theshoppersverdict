If you're staring at a stack of 4-inch junction boxes in your ceiling and dreading the drywall repair that comes with typical can lights, this JULLISON 6-inch disk light is the shortcut you've been looking for. It's a flat, surface-mount wafer that covers the hole entirely — no recessed housing, no insulation contact issues, no trim ring to fuss with.

**What's Actually in the Box**

Each unit ships with the LED disk light itself, a mounting bracket, and a gasket. That's it. The bracket attaches to standard 4-inch octagonal or round junction boxes with the included hardware (wait, no — it doesn't include screws, so grab some #6-32 machine screws). The wiring is straightforward: black to hot, white to neutral, green to ground. The entire package is about 1.5 pounds, and the light body is a hair under 6 inches in diameter with a 0.5-inch profile once mounted. As shown in the product image, it's a clean, minimalist disc that sits nearly flush against the ceiling.

**Real-World Performance**

This thing puts out a legitimate 900 lumens at 13 watts. In a typical 10x10 bedroom, one unit provides comfortable general lighting; two units make the space feel bright and open. The 4000K color temperature is the sweet spot — not the sterile blue of 5000K, not the yellow warmth of 3000K. It's neutral daylight that works well in kitchens, hallways, and bathrooms. The CRI 90+ rating means colors don't look washed out; that matters if you're picking paint or checking makeup.

The surface mount design creates a tiny gap between the trim and ceiling — maybe 1/8 inch — which some folks notice. It's not a big deal visually, but if you're a perfectionist, you'll want to caulk the seam.

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

The overwhelming sentiment across 229 ratings is "easy install, looks great." One reviewer noted, "Replaced five old can lights in an afternoon. These are so much brighter and the color is perfect." Another mentioned, "The low profile is exactly what I needed for my basement ceiling — no light fixtures sticking down." The wet rating gets consistent shout-outs, with buyers installing them in shower enclosures and covered porches without issues.

**Where It Falls Short**

Here's the honest truth: this specific SKU is NOT dimmable. If you want dimming, you need the separate dimmable version — and they look nearly identical, so read the title carefully before adding to cart. Several reviews mention this confusion, which is a fair criticism of the product listing.

The lack of included wire nuts and screws is another minor annoyance. You'll likely have them in your toolbox, but if you're a first-time DIYer, you'll need a trip to the hardware store mid-project.

Finally, the plastic housing feels less premium than aluminum alternatives. It's well-built for the price, but if you tap it with a knuckle, it sounds hollow.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **DIY homeowners** replacing outdated flush-mount fixtures — this is a 20-minute swap per light
- **Basement or garage finishers** needing bright, code-compliant lighting on a budget
- **Bathroom remodelers** who want wet-rated lighting without the bulk of a shower trim

**Skip it if** you need dimming (get the dimmable SKU) or if you're installing in a tight space where the surface-mount profile would interfere with shelving.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Can this be used outdoors?**
A: Yes, it's wet-rated (IP65 equivalent). Covered porches and eaves are fine. Direct rain exposure is technically okay, but I'd keep it under an overhang for longevity.

**Q: Does it work with a standard dimmer switch?**
A: Only if you buy the dimmable version. This standard SKU will flicker or not dim at all.

**Q: What size junction box does it need?**
A: A standard 4-inch round or octagonal box. It won't cover a 4-inch square box without the adapter plate, so check your box type first.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want an affordable, bright, low-profile light that installs in minutes and looks clean in any room. At 4.6 stars, this is a solid performer that delivers exactly what it promises — mostly.

**Skip it if** dimming is non-negotiable, or you prefer the sturdier feel of metal housings.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Subtract half a star for the dimming SKU confusion and missing accessories. Everything else is genuinely good value for the price.
