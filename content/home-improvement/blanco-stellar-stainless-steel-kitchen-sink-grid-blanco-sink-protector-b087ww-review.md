# BLANCO STELLAR Sink Grid Review: The $100 Question

Here's the thing about stainless sink grids: they all look the same in the Amazon thumbnail. Then you get one in your hands, and within a week you know whether you wasted your money. I've tested budget grids that rusted at the welds and premium ones that wobbled like a card table. The BLANCO STELLAR grid sits in a different category entirely — but it demands a premium price to get there.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the grid itself and nothing else — no mounting hardware, no adhesive strips, no installation guide. That's fine, because installation is literally "drop it in the sink." What matters is what's in your hands: a grid constructed from 18/10 stainless steel with a brushed finish that matches BLANCO's sink line almost perfectly.

The wire gauge is noticeably thicker than the sub-$40 competitors. Each crossbar is welded at every junction — not just spot-welded at the perimeter like cheap grids. The perimeter frame is rounded, not squared off, which is critical because squared edges catch on sink walls and scratch the finish over time.

## Performance and Real-World Testing

I ran this grid through a month of daily use — cast iron skillets, heavy Dutch ovens, the occasional dropped glass (the grid saved the sink, not the glass). The verdict: it's genuinely stable. The four corner feet are hollow-backed rubber that compress slightly under weight, which keeps the grid from sliding even when you're scrubbing a stubborn pan with force.

Drainage is where this grid earns its keep. The wire spacing — roughly 1.5 inches between bars — allows water to flow through freely without letting silverware slip through. The grid sits about a half-inch above the sink floor, which means standing water doesn't pool under your dishes. That alone prevents the dreaded water spots and mineral buildup.

One detail I appreciate: the grid doesn't completely cover the sink bottom. There's a deliberate gap at the rear edge, which means the drain area stays clear and debris doesn't get trapped under the grid. Smart design that most third-party grids miss.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. At this price point, you're paying a 50-100% premium over generic grids that fit BLANCO sinks. The build quality justifies some of that, but not all of it — you're also paying for the brand name on the box.

The rubber feet are the weak point. They're held in by friction, and if you drag the grid across the sink to rinse it off, they can pop out. It's a minor annoyance, but at this price, you shouldn't have to re-seat feet every few weeks.

Also — this is not a universal product. It fits BLANCO STELLAR sinks specifically. Measure your sink interior dimensions before ordering. The product listing has a fit guide; use it.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You own a BLANCO STELLAR sink and want a grid that fits perfectly without measuring, adjusting, or compromising on finish.
