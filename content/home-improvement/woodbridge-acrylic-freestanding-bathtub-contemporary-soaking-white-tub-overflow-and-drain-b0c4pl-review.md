Let's cut through the marketing. A freestanding tub is the centerpiece of a bathroom remodel, but it's also the item most likely to disappoint if you buy on looks alone. The WOODBRIDGE Contemporary Soaking Tub has earned a 4.5-star average across 225 Amazon ratings, which tells me it's doing something right. After digging into the specs, owner feedback, and the realities of installing one of these, here's what you need to know before dropping the cash.

**What You Actually Get**

The box includes the tub itself, a chrome drain assembly, an overflow cover, and the adjustable feet. That's it. No faucet — you'll need to source a floor-mounted or wall-mounted filler separately. The tub measures 59 inches long, 30.5 inches wide, and 23.5 inches high, with a claimed 55-gallon capacity. That's a deep soak, not a standard shallow dip.

The construction is where WOODBRIDGE separates itself from the budget pack. This is a 3/8-inch thick acrylic shell reinforced with fiberglass. Cheaper tubs in this price range use thinner material that flexes when filled and can develop stress cracks within a year. The pre-leveled base with adjustable feet is a genuinely thoughtful touch — several reviewers noted they didn't need to shim at all, which is rare for a freestanding install.

**Real-World Performance**

Owners consistently praise the soaking depth. One reviewer put it plainly: "I'm 5'8\" and can fully submerge to my shoulders. The water stays hot for a solid 30-40 minutes." That thermal retention comes from the thick acrylic, which holds heat better than thinner shells or cast iron (though cast iron still wins on heat retention overall).

The interior is smooth and non-porous, so cleaning is straightforward with mild soap and water. The included drain is standard chrome with a pop-up stopper — nothing fancy, but it works and matches the overflow cover. As shown in the product image, the silhouette is a clean, modern oval that transitions well into the contemporary design of your bathroom. It looks far more expensive than it is.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Beyond the soaking depth, owners repeatedly mention the ease of installation. The tub comes with a template for the drain hole, which eliminates the "measure twice, drill once" anxiety. One buyer noted: "The pre-drilled overflow and drain holes lined up perfectly. I had it set in place in under an hour." Another praised the customer service: "My tub arrived with a small scratch. WOODBRIDGE sent a repair kit and a partial refund without any hassle."

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. At roughly 90 pounds, this tub is not a one-person job. You'll need help getting it into the bathroom and onto the adjustable feet. Plan for two strong adults and possibly a furniture dolly.

The white acrylic finish is unforgiving. Hard water stains and soap scum show quickly, especially if you have mineral-heavy water. You'll need to wipe it down after each use to keep it looking pristine. That's a maintenance reality, not a flaw specific to this unit, but worth knowing before you commit.

Some reviewers also noted the tub's size can be tight for taller users. At 59 inches, someone over 6 feet tall will have their legs bent at the knees. If you're tall, measure your bathroom and your body before ordering.

**Who Should Buy This**

This tub is for the homeowner doing a full master bath remodel who wants a freestanding look without paying $2,000+ for a designer name. It's ideal for anyone who values a deep, hot soak — this is a soaking tub, not a whirlpool. If you want jets, look elsewhere. It's also a solid choice if you're confident doing your own installation or have a contractor who's comfortable with plumbing.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this tub come with a faucet?**
A: No. You'll need to purchase a freestanding or wall-mounted filler separately. Budget $150-400 depending on the finish and style you choose.

**Q: How much water does it hold, and will my water heater keep up?**
A: It holds about 55 gallons. For a comfortable hot soak, you'll need roughly 35-40 gallons of hot water — which means a 50-gallon water heater will work, but a 40-gallon model will run out before the tub is full.

**Q: Can I install this myself?**
A: If you're comfortable with basic plumbing (connecting the drain and overflow), yes. The tub arrives pre-leveled and the drain template is accurate. The heavy part is moving the 90-pound tub into place — get help.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a deep, contemporary soaking tub that performs like a premium model at a fraction of the price, and you're ready to handle the weight and the water-spot maintenance.

**Skip it if:** You're over 6 feet tall, you want jet features, or you're looking for a low-maintenance fixture that can go weeks without wiping down.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The WOODBRIDGE Contemporary Soaking Tub delivers on the essentials: deep soak, solid construction, and an elegant look. The maintenance and weight are real tradeoffs, but for the price, this is one of the best freestanding values on Amazon right now.
