## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Beech is the right wood for this job. It's hard enough to hold a tight joint without crushing, but it has enough give to compress slightly when you clamp. The result: joints that lock up solid without you needing to wrestle the pieces together.

The critical thing about DOMINO tenons is the compressed center section. Festool designs these with a slightly narrower middle that allows the tenon to expand when glued, pulling the joint tight. The factory milling on these is clean — no fuzzy edges, no inconsistent widths. One reviewer noted, "Every single tenon is exactly the same size. No sorting, no rejects." That's the QC you're paying for.

They fit the DOMINO cutters precisely. Not loose, not tight — just right. You'll feel the difference if you've ever used cheaper generic tenons that rattle in the mortise or require a mallet to seat.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the drawbacks.

**Price per tenon is steep.** You're paying a premium for the Festool name and the consistent milling. If you're building cabinets by the dozen, generic tenons will save you money — just be prepared to sort through them for the occasional reject.

**The 10x50 size is missing.** For heavy structural work like table legs or large frame-and-panel doors, you'll need to buy the larger tenons separately. This set covers light-to-medium joinery well, but it's not a one-stop shop.

**The case isn't dust-proof.** It's a simple hinged box, not a sealed container. If you leave it open in the shop, sawdust gets in. Minor issue, but worth knowing.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these work with the Festool DF 500 and DF 700?**
A: The 4mm through 8mm tenons work with the DF 500. The DF 700 uses larger sizes (8x80, 10x50, etc.), so this set is really aimed at DF 500 owners. Check your machine's cutter sizes before ordering.

**Q: Can I use these with other domino-style joiners?**
A: The geometry is designed for Festool DOMINO cutters. Some third-party machines use similar dimensions, but there's no guarantee of a perfect fit. Stick with Festool cutters for best results.

**Q: Are these pre-glued or loose?**
A: Loose. You'll apply wood glue to the tenon and the mortise during assembly. The compressed center section expands when glue is applied, which is what creates the strong joint.
