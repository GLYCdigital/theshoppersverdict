**The short version:** The Mlambert Motion Sensor LED Night Light is the kind of product you don't think about until 2 a.m., when you're stumbling to the bathroom and don't want to blind yourself or wake your partner. It does that job well. But it's not the do-everything light some listings imply, and knowing its limits before you buy saves disappointment.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the light unit itself, a separate magnetic mounting plate with 3M adhesive backing, and a USB-C charging cable. No wall adapter — you'll use your phone charger or a USB port. The light body is compact, roughly the size of a hockey puck, and the magnetic connection is strong enough that you can yank the light off its base without pulling the plate off the wall. That's a thoughtful design touch: charge the light at your desk, snap it back, no re-leveling required.

**Real-world performance**

The motion sensor is the headline feature, and it works as advertised within its limits. It picks up movement at about 8–10 feet in a hallway, with a roughly 120-degree detection cone. In a narrow hallway or closet, that's plenty. In a corner of a large room, you'll walk halfway to it before it wakes up. The light itself puts out a warm 3000K glow — enough to see where you're going, not enough to read by. That's by design, but worth knowing if you're considering it for a desk or workspace.

Battery life is the standout. On the default motion-activated mode with moderate use (a dozen triggers per night), I got about six weeks between charges. The USB-C port means topping up takes under two hours. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"I've had it for three months and charged it twice. The magnetic mount is the reason I bought it, and it's better than I expected."* That matches my experience.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The magnetic mount is the recurring highlight in customer feedback. People use it in stairwells, under cabinets, inside pantries, and along baseboards — places where a permanent hardwired light isn't practical. The three-mode switch (off / motion / always-on) also gets consistent positive mentions, since the always-on mode doubles as a dim emergency light during power outages.

**Where it falls short**

The motion sensor has a noticeable delay — about half a second from detection to full brightness. That's normal for this price range, not a flaw, but if you're moving quickly in a dark hallway, you'll notice it. The adhesive plate also leaves a sticky residue if you remove it from painted drywall; use a bit of rubbing alcohol and it comes off clean, but it's not residue-free like some competing products claim.

The biggest limitation is brightness. At roughly 20 lumens, this is a navigation light, not a task light. If you need to read a label in a dark closet, you'll find yourself squinting. Buy it for wayfinding, not for detail work.

**Who should buy this**

- **Parents of toddlers** who need a soft night light in a nursery or hallway that doesn't require a dimmer switch
- **Renters** who can't hardwire lights and want something removable without leaving holes
- **Anyone with a dark stairwell or hallway** between bedroom and bathroom
- **Campers or RV owners** who want a rechargeable, portable light that doesn't depend on wall outlets

**Skip it if** you need bright task lighting, or if your hallway is longer than 15 feet — you'll need two units to cover the full length.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does the battery last on a single charge?**  
A: In motion-activated mode with typical overnight use (10–15 triggers), expect 4–6 weeks. Always-on mode drops that to roughly 8–10 hours.

**Q: Can I mount it outdoors?**  
A: No. It's not weather-sealed, and the adhesive won't hold up to moisture or temperature extremes. Keep it indoors.

**Q: Does it work through the magnetic base, or does the light need to face open air?**  
A: The sensor is on the front face of the light, so it needs a clear line of sight. Mounting it behind a shelf edge or under a deep cabinet lip will reduce detection range significantly.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, rechargeable motion light for hallways, closets, stairwells, or anywhere you need soft illumination without wiring. The magnetic mount alone justifies the price.

**Skip it if** you need bright task lighting, long-range detection, or a permanent hardwired solution.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A well-executed product with a genuinely useful design feature (the magnetic base) that most competitors don't offer at this price. The dim output and modest sensor range keep it from a perfect score, but for its intended purpose — safe nighttime navigation — it's hard to beat.
