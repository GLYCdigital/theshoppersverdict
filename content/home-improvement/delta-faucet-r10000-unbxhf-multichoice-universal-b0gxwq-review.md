If you're staring at a bare rough-in valve sticking out of your shower wall, you already know the drill: the hard part is over, and now you just need the trim. The Delta Faucet R10000-UNBXHF MultiChoice Universal is the piece that finishes the job. With a 4.0-star average across 200 ratings, it's a solid performer—but it's not without quirks. Here's what you need to know before you buy.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the handle, the escutcheon (the cover plate that hides the ugly hole in your tile), and the necessary mounting hardware. That's it. No cartridge, no valve body. As one buyer noted, "I assumed this was the whole valve assembly—it's just the trim. Make sure you have the rough-in valve already installed." That's the single biggest source of confusion in the reviews, so read carefully.

The finish is polished chrome, and the handle is a classic lever style. It's a clean, modern look that won't clash with most bathroom decor. The escutcheon is generously sized, which helps if your tile cutout is slightly oversized—always a plus during renovations.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

Delta's MultiChoice line is designed around a simple premise: one rough-in valve, multiple trim options. This universal trim is built to match that valve, and the construction feels solid. The handle is zinc alloy with a brass stem, and the chrome plating is thick enough to resist the water spotting that plagues cheaper finishes. In daily use, the lever moves smoothly with a positive stop—no mushy feel, no wobble.

One thing reviewers consistently mention is how easy the installation is. Since the rough-in valve is already set, you're just sliding the escutcheon over the body, threading the handle onto the stem, and tightening a set screw. Most DIYers can finish in under 15 minutes with a Phillips screwdriver and an Allen wrench.

## What Buyers Praise

The universal compatibility is the star here. "I replaced an old Delta trim that had cracked and this fit perfectly on the existing valve," one reviewer wrote. "No re-plumbing, no drywall repair." That sentiment shows up again and again—people buying this as a replacement for a worn-out trim on an existing MultiChoice valve are overwhelmingly satisfied.

The included hardware also earns points. Delta ships it with the right screws and the Allen key, so you're not hunting through your toolbox mid-install. And the chrome finish holds up well; multiple buyers mention it still looks new after a year or more of daily showers.

## Where It Falls Short

The set screw is the weak point. Several reviewers report stripping it if they apply too much torque. The fix is simple—use a light touch and a drop of thread locker—but it's worth knowing before you crank on it. One frustrated buyer said, "The screw stripped on the first install. Delta sent a replacement, but it was still annoying."

Also, double-check your valve version. This trim only works with Delta MultiChoice rough-in valves (model R10000 series). If you have an older Delta valve from the pre-1993 era, it won't fit without a valve replacement. Measure and verify before you order.

## Who Should Buy This

This is for the homeowner who already has a Delta MultiChoice valve in the wall and needs a clean, reliable trim. It's also a smart buy for anyone renovating who's installing a new Delta rough-in and wants a no-fuss finish. If you're starting from scratch with no valve at all, skip this and buy the full valve-and-trim kit instead.

Skip it if you need a pressure-balancing valve with an integrated handle, or if you're working with a non-Delta rough-in. This is a trim-only solution, and it won't save you if your plumbing is incompatible.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this include the cartridge?**  
No. It's just the handle, escutcheon, and mounting screws. The cartridge and valve body are sold separately—make sure you have the rough-in installed.

**Will it fit a Delta valve from the 1990s?**  
Only if it's a MultiChoice R10000 series. Older Delta valves use a different mounting pattern and won't work. Check the model number on your valve body before ordering.

**Can I install it without removing the old trim?**  
No, you'll need to remove the existing handle and escutcheon first. It's a straightforward process—just unscrew the old handle and pull the cover plate off.
