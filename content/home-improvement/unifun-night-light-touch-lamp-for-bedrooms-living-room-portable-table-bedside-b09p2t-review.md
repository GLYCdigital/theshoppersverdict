Let's be direct: most cheap night lights are either blindingly bright or uselessly dim. The UNIFUN Touch Lamp sits somewhere in the middle, and for most people, that's exactly the sweet spot. After digging through 220 ratings and the specs, here's what actually matters before you buy.

**What you're actually getting**

The UNIFUN is a compact, cordless touch lamp measuring roughly 4.5 inches tall with a weighted base that keeps it planted. It's not a full bedside reading lamp — it's a glow light. The built-in rechargeable battery means no cords cluttering your nightstand, and the frosted dome diffuses light evenly so there's no harsh hotspot.

The touch controls are the headline feature: tap once for low, again for medium, a third time for high, and a fourth tap shuts it off. It's that simple. No buttons to hunt for at 2 AM when you're half-asleep.

**How it performs in real use**

The 3000K warm color temperature is the smartest design choice here. It's similar to what you'd get from a dimmed incandescent bulb — gentle on melatonin production, so it won't wreck your sleep cycle if you use it before bed. At the lowest setting, it's bright enough to navigate a dark room without stubbing your toe. On high, it's adequate for reading, though it won't replace a dedicated task lamp.

One thing buyers consistently mention is the portability. As one reviewer put it, "I take this camping and it's perfect for the tent — no cords, just tap and it lights up." Another noted, "Great for power outages. I charged it once and forgot about it for two weeks."

That last point matters. The battery holds a charge well in standby, so it's genuinely useful as an emergency light. Just don't expect marathon runtime — at full brightness, you're looking at roughly 4-6 hours per charge.

**Where it stumbles**

The touch sensor has a quirk. It's responsive — almost too responsive. Several users report accidentally cycling through brightness levels just by brushing the lamp while reaching for something on the nightstand. You'll learn to grab it from the base, but it's an adjustment.

The micro-USB charging port is the other annoyance. In 2026, that's a dated choice. You'll need to keep a legacy cable around or buy an adapter, which feels unnecessarily inconvenient for a product released in this era.

Battery life at higher settings is also pedestrian. Heavy users — those who run it on high for hours nightly — will be charging every two or three days. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing.

**Who should buy this**

Buy it if you want a soft, portable light for the nightstand, a nursery, a hallway, or a camping kit. It's also a solid pick for anyone who hates the harsh blue-white glow of phone screens at night.

Skip it if you need a proper reading lamp with focused light, or if you're the type who wants USB-C everything. Also skip if you have pets that might knock it over — it's weighted, but not indestructible.

**FAQ**

**How long does the battery last on a single charge?**
At the lowest brightness setting, you'll get roughly 20+ hours. On high, expect 4-6 hours. Real-world use falls somewhere in between.

**Can you use it while charging?**
Yes, it works plugged in, though the charging cable is short — about 3 feet — so plan your outlet placement accordingly.

**Is the light warm or cool?**
Warm. It's rated at 3000K, similar to soft white bulbs. No harsh blue light here.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a versatile, cordless glow light that works at home and on the go. **Skip it if** you need a full-function reading lamp or refuse to keep a micro-USB cable around.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A genuinely useful little lamp with minor design flaws that don't outweigh its practicality. For the price, it's a smart buy for anyone who's tired of phone screens as their only source of midnight light.
