## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest catch: these bulbs absolutely require an **electronic transformer**. If your home still runs on a magnetic transformer from the halogen era, you'll get visible flicker that's genuinely headache-inducing. Check your transformer before ordering. Also, these are non-dimmable — if your track lighting uses dimmer switches, look elsewhere. Finally, the 400-lumen output is fine for accent and task lighting, but don't expect to replace 50W halogens illuminating a whole kitchen island with these alone.
