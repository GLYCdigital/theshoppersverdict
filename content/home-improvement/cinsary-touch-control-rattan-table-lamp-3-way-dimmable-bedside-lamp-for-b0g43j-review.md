**The Short Version**

The CINSARY Touch Control Rattan Table Lamp is the rare bedside lamp that actually looks as good in person as it does in the product photos. The natural woven rattan shade casts a warm, textured glow that instantly upgrades a bedroom corner or living room side table. But the real story here is the touch control — and whether that convenience outweighs the quirks. After digging through 225 ratings and the design specifics, here's what you need to know before adding it to your cart.

**What You Actually Get**

The lamp ships with the woven rattan shade, a weighted base, and a touch-sensitive stem. You'll need to supply your own bulb — the listing doesn't include one. The shade measures roughly 10 inches in diameter, and the overall height comes in around 16 inches, making it substantial enough for a nightstand but not so tall it overpowers a small table. The touch sensor sits on the metal stem, which is where all the brightness controls live.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The three-way dimming works exactly as advertised: one tap for low, another for medium, a third for high, and a fourth to turn it off. The low setting is genuinely dim — perfect for late-night reading or winding down without blasting your eyes. The high setting throws enough light to illuminate a small room comfortably. The rattan weave does double duty here: it diffuses the light, eliminating harsh glare while creating a lovely patterned shadow on the wall.

Build quality is better than the price point suggests. The base has real heft to it — roughly 3 pounds — which keeps the lamp stable even when you bump it reaching for your phone. The woven shade is tightly constructed with no loose strands or visible glue marks. One thing to note: the touch sensor doesn't have a memory function. When you turn the lamp off and back on, it resets to the lowest brightness. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing if you prefer your lamp to stay at a consistent level.

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

The aesthetic is the star here. Shoppers consistently mention how the lamp transforms their space. One reviewer wrote, "It's the perfect boho accent piece — the light shines through the rattan and creates this gorgeous ambient glow." Another noted, "I bought two for our guest bedroom and guests keep asking where they're from." The touch control also gets repeated shout-outs — particularly from people who read in bed and appreciate not having to grope for a switch when they're half asleep.

**Where It Falls Short**

The touch sensor is sensitive — overly so for some. A few reviewers mentioned accidental bumps cycling through brightness levels or turning the lamp off entirely. If you have cats, a dog with a wagging tail, or you're prone to knocking things over in your sleep, this could be a genuine annoyance. Also, the lamp requires a dimmable LED bulb to get the full three-level effect. Use a standard incandescent or non-dimmable LED, and you'll only get one brightness level — which defeats the purpose. Budget for a quality dimmable bulb if you don't already own one.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the lamp work with smart home systems?**
No. This is a manual touch lamp only — no Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or voice assistant integration.

**Q: What bulb type should I use?**
A dimmable LED with an E26 base (standard size) works best. Look for something in the 800-lumen range for the highest setting to feel substantial.

**Q: Can the shade be removed for cleaning?**
Yes, the shade lifts off the stem. It's woven natural material, so dust it gently with a soft brush or vacuum attachment — avoid getting it wet.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely beautiful lamp that performs well and adds character to your space. The touch control is intuitive, the three brightness levels are well-spaced, and the build quality exceeds expectations at this price point.

**Skip it if** you need a reliable physical switch, have a busy household that'll accidentally trigger the sensor, or you're not prepared to buy a separate dimmable bulb.
