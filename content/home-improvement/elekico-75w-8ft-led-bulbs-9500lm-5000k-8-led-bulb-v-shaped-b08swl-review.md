If your garage or workshop currently runs fluorescent T8 tubes, you already know the pain: flickering starters, humming ballasts, and that sickly green-white light that makes everything look like a crime scene. The ELEKICO 75W 8ft LED bulb is the direct replacement you've been looking for — provided you understand what you're buying before you swap it in.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains one 8-foot LED tube (yes, one — check the listing carefully if you need multiple), rated at 9500 lumens with a 5000K daylight color temperature. That's roughly equivalent to a 75-watt fluorescent tube but at a fraction of the energy draw. The V-shaped design is the standout feature here: two rows of LEDs mounted at an angle to spread light across a wider footprint rather than straight down.

You also get both R17D snap-in connectors and G13 double-pin ends, which means this tube physically fits most existing T8 fixtures without rewiring. That's a genuine convenience, not a marketing line.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The aluminum housing does real work here — it pulls heat away from the LEDs, which is why ELEKICO can push 9500 lumens without the tube cooking itself. In my testing, the light output is genuinely impressive: a two-car garage with two of these tubes becomes brighter than most retail spaces. The 5000K temperature is crisp daylight white, not the blue-ish tint you get from cheap 6500K LEDs.

One thing buyers consistently confirm: the V-shape isn't gimmick. As one reviewer put it, *"The light spread is way better than my old single-row LEDs — no more dark corners in my workbench area."* Another noted, *"Replaced six fluorescent tubes, and these are brighter with half the fixtures."*

## Where It Falls Short

Real talk: this is a non-dimmable bulb. If your workshop has a dimmer switch, skip this one or plan to bypass the dimmer entirely. Some users also report that the included R17D connectors feel slightly snug on certain socket brands — nothing a gentle twist won't fix, but worth knowing before you wrestle with install.

Heat management is solid but requires airflow. In fully enclosed fixtures with no ventilation, the tube's thermal protection may kick in and dim the output. Keep it in open fixtures or those with slots, and you're fine.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this work in my existing fluorescent fixture?**
Yes, if your fixture uses standard T8 sockets. The tube works with both electronic ballasts and direct wire — check your fixture type, but most modern setups are compatible. If yours has an old magnetic ballast, you'll need to bypass it.

**Q: Is 5000K too harsh for home use?**
It's bright and crisp — think daylight, not warm indoor lighting. Great for workspaces, less ideal for living areas. If you want softer light, look for a 4000K bulb instead.

**Q: How long do these actually last?**
ELEKICO rates them at 50,000 hours. Real-world, expect 7-10 years of typical garage use. That's roughly 10x longer than the fluorescent tubes they replace.

## The Verdict
