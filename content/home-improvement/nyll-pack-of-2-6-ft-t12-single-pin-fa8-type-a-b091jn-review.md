## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest part. These are Type A tubes, which means they're picky about what they plug into. If your fixture has an electronic ballast, these won't work—you need a magnetic ballast. If you're not sure what you have, that's a problem, because installing these into the wrong fixture can damage the tube or the ballast.

Some buyers also reported that the single-pin design can be loose in certain older tombstones. One reviewer noted: *"Had to wedge a small piece of cardboard under one end to keep it seated properly."* That's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you commit. If your sockets are worn, budget for a few dollars in shims or replacement tombstones.

## Who Should Buy This

This is for the homeowner with a T12 shop light or basement fixture who wants LED performance without hiring an electrician. It's also a smart pick for landlords or property managers with multiple T12 fixtures who want to upgrade without rewiring every unit.

Skip it if you have T8 fixtures, electronic ballasts, or if you're planning a full LED retrofit with ballast bypass. In those cases, you need Type B tubes or a different approach entirely.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these work with electronic ballasts?**
A: No. These are Type A tubes designed for magnetic (inductive) T12 ballasts. Installing them on an electronic ballast will likely damage the tube. Check your ballast label before buying.

**Q: Can I use these in a T8 fixture?**
A: Not safely. T8 fixtures have different spacing and typically use electronic ballasts. Stick with T12 fixtures for these tubes.

**Q: How much energy do they save compared to fluorescent?**
A: Most buyers report roughly 40-50% lower energy use per fixture, depending on the wattage of your old tubes. The instant-on feature also means no wasted energy during warm-up.
