Let me be blunt: buying recessed lighting is usually a game of compromises. You want color temperature control, but that means paying more per fixture. You want a low profile, but that usually means sacrificing brightness. The NUWATT 12-pack of 4-inch square LED recessed lights tries to sidestep all those tradeoffs. For the most part, it succeeds.

## What You Actually Get

Open the box and you'll find 12 complete fixtures — driver, trim, and all — plus a template for cutting your ceiling holes. Each unit measures 4 inches square with a slim 0.5-inch profile. That's the headline feature: these are wafer-thin, IC-rated housings with a built-in junction box. Translation: you can put them directly against insulation (that's what IC means), and you don't need a separate can or housing. For a remodel project where you're cutting holes in an existing ceiling, this is the easiest path forward.

The integrated LED driver is rated for 120V hardwiring, and the included E26 connector lets you wire them daisy-chain style. Each fixture draws 12 watts and puts out 800 lumens — that's roughly equivalent to a 60-watt incandescent bulb.

## The 5CCT Selectable Feature — The Real Story

The "5CCT" in the name means each fixture has a small toggle switch on the driver that lets you choose from five color temperatures: 2700K (warm), 3000K (soft white), 3500K (neutral), 4000K (cool white), and 5000K (daylight). This is genuinely useful — you can set your kitchen at 4000K for task lighting and your living room at 2700K for evening ambiance, all from the same box.

But here's the honest tradeoff: that switch lives on the driver, which means once the fixture is installed in a finished ceiling, changing the color temperature requires pulling the entire unit out. Set your color temps *before* you install, and double-check them. One angry reviewer noted: "I wish I'd set all of them before I put them in — now I have to climb back into the attic to change one."

## Real-World Performance

At 800 lumens per fixture, these are bright for a 4-inch light. In a typical 10x10 room, four of these spaced evenly will give you solid general illumination without hot spots. The square trim throws a clean, even beam pattern with no visible glare at standard viewing angles — the diffuser does its job.

The color rendering is solid for the price point. At 2700K, the light is warm and flattering; at 5000K, it's crisp and clinical, good for garages or workspaces. Skin tones render naturally at the middle temps, which is what you want in living spaces.

Buyers consistently praise the ease of installation. One verified purchaser wrote: "Took me about 20 minutes to wire all 12. The spring clips hold tight, and the junction box is big enough to work in." That matches my assessment — the integrated junction box has room for wire nuts, and the push-in connectors speed things up.

## Where It Falls Short

- **Dimmer compatibility is finicky.** These are LED fixtures, and if you pair them with an old trailing-edge dimmer, you'll get flicker at low levels. You need a compatible LED dimmer — budget for that if you want dimming.
- **No color memory.** If you lose power, the fixture resets to whatever color temp is set on the switch — which is fine, but don't expect smart-home integration out of the box.
- **The 4-inch size is smaller than it sounds.** If you're replacing 6-inch cans, you'll notice the difference in light spread. These work best in clusters or smaller rooms, not as single fixtures in large open areas.

## Who Should Buy These

- **DIY remodelers** replacing old cans or adding new lighting to a finished ceiling. The wafer design and included template make this a weekend project.
- **Homeowners who want color flexibility** without committing to one temperature. Set the switch, install, forget.
- **Anyone doing a modern or minimalist interior** — the square trim genuinely looks better than round fixtures in contemporary spaces.

Skip them if you need a single large light in a big room, or if you're married to smart-home integration without doing extra research on compatible dimmers.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these be used in bathrooms or outdoor covered areas?**
A: They're wet-rated for damp locations, so bathrooms and covered patios are fine. Not for direct outdoor exposure.

**Q: Do I need a junction box?**
A: No — each fixture has a built-in junction box rated for direct ceiling contact. That's the whole point of the IC housing.

**Q: What's the actual cutout size?**
A: You'll cut a 4.25-inch hole. The included template matches exactly, so use it.
