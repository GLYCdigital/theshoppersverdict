Got a product you think deserves a verdict? Found something in one of our reviews that doesn't sit right? Just want to say hi?

We read every message. We can't promise a personal reply to every single one, but if you're suggesting a product for review — tell us what it is and why it matters. We'll add it to our queue.

**Product review requests:** Include the exact product name and Amazon ASIN (if you have it). The more specific, the faster we can get to it.

**General inquiries:** Partnerships, press, or anything else — we're all ears.

**No sponsored review requests, please.** We don't accept payment for coverage. Every product on our site earns its place through customer data, not marketing budgets.

Drop us a line at **[gemma@glycdigital.com](mailto:gemma@glycdigital.com)** — we read every message.
