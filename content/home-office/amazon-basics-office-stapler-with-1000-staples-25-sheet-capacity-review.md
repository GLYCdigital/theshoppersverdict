Sometimes the best office upgrade isn't a $1,000 standing desk — it's a stapler that just works. The **Amazon Basics Office Stapler** is a $6.31 workhorse with 52,636 reviews and a 4.6/5 rating. We dug through the feedback to see if a budget stapler can actually be great.

## The Good

### It Does Exactly What You Need — No Drama

Strip away the marketing and a stapler has one job: put staples through paper without jamming. The Amazon Basics unit nails this. Reviewers report "jam-free" operation consistently. One user who bought it to replace a stolen office stapler said it "does what it's supposed to do and does it well." Another managed to staple 29 sheets — four more than the rated 25-sheet capacity — and reported "the crimp on the last sheet was still sufficient to hold them together."

### The Non-Slip Base Is a Small Touch That Matters

The rubberized base keeps the stapler planted when you press down. No sliding across the desk mid-staple, no chasing it around. For a $6 product, this is the kind of detail that makes it feel designed rather than just manufactured.

### Easy Loading, Easy Living

Top-loading the staples is straightforward — press the button, the tray slides out, drop in a strip, close it. No fighting with springs or figuring out which way the staples go. Multiple reviewers mention how painless refills are compared to older staplers they've owned.

### Value That's Almost Absurd

$6.31 gets you the stapler AND 1,000 staples. At that price, you could buy one for every room in the house and still spend less than a single "premium" stapler. One reviewer bought a 3-pack and called it "essential office workhorse, great value."

## The Not-So-Good

### Plastic Build — Manage Expectations

The body is all plastic. It doesn't feel premium, and if you're stapling hundreds of documents daily in a mailroom, it won't hold up like a metal Swingline. But for home offices, dorm rooms, and light business use, the plastic construction is perfectly adequate. The hinge is the most common long-term wear point — after several years of heavy daily use, it may loosen.

### No Extras

You get a stapler and staples. No staple remover, no anvil adjustment for pinning vs. stapling. It's a basic stapler at a basic price — that's the whole proposition. If you need features, spend more.

## Who Should Buy It

Anyone who needs a reliable stapler without overthinking it. **Home offices, students, teachers, small businesses** — this is your stapler. At this price, it's also perfect as a backup, a desk drawer spare, or a "starter" stapler for kids doing homework.

## Who Should Skip It

- High-volume commercial environments (mailrooms, print shops) — invest in a metal stapler
- Anyone who needs tacking/pinning functionality
- People who want a stapler that feels premium to hold

## Bottom Line

The Amazon Basics Office Stapler is the definition of "good enough" — which, for a stapler, is actually the highest compliment. It staples reliably, doesn't jam, loads easily, and costs less than a lunch. With a 4.6/5 across 52,000+ reviews, the verdict is overwhelming: **this is the stapler most people should buy**. Don't overthink it.
