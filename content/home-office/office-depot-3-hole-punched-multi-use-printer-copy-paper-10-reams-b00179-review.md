But if you’re the person who prints meeting agendas, student handouts, or client proposals, the **Office Depot® 3-Hole Punched Multi-Use Paper** deserves more than a glance. It solves one of the most annoying office tasks — manually punching three holes into every single page so it fits a binder. This paper arrives pre-punched, which sounds trivial until you’ve spent 20 minutes feeding sheets through a hole punch that jams halfway through a 50-page packet.

This is the 10-ream case (5,000 sheets total), and it’s priced to compete with generic store brands while offering the convenience of pre-drilled holes. At 20 lb. weight and 92 brightness, it’s not premium paper — but it’s not trying to be. It’s a workhorse.

## The Catch — Honest Tradeoffs

Here’s where I’ll temper expectations. The pre-punched holes mean paper dust. When you punch 5,000 sheets, tiny paper fibers are inevitable. Over time, this dust can accumulate on printer rollers, potentially causing feed issues. A regular cleaning cycle with a lint-free cloth keeps things running smooth, but it’s an extra maintenance step you don’t get with unpunched paper.

Second, this is not archival-grade paper. If you’re storing documents for decades — think legal files or historical records — the brightness will fade and the paper can yellow. It’s fine for 3–5 year storage, but don’t use it for permanent records.

Finally, the packaging is basic. The reams lack the reinforced handles or resealable bags you see on premium brands like Hammermill. If you stack these in a damp garage or closet, the paper can absorb moisture and warp. Store them flat in a dry area.
