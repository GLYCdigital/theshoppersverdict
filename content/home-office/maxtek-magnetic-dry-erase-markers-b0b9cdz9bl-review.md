Whiteboard markers are one of those things where you can spend $2 or $20 and get roughly the same experience. The maxtek Magnetic Dry Erase Markers land at the $6.99 sweet spot — cheap enough to replace without guilt, good enough to actually enjoy using.

**The magnetic cap is a genuinely useful feature.** Stick the marker to your whiteboard when you're mid-thought and don't want to fumble for a tray. They stay put. Not "might slide off eventually" — they actually grip the board. For brainstorming sessions and meetings, this is one of those small conveniences you quickly miss when it's gone.

**The built-in eraser on every cap** means you can fix a typo or clean up a small area without grabbing the full eraser. It's not going to clear an entire whiteboard — the eraser surface is too small for that — but for day-to-day corrections, it's perfect.

**The colors are vibrant, mostly.** Black, blue, green, purple, orange — all crisp and readable from across the room. The one exception is red, which multiple reviewers note comes out closer to pink. If bold red is critical for your color coding, that's a real issue.

**Low odor is a bigger deal than it sounds.** If you've spent time in a meeting room with cheap dry erase markers, you know the headache. These are genuinely low-odor, making them fine for enclosed office spaces and home offices alike.

**The fine tip writes cleanly.** Fine enough for detailed notes, charts, and calendar entries. Some colors run slightly thicker than others, but it's within normal variance.

At $6.99 for 12 markers across 10 colors, this is straightforward value. The magnetic caps and built-in erasers add genuine utility that more expensive sets often skip. Just budget for a separate bold red marker if that color matters to you.
