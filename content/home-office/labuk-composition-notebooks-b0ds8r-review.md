Let's be blunt: most composition notebooks are disposable junk. Thin paper that ghosts, covers that curl, and binding that cracks by page 50. The LABUK Composition Notebooks are not that. At 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, they're quietly becoming the default pick for people who actually write daily. Here's the full breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

Each notebook measures 9.75" x 7.5" — the standard composition size — with 80 sheets of 100gsm paper. That paper weight is the headline feature. For context, standard composition books run 50-60gsm. The jump to 100gsm means this notebook is built for wetter inks and heavier-handed writers. The sewn binding is the other differentiator; it's stitched, not glued, so pages don't detach.

The poly cover is textured and flexible, not that cheap glossy cardstock that peels after a week in a backpack. You get one notebook per purchase, not a multi-pack.

## Real-World Performance

I tested the 100gsm paper with a Pilot G2 (gel), a TWSBI Eco (fountain pen, medium nib), and a Sharpie pen. No bleed-through on any of them. The gel ink sat on the surface without ghosting to the reverse side. The fountain pen laid down a wet line with zero feathering. That's genuinely impressive at this price.

The sewn binding does what it's supposed to — the notebook lays flat at any page, and the pages stay put when you fold it back. The paper has a slight tooth to it. It's not smooth like Rhodia, but it's not rough like newsprint. If you use ultra-fine 0.38mm rollerballs, you'll feel a bit of drag. That's the tradeoff for paper that handles ink so well.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The recurring praise centers on durability and ink compatibility. One verified buyer wrote, "I've been through dozens of cheap notebooks. This is the first one where my fountain pen doesn't soak through to the next page." Another noted, "The cover has survived three months in my work bag without bending." The consistent theme is that this notebook behaves like a premium product at a budget price.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest complaints. First, 80 sheets is lighter than the standard 100-sheet composition book. If you burn through pages quickly, you'll replace it sooner. Second, the paper's texture isn't for everyone — fountain pen users will love it, but fans of ultra-smooth paper like Moleskine might find it slightly rough. There's also no page numbering or index, so if you need structured organization, you'll need to add your own system.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Fountain pen users** who are tired of feathering and bleed-through on cheap paper — this is your budget fix
- **Students** who need a notebook that survives a semester in a backpack without falling apart
- **Journalers** who want a durable, lay-flat option that won't ghost or bleed

Skip it if you need 100+ sheets per notebook, or if you exclusively use ultra-fine pens and hate any paper tooth.

## FAQ

**Will my Sharpie bleed through?**
No. The 100gsm paper handles heavy markers well. Alcohol-based markers won't bleed, though you'll see slight shadowing if you press hard.

**Does it lay completely flat?**
Yes. The sewn binding allows it to open fully flat from the first page to the last. No spine cracking or pages popping out.

**Is the paper fountain-pen friendly?**
Yes — this is the notebook's strongest suit. Medium and broad nibs work great. No feathering, no bleed-through. Dry time is slightly longer than on smooth paper, so give it a second before closing.
