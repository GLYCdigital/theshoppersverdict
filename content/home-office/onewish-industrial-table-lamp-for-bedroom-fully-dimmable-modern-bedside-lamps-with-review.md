**The $40 bedside lamp that actually gets dimming right**

Most lamps in this price range give you a cheap inline switch and a bulb that's either blindingly bright or uselessly dim. The ONEWISH industrial table lamp does something different: it puts a real dimming knob right on the lamp body, and it works smoothly across the entire range. At $39.99, that alone makes it worth a look. But let's dig into whether the rest of the lamp holds up.

## What's in the box

You get the lamp (fully assembled), one 2700K LED bulb, and a small instruction card. That's it. No tools, no confusing multi-step assembly. The included bulb is a welcome touch — many competitors in this price range make you buy a separate bulb that costs nearly half the lamp's price.

The lamp itself stands about 15 inches tall with a 6-inch diameter shade. The base is a solid metal disc that's noticeably heavier than it looks — that's a good thing, because it keeps the lamp stable on uneven nightstands.

## Build quality and real-world performance

The all-metal construction (steel body, aluminum shade) gives this lamp a substantial feel that belies its price. The matte black finish resists fingerprints well, and the industrial design — think exposed hardware and clean angles — fits both modern lofts and traditional bedrooms.

The dimming knob is the star here. It's a stepless rotary dial that takes the light from a soft 10% glow to full brightness with no visible flicker. I tested it with a phone camera (which usually catches LED flicker) and saw nothing. That's rare at this price point.

The 2700K warm light is genuinely warm — not the harsh yellow-white you get from cheap "warm white" bulbs. For reading, I found the sweet spot around 60% brightness, which provides enough light without eye strain. At full brightness, it easily illuminates a 10x10-foot room.

## Where it falls short

The dimming knob is small — about the size of a dime — and it's stiff when new. It loosens up after a week of use, but expect to use two hands initially. Left-handed users may find the knob position (right side of the lamp body) awkward.

The 5-foot power cord is shorter than I'd like. Most bedside tables with outlets behind them are fine, but if your outlet is more than 4 feet from where the lamp sits, you'll need an extension cord.

Finally, the shade is open at the top. This creates nice ambient light on the ceiling, but it also means the bulb is visible from above. If you're sensitive to exposed bulbs, this could be a dealbreaker.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the dimming work with any bulb, or only the included one?**
A: The dimming is controlled by the lamp's built-in rotary switch, so it works with any standard A19 bulb. However, the included 2700K LED is specifically matched to the lamp's dimming range. If you swap in a different LED, test it immediately — some cheap LEDs flicker when dimmed.

**Q: Is the lamp heavy enough to stay put with kids around?**
A: Yes. The weighted base is solid metal and the lamp has a low center of gravity. It's not going to tip over unless deliberately knocked hard. That said, the open-top shade means it's not ideal for a toddler's room where small objects might get thrown.

**Q: Can this be used as a primary room light?**
A: At full brightness, it covers roughly a 10x10-foot area adequately. For larger rooms, it works better as a task or accent light. The 2700K temperature makes it a poor choice for tasks needing bright, cool light (like detailed crafts).
