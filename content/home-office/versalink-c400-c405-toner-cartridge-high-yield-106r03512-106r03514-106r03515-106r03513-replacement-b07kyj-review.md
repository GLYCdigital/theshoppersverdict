## What You're Actually Getting

This is the genuine Xerox 106R03512 black toner cartridge, rated for approximately 10,000 pages at 5% coverage. That's the high-yield version — the standard-yield 106R03510 only manages around 2,400 pages by comparison. The cartridge arrives sealed in the familiar Xerox packaging with the chip already installed. No assembly, no shaking required — it drops into the C400 or C405 and the printer recognizes it immediately.

For reference, this is the black cartridge only. If you're running a full-color VersaLink C405, you'll need to separately source the cyan (106R03513), magenta (106R03514), and yellow (106R03515) cartridges. This listing is strictly the black.

The print quality here is what you'd expect from genuine Xerox toner: sharp, dense blacks with no banding or streaking on standard office paper. Text at 10-point and below stays crisp, and graphics hold up well for internal documents and client-facing materials alike. The 10,000-page yield is the real selling point. For a busy office printing 50-100 pages daily, that's three to six months between swaps. The chip tracks usage accurately, and the printer gives you plenty of warning before the cartridge truly runs dry — no sudden mid-job failures.
