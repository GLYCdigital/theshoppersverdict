EOOUT packs a complete desk setup: a stapler with 1,000 staples, tape dispenser with two rolls, scissors, 20 push pins, 20 paper clips, a 40-page memo pad, and a pen holder. ## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The stapler is the standout. It punches through 20 sheets without the jam issues that plague budget staples. The reinforced spring mechanism gives it a satisfying, solid action. One buyer noted, "I've had mine for six months of daily use and it still staples like day one." That tracks with the materials — the metal body has real weight, and the gold coating is baked on, not painted over.

The tape dispenser works smoothly, with a serrated edge that cuts cleanly. The scissors handle standard office cutting — paper, tape, packaging — with decent grip. The memo pad uses decent-weight paper that doesn't bleed through with ballpoint pens.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the tradeoffs. The tape dispenser is lighter than premium brands like Scotch or Muji. If you tug tape at an angle, it'll slide across your desk. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

The scissors are serviceable, not exceptional. For detailed craft work or heavy cutting, you'll want a dedicated pair. They're fine for general office use — think cutting envelopes, not precision paper cutting.

The push pins have plastic heads that feel slightly less premium than the rest of the set. They work fine, but if you're buying this for the aesthetic, the pins are the weakest link visually.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're setting up a home office, dorm desk, or gifting for a graduate or new professional.
