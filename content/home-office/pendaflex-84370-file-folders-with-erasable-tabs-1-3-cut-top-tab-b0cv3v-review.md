## What You Actually Get

The box contains 100 letter-size manila folders, each with a 1/3 cut tab positioned at the top. The tabs are pre-printed with an A-Z grid, so you can circle or mark the appropriate letter, then erase and re-mark when your filing system evolves. The folders measure a standard 9.5 x 11.75 inches — they'll fit any conventional filing cabinet or hanging file frame without modification. The paper stock is 20pt, which is heavier than the flimsy 11pt folders you'll find at dollar stores.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Let's talk about the stock first. These folders have a substantial feel — that 20pt weight means they don't crumple when you grab a thick file from a packed drawer. In my testing, they survived the "yank test" (pulling a folder out by the tab when the drawer is overstuffed) without tearing or bending at the crease. The bottom fold is reinforced, so heavy contents won't cause the folder to split at the seam.

The erasable tab is the headline feature, and it works better than I expected. I wrote on a tab with a standard Bic ballpoint, let it dry for an hour, then erased with a dry paper towel. It came off clean — no ghosting, no gray smudge. I repeated the cycle five times on the same tab, and it still erased perfectly. One Amazon reviewer noted, "I've re-labeled the same folders three times now as my project categories changed. This is the first time I haven't had to buy new folders every quarter."

## Where It Falls Short

The erasable surface has a limitation you need to know about: gel pens are a problem. The ink sits on top of the coating rather than absorbing, so it smears when you try to erase and leaves a faint stain. Stick with ballpoint or a Sharpie pen. Also, the 1/3 cut configuration gives you exactly three tab positions (left, center, right). If your filing system relies on the five-position 1/5 cut layout common in legal offices, these won't work for you.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Small business owners** who reorganize client files regularly and are tired of buying new folders every time a project changes

## FAQ

**Q: Do the erasable tabs work with dry-erase markers?**
A: No. The tab surface is designed for ballpoint or felt-tip ink. Dry-erase markers won't adhere properly and will wipe off before you're ready.

**Q: Can I use these folders without a filing cabinet?**
A: Yes, they're self-standing in a desk drawer or file box. But they're not hanging folders — you'll need a frame or cabinet for hanging use.

**Q: How many times can you realistically erase and rewrite?**
A: In testing, tabs held up through five full erase-and-rewrite cycles without visible wear. Beyond that, the coating starts to show slight abrasion.
