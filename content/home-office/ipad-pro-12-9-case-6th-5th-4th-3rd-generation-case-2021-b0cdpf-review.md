## What You Actually Get

The package includes the case itself, a foldable front cover, and an integrated Apple Pencil holder. No screen protector, no extras — just the shell. The material is a matte-finish polyurethane with a microfiber lining inside. The front cover folds into a triangular stand that offers multiple viewing angles, from a low typing position to a nearly upright display mode.

As shown in the product image, the Pencil holder sits on the right edge in landscape orientation. It's a slot-style holster rather than a flap, which means your Pencil stays put but you can't charge it wirelessly while it's in the holder — you'll need to pop it out and attach it to the magnetic strip on the iPad itself.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The universal fit claim is legitimate. The case uses a precisely molded tray with cutouts that align correctly across the 3rd, 4th, 5th, and 6th generation 12.9-inch models. Camera lens openings align, speaker grilles line up, and the button covers register presses without excessive force. For a case spanning four hardware revisions, that's genuinely impressive engineering.

The stand mechanism deserves specific praise. Unlike flimsy origami-style covers that collapse under pressure, this one locks into place with a firm click. You can set it on a desk, coffee table, or even your lap and take notes without it folding flat. That's the difference between a case you'll actually use and one that ends up in a drawer.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has two notable tradeoffs. First, the bulk. This isn't a slim folio — it adds substantial thickness to an already large tablet. If you use your iPad primarily as a reader or carry it in a tight sleeve, this case will make that frustrating.

Second, the lack of a built-in screen protector. The front cover offers basic protection, but it doesn't seal the edges. A drop on a corner could expose the display to direct impact. For full protection, budget for a separate tempered glass screen protector.

## Who Should Buy This

This case is ideal for three specific buyers: students who need a sturdy stand for note-taking during lectures, professionals who use their iPad as a secondary display in meetings, and anyone who plans to keep their iPad Pro for multiple years and doesn't want to rebuy a case with each upgrade.

Skip it if you've already invested in a Magic Keyboard or prefer a minimal, ultra-thin profile. This case prioritizes protection and function over elegance.

## FAQ

**Will this case fit the new M4 iPad Pro?**
No. This case is designed for the 3rd through 6th generation 12.9-inch models (2018-2022). The M4 (7th gen) uses a different form factor and won't fit.

**Can you charge the Apple Pencil while it's in the holder?**
No. The holder is a passive slot. You must remove the Pencil and attach it magnetically to the iPad's edge for charging.

**Does the stand have a typing angle?**
Yes. The first fold position creates a low angle suitable for on-screen typing, while the second position is better for viewing and note-taking.
