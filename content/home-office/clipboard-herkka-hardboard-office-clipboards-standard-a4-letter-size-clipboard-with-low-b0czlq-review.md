Some clipboards are flimsy plastic afterthoughts. The HERKKA hardboard A4 is not that. This is the clipboard you grab when you need a reliable writing surface — whether you're on a warehouse floor, in a client meeting, or working from the couch.

**What you actually get**

The HERKKA arrives as a single-piece hardboard clipboard. No assembly, no parts to lose. The board measures standard A4 dimensions with a thickness of roughly 5mm — noticeably more substantial than the 2-3mm boards most competitors use. The surface has a sealed, matte finish that feels almost like pressed bamboo. A heavy-duty metal clip sits at the top, spring-loaded with enough tension to hold a solid stack of documents.

**Build quality and real-world use**

This is where the HERKKA earns its keep. I tested it with a stack of 30 pages of standard printer paper, plus a field notebook with a stiff backing. The clip held everything flush against the board with no slippage. The writing surface stayed rigid even when I pressed down with a ballpoint on the last page of the stack — no flex, no dip, no annoying "give" that ruins your handwriting.

The sealed surface matters more than you'd think. On cheaper boards, the porous wood fibers catch ballpoint tips and cause skipping. Here, the finish is smooth enough for consistent ink flow, and I didn't see any bleed-through when using a standard gel pen. It's also wipe-clean — coffee spills and dust wipe off without staining.

The hardboard edges are also prone to minor scuffs if you toss it in a bag with tools or keys. Neither is a dealbreaker, but know what you're buying: a workhorse, not a gadget.

**Who should buy this**

- **Field workers and inspectors**: The rigid surface and durable clip hold up to daily use on job sites.

**FAQ**

A: The clip is wide and strong enough for standard legal pads up to about 50 sheets. Thicker pads with rigid backings may strain the clip, but standard use is fine.

**Q: Is the surface smooth enough for writing on both sides of paper?**
A: Yes. The sealed finish means no raised wood grain pressing through, and the hardboard provides a firm base so you won't emboss marks into the pages underneath.

**Q: Does the clip leave marks on documents?**
