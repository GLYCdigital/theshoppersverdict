**What you actually get**

The box contains 22 individual privacy shields made from corrugated plastic — the same material used for yard signs, not the thin folder-stock you might expect. Each divider stands tall enough to block a student's view of a neighbor's paper while still letting the teacher see over them during exams. **Real-world performance**

Here's where this pack separates itself from the cheap alternatives. The waterproof claim isn't marketing fluff — the plastic surface wipes clean with disinfectant wipes or hand sanitizer without warping, smearing, or degrading. In a post-pandemic classroom, that's non-negotiable. Teachers in the reviews repeatedly mention using them week after week, wiping down between classes, and still having intact dividers at year's end.

The durability comes from the material choice. Corrugated plastic flexes rather than cracks when a student accidentally knocks one over. It's lightweight enough for kids to handle independently but rigid enough to actually serve as a visual barrier. The 22-count means you can outfit a standard classroom with spares to spare — and several reviewers noted that's exactly why they chose this pack over smaller sets.

**What buyers consistently praise**

One teacher wrote, "These held up through an entire semester of weekly tests — no curling, no tears, no fading." Being honest: these are bare panels. There's no adhesive strip, no weighted base, and no fold-out stand. A few buyers mentioned creating their own tape or velcro solutions to keep them anchored. Also, because they ship flat and compressed, some units arrived with crease lines that needed a few days to relax into shape.

**Who should buy this**

This is a teacher's tool first. If you're running a classroom with 20+ students and need privacy shields that survive the school year, this 22-pack is the right call. If you only need two or three dividers for occasional use, you'd be better off with a smaller pack — 22 is a lot of plastic to store.
