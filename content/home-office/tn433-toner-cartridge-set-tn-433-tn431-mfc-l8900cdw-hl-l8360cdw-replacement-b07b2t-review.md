## What's actually in the box

You get four individually sealed cartridges: one TN433 black and three TN431 color units (cyan, magenta, yellow). ## Real-world performance

I ran these through a gauntlet: dense text documents, full-bleed color presentations, photo prints on glossy paper, and everyday mixed office work. The first thing that impressed me was the instant chip recognition. My MFC-L8900CDW fired up without a single "toner not detected" error—a common complaint with cheap compatibles.

Print quality holds its own against Brother OEM. Text is crisp with no feathering at 600 DPI. Color saturation is solid, though not quite as punchy as OEM in photo-heavy prints. Some buyers reported toner dust residue inside the packaging—not a defect, but messy if you're not careful during installation. And here's a gotcha: the listing title mentions TN431, but these are NOT compatible with single-cartridge TN431 models. This is for the TN433/TN431 multi-pack configuration only. Double-check your printer model before ordering.
