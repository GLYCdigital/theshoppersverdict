## What You Actually Get

The box contains the slant board itself (roughly 13 x 19 inches — fits standard letter-sized paper with room to spare), two dry-erase markers, a small eraser, and a clip mechanism built into the top edge. No assembly required — it arrives ready to use. The frame is ABS plastic with a textured whiteboard surface that's also magnetic, so you can pin notes, checklists, or reference sheets while you work. The base has a rubberized non-slip pad that grips smooth desks and tables firmly.

## Performance and Build Quality

The adjustable angle is the headline feature, and it works exactly as advertised. You can pivot the board from a relatively flat position up to roughly 45 degrees, locking it into place with a firm detent mechanism. At the steeper angles, your wrist stays neutral and your neck doesn't crane forward — I felt the difference within minutes during a focused writing session. The surface itself takes dry-erase markers cleanly, and the magnetic hold is strong enough to secure multiple sheets of standard printer paper without sagging.

The frame feels solid for the price point. It's not premium aluminum, but the ABS plastic has zero flex during use, and the hinge mechanism shows no looseness after weeks of angle adjustments. As shown in the product image, the board sits at a natural reading and writing angle that also works well for tablets or cookbooks if you want to repurpose it.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The ergonomic angle gets the most love in customer feedback. "My neck and shoulder pain improved within a week of switching to this board for daily journaling," one verified buyer noted. ## Where It Falls Short

Nothing's perfect, and this board has two real quirks. First, the whiteboard surface ghosts after repeated marker use. You'll need a proper dry-erase cleaner — not just the included eraser — to keep it looking fresh. Second, the clip pressure is serious. It holds paper firmly, but if you're working with delicate cardstock or thin paper, insert carefully or you'll end up with a visible crease. And a minor gripe: there's no storage for the markers or eraser, so they'll end up rolling around your desk unless you have a tray handy.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this board work with an iPad or tablet on it?**
A: Yes, the flat whiteboard surface accommodates tablets up to roughly 13 inches. The angle positions work well for both writing and touchscreen use. **Q: How do I clean the whiteboard surface properly?**
A: Use a dry-erase board cleaner or a vinegar-water solution. Standard erasers will remove fresh marks, but ghosting builds up over time. Avoid abrasive cleaners — they'll scratch the surface and ruin the writing experience.

**Q: Is the angle adjustment stable during active writing?**
A: Yes. The detent mechanism locks firmly at each angle position, and the non-slip base keeps the board planted even with heavy-handed writing. Multiple customer reviews confirm it doesn't wobble or collapse mid-sentence.
