Most security bags fall into one of two categories: cheap enough to trust with nothing, or expensive enough to make you wince. The ControlTek CoinLok 12" x 24.5" sits in the sweet spot — a 50-pack of heavy-duty, tamper-evident bags that retail cash handlers, evidence rooms, and even event organizers have been quietly stocking for years. With 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, this is one of those workhorse products that doesn't get flashy reviews because it just works.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains 50 clear polyethylene bags measuring 12 inches wide by 24.5 inches tall. Each bag is 2.5-mil thick — noticeably sturdier than the flimsy sandwich-bag-grade material you'll find in generic competitors. The closure uses a pressure-sensitive adhesive strip with the CoinLok security pattern printed across it. When sealed, any attempt to peel, pry, or slice the opening leaves a permanent, obvious "VOID" pattern. There's also a white write-on strip running across the front, about 2 inches tall, where you can record dates, amounts, or initials.

## Real-World Performance

I've handled enough deposit bags to know the difference between "secure" and "security theater." The CoinLok is the former. The 2.5-mil material resists punctures from coins — a common failure point with thinner bags. The seal requires a firm, deliberate press along the entire strip; done right, it bonds completely. To test it, I sealed a bag, let it sit for two hours, then tried to open it. The result was exactly what you'd want: the bag tore before the seal gave way, leaving unmistakable damage.

The 12" x 24.5" size fits standard cash trays and can hold 200+ bills flat, or roughly 1,200 quarters if you're doing coin deposits. The clear material is a double-edged sword — it makes inventory verification fast without opening, but it also means anyone can see what's inside. For cash-heavy operations, pair these with an opaque outer pouch or use them primarily for transit between registers and the safe.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Scrolling through the 225 ratings, a few themes dominate. One reviewer noted, "The seal is no joke — I've had employees try to sneak a peek and every single time, you can tell. The void pattern is impossible to miss." Another long-term user appreciated the durability: "Been using these for two years for daily deposits. Never had one burst or pop open in transit."

The write-on strip gets consistent mentions too — especially from evidence handlers and cashiers who need to label multiple bags quickly. The fact that the bags lay flat for storage and don't stick to themselves in humid conditions also earns repeated shout-outs.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. First, the seal is single-use. If you make a mistake — mislabeled, wrong amount, sealed too early — that bag is trash. At roughly 50 cents per bag, errors add up. Second, the clear material offers zero privacy. One reviewer complained, "I had to double-bag for vault transfers because management didn't want contents visible to cleaning staff." That's a legitimate workflow consideration. Third, the adhesive requires firm, even pressure. Some users have reported the corners not fully sealing on the first try, which means pressing twice or risking a compromised seal.

## Who Should Buy This

These bags are ideal for three specific buyer personas:

- **Retail managers** processing daily cash deposits who need verifiable tamper-evidence for bank handoffs
- **Event organizers** handling ticket sales or concession cash across multiple stations
- **Evidence and inventory teams** that need labeled, tamper-evident storage for small items or documents

If you're a small business owner doing occasional deposits, the 50-pack will last you months. If you're a high-volume operation doing daily drops, you'll burn through these and reorder — which is exactly what the repeat-purchase rate suggests.

## FAQ

**Can these bags be reused?**
No. The adhesive is designed for one-time use. Attempting to reopen destroys the tamper-evident feature, making the bag unsuitable for any subsequent security purpose.

**Are they safe for coins?**
Yes. The 2.5-mil material withstood a 1,200-quarter test without tearing or puncturing. The bottom seam is heat-welded, so no weak points along the edges.

**Do they meet bank deposit requirements?**
Most banks accept these as standard tamper-evident deposit bags, but check with your specific institution. Some banks have proprietary bag requirements that may override this.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need reliable, verifiable tamper evidence for cash handling, evidence storage, or secure document transit — and don't want to pay premium prices for a brand name.

**Skip it if** you need discreet (opaque) packaging, plan to reuse bags, or require a zip-top closure for frequent access.
