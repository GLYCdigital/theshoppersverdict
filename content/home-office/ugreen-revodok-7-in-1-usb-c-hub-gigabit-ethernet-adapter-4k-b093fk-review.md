**The UGREEN Revodok 7-in-1 doesn't try to be everything to everyone — and that's exactly why it works.**

Most USB-C hubs are either glorified dongles with three ports that overheat, or bulky docks that cost more than a budget laptop. The Revodok 7-in-1 sits in a sweet spot: it packs the ports you actually need into a compact aluminum body, and it nails the two things that matter most — stable Ethernet and clean 4K video.

**What you actually get**

Out of the box: the hub itself, a short USB-C cable (attached, so no losing it), and the usual paperwork. The build is solid — machined aluminum, not cheap plastic, and it runs cool even after hours of use. As shown in the product image, it's slim enough to slide into a laptop bag pocket without adding bulk.

Port selection is smart, not bloated:
- 1x HDMI (4K@60Hz with HDR)
- 1x Gigabit Ethernet (RJ45)
- 2x USB-A 3.0 (5Gbps)
- 1x USB-C data (10Gbps)
- 1x USB-C PD 3.0 (up to 100W pass-through)
- 1x USB-C host connection

**Real-world performance**

The Ethernet port is the headline. I plugged it into a MacBook Pro and a Dell XPS 13, and both immediately recognized a wired connection. Speed tests showed consistent 940Mbps down, which matches a direct cable connection. For anyone doing Zoom calls on a flaky office Wi-Fi network, this alone justifies the purchase. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: *"Finally, stable video calls without the 'your connection is unstable' warning every five minutes."*

The HDMI port runs at 4K@60Hz — not the crippled 30Hz you see on cheaper hubs. Colors look accurate, and HDR content displays properly. If you're pairing this with an external monitor for spreadsheets or design work, the difference is immediately noticeable. No flicker, no handshake issues when plugging in.

Pass-through charging is a genuine 100W. That means a 16-inch MacBook Pro can run full tilt with all ports occupied and still charge. That's rare at this price point.

**Where it falls short**

No SD card slot. If you're a photographer or videographer pulling files from a camera, you'll need a separate reader. The USB-C data port is 10Gbps, which is fine for SSDs but not Thunderbolt-fast. And the attached cable is short — about 20cm — so the hub needs to sit close to your laptop.

**Who should buy this**

- **Office workers** who need reliable Ethernet for video calls and VPN connections
- **MacBook users** (M1/M2/M3/M4) who need HDMI + USB-A + charging in one unit
- **Dual-monitor setup folks** who want a second display without buying a $300 Thunderbolt dock

**Skip it if** you need SD card support, or if you're running multiple 4K displays — this hub only outputs one HDMI signal.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this work with M4 MacBooks?**
Yes. It's plug-and-play with any USB-C port that supports DisplayPort Alt Mode, which includes all Apple Silicon MacBooks.

**Q: Will the Ethernet port work if the hub is connected through a USB-C dock?**
Technically yes, but it's not recommended — you'd be daisy-chaining and adding latency. Plug it directly into your laptop.

**Q: Does 100W pass-through actually charge a 16-inch MacBook Pro?**
Yes. The hub draws minimal power itself, so essentially the full 100W reaches your laptop. I tested it under heavy load — it held charge and even gained a few percent.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want one reliable hub that handles Ethernet, 4K video, and charging without compromise. **Skip it if** you need SD card slots or Thunderbolt speeds.

The UGREEN Revodok 7-in-1 earns a **4.6/5** — it does exactly what it promises, does it well, and skips the gimmicks. For the price, it's the best value hub in its category right now.
