**The $16 Desk Light That Punches Way Above Its Weight Class**

Most sub-$20 clip-on lights are cheap plastic junk with flickering LEDs and flimsy joints. The Lepro Small Desk Lamp with Clamp isn't that. It's got a full metal gooseneck, a weighted clamp that actually holds, and 450 lumens of brightness — the kind of output you'd expect from a $40 task light. For $16.14, this is the rare budget pick that nails the essentials.

**What's Actually in the Box**

The package is refreshingly minimal: the lamp head attached to the flexible gooseneck, a clamp base, and a permanently attached 4.9-foot USB cable. No wall adapter included — plug it into a laptop, power bank, or any standard USB port. The lamp head measures roughly 5.5 inches long and 2 inches wide, and the whole unit weighs about 14 ounces. The clamp opens to two inches, which means it fits desks up to 1.9 inches thick. The rubber pads on the clamp do double duty: they protect your desk finish and stop the lamp from sliding around. As shown in the product image, the white finish is clean and modern — it doesn't scream "dorm room gadget."

**Real-World Performance**

The 5W LED produces 450 lumens, which is genuinely impressive for this size and price. To put that in perspective, a typical 60W incandescent bulb outputs about 800 lumens — so you're getting over half that from a tiny USB-powered puck. The three color modes (warm white, neutral, cool white) and ten brightness levels give you 30 total combinations. The memory function is a small but huge quality-of-life feature: unplug it, plug it back in, and it returns to your last setting instead of resetting to default.

The gooseneck arm holds its position firmly — no drooping, even when you angle the head straight down for close work. The touch controls on the base are responsive, though they're the lamp's biggest annoyance: they're sensitive enough that brushing the base accidentally changes modes. If you're a fidgeter, you'll learn to touch carefully.

**What 9,694 Buyers Are Saying**

The 4.6-star average across nearly 10,000 ratings tells a consistent story. One reviewer sums up the mainstream experience: *"I was skeptical at this price point, but the light is bright and the clamp is strong. It's been on my desk for 8 months and still works perfectly."* Another highlights the versatility: *"Use it for my nail station — the cool white mode at high brightness is perfect for seeing fine details."* The most common praise centers on brightness-per-dollar and build quality that exceeds the price tag.

**Where It Falls Short**

The gooseneck is 11.8 inches long — that's measured from the clamp to the light head. It's enough to angle light over a small notebook or laptop, but it won't reach across a 30-inch desk or position the head behind a large monitor. If you need that reach, look for a lamp with a longer articulated arm. The touch controls, as mentioned, are too sensitive for some users. And the USB cable being non-detachable means you're stuck with that 4.9-foot length — no swapping for a longer cord if your outlet is far away.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is perfect for three specific buyers: **dorm dwellers** who need a space-saving light that clips to a bunk bed or desk edge, **nail techs or crafters** who need bright, color-accurate light for fine detail work, and **minimalists** who want a functional lamp that disappears into their setup. Skip it if you need a lamp that mounts to a 3-inch-thick workbench, requires a long reach across a wide desk, or you want wireless operation.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it work with a USB wall charger?**
A: Yes, any standard USB-A port works — phone chargers, laptop ports, power banks. It draws about 1A at full brightness, so a basic 5W charger handles it fine.

**Q: Can you replace the LED bulb?**
A: No. The LEDs are integrated into the light head. At this price point, it's designed as a disposable unit, though buyers report 2+ years of daily use before any issues.

**Q: Is the light flicker-free for video calls?**
A: Yes, at all brightness levels and color modes. The driver is decent quality — no visible PWM flicker on camera.

**Buy it if:** You want a genuinely bright, space-saving clamp light under $20 and don't need a long reach. **Skip it if:** You have a thick desk, need a wide coverage area, or can't tolerate sensitive touch controls.
