## Real-World Performance

I tested this in an HL-L2320D that's been running for two years. Installation took under a minute: open the front flap, pull the drum unit, slide out the old cartridge, shake the new one gently, and click it in. No chip errors, no "Toner Life End" warnings that shouldn't be there. The printer recognized it immediately.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has two honest tradeoffs.

First, it's third-party. Brother's official warranty technically doesn't cover damage caused by non-OEM consumables. In practice, Brother rarely denies claims over toner — but if you're a stickler for warranty purity, that's the risk you take.

Second, packaging consistency. A handful of reviews (maybe 5-8%) mention toner powder loose in the box or a slightly loose seal on the foil tab. This isn't a dealbreaker — shaking the cartridge before install fixes most of it — but it's worth knowing before you open it over a white carpet.
