When black dies, you're shelling out for three colors you don't need yet. That's not a flaw in print quality — it's a flaw in purchasing flexibility.

There's also a small compatibility risk. E-Z Ink states these work with a specific list of Canon Pixma models. If you have a newer printer that received a firmware update after these cartridges were manufactured, there's a small chance the chip won't be recognized. E-Z Ink's customer service handles replacements, but the hassle is real.
