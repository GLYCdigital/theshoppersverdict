Most wall calendars treat Julian dates like an afterthought — a tiny number in the corner you squint at. The XISIDO Wall Calendar flips that. Every single day cell has the Julian date printed prominently right next to the standard date. For anyone in logistics, inventory management, food service, or military-adjacent work, that's not a nice-to-have. That's the entire point of the product.

## What you actually get

The pack contains two full-year wall calendars, each measuring 15.7 x 11.4 inches — large enough to read from across a warehouse aisle but not so big they dominate your wall. The layout runs July 2026 through December 2027, which is a smart 18-month span if you're planning into next year.

Each month gets a full two-page spread. The left page has the main grid; the right page provides a notes section plus the next month's overview. ## Real-world performance

I put this calendar through what I'd call "operations room" conditions — multiple people writing in it daily, erasing mistakes, marking shifts. The paper held up. No bleed-through with gel pens. ## Where it falls short

Two legitimate gaps. First, no pre-printed holidays or moon phases. If you rely on those, you're writing them in yourself. For a product aimed at logistics and operations, that's a minor miss. Second, the day cells aren't oversized. If your workflow requires dense daily logging, look at a larger-format planner.

Also worth noting: this is a 2026-2027 calendar. If you're buying mid-2026, you get solid value. If you're late in the year, half the calendar is already consumed. Check the date range before you order.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the Julian date match the calendar year or the fiscal year?**
A: It matches the standard calendar year. January 1 is Julian day 001, December 31 is Julian day 365 (or 366 in leap years). Standard convention, no surprises.

**Q: Can I write on it with dry-erase markers?**
A: Dry-erase works but can leave ghosting if left on too long. Gel pens and ballpoints are the sweet spot. The 120gsm paper handles both well.
