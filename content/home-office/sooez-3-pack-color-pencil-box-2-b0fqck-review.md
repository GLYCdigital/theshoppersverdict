If you've ever watched a kid (or an adult) dump 40 loose colored pencils onto a kitchen table just to find the one they need, you already know the pain this product solves. The **Sooez 3 Pack Color Pencil Box 2** is a simple, no-nonsense storage solution that does exactly what it promises — and at a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, it's clearly resonating with buyers.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes **three identical pencil cases**, each measuring roughly 7.5 x 3.5 x 1.5 inches. That's enough interior depth for standard-length pencils (up to 7 inches) plus a bit of headroom for erasers or sharpeners. The case is made from clear polypropylene — not the flimsy acetate you see on cheap dollar-store boxes. The lid is hinged along the long edge and secures with a single snap latch.

As shown in the product image, each box holds **24 to 30 standard colored pencils** snugly in molded slots. There are no loose dividers to fiddle with — the slots are built into the base, which means zero setup time.

## Real-World Performance

I've tested similar cases that crack at the hinge within a month. The Sooez feels different. The plastic has a slight flex to it — enough to absorb drops, not so much that it feels rubbery. The hinge is molded as a living hinge, which is the same design used in industrial parts bins, and it held up through repeated open-close cycles in my testing.

The latch is the one thing that takes getting used to. It's **deliberately stiff** on the first several uses — one Amazon reviewer noted, "My 7-year-old couldn't open it alone for the first week." That's a safety feature as much as a flaw; it prevents accidental spills in backpacks. After a few dozen opens, it loosens to a satisfying click.

One detail that impressed me: the **clear lid isn't just for looks**. Multiple reviewers mentioned using these on art class days, and the transparency means kids can grab the exact pencil they need without unclipping the latch. That's a small win that adds up over a school year.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring theme in the 235 ratings is durability relative to price. One verified purchase wrote, "Bought these for my daughter's art class. We're three months in and they still look brand new — no cracks, no broken hinges." Another parent highlighted the multi-pack value: "Perfect for splitting between home and school. One stays in the backpack, one on the desk, one in the car."

Teachers also seem to like them. One reviewer mentioned using them to organize classroom supplies by color family, and the stacking design (the flat top lets them stack securely) makes storage on a shelf effortless.

## Where It Falls Short

The **fixed slots are a double-edged sword**. If you use jumbo crayons, gel pens, or pencils with thick erasers, they won't fit in the molded compartments. You can lay them flat on top of the slots, but then the lid doesn't close flush. If your toolkit includes anything beyond standard #2 or colored pencils, measure your supplies before buying.

The latch stiffness mentioned earlier is worth repeating — it's genuinely difficult for small children to open at first. And while the plastic is sturdy, it's not indestructible. Dropping it on concrete from waist height could crack the lid.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Parents of school-age kids** — the 3-pack covers home, school, and backup.
- **Art teachers** — stackable, clear, and durable enough for classroom rotation.
- **Adult hobbyists** — perfect for organizing a specific color family or keeping a travel set separate.

Skip it if you exclusively use oversized art supplies, or if you need a case that holds more than 30 pencils per box.

## FAQ

**Q: Will these fit standard Crayola colored pencils?**
A: Yes. Standard Crayola, Prang, and comparable brands (about 7mm diameter, 7 inches long) fit perfectly in the molded slots.

**Q: Can I remove the dividers to store larger items?**

**Q: Are the three boxes identical?**
