## What You Actually Get

As shown in the product image, this isn't a single-color brick. You're getting 100 letter-size folders (11.75″ x 9.5″) spread across 10 distinct colors — 10 folders per color. That's enough to set up a full color-coding system for clients, projects, tax years, or employees without ordering multiple boxes. Each folder has a standard 1/3-cut tab on the right side, which is the industry default for most filing systems.

The paper stock is a 11-point manila-style weight, which puts it in the "will survive daily use" category rather than the "flimsy office supply that collapses after three weeks" category. The bottom fold is scored properly, so folders sit flat in the drawer without that annoying tenting at the base.

## Performance in Real-World Use

The durability here is the headline. The reinforced top tab — the part that gets gripped, pulled, and thumbed a hundred times a week — holds up noticeably better than generic store-brand folders. One buyer noted, *"I've been through three boxes of cheap folders this year that all tore at the tab. The color differentiation is functional but honest about its limits. Think "office pastel" rather than "neon highlighter." For color-coding purposes, they're distinct enough at a glance, which is what matters.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has two honest weaknesses.

**The colors are muted.** If you're expecting eye-popping, Instagram-ready hues, you'll be slightly disappointed. They're closer to a faded crayon palette than a vibrant one. This doesn't affect function, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

**Occasional manufacturing quirks.** A small number of reviewers reported tabs that were slightly off-center or die-cuts that didn't align perfectly. It's not a widespread defect — probably one or two folders per box — but if you're a perfectionist about label placement, it could bother you. Most users just wrote it off as a minor nuisance.
