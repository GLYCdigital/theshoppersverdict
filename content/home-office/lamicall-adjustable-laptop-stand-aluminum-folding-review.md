If you've been hunching over a laptop at a coffee shop desk and wondering why your neck hurts, the Lamicall Adjustable Laptop Stand is the $36 fix you've been looking for.

Rated 4.8 out of 5 stars from over 10,000 Amazon reviews, this aluminum riser solves the fundamental ergonomic problem of laptop use: the screen and keyboard are attached to each other, so if you raise the screen to eye level, your arms have to reach up to type. Lamicall's solution is straightforward — raise the screen, then use a separate keyboard and mouse at desk height.

One reviewer who bought the stand after a physical therapist suggested it says: *"I was having neck problems. She suggested I get a laptop stand so I wouldn't be sitting looking down, shoulders hunched, etc."* After a week of use, the stand worked as intended — though they also ended up buying a Magic Keyboard, a mouse, and reading glasses. Make of that what you will.

The aluminum build genuinely feels premium. It's not one of those flimsy wire racks that bends under pressure. The vented base lets your laptop breathe, and the rubber grips prevent sliding. The hinges are stiff by design — which is good for stability (it won't sag over time) but means you'll need some muscle to shift positions.

The 10,000+ reviews reveal one consistent debate: is it wobbly? Most users say no — it's rock solid. One user tried to make it jiggle on purpose and couldn't. But a reviewer with a 17-inch HP touchscreen says typing causes noticeable wobble. The difference seems to be laptop size and typing force.

**Who should buy it:** WFH workers dealing with neck strain. Anyone using a laptop as a desktop replacement with external peripherals. People who want a stand that looks as good as it works.

**Who should skip it:** People who plan to type directly on their laptop keyboard while using the stand (the angle makes this awkward). Anyone on a tight budget.
