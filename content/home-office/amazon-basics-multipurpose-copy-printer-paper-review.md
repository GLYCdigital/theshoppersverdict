## Verdict

Amazon Basics Multipurpose Copy Paper costs $6.97 per ream. It feeds smoothly, prints crisply, and jams are virtually nonexistent across hundreds of thousands of reams sold. With a staggering 4.8-star average from over 219,000 reviews, this is one of the highest-rated products on Amazon — and it's just paper.

## Who Is This For?

Anyone who needs standard 20 lb, 92 brightness letter-size paper for everyday printing. This includes home offices, schools, small businesses, and general household printing. It's designed for inkjet and laser printers, copiers, and fax machines. If you're printing documents, forms, schoolwork, or anything that doesn't require heavy ink coverage, this is your paper.

## The Good

**Reliability is the theme.** The word "reliable" appears in almost every positive review. One reviewer who's used multiple reams across different printers says it "feeds consistently without jamming across multiple printers." Another calls it "good, dependable paper" that "works perfectly — no jams, feeds smoothly, and prints come out clean and sharp." At 219,000+ reviews, this consistency is the product's defining characteristic.

**Value is undeniable.** At $6.97 per ream (500 sheets), you're paying about 1.4 cents per sheet. That's below most store-brand options and well below premium paper brands. Multiple reviewers note that they've switched from more expensive brands and get identical or better results.

**Brightness is genuinely good.** The 92 brightness rating produces clean, crisp text that looks professional. One reviewer says, "The brightness level is good and text prints crisp and clear." Another calls it "bright white, smooth" and says it "works perfectly in my printer." You're not getting photo-lab brightness, but for office documents, it's more than adequate.

**Consistent quality across batches.** This is a surprisingly important point for a commodity product. Multiple long-term buyers confirm that every pack they've purchased has been consistent in brightness, thickness, and feeding behavior. No variation between batches.

## The Not-So-Good

**It's 20 lb paper.** This is standard office weight. If you're printing high-resolution photos, double-sided presentations, or folding heavy-ink documents, you'll want something heavier (24 lb or 28 lb). But for 99% of everyday printing, 20 lb is the right choice.

**Rare quality artifacts.** A very small number of reviews mention finding paper dust or minor debris in the ream. This is extremely rare given the volume sold, but worth noting if you have a finicky high-end printer that's sensitive to dust.

## Bottom Line

Amazon Basics Multipurpose Copy Paper is the platonic ideal of a commodity product done right. It's affordable, reliable, and performs exactly as expected every single time. At 1.4 cents per sheet with a 4.8-star rating from over 219,000 reviews, this is as close to a universal recommendation as any product gets. **An essential buy for any home office.**
