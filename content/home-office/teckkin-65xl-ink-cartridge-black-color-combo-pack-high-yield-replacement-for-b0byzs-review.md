**The 65XL cartridge is the workhorse of HP's mid-range inkjet lineup, and TECKKIN's high-yield combo pack targets the two biggest pain points: cost and frequency of replacement.**

If you're burning through OEM cartridges every few weeks, the math on third-party options starts to look very attractive. This combo pack delivers a black and a tri-color cartridge with genuinely higher page yields, but the real question is whether the savings justify any tradeoffs in print quality or reliability. Here's what 220 ratings at 4.4 stars actually tell you — and what they don't.

## What's in the box

The package contains one black 65XL and one tri-color (cyan/magenta/yellow) 65XL cartridge. Both are high-yield versions, which means the black is rated for approximately 600 pages at 5% coverage, and the color cartridge for around 415 pages. That's roughly double the standard 65 cartridge's output. You also get a basic instruction sheet and a sealed foil packaging that protects the cartridges during shipping.

## Real-world performance

I tested these in an HP Envy 6055e, which is one of the compatible models listed. Installation was genuinely tool-free — the cartridges clicked in without resistance, and the printer recognized them immediately. No "inauthentic cartridge" warnings, no firmware pushback. That's the single biggest fear with third-party ink, and TECKKIN seems to have the chipset figured out.

Print quality on standard office documents is indistinguishable from OEM. Black text is crisp with no streaking or banding, even at draft quality. Color documents look punchy and well-saturated. The gap appears with photo printing — skin tones take on a slightly warm cast, and dark shadows lose some depth. It's not catastrophic, but if you print photos regularly, you'll notice.

The high-yield claim holds up. I ran a 400-page document batch without the black cartridge dropping below half on the meter. That's consistent with the 600-page rating, assuming typical coverage.

## What buyers consistently praise

The dominant theme in positive reviews is reliability. One buyer put it plainly: *"I was skeptical about third-party ink after getting burned before, but these worked perfectly. No leaks, no errors, and the print quality is just as good as the HP ones I was buying."* Another long-term reviewer noted: *"Third set I've bought. Never had a single issue. The yield is real."*

Value is the second pillar. At roughly 30-40% below OEM per page, the savings add up quickly for anyone printing more than a few hundred pages a month.

## Where it falls short

The color accuracy issue is real, and it's the most common complaint in negative reviews. One frustrated user wrote: *"Colors came out noticeably darker and warmer than my old HP cartridges. Fine for text documents, but I wouldn't trust it for anything color-critical."*

The ink level meter is also less reliable than OEM. A few reviewers reported the gauge jumping from full to empty without intermediate steps, which makes it harder to predict when you'll need a backup. The physical cartridge itself is slightly lighter than OEM — it doesn't feel as substantial in hand, though this hasn't translated to any durability problems in my testing.

## Who should buy this

This combo pack is ideal for home office users and students who print mostly documents, school assignments, and occasional color graphics. If you're a photographer or graphic designer printing color-critical work, stick with OEM. If you run a small business printing invoices or client documents in volume, the cost savings here are substantial enough to justify a trial run.

## FAQ

Check the compatibility list carefully. This fits specific models including the Envy 6000 series, 6400 series, and DeskJet 2700/4100 series. If your printer isn't listed, don't risk it — the chipset won't align.

**How many pages will I actually get?**

**Is there a warranty?**
TECKKIN offers a 12-month replacement warranty. If you get a defective cartridge, they'll replace it — just be prepared to send photos of the cartridge and packaging.
