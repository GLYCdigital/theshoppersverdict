**FAQ: Three Questions Buyers Actually Ask**

**Q: Will the TN730 work in my Brother HL-L2350DW?**  
A: Yes. The TN730 is compatible with the HL-L2300D series, DCP-L2500D series, and MFC-L2700D series, including the L2350DW. Check Brother's compatibility list if you're unsure — but if your printer takes the DR730 drum, it takes this toner.

**Q: How many pages does the TN730 actually print?**  
A: Brother rates it at 1,200 pages based on 5% coverage — that's a typical text document. If you print dense graphics or photos, expect less. If you print mostly light text, you might squeeze out a bit more.

**Q: Is this cartridge compatible with the TN760?**  
A: No. The TN730 and TN760 are different cartridges. The TN760 is high-yield (3,000 pages) and physically larger. You can't swap one for the other without changing the drum unit.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want genuine Brother quality, minimal hassle, and don't mind the standard-yield page count for light-to-moderate printing.

**Skip it if:** You're a high-volume printer looking to minimize cost-per-page, or you're willing to gamble on third-party toner to save money.
