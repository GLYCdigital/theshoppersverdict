There's a special satisfaction in buying something that costs less than a coffee and performs like it cost ten times more. The Amazon Basics Stapler is exactly that.

**The core question with any $6.31 office supply:** does it do the basic job without frustration? The answer, according to 52,000+ reviewers, is a resounding yes. This stapler handles the rated 25 sheets without complaint, and several reviewers discovered it staples up to 29-30 sheets with the crimp still holding strong. That's exceeding spec in a meaningful way.

**The build is plastic, and that's fine.** Not everything needs to be a metal Swingline from the 1980s. The plastic body keeps the weight down and the cost low. The non-slip rubber base does its job well — the stapler stays where you put it, even on smooth desk surfaces. The only risk is if you knock it off a desk onto a hard floor; plastic can crack where metal would dent.

**Loading staples is genuinely easy.** Push the rear release, slide a strip into the bottom channel, close it up. No springs flying across the room, no wrestling with complicated mechanisms. The included 1000-staple strip will last most home users months.

**Jam performance is excellent.** Multiple reviewers specifically mention zero jams over extended use. The spring-loaded mechanism feeds staples one at a time without the misfeeds that plague cheaper bargain-bin staplers.

At $6.31, this is one of the easiest buying decisions you'll make. It staples, it doesn't jam, and it costs less than lunch. For home office use, it's all the stapler you need.
