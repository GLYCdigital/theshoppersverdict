## Performance That Speaks for Itself

The Energel's party trick is its liquid gel ink formula. It flows effortlessly — think "glides like butter" but without the cliché, because it's genuinely that smooth. There's zero drag on the page, which means less hand fatigue during long journaling sessions or meeting notes.

The ink dries remarkably fast. This is the pen's biggest flex. Left-handed writers, take note: this is one of the few gel pens that won't leave a gray smear trail across your page. The quick-dry formula handles glossy paper and standard copy paper alike without ghosting or transfer.

One thing I appreciate is the consistency. The ink flow doesn't get lighter as the cartridge depletes, and it doesn't blob or skip, even when you're writing quickly to catch up with a fast talker. As one Amazon reviewer put it, "The line is always crisp, never scratchy. It's the only pen I'll use for my bullet journal." ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, this is a single-pen listing. If you're outfitting a desk drawer or an office supply cabinet, you're better off buying the multi-pack version — the per-pen cost here is higher than it should be.

Second, the cap has no clip. That sounds minor until you try to slip it into a shirt pocket or attach it to a notebook. It rolls off desks, and if you're someone who carries a pen daily, that's a genuine inconvenience.

Finally, the ink is water-soluble. A spilled drink or a rainy commute can ruin whatever you've written. If archival permanence matters, look elsewhere.
