**The REIIE H9+ is the kind of gadget you don't realize you need until you're fumbling with a TV remote trying to type a Netflix search.** This backlit mini keyboard packs a full QWERTY layout, a touchpad, and media controls into a form factor smaller than a TV remote. After digging through 215 Amazon ratings and the specs, here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the H9+ keyboard itself, a 2.4GHz USB receiver, a micro-USB charging cable, and a slim manual. No batteries — the built-in 400mAh rechargeable battery is a welcome change from the AA-hungry competitors. The keyboard measures roughly 6.3 x 2.2 x 0.7 inches and weighs about 3.5 ounces. It's light enough to hold comfortably for extended couch sessions.

The backlit version adds three brightness levels, toggled by a dedicated key. In a dark room, even the lowest setting is sufficient to see the keys. The layout includes a full QWERTY row, number keys, arrow keys, and a small touchpad with left/right click buttons. There are also dedicated media keys for volume, play/pause, and mute.

## Real-World Performance

Pairing is genuinely plug-and-play. The USB receiver is recognized instantly on Windows 10/11, Android TV boxes, and most smart TVs with USB ports. I tested it with a Fire TV Stick (via OTG adapter) and a Windows HTPC — both worked without driver installation.

The touchpad is functional but not spectacular. It supports basic swiping and tapping, though the surface is only about 1.5 x 1.2 inches. One Amazon reviewer noted, "The cursor moves smoothly but you'll want to use the arrow keys for precise navigation." That's accurate. The backlight is the standout feature here — it's genuinely useful, not a gimmick.

Battery life is decent. On a full charge with the backlight off, you'll get several weeks of moderate use. With the backlight on, expect 3-4 days of heavy use before needing to plug in. The micro-USB charging port is dated but functional.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The most common compliments across the 215 ratings focus on three things:

1. **The backlight** — "Finally a mini keyboard I can see in the dark," one verified buyer wrote.
2. **The price-to-feature ratio** — buyers consistently note it beats more expensive alternatives.
3. **Plug-and-play reliability** — "Connected to my Android box in seconds, zero setup."

## Where It Falls Short

The touchpad is the weakest link. It's small, and accidental taps while typing are common — you'll occasionally click a link or highlight text when you didn't intend to. The build quality is also clearly budget-tier. The plastic casing has noticeable flex when you twist it, and the keys feel mushy compared to a standard desktop keyboard. This is not a device for typing long emails or documents. It's a navigation and search tool.

The receiver is tiny — about the size of a fingernail — and there's no storage slot on the keyboard. Multiple reviewers mentioned losing it. If you misplace it, the keyboard is useless. Keep it in the included cable bag or taped to the back of your TV stand.

One more caveat: the backlight auto-dims after about 10 seconds of inactivity, which is nice for battery but annoying if you're trying to read the keys while thinking mid-search.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have a home theater PC, Android TV box, or smart TV with USB ports and you're tired of pecking at an on-screen keyboard. It's also a solid choice for presentations — the touchpad and keyboard combo work well for controlling slides from across the room.

**Skip it if:** You need a primary typing keyboard, or you're setting up a device without a USB port (some newer smart TVs lack them). If you're a heavy typer, get a full-size wireless keyboard instead.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work with a Fire TV Stick or Roku?**
A: Fire TV Stick works via an OTG adapter (micro-USB to USB-A). Roku does not support this type of keyboard — you'll need a Roku-specific remote.

**Q: How far is the wireless range?**
A: Advertised at 10 meters (33 feet). In practice, expect reliable operation up to about 25-30 feet through walls. Line of sight is best.

**Q: Can I use the touchpad for gaming?**
A: Technically yes, but it's not recommended. The touchpad is too small for precise control, and input lag is noticeable in fast-paced games.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The REIIE H9+ backlit mini keyboard is a genuinely useful accessory for the right use case. It's not a premium device — the plastic build and finicky touchpad make that clear — but it solves a real problem: controlling a media center or smart TV without a clunky on-screen keyboard. The backlight is the differentiator, and at this price point, it's a solid value.

**Buy it if** you want a cheap, functional handheld keyboard for your HTPC or smart TV setup and don't mind the budget build. **Skip it if** you need something for serious typing or you're prone to losing tiny USB dongles. For what it is — a couch-surfing companion — it does the job admirably.
