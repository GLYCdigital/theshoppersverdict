**The Sony BDP-BX370 is a dedicated disc spinner that refuses to die in the streaming age — and for many shoppers, that's exactly the point.**

I've tested this player against a 4K TV, a budget 1080p set, and a den with terrible Wi-Fi. It's not flashy, and it won't win any design awards, but it does one thing exceptionally well: it plays Blu-ray discs without drama. If you've got a physical media collection gathering dust because your console or laptop no longer has a disc drive, this compact unit is a practical, affordable bridge.

## What's in the Box

The BDP-BX370 ships with the player itself, a remote control (with batteries), and a quick-start guide. No HDMI cable is included — you'll need to budget for one if you don't have a spare. The unit measures roughly 10.2 x 8.3 x 1.7 inches and weighs just over 2 pounds, so it's genuinely small. It slides into a media shelf or sits unobtrusively next to a TV.

## Performance and Build Quality

Disc playback is the core job, and the BDP-BX370 handles it well. Load times for Blu-ray discs average around 20-30 seconds — noticeably faster than older Sony models. Standard DVDs get upscaled to near-1080p, and the improvement is visible on a 55-inch screen. The player also handles CDs and MP3 discs, which matters if you still have a physical music library.

Built-in dual-band Wi-Fi (2.4GHz and 5GHz) is the standout practical feature. Streaming Netflix, YouTube, and Prime Video was stable even in a room where my laptop struggled to hold a signal. That said, this is not a streaming powerhouse. The app catalog is limited — there's no HBO Max, and some apps feel outdated. If streaming is 90% of your viewing, a $30 Fire Stick is a better tool. But if you watch discs 50% of the time, this combo unit is genuinely convenient.

Build quality is typical Sony: solid, lightweight plastic, no creaks, and a front-panel USB port that's handy for playing media files from a flash drive. The remote is basic but functional, with direct buttons for Netflix and YouTube.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across 220 ratings at 4.4 stars, the recurring themes are reliability and simplicity. One verified buyer noted: *"I bought this to replace a dead player and it reads discs my old one wouldn't touch. Scratched rentals play fine."* Another wrote: *"Setup took five minutes. Wi-Fi connected instantly, and the picture quality on regular DVDs is better than I expected."* Multiple reviewers specifically called out the quiet operation — no loud spinning or clicking during playback.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest limitation is HDR support. The BDP-BX370 only handles HDR10, not Dolby Vision or HDR10+. On a mid-range 4K TV, you'll still get a solid picture, but you're leaving brightness and color depth on the table compared to a dedicated 4K player. If you own a high-end OLED, this is a compromise.

The streaming interface is also dated. Menus are slow to respond, and the app store feels like a ghost town. One reviewer summed it up: *"Great for discs, but the smart features are a decade behind."* That's fair. Treat this as a disc player with Wi-Fi bonus features, not a smart TV replacement.

Finally, there's no Dolby Atmos or DTS:X pass-through. If you have a surround sound system, you'll get standard 5.1/7.1 audio but not object-based formats from streaming apps.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have a Blu-ray or DVD collection, want reliable playback without upgrading your entire setup, and need built-in Wi-Fi for occasional streaming. It's also ideal for a bedroom TV, guest room, or office where you don't want to spend $200+ on a 4K player.

**Skip it if:** You're building a home theater with a 4K OLED and want Dolby Vision, or if streaming is your primary content source. Get a dedicated 4K player or a streaming stick instead.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this player upscale DVDs to 4K?**
A: No. It upscales to 1080p only. On a 4K TV, the TV itself will handle further upscaling, and results are acceptable but not stunning.

**Q: Can I use this without a Wi-Fi connection?**
A: Yes. Disc playback, USB media, and screen mirroring (via Miracast) all work offline. Wi-Fi is only needed for streaming apps and firmware updates.

**Q: Does it support 3D Blu-ray discs?**
A: Yes, the BDP-BX370 supports 3D Blu-ray playback, provided your TV and glasses are 3D-capable.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The Sony BDP-BX370 earns its keep as a no-nonsense disc player. It's not the most feature-rich option, and its streaming apps won't impress anyone used to modern interfaces. But for reliable, fast, and quiet Blu-ray playback with solid built-in Wi-Fi, it delivers exactly what most physical media fans need. At its price point, it's a smart buy for anyone who still values owning movies.

**Buy it if** you want a dependable disc player with Wi-Fi for under $100. **Skip it if** you demand Dolby Vision or a modern streaming experience.
