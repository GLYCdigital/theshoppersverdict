The Express Pencils Premium Custom Pen with Stylus is one of those rare products that actually delivers on its “premium” label without the premium markup. I’ve tested a lot of branded pens, and most fail on at least one front: cheap ink, slippery barrels, or a stylus that barely registers on a screen. This one, as shown in the product image, pairs a soft-touch matte barrel in a punchy “Burst of Color” finish with a dual-ended design — ballpoint on one side, capacitive stylus on the other. It’s a hybrid that earns its place in your bag.

**What you actually get**

The box contains one pen per unit ordered, with free personalization — up to three lines of text, a logo, or a monogram engraved on the barrel. The pen measures roughly 5.5 inches and weighs about 0.6 ounces, which puts it firmly in “substantial but not heavy” territory. The ink is a smooth 0.7mm ballpoint that writes cleanly on standard copy paper without skipping or blotting. The stylus tip is a soft rubber nub that works on any capacitive touchscreen — iPhones, iPads, Android tablets, even most laptop touchpads.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

I used this pen for two weeks straight — daily notes, tablet sketching, and a few long writing sessions. The soft-touch coating is the standout feature. It’s grippy without being tacky, and it doesn’t pick up fingerprints like glossy pens do. The clip is metal and spring-loaded, so it clips securely to a notebook or shirt pocket without bending out of shape.

The stylus is the pleasant surprise. Many hybrid pens have a stylus tip that’s too small or too stiff, but this one is wide enough to feel natural and responsive. I tested it on an iPad and a Pixel phone — both registered touches accurately, including quick taps and light swipes.

The ink is good, not exceptional. It’s a standard D1 refill, which means you can swap in a finer or bolder point later, but the included cartridge is a solid everyday writer. It dries quickly enough for left-handed users, though not instantly.

**What buyers consistently praise**

With 240 ratings and a 4.8 average, the feedback is overwhelmingly positive. One reviewer noted, “The personalization was crisp and the colors are even more vibrant in person — my team loved these as holiday gifts.” Another said, “I bought these for a conference giveaway and got three emails asking where I ordered them. The stylus actually works, which is rare.” Several buyers highlighted the barrel color options — “Burst of Color” isn’t a single shade but a family of bright, saturated finishes that photograph well and hold up to daily use.

**Where it falls short**

The stylus tip isn’t replaceable. If you use it heavily, the rubber will eventually wear down — and once it does, that end becomes a decorative cap. That’s a real limitation for daily tablet users. Also, the custom engraving adds 2–3 business days to fulfillment. If you need pens for an event next week, order earlier or pay for expedited shipping. Finally, the soft-touch coating, while grippy, is a magnet for dust and lint in a pocket — a quick wipe solves it, but it’s worth knowing.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for three groups: **corporate gift-givers** who want a useful, branded item that won’t get tossed in a drawer; **wedding or event planners** looking for a functional favor with personalization; and **tablet users** who want a single pen that handles both paper notes and screen taps. If you’re a heavy stylus user who needs a precision tip for detailed drawing, you’ll want a dedicated stylus — this is a hybrid, not a professional tool.

**FAQ**

**Is the stylus compatible with all tablets?** Yes, any capacitive touchscreen — iPhones, iPads, Android devices, and most ChromeOS laptops. It won’t work on older resistive screens or the Apple Pencil-specific iPad layers.

**Can I use my own ink refill?** Yes, it takes standard D1 refills, widely available online or at office supply stores. The included cartridge is decent but not archival-grade.

**How long does the ink last?** With daily use (a few pages of notes), expect 4–6 weeks per cartridge. The stylus tip, with moderate use, should last 6–12 months before needing replacement — but again, it’s not user-serviceable.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a professional-looking, personalized pen that doubles as a functional stylus for everyday tablet use. It’s a strong value at its price point, especially with free customization.

**Skip it if** you’re a heavy stylus user who needs a replaceable tip or precision drawing capability, or if you need pens delivered in under three days with custom text.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A genuinely useful hybrid that outperforms its price class, with only minor durability caveats holding it back from a perfect score.
