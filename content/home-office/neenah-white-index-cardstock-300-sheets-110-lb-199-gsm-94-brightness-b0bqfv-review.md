## Where It Falls Short

Honest downsides, because every product has them:

1. **No color options.** This listing is white only. If you need cream, ivory, or colored cardstock, this isn't your pack.
2. **Surface texture.** The "index" finish has a slight tooth. It's fine for most printing, but if you're doing high-resolution photo reproduction, you'll see a subtle texture in large solid areas. Pick a smooth cover stock for that.
3. **Not fold-friendly for thick stacks.** It folds cleanly once, but double-folding (like a tri-fold brochure) can crack at the crease. Use a scoring tool first.

## FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Will this work in my HP or Epson inkjet printer?**
A: Yes. The 110 lb weight is within the range most consumer printers accept. Set your paper type to "cardstock" or "heavy" in print settings for best results. If your printer has a rear feed tray, use it — it creates a straighter paper path.

**Q: Is this the same as Neenah Astrobrights cardstock?**
A: No. Astrobrights is a different line with a smoother finish and more color options. This is Neenah's index cardstock — it's stiffer and has a more visible texture. Both are quality papers, but they serve different purposes.

**Q: Can I use this for double-sided printing?**
A: It's possible, but expect some show-through if you're printing heavy ink coverage on both sides. For light text, it's fine. For full-bleed color on both sides, you'll want a heavier cover stock or accept the ghosting.
