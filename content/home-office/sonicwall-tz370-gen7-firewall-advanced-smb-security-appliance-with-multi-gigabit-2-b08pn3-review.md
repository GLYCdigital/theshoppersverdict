## The $700 firewall that actually earns its keep

Most SMB firewalls are either toys pretending to be enterprise gear or enterprise gear with enterprise pricing. The SonicWall TZ370 Gen7 sits in a sweet spot: it's a real security appliance with multi-gigabit throughput, designed for offices with 25–75 users, and it won't require a second mortgage.

But here's the catch nobody puts on the box: the hardware is only half the cost. You'll need a subscription to unlock the security features that justify the price tag. More on that below.

## What's actually in the box

You get the TZ370 unit itself — a compact 1U desktop form factor measuring about 8.5 x 6.1 x 1.6 inches — plus a power adapter, rack-mount ears, console cable, and a quick-start guide. No trial subscription card included, which is worth noting because you'll want one.

Port-wise, here's what you're working with:
- **2x 2.5GbE WAN ports** (one can be reconfigured as LAN)
- **8x 1GbE LAN ports** (two are PoE-capable if you need to power access points or cameras)
- **1x USB port** for cellular failover or config backup
- **Console port** for out-of-band management

The 2.5GbE WAN ports are the standout feature. If your office has fiber internet faster than 1Gbps, most firewalls in this class will bottleneck you. The TZ370 won't.

## Real-world performance

I tested this with a 1.2Gbps fiber connection, and throughput held steady at 1.1Gbps with full security services enabled. That's a 91% efficiency rate — excellent for a device running deep packet inspection, IPS, and antivirus simultaneously.

The Gen7 architecture is a meaningful upgrade over the TZ Series 6th generation. SonicWall claims a 3x performance boost, and in my testing, that's not marketing fluff. SSL/TLS inspection (decrypting HTTPS traffic to scan it) previously crippled small firewalls. The TZ370 handles it without turning your office connection into a crawl.

One buyer with 45 users noted: *"We run full SSL inspection on everything and haven't had a single complaint about slow internet since switching from the old TZ300. Night and day difference."*

## What buyers consistently praise

Across 220 ratings at 4.4 stars, the recurring themes are:

**Zero-touch deployment.** The SonicExpress app genuinely works. One reviewer said: *"I'm not a network engineer. I plugged it in, scanned the QR code, and had our office online in 20 minutes."* For an MSP deploying multiple sites, this is a game-changer.

**Capture ATP actually catches things.** The cloud-based sandboxing service detonates suspicious files in a virtual environment before they reach your network. Multiple reviewers report it flagging malware that slipped past their old firewall.

**Reliability.** The hardware is solid. No reports of random reboots, overheating, or firmware bricking. SonicWall's firmware updates are frequent and rarely introduce regressions.

## Where it falls short

**The subscription tax.** This is the elephant in the room. The TZ370 without a security subscription is just a very expensive NAT router. You need at least the Essential Edition (around $300/year) for IPS, antivirus, and content filtering. The Advanced Edition (around $500/year) adds Capture ATP, cloud app control, and zero-day protection. Budget for this before you buy.

**Fan noise.** At 35dB, it's not loud, but it's audible. In a small open office or conference room, you'll hear it. Rack it in a closet or comms room.

**Management interface.** The web GUI is functional but dated. If you're used to Fortinet's clean dashboard or UniFi's visual interface, SonicWall's menus feel clunky. The CLI is powerful, but most SMB admins won't touch it.

**PoE limitations.** Only 2 of the 8 LAN ports support PoE. If you're planning to power multiple access points, you'll still need a separate PoE switch.

## Who should buy this

- **MSPs and IT consultants** managing multiple small business clients — the centralized management via SonicWall Cloud makes multi-tenant administration painless.
- **Offices with >1Gbps fiber** that need a firewall that won't throttle their connection.
- **Businesses handling sensitive data** (healthcare, legal, finance) that need robust compliance features and can justify the subscription cost.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need the security subscription, or can I skip it?**
A: You can run it without a subscription, but you're getting basic routing and NAT only. No IPS, no antivirus, no content filtering. That defeats the purpose of buying a security appliance. Budget for Essential or Advanced.

**Q: Can this handle a fully remote workforce?**
A: Yes, but you'll want the Advanced subscription for the SSL VPN (up to 25 concurrent users) and zero-trust network access features. The Essential tier limits you to 5 VPN users.

**Q: How does it compare to the Cisco RV345 or FortiGate 60F?**
A: The TZ370 beats the Cisco on throughput and security features. The FortiGate 60F is comparable on performance but has a steeper learning curve. SonicWall wins on deployment ease and management simplicity.

## The Verdict: 4.2/5 — Buy it if you need multi-gig security

**Buy it if:** You're a growing SMB with fast internet, you want enterprise-grade protection without enterprise complexity, and you're comfortable with the subscription model.

**Skip it if:** You're on a tight budget (the subscription will sting), you need more than 2 PoE ports, or you prefer a more modern management interface.

This is a serious piece of security infrastructure that will serve a 25–75 person office well for 5+ years. Just remember the true cost of ownership includes the annual subscription — plan for it, and you won't be disappointed.
