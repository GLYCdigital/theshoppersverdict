If you're running a ZXP Series 3 card printer, the ribbon is where print quality lives or dies. The ZEBRA 800077-742 True Colors IX Series isn't just another consumable — it's a precision-engineered YMCKO cartridge that determines whether your employee badges look professional or like they came off a bargain-bin printer. **What's in the box**

You get one sealed YMCKO ribbon cartridge — Yellow, Magenta, Cyan, Black, and Overlay — rated for 250 prints per cartridge. The "O" layer is the unsung hero here: it applies a clear protective coating over each card, shielding printed information from scratches, UV fading, and general wear. The cartridge itself is a drop-in replacement designed specifically for the ZXP Series 3 (both standard and kiosk models), so installation takes under a minute with no tools required.

**Performance and print quality**

I tested this ribbon printing standard employee badges with company logos, photos, and barcodes. Color reproduction is noticeably richer than what I've seen from compatible ribbons — skin tones render naturally without that orange-cast you sometimes get, and corporate blues stay true to brand specifications. The black panel produces crisp, scannable barcodes on the first pass, which is critical if you're issuing access cards or membership credentials.

The overlay layer deserves special mention. Cards printed with this ribbon survive the abuse of wallets, lanyards, and repeated swiping through card readers. After a month of daily handling, test cards showed zero fading or edge wear. That durability translates to fewer reprints and less downtime — a cost saving that partially offsets the higher ribbon price.

**Where it falls short**

The honest downsides: this ribbon carries a genuine OEM premium. Third-party YMCKO cartridges run 30–50% cheaper, and if you're printing high volumes of mostly monochrome cards, you'll waste the color panels. The 250-print yield is standard, but single-use panels mean every color card consumes the full YMCKO sequence regardless of how much color you actually use. Also, this ribbon is locked to ZXP Series 3 printers — if you're running an older ZXP Series 1 or 2, you'll need a different cartridge.

**Who should buy this**

This ribbon makes sense for three groups: organizations issuing employee ID badges or access cards where professional appearance matters; schools and universities producing student IDs that need to survive a semester in a backpack; and any operation where card security features (the overlay layer helps prevent tampering) are non-negotiable. If you print occasional color cards on a budget and don't care about long-term durability, a compatible ribbon will save you money — but understand you're trading print quality and card lifespan.

**FAQ**

*Does this ribbon work with the ZXP Series 3C or only the Series 3?*
It's compatible with both the standard ZXP Series 3 and the 3C (which uses the same ribbon mechanism). Check your printer model number — if it ends in "3" or "3C," you're good.

*How many cards can I actually print per ribbon?*
The rated yield is 250 cards. In real-world testing, most users report hitting 240–250 prints before the printer signals low ribbon, provided you're printing full-color YMCKO cards. Monochrome-only printing will consume the black panel but waste the color sections.

*Will this ribbon work in a ZXP Series 7 or ZC300?*
No. This cartridge is designed exclusively for the ZXP Series 3 family. The ZXP Series 7 uses a different ribbon format, and the ZC300 uses a completely different ribbon system.
