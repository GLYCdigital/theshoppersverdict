**The psychology is simple: most thieves don't case a house for 20 minutes. They spot a camera, a sign, a dog — and move to the next target.**

That's the entire business case for these Video Surveillance Signs. At roughly the price of a coffee run, they promise to do one thing: make people think twice. The question is whether a stamped piece of metal actually delivers.

Let me save you the research. I've combed through the 235 ratings (4.7 stars), the buyer photos, and the complaints. Here's what you need to know before you add these to your cart.

## What You Actually Get

This is a 10-pack of rigid aluminum composite signs, each measuring 7 x 10 inches. That's a proper size — not those flimsy 4-inch stickers you see peeling off storefront doors. The material is a sandwich of aluminum sheets around a polyethylene core, which is why buyers consistently describe them as "heavy" and "solid."

Each sign features the standard eye-and-camera graphic with "VIDEO SURVEILLANCE IN PROGRESS" in bold lettering. You get pre-drilled holes in all four corners, plus a strip of double-sided tape. One buyer noted, "I was expecting cheap plastic. These are legit metal signs that feel like they'll outlast my fence."

## How They Hold Up

The build quality is where these earn their keep. The aluminum composite won't rust, and the UV-resistant laminate keeps the ink from fading. Multiple reviewers mention leaving them mounted through harsh winters and summer sun without any warping or color loss.

Installation is genuinely simple. The pre-drilled holes accept standard #8 screws, and if you're renting, the included tape holds firmly on clean surfaces. One reviewer said, "Had them up in 10 minutes with just a drill. The tape worked fine on my garage door, so I didn't even bother with screws."

**The honest downside:** the print quality is good but not premium. Up close, the graphics show slight pixelation. From a distance — which is how you'll actually see them — it's a non-issue.

## Where They Fall Short

The text size issue is the biggest gap between the product photos and reality. The photos suggest large, bold lettering that's visible from a distance. In practice, you need to be within about 15 feet to read it clearly. For a residential driveway or a small office, that's fine. For a large commercial lot, you'll want bigger signage or actual cameras.

The sharp corners are a minor but real annoyance. It's a five-minute fix with a file, but it's worth knowing before you handle them.

## FAQ

**Do these work without actual cameras?** Technically yes, but ethically and practically, you're better off pairing them with at least one real camera. If someone calls your bluff and nothing's there, you've lost all credibility.

**Will the tape hold outdoors?** Most buyers say yes for flat, clean surfaces. But for anything exposed to extreme heat or moisture, use screws. The tape is a convenience, not a permanent solution.

**Are they weatherproof?** Yes. The aluminum composite and laminate are designed for outdoor use. Rain, snow, and UV exposure won't damage them.
