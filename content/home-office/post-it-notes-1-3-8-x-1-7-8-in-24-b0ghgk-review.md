**The 2,400-Sheet Workhorse: Why This Mini Post-it Pack Earns Its 4.7 Stars**

Most sticky note packs are a joke — 3 pads, 100 sheets each, gone in a week. This 24-pad bulk set changes the math. At 1 3/8 x 1 7/8 inches per note, you're getting 2,400 individual sheets. For context, that's roughly 10 months of daily use for someone who jots down 7-8 notes per workday. The per-sheet cost undercuts the small packs significantly, and these are genuine 3M Post-its, not a knockoff that loses tack in a week.

**What's in the box**

You get 24 pads, each with 100 sheets — no dispenser, no plastic sleeve, just a stack of pads shrink-wrapped together. The paper is the standard canary yellow you expect from Post-it. Each sheet measures exactly 1 3/8 x 1 7/8 inches, which is the "mini" format. They're lightweight enough to keep in a pocket or pencil case, but you'll likely keep them on a desk given the quantity.

**Real-world performance**

The adhesive is the star. I tested these on a glossy monitor bezel, a spiral notebook, a painted wall, and a whiteboard. They stuck immediately and held for 48 hours without curling at the edges. Removal left zero residue on all surfaces except the painted wall, which showed slight tackiness — that's normal for any sticky note. The paper weight is solid; it doesn't tear when you peel it off the pad, and it holds up to pen pressure without bleeding through.

Writing space is the real constraint. With a standard ballpoint, you get about 15-18 characters per line, and maybe 4 lines total. These are for single words, short reminders, and page flags — not meeting notes. If you need more space, the 3x3 or 3x5 sizes are better suited.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The bulk quantity is the recurring theme in user feedback. One reviewer noted, "I use these for book page flags and recipe annotations — bought this in January and still have 14 pads left in August." **Where it falls short**

The single-color design is the most common criticism. If you color-code your notes by project or priority, this pack won't help — every pad is the same yellow. The small size also limits utility; this isn't a replacement for standard 3x3 notes. And while the adhesive is strong, it's not permanent — notes on vertical surfaces will eventually lose grip after a few weeks, especially in humid environments.

**Who should buy this**
