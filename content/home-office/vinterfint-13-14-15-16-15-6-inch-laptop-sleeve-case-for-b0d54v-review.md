## What You Actually Get

The Vinterfint sleeve is a padded, zip-closure case available in several colorways (the product images show a textured, woven exterior that looks more premium than the price point suggests). It's designed to fit laptops from 13 inches up to 15.6 inches — a genuinely useful range if you switch between devices or have a family with mixed hardware.

The exterior is a soft-but-durable fabric with a subtle texture that resists scratches. Inside, you get a plush lining — the kind of microfiber that won't scuff your laptop's finish. There's a single front pocket that's big enough for a slim charger or a mouse, but don't expect to stuff a power brick and cables in there. The zipper is smooth and doesn't catch, which matters more than most people realize.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this with a 14-inch work laptop and a 15.6-inch personal machine. The fit is snug but not tight — the sleeve holds the laptop securely without making you wrestle to get it out. That's the sweet spot. Too loose and your laptop slides around; too tight and you'll rip the zipper in frustration. Vinterfint nailed it.

The padding is substantial enough to absorb the minor bumps of daily life — tossing the sleeve into a backpack, setting it on a train seat, shoving it under a plane seat. It's not a hard-shell case, so drop protection is limited. But for scratch prevention and general commuter safety, it does the job.

One thing worth noting: **this is a sleeve, not a bag.** There's no handle, no strap, no way to carry it on its own. You're meant to slide it into another bag. If you wanted a standalone laptop carrier, this isn't it.
