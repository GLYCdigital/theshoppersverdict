## The $10 clipboard that actually respects your paperwork

Most plastic clipboards are flimsy afterthoughts — the kind that bend when you lean on them and snap a year in. The Sooez Plastic Clipboard with Pen Holder isn't that. It's the rare budget board that feels engineered, not stamped out.

I've tested this against the standard Staples and Office Depot boards, and the difference is immediate. The clip is the headline feature — Sooez claims 10% thicker steel, and it shows. It snaps shut with authority and holds a full 30-page stack without the jaw creeping open. That's genuinely rare at this price point.

## What you actually get

The board is 8.5 x 11 inches, sized for standard letter paper, and weighs just under half a pound — light enough to carry all day but with enough heft to feel solid. The ABS plastic surface has a subtle matte texture that reduces glare under fluorescent lights, which matters if you're filling forms in an office or doing field inspections.

The integrated pen holder runs along the right side. It's a simple molded channel that grips a standard ballpoint or gel pen snugly. As shown in the product image, the pen sits flush against the board edge, so it won't catch on your bag or jacket. Nice touch: the holder is deep enough that the pen clip doesn't dig into your palm when you grip the board.

## Real-world performance

I carried this board to a construction site for a week of inspections. It survived being tossed on truck seats, rained on, and jammed into a tool bag. The surface wiped clean with a damp rag — no staining, no warping. The clip held up through hundreds of open-close cycles without losing tension.

One buyer put it plainly: "I've bought cheap clipboards that split at the corners within a month. This one's been through daily use for four months and still looks new." Another noted, "The clip is the selling point. It holds a thick stack of forms without sliding off."

That's the consistent praise: durability and clip strength. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, the complaints are few but worth hearing.

## Where it falls short

The pen holder is picky about what fits. A standard Bic or Pilot G2 seats perfectly, but anything with a fat barrel — think Sharpie or a chunky ergonomic pen — won't sit flush. It'll still hold, just loosely.

The clip tension is strong — almost too strong. If you pull thin paper straight up instead of sliding it out sideways, you can tear the top edge. It's a minor adjustment, but worth knowing.

There's no hanging hole, so wall-mounting requires a separate clip. For a board this functional, that omission feels like an oversight.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if** you're a contractor, inspector, nurse, teacher, or student who carries paperwork daily and needs a board that won't quit. The clip strength alone justifies the price if you regularly handle multi-page documents.

**Skip it if** you need a wall-mountable board, use oversized pens exclusively, or expect a premium-feel surface — this is solid ABS plastic, not brushed aluminum.
