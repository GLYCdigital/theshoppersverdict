If you've ever propped a tablet on your chest and watched it slide off at 2 a.m., you already know why the Lamicall Gooseneck Tablet Mount exists. This is a 31-inch flexible arm with a C-clamp on one end and a 4.7-11 inch device cradle on the other, designed to hover your screen exactly where you want it — over your face in bed, beside your desk, or clamped to a kitchen cabinet while you cook.

The build quality justifies the price. The arm is wrapped in a matte silicone that feels premium, not sticky or cheap. Inside, it's a rigid metal gooseneck that holds its position remarkably well. I've seen cheaper arms droop under the weight of a heavy tablet; this one stays put. The cradle has rubber grips on all four corners and an open back, so your camera, buttons, and speakers stay accessible. The clamp opens up to 3.6 inches and is lined with thick padding, which protects your furniture and gives the mount a surprisingly solid grip.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The #1 thing owners mention is that the arm actually stays where you put it. One verified buyer wrote, *"I've tried three other gooseneck mounts and this is the first one that doesn't slowly sag during a movie. The tension is perfect."* Another noted, *"The clamp is a beast — I have it on a 2-inch thick headboard and it doesn't budge."* Multiple reviewers also highlighted the universal compatibility, with one saying, *"Works great with my iPad Mini and my Kindle. Switching devices takes five seconds."*

**Where it falls short**

The biggest limitation is the clamp design. It needs a flat, horizontal surface at least 1.2 inches deep. Round bedposts? Tubular metal frames? Out of luck. The arm is also stiff — deliberately so, to prevent sagging — but that means adjusting it requires two hands and some muscle. If you want to fine-tune the angle mid-scroll, you'll be fighting the arm a bit. Finally, there's no cable management. If you charge while you use it, the cord dangles awkwardly and can pull the mount off-angle if you yank it.

**Who should buy this**

- **Bed readers and binge-watchers**: If your headboard is flat, this is the best position for hands-free viewing. Period.
- **Desk multitaskers**: Clamp it to the edge of your desk for a secondary screen for notes, references, or video calls.
- **Kitchen cooks**: Follow recipes hands-free by clamping it to an upper cabinet or shelf.

**Skip it if** your bed frame has round posts, you need a mount that rotates 360 degrees, or you want something you can reposition with one hand.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this hold a 12.9-inch iPad Pro?**
Yes. The cradle expands to fit tablets up to 11 inches, but the listed spec says 4.7-11". The 12.9-inch iPad Pro exceeds that — many users report it works but barely fits. For a 12.9-inch, you're safer with a dedicated iPad Pro mount.

**Q: Can I use it on a headboard that's not flat?**
Only if the surface is at least 1.2 inches deep and flat. Rounded or angled headboards won't give the clamp enough surface to grip. If your headboard is curved, look for a weighted-base floor stand instead.

**Q: Does it work with a phone?**
Yes. The cradle grips devices as small as 4.7 inches, meaning most modern smartphones fit. Just note that with a phone, the arm's stiffness is overkill — you might prefer a lighter mount.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you have a flat surface to clamp to and want a rock-solid, no-sag tablet arm for bed, desk, or kitchen use. **Skip it if** you have round bedposts, need one-handed adjustment, or want cable management built in.

The Lamicall Gooseneck Tablet Mount earns a 4.2 out of 5. It's not perfect — the clamp limits where you can use it, and the stiffness is a tradeoff — but for the vast majority of buyers with a flat headboard or desk, it's the last tablet mount you'll need to buy.
