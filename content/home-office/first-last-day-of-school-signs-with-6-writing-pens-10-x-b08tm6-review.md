**The Short Version**

If you’ve ever fumbled with a flimsy chalkboard marker or watched your kid’s first-day photo get ruined by a crooked sign, this set is the quiet fix you didn’t know you needed. It’s not flashy, but it’s the kind of practical purchase that makes a chaotic September morning just a little smoother.

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes two double-sided signs (one for the first day, one for the last day) and six white ink pens. Each sign measures 10 x 10 inches—big enough to be clearly legible in a photo, but not so large that it’s awkward for a kindergartner to hold. The signs are made from a thick, rigid cardboard stock that feels sturdier than the typical poster paper you’d pick up at a dollar store. The pens are fine-tipped, which matters more than you’d think: you can write a full sentence without the ink bleeding into a blob.

**How It Holds Up in Real Life**

Here’s where this set earns its keep. The signs are double-sided, so you flip to the back for the last day of school—no need to buy a second set. The white ink is genuinely opaque. Most comparable products use a watery white that requires three passes to look solid. These pens lay down a clean line on the first stroke, and they dry quickly enough that a wiggly first-grader won’t smear it all over their shirt.

The cardboard has a slight gloss coating, which is a double-edged sword. It makes the signs wipe clean with a damp cloth if you want to reuse them year after year. But it also means you should let the ink cure for about 30 seconds before handling. One reviewer noted, “I wrote the sign, handed it to my son, and he immediately smudged the date. My fault, not the product’s.” That tracks.

**What Buyers Keep Mentioning**

The 4.7-star rating holds up when you read the pattern of feedback. The most common praise centers on the pen quality. One verified purchase said, “The markers actually work. I’ve bought three of these sets over the years, and the ink is always smooth, never patchy.” Another parent appreciated the size: “Perfect for a 5-year-old to hold. Not too heavy, not too flimsy.”

The double-sided design gets consistent shout-outs too. Several reviewers specifically called out that they didn’t realize it was two signs in one until it arrived, and they were pleasantly surprised.

**Where It Falls Short**

The signs are not fully waterproof. A light drizzle during the walk to school will cause the ink to run. If you live in a rainy climate, you’ll want to snap the photo indoors or under a covered porch.

The pen caps are also a minor frustration. They fit snugly, which is good for preventing dry-out, but a 6-year-old will struggle to pop them off. You’ll be doing the uncapping, and you’ll want to keep the pens out of reach until the photo is taken.

Finally, the set only includes white ink. If you want to add a pop of color—like a red heart or a blue crayon drawing—you’ll need to buy additional pens separately.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Parents of elementary-age kids:** The size and sturdiness are ideal for small hands.
- **Teachers:** Use one set for a class photo board or a “first day of school” station in the hallway.
- **Grandparents:** A thoughtful, low-cost gift for a grandchild’s milestone moment.

**Skip It If:**

- You need a reusable dry-erase board. The cardboard is durable but not meant for repeated erasing.
- You want a fully waterproof option for outdoor photo shoots in unpredictable weather.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use regular dry-erase markers on these signs?**
A: Technically yes, but they’ll smudge more easily than the included white pens. Stick with the provided ink for the cleanest result.

**Q: How many uses do the pens last?**
A: The six pens will easily cover two full school years if you store them cap-down. The ink flow stays consistent until the very end.

**Q: Are the signs really double-sided?**
A: Yes. One side says “First Day of School” and the reverse says “Last Day of School.” You flip it, not replace it.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if** you want a simple, well-made photo prop that won’t fall apart or smear at the worst moment. The pen quality alone justifies the price.

**Skip it if** you need something waterproof or if you’re looking for a reusable dry-erase board. For a one-time (or two-time) annual photo, this is the best-value option I’ve found.
