**The $20 desk declutter that actually works — if your monitor qualifies.**

You've seen the ads: a clean desk, cables floating in mid-air, a webcam magically attached to a monitor. The screenbuddy Set of 2 promises exactly that — magnetic mounting points that stick to your screen's back, turning any flat surface into a docking station for your gear. After 215 ratings averaging 4.3 stars, the consensus is clear: this thing works, but it's picky about what it sticks to.

**What you actually get**

The box contains two identical magnetic clips, each with a strong neodymium magnet embedded in a slim plastic housing. One side has a 3M adhesive pad that bonds permanently to your monitor's rear panel. The other side is the magnetic face — you attach small metal plates (included) to your webcam, cable clips, or other accessories, and they snap into place.

Each unit is roughly the size of a large postage stamp — dimensions around 1.5 x 1 x 0.4 inches. The included metal plates are thin enough that they don't interfere with most device functionality. As shown in the product image, the setup looks clean and minimalist once installed.

**Real-world performance**

The magnet strength is the headline feature. It's genuinely strong — these aren't fridge-magnet toys. A standard Logitech C920 webcam attaches firmly, and the included cable clips hold USB-C and HDMI cables without sagging. Users consistently praise this: "I was skeptical about the magnet holding my heavy work webcam, but it's rock solid all day."

The adhesive bond is equally impressive. Once applied and left to cure for 24 hours, it takes serious force to remove. Multiple reviewers note they've had theirs mounted for months with zero shifting.

**Where it falls short**

Here's the catch: the adhesive is permanent. If you misalign the clip or upgrade to a new monitor, you're dealing with a stubborn removal process that can leave sticky residue. Plan your placement carefully.

More importantly, the design assumes a flat monitor back. Curved displays — increasingly common in gaming and ultrawide monitors — defeat the entire concept. The rigid plastic base won't conform to a curve, leaving gaps that compromise adhesion. Textured or vented backs are equally problematic. If your monitor has any significant curvature or surface texture, skip this product.

The magnet's strength also has limits. While it handles webcams and cables effortlessly, heavier items like over-ear headphones or a full-frame camera used as a webcam will likely slide off, especially with vibration or movement.

**Who should buy this**

- **Remote workers** with a permanent home setup who are tired of cable spaghetti
- **Streamers** who need their webcam positioned precisely without bulky mounts
- **Minimalists** who want zero desk clutter and don't mind the permanent commitment

**Skip it if** you have a curved monitor, plan to swap displays frequently, or need to hold anything heavier than a standard webcam.

**FAQ**

*Will it work on my iMac or MacBook?*  
Yes, if the back is flat. The aluminum backs of modern Macs are ideal. However, the magnet will interfere with the built-in magnetic charging on recent MacBooks — don't place it near the charging area.

*Can I remove it without damaging my monitor?*  
Yes, but it's not easy. Use dental floss or fishing line to saw through the adhesive, then clean residue with isopropyl alcohol. Expect some effort and potential light residue.

*Does it come with everything I need?*  
Yes — two magnetic clips, four metal plates, and adhesive pads are pre-installed. No extra tools required.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you have a flat-backed monitor and want a clean, cable-free workspace for under $25. It's a simple, effective solution that delivers exactly what it promises.

**Skip it if** you're working with a curved or textured screen, or you're not ready for a permanent attachment.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — a genuinely useful desk accessory with a clear compatibility limitation.
