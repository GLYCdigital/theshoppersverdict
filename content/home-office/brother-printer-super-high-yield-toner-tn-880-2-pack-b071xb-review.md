**FAQ**

**Q: Will this work in my Brother HL-L3210CW?**  
A: No. The TN-880 fits Brother models like the HL-L8350CDW, HL-L8360CDW, MFC-L8850CDW, and MFC-L8900CDW. Check your printer’s manual or Brother’s compatibility tool—the TN-820/TN-880 series is for the "pro" line, not the entry-level 3000-series.

**Q: How do I know when the cartridge is actually empty?**  
A: The printer will show a "Replace Toner" message when the sensor detects low levels. You can override it once via the printer menu to stretch the last few hundred pages, but Brother’s firmware is strict—no infinite resets.

**Q: Is the 2-pack actually cheaper than buying two singles?**  
A: Usually, yes—by 10–20%, depending on current pricing. But watch for coupons or Subscribe & Save on the single-pack, which can sometimes close that gap. The 2-pack’s real advantage is fewer reorder interruptions, not a massive discount.
