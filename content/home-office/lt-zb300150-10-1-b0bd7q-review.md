The LT-ZB300150-10+1 lives up to its name: 10 smaller compartments plus one larger bay. As shown in the product image, it's a tiered organizer—think stadium seating for your supplies. The base unit is roughly the size of a standard keyboard tray, so it won't swallow your entire desk surface.

Materials matter here. The body is a thick ABS plastic that feels more premium than the flimsy stuff you find at dollar stores. There's a subtle matte texture that resists fingerprints—a small touch, but one you'll appreciate after a week of use.

## Where It Falls Short

Honest talk: no product is perfect, and this one has two notable gaps.

First, there's no cable management. If your desk is a spaghetti junction of charging cables and monitor wires, this organizer won't help—it'll actually make things worse by adding another hard surface for cords to tangle around. You'll need separate cable clips or sleeves.

Second, the color options are limited to neutral tones. If your workspace is bold and colorful, this will look out of place. It's a functional tool, not a design statement.

A few reviewers also mentioned the compartments can be tight for oversized items—thick markers or chunky highlighters might not fit in the narrow slots. Check your supply sizes before committing.
