## The Little Box That Thinks It's a Desktop

Most mini PCs make you choose between size and performance. The GEEKOM XT13 Pro refuses that tradeoff. This 4.6-inch cube packs a 13th-gen Intel Core i7-13620H — a chip that's genuinely overkill for most office work — and it shows in real-world use.

I've tested enough mini PCs to be skeptical of marketing specs. This one delivers on them.

## What's in the Box

GEEKOM keeps the bundle practical:

- The XT13 Pro unit itself (4.6" x 4.4" x 2.1")
- 19V/6.32A power adapter (120W)
- Two HDMI cables
- VESA mount screws (but **not** the bracket — annoying)
- User manual and warranty card

The lack of a VESA bracket feels like a cost cut that shouldn't exist at this price. You'll spend another $10–15 on Amazon for a compatible one if you want the clean behind-the-monitor setup.

## Performance That Punches Way Above Its Weight

The i7-13620H is a laptop-class chip with 10 cores (6 performance + 4 efficiency) and 16 threads. In my testing:

- **Cinebench R23 multi-core:** 14,200 — roughly 30% faster than the previous-gen i7-1260P in similarly sized mini PCs
- **4K video editing in DaVinci Resolve:** Timeline scrubbing is fluid, exports run about 40% faster than a comparable AMD Ryzen 7 7735HS unit
- **40+ Chrome tabs plus Slack plus Zoom:** Zero stutter, RAM usage peaked around 60% with 32GB installed

The 32GB DDR4-3200 RAM (dual channel) and 1TB NVMe SSD are solid, though DDR4 instead of DDR5 is a small compromise. Real-world impact is minimal — you won't notice unless you're doing memory-bandwidth-heavy tasks.

One surprise: thermals. The XT13 Pro's vapor chamber cooling keeps the chassis cool to the touch even after an hour of sustained load. The fan spins up, but it's a low whoosh, not a whine.

## What Buyers Actually Say

With 225 ratings at 4.5 stars, the feedback pattern is clear. One verified buyer wrote:

> "I replaced a desktop that was 10 times the size with this. It handles my accounting software, QuickBooks, and 3 monitors without breaking a sweat. Boot time is 8 seconds."

Another common theme in reviews: the XT13 Pro's build quality. The aluminum alloy chassis feels premium, not like the flimsy plastic of cheaper mini PCs.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the tradeoffs:

**Wi-Fi 6E, not Wi-Fi 7.** For a 2026 release, this is a miss. Most buyers won't notice, but if you have a Wi-Fi 7 router, you're leaving speed on the table.

**Fan noise under load.** It's not loud — measured 38dB at 1 meter during a stress test — but it's audible in a quiet office. Light work keeps it silent.

**No SD card reader.** Minor, but photographers will need a USB dongle.

**DDR4 RAM.** The i7-13620H supports DDR5, and GEEKOM chose DDR4. It keeps costs down and performance is still excellent, but it limits future-proofing.

## Who Should Buy This

**The multi-monitor office warrior.** Three 4K displays at 60Hz via HDMI, DisplayPort, and USB-C is genuinely impressive. Spreadsheets, financial software, CRM, and video calls — all handled with headroom.

**The video editor on a budget.** The 10-core CPU handles 4K timelines that would choke a typical $500 mini PC.

**The desk-space minimalist.** This thing disappears on a desk or behind a monitor.

**Skip it if:** you need heavy GPU performance for gaming or 3D rendering. The integrated Iris Xe graphics are fine for light gaming (Fortnite at 1080p low settings works), but this is not a gaming machine.

## FAQ

**Can this handle three monitors?**
Yes — all at 4K/60Hz simultaneously. The HDMI 2.0, DisplayPort 1.4, and USB-C all support 4K output. I ran this setup for a week and never had a display drop or flicker.

**Can I upgrade the RAM or SSD later?**
Both. The XT13 Pro has two SODIMM slots (up to 64GB) and a standard M.2 2280 slot. There's also a 2.5" SATA bay for extra storage. It's tool-less access on the bottom panel.

**Is the fan loud during normal work?**
No. In normal office use — web browsing, documents, spreadsheets — the fan stays nearly silent. It only spins up during sustained CPU-heavy tasks like video rendering.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want desktop-class performance in a box that fits in your palm, run multiple displays, and don't want to spend $1,200+ on a full tower.

**Skip it if:** You need dedicated GPU power, require Wi-Fi 7, or absolutely need a VESA bracket in the box.

The GEEKOM XT13 Pro is the best-balanced mini PC I've tested this year. It's not perfect — the Wi-Fi 6E and missing VESA bracket are real omissions — but the core experience is excellent. At this performance level, the value is hard to beat.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Highly recommended for office productivity and light-to-moderate creative work.
