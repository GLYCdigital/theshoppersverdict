## Verdict

At $12.97 for a 25-pack of hanging file folders, the Amazon Basics option is significantly cheaper than what you'd pay at an office supply store — and according to over 126,000 reviews averaging 4.7 stars, it's also better. Sometimes the basics are all you need.

## Who Is This For?

Anyone with a filing cabinet, file box, or drawer that needs organizing. These are standard letter-size (8.5x11) hanging folders with adjustable tabs and label inserts. They work for home offices, medical clinics, small businesses, and personal filing systems. One reviewer bought them for their clinic office and was so impressed they bought two more packs.

## The Good

**Price-to-quality ratio is exceptional.** Multiple reviewers note that they bought these expecting cheap folders — and were surprised by the quality. One reviewer admitted, "I bought these because of their low price but have been impressed by the high quality." The folders are sturdy, the hanger rods are solid, and the colors are vibrant and consistent.

**The colors are a surprising highlight.** One reviewer said, "The color is spot-on and very vibrant." Another was practically giddy: "I feel like a huge dork getting so excited over folders, but I just got through replacing my ugly old red and yellow hanging file folders with these pretty aqua ones, and I can't believe what a difference it makes." Turns out organization aesthetics matter more than you'd think.

**Adhesive tabs and labels included.** The package comes with adjustable tabs and pre-sized label inserts that print or write cleanly. You can reposition them as your filing system evolves. For the price, having these included (instead of as a separate purchase) is a nice bonus.

**Proven at scale.** With over 126,000 reviews and a 4.7 average, this is one of the most-reviewed file folder products on Amazon. That's not a fluke — it's a product that consistently meets or exceeds expectations across tens of thousands of buyers.

## The Not-So-Good

**Tabs take a minute to set up.** The adjustable tabs require you to snap or slide them into position and insert the labels. It's not difficult, but it's not instant-out-of-the-box either. If you're organizing a whole filing cabinet at once, budget a few extra minutes.

**Not industrial-grade.** These are perfect for home and small office use. If you're filing hundreds of documents daily in a high-volume environment, you might want a heftier option. For everyone else, these are more than adequate.

## Bottom Line

At $12.97 for 25 folders — roughly 52 cents each — the Amazon Basics Hanging File Folders are a no-brainer. They're sturdy, color-matched, and include everything you need to get organized. With 126,000 positive reviews backing them up, this is one of those rare products where the hype and the reality align. **Highly recommended.**
