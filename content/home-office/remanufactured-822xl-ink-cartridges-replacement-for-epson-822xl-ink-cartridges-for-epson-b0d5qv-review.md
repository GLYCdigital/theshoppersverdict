**Q: Will these void my Epson warranty?**
A: In the US, the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act prevents manufacturers from voiding warranties solely for using third-party consumables. Epson could deny a claim if the cartridge caused the damage, but normal use is protected.

**Q: How many pages will I actually get?**

**Q: Can I mix these with OEM cartridges?**
