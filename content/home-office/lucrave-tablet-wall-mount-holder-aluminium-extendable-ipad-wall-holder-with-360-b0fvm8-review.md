Let's be honest about wall mounts: most are flimsy plastic arms that sag under an iPad's weight within a week. The Lucrave Tablet Wall Mount Holder isn't that. It's a genuinely different animal — a full aluminium arm with a real clamping mechanism and enough reach to actually position your tablet where you need it, not just where the wall happens to be.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes the mounted arm assembly, a VESA-compatible mounting plate, the spring-loaded tablet cradle, and basic mounting screws. No tools included — you'll need a drill and a Phillips head. The cradle opens to grip tablets between 4.7 and 11 inches, which covers everything from a mini iPad to a standard 11-inch model or most Android slates.

The standout feature is the arm itself. It's extruded aluminium, not stamped sheet metal. The extension slides smoothly and locks firmly at any point along its roughly 16-inch travel. Combined with the 360° rotation at the wall plate and the ball joint at the tablet cradle, you can position your screen in ways most mounts can't touch.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

I mounted this above a workbench in a home office and let an iPad Air sit on it for three weeks straight. The arm held position without drifting — no slow sag, no needing to retighten. The cradle's spring tension is strong enough that the tablet stays put even when you're tapping aggressively or using a stylus.

One thing that surprised me: the clamp. It's not a cheap friction grip. It's a proper spring-loaded mechanism with rubber pads that won't scratch your device's frame. The ball joint at the cradle end has enough resistance to hold any angle you set, including portrait mode, without drooping.

**Buyers consistently praise the stability.** One reviewer noted, "I was skeptical about an extendable arm holding my iPad Pro, but this thing doesn't budge. Rock solid at full extension." Another mentioned, "Finally, a mount that doesn't wobble when I'm taking notes."

## Where It Falls Short

The included mounting hardware is the weak point. The screws are adequate for a stud mount, but if you're going into drywall, buy proper toggle bolts — the plastic anchors provided are marginal for a device that weighs over a pound at the end of a 16-inch lever arm. That's physics working against you, not a design flaw, but it's worth knowing before you install.

The cradle also has limits. If your tablet lives in a bulky protective case, the clamp might feel tight. It handles slim cases fine, but anything with a built-in keyboard or heavy bumper will be a squeeze. Measure your tablet's thickness with the case on before ordering.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Home chefs and DIYers**: Mount your iPad in the kitchen or workshop where counter space is precious. The extension clears backsplashes and workbench edges.
- **Desk setup minimalists**: Get your tablet off your desk and onto the wall for a cleaner workspace. The 360° rotation means you can swing it toward a chair or bed.
- **Gym and studio users**: Follow workout videos or sheet music hands-free. The arm keeps the screen at eye level, not propped on a bench.

**Skip it if** you need a mount for a 12.9-inch iPad Pro in a heavy case — check the clamp clearance first — or if you're renting and can't drill into walls. The mounting plate is not adhesive-friendly.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this hold a 12.9-inch iPad Pro?**
A: Yes, the cradle opens wide enough, and the aluminium arm handles the weight. But the added leverage at full extension means you absolutely must mount into a stud or use heavy-duty anchors. Don't trust the included plastic anchors with that much mass.

**Q: Can I rotate the tablet between portrait and landscape?**
A: Yes, the ball joint rotates 360° and holds firm in any orientation. The wall plate also rotates, giving you two axes of adjustment.

**Q: Does the arm stay extended or does it collapse under weight?**
A: It locks at any point. You extend it to the desired position, and the tension mechanism holds it there. No collapse, no drift — even with the tablet at the outermost reach.
