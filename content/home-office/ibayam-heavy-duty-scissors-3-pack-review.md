You don't realize how many reasons you need a good pair of scissors until you own three of them. Suddenly, you have kitchen shears, office scissors, and craft scissors — and nobody's fighting over any of them. ## The Good

**These things are actually sharp.** Not "sharp for a cheap scissors" — genuinely sharp. Reviewers call them "ultra sharp" and "awesome scissors" that slice through paper, cardstock, tape, and light fabric on the first cut. The blades come with a protective coating out of the box, and when you peel it off, they're ready to work. Multiple users confirm they're "very sharp" and "comfortable to use."

**The hinge nut changes everything.** Here's a detail that sounds small but matters enormously: "Made with an actual nut at the hinge that can be retightened." Cheap scissors develop a loose pivot over time and become useless. iBayam put a real screw-and-nut assembly in there so you can re-tighten as the blades wear. For $3.33 per scissors, that's downright luxurious. You'll get years more life out of these than a comparable budget pair with riveted hinges.

**Ergonomics that work.** The grips are comfortable for extended use — important if you do a lot of cutting for crafts, shipping prep, or sewing. The finger loops are generously sized without being too large. Color-coded handles (red, black, blue) are a nice touch for households or offices where people want their own pair.

**Three pairs for $9.99.** Let's do the math: that's $3.33 per scissors. A single pair of Fiskars runs $8-$12. You could buy this whole pack for the price of one mid-range scissors from a brand name. The value proposition is absurd, and 85,889 verified buyers have already figured this out.

## The Bad

**Plastic handles feel budget.** The heavy-duty plastic handles are functional but they don't feel premium compared to all-metal or composite-handle alternatives. They won't break under normal use, but the tactile experience is distinctly "this cost $3." For most people, this is a fair trade-off at the price point. If you want scissors that feel like heirlooms, these aren't them.

**Not for precision work.** These are general-purpose scissors with straight blades. If you need curved surgical shears, embroidery snips, or fine detail blades, this pack won't cover those needs. The slightly bulky handle design also makes precision control harder for small cuts.

**Blade longevity is adequate, not exceptional.** The stainless steel is good for the price, but it won't match premium titanium-coated or high-carbon steel blades for edge retention. With the hinge nut adjustment, you can get good life — but expect to sharpen or replace after heavy use.

## The Verdict

The iBayam Heavy Duty Scissors 3-Pack is one of the best deals on Amazon. For $9.99, you get three sharp, comfortable, functional scissors with an adjustable hinge that cheap alternatives skip entirely. The 4.8-star rating from 85,889 reviews isn't a fluke — this is a product that understands exactly what its buyers need and delivers it at a price that makes competing options look overpriced.

Plastic handles and non-premium blade steel are the compromises you accept for the savings. For what most people need — cutting paper, opening packages, snipping fabric, kitchen tasks — these are more than good enough. Buy a pack for the house, a pack for the office, and yes, don't run with them.
