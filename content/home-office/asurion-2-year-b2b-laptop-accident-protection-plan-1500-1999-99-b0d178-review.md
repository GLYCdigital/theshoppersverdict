**Is ASURION's 2-Year B2B Laptop Accident Protection Plan worth your money?**

Here's the honest math: if you carry a $1,500+ laptop through airports, coffee shops, and client meetings, you've got roughly a 1-in-4 chance of dropping it or spilling something on it within two years. That's why this plan exists. The question is whether ASURION's execution matches the need.

**What you actually get**

The plan covers accidental damage — drops, liquid spills, cracked screens, and electrical surges — for laptops in the $1,500–$1,999.99 price tier. It's a two-year term, not a perpetual warranty. You get unlimited claims up to the laptop's purchase price, though each claim carries a $99 deductible.

The claim process is refreshingly modern: you file online, upload photos of the damage, and ASURION either repairs the unit or ships a replacement — typically within 3-5 business days after approval. No boxing up your dead laptop and waiting weeks for a depot repair.

**Where it shines**

The B2B angle matters. Unlike consumer plans that tie coverage to a single owner, this plan is transferable if you sell the laptop — a real advantage for businesses that rotate hardware. It also covers electrical surges, which fried more than a few laptops during last summer's storm season.

Customer feedback at 4.0 stars across 200 ratings is solid for an insurance product. One verified buyer noted: *"Dropped my ThinkPad off a table, screen cracked. Filed Tuesday, new laptop Friday. Zero hassle."* Another praised the photo-based claims: *"Didn't have to ship my broken laptop anywhere first. They just approved and sent a replacement."*

**Where it falls short**

The $99 deductible is the friction point. If you crack a screen that costs $180 to repair, you're paying $99 out of pocket plus whatever ASURION charges for the repair itself. For minor damage, you might be better off paying out of pocket and saving the plan premium for a real catastrophe.

The 30-day cancellation window is tight and non-refundable after that. Unlike some competitors that prorate refunds, ASURION keeps your money even if you never file a claim. And cosmetic damage — dents, scratches, worn hinges — isn't covered, which surprises some buyers expecting "accidental damage" to include everything.

**Who should buy this**

- **Travel-heavy professionals** who log 50+ flights a year with a laptop bag
- **Business fleet managers** outfitting 10+ employees who can't be trusted with hot coffee
- **Anyone with a $1,500+ laptop** who's had a close call in the past and knows the feeling of watching a laptop tip toward a tile floor

**Who should skip it**

- **Desk-bound users** whose laptop never leaves a docked setup
- **Budget-conscious buyers** who'd rather self-insure and bank the premium
- **Anyone with a laptop under $1,200** — the deductible-to-value ratio gets ugly

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this cover theft?**
A: No. This is accident-only coverage. Theft and loss require a separate policy like Worth Ave Group or your business insurer.

**Q: Can I file multiple claims?**
A: Yes, up to the laptop's purchase price. Each claim still carries the $99 deductible, so three claims means $297 in deductibles.

**Q: What happens if my model is discontinued?**
A: ASURION replaces it with a comparable current model at their discretion. You don't get to pick — but you do get a working laptop, which is the point.

**The Verdict: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if** you're a road warrior or managing a fleet of expensive business laptops. The transferable coverage and fast replacement process are genuinely valuable.

**Skip it if** your laptop rarely moves, you're on a tight budget, or you're confident in your ability to not spill coffee on $1,800 worth of silicon.

This plan won't make financial sense for everyone. But for the mobile professional whose laptop is their livelihood, $99 and a two-day turnaround beats a $700 motherboard replacement every time.
