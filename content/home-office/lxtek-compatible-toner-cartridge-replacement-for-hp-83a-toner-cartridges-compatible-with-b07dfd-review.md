## Where It Falls Short

Let's be real about downsides. A handful of users (maybe 3–4%) report **toner dust around the cartridge after installation**. It's not a dealbreaker — a quick wipe with a dry cloth fixes it — but it suggests the sealing isn't quite as clean as OEM.

The bigger concern: **printer warranty**. Using a compatible cartridge *can* void your HP warranty if the printer malfunctions. In practice, most buyers never hit this issue, and the Magnuson-Moss Act protects consumers in the US unless HP can prove the cartridge caused the damage. But if you're running a mission-critical office printer under active warranty, weigh the risk.

Finally, there's no OEM-grade recycling program. LxTek does accept used cartridges back, but you pay return shipping — not the convenience of HP's prepaid labels.
