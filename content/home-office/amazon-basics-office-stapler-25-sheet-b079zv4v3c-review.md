The Amazon Basics Office Stapler is $6.31. That's cheaper than lunch at a fast-casual restaurant. And it comes with 1000 staples included.

**It staples paper.** It staples 25 sheets with authority. Multiple reviewers pushed it past 29 sheets and the crimp held. The mechanism is smooth, doesn't jam, and the non-slip rubber base keeps it planted during one-handed operation. The staple tray loads with a satisfying push and click — no fiddling with tiny springs or misaligned staple strips.

**The all-plastic construction is the trade-off.** At $6.31, nobody is machining aluminum. The plastic is serviceable and the mechanism is solid, but drop this on tile and you're buying another one. For a desk stapler that lives on your home office surface, that's fine. For a shared office stapler that gets thrown in a drawer and knocked off the counter weekly, get something with a metal base.

**What's the catch?** There isn't really one. Reviewers across 52,000+ ratings are broadly in agreement: it staples, it doesn't jam, it costs less than a burrito. The lack of a built-in staple remover is a minor knock, but desk staple removers cost a dollar.

**Who is this for?** Everyone with a home office. Remote workers. Students. Anyone who needs a stapler that lives on a desk and does the job without drama. At this price, there's no reason to have "no stapler" when you need to staple something.

**The verdict:** The best $6.31 you'll spend on your home office this year. It's not going to win any design awards, and you probably won't pass it down to your grandchildren. But it will staple every piece of paper you put in front of it without complaint, and that's literally all a stapler needs to do.
