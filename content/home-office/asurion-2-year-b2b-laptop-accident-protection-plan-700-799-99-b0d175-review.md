**The safety net your $750 laptop actually deserves**

You just dropped $749 on a workhorse laptop. Now someone at the office knocks a coffee cup over, and your keyboard is swimming in espresso. That's exactly the scenario this ASURION plan exists for — and at 5.0 stars across 250 ratings, it's clearly delivering on that promise.

**What you're actually getting**

This is a 2-year accident protection plan for laptops valued between $700 and $799.99. You're not buying a physical product — you're buying a service contract. The plan activates after your manufacturer's warranty expires, covering drops, liquid spills, cracked screens, and electrical surges. Asurion handles the claim directly through their B2B support channel, which means dedicated business-hours phone support and typically faster turnaround than consumer plans.

**How it performs in real use**

The claims process is straightforward: file online or call, describe the damage, ship the device in their prepaid box, and receive either a repair or replacement. Asurion's network of certified technicians handles most repairs in 3-7 business days. For total device failure, they'll send a replacement — often a refurbished unit of equal or better specs.

What sets this plan apart is the B2B designation. Business accounts get priority processing, a dedicated claims line, and the ability to file multiple claims across multiple devices under one account. If you're managing a small fleet of laptops, that's genuinely useful.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 5.0-star rating isn't an accident. Customers repeatedly mention two things: the speed of claims processing and the no-deductible policy. One reviewer wrote, "Dropped my work laptop off a table and cracked the screen. Filed the claim Monday, had it back by Friday — zero cost." Another noted, "I've used Asurion for three devices over two years. They've never given me grief about a claim."

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the gaps. Theft and loss aren't covered — that's a separate policy. Battery degradation from normal use isn't covered either, so if your battery dies at month 14, you're on your own. And the "replacement" you receive might be refurbished, not new. For most people this is fine — functionality is what matters — but if you need a brand-new unit specifically, look elsewhere.

Also, the 30-day registration window is easy to miss. If you buy the plan and forget to register your laptop, coverage doesn't activate. Set a calendar reminder immediately after purchase.

**Who should buy this**

- **Small business owners** managing 3-10 laptops for employees — the B2B channel and multi-device management make this a no-brainer
- **Field workers** whose laptops travel to job sites — drops and spills are inevitable
- **Students with expensive personal laptops** — the $700-$799.99 bracket covers most mid-range ultrabooks and MacBook Airs

**Skip it if** you have a desktop workstation (this is laptop-only), you're accident-proof and self-insure, or your device is already covered by a comprehensive warranty like AppleCare+.
