Let's be honest: paper clips are about as exciting as printer paper. But the right ones save you from the silent rage of a clip that snaps mid-staple or gouges your thumb when you're shuffling a 40-page report. The 700pcs Assorted Sizes Large set isn't trying to reinvent the office — it's just trying to give you every clip size you'll ever need in one box. And for the most part, it succeeds.

**What's actually in the box**

The product image shows a clear plastic organizer with seven compartments — and that's exactly what you get. No deceptive marketing here. The breakdown is roughly:

- 1.1-inch mini clips (about 100)
- 1.3-inch standard clips (about 150)
- 1.6-inch medium clips (about 150)
- 1.9-inch large clips (about 100)
- 2.2-inch jumbo clips (about 100)
- Plus a small quantity of binder-style clips in the two largest sizes

The storage case is the unsung hero. It's sturdy enough to survive a desk drawer and the compartments are deep enough that clips don't spill when you close it. That alone is worth something — most clip sets ship loose in a flimsy bag and become a tangled metal spaghetti within a week.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

These are electroplated steel, and you can feel the difference from dollar-store clips. The wire gauge is consistent — no skinny, bendable junk that warps the first time you clip a 5-page document. The plating is smooth with no burrs or rough edges along the seams, which matters more than you'd think when you're handling them all day. I've had cheap clips catch on cuticles and leave tiny metal slivers. Not here.

The assorted sizing is genuinely useful. Mini clips handle receipts and sticky notes. Standard clips manage a few sheets. The jumbo sizes will hold a 50-page contract without bending or popping off. One thing to note: a few of the jumbo clips arrive stiff — you'll need a firm pinch on the first few uses before they loosen to a comfortable tension. It's not a dealbreaker, but don't expect butter-smooth action out of the box.

**What buyers consistently praise**

With 240 ratings averaging 4.8 stars, the feedback is overwhelmingly positive. A common thread in reviews: "Finally a set where the sizes are actually mixed and useful, not just 700 of the same tiny clips." Another frequent comment: "The case keeps everything organized — I can grab the size I need without sorting through a pile." Several reviewers also noted the clips hold up better than name-brand options from big-box stores, which is a solid endorsement for a product at this price point.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest limitation is the lack of color. If you use clips to color-code projects or clients, this set won't help — every clip is the same silver metal finish. You'll need to buy a separate colored set, which somewhat defeats the "all-in-one" value proposition.

Also, the case is functional but not beautiful. It's a plain clear plastic box, not a polished desk accessory. If you want something that looks good sitting out on your workspace, you'll want to transfer clips to a nicer container. And as mentioned, those stiff jumbo clips require a bit of breaking in.

**Who should buy this**

- **Home office users** who want one box that covers everything from light paper sorting to heavy document binding
- **Teachers or admin staff** who go through clips quickly and need multiple sizes on hand for different materials
- **Students** organizing notes, flashcards, and printed readings — the mini and standard sizes are perfect for this
- **Anyone tired of buying five separate clip packages** and ending up with a drawer full of mismatched sizes

**Skip it if** you need color-coded organization or you only ever use one clip size — you'll be paying for variety you don't need.
