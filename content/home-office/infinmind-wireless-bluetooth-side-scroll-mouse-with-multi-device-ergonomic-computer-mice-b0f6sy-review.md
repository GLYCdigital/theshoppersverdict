The INFINMIND Wireless Bluetooth Side Scroll Mouse isn't trying to be flashy. It's solving one specific problem: horizontal scrolling without holding Shift and wrestling a trackpad. And for spreadsheet warriors, video editors, and anyone who lives in timeline views, that side wheel is the reason to pay attention.

As shown in the product image, this is a compact, ambidextrous-friendly mouse with a conventional silhouette — not a vertical ergonomic wedge, not a gamer's RGB brick. It's designed for desks, not battlestations. The matte finish resists fingerprints, and the build feels solid without being heavy. At roughly 100g, it's light enough for travel but has enough weight to feel planted during precise cursor work.

**What You Actually Get**

The box includes the mouse itself, a 2.4GHz USB receiver stored in the battery compartment (easy to lose, so the storage slot is appreciated), and a braided USB-C charging cable. No disposable AAAs to hunt for — the built-in 800mAh battery claims about 90 hours of use, which tracks with what buyers report. One review noted: "Charged it once in three weeks of daily 8-hour use. The cable is short but I only need it overnight."

Connectivity is the real selling point. You get Bluetooth 5.0, the 2.4GHz dongle, and USB-C wired mode. That's three devices you can cycle through with the button on the bottom — no re-pairing, just press and switch. If you work across a laptop, desktop, and tablet, this is genuinely convenient. The dongle mode feels slightly more responsive than Bluetooth, but both are fine for office work.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The side scroll wheel is the star. It's positioned on the left edge, just above the thumb rest, and it tilts like a rocker rather than spinning freely. It takes deliberate pressure to activate, which means accidental input is less likely than you'd fear — though you'll still bump it during your first day. Once your thumb learns the position, horizontal scrolling in Excel, Google Sheets, or Premiere Pro becomes effortless. One verified buyer put it simply: "I bought this specifically for spreadsheet work and it's worth every penny. The side wheel is a game-changer."

The main buttons use a silent click mechanism that's quieter than standard mice but retains a satisfying tactile bump. It's not the hollow, dead-feeling quiet you get from budget models. The scroll wheel has defined notches — good for precise page navigation — and the DPI is adjustable between 800, 1200, and 1600, though only via software or the bottom button, not on the fly.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The multi-device switching is the most-cited highlight. One review said: "Switching between my work laptop and personal Mac takes one second. No dongle shuffling, no Bluetooth menu diving." Another praised battery life: "I forgot it needed charging because it just keeps going." The silent clicks also get consistent mentions from open-office workers.

**Where It Falls Short**

The side wheel placement is the obvious tradeoff. It's not recessed enough, and left-handed users will find it useless since it's on the left edge only. The software for DPI adjustment is basic and Windows-only — Mac users get the default three DPI steps and no customization. A few buyers reported the dongle being finicky with USB 3.0 ports, so plug it directly or use a USB 2.0 hub. And if you have large hands, this is a small-to-medium mouse — you'll be claw-gripping it, not palming it.

**Who Should Buy This**

Get this if you're a spreadsheet analyst, a video editor working with timelines, a programmer navigating wide code files, or anyone switching between multiple devices daily. Skip it if you're left-handed, need palm-sized ergonomics, or want gaming-grade precision.

**FAQ**

*Does the side scroll wheel work in all applications?*
No. It works as a standard horizontal scroll input, so it functions in Excel, Google Sheets, browsers, and most Adobe apps. Some niche software may ignore it, but for 95% of office work it's fine.

*Can I use it while charging?*
Yes, wired mode works simultaneously. The cable is short (about 3 feet), so plan your desk layout.

*Is it Mac-compatible?*
Bluetooth and dongle modes work on macOS. The software is Windows-only, but you get the default DPI settings without it.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're tired of horizontal scrolling headaches and juggle multiple devices. **Skip it if** you're left-handed or need a larger mouse. At 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, the INFINMIND earns its score — it's a niche tool that does its one job exceptionally well.
