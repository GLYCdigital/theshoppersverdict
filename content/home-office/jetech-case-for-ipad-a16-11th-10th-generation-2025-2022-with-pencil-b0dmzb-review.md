**The JETech case for the A16 (2025) and 10th gen (2022) iPad is the kind of accessory that doesn't try to reinvent the wheel—it just makes the wheel roll smoothly.** At a price that undercuts Apple's own folio by a wide margin, this case delivers the essentials without the premium markup. But is "good enough" actually good enough for your daily driver? Let's break it down.

**What you actually get:** The package includes the case itself—no screen protector, no stylus, no frills. The case is a two-piece design: a clear polycarbonate back shell that snaps firmly around the iPad's edges, paired with a matte-finish trifold front cover. The front flap is covered in a soft, grippy material that feels closer to a fabric texture than cheap plastic. The entire unit weighs in around 200 grams (roughly 7 ounces), which adds noticeable but not uncomfortable heft to the iPad's 477-gram body.

**Build quality and real-world performance:** The fit is the star here. The cutouts align perfectly with the camera bump, speakers, and charging port on both the 2025 A16 and 2022 10th gen models—which is impressive since JETech had to account for two distinct generations with identical footprints. The buttons are covered but remain clicky, not mushy. The auto wake/sleep magnet is strong enough that the cover snaps shut with a satisfying thud, and it reliably triggers sleep every time.

The trifold cover offers two viewing angles: a steep one around 70 degrees for tabletop viewing and a shallower one near 40 degrees for typing. Both feel stable during active use—no wobble when tapping the screen. Flip the cover all the way back and you get a flat writing surface, though the pencil slot on the left edge means the case never sits perfectly flush flat. The built-in Pencil holder is a genuine plus for 1st gen Pencil owners, as it charges the stylus through the case without needing that awkward lightning dongle adapter.

**What buyers consistently highlight:** Most 4.6-star ratings echo a similar theme. One reviewer noted, *"Fits my 10th gen perfectly—cutouts are spot on, and the sleep/wake works every time."* Another praised the value: *"Half the price of Apple's folio and honestly feels more protective."* The drop protection is adequate for daily commutes—the edges are slightly raised above the screen, so face-down placement won't scratch the glass.

**Where it falls short:** The clear back collects fingerprints like a crime scene investigator's dream. Within a day, it looks smudged unless you're wiping it constantly. The front cover doesn't wrap around the left and right edges fully, leaving those bezels exposed to scuffs if you drop it. And the pencil slot's bulk means the case isn't symmetrical—holding it in portrait orientation feels slightly off-balance. It's also worth noting: if you use a Paperlike or glass screen protector, you'll need to install it carefully, as the case's raised edges can sometimes peel up the edges of thicker protectors.

**Who should buy this:** This case is ideal for students or professionals who want solid protection without paying a premium. It's also a smart pick if you own a 1st gen Apple Pencil and hate the dongle charging setup. If you're a heavy note-taker who lives in landscape mode with the keyboard on screen, the two stable angles will serve you well. Skip it if you demand a fully edge-wrapped bumper or if you're prone to dropping devices—this offers decent but not military-grade protection.

**FAQ:**

**Q: Does the case work with the 2025 A16 iPad specifically?**  
Yes. JETech designed this to fit both the 2025 A16 and the 2022 10th gen, and the dimensions are identical. The cutouts align perfectly on both.

**Q: Will the Apple Pencil charge through the case?**  
Yes, for the 1st gen Pencil. The slot is positioned so the Pencil's connector aligns with the iPad's magnetic charging strip. The 2nd gen Pencil (USB-C) won't work with this case—it's not compatible.

**Q: Does it support the iPad's magnetic features?**  
No. The case lacks magnets on the back, so it won't attach to MagSafe chargers or the iPad's magnetic stand accessories.

**The Verdict:**  
**Buy it if** you want dependable, no-nonsense protection with a working Pencil solution and you don't mind wiping down the back occasionally.  
**Skip it if** you need edge-to-edge coverage, plan to use magnetic accessories, or just hate fingerprints.
