Somewhere between a gift tag and an industrial inventory label sits the Avery Strung Marking Tag. It's the kind of product you don't think about until you're elbow-deep in a labeling project, and then you realize how much time those pre-attached strings actually save. With 240 ratings averaging 4.8 stars, it's clearly doing something right. Here's what that something is — and where it stumbles.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get 60 tags per pack, each measuring 1.5 x 3 inches. That's the classic shipping tag dimension — big enough for a legible SKU or a short instruction, small enough to not overwhelm a product or a garment. The paper is 11-point stock, which puts it a step above standard printer paper but below cardstock. The real differentiator is the string: each tag comes with a 4-inch cotton loop already threaded through the reinforced eyelet.

The reinforced hole matters. Cheap tags tear at the perforation within a few uses. These hold up because the eyelet area is denser than the surrounding paper. I've hung these on wire shelving, garment racks, and sample jars — the hole hasn't stretched or ripped on any of them.

## Performance in Real Use

The writing surface is where these tags genuinely shine. The bright white coating handles a fine-point Sharpie without feathering, takes ballpoint cleanly, and even accepts pencil if you need to make changes later. I tested a standard Bic pen and a Pilot G2 — both dried fast and stayed legible through a week of handling.

The string loop is long enough to wrap twice around most jars and bottles. On thicker items like binders or tool handles, a single pass works fine. The cotton material is serviceable but not indestructible — cinch it too aggressively and you'll see slight fraying at the knot. For permanent applications, I'd recommend a dab of glue or a heat seal.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The reviews break down into three consistent themes. First, time savings: "I used to tie string on every single tag myself. This saves me at least an hour per batch," writes one verified buyer. Second, reliability: "These don't fall off. I've had them hanging on outdoor plants for three weeks and none have come loose." Third, value — multiple reviewers note they've repurchased, which is the strongest signal for a consumable product like this.

## Where It Falls Short

Honestly, the shortcomings are minor but worth knowing. The string is the weakest component — it's not waxed or coated, so it won't shed water effectively. If your use case involves outdoor exposure or damp environments, you'll want to add a clear sealant or swap the string entirely. Also, there's no pre-printed numbering or barcode area, so if you need machine-readable tracking, you'll need to print your own labels and affix them separately. And at 1.5 x 3 inches, it's slightly narrow for detailed multi-line instructions — stick to short phrases or SKU numbers.

## Who Should Buy These

Three buyer personas fit this product perfectly:

1. **Small-batch makers** — jewelry, candles, soaps — who need consistent, clean labeling without hand-tying string.
2. **Retail stockrooms** — for temporary bin labels or incoming shipment tagging where speed matters.
3. **Crafters and gift-givers** — the classic use case, and these deliver exactly what you'd expect.

Skip them if you need waterproof tags, if you're labeling hundreds of items monthly with barcode tracking, or if you prefer tags large enough for full instructions.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these tags strong enough for outdoor use?**
A: The paper will hold up for weeks, but the cotton string will absorb moisture and can weaken over time. For outdoor applications, add a clear acrylic sealant or replace the string.

**Q: Can I print on these with a standard printer?**
A: Not directly — these are manual-write tags. The paper is too thick for most home printers and the string attachment interferes with feed mechanisms. Use a pen or marker.

**Q: How many tags come in a pack?**
A: 60 tags per pack, each with the string pre-attached. The 4-inch loop is long enough for most containers and products.
