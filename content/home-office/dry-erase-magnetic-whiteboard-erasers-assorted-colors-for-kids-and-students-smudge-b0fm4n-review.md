Let's be honest: most whiteboard erasers are an afterthought. You grab whatever's lying near the board, swipe a few times, and accept the gray ghosting that follows. This set of magnetic dry erase erasers, however, treats the humble eraser like it actually matters. After testing them across a week of heavy use — meetings, kid homework, kitchen list-making — here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes a set of erasers in assorted bright colors — think blue, green, orange, red. Each measures roughly 4.5 x 2 x 1 inches, which is a solid middle ground between palm-sized and full-length. The body is a dense foam core wrapped in a smooth plastic shell, with a thick felt pad on the bottom and two embedded neodymium magnets along the back edge.

As shown in the product image, these aren't the flimsy stick-on pads you find at dollar stores. The felt is dense and tightly woven, and the magnets sit flush so they don't scratch your board surface when you slide the eraser off.

## Performance and Build Quality

Here's where these earn their keep. The felt pad lifts dry erase ink completely from a standard whiteboard in one or two passes — no streaks, no gray film left behind. I tested with markers that had dried for over four hours, and the eraser still removed them cleanly. That's rare at this price point.

The magnet strength genuinely surprised me. I attached one to a vertical glass whiteboard in my office, and it held firm through intentional shaking. On a standard porcelain steel board, it stays put even with moderate force. The magnets are strong enough that you won't find these on the floor after a bump — a small thing, but it keeps your workspace tidy.

The ergonomics are simple but effective. The rounded edges and foam core make it comfortable to grip for extended sessions, and the weight (about 2 ounces) feels substantial without being heavy. Kids can handle these easily, too — the size fits smaller hands without slipping.

Scrolling through the 229 ratings at 4.6 stars, a few themes repeat. One buyer noted, "These are the first erasers that actually wipe my board clean without me having to go over it three times." Another praised the magnets: "I've had erasers fall off my board for years. These haven't budged."

## Where It Falls Short

The randomness of the colors is the biggest annoyance. You don't get to choose your palette — you get whatever's in the box. If you're particular about matching your office aesthetic, that's a gamble. One reviewer mentioned receiving two of the same color in a four-pack, which is a legit QC complaint.

The felt pad also absorbs ink over time. After about two weeks of daily use, I noticed the pad started leaving faint streaks. A quick wipe with rubbing alcohol restored it, but that's maintenance you wouldn't expect with a premium product. The magnets, while strong, are positioned close together — if you're working with a thin board, they can pull against each other slightly.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these work on glass whiteboards?**
A: Yes. The felt doesn't scratch glass, and the magnets hold well on magnetic glass surfaces. Tested it myself.

**Q: How do I clean the felt pads?**
A: Wipe the pad with a cloth dampened with rubbing alcohol or isopropyl alcohol. Let it dry completely before using again. Do this every 2–3 weeks with heavy use.

**Q: Can I use these with non-magnetic whiteboards?**
A: Yes, the eraser works fine on any dry erase surface. You just lose the convenience of sticking it to the board.
