## What You Actually Get

Open the box and you'll find 150 individual mesh zip pouches, each measuring approximately 8.7 x 4.3 inches — roughly the size of a standard pencil case. The construction is straightforward: a clear vinyl front panel for visibility, mesh backing for breathability, and a metal zipper running across the top. The colors arrive mixed randomly, so you'll get a rainbow assortment rather than a coordinated set.

For the price point, the build quality is the real story here. The zippers aren't the flimsy plastic pulls you'd expect at this volume — they're actual metal hardware that moves smoothly. The vinyl front is thick enough to resist punctures from sharp pencils, and the stitching along the edges held up during my testing without any loose threads or separation.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. The mesh weave is open enough that tiny items — think pencil shavings, small erasers, or individual crayon stubs — can work their way through if you're not careful. For fine-item storage, you'll want to use these for bulkier supplies or add a small interior bag.

The random color assortment is another genuine frustration. If you're trying to implement a color-coding system across multiple classes or departments, you can't specify which colors you receive. You get what you get. For most bulk buyers this is a non-issue, but it's worth knowing before you plan around specific hues.

Also, these are lightweight pouches — not reinforced cases. They'll protect against dust and light compression, but don't expect them to shield contents from a heavy drop or crush damage.
