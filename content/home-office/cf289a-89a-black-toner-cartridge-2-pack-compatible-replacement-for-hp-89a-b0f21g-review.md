You get two individually sealed CF289A cartridges. The cartridges come pre-installed with a chip that HP printers recognize without complaint. The print quality holds up where it matters. Multiple buyers describe output as "crisp" and "identical to OEM." ## Where It Falls Short

No product is flawless, and this one has two genuine tradeoffs.

First, there's no recycling program. HP's OEM cartridges come with a prepaid return label through their Planet Partners program. This compatible brand doesn't offer that. You'll need to check if your local office supply store accepts toner cartridges for recycling — Staples and Office Depot do, but it's an extra step.

Second, the packaging is functional but minimal. The cartridges come in basic plastic wrapping without heavy foam protection. Shipping damage is rare but not impossible. One buyer mentioned their box arrived slightly dented, though the cartridges inside were fine. If you're buying a large quantity, inspect the packaging on arrival.
