**Performance and build quality**

The printhead is where budget cartridges usually fall apart. This one holds up better than most in its price range. Text documents come out crisp and dark on standard copy paper — I’d rate it 90% of the way to genuine HP output. Third, the color match with genuine HP cartridges in a mixed setup (one OEM, one this brand) can produce slight banding on color graphics. Not a dealbreaker for text, but worth knowing.

**FAQ**

*Will this void my HP printer warranty?* No. In the U.S., the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act protects you from being forced to use OEM consumables. HP cannot void your warranty just for using third-party ink, provided the cartridge doesn’t physically damage the printer.

*Does it work with HP Instant Ink?* No. This is a standard cartridge, not an Instant Ink subscription cartridge. You can’t use it in a printer enrolled in that program.

*How do I know my printer model is compatible?* The HP 65/65XL cartridge fits the Envy 6000 series, 6400 series, 1200 series, and the DeskJet 2700-4100 series. Check your printer’s manual or the cartridge slot — if it takes the black 65 cartridge, this fits.
