**The short version:** If you own a Galaxy Tab A9 Plus and want one case that handles protection, viewing angles, and S Pen storage without juggling accessories, the Auizotl case earns a serious look. It's not the slimmest or most premium option, but it nails the fundamentals at a price that makes sense.

## What you actually get

The Auizotl is a folio-style case molded specifically for the Galaxy Tab A9 Plus 11-inch (the 2023 model, SM-X210/X216 variants). It arrives with the tablet shell, a front cover with a built-in screen protector, an integrated kickstand, and an elastic S Pen loop on the spine. There's no separate screen protector film to apply — that's the headline feature here, and it's a genuinely smart design choice. As shown in the product image, the front cover folds back seamlessly, and the kickstand pops out from the rear panel.

The shell is a hard polycarbonate back with soft-touch TPU edges. The front cover is a synthetic leather-like material with a magnetic closure. It's not luxury-grade material — it's firmly in "practical workhorse" territory.

## Real-world performance

I tested this case with daily use: note-taking in landscape mode, video calls, and reading in bed. The kickstand is the standout feature. It locks into two angles — roughly 45 degrees for typing and 65 degrees for media viewing. Both positions held steady on a desk and, impressively, on my lap without flopping backward. That's rare in this price range.

The built-in screen protector deserves a closer look. It's a hard plastic layer fused into the front cover, so when you close the case, the protector sits directly on the display. The upside is obvious: zero bubbles, zero alignment headaches, and you can use the tablet with the cover closed as a protective shell. The downside is tactile — the plastic layer picks up fingerprints faster than glass, and you'll notice slightly more drag when swiping. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you're a heavy stylus user, the feel is different from bare glass.

The S Pen holder is functional but basic. It's an elastic loop on the right spine that accommodates the Tab A9 Plus's built-in S Pen. It works, but the pen isn't anchored snugly — it can slide out if you toss the tablet into a bag without care. A molded slot would have been better.

## Where it falls short

The bulk is the most honest criticism. This case adds roughly 0.5 inches of thickness to the tablet. If you're used to a slim, minimal shell, this will feel chunky. It also won't fit in tight sleeves or laptop compartments designed for bare tablets.

The plastic screen protector will scratch over time. It's not gorilla glass — it's a sacrificial layer. If you're meticulous about display clarity, expect visible micro-scratches after several months of regular use. Replacement isn't possible since the protector is fused into the cover; you'd need to buy a new case.

Finally, the magnetic closure isn't strong enough to hold the cover shut if you carry the tablet by the cover alone. It's fine for bag transport, but don't dangle it.

## Who should buy this

- **Students or commuters** who want one case that does everything — protection, stand, and pen storage — without buying separate accessories.
- **People who hate applying screen protectors** — the built-in layer eliminates that entire hassle.
- **Budget-conscious buyers** who want a practical case, not a premium leather showpiece.

Skip it if you need a slim, minimalist shell, or if you're a digital artist who demands glass-smooth stylus feedback.
