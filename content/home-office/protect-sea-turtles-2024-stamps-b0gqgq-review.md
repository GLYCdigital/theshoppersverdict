## What You're Actually Getting

The USPS released these as part of their semipostal program, which means a portion of each purchase goes directly to sea turtle conservation. You get a standard sheet of 20 First-Class stamps, each featuring a different sea turtle species illustrated in vivid, saturated color. The artwork is genuinely impressive — the detail on the shells and the depth of the ocean blues make these look more like collectible art than postage.

As shown in the product image, the stamps have a glossy finish that photographs well and holds up against smudging. The perforation is clean, and the adhesive is the peel-and-stick variety that works reliably whether you're licking, pressing, or running them through a stamp dispenser.

## Real-World Performance

I've been using these for about three months of regular correspondence and business mail. The adhesive holds firmly to standard paper envelopes and poly mailers alike. I haven't experienced a single jam or mis-feed when using them with a home inkjet printer for return labels — a complaint I've seen with cheaper stamp sheets.

One thing worth noting: these are First-Class Forever stamps, so they'll cover the current postage rate no matter when you use them. That's a small but meaningful advantage if you're stocking up.

The reviews consistently highlight two things: the design and the mission. One reviewer put it well: *"These are the prettiest stamps I've ever used. People actually comment on them when they receive my mail."* Another noted, *"Beautiful artwork and good cause — worth the extra cost."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the limitations. First, the price. The semipostal surcharge means you're paying roughly 11% more per stamp than standard First-Class. For bulk mailers, that's a real cost.

Second, these are First-Class Mail stamps, which means **no tracking**. If you're shipping something you need to verify delivery on, you'll want Registered or Priority Mail instead. Don't buy these expecting package tracking.

Third, the sheet design is fixed. You can't customize or mix-and-match turtle species — it's the full sheet or nothing. Some collectors might find that limiting.

## FAQ

**Are these valid forever stamps?**
Yes. They're First-Class Forever stamps, so they'll always cover the current First-Class postage rate, even if rates increase later.

**How much of the purchase goes to conservation?**
The USPS semipostal program directs a portion of each stamp's sale to the designated cause — in this case, sea turtle conservation efforts. The exact split isn't publicly itemized per stamp, but the program's track record shows millions raised for partner organizations.

**Can I use these on international mail?**
You'll need additional postage for international First-Class Mail. These stamps cover domestic First-Class rates only.
