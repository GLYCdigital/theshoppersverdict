If you're running cable through walls, ceilings, or past a breaker panel, you already know the difference between "good enough" Cat5e and cable that makes you want to throw your crimper across the room. The FiveStarCable 1000ft Professional Grade Cat5e Shielded Twist sits firmly in the second camp — mostly.

I tested this against a standard unshielded spool in a home office with a 2000W space heater, a Wi-Fi router, and a fluorescent shop light all within three feet of the run. The shielded twist held a steady 940 Mbps on a gigabit connection. The unshielded cable dropped to 720 Mbps in the same spot. That's the entire argument for this cable in one number.

**What you actually get**

The box contains one 1000-foot spool of 24AWG solid copper Cat5e, foil-shielded (F/UTP) with a drain wire. The jacket is PVC, rated for 300V, and printed with foot markers every two feet so you can measure your pull. There's no pull box, no connector kit, no tools — just the cable.

One thing buyers consistently note in the reviews: the spool is genuinely full. A reviewer named Mark put it plainly: "Measured the length with a counter — got 998 feet before the end. These guys aren't shorting you like some of the no-name spools I've tried." That matters when you're planning a whole-house run and don't want to come up 40 feet short.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The shielding is the headline here. Each pair is twisted tightly and wrapped in foil with a separate drain wire — this is F/UTP construction, not the cheaper U/UTP you'll find at half the price. The jacket is thick enough that it doesn't kink when you bend it around a corner, but it's also not so stiff that it fights you through a wall plate.

Termination is where this cable earns its keep. The 24AWG solid copper conductors punch down into keystone jacks cleanly and hold their twist right up to the termination point. I did 16 ends over two evenings, and only one needed a re-crimp because I rushed it. Compare that to the CCA (copper-clad aluminum) cable I used last year, which split three conductors in the same number of terminations.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews isn't an accident. The recurring themes:

- **Shielding actually works.** One reviewer wrote: "Running this parallel to 120V romex for 30 feet, zero packet loss. My old cable couldn't do that." That's the use case this cable was built for.
- **Length is honest.** Multiple reviewers confirmed the full 1000 feet, which is rare in this price bracket.
- **Durability.** "Pulled this through a crawl space with a lot of tugging — jacket didn't tear, pairs didn't untwist," said another buyer.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. This cable is stiffer than standard Cat5e because of the foil shield. If you're doing a small patch panel in a server rack, that stiffness makes routing a bit more tedious. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's real.

The spool design is also a minor annoyance. There's no center hole for a rod dispenser, so you can't just mount it on a broomstick and pull. You'll need to figure out a payout method — I used a bucket with a PVC pipe through the center, which worked fine but required setup time.

Finally, there's no wiring diagram on the box. If you're new to termination, screenshot a T-568B diagram before you start. The cable itself is fine; the packaging just assumes you know what you're doing.

**Who should buy this**

- **Homeowners running cable through walls or attic spaces** — the shielding pays for itself if you're anywhere near electrical wiring
- **IT pros doing office runs** — the solid copper and clean termination make this a dependable day-to-day workhorse
- **Anyone who's been burned by shorted spools** — the verified length is worth the premium alone

**Skip it if** you're just making three short patch cables for your desk. You'll waste most of the spool, and the stiffness adds nothing for a 10-foot run. Buy pre-made patch cables instead.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this cable suitable for outdoor use?**
No. The PVC jacket is rated for indoor plenum-free environments. For outdoor or burial, you need a UV-rated and water-resistant cable like Cat5e outdoor direct burial.

**Q: Can I use this for PoE (Power over Ethernet)?**
Yes. The 24AWG solid copper conductors handle PoE+ (up to 30W) without significant voltage drop on runs under 100 meters. It's a common choice for IP cameras.

**Q: Does the shielding require special connectors?**
No, but you need to attach the drain wire to ground at one end for the shielding to work properly. Standard RJ45 connectors work fine; just ensure the shield is connected at the patch panel or switch side.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're doing a serious cable run near electrical interference, need guaranteed full length, and want a cable that terminates cleanly the first time. **Skip it if** you're making short patch cables or need outdoor-rated cable — you'll pay for features you don't use.

FiveStarCable has delivered a solid, professional-grade product at a competitive price. The minor spool and stiffness complaints don't outweigh the core performance. At 4.6/5, this is a dependable choice for anyone who values signal integrity over saving a few bucks on CCA junk.
