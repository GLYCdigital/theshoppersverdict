If you own a Canon imageCLASS printer that takes CRG-128 toner, you already know the frustration of gambling on third-party cartridges. The good news: this 2-pack from Canon eliminates that gamble entirely. The bad news: you'll pay for the peace of mind. Here's the full picture.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains two sealed Canon CRG-128 black toner cartridges (model 3500B001AA). Each cartridge is rated for approximately 2,500 pages based on the ISO/IEC 19752 standard — that's the industry benchmark, not Canon's wishful thinking. Installation takes about 60 seconds: pull the orange tab, shake gently, slide into your printer. No chips to reset, no firmware error messages.

## Real-World Performance

I tested these in an imageCLASS MF267dw over a three-week stretch of mixed printing — text documents, shipping labels, and the occasional grayscale graphic. Text comes out razor-sharp at 600 dpi with no streaking or ghosting. The black density is deep and consistent from page one through the final pages. One thing that stood out: no toner dust residue inside the printer after swapping cartridges, which is a common complaint with cheaper compatibles.

The yield claim holds up. I tracked print counts and hit roughly 2,400 pages before noticing lighter output on dense areas — close enough to the stated 2,500 that I don't consider it misleading. For a home office printing 200-300 pages a month, one cartridge should last 8-12 months.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides. First, price. OEM toner always carries a premium, and this 2-pack is no exception. You'll likely find third-party options at 30-50% less. If you print high volume and don't care about warranty implications, that's worth considering — though you risk the quality issues that prompted many of those same Amazon reviewers to switch back to Canon.

Second, there's no XL or high-yield version of the CRG-128. If you're burning through 1,000+ pages a month, you'll be replacing these frequently. That's a design limitation of the cartridge family, not this specific listing.
