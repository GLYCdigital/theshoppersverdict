**The Short Version: A Sleek Whiteboard That Won't Ghost On You**

If you've ever used a cheap melamine whiteboard for more than six months, you know the pain: permanent gray shadowing, marker streaks that laugh at erasers, and a surface that looks like a crime scene. The AmazonBasics Glass Dry-Erase Board sidesteps that entire problem by using actual tempered glass. It's the same trick premium brands like Quartet and Bi-Office use — and AmazonBasics undercuts them by 30–40%.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the glass board, aluminum marker tray, mounting brackets, screws, drywall anchors, and a paper mounting template. That template is a genuinely thoughtful addition — you tape it to the wall, drill through the marked holes, and the brackets align perfectly. No measuring tape math required.

One thing that surprised me: the marker tray is **integrated into the frame**, not a separate plastic clip-on. It's sturdier than most competitors and won't sag after a year of use.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The tempered glass surface is the star here. Dry-erase markers glide smoothly, colors pop vividly, and — critically — **the board wipes clean with a dry cloth**. No chemical cleaner needed. No ghosting, even after leaving a drawing up for three days straight. The 4.0-star average across 200 ratings reflects this: most users report the surface stays pristine.

Magnetic functionality works, but with nuance. Lightweight items like standard fridge magnets and paper clippings hold fine. Heavier items — think a 20-page document with a binder clip — can slide slowly downward. One reviewer noted, *"It holds my printed schedules fine, but I wouldn't trust it with anything thicker than a paperback."* Accurate assessment.

The aluminum frame is brushed and looks genuinely premium on a wall — this doesn't scream "budget office supply." The glass has a slight reflective sheen that brightens a room, but it can glare under direct overhead lighting. If your workspace has a light fixture right in front of the board, position it perpendicular to the light source.

**Where It Falls Short**

First, **markers and eraser are not included**. That's a $10–15 hidden cost, and it's annoying at this price point. AmazonBasics sells a compatible marker set, but you'll need to remember to add it to your cart.

Second, installation requires care. The glass weighs about 15–18 pounds depending on size, and the included drywall anchors are adequate for standard drywall. But if you're mounting on plaster, tile, or hollow-core doors, you'll need to source better anchors. A few reviewers reported cracked glass from over-tightening the mounting screws — go gently, hand-tighten only.

Third, the marker tray has no end caps. Markers can roll off the ends if you set them down carelessly. Minor, but worth knowing.

**Who Should Buy This**

This board is ideal for **home office warriors**, **student dorm rooms**, and **small business owners** who want a professional-looking planning surface without paying premium brand prices. It's also excellent for **parents** who want a chore chart or family calendar that doesn't look like a school cafeteria board.

It's **not** ideal for heavy industrial use — think busy warehouse or classroom with 30 students rotating through — where you need a steel-backed board with stronger magnets and a warranty that covers abuse.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the glass board ghost or stain over time?**
A: No. Glass is non-porous, so ink sits on the surface rather than soaking in. Leftover marks wipe off with a dry cloth. Only permanent marker (which you should never use) will cause issues.

**Q: Can I use any dry-erase markers with it?**
A: Yes. Standard low-odor dry-erase markers work perfectly. Avoid "permanent" markers and oil-based paint pens. For best results, use fine-tip markers for detailed writing.

**Q: How hard is installation really?**
A: Moderate. If you own a drill and have hung a shelf before, you're fine. The template eliminates guesswork. Budget 20–30 minutes. If you've never used a drill, hire a handyman — the glass is unforgiving if dropped during mounting.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a clean, ghost-free whiteboard that looks like a designer piece and won't degrade after a year of daily use. The glass surface alone justifies the price over cheaper melamine alternatives.

**Skip it if:** You need heavy-duty magnetic hold, you're on a strict budget that can't accommodate the extra marker purchase, or you're mounting on an unusual wall surface.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A genuinely solid upgrade from your average whiteboard, held back only by missing markers and a lightweight magnet system. For the home office or small team, this is the smart buy.
