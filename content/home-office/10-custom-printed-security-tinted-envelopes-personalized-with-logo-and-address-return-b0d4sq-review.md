**What you get**

The box contains your chosen quantity of standard #10 envelopes (4.125" x 9.5" — the size that fits a standard letter without folding). Each one has your logo and return address printed in the corner, plus the interior security pattern that's the headline feature here. The printing is applied via a digital process that produces sharp, consistent results — multiple buyers noted the colors matched their brand guidelines exactly. One reviewer put it simply: "The print quality is honestly better than what I get from my local print shop."

The envelopes also feature a self-seal adhesive strip. For those who've dealt with cheap self-seal envelopes that pop open in transit, this is worth noting: the adhesive here is aggressive. Once you press it down, it's sealed. You won't be reopening it without tearing the paper.

**Real-world performance**

The security tint is the reason to buy these. It's not a gimmick — the interior pattern is dense enough that holding the envelope to a bright light reveals nothing but a dark blur. That matters for anyone mailing checks, invoices with bank details, or confidential client documents. The security tint has a visible side effect: the outside of the envelope carries a faint blue-gray cast from the interior pattern. It's subtle, but if you're comparing them side-by-side with standard white envelopes, you'll notice. A few customers specifically mentioned this, with one saying, "They look slightly 'office-y' gray rather than crisp white. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing."

The bigger issue is the ordering structure. You're committing to a minimum of 100 envelopes per design. That's fine for small businesses, medical offices, or anyone who sends regular mail. But if you're an individual who mails ten letters a month, you'll be sitting on these for years. There's no smaller trial size to test print quality or color matching before committing to a full run.
