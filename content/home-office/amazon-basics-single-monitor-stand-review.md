The Amazon Basics Single Monitor Stand is the pragmatic choice in a world of premium monitor arms. With over 18,000 reviews and a 4.4-star average, it's one of the most-reviewed monitor mounts on Amazon. At roughly $24, it costs less than a tank of gas while delivering the same core functionality as arms costing three times as much. How does it manage to be this good at this price?

## Where Amazon Basics Gets It Right

The key to this mount's success is its design simplicity. The vertical support pole is a chunky 1.9 inches in diameter — significantly thicker than many competing arms — which eliminates the wobble and sag that plague cheaper alternatives. Once clamped to your desk, the monitor stays exactly where you put it.

The cable management is genuinely effective. Cables run inside the hollow pole and exit through a slot at the base, keeping your desk free of dangling wires. For a $24 arm, this is a feature you'd expect to be an afterthought, but it works as well as premium options.

Range of motion covers the essentials: tilt forward and backward, swivel left and right, rotate to portrait mode, and height adjustment along the pole. The head uses a tool-free tension adjustment that makes repositioning easy without needing to hunt for an Allen key.

Users consistently describe it as "rock solid" and "surprisingly good for the price." Many reviewers upgraded from generic unbranded arms and noted a significant improvement in stability and build quality.

## Where It Shows Its Price

The main limitation is height range. The arm is best suited for seated desk setups. Taller users and those with standing desks sometimes find that the maximum height isn't enough to keep the monitor at eye level when standing. If you use a sit-stand desk, you may need to plan around this constraint.

Quality control is generally good, but not flawless. The vast majority of units are fine, but a small percentage of reviewers report issues with stiff joints, scratches, or missing hardware. Amazon's return policy makes these easy to resolve, but it's worth noting.

The clamp design is solid but basic. It works well on standard desktops (up to roughly 2 inches thick), but may not fit desks with thick edges or metal frames.

## Who Should Buy It

This is the easiest recommendation in the monitor arm category. If you have a single 13-30 inch monitor on a standard desk and want to reclaim space, improve ergonomics, and clean up cable clutter, the Amazon Basics stand delivers at a price that's hard to justify passing up.

It's also ideal for office deployments, secondary workstations, or any situation where you need multiple arms without a premium budget. At $24, you could buy five of these for the price of one premium Ergotron arm.

For ultrawide monitors, heavy displays, or users with very specific height needs, premium options offer more range and capacity. But for the majority of standard monitor users, the Amazon Basics Single Monitor Stand is the best value in the category — and 18,000 reviewers agree.
