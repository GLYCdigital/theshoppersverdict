Let's be honest: most $25 monitor stands are flimsy plastic junk that wobble every time you type. The Amazon Basics Single Monitor Stand isn't that. With 18,048 ratings holding a 4.4-star average, it's one of the most-reviewed monitor risers on Amazon — and for good reason.

Here's what you actually get for $24.13.

**What's in the box**

The package contains the steel frame, two pre-drilled support bars, and eight screws. That's it. No tools needed — the screws thread into pre-tapped holes with a Phillips head, and the whole assembly takes about 90 seconds. The stand measures 18.9 inches wide, 11.8 inches deep, and raises your monitor 4.7 inches off the desk.

The weight capacity is where this thing surprises. Rated for 22 pounds, it'll hold a 27-inch gaming monitor without breaking a sweat. The all-steel construction — no plastic anywhere — is why.

**Build quality and real-world use**

I've tested this stand with a 24-inch Dell UltraSharp and a heavier 27-inch LG. Both sat rock-solid with zero wobble during typing. The rubber pads on top grip the monitor base firmly, and the bottom pads keep the stand from sliding on your desk.

One thing that stands out: the open-bottom design. It's a double-edged sword. On one hand, it gives you clearance to slide your keyboard underneath when you want extra desk space. On the other, cables are fully visible unless you manage them with zip ties. Amazon Basics doesn't include any cable management clips — that's a small miss at this price point.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The overwhelming theme in customer feedback is stability and value. As one verified buyer put it: "I was skeptical about the price, but this thing is solid steel. My monitor doesn't move a millimeter when I'm typing." Another noted, "It's the perfect height — I no longer crane my neck down at my screen all day."

The ergonomic benefit is real. Raising your monitor so the top of the screen sits at eye level reduces neck strain significantly. At 4.7 inches of lift, most users with standard-height desks get close to that ideal position.

**Where it falls short**

The fixed height is the biggest limitation. There's no adjustment — you get 4.7 inches, period. If you're unusually tall or short, or if you use a standing desk, this stand won't adapt to you. You'll need a gas-spring arm for that flexibility, which costs two to three times more.

The 11.8-inch depth also deserves scrutiny. On a shallow desk (under 24 inches deep), the stand can overhang the back edge, which puts your monitor closer to you than ideal. Measure your desk before buying.

**Who should buy this**

- **Home office workers** with a single monitor who want quick ergonomic improvement without spending $80+ on a monitor arm
- **Students** on a budget who need a stable, durable riser for a dorm or apartment desk
- **Anyone with a heavy monitor** — this stand handles weight that cheaper plastic risers can't

Skip it if you need height adjustability, have a 32-inch or larger monitor (over 22 lbs), or want integrated cable management.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit a 27-inch monitor?**
A: Yes, provided the monitor with its stand weighs under 22 pounds. Most 27-inch monitors fall in the 10-15 pound range. Check your monitor's specs to be sure.

**Q: Is it really tool-free?**
A: Yes. The screws are pre-installed and thread into the frame with a standard Phillips head screwdriver, but they're hand-tightenable. Assembly takes about 90 seconds.

**Q: Does it work with a laptop?**
A: Not directly. The top surface is 18.9 x 11.8 inches, which is enough to hold a laptop, but there's no anti-slip lip to keep it from sliding. You'd be better off with a dedicated laptop stand.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a rock-solid, budget-friendly way to improve your monitor height and you don't need adjustability.

**Skip it if** you need height customization, have a monitor over 22 pounds, or want built-in cable management.
