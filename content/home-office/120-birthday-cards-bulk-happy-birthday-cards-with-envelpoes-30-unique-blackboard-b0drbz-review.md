Let's be honest: buying 120 birthday cards in bulk feels like a decision you make once and regret for a decade if you get it wrong. The good news? This set from the blackboard collection is one of the rare bulk purchases that actually makes sense for offices, small businesses, and families who send a lot of mail.

**What You Actually Get**

Open the box and you're looking at 120 cards total, broken into 30 unique designs with four copies of each. Every card comes with its own envelope — that's 120 envelopes, not the 100 you sometimes get with cheaper sets. The cards measure a standard 5x7 inches, which means they'll fit in standard mailing envelopes without extra postage.

The blackboard design is the headline here. Each card features a chalkboard-style background with hand-lettered "Happy Birthday" typography in different color combinations. As shown in the product image, the designs range from bold neon chalk colors to more subdued white-on-black options. The variety is genuinely useful — you won't accidentally send the same card to two people in the same office.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The cardstock is 300gsm, which is noticeably thicker than the flimsy 200gsm cards you'll find at dollar stores. It holds its shape in the mail, and the matte finish doesn't smudge when you're writing on it. The envelopes are standard weight with a self-seal adhesive strip — nothing fancy, but they close securely.

One thing that surprised me: the interior is completely blank. No pre-printed message, no sentiment. That's actually a plus for a bulk set because you're not stuck with one generic verse. But it does mean you should test your pen before committing. Regular ballpoint pens work fine, but gel pens can smear on the textured surface. A fine-tip Sharpie or a chalk marker is your best bet.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews isn't accidental. The most common praise centers on the design variety and the envelope fit — two things that sound minor but matter enormously when you're sending dozens of cards.

One reviewer noted: *"I run a real estate office and we send these to clients all year. The variety means I never send the same card twice to the same person, and the quality is better than what I was buying at the office supply store."*

Another buyer mentioned: *"I was skeptical about the envelopes — usually they're way too small or too loose. These fit perfectly, no jamming in the printer, no licking envelopes that don't seal."*

**Where It Falls Short**

The blackboard aesthetic is a double-edged sword. It reads as trendy and fun, but it's not appropriate for milestone birthdays. If you're sending a card to a retiree turning 65 or a grandparent hitting 80, the chalkboard look might come across as too casual. This is a set for everyday birthdays, not formal occasions.

Also, the 120-card quantity is a commitment. If you're not actively sending cards on a monthly basis, you'll be storing a large box for a long time. It's not a problem if you have closet space, but it's worth considering before you buy.

**Who Should Buy This**

This set is ideal for three specific types of buyers:

- **Office managers** who want a professional-yet-friendly card supply that covers birthdays for staff and clients all year.
- **Real estate agents and financial advisors** who send cards as relationship-building touches and need a reliable bulk supply.
- **Large extended families** with a constant stream of birthdays — the variety keeps things interesting across generations.

Skip it if you only send a handful of cards per year, or if you need formal, traditional designs.

**Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask**

**Do the envelopes have a self-seal strip?**
Yes, they do. You peel and press — no licking required. The adhesive is strong enough that cards won't pop open in transit.

**Are all 120 cards identical?**
No, that's the whole point. You get 30 unique designs, four copies of each. That means you can send 30 different cards before you repeat a design.

**Can I write on the blackboard surface with a regular pen?**
Ballpoint pens work but can feel scratchy. Gel pens may smear. For the best results, use a fine-tip permanent marker or a white chalk marker — both write cleanly and dry quickly.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a year-round supply of birthday cards in bulk and want design variety without sacrificing quality. The 300gsm cardstock, properly sized envelopes, and 30 unique designs make this a solid value for offices and busy households alike.

**Skip it if** you need formal, traditional cards or only send a few birthday cards per year. The 120-card quantity will feel excessive, and the casual blackboard aesthetic won't suit every occasion.

At 4.6 out of 5, this is one of the better bulk card purchases you can make. The quality holds up, the variety keeps things fresh, and the price per card works out to pennies when you factor in the envelopes. For a product that sits in your supply closet until you need it, that's a solid return.
