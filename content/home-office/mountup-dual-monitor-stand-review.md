## The $60 Desk Upgrade That Actually Delivers

Most dual monitor arms under $70 feel like they were designed by someone who's never mounted a monitor. The MOUNTUP Dual Monitor Stand is the exception. With 7,000+ ratings holding a 4.4-star average, it's become the default budget pick for good reason — but it's not without compromises. Here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The box includes two fully assembled gas-spring arms, a C-clamp and grommet mount base, VESA plates with quick-release tabs, and all necessary hex tools. No drill required. The arms are rated for 4.4 to 17.6 pounds per side, supporting screens from 17 to 32 inches. The pole itself is solid steel with a powder-coated finish that won't scratch easily.

As shown in the product image, the arms extend from a central pole, giving you independent height, tilt, and rotation on each side — not a fixed bar that forces both monitors to move together. That's a significant difference from cheaper twin-arm designs.

## Performance and Build Quality

The gas-spring mechanism is the star here. It holds position without drifting, even after weeks of daily repositioning. One reviewer noted, *"I've had this for six months and the arms still stay exactly where I put them — no sag, no droop, no needing to retighten."* That's consistent with the broader feedback pattern.

The 360° rotation on each VESA plate means you can flip a monitor vertical for coding or document work without swapping hardware. The 15° tilt and 180° swivel give enough range for most desk setups, though you won't get the full ergonomic range of a $200 Ergotron — and you shouldn't expect it at this price.

Assembly takes about 15 minutes if you follow the included manual. The VESA plates attach with a tool-free quick-release mechanism, which is genuinely convenient for single-handed monitor mounting. One caveat: the hex bolts on the arm joints require tightening with the provided wrench, and you'll want to check them after a week of use as they can loosen slightly.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The dominant theme in reviews is value. At $59.99, this competes with arms costing twice as much. Specific praise centers on:

- **Stability**: Multiple reviewers with ultrawide monitors report no wobble during normal typing, even at full extension
- **Desk space recovery**: The clamp base frees up 8-10 inches of desk depth compared to stock monitor stands
- **Cable management**: The integrated channels on each arm keep cords contained and out of sight

## Where It Falls Short

The clamp mount only fits desks up to 3.15 inches thick. If you have a solid hardwood desk with an apron, you'll need the grommet mount — which is included, so you're not stuck, but it requires drilling a hole if your desk doesn't already have one.

Cable management channels are narrower than ideal. Thick braided cables (like many DisplayPort cords) require significant force to snap the covers closed. Plan to use slim cables or accept a slightly open channel.

Some users report that the arms don't extend quite far enough for a 32-inch dual setup, especially if your desk is shallow. At full extension, the monitors sit closer together than some prefer. Measure your desk depth before buying.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Hybrid workers** setting up a home office on a budget who want ergonomic monitor positioning without paying premium prices
- **Programmers or analysts** who need one monitor in portrait mode and one in landscape — the 360° rotation handles this effortlessly
- **Anyone with a desk under 3 inches thick** who wants to reclaim workspace from bulky stock stands

Skip it if you have an unusually thick desk, need arms rated for monitors over 18 pounds, or want tool-free height adjustment on the fly — this requires a hex wrench for vertical height changes on the pole.

## FAQ

**Will this hold two 32-inch monitors?**
Yes, up to 17.6 pounds per arm. Most 32-inch monitors weigh 12-15 pounds, so you're within spec. However, the arms have a limited depth range — measure your desk to ensure the monitors won't extend past the front edge.

**Does it work with a standing desk?**
Yes, and this is a popular use case. The gas springs maintain tension as the desk height changes, so you won't need to readjust when transitioning from sitting to standing.

**Is the grommet mount usable without drilling?**
Only if your desk already has a hole. The grommet base fits standard 0.3-2.4 inch diameter holes. If you don't have one, the included clamp is your better option.
