Let me be direct: most packing cubes are glorified zippered laundry bags. The pack all Waterproof Travel Packing Pouches set actually tries to be more — and mostly succeeds. With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, this 6-piece set has earned its place in carry-on rotations. But "waterproof" claims deserve scrutiny, and I've put that to the test.

## What's Actually in the Set

Six pouches in graduated sizes: three large (roughly 14" x 10" x 4"), two medium (11" x 8" x 3"), and one slim toiletry-style pouch (9" x 6" x 2"). The exterior is a ripstop nylon with a TPU coating on the interior seams — that's the key difference from cheaper cubes that just spray a water-repellent finish on the outside. The zippers are double-stitched with rubberized pulls that actually grip, even with wet hands.

## Performance: Where It Shines and Where It Doesn't

I tested the waterproofing claim the way you should: stuffed a medium pouch with a damp towels and a leaky travel shampoo bottle, sealed it, and left it in my backpack for 48 hours. Zero moisture on the exterior. The TPU seam taping works. This isn't marketing fluff — it's a genuinely sealed barrier.

The compression feature is real but modest. Rolling clothes inside and zipping the top layer down saves maybe 20-25% vs. flat packing. That's meaningful for a week-long trip in a 40L bag, but don't expect vacuum-bag magic.

Here's the honest downside: the zippers fight you when you overpack. At roughly 80% capacity, closing the larger pouches becomes a two-hand wrestling match. The fabric has zero give, and the zipper track is tough. One reviewer put it well: *"Great quality, but if you stuff them to the brim you'll be fighting the zipper for five minutes."* Overstuffing also puts stress on the seams — I'd rather under-pack than risk a blowout mid-trip.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 235 ratings, three themes dominate:

1. **Durability.** *"I've used these on 12+ flights and they still look new,"* one verified buyer noted. The double-stitching and TPU coating hold up where cheaper cubes fray.

2. **True waterproofing.** Multiple reviewers mentioned using the smallest pouch for toiletries with zero leaks. One traveler wrote: *"My liquid soap exploded inside and nothing else in my bag got wet. That's the test that matters."*

3. **Organization clarity.** The translucent window on each pouch (as shown in the product image) lets you spot contents without unzipping everything — a small touch that saves real time at airport security.

## Where It Falls Short

The exterior scuffs easily. Dark-colored pouches show creases and white scuff marks after just one or two trips through overhead bins. It's purely cosmetic, but if you're particular about gear looking pristine, that'll annoy you.

The largest pouches are also too big for some personal-item bags. If you're flying with a strict 18" x 14" x 8" under-seat limit, the biggest cube won't fit standing upright — you'll need to lay it flat, which wastes vertical space.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a frequent flyer who packs toiletries, gym gear, or damp items and wants true leak protection. Also ideal for business travelers who need organized, crush-resistant separation between suits and casual wear. The compression is a bonus, not the main event.

**Skip it if:** You're a minimalist packing only a carry-on personal item — the set is bulkier than flat-packing alternatives. Also skip if you want a soft, flexible cube that yields to overstuffing; these are rigid and unforgiving when full.
