**The 240Hz Sweet Spot: Is LG's Ultragear the Best QHD Value of 2026?**

Most 240Hz QHD monitors cost a fortune or cut corners on panel quality. The LG 27GR83Q-B sits in a rare middle ground—it's not the cheapest, but it delivers the performance where it actually counts. After spending time with this 27-inch Ultragear, here's the honest breakdown for anyone eyeing that "buy" button.

**What's in the Box**

You get the monitor panel, a sturdy height-adjustable stand, power adapter, DisplayPort cable, and HDMI cable. No USB hub, no speakers, no RGB lighting—LG kept the extras minimal to hit a competitive price point. The stand is a highlight though: it's rock-solid with full tilt, swivel, and pivot adjustment. As shown in the product image, the three-sided "virtually borderless" design makes it look clean on any desk.

**Performance: Where It Earns Its Keep**

The headline spec is the 240Hz refresh rate on a 2560x1440 IPS panel, and it delivers. In fast-paced titles like Valorant or Apex Legends, motion clarity is exceptional—you'll notice the difference from a 144Hz panel immediately. The 1ms GTG response time holds up in practice, with minimal ghosting in normal use. You'll need a DisplayPort connection to unlock the full 240Hz; the HDMI 2.1 port caps at 144Hz, which is a minor annoyance but not a dealbreaker.

Color accuracy is genuinely impressive for a gaming-focused display. LG claims 95% DCI-P3 coverage, and in real-world testing, it holds up. Out of the box, the sRGB mode is well-calibrated—no fiddling required for accurate colors out of the box. Brightness peaks at 400 nits, which handles bright rooms but isn't HDR-worthy despite the marketing label.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews reflects genuine satisfaction. One top reviewer noted: *"Upgraded from a 144Hz VA panel and the difference is night and day—no smearing, colors pop, and the stand is actually usable."* Another common thread: *"Plug and play with zero dead pixels or backlight bleed out of the box."* Build quality gets consistent thumbs-up, with the matte finish resisting fingerprints and glare well.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the flaws. Black uniformity is only average—IPS glow is noticeable in dark scenes, especially in the corners. If you're a horror game fan or do dark-room editing, this will bother you. There are no built-in speakers, which is unforgivable at this price point when cheaper monitors include basic audio. The on-screen display menu is also dated; the joystick control works well, but the UI feels clunky compared to competitors like ASUS or Gigabyte.

**Who Should Buy This**

This monitor is perfect for competitive gamers who want esports-level refresh rates without sacrificing color quality for single-player titles. It's also a strong pick for hybrid use—you can game at 240Hz and then do color-sensitive photo editing thanks to the accurate IPS panel. Budget-conscious buyers with mid-range GPUs (RTX 3060 Ti or better) will appreciate that QHD resolution is far more achievable than 4K at high frame rates.

**Skip It If...**

You're on a strict budget—there are solid 144Hz QHD options for significantly less. If you need built-in speakers or USB-C connectivity for a laptop setup, look elsewhere. And if you're sensitive to IPS glow or play mostly in dark rooms, a VA panel might serve you better despite the motion blur tradeoff.

**FAQ: Quick Answers**

**Q: Does this monitor support G-Sync or FreeSync?**
A: It's officially G-Sync Compatible and supports FreeSync Premium. Both work over DisplayPort with minimal flicker issues reported.

**Q: Can my console run 240Hz?**
A: The HDMI 2.1 port supports up to 144Hz at QHD. PS5 and Xbox Series X will cap at 120Hz via HDMI—still smooth, but not the full 240Hz.

**Q: Is the stand worth keeping or should I mount it?**
A: The stock stand is excellent—height, tilt, swivel, and pivot all work smoothly. Unless you need specific desk space, save your money on a VESA mount.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You're a competitive gamer who wants a fast, color-accurate QHD panel that doubles as a productivity display. The 240Hz refresh rate and solid build quality justify the price for serious players.

**Skip it if:** You're a casual gamer on a budget, need speakers, or prioritize deep blacks over motion clarity.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A genuinely great gaming monitor held back by a few cost-cutting omissions. For the right buyer, it's worth every penny.
