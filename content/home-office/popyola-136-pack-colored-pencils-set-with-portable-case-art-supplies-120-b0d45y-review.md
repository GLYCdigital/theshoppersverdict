**What you actually get:** The star of the show is the portable case itself. As shown in the product image, the case displays the full spectrum at a glance, which makes color selection genuinely fast. **How they perform:** The cores are soft enough to lay down rich pigment in two passes, but firm enough to hold a point for detailed linework. The color range is genuinely the selling point; you get multiple transitions of every hue, so shading gradients feel seamless. It's manageable — sharpen past the break and you're fine — but it's a real fragility concern for younger kids who aren't gentle. Also, the case is about the size of a large tablet, so it won't slide into a standard backpack pencil pouch. You'll be carrying it as its own item.

**Who should buy this:**

**FAQ:**

**Q: Do the colors match what's shown on the case?**
A: Mostly yes, but like any dyed pencil, the printed color names are approximations. The actual pigment leans slightly warmer than the case swatches for reds and oranges. Check the physical swatch chart included inside the case lid before you commit to a dark shading pass.

**Q: Are the pencil tips pre-sharpened?**
A: Yes, all 136 arrive sharpened. The included sandpaper block is enough to maintain points for weeks, but you'll eventually want a quality manual sharpener for when tips get dull.

**Q: Will these work for watercolor techniques?**
A: No. These are standard wax-based colored pencils, not water-soluble. If you want watercolor effects, look for a watercolor pencil set instead.
