Paper clips are supposed to be boring. You grab a handful, clip a report, and forget they exist. But the ACCO Jumbo Smooth clips (model 72580) have quietly earned a 4.7-star rating from 235 shoppers — and after testing a box, I understand why.

These aren't the flimsy wire clips that ping across your desk when you look at them wrong. The jumbo size measures roughly 2 inches from end to end, with a beefier gauge wire than standard clips. That extra metal translates into real holding power. I clipped a 25-page contract and a 40-page manual side by side. Neither budged when I shook them or slid the papers into a file folder. The clips held their shape through repeated use — no permanent bending, no spring-back failure.

**What you actually get**

The box contains 100 clips finished in a classic silver tone. The "smooth" in the name refers to the coating — it's a slick, snag-free surface that glides over paper instead of catching on fibers. That's a meaningful upgrade over cheap clips that scratch or tear documents. As one reviewer put it: "These don't leave marks on my papers like the cheaper brands do. They just feel more polished."

The clips arrive loose in the box, not individually wrapped, which is standard for this category. Nothing fancy — just a functional cardboard box that's easy to store in a drawer or toss in a desk organizer.

**Where they shine**

The holding power is the headline feature. The jumbo size distributes pressure across more surface area, so you can clip thick stacks without the clip bowing outward. I also appreciate the smooth finish for a practical reason: it doesn't snag on sweater sleeves or bag linings when you're carrying documents. Several Amazon reviewers noted the same thing, with one writing, "I've had other clips tear holes in my shirt pockets. These haven't done that once."

They're also genuinely reusable. I've bent and rebent the same clips dozens of times over two weeks of testing. The steel springs back to its original shape without losing tension. That's not something I can say for the dollar-store clips I've used in the past.

**The honest downsides**

The smooth coating has a tradeoff: on glossy or coated paper stock, these clips can slide off more easily than textured clips. If you're working with presentation covers or laminated documents, you'll want to double-check the grip before handing the stack to someone. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing.

The 100-count box is also a modest quantity. If you're clipping documents all day, this box will disappear in a few weeks. There's no bulk pack option in this listing, so high-volume users should factor in repurchasing.

**Who should buy this**

- **Legal or medical office staff** who handle thick client files daily — the jumbo size handles the bulk
- **Students** wrestling with multi-chapter readings and printed syllabi
- **Anyone tired of cheap clips** that bend, snap, or leave rust marks on important papers

**Skip it if** you only need to clip a few pages occasionally — standard-size clips will do the job for less money.

**Three questions shoppers ask**

**Q: Are these clips rust-resistant?**
The steel has a protective coating, and reviewers report no rust issues after months of use. That said, no paper clip survives being submerged in water long-term. Keep them dry and they'll last.

**Q: How many pages can one clip hold?**
I comfortably clipped 40 pages without the clip distorting. Past 50 pages, the tension starts to strain, but for everyday use, 30–40 pages is a safe range.

**Q: Do the clips leave marks on documents?**
The smooth finish leaves minimal indentations compared to standard clips. Heavy stacks will leave a subtle crease after extended clamping, but nothing that damages the paper.

**The verdict**

The ACCO Jumbo Smooth clips do exactly what a paper clip should do — hold firmly, release cleanly, and survive reuse. They're not flashy, but they're dependable. The 4.7-star rating is earned, not inflated.

**Buy it if** you need reliable clips for thick document stacks and want a smooth finish that won't shred paper or snag clothing.
**Skip it if** you rarely clip more than a few sheets or need a bulk supply — this single box won't last heavy daily use.
