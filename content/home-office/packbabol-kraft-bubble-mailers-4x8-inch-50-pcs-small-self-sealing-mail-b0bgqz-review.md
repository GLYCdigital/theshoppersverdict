**Build quality and real-world performance**

The kraft paper is the standout. It's not that flimsy recycled stock you get with budget brands—this has real rigidity. The self-seal adhesive deserves specific praise. It's aggressive enough to stick on first contact—you can't reposition the flap once it's pressed. **FAQ**

**Are these really self-sealing, or do I need tape?**  
They're genuinely self-sealing. Peel the release strip, press the flap down for two seconds, and it holds. I'd still add a strip of tape if you're shipping something heavy and the package might be tossed.

**What's the actual internal size?**  
The external dimensions are 4x8 inches. Inside, you lose about half an inch from the bubble lining and flap overlap—so plan for items up to roughly 3.5x7.5 inches.

**Can I write on these with a sharpie?**  
Yes, but use a fine-point marker. The kraft surface absorbs ink quickly, and broad-tip markers can bleed slightly.
