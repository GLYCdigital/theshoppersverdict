**What you actually get**

This is a bulk box. Inside, you’re getting 250 letter-size folders in five colors — typically red, yellow, blue, green, and orange. Each folder uses the standard 1/3-cut tab system: three tab positions along the top edge, allowing you to stagger labels across a drawer for quick scanning. The stock is 11-point manila-weight cardstock, which is thicker than the cheap 9-point folders you’ll find at dollar stores. RAZCC also pre-scores the tabs so they pop out cleanly — no jagged perforation edges that catch on your fingers.

**Performance and build quality**

The 11-point stock is the headline feature here. In practice, that means a folder packed with 50–75 sheets still holds its shape when you pull it from a drawer. The color coating is applied on both sides, and it’s a matte finish that resists fingerprints — important when you’re handling these all day. Customers consistently confirm the durability. One reviewer put it plainly: *“I've had these for a few months of heavy daily use and they don't show wear. The color hasn't faded or rubbed off on my hands.”* Another noted, *“They're stiffer than the ones I used to buy at the office supply store, and the tabs fold cleanly every time.”*

The colors deserve specific praise. These aren’t pastel variations that blur together under fluorescent lighting. The red is genuinely red, the green is forest-toned, and the orange is vivid. If you’re color-coding clients, projects, or months, the distinction actually works.

**Where it falls short**

No product is perfect, and this one has a few honest tradeoffs. First, there are no interior dividers. If you’re filing multi-part projects, you’ll need separate divider sets — this is purely a folder, not a filing system. Second, because the stock is stiff, the folder doesn’t “hug” a small stack of papers the way flimsier folders do. If you have a drawer with only a few sheets per folder, they can stand a bit rigid and lose the natural slump that keeps tabs aligned. A handful of reviewers mentioned this. One said: *“Great folders, but they're so thick they take up slightly more drawer space than I expected. I had to remove one row to fit everything.”*

Also, the color distribution in the box is not individually sorted. You get roughly 50 of each color, but RAZCC doesn’t guarantee exact counts per shade. For most people this is irrelevant; for a strict color-coder who needs 50 exactly of each, it’s a minor gamble.

**Who should buy this**

This pack is ideal for three buyer types. First, the small-business owner setting up a client file system — the color coding alone saves minutes per day. Second, the home organizer tackling a multi-year paper purge; 250 folders will cover an entire home office reconfiguration. Third, the teacher or administrator managing student records, where the 1/3-cut tabs allow alphabetical staggering without custom labels.

It’s less ideal for someone who needs just 20 folders, or for archival storage where you want acid-free, lignin-free stock. RAZCC doesn’t advertise archival-grade materials, so if you’re storing irreplaceable documents for decades, spend more on museum-grade folders.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these folders letter-size or legal-size?**
A: These are letter-size (8.5 x 11 inches). If you need legal-size (8.5 x 14), look for a different listing.

**Q: Can I write directly on the folders?**
A: Yes. The matte finish takes ballpoint, gel, and marker without smearing — just give marker a few seconds to dry.

**Q: How much weight can one folder hold before the tab tears?**
A: In practice, customers report success up to roughly 100 sheets (about an inch thick). Beyond that, the tab position can strain. For heavy files, use a reinforced fastener folder instead.
