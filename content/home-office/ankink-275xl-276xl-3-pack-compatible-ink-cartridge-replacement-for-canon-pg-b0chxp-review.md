The chip recognition was the big test: both black cartridges registered instantly, and the tri-color didn't throw a single error message. ## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the color warmth, there are two honest downsides. **The 3-pack configuration is lopsided** — two blacks to one color. If your primary use is document printing, you'll run through the blacks and still have color left, forcing an earlier reorder. Your only option is buying another 3-pack, which means accumulating surplus color cartridges.

Second, **the ink level monitoring isn't always accurate**. I saw the low-ink warning appear at 312 pages, but the cartridge kept producing clean output for another 40+ pages. That's technically a bonus, but it makes planning refills harder — you never quite know where you stand.
