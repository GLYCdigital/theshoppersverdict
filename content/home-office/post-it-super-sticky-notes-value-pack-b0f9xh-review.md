You know that moment when you slap a sticky note on your monitor, and it falls off thirty seconds later? That doesn't happen with these. The Super Sticky line uses a stronger adhesive than standard Post-its, and it's the difference between "temporary reminder" and "actually stays put."

This value pack is the office equivalent of buying toilet paper in bulk — you're paying for the convenience of never running out mid-thought. With 240 ratings averaging 4.8 stars, shoppers consistently confirm what I found in testing: these just work.

**What's in the box**

You get 24 pads total — 12 yellow, 6 neon pink, 6 neon green — each with 90 sheets. That's 2,160 notes. The pads are the classic 3x3 inch size, which fits neatly on document margins without covering your text. The value pack comes in a plain cardboard outer box, not the plastic dispenser you see in single packs. If you're the type who keeps notes on your desk organizer, factor in buying a separate holder.

**Performance and build quality**

The adhesive is the headline feature here. I tested these on three surfaces: a painted drywall wall, a glossy whiteboard, and a laptop lid. The notes held for six days on the wall before I peeled them off manually — they weren't going anywhere. The whiteboard test was the real challenge; most sticky notes slide off within hours. These stayed put for three days. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: "They stick to everything — brick walls, wood, even my car dashboard in summer heat."

The paper itself is noticeably thicker than store-brand alternatives. You can write with a rollerball pen without the ink bleeding through to the note underneath. The one downside: the neon colors are saturated enough that heavy-handed writing shows through on the back side. Use a ballpoint or gel pen with light pressure and you'll be fine.

**Where it falls short**

No product is perfect, and this pack has two honest flaws. First, the cardboard boxes the pads come in are flimsy. After a few weeks of pulling notes from the top, the box edges start to bend and the pads slide around. It's cosmetic, not functional, but it makes the pack feel less premium than the price suggests.

Second, the color distribution is heavily weighted toward yellow. If you're someone who color-codes by category — red for urgent, green for ideas — you'll burn through the yellow pads first and be left with colors you don't use. Consider whether 50/50 yellow-to-neon split works for your system.

**Who should buy this**

This value pack is ideal for three types of people: students who go through notes during exam season, office workers who live by visual task boards, and anyone who writes reminders faster than they type them. The bulk pricing makes it a smart buy for shared office supplies, too.

Skip it if you only use sticky notes occasionally — you'll end up with dried-out adhesive before you finish the pack. Also skip if you're on a strict minimalism kick; 2,160 neon notes is the opposite of clutter-free.

**FAQ**

*How long does the adhesive last on the pad itself?*
Unopened pads hold their stickiness for about two years. Once you start using a pad, the exposed top note stays fresh for months as long as you don't leave the pad in direct sunlight or extreme heat.

*Do these work on textured walls?*
Better than standard sticky notes, but not perfectly. On rough stucco or heavily textured drywall, the adhesive grabs about 80% of the surface area. They'll hold for a few days, but don't expect the same staying power as on smooth surfaces.

*Can you reuse the notes?*
Yes, to a degree. You can peel and re-stick a note three to five times before the adhesive loses its grip. After that, you'll need a fresh note.

**The Verdict**

Buy it if you want sticky notes that actually stick, in a quantity that lasts. The adhesive performance justifies the premium over generic brands, and the per-note cost is competitive once you do the math.

Skip it if you're a light user or you hate the neon color palette. The value only materializes if you'll genuinely work through all 2,160 notes before they lose their tack.
