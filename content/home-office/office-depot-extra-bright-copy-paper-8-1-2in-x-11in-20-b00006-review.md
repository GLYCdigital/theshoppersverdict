This is a standard 500-sheet ream of 8.5 x 11-inch paper, 20 lb bond weight (75 gsm equivalent). The headline feature is the **94 brightness rating**—that’s a solid step above generic 92-brightness office paper. ## Performance and Build Quality

I tested this paper in a laser printer, an inkjet, and a high-volume copier. The standout trait is **jam-resistance**. The 20 lb weight has enough stiffness to feed reliably, and the clean-cut edges prevent the multi-sheet grabs that plague cheaper paper. Text comes out sharp with no ghosting, and color graphics have decent vibrancy—not photo-lab quality, but perfectly acceptable for client handouts or presentations.

The 94 brightness matters more than you'd think. Side-by-side with standard 92-brightness paper, the Office Depot sheets look visibly whiter and cleaner. That subtle difference makes your documents feel more polished, especially for contracts, resumes, or anything you're handing to a client. The paper is also **acid-free**, so archived files won't yellow after a few years in a filing cabinet.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be clear about the tradeoffs. At 20 lb, this paper is **too thin for heavy ink coverage**. If you're printing photo-heavy documents or using it for watercolor or marker work, you'll see bleed-through. It's also **not recycled content**, so if your office has strict sustainability procurement standards, this won't meet them. Finally, the packaging isn't resealable—in humid climates, an opened ream can absorb moisture and cause wavy edges after a few weeks. Store it in a dry drawer or use a paper storage box.
