The ASUS A21 isn't trying to be flashy. It's a black box with a mesh front — but that's exactly the point. This is a case built for people who care more about clean cable routing and airflow than RGB spectacle. And with BTF Hidden Connect support, it's quietly one of the more interesting mATX options in 2026.

## What You Actually Get

Out of the box, you get the case itself, two pre-installed 140mm ARGB fans up front, and the standard hardware bag with standoffs, screws, and zip ties. No rear fan — that's the first thing you'll notice. The tempered glass side panel swings open on a hinge, which is a nice touch at this price. The front I/O includes two USB 3.2 Gen 1 Type-A ports, one USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-C, and a combined audio jack. That's a solid modern selection for a mATX chassis.

## BTF Hidden Connect — The Main Event

If you're not familiar, BTF (Back To Future) Hidden Connect is ASUS's design that moves the 24-pin motherboard power connector to the back of the board. The A21 accommodates this by routing those cables behind the motherboard tray. The result is a front view with almost no visible cabling — just clean, uninterrupted component lines.

The catch? You need a BTF-compatible motherboard (like ASUS's own TUF Gaming BTF series) to take full advantage. If you're using a standard board, the A21 still works fine — the rear cutouts are there, but you won't get the hidden-cable benefit. That's worth knowing before you buy.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The steel chassis feels sturdy — no flex in the panels, and the paint job is uniform. The mesh front uses a fine honeycomb pattern that does a good job filtering dust while letting air through. With the two 140mm fans spinning at full tilt, you're looking at solid positive pressure — but without a rear exhaust fan, hot air has nowhere to go but out the top. That's a design choice that works if you add an exhaust fan, and it's a minor annoyance that it's not included.

Cable management is workable but not generous. The 22mm of space behind the tray is enough for standard routing, but if you're using braided cables or the BTF bundles, you'll need to be patient. There are multiple tie-down points, so it's manageable — just not effortless.

## What Buyers Are Saying

The 4.3-star rating across 215 reviews reflects a generally satisfied customer base. One buyer noted, *"The BTF support is the reason I bought this — my build looks cleaner than anything I've done before."* Another praised the airflow: *"Temps dropped about 8 degrees compared to my old case with the same fans and cooler."*

## Where It Falls Short

The missing rear fan is the most common complaint. At this price, including a single 120mm exhaust fan would've made this a near-perfect value. The glass side panel is also a fingerprint magnet — you'll be wiping it down more than you'd like. And if you're not going BTF, some of the case's appeal is lost; you're paying a slight premium for a feature you're not using.

## Who Should Buy This

- **BTF adopters** — if you're already planning a TUF Gaming BTF motherboard build, this is the case that makes it shine.
- **First-time mATX builders** — the layout is forgiving, and the pre-installed fans get you started without extra purchases (aside from that rear fan).
- **Aesthetic-focused builders** — if you want a clean, minimal look without going full custom-loop, the A21 delivers.

Skip it if you need a rear fan included, want tons of drive bays for mass storage, or prefer a more compact case — the A21 is on the larger side for mATX.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the ASUS A21 fit a standard ATX power supply?**
A: Yes, it supports ATX PSUs up to 200mm long, which covers virtually all standard units. Just route it before installing the GPU for easier access.

**Q: Can I use a non-BTF motherboard with this case?**
A: Absolutely. The BTF support is an option, not a requirement. You'll use the standard cable routing holes, and the case functions like a normal mATX chassis.

**Q: How many fans can the A21 hold total?**
A: Up to six: two 140mm front (included), one 120mm rear, two 120mm or 140mm top, and one 120mm bottom. The included fans are enough to start, but adding a rear exhaust is recommended.
