**Q: Does the jute rope hold the board securely?**
A: Yes. The rope is knotted through two drilled holes and supports the board's weight without tearing. Just make sure the nail or hook you use is rated for at least a couple of pounds.

**Q: Is this suitable for outdoor use?**
A: No. The pine wood frame and chalkboard surface aren't weather-sealed. Keep it indoors or in a covered area away from moisture and direct sunlight.
