If you're a teacher, trainer, or parent who's burned through reams of paper on worksheets, the SUNEE Assorted Colors Dry Erase Pockets deserve a hard look. These reusable sleeves promise to turn any printed page into a wipeable surface — and with a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, they're clearly delivering for most buyers.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes 10 pockets in assorted colors (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, and a few in between). Each pocket is standard letter size — 9" x 12.5" — and made from 10-mil polypropylene. That's notably thicker than the flimsy 5-mil sleeves you'll find from no-name brands.

The top has a reinforced binding with a hole for hanging. You get a pack of 10, which is enough for a small classroom rotation or a solid home organization setup. Markers are not included — factor that into your total cost.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The 10-mil thickness is the headline feature here. It's not just marketing copy — the material feels substantial, almost like a laminated folder. It withstands the "shove it in a backpack" test without creasing or splitting at the seams. The sealed edges are heat-welded, not glued, which eliminates the peeling problem cheaper pockets develop after a few weeks.

In practice, the dry erase surface works exactly as intended. Standard Expo markers write smoothly, and a dry microfiber cloth wipes it clean with zero ghosting — even after leaving marks on overnight. One reviewer noted, "I've used these daily for three months and they still look brand new. The ink wipes right off every time." That's consistent with what I found: no staining, no residue buildup on the corners.

The color-coding system is genuinely useful. Red for math, blue for reading, green for science — you can organize by subject at a glance. The colors are saturated enough to distinguish easily, but not so dark that printed worksheets become hard to read through the plastic.

## Where It Falls Short

**Markers not included.** This is the most common complaint in the reviews. You're spending money on the pockets and then need to buy markers separately. Not a dealbreaker, but it's an extra $8-10 you should budget for.

**Letter size only.** If you work with legal-size documents or larger worksheets, these won't fit without folding — which defeats the purpose. Check your paper sizes before ordering.

**Initial stiffness.** The reinforced top binding is rigid when new. It takes a few days of use before the pockets relax and lay completely flat. Minor, but worth knowing if you're stacking them in a binder immediately.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these work with any dry erase marker?**
A: Yes, standard dry erase markers work fine. Avoid permanent markers — they will stain the surface. If you accidentally use one, trace over it with a dry erase marker and wipe immediately.

**Q: Can I use these in a three-ring binder?**
A: The reinforced top has pre-punched holes that fit standard binders. The pockets hang securely and don't tear at the holes, even with frequent page turning.

**Q: How do I clean them properly?**
A: A dry microfiber cloth or tissue works for most marks. For stubborn residue, use a small amount of isopropyl alcohol on a cloth, then wipe dry. Avoid abrasive cleaners.
