**The Redragon M690: A budget wireless mouse that actually delivers**

You don't need to spend $100 to get a wireless mouse that doesn't suck. The Redragon M690 proves it — this is a $20-ish device that handles office work and casual gaming without the cable clutter. After 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, it's clearly earning its keep. Here's the honest breakdown.

**What's in the box**

The M690 arrives with the mouse itself, a 2.4GHz USB receiver, and a charging cable. That's it. No software CD, no manual thicker than a receipt. It pairs instantly — plug in the receiver or connect via Bluetooth and you're moving within 10 seconds. The mouse weighs about 4.2 ounces, which feels substantial without being heavy. Dimensions are roughly 5 x 3 x 1.5 inches, a medium size that suits most hand shapes.

**Performance and build quality**

The M690 uses a 2400 DPI optical sensor with four adjustable DPI levels (800/1200/1600/2400). For office work, spreadsheets, and web browsing, that's more than enough. For fast-paced FPS gaming, it's adequate but not competitive-grade — you'll notice the sensor isn't as precise as higher-end mice. But that's not what this is for.

Build quality is genuinely impressive at this price point. The shell is matte plastic that resists fingerprints, and the textured side grips actually work — your thumb and ring finger stay planted during extended use. The contoured shape follows your palm naturally. As one reviewer put it, "Feels like a $50 mouse in my hand. No creaking, no flex, buttons are crisp."

The six buttons are all programmable through Redragon's software (available for Windows and Mac). The scroll wheel has solid detents — no free-spinning here, which I prefer for precise document navigation. Click latency is acceptable for productivity and light gaming; you won't notice it unless you're a competitive player.

**What buyers consistently praise**

- **Wireless reliability**: Multiple reviewers note zero dropouts or lag. One wrote, "I was skeptical about the wireless claim at this price, but it's been rock solid for two months straight."
- **Ergonomics**: The shape fits naturally, reducing wrist fatigue during long work sessions.
- **Value**: At this price point, buyers repeatedly mention it outperforms expectations.

**Where it falls short**

Battery life is the biggest compromise. You'll get roughly 30 hours per charge — that's about three full workdays. If you're an all-day user, you'll be plugging in weekly. The RGB lighting is also minimal: just the scroll wheel and logo glow, not the full-spectrum show some gaming mice offer. It's tasteful, but if you want disco lighting, look elsewhere.

The software is functional but clunky. It gets the job done for remapping buttons and adjusting DPI, but it's not going to win design awards. Also, the USB receiver has no storage slot in the mouse — easy to lose if you're not careful.

**Who should buy this**

- **Office workers** who want to ditch the cable without spending $80+ on a premium wireless mouse
- **Casual gamers** who play a few hours a week and prioritize comfort over competitive specs
- **Laptop users** who want Bluetooth capability for travel and 2.4GHz for desktop use
- **Anyone** on a budget who wants a reliable, ergonomic wireless mouse that won't fall apart in six months

**Skip it if** you're a competitive gamer needing high-precision sensors, or if you need week-long battery life without recharging.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it work with Mac?**
A: Yes, both the 2.4GHz receiver and Bluetooth work with macOS, though the software is more limited than the Windows version.

**Q: How long does the battery take to charge?**
A: About 2-3 hours via the included USB cable. You can use it while charging if you plug in the cable.

**Q: Is it comfortable for small hands?**
A: It's medium-sized. If you have smaller hands, it might feel slightly large, but the contoured shape helps. Reviewers with average-sized hands report excellent comfort.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a dependable, ergonomic wireless mouse for work and light gaming without breaking the bank. **Skip it if** you need top-tier gaming performance or multi-week battery life.

The Redragon M690 does exactly what it promises: reliable wireless, solid ergonomics, and surprising build quality for the price. It's not flashy, but it's honest — and that's worth more than marketing hype.
