There's a reason EXPO is the default dry erase marker in offices, classrooms, and meeting rooms across America. It's not because they're the cheapest — they're not. It's because every alternative feels like a compromise.

The EXPO chisel-tip black marker does one thing and does it perfectly: it puts visible, erasable ink on a whiteboard without smelling like a chemical factory. That's the whole brief, and EXPO nails it.

**The ink is genuinely low odor.** If you've ever used a cheap dry erase marker in a small meeting room, you know the headache that follows — literally. The chemical fumes from no-name markers can be overwhelming. EXPO's low-odor formula is a significant improvement. One reviewer notes: *"I like that they don't have a strong smell."* It's not odorless, but it's miles better than the alternatives.

**The chisel tip gives you two tools in one.** Use the narrow edge for fine writing, detailed diagrams, and small text. Flip to the wide edge for bold headers, highlighting, and filling large spaces. It takes a minute to get used to the angle, but once you do, you won't want to go back to a bullet tip. One reviewer: *"I like that you can use the tip of marker to write either thin or thick depending on how you hold it."*

**The ink is bold and visible.** EXPO's formula produces dense, opaque black marks that read clearly from across a conference room. Cheap markers produce grayish, translucent ink that fades. EXPO marks stay bold until you erase them. And they erase cleanly — no ghosting, no staining, no residue.

**The durability concern is real.** Some reviewers report that EXPO markers dry out faster than expected. One notes: *"Writes smooth, but dries out fast."* This is often a storage issue — if the cap isn't fully seated or the marker is stored vertically, ink can dry out prematurely. EXPO markers last well with proper care, but they're not indestructible.

**Who is this for?** Teachers who write on whiteboards all day and want markers that last. Office workers who present in meetings and need clean, readable diagrams. Students with personal whiteboards for studying. Anyone who's ever been in a meeting with a dying marker and vowed never to experience that frustration again.

At $38.44 for a 4-pack of black markers, the per-marker cost is high. This is clearly priced for institutional buyers, not individual consumers. If you're a teacher or heavy whiteboard user stocking up, the bulk pricing makes sense. If you just need a couple of markers for home use, look for smaller packs. But if you want the best dry erase markers money can buy, EXPO is the answer.
