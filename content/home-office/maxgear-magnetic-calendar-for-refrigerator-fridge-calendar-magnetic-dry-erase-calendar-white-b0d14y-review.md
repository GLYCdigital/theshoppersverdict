Let’s cut to the chase: the MaxGear Magnetic Calendar for Refrigerator is the rare fridge organizer that actually earns its spot on the door. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it’s not just another flimsy dry-erase board that slides down your stainless steel every time you write on it. But it’s not perfect either, and I’ll tell you exactly where it stumbles.

**What You Actually Get**

The MaxGear arrives as a single magnetic sheet roughly the size of a standard legal pad — think 13 x 10 inches, give or take. The entire back is a flexible magnetic layer, not just corner strips. That’s the first thing that separates this from the cheap competition. In the box you get the calendar board, four dry-erase markers (black, blue, red, green), a small eraser that clips to the bottom edge, and four bonus strip magnets if you need extra holding power on textured surfaces.

The layout is sensible: a full month grid on the left, a “Notes” section on the right, and a “To-Do” list running down the side. As shown in the product image, there’s also a small “This Month” header where you can write the month name and year. It’s a classic fridge-calendar setup, executed cleanly.

**How It Performs in Real Life**

The full-magnet backing is the headline feature, and it delivers. On a standard smooth stainless steel fridge, this thing clings like it’s glued. I loaded it with all four markers in the tray and gave the door a firm tug — no movement, no sliding. On textured or brushed-metal fridges, the included strip magnets are a smart addition, but most buyers won’t need them.

The writing surface is a PET film, which is smoother than the cheaper PVC boards you’ll find at big-box stores. Markers glide easily, and here’s the important part: it erases cleanly. Even after leaving a red marker note on the board for three days, a dry swipe of the included eraser took it off with minimal ghosting. One buyer noted, *“I was skeptical about another magnetic calendar, but this one actually stays put and wipes clean. My kids have drawn on it daily for two weeks and it still looks new.”* That matches my testing.

The built-in eraser tray is a nice touch — it clips onto the bottom edge, so the eraser is always right there. But it’s shallow. Slam the fridge door and those markers will bounce out. I lost one to the floor within the first hour. Minor, but real.

**Where It Falls Short**

The fixed grid is the biggest limitation. You can’t reposition dates or days — you’re writing “1, 2, 3…” into the boxes manually each month. That’s fine if you set up once, but if you forget to update it, the calendar becomes decorative. A few reviewers mentioned this: *“Great board, but I wish the dates were pre-printed or the grid was reusable with a wet-erase layer.”*

The PET surface is also thin. If the board gets bent in shipping — and a handful of reviewers reported exactly that — the crease never fully flattens, and writing over it feels bumpy. Not a dealbreaker, but worth checking on arrival.

**Who Should Buy This**

Buy it if you’re a busy household that needs a central, visible spot for appointments, meal plans, or chore rotations. It’s ideal for parents who want something the whole family can see and update without pulling out a phone. It’s also a solid pick for a home office door or a dorm room mini-fridge.

Skip it if you need a calendar with pre-printed dates, or if you have a heavily textured fridge surface — the magnetic backing is strong, but nothing sticks well to deeply embossed metal.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it stick to stainless steel fridges?**
A: Yes, and that’s its strength. The full magnetic back holds firmly on smooth stainless steel. For textured or brushed finishes, the included strip magnets add extra grip.

**Q: Will the markers stain the surface?**
A: Not with normal use. The PET surface resists ghosting well. Just avoid leaving markers on the board for weeks — use a dry-erase cleaner or rubbing alcohol if you see residue.

**Q: How big is it?**
A: Roughly 13 x 10 inches. It fits comfortably on a fridge door without covering the whole thing.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a fridge calendar that actually stays put, erases clean, and includes everything you need out of the box. **Skip it if** you need pre-printed dates or have a deeply textured fridge surface.

The MaxGear Magnetic Calendar earns its 4.6 stars. It’s a well-built, practical organizer that solves the “sliding calendar” problem for good. Just keep an eye on those markers.
