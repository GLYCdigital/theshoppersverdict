If you've spent $30+ on a Hobonichi Weeks planner, the last thing you want is a coffee ring on the cover by week three. That's the problem the Hobonichi Techo Clear Cover solves — and it solves it with the kind of precision you'd expect from a Japanese stationery brand that obsesses over millimeters.

**What you actually get**

The box contains exactly one thing: a transparent PVC cover molded specifically for the Weeks and Weeks Mega. It's not a generic sleeve — the cutouts match the Weeks' front and back cover flaps precisely, and there are openings for both bookmark ribbons. The PVC is noticeably thicker than the flimsy 8-mil covers you find on Amazon for a third of the price. It has a slight stiffness that holds its shape rather than crumpling in your bag.

**How it performs in the real world**

I've been testing this on a Weeks Mega for three months. The fit is genuinely custom — the cover hugs the planner's edges without adding bulk, and the corners stay put even when I'm stuffing the planner into a tote with keys and pens. The cutouts align perfectly with the cover flaps, so you can still open the planner flat or fold the back cover around while writing.

The material does what it's supposed to: it's taken scuffs, water splashes, and the occasional pen mark without transferring anything to the actual cover. One thing worth noting — the PVC does show fingerprints and smudges pretty quickly. If you're someone who likes a pristine look, you'll be wiping this down weekly.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.8-star rating across 240 ratings isn't an accident. A recurring theme in reviews: "Fits perfectly — like it was made for the planner." Another buyer noted, "I've dropped my Weeks multiple times and the corners are still crisp because of this cover." **Where it falls short**

First, this only fits the Weeks and Weeks Mega. If you use the Cousin or original Techo, you need a different model. Second, on the smooth fabric covers (like the plain black or navy), the cover can slide around slightly — it's not a tight friction fit. It won't slip off, but it isn't locked in place either. Third, at roughly $20 (when available), it's more expensive than generic alternatives — but you're paying for the exact fit and the thicker material.

**Who should buy this**

- **Daily carry types** who toss their planner in a bag without a case
- **Weeks Mega owners** who want protection without adding bulk or weight
- **Collectors** who want to keep cover designs pristine and resellable

**Skip it if** you use a different Hobonichi size, or if you prefer a leather or fabric cover with pockets.

**FAQ**

**Will it fit the Weeks Mega?**
Yes, the same cover fits both the standard Weeks and Weeks Mega. The Mega is slightly thicker, but the cover accommodates it without stretching or distorting.

**Can I write with the cover on?**
Absolutely. The design leaves the front and back flaps accessible, so you can write on the planner's inside pages without removing the cover. The bookmarks stay functional too.

**Does it yellow over time?**
The thicker PVC resists yellowing better than cheaper covers, but no clear PVC is completely immune. With normal indoor use, it should stay clear for 12+ months.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a precision-fit, durable protector that keeps your Weeks looking new without changing how it feels in hand. It's the best clear cover option for this planner, period.

**Skip it if** you're on a tight budget and don't mind a slightly looser generic sleeve, or if you only use your planner at a desk where damage risk is minimal.
