**What's in the Box**

Sixteen pre-sharpened #2 HB pencils, each 7.5 inches long with a standard hexagonal barrel. The erasers are attached — a small pink rubber block that's crimped in metal, not glued. **Performance: Where It Counts**

The graphite core is the real story. It's rated HB — the sweet spot between hard and soft — and it delivers. On standard printer paper, it lays down a medium-dark line that's readable but not smudgy. On Scantron bubble sheets, it reads perfectly without the pressure-induced indentations you get from softer pencils. I've snapped exactly zero leads in two weeks of use, including when I accidentally dropped one point-first on tile.

The pre-sharpening is legitimate. Each tip arrives with a solid point, not a wimpy shave. One pencil survived a full school day of dense note-taking before needing a re-sharpen. The ferrule — the metal band holding the eraser — is crimped tightly enough that it doesn't wiggle loose in your pocket.

**What Buyers Keep Saying**

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest. The erasers are small — about half the size of a Ticonderoga's. If you're a heavy editor or a student who erases constantly, you'll replace these faster than you'd like. Finally, the wood is good but not exceptional — occasional sharpening produces a slightly rough edge on the first pass.
