The Lorell Fortress Series Double Pedestal Desk promises professional-grade office furniture at a reasonable price. At $740 for a 60x30 double pedestal desk with a walnut top, it competes with offerings from Bush, Sauder, and Amazon's own brands. But the customer feedback reveals a critical flaw.

## When It Arrives Intact, It's Great

"Good quality, durable and sturdy," writes one satisfied buyer. "Product looks good and appears to be sturdy and well-constructed so far," echoes another. "Item was reasonably easy to assemble and it is sturdy item and great match to existing desk that we already had."

When the Lorell Fortress arrives in good condition, reviewers are uniformly positive about the build quality. The black steel frame provides a solid foundation. The walnut laminate top looks appropriate for a professional office setting. Assembly, while requiring two people for the heavier components, is manageable.

## The Delivery Problem

Here's the recurring issue: shipping damage.

"Product delivered with significant damage. Dents, scratches and chips in the laminate. Scratches and dents in the metal. Key for the lock drawer not with the package so the middle lock drawer is not operable."

"Very disappointed. Huge dent at the back. The desk is very heavy and will be a hassle to return. Also, I was waiting for this table for a while to be delivered and I don't have anything to use so I just have to ignore with the damage."

These are not edge cases — with only 14 total reviews, two detailed reports of damage represent a significant percentage of buyers. The desk is heavy, which makes it sturdy once assembled but also means that shipping impacts during transit can cause damage that's difficult to return.

## The Verdict

The Lorell Fortress Series desk is a solid product that's let down by inconsistent quality control and packaging. If you receive one in good condition, it's a well-built desk that will serve you for years. But the risk of receiving a damaged unit — and the hassle of returning a 100+ pound desk — is real.

Consider buying from a local office furniture store where you can inspect before purchase, or budget for potential return shipping costs. At this price point with these quality concerns, it's a gamble.
