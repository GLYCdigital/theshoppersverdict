If you've ever tried to cram hanging files into a standard 2-drawer cabinet, you know the pain. The Alera Soho 3-drawer vertical file cabinet solves a specific problem: it gives you a dedicated pencil drawer up top, a full letter-size file drawer in the middle, and another file drawer below — all in a footprint that's narrower than most desks.

The layout is the headline feature. As shown in the product image, the top drawer is a shallow pencil tray (interior height around 2.25 inches), the middle drawer holds letter-size hanging files, and the bottom drawer is also letter-size. That's two full file drawers plus a supply drawer, which is a combination you simply don't find in most vertical cabinets at this price point.

## What's in the box

You get the cabinet in three main pieces: the body, the top, and the drawers pre-assembled but not installed. The box also includes the anti-tip bracket, leveling glides, and the necessary screws. There's no included tool — you'll need a Phillips head screwdriver. The 86-pound shipping weight tells you this isn't a flimsy pressboard unit; it's all-steel with a baked-on charcoal finish.

## Build quality and real-world use

The all-steel construction is the star here. The cabinet doesn't wobble, and the full-extension ball-bearing slides on both file drawers are genuinely smooth. One buyer noted, "The drawers glide like they're on glass — no sticking, no wobble, even when fully loaded." That matches my assessment. The file drawers accommodate both hanging files and standard manila folders, and the full-extension slides mean you can actually see the back of the drawer without contorting.

The pencil drawer is a nice touch but has limits. It's designed for pens, sticky notes, scissors, and small gadgets — not binders or a laptop. If you need a deep utility drawer, this isn't it. But for keeping your desk surface clear of clutter, it works exactly as intended.

A few buyers reported minor scuffs on arrival, which is almost inevitable with a cabinet this heavy. One reviewer said, "The box arrived with a dented corner but the cabinet itself was fine — the packaging did its job." That's the norm, but inspect the cardboard before you sign.

## Where it falls short

Assembly is the biggest friction point. It's not hard, but the drawer slides must be aligned precisely. If you rush, you'll get a drawer that binds halfway. Plan for 45–60 minutes and follow the sequence in the manual — don't skip the anti-tip bracket installation, especially if you have kids or pets.

The finish is matte charcoal, which looks professional but shows fingerprints and dust more readily than a textured or metallic finish. A quick wipe with a microfiber cloth keeps it presentable, but it won't stay pristine on its own.

## Who should buy this

- **Home office workers** who need letter-size file storage plus a spot for desk supplies without sacrificing floor space.
- **Small business owners** who want a fire-resistant, all-steel cabinet that won't tip over when the top drawer is open.
- **Anyone upgrading from a flimsy particleboard cabinet** — the weight and stability are immediately noticeable.

Skip it if you need legal-size filing (this is letter-size only) or if you want a cabinet that arrives fully assembled.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the pencil drawer lock?**
A: Yes — the top lock engages all three drawers. One key locks the entire cabinet, so you can secure sensitive files and supplies in a single action.

**Q: Can I use it as a printer stand?**
A: The top is flat and rated to hold a standard office printer, but it's not a dedicated stand. If you're placing a heavy multifunction device, use the included anti-tip bracket to anchor the cabinet to the wall.

**Q: What are the exterior dimensions?**
A: It's 18.5 inches wide, 24.25 inches deep, and 28.75 inches tall. The file drawers accommodate letter-size hanging files (12.25 inches deep).

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need the pencil/file/file combination in a compact, stable, all-steel package. **Skip it if** you need legal-size storage or want a zero-assembly solution.
