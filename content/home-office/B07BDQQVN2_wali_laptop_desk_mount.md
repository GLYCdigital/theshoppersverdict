If your laptop is living on your desk like a permanent resident, you're wasting space that could hold a coffee, a notebook, or literally anything else. The WALI Laptop Desk Mount (model M00LP) is a $29 solution to that problem, and with 5,882 reviews and a 4.5-star average, it's a proven one.

## What You Get

This mount consists of a heavy-duty C-clamp base, a gas spring arm, and a ventilated laptop tray. The arm supports up to 22 lbs, which covers everything from ultrabooks to beefy 17-inch gaming laptops. The tray has adjustable side brackets and a raised back lip to keep the laptop from sliding off.

The build quality is where this stands out. Multiple reviewers call it "heavy duty" and "sturdy." One user uses it in a CNC workshop environment: "I recently built a new stand/enclosure for my CNC machine and needed a new place to put my laptop. When I found this item, saw the price, and read a lot of great reviews I figured it was worth a try."

## Real-World Performance

Installation is straightforward: clamp the base to the desk edge, attach the arm, bolt on the laptop tray. The C-clamp is padded and grips securely. One reviewer got creative and mounted it to a patio propane heater pole: "I was pleasantly surprised to find that the included long screws fit onto my propane heater pole." The ventilation slots on the tray are well-placed and allow airflow underneath the laptop, preventing heat buildup during long work sessions.

The arm motion is smooth and the tension is adjustable. You can position the laptop anywhere from right above the desk surface to eye level, and the gas spring holds the position once set. Users who pair this with a second monitor arm report a clean, professional dual-screen arrangement.

## The Notable Gripes

The biggest recurring complaint: adjustments require the Allen key. There's no quick-release lever for on-the-fly angle changes. If you shift between sitting and standing frequently or share the desk, this gets tedious. A few users also mention the tray develops a slight sag when loaded with heavier laptops — about 30 degrees of tilt according to one 3-star reviewer — though this can be mitigated by tightening the tilt tension screws.

## The Bottom Line

For $29.44, the WALI Laptop Desk Mount delivers exactly what it promises: a sturdy way to get your laptop off the desk and into an ergonomic position. The Allen-key adjustment is a minor inconvenience for the price point, and the build quality is well above what you'd expect in this range. If you're tired of hunching over a laptop or just want your desk space back, this is a solid investment.

**Verdict: 4.4/5** — Sturdy, affordable, and effective. The Allen-key adjustments hold it back from perfection, but at this price, nothing else in the category comes close.
