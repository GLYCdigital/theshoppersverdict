If your home office setup is starting to look like a crime scene of scattered papers, the STOCKPILE Curve Seated 3-Drawer Mobile File Cabinet is the kind of product that quietly solves your chaos. With a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. But as with any piece of furniture you assemble yourself, the real story is in the details.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the cabinet body, three drawers, four locking casters, the signature removable magnetic cushion, and the hardware. Assembly takes roughly 20–30 minutes if you've got a basic screwdriver and some patience. The dimensions are compact enough to slide under a standard-height desk — roughly 15.5" wide, 23" deep, and about 26" tall with the cushion on. It's designed for seated use, which means you can grab files without leaving your chair.

**Real-world performance**

The standout feature here is the removable magnetic cushion. It's not just a marketing gimmick — the magnet is strong enough to hold the cushion in place while you're sitting on it, but it pops off cleanly when you need the top surface for something else. One buyer noted, "I was skeptical about the cushion, but it's actually comfortable enough to lean on while I'm reading documents at my desk."

The drawers are the selling point for anyone drowning in paperwork. They're sized to fit both letter and legal hanging files straight out of the box — no adapter frames required. The glides are smooth even when loaded with heavy folders. Another verified purchase said, "The drawers slide like butter even when completely full. I've had cheaper cabinets that stick halfway, and this one doesn't."

The casters lock reliably, which matters more than you'd think. Even on carpet, the cabinet stays put when you're pulling drawers open. On hard floors, though, the plastic casters are noticeably louder than rubber ones — not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing if you're in a quiet office.

**Where it falls short**

Assembly is the most common complaint. Not because it's hard, but because the cushion's magnetic alignment needs to be precise. If you rush it, the cushion sits slightly crooked, and that bugs some people more than it should. Give yourself an extra ten minutes and check the alignment before you torque down the screws.

The curve design is aesthetically pleasing, but it means the top surface isn't perfectly flat. You can't stack a heavy printer or a stack of binders on it without them sliding slightly. It's a tradeoff — you get the sleek look and the cushion, but you lose genuine flat-top utility.

**Who should buy this**

This cabinet is ideal for a few specific buyers:

- **Home office workers** who need letter and legal filing capacity in a small footprint
- **People with limited floor space** who want storage that tucks under a desk
- **Anyone who spends long hours at a desk** and wants a comfortable surface to lean against while reviewing documents
- **Renters** who need something mobile that moves easily between rooms

It's less ideal for anyone needing a hard, flat top surface for equipment, or for those who want whisper-quiet casters on hardwood floors.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Can it hold legal-size hanging files?*
Yes. The drawers accommodate both letter and legal hanging files without needing adapters. That's a genuine plus at this price point.

*Is the cushion really removable?*
Yes, it's held on with magnets. It pops off for cleaning or when you need a flat surface. It stays put during normal use.

*Does it come fully assembled?*
No. You'll need to attach the casters and the cushion. Plan for 20–30 minutes of assembly time.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need reliable, mobile filing that fits letter and legal files, and you like the idea of a comfortable cushion for those long desk sessions.

**Skip it if** you need a perfectly flat top for equipment, or you're sensitive to caster noise on hard floors.

This is a genuinely well-designed product that earns its 4.8-star rating. The cushion feature is a thoughtful addition, the drawers handle real-world file volumes without issue, and the locking casters do their job. It's not perfect — assembly requires attention, and the curved top limits stacking — but for the core job of organized, accessible filing from your chair, it delivers.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A solid, practical upgrade for any cluttered workspace.
