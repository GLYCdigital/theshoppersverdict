Most note card sets force you to choose between print quality and hand-written charm. The Avery Printable Note Cards with Envelopes try to deliver both, and with a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, they're clearly doing something right. But are they worth your money for everyday correspondence, or are they better suited to specific use cases? Let's dig into what you're actually getting.

## What's in the Box

You get a box of pre-scored note cards and matching envelopes. The cards are 5" x 7" (standard A7 size) and the envelopes match perfectly — no hunting for a separate size. The card stock is a substantial 110 lb weight, which is noticeably thicker than typical store-bought thank-you cards. As one buyer put it, "These feel like cards you'd buy at a boutique stationery shop, not something you printed in your home office."

## Print Quality and Real-World Performance

This is where the Avery set earns its keep. The cards are designed to work with both inkjet and laser printers, and the coating handles both well. I've seen complaints about other printable cards smudging or leaving roller marks; that's not the case here. The surface takes ink evenly, and even dense, full-color designs come out sharp.

The perforation deserves special mention. It's clean and consistent — cards separate from the sheet without ragged edges or torn corners. If you've ever wrestled with a cheap perforated product, you'll appreciate the difference.

Paper thickness matters more than most people think. At 110 lb, these cards hold up to fountain pens, gel pens, and even light watercolor washes without bleeding through to the other side. That's a significant advantage over cheaper 80 lb alternatives that turn into a mess with any wet media.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring theme in positive reviews is versatility. Users are printing everything from baby shower invitations to corporate thank-you notes with the same box. One reviewer noted, "I've used these for three different events — a wedding RSVP, a client thank-you, and my daughter's graduation announcements. They look different every time because the printing is so reliable."

Another common point of praise: the envelopes. They're standard weight (lighter than the cards), but they seal well and stay closed in the mail. No buyers reported envelopes popping open in transit.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the envelopes are noticeably thinner than the cards. They're fine for mailing, but if you're looking for a cohesive heavyweight feel, the mismatch is noticeable when you hold the set together.

Second, Avery's template situation is a bit closed-off. While the cards work with any standard design software, Avery pushes you toward their own free Design & Print platform. It works fine, but if you're a Canva or Adobe user, you'll need to create your own print layouts. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

Finally, the 5" x 7" size is larger than a standard note card. That's great for design flexibility but means they won't fit in standard 4" x 6" photo storage boxes or smaller desk organizers.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy them if:** You're someone who sends regular handwritten correspondence — thank-you notes, holiday cards, client follow-ups — and wants a professional look without paying boutique prices. Also ideal for small business owners who want branded note cards without a minimum order from a print shop.

**Skip them if:** You only send a handful of cards a year. The 110 lb stock and envelope quality are wasted on occasional use, and you'd be better served by a smaller pack of pre-printed cards.

## FAQ

**Q: Will these work in my laser printer?**
A: Yes. The card stock is rated for both inkjet and laser. Just ensure your printer has a straight paper path or a rear feed slot for thicker media.

**Q: Can I print on both sides?**
A: Technically yes, but the 110 lb weight makes double-sided printing tricky on some printers. Test with a single card first.

**Q: Do I need Avery's software to use these?**
A: No. They're standard 5" x 7" cards. You can use any design software or even a word processor. Avery's free platform is a convenience, not a requirement.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Buy it if you value print reliability and substantial card stock for regular correspondence or small business use. Skip it if you're a once-a-year card sender who doesn't need heavyweight stock. The envelope-to-card weight mismatch is a minor flaw, but the overall quality and consistency make this an easy recommendation for anyone who prints their own stationery.
