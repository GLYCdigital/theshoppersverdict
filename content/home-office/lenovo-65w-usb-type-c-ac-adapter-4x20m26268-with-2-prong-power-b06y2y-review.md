The non-detachable 2-prong cord is also a long-term concern. If the AC cord gets damaged — say, a pet chews it or it catches in a drawer — you're buying a whole new adapter rather than just a cable. Amazon reviewers have flagged this in a few one-star complaints. And while this is a minor point, the brick runs noticeably warm during sustained high-wattage use. It never got hot enough to be a safety concern, but it's not a charger you'd want to leave buried under a pile of papers.
