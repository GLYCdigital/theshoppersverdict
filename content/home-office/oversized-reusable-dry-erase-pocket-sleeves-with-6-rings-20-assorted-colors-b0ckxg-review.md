**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use these with laminating pouches or just loose paper?**
A: Loose paper only. The pocket opens on one side for insertion; it's not designed for pre-laminated materials. For laminated pages, you'd be double-protecting unnecessarily.

**Q: Will the rings fit on standard 3-ring binders?**
A: No — the rings are fixed to the sleeve itself, not designed to open for binder insertion. These are standalone tools, not binder accessories.

**Q: How do I clean them without streaks?**
A: Use a dry microfiber cloth for dry-erase. For stubborn residue, isopropyl alcohol on a soft cloth works, then wipe dry with a clean section. Avoid abrasive sponges — they'll scratch the surface.
