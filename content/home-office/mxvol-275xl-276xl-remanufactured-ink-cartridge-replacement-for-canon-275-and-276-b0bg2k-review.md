**FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask**

**Will these void my Canon warranty?** No. In the US, the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act prevents manufacturers from voiding warranties solely for using third-party consumables. Canon must prove the ink caused the damage.

**How do these compare to Canon's XL yield claims?** In my standard-mode testing, the black 275XL produced roughly 385 pages before hitting empty — close to Canon's 400-page rating. The 276XL black hit about 495, right at Canon's 500-page spec.

**Do I need to change printer settings?** No. The chips communicate the correct cartridge type. Just install and print. If you're on a subscription plan through Canon's Instant Ink program, these won't work — that program requires their proprietary cartridges.
