**Review Body:**

If your employer thinks "productivity" means watching your Teams status like a hawk, you already know the drill: step away for a coffee, come back to a yellow dot and a passive-aggressive ping from your manager. The **AUEDROT Mouse Jiggler Metallic** is the quiet rebellion that keeps your status green — and it's genuinely better built than most of what's on the market.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the jiggler itself, a braided USB-C cable, and a short manual. That's it. No software, no drivers, no install. The device is a machined aluminum puck roughly the size of a hockey puck — heavier than the plastic ones, which matters because it doesn't slide around your desk when the cable tugs at it. The metallic finish isn't just for looks; it dissipates heat better than the ABS plastic shells on cheaper units, so it stays cool even after 8 hours of continuous use.

**Performance and Build**

Here's how it works: you plug it in, place your mouse on top, and the internal mechanism shifts the optical sensor just enough to register movement. The key detail is that the movement is *randomized* — it's not a steady circular pattern that a savvy IT admin or motion-detection algorithm can spot. One reviewer put it plainly: *"My Teams status stays green all day. My boss thinks I'm a machine. I'm actually napping."* Another noted the build quality directly: *"I've burned through three plastic jigglers in a year. This one feels like it's actually engineered, not stamped out of a mold."*

The USB-C connection is a genuine improvement over the micro-USB ports on older models — the cable seats firmly, doesn't wobble, and you can use any modern charger or laptop port. It draws negligible power, so it won't drain a laptop battery if you're running it off a USB port.

**Where It Falls Short**

The biggest frustration is the lack of a power switch. To stop the jiggling, you have to physically unplug the cable. It's a minor annoyance, but when you're packing up to leave or stepping away for a meeting you *actually* want to attend, it's an extra step you'll resent by week two.

Also worth noting: this jiggler is *subtle*. That's a feature — it won't trigger motion-detection software — but it means you can't leave it running overnight and expect your status to stay green at 3 AM. If you're trying to fool a system that flags inactivity after hours, this won't do it.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Remote workers on teams with aggressive activity tracking** — this is your lifeline. It's undetectable, reliable, and won't get you flagged like those app-based jigglers that trigger endpoint security alerts.
- **People with multiple monitors and a strict IT policy** — the randomized movement keeps your status active without drawing the kind of attention a macro script would.
- **Anyone tired of replacing cheap plastic jigglers** — the aluminum body justifies the modest price premium if you're on your third unit in a year.

**Skip it if** you need a jiggler with scheduling features, an on/off toggle, or something that works while your computer is locked. This is a simple, mechanical tool — and that's exactly what makes it reliable.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this trigger my company's security software?**
A: It simulates mouse movement at the hardware level, so it doesn't install anything or modify system files. Most endpoint tools that scan for virtual jigglers won't detect it. That said, no device is 100% invisible — if your IT department runs aggressive behavioral analytics, nothing is guaranteed.

**Q: Does it work with any mouse?**
A: Yes, as long as your mouse uses an optical or laser sensor on the bottom. It won't work with trackballs or touchpads. Place the mouse flat on the surface and it'll track the movement.

**Q: Can I use this with my work laptop without admin rights?**
A: Absolutely. That's the point — it's a USB peripheral, not software. Plug it in, and it works. No drivers, no permissions, no IT ticket.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a reliable, well-built way to stay "active" during the workday and you're tired of flimsy plastic alternatives. **Skip it if** you need scheduling features or an on/off switch — those don't exist here.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — loses half a star for the missing power switch, but this is the best-built mouse jiggler I've tested in this price range. It does exactly what it claims, does it quietly, and does it without breaking.
