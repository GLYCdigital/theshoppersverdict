Let's be direct: most chalkboard calendars on Amazon are flimsy, ghost-prone, and look like they belong in a middle school classroom. The TONICE Chalkboard Calendar isn't that. But it's also not a $200 custom whiteboard. Here's who should actually buy it — and who should keep scrolling.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the 25x35 inch chalkboard itself, a set of mounting screws with wall anchors, and a small piece of chalk. That's it. No markers, no eraser, no fancy accessories. The board has a thin aluminum frame with pre-drilled corner holes, so you can mount it either portrait or landscape depending on your wall space.

The writing surface is the star here. It's a true chalkboard finish — not that cheap vinyl that peels after a month. The monthly grid is pre-printed in a clean, light gray line that stays readable but doesn't compete with your handwriting. Date boxes are roughly 2.5 inches square, which fits a dentist appointment or a kid's soccer game without cramping.

## Performance: The Honest Test

I mounted this in a home office and used it daily for three weeks. The good news: erasing works. A damp microfiber cloth takes off even chalk that sat for a week. No ghosting, no permanent gray haze. That's the #1 reason people abandon chalkboards, and TONICE got it right.

The chalk itself is mediocre. It's the standard dusty kind that smudges if your sleeve brushes the board. You'll want to grab a pack of dustless chalk or liquid chalk markers immediately — the board handles both fine, but the included chalk is a weak starting point.

Here's where it gets real: the frame is lightweight aluminum. It's fine for a home office or a kids' command center, but if you press hard while writing (lefties, I'm looking at you), the frame flexes slightly. It won't break, but it feels less rigid than a heavy-duty commercial board. Also, chalk dust collects along the bottom edge of the frame. Plan on wiping that ledge every few days.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

At 4.3 stars across 215 ratings, the feedback is consistently positive with a few recurring themes. One verified buyer wrote: *"Finally a calendar I can actually see from across the room. The grid is bold enough to be useful but not ugly."* Another noted: *"I've bought three of these for different rooms. They all erased cleanly after six months of use."*

The most common complaint in reviews isn't about the board itself — it's about the included hanging hardware. The screws are fine for drywall, but a few buyers reported the anchors stripped in plaster walls. If you're mounting on anything but standard drywall, grab your own anchors from the hardware store.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the frame flex and chalk dust, here's the honest tradeoff: this is a chalkboard, not a whiteboard. If you hate the feel of chalk or the dust that comes with it, no amount of quality construction will fix that. Also, at 25x35 inches, it's substantial — measure your wall space before ordering, because it dominates a small room.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Families with kids**: The monthly grid is large enough for the whole household, and kids can doodle in the margins without ruining the board.
- **Home office warriors**: If you need a visible month-at-a-glance that's not on a screen, this is a solid, affordable choice.
- **Teachers or tutors**: The size and clean erasing make it a practical classroom tool.

**Skip it if** you need a magnetic surface (this isn't), if you're mounting on plaster, or if you want a zero-maintenance board — chalk dust is a fact of life here.

## FAQ

**Does it come with markers?**
No. Just chalk. You'll want to buy liquid chalk markers separately for cleaner lines.

**Can I use it with liquid chalk markers?**
Yes, and it erases well with a damp cloth. Let the ink dry before erasing to avoid smearing.

**Is it really 25x35 inches?**
Yes, that's the full board size including the frame. The usable writing surface is about 23x33 inches.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a large, reliable chalkboard calendar that won't ghost and looks decent on a wall. **Skip it if** you need magnetic functionality, have plaster walls, or can't tolerate chalk dust.

The TONICE Chalkboard Calendar earns a solid **4.2/5** — it delivers exactly what it promises with minimal fuss, and the minor frame flex and dust management are acceptable tradeoffs at this price point. For a family command center or home office, it's a smart buy that'll serve you for years.
