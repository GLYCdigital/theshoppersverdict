Let’s be honest: paper clips are the unsung workhorses of any desk. You don't think about them until the cheap ones snap in half or leave rust stains on a client contract. The Amazon Basics No. 1 Paper Clips aim to be the quiet, dependable option — and at 4.7 stars from 235 ratings, they're clearly doing something right.

## What's in the Box

You get 200 standard No. 1 size clips (1 inch long) in a simple cardboard box. No plastic tray, no dispenser — just a straight-up refill pack. The clips are loose in the box, which is fine for home use but worth noting if you're buying for a shared office where organization matters.

## Performance and Build Quality

The first thing you'll notice is the finish. These have a smooth, bright silver coating that doesn't have the rough edges you sometimes find on bargain clips. I tested them against a stack of 20 sheets of standard copy paper — they gripped firmly without leaving permanent kinks in the paper. That's the sweet spot: strong enough to hold, gentle enough to remove cleanly.

The steel wire is a standard 0.9mm gauge, which is typical for No. 1 clips. They won't win any awards for bending cardstock or chipboard, but for everyday document organization, they do the job. One thing I appreciate: the wire ends meet cleanly, so you won't catch your fingernail on a sharp edge when sliding them onto paper.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Looking through the verified purchase reviews, a few themes keep coming up:

- **Value per clip**: Multiple reviewers note these cost about half what you'd pay for brand-name clips at an office supply store. One reviewer said, "I was skeptical of buying office supplies from Amazon Basics, but these are just as good as the ones I used to get at Staples."
- **Reliability**: No bent clips, no rust spots, no manufacturing defects. The quality control is consistent across batches.
- **Perfect size**: The No. 1 size is the most versatile for standard document clipping — not too small to lose in a drawer, not so large they leave big marks.

## Where It Falls Short

Let me be straight about the downsides:

1. **No corrosion protection**: These clips lack a rust-resistant coating. If you live in a humid climate or plan to keep documents in storage for years, you'll want to spring for stainless steel clips instead. One reviewer mentioned finding light rust on clips stored in a damp basement after six months.

2. **Cardboard box design flaw**: The box is simple and functional, but once you open it, there's no way to close it securely. If you tip the box over, you'll have 200 clips scattered across your desk. Transfer them to a small container or jar.

3. **Thin gauge for thick stacks**: If you regularly clip 30+ pages or heavy cardstock, these will struggle. The wire will bend out of shape, and the clips lose their grip permanently.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Home office workers** who need a dependable bulk supply without the retail markup
- **Students** who go through clips quickly and don't want to overthink a purchase
- **Anyone** who needs a solid emergency stash for tax documents, receipts, or filing

Skip these if you work in a humid environment, need clips for heavy archival work, or you're a stationery enthusiast who wants premium-gauge wire.

## FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Are these actual metal clips or plastic-coated?**
A: They're bare steel with a silver plating. No plastic coating, which is good for grip but means no rust protection.

**Q: How many sheets can they hold without bending?**

**Q: Do they come in different sizes?**
A: No, this listing is specifically No. 1 (1-inch) clips. Amazon Basics also sells No. 2 and No. 3 sizes separately if you need larger.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, budget-friendly workhorse clip for everyday use and don't need fancy corrosion resistance.

**Skip it if** you need clips for long-term archival storage, work in high humidity, or regularly clip thick stacks of paper.

At 4.6/5, these are a solid value buy that outperforms their price point. For the cost of a coffee, you get six months of dependable paper management. That's a deal worth clipping to your shopping list.
