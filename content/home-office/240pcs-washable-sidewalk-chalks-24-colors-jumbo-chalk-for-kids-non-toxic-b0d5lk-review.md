**FAQ**

**Is this chalk actually washable from all surfaces?**
From concrete, asphalt, and most patio stones — yes, with water alone. From porous brick or rough untreated wood, you'll need a stiff brush. From fabric (like if it goes through the wash), it fades but may leave a ghost stain.

**Are the colors as bright as the photos suggest?**
On concrete, yes. On darker surfaces like blacktop, they're slightly less vivid but still clearly visible. The whites and pastels show up best on dark surfaces.

**Is this safe for 2-year-olds?**
The chalk is non-toxic, but the sticks are large enough to be a choking hazard only if a child manages to bite off a chunk. Supervision is still recommended for under-3s. The box itself has small dividers that could tear off — keep the box away from toddlers.
