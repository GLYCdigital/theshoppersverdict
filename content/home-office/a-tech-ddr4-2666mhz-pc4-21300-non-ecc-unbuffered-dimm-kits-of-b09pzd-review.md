**FAQ: What Shoppers Ask**

**Q: Will this work in my laptop?**
A: No. This is a 288-pin DIMM for desktop PCs. Laptops require SODIMM modules. Check your machine's form factor before ordering.

**Q: Can I mix this with my existing 2400MHz RAM?**
A: Yes, but the system will run both sticks at the slower speed (2400MHz). To get the full 2666MHz benefit, replace both sticks with matching A-Tech modules.

**Q: Is this compatible with AMD Ryzen systems?**
A: Yes, but check your motherboard's QVL (qualified vendor list) first. Most Ryzen 3000/5000 series boards handle 2666MHz fine, though some older boards may need a BIOS update to recognize the full capacity.
