## The Eco Label That Actually Prints Well

Most "green" labels force a compromise — you get compostable materials but washed-out prints, or labels that peel off packages before they reach the customer. The Zebra ZSB Series potato starch-based labels sidestep that tradeoff with surprising competence. These aren't just another paper stock; they're engineered for the ZSB thermal printer line while delivering legitimate environmental credentials.

## What You Actually Get

Each cartridge contains a continuous roll of 2.25" x 4" labels engineered specifically for Zebra's ZSB series direct thermal printers. The face stock uses potato starch as a binder replacement for traditional tree-based paper pulp, which means the labels qualify for industrial composting — not just curbside recycling. The roll sits inside a recyclable cartridge that clicks into the printer without threading, and the built-in liner recycling program (via TerraCycle) closes the loop on the backing paper most label users toss.

## Performance and Real-World Use

Print quality holds up impressively. Direct thermal printing produces sharp, dark barcode lines that scan reliably on the first pass — critical when you're shipping dozens of packages daily. The adhesive grips immediately on cardboard boxes, poly mailers, and even slightly textured surfaces without edge lifting during transit.

Durability is where you need to be careful. These labels handle normal handling, stacking, and temperature changes fine. But they're not waterproof. Rain, condensation from cold storage, or a spilled drink will cause the starch-based face stock to soften and the print to blur. If your shipping environment involves moisture, pair these with a clear overlaminate or stick with standard synthetic labels.

## Where It Falls Short

The premium price is the most obvious friction point. Expect to pay roughly 20-30% more per label compared to standard paper alternatives. For low-volume shippers, that difference is negligible; for operations pushing thousands of labels monthly, it adds up fast.

The cartridge system, while convenient, locks you into Zebra's ecosystem. Third-party refills don't exist yet for this format, so you're committed to Zebra's pricing. And if you're using a non-ZSB printer, these won't fit — this is a proprietary format, not a universal solution.

## FAQ

**Are these labels truly compostable?**
Yes, the face stock and adhesive are certified for industrial composting (ASTM D6400). The liner and cartridge recycle through TerraCycle. Home composting won't work — they need commercial facilities.

**Will these fit my Zebra ZD421 or ZD621 printer?**
No. These are designed exclusively for the ZSB series printers. The cartridge form factor and label dimensions are proprietary to that line.

**How many labels per cartridge?**
Each cartridge holds 110 labels. For higher volume, Zebra sells multi-packs, but single cartridges are the most common purchase unit.
