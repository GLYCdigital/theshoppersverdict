## Where It Falls Short

Honest downsides exist. The premium price is real — expect to pay 30-40% more than generic multipurpose paper. For high-volume internal printing, that adds up quickly.

The stiffer 24 lb sheet can cause issues in high-heat laser printers. I noticed slight curling on long duplex runs — nothing that prevented printing, but it's there. If you're in a humid environment, this becomes more pronounced.

There's also no recycled content option. If your company has sustainability requirements, this paper won't meet them.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You print client-facing documents, presentations, marketing materials, or anything where print quality matters. You run a home office and want reliability without babysitting the printer. You print double-sided regularly and are tired of show-through.

**Skip it if:** You're printing internal drafts by the case. You need recycled paper. You're on a tight budget and print 100+ pages daily — the cost difference becomes significant.
