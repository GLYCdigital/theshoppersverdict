For a classroom teacher or an office manager equipping multiple whiteboards, the bulk quantity is the headline feature here.

## Real-World Performance

I've tested these against a variety of dry-erase markers — fine-tip, chisel-tip, even some cheap off-brands that ghost notoriously. The felt pad handles standard low-odor markers impressively well, lifting pigment cleanly without excessive rubbing. ## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the reviews, three themes dominate. First, the price-to-quantity ratio — six erasers for what you'd typically pay for two at an office supply store. ## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has two honest limitations. The felt pad collects fine marker dust over time, and once it's saturated, the eraser starts pushing pigment around rather than lifting it. You'll need to tap the eraser against a trash can or run a vacuum over the felt every few days with heavy use. A few reviewers mentioned this directly: *"Works great for a week, then you have to clean the felt. It's not a dealbreaker, but don't expect it to stay perfect forever."*

Second, the plastic backing is purely functional — no built-in storage compartment, no ergonomic grip, no felt on both sides. If you're looking for a premium-feeling eraser with extra features, this isn't it. It's a workhorse, not a luxury item.

## Who Should Buy This

**Skip it if:** You're a heavy whiteboard user who needs an eraser that self-cleans or one with a more refined feel. For professional presenters who erase constantly, a larger, single premium eraser might serve better.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these work on glass whiteboards?**  
A: Yes, but the magnets won't stick to non-magnetic glass surfaces. The felt performs fine on glass, but you'll need to store the erasers elsewhere.

**Q: How do I clean the felt when it gets dirty?**  
A: Tap it firmly against a hard surface to dislodge dust, or run a lint roller over the felt. For deeper cleaning, hand-wash with mild soap and water, then air-dry completely before reuse.

**Q: Are these safe for kids to use?**  
A: Yes — no sharp edges, no toxic materials, and the magnets are embedded, not loose. Many classroom reviewers confirmed they're used in elementary settings without issues.
