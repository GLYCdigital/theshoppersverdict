You can spot a cheap marker set from across the room: watery ink, frayed tips, colors that look nothing like the barrel. The Caliart Dual Brush Pens aren't that. This 36-piece set has earned a 4.6-star rating from 229 Amazon shoppers, and after digging through the feedback, the praise is consistent — these punch well above their price point for a dual-tip marker set.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains 36 markers, each with a brush tip on one end and a fine tip on the other. The brush tip is the star — it's flexible enough to create both thick, sweeping strokes and hairline details. The fine tip on the opposite end handles outlines, borders, and small text. As shown in the product image, the color range runs from deep blacks and navies to bright pinks, teals, and warm earth tones. The plastic body is clear, so you can see remaining ink levels — a small detail that saves you from grabbing a dead marker mid-project.

**Real-World Performance**

The ink is water-based and alcohol-free, which means it won't bleed through most marker paper, but it's not bleed-proof on standard copy paper. One reviewer noted, "I use these for my bullet journal and they work great on the thicker pages, but I had to switch to cardstock for anything with two-sided printing." That's honest feedback worth heeding — plan your paper accordingly.

The brush tip is where Caliart invested their engineering. It holds its shape through extended use. Multiple reviewers with months of ownership report no fraying or splitting, which is the most common failure mode in this price bracket. The fine tip is equally durable — it doesn't mushroom out after repeated pressure.

Color saturation is strong. The ink applies evenly without streaking, and the colors dry quickly — typically under 30 seconds on most surfaces. One buyer summed it up: "I've bought three different brands of brush pens this year. These are the first ones that didn't dry out in a week or fray after a few uses."

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The durability thread runs through nearly every positive review. Reviewers who use these daily for hand-lettering, coloring books, and journaling report the tips staying crisp far longer than expected at this price. The color payoff also gets repeated mentions — "The colors are so vibrant and they blend beautifully" is a common refrain. For a set in this range, that's not guaranteed.

**Where It Falls Short**

Two real limitations. First, the bleed-through issue on regular paper is genuine. If you're planning to use these in a standard notebook with thin pages, you'll see ghosting on the reverse side. Second, there's no replacement program. When a color runs dry — and if you use one color heavily, that'll happen — you're buying a full new set. That's a frustration for serious users, though at this price point, it's the norm rather than the exception.

**Who Should Buy These**

- **Bullet journalers** who need consistent, reliable color for layouts and trackers — these hold up to daily use.
- **Hand-lettering beginners** who want a brush tip that forgives mistakes and doesn't require a heavy hand.
- **Coloring enthusiasts** who burn through sets quickly and want something that lasts.
- **Students** who need an affordable, versatile marker set for notes, diagrams, and study materials.

Skip these if you're a professional illustrator needing archival-grade ink or alcohol-blend markers for smooth gradients — this isn't that tool.

**FAQ**

**Do these markers bleed through paper?**
On standard 20-lb printer paper, yes — you'll see ghosting. On marker paper, cardstock, or thick journal pages (90gsm+), they perform cleanly. Test a small corner first.

**Are the brush tips good for calligraphy?**
Yes, for beginners and intermediate users. The tip holds a point well and offers good line variation. Advanced calligraphers may prefer a more expensive brush pen with a longer taper, but these are excellent for practice.

**How long do they last?**
With moderate use (a few times per week), expect 3-6 months per marker depending on how heavily you use each color. The clear barrel lets you monitor ink levels.

**Skip it if** you need bleed-proof ink on thin paper or require individual replacement pens — neither is available here.
