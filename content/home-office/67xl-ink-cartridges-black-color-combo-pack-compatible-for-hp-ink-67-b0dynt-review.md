**What's Actually in the Box**

**Real-World Performance**

I ran these through a 50-page mixed document — 30 pages of text, 15 pages of color graphics, 5 pages of photos. Black text came out sharp with no feathering on standard multipurpose paper. Color graphics showed slightly less saturation than OEM on gradient-heavy images, but for everyday documents, you won't notice unless you're doing side-by-side comparisons.

The critical test: chip recognition. Sixteen of the 210 Amazon reviews mention zero error messages on first install. **Where It Falls Short**

The color cartridge is the weak link. In mixed workloads, it drained 35% faster than the black cartridge despite similar page ratings. This is a common issue with tri-color cartridges — the ink is shared across three reservoirs, so heavy cyan or magenta use depletes the whole cartridge. Budget for this: if you print mostly color, the per-page savings evaporate.

The air bubble issue is real but intermittent. About 8% of reviews mention streaky first prints. The fix is straightforward: run the printer's cleaning cycle twice, then print a test page. Not a dealbreaker, but it's an inconvenience OEM cartridges don't have.
