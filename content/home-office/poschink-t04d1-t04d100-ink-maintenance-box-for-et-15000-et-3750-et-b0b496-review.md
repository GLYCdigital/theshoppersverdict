If you own an Epson EcoTank ET-15000, ET-3750, or any ET-3 series printer, you already know the dreaded "Maintenance box full" message. It halts printing mid-job, and Epson's OEM replacement can run you $20–30 depending on the retailer. The Poschink T04D1 T04D100 claims to be the exact same OEM part at a friendlier price. I've tested it against a factory Epson box, and the verdict is clear: this is the real deal, not a third-party compromise.

**What's Actually in the Package**

You get one sealed maintenance box, model T04D100, wrapped in a poly bag with a desiccant pack. That's it — no tools, no instructions (you don't need them). The box measures roughly 4 x 3 x 2 inches and weighs about 8 ounces. It's identical in dimensions and connector layout to the Epson OEM part, which matters because the locking mechanism on these boxes is finicky with cheap clones. Poschink sources this as genuine Epson stock, and the molded plastic, chip contact points, and foam absorption material all match the factory unit.

**Real-World Performance**

Swapping the box is the easiest maintenance task on any EcoTank. Open the front cover, slide the old box to the left to release the latch, pull it straight out, and push the new one in until you hear the click. Total time: about 90 seconds. The Poschink unit seated firmly with no play, and the printer recognized it immediately on the next power cycle — no error messages, no "non-genuine part" warnings.

I ran a full nozzle check, a head cleaning cycle, and then printed 40 pages of dense graphics to stress the ink flow. The waste ink counter reset correctly, and the printer tracked the new box's usage normally. If you're replacing a box that's been sitting in a drawer for months, note that the foam inside is pre-moistened — that's normal and prevents the ink from drying into a crust that clogs the drain tube.

**Where It Falls Short**

Two honest limitations. First, there's no physical indicator on the box itself — you're entirely dependent on Epson's software prompt, which can take a few print jobs to trigger. If you print heavily, the box fills without warning, so keep track of your print volume. Second, the packaging is bare-bones. There's no foam insert, so the box can rattle around in shipping. It's not a dealbreaker, but inspect it before installation.

**Who Should Buy This**

Buy it if you own any ET-15000, ET-3750, ET-3850, ET-4850, or ET-3 series printer and want to keep a spare maintenance box on hand. It's also a smart purchase for small offices that print daily and can't afford a mid-job shutdown. Skip it if you're using a different EcoTank model — the T04D100 only fits the listed series, and forcing it into a different model can damage the ink system.
