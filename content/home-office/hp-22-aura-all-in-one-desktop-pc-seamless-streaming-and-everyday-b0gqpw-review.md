### The All-in-One That Cleans Up Your Desk — But Don't Expect a Powerhouse

If your home office looks like a cable spaghetti monster attacked it, the HP 22 Aura All-in-One is the anti-clutter solution. This 21.5-inch desktop packs everything into the display itself — no tower, no tangled wires, just a keyboard and mouse to plug in. It's a space-saving play, and for the right buyer, it works well. But as the 3.9-star average suggests, there's a gap between what HP markets and what this machine actually delivers.

### What You Actually Get

The box contains the all-in-one unit (display plus internals), a wireless keyboard and mouse combo, power adapter, and setup documentation. As shown in the product image, the slim profile is genuinely attractive — it doesn't look like a budget office machine sitting on your desk. The 21.5-inch IPS display runs at 1920x1080, which is sharp enough for everyday work. You also get a pop-up webcam with a privacy shutter, which is a thoughtful touch for remote workers.

Ports are where you need to pay attention. You're looking at two USB-A ports, one USB-C, HDMI-in (not out — that's a limitation), and an Ethernet jack. No DisplayPort, no Thunderbolt. For a basic office setup, that's adequate. For someone wanting to hook up a second monitor or external GPU, it's a non-starter.

### Real-World Performance

The 22 Aura is powered by an Intel processor — typically an i3 or i5 depending on configuration — with 8GB RAM and a 256GB or 512GB SSD. That's fine for the basics: web browsing, email, document editing, video calls, and streaming. It handles 1080p video playback smoothly, and the built-in speakers are surprisingly decent for a slim chassis.

Where it stumbles is multitasking. Open more than 10 browser tabs plus a video call plus a spreadsheet, and you'll feel the slowdown. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"Works great for what it is, but I wouldn't want to run any serious software on it."* Another noted, *"The fan gets audible under load, which surprised me for such a compact unit."*

Gaming is off the table beyond casual titles. The integrated graphics won't run modern games at playable framerates, and you can't upgrade the GPU. This is strictly a productivity and streaming machine.

### What Buyers Consistently Praise

The clean setup and included peripherals get consistent thumbs-up. *"No tower, no mess, just plug in and go"* is a recurring sentiment. The webcam quality for video calls is also frequently highlighted — it's better than most built-in laptop cameras. The display's anti-glare coating helps in bright rooms, which several reviewers appreciated.

### Where It Falls Short

The biggest complaint across reviews is performance per dollar. At its typical price point, you could build a more powerful mini-PC plus a separate monitor — but you'd lose the all-in-one convenience and clean aesthetics. The 8GB RAM is the weak point; upgrading requires opening the unit and even then, some configurations have soldered memory. The 256GB base storage also fills up fast with Windows updates and applications.

Another frustration: the HDMI port is input-only, so you can't use the Aura's screen as an external display for a laptop. That's a missed opportunity for a hybrid work setup.

### Who Should Buy This

- **Remote workers and students** who need a capable machine for Zoom, documents, and streaming — and want zero desk clutter.
- **Seniors or less tech-savvy users** who want a simple, all-in-one solution without worrying about cables and separate components.
- **Small home offices** where space is premium and the aesthetic of the machine matters.

**Skip it if** you're a power user, gamer, or anyone who needs to run demanding software. Also skip if you plan to upgrade components later — this isn't that machine.

### FAQ

**Q: Can I upgrade the RAM or storage later?**
A: It depends on the configuration. Some models have accessible SODIMM slots for RAM, but others are soldered. Storage is typically a 2.5-inch drive or M.2 SSD — check the specific SKU before buying. Don't assume upgradeability.

**Q: Does it come with a keyboard and mouse?**
A: Yes, a wireless keyboard and mouse are included. They're basic but functional, and they pair automatically out of the box.

**Q: Can I use this as a monitor for my laptop?**
A: No. The HDMI port is input-only, not output. You cannot connect this to a laptop or another PC to use as an external display.

### The Verdict

**Buy it if** you prioritize a clean, compact workspace and your computing needs are basic — web, email, streaming, and video calls. It delivers a polished all-in-one experience with zero setup hassle.

**Skip it if** you need real performance, plan to upgrade components, or want a machine that'll last more than 3-4 years without feeling sluggish. For the price, you're paying a premium for the form factor — make sure that's worth it to you.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — A well-designed space saver that's hamstrung by modest specs and limited upgrade paths. Perfect for the right user, frustrating for everyone else.
