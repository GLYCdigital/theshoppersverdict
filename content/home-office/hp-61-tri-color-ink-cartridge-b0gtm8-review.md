If you own an HP DeskJet or ENVY printer, the HP 61 Tri-Color Ink Cartridge is likely the exact replacement you're looking for. With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, it's one of the better-reviewed OEM cartridges in its class. But is it worth the premium over generics? Let's break down what you're actually getting.

## What's in the box

You get a single, sealed tri-color cartridge (cyan, magenta, yellow) that's ready to install. The packaging includes the standard orange protective cap and a clear authenticity hologram on the carton. No tools, no instructions needed — if you've replaced an HP cartridge before, you know the drill: pop out the old one, peel the tape, snap in the new one. The cartridge fits most DeskJet 1000/2000/3000 series and ENVY 4500/5500 series printers, among others.

## Real-world performance

The first thing you'll notice is the print quality. Text is crisp, and color graphics are vibrant without the oversaturation you sometimes get from cheaper inks. I tested it on standard copy paper and photo paper — colors held up well in both, with no smudging or banding on a standard quality setting. The ink dries fast, so you won't ruin a print job by stacking pages immediately after they come out.

One thing that stands out: this cartridge handles mixed content well. Whether you're printing a presentation with heavy graphics or a simple flyer with a photo, the color balance stays consistent. It's not a photo-lab-grade cartridge — if you print 4x6 photos weekly, you'll burn through it fast — but for everyday office use, it's genuinely solid.

## What buyers consistently praise

Across the reviews, three themes come up repeatedly: reliability, ease of installation, and genuine HP quality. One verified buyer noted, *"I was worried about buying a 'genuine' cartridge online, but the hologram was intact and it printed perfectly right out of the box."* Another reviewer highlighted the consistency: *"I've bought these three times now. Every single one has worked without a single error message."* That consistency matters — third-party cartridges often trigger printer warnings or fail to recognize entirely, and this one avoids those headaches.

## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The page yield is roughly 200 pages, which is on the lower end for a tri-color cartridge. If you're printing a lot of color-heavy documents, you'll be replacing this more often than you'd like. At typical retail pricing, the cost per page is noticeably higher than what you'd get with a high-yield XL cartridge or a generic brand. There's also no way to reset the ink level sensor manually — if your printer misreads the level, you're stuck with the error until you replace it.

Another quirk: if you've ever enrolled in HP's Instant Ink program, this cartridge may trigger a "non-HP" warning if it's not properly registered. It's a minor annoyance, but worth knowing if you're switching between subscription and standard cartridges.

## Who should buy this

- **Home office users** who print a moderate volume of color documents (20-40 pages per week) and want hassle-free, accurate color output.
- **Students** who need reliable printing for assignments and presentations without wanting to troubleshoot printer errors.
- **Anyone who's been burned by generic cartridges** — if you value "it just works" over saving a few dollars per cartridge, this is your pick.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this work in my HP ENVY 4520?**
A: Yes. The HP 61 cartridge is compatible with most ENVY 4500 and 5500 series models, including the 4520, 4525, and 5530. Check your printer's manual if you're unsure — it's listed on the compatibility chart inside the box.

**Q: How many pages can I expect?**
A: HP rates this at approximately 200 pages for tri-color printing at 5% coverage. If you're printing full-page photos, expect far fewer — maybe 30-50 prints before it runs dry.

**Q: Is the HP 61XL a better deal?**
A: The XL version offers roughly double the yield for about 30-40% more cost. If you print regularly, the XL is the better value per page. The standard 61 is fine for light, occasional use.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want genuine HP reliability without printer errors, you print color a few times a week, and you prefer OEM quality over saving a couple of dollars.

**Skip it if:** You're a high-volume printer who needs the best cost-per-page, or you only print in black and white — in that case, grab the HP 61 Black instead.

For the average home office user, this is a dependable, no-surprises cartridge that does exactly what HP promises. The premium price is the only real drawback, but for many, the peace of mind is worth it.
