## What You Actually Get

The BRAECNstock is a folio-style case with a hard back panel, soft microfiber interior, and a tri-fold front cover that doubles as a stand. It's designed to fit two specific devices: the 13-inch iPad Air (M4, M3, or M2) and the 12.9-inch iPad Pro 6th generation. The cutouts are positioned for the Apple Pencil Pro's magnetic charging side, and there's a dedicated slot for the pencil on the edge.

The case weighs in light enough that you won't notice it in a bag, but the material feels denser than the cheap TPU cases that dominate the budget end of Amazon. The front cover has a sleep/wake magnet built in, and the hinge area is reinforced — that's where most folio cases fail within six months.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The stand is the star here. The tri-fold design offers multiple viewing angles, and the hinge holds its position without sagging. I've tested cases where the stand collapses the moment you tap the screen — that's not happening here. The magnet closure is strong enough to keep the cover shut in a backpack, but not so strong that you struggle to open it one-handed.

The fit is precise. The camera bump has a raised lip that protects the lenses when you set the iPad face-down, and the screen bezel is slightly elevated so the glass doesn't touch surfaces. The Apple Pencil Pro sits flush in its charging position with the case on — that's a detail many cases get wrong.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this case has two real drawbacks. First, there's no keyboard or trackpad integration. If you're buying this for your M4 iPad Air expecting a laptop replacement, you'll be disappointed. This is a protective case with a stand — nothing more.

Second, the side buttons are stiff. The protective lip around the volume and power buttons adds thickness, which means you'll press harder than you would on a bare iPad. It loosens up slightly with use, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

The back panel also shows fingerprints and smudges easily. If you're using this in a professional setting where the iPad sits on a desk visible to clients, you'll be wiping it down regularly.

## Who Should Buy This

This case is ideal for three specific types of buyers:

1. **Students and note-takers** who use the Apple Pencil Pro constantly and need a case that protects the pencil without adding bulk
