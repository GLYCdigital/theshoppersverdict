## The 4-pack that actually makes sense

Most cable multipacks are a trap: you get five 6-foot cables you'll never use and one you'll lose. This 3-foot, 4-pack configuration is different. It's a practical quantity for someone who wants one cable at the desk, one at the nightstand, one in the work bag, and one spare without thinking about it. At 3 feet each, they're short enough to stay tidy but long enough for typical charging positions.

## What you actually get

Four USB-C to USB-C cables, each 3 feet (0.9 meters). They support 3A current — that's 60W at 20V — which covers nearly every phone, tablet, and many ultrabooks. The connectors are standard USB-C on both ends, so they work with any modern device. No adapters, no wall bricks, no carrying case. It's just the cables, which is exactly what a multipack should be.

## Real-world performance

I tested these with a 65W GaN charger and a Pixel 8 Pro. Charging hit full speed — the phone reported "fast charging" within seconds. The 3A rating is the honest spec here: it's not a 5A/100W cable, but unless you're driving a 16-inch MacBook Pro at full tilt, you won't notice the difference. For a phone, tablet, e-reader, or wireless earbuds, 60W is overkill anyway.

Build quality is solid for a non-braided cable. The connectors seat firmly without wobble, and the cable itself is flexible enough to route behind a monitor without kinking. I've had cheaper cables die at the connector within weeks; these feel like they'll hold up through normal daily use.

## Where it falls short

Be honest about the tradeoffs before you click buy.

**No USB-IF certification.** This is the big one. The listing doesn't show official USB-IF certification, which means the 60W rating is a claim, not a verified spec. In practice, most users report consistent performance, but if you're powering expensive hardware at the upper limit of the cable's rating, there's some risk.

**3 feet is limiting.** If your outlet is behind a desk or under a bed, 3 feet won't reach. This is a desk-and-nightstand cable, not a "charge from across the room" cable. Buy the 6-foot version if your layout demands reach.

**No strain relief reinforcement.** The connector ends are standard molded rubber, not the reinforced boots you see on premium cables. They'll survive normal use, but if you're rough on cables — wrapping them tight, stepping on them — expect a shorter lifespan.

## FAQ

**Q: Will these charge my laptop?**
A: If your laptop charges over USB-C and needs 60W or less, yes. Most ultrabooks (MacBook Air, Dell XPS 13, ThinkPad X1) will charge fine. If your laptop has a 100W+ power brick requirement, buy a 5A/100W cable instead.

**Q: Do these support data transfer?**
A: Yes, they'll handle USB 2.0 data speeds (480Mbps). Fine for syncing files or connecting peripherals, but not for high-speed external SSDs. If you need 10Gbps+ transfer, look for a USB 3.1 or Thunderbolt-certified cable.

**Q: Are these compatible with Samsung Super Fast Charging?**
A: Yes, in most cases. Samsung's 25W charging works over 3A cables. The 45W "Super Fast Charging 2.0" requires a 5A cable, so double-check your phone's spec if you own a Galaxy S24 Ultra or similar.
