Let me be blunt: most extended warranties are overpriced peace of mind that rarely pays out. The ASURION 4 Year B2B Laptop Accident Protection Plan for $1000–$1249.99 laptops is a different animal — but it's not perfect. Here's what you need to know before you click "add to cart."

**What you actually get**

This plan covers your laptop for four full years against accidental damage: drops, spills, cracked screens, and electrical surges. That's it — no theft, no loss, no mysterious "malfunction" coverage beyond what the manufacturer already provides. You're buying accident insurance, not a replacement policy.

The price point targets laptops in the $1000–$1249.99 range, so think MacBook Airs, mid-tier ThinkPads, or Dell XPS 13s. Asurion's claims process is direct — you file online or by phone, ship your device in, and they repair or replace it. No per-incident deductible, which is genuinely rare in this category.

**How it performs in the real world**

The 4.0-star rating across 200 reviews tells the real story. Buyers consistently report smooth claim experiences. One reviewer noted, "Dropped my work laptop off my desk, screen cracked completely. Filed the claim Sunday night, had a prepaid label by Monday morning, and got my laptop back in six days. Painless."

That speed matters. Asurion runs their own repair centers rather than outsourcing to random third-party shops. You're getting authorized repairs with genuine parts, not refurbished knockoffs.

The four-year term is the sweet spot. Business laptops typically start showing wear — hinges, keyboards, battery degradation — right around the 24-36 month mark. This plan covers you through that entire window.

**Where it stumbles**

The 30-day purchase window is the biggest gotcha. If you bought your laptop three weeks ago, you're fine. Three months? Out of luck. This plan must be purchased at the same time as — or within 30 days of — your laptop. That's restrictive, especially for businesses that buy in batches.

The repair turnaround is another consideration. You're shipping your laptop away for 5-10 days. If you're a road warrior or run a one-laptop operation, that downtime hurts. There's no loaner program and no advance replacement option.

And here's the thing that surprises some business buyers: theft and loss are not covered. The name says "B2B," but this is strictly accident coverage. If your laptop gets stolen from a coffee shop, you're eating the cost.

**Who should buy this**

This plan is ideal for three types of buyers: business owners who equip employees with laptops in this price range (one claim pays for the plan many times over), remote workers who travel with their machines, and anyone who's ever watched a $1,200 laptop die from a 3-foot drop.

Skip it if you have a desktop setup, you're buying a budget laptop under $700, or your business already has a comprehensive device protection policy through your insurance broker.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this cover keyboard spills like coffee?**
A: Yes. Liquid damage from spills is explicitly covered under accidental damage. That includes keyboards, trackpads, and internal components affected by the liquid.

**Q: Can I transfer the plan if I sell the laptop?**
A: Yes, Asurion allows plan transfer to the new owner. You'll need to contact customer service and provide proof of sale.

**Q: What happens if they can't repair my laptop?**
A: If the device is beyond repair, Asurion will replace it with an equivalent model or issue a reimbursement for the original purchase price, up to $1249.99.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You're a business owner, frequent traveler, or anyone who carries a $1000+ laptop daily. The no-deductible accident coverage and 4-year term deliver real protection at a reasonable price point.

**Skip it if:** You're outside the 30-day purchase window, your laptop lives on a desk, or you're expecting theft/loss protection.
