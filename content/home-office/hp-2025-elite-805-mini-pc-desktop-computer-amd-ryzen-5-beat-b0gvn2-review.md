The HP Elite 805 Mini PC is the kind of machine that disappears into your workspace — literally. If you're tired of a tower taking up half your desk, this 7-inch-square box mounts behind a monitor and frees your entire surface. But small size means nothing if the performance isn't there. After digging through 215 Amazon ratings and the spec sheet, here's the honest breakdown.

## What's actually in the box

HP ships the Elite 805 with the Mini PC unit, a 90W external power brick, a VESA mounting plate with screws, and a quick-start guide. No keyboard, mouse, or monitor — this is a bare-bones business desktop intended for people who already have peripherals. The chassis is brushed aluminum over a steel frame, and it feels substantially built, not like a cheap plastic stick PC. Dimensions are roughly 7 x 7 x 1.5 inches, and it weighs just over 2 pounds.

## Performance in the real world

The AMD Ryzen 5 processor in this configuration is a genuine six-core chip, not a low-power U-series compromise. In day-to-day office work — think 20+ Chrome tabs, a 100,000-row Excel sheet, and a video call running simultaneously — it doesn't break a sweat. Multiple reviewers specifically note the speed. As one buyer put it: "Boots in seconds, and I've thrown everything at it — it just doesn't care."

That said, the integrated Radeon graphics are the ceiling here. You can drive two 4K displays for productivity, but don't plan on gaming or rendering 4K video timelines. This is a work machine, and it knows it.

## Build quality and thermals

The Elite 805's cooling solution is a single blower fan pushing air across a copper heat sink. Under normal office loads, it's near-silent — you'll forget it's running. But push sustained CPU-heavy tasks like video encoding or large data analysis, and the fan becomes clearly audible. Not annoying, but present. One reviewer noted: "It's fine for spreadsheets, but when the fan spins up, you'll know."

Upgradeability is a genuine strong suit. The bottom panel pops off with one thumbscrew, giving tool-free access to two SODIMM RAM slots and a single M.2 NVMe slot. Memory is upgradeable to 64GB, which extends the machine's lifespan significantly.

## What buyers consistently praise

Across the ratings, three themes dominate. First: the footprint. "I forgot it was even there — mounted it behind my monitor and my desk is finally clean." Second: reliability. Multiple long-term users report zero crashes or driver issues over months of daily use. Third: the price-to-performance ratio for a business-class machine with a three-year warranty.

## Where it falls short

The single M.2 slot is the biggest practical limitation. If you buy the 512GB version and need more storage later, you're replacing the drive and reinstalling your OS — not just adding a second SSD. There's no 2.5-inch bay internally, so expansion means an external USB drive. Also, the included 90W power brick is proprietary, so if you lose it, replacement isn't a trip to Best Buy.

## Who should buy this

This is for two specific groups. First: office workers and remote employees who want a clean, minimal desk setup and don't need discrete graphics. Second: IT managers deploying fleets of identical, manageable desktops — the VESA mount and tool-free access make deployment and servicing fast. Skip it if you're a gamer, video editor, or someone who needs more than one internal drive.

## FAQ

**Can this handle dual 4K monitors?**
Yes. The Ryzen 5's integrated graphics drive two 4K displays at 60Hz via DisplayPort and HDMI. Text stays sharp; no flicker reported.

**Is the RAM user-upgradeable?**
Absolutely. Two SODIMM slots, accessible with one thumbscrew. Most buyers upgrade to 32GB for under $60.

**Does it come with Windows 11 Pro?**
Yes, this configuration ships with Windows 11 Pro pre-installed, which is important for BitLocker encryption and remote desktop features.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a compact, dependable office desktop that mounts out of sight, handles heavy productivity work without complaint, and offers easy future upgrades.

**Skip it if:** You need gaming performance, plan to expand storage without replacing the boot drive, or require absolute silence under load.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, well-built mini PC that nails the office use case, with honest limitations that are clearly communicated upfront. The 4.3-star Amazon average matches the real experience: very good, not perfect, and worth the money for the right buyer.
