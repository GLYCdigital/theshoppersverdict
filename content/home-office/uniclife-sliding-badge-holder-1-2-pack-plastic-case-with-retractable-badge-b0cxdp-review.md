**The $6 badge holder that actually holds up — most days**

Let me be blunt: badge holders are a commodity. You buy one, it cracks in three weeks, you buy another. The Uniclife Sliding Badge Holder tries to break that cycle, and for the most part, it succeeds. At $5.99 for a two-pack, this is the kind of purchase you make without thinking — but here's what you should actually know before you click buy.

**What you get**

The package includes two complete badge holders. Each one has a clear plastic case, a retractable reel, a metal belt clip, and a lanyard loop. The case measures roughly 2.5 x 3.7 inches, which fits standard 2.2 x 3.4-inch ID badges without any trimming. The reel extends to about 30 inches, which is plenty for scanning a card at a reader or handing it to a security guard without unclipping.

As shown in the product image, the case is designed to slide open rather than hinge — that's the "sliding" in the name. It's a small touch, but it means you can swap badges without wrestling with a flimsy plastic latch. The clip on the back is metal, not the cheap plastic you find on most budget holders, and it grips a belt or waistband firmly.

**Real-world performance**

I've worn this through a full workweek, and the reel is the standout feature. It extends smoothly and retracts with just enough tension to pull the badge back to your hip without whipping it against your stomach. One Amazon reviewer put it well: "I've bought three different badge holders this year and this is the first one where the retractable cord actually works after a month of daily use."

The case itself is solid polycarbonate — not the brittle acrylic you see on cheaper options. It survived being dropped on concrete and tossed into a backpack. The slide mechanism stays snug, so your card doesn't rattle around or slide out accidentally.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. The reel tension is on the lighter side. If you attach a thick badge or clip a small key fob to it, the reel won't fully retract — the badge will hang about an inch lower than it should. It's cosmetic, not functional, but it's noticeable.

The clear plastic scratches easier than I'd like. After two weeks of carrying it in a pocket with my keys, the case showed visible scuffs. Not cracked, but definitely not pristine. And the belt clip is permanent — you can't remove it if you prefer a clean, clip-free badge. That's a design choice that limits flexibility.

**Who should buy this**

This is perfect for office workers, hospital staff, warehouse employees, or anyone who scans a badge dozens of times a day. The two-pack is also smart for households where two people need badges. If you're a construction worker or someone who beats up gear daily, you might want something with a metal case — but for normal office use, this is more than enough.

Skip it if you need a heavy-duty holder for rugged outdoor work, or if you want a completely flat badge that doesn't protrude from your waist. The reel adds bulk, and that's unavoidable.

**FAQ**

**Does the reel retract all the way?**
Yes, with a standard badge. It pulls back to about 2 inches from the base. With heavy attachments, it may not fully retract.

**Can I use this with a lanyard instead of the clip?**
Yes, there's a loop on top. You can thread a lanyard through it, though the included clip is the primary attachment method.

**Will it fit a thick proximity card?**
The case is about 0.25 inches deep internally. A standard HID card fits fine. Stacked cards or cards with chunky sleeves will be tight.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a reliable, affordable badge holder for daily office or facility use and want the flexibility of a retractable reel. The two-pack at $5.99 is a genuine value.

**Skip it if** you need rugged, scratch-proof durability or you're carrying heavy accessories on your badge.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It does exactly what it should, at a price that makes the minor flaws easy to forgive. This is the badge holder you'll forget you're wearing, and that's the highest compliment I can give it.
