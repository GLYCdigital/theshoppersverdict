**Bottom line up front:** If you want a minimalist sleeve that actually protects a 15.6-inch laptop without adding bulk, the Prote is a strong contender. But "durable computer carrying bag" in the title is overselling it — this is a sleeve, not a briefcase. Know the difference before you buy.

## What You Actually Get

The Prote is a straightforward padded sleeve. No shoulder strap, no extra compartments, no frills. You get the main compartment with a zipper closure, a slim front pocket (fits a tablet or documents, but not much else), and a top grab handle. The exterior is a tight-weave nylon with a water-resistant coating. Interior is a soft faux-fleece lining that won't scratch your machine.

Dimensions run about 16.5 x 11.5 x 1.5 inches. If your laptop is a chunky gaming rig or a thicker workstation, measure first — this fits standard 15.6-inch ultrabooks and business laptops, not the 2-inch-thick behemoths.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

I've tested this sleeve for three weeks of daily commutes, airport runs, and coffee shop sessions. The stitching is clean throughout — no loose threads, no stress points pulling apart at the handle attachment. The zipper is smooth and doesn't snag on the lining, which is a common failure point on cheaper sleeves.

The real standout is the protection. There's shock-absorbing foam at all six corners — not just flat padding on the front and back. That corner protection is what saves your laptop when you set the bag down a little harder than you meant to. The raised bottom lip is a thoughtful touch: set the sleeve on a wet counter or damp grass, and your laptop stays elevated.

One thing I appreciate: the fit is snug. Many sleeves are baggy, letting the laptop shift around inside. The Prote holds your machine in place. That snugness means no rattling, no sliding, and better impact absorption because the laptop isn't bouncing inside the sleeve.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations.

**No padding on the handle.** Carrying a 4-5 pound laptop for more than a few minutes gets uncomfortable. The handle is just a nylon loop — adequate, but not comfortable for long hauls.

**The front pocket is shallow.** It fits a tablet or a few documents, but don't expect to cram in a charger, mouse, and cables. This is a sleeve with a small pocket, not an organizer.

**Not for thick laptops.** If your 15.6-inch machine is over 1 inch thick, this won't zip comfortably. The dimensions are designed for slim ultrabooks and standard business laptops.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want a dedicated, high-quality sleeve for daily commutes where the laptop goes into a backpack or tote. You value corner protection over extra pockets. You want something that will survive years of daily use.

**Skip it if:** You need to carry a charger and peripherals in the same bag. You have a thick gaming laptop. You want a briefcase-style bag with a shoulder strap.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my 15.6-inch MacBook Pro or Dell XPS?**
A: Yes for standard 15.6-inch models like the XPS 15, MacBook Pro 16 (it's technically a 16-inch but fits if your model is under 16.5 x 11 inches), and most Lenovo ThinkPads. Measure your laptop's dimensions first — if it's under 16.5 x 11.5 inches, you're good.

**Q: Is the water resistance real or just a coating that wears off?**
A: It's a durable water-repellent (DWR) coating on the nylon. It handles light rain and splashes well. It's not waterproof — don't submerge it or leave it in a downpour. The coating will degrade over time but should last a year or two with normal use.

**Q: Can I fit a tablet and charger in the front pocket?**
A: A slim tablet (iPad or similar) fits. A charger does not — the pocket is too shallow. If you need to carry accessories, get a backpack or a proper briefcase instead.
