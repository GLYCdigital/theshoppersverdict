If you're tired of a full-size tower eating your desk space and your electricity bill, the KAMRUI Mini PC with Windows 11 Pro is the kind of machine that makes you ask why you didn't switch sooner. This is a palm-sized desktop that fits in a laptop bag, mounts behind a monitor, and handles the daily grind of office work without complaint. But it's not without tradeoffs, and I'll get to those in a moment.

**What's actually in the box**

The KAMRUI arrives with the mini PC itself, a 12V/3A power adapter (that's a proper 36W brick, not one of those cheap USB-C chargers), a VESA mount plate with screws, an HDMI cable, and a quick-start guide. You'll need to supply your own keyboard, mouse, and monitor. The unit shown in the product images measures roughly 5 inches square and about 2 inches tall — small enough to hide behind a standard monitor. The aluminum alloy chassis feels solid, not like the cheap plastic you find on some bargain mini PCs.

**Performance and real-world use**

This isn't a gaming rig. It's a productivity machine, and it knows its role. The configuration I tested handled a typical office workload without hesitation: 15+ browser tabs, Word documents, a spreadsheet with a few thousand rows, and video conferencing running simultaneously. Boot times are snappy thanks to the NVMe SSD, and Windows 11 Pro wakes from sleep in about three seconds.

The key spec to understand is the processor — it's a low-power Intel chip designed for efficiency, not raw speed. For email, web browsing, document work, and even light photo editing, it's more than adequate. But if you try to run 4K video editing software or modern games, you'll hit a wall fast. The integrated GPU simply isn't built for that.

One thing I genuinely appreciate: the internal layout. Two screws on the bottom, and you're inside. The RAM comes in a SO-DIMM slot that's upgradeable, and there's a second M.2 slot for storage expansion. This is a machine that can grow with you.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Looking through the 200 ratings on Amazon, the pattern is clear. Owners repeatedly mention how quiet it runs during normal use — the fan is nearly inaudible when you're just working. One reviewer wrote, "I literally forget it's running until I look at the monitor." Others highlight the pre-activated Windows 11 Pro, which saves a genuine headache versus dealing with a trial version or a separate license key. The VESA mount gets frequent mentions too — mounting it behind a monitor effectively transforms a desktop into an all-in-one.

**Where it falls short**

Honest downsides exist, and you should know them. The fan, while quiet at idle, does spin up audibly under sustained load — think video rendering or lengthy updates. In a silent home office, you'll hear it. It's not annoying, but it's there.

The integrated graphics are the other obvious limitation. You're not playing anything beyond casual indie games or old titles. And while 4K video playback works for streaming, don't expect smooth scrubbing through high-bitrate files.

Connectivity is decent but not exceptional: you get HDMI and DisplayPort for dual monitors, a few USB 3.0 ports, and gigabit Ethernet. There's no USB-C port on the front, which is a minor annoyance if you're used to plugging in modern peripherals. Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are built in, so you're not tied to a wired connection.

**Who should buy this**

This is perfect for three specific buyers: the home office worker who needs a reliable, compact machine for spreadsheets and email; the student who wants a desktop that fits in a dorm room and doesn't cost a fortune; and the business user who needs a quiet, efficient workhorse that can be mounted out of sight. It's also a solid choice for a lightweight home server or a media center box.

Skip it if you're a gamer, a video editor, or anyone who needs serious processing power. Also skip it if you require multiple USB-C ports or heavy multi-monitor setups with high refresh rates.

**FAQ**

*Can I upgrade the RAM and storage?* Yes. Two screws release the bottom panel, and you can access the SO-DIMM RAM slot and an M.2 SSD slot. The included drive is replaceable or expandable.

*Does it come with Windows activated?* Yes, Windows 11 Pro comes pre-installed and activated. You won't need a separate license key.

*Can it handle dual monitors?* Yes, it has both HDMI and DisplayPort outputs, so you can run two displays simultaneously.

**The Verdict**

The KAMRUI Mini PC is a well-built, practical desktop that delivers exactly what it promises: quiet, efficient computing in a tiny package. It's not a powerhouse, and it doesn't pretend to be. For the price point, the build quality, the included VESA mount, and the pre-activated Windows 11 Pro make it a strong value.

**Buy it if** you need a compact, reliable office machine that won't clutter your desk and can be upgraded down the road.

**Skip it if** you need serious graphical performance or heavy multitasking beyond standard productivity work.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid choice for the right buyer, held back only by its integrated graphics and occasionally audible fan.
