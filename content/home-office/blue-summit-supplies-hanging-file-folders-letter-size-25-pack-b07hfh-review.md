Let me be direct: buying hanging file folders is about as exciting as buying printer paper. But get it wrong — flimsy rods, tearing slots, labels that peel — and you'll curse that decision every single time you open your drawer. I've tested dozens of these over the years, and the Blue Summit Supplies 25-pack actually earns its 4.8-star rating. Here's the real story.

**What You Actually Get**

This is a straightforward 25-pack of letter-size hanging folders. Each folder measures 11-3/4" x 9-1/4" with a 1/8" scored fold at the bottom — standard dimensions that fit any conventional file drawer or hanging file box. The tabs are full-cut (meaning the tab position runs the entire length of the folder), which gives you maximum flexibility in positioning. The package includes clear plastic tab inserts, but you'll need to print or handwrite your own labels.

**Build Quality That Surprises**

The spec sheet says these are made from 11-point manila stock. In plain English: that's noticeably stiffer than the cheap 9-point folders you find at dollar stores. I packed one folder with about 80 sheets of standard copy paper, and the folder held its shape without bowing or sagging at the bottom. The reinforced rod slots — the metal-reinforced holes where the drawer rails slide through — are the weak point on most budget folders. After two weeks of daily drawer access, mine showed zero signs of tearing or stretching.

The plastic tab inserts are a small but meaningful upgrade. They're rigid enough to hold a label flat, and the tab itself locks into the folder's cutout without popping loose. One reviewer noted, "I've bought these twice now. The tabs don't slide around, and the folders don't bend like the cheap ones from the office supply store." That matches my experience.

**Where It Falls Short**

The 25-count is honestly the most frustrating thing about this product. If you're organizing an entire home office or a small business filing system, you'll burn through this box in an afternoon. You're better off buying two or three packs upfront — the per-folder cost drops slightly with multi-pack purchases, and you won't run out mid-project.

There's also no color-coding system built in. If you're the type who color-codes by client or department, you'll need to buy separate colored tabs or invest in a different product entirely. That's not a flaw in the folder itself, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

Finally, the folders ship tightly compressed in the box. During the first few days, you may notice creasing along the folds. It flattens out once the folders hang in a drawer, but if you're the type who notices small cosmetic details, just know it's temporary.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Home office organizers**: If you're finally tackling that pile of paper, these are the right foundation. The stiffness handles a mix of thick and thin documents without collapsing.
- **Small business owners**: For client files, invoices, and project folders, this 25-pack is a solid starter. Buy two boxes if you have more than one drawer to fill.
- **Anyone tired of cheap folders**: If you've been through flimsy folders that bend, sag, or tear, these will be a noticeable upgrade at a reasonable price.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Will these fit in a standard filing cabinet?**
Yes. These are true letter-size hanging folders (11-3/4" x 9-1/4"), designed to fit any standard letter-size file drawer or hanging file box. They will not fit legal-size drawers without adapters.

**Q: Can I write directly on the tabs?**
You can, but the tabs are made of clear plastic. For best results, print labels or use a permanent marker. Pencil and standard pens tend to smear on the plastic surface.

**Q: How many sheets can one folder hold?**
The 11-point stock handles roughly 75–100 sheets of standard copy paper without sagging. Heavier documents like legal pads or bound reports will reduce that capacity.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need dependable hanging folders that won't sag, tear, or fall apart after six months of daily use. The 11-point stock and reinforced rod slots make these a legitimate value pick.

**Skip it if:** You need color-coded organization, require a larger quantity immediately, or you're on a budget tight enough that every dollar matters — cheaper options exist, but you'll likely replace them sooner.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Solid, no-nonsense folders that do exactly what they should. The only real complaint is the pack size, not the product itself.
