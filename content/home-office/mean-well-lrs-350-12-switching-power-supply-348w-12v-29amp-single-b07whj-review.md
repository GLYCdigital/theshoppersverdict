## Where It Falls Short

The fan is the most common complaint. It's a 12V/0.5A sleeve-bearing fan that only spins up under load — great for quiet bench work, but it gets audible above 60% capacity. If your setup draws 20+ amps continuously in a quiet room, you'll hear it. That's a tradeoff for the compact size.

The lack of a power switch and fuse is another practical issue. You'll need to wire in your own switch and protection circuit, which adds cost and complexity. Also, the terminal block screws are tight — if you're using 14AWG wire, you'll struggle to get a clean fit. Use ferrules or step down to 16AWG.
