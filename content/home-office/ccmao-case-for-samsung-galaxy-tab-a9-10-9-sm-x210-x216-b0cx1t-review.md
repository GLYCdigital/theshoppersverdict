If you've ever dropped a tablet face-down on tile, you know the sinking feeling. The CCMAO case for the Samsung Galaxy Tab A9+ is designed to prevent that exact moment — and it does the job without making you hate carrying the thing. After digging through 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, here's what actually matters.

**What you get in the box**

The package includes the two-piece case (a hard polycarbonate back shell and a soft TPU bumper), plus a built-in kickstand and an integrated S Pen holder. There's no screen protector included — that's sold separately. The case is specifically molded for the SM-X210, SM-X216, and SM-X218 variants, so the camera cutout, speaker grilles, and charging port align perfectly. No guessing, no "universal fit" nonsense.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The dual-layer construction is the headline here. The hard back resists flexing, while the TPU bumper absorbs shock at the corners — which is exactly where drops typically land. The raised edges around the screen and camera lens sit about 2mm proud of the surfaces, so flat-on-the-table placement won't scratch your display. Button covers are responsive; you won't be mashing the power key hoping it registers.

The kickstand deserves specific praise. It offers two viewing angles — roughly 45 and 60 degrees — and locks into place with a satisfying click. It's stable enough for typing with a Bluetooth keyboard on a desk, and it holds position when you're tapping the screen. One buyer noted, *"The stand is actually usable on my lap, which I didn't expect from a case this affordable."* That tracks with the general sentiment across reviews.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. This is a rugged case, and it shows. The added thickness is noticeable — you're looking at roughly half an inch of extra bulk around the edges. If you want a slim folio that slides into a briefcase pocket, this isn't it. The S Pen holder is also positioned on the right side, which is fine for right-handed use but gets in the way if you're left-handed and hold the tablet in landscape mode. A few reviewers mentioned the holder feels snug at first — it loosens up over a week or two, but don't expect a friction-free slide-in on day one.

**Who should buy this**

- **Students and commuters** who toss a tablet in a backpack with books and laptops. The drop protection is real.
- **Parents** handing a Tab A9+ to kids. The TPU bumper survives the inevitable floor toss.
- **Note-takers** who use the S Pen daily and don't want to carry a separate stylus sleeve.

**Skip it if** you prioritize a slim profile or use the tablet primarily docked on a desk — there are sleeker folio options that sacrifice protection for elegance.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this case work with the S Pen charging?**
A: The Tab A9+ S Pen doesn't charge wirelessly like the Tab S series — it's a basic stylus. So yes, the holder just stores it. No charging issues because there's no charging to worry about.

**Q: Will it fit with a screen protector installed?**
A: Yes. The raised edges clear most standard tempered glass protectors. Just make sure your protector isn't one of those oversized "full coverage" versions that extend to the very edge of the glass — those can push against the case lip.

**Q: Can I use the kickstand in both portrait and landscape?**
A: Landscape only. The hinge mechanism is mounted to the long edge, so portrait mode requires holding the tablet or propping it against something.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need dependable drop protection, a functional kickstand, and a secure S Pen slot at a reasonable price. The 4.5-star average holds up.

**Skip it if** you want a slim, minimal case and don't mind risking a cracked screen for the sake of aesthetics.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It's not the sleekest option, but it's the one you'll be glad you had when the tablet slips.
