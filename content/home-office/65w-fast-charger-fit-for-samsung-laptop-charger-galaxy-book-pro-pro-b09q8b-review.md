Losing your Samsung laptop charger is a specific kind of panic. The OEM brick is proprietary-ish, expensive to replace, and the generic knockoffs on Amazon are a gamble. This 65W USB-C Power Delivery charger from the "Fit for Samsung" line attempts to solve that — and for most Galaxy Book owners, it mostly does. But "mostly" carries some caveats worth knowing before you click buy.

## What you actually get

The box contains the wall adapter (foldable prongs, compact brick) and a 6-foot USB-C to USB-C cable rated for 100W. That cable length is a quiet win — Samsung's stock charger gives you about half that, which always felt stingy when your outlet is behind the desk. The adapter itself is small: roughly 2.5 x 1.5 x 1 inch, about half the volume of the OEM brick. It's a genuine travel upgrade, not just a replacement.

## Performance: Does it actually fast-charge?

Yes — if you have the right Samsung laptop. The charger outputs 65W over USB-C Power Delivery, which matches what the Galaxy Book Pro, Pro 360, and Book2 series draw. Real-world testing shows the Galaxy Book Pro 360 going from dead to about 50% in 45–50 minutes, which is consistent with Samsung's own "fast charging" marketing. It also works for Galaxy phones at lower wattage, though you'll want to check if your phone negotiates properly — some users report it defaults to slower charging on older Galaxy models.

The unit supports PD 3.0 and PPS, which matters for Samsung devices. PPS (Programmable Power Supply) is what lets Samsung phones pull their optimal charging curve, and it's missing from many cheaper third-party bricks. This one has it.

## What buyers consistently praise

The 4.2-star rating across 210 reviews reflects a split between genuine satisfaction and some real frustration. The praise is consistent:

- **Size and portability** — "Way smaller than the charger that came with my Galaxy Book Pro. Fits in the pocket of my laptop sleeve." This is the most repeated compliment.
- **Charging speed** — "My Book Pro 360 went from 15% to 80% in about an hour. Same as the Samsung charger."
- **Cable quality** — The included 6-foot cable gets specific shout-outs for durability and length.

## Where it falls short

Here's the honest part. A meaningful number of reviews mention **intermittent connection drops** — the laptop stops charging randomly, requiring a replug. This appears more common with non-Samsung laptops (Dell XPS, HP Spectre) trying to draw full 65W. If you're buying this as a universal laptop charger, temper expectations.

Second, **there's no USB-A port**. The OEM Samsung charger for many Galaxy Book models has one. If you're used to charging your phone and laptop from one brick at a coffee shop, this forces a choice.

Third, and this is the big one for older models: **if you have a Galaxy Book with a barrel connector** (the round plug, not USB-C), this charger won't work without a separate tip adapter that isn't included. Check your laptop's port before ordering. Multiple 1-star reviews are from people who bought this assuming it worked with their older Galaxy Book — it doesn't.

## Who should buy this

- **Galaxy Book Pro/Pro 360/Book2 owners** who need a second charger for the office or travel bag — this is a solid, cheaper alternative to Samsung's $70+ OEM replacement.
- **Frequent flyers** who want something compact without sacrificing full 65W output.
- **Samsung phone + laptop households** that want one USB-C charger that handles both — PPS support makes this work.

Skip it if you have an older Galaxy Book with a barrel connector, or if you need a do-everything travel charger with multiple ports.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this charge my Galaxy Book Pro at the same speed as the original Samsung charger?**
Yes. It delivers the same 65W over USB-C PD with PPS support, so charging times match the OEM brick for compatible models.

**Q: Can I use this with a non-Samsung laptop?**
Technically yes, since it's standard USB-C PD. But some users report connection drops at full 65W on Dell and HP laptops. It works — just less reliably than with Samsung hardware.

**Q: Does it come with the barrel connector tip for older Galaxy Book models?**
No. That tip must be purchased separately, which adds cost and complexity. If your laptop uses a round barrel plug, this isn't the charger for you.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you own a USB-C Galaxy Book and want a compact, capable spare charger at a reasonable price. **Skip it if** you have an older barrel-connector model or need multi-device charging from a single brick.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — It does the core job well for the right laptop, the form factor is genuinely better than OEM, and the cable is a thoughtful inclusion. The connection quirks and missing barrel tip keep it from being a universal recommendation.
