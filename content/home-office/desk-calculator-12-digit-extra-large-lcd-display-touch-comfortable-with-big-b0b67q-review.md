**Performance & Build**

Functionally, it's a standard 12-digit calculator: basic arithmetic, square root, percentage, and a grand total key. Nothing fancy — no scientific functions or printing capability. But what it does, it does reliably. The dual power system (solar cell + LR44 battery) is a smart touch. Office lighting alone keeps it alive, and the battery is a backup for dimmer rooms.
