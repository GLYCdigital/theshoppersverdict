Let's be honest about flash drives: most people buy them because they lost the last one, or because they're tired of digging through a drawer of 2GB relics. The KOOTION 5-pack solves both problems with color coding and mixed speeds — but it's not without tradeoffs.

**What you actually get**

Five drives in a mix of USB 3.0 and USB 2.0, distinguished by color. The blue and red units are the USB 3.0 speedsters; the lighter tones (white, orange, green) are USB 2.0. Each drive has a rotating metal swivel cap that flips out of the way rather than detaching — a genuinely thoughtful design choice. There's also a small LED indicator on each unit that blinks during transfers, which sounds minor until you're staring at a frozen file copy wondering if it's working.

No software, no encryption suite, no bloatware. You get five bare drives in a clear plastic case. That's it.

**Performance: the split personality**

This is where the pack gets interesting. The USB 3.0 drives (red and blue) hit read speeds around 80-100 MB/s and writes around 30-40 MB/s in real-world use. That's not flagship territory, but it's respectable for the price. Moving a 4GB video folder takes about two minutes — acceptable for office work.

The USB 2.0 drives are a different story. Writes hover around 5-8 MB/s. Transferring a single 1GB file takes roughly two and a half minutes. For Word docs, spreadsheets, and PDFs, it's fine. For photo backups, you'll want to reach for the blue or red one every time.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The color-coding system is the standout feature in customer feedback. One reviewer noted: "I work in a print shop and needed to keep client files separate. The colors make it idiot-proof — no more picking up the wrong drive mid-project." Another mentioned the swivel caps: "Lost the cap on every flash drive I've owned. These actually stay attached."

Build quality gets mixed but mostly positive comments. The plastic bodies are light — almost too light — but the USB connectors themselves are sturdy and fit snugly into ports on laptops and desktops alike. No loose connections, no "wiggle to get it to read" issues.

**Where it falls short**

The USB 2.0 drives are the weak link. They're fine for document transfer, but anyone buying this pack expecting uniform speed will be disappointed. The plastic housing also feels a bit hollow — one reviewer described it as "cheap but functional," which is accurate. And there's no lanyard hole, so if you're the type to keep a drive on your keychain, these aren't it.

One more thing: the LED light is bright. Blinking blue light in a dark office can be a distraction, though it does turn off when not in use.

**Who should buy this**

- **Office managers** stocking a shared supply drawer — the color coding means employees stop asking "which one is mine?"
- **IT pros** who need to keep multiple bootable or diagnostic tools separated by color
- **Students** who want a dedicated drive per class without buying individually
- **Anyone** who's lost more than two flash drives in the past year

Skip it if you need high sustained write speeds for video editing, or if you need a rugged, waterproof drive for fieldwork.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are the USB 3.0 drives backwards compatible with USB 2.0 ports?**
A: Yes. They'll work in any USB-A port, but you'll get USB 2.0 speeds if plugged into an older port. The color coding helps you remember which is which.

**Q: Can I format these for Mac and Windows?**
A: Yes. They come formatted as FAT32, which works on both. For files over 4GB, you'll need to reformat to exFAT — a 30-second process in Disk Utility or File Explorer.

**Q: How durable are the swivel caps?**
A: They're spring-loaded and held in place by friction. They've held up fine in normal use, but they're not designed for rough handling. Treat them like a normal office pen, not a tactical gear item.

**The Verdict**

The KOOTION 5-pack is a practical, well-priced solution for anyone who needs multiple reliable drives without the hassle of labels and lost caps. The mixed speeds are a feature, not a bug — you always have a fast option for big files and a cheap option for documents. It's not going to win awards for premium build quality, but at this price point, the color-coding system alone justifies the purchase.

**Buy it if** you need organized, color-coded storage for work or school and don't mind slower speeds on the 2.0 drives.

**Skip it if** you need uniform high-speed performance across all drives, or if you're looking for rugged, keychain-ready hardware.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A smart, practical purchase with one intentional compromise.
