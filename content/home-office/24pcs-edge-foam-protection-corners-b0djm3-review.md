You've bumped your hip into that sharp desk corner one too many times. Or maybe a toddler is now cruising around your home office, eye-level with every lethal edge in the room. The 24Pcs Edge Foam Protection Corners ($20.99) is a simple, effective fix — but let's be clear about what you're actually getting before you hit buy.

**What's in the box**

Twenty-four individual L-shaped foam corners. Each piece measures roughly 2 inches along both arms with a 10mm thickness — that's substantial cushioning, not the thin foam tape you find at dollar stores. The backing reveals a strong adhesive strip along both edges of the L. As shown in the product image, they're designed to wrap around the top edge and down both sides of a corner, giving full coverage where impacts happen.

The set covers a standard 60-inch desk (four corners) plus a bookshelf, coffee table, and nightstand — or you can double up on your most dangerous edges.

**Real-world performance**

Installation takes about 30 seconds per corner. Clean the surface with alcohol, peel the backing, press firmly for 10 seconds. That's it. The adhesive is the standout feature here — it's genuinely aggressive. One reviewer noted, *"I stuck these on my son's dresser and they haven't budged in three months, even with him yanking on them."* That tracks with the 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews.

The 10mm thickness does real work. A direct hip check into a protected corner produces a soft thud, not a bruise. For households with elderly family members or young children, this is meaningful protection. The foam is closed-cell, so it won't absorb moisture and degrade in humid environments.

**Where it falls short**

The bright white color is the biggest tradeoff. On white or light furniture, it's nearly invisible. On walnut or black desks, it looks like a bandage. After a few weeks of contact, the corners will show scuff marks — there's no way around it with light-colored foam. Some buyers have painted them with acrylic craft paint to match darker furniture, which works but adds effort.

The adhesive is also permanent. If you misalign a piece, peeling it off tears the foam and leaves sticky residue. Measure twice, stick once. A few reviewers mentioned the corners arriving slightly compressed in the packaging, but they fluffed back to shape within a day.

**Who should buy this**

- **Parents of crawlers and toddlers:** This is the primary use case. Childproofing sharp furniture corners is exactly what these are designed for.
- **Home office workers with tight spaces:** If you're constantly squeezing past a desk corner in a small room, these will save your hips.
- **Elderly household members:** Fall protection around hard furniture edges is worth the modest price.

**Skip it if** you have dark furniture and care deeply about aesthetics, or if you need something repositionable.

**FAQ**

**Will these stick to textured or laminate surfaces?**
Laminate, yes. Textured wood or brick, no — the adhesive needs a smooth surface for full contact. Clean with rubbing alcohol first for best results.

**How long do they last?**
Most reviewers report 6+ months with daily use. The foam compresses slightly over time but maintains impact protection. Replacement is cheap enough that yearly swaps are reasonable.

**Can I cut them to fit smaller corners?**
Yes, a sharp utility knife cuts them cleanly. Just recut the adhesive backing too, or the exposed edge won't stick.
