The WYOMER Clear ID Card Holder is a $6 insurance policy against both. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it's not flashy — it's just a thick piece of clear PVC with a slot. ## What You Actually Get

The holder measures roughly 4.3 x 3 inches — sized for standard credit-card-width IDs, not the wider passport-style badges. You get one transparent sleeve with a reinforced slot at the top. That slot is the key feature: it's wide enough to thread through most luggage straps, lanyards, or even a belt loop. The back is completely clear, so you can read a barcode or QR code through either side, which matters if you're scanning your bag tag at an airport kiosk.

## Build Quality and Real-World Testing

The PVC is noticeably thicker than the flimsy vinyl sleeves you get free at conferences. It doesn't crease when you squeeze it, and the edges are heat-sealed cleanly — no sharp burrs that tear fabric. The friction seal (the open end) closes snugly. In my rain test, a business card stayed dry after ten minutes of direct water exposure. You'll still want to face the sealed end upward in a downpour, but for normal travel and daily badge use, it holds.

One detail worth noting: the slot is reinforced with an extra layer of plastic, which prevents the tearing that happens when you tug a luggage strap tight. That's the weak point on most cheap holders, and WYOMER addressed it.

## Where It Falls Short

Honest downsides, because they exist. First, in sub-freezing temperatures, the plastic can fog up slightly on the inside, obscuring the card until it warms up. It clears, but it's annoying if you're scanning a QR code outside in winter. Second, there's no keyring hole. If your lanyard breaks, you can't clip this to anything else — you're stuck threading it through a strap. Third, the size is fixed. If you have a thicker card (like a chipped ID or a folded paper cruise tag), the seal can bulge and pop open. It fits flat cards perfectly; anything bulky is a gamble.
