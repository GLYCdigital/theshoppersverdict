## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and the tounee has two honest limitations.

First, the clamp requires a **flat desk edge**. If your desk has a rounded or beveled lip, the clamp won't seat flush, and you'll get a wobbly mount. Measure your desk edge before ordering.

Second, the tension arm needs **firm adjustment**. If you don't tighten the hex bolt enough, a heavier 16-inch+ laptop will cause slight droop over time. It's fixable — the Allen key is included — but it's a step some buyers miss, leading to frustration.

Also note: this is a **desk-mount**, not a portable stand. You're committing it to one workspace. If you move between home and office, you'll want a second one or a different solution.

**Q: Can I use it with a laptop that has a thick base?**
The adjustable rubber grips accommodate most laptop thicknesses, but ultra-thick gaming laptops with rear vents might sit awkwardly. Check the cradle dimensions against your laptop's base width.

**Q: Does the clamp damage the desk?**
The clamp has rubber padding, so it won't scratch most finished surfaces. On soft woods, consider a thin felt pad between the clamp and the desk for extra protection.
