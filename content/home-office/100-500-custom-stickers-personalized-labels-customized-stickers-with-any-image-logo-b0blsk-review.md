Most sticker shops make you choose between cheap quality and expensive minimums. This custom sticker listing sits in a sweet spot: 100–500 die-cut, full-color vinyl stickers at a price that makes sense for small businesses, event planners, and Etsy sellers who need actual branding — not just a logo slapped on a white square.

## What You Actually Get

The listing lets you upload any image, logo, or design, then pick your quantity (100, 250, or 500) and shape. The stickers arrive on glossy vinyl with a permanent adhesive backing. As shown in the product image, they're die-cut to your design's outline — no rectangular white borders unless you specifically want them.

The vinyl is rated for outdoor use, which means it handles water, UV exposure, and general wear without fading or peeling. That's a meaningful upgrade over paper labels that curl up after one dishwasher cycle.

## Print Quality and Real-World Performance

The print quality is the standout here. Colors come out saturated, and fine details — think small text, gradients, or complex logos — hold up well. One buyer noted, *"I've ordered custom stickers from three different shops and these are the sharpest. The colors are vibrant and the cut is perfect."* Another reviewer echoed that sentiment: *"The die-cut is precise, no jagged edges, and the adhesive is strong. They stuck to my laptop and water bottles without issue."*

For durability, users report the stickers surviving outdoor use, dishwashing, and repeated handling. The glossy finish resists scratches better than matte alternatives, and the adhesive holds firm on metal, plastic, and glass.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be straight about the tradeoffs. The 100-sticker minimum is a real hurdle if you're a first-time buyer or just testing a design. You can't order 25 to see if the colors match your screen — you're committing to a full batch.

The production timeline is also slower than some competitors. Expect 7–10 business days for printing and shipping, so this isn't your "I need stickers by Friday" option. If you're prepping for an event, order at least two weeks out.

One thing a few customers flagged: if your design has large areas of white or very light colors, those sections can look slightly translucent when applied to dark surfaces. It's a minor issue, but worth knowing if you're slapping these on a black laptop or dark-colored water bottle.

## FAQ

**Can I upload a low-resolution image?**
You can, but don't expect magic. The printer needs at least 300 DPI for crisp results at standard sizes. A blurry phone screenshot will look blurry on the sticker. If you're unsure, reach out to the seller with your file before ordering.

**Do these work in a dishwasher?**
Yes, they're water-resistant and held up in customer testing. But after repeated hot wash cycles, the adhesive will eventually weaken. For permanent application, stick them on smooth, clean surfaces and they'll last.

**Can I order different designs in one batch?**
This listing is for a single design per order. If you need multiple designs, you'll need to place separate orders or look for a multi-design listing.
