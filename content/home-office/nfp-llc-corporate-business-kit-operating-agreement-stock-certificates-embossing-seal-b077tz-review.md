Let's be direct about the limitations:

- **The operating agreement is generic.** It works for a single-member LLC or a straightforward multi-member setup. But if you have complex profit-sharing arrangements or investor protections, you need a lawyer to draft custom language. This kit is a starting point, not a substitute for legal counsel.

- **No state-specific forms.** You get the operating agreement template, but you'll still need to file your Articles of Organization with your Secretary of State separately. The kit doesn't include state filing instructions.
