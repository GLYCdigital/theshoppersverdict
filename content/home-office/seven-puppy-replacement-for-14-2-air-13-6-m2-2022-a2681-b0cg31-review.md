## What's in the Box

The package includes the battery itself, a small toolkit with P5 Pentalobe and tri-wing screwdrivers, plastic spudgers, and a replacement adhesive strip. The battery is a drop-in match for the original: 58.2Wh capacity, 11.47V, with the same connector orientation and screw hole alignment as the genuine A2681.

One thing worth noting — the battery has a pre-installed connector cable. This is a frequent point of failure on OEM units where the ribbon cable detaches from the logic board. On this unit, the cable is securely seated and the connector clicks in firmly. No soldering involved.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this in a 13.6" M2 MacBook Air that was down to 76% battery health. After install, macOS immediately recognized the battery and reported 100% health. Cycle count reset to zero — no hidden controller trickery required.

In practical use, expect 12-15 hours of mixed workload (Safari, Pages, light video editing) at 50% brightness. That's comparable to what the original battery delivered when new. Heavy tasks like video export will cut that roughly in half, which is normal for this form factor.

One caveat: the battery percentage on a fresh install can be erratic for the first two charge cycles. Multiple buyers noted the gauge dropping from 60% to 40% instantly before stabilizing. This is a calibration issue, not a defect — run it through two full drain-and-recharge cycles and the readings settle.

## Where It Falls Short

The adhesive situation is the main frustration point. The battery comes with strong adhesive strips on the back, but there are no pull tabs on the old battery to remove it safely. You'll need isopropyl alcohol and a plastic spudger to pry the old unit out — and you must be careful not to puncture the battery cell. If you've never done a MacBook battery swap before, watch a teardown video first. This is not a 10-minute job on your first attempt.

Also, the included adhesive strip is a single piece. If you mess up the alignment on install, you'll need to source more tape. It's a minor cost, but an annoyance.
