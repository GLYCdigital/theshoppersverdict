**The Tablet Floor Stand with 4.2LB Base** is the rare accessory that actually solves the "tablet in bed" problem without requiring you to rig a pillow fort or buy a separate over-bed mount. At 4.1 stars from 204 shoppers, it's not perfect — but for the right buyer, it's a game-changer.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the weighted base, three arm segments, a universal clamp, and the assembly hex wrench. The base is exactly as advertised — 4.2 pounds of solid metal, not sand-filled plastic. The arm extends to roughly 31 inches from base to clamp, and the clamp opens wide enough for tablets from a 4.7-inch phone up to a 12.9-inch iPad Pro.

Assembly takes about 10 minutes. You thread the arm segments, tighten the joints with the included wrench, and you're done. The joints use a friction-lock design — you position the arm, then twist the collar to lock it in place.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this stand with an 11-inch iPad Pro, a Kindle, and a phone. The 4.2-pound base is the headline feature, and it earns the hype. Even with the tablet extended fully overhead at a 70-degree angle, the base held firm on carpet and hardwood. No wobble, no tipping. That's the difference between this and the $20 plastic stands that collapse the moment you touch the screen.

The clamp is well-designed. It has silicone pads that grip without scratching, and the tension is adjustable — you can set it snug for a tablet or lighter for a phone. One-handed removal works: you squeeze the release lever, and the tablet pops out.

The arm's articulation is genuinely useful. You can position the screen at eye level while sitting at a desk, angle it downward for bed use, or swing it completely out of the way when you're done. The joints hold their position once locked — no sagging after 30 minutes of use.

## What Buyers Praise

The stability is the recurring theme in positive reviews. One verified purchaser noted, "The base is heavy enough that I don't worry about my iPad falling on my face at 2 a.m." Another praised the versatility: "Works for my 12.9-inch iPad Pro and my phone — I switch between them daily."

The build quality also gets consistent shout-outs. The aluminum arms feel premium, not like the flimsy hollow tubes on cheaper stands. Several reviewers mention it's held up for months of daily use without loosening.

## Where It Falls Short

The 31-inch arm reach is the biggest limitation. If your bed is tall or you want to use this from a recliner positioned several feet from the wall, the stand won't reach overhead — you'll need to place it right next to the bed. One reviewer put it plainly: "Great stand, but I needed it to reach about 6 inches further for my setup."

Cable management is an afterthought. There's a single clip on the arm, and during overhead use, your charging cable will dangle in your face unless you route it carefully. This is a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

Assembly isn't tool-free. You'll need the included hex wrench, and the joints require decent hand strength to tighten fully. Not a problem for most, but if you have arthritis or limited grip, factor that in.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you're a tablet user who reads, watches, or works in bed and wants a stable, adjustable stand that doesn't wobble. It's also ideal for desk setups where you need a second screen at odd angles.

**Skip it if** you have a tall bed or want to position the stand far from your seating area — the 31-inch reach won't cut it. Also skip if you want tool-free, instant assembly.
