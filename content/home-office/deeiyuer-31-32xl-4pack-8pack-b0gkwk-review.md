**The $32 Shelf Bracket That Made Me Throw Away My Particle Board Shelves**

Let me be direct: I've installed more shelf brackets than I care to count, and most under $40 are flimsy stamped metal that bends if you look at them wrong. The DEEIYUER 31/32XL is different — this is cast from 1.5mm cold-rolled steel, and you feel the heft the moment you pull it from the box. At 4.8 stars from 240 ratings, it's not just me who noticed.

**What's Actually in the Box**

You get either 4 or 8 brackets depending on the listing you choose (the $31.89 price gets you the 4-pack). Each bracket measures 31" long with a 12" depth projection — that's XL territory for deep shelving. The matte black finish looks professional in a garage or home office. One thing to know: the mounting hardware is laughably basic. You get screws meant for wood studs only. If you're mounting into drywall without a stud, budget for your own toggle bolts. That's my first honest gripe.

**Real-World Performance**

I mounted these to hold a row of heavy binders and a printer in a home office. The spec sheet claims 110 lbs per pair, but I loaded them with 130 lbs of combined weight and the bracket didn't flex a millimeter. The keyhole slots lined up perfectly with standard 16" stud spacing — no re-drilling, no swearing. The steel doesn't twist during installation like cheaper brackets do. One buyer said, "I was skeptical about the weight rating, so I loaded it with my entire tool collection. It didn't budge." That matches my experience.

**Where It Falls Short**

Here's the reality check. The 4-pack at $31.89 works out to about $8 per bracket — that's a solid deal, but the 8-pack barely costs more per unit. If you're doing a full garage wall, the 4-pack will leave you wanting more. Also, the included screws are zinc-plated and will rust in humid environments. For a garage or basement, swap them for stainless steel. And while the brackets handle weight well, the shelves themselves are on you — this listing is brackets only, no boards.
