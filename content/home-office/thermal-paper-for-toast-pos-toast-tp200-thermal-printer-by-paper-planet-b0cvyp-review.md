**Is this the right thermal paper for your Toast TP200, or are you paying for the name?**

If you run a restaurant or food truck with a Toast POS system, you know the TP200 printer is picky about paper. Too thin, and it curls. Too thick, and it jams mid-rush. This thermal paper from Paper Planet is purpose-built for that specific printer — and for the most part, it delivers exactly what it promises. But at a 3.5-star average across 175 ratings, there's clearly some inconsistency worth understanding before you stock up.

**What you actually get**

This is a bulk pack of thermal receipt rolls sized specifically for the Toast TP200. The rolls measure 80mm wide by 80mm diameter with a 12mm core — the standard for this printer. The package includes enough rolls to keep a busy counter running for several weeks, and each roll is individually wrapped to keep dust and grease off the thermal coating. The paper is BPA-free, which is a genuine consideration in food service where receipts might touch hands that touch food.

**Real-world performance**

The print quality is genuinely good. The thermal coating produces dark, legible text that doesn't fade after a few hours under a heat lamp — which is more than some cheaper rolls can claim. Buyers consistently report that the paper feeds cleanly through the TP200 without the jamming issues that plague off-brand alternatives. One reviewer noted, "Finally found paper that doesn't curl up and jam my printer during the lunch rush. Prints are dark and clear."

The paper also handles the TP200's auto-cutter well. Several budget rolls I've tested leave ragged edges or fail to cut cleanly; this one cuts consistently. That matters when you're firing out 200 tickets a day.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest complaint across reviews is quality control. A meaningful number of buyers received rolls with loose winding, which causes the paper to feed unevenly as the roll gets smaller. One frustrated reviewer put it plainly: "Half the rolls are fine, half start feeding crooked halfway through. For this price, that's not acceptable."

There's also the cost factor. This paper costs noticeably more than generic 80mm thermal rolls that technically fit the TP200. You're paying for the guaranteed compatibility — which is worth it if you've had jams with cheaper paper, but it's a hard sell if your current generic rolls work fine.

**Who should buy this**

- **Restaurant owners who've had jams with generic paper** — the compatibility guarantee is worth the premium

- **Food trucks and pop-ups** where BPA-free paper matters for health inspection credibility

**Skip it if** your current paper works without issues, or if you're willing to test cheaper alternatives to save money. Also skip if you use a different POS printer — this is sized for the TP200 specifically.
