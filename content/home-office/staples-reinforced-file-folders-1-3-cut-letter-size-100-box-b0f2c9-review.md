The reinforcement runs along the entire top edge and wraps around the tab itself, which is the smart engineering here — that's where most folders tear when you're yanking them out of an overstuffed drawer.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these hanging folders or manila folders?**
A: Standard manila folders with 1/3-cut tabs. They go inside hanging folders or directly in a file drawer. Not hanging folders themselves.

**Q: Can I print directly on the tabs?**
A: The tabs are plain manila cardstock. You can write on them with pen or marker, or use adhesive labels. Printing directly on the tab requires feeding it through a printer, which is awkward — use labels instead.

**Q: How do these compare to the more expensive "heavyweight" folders?**
A: These are 11-point stock, which is solid for everyday use. True heavyweight folders run 14-point and cost noticeably more. For most offices, 11-point reinforced is the sweet spot — durable without the premium price.
