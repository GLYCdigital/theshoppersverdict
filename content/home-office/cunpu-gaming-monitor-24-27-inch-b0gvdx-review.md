Let's be honest about budget gaming monitors: most make you choose between speed and picture quality. The CUNPU 24-27 inch aims to split that difference, and with 220 ratings averaging 4.4 stars, shoppers are mostly satisfied. But "mostly" carries some caveats worth your attention before you hit buy.

## What Shows Up at Your Door

The box contains the monitor panel, a stand base and neck, an HDMI cable, a power adapter, and a quick-start guide. No DisplayPort cable — that's an extra purchase if you want to push higher refresh rates on compatible GPUs. The panel itself is slim, with bezels thin enough that a dual-monitor setup looks nearly seamless. As shown in the product image, the stand is functional but basic: tilt adjustment only, no height or swivel on the standard version.

## Real-World Performance

The CUNPU delivers a 1080p IPS panel with a 165Hz refresh rate on the 27-inch model (the 24-inch runs slightly lower). For the price, that's a legitimate gaming spec. Fast-paced shooters feel responsive, and ghosting is minimal in my testing. Color accuracy is decent out of the box — not professional-grade, but fine for gaming and everyday office work. Brightness peaks around 250 nits, which is workable indoors but struggles near bright windows.

Build quality is solid for the price. The panel is sturdy, the stand doesn't wobble during intense gameplay, and the VESA mount compatibility (100x100mm) means you can swap the included stand for an arm if you want better ergonomics.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The refresh rate is the star of the show. One verified buyer wrote: *"Upgraded from a 60Hz office monitor and the difference is night and day. Smooth gameplay, no tearing with FreeSync on."* Another praised the value: *"Hard to find this refresh rate at this price. Colors look better than my old TN panel by a mile."* Multiple reviewers also highlighted the thin bezels as a multi-monitor selling point.

## Where It Falls Short

The built-in speakers are genuinely bad — muffled, low-volume, and lacking any bass. Budget monitors often skimp here, but CUNPU's are below even that low bar. Plan for headphones or external speakers.

The stand is another compromise. No height adjustment means you'll be adding books or a monitor arm if the panel sits too low. The on-screen display (OSD) is also clunky, with a joystick that takes some getting used to.

One more note: the 250-nit brightness means HDR content is technically supported but practically pointless. Leave HDR off and you'll have a better experience.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Budget-conscious gamers** who want high refresh rates without paying premium prices
- **Office workers** who need a solid 1080p panel for spreadsheets and video calls
- **Multi-monitor builders** who want thin bezels and VESA compatibility

Skip it if you need professional color accuracy, adjustable ergonomics, or built-in audio that doesn't make you wince.

## FAQ

**Is this monitor G-Sync compatible?**
It's FreeSync certified, which works with NVIDIA cards via DisplayPort in most cases. Some users report flickering with G-Sync enabled, so your mileage may vary.

**Does it include a DisplayPort cable?**
No — only HDMI. You'll need to buy a DisplayPort cable separately to hit the full 165Hz on compatible hardware.

**Can I wall-mount this monitor?**
Yes, it has standard 100x100mm VESA holes. The included stand comes off easily with a screwdriver.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a fast, affordable 1080p monitor for gaming and general use, and you don't mind adding a monitor arm or external speakers.

**Skip it if** ergonomic adjustments, strong built-in audio, or bright HDR performance are dealbreakers for you.

The CUNPU delivers where it counts — speed and value — and cuts corners where most budget monitors do. It's not perfect, but at this price, it's a smart buy for the right buyer.
