## Where It Falls Short (Be Honest)

The sizing is the biggest complaint. A standard 8.5 x 11 sheet fits, but it's tight. The envelope is cut close to letter dimensions, so if you're storing documents with tabs, sticky notes, or anything that extends past the edge, you'll feel the squeeze. One reviewer noted, *"I was expecting a little more room. My documents go in flat, but I can't add a cardstock cover without it bunching."*

The material is also thinner than the product images suggest. It's not flimsy, but it's not heavy-duty either. A sharp staple or paperclip can puncture through if you press hard enough. And there's no expansion gusset — overstuffing will just break the zipper seal.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Will these fit standard A4 documents?**
A: Yes, but just barely. A4 is slightly taller than US Letter, and these envelopes are cut for US Letter. If you're storing A4 sheets, they'll fit but may curl at the edges. Measure your documents first.

**Q: Can you write on the envelopes with a permanent marker?**
A: Yes, but removal is tricky. Permanent marker won't come off cleanly — use a label or a dry-erase marker instead. Some buyers reported success with rubbing alcohol, but it's messy.

**Q: Are these safe for shipping?**
A: Not recommended. The zipper isn't a tamper-proof seal, and the plastic can tear if punctured in transit. Use these for internal organization, not mailing.
