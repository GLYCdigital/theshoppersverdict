The Mastten Premium TPU Stand Case for iPad 10.2 is the rare accessory that understands how people actually use a tablet in a workspace. It's not trying to be a laptop replacement or a folio with twenty useless angles. It's a protective shell with a kickstand that works, period. After 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, the consensus is clear: this case does its job without fuss.

## What you actually get

The package is straightforward: the case itself, molded from a soft TPU bumper fused to a rigid polycarbonate backplate. There's no screen protector included, so budget for one separately. The case weighs about 8 ounces — noticeable but acceptable for the protection level. It fits both the 2020 and 2019 iPad 10.2-inch models (7th and 8th generation), which is confirmed by the precise cutouts for the camera bump, volume buttons, and the Lightning port. The Apple Pencil (1st gen) slot is carved into the right edge, holding the stylus magnetically while still allowing pass-through charging.

## Build and real-world performance

The dual-layer construction is the star here. The TPU bumper wraps around all four corners with raised lips that sit about 2mm above the screen. Drop your iPad face-down and the screen won't touch the surface. The backplate is a hard polycarbonate that resists flexing — no creaking when you grip it tightly during a video call.

The kickstand deserves specific praise. It's a circular panel that rotates 360 degrees and locks into two detents: roughly 60° for typing and 75° for viewing. The hinge is stiff enough that it won't collapse when you tap the screen, but smooth enough to adjust with one hand. In landscape mode, it's stable on both a desk and a kitchen counter. In portrait, it's less stable — the narrow base makes it tippy if you're heavy-handed with the stylus.

## What buyers consistently praise

Reviewers repeatedly mention the case's durability. One buyer wrote, "Dropped my iPad from waist height onto tile — the case took the impact, no damage to the tablet." Another noted, "The kickstand is actually sturdy, unlike my last case that flopped over every time I typed." The fit precision also gets consistent shoutouts: "Every port and button lines up perfectly. No guessing."

## Where it falls short

The biggest omission is the lack of auto sleep/wake support. The front cover is just a protective flap, not a smart cover, so you'll press the power button manually each time you open or close it. Over a workday, that adds up to dozens of extra button presses.

The bulk is the second tradeoff. At roughly 15mm thick with the kickstand folded flat, it's chunky compared to slim folio cases. It won't slide easily into a tight laptop sleeve. And the TPU bumper collects lint and dust in pockets over time — cosmetic, but worth knowing.

## Who should buy this

This case is ideal for students, field workers, or anyone who carries their iPad between rooms and needs drop protection without a full rugged case. It's also great for tablet-based point-of-sale use — the kickstand holds steady on a counter, and the TPU grip prevents slips. Skip it if you primarily use your iPad on a lap, want auto sleep/wake, or need the thinnest possible profile.

## FAQ

**Does this case work with a screen protector?**
Yes, but only with thin glass or film protectors. The raised bezel leaves about 1mm of clearance, so thick tempered glass with rounded edges may lift the case corners.

**Will the kickstand break off over time?**
The rotating mechanism is a metal pin inside a reinforced polycarbonate ring. No widespread reports of failure in the 229 ratings, but it's the most stressed component — treat it with reasonable care.

**Can I charge the Apple Pencil with the case on?**
Yes. The Pencil slot has an opening at the bottom that aligns with the iPad's magnetic charging connector, so it charges while seated.
