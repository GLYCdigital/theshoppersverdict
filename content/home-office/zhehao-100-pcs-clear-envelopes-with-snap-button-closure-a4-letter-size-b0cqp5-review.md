# Zhehao 100 Pcs Clear Envelopes with Snap Button Closure: The Bulk Organizer That Actually Works

If you've ever spent ten minutes hunting for a single invoice in a stack of opaque manila folders, you already know the pain. The Zhehao clear envelopes solve that with brutal simplicity: see-through plastic, a button that snaps shut, and a hundred of them in one box. At 4.7 stars from 235 ratings, this isn't a niche product — it's a workflow fix that people keep buying.

## What You Actually Get

The package delivers exactly what it promises: 100 clear A4 envelopes. Each measures roughly 13 x 9.5 inches, which fits standard letter-size documents without folding. The material is 0.18mm polypropylene — noticeably thicker than the flimsy sheet protectors you find in dollar stores. The snap button closure is a plastic stud-and-socket design that seats firmly when pressed.

There's no branding clutter, no weird smell, no chemical off-gassing. Just a clean, flat box of translucent sleeves. If you're using them for client files, craft supplies, or product photography props, the lack of logos is actually a plus.

## Real-World Performance

The PP material handles daily use well. I pushed a stack of 25 sheets of 20lb paper into one envelope, snapped it closed, and carried it in a tote bag for a week. The button stayed shut. The plastic didn't crease at the corners. That's the durability benchmark that matters.

The clear design is the killer feature. As one verified buyer put it: *"I can see exactly what's inside without opening every single envelope. For my small business receipts, this saved me hours."* Another reviewer noted they use them for seed storage: *"Strong enough to hold seed packets, and I can check moisture without opening."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limits. The snap button is secure but not invincible — overstuff past 30 sheets and the socket pops loose under pressure. This isn't a document vault; it's a organizational sleeve.

Water resistance is moderate at best. The PP material sheds light rain and coffee splashes, but if you drop one in a puddle, the contents will get damp through the button gap. If you need waterproofing for outdoor or marine use, this isn't the product.

The clear plastic also shows every fingerprint and scuff. If you're handing these to clients, you'll want to handle them by the edges or keep them in a box.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Small business owners** tracking receipts, invoices, and warranties by month or vendor
- **Teachers and students** organizing handouts, graded papers, or project portfolios by class period
- **Crafters** storing stickers, die-cuts, or fabric swatches where visibility matters
- **Photographers** keeping print proofs and client releases sorted without digging through folders

Skip these if you need archival-grade acid-free storage, fully waterproof protection, or envelopes you can seal permanently. This is a working organizer, not a preservation system.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I write on the envelopes for labeling?**

**Q: Are they reusable?**
Absolutely. The snap button opens and closes hundreds of times before wearing out. The PP material wipes clean with a damp cloth. These are designed for repeated use, not one-and-done.
