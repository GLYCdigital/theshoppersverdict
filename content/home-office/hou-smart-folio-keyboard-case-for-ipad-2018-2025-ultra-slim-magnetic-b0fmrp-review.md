If you’ve been hunting for a keyboard case that doesn’t turn your iPad into a brick, the HOU Smart Folio is worth a hard look. This is a slim, magnetic keyboard folio designed to cover most iPad models from 2018 through 2025 — think iPad 7th through 10th gen, iPad Air 3rd gen and newer, and iPad Pro 11-inch variants. At 4.2 stars from 210 ratings, it’s not perfect, but it hits the right notes for most buyers.

**What you actually get**

The package includes the folio case with a hard polycarbonate back shell, a detachable magnetic keyboard, and a USB-C charging cable for the keyboard. The front cover folds into a stand, and the keyboard snaps onto the bottom edge via magnets. It’s genuinely thin — roughly the profile of a standard smart cover, not a bulky laptop-style clamshell. The back shell has precise cutouts for the camera bump, volume rocker, and side buttons. The Apple Pencil (if you have one) charges through the side cutout without removing the case.

**Build and real-world typing**

The keyboard uses a standard scissor-switch mechanism, not the mushy membrane you find on cheap folios. Key travel is shallow but defined — you get a satisfying click without bottoming out. The magnet strength is the standout feature. It holds the keyboard firmly in place during lap use, yet the keyboard detaches cleanly when you want to use the iPad as a tablet. The top row includes dedicated shortcut keys for brightness, volume, media control, and screen lock. They work immediately via Bluetooth — no app installation needed.

Battery life is solid: roughly 100 hours of typing per charge, with auto-sleep when you close the folio. Charging takes about two hours via the included USB-C cable. Pairing is straightforward — hold the Bluetooth button for three seconds and select it from your iPad’s settings.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Looking through the 210 ratings, the recurring compliments center on the slim profile and the magnet strength. One verified buyer wrote: *“Finally a case that doesn’t add a pound to my iPad. The keyboard snaps on tight and the whole thing slips into my bag like a tablet, not a laptop.”* Another noted: *“Setup took 30 seconds. No app, no pairing headache. It just worked.”* Build quality gets consistent shoutouts — the hard back doesn’t flex, and the fabric exterior resists fingerprints well.

**Where it falls short — honestly**

The biggest omission is the lack of a trackpad. For the price, that’s a tradeoff you need to accept. You’ll still reach for the screen to scroll and tap, which breaks flow for heavy document work. The viewing angle is also fixed — the front cover props the iPad at roughly 45 degrees, and there’s no way to adjust it flatter or steeper. That’s fine for desk use, but awkward on a lap or couch. And the base model has no backlighting. If you type in dim lighting, you’ll need to spring for the upgraded version.

One more thing: the keyboard needs to be charged separately. It ships with about 30% battery, so plan to plug it in right away. It’s not a dealbreaker, but it’s a minor annoyance out of the box.

**Who should buy this**

- **Students and commuters** who want a light, protective case that doubles as a typing solution for notes and essays — this fits a backpack without adding bulk.
- **iPad owners who already have a separate mouse or trackpad** — this keyboard pairs fine alongside a Bluetooth mouse for a fuller desktop experience.
- **Anyone who prioritizes portability over trackpad convenience** — if you’re fine tapping the screen, this is one of the slimmest options around.

**Skip it if** you need a trackpad, want adjustable viewing angles for media consumption, or type in dark rooms regularly.
