**The $15 tape dispenser that actually stays put — mostly**

Most desktop tape dispensers are junk. They slide across your desk the moment you pull, they only fit one specific tape width, and the blade dulls after a week. The Lichamp Desktop Tape Dispenser Holder with Large 3 inch Core doesn't solve every problem, but it solves the big ones — and for under $20, that's worth talking about.

## What you actually get

The box contains the dispenser base, the spindle arm, and the metal cutting blade assembly. No screws, no adhesive pads, no instructions needed — it's assembled out of the box. The base measures roughly 6 inches long and 4 inches deep, and it's got a rubberized bottom layer that does most of the heavy lifting for stability.

The headline feature is the 3-inch core compatibility. That's the larger hole in the middle of heavy-duty tape rolls — the kind you get with professional-grade masking tape, duct tape, or packaging tape. Standard 1-inch and 1.5-inch core rolls fit too, so you're not locked into one tape type.

## How it performs in real use

I loaded it with a 2-inch wide roll of 3M masking tape (the orange contractor stuff) and put it through a week of packing, labeling, and general shop use. The first thing you notice is the weight. The base is solid enough that a firm one-handed pull doesn't move it — the rubber grip does its job on a wooden desk and a painted workbench.

The blade is the real differentiator. It's a serrated metal edge that cuts cleanly across masking tape and kraft paper tape without leaving a jagged mess. I've used cheap dispensers where the blade catches and rips the tape sideways; this one doesn't. The cutting action is quick — press down, pull slightly, and it's done.

One detail that impressed me: the spindle is long enough to hold a full 2-inch wide roll without the tape rubbing against the base or the blade housing. On narrower dispensers, wide tape drags and frays. Not here.

## Where it falls short

Be honest with yourself about what tape you're using. The Lichamp is designed for rolls up to 2 inches wide. If you regularly use 2.5-inch or 3-inch wide tape — common for heavy-duty sealing or crafting — this dispenser will frustrate you. The spindle is too short, and the blade can't span the full width, leaving you to tear manually.

The cutting teeth are also aggressive. On thin, slick tapes like clear cellophane or cheap gift wrap tape, pulling at an angle can cause the tape to tear mid-strip instead of cutting cleanly. You need to pull straight down, not at an angle. It's a minor learning curve, not a dealbreaker.

And there's no adhesive backing. The rubber base is good, but on glossy surfaces or slightly uneven wood, it can shift. If your desk is glass or polished metal, you might find yourself holding the base with one hand while pulling with the other.

## Who should buy this

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit Scotch brand 3-inch core tape rolls?**
Yes. The spindle accepts any 3-inch core roll up to 2 inches wide. Scotch, 3M, Duck — they all fit snugly without wobble.

**Q: Can I use it for double-sided tape?**
Technically yes, but the aggressive blade can shred thin double-sided tape. Use a lighter touch and pull straight down. It's not ideal for that use case.

**Q: Does it come with extra blades?**
No. The blade is fixed and non-replaceable. It's sharp enough for years of normal use, but once it dulls, you'll need a new dispenser.
