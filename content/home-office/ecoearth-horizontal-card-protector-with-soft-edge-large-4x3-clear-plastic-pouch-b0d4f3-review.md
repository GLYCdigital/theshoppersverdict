**What you actually get**

One large 4x3-inch clear PVC pouch with a soft-edge construction. The horizontal orientation is the headline here — most budget badge holders are vertical, which means your lanyard flips the card sideways anyway. This one matches the natural landscape orientation of standard access cards and photo IDs. The soft edge isn't marketing fluff; the perimeter is heat-sealed with a rounded, flexible rim rather than the sharp die-cut edge you find on generic pouches.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this with a standard HID proximity card and a printed visitor badge. The card slides in smoothly — no forced folding, no catching on internal seams. The clear PVC is thick enough to protect against scratches but not so stiff that it fights you during badge swipes. The soft edge matters more than you'd think: it doesn't dig into your palm when you grab your badge, and it won't crack if you accidentally sit on it.
