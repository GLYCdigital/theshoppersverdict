If you're shopping for a budget office monitor, you've probably waded through dozens of options that all blur together. The HP E22 G5 stands apart — not because it does anything flashy, but because it nails the fundamentals. With a perfect 5.0-star average across 250 ratings, this 22-inch IPS display has quietly become a favorite for home offices and corporate cubicles alike. Here's the real story after digging into the specs and what buyers actually say.

## What's in the Box

You get the monitor, an adjustable stand that snaps in without tools, a VGA cable, a power cord, and a quick-start guide. That's it. No HDMI cable included — a minor annoyance, but worth knowing before it arrives. The stand itself is the headline feature here: full height adjustment, tilt, swivel, and even a 90-degree pivot to vertical orientation. That's functionality typically reserved for monitors costing twice as much.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The E22 G5 uses an IPS panel with a 1920x1080 resolution at 60Hz. Color accuracy is solid out of the box — roughly 99% sRGB coverage — and the matte anti-glare coating handles bright offices without annoying reflections. The 250-nit brightness is adequate for indoor use, though it's not going to compete with a sunny window directly behind you.

The bezels are thin on three sides, which makes dual-monitor setups feel seamless. One buyer noted, "I bought two for my WFH setup and the color match between them is nearly identical — no fiddling with calibration." That consistency is a genuine plus, as mismatched panels are a common complaint even on pricier displays.

The stand is the star. It's sturdy with no wobble, and the height range is generous — roughly 4 inches of travel. For anyone with neck or back pain from craning at a fixed-height monitor, this alone justifies the purchase.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 250 ratings, a few themes dominate:

- **Plug-and-play simplicity**: Multiple reviewers mention zero driver issues, including Mac users. "Connected to my M1 MacBook with a USB-C to HDMI adapter and it just worked," reads one typical review.
- **Text clarity**: For spreadsheet and document work, the 22-inch size at 1080p gives comfortable pixel density — not too small, not chunky.
- **Reliability**: Several reviewers mention using these in office deployments with dozens of units over years without a single failure.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The built-in speakers are genuinely bad — 2W per channel with tinny output. Plan on external speakers or a headset.

Input selection is limited to HDMI and VGA. No DisplayPort, no USB-C, no USB hub. If you're on a modern ultrabook with only USB-C, you'll need an adapter. VGA is a legacy inclusion that feels dated, but it does keep older office PCs compatible.

The 60Hz refresh rate and lack of adaptive sync mean this isn't a gaming monitor. Fast-paced titles will show slight ghosting. This is a work tool, not a plaything.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Home office workers** who sit at a desk 8+ hours daily and need ergonomic adjustability without spending $300+
- **IT managers** outfitting cubicles or call centers where reliability and consistent color matter more than specs
- **Dual-monitor users** who want a matched pair without breaking the bank

Skip it if you're a gamer, a video editor needing 4K or HDR, or someone who wants a single USB-C cable for power and display.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the HP E22 G5 work with Mac?**
A: Yes, via HDMI or VGA with an adapter. Several Mac users report no issues with text clarity or scaling.

**Q: Is the stand really that good?**
A: Yes — full height, tilt, swivel, and pivot. This is a $100+ stand included with a budget monitor. It's the best feature here.

**Q: Can I mount it on a VESA arm?**
A: Yes, it has standard 100x100mm VESA holes. The included stand detaches easily.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a dependable, ergonomic workhorse for spreadsheets, documents, and web work — especially for dual-monitor or multi-unit office setups.

**Skip it if** you need USB-C connectivity, decent speakers, or any gaming capability.

The HP E22 G5 isn't exciting. It's better than that — it's trustworthy. For the price, you're getting an adjustable IPS panel with proven reliability and a 5.0-star track record. That's a rare combination in the budget monitor market.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — loses half a star for the weak speakers and dated ports, but earns its place as a top-tier office essential.
