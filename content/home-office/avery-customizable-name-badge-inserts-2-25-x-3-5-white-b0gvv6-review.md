**Are you still printing name badges on flimsy paper that jams your printer, or worse, smudges the moment someone sweats?** Avery's Customizable Name Badge Inserts solve both problems — but they're not the beefiest cardstock on the shelf. After testing a full 100-pack, here's the honest breakdown.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get 100 white inserts, each measuring exactly 2.25" x 3.5" — the industry-standard size that fits virtually every vertical badge holder sold on Amazon. The sheets are perforated with eight inserts per 8.5" x 11" page. Avery includes access to their Design & Print Online software, which is where the real value hides: dozens of free templates for conference badges, visitor passes, and employee ID cards. No need to fiddle with Word tables or guess margins.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The paper weight is 60-lb bond — noticeably lighter than the 80-lb cardstock premium brands use. In a badge holder, that's fine. The plastic pocket provides rigidity. But if you're handing these out at a busy expo and someone yanks the card out to write a note on the back, it will crease. I'd call this a minor tradeoff for the price point, not a dealbreaker.

Printing is where these shine. The perforation lines are cleanly cut — I ran three sheets through a Brother laser printer and two through an Epson inkjet. Zero jams, zero paper curl, and the ink dried instantly without bleeding into the white border. The perforations tear apart with a satisfying snap, leaving no fuzzy edges that look unprofessional up close.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The 4.8-star rating across 240 reviews isn't inflated. The most common theme is reliability. One reviewer noted: *"I've bought cheaper no-name inserts before and half of them would rip along the perforation. These came out perfect every single time — all 100."* Another mentioned the template compatibility: *"The Avery software recognized my printer automatically. I had 50 badges done in 10 minutes."*

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest gripes. First, the 60-lb stock. If you need badges that survive a week-long conference where attendees swap them in and out of lanyards constantly, spend a few extra dollars on heavier cardstock. Second, these are strictly vertical-format. If your badge holder loads from the top (horizontal orientation), you'll need to trim the cards yourself — a fiddly process that wastes time.

Also note: the white finish is plain matte. No glossy coating for extra durability. Smudging isn't an issue with laser or pigment-based ink, but water-based inkjet prints will smear if they get wet. Keep a pen away from these after printing.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Event planners** managing conferences or trade shows who need 100+ badges quickly with zero waste
- **Small business owners** printing employee name tags in-house without a dedicated badge printer
- **Teachers or volunteers** making visitor passes for school events — the perforation reliability saves real headaches

Skip them if you need heavy-duty cards for daily use in a high-traffic environment, or if you only print a dozen badges a year — you'll pay for the pack's bulk before you use it.

## FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Will these work in my HP or Canon inkjet?**  
A: Yes, both inkjet and laser printers handle the 60-lb stock without issues. Just set your printer to "cardstock" or "heavy paper" mode to avoid ink pooling.

**Q: Can I print double-sided?**  
A: Technically yes, but the paper is thin enough that text will show through. Stick to single-sided printing for a clean look.

**Q: Do I need Avery's software, or can I use Canva?**  
A: Use whatever you like. The inserts are standard US Letter size with perforations at exact 2.25" x 3.5" increments, so any design software with custom dimensions works. Avery's free software just makes it easier for beginners.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a no-fuss, jam-free badge solution for events or offices, and you don't mind the lighter paper weight when protected by a plastic holder. **Skip it if** you need industrial-strength cards for daily use or prefer heavier cardstock for a premium feel.

For the price per badge and the reliability, this is a solid 4.6/5. The perforation quality alone justifies the purchase over cheaper alternatives. Just pair it with decent badge holders and you're set.
