**Real-world performance**

I tested these in an OfficeJet 6700. Black text output is crisp with good density — honestly indistinguishable from HP OEM for standard document printing. For a budget cartridge, the black ink handles bold fonts and small text (8pt and below) without smudging or feathering on standard copy paper.
