If you're reading this at 3 PM with a crick in your neck, you already know the problem: your monitor sits too low. The standard fix—stacking books—looks terrible and collapses at the worst moment. ## What You Actually Get

The box contains the tempered glass top plate, steel legs, four pole sections, and the necessary screws. Assembly means attaching the legs to the poles and securing the top plate—expect about 5 to 10 minutes with the included hex wrench. The finished stand measures roughly 20 inches wide and 9.5 inches deep, giving you a stable platform for monitors up to 27 inches. The surface also fits a laptop alongside your display, which is a nice bonus for dual-screen setups.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The steel frame is the standout feature here. Rated for 33 pounds, it handles a heavy 27-inch monitor plus a 15-inch laptop without flexing. The tempered glass top is a practical choice—it's scratch-resistant, and a quick wipe removes dust and coffee rings. The non-slip rubber pads on the base and top keep everything firmly in place, even if you bump the desk while typing.

The four height settings are where this product earns its keep. You can adjust from 2.6 inches up to 5.1 inches, which means you can match the stand to your chair height and desk setup. For reference, most people need the top of their monitor at or slightly below eye level—this stand covers that range well for standard office chairs and desks.

## Where It Falls Short

The assembly requirement is the most common complaint. Unlike some competitors that snap together in seconds, the HUANUO needs screwdriver work. One reviewer puts it bluntly: "Took me 15 minutes to put together. Not hard, but I wasn't expecting to need tools." It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

The base is also narrower than the top plate. If you load the front edge with heavy peripherals and the back with nothing, the stand can feel slightly front-heavy. Keep heavier items centered and you'll never notice it. For most setups, this is a non-issue.

## Who Should Buy This

This is the right stand for three types of people: remote workers stuck on a laptop who need eye-level relief, anyone with a monitor that lacks built-in height adjustment, and home office users who want a clean glass surface that doesn't look like dorm furniture.
