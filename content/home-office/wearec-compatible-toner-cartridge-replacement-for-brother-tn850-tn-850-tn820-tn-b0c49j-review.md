## Where It Falls Short

The most common complaint involves the toner sensor. A handful of buyers report the printer flashing a "toner low" warning when the cartridge is still 20-30% full. It doesn't stop printing, but it's annoying if you rely on those warnings to time your reorders. One user summed it up: "The warning came on way too early, but it kept printing fine for another couple hundred pages."

The second issue is packaging. The three-pack ships with the cartridges in a single box without individual protective sleeves. A few reviewers received units with cracked drum covers or loose seals — likely shipping damage rather than manufacturing defects. If you're ordering the multi-pack, inspect each cartridge before opening the seals.
