If you've ever cursed a ten-key-less laptop while wrestling a spreadsheet, you already know the pain. The Nulea Wireless Number Pad aims to fix that with a dual-mode connection and a form factor that slips into a laptop bag like it belongs there. After digging through the specs, user experiences, and the 4.6-star aggregate across 229 ratings, here's what actually matters before you click buy.

**What's in the box**

The Nulea ships with the numeric keypad itself, a 2.4G USB receiver tucked into a magnetic storage slot on the underside, a USB-C charging cable, and a basic instruction sheet. No batteries to hunt down — the built-in 300mAh cell handles power. The keypad measures roughly 5.5 x 3.9 inches and weighs about 3.2 ounces, which makes it light enough to forget it's in your bag.

**Performance and build quality**

The star feature is the dual connectivity. Bluetooth 5.0 handles wireless duty with solid range, while the 2.4G dongle offers a low-latency fallback. You can pair up to three devices and toggle between them with a dedicated key — handy if you bounce between a desktop, laptop, and tablet. In practice, switching takes about two seconds, and the connection holds steady without noticeable input lag.

The keys use scissor-switch mechanics with roughly 1.5mm of travel. That's comparable to most ultrabook keyboards, so if you're used to typing on a MacBook or Dell XPS, this will feel familiar rather than foreign. The layout is full-size, including a double-height Enter key and a 00 key that spreadsheet jockeys will appreciate. The top row adds shortcuts for calculator, media volume, and mute.

Battery life is the quiet win here. Multiple users report weeks of daily use between charges, which aligns with the advertised 90-hour figure. The USB-C charging port is a welcome modern touch — no proprietary cables to lose.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the ratings, three themes dominate. First is compatibility: "Works flawlessly with my MacBook Air and Windows desktop — I just press the button to switch," one reviewer notes. Second is the quiet key action, with another user stating, "The keys are soft and silent; I can enter numbers during conference calls without anyone hearing." Third is the build quality relative to price — several buyers mention it feels more premium than other sub-$30 number pads they've owned.

**Where it falls short**

No backlighting is the biggest omission. If you work in dim environments or frequently pull late-night data entry sessions, you'll be hunting for keys by feel or adding a desk lamp. Several reviewers also note the scissor-switch feel gets slightly less crisp toward the edges of wide keys — the Enter key especially. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's noticeable if you're a heavy Enter-key user. Finally, the 2.4G dongle is small and easy to misplace, so use the magnetic slot when it's not plugged in.

**Who should buy this**

- **Spreadsheet professionals** — accountants, analysts, bookkeepers who live in Excel or Google Sheets
- **Laptop users with no number row** — anyone on a 13-inch or 14-inch ultrabook misses these keys more than they think
- **Multi-device workers** — the three-device switching makes this a single tool for a desktop-laptop-tablet workflow

**Who should skip it**

- **Gamers needing macro keys** — this has no programmability or extra buttons
- **Night workers** — the lack of backlighting is a genuine frustration in dark rooms
- **Anyone wanting a mechanical feel** — scissor switches are quiet and responsive, but they're not clicky

**FAQ**

*Does it work with iPad or Android tablets?*  
Yes, via Bluetooth. It pairs as a standard HID keyboard, so it works with iPadOS and Android without extra apps.

*Can I use it while charging?*  
Yes, the USB-C port supports data and power simultaneously, so you can keep working while it tops up.

*How do I switch between the 2.4G dongle and Bluetooth?*  
There's a physical toggle on the side. Slide it to the appropriate position and the keypad connects to whichever mode you selected.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a dependable, portable number pad for productivity work and value dual-mode flexibility over flashy features.

**Skip it if** you require backlit keys or want a heavy, mechanical typing experience.

The Nulea Wireless Number Pad earns a 4.6/5 for nailing the fundamentals: reliable connectivity, quiet keys, and genuine portability. It's not the flashiest option, but for getting numbers into a spreadsheet without friction, it gets the job done — and keeps doing it for weeks on a single charge.
