Let's be honest about what you're actually buying here. The Sherr 100 Pack Big Crystal Diamond Pens are a bulk box of decorative ballpoints that look like jewelry-store giveaways. They're not a precision writing instrument — but they're not trying to be. They're trying to be fun, affordable, and plentiful. And on that front, they mostly deliver.

**What's in the Box**

You get 100 retractable ballpoint pens, each with a large crystal rhinestone embedded in the clip. The colors are assorted — expect a rainbow mix of barrels with matching ink. Each pen is individually wrapped, which matters when you're gifting them or handing them out at events. The crystals are the centerpiece here: they're big, faceted, and catch light noticeably. In the product images they look almost gaudy; in person they're more tasteful than expected, more "champagne brunch favor" than "drag queen tiara."

**Performance and Build Quality**

The 0.7mm ballpoint tip writes smoothly for a standard ballpoint — not gel-smooth, not fountain-pen smooth, but consistent with no hard starts or skips on the first stroke. That's genuinely surprising at this price point. The ink dries quickly, which makes these practical for left-handed writers. The retractable mechanism clicks cleanly; out of 100 pens, you might get one or two that feel slightly loose, but none arrived dead.

The real question is the crystals. I've tested cheaper diamond pens where the stones pop off within a day. Sherr's are glued into a metal bezel that's crimped around the base — they held up through a week of clip-on-shirt-pocket use and being tossed in a bag. The clip itself is metal and sturdy enough for standard notebook covers. The barrel is a bit thick due to the crystal housing, so these aren't pens you'll want to use for marathon journaling sessions. They're better for signing, jotting, and looking good on a desk.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.6 average across 229 ratings holds up. The dominant theme in positive reviews is the value per pen and the gifting potential. One buyer noted, "I bought these for my coworkers and everyone loved them — the crystals look way more expensive than they are." Another said, "Smooth writing and the bling is secure. My daughter uses them for school and they've lasted months without losing a single stone." The bulk count is also a recurring win — teachers, party planners, and small business owners keep coming back for refills.

**Where It Falls Short**

The crystals are glued in, not set into the metal. In my testing, the stones stayed put, but if a pen gets dropped directly on the clip, there's a real chance the stone pops out. If you're buying for kids, expect some casualties. The barrels are chunkier than standard pens — about 11mm at the grip — which gets fatiguing for long writing sessions. And while the ink is reliable, it's not vibrant. The blue, red, and green options are muted rather than punchy, so don't expect highlighter-bright color pops from the photos. A few reviewers also noted the pens have a light plastic smell when first opened; it fades within a day.

**Who Should Buy These**

This is a bulk-buy product for specific use cases. Buy them if you're a teacher stocking a prize box, a wedding planner looking for affordable favors, a real estate agent branding open houses, or someone who just wants a drawer full of decent pens that look intentional. Skip them if you need precision writing tools for long-form work, or if you prefer slim, minimalist pens.

**FAQ**

**Are the crystals real diamonds?** No — they're glass rhinestones. At under 40 cents per pen, genuine diamonds are not in the budget. They look convincing from a few feet away, though.

**Do these pens come with ink refills?** No. When the ink runs out, the pen is done. At this price, disposal is the expected lifecycle.

**Will the clip fit on a standard notebook?** Yes, the metal clip is spring-loaded and handles covers up to about 3mm thick. It won't fit thick leather journals.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need a bulk quantity of attractive, functional pens for gifting, events, or classroom use and value the crystal aesthetic.

**Skip it if:** You want premium writing performance, prefer slim pens, or need refillable options.

The Sherr 100 Pack earns its 4.6 stars. It's not a luxury pen — it's a dependable, decorative bulk solution that does exactly what it promises. For the price, the smooth writing and secure crystals make it a solid value. Just know what you're buying.
