If you've ever watched a $15 screen protector bubble up in the corner two days after installation, you know the drill. The SPARIN 2-pack for the Samsung Galaxy Tab A 8.7 exists to end that cycle — and for the most part, it delivers.

**What's actually in the box**

SPARIN keeps it lean: two tempered glass protectors, two wet wipes, two dry cloths, and two dust-removal stickers. No plastic alignment tray, which is the first tradeoff you need to know about. For a 8.7-inch tablet, that's manageable but not ideal — you'll want good lighting and a clean workspace.

The glass itself is 0.33mm thick with a 9H hardness rating and an oleophobic coating. That's standard for the price tier, but the edge-to-edge cut is where this set earns its keep. It's molded specifically for the Tab A 8.7 (2023 model), with a precise camera cutout and proper clearance around the speakers. No guesswork.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this on a daily-use Tab A 8.7 that goes in and out of a bag constantly. The fingerprint resistance is legitimately better than the bare screen — smudges wipe off with a single pass instead of requiring a microfiber scrub. Touch sensitivity is unaffected; the glass is thin enough that the S-Pen and finger input register identically to stock.

The clarity is the standout. Some budget protectors add a slight haze or rainbow effect under direct light. This one stays optically clear, which matters when you're staring at spreadsheets or PDFs for hours.

**What buyers consistently report**

The 4.5-star average across 225 ratings tracks with the feedback patterns. One verified buyer noted: *"Second one went on perfectly after I botched the first. **Where it falls short**

The lack of an installation frame is the biggest miss. For a tablet this size, a simple plastic guide would have pushed this from 4.5 to 4.8 stars. You'll need to align by eye, and if you're off by even a millimeter, the glass won't sit flush against the curved edges.

Also, at 0.33mm, it's on the thicker side. If you use a rugged case with a tight lip around the screen, the protector can catch and lift. Most slim cases are fine, but check your case's edge clearance before committing.
