**FAQ**

**Q: Are these cards compatible with the Raspberry Pi?**
A: Yes, for Pi models that accept full-size SD cards (Pi 1, 2, and 3). The 16GB and 32GB versions work well for lightweight Linux distros like RetroPie. Newer Pi models need microSD, so check your board first.

**Q: Can I use these cards in a Nintendo Switch?**
A: No. The Switch only accepts microSD cards, not full-size SD. These are strictly for cameras, printers, and older laptops with SD slots.

**Q: What's the actual write speed?**
A: The Class 10 rating guarantees 10 MB/s minimum, but real-world testing typically shows 15–20 MB/s on the 16GB and 32GB cards. That's enough for 1080p video but will buffer if you try 4K.
