Most budget notebooks cheat on paper. Not these. A detailed US reviewer put it plainly: "Right away I noticed how solid the paper quality is. That last point matters more than it sounds. Most notebooks under $5 claim double-sided usability. Most lie. The Oxford paper is genuinely thick enough for both sides — gel pens included — without the ghosting that makes the reverse side look like a palimpsest.

## Designed for the Backpack Life

The 8 x 10-1/2 inch size is compact enough to fit in a standard backpack pocket but large enough for real note-taking. The spiral binding is the thing that usually breaks first on cheap notebooks. Oxford's holds. "Pages turn easily and lie flat when open," one student noted. The binding doesn't snag on other books or bag contents.

The micro-perforated pages tear cleanly — not that ragged edge you get with cheaper perforation. And they're already 3-hole punched. When exam season hits and you need to reorganize, you can tear pages out and drop them straight into a binder without re-punching.
