**Where it falls short**

Here's the honest tradeoff. There's no E-Marker chip in these cables. That means data transfer caps at USB 2.0 speeds — 480Mbps. If you're using this for file transfers between a laptop and external SSD, you'll be waiting. It's a charging-first cable. Also, the nylon braid is stiffer than silicone or fabric cables. It's not uncomfortable, but if you need to route cables through tight cable management trays or bend them at sharp angles, you'll feel the resistance. The stiffness is the price of durability.
