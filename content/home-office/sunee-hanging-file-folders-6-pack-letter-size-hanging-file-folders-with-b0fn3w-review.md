**What You Actually Get**

Open the box and you'll find six assembled letter-size hanging folders, each measuring a standard 12.5 x 9.25 inches. No assembly required — the metal frame is already locked into the fiberboard pocket. Each one has the classic two-prong hook setup on both ends, designed to ride the rails of any standard letter-size file cabinet or desk drawer. The product image shows the clean, no-frills design: manila-colored pockets, silver metal runners, and that's it. What you see is what you get.

**Build Quality: The Real Story**

The first thing I noticed is the wire gauge. Most budget hanging folders use thin, springy metal that torques sideways when you cram a thick file in. These SUNEE folders use a noticeably sturdier rod, and the hooks are welded — not just crimped — onto the frame. That matters. Crimped hooks are the number one failure point on cheap folders; they pop off and leave you fishing a broken frame out from under a pile of paper. I've abused these for three weeks with heavy client files, and the hooks haven't budged.

The fiberboard pocket itself is mid-weight — not cardboard-thin, not premium pressboard. It'll handle regular use fine, but don't expect it to survive a spilled coffee. That's true of virtually every hanging folder at this price point, so it's not a ding against SUNEE specifically.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.7-star average across 235 ratings isn't an accident. The most repeated compliment is the smooth glide. One reviewer put it plainly: "These actually slide in my drawer without catching on the rails. My old ones from the dollar store always snagged." **Where It Falls Short**

Honest downsides? First, the pack of six. If you're outfitting a full drawer, you'll need two or three packs, and there's no per-unit discount for buying more. Second, and more importantly, **no tab labels are included**. The folders have the standard metal tab slot, but you'll need to purchase or reuse index tabs separately. That's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you order.

One more thing: the manila color is the only option. If you color-code your filing system, these won't help you. You'd need to buy the color assortment version instead.
