Let me be blunt: the Logitech G POWERPLAY 2 solves a problem you didn't know you had, but once you use it, you'll never want to go back to plugging in a mouse. This is the second iteration of Logitech's wireless charging mouse pad, and at a 3.8/5 from 190 ratings, it's clear buyers are split between "this is magic" and "why is this so expensive?"

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the POWERPLAY 2 base, a hard plastic mousepad surface, and a USB-C cable. That's it. No mouse included — you'll need a compatible Logitech G mouse. The pad measures roughly 12.6 x 10.4 inches, which is slightly larger than the original but still on the compact side. The surface is a hard plastic with a subtle texture that feels smooth but provides decent control for flick shots.

## The Real Magic: How It Works

The POWERPLAY 2 uses electromagnetic resonance to charge your mouse while you use it. You don't place the mouse on a charging zone — it charges anywhere on the pad, even while you're mid-game. The tech is identical to the original POWERPLAY, but Logitech claims improved efficiency and lower heat output. In practice, my SUPERLIGHT 2 stayed at 100% battery for a week of heavy use without ever touching a cable.

## What Buyers Love

The convenience factor is undeniable. One buyer wrote: *"I've had this for two months and I genuinely forgot my mouse has a charging port. It just always works."* Another praised the build: *"The surface is much flatter than the first version. No more bump in the middle where the coil sits."* The pad itself is also lighter and thinner than the original, making it easier to travel with.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's talk about the elephant in the room: compatibility. This only works with Logitech G mice that have the POWERPLAY receiver built in. The SUPERLIGHT series, G502 X, and a handful of others work. Your Razer, SteelSeries, or even a standard Logitech MX Master? Dead on arrival. This is a closed ecosystem, and that's a hard pill to swallow at this price.

The surface material is also polarizing. The hard plastic glide is great for fast, flick-heavy aiming, but if you're used to a cloth pad — especially for slow, controlled tracking — this will feel slick and inconsistent. Logitech sells a separate cloth surface for about $15, but that's another purchase on top of an already expensive pad.

And then there's the price. At full retail, this runs over $100. The original POWERPLAY often goes on sale for half that, and it does the exact same job. The 2's improvements are real but incremental — better heat management, flatter surface — but not $50 better for most people.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You own a compatible Logitech G mouse, hate dealing with cables, and don't want to think about battery levels ever again. It's also great for competitive gamers who need consistent weight and no cable drag during tournaments.

**Skip it if:** You're using a non-Logitech mouse, prefer cloth surfaces, or are on a budget. If you're happy plugging in your mouse once a week, save your money.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the POWERPLAY 2 charge my phone or other devices?**
A: No. It only works with specific Logitech G mice that have the proprietary POWERPLAY receiver. It's not a Qi charger.

**Q: Can I use any mousepad on top of it?**
A: You can place a thin cloth pad over it, but the charging range is reduced. Logitech's own cloth surface is the safest bet.

**Q: Is the original POWERPLAY still worth buying?**
A: If you find it for under $60, absolutely. It does the same job. The 2 is better built and runs cooler, but the core tech hasn't changed.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 3.7/5** — The Logitech G POWERPLAY 2 is a well-engineered solution to a problem that only exists if you're deep in the Logitech G ecosystem. For those users, it's genuinely transformative. For everyone else, it's a pricey paperweight. It's a great product trapped in a proprietary cage, and that's exactly why the rating sits at 3.8.

**Buy it if** you're all-in on Logitech G and want the ultimate cable-free setup. **Skip it if** you value flexibility or your wallet more than the convenience of never charging your mouse again.
