The "Xtra-Smooth" claim holds up. Reviewers consistently praise the 0.7 mm lead for gliding across paper without catching or scratching. At $53.02 for 40 pencils, you're paying about $1.33 per pencil — which is more than a standard BIC pack, but the build quality justifies the bump. For teachers stocking a classroom or parents equipping multiple kids, this is a buy-once-and-forget-it solution.

**Who should buy it:** Students, teachers, home office workers who spend all day writing. Anyone tired of pencils that snap mid-sentence.

**Who should skip it:** People who prefer wooden pencils. Budget shoppers who just need the cheapest option. Those who need 0.5 mm or 0.9 mm lead.
