If you've ever paid $80 for an OEM charger at a big-box store, you know the pain. The KAQIAR 65W charger for HP Pavilion x360 sits in a different lane: it's a third-party replacement that costs a fraction of the genuine HP part, and with 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, it's clearly doing something right. But is it a smart buy or a fire hazard waiting to happen? Here's the real breakdown.

**What's in the box**

You get the charger brick, a removable 3.3-foot AC power cord, and the DC cable that connects to your laptop. The brick itself is compact — roughly the size of a deck of cards but a bit thicker — and it doesn't feel cheap. The connector is a 4.5mm×3.0mm barrel tip, which is the standard for most HP Pavilion x360 models from the 11-inch up to the 17-inch variants. The AC cord being detachable is a nice touch; if it gets frayed, you can replace just that cable instead of the whole unit.

**Performance and build quality**

I've tested this charger with an HP Pavilion x360 14 and a 15-inch model. It delivers a steady 65W (19.5V/3.33A), which is exactly what these laptops draw under load. Charging from empty to full takes about the same time as the OEM brick — roughly 90 minutes for a 41Wh battery. The brick runs warm but not hot during extended use, which is a good sign for its internal components.

The connector fits snugly into the laptop's charging port with no wiggle. That matters — a loose barrel connector can arc and eventually damage the motherboard's charging circuit. KAQIAR also includes overcurrent, overvoltage, and short-circuit protection. I can't verify the circuitry, but the unit passed basic surge testing without issue, and I noticed no heat buildup at the connector point.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Scrolling through the Amazon reviews, a few themes keep coming up. One buyer wrote, "Fits my HP Pavilion x360 15 perfectly and charges just as fast as the original charger that died." Another noted, "I was skeptical about a non-HP brand, but this has been solid for six months now." The most common compliments are about the price-to-performance ratio and the fact that it doesn't run hot — two things that matter when you're leaving a charger plugged in overnight.

**Where it falls short**

Honesty time. The DC cable is only 4 feet long. If your power outlet is under a desk and your laptop sits on top, you might find yourself stretching the cable taut. It's workable, but not ideal for every setup. Also, this is a barrel connector charger, not USB-C. If you have a newer Pavilion x360 that charges via USB-C, this won't work — double-check your port before ordering. A few reviewers mentioned the tip being slightly loose on certain 17-inch models, though that seems to be the exception rather than the rule.

**Who should buy this**
