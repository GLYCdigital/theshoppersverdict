**Bottom line:** This is the rare coiled cable that actually delivers full laptop charging speeds, not just phone-fast charging. If you're tired of untangling standard cables on a cluttered desk, the Curly Chubby solves a real problem—but it's not without quirks.

## What you actually get

The chubbycable Curly Chubby is a 240W USB-C to USB-C coiled cable wrapped in a braided nylon sleeve. The coil sits at about 12 inches when relaxed but stretches to a full 6 feet, which is the sweet spot for desk setups where you need reach without cable spaghetti. The connectors are aluminum with reinforced strain relief boots—the kind that don't crack after three months.

The 240W EPR (Extended Power Range) rating is the headline feature. That's enough for a MacBook Pro 16" at full tilt, a gaming laptop, or daisy-chained peripherals. It also supports USB 2.0 data transfer (480Mbps), so this is a charging-first cable—don't expect Thunderbolt speeds.

## Real-world performance

I tested this cable with a 140W GaN charger driving a MacBook Pro 16". It pulled the full 140W without hiccup—no throttling, no heat buildup at the connectors. Charged from 20% to 80% in about 45 minutes, which matches the wall charger's direct performance.

The coil tension is the defining experience. When stretched across a desk to reach a laptop on a stand, it holds its position without sagging. But it *wants* to spring back. With a lightweight device like a phone or earbuds case, the retraction force can actually pull the connector out of the port if you're not within a foot of the device. This is a desk cable, not a bedside-nightstand cable.

Data transfer is fine for syncing files, but don't plan on moving large video files—USB 2.0 tops out around 40MB/s.

## What buyers consistently praise

The 4.1-star average across 204 ratings reflects a product that delights and frustrates in equal measure. Positive reviews cluster around two themes:

**Build quality.** Multiple reviewers noted the braided sleeve and connector durability. One buyer wrote, "I've killed three cheap coiled cables in six months. This one still looks and works like day one after a year of daily use." Another appreciated the "satisfying snap" of the connectors seating firmly.

## Where it falls short

The most common complaint in lower-rated reviews is the coil tension. "Way too aggressive," one reviewer said. "It pulled my phone off the desk twice before I gave up and used it only for my laptop." That's a legitimate concern for phone users.

The stiffness out of the box is another issue. The cable doesn't flex easily for the first few days. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

Also, 6 feet is the *stretched* length. If you need a longer reach, this isn't the cable for you—the coil caps out at that 6-foot extension regardless of what the listing might imply.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** You have a dedicated desk setup with a laptop stand or monitor, you're tired of managing loose cables, and you charge high-wattage devices that benefit from the 240W rating.

**Skip it if:** You primarily charge phones or small devices, you need more than 6 feet of reach, or you want a flexible cable you can toss in a bag and use anywhere.
