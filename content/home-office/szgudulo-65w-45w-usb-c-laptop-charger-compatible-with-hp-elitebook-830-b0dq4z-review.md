## Is the SZGUDULO 65W/45W charger actually worth replacing your HP OEM brick?

If you've ever lugged the factory HP EliteBook charger through airport security, you know the pain. That brick is heavy, bulky, and the cable management is a nightmare. The SZGUDULO 65W/45W USB C laptop charger promises to fix that — and for the most part, it does. But before you buy, there are a few things you need to know.

## What's in the Box

Nothing fancy here. You get the charger itself with a permanently attached 5-foot USB C cable. No travel pouch, no extra tips, no adapters. Just the power adapter. The unit measures roughly 2.5 x 1.5 x 1 inch and weighs about 4 ounces — noticeably lighter than the HP OEM brick.

## Performance and Build Quality

The headline feature is the dual wattage output. A physical switch lets you toggle between 65W and 45W. The 65W setting is what you'll use for most EliteBook 830, 840, and 850 series laptops — it'll push a full charge in about 90 minutes from dead, matching the OEM charger's speed. The 45W setting is for smaller devices like tablets, phones, or older UltraBooks that don't need the full juice.

Here's the thing about that switch: it's manual. The charger doesn't auto-detect what you've plugged in. If you forget to flip it back to 65W after charging your phone, your laptop will charge — but slowly, and it may not keep up under heavy load. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, but worth noting.

Build quality is solid for the price point. The casing is hard plastic with a matte finish that resists scratches. The folding prongs click into place with a satisfying snap. The cable is the weak link — it's thin, and several buyers report it becoming finicky after 6-8 months of daily use. One Amazon reviewer wrote, *"Worked great for 7 months, then the cable started losing connection unless I bent it at a specific angle."* That's the tradeoff for the compact design.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The portability factor comes up again and again. One verified purchase noted, *"Replaced the massive HP brick in my travel bag. This is maybe a third of the size and does the exact same job."* Another praised the safety features: *"Gets warm but never hot, even after a full day of use. Feels well-engineered."* The dual output also gets love from people who carry both a work laptop and personal devices — one charger handles everything.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the cable and manual switch issues, there's a compatibility catch. This charger works exclusively with USB C power delivery. If your EliteBook is from 2019 or earlier and uses the round barrel connector, this isn't your charger. Also, the 5-foot cable is shorter than the OEM's 6-foot offering — if your desk outlet is far from your laptop position, measure first.

## Who Should Buy This

Three specific buyer personas:

1. **The frequent traveler** — anyone who flies weekly and wants to shed OEM brick weight from their bag
2. **The multi-device user** — carrying both a USB C laptop and a phone/tablet, wanting one charger for everything
3. **The home-office minimalist** — someone who wants a smaller footprint on their surge protector

Skip it if you need a rugged daily-driver for a desk where the charger never moves — the OEM brick is more durable for that use case.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this charge my EliteBook while I'm using it for video calls or heavy workloads?**
A: Yes, at 65W it handles full-load operation while charging. At 45W, it'll charge slowly or hold steady depending on your CPU load.

**Q: Does it work with non-HP USB C laptops?**
A: Generally yes, for any laptop that charges over USB C PD at 65W or less — think Dell XPS, MacBook Air, Lenovo ThinkPad. Just verify your laptop's wattage requirement.

**Q: Is the 45W setting safe for phones and smaller devices?**
A: Yes. The charger negotiates power delivery with the connected device, so a phone will only draw what it needs — the switch just caps the maximum available output.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're tired of the HP OEM brick's size and want a genuinely portable dual-output USB C charger that handles both your laptop and smaller devices. The 4.3-star rating across 215 reviews reflects a product that does the core job well.

**Skip it if** you need a bombproof daily charger that'll survive years of abuse, or if your HP laptop uses the older barrel connector.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, travel-friendly replacement with a couple of design quirks that keep it from being perfect. At this price point, it's a smart buy for anyone with a USB C EliteBook.
