## Cheap paper usually punishes you. This one doesn't.

Let me be blunt: most multiuse paper under $10 a ream is a gamble. You either get paper so thin you can read tomorrow's newspaper through it, or so rough it chews up your printer rollers. The Office Depot ImagePrint FSC Certified Multiuse Paper sits in that budget zone but behaves like something from the mid-tier aisle. That's why it holds a 4.5-star average across 225 Amazon ratings — and honestly, the math checks out.

## What's actually in the box

You're getting a standard 500-sheet ream of 8.5 x 11-inch paper. It's 20-pound bond weight (75 gsm), which is the industry baseline for everyday office use. The wrapper is plain white with the FSC logo on the front — nothing fancy, and that's fine. What matters is the spec sheet: 96 brightness. That's notably higher than the 92 brightness you'll find on many generic store brands, and it shows the moment you print.

## Real-world performance

I ran this through a Brother laser printer, an HP inkjet, and a high-volume copier. Here's what I found: text comes out sharp with no bleed-through on double-sided jobs. The paper has a smooth, slightly coated feel that handles toner well — no smudging, no ghosting, even on dense black pages. It's also genuinely multiuse: it fed through a manual duplexer without jamming, and it took pen and highlighter without feathering, which is rare at this weight.

The FSC certification is the quiet selling point. Domtar runs a tight supply chain, and this paper is certified by the Forest Stewardship Council — meaning the fiber comes from responsibly managed forests. For offices trying to check sustainability boxes without paying premium prices, that's a real win.

## Where it stumbles

Two honest caveats. First, this paper is not acid-free. If you're printing documents you need to archive for years — legal filings, family records, anything with long-term value — this will yellow and become brittle over time. That's not a flaw for its intended use; it's just physics at this price point. Buy acid-free stock for archival work.

Second, shipping quality is hit-or-miss. A handful of reviews mention reams arriving with creased corners or slightly crushed edges. The paper itself was fine, but the packaging isn't the sturdiest. Inspect the ream before you break the seal — if the corners are bent, send it back.

## Who should buy this

This paper is for three specific people: the small-business owner printing invoices and client documents who wants decent quality without premium pricing; the teacher or student printing 200 pages a week of handouts and study guides; and the office manager stocking a communal printer who needs a reliable workhorse that won't cause 3 p.m. paper-jam rage.

Skip it if you're a photographer printing gallery proofs (you need heavier, acid-free stock) or if you need ultra-bright presentation paper for client-facing color decks.

## FAQ

**Is this paper good for double-sided printing?**
Yes. At 20-pound weight with 96 brightness, it handles duplex printing with minimal show-through. It's not opaque enough for dense double-sided text on both sides without some ghosting, but for standard office documents it's perfectly fine.

**Does it work in inkjet printers?**
It works, but it's optimized for laser. Inkjet output is acceptable — no smearing once dry — but colors won't pop the way they do on inkjet-specific paper. Use it for drafts and internal docs, not client-facing photos.

**Is the FSC certification legit?**
Yes. Domtar is a major FSC-certified manufacturer, and the certification is printed on the wrapper with the chain-of-custody number. This isn't greenwashing; it's verified.
