The Anker Soundcore Q20i is the latest proof that you don't need to spend $300 to get good active noise cancellation. With over 63,000 reviews and a 4.6-star rating at just $39.99, this is the budget ANC headphones market's current heavyweight champion.

## The ANC Experience

The hybrid noise cancellation on the Q20i is genuinely impressive. Multiple users note that it provides immediate isolation the moment the headphones connect — before music even starts playing. One reviewer describes it as "amazing" how the world disappears when you put these on. For the price, the ANC performance borders on unbelievable.

The transparency mode is a welcome inclusion. When you need to hear your surroundings — crossing streets, grabbing coffee, quick office conversations — a single button press lets ambient sound in without removing the headphones.

## Sound Quality

These sound excellent for the price. The bass is the standout — punchy and present without overwhelming the mids. Vocals are clear, and the Hi-Res Audio certification is backed up by user experience. One reviewer who replaced a broken premium pair says "I don't regret it at all" and calls the sound quality "surprisingly good with strong bass, clear vocals, and enough detail for music, movies, and podcasts."

The Soundcore app lets you adjust EQ to your preference, which adds versatility. The bass-up preset is popular among hip-hop and electronic listeners.

## Comfort and Battery

At 40 hours with ANC on, battery anxiety isn't a thing here. Users report going weeks between charges with regular use. The Bluetooth connection pairs easily with iPhones, Android devices, and LG TVs according to one reviewer.

Comfort is where opinions diverge. The plush ear pads are comfortable for shorter sessions, but some users report their ears getting "very hot quickly" during extended wear. One reviewer specifically warns about this for people with sensory sensitivities. The fit is on the snugger side, which helps with noise isolation but may feel tight after hours of use.

## The Bottom Line

At $39.99, the Soundcore Q20i delivers ANC performance and sound quality that undercuts the competition by a wide margin. The heat issue is real for long sessions, and the app could be friendlier, but for commuting, studying, working from home, or drowning out a noisy environment on a budget, these are hard to beat.

**Verdict: 4.5/5** — Budget ANC done right. Excellent noise cancelling, great sound, and absurd battery life for the price. Just be aware of the heat buildup on longer sessions.
