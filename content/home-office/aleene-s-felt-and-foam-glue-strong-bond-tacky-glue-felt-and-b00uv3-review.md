If you've ever watched hot glue string across a felt project or watched school glue soak through craft foam and warp it, you know the pain this bottle is trying to solve. Aleene's Felt and Foam Glue is a purpose-built adhesive that does exactly what the label promises — and for the most part, it delivers.

## What's actually in the bottle

You get a 4-ounce squeeze bottle with a fine-tip applicator that's genuinely useful, not just a marketing gimmick. The nozzle lets you trace precise lines along felt edges or dot glue onto small foam pieces without flooding the surface. It's a white tacky glue that dries crystal clear, which matters when you're working with light-colored felt where yellowing would ruin the project.

## How it performs in real use

The bond strength is the headline here. This glue grabs fast — you get about 60 seconds of working time before it starts setting, which is the sweet spot for crafters who need to position pieces without wrestling them. Once dry, the bond is flexible rather than brittle, which is critical for felt that gets bent, folded, or worn. I tested it on both standard craft felt and 2mm foam sheets; the foam held without the edges curling up, and the felt didn't bleed through or stiffen into a cardboard-like texture.

One thing that surprised me: it's actually repositionable for those first few seconds. If you misalign a piece, you can peel it up immediately and reapply without tearing the felt. That's rare in this category.

## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the limitations. This glue is **not waterproof**. If your project will see moisture or outdoor elements, you need a different adhesive. Also, the 4-ounce size runs out faster than you'd think — enthusiastic felt crafters report going through a bottle in a single weekend of production. Buy the larger size if you're planning a big project.

The fumes are present, though mild. It's not overpowering like super glue, but sensitive noses will notice. Ventilation helps. Finally, it's not a substitute for hot glue on heavy materials — this is specifically formulated for lightweight fabrics and foam, so don't expect it to hold thick ribbons or embellishments with heavy hardware.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work on polyester felt or only wool felt?**
A: It works on both, but synthetic felt is less absorbent so the glue takes slightly longer to set. Give it an extra 30 seconds before bonding pieces together.

**Q: How long does it take to fully cure?**
A: It sets in about 60 seconds but reaches full strength in 24 hours. Don't stress-test your project immediately.

**Q: Can you dilute it or use it in a stencil?**
A: Not recommended. The formula is designed for direct application. Thinning it changes the bond strength and drying time.
