## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs.

**No handle.** This is a sleeve, not a briefcase. If you want to carry it standalone, you're gripping the zipper pull or tucking it under your arm. That's fine for slipping into a backpack, but it limits standalone use.

**Sizing runs snug at the top of each range.** If you have a 15.6-inch laptop, don't order the 15.6 size if you want room for a charger or accessories. Go up a size. I've seen this complaint in reviews — people assuming the size is generous when it's actually true-to-dimension.

**Zero internal organization.** No pen slots, no accessory pocket, no document sleeve. This is a pure protective shell. If you need to carry a mouse and charger alongside your laptop, you're buying a separate organizer or a different product entirely.
