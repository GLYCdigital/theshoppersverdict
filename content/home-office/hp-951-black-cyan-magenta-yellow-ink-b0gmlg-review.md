If you own an OfficeJet Pro 8600, 8610, or 8620, you already know the HP 951 cartridge family is your only genuine OEM option. This four-pack bundles the black, cyan, magenta, and yellow standard-yield cartridges into one box, which is convenient for restocking all at once — but is it the smartest buy for your printing habits? Let's dig into what these actually deliver beyond the packaging.

**What's in the box**

You get four individually sealed cartridges: one HP 951 black (CN052AN), plus cyan, magenta, and yellow (CN053AN, CN054AN, CN055AN). Each is the standard-yield version, not the XL. That matters more than you think — the black is rated around 400 pages, and each color roughly 330 pages, based on ISO/IEC 24711 testing with 5% coverage. The XL variants roughly double those numbers, so this set is really for light-to-moderate home office use, not high-volume document production.

**Performance and real-world output**

The pigment-based black ink dries quickly and resists water smudging — a genuine advantage for documents that get handled immediately after printing. Color output is punchy and accurate for a business inkjet, with no banding on standard copy paper. I printed test pages across the full OfficeJet Pro 8600 series, and color registration stayed crisp even on dense graphics.

Installation is genuinely tool-free: cartridges click in with a satisfying snap, and the chip recognition works on the first try. I've seen plenty of printer frustration in my time, but this is one area where HP's OEM quality shows — no firmware errors, no "incompatible cartridge" warnings, no wrestling with tape seals.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.5-star average across 225 ratings reflects steady satisfaction. One verified purchaser noted: "Replaced all four at once and the printer recognized them instantly. Print quality is exactly what I expect from genuine HP." Another echoed a common theme: "Reliable, no leaks, no clogs — these just work." Multiple reviewers specifically called out the convenience of buying the full set instead of tracking individual cartridges.

**Where it falls short**

The honest tradeoff is cost per page. At standard yield, you're looking at roughly 3–4 cents per page for black and 8–10 cents for color — significantly more than third-party options. If you print more than 200 pages a month, the XL versions (sold separately) will save you money in the long run despite the higher upfront cost. Also, this set doesn't include the high-yield black, so heavy text printers will replace the black cartridge first and often.

**Who should buy this**

The HP 951 4-pack is ideal for the home office user who prints 50–150 pages a month, values zero-fuss reliability, and doesn't want to gamble on remanufactured cartridges that sometimes leak or throw error codes. It's also a smart choice if you're stocking an office with multiple OfficeJet Pro units — standardizing on OEM cartridges reduces troubleshooting across machines.

Skip it if you're a high-volume printer. Buy the XL versions individually for better economics, or consider a genuine HP Instant Ink subscription if you print consistently.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this set work with the OfficeJet Pro 8600 Plus and 8610?**
A: Yes. The HP 951 cartridges are compatible across the entire OfficeJet Pro 8600, 8610, and 8620 series, including the Plus and Premium models. Check your printer's model number, but the vast majority of these units use the same cartridge family.

**Q: How long do these cartridges last?**
A: Real-world yield depends on your print coverage. With typical text documents at 5% coverage, expect around 400 pages from black and 330 per color. Photos or heavy graphics will drain them considerably faster.

**Q: Are these the same as the "XL" cartridges?**
A: No. This set is standard yield. XL cartridges are sold individually and roughly double the page count. You'll pay more upfront for XL but save per page if you print heavily.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want genuine HP quality without compatibility headaches, print moderate volumes, and prefer the simplicity of replacing all four colors at once.

**Skip it if:** Your printing volume exceeds 200 pages monthly, or you're trying to minimize per-page costs — in that case, XL cartridges or a subscription model make better financial sense.

The HP 951 four-pack earns a 4.6/5 rating. It's not the cheapest way to print, but it's the most dependable — and for a home office that can't afford printer downtime, reliability is worth the premium.
