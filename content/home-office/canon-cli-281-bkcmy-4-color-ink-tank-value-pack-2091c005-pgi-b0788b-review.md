Let's be honest about printer ink: it's the most expensive liquid you'll ever buy per ounce, and Canon's CLI-281 series is no exception. But if you own a compatible PIXMA printer, this four-pack is the smartest way to buy the official stuff without getting gouged per cartridge.

**What's Actually in the Box**

You get four individually sealed cartridges: one PGI-280 Pigment Black, one CLI-281 Cyan, one CLI-281 Magenta, and one CLI-281 Yellow. The packaging is standard Canon — foil-sealed, vacuum-packed, with a freshness date stamped on each cartridge. No surprises, no proprietary adapters, just drop-in replacements for any printer using the CLI-281/PGI-280 system (think PIXMA TS8320, TS9520, MG7720, and similar models).

**Performance and Real-World Print Quality**

I've run these through a TS8320 across photo paper, matte presentation stock, and plain copy paper. The dye-based color inks produce rich, saturated prints with smooth gradients — skin tones stay natural, skies don't turn teal, and reds don't bleed into oranges. That's the advantage of OEM ink: the color profiles are tuned to Canon's print heads, so what you see on screen is what lands on paper.

The PGI-280 black cartridge uses pigment ink, which is the right call for text. It dries fast and resists water better than dye-based black. One quirk to know: if you print documents and immediately run a highlighter over the text, you might get slight smearing on cheap 20-lb paper. Let it dry for 30 seconds and you're fine.

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

Scrolling through the 235 Amazon ratings (4.7 stars), a few themes repeat. One reviewer notes, "I've tried off-brand carts and always come back to these — the colors are just more accurate, especially for photo prints." Another mentions the convenience factor: "Having all four in one box means I'm never caught with an empty magenta at 11 PM before a school project is due."

The yield tracking is also genuinely useful. The chip communicates with Canon's ink status monitor, so you know exactly when each color is running low — no guessing, no opening a cartridge to find it's dry.

**Where It Falls Short**

The biggest complaint I see in the review data isn't about print quality — it's about value. The dye-based black cartridge (CLI-281) is the odd one out here. The box includes PGI-280 for pigment black, which lasts longer, but the three color cartridges are standard yield. If you print a lot of photos or color documents, you'll replace cyan or magenta faster than you'd like. There's no XL version in this bundle, so heavy users will feel the cost per page.

Also worth noting: this pack doesn't include the PGI-280 XL black that some Canon bundles offer. If you're a text-heavy printer, you might pair this pack with a separate XL black cartridge to balance your usage.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Photo enthusiasts** with a PIXMA who demand accurate color and don't want to gamble on third-party ink ruining prints
- **Home office users** who print 50-100 pages monthly and want the convenience of having all colors on hand
- **Anyone who's been burned by cheap compatible carts** causing clogs or banding — OEM ink protects your print head warranty

Skip it if you print 500+ pages a month; a tank-based printer like the EcoTank or MegaTank will save you serious money long-term.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will these fit my PIXMA TS8320?**
A: Yes. The CLI-281/PGI-280 combo fits TS series (8320, 9520, 9521), MG series (7720, 6820), and TR series (8520, 8620). Check your printer's manual for the exact cartridge codes — Canon lists compatible models on the box.

**Q: How many pages will I get per cartridge?**
A: Standard yield is roughly 200-300 pages for color at 5% coverage, and about 400 pages for the PGI-280 black. Real-world photo printing will drop that significantly — expect 30-50 4x6 prints per color cartridge.

**Q: Is this the same as the CLI-281XL?**
A: No. The XL versions hold more ink and cost more upfront but lower your cost per page. This value pack uses standard-yield cartridges only.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You own a compatible PIXMA, want genuine Canon quality, and appreciate the convenience of a complete set with modest savings over individual cartridges. 4.6/5.

**Skip it if:** You're a high-volume printer who needs XL cartridges, or you're willing to accept the risks of third-party ink to save money.

For the average home user, this is the right balance of reliability, quality, and convenience. Canon's OEM ink is expensive — but it's expensive for a reason, and this pack makes the sting a little easier to take.
