**FAQ**  

*Q: Can I reuse the tags when I change the label?*  
Yes. The casing snaps open and closed repeatedly. Just pop it open, swap the insert, and close it again. No adhesive, no mess.  

*Q: Are the rings big enough for standard keys?*  
The rings are about 0.6 inches in diameter — enough for two or three standard keys. For a thick key fob or a car key with a remote, you might need a larger ring, but the tag itself attaches fine.  

*Q: Will the plastic yellow over time?*  
Clear plastic can yellow with UV exposure, but these are meant for indoor or bag use. If left in direct sunlight for months, they may discolor. Keep them out of a car dashboard and they’ll stay clear.
