You'd think a label is a label. Then you run a batch of wedding invitations through a printer and watch the ink smear, or the corner catches and peels off in the mailbox. That's where Avery's printable address labels earn their keep. With a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, these sheets have clearly survived some serious mailing seasons.

**What you're actually getting**

Each pack contains letter-sized sheets of standard address labels — the classic 1" x 2-5/8" size that fits most envelope windows. The labels use a matte paper stock that's engineered for both inkjet and laser printers, which matters more than most buyers realize. Laser printers run hot; cheap labels curl at the edges. These stay flat through the fuser. The adhesive is permanent, not repositionable, which is the right call for anything going through the mail stream.

**Real-world performance**

I tested these with a standard HP inkjet and a Brother laser. Inkjet output dried quickly with zero smudging, even when I stacked envelopes immediately after printing. The laser run was clean too — no toner flaking, no sheet curl. The die-cut is precise; labels separate cleanly without tearing or leaving jagged edges.

The matte finish deserves specific praise. Glossy labels reflect light and make addresses harder to read under bright conditions. This matte surface keeps text sharp and scannable, which postal sorting machines appreciate. One Amazon reviewer noted, "Used these for my small business shipping labels — they stick to poly mailers and stay put through transit. Zero returns from lost packages." Another said, "The template integration is seamless. I imported a CSV and printed 500 labels in one session without a single jam."

**Where it falls short**

No product is flawless. In humid conditions, the sheets have a mild tendency to stick together if you stack them fresh out of the printer. Let them cool and dry for a minute before stacking. Also, this listing is white labels only — if you need clear labels for branding purposes or kraft-paper envelopes, you'll need to look at Avery's other product lines. The price per sheet is slightly higher than off-brand alternatives, but you're paying for reliable template compatibility and consistent adhesion.

**Who should buy these**

- **Small business owners** shipping 20-50 packages weekly — the template integration alone saves hours.
- **Event planners** handling wedding or party invitations where presentation matters.
- **Etsy sellers** who need clean, professional addressing on branded packaging.
- **Home offices** that occasionally mail documents and want a box of labels that lasts years without drying out.

Skip these if you need clear or colored labels, or if you're on a razor-thin budget and don't care about template compatibility.
