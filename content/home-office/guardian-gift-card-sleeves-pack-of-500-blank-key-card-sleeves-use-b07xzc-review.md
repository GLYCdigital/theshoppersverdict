Let's be honest: a gift card sleeve isn't the kind of product you think about until you're standing at a register at 11 PM on Christmas Eve, holding a card that won't fit in any envelope you own. The Guardian 500-pack solves that problem permanently — and then some.

## What You Actually Get

This is a plain white box containing 500 blank key card sleeves. Each sleeve measures the standard 2.125 x 3.375 inches — the same dimensions as a typical credit card or gift card. The cardstock is 12pt, which translates to roughly 0.012 inches thick. That's noticeably sturdier than the flimsy paper sleeves you get free with greeting cards.

The sleeves arrive folded in half with a scored center crease. You slip the card in, fold, and seal. That's the entire process.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

I tested these with a standard Visa gift card, a hotel key card, and a thicker rewards card with an embossed number. The standard cards slid in easily, and the scored crease folded cleanly without cracking or splitting. The 12pt stock held up to being shoved into pockets, tossed into purses, and handled by impatient recipients.

The thicker card was a problem. The sleeve's tight dimensions don't accommodate cards with raised lettering or embedded chip antennas. It fit, but the sleeve bulged and looked sloppy. Stick to standard flat cards.

One thing that surprised me: the sleeves aren't self-sealing. There's no adhesive strip on the flap. You'll need tape or a sticker to keep them closed. For a pack of 500, that's a minor annoyance, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews reflects genuine satisfaction. One recurring theme is the cardstock quality. As one verified buyer put it: "These are thick, not the cheap flimsy ones you get at the drugstore. I've used them for three different events and they look professional every time."

Another common praise point is the blank surface. Since both sides are completely bare, businesses and crafters can stamp, print, or hand-write whatever they need. A small business owner noted, "I print my logo on these for client gifts. They take ink well and don't smudge."

## Where It Falls Short

The lack of adhesive is the most consistent complaint. Multiple reviewers mention needing to buy tape or stickers separately. For a product marketed as "sleeves," you'd expect a simple seal.

The other limitation is size rigidity. These are strictly standard-thickness cards. If you're gifting a card with a pop-up design, a metal gift card, or anything with significant thickness, these won't work.
