**What you actually get**

The package delivers 50 individual vinyl stickers, each with a distinct Mickey design. **Performance and build quality**

The vinyl material is the star here. It's thick enough to resist tearing but flexible enough to conform to curved surfaces — critical for water bottles. The adhesive uses a medium-tack formula that bonds firmly to stainless steel, plastic, and laptop lids. Multiple buyers report these surviving full dishwasher cycles, which is the torture test most stickers fail. It's noticeable if you're examining them up close, but invisible at arm's length.

The designs are clearly Mickey Mouse, but they lack the holographic authenticity sticker and official quality control of licensed products. For casual use, that's fine. For a strict collector's item, it's not.
