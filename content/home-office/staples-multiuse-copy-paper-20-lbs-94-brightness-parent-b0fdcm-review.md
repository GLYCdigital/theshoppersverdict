Let’s be honest: copy paper is copy paper until it jams your printer at 11 PM before a deadline. Then it matters a lot. The Staples Multiuse 20 lb / 94 brightness ream sits in that middle ground between bargain-bin flimsy and premium presentation stock. After running this through a laser printer, an inkjet, and a high-volume office copier, here’s what actually matters.

**What you get**

A standard 500-sheet ream, 8.5 x 11 inches, wrapped in the familiar Staples white packaging. The paper is 20 lb basis weight (about 75 gsm), which is the industry standard for everyday copying. The 94 brightness rating is the headline spec here — that’s higher than the typical 92 for generic store brands and noticeably whiter than the 90-brightness budget reams. If you’re scanning documents or making copies that will be re-scanned, that brightness bump keeps text sharp and backgrounds clean.

**Performance under real use**

I tested this in three machines: an HP LaserJet, a Canon inkjet, and a Brother all-in-one. Feed reliability was the standout. I ran 200 sheets through the laser printer without a single misfeed or double-feed. The paper has a consistent cut with clean edges — no stray fibers or rough spots that cause pickup rollers to skip. In the inkjet, drying time was acceptable; text didn’t smear when I touched it after 30 seconds. Color graphics came out decent for a multiuse sheet, though this isn’t the paper for photo prints — colors look slightly washed compared to coated photo paper, which is expected at this price point.

One thing I noticed: the surface is slightly more textured than premium sheets like Hammermill Premium. With a standard ballpoint or rollerball, no issue. With a heavy gel pen or fountain pen, you’ll feel a bit more drag and see minor feathering. If you do a lot of handwritten notes on printed pages, keep that in mind.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The Amazon reviews (4.7 stars from 235 ratings) cluster around two themes: value and reliability. One reviewer noted, “Best value for the price — I’ve bought three cases and never had a jam.” Another said, “Whiter than the generic office brand I used before, and prints look sharper.” Several small business owners mention using it for client-facing documents and being satisfied with the professional look without paying premium prices. The durability also gets mentioned — sheets hold up well to double-sided printing without ghosting or show-through.

**Where it falls short**

The honest tradeoffs: This is not acid-free paper. If you’re printing documents you need to archive for years, it will yellow and become brittle. That’s fine for memos, drafts, and school handouts — not fine for legal filings or family history documents. Also, the 94 brightness, while good, is not the 96+ you get from ultra-premium sheets. If you’re doing color presentations or marketing materials where every percent of whiteness matters, step up. And the rougher surface texture I mentioned is real — it’s fine for most uses, but it’s not buttery smooth.

**Who should buy this**

This is the right paper for home offices, small businesses, schools, and anyone who prints more than a few reams a month. If you need a workhorse sheet for reports, invoices, homework, or internal documents, this delivers 90% of the premium experience at a better price. Skip it if you need archival quality, do professional color printing, or use fountain pens regularly.

**FAQ**

*Does this paper work in laser printers?* Yes, it’s rated for laser and inkjet. I tested it in both — laser output was crisp, no curling or fuser issues.

*How does the brightness compare to standard office paper?* Most generic store brands are 92 brightness. This is 94, which is visibly whiter. You’ll notice the difference side-by-side, especially with black text.

*Can I use this for double-sided printing?* Yes. The 20 lb weight prevents noticeable show-through. I ran duplex printing and both sides were legible with no ghosting.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if:** You want reliable, bright, everyday copy paper that feeds cleanly and won’t embarrass you in front of clients. It’s the smart pick for high-volume printing where you don’t want to pay premium prices.

**Skip it if:** You’re archiving documents long-term, doing professional color work, or you write with fountain pens and hate any tooth on the paper.

For most people, this is the ream to keep on the shelf. It does the job without drama, and the 94 brightness gives your documents a polish that budget paper can’t match.
