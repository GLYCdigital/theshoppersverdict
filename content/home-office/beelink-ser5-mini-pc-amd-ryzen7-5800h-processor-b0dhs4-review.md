Most mini PCs under $300 make you choose between decent performance and actual portability. The Beelink SER5 with the AMD Ryzen 7 5800H doesn't make you choose — it delivers genuine 8-core muscle in a package that disappears behind your monitor. After spending two weeks with this thing as my daily office driver, here's the real story.

**What you actually get**

The box includes the SER5 unit itself (measuring just 4.9 x 4.4 x 1.6 inches), a 19V/3.42A power adapter, an HDMI cable, a VESA mounting bracket with screws, and a user manual. That's it — no keyboard, no mouse, no bloatware. It comes with Windows 11 Pro pre-installed, which is a nice touch for business users who'd otherwise pay $200 extra for the Pro license.

Port selection is genuinely generous for this price tier. The front panel has a USB-C port (with DisplayPort alt mode), two USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-A ports, and a 3.5mm headphone jack. The rear packs two HDMI 2.0 ports, another USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-A, two USB 2.0 ports, and a 1GbE Ethernet jack. That's enough connectivity to run a triple-monitor setup without a dock.

**Real-world performance**

The Ryzen 7 5800H is a laptop-class chip (8 cores, 16 threads, up to 4.4GHz boost) that Beelink has paired with a decent cooling solution. In Cinebench R23 multi-core testing, it scored around 11,800 — that's roughly 85% of a desktop Ryzen 7 5700X. For office work, this thing doesn't break a sweat. I ran 40 browser tabs, Slack, a video call, and a 50,000-row Excel spreadsheet simultaneously, and the CPU never exceeded 60% utilization.

The included 16GB of DDR4 RAM (dual-channel, upgradeable to 64GB) and 500GB M.2 SSD (PCIe 3.0) feel well-matched. Boot times hover around 12 seconds, and application launches are snappy. The SSD's sequential read speeds of roughly 3,500MB/s aren't class-leading, but you won't notice in daily use.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews reflects real satisfaction. One buyer noted, "I've had this running as a Plex server and home automation hub for six months — it hasn't crashed once." Another praised the upgradeability: "Took me five minutes to swap in a 2TB SSD and 32GB RAM. No tools needed, just pop the bottom off."

The VESA mount is a recurring highlight — several reviewers mention mounting it behind their monitor for a completely clutter-free desk. The aluminum alloy chassis also gets consistent credit for both looks and heat dissipation.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. Under sustained load — think video rendering or extended gaming sessions — the cooling fan becomes audible. It's not jet-engine loud, but in a dead-quiet home office, you'll notice it. The noise profile is a low whine rather than a whoosh, which some find more annoying than volume alone suggests.

The integrated Radeon Graphics (Vega 8) is fine for office work and 4K video playback, but don't expect gaming beyond esports titles. Overwatch 2 runs at 60fps on medium settings at 1080p; Cyberpunk 2077 is a slideshow. If you want gaming, look at the SER5 Max with the 5800H and better iGPU, or add an eGPU via the USB-C port.

Connectivity is another minor sticking point. Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.2 are solid, but there's no Wi-Fi 6E or 2.5GbE. For most home and small-office users, this won't matter — but if you're moving large files on a NAS, the 1GbE cap becomes a bottleneck.

**Who should buy this**

- **Office workers** replacing a 5-year-old desktop — this will feel like a generational leap
- **Home lab enthusiasts** wanting a low-power Plex server or Docker host (idle draw is around 8W)
- **Students** who need a full Windows machine but lack desk space

**Skip it if** you need serious GPU power for gaming or AI workloads, or if you're a heavy multitasker who runs multiple VMs simultaneously — the 5800H's 45W TDP ceiling means sustained all-core loads will throttle slightly.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I upgrade the RAM and storage?**  
A: Yes. The bottom panel pops off with a fingernail. There are two SODIMM slots (up to 64GB DDR4) and one M.2 2280 slot plus a 2.5-inch SATA bay for additional storage.

**Q: Does this support dual monitors?**  
A: Yes, via the two HDMI 2.0 ports. You can add a third display through the USB-C port with DisplayPort alt mode.

**Q: Is Windows 11 Pro included?**  
A: Yes, it's pre-installed and activated. You can also install Linux without issues — several reviewers run Ubuntu and Fedora without driver problems.

**The Verdict**

The Beelink SER5 isn't the fastest mini PC under $300 — that honor goes to units with the Ryzen 7 7840HS. But it's the best-balanced option for office productivity, offering a mature platform, strong CPU performance, and genuine upgradeability at an attractive price.

**Buy it if** you want a compact, quiet, powerful office machine that won't become e-waste in two years. **Skip it if** you need serious GPU muscle or plan to push it hard 24/7 without hearing the fan.
