## What's in the Box

The package is refreshingly simple: the case itself, no fluff. It's a one-piece folio design — the back shell is rigid polycarbonate, the front cover is a soft-touch PU leather with a microfiber lining. The tri-fold front flap doubles as a stand. You also get a cutout on the right edge that houses the Apple Pencil (1st gen) charging slot. No screen protector, no extras. That's fine — you're paying for the case, not landfill fodder.

---

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

I've handled a dozen iPad cases in this price range, and the Aoub stands out for one reason: the fit. The 2025 A16 iPad is a slightly different chassis than the 10th gen, and many generic cases get this wrong. This one doesn't. The camera bump aligns perfectly, the buttons are clicky through the cutouts, and the corners don't peel away when you grip the tablet. If you use your iPad on a desk for 8 hours a day, that precision matters.

The tri-fold stand is the star here. In typing mode (about 20 degrees), I hammered out a 2,000-word document without the case collapsing or the iPad sliding forward. In viewing mode (about 60 degrees), it held steady during a 45-minute video call. The magnets that hold the flap closed are strong enough to keep it shut in a backpack, but not so strong you fight to open it.

One thing the product images don't show clearly: the back panel is glossy, not matte. It looks sharp out of the box, but it's a fingerprint magnet. If you're a clean-freak, you'll be wiping it down daily. Not a dealbreaker, but know it going in.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest miss is the lack of auto wake/sleep. When you open the front cover, the iPad doesn't wake automatically — you have to press the power button. For $25-$30, that feature is common on competitors like ESR or Spigen. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's an extra tap you'll make dozens of times a day.

Also, the glossy back panel. If you're prone to dropping your iPad, this case offers decent drop protection (raised edges around the screen, reinforced corners), but the slippery plastic back reduces your grip. Consider a skin or a pop-socket if you're a klutz.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this case fit the 2025 iPad A16 11-inch exactly?**  
A: Yes. It's molded specifically for the A16 11th gen chassis. The camera cutout, button alignment, and speaker grilles all match perfectly. It will not fit the 10th gen or Air models.

**Q: Does the Apple Pencil charge through the case?**  
A: Yes, for the 1st gen Pencil. The cutout exposes the charging port and the magnetic strip holds it in place. It's snug enough that the pencil won't fall out in a bag.

**Q: Can I use a screen protector with this case?**
