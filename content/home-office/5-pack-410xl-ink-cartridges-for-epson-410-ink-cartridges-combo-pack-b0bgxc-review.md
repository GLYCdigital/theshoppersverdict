**Where it falls short**

That's a quality-control inconsistency, not a widespread defect, but it's worth knowing before you buy. If your printer is still under warranty, some Epson service agreements technically require OEM ink — using third-party cartridges won't brick the machine, but it could void coverage if a printhead issue arises.
