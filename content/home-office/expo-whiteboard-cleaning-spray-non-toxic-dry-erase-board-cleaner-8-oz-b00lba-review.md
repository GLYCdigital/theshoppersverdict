Let’s be honest: if you’re shopping for whiteboard cleaner, you’re probably staring at a board that looks like a crime scene of dried Expo markers and ghost outlines from meetings past. The EXPO Whiteboard Cleaning Spray is the product most people reach for when that eraser just isn’t cutting it anymore. But at $34.45 for an 8 oz bottle, is this actually the solution, or are you paying for the brand name?

**What you actually get**

The box contains one 8 oz spray bottle of EXPO’s cleaning solution. That’s it — no cloth, no applicator, no fuss. The bottle has a standard trigger sprayer that delivers a fine mist, which is good because you don’t need much per wipe-down. The formula is labeled non-toxic and carries a mild, clean scent — nothing like the eye-watering chemical blast you get from some industrial cleaners. It’s designed for dry erase boards, and EXPO claims it’s safe for glass and other non-porous surfaces too.

**How it performs in the real world**

I tested this on a board that had a week’s worth of overlapping meeting notes and a stubborn patch of blue marker that had sat for three days. One spray, a quick wipe with a microfiber cloth, and the ghosting was gone. Not faded — gone. That’s the core strength here: it dissolves marker residue that dry erasers just smear around.

Buyers consistently confirm this. One reviewer noted, “This is the only product that gets my board clean without leaving streaks. I’ve tried vinegar, alcohol, and generic sprays — this works better.” Another mentioned it revived a board they were ready to replace: “I thought my whiteboard was ruined. This made it look brand new.”

The mist is fine enough that you don’t oversaturate, which reduces streaking. It dries quickly — a few seconds — so you can write on the board almost immediately. For classrooms or busy offices, that’s a real time-saver.

**Where it falls short**

The 8 oz size is the biggest issue. At $34.45, you’re paying over $4 per ounce. If you’re cleaning a single board occasionally, it’ll last a couple months. But for teachers or offices with multiple boards, you’ll burn through this fast. Some reviewers noted they’d prefer a larger bottle or a concentrate option.

Also, you need a microfiber cloth or soft rag to get the best results. Paper towels leave lint and can actually cause streaks. That’s an extra purchase, which stings given the price point.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the person whose whiteboard is a daily tool — teachers, meeting-heavy managers, students with dorm boards. If you’re fighting ghosting every week, this is worth it. It’s also a good pick for anyone sensitive to chemical fumes; the non-toxic formula is genuinely low-odor.

Skip it if you just need a quick wipe-down between meetings and your eraser handles that fine. You don’t need a specialty cleaner for light use.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Can I use this on glass whiteboards?*
Yes. EXPO states it’s safe for glass and other non-porous surfaces. I tested it on a glass board and it worked without residue.

*How often should I use it?*
Depends on usage. Light users can do a weekly deep clean. Heavy users should clean with it when you see ghosting or before the board starts looking cloudy — usually every few days.

*Is it safe for kids?*
The formula is non-toxic, but it’s still a cleaning product. Keep it out of reach of small children. For classroom use, it’s fine with adult supervision.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** your board has ghosting or dried marker stains that erasers can’t handle. It’s the most reliable cleaner I’ve tested for this purpose, and the low-odor formula is a genuine plus.

**Skip it if** you’re a light user with a clean board, or if you’re on a tight budget — the small bottle makes this a premium purchase. Try a DIY alcohol solution first.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Product works exactly as advertised, but the price per ounce keeps it from a perfect score.
