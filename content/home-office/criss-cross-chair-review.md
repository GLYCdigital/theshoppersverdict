The Criss Cross Chair is exactly what its name suggests — an office chair designed for people who like to sit cross-legged. With a wide, flat seat, flip-up armrests, and a price tag under $100, it promises to solve the perennial problem of finding an ergonomic chair that accommodates non-traditional sitting positions.

## The Cross-Legged Difference

Most office chairs are designed for the standard 90-degree sitting position — feet flat on the floor, knees at a right angle. If you're the type who naturally curls up, tucks one foot under you, or sits cross-legged, standard chairs fight against you. The armrests dig into your thighs, the seat bucket is too narrow, and you end up perched awkwardly.

The Criss Cross Chair solves this with a genuinely wide, flat seat cushion that gives you room to shift positions. The armrests flip up completely out of the way, giving you full freedom to sit however you want. For anyone who works from home and finds themselves adjusting position constantly, this is a genuine quality-of-life improvement.

Users consistently praise the comfort. One reviewer describes it as "perfect for sitting, planking, criss-cross applesauce, or whatever seating position you wanna call it." The seat cushion is thick and supportive, and the back provides good lumbar support for a chair in this price range.

## Practical Features That Work

The flip-up arms are well-executed — they lock securely in the down position, fold up with one hand, and stay out of the way when not needed. The side pockets on the seat base are a clever touch: they're perfect for holding your phone, remote, or a small notebook.

The chair rolls smoothly on hard floors, though some users note the wheels are a bit stiff on carpet. This is a common issue with budget office chairs, and the fix is simple — swap in rollerblade-style casters if you have thick carpet.

Assembly is straightforward, with all tools included. Most users report about 20 minutes from box to sitting.

## Considerations

With only 38 reviews at the time of writing, this is a relatively new product. The early feedback is overwhelmingly positive, but long-term durability data is thin. The build quality feels solid — the frame, base, and gas lift all pass the shake test — but only time will tell how the cushion holds up under daily use.

The wheels-on-carpet issue is worth noting but easy to fix. And while the cushion is comfortable, a few users mention they'd prefer slightly more padding for all-day use.

## Who Should Buy It

This chair is for anyone who doesn't sit "normally" at their desk. If you work from home, game, or study and find yourself shifting positions throughout the day, the Criss Cross Chair accommodates you in ways a standard task chair can't. It's also an excellent vanity chair or gaming chair for those who prefer a more relaxed seating posture.

At $99.99, it's competitively priced for a chair with this much versatility. If you've ever wished your office chair had armrests that actually got out of your way, this is the answer.
