There are two kinds of desk upgrades: the kind that costs a lot and the kind that costs $9.99. The YSAGi Leather Desk Pad is firmly in the second category and punches well above its price.

**It makes your desk look like an adult works here.** That's the real value. A bare particle-board desk or a scratched laminate surface screams "temporary setup." The YSAGi pad, in its clean black PU leather, instantly gives your workspace a finished, intentional look. Multiple reviewers mention getting compliments on it.

**Functionally, it replaces three things:** a mouse pad, a writing blotter, and a desk protector. The surface is smooth enough for precise mouse tracking — optical and laser sensors both glide without issue. The full 23.6" x 13.8" size gives you room for a laptop, a notebook, and a coffee mug with space to spare.

**The non-slip backing earns its name.** Put it down, and it stays. No creeping, no sliding, no straightening it every hour. It's particularly good on glass desks where smaller mouse pads tend to wander.

**Maintenance is trivial.** Coffee spill? Wipe. Cookie crumbs? Wipe. Dust? Wipe. The PU leather doesn't absorb anything, which is the entire point of a desk protector.

**The one real downside:** it dents. Set a heavy laptop stand or monitor base on it for a few days, and you'll see impressions. They're cosmetic — the pad still functions perfectly — but if pristine appearance matters to you, you'll notice it.
