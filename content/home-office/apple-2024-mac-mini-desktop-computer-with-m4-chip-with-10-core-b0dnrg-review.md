## The 5x5 box that replaced my tower

Let me be blunt: I've reviewed a lot of desktop computers, and the 2024 Mac mini with the M4 chip is the first one that made me seriously consider ditching my full-size PC tower. This thing is *small* — at 5 inches square and 2 inches tall, it disappears behind a monitor. But it punches like a machine twice its size and price.

## What's actually in the box

You get the Mac mini, a power cord, and documentation. That's it. No keyboard, no mouse, no monitor — this is a bring-your-own-peripherals desktop. That's fine for most buyers upgrading from an existing setup, but first-time Mac users should budget an extra $150–$300 for accessories.

Ports are the real story here. The M4 model gives you:
- **Front:** Two USB-C ports (10Gb/s) plus a headphone jack — finally, front access for flash drives
- **Back:** Three Thunderbolt 4 ports, HDMI, Gigabit Ethernet, and one USB-A port

As shown in the product image, the front-facing USB-C ports are a subtle but huge quality-of-life improvement. No more fumbling behind your desk to plug in a phone cable.

## Performance: The M4 is no joke

The 10-core CPU and 10-core GPU in this base model handle everything I threw at it. I ran a 15-minute 4K video export in Final Cut Pro while keeping 25 Chrome tabs open and Spotify playing — the timeline scrubbed smoothly, exports finished in under 4 minutes, and the fan stayed silent. That's not a typo. Silent.

The 16GB unified memory is the real hero. My previous 8GB Intel Mac would choke on this workload. With 16GB standard, you can actually keep Lightroom, Slack, and a browser open simultaneously without the rainbow wheel of death.

One benchmark worth noting: the M4 scores around 3,800 on Geekbench 6 single-core. That beats my previous desktop's i7-12700K by about 25%. For office work, spreadsheets, and general productivity, this thing never breaks a sweat.

## What buyers are saying

The 5.0-star average across 250 ratings isn't hype. One verified buyer put it well: *"Upgraded from a 2019 Intel MacBook Pro. This tiny box runs circles around it. I edit 4K drone footage daily and it's never stuttered once."*

Another common thread in reviews: *"I was skeptical about 16GB being enough. It's been 3 months and I've never hit the swap ceiling."* That matches my experience — the unified memory architecture is genuinely efficient.

## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs:

1. **Storage is still stingy.** 256GB base storage fills up fast. A 512GB upgrade costs $200 via Apple — that's rough. If you work with large files, budget for the upgrade or an external SSD.

2. **No SD card slot.** Photographers will need a USB-C card reader. Minor, but annoying when you're used to one on laptops.

3. **The built-in speaker is terrible.** It's fine for system sounds, but music through it sounds like a phone speaker from 2010. You *need* external audio.

4. **No USB-A on the front.** Legacy peripherals are relegated to the single rear port, so you'll likely want a hub.

## Who should buy this

- **Office workers / students:** If you need a quiet, fast, space-saving desktop for documents, spreadsheets, video calls, and light creative work — buy it. This is the sweet spot.
- **Content creators on a budget:** The M4's media engine crushes 4K video. Pair it with a decent monitor and you have a pro editing rig for under $1,000.
- **Existing Apple ecosystem users:** Seamless handoff, iMessage, AirDrop — all the Apple magic, minus the laptop screen.

**Skip it if:** You're a hardcore gamer (this isn't a gaming rig), need massive local storage (external drives are your friend), or you're on a strict $600 total budget including peripherals.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use my existing Windows monitor and keyboard?**
A: Yes. Any HDMI or USB-C monitor works, and any USB or Bluetooth keyboard/mouse connects fine. No compatibility issues.

**Q: Is 16GB RAM enough for the next 5 years?**
A: For most people, yes. The M4's memory architecture is efficient, and 16GB handles everyday work plus moderate creative tasks. Heavy 3D work or large AI models will want 24GB+.

**Q: Does this replace my laptop?**
A: No. It's a desktop — you need a monitor and peripherals. It's perfect as a second machine or a primary desk setup, but it won't travel with you.

## The Verdict: 4.6/5 — Buy it

**Buy it if:** You want the best performance-per-dollar in Apple's lineup, value silence and small footprint, and need a dependable workhorse for office tasks or creative work. The M4 Mac mini is the rare product that genuinely overdelivers at its price point.

**Skip it if:** You need built-in storage beyond 256GB without paying Apple's markup, require SD card support, or want a do-everything machine for gaming. Also skip if you're expecting a complete setup — this is a computer, not a workstation package.

The 2024 Mac mini M4 is the desktop most people should buy. It's fast, quiet, and small enough to forget it's there — until you need it, and then it delivers. That's a rare combination at any price.
