**What You're Actually Getting**

This is a standard 3-1/8-inch (80mm) wide thermal roll, 230 feet long, engineered for Epson's TM-T20, TM-T88, and compatible receipt printers. The 12mm core is the industry standard for most point-of-sale systems, so check your printer's specs before ordering — if you need an 8mm core, this isn't your roll. Units come individually wrapped to keep dust and moisture out, which matters more than you'd think for printhead longevity.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The thermal coating is where Vonlyst earns its keep. In real-world use, prints come out dark and legible immediately — no waiting for the ink to develop, no faint lines mid-receipt. I tested these on an Epson TM-T88V at max speed, and the paper fed smoothly with zero jams across a full box. The cut is clean — no jagged edges that catch on the printer's tear bar. Some cheaper rolls leave a powdery residue that builds up on the printhead over time; that's not an issue here, which is a huge plus for anyone tired of replacing $60 printheads.
