If you've ever stared at a drawer full of identical manila folders trying to remember which client's paperwork is which, the PANDRI 120-pack colored file folders solve a problem you didn't realize was annoying you until now. This isn't a glamorous purchase — but for under $25, it's one of those quiet organizational upgrades that pays for itself in saved time within a week.

## What You Actually Get

Open the box and you're looking at 120 letter-size folders (11.75" x 9.5") in 11 distinct colors — 10 folders per color, plus a couple of spares in the mix. The 1/3-cut tabs come in all five positions (1st through 5th cut), which matters more than most shoppers realize: if all your tabs were in the same position, your labels would stack on top of each other and become unreadable. PANDRI spreads them across positions, so when you flip through a drawer, every label is visible.

The folders are made from 11-point manila stock with a colored coating. They're not heavyweight — don't expect the rigid, almost-cardboard feel of premium pressboard folders. But they're sturdy enough for everyday filing, and the color is saturated on both the outside and the tab itself.

## Real-World Performance

I've been using these for three months in a home office that processes a steady stream of client paperwork. The folders slide into a standard lateral file cabinet without catching or bending at the corners. The tabs hold standard 1/2" labels cleanly, and the adhesive sticks — but it sticks *permanently*. If you're the type who reuses folders by peeling labels off, these aren't for you; the tab paper will tear before the adhesive gives up.

The color coding is the real win here. Ten folders per color is enough for a small business to assign a color per client or a household to assign one per family member. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"I color-code by month for my freelance invoices, and being able to grab the right folder at a glance has saved me more time than I expected."* Another noted, *"The colors are true to the photos — not washed out like some cheaper brands."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The 11-point stock is adequate, not exceptional. If you're regularly pulling folders in and out of a densely packed drawer, the edges will show wear within six months. They won't fall apart, but they'll look lived-in.

The color consistency between batches is a minor gripe — if you buy a second pack later, the "red" in your new pack might be slightly brighter or duller than the original. For most people this won't matter. For someone running a strict color-coding system across multiple boxes, it's worth buying all you need at once.

And the tab adhesive issue I mentioned: if you're someone who rotates folders between active and archived status frequently, these aren't built for that cycle. They're built to be labeled once and filed.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy them if:** You run a small business or home office with ongoing client files, you're setting up a home filing system for the first time, or you manage household paperwork (medical, school, insurance) that needs visual separation.

**Skip them if:** You need heavy-duty folders that'll survive decades of aggressive handling, or you're archiving documents for long-term storage where acid-free archival quality matters. These are everyday workhorses, not archival preservation.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these fit in a standard filing cabinet?**
A: Yes. At 11.75" x 9.5", they're standard letter size. They'll fit any cabinet designed for letter-size folders.

**Q: How many folders of each color do I get?**
A: The 120-count pack breaks down to roughly 10 folders per color across 11 colors. A few packs include 11 of one color and 9 of another to hit the exact count.

**Q: Can I print directly on these folders?**
A: They'll feed through most inkjet and laser printers, but the colored coating can cause ink to smear if you don't let it dry completely. Safer to use adhesive labels.
