The traditional office chair assumes you sit one way: feet on the floor, knees at 90 degrees, back straight. But not everyone works that way. The Criss Cross Chair with Flip-Up Arms is built for people who like to sit cross-legged, tuck a foot under, or just change positions frequently. At $99.99 with a 4.5-star average across initial reviews, it's an intriguing alternative to rigid ergonomic chairs.

## The Cross-Legged Design

The selling point is the wide, flat seat cushion. Unlike most office chairs that taper at the front and have raised edges, this one gives you room to actually sit cross-legged without your knees hanging off or the edge digging into your thighs. Reviewers consistently highlight this: "Perfect for sitting criss-cross applesauce, or whatever seating position you wanna call it," writes one buyer. Another calls it "super comfortable, especially for sitting cross-legged."

The seat cushion itself is thick enough to be comfortable, though some users mention it could be fluffier. One 4-star review notes, "the cushion could / should be more fluffy." It's supportive but not plush — think firm sofa rather than memory foam cloud.

## Flip-Up Arms and Flexibility

The armrests flip up out of the way, giving you free range of motion. This is critical for the cross-legged functionality — with arms down, you can rest your elbows; with arms up, you've got full lateral space. It also means the chair fits under desks more easily. One reviewer says it "fits under my L-shaped desk perfectly even when I work in the corner section."

The side pockets on the seat are a nice touch. They're not huge, but they hold a phone, TV remote, or small notebook without taking up desk space.

## Build and Assembly

Assembly is straightforward. The chair comes with all necessary tools, and most reviewers report being set up in 15-20 minutes. "Very sturdy and durable for everyday use in the office or at home," writes one enthusiastic buyer. The chair rolls smoothly on hard floors, though the free-spinning casters might be too eager on some surfaces.

## The Downsides

At only 38 reviews, this is a newer product with a limited long-term track record. The cushion comfort is good but not exceptional for all-day seating — it's more of a flexible-position chair than a marathon-workstation option. There's no lumbar support adjustment, so if you need specific back support, you might miss it during longer sessions.

## The Bottom Line

The Criss Cross Chair with Flip-Up Arms fills a real niche: it's a comfortable, affordable chair for people who don't want to sit conventionally. The wide seat, flip-up arms, and reasonable $99.99 price make it a solid choice for home offices, vanity setups, or anyone who values position flexibility over strict ergonomics. The short review count means we'll know more about long-term durability in the coming months, but early signs are strong.

**Verdict: 4.5/5** — If you've ever wanted to sit cross-legged at your desk without buying a floor cushion, this is your chair. Comfortable, practical, and priced right.
