## These Clear Labels Disappear on the Bottle — But Only If You Respect the Drying Time

Most label sheets feel like a compromise: you either get adhesive that actually sticks or print quality that doesn't look like a laser printer spat it out. The Avery 2" x 3" Glossy Crystal Clear labels (product #5664, 80 sheets per pack) sidestep that tradeoff more successfully than most. But they're not a set-and-forget solution. Here's what you need to know before you buy.

## What You Actually Get

Each pack contains 80 letter-size sheets, with 8 labels per sheet — 640 labels total. Each label measures exactly 2 inches by 3 inches. The material is a thin, flexible polyester film with a glossy finish and permanent adhesive. You're getting a full bleed, edge-to-edge print area with no white border, which is the whole point of clear labels. The sheets are designed for inkjet printers specifically; don't run these through a laser printer, or you'll risk melting the film.

## Real-World Performance

Print quality is where these shine. The glossy surface takes ink exceptionally well — colors come out saturated, text stays sharp, and the finished labels look like professionally manufactured product stickers. The crystal-clear material genuinely disappears on glass, plastic, and light-colored packaging. As shown in the product image, the effect is striking: your design appears to float directly on the container.

Adhesion is a genuine strength. The permanent adhesive grips hard on smooth surfaces, including curved plastic bottles and glass jars. Multiple Amazon reviewers noted labels stayed put through refrigeration, condensation, and normal handling. One buyer wrote, *"I've had these on my homemade hot sauce bottles for three months now and not a single one has peeled or bubbled."* That's the durability you want for products that will sit on shelves or ship through the mail.

## Where It Falls Short

The glossy finish has a real downside: it smudges. If you touch the printed surface before the ink fully cures (give it 3–5 minutes), you'll leave fingerprints and smear the design. This caught several reviewers off guard. One noted, *"Great labels, but I ruined two sheets by stacking them too quickly after printing."*

The bigger limitation is the clear material itself. On dark surfaces — amber glass bottles, black plastic, deep-colored packaging — the printed text becomes difficult to read because there's no white backing. You'll need to design with this in mind, either by adding a white box behind your text or sticking to light-colored containers.

Also, there's no room for alignment errors. The labels are kiss-cut on the sheet, but if your printer skews even slightly, you'll waste labels. At roughly 80 sheets per pack, that's tolerable, but not ideal if you're printing a small batch of critical items.

## Who Should Buy These

These labels are perfect for:
- **Small-batch product makers** (candles, soaps, sauces, cosmetics) who want a polished, professional look without ordering custom printed labels
- **Home organizers** labeling glass jars, storage bins, or pantry containers where you want the label to blend in
- **Crafters** making custom stickers or product samples that need a durable, water-resistant finish

Skip them if you're labeling dark-colored containers, need matte-finish labels for writing with markers, or you're printing a massive volume where per-label cost matters more than appearance.

## FAQ

**Can I use these with a laser printer?**
No. The polyester film will melt or warp in laser printers. These are strictly for inkjet printers.

**Are they waterproof?**
The labels themselves are water-resistant, and the adhesive holds through condensation and light moisture. But they're not fully waterproof for submerged applications — a dishwasher or ice bath will eventually compromise them.

**Do they work with the free Avery templates?**
Yes. The 2" x 3" size is supported in Avery Design & Print, and you can also use standard 5160-style template layouts with manual adjustment.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're creating professional-looking labels for products or organization and you're printing on light-colored surfaces. The print quality and adhesive strength justify the price.

**Skip it if** you need labels for dark containers, you're using a laser printer, or you can't afford to wait a few minutes for ink to dry before handling.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — These are the best clear labels I've tested for inkjet printing, with only the smudging issue and dark-surface limitation holding them back from perfection.
