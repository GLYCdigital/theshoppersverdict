If you own an imageCLASS MF634Cdw, MF632Cdw, or LBP612Cdw, you already know these printers are picky about toner. I've tested both OEM and third-party options, and the Canon 045 Color Pack 1 is the safe bet — but it comes at a premium. Here's what you're actually paying for.

**What's in the box**

This pack contains three separate cartridges: cyan, magenta, and yellow. Each is rated for approximately 1,200 pages based on the ISO/IEC 19798 standard. The black toner (Canon 045 Black) is sold separately, which is worth noting before you order — a common surprise for first-time buyers. Each cartridge comes individually sealed, and the packaging is straightforward Canon OEM quality.

**Real-world performance**

The print quality is where these cartridges justify their existence. Colors are rich and consistent, with no banding or streaking across solid fills. I ran a 50-page color document with mixed graphics and photos, and the output held up impressively — no color drift between pages, no faint lines developing mid-run. The chip communicates cleanly with the printer, so you get accurate toner level readings and no "non-genuine toner" warnings that plague third-party options.

One thing I noticed: these cartridges handle lighter coverage documents efficiently. If you're printing mostly text with occasional color accents, you'll likely exceed the 1,200-page rating. Heavy photo printing, though, will burn through them noticeably faster.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews isn't accidental. Multiple verified purchasers echo the same themes. One reviewer noted, "These are the only cartridges I trust in my MF634Cdw. Tried remanufactured once and got streaky prints immediately." Another long-term owner commented, "Three years and zero problems with OEM toner. The color match is perfect every time." The reliability factor is the recurring headline — people aren't just satisfied, they're loyal.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be direct: these are expensive. At roughly $70–$90 for the three-color pack, you're looking at a cost per page of about 2.5–3 cents per color page. Compatible cartridges from brands like LD or V4ink can cut that in half. The tradeoff is reliability — I've seen enough third-party horror stories (leaking toner, chip failures, uneven color) to understand why people pay the premium, but budget-conscious shoppers should weigh that risk.

Also, the 1,200-page yield is modest. If you're a high-volume color office, you'll be replacing these monthly. There's an XL version of these cartridges for some Canon models, but unfortunately not for the 045 series — 1,200 pages is the ceiling.

**Who should buy this**

- **Small business owners** printing client-facing materials where color accuracy matters — brochures, proposals, product sheets
- **Home office users** with light-to-moderate color printing who want zero troubleshooting
- **Anyone still under warranty** — using non-OEM toner can void your Canon coverage, and these printers are not cheap to service

**FAQ**

**Is this compatible with my MF644Cdw?**
No. The 045 series fits MF634Cdw, MF632Cdw, MF633Cdw, and LBP612Cdw, plus the MF634Cdw's sibling models. Check your printer's manual — the 046 series is for newer models like the MF654Cdw, and they are not interchangeable.

**How do I know when to replace each cartridge?**
Your printer will display individual toner levels for each color. The chip on each cartridge provides real-time data, so you'll get a warning well before the cartridge is actually empty. You can also check levels from the Canon PRINT app.

**Can I use just one cartridge from this pack?**
Yes, each cartridge is independently replaceable. If you run out of magenta only, you can buy a single 045 magenta cartridge. This pack just offers the convenience of having all three ready.

**Skip it if:** You're printing high volumes of color on a tight budget. The cost per page is steep, and a quality third-party option might serve you well enough — just be prepared for potential quality variance.
