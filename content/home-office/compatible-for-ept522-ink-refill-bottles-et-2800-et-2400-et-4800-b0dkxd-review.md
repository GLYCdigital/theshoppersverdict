**FAQ**

**Q: Will this void my Epson warranty?**
A: In the US, the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act prevents manufacturers from voiding warranties solely for using third-party consumables—unless they can prove the ink caused the damage. In practice, Epson would need to demonstrate that. Most users report no issues, but keep your receipt.

**Q: Can I mix this with existing OEM ink in the tanks?**
A: Yes, the dye-based formulation is compatible with Epson's ink. However, topping off partially filled tanks with a different brand can slightly alter color consistency. For best results, wait until a tank is low before refilling.

**Q: How many refills can I get from one set?**
A: Each 70ml bottle roughly equals one full tank refill for a standard EcoTank. For the ET-2800, that's about 4,500 black pages and 7,500 color pages per set, assuming standard 5% coverage. Heavy printing will reduce that.
