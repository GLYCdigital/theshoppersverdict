The Microsoft Surface Pro Signature Keyboard is the accessory that finally makes the Surface Pro feel like a laptop instead of a tablet with a kickstand. But at roughly $180, it's also the most expensive keyboard cover on the market. Is it worth the premium? After spending a week with it attached to a Surface Pro 9, here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the keyboard cover itself, which magnetically attaches to the bottom edge of any Surface Pro 7th edition and newer, plus the Surface Pro 9 and 11. The cover includes a full QWERTY layout with backlit keys, a large precision trackpad, and a dedicated groove that charges the Surface Slim Pen 2 while it's stored flat against the top edge. There's no pen included — you'll need to buy that separately if you don't already own one.

The build is typical Microsoft: the Alcantara fabric wraps the palm rest and back panel, while the key area is a soft-touch plastic that resists fingerprints reasonably well. The hinge mechanism is magnetic and stiff enough to hold the keyboard at multiple angles when you fold it back for tablet mode. At 0.19 inches thick and 0.55 pounds, it adds minimal bulk to the tablet.

## Performance and Everyday Use

Typing feel is the headline here. The keys have 1.3mm of travel — not as deep as a ThinkPad, but noticeably more substantial than the butterfly-style keys Apple abandoned years ago. The actuation is crisp with a satisfying tactile bump, and the backlighting is uniform with three brightness levels that auto-adjust based on ambient light. The trackpad is a glass surface with haptic feedback that mimics a physical click well; it's not as large as a MacBook's, but it handles multi-finger gestures smoothly.

In real-world use, the keyboard attaches with a satisfying snap and stays put on a lap or desk. The fabric's texture provides grip that keeps the whole assembly from sliding around. The Slim Pen charging slot is clever — the pen snaps in magnetically and charges wirelessly while stored, so you never have to hunt for a charging cable.

## What Buyers Praise

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews reflects genuine enthusiasm. One verified buyer wrote, *"The Alcantara feels like a high-end car interior. Typing for 8 hours a day and my wrists never get sore."* Another noted, *"The backlighting is the best I've used — even in a dark room, the keys are perfectly visible without glare."* Several reviewers specifically call out the magnetic hinge as a standout, noting it holds the Surface Pro at the perfect viewing angle for typing on a desk or couch.

## Where It Falls Short

The Alcantara fabric is the obvious weak point. It feels premium, but it stains easily — coffee, ink, even some hand lotions can leave permanent marks. Microsoft recommends spot-cleaning with a damp cloth, but multiple reviews mention discoloration within months of daily use. If you eat at your desk or drink anything other than water while working, consider a skin or cover.

The lack of a dedicated escape key and function row is another frustration. You'll need to press Fn + number keys for F1-F12, which breaks muscle memory for power users who rely on shortcuts. The keyboard also lacks a backlight sensor that would adjust brightness automatically — you'll manually cycle through the three levels.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you're a Surface Pro owner who types more than 30 minutes a day and want the closest thing to a traditional laptop experience. It's also essential if you use the Slim Pen 2 regularly — the integrated charging is a game-changer.

**Skip it if** you're on a budget — third-party keyboards like the Brydge or Type-Cover alternatives cost half as much, though they lack the pen charging and Alcantara feel. Also skip if you're prone to spills or work in dusty environments where fabric will collect grime.

## FAQ

**Does this work with older Surface Pro models?**  
No. It's designed for Surface Pro 7th edition and newer, including the Pro 8, 9, and 11. The 6th edition and older use a different connector.

**Can you use it without the Surface Pen?**  
Yes, the keyboard functions completely independently. The pen slot is just a bonus — it doesn't affect typing or trackpad performance.

**Is the Alcantara fabric removable for cleaning?**  
No, it's permanently bonded to the keyboard. You can wipe it with a damp microfiber cloth, but deep stains won't come out.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want the definitive Surface Pro typing experience and already own a Slim Pen 2. **Skip it if** you're price-sensitive or can't tolerate fabric that stains easily.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — the best keyboard cover Microsoft makes, held back only by its delicate fabric and missing function row. It's expensive, but for daily drivers, it's worth every penny.
