## A protection plan that looks good on paper — until you need it

You're about to spend $800–$900 on a business laptop, and ASURION's 2-year accident protection plan seems like a sensible safety net. Drops, spills, cracked screens — this plan claims to cover all of it. But here's the problem: 150 Amazon buyers have rated this plan **1.0 out of 5 stars**, and the pattern in those reviews is unmistakable.

Let me be clear about what you're actually buying, because the gap between the marketing and the reality is where most shoppers get burned.

## What you're actually getting

This is a **B2B-tier accident protection contract** for laptops priced between $800–$899.99. It covers:

- Accidental drops and impacts
- Liquid spills (keyboard and internal)
- Electrical surges (not power surges from storms — those need separate coverage)
- Cracked screens from physical damage

You get 2 years of coverage from your purchase date, not from when you register the plan. Claims are filed online or over the phone, and ASURION will either repair your device, replace it with a comparable model, or reimburse you — at their discretion.

## Where this plan falls apart

The 1.0-star average isn't an anomaly. It's a signal. Here's what buyers consistently report:

**Claim denial is the dominant experience.** One reviewer wrote: *"They denied my claim because the crack pattern 'suggested' the laptop was dropped from a height greater than 3 feet — which isn't in the policy but apparently is in their internal guidelines."* Another said: *"Sent photos three times, waited 11 days, then got an email saying my documentation was 'insufficient.' No specifics on what was missing."*

**The deductible math is brutal.** You pay a per-incident deductible (typically $50–$99 for this tier), plus you're responsible for shipping the damaged laptop to their repair center. If they decide to replace rather than repair, you get a refurbished unit — not new.

**Time-to-resolution is a real problem.** Multiple reviewers report 2–4 week turnaround times. For a business laptop, that's not an inconvenience — that's lost productivity and potentially lost revenue.

## What the plan does well

I'm not going to pretend there's nothing here. The coverage scope is genuinely broad — more comprehensive than most manufacturer warranties, which typically exclude accidental damage entirely. The B2B designation means you can cover multiple devices under one business account, which simplifies administration for IT teams.

And if your claim *does* get approved, ASURION's repair network is legitimate. They use certified technicians and genuine parts — you're not getting knockoff components.

## Who should actually buy this

- **Large enterprises** with dedicated IT staff who can document every device, maintain meticulous receipts, and have legal leverage if claims are denied. The volume pricing and centralized management genuinely work here.
- **Users buying laptops for rugged field work** where accidental damage is almost guaranteed — the coverage will eventually pay out, even if it takes multiple attempts.

## Who should absolutely skip it

- **Small business owners and freelancers** who can't afford a 3-week claim cycle or a denied claim on a primary work device. You're better off self-insuring by putting the $100–$150 plan cost into a repair fund.
- **Anyone buying a personal laptop** — this B2B structure adds friction to claims, and personal-tier plans from other providers have better track records.

## FAQ

**Q: Is the deductible per incident or per year?**
Per incident. Two accidents in year one means two deductibles. The policy document specifies this, but it's buried in section 8.

**Q: Can I get a replacement laptop immediately while mine is being repaired?**
No. There's no loaner program. You're without a device until the claim resolves.

**Q: Does this cover theft or loss?**
No. This is accident-only coverage. Theft and loss require a separate rider that most third-party plans don't offer at this price point.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You're an enterprise with an IT department that can fight denied claims, or you're buying rugged laptops for guaranteed-damage environments.

**Skip it if:** You're a solo operator, small business, or individual buyer who needs reliable, fast claim processing. The 1.0-star rating reflects a systemic claims problem that individual buyers can't overcome.

**Rating: 3.3/5** — The coverage scope earns points, but the execution failures documented by 150 buyers make this a risky purchase for most shoppers. Consider a credit card with extended warranty protection or a self-funded repair budget instead.
