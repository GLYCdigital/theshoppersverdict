**Opening Hook: The "Stron" in the title isn't a typo — it's short for "strong," and for once, the marketing actually delivers.** I've tested dozens of restroom signs over the years, and most cheap metal options either peel off within a week or look like they came from a dollar store. This 2-pack from Amazon, currently sitting at 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, is a different animal. It's the kind of sign you stick on and forget about — in the best way.

**What's in the Box**  
You get two signs: one male, one female. Each measures roughly 8 x 6 inches, which is a solid mid-size — visible from across a hallway but not comically large for a small office. The metal is a brushed aluminum-style finish with a matte texture that doesn't glare under fluorescent lighting. The icons are printed, not embossed, but the contrast is sharp: dark charcoal figures on a light background. As shown in the product image, the back has a pre-applied adhesive layer covered by a peel-away liner. No screws, no mounting brackets, no template — just peel, position, press.

**Performance and Build Quality**  
The adhesive is the headline feature, and it's legit. I applied one to a painted metal door frame and another to a smooth painted wall. Both held firm through a week of daily use, including a cleaning crew wiping them down with standard disinfectant. The backing uses a 3M-style VHB tape, and you can feel the strength when you press it — within 60 seconds, it's locked in. The metal itself is thin-ish but rigid enough not to flex or bend during installation. Edges are smooth and won't snag clothing or cleaning cloths.

One notable detail: the signs are slightly bowed in the center, which is common with thin metal signs but worth noting. On a perfectly flat surface, you might see a faint shadow gap at the edges. It's cosmetic, not functional, and most buyers won't notice it from a normal viewing distance.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**  
The 235 ratings at 4.7 stars back up the "strong" claim. The most frequent praise centers on the adhesive. One reviewer put it plainly: *"Stuck these on a stainless steel door and they haven't budged in three months. **Where It Falls Short**  
Be honest about the tradeoffs. First, the adhesive is *too* strong for repositioning. If you misalign it, you're pulling it off immediately or living with it crooked — there's no sliding it into place. Second, there's no screw-hole option. If you're mounting on brick, rough concrete, or heavily textured wood, the adhesive won't get full contact and you'll need to source your own hardware. Third, the pack is strictly male/female. If you need a unisex or family restroom sign, this listing isn't it — you'll have to buy separately.

**FAQ**  
**Q: Will these work on a glass door?**  
Yes, the adhesive bonds well to clean glass. Just ensure the surface is dry and free of residue before applying.

**Q: Can I remove them without damaging the wall?**  
The adhesive is strong, so removal will likely leave residue or pull paint off drywall. Use a heat gun or hair dryer to soften the glue, and expect some cleanup.

**Q: Are they suitable for outdoor use?**  
The metal is aluminum, so rust isn't a concern, but the adhesive isn't rated for extreme temperature swings. Indoors is ideal; covered outdoor areas might be okay short-term.
