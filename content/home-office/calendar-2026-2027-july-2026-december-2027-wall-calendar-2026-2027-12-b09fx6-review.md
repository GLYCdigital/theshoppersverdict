The 18-month span is the real selling point here. ## Where It Falls Short

Honest tradeoffs exist. There's no hanging hardware included — no pre-punched holes or adhesive strips. You'll need your own clips, hooks, or mounting putty. For a product in this price range, that omission feels like a minor cost-cutting move that shifts the burden to you. Also, the spiral top binding, while fine for flipping, can snag if you hang the calendar too close to a shelf or another framed item. One reviewer mentioned needing to leave a few inches of clearance on either side to avoid catching the wire.
