Let me be straight with you: most kids’ chore charts are flimsy paper printables or magnetic sheets that curl after a week. The PigPotParty Acrylic Magnetic Chore Chart is neither. It’s a 12.5 x 9.5-inch rigid acrylic panel with a full magnetic back, and it genuinely feels like a grown-up product — because it’s built like one. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it’s clearly working for families. Here’s the real breakdown after digging into what buyers actually say.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the acrylic chart, 24 task magnets (pre-printed with chores like “Brush Teeth,” “Make Bed,” “Homework,” and “Tidy Toys”), 2 blank magnets, a dry-erase marker, and an eraser. The design is clean: a “To Do” column on the left, a “Done” column on the right, and a reward tracker strip at the bottom. As shown in the product image, it’s color-coded and kid-friendly without being cartoonish — which means it won’t clash with your kitchen decor.

The key spec here is the 3mm thickness. That’s what separates this from the $10 vinyl sheets. It doesn’t bend, doesn’t warp near heat, and the magnetic backing is full-surface — not just strips. One reviewer put it simply: *“Stuck it on the fridge three months ago, hasn't moved an inch.”*

## Performance and Real-World Use

The dry-erase surface is the star. Multiple parents noted that even after weeks of daily use, it wipes clean without ghosting. That’s rare — most cheap acrylics stain within days. The magnets are strong enough for little hands to move without frustration, but not so strong that a toddler can’t slide them.

The reward system is simple: kids move a magnet from “To Do” to “Done,” and when they hit a pre-set goal, they earn a star on the tracker. It’s visual and immediate, which is exactly what works for ages 3–8. Parents of older kids (9–12) said the blank magnets let them customize for homework and reading time.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

- **Durability:** *“Survived two kids and a move. Still looks brand new.”*
- **Visibility:** It’s magnetic, so it lives on the fridge — not hidden in a drawer like sticker charts.

## Where It Falls Short

The included marker is genuinely bad — thin, faint, and it dries out fast. Plan to buy a proper set of fine-tip dry-erase markers for $6. Also, you only get 2 blank magnets. If you want a fully customized chart (e.g., “Feed Dog,” “Practice Piano”), you’ll need to buy additional blank magnets separately. Minor, but worth knowing.

One more honest note: the acrylic is glossy, so it shows fingerprints. On a fridge, that’s unavoidable. Wipe it with a microfiber cloth and move on.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Parents of 3–8 year olds** who want a visual, no-fuss chore system that actually sticks to the fridge.
- **Families with multiple kids** — the magnets are color-coded, so you can assign each child a color.
