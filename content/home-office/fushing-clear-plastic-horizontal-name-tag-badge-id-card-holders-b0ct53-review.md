You've bought cheap badge holders before. They arrived cloudy, split at the seam by week two, and made your ID look like it was laminated in 1987. The Fushing Clear Plastic Horizontal Name Tag Holders are not that product — but they're also not the premium option. Here's where they actually land.

**What you get**

The package includes 50 horizontal holders, each sized for the standard 3.375" x 2.125" ID card. The PVC material is 0.5mm thick — thin enough to stay flexible, thick enough to survive daily use. Each holder has a reinforced top seam and a pre-punched slot that fits standard badge reels and lanyard clips. As shown in the product image, the horizontal orientation makes them ideal for office buildings where badge readers are mounted at chest height.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The standout feature is the clarity. These holders are genuinely transparent — not the hazy, frosted plastic you get with budget packs. One Amazon reviewer noted: *"I've had these for three months and they still look brand new. No yellowing, no scratches from sliding my badge across the reader."* That matches the 4.7-star average across 235 ratings.

The reinforced top seam addresses the most common failure point. I've seen cheaper holders split within days when users yank them off badge readers. These hold up to that abuse. However, the thin PVC means they flex noticeably when attached to a lanyard — if you're someone who fidgets with your badge, expect some bending.

**Where they fall short**

The biggest usability issue is glare. Under direct overhead office lighting, the smooth PVC creates a reflective sheen that can make badges hard to scan. If your building uses older optical scanners rather than RFID readers, you may occasionally need to tilt the badge to get a clean read.

The thinness is a double-edged sword. While it keeps the holders flexible and prevents cracking, it also means they don't have the rigid, premium feel of thicker polycarbonate options. If you're looking for a holder that feels substantial in hand, these aren't it.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will these fit badge reels or only lanyards?**
A: Both. The pre-punched slot at the top accepts standard badge reel clips and lanyard hooks. Just note the slot is narrow — it won't fit oversized carabiner-style clips.

**Q: How do I insert the card without creasing it?**
A: Slide the card in from the top open edge, starting with one corner and working the rest in gradually. The opening is snug by design — this prevents cards from sliding out accidentally.

**Q: Are these reusable after removing the card?**
A: Yes. The PVC doesn't stretch permanently when you remove a card, so you can swap badges as needed. Just don't force a card that's slightly oversized.
