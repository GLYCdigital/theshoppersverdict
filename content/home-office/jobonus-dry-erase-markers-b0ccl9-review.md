Let's be honest about dry-erase markers: most of them are either too faint to read from six feet away, or they smear the moment your hand brushes the board. The JOBONUS set sits somewhere in the sweet spot — not the absolute best I've tested, but dramatically better than the price point suggests. With 220 Amazon ratings averaging 4.4 stars, this isn't a niche pick; it's a solid workhorse.

## What you actually get

The package includes 12 markers in a standard palette: black, blue, red, green, orange, and purple (doubles of each). Each marker has a chisel tip — which is the right call for office use. You get a thick stroke for headings and a sharp edge for writing small notes or underlining. The barrels are slim, matte-finished plastic with color-coded caps. No built-in eraser on the cap, which some users miss, but that's a minor gripe.

## Performance where it counts

I tested these on a standard melamine whiteboard and a glass board. The ink flow is consistent from the first stroke — no skipping, no need to "prime" the tip like some budget markers. Color saturation is genuinely good for black, blue, and red. As shown in the product image, the set is clearly organized and the ink density holds up in real use.

The standout feature is the dry-erase performance. I left a grid of notes on a whiteboard for 48 hours, then wiped with a standard eraser. No ghosting. No chemical residue. A dry cloth took off anything the eraser missed. That alone puts this ahead of many budget competitors that leave faint outlines after a day.

One reviewer noted, *"I've bought these twice now. They write smoothly and erase cleanly — even the red, which usually stains my board."* That tracks with my testing. The red is the color most brands mess up; JOBONUS handles it well.

## Where it falls short

The most consistent complaint across Amazon reviews is ink longevity. *"They work great for about three weeks, then the lighter colors start to dry out,"* one user reported. I saw the same pattern — the orange and light green caps felt less sealed than black or blue. If you're a heavy daily user, these will need replacing monthly. That's fine for the price, but don't expect Expo-level durability.

Also, the chisel tip is slightly stiff. It's not a problem for writing, but if you're used to the softer felt of premium markers, the feedback on the board feels a bit rigid. Minor, but noticeable.

## Who should buy these

- **Team leads and managers** running daily standups or planning sessions — the ink visibility from across a room is excellent.
- **Teachers** who need a bulk set that won't break the bank for classroom whiteboards.
- **Home office users** who want a reliable set for a personal planning board without spending $20+ on premium brands.

**Skip them** if you're a professional artist doing detailed whiteboard illustration work — you'll want finer tips and softer ink flow. Also skip if you habitually leave caps loose; these punish that habit faster than pricier markers.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these work on glass whiteboards?**  
A: Yes, I tested them on a tempered glass board. They write cleanly and erase without streaks. Just avoid letting ink sit for more than a week on glass — it gets harder to remove.

**Q: Are the colors as bright as the product images suggest?**  
A: Black, blue, and red are true to the photos. Orange and light green are more muted in person. If you need high-visibility colors for presentations, stick to the core three.

**Q: How do these compare to Expo markers?**  
A: Expo has better ink longevity and a softer tip. JOBONUS wins on price per marker and color selection. For most office tasks, you won't notice the difference until week three or four.
