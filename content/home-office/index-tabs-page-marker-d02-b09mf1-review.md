## What You're Actually Getting

The D02 comes as a single plastic dispenser loaded with tabs in five neon colors (pink, yellow, green, blue, orange). Each tab measures roughly 1.5 inches long by 0.75 inches wide — noticeably larger than standard flags. The dispenser has a serrated edge for clean tearing, and the tabs have a clear film section that leaves text visible underneath while the colored portion sticks out from the page edge.

As shown in the product image, the design is straightforward: pull, tear, stick. No fiddly backing paper to peel, which is a genuine time-saver when you're mid-research and don't want to break focus.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the D02 separates itself from the cheap stuff. The adhesive is a medium-tack formula — strong enough to stay put on a textbook you're hauling in a backpack, but repositionable if you misplace a tab. One reviewer noted, "I've had these on a paperback for three weeks and they haven't curled or fallen off, even with daily flipping." That matches my testing: tabs survived repeated page turns without sliding, and when I deliberately removed one, it came off clean with zero residue.

The writeable surface is the standout feature. Unlike standard flags where you're scrawling a single letter with a fine-tip pen, the D02 gives you room for a short phrase like "budget" or "key quote." Ballpoint works, gel pens work, and — impressively — the tabs don't smudge immediately after writing.

## Where It Falls Short

Be honest about the cons. **The quantity is the biggest gripe.** You get one dispenser with roughly 100 tabs (about 20 per color), not a bulk pack. For students marking an entire semester's readings, that's a reorder in a few weeks. There's no refill option listed, so you're buying a whole new dispenser each time.

Second, the neon colors are bright — arguably too bright for dense academic texts. On a page with small print, the colored portion can visually compete with the text you're trying to highlight. It's fine for most users, but if you're a minimalist who wants subtle flags, look elsewhere.

Third, the tabs are not fully transparent. The colored section is opaque, so if you stick it directly over a line of text, you'll obscure it. Stick to the edge as intended.
