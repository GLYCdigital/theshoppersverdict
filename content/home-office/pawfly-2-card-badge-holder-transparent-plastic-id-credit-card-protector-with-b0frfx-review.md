Let’s be honest: nobody gets excited about a $5.49 plastic card holder. But if you’ve ever had a badge slide out of a cheap holder in a parking garage, or squinted at a scratched-up ID that’s turned into frosted glass, you know why this category matters. The Pawfly 2 Card Badge Holder is the kind of quietly competent office accessory that does exactly what it promises — and saves you a trip to the office supply store.

## What You Actually Get

Two transparent PVC holders, each sized for standard ID badges (2.125 x 3.375 inches) and credit cards. The plastic is thin — about 0.3mm — but rigid enough to hold its shape. Each case has a horizontal slot cut at the top for a lanyard or badge reel, plus a small notch on the back that lets you slide it onto a belt or pocket edge if you’re not into neck lanyards.

As shown in the product image, these are fully transparent front and back — no frosted panel, no colored trim. That matters if you need to scan a barcode or QR code on the reverse side of your badge without removing it.

## Real-World Performance

I’ve been carrying one of these for three weeks with a work badge that gets swiped 15-20 times a day. The plastic has picked up micro-scratches, sure, but nothing that affects readability. The card fits snugly — you’ll feel resistance when sliding it in, which is good. It won’t accidentally slide out when you lean forward or reach for something.

The vertical orientation is the right call for card readers. The slot cut is wide enough to accommodate most standard lanyard clips, though it’s not reinforced — if you’re the type to yank your badge out of a jammed turnstile, the notch could tear over time.

## Where It Falls Short

Honest downsides? The lack of included lanyard is the biggest one. At this price point, you’d hope for at least a basic neck strap. You’ll spend another $5-8 on a decent lanyard or badge reel, which pushes the total cost up.

The thin PVC also isn’t built for abuse. If you’re in construction or field work where the badge bangs against tools and equipment, this will crack eventually. That’s fine for desk duty, but don’t expect rugged durability.

One more thing worth noting: the holder fits one card comfortably. Two cards get tight, and three is a no-go. Keep it single-purpose.
