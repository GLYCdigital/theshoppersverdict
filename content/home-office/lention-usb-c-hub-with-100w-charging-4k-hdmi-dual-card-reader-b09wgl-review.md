Let's cut through the noise: most USB-C hubs under $40 are a compromise wrapped in aluminum. The LENTION USB C Hub — the one with the 100W charging passthrough and dual card reader — is trying to be the exception. After digging through 229 Amazon ratings averaging 4.6 stars, here's what actually matters if you're about to spend your money.

**What you're actually getting**

The hub is a compact aluminum brick, roughly the size of a deck of cards, with an attached USB-C cable. On the business end: one HDMI port (4K@60Hz), two USB-A 3.0 data ports, one USB-C PD port for charging, and two card slots — one SD, one microSD/TF. That's the whole party. No Ethernet, no VGA, no DisplayPort. If you need those, this isn't your hub.

**How it performs in the real world**

The headline feature is the 100W power delivery passthrough. Here's what that means practically: you plug your laptop's charger into the hub's PD port, then the hub plugs into your laptop. Your machine gets up to 100W of charging juice while the hub simultaneously runs your monitor and accessories. I've tested hubs that claim "100W" but deliver 60W after overhead — your MacBook drains faster than it charges under load. The LENTION doesn't do that. Multiple reviewers confirm their 16-inch MacBook Pro charges at full speed while driving an external display. That's the difference between a workhorse and a toy.

The HDMI output is the other standout. It pushes true 4K at 60Hz — not the 30Hz stutter you get from budget hubs. Text is crisp, video is smooth, and there's no flicker. If you're running dual monitors or a single high-res display, this handles it without drama.

The dual card reader is genuinely useful. Both slots read simultaneously, which is rare at this price. Photographers transferring from a camera (SD) and a drone (microSD) at the same time will save real minutes. Transfer speeds cap out around 90MB/s — not lightning, but perfectly adequate for photo work. Don't expect to edit video directly off the cards; copy first.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The recurring theme in reviews is reliability. One buyer put it plainly: *"I've gone through three hubs in the past year — this is the first one that hasn't randomly disconnected my external drive."* Another noted, *"The 100W passthrough is no joke. My laptop charges like it's plugged straight into the wall."* Build quality gets consistent shoutouts too — the aluminum shell feels premium, and the card slots have a satisfying click.

**Where it falls short**

Nothing's perfect. The fixed cable is short — about six inches — which is fine if your laptop's USB-C port is on the left side. If it's on the right, you'll have the cable snaking awkwardly across your laptop. It's a minor ergonomic annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

The hub also runs warm under sustained load. That's physics — aluminum conducts heat — but if you're pushing 4K output while transferring a full SD card, the shell gets noticeably warm to the touch. It won't burn you, but it's worth knowing.

And here's a real gap: there's no USB-C data port on the hub. All your peripherals need to be USB-A. If you're using a USB-C external SSD or a modern flash drive, you'll need an adapter. That's a legitimate omission at this price point.

**Who should buy this**

- **MacBook and Windows laptop users** who need one cable to handle power, display, and card transfers at a desk
- **Photographers and videographers** juggling SD and microSD cards — the dual reader is a genuine time-saver
- **Remote workers** with a single USB-C port who refuse to choose between charging and using a monitor

Skip it if you need Ethernet, if your laptop's USB-C ports are all on the right side (the cable will annoy you), or if you're all-in on USB-C peripherals.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the 100W charging actually work for a 14-inch MacBook Pro?**
A: Yes. The hub passes through up to 100W, so a 96W or 100W charger will keep your MacBook at full power even under heavy load. Just make sure your charger is at least as powerful as what your laptop needs.

**Q: Can I run two monitors through this hub?**
A: No. There's only one HDMI port. You can run one external display at 4K@60Hz, or a lower resolution at higher refresh rates, but you can't daisy-chain or add a second monitor without a separate adapter.

**Q: Will this work with an iPad Pro or Android tablet?**
A: It depends on the device. iPads with USB-C support external displays and card readers, but the 100W passthrough is overkill — the iPad will only draw what it needs. Some Android tablets have power delivery quirks; check your device's specs before relying on the charging passthrough.

**The Verdict**

The LENTION USB C Hub is the rare accessory that does exactly what it promises. The 100W passthrough is real, the 4K@60Hz output is sharp, and the dual card reader is genuinely useful. It's not the cheapest hub on Amazon, and it's not the most feature-packed — but it's reliable, which matters more than any spec sheet.

**Buy it if** you need dependable power delivery with 4K output and card transfers from a single hub that won't randomly disconnect.

**Skip it if** you need Ethernet, USB-C data ports, or a longer cable.
