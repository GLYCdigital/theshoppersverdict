## Where It Falls Short

Honest tradeoffs exist. The cover is flexible cardstock, not rigid board — if you're shoving these into a backpack with textbooks, expect bent corners within a week. A hardcover option would solve this, but it would also raise the price.

The pages aren't numbered, and there's no table of contents or index. For simple note-taking, that's irrelevant. For long-term project tracking or a serious bullet journal setup, you'll need to add your own numbering system. Some buyers also mention that the ruling is slightly narrower than standard college rule — about 7mm vs. the typical 7.5mm — which takes a day to adjust to if you write large.
