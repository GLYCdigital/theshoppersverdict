The Lorell Fortress Series Double Pedestal Desk is the kind of office furniture that means business. At 60" x 30", it's a proper executive desk with a steel frame and walnut laminate top. With only 14 Amazon reviews, this is a niche product — but those reviews tell a clear story.

## The Foundation
When a desk from the Lorell Fortress Series arrives, you're getting commercial-grade weight. The all-steel frame and double pedestal design provide rock-solid stability — no wobble, no flex. The walnut top gives it a professional look that fits both home offices and corporate settings.

Assembly is reasonable for a desk this size. "Item was reasonably easy to assemble and it is sturdy," one reviewer notes. The built-in drawers include a locking center drawer, adding security for sensitive documents.

## The Shipping Risk
The major red flag is delivery damage. At 60" x 30", this is a massive, heavy desk that ships in multiple boxes. Multiple reviewers report dents, scratches, and chipped laminate on arrival. "Product delivered with significant damage. Dents, scratches and chips in the laminate," one reviewer writes. "The desk is very heavy and will be a hassle to return."

At .99, this is a significant investment, and the shipping damage issue is real. If you order, make sure you're prepared to inspect and potentially return a heavy item.

## The Verdict
The Lorell Fortress Double Pedestal Desk is a quality piece of furniture when it arrives undamaged. The steel frame and double pedestal build is commercial-grade, and the walnut top looks great. But at only 14 reviews, the data is thin, and the shipping damage pattern is concerning. If you need a serious executive desk and can inspect delivery thoroughly, this is a solid choice. Otherwise, look at options with more buyer history.
