Let's cut through the noise: the JETech case for the 12.9-inch iPad Pro is the budget option everyone recommends, and for good reason. But it's not without compromises. Here's what 215 Amazon reviewers (averaging 4.3 stars) actually think, and what you should know before you click buy.

## What's in the box

You get the case itself — no screen protector, no extras. The front cover is a textured polyurethane material that feels surprisingly premium for the price point. The back is a hard polycarbonate shell with a soft-touch coating that resists fingerprints. As shown in the product image, the case is slim — it adds minimal bulk to an already thin tablet.

## Build quality and real-world use

The fit is the star here. JETech nailed the cutouts: the speakers, charging port, and camera bump all align perfectly. The case clicks onto the iPad Pro with a satisfying snap, and the corners hold firm. One reviewer noted, "The fit is perfect. Snaps on tight, no wiggle." That sentiment echoes across the ratings.

The magnetic closure is strong enough to keep the cover shut in a bag, and the auto wake/sleep function works reliably — a feature that's hit-or-miss on cheaper cases. The front cover folds into a single triangle stand position that works fine for watching video or FaceTime calls. But that's the only angle you get. There's no typing wedge or adjustable kickstand. If you plan to type extensively, this is a real limitation.

The Apple Pencil 2 support is a highlight. The case leaves the right side of the iPad completely exposed, so your Pencil snaps onto the magnetic charging strip without obstruction. The front cover also has a small flap to hold the Pencil in place when closed — a thoughtful touch.

## What buyers consistently praise

The value proposition is the recurring theme. One verified purchase put it plainly: "Does everything the $100 Apple case does for a fraction of the price." Others mention the slim profile and how it doesn't add significant weight to an already heavy 12.9-inch tablet.

## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The front cover doesn't wrap around the edges of the screen — it just lies flat on top. That means if you drop the iPad face-down, the edges of the screen are vulnerable. It's not a protective case; it's a convenience case.

The rigid back panel has no shock absorption. Drop protection is minimal. A few reviewers mentioned scratched backs after sliding the iPad across desks. And as mentioned, the single viewing angle is a genuine drawback for productivity use.

## Who should buy this

This case is ideal for the person who wants basic protection and a stand without spending $80-100 on Apple's Smart Folio. It's perfect for students who need a lightweight case for note-taking and media consumption, or for anyone who keeps their iPad mostly on a desk and needs a reliable stand.

Skip it if you're rough on your gear, need multiple viewing angles for typing, or want military-grade drop protection. For that, you'd be better served by a rugged case like the OtterBox or a folio with a built-in keyboard.

## FAQ

**Does this case fit both the 2020 and 2018 iPad Pro 12.9-inch?**
Yes. The 3rd-gen (2018) and 4th-gen (2020) iPad Pro 12.9-inch share the same physical dimensions, so this case fits both perfectly. It will not fit older 12.9-inch models — those are different sizes entirely.

**Does the Apple Pencil 2 charge with this case on?**
Yes. The right side of the case is completely open, so the Pencil 2 charges and pairs normally. The front cover flap keeps it secure when closed.

**Is the back transparent or solid?**
Solid. It's a dark gray/black polycarbonate shell with a soft-touch finish. If you want to show off your iPad's back, this isn't the case for you.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a slim, functional case with reliable wake/sleep, solid Pencil 2 support, and a stand for media — at a price that won't hurt. It's the best budget option for the 12.9-inch iPad Pro.

**Skip it if:** You need drop protection, multiple viewing angles for typing, or edge-to-edge screen coverage. Those features cost more, and this case doesn't have them.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, honest case that does exactly what it promises, with clear limitations you can live with if you're not expecting armor.
