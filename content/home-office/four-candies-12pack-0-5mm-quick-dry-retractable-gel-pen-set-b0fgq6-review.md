Let me cut to the chase: if you're left-handed or just tired of ink smeared across your fingers and page, the Four Candies 12-pack deserves a hard look. This is a budget-friendly gel pen set that claims quick-dry performance, and for the most part, it delivers.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains 12 retractable gel pens with 0.5mm tips. As shown in the product image, they feature a sleek, minimalist barrel with a rubberized grip section and a metal clip. The retractable mechanism uses a click-top design — no caps to lose, which is a genuine convenience for desk use or tossing into a bag. The pens are on the lighter side, which some prefer for extended writing; others might find them a bit insubstantial in hand.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The 0.5mm tip strikes a nice balance between fine detail and smooth flow. On standard printer paper, the ink dries in about 2-3 seconds — not instant, but fast enough that you won't drag your hand through wet ink if you're writing at a normal pace. Left-handed writers will still need to be slightly careful with fast cursive, but this is far better than most budget gel pens.

The ink itself is consistent. I've tested cheap pens that start skipping halfway through the cartridge — not here. The line stays uniform, and the color is saturated without bleeding through 80gsm notebook paper. The retractable mechanism feels solid; no wobble in the tip, and the clip holds firmly to a pocket or notebook cover.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. The 12-pack sounds generous, but the color selection is a letdown — you're mostly getting black and blue, with maybe a red or two. If you're a color-coder for work or study, look elsewhere.

More importantly, ink longevity is a concern. Several reviewers reported the ink running out faster than expected. For a full-time student or someone who writes pages daily, expect roughly 2-3 weeks per pen. That's not terrible at this price point, but it's worth knowing before you stock up.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these pens refillable?**
A: No. The cartridges are sealed and not designed for replacement. When they're empty, you toss the whole pen. This is standard at this price point.

**Q: Will they smear on glossy or coated paper?**
A: Yes, possibly. Quick-dry works best on standard absorbent paper. On glossy surfaces, drying time increases significantly — stick to regular paper.

**Q: How does the 0.5mm tip compare to a 0.7mm?**
A: The 0.5mm produces a finer, cleaner line — better for small handwriting and detailed work. The 0.7mm is bolder and smoother but more prone to smudging with quick-dry ink.
