Let me be blunt: there are roughly 4,000 24-inch 1080p monitors on Amazon, and most of them are forgettable. The Dell S2421NX is not one of them. With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, this monitor has earned its place in the upper tier of budget office displays — but it's not perfect, and I'll tell you exactly where it stumbles.

## What You Actually Get

Out of the box, you're looking at a 23.8-inch IPS panel with a 1920x1080 resolution, 75Hz refresh rate, and AMD FreeSync support. The box includes the monitor, a tilt-adjustable stand, HDMI cable, and power cord. No DisplayPort cable — that's worth noting since the monitor only has HDMI and VGA inputs anyway.

The build quality is typical Dell: solid, matte black plastic that doesn't creak or flex. The stand snaps in without tools, and the 6.3mm bezels are genuinely thin — as shown in the product images, this thing looks modern on any desk.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where this monitor punches above its price class. The IPS panel delivers 99% sRGB coverage straight from the factory, which is rare at this price point. Colors look accurate without calibration — skin tones are natural, whites are clean, and viewing angles hold up well at 178 degrees.

One thing that surprised me: the 75Hz refresh rate with FreeSync. Most office monitors cap at 60Hz. While you're not gaming competitively on this thing, the extra 15Hz makes scrolling feel smoother and reduces eye strain during long document sessions. FreeSync over HDMI means even budget AMD laptops can take advantage of it without needing DisplayPort.

The anti-glare coating is handled well. In a bright office with overhead fluorescents, reflections are minimal — a detail many budget monitors get wrong.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

Reviewers consistently mention two things: color quality and value. One buyer noted, *"I was shocked at how accurate the colors are right out of the box. I do light photo editing and this holds up against monitors twice the price."* Another mentioned, *"The thin bezels make my dual-monitor setup look seamless — there's barely a gap between the two screens."*

Dell's warranty also gets frequent shoutouts. The 3-year advanced exchange program means if your panel dies, Dell ships a replacement before you send the old one back. That's a level of support you rarely see at this price.

## Where It Falls Short

The stand is the biggest compromise. It only tilts — no height adjustment, no swivel, no pivot. For a monitor marketed toward office use, that's a real ergonomic miss. You'll likely need to budget for a VESA arm (the 100x100mm mount is there, but you have to buy the arm yourself).

The 60Hz-75Hz refresh rate and VGA input also date this monitor slightly. If you're a gamer, even casual, skip this — there are better options with 144Hz panels for not much more. And if you need USB-C connectivity or built-in speakers, keep looking. This is a pure display, nothing more.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Office workers** who stare at spreadsheets and documents all day and want accurate colors without eye strain — this is your monitor.
- **Dual-monitor users** — the thin bezels make this ideal for multi-screen setups.
- **Budget-conscious creatives** doing light photo editing or design work who can't justify a $400+ professional panel.
- **Not for gamers** — the refresh rate and lack of gaming-focused features make this the wrong tool.

## Common Questions, Answered

**Is this monitor good for MacBooks?**
Yes, but you'll need a USB-C to HDMI adapter. No USB-C port here, so plan accordingly.

**Will FreeSync work with an NVIDIA GPU?**
Yes, but only over HDMI. NVIDIA's G-Sync compatibility works with FreeSync monitors over HDMI, though results vary by GPU generation. Check your specific card.

**Does the stand have VESA mounting?**
The monitor is VESA-compatible (100x100mm), but you must remove the stand and use your own arm or bracket. The monitor itself is VESA-ready, not the stand.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You need a reliable, color-accurate 24-inch monitor for office work, and you're willing to add a VESA arm for proper ergonomics.

**Skip it if:** You're gaming, need height-adjustable stand out of the box, or require USB-C connectivity.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The S2421NX nails the fundamentals with factory-calibrated colors and a rock-solid panel, but the limited stand and 75Hz ceiling keep it from perfection. For under $150, though, this is one of the best values in office monitors right now.
