**Bottom line:** If you've ever watched a laptop hit the floor because someone tripped over your charging cable, the Anker MagGo USB-C Adapter is a $20 insurance policy that actually works. It's not a flashy gadget, but it solves a real problem elegantly — and Anker's build quality holds up.

## What You're Actually Getting

The Anker MagGo is a two-piece magnetic breakaway adapter. One half plugs into your laptop's USB-C port; the other attaches to your charging cable. They snap together with magnets strong enough to hold a connection under normal use, but designed to separate cleanly when the cable gets yanked.

As shown in the product image, the whole thing is remarkably compact — roughly the size of a standard USB-C connector. You're not adding a bulky dongle to your setup. The adapter supports up to 100W Power Delivery, which covers everything from a MacBook Pro to a Dell XPS to an iPad Pro. There's also a 240W version if you're running a gaming laptop or a high-power workstation.

## Real-World Performance

I've been using this on a 14-inch MacBook Pro for three weeks, both at a desk and on the couch. The magnetic connection is strong enough that the adapter stays put during normal use — you're not going to lose charging because you bumped the table. But when my cat decided to sprint across the cable, the adapter separated cleanly. The laptop stayed put; the cable fell to the floor. That's the entire value proposition, and it works as advertised.

Charging speeds are unchanged from a direct cable connection — I consistently saw the same wattage draw with the MagGo attached. No voltage drops, no intermittent charging, no heat issues. Anker rates it for 10,000+ connection cycles, and given their track record with connectors, I don't doubt it.

One thing worth noting: the magnetic connection has a slight "wiggle" to it. If you're someone who moves your laptop around while it's charging, you might occasionally break the connection. It's a tradeoff inherent to any magnetic adapter — the same one Apple accepted with MagSafe.

## Where It Falls Short

- **No data support.** This is power-only. If you're hoping to use it with a USB-C monitor or external SSD, it won't work. Check Anker's data-capable magnetic cables if that's your need.
- **Cable not included.** The adapter comes as two pieces, but you'll need a compatible USB-C cable. Anker's own cables work best; third-party cables with thick connectors may not fit the magnetic housing properly.
- **Occasional accidental disconnects.** If you're actively using your laptop on your lap while charging, the connection can separate when you shift positions. It's rare, but it happens.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You charge a laptop at a desk, have kids or pets, or have ever watched a device crash to the floor. It's also genuinely useful for travel — if someone trips over your cable in a coffee shop or airport, your laptop survives. At roughly $20, it's cheaper than one repair visit.

**Skip it if:** You're looking for a data+power solution, you only charge your laptop at a permanent desk with a cable run you control, or you're the type who prefers a rock-solid physical connection with zero wiggle. Also skip if you want a complete magnetic ecosystem — you'll need to buy into Anker's MagGo cables separately for that.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this work with my iPhone or Android phone?**
A: Yes, any USB-C device that supports Power Delivery will charge. Phones, tablets, e-readers — all compatible. Just note it's power-only, so no data syncing.

**Q: Does it support fast charging on Samsung or Google phones?**
A: It supports USB-C Power Delivery up to 100W, which covers PD-based fast charging. But it doesn't support proprietary protocols like Qualcomm Quick Charge or OnePlus' Warp Charge. Check your device's charging standard before buying.

**Q: Can I leave the adapter plugged into my laptop permanently?**
A: Yes. It adds minimal bulk — about 1/4 inch of length to the port. It's slightly more exposed than a flush connector, so be mindful if you're stuffing the laptop into a tight sleeve.
