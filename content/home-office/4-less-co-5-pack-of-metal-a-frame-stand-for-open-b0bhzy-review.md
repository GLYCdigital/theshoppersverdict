If you're a realtor, property manager, or weekend flipper, you know the drill: cheap plastic A-frames crack after one season, and the "premium" ones cost as much as a tank of gas. The 4 Less Co 5-pack sits in a sweet spot — it's a metal frame priced like a disposable, but built like something that'll survive multiple listing seasons.

## What's Actually in the Box

Five steel A-frame stands, each with a clear plastic sign sleeve already attached. The frames measure 24 inches wide by 18 inches tall — the standard size for open house riders you pick up at the office supply store. Each unit folds flat and locks open with a sliding latch on the top hinge. No tools, no assembly beyond snapping the sleeve over the frame's top edge.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The frame is 14-gauge steel tubing with welded corners, not the spot-welded tabs you see on bargain brands. I've had cheap stands fail where the legs meet the crossbar — these hold firm. The black powder coating resists scratches from gravel driveways and concrete sidewalks, which is the main wear point after a season of hauling them in and out of a car.

The plastic sleeve is the weak link, and buyers confirm it. One reviewer noted, "The frame is solid, but the plastic sleeve is thin — it works fine with standard signs, but don't expect it to survive a frozen sign being shoved in." That's accurate. In normal use — slide a coroplast sign in, set it on the lawn — it's fine. If you're dealing with sub-zero temps, warm the sign up first or expect the sleeve to crack at the corners.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The value proposition is the headline. At this price point, you're getting five metal frames for roughly the cost of two at a big-box store. One verified buyer put it plainly: "Bought these for my team and they've held up through three months of daily use. The metal is thicker than I expected for the price." Another mentioned the folding mechanism specifically: "They fold flat enough to stack all five in the back of my SUV with room to spare."

## Where It Falls Short

The lack of stakes or weight bags is the biggest gap. These frames are light — about 3.5 pounds each — and a strong gust will knock them over. If you're setting up on a breezy corner lot, you'll need to buy sandbags or stake kits separately. That's an extra $10-15 per stand, which changes the value math.

The sleeve attachment is also a minor annoyance. It's a friction fit over the top bar, not a sewn or riveted pocket. If you're constantly swapping signs, the sleeve can slide sideways. A couple of zip ties through the pre-drilled holes solves it, but it's an extra step.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Newer agents** building a sign kit on a budget — this gives you five functional stands for less than one "premium" unit.
- **Property managers** who need multiple stands for open houses and don't want to cry when one walks off.
- **Weekend flippers** who want metal durability without the $40-per-unit boutique price.

Skip it if you're staging luxury listings on windy waterfront property — you'll need weighted bases regardless, and the thin sleeve won't match a high-end presentation.

## FAQ

**Q: Will these hold a standard 24x18 sign?**
A: Yes, that's the exact size they're built for. The sleeve is slightly oversized, so coroplast signs slide in easily. Larger signs won't fit without modification.

**Q: How do they handle rain and sun?**
A: The powder coating resists rust on the frame. The plastic sleeve will yellow over time with heavy UV exposure, but it's a $2 replacement part if you care about appearance.

**Q: Can I leave them out overnight?**
A: You can, but they're not theft-proof. The latch is simple — a determined person can open it in seconds. Bring them in if you're in a high-traffic area.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need multiple durable stands without spending $200+ on a set. The metal frame is genuinely solid, and the 5-pack price is the best value I've found for working agents.

**Skip it if** you're in a permanent windy location or need a premium look for luxury listings — you'll need to buy weights anyway, and the thin sleeves won't match a high-end presentation.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting half a star for the missing stakes and thin sleeve. The frame itself is a five-star value.
