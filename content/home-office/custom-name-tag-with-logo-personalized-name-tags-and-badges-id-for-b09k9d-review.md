## Where It Falls Short

Two honest tradeoffs keep this from a perfect score.

First, sizes run small. The listed 2"x3" badge measures closer to 1.8"x2.8" edge-to-edge. That's a meaningful difference if you're planning to put a lot of text on it. If you need a badge readable from across a room, order up. This isn't a dealbreaker, but it's the kind of thing that annoys people who buy expecting exact dimensions.

Second, the glossy finish is a fingerprint magnet. In high-touch environments — think healthcare, hospitality, or anywhere customers lean in close — the badge will show smudges within hours. It wipes clean easily, but you'll be doing it regularly. A matte option would solve this, but it's not currently available.

## Who Should Buy This

This badge fits three specific buyer profiles best:

You can upload your own logo file (PNG, JPG, or PDF works best). They'll include it in the digital proof so you can see exactly how it'll look before anything goes to print. Just make sure your file is high-resolution — low-res images will look soft and pixelated on the glossy finish.

**How long does shipping take?**
Most orders arrive within 7-10 business days after you approve the digital proof. The proof typically comes within 24-48 hours of ordering. If you're on a tight deadline, factor in an extra day or two for the approval step — it's worth it to catch errors.

**Is the magnetic backing strong enough for thick clothing?**
Yes — this is actually the standout feature. The magnet pair is rated to hold through multiple fabric layers. It works through hoodies, blazers, and even light jackets. Just don't expect it to hold through a heavy winter coat; no badge magnet will.
