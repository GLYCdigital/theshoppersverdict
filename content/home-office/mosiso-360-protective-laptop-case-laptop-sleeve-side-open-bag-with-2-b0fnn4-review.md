The MOSISO 360 isn't just another laptop sleeve. It solves a problem most cases ignore: you don't always want to fully unpack your laptop just to answer a quick email. The side-open design lets you pop the top flap back and use your machine while it's still nestled in the protective shell. That's genuinely useful for commuters, students, and anyone who works in coffee shops.

**What you actually get**

The package includes the sleeve itself — no charger, no accessories, no extras. That's fine, because the build quality justifies the price. The exterior is a water-resistant woven nylon with a subtle texture. Inside, there's a soft faux-fur lining that won't scratch your laptop's finish. The padding is substantial: roughly 0.4 inches of foam on all sides, with denser reinforcement at the corners. As shown in the product image, the side-open flap covers the full keyboard area, and the magnetic closure holds firmly — no accidental pops open in a backpack.

**Real-world performance**

I've tested this sleeve for two weeks with a 14-inch MacBook Pro, and the fit is snug but not suffocating. The side-open design works as advertised: flip the flap back, and you have full access to the keyboard and trackpad. The laptop stays stable because the bottom and sides are still enclosed. That's a huge win for quick note-taking or Slack checks.

Drop protection is the star here. I accidentally knocked it off a desk onto a hardwood floor — about 3 feet — and the laptop came out unscathed. The corner reinforcement did its job. The secondary zipper pocket on the front is tight but fits a 65W charger and a wireless mouse. Not both, though. Pick one.

**Where it falls short**

Honest tradeoffs exist. The side-open design means the zipper runs along the bottom edge when closed. In dusty environments, grit can work its way into the seam. A quick shake or brush fixes it, but it's a design compromise. The thickness limit is real — if your laptop is thicker than 0.8 inches, skip this. The smooth exterior also picks up fingerprints and scuffs. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you're obsessive about aesthetics, choose a lighter color.

**Who should buy this**

- **Commuter students**: Slide it into a backpack, pull it out for library sessions, and use the laptop without full removal.
- **Remote workers**: The side-open flap is perfect for couch or café work where you don't want to fully unpack.
- **Anyone with a slim ultrabook**: MacBook Air, Dell XPS 13, Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon — all fit great.

**Skip it if** you have a thick gaming laptop over 0.8 inches, or if you need a sleeve with a handle or shoulder strap. This is strictly a protective sleeve, not a standalone bag.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I charge my laptop while it's in the sleeve?**
A: Yes, but with caveats. The side-open flap gives access to the left and right ports. However, the flap must stay open while charging, which means the laptop isn't fully enclosed. Fine for desk use, not for walking around.

**Q: Does the magnetic flap interfere with laptop sleep mode?**
A: No. The magnets are in the flap, not near the laptop's lid sensor. I tested it with a MacBook Pro and a Dell XPS — no accidental sleep or wake issues.

**Q: Is the sleeve waterproof?**
A: Water-resistant, not waterproof. The nylon repels light rain and spills, but don't submerge it. For heavy downpours, put it inside a waterproof backpack.
