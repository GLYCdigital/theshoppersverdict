**What You Actually Get**

The package contains 100 individual rings, each measuring exactly 1 inch in diameter when fully opened. That’s roughly the size of a standard keyring but with a split design. The metal is nickel-plated steel — not the flimsy zinc alloy you see on cheap keychains. Each ring weighs about 3 grams, giving them a reassuring heft. You also get the expected split-ring mechanism with a snap closure, and the finish is uniformly bright silver with no visible plating flaws across the entire batch.

**Performance and Build Quality**

I ran these through three real-world tests. First, the paper test: each ring comfortably held 80 sheets of standard 20lb printer paper without any gap distortion. Pushing to 100 sheets, the ring still closed flush, though it took a bit more finger pressure. Second, the durability test: I opened and closed a single ring 50 times.
