## The A5 Refill That Finally Gets Paper Right

If you've been cycling through cheap A5 refills that feather under a fountain pen or ghost so badly you can't use both sides, this pack from B08PCL is the upgrade you've been hunting. With a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, it's clearly earning its keep in real planners — not just on a spec sheet.

## What's Actually in the Pack

You get 60 sheets of A5 (148 x 210mm) lined paper, pre-punched with 6 holes. The punch pattern is the standard A5 Filofax layout, but it also fits most personal-size ring binders and many disc-bound systems like Arc or Circa — though those need their own punch. The lines sit at a comfortable 6mm spacing, narrower than the typical 8mm you'll find in cheaper refills. That's a deliberate choice: you fit noticeably more per page without the writing feeling crowded.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The headline feature here is the 100gsm paper weight. Most budget refills hover around 70-80gsm, which is why your ink bleeds through. This paper is thick enough that my fountain pen (a medium-nib Lamy, for reference) laid down wet ink with zero bleed and only the faintest shadow on the reverse side — and I had to actively look for it.

The surface is smooth but not glossy; it gives just enough tooth for a satisfying pen glide without catching or dragging. Drying time is reasonable, though lefties should still be careful with wetter inks. The sheets are punched cleanly — no torn holes or misaligned punches — and they flip easily in a binder without catching.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

Scrolling through the reviews, one theme dominates: ink compatibility. One buyer wrote, "I use a TWSBI Eco with Noodler's ink and this is the first refill that doesn't turn my writing into a blurry mess." Another noted, "The paper is thick enough that I can use both sides with a gel pen — that's rare at this price."

The hole punch alignment also gets consistent shout-outs. As one reviewer put it, "Every sheet fits my Filofax perfectly. No trimming, no fighting the binder rings." That's a small thing that makes a huge difference over the course of a year.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be blunt: 60 sheets is thin for the price point. If you're a daily journaler or heavy note-taker, that's roughly a month of use — then you're reordering. Some users also note the paper has a slight cream tint rather than bright white. It's not yellow, but if you're used to stark white paper, you'll notice it. It's actually easier on the eyes for long writing sessions, but it's worth knowing before you buy. A few reviewers mentioned the lines are a touch lighter than they'd prefer, though that's arguably a feature if you like a cleaner page.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You use fountain pens, gel pens, or any wet ink and are tired of bleed-through. You run an A5 Filofax or similar 6-ring binder and want a reliable, consistent refill that just works. You're someone who values paper feel and doesn't want to compromise on every page you write.

**Skip it if:** You're on a strict budget and write a lot — the cost per sheet adds up. You prefer pure white paper or wider rule lines. You use a disc-bound system and don't want to deal with a separate punch.
