## Where It Falls Short

Honest tradeoffs you need to know before buying:

**No interior pockets.** If you're someone who tucks loose sheets, business cards, or sticky notes into binder pockets, these have none. Zero. You'll need to hole-punch everything or keep a separate folder system. Several reviewers mentioned this as their only gripe.

**Fingerprint magnet.** Clear PVC shows every smudge, every fingerprint, every dust particle. As one reviewer put it, "They look great on day one, but after a week of handling, they need a wipe-down to look presentable." Not a dealbreaker, but if you're handing these to clients, keep a microfiber cloth nearby.

**Limited capacity tolerance.** While rated for 300 sheets, the rings get tight around 250. Overstuffing makes the covers bow slightly. Stay under 200 for optimal flatness.
