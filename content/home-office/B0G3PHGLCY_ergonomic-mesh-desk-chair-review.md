### Assembly

Assembly is reported as straightforward with included tools. Expect:

1. Attach backrest to seat frame (4–6 bolts)
2. Insert gas cylinder into base
3. Mount casters onto base legs
4. Slide seat assembly onto gas cylinder
5. Flip armrests into position

Total time: approximately 20–30 minutes.
