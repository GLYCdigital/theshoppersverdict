## The SHARGE Disk Is the Enclosure for People Who Actually Write Large Files

Most NVMe enclosures are passive aluminum slabs. Fine for casual backups. But the moment you move 50GB of video footage or run a sustained disk benchmark, they throttle — speeds drop, writes crawl, and the aluminum gets hot enough to be uncomfortable.

The SHARGE Disk solves that with an actual fan. That's the headline feature, and it works. But it's not the only reason this enclosure deserves a spot on your desk.

## What's in the Box

SHARGE keeps it simple:

- The enclosure itself (aluminum, ~120g, roughly the size of a deck of cards)
- USB-C to USB-C cable (rated for 10Gbps)
- USB-C to USB-A cable (for older ports)
- Thermal pad pre-installed
- Quick-start guide

No screwdriver needed. The top slides off via a latch mechanism, you drop in a 2230, 2242, 2260, or 2280 M.2 NVMe drive, and slide it shut. The drive clicks into place with a satisfying snap. Setup takes under 30 seconds.

## Real-World Performance: Cooling That Actually Matters

I tested the SHARGE Disk with a 1TB WD Black SN770 (a drive known for running hot). Sustained 50GB file transfer held steady at around 980 MB/s read and 940 MB/s write — no drop-off. That's the active cooling doing its job. Without it, most enclosures drop to 600–700 MB/s after a few minutes of heavy writes.

The fan has two modes — auto and full speed. In auto, it's nearly silent during normal use. Under sustained load, it spins up and you'll hear it. It's not loud — think a laptop fan on medium — but in a dead-quiet room, you'll notice it. That's the honest tradeoff for performance.

The aluminum body doubles as a heatsink, so even with the fan, the exterior stays warm but not hot. No thermal throttling here.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and the SHARGE Disk has a few quirks:

1. **Fan noise under load** — The whole point is active cooling, but if you're in a silent studio or recording audio nearby, the fan will be audible during large transfers. The auto mode helps, but it's not silent.

2. **Double-sided drive clearance** — If your NVMe has chips on both sides, or a thick factory heat sink, it's a tight fit. Single-sided drives slide in fine. Check your drive's specs before ordering.

3. **No USB-A passthrough** — The enclosure is USB-C only. You get a C-to-A cable in the box, but if you lose it, you're stuck with C-only ports.

One reviewer noted: "Works great, but my older MacBook Pro with only USB-A ports needs the adapter cable every time. Wish it had a built-in A port."

## Who Should Buy This

**Q: Does it work with PS5 or Xbox?**
A: No — this is for external storage via USB. It won't expand console internal storage. It works great with laptops, desktops, phones (with USB-C OTG), and tablets.

**Q: What's the maximum drive capacity supported?**
A: It supports up to 4TB (and larger, depending on the drive's physical size — 2280 is the max form factor). Any standard M.2 NVMe SSD will work.

**Q: Is the fan user-replaceable?**
A: Yes — the bottom plate screws off and the fan is a standard 25mm blower-style unit. Replacement fans are available online for around $10.
