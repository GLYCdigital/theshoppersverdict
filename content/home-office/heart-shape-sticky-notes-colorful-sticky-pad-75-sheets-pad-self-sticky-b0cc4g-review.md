Some products are pure function. These heart-shaped sticky notes are pure personality with enough utility to justify the desk space. After 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, the consensus is clear: these are genuinely good sticky notes hiding behind a gimmicky shape. I've tested dozens of note pads over the years, and here's what actually matters about this one.

**What you're actually getting**

Six pads, 75 sheets each — that's 450 total notes. The "S" in the product name refers to the size, which runs about 2.5 inches across at the widest point. That's smaller than a standard 3x3 sticky note, so adjust your expectations for how much you can write. The paper weight is solid — heavier than the flimsy sheets on dollar-store pads — and the colors are genuinely vibrant: pink, yellow, green, blue, orange, and purple. As shown in the product image, they come stacked neatly in a clear plastic sleeve that keeps the pads from getting crushed in transit.

**How they actually perform**

The adhesive is the make-or-break on any sticky note, and these hold up. I stuck one to a vertical monitor bezel and it stayed put for 36 hours before I peeled it off — no curling at the edges, no slide. On notebook covers and refrigerator doors, the bond is equally reliable. The notes also peel off the pad cleanly without tearing or taking the next sheet with them, which is a pet peeve with cheaper options.

Writing surface is decent for a shaped note. Ballpoint and gel pens write cleanly without the paper tearing. The heart shape does cut into your usable space, though — the top lobes are essentially wasted area. You'll get maybe two short lines of legible handwriting per note. Fine for quick reminders, frustrating for anything detailed.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The color variety comes up repeatedly in customer feedback. One reviewer noted, "The colors are so much more vibrant than the picture shows — they brighten up my whole desk." Others mention the sheet count as a deciding factor, with several noting these last longer than expected because the 75-sheet count is legit. One verified purchase said, "I've bought these twice now. The sticky backing is strong but doesn't damage my wall when I remove them."

**Where it falls short**

The heart shape is the double-edged sword here. It's cute, but it wastes roughly 20% of each note's surface area. If you're the type who writes multi-step to-do lists on notes, you'll find yourself reaching for a second note constantly. The small size compounds this — you can't fit a phone number plus a name plus a date on one note without writing like a doctor. Also, the pads are loose in the packaging — if you toss them in a bag, the top sheets can peel off and stick to other items.

**Who should buy this**

Grab these if you're a teacher, therapist, or anyone whose workspace benefits from visual warmth. They're also great for crafters, journaling enthusiasts, or as a small gift for a coworker. Skip them if you need maximum writing space per note, or if you're stocking an office supply closet where function trumps form.

**FAQ**

*Are these notes actually heart-shaped, or just printed?*
They're die-cut hearts — the entire note is the shape, not just a printed design.

*Do they stick to painted walls?*
Yes, but the adhesive is strong enough that you should peel them off slowly to avoid lifting fresh paint.

*How do they compare to standard 3x3 sticky notes?*
They're about 30% smaller and lose usable space to the heart shape, but the adhesive quality is comparable to name-brand options.

**The Verdict**
