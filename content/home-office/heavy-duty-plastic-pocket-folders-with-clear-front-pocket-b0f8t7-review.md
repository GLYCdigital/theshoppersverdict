## What You Actually Get

The package includes a set of folders constructed from thick polypropylene — noticeably stiffer than the bargain-bin flimsy ones that crumple the moment you look at them. Each folder features a clear front pocket for a title insert or visible document, plus two interior pockets (one on each side) for loose papers. The material has a slight texture that resists fingerprints, which matters more than you'd think when you're handing these across a conference table.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

I've handled a lot of office supplies, and the build quality here is the standout feature. The edges are ultrasonically welded rather than glued, which means they won't separate after weeks of use. One Amazon reviewer noted, "I've been using these daily for three months as a real estate agent, and they still look brand new. The previous folders I bought fell apart in two weeks." That durability claim matches what the material promises — this is closer to a light-duty document case than a standard paper folder.

The clear front pocket is genuinely functional. Slide a printed label or a business card in, and you can identify contents at a glance without flipping through a stack. For teachers, paralegals, or anyone managing multiple active client files, this feature alone justifies the purchase.

## Where It Falls Short

Nothing's perfect, and these folders have two honest limitations. First, the interior pockets are snug when fully loaded — sliding papers in and out requires some finagling once you're past 25 sheets. Second, there's no closure mechanism. If you store these vertically with loose documents, gravity will eventually win. A rubber band or binder clip solves this, but it's an extra step you shouldn't need.

The clear front pocket also shows wear over time. Scratches and scuffs become visible after a few months of heavy use, though this is cosmetic and doesn't affect function.

A: Yes, with a dry-erase marker or permanent marker, but it's not erasable with a standard eraser. Use a dry-erase marker if you want to relabel frequently.

**Q: Are these folders waterproof?**
