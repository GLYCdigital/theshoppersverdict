**The PANDAFLY Calligraphy Pen Set: One Box, Ten Nib Sizes — But Is It Enough?**

Most calligraphy sets give you one nib and a prayer. PANDAFLY took the opposite approach: 10 distinct nib sizes, from a hairline 0.5mm up to a bold 6mm, all packed into a single case. For beginners who aren't sure which stroke width they need, that range alone justifies a look. For experienced letterers, it means you're not swapping handles mid-project. But the real question is whether the pens themselves hold up — and where this set cuts corners.

**What's Actually in the Box**

The kit includes 10 steel-nibbed pens, each with a different tip size (0.5, 0.8, 1.0, 1.2, 1.5, 2.0, 3.0, 4.0, 5.0, and 6.0mm), plus a plastic nib holder and a small ink bottle. Each pen arrives individually sealed in its own plastic sleeve — a thoughtful touch that prevents the dried-out tips that plague cheaper sets. The nibs are mounted on slim, comfortable barrels with a matte finish that grips well even with sweaty hands. As shown in the product image, the set lays out flat in a sturdy cardboard case, which keeps everything organized but isn't durable enough for heavy travel.

**Performance and Build Quality**

I tested these across three surfaces: standard copy paper, smooth cardstock, and cold-press watercolor paper. On cardstock and copy paper, the ink flow was steady and consistent across all ten sizes. The 0.5mm nib produces crisp hairlines for pointed-pen calligraphy, while the 6mm lays down a satisfyingly bold stroke for banners and signage. The steel nibs flex slightly under pressure, which gives you line variation without needing to switch pens mid-word — the hallmark of a decent calligraphy tool.

The weak point is the nib holder. It's functional but lightweight, and the fit is a bit loose. If you bear down hard, the nib can shift slightly, which throws off your angle mid-stroke. It's usable, but it's the first thing I'd upgrade.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.6-star rating holds up in the reviews. One buyer noted, "I was surprised by how many nib sizes came in one set. The 0.5mm is perfect for addressing envelopes, and the 6mm is great for making signs." Another mentioned, "The ink is vibrant and dries fast enough that I don't smear it when I'm left-handed." Multiple reviewers also flagged the sealed packaging as a reason they chose this over competitors, since dried-out pens are a common complaint with budget calligraphy kits.

**Where It Falls Short**

The obvious gap: this set ships with colored inks only — no black. If you're doing everyday writing, formal invitations, or professional commissions, you'll need to buy black ink separately. The plastic nib holder also feels like an afterthought, and on textured paper, the larger nibs can skip if you don't adjust your pressure. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Beginners** who want to experiment with multiple nib sizes without committing to a single style
- **Card makers and DIY crafters** who need varied stroke widths for projects
- **Sign painters or poster letterers** who need the larger 5mm and 6mm nibs for bold work
- **Not for** professional calligraphers who already own premium holders and want a basic ink set

**FAQ**

**Q: Do the pens come pre-filled with ink?**
A: No, they're dip pens. You dip the nib into the included ink bottle or your own ink. The set includes one bottle of colored ink.

**Q: What kind of ink works best with these nibs?**
A: Any fountain pen ink or calligraphy ink works well. Thicker acrylic inks can clog the finer nibs, so stick to water-based inks for the 0.5mm and 0.8mm sizes.

**Q: Are the nibs interchangeable with other holders?**
A: Yes, the nibs use a standard flange size and fit most universal nib holders. You can swap them out if you want a heavier grip.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're exploring calligraphy and want a range of nib sizes in one affordable kit, or you need bold nibs for craft projects. **Skip it if** you need black ink included, or you're a working calligrapher who'll replace the holder immediately anyway.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The PANDAFLY set delivers real value for the price, with a nib size for nearly every application. It's not a premium tool, but it's an honest, functional kit that gets the job done.
