Let's cut through the noise. The Patriot P400 is a PCIe Gen 4x4 NVMe SSD that promises up to 5,000 MB/s sequential reads and 4,250 MB/s writes. In real-world office and workstation use, those numbers translate to boot times under 10 seconds, instant application launches, and no more staring at the spinning wheel when you open a 2GB spreadsheet.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the drive itself — a single-sided M.2 2280 stick with a graphene heat spreader already applied — and a basic installation guide. That's it. No screw, no cloning software license, no heatsink. The graphene layer is thin enough to fit under most laptop heatsinks and motherboard shields, which is a plus for tight builds. If you're upgrading a PS5, you'll need to supply your own heatsink anyway, since the graphene pad alone won't cut it for sustained console workloads.

**Performance and build quality**

The P400 uses a Phison PS5019-E19 controller paired with TLC NAND. In testing, it hits the advertised sequential numbers in CrystalDiskMark with a full queue depth. More importantly, random 4K read/write performance — the metric that actually matters for office work — is snappy at around 500K IOPS read. Files copy fast, Windows Search stops indexing lag, and large Excel files with external links open quicker than you can make coffee.

The graphene heat spreader does its job. After a 100GB continuous write test, the drive topped out at 62°C in a well-ventilated case — well below the 70°C throttle point. No performance dips during long video exports or game installs. That said, the drive has no DRAM cache. Patriot compensates with HMB (Host Memory Buffer), which borrows a small slice of system RAM for the mapping table. In practice, you won't notice the difference unless you're running heavy server-grade workloads.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews reflects a pattern. One verified buyer wrote, "Upgraded my Dell XPS from a SATA SSD to this and it's night and day. Boot went from 25 seconds to 7." Another noted, "Installed in a PS5 with a cheap heatsink — loaded Cyberpunk in 8 seconds." Multiple reviewers mention the price-to-performance ratio, calling it "the best value Gen 4 drive under $60." The most common compliment is reliability — no failures reported in the first year across dozens of long-term reviews.

**Where it falls short**

The P400's endurance rating is 400 TBW for the 1TB model — roughly half of what you'd get from a WD Black SN770. For typical office users writing 20-30GB daily, that's still 30+ years of life. But if you're editing 4K video daily or running database servers, the lower endurance is a real consideration.

The SLC cache behavior is the other tradeoff. The drive can absorb about 120GB of writes at full speed, then drops to roughly 1,800 MB/s sustained. Unless you're moving massive media files every day, you'll rarely hit this. But it's worth knowing before you buy.

**Who should buy this**

- **Office workers and general users** upgrading from SATA or older NVMe drives — this is the sweet spot
- **Gamers** on a budget who want fast load times without paying Samsung tax
- **Laptop owners** who need a low-profile, single-sided drive with good thermals
- **PS5 owners** who want a Gen 4 drive with real speed at sub-$60 pricing

**Skip it if** you're a professional video editor moving 200GB+ files daily, or if you need a drive with a 5-year warranty and higher TBW for enterprise-adjacent workloads.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Q: Does the Patriot P400 work in a PS5?**
A: Yes, but you must add a heatsink — the graphene pad alone won't dissipate enough heat in the console's enclosed space. A $10 aluminum heatsink works fine.

**Q: Is this drive compatible with older motherboards that only have PCIe Gen 3 slots?**
A: Yes, it will work at Gen 3 speeds (around 3,500 MB/s read), which still outperforms most SATA SSDs by 5-6x. You'll see the full Gen 4 speed only on compatible boards.

**Q: Does it come with cloning software for migrating from an old drive?**
A: No. Download Patriot's Acronis True Image OEM version from their website — it's free and supports the P400 directly.

**The Verdict**

The Patriot P400 is a legitimate Gen 4 performer at a Gen 3 price. It's not the fastest drive on the market, but it's fast enough for 99% of users, runs cool, and costs significantly less than comparable drives from Samsung or WD. The lack of bundled software and lower endurance rating are minor quibbles for the price point.

**Buy it if** you want real Gen 4 speed without spending $100+ on a premium drive. **Skip it if** you need maximum endurance for professional content creation.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A smart, cost-effective upgrade for anyone still running a SATA drive.
