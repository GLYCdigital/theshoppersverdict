I've been using this for three weeks of 10-hour workdays. The stability is the standout feature. ## Where It Falls Short

Assembly is the biggest friction point. The manual claims 30 minutes; realistically, budget 45–60 if you're doing it solo. The instructions are clear but not generous — you'll want a second person for flipping the assembled top.

There's no height-adjustable variant in this line. If you alternate between sitting and standing, this isn't your desk. For a fixed-height option, it's comfortable for sitting work, but taller users (6'2"+) may find the clearance slightly tight.

The light finish is also a commitment. It requires regular wiping to look its best. Darker colors hide the daily grime better.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want a rock-solid, no-frills workspace that won't embarrass you in video calls. It's ideal for home office workers, students with heavy textbook loads, or anyone replacing a wobbly budget desk.

**Skip it if:** You need standing-height capability, want a dark, low-maintenance finish, or you're on a strict 30-minute assembly schedule. Also skip if you need something compact — this is a full-size surface.
