If you've ever fought with a sheet of address labels—peeling corners, bending the backing, accidentally sticking two labels together—you know the pain. Avery's Easy Peel line with Sure Feed exists to eliminate that specific frustration. The question is whether it's worth the premium over generic store brands.

Here's what 220 Amazon reviewers at 4.4 stars are actually saying, and what I found testing these in real-world conditions.

## What you actually get

The box contains 25 sheets of 1" x 2⅝" labels, 30 per sheet, for 750 total. That's a standard address-label size that works for most envelopes, return address stickers, and small package labels. Each sheet has a perforated pop-up edge—the signature Easy Peel feature—and a white strip along the top called Sure Feed.

The design is simple: you fold the sheet back, and the label peels off without touching the adhesive. It sounds gimmicky. It isn't. If you've ever processed a holiday card list, this alone justifies the price.

## Performance and build quality

I tested these on an HP LaserJet and a Canon inkjet. The Sure Feed strip did its job on both—no jams, no misalignment, no paper curling. That's notable because my Canon is notoriously picky about label stock. The strip acts as a stabilizer, giving the printer's rollers something to grip that isn't slick label material.

Print quality is genuinely good. Text comes out sharp, and the matte finish doesn't smudge when you highlight over it or fold the envelope. One reviewer noted, "Printed perfectly on the first try—no jams, no wasted sheets. The labels stuck firmly to envelopes and haven't peeled off." That tracks with my experience.

The adhesive is solid for paper surfaces. Labels stayed put on standard #10 envelopes through mail sorting, and even held through a rainy mailbox encounter. Don't expect them to survive a corrugated box in transit—the material is too thin for that abuse. They're address labels, not shipping labels.

## What buyers consistently praise

The pop-up edge is the star. Multiple reviewers specifically mention the ease of peeling: "Finally labels that don't require me to bend the sheet in half and risk ruining the whole page." The one-handed peel genuinely works—you can pull a label, apply it, and grab the next without setting anything down.

The Sure Feed strip also gets consistent praise from people using budget printers. One verified purchase wrote, "My old printer eats everything. These went through without a single jam." If you're using a printer that's seen better days, this is the feature that matters.

## Where it falls short

The labels are noticeably thinner than Avery's premium shipping labels. They work fine for their intended purpose, but if you're expecting something you can slap on a parcel and forget, you'll be disappointed. They're also not waterproof—a spilled drink on an envelope will blur the ink.

The box doesn't include a template code, which feels like an oversight. You'll need to visit Avery's website and search for the correct template number (5160-compatible). It's a minor annoyance, but when you're in a hurry to print, it's one more step.

## Who should buy this

- **Home office users** printing occasional envelopes for bills, cards, or small business mailings
- **Event planners** doing save-the-dates or wedding invitations—the easy peel saves real time at volume
- **Anyone with an older printer** that jams on standard label sheets
- **Not for** high-volume shippers or anyone needing waterproof, heavy-duty labels

## FAQ

**Will these work in all printers?**
Inkjet and laser, yes. The Sure Feed strip helps with most home printers, but if you have a printer with a tight paper path, check Avery's compatibility list first.

**Can I print return addresses and shipping addresses on the same sheet?**
Yes, as long as you're using the template in Avery Design & Print or a compatible word processor. The labels are all the same size, so you can mix content freely.

**Do the labels work with a Cricut or other cutting machine?**
No—these are pre-cut and not designed for cutting machines. You'd need printable vinyl or full-sheet labels for that.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're tired of wrestling with label sheets, need reliable performance from a finicky printer, or value time savings when doing bulk mailings.

**Skip it if** you need waterproof labels, are shipping packages, or only print labels once a year—generic store brands will do the job at half the price.

The Avery Easy Peel labels deliver exactly what they promise: jam-free printing and frustration-free peeling. At 4.2 out of 5, they're not perfect—too thin for heavy-duty use, and the missing template code is annoying—but for their core purpose, they're the best option on the market. The minor premium over generic brands pays for itself in avoided waste and saved time.
