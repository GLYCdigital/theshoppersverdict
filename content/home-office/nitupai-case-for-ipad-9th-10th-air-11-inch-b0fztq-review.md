## What You Actually Get

The package includes the folio case itself and nothing else — no screen protector, no bonus accessories. What you're paying for is the construction. The outer layer is a textured synthetic leather that feels denser than the typical $15 tablet sleeve. The interior is a soft microfiber that won't scratch the glass. The front flap uses a tri-fold design that gives you two viewing angles and one typing position. There's also a dedicated Apple Pencil slot on the right edge — it's a cutout with a magnetic anchor, not a stretchy loop, so it charges wirelessly while seated.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this case with an iPad 10th Gen for a week of daily use — emails, note-taking, video calls. The magnetic stand is the standout feature. That's rare at this price point; most folios rely on friction alone and collapse under aggressive typing.

**Q: Does the Apple Pencil charge through the case?**
Yes, for the 2nd Gen Pencil and newer. The side cutout exposes the magnetic charging strip, so the pencil attaches directly to the iPad's magnetic rail. The 1st Gen Pencil (lightning connector) will not charge here.

**Q: Will this case fit a tempered glass screen protector?**
Yes, with most standard 0.3mm glass protectors. The raised bezels are about 2mm tall, leaving clearance. If you use a thicker privacy glass, you may find the edges slightly tight.
