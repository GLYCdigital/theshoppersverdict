The BIC Xtra Smooth isn't trying to be a luxury writing instrument. It's trying to be the pencil you grab when you need to fill out forms, scribble margin notes, and survive a double shift without your hand cramping. And for the most part, it succeeds — with a few caveats worth knowing before you stock up.

**What you actually get**

The package includes one mechanical pencil pre-loaded with a single 0.7mm lead, plus the twist-up eraser. That's it. No refill pack, no extra leads tucked into the barrel like some competitors include. As shown in the product image, the pencil features a clear barrel with a colored grip section that matches the lead color you choose. The grip itself is the standout feature — a soft, cushioned sleeve that extends over half the barrel length, giving your fingers a consistent resting spot whether you hold the pencil high or low.

**Performance and build**

The 0.7mm lead is the smart choice here. It's thick enough to handle normal writing pressure without snapping every few sentences, and it produces a dark, legible line without needing to press hard. The lead advance mechanism clicks forward reliably with a satisfying single click, and I didn't experience any lead slipping back into the barrel during extended use — a common issue with budget mechanical pencils.

The eraser deserves special mention. Most mechanical pencil erasers are tiny pink nubs that smear graphite around and leave gray ghosts. The BIC Xtra Smooth's eraser is a full-length twist-up that actually lifts graphite from standard printer paper. It won't match a dedicated eraser, but it's genuinely usable — a rarity at this price point.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The comfort grip is the feature that shows up in review after review. One Amazon reviewer noted, "I write for hours at work and this is the only pencil that doesn't leave a dent in my middle finger." Another mentioned, "The grip is soft but not squishy — it doesn't slide around when your hands get sweaty." Multiple reviewers also praised the lead strength, with one stating, "I press hard when I write and I've only broken lead twice in three months."

**Where it falls short**

The hollow plastic barrel flexes noticeably under pressure. If you're a heavy-handed writer, you'll feel the barrel compress slightly between your fingers — not enough to break it, but enough to feel cheap. The single lead refill is also frustrating. You'll likely blow through it in a day or two of heavy note-taking, and then you're making a second purchase immediately. The clip is functional but tight, and it can scratch surfaces if you're not careful sliding it onto a pocket.

**Who should buy this**

- **Students** who need a reliable pencil for exam days and don't want to fuss with sharpening
- **Office workers** who write for extended periods and need the comfort grip
- **Left-handed writers** who struggle with smudging from traditional pencils — the 0.7mm lead dries instantly and doesn't smear
- **Budget-conscious shoppers** who want a solid performer without paying premium prices

**Who should skip it**

- **Artists or detail-oriented writers** who need 0.5mm or finer lead for precision work
- **People who hate buying refills** — you'll need to purchase lead packs regularly
- **Those who prefer weighted, premium-feel instruments** — this is unapologetically plastic

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this pencil come with extra lead?**
A: No, just the single lead pre-loaded inside. Buy a 0.7mm lead refill pack at the same time.

**Q: Is the grip removable?**
A: No, the cushioned grip is permanently attached to the barrel. It's not replaceable if it wears out.

**Q: Does the eraser actually work or just smear?**
A: It genuinely erases cleanly on standard paper. It's not as good as a block eraser, but it won't leave you frustrated.

**The Verdict**

The BIC Xtra Smooth is a straightforward, no-nonsense mechanical pencil that prioritizes comfort and reliability over flash. It's not built to be a lifetime tool, but it's built to get the job done without annoying you.

**Buy it if** you want a comfortable, dependable pencil for everyday writing and don't mind stocking up on lead refills.

**Skip it if** you need fine-point precision or prefer a heavier, more substantial writing instrument.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Comfortable, reliable, and honest about what it is. The hollow barrel and single lead keep it from being perfect, but for the price, it's hard to beat.
