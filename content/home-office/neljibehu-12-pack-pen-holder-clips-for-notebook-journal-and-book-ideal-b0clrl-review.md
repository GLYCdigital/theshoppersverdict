### Small Clip, Big Function: Does It Actually Hold?

You know the drill. You toss your notebook in your bag, and your pen vanishes into the abyss. Or worse, it clicks open and inks up your pages. The NELJIBEHU 12-pack of pen holder clips aims to kill that problem for less than the cost of a decent coffee. But with 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, is it genuinely useful or just another desk-drawer novelty? After testing a pack, here's the straight story.

### What You Get

This is a straightforward package: **12 individual metal clips**, each with a spring-loaded hinge and a flat face that slides over your notebook cover. The design is minimalist — no branding, no plastic casing, just a functional grip. Each clip is roughly 1.5 inches long, and the opening accommodates pen barrels up to **about 10mm in diameter**. That covers standard ballpoints, gel pens, and most fineliners. If you're using a chunky fountain pen or a thick-barreled marker, measure first — it might not fit.

### Real-World Performance

The core question: does it actually keep a pen attached? In my testing, yes — mostly. The spring tension is firm enough to hold a pen securely through a week of commuting, but not so stiff that it damages the pen's own clip. The metal construction feels durable, and the grip on the notebook cover is solid when the cover is firm (like a hardcover journal or a spiral notebook's back board).

One detail I appreciated: the clip **doesn't mar leather or vinyl covers**. The edges are smooth, so you won't scratch the finish on a nice journal. And because the clip is low-profile, it doesn't create a noticeable bulge when you stack books.

### What Buyers Consistently Praise

The Amazon reviews repeatedly mention two things: **value and simplicity**. One reviewer noted, "Finally, pens stay where I put them. I've bought three packs already — one for home, one for work, one for my bag." Another said, "These are sturdier than the plastic ones I've tried. The metal spring hasn't loosened after months of use."

That durability point matters. Cheap plastic clips often stretch out after a few weeks. The NELJIBEHU clips use a metal spring that holds tension. In my experience, they've kept their grip without becoming loose or slipping off.

### Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. **Slick covers are a problem.** If your notebook has a glossy or plastic-coated cover (think Moleskine or similar), the clip can slide side-to-side under pressure. It won't fall off on its own, but it might shift enough that the pen angle changes. A textured or matte cover holds it firmly in place.

Also, **thick pens are a no-go**. The 10mm opening is the ceiling. If you use a tactical pen or a gel pen with a rubber grip, you'll need to check the diameter before buying. The clip won't stretch to accommodate.

### Who Should Buy These

- **Students and commuters** who carry notebooks in backpacks and need pens to stay put.
- **Journalers and bullet journalers** who want a pen always attached to their current notebook.
- **Bulk buyers** who want to outfit multiple notebooks, planners, or spiral pads at once.

Skip them if you exclusively use oversized pens, or if you're working with ultra-thin paperback covers that can't support the clip's weight.

### FAQ

**Q: Will these damage my notebook cover?**
A: No. The clip is smooth-edged and doesn't dig into the cover. It grips via spring tension, not friction. That said, I'd avoid using them on ultra-thin paper covers — the pressure could leave a slight crease over time.

**Q: Can I use these on a spiral notebook?**
A: Yes, but position them on the back board, not over the spiral wire. The clip needs a flat surface to grip.

**Q: How do I remove the clip without breaking it?**
A: Squeeze the two prongs together and slide it off. Don't pull straight up — that puts stress on the hinge. Done right, the clips will last for years.
