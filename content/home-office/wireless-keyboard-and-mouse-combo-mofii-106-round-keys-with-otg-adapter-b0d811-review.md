Let me save you some scrolling: this Mofii combo isn't a mechanical keyboard, doesn't have RGB, and won't impress your gaming friends. But if you want a quiet, affordable, and genuinely portable typing setup that works across nearly every device you own, it deserves a serious look.

**What's actually in the box**

You get the 106-key keyboard, a compact wireless mouse, a 2.4GHz USB receiver that both devices share, and — this is the differentiator — an OTG adapter. That little dongle means you can plug the receiver into a USB-C phone or tablet and get a full keyboard experience on the go. The keyboard measures roughly 17.3 x 5.5 inches, which is standard full-size, and the mouse is a slim, low-profile unit that runs on a single AA battery. The keyboard needs two AAA batteries. Nothing is rechargeable, so budget for batteries every few months.

**How it actually performs**

The round keys are the headline feature. They're concave, reminiscent of vintage typewriters, and they sit on scissor switches with about 2.5mm of travel. The result is a soft, cushioned typing feel that's significantly quieter than your typical membrane board. I ran through a full day of drafting emails and reports, and my fingers didn't fatigue. That said, if you're coming from a mechanical keyboard, the actuation will feel indistinct — there's no tactile bump, just a gentle bottom-out.

The mouse tracks accurately on both a desk mat and bare wood, and the 2.4GHz connection held steady from about 10 feet away through a desk partition. The receiver plugs into the keyboard's USB port when not in use, which is a nice touch to avoid losing it.

**What buyers consistently mention**

Scrolling through the 220 ratings, a clear pattern emerges. One verified buyer writes, "The round keys are so comfortable — I typed a 20-page report without wrist pain." Another notes, "The OTG adapter is genius. I plugged it into my iPad and had a full setup in seconds." Connection reliability gets frequent praise too: "No lag, no dropouts, works flawlessly with my laptop and smart TV."

**Where it falls short**

Be honest with yourself about the mouse. It's small — roughly 4 inches long and flat. If you have larger hands or prefer palm grip, you'll likely find it cramped after an hour. This is a travel companion, not an ergonomic daily driver.

The lack of backlighting is another genuine limitation. In a dim office or during late-night work, you'll be hunting for keys. Also, because the keys are round and slightly smaller than standard square keys, touch typists might initially mistype until their muscle memory adjusts. Give it a few days.

**Who should buy this**

This combo is ideal for three specific buyers: students who want a compact setup that works with both a laptop and tablet via the OTG adapter, office workers replacing a clunky wired keyboard on a budget, and anyone who types in shared spaces and needs whisper-quiet keys. Skip it if you're a mechanical keyboard enthusiast, need backlighting, or require a full-size ergonomic mouse.

**FAQ**

*Is the keyboard compatible with Mac?*
Yes, it works with macOS, Windows, Android, and iOS via the OTG adapter. The Command key functions as Windows key and vice versa, but all standard shortcuts work.

*Can I connect it to multiple devices?*
Not simultaneously. It uses a single 2.4GHz receiver, so you'd need to unplug and move it between devices. There's no Bluetooth option on this model.

*How long do the batteries last?*
Most users report 3-4 months of daily use from the keyboard and 4-5 months from the mouse, depending on usage. There's no auto-sleep indicator, so you'll want spares on hand.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want an affordable, quiet, multi-device typing solution with a comfortable key feel and don't mind a compact mouse.

**Skip it if:** You need backlighting, prefer mechanical switches, or have large hands that demand a bigger mouse.

At 4.4 stars from 220 ratings, this is a solid 4.2/5 product — not perfect, but genuinely useful for the right buyer. For the price, you're getting a reliable daily driver that punches above its weight class in portability and compatibility. Just pair it with a separate ergonomic mouse if that's your primary tool.
