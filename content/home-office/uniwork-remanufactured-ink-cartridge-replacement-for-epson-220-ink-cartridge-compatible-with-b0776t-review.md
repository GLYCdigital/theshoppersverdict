**Q: Do these void my Epson warranty?**
A: In the US, the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act prevents manufacturers from voiding warranties solely for using third-party consumables — unless they can prove the cartridge caused the damage. Practically, Epson may push back, but legally you have protection.
