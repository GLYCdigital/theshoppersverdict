Let’s cut to it: the Nadex CR600 is a workhorse, not a show pony. If you're running a coffee shop, food truck, or small retail counter, this machine does exactly what a cash register should do — count money, print receipts, and track sales — without the subscription fees or Wi-Fi dependency of modern tablet POS systems. But it’s not without compromises, and the 3.9-star average tells you real users have found a few.

**What you actually get**

Out of the box: the CR600 main unit, a heavy-gauge steel cash drawer (which locks and unlocks automatically with each sale), a thermal receipt printer, and a power adapter. As shown in the product image, the layout is classic register — 72 programmable keys, a two-line LED display for you and a rear-facing display for customers. Setup is genuinely simple: plug in, set the date/time, program your tax rate, and assign department keys. Most users report being operational in under 30 minutes.

**Real-world performance**

The thermal printer is the standout feature. No ribbons, no ink cartridges — just drop in a 40mm roll and it prints crisp receipts in about two seconds. That's a recurring-cost win over older impact printers. The 72-key layout is generous for a machine at this price point, letting you assign dedicated keys for bestsellers, which speeds up transaction time noticeably.

The battery backup is another quiet hero. Several reviewers mention power blips at farmers' markets and outdoor events; the CR600 holds your data and clock settings, so you're not reprogramming tax rates mid-shift. One user noted, “Lost power twice during a festival weekend and didn’t lose a single transaction record.”

**Where it stumbles**

The keypad is the most common complaint. The keys require a firm, deliberate press — this isn't a keyboard with feather-light travel. If you're used to touch-screen POS or a quick-fingered colleague, you'll occasionally miss a keystroke and have to re-enter the price. It's a minor annoyance, but at 10-15 transactions an hour, it adds up.

Reporting is basic. You get end-of-day totals by department and payment type, but no per-item sales analysis. If you need to know exactly how many blueberry muffins sold versus banana bread, this machine won't tell you. You'll need to keep manual counts or upgrade to a POS system. Also noteworthy: no barcode scanner port. If your inventory has SKUs, you'll need a separate USB scanner and a workaround, which diminishes the point of a dedicated register.

**Who should buy this**

The CR600 is ideal for:
- Food trucks and concession stands needing reliable, offline operation
- Small cafes and bakeries with fewer than 20 SKUs
- Pop-up shops and market vendors who need portability and battery backup
- Anyone tired of monthly POS subscription fees

Skip it if you need detailed inventory tracking, run a high-volume retail store with barcoded products, or have staff who struggle with physical keypads.

**FAQ**

*Does it track sales tax automatically?*
Yes, once you program the rate, it applies tax per-department. You can set different rates for different keys — useful if you sell both taxable goods and non-taxable services.

*Can it handle refunds and returns?*
Yes, there's a refund function that reverses the transaction and prints a return receipt. It's straightforward but requires the original transaction details.

*Is the cash drawer removable?*
The drawer is attached but can be detached for under-counter mounting if you prefer. The locking mechanism works reliably — multiple reviewers confirm it pops open smoothly with each sale.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a dependable, low-cost register that works offline and won't nickel-and-dime you with subscriptions. It's a solid 3.7-star value for simple retail operations.

**Skip it if** you need inventory analytics, barcode scanning, or a more forgiving keypad for high-speed transaction environments.

For independent sellers who want something that just works without the cloud, the Nadex CR600 delivers. Just know its limits before you buy — it's a cash register, not a business intelligence tool.
