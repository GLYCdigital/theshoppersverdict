Printer ink is the quiet tax of home office life. The HP 67 Black/Tri-Color Ink Cartridge 2-Pack is one of the most common refill kits on the market, supporting a wide range of DeskJet and Envy printers. With a 4.6-star rating across over 62,000 reviews, it's a well-understood product — and the consensus is generally positive, with a few important caveats.

## Installation and Compatibility

This is the smoothest part of the experience. The cartridges click into place, the printer recognizes them immediately, and you're printing within minutes. One reviewer sums it up neatly: "Installation is simple. The cartridges click right into place and the printer recognizes them immediately with no error messages or fussing."

The 2-pack includes one black (standard yield) and one tri-color cartridge. They work with DeskJet 1255, 2700, and 4100 series printers, plus Envy 6000 and 6400 series. If your printer takes HP 67, these will fit without any adapter trickery.

## Print Quality

For a standard-yield cartridge, the output is solid. Documents come out sharp and clear, with crisp text and no streaking. Color prints — family photos, school projects, basic graphics — produce accurate, well-saturated results. One reviewer says the prints "look fantastic and have good color accuracy."

Reviewers who print photos regularly note that the quality is good for a standard office printer but won't rival dedicated photo printers. For homework, invoices, shipping labels, and casual photo prints, it's more than adequate.

## The Yield Problem

Here's where the standard 2-pack shows its limits. The black cartridge yields roughly 120 pages, and the tri-color yields about 100 pages. For light home users printing a few pages a week, this pack will last months. For anyone printing worksheets, shipping labels, or regular documents, you'll be replacing these in weeks.

Multiple reviewers express the same wish: longer life. One says the "only thing is I wish it lasted longer." This is the fundamental tension with standard-yield cartridges — lower upfront cost, higher per-page cost due to frequent replacement.

If you print regularly, the HP 67XL high-yield black cartridge (about 300 pages) paired with the standard color is a much better value proposition. The XL black costs more upfront but dramatically reduces cost per page.

## Instant Ink Compatibility

These cartridges work with HP's Instant Ink subscription program, which can be a smart option for moderate-to-heavy printers. The service monitors your ink levels and ships replacements automatically. It's worth considering if you're tired of emergency trips to the store mid-print job.

## What Reviewers Love

Genuine HP cartridges — no knockoffs, no third-party tricks. The "no issues" feedback comes up repeatedly: printers recognize them, setup is seamless, and the quality is exactly what you expect from an OEM product. For reliability-sensitive use — work documents, school submissions — that certainty has real value.

## What's Less Great

The standard yield is the main frustration. At $43.89 for this 2-pack, the per-page cost is higher than high-yield alternatives or third-party compatibles. Some reviewers mention the tri-color cartridge draining noticeably faster than the black, especially if you print mixed-content documents regularly.

There's also the inherent frustration of ink cartridge pricing. These are commodity items with thin margins, but the psychology of spending $44 on printer consumables never feels great — even when the product works perfectly.

## The Bottom Line

The HP 67 Black/Tri-Color 2-pack is exactly what you expect: genuine HP ink that installs easily and prints well. It's a reliable choice for standard yield needs, especially if you value the certainty of OEM quality over third-party savings. But if you print more than a few pages a week, do yourself a favor and buy the XL black cartridge instead.

**Verdict: 4.4/5** — Reliable OEM quality with easy installation. The standard yield is the weak link — buy XL for regular use.
