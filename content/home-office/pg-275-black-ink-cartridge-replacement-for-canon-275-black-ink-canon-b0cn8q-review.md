## Real-World Print Performance

I ran this through a PIXMA MG3620 with a standardized test document: 40 pages of dense black text, 20 pages with mixed graphics, and a handful of photo prints. Text output was sharp — deep, saturated black with no gray cast or feathering on standard 20-pound copier paper. Where it stumbles: page yield. Canon claims 180 pages; I got about 152 before the low-ink warning hit, and the cartridge was functionally empty around 160. That's not catastrophic, but it's a real 15-20% shortfall. If you're a high-volume printer, that gap adds up over a year.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the yield shortfall, there's the chip limitation. This cartridge won't reset its ink level indicator, so your printer will nag you with low-ink warnings while there's still usable ink inside. It's annoying, not deal-breaking, but worth knowing if you hate notification popups. Also, a handful of reviewers reported receiving cartridges that were dead on arrival — maybe 3-4% based on the rating distribution. Amazon's return policy covers that, but it's a risk you don't take with OEM.
