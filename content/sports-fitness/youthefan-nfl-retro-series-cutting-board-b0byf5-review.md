## What You Actually Get

The board measures roughly 18 by 12 inches with a 1.5-inch thickness — substantial enough to stay put on the counter without a damp towel underneath. The bamboo surface is sealed with a food-safe finish, and the edges are rounded rather than sharp-cut, which matters when you're scraping chopped onions into a pan at 6 PM on a Tuesday.

The retro team logo is heat-branded into the bamboo surface, not printed on top. That's a meaningful distinction — printed graphics on cutting boards peel after a few washes, but branding burned into the wood is part of the board itself. The product images show this clearly: the logo has depth, not just color.

You also get a juice groove routed around the perimeter and a cutout handle near the top. The handle is wide enough for gloved hands, which you'll appreciate if you're carrying this from kitchen to grill.

## Real-World Performance

Bamboo is a smart choice here. It's harder than maple or walnut, so it resists deep knife scarring, and it's naturally antimicrobial — one less thing to worry about when you're prepping raw chicken. The juice groove actually holds liquid (some cheap boards have grooves so shallow they're decorative), and cleanup is straightforward with warm water and soap.

One buyer noted: *"I've had this board for six months and use it every Sunday. The logo still looks brand new, and there's not a single crack."* That tracks with the heat-branding approach — there's nothing to peel.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the limitations.

**Size is the biggest tradeoff.** At roughly 18 x 12 inches, this is a prep board, not a carving board. If you're breaking down a full turkey or slicing a 14-pound brisket, you'll run out of real estate fast. It's ideal for vegetables, steaks, chicken breasts, and moderate quantities — not large-scale catering.

**Bamboo is thirsty wood.** It requires hand-washing and periodic oiling to maintain the surface. If you're someone who throws everything in the dishwasher, this board will crack within months, and the brand will dull. The care instructions are clear, but they're also non-negotiable.

**Retro-only designs.** The product line offers vintage logos exclusively. If you want the current uniform or helmet design, this isn't the product for you.

## FAQ

**Is the logo food-safe?**
Yes. The heat-branding process doesn't introduce any coatings or dyes that contact your food. The board is finished with food-safe mineral oil.

**Can this go in the dishwasher?**
No. Dishwasher detergent and heat will warp bamboo and degrade the brand. Hand-wash with mild soap and dry immediately.

**Does it come with a care kit?**
No, but you can use any food-grade mineral oil or beeswax conditioner. Reapply every few months or when the surface looks dry.
