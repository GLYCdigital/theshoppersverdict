## Where They Fall Short

Let's be direct about the limitations. The velcro straps have a finite length. If your calves measure 16 inches or more around, the overlap will be minimal and might not hold securely during vigorous movement. Measure before you buy — this isn't a one-size-fits-all situation.

The plate system adds vertical bulk. During high-knee cardio or anything where the weight swings freely, you'll feel the clunk. These are best suited for controlled, ground-based movements — not plyometrics.

Finally, the zipper is the most likely failure point. It's functional now, but frequent plate changes will stress it over time. If you plan to adjust weights daily, treat the zipper gently.
