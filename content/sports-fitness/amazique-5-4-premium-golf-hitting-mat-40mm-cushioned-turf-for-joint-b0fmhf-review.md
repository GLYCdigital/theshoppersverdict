# Amazique 5×4 Premium Golf Hitting Mat Review: Does the 40mm Cushion Actually Protect Your Joints?

**The short answer:** Yes, but with caveats. Here's what 215 buyers found when they put this mat through real practice sessions.

## What You're Actually Getting

The Amazique 5×4 Premium Golf Hitting Mat is exactly what the name says — a five-foot by four-foot hitting surface with a 40mm cushioned base. That's not a typo. Forty millimeters of padding sits between your club and whatever hard surface you're hitting from — concrete garage floors, patio slabs, or that uneven spot in the backyard.

As shown in the product image, the mat has two distinct layers: a dense turf top for club interaction and a thick rubber/cushion underlayment for impact absorption. At 5×4 feet, it's large enough to stand on while you swing — not one of those narrow strips where your left foot hangs off the edge during your follow-through.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The turf density is the first thing you'll notice. It's firm — firmer than most range mats I've tested — but that firmness translates to durability. Buyers who've put hundreds of swings into this mat report minimal wear at the impact zone. One reviewer noted, *"I've hit 500+ balls with my wedges and there's no visible fraying or tearing. The fibers bounce back."*

The 40mm cushion is the headline feature, and it delivers where it matters. The mat absorbs shock at the point of impact rather than transferring it up the shaft into your elbows. For anyone with joint issues — or anyone who's felt that dull ache after 50 balls on a thin mat — this is a meaningful upgrade.

The rubber base grips the ground well. You won't find the mat sliding during aggressive swings, which is a common complaint with cheaper, lighter options. At roughly 30 pounds, it stays put.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The joint protection is the runaway winner in customer feedback. Multiple reviewers with elbow or wrist sensitivity specifically called out the 40mm cushion as the reason they can practice daily without pain. One golfer put it plainly: *"I was ready to quit practicing on mats entirely until I tried this. Night and day difference for my elbows."*

The size also earns consistent praise. At 5×4, you get a legitimate practice station — room for alignment sticks, a stance that doesn't feel cramped, and enough surface to practice ball position changes.

## Where It Falls Short

The mat is heavy. At around 30 pounds and 5 feet long, it's not something you'll casually drag from garage to backyard every day. It's a "set it up and leave it" product. If you need portability, look elsewhere.

The turf plays fast and firm. You won't get that "divot" feel of real turf — the club skips slightly on impact. This is typical of durable mats, but it's worth knowing before you buy. If you're practicing feel, this mat won't replicate real fairway conditions.

And there's no built-in tee holder. For driver practice, you'll need a separate system or a rubber tee. Slightly annoying for a mat at this price point.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You practice at home on hard surfaces, you've had joint discomfort from thinner mats, or you want a durable stationary practice station that'll last years.

**Skip it if:** You need something portable you can roll up, you're a beginner working primarily on short game feel, or you're looking for a mat that simulates real turf divots.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this mat damage my clubs?**  
No. The turf is dense but synthetic — it won't scratch or mar clubfaces. It's actually gentler on clubs than hitting off concrete or worn range mats with exposed rubber.

**Q: Can I use it outdoors in the rain?**  
The rubber base and synthetic turf handle moisture fine, but the mat will get slippery when wet. Best used in covered areas or dry conditions.

**Q: How does the 40mm cushion compare to standard 10-15mm mats?**  
It's a completely different experience. Standard mats transfer most of the impact energy. The 40mm cushion absorbs a significant portion — you'll notice the difference on your first swing.
