## What You Actually Get

For $16.99, the box contains one pair of gloves (yes, both hands — a detail some budget brands get wrong), with a 10-ounce weight class that works for bag work, pad drills, and light sparring. The shell is synthetic leather — nothing premium, but stitched cleanly with reinforced seams at the stress points. The padding is where Valchiria earns its keep. The multi-layer foam is dense enough to absorb impact on a heavy bag without transmitting shock to your knuckles, yet not so mushy that you lose feedback on your punches. For pad work with a partner, the gloves hold up well at moderate power. This is not a competition sparring glove — don't expect to go 12 rounds at full intensity — but for fitness boxing, technique drills, and beginner-to-intermediate training, it delivers.

The breathable mesh palm is a genuinely thoughtful touch. After a 45-minute session, your hands stay noticeably drier than with fully synthetic-lined gloves. Small detail, big comfort difference.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest drawbacks. First, sizing runs small. The 10-ounce gloves fit average adult hands fine, but men with larger hands — think XL glove size or bigger — will find the palm snug. Check the size chart against your hand measurements before ordering.

Second, the synthetic leather scuffs quickly. Heavy bag work leaves visible marks on the exterior within a few weeks. It's cosmetic, not structural — the stitching and padding hold up — but if you want gear that looks pristine, this isn't it.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy these if:** You're starting boxing or kickboxing, need a reliable pair for fitness classes, or want a backup pair for guests and training partners. They're also excellent for kids and teens getting into the sport without committing to premium gear.

**Skip these if:** You're sparring regularly at high intensity, competing, or have hands large enough to require 12-14 ounce sizing. You'll want a heavier glove with more wrist support.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use these for actual sparring?**
A: Light technical sparring, yes. Full-contact sparring, no — the 10-ounce weight and padding level aren't sufficient for hard exchanges.

**Q: How do I clean them?**
A: Wipe down with a damp cloth after each use and air dry. Avoid submerging them or putting them in a washing machine — it'll break down the foam padding and synthetic leather.

**Q: Do I need hand wraps with these?**
A: Yes, absolutely. The padding protects your knuckles, but the wrist support in any glove is limited. Wraps add essential stability for your wrist and base of thumb.
