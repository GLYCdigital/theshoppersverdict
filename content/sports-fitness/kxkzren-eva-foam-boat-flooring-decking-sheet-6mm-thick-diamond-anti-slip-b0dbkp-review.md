The 6mm thickness is the standout feature here. That's noticeably thicker than the 4mm sheets that dominate this price bracket, and it translates directly to comfort. The adhesive backing works, but set expectations. It holds the sheet in place during normal use, but it's not a permanent marine-grade bond. ## Where It Falls Short

**Cutting quality matters.** Reviews mention that a dull blade will cause the EVA to tear rather than cut cleanly, leaving ragged edges that look unfinished. Use a fresh sharp utility knife, and cut from the adhesive side for cleaner results.

**Adhesion is adequate, not exceptional.** If you're installing on a surface with any texture or old adhesive residue, the included backing won't hold reliably. Surface prep is critical — clean thoroughly with acetone or denatured alcohol before applying.

**No radius corners.** Some competitors offer pre-rounded corners or contoured shapes. This is a straight sheet. You're doing all the shaping yourself.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work for paddle board tops?**
A: Yes, but only on flat or gently curved surfaces. The 6mm thickness provides good cushioning, but you'll want to add extra adhesive for a permanent bond on a SUP.

**Q: How do I get the best adhesion?**
A: Clean the surface with acetone, let it dry completely, then apply. For permanent installs, roll on a thin layer of contact cement on both surfaces before pressing the foam down.

**Q: Will it fade or degrade in sun and saltwater?**
A: EVA is UV-resistant, but all foam degrades under prolonged direct sun. Expect 2-3 seasons before noticeable color fade. Rinse with fresh water after saltwater use to extend lifespan.
