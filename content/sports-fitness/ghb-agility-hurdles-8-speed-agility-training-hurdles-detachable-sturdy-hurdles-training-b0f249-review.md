If you're coaching youth sports or just starting your own agility work, the GHB 8" Speed Agility Hurdles hit a sweet spot between functionality and price. These aren't the flimsy cone-style markers you'll find at big-box stores — but they're also not competition-grade steel hurdles. They sit somewhere in the practical middle, and for most buyers, that's exactly where you want to be.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes six hurdles, each measuring 8 inches tall. The detachable design breaks each hurdle into four connected sections that snap together without tools. Assembly takes about ten seconds per hurdle — you press the joints together and they lock firmly. The plastic is a medium-density polyethylene that bends slightly under pressure but springs back to shape.

Each hurdle spans roughly 18 inches wide, which gives you enough clearance for proper stride work without being so wide that they're awkward to carry. The total weight per hurdle is negligible — you can hold all six in one hand.

## Real-World Performance

I've put these through the paces on grass, turf, and concrete. The 8-inch height forces proper knee drive without being tall enough to cause hip strain or tripping injuries for most athletes. For youth players (ages 8–14), this height is nearly perfect for teaching quick feet and lateral movement.

The detachable joints are the standout feature here. They hold up to repeated stomping and accidental side impacts — I've had players clip them mid-drill and the joints didn't pop loose. The plastic does scratch and scuff, but that's cosmetic.

One buyer noted, *"We use these for our youth soccer team three times a week. They've held up for six months without breaking, which I can't say for the cheap cones we used before."*

Another reviewer mentioned, *"The 8-inch height is perfect for my 10-year-old. She's gained real confidence in her footwork drills."*

## Where They Fall Short

Be honest with yourself about your training surface. On hardwood gym floors or smooth concrete, these hurdles slide. The lightweight design that makes them portable also means they'll skid on hard surfaces when clipped. You can mitigate this with the included ground stakes (yes, they're in the box), but those only work on grass or soft turf.

There's no carry bag. For a product marketed on its portability, that's a genuine oversight. You'll either need to buy a separate storage bag or keep them in the original packaging, which degrades quickly.

And if you're an advanced athlete working on explosive speed, 8 inches won't challenge you. These are development tools, not elite training equipment.

## Who Should Buy These

**Youth sports coaches** — these are nearly indestructible for practice use. **Parents of young athletes** — the height is safe and appropriate for developing coordination. **Home gym users with grass or turf** — they're compact enough to store between workouts.

Skip them if you train exclusively on hard indoor surfaces or if you're a collegiate-level athlete needing taller, weighted hurdles.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these be adjusted to different heights?**
No. The 8-inch height is fixed. The pieces don't stack or extend. If you need variable heights, look for an adjustable set.

**Q: Do the ground stakes actually hold on grass?**
Yes, but they're basic plastic spikes. They work fine for practice, but they won't survive being driven into hard, dry soil. Angle them slightly and they hold better.

**Q: How many come in the package?**
Six hurdles are included. That's enough for a basic agility ladder setup or a five-hurdle sprint drill with a spare.
