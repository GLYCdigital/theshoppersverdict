## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. These balls are **lighter than regulation baseballs** — noticeably so. If your primary goal is perfecting timing for live pitching, you'll need to supplement with real balls. The weight difference means your bat speed and contact point won't transfer perfectly to game situations.

Second, skip these if you're swinging a wooden bat. The rigid plastic leaves scuff marks on wood barrels. They're fine for aluminum, composite, or plastic bats — which is what most players use for practice anyway.

Finally, the hollow design means they don't fly as far as real balls. That's actually a feature for contained practice spaces, but don't expect true batted-ball distance.
