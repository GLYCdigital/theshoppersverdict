**Bottom Line Up Front:** The We Sell Mats 5x10 is the Goldilocks option for home gymnasts — thick enough for real tumbling skills, large enough for actual routines, and foldable enough to live in a garage or spare room. It's not the cheapest or lightest mat in its class, but it's the one most buyers say they'd repurchase.

## What You Actually Get

This is a 5-foot by 10-foot folding tumbling mat with a 2-inch thick high-density foam core wrapped in a 14-oz vinyl cover. As shown in the product image, it folds into three sections and lays flat without curling at the seams — a common complaint with cheaper folding mats. The surface has a subtle pebbled texture that provides grip without being abrasive on knees or hands.

The mat ships rolled in a box, and it's ready to use immediately. There's no off-gassing period like you get with some PVC-based mats; the vinyl has a mild "new mat" smell that dissipates within a day or two.

## Real-World Performance

The 2-inch thickness is the sweet spot for home use. It's thick enough to absorb landings from back handsprings and round-offs, but not so thick that it becomes unstable or feels like you're tumbling on a mattress. Several reviewers noted it works well on both carpet and hardwood — the bottom vinyl has enough grip to stay put during dynamic movements.

One thing that surprised me reading through the 220 ratings: how many buyers use this mat for things beyond gymnastics. Cheerleading tumbling passes, martial arts drills, yoga, and even at-home CrossFit workouts come up repeatedly. The 5x10 footprint gives you enough room for full-body movements that a standard 4x6 mat doesn't allow.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The durability gets the most love. One verified buyer wrote: *"We've had this mat for 8 months with three kids doing daily tumbling practice. The seams are still tight, no creases where it folds, and the foam hasn't compressed at all."* That tracks with the 4.4-star average — this is a mat that survives actual use, not just occasional stretching.

Another common theme is how flat it lays. Folding mats often develop a "tent" at the fold lines where the foam creases. Multiple reviewers specifically mentioned this mat stays flat with no bumpy sections, which matters when you're landing a back tuck on a seam.

## Where It Falls Short

The weight is the biggest tradeoff. At nearly 40 pounds, this is not a mat a child can drag around solo. The built-in handles help, but they're positioned at the fold points, which means the mat wants to fold in half when you lift it. It's manageable for one adult, but plan on having help if you're moving it regularly between storage and use areas.

The vinyl surface can get slick when you're sweating. This is true of essentially all vinyl-covered mats, but it's worth noting if you plan on using it for high-intensity sessions without a towel. A light chalk application helps, but this isn't a mat you'll want to use in a humid garage during summer without addressing the moisture factor.

There's also no strap or carry bag included. For the price point, a simple shoulder strap would be a meaningful addition — the handles work, but they're not comfortable for carrying more than 20-30 feet.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have a child in gymnastics or cheer who needs a home practice mat, you're an adult doing tumbling or martial arts drills, or you want a versatile fitness mat for a home gym that can handle impact.

**Skip it if:** You're a small child needing a mat you can move yourself, you need something that packs into a car trunk on the regular, or you're only doing light stretching (a 1-inch mat will save you money and weight).
