## The castable sonar that thinks it's a fish finder

You've seen the $50 castable depth finders that ping a number and call it a day. The Deeper PRO+ 2 is not that. This is a legitimate dual-frequency CHIRP sonar unit packed into a 4.1-ounce pod that you lob into the water like a lure. It's the middle child of Deeper's lineup — more capable than the entry-level START, less pricey than the CHIRP+ — and for shore anglers, kayakers, and ice fishermen, it might be the only fish finder you need.

## What's actually in the box

The retail package includes the PRO+ 2 sonar unit, a USB-C charging cable, a woven lanyard, and a foam carrying case with a carabiner. There's no charger brick — you'll use your phone's. The unit itself measures roughly 2.6 inches in diameter and weighs about 4.6 ounces, which is light enough to cast with a medium-action rod without feeling like you're lobbing a sinker. The app (Deeper Smart Sonar) is free for iOS and Android, and you'll need it — the unit has no display of its own. Everything runs through your phone via Wi-Fi.

## How it performs on the water

The dual-frequency CHIRP is the headline. At 240kHz, you get high-resolution returns for shallow water and tight target separation — meaning you can distinguish a school of crappie from a single bass hugging the bottom. At 100kHz, the cone widens and penetrates deeper, hitting that advertised 90-meter max depth. In practice, most anglers running freshwater lakes won't exceed 30-40 meters, and the unit handles that range confidently.

The GPS mapping is where this earns its keep. While casting and retrieving, the PRO+ 2 records bottom contours and water temperature, letting you build a bathymetric map of your local lake in real time. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"I've fished this same pond for 10 years and never knew there was a 14-foot hole 20 yards off the bank. The Deeper found it in 15 minutes."* That's the kind of insight that turns a mediocre spot into a reliable producer.

Battery life is genuinely impressive. Deeper advertises 9 hours, and user reports consistently confirm 7-9 hours depending on temperature and Wi-Fi use. The USB-C charging is a welcome upgrade over older micro-USB models — a full charge takes about 2.5 hours.

## What buyers consistently praise

The portability factor dominates praise across 210+ ratings. Kayak anglers love that it eliminates transducer mounting headaches. Ice fishermen appreciate being able to drop it down a hole without drilling a second one for a transducer. Shore anglers like that they can scout structure before committing to a spot.

Another recurring theme: the app's fish icon feature. When sonar returns look like a fish, the app marks it with a fish icon — but it also shows raw sonar data, so you're not blindly trusting an algorithm. One reviewer noted, *"The fish icons are a nice touch, but the raw sonar history is the real tool. I caught my PB bass by reading the arch pattern, not the icons."*

## Where it falls short

Honest downsides matter. The Wi-Fi range is the biggest practical limitation. Deeper claims a 100-meter operating range, but that's optimistic. In real-world conditions — light chop, your phone in a pocket, the unit bobbing at the surface — expect reliable connection to about 30-40 meters. Beyond that, the app buffers or disconnects, and reconnecting mid-cast is annoying.

The plastic housing is another concern. It's not fragile, but it's not rugged either. Several reviewers report hairline cracks after repeated hard casts into rocky banks. The included lanyard helps, but if you're a heavy shore caster, consider using a braided line with a heavier leader to reduce impact force.

One design quirk: the unit floats with the transducer facing downward, but if it lands upside down in heavy chop, the Wi-Fi signal can degrade. It self-corrects eventually, but it's a momentary frustration.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it for:** Shore anglers who fish multiple spots in one session. Kayak and canoe fishermen who want structure mapping without permanent transducer installation. Ice anglers who want to scout holes quickly. Anyone who wants GPS bathymetry without buying a $500 chartplotter.

**Skip it if:** You fish from a boat with a bow-mounted transducer and already have a networked fish finder. You need vibration alerts or audible alarms — this unit has none. You're on a strict budget and only fish shallow, familiar water — the cheaper Deeper START handles basic depth and temp well enough.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use the Deeper PRO+ 2 from a kayak or moving boat?**
A: Yes, but with limits. It works while trolling at slow speeds, but the Wi-Fi connection gets unreliable above 2-3 mph. For structure mapping, cast it out, let it drift, and retrieve slowly.

**Q: Does it work in saltwater?**
A: Yes, the housing is sealed and rated IPX7 waterproof. However, if you fish saltwater regularly, rinse the unit in fresh water after each trip to prevent corrosion on the charging port and seams.

**Q: How does it compare to the Deeper CHIRP+?**
A: The PRO+ 2 uses the same dual-frequency CHIRP but has a longer battery life (9 hours vs 7) and a slightly smaller form factor. The CHIRP+ has a built-in memory card slot for storing sonar logs — the PRO+ 2 does not. If you want to review past trips, get the CHIRP+.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The Deeper PRO+ 2 is a genuinely useful tool, not a gimmick. It delivers real sonar data, GPS mapping, and portability in a package that fits in your tackle box. The Wi-Fi range and plastic build are legitimate compromises, but for the price, you're getting capabilities that would cost 2-3x more in a traditional fish finder setup.

**Buy it if** you fish from shore, a kayak, or through the ice and want to understand what's under the water.

**Skip it if** you need rugged durability for heavy casting, boat-mounted convenience, or audible alerts — this unit requires deliberate use and a phone in hand.

The 4.2-star rating from 210 buyers reflects a product that delivers on its core promise with minor, manageable flaws. For the shore angler looking to level up, this is the one.
