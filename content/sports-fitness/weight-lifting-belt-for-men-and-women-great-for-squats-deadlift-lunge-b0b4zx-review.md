## Where It Falls Short

This is not a powerlifting belt. The 6mm neoprene construction provides support, but it lacks the rigid, uncompromising stiffness of a 10mm or 13mm leather belt. If you're pulling or squatting over 405 pounds regularly, you'll eventually outgrow this belt's ceiling.

The Velcro secondary strap is the weak point. Several long-term reviewers report that grip diminishes after 6-12 months of heavy use. One reviewer stated, "The velcro started slipping on heavy squats around month eight. I had to replace it." It's a wear-and-tear issue, not a design flaw, but worth knowing before you commit.

Sizing is a minor consideration. The belt runs slightly large — order down if you're between sizes. The medium fits a 28-34 inch waist, which covers most women and lighter men, but check the size chart carefully against your measured waist, not your pant size.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this belt legal for powerlifting competitions?**
A: No. Most federations require a single-prong or lever belt made of leather or rigid material. The neoprene construction disqualifies this from sanctioned meets.

**Q: How tight should I wear it?**
A: Tight enough that you can't slide a flat hand between the belt and your abdomen, but not so tight that you can't take a full breath. You should be able to brace against the belt, not have it compress you.

**Q: Can I use this for CrossFit or general gym work?**
A: Yes, and it's actually better suited for that than strict powerlifting. The comfort and adjustability make it ideal for workouts where you're moving between different lifts quickly.
