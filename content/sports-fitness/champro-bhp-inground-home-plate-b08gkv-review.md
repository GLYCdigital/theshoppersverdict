## What You Actually Get

This isn’t a surface-mount mat. The CHAMPRO BHP is a regulation 17-inch inground plate with a 1-inch tapered profile that transitions smoothly from dirt to rubber. The box contains just the plate itself — no anchor spikes or installation kit. You supply the shovel, the level, and the anchoring hardware. The five pre-drilled holes accept standard ground spikes or screws, which is exactly what you want for a permanent installation.

As shown in the product image, the design is straightforward: solid black rubber, beveled edges, and a slightly hollowed underside that channels water instead of trapping it. That hollow base is smarter than it looks — it prevents the plate from becoming a mud cup after rain.

## Real-World Performance

Here’s where this plate earns its keep. The tapered edges sit flush with the surrounding dirt, so cleats don’t catch and slides don’t end with a foot jammed under the lip. Multiple reviewers mention this exact benefit. One buyer noted, "My rec league team plays 3 nights a week and this plate hasn’t moved an inch since I anchored it. The bevel is perfect — no more tripping over the edge." That consistent contact with the ground also means fewer bad hops for catchers.

The rubber compound is noticeably denser than the $25 plates you’ll find at big-box stores. It doesn’t flex under a 200-pound catcher’s block, and it holds up to sun exposure without cracking. The black color fades slightly over a full season in direct sun, but that’s cosmetic — the structural integrity stays intact.

## Where It Falls Short

You need to do the installation right. This is not a "lay it on the grass and go" product. You’ll dig a hole about 2 inches deep, set the plate, backfill, and tamp. Skip the anchoring and the plate will shift laterally during slides. A few reviewers complained about this, but honestly, that’s user error — the product description clearly states it’s an inground plate.

Also, a handful of buyers received units with surface scuffs or small mold marks from the manufacturing process. None reported functional defects, but if you’re particular about aesthetics, inspect it on arrival and don’t hesitate to use Amazon’s return policy for a replacement.

## Who Should Buy This

This plate is ideal for three specific buyers: youth baseball leagues that need a durable plate for multiple games per week, backyard parents building a permanent practice mound who want something that lasts longer than one season, and coaches maintaining a field who are tired of replacing warped plates annually.

Skip it if you need a portable plate for driveway practice or you’re renting a field where you can’t dig. For those uses, a surface-mount base or a weighted rubber mat makes more sense.

## FAQ

**Q: Does it come with anchor spikes?**
A: No. You’ll need to supply your own ground spikes or heavy-duty screws. The five pre-drilled holes are standard size, so most hardware-store anchors work fine.

**Q: Can I use this on artificial turf?**
A: Technically yes, but inground plates are designed for dirt. On turf, the hollow base creates a slight gap underneath that could cause movement. Use an anchor specifically designed for turf, or stick with a surface-mount plate for artificial surfaces.

**Q: How deep does the hole need to be?**
A: Aim for 1.5 to 2 inches deep, matching the plate’s height. The goal is to have the top surface level with the surrounding ground. Use a rubber mallet to seat it, then backfill and tamp firmly.
