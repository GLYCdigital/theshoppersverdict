If you own PowerBlock adjustable dumbbells, you already know the problem: where do you put the thing between sets? Setting it on the floor risks scratching the weight plates and bending over for every rep. The PowerBlock Large Compact Stand solves that — mostly.

This isn't a glorified shelf. It's a welded steel frame with a wide, stable base and a foam-padded cradle that holds your dumbbell securely at waist height. The stand measures roughly 24 inches deep, 29 inches wide, and 20 inches tall, designed specifically for the PowerBlock Elite EXP and Pro EXP series (both the 50 lb and 70 lb versions). It's rated to hold up to 175 lbs, so even the fully loaded Pro EXP with expansion kits sits safely.

The headline feature is the fold-flat design. A single locking mechanism lets the legs collapse so the entire stand compresses to about 8 inches deep. That's a genuine space-saver for apartment gyms or anyone who shares a room with their workout equipment. One buyer noted, "I live in a 700 sq ft apartment and this thing disappears behind my door when I'm done." That tracks with what I found — the folding mechanism is smooth, locks solidly in both positions, and doesn't loosen up over time.

Build quality is where PowerBlock earns its reputation. The frame is 14-gauge steel, powder-coated, with no sharp edges or exposed welds. The foam padding on the cradle is dense enough to protect the urethane coating on your dumbbells but not so soft that the weight sinks in. In use, there's zero wobble even when you're racking the dumbbell aggressively between heavy sets. The base has rubber feet that grip most flooring types, though on smooth concrete you might want to add your own non-slip pad.

What buyers consistently praise (4.8 stars across 240 ratings isn't an accident):

- **Stability under load** — multiple reviewers mention it feels "rock solid" even with the 90 lb expansion kit loaded. One wrote: "I was worried the folding mechanism would flex under heavy weight. It doesn't. This thing is a tank."
- **Assembly is quick** — most people report 10-15 minutes with the included hex wrench and bolts. The instructions are clear, and parts are labeled.
- **Height is right** — the cradle sits at roughly 20 inches, which puts the dumbbell at a natural grabbing height for most users (5'5" to 6'2"). No bending or awkward reaching.

Where it falls short:

- **No wheels** — at about 20 lbs, it's light enough to carry, but you won't want to drag it across the room mid-workout. If your gym setup changes frequently, this is an annoyance.
- **PowerBlock-specific only** — this is a one-trick pony. If you switch to traditional dumbbells or another adjustable brand, this stand is useless. It's a commitment to the ecosystem.
- **Small parts frustration** — the included Allen wrench is cheap and strips easily. A few buyers recommend having your own hex key set on hand before you start assembly. Minor, but worth knowing.

**Who should buy this:**

- **PowerBlock owners with limited floor space** — the fold-flat design genuinely earns its keep in small rooms, apartments, or garage gyms where you park a car.
- **Anyone doing drop sets or supersets** — having the dumbbell at waist height instead of the floor saves your lower back and keeps your pace up.
- **Buyers who value stability over portability** — if the stand stays in one spot, this is a permanent, wobble-free home for your weights.

**Who should skip it:**

- **Multi-brand dumbbell owners** — you need something more universal, like a traditional dumbbell rack.
- **People who move their equipment constantly** — the lack of wheels and the 20-lb weight make this more of a "set it and forget it" piece.

**FAQ:**

**Q: Will this hold the 90 lb PowerBlock expansion?**
A: Yes. The frame is rated for 175 lbs, so even the fully expanded Pro EXP at 90 lbs per dumbbell sits with room to spare. The foam cradle is sized for the wider Pro EXP handle too.

**Q: Does it fit the older PowerBlock Sport models?**
A: The Sport series uses a different base shape, so it's a tighter fit. It'll rest on the cradle but won't sit as securely. If you have the Sport 2.0 or earlier, measure your dumbbell base before buying.

**Q: Can I use it for push-ups or as a bench?**
A: No. It's a rack, not a bench. The top surface is a flat cradle, not padded for bodyweight exercises. Don't try it — you'll damage the foam and potentially the frame.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5 — Buy it if you own PowerBlocks and value floor space. Skip it if you're not committed to the PowerBlock ecosystem.**

This is a purpose-built accessory that does exactly what it's supposed to do, does it well, and doesn't pretend to be anything else. The folding mechanism, build quality, and stability justify the price for anyone who's already invested in PowerBlock dumbbells. It's not a glamorous purchase, but it's one you'll use every single workout — and that's what matters.

The only real reasons to pass are if you don't own PowerBlocks or you need something with wheels. Otherwise, this is the stand your dumbbells deserve.
