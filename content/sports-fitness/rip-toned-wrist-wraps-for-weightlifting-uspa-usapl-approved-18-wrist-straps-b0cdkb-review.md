## Where They Fall Short

Nothing’s perfect. The thumb loop is on the snug side – if you have larger hands or wear lifting gloves, it might feel restrictive. I’d recommend sizing up if you’re between sizes. Also, these are *powerlifting* wraps, not cross-training bands. They’re meant to lock your wrist in a fixed position. If you do Olympic lifts or kettlebell work that requires wrist mobility, you’ll find them too stiff. They’re a specialized tool, not a one-size-fits-all solution.
