Let's be direct — no mat is perfect. The rubber backing has a noticeable chemical odor when you first unbox it. It dissipates after a few days, but if you're setting this up indoors, plan to air it out in the garage first. Several reviewers flagged this, and it's a legitimate nitpick.

The other issue is wind. At 4x6 feet, the mat has enough surface area to act like a sail in gusty conditions. The rubber backing helps, but a strong breeze can still lift the corners. You'll want to anchor them with some weight if you're practicing outside on a windy day. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

And while the turf holds up well to bat drag, the color will fade if you leave it in direct sunlight for months at a time. It doesn't affect performance, but if you care about aesthetics, store it or flip it when not in use.
