After repeated machine washing (which the manufacturer warns against), the dye fades to a washed-out salmon. Air-dry only, and spot-clean if you want the color to last.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will these work with other cable machines, not just Tonal?**
Yes, the carabiner fits standard 10mm cable attachments. The clip is slightly oversized for Tonal’s ball mount, but it works on universal machines too.

**Q: Are they machine washable?**
Technically yes, but hand-wash cold and air-dry. Machine washing accelerates the fading and can weaken the neoprene bonding over time.

**Q: How much resistance can they handle?**
The hardware is rated well above home-gym use. The limiting factor is your ankle’s tolerance, not the strap. I’ve used them at 60 pounds without any concern.
