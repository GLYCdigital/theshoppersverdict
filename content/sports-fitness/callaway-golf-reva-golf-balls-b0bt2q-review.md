If you're a woman golfer who's tired of playing "shrunken men's balls" that feel like rocks off the putter face, the Callaway Reva is the ball you've been waiting for. With a 4.7-star average across 235 Amazon ratings, this isn't a niche product — it's a legitimately popular option that's clearly resonating with its target audience.

## What's in the Box

You get a standard dozen of Callaway Reva golf balls, available in white, pink, and a bright optic colorway. The Reva line is Callaway's first women's-specific ball in years, and it shows in the construction. These are two-piece balls with a large core designed around one thing: getting the ball airborne with moderate swing speeds.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the Reva earns its keep. The ball features a high-launch design that's genuinely noticeable on the course. If your driver swing speed sits between 60 and 85 mph — which is the reality for most amateur women players — this ball gets up quickly and carries further than a standard men's ball of similar compression.

The soft feel is the standout feature. At roughly 50 compression, the Reva feels almost marshmallow-like off the putter and wedges. One Amazon reviewer summed it up: "I've played 20 years and these are the first balls that feel truly designed for my swing. The feel around the greens is incredible compared to the hard balls I was using before."

The hex aerodynamics — borrowed from Callaway's premium Chrome Soft line — keep the ball stable in crosswinds better than you'd expect from a value-priced ball. It won't cut through wind like a Tour ball, but it's noticeably more predictable than other balls in this price tier.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be straight about the limitations. If you swing faster than 95 mph, the Reva's soft core will produce too much spin and the ball will balloon on you, costing distance. This is a ball designed for a specific player profile, and it commits to that profile fully.

The white version also shows scuffs easily. The cover is soft (which gives that great feel), but that means it's more prone to marking on cart paths or hardpan. The pink and optic versions hide cosmetic wear better.

Some golfers have also noted the ball doesn't spin much on approach shots into greens. For players who like to stop the ball quickly on firm greens, the Reva will release and roll out more than a urethane-covered premium ball.

## Who Should Buy This

The Reva is perfect for:

- **Women golfers with moderate swing speeds** — if you're just learning or play recreationally, this ball is forgiving and helps you get the ball in the air
- **Juniors and seniors** — anyone with a slower swing speed benefits from the high launch and soft feel
- **Golfers who want confidence off the tee** — the ball launches high and straight, which helps with both distance and confidence

Skip it if you're a low-handicap woman with a fast swing who needs maximum spin control around greens, or if you're a male golfer with above-average swing speed.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these balls legal for tournament play?**
A: Yes, the Reva is on the USGA conforming ball list. It's a standard two-piece ball with no performance-enhancing tricks.

**Q: How do these compare to the Callaway Supersoft?**
A: The Reva has a slightly larger core and is designed specifically for lower swing speeds. For women or seniors, the Reva will launch higher and feel softer. For faster swingers, the Supersoft is a better fit.

**Q: Do these come in different compression options?**
A: No, there's only one compression level (around 50). That's the point — Callaway designed one ball specifically for the moderate-swing-speed player.
