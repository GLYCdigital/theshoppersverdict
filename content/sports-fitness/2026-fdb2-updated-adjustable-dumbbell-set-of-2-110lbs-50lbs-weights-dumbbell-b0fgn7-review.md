**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs:

**Not drop-proof.** The instructions are clear: no dropping from overhead. The internal mechanisms can shift if you slam these down hard. If you're a CrossFit-style lifter who throws dumbbells after sets, buy a different product.

**The 50lb ceiling.** Strong lifters will hit this limit fast. If you're already dumbbell-pressing 80s or 90s, skip these.

**Cradle footprint.** The cradles are wider than some competitors, so measure your space. Each dumbbell in its cradle is roughly 17 inches long.
