## Where It Falls Short

Be honest about the limitations. **Sizing is the biggest complaint.** Multiple reviewers state the shirt runs small — one parent said, “My 10-year-old wears a youth large in everything, but needed the XL in this.” Measure your kid’s chest before ordering and size up.

Second, the padding is moderate, not heavy-duty. It’s ideal for recreational and middle school play. If your kid is facing 70+ mph pitching in competitive travel ball, this won’t provide the impact absorption of a hard-shell protector. It’s a compression shirt with foam, not a catcher’s chest guard.

Finally, the sleeveless design leaves shoulders and upper arms unprotected. That’s fine for most positions, but if your kid plays first base or pitches regularly, consider whether they need additional coverage.
