If you're over 6 feet tall, you already know the struggle: every "standard" 68-inch yoga mat leaves your heels hanging off the back edge and your palms searching for grip at the front. This 84-inch mat addresses that exact problem — and based on 235 ratings averaging 4.7 stars, it's solving it well for a lot of tall practitioners.

## What You Actually Get

This is a full-length mat that measures 84 inches long, giving you a solid 16 inches more than a standard mat. The width is also generous, so you're not trading length for cramped lateral space. The thickness is the other headline feature — this is a plush mat designed for joint comfort, not a thin travel mat. It arrives rolled tight, and you'll want to give it a day to flatten out completely. There's no carry strap or bag included in what I can see, so factor that in if you plan to move it between home and studio.

## Performance and Build Quality

The surface texture offers solid grip for both palms and feet during downward dog and sun salutations. It's not a sticky, rubbery feel like some premium natural mats — it's more of a soft, slightly cushioned grip that works well for most flows. As shown in the product image, the mat's thickness is clearly visible at the edges; it's substantial enough that you won't feel the floor through it during knee-down lunges or seated poses.

Where it really shines is floor work. If you have sensitive knees or wrists, the extra cushioning is a genuine game-changer. One reviewer noted, *"I'm 6'4\" and for the first time my feet don't hang off the end. The padding saved my knees during low lunges."* Another mentioned that the width gave them confidence during wide-legged forward folds without worrying about falling off the edge.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The overwhelming theme in positive reviews is simple: finally, a mat that fits. Tall users repeatedly mention that they no longer have to adjust mid-pose to stay on the mat. The thickness also gets consistent shoutouts for making floor poses comfortable. Durability seems solid — multiple reviewers report using it daily for months with no peeling or compression at the edges.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. This mat is heavy and bulky. At this size and thickness, it's not something you'll want to strap to your backpack and bike to class. It's a home-practice mat through and through. The thickness also creates a slight instability for balance poses — one reviewer put it well: *"It's like practicing on a supportive cloud, but tree pose feels a bit wobbly compared to my thin mat."* If you're primarily doing hot yoga or power vinyasa where you sweat heavily, the surface may get slicker than you'd like without a towel.

## Who Should Buy This

This mat is built for three specific groups: tall practitioners (6'2" and up) who are tired of their limbs hanging off the edge, anyone with knee or wrist sensitivity who needs extra padding for floor work, and people who practice at home and want a comfortable, forgiving surface. If you're petite, travel regularly with your mat, or prioritize maximum grip for hot yoga, this probably isn't your best bet.
