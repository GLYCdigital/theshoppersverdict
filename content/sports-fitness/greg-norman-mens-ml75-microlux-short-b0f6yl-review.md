The Greg Norman ML75 Microlux Short is the kind of product that doesn't look revolutionary on paper — a polyester-blend golf short with stretch and a moisture-wicking finish. But 220 Amazon reviewers have pushed it to a 4.4-star average, and after digging through the feedback and specs, I get why. This is a genuinely well-executed take on the modern athletic short that happens to work for golf, the gym, and casual wear without trying too hard.

**What You Actually Get**

The ML75 uses Greg Norman's Microlux fabric — a lightweight performance weave that's noticeably thinner than the brand's standard golf shorts. You're getting a 9-inch inseam (based on the product images and sizing chart), a flat-front waistband with belt loops, and five pockets: two front, two rear, plus that hidden zip pocket on the right hip. No liner. No compression layer. Just a clean, simple short that weighs almost nothing in hand.

**How It Performs in Real Use**

The fabric is the headline here. It's soft — not that stiff, swishy polyester you get from budget golf brands — and it stretches in all directions without losing shape. Multiple reviewers mention wearing these for 18 holes and forgetting they're on. One buyer wrote, "These are the most comfortable golf shorts I've owned. The material is so light you don't feel it, and they dry fast if you get caught in a shower." That tracks with the Microlux claim: it's a quick-dry weave that handles humidity better than cotton or standard poly.

Fit is where this short earns its keep. The waistband sits true to size — most reviewers say order your normal pant size — and the cut is athletic without being skinny. The rear doesn't gap when you bend or squat, which is a complaint you see constantly on cheaper golf shorts. The 9-inch inseam hits right around the knee for most guys, giving you a modern but not trendy look.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Scanning the reviews, three themes dominate. First, comfort: "The best feel of any golf short I've tried. It's like wearing nothing." Second, breathability: the Microlux fabric genuinely moves air, making these a warm-weather pick. Third, value — at this price point, buyers repeatedly note these outperform shorts costing twice as much from bigger names.

**Where It Falls Short**

Nothing's perfect, and the ML75 has two real issues. The light colorways — khaki, white, light gray — are semi-sheer in direct sunlight. Multiple reviewers flagged this. If you're wearing these to the gym or running errands, it's not a big deal. On a sunny fairway with people behind you, it's noticeable. Sizing up or sticking to darker shades solves it, but it's worth knowing before you order.

Second, there's no liner. If you're buying these for the gym or running, you'll need to wear underwear or compression shorts underneath. The fabric is breathable, but it's not designed to be worn commando during high-sweat activities. Golfers won't care; gym-goers might.

**Who Should Buy These**

Golfers who want a lightweight, breathable short for warm-weather rounds — these are ideal. Guys who need one short that works for the course, the gym, and casual Friday at the office — this fits that bill. Avoid them if you're looking for a lined athletic short or if you only wear darker colors and want maximum opacity in light shades.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Do these shrink in the dryer?**  
No. Multiple reviewers confirm they hold their size after washing and drying on normal heat. The Microlux fabric is stable — no surprises after the first wash.

**Are they true to size?**  
Yes. Order your normal waist size. If you're between sizes, size up — the waistband runs slightly snug on the larger end of each range.

**Can I wear these casually, or are they strictly golf?**  
Both. They look clean enough for casual wear and the fabric doesn't scream "golf." Pair them with a polo or a plain tee and they work fine.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a lightweight, breathable short that performs on the course and looks decent off it. The Microlux fabric is genuinely comfortable, the fit is solid, and the value is strong.

**Skip it if:** You need a lined athletic short for the gym, or you're set on light colors and concerned about sheerness.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A well-made, comfortable golf short with one fixable flaw in light colors. At this price, it's an easy recommendation for most guys.
