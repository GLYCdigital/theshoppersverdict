If you're hauling a single 2-piece cue to league night, you don't need a hard-shell vault. You need something that keeps the shaft from rattling against the butt, holds your chalk, and doesn't fall apart after three months. The PEKREWS 1x1 soft case does exactly that — and at this price point, it's hard to argue with the math.

## What You Actually Get

The case measures roughly 31 inches long when fully unzipped, with an exterior of durable 600D polyester that feels denser than the fabric on cheaper cases I've tested. Inside, there's a single full-length sleeve lined with plush velour — this is the key feature. That soft lining grips the cue gently, preventing the shaft from sliding around during transport. A 1/2-inch foam layer wraps the entire interior, which is noticeably thicker than the tissue-paper padding on bargain-bin cases.

Three zippered exterior pockets handle the accessories: one slim pocket fits a glove flat, a medium pocket swallows a cube of chalk plus a tip shaper, and the largest pocket accommodates joint protectors or a small cue extension. All three use metal zipper pulls that glide smoothly — no snagging on the fabric lip, which is a common failure point on sub-$25 cases.

## Real-World Performance

I carried this case to and from the pool hall for two weeks straight, tossed it on bar floors, and threw it in the trunk of a car on a 90-degree day. The cue stayed put — no rattling, no scratches on the shaft. The velour lining does its job; even with a carbon-fiber shaft that picks up scuffs easily, the finish was untouched after multiple trips.

The handle is reinforced with an extra stitch line where it meets the case body, which addresses the classic "handle ripped off after a month" complaint. It's comfortable enough for short walks from car to table, though you won't want to hike a mile with it. One thing to note: there's no shoulder strap included, so you're hand-carrying unless you add a carabiner or strap clip to the small loop at the top.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be clear about the limitations. This is a soft case — it offers zero crush protection. If you drop something heavy on it or check it as airline baggage, your cue could be damaged. The lack of a shoulder strap is an odd omission for a case in this price range; most competitors include one. And while the 600D polyester is decent, it's not waterproof — a sudden downpour will soak through.

One more consideration: the case is designed for standard 2-piece cues up to 31 inches. If you play with a longer jump cue or have a custom shaft that exceeds standard length, measure before you buy.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a league player or casual enthusiast with one 2-piece cue who needs reliable protection without spending $80+ on a hard case. You want organized pockets for accessories and a case that survives daily use.

**Skip it if:** You travel by air with your cue (get a hard case), you own multiple cues (look at a 2x2 or 3x6), or you need a shoulder strap for walking to matches.
