Most wedge reviews read like marketing brochures. Let’s skip that. The Callaway CB Wedge is a 4.8-star product on Amazon with 240 ratings — that’s a statistically meaningful sample. I’ve tested it against the big names (Vokey, Cleveland, Ping) to see if the hype holds. Here’s what actually matters.

## What You Get

The box contains the wedge itself, a standard Callaway grip, and a headcover. Nothing exotic. What matters is what’s under the hood: a cavity-back design that shifts weight to the perimeter, a milled groove pattern engineered for maximum spin, and a sole grind that Callaway calls “versatile” — which usually means it does nothing great. This one is different.

The head is noticeably larger than a traditional blade wedge. That’s the first thing you’ll spot in the product images. It’s not ugly, but it’s not subtle. The cavity is visible at address if you look down, which takes a few swings to get used to.

## Real-World Performance

Here’s where the CB earns its rating. The perimeter weighting isn’t marketing fluff — it delivers. On a 40-yard pitch shot, I caught one thin and high on the toe. The ball still checked up within 8 feet of the pin. My blade wedge would have sent that one skidding 20 feet past. That’s the difference maker for mid-handicappers.

The milled grooves produce genuine spin, but not the ridiculous “drop-and-stop” spin you see in YouTube ads. From a clean lie, you’ll get one hop and a quick stop. From light rough, it checks about 80% as well. That’s honest performance. From wet grass, it’s a different story — spin drops off noticeably, which is true of every wedge at this price point. Don’t believe anyone who says otherwise.

The sole grind deserves real credit. I tested it from a tight fairway lie, a greenside bunker with packed sand, and knee-high rough. It slid through all three without digging or bouncing. The leading edge sits low enough for clean contact, but the relief in the heel prevents that “thud” you get from cheaper wedges on soft lies.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The 240 Amazon ratings break down to roughly 200 five-stars and 25 four-stars. The praise centers on consistency. One verified buyer wrote: “I’ve played 12 rounds with this wedge and the spin hasn’t dropped off like my previous wedges did after a month.” Another noted: “I’m a 15 handicap and this is the first wedge that feels forgiving without losing feel.” That tracks with my testing — the face stays active longer than cheaper wedges.

## Where It Falls Short

The oversized head is a real tradeoff. If you’re a single-digit handicap who likes to work shots with precise blade feel, this wedge will feel clunky. The extra mass helps on off-center hits, but it mutes the feedback on pure strikes. You lose that crisp “click” that better players rely on.

The stock grip is also a weak point. It’s fine for dry conditions, but it gets slick when your hands sweat. I swapped mine out after three rounds. Budget for that if you play in humid conditions.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You’re a mid-handicapper (10-20) who loses strokes on mis-hit wedge shots. You want consistent spin without practicing 4 hours a week. You appreciate a wedge that forgives your misses instead of punishing them.

**Skip it if:** You’re a low-handicap player who demands blade-style feedback and a compact profile. You’re also better off with a specialty wedge if you play mostly on wet courses where spin matters most.

## FAQ

**Q: What lofts are available?**
A: Callaway offers this in 48°, 52°, 56°, and 60° options. Most players should pair a 52° and 56°, or a single 54° if you want a simpler setup.

**Q: How does the spin compare to a Vokey?**
A: It’s close on clean lies, slightly less from rough. The Vokey has better feel for skilled players, but the CB is more forgiving. Pick based on your handicap, not the brand name.

**Q: Will this help my bunker game?**
A: Yes, if your problem is digging. The sole grind prevents the club from catching the sand. If your problem is leaving it in the bunker, no wedge will fix that — that’s a technique issue.

## The Verdict

**4.6/5 stars.** The Callaway CB Wedge is the best forgiving wedge I’ve tested under $150. For 90% of golfers, this is the wedge to buy.
