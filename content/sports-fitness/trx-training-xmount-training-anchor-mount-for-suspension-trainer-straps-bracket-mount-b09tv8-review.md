If you've been hanging your TRX straps over a doorframe with that cheap foam anchor, you already know the problem: the door closes on the strap, the foam compresses, and you're never quite sure the thing won't slip mid-row. The TRX Training XMount solves that permanently. This is a machined aluminum bracket that bolts directly into your wall stud — rated to 2,500 pounds — and gives your suspension trainer a rock-solid pivot point that swivels 360 degrees and pivots 90.

**What's in the box:** You get the XMount bracket itself, a swivel anchor head, wood stud screws, drywall anchors, steel stud hardware, and an installation template sticker. Everything you need to mount it is there except one thing: a 3/4-inch spade bit or hole saw to drill the pass-through hole for the swivel head. That's a small annoyance — check your toolbox before ordering or add one to your cart.

**Build quality and real-world use:** The XMount is milled from aircraft-grade aluminum with a matte black finish that looks genuinely premium on a wall — far better than a plastic door anchor. The swivel mechanism is the standout feature. Once mounted, the anchor head rotates a full circle and tilts 90 degrees, which means you can attach your TRX straps from any angle without twisting the webbing. That matters during transitions between exercises, especially when you're moving from rows to chest presses and don't want to fight the straps.

The 2,500-pound rating is overkill for a suspension trainer — your body weight is nowhere near that — but it gives you confidence the mount isn't ripping out mid-session. What's actually more relevant is the stud requirement. This needs a stud, not just drywall. The included drywall anchors are rated for lighter loads and are really only there for temporary setups. If you can't find a stud, you should reconsider the purchase.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The 4.8-star rating across 240 reviews reflects genuine satisfaction. One reviewer summed it up: "This thing is rock solid. No more worrying about the door anchor slipping. Install took 15 minutes and the swivel is smooth." Another noted, "I've used it daily for six months and it hasn't budged an inch." Multiple users highlight how the 360-degree swivel eliminates strap twisting — a detail TRX clearly thought through.

**Where it falls short:** The swivel joint arrives stiff. It loosens up after a few workouts, but the first sessions will have you adjusting your movements to accommodate the resistance. More importantly, the installation process is more involved than a door anchor. You're drilling a 3/4-inch hole through your wall, so this is a permanent commitment — not a rental-friendly solution. And if you're mounting into steel studs, the included hardware works, but it's fiddly and takes longer.

**Who should buy this:** If you own a TRX or any suspension trainer and train at home more than twice a week, this mount is worth the investment. It's also ideal for garage gyms or home gyms where you want a dedicated station that doesn't require setup and teardown each session. If you're a renter, train once a month, or prefer non-invasive solutions, skip it and stick with the door anchor.

**FAQ:**

*Does it fit all TRX models?* Yes — the swivel head accepts any TRX strap with the standard snap hook, including the TRX Pro, Home2, and the cheaper TRX Go.

*Can I mount it outside?* Not recommended. The aluminum is corrosion-resistant, but the swivel mechanism isn't sealed. Keep it indoors or in a covered area.

*What if my stud isn't exactly 16 inches on center?* Doesn't matter. The mounting plate is a single point — you just need to find any stud, not match a spacing pattern.

**The Verdict:** The TRX Training XMount is the best wall anchor option for suspension trainers on the market — period. It's overbuilt, thoughtfully designed, and once installed, you'll forget it's there because it just works. The stiff swivel and the extra drill bit requirement are minor friction points, not dealbreakers.

**Buy it if** you have a permanent training space and want a rock-solid, twist-free anchor for your TRX. **Skip it if** you rent, move often, or want a tool-free setup.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting a half-star for the stiff initial swivel and the missing drill bit, but this is a buy-it-once, use-it-forever piece of gear.
