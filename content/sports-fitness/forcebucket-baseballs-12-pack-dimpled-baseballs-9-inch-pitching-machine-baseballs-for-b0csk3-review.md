## Where It Falls Short

Be honest about the limits. These are **not** regulation-weight balls. They’re lighter than a standard 5-ounce baseball, which means your swing timing will be slightly off when you transition to live pitching. If you’re training for game-day feel, you’ll need weighted balls for the final reps.

Second, the dimple pattern will scuff on rough concrete or chain-link cages. It doesn’t affect flight, but if you’re particular about equipment looking new, expect cosmetic wear within a few weeks. Finally, the 9-inch size is a hair under standard, so if your machine is calibrated for 9.25-inch balls, you may need to adjust the feed speed — a minor tuning issue, not a dealbreaker.
