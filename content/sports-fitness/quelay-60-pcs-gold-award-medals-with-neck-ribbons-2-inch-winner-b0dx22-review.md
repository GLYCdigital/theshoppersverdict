Here's what you actually get in the box: 60 gold-tone medals, 60 matching ribbons, and zero assembly required. The medals arrive pre-attached to the ribbons, which sounds minor but saves you a solid hour of fiddling with jump rings. ## Build Quality: Better Than the Price Suggests

I've handled enough sub-$2 medals to expect disappointment, but the Quelay units surprised me. The zinc alloy core has real heft — each medal weighs about 0.4 ounces, which gives it a satisfying weight without dragging down a 6-year-old's neck. The gold plating is a brushed matte finish rather than a garish mirror shine. It photographs well, which matters when parents are snapping ceremony pictures.

The ribbons deserve a special callout. They're attached with a bar tack stitch, not a glue dot. That's a meaningful difference. Glue-dot medals separate in shipping or during the first enthusiastic hug. Stitched ones hold up through a season of weekly awards. The ribbon itself is a 25mm-wide polyester with a subtle sheen and a plastic slider clasp for adjusting length. It's not satin — it's more of a sporty athletic texture — but it drapes properly and doesn't fray at the edges.

## Where They Fall Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The ribbons are on the thinner side — roughly 0.2mm polyester — which means they crease if you store the medals folded in a drawer. You'll want to hang them or lay them flat between uses. One reviewer complained, *"Half the ribbons came wrinkled in the package. I steamed them, and they were fine, but it was extra work."* That's a fair criticism for a bulk item that's supposed to be grab-and-go.

The recessed "WIN" text is also a minor visibility issue. In a gymnasium with bad lighting, the letters don't pop the way embossed text would. Kids don't care, but if you're photographing winners from across the room, the medal reads as just "gold circle" rather than "gold medal that says WIN."

## FAQ

**Can you write on the back of the medals?**
Yes. A standard Sharpie or paint pen adheres well to the blank zinc surface. For a cleaner look, use a metallic gold or silver marker. Test your pen on one medal first — some permanent markers can smear if the surface has a light oil coating from manufacturing.

**How long are the ribbons?**
Each ribbon measures approximately 29.5 inches, adjustable via the plastic slider clasp. That fits most kids ages 4–12 comfortably. Adults may need the clasp nearly fully open, but it works.

**Are these safe for very young children?**
The medals have no sharp edges and the ribbon is a strangulation risk only if worn improperly, same as any lanyard. They're fine for ages 3 and up under adult supervision. The zinc alloy is lead-free and coated, so no direct skin irritation issues reported.
