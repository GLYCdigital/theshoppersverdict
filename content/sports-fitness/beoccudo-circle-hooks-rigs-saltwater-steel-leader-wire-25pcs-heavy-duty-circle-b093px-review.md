## Where it falls short

The hook sizing runs small. A labeled 4/0 feels closer to a 3/0 from Gamakatsu or Owner. If you're targeting stripers or large redfish, size up one notch from what you'd normally grab.

The snap swivel is compact. It handles standard spoons, bucktails, and cut bait fine, but oversized plugs or heavy chunk baits will strain the connection point. For big bait presentations, I'd replace the snap with a heavier duty swivel.

The steel leader is 7-strand, not solid wire. That's actually a plus for flexibility and knot strength, but it can kink if you're not careful with twist-heavy lures. A slow retrieve with a spoon will eventually twist the leader.

**Q: Can I reuse these rigs after catching fish?**

**Q: Do these work for freshwater fishing?**
