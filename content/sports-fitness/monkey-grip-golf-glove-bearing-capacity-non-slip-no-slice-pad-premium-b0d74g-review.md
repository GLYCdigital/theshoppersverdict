The MONKEY GRIP golf glove makes a bold promise in its name: bearing capacity, non-slip, no slice. That's a lot of claims for a $15-ish glove. After digging through 210 ratings and buyer feedback, here's the honest breakdown of whether this glove earns a spot in your bag or just takes up space in the drawer.

## What You Actually Get

Out of the package, the glove feels denser than typical cabretta leather gloves. The standout feature is the palm-side silicone pad — a textured, almost rubberized layer that covers the full palm, thumb base, and extends up the fingers. This isn't a subtle texture; it's aggressive and clearly designed for one purpose: stopping the club from twisting in your hands.

The glove uses a synthetic leather back with perforations, plus a standard Velcro closure. It's available in the usual sizes, though we'll get to that sizing quirk in a moment.

## Real-World Performance

The "non-slip" claim holds up. Multiple buyers report the grip advantage in conditions where standard gloves fail. One reviewer noted, "Played 18 in light rain and my driver didn't twist once — my old FootJoy would've been soaked through." The silicone pad genuinely locks the club in place, which is particularly valuable for players with aggressive swings or sweaty palms.

The "no slice" claim is marketing overreach — a glove can't fix a swing path issue. But what it *can* do is prevent the club face from rotating in your hands at impact, which does help reduce the occasional push-slice caused by grip slippage. That's a real, if modest, benefit.

The bearing capacity pad across the palm adds durability where gloves typically wear out first. Buyers report the pad area showing minimal wear after 10+ rounds, which is solid for the price point.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

- **Grip in wet conditions**: The silicone pattern outperforms standard leather when moisture is a factor
- **Durability at wear points**: The reinforced palm outlasts cheaper gloves
- **Secure fit**: The Velcro closure and snug athletic cut hold through a full round

## Where It Falls Short

**Sizing runs small.** This is the most consistent complaint. One buyer put it bluntly: "Ordered my usual ML, couldn't get it on. Exchanged for L, fits perfect." If you're between sizes, go up.

**Initial tackiness.** The silicone texture feels odd for the first few swings — like your hand is stuck to the club. Most players adapt quickly, but if you prefer a traditional leather feel, this will take getting used to.

**Heat retention.** The non-slip layer reduces breathability. In humid summer rounds, your hand will sweat more than with a standard glove. Ironically, the glove handles that sweat well grip-wise, but comfort suffers.

**Limited color options.** You're getting black or white variations — no style choices if that matters to you.

## Who Should Buy This

- **High-handicap players** whose grip slips during the swing — this glove genuinely helps
- **Golfers in rainy or humid climates** who need reliable grip when conditions turn
- **Players who burn through gloves** at the palm and want extended durability

**Skip it if** you're a purist who wants traditional cabretta leather feel, or if you play in consistently cool, dry conditions where standard gloves already work fine.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this glove actually fix a slice?**
No. It prevents club slippage that can *contribute* to errant shots, but a slice is a swing path issue. Don't expect a miracle.

**Q: How does sizing compare to FootJoy or Titleist?**
Buyers consistently report MONKEY GRIP runs about half a size small. If you wear a regular in FootJoy, order a cadet or full size up here.

**Q: How long does the silicone pad last?**
Based on buyer feedback, the pad shows minimal wear through 10-15 rounds. It's more durable than the glove material itself.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if** you struggle with grip stability, play in wet conditions, or burn through gloves quickly. The non-slip technology works as advertised — it's a functional upgrade over standard gloves at a budget-friendly price.
