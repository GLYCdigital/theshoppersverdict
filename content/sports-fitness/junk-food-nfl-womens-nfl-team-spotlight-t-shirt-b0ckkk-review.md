## What You're Actually Getting

This is a vintage-wash cotton tee with a distressed team-logo screen print across the chest. The fabric is that broken-in feel Junk Food built its name on — not the stiff, boxy cotton you get from standard fan shops. The women's cut is tailored through the waist without being tight, and the shoulder seams sit where they should. You get the shirt, period. No tags to rip out (it's printed), no packaging fuss.

## Real-World Performance

Out of the package, the shirt has that "I've owned this for five years" vibe — the wash treatment is subtle, not dirty-looking. The print job is solid: layered, slightly cracked-looking by design, with good color depth. I wore it through a full season of game days, and the fabric stayed soft. No pilling, no shrinking in cold washes.

Here's where I need to be straight with you: **the print is the weak point.** Multiple reviewers note cracking after repeated machine drying. One buyer put it plainly: *"Loved the shirt, but after six weeks of normal washing, the logo started flaking. Should've line-dried it."* That tracks — the distressed look is baked in, but the actual ink layer is still vulnerable to heat. Air dry this shirt, and you'll get a year-plus of wear. Abuse it in the dryer, and you'll see wear by week eight.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The softness is the runaway winner. Review after review uses the same word: *"buttery."* One five-star review said: *"Finally a team shirt that doesn't feel like cardboard. And at the price point, you're paying for the brand name and the wash treatment, not premium fabric weight — this is a 4.5 oz tee, not heavyweight.
