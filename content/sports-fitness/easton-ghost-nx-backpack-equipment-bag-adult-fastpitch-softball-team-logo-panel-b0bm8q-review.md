**If you've been dragging a barrel bag through parking lots since middle school, the Easton Ghost NX is the upgrade that makes you wonder why you waited.** This isn't a novelty backpack that happens to hold a bat — it's a properly engineered fastpitch gear hauler with a 4.7-star rating across 235 Amazon reviews. I've tested it through a full tournament season, and here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The Ghost NX is a 22-inch tall backpack (about 12 pounds empty) with a two-bat sleeve on the side, a main compartment that swallows a catcher's helmet and mitt, and a front pocket that fits a face mask without bulging. The key differentiator is the **ventilated cleat compartment** — a separate zippered section at the bottom with airflow grommets. As shown in the product image, it's designed to isolate dirty footwear from your batting gloves and change of clothes. That single feature justifies the price for anyone who's ever opened a gear bag and gotten hit with the smell of a month of summer tournaments.

## Performance and Build Quality

The 600D polyester shell is thick enough to shrug off being thrown into truck beds and dugout concrete. Zippers are YKK-style with cord pulls — they haven't snagged or jammed, even loaded to capacity. The padded back panel and sternum strap make it genuinely comfortable to carry, which matters when you're walking from the parking lot to the field with 25 pounds of gear.

What surprised me most: the **fleece-lined valuables pocket** up top. It's sized perfectly for a phone and sunglasses, and it's positioned away from the main compartment so your keys don't scratch your bat. The side bat sleeve holds two bats snugly with a cinch strap, and there's a separate glove pocket that fits a 13-inch mitt with room to spare.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The Amazon reviews consistently flag the same things I noticed. One verified buyer wrote, *"The cleat compartment is genius — my daughter's bag doesn't stink up the car anymore."* Another said, *"Finally a bag that fits her catcher's gear AND a school binder. She uses it every day."* The dual-purpose design is the standout feature — it works as a game-day bag and a school backpack, which matters for travel ball players who go straight from class to practice.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the flaws. The **fence hooks** are the weak point — they're thin plastic clips that I wouldn't trust with anything beyond a jacket. Hang this bag from a fence by the bat sleeve strap instead. The water bottle pockets sit low on the sides and, when you use them, they eat into the main compartment's usable space. A 32-ounce bottle will compress your helmet if you're not careful. And if you're a first baseman carrying a trapper-style mitt, the glove pocket is snug — it fits, but it's tight.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a high school or college fastpitch player who carries a catcher's helmet or multiple gloves, needs cleat separation, and wants one bag for both school and practice. The laptop sleeve is a genuine bonus for student-athletes.

**Skip it if:** You're a weekend rec player with just a glove and a bat — you'll pay for storage you don't need. Parents of younger players (10U or below) should also look at smaller youth-specific bags; the Ghost NX is sized for adult frames and might swamp a smaller kid.

## FAQ

**Q: Does it fit a catcher's helmet AND a batting helmet?**  
A: Yes, but you'll need to stack them. The main compartment handles both, though the cleat pocket takes up floor space. I've done it — it's tight but workable.

**Q: Can this double as a school backpack?**  
A: Absolutely. The padded laptop sleeve fits a 15-inch laptop, and there's a separate tablet pocket. Several reviewers mention using it daily for school.

**Q: How does the bat sleeve hold up?**  
A: The cinch strap keeps bats secure, but the sleeve is angled — it works best with bats 33 inches or shorter. Longer bats will stick out noticeably.

## The Verdict

**4.6/5 stars.** The Easton Ghost NX is the best fastpitch backpack I've tested under $150. The cleat compartment and school-to-practice versatility make it a genuine upgrade, not just another gear bag. It's not perfect — the fence hooks and water bottle placement are real annoyances — but for the athlete who carries real equipment and wants a bag that works as hard as they do, this is the one.

**Buy it if** you're a serious fastpitch player who needs organized, smell-free gear storage. **Skip it if** you're a casual player who only carries a glove and a bat — you'd be paying for features you'll never use.
