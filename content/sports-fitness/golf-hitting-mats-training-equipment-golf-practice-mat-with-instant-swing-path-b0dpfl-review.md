Most hitting mats treat you like a robot — whack balls, repeat, hope something sticks. This one actually tells you when you're wrong. That's the hook, and it's a genuinely useful one.

The Golf Hitting Mats Training Equipment (with the swing path guides built right into the turf) solves the biggest problem with at-home practice: you have no idea if your swing is improving or just getting more consistent at being broken. The printed alignment arrows and angled path lines give you instant feedback on every shot. Hit over the top and you'll see the club track outside the line. Come from the inside and the path reads clean. It's not a $500 launch monitor, but for grooving a repeatable swing path, it works.
