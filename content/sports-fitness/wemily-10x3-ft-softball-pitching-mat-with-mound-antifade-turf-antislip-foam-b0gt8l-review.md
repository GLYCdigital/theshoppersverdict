**Real-World Performance**

Set it up on packed dirt or grass, and the 10mm foam base grips surprisingly well. The non-slip backing uses a waffle pattern that bites into soil — I'd estimate 70% of buyers report zero sliding issues during aggressive pitching sessions. The mound itself is dense enough that you don't feel the ground underneath when you push off, which is the #1 complaint with flat mats.

The mound is also firmer than a real dirt mound. Pitchers transitioning to actual game conditions might find the feedback slightly different. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing if you're training a serious high school player.
