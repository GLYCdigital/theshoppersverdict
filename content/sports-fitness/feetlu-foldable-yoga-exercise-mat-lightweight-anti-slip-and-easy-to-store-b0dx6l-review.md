**The Verdict: A Smart Middle Ground Between Travel Mat and Studio Mat**

Most yoga mats force you to choose: buy a heavy, thick mat that stays put but is a pain to carry, or buy a flimsy travel mat that rolls up small but slides all over your floor. The Feetlu Foldable Yoga Exercise Mat tries to split the difference — and for the most part, it actually works.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the mat itself (no carry strap, no bag — worth noting if you're planning to commute with it), plus a small instruction card. The mat measures 72" x 24" at 5mm thickness, which is slightly narrower than the standard 26" width most yogis are used to. The foldable design is the headline feature: it folds into four panels, roughly 18" x 12" when collapsed, making it easy to stash in a drawer, suitcase, or under a couch. That's a genuinely useful feature if you live in a small apartment or want to bring your own mat to a studio without wrestling a roll.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The surface is textured TPE (thermoplastic elastomer), which gives it a slightly grippy feel right out of the box. In practice, this means your hands and feet stay planted in downward dog even when you're sweating. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"I was skeptical about the grip, but I didn't slip once during a 60-minute vinyasa class. My old mat was like a slip-n-slide compared to this."*

The non-slip backing does its job on hardwood and tile. The mat stayed put during my test on both surfaces, though it does shift slightly on carpet — a common issue with any non-rubber mat. At 5mm, it's thinner than the 6mm standard, so you'll feel the floor more in kneeling poses. Several reviewers noted this as a tradeoff for the lightweight, foldable design. One customer summed it up: *"Great for travel and home use, but I add a blanket under my knees for floor work."*

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Reviews cluster around three themes: portability, grip, and value. The foldable design gets repeated shoutouts from travelers. One reviewer wrote, *"I fit this in my carry-on luggage for a two-week trip. No annoying roll sticking out of my bag."* The grip holds up during hot yoga, which is where cheaper mats fail. And at this price point, buyers feel they got a solid deal for a mat that does double duty as a home mat and travel companion.

**Where It Falls Short**

The fold creases are the most common complaint. The mat arrives folded, and those creases create slight bumps for the first several sessions. They do flatten out — most reviewers say within a week of regular use — but your first few practices might feel a bit uneven. The narrower width is another consideration. If you're broader-shouldered or like to spread wide in warrior poses, you'll find your hands hanging off the edges. And there's no included carry strap, which feels like an oversight for a mat marketed on portability.

**Who Should Buy This**

This mat is ideal for three types of people: travelers who want a real mat (not a thin travel sheet) that fits in luggage, apartment dwellers who need to store their mat flat and out of sight, and hot yoga practitioners who need reliable grip without paying premium prices. Skip it if you need maximum cushioning for knee-heavy practices or if you prefer a wide mat with extra real estate.

**FAQ**

*Does it actually fold flat without curling?*
Yes. The fold design means it lies flat in storage, unlike rolled mats that develop curl memory. The creases from the factory take about a week to settle.

*Is it suitable for hot yoga?*
The TPE surface grips well when wet. Multiple reviewers confirmed no slipping during sweaty sessions. Just wipe it down after — TPE doesn't absorb moisture like PVC.

*How do you clean it?*
Wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid machine washing or harsh chemicals, which can break down the TPE over time.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a portable, grippy mat that stores flat and won't break the bank. It's a genuinely versatile option that handles both home practice and travel duty.

**Skip it if** you require thick cushioning for joint-sensitive practices or want a wide mat for larger frames — the 24" width and 5mm thickness will leave you wanting more.

For the right buyer, the Feetlu Foldable Yoga Exercise Mat earns its 4.6-star rating. It's not perfect, but it nails the fundamentals: grip, portability, and price.
