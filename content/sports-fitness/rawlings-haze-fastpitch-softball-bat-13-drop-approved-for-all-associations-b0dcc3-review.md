## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The -13 drop is a double-edged sword. The light swing weight means **significantly less mass behind the ball** — older or stronger players will notice the exit velocity drop-off immediately. If your daughter is already hitting with power in 12U or above, this bat will feel like a whiffle ball bat on contact.

The alloy barrel also lacks the "hot out of the wrapper" pop that higher-end composite bats deliver. It's consistent, but not explosive. And a few buyers mentioned paint chipping near the barrel edge after heavy cage use — cosmetic, not performance, but worth knowing.
