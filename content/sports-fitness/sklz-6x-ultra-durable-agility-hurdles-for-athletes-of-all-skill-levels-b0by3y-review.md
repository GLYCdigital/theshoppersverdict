If you coach youth sports or train for speed, you know the drill: flimsy plastic hurdles that shatter on the first sidestep, or metal ones that leave bruises when you clip them. The SKLZ 6X set aims to end that cycle. After digging through 235 ratings and the engineering specs, here's the honest breakdown.

**What you actually get**

Six 6-inch hurdles, each with a spring-loaded base and a rigid top bar. The base flexes on impact and snaps back—that's the "6X" claim, meaning it's designed to take six times the abuse of standard PVC hurdles. They're molded as one piece, so there's nothing to assemble or adjust. The set stacks via interlocking slots, which means the whole thing fits in a standard duffel bag. As shown in the product image, they're low-profile and bright orange—hard to lose on a grass field.

**Real-world performance**

The 6-inch height is the sweet spot for quick-feet drills, lateral shuffles, and acceleration work. It forces high knees without over-striding. The breakaway base is the standout feature. One verified buyer wrote: *"My 230-pound linebacker fell on one during a drill. It folded flat, then popped right back. Not a crack."* That tracks with the engineering—the base is a living hinge design, not a brittle joint.

On turf and grass, the base grips well enough to stay put during hard cuts. On gym floors, they slide a bit, but that's expected—no rubberized feet on the base. Weight is light enough to carry, heavy enough to stay planted during normal drills.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The durability narrative is consistent across the ratings. **Where it falls short**

The 6-inch fixed height is limiting. If you're training for sport-specific hurdle work that requires 12- or 18-inch heights, this set won't cut it—you'll need a second, taller set. The orange color does fade after a few months in direct sun. It doesn't affect function, but if you're particular about equipment looking new, note that. Finally, there's no carrying case included—just the interlocking stack. It's fine for a gym bag, but if you're hauling these across a parking lot, you'll want a mesh bag.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these safe for young kids?**
Yes. The breakaway base means they fold flat on impact rather than catching a foot. The plastic is rigid enough for drills but not weaponized. Recommended for ages 8 and up, but a 5-year-old can run through them safely.

**Q: Can I use these for hurdle jumps?**
Not effectively. At 6 inches, they're for stepping over and quick-feet work, not jumping. If you want to clear hurdles, look at 12- or 18-inch versions.

**Q: Do they work on concrete?**
Technically yes, but they'll slide more than on turf. The base grips better on porous surfaces. For driveway training, use them on a mat or accept the slide.
