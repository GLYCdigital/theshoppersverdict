## What You Actually Get

This is a lightweight, long-sleeve training jacket purpose-built for batting practice and indoor drills. It's not a fashion piece — it's a functional tool. The shell is a durable polyester that handles bat drag and ball contact without pilling, while the interior mesh lining promotes airflow. The full-length zipper runs smooth and includes a chin guard, which sounds minor until you've had a zipper catch your jaw mid-swing. Adult sizes run S-XXL, and youth sizes are available, which is a genuine plus for families equipping multiple players.

## Real-World Performance

I've worn this jacket through live batting practice, tee work, and soft toss sessions. The mesh lining is the standout feature — it wicks moisture effectively, and you won't feel like you're wearing a garbage bag after 30 minutes of swings. The athletic cut is tailored through the shoulders and torso, so there's no excess fabric flapping when you rotate through your swing. The cuffs are snug but not restrictive, staying put during swings without riding up your forearms.

The fabric has a bit of stretch to it, which helps with full range of motion on high and inside pitches. It's also quiet — no swish-swish noise between pitches that can get distracting in a quiet cage.

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty time. The sizing runs snug — several reviewers mention needing to size up, especially youth players who are between sizes. If you plan to layer this over a hoodie or thick compression shirt, order up. The color options are also limited to standard team colorways. There's no black-and-white, no alternate accent combos. It's functional, not flashy. One reviewer mentioned the sleeves run slightly long on shorter players, so if you're buying for a smaller youth player, pay attention to the size chart measurements, not just the age range.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this jacket warm enough for outdoor spring practices?**
A: It's designed for active wear, not passive warmth. You'll stay comfortable during movement, but it's not a substitute for a heavy warm-up jacket on cold days. Layer it over a thermal if temps drop below 50°F.

**Q: How does the youth sizing compare to adult sizing?**
A: Youth sizes run true to Easton's standard chart but trend toward the snug side. If your player is at the top of a youth size range, move up to the next size or adult small.

**Q: Can I machine wash it without damaging the mesh lining?**
A: Yes, cold wash and hang dry. Multiple reviewers confirm it holds up fine. Avoid the dryer — the polyester mesh can shrink or warp.
