If you've ever bought a "throw blanket" for game day only to find it barely covers your knees, you know the disappointment. The FOCO NFL Team Color Big Logo Cloud Sherpa Blanket doesn't have that problem. At a true 60 x 70 inches, this is a full-size blanket that covers you from shoulders to feet — even if you're six feet tall. And with a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, it's clearly doing something right.

**What you actually get**

The blanket arrives folded and compressed in a clear plastic bag — no fancy packaging, no gift box. But that's fine, because what matters is what's inside. You're getting a double-sided blanket: one side is plush sherpa fleece (the "cloud" material), and the other is a smooth minky fabric printed with your team's colors and a large, centered team logo.

The print quality is the standout feature here. The logo is screen-printed directly into the fabric rather than heat-transferred or ironed on. That distinction matters for longevity — screen printing embeds the design into the fibers, so it won't peel, crack, or flake after repeated washes. One buyer noted, "The logo is huge and vibrant, exactly like the picture. I've washed it three times already and it looks brand new."

**Real-world performance**

I've tested this blanket in three scenarios: couch surfing, bed layering, and stadium-adjacent tailgating. The sherpa side is genuinely soft — not the stiff, scratchy fleece you get on budget blankets. It's a medium-weight warmth, not a heavyweight winter blanket. If you run cold, you'll want to layer it. If you run normal, it's perfect for 68-72 degree rooms.

The minky side is slick and cool to the touch, which makes the blanket reversible for warmer evenings. The stitching around the edges is double-needled and held up through multiple wash cycles without fraying. One consistent piece of buyer feedback: "This is heavier than I expected. Not weighted-blanket heavy, but substantial." That's a good thing — the weight makes it feel premium rather than flimsy.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The overwhelming theme in the 240 reviews is how true-to-life the team colors are. FOCO nailed the color matching. One reviewer wrote: "I'm a Raiders fan and I've bought so many 'silver and black' blankets that look more like gray and dark blue. This one is actually right." Another praised the size: "Finally a blanket that isn't a decorative square. I'm 6'1\" and this covers me completely."

The logo size is also a recurring positive. It's genuinely "big" — roughly 18 inches across at the center — so it reads clearly whether the blanket is folded on a couch or draped over a bed.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest issue is durability of the sherpa pile. It's soft out of the package, but sherpa fleece is prone to matting and pilling if you wash it with heavy items or use high heat. The care label says machine wash cold and tumble dry low, but multiple buyers noted the sherpa side started to look flattened after several washes. This isn't a defect — it's the nature of the material — but it's worth knowing before you buy. Wash it on gentle with similar fabrics and it'll hold up fine.

The other limitation is selection. FOCO produces these for all 32 NFL teams, but Amazon stock fluctuates wildly. If you're a fan of a smaller-market team like the Jaguars or Cardinals, you may have to wait for restocks or pay inflated third-party prices.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this blanket machine washable?**
A: Yes, but wash it cold on a gentle cycle and tumble dry low. Avoid fabric softeners — they break down sherpa fibers faster. Air drying is even better for preserving the fluff.

**Q: Does the logo fade after washing?**
A: Based on buyer feedback and my testing, no. The screen-printed design is embedded in the fabric, not applied on top. Multiple reviewers reported crisp logos after several washes.

**Q: Is this big enough for two people?**
A: Not really. It's a generous single-person blanket. At 60 x 70 inches, it covers one adult fully but won't comfortably share between two. For couples, you'd want a bedspread or larger throw.
