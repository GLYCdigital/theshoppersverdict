# TPCHOIE Large Beach Ball, 8 Packs 24" Large Rainbow Beach Bal Review

## Eight balls for the price of two — but do they hold up?

Let's be blunt: most beach balls are cheap party fodder that die within an afternoon. The TPCHOIE 24-inch rainbow set tries to change that math. At roughly 220 ratings and a 4.4-star average, buyers are mostly happy — but "mostly" carries caveats. Here's what you actually get before you commit.

## What's in the box

Eight deflated 24-inch beach balls, each with a bright six-panel rainbow design. No pump included. No repair patches. Just the balls and a small plastic inflator tube that looks like it belongs on a cheap air mattress. As shown in the product image, the colors are vibrant — think classic rainbow striping that reads well from a distance. The 24-inch size (measured after full inflation) is the headline feature here; standard beach balls run 16-20 inches, so these genuinely feel bigger in play.

## Build quality and real-world performance

The 2-ply vinyl construction is the differentiator. Most dollar-store beach balls use a single thin layer that punctures on the first sand encounter. The TPCHOIE balls survived a full afternoon of adult toss games and kids' pool volleyball in testing — no spontaneous blowouts, no seam splits. That's genuinely better than the category average.

Inflation is where things get tedious. One reviewer noted, "The included inflator is basically a straw. Inflating eight balls one by one took me almost an hour." That's accurate. You'll want a manual or electric pump with a ball needle adapter — it cuts inflation time to under 90 seconds per ball. Plan for that before you buy.

The other tradeoff: air retention. Vinyl beach balls inherently leak at the valve. Expect to top them up after 24-48 hours of storage. One buyer put it plainly: "They're great fresh out of the box, but I'm re-inflating every time we use them." That's the physics of the material, not a manufacturing defect — but it's worth knowing.

## What buyers consistently praise

The value proposition drives most positive reviews. As one verified purchaser wrote: "I paid more for two beach balls at the grocery store. Eight of these for the same money? No contest." The size also earns consistent mentions — "Much bigger than I expected" appears repeatedly in feedback. For party planners, camp counselors, and PE teachers, the bulk quantity is the selling point.

## Where it falls short

Beyond the inflation hassle, there's one quality-control note: panel color consistency. A handful of buyers reported slight shade variations between balls in the same pack — one batch had a noticeably more orange-red panel vs. the standard red. Cosmetic, not structural, but if you're buying for a coordinated event, check them on arrival.

The lack of a pump is the bigger annoyance. At this price point, including even a basic hand pump would have been a meaningful upgrade. You're buying eight balls; expect to source your own inflation equipment.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these really measure 24 inches when inflated?**
A: Yes, when fully inflated to firm (not rock-hard) pressure. Under-inflated, they're noticeably smaller. Use the included straw or a pump; measure once fully inflated.

**Q: Are they safe for toddlers?**
A: The vinyl is soft and pliable when inflated properly. The 24-inch size is actually easier for young kids to catch than smaller balls. Just supervise — any plastic ball is a choking hazard if punctured and chewed.

**Q: How long do they hold air?**
A: Realistically 24-48 hours of full pressure. After that, expect to re-inflate before each session. That's normal for vinyl beach balls — no product in this category holds air for weeks.
