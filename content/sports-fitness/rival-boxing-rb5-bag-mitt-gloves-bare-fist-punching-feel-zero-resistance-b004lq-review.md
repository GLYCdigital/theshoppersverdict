**The Verdict: 4.6/5 — Buy it if you're a serious boxer who wants honest feedback on your punches. Skip it if you're a casual gym-goer looking for maximum hand protection.**

Let's be clear about what the RIVAL RB5 Bag Mitt Gloves are not: they're not a beginner's glove, and they're not a heavy bag glove in the traditional sense. These are specialized tools designed for one specific job — giving you the closest thing to bare-fist punching without actually breaking your hands.

**What You Actually Get**

The RB5 is built around a deceptively simple concept: remove everything that gets between your fist and the bag. The open-finger design leaves all four fingers completely exposed, while a leather strap wraps across your knuckles and a padded palm section protects the impact zone. The thumb is also exposed, which takes some getting used to if you're coming from full gloves.

Construction is where RIVAL earns their reputation. The full-grain leather outer shell feels substantial, and the reinforced stitching around the palm pad held up through three months of twice-weekly bag work in my testing. The hook-and-loop wrist strap is wide enough to lock down securely, though it's not a substitute for proper hand wraps.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

Here's the thing that surprised me: these gloves actually made my punching better. With traditional 14oz or 16oz gloves, you can get sloppy because the padding masks poor wrist alignment. The RB5 gives you immediate feedback — hit with a bent wrist and you'll feel it. Hit with proper form and the "bare-fist" sensation is genuinely addictive.

The impact protection is adequate but not luxurious. The palm padding absorbs the brunt of the strike, but your exposed fingers and knuckle area will take some abrasion. One Amazon reviewer put it well: "These aren't gloves for protection, they're for perfecting your form. My hand speed increased dramatically after switching."

Another verified buyer noted, "The leather quality is excellent. I've had mine for two years of heavy use and they still look new." That durability claim matches my experience — the stitching shows no signs of wear, and the leather has developed a nice patina without cracking.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. The lack of any finger protection means you'll want to use hand wraps every session, and even then, the exposed knuckles take a beating on a heavy bag. This isn't a glove for someone with sensitive hands.

The open-finger design also means your hands are fully exposed to the elements — if you're training in a cold gym, your fingers will feel it. And the price point is premium for what is essentially a specialized tool, not an all-purpose glove.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Intermediate to advanced boxers** who want to refine their punching mechanics without the bulk of traditional gloves
- **Trainers and coaches** who need to demonstrate proper form while still protecting their hands
- **Fitness enthusiasts** who've plateaued with traditional gloves and want a new challenge

Skip it if you're a beginner building up hand strength, or if you're looking for a glove that can double for sparring and bag work. This is a one-trick pony — but it executes that trick exceptionally well.

**FAQ**

*Can I use these on a heavy bag?* Yes, but with caveats. The open-finger design means your knuckles take direct friction. Use hand wraps and start with lighter power.

*Are these worth the premium price?* If you're serious about boxing technique, absolutely. The feedback these provide is unmatched by any padded glove I've tested. For casual use, cheaper options make more sense.

*Do they run true to size?* Yes, but they fit snugly by design. If you're between sizes, size up — the leather will stretch slightly with use.

The RIVAL RB5 Bag Mitt Gloves are a specialized tool for a specific purpose, and they execute that purpose flawlessly.
