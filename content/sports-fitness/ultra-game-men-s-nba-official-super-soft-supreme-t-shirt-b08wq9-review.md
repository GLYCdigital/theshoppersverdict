**How It Performs in Real Life**

I wore this for a week straight (yes, I washed it between wears) to test the "super soft" claim. It's legitimately soft out of the package — that garment-washed feel you usually only get after a year of ownership. The real test was durability.

That last point is rare for licensed apparel — most screen-printed fan tees start flaking by wash three. The cotton breathes well, making it fine for moderate activity, but here's the honest caveat: this is not a performance shirt. It's a 100% cotton tee. If you're planning to sweat heavily in it, pick a technical fabric instead.
