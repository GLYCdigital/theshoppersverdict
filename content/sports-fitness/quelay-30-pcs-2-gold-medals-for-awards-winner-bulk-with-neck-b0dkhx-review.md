Let's cut to the chase: if you're running a youth soccer tournament, a school field day, or a church carnival, you need medals in bulk without emptying your budget. The Quelay 30-pack of 2-inch gold medals delivers exactly that — but with a few caveats worth knowing before you click buy.

## What's Actually in the Box

Open the package and you'll find 30 medals, each with a red, white, and blue neck ribbon already attached. The medals measure 2 inches in diameter — smaller than the standard 3-inch medals you see at Olympic-style events. The zinc alloy body has a gold-tone finish, and the front features a classic laurel wreath design with a star in the center. The back is blank, which is actually a plus if you want to engrave or sticker your event name.

The ribbons are 30 inches long, which comfortably fits over a child's head and still works for most adults. Each medal comes individually wrapped in a thin plastic sleeve — good for keeping them clean if you're storing them between events.

## Build Quality and Real-World Handling

Here's where the Quelay medals earn their 4.8-star rating. The zinc alloy construction feels denser than the cheap plastic medals you'll find at dollar stores. The gold finish has a brushed texture that hides fingerprints well, and buyers consistently report the plating doesn't chip or peel during normal handling.

One Amazon reviewer noted: "I've bought cheaper medals before and the gold flaked off by the end of the day. These held up through a full weekend of gymnastics meets with kids constantly handling them."

That durability matters when you're handing out 30 medals to excited kids who will immediately start swinging them around. The attachment ring connecting medal to ribbon is crimped securely — no reports of medals popping off mid-ceremony.

The tradeoff? At 2 inches, these are visually smaller than what most people picture when they think "award medal." They're closer to a participation medal size than a first-place trophy piece. If you're photographing winners, they'll read clearly on camera, but the visual impact from a distance is modest.

## Where They Fall Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. The ribbons are the weakest link. They're made from a thin polyester satin that looks nice but feels flimsy. If you're planning to use these medals for multiple events across several seasons, the ribbon edges will start to fray. One buyer mentioned: "The ribbon material is a bit cheap — it snagged on a Velcro strap and pulled a thread. Fine for one-time use, but I wouldn't reuse them."

The blank back is another consideration. If you wanted engraved medals with your event name, you'll need to budget for custom engraving, which adds significant cost per unit. At this price point, you're getting a generic medal that works for any occasion but doesn't carry any personalization.

Also worth noting: the gold tone leans slightly yellow-orange rather than true gold. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you're pairing these with silver and bronze medals from a different brand, the color mismatch will be noticeable.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy if you're:** A youth sports coordinator, elementary school teacher planning field day, church or camp organizer running a summer program, or anyone who needs 30+ medals for a single event and wants them to look respectable without spending $5+ per medal.

**Skip if you're:** Planning a formal championship event where medals will be photographed up close, need personalized engraving, or expect medals your winners will keep on display for years. These are functional, not heirloom-quality.

## FAQs

**Q: Can these be engraved?**
A: Yes, the blank back can be laser-engraved, but you'll need to find a local trophy shop or use a service. Factor in $2-4 per medal for custom engraving, which erases the cost advantage.

**Q: How do the medals fit children vs. adults?**
A: The 30-inch ribbon fits most elementary-age kids with room to spare and sits comfortably on adults. For very young children (under 5), the ribbon may hang to their waist, but it's not uncomfortable.

**Q: Are these real metal or plastic?**
A: The medal body is zinc alloy — a genuine metal, not plastic. The gold finish is plated, not solid gold, but it has a nice weight and feel.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The Quelay 30-pack hits the sweet spot for event organizers who need quantity without sacrificing basic quality. The metal construction is genuinely durable, the pre-attached ribbons save setup time, and at roughly $1 per medal, the value is undeniable.

The 2-inch size and thin ribbons are real compromises, but they're acceptable tradeoffs at this price point. If you're handing out 30 medals at a weekend event and want kids to feel proud without you feeling broke, this is your pack.

**Buy it if** you need serviceable, good-looking medals for a group event and care more about durability than showpiece size. **Skip it if** you need something with more visual heft or plan to customize each medal. For most bulk needs, the Quelay medals deliver exactly what they promise.
