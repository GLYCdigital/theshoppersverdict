Let’s be blunt: boat decking is one of those upgrades you don’t think about until you’re slipping on a wet fiberglass floor and nearly going overboard. The HZCHIONE EVA Foam Boat Flooring in camo is designed to fix that problem. At roughly 94 inches by 45 inches, this single sheet covers about 29 square feet — enough for a small jon boat floor, a center console deck, or a casting platform. It’s not a full-kit solution, but for DIY boat owners, it’s a solid starting point.

**What you actually get**

The package contains one pre-cut sheet of closed-cell EVA foam, 94 inches long and 45 inches wide, with a peel-and-stick adhesive backing. The camo pattern is printed into the material, not just painted on top, so it won’t flake off with constant foot traffic. The foam itself is roughly 6mm thick — thick enough to cushion knees during a long day of fishing but thin enough to trim with a sharp utility knife without much effort. You’ll also want to grab a roller and some isopropyl alcohol before installation, because surface prep matters here more than anything else.

**Real-world performance**

The EVA foam does exactly what it promises: it provides grip. Multiple reviewers noted that the texture holds up even when soaked. One buyer wrote, “Walked on this all summer in wet shoes, never slipped once.” That’s the core value proposition — safety and comfort on a boat deck that gets slick with water, fish slime, and sunscreen.

The camo pattern is a practical choice, not just a style statement. Darker patterned surfaces hide scuffs, dirt, and minor stains far better than light gray or white decking. Several customers mentioned this specifically, with one noting, “Scratches and mud don’t show nearly as bad as the old gray carpet.”

Heat is another consideration. EVA foam can get hot under direct sun, but the camo pattern seems to mitigate that somewhat — dark green and brown patches don’t absorb heat as aggressively as solid black. It’s not cool to the touch, but it’s tolerable with bare feet on a July afternoon.

**The honest downsides**

Here’s where you need to pay attention. This is a single sheet, not a custom-cut kit. That means you’re responsible for measuring, cutting, and fitting it to your deck. If your boat has curves, hatches, or irregular shapes, you’ll be doing some careful template work. It’s manageable, but it’s not a 15-minute install.

Adhesion is the bigger concern. The 3M-style adhesive backing is strong, but it only works on clean, dry, and properly prepared surfaces. Several reviewers reported peeling issues, and in nearly every case, it traced back to inadequate surface prep. One customer wrote, “I didn’t clean the deck well enough and it started lifting after two weeks. Redid it with alcohol and it’s been fine since.” That’s not a product flaw — that’s an installation reality. If you skip the prep step, you’ll be disappointed.

One more thing: this is a 94x45 sheet, and if your deck is larger, you’ll need to buy two and deal with a seam. The seam can trap water if not sealed properly, so plan your layout carefully.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for the DIY boat owner with a flat or gently curved deck who wants better traction without spending hundreds on professional marine flooring. It’s perfect for jon boats, bass boats, pontoon platforms, or even kayak cockpits. If you’re handy with a utility knife and have an afternoon to spare, you’ll get great results.

Skip it if you expect a plug-and-play kit with pre-cut sections, or if your deck has complex contours that require a custom-fit solution. Also skip it if you’re not willing to do proper surface prep — this product rewards patience.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Can I install this over old carpet?* No. Remove all existing flooring and clean the surface thoroughly. Adhesive will not bond reliably to carpet or deteriorated materials.

*How do I cut this cleanly?* Use a sharp utility knife with a straightedge. Score the foam firmly, then bend and snap. For curves, cut slowly with scissors. Replace blades often — dull blades tear the foam.

*Will this work in saltwater?* Yes. The closed-cell EVA structure resists water absorption and won’t rot. Rinse with fresh water after saltwater trips to keep the adhesive edge clean.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want affordable, grippy, comfortable decking and you’re willing to spend an afternoon on proper installation.

**Skip it if:** You need a full custom kit or you’re not committed to cleaning and prepping your deck surface first.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The product delivers on its core promise of grip and cushioning, with the camo pattern adding practical durability. Just respect the installation requirements and you’ll be happy with the result.
