## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The fine mesh creates drag. On fast, hard swings — the kind you need for fast-flying dragonflies — you'll feel the net push back against the air. It's manageable, but it means you need to commit to your swing rather than doing quick jabs.

The fixed 24-inch handle is a double-edged sword. For adults, it's comfortable. For smaller children (under 8), it can feel unwieldy for low-to-the-ground sweeping. If you're buying strictly for a toddler, look for a shorter-handled net. Also, the net bag is not removable for washing — you'll need to hand-rinse it and air dry.

One more thing: this is not a fishing net, and it's not designed for heavy aquatic use. The mesh will hold up to occasional pond dipping, but don't expect it to survive repeated submersion in rocky water.
