## What you're actually getting

The package includes the 29-inch magnetic holder bar, three adjustable magnetic date cards, and a set of metal hooks for hanging chart paper. The bar itself is a rigid PVC extrusion with embedded neodymium magnets across its length. It's not a flimsy strip — it has enough structural integrity to hold a full-size laminated anchor chart without bowing in the middle.

The date cards are the standout feature here. Each card has its own small magnet, allowing you to arrange month, day, and year in whatever order your lesson requires. As shown in the product image, the cards mount directly to the holder or to the board itself, giving you flexibility that most competing products lack.

## Real-world performance

I tested this across a week of daily classroom use. The magnets are genuinely strong — a standard 24x32-inch anchor chart stayed put through an entire day of teaching, including when kids bumped the board during transitions. The 29-inch width is the sweet spot: it holds standard flip chart paper with about an inch of clearance on each side, and it accommodates poster board up to 28 inches wide without any overhang.

Customer feedback consistently highlights the same strengths. One verified buyer noted, *"I've bought three of these for different classrooms. ## Where it falls short

No product is perfect, and this one has two genuine drawbacks. First, the date cards are not independently repositionable once you've mounted the full assembly — they're designed to slide along the bar, but the friction fit can be tight. If you want a completely custom layout, you'll spend an extra minute fiddling.

Second, the magnets are almost too strong for older whiteboards. On porous or worn surfaces, they can leave faint ghost marks that require a bit of extra elbow grease to remove. It's not damaging, but it's worth knowing if your board is already showing its age.

## Who should buy this

This is a no-brainer for elementary and middle school teachers who use anchor charts daily. ## FAQ

**Will this hold a heavy laminated chart?**
Yes — the neodymium magnets are rated for up to 5 pounds of pull force. A full-size laminated anchor chart weighs about 1-2 pounds, so you have significant margin.

**Can I use this on a chalkboard or glass dry-erase board?**
It works on any ferromagnetic surface. Glass boards are fine, but chalkboards with a rough texture may reduce magnet grip slightly.

**Are the date cards removable?**
Yes, they slide off the bar. They also stick directly to the board thanks to their built-in magnets, so you can reposition them independently if needed.
