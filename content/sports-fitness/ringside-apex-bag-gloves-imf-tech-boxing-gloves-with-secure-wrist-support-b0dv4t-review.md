**What You Actually Get**

The box contains one pair of gloves (obviously), and that’s it — no hand wraps, no bag, no frills. What matters is what’s built into the glove itself. The headline feature is IMF-Tech, Ringside’s layered foam system. Unlike single-density foam that compresses flat after a few months, the Apex uses multiple foam layers with different densities. The outer layer takes the abrasion from the bag; the inner layer absorbs shock without transferring all that vibration straight into your knuckles. The shell is engineered leather, which won’t crack like cheap vinyl after a few weeks of sweat. The wrist closure is a wide, 4-inch hook-and-loop strap that wraps fully around, plus an internal elastic band that keeps your hand seated.

**Real-World Performance**

I’ve trained with these on heavy bags, double-end bags, and focus mitts. The difference is immediate: you feel the impact in the glove’s structure, not your wrist joint. The IMF-Tech layering gives a satisfying “thud” on impact rather than the hollow “smack” of thinner gloves. The wrist support is the standout feature — the strap cinches down securely and stays put through rapid combinations. No re-tightening between rounds. The 10-ounce size I tested offered enough padding for bag work while still allowing fast hand speed. The perforated leather palm actually works; after a 45-minute session, my hands were damp but not soaked, which is a minor miracle for a training glove.

**Where They Fall Short**

No glove is perfect. First, the break-in period is noticeable. For the first few sessions, the IMF foam feels stiff, almost board-like. You’ll need 3-4 workouts before the glove molds to your hand shape. Second, these are strictly bag gloves. The dense padding is designed to protect *your* hands, not your sparring partner’s face. Using these for partner work will earn you enemies. Also, the thumb is attached in a semi-opposed position, which is great for clinch work but takes a session to get used to if you’re coming from gloves with a fully rolled thumb.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do I still need hand wraps with these gloves?**
A: Yes. The IMF-Tech foam and wrist strap provide structure, but wraps protect your knuckles and metacarpals from long-term impact. Don’t skip them.

**Q: How do these run for sizing?**
A: True to size. Measure your hand circumference around the knuckles. If you’re between sizes, go up — the break-in period will tighten the fit slightly.

**Q: Can I use these for Muay Thai clinch work or kickboxing?**
A: They’ll work for basic pad work, but the cuff is shorter than dedicated Muay Thai gloves. For heavy clinch or knee drills, look elsewhere.
