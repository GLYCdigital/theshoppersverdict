## Performance: Built for Forgiveness, Not Heroics

The SGI acronym stands for Super Game Improvement, and Wilson means it. The irons use deep perimeter weighting that pushes mass to the edges — miss the sweet spot by half an inch and you'll still get a playable shot. The graphite shafts are women's-specific flex, which is the right call for the vast majority of female golfers who swing between 55-75 mph.

The hybrid deserves special mention. Many beginners chunk 5-irons, but this hybrid's wide sole glides through turf and its low CG launches the ball high with a soft landing. It's the club you'll reach for on approach shots when you just want to get on the green.

The driver is forgiving, but it's not long. Expect carry distances around 130-150 yards for average swing speeds. That's not a flaw — that's a design choice prioritizing accuracy over distance. The 12.5° loft helps get the ball up quickly, which is exactly what most beginners need.

## Where It Falls Short — Be Honest With Yourself

The most significant gap: **there's no sand wedge or lob wedge.** The pitching wedge is 44°, which leaves a big distance gap between your PW and your highest-lofted option for chips and bunker shots. For beginners just learning to chip, this is workable — you'll use the PW or hybrid around the green. But you'll eventually want to add a 56° sand wedge for $40-60.

The bag is functional but not premium. The zippers feel thin, and the strap padding is adequate rather than plush. For the price point, this is expected — but if you plan to walk 18 holes weekly for years, budget for an eventual bag upgrade.

The putter is basic. It gets the job done, but serious golfers will likely replace it within a year. That's normal for package sets, but worth knowing.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these clubs suitable for tall women?**
The standard women's length is designed for heights between 5'3" and 5'7". If you're taller than 5'8", consider adding 0.5" extensions or looking at men's petite/club-fit options.

**Q: Is the bag cart-friendly?**
Yes, the bag has a pass-through slot for cart straps and fits standard golf cart compartments without issue.

**Q: Can I hit these off the fairway?**
The fairway wood and hybrid are both designed for fairway use. The driver, like most drivers, is best used on the tee.
