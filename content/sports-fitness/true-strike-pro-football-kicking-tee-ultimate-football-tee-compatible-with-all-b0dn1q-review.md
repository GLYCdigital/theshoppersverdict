Kicking tees are a dime a dozen. Most are flimsy plastic blocks that crack by midseason or wobble when you need them most. The True Strike Pro claims to be different. After digging through 229 ratings and the specs, here's the real story.

**What You Actually Get**

The True Strike Pro is a one-piece molded tee, not the cheap two-piece snap-together design you see everywhere. That means no separation point to fail. It's built for the standard NFL-size football, and the ball sits in a raised cradle with adjustable height settings. You can switch between low and high placements depending on whether you're doing kickoffs or working on field goal approach.

The base is wider than most tees in this category. That footprint, combined with the weight distribution, keeps it planted when you catch the ball slightly off-center. No more chasing your tee across the field after a shanked kick.

**Performance and Build Quality**

This is where the True Strike Pro earns its keep. The rigid plastic doesn't flex on impact, so you get consistent ball position every rep. For kickers, that's everything — you can't groove a swing if your tee shifts between kicks.

Buyers consistently mention this stability. One verified purchaser said: "I've used it for two seasons now and it hasn't cracked or warped. My old tee would tip over if I didn't place it perfectly. This one just sits there." Another noted: "The height adjustment is actually useful. I set it lower for field goals and higher for kickoffs."

The material feels dense and durable, not like the hollow, brittle plastic you find on bargain tees. It's clearly meant to survive a season of abuse. The rubberized base grips grass and turf well, and on hard ground it doesn't slide.

**Where It Falls Short**

Honestly, the flaws here are minor. The tee is bulkier than a basic block tee, so it takes up more room in your gear bag. There's no strap or carry loop, which feels like a missed opportunity for a product at this price point.

A few reviewers noted that if you're using it on extremely wet or muddy ground, the base can lose some grip. That's physics more than design, but worth knowing if you practice in sloppy conditions. Also, if you're a youth player just starting out, this might be more tee than you need — a basic block tee will do the job until you're consistently striking the ball well.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for the serious kicker — high school, college, or adult league players who want consistent reps without equipment failure. It's also a solid pick for coaches who run kicking drills and need a tee that won't need replacing every few months. If you're a parent buying for a middle schooler, the adjustable height gives room to grow as their technique improves.

**FAQ**

**Does it work with youth-size footballs?**
It's designed for standard regulation balls. A smaller youth ball will sit lower in the cradle and might not get the same clean strike. Stick to full-size balls.

**Will it scratch or scuff footballs?**
The cradle edges are smooth, so you won't see marking on your ball. The plastic is hard but not abrasive.

**Can you use it on artificial turf?**
Yes. The base grips turf well, and several reviewers use it on field turf without issues.
