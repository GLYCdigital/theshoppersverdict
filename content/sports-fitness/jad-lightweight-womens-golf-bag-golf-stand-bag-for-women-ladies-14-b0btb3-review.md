The integrated rain hood is the weakest link. It’s thin, doesn’t fully cover the clubs, and feels like an afterthought. If you play in serious weather, budget for a separate rain cover.

The pockets are water-resistant at best, not waterproof. One reviewer reported, *"My phone got damp in the valuables pocket during a sudden downpour."* Keep electronics in a dry bag inside the apparel pocket.

Finally, the color options — the pink and teal variants — look slightly more saturated in the product photos than in person. They’re still attractive, just less vivid.
