**What You Actually Get**

The package includes a set of small plastic cones—roughly 7 inches tall—in classic bright orange. The plastic is flexible enough that a stray cleat or knee won't snap them instantly, but rigid enough to hold their shape over time.

**Real-World Performance**

Here's where these cones shine. For soccer drills, ladder work, and basic agility courses, the 7-inch height is a sweet spot. **Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the limitations. These cones are light—that's a feature, but it's also a flaw. On smooth indoor surfaces like gym floors or concrete, they'll slide if you clip them. On windy days, a strong gust can scatter a course you just set up. If you're doing outdoor agility work in breezy conditions, you'll want to weigh down the bases or use a different product.

The base is also narrower than full-size cones, so on uneven ground—think bumpy backyard grass or gravel—they can tip over more easily. That's a minor annoyance for casual use, but if you're setting up a serious training course on rough terrain, you'll need to account for it.

**Who Should Buy These**

These are ideal for three specific types of buyers: youth coaches who need a safe, durable marking solution; parents setting up backyard drills for kids; and anyone doing light agility training at home or the park. They're also a great value for group fitness instructors who need to mark stations without hauling heavy equipment.

Skip them if you're a serious athlete doing high-intensity field work on windy days, or if you need cones for indoor use on slick surfaces. In those cases, invest in weighted or larger cones.

**FAQ: What Shoppers Ask**

*Are these big enough for full-size soccer field drills?*  
They work well for marking positions, zones, or small grids. For full-field scrimmages, you'll want taller cones that players can spot from a distance.

*Will they survive being run over by a lawnmower or a car?*  
No. They're flexible, not indestructible. A car will crush them. A lawnmower will destroy them. Keep them on training surfaces.

*Do they come with a carrying bag?*  
Most listings I've seen ship just the cones. The stacking holes make it easy to rig your own carry solution, but don't expect a storage bag in the box.
