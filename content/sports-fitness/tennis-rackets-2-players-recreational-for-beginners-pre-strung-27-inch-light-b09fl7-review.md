**Performance and Real-World Feel**

Here's where this set earns its rating. The pre-strung tension sits around 50–55 pounds, a forgiving range that gives you decent pop without launching every ball into the fence. The oversized head means your mishits — and as a beginner, there will be many — still clear the net with reasonable pace. One buyer noted, *"My wife and I are in our 50s, just starting out.
