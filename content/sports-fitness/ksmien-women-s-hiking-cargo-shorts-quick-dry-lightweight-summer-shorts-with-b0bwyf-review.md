**The Ksmien Women's Hiking Cargo Shorts Review: Solid Trail Value With One Size Catch**

You're standing in front of your closet at 6 AM, pulling on the same pair of leggings you've worn three days straight because nothing else works for a humid summer hike. I've been there. These Ksmien cargo shorts won't fix your laundry pile, but they just might give you a second option that doesn't feel like a compromise.

**What You Actually Get**

The package contains exactly what the listing promises: one pair of lightweight hiking shorts in a polyester-spandex blend. No liner, no belt, no extras. The fabric is thin — noticeably thin — which is either a blessing or a concern depending on how much abrasion you expect from your trails. The waistband uses an elastic band with an internal drawcord, giving you roughly three inches of adjustability. Five pockets total: two standard front, two zip-secure cargo pockets on the thighs, and one back pocket.

**Performance and Build Quality**

I've worn these on three separate day hikes — one along a rocky ridgeline, one through overgrown single track, and one casual lakeside walk. The quick-dry claim holds up. After accidentally stepping into a creek crossing, the shorts were dry within 25 minutes of continuing movement. That's genuinely impressive for the price point.

The zip pockets are the standout feature. They're positioned high enough on the thigh that a phone doesn't slap against your leg with every stride, and the zippers don't dig into your hip when you sit. One buyer noted, "The zipper pockets are perfect — I can carry my phone and keys without worrying about losing them on the trail." That tracks with my testing.

The fabric's stretch is adequate for normal walking and moderate scrambling, but it's not athletic-grade. You won't want to attempt any serious bouldering in these. The thin material also shows wear quickly — after two outings, I noticed small snags along the hem where I brushed against scrub brush. Nothing structural, but visible if you're particular.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

With 220 ratings averaging 4.4 stars, the consensus centers on comfort and utility. Multiple reviewers highlight the lightweight feel — one wrote, "Forget you're wearing shorts" — and the breathability in direct sun. The waistband's adjustability gets repeated mentions for accommodating post-hike meals without needing to undo the button.

**Where It Falls Short**

Here's the honest part. The sizing is the biggest complaint across reviews. This is not a vanity-size situation — these run small. If you're between sizes, size up. Multiple reviewers report the waistband fitting but the hip and thigh area being tight, which suggests the cut leans narrow through the lower half.

The lack of a liner is another consideration. These shorts have no built-in brief, and during steep climbs, the fabric rides up noticeably. You'll want compression shorts or a liner underneath if you're doing serious elevation gain. Also worth noting: the back pocket is essentially useless for anything beyond a folded bandana — it's too shallow for a phone and the fabric doesn't secure contents well.

**Who Should Buy These**

These are ideal for casual-to-moderate hikers who prioritize packability and quick drying over durability. If you're a day-tripper hitting well-maintained trails in warm weather, these work great. If you're a weekend backpacker who'll be bushwhacking or brushing against granite all day, look elsewhere — you'll tear through these quickly.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Do these shorts have a liner?**
No. They're unlined, so plan for separate base layers or compression shorts if you need them.

**Will a phone fit in the zip pockets?**
Yes, standard smartphones fit — I tested with a 6.1-inch iPhone. The zip closure keeps it secure.

**How do I wash them?**
Machine wash cold and hang dry. Avoid fabric softener — it degrades the quick-dry treatment over time.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want an affordable, packable, quick-drying short for warm-weather day hikes and you're willing to size up.

**Skip it if:** You're a serious trail user who needs abrasion resistance, or you're between sizes and don't want the gamble.

**Rating: 4.2/5 — Good value with a sizing caveat that's easy to work around.**

These shorts deliver on their core promises — quick dry, lightweight, secure pockets — at a price that won't make you wince. Just order a size up and keep them on the easy trails.
