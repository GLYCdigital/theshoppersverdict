**FAQ**

**Q: Do these gloves require hand wraps underneath?**
A: Not for casual training. The foam padding is sufficient for bag work and pad drills. If your kid is sparring (which I don't recommend at this age without proper supervision), add wraps.

**Q: Are they machine washable?**
A: Technically yes, but don't. Wipe the exterior with a damp cloth and air dry. Machine washing will degrade the foam and velcro.

**Q: How do I know which size to buy?**
A: Measure your kid's hand circumference at the widest point. If it's over 6 inches, size up. When in doubt, go bigger — a slightly loose glove is safer than one that restricts circulation.
