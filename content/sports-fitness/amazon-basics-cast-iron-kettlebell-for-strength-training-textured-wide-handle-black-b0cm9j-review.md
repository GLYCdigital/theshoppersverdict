**Where It Falls Short**

The most common complaint is the rounded bottom. Unlike some pricier models with flat bases, this bell rolls when you set it down. For a home gym with hard floors, it's a real consideration.

The lack of color-coding is another small gripe. If you own multiple weights, you'll be checking the embossed numbers every time. It's a minor inconvenience, but worth knowing. Finally, the powder coat will chip if you drop it on concrete. That's cosmetic, not structural, but buyers should know it's not indestructible.
