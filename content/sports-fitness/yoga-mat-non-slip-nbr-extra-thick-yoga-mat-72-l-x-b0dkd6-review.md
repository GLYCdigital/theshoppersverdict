**Where it falls short**

Be honest about the downsides before you buy. NBR is a closed-cell foam, which means it doesn't absorb water — but it also doesn't breathe well. During hot yoga or intense flow, sweat sits on the surface. You'll need to wipe it down after every session or it will develop a smell over time. That's not a dealbreaker, but it's a maintenance commitment.

The second issue is portability. This mat is heavy. At roughly 3-4 pounds and rolled up, it's about the diameter of a rolled sleeping bag. If you're walking to a studio or flying with it, you'll wish you'd bought a lighter TPE travel mat instead.

Finally, the surface scuffs. If you use this for HIIT workouts or standing exercises with shoes, you'll see marks. It's fine for yoga — just don't expect it to double as an all-purpose exercise mat without showing wear.

**Q: How thick is it actually?**
