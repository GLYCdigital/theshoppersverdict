## What You Actually Get

The set arrives with two identical graphite paddles — same weight, same grip, same balance. That consistency matters more than most buyers realize. When you're drilling with a partner or playing doubles, mismatched paddles create inconsistent reps. Uteeqe solved that by making both paddles from the same production run. You also get two edge guards installed (not the stick-on kind that peel off) and basic grip tape that's serviceable but not premium.

Weighing in around 7.8–8.0 ounces per paddle with a 16mm edge guard, these sit in the "tweener" zone — heavy enough for stable drives, light enough for quick hand battles at the kitchen.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The graphite face has a subtle textured peel-ply finish. It's not aggressive like some spray-on coatings, but it's enough to grab the ball on slice serves and backhand rolls. One buyer put it plainly: *"The spin on these is legit — I'm getting topspin drives that dip and bite that I couldn't produce with my old wooden paddle."*

The thermoformed core is where the upgrade shows. Thermoforming bonds the face to the core under heat and pressure, eliminating the hollow "tink" sound and dead spots common in cheaper paddles. The result is a solid, muted thwack at contact and a larger sweet spot that extends toward the edges. Drop volleys feel controlled, and when you need to put pace on a drive, the paddle responds without excessive dwell time.

One thing that surprised me: the sweet spot is genuinely forgiving. Mishits near the throat still produce playable shots rather than dying off the face. That's the thermoformed construction doing its job.

## Where It Falls Short

The grip circumference runs closer to 4.125 inches than the standard 4.25. Players with larger hands or those who play with a two-handed backhand will need to add an overgrip immediately, which changes the balance slightly. It's a minor issue, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

Also, no paddle cover is included. At this price point, most competitors throw in at least a basic sleeve. You'll want to buy a padded case to protect the graphite face during transport — edge chips on unprotected paddles are the most common failure mode.
