More significantly for musicians: these eggs are not pitched. Each one has a slightly different tonal quality due to natural variation in wood density and fill. In a casual setting, this is charming. In a recording session or with a pitch-sensitive ear, it's a limitation. One reviewer noted, "I use these for percussion in my acoustic sets. The pitch variation between eggs is noticeable. If you need consistent tone, buy a matched set from a music supplier instead."

The eggs also don't come with a storage bag, which seems like a missed opportunity given how easily they roll off tables.
