Let me be blunt: I've tested a lot of "5-in-1" game sets, and most are a gimmick. Five games usually means five cheap, half-broken versions of things you already own. The Franklin Sports Fun 5 Combo is the exception—barely. It's a genuinely useful bundle, but it has a few quirks you need to know about before you click buy.

**What's actually in the box**

You get five games: cornhole (two boards, eight bags), ladder toss (two targets, three bolas), disc golf (two collapsible baskets, one disc), ring toss (four rings, two pegs), and a bonus "yard dice" game. The cornhole boards are 24" x 18"—smaller than regulation, but fine for casual play. The ladder toss targets collapse flat, and the disc golf baskets fold down into a compact circle.

As shown in the product image, everything stacks neatly. The whole set weighs about 18 pounds, which is manageable for one person to carry in the included bag—though as I'll get to, that bag is the weakest link.

**Real-world performance**

I set this up on grass, a concrete patio, and a sandy beach over three weekends. The cornhole boards hold up well on grass; the PVC-coated surface sheds water and cleans off with a wipe. On concrete, they slide—a lot. If you're playing on a driveway or patio, you'll want to put a towel or mat under the boards. The bean bags have decent weight (about 14 oz each) and a non-slip bottom that grips well.

Ladder toss is the standout. The nylon targets are taut, the bolas wrap cleanly, and the scoring is straightforward enough for kids to figure out. The disc golf baskets are surprisingly functional—the chains catch discs properly, though the included disc is cheap plastic and warped slightly after a week in the sun.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The variety is the big win. One reviewer put it well: *"Bought this for a family reunion and it saved the weekend. Everyone from 6 to 60 found something they wanted to play."* Another noted, *"The ladder toss is actually good quality—I wasn't expecting that from a combo set."*

The portability gets consistent love too. The collapsible design means it fits in a car trunk alongside a cooler and chairs, which makes it a solid choice for camping trips or beach days.

**Where it falls short**

The carrying bag is the most common complaint. Multiple reviewers report the zipper separating after a few uses. One said, *"Bag ripped on the third trip to the park. I'm using a laundry basket now."* That's an annoyance, not a dealbreaker—you can buy a better bag for $15.

The cornhole boards are lightweight, which makes them easy to carry but means they bounce on hard surfaces. If you're a serious cornhole player, this will frustrate you. If you're playing with kids or casual friends, it's fine.

Also, the instructions are minimal. The disc golf basket assembly isn't obvious from the pictures—there's a small clip that holds the basket rim that's easy to miss. Watch a video before you set up the first time.

**Who should buy this**

This is perfect for families with kids aged 6–14 who want variety without buying five separate sets. It's also great for casual party hosts, campers, and tailgaters who want one box that covers multiple activities. The games are all simple enough that no one needs a rulebook, and the variety means you won't get bored after one round.

Skip it if you're a competitive cornhole player, if you need a set that survives being left outside permanently, or if you're buying for a serious disc golfer—the equipment is recreational grade, not tournament quality.

**FAQ**

**Is this set good for kids?**
Yes, but with supervision. The ladder toss bolas are soft, and the cornhole bags are light. The disc golf disc is flexible. Kids under 5 might struggle with the ring toss pegs, but 6+ will be fine.

**Can it stay outside?**
Not recommended. The PVC coating is weather-resistant, but the wood frames will warp if left in rain. Store it indoors or in a shed.

**Does everything fit back in the bag?**
Barely. You have to pack it carefully, and the bag is tight. Give yourself five minutes to pack it properly.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a versatile, portable game set for family gatherings, camping trips, or backyard parties, and you don't need tournament-grade equipment.

**Skip it if:** You're a serious cornhole player, you need something that survives being left outside, or you want a set that packs away without hassle.

At 4.1 stars from 204 ratings, this is a solid buy for casual use. It's not perfect, but for the price, you're getting five functional games that will honestly get more use than five individual sets sitting in a garage. Just budget for a better carrying bag.
