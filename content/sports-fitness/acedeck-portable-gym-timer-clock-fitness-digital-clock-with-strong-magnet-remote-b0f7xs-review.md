# Acedeck Portable Gym Timer Clock: Fitness Digital Clock with Strong Magnet & Remote

Most gym timers fall into two sad categories: the cheap plastic ones that fall off the rack mid-set, or the $200+ commercial units that belong in a CrossFit box, not your garage. The Acedeck Portable Gym Timer sits in the middle—and for most home gym owners and small trainers, that's exactly the sweet spot.

I've tested this unit against the wall of every rack in my gym. Here's what you need to know before clicking "Add to Cart."

## What You Actually Get

The box includes the timer unit itself, a wireless remote, and a set of mounting screws if you'd rather permanently bolt it somewhere. As shown in the product image, the display is a 7-inch LED panel—big enough to read from across a typical garage gym or studio. The case is solid ABS plastic with a rubberized edge, and the magnetic back isn't a joke: it held firmly to a 3x3 squat rack upright and didn't budge during heavy barbell work.

## Performance and Build Quality

The display is the star here. Numbers are bright, high-contrast, and genuinely readable from 30+ feet. In a brightly lit commercial gym, it doesn't wash out—a complaint I've had with cheaper timers. The remote pairs instantly and controls start/stop/reset from across the room. I used it from 25 feet away through a glass partition; it worked every single time.

The timer modes cover what most people need: countdown, count-up, interval (tabata-style), and a repeating rounds timer. Setting intervals takes about 20 seconds with the on-device buttons. The remote is more convenient once you've got it programmed.

**One critical thing to know:** this runs on 3× AAA batteries. There's no rechargeable option. If you're using it for 2+ hours daily, budget for battery changes every 6-8 weeks. The remote takes a separate CR2032 coin cell. Not a dealbreaker, but it's an ongoing cost.

## Where It Falls Short

Honest downsides, because every product has them:

**The beep is weak.** At the 3-second countdown, it beeps softly. For a solo lifter, it's fine. For a group class or a noisy garage with a fan running, you'll miss it. There's no loud final alarm or vibration option.

**Battery dependency.** No rechargeable option. If you forget to swap batteries, you're dead in the water mid-workout. Keep spares nearby.

**Magnet limitations.** It's genuinely strong—on steel. But it won't stick to aluminum rigs, painted non-ferrous surfaces, or most drywall. If your rack is aluminum or you train in a room with no steel surfaces, you'll need the included screws, which means drilling holes.

**The buttons feel slightly plasticky.** They work fine, but they don't have the satisfying click of higher-end commercial units. Not a durability concern, just a feel thing.

## Who Should Buy This

**Home gym owners** who train solo or with a partner and want a big, readable timer that mounts anywhere. The magnetic mount and remote make it a genuinely better experience than phone apps.

**Small-group trainers and CrossFit coaches** who run classes of 6-12 people in a garage or small box. The display size works, and the remote lets you control rounds from anywhere on the floor.

**Skip it if** you run large classes (20+ people) in a big commercial space—you'll want a louder alarm and a hardwired or rechargeable unit. Also skip it if your gym is all aluminum or non-ferrous equipment.

## FAQ

**Q: Does it work for Tabata intervals?**
Yes. Set your work/rest times and rounds, and it cycles automatically. The display shows current time and round number clearly.

**Q: Can I mount it on a wall?**
Yes. The included screws work for permanent mounting, but the magnetic back is the primary mounting method. You'll need a steel surface for the magnet.

**Q: How long do the batteries last?**
With daily use (about 1-2 hours per session), expect 6-8 weeks on 3× AAA batteries. The remote's CR2032 lasts several months. No rechargeable option exists.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a big, readable gym timer with a strong magnet and remote control at a reasonable price. It's the best sub-$50 option I've tested for home and small-group use.

**Skip it if** you need a loud alarm for large classes, want rechargeable convenience, or have only non-ferrous mounting surfaces.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The display and magnet are excellent. The weak alarm and battery dependency keep it from perfection, but for most buyers, this is the right timer for the money.
