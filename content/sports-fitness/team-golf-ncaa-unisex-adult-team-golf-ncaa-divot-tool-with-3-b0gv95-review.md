**What you actually get**

The package includes one divot tool and three metal ball markers, all featuring your chosen NCAA team's logo. The tool itself is made from zinc — solid, with a decent weight that tells you it's not going to snap in your pocket. The markers are flat discs, roughly the size of a quarter, with painted enamel logos. As shown in the product image, the branding is clear and vibrant; it's officially licensed, so the colors match your team's actual palette.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The divot tool's prongs are fixed and sturdy. On typical greens — not rock-hard municipal courses — they sink in cleanly and lift the turf without tearing. The textured grip on the handle is a subtle touch that matters when your hands are damp from morning dew or a light rain. I've tested similar tools that feel like cheap tin; this one has actual heft.

The markers are functional but basic. They're not magnetic, so they won't stick to your putter grip or a magnetic hat clip. You'll want to keep them in a coin pocket or a dedicated marker slot in your bag. One buyer noted, "The markers are a nice bonus, but I'd have paid for just the tool — it's that well made." Another echoed the durability: "Used it every round for three months. No rust, no fading on the logo."

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the limitations. The prongs are fixed, not adjustable. On fast, firm greens, you'll need to apply more pressure than you might with a two-prong adjustable tool. It's a minor gripe, but for golfers who play high-end courses with lightning-fast surfaces, it's worth knowing.

The markers also lack any retention system. They're loose discs. If you're someone who keeps markers in a pocket with loose change or tees, they'll slide around. A magnetic backing would have made this a near-perfect product at this price point.
