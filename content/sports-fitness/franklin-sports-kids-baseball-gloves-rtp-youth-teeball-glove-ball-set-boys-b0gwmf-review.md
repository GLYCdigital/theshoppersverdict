If you've ever watched a 4-year-old try to squeeze a stiff adult glove, you know the struggle — ball pops out, tears follow, and the glove ends up in the dirt. The Franklin Sports RTP Youth Teeball Glove exists specifically to solve that problem. It's the glove you buy when you want your kid to actually enjoy catching, not fight their equipment.

You get the glove itself plus a soft teeball — that's it. No mitt bag, no extras. But the ball inclusion matters more than it sounds: it's the right size and weight for tiny hands, so you're not scrambling to find a ball that fits the glove's proportions. **Build quality and real-world performance**

The headline feature is Franklin's RTP (Ready to Play) construction. This isn't marketing fluff — the synthetic leather is pre-softened at the factory, so the glove is flexible right out of the box. Parents consistently mention this in reviews: "My son was catching balls within five minutes of opening it." The 9-inch pattern is genuinely sized for youth players, not shrunk-down adult dimensions. The finger stalls are shorter and the pocket is shallower, which means kids can actually close it. One reviewer noted, "My 6-year-old can open and close it easily — his previous glove just flopped open."

**Where it falls short**

Be honest about what this is: a starter glove, not a long-term investment. The synthetic leather will soften further and eventually lose its shape, especially if left in a hot car. If your kid is 8+ or playing competitive kid-pitch, this won't cut it — you'll want a real leather glove in the $40-60 range.

Also, some colorways lack an adjustable wrist strap. If your child has notably small hands or between sizes, this can make the glove feel loose. Check the specific colorway's listing photos before buying.

**Skip it if** your kid is 8+, playing competitive baseball, or you want a glove that'll last until middle school. This is a stepping stone, not a destination.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this glove for lefties or righties?**
A: Both — you can order either throw hand. Just match it to the hand your kid throws with, not the hand they write with. Most kids under 7 haven't established dominance yet, so watch which hand they naturally toss a ball with.

**Q: How does sizing work?**
A: The 9-inch pattern fits most kids 3-7. It's measured from the base of the palm to the tip of the index finger, along the inside of the glove. If your kid is between sizes, size down — a snug glove breaks in better than a loose one.

**Q: Can this handle real baseballs?**
A: It's designed for soft tee-balls. A hard baseball will sting young hands and wear out the synthetic leather faster. Stick to tee-ball or tennis balls with this one.
