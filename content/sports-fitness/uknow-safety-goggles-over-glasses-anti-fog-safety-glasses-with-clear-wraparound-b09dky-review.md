## Where It Falls Short

Be honest with yourself about the tradeoffs. The head strap is the weak point — it's not replaceable, and several long-term reviewers report it losing elasticity after 6-8 months of daily use. When that happens, the goggles sit looser and the seal breaks, which compromises both fog resistance and impact protection.

Also, the wraparound curvature creates slight peripheral distortion. It's not noticeable during normal work, but if you're doing fast head movements — say, cycling in traffic or playing sports — you'll catch a subtle "fisheye" effect at the edges. This is a work goggle, not a sport goggle, despite being listed under sports equipment.
