Here's the thing about buying a volleyball online: you're rolling the dice on whether it'll feel like a rock or a balloon when it arrives. The Saretas Soft Beach Volleyball splits the difference, and for most casual players, that's exactly the sweet spot.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The box contains the ball itself — no pump, no needle, no carrying bag. That's worth knowing upfront because you'll need a pump with a needle adapter before your first game. The ball arrives deflated, so budget a few extra minutes and a trip to the garage before you hit the sand.

At official size (65-67cm circumference) and roughly 260-280g, it matches the dimensions of regulation balls. The composite cover is where Saretas made their design bet: it's noticeably softer than a standard leather or synthetic indoor ball. Think recreational-grade soft touch rather than competition stiffness.

**How It Performs in Real Use**

I tested this on both sand and grass, and the softness is the headline feature. Forearm passing — the skill that scares off most beginners because of the sting — feels genuinely forgiving. Kids and recreational players won't flinch when the ball connects with their arms. That alone makes it a solid teaching tool.

The flight characteristics are decent for the price point. It tracks predictably on serves and sets in calm conditions. But here's the tradeoff: because it's lighter than a FIVB-regulation beach ball, wind affects it more. If you're playing on a breezy beach day, expect the ball to drift on deep serves. Experienced players will notice the difference immediately; casual players won't care.

Durability has held up across multiple sessions. The composite cover resists scuffs from sand and concrete reasonably well. The valve seals properly after repeated inflation — a common failure point on cheap balls that this one avoids.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be direct about the limitations. This is not a waterproof ball. Leave it out in rain and the composite cover will degrade, affecting both grip and flight. It's also not a tournament-grade ball — if you're training for competitive beach volleyball, you'll want a heavier, more wind-stable option. And the soft surface picks up sand and dirt readily, so it's not ideal for transitioning between outdoor and indoor play without a wipe-down first.

**Who Should Buy This**

This ball is purpose-built for three groups: beginners learning fundamental skills without the fear of stinging forearms, schools and rec programs needing durable balls that won't intimidate new players, and casual backyard or beach players who want an affordable, reliable ball for weekend games. If you're a competitive player, skip it — you'll outgrow it quickly.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this ball suitable for indoor play?**
A: Yes, but wipe it down first. The soft cover grips well on gym floors, but sand and dirt from outdoor use will reduce traction and could mark floors.

**Q: How much air pressure does it need?**
A: Inflate to a soft-but-firm feel — roughly 4-5 psi. It shouldn't feel rock hard like an indoor ball. Squeeze it; it should give slightly under firm pressure.

**Q: Will it work for kids?**
A: Absolutely. The soft cover makes it ideal for younger players aged 8-12 who are still developing arm strength and technique.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You're a beginner, buying for kids, or need a durable practice ball that won't bruise forearms. The price-to-value ratio is strong for casual and instructional use.

**Skip it if:** You're a competitive player needing regulation weight and wind stability, or you plan to play in wet conditions regularly.
