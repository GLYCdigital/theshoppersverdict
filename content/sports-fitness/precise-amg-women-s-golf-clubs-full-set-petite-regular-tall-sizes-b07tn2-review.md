Let’s be straight: most complete women’s golf sets under $400 are junk dressed up with pink grips. The Precise AMG Full Set is not that. It’s got real engineering where it matters — sizing, forgiveness, and a complete package — but it’s also not a set you’ll keep for a decade. Here’s the breakdown.

### What’s Actually in the Box

This is an 11-piece set: a 460cc titanium driver, 3-wood, 4- and 5-hybrids, 6- through 9-irons, pitching wedge, putter, a lightweight stand bag, and headcovers for the woods. That’s a full bag — no gaps. The bag has a 14-way divided top, which is genuinely useful for club organization, and a rain hood is included, which most budget sets skip.

The standout feature is the sizing. Most manufacturers offer “one size fits most” and call it a day. Precise offers Petite, Regular, and Tall. Petite is 1 inch shorter than standard with a flatter lie angle — that’s not cosmetic. That’s the difference between a club that digs into the turf and one that actually makes contact. For a 5’1” golfer, this set is one of very few that won’t require a custom fitting.

### Performance on the Course

The driver is genuinely forgiving. The 460cc head has a deep face and perimeter weighting, which means off-center hits still get airborne. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: “I’m a beginner and was hitting the ball straight by my third round. The driver is very forgiving.” That matches the 4.4-star average across 220 ratings.

The hybrids are the star of the set. The 4- and 5-hybrids are easy to launch from the fairway and rough, and they replace the long irons most women struggle with. The irons are cavity-backed with a wide sole, which helps on thin hits and keeps the club from digging on wet turf.

But here’s the honest tradeoff: the stock graphite shafts are soft. If you have a fast, aggressive swing — say you consistently drive over 200 yards — these shafts will flex too much and you’ll lose accuracy. This set is built for moderate swing speeds, which is the right call for its target buyer, but it’s a hard ceiling for stronger players.

### What Buyers Love (and What They Don’t)

The praise is consistent across reviews. One buyer noted, “The bag is much nicer than I expected for the price — it stands on its own and the straps are padded.” Another said, “I’m 5’8” and the Regular size fits me perfectly. No hunching over the ball.” The size-specific fitting is the recurring reason people choose this over cheaper sets.

The criticisms are also consistent. The putter is the weak link — it’s a basic blade with a lightweight feel. If you’re serious about short game, budget $60–80 for a better putter. Also, the stock grips are thin. Several reviewers noted they replaced them within a few months for a thicker, more cushioned grip. That’s a $30 fix, but it’s worth knowing.

One more thing: the headcovers are not the fleece-lined type you’d find on premium sets. They’re basic, which is fine — but they’ll wear out with regular use.

### Who Should Buy This

This set is for three specific buyers:

1. **The beginner woman golfer** who wants a complete, playable set without spending $800+. You’ll shoot better scores with these hybrids than with a mishmash of used clubs.
2. **The petite golfer (5’0”–5’3”)** who has never found a set that fits. The Petite sizing is the real deal.
3. **The casual golfer** who plays 5–10 rounds a year and wants a bag that’s complete and easy to carry.

Skip it if you’re a strong swinger (over 200-yard driver) or if you’re already shooting in the 80s — you’ll outgrow these clubs within two seasons.

### FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Do the clubs come with a warranty?**
A: Yes, Precise offers a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects. It covers shafts and heads, not normal wear.

**Q: How does the Petite size differ from Regular?**
A: Petite clubs are 1 inch shorter with a flatter lie angle, which helps shorter players avoid hitting the turf before the ball. The grip is also slightly thinner.

**Q: Is the bag good enough for a cart?**
A: Yes. It fits standard cart straps and has a 14-way divider top. It’s not a cart bag — it’s a stand bag — but it works fine on a cart.

**Buy it if** you’re a beginner or casual golfer who wants a properly fitted, complete set that will make the game more fun. The size options alone justify the price.

**Skip it if** you have a fast swing speed or you’re already an experienced player — you’ll need upgrades within a year.
