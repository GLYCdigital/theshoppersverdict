The headline spec is the 2-inch multi-layer foam cushion. It's noticeably denser than the cheap tables you'll find in the $200–$300 range. At 450-pound capacity, this table won't wobble or creak under heavier clients. One verified buyer noted: *"I've had clients up to 300 pounds and the table doesn't flex at all. The Reiki endplate deserves specific mention. On most portable tables, the face cradle hole is a limitation — you can't do supine ankle or head work without awkward positioning. The AVALON's endplate snaps in place and locks securely, giving you a flat, padded surface for those positions. ## Where It Falls Short

**The weight is the big one.** At 33 pounds, this is not a lightweight table. If you're hauling it up apartment stairs or through subway stations for mobile work, you'll feel it. There are tables in this category that weigh 10 pounds less, though they typically sacrifice the Reiki endplate and the thicker foam.

**The face cradle foam is firmer than the main table.** Several reviewers noted this, and it's worth mentioning: clients with sensitive faces or TMJ issues may find the cradle uncomfortable on longer sessions. The fix is a $30 contoured face pillow, but it's an extra purchase you shouldn't have to make at this price point.

**It's not cheap.** The AVALON costs more than entry-level tables. But the math works out if you're professional: a cheap table that needs replacing every 18 months costs more than this table over a 5-year span.
