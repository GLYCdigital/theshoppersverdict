# EastPoint Sports Go! Gater Cornhole: The Travel Set That Actually Plays at Night

Most portable cornhole sets are junk. Flimsy cardboard-ish boards, bags that feel like beanbags from a kindergarten classroom, and a "carry case" that's really just a thin nylon sack. The EastPoint Go! Gater breaks that pattern — mostly. It's not a tournament-grade set, but for tailgates, camping trips, and backyard evenings, it earns its keep. Here's the honest breakdown after digging through buyer experiences and the specs.

## What You Actually Get

The set ships with two foldable boards (each roughly 24" x 48" when open, standard regulation size), eight all-weather resin bags, and two padded carry bags with shoulder straps. The headline feature is the LED light frame that outlines each board's scoring zone — powered by batteries you'll need to supply. The boards fold in half via a hinge system, collapsing to about the size of a large rolling suitcase. Total weight lands just under 30 pounds, so one person can manage both bags, though it's not exactly a breeze.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the Gater surprises. The LED system isn't decorative — it genuinely illuminates the target area enough to play in near-darkness. Buyers consistently mention this as the standout feature. One reviewer put it plainly: *"We played until 11 PM at the campsite and could see the holes perfectly. Nobody else's set could do that."* The light-up feature transforms evening gatherings from "pack up at sunset" to "keep going."

The boards themselves are plastic composite with a slicker surface than regulation wood. This matters more than you'd think. Bags tend to slide and push rather than stick where they land. Some players like that — it rewards finesse shots. Others find it frustrating when a perfectly tossed bag glides right off the back. The folding hinge also creates a subtle seam in the middle of each board. It's not a dealbreaker, but a bag landing dead-center will bounce slightly differently than one landing on solid surface.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Beyond the lights, the portability earns repeated shout-outs. The padded bags are genuinely protective — you can toss them in a trunk without worrying about cracked corners. The all-weather bags hold up to damp grass and spilled drinks without turning into mushy messes. Assembly is tool-free: unfold, lock the hinge, drop in batteries, play. Multiple reviewers note it's the easiest setup they've dealt with.

## Where It Falls Short

Battery life is the biggest gripe. The LEDs chew through batteries faster than most expect — several buyers report replacing them after just two or three sessions. If you're playing weekly, budget for rechargeable AAs or invest in a battery pack. The slick surface is the second most common complaint. Players coming from wooden boards notice the difference immediately. It's not bad, just different — and it changes the game's feel.

The hinge seam is a minor but persistent annoyance. It doesn't affect scoring, but purists will notice the inconsistency. Also worth noting: the carrying bags, while padded, don't have wheels. Hauling them across a parking lot is doable but not exactly effortless.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a casual player who wants a set that travels well and extends play past sunset. Campers, tailgaters, and backyard hosts will get the most value. The light-up feature is a genuine differentiator that justifies the price premium over basic travel sets.

**Skip it if:** You're a serious player who cares about consistent board surface and regulation feel. This is a recreational set, not a competitive one. Also skip if you hate replacing batteries — the LED feature will test your patience.

## FAQ

**Do the lights require assembly?**
No. They're pre-installed in the board frames. You just snap in four AA batteries per board (eight total) and they work. The battery compartment is accessible via a small panel on the underside.

**Are the boards regulation size?**
Yes, roughly 24" x 48" when open, with a 6-inch hole centered 9 inches from the top edge. The surface is slicker than regulation, but dimensions are standard.

**Can you play in daylight?**
Absolutely. The lights are just an added feature, not a requirement. The boards work fine during the day — the LEDs are simply off or barely noticeable.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The EastPoint Go! Gater Cornhole solves a real problem: how to keep the game going when the sun drops. It's not perfect — battery life and surface slickness are legitimate tradeoffs — but for its intended audience of casual, on-the-go players, it delivers. The portability is excellent, the lights are genuinely useful, and the build quality holds up to regular abuse. If you're shopping for a set that lives in your trunk and comes out at every gathering, this is a solid pick. Just bring spare batteries.
