The silicone gasket is soft without being flimsy. It's the kind of material that seals properly against the face without needing to crank the strap down to tourniquet levels. The UV protection is another strong point. The lenses block out harmful rays, which means these work for outdoor pools and lake swims, not just the chlorinated indoor circuit. **Where They Fall Short**

The earplugs are the weakest link. They're a one-size-fits-all silicone plug, and for kids on the younger end of the 3-15 range, they can work loose mid-swim. My advice: test them out at home first, and don't be afraid to ditch them if they cause fuss — the goggles work fine without them.

The strap tension is a minor issue. For smaller heads, maxing out the tightness can create a pinch point behind the ears. It's not a deal-breaker, but if your kid is on the smaller side, you'll want to adjust the strap before they hit the water, not after.
