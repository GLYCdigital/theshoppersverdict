# MOUNT OLIVE Organic Dill Sandwich Stuffers, 16 FZ Review

Let's be honest — most sandwich pickles are an afterthought. You grab whatever jar is on sale, slap a few limp slices on your turkey club, and move on. The MOUNT OLIVE Organic Dill Sandwich Stuffers are not that jar. These are the pickles that make you actually notice the pickle.

## What You're Getting

The 16 fluid ounce glass jar arrives filled with crinkle-cut dill slices packed in an organic brine. The slices are wide — about 2 inches across — and uniform enough that three or four cover a standard piece of bread completely. That matters when you're building a deli-style sandwich and don't want pickle pieces sliding out the sides.

The ingredient list is refreshingly short: organic cucumbers, water, organic vinegar, salt, and organic dill. No artificial preservatives, no yellow dye. If you're someone who reads labels, this checks the boxes.

## How It Performs

The first thing you notice is the crunch. These slices have a genuinely snappy texture that survives refrigeration. They're not mushy or mealy like some mass-market pickles. The crinkle cut helps — it gives the slices structural integrity and a satisfying bite.

The flavor is where these shine. The brine is tangy but not aggressive. There's a clean vinegar presence with a subtle dill herbaceousness that doesn't overpower. It's a classic deli-style pickle that's salty enough to complement meats and cheeses without dominating them. One buyer noted, "Finally a pickle that tastes like it came from a Jewish deli, not a factory."

## What Buyers Love

Across 229 ratings at 4.6 stars, the praise is consistent. Buyers repeatedly mention the organic certification, the crunch, and the convenience of having sandwich-ready slices. As one reviewer put it, "I've bought three jars already. These are the only pickles my kids will eat on their school lunches."

Another common theme: the slices are actually sized for sandwiches. "Most pickle chips are too small and I end up stacking four to cover a bun. These are one-and-done," said a verified purchaser.

## Where It Falls Short

The honest downsides matter. First, the 16 oz jar is smaller than you might expect. If you're making sandwiches daily for a family, this jar might last a week and a half. You'll be reordering frequently.

Second, some buyers want a more robust brine. The liquid here is thinner and lighter than what you'd find in a barrel-aged pickle. If you love an intensely garlicky, heavily spiced brine, this isn't that.

Finally, the price. Organic pickles cost more than conventional ones, and this jar sits at a premium. If budget is your primary concern, you can find cheaper dill slices — though you'll likely sacrifice the crunch and clean ingredient list.

## Who Should Buy This

This jar is for the sandwich enthusiast who treats pickles as a component, not a garnish. It's also great for parents packing school lunches who want organic options without negotiating with a picky eater. If you're building burgers, deli sandwiches, or even chopping pickles into tuna salad, these slices work beautifully.

Skip it if you're looking for a massive value jar to last a month, or if you prefer bold, spicy, garlic-forward pickles.
