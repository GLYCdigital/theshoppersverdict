Let's get this out of the way: Kirkland Signature Peanut Butter Pretzels are the Costco-sized snack you didn't know you needed until you're elbow-deep in the bag wondering where an afternoon went. At 52 ounces split across two bags, this isn't a casual purchase — it's a commitment.

## What you're actually getting

The box contains two 26-ounce bags. That's roughly 3.25 pounds of pretzels per bag, which translates to about 22 servings per bag if you stick to the listed 1-ounce portion. Nobody sticks to one ounce, but the math is there for the tracking-minded.

Each pretzel is a classic twisted shape, generously filled with peanut butter. The filling is the standout — it's thick, smooth, and tastes like actual roasted peanuts rather than the chalky, overly sweet paste you find in cheaper brands. The pretzel shell has a solid crunch with a noticeable salt presence that cuts through the richness of the peanut butter.

## How they hold up in real use

I've run through a bag over two weeks. The texture stays consistent — the pretzels remain crisp, and the peanut butter doesn't dry out or develop that weird stale taste some filled snacks get. The salt crystals on the outside are prominent without being aggressive, landing the salty-sweet balance that keeps you reaching for another.

One thing worth noting: the peanut butter filling is more savory than sweet. If you're expecting a dessert-level sweetness like a Reese's, you'll be surprised. This leans snack territory — closer to a savory trail mix vibe than candy.

## What buyers consistently say

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews tracks with what I found. The praise centers on value and flavor. One reviewer put it simply: "These are dangerously good. I can't stop eating them." Another noted the quality compared to name brands: "Better than the Snyder's version. More filling, fresher taste."

The recurring complaint? Freshness and breakage. Multiple reviewers mentioned receiving bags with a fair amount of broken pieces at the bottom. It's cosmetic — the broken bits taste identical — but if you're particular about presentation, it's worth knowing.

## Where it falls short

The biggest practical issue is the portion control problem. Two 26-ounce bags is a lot of pretzels, and unless you have a household of snackers or are disciplined about repackaging, you'll likely eat more than you planned. The salt content is also significant — about 300mg per serving — which is worth considering if you're watching sodium.

The freshness window is real. Once opened, these are best within 2-3 weeks. They don't go stale quickly, but the peanut butter filling does lose some of its moisture over time. If you're a solo snacker, consider repacking into smaller airtight containers immediately.

## Who should buy this

This is for households that move through snacks quickly, office snack drawers, or anyone hosting game nights. It's also a solid choice if you're looking for a higher-quality filled pretzel that beats the grocery store standard without the boutique snack brand markup.

Skip it if you're a light snacker, if you're watching sodium, or if you need a sweet dessert snack rather than a savory one.

## FAQ

**Are these the same as Snyder's or Dot's peanut butter pretzels?**
Not quite. The filling is thicker and more peanut-forward than Snyder's, and the pretzel texture is crunchier than Dot's. They're comparable in quality to premium brands but at a significantly better price per ounce.

**How long do they stay fresh after opening?**
Expect 2-3 weeks of peak quality. The pretzels stay crisp, but the peanut butter filling gradually loses moisture. Airtight storage extends this.

**Are they gluten-free?**
No. These are traditional wheat-based pretzels. The peanut butter is gluten-free, but the pretzel shell isn't.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a high-quality, generously filled peanut butter pretzel at a price that beats the competition by a mile. The 2-pack format works great for families, offices, or anyone with average-to-poor self-control.

**Skip it if:** You're a solo snacker, need something sweet, or want a snack that won't tempt you to eat a third of the bag in one sitting.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The breakage issue and bulk format cost it a half-point, but the flavor-to-value ratio is hard to beat. This earns a solid recommendation for anyone who actually likes peanut butter pretzels.
