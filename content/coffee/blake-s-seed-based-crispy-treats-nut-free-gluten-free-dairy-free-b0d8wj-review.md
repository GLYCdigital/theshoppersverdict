**Performance and real-world use**

I tested these three ways: straight from the wrapper, slightly warmed in the microwave (8 seconds), and crumbled over dairy-free yogurt. The bars hold their shape through a backpack or lunchbox without disintegrating, which is more than I can say for most gluten-free baked goods.
