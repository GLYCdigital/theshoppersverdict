## FAQ

**Q: Are these seeds pre-toasted?**
A: No, they're raw. Toast them in a dry pan over medium heat for 1-2 minutes, shaking frequently, until golden and fragrant.

**Q: How should I store them after opening?**
A: Keep them in the resealable jar in a cool, dark place. For longer storage (6+ months), refrigerate or freeze — sesame seeds have high oil content and can go rancid.

**Q: Can I use these to make tahini?**
A: Yes, absolutely. Toast lightly, then blend in a food processor with a neutral oil until smooth. Expect a slightly darker, more robust tahini than store-bought versions.
