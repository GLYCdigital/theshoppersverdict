Let me be direct: this is not your standard grocery store lunch meat. Applegate's Natural Uncured Black Forest Ham is the kind of product that makes you read the back of the package twice — because the ingredient list is actually short. Pork, water, sea salt, cane sugar, celery juice powder, cherry powder, spices. That's it. No sodium phosphates, no carrageenan, no "natural flavors" hiding who-knows-what.

**What's actually in the box**

You get one 7-ounce package of pre-sliced ham. The slices are noticeably thicker than typical Oscar Mayer or Hillshire Farm offerings — closer to what you'd get from a deli counter, just pre-portioned. The package is resealable, which matters because you won't finish it in one sitting unless you're feeding a crowd.

**How it performs in real-world use**

The smoke flavor is the star here. It's genuinely Black Forest-style — deep, slightly sweet, with that distinctive hickory character that cheap hams fake with liquid smoke. The texture is firm but tender, not mushy or slippery like some mass-market brands. It slices cleanly for sandwiches, rolls well for pinwheels, and crisps up nicely if you're making a grilled ham and cheese.

One thing worth noting: because there are no added nitrates, the color is more rosy-pink than the bright red you see in conventional ham. That's normal. Don't let it throw you.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The reviews — 225 of them, averaging 4.5 stars — keep circling back to the same wins. One shopper put it simply: "Finally a ham that tastes like ham, not salt water and gelatin." Another mentioned the ingredient transparency: "I feel okay giving this to my kids. I can pronounce everything on the label." Multiple reviewers who avoid nitrates for health reasons called this their go-to lunch meat.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, the price per ounce is steep. You're paying a premium for the clean label and better sourcing, and the 7oz size means you'll be reordering frequently if this becomes a staple. Second, the slice integrity varies by batch. A few reviewers reported slices arriving stuck together in a way that made separating them a frustrating, tear-prone exercise. Third, at 460mg of sodium per serving, it's not low-salt — it's just *better* salt from sea salt and celery juice.

**Who should buy this**

- Parents who want lunch meat without nitrates or artificial preservatives for their kids' school lunches
- Anyone following a clean-eating or Whole30-adjacent approach who still wants sandwich meat
- People who've tried conventional Black Forest ham and found it too salty or artificial-tasting
- Keto and low-carb dieters looking for a protein-rich, portable snack

**Skip it if** you're on a tight grocery budget, need bulk quantities for meal prep, or expect the neon-pink color of commercial ham.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this actually uncured, or just a marketing trick?**
A: It's genuinely uncured in the conventional sense — no sodium nitrite or nitrate added. Instead, it uses celery juice powder, which naturally contains nitrates. The USDA still requires the "uncured" label because no direct nitrates are added, but the celery powder does provide some preservation. It's cleaner than standard ham, but not a zero-nitrate product.

**Q: How long does it stay fresh after opening?**
A: Plan on 5-7 days in the fridge once opened. The resealable package helps, but because it's free of artificial preservatives, it won't last as long as conventional deli meats. Buy what you'll use within a week.

**Q: Can I freeze it?**
A: Yes, but expect some texture change. Freeze in a single layer with parchment between slices, and thaw in the fridge overnight. Works fine for cooking; less ideal for fresh sandwiches.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you prioritize clean ingredients and real smoke flavor over price and convenience. **Skip it if** you need a budget-friendly, long-lasting lunch meat for a large family.

This is a 4.6-star product for a specific buyer — one who's willing to pay more for fewer ingredients and genuinely better taste. It delivers exactly what it promises: honest ham, without the chemical aftertaste. Just budget for the small package size and reorder before you run out.
