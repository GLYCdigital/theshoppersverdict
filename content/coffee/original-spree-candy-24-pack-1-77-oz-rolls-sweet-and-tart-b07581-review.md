**Is this the Spree you remember? Probably not — and that's the point.**

Let's get the elephant in the room out of the way immediately. If you grew up eating Spree in the 90s, you remember those hard, chalky discs that made your tongue tingle. The Original Spree Candy you're looking at today is a different beast. These are chewy, fruit-flavored candies in a sweet-tart coating, closer in texture to a Starburst than a classic Spree. That's not a flaw — it's a repositioning. But you need to know what you're actually buying before you commit to a 24-pack.

**What's in the box**

You get 24 individually wrapped rolls, each weighing 1.77 oz. That's roughly 15-16 candies per roll, which works out to around 370-380 total pieces. For context, that's a substantial stash — enough to fill a candy dish, stuff a piñata, or survive a month of desk drawer grazing. Each candy is individually wrapped in twist-style plastic, which means no sticky mess in your bag or pocket.

**Performance and build quality**

The flavor profile is where these shine. The outer coating delivers an immediate tang that hits the front of your tongue, followed by a fruity sweetness as you chew. The flavors — cherry, orange, lemon, grape, and lime — are distinct and don't blur into a generic fruit punch taste. One Amazon reviewer noted, "The tart coating is just right — not mouth-puckering, but enough to make you notice." Another said, "These are dangerously easy to plow through in one sitting."

The chew itself is pleasantly soft without being gummy. They don't stick to your teeth the way caramel does, and they don't require the jaw workout that old-school hard candies demanded. That said, if you're specifically nostalgic for the crunchy, crunchable Spree of yesteryear, these will feel like a bait-and-switch. Read the label carefully — this is a chewy candy, period.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest recurring complaint in the 204 ratings: broken pieces. Several buyers reported receiving rolls where candies had cracked or fused together, likely from heat exposure during shipping. This doesn't affect taste, but it does affect presentation — if you're buying these for party favors or a candy buffet, you'll want to inspect each roll first.

Also worth noting: the "original" branding is doing a lot of heavy lifting. Longtime fans who remember the hard version may feel misled. The 4.1-star average reflects that tension — people love the flavor, but some dock stars because it's not what they expected.

**Who should buy this**

- **Parents packing lunchboxes**: Individually wrapped, portion-controlled, and no sticky residue. A clear win.
- **Office candy bowl keepers**: The tart flavor keeps people from grabbing handfuls, so a roll lasts longer than you'd think.
- **Nostalgia seekers willing to embrace change**: If you can accept that this is a chewy candy wearing a familiar name, you'll enjoy it.
- **Not for**: Anyone specifically craving the old hard-shell texture. You'll be disappointed.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these gluten-free?**  
A: Yes, Spree candies are generally gluten-free, though they're produced in facilities that also process wheat. If you have celiac disease, proceed with caution.

**Q: How long do they stay fresh after opening?**  
A: Opened rolls keep for 2-3 weeks if you reseal the twist ends properly. Unopened rolls last months — check the best-by date on each roll.

**Q: Can I buy these in bulk for events?**  
A: Yes, but order at least a week ahead. The 24-pack is great for goodie bags, but inspect for broken pieces and consider that some rolls may arrive damaged in transit.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, tasty, individually wrapped chewy candy with a proper sweet-tart kick and decent value per piece.  
**Skip it if** you're chasing the hard, crunchy Spree of your childhood — this isn't that, and you'll be frustrated.

These aren't revolutionary, but they do exactly what they claim: deliver a solid sweet-and-tart chew. At this price point, the 24-pack is a fair deal. Just manage your expectations about the texture and you'll be satisfied.
