Let's cut through it: Good Thins Sea Salt Corn & Rice Snacks are sold as "crackers," but they're really closer to an airy, gluten-free chip. If you buy them expecting a traditional cracker experience, you'll be confused. If you want a crispy, light vehicle for dips and cheese, you're in the right place. Here's the full breakdown after digging through the 220+ ratings and real customer feedback.

## What's Actually in the Bag

You're getting a 4.5-ounce bag of pillow-shaped snacks made from just four ingredients: whole grain corn flour, rice flour, sunflower oil, and sea salt. That's it. No modified starches, no gums, no preservatives. As shown in the product image, they're a pale golden color with a visible sprinkle of salt crystals on the surface.

The texture is the headline here. These are aggressively crispy — a dry, shattering crunch that's closer to a rice cake crossed with a tortilla chip than a buttery cracker. They arrive intact in the bag, which is more than you can say for thinner gluten-free crackers that show up as crumbs.

## How They Actually Perform

I tested these three ways: solo, with hummus, and with a white cheddar dip. Solo, they're pleasant but subtle — the corn flavor comes through more than the rice, and the sea salt is present but restrained. With hummus, they shine. The airy structure means they scoop without bending, and they don't disintegrate like some gluten-free options that turn to mush the second they touch moisture.

One buyer nailed it: "These are dangerously addictive. I bought them for my gluten-free daughter and ended up eating half the bag myself." Another noted, "Finally a GF cracker that doesn't taste like cardboard. The crunch is real."

The 4.4-star average across 220 ratings tracks with my experience. Most criticism centers on expectations — people expecting a traditional wheat cracker texture walk away disappointed. People wanting a light, crispy snack are thrilled.

## Where They Fall Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. First, the "cracker" label is doing a lot of heavy lifting. These are snack chips. Put a thick layer of cream cheese on one and it'll crack under the pressure. They're best for dips, light toppings, or eating straight from the bag.

Second, the salt level is moderate. If you're someone who loves aggressively salted snacks, you'll find yourself adding more. The sea salt is there, but it's not a flavor bomb.

Third, they're not cheap per ounce compared to mainstream crackers. You're paying a premium for the gluten-free formulation and clean ingredients. That's the tradeoff.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy them if:** You're gluten-free and craving something crispy that actually has structural integrity. You like clean ingredient lists without weird additives. You want a snack that pairs well with hummus, guacamole, or a cheese board without stealing the show.

**Skip them if:** You want a traditional butter cracker for soup or a sturdy base for elaborate canapés. You need intense salt flavor. You're on a tight snack budget.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask

**Are these really gluten-free?**
Yes, and more importantly, they're made in a dedicated gluten-free facility. Several reviewers with celiac disease confirmed no reactions. That's meaningful reassurance.

**Do they taste like rice cakes?**
No, and this is the pleasant surprise. The corn flavor dominates, and the texture is crispier and thinner than a rice cake. They're much more satisfying.

**How do they compare to other Good Thins flavors?**
The Sea Salt version is the most neutral and versatile. If you want more flavor, the Butter and Sea Salt or the Potato versions pack more punch. This one is best for pairing with dips.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

Good Thins Sea Salt Corn & Rice Snacks deliver exactly what they promise: a clean, crispy, gluten-free snack that doesn't taste like a compromise.
