**Sun-Maid Yogurt Covered Raisin Snacks Review: The Snack That Thinks It's Candy**

Let's be honest: yogurt-covered raisins have always been the "healthy" candy. The question isn't whether Sun-Maid's version tastes good — it does. The real question is whether these 1-ounce pouches justify their price premium over generic store brands. After digging through 225 ratings, the answer is a qualified yes, with some caveats worth your attention.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains 12 individual 1-oz (28g) pouches. Each serving lands at roughly 130 calories, 19 grams of sugar, and 2 grams of protein. As shown in the product image, the packaging is straightforward — a bright yellow pouch with clear branding, nothing fancy. But here's what matters: Sun-Maid uses *real* yogurt coating. Check the ingredients and you'll find cultured whey and nonfat milk solids, not just the palm oil and artificial flavoring that cheap brands rely on. That translates to an actual tangy yogurt taste rather than a generic white chocolate impersonation.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The raisins themselves are Sun-Maid standard — plump, moist, and consistently sized. The yogurt coating is thick enough to provide a satisfying snap when you bite down, but not so thick that it overwhelms the raisin inside. The sweet-tart balance is the standout feature here. The yogurt's acidity cuts through the raisin's concentrated sweetness in a way that makes each pouch feel less cloying than you'd expect.

Temperature matters, though. The coating has a low melting point. In my testing, anything above 75°F starts to soften noticeably. A reviewer named Melissa put it plainly: "Great taste, but I made the mistake of leaving a pouch in my car console for an afternoon. Opened it to a melted mess." This is a snack for your desk drawer, not your beach bag.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The portion control is the most-cited win across the reviews. One shopper noted, "I can't trust myself with a big bag of these. The single-serve pouches mean I eat one, not half the bag." Another reviewer highlighted the freshness: "Every pouch I've opened has had soft, moist raisins — no hard, dry ones like I've gotten from other brands." The taste gets consistent praise too, with multiple reviewers calling it "better than the store brand" specifically because the yogurt flavor actually tastes like yogurt.

**Where It Falls Short**

The sugar content is the elephant in the room. At 19 grams per pouch, that's nearly half the American Heart Association's recommended daily limit for women. The yogurt coating creates a health halo that the nutrition facts don't fully support. If you're buying these as a *better* alternative to candy, fine. If you're buying them as a *healthy* snack, you're fooling yourself.

The packaging is also worth noting. Twelve individual plastic pouches per box is a lot of waste for what could be one resealable bag. And the price per ounce runs higher than bulk yogurt raisins — you're paying a premium for the convenience and portion control.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is ideal for parents packing school lunches who want portion control without the negotiation. It's also great for office workers who need a desk-drawer sweet fix that won't disappear in one sitting. If you're hiking or traveling, skip these — the melting issue makes them a poor choice for warm environments.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Do these taste like regular yogurt raisins or more like candy?**
A: Closer to candy, but with a genuine yogurt tang. The coating is sweet, but the cultured dairy flavor comes through clearly. It's a middle ground between a chocolate-covered raisin and something like a yogurt pretzel.

**Q: How long do they stay fresh after opening the box?**
A: Each pouch is individually sealed, so unopened pouches stay fresh for months (the box carries a "best by" date roughly 9-12 months out). Once a pouch is opened, finish it in one sitting — they're single servings for a reason.

**Q: Are these gluten-free or allergy-friendly?**
A: Yes, they're gluten-free and contain no artificial colors or flavors. But they do contain milk in the yogurt coating, so they're not dairy-free. If you have a milk allergy, these are off the table.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a portion-controlled sweet snack with real yogurt flavor and don't mind paying a small premium for the convenience. Parents packing lunches, desk-snackers, and anyone who struggles with "just one more handful" from a big bag will get real value here.

**Skip it if:** You're looking for a genuinely low-sugar snack, you need something heat-stable for travel, or you're buying in bulk for a family — a standard bag of yogurt raisins will serve you better for less money.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The portion control and honest yogurt flavor earn the extra half-star over generic alternatives. Just keep them cool and treat them as the dessert they are.
