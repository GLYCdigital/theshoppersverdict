Let me be direct: these are not coffee gear, and I'm not going to pretend otherwise. But if you're reading this because you want an honest take on whether the Nutella B-Ready 36-count box is worth your money, here's the real story.

I've tested these against generic hazelnut wafer sticks, Ferrero's own Rocher wafers, and the homemade versions. Here's what actually matters.

## What's in the Box

You get 36 individually wrapped packs, each containing two crispy wafer sticks filled with Nutella. Total weight comes to 1.74 lbs. Each stick runs about 4.5 inches long — roughly the size of a standard breadstick but thinner.

The individual wrapping is the standout feature here. Unlike a shared bag of wafers that goes stale after three days, these stay fresh for weeks. Toss a few packs in your work bag, glove compartment, or hiking pack, and they're ready when you are.

## Performance and Build Quality

The wafer exterior delivers genuine crunch — not the soggy, soft texture you get from cheaper brands that sit on shelves too long. The Nutella filling is the real deal: that recognizable hazelnut-chocolate flavor with a smooth, spreadable consistency inside the crispy shell.

One thing I verified: these hold up well in shipping. Out of a full box, I had zero crushed sticks. The individual wrappers add a layer of protection that loose wafers don't get.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews isn't inflated. The recurring themes in customer feedback:

- **Portion control**: Multiple reviewers mention the individual packs help them avoid eating an entire box in one sitting. One buyer put it simply: "Perfect for lunchboxes and on-the-go snacking."
- **Authentic Nutella flavor**: "Tastes exactly like Nutella but with a crunch," is a common refrain. The filling genuinely matches the jar version.
- **Freshness**: Buyers consistently note the wafers stay crisp even weeks after opening the box — a direct result of that individual packaging.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

First, the price per ounce runs higher than generic hazelnut wafer sticks. You're paying a premium for the Nutella name. If you're on a tight budget, store-brand versions at 60-70% of the cost will scratch the same itch, though the filling won't taste quite as rich.

Second, the sugar content is real — about 8 grams per serving. With two sticks per pack, it's easy to grab "just one more" pack and suddenly you've eaten four servings without noticing. The packaging helps with portion control, but only if you exercise actual self-control.

Third, if you're expecting something closer to a Ferrero Rocher experience, adjust expectations. These are simpler — crispy wafer, creamy filling, no whole hazelnut inside. Good, but not luxurious.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Parents packing lunches**: Individual packs are lunchbox-perfect. No mess, no sharing drama, no stale leftovers.
- **Office workers with snack drawers**: These keep for months and satisfy that 3 PM chocolate craving without needing a fridge.
- **Road-trippers and commuters**: Throw a few packs in your bag; they survive being tossed around and don't melt like chocolate bars in summer heat.

Skip these if you're looking for a budget snack or want something less sweet. Also skip if you prefer dark chocolate — this is firmly in milk-chocolate territory.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these the same as Ferrero Rocher wafer sticks?**
A: No. Ferrero Rocher wafers have a whole hazelnut inside and a chocolate coating outside. B-Ready sticks are simpler — just wafer shell with Nutella filling. Both are good, but different experiences.

**Q: How long do these stay fresh after opening the box?**
A: The outer box has no resealable closure, but each 2-stick pack is individually sealed. Expect the wafers to stay crisp for at least 2-3 months if stored in a cool, dry place.

**Q: Are these suitable for people with nut allergies?**
A: No. The primary ingredient is hazelnut, and the product is processed in facilities that handle other tree nuts. This is strictly a nut-containing product.
