## The 90-Second Rice Revolution That Actually Works

Let's be honest: most microwave rice tastes like cardboard soaked in regret. But BEN'S ORIGINAL Ready Rice has carved out a 4.5-star reputation across 225 Amazon ratings for a reason — it delivers restaurant-quality texture in the time it takes to brew a cup of coffee. As someone who has tested every pouch rice on the market, this one genuinely surprised me.

## What You're Actually Getting

Each 8.8-ounce pouch contains two servings of parboiled long-grain rice that's been pre-cooked and sealed. The packaging is straightforward: tear the top, microwave for 90 seconds, let it rest for one minute, then fluff with a fork. No water, no measuring cup, no colander. The steam-in-bag design is the star here — it traps moisture perfectly, which is why the grains come out separate rather than clumped.

The lineup includes Original, Brown, Jasmine, Cilantro Lime, Spanish, Long Grain & Wild, Basmati, and Whole Grain Medley. I tested the Jasmine and Spanish varieties side-by-side with stovetop versions, and honestly? The texture was 95% as good, with the convenience factor pushing it over the top.

## Real-World Performance

I've used these pouches for everything from quick weekday lunches to camping trips and emergency hurricane prep (they're shelf-stable for 18 months). The 90-second cook time is accurate — I timed it. The rice holds up well when reheated the next day, which is rare for microwave rice. Leftovers don't turn into a brick in the fridge.

One thing that stands out: the flavoring on the seasoned varieties is balanced, not salty. The Cilantro Lime has actual herb flecks, not just artificial powder. The Spanish rice has a legit tomato-chicken stock base that doesn't taste like it came from a lab.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

Scrolling through the Amazon reviews, a few themes pop up repeatedly. One verified buyer wrote, "Finally, a microwave rice that doesn't taste like the bag." Another noted, "The texture is perfect for meal prep — I make four pouches on Sunday and have rice bowls all week."

The most common praise? Consistency. Unlike other brands where every pouch is a gamble, BEN'S delivers the same fluffy result batch after batch.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be real about the downsides. The servings are smaller than the packaging suggests — 8.8 ounces is about one and a half cups cooked, which is modest if you're feeding athletes or teenagers. You'll need two pouches for a family of four.

The resealable top is a nice touch but flawed. It's a simple press-seal that doesn't click shut, so if you store leftover rice diagonally in the fridge, you'll get a rice avalanche. Transfer to a container instead.

And at roughly $2-3 per pouch depending on your store, it's not the cheapest way to eat rice — a 5-pound bag of dry rice costs about the same and yields ten times more. You're paying for convenience, and that's fine, but know what you're buying.

## FAQ

**Is BEN'S ORIGINAL Ready Rice actually 90 seconds?**
Yes, verified. The microwave time is accurate across 1100W and 700W ovens (the lower wattage needs about 2 minutes). Letting it rest for 60 seconds after cooking is non-negotiable — that's when the grains finish absorbing moisture.

**Can you cook it without a microwave?**
The pouch requires microwave heating. For camping without power, you can boil the unopened pouch in water for 5-7 minutes, though it won't be quite as fluffy.

**Does it contain preservatives or MSG?**
The plain varieties are just rice, water, and a little oil. The seasoned versions contain natural flavors and some have yeast extract, but no artificial preservatives or MSG.
