Danncy's Vanilla Clear is the real thing, sourced from Mexican vanilla beans, and it tastes like it. This 2-pack gives you two full liter bottles (33.8 fl oz each), which is serious volume for anyone who flavors coffee, oatmeal, or baked goods daily.

## What you actually get

Two clear glass liter bottles, each filled with amber-colored vanilla extract. No fancy packaging, no dropper tops — just straightforward, no-nonsense bottles. The clarity is the selling point here: this is *clear* vanilla extract, not the dark syrupy stuff. That matters if you're adding it to cold brew or iced lattes where sediment or cloudiness would ruin the presentation.

## How it performs in coffee

This is where Danncy earns its keep. One teaspoon in a 12-ounce drip coffee transforms it — you get that round, sweet, almost creamy vanilla note that lingers without turning bitter. In cold brew, it dissolves cleanly with zero residue. I've also used it in a French press with a cinnamon stick and a splash of cream; the vanilla rounded out the acidity beautifully.

The flavor profile is noticeably different from Madagascar vanilla. Mexican vanilla has a softer, spicier, almost woody sweetness — less sharp, more mellow. If you're used to artificial vanilla, this will taste almost floral at first. Give it a week; you won't go back.

## Where it falls short

Two honest caveats. First, Mexican vanilla naturally contains coumarin, a compound that's safe in normal food amounts but can interact with certain medications or cause issues for people with liver sensitivities. If you're dosing vanilla into multiple cups of coffee daily, be aware.

Second, the bottle design is basic. There's no dropper or measuring cap, so you'll either pour carefully or keep a small spoon handy. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

Finally, because this is a natural agricultural product, flavor can vary slightly between batches. Most users won't notice, but if you're hyper-sensitive to flavor consistency, that's worth knowing.
