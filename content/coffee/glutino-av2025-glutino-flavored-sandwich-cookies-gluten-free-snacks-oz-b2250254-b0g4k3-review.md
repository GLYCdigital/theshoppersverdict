**What you're actually getting**

Each box contains about 7 ounces of chocolate sandwich cookies — roughly 21 cookies per package depending on the batch. The cookies are individually arranged in plastic trays to minimize breakage, though as one reviewer put it, "about 1 in 10 arrive cracked — it's the nature of gluten-free baking."

The ingredient list is reassuringly short: gluten-free flour blend (rice flour, potato starch, tapioca flour), sugar, palm oil, cocoa, and natural flavors. No artificial preservatives. Each serving runs about 140 calories — comparable to the mainstream competition.

**How they actually perform**

The texture is where Glutino wins. The cookie shell is crisp with a satisfying snap — not the gritty, sandy texture that plagues many GF products. The cocoa flavor is present but not bitter, and the cream filling has a proper vanilla-forward sweetness. One Amazon reviewer nailed it: "My kids couldn't tell the difference in a blind taste test. That's the highest compliment I can give."

Dunking test: they hold up for about 8–10 seconds in milk before softening. That's respectable — standard Oreos last maybe 12 seconds. Close enough.

**What buyers consistently praise**

- Taste authenticity: "These are the closest thing to Oreos I've found in 12 years of eating gluten-free."
- Celiac safety: "Certified GF facility — I've never had a reaction."
