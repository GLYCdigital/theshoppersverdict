**Review Body:**

You’ve seen the hard candies. You’ve sucked on them until your tongue changed color. But the JOLLY RANCHER Assorted Fruit Flavored Gummy Candy takes that iconic flavor profile and translates it into a soft-chew format that’s surprisingly faithful — and dangerously snackable.

This 5-pound bulk bag is a Halloween strategy play. At roughly 240 pieces per bag (based on the 9.5-gram serving size), you’re looking at enough candy for 60 to 80 trick-or-treaters depending on how generous you’re feeling. That’s the math that matters on October 31st, and it’s why this bag consistently lands in shopping carts.

**What’s Actually in the Bag**

You get six flavors: Blue Raspberry, Cherry, Green Apple, Grape, Orange, and Strawberry. Each gummy is shaped like the classic Jolly Rancher rectangle, just squished into a chewy format. The bag itself is straightforward — a clear poly bag with a printed label, nothing fancy. As shown in the product image, it’s a no-frills bulk package designed for utility, not presentation.

The texture is where this product earns its keep. Unlike some gummies that alternate between rubbery and mushy, these have a consistent medium-soft chew. They’re not as firm as Albanese or as soft as Haribo’s classic bears — they sit comfortably in the middle. In warmer conditions, they hold their shape without turning into a sticky blob, which is a real concern for anyone leaving a bowl out on the porch.

**Flavor Accuracy: The Real Test**

Here’s the thing that separates these from knockoff gummies: the flavor intensity. Jolly Rancher’s hard candies are known for aggressive, tart-forward fruit notes, and the gummies deliver that same punch. Blue Raspberry is the standout — it hits with that signature tang before settling into sweetness. Green Apple follows close behind with a sour kick that doesn’t overwhelm. Cherry and Grape are solid, if slightly more conventional. Orange and Strawberry round out the mix without feeling like filler.

One buyer put it plainly: “Taste just like the original Jolly Ranchers, but chewy. My kids fought over the blue ones.” Another noted, “These are dangerously good. I bought them for trick-or-treaters and the bag was half empty before Halloween.”

**Where It Falls Short**

The packaging is the biggest miss. There’s no zip closure, so once you open a 5-pound bag, you’re committed to either transferring the contents or wrestling with a chip clip. For a product designed for bulk use, this feels like an oversight.

The sugar coating also deserves a mention. The gummies are dusted with fine sugar that keeps them from sticking together, but it transfers onto fingers quickly. Kids with sticky hands plus a white couch equals a problem. It’s a minor complaint, but worth knowing before you hand the bag to a toddler.

**Who Should Buy This**

This bag is for three specific people: the Halloween host who wants to buy candy once and not stress about running out; the classroom parent bringing treats for 30 kids; and the household that goes through gummy candy fast enough that a 5-pound bag won’t go stale. If you’re buying for a single child or a small family, this is overkill — you’d be better off with a smaller bag.

**FAQ**

**How long do these stay fresh after opening?**
Transferred to an airtight container, they’ll stay good for 3-4 weeks. In the original bag with a clip, expect about 2 weeks before they start hardening slightly.

**Are these gluten-free?**
Yes, they’re gluten-free and contain no artificial colors — the color comes from fruit and vegetable juice concentrates.

**How many pieces are in the 5-pound bag?**
Approximately 240 pieces based on the 9.5-gram serving size. That’s enough for 60+ trick-or-treaters at 3-4 pieces each.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need a bulk candy solution for Halloween, a classroom event, or you’re a household that plows through gummies quickly. The flavor fidelity to the original hard candies is exceptional, and the texture holds up in real-world conditions.

**Skip it if:** You’re buying for one or two kids, you hate sugar-coated candy, or you need resealable packaging. For those cases, a smaller bag with better closure makes more sense.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A strong bulk buy with minor packaging flaws that don’t overshadow the product’s core quality.
