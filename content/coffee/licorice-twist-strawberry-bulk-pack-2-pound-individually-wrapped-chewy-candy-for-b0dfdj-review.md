# Licorice Twist Strawberry Bulk Pack 2 Pound: A Sweet Deal With a Sticky Catch

Let's be honest — when you're buying two pounds of strawberry licorice, you're not looking for gourmet. You want a reliable, tasty candy that disappears quickly from the break room or the snack drawer. The Licorice Twist Strawberry Bulk Pack delivers on that promise, but not without a few wrinkles in the wrapper.

## What's Actually in the Bag

The 2-pound bulk pack arrives as a single resealable plastic bag filled with individually wrapped twists. Based on what buyers report, you're getting roughly 60 to 70 pieces depending on twist size. Each twist runs about 5-6 inches long and is wrapped in a clear plastic sleeve with a simple twist-tie style closure.

One thing worth noting right away: the bag says "2 pound" but there's no nutrition panel or ingredient list printed on it. If you have allergies or just like knowing what you're eating, that's a legitimate concern. Several reviewers mentioned this, and it's the kind of thing Amazon shoppers should know before buying.

## How It Actually Tastes and Feels

The strawberry flavor here is the highlight. It's genuinely fruity — think fresh strawberry jam rather than the fake, candy-sweet strawberry you get from cheaper brands. There's a light tartness on the back end that keeps it from being cloying. If you've had classic strawberry licorice and found it too sweet, this one strikes a better balance.

Texture-wise, these twists are soft and chewy without being tough. They bend easily and don't require aggressive jaw work. They also hold their moisture well — reviewers note the candy stays pliable even after the bag has been open for weeks. That's a real plus if you're buying in bulk and plan to snack over time.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The flavor and value come up again and again. One reviewer put it plainly: "Great strawberry flavor, not too sweet, and the kids love them." Another said, "Perfect for party favors and stocking stuffers — you get a lot for the price."

The individual wrapping is also a point in its favor. It makes these easy to toss into lunchboxes, hand out at events, or keep in a desk drawer without worrying about the whole bag going stale.

## Where It Falls Short

Here's where things get honest. The most common complaint is inconsistent twist sizes and breakage. You'll open the bag to find some full-length twists, but also a fair number of broken pieces and half-sections. It's not a dealbreaker for snacking, but if you're buying these for a specific presentation — like wedding favors or a candy buffet — the uneven pieces will be noticeable.

The wrappers are the bigger issue. In humid environments, the plastic sticks to the candy surface. When you peel it off, it takes a thin layer of licorice with it, leaving the twist sticky and rough. It's cosmetic, not a flavor problem, but it's frustrating when you're trying to hand these out.

Finally, the lack of ingredient labeling on the bag is a real oversight. For a product sold in bulk, buyers should know exactly what's in it. Several reviewers flagged this, especially those shopping for kids with dietary restrictions.
