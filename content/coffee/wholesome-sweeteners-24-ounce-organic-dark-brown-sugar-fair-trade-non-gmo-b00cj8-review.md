I tested this sugar in chocolate chip cookies, a bourbon glaze, and sprinkled it over oatmeal. The texture is what stands out immediately — it's soft, slightly damp, and packs easily. ## FAQ

**Q: Is dark brown sugar interchangeable with light brown sugar in recipes?**
A: Yes, but expect a stronger molasses flavor and slightly more moisture. If a recipe calls for light brown sugar and you use dark, the result will be richer — good for gingerbread, molasses cookies, and glazes. For delicate cakes where you want pure sweetness, light is safer.

**Q: How do I soften it when it hardens?**
A: The classic trick: place a slice of fresh bread in the container overnight, or microwave with a damp paper towel for 20-second bursts. With Wholesome's molasses content, it re-softens more readily than conventional brands.

**Q: Is this actually non-GMO?**
A: Yes. It's certified organic (which prohibits GMOs), and it's made from cane sugar, not beet sugar. Most conventional brown sugar in the U.S. comes from GMO sugar beets.
