Let's be honest about bulk nuts on Amazon: you're gambling on freshness, storage conditions, and whether that "raw" label actually means anything. The Yupik Raw California Walnuts in the 2.2 lb bag is one of the better bets I've tested — but it's not without its quirks.

## What you actually get

The package is a 2.2 lb (35.2 oz) stand-up pouch with a resealable zipper top. That's about 10 cups of walnut pieces, which translates to roughly two months of daily snacking or a solid baking season. The bag itself is sturdy — not flimsy foil that tears at the seam. As shown in the product image, the walnuts are medium-cut pieces, meaning you'll see mostly broken halves and quarters rather than dust or whole nuts.

## Freshness and flavor — the real test

I opened my bag and immediately did the sniff test. These smell like fresh walnuts — that slightly sweet, earthy aroma with no rancid or musty undertone. The texture is where Yupik earns its keep: crisp snap when you bite into them, not the soft, chewy texture you get from walnuts that sat too long on a shelf.

Taste-wise, they're genuinely raw. There's no roasted bitterness, no added salt masking anything. You get that clean, slightly buttery walnut flavor that works equally well in oatmeal, salads, or straight out of the bag. One buyer put it simply: "These are the freshest walnuts I've bought online. No rancid taste, no weird aftertaste." Another noted, "I've ordered these three times now. Consistent quality every single time."

## Where it falls short

Here's the honest part. First, there's no harvest date on the packaging. You're trusting Amazon's inventory turnover, which is usually fine but occasionally means older stock. Second, shell fragments. It's not egregious — maybe a few small pieces per cup — but if you're using these for baking and don't want to pick through them, that's a mild annoyance. Third, the "medium pieces" cut means no intact halves. If you're topping a salad for presentation or garnishing a cake, you'll need a different product.

## Who should buy this

This bag is ideal for three types of shoppers: the daily snacker who wants a large, cost-effective supply; the baker making batches of banana bread, brownies, or granola where walnut pieces are getting folded into batter anyway; and the meal-prep person adding crushed walnuts to oatmeal, yogurt, or salads throughout the week.

It's less ideal if you need picture-perfect walnut halves for presentation, if you're sensitive to occasional shell bits, or if you want a smaller quantity that you'll finish within a month.

## FAQ

**How long do these stay fresh after opening?**
Stored in the resealable bag in a cool, dark place, expect 3-4 months of good quality. For longer storage, freeze them — walnuts freeze exceptionally well for up to a year.

**Are these truly raw and unsalted?**
Yes. The ingredient list is just "walnuts." No oil, no salt, no preservatives. They're raw and unroasted.

**Can I roast them myself?**
Absolutely. Spread on a baking sheet at 350°F for 8-10 minutes, watching carefully. They'll develop a deeper, toastier flavor if that's your preference.

## The Verdict
