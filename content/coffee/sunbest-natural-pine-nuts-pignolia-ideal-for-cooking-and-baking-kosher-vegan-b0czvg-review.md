**FAQ**

**Q: Do these need to be toasted before use?**
A: No. They're raw and taste great as-is. Toasting enhances the nuttiness but watch them closely — they burn in seconds due to high oil content.

**Q: How should I store them after opening?**
A: Airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 months, or freezer for up to 6. Do not leave them in the original bag at room temperature — they'll go rancid fast.

**Q: Are these the same as the pine nuts that cause "pine mouth"?**
A: That rare, bitter aftertaste is linked to certain Chinese pine nut varieties. These are Mediterranean-sourced, and buyer feedback shows no reports of that issue.
