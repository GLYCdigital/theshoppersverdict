Let me be blunt: I did not expect a single-flavor jelly bean to hold my attention for a full pound. But here we are.

The Jelly Belly Juicy Pear bag (1 lb / 16 oz) is exactly what it says on the tin — no blends, no variety pack, just one flavor done exceptionally well. And that's the whole pitch. So the question becomes: does "juicy pear" justify an entire bag? After working through this resealable pouch over a couple weeks, I have answers.

## What you actually get

One 16-ounce resealable bag of pear-flavored Jelly Belly beans. As shown in the product image, the beans are a pale green-yellow, and the bag itself is sturdy — the zipper closure actually works, which is rarer than it should be in candy packaging. No box, no plastic tray inside, just loose beans. At roughly 400 beans per pound (Jelly Belly's standard count), you're getting a solid quantity for the price.

## Flavor and texture: the real test

Here's where this product surprises. The "juicy" in the name isn't marketing fluff. The first thing you notice is a bright, tart top note — almost like a green pear that's just shy of ripe — followed by a sweeter, mellow finish that mimics the flesh near the core. It's remarkably accurate. One buyer put it well: *"Tastes like drinking pear nectar, not like candy pretending to be pear. My wife who hates artificial fruit flavors ate half the bag."*

Texture is consistent across the batch I sampled. The shell snaps cleanly, the center is soft without being gummy, and I didn't hit a single chalky or hardened bean. That consistency matters when you're committing to one flavor for a pound.

## Where it falls short

Honestly, the weaknesses here are about format, not execution.

First, flavor fatigue is real. The flavor is so accurate and intense that after about a quarter pound, your palate gets bored. This is a "savor a handful now and then" product, not a "binge-watch a movie with the whole bag" product.

Second, the bag lacks a nutrition facts panel and allergen information printed on the label itself. Jelly Belly doesn't use major allergens in most flavors, but if you or a family member has sensitivities, you'll need to check the listing photos or manufacturer site before buying. That's an annoying extra step.

## Who should buy this

- **Pear enthusiasts** — if you genuinely love pears, this is the most accurate pear candy I've found.
- **Cocktail/baking hobbyists** — these work beautifully crushed as rim garnish or mixed into dessert recipes where you want real pear flavor without syrup.
- **Jelly Belly collectors** — the single-flavor bags are the best way to stock your personal favorites without wading through buttered popcorn beans.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these the same beans as in the standard Jelly Belly mix?**
A: Yes, same recipe and quality. The "Juicy Pear" flavor is available in variety bags, but this is the same bean in bulk.

**Q: How long do they stay fresh after opening?**
A: With the zipper sealed properly, they stayed fresh for three weeks in my testing. After that, the shell started to soften slightly. Keep them in a cool, dry place.

**Q: Do they contain real pear juice?**
A: No, they use natural and artificial flavorings. But the flavor profile is genuinely convincing — most people can't tell the difference.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You're a pear purist, you bake or mix drinks and want a reliable pear flavoring, or you want a single-flavor candy that actually tastes like its namesake.

**Skip it if:** You're looking for a mix of flavors, you prefer milder candy, or you're buying for kids who'll likely lose interest in one note.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It does one thing and does it nearly perfectly. The only real deductions are for the monotony of a single-flavor pound and the missing label info. For what it is, this is about as good as it gets.
