**But let's talk weaknesses, because they're real.** First, this is an oily bean — a hallmark of darker roasts. That oil is flavor, but it's also residue. Owners of super-automatic espresso machines with built-in grinders and brew units should expect more frequent cleaning cycles. I'd steer this coffee toward drip, press, or manual espresso setups instead. Second, there's no roast date printed on the bag. You're trusting Amazon's stock rotation, which is a gamble on freshness. Buy from sellers with recent reviews to minimize risk.

**Who should buy this?** Three groups: (1) drip coffee drinkers who want a bold, low-acid morning cup without specialty pricing; (2) French press users tired of weak commercial blends; (3) anyone adding milk or cream — this roast holds its character through dairy better than lighter beans. Skip it if you're chasing bright, fruity single-origin notes, or if you run a high-end super-auto espresso machine that demands dry, lighter-roasted beans.

**Does it work for cold brew?**
