Let's be direct about what this is: the Gold Medal Popcorn Kit Coconut is a flavor powder, not a popcorn seasoning salt. And that distinction matters more than you'd think.

Most "coconut" popcorn products taste like sunscreen or artificial piña colada. This one doesn't. The 8oz kit contains a finely milled coconut powder that smells like toasted coconut flakes straight out of a bakery. If you've ever had fresh macaroon filling, that's the closest comparison. It's sweet, but it's not sugar-forward — the coconut comes through as the primary note, with a light vanilla undertone that doesn't overwhelm.

**What's actually in the box**

You get one 8oz pouch of coconut-flavored powder. That's it. No butter, no oil, no measuring spoon. The kit is designed for a 6oz popcorn popper (the standard home theater size), and Gold Medal engineered the ratio so one pouch seasons six full batches. If you're using a smaller 4oz popper, expect closer to eight batches. If you're using a larger 8oz machine, you'll need to stretch it or double up.

The powder itself is fine-milled — almost like confectioner's sugar in texture. That's a good thing because it clings to popcorn without needing a heavy oil coating. A light mist of butter or coconut oil is enough for it to stick evenly.

**Performance and real-world use**

I tested this on a standard 6oz Whirley Pop and a microwave batch. The microwave batch was the more forgiving test, which tells you something about how forgiving this powder is. It doesn't clump into salty chunks like many commercial seasonings. It distributes evenly with a simple toss, and you don't need to drench the popcorn in butter for it to adhere.

The flavor intensity is the standout. One batch with the full suggested amount gives you a pronounced coconut profile — think sweetened coconut flakes, not a hint. If you want a milder touch, use two-thirds of the packet and save the rest. That's a practical tip most reviews don't mention: the kit is calibrated for bold flavor, not subtlety.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The Amazon reviews at 4.6 stars out of 229 ratings cluster around two themes: flavor authenticity and consistency. One reviewer put it simply: "Finally a coconut popcorn that doesn't taste like a candle." Another noted, "Used it for a movie night and my kids thought I bought gourmet popcorn." The consistency point keeps coming up — batches come out the same every time, which is rare for flavored popcorn powders.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest drawback is the packaging. The pouch isn't resealable. You'll need a clip or a separate container, and if you leave it open, the powder absorbs moisture and clumps. That's a genuine annoyance for a product designed for six separate uses.

Second, this is sweet. If you were hoping for a savory coconut — think coconut oil popcorn with a hint of flavor — this isn't it. The sugar content is noticeable, and it's closer to kettle corn territory than movie theater popcorn. Third, the flavor fades if you store the popped popcorn. It's best eaten fresh; by day two in a bowl, the coconut note drops off significantly.

**Who should buy this**

- **Home theater owners** with a 6oz popper who want consistent, flavored batches without measuring.
- **Coconut lovers** who want a genuine toasted coconut flavor, not artificial extract.
- **Party hosts** making multiple batches — the pre-measured ratio removes guesswork.

Skip it if you want a subtle seasoning or prefer savory popcorn. Also skip if you're sensitive to sweetness in your snacks.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this with an air popper?**
A: Yes, but you'll need a light oil spray first. The powder needs something to cling to; dry popcorn will just shed it.

**Q: How many batches does one kit make?**
A: Six full batches with a 6oz popper. Use less for lighter flavor and it stretches further.

**Q: Is this gluten-free?**
A: Yes, the powder is gluten-free and contains no artificial coconut flavoring.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a reliable, genuinely coconut-flavored popcorn that tastes like toasted coconut, not candy, and you don't mind a sweet profile.

**Skip it if:** You need resealable packaging or you prefer savory or subtle seasonings.

**Rating:** 4.6/5 — the flavor is excellent, the value is fair at roughly $1.50 per batch, but the non-resealable pouch and sweetness keep it from perfection.
