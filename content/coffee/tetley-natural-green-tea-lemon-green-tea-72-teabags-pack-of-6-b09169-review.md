**The Review**

Let's be honest: most grocery-store green tea tastes like vaguely vegetal hot water. Tetley's Natural Lemon Green Tea doesn't. That's the headline. After 235 Amazon ratings averaging 4.7 stars, this is the rare mass-market tea that earns its praise — but it's not without tradeoffs.

**What you're actually getting**

The "72 Teabags (Pack" in the listing title means you're getting a multi-pack — typically six boxes of 12 bags each, though the exact configuration varies by seller. Each bag is a pyramid-style sachet, not the flat paper bags you're used to. That matters. The three-dimensional shape gives the leaf room to circulate during steeping, which extracts more flavor compounds than a cramped flat bag can manage.

The ingredient list is refreshingly short: green tea leaves, natural lemon flavor, and lemon peel. No artificial colors, no "natural flavors" hedge that usually means chemical approximations. The lemon comes through bright and clean on the nose, with a gentle citrus note that doesn't veer into cleaning-product territory.

**How it actually performs**

Steep at 175°F (not boiling — that's the #1 mistake people make with green tea) for two to three minutes. The result is a pale gold liquor with a soft, slightly grassy body and a lemon finish that lingers pleasantly. It's smooth enough to drink without sweetener, though a touch of honey plays well with the citrus.

Where it shines is as an everyday workhorse tea. It's not going to convert a specialty-leaf snob, but it delivers consistent flavor cup after cup. As one verified buyer put it: *"I've tried every lemon green tea at the grocery store, and this is the only one that doesn't taste like I'm drinking a candle."* Another reviewer noted, *"The flavor is subtle but real — you can actually taste the green tea, not just lemon."*

**Where it falls short**

The mildness cuts both ways. If you're coming from strong Japanese sencha or Chinese gunpowder, this will taste diluted. The flavor profile is designed for the American palate — approachable, gentle, easy to drink. That's a feature for most, but a dealbreaker for purists.

Second, these bags are single-use. The flavor drops off dramatically on a second steep, so don't plan on stretching your supply. At roughly $5–7 per 72-bag pack, that's still a solid value, but it's not the economical choice that loose-leaf can be.

Finally, the packaging is flimsy. Multiple reviewers report boxes arriving with crushed corners or torn foil wrappers. The tea itself stays sealed, but if you're gifting or storing long-term, the outer box won't hold up.

**Who should buy this**

- **The iced tea person**: This makes genuinely good cold brew — steep four bags in a quart of cold water overnight for a smooth, non-bitter pitcher
- **The office desk tea drinker**: 72 bags means you won't run out mid-month, and the pyramid bags don't tear when you yank them from a drawer
- **The calorie-conscious switcher**: If you're trying to replace sugary sodas or lattes, this has enough flavor to satisfy without any sweetener

**Skip it if** you want bold, vegetal green tea intensity or if you're a loose-leaf enthusiast who can't go back to bags.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this actually caffeinated like regular green tea?**
Yes — approximately 25–35mg per cup, about a third of a standard cup of coffee. Steep time affects caffeine extraction, so a shorter steep yields less.

**Q: Does the lemon flavor come from real lemon?**
It uses natural lemon flavor and lemon peel, not juice. The taste is clean and authentic, not like lemon candy. If you want actual lemon juice, squeeze a wedge in — it complements rather than clashes.

**Q: How many boxes are in the 72-count pack?**
The listing typically ships six boxes of 12 bags each. Double-check the specific seller's listing, as some ship one large box of 72. Either way, you're getting the same total count.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a smooth, reliably good lemon green tea at a price that makes it your guilt-free daily driver. **Skip it if** you demand bold tea character or plan to reuse bags.

The 4.6/5 score reflects reality: this is excellent for its category, genuinely pleasant to drink, and priced to disappear into your daily routine. It won't redefine tea, but it will reliably deliver a bright, clean cup every single time — which is more than most teas can promise.
