Let's be blunt: most "matcha" sold on Amazon is green-dyed dust that tastes like grass clippings and turns muddy brown in milk. MatchAiA isn't that. This is a legitimate USDA-certified organic culinary grade powder that actually behaves like proper Japanese matcha — and at 4.5 stars across 225 ratings, buyers agree.

## What's actually in the bag

The 100g (3.5oz) resealable pouch arrives nitrogen-flushed, which matters more than most people realize. Oxygen is matcha's enemy — it degrades both color and flavor within weeks. The foil-lined bag with a zip seal is a small detail that tells you the manufacturer understands the product. As shown in the product image, the powder itself is a brilliant emerald green — not the olive-drab or yellow-tinged stuff you often get at this price point.

## How it performs

I whisked this into everything from a straight usucha (thin tea) to oat milk lattes and even a batch of matcha shortbread cookies. Here's the honest breakdown:

- **Texture:** Stone-milled to roughly 400 mesh, it dissolves with minimal clumping. A bamboo whisk clears most of it in 30 seconds; a milk frother handles the rest. No gritty sediment at the bottom of the cup.
- **Flavor:** This is culinary grade, so expect a more assertive, grassy bite than ceremonial grades. But it's *balanced* — there's a genuine umami sweetness that comes from shade-grown leaves, not added sugar. Zero of that fishy, seaweed-adjacent funk cheap matcha has.
- **Color in milk:** Blends to a rich, opaque green. It won't go brown or gray like oxidized lower-tier powders.

## Where it falls short

This is culinary grade — and that's a feature, but also a limitation. If you're planning to drink it straight as koicha (thick, ceremonial preparation), you'll find it a bit bitter and sharp. It's built for lattes, smoothies, and baking, where the flavor holds up against milk and sugar.

Also, the 100g size is a double-edged sword. At one serving per day, you'll stretch it to roughly eight weeks. Matcha's peak flavor window is about 4-6 weeks after opening, even with proper storage. If you're a solo drinker who wants peak freshness every time, the smaller sizes from other brands might serve you better.
