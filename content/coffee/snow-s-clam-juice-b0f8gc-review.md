**FAQ**

*Can I freeze leftover clam juice?*
Yes. Pour it into an ice cube tray, freeze, then transfer cubes to a zip-top bag. Each cube is roughly 1-2 tablespoons — easy to add to sauces or soups.

*How long does it stay fresh after opening?*
About 2-3 weeks refrigerated. Check for any off-odor or cloudiness before using — if it smells overly fishy, it's past its prime.

*Is this the same as clam broth?*
No. Clam juice is the strained liquid from steamed clams — more concentrated and saltier. Clam broth is typically a lighter, seasoned stock. They're not interchangeable in recipes.
