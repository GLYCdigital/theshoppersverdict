If you've tried dairy-free chocolate chips before, you know the struggle: they either refuse to melt, taste like chalk, or seize into a gritty paste the second they hit heat. The Enjoy Life Ricemilk Baking Morsels are the exception that actually works. After testing these in cookies, melted down for drizzling, and straight from the bag (yes, I did that), here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The 9-ounce bag arrives as a standard resealable pouch — nothing fancy, but the zip closure is genuinely functional and keeps chips fresh between uses. The chips themselves are smaller than standard semi-sweet morsels, roughly lentil-sized, which means they distribute more evenly through batter. As shown in the product image, they're a dark brown color that looks like traditional chocolate, not the pale, waxy appearance many dairy-free alternatives have.

The ingredient list is refreshingly short: rice milk powder, unsweetened chocolate, sugar, and cocoa butter. That's it. No palm oil, no soy lecithin, no weird stabilizers.

## Performance and Real-World Use

Here's where these morsels separate themselves from the competition. I melted a half-cup in a double boiler and it went smooth and glossy in about three minutes — no graininess, no seizing. That's rare for a rice milk-based chip. In cookies, they hold their shape during baking but stay soft and creamy once cooled, which is exactly what you want.

The flavor profile is worth noting: these are sweet, closer to milk chocolate than dark. The rice milk adds a subtle creaminess that masks the bitter notes you'd expect from the cocoa content. One Amazon reviewer put it well: "These are the only allergy-friendly chips my son can eat that actually taste like chocolate, not artificially flavored wax."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 225 ratings, three themes dominate. First, the safety factor — parents of children with multiple allergies repeatedly mention these are one of the only safe options. ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides. The sweetness level is higher than traditional dark chocolate chips — if you're making something that relies on bitter cocoa notes to balance sugar, you'll need to adjust your recipe. The price is also a real consideration; you're paying a premium for the allergen-free certification. And while the 9-ounce bag is standard, if you bake frequently, you'll go through these quickly.

## FAQ

**Do these taste like real chocolate?**
Close. They're sweeter and milder than dark chocolate, but the cocoa butter base gives them a genuine chocolate flavor that most dairy-free alternatives miss.

**Can I use these in a chocolate fountain or for candy making?**
They melt smoothly, but they're not formulated for high-heat applications. For dipping and drizzling, yes. For fountain use, you'd need a chocolate with more cocoa butter.

**Are these truly free of all major allergens?**
Yes. They're certified free of the top 8 allergens (milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soy) and produced in a dedicated nut-free facility.
