**Real-World Performance**  

Stovetop method: bring 1 cup water or milk to a boil, stir in ½ cup oats, reduce heat, simmer 5 minutes. You'll get a creamy but chewy texture — the rolled flakes absorb liquid without disintegrating. Microwave works too: 2 minutes at 50% power, stir, then 1 more minute. The texture holds up better than quick oats, which turn gluey fast.

Baking is where these shine. I swapped them into a banana bread recipe (grinding 1 cup into flour in a blender first) — the crumb stayed moist and the oat flavor came through clean. **FAQ**  

**Q: Are these gluten-free?**  
A: Quaker labels them "gluten free" but they're processed in facilities that also handle wheat. If you have celiac, choose the certified gluten-free line instead. For sensitivity, most reviewers report no issues.

**Q: Can I eat these raw?**  
A: Yes — rolled oats are steamed during processing, so they're safe uncooked. That's why overnight oats work. Just don't dry-crunch them like cereal; they're dense.

**Q: How do these compare to steel-cut oats?**  
A: Steel-cut takes 20–30 minutes and has a much firmer, nuttier bite. These rolled oats cook in 5 minutes and are softer — better for baking and kids.
