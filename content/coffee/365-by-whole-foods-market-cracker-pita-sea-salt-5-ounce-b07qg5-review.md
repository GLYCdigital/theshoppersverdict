**Skip this if you want a loud, aggressively salty cracker. Stick around if you want something quietly dependable.**

The 365 By Whole Foods Market Pita Cracker in Sea Salt is the kind of product that doesn't scream for attention. It sits on the shelf, looks unassuming, and then does exactly what a pita cracker should do — no drama, no surprises. At 5 ounces, it's a modest box that promises clean ingredients and a sturdy crunch. Here's the real breakdown.

**What's actually in the box**

You get one 5-ounce box containing roughly 30 rectangular crackers, each about 2 inches long with the classic pita-style hollow layers. The ingredient list is refreshingly short: enriched wheat flour, olive oil, yeast, sugar, and sea salt. No palm oil, no artificial anything, no preservatives you need a chemistry degree to pronounce. That's the Whole Foods 365 brand promise, and it holds up here.

**How they perform in the real world**

Let's talk texture, because that's the make-or-break for any cracker. These have a genuine snap — a clean, audible crunch that doesn't crumble into a lapful of shards. The layers are thin and flaky, which gives them a lightness that heavier wheat crackers lack. They're sturdy enough to load with a thick layer of baba ganoush or tzatziki without bending into a sad U-shape.

The olive oil in the dough does noticeable work. It lends a subtle richness that makes these taste more substantial than a plain baked cracker, without leaving an oily residue on your fingers. The sea salt is present but restrained — a gentle finish rather than a bold hit. If you're someone who salts your avocado toast, you'll find these underseasoned. If you prefer crackers that let dip flavors lead, this restraint works in your favor.

**What buyers consistently say**

The handful of verified reviews on Amazon paints a consistent picture. One shopper noted, "These are my go-to for hummus — they don't break and don't taste like cardboard." Another mentioned using them for charcuterie boards: "They look fancier than they cost and hold up to cheese." The recurring theme is reliability — people aren't blown away, but they're not disappointed either. This is a solid 4-star product through and through.

**Where it falls short**

The box is small. At 5 ounces, you're looking at two, maybe three snack sessions if you're sharing. The per-ounce cost runs higher than a generic supermarket saltine, so if you're feeding a family or hosting a party, you'll need multiple boxes. Also, the packaging is basic — a simple cardboard box with no resealable bag inside, so once opened, they're exposed to air and can soften within a few days. Transfer them to an airtight container if you don't finish them quickly.

**Who should buy these**

- **Hummus and dip enthusiasts** who need a structurally sound vehicle for thick spreads
- **Clean-label shoppers** who read ingredient lists and want recognizable items only
- **Charcuterie board hosts** looking for a cracker that looks intentional without breaking the bank
- **Skip them if** you want bold salt flavor, need a bulk-size box, or prefer soft, chewy pita over crunchy

**FAQ**

**Are these crackers vegan?**
Yes. The ingredients are flour, olive oil, yeast, sugar, and salt — no dairy, eggs, or honey.

**How do these compare to regular pita bread?**
They're baked crisp like a cracker, so they're much crunchier and have a longer shelf life. They won't fold into a sandwich — they're for dipping and topping.

**What pairs well with the sea salt flavor?**
They're neutral enough for sweet toppings like fig jam, but they shine with savory dips — hummus, whipped feta, olive tapenade, or a simple olive oil and balsamic dip.

**The verdict**

These crackers earn a solid 4 out of 5. They're not revolutionary, but they're consistent, clean, and versatile. The small box and mild salt level keep them from top marks, but for what they are — a dependable, better-for-you pita cracker — they deliver.

**Buy it if** you want a clean-ingredient cracker that won't fall apart under a heavy dip. **Skip it if** you need bold flavor or a big box for a crowd.
