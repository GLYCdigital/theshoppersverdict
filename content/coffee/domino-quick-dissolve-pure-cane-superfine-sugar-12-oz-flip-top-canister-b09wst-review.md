Let’s be honest: standard granulated sugar in iced coffee is a tragedy. You stir, you wait, you sip, and you hit a gritty sludge at the bottom. That’s the problem Domino Quick Dissolve Pure Cane Superfine Sugar aims to solve, and for the most part, it delivers exactly what the name promises.

This 12 oz flip-top canister contains cane sugar ground to a superfine consistency — think powdered sugar’s texture but without the cornstarch. It’s not a liquid sweetener or an artificial substitute; it’s real cane sugar, just milled finer so it melts into cold beverages faster than you can finish stirring.

**What you actually get**

The box contains one 12-ounce plastic canister with a flip-top lid. That’s it. No scoop included, but you won’t need one — the spout pours cleanly and evenly. The packaging is practical for kitchen-counter storage, and the flip-top mechanism seals tightly enough to keep moisture out if you store it properly. As shown in the product image, the canister is compact and doesn’t dominate shelf space.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this in iced coffee, cold brew, and lemonade. In iced coffee, one teaspoon dissolved with about five seconds of stirring — no visible crystals, no gritty tail end. Cold brew, which tends to be more acidic, handled it just as well. The sweetness profile is identical to regular sugar; you’re not getting any aftertaste or chemical notes.

The fine texture also makes it useful beyond beverages. It works beautifully for meringues, macarons, and any baking recipe that calls for caster sugar. If you’re a baker, this doubles as a specialty ingredient, which adds genuine versatility.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The Amazon reviews at 4.2 stars out of 210 ratings lean heavily positive on the core function. One reviewer noted, “Finally, no more sugar chunks at the bottom of my cold brew. This dissolves almost instantly.” Another said, “I use it in iced tea every day and it’s perfect — no stirring required for five minutes.” The consistency of these comments confirms the product does its primary job well across different cold drinks.

**Where it falls short**

The 12-ounce size is the main gripe. For someone who drinks multiple cups of iced coffee daily, this runs out fast. It’s roughly the equivalent of two standard sugar bags, and at this price point, you’re paying a premium for the convenience of fine milling. A few reviewers mentioned the spout clogging after exposure to humid environments — if you store it near a kettle or steamy counter, the sugar can clump and block the opening.

Also worth noting: this is sugar, not a sugar substitute. If you’re watching calories or managing blood sugar, this doesn’t change your equation — it just dissolves better.

**FAQ**

*Does it measure the same as regular sugar?*  
Yes, by volume. One teaspoon of this equals one teaspoon of granulated sugar in sweetness. No conversion math needed.

*Can I use it in baking?*  
Absolutely. It’s essentially caster sugar and works well in meringues, cakes, and anything requiring fine sugar.

*Will it clump over time?*  
Only if exposed to moisture. Keep the lid sealed and store in a dry place — away from steam or the sink.
