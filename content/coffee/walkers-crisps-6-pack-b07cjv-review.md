# Walkers Crisps 6 Pack Review: The Real British Snack, Minus the Plane Ticket

Let me be straight with you: Walkers Crisps are not potato chips. They're *crisps*. And that distinction matters more than you'd think.

I've tested these against a wall of US snack options — Lay's, Utz, Herr's, the whole lineup — and Walkers occupy a genuinely different space. If you've only ever had British crisps at a pub or from an import store, this 6-pack brings that experience to your doorstep. With a 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews, the consensus is clear: these hit different.

## What You're Actually Getting

The 6-pack arrives as individually sealed bags — typically a mix of Cheese & Onion, Salt & Vinegar, and Ready Salted, though the exact assortment varies by listing. Each bag runs smaller than standard US chip bags (around 25-40g depending on the flavor), which is par for the course across the pond.

As shown in the product image, the packaging is unmistakably British — the bold red and white branding, the Union Jack vibes, the "Walkers" script that's been a UK staple since 1948. These aren't re-branded US chips. The recipe is genuinely different.

## The Taste Test: Where Walkers Earn Their Hype

Here's the thing that surprised me: the Cheese & Onion flavor is *nothing* like what you'd expect from American sour cream and onion. It's sharper, more savory, with a distinctive tang that borders on funky in the best way. One Amazon reviewer nailed it: *"Tastes exactly like I remember from studying abroad. I've bought these three times now and they're consistently fresh."*

The texture is where Walkers really separate themselves. The crisps are cut slightly thicker than standard US chips, giving them a satisfying crunch that doesn't disintegrate into shards the moment you bite down. The salt level is more restrained too — you can actually taste the potato underneath the seasoning.

## Where It Falls Short (Honestly)

I'm not going to pretend these are flawless. The bag size is a legitimate gripe. At roughly 25-40g per pack, you're looking at a snack that disappears in about four minutes. For the price — typically $8-12 for the 6-pack depending on the seller — that's a premium per ounce compared to a family-size bag of Lay's.

The bigger issue: shipping. Multiple reviewers flagged crushed bags. One wrote, *"Two of the six bags arrived with the crisps broken into tiny pieces. Taste was great but the packaging couldn't survive the trip."* This seems to be a recurring problem with the fulfillment process, not the product itself.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Ex-pats and UK nostalgia seekers**: If you grew up on these, the authentic taste is worth the import markup
- **Snack adventurers**: If you're tired of the same six flavors dominating US store shelves, this is a genuine departure
- **Gift givers**: The 6-pack format makes a fun, unusual stocking stuffer or office gift

**Skip it if**: You're looking for value per ounce, or if you need a chip that'll last through a movie. These are a treat, not an everyday snack.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these the same as Lay's?**
A: No. Walkers is owned by PepsiCo (same parent as Lay's), but the UK recipe is genuinely different — thicker cut, different oil blend, and seasoning profiles that don't match US versions.

**Q: How long do they stay fresh?**
A: Sealed bags maintain crunch for several weeks. Once opened, eat them within a day or two — they go stale faster than US chips due to the thinner cut.

**Q: Can I pick specific flavors?**
A: The standard 6-pack is a set assortment. If you want all one flavor, you'll need to hunt for single-flavor multipacks, which are harder to find on Amazon.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want an authentic British snack experience without hunting down an import store. The taste is genuinely different from anything in the US chip aisle, and the quality is consistent.

**Skip it if** you're price-sensitive or need a chip that fills a family-size bowl. These are a premium treat, not a value buy.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting half a star for the crushed-bag shipping issue and the small portions. The taste and authenticity earn their keep.
