Let's be blunt: most "premium" olive oil on Amazon is bottled marketing. You're paying for a pretty label and a story. The Blueprint Bryan Johnson Extra Virgin Olive Oil is different — it puts the receipts on the bottle. Whether that justifies the price tag is the real question.

## What You're Actually Getting

This is a 500ml bottle of early-harvest Koroneiki olive oil from Greece. The headline spec is 400+ mg/kg polyphenols — the antioxidants that give EVOO its health halo. For context, standard grocery store EVOO typically runs 100-200. The label also prints the harvest date (not a "best by" date, which is a red flag on most competitor products) and includes third-party lab results for purity markers like DAGs and PPP.

As shown in the product image, the bottle is dark glass — essential for protecting the oil from light degradation. The packaging is functional, not flashy. No gold leaf, no nonsense.

## Performance and Real-World Use

I've used this oil for about three weeks: drizzled over salads, used in low-heat sautéing, and finished soups. The flavor is assertive — grassy, with a serious peppery kick at the back of the throat. That burn isn't a flaw; it's the polyphenols doing their thing. If you've only had bland, mass-market olive oil, this will taste almost spicy.

The texture is smooth and coating, not watery. It emulsifies well in vinaigrettes. For cooking, it holds up fine at medium heat, but this is really a finishing oil — don't waste it in a deep fryer.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**Price:** At roughly $30+ per 500ml, you're paying a significant premium. You can find high-polyphenol Greek olive oils for less if you shop around. Part of what you're paying for is the Bryan Johnson brand and the rigorous testing.

**Flavor profile:** The bold, peppery character is a feature for some, a flaw for others. If you want a neutral oil for baking or delicate fish dishes, this will overpower them. This is not a versatile kitchen workhorse — it's a specialty ingredient.

**Bottle size:** 500ml goes fast if you use olive oil daily. At this price point, that's a recurring cost you'll feel.
