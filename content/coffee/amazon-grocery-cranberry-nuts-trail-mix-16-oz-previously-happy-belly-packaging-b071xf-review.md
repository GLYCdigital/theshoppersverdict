**The trail mix you actually reach for — not the one you settle for.**  

Most budget trail mixes are 60% peanuts, 30% M&M's, and 10% everything else. This Amazon Grocery Cranberry & Nuts mix flips that script. It's a nut-forward blend — primarily almonds, cashews, and pecans — with dried cranberries playing a supporting role, not the lead. If you've been burned by stale, dusty "premium" mixes at triple the price, this 16-ounce bag is worth a serious look.

**What's in the bag (and what's not)**  

Open the resealable pouch and you get: whole almonds, cashew halves, pecan pieces, and dried cranberries. That's it. No peanuts as filler, no sunflower seeds pretending to be nuts, no chocolate chips that melt into a mess in your car. The mix is lightly salted — enough to bring out the nut flavor but not so much that your lips feel like the Dead Sea. The cranberries are soft, chewy, and slightly sweet with a tart finish. As shown in the product image, the bag is straightforward: clear packaging so you can see exactly what you're getting, with the "Happy Belly" branding (now under Amazon Grocery) on the label.

**Performance and real-world use**  

I've had this bag open on my desk for two weeks. The nuts have stayed crunchy — no rancid oil smell, no softness. The resealable zipper actually works, which is rarer than it should be. Portion-wise, a quarter-cup is a solid snack: about 170 calories, 5 grams of protein, and enough healthy fats to kill a mid-afternoon hunger spike without making you sluggish. It holds up in a backpack for hiking, in a glovebox for emergencies, and on a conference table without making a mess.

**What buyers consistently say**  

The 229 ratings at 4.6 stars mostly echo the same themes. One reviewer calls it "the best trail mix I've found on Amazon that doesn't taste like cardboard," while another notes, "Finally, a mix where the nuts aren't all broken bits." A frequent comment is about the cranberry-to-nut ratio: "Some bags have more cranberries than others, but when it's right, it's perfect." That inconsistency is the one real complaint I see repeated — not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing if you're picky about proportions.

**Where it falls short**  

Two honest tradeoffs. First, if you're expecting a sweet, candy-laden trail mix, this will disappoint. It's savory-sweet, not dessert. Second, the bag-to-bag variation on cranberries is real. I've had bags that were 70/30 nuts-to-cranberries and one that was closer to 50/50. Both tasted fine, but if you're buying this specifically for the cranberry hit, your mileage may vary. Also, no peanuts means it's not the cheapest per-ounce option — but you're paying for better nuts.

**Who should buy this**  

- **Hikers and travelers** who want a shelf-stable, non-melting snack with real protein  
- **Office workers** tired of vending-machine mixes full of raisins and peanut skins  
- **Parents** packing school lunches — no chocolate to stain, no peanuts for allergy-sensitive classrooms  
- **Keto or low-sugar folks** who need a sweet-ish fix without added junk  

**Skip it if** you want a classic trail mix with M&M's, or if you're on a tight budget and just need peanuts and raisins.

**The Verdict**  

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely nut-heavy trail mix that tastes fresh, doesn't fall apart, and doesn't cost $12 for a tiny bag. **Skip it if** you need chocolate or peanuts in your mix.
