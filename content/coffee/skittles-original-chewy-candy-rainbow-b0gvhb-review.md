Let's be honest: when you order a bag of Skittles on Amazon, you're not solving a problem. You're feeding a craving. And the Original Chewy version — a spin on the classic that swaps the hard shell for a soft, gummy-like texture — has earned its 4.8-star rating from 240+ buyers for one simple reason: it fixes the original's biggest flaw.

The original Skittles are gone in three seconds flat. The shell shatters, the candy dissolves, and you're left reaching for another handful. The Chewy version takes the same five flavors — strawberry, orange, grape, lemon, and green apple — and gives them actual staying power. Each piece is denser, softer, and takes real chewing. That's the entire pitch, and it works.

**What you actually get**

The standard order is a 14-ounce bag (about 400 pieces, if you're counting). The bag is resealable, which matters more than you'd think — these candies stay soft and fresh for weeks if you don't demolish them in one sitting. The pieces are slightly larger than original Skittles, with a matte finish instead of the glossy shell.

**How they perform in the real world**

Texture is the story here. The chew is closer to a Starburst than a gummy bear — firm, slightly springy, and it holds its shape as you work through it. The flavors are identical to the original lineup, but they release more slowly because the candy lasts longer. The grape is still the strongest, the green apple still has that tart kick, and the lemon still tastes like lemon. No surprises.

The one physical tradeoff: these stick to dental work. Braces, retainers, partials — anything metal or acrylic in your mouth will collect chewy candy residue. If you've got orthodontics, skip these. Your future self will thank you.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The reviews cluster around two themes: texture and portion control. One buyer wrote, "I always loved Skittles but hated how fast they were gone. These actually make you chew, which sounds dumb, but it's exactly what I wanted." Another noted, "My kids go through a bag in half the time of regular Skittles because each piece takes longer to eat." That slower consumption is the practical benefit — you're getting more minutes of candy per dollar.

**Where it falls short**

The resealable bag is good, not great. The zipper seal works, but if you're not careful, it can catch and tear after a few opens. Also, the chewy texture isn't for everyone — some buyers specifically wanted the hard shell crunch and felt misled. The product description is clear that this is the chewy version, but if you're a purist, the original is still available.

**Who should buy this**

- **Orthodontics-free candy lovers** who want Skittles flavor with actual chew time
- **Parents** looking for a candy that slows kids down (it works, trust me)
- **Anyone who buys Skittles in bulk** and wants them to stay fresh past week one

**Skip it if** you have braces, want the classic crunchy shell, or prefer portion-controlled individual packs over a family-size bag.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these just gummy Skittles?**
A: No. They're a different product — denser and softer than the original, but not gummy. Think of a softer Starburst with Skittles flavor.

**Q: How long does a bag last?**
A: If you're honest about portions, about two weeks. Realistically, most people finish a 14-oz bag in three to four days.

**Q: Do they melt or get hard in transit?**
A: Amazon reviews report no major melting issues, and the resealable bag prevents hardening over time.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you like Skittles flavor but want more chew per piece, or you're tired of blowing through a bag in one sitting.

**Skip it if** you have dental work, or you specifically want the crunchy shell of the original.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It's a smart variation on a classic, priced fairly, and delivers exactly what it promises. The dental-work caveat is the only real reason to hesitate.
