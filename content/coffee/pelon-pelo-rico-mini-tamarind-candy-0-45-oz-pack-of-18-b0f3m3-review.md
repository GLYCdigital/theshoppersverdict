**The flavor reality check**

Tamarind is not for everyone. It's naturally sour and slightly funky — think dates crossed with lemon and a hint of smoke. Pelon Pelo Rico balances that with sugar and chili, but the tamarind is unmistakably the star. The first squeeze hits you with tangy fruit, then a slow-building warmth from the chili powder settles in. It's not spicy-hot like a flaming hot Cheeto; it's more of a gentle tingle that makes you want another squeeze.

One Amazon reviewer put it simply: *"Tastes just like the ones I remember from my childhood in Mexico. Perfect balance of sweet and sour."* Another noted, *"My kids are obsessed. **Where it falls short**

The portion size is the obvious tradeoff. At 0.45 ounces per tube, these are designed for a quick fix, not a long snack session. A determined eater can finish one in about 30 seconds. Also, the texture can be polarizing. Some people find the thick, sticky paste pleasant; others describe it as "gloopy." If you prefer crunchy or chewy candies, this might not hit the spot.

**FAQ**

**Is this candy actually spicy?**  
Mildly. The chili powder adds warmth and depth, but it's not hot. Kids typically handle it fine. If you want serious heat, look for the "extra picante" versions of tamarind candy.

**How long does one tube last?**  
Realistically, a few minutes. It's a small portion meant for a quick flavor hit, not prolonged chewing.

**Does it need refrigeration?**  
No. The paste is shelf-stable. Just keep the tubes sealed and they'll last months. Once opened, they're meant to be finished in one go.
