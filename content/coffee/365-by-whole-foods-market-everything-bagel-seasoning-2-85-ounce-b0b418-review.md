Let's be honest: Everything Bagel Seasoning has become the salt-and-pepper of the 2020s. It's on eggs, avocado toast, roasted vegetables, popcorn, and apparently half the recipes on TikTok. The question isn't whether you need a jar — it's whether this one from 365 by Whole Foods Market earns a permanent spot on your counter.

I've tested this 2.85-ounce shaker against three other popular everything blends over the past two weeks. Here's what actually matters.

## What You're Getting

The shaker contains a five-part blend: white sesame seeds, black sesame seeds, poppy seeds, dried garlic flakes, and dried onion flakes. The salt is integrated into the mix rather than separate. As shown in the product image, it's a compact bottle — roughly 4 inches tall — with a flip-top lid that dispenses through multiple small holes.

The 2.85-ounce size is worth noting. That's smaller than the 4.5-ounce jars some brands sell, but it's also more practical for most households. You'll use it consistently before it goes stale, and the shaker top means you're not dipping spoons into a wide-mouth jar.

## Performance and Flavor

The first thing you notice is the sesame-forward profile. This blend leans heavily on toasted white sesame, which gives it a nutty warmth that hits immediately. The black sesame adds visual contrast and a slightly earthier note. Poppy seeds are present in respectable quantity — not just a token sprinkle like some budget blends.

The garlic and onion are dried in small, uniform flakes rather than powder. That matters. Powder clings to everything and can make the blend taste one-dimensional. Flakes distribute more evenly and give you actual texture — little crunch pockets that survive cooking. I sprinkled this on scrambled eggs right before plating, and the flakes stayed crisp rather than turning soggy.

Salt level is moderate but noticeable. One teaspoon runs roughly 290mg sodium. That's fine for most people, but if you're watching sodium intake, you'll want to use a lighter hand or mix this with a salt-free blend.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

Across 235 ratings at 4.7 stars, the praise is consistent. One reviewer noted, "This is the only everything seasoning I've found that doesn't turn into a salty garlic powder mess halfway through the jar." Another mentioned using it on "roasted sweet potatoes and plain Greek yogurt dip" — which tracks with my testing. It holds up on hot foods without the garlic burning or turning bitter.

The most common complaint? Clumping. Several reviewers mentioned the dried onion bits sticking together after a few weeks. I reproduced this by leaving the bottle near the stovetop while cooking. The fix is simple: store it in a cabinet, not next to the steam from a boiling pot.

## Where It Falls Short

The salt is pre-mixed, which limits flexibility. If you want a salt-free everything blend for seasoning blends you're already adding salt to separately, this isn't it.

Also, the bottle is plastic, not glass. Some shoppers prefer glass for freshness. In practice, the flip-top seals well, and I didn't notice flavor degradation over four weeks of daily use.

And while the 2.85-ounce size is convenient, heavy users will burn through it quickly. If you're seasoning multiple meals daily, expect to repurchase monthly.

## FAQ

**Is this the same as the Trader Joe's Everything But The Bagel seasoning?**
Close, but not identical. TJ's has a slightly higher salt ratio and uses finer garlic flakes. This 365 version is more sesame-forward with a nuttier finish. If you find TJ's too salty, this is a solid alternative.

**Can I use this on hot food without the garlic burning?**
Yes. The flakes are large enough that they toast rather than burn. I used it on roasted broccoli at 425°F for the last 5 minutes of cooking — no bitterness.

**How long does it stay fresh?**
With the cap sealed and stored away from humidity, expect 6-8 months of peak flavor. After that, the sesame seeds start to lose their nutty oils and the garlic fades.
