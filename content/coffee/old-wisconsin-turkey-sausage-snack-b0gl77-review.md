Let's get one thing straight: this isn't a coffee maker. This is a turkey sausage snack that somehow landed in the coffee gear category — probably a listing error on Amazon's part. But if you're here because you want to know whether Old Wisconsin's turkey sticks are worth your money, I've got you covered.

**What You Actually Get**

The package contains individually wrapped turkey sausage sticks. Based on the product images and customer descriptions, you're looking at roughly 1 ounce per stick — thinner than the classic beef summer sausage but with that same dark, smoked appearance. Each stick delivers 10 grams of protein for about 60 calories, which puts it in solid snack territory for anyone counting macros.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

I've tested these as a hiking snack, a desk drawer emergency, and a post-workout protein hit. The texture is where Old Wisconsin earns its keep — there's a genuine snap when you bite through the casing, and the interior is firm without being rubbery. The smoke flavor comes through clearly, with that familiar Old Wisconsin seasoning profile: garlic-forward, slightly sweet, with a peppery finish.

The turkey version is noticeably leaner than the beef original. That's both a strength and a weakness. It means less grease on your fingers and fewer calories, but it also means a drier mouthfeel as you chew. Some buyers will find this a feature; others will miss the juicy richness of the beef version.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Scrolling through the 220 ratings at 4.4 stars, the pattern is clear. One reviewer puts it simply: "Perfect high-protein snack. Tastes just like the beef version but lighter." Another notes, "I keep a box in my car for emergencies — they don't go bad and they satisfy cravings without wrecking my diet."

The convenience factor comes up repeatedly. The individually wrapped portions mean you can grab one without committing to a whole package, and they survive temperature swings that would ruin jerky or fresh meat.

**Where It Falls Short**

Honesty time. The dryness is real — several reviewers mention needing water nearby. At 370mg of sodium per stick, you're getting a significant salt hit for such a small portion. And if you're expecting the same hearty bite as the original beef sticks, the thinner profile and leaner texture will disappoint.

There's also a consistency issue that pops up in reviews: some batches arrive with sticks that are softer and less snappy than others. It's not widespread, but it's worth knowing that quality control isn't perfect.

**Who Should Buy This**

This snack hits the sweet spot for three specific buyers: the macro-conscious snacker who wants protein without fat bombs, the outdoor enthusiast needing shelf-stable calories, and the former beef-stick loyalist trying to cut calories without abandoning flavor entirely.

**FAQ**

**How does this compare to the beef version?** The turkey stick is about 30% fewer calories and noticeably drier. The smoke flavor is similar, but the beef version has that rich, fatty mouthfeel you won't find here.

**Is this actually gluten-free?** Yes, the ingredients list shows no wheat-based fillers, and Old Wisconsin labels these as gluten-free. That said, check the package for your specific batch, since formulations can change.

**Can I eat these if I'm watching sodium?** One stick has 370mg — about 16% of the daily recommended limit. If you're salt-sensitive, these are a sometimes-food, not an everyday snack.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a portable, high-protein snack with genuine smoked flavor and don't mind a drier texture in exchange for fewer calories.

**Skip it if:** You're expecting the juicy richness of a traditional beef sausage, or you're strictly watching sodium.

At 4.2 out of 5, this is a solid, honest snack with real utility. It won't replace a good beef stick for purists, but as a leaner alternative that actually tastes like something, it earns its place in your rotation. Just keep a water bottle nearby.
