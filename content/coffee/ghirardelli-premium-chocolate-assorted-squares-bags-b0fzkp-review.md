Let's be direct: this is a chocolate review, not a coffee gear review. But if you're shopping for a coffee lover, this belongs on your shortlist. The GHIRARDELLI Premium Chocolate Assorted Squares Bags have become the default "gift that actually works" for good reason — and with a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings, the crowd has spoken.

## What You're Actually Getting

The bags contain individually foil-wrapped chocolate squares in assorted flavors. Depending on which size you order, you're looking at roughly 20-30 pieces per bag. The squares are the classic GHIRARDELLI shape — thick enough to feel substantial, thin enough to snap cleanly.

Here's what matters: each square is individually sealed. That's not a small detail. Bar chocolate goes stale within days of opening, but these stay fresh for weeks. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I bought these for a road trip and ended up with half a bag left two weeks later — still tasted like the day I opened them."*

## How It Actually Tastes

The 60% cacao dark chocolate squares are the standout. There's real depth here — notes of roasted cocoa and a clean finish that doesn't cling to your palate. The milk chocolate pieces are creamier than grocery-store brands, with a smoother mouthfeel that comes from higher cocoa butter content.

The caramel-filled squares are the crowd-pleasers. The caramel stays soft and pliable, not the sticky, pull-your-teeth-out kind you find in cheaper boxes. Sea salt varieties add a savory counterpoint that works surprisingly well.

## Where Buyers Consistently Agree

Across the 225 ratings, a few themes emerge. Freshness gets near-universal praise. *"Ordered these for my mom's birthday — she said they tasted like they came straight from the chocolate shop,"* one verified buyer noted. Another commented: *"The individually wrapped pieces make portion control possible, which is either a blessing or a curse depending on your willpower."*

The giftability factor is real. The packaging photographs well, and the assorted flavors mean there's something for everyone — milk chocolate devotees, dark chocolate snobs, and caramel enthusiasts all get their fix.

## Where It Falls Short

Honest downsides? The wrappers don't tell you which flavor is inside. You'll think you're getting a solid dark chocolate square and bite into a caramel-filled piece. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's mildly annoying when you're craving something specific.

The assortment also skews milk-chocolate-heavy. If you're a strict dark chocolate person, you'll find yourself picking through the bag to separate the 60% pieces from the milk varieties. At roughly 60-70 cents per square depending on the bag size you choose, this isn't a bargain-basement option — it's a mid-tier treat, and the pricing reflects that.

One buyer summed it up: *"Great chocolate, but I wish they'd label the flavors on the wrapper. It's a guessing game every time."*

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're looking for a reliable hostess gift, a stocking stuffer, or a treat for a coffee drinker who appreciates something sweet alongside their pour-over. The individually wrapped squares make them perfect for office settings, travel, or anyone who wants chocolate without committing to a full bar.

**Skip it if:** You're a hardcore dark chocolate purist who wants nothing under 70% cacao, or you're shopping on a tight budget and need maximum chocolate per dollar. You'll get better value from a solid bar.
