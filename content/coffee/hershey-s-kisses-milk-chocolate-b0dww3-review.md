Here's where these shine for coffee use. The chocolate-to-cocoa-butter ratio in HERSHEY'S KISSES is forgiving. That's because they lack the stabilizers found in many baking chips, which are designed to hold shape under heat.

**FAQ**

**Q: How many kisses do I need for a 12-oz mug of mocha?**
A: Start with 4-5 kisses for a balanced sweetness. Add 1-2 more if you want a richer, dessert-like drink. The milk chocolate is sweeter than dark, so err on the lower side.

**Q: Can I use these in a French press without it clogging?**
A: Melt them into the milk or cream *before* adding to the press, not directly in the grounds. The chocolate can coat the mesh filter and slow extraction.
