Let's be clear about what this is: HERSHEY'S KISSES Snickerdoodle Cookie Flavored coffee is a dessert in a mug, not a serious single-origin pour-over experience. And honestly? That's exactly what most buyers want from it.

**What you actually get**

The package contains whole coffee beans — not grounds — which is a smart call from Hershey's. Whole beans preserve flavor longer, and you control the grind size for your specific brewing method. As shown in the product image, the bag's design leans into the Snickerdoodle branding with warm cinnamon tones. Expect a 12-ounce bag, standard for specialty coffee at this price point.

**The real-world performance**

Here's where this coffee surprised me. Flavored coffees often taste like someone sprayed artificial syrup on cheap beans. That's not the case here. The snickerdoodle profile comes through as cinnamon and vanilla notes layered over a solid medium roast base. It's sweet without being cloying, and the cookie flavor doesn't nuke the coffee character underneath.

One Amazon reviewer put it simply: "Tastes exactly like a snickerdoodle cookie but without the sugar crash. I drink it black and it's perfect." Another noted, "My husband hates flavored coffee and even he asked for a cup."

The beans grind cleanly and produce a smooth cup with minimal bitterness. The cinnamon aroma is the star — it hits you while brewing and carries through to the finish. It's the kind of coffee that makes your kitchen smell like a bakery at 7 AM.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, three themes dominate:

- **Authentic flavor**: Buyers repeatedly mention the snickerdoodle taste is genuine, not artificial.
- **Versatility**: It holds up in drip machines, French presses, and even cold brew. Several reviewers mentioned using it for iced coffee with cream and loving the result.
- **Smoothness**: Even those who typically add sugar found they didn't need it with this blend.

**Where it falls short**

No product is flawless, and this one has real tradeoffs. First, flavored beans coat your grinder in oils. If you switch between this and unflavored coffee, you'll need to clean your grinder between uses or your next batch will taste faintly of cinnamon.

Second, the bag isn't resealable. Once opened, you need a separate airtight container or the beans go stale faster than they should. Minor, but worth knowing.

Third — and this is the elephant in the room — if you're a coffee purist, skip this. The flavoring is the point. You're not buying this for terroir or tasting notes of "hazelnut and dark chocolate." You're buying it because you want your coffee to taste like a cookie. That's fine. Just know what you're getting.

**Who should buy this**

- **Dessert coffee lovers**: If your ideal morning involves something that tastes like a bakery treat, this is your cup.
- **Gift givers**: It's an easy, crowd-pleasing gift for coworkers, teachers, or hosts — especially around the holidays.
- **Cold brew fans**: The cinnamon and vanilla hold up beautifully in cold brew concentrate.

**Skip it if** you prefer unflavored beans, you're sensitive to artificial sweeteners (though this isn't sugar-coated), or you're shopping for a serious specialty coffee snob.

**FAQ**

**Does this contain actual sugar or sweetener?**
No. The snickerdoodle flavor comes from natural and artificial flavorings added to the beans. The coffee itself has no sugar — any sweetness you taste is from the flavor profile, not added sweeteners.

**Can I use this in an espresso machine?**
Yes, but grind it fine and expect a slightly sweeter, cinnamon-forward shot. It works well in milk drinks like lattes or cappuccinos.

**How should I store it?**
Transfer the beans to an airtight container after opening. Keep them in a cool, dark place. Avoid the fridge — condensation will ruin the beans.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a reliably delicious, dessert-flavored coffee that actually tastes like a snickerdoodle and works across brew methods. It's a 4.6/5 product that delivers exactly what it promises.

**Skip it if** you're a purist who drinks coffee black and wants nothing but the bean's natural character.

At 4.5 stars from 225+ buyers, the consensus is clear: this is one of the better-flavored coffees on the market. It's not trying to be something it's not, and that honesty is refreshing. Grab a bag, brew a cup, and enjoy your cookie coffee.
