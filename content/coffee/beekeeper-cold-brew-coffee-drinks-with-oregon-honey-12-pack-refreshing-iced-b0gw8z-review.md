If you've ever winced at the bitter bite of mass-market iced coffee, the Beekeeper Cold Brew pitch is simple: coffee sweetened only with Oregon honey, slow-steeped for 16 hours, and canned for grab-and-go convenience. After working through a full 12-pack, here's the honest breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains twelve 10.5-ounce aluminum cans. Each can lists two core ingredients — cold brew coffee and Oregon honey — plus a small amount of natural flavors. The cans are sleek, matte-finish, and notably easy to recycle. No bottle opener needed, no refrigeration required until you crack one open, which makes these genuinely portable for road trips, camping, or a desk drawer stash. As shown in the product image, the branding leans rustic-apothecary, and the cans themselves are sturdy enough to survive a tossed backpack.

**Taste and Performance**

The cold brew base is legitimately smooth. The 16-hour steep extracts the coffee oils without the acidic sharpness you get from hot-brewed methods. I drank a can straight from the fridge and another over ice; both held up well without turning watery. The honey sweetness lands around a medium-sweet level — think lightly sweetened, not dessert-like. The Oregon honey's floral notes are present but subtle; the coffee itself is the star here. If you're expecting a honey-forward profile like a honey latte, you'll find the honey more of a background harmonizer than a lead instrument.

The caffeine kick is solid but not jitter-inducing — roughly comparable to a standard 12-ounce cup of drip coffee. I didn't experience the acid reflux that sometimes follows cheap iced coffee, which several Amazon reviewers echoed. One buyer noted, "Finally a cold brew that doesn't wreck my stomach," and I'd agree — the low-acid brewing process makes a real difference for sensitive drinkers.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be direct about the downsides. First, the sweetness is fixed. There's no unsweetened variant in this pack, so if you take your coffee black or prefer stevia, this isn't your match. Second, the honey flavor is more whisper than shout — at $2.50–$3 per can, you're paying a premium for a subtle nuance that some drinkers might not even notice. Third, the 10.5-ounce size is smaller than a typical 12-ounce coffee can; heavy coffee drinkers may find it a quick finish. A few reviewers also mentioned that the cans arrive warm and need a full chill before drinking — plan ahead.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The acid-sensitive coffee lover** who wants real cold brew without the stomachache
- **The clean-label shopper** who reads ingredient lists and refuses artificial sweeteners
- **The on-the-go professional** who needs a reliable, no-fuss coffee that survives a glovebox in summer
- **Campers and road-trippers** who want decent coffee without brewing equipment

Skip it if you prefer intensely sweet coffee drinks, want a strong honey flavor, or are on a tight budget — a homemade cold brew concentrate costs a fraction per serving.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this need to be refrigerated before opening?**
A: No. The cans are shelf-stable until opened. Once you crack one, refrigerate any leftover (though at 10.5 ounces, you'll likely finish it in one sitting).

**Q: How much caffeine is in each can?**
A: The company doesn't publish an exact number, but based on taste and effect, it's comparable to a standard 8–12 ounce cup of hot coffee — enough for a morning boost without the shakes.

**Q: Is the honey flavor strong?**
A: Honestly, no. It's subtle. The Oregon honey rounds out the bitterness and adds a gentle sweetness, but this tastes more like smooth coffee with a hint of sweetness than a honey-forward drink.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely clean-label, low-acid cold brew that's ready when you are and easy on your stomach.

**Skip it if** you need a strong honey punch, prefer adjustable sweetness, or balk at roughly $2.50+ per can.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, well-executed cold brew that delivers on its core promise, held back only by its fixed sweetness and subtle flavor profile. For the convenience and clean ingredients, it earns its place in the rotation.
