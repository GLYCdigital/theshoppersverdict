## What You're Actually Getting

Each bottle delivers 30 servings of a 10mL liquid shot — essentially a small squeeze bottle that fits in your palm. The serving size is 5mg of liposomal creatine monohydrate, which sounds low until you understand the delivery mechanism. Liposomes are microscopic fat bubbles that protect the creatine molecule from degradation in the digestive tract, allowing far more of it to actually reach your muscles. Rho claims this means you need roughly half the creatine of standard powder to get the same effect.

The bottle itself is functional but nothing special — a standard amber plastic squeeze bottle with a measuring cap. As shown in the product image, the labeling is clean and clinical, which fits the brand's performance-focused positioning. No scoops, no mixing, no mess.
