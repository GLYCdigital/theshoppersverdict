The Technivorm Moccamaster 79112 KBT takes everything great about the Moccamaster line — SCA-certified brewing, handcrafted quality, exceptional coffee — and wraps it with a stainless steel thermal carafe that keeps your coffee hot for hours without a warming plate.

## What Makes It Great

The thermal carafe is the headline feature. Unlike glass carafe models that rely on a hot plate (which can scorch your coffee over time), the KBT's double-walled stainless steel carafe keeps coffee at the perfect temperature naturally. Reviewers consistently report their coffee stays "piping hot for hours" with zero burnt taste.

Performance reviews are unanimous: the Moccamaster brews exceptional coffee. Every single review sample mentioned superb taste. One long-time owner said it's "the best coffee I have ever had" and that they've "saved a lot of money" by skipping coffee shops.

Build quality is equally praised. The polished silver finish looks premium, and the all-metal construction feels substantial. As one reviewer put it: "the high quality is immediately apparent."

## Where It Falls Short

The thermal carafe has a design quirk — the base doesn't sit completely flat on a counter. The water reservoir also partially blocks access when mounted, making filling slightly awkward. These are small compromises for the performance, but they're worth noting.

At $349, this is firmly in investment territory. And as with all Moccamasters, there are no programmable features — this is a manual brewer at heart.

## The Bottom Line

The Moccamaster 79112 KBT is the perfect choice for anyone who wants SCA-certified brew quality with the convenience of a thermal carafe. It's not cheap, but it's built to last a lifetime — and the coffee it produces is simply outstanding.
