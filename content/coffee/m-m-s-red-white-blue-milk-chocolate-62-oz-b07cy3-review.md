# M&M's Red White & Blue Milk Chocolate (62 Oz) Review

Let's be honest: you're not buying these for culinary sophistication. You're buying a 62-ounce bag of America's favorite candy-coated chocolate for a cookout, a parade-watching party, or because you have a serious sweet tooth and a Costco-sized appetite. The question is whether this patriotic edition delivers enough value to justify the bulk commitment.

## What You Actually Get

The 62-ounce bag is genuinely massive — we're talking roughly 1,300 individual M&M's. As shown in the product image, it's a single stand-up pouch with red, white, and blue candies mixed throughout. The bag itself is sturdy enough for the weight, though it lacks any resealable mechanism, which is a real oversight at this size.

Inside, you get the exact same milk chocolate M&M's you know — the familiar thin candy shell, the creamy center, that satisfying crunch-then-melt texture. The patriotic colors are applied as a seasonal coating, but the chocolate itself is unchanged.

## Performance and Real-World Use

In terms of taste, these are indistinguishable from standard M&M's. That's either a pro or a con depending on your expectations. The milk chocolate is sweet, creamy, and reliably consistent. The color shells don't add any flavor — they're purely decorative.

The real issue emerges at summer events, which is presumably when most people buy these. The red and blue dyes can transfer to your fingers, especially in warm weather. Several reviewers noted this, with one writing, "The blue ones left streaks on my kids' hands within minutes of opening the bag." It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing if you're serving these at an outdoor party where hands get sweaty.

Freshness is another consideration. At this size, you're unlikely to finish the bag in one sitting. Without a resealable closure, the candies can absorb moisture and lose their snap within a week or two of opening. Plan ahead with a container or clip.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The value proposition is the standout. Multiple reviewers called out the per-ounce price as significantly better than buying multiple smaller bags. One verified purchase noted: "Bought this for our block party — way cheaper than the 12-ounce bags and lasted all afternoon."

The festive appearance also earns consistent praise. For anyone hosting a July 4th gathering, the red-white-blue mix eliminates the need for separate candy bowls or decorative sorting. Another reviewer mentioned: "These were perfect for the dessert table — didn't even need to add extra decorations."

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the dye-transfer issue and lack of a resealable zipper, the biggest complaint across reviews is the seasonal availability. This is a summer-only product. If you're reading this in October, you're either paying double from third-party sellers or waiting until next Memorial Day.

There's also the quality consistency question. A few reviewers reported receiving bags with melted or clumped candies during summer shipping. That's a risk with any chocolate product ordered in warm months — Amazon's warehouses aren't climate-controlled throughout transit.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're hosting a patriotic event with 10+ guests, you have a large family that will finish the bag within a couple weeks, or you're planning dessert for a Memorial Day or July 4th cookout.

**Skip it if:** You're buying for a small household, you live in a hot climate and worry about melting during shipping, or you're purchasing outside the summer season and would have to pay inflated reseller prices.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these the same chocolate as regular M&M's?**
Yes, identical formula. Only the candy shell colors change.

**Q: How long do they stay fresh after opening?**
About 2-3 weeks if stored in a cool, dry place. Without a resealable bag, transfer to an airtight container to extend freshness.

**Q: Can I use these for baking?**
Technically yes, but the red and blue colors can bleed into lighter batters. They work best in cookies where the colors are visible in the final product.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 3.7/5**

The M&M's Red White & Blue 62-ounce bag is a solid value for its intended purpose — feeding a crowd at a patriotic gathering without breaking the bank. The lack of a resealable closure and the dye-transfer issue keep it from being a perfect score, but for the price per ounce and the festive presentation, it delivers exactly what it promises. Buy it if you're planning a summer event and can finish the bag. Skip it if you're buying for one or two people or shopping off-season.
