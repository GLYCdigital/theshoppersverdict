## FAQ

**Q: How much xanthan gum do I use per cup of liquid?**
A: For thin sauces, start with 1/8 teaspoon per cup. For thicker gravies or doughs, use 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon. Always whisk in slowly to avoid clumping.

**Q: Can this be used in cold drinks like smoothies or cold brew?**
A: Yes. It hydrates in cold liquids effectively, but add it while blending or whisking vigorously. Sprinkling it on top of a still drink will create lumps.
