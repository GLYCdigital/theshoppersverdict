**What you actually get**

The bulk bag ships as a single, non-resealable pouch. As shown in the product image, it's a straightforward package — no frills, no fancy box. The bag holds a generous quantity of individually wrapped or loose pieces (depending on the listing variant), and the pieces themselves are the classic REESE'S PIECES shape: small, rounded, with that signature orange, yellow, and brown candy shell. The "crunchy shell" here isn't a marketing gimmick — it's a real, brittle sugar-based coating that gives a satisfying snap before you hit the peanut butter center.

**Performance and real-world use**

The peanut butter filling is the star. It's dense, salty-sweet, and has that unmistakable REESE'S flavor — more peanut-forward than the milk chocolate cups, which makes it a better choice if you want pure peanut butter without chocolate interference. The shell holds up well in transit; I didn't find crushed or broken pieces in my bag, which is a common issue with bulk candy. The crunch factor is consistent across pieces — not overly hard, not chalky.

One thing to note: the shell can leave a slight gritty residue on your tongue after a few pieces. It's not unpleasant, but if you're someone who chews slowly and savors, you'll notice it. Most buyers don't care — one Amazon reviewer put it simply: "Taste is great, fresh, and crunchy. Exactly what I expected." Another noted, "Perfect for movie nights and lunch boxes. No complaints."

**Where it falls short**

Honestly, the downsides are minor but worth knowing:

1. **The bag isn't resealable.** Once you open it, you need a clip or a separate container. If you're not going to finish the bag quickly, this is an annoyance.
2. **Texture variability.** A small percentage of pieces have a slightly softer shell — not stale, just less crunchy. It's random, not batch-wide, but it exists.
3. **No portion control.** This is a "bulk" bag by design, and if you have low self-control around peanut butter candy, you'll eat a quarter of the bag in one sitting. That's on you, not the product, but it's worth flagging.
