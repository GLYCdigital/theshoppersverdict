Here's where Kraft Real Mayo separates itself from store brands. **Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The 12 oz bottle is small for a household that uses mayo daily. If you're making big-batch salads or feeding a family of four, you'll be reordering every couple of weeks. The value per ounce is also weaker than buying a 30 oz jar — you're paying for convenience here.

Ingredient purists should note this contains soybean oil and added sugar. It's not the cleanest label on the shelf. If you're strict about avoiding seed oils or keeping added sugars out of your diet, this isn't your mayo. You'd want an olive oil-based or avocado oil-based option instead.
