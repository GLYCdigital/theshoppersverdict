**What You Actually Get**

The Prince Of Peace SUGAR_CANDY comes as a bag of individually wrapped amber crystal sugar chunks. These aren't the fine granulated sugar you're used to — they're larger, semi-transparent crystals that dissolve slowly in hot liquids. The packaging is simple and functional: a resealable pouch that keeps the candies dry and protected from humidity. Each piece is roughly the size of a small jellybean, and you'll get enough in a standard bag to sweeten 40-50 cups of coffee or tea, depending on how sweet you like it.

---

**Performance, Build Quality, Real-World Use**

Here's where this product shines. The rock sugar dissolves gradually in hot coffee — not instantly like granulated sugar — which means you get a more controlled sweetness. It's particularly effective in black coffee, where you can stir and watch the crystals break down. The flavor profile is cleaner than white sugar; there's a subtle, almost honey-like undertone that complements rather than masks the coffee's natural notes.

The slow dissolve also makes it excellent for iced coffee. Since it doesn't fully dissolve at cold temperatures, you can add it before pouring hot coffee over ice, and it'll sweeten the drink evenly as it chills. That's a genuine advantage over standard sugar.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the price per ounce is higher than regular sugar — you're paying for the convenience and the specific crystal form. Second, the slow dissolve can be a drawback if you're in a rush; you'll need to stir longer than with regular sugar. Third, some users reported that the pieces can be too large for smaller mugs — you might need to break them or use two for a standard cup, which throws off the portioning. Finally, if you're looking for a zero-calorie option, this isn't it. It's real sugar, with all the calories that entails.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this in cold drinks?**
A: It dissolves very slowly in cold liquid. Best approach: dissolve in a small amount of hot water or hot coffee first, then add to your cold drink.

**Q: Is this just regular sugar in a fancy form?**
A: It's cane sugar processed into larger crystals. It's not chemically different from regular sugar, but the form affects how it dissolves and how you perceive sweetness.

**Q: How long does a bag last?**
