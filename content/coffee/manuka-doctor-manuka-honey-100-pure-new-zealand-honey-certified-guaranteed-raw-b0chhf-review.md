**FAQ**

**Q: Is UMF 10+ strong enough for medicinal use?**  
A: It’s the minimum level considered therapeutic. For minor sore throats or general immunity support, it works. For serious wound care, you’d want UMF 15+ or higher — check with a healthcare provider.

**Q: Does it crystallize in the jar?**  
A: Raw honey will eventually crystallize, but this one stays creamy for months thanks to its low moisture content. If it does solidify, place the jar in warm water (not boiling) to restore it.

**Q: Can I use this in baking or cooking?**  
A: Yes, but the strong flavor will come through. It’s better suited to glazes for meats or drizzling over cheese than as a neutral sweetener in pastries.
