## Performance and Build Quality

Let's be honest about what we're evaluating: taste, texture, and freshness.

The milk chocolate shell is thin enough to crack cleanly when you bite, but substantial enough to hold its shape. Inside, the peanut butter filling is dense, slightly salty, and creamy — the contrast with the sweet chocolate is what makes these work. The ratio is roughly 60% peanut butter to 40% chocolate, which is the correct formula. REESE'S has perfected this over decades, and the consistency across batches is remarkable.

In the box I received, every cup was intact. No crushed edges, no melted chocolate, no bloom (that white discoloration chocolate gets when it's exposed to temperature swings). The cups peeled cleanly from their paper wrappers — something that can be an issue in warmer weather, but wasn't here.

## Where It Falls Short

If you have a peanut allergy, this is an absolute no-go. The product contains peanuts, milk, and soy, and the facility processes tree nuts. That's not a flaw — it's a hard stop.

The sugar content is also worth flagging. Two cups pack 21 grams of sugar. That's over half the American Heart Association's recommended daily limit. This is a treat, not a snack. Treat it as such.

The only real complaint I found among critical reviews was about wrapper sticking in warm conditions. If you store these somewhere above 75°F, the chocolate softens and the paper liner can adhere to the cup. Keep them in a cool, dry place and you'll avoid this entirely.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy these if:** You're someone who appreciates a classic American candy that hasn't been messed with. You want individually wrapped portions for lunchboxes, office drawers, or hiking packs. You value consistency — these taste exactly like the ones you had as a kid.

**Skip these if:** You're looking for a premium chocolate experience with complex cocoa notes or artisanal ingredients. This is Hershey's milk chocolate — sweet, straightforward, and not trying to be anything else. Also skip if you're managing your sugar intake or have peanut allergies.
