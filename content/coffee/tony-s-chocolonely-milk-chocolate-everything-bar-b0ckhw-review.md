Let's be honest about what this is: a 7-ounce slab of Belgian milk chocolate loaded with pretzels, salted caramel, and toffee that costs about what you'd pay for a six-pack of craft beer. The question isn't whether it's good chocolate — it's whether the hype, the price, and the packaging justify a permanent spot in your pantry. After eating through an entire bar in three sittings (I'm not proud of it), here's the full breakdown.

**What's Actually In The Wrapper**

The Everything Bar is exactly what the name promises. The chocolate itself is a 32% cocoa Belgian milk blend — noticeably richer than Hershey's or Cadbury, but not so dark that picky kids will complain. Mixed throughout are three distinct add-ins: crunchy salted pretzel pieces, chewy caramel bits, and buttery toffee chunks. The texture contrast is genuinely well-executed. The pretzels stay crisp, the caramel stays pliable, and the toffee doesn't disintegrate into a powdery mess. As shown in the product image, the bar divides into the brand's signature 12 uneven chunks — a design choice that references the unequal division of wealth in cocoa farming. It's a nice conversation starter during a dinner party, but it makes portion control frustrating if you're tracking calories.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

Tony's Chocolonely isn't a novelty brand coasting on a mission. The chocolate formula is legitimately good. It snaps cleanly at room temperature, melts smoothly on the tongue, and doesn't leave that waxy film you get from cheaper mass-market bars. The mix-ins are distributed generously across every section — I didn't find a single bite of plain chocolate. That's a meaningful detail because most "with pieces" bars skimp on the add-ins. This one doesn't.

One practical consideration: this bar is soft. At 72°F or warmer, it starts to lose its snap quickly. If you're shipping it or storing it in a warm kitchen, keep it in the fridge until ready to eat. Multiple Amazon reviewers noted this, with one saying, "Delicious but arrived slightly melted in summer — order during cooler months or pay for expedited shipping." That's fair warning.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, the consensus is strong. The most common praise centers on the mix-in ratio and the ethical sourcing. One reviewer wrote, "Finally a chocolate bar where the toppings aren't a tease — every bite has pretzel or caramel." Another highlighted the brand's transparency: "The wrapper tells you exactly which cooperatives the cocoa came from. That matters to me." Several reviewers also noted it's the rare candy that satisfies a full sweet tooth craving in a single serving — the bar is large enough that a quarter of it feels like a treat, not a tease.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's talk about the elephant in the room: the price. At typical retail of $5-7 per bar, you're paying a significant premium over a standard candy bar. You're paying for fair trade certification, transparent sourcing, and a brand mission — all legitimate things, but you should know what the premium covers.

The uneven chunk sizing is also genuinely annoying for baking or sharing. If you're breaking pieces off for kids or using it in s'mores, you'll need to cut it with a knife. And as mentioned, heat sensitivity is a real issue. This isn't a bar you can toss in a glovebox or backpack on a summer day.

**Who Should Buy This**

Buy it if you're a chocolate snob who cares about where your cocoa comes from and wants a genuinely loaded mix-in bar for snacking or gifting. It's also a great choice for a pantry staple during cold months when melting isn't a concern. Skip it if you're looking for a budget-friendly everyday candy, or if you need a bar with uniform pieces for precise baking.

**FAQ**

**Is the Everything Bar actually fair trade?**
Yes. Tony's Chocolonely is certified Fairtrade and sources cocoa through its own direct trade program in Ghana and Ivory Coast. The wrapper includes a QR code and detailed supply chain info.

**Does it contain gluten or nuts?**
The pretzels contain wheat (gluten). The bar may contain traces of nuts and soy. If you have celiac, this is not safe for you.

**How should I store it?**
Keep it in a cool, dry place below 70°F. In summer, refrigerate. Bring it to room temperature before eating for the best flavor and texture.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a genuinely delicious, ethically-sourced chocolate bar with a satisfying crunch in every single bite, and you're willing to pay for the quality and mission.

**Skip it if:** You're on a budget, need uniform pieces for baking, or live in a hot climate without reliable cool storage.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The premium price and awkward chunk sizing keep it from a perfect score, but the chocolate quality, generous mix-ins, and transparent sourcing make this one of the best candy bars available on Amazon today.
