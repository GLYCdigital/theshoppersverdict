## Flavor and Texture: The Real Test

Here's where Arcor earns its rating. The butterscotch flavor is genuinely rich — buttery, slightly caramelized, with a depth that most sugar-free candies can't touch. One reviewer put it plainly: "I've tried every sugar-free butterscotch on Amazon. This is the only one that actually tastes like butterscotch, not vanilla-flavored chalk."

The texture is where opinions split. Traditional butterscotch has a hard, brittle snap. Arcor's version is slightly softer — closer to a medium-hard candy. It dissolves slowly and evenly, which makes it ideal for coffee drinkers who want something to suck on while sipping. But if you're expecting that brittle crack of a Werther's Original, you'll notice the difference.

The sweetness level is also dialed back compared to sugar-sweetened butterscotch. It's noticeable but not unpleasant — actually a plus if you're pairing it with coffee, since it won't overwhelm your palate.

**Q: Will these cause digestive issues like other sugar-free candies?**
A: The maltitol/isomalt blend is gentler than sorbitol, and most reviewers report no problems. But sugar alcohols affect everyone differently — start with 2-3 pieces and see how you react. Don't eat half the bag in one sitting.

**Q: Are these actually sugar-free or just reduced sugar?**
A: Genuinely sugar-free. No sugar, no corn syrup. The sweetness comes from maltitol and isomalt, which are sugar alcohols that don't spike blood glucose the way sugar does.

**Q: How long do they stay fresh in the bag?**
A: The individual wrappers protect them well. Stored in a cool, dry place, the bag should last several months. The twist wrappers keep air out effectively, and there's no powder residue or sticking between pieces.
