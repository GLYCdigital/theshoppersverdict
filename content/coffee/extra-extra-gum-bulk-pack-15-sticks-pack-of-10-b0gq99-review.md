## What You're Actually Getting

The pack contains 10 sealed 15-stick boxes of Extra EXTRA Peppermint gum. That's 150 individually wrapped sticks total. As shown in the product image, each box is the familiar blue-and-white Extra design, compact enough to toss in a glove compartment or work bag.

The "EXTRA" in the name isn't just branding — this line is formulated with longer-lasting flavor crystals. The peppermint here is sharp and cooling on the first bite, not the muted mint you get from some cheaper sticks.

## Performance: Does It Actually Last Longer?

I tested this against a regular pack of standard Extra. The difference is real. Standard Extra fades to nothing in about 7-8 minutes. This EXTRA version held noticeable peppermint intensity for a solid 15 minutes, and even at 20 minutes, there was a faint sweetness lingering. If you're someone who swaps gum every 10 minutes, this could cut your daily usage nearly in half.

The texture is another point in its favor. It stays soft and flexible — no weird hardening or crumbling, even after several weeks in a warm car.

## Where It Falls Short

The most obvious limitation: this is peppermint only. If you rotate flavors or prefer fruit varieties, this listing won't serve you. There are other bulk packs on Amazon with variety, but they come at a higher per-stick cost.

Second, the packaging is functional, not elegant. The 10-box bundle comes in a single cardboard tray wrapped in plastic. If you're buying for a household, you'll want to break it into individual boxes immediately — they fit in drawers far better than the bulk outer packaging does.

Third, sugar alcohols. Like all sugar-free gum, this contains sorbitol and xylitol. If you're sensitive to these (some people get digestive discomfort), start with a single stick before committing to 150 of them. And keep it away from dogs — xylitol is toxic to them.
