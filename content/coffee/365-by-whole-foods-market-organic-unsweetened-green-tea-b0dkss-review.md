**The Shopper's Verdict: 365 by Whole Foods Market Organic Unsweetened Green Tea**

You're staring down a wall of green tea options on Amazon, and most of them are either overpriced, loaded with hidden sweeteners, or taste like dust. The 365 by Whole Foods Market Organic Unsweetened Green Tea cuts through that noise. It's not flashy, but 215 Amazon shoppers have pushed it to 4.3 stars for a reason: it's a dependable, clean, everyday green tea that doesn't punish your wallet.

**What You Actually Get**

This is a straightforward box of tea bags — no individual foil wrappers, no fancy pyramid sachets. The box contains your standard string-and-tag bags, and each one holds a finely cut organic green tea leaf. The ingredient list is exactly one item: organic green tea. That's it. No "natural flavors," no citric acid, no sweetener hiding behind an "unsweetened" label. As shown in the product image, the packaging is the familiar minimalist 365 aesthetic — clean, honest, and functional.

**Brewing Performance and Taste**

Here's where this tea earns its keep. Brewed properly — 175°F water, not boiling, and a 2-3 minute steep — you get a pale golden cup with a gentle, slightly vegetal flavor. It's smooth and almost sweet on its own, with none of the astringent bite that plagues cheap green teas. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"Tastes like actual green tea, not grass clippings. I've repurchased three times."*

The downside? If you're coming from a bold Japanese sencha or a smoky gunpowder green, this will taste thin. It's a middle-of-the-road Chinese-style green tea — pleasant, but not complex. And if you forget it and let it steep for five minutes, it *will* turn bitter. That's on you, not the tea.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the packaging. The tea bags are unbleached, which is great for the environment, but they're on the delicate side. Squeeze the bag against your spoon to get the last drop out, and you risk a tear. Also, without individual wrappers, the remaining bags lose freshness faster once you crack the box open. If you drink tea slowly, transfer the bags to an airtight container.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for the daily green tea drinker who wants a reliable, organic cup without paying boutique prices. It's also a smart choice if you're using green tea for its antioxidants and want zero additives getting in the way. If you're someone who wants a flavored blend — jasmine, lemon, or peach — look elsewhere. And if you're a tea snob chasing a specific terroir, this isn't it. But for iced tea pitchers, office desks, or a morning ritual that doesn't require ceremony, this box delivers.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

*Is this tea actually unsweetened?*
Yes. The only ingredient is organic green tea leaves. There's zero sugar, zero stevia, zero artificial sweetener. The mild natural sweetness comes from the leaf itself.

*How should I brew it to avoid bitterness?*
Use water around 175°F — not rolling boil. Steep for 2-3 minutes. If you like it stronger, add more tea rather than steeping longer.

*Is this good for making iced tea?*
Absolutely. Double up the bags, steep in hot water for 3 minutes, then pour over ice. It won't cloud up or turn bitter like some black teas.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a clean, organic, everyday green tea that doesn't taste like a chemistry experiment, and you value getting a quality product at a fair price.

**Skip it if:** You need individually wrapped bags for travel, or you demand a bold, distinctive green tea character.
