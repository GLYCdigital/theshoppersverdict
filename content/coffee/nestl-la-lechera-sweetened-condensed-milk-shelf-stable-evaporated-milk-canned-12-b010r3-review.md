**FAQ**  
*Can I use this in place of evaporated milk?*  
No — they’re different. Sweetened condensed milk has added sugar and is much thicker. Evaporated milk is unsweetened and thinner. Substituting will drastically change the sweetness and texture.

*Does it need refrigeration after opening?*  
Yes. Once opened, store it in an airtight container in the fridge. It will keep for about two weeks. Unopened cans are shelf-stable for months.

*Can I use it in an espresso machine or pour-over?*  
You can add it to brewed coffee, but don’t put it in a machine’s water tank — it’s too thick and will clog. Stir it into the cup after brewing.
