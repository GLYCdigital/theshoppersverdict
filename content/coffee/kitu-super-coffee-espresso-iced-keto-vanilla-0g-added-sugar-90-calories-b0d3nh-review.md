**The 90-Calorie Espresso That Doesn't Taste Like a Diet Product**

Most "keto" coffee drinks taste like someone whispered "coffee" into a vat of artificial sweetener. KITU Super Coffee's Iced Keto Vanilla isn't that. It's a legitimately drinkable cold coffee that happens to fit a macro-conscious lifestyle — and at 4.0 stars across 200 ratings, most buyers agree it's doing something right. But is it worth your money versus a standard cold brew? Let's break down what you're actually getting.

**What's In the Bottle**

Each 14-ounce bottle delivers 200mg of caffeine (roughly two espresso shots), 90 calories, and 0g added sugar. The sweetening comes from monk fruit and stevia, with 2g of sugar alcohols from the MCT oil base. The ingredients list is short: brewed coffee, MCT oil, milk protein concentrate, natural flavors, and those sweeteners. No carrageenan, no gums, no weird stabilizers. That's genuinely cleaner than most canned coffee options.

**Real-World Performance**

I'll cut to the chase: this tastes like actual coffee, not a protein shake pretending to be one. The espresso base is forward and slightly bitter in a good way — think cold brew, not gas station cappuccino. The vanilla is there but restrained; it rounds out the edges rather than smacking you with artificial birthday-cake flavor. If you're used to Starbucks' vanilla sweet cream cold brew, this will taste almost savory by comparison.

The MCT oil does its job — you get a subtle creaminess without dairy heaviness — but it means the texture runs thinner than a latte. And yes, you'll want to shake it hard. Buyers consistently note the separation issue. One reviewer put it plainly: "Great taste but you HAVE to shake it or the last sip is a mouthful of oil."

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

The consistency of feedback is telling. Over and over, reviewers mention three things: the clean energy (no jitters), the believable coffee flavor, and the portion control. One verified buyer wrote, "Finally a keto coffee that doesn't taste like cardboard. The 14oz bottle is perfect for my afternoon slump." Another noted, "I've tried every brand — this is the only one where I can't taste the stevia." That last point matters more than most people realize; artificial sweeteners are the #1 reason canned keto coffees fail.

**Where It Falls Short**

The vanilla is genuinely subtle. If you're expecting a sweet, dessert-like iced coffee, this will disappoint. It's closer to a lightly flavored cold brew than a vanilla latte. The price per bottle also runs higher than making your own cold brew at home — you're paying for convenience. And the MCT oil separation is real; it's not a dealbreaker, but it's annoying if you're drinking on the go without a way to shake thoroughly.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Keto/low-carb dieters** who miss the ritual of a real coffee drink and want something that doesn't blow their macros
- **Intermittent fasters** needing a low-calorie caffeine hit that won't spike insulin (the 90 calories technically breaks a strict fast, but it's minimal)
- **Busy professionals** who want better-than-gas-station coffee without brewing a thing

**Skip it if** you want a sweet vanilla iced coffee, you're sensitive to MCT oil (digestive upset is possible), or you're fine drinking black coffee and want to save money.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this actually taste like coffee or like a protein shake?**
A: Coffee, genuinely. The espresso base is the dominant flavor. The vanilla and MCT oil are supporting players, not the main event.

**Q: How does the caffeine hit compare to a standard iced coffee?**
A: 200mg is roughly equivalent to a venti cold brew from Starbucks or two standard espresso shots. Expect a smooth, sustained energy lift — no crash-and-burn.

**Q: Is this OK for non-keto people?**
A: Absolutely. It's just a low-sugar iced coffee with extra fat. If you don't mind the MCT oil texture, it works as a regular cold brew alternative.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely good-tasting, low-sugar iced coffee that fits a keto lifestyle and you're fine paying a premium for convenience. **Skip it if** you expect a sweet vanilla latte or you're sticker-shocked by the per-bottle cost.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — It's not perfect (the separation issue and subtle flavor are real), but it's one of the few keto coffee products that actually delivers on its promises. If you're in the market, this is a solid choice — just remember to shake it like you mean it.
