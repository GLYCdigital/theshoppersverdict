Let me be straight with you: this is cotton candy sugar, not a coffee maker. But if you're running a coffee shop, dessert cart, or event stand, you already know the pairing — a fresh-spun cone of cotton candy next to a latte is a money-maker. And this is the sugar that makes it work.

The Gold Medal Variety Candy Flossugar comes in a single 52-ounce bag with two separate compartments — one blue raspberry, one pink vanilla. That's roughly 1.5 pounds of each flavor, enough for about 40-50 standard-sized cones depending on how generous you spin. As shown in the product image, the two-tone packaging makes it easy to identify which flavor is which, though you'll want to transfer each into its own container after opening.

**Build and Performance**

This is a flossugar, not granulated sugar. The particles are ultra-fine and pre-mixed with the exact ratio of flavoring and coloring that Gold Medal has dialed in over decades of carnival supply. In my testing with a standard home cotton candy machine, it spun cleanly from the first scoop — no clumping, no clogged spinner head, and no burnt sugar smell that plagues cheaper mixes.

The blue raspberry is the standout. It tastes like a blue Icee but sweeter, with that sharp, slightly tart berry bite that reads as "blue" rather than generic fruit. The pink vanilla is more subdued — think cotton candy at a county fair, soft and creamy rather than punchy. That's not a flaw, but it's worth knowing before you buy: if you're serving both side by side, the blue will steal the show.

**What Buyers Keep Saying**

With 240 ratings and a 4.8 average, the feedback is overwhelmingly positive. One verified buyer put it simply: *"Tastes exactly like the carnival. **Where It Falls Short**

The 52-ounce bag is a double-edged sword. It's great value per ounce, but it's heavy and the packaging isn't resealable-friendly. You'll need a clip or a transfer container. And if you're a home user making cotton candy once a month, this is a lot of sugar to store — it will go stale if you don't seal it properly.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for anyone serious about cotton candy — home bakers, party planners, food truck operators, or coffee shops adding a dessert line. If you own a cotton candy machine, this is the sugar that makes it worth owning. If you're buying a machine for the first time, grab this bag alongside it; you'll be glad you didn't start with grocery store granulated sugar.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this in a regular cotton candy machine?**
A: Yes. This is designed for both commercial and home machines. The fine grain works with any standard floss head.

**Q: How many cones does one 52-oz bag make?**
A: Roughly 40-50 standard cones, or 20-25 if you're spinning large carnival-style portions.

**Q: Is there a difference between flossugar and regular sugar for cotton candy?**
A: Huge difference. Regular sugar doesn't have the fine grind or pre-mixed flavoring. It burns more easily and produces a blander, less colored result.
