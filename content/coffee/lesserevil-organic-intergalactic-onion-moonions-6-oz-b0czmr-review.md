**The Short Version: Is This Worth Your Snack Budget?**

Lesserevil's Intergalactic Onion Moonions are a weird little product that somehow works. They're organic corn puffs dusted with a serious onion seasoning that tastes like someone actually cared about the flavor profile — not the faint "hint of onion" you get from most mass-market snacks. At 4.4 stars across 220 Amazon ratings, it's clearly finding its audience. But it's not for everyone, and I'll tell you exactly who should buy it.

**What You Actually Get**

The 6 oz bag arrives looking like a prop from a low-budget sci-fi film — matte black with a cartoon moon wearing sunglasses. Inside: roughly four cups of crunchy, irregularly-shaped corn puffs. The texture is the first surprise. These aren't airy, melt-in-your-mouth cheese-puff clones. They're dense, with a satisfying snap that holds up to dipping (try them with hummus — it works). The onion coating is visible as a fine, pale powder clinging to every piece. It's not subtle.

**The Flavor Reality Check**

The onion flavor is the whole show here. It's tangy, slightly sweet, and unmistakably onion-forward — think French onion dip concentrate, not raw onion bite. The seasoning blend uses organic onion powder, cane sugar, and a touch of sea salt, and it nails a savory-sweet balance that keeps you reaching for another handful. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"These taste like Funyuns but cleaner and with better crunch. I killed the bag in one movie."* Another noted: *"If you hate onion, run. If you love it, buy three bags."*

That's the honest split. This is a polarizing flavor.

**Where It Falls Short**

The 6 oz size is the biggest practical complaint. At this price point, you're paying for a premium snack, but the bag feels light for the cost. Multiple reviews mention finishing it in one sitting — not because it's addictive (though it is), but because the portion is genuinely modest. Also, the oil-based seasoning can cause the puffs to stick together in clumps if the bag sits in a warm delivery truck. A quick shake fixes it, but it's a minor annoyance right out of the box.

**Who Should Buy These**

- **The "I want a clean-ish snack" person** who's tired of ingredient lists that read like a chemistry exam. These are organic, non-GMO, and use sunflower oil instead of palm oil.
- **The onion enthusiast** who thinks most savory snacks are too timid. This delivers.
- **The texture snob** who wants crunch that survives shipping without turning to dust.

**Skip These If:**

- You prefer subtle flavors. This is not that.
- You need a budget-friendly bulk snack. Per-ounce, this is pricier than conventional options.
- You're expecting a cheese puff. It's not. It's its own thing.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Are these actually vegan?**
Yes — the ingredients list is just organic corn, sunflower oil, onion powder, cane sugar, sea salt, and spices. No dairy, no animal products.

**Q: How do they compare to Funyuns or other onion snacks?**
They're crunchier and less salty than Funyuns, with a cleaner finish. More flavor depth, less artificial tang.

**Q: Do they go stale quickly once opened?**
The bag has a resealable zipper, but the dense texture means they stay crunchy for 3-4 days. After that, they soften slightly but still taste fine.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely flavorful, organic snack that doesn't taste like cardboard pretending to be food. **Skip it if** you're on a budget or you need something your whole family will agree on — this is a love-it-or-hate-it flavor profile.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Deducting half a star for the skimpy portion size and another third for the clumping issue. The flavor and ingredient quality are legitimately excellent. It's a premium snack that knows exactly what it is, and it delivers on that promise.
