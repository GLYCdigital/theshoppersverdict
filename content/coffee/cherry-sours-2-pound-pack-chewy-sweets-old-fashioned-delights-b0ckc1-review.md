Let's be honest about what this is: a two-pound bag of chewy cherry candies that tastes like the stuff your grandpa kept in a dish by the recliner. If that's what you're after, this delivers. If you're expecting modern, glossy, perfectly-uniform gummies, you'll be disappointed — and that's okay.

**What's in the bag**

You get a single 32-ounce (907g) clear bag of individually-wrapped cherry sours. No box, no fancy packaging, no frills. The candies are roughly nickel-sized, slightly flattened ovals with a matte, sugar-dusted exterior. As shown in the product image, they're a deep red that darkens toward burgundy — the color of real cherry, not the neon pink of artificially-flavored imposters. The bag lists roughly 40-45 pieces per pound, so you're looking at 80-90 candies total.

**Flavor and texture — the real test**

The first bite gives you a legitimate sour punch — citric acid sharpness that makes your cheeks tighten. That fades within five seconds into a sweet, slightly tangy cherry flavor that actually tastes like cherries, not cough syrup. This is where the 4.1-star rating makes sense. The flavor profile is old-school in the best way: simple, direct, and satisfying.

Texture-wise, these are chewy but not tough. They're firmer than a gummy bear but softer than a hard candy. You can bite through them cleanly, and they soften nicely in your mouth without getting gummy-stuck to your teeth. One reviewer noted, "Taste just like the ones from the corner store when I was a kid. Soft, sour, and the cherry flavor is spot-on." Another added, "Great value for the price. The bag is huge and the candies stay fresh for weeks."

**Where it falls short**

The sour coating is inconsistent. Some pieces come heavily dusted with the tart mix; others are nearly bare, giving you a mostly-sweet candy. If you're buying these specifically for the sour hit, that variance will frustrate you. One buyer put it plainly: "Half the bag is really sour, the other half is just sweet cherry chews. Still good, but I wish the sour was uniform."

The packaging is also a miss. The bag has no zipper seal — once opened, you'll need a clip or a separate container. In humid climates, that means the candies can absorb moisture and get sticky if you don't store them right. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

**Who should buy this**

- **Bulk candy buyers**: If you're filling a candy dish, stocking a break room, or prepping party favors, this two-pound bag is cost-effective and the individually-wrapped pieces stay fresh longer than loose candy.
- **Old-fashioned candy nostalgists**: The flavor genuinely matches classic cherry sours from decades past.
- **Chewy candy fans who like sour varieties**: If you alternate between sweet and sour flavors, this gives you both in one bag.

**Skip it if** you want uniform sour intensity, need resealable packaging, or prefer modern gummy textures.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long do these stay fresh?**
A: In a cool, dry place with the bag sealed (use a clip), they'll stay chewy for 4-6 weeks. Past that, they start to harden and lose the sour kick.

**Q: Are these gluten-free?**

**Q: How many pieces are in the 2-pound bag?**

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want an authentic, old-fashioned cherry chew at a solid per-ounce price and don't mind inconsistent sour coating. **Skip it if** you demand uniform tartness or need resealable packaging.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The flavor and value are genuinely good, but the sour inconsistency and packaging issue keep it from being a home run. For the price per pound, it's a solid pantry staple for candy lovers.
