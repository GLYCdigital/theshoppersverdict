**The 5-Pound Commitment: Is This Watermelon Candy Worth the Real Estate?**

Here's the thing about buying candy in bulk: you're either a person who will demolish 5 pounds of sour-sugar goodness in a month, or you're a person who will regret the purchase by week two. The SOUR PATCH KIDS Watermelon 5-lb bag forces you to pick a side. With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, most buyers are firmly in the former camp — but let's dig into what you're actually getting before you commit your pantry shelf.

**What's in the Bag**

This is not the standard red-and-blue SOUR PATCH KIDS mix. These are watermelon-shaped wedges — green rind on the outside, pink center — each piece individually wrapped in clear plastic. That's a significant detail. The bag contains roughly 500 pieces, which sounds impressive until you realize that's about 14 pieces per pound. For context, a standard 4-oz theater box has about 15 pieces. So you're getting the equivalent of about 33 theater boxes for the bulk price.

Each wedge is coated in the classic sour sugar blend — that initial tart punch that makes your cheeks tighten, followed by a sweet watermelon flavor that lingers. The texture is softer than the original Sour Patch Kids; these are more yielding, closer to a firm gummy than the chewy-taffy consistency of the originals. If you've had the Sour Patch Kids Watermelon singles, these are identical — just in bulk form.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The candy itself is consistent. Every piece I sampled had the same sour-to-sweet ratio, the same chew. No stale pieces, no melted clumps (assuming your bag hasn't been sitting in a hot warehouse — check the expiration date on arrival). The individually wrapped format is a double-edged sword: it's great for portion control and sharing, but it generates a mountain of plastic wrappers that you'll be recycling for weeks.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The Amazon reviews paint a clear picture. One verified buyer writes, "These are dangerously good. I bought them for a classroom party and ended up hiding a handful for myself." Another notes, "The sour coating is actually sour — not like some brands that just call themselves sour and deliver sweet." The individual wrapping gets frequent mention: "Perfect for my office candy dish. Everyone thinks I'm fancy because the pieces are wrapped."

The watermelon flavor specifically earns repeated praise. Unlike artificial watermelon candies that taste like melon-scented soap, this one has a genuine juiciness to it. One reviewer sums it up: "Tastes like summer. My kids fight over the last pieces."

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be direct about the downsides.

First, the bag has no resealable closure. It's a simple open-top bag with a heat-sealed edge. Once you cut it open, you need a chip clip or a transfer to an airtight container. In humid environments, the surface sugar will absorb moisture and the pieces will stick together. This is a genuine oversight for a product marketed for bulk storage.

Second, if you're a purist who loves the classic Sour Patch Kids texture — that dense, almost rubbery chew — these watermelon wedges are different. They're softer, more forgiving. Some buyers specifically note this as a disappointment: "I expected the same chew as the original. These are more like gummy worms." It's not a flaw, but it's a distinction worth knowing.

Third, the 5-pound commitment is real. Even with a 12-month shelf life (check your bag — it varies), you're looking at a lot of candy. If you're buying for a single household, consider whether you'll actually finish it before the texture degrades.

A: Typically 9–12 months from production, but once opened, transfer to an airtight container. In a sealed container at room temperature, the texture holds for 4–6 weeks. In the original opened bag, expect clumping within 2–3 weeks in humid conditions.

**Q: Is this the same recipe as the regular SOUR PATCH KIDS Watermelon?**
A: Yes, same candy, same ingredients. The only difference is the bulk packaging and the individually wrapped format. If you've had the single-serve watermelon, you know exactly what to expect.

**Q: Are these gluten-free and kosher?**
A: Yes, SOUR PATCH KIDS Watermelon are gluten-free and certified kosher (OU). They contain no artificial colors — the pink and green come from natural sources. They do contain corn syrup and sugar, so this is not a low-sugar option by any stretch.

**The Verdict**
