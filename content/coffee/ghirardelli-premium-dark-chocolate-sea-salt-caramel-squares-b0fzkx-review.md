Let’s get one thing straight: this isn’t a coffee maker, and it won’t brew you a single cup. But if you’re looking at the GHIRARDELLI Premium Dark Chocolate Sea Salt Caramel Squares, you already know that. This is the finishing move for your coffee ritual — the square you reach for when your espresso is poured and you need something to cut through the bitterness. And honestly? It does that job exceptionally well.

## What You Actually Get

A 5.2-ounce bag of individually wrapped squares. You’ll get roughly 12-13 pieces depending on the batch. Each square is a layered construction: 60% cacao dark chocolate on the outside, a soft caramel core, and visible sea salt crystals pressed into the top. As shown in the product image, the squares are thick — about a third of an inch — which gives you that satisfying snap when you bite through.

## Performance and Build Quality

The chocolate-to-caramel ratio is the star here. The 60% cacao shell is dark enough to taste grown-up but not so bitter it punishes you. It melts at body temperature, which means it lingers on your tongue exactly as long as you want it to. The caramel inside is genuinely soft — no microwaving, no stale, chewy-taffy texture. It pulls apart cleanly when you break a square in half.

The sea salt is where GHIRARDELLI earns its keep. These aren’t micro-fine flakes that vanish on contact. They’re visible crystals that crunch between your teeth and hit your palate about two seconds after the chocolate starts melting — a timing trick that makes the caramel taste saltier and the chocolate taste sweeter. It’s a well-engineered bite.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews holds up when you read the actual feedback. One recurring theme: “These are dangerously good — I can’t keep them in the house.” Another buyer noted, “The sea salt actually tastes like salt, not like a gimmick.” Multiple reviewers mention using them as an after-dinner treat alongside coffee or bourbon, which tracks with the flavor profile.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest complaints surface repeatedly. First, packaging. The squares rattle around loose in the bag, and by the time your order arrives, you’ll likely find a few shattered pieces. The taste isn’t affected, but if you’re buying these as gifts, plan on some breakage. Second, the dark chocolate is assertive. If you’re coming from GHIRARDELLI’s milk chocolate lineup, the 60% cacao will surprise you — it’s closer to a bittersweet bar than a sweet treat.

## Who Should Buy These

Three types of shoppers will love this bag:

- **The espresso drinker** who wants a small, intense pairing — one square with a double shot is a perfect ratio.
- **The gift-giver** who needs a pantry-stable present that looks premium without being fragile (though see the breakage note above).
- **The dark chocolate convert** who finds milk chocolate too cloying and wants something with actual cocoa depth.

Skip these if you prefer milk chocolate, hate salty-sweet combinations, or need an individually boxed presentation for gifting.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these gluten-free?**
A: Yes, GHIRARDELLI lists these as gluten-free, though they’re produced in a facility that also processes wheat. If you have celiac disease, check the current packaging for allergen statements.

**Q: How should I store them?**
A: Keep them in a cool, dry place below 70°F. Don’t refrigerate — cold temperature dulls the caramel and makes the chocolate less aromatic.

**Q: How many squares are in a bag?**
A: The 5.2-ounce bag yields roughly 12-13 individually wrapped squares, though piece count varies slightly by batch weight.
