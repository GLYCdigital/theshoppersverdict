Let's be clear about one thing upfront: these are not your standard salted cocktail cashews. The Wickedly Prime Roasted Cashews in Coconut Toffee are a dessert masquerading as a snack nut, and that's exactly why they've racked up a 4.7-star average across 235 Amazon ratings. If you're expecting the savory crunch of a typical roasted cashew, recalibrate. If you want something that tastes like a candy shop collided with a nut roaster, you're in the right place.

**What's Actually in the Bag**

You're getting a 20-ounce pouch — that's a solid pound and a quarter of nuts. The bag itself is a heavy-duty resealable zip-top, which matters more than you'd think. Cashews go stale fast once exposed to air, and the seal genuinely holds. As one reviewer put it, "I've had this bag open for three weeks and the nuts are still as crunchy as day one." That's not typical for flavored nuts, which tend to soften as the coating absorbs moisture.

The nuts themselves are dry-roasted then coated in a coconut toffee glaze. You can see the toasted coconut flakes clinging to each piece, and the coating has that classic toffee snap — it cracks when you bite down before giving way to the buttery cashew interior.

**How They Actually Perform**

Here's where these shine: the flavor balance. Most sweet-and-nutty products overdo the sugar and bury the nut. Wickedly Prime got the ratio right. The toffee is present but not aggressive, and the coconut adds texture rather than just flavor. You get a nut-first experience with a sweet finish, not a sugar bomb with cashew dust.

The roasting is even — no scorched pieces, no raw centers. The cashews are the plump, whole halves you'd expect from a premium brand, not the broken bits you find in bargain bins. That said, a few reviewers noted their bags contained more broken pieces than they'd like, particularly toward the bottom. It's a minor gripe, but if you're buying these for gifting or presentation, know that the last handful might be crumbs.

**Where They Fall Short**

The sweetness is the double-edged sword. These are dangerously easy to overeat. A serving is about 12-15 nuts, and honestly, who's stopping there? If you have any tendency to snack mindlessly, these will disappear faster than you expect. They're also not a great fit for savory snack boards — the coconut toffee flavor will clash with cheese and charcuterie.

One more thing: the price fluctuates on Amazon, and these aren't cheap when not on sale. Watch for Subscribe & Save discounts or bulk deals. At full price, you're paying a premium for the flavor, and it's worth it only if you genuinely want the sweet profile.

**Who Should Buy These**

- **The dessert-snacker**: If your ideal snack falls somewhere between candy and nuts, this is your bag.
- **The charcuterie rebel**: These actually work surprisingly well with sharp cheddar or blue cheese — the sweet-savory contrast is legit.
- **The gift-giver**: The bag looks premium and the flavor is unusual enough to feel thoughtful.

**Skip them if** you want a versatile, everyday nut for snacking or cooking. For that, grab plain roasted cashews. This is a specialty product, not a pantry staple.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these gluten-free?**
A: Yes, the coconut toffee coating contains no wheat-based ingredients. That said, if you have severe gluten sensitivity, always check the current packaging for facility cross-contamination warnings.

**Q: How long do they stay fresh after opening?**
A: With the zip seal closed, they'll stay crunchy for 3-4 weeks. Beyond that, the toffee coating can start to soften slightly. If they do soften, a few minutes in a low oven (250°F) will crisp them back up.

**Q: Are the cashews actually from the Amazon brand?**
A: Yes, Wickedly Prime is Amazon's own snack line. That's not a mark against quality here — this line is specifically positioned as their premium snack offering, and the quality reflects it.
