Let's be honest: most strawberry jams are basically sugar delivery systems with a vague strawberry afterthought. That's not what CROFTERS is doing here, and it's exactly why this jar has earned a 4.7-star average across 235 Amazon ratings.

## What's Actually In the Jar

Flip this jar over and you'll see something rare in the jam aisle: a three-item ingredient list. Organic strawberries, organic cane sugar, and fruit pectin. That's it. No high-fructose corn syrup, no artificial preservatives, no "natural flavors" (which are rarely natural), no food coloring. The deep red color in the product image is the strawberries themselves, not Red 40.

The 10-ounce jar is smaller than the standard 18-ounce Smucker's you're used to, but that's deliberate — this is a concentrated product, not a sugar-water spread.

## Taste and Texture: The Real Story

Here's where CROFTERS separates itself from the pack. The sugar content sits at about 40% less than conventional jams, which means you actually taste strawberries. Real ones. The flavor profile is bright and slightly tart on the front, then sweetens on the back end — that's the mark of fruit doing the heavy lifting rather than sugar.

Texture-wise, this spread has a clean, thick consistency. It doesn't run off your toast, but it's also not so stiff that you tear through bread trying to spread it. It holds its shape on a PB&J, which parents will appreciate. As one verified buyer put it: "Finally a strawberry spread that doesn't taste like candy. My kids actually prefer this to the sugary stuff."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the reviews, three themes keep coming up:

- **The ingredient list.** Shoppers who read labels — and aren't afraid to put a jar back on the shelf — consistently call this out. "I feel good giving this to my toddler. It's just fruit and a little sugar," one reviewer noted.
- **The fruit-forward taste.** Multiple reviewers mentioned that it tastes like "real strawberries" rather than jam-flavored sugar.
- **Versatility.** Beyond toast, buyers use it in yogurt, on pancakes, in thumbprint cookies, and even as a glaze for pork or chicken.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be straight about the tradeoffs.

First, the price. This runs roughly $2-3 more per jar than conventional strawberry jam. If you're feeding a family of four, that difference adds up, especially since the 10-ounce size goes faster than you'd expect. A few reviewers mentioned this exact issue — one said, "Great taste, but I'm buying two jars a week with three kids."

Second, the texture can change after opening if you're not careful. Some reviewers reported separation or slight crystallization after several weeks in the fridge. It's not a product flaw — it's what happens when you don't load a product with stabilizers — but it's worth knowing.

## Who Should Buy This

## FAQ

**Is this actually organic?**
Yes, it's USDA-certified organic. The strawberries and cane sugar are both organically grown, and the product carries the certification on the label.

**Does it need to be refrigerated after opening?**
Yes, like any natural fruit spread without preservatives, refrigerate after opening and use within a few weeks for best quality.

**Is this suitable for diabetics or low-sugar diets?**
It's lower sugar than conventional jam (about 40% less), but it's not sugar-free. Each serving still contains around 8 grams of sugar from the fruit and cane sugar. If you need sugar-free, look elsewhere.
