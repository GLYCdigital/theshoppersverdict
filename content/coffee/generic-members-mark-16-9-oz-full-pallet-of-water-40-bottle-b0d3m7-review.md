## Real-World Performance and Build Quality

The bottles themselves are noticeably thicker than store-brand equivalents. I've handled enough bulk water to know the difference: cheap bottles buckle under weight, these hold their shape. The caps are the weak point — they're molded with a tight seal, and several Amazon reviewers specifically mention needing two hands or a grippy towel to open them. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

Taste is clean and neutral. No metallic notes, no plastic aftertaste, even after the pallet sat in a warm garage for two months. That's the benchmark for purified water, and it passes.

The real constraint is logistics. A full pallet weighs roughly 900–1,000 pounds. It won't fit through a standard residential doorway. You need either a ground-level entrance with wide access, a forklift, or a plan to break the pallet down on the driveway and shuttle cases inside. Multiple reviewers mention this in different ways — one wrote, "I didn't think about the weight. We ended up carrying 40 cases one by one into the house. Took an hour."
