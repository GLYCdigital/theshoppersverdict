**Fresh Roasted Coffee's fractional packs solve a real problem: stale coffee.** Most ground coffee sits in warehouses for months before it hits your shelf. These individual foil packs arrive with a roast date printed right on the box — typically within days of leaving the roaster. That's a meaningful difference you can taste.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains 10 individually sealed fractional packs — each holding enough ground coffee for one standard pot of drip brew (roughly 2.5 tablespoons). The packaging is the star here. Each pouch is nitrogen-flushed and airtight, which means the coffee inside stays at peak freshness until you tear it open. As shown in the product image, the packs are compact, stackable, and easy to toss in a drawer, camper, or office desk.

This is a K-Cup alternative for people who refuse to drink from plastic pods but still want portion control. You get real coffee — not a plastic cup of heated dust.

## Performance and Build Quality

I brewed these in a standard Mr. Coffee drip machine and a pour-over. The grind is consistent, medium-fine, which works well for most automatic drip brewers. The roast profile leans toward the darker side of medium — expect notes of chocolate and a slight smokiness without the burnt bitterness that cheap dark roasts deliver.

The coffee holds up well in a thermal carafe for about an hour before turning bitter. That's normal for any drip coffee, but worth noting if you sip slowly.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The freshness factor dominates the reviews. One buyer wrote: *"I forgot what real coffee tasted like until I tried these."* Another noted: *"Finally, a company that prints the roast date instead of a 'best by' date that's already passed."* Multiple reviewers mention using these for travel and camping — the sealed packs survive luggage abuse and keep coffee drinkable in a motel room, which is a niche but genuinely useful benefit.

## Where It Falls Short

**The grind size is fixed.** If your drip machine runs fast or slow, you can't adjust. Some buyers reported their machines brewed a bit weak because the grind was slightly too coarse for their specific basket. You can't fix that with these packs.

**The per-cup cost is higher** than buying a standard 12-ounce bag. You're paying for the convenience and the packaging. If you drink two pots a day, this gets expensive fast.

**Packaging waste** — each pack is individually wrapped, and while the foil is recyclable in some programs, most municipal recycling won't take it. That's a real environmental tradeoff.

## Who Should Buy This

## FAQ

**Q: How much coffee is in each pack?**
A: Each pack contains roughly 2.5 tablespoons — enough for a standard 6-8 cup drip pot. If you like strong coffee, you'll want to use one full pack per 4 cups.

**Q: Is this actually fresh, or is that marketing?**
A: The roast date is printed on the box. Buyers consistently report receiving coffee roasted within days of shipping. That's unusually fresh for Amazon coffee.

**Q: Can I use these for cold brew?**
A: Technically yes, but the grind is designed for drip. Cold brew prefers a coarser grind. One pack makes about 12 ounces of concentrate — you'd need several packs for a full batch.
