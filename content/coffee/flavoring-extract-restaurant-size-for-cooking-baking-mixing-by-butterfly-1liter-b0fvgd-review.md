**FAQ**

**How much should I use in coffee?**
Start with 1–2 drops for an 8-ounce cup. Adjust from there. It's concentrated, so err on the low side — you can always add more.

**Does it contain real alcohol?**
Yes, most extracts use alcohol as the carrier. It evaporates in baking but adds a slight bite if you use too much in cold drinks. Let cold coffee sit 30 seconds after adding before stirring.

**Can I use it in a coffee machine reservoir?**
No. Never put extract in the water tank — it will damage internal components. Add it to the brewed cup or the ground coffee before brewing.
