Let's be direct: this is a 24-pack of 0.5L plastic bottles of S.Pellegrino. If you've ever bought the glass 750ml bottles at a restaurant, you know the brand. But the 0.5L plastic format is a different animal — and it deserves a closer look before you click buy.

## What You Actually Get

A case of 24 individual 500ml PET plastic bottles. Each bottle is 16.9 fluid ounces — the same size as a standard soda bottle. Total volume is 12 liters (about 3.2 gallons) of sparkling mineral water. The bottles arrive shrink-wrapped in a cardboard tray, typically well-packed for shipping. Amazon reviewers consistently note that damage in transit is rare, which matters when you're ordering a case of carbonated water.

The water itself is sourced from the S.Pellegrino spring in San Pellegrino Terme, Italy. The mineral content is what gives it that signature taste: calcium, magnesium, and a hint of sulfate. It's naturally carbonated at the source, with about 250-300 mg/L of bicarbonates. That's the chemistry behind the "clean, crisp" finish that regular seltzer just doesn't match.

## Real-World Performance

This is where the 0.5L format shines. The plastic bottle is thin and flexible — easy to squeeze, which means you can control the pour. Carbonation retention is genuinely impressive. One buyer noted: "The bubbles last all day even after opening — I keep a bottle on my desk and it stays fizzy for hours." That's a real advantage over cheaper brands like LaCroix or store-brand sparkling water, which tend to go flat within an hour of opening.

The taste profile is distinctly mineral — a light salinity that makes it excellent as a palate cleanser or a mixer. For coffee drinkers, this is a fantastic companion to espresso: the carbonation and mineral notes cut through the bitterness and cleanse the palate between sips. It's also a solid base for a splash of citrus if you want to dress it up.

The 0.5L size is portable. It fits standard car cup holders, gym bags, and lunch boxes. The 750ml glass bottles don't — they're taller and heavier. This is the format you grab when you're heading out the door.

They're single-use PET plastic, which is recyclable but still plastic. Compare this to the glass 750ml bottles — heavier, more expensive, but more sustainable and better for the environment. If eco-consciousness is a priority, this isn't your product.
