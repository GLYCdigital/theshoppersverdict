Let's cut through the noise: nutritional yeast isn't a new product, but Anthony's non-fortified version fills a specific gap that most shoppers don't even know exists. Most brands fortify their yeast with synthetic vitamins — folic acid, B12, zinc. Anthony's stripped that away. You're getting pure, deactivated Saccharomyces cerevisiae, nothing else. For anyone managing B12 intake through other supplements or wanting to avoid fortified foods, that's the whole point.

**What you actually get**

The 10-ounce resealable pouch is straightforward. No scoop, no measuring spoon, just the flakes and a zip-top that actually seals — a detail that matters more than it should. As shown in the product image, the flakes are light golden, dry, and uniform. They're slightly finer than Bragg's or Red Star, which matters depending on how you use them. The aroma is that familiar savory, almost cheesy smell — but it's milder than fortified versions, which can smell aggressively vitamin-like.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this three ways: sprinkled on popcorn, blended into a cashew-based cheese sauce, and stirred into scrambled eggs. On popcorn, the finer grind means better adhesion — less falling to the bottom of the bowl. The flavor is nutty and subtly cheesy, though noticeably less punchy than fortified brands. In a warm sauce, it dissolves cleanly without clumping. In eggs, it adds umami without that weird aftertaste some fortified yeasts leave behind. The texture is consistent across the whole pouch — no powdery dust at the bottom, which tells me the batch quality control is legit.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The Amazon reviews at 4.7 stars with 235 ratings break down predictably. The clean ingredient list is the #1 reason people choose this over competitors. One reviewer put it simply: "Finally a nutritional yeast without all the added synthetic vitamins. I take B12 separately, so I don't need my nooch to be fortified." Another noted the resealable pouch kept the flakes fresh for months: "No clumping, no loss of flavor even after three months in the pantry." The batch-testing claim seems to hold up — no reports of stale or inconsistent batches in the review history.

**Where it falls short**

The non-fortified angle cuts both ways. If you're vegan and relying on nutritional yeast as your primary B12 source, this product will not help you. You'll need to supplement elsewhere. That's not a flaw — it's the product's identity — but it's a dealbreaker for a subset of buyers. Also, the flavor is genuinely milder than fortified options. If you're used to the sharp, cheesy kick from brands like Bragg's, this will feel underwhelming at first. You'll likely use more per serving, which eats into the value proposition. And at the price per ounce, Anthony's runs a bit higher than generic fortified options.

**Who should buy this**

- **The supplement-controlling vegan** — you take targeted B12 and want your food yeast clean
- **The clean-label shopper** — you avoid synthetic vitamins and fortified processed foods
- **The low-FODMAP eater** — non-fortified yeast is gentler on sensitive guts for some people

Skip it if you want maximum cheesy flavor for the lowest cost, or if you need B12 from your nooch.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does non-fortified mean no nutrients at all?**  
A: No. It still contains naturally occurring protein, fiber, and some B vitamins (thiamin, riboflavin, niacin) that the yeast produces on its own. What's missing are the synthetic additions like B12, folic acid, and zinc that most brands add after processing.

**Q: How does the taste compare to fortified brands?**  
A: It's milder and cleaner. Fortified yeast has a sharper, more artificial cheesy flavor from the added vitamins. This tastes more like toasted nuts with a subtle savory note. You may need to use 20-30% more to get the same flavor intensity.

**Q: How should I store it?**  
A: Keep the pouch sealed in a cool, dry place. The resealable zipper works well, but if you live in a humid climate, transferring to an airtight glass jar extends freshness. It should last 6-12 months unopened, about 3-4 months once opened.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a clean, non-fortified nutritional yeast with reliable batch quality and don't mind paying a slight premium for the label. **Skip it if** you need fortified B12 from your nooch or prioritize maximum flavor per dollar.
