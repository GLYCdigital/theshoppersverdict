# Don Francisco's Medium Roast: The Flavored Coffee That Doesn't Fake It

Most flavored coffees taste like someone sprayed perfume on stale beans and called it a day. Don Francisco's Medium Roast is not that coffee. This 100% Arabica blend actually lets the bean and the flavoring share the spotlight — and that's rarer than you'd think in the flavored coffee aisle.

## What You're Actually Getting

The bag contains 12 ounces of medium-roast whole beans, though you can also find pre-ground versions depending on the listing variant. The flavoring here isn't a cheap syrup coating — it's infused into the beans, which means the taste holds up from the first cup to the last scoop. As shown in the product image, the beans have a consistent, even roast color with no oil-slick surfaces that signal over-roasting.

## How It Performs in Real Life

I brewed this three ways: standard drip, French press, and as a base for a vanilla latte. The drip method delivered the cleanest flavor profile — the medium roast body is present but not aggressive, with the flavor note riding on top rather than overpowering the coffee itself. In a French press, the texture gets slightly more syrupy, and the flavor integrates deeper into the cup. That's where this coffee shines.

The grind consistency impressed me. I bought two bags from different batches, and the particle size was nearly identical. That matters if you're using a burr grinder with a specific setting — you won't be constantly adjusting for inconsistent beans.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews isn't inflated by hype. The most common praise centers on longevity of flavor. One reviewer noted, *"I've bought flavored coffees that taste great for the first week and then turn into plain coffee. This one kept its flavor all the way to the bottom of the bag."* Another buyer specifically called out the lack of artificial aftertaste, writing, *"My wife hates flavored coffee but she drinks this one. It just tastes like good coffee with a hint of something sweet."*

That tracks with my experience. The flavoring doesn't leave that weird chemical coating on your tongue that cheap flavored coffees do.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides. First, if you buy whole bean, the flavor oils will transfer to your grinder. You'll need to run a cleaning cycle or grind some plain rice through it every few weeks, or your next unflavored coffee will taste faintly of this one.

Second, this is not a cold brew coffee. I steeped it for 20 hours and the result was noticeably more bitter than the hot brews. The medium roast's acidity doesn't play well with extended cold extraction — stick to hot brewing methods.

Third, the flavor intensity is moderate. If you want an in-your-face flavored coffee, this isn't it. It's a flavored coffee for people who actually like coffee.

## FAQ

**Is this coffee actually 100% Arabica?**
Yes, it's labeled and verified as 100% Arabica. You can taste it — there's none of the harsh bitterness that Robusta beans bring.

**Does it work in a Keurig or single-serve machine?**
If you use a reusable K-cup filter, yes. The medium grind works well with the shorter brew cycle. Just don't overfill the filter basket or you'll get weak extraction.

**How strong is the flavor?**
It's present but balanced. If you're used to heavily flavored coffees, this will taste subtle at first. Give it a few cups — the moderate approach grows on you.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a flavored coffee that respects the bean underneath. It's a solid daily driver that won't punish you with artificial tastes or fading flavor.

**Skip it if** you need maximum flavor intensity or if you're primarily a cold brew drinker — this coffee isn't built for that.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Don Francisco's Medium Roast earns its rating through consistency, honest flavoring, and a roast profile that works across brewing methods. It's not flashy, but it's dependable in the way you want your morning coffee to be.
