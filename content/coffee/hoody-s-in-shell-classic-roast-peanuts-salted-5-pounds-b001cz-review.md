Let's be honest: you don't buy a 5-pound bag of in-shell peanuts because you're planning a light snack. You buy it because you're settling in for a ballgame, a road trip, or a serious Netflix marathon. The question is whether Hoody's delivers the kind of quality that justifies buying in bulk, and the short answer is yes — with a few caveats worth knowing before you hit add to cart.

**What you actually get**

The package is straightforward: a 5-pound bag of Virginia-style in-shell peanuts, roasted and salted. No gimmicks, no flavor variations, no "premium" positioning. As shown in the product image, the peanuts arrive in a clear plastic bag that lets you see exactly what you're getting. The shells are a uniform tan color with that classic roasted look — not pale, not scorched.

The bag itself is substantial. At 5 pounds, you're getting roughly 20 cups of in-shell peanuts, which translates to about 5-6 cups of actual nutmeat once shelled. That's a lot of snacking. The peanuts are medium-to-large size, with most shells containing two solid kernels.

**Performance and real-world use**

Cracking these open is satisfying. The shells are sturdy enough that they don't shatter into a million fragments, but they yield easily to thumb pressure. The peanuts inside are crunchy with that characteristic roasted depth — not raw-tasting, not burnt. The salt level is the standout feature here. It's applied generously enough that you taste it immediately, but it doesn't leave your fingers coated in a gritty residue that makes you reach for a napkin after every handful.

One thing reviewers consistently mention is freshness. As one buyer put it: "These taste like they were roasted yesterday. Far better than the dusty bags I've gotten from the grocery store." That's been my experience too — there's no stale oil flavor that plagues bulk nuts that sit on shelves too long.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The value proposition is the loudest theme in the reviews. At this price point, you're paying significantly less per ounce than the 16-ounce grocery store bags that run $6-8 each. Buyers also repeatedly mention the salt-to-peanut ratio. One reviewer noted, "The salt is just right — not like those brands that dump half the salt in the bottom of the bag." Another appreciated that the peanuts weren't overly oily, which helps them stay crisp longer.

**Where it falls short**

The most practical gripe: no resealable closure. Once you open this 5-pound bag, you're committed to either transferring the peanuts to an airtight container or trusting a chip clip for the duration. If you're a slow snacker, they can go slightly soft after a few weeks in a humid environment.

There's also the occasional consistency issue. A handful of reviews mention receiving batches with some under-roasted peanuts — pale shells and softer kernels. It's not widespread, but it's worth knowing that bulk food products sometimes have batch-to-batch variation. One reviewer said, "Some peanuts were perfect, others were a bit chewy. It's a mixed bag, literally."

**Who should buy this**

This is for the person who knows they'll work through 5 pounds before it goes stale. If you host game nights, have a family of peanut eaters, or just want a cost-effective snack for a long road trip, this is your bag. It's also great for anyone making homemade peanut butter — you'll get plenty of nutmeat for the effort.

Skip it if you're a casual snacker who wants a small bag for occasional cravings. The bulk format will go stale before you finish it, and you'd be better served by a smaller package.

**FAQ**

*Do these come salted or unsalted?*  
This specific listing is salted — classic roast with salt. Hoody's offers unsalted varieties if that's your preference, but this one delivers a solid salty kick.

*How long do they stay fresh after opening?*  
Two to three weeks in a cool, dry place with the bag clipped closed. Transferring to an airtight container extends that to about a month. Don't refrigerate — it can introduce moisture.

*Are these peanuts shelled or in-shell?*  
In-shell, as the product name states. You crack them open yourself. Great for the experience, less great if you want quick snacking.

**The Verdict**

Hoody's In-Shell Classic Roast Peanuts deliver on the essentials: fresh, crunchy, well-salted peanuts at a price that makes sense for bulk buyers. The lack of a resealable bag and occasional batch inconsistency are minor annoyances, not deal-breakers.

**Buy it if** you want a cost-effective, satisfying snack for gatherings or road trips and don't mind storing them properly after opening.

**Skip it if** you're a light snacker or prefer shelled peanuts for convenience.
