**FAQ**

**Q: Will these fit my antique gumball machine?**
Most standard machines from the 1950s onward accept 1-inch gumballs. If your machine is vintage and uses a narrow metal chute, measure the opening first — some very old models are sized for 5/8-inch gumballs and will jam.

**Q: How many gumballs are in a bag?**
It depends on the weight option you select. A 1-inch gumball weighs roughly 0.11 ounces, so a standard 1-pound bag contains about 145 balls. Check the listing's specific weight before ordering.

**Q: Are these safe for young kids?**
The 1-inch size is actually a positive for safety — it's large enough to reduce choking risk compared to smaller gumballs. However, the gum itself is standard chewing gum, so it's not recommended for children under 3 who might swallow it.
