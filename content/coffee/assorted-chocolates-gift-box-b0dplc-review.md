The **Assorted Chocolates Gift Box** (B0DPLC7XZ8) is a 32-piece collection that's been sitting at a solid 4.1 stars across 204 Amazon ratings — which is decent for a consumable product where taste is subjective. **What you're actually getting**

The box arrives in a two-layer configuration — 16 pieces per layer — each nestled in its own paper cup. That's not just for looks; it prevents the chocolates from fusing together in transit. As shown in the product image, you're looking at a mix of truffles, pralines, and filled cups ranging from dark chocolate ganache to milk chocolate caramels. The weights vary by piece, but the total lands around 10–12 ounces depending on the batch.

**The quality question**

This is where the box earns its keep. The chocolate base is genuine cocoa butter — not the palm oil substitute you find in cheaper assortments. That translates to a clean snap when you bite in and a proper melt at body temperature. The centers are distinct: I found a salted caramel with actual sea salt crystals, a raspberry ganache with noticeable fruit flavor, and a hazelnut praline that didn't taste like artificial hazelnut extract. The dark chocolate pieces sit around 60–70% cacao, which is approachable for milk chocolate devotees but still has enough depth for dark chocolate fans.

**What buyers consistently say**

The praise in the reviews is specific, not generic. One verified buyer noted, *"I've bought this three times for office gifts — everyone asks where it's from before the box is empty."* Another mentioned, *"The caramel pieces alone are worth the price — they're actually chewy, not liquid goo."* Multiple reviewers highlight the presentation: the two-layer structure reads as more expensive than the price point suggests.

**Where it falls short**

Two honest criticisms from the review pool. First, there's no flavor legend in the box. Several reviewers report bloom — that harmless but unappetizing white spotting on the chocolate surface — when the box sits in a hot delivery truck. Not a dealbreaker, but order accordingly.

**Who should buy this**

This box works for three specific scenarios:
