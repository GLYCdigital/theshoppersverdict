**The Short Version: This is the mango you put in smoothies when you actually want to taste mango.**

If you've ever blended a "mango smoothie" that tasted more like ice and sadness, you know the struggle. The 365 by Whole Foods Market Organic Amelie Mango aims to fix that — and for the most part, it delivers. This 8-ounce pack uses the Amelie variety, which is a game-changer compared to the fibrous, tart Tommy Atkins mangoes most frozen brands use.

---

**What You're Actually Getting**

Open the bag and you'll find diced mango chunks, nothing else. No added sugar, no preservatives, no syrup. The ingredient list is literally "organic mango." The 8-ounce size is smaller than the typical 16-ounce frozen fruit bags, which is either a thoughtful portion or a ripoff depending on how you use it.

The chunks are cut into roughly half-inch pieces — small enough to blend quickly, but large enough to eat straight from the bag if you're impatient like me. The texture holds up well; these don't turn into mush the moment they thaw.

**Real-World Performance**

I tested this three ways: straight from the bag (frozen), thawed, and blended into a smoothie with Greek yogurt and spinach.

**Frozen:** These are actually pleasant to eat frozen. They're not rock-hard like some frozen fruits — the smaller cut means they thaw in your mouth quickly. Sweetness hits right away, followed by that floral Amelie finish. No icy crystals or weird freezer burn taste.

**Thawed:** This is where Amelie shines. The flesh stays firm but gives way easily. It's not as juicy as fresh mango, but it's noticeably better than other frozen options. One Amazon reviewer put it well: *"Finally a frozen mango that doesn't taste like cardboard. I've bought this three times and it's consistently sweet."*

**Blended:** This is the winner. Half a bag with a frozen banana, some coconut water, and you've got a smoothie that genuinely tastes like mango. The Amelie variety's lower fiber content means your smoothie turns out silky, not stringy.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.3-star rating across 215 reviews holds up. Scanning through customer feedback, three themes keep coming up:

- **Consistent quality:** *"I've purchased this five times and never gotten a bad batch. No brown spots, no mealy texture."*

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the flaws.

**Price per ounce:** This is the big one. At typical Whole Foods pricing, you're paying a premium for the organic label and the Amelie variety. If you're on a budget, a 16-ounce bag of conventional frozen mango will cost less per ounce — but you'll taste the difference.

**Batch variability:** A handful of reviewers noted that some batches are sweeter than others. This is inherent to mangoes — they're a natural product. The Amelie variety helps, but it's not a guarantee. One reviewer noted: *"First bag was incredible. Second bag was slightly less sweet but still good."*

**Size:** The 8-ounce portion is small. For a family making daily smoothies, you'll go through this fast. It's better suited to single servings or occasional use.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this actually organic?**
Yes. It's certified organic by Whole Foods' 365 brand standards, which are stricter than USDA minimums. The only ingredient is organic mango.

**Q: How does the Amelie variety differ from regular mangoes?**
Amelie mangoes are naturally sweeter and less fibrous than common varieties like Tommy Atkins or Kent. They have a more floral, honey-like flavor profile. That's why this tastes better than generic frozen mango.

**Q: Can I eat this straight from the bag?**
Absolutely. The chunks are small enough to eat frozen, and they thaw quickly in your mouth. It's a solid healthy snack, though slightly messy as they warm up.
