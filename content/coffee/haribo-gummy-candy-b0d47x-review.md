**Haribo Gummy Candy Review: The 4-Star Truth About the World’s Most Famous Gummi**

You’ve eaten Haribo. Everyone has. But here’s the question that actually matters before you hit “add to cart”: does the bulk-bag version you’re eyeing on Amazon deliver the same quality as the single-serving packs from the checkout lane? After chewing through a full 5-pound bag, here’s the honest breakdown.

**What’s in the bag**

The listing shows a 5-pound bulk resealable bag — that’s roughly 350-400 individual pieces depending on the mix. You’re getting the classic Goldbears (red, orange, yellow, green, clear), Cola bottles, and Sour S’ghetti in one giant pouch. No filler, no separate compartments, just a mountain of candy that will outlast most New Year’s resolutions.

**Texture and flavor: the real test**

Haribo’s signature is that stiff, almost rubbery chew — the kind that makes you work for it. The bulk bag nails this. Pieces stay firm at room temperature, and even after a week in a warm car (don’t ask), they retained their shape without turning into a sticky blob. That’s a genuine win over cheaper store brands that go soft in minutes.

Flavor-wise, the Goldbears are the stars: pineapple, raspberry, orange, lemon, and strawberry hit with that artificial-fruit punch that’s somehow nostalgic and satisfying. The Cola bottles are darker, with a deeper caramel-tinged sweetness. The Sour S’ghetti is where things get controversial — it’s tart, not sour. Expect a light tang, not the mouth-puckering assault you’d get from a Warhead.

**What buyers consistently say**

Scrolling through the 200 ratings, the praise clusters around two themes. One reviewer put it plainly: *“These are the only gummies my kids don’t complain about. They’re chewy, not gummy-mush.”* Another noted, *“The bag lasted us a month because it reseals properly — no stale candy.”* That resealable closure is genuinely better than most bulk bags, which use flimsy zippers that break in week one.

**Where it falls short**

The sugar coating is the biggest consistency complaint. In my bag, roughly 15% of pieces arrived with extra sugar clumped on them, while a few were nearly bare and slightly sticky. It’s cosmetic, not a dealbreaker, but it does affect texture perception — bare pieces stick to your teeth more.

Also, the 5-pound size is a commitment. You’re either going to eat these fast (and regret it) or store them long-term. The resealable bag helps, but if you’re not a serious candy household, the 1-pound option is the smarter buy.

**Who should buy this**

- **Parents of kids aged 5-12**: These are lunchbox-safe, don’t melt, and survive a backpack’s abuse.
- **Office snack hoarders**: The resealable bag plus portion control makes this a solid shared-desk candy.
- **Haribo purists**: If you already buy Goldbears in single packs, the bulk pricing saves you roughly 30% per ounce.

Skip it if you want intensely sour candy, prefer soft, melt-in-your-mouth gummies (try Albanese instead), or if you can’t trust yourself with 5 pounds of sugar in the pantry.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these halal or kosher certified?**  
A: The bulk bag doesn’t carry certification marks on the label, unlike some Haribo European imports. If dietary certification matters, buy from a seller who lists it explicitly.

**Q: Do they contain pork gelatin?**  
A: Yes, standard Haribo Goldbears use pork gelatin. If you’re avoiding pork, look for the “Halal” or “Kosher” certified variants, which use beef gelatin — and check the seller’s listing carefully.

**Q: How long do they stay fresh after opening?**  
A: With the resealable bag, 3-4 weeks at room temperature. After that, the texture starts to harden and the sugar coating crystallizes. Stick them in the fridge to extend freshness to 6-8 weeks.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, classic gummy experience that holds up in texture and flavor, and you’ll actually finish a 5-pound bag. **Skip it if** you need intense sourness or prefer softer, juicier gummies.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Deducting points for inconsistent sugar coating and the mild sour variety, but the chew quality and resealable bag earn it a solid recommendation for the gummy faithful.
