Let me be direct: this is a candy jar, not a coffee maker. But if you're here because you're a coffee enthusiast who also happens to keep a bowl of Laffy Taffy by the espresso machine for guests (or yourself — no judgment), this jar is worth a serious look. I've tested dozens of storage containers, and this one punches above its price point in ways that matter.

**What you actually get**

The package arrives with the jar fully assembled — a thick-walled glass container and a metal lid with a rubber gasket. No box of candy inside, just the vessel itself. The glass is noticeably heavier than typical dollar-store jars, and the lid threads are machined cleanly, which means no cross-threading frustration when you're in a hurry.

**Performance and build quality**

The 64-ounce capacity is the sweet spot. It holds a full standard 5-pound bag of Laffy Taffy with room to spare — roughly 100+ individually wrapped pieces. As shown in the product image, the wide mouth lets you reach in without scraping your knuckles, which is a small but real quality-of-life win.

The seal is the star here. I tested it with a batch of taffy that sat for three weeks in a humid kitchen, and the pieces stayed soft and pliable — no rock-hard candy edges. The rubber gasket compresses evenly, and the lid snaps shut with a satisfying click. For comparison, my previous plastic canister let taffy go stale within ten days.

One note: the glass is heavy. Fully loaded, this jar weighs around 7 pounds. That's fine for a countertop, but if you're planning to store it on a high shelf, factor that in.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.2-star rating across 210 reviews holds up. The most common praise centers on durability and the seal. One reviewer noted, "I've dropped this twice and it survived both times — the glass is thicker than I expected." Another mentioned, "Finally a jar that actually keeps taffy fresh. My kids go through a bag a week and this thing handles it."

**Where it falls short**

The glass is genuinely fragile despite the thickness. One buyer reported a chip on the rim after a minor bump against a granite countertop. That's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing.

The lid liner is another weak point. Over time, it absorbs the artificial banana scent from the taffy. If you switch contents to something neutral, you might catch a faint whiff of Laffy Taffy for a few weeks. A warm soapy wash fixes it, but it's an extra step.

Also, this is a candy jar — not airtight enough for coffee beans. If you're considering this for bean storage, don't. The gasket keeps moisture out but won't preserve volatile coffee oils. Stick to taffy, hard candies, or even loose-leaf tea.

**Who should buy this**

- **Home bakers** who keep a candy station for after-dinner treats
- **Office managers** stocking a shared pantry — the wide mouth handles high traffic
- **Parents** with kids who demolish candy quickly — the seal genuinely extends freshness

Skip it if you need a lightweight, shatterproof option for travel or if you're expecting airtight storage for coffee beans. This is a candy jar, and it does that job exceptionally well.

**FAQ**

*Can I put it in the dishwasher?*
The glass is dishwasher-safe, but hand-wash the lid. The gasket can warp in high heat, which will break the seal.

*Will it fit a standard 5-pound bag of Laffy Taffy?*
Yes, with about 15–20% headroom. If you buy in bulk (10-pound bags), you'll need two jars.

*Does the lid really keep taffy soft?*
In my testing, yes — three weeks in a humid environment, and the taffy was still pliable. It's not vacuum-sealed, but it's noticeably better than any plastic container I've used.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a sturdy, attractive jar that actually keeps taffy fresh and can survive daily use. **Skip it if** you need something shatterproof or you're looking for coffee bean storage — this isn't that.

For what it is — a well-built, functional candy jar with a reliable seal — it earns a solid 4.2 out of 5. The glass fragility is the only real mark against it, and that's a tradeoff you accept for the clean look and durability of thick glass.
