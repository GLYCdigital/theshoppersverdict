## FAQ

**Q: Can I reuse this oil for deep frying?**
A: Yes, with proper straining. After cooling, filter out food particles and store in a sealed container. It holds up for 2-3 frying sessions before flavor degradation becomes noticeable.

**Q: Is this oil suitable for high-heat wok cooking?**
A: The ~400°F smoke point handles standard wok heat on home stoves. For restaurant-grade flame wok cooking, you'd want an oil with a higher smoke point like peanut or avocado oil.

**Q: How long does it stay fresh after opening?**
A: Stored in a cool, dark cabinet, expect 6-8 months of peak quality. Heat and light accelerate rancidity, so keep it away from the stove.
