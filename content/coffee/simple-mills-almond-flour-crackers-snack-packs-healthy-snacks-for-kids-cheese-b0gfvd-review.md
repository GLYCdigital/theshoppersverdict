Let me be straight with you: I've tested a lot of "healthy" crackers that taste like compressed sawdust. The Simple Mills Almond Flour Crackers Snack Packs aren't that. They're actually good — and that's coming from someone who usually reaches for Cheez-Its without shame.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains 6 individually wrapped snack packs, each weighing 0.8 ounces. That's roughly 12-14 crackers per pouch depending on the variety you grab. The cheese flavor is the one I tested most thoroughly, but the lineup includes Farmhouse Cheddar, Sea Salt, and Rosemary & Sea Salt. The snack pack format is the real selling point here — no more portioning crackers into baggies that rip at the seam or dumping the whole box into a lunchbox to find crumbs later.

## The Real-World Test

The texture is where these shine. Almond flour gives them a noticeable crunch that holds up even when they sit in a backpack for hours. They don't get soggy, they don't taste like cardboard, and they actually have a satisfying snap when you bite into them. The cheese flavor is subtle — think "hint of cheddar" rather than "cheese dust explosion." If you're transitioning from processed snacks, this is a feature, not a bug. If you're expecting Cheez-It intensity, you'll be let down.

One thing I noticed: the crackers are thinner than your standard supermarket cracker. That means they're more fragile. A few reviewers noted arriving boxes with broken pieces. My box had maybe 15% cracker shrapnel at the bottom — annoying, but not a dealbreaker since they still taste fine.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews reflects a consistent pattern. Parents specifically mention the lunchbox convenience and the ingredient list. ## Where It Falls Short

Let's talk price. At $5.86 for six snack packs, you're paying roughly 97 cents per serving. Compare that to a box of conventional crackers at half the price, and there's a real cost premium. That's the tradeoff for almond flour and no junk ingredients — and it's worth naming plainly.

The flavor intensity is also worth flagging. If your family is used to bold, processed cheese flavors, these will taste muted. It's a clean, nutty cracker with a light cheese note, not a flavor bomb.

## FAQ

**Are these actually gluten-free?**
Yes. They're made with almond flour and cassava flour — no wheat at all. They're also certified gluten-free.

**How do they compare to regular crackers in calories?**
Each snack pack runs about 130 calories with 2g protein and 2g sugar. That's comparable to conventional crackers but with more fiber and better fats from the almonds.

**Can adults use these for meal prep?**
Absolutely. The individual packs are great for desk drawers, hiking bags, or portion-controlled snacking. They don't require refrigeration and hold up well.
