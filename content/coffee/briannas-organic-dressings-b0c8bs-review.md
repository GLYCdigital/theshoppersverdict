Glass doesn't leach into the dressing the way plastic can over time, and it keeps the contents colder in the fridge. The organic certification covers the base ingredients: expeller-pressed oils, organic vinegar, and real herbs and spices. No artificial anything. That's verifiable on the label, not just marketing copy.

**Real-World Performance**

The flavor profile is where this dressing earns its keep. It's bold — noticeably more assertive than mass-market brands. The acidity is balanced, the oil tastes clean, and the herbs actually taste like herbs rather than dust. One buyer put it this way: "Tastes like something from a high-end bistro, not something from a bottle." I'd agree.
