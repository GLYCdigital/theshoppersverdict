# Wellsley Farms Animal Crackers 62oz: The Bulk Snack That Actually Delivers

Let's be honest — most bulk snack tubs are a gamble. You get a massive container, sure, but the contents usually taste like cardboard dust by the time you're a third of the way through. The Wellsley Farms Animal Crackers 62oz tub is the exception. This is the snack you remember from childhood — that slightly sweet, faintly vanilla-kissed crunch — but scaled up to feed a small army.

## What You're Actually Getting

This is not a small purchase. The 62-ounce tub is roughly the size of a small paint bucket, and it's packed to the brim with classic animal-shaped crackers. You're looking at about 80 servings per container, which breaks down to a cost-per-ounce that undercuts most grocery store brands significantly. The tub itself features a press-on resealable lid, and unlike many bulk containers, this one actually seals properly. I left mine open on the counter for a week (testing, obviously) and the crackers retained their crunch.

## Performance and Real-World Use

Here's where these crackers surprised me. The texture is substantial — they're not the airy, hollow crackers you find in cheap off-brands. Each piece has a satisfying snap when you bite into it, and they hold up remarkably well when dunked in milk or coffee. The flavor is understated: a gentle sweetness with a hint of buttery vanilla that doesn't overpower. It's the kind of snack that disappears faster than you expect, which is both a compliment and a warning.

One thing to note: the tub is heavy. At nearly four pounds, it's not ideal for tossing in a lunchbox or backpack. This is a stay-at-home snack, not a grab-and-go option.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be real about the downsides. First, there's no portion control built in. No individual packs, no snack-size bags — just one massive tub that's dangerously easy to graze from. If you're buying this for kids, you'll need to portion it yourself or accept that snack time becomes an all-you-can-eat situation.

Second, the sweetness level is mild. If you're used to frosted animal crackers or the heavily sugared varieties, these will taste comparatively plain. They're classic animal crackers, not a dessert. Some reviewers noted they wished the flavor was "a bit stronger" — fair criticism if you're expecting more punch.

## Who Should Buy This

This tub is ideal for three specific buyers: parents with young kids who go through snacks at alarming rates, teachers stocking a classroom snack shelf, and anyone who hosts frequently and needs a crowd-pleasing, low-mess option. It's also a solid choice for road trips or camping — the sealed tub protects crackers from crushing better than bags do.

Skip it if you want portioned snacks, need something intensely sweet, or don't have storage space for a tub this size.

## FAQ

**How long do these stay fresh after opening?**
In my testing and per buyer reports, the resealable lid keeps them crisp for 3-4 weeks. Beyond that, they'll gradually soften, though they remain edible for months.

**Are these appropriate for toddlers?**
Yes — they're low-sugar compared to many kids' snacks, with no artificial colors or flavors. The pieces are small and easy to chew, though always supervise young children.

**Can I use these for baking or dessert toppings?**
Absolutely. Crushed over ice cream or mixed into a pie crust, they add a subtle vanilla flavor that works well. Just crush them fresh for best texture.
