# Selena Gomez OREO Cookies: Limited Edition — Worth the Hype or Just a Gimmick?

Let's be real: celebrity-branded snacks usually disappoint. They're either overpriced, under-flavored, or both. The Selena Gomez OREO collaboration, though, has generated genuine buzz — and with a 4.3-star average from 215 ratings, it's not just hype. I picked up a pack to see if this limited-edition release deserves your money or belongs in the novelty bin.

## What You're Actually Getting

The 10.68 oz package is a standard OREO box — nothing fancy. Inside, you get roughly 36 cookies arranged in the classic plastic tray. As shown in the product image, the branding leans into purple and pink tones with Selena's name front and center. But the real story is what's between the wafers.

The creme is where this collaboration lives. It's a custom flavor that OREO describes as inspired by Selena — and without spoiling the exact profile, it's a subtle, creamy twist that's more sophisticated than your average limited drop. The cookie wafers themselves are identical to classic OREO: same dark cocoa, same satisfying snap.

## Performance and Real-World Testing

I tested these three ways: straight from the pack, dunked in milk, and crushed over vanilla ice cream.

**Straight up:** The creme is the star. It's smoother and less cloying than standard vanilla filling. The flavor is present but restrained — think "elevated" rather than "screaming novelty." If you were hoping for something aggressive like birthday cake or red velvet, this isn't that.

**Dunked:** This is where the cookie shines. The wafer holds up beautifully — no immediate sogginess, and the creme flavor actually intensifies slightly with the milk. Classic OREO behavior, executed well.

**Crushed:** As a topping, the subtle creme gets lost a bit against strong ice cream flavors. It works, but you're better off with the standard version if this is your intended use.

The 4.3-star rating checks out. Buyers consistently mention the balanced sweetness and the fact that it doesn't taste artificial. One reviewer noted, "I was skeptical about a celebrity collab, but the creme is genuinely different — smoother and less sugary than regular OREOs." Another said, "My kids loved them, but I finished the pack myself. The flavor is subtle and addictive."

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest part. The flavor is **subtle** — dangerously so. If you're paying a premium for a limited edition, you expect a flavor you can identify blindfolded. This one, you might struggle to pick out of a lineup against regular OREOs. It's good, but it's not *distinctive*.

The packaging is also a missed opportunity. For a celebrity collaboration, the box is plain — no special artwork, no collector's appeal. If you're buying this as a gift or for the novelty, the presentation underwhelms.

And at 10.68 oz, this disappears fast. There's no resealable bag, so if you don't finish it in one sitting, you're dealing with an open tray in your pantry. Minor, but worth noting.

## Who Should Buy This

- **OREO completists** who need every limited drop in their collection
- **Selena Gomez fans** looking for a fun, edible piece of merch
- **Bakers** who want a subtle twist in their cookie crusts or cheesecake bases

**Skip it if** you're expecting a bold, candy-like flavor explosion. This is a refined, quiet release — not a party in your mouth.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're an OREO enthusiast or want a genuinely good-tasting cookie that's slightly more refined than the original. The creme is well-executed, the wafer quality is top-tier, and at this price point, it's a reasonable splurge.

**Skip it if** you're expecting a bold, distinctive flavor or collectible packaging. This is a quiet, subtle release that leans on the OREO name more than the celebrity one.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, enjoyable cookie that earns its rating through execution rather than novelty. Just don't expect the flavor to knock your socks off.
