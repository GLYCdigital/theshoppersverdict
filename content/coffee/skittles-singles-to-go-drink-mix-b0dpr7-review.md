Let's be honest: flavored drink mixes are a dime a dozen. But when a product carries the Skittles name and still pulls a 4.7-star average from 235 ratings, that's worth investigating. I've tested every variant of powdered drink enhancer from the grocery aisle to the deep-cut imports, and here's the real story on Skittles Singles To Go.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains 10 single-serve stick packets, each designed to mix with a standard 16.9 oz bottle of water. The Original variety delivers the classic five-flavor lineup — strawberry, lemon, green apple, grape, and orange — all in one packet. The powder dissolves clear, so you're not left with that chalky residue some cheaper mixes have.

As shown in the product image, these are slim, portable sticks that slip into a purse, gym bag, or desk drawer without taking up space. Packaging is straightforward with no gimmicks.

## Real-World Performance

The critical question: does it taste like Skittles? Surprisingly, yes. The flavor engineers nailed the profile. The citrus notes come through sharp and clean, while the berry flavors have that distinctive artificial-but-beloved character that made the candy famous. It's not a subtle "hint of fruit" situation — this is unmistakably Skittles-flavored water.

Mixability is where this product genuinely impressed me. Drop the powder into cold water, give it a few shakes, and it's fully dissolved in under 30 seconds. No clumping, no floating granules, no need for a shaker bottle. That's a small but meaningful detail for anyone who's dealt with stubborn powder residue in other brands.

With zero sugar and 5 calories per packet, it works as a hydration tool if you're trying to drink more water but find plain water boring. The sweetness level assumes you're using the full 16.9 oz — if you're mixing into a smaller bottle, dial back the powder because it gets intense quickly.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The praise across the 235 ratings is remarkably consistent. Multiple reviewers describe it as "better than expected" for a candy-branded product. One buyer noted it "actually tastes like the candy, not like a cheap knockoff." Another highlighted the portability: "Perfect for travel — I toss a few in my carry-on and have flavored water anywhere."

The most common theme in positive reviews is that it satisfies a sweet craving without the sugar crash or calories of actual candy. Several mention using it as a way to increase daily water intake.

## Where It Falls Short

The sweetness is the main complaint. If you're not careful with water ratios, it can taste syrupy rather than refreshing. Some reviewers found the flavor slightly artificial compared to high-end fruit-infused water drops, though that's arguably part of the charm.

There's also no functional benefit beyond flavor — no electrolytes, no caffeine, no vitamins. If you're looking for a workout recovery drink or an energy boost, this isn't it. It's purely a taste enhancer.

- **Travelers and commuters**: The stick packets take up almost no space and need no refrigeration.
- **Parents of picky kids**: A fun way to encourage water consumption instead of juice or soda.

## FAQ

**Can I use this in hot water for tea?**
Technically yes, but it's not recommended. The flavors are designed for cold water and become muted and slightly chemical when heated.

**How long does an opened packet stay fresh?**
Since they're individually sealed, unopened packets maintain freshness well past the date on the box. Once opened, use immediately — the powder absorbs humidity quickly.

**Is this suitable for keto or diabetic diets?**
Yes. With zero sugar, zero carbs, and 5 calories per serving, it fits into most low-carb eating plans. The sweetener is sucralose-based, which most people on keto tolerate fine.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely good-tasting, zero-calorie flavored water option that lives up to its candy branding. It's portable, mixes clean, and delivers the nostalgia factor without the sugar.

**Skip it if** you're sensitive to artificial sweeteners, prefer electrolyte-enhanced hydration drinks, or want something more subtle than a candy-flavored beverage.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A solid, reliable product that does exactly what it promises. The flavor accuracy and mixability elevate it above typical powdered drink mixes.
