# CORE Hydration Nutrient Enhanced Water Review: Premium Hydration or Overpriced H2O?

You're standing in the water aisle, staring at a wall of identical plastic bottles. Then you see it — the distinctive dark blue square bottle that looks like it belongs in a design museum. CORE Hydration has carved out a serious following with 4.8 stars across 240 Amazon ratings. But is this premium water actually worth the markup, or are you paying for clever packaging?

## What You're Actually Getting

Each bottle delivers 16.9 fluid ounces of water that's been purified through reverse osmosis, then enhanced with a specific mineral blend: potassium, magnesium, and calcium. The headline feature is the pH level — 7.4, which CORE claims is the "optimal" balance for your body. Whether that's scientifically meaningful is debatable, but what matters is how it tastes.

The water goes through a 7-stage purification process, which strips out everything, then minerals are added back in a precise ratio. The result is water that tastes clean without tasting flat.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this water across different scenarios — gym workouts, office days, and long drives. The first thing you'll notice is the bottle itself. The square shape isn't just for looks; it fits standard cup holders without rocking, and the wider base means it's harder to knock over. The matte finish gives you a solid grip, even when your hands are slick with sweat.

But here's where it matters: the taste. Multiple buyers on Amazon echo this sentiment. One reviewer wrote, *"I never realized I hated the taste of water until I tried this. Now I actually look forward to drinking it."* Another noted, *"I drink way more water now because it doesn't have that weird plastic or mineral taste."*

The electrolyte blend does seem to make a difference. I found myself reaching for CORE during afternoon slumps instead of coffee, and the hydration effect felt more substantial than with tap water. That said, if you're expecting a dramatic performance boost, you'll be disappointed — this isn't a sports drink.

## What Buyers Rave About

The consistency is the biggest theme in positive reviews. Unlike some bottled waters that taste different depending on the batch or storage conditions, CORE delivers the same clean profile every time. Multiple reviewers mention the "smoothness" as the top reason they repurchase.

The bottle design earns genuine praise too. One Amazon reviewer said, *"The bottle is the star here. It fits everywhere, doesn't sweat condensation all over my desk, and I actually reuse them for my gym water."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The price is the biggest obstacle — you're paying premium water prices for what is, at the end of the day, still water. If you're going through multiple bottles a day, this adds up quickly.

The cap design is genuinely frustrating. It requires a firm twist that's manageable with dry hands but becomes a struggle when you're post-workout or holding a sweating bottle. I've had several moments where I nearly launched the cap across the gym.

Also worth noting: there's only one flavor option — plain. If you want citrus or berry varieties, CORE isn't your brand.

## Who Should Buy This

**The fitness enthusiast**: The electrolyte blend and easy-grip bottle make this ideal for gym bags and workout hydration.

**The taste-sensitive drinker**: If you've always hated the metallic or plastic taste of regular bottled water, this is worth the premium.

**The office worker**: The bottle is spill-resistant and fits perfectly in desk cup holders, and the clean taste beats the office water cooler.

**Skip it if**: You're on a tight budget, you prefer flavored waters, or you're content with tap water.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is CORE Hydration actually alkaline water?**
No — it's pH-balanced at 7.4, which is neutral on the pH scale. True alkaline waters run 8.0-9.5. CORE's positioning is about optimal balance, not alkalinity.

**Can I reuse the bottles?**
Yes, physically. They're made from #7 plastic, which is BPA-free and durable. Many reviewers report reusing them for weeks. Just note that the square base makes them less efficient for backpack side pockets.

**How does it compare to Smartwater or Essentia?**
Smartwater has a similar electrolyte profile but a slightly flatter taste. Essentia runs more alkaline (9.5 pH) with a distinct mineral tang. CORE sits in the middle — cleaner than Smartwater, less aggressive than Essentia.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You value clean, consistent-tasting water and are willing to pay for it. The design and electrolyte blend make it genuinely better than standard bottled water.

**Skip it if:** You're price-sensitive or expect a dramatic hydration difference. This is still water — great-tasting, well-packaged water, but water nonetheless.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A premium product that delivers on its promises, with minor flaws that don't overshadow its strengths. The taste and bottle design alone justify the premium for many buyers, but the cap issue and price point keep it from perfection.
