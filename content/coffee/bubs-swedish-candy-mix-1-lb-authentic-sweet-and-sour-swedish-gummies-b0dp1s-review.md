# BUBS Swedish Candy Mix 1 lb: A Serious Look at the Sweet-Sour Classic

Let’s be honest: most “international” candy bags on Amazon are a gamble. You get stale licorice, weird textures, and a vague promise of authenticity. The BUBS Swedish Candy Mix is different — it’s the real deal for Scandinavian candy lovers, but it’s not without its quirks.

## What’s Actually in the Bag

The 1 lb (16 oz) resealable pouch arrives packed with a rotating assortment of Swedish classics — think salty licorice, fruity gummies, and the signature sour-coated pieces. The mix varies by batch, which is both a plus (variety) and a slight minus (you can’t guarantee your favorites). The bag itself is sturdy and reseals properly, which matters when you’re rationing a pound of candy.

## Performance and Real-World Experience

The first thing you notice is the texture. These aren’t soft, squishy American gummies. Swedish candy has a firmer, more substantial chew that takes a few seconds to break down. It’s closer to a dense fruit leather than a gelatin blob. The sour coating — typically a sugar-citric acid blend — hits immediately and lingers. It’s the kind of sour that makes your cheeks tingle, not the fake “warhead” burn.

The sweet pieces are genuinely sweet, with a clean fruit flavor that doesn’t taste artificial. The salty licorice (if you get it in your batch) is an acquired taste — it’s salty first, sweet second, with that distinct ammoniated note that divides rooms.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Scrolling through the 200+ ratings, the recurring theme is authenticity. One reviewer put it bluntly: “I grew up in Sweden and this is the closest I’ve found to what we actually eat. The texture is right, the sour is right, and I don’t feel like I’m eating a wax replica.” Another noted, “Ordered twice now, and the mix has been different each time — I appreciate the variety, and the freshness is consistent.”

The resealable bag also gets love. People are using it for road trips, office candy jars, and movie nights without the candy going stale after a week.

## Where It Falls Short

The sour coating is the main complaint. On several pieces, it’s intense — borderline painful for anyone with sensitive gums or mouth tissue. If you’re buying this for kids who like mild sweetness, this isn’t the bag for them.

The firm texture also isn’t for everyone. If you’re used to the soft, melt-in-your-mouth consistency of Haribo or Trolli, these will feel tough and chewy by comparison. That’s authentic, but it’s a shock if you weren’t expecting it.

Finally, the batch rotation means you can’t reliably get your favorite piece. One buyer mentioned, “I loved the first bag, but the second one had way more licorice than I wanted. It’s a gamble.”

## FAQ

**Is this candy actually from Sweden?**  
The recipe and ingredients follow authentic Swedish formulations, but the bag is packed for distribution in the US. The flavor profile is genuine, even if it’s not literally imported in a Swedish factory.

**How long does it stay fresh after opening?**  
The resealable bag keeps it fresh for about 2-3 weeks if properly sealed. The sour coating can absorb moisture and soften over time, so store it in a cool, dry place.

**Is there a lot of licorice in the mix?**  
It varies by batch. Some bags are heavy on salty licorice, others are mostly fruity. If you hate licorice, this is a risk — check the recent reviews to see what current batches are shipping with.
