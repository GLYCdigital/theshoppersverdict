**Spike Original All-Purpose Seasoning Salt Review: The 39-Ingredient Blend That Actually Earns Its Hype**

Let’s cut through the noise: most "all-purpose" seasonings are just salt with a whisper of garlic powder and a few dried parsley flakes for color. Spike Original is not that. This is a dense, fragrant, almost chunky blend of 39 herbs, veggies, and spices that looks more like a gourmet rub than a typical shaker-top seasoning. And with a 4.9 out of 5 rating across 245 Amazon reviews, it's clearly resonating with home cooks. But does it live up to the cult following? Here's the real breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The classic Spike comes in a 5.5-ounce plastic jar with a flip-top shaker lid. As shown in the product image, the blend itself is a mix of coarse salt crystals, visibly green herb flakes, dried onion and garlic pieces, and a rust-colored dusting of paprika and other spices. It's not a fine powder — it's rustic and textured. The ingredient list is genuinely impressive: celery, carrot, onion, parsley, tomato, mushroom, and a slew of spices like turmeric, kelp, and dill. It's MSG-free, which is a major selling point for many shoppers.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

I tested this on scrambled eggs, roasted potatoes, and a grilled chicken breast. The first thing you notice is the aroma — it's savory, almost broth-like, with a pronounced celery and vegetable undertone. On eggs, it delivered a rich, complex flavor that made me forget the bottle of hot sauce sitting nearby. On roasted potatoes, it created a crispy, seasoned crust that was genuinely addictive.

The key here is that Spike is *salt-forward but not salt-only*. You get a legitimate depth of flavor — the kelp and mushroom contribute umami, the celery seed adds brightness, and the herbs provide an earthy finish. One Amazon reviewer put it perfectly: *"This is the only seasoning I've found that makes plain chicken taste like it came from a fancy restaurant. I've bought six jars already."*

**Where It Falls Short**

Honestly, the flaws are minor but worth noting. First, the coarse texture means the shaker holes can clog, especially if the jar sits in a humid cabinet. I found myself unscrewing the lid and using a spoon more often than not. Second, while it's MSG-free, it's still a high-sodium product — about 310mg per 1/4 teaspoon. If you're on a low-sodium diet, this isn't your hero. Finally, the flavor is bold. Really bold. If you sprinkle it on delicate fish or a light vegetable soup, it can dominate rather than complement. One reviewer noted, *"Love it on beef and potatoes, but it completely overpowered my tilapia."*

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The "one-bottle pantry" cook:** If you want to declutter your spice rack and replace garlic salt, onion powder, seasoned salt, and dried herbs with a single jar, this is it.
- **Grill masters and meat lovers:** It works wonders as a dry rub on steaks, pork chops, and burgers.
- **Egg and potato enthusiasts:** If you eat breakfast food regularly, this will become your go-to.
- **Skip it if:** You prefer subtle, single-note seasonings, or if you're strictly watching sodium intake.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is Spike seasoning the same as Lawry's Seasoned Salt?**  
A: No. Lawry's is primarily salt, sugar, paprika, and turmeric — simpler and sweeter. Spike has a much broader herb and vegetable profile, making it more complex and savory. It's not a direct substitute; Spike is bolder and more aromatic.

**Q: How much should I use compared to regular salt?**  
A: Start with about half the amount you'd normally use of regular salt. Since Spike includes salt plus other flavoring agents, a little goes further. You can always add more, but you can't take it out.

**Q: Does it contain MSG?**  
A: No, the manufacturer explicitly states it's MSG-free. The umami flavor comes from natural ingredients like kelp, mushroom, and tomato.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a versatile, high-quality seasoning that'll make weeknight dinners taste like you actually tried. **Skip it if** you're watching sodium or prefer minimalist flavors.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A genuinely excellent all-purpose seasoning that earns its 4.9 Amazon rating. The minor clumping issue and high sodium keep it from a perfect score, but for flavor-per-dollar, it's one of the best pantry upgrades you can make.
