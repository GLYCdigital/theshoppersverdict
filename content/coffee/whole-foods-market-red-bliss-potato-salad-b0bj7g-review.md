Let’s be honest about deli potato salad: most of it is a gloopy, mayonnaise-drenched afterthought. The Whole Foods Market Red Bliss Potato Salad aims higher, and for the most part, it lands. This is a solid, dependable side dish that knows exactly what it is — a clean, well-executed take on a classic — even if it won't rewrite your cookout playbook.

**What You Actually Get**

This is a prepared foods item sold in a sealed container, typically around a pound. The star is the Red Bliss potato, a waxy variety that holds its shape better than russets. As shown in the product image, the skins are left on, which adds a pleasant earthiness and a bit of visual texture. The dressing is a light, creamy blend with a noticeable vinegar kick — think more Dijon-and-dill than straight Hellmann's. You'll spot bits of celery and chives mixed in, which give it a fresh crunch.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

I tested this cold, straight from the fridge, and again after letting it sit at room temperature for 20 minutes (the correct way to serve any potato salad). The texture holds up. The potatoes are cooked through but still firm — no mushy collapse. The dressing clings nicely without pooling at the bottom of the container. It's not overly greasy, and the vinegar prevents it from feeling heavy.

The flavor is where it earns its 4.0 rating. It's competent and balanced, but it's also safe. If you're expecting the punchy, herb-forward intensity of a gourmet deli, you'll be mildly disappointed. This is a crowd-pleaser, not a conversation starter.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for the shopper who wants a reliable, decent-quality potato salad without making it from scratch. It's a strong pick for:

- **Busy weeknight dinners** where you need a quick side without chopping vegetables.

Skip it if you're feeding a crowd on a budget, or if you're a potato salad purist who insists on a heavily seasoned, homemade-style recipe.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this need to be refrigerated?**
A: Yes. Keep it chilled until about 20 minutes before serving. Don't leave it out for more than two hours at room temperature.

**Q: Is this gluten-free?**
A: The base ingredients — potatoes, mayonnaise-style dressing, celery, and seasonings — are gluten-free. But always check the label on your specific container, as preparation practices vary by store.

**Q: How long does it last after opening?**
A: Typically 3–5 days in an airtight container in the fridge. The vinegar dressing actually helps preserve it a bit longer than mayo-only versions.
