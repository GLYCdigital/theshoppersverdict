## Installation

Installation couldn't be easier:

1. Remove the old filter from the water reservoir
2. Soak the new filter in fresh water for 15 seconds
3. Insert into the filter holder
4. Place back into the water reservoir
5. Run a brew cycle with water only before making coffee

The entire process takes about a minute.
