Let me start with the obvious: I'm reviewing a seltzer water on a page that says "coffee maker or coffee gear." That's the kind of category confusion Amazon sometimes throws at you. But here's the thing — if you're reading this, you're probably a seltzer loyalist who's tired of paying $8 for a 12-pack of LaCroix that tastes like someone whispered "lime" near a can of carbonated water. Polar's Cranberry Lime is a different animal entirely.

**What you actually get**

Twelve 12-fluid-ounce cans, each holding 0 calories, 0 grams of sugar, 0 sodium, and zero artificial anything. The ingredient list is exactly two things: carbonated water and natural flavor. As shown in the product image, the cans feature Polar's signature retro design with a bold cranberry-red background and a lime slice graphic — it stands out on a shelf full of minimalist seltzer branding.

**The flavor: this is not a shy seltzer**

Polar's carbonation is famously aggressive, and the Cranberry Lime lives up to that reputation. The first sip hits with a sharp, effervescent bite — noticeably more carbonated than LaCroix or Spindrift's lighter options. The cranberry flavor is tart and forward, not an afterthought. It lands on the front of your tongue like a cold cranberry juice cut with soda water, then the lime kicks in on the finish, adding a citrus brightness that keeps it from feeling heavy.

This is the seltzer you drink when you want to feel like you're drinking something. It's not a "water with a suggestion of fruit" situation. If you've tried Polar's other flavors — their Black Cherry and Pomegranate are cult favorites — this sits on the same intensity level.

**What buyers consistently say**

With 235 ratings averaging 4.7 stars, the feedback skews heavily positive. One reviewer put it well: *"This is the only seltzer I've ever bought twice. The cranberry is actually tart, like real cranberry, not candy-sweet. The lime makes it feel fancy."* Another noted the carbonation specifically: *"Polar is the fizziest seltzer brand I've tried. These stay bubbly even if you sip them slowly for an hour."*

Multiple reviewers also mentioned using it as a mixer — it works well with vodka or tequila because the tartness stands up to alcohol instead of disappearing.

**Where it falls short**

The tartness is a double-edged sword. Several reviewers described it as "sharp" or "sour," and one said it *"tastes like cranberry juice that went slightly off."* If you're coming from sweet sodas or mellow seltzers, this will be a shock. There's no sweetness whatsoever — no stevia, no erythritol, no sugar. That's a pro for clean eating, but it's a con if you're trying to transition off soda and need something with a softer edge.

There's also a packaging complaint worth noting. The cans ship in a cardboard tray with no plastic rings, and a few buyers reported dented cans upon arrival. It's cosmetic — the cans still sealed fine — but if you're gifting or stocking a pantry, inspect them on delivery.

Finally, the cranberry-lime combo is polarizing. You'll either love it or find it medicinal. There's no in-between, and Amazon reviews reflect that split.

**Who should buy this**

- **Seltzer purists** who want real carbonation and don't mind tart flavors
- **Low-sugar dieters** who need a flavorful zero-calorie drink that doesn't taste like artificial sweetener
- **Cocktail enthusiasts** looking for a mixer that adds flavor without sugar
- **LaCroix refugees** who are tired of "barely there" flavor profiles

**Skip it if** you prefer sweet drinks, mellow carbonation, or flavors like lemon-lime that play it safe. Also skip if you're sensitive to tartness — the cranberry here is genuinely sour.

**FAQ**

**Is this sweetened at all?** No. Zero sugar, zero artificial sweeteners. The flavor comes entirely from natural extracts. It's 100% unsweetened, which is why it tastes tart rather than candy-like.

**How does this compare to LaCroix's Cran-Raspberry?** Polar's is significantly more carbonated and has a stronger, more authentic cranberry flavor. LaCroix's version is more subtle and slightly fruitier. If you want intensity, go Polar.

**Does the lime flavor actually come through?** Yes, but it's a supporting note, not the star. You taste cranberry first, then lime on the finish. It brightens the tartness without turning it into a citrus drink.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a bold, zero-sugar seltzer with real flavor and aggressive carbonation — especially if you're tired of watered-down options.

**Skip it if** you prefer sweet or gentle seltzers, or if tart cranberry isn't your thing.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It loses half a star for the shipping dents and the polarizing flavor profile, but for what it is — a clean, crisp, genuinely flavorful seltzer — it's one of the best on Amazon.
