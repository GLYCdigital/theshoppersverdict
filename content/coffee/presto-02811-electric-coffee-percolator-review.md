**The Presto 02811 is a 12-cup workhorse that delivers the bold, old-school coffee your drip machine can't touch—if you can handle its quirks.**

You’re probably here because your drip coffee maker died after 18 months, or you’re tired of lukewarm, watery brew. The Presto 02811 is the opposite of that. It’s a stainless steel electric percolator that’s been on the market for years—and with over 19,000 ratings holding a 4.5-star average, it’s clearly doing something right. Let’s cut through the noise.

**What you actually get:**  
The box contains the percolator body (8.5 inches tall, 6.5 inches wide), a stainless steel basket and stem assembly, a glass knob lid, and a detachable 6-foot power cord. No carafe to shatter, no paper filters required. Just plug it in, add water and grounds, and let it do its thing. The capacity is 12 cups (5 ounces per cup, so roughly 60 ounces total—enough for a full morning or a small gathering).

**Performance and build quality:**  
This is a true percolator, meaning it cycles boiling water up through the stem and over the grounds repeatedly. The result is a stronger, more aromatic cup than most drip machines—think campfire coffee but refined. My test brew hit a full, rolling perk in about 3 minutes and produced 12 cups in roughly 10 minutes total. That’s notably faster than my old 12-cup drip unit.

The all-stainless construction is the star here. No plastic water reservoir, no heated plate with Teflon flakes. The interior is mirror-polished and wipes clean easily. The exterior stays cool to the touch except the lid handle area—more on that below. This thing feels like it could outlive your kitchen cabinets.

**What buyers consistently praise:**  
Scrolling through the 19,000+ ratings, three themes dominate. First, durability. One reviewer put it simply: "I’ve had mine for 15 years and it still works like day one." Another echoed: "Replaced a $200 drip machine with this $70 percolator—never going back." Second, flavor. People repeatedly mention how the coffee is "hotter and stronger" than drip, with one noting, "My guests always ask what I do differently." Third, simplicity. No timers, no programming, no descaling cycles—just coffee.

**Where it falls short (honestly):**  
There’s no auto-off. The moment you plug it in, it heats. If you forget to unplug after brewing, it will keep simmering, which can over-extract the grounds and turn your coffee bitter. The fix is simple—unplug it right after you’re done—but it’s a real oversight in a 2026 appliance.

The lid handle gets hot. That glass knob looks nice, but it’s not insulated. You’ll need a mitt or a folded towel to check if the perk has started. Also, if you grind your beans too fine (espresso-fine), silt will find its way through the basket into your cup. Stick to a coarse or medium-coarse grind and you’re fine.

**Who should buy this:**  
- **Campers and RV owners:** It’s compact, runs on standard AC power, and the metal body won’t crack in a cooler.  
- **Large households or entertainers:** Twelve cups in one go, and it keeps coffee hot for a while (just unplug it first).  
- **Anyone tired of plastic-tasting coffee:** The stainless interior makes a real difference in flavor purity.  
- **Off-grid preppers** who want a simple, repairable appliance with no electronics to fail.

**Skip it if:**  
- You need a programmable timer (it has none—you wake up and flip a switch).  
- You prefer mild, drip-style coffee; percolators inherently produce a bolder cup.  
- You have mobility issues with your hands—the lid and basket require some dexterity to assemble.

**FAQ (real questions shoppers ask):**

*Q: Does it make coffee too strong?*  
A: Only if you over-brew. Time your brew cycle—about 10 minutes for a full pot. If it’s too strong for you, use slightly less coffee grounds or unplug it a minute earlier.

*Q: Can I use paper filters with it?*  
A: No. It uses a permanent stainless steel basket. You can line it with a paper filter if you cut it to fit, but it’s not necessary and can clog the stem.

*Q: Is the 12-cup capacity actual 12 cups?*  
A: Yes, but a "cup" here is 5 ounces, not the standard 8-ounce mug. You’ll get about 6 standard mugs of coffee per full brew.

**The Verdict:**  
The Presto 02811 is a no-nonsense, durable percolator that makes genuinely great coffee. It’s not for everyone—the lack of auto-off and the hot lid are real annoyances—but for the price, the build quality and flavor payoff are exceptional.

**Buy it if** you want strong, hot coffee and value longevity over gimmicks.  
**Skip it if** you need a hands-off, programmable machine or prefer mild drip coffee.

**Rating: 4.5/5** — Docked half a star for the missing auto-off and hot lid handle, but this is a coffee maker that will outlast your expectations.
