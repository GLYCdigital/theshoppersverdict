**The Short Version**

Most fruit jellies are either too gummy (chewing gum texture) or too soft (melt in the bag). Sperlari nails the middle ground — a tender, almost jam-like chew that dissolves cleanly without that waxy residue cheaper candies leave behind. At 4.5 stars from 225 Amazon ratings, this Italian import has earned its reputation.

**What You Actually Get**

The 14-ounce bag delivers roughly 40-50 individually wrapped jellies (the wrapping is a nice touch for portion control — you're not grabbing fistfuls of sticky candy). As shown in the product images, the assortment features classic fruit shapes in lemon, orange, cherry, and mixed berry varieties. The packaging includes a functional zip-top seal, which matters more than you'd think — these jellies stay soft for weeks if stored properly.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

Sperlari's texture is the standout feature. These jellies use real fruit juice concentrate rather than artificial flavoring, and you can taste the difference immediately. The lemon has actual tartness. The orange tastes like the fruit, not orange soda. The chew is firm enough to feel substantial but yields easily — no jaw fatigue after three pieces.

Temperature matters. In cooler conditions (below 65°F), the jellies firm up noticeably and require more effort to chew. In warm weather, they soften and the sugar coating becomes slightly tacky. Either way, they hold their shape — no melting into a blob in your car console.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The overwhelming sentiment across reviews centers on authenticity. One verified purchaser noted, *"These taste like the candies I grew up with in Italy — not the artificial stuff you find in American grocery stores."* Another wrote, *"Finally, a fruit candy that doesn't taste like wax. The texture is perfect and the flavors are actually distinguishable from each other."*

Multiple reviewers specifically called out the lack of that chemical aftertaste common in budget gummies. The natural fruit flavoring carries through from first bite to finish.

**Where It Falls Short**

The citrus dominance is the most common complaint. If you're expecting a balanced assortment, prepare for lemon and orange to make up roughly 60% of the bag. Berry lovers specifically mentioned wishing for more cherry and strawberry pieces.

The sugar coating is the second issue. These jellies are rolled in granulated sugar, which sheds onto everything. Keep them away from keyboards and wear light-colored clothes at your own risk. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you toss a handful in your bag.

**FAQ**

**Are these suitable for vegetarians?**
Yes, they contain no gelatin. The texture comes from a plant-based pectin base, making them suitable for vegetarian diets.

**How long do they stay fresh after opening?**
With the zip-top sealed properly, they maintain texture for 3-4 weeks. Beyond that, they'll firm up but remain edible.

**Are they hard or soft candies?**
Soft jellies with a firm chew. Comparable to a firm fruit gummy but less rubbery — closer to a high-quality pâte de fruit.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want European-quality fruit candies that taste like actual fruit and don't leave that waxy film on your palate. The texture alone justifies the price.

**Skip it if:** You're indifferent about candy quality and just need something cheap to satisfy a sugar craving.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting half a star for the unbalanced assortment and the sugar-shedding issue. Everything else delivers exactly what a premium fruit jelly should: honest flavor, satisfying texture, and quality ingredients you can taste.
