**Is this the cleanest chip in the snack aisle, or just another overpriced bag of air? Here's the breakdown.**

You've seen the trend. Every brand is suddenly frying in "avocado oil" and slapping "clean ingredients" on the bag. JACKSON'S CHIPS actually walks the walk. But is that worth the premium? Let's dig in.

**What's actually in the bag**

The 5-ounce bag contains exactly three ingredients: sweet potatoes, avocado oil, and sea salt. That's it. No sunflower oil, no cane sugar, no "natural flavors" (which are never natural). For anyone who reads labels obsessively — and you probably do if you're looking at avocado oil chips — this is a genuine win.

**How they actually perform**

I've tested these against the usual suspects. The crunch is legit — audible, satisfying, and it holds up even after the bag's been open a few days. The chips arrive in one piece. That alone puts them ahead of half the snack market.

The texture is crisp without being brittle. They're not greasy, which is the big advantage of avocado oil over standard frying oils. The oil has a higher smoke point, so the chips don't soak up as much fat. You'll notice your fingers aren't slick after a handful.

Flavor-wise, expect a gentle sweetness with a light salt finish. The sea salt is understated — some reviewers noted they wished for more. One 5-star reviewer summed it up: "Perfect crunch, perfect amount of salt, and I don't feel guilty eating the whole bag." Another buyer was less enthused about the volume: "Great chips but way too expensive for what you get in the bag."

**What buyers consistently praise**

The clean ingredient list is the main driver for repeat purchases. People on keto, paleo, or Whole30 appreciate that this is a snack they can actually eat without breaking their rules. The avocado oil is a plus for anyone watching their omega-6 intake — standard vegetable and sunflower oils are heavy on inflammatory fats, and this is a meaningful swap.

The crunch consistency also gets consistent mention. Multiple reviewers noted the chips stayed whole and crunchy, which is rarer than it should be in the snack world.

**Where it falls short**

Two real issues. First, the bag is 5 ounces. That's smaller than a standard 8-ounce chip bag. You'll finish it in one sitting without trying. At the current price point, that's a premium cost per ounce. This is a "snack occasionally" product, not a "stock the pantry" product.

Second, the sweet potato flavor is dialed back. If you're expecting that bold, caramelized sweet potato punch you get from baked sweet potato fries, you'll be underwhelmed. The flavor is there, but it's muted. The avocado oil is clean and neutral, which means the sweet potato doesn't get much help in the flavor department.

**Who should buy these**

- **Keto/paleo/Whole30 folks**: This is one of the few chips you can eat without guilt. The macros work.
- **Parents who want better snacks**: Three ingredients you can pronounce. No hidden junk.
- **Chip purists who hate greasy fingers**: These stay dry and crisp.

**Skip these if** you're on a tight budget or you want an aggressive, in-your-face flavor. These are subtle, clean, and premium-priced.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these actually keto-friendly?**
A: Sweet potatoes are higher in carbs than regular potatoes — about 24g net carbs per serving. They're not strictly keto. But the fat-to-carb ratio is better than standard chips, and for low-carb (not strict keto) diets, they work well.

**Q: How do these compare to regular sweet potato chips?**
A: They're less greasy, less sweet, and have a cleaner finish. Standard brands often use peanut or sunflower oil, which adds a heavier mouthfeel. JACKSON'S feels lighter on the palate.

**Q: Are these good for kids?**
A: Yes, but note they're salted, not sugar-coated. If your kid expects candied sweet potato flavor, they'll be disappointed. For a savory snack, they're great.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you prioritize clean ingredients and are willing to pay for them. The crunch is excellent, the oil is legitimately better for you, and you won't feel like you just ate a bag of chemicals.

**Skip it if** you're looking for value or bold flavor. The bag is small, the taste is subtle, and cheaper options exist if you don't care about the oil quality.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Deducting half a star for the small bag at that price, and a bit more for the neutral flavor. But if clean snacking is your priority, this is one of the best options on Amazon.
