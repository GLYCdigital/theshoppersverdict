**The Short Version:** If you live with IBS or follow a strict low FODMAP elimination phase, this is the olive oil you've been searching for. Fody Foods has taken something as simple as extra virgin olive oil and given it the certification treatment — and it genuinely matters.

**What You Actually Get**

Fody sends you a 500ml (16.9 fl oz) bottle of single-origin extra virgin olive oil. The packaging is honest: dark glass to protect the oil from light degradation, a pour spout that doesn't drip (refreshingly rare), and a label that clearly states the Monash University Low FODMAP certification. You're not paying for fancy packaging — you're paying for the verification.

**The Certification Is the Real Story**

Here's what most shoppers don't realize: *all* pure olive oil is naturally low FODMAP. So why does Fody charge a premium? Because when you're in the elimination phase of the low FODMAP diet, you don't want to wonder whether the "olive oil" you bought is actually blended with garlic-infused oil or processed in facilities handling high-FODMAP ingredients. Fody's certification removes that anxiety completely. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"As someone with severe IBS, I don't have to think twice before using this. That peace of mind is worth every penny."*

**Taste and Performance**

I tested this oil three ways: raw as a salad dressing base, sautéed at medium heat, and drizzled over finished pasta. The flavor profile is distinctly *early harvest* — grassy, with a pronounced peppery finish that lingers pleasantly at the back of the throat. It's not a neutral oil. If you're accustomed to mass-market EVOO from the grocery store, this will taste noticeably more vibrant and complex.

At medium heat, it holds up without smoking or turning bitter — fine for sautéing vegetables or eggs. I wouldn't push it to high-heat searing; that's not what this oil is for, and the delicate flavors would be wasted anyway.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Is this oil actually different from regular olive oil?**
A: Chemically, no — all pure olive oil is low FODMAP. The difference is the third-party certification and single-origin sourcing, which guarantees no cross-contamination with high-FODMAP ingredients during processing.

**Q: Can I cook with this at high heat?**
A: It's fine for medium-heat cooking (sautéing, roasting up to 350°F). For high-heat searing or deep frying, use a neutral oil with a higher smoke point like avocado oil.

**Q: How long does a bottle last?**
A: With daily use for dressings and light cooking, expect 6-8 weeks. Store it in a cool, dark place — the dark glass helps, but avoid keeping it near your stove.
