Let's be honest: feeding a toddler is a battlefield. You want something that won't rot their teeth or pump them full of high-fructose corn syrup. They want something that tastes like candy and ideally features Elmo's face. The Earth's Best Organic Sesame Street Toddler Snacks sit right in that intersection — and with a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, they're clearly doing something right.

**What you actually get**

Each box contains six 0.7-ounce pouches of organic fruit snacks. The ingredient list is refreshingly short: organic apple juice concentrate, organic tapioca syrup, organic pear puree, organic strawberry puree, pectin, and natural flavors. That's it. No artificial red 40, no corn syrup solids, no "natural flavors" that are anything but. Each pouch delivers 100% of your toddler's daily vitamin C needs — which is a nice bonus during cold season.

**How they perform in the real world**

The texture is where these shine. They're soft enough that a 15-month-old with four teeth can gum them down, but firm enough that they don't turn into a sticky mess in a diaper bag. They hold their shape surprisingly well, even in a warm car. The strawberry flavor is mild and genuinely tastes like fruit, not like a chemical facsimile. One parent in the reviews noted, "My 2-year-old who refuses most fruit will eat these without a fight. They're the only 'healthy' snack she actually finishes."

The Sesame Street packaging isn't just gimmick — it's functional. The characters printed on each pouch give toddlers a sense of ownership. Several reviewers mention their kids specifically asking for "the Elmo snack," which makes snack time less of a negotiation. The resealable pouches are also a thoughtful touch, though at 0.7 ounces, most toddlers will polish one off in a single sitting.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the 240 ratings, three themes dominate. First, the clean ingredients — parents repeatedly mention feeling good about what their kids are eating. Second, the taste — kids actually like these, which is the whole battle. Third, the value for an organic product. **Where they fall short**

The portion size is the biggest complaint. At 0.7 ounces, these are snack-sized in the truest sense — a hungry toddler could easily want two pouches. That means the six-count box may only last a few days in a house with multiple kids. Some reviewers also noted shipping issues, with pouches arriving slightly crushed or dented. It doesn't affect the contents, but it's not the prettiest presentation. And if you're looking for variety, you're limited to a few flavor options — this isn't a big mixed pack.

**Who should buy these**

These are ideal for parents transitioning a toddler from conventional fruit snacks to something cleaner. They're also perfect for diaper bags, car rides, or any situation where you need a mess-free snack that won't stain upholstery. If you have a toddler who's picky about textures, the soft-but-firm consistency makes these a safer bet than gummy-style snacks.

**FAQ**

*Are these actually organic?* Yes — USDA-certified organic, which means no synthetic pesticides or genetically modified ingredients.

*Can these be given to babies under 12 months?* The packaging says "toddler," and the texture is soft enough, but the fruit juice concentrate makes them higher in sugar. Best to wait until after the first birthday and introduce in small amounts.

*Do these melt or get sticky in warm weather?* They hold up better than most gummy snacks. They'll soften but won't turn into a puddle, even in a hot car.
