## The Salsa That Actually Tastes Like Salsa

Most jarred salsas fall into two camps: watery tomato juice pretending to be chunky, or a mush that's been blended into submission. The 365 By Whole Foods Market Organic Thick & Chunky Medium Salsa dodges both traps. As shown in the product image, this is a jar packed with visible diced tomatoes, onion pieces, and flecks of jalapeño and cilantro — the kind of texture you'd expect from a fresh batch at a taqueria, not a shelf-stable grocery staple.

At 4.7 stars from 235 Amazon ratings, it's clearly earning its place in shoppers' carts. But let's dig into whether it's actually worth your money.

## What You're Actually Getting

The 16-ounce jar arrives sealed and shelf-stable, which makes it a solid pantry backup. The ingredient list is refreshingly short: organic tomatoes, organic onions, organic jalapeño peppers, organic cilantro, lime juice, sea salt, and organic spices. No corn syrup, no artificial preservatives, no xanthan gum holding things together.

The medium designation is honest. It's not a timid "mild" that leaves you wondering if there's any pepper at all, but it won't have you reaching for milk either. The heat builds gradually on the back of your tongue and lingers pleasantly. If you're a heat-seeker, you'll want to step up to their hot version — but for everyday dipping, this is the sweet spot.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The texture is the runaway favorite. One reviewer noted, "Finally a salsa that doesn't look like it came out of a blender — you can actually see and taste the chunks." Another emphasized the freshness: "Tastes like something from a Mexican restaurant, not a jar." Multiple reviewers mentioned using it as a topping for eggs and tacos, not just a chip dip — a versatility that speaks to the quality of the flavor base.

## Where It Falls Short

The sodium content is the most common complaint. At 230mg per 2-tablespoon serving, it adds up quickly if you're eating it by the bowlful (and you will). If you're on a low-sodium diet, this isn't your jar — look for a no-salt-added version or make your own.

The cilantro is also assertive. If you're among the estimated 4-14% of people who find cilantro soapy, skip this one entirely. It's front and center in every bite.

Finally, the opened shelf life is short: 7-10 days refrigerated. For singles or small households, that's a lot of salsa to work through before it turns. Consider freezing portions if you can't finish it in time.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this salsa actually organic?**
A: Yes, it's USDA Organic certified. The first ingredient is organic tomato puree, followed by organic diced tomatoes and organic onions. You'll find the USDA seal on the label.

**Q: How spicy is "medium" really?**
A: It's a true medium — noticeable warmth that builds, but it won't ruin your meal. Think jalapeño-level heat, not habanero. If you're sensitive to spice, start with a small scoop.

**Q: Can I freeze this salsa?**
A: Yes. Portion it into freezer-safe containers, leaving headroom for expansion. Thaw overnight in the fridge. Texture may change slightly, but flavor holds up well for up to 3 months.
