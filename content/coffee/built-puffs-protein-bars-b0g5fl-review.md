**BUILT Puffs Protein Bars Review: Marshmallow Texture Without the Candy-Bar Guilt?**

Let’s be blunt: most protein bars fall into two camps—dry cardboard bricks or sticky caramel traps that glue your molars together. The BUILT Puffs line tries to split the difference by wrapping a marshmallow-flavored core in a chocolate coating, and at 4.4 stars from 220 ratings, it’s clearly resonating with someone. But is it *you*? Let’s break down the texture, the macros, and the tradeoffs before you add to cart.

**What’s Actually in the Box**

You’re getting a 12-count box of 2.1-ounce bars. The star is the “puff”—a whipped, airy center that mimics a marshmallow’s chew without the sugar spike. The coating is a thin milk chocolate shell. Flavors in this specific listing vary, but the core lineup includes Chocolate Chip Cookie Dough, Salted Caramel, and Birthday Cake. Each bar runs roughly 140-150 calories, 17-18g protein, and 4-5g sugar. Notably, these are *not* meal replacements—they’re snack-sized, which matters if you’re used to 300-calorie behemoths.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The texture is the entire story here. The puff is genuinely light—closer to a Rice Krispie Treat’s density than a dense whey brick. The chocolate shell snaps cleanly, and the interior doesn’t stick to your teeth like a Tootsie Roll. That’s a win. However, the bars are fragile. The puff crumbles easily in transit, especially in warm weather. Multiple reviewers mention opening the box to find broken bars or chocolate that’s bloomed (that white, chalky film from temperature swings). If you’re a meal-prepper who tosses bars in a gym bag, expect some casualties.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The protein-to-sugar ratio gets real love. One verified buyer wrote: *“Finally a bar that tastes like a treat but doesn’t send my blood sugar on a rollercoaster. I eat one post-run and don’t crash.”* Another praised the texture: *“The marshmallow center is legit—soft, not chewy, and the chocolate is actually decent, not waxy.”* The portion control also earns points—at 140 calories, it’s easy to justify as a daily snack without wrecking your deficit.

**Where It Falls Short**

First, the sweetness. These bars are *sweet*—closer to candy than a balanced snack. If you prefer savory or earthy protein sources, this will taste artificial. Second, the price per bar is high compared to standard whey bricks (roughly $1.50-$2.00 per bar depending on sales). Third, the fragility issue is real. One reviewer noted: *“Loved the taste, but half my box arrived crumbled. I’d repurchase only in winter.”* That’s a legitimate logistics concern.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The sweet-tooth dieter**: If you crave chocolate but need to cap calories, this hits the spot without a 400-calorie candy bar.
- **The texture snob**: If you hate chalky or dense bars, the puff is a genuine upgrade.
- **The post-workout grazer**: 17g protein is solid for recovery, and the light texture sits well on a full stomach.

**Skip it if**: You want a meal-replacement bar, you hate artificial sweeteners (these use erythritol and stevia), or you live in a hot climate without climate-controlled shipping.

**FAQ**

1. **Do these melt in transit?** Yes, if exposed to heat. The chocolate coating is thin and susceptible to melting. Buy during cooler months or choose expedited shipping.
2. **Are they keto-friendly?** Not strictly—12g net carbs per bar. They’re low-sugar but not low-carb.
3. **How do they compare to the original BUILT bars?** The Puffs are lighter and airier. The original line is denser and chewier. If you liked the original, you’ll likely prefer the Puffs for the texture change.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely good-tasting, low-sugar protein snack that doesn’t feel like a chore to eat. **Skip it if** you need a sturdy, portable bar or you’re on a strict budget.

The BUILT Puffs are a solid 4.2-star product—great execution on texture and macros, but let down by fragility and a premium price. They’re a treat, not a staple.

**Rating: 4.2/5 (⭐⭐⭐⭐)**
