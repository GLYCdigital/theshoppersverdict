## Performance and Real-World Use

The cake arrives chilled and properly packaged. Multiple reviewers note it holds its structure well — no sliding layers or crushed berries upon arrival. The sponge cake is notably light and moist, not dense or dry like some grocery store cakes. The Chantilly cream is the star: it's rich but not heavy, sweet but not cloying, and it doesn't weep or deflate as quickly as standard whipped cream frostings.

One thing worth knowing: this cake needs to stay refrigerated. It's fine on a counter for a couple of hours during serving, but it's not a "leave it out all day at the picnic" kind of dessert. Plan to keep it chilled until serving time.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the price — this is a premium product. You're paying for Whole Foods' ingredient sourcing standards, and it shows in the taste, but it's not a budget option.

The freshness window is also real. This cake is best within 2-3 days of purchase. If you're planning a party a week out, you can't buy it ahead and freeze it — the berries and cream won't hold up well. You'll need to order it close to your event date.

Finally, berry tartness varies seasonally. A few reviewers noted that their berries were slightly tart, especially in winter months. The cream balances this out, but if you're expecting candy-sweet berries every time, you might be disappointed.
