Some products sell on nostalgia. Some sell on taste. The Anthony-Thomas Peanut Butter & Milk Chocolate Buckeyes sell on both — but they also sell on the fact that they're the official candy of Ohio State fandom. If you're a Buckeye alum, a Columbus transplant, or just someone who appreciates a proper peanut butter-and-chocolate ratio, this box deserves a look. But let's be clear: this isn't a novelty item. It's a legitimate confection that happens to wear a scarlet and gray jersey.

**What's in the box**  
You get a sturdy, foil-lined gift box — the kind that feels substantial, not flimsy. Inside are individually wrapped buckeyes, each about 1.25 inches in diameter. The packaging is clean and protective, and the Ohio State branding is tasteful, not garish. This is the kind of box you'd actually hand to a friend, not relegate to a desk drawer.

**How they actually taste**  
The buckeye formula is deceptively simple: a peanut butter center dipped in milk chocolate, leaving a trademark circle of peanut butter exposed on top. Anthony-Thomas nails the ratio. The center is dense and slightly salty — it tastes like real peanut butter, not a sugar-flavored paste. The chocolate shell is thin enough to crack cleanly, and it melts at body temperature without turning greasy. There's no artificial flavoring here, no waxy coating. It tastes like something a grandmother would make, if that grandmother happened to own a chocolate factory in Columbus.

**What buyers consistently say**  
Scrolling through the 225 ratings, a few patterns emerge. The most common praise centers on freshness — multiple reviewers note the buckeyes arrive soft and "just made" rather than stale. One reviewer put it simply: *"These are the real deal. Tastes exactly like the ones I got at the shop on Lane Avenue."* Another echoed the gift angle: *"Bought these for my dad who went to OSU. He said they're better than the ones from the stadium gift shop."*

**Where it falls short**  
Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, portion size: these are not jumbo buckeyes. If you're used to the massive, hand-dipped ones from a farmers' market, these will feel modest. For $25–$30 depending on the size you choose, you're paying a premium for the branding and the convenience, not sheer volume.

Second, shipping is a real issue in warm weather. The chocolate shell is thin, and multiple reviewers reported melted or misshapen buckeyes arriving in summer months. Anthony-Thomas does include a cold pack for certain shipping options, but it's not guaranteed. If you're ordering between June and September, spring for expedited shipping or wait for cooler temps.

**Who should buy this**  
- **Ohio State alumni or fans** — this is the definitive Buckeye candy gift. It's the brand sold at the Schottenstein Center and the Columbus airport, so it's authentic, not a knockoff.  
- **People who actually like peanut butter** — if you're the kind of person who scrapes the last bit of PB from the jar, this will hit. The center is genuinely peanut-forward.  
- **Gift-givers with a deadline** — the box is presentable out of the gate. No extra wrapping required.

**Skip it if**  
- You're expecting a massive, indulgent treat. These are refined, not oversized.  
- You're ordering for a summer event without temperature-controlled shipping.  
- You're strictly a dark chocolate person. The milk chocolate is sweet and mild, not bitter.

**FAQ**  

*How long do they stay fresh?*  
Sealed, they'll hold for about 2–3 weeks. Once opened, eat them within 5–7 days. The peanut butter center dries out faster than you'd think, so don't leave the box open overnight.

*Are they gluten-free?*  
Yes. The ingredients list shows no wheat, barley, or rye. That said, they're made in a facility that processes other products, so if you have celiac, contact Anthony-Thomas directly for their allergen protocols.

*Can I freeze them?*  
Absolutely. They freeze beautifully for up to 3 months. Thaw in the fridge for 2 hours before serving — this actually prevents the chocolate from cracking.

**The Verdict**  
**Buy it if** you want an authentic taste of Ohio State fandom that also happens to be a genuinely well-made candy. **Skip it if** you're looking for a bargain or you're ordering in July without a cold pack.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — loses a half-star for the shipping fragility and the modest size relative to price, but the flavor and authenticity are unimpeachable.
