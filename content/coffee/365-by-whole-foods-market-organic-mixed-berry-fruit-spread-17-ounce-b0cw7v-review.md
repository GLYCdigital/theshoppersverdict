Let’s be clear about something upfront: this is a fruit *spread*, not a jam. That distinction matters more than you'd think. The 365 by Whole Foods Market Organic Mixed Berry Fruit Spread is made with strawberries, blackberries, raspberries, and blueberries — and it tastes like it. At 4.7 stars from 235 Amazon ratings, it's clearly doing something right. Here's the full story.

**What you actually get**

The 17-ounce jar arrives sealed and shelf-stable. As shown in the product image, the spread has a deep, dark berry color with visible seeds and fruit pieces suspended throughout. The ingredient list is refreshingly short: organic mixed berries, organic cane sugar, fruit pectin, and citric acid. No corn syrup, no artificial flavors, no preservatives. That's it.

**Real-world performance**

This spread behaves differently than Smucker's or Welch's. Because it uses less sugar and no added pectin beyond what's naturally needed, the texture is looser — more like a compote than a stiff jelly. On warm toast, it soaks in beautifully. On a cold bagel, it can slide if you're generous with the knife. The flavor, though, is where it wins. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: "Tastes like actual berries, not sugar." Another noted, "My kids refuse to eat anything else now — it's the only one that doesn't taste fake."

The berry blend is balanced: raspberry tartness leads, blackberry depth follows, and strawberry sweetness rounds it out. Blueberry is subtle but present. It's noticeably less sweet than mainstream jams, which is a pro for adults and a potential con for kids raised on grape jelly.

**What buyers consistently praise**

- **Clean ingredients.** Multiple reviewers explicitly called out the lack of high-fructose corn syrup and artificial junk.
- **Versatility.** Beyond toast, people use it in overnight oats, as a yogurt swirl, in peanut butter sandwiches, and even as a pork glaze.
- **Value.** The 17-ounce jar is larger than standard 12-ounce jam jars, and at Whole Foods' house-brand price point, it undercuts most organic competitors.

**Where it falls short**

Be honest about the texture. If you want a thick, spoonable jelly that holds its shape on a PB&J and survives a lunchbox, this isn't it. The spread is runny — one reviewer complained, "It drips off the bread before my kid can take a bite." That's a fair criticism. Also, because it's low-pectin, you may see liquid separation on top after the jar sits in the fridge. A quick stir fixes it, but it's an extra step. Finally, the tartness isn't for everyone. If your benchmark is Smucker's Strawberry, this will taste sharp.

**Who should buy this**

- **The clean-label shopper** who reads every label and refuses corn syrup in any form.
- **The adult toast-and-coffee person** who wants a sophisticated, less-sweet spread with actual fruit character.
- **The home baker** who wants a fruit spread that won't overwhelm pastries or thumbprint cookies with cloying sugar.

Skip it if you need a stiff, kid-friendly jelly that survives lunchboxes without dripping, or if you prefer spreads that taste like candy rather than fruit.

**FAQ**

*Does this need refrigeration after opening?*
Yes. Once opened, store it in the fridge. It'll keep for several weeks, though you'll likely finish it faster.

*Is it actually organic?*
Yes, it's USDA-certified organic, and the berries are listed as organic on the label. Whole Foods' 365 line is generally reliable on this front.

*Is this suitable for a low-sugar diet?*
It's lower sugar than conventional jam — organic cane sugar is the second ingredient — but it's not sugar-free. One serving has about 8 grams of sugar, all from fruit and cane sugar. If you're strictly low-carb, this isn't it.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely berry-forward, clean-ingredient spread that elevates your morning toast or yogurt. **Skip it if** you need a thick, kid-proof jelly or you prefer aggressively sweet spreads.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — it loses a half-star for the runny texture and separation, but the honest flavor and clean label earn it a spot in my fridge permanently. For the price, it's the best organic mixed-berry spread on Amazon.
