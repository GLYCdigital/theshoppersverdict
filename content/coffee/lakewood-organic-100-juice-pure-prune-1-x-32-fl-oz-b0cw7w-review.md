**FAQ**

**Q: Does this need to be refrigerated before opening?**  
No, but store it in a cool, dark place until opened. After opening, refrigerate and use within 7-14 days. Because there are no preservatives, it will ferment if left out.

**Q: How much should I drink?**  
Start with 4 oz (half a glass). This is much more concentrated than standard prune juice. You can increase to 8 oz if needed, but most reviewers find 4 oz sufficient.

**Q: Is this safe for daily use?**  
Yes, but monitor your body's response. Prune juice is a natural laxative; daily use at higher volumes can cause dependency or discomfort. For most people, 4 oz every other day is plenty.
