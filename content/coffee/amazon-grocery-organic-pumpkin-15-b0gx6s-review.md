I tested this in three applications: classic pumpkin pie, pumpkin bread, and a savory pumpkin soup. The texture holds up. ## FAQ

**Is this the same as pumpkin pie filling?**
No. This is 100% pure pumpkin. Pie filling comes pre-sweetened and spiced. If you follow a recipe calling for "pumpkin puree," this is what you want. If it calls for "pumpkin pie filling," you'll need to add your own sugar and spices.

**Can I substitute this in recipes calling for fresh pumpkin?**
Yes, and it's actually more consistent. Fresh pumpkin varies in water content depending on the variety and growing conditions. Canned puree gives you reliable results every time.

**How long does an opened can last?**
Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate — it'll keep 5-7 days. You can also freeze it in portions for up to three months.
