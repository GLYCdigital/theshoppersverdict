Café Bustelo brings its iconic dark roast to the Nespresso Original Line with these aluminum capsules, and the result is predictable: fantastic coffee at a fraction of the price of the branded stuff. With a 4.8-star rating across nearly 1,800 reviews, pod drinkers have spoken.

## The Coffee

These capsules deliver a rich, bold shot that cuts through milk beautifully. At intensity 11 out of 13, this is serious espresso — one reviewer calls it "almost too strong for me, used as one shot," which is high praise from a self-described coffee snob who likes "really strong, strongly flavored coffee."

The flavor is unmistakably Bustelo: smooth, dark, with that signature roast depth that made the ground version a household name. The aluminum capsule construction makes a real difference here — it preserves freshness better than plastic pods and produces a more consistent extraction. Every shot tastes the same, which is exactly what you want from a pod system.

## Everyday Use

Subscribers love these. One reviewer has been on auto-delivery for months and "couldn't be happier." Another says they've "tried many, many brands, strengths, from various countries and types" and these are "REALLY good for the price and ease" — the whole family is happy with the quality and flavor.

The versatility is a bonus. They work for standard espresso shots and lungo pulls. One user specifically calls out making Lungo shots with these and loving the result. The 40-count pack is a solid volume for daily drinkers — subscribe and save for the best per-cup price.

## The Catch

If you're not a dark roast drinker, move along. There's no mild or medium option in this line, and no variety pack. The intensity 11 is also genuinely potent — if you like a lighter, more sip-friendly espresso, this might be too much for a single shot.

And of course, these are Original Line only. If you have a Vertuo machine, you're out of luck.

## The Bottom Line

For Nespresso Original Line owners who want bold, authentic espresso without paying Nespresso-brand prices, these capsules are a no-brainer. The flavor is excellent, the build quality is solid, and the value is hard to beat. At $24.98 for 40 capsules, you're looking at about $0.62 per shot — substantially less than the branded alternative.

**Verdict: 4.7/5** — Café Bustelo delivers its iconic bold roast in pod form without compromise. Rich, consistent, and affordable. Keep your kitchen stocked.
