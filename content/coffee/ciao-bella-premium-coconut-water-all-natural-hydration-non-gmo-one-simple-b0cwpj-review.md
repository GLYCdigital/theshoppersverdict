The non-GMO claim matches the ingredient list, which is refreshingly short. Each serving delivers the electrolytes you'd expect: potassium, magnesium, and sodium in naturally occurring amounts. **Real-World Performance**

The 4.4-star average across 220 ratings tells a consistent story. Buyers repeatedly mention the taste being "fresh" and "not overly sweet" — a common complaint with competing brands that add sugar or use concentrate. One verified purchaser noted, "This doesn't have that weird aftertaste that other coconut waters leave behind." That tracks with the ingredient profile; when you're only putting one thing in the bottle, there's nowhere for off-flavors to hide.

The electrolyte content is respectable for post-workout hydration or a hot afternoon. It's not a medical-grade rehydration solution, but for everyday use, it delivers what you'd expect from a clean coconut water.

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

The consistency stands out. Multiple reviewers mention buying this repeatedly and getting the same flavor profile each time — which is genuinely rare in coconut water, where crop variations can make batches taste noticeably different. **Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The price per ounce runs higher than generic store brands — you're paying a premium for the single-ingredient guarantee and the non-GMO certification. And a few reviewers mentioned seeing slight sediment at the bottom of the carton. It's harmless — just coconut solids settling — but shake it well before pouring or you'll get a cloudy last glass.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for you if you're someone who checks ingredient lists and refuses to drink anything with added sugar or "natural flavors" that aren't actually natural. **Does it need to stay refrigerated?**
Before opening, it's shelf-stable. Once you crack it open, refrigerate and finish within 3-5 days. That's standard for clean coconut water without preservatives.

**Is there added sugar?**
