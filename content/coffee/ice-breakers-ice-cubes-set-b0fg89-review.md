The set includes six stainless steel cubes, each roughly 1-inch square. They're solid — not gel-filled — which means no risk of leaking or weird chemical tastes. Here's the honest truth: these cubes work, but they work differently than regular ice. A single cube cools a standard 12-ounce cup of hot coffee to drinking temperature in about 3-4 minutes. That's slower than conventional ice, but the payoff is that your coffee stays at that temperature longer without becoming watery.
