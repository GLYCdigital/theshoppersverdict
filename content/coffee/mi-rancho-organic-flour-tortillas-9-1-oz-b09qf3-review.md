Let's cut to the chase. You've bought flour tortillas before, and you've been burned — literally. They crack when you roll them, taste like cardboard, or turn into stiff little frisbees after 30 seconds on a hot skillet. Mi Rancho's organic flour tortillas are different, and here's why you should care.

**What you're actually getting**

This 9.1-ounce pack contains six medium-sized tortillas (roughly 8 inches across). As shown in the product image, they come in a sealed, resealable bag — which matters, because these are genuinely fresh tortillas, not the shelf-stable kind that last for months. The ingredient list is refreshingly short: organic wheat flour, water, organic expeller-pressed canola oil, organic palm fruit oil, sea salt, and baking powder. No hydrogenated oils, no high-fructose corn syrup, no chemical dough softeners. If you're feeding a family and trying to keep processed junk out of their bodies, that alone is a reason to pay attention.

**How they perform in real use**

I heated a few on a dry cast-iron skillet over medium heat — about 20 seconds per side — and they puffed up nicely with those characteristic leopard-spot blisters you want. The texture is where these shine: soft and pliable with a slight chew, not doughy in the middle, not brittle at the edges. They fold cleanly for tacos, roll without splitting for enchiladas, and hold a generous filling without tearing. One buyer captured it well: *"These are the closest thing to homemade tortillas I've found at the store. They don't crack, they don't taste like plastic, and they're actually organic."*

Another reviewer noted: *"I've been buying these for months. They're consistently fresh — no dry spots, no weird aftertaste. My kids eat them plain, which says everything."*

**Where they fall short**

The honest truth? These are not burrito tortillas. At 8 inches, they're perfect for tacos, quesadillas, and small wraps — but if you're trying to wrap a massive California burrito, you'll need to double up or look elsewhere. The pack of six is also a limitation; for a family of four, that's one meal, maybe two. At roughly $4–5 per pack, that's a premium price for a staple. And while I didn't have issues, several reviewers mentioned that the tortillas can arrive slightly dried out if the bag gets punctured during shipping — so inspect the packaging when it lands.

**Who should buy these**

Get these if you're someone who actually tastes the difference between a real tortilla and a processed one. If you're making tacos, enchiladas, or quesadillas at home and want something that behaves like fresh-made tortillas without the effort of making your own dough, these are worth every penny. They're also a solid choice for anyone avoiding lard or animal fats — these are fully plant-based.

Skip them if you need massive burrito-sized tortillas, if you're on a tight budget and go through tortillas quickly, or if you prefer the longer shelf life of commercial brands. These are fresh products — they belong in the fridge, and they should be eaten within a week or two.

**FAQ**

*Do these freeze well?* Yes. Wrap the unopened pack in foil and store in a freezer bag for up to 3 months. Thaw in the fridge overnight, then heat on a skillet. Texture holds up surprisingly well.

*Are these vegan?* Yes — no lard, no dairy, no animal products. The oil blend is plant-based.

*How do they compare to raw tortilla dough kits?* They're more convenient and still taste great, but raw dough kits give you that fresh-off-the-comal aroma and texture. These are 90% of the way there with 10% of the effort.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want genuinely good, organic flour tortillas that taste homemade and handle beautifully in the kitchen. **Skip it if** you need giant burrito sizes or you're looking for the cheapest option per tortilla.

These earn a 4.6 out of 5 — knocked down slightly for the pack size and the occasional shipping issue. But for quality and clean ingredients, Mi Rancho is the best option I've found on Amazon.
