The product data says this is a coffee category listing, but that's a categorization error — this is Freedent Peppermint Gum, a 24-pack bulk buy of 15-stick Plen-T-Paks. ## What You Actually Get

The package contains 24 individual Plen-T-Paks, each with 15 sticks of gum. That's 360 sticks total. Each Plen-T-Pak is a slim, flat cardboard sleeve — easy to toss in a glovebox, desk drawer, or purse without taking up space. The packaging is utilitarian and dated; don't expect flashy design. But for a bulk purchase, the individual sleeves actually work well for distribution — grab one for the office, one for the car, one for your bag.

## The Freedent Difference

Freedent's entire reason for existing is that it doesn't stick to dental work. That's not marketing fluff — the gum base is formulated specifically to resist adhering to crowns, bridges, and partial dentures. If you've ever had a piece of Trident yank a temporary crown loose, you understand why this matters. The peppermint flavor here is straightforward and clean — it tastes like peppermint, not like mint-flavored candy. There's no weird cooling agent burn or artificial sweetness overload.

Texture-wise, this gum is softer than average. It starts out with a pleasant chew and stays pliable. It won't give you the same firm resistance as Extra or 5 Gum. Some people like that; others find it disintegrates too quickly.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The dominant theme in reviews is dental compatibility. One buyer wrote, "Finally a gum I can chew without worrying about my bridge. I've been through every brand and this is the only one that truly doesn't stick." Another noted, "My dentist recommended this and I've bought it for years — the bulk pack is the only way to go."

The value angle also comes up repeatedly. At 360 sticks per order, heavy chewers get roughly two to three months of gum from a single purchase. That's the real selling point here — this isn't about gourmet flavor; it's about having a reliable, dentist-safe gum always on hand without paying convenience-store markup.

## Where It Falls Short

Flavor longevity is the biggest complaint. Expect the peppermint to fade noticeably after about 10 minutes. If you're someone who chews the same piece for half an hour, this isn't your gum — you'll be reaching for a fresh stick long before you would with competitors.

The texture also isn't for everyone. The softer base means it can lose its structure faster, especially if you chew aggressively. A few reviewers mentioned the sticks breaking into smaller pieces during vigorous chewing. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

There's also the packaging — the Plen-T-Paks are functional but not resealable. Once opened, the gum can dry out if you don't finish the sleeve within a few weeks.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Dental work wearers** — if you have crowns, bridges, or partials, this is the safest bet on the market
