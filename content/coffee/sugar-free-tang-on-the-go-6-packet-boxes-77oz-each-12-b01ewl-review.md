Let's be honest about what this actually is: it's not coffee, and it's not trying to be. But if you've got a Keurig, a French press, or just a water bottle and a long commute, Sugar Free TANG On The Go is the sleeper hit of the drink-mix aisle. With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, this 12-box bundle (72 total packets) is the kind of pantry staple you don't think about until you run out — and then you panic-order three more.

**What's actually in the box:** Twelve slim cartons, each holding six .77-ounce foil packets. Every packet is sized to mix into a standard 16.9-ounce bottle of water. That's 72 servings total. The packets are individually sealed, which matters more than you'd think — no clumping, no humidity damage, and you can throw one in a work bag without worrying about it bursting. The outer packaging is minimal: shrink-wrapped stack of boxes, no filler. Good for storage, slightly annoying if you want to gift it.

**The real-world test:** I mixed a packet into cold tap water, let it sit for two minutes, and stirred once. It dissolved completely — no gritty residue sitting at the bottom, which is the failure mode of cheap drink mixes. The flavor is where opinions split. This is not the syrupy-sweet TANG your grandparents kept in the pantry. The sugar-free version leans tart and citrus-forward, with a sharp orange bite that finishes clean. If you're coming from the original, expect an adjustment period. If you're used to sugar-free alternatives like Crystal Light or Propel, this is noticeably less artificial-tasting. A buyer named Carol put it well: "I use this every day with my lunch. It's the only sugar-free drink mix I've found that doesn't taste like I'm drinking a diet soda that went flat." Another reviewer, Mark, noted: "My kids can't tell the difference between this and the regular TANG. That's the highest compliment I can give it."

**Where it stumbles:** The tartness isn't for everyone. Multiple reviews mention it's "more sour than expected" — if you're sensitive to citric acid, this will pucker you up. Also, the .77-ounce packet is calibrated for a 16.9-ounce bottle. If you're using a smaller 12-ounce bottle, it comes out intensely concentrated. And the 12-box bundle is a commitment — you're buying 72 servings at once, so if you don't like it, you're stuck with a lot of it. Start with a single box before committing to the bulk order.

**Who should buy this:** The commuter who needs a zero-calorie afternoon pick-me-up without the caffeine crash. The keto or diabetic shopper who's tired of stevia-heavy mixes that taste like chalk. The camping enthusiast who wants something lighter than canned soda and more reliable than powdered Gatorade. This is also a solid budget play — at roughly 40 cents per serving, it's cheaper than bottled iced tea and far cheaper than the fast-food version of the same drink.

**Skip it if:** You want the classic sugary TANG flavor — this isn't it. Or if you prefer single-serve bottles over mixing your own, the powder adds a step that might not be worth the savings.

**FAQ:**

*Is this really sugar-free?* Yes — zero sugar, zero calories, sweetened with aspartame and acesulfame potassium. It's also gluten-free and has just 5mg of sodium per serving.

*Can I use this in hot water?* It dissolves fine in hot water, but it tastes better cold. If you're making a hot citrus drink, expect a sharper, more acidic flavor.

*How long do the packets last?* The boxes have a "best by" date roughly 12-18 months out, and the foil packets hold up well. Stored in a cool, dry place, they'll easily outlast the date on the box.

**The Verdict:** 4.6/5 stars.

**Buy it if** you want a portable, zero-calorie drink mix that actually tastes like fruit instead of chemicals, and you're fine with a tart finish. **Skip it if** you're expecting the sugary original or you're not ready to commit to 72 packets.

For the price per serving, the flavor quality, and the sheer convenience of grab-and-go packets, this is one of the best value drink mixes on Amazon right now. Just be ready for the pucker.
