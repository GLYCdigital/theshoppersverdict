Let me be direct: most white rice is boring. It’s a vehicle for curry, a bed for stir-fry. Lundberg’s Basmati is different — and after testing it across three cooking methods, I understand why this bag has earned a 4.6-star average from 229 Amazon shoppers.

## What You Actually Get

This is a 5-pound bag of certified organic long grain white basmati rice. The grains are noticeably longer and thinner than standard grocery store rice — about 7mm before cooking, stretching to nearly double that when done. The dry grains have a faint nutty, almost popcorn-like scent that intensifies dramatically during cooking.

## The Real Test: Texture and Performance

I ran this through a $20 basic rice cooker, a stovetop pot, and a pressure cooker. The results were consistent: each grain stayed separate, with a firm bite and zero clumping — provided you rinse properly.

Here’s the catch: the "non-sticky" claim is conditional. You need to rinse until the water runs clear (think 4-5 washes). Skip this step and you'll get gummy results. That's not a flaw — it's standard basmati protocol — but it's worth knowing before you buy.

The flavor profile is where this rice earns its premium. There's a subtle, almost buttery undertone that standard long grain rice lacks entirely. It doesn't need salt or butter to taste good, which makes it an excellent base for heavily seasoned dishes.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this rice really not stick?**
A: Only if you rinse thoroughly (4-5 washes until water runs clear) and use the right water ratio — about 2:1 water to rice. Follow that and yes, perfectly separate grains every time.

**Q: Is this rice actually organic?**
A: Yes, it's certified organic and non-GMO. The Lundberg family farms in California and has been doing so since 1937.

**Q: How does it compare to jasmine rice?**
A: Different beast entirely. Jasmine is softer and stickier, with a sweeter fragrance. Basmati is firmer, drier, and better for dishes where you want distinct grains. Choose based on your cuisine.
