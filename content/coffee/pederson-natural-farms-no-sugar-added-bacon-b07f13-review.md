**The Bacon That Actually Respects Your Macros**

Most "no sugar added" bacon is a lie wrapped in marketing. Pederson Natural Farms doesn't play that game. This is the real deal for anyone tracking carbs, doing keto, or just tired of bacon that tastes like candy with a pork garnish. And with 229 ratings holding a 4.6-star average, it's not just me saying so.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The package arrives as a 12-ounce pack of thick-cut strips. Each serving (two slices) delivers 8 grams of protein, 5 grams of fat, and — here's the kicker — literally zero carbohydrates and zero sugars. That's verified on the label, not fudged with rounding tricks. The bacon is uncured, meaning no sodium nitrites or nitrates. Instead, Pederson uses celery juice powder and sea salt for preservation. It's humanely raised, pasture-raised pork with no added hormones or antibiotics.

**How It Cooks and Tastes**

I'll be straight with you: this bacon doesn't smell like a Texas smokehouse while it's frying. The smoke flavor is there, but it's restrained — more like a gentle hardwood whisper than a hickory punch. What you get instead is pure pork flavor, clean and uncluttered. The texture is where this bacon shines. These are thick-cut slices that hold up beautifully in a cast iron skillet. They crisp at the edges while keeping a satisfying chew in the center. No paper-thin shattering, no curling into useless bacon spirals. It also performs admirably in the oven at 400°F for about 15-18 minutes if you want perfectly flat strips for sandwiches or salads.

No preservatives means no forgiveness for forgetful shoppers.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this bacon actually sugar-free or just "no added sugar"?**
A: Genuinely zero sugar and zero carbs per serving. The pigs aren't fed sugar either, so there's no hidden source. It's verified on the nutrition label.

**Q: How does it compare to regular bacon for cooking?**
A: It cooks similarly but with less shrinkage due to the thicker cut. Expect about 8-10 minutes in a skillet over medium heat, flipping once. It holds its shape better than thin-cut commercial bacon.

**Q: Can I freeze it?**
A: Yes. Portion it into individual servings, wrap tightly, and freeze for up to 3 months. Thaw in the fridge overnight before cooking.
