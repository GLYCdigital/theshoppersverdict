## FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Does this honey need to be refrigerated?**
A: No. Store it in a cool, dark cabinet. Refrigeration accelerates crystallization and makes it rock-hard. Room temperature is ideal.

**Q: Why is it so thick and cloudy?**
A: That's raw honey's natural state. Unfiltered honey contains pollen, tiny wax particles, and crystallized sugars — all of which are nutrients and flavor compounds. The cloudiness is proof it hasn't been heat-processed.

**Q: Is this safe for infants?**
A: No. No honey of any kind — raw or processed — is safe for children under 12 months due to botulism risk. This applies to all honey products.
