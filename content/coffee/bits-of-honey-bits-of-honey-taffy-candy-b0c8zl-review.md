Let's be honest: most mass-market taffy tastes like sugar with a vague flavor ghost. Bits Of Honey is different. This small-batch honey taffy has earned a 4.6-star rating across 229 Amazon reviews, and after digging into what buyers actually say, the praise is specific and consistent. Here's the real story.

**What You Actually Get**

The bag contains individually wrapped, bite-sized taffy pieces. Each piece is roughly the size of a standard caramel square — perfect for portion control, which matters when you're eating something this moreish. The pieces arrive in a resealable bag, which is a small touch but genuinely useful for maintaining freshness over weeks rather than days.

**The Flavor and Texture Reality**

The headline here is the honey. This isn't honey-flavored candy; it's candy made with actual honey. The difference is noticeable. The flavor profile is warm and floral rather than cloying, with a depth that artificial honey flavoring can't replicate. One reviewer put it plainly: "Tastes like real honey, not the fake stuff you get in most candy. My whole family was impressed."

Texture-wise, this sits in a sweet spot between saltwater taffy and soft caramel. It's chewy but not tough, and multiple reviewers specifically noted it doesn't stick to dental work or pull out fillings — a real concern for anyone who's had a bad taffy experience. The chew is satisfying without being a jaw workout.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The recurring themes across reviews are authenticity and portion control. Beyond the honey flavor, people repeatedly mention the individually wrapped pieces. "Perfect for my desk drawer at work. One piece satisfies my sweet tooth without eating half the bag," noted one reviewer.

Another consistent point: this isn't overly sweet. Several reviewers with a low tolerance for sugary candy specifically called out the balanced sweetness. That's rare for any candy, let alone taffy.

**Where It Falls Short**

The most common complaint involves shipping in cold weather. Taffy is temperature-sensitive, and several buyers received pieces that were hard or slightly brittle. The fix is simple — pop the bag in the microwave for five seconds or let it sit at room temperature for an hour — but it's worth knowing if you're ordering during winter months.

Also, this is unapologetically single-flavor. If you want variety, this isn't your bag. You're getting honey, and that's it. Some buyers found the flavor too subtle if they were expecting the punch of artificial honey candy.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Honey purists**: If you appreciate real honey flavor, this delivers in a way mass-produced candy doesn't
- **Office snackers**: The individual wrappers make this ideal for desks, lunch bags, or car consoles
- **Gift givers**: It's distinctive enough to stand out in a gift basket, and the quality justifies the price
- **Anyone who's been burned by sticky taffy**: The non-stick texture is a genuine selling point

**Skip it if** you want variety packs, prefer intensely sweet candy, or live in extreme cold climates without the patience to warm it up.

**FAQ**

**Does this taste like real honey or honey flavoring?**
Real honey. Multiple reviewers confirmed the flavor is authentic, not the artificial honey taste you get from most commercial candy.

**Is this sticky or hard to chew?**
No. The texture is soft and chewy without being adhesive. Several reviewers with dental work specifically noted it didn't cause problems.

**How should I store it?**
Keep it in the resealable bag at room temperature. If it hardens, a few seconds in the microwave or a warm room will restore the texture.
