**Is Gold Medal's resealable bag a gimmick or a genuine upgrade? After 240 ratings averaging 4.8 stars, I put this pantry staple through real baking tests — here's the honest breakdown.**

Let's be clear about what you're getting: this is the same Gold Medal All Purpose Flour your grandmother used, now in a bag that actually closes. The 5-pound resealable option addresses the single most annoying thing about flour storage — the inevitable twist-tie failure that leaves flour everywhere. The zipper is substantial, not the flimsy press-to-seal you find on cheap snack bags. It clicks shut with authority and stays shut.

## What's in the Bag

One 5-pound bag of pre-sifted, bleached, enriched all-purpose flour. Nothing else. No scoop, no measuring cup, no storage container. The bag itself is the standard paper-plastic hybrid, but the top 4 inches feature a rigid plastic zipper track. As shown in the product image, the bag stands upright reasonably well when full, though it flops once you're below the halfway mark.

## Performance and Build Quality

This flour performs exactly as all-purpose flour should — predictably. At 11.5% protein, it sits in the sweet spot: high enough for yeast breads to develop proper gluten structure, low enough for tender cakes and pastries. I tested it across three applications:

- **Bread**: A standard white loaf rose beautifully, with a chewy crumb and golden crust. No surprises.
- **Cookies**: Chocolate chip cookies spread evenly and held their shape — no excessive spreading, no hockey pucks.
- **Gravy**: Lump-free roux every time. This is where cheap flour fails; Gold Medal doesn't.

The resealable zipper is the headline feature, and it works. I left a half-full bag on the counter for three weeks — the flour stayed dry, clump-free, and bug-free. That's a real win for anyone who bakes occasionally rather than daily.

## Where It Falls Short

The zipper has a genuine flaw: if flour dust gets into the track, it stops sealing properly. You'll need to wipe the track clean before closing — a small annoyance, but one that defeats the convenience if you're in a rush. Also, this is bleached flour. If you prefer unbleached for a more rustic texture or a cleaner flavor profile, look elsewhere. Finally, the bag lacks any measurement markings, so you'll still need your own measuring tools.
