**FAQ**

*Do these need to be cooked before using?*
No. They're fully cooked. Drain, rinse if desired, and add directly to your dish. For rice recipes, add them in the last 10 minutes of cooking to preserve texture.

*Are green pigeon peas the same as regular pigeon peas?*
These are the green (immature) version — firmer, brighter flavor, and better for rice dishes. The dried brown version requires soaking and longer cooking. Don't substitute them interchangeably.

*Can I freeze leftovers?*
Yes. Drain and freeze in an airtight container for up to 3 months. They thaw well and retain most of their texture.
