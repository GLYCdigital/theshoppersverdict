## Where It Falls Short

Let's be real about the limitations. French's is a mild, American-style yellow mustard — it's not going to satisfy anyone craving the sinus-clearing heat of a good English or Chinese hot mustard. If you're a Dijon devotee or a whole-grain snob, this isn't your bottle.

The ingredient list includes distilled vinegar, water, mustard seed, salt, turmeric, paprika, and natural flavors. It's not organic, and if you're hunting for a clean-label, small-batch craft mustard, you're shopping the wrong aisle.

One more honest note: the 20-ounce bottle is great for families, but if you're single or cooking for one, it might go stale before you finish it. Mustard doesn't spoil quickly, but it does lose its punch over time.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a parent packing school lunches, a grilling enthusiast who needs a reliable cookout staple, or someone who just wants a dependable, affordable mustard that tastes like childhood.

**Skip it if:** You're chasing complex flavor profiles, need a spicy kick, or prefer the sophistication of Dijon or stone-ground varieties.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Ask

**Is French's mustard gluten-free?**
Yes. The classic yellow mustard contains no wheat, barley, or rye ingredients. It's safe for most gluten-sensitive individuals, though always check the label for the most current allergen information.

**How long does an opened bottle last?**
Properly refrigerated after opening, French's maintains quality for about 12-18 months. The vinegar content acts as a natural preservative, so it stays safe well beyond that — just expect flavor to fade gradually.

**Can I use this in recipes beyond sandwiches?**
Absolutely. It's a workhorse in potato salads, mac and cheese, salad dressings, and marinades. The mild tang brightens dishes without dominating them.

**Buy it if** you want the gold standard of American yellow mustard — reliable, versatile, and consistently satisfying. **Skip it if** you're looking for gourmet complexity or serious heat.
