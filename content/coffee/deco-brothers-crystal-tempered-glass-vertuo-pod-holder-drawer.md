If you own a Vertuo machine, you already know the problem: those bulky pods eat counter space and vanish into dark cabinets. The DecoBrothers Crystal Tempered Glass Pod Holder Drawer solves that with something surprisingly simple — a clear glass top that lets you see all 40 pods at a glance.

**What you actually get**

The package includes the drawer itself (pre-assembled, no tools needed), a black plastic pod insert with cutouts for 40 standard Vertuo pods, and a clear tempered glass lid that sits flush on top. The frame is brushed stainless steel measuring roughly 13.5 x 10.5 x 2.5 inches. It's heavier than it looks at about 4.5 pounds — that's a good thing. It won't slide around when you pull the drawer open.

The selling point is the tempered glass. Unlike opaque acrylic organizers, you can see exactly what's inside without opening anything. The drawer slides on full-extension rails, so you get complete access to the back row. That matters more than you'd think — most pod holders are shallow trays where back pods become lost pods.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this with a mix of standard VertuoLine pods (the double espresso and mug sizes). The insert holds them snugly but not tightly — pods drop in and out easily without sticking. The drawer action is smooth and quiet, with no wobble or catch. The glass top sits securely but lifts off for cleaning.

One thing that surprised me: the build quality. At $30, I expected something flimsy. The steel frame has real heft, and the glass is legitimately tempered — it survived a dropped coffee mug without a scratch. The rubber feet on the bottom keep it from scratching your counter.

**What buyers consistently praise**

With 35,000+ ratings at 4.7 stars, the feedback is remarkably consistent. The most common compliments are visibility and capacity. One verified buyer put it plainly: *"I bought another pod holder first and returned it. This one actually holds 40 pods and I can see every single one. **Where it falls short**

The biggest limitation: Vertuo XL pods (the 7.77oz carafe sizes) don't fit. They're taller than the insert's cutouts. You can remove the plastic insert and lay XL pods flat, but they'll roll around. If you primarily drink XL sizes, this isn't your drawer.

The glass surface also shows fingerprints and dust. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you're particular about kitchen aesthetics, you'll be wiping it down regularly. A few reviewers mentioned the glass arrived with minor scratches — worth inspecting immediately and contacting support if you see any.

**Who should buy this**
