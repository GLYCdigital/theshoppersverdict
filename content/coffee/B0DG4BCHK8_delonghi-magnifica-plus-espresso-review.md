## De'Longhi Magnifica Plus Fully Automatic Espresso Machine Review

The De'Longhi Magnifica Plus sits at the top of De'Longhi's consumer espresso lineup. It promises barista-quality drinks at the push of a button — espresso, cappuccino, latte, flat white, and more — from freshly ground beans.

With a **3.8-star average from 382 ratings**, the reviews tell a more complicated story than the features list suggests. We dug into both the praise and the complaints to give you the full picture.

### Features and Design

The Magnifica Plus is a true superautomatic: it grinds, doses, tamps, brews, and steams milk automatically. The centerpiece is a bright **touchscreen display** that guides you through drink selection and settings.

**Key specs:**
- **Drink recipes:** 18+ one-touch options
- **User profiles:** Up to 4, with customizable strength, temperature, and volume
- **Grinder:** Stainless steel conical burr, 13 settings
- **Milk system:** Automatic steam wand with LatteCrema System
- **Water tank:** 60 oz
- **Bean hopper:** 8.8 oz
- **Puck container:** Holds up to 18 used pucks

The **LatteCrema milk system** is a genuine highlight. It automatically steams and froths milk to your preference, then cleans itself with a rinse cycle. The texture of the microfoam rivals what many home baristas achieve with manual steaming.

### Coffee Quality

When operating correctly, the Magnifica Plus produces outstanding coffee.

> *"I bought the De'Longhi Magnifica Plus as a transition away from the Breville Barista Express. The cappuccino was excellent. Plain coffee was fantastic."* — Verified 5-star review

**Brew quality highlights:**
- **Espresso:** Rich crema, proper extraction, good temperature control
- **Milk drinks:** Microfoam quality is excellent — creamy, smooth, latte-art-worthy
- **Profile system:** Each user can save their preferred strength, volume, and temperature
- **Grinder:** 13 settings cover everything from fine espresso to coarser drip

### Reliability Concerns

This is the Magnifica Plus's biggest weakness. The 3.8-star rating is below what you'd expect for a $1,300 appliance, and the negative reviews follow a pattern.

**Common failure modes reported:**
- **Milk frother detection fails** — machine stops recognizing the cleaning attachment
- **Water spouting issues** — leaks or inconsistent water flow
- **Full failure within 6 months** — stops brewing entirely
- **Grinder jams** — particularly with darker, oilier roasts

Approximately **4 out of 10** negative reviews cite quality issues. For context, most superautomatic espresso machines in this price range (Breville, Jura, Philips) hover around 4.2–4.5 stars.

### Verdict

The De'Longhi Magnifica Plus delivers excellent coffee with unmatched convenience. The touchscreen interface, user profiles, and automatic milk system are genuinely impressive.

However, the **3.8-star rating and recurring reliability complaints** are impossible to ignore. At this price point, you should expect both performance and durability.

**Who should buy:** Tech-savvy coffee lovers who want maximum convenience and are willing to accept some risk. Buy with an extended warranty.

**Who should skip:** Budget-conscious buyers, those who want proven reliability, or anyone who enjoys the hands-on espresso ritual.

### FAQ

#### Does the Magnifica Plus work with pre-ground coffee?
Yes, there's a bypass doser for pre-ground or decaf coffee. However, the machine is optimized for freshly ground beans from the built-in grinder.

#### How long does the Magnifica Plus take to heat up?
The machine reaches brewing temperature in about 30-40 seconds. It can pull shots immediately after heating, and the steam wand is ready within a minute.

#### Can I use plant-based milk in the Magnifica Plus?
Yes, the LatteCrema system handles oat, almond, and soy milk well. Some plant milks produce thinner foam than dairy, but the system adjusts automatically.

#### Is the Magnifica Plus loud?
The built-in grinder produces a moderate noise level — comparable to other superautomatic machines. The brewing process itself is quiet. It's not silent, but not disruptive for a morning routine.
