If you’ve only ever had grocery-store ginger snaps — thick, doughy, and vaguely spiced — Nyakers is a different animal entirely. These Swedish thins are the kind of cookie you dip into black coffee and suddenly understand why Scandinavians have built entire holiday traditions around them. At 4.6 stars from 229 ratings, the consensus is clear: this is the real deal, not a cinnamon-sugar imposter.

## What’s actually in the bag

You get a 7-ounce resealable pouch of round, wafer-thin cookies — about the size of a silver dollar. The ingredient list is refreshingly short: wheat flour, sugar, butter, ginger, cloves, and a few standard binders. No palm oil, no artificial flavoring, no preservatives. The aroma when you open the bag hits you immediately — sharp, warm ginger with a peppery clove note that tells you this isn’t playing around.

## How they perform in the real world

The texture is the headline. These snaps are brittle in the best way — a clean, satisfying *snap* when you break one, then a dry, crumbly crunch that dissolves slowly. The spice profile is bold but balanced: ginger leads, clove follows, and there’s a subtle molasses-like sweetness underneath. They’re not overly sugary — more like a crispbread with cookie ambitions. That makes them dangerously easy to eat by the handful, especially with something bitter to cut the heat.

I tested them three ways: solo, with black coffee, and with a strong espresso. The coffee pairing is where they shine. The ginger and clove stand up to dark roasts without getting lost, and the cookie’s low sweetness actually complements the bitterness rather than fighting it. One Amazon reviewer put it well: *“These are perfect for coffee — not too sweet, just spicy enough to wake up your palate.”* Another noted, *“I grew up in Sweden; these taste exactly like home. Crisp, spicy, and nothing like American gingerbread.”*

## What buyers consistently praise

Across the 229 ratings, three themes dominate. First, authenticity — Swedish buyers and travelers consistently say these match what they remember from local fika. Second, the crunch — multiple reviewers called out the thin, brittle texture as the standout feature. Third, the versatility — people use them for gingerbread cheesecake crusts, crumbled over ice cream, or just as a coffee-side snack. The resealable bag also earns repeated mentions for keeping the cookies fresh for weeks, which matters when the package is this big.

## Where it falls short

Let’s be honest about the tradeoffs. If you’re expecting a soft, chewy ginger cookie, you’ll be disappointed — these are unapologetically crisp. The sweetness level is also moderate; if your benchmark is frosted gingerbread, these will taste almost savory. And while the bag looks generous, the cookies are small — you’ll go through them faster than you think. One reviewer noted, *“Great flavor, but I finished the bag in two sittings without meaning to.”* Also, shipping can be a minor gamble in warm months — some buyers reported crumbled cookies on arrival, though Amazon’s packaging generally handles it well.

## Three questions shoppers actually ask

**Are these vegan?**  
No — they contain butter. Not suitable for a strict vegan diet.

**How long do they stay fresh after opening?**  
The resealable bag keeps them crunchy for 2–3 weeks if sealed properly. Past that, they soften but remain edible.

**Can I substitute these in recipes that call for American ginger snaps?**  
Yes, but adjust for texture — they’re thinner and drier, so they crush more finely and absorb liquid faster. Works great for crusts.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want an authentic, crisp, boldly spiced Swedish cookie that makes coffee taste better. **Skip it if** you need a soft, sugary dessert or have texture sensitivities to brittle cookies.

At 4.6/5, Nyakers earns its rating — it’s a niche product, but it nails that niche completely. For the price, you’re getting quality and authenticity that most mainstream brands can’t touch.
