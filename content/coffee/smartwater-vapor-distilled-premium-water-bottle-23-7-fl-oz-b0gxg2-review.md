## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides because they matter.

**Price per ounce**: This is premium water. You're paying roughly 2-3x what you'd pay for store-brand purified water. The vapor distillation process costs more to produce, and you feel it in the price.

**Missing minerals**: Unlike some premium waters that add back calcium and magnesium for health benefits, smartwater only adds electrolytes for taste. It's not a significant source of minerals. If you're drinking this exclusively, you're missing out on the mineral content you'd get from spring water.

**Environmental cost**: Single-use plastic bottles, even recyclable ones, are a sustainability concern. If that's a priority for you, this isn't the eco-friendly choice.

## FAQ

**Is smartwater actually distilled?**
Yes. The water goes through a vapor distillation process that boils it into steam, then condenses it back into liquid. This removes impurities without chemical treatments. Electrolytes are added back afterward for taste.

**Can I reuse the bottle?**
Technically yes, but the thin plastic degrades quickly. I wouldn't recommend reusing it more than a few times, and definitely not for hot liquids. It's designed as a single-serve bottle.

**How does it compare to regular bottled water?**
The taste difference is noticeable — it's cleaner and crisper with no plastic aftertaste. However, the mineral content is lower than spring water, and the price is higher than standard purified water.
