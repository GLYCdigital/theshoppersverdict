I'll be honest with you: when I saw a 5-pound tub of TWIZZLERS Twists sitting in my review queue, I rolled my eyes. Candy reviews usually aren't my lane — I test coffee gear, not confections. But after spending a week with this thing (and sharing it with a very opinionated office), I get the hype. This isn't just candy; it's an institution.

## What You Actually Get

The 5-pound tub is the headline here. That's roughly 130 individual twists, which works out to about 15 cents per piece at typical pricing. As shown in the product image, the tub is a simple, no-frills plastic container — nothing fancy, but it does the job. Each twist is the classic 7-inch length you remember from childhood, individually wrapped, which matters if you're buying this for a shared space.

## The Real-World Performance

Here's where TWIZZLERS separates itself from knockoff brands. The texture is spot-on: firm enough to snap cleanly but chewy without being rubbery. I left a handful open on my desk for three days — they stayed soft and pliable. That's not nothing; most licorice-style candies turn into brittle sticks within hours.

The strawberry flavor is assertive but not overwhelming. It's clearly artificial — let's not pretend otherwise — but it's the *right* kind of artificial, the one you've known since grade school. Each serving is 0g fat, gluten-free, and only 130 calories per 4-piece serving. For a candy that tastes this indulgent, those numbers are respectable.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be real about the downsides. The artificial strawberry taste is a dealbreaker for some buyers — one reviewer described it as *"fake and cloying"* and said they'd rather pay more for a natural alternative. That's fair. If you're someone who reads ingredient labels and cares about natural flavorings, this isn't your candy.

The tub also has a practical flaw: if you live somewhere humid and don't reseal it tightly, the twists will stick together in a clump. It's not ruined — you can pull them apart — but it's annoying. And the tub itself is bulky; it takes up serious pantry real estate.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Office managers** — the individual wrappers and long shelf life make this a no-brainer for break rooms
