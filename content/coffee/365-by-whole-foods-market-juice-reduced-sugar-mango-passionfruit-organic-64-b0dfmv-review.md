Let's be clear about what this isn't: this is not a coffee maker, and it's not coffee gear. This is a 64-ounce bottle of reduced-sugar mango passionfruit juice from Whole Foods' private label. If you clicked expecting a brewer review, you're in the wrong aisle — but if you're here because you want a genuinely honest take on whether this juice is worth your money, keep reading.

**What you're actually getting**

The 365 By Whole Foods Market Reduced Sugar Mango Passion Juice comes in a standard 64-ounce plastic bottle. The ingredient list is refreshingly short: filtered water, mango puree, passionfruit juice, citric acid, and stevia leaf extract. That's it. No artificial colors, no high-fructose corn syrup, no "natural flavors" hiding proprietary blends.

As shown in the product image, the juice has that pale golden-orange color you'd expect from mango — not the neon orange of artificially colored juices. One 8-ounce serving runs 60 calories with 12 grams of sugar. For context, a typical mango nectar from Jumex or Goya packs 25-30 grams of sugar per serving. This cuts that roughly in half.

**How it actually tastes**

The mango flavor comes through first — ripe, sweet, slightly floral. The passionfruit shows up in the finish with that tart, tropical kick that keeps the juice from tasting flat. The stevia is present but not aggressive; it adds a lingering sweetness on the back of the tongue that some tasters describe as "cool" or "slightly herbal."

One Amazon reviewer put it well: *"I was skeptical about reduced sugar juice with stevia because most taste like diet soda. This one actually tastes like real juice, just less sweet. My kids drink it without complaining."* Another noted: *"Great for mixing with sparkling water. Half juice, half seltzer makes a perfect summer drink."*

That's the use case this juice shines in — as a base for spritzers, smoothies, or cocktails where you want fruit flavor without the sugar bomb.

**Where it falls short**

The stevia aftertaste is real. If you're someone who can detect stevia in anything, this will bother you more than it bothers others. It's not the bitter, metallic aftertaste of sucralose — it's more of a lingering sweetness that doesn't quite taste like sugar.

The texture is thinner than full-sugar mango nectars. Mango puree naturally has some body to it; this juice is closer to a clear juice consistency. If you're used to thick, pulpy mango drinks, this will feel watered down by comparison.

And the shelf life after opening is short — 7 to 10 days refrigerated. A 64-ounce bottle is eight servings; if you're not drinking it daily, you might waste some. Several reviewers mentioned this, with one saying: *"Tastes great but I couldn't finish the bottle before it started tasting fermented. Buy the smaller size if you're solo."*

**Who should buy this**

This is for you if you're watching sugar intake but don't want to drink artificially sweetened diet juices. It's also great for parents trying to transition kids off sugary juice — the sweetness level is moderate, and the tropical flavor keeps it interesting. Mixers and smoothie makers will appreciate that it adds flavor without dominating the sugar count.

Skip it if you want a thick, indulgent mango nectar or if stevia is a dealbreaker for you. Also skip if you don't think you'll finish 64 ounces within a week and a half.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Is this actually organic?*
The product name includes "Organic" and it carries USDA Organic certification. The mango and passionfruit are organically grown, and the juice is processed without synthetic preservatives.

*Does it need to be refrigerated before opening?*
No — it's shelf-stable until opened. After opening, it must be refrigerated and consumed within 7-10 days.

*Can I use this in cocktails?*
Yes, and it works particularly well. It pairs with rum, tequila, or vodka. The reduced sugar means you can add a touch of simple syrup if needed without over-sweetening the drink.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want real tropical fruit flavor with half the sugar of standard juice, and you're okay with stevia as the sweetener.

**Skip it if:** You hate stevia, want a thick pulpy mango nectar, or can't finish a big bottle before it spoils.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, honest reduced-sugar juice that does exactly what it claims. It's not a replacement for the full-sugar experience, but it's a genuinely good option for everyday drinking.
