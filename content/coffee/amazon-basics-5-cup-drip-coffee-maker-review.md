## Verdict

The Amazon Basics 5 Cup Drip Coffee Maker costs $19.94. That's less than a bag of specialty coffee. And somehow, it actually works — reliably, consistently, and without drama. With a 4.2-star average from over 1,200 reviews, this is the budget coffee maker that punches well above its weight class.

## Who Is This For?

This is for anyone who needs a simple, no-fuss drip coffee maker and doesn't want to spend more than $20. It's perfect for a single person or couple, a dorm room, an RV, a vacation home, a small office, or even a baseball concession stand (yes, one reviewer uses it exactly for that). It makes 5 cups of coffee — figure 1.5 to 2 standard mugs — which is plenty for one person or a shared small pot.

## The Good

**The price is the story.** At $19.94, this is competing with the "I need coffee and have $20" crowd, and it wins. One reviewer called it "great value for the price" and said it exceeded their expectations. Another, who needed it specifically for using with a Ninja slushy maker for frappes, said "it does exactly what I wanted."

**Built-in reusable filter is a nice touch.** Unlike many budget brewers that force you to buy paper filters forever, the Amazon Basics comes with a reusable mesh filter basket. That's a small ongoing savings that adds up. It also comes with a measuring spoon, which is a surprisingly thoughtful inclusion at this price.

**Simple and compact.** This machine does exactly one thing — brew coffee — and does it without complication. The auto shut-off adds safety. The auto-pause lets you pour a cup mid-brew. The footprint is small enough for tight spaces. One reviewer called it "simple, compact, and works great for the price."

**Surprisingly capable.** Multiple reviewers note that the coffee tastes excellent for a machine at this price. One reviewer, who normally makes lattes with a manual pour-over setup, called the coffee "excellent tasting" and praised the brewer for making lattes easier.

## The Not-So-Good

**Quality consistency is a concern.** A small but notable number of reviewers report quality issues — units that don't heat properly, carafes that drip, or machines that stop working within weeks. At this price point, manufacturing tolerances are loose. Buy from Amazon so returns are easy.

**5 cups is really 2 mugs.** The 5-cup capacity is measured in the standard 5-oz coffee cup measurement, not actual mug sizes. Realistically, you're getting 2 standard mugs or 1 very large one. If you're serving more than two people, look elsewhere.

**No bells and whistles.** No programmable timer, no brew strength selector, no thermal carafe. This is a basic machine. If you want features beyond "put water in, get coffee out," this isn't it.

## Bottom Line

For $19.94, the Amazon Basics 5 Cup Drip Coffee Maker is exactly what it needs to be — a reliable, no-fuss brewer that makes decent coffee without any pretense. Yes, there are quality consistency concerns, and no, it's not going to impress your coffee snob friends. But if you need a cheap coffee maker that works, this is it. **A solid buy at the price.**
