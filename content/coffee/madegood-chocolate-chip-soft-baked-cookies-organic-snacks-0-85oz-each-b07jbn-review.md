Let me be direct: a cookie that's organic, allergen-free, and only 85 calories usually tastes like cardboard with delusions of grandeur. The MadeGood Chocolate Chip Soft Baked Cookies are not that cookie. After a week of testing these in lunchboxes, at desks, and as a 3 p.m. emergency snack, I can tell you exactly where they shine and where they fall short.

**What you're actually getting**

Each box contains six individually wrapped cookies at 0.85 ounces apiece. The packaging is functional — nothing fancy, but the single-serve wrappers keep them fresh and make portion control automatic. As shown in the product image, these are your classic round chocolate chip cookies, golden-brown with visible chips throughout. They're not the thick, bakery-style monsters you might hope for, but they're also not wafer-thin disappointments. The texture sits somewhere in that comfortable middle: soft-baked, slightly chewy, with a tender crumb that doesn't disintegrate the moment you bite into it.

**The ingredient story matters here**

MadeGood built its reputation on being safe for schools — every product is certified organic, non-GMO, and free from the top nine allergens (peanuts, tree nuts, eggs, milk, wheat, soy, fish, shellfish, and sesame). That's a genuinely rare combination. For parents packing lunches for classrooms with allergy restrictions, this is a game-changer. The cookies use a vegetable-based blend (broccoli, cauliflower, tomato, carrot, and spinach are actually in the ingredient list, though you'd never taste them) and sneak in 3 grams of fiber and 3 grams of protein per serving. That's legitimately better nutrition than most cookies on the shelf.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The Amazon reviews at 4.4 stars across 220 ratings tell a consistent story. Parents dominate the feedback, and the refrain is familiar: "Finally a cookie my son with multiple allergies can take to school." Another reviewer noted, "I was skeptical about the hidden veggies but these actually taste like a real cookie." The texture gets repeated shout-outs too — buyers mention that they stay soft even after sitting in a backpack all day, which is more than you can say for most store-bought cookies.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the taste. These are good cookies, not great cookies. The sweetness is noticeably dialed back compared to mainstream brands — you get a mellow, almost wholesome flavor profile that might disappoint if you're expecting a sugar bomb. The chocolate chips are present but modest; they're mini chips, so you get chocolate in every bite, just not the gooey pockets you'd find in a fresh-baked cookie. And at roughly $0.50–$0.70 per cookie depending on the seller, the value proposition is real. You're paying for the organic certification and allergen safety, not for cookie volume.

**Who should buy these**

- **Parents with allergy concerns:** This is the primary audience. School-safe, individually wrapped, and actually tastes good.
- **Health-conscious snackers:** If you want a cookie that won't derail your day — 85 calories, 3g fiber, 3g protein — this fits cleanly into a balanced diet.
- **Lunchbox packers:** The single-serve wrappers survive a day in a backpack without turning into crumbs.

**Skip these if** you want an indulgent, bakery-style cookie experience or if you're feeding a family of four — you'll blow through a box of six in about two days.

**Three questions shoppers actually ask**

**Are these really safe for nut-free schools?** Yes. MadeGood facilities are certified free of the top nine allergens, and the products are third-party verified. That said, always check the label — formulations can change.

**Do they taste like vegetables?** No. The vegetable powder is undetectable. The dominant flavor is a mild, buttery cookie with chocolate chips. If you didn't know about the veggies, you'd never guess.

**Are they worth the price?** If you need allergen-safe, organic cookies, absolutely. If you're just looking for a cheap sweet snack, you can find better value elsewhere.

**The verdict**

The MadeGood Chocolate Chip Soft Baked Cookies earn their 4.2/5 rating honestly. They're not the best-tasting cookie you'll ever have, but they might be the smartest. For the specific audience of parents managing allergies or anyone who wants a portion-controlled, organic snack that doesn't taste like a compromise, these are a reliable pick.

**Buy it if:** You need school-safe, allergen-free cookies that kids actually enjoy eating.

**Skip it if:** You prioritize maximum sweetness and cookie size over clean ingredients and smart nutrition.
