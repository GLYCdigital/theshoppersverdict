Let's be blunt: you don't buy a 27-pack Tic Tac box because you're low on mints. You buy it because you're tired of paying $1.29 for a tiny tin that vanishes in a week. This Mega Box solves that — but not without a couple of quirks worth knowing before you click.

**What you actually get**  
The box contains 27 standard 1.0-ounce Tic Tac tins, divided into three flavors: 9 Cherry, 9 Freshmints, and 9 Orange. That's roughly 8–9 weeks of daily carry if you're a moderate user. Each tin is factory-sealed with the standard flip-top lid — no shared air, no crushed pellets from shipping. As shown in the product image, the box itself is compact (about the size of a shoebox), so it tucks into a pantry shelf or under-desk bin without drama.

**Performance and build quality**  
Tic Tacs are Tic Tacs — the pellet texture is identical to what you've had since childhood. The real story here is the flavor ratio. Cherry is the standout: it's tart-forward with a sweet finish, closer to a cherry Jolly Rancher than the artificial stuff you find in cheaper mints. Freshmints are the workhorse — stronger than the standard white mint, with a cooling effect that lingers a solid 15 minutes. One reviewer put it well: *"I keep a tin in my car and one at my desk; the Freshmints are the only ones that actually kill coffee breath before a meeting."*

Orange is where the box stumbles. The flavor is there, but it's muted — more like orange zest than orange candy, and it fades fast. If you're exclusively an orange person, this isn't the pack for you.

**What buyers consistently praise**  
Scrolling through the 235 ratings, three themes dominate. First, value: *"I was paying $4 for a 6-pack at the grocery store. This is the same product at half the cost per tin."* Second, freshness: multiple reviewers noted the sealed tins mean no "stale" disappointment from a box that's been sitting on a shelf. Third, the variety ratio — 9/9/9 is a fair split, so you're not stuck with 15 packs of a flavor you hate.

**Where it falls short**  
The biggest gripe isn't the mints — it's the packaging. The outer box has no resealable closure. Once you tear it open, you're dealing with 27 loose tins that will scatter if you don't transfer them to a container. Minor, but annoying if you're buying this for office storage. Also, note that the Orange flavor is the clear weak link. If you're buying for a household where someone *only* eats orange, they'll be disappointed by the intensity.

**Who should buy this**  
- **The desk-drawer hoarder**: Keep one tin at work, one in the car, one in your bag — you'll never run out mid-afternoon.  
- **The host/office manager**: 27 tins is enough to stock a communal candy dish for months without restocking.  
- **The Cherry loyalist**: If cherry is your non-negotiable, this is the most cost-effective way to keep a steady supply.  

**Skip it if** you're a strict single-flavor buyer (buy a 6-pack of your preferred flavor instead), or if you want a resealable storage solution — you'll need to supply your own container.

**FAQ**  
**Q: Are these the same size as single Tic Tac tins?**  
A: Yes, each is the standard 1.0-ounce tin with about 50 pellets. No mini or sample sizes here.  

**Q: Do the Freshmints contain sugar?**  
A: They do — about 1 gram per serving. If you're watching sugar, the Cherry and Orange varieties have the same content.  

**Q: How long do they stay fresh after opening the box?**  
A: Indefinitely, as long as the individual tins stay sealed. Once you pop a tin, expect the standard 2–3 month shelf life before the pellets start to soften.  

**The Verdict**  
**Buy it if** you want the lowest cost-per-tin for a flavor variety pack and don't mind the lack of outer resealability. **Skip it if** you're a hardcore Orange fan or need something you can toss in a drawer without organizing. At a 4.6/5, this is a solid value for the Cherry and Freshmint lovers out there — just plan for the packaging quirk.
