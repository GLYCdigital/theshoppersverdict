Let's be blunt: most grocery store flour tortillas taste like cardboard wrapped in plastic. So when the 365 By Whole Foods Market Family Pack showed up with a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, I was skeptical. But after working through all 18 of these, I get it.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The family pack contains 18 flour tortillas, typically in the 8-inch size (verify the exact diameter on the listing before ordering, as packaging can vary by region). They arrive sealed in a resealable bag, which matters more than you'd think — more on that later. These are shelf-stable until opening, then they live in the fridge.

**The Ingredient Story**

What separates these from the competition is what's *not* in them. No hydrogenated oils, no artificial preservatives, no high-fructose corn syrup. The base is unbleached enriched flour, water, and canola oil. That's it. For anyone trying to avoid the chemical cocktail found in many mainstream brands, this is a meaningful difference that shows up in the flavor — clean, neutral, and slightly buttery without being greasy.

**Real-World Performance**

The texture is where these shine. They're soft and pliable straight from the bag, warming up beautifully in a dry skillet for about 20 seconds per side. Rolled around filling, they bend without cracking — something cheaper tortillas fail at constantly. One reviewer noted, "These are the only store-bought tortillas that don't tear when I make burritos. I've bought them three times now."

They also hold up for quesadillas and wraps. The downside: they're noticeably thinner than what you'd get at a taqueria. Load them with a pound of carne asada and they'll struggle. Keep fillings reasonable and you're fine.

**Where It Falls Short**

The thinness is the main tradeoff. If you want hearty, bread-like tortillas for massive burritos, these aren't it. Several reviewers mentioned this too — one wrote, "Great for tacos, but I need two for a proper burrito."

Storage is the other issue. Once opened, the bag's zipper seal works, but these dry out within 3-4 days in the fridge if you're not careful. Keep them in an airtight container or use them within the week. They freeze well though — separate with parchment paper and thaw as needed.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Taco-night families** — 18 tortillas means two dinners for a family of four
- **Clean-label shoppers** who want recognizable ingredients without a trip to a specialty store
- **Wrap and quesadilla enthusiasts** who prioritize pliability over heft

Skip it if you need restaurant-thick tortillas for oversized burritos, or if you only make tortillas occasionally — you'll likely waste half the pack.

**FAQ**

*Are these vegan?*
Yes. No dairy, eggs, or animal products in the ingredients.

*Can you freeze them?*
Absolutely. Stack with parchment between each tortilla, seal in a freezer bag, and they'll keep for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge.

*How do they compare to Mission brand?*
Cleaner ingredients and better pliability, but thinner. Mission is fluffier; 365 is more delicate.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want dependable, clean-ingredient tortillas for regular taco nights, wraps, or quesadillas at a fair per-unit price.

**Skip it if** you need thick, hearty tortillas for heavy fillings or only eat tortillas occasionally.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting a half-star for the thinness and the drying-out issue, but for the price and ingredient quality, this is the best big-pack flour tortilla on Amazon right now.
