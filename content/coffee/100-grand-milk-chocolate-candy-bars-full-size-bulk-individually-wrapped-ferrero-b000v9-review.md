**The 100 Grand bar is the quiet overachiever of the candy aisle.** It's not the first chocolate bar you grab, but it's often the one you finish and think, "Why don't I buy these more often?" This bulk pack of full-size, individually wrapped bars gives you 24 of them in one shot — and at 4.6 stars from 229 Amazon ratings, I'm not the only one who feels that way.

Here's what you actually get in the box: 24 full-size 1.5-ounce bars, each individually wrapped in the classic red and gold foil. These are the regular grocery store size, not the miniatures you hand out at Halloween. That distinction matters — the full-size format gives you the proper ratio of chocolate to caramel to crispy rice. The mini versions skew too heavy on chocolate and lose the textural balance.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The construction is straightforward: a milk chocolate shell wrapped around a chewy caramel core studded with crispy rice pieces. The chocolate is Ferrero's standard milk chocolate — creamy, sweet, not particularly complex, but reliably smooth. The caramel is the star here. It's dense and chewy, with the crisped rice adding a satisfying crunch that breaks up the sweetness.

One thing buyers consistently mention is how well these hold up in shipping. One reviewer noted, "Ordered these for a classroom reward stash — every single bar arrived intact, no melted chocolate, no crushed caramel." That's not nothing, given how many candy shipments arrive as a melted mess. The individually wrapped format also means you can toss a bar in a backpack or lunchbox without worrying about it sticking to everything else.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The freshness factor comes up repeatedly. "These taste significantly fresher than the ones I buy at the checkout lane," one verified purchase reads. Another buyer who ordered for an office candy dish said, "They're gone in two days. People specifically reach for these over the Snickers and KitKats." The crispy rice texture seems to be the differentiator — it holds up better than the wafer in a KitKat and doesn't get stale the way peanut-based bars can.

**Where it falls short**

Temperature sensitivity is the real issue. If your storage area runs warm — above 70°F — the chocolate develops a whitish bloom. It's harmless but looks unappealing, and it changes the texture slightly. Several reviewers mentioned this happening during summer months. The fix is simple: store them somewhere cool, but it's worth knowing before you order 24 bars in July.

There's also no meaningful price break versus buying these individually at a store. You're paying for convenience — the bulk format, the shipping, the fact that you don't have to make a separate trip. If you're looking for a deep discount, you won't find it here.
