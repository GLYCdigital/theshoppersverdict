Let's be blunt: most in-shell walnuts you buy online are a gamble. Either they're stale, rancid, or so brittle they shatter into dust when you crack them. The Smarty Stop Raw Walnuts in Shell, at 4.5 stars across 225 ratings, claims to do better. After handling these, I believe it — mostly.

**What you actually get**

The 2-pound bag is straightforward. No frills, no fancy packaging. Just walnuts in their natural state. As shown in the product image, the shells are a light tan, clean, and relatively uniform in size — running about 1.5 to 2 inches in length. That consistency matters more than you'd think, because it means your nutcracker actually works the same way on every single piece.

**Cracking experience and kernel quality**

Here's where these shine. The shells crack with a satisfying, predictable snap — not the frustrating crumble you get with older stock. Most walnuts come out in near-perfect halves, which is rare for in-shell product. The kernels are plump, pale golden, and taste distinctly fresh. There's none of that bitter, papery undertone that signals oxidation.

One buyer put it simply: *"Finally, walnuts that don't taste like they've been sitting in a warehouse for a year. Fresh and sweet."* That tracks with my experience. The moisture content is right — not so dry they crumble, not so damp they feel rubbery.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, you'll get some casualties. A few shells in every bag arrive cracked or chipped, which means fragments of shell mixed in with your kernels. It's not a dealbreaker, but plan to pick through them.

Second, and this is the bigger issue: the bag isn't resealable. Once you open it, you're committed to either eating a lot of walnuts quickly or transferring them to an airtight container. In-shell walnuts stay fresher longer than shelled, but only if stored properly. Skip the transfer and you'll notice them going stale within a few weeks.

**Who should buy these**

If you're a serious baker who goes through nuts quickly — say, for baklava, banana bread, or walnut pesto — these are a strong value. You get the freshness advantage of in-shell without paying artisan prices. Same if you enjoy the ritual of cracking nuts as a snack or appetizer, especially around the holidays.

If you're looking for convenience — shelled, ready-to-eat walnuts for salads or snacking — these aren't for you. The cracking takes time, and you'll deal with shell fragments. Also, if you're feeding a crowd, the 2-pound bag might feel small. There's a 5-pound option, but this review covers the 2-pounder specifically.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Q: Are these walnuts actually raw and unsalted?**
A: Yes. No roasting, no salt, no oil. Just raw walnuts in their natural shells. The flavor is mild and slightly sweet, which is what you want for baking.

**Q: How long do they stay fresh?**
A: In a cool, dry pantry, unopened, they'll hold for several months. Once opened, transfer them to an airtight container — they'll keep for 3-4 weeks at room temperature, longer in the fridge.

**Q: Are there any allergy concerns?**
A: Yes — these are tree nuts. The packaging notes they may contain traces of other tree nuts and peanuts due to shared processing. If you have severe nut allergies, exercise caution.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want genuinely fresh, crackable walnuts for baking or snacking and don't mind the hands-on effort.

**Skip it if** you need convenience, hate shell fragments, or only use walnuts occasionally — you'll lose the freshness advantage before you finish the bag.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Fresh, consistent, and fairly priced, with minor packaging and shell-fragment issues holding it back from perfection. Worth your money if you're willing to crack your own.
