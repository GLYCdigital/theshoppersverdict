**The Original Donut Shop Chocolate Glazed Donut K-Cup Review: Worth the Hype or Just Gimmick?**

Let's be honest: flavored coffee pods are a gamble. Half the time you get a coffee that tastes like a melted candle, the other half you get something that barely tastes like coffee at all. The Original Donut Shop's Chocolate Glazed Donut K-Cups land somewhere in the sweet spot — and that's exactly why 220 Amazon reviewers have pushed this to a 4.4-star average.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The box contains 24 standard Keurig-compatible K-Cups, each holding 0.39 ounces of ground coffee. The roast is medium, and the flavor profile is described as "chocolate glazed donut" — which translates to a chocolate-forward coffee with a sweet, bakery-like finish. As shown in the product image, the packaging is consistent with the rest of The Original Donut Shop line: bright, playful, and clear about what's inside. No surprises there.

**How It Performs in Real Life**

I brewed this at both the 6oz and 8oz settings on a standard Keurig 2.0. At 6oz, you get the fullest chocolate expression — think a mild mocha without the milk. At 8oz, the coffee character comes forward more, and the chocolate recedes to a background note. Neither setting produced a bitter or burnt cup, which is a genuine win for a flavored pod in this price range.

The texture is smooth, not watery. There's a slight oily sheen on top, which is typical for flavored coffees — that's the flavoring oils doing their job. The finish is where you'll notice the "glazed donut" part: a subtle sweetness that lingers, almost like a powdered sugar aftertaste. It's pleasant, not cloying.

**What Buyers Keep Saying**

Scrolling through the reviews, a few themes come up repeatedly. One buyer put it simply: "Finally a flavored coffee that actually tastes like coffee." Another noted, "I've repurchased these four times. They're my everyday morning cup." The most common praise centers on the balance — the chocolate is detectable but not overwhelming. Multiple reviewers compare it favorably to more expensive Dunkin or Starbucks flavored pods, noting you get similar quality for less per cup.

**Where It Falls Short**

Now the honest part. The chocolate flavor is more aroma than taste. If you're expecting a rich, fudgy dessert coffee, you'll be disappointed. This is a medium roast with chocolate *notes*, not a chocolate-flavored coffee. The sweetness is also worth flagging: there's a distinct sugary undertone even when brewed black. If you prefer zero sweetness in your coffee, skip this one.

The flavored oils can also leave a residue in your mug. A quick rinse with hot water handles it, but it's worth knowing if you're switching between this and a regular unflavored pod throughout the day.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is an easy recommendation if you fall into any of these groups:

- **Dessert-coffee lovers** who want a daily cup that feels like a treat without the calories of a mocha
- **Keurig owners on a budget** who are tired of paying premium prices for flavored pods
- **Anyone transitioning from sugary coffee drinks** to black coffee — this is a great middle ground

Skip it if you're a black-coffee purist, or if you want an intense chocolate bomb. This isn't that.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this coffee sweetened?**
A: No sugar is added, but there's a natural sweetness from the flavoring. It's not sugar-free-syrup sweet, but it's noticeably sweeter than unflavored coffee.

**Q: Does it work in all Keurig models?**
A: Yes, standard K-Cups work in all Keurig 1.0 and 2.0 brewers. It also works in most other single-serve machines that accept K-Cup pods.

**Q: How does it compare to the regular Original Donut Shop coffee?**
A: The base coffee is the same medium roast. The chocolate glazed version adds a cocoa note and sweetness you won't find in the original. If you like the original, you'll likely enjoy this; if you found the original too plain, the chocolate version adds enough interest.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a reliably good flavored coffee that doesn't taste artificial, at a price that won't make you wince.

**Skip it if:** You demand intense flavor punch, or you drink your coffee strictly black and unsweetened.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, repeatable purchase for flavored-coffee fans. It won't change your life, but it'll make your mornings a little more pleasant.
