## The cracker that thinks it's a chip

Most "healthy" crackers taste like cardboard with ambition. The Craize Thin & Crunchy Toasted Corn Crackers in Sweet Corn flavor aren't that. These are wafer-thin, intensely crispy squares that genuinely deliver on the "toasted corn" promise — think the texture of a high-end tortilla chip but lighter, with a flavor that's closer to roasted corn kernels than anything from a standard chip aisle.

As shown in the product image, these come in a slim, resealable bag with a clean, minimalist design — the kind of packaging that tells you the brand is betting on the product, not the marketing. The crackers themselves are about 2 inches square, uniformly thin, and studded with visible corn bits.

## What you actually get

One 4-ounce bag contains roughly 40–50 crackers, depending on breakage. That's the first honest caveat: these are fragile. Multiple reviewers note the "crumbs at the bottom of the bag" problem, and one customer put it plainly: "Love the taste, but half the bag was broken pieces by the time it arrived." Budget for that.

The ingredient list is refreshingly short: stone-ground corn, high oleic sunflower oil, sea salt, and natural sweet corn flavor. No preservatives, no weird stabilizers, no palm oil. That's a genuine plus for anyone scanning labels.

## Real-world performance

**Texture:** This is where Craize wins. The crackers are thin enough to snap cleanly but sturdy enough to scoop hummus, guacamole, or a chunky salsa without disintegrating. The "toasted" flavor comes through distinctly — there's a nutty, slightly charred note that elevates them beyond plain corn.

**Flavor:** Here's the honest tension. The "sweet corn" moniker isn't decorative. These are noticeably sweet — closer to a honey-roasted corn snack than a savory tortilla chip. Some shoppers love it. One verified buyer said, "Tastes like roasted corn with a hint of sweetness — perfect with salsa." Others found it off-putting: "Too sweet for my taste. Expected savory, got dessert-ish."

**Use cases:** They shine as a standalone snack, work well crumbled over salads for crunch, and pair surprisingly well with soft cheeses or white bean dips. They're less ideal with heavy, spicy salsas where the sweetness clashes.

## Where it falls short

Beyond the fragility and sweetness, the price is the third real issue. At roughly $1 per ounce, you're paying premium prices for a snack that disappears fast — the thin texture makes them easy to polish off in one sitting. If you're feeding a family, this is not an economical choice.

## Who should buy this

- **Gluten-free eaters** who want a chip-adjacent snack that doesn't taste like a substitute
- **Clean-label shoppers** who actually read ingredient lists and care about non-GMO, vegan, and minimal processing
- **Snackers who prefer sweet-salty profiles** — think kettle corn fans, not classic tortilla chip loyalists

**Skip it if** you want a classic savory chip, need something sturdy for heavy dips, or you're feeding more than two people on a budget.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these actually gluten-free?**
Yes, they're made from stone-ground corn only — no wheat, barley, or rye. The facility processes corn exclusively, and the brand is certified gluten-free.

**Q: How do they compare to corn tortilla chips?**
They're thinner, lighter, and sweeter. Texture is closer to a rice cracker than a tortilla chip. They don't hold up as well to heavy toppings but are superior for light dips and snacking.

**Q: Do they taste strongly of corn?**
Yes, but it's a roasted, toasted corn flavor — not the raw, starchy taste of uncooked masa. The sweetness is the dominant first impression, though.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want an unusually crispy, clean-ingredient snack that works for gluten-free, vegan, or clean-eating lifestyles and you enjoy a sweet-salty flavor profile.

**Skip it if** you're expecting a savory chip substitute, need something durable for party dips, or you're on a tight snack budget — the price-per-ounce and fragility make it a specialty purchase, not a pantry staple.
