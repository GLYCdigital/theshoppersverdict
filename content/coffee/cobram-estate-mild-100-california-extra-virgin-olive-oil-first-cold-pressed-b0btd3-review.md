**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Is this oil really from California?**
A: Yes, 100%. It's certified by the California Olive Oil Council, which enforces stricter freshness and purity standards than most imported oils. The harvest date is printed on the bottle, so you can verify how fresh it is.

**Q: How does "Mild" compare to their other varieties?**
A: Mild has lower bitterness and pungency—think gentle, buttery, and grassy. Their "Robust" is more peppery with a stronger throat burn. If you're new to quality EVOO, start with Mild.

**Q: Can I cook with this at high heat?**
A: Moderate heat only. It's perfect for sautéing vegetables or roasting at up to 400°F, but for high-heat searing or frying, use a neutral oil like avocado or canola to avoid burning and off-flavors.
