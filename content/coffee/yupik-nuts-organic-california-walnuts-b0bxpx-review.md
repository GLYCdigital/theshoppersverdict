## What You Actually Get

The package is a simple, clear resealable pouch — nothing fancy, but functional. Inside, you get 2.2 pounds (1 kg) of raw, organic California walnut halves and pieces. That's roughly double what a standard supermarket jar holds, at a better per-ounce price if you're buying in bulk. As shown in the product image, the walnuts are light golden-brown, which is a good sign — darker coloring often indicates older stock.

## Real-World Performance

Freshness is the make-or-break factor with walnuts, and these deliver. Across multiple batches, the flavor is consistently sweet and buttery with no trace of the bitterness that signals rancidity. That's a legitimate concern with bulk nuts, and Yupik handles it well. The texture holds up too — they're crunchy, not chewy or stale.

One caveat: the "halves and pieces" label isn't kidding. Some bags lean heavily toward small fragments, which is fine for baking and oatmeal but disappointing if you want pretty halves for a charcuterie board or salad topping. If presentation matters, budget for that inconsistency.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The freshness theme dominates the feedback. One reviewer put it simply: "These are the freshest walnuts I've had from Amazon — no bitter aftertaste at all." Another noted, "Great for my morning yogurt, and the bag lasts me over a month." The organic certification and clean ingredient list (just walnuts, nothing else) also get repeated shout-outs from health-conscious shoppers.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest practical gripe is the packaging. The resealable zipper is thin and can fail after a few uses — and once that happens, the flimsy bag doesn't protect the walnuts from going stale. My advice: transfer them to an airtight glass jar or freezer bag immediately. They'll keep for months in the fridge or freezer.

Also, there's no harvest date on the label. That's a transparency miss for a product where freshness is the entire game. You're trusting the supply chain, and while Yupik has been solid, it's worth knowing.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You bake regularly, add walnuts to salads or oatmeal, or want a cost-effective organic option for smoothies. The 2.2-pound size is ideal for households that go through nuts within 6–8 weeks.

**Skip it if:** You need uniform, picture-perfect walnut halves for elegant desserts or gifts. This is a working ingredient, not a premium presentation piece. Also skip if you don't have proper storage — without airtight containment, you'll waste half the bag.

## FAQ

**Are these walnuts truly raw and unsalted?**
Yes. The ingredient list is just one item: organic walnuts. No oil, no salt, no preservatives. They're raw, so they're suitable for baking, roasting yourself, or eating straight.

**How should I store a 2.2 lb bag?**
Immediately transfer to an airtight container. Walnuts are high in polyunsaturated fats and go rancid quickly at room temperature. Fridge storage extends freshness to about 6 months; the freezer pushes that to a year.

**Are these good for making walnut milk or nut butter?**
Excellent. The pieces process easily in a blender or food processor, and the fresh, sweet flavor carries through without the bitterness that ruins homemade nut milks.
