Let's be direct: this is not your grocery store mozzarella. BUF CREAME's bocconcini are made from 100% water buffalo milk, and you can taste the difference from the first bite. At 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, this is one of the better-rated buffalo mozzarellas on Amazon. Here's the real story.

## What you actually get

The 7-ounce package contains roughly 12-14 small mozzarella balls, each about the size of a cherry tomato. They arrive submerged in a whey-based brine solution — that liquid matters. It's not just packaging; it's what keeps the cheese from drying out and developing that rubbery texture cheap mozzarella gets.

Pop one open and you'll notice the texture immediately: soft, slightly springy, with a delicate outer skin that gives way to a creamy, almost buttery interior. The flavor is mild but distinct — grassy, slightly sweet, with that signature buffalo milk tang you don't get from cow's milk versions.

## How it performs in real use

I tested these three ways: straight from the brine, on a margherita pizza, and in a caprese salad.

**Straight from the package**: Best at room temperature. Cold, the flavor is muted; give it 15-20 minutes out of the fridge and the creaminess really comes through.

**On pizza**: This is where buffalo mozzarella shines — and where you need to be careful. The higher moisture content means it can make your crust soggy if you're not careful. Pat the balls dry with paper towels before using, and add them halfway through baking rather than at the start. Done right, you get that beautiful, milky pull that cow's milk mozzarella just can't replicate.

**In caprese**: This is the winner. Sliced tomatoes, fresh basil, olive oil, a pinch of flaky salt — the buffalo mozzarella is the star here. The creaminess balances the acidity of the tomatoes perfectly.

## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The 7-ounce size is small. If you're making pizza for a family or entertaining, you'll need two or three containers — and that gets expensive fast. This is a treat, not a staple.

The shelf life is also a real constraint. You've got about two weeks from delivery, and once opened, you should finish it within 3-4 days. The brine helps, but this cheese is meant to be eaten fresh. Don't buy it planning to stash it in the back of your fridge.

One more thing: some reviewers mentioned the cheese arriving warmer than ideal during summer months. Amazon's cold chain is usually solid, but if you live in a hot climate, it's worth ordering during cooler weather or checking the package immediately.

**Q: Can I freeze the extra bocconcini?**
A: Technically yes, but don't. Freezing ruins the texture — it becomes grainy and rubbery. This cheese is meant to be eaten fresh. Buy only what you'll use in a week.
