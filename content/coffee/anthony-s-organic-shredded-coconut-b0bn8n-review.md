Let me be direct: this isn't a coffee maker. It's a bag of shredded coconut. But if you're serious about your morning latte or bulletproof coffee, this is the ingredient that takes it from "fine" to "actually good." And with 235 ratings holding a 4.7-star average, I'm not the only one who thinks so.

Here's the deal. Anthony's Organic Shredded Coconut is exactly what the label says: one ingredient, certified organic, unsweetened, finely shredded. No sulfites, no added sugar, no preservatives. The bag is 2.5 pounds, which sounds like a lot until you realize you'll go through it faster than you expect if you're adding a tablespoon to your coffee or blending it into smoothies.

**What you actually get**

Open the bag and you're hit with that unmistakable fresh coconut aroma—sweet, nutty, and nothing artificial. The shreds are fine and uniform, closer to a desiccated texture than the big chunky flakes you find in the baking aisle. As shown in the product image, the coconut is light and airy, not clumped or stale. The resealable zipper works well, which matters because this is a big bag and you won't finish it in a week.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this three ways: in coffee, in baking, and straight off the spoon. The unsweetened profile means it won't sweeten your drink—you'll want to add your own sweetener if that's your thing. But for bulletproof coffee, it blends cleanly without leaving gritty residue. One buyer noted, "I add this to my morning smoothies and it gives a nice texture without overpowering the other flavors." Another praised it for keto fat bombs: "Finally a coconut that doesn't have sugar hiding in it. Perfect for my macros."

For baking, the fine shred is a double-edged sword. It hydrates quickly and distributes evenly through batter, which is great for macaroons or coating truffles. But if you're making a trail mix or granola where you want that chewy, chunky coconut bite, this isn't it. You'd want larger flakes for that.

**Where it falls short**

The fine texture is the main tradeoff. One reviewer complained, "The shred is almost powdery in places. It works for baking but I was hoping for chunkier pieces for snacking." There's also the dust factor—expect a fine coconut powder settled at the bottom of the bag. It's not a defect, just a byproduct of the shredding process, but it can throw off measurements if you're not paying attention.

The other thing: this is a big bag. If you're an occasional baker, you might find yourself with stale coconut six months from now. The resealable bag helps, but I'd recommend transferring it to an airtight container if you're not using it weekly.

**Who should buy this**

Buy it if you're on a keto or paleo diet and want a clean, unsweetened coconut for fat bombs, smoothies, or low-carb baking. Buy it if you go through coconut regularly and want the per-ounce savings of a bulk bag. Buy it if you're tired of grocery store coconut that's loaded with sugar and preservatives.

Skip it if you want chunky, sweetened coconut for snacking or trail mix. Skip it if you only bake once or twice a year—you'd be better off with a smaller bag.

**FAQ**

*Is this coconut sweetened?*
No. It's 100% unsweetened organic coconut. If you need sweetness, you'll add your own sweetener.

*How should I store it after opening?*
The resealable bag works, but for maximum freshness, transfer to an airtight container in a cool, dark place. You can also freeze it—coconut freezes well and thaws without losing texture.

*Can I use this for homemade coconut milk?*
Yes, but the fine shred means you'll need to strain it thoroughly. A nut milk bag is essential, or you'll get cloudy milk with sediment.
