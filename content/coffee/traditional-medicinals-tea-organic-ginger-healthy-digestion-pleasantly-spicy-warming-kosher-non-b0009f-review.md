## What You're Actually Getting

This is a straightforward product: a box of 16 tea bags, each containing 1,000 mg of certified organic ginger root. That's it. No fillers, no artificial flavors, no added sweeteners. ## How It Performs

Steep this for 5-10 minutes (the box recommends 10 for full potency) and you'll get a deep amber cup with a genuinely spicy, warming bite. The ginger flavor is forward and unmistakable — there's a pleasant heat that builds on the back of the throat, similar to what you'd get from fresh ginger sliced into hot water.

For digestion, this is where the tea shines. The gingerols and shogaols in ginger root are well-documented digestive aids, and at 1,000 mg per bag, you're getting a therapeutic dose, not a symbolic one. Drink it after a heavy meal and you'll notice the difference within 15-20 minutes.

One thing to note: this is not a sweet tea. If you're used to ginger snaps or ginger ale, the unsweetened, unadulterated ginger flavor will be a shock. A touch of honey or lemon helps if you need it, but give it a try straight first — the clean ginger profile is genuinely good.

## Where It Falls Short

The spiciness is the double-edged sword here. If you're not a ginger enthusiast, this tea will be too strong. Several reviewers noted it "burns a little going down" when steeped for the full 10 minutes. If that's you, steep for 3-4 minutes instead — you'll still get ginger flavor, just with less bite.

The lack of individual wrappers is a minor but real issue. Once you open the box, the tea bags are exposed to air. If you're an occasional tea drinker, the flavor can degrade over a few months. Transfer the bags to an airtight container if you don't plan to finish the box within 6-8 weeks.
