## FAQ

**Q: Is UMF 15+ actually stronger than lower ratings?**
Yes. UMF measures methylglyoxal content. UMF 15+ has roughly double the MG of UMF 10+ brands. If you’re using it for therapeutic purposes, the higher rating matters. For everyday use, it’s overkill.

**Q: How do I handle crystallization?**
Place the sealed jar in a bowl of warm — not boiling — water for 10–15 minutes. Stir gently. Never microwave it; that destroys the beneficial enzymes.

**Q: Can I use this in coffee or cooking?**
Yes, but heat degrades some beneficial compounds. For maximum benefit, stir it into warm — not boiling — beverages. For cooking, save your cheaper honey.
