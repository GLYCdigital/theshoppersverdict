You're not buying these for the packaging. You're buying them because you want a big bag of decent cashews delivered to your door without paying premium brand prices. Amazon Grocery's Whole Cashews — previously sold under the Happy Belly label — deliver exactly that, with a few caveats worth knowing before you add to cart.

**What you actually get:** A 16-ounce (1 lb) resealable stand-up pouch. The bag is sturdy, with a clear window so you can see the nuts before opening. As shown in the product image, these are roasted cashew kernels — not raw, not in-shell. Lightly salted means just that: a subtle dusting that lets the natural buttery flavor come through. No weird additives, no palm oil coating, just cashews, salt, and heat.

**Performance and build quality — the real test:** I've gone through two bags. The roasting is consistent — a warm golden-brown across the board, with no scorched pieces or pale, under-roasted stragglers. The crunch is solid, not stale or chewy. Salt distribution is even; you won't hit a mouth-puckering cluster in one handful and a bland one in the next. For snacking, baking, or topping salads, these perform like a mid-tier grocery brand, not a bargain-bin afterthought.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The value-to-quality ratio keeps coming up. One reviewer summed it up: "These taste as good as the $12 brand at my local market, but I paid half that." Another noted, "The salt is perfect — I can actually taste the cashew, not just salt." The resealable bag also gets consistent shout-outs — it's actually functional, not a flimsy zipper that breaks on day two.

**Where it falls short — be honest with yourself:** The "whole" in the name is aspirational. Expect maybe 20–25% broken pieces and some fine dust at the bottom of the bag. It's not a dealbreaker for snacking or cooking, but if you're using these for gift-giving or careful plating, you'll be picking through. Also, roast darkness varies slightly between bags — one batch I got was noticeably oilier and darker than the first. Not bad, just inconsistent. And if you're salt-sensitive, even "lightly salted" might run a touch high for you.

**Who should buy this:**  
- **The volume snacker** — you go through a pound of nuts weekly and want a decent product at a fair price.  
- **The home baker** — broken pieces don't matter when they're going into cookies or granola.  
- **The budget-conscious shopper** — you want name-brand quality without the name-brand markup.  

**Who should skip it:**  
- **The perfectionist** — if your cashews must be 100% whole and uniformly roasted, spend more on a premium brand.  
- **The strict low-sodium dieter** — even lightly salted here carries more sodium than raw or unsalted options.

**FAQ shoppers actually ask:**  

*Are these truly "whole" cashews?*  
Mostly, but not entirely. You'll get a mix of whole kernels, halves, and some broken bits. The bag doesn't lie outright, but it does exaggerate.

*How long do they stay fresh after opening?*  
About two to three weeks if you reseal properly and store in a cool, dry place. Transfer to an airtight container if you're in a humid climate — the bag is good, not perfect.

*Can I get these unsalted?*  
Not in this listing. Amazon Grocery offers a raw/unsalted version separately, but this specific product is roasted and lightly salted only.

**The Verdict:**  
This is a solid 4.2-star product — good value, reliable roasting, and a salt level that most people will find just right. It's not premium, and the broken pieces are a real (if minor) flaw. But for everyday snacking and cooking, it's hard to beat the price-to-quality ratio.

**Buy it if** you want a big, dependable bag of cashews without overspending.  
**Skip it if** you demand uniformly whole nuts or need strict sodium control.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Recommended for most shoppers, with eyes open about the broken pieces.
