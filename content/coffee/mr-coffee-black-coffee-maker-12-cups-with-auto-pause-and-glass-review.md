Not every coffee maker needs a touchscreen, an app, and a subscription. The **Mr. Coffee 12-Cup Black Coffee Maker** is the anti-smart appliance: a no-frills drip brewer that costs $40 and does exactly one thing. With 44,539 reviews and a 4.4/5 rating, it turns out that's exactly what a lot of people want.

## The Good

### Simplicity Is the Feature

There's no clock to set, no timer to program, no brew-strength setting to debate. Flip the switch, coffee comes out. One reviewer who replaced a 20-year-old Mr. Coffee said, "This is the coffee maker I've been looking for for years and I thought no longer existed." Another called it "no-nonsense, inexpensive, great coffee." If you've ever cursed at a coffee maker's blinking 12:00, this is your machine.

### Auto-Pause Works as Advertised

The auto-pause feature lets you pull the carafe out mid-brew to pour a cup without coffee dripping everywhere. It's a simple mechanism but it works reliably. Morning person wants the first cup before the pot is done? No problem.

### Dishwasher-Safe Carafe

The glass carafe goes straight into the dishwasher. No hand-washing, no coffee stains building up over time. It's a small quality-of-life feature that long-term users consistently appreciate. The filter basket lifts out easily too.

### Pouring That Doesn't Make a Mess

Multiple reviews mention the water reservoir is "easy to pour into without spilling" — something surprisingly rare in budget coffee makers. The carafe pours cleanly too, no dribbling down the side.

## The Not-So-Good

### No Auto Shut-Off — The Biggest Miss

This is the #1 complaint and it's a legitimate one. The Mr. Coffee basic model does not turn itself off. If you leave the house and forget to flip the switch, the warming plate stays on. One reviewer called it "good coffee pot with one caveat." For households where someone always remembers to turn it off, it's fine. For everyone else, it's a fire-safety concern and a reason to spend $10 more on the programmable version.

### No Timer, No Programmability

You can't set this to brew at 7 AM. You wake up, you turn it on, you wait 10 minutes. If "set it and forget it" is a must-have, this is the wrong Mr. Coffee.

### Plastic Everywhere

The housing is all plastic, the filter basket is plastic, and it feels like a $40 appliance. It doesn't creak or flex worryingly, but it's not going to impress anyone with build quality. The upside: it's lightweight and easy to move.

### Warming Plate Vigilance Required

Leave the pot on the warming plate too long and the coffee develops that burnt, bitter taste. This isn't unique to Mr. Coffee — it's a drip-brewer problem — but without auto shut-off, it's easier to forget.

## Who Should Buy It

The Mr. Coffee 12-Cup is for **people who want coffee without ceremony**. It's perfect for offices, break rooms, Airbnb rentals, and homes where someone is always around to turn it off. If you're replacing a 10+ year old coffee maker and don't want features you'll never use, this is the one.

## Who Should Skip It

- Anyone who needs auto shut-off (safety concern or forgetfulness)
- Programmable brewing fans (wake up to hot coffee)
- Thermal carafe buyers (keeps coffee hot without a warming plate)
- Build quality snobs

## Bottom Line

The Mr. Coffee 12-Cup Black Coffee Maker is the **dictionary definition of "good enough."** It brews coffee, pours without dripping, cleans up in the dishwasher, and costs $40. The missing auto shut-off is the only real flaw, and whether it bothers you depends entirely on your habits. With 44,000+ reviews and a 4.4/5, the consensus is clear: for a basic drip brewer, this is the one to beat.
