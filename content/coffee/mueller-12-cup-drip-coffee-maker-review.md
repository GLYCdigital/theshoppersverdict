At under $30, the Mueller 12-Cup Drip Coffee Maker is one of the best bargains in drip coffee. But does budget pricing mean a budget brew? Not according to over 8,000 reviewers who've given it a solid 4.2 stars.

## What Makes It Great

The borosilicate glass carafe is the star here. Unlike cheap soda-lime glass, borosilicate handles thermal shock better — so pouring hot coffee into a cold mug is less likely to cause cracks. The spout is drip-free, which one reviewer called "a lifesaver at 6 AM."

The reusable filter is another win. No buying paper filters, no waste. Just rinse and reuse. Reviewers consistently praised how easy the machine is to use and clean. One five-star review described it as "simple, reliable brewing with great everyday value."

It's also surprisingly rugged. One reviewer reported leaving it outside in 36-degree weather while camping and it still "stood strong and made great hot coffee each morning."

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest quirk: the filter basket has to be seated *just right* or the coffee won't flow through. A few reviewers mentioned getting a stalled brew on their first try until they figured out the alignment.

The keep-warm function works, but some reviewers wished it ran hotter. And there's no programmable timer — you can't set it to brew automatically before you wake up.

## The Bottom Line

The Mueller 12-Cup Drip Coffee Maker delivers exactly what it promises: a simple, reliable, affordable coffee maker with quality materials. If you don't need programmable features and just want great coffee at a great price, this is a no-brainer.
