You're expected to know this already. For a first-time buyer, that's a serious omission.

**FAQ**

**Q: How many seeds should I take per day?**
A: Most sources recommend 1-2 kernels daily for adults, never exceeding that. Start with one to assess tolerance. Do not take more — this is genuinely a safety issue, not a suggestion.

**Q: Do these need to be refrigerated?**
A: Not required, but recommended for long-term storage. Keep them in the resealable bag or transfer to an airtight jar in a cool, dark place. They'll stay fresh for 6-12 months.

**Q: Are these the same as sweet almonds?**
A: No. Sweet almonds are safe to eat by the handful. Bitter apricot seeds are a different product with different chemistry. The bitterness is the giveaway — if they taste like a normal almond, they're not what you ordered.
