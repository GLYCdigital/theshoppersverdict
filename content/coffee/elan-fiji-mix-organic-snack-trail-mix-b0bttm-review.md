**The honest scoop on Elan Fiji Mix — before you add to cart**

You’ve seen a dozen trail mixes with “organic” slapped on the label. Most taste like cardboard with raisins. The Elan Fiji Mix is not that. This one earns its 4.5-star rating from 225 Amazon shoppers — and after testing a full bag, I get it. But it’s not perfect, and you should know where before you buy.

**What you actually get**

The 12-ounce resealable bag arrives packed with a genuinely interesting blend: roasted cashews, almonds, macadamias, dried pineapple, coconut flakes, and a subtle hint of sea salt. No chocolate chips, no yogurt drops — just nuts and fruit. As shown in the product image, the pieces are generous, not the dusty crumbs you find in cheaper bags.

The ingredient list is refreshingly short. Everything is certified organic. No added sugar, no palm oil, no preservatives. For shoppers who read labels — and you should — that’s a real selling point.

**How it performs in real life**

The macadamias are the star. They’re buttery and fresh, not stale or rancid like some budget mixes. The pineapple is chewy-sweet without being cloying, and the coconut adds a subtle tropical note that pairs well with the roasted nuts. The sea salt isn’t overpowering — it just brightens the flavors.

One thing that surprised me: the mix holds up well in a backpack for a day hike. No melting, no oil stains, no soggy fruit. It stays crunchy and separate. That’s more than I can say for several “premium” mixes I’ve tested.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Scrolling through the reviews, a few themes repeat. “Fresh” and “not too sweet” come up constantly. One shopper wrote, *“Finally a trail mix that doesn’t taste like candy. The coconut and pineapple are actually recognizable.”* Another said, *“I’ve ordered this three times now. It’s my office snack and I never get tired of it.”* The resealable bag also earns mentions — it actually seals, unlike some flimsy zip-tops.

**Where it falls short**

The price is the biggest hurdle. Per ounce, this costs more than conventional trail mixes at mainstream grocers. If you’re feeding a family or just want a cheap desk snack, this isn’t it. This is a premium product with a premium price.

Also, after a few days, the pineapple pieces can stick together. It’s not a dealbreaker — a quick shake fixes it — but it’s worth knowing if you’re prone to grabbing a handful without mixing first.

One more note: the bag is 12 ounces, not the 16-ounce size you might expect from similar products. Check the weight before you compare prices.

**Who should buy this**

- **Trail hikers and backpackers** who want a calorie-dense, no-melt snack with clean ingredients  
- **Office workers** who need a satisfying mid-afternoon pick-me-up without a sugar crash  
- **Clean-eaters** who avoid added sugar and artificial anything  
- **Gift-givers** looking for a thoughtful, organic food gift for a health-conscious friend  

Skip it if you’re on a tight budget, need a bulk-size bag, or prefer chocolate in your trail mix.

**FAQ: Three questions shoppers actually ask**

*Is this mix keto-friendly?*  
Not strictly. The pineapple and coconut add natural sugars — about 9 grams per serving. It’s low-carb enough for many low-sugar diets, but not keto.

*Does it contain peanuts?*  
No. The blend uses cashews, almonds, and macadamias. But it’s processed in a facility that handles tree nuts and peanuts, so cross-contamination is possible.

*How long does it stay fresh?*  
The resealable bag keeps it crunchy for about two to three weeks after opening. Stored in a cool, dry place, it lasts longer. The nuts don’t go rancid quickly — impressive for a mix with macadamias.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely organic, well-balanced trail mix with real flavor and no junk. It’s worth the premium for the quality.

**Skip it if** you need a budget option or a large family-size bag.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Fresh, honest, and satisfying. The price is the only real friction point. This is a trail mix you’ll actually finish, not one that sits in the pantry for months.
