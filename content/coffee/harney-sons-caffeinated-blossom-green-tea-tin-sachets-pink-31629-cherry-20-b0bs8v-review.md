Let's be honest about what this is: a green tea that smells like a spring garden and tastes like someone infused it with cherry candy. The Harney & Sons Caffeinated Blossom Green Tea isn't trying to be your everyday builder's brew — it's a step-up experience, and at 4.5 stars across 225 Amazon ratings, most buyers agree it delivers.

**What's actually in the pink tin**

You get 20 individually wrapped sachets inside a sturdy, hinge-lid tin that's genuinely reusable. The sachets themselves are larger than standard tea bags — they're more like small pouches that give the tea leaves room to expand, which matters for flavor extraction. The tin measures about 4 inches across and stands roughly 5 inches tall, so it fits neatly on a counter or shelf without dominating the space.

The tea base is a solid Chinese green tea — not the dusty, broken-leaf stuff you find in bargain bags. The flavor profile leans on cherry and a soft floral note that the brand calls "blossom." It's sweet without added sugar, and there's a gentle astringency at the finish that reminds you it's a real green tea, not fruit juice pretending to be tea.

**How it performs in real use**

Steep it at 175°F (not boiling — that's the mistake that makes green tea bitter) for 2–3 minutes, and you get a pale golden liquor with a pronounced cherry aroma. The caffeine content sits somewhere between a standard black tea and coffee — enough to take the edge off a mid-afternoon slump, but don't expect it to replace your espresso shot.

One thing that surprised me: the sachet quality. The paper doesn't tear or burst, even when you give it a squeeze to get the last drops out. That's a small thing, but it speaks to the overall build quality.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The flavor balance gets the most love. One reviewer put it simply: "Finally a fruity green tea that doesn't taste like perfume." Another noted, "The cherry is there but not overwhelming — it's like a subtle sweetness that lingers." The individually wrapped sachets also earn repeated mentions for office use and travel — no stale tea, no mess in a bag.

**Where it falls short**

The cherry note dominates more than the "blossom" suggests. If you're expecting something delicate and jasmine-forward, you'll be surprised. Several reviewers mentioned this exact thing — one said, "I bought it for the blossom, stayed for the cherry, but they're not equal partners in the cup."

The caffeine level is also a point of contention for some. It's a green tea, so it's moderate — if you're someone who needs serious stimulation, this won't cut it. And at roughly $10–12 for 20 sachets, you're paying about 50–60 cents per cup, which is more than loose-leaf or standard bags.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the person who wants a step up from grocery-store tea bags without committing to a full loose-leaf ritual. It's ideal for office workers who want something special in the afternoon, travelers who appreciate the individually wrapped sachets, and anyone who enjoys floral-fruity teas but finds most commercial options too artificial.

Skip it if you need a serious caffeine hit, prefer pure unflavored green tea, or you're on a tight budget.

**FAQ**

*Is this actually caffeinated?*
Yes, it's a real green tea with natural caffeine — roughly 25–35mg per cup, about a third of what you'd get in coffee.

*Can I cold brew these sachets?*
Absolutely. Drop one in cold water and refrigerate for 4–6 hours. The cherry notes come through even more clearly.

*How many cups per tin?*
Twenty sachets, and each one gives you one solid steep. You can get a weaker second steep, but the flavor drops off noticeably.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely pleasant, naturally flavored green tea with the convenience of individual sachets and a tin you'll actually reuse. **Skip it if** you're looking for a budget option or a serious caffeine replacement.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It's not perfect (the cherry-to-blossom ratio is debatable, and it's not cheap), but it's one of the better flavored green teas you'll find in this format, and the overwhelmingly positive reviews back that up.
