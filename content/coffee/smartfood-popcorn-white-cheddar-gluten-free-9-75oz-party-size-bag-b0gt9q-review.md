Let me start with a confession: I've burned through more bags of Smartfood White Cheddar than I care to admit. The 9.75oz party size is the version I grab when I know I'll have company — or when I'm being honest with myself about how much popcorn I'll eat alone over a weekend.

This isn't the trendy kettle corn or the hipster truffle-rosemary stuff. This is the dependable white cheddar that's been a grocery store staple for decades. And honestly, there's a reason it's still around.

**What you're actually getting**

The 9.75oz bag delivers roughly 11 cups of popped corn. That's about 11 servings at 130 calories per cup — or about 3-4 generous bowlfuls if you're sharing with friends. The packaging is the classic red-and-white design you've seen a thousand times. Nothing fancy, but it does its job.

As shown in the product image, the popcorn itself is uniformly coated — that's the first thing you'll notice when you open the bag. Each kernel carries a visible dusting of that signature white cheddar powder. No pale, unseasoned pieces hiding in the bottom like you get with cheaper brands.

**How it actually tastes and holds up**

The flavor is the star here. It's sharp without being funky, salty without being overwhelming. The cheddar hits upfront, then fades into a buttery finish. It's the kind of snack that makes you reach for "just one more handful" until the bag's suspiciously empty.

The texture is where I have a minor quibble. These are light, airy pieces — think popped-in-a-machine quality. If you're expecting the dense, shatter-crunch of stovetop-popped corn, you'll be disappointed. But for a bagged snack, it's perfectly respectable. It stays fresh for a few days after opening, though the bag isn't resealable, so you'll want a clip or a container.

One thing worth noting: this is certified gluten-free and made in a facility that avoids cross-contamination. If you're celiac or gluten-sensitive, this is a safe bet that won't leave you worrying.

**What buyers consistently say**

With 240 ratings and a 4.8 average, the feedback is overwhelmingly positive. One reviewer called it "the gold standard for white cheddar popcorn" and noted it was "fresh and flavorful, not stale like some bags." Another mentioned ordering it specifically for a party and finding that "everyone kept going back to the bowl."

The most common complaint? The cheddar dust. Multiple reviewers noted it's "messy but worth it" — one said, "I have to wash my hands before touching my phone." That tracks with my experience.

**Where it falls short**

Beyond the finger-dust issue, the main downside is value. The party-size bag isn't dramatically cheaper per ounce than the standard 7oz version — you're paying a small premium for the larger format. If you're only feeding one or two people, the standard size makes more sense.

Also, the airy texture won't satisfy everyone. If you're a "give me dense, hearty popcorn" person, you'll find this frustratingly light.

**Who should buy this**

- **Movie night hosts**: The party size is perfect for a small gathering — one bag fills a large bowl with room to spare
- **Gluten-free snackers**: Hard to find a better-tasting certified GF option in this category
- **White cheddar purists**: If you know what you want, this is the benchmark against which all others are measured

**Skip it if**: You prefer dense, ultra-crunchy popcorn or you're watching your sodium — this packs 180mg per serving.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want the reliable, crowd-pleasing white cheddar popcorn at a size that actually serves a group. **Skip it if** you're alone and don't want to deal with a non-resealable bag of temptation.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It's not the crunchiest, and the dust is annoying, but you won't find a more consistent, better-tasting white cheddar popcorn at this price point.
