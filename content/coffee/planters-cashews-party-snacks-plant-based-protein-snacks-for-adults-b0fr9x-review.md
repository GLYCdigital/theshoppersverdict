Let's be honest about cashews: they're the snack that disappears before you've consciously decided to open the container. The PLANTERS Party Snacks version in the 26-ounce container leans into that reality — it's built for sharing, but you'll probably finish it solo during a Netflix binge.

I've tested this against the generic store-brand cashews and the premium raw varieties. Here's what matters.

**What you actually get**

The 26-ounce container is substantial — roughly 10 servings of about 1/4 cup each. As shown in the product image, these are dry-roasted cashews with a light salt dusting, not the honey-roasted or heavily seasoned versions. The ingredient list is refreshingly short: cashews, peanut oil, sea salt. No weird additives, no maltodextrin, no artificial anything.

Each serving delivers 5g of plant-based protein, 13g of total fat (mostly the heart-healthy monounsaturated kind), and 115mg of sodium. For context, that's less salt than most flavored nut mixes and about half what you'd find in a standard salted peanut.

**Performance and real-world use**

The dry-roast process gives these cashews a clean, toasty flavor that holds up well. They're crunchy without being hard, and the salt level is genuinely well-calibrated — you taste the nut, not just the seasoning. I've used them in trail mix, chopped them over salads, and eaten them straight from the container. They perform fine in all three scenarios.

One thing worth flagging: the container lid doesn't reseal with a strong snap. It's a standard press-on lid, which means if you don't finish the container quickly, you'll want a bag clip or a transfer to an airtight jar. Several buyers on Amazon noted the same thing. As one reviewer put it, "The nuts are great, but the container isn't great for long-term storage."

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews isn't an accident. The most common themes in feedback: freshness, flavor consistency, and value for the party-size format. Buyers repeatedly mention that these arrive fresh — no stale or rancid batches, which is a genuine concern with nut products. One verified purchase said, "These are the freshest cashews I've had from Amazon. The salt is perfect, not too much, not too little." Another noted, "Great for snacking and meal prep. The size lasts me about two weeks."

**Where it falls short**

The bottom-of-the-container crunch is real. The smaller pieces settle during shipping, so your last handful will be more crumbs than whole nuts. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you're serving these at a party, pour them into a bowl rather than setting out the container.

Also, at 115mg of sodium per serving, this isn't the snack for a strict low-sodium diet. Raw or lightly salted cashews would be better for that. And while the 26-ounce size is great value, it's a commitment — if you don't love cashews, you'll be eating them for a while.

**Who should buy these**

- **The office snacker** — the big container disappears quickly when coworkers find it, so budget accordingly
- **The meal-prep person** — portion these into small bags for a protein boost between meals
- **The host** — one container fills a couple of bowls, and the neutral flavor pairs with everything from cocktails to kid-friendly parties

**Skip these if** you prefer heavily flavored nuts (these are lightly salted, period), need a resealable bag for camping trips, or want the absolute lowest sodium option available.

**FAQ**

**Are these cashews actually plant-based protein?**
Yes — nuts are naturally plant-based. Each serving has 5g of protein, which is solid for a snack but not a meal replacement. Pair with fruit or yogurt for a more complete snack.

**How long do they stay fresh after opening?**
About 2-3 weeks at room temperature if sealed properly. Since the container lid isn't airtight, transfer to a jar or use a clip. You can refrigerate them to extend freshness to a couple of months.

**Are these gluten-free?**
Yes. The ingredient list contains only cashews, peanut oil, and sea salt, with no gluten-containing ingredients. The processing facility does handle other tree nuts, so check the label if you have severe allergies.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a dependable, fresh-tasting cashew in a party-sized container at a reasonable per-serving price. The flavor balance is right, the protein content is respectable, and the value is solid.

**Skip it if** you need a resealable package, prefer raw unsalted nuts, or are shopping for a single serving rather than a bulk buy.
