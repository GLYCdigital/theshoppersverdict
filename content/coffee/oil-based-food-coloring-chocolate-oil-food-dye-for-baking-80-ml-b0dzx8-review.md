**Performance in real use**

The core promise here is that this dye won't seize your chocolate. In testing, that holds up. I melted white candy melts, added drops of the red and blue, and the texture stayed smooth and pourable throughout. The color disperses evenly without streaking, which is the second-most-common complaint with oil dyes. You do need to stir thoroughly — this isn't a "shake and pour" product.

The black requires more effort — plan on 6-8 drops and patience if you want true black rather than dark gray. **Honest downsides**

The oily sheen issue is real. If you go heavy-handed, finished chocolate can look slightly greasy rather than glossy. It's cosmetic, not a flavor problem, but it matters if you're selling your baked goods. The fix is simple: use fewer drops and accept slightly lighter colors, or add a touch of cocoa butter to balance.

The 80ml bottles are also bulky. If you're a casual baker who makes colored chocolate once a year, this is more than you'll ever use. Colorants do degrade over time — plan to use these within 18-24 months of opening.
