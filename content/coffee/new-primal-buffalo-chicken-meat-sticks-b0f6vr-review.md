Let me be direct: I didn't expect to be impressed by a chicken stick in a buffalo sauce flavor. Most meat snacks in this category are either overly processed, taste like cardboard with seasoning dust, or cost more per ounce than a steak dinner. The New Primal Buffalo Chicken Meat Sticks are none of those things — and they've earned their 4.6-star rating across 229 Amazon reviews.

**What You're Actually Getting**

Each package contains 10 individually wrapped sticks weighing 1 ounce each. As shown in the product image, the sticks are a uniform reddish-brown from the buffalo seasoning, with a texture that's clearly visible — dried, slightly crinkled, and nothing like the glossy, wet jerky you might expect. The ingredient list is refreshingly short: chicken, water, sea salt, spices, celery juice powder, and natural smoke flavor. No MSG, no nitrites, no sugar, no soy, no gluten. That's it.

**The Real-World Test**

I've eaten these as a desk snack, on a hiking trail, and post-workout. The flavor is the standout — it genuinely tastes like buffalo wing sauce, not just cayenne dust. There's a vinegar tang up front, followed by a slow-building warmth that never crosses into "I need milk" territory. One Amazon reviewer summed it up: *"Taste just like buffalo wings without the mess. I keep a box in my glove box for emergencies."* That sentiment appears consistently — the flavor wins people over.

Texture is where opinions split. These are dense, chewy, and definitely drier than mainstream jerky brands. If you've had grass-fed beef sticks, you know the drill — it's a natural, unmarinated chew that takes effort. One reviewer noted: *"A bit tough, but that's what I expect from something without fillers. My jaw got a workout."* That's accurate. It's not unpleasant, but it's not the soft, processed texture most snack sticks offer.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The macros are the headline. Each stick delivers 10g protein, 60 calories, and zero sugar. For anyone tracking intake, that's nearly perfect. Multiple reviewers mention using these for keto, paleo, and Whole30 — and the fact that they're shelf-stable without refrigeration makes them a reliable travel snack. One parent reviewer specifically praised them as a school-safe snack because there's no peanut or soy in the facility — a detail that matters for allergy households.

**Where It Falls Short**

The heat level is the most common criticism. If you're a buffalo sauce enthusiast who orders "extra hot" at wing joints, this won't satisfy. The spice is present but restrained — more mild wing sauce than killer heat. One reviewer put it plainly: *"Good flavor, but I wish they were spicier. My 8-year-old eats them without complaint."* That tells you the heat ceiling.

Second, the price per ounce runs higher than conventional jerky. You're paying a premium for the clean ingredients and ethical sourcing. If your budget is tight, this is an occasional treat, not an everyday stock-up.

**Who Should Buy These**

Get these if you're a macro-tracker who wants a portable protein source that doesn't come wrapped in questionable ingredients, or if you follow paleo/Whole30 and need something that actually fits the rules. They're also ideal for road trips, camping, or desk drawers where refrigeration isn't an option. Skip them if you want soft, juicy jerky texture or genuine spicy heat — look elsewhere for those qualities.

**FAQ**

**Are these actually Whole30-approved?**
Yes. The New Primal's entire meat stick line, including the Buffalo Chicken flavor, carries Whole30 Approved certification. No sugar, no additives, no non-compliant oils.

**How many sticks come in a box?**
Ten individually wrapped sticks, each 1 ounce. The wrappers are easy to tear open and resealable isn't an issue since they're single-serve.

**Are these safe for people with common allergies?**
The facility processes no peanuts, tree nuts, soy, or gluten. The sticks themselves contain chicken, salt, spices, and celery juice powder. Always check current packaging for the most up-to-date allergen statements.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a clean-ingredient, high-protein snack with legit buffalo flavor that fits strict dietary programs. The taste-to-nutrition ratio is excellent.

**Skip it if:** You need soft, moist jerky texture or serious heat. This is a dense, dry, mildly-spiced stick — and that's by design.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The flavor, clean label, and macros justify the premium price. It's a genuinely good product held back only by texture preferences and a timid spice level.
