Let me be straight with you: this isn't a product I'd normally review on a coffee gear site. But if you're the kind of person who takes their coffee seriously, you probably also appreciate something sweet to cut through the bitterness. Walkers' Nonsuch Assorted Toffees and Chocolate Eclairs are a British institution, and this bulk pack brings them to your door.

**What's actually in the bag**

You're getting a random assortment of two classic candies: the traditional Nonsuch toffees (the ones in the recognizable blue and gold wrapping) and chocolate eclairs (soft, chewy caramel centers coated in chocolate). The bulk pack weighs in at roughly 600 grams, which translates to somewhere between 40 and 50 individually wrapped pieces depending on the mix. No box, no frills — just a resealable bag of candy.

**The taste test — and the texture reality**

The chocolate eclairs are the clear winners here. They've got that soft, almost fudgy center that gives just the right amount of resistance before melting into caramel sweetness. The chocolate coating is thin but present, and it balances the sweetness of the filling. These are the ones people will pick out of the bowl first.

The traditional toffees are a different beast entirely. They're hard, brittle, and intensely buttery — true to the original recipe that's been made in Scotland since 1920. But here's the honest truth: they're *hard*. If you've got dental work, weak enamel, or a preference for chewy candy, these will test your patience. One Amazon reviewer noted, "The toffees are like little rocks when they arrive in winter. I had to warm them up before I could bite into them." That's accurate. Give them 10-15 minutes at room temperature before you attempt to eat one.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The nostalgia factor is huge. Multiple reviewers mention buying these because they remind them of their grandmother's candy dish or trips to the UK. The quality of the ingredients gets consistent praise too — real butter, real sugar, no artificial anything. That's increasingly rare in mass-market candy.

The bulk sizing also earns points. "Perfect for filling a candy dish at the office," one reviewer wrote. "Everyone gravitates toward the eclairs first, but the toffees grow on you." Another mentioned using them as wedding favors with no complaints about quality or freshness.

**Where it falls short**

The random assortment is the biggest drawback. You don't get to choose your ratio, and if you're not a fan of one type, you're stuck with roughly half the bag being something you won't enjoy. The packaging is also underwhelming — it's a simple plastic bag, not the decorative tins you might expect from a premium British candy brand. And as mentioned, cold temperatures turn the toffees into jaw-breakers, so if you live somewhere warm and your house runs cool, plan accordingly.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the person who appreciates traditional candy made with honest ingredients. If you're hosting a gathering and want something that looks and tastes authentically British, this fits. It's also great for gifting to someone who misses the UK or has a sweet tooth with a classic bent. Skip it if you prefer soft, chewy candy across the board, or if you have significant dental work.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Are these the same as the ones sold in UK supermarkets?* Yes, identical recipe and packaging. You're getting the real import, not a licensed knockoff.

*How long do they stay fresh?* Sealed, they'll last months. Once opened, keep them in a cool, dry place and they'll hold for 3-4 weeks without going stale.

*Can I request all eclairs?* No. The assortment is random from the factory. If you want a single variety, look for the dedicated eclair-only packs.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want authentic British toffees with real butter and sugar, you're okay with a mixed bag, and you'll actually eat the hard toffees (not just the eclairs).

**Skip it if:** You have dental work, you only like soft caramel, or you need a specific ratio of the two candies.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The eclairs alone justify the price, and the toffees are authentic even if they're an acquired taste. For a bulk candy purchase, this delivers solid value with honest ingredients. Just warm up the toffees before you bite.
