## What You Actually Get

The listing ships with multiple 14-piece blister packs rather than a single bulk bottle. Each piece is individually sealed, which is great for tossing a few in a bag without the whole pack going stale. As shown in the product image, the packaging is the familiar Trident blue with bold flavor labeling — nothing fancy, but functional.

## Performance and Build Quality

This is where Trident earns its rating. The flavor release curve is well-engineered: it hits strong in the first 30 seconds, settles into a consistent mid-profile, and keeps going for roughly 15-20 minutes. That's about double what you get from generic store brands that die in under eight minutes.

The texture also holds up. The gum maintains its chew integrity through the flavor's lifespan — no crumbling, no turning into a hard pellet. That matters when you're mid-meeting and can't discreetly spit it out. One 4-star reviewer noted, "Flavor lasts longer than Orbit, but the initial sweetness is a bit sharp." That tracks.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 229 ratings, three themes dominate:

1. **Flavor longevity** — "Best sugar-free gum for the price. I chew one piece after lunch and it still has taste when I get back to my desk."
2. **Portability** — Multiple reviewers mention the blister packs surviving gym bags and glove compartments without turning into a sticky mess.
3. **Dental-friendly formulation** — The xylitol content gets consistent positive mentions from buyers who chew for oral health, not just fresh breath.

## Where It Falls Short

Be honest about two things before buying.

First, the aspartame aftertaste. It's present in the first few chews, particularly with fruit or citrus flavors. Most people adapt within a week, but if you're sensitive to artificial sweeteners, this will be a dealbreaker.

Second, the packaging. Individual blister packs mean more plastic waste per piece than a standard 12-stick wrapper. There's no resealable bag option, so if you want a bulk supply at your desk, you're managing 10+ separate packs. Minor, but worth noting.
