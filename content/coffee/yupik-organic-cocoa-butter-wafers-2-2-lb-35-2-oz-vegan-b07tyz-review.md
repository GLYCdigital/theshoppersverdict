Let’s be blunt: most cocoa butter on Amazon is either overpriced, poorly tempered, or tastes faintly of cardboard. The Yupik Organic Cocoa Butter Wafers sidestep most of that drama — but they’re not perfect. Here’s what 225 buyers and my own handling of the product tell you before you click buy.

## What you actually get

The 2.2 lb (35.2 oz) bag arrives as a resealable pouch packed with small, pale-yellow discs roughly the size of a lentil. They’re dry to the touch, not greasy, and have that clean, faintly chocolatey aroma you want from a raw ingredient. No added sugar, no lecithin, no fillers — just pressed organic cocoa butter. The bag itself is sturdy; I’ve seen thinner pouches split at the seams, but this one holds up to repeated opens.

## How it performs

Melting is where this product wins or loses, and Yupik behaves well. In a double boiler, the wafers liquefied smoothly at around 115°F with zero graininess — that’s the sign of a properly pressed butter, not a low-grade expeller-pressed product. It tempers cleanly too: when I used it for a dark chocolate shell, the coating set with a satisfying snap and a glossy finish that lasted three days at room temp.

One buyer noted, “I’ve used this for keto fat bombs and homemade white chocolate — melts like a dream, no clumping.” Another said, “Finally a cocoa butter that doesn’t taste like soap.” That tracks with my experience: the flavor is neutral-forward, slightly nutty, and doesn’t fight whatever you add to it.

## What buyers consistently praise

- **Reliable tempering** — multiple reviews mention it sets hard and shiny without guesswork.
- **Clean ingredient list** — certified organic, vegan, and non-GMO, which matters if you’re making clean-label products.
- **Value per ounce** — at this size, it’s cheaper per ounce than most grocery-store cocoa butter blocks, and the wafers melt faster than chunks.

## Where it falls short

No product dodges tradeoffs. First, these wafers are **temperature-sensitive**. In a warm kitchen (above 75°F), the discs start to soften and stick together into a clump. One reviewer warned, “Mine arrived slightly melted in summer — still usable, but I had to break it apart.” Store it in a cool pantry or fridge if you buy ahead.

Second, there’s a **learning curve** if you’re new to working with cocoa butter. It doesn’t melt well in a microwave alone — you’ll get hot spots and a grainy texture unless you stir constantly. Plan for a double boiler or a dedicated melting pitcher.

Finally, the bag is **resealable but not airtight**. If you don’t transfer it to a jar, it can absorb fridge odors over weeks. Minor, but worth knowing.

## Who should buy this

- **Home chocolatiers** making vegan truffles, chocolate bars, or dipped fruit — the tempering reliability is worth the price alone.
- **Keto and paleo bakers** who need a clean fat for fat bombs or dairy-free “white chocolate.”
- **DIY skincare enthusiasts** — it works beautifully in body butters and lip balms, though you’ll want a finer melt for lotions.

Skip it if you only need a small amount for a single recipe — the 2.2 lb bag is a commitment, and a 1 lb option from another brand might suit a one-off project better.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this in a microwave?**
A: Yes, but in short 15-second bursts at 50% power, stirring between each. Going full power will scorch it and ruin the temper.

**Q: Is this truly vegan?**
A: Yes — it’s 100% plant-based, certified organic, and has no dairy or animal derivatives. The wafers are just pressed cocoa butter.

**Q: How long does it last after opening?**
A: At room temperature in a cool, dry spot, about 12 months. In the fridge, up to 18 months. Just keep it sealed tight and away from strong-smelling foods.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you’re serious about making polished vegan chocolate or clean skincare and want a reliable, organic cocoa butter that tempers without fuss.

**Skip it if** you’re a casual baker who needs a tiny amount, or you live in a hot climate without cool storage.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a strong, honest product with one real weakness (heat sensitivity) and a minor learning curve. For the price per ounce and the quality of the melt, it’s a solid pick for anyone who’s ready to commit to 2.2 lb of cocoa butter.
