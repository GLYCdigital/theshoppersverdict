## Performance and Real-World Use

I’ve run about two weeks of daily brews through this unit — 12 cups on weekdays, 4 on weekends. The brew time for a full pot is around 9 minutes, which is average. The coffee comes out hot enough to stay drinkable for about 40 minutes on the hot plate before it starts tasting burnt, which is typical for this class of machine.

One thing that genuinely surprised me: the showerhead distributes water evenly over the grounds. No dry spots, no channeling. That’s a $200-machine feature at a $50-machine price. The pause-and-pour function works as advertised — you can pull the carafe mid-brew to grab a cup without a mess, and it doesn’t drip all over the burner when you put it back.
