## OXO Brew Conical Burr Coffee Grinder Gen 2 Review

The OXO Brew Conical Burr Grinder has been a staple recommendation for home coffee enthusiasts on a budget. The original model (B07CSKGLMM) built a loyal following with over 22,000 reviews. Now the Gen 2 (B0CCCPK6ZQ) arrives with a lower price and key improvements — most notably a stainless steel catch cup that addresses the original's biggest headache.

We tested the Gen 2 for two weeks across multiple brew methods. Here's the full verdict.

### What's New in Gen 2?

If you read reviews of the original OXO grinder, one complaint surfaces over and over: static. The plastic grounds bin generated enough charge to make coffee dust cling like it was glued. The Gen 2 swaps that plastic bin for a **stainless steel catch cup**, and the difference is immediate. Grounds slide cleanly into your filter or portafilter with minimal mess.

OXO also refined the conical burr geometry and dropped the price by $10. It's rare to see a second generation that's both better and cheaper.

### Grind Performance

The 15 grind settings span from fine enough for espresso to coarse enough for French press. We tested across three common brew methods:

**Espresso (Settings 1-3):** The finest settings produce a fluffy, uniform grind suitable for home espresso machines. We tested with a Breville Barista Express and got decent shots at setting 2 with medium-roast beans. It won't rival a $500 commercial-grade grinder, but for entry-level espresso, it's more than adequate.

**Pour-Over / Drip (Settings 6-10):** This is where the OXO shines. Settings 7-8 produce a textbook medium grind with minimal fines. We brewed V60s and drip carafes with noticeably clean flavor profiles — no bitter over-extraction from excessive fines.

**French Press / Cold Brew (Settings 12-15):** The coarser settings are consistently flaky with minimal boulders. French press coffee came through clean with no muddy silt at the bottom of the cup.

**Timed Dosing:** The one-touch timer lets you dial in exactly how many seconds to grind. Once you find your dose (e.g., 12 seconds for 20g of coffee), it's perfectly repeatable. This is a small detail that makes morning routines effortless.

### Build and Design

The Gen 2 keeps the same overall silhouette as the original — a compact footprint that fits easily under standard cabinets. Key build notes:

- **Slim profile:** 6.7 inches wide, 11.8 inches deep — fits on most counters
- **Stainless steel catch cup:** 4 oz capacity, easy to pour, dishwasher-safe
- **Bean hopper:** Holds about 8 oz of beans (enough for 4-5 brews)
- **Removable upper burr:** Simple to clean for maintenance
- **Controls:** One dial for grind setting, one dial for timer, one button to start

The only build compromise is the plastic grind chamber lid, which feels a bit thin compared to the rest of the machine. It's functional, but it's the one part that reminds you this is a $100 grinder.

### Long-Term Considerations

Multiple reports from Gen 1 users who upgraded confirm the burrs hold up well over years of daily use. OXO offers a 2-year warranty, and the removable upper burr makes deep cleaning straightforward.

The motor is rated for home use — not commercial. If you're grinding a pound of coffee daily, look at a higher-duty option. For a 2-3 cup morning habit, this grinder will last years.

### Who Should Buy It

**Buy the OXO Brew Gen 2 if:**
- You're upgrading from a blade grinder and want consistent results
- You brew a mix of pour-over, drip, and occasional espresso
- You want a grinder that doesn't take up half your counter
- The $100 price point fits your budget

**Skip it if:**
- You need single-dose weighing accuracy
- You brew multiple pounds of coffee weekly
- You want stepless adjustment for precision espresso dial-in

## Final Verdict

The OXO Brew Conical Burr Grinder Gen 2 is exactly what a second-generation product should be: better, cheaper, and quieter. The stainless steel catch cup fixes the original's most frustrating flaw, and the refined burrs deliver consistent grounds across all 15 settings. At $99, it's the best value in home burr grinding right now.

**Score: 8.8/10** — A refined, affordable burr grinder that delivers consistent results and fixes its predecessor's biggest issues.
