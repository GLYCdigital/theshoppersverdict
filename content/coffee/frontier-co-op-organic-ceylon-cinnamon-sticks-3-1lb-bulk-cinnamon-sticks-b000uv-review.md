## Real-World Performance

I tested these two ways: ground in a blade coffee grinder and steeped whole in hot coffee. The grinding is where the three-inch length earns its keep. Standard blade grinders handle these without pre-breaking — drop two or three sticks in, pulse for 15 seconds, and you get a fine, aromatic powder. No wrestling with a hammer or struggling to fit five-inch sticks into a grinder lid.

The flavor is where Ceylon separates itself from cassia. It's delicate, sweet, and citrusy — almost floral. In coffee, it doesn't fight the beans; it complements them. One stick steeped in a French press adds warmth without the aggressive, almost spicy burn you get from cassia. That's because Ceylon contains roughly 0.004% coumarin versus cassia's 1% — a real health consideration if you consume cinnamon daily.

A quarter teaspoon of freshly ground Ceylon in your morning oatmeal or smoothie is noticeably smoother than the dusty, bitter cassia powder that's been sitting in your pantry for a year.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The bag isn't resealable, which is a real oversight for a one-pound purchase. Unless you transfer these to an airtight container within a few days of opening, the volatile oils will start evaporating — you'll notice the aroma fading after three or four months.

The brittleness is also worth noting. Ceylon is naturally more fragile than cassia, so you'll likely find some broken pieces at the bottom of the bag. It's cosmetic, not a quality issue, but if you're gifting this or need uniform sticks for a project, expect some casualties.

Finally, the paper bag offers zero protection against humidity. If you live somewhere humid, transfer immediately or you'll be dealing with soft, clumping sticks.

## Who Should Buy This

## FAQ

**How does this compare to supermarket cinnamon sticks?**
Those are almost always cassia — cheaper, stronger, higher in coumarin. This is true Ceylon, which is sweeter, more delicate, and better for coffee and lighter baking.

**Can I use these in a coffee grinder?**
Yes. Three-inch sticks fit most blade grinders without pre-breaking. Clean the grinder after use or reserve a dedicated grinder for spices.

**How should I store them?**
Transfer to an airtight glass jar immediately and keep in a cool, dark spot. Properly stored, they'll stay potent for 12-18 months.
