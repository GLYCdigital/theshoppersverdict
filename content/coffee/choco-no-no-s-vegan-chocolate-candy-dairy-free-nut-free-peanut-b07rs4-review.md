**The Short Version:** If you've been burned by chalky vegan chocolates that taste like frozen cardboard, Choco No No's is the exception. This allergen-friendly candy actually delivers the creamy, melt-in-your-mouth experience you'd expect from a quality milk chocolate — minus the dairy, nuts, soy, and gluten. It's not cheap, and it's not trying to be artisanal dark chocolate. But for anyone navigating serious food allergies, this is the closest thing to "normal" candy you'll find.

**What You Actually Get**

The product lineup runs from a 5-ounce trial bag up to a 30-ounce party-size pouch. The pieces are small, round, coin-sized discs — think flattened chocolate chips, not truffles. The ingredient list is refreshingly short: organic cane sugar, cocoa butter, rice milk powder, and vanilla. That's it. No palm oil, no lecithin, no weird stabilizers.

The packaging is a simple resealable pouch. Nothing fancy, but functional for keeping the chocolate fresh over multiple snacking sessions.

**Performance and Real-World Testing**

I put a 5-ounce bag through a week of real-world conditions. The texture is where this product surprises. Most dairy-free chocolates either snap too hard (coconut oil-heavy formulas) or turn greasy at room temperature. Choco No No's sits in a sweet spot — it melts slowly on the tongue with a smooth, creamy mouthfeel that mimics milk chocolate far better than its rice milk base suggests.

The flavor is sweet-forward with a mild cocoa undertone. It's closer to a Hershey's bar than a 70% dark chocolate. If you're expecting deep, complex cocoa notes, you'll be disappointed. If you're expecting a satisfying, nostalgic candy experience, this delivers.

Heat stability is decent. The pieces held their shape reasonably well at 75°F, though I wouldn't leave them in a hot car. They soften but don't turn into a puddle.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews reflects two dominant themes. First, safety. One reviewer put it plainly: "Finally a chocolate my daughter with a severe nut allergy can take to school without me panicking." That sentiment repeats throughout the reviews — parents of kids with allergies, adults with dairy sensitivities, and people with celiac disease all cite the certified allergen-free production as the deciding factor.

Second, taste. Multiple reviewers describe it as "the best vegan chocolate I've tried" and note that non-vegan family members can't tell the difference. One reviewer said, "I've bought this three times now. My husband, who eats regular chocolate, steals it from my stash."

**Where It Falls Short**

Honesty requires addressing the downsides. The price-per-ounce is roughly double what you'd pay for mainstream candy. That's the cost of certified allergen-free production, but it's still a real budget consideration.

The flavor profile has limitations. It's sweet, period. There's no roasted, bitter, or fruity complexity. If you're a dark chocolate enthusiast who happens to be vegan, this won't scratch that itch — look for a high-percentage dark chocolate bar instead.

Finally, the resealable pouch is functional but flimsy. The zipper seal started failing after about a week of use on my test bag. If you buy the larger sizes, transfer the chocolate to an airtight container.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Allergy-affected families:** This is the primary audience. The top-9-allergen-free certification makes it a safe choice for school, camp, and shared spaces.
- **Vegans who miss milk chocolate:** If you want that nostalgic, creamy sweetness without the dairy, this hits the spot.
- **Anyone skeptical of vegan chocolate:** This is the product that converts skeptics. The taste-to-texture ratio is genuinely impressive.

**Skip it if:** You prefer dark, intense chocolate, or you're on a tight snack budget.

**Three Questions Buyers Ask**

**Does it actually taste like milk chocolate?**
Closer than any other dairy-free option I've tried. It's sweeter and less complex, but the mouthfeel is remarkably similar. Think of it as the vegan equivalent of a classic milk chocolate bar.

**Is it safe for severe nut allergies?**
Yes. The product is certified free of all major allergens including peanuts, tree nuts, dairy, soy, egg, wheat, and gluten. It's produced in a dedicated allergen-free facility.

**How should I store it?**
Keep it in a cool, dry place below 70°F. The resealable pouch works fine short-term, but for long-term storage, transfer to an airtight container to maintain freshness and texture.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need a genuinely safe, great-tasting chocolate for someone with food allergies, or you're a vegan craving that classic candy experience.

**Skip it if:** You're a dark chocolate purist or you need a budget-friendly everyday snack.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It does exactly what it promises, does it well, and fills a real gap in the market. The premium price and sweet-only flavor profile keep it from a perfect score, but for its intended audience, this is a winner.
