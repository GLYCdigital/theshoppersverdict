The Simply Charmed Pug brewer is 100% ceramic — no plastic, no metal, nothing that can leach off-flavors into your coffee. It sits securely over any standard mug via a wide, stable base.

### Brew Quality

This is where the Simply Charmed Pug shines. The ceramic body retains heat throughout the brew cycle, maintaining consistent water temperature for optimal extraction.

Reviewers consistently mention the same thing: this produces the best coffee they've made at home. The clean, bright flavor profile is a direct result of the plastic-free construction and proper heat retention.

**Best results come from:**
1. Freshly ground beans (medium grind)
2. Water just off the boil (195-205°F)

4. The bloom-pour method: 30-second bloom, then slow concentric pour

### Ease of Use

Pour over brewing isn't complicated, but it's more involved than a drip machine. Expect a 3-4 minute brew time for a single cup. The payoff is flavor that pod machines simply can't match.

Cleanup is straightforward: discard the filter and grounds, rinse the cone. That's it.

### FAQ

#### Is the Simply Charmed Pug pour over easy to clean?
Yes. Just remove the filter and grounds, rinse the ceramic cone under warm water, and let it air dry. Most users hand-wash rather than using the dishwasher.

#### Does the ceramic get too hot to handle?
The cone has a rim designed to stay cool enough to handle during brewing. The body does get hot, but the grip points remain comfortable.

#### Can I use this for cold brew?
This is designed for hot pour over brewing, not cold brew. However, you can brew concentrated coffee with it and pour over ice for iced coffee.

#### How many cups does it make at once?
It's a single-cup brewer. Each batch makes 8-12 oz of coffee. For multiple servings, you'll need to brew sequentially.
