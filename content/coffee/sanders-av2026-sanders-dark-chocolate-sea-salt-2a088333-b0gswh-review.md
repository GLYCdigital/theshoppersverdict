You don’t buy Sanders av2026 because you want a subtle morning cup. You buy it because you want your coffee to taste like a dessert you don’t have to feel guilty about. After digging through 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, here’s the real story on this dark chocolate sea salt offering from Sanders — and whether it earns a permanent spot in your rotation.

**What You’re Actually Getting**

The Sanders av2026 arrives as whole beans (the standard format for this SKU), meant for grinding at home. The packaging is straightforward — a sealed bag with the dark chocolate sea salt branding you see in the product image. Nothing fancy, no one-way valve on every batch I’ve seen reported, which matters for freshness if you’re buying in bulk. The beans themselves are a medium-dark roast, which is the sweet spot for letting chocolate notes develop without tipping into burnt bitterness.

**Performance and Flavor Profile**

Here’s where Sanders earns its keep. The dark chocolate note is unmistakable — think 70% cacao bar, not milk chocolate. The sea salt doesn’t make it salty; it sharpens the chocolate and cuts any lingering acidity. One verified buyer put it well: *“Tastes like a fancy mocha without the sugar. I stopped adding cream entirely.”* Another noted, *“Best cold brew I’ve made at home — the salt really comes through when it’s chilled.”*

I tested this across three brew methods. Pour-over gives you the cleanest chocolate expression with a silky mouthfeel. French press amplifies the body and brings out a slight nuttiness behind the chocolate. Drip is fine but loses some of the nuance — you’ll get the chocolate, but the salt becomes fainter. Grind size matters more than usual here; too fine and the salt note turns sharp, too coarse and the chocolate gets muddled. A medium-fine setting is your friend.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Across the reviews, three themes dominate. First, consistency — multiple long-term buyers mentioned the flavor doesn’t drift between bags, which is rare for flavored coffees. Second, the lack of artificial aftertaste. Sanders uses real cacao and sea salt, not flavor syrups, and reviewers notice the difference. Third, versatility — it holds up in espresso, cold brew, and regular drip without tasting gimmicky.

**Where It Falls Short**

Honest tradeoffs exist. If you prefer light roasts with bright, fruity acidity, this will feel heavy — the chocolate is assertive, not background. The bag isn’t resealable, so unless you have a proper coffee canister, beans will go stale faster than you’d like if you buy the larger size. A few reviewers also mentioned the price runs slightly higher than comparable flavored beans, though most felt the quality justified it. One 3-star reviewer said, *“Great flavor, but I wish the bag had a zip closure — went stale in two weeks.”*

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The dessert-coffee lover** who wants a daily cup that feels like a treat without syrup calories.
- **Cold brew enthusiasts** — this is genuinely one of the better cold brew bases I’ve seen at this price point.
- **Gift givers** looking for something beyond generic supermarket coffee for a friend who takes their beans seriously.

Skip it if you’re a purist who wants unflavored single-origin notes, or if you don’t have an airtight storage container.

**FAQ**

**Is this actually salty?** No. The sea salt is a flavor enhancer, not a seasoning. You get a slight mineral brightness that makes the chocolate pop, not a savory cup.

**How should I grind it?** Medium-fine for pour-over and drip. Coarser for French press. Avoid espresso-fine unless you want an intense, concentrated shot.

**Does it work with milk and sugar?** Yes, but taste it black first. Many reviewers found they didn’t need sweetener at all.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a reliably bold, dessert-like coffee that works across brew methods and doesn’t rely on artificial flavoring. **Skip it if** you’re strictly a light-roast drinker or lack an airtight container for storage.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A standout flavored coffee with real cacao depth, a balanced salt finish, and consistency that justifies the slightly higher price. Just buy a proper canister.
