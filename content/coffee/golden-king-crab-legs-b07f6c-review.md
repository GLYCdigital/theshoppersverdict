## Golden King Crab Legs Review: The Real Story on This 4.3-Star Seafood Staple

## What You're Actually Getting

The package arrives frozen solid — which is exactly what you want. These are flash-frozen at sea, meaning the crab is cooked and frozen within hours of being caught. That's the single biggest factor in how the final product tastes. You get whole legs and claws, typically running 1-2 pounds per order depending on the size you select.

As shown in the product image, the legs are a deep golden-red with visible shell intact. The meat-to-shell ratio is genuinely impressive for the price point. Most legs crack cleanly and yield thick, solid chunks of meat — not the watery, shredded texture you get from lower-tier crab.

## Performance and Real-World Use

Here's where this product earns its keep. I've tested a lot of seafood delivery, and the consistency on these is notable. The meat is sweet, with that clean, briny finish you want from king crab. It's not mushy, not fishy, and the texture holds up whether you steam, broil, or eat it cold with melted butter.

Thawing is straightforward: overnight in the fridge, or quick-thaw in cold water for about 20 minutes. The legs are pre-cooked, so you're really just reheating — don't overcook them or the meat will toughen.

One thing that surprised me: the salt level. Most batches are well-seasoned but balanced. A few reviewers noted some batches run brinier than others. That's a real inconsistency to flag, though it's not a dealbreaker — a quick rinse handles it.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this crab pre-cooked?**
Yes. It's cooked and flash-frozen at sea. You're just thawing and reheating — steam for 5-7 minutes or broil briefly with butter.

**Q: How many legs come in a pound?**
Typically 3-4 full legs plus a claw, depending on size. Smaller legs mean more pieces per pound; larger legs are fewer but meatier.

**Q: Can I refreeze what I don't use?**
Technically yes, but it degrades the texture. Better to thaw only what you'll eat in one sitting. The meat gets watery and mushy if refrozen.
