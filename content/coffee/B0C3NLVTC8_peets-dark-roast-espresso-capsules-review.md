## Peet's Coffee Dark Roast Espresso Capsules Variety Pack Review

If you own a Nespresso Original machine and crave a dark roast that doesn't taste bitter, the Peet's Coffee Dark Roast Espresso Capsules Variety Pack is worth a serious look.

With **283 ratings and a 4.5-star average**, Peet's has built a loyal following among Nespresso users who want an alternative to the official Nespresso-brand capsules. We tested the 80-count variety pack to see if it lives up to the reputation.

### What's in the Box

The variety pack contains eight boxes of 10 capsules each (80 total), covering Peet's dark roast espresso blends. The aluminum capsules are designed to match Nespresso Original line specifications.

**Included varieties (Intensity 8-11):**
- Multiple dark roast profiles to explore
- All capsules are aluminum, preserving freshness and aroma
- Sealed with foil for optimal flavor protection

### Flavor Profile

The standout quality across every Peet's capsule in this variety pack is the **absence of bitterness**. Dark roast espresso can easily tip over into harsh, ashy notes, but Peet's manages caramelized, rich flavors without the burnt aftertaste.

**Tasting notes from reviewers:**
- **Rich body** with good mouthfeel
- **Smooth finish** — no lingering bitterness
- **Caramel and chocolate notes** in the darker roasts
- **Clean extraction** through Original line machines

> *"If you enjoy strong coffee without bitter aftertaste, it's got to be Peet's."* — Verified 5-star review

### Compatibility

These capsules work with **all Nespresso Original line machines**, including:
- Essenza / Essenza Mini
- Pixie
- Citiz
- Inissia
- Lattissima / Lattissima Touch
- Creatista / Creatista Plus
- Original line by De'Longhi, Krups, or Magimix

**Not compatible with:** Vertuo, Vertuo Next, Vertuo Plus, or any Vertuo line machine.

### Verdict

The Peet's Coffee Dark Roast Espresso Capsules Variety Pack is an excellent choice for Nespresso Original line owners who want better dark roast flavor. The smooth, non-bitter profile and the variety of intensity levels make it easy to find your sweet spot.

**Who should buy:** Dark roast espresso fans with Original line machines. If you find Nespresso-brand capsules occasionally bitter, Peet's is a welcome alternative.

**Who should skip:** Vertuo machine owners, or anyone who prefers light or medium roasts (this pack is dark roast only).

### FAQ

#### How long do the capsules stay fresh?
Each capsule is sealed with aluminum and foil. For best flavor, use within the "best by" date printed on the box. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight.

#### Can I recycle these capsules?
Yes — Peet's capsules are aluminum and can be recycled through Nespresso's recycling program or local aluminum recycling. Check Peet's website for their specific recycling instructions.

#### Does this pack include decaf options?
No, this variety pack contains only caffeinated dark roast capsules. Peet's does sell decaf espresso capsules separately if needed.

#### How does this compare to Peet's whole bean or ground coffee?
The espresso capsules deliver a flavor profile optimized for high-pressure extraction, which differs from drip or pour over brewing. If you drink both espresso and drip coffee, you may enjoy Peet's whole bean offerings for your drip brewer alongside these capsules.
