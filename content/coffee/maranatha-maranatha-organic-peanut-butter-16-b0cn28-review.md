Let’s be clear about what this is: Maranatha Organic Peanut Butter is a two-ingredient product. Actually, it’s a one-ingredient product — organic dry roasted peanuts. That’s it. No palm oil to keep it emulsified, no sugar to mask the roast, no salt to punch up the flavor. What you get is peanut butter in its purest form, and that comes with both rewards and responsibilities.

## What’s Actually in the Jar

The 16-ounce jar arrives with a visible layer of peanut oil sitting on top. This isn’t a defect — it’s the natural separation that happens when you don’t add stabilizers. As shown in the product image, the oil layer is substantial, and it will take a solid two to three minutes of vigorous stirring to incorporate it back into the paste. Use a butter knife, not a spoon — the narrow blade reaches the bottom corners more effectively.

Once stirred, the texture is surprisingly smooth. It’s not the thick, dry paste you might expect from natural peanut butter. Maranatha dry-roasts the peanuts long enough to release their natural oils, which yields a creamier result than brands like Santa Cruz or Kirkland’s organic option.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The flavor is where this jar earns its keep. The roasting is aggressive — you get a deep, almost toasted aroma that hits your nose the moment you open the lid. It tastes noticeably fresher than mass-market brands, with a clean finish that doesn’t coat your mouth with residual sweetness.

Spreadability is good, but it’s temperature-dependent. Straight from a cold pantry, it’s stiff. Give it 10 minutes at room temperature, and it glides onto bread without tearing the slice. For baking or smoothies, it incorporates cleanly without clumping.

The 16-ounce jar is the one practical gripe. Most competitors ship 18-ounce jars at a comparable price point, so you’re getting roughly 11% less product for the same money. Over multiple purchases, that adds up.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews holds up. The recurring theme in customer feedback is the ingredient list. One verified buyer put it simply: “Finally a peanut butter my kids and I can agree on — nothing weird in it.” Another noted the freshness: “You can taste the difference immediately. It’s like the peanuts were roasted yesterday.”

The organic certification matters to many buyers who’ve switched from conventional brands to avoid pesticide residues. The dry-roasting process also means the peanuts aren’t cooked in oil, which reduces the overall fat content slightly compared to oil-roasted alternatives.

## Where It Falls Short

The stirring requirement is the most common complaint in negative reviews. If you’re not prepared to work for your peanut butter, this jar will frustrate you. One reviewer summed it up: “Great taste, but I feel like I need a workout before I can make a sandwich.”

The lack of salt is a legitimate flavor consideration. If you’ve grown up on Jif or Skippy, this will taste flat initially. Your palate will adjust after a jar or two, but it’s a real adjustment period. The consistency can also vary between batches — some jars come out thicker, some thinner, which is inherent to single-ingredient production.

## Who Should Buy This

This jar is for you if you’re already eating clean, checking labels, and willing to put in a few minutes of stirring for a product with zero additives. It’s ideal for families avoiding palm oil, which is a common additive in commercial peanut butters and has environmental concerns attached to its production.

Skip it if you want convenience above all else, or if you need that salty-sweet balance in your peanut butter. If you’re baking, the unsalted version actually gives you better control over your recipe’s sodium content, so this is a plus for bakers.

## FAQ

**Is this peanut butter really just peanuts?**
Yes. The label lists one ingredient: organic dry roasted peanuts. No salt, no sugar, no palm oil, no preservatives.

**How long does it last after opening?**
Maranatha recommends consuming within 3-4 months of opening. Store it in the pantry, not the fridge — cold temperatures make it nearly impossible to spread.

**Does it need to be refrigerated?**
No. Refrigeration will harden the oil and make stirring even more difficult. Keep it at room temperature and stir before each use.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely clean peanut butter with exceptional roasted flavor and you don’t mind the stirring ritual. **Skip it if** you want grab-and-go convenience or need that salty-sweet profile.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting half a star for the smaller jar size and the aggressive oil separation that makes first-time stirring a chore. The flavor and ingredient purity are otherwise outstanding.
