I've tested this against GT's and Brew Dr. brands. Health-Ade has a thicker mouthfeel — almost like a light juice — and the carbonation is assertive without being aggressive. If you're drinking this for gut health, the potency is legit.

As shown in the product image, the cans have a clean, modern design — the pink apple illustration pops on the shelf, and the branding is clear about what's inside. No cryptic "natural flavors" hiding questionable additives.
