When you're staring down a sad desk salad or a bowl of soup that needs a texture intervention, a bag of crispy wonton strips is the cheap thrill that saves the meal. The 365 by Whole Foods Market Organic Wonton Strips do exactly that job — and they do it with a cleaner ingredient list than the standard grocery store options.

**What you actually get**

The 3.5-ounce bag is modest. To give you a visual, that's roughly the size of a small chip bag — enough for about 4–6 salad servings or 2–3 soup bowls if you're generous. The strips themselves are the classic crinkle-cut fried wonton shape, light golden brown, with visible seasoning clinging to the surface. They're made with organic wheat flour and organic soybean oil — nothing artificial in the ingredient lineup, which is the main reason you'd reach for this brand over the store-brand alternative.

**Real-world performance**

I tested these three ways: on a chopped romaine salad with sesame dressing, floating on top of hot miso soup, and straight from the bag like a snack (I have no shame). The texture holds up. They arrive crunchy, not stale, and that crunch survives contact with moist ingredients longer than you'd expect. On the salad, they stayed crisp for a solid 10–15 minutes before softening — plenty of time to eat without rushing. In soup, they go soft fast, which is fine if that's your thing, but if you want the crunch, add them right before eating.

The flavor is where these shine. They're salted well — maybe a touch aggressively — with a savory, almost buttery note from the frying oil. One reviewer put it simply: "These are dangerously snackable. I put them on a salad and then just ate the rest of the bag watching TV." Another noted, "Crunchier than the ones I usually buy from the Asian grocery store. Perfect for my chicken salad." That tracks with what I found — the texture-to-flavor ratio is better balanced than most comparable products.

**Where it falls short**

The honest downsides: the bag is small, and the price per ounce is higher than non-organic wonton strips. If you're feeding a family or making a big batch of salad for meal prep, buy two bags. The salt level is also worth flagging — one Amazon reviewer said "a bit too salty for me to eat alone, but great on salads." If you're watching sodium, these might be a once-in-a-while treat rather than a pantry staple.

Also, once you open the bag, you need to re-seal it well. These strips absorb humidity and go stale faster than you'd think. I left one bag half-open overnight and the next day's crunch was noticeably muted.

**Who should buy this**

- **Salad lovers** who want a crunchy topper without making croutons from scratch
- **Soup fans** who like wonton strips floating on top of ramen, pho, or egg drop soup
- **Clean-label shoppers** who prefer organic ingredients over the conventional alternatives
- **Snackers** who want something salty and crunchy but don't care about portion control (fair warning)

**FAQ**

*Are these gluten-free?*
No. They're made with organic wheat flour, so they contain gluten.

*Can I use these in place of fried noodles for Asian dishes?*
Yes. They're essentially the same product — crispy wonton strips work interchangeably with chow mein noodles in most recipes.

*How should I store them after opening?*
Re-seal the bag tightly and keep them in a cool, dry place. They'll stay crunchy for 2–3 weeks. For longer storage, you can freeze them, though I haven't tested that myself.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a clean, organic crunchy topping that performs well on salads and soups and you're willing to pay a small premium for the ingredient list.

**Skip it if** you're on a strict budget, need a large quantity, or you're sensitive to salt levels.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A smaller bag than I'd like, but the quality and crunch make it a repeat purchase.
