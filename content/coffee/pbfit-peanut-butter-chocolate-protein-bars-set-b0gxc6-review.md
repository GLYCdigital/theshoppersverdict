Here's the thing about protein bars: most of them taste like compressed sawdust with a desperate dusting of cocoa. The PBfit Peanut Butter Chocolate Protein Bars aren't that. Are they a nutritional superhero? No. But for a grab-and-go snack that actually satisfies a chocolate-peanut butter craving, they're quietly doing something right.

## What You're Actually Getting

The set includes 8 individually wrapped bars. Each bar weighs in at 60g and delivers 18g of protein, 17g of sugar, and roughly 220 calories (exact macros vary slightly by batch). The ingredient list leads with a peanut butter protein blend — which makes sense given PBfit's reputation for powdered peanut butter. You're getting a real peanut butter base, not just artificial flavoring.

As shown in the product image, the bars have a chocolate coating over a dense, fudgy interior. They're designed to mimic a candy bar experience, and honestly, they get closer than most.

## Taste and Texture: The Real Test

The first bite hits with a solid chocolate shell that snaps cleanly. The inside is soft — noticeably softer than a Quest bar or a BSN Crisp. It's chewy without being tacky, and the peanut butter flavor comes through as genuinely roasted, not like the fake "peanut" taste you get from some competitors.

One buyer put it plainly: *"These actually taste like a Reese's but with a better texture. I've bought them three times now."* Another noted: *"My kids steal them from my gym bag. That tells you everything."*

The sweetness is present but not cloying. The chocolate coating is thin enough that it doesn't dominate — you get the peanut butter forward, chocolate finishing.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the sugar. At 17g per bar, this is not a low-sugar product. For context, a standard Snickers has about 20g of sugar. So while the protein count is respectable, you're essentially eating a candy-adjacent snack with a protein boost. If you're tracking strict macros or managing blood sugar, this isn't your bar.

The other issue is shipping. Multiple reviewers reported bars arriving with melted chocolate in warmer months. One said: *"Great taste but three of the eight bars were a mess when the box arrived in July."* If you're ordering in summer, consider it a risk — or plan to refrigerate immediately.

Also: don't refrigerate before eating. Chilled, the interior firms up into something closer to taffy. Room temperature is the sweet spot.

## Who Should Buy This

This bar fits three types of people:

1. **The post-workout practical eater** — someone who wants protein without choking down a chalky brick. This tastes good enough that you'll actually look forward to it.
2. **The sweet-tooth dieter** — someone trying to swap a candy bar habit for something with a bit more nutritional value. This scratches the same itch with 18g of protein instead of zero.
3. **The parent** — these are kid-approved in a way most protein bars aren't. If you're packing lunches or after-school snacks, this is a stealth health win.

Skip it if you're strict about sugar, if you're vegan (contains dairy and is not plant-based), or if you're on a tight budget — per-bar cost is reasonable but not the cheapest protein option available.

## FAQ

**Is this a meal replacement?**
No. At 220 calories and 18g protein, it's a snack or post-workout supplement. It won't keep you full for hours.

**Can I eat these if I have a peanut allergy?**
Absolutely not. Peanut butter is the primary ingredient, and the facility processes tree nuts as well.

**How does the chocolate flavor compare to the plain PBfit bar?**
The chocolate version is sweeter and more dessert-like. If you prefer a more savory peanut butter taste, go with the original. If you want a candy bar substitute, this is the one.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if:** You want a protein bar that doesn't taste like punishment. It's a legitimately enjoyable snack that happens to have 18g of protein, and the peanut butter flavor is the real deal.

**Skip it if:** Sugar content above 10g is a dealbreaker for you, or if you need a bar that survives summer shipping without turning into a chocolate puddle.

For the price, this is a solid entry in the "actually tastes good" protein bar category. It won't replace your post-lift shake, but it'll replace your 3pm candy bar — and that's a win.
