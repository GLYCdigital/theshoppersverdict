**The sugar-free candy that actually tastes like candy — mostly**

Most sugar-free gummies taste like a compromise: artificial, chalky, or with a weird fiber aftertaste that makes you regret the whole experiment. SmartSweets Hard Candy is better than that. It's not perfect, but it's the closest thing I've found to real gummy candy that won't spike your blood sugar.

**What you actually get**

Each box contains six 1.8-ounce bags of fruit-flavored hard candy. The flavors skew classic — peach, watermelon, sour apple, and berry blends — and the pieces are small, roughly the size of a jelly bean. As shown in the product image, the bags are bright and colorful, but don't expect a massive haul. At 1.8 ounces per bag, this is a snack-sized portion, not a shareable bag.

**Performance and real-world use**

The candy delivers on its core promise: 3 grams of sugar per whole bag, with 5 grams of fiber and only 90 calories. The sweetness comes from a blend of stevia and monk fruit, which is a smart choice — no artificial sweeteners like sucralose or aspartame. The flavors are genuinely good. The peach is juicy, the watermelon actually tastes like watermelon, and the sour apple has a pleasant tart kick that doesn't overwhelm.

The texture is where things get interesting. These are "hard candy" in name, but they chew more like a dense gummy. They're softer than traditional hard candies and require a bit more work than a standard gummy bear. Some buyers love this — one reviewer noted, "Finally a candy that doesn't feel like I'm eating a vitamin." Others found it too chewy, with one saying, "The texture took some getting used to, but the flavor won me over."

**What buyers consistently praise**

The low sugar count is the headline feature, and it's earned. Reviewers on keto, diabetic, and low-carb diets repeatedly mention this as a staple. One Amazon reviewer wrote, "I've tried every sugar-free gummy on the market. These are the only ones I actually finish the bag." Another praised the lack of digestive issues: "No bloating, no stomach cramps. Just candy."

**Where it falls short**

The stevia-monk fruit blend leaves a subtle cooling sensation on the back of the tongue. It's not overpowering, but it's noticeable — especially with the sour apple flavor. If you're sensitive to stevia's bitterness, this might be a dealbreaker.

The portion size is also a sticking point. At roughly $2.50 per bag (when bought in the six-pack), you're paying a significant premium over conventional candy. The bags are small enough that you'll likely finish one in a single sitting — which is arguably the point, but it makes the price hard to swallow if you're a volume snacker.

**Who should buy this**

- **Keto and low-carb dieters** who need a candy fix without blowing their macros
- **Diabetics** looking for a sweet treat that won't spike glucose
- **Parents** who want to limit their kids' sugar intake without making them feel deprived

Skip it if you're looking for a cheap sugar-free option — this is a premium product with a premium price. Also skip it if you're sensitive to stevia's aftertaste.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this taste like real gummy candy?**
A: Close. The flavors are authentic and the sweetness is convincing, but the texture is denser and chewier than standard gummies, and there's a mild stevia finish.

**Q: Is this keto-friendly?**
A: Yes. Each bag has 3g net carbs (5g fiber, 3g sugar, 0g sugar alcohols). It fits standard keto macros.

**Q: How much is in a bag?**
A: 1.8 ounces — roughly 20-25 small pieces. It's a snack portion, not a shareable bag.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely tasty sugar-free candy that won't wreck your diet. **Skip it if** you're on a tight budget or can't tolerate stevia's aftertaste.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — a solid, honest sugar-free option that delivers on flavor and macros, with minor texture and price tradeoffs.
