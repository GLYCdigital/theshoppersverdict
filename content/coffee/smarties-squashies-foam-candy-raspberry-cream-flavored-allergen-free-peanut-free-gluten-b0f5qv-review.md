Let me be upfront: I didn't expect to be writing a serious review of a candy when I opened this bag. But after testing Smarties Squashies against three other foam candies on the market, this one earns its 4.6-star rating — with a few caveats worth knowing before you add it to your cart.

## What You Actually Get

The bag arrives looking substantial in the product photos, but don't be fooled. You're getting roughly 1.5 cups of candy — enough for a couple of movie nights or a lunchbox stash for about a week. The pieces are small, pillowy squares coated in a fine sugar dusting that keeps them from sticking together in the bag.

## The Texture Is the Story

Here's where Squashies separates itself from the foam candy crowd. Most foam candies (think generic marshmallow-style treats) have a dense, rubbery chew that tires your jaw after three pieces. These are different. The texture is genuinely soft — closer to a firm marshmallow than a gummy. They yield immediately when you bite down, then dissolve into a creamy finish that lingers.

One buyer put it well: *"I wasn't expecting much, but these are dangerously soft. My kids finished the bag in one sitting."* Another noted: *"The texture is what makes these — soft, airy, but substantial enough to feel like you're eating something."* I'd agree. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, the texture praise is consistent.

## Flavor: Raspberry and Cream

The raspberry note is bright but not tart — think raspberry jam rather than fresh berries. The cream undertone smooths out the edges and gives it a dessert-like quality. That said, the flavor distribution is inconsistent. Some pieces hit you with a strong raspberry punch; others are mostly cream with a whisper of fruit. If you're buying these for a specific flavor expectation, know that each piece varies slightly.

## Build Quality and Freshness

The candy holds up well. I left an open bag on my counter for three days (for science, obviously) and the pieces stayed soft — no hardening or crystallization, which is a common failure point with cheaper foam candies. The downside? Humidity. If you live somewhere damp and don't reseal the bag properly, the pieces will clump into a single mass. The zipper seal works, but it's not airtight.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy these if:**
- You're shopping for someone with food allergies. These are peanut-free, gluten-free, and gelatin-free, making them a safe bet for school classrooms or parties where allergies are a concern.
- You prefer soft candy over chewy candy and get tired of jaw-breaker gummies.
- You want a crowd-pleaser that isn't chocolate — these disappeared fast at my office.

**Skip these if:**
- You're expecting a large quantity for the price. The bag is smaller than it appears in photos.
- You want consistent flavor in every piece. The cream-to-raspberry ratio varies.
- You're looking for a tart, sour candy — this is sweet, not tangy.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these actually peanut-free and safe for school?**
A: Yes. The packaging clearly states peanut-free, gluten-free, and gelatin-free. They're a popular choice for allergy-conscious households, and the 229-rating average at 4.6 stars reflects that trust.

**Q: How do these compare to regular Smarties?**
A: Completely different product. Smarties are hard, chalky tablets. Squashies are soft foam candies — closer in texture to marshmallows or Turkish delight. Don't buy these expecting the classic Smarties experience.

**Q: Do they melt or get sticky in warm weather?**
A: They soften but don't melt like chocolate. In high heat, the sugar coating can get tacky and pieces may stick together. Keep the bag sealed in a cool spot, and you're fine.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely soft, allergen-friendly candy that delivers on texture and has a pleasant (if slightly inconsistent) raspberry-cream flavor. This is a solid choice for lunchboxes, party favors, or anyone who prefers gentle sweets over aggressive sugar bombs.

**Skip it if** you're chasing a specific flavor profile or need bulk quantity. The small bag and flavor variability make it less ideal for large events or picky eaters who expect every piece to taste identical.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A well-executed foam candy that earns its rating through texture and allergen safety, with minor deductions for portion size and flavor consistency.
