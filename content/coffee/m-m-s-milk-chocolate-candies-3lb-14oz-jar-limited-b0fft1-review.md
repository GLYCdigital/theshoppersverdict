The candies themselves are standard M&M's — that familiar thin candy shell over milk chocolate. No surprises in flavor, which is exactly what most people want. The color mix appears consistent with what you'd find in regular bags, so don't expect any special-edition hues here.

Where this jar shines is versatility. Reviewers report using it for:
- Party favors and candy buffets
- Baking (chocolate chip cookies, brownies, trail mix)

## Where It Falls Short

Let's not sugarcoat it. The biggest issue is portion control. With no individual packs, it's dangerously easy to grab handful after handful. If you're someone who can't trust yourself with a bottomless candy supply, this jar is a temptation machine.

The footprint is also substantial. The jar is roughly the size of a small paint bucket, so it won't fit neatly in most cabinet shelves. You'll want dedicated counter or pantry space.

Finally, a few reviewers reported receiving jars with some broken or cracked candies from shipping. This isn't widespread — the majority had intact product — but if you're a stickler for perfect shells, know that transit can cause some breakage.
