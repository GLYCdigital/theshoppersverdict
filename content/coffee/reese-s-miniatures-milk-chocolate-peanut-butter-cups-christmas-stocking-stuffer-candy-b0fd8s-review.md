You’re getting a bag of individually wrapped miniature peanut butter cups — roughly 1.5-inch diameter each, about half the size of a standard cup. **How they perform in the real world**

The chocolate is smooth, the peanut butter filling is that signature sweet-salty blend that’s earned REESE’S its cult following. The miniature size actually works in their favor — you get the full flavor hit without committing to a full 200-calorie cup. That makes them dangerously easy to keep eating, but for stocking stuffers or office candy bowls, that’s a feature, not a bug.

One thing buyers consistently mention in reviews is freshness. Because each piece is foil-wrapped, they hold up well — one reviewer noted, “They were fresh and didn’t have that stale taste you sometimes get with holiday candy.” Another said, “Perfect for my son’s Christmas countdown — he loves the mini size.” That tracks with what you’d expect from a sealed, individually wrapped product.

**Where they fall short**

Let’s talk about the ratio. Miniatures have a slightly higher chocolate-to-peanut-butter ratio than full-size cups. If you’re a purist who lives for that thick peanut butter center, you’ll notice the difference. It’s not a dealbreaker, but it’s real.

The bigger issue is value. You’re paying a premium for Christmas wrappers. The same weight of standard REESE’S Miniatures is typically cheaper year-round. You’re not getting more candy — you’re getting holiday-themed foil. If you’re buying these for a stocking, that’s fine. If you’re buying them for everyday snacking, skip the holiday markup.

Also worth noting: these are seasonal. Once the holidays are over, they vanish from shelves until next year. If you fall in love with the festive wrappers, buy multiple bags.
