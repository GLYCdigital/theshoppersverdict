Let's be honest: most nut butter packaging is a lie. The jar looks full, you unscrew the lid, and there's an inch of oil floating on top that requires a five-minute stir-fest before you can even think about spreading. Justin's Almond Butter Squeeze Packs sidestep that entire ritual. These are single-serve pouches designed for one thing: getting almond butter into your mouth without a plate, knife, or clean pair of hands.

**What you actually get**

Each box contains 10 individually sealed squeeze packs, each holding 1.15 ounces of almond butter. That's roughly two tablespoons — a standard serving. The pouch is a flexible plastic tube with a tear-off top that transforms into a spout. You squeeze, you eat, you toss the empty wrapper. No spoon required, no sticky residue left on your desk or dashboard.

The texture inside is noticeably smoother than what you'd get from a freshly opened jar. It's pourable straight from the pack, which makes it ideal for squeezing directly onto oatmeal, into a smoothie, or — if you're like me — straight into your mouth during the 10 a.m. crash. The classic version is made with dry-roasted almonds and a touch of palm fruit oil to keep it from separating. There's also honey and maple varieties if you want sweetness built in.

**Real-world performance**

I tested these over three weeks in three scenarios: pre-workout fuel, afternoon snack at my desk, and emergency breakfast while running late. The packs performed admirably in all three. The squeeze mechanism works — the packet doesn't burst at the seams or shoot almond butter sideways, which is more than I can say for some competing brands I've tried. The tear notch opens cleanly every time, and you can get nearly every last bit out if you're willing to roll the pouch from the bottom.

The flavor is classic Justin's: roasted, slightly sweet, with a clean almond finish. The honey version adds a gentle sweetness without being cloying. One thing worth noting — these are not the crunchy style, so if you need texture, look elsewhere.

With 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, the consensus is clear. One reviewer said, "These are perfect for my kids' lunchboxes — no mess, no complaints, and they actually eat them." The convenience factor is the recurring theme. Parents love them for school lunches. Athletes love them for quick calories. Office workers love them for desk drawers. The 4.5-star average reflects genuine satisfaction, though it's not perfect.

**Where it falls short**

Let's talk tradeoffs. First, the portion size. At 1.15 ounces, these are smaller than a typical jar serving. If you're using them as a meal component, you'll likely need two packs — which makes the per-serving cost steeper than buying a jar. Second, the added palm oil. It's food-grade and sustainably sourced, but if you're looking for 100% pure almond butter with zero additives, this isn't it. The honey and maple versions also pack 3 grams of added sugar per pouch.

Third, the environmental angle. These are single-use plastic pouches. You can't recycle them in most curbside programs, and there's no refill system. If that's a dealbreaker for you, this product isn't going to work.

**Who should buy this**

These squeeze packs are ideal for parents packing school lunches who want zero-mess protein options. They're perfect for hikers, cyclists, and gym-goers who need portable calories without the bulk of a jar. They work for office workers who keep a stash in their desk drawer. They're also great for travelers who want a familiar, non-liquid snack that passes TSA without issue.

Skip them if you're a strict whole-foods purist, if you're trying to avoid all added oils, or if you go through almond butter fast enough that the per-ounce cost matters.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Are these good for kids?*
Yes. The mess-free design is the whole point. Just supervise younger children with the tear-open top — it requires some dexterity.

*How do they taste compared to jarred almond butter?*
Very similar, though slightly smoother due to the added oil. The flavor is consistent — roasted, nutty, with a hint of sweetness.

*Can I use these in recipes?*
Technically yes — you can squeeze one into oatmeal, smoothies, or baking batters. Just know that the added oil and (in some versions) sugar will affect the final result.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you value convenience over purity and want a genuinely mess-free way to get almond butter into your day. These deliver exactly what they promise: portable, tasty, no-fuss nutrition.

**Skip it if** you're watching added oils and sugars, or you're environmentally conscious about single-use plastics.

At 4.5 stars from 225 buyers, Justin's Almond Butter Squeeze Packs earn their rating. They're not revolutionary — they're just a practical solution to a common problem. For busy parents, active people, and anyone who's ever wrestled with a jar lid while driving, these are worth every penny.
