## Performance and Build Quality

Here's where this decaf separates itself from the pack. It uses the Swiss Water Process, which strips caffeine using water and charcoal filtration rather than chemical solvents like methylene chloride. That's not just a marketing talking point — it genuinely affects flavor. Chemical-processed decafs often carry a sharp, acrid note. This one doesn't.

In testing across multiple brew methods, the medium roast holds its own. In a drip machine, you get a clean, balanced cup with low acidity and a smooth finish. In a French press, it gains a bit more body and brings out some subtle chocolate notes. It's not going to knock your socks off like a single-origin light roast, but for a decaf, it's remarkably close to the real thing.

The grind consistency is solid — uniform particles, no excessive fines that clog filters or cause over-extraction. That's a detail budget brands often skip.

## FAQ

**Is this actually 100% caffeine-free?**
Swiss Water Process removes 99.9% of caffeine. Trace amounts remain, but it's negligible — safe for most people avoiding caffeine for medical reasons.

**How should I store the grounds after opening?**
Keep the bag sealed with the zipper, store in a cool dark place. Don't refrigerate — the moisture and odors from your fridge will ruin the grounds. Use within 3-4 weeks for best flavor.

**Can I use this in a Keurig or Nespresso?**
No — it's ground coffee, not pods. You'd need the My K-Cup reusable filter or an equivalent. It works fine, but you'll need to adjust the scoop size.
