Let me be blunt: True Citrus Iced Tea isn't actually tea. It's a powdered drink mix with tea flavoring. But if you're looking for a zero-sugar, instant cold beverage that tastes like a lightly sweetened Arnold Palmer, this 4.2-star rated canister (210+ Amazon reviews) might be exactly what your kitchen needs.

**What's actually in the box**

You get one plastic canister containing roughly 30 servings of powder. The scoop is inside. That's it. No loose tea, no bags, no brewing equipment required. As shown in the product image, it's a straightforward white canister with citrus branding — nothing fancy, but the packaging does a decent job of keeping the powder dry.

**The real-world performance**

Here's where this product shines: it dissolves immediately in cold tap water. No hot water step, no steeping timer, no waiting for a pitcher to chill. Stir for ten seconds and you have a full glass of iced tea-flavored beverage. That convenience factor alone justifies the price point for anyone who drinks iced tea daily.

The flavor profile is lemon-forward with a mild black tea background. It's not going to fool a tea snob — this is clearly a mix, not a brewed product. But for what it is, it's refreshing and balanced. The sweetness comes from a blend of stevia and erythritol, which keeps it at 5 calories per serving.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The sugar-free angle gets the most love in the reviews. One verified purchaser wrote, "Finally an iced tea mix that doesn't spike my blood sugar. I've replaced my afternoon soda habit with this." Another noted, "I take this to work and just add water from the cooler. Tastes better than the bottled stuff and costs way less."

Multiple reviewers also highlight the value proposition. At roughly 30 servings per canister, the per-glass cost lands well below bottled iced tea. For heavy iced tea drinkers, that adds up fast.

**Where it falls short**

The artificial sweetener aftertaste is the most consistent complaint. Stevia has a distinct cooling, slightly bitter finish that not everyone enjoys. If you're sensitive to sugar substitutes, this will bother you more than most.

There's also a freshness issue. Once opened, the powder can clump if moisture gets in. Store it away from the sink or dishwasher. And the plastic lid isn't the sturdiest — several reviewers mention it cracking after a few months of use. Handle it gently.

**Who should buy this**

- **Keto and low-carb dieters**: This fits cleanly into your macros with zero net carbs
- **Busy professionals**: 10 seconds to a full glass of iced tea beats any drive-thru
- **Diabetics**: The stevia/erythritol blend doesn't spike blood glucose
- **Budget-conscious tea drinkers**: Noticeably cheaper per serving than bottled options

**Skip it if** you want authentic brewed tea flavor, if you hate stevia aftertaste, or if you're looking for a hot tea experience.

**FAQ**

**Q: How does it taste compared to brewed iced tea?**
A: It's closer to a flavored sparkling water than brewed tea. Lighter, sweeter, with a citrus-forward profile. Fine for what it is, but not a substitute if you want real tea depth.

**Q: Can I use it in hot water for hot tea?**
A: Technically yes, but it's formulated for cold. Hot water makes the sweetener taste more pronounced and artificial. Stick to cold.

**Q: Is it truly zero sugar?**
A: Yes. Zero grams of sugar per serving. The sweetness comes from stevia leaf extract and erythritol, which are sugar alcohols and natural sweeteners.
