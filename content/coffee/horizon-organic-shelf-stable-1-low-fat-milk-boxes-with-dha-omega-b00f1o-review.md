**The Short Version:** If you’ve ever dumped a half-gallon of spoiled milk down the drain, these Horizon Organic shelf-stable boxes are your insurance policy. They’re not a perfect fresh-milk substitute, but for coffee creamer, cereal, camping, or emergency backup, they deliver organic 1% milk with added DHA that actually tastes decent.

---

**What You’re Actually Getting**

Each pack contains eight 8-ounce Tetra Pak cartons — the same aseptic packaging used for broth and coconut milk. The key spec: **unopened boxes last 6+ months at room temperature**. Once opened, treat them like regular milk — fridge and finish within 7-10 days.

Nutrition per box: 120 calories, 8g protein, 25% of your daily calcium, plus **32mg of DHA omega-3** from algal oil. That DHA is the differentiator — most shelf-stable milks skip it entirely.

---

**Real-World Performance**

I tested these three ways: straight from the box, over cereal, and as coffee creamer.

- **Straight up:** There’s a noticeable “UHT twang” — that slightly cooked, slightly sweet note from the ultra-high-temperature pasteurization. It’s milder than some brands, but if you’re a fresh-milk purist, you’ll notice it.
- **Over cereal:** Honestly indistinguishable from fresh. The cold cereal masks any processing flavor.
- **In coffee:** Works great. The 1% fat steams and mixes without separating, and the subtle sweetness actually complements dark roasts.

**The DHA factor:** Zero fishy taste. Horizon uses algae-derived DHA, and the 32mg per serving is meaningful for brain health — about 20% of the daily recommendation for adults.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let’s be honest about the tradeoffs:

1. **The taste difference is real.** If you drink milk by the glass daily, you’ll detect the UHT flavor. It’s not bad — it’s just different.
2. **Cost per ounce stings.** You’re paying roughly $0.50-0.70 per 8oz box versus $0.15-0.20 for conventional fresh milk. Organic + convenience + DHA = premium pricing.
3. **Packaging waste.** Eight individual cartons per pack. Yes, Tetra Paks are recyclable where facilities exist, but that’s not everywhere.

**FAQ**

No. Store at room temperature for up to 6 months. Refrigerate only after opening, then consume within 7-10 days.

**Q: Is the DHA from fish or algae?**
Algae. That’s why there’s no fishy taste. It’s a vegan-sourced omega-3, safe for kids and adults.

**Q: How does this taste compared to fresh organic milk?**
