**What You Actually Get**

Each 1.5-ounce bag contains oven-roasted chicken breast meat — nothing more than chicken, water, and sea salt. That's the entire ingredient list. No nitrates, no preservatives, no sugar hiding in the mix. The packaging is straightforward: a resealable pouch that's easy to toss in a backpack or glovebox. **Performance and Real-World Use**

I've tested this against the usual jerky suspects, and the texture is where True Story separates itself. The chicken is sliced thin but retains a tender, almost rotisserie-like bite. It's not chewy-tough like beef jerky; it pulls apart cleanly and doesn't leave that greasy residue on your fingers. The flavor is mild, lightly salted, with a subtle roasted finish that tastes like actual chicken rather than smoke-flavored chemicals.

One thing that stands out: it doesn't need refrigeration, which makes it a legit option for hiking, travel, or desk drawers. Each serving delivers 12 grams of protein for 70 calories — that's a solid macro ratio if you're tracking intake.

**Where It Falls Short**
