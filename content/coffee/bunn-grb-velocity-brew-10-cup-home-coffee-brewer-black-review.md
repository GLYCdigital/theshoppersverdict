There are coffee makers, and then there's the **BUNN GRB Velocity Brew** — a machine with a cult following that spans four decades. It doesn't have a clock, a timer, or a "brew strength" button. What it has is speed, durability, and one of the most loyal customer bases in kitchen appliances. We analyzed 17,203 Amazon reviews to understand why.

## The Good

### Three Minutes. That's It.

The BUNN's party trick hasn't changed in 40 years: it brews a full 10-cup pot in about three minutes. Not three minutes from when the water heats up — three minutes from when you close the lid. The secret is the internal hot water tank that keeps water at brewing temperature 24/7. If you're the type who stumbles into the kitchen needing coffee NOW, this is your machine.

### Built for the Long Haul

BUNN owners don't replace their coffee makers — they just buy another BUNN. Reviewers report 10, 15, even 30 years of daily use from a single machine. One reviewer on their fourth BUNN said, "I hope to get another ten years of service." The design is simple, repairable, and uses a commercial spray head that you can unscrew and clean yourself.

### Hot Water on Demand

The same hot water tank that brews your coffee also dispenses hot water instantly for tea, oatmeal, instant soup, or cleaning. No waiting for a kettle. It's a small feature that becomes indispensable once you're used to it.

### Coffee Quality That Converts Skeptics

Multiple reviewers switched to BUNN after disappointing experiences with programmable machines. The commercial spray head saturates grounds evenly, producing what users describe as consistently "great tasting coffee." The 10-cup carafe is glass with a drip-free pour spout.

## The Not-So-Good

### The Learning Curve Is Real

This is not a plug-and-play machine. The initial priming process (filling the internal tank) can take 15–20 minutes, and the first pot or two may not be hot. One reviewer warned, "Don't expect the first, second, or maybe third pot when priming to be hot." Read the manual. Run water through before brewing coffee. Once primed, it's smooth sailing — but those first 30 minutes can be confusing.

### No Thermal Carafe

The glass carafe sits on a warming plate, which keeps coffee hot but not indefinitely. After an hour or so, the temperature drops. If you sip coffee all morning, the last cup won't be as hot as the first. BUNN sells thermal carafe models, but this isn't one of them.

### Counter Space Commitment

The GRB is taller and wider than a typical drip maker. If you have low-hanging cabinets, measure first. It's not massive, but it's not compact either.

### Maintenance Is Mandatory

The spray head needs periodic unscrewing, rinsing, and reaming of the water line to prevent mineral clogging. Skip this and brew quality degrades. Users who stay on top of it report years of flawless performance; those who don't end up frustrated.

## Who Should Buy It

The BUNN GRB is for **coffee drinkers who value speed and longevity over features**. If you want to press one button and have a full pot in 3 minutes — and you're willing to do minimal maintenance — this is the coffee maker for you. It's also perfect for households that need hot water frequently throughout the day.

## Who Should Skip It

- Programmable-brew fans (wake up to auto-brewed coffee) — this has no timer
- Thermal carafe enthusiasts — look at BUNN's insulated models
- Compact kitchen owners — this needs space
- Anyone unwilling to do basic maintenance

## Bottom Line

The BUNN GRB Velocity Brew has survived 40 years of coffee maker innovation by doing one thing better than anyone else: **brewing fast, consistent coffee from a machine that outlasts its competitors**. There's a learning curve and the lack of a thermal carafe is the one real miss, but with a 4.6/5 rating and over 17,000 reviews, the BUNN faithful aren't wrong. If speed and durability matter more than features, this is your coffee maker.
