**How It Performs in the Pan**

I tested this rice the way it's meant to be used: soffritto sizzling, broth hot, saffron steeped. The results were textbook.

- **Absorption:** This rice took on roughly three times its volume in liquid and still held its structure. Each grain plumped up individually without fusing into a starchy mass.
- **Socarrat:** The bottom crust formed exactly as it should — golden, caramelized, and lifting cleanly off the pan with a spatula. No scorched bitterness.
- **Texture:** After 20 minutes of simmering, the grains were tender but had that slight al dente bite in the center. That's the signature of proper paella rice.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this rice gluten-free?**
A: Yes. Rice is naturally gluten-free, and this product contains no additives or cross-contaminated grains.

**Q: How much liquid does this rice need?**
A: Roughly 3 parts liquid to 1 part rice. Use warm broth for best absorption. Don't stir after the first few minutes — that releases starch and kills the socarrat.

**Q: Can I use this for sushi or risotto?**
A: Technically yes, but it's not ideal. The grains are too firm for creamy risotto and too short for sticky sushi. Stick to paella and other Spanish rice dishes.
