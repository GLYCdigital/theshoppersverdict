# YUZUCO - 100% Juice - 8oz Review: Is This Pure Yuzu Worth the Splurge?

Most citrus juices on the shelf are a compromise — diluted, sweetened, or pasteurized into submission. YUZUCO's 100% Juice 8oz bottle takes the opposite approach. This is unadulterated yuzu, the Japanese citrus that tastes like a lemon crossed with a mandarin and a hint of grapefruit. The question isn't whether it's good — it's whether your cooking and cocktail game justifies the cost per ounce.

## What You Actually Get

The 8oz glass bottle contains exactly one ingredient: yuzu juice. No water, no cane sugar, no citric acid "for flavor," no preservatives. As shown in the product image, it's a straightforward amber glass bottle with a clean label — this is a product that trusts its ingredient list to sell itself.

For context, 8 ounces is about half the volume of a standard lemon juice bottle. That matters because yuzu is potent. You're not squeezing this over pancakes; you're adding a teaspoon to a ponzu sauce or a splash to a cocktail. The bottle lasts longer than its size suggests if you use it strategically.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The flavor is the headline here. Yuzu has a distinctive floral, tart character that lemon juice can't replicate. It's brighter and more complex, with a bitterness that cuts through rich foods. I used it three ways: a simple yuzu vinaigrette, a whiskey cocktail, and a marinade for salmon.

The vinaigrette was the standout — two teaspoons of YUZUCO, olive oil, and a pinch of salt made a dressing that tasted like something from a high-end Japanese restaurant. The cocktail worked well too, but here's the honest caveat: at this price point, you'll think twice before using it casually. This is a finishing ingredient, not a workhorse.

The juice arrived well-sealed and the glass bottle protected the contents effectively. Cold-pressed and unpasteurized, it needs refrigeration after opening — and you should use it within a few weeks, not months.
