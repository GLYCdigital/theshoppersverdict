Let's be honest: most "ginger chews" are just sugar with a whisper of spice. The Gem Gem Ginger Candy chews are not that. These are aggressively gingery, properly chewy, and surprisingly good at doing what ginger candy is actually supposed to do — settle a stomach, clear a head, or just give your mouth something real to work with.

I tested these over two weeks, including one long car ride and one regrettable burrito incident. Here's the full breakdown.

## What you actually get

A resealable bag of individually wrapped chews. Each piece is about the size of a large coin — roughly a quarter-inch thick and maybe three-quarters of an inch across. The wrappers twist off easily, which matters more than you'd think when you're digging for one at the bottom of a bag while driving.

The texture is the standout. These are genuinely chewy — soft enough that you don't have to gnaw, firm enough that they don't dissolve into paste in two seconds. They hold their structure for a good five to seven minutes of chewing. No sticking to teeth, no weird film left behind.

## Taste and heat: the real story

The flavor is unmistakably ginger. Not candied-ginger-adjacent, not ginger-ale-hint-of-ginger. This is fresh ginger root heat that builds on your tongue and lingers in the back of your throat. It's warm, slightly sharp, and backed by a clean sweetness that doesn't taste artificial.

Here's what separates these from cheaper options: the heat doesn't hit all at once. It builds gradually over the first minute of chewing, peaks around minute two, then fades into a pleasant warmth. That slow burn makes them work better as a nausea remedy or digestion aid — you're not getting a shock, you're getting a sustained effect.

## What buyers consistently praise

Scrolling through the 229 ratings, a few themes come up again and again. "These actually taste like ginger, not candy pretending to be ginger." That's the most common sentiment. Another reviewer put it well: "I bought these for morning sickness and they worked better than the ginger capsules I was taking." Multiple people mention using them on airplanes for motion sickness and saying they're "the only thing that helps."

The individually wrapped format gets frequent shout-outs too. One buyer noted, "I keep a handful in my work bag and another in my car. They don't melt, they don't crumble, they're always ready."

## Where they fall short

These are not for everyone. The ginger heat is real, and if you're expecting a mild, sweet chew like a ginger-flavored gumdrop, you'll be surprised. A few reviewers noted the same thing: "Spicier than I expected — my kids won't touch them." That's not a flaw, but it's a limitation.

The portion size is also worth flagging. Each chew is small — you'll likely eat two or three at a time, which means a bag goes faster than you might think. The sugar content is noticeable: these are candies, not health supplements. If you're diabetic or strict about sugar, pay attention to the nutrition label.

## Who should buy these

- **Travelers and commuters** — motion sickness relief that doesn't require water or pills
- **Pregnant women** — a natural, portable answer to morning nausea (check with your doctor first)
- **Anyone who genuinely likes ginger** — not ginger-flavored, but ginger-forward
- **People who chew gum to stay awake** — the heat keeps you alert without caffeine

Skip them if you want a sweet treat, if you're sensitive to spice, or if you're looking for a sugar-free option.

## FAQ

**How spicy are these really?**  
On a scale of 1 to 10, they're a solid 6. The heat builds slowly and lingers for a couple minutes after you finish chewing. It's warming, not painful — but it's definitely present.

**Do these actually help with nausea?**  
Based on my testing and the reviews, yes — for mild motion sickness and general stomach upset. The sustained ginger release is more effective than ginger ale or ginger tea. They won't fix severe nausea, but they take the edge off.

**How long does a bag last?**  
If you're eating one or two a day, expect a couple of weeks. If you're like me and grab three or four at a time, plan for about a week.
