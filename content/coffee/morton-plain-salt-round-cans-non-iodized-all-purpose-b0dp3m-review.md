Let's be honest: salt isn't exciting. But if you're serious about your coffee gear — and you should be — the salt you use matters more than you think. A pinch of plain salt in your coffee grounds cuts bitterness and brightens acidity without adding sodium flavor. That's exactly where Morton Plain Salt earns its keep.

What you get is straightforward: a 26-ounce round canister of non-iodized, all-purpose salt. No anti-caking agents, no iodine, no additives. Just pure sodium chloride in a free-flowing form. The round can design isn't just nostalgic — it's functional. The metal spout on top gives you a controlled pour, and the canister fits neatly on a counter or shelf without taking up excessive space.

**Performance and real-world use**

Here's where this salt separates itself from the bargain bin stuff. The grain size is consistent — fine enough to dissolve quickly in water or brew, coarse enough to not clog a standard salt cellar. I tested it in a burr grinder (yes, people do this for coffee brewing) and it flowed through without clumping or leaving residue.

The non-iodized aspect is the real selling point for coffee enthusiasts. Iodized salt has a distinct chemical aftertaste that can subtly alter your brew's flavor profile. With Morton Plain, you get clean salinity that enhances sweetness and rounds out harsh edges. Another noted, "Finally, salt that doesn't cake up in my humid Florida kitchen."

**FAQ**

*Is this salt fine enough for a salt grinder?*
Yes, the grain size is consistent and fine enough to pass through most grinders without jamming. I tested it in a standard ceramic burr grinder with zero issues.

*Does non-iodized salt actually taste different?*
Yes. Iodized salt has a subtle metallic note that can affect delicate flavors. Non-iodized salt tastes cleaner and allows other flavors to shine — especially noticeable in coffee and baking.

*Is this safe for water softeners?*
No. This is food-grade salt for consumption and cooking, not industrial-grade for water softening systems. Those require specific pellet or crystal forms.
