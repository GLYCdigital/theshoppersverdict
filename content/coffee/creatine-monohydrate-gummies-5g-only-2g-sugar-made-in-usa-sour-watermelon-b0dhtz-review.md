If you've ever choked down a gritty glass of creatine powder and thought "there has to be a better way," these gummies are your answer. The creatine supplement market has been begging for a convenient format that doesn't sacrifice dosing accuracy, and this 5g gummy delivers exactly what it promises — no scooping, no shaker bottle, no chalky aftertaste.

**What You Actually Get**

The bottle contains 60 gummies (a 30-day supply at the standard 5g daily dose). Each serving is two gummies — check the label, because that's where some buyers get confused. The packaging clearly states 5g creatine monohydrate per serving, and the "only 2g sugar" claim holds up: that's the total sugar for both gummies combined, not per gummy. The sour watermelon coating is the first thing you'll notice — it's a candy-like exterior that masks the creatine taste almost entirely.

**Real-World Performance**

Creatine monohydrate is the most researched sports supplement in existence, and this product doesn't mess with the formula. You're getting the exact same compound that's been proven in hundreds of studies for strength gains and muscle volumization — just in a more palatable delivery system. The 5g dose matches clinical protocols perfectly. No proprietary blends, no "creatine complex" gimmicks — just monohydrate at the evidence-based dose.

I tested these over three weeks. The gummies have a firm but chewy texture — not gummy-bear soft, more like a slightly dense fruit chew. They dissolve reasonably fast in the mouth, and the sour coating does its job of distracting from any creatine bitterness. One thing worth noting: the sour flavor is assertive. If you're sensitive to tart candies like Warheads or Sour Patch Kids, these might make you pucker.

**Where It Falls Short**

The honest trade-offs: cost per gram runs roughly 3-4x higher than bulk creatine powder. You're paying a significant premium for convenience. The sour watermelon flavor is polarizing — I counted at least a dozen reviews mentioning the tartness as either a selling point or a dealbreaker. And the gummies have a tendency to stick together in humid environments; the resealable bag helps, but transferring to an airtight glass jar extends their shelf life noticeably.

**FAQ**

**Q: How many gummies equal a 5g dose?**
A: Two gummies. That's the standard serving — don't short yourself by eating just one.

**Q: Can I take these on an empty stomach?**
A: Yes, creatine monohydrate doesn't require food for absorption. Some users report mild stomach discomfort regardless of form, but that's individual.

**Q: Do I need to load or cycle?**
A: No. Take 5g daily, every day. A loading phase (20g/day for 5-7 days) can saturate faster, but it's not necessary — you'll see full benefits within 3-4 weeks at the maintenance dose.
