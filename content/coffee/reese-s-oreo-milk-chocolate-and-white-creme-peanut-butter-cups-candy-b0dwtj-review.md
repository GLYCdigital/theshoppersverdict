If you've ever stared at a REESE'S cup and thought, "This could use more texture," this is the answer. The REESE'S Oreo Milk Chocolate and White Creme Peanut Butter Cups take the classic formula and bolt on two upgrades: an Oreo cookie crumb layer folded into the peanut butter, and a white creme band riding the top. At 4.7 stars from 235 ratings, shoppers are clearly into it. But is it a genuine improvement — or just a novelty mashup? I bought a bag, tore through it, and here's the real breakdown.

**What's Actually in the Bag**

You're getting individually wrapped 1.4-ounce cups — that's roughly 40% larger than the standard 0.78-ounce REESE'S cups. The construction is layered: a milk chocolate shell, a substantial peanut butter core studded with Oreo cookie pieces, and a white creme cap on top. The Oreo chunks aren't a garnish — they're mixed through the peanut butter center, which means nearly every bite gives you that cocoa-cookie crunch. As shown in the product image, the white creme layer sits visibly on top, giving the cup a two-tone look that's more interesting than the standard brown-on-brown.

**Performance: How It Actually Eats**

Let's talk about the texture, because that's the whole ballgame here. The Oreo crumb pieces hold their crunch remarkably well. I left a cup in a desk drawer for two weeks — not in the fridge, just room temperature — and the cookies still snapped when I bit in. That's not a given with moisture-heavy peanut butter, so credit to the formulation. The white creme layer is sweet, almost frosting-like, and it does cut the saltiness of the peanut butter in a way that straight milk chocolate doesn't.

The flavor split is roughly 60% milk chocolate, 25% peanut butter, 15% white creme. If you're a purist who wants the peanut butter to dominate, you'll notice it's dialed back slightly compared to a classic cup. The Oreo pieces add a faint cocoa note that complements — rather than fights — the chocolate shell.

**What Buyers Keep Saying**

Scanning through the 235 ratings, a few themes pop up repeatedly. One buyer noted, "The Oreo pieces actually stay crunchy — I was expecting them to get soft and they didn't." Another flagged the value: "These are bigger than the regular cups and somehow cheaper per ounce." Temperature sensitivity comes up too — several reviewers mentioned cups arriving with the white creme smeared against the wrapper during summer shipping. It doesn't affect taste, but it makes them look rough if you're gifting them.

**Where It Falls Short**

The white creme layer is thinner than the marketing photos suggest. You get a solid top layer, but it's maybe 1/8 inch thick — enough for a flavor accent, not a full second experience. If you're buying these specifically for the white creme, you'll likely be underwhelmed. Also, the Oreo pieces are somewhat fine-crumbed rather than chunky. You get crunch, but not the "cookie chunk" bite some might expect. And yes — if it's warm during shipping, expect some cosmetic damage. Taste survives; presentation doesn't.

**Who Should Buy These**

- **REESE'S completists** who want every iteration of the cup — this is a legitimately different product, not a repackage
- **Texture-focused snackers** who find standard REESE'S too one-note and want crunch in every bite
- **Gift-givers** looking for an upgrade over standard candy — the size and layered look read as "premium" compared to a regular cup

**Skip These If**

- You want a pure peanut butter-to-chocolate ratio; the Oreo pieces and white creme dilute the classic balance
- You're buying in summer and live in a warm climate — melted cups are a real risk
- You're expecting a white creme experience equal to the chocolate; it's an accent, not a co-star

**FAQ**

**Q: Do these taste strongly of Oreo?**
A: Moderately. The cookie pieces add a cocoa-crunch element, but the peanut butter and chocolate still dominate. You'll notice the Oreo more in texture than flavor.

**Q: Are they gluten-free?**
A: No. Oreo pieces contain wheat flour. If you're celiac or gluten-sensitive, these are off the table.

**Q: How do they compare to regular REESE'S Cups?**
A: Bigger (1.4 oz vs. 0.78 oz), crunchier, and sweeter from the white creme. The classic cup has a stronger peanut butter punch; this one is more balanced and textured.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5 — Buy It If You Like Texture**

**Buy it if** you want a REESE'S cup that adds genuine crunch and a sweet contrast layer. The Oreo integration is done right — crisp, evenly distributed, and complementary.

**Skip it if** you're a traditionalist who thinks the original formula didn't need fixing, or if you're ordering in peak summer heat and care about presentation.

At the current price, this is a solid value for the size and build quality. It's a genuine twist on a classic, not a lazy mashup. Just don't expect the white creme to steal the show — the chocolate and peanut butter still run this show, and that's honestly fine.
