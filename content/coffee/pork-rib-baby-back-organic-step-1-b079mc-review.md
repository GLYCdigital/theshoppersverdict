"Step 1" in the product name refers to the pre-trimming stage: the silverskin (that tough membrane on the bone side) is already removed. That's the most tedious part of rib prep, and removing it yourself usually costs you 15-20 minutes and a few nicked fingers.

The ribs come from organic-certified pork, meaning no antibiotics or growth hormones, and the animals are raised on organic feed. The vacuum seal is legitimate — reviewers consistently mention the ribs arriving cold, fresh, and without freezer burn. One buyer noted, "I've ordered these three times now and every single rack was perfectly sealed with no leaks."

**How they actually cook and taste**

I'll be direct: these ribs reward a low-and-slow approach. At 225°F with a good dry rub, they hit that sweet spot around 3.5-4 hours — tender enough to pull cleanly off the bone without falling apart into mush. The fat content is well-marbled but not excessive, which means you get juicy meat without the greasy mouthfeel that cheaper ribs often have.

The organic difference shows up in the flavor. There's a cleaner pork taste — no off-notes or gamey undertones that can sneak into conventional mass-market ribs. One long-time reviewer put it simply: "These are the best baby backs I've bought online, period. The meat-to-bone ratio is excellent."
