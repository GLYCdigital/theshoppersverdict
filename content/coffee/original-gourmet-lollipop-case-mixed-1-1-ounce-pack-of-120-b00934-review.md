For context, a standard Dum Dum or Charms pop runs around 0.2 to 0.3 ounces. You're looking at roughly four times the candy per stick. The wrappers are twist-sealed and the sticks are sturdy paper, not flimsy plastic — a small detail that matters when you're handing these to kids or using them for baking projects.

The "mixed" designation means you'll get a rotating assortment of their core flavors, though exactly which ones varies by batch. **Real-world performance**

The hard candy shell is the star here. It's dense, glossy, and doesn't shatter into shards when you bite down — it dissolves slowly and evenly. **Where it falls short**

The flavor rotation is the biggest gamble. Because there's no set assortment guarantee, you might receive a case heavy on flavors you don't love. One reviewer mentioned, *"I got mostly watermelon and grape, which my kids don't like as much as cherry."* That's the luck of the draw with mixed packs — if you have strong flavor preferences, buy single-flavor boxes instead.

The calorie count is also worth flagging. At roughly 110 calories per pop, these are treats, not snacks. If you're someone who can't stop at a few licks, you'll finish one and immediately want another. That's a you problem, but it's worth knowing going in.
