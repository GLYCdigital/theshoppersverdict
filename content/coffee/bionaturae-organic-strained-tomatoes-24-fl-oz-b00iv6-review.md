**FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask**

**Q: Is this the same as tomato puree or tomato sauce?**
A: Not quite. Tomato puree is often cooked down and may have added salt. This is raw, strained tomato — essentially a thick juice with no pulp solids. It's the blank canvas you build a sauce from. Add your own salt, olive oil, and herbs.

**Q: Can I use this for pizza sauce?**
A: Absolutely. In fact, it's ideal. Spread a thin layer directly on the dough — it won't make the crust soggy because it's thick and not watery. Just add a pinch of salt and oregano.

**Q: How long does it keep after opening?**
A: About 5-7 days in the fridge. Because there are no preservatives, it will oxidize faster than canned alternatives. Freeze the remainder in ice cube trays if you don't use it all quickly.
