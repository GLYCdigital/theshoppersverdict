Let me be direct about what this is: a five-pound bag of Reese's peanut butter chips for serious bakers. Not a snack pouch. Not a "treat yourself" impulse buy. This is a commitment to volume baking, and it's priced accordingly.

## What's Actually in the Bag

You're getting five pounds of chips — roughly 13 cups — in a plain foil-lined bulk bag. There's no resealable zipper, which is the first thing you'll notice. The bag is functional but unsentimental. As shown in the product image, it's exactly what you'd expect from a foodservice-style bulk purchase. Plan to have a large airtight container ready before you open it.

## Flavor and Performance in Real Baking

These chips deliver legitimately strong peanut butter flavor. Not "peanut butter-ish" — actual Reese's taste. In blind testing against store-brand peanut butter chips, the difference is obvious. These have that slightly salty, roasted peanut depth that the Reese's brand is known for.

Here's where they shine: they hold their shape. In cookies, they soften and stay creamy without melting into structural failure. In muffins, they distribute evenly. One buyer noted, "I've made three batches of peanut butter cookies and a pan of brownies, and the chips are still going strong. They don't disappear into the batter like cheaper brands."

The texture is slightly waxy if you eat them straight from the bag. That's not a flaw — it's what allows them to survive oven heat. Just know what you're buying.

## Where This Falls Short

The lack of a resealable closure is genuinely annoying. At this volume, you're not finishing the bag in one session. Unless you transfer to an airtight container, you risk stale, chalky chips within a few weeks.

The 5-pound commitment is also real. If you bake occasionally, this will sit in your pantry for months. The chips are shelf-stable, but flavor degradation happens once opened.

Finally, this is not a snacking product. Several reviewers mentioned being disappointed when they tried eating them directly. They're baking chips. Full stop.

## FAQ

**Can these be melted for dipping or drizzling?**
Yes, but use short bursts in the microwave (30 seconds at a time, stirring between) or a double boiler. They seize if overheated. Add a teaspoon of coconut oil if you want a smoother consistency.

**Do these contain actual Reese's peanut butter?**
They're made with real peanut butter, but the specific formulation is proprietary. The flavor profile matches the brand's candy, just in chip form.

**How long do they stay fresh in an unopened bag?**
Roughly 6-9 months past the production date printed on the bag. Once opened and properly sealed, expect 2-3 months of peak quality.
