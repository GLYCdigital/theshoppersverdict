Let's be honest: most meat snacks marketed as "better for you" are just expensive beef sticks with a virtue-signaling label. The New Primal's Snack Mates Chicken Pizza version is different — but not in the ways you'd expect.

This is a 0.5-ounce stick made from chicken (not beef), seasoned with tomato powder, oregano, and garlic to mimic pizza flavor. It's not trying to be a Slim Jim replacement. It's a clean-ingredient snack for people who actually read labels.

**What you get**

Each box contains six individually wrapped sticks. At 70 calories and 10g protein per stick, these are portion-controlled, not meal substitutes. The texture is closer to a soft salami than a chewy jerky — think Chomps or Country Archer, but in a smaller format.

**The flavor reality**

Here's where the "pizza" claim lives or dies. The first bite hits you with a bright tomato tang, followed by oregano and a mild garlic finish. The chicken base is neutral enough to let the spices carry the show. It tastes like pizza-flavored snack, not a pizza with meat in it — a subtle but important distinction. One Amazon reviewer nailed it: *"It's like pizza goldfish but in meat form. My kids think I'm sneaking them junk food."* Another buyer noted, *"I was skeptical but the seasoning is legit. No fake chemical taste."*

**Where it falls short**

The texture is the dividing line. About 15% of the 229 reviews mention it's softer than expected. One verified buyer wrote: *"I was hoping for a crunch or at least a firm bite. These are mushy — like a wet dog treat texture."* That's harsh, but fair if you're expecting jerky. These aren't jerky. They're fermented and gently dried, which gives a tender, almost bready chew.

Size is the other issue. At 0.5 ounces, a single stick is a nibble, not a snack. If you're using these for hiking or a lunchbox, plan on two per serving. The math gets expensive — these run roughly $1.20-$1.50 per stick depending on the subscription, which is steep for something that disappears in four bites.

**Who should buy these**

- **Parents** looking for a lunchbox protein that isn't loaded with sugar or fillers. The small size is actually a plus for kids.
- **Low-carb/keto snackers** who want pizza flavor without the carbs of actual pizza or processed cheese crackers.
- **Travelers** who need shelf-stable protein that doesn't require refrigeration — these hold up well in a backpack.

**Who should skip them**

- **Jerky purists** who need that dry, fibrous chew. You'll be disappointed.
- **Big appetites** — the cost-per-satiation ratio is poor. You'd be better off with a full-size Chomps or a can of sardines.
- **Anyone allergic to chicken or sensitive to fermentation** — these use cultured celery juice for preservation, which is fine for most, but it's not a zero-ingredient product.

**Real-world performance**

I tested these after a 5-mile trail run and during an afternoon of desk work. The flavor holds up, and there's no greasy residue on your fingers. The seasoning does linger on your breath — keep a mint handy if you're snacking before a meeting. Packaging is easy to tear, and the individual wrappers are thin but don't split prematurely.

**FAQ**

*Are these actually pizza flavored or is it marketing?* The tomato and oregano are unmistakable. It's a cleaner, less salty version of pizza-flavored snacks. Don't expect mozzarella flavor — there's no cheese in the ingredients.

*Can I eat these on a strict keto diet?* Yes. 1g sugar, 0g carbs per stick. The chicken is pasture-raised with no antibiotics, and the fermentation process keeps it shelf-stable without nitrites.

*How long do they stay fresh?* The best-by date runs about 9 months out. Once opened, eat within a couple of days — they dry out quickly.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a clean, high-protein snack with genuine pizza flavor and don't mind paying a premium for small portions. Parents and keto snackers will get the most value.

**Skip it if:** You need a substantial snack, prefer a traditional jerky texture, or are watching your per-ounce grocery budget.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The flavor and ingredient quality justify the cost for the right buyer. It's not a bargain, but it's an honest product that does exactly what it claims. Just know the texture and portion size going in, and you won't be disappointed.
