**Performance and Real-World Use**

The carbonation level on arrival is consistently solid. Multiple recent buyers noted the fizz holds up well if you're drinking within a few weeks of delivery. **Where It Falls Short**

The honest tradeoff is plastic versus cans. Plastic bottles let CO2 escape more readily than aluminum over time. If you're the kind of person who buys a case and sips one a week, the last few bottles will taste noticeably flatter than the first few. Cans hold carbonation for months; these bottles are best consumed within 4-6 weeks of purchase.

Also, this is caffeine-free. If you're expecting a soda that gives you any kind of lift, that's not what this is. It's purely a refreshment drink, not an energy source.

**Who Should Buy This**

This pack nails three specific use cases: households with kids who want single-serve portions without fighting over cans, road-trippers who need resealable bottles for the car, and anyone who drinks Sprite regularly enough to finish a case within a month.
