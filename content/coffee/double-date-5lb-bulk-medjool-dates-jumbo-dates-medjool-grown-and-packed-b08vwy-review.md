A 5lb (2.27kg) bag of Medjool dates, grown and packed with the "Gr" label indicating the origin. As shown in the product image, these arrive in a clear poly bag — no box, no frills. You're paying for dates, not packaging. The dates themselves are the headline: most weigh in at 20-28 grams each, which is genuinely jumbo territory. ## Performance and real-world use

Here's where this product earns its keep. Buyers consistently report the dates arrive soft and pliable — that's the single most important quality marker for Medjools. Dry, crystallized dates mean old stock or poor storage. One reviewer noted, "These are the freshest bulk dates I've ordered — soft, caramel-like, and the pits come out cleanly." That tracks with the 4.2 rating.

The flavor profile is classic Medjool: deep caramel sweetness with a honeyed finish. They're not sickly sweet like some cheaper varieties — the texture is dense and meaty rather than syrupy. For snacking, baking, or blending into smoothies, they hold up well. The 5lb size means you're getting roughly 80-100 dates depending on individual weights.

## What buyers consistently praise

The freshness is the recurring theme. Multiple reviews use words like "moist," "soft," and "fresh." One 5-star review puts it plainly: "Best dates I've found on Amazon. They're huge and taste like they were just picked." The bulk value also gets consistent callouts — at this size, you're saving 30-40% versus buying 12oz tubs at retail.

## Where it falls short

Honesty time. The biggest complaint isn't about the dates themselves — it's the inconsistency in pitting. Some customers report all dates pitted, others find a mix. One reviewer warned: "Check every date before eating — I bit into one with a pit still in it." That's a real annoyance when you're snacking mindlessly.

The packaging is also a practical issue. The 5lb bag isn't resealable, and once opened, dates dry out fast. You'll need to transfer them to an airtight container immediately. A few reviewers noted the dates arrived slightly sticky from condensation during shipping — not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

## Who should buy this

This is for the date-heavy household. If you're using dates daily for smoothies, energy balls, or baking, the 5lb bulk format makes sense. It's also great for families — kids tear through these as a healthier candy alternative. If you're a casual dater (pun intended) who eats a few a week, skip it. You'll be fighting to keep them fresh past week three.

## FAQ

**Are these dates actually pitted?**
It varies batch to batch. Some arrive fully pitted, others don't. Plan to check each one before eating — especially if serving to kids.

**How should I store 5lbs of dates?**
Transfer to an airtight container immediately. Room temperature works for 2-3 weeks; refrigerate for up to 6 months. Frozen dates keep for a year and thaw perfectly.

**Can I use these for baking and cooking?**
Yes — they're ideal for date paste, caramel sauce, and energy balls. The moisture content is high enough to blend smoothly without soaking.
