Let me be upfront: I'm not entirely sure why this landed in the coffee gear category either. But if you're looking for a pick-me-up that pairs dangerously well with your morning brew, these oversized peanut butter cups deliver exactly what the packaging promises — just maybe more than you bargained for.

**What's Actually in the Box**

The pack contains multiple individually wrapped REESE'S Big Cups — the ones that run roughly double the size of a standard cup. Each one is sealed in its own foil wrapper, which matters more than you'd think. No fighting with a shared tray, no half-eaten cups going stale in the pantry. You get the classic milk chocolate exterior with that dense, salty-sweet peanut butter filling that's made REESE'S a household name for decades.

**The Real-World Experience**

Here's the thing about Big Cups that separates them from their smaller siblings: the ratio. Standard REESE'S cups are mostly chocolate with a peanut butter center. The Big Cup flips that dynamic — you get a substantially thicker peanut butter layer, which is exactly what most fans actually want. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: *"These are the only Reese's I buy now. The extra peanut butter makes a huge difference."* Another noted, *"My husband said these taste fresher than the regular ones from the gas station."*

The texture holds up well. The chocolate snaps cleanly when you bite into it, and the peanut butter is creamy without being runny. They're best straight from the fridge — room temperature gets a bit soft, especially in warmer months.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's talk honestly about the downsides. First, portion control. At roughly 200 calories per cup, it's real easy to eat two and realize you've consumed a quarter of your daily intake before lunch. The individual wrapping helps, but it doesn't stop you from just opening another one.

Second, shipping durability. Multiple buyers reported receiving cups with cracked chocolate shells, particularly during summer months. The chocolate doesn't melt completely, but it does soften and shift, leaving an ugly (though still edible) product. If you're buying these as gifts, budget for potential cosmetic damage.

Third, the price per ounce is higher than buying standard REESE'S cups in bulk. You're paying a premium for the Big Cup experience, and if you're purely chasing peanut butter-to-chocolate ratio, you might be better off with REESE'S Pieces or a jar of actual peanut butter.

**Who Should Buy These**

- **The peanut butter purist** — if you've always wished REESE'S had more filling and less chocolate, this is your product
- **The office snack person** — individually wrapped portions are perfect for sharing (or hiding in your desk)
- **The coffee companion** — one cup with a dark roast is a legitimately great pairing

**Skip these if** you need a budget-friendly candy in bulk, or if you have zero self-control around peanut butter cups. The "Big" in the name is not a joke.

**FAQ**

A: Yes, same product. The value here is in the multi-pack pricing versus paying $1.50+ per cup at a register.

**Q: Do they need refrigeration?**
A: No, but they're better cold. Room temperature cups get soft and the chocolate loses its snap.

**Q: How many cups come in the pack?**
A: It varies by listing — check the specific option you're ordering. Most multi-packs contain 8-12 individually wrapped cups.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're a REESE'S fan who wants the superior peanut butter-to-chocolate ratio, or if you need individually portioned snacks that travel well.

**Skip it if** you're on a tight budget, ordering in hot weather, or looking for a healthier snack alternative — this is a treat, not a health food.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Great product with a real premium taste, docked slightly for shipping fragility and the price per ounce premium.
