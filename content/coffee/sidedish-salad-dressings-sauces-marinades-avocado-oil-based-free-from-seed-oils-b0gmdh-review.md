**The Short Version: A Clean-Eating Dressing That Actually Tastes Good**

Most "healthy" salad dressings taste like watered-down vinegar with a sad herb float. SideDish's Avocado Oil line isn't that. This is a dressing built around a genuinely good oil base, with flavor profiles that hold up on both greens and grilled proteins. It's not cheap, and the bottles aren't big — but for anyone actively avoiding seed oils or added sugar, this is one of the better options on Amazon right now.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The product ships as a multi-pack (typically three bottles, depending on the listing variation you select) with flavors like Classic Balsamic, Lemon Herb, and Sesame Ginger. As shown in the product image, the bottles are squat, modern, and clearly labeled. Each one runs about 8 ounces — which is smaller than the standard 12-16 oz bottles you're used to from Kraft or Newman's Own.

The ingredient list is where SideDish separates itself. The base is avocado oil (hence the name), and the rest is straightforward: vinegar, citrus, herbs, spices. No xanthan gum, no cane sugar, no "natural flavors" hiding industrial additives. For anyone who's been reading labels and feeling frustrated, this is genuinely refreshing.

**Real-World Performance**

I tested the Classic Balsamic and Lemon Herb across salads, roasted vegetables, and as a chicken marinade. The balsamic is properly tangy — not sweet like most commercial versions, which I appreciated. The Lemon Herb has a bright, clean citrus note that doesn't taste artificial. Both emulsify well when shaken, though you absolutely must shake them hard. The avocado oil separates fast — within minutes of sitting, you'll see a clear layer form on top. That's normal for a product without stabilizers, but worth knowing before your first pour results in a mouthful of straight oil.

As a marinade, these perform better than expected. The acid content is high enough to tenderize chicken in 30-45 minutes, and the avocado oil holds up to medium-high heat without smoking or turning bitter. I wouldn't use the balsamic on a delicate white fish, but for chicken, pork, or firm vegetables, it's solid.

And if you're used to sweeter commercial dressings, the acidity here will take some adjusting. These are not crowd-pleasing, middle-of-the-road flavors. They're assertive.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this need refrigeration after opening?**
A: Yes. The avocado oil base and lack of preservatives mean you should refrigerate after opening. The oil will solidify slightly when cold — let it sit out for 5-10 minutes and shake well before using.

**Q: Is this actually free of seed oils?**
A: Yes. The primary fat is avocado oil. No canola, soybean, sunflower, or safflower oil appears in the ingredient list. As with any packaged product, check the specific flavor's label since ingredient profiles vary slightly.

**Q: How long does a bottle last?**
A: With daily salad use, expect 1-2 weeks per bottle. The 8-ounce size is roughly 16 servings if you use the standard 2-tablespoon dressing portion.
