If you've scrolled past Hu's minimalist packaging a hundred times and wondered whether the hype justifies the price tag, here's the honest breakdown. This is chocolate that *knows* it's expensive and tries to earn it — mostly succeeding, with a few caveats worth knowing before you buy.

## What You're Actually Getting

The Hu product line — the av2023 assortment referenced in the listing — includes their signature simple bars. The base ingredients read like a clean-label bingo card: organic cacao, organic cocoa butter, organic coconut sugar, and organic vanilla. No soy lecithin, no dairy, no gluten, no GMOs, no refined sugar. That's it.

As shown in the product image, these come as solid chocolate bars — typically 70% dark chocolate with variations like almond, hazelnut, or crunch depending on the bundle you select. The bars are approximately 2.8 oz each, which is smaller than a standard Hershey's bar but denser and richer.

## How It Actually Tastes

The 70% dark chocolate base is where Hu shines. It hits a genuinely impressive balance — bitter enough to feel sophisticated, but with enough coconut sugar sweetness to avoid that chalky, puckering sensation cheap dark chocolate delivers. The texture is smooth, snappy, and melts cleanly without that waxy residue you get from bars loaded with lecithin.

One thing to know: this is not "dessert" chocolate. If you're expecting milk-chocolate sweetness, you'll be disappointed. It's earthy, slightly fruity in the finish, and closer to what you'd pair with espresso than hand to a kid.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides.

**Price.** At $5-7 per bar depending on size and retailer, you're paying a 40-60% premium over brands like Alter Eco or Pascha that offer similar clean ingredients. You're paying for the brand, the certification, and the convenience of one bar that checks every dietary box.

**Sweetness profile.** The coconut sugar substitution means a lower glycemic impact, but it also means a slightly caramel-like undertone that some tasters find distracting. If you prefer pure, unadulterated dark chocolate without any sweetness, you might actually prefer a simple 85% bar from a cheaper brand.

**Texture ceiling.** All Hu bars are solid chocolate. No filled centers, no truffle textures, no crispy inclusions (except the Crunch variety). If you want variety in mouthfeel, this isn't it.
