Let's be honest: this isn't a coffee maker. But if you take your coffee with a little something sweet on the side — and you're tired of hunting for a decent caramel candy that doesn't destroy your fillings — this Sweetzo offering of individually wrapped Werther's Original hard candies deserves a look.

Here's what you actually get: a resealable bag of Werther's Original caramel hard candies in a bite-sized, individually wrapped format. The classic cream-and-butter caramel flavor is all there — that deep, slightly salty-sweet caramel that melts slowly rather than shattering. The individually wrapped pieces mean you can toss a handful in your work bag, glove compartment, or desk drawer without them fusing into one giant caramel blob by Tuesday.

**The real-world performance**

I've tested these alongside the standard full-size Werther's, and the flavor profile is essentially identical. The smaller size actually helps — you get the same rich caramel depth without committing to a 10-minute suck. The texture is smooth, not gritty, and each piece holds up well in the mouth without that weird chalky breakdown cheaper caramels have.

The individually wrapped aspect isn't just about convenience — it genuinely preserves freshness. I left a bag open on my counter for two weeks (an experiment, not a confession) and the candies at the top stayed as good as the ones at the bottom. That's not typical for bulk candy bags.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.7 average across 235 ratings isn't inflated. Buyers repeatedly mention the authentic taste. One reviewer noted, *"Tastes exactly like the originals, just smaller — perfect for when I want a little treat without going overboard."* Another said, *"My husband keeps these in his truck. They don't melt or stick together, even in summer."* Multiple reviews highlight the resealable bag as a genuine plus for freshness.

**Where it falls short**

The smaller size cuts both ways. If you're used to standard Werther's, these are noticeably smaller — about two-thirds the size. For some buyers this is a feature (portion control), for others it's a mild annoyance (you'll reach for two). The other issue is shipping. During warm months, the bag can arrive with some candies slightly misshapen from handling. The flavor isn't affected, but if you're gifting these, note that they may not be picture-perfect on arrival.

Yes, the taste and texture match the classic Werther's Original formula. The only difference is the smaller size and individual wrapping.

**Do they melt in shipping?**
