# Beyond Good Organic Chocolate Bar Review: Is This the Dark Chocolate You've Been Missing?

Most "organic" chocolate bars taste like a compromise — you get the virtue signal but lose the flavor. The Beyond Good Organic Chocolate Bar flips that script. This 71% dark bar, sourced from a single cooperative in Madagascar, delivers the kind of bright, almost berry-like cacao notes you'd expect from a $12 craft bar, not something you'd grab alongside your coffee order.

## What You Actually Get

The package is straightforward: one 2.8-ounce bar wrapped in compostable kraft paper. No fancy box, no inner foil sleeve. The bar itself snaps cleanly into 12 segments — a good sign that the tempering is right. The ingredient list is refreshingly short: organic cacao beans, organic cane sugar, organic cacao butter, and vanilla. Nothing else.

## How It Performs

Let's talk about the flavor because that's the whole point here. Madagascar cacao is known for its red fruit and citrus notes, and Beyond Good delivers on that reputation. The first bite hits with a sharp, almost wine-like brightness before settling into a deep, earthy dark chocolate finish. It's genuinely interesting — the kind of chocolate that makes you stop and pay attention rather than mindlessly snacking.

The texture holds up too. The bar melts smoothly without that waxy residue cheaper chocolates leave behind. The snap is crisp, not crumbly. It pairs exceptionally well with black coffee or espresso — the bitterness of the chocolate and the acidity of the coffee actually complement each other rather than competing.

## What Buyers Are Saying

The 4.3-star rating across 215 reviews tells a consistent story. One reviewer put it well: *"Finally a dark chocolate that doesn't taste like cardboard. You can actually taste the fruitiness from the cacao."* Another noted, *"I've bought this three times now. It's my go-to for baking because the flavor comes through even when mixed with other ingredients."*

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest part. At 2.8 ounces, this bar is on the thinner side. If you're used to 3.5-ounce premium bars, you'll notice the difference — it's roughly 20% less chocolate for roughly the same price. And while the 71% cacao content is clearly stated, the bitterness level runs higher than comparable bars at the same percentage. If you're new to dark chocolate, this one might be an acquired taste rather than an instant favorite.

The other consideration: the flavor profile is distinctly single-origin. That means it's not the universal crowd-pleaser you'd get from a blended bar. Some tasters find the fruity notes almost sour. It's a feature, not a bug — but worth knowing before you commit.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Coffee enthusiasts** who want a chocolate that holds its own against a strong pour-over or espresso
- **Bakers** looking for a dark chocolate that doesn't disappear into batters and doughs
- **Ethical shoppers** who care about direct trade sourcing — Beyond Good works directly with growers in Madagascar

Skip it if you prefer milk chocolate, want a bigger bar for your money, or need something that'll please a crowd with mixed chocolate preferences.

## FAQ

**Is this chocolate actually organic?**
Yes. It's USDA Organic certified, and the cacao is grown at a single cooperative in Madagascar's Sambirano Valley. The sourcing is traceable to the farm level.

**Does it contain dairy?**
No. It's vegan — just cacao, sugar, cacao butter, and vanilla. No milk solids, no soy lecithin.

**How does it compare to a grocery store bar like Green & Black's?**
The flavor is more distinctive — brighter, more acidic, with a cleaner finish. But it's also less universally appealing. Green & Black's is smoother and sweeter at the same percentage. Beyond Good is for people who want to taste where their chocolate comes from.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely interesting, ethically sourced dark chocolate that brings real flavor complexity to the table. **Skip it if** you're on a budget, prefer milder chocolate, or want a bigger bar for snacking.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A well-executed single-origin bar that earns its premium positioning, even if the size and bitterness won't suit everyone.
