Let me be straight with you: this isn't a coffee maker or coffee gear. It's a jar of raw cashew butter. But if you're reading this because you searched for something coffee-adjacent and landed here, you're probably one of the many people who blend nut butter into smoothies, stir it into oatmeal, or dollop it on toast alongside your morning brew. Artisana Organics has built a name in the raw foods space, and their Raw Cashew Butter holds a solid 4.6 out of 5 across 229 Amazon ratings. Here's what you actually need to know before spending your money.

**What you get**  
One 16-ounce glass jar of raw, organic cashew butter. That's it. No salt, no sugar, no palm oil, no "natural flavors" hiding anything. The ingredient list is literally one word: cashews. The raw processing means the nuts never exceed 118°F, which preserves enzymes and gives the butter a noticeably lighter, sweeter flavor than roasted versions. The glass jar is a nice touch — easy to recycle, and you can see the thick, creamy texture before you open it.

**Real-world performance**  
Here's where this butter stands apart from cheaper supermarket options. The texture is genuinely thick — almost fudge-like at room temperature. It spreads cleanly on toast without tearing the bread, and it doesn't turn into a puddle when you add it to warm oatmeal. Unlike many natural nut butters that separate into a brick of solids and a pool of oil within days, Artisana's holds together remarkably well. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I've bought every raw cashew butter on Amazon. This one is the only one that doesn't turn into a rock in the fridge or a soup on the counter."*

The flavor is where raw really shows up. It's sweet and mild with that distinct fresh-cashew taste that roasted versions lose. Several buyers mention using it in smoothies and noticing it doesn't overpower other ingredients — a subtlety you don't get with peanut or almond butters.

**Where it falls short**  
Honest talk: it's expensive. Per ounce, you're paying a premium over jarred roasted cashew butter from bigger brands. That's the cost of organic, raw, single-ingredient sourcing. Also, while separation is less of an issue than with other natural butters, it's not zero. A handful of reviewers report oil pooling at the top when the jar arrives — especially in warmer months. It's fixable with a good stir, but if you're expecting "open and use without fuss," this may annoy you.

**Who should buy this**  
- **Smoothie and acai bowl makers** who want a clean, unsweetened nut butter that blends smoothly  
- **Clean-label shoppers** who refuse palm oil, added sugar, or preservatives  
- **Raw food enthusiasts** who care about enzyme preservation  
- **Cashew butter loyalists** who've tried the cheap stuff and want the upgrade  

Skip it if you're on a tight budget, prefer roasted nut butters with deeper flavor, or you want something you can spoon straight from the jar without stirring.

**FAQ**  
**Q: Does this need to be refrigerated after opening?**  
A: Not strictly, but cooler storage extends freshness and reduces oil separation. If you use it within a month, pantry storage is fine. Beyond that, fridge it.

**Q: Is this actually raw?**  
A: Yes. The cashews are never roasted above 118°F, which is the industry standard for "raw" certification. This preserves more nutrients but gives a milder flavor than roasted butter.

**Q: How does it compare to Artisana's other nut butters?**  
A: Their almond and pecan butters are also excellent, but cashew is consistently the smoothest and sweetest. If you're new to the brand, this is the best entry point.

**The Verdict**  
Buy it if you want a genuinely clean, single-ingredient cashew butter with a thick, spreadable texture and fresh raw flavor. The premium price is justified by the quality of the nuts and the absence of any fillers. Skip it if you're looking for a budget option or prefer the deeper, roasted taste of conventional butters.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a top-tier raw nut butter with only price and occasional separation holding it back from perfection.
