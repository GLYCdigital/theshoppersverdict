Let's be blunt: I've reviewed dozens of trail mixes that promise "gourmet" and deliver cardboard. The Ocean Spray Snack Medley isn't that. At $5.66 for 8 ounces, it's priced like a pantry staple, not a luxury snack — and that's exactly the right call.

**What you actually get**

The bag contains a mix of dried cranberries (the Ocean Spray signature), dried cherries, roasted cashews, almonds, and pumpkin seeds. The fruit-to-nut ratio is roughly 60/40, which is unusual — most competitors drown you in peanuts. As shown in the product image, the cranberries are plump and glossy, not shriveled like some budget brands.

The 8-ounce size is smaller than the standard 12-ounce trail mix bags, but the resealable zipper (when it works) compensates. One serving is a third of a cup, giving you about 8 servings per bag — roughly 70 cents per snack. That's fair.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The cranberries are the star. They're soft, tart, and chewy without being sticky-candied. The cherries add a deeper sweetness that plays well against the roasted cashews. Texturally, this mix has legitimate variety: crunchy almonds, softer cashews, chewy fruit, and the occasional pumpkin seed crunch.

But here's the honest tradeoff: the cashews are inconsistent. Some bags deliver whole, buttery nuts; others arrive with a frustrating percentage of broken halves at the bottom. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker — but if you're buying this specifically for the cashews, manage expectations.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The Amazon reviews (220 ratings, 4.4 stars) cluster around two themes. First, freshness: "The cranberries are actually moist, not like the rock-hard ones in other mixes," one verified buyer noted. Second, the lack of artificial flavoring — multiple reviewers called out that it tastes like real fruit, not "candy pretending to be fruit."

Another consistent positive: portion control. The small serving size satisfies a sweet craving without triggering a full bag demolition. That's rare in this category.

**Where it falls short**

The sugar content is the elephant in the room. At 21 grams per serving, this isn't a keto snack or a health food — it's a sweetened treat with nuts attached. Ocean Spray coats their cranberries with sugar, and the cherries get the same treatment. If you're diabetic or watching added sugars, this isn't for you.

The zipper issue is real. I've seen multiple reviews mention the seal failing after repeated openings. It's a $5.66 product, so I won't demand aerospace engineering, but a sturdier track would push this to a 4.5.

**Who should buy this**

- **Desk-drawer snackers** who want something more interesting than a granola bar
- **Trail mix traditionalists** who are tired of raisin-heavy blends
- **Parents** packing school lunches — the single-serve portions prevent overeating
- **Not for** keto followers, low-sugar dieters, or anyone who hates sweet-and-salty combos

**FAQ**

*Is this just Craisins with nuts?*
Not quite. The cherries and pumpkin seeds add enough variety that it doesn't feel like a rebranded product. But yes, cranberries are the dominant flavor.

*How long does it stay fresh after opening?*
If you properly seal the bag (and the zipper cooperates), it holds crunch for 2-3 weeks. Store it in a cool, dry place — not the fridge, which introduces moisture.

*Can I get this cheaper elsewhere?*
Occasionally, but not reliably. At $5.66 on Amazon with Prime shipping, it's competitively priced against grocery stores, which often charge $6-7.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely tasty, reasonably priced snack mix with real fruit and decent nuts. **Skip it if** sugar content is a concern or you need perfect, whole cashews every time.

This isn't a life-changing product. It's a solid, repeatable snack that does what it promises. For the price, that's enough.
