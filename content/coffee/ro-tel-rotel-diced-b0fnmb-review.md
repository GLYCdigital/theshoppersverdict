The biggest surprise? **The texture.** These diced tomatoes hold their shape through simmering. Unlike cheaper brands that dissolve into mush after 10 minutes on the stove, RO*TEL maintains those distinct chunks. That's critical for chili, where you want tomato presence, not tomato paste.

## Where It Falls Short

I'm not going to pretend this is flawless. Here's what you should know:

- **The "Original" is mild.** If you're a spice lover, you'll find the heat underwhelming. The "Hot" version exists, but it's not always in stock. Buy accordingly.
- **It's not a fresh-tomato replacement.** If you're making a delicate tomato sauce where the tomato is the star, use fresh. RO*TEL is a seasoning vehicle, not a gourmet ingredient.
- **Sodium is present.** One can runs about 380mg. That's fine for most, but if you're watching salt, account for it in your recipe.

**Q: Is RO*TEL Rotel Diced spicy?**

**Q: Can I use this in place of regular diced tomatoes?**
