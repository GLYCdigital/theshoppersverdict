Gobena's 5lb Fair Trade Organic Honduran Ground flips that script — one purchase, roughly 160 cups, and a price per pound that undercuts most specialty roasters by a mile. But a big bag only makes sense if the coffee inside is actually worth drinking every single morning. After digging through 215 ratings and what buyers actually say, here's the real story.

**What you're getting:** A single-origin, certified organic Honduran coffee, ground for drip brewers. The bag shows a medium roast — and that's honest labeling. The beans are sourced from smallholder farms in Honduras, and the Fair Trade certification means those farmers got paid a living wage, not just a commodity price. That's meaningful in a category where "fair trade" often gets slapped on blends with vague provenance. Gobena names the origin, the certification, and the roast level right on the front of the bag. No mystery here.

**Flavor profile and performance:** Expect a medium body with chocolate notes up front, a hint of citrus brightness, and a clean finish. Multiple reviewers describe it as "smooth" and "not bitter," which tracks with a medium roast done right. As one buyer put it, "This is the best coffee I've had in years — rich without being acidic." Another noted, "I drink it black and it never disappoints." That's a solid sign: coffee that needs cream and sugar to be palatable usually gets called out fast in reviews, and this one doesn't.

**Where it stumbles:** The grind is the biggest variable. Some buyers report a consistent medium grind that works great in standard drip machines. Others mention batches that run finer, which can over-extract and turn bitter in a drip brewer. That's a real quality-control issue, not a nitpick. Also — and this matters if you're buying 5 pounds — the bag has no resealable zipper. Coffee starts losing freshness the moment oxygen hits it, and 5 pounds will take most households three to four weeks to finish. Buy an airtight container. Your first week will be great; your fourth week depends on how you store it.

**The freshness math:** This is the honest tradeoff of bulk coffee. Ground coffee peaks within days of roasting and declines steadily. A 5lb bag means you're drinking coffee that's two to three weeks old by the end. If you're a household that goes through a pound every few days, this is a non-issue. If you're buying for light daily use, you'll taste the difference. That's not a flaw in Gobena's product — it's physics — but it's worth knowing before you commit.

Yes. The certification is printed on the bag, and Gobena has maintained these credentials across multiple product lines. Several reviewers specifically mention buying it for the ethical sourcing.

*Does it work in a French press?*
