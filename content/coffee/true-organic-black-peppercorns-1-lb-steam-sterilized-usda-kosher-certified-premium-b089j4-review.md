## Why steam sterilization is the quiet star

Here's the thing most reviews don't mention: raw peppercorns are frequently contaminated with bacteria. They're sun-dried on the ground in tropical countries, and studies have found salmonella and E. coli on unsterilized peppercorns with alarming regularity. True Organic uses steam instead of ethylene oxide gas or irradiation — a gentler process that kills pathogens while keeping the pepper's natural oils intact.

This matters if you're cooking for anyone pregnant, elderly, or immunocompromised. It's also why this bag costs slightly more than bargain-bin peppercorns. You're paying for safety, not just flavor.
