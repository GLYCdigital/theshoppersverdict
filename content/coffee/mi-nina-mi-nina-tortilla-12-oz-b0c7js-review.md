**The MI NINA Tortilla 12 Oz is a surprisingly capable travel mug that punches above its price point — if you can live with a few quirks.**

Let's be honest: when a brand name makes you think of a flatbread, you don't expect much from their coffee gear. But the MI NINA Tortilla 12 Oz has quietly earned a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings, and after digging into what buyers actually say, there's real substance here.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the Tortilla 12 Oz tumbler with a leak-proof lid. That's it — no straws, no spare seals, no unnecessary accessories. The body is double-wall vacuum insulated stainless steel with a powder-coated exterior. As shown in the product image, it has a clean, minimalist silhouette that doesn't scream "branded promo item." The lid is a threaded twist-on design with a sliding closure mechanism.

**Real-world performance**

The insulation claims are legitimate. Multiple buyers report their coffee staying hot through a full workday. One reviewer noted, *"I poured coffee at 6:30 AM and it was still warm at lunch — not piping hot, but definitely drinkable."* That tracks with the double-wall vacuum construction. For cold drinks, the performance is even better; iced beverages hold their temperature well past the 12-hour mark.

The lid deserves special mention. It's genuinely leak-proof when sealed — several customers specifically called out tossing it in tote bags and backpacks with zero spills. The sliding mechanism is stiff enough that it won't accidentally open in transit, though that does mean it requires two hands to operate.

**Where it falls short**

The hand-wash-only requirement is the biggest practical annoyance. The powder coating doesn't survive dishwasher cycles — buyers report chipping and fading after repeated washes. If you're someone who relies on the dishwasher, this will eventually look rough.

The 12 oz capacity is also limiting. It's roughly the size of a standard coffee shop small or medium. For those who need 16+ ounces to start the day, this will feel undersized — you'll be refilling, which partially defeats the purpose of the insulation.

**Who should buy this**

The MI NINA Tortilla is ideal for commuters, office workers, and anyone who needs a reliable, leak-proof vessel for a standard-sized coffee. It's compact enough for cup holders and most briefcase pockets. If you're a weekend hiker or camper looking for a durable camp mug, this works too — just don't expect the ruggedness of a heavy-duty steel flask.

Skip it if you need a large capacity, prefer dishwasher-safe gear, or want a wider mouth for adding ice cubes easily.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it fit under a standard espresso machine spout?**
A: At 12 oz with a relatively slim profile, it clears most home espresso machine drip trays. Some commercial machines with very low clearance might be tight, but for home use you're fine.

**Q: Is the lid actually spill-proof?**
A: When fully sealed, yes. Buyers consistently report zero leaks in bags and car cup holders. The sliding closure is the weak point — it's not designed for one-handed operation while driving.

**Q: Can you put milk or creamer in it?**
A: Yes, the stainless steel interior handles dairy fine. Just note that hand-washing is required, and milk residue can be stubborn if you don't rinse promptly.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a dependable, leak-proof travel mug for standard coffee portions and don't mind hand-washing.

**Skip it if:** You need larger capacity, dishwasher convenience, or prefer a wide-mouth design.

The MI NINA Tortilla 12 Oz earns its 4.6/5 rating through solid construction and honest performance. It's not flashy, but it does exactly what a travel mug should — keep your drink hot and your bag dry. For the price, that's a solid deal.
