## This Isn't Your Grocery-Store Wheat Bread — And That's the Point

Most "whole wheat" bread at the supermarket is a lie. It's white flour dyed brown with molasses, loaded with dough conditioners, and marketed as healthy. The Rudis Bakery Organic 100% Whole Wheat Sliced Bread is the opposite of that. It's dense, honest, and tastes like actual wheat — because that's literally all it is.

If you've been burned by "healthy" bread that tastes like cardboard, this one might surprise you. It has real flavor — nutty, slightly sweet, with a texture that reminds you bread is supposed to have substance.

## What You Actually Get

A 22-ounce loaf, pre-sliced, in a simple clear bag. As shown in the product image, it's not fancy packaging — just honest bread. Each loaf runs about 18-20 slices depending on where the cutter lands.

The ingredient list is refreshingly short: organic whole wheat flour, water, organic honey, organic wheat gluten, yeast, salt, and a couple of organic enzymes. That's it. No high-fructose corn syrup, no soybean oil, no calcium propionate.

## Performance and Real-World Use

Here's where this bread separates itself. **Toast it.** The exterior crisps up beautifully while the interior stays chewy — it makes a PB&J feel like a restaurant dish. For sandwiches, the density means it won't turn to mush with wet ingredients like tomato or mayo.

One thing to know: the slices are thinner and smaller than standard sandwich bread. If you're used to big, fluffy commercial slices, these will look modest. That's a tradeoff for the density — you get more bread per bite, just less surface area.

**The crumble issue is real.** Fresh out of the bag, the slices can crack and crumble at the edges if you handle them roughly. It's not a dealbreaker, but you'll want to toast it first or use a gentle hand. One Amazon reviewer noted, *"Love the taste but you have to be careful taking slices out or you'll lose the crust."* That matches my experience.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**Shelf life is short.** No preservatives means this bread molds faster. You're looking at 5-7 days at room temperature, sometimes less in humid climates. The fix is simple — freeze half the loaf immediately and pull slices as needed. It thaws in minutes.

**The crumble factor** we already covered. It's not fragile like a croissant, but it lacks the structural integrity of commercial bread.

**Price.** Organic, small-batch bread costs more per ounce than store brands. You're paying for quality ingredients and real production methods.

## FAQ

**Q: Does it taste like regular whole wheat bread?**
A: No. It's more robust — nuttier, denser, with a slight honey sweetness. It's closer to artisanal bakery bread than grocery-store whole wheat.

**Q: Is it actually 100% whole grain?**
A: Yes. The only flour is organic whole wheat flour. There's no white flour, no enriched flour, no "wheat flour" that's secretly refined.

**Q: How should I store it?**
A: Freeze half immediately if you won't finish it in a week. Thaw at room temperature or toast straight from frozen. Don't refrigerate — it dries out faster.
