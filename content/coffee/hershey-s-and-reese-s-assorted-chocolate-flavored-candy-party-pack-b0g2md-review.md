Look, this isn't a coffee maker. It's not even coffee-adjacent. But if you're the person who keeps a jar of candy next to the espresso machine for after-work treats, this party pack earns its spot on your counter. The HERSHEY'S and REESE'S Assorted Chocolate Flavored Candy Party Pack delivers exactly what the label promises: a bulk bag of miniature classics that disappear fast.

## What You Actually Get

The bag contains a mix of miniature-sized versions of eight Hershey staples: Hershey's Milk Chocolate, Hershey's Special Dark, Kit Kat, Reese's Peanut Butter Cups, Reese's Pieces, Krackel, Mr. Goodbar, and sometimes York Peppermint Pattie depending on the production run. The minis are the unwrapped variety — the ones that look like tiny silver bullets in the product image, not the foil-wrapped holiday versions.

Portion-wise, these are the "snack size" candies you'd find in a variety box. Each piece is roughly one to two bites. The bag weight varies by listing, so check the specific pack size before ordering — some buyers reported receiving the 35-ounce club pack, others got the smaller 20-ounce version.

## Real-World Performance

The chocolate quality is standard Hershey — consistent, familiar, and reliably fresh. Multiple Amazon reviewers noted the candy arrived with good shelf life and no melting issues, even in warmer shipping conditions. One buyer wrote, "Came fresh and not melted. Great variety for the kids' lunchboxes."

The Reese's cups are the standout. The peanut butter-to-chocolate ratio holds up even in miniature form. The Kit Kats snap cleanly, and the Krackel pieces add the rice-crisp texture that keeps things interesting.

The unwrapped format has a real advantage: no foil wrappers to peel off during movie night or while driving. Just grab and go. The downside is that unwrapped minis don't stay as fresh once the bag is opened — if you don't seal it properly, they'll go stale within a week.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The variety gets the most love. One reviewer noted, "Great assortment, not just the same chocolate over and over. My kids love picking different ones each day." Another said, "Perfect for Halloween treat bags or pinata filler. Good value for the quantity."

The sharing factor is real. This bag works for classroom parties, office snack bowls, or road trips. The miniature sizes mean everyone can sample multiple flavors without committing to a full bar.

## Where It Falls Short

The ratio problem is the most consistent complaint. Several reviewers mentioned their bags skewed heavily toward Kit Kat and Krackel, with fewer Reese's cups than expected. If you're buying specifically for the peanut butter cups, prepare for potential disappointment.

The unwrapped minis also lack the nostalgic unwrapping experience. For some, half the fun of a Reese's cup is peeling back the foil. You don't get that here.

And yes — if you were hoping this was coffee gear, it's not. This is candy. Pure, simple, and delicious.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these the same size as the candy bars in the grocery store variety boxes?**
A: Close, but not identical. These are the miniature unwrapped versions, slightly smaller than the foil-wrapped snack sizes you find in seasonal variety packs. They're one to two bites each.

**Q: How should I store these to keep them fresh?**
A: Keep the bag tightly sealed in a cool, dry place — ideally below 70°F. Since they're unwrapped, they absorb odors and moisture faster than foil-wrapped candies. A resealable container works better than the original bag once opened.

**Q: Can I use these for baking or melting?**
A: Not ideal. The miniature pieces melt inconsistently because of the varying fillings (peanut butter, crisped rice, etc.). For baking, buy standard chocolate bars or chips instead.
