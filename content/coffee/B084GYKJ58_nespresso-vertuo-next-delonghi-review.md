## Nespresso Vertuo Next Coffee and Espresso Maker Review

The Nespresso Vertuo Next by De'Longhi promises café-quality coffee and espresso at the touch of a button. With over 2,800 reviews on Amazon and a 4.1-star average, it's one of the most popular pod machines on the market.

We examined user experiences to determine whether it delivers on that promise — and whether the reliability concerns are dealbreakers.

### Design and Features

The Vertuo Next has a sleek, modern silhouette that takes up minimal counter space. The **white finish** is clean and contemporary, though it shows fingerprints more readily than darker models.

**Key specs:**
- **Brew sizes:** Espresso (1.35 oz), Double Espresso (2.7 oz), Gran Lungo (5 oz), Mug (7.7 oz), Carafe (12 oz)
- **Water tank:** 37 oz
- **Heat-up time:** 30–40 seconds
- **Used pod capacity:** Up to 10 before needing to empty
- **Weight:** 8.8 lbs

The barcode-reading technology is the Vertuo's signature feature. Each pod has a barcode on the rim that tells the machine the exact brew parameters — temperature, water volume, rotation speed — for that specific coffee blend.

### Coffee Quality

When it works, the Vertuo Next produces excellent coffee. The centrifugal brewing spins the pod at up to 7,000 RPM, creating a thick, velvety crema that instant coffee and standard drip machines simply cannot match.

Reviewers consistently praise:

- **Crema quality:** Thick, persistent, café-level crema on every brew
- **Brew temperature:** Coffee comes out hot — a common complaint with other pod machines
- **Flavor consistency:** Each cup tastes the same as the last
- **Speed:** From button press to finished cup in under a minute

> *"This coffee maker has actually been a game changer! Our coffees are just as good at home."* — Verified 5-star review

### Aeroccino Milk Frother

The included Aeroccino frother is a genuine differentiator. It heats and froths milk in about 60 seconds, producing dense microfoam that rivals what you'd get from a café steam wand.

**Frother highlights:**
- Hot froth for cappuccinos and lattes
- Cold froth for iced beverages
- Easy-pour spout design
- Removable whisk for cleaning

### Reliability Concerns

This is the Vertuo Next's Achilles' heel. While many users report years of flawless operation, the negative reviews tell a consistent story:

**Common reported issues:**
- Machine stops recognizing pods or reading barcodes
- Water flow becomes inconsistent after several months
- Descaling alerts persist even after cleaning
- Complete failure within the first year

The **3 out of 10** negative review rate on quality issues is higher than we'd like to see. If you buy the Vertuo Next, registering the warranty and using filtered water are smart precautions.

### Value Analysis

At **$160.30**, the Vertuo Next is competitively priced for a machine that brews both coffee and espresso with a milk frother included.

**Cost per cup:** Vertuo pods range from $0.80–$1.50 each, significantly less than a $5–$7 café drink. A daily latte habit pays for the machine in about a month.

**Ongoing costs:** This is the main consideration. Proprietary Vertuo pods mean you're locked into Nespresso's ecosystem. There are no third-party Vertuo-compatible pods — unlike the original Nespresso line, which has many compatible options.

### Verdict

The Nespresso Vertuo Next by De'Longhi delivers excellent coffee with minimal effort. The Aeroccino frother is a genuine bonus, and the brew quality justifies the pod costs for most users.

However, the reliability concerns are real. We recommend buying from a retailer with a good return policy and registering the warranty immediately.

**Who should buy:** Convenience-first coffee drinkers who want espresso and coffee from one machine, and don't mind the ongoing pod cost.

**Who should skip:** Anyone on a tight budget, or those who want maximum reliability and third-party pod options.

### FAQ

#### Can the Nespresso Vertuo Next make regular coffee or only espresso?
It makes both — from a 1.35 oz espresso shot to a full 12 oz mug of coffee. The barcode-reading technology adjusts brewing parameters based on the pod type.

#### How often do you need to descale the Vertuo Next?
Nespresso recommends descaling every 300 cups or 3 months, whichever comes first. The machine will alert you when descaling is needed.

#### Is the Aeroccino dishwasher safe?
The Aeroccino jug and whisk are hand-wash recommended. The non-stick coating can deteriorate in the dishwasher over time.

#### Does the Vertuo Next use Keurig K-Cups?
No. The Vertuo Next only works with Nespresso Vertuo pods. It does not accept original Nespresso capsules or K-Cups.
