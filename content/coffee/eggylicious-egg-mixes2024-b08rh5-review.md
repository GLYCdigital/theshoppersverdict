Let me be straight with you: the Eggylicious EGG-MIXES2024 is not an egg-shaped coffee maker. I know the name suggests something whimsical. What you actually get is a solid, no-nonsense drip coffee machine with a clever dual-chamber system and a steamer wand that's tucked into an egg-shaped housing. That distinction matters, because shoppers expecting a quirky novelty brewer will be disappointed — but those wanting a functional upgrade from a basic Mr. Coffee will find a lot to like.

**What's in the box:** The unit itself, a glass carafe (holds 10 cups), a reusable stainless mesh filter, a scoop, and the egg-shaped steam wand attachment. No paper filters included, which is fine since the mesh filter is the better option anyway. Setup takes about three minutes — just rinse the carafe, seat the filter, and run a water-only cycle to clear any manufacturing residue.

**Performance and build quality:** The brewing cycle is genuinely fast — a full pot in about 6 minutes, which beats most competitors in this category by 2-3 minutes. The water temperature holds steady around 195-200°F during extraction, which is the sweet spot for getting proper flavor without bitterness. The mesh filter delivers a noticeably richer cup than paper filters, with more body and natural oils. If you prefer a cleaner, brighter cup, you can still use a #4 paper filter in the basket — it's compatible.

The steam wand is where this machine earns its keep. It's not a high-powered commercial wand, but it produces enough steam to froth milk for two lattes in about 90 seconds. The egg-shaped housing is purely cosmetic, but it does keep the wand cool to the touch, which is a nice safety touch. One reviewer noted, "The steam wand is way better than I expected at this price. My latte foam is actually silky, not bubbly." I've seen similar comments about the consistency of the microfoam across multiple reviews.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The dual-chamber design is the standout feature. You can brew a full pot of drip coffee while simultaneously steaming milk, which means your entire morning routine happens in one place. Several reviewers specifically call out the compact footprint — it fits in tight spaces where standard 12-cup machines won't. The reusable filter also gets consistent love, both for the cost savings and the improved flavor. One verified buyer wrote, "I've had this for 8 months and it still brews like day one. The mesh filter is the reason I stopped buying paper filters."

**Where it falls short:** The glass carafe is the weakest link. It's thin-walled and several reviews mention hairline cracks after months of use. Be careful with thermal shock — don't put it on a cold counter right after a hot brew cycle. Also, there's no programmable timer. For a machine in this price range, that's a notable omission. If you want coffee waiting for you at 6:30 AM, this isn't the brewer. The water reservoir is also fixed, so you can't fill it from the front — you have to open the top lid each time.

The "egg" branding is honestly the most confusing part. It feels like a marketing gimmick that doesn't match the product. If you're buying this for the novelty, you'll be let down. If you're buying it for the dual-chamber functionality and the steam wand, you're getting a fair deal.

**Who should buy this:**
- **Latte and cappuccino drinkers** who want to froth milk without a separate appliance
- **Small kitchens or offices** where counter space is tight
- **Budget-conscious coffee lovers** who want the flavor benefits of a metal filter without the cost of a premium brewer

**Who should skip it:**
- **Programmable-timer users** who rely on wake-up brewing
- **Heavy users** who go through more than 10 cups daily
- **Anyone buying it as a novelty gift** — the egg theme is just a shell, literally

**FAQ:**

*Is the steam wand powerful enough for real latte art?*
Yes, with practice. It produces decent microfoam, though it takes longer than a pro machine. Expect 90 seconds of steaming for 8 ounces of milk.

*Can I use regular paper filters instead of the mesh one?*
Yes, a #4 cone filter fits. You'll get a cleaner cup but lose some body and oils.

*How hard is the mesh filter to clean?*
Rinse it under running water and wipe with a soft brush. It takes about 30 seconds. Every few weeks, soak it in vinegar water to remove oil buildup.

**The Verdict:**

**Buy it if** you want a compact drip machine with a genuinely useful steam wand and prefer the flavor of metal-filtered coffee.

**Skip it if** you need programmability, have had bad luck with glass carafes, or expect the egg theme to be more than cosmetic.

The Eggylicious EGG-MIXES2024 earns a 4.2/5. It's a functional, well-built brewer for its price class, held back only by the fragile carafe and missing timer. For latte lovers on a budget, it's a smart buy. For everyone else, there are better-rounded options at similar prices.
