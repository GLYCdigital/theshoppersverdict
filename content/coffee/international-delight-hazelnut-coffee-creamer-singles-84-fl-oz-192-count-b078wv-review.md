You know that moment when you're at work, the office coffee is terrible, and you'd kill for something that makes it drinkable? That's exactly who International Delight Hazelnut Coffee Creamer Singles were designed for — and at 192 cups per box, this is the long-haul solution.

## What You're Actually Getting

This box contains 192 individually sealed portions of International Delight's Hazelnut creamer. Each 0.375 fluid ounce cup is the standard "one serving per coffee" size — roughly a tablespoon and a half. The packaging is straightforward: foil-sealed plastic cups in a cardboard box, designed to sit in a drawer or on a shelf without refrigeration until you pop one open.

The math matters here: at 84 total fluid ounces, you're getting roughly four and a half standard 16-ounce bottles' worth of creamer in portion-controlled form. That's the real value proposition — convenience you pay a premium for, but the per-cup cost still beats buying individual bottles that go bad before you finish them.

## Flavor and Performance

International Delight's hazelnut is one of the better commercial creamers on the market. It tastes like toasted hazelnuts with a buttery sweetness, not the artificial "nut-flavored candy" profile you get from cheaper brands. It's sweet — noticeably sweet — so if you take your coffee black or with a splash of milk, this will be a flavor shift, not an addition. It's closer to a flavored latte experience than a traditional creamer.

The texture is what you'd expect from a commercial liquid creamer: it thickens coffee slightly, adds a creamy mouthfeel, and blends instantly without needing aggressive stirring. No clumping, no separation, even in cold brew. The singles work exactly like the bottled version — same formula, same result.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct: this contains partially hydrogenated oil. That's trans fat, the kind nutritionists have been telling us to avoid for years. The amount per serving is small (0.5 grams), but if you drink multiple coffees daily, it adds up. For a product marketed as an everyday staple, that's a legitimate concern.

The sweetness is also non-negotiable. There's no "less sweet" version of this product — it's one flavor profile, locked in. If you're someone who likes adjusting sweetness independently of creaminess, you'll be frustrated.

Finally, the singles generate waste. 192 tiny plastic cups and foil lids is a lot of packaging compared to one bottle. If you're eco-conscious, that's a real tradeoff to weigh.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a desk-bound professional who wants reliable creamer without fridge access. You travel or camp and want coffee that tastes decent anywhere. You struggle with portion control and want a built-in stop. You find yourself throwing away half-empty bottles of creamer because they spoil before you finish them.

**Skip it if:** You're avoiding trans fats or processed ingredients. You prefer unsweetened or lightly sweetened coffee. You're concerned about single-use plastic waste. You drink decaf or specialty coffee where you want the bean flavor to shine through — this will dominate it.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these need refrigeration?**
A: No. The sealed cups are shelf-stable until opened. Once you peel the foil, use it immediately — there's no resealing.

**Q: How much coffee does one single cream?**
A: Each cup is 0.375 fl oz, designed for one standard 8-ounce cup. For larger travel mugs (16 oz+), you'll typically want two.

**Q: Do these work in hot and cold coffee?**
A: Yes. They blend into hot coffee instantly and mix fine into iced coffee, though cold drinks may need a quick stir to fully incorporate.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

International Delight Hazelnut Creamer Singles solve a real problem — keeping decent coffee available anywhere, anytime, without refrigeration or measuring. The flavor is solid, the convenience is unmatched, and 192 servings means you won't restock for months.

But the trans fat content and inflexible sweetness keep this from a higher score. If you're not concerned about partially hydrogenated oil and you like sweet, nutty coffee, this is a no-brainer purchase. If you've moved past processed creamers, this isn't going to change your mind.

**Buy it if** convenience and consistency matter more than clean ingredients. **Skip it if** you're watching your trans fat intake or prefer unsweetened coffee. For the right buyer, this box pays for itself in saved coffee-shop runs within weeks.
