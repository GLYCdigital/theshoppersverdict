You're getting one full pound of small, heart-shaped red candies — roughly 200 to 220 individual pieces depending on size variation. ## The Heat Factor: Real Deal or Weak Sauce?

Here's the honest breakdown. These candies hit with immediate warmth that builds into a proper burn — not the fake, cloying "Red Hots lite" experience you get from cheaper cinnamon candies. The heat is front-loaded but lingers pleasantly, leaving that warm tingle on your tongue for a minute or two after the candy dissolves.

Texture-wise, they're firm and snappy when fresh. You get a satisfying crunch before the candy starts breaking down, and they don't turn into a gummy mess halfway through. One reviewer put it plainly: "These are the real deal — my mouth is on fire but I can't stop eating them." Another noted, "Finally a cinnamon candy that actually has heat. My wife thinks I'm crazy but I love these."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The dominant theme across positive reviews is authenticity. People who grew up on intense cinnamon candies say these match or exceed their expectations. ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides. First, this is genuinely spicy — if you're buying these for a party or expecting a mild cinnamon flavor, you'll be surprised. Several reviewers warned that the heat is "too much" for casual candy eaters, and one noted their kids wouldn't touch them after one piece.

Second, the bag isn't resealable. Once you open it, you need a clip or a separate container. In humid environments, the candies can absorb moisture and get slightly tacky over time. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing if you're not planning to plow through the whole pound quickly.

Third, there's meaningful size variation between pieces. You'll find some smaller hearts and some chunky ones in the same bag. Doesn't affect flavor, but if you're using them for baking or precise portioning, expect some inconsistency.
