## The Chocolate Bar That Gives Back — Without Skimping on Taste

Most "ethical" chocolates taste like a compromise — you feel good about buying them, then immediately wish you'd grabbed a Snickers instead. The Endangered Species Dark Chocolate bar (72% cacao) sidesteps that trap entirely. This is a genuinely good dark chocolate that happens to fund wildlife conservation, not a charity product masquerading as a treat.

## What You Actually Get

The bar arrives in a sturdy 3-ounce wrapper featuring striking animal artwork — the jaguar edition shown in the product image is particularly sharp. Inside, you get a single 85g block of 72% dark chocolate. The packaging is fully recyclable, which matters more than most brands acknowledge. Each bar's wrapper highlights a different endangered species, and 10% of net profits go to conservation partners like the National Wildlife Federation.

## Real-World Performance

I broke off a square and let it melt slowly. The texture is smooth — no chalky grit or waxy residue that plagues cheaper dark chocolates. The flavor profile opens with a deep, almost fruity note, then transitions into a satisfying bitterness that lingers without being harsh. It's a well-executed 72% bar: assertive enough for dark chocolate purists, but with enough natural sweetness to stay approachable.

This pairs exceptionally well with coffee — the chocolate's bitterness complements a medium or dark roast without competing. If you're looking for something to keep on your desk for afternoon coffee pairings, this fits the role perfectly.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The conservation angle isn't just marketing fluff — it's baked into the business model. As one verified buyer put it: *"Finally a chocolate that tastes expensive AND supports a cause. My go-to gift for fellow coffee and chocolate lovers."* Another reviewer noted: *"Smooth, rich, not overly sweet. ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The 3-ounce bar is noticeably smaller than comparable premium chocolates at this price point — if you're a frequent snacker, you'll blow through this quickly. Some buyers on Amazon explicitly noted the size-to-price ratio as a sticking point.

Also, this is a legitimately bitter chocolate. If your baseline is milk chocolate, the 72% cacao content will hit you like a brick wall. Several lower-rated reviews came from buyers who expected something sweeter — check your tolerance before buying a multi-pack.
