# Nespresso Vertuo Orafio, Medium Roast Espresso Coffee capsul

**The short version:** If you own a Vertuo machine and want a dependable, everyday espresso capsule that doesn't demand your full attention, the Orafio delivers. It's not the most exciting coffee you'll brew, but it's consistent, smooth, and produces a crema so thick it looks like it belongs in a café window.

## What you actually get

This is a 50-pack of Vertuo-sized espresso capsules, each holding roughly 5.5 grams of coffee — the standard for Vertuo espresso shots. The barcode on each capsule rim tells your machine exactly how much water to push through, so you'll get a consistent 1.35 oz shot every time. No guesswork, no dialing in.

The box itself is straightforward: 50 aluminum-plastic composite capsules, neatly stacked. Nothing fancy, but you're paying for the coffee, not the packaging.

## Performance and real-world use

Here's where the Orafio earns its keep. The medium roast profile sits at a 6 out of 11 on Nespresso's intensity scale — squarely in the middle. What that means in practice: you get a coffee with noticeable body and a nutty, cereal-like sweetness that doesn't wallop you with bitterness. The acidity is low, which makes it forgiving for people who find many espressos too sharp.

I brewed these back-to-back over two weeks. The crema — that golden-brown foam on top — was consistently thick and lingered for minutes. That's the Vertuo system's centrifusion extraction at work, and it's genuinely impressive for a capsule format. The flavor held steady across the entire pack; no weak or off-tasting capsules in the batch.

One thing worth noting: this is a *medium* roast, not a dark one. If you're coming from something like the Vertuo's Altissio or Il Caffè, the Orafio will taste noticeably lighter and smoother. It's closer to a traditional Italian-style espresso in body but with less of that ashy, charred finish.

## What buyers consistently praise

The 4.0-star average across 200 ratings tells a consistent story. Reviewers repeatedly mention the crema quality and the smooth, balanced flavor. One verified buyer put it plainly: *"This is the best medium roast I've tried in my Vertuo. Rich without being bitter."* Another noted, *"Perfect for my morning latte — strong enough to taste the coffee through the milk."*

The value angle comes up often too. A 50-pack at typical pricing lands around $0.70–$0.80 per capsule, which undercuts most specialty coffee shops by a wide margin while delivering comparable quality.

## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, the Vertuo lock-in: these capsules only work in Vertuo machines. If you have an original-line Nespresso, this pack is useless to you. That's a deliberate ecosystem choice, and it's worth acknowledging before you commit to a 50-count box.

Second, the finish has a mild, lingering bitterness. It's not offensive, but it's there — and it's more pronounced if you let the shot cool. Dark roast fans will find it thin on character.

Third, the waste factor. These capsules are plastic-aluminum composites. Nespresso's recycling program exists, but you have to collect the used pods in the special bags and drop them off or mail them in. It's more effort than tossing them in the bin, and plenty of reviewers note they end up in the trash anyway.

## Who should buy this

- **Daily espresso drinkers** who want a reliable, no-fuss shot without chasing single-origin variations
- **Latte and cappuccino makers** who need a coffee that cuts through milk without getting lost
- **Vertuo owners** looking for a bulk buy that won't break the bank

Skip it if you're a dark roast purist, if you hate the Vertuo ecosystem, or if you're looking for a complex, fruity, specialty-level espresso.

## FAQ

**Is this a strong espresso?**
Medium strength — intensity 6 out of 11. It has good body and a caffeine kick, but it's not a dark, heavy roast. Think approachable and smooth, not bold and aggressive.

**Will these work in my original-line Nespresso machine?**
No. These are Vertuo-only capsules, recognized by the barcode on the rim. Original-line machines use smaller, different-shaped pods.

**How does the crema compare to a real espresso machine?**
Surprisingly well. The centrifusion extraction creates a thick, stable crema that holds for minutes. It won't match a $2,000 commercial machine, but for a capsule system, it's at the top of the class.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a consistent, smooth medium roast espresso for your Vertuo machine and don't mind the ecosystem lock-in. It's a solid daily driver at a reasonable per-cup cost.

**Skip it if** you prefer bold, dark roasts, hate plastic waste, or own a non-Vertuo machine.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The Orafio doesn't try to be the most exciting coffee you'll ever brew. It tries to be dependable, smooth, and affordable. In all three, it succeeds. The slight bitterness and plastic waste keep it from a perfect score, but for everyday espresso, you could do a lot worse.
