Let's be honest: "diet brownie" usually means "tastes like compressed cardboard with cocoa powder." The Fiber One 70 Calorie Brownies in Chocolate Chip Cookie flavor are better than that — but they're not a magic trick. After a week of testing these against my usual afternoon snack rotation, here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains five individually wrapped brownies, each weighing in at a modest size — roughly 1.5 inches square and about half an inch thick. As shown in the product image, each brownie is studded with visible chocolate chips, which is a good sign. The packaging is straightforward: a standard cardboard box with foil-wrapped brownies inside. Nothing fancy, but the individual wrapping is genuinely useful for portion control and tossing one in a bag on the way out the door.

## The Taste and Texture Reality Check

Here's the honest breakdown: these brownies taste like a slightly denser, less fudgy version of a standard snack cake. The chocolate chip cookie flavor is actually one of the better variants in the Fiber One lineup — the chips provide real chocolate flavor bursts rather than just a flat cocoa taste. The brownie itself has a cake-like crumb that's drier than you'd get from a full-calorie equivalent.

The texture is where you'll notice the compromise. It's not stale-dry, but it's definitely not moist. A bite with a chip is significantly better than a bite without one. I found that eating these with coffee or tea elevates the experience considerably — the moisture from the drink compensates for the dryness. If you're expecting a Ghirardelli-style brownie experience, you'll be disappointed. If you're expecting a reasonable sweet treat that won't blow your calorie budget, this delivers.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews reflects a pattern. Buyers consistently praise the calorie count and portion control. One reviewer noted, "These actually satisfy my chocolate craving for only 70 calories — I keep a box in my desk drawer." Another mentioned, "The chocolate chip version is way better than the regular brownie flavor — the chips make a huge difference."

The complaints cluster around two issues: texture and digestive effects. Multiple reviewers mention the dryness, with one saying, "They're good but you definitely need something to drink with them." Several others flagged the maltitol content causing stomach upset when eaten in quantity. That's worth taking seriously if you're sensitive to sugar alcohols.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the texture, the biggest issue is the sweetener situation. These use maltitol (a sugar alcohol) and sucralose to achieve the low calorie count. The aftertaste is milder than some competing products, but it's present. If you're someone who can detect artificial sweeteners easily, you'll notice it.

The serving size is also worth considering. At 70 calories each, it's easy to eat two or three without thinking — and at that point, you're at 210 calories of processed snack food that's mostly fiber and sweeteners. The fiber content (5g per brownie) does help with satiety, but it's not a meal replacement.

## FAQ

**Are these actually 70 calories per brownie?**
Yes. Each individually wrapped brownie is 70 calories with 5g of fiber and 4g of protein. The packaging is accurate.

**Do these taste like real brownies?**
No — they taste like a lighter, drier cake-like version. The chocolate chips help significantly, but regular brownies are moister and richer. Think "snack cake" rather than "bakery brownie."

**How many come in a box?**
Five individually wrapped brownies per box. If you're buying these regularly, consider multi-pack options for better value.
