**FAQ**

**Q: Are these cloves strong enough for tea?**
A: Yes, but use fewer than you think — 2-3 cloves per cup is plenty. The oil content is high, so the flavor is concentrated. And pick out any stems first.

**Q: How should I store them after opening?**
A: Airtight glass jar in a cool, dark cabinet. Don't refrigerate — condensation introduces moisture that degrades the cloves. They'll hold potency for about a year this way.

**Q: Do these work in a coffee grinder for spice grinding?**
A: Yes, a blade grinder works fine. Use short pulses so you don't heat the oils and lose aroma. You'll want a dedicated spice grinder — cloves will leave a lingering scent.
