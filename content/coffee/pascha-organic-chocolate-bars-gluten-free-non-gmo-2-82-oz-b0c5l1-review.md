You've got your pour-over dialed in, your beans are fresh, and then you realize: there's nothing to nibble alongside that first bitter sip. Pascha's Organic 55% Dark Chocolate Bar steps into that gap — a clean-label bar that's become a quiet favorite among coffee drinkers who want something sweet that doesn't fight the roast.

First, let's be clear about what this actually is. This is a 2.82-ounce (80-gram) bar of 55% cacao dark chocolate. That's mid-range darkness — not the 85%+ stuff that puckers your face, not milk chocolate's sugar bomb. It's the sweet spot for coffee pairing. The ingredient list is refreshingly short: organic cacao beans, organic cane sugar, organic cocoa butter, and vanilla. No soy lecithin, no dairy, no weird emulsifiers. It's certified organic, gluten-free, and non-GMO — and Pascha backs that up with third-party certifications rather than marketing buzzwords.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The bar breaks into clean, even squares — about 12 to 15 pieces, depending on how careful you are. As shown in the product image, it's a classic single-origin-style bar with a smooth, matte finish. The texture is where things get slightly interesting: this bar leans softer at room temperature than typical dark chocolate. That's not a defect — it's the lack of lecithin and the higher cocoa butter content — but it means bars can arrive with minor scuffs or slight bending if they sit in a warm delivery truck. Nothing that affects taste, but if you're gifting these, order when weather is cool.

Melt performance is excellent. If you're making a mocha or stirring a square into hot coffee, it dissolves smoothly without clumping or leaving that waxy film cheaper chocolates produce. The flavor profile: opens with a caramel-like sweetness from the cane sugar, then a clean, slightly fruity cacao note that finishes dry enough to complement — not mask — your coffee's origin characteristics. It won't overpower a light Ethiopian roast, and it holds its own against a bold Sumatra.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.2-star rating across 210 reviews reflects genuine satisfaction. **Where it falls short**

Honest tradeoffs exist. The single-flavor listing means you're locked into 55% dark — no sea salt, no orange, no mint variations in this particular listing. Some buyers found the bar sweeter than expected for "dark" chocolate; if you're a true 70%+ person, this will read more like a dessert chocolate. And the soft texture issue is real — a handful of reviewers mentioned receiving slightly melted or misshapen bars during summer months. It's cosmetic, but worth knowing.

**Who should buy this**

- **Coffee purists** who want a clean, allergen-free nibble alongside their brew
- **Bakers** who need reliable melting chocolate for desserts or homemade mochas
- **Parents** shopping for school-safe treats with transparent labeling
- **Anyone** avoiding dairy, soy, or gluten but tired of chalky "free-from" chocolates

**Skip it if** you want intense, high-percentage dark chocolate or if you're looking for a variety pack.

**FAQ**

**Is this bar actually dairy-free?** Yes — the facility is certified free of the top 14 allergens, and the ingredient list contains no dairy. It's a solid choice for sensitive individuals.

**Can I use this for baking?** Absolutely. It melts smoothly and tempers reasonably well for a home kitchen. The 55% cacao content works for brownies, ganache, or stirred into coffee.

**How does this compare to Lindt or Ghirardelli?** It's less sweet than Lindt's milk chocolate, cleaner-tasting than Ghirardelli's baking bars (no soy lecithin), and notably more transparent about sourcing and certifications.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want an honest, clean-label dark chocolate that pairs beautifully with coffee and won't trigger dietary restrictions. **Skip it if** you demand snappy texture or intense 70%+ darkness.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — It's not flashy, but it's reliable, honest, and genuinely good at what it does. For the coffee lover who wants a guilt-free companion to their morning ritual, this is a solid, repeatable choice.
