If you've ever flipped a jar of "natural" peanut butter and seen a paragraph of ingredients (palm oil, sugar, salt, mono-and-diglycerides), you already know why Crazy Richard's stands out. This is peanut butter in its purest form: peanuts, and nothing else. Both the creamy and crunchy versions are vegan, gluten-free, and keto-friendly — but the real question is whether the purity translates to actual eating enjoyment. After digging through 210 ratings and the specs, here's the honest breakdown.

**What you actually get:** Two 16-ounce jars (the listing typically offers a creamy and crunchy bundle, or individual packs — check the current options). No salt. No sugar. No added oil. The ingredient list on both jars reads exactly one word: peanuts. That's it. The crunchy version uses peanut pieces that are noticeably larger than what you'll find in most supermarket brands, so you get an actual textural contrast rather than a token crunch that disappears by mid-sandwich.

**Performance and real-world use:** This is where honesty matters — pure peanut butter is not a grab-and-spread product. The oil separates, hard. Buyers consistently note that the first stir takes real effort. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"I knew it would separate, but I didn't expect to need a workout before breakfast. Once I got it mixed and refrigerated, it was perfect."* That's the key tip: stir thoroughly once, then store upside down or in the fridge, and it stays spreadable.

The creamy version has a slightly grittier mouthfeel than commercial peanut butters because there are no hydrogenated fats smoothing things out. It's not unpleasant — it's just *real* peanut texture. The flavor is genuinely roasted and nutty, which is why many buyers say they'll never go back to sugary brands. As one reviewer noted: *"Tastes like peanuts were meant to taste. I add a pinch of salt myself and it's better than anything from the grocery store."*

**Where it falls short:** The lack of salt is a double-edged sword. If you're coming from Jif or Peter Pan, this will taste flat — you'll likely need to add your own salt or honey. The stirring requirement also filters out people who want convenience. And while the crunchy version is great for texture lovers, the pieces can be unevenly distributed, so the bottom half of the jar tends to be less chunky than the top.

**Who should buy this:**  
- **Clean-label shoppers** who read every ingredient label and refuse palm oil or added sugar  
- **Keto and Whole30 followers** who need a compliant nut butter  
- **DIY types** who don't mind a little stirring and prefer controlling their own salt/sweetness  

**Who should skip it:**  
- Anyone who wants a no-stir, ready-to-use spread  
- People who need sweetness or saltiness straight from the jar  
- If you're texture-sensitive and prefer ultra-smooth commercial butters, this isn't it  

**Three questions shoppers actually ask:**  
- *Is the oil separation normal?* Yes, completely. Peanuts are about 50% oil, and without stabilizers, it separates. Stir it, refrigerate, and you're set.  
- *Does it taste like roasted peanuts?* Yes — it's the closest thing to freshly ground peanut butter you'll find in a jar.  
- *Is the crunchy version actually crunchy?* Yes, more so than most brands. The pieces are substantial and hold up through the jar.  

**The Verdict:** Crazy Richard's delivers exactly what it promises: 100% pure, single-ingredient peanut butter with no compromises. It's not the most convenient product, and it won't satisfy a sweet tooth, but for clean eaters and purists, it's a top-tier choice at a fair price.

**Buy it if** you want honest-to-goodness peanut butter with nothing to hide. **Skip it if** you want a no-stir, pre-seasoned spread that tastes like dessert.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — loses points only for the stirring hassle and lack of seasoning. The purity earns the rest.
