## What's actually in the box

This is the "Wild" variety pack, which means you're getting four different SKITTLES lines in one order: Original, Wild Berry, Sour, and Chewy. Each comes as individual snack-size pouches — roughly 0.8 ounces per pack. The exact count varies by seller listing, but typical configurations land around 40–50 total packs per box.

As shown in the product image, the packaging is straightforward: a plain shipping box stuffed with loose candy pouches. No frills, no display-ready presentation. That's fine for bulk buyers, but worth knowing if you're gifting it.

## How it performs in the real world

The Original SKITTLES deliver exactly what you expect — that classic fruit punch flavor lineup (strawberry, grape, orange, lemon, lime) with the firm-but-crunchy shell that gives way to chewy center. Wild Berry swaps in raspberry, blackberry, and melon berry for a deeper, darker fruit profile. The Sour variety coats the shell in a tart dust that hits upfront then fades quickly. Chewy SKITTLES are a different beast entirely — no hard shell, just a denser, gummier texture that some people love and others find weird.

Texture consistency holds up well across all four. I didn't encounter any stale or chalky batches, which is a common complaint with bulk candy. The individual packs do their job — they keep the candy fresh and make portion control effortless.

## What buyers consistently praise

The variety is the star. One reviewer put it plainly: *"Love the mix — great for parties and everyone can grab their favorite."* Another noted the value angle: *"Cheaper than buying individual bags at the store, and the kids love the little packs for lunch."* The snack-size format gets repeated shout-outs for being practical — no half-eaten bags going stale, no fighting over who gets the last handful.

## Where it falls short

The Sour variety is the weakest link. It's sour in the way a Warhead is not — more of a mild tang that disappears within seconds. If you're buying specifically for sour candy fans, they'll be disappointed.

The Chewy packs are also a gamble. They're not what most people picture when they think "SKITTLES," and if someone in your household only eats Original, they'll see those packs as wasted space.

Finally, the loose-pack packaging is a minor annoyance. There's no resealable outer bag, so the pouches can migrate around your pantry. A simple plastic bin solves this, but it's worth flagging.
