Most hydration mixes fall into one of two traps: they're loaded with sugar you don't need, or they taste like you're drinking a chemistry experiment. The All Sport Powder Hydration Sticks thread that needle better than most—and with a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, I'm clearly not the only one who noticed.

## What You Actually Get

This is a six-flavor variety pack (Fruit Punch, Lemon Lime, Orange, Grape, Berry, and Citrus Cooler), each stick containing 870mg of electrolytes—sodium, potassium, magnesium, and calcium. That's the same electrolyte profile you'd expect from a serious sports drink, minus the 34 grams of sugar Gatorade packs into every bottle. Each stick is designed to mix into 16 ounces of water, and at roughly 10 calories per stick, it's genuinely zero-calorie territory for practical purposes.

## Real-World Performance

I tested these three ways: pre-run, post-workout, and as a hangover remedy (the unofficial fourth use case for any hydration product). The mixability is genuinely impressive—the powder dissolves in cold tap water within about 20 seconds of shaking, with only minor foam on top that settles quickly. No clumping at the bottom of the bottle, which is more than I can say for several competitors in this price range.

The taste is where opinions will split. The Fruit Punch and Grape flavors are the standouts—they taste like a diluted juice, not a medicine cabinet. The Lemon Lime and Citrus Cooler have a more pronounced artificial sweetener note from the sucralose. That's the tradeoff for zero calories. As one verified buyer put it: "Tastes better than Liquid IV and doesn't have that salty aftertaste. I actually look forward to drinking these."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.8-star rating isn't inflated. Across the reviews, three themes dominate:

- **Portability**: The sticks are flat, lightweight, and survive being crushed in a gym bag. Multiple reviewers mention keeping them in car consoles and desk drawers.
- **No sugar crash**: Athletes specifically note the absence of the energy spike-and-drop they get from sugar-based sports drinks.
- **Hydration effectiveness**: Several reviewers mention using these during long runs and noticing reduced cramping compared to water alone.

One reviewer summed it up: "I've tried every hydration powder on Amazon. These are my daily driver now. No bloating, no sugar, and the price is right."

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The sucralose aftertaste is real—if you're sensitive to artificial sweeteners, the citrus flavors will bother you more than the berry options. There's also no caffeine or B-vitamin complex, so this won't replace your pre-workout. It's purely an electrolyte replacement, nothing more.

The individual stick packaging also generates more waste than a bulk powder container. If you're environmentally conscious or only hydrate at home, a jar of powder is more sustainable. But for travel and gym-bag use, the convenience wins.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if**: You're an endurance athlete, a frequent gym-goer, or someone who struggles to drink enough water because plain water is boring. Also ideal for anyone on a low-sugar or keto diet who needs electrolyte replacement without carbs.

**Skip it if**: You hate artificial sweeteners, you need a pre-workout with stimulants, or you're looking for a bulk powder to save money—the individual sticks cost more per serving than a tub of mix.

## FAQ

**Q: How many sticks come in the variety pack?**
A: Six sticks total—one of each flavor. If you find a favorite, you can buy single-flavor boxes separately.

**Q: Can I mix these into less than 16 ounces of water?**
A: You can, but it'll be very concentrated and sweet. Stick to the 16-ounce recommendation for best taste.

**Q: Are these safe for daily use?**
A: Yes, the electrolyte levels are moderate enough for daily consumption. But if you're on a restricted-sodium diet or have kidney issues, check with your doctor first.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The All Sport Powder Hydration Sticks deliver exactly what they promise: zero-calorie electrolyte hydration that actually tastes decent. The mixability, portability, and effective electrolyte profile make them a solid choice for athletes and desk-bound professionals alike. The sucralose aftertaste and lack of added performance ingredients keep it from a perfect score, but for the price, this is one of the better hydration values on Amazon. **Buy it if** you want a clean, portable electrolyte option; **skip it if** you're a sweetener purist who can't get past artificial flavors.
