The Breton CRACKER doesn't look like much. It's a boxy, utilitarian drip machine with a plastic shell and a glass carafe — the kind of thing you'd find in a hotel lobby or a break room. But 220 Amazon reviewers have pushed it to a 4.4-star average, and that's not nothing. After digging through the feedback and the specs, here's the real story.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the brewer itself, a 10-cup glass carafe, a permanent mesh filter (so no paper filters needed), and a measuring scoop. No fancy accessories, no descaling kit, no nonsense. The machine measures roughly 11 inches tall and 8 inches wide, which means it slides under standard upper cabinets without issue — a detail multiple reviewers specifically called out.

**Performance that punches above its weight**

Here's where the CRACKER surprises. In real-world testing, several long-term owners noted the brew temperature runs hot enough to actually extract flavor properly. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I've owned $200 machines that brewed weaker coffee than this $40 unit."* The shower head distributes water evenly across the grounds basket, and the 10-cup carafe pours cleanly without the dribble-down-the-side problem that plagues cheaper glass carafes.

The permanent filter is a genuine win. It's a fine-mesh cone that catches sediment well — you won't get grit in your cup — and it rinses clean in about ten seconds. If you're tired of buying #4 paper filters, that's an ongoing cost savings.

**Where it stumbles**

The lack of a programmable timer is the big one. If you want coffee waiting when you wake up, this isn't your machine. You're setting it manually every single morning. At this price, that's a tradeoff, not a dealbreaker — but worth knowing.

The fixed water reservoir is another minor annoyance. You can't detach it to fill at the sink; you're either using a pitcher or filling it in place, which is awkward if your faucet doesn't reach. Several reviewers mentioned this specifically.

The warming plate runs hot. Coffee left on the burner for over an hour develops that burnt, bitter edge. If you pour a cup and walk away, you'll taste the difference. Solution: turn it off after brewing and use a thermal carafe if you need to hold coffee longer.

**Who should buy this**

The Breton CRACKER is for the practical, no-fuss coffee drinker. You want a reliable drip brewer that makes solid coffee without the smart-home integration, the Bluetooth app, or the $300 price tag. It's ideal for:

- First apartments or dorm rooms where budget matters more than bells and whistles
- Small kitchens where counter space is tight
- Anyone who drinks coffee within an hour of brewing and doesn't need to hold it hot all morning
- Office break rooms where a permanent filter means no one has to restock paper filters

It's not for the pour-over purist, the espresso enthusiast, or anyone who needs programmable wake-up brewing. Those people should look elsewhere.

**FAQ**

**Does the permanent filter really work, or does coffee taste muddy?**
It works. The mesh is fine enough to catch grounds and fines, and the shower head distributes water evenly enough that you don't get channeling. Rinse it immediately after brewing and it stays clean.

**Can I use paper filters instead?**
Yes — a standard #4 cone filter fits if you prefer the cleaner cup and easier cleanup. The basket accommodates both.

**How loud is it during brewing?**
It's a standard drip machine — a mild gurgle during the brew cycle and a soft beep when done. Nothing obnoxious, but it's not silent either.

**The Verdict**

The Breton CRACKER is a genuinely good budget brewer that understands its lane. It doesn't try to be a $500 espresso machine; it just makes a hot, properly extracted pot of coffee with minimal fuss and zero ongoing filter costs. The missing timer and hot warming plate are real limitations, but they're the kind of compromises you accept at this price point.

**Buy it if** you want a no-nonsense drip machine that makes good coffee without breaking the bank. **Skip it if** you need programmable brewing or plan to hold coffee hot for hours.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, honest workhorse that earns its keep.
