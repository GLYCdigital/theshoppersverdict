**Is this ceramic pour-over worth $24.59, or should you keep scrolling?**

Here's the thing about pour-over coffee: you don't need a $150 glass contraption with a built-in scale and Bluetooth. You need a solid brewer, a decent filter, and water at the right temperature. The Simply Charmed ceramic pour-over does exactly that — and it does it with a bit of personality that most stainless steel cones lack.

**What you actually get**

The box contains one ceramic pour-over brewer, a pack of Melitta #2 cone filters, and nothing else. No measuring scoop, no carafe, no stand. You'll supply your own mug or server. The brewer itself measures roughly 5 inches across the top and stands about 3.5 inches tall — compact enough to tuck into a cabinet but substantial enough to feel like a real kitchen tool.

The ceramic construction is the headline here. It's not a thin, fragile shell — this thing has heft. It holds heat noticeably better than the metal cones I've tested, which means your slurry stays at extraction temperature longer. That translates to a more consistent cup, especially if you're brewing slowly.

**How it performs in real use**

Place the brewer directly on your mug, insert a #2 filter, add grounds, and pour. The brew basket handles anywhere from 8 to 16 ounces depending on how much water and coffee you use. For a standard 12-ounce mug, I used about 22 grams of coffee with a medium grind. The flow rate is well-balanced — it doesn't stall like some ceramic brewers with overly tight drainage holes, and it doesn't rush through so fast that you get weak, under-extracted coffee.

One thing worth noting: there are no interior measurement markings. You're eyeballing water levels unless you measure separately. For a brewer in this price range, that's not a dealbreaker — but it's an omission that makes consistency harder for beginners.

**What 1,900+ buyers consistently say**

With a 4.7-star average across nearly 2,000 ratings, the pattern is clear. Buyers repeatedly mention durability and the adorable design. One reviewer noted, *"I've dropped this twice on my tile floor and it hasn't chipped. I was sure it was going to shatter."* Another said, *"The golden lab looks even better in person — my wife uses it every morning now."* The Melitta filter compatibility also gets frequent shout-outs because you can find those filters at any Target or Walmart without hunting for specialty supplies.

**Where it falls short**

The design isn't perfect. The brewer and base are one molded piece — you can't detach the cone for deep cleaning. Coffee oils will accumulate in the drainage holes over time, and you'll need a brush to get in there properly. The printed lab design sits on the surface rather than being fully glazed over. It looks great new, but if you're aggressive with abrasive scrubbers, it will fade. Hand-washing is the safer play here.

Also, if you're brewing directly into a standard 8-ounce mug, the base fits fine. But if you use a wider travel mug or a thick-walled ceramic mug with a smaller opening, check the fit before you buy — the brewer's base is about 4 inches in diameter.

**Who should buy this**

- **Dog lovers** who want a brewer that's actually fun to leave on the counter
- **Single-serve coffee drinkers** who want pour-over quality without a $60+ setup
- **Campers and travelers** who want something unbreakable-ish (it's ceramic, not indestructible — but it survives drops better than glass)

**Skip it if** you need precise measurement markings, want dishwasher-safe convenience (the printed design won't survive the dishwasher long-term), or you're brewing for more than one person at a time.

**FAQ**

**Does this work with a Chemex or V60 filter?**  
No. It's designed for Melitta #2 cone filters specifically. Standard V60 filters are too tall and Chemex filters are far too large.

**Can I brew directly into a travel mug?**  
It depends on the opening diameter. The base is about 4 inches across. If your travel mug opening is smaller than that, you'll need to brew into a regular mug and transfer.

**Is the golden lab design painted on or embedded?**  
It's printed onto the ceramic surface, not glazed over. Hand-wash with a soft sponge and it'll last — abrasive scrubbing will fade it.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want an affordable, genuinely well-made ceramic pour-over with personality that brews a consistent cup. **Skip it if** you need measurement guides or plan to be rough with it in the dishwasher.

For $24.59, this is one of the better value pour-over brewers on Amazon right now. It's not a fancy gooseneck-kettle setup, but it's honest, functional, and charming in a way that makes you want to use it every morning.
