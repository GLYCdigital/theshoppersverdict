## What You're Actually Getting

The package contains six 8-fluid-ounce plastic bottles — 48 ounces total. That's the equivalent of one and a half standard juice jugs, but in single-serve form. The bottles are BPA-free, easy for small hands to open, and resealable if someone doesn't finish one in a sitting. As shown in the product image, the labeling is clean and straightforward — no cartoon characters plastered everywhere, just the Mott's branding and a clear "100% Juice" claim.

The ingredient list is refreshingly short: apple juice, white grape juice, ascorbic acid (vitamin C), and natural flavors. That's it. No high fructose corn syrup, no artificial colors, no preservatives. Each 8 oz serving clocks in at 110 calories with 26 grams of naturally occurring sugar — worth noting if you're watching sugar intake, but it's coming from fruit, not added sweeteners.

## How It Tastes and Performs

The flavor profile is where this juice earns its keep. White grape juice adds a gentle sweetness and a slightly floral note that rounds out the tartness of the apple. The result is smoother than a standard apple juice — think of it as apple juice with the rough edges filed off. It's not overly sweet, and there's no metallic aftertaste that sometimes plagues bottled juices.

Cold, it's genuinely refreshing. Room temperature, it's still drinkable, which matters if these sit in a backpack until lunch. The bottles are sturdy — I've dropped one on tile from counter height and it didn't crack or split. The flip-top cap seals firmly and doesn't leak in a lunchbox.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, the 6-pack quantity is a real limitation. Second, a handful of reviewers reported bottles arriving with compromised seals — not widespread, but worth checking each bottle when your order lands. Third, if you're expecting the fiber and nutrients of whole fruit, this isn't that. It's juice, plain and simple.

**Do these need refrigeration before opening?**
No. They're shelf-stable until opened, which makes them great for pantry storage. Once opened, refrigerate and drink within 7-10 days.
