## The $27 Coffee Maker That Just Works — With Caveats

Let’s be honest: at $26.99, you’re not buying a coffee experience. You’re buying a reliable, no-nonsense machine that makes a decent cup of drip coffee without fuss. And for that specific job, the Mr. Coffee 5-Cup Mini Brew Switch delivers exactly what it promises — no more, no less.

This is the coffee maker for people who don't want to think about their coffee maker. There's no programmable timer, no Bluetooth app, no built-in grinder. Just a rocker switch, a water reservoir, and a glass carafe. That simplicity is the entire appeal.

## What You Actually Get

Open the box and you'll find the main unit, a 5-cup glass carafe, a permanent filter basket, and the warming plate. That's it. No measuring scoop (use a standard tablespoon — 1 tbsp per cup of water), no water filter, no frills.

The machine itself measures roughly 10.8 inches wide by 7.4 inches deep by 10.2 inches tall. That's compact enough for a dorm room, a small apartment kitchen, or an office break room. The black plastic body won't win design awards, but it's unobtrusive and matches just about anything.

## Real-World Performance

The brew cycle takes about 6 to 8 minutes for a full pot — noticeably slower than larger machines. The water heats in a small internal tube, which is why it takes longer. The payoff is coffee that brews at a proper temperature (around 195-205°F), extracting decent flavor from your grounds.

The grab-a-cup feature is genuinely useful. As shown in the product image, the carafe has a drip-stop mechanism in the basket. Pull the carafe mid-brew, and the flow stops almost immediately. You can pour a cup without leaving a coffee-colored puddle on the warming plate. This is a feature usually reserved for pricier machines.

The warming plate keeps coffee hot, but there's no auto shut-off. This is the biggest safety and energy concern. If you forget to flip the switch, the plate stays on indefinitely. Several reviewers mention this as their primary gripe. One Amazon customer noted, "I've accidentally left it on overnight twice. Nothing bad happened, but it's a real design flaw."

## Where It Falls Short

The glass carafe is thin. It's dishwasher-safe per the manufacturer, but hand-washing is the safer play. Many reviewers report the carafe cracking after a few months of dishwasher cycles. Buy a spare carafe when you buy the machine — they're cheap and you'll eventually need one.

The lack of auto shut-off is a legitimate safety concern, not just a convenience issue. If you're the type who forgets things, set a reminder or consider a machine with this feature.

Also, the "5 cups" is measured in 5-ounce coffee cups, not the 8-ounce mugs most people use. Realistically, you're getting about 2.5 standard mugs per full brew. This is standard industry labeling, but it catches people off guard.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the Mr. Coffee 5-Cup Mini Brew use paper filters or a permanent one?**
A: Both. It comes with a permanent mesh basket, but the basket is sized to accept standard #4 cone paper filters. Most users report better-tasting coffee with paper filters since they catch more oils and fines.

**Q: Can I brew a single cup instead of a full pot?**
A: Yes, but with a caveat. The machine will brew as little as one cup, and the grab-a-cup feature means you can pull the carafe as soon as you have enough. The coffee may be slightly stronger or weaker depending on how quickly you pull it.

**Q: How do I descale this machine?**
A: Run a cycle with a 50/50 mix of white vinegar and water, then run 2-3 cycles with clean water to rinse. Do this every 1-2 months depending on your water hardness. This is essential for maintaining brew temperature and flow rate.
