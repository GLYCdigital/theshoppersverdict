Each batch carries a UMF (Unique Manuka Factor) rating with a corresponding MGO (Methylglyoxal) number printed right on the label—that's the compound responsible for manuka's distinctive antibacterial properties. You're not guessing at potency; it's measured and verified.

The honey itself arrives thick and creamy. As shown in the product image, it's a dark amber with a smooth, almost buttery texture. It doesn't drip off a spoon—it slowly folds over itself. That density is a hallmark of genuine high-grade manuka.
