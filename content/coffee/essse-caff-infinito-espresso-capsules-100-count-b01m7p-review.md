**The 100-count bulk espresso pod that actually respects your wallet — and your machine**

Let's be honest: the espresso capsule game is full of overpriced, under-flavored aluminum pucks that cost more per ounce than a craft cocktail. Essse CaffÃ© Infinito isn't trying to reinvent the wheel — it's trying to give you a dependable, consistent shot of espresso for a fraction of the Nespresso-branded price. And for the most part, it succeeds.

**What's actually in the box**

You're getting 100 aluminum capsules, each holding 5.5 grams of ground coffee. That's a full two months of daily shots for most people. The capsules are compatible with original-line Nespresso machines (the round ones, not Vertuo), plus most third-party brewers that accept standard Nespresso pods. The box itself is sturdy, with the capsules arranged in neat rows — no crushed pods, no torn foil. As shown in the product image, the black-and-gold branding is understated; this isn't flashy packaging, it's bulk utility.

**Flavor and performance**

Infinito sits at an 8/10 intensity rating, which is the sweet spot for most drinkers. It's a medium-dark roast with notes of dark chocolate and a faint nuttiness that comes through more in an americano than a straight shot. The crema is decent — golden and persistent, though not as thick and velvety as what you'd get from a freshly roasted specialty pod. Extraction runs clean through my Breville machine, with no channeling or sputtering. The shot pulls at a reasonable rate, and the capsule doesn't collapse or deform mid-brew.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The overwhelming theme in customer feedback is value without sacrifice. One verified buyer put it plainly: "I've bought these three times now. Same quality as the big brand at half the price." Another noted the consistency: "Every single capsule brews identically — no weak shots, no duds." That consistency matters when you're buying in bulk; a bad batch would mean 50 wasted mornings.

**Where it falls short**

The crema is the first tradeoff. If you're the type who judges espresso by the thickness of that golden layer, Infinito will disappoint. It's present but thin compared to Illy or even Starbucks-branded pods. Second, there's no roast date anywhere on the packaging. For a product that lives or dies on freshness, that's a transparency gap. Third — and this is a compatibility warning — these will *not* fit Vertuo machines. If you've got a Vertuo, skip this listing entirely.

**Who should buy this**

- **Daily espresso drinkers** who go through 2+ shots a day and are tired of paying $0.75 per pod

**Skip it if** you're a straight-espresso purist chasing café-level crema, or you own a Vertuo machine.
