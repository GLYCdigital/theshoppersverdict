## What's Actually in the Box

This is not a box of mystery off-brand snacks. CRAVEBOX packs recognizable names: Lay's potato chips, Oreo cookies, Welch's fruit snacks, Nature Valley granola bars, and various candies and trail mixes. You're getting roughly 100 individual items, each separately wrapped — which matters more than you'd think.

The mix skews balanced between sweet (cookies, candies, fruit snacks) and savory (chips, pretzels, crackers). There's enough variety that even picky eaters will find several things they like. As one verified buyer put it: *"Bought this for my college kid during finals week. She said it was the best thing I've sent her all semester."*

## Real-World Performance

The individual wrapping is the unsung hero here. You can toss the whole box in a car for a road trip, keep it in a dorm room, or stash it in a cubicle without worrying about opening a giant bag and having everything go stale. The resealable nature of most items means you'll actually finish everything instead of throwing out half-eaten bags.

Shipping quality is solid. The box arrives packed tight, and while I've seen occasional reports of slightly crushed chips — one reviewer noted *"some chip bags were a bit squished but nothing was broken open"* — the seals hold and the product remains edible. If you're gifting this, the presentation is genuinely nice: the box itself is sturdy and the assortment looks impressive when opened.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is flawless, and CRAVEBOX has three honest limitations.

First, you cannot customize contents. If someone in your house hates chocolate or has a nut allergy, this box includes both. It's a gamble on preferences, not a tailored selection.

Second, portion sizes vary. Some items are full-size, but several are single-serve or snack-size. The "100 count" number sounds massive, but a determined snacker can work through a meaningful chunk in a week. For a family of four, expect 2-3 weeks of steady snacking.

Third, repeat purchases may feel repetitive. CRAVEBOX rotates items, but if you're buying this monthly, you'll see overlap. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing.
