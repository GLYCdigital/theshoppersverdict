**Opening Hook**

Most coffee-flavored candy is a lie. You bite in expecting that deep, roasty kick and instead get a mouthful of artificial mocha syrup and sugar. Coffee Rio's Original Roast Caramels are not that. These are the real deal — actual coffee extract folded into a soft, buttery caramel that tastes like someone melted a pour-over into a candy kitchen. If you're a black-coffee drinker who also has a sweet tooth, this is your candy.

**What You Actually Get**

The pack of 3 contains three 5.5-ounce bags, each filled with individually wrapped caramels. The pieces are small — roughly thumbnail-sized squares — which is smart because they're potent. Each piece delivers a concentrated coffee punch that lingers pleasantly. The wrapping is simple foil, nothing fancy, but it keeps the caramels fresh and makes them easy to toss in a bag without melting into a sticky mess.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

I tested these over a week, keeping a bag on my desk and another in my car. The texture is the standout feature. These caramels are soft — almost fudge-like — and they chew easily without that annoying stick-to-your-molars fight you get from brands like Kraft. The coffee flavor hits immediately, then fades into a smooth caramel finish. It's not bitter, but it's also not shy. Think espresso bean dipped in caramel, not caramel with a hint of coffee.

One thing worth noting: these are caffeinated. Each piece packs a small but noticeable kick. Three or four pieces give you a mild buzz, which is either a feature or a warning depending on your sensitivity. If you're eating these at 9 PM, don't say I didn't warn you.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Scrolling through the 229 ratings, the word "fresh" comes up repeatedly. One reviewer put it plainly: *"These taste like real coffee, not the fake stuff in most coffee candies. The caramel is soft and the coffee flavor is strong without being bitter."* Another noted the packaging: *"Individually wrapped means they don't stick together, and they're perfect for my office drawer."* The 4.6-star average holds up because the product delivers exactly what it promises — no surprises, no gimmicks.

**Where It Falls Short**

Honest tradeoffs exist. First, the price per ounce is steep compared to standard caramels. You're paying a premium for the coffee quality, and if you're expecting a bargain bulk candy, this isn't it. Second, the flavor intensity is polarizing. If you're someone who takes coffee with cream and three sugars, this might hit too hard. One reviewer mentioned, *"I love coffee but these are almost too strong for me — I can only eat one at a time."* That's not a flaw, but it's a real consideration. Finally, the bags are small. A 5.5-ounce bag disappears quickly if you're sharing — or if you're not.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Black coffee enthusiasts** who want a candy that actually tastes like their morning brew
- **Office workers** who need a discreet, non-melting pick-me-up that isn't another energy drink
- **Gift givers** looking for a unique stocking stuffer for a coffee lover who has everything

**Skip it if** you prefer milk chocolate or sweet, mild coffee flavors, or if you're shopping on a tight per-ounce budget.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these actually caffeinated?**
Yes. Each piece contains real coffee extract, so there's a mild caffeine content. It's not enough to replace your morning cup, but a few pieces can give you a noticeable lift.

**Q: Do they melt in transit or in a hot car?**
The individually wrapped pieces hold up surprisingly well. I left a bag in my car on an 85-degree day, and the caramels stayed intact — they softened slightly but didn't turn into a puddle.

**Q: How do these compare to coffee-flavored hard candies?**
These are softer and richer. Hard candies give you a quick flavor hit that fades; these have a slow-release coffee flavor that builds as you chew. The texture alone makes them worth trying.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely coffee-forward candy with premium ingredients and a satisfying chew. **Skip it if** you're looking for a cheap bulk candy or prefer milder, sweeter flavors.

**Rating: 4.6/5 stars** — A well-executed niche product that earns its premium price through real coffee flavor and quality texture. The only real knock is the cost per ounce and the intensity, which won't suit everyone.
