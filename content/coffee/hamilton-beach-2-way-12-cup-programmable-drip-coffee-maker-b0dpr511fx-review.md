There's a gap in the coffee maker market that's been annoying me for years: you either get a full pot machine or a single-serve unit. Nobody makes both well. The Hamilton Beach 2-Way tries to be that unicorn, and honestly? It gets closer than most.

This machine has two brewing chambers — a standard 12-cup carafe side and a single-serve side that brews directly into your mug. It's a clever solution for households where one person wants a pot and another wants a quick cup, or for days when you're alone and don't want to commit to a full pot.

**The dual functionality works better than expected.** The single-serve side handles up to 14 oz and uses its own reusable filter basket. The carafe side is a proper 12-cup programmable drip maker with a 2-hour auto shut-off. One reviewer says: *"You can brew any size pot you want or just one cup up to 14 oz. Put in water, put in grounds and turn it on...super simple."* Another confirms: *"It allows me to make one cup or a full pot. The coffee tastes very good."*

**The carafe design has a problem.** Multiple reviewers mention the same issue: the carafe drips when you pour. It's not a deal-breaker, but it's annoying. One puts it bluntly: *"My only complaint is that I end up dripping coffee every time I pour. The pot is just poorly designed for pouring. I just pour over the sink to avoid the issue."* That's a real usability flaw in an otherwise solid machine.

**The iced coffee feature is marketing, not engineering.** There's a cold brew setting, but it just brews hot coffee. You add ice. That's not cold brew. One reviewer bought this specifically for the cold brew option and regrets it: *"The cold brew option is just as hot as the regular brew so it wasn't worth the purchase."* If iced coffee is your primary use case, look elsewhere.

**But the basics are solid.** The coffee brews hot, the programmable timer works, and the machine is compact enough for tight countertops. A reviewer coming from a broken Keurig says: *"I am loving my new Hamilton coffee maker. It was super easy to set up and it's compact. I don't have a lot of counter space so it's nice that it does not take up very much space."* At $89.99, it's a reasonable price for the dual functionality.

**Who is this for?** Households with mixed coffee needs — pot drinkers and single-cup drinkers under one roof. People who want to ditch K-Cups but keep the single-serve option. Anyone who wants programmable wake-up coffee and the flexibility to brew just one cup on weekends.

It's not perfect. The carafe drips, the cold brew is a gimmick, and you'll need to buy filters for the carafe side. But if you're looking for one machine that does two jobs adequately instead of two machines that do one job well, the Hamilton Beach 2-Way is a practical, affordable choice.
