**What you actually get**

Two 3.25-ounce plastic cups. Each has a layer of Philadelphia-style cheesecake filling (creamy, dense, not airy) topped with a graham cracker crumble and strawberry pieces. The product image shows a generous crumble layer — and that's accurate. No spoon included, so grab one from the drawer.

**How it performs in the real world**

The texture is where Philadelphia gets it right. The cheesecake layer is thick enough to hold its shape but soft enough to scoop cleanly. The strawberry pieces are actual fruit chunks, not just flavoring syrup — you'll hit a real berry bite every few spoonfuls. That matters because it adds a tart counterpoint to the sweet cream cheese base.

The crumble is the star until it isn't. Eaten immediately, it's crunchy, buttery, and genuinely satisfying. Let it sit for 20 minutes or stir it in, and it turns into a paste. The fix is simple: eat the crumble layer first, then the cheesecake. That's the pro move.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.6-star rating isn't a fluke. Recurring themes in the feedback: portion control that actually works, the "real" strawberry flavor, and the convenience factor. **Where it falls short**

Three honest gripes. First, the cups are small. 3.25 ounces is about five or six solid spoonfuls. If you're expecting a dessert-for-two, this is a snack-for-one. Second, the crumble-to-cheesecake ratio is slightly off — you get more crumble than you need, and it goes stale fast if you don't finish the cup. Third, the sweetness is aggressive. This is a dessert, not a subtle treat. If you like your cheesecake tart, this will read as candy.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do these need refrigeration?**  
Yes. They ship cold and should stay refrigerated until opened. Unopened, they last a few weeks past the printed date. Once opened, eat within the hour — they don't sit well at room temperature.

**Q: Are the strawberry pieces real fruit?**  
Yes. They're small, soft chunks, not a gel or syrup. You'll taste the difference.

**Q: Can I freeze them?**  
Technically yes, but the texture suffers — the crumble gets soft and the cheesecake turns icy. Just eat them fresh.
