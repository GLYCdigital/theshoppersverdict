# Snack Factory Pretzel Crisp Everything: The Bagel-Toast Crunch You Didn't Know You Needed

Let's be honest — most everything-flavored snacks are a lie. You get one sad fleck of garlic on top and a mouthful of salt. Not this one. Snack Factory's Pretzel Crisp Everything actually delivers the full savory lineup — poppy seeds, sesame, dried garlic, onion, and salt — on every single crisp. As shown in the product image, the seasoning isn't sparse; it's a proper coating, which is exactly why this bag has earned a 4.5-star rating from 225 Amazon shoppers.

## What You Actually Get

A standard 7.2-ounce bag of pretzel crisps, each one about the size of a saltine but thinner and snappier. The shape is the signature Pretzel Crisp rectangle with scalloped edges — designed for dipping, not just snacking. The texture is the real star: a tight, baked crunch that snaps cleanly without the jaw workout of a traditional hard pretzel. These aren't puffy or doughy; they're engineered for maximum surface area, which means maximum seasoning contact.

## Performance and Real-World Use

I tested these three ways: straight out of the bag, with hummus, and with a sharp cheddar dip. Straight up, they're dangerously snackable — the garlic-onion combo hits first, followed by a clean salt finish. With hummus, they hold their structure; no mid-dip breakage disasters. With cheese dip, the everything seasoning actually complements the dairy instead of fighting it. That's the mark of a well-balanced snack.

The baked-not-fried claim checks out — no greasy residue on your fingers, and the crisps stay crunchy for days after opening, provided you seal the bag properly. The resealable packaging is functional, though the zipper takes a couple of tries to line up.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The consensus among reviewers mirrors my testing. One shopper noted, "Best crunch without the guilt — my whole family fights over the bag." Another praised the flavor distribution: "Finally a snack where every piece tastes seasoned, not just the ones on top." Multiple buyers mentioned using them as a lower-carb alternative to crackers for charcuterie boards, which makes sense given the sturdy build.

## Where It Falls Short

The salt is the main complaint, and it's legitimate. If you're sodium-sensitive, this might be too aggressive — one serving packs a noticeable punch. The bag also arrives with a disappointing layer of broken pieces at the bottom. It's cosmetic, but when you're paying for a premium snack, you want intact crisps. And if you're expecting the dense, chewy character of an actual everything bagel, adjust your expectations — this is a crisp cracker with bagel-inspired seasoning, not a bagel substitute.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Dip enthusiasts** who want a sturdy vehicle for hummus, guacamole, or creamy spreads
- **Snackers watching calories** — 110 calories per serving beats most chips and crackers
- **Charcuterie board hosts** looking for a crunchy, savory element that won't crumble
- **Everything-bagel lovers** who want the flavor without the carb load of a full bagel

Skip it if you're on a low-sodium diet or if you prefer soft, doughy pretzel textures.
