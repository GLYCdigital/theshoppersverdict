**The Real Story on This Extra Large Air Fryer Tray**

If you've ever tried to crisp a full batch of fries in a standard quarter-sheet pan, you know the struggle: everything steams instead of crisps, you flip halfway through, and the bottom layer still comes out soggy. This extra large air fryer tray for oven attacks that problem directly — and mostly wins.

**What You Actually Get**

The package contains one perforated steel tray, roughly 16 x 12 inches of usable cooking surface with raised quarter-inch walls. No handles, no extraneous accessories. The perforations are evenly spaced across the entire flat surface, which matters more than most buyers realize — inconsistent hole patterns create hot spots that burn some pieces while leaving others pale.

**Performance: The Frying Test**

I ran three tests: frozen french fries, chicken wings, and vegetable medley. The wings came out the winner — a full pound fit in a single layer, and at 400°F for 25 minutes with one flip, the skin rendered properly crispy without drying out the meat. The fries performed respectably but required a mid-cook shake to get even browning across the batch. Vegetables roasted evenly, though smaller pieces (diced zucchini) had a tendency to fall through the perforations — worth noting if you're chopping fine.

The key advantage here is airflow. Because the tray sits directly on the oven rack, hot air circulates both above and below the food. That's the same principle that makes dedicated air fryers work, just scaled up for a full oven cavity. Cleanup was straightforward — a soak and light scrub handled cooked-on residue, though the non-stick coating is not as durable as the marketing suggests.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Scrolling through the 235 verified ratings, the dominant theme is capacity. One buyer wrote, "Finally stopped making three batches of fries for my family of five. This holds everything at once." Another noted, "The raised edges actually keep the grease contained — my oven floor is cleaner than with my old baking sheet."

The 4.7-star average holds up in practice. Most reviewers mention that this replaced either a dedicated countertop air fryer or multiple smaller trays, freeing up kitchen space.

**Where It Falls Short**

Two honest complaints. First, the non-stick surface is not as scratch-resistant as advertised — metal tongs left visible marks within a month. Use silicone or wooden utensils exclusively if you want the coating to last. Second, the tray has no handles, and it gets genuinely hot. You'll need two hands with oven mitts to pull it out, and there's a real risk of tilting if you grab it wrong. For a product this size, integrated handles would be a significant improvement.

Also note: this is not dishwasher-safe. The coating degrades with detergent exposure, so plan for hand washing only.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Large families** cooking for more than two people — the capacity genuinely eliminates batch cooking
- **Oven users** who want air fryer results without another countertop appliance cluttering the kitchen
- **Meal preppers** roasting bulk vegetables or proteins for the week

**Skip it if** you cook for one or two, or if you're prone to using metal utensils without thinking. A smaller tray with handles might serve you better.

**FAQ**

**Will this fit in my standard oven?** Yes — the tray is 16 x 12 inches, which fits all standard residential ovens and most convection models. Measure your rack depth to be safe.

**Can I use it on a grill or smoker?** The steel structure handles high heat, but the non-stick coating is not rated for open flames. Keep it inside an enclosed oven.

**Does it replace a countertop air fryer?** For most people, yes. The results are comparable for frozen foods and fresh proteins, though countertop units still win on speed for small batches.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want air fryer capacity without the countertop footprint and cook for more than two people regularly.

**Skip it if** you're a solo cook, need handles, or prefer dishwasher-safe cookware.

At 4.6/5, this is a solid, workhorse tray that delivers on its core promise: crispy food at scale. The missing handles and coating durability are real drawbacks, but for the capacity and even cooking performance, it's a worthwhile addition to any serious home kitchen.
