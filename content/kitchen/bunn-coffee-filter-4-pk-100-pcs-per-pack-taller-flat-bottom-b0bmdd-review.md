If you own a BUNN coffee maker, you already know the frustration: standard flat-bottom filters sit too short in the basket, letting coffee grounds sneak over the top and into your carafe. BUNN's taller filters solve that specific problem, and they do it without the premium price tag of name-brand alternatives.

Let me be clear about what you're getting: four boxes, each holding 100 filters — 400 total. These are flat-bottom basket filters, not cone-shaped, and they're sized specifically for BUNN's taller brew baskets. The dimensions match the proprietary BUNN filter size, which is slightly taller than standard Mr. Coffee-style baskets.

## What's Actually in the Box

Four identical cardboard boxes, each with 100 perforated, crimped-seam filters. The paper is a medium-weight, natural brown (unbleached) stock. As shown in the product image, the filters have the characteristic BUNN basket shape — wider at the top, tapering slightly to a flat base. The crimped seams run vertically down both sides, and the bottom is sealed with a clean fold.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

I've run these through a BUNN Velocity Brew and a BUNN CSB3T, and the fit is correct. The taller sidewalls extend above the basket rim, which means grounds stay put during the brew cycle. No overflow, no sediment in the carafe. The paper holds up under a full 12-cup load without splitting at the seams — a common failure point on cheaper filters I've tested.

One thing that surprised me: the filter paper is slightly thicker than the generic store-brand options. That's good for durability but means you'll want to rinse the basket with hot water before brewing to knock out any paper taste. Skip that step and your first cup will carry a faint cardboard note. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing.

## Where It Falls Short

The packaging is the weakest link. These come in thin cardboard boxes with no inner plastic liner. If you live in a humid climate or store them in a cabinet near the stove, the filters will absorb moisture and can develop a musty smell over time. Transfer them to a sealed container if you're not going through them quickly.

Also, these are BUNN-specific. If you own a standard drip machine from Cuisinart, Hamilton Beach, or Black+Decker, these will be too tall and won't sit flush in your basket. Don't buy these hoping for universal compatibility.
