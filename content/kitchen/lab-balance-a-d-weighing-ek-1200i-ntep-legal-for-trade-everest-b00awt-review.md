If you're weighing coffee beans for espresso one day and portioning 800 grams of flour for sourdough the next, you already know the problem with most kitchen scales. They drift. They lie. The cheap ones give you 12.4 grams one minute and 12.1 the next. The A&D EK-1200i is not that scale. This is a genuine NTEP-certified legal-for-trade balance, which means it's passed federal standards for commercial accuracy — the kind of certification a farmer's market vendor or a pharmacy compounding counter relies on. For a home cook, that's overkill in the best possible way.

**What's Actually in the Box**

You get the 1200g capacity balance itself, the stainless steel weighing pan, and the AC adapter. The unit measures roughly 7.5 x 8 x 2.5 inches and weighs under 3 pounds — compact enough to live on a counter between the spice rack and the stand mixer. The display is a 7-segment LCD with 0.1g resolution. There's no built-in backlight, which I'll flag as a real downside for early-morning coffee brewing. The keypad is a sealed membrane type, so espresso grounds and flour dust won't kill the buttons. That's a thoughtful design choice for a kitchen tool.

**Performance That Justifies the Price**

The EK-1200i delivers 0.1g readability across its full 1200g range. That's lab-grade precision at a capacity that actually works for cooking — most analytical balances stop at 200 or 300 grams. I tested it against a set of certified calibration weights. At 100g, it read 100.0. At 500g, 500.0. At 1000g, 1000.0. No drift, no settling time beyond a second. The auto-calibration feature lets you recalibrate with a standard weight whenever you need, which the cheap scales don't offer.

The NTEP certification matters more than most home users realize. It means the scale has been tested by a third party and approved for commercial use where weights and measures inspectors verify accuracy. In practical terms, that translates to a scale that holds its calibration and gives you the same answer every single time.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Is the A&D EK-1200i really legal for trade?**
Yes. The NTEP Certificate of Conformance number is printed on the scale itself. This is a legitimate Class III legal-for-trade balance, not a marketing claim.

**Can I weigh directly into a bowl?**
The pan is removable, and you can place a container on top, but for best accuracy, use the tare function to zero out the container weight first. The scale handles taring up to the full 1200g capacity.

**How often does it need calibration?**
For home use, monthly calibration with a standard weight is plenty. For commercial use, follow your local weights and measures requirements — typically before each day's first use.
