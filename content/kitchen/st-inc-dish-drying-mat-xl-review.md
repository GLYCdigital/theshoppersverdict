Dish drying mats are one of those kitchen accessories you don't realize you need until you get one, and then you wonder how you lived without it. The S&T INC. Dish Drying Mat XL has become a wildly popular choice, with a 4.7-star rating from over 37,000 reviews. It's the perfect upgrade from the crumpled kitchen towel that used to live next to your sink.

## First Impressions

Out of the package, the mat comes folded, which means it has deep crease lines. This is expected — it's a textile product shipped in a small box. A quick run through the wash solves it completely. One reviewer notes: "It did have creases from being folded in its package, so I washed it with some kitchen towels. The creases came out and the mat did very well in both the washer and dryer."

After that initial wash, the mat lays flat and measures a generous 18 by 24 inches — enough room for a full drying rack worth of dishes. The charcoal color looks clean and modern on any countertop.

## Absorption Performance

The microfiber material is genuinely effective. Water from freshly washed dishes soaks into the mat quickly, then evaporates over time. The counter underneath stays completely dry. Multiple reviewers specifically mention this as the standout feature: "So soft yet very absorbent and have saved my counters."

The mat dries out reasonably fast between uses. The 18x24 size is large enough that you can rotate your drying position, giving parts of the mat time to air out. After a full drying cycle, the mat itself dries within a few hours in normal kitchen conditions.

## Versatility Beyond Dishes

Reviewers have found creative uses for this mat beyond its intended purpose. Some use it as a protective surface for drying herbs and produce. Others use it as a baking mat for kneading dough or rolling pastry — the microfiber grips the counter and catches spills. One uses it "while baking and ingredients don't get all over."

The soft texture also makes it a good counter protector for fragile glassware and ceramic dishes. It provides enough cushion to prevent scratching while keeping items stable.

## Durability and Care

After five weeks of daily use, one reviewer reports the mat still looks and performs like new: "I was hesitant about microfiber and looking cheap, but I have been pleasantly surprised with this purchase." The mat holds up well in the washer and dryer on normal settings.

The main durability concern is staining. Coffee, tea, and deeply colored liquids can leave marks on the light-colored mat. One reviewer notes "some stuff like coffee might stain it." If you're careful about what you set on it, this isn't an issue. If you regularly drip coffee while transferring mugs, the charcoal color helps hide marks better than lighter options.

## The Little Things

The mat has a soft, pliable feel — thick enough for absorption and cushioning but not so thick that cups wobble when set down. Multiple reviewers praise the look: "Looks much better than my old towels spread across my counters." The two-pack option means you can rotate mats while one is in the wash.

## The Bottom Line

The S&T INC. Dish Drying Mat XL is a simple product executed well. It's absorbent, durable, machine washable, and looks way better than the towel it replaces. For $13.99, it's one of those kitchen upgrades that improves your daily routine in a small but noticeable way. The XL size handles a full load, the microfiber keeps counters dry, and it holds up through repeated washing. If you still use a towel to dry your dishes, it's time to upgrade.

**Verdict: 4.6/5** — Absorbent, durable, and a clear upgrade from the dish towel era. One of the best kitchen accessories under $15.
