**Is an extended warranty on a $200 toaster ever worth it?** Depends on the toaster. But for a $1,500 refrigerator or a $2,000 range, ASURION's 4-Year Kitchen Protection Plan fills a real gap — the period after your manufacturer's warranty expires but before your appliance gives up entirely. At $175–$199.99, this plan isn't cheap, but neither is replacing a compressor.

### What You Actually Get

This is a service contract, not an insurance policy. For four years from your appliance's purchase date, ASURION covers mechanical and electrical failures caused by normal use. That includes things like a fridge that stops cooling, a dishwasher that won't drain, or an oven element that burns out. The plan covers parts and labor, and there's no deductible. If the repair costs more than the appliance is worth, ASURION replaces the unit — typically with a gift card for the current value.

You buy the plan through Amazon, and it's tied to the specific appliance in your order. Registration is automatic if you buy it alongside the appliance; if you add it later, you have 30 days from delivery to register online. Claims are filed through ASURION's website or app — no phone calls needed unless you prefer them.

### Real-World Performance

The claims process is where most warranty companies fail. ASURION doesn't. Buyers consistently report fast approvals and straightforward service. One reviewer noted, *"My dishwasher died 14 months in — manufacturer warranty was gone. Filed a claim Sunday night, had a repair tech scheduled by Tuesday morning."* Another said, *"They replaced my refrigerator outright instead of attempting a third repair. No pushback, no paperwork runaround."*

The catch? You're using ASURION's repair network, not your local independent tech. Some buyers have had to wait a few extra days for a technician in rural areas. And if your appliance has a known, recurring defect, ASURION will cover repairs — but they won't keep throwing money at a lemon. After two or three repair attempts, they'll replace the unit.

### What Buyers Consistently Praise

- **Speed:** Most claims are approved within 24–48 hours.  
- **No deductibles:** Unlike some competitors, there's zero out-of-pocket cost per claim.  
- **Replacement over repair:** When repair isn't cost-effective, ASURION issues a replacement value card quickly. One reviewer shared, *"They gave me a $1,800 card for my 3-year-old oven that was beyond repair. I put it toward a new model the same week."*

### Where It Falls Short

The plan excludes accidental damage — drop a cast-iron skillet on your glass cooktop and you're out of luck. It also doesn't cover normal wear items like seals, gaskets, or filters. Cosmetic damage (dings, scratches, rust) is excluded too. And you have to keep your receipt and register within 30 days of purchase; miss that window and the plan is void.

The bigger issue: price. At $199.99, this plan adds roughly 10–15% to the cost of a mid-range appliance. On a $300 microwave, that's a bad deal — you're better off self-insuring. On a $2,000+ appliance, the math works out.

### Who Should Buy This

- **Buyers of major appliances** (refrigerators, ranges, dishwashers, built-in microwaves) priced over $1,000.  
- **Anyone who keeps appliances longer than 5 years** — this bridges the gap between the 1-year manufacturer warranty and year five.  
- **Households with busy schedules** — the convenience of an online claim and a scheduled technician is worth something.

Skip it if you're protecting a countertop appliance under $500, or if you're the type who replaces appliances at the first sign of trouble.

### FAQ

**Q: Does this cover my existing appliances?**  
A: No. It only covers the specific appliance purchased alongside the plan (or registered within 30 days of delivery). It won't cover your other kitchen gear.

**Q: What happens if the appliance is discontinued?**  
A: ASURION will either repair it, replace it with a comparable model, or issue a replacement value card based on the appliance's current market value at the time of claim.

**Q: Can I cancel for a refund?**  
A: Yes, within the first 30 days for a full refund. After that, you get a prorated refund if you cancel before the plan expires.

### The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You're purchasing a major kitchen appliance over $1,000 and plan to keep it past the manufacturer's warranty. The no-deductible structure and reliable claims process make this one of the better extended warranty options on Amazon.

**Skip it if:** You're covering a small appliance, you're handy with repairs, or you'd rather bank the $200 toward a future replacement.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Solid coverage, honest exclusions, and a claims process that actually works. Just don't waste it on a $200 blender.
