**The $35 Fix That Saves You From a $600 Dryer**

Your dryer runs but produces no heat. The clothes take three cycles to dry. Before you call a repair tech and drop $200 on a service call — stop. The 279838 heating element is the single most common failure point on Whirlpool-built dryers, and this replacement part is what you need.

I've installed this exact element twice now — once on a Whirlpool Duet and once on a Kenmore 700 series. Here's the honest breakdown.

**What's Actually in the Box**

You get the heating element assembly itself — a coiled nickel-chromium wire wrapped around a ceramic frame with a metal housing. Also included are a thermal fuse and a hi-limit thermostat. That's a smart inclusion because those two small parts fail almost as often as the element itself, and they're cheap to include but annoying to buy separately.

The element measures roughly 8" x 3" x 2.5" and weighs about a pound. The mounting bracket has the standard two-screw pattern that lines up with the blower housing on most Whirlpool, Kenmore, Maytag, Amana, and Roper dryers.

**Real-World Performance**

After installation, my Whirlpool Duet went from "no heat at all" to "hot enough to scorch a button" in one cycle. The element pulls around 4,800 watts at 240V, which matches the OEM spec. Dry times went back to normal — a heavy towel load that took 90 minutes cold took 45 minutes hot.

One thing to note: this element runs hot. That's normal. The ceramic insulators do their job, but the outer housing will be too hot to touch within a minute of the dryer starting. Don't panic — that's how it's designed.

**FAQ**

**Q: How do I know if my dryer needs this element or a different part?**
A: If the drum turns and air flows but no heat comes out, it's almost always the element, thermal fuse, or thermostat. Test continuity with a multimeter — if the element reads infinite resistance, it's burned out. If it reads near-zero, check the fuses.

**Q: Is this compatible with my model?**
A: Check the model number on your dryer's door frame sticker. Cross-reference it against the 279838 OEM part number. The product listing shows the full compatibility list — confirm before ordering.

**Q: How long does installation take?**
A: 30-60 minutes for a first-timer. You'll need a screwdriver and possibly a socket set. Disconnect power first, remove the rear panel, and you'll see the element housing on the blower assembly.
