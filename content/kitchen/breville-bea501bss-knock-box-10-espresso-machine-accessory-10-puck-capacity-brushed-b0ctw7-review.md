If you've been knocking your spent espresso pucks directly into the trash, you're doing it wrong — and you probably have coffee grounds smeared across your counter to prove it. The Breville Knock Box 10 solves that mess with a dedicated bin that holds up to 10 used pucks before needing a trip to the compost or garbage. It's not a flashy purchase, but for anyone pulling daily shots, it's the kind of accessory you wonder how you lived without.

## What You Actually Get

The Knock Box 10 is a compact, countertop disposal unit measuring roughly 7 inches tall with a 6-inch diameter footprint. It ships fully assembled — no tools, no setup. Inside the brushed stainless steel shell sits a removable plastic bucket that captures the grounds. The top features a rubberized knock bar positioned across the opening, designed to take the impact of your portafilter without damaging the basket's ears.

The base has a non-slip rubber ring that keeps the box from sliding around when you're knocking with real force. It's a simple design executed with the build quality you'd expect from Breville — nothing rattles, nothing feels flimsy.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this over three weeks of daily use with a Breville Barista Express and a Rancilio Silvia. The rubberized bar does exactly what it should: it absorbs the knock cleanly, and the puck releases in one solid disc about 90% of the time. When it doesn't release cleanly, a second tap handles it without sending grounds scattering.

The 10-puck capacity is realistic, not marketing math. If you're pulling two doubles a day, you're emptying this roughly every five days. That's the sweet spot — frequent enough to avoid mold or odor buildup, infrequent enough that you're not fussing with it constantly.

One thing I appreciate: the knock bar is removable for cleaning. Coffee oils accumulate quickly, and being able to wash the bar and bucket under running water beats trying to wipe out a fixed design. The plastic bucket rinses clean in about 30 seconds.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.8-star average across 240 ratings reflects genuine satisfaction. The most common praise centers on build quality and quiet operation. One reviewer noted, "The rubber bar makes knocking nearly silent — my family can sleep while I make my morning shot." Another highlighted the capacity: "I brew three to four shots a day and only empty it weekly. No mess, no fuss."

The non-slip base gets repeated mentions too — several buyers noted it doesn't budge even with aggressive knocking, which matters more than you'd think when you're half-asleep and making coffee.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. At 7 inches tall, this box won't fit under most standard upper cabinets. If you're tight on counter space, that's a genuine problem — you'll either need to find a spot with clearance or accept it as a permanent countertop fixture.

The brushed stainless finish looks premium but shows fingerprints and water spots almost instantly. If you're obsessive about a clean kitchen aesthetic, you'll be wiping this down regularly.

Finally, the plastic bucket will absorb coffee odors over time. Regular rinsing mitigates this, but it's not a set-and-forget solution. Some buyers have reported needing to deep-clean with baking soda every few weeks to keep the smell neutral.

## Who Should Buy This

This is for anyone pulling two or more espresso shots daily who's tired of the trash can routine. It's also ideal for home baristas who value a clean, organized workflow — the Knock Box 10 keeps your station tidy between shots.

Skip it if you're a once-a-week espresso drinker. At that frequency, you're better off knocking into the sink and rinsing. Also skip it if counter space is at a premium — this needs a dedicated spot.

## FAQ

**Will this fit my Breville portafilter?**
Yes, it's designed to accommodate standard 58mm portafilters. The opening is wide enough for Breville's own models and most aftermarket options.

**Can I use this without a Breville espresso machine?**
Absolutely. Any portafilter with a 58mm basket works. The knock bar is universal in that sense.

**How do I clean it?**
The bucket and knock bar are removable and dishwasher-safe, though hand washing is gentler. The stainless exterior wipes down with a damp cloth.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You're a daily espresso drinker who wants a clean, quiet, and efficient puck disposal system. The build quality justifies the price, and the 10-puck capacity means less frequent emptying.

**Skip it if:** You're a casual brewer or you genuinely lack counter space for another appliance. This is a nice-to-have, not a necessity.
