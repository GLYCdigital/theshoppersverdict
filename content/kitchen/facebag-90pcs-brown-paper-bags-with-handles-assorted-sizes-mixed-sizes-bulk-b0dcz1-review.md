Let me be blunt: you don't buy a 90-pack of paper bags because you're excited. You buy them because you're tired of running out, or tired of flimsy bags that split the moment you look at them wrong. The FACEBAG assortment aims to solve both. Does it? Mostly, yes — with a few caveats worth knowing before you click buy.

**What you actually get**

Open the box and you'll find 90 bags across three sizes: roughly 20 small, 50 medium, and 20 large (the exact count varies slightly by batch). The medium bags are the workhorses here — about 8.5 x 4.5 x 11.5 inches — and they'll handle a loaf of bread, a couple of pastries, or a small gift with room to spare. The smalls are good for cookies or party favors; the larges fit a full baguette or a small bouquet.

The paper is 100gsm kraft, which is noticeably thicker than the grocery-store standard. The twisted paper handles are glued into reinforced patches, not just punched through the top flap. That matters — it's where cheap bags fail first.

**Real-world performance**

I loaded a medium bag with a 2-pound sourdough loaf and a jar of honey. The handles held without stretching. The seams held. I carried it by the handles for a solid five minutes and the bag kept its shape. That's the test most paper bags fail, and the FACEBAG passed it.

The kraft paper has a clean, natural look that works for bakery counters, farmers market setups, or wedding favors. It photographs well, which is why you see these in so many small-business product shots.

**What buyers consistently say**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews tracks with what I found. One reviewer put it well: *"These are sturdier than I expected for the price. I've reordered twice."* Another noted, *"Perfect for my cookie business — customers comment on how nice they look."* The durability and presentation are the two things that come up again and again.

**Where it falls short**

The size distribution is the biggest gripe. If you need mostly large bags, this pack isn't it — you're getting roughly 20 of those. The mediums dominate, which works if you bake bread or sell pastries, but not if you're packaging larger items.

The bags are also not grease-proof. A croissant with butter will leave an oil spot within minutes. For anything fatty, you'll need a liner or a different bag. And while the handles are strong, the bag itself has no reinforced flat bottom — heavy round items like oranges or apples will shift and stress the seams over time.

**Who should buy this**

- **Home bakers** selling at farmers markets or to neighbors — the medium size is perfect for bread and pastries
- **Small business owners** needing professional-looking packaging without custom printing costs
- **Event planners** making favor bags or welcome kits — the natural kraft look works with any theme
- **Anyone tired of flimsy bags** that tear on the way to the car

**Skip it if** you need uniform sizing, grease-resistant paper, or mostly large bags.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these bags food-safe?**
Yes — they're unbleached kraft paper with no chemical coatings. Fine for dry foods like bread, pastries, and cookies. Not for oily or wet foods without a liner.

**Q: Can I write on these bags?**
Absolutely. The kraft paper takes pen, marker, and even stamp ink well. That's a big reason small businesses like them for custom branding.

**Q: How much weight will they hold?**
Realistically, 2–3 pounds comfortably for the medium size. The handles are the strong point; the bottom seams are the weak link. Distribute weight evenly and you're fine.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5 — Buy it**

**Buy it if** you want a solid, versatile stash of attractive kraft bags that won't embarrass you in front of customers or guests. The price-per-bag is reasonable, the durability is legit, and the natural look works for just about any occasion.

**Skip it if** you need uniform sizes, grease resistance, or heavy-duty bottom reinforcement. For those jobs, you'd be better off with a specialty bag.

For the home baker, the small business owner, or the organized host who's tired of scrambling for bags at the last minute — this is a solid, no-regrets buy. Just be aware of the size skew before you order.
