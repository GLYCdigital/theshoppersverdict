The Cosori TurboBlaze is the air fryer I'd buy if I were starting over today — but it's not perfect. After 19,790 Amazon ratings averaging 4.8 stars, the consensus is clear: this thing cooks fast and cooks evenly. The real question is whether the upgrades over Cosori's own Pro II justify the $100 price tag. Short answer: yes, with caveats.

**What's in the box**

You get the 6-quart TurboBlaze unit, a square nonstick basket with a removable crisper plate, and a quick-start guide. No recipe book this time — Cosori moved those to their app. The unit measures 11.8 x 10.6 x 13.7 inches. It's compact for a 6-quart, but the square footprint means it takes up real counter space. The basket is dishwasher-safe, which is a genuine convenience, though hand-washing preserves the coating longer.

**Performance where it counts**

The headline feature is the 450°F max temperature — 50 degrees hotter than most competitors, including the previous Cosori models. That extra heat translates to genuine speed. I preheated in 4.5 minutes and air-fried frozen french fries at 400°F in 14 minutes total, no oil spray needed. They came out with a proper crunch, not the chewy exterior you get from lower-heat units.

The square basket is the sleeper feature. Round baskets force you to shake food to hit hot spots; the TurboBlaze's square design distributes heat more evenly across the full surface. One reviewer put it simply: "I cooked a full rotisserie chicken in 45 minutes and it was crispy all over, not just on the sides." That matches my experience — wings, chicken thighs, even a small brisket came out consistently browned.

The 9-in-1 functions include air fry, roast, broil, bake, reheat, dehydrate, proof, keep warm, and a "Max Crisp" mode. Max Crisp is not a gimmick — it combines the 450°F heat with an extended fan cycle that genuinely produces a deeper crunch on breaded foods. I used it for frozen chicken tenders and got a texture close to deep-fried.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across nearly 20,000 reviews, the recurring themes are speed and ease of cleaning. One verified buyer wrote: "This replaced my Instant Pot Vortex and it's not even close — half the cook time, twice the crisp." Another mentioned: "The beep when you shake the basket is loud enough to hear from the living room, which I actually love."

The keep-warm function is getting specific compliments too — it maintains 140-160°F for up to 12 hours without continuing to cook. That's genuinely useful for meal prep or family dinners where everyone eats at different times.

**Where it falls short**

The touchscreen controls are the weak point. There's no physical dial, and the touch panel is hypersensitive. Two separate reviews mention accidentally changing temperature settings when pulling the basket open — the vibration triggers a touch. It's manageable, but you'll learn to grab the handle carefully.

The nonstick coating is PTFE-based (Teflon). If you're in the PFAS-avoidance camp, this is a dealbreaker. Cosori doesn't offer a ceramic-coated version of the TurboBlaze at this price point.

At 62 dB during operation, it's one of the louder air fryers I've tested. It's a low hum, not a high whine, but in a small apartment with thin walls, you'll hear it from the next room.

**Who should buy this**

- **The speed-focused home cook**: If you're tired of 25-minute "quick" dinners, the 450°F heat plus fast preheat cuts real time off your routine.
- **Families cooking for 3-5 people**: The 6-quart capacity handles a whole chicken or a pound of wings in a single batch.
- **Anyone upgrading from a first-gen air fryer**: The square basket, Max Crisp mode, and keep-warm feature are meaningful improvements.

**Skip it if** you need physical controls, are avoiding PFAS coatings, or you're on a strict budget — the 4-quart version is cheaper but loses the square basket advantage.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the TurboBlaze require preheating?**
A: No, but you should. The 5-minute preheat is fast enough that skipping it isn't worth the uneven cooking. The unit tells you when it's ready.

**Q: Can it replace a toaster oven?**
A: For most things, yes. It broils and bakes up to 450°F, but it's smaller than a full toaster oven. If you regularly cook frozen pizzas larger than 12 inches, keep the toaster oven.

**Q: Is the basket really dishwasher-safe?**
A: Yes, both the basket and crisper plate are top-rack dishwasher-safe. Hand-washing extends the nonstick life, but I've run mine through 30+ cycles with no peeling.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want the fastest, most even air frying under $120 and don't mind touch controls.
**Skip it if** you need physical dials, avoid PTFE coatings, or have a noise-sensitive household.

The TurboBlaze earns a 4.5/5. It's the best performing air fryer Cosori has made, and at $100, it undercuts competitors like the Instant Pot Vortex Plus on both price and cook speed. The touchscreen and coating are the tradeoffs — know them before you buy.
