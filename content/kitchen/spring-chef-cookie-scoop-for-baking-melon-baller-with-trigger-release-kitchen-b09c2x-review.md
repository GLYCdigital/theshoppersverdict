## FAQ

**Is the trigger mechanism hard to squeeze?**
No. The spring is tuned for a light squeeze — roughly the same effort as clicking a retractable pen. It's not stiff, even for smaller hands.

**Can I use this for hot foods?**
The stainless steel head handles hot mashed potatoes or thick batters fine, but the plastic handle isn't meant for prolonged heat exposure. Let hot foods cool slightly before scooping.

**How do I clean it properly?**
