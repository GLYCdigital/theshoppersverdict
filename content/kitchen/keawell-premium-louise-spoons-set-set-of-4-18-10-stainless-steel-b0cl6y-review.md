**What You Actually Get**  

The box contains four identical spoons, each measuring 7.8 inches long with a bowl width of 1.5 inches. I've been using these daily for three months — morning oatmeal, afternoon soup, evening ice cream. The mirror finish hasn't dulled, and after 40+ dishwasher cycles, there's zero pitting or water spotting. One detail that surprised me: the bowl edge is rolled, not stamped. That means no sharp lip catching on your lower lip when you're eating — a small thing that makes a big difference in comfort.

A family of five (or anyone hosting dinner) needs two sets minimum, which doubles the cost.
