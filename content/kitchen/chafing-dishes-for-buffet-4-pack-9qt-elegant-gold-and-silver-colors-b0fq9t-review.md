## Where It Falls Short

The fingerprint issue is real. The mirror polish looks stunning in product photos, but it shows every touch. If you're setting up a self-serve buffet, expect to wipe the lids and frames down periodically. It's cosmetic, not functional, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

The fuel holders are serviceable but not premium. Standard 2-hour chafing fuel cans fit snugly, but taller catering-grade cans sit loose and can shift during transport. If you're planning a long event, either use the standard cans or plan to replace them mid-event.

Lids don't lock onto the pans. This is a minor gripe for stationary buffet use, but if you're transporting loaded dishes from kitchen to table, you'll need a steady hand and possibly a second person.

## FAQ

**Can these go in the dishwasher?**
Yes. The 18/8 stainless steel handles dishwasher cycles without warping or spotting. That said, hand-washing preserves the mirror finish longer — hard water deposits can dull the polish over time.

**What size fuel cans fit the holders?**
Standard 2-hour chafing fuel cans fit perfectly. The holders are about 2 inches deep, so taller catering-grade cans (the 4-hour ones) sit higher and can wobble. Stick with standard cans for stability.

**How many people will this feed?**
With 9QT per dish and four dishes, you're looking at comfortable coverage for 60-80 guests with one protein, one starch, a vegetable, and a side. For heavy eaters or multiple proteins per course, plan to replenish.
