If you've ever watched a mason jar leak oat milk all over your laptop bag, you already know why the LANDNEOO coffee mug exists. This 4.8-star jar-and-mug hybrid (240 ratings) solves the two biggest problems with overnight oats containers: leaks and temperature. But it's not perfect — and the lid situation deserves your attention before you buy.

**What you actually get**

The LANDNEOO set includes a 12-ounce glass jar with a double-wall insulated body, a threaded bamboo lid with a silicone sealing gasket, and a stainless steel spoon that nests in a side slot. As shown in the product image, the jar has a wide mouth that fits standard ice cubes and makes scooping thick oats easy — a detail that matters more than you'd think when you're scraping the bottom at 7 AM.

The double-wall construction is the standout feature. Overnight oats stay cold until lunch without sweating condensation onto your desk, and the same jar doubles as a coffee mug that keeps drinks hot for roughly 2 hours. That's genuinely two products in one, which justifies the price if you're tired of buying separate containers.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this jar with a standard overnight oat recipe (½ cup oats, ½ cup milk, chia seeds, honey) and left it sitting upright in the fridge for 8 hours. The seal held perfectly — no leaks, no seepage around the threads. The spoon slot is cleverly designed so the utensil doesn't touch the food when closed, which keeps things hygienic when you're carrying it to work.

One buyer summed it up: *"I've thrown this in my work bag sideways and nothing spilled. The glass keeps everything cold and the lid is actually tight."* That matches my experience — the silicone gasket creates a proper vacuum seal, not the flimsy press-fit you get with cheap bamboo lids.

The 12-ounce capacity is the real tradeoff. Standard overnight oat recipes call for 1 cup of liquid plus ½ cup of dry oats, which fills the jar to the brim. You can't shake it to mix — you'll need a spoon to stir. If you make overnight oats in bulk on Sunday, you'll need two or three of these jars, not one.

**Where it falls short**

The bamboo lid is the weak point. It's hand-wash only, and the product listing makes that clear, but it's easy to miss when you're in a hurry. Dishwasher heat will warp the wood and ruin the seal — a common complaint buried in the review section. One reviewer noted: *"Lid cracked after a few dishwasher cycles. Wish I'd read the care instructions first."* That's a user error, but it's also a design choice that requires more upkeep than a plastic or glass lid would.

Also, there are no volume markings inside the jar. For a product marketed at overnight oats, that's a miss — you're guessing on ratios or dirtying a measuring cup.

**FAQ**

**Is the lid actually leak-proof?**  
Yes, when the silicone gasket is seated properly. I tested it sideways for 15 minutes with liquid inside — zero leaks. Just make sure you thread it on straight.

**Can this go in the dishwasher?**  
The glass jar is top-rack safe, but the bamboo lid and spoon are hand-wash only. Wash the lid with soap and warm water, then air dry completely before storing.

**Does it work as a coffee mug?**  
It does, and well. The insulation keeps coffee hot for about 2 hours and cold drinks cold for 4+. The 12-ounce size mirrors a standard travel mug.
