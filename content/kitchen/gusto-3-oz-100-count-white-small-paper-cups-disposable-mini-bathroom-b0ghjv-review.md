Let's be honest: a 3-ounce paper cup isn't a product you agonize over. It's a utility item you grab without thinking, use for 30 seconds, and toss. But GUSTO's 100-count pack of these mini cups has quietly earned a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, which tells me people are actually paying attention to something they normally ignore. I tested these for a week in the two places they matter most: the bathroom sink and the kitchen counter.

**What you actually get**

The box contains 100 pristine white cups, each holding exactly 3 ounces. That's roughly 89 milliliters—perfect for a mouthwash rinse, an espresso shot, a medicine dose, or a paint-mixing dab. The rolled rims are the standout detail here; they're stiff enough that the cups don't buckle when you squeeze them, which is the failure point on most bargain mini cups. As shown in the product image, they stack cleanly without sticking together, so you can grab one one-handed even when your other hand is wet.

**Real-world performance**

I filled one with water and let it sit for 20 minutes. No leakage, no soft spots on the bottom. The paper is a touch thicker than the flimsy 2-ounce cups you find at dentist offices—enough structure that you can safely carry it across a room without spilling. In the bathroom, they work flawlessly for post-brushing rinses or diluted mouthwash. On the kitchen side, they're ideal for portioning out dry spices, holding dipping sauces, or serving tasting samples.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The reviews cluster around two themes: durability and value. One reviewer wrote, "These are sturdier than the ones I get at Costco—they don't collapse when you pick them up." Another noted, "Perfect for my medication routine. I use two a day and the pack lasts me months." The 100-count size hits a sweet spot—enough to be practical without committing to a 500-pack that sits in your pantry for two years.

**Where it falls short**

These are paper cups, and paper has limits. Grease is the enemy—olive oil, salad dressing, or any fatty liquid will soak through the bottom within five minutes, leaving a ring on your counter. They're also single-wall construction, so anything hotter than lukewarm makes the outside uncomfortably hot to hold. Don't use these for hot coffee or tea; you'll burn your fingers. And if you're looking for something to hold up to repeated handling, this isn't it—they're disposable by design, meant for one use then the trash.

**Who should buy this**

- **Bathroom users**: If you rinse with mouthwash or use a water flosser, these are the perfect companion cups. They eliminate the "shared bathroom cup" hygiene issue entirely.
- **Home entertainers**: For charcuterie boards, sauce flights, or sample tastings, these look clean and professional without the cost of ceramic ramekins.

**Skip it if** you need something for hot liquids, oily foods, or if you're environmentally conscious enough that single-use paper bothers you—though these are at least recyclable in most curbside programs.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Are these cups leak-proof?**
For water and thin liquids, yes—they hold up for 20+ minutes without seepage. For oil-based liquids or anything acidic like lemon juice, expect breakdown within 5-10 minutes.

**Can I microwave these?**
No. These are not microwave-safe. The paper will scorch and potentially catch fire. Transfer liquid to a microwave-safe container first.

**What are the exact dimensions?**
Each cup is about 2 inches tall with a 2.5-inch top diameter. They fit perfectly in standard bathroom cup dispensers.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a dependable, reasonably priced mini cup for rinsing, tasting, or portioning that won't disintegrate in your hand. The rolled rims and thicker paper put these a notch above the generic store-brand options.

**Skip it if** you need grease resistance, heat tolerance, or a reusable solution—this is strictly a cold-liquid, single-use product.
