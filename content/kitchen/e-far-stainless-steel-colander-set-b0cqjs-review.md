**Q: Are these dishwasher safe?**
Yes, but hand-washing is recommended to maintain the brushed finish. The steel itself won't rust, but hard water deposits can dull the surface over time.

**Q: Will the handles get hot when draining boiling water?**
The riveted handles stay cool to the touch during normal draining. The colander body itself will be hot, so use the handles.

**Q: Do the colanders scratch nonstick cookware?**
The rolled rims and smooth edges make them safe for most nonstick surfaces. Just don't use them as a mixing bowl with metal utensils.
