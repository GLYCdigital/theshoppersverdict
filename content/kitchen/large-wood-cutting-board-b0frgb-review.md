Here's where I'm honest with you. This board requires maintenance. Wood is a living material, and if you don't oil it every few weeks, it will dry out, dull in appearance, and potentially crack. If you're someone who wants to toss a board in the dishwasher and forget about it, this is not your product. You're committing to hand-washing and periodic mineral oil treatments.

The size is also a real consideration. At 20 inches long, it's going to dominate a small kitchen counter. If you're short on space, this board becomes a storage problem rather than a prep asset.

## FAQ

**Q: How often do I really need to oil this board?**
A: Every 2–4 weeks for the first few months, then monthly maintenance. If the wood looks dry or dull, it's time to oil. Use food-grade mineral oil, not vegetable oil — vegetable oil can go rancid.

**Q: Can I put this in the dishwasher?**
A: Absolutely not. Dishwasher heat and moisture will warp and crack the wood. Hand-wash with mild soap, dry immediately, and stand it on edge to air dry.

**Q: Is this board good for raw meat?**
A: Yes, but clean it thoroughly after use. Wood is naturally antibacterial when properly maintained, but you should wash with hot, soapy water after raw meat contact and oil regularly to keep the surface sealed.
