## What’s in the Box

You get one spatula, about **13 inches total length** with a **7.5-inch handle** and a **5.5-inch tapered head**. It’s a one-piece molded design — no glued-on handle, no crevices where grease collects. The silicone is food-grade and rated to **450°F**, so it’s safe for most stovetop work but not for resting on a screaming-hot grill grate. ## Real-World Performance: Where It Shines

The lengthened handle is the headline feature, and it delivers. At 13 inches, your hand sits a full **5 inches farther from the heat** than a standard 8-inch spatula. That matters when you’re frying bacon or searing eggs — no more pulling your hand back mid-flip. The silicone head is **flexible but not floppy**; it slides under pancakes and eggs with a satisfying glide, and the thin front edge (about **2mm**) means you can get under delicate items without crushing them.

I tested it on a **nonstick skillet** and a **pre-seasoned cast iron pan**. Zero scratches on both. The silicone is smooth, not grippy, so it won’t grab at seasoning. Cleanup is instant — a quick rinse or a wipe with a paper towel removes batter and oil residue.

## Where It Falls Short — Be Honest With Yourself

The flexibility that makes it great for eggs works against it for heavy lifting. Flipping a **thick smash burger** or a **6-ounce salmon fillet** requires more rigidity than this spatula offers — the head bends noticeably under weight. It’s also **narrower than a traditional turner** (2.8 inches across), so large pancakes or jumbo omelets need multiple passes. And while the handle is long, it’s not insulated — if you leave it resting against a hot pan edge, the silicone will conduct heat up to your hand. It’s not a dealbreaker, but it’s a limitation.
