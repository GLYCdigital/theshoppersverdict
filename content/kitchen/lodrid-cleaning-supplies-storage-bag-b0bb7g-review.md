Most cleaning caddies are flimsy plastic baskets that crack within months or fabric totes that collapse the moment you load a spray bottle. The LoDrig Cleaning Supplies Storage Bag takes a different approach — and with 240 ratings averaging 4.8 stars, shoppers have clearly noticed.

This isn't a glamorous purchase. It's a utility item. But if you're tired of cleaning supplies scattered under your sink or hauling armfuls of bottles between rooms, this bag solves a genuinely annoying problem. Here's the honest breakdown after digging through the specs and what real buyers report.

**What You Actually Get**

The LoDrig is a large, rectangular organizer bag with a rigid base that holds its shape. The main compartment is generous enough for tall trigger spray bottles standing upright. Around the outside, you get six individual pockets — some elasticized, some open — designed for brushes, scrubbers, and smaller containers. The top closes with a full-length zipper, which is rare at this price point. The material is a thick, water-resistant fabric with a wipe-clean interior lining. As shown in the product image, it's designed to sit upright like a structured tote rather than flop over like a grocery sack.

**Real-World Performance**

The standout feature is the rigid base. Fill it with four or five full-size spray bottles plus a roll of paper towels, and the bag still stands squarely on its own. That matters when you're reaching under the sink or parking it next to the stove while cleaning. The waterproof lining is another practical win — bottles leak, nozzles drip, and this bag contains the mess rather than letting it soak through to your floor or cabinet.

Buyers consistently mention the capacity. One reviewer noted, *"I fit my entire under-sink collection in here — Windex, bleach spray, scrub brushes, gloves, and a sponge holder. Everything stays upright and I can grab the whole thing in one trip."* Another praised the zipper: *"Nothing falls out when I carry it to the garage. That never happened with my old open basket."*

**Where It Falls Short**

The lack of a shoulder strap is the biggest miss. When the bag is loaded with heavy bottles, the top handles dig into your hand. For moving between rooms or up stairs, a crossbody strap would make a significant difference. This is a design gap, not a dealbreaker — but it's worth knowing before you buy.

The exterior fabric also shows dust and lint quickly. On lighter colorways, it looks tired after a few weeks of regular use. It wipes clean easily, but if you're picky about appearance, choose a darker option or keep it inside a cabinet.

**Who Should Buy This**

This bag is ideal for three types of shoppers:
- **Under-sink organizers** replacing wire racks or plastic bins that waste vertical space
- **Home cleaners or professional housekeepers** who carry supplies between rooms or jobs
- **RV, boat, or dorm dwellers** who need portable, contained storage in tight spaces

Skip it if you only own two or three cleaning products — a simple caddy would be overkill. Also skip if you plan to carry it long distances regularly; the handle-only design will frustrate you.

**FAQs**

**Q: Can it hold tall spray bottles upright?**
Yes. The main compartment accommodates standard 32-ounce trigger bottles standing vertically, with room to spare for shorter items around them.

**Q: Is it truly waterproof?**
The lining is water-resistant and wipeable. Small drips and spills won't soak through. It's not submersible, but for cleaning product storage, it does the job.

**Q: How does it stay standing when loaded?**
A reinforced, rigid base panel runs the full width of the bag. Even with uneven weight distribution, it won't tip over easily.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a durable, structured organizer that consolidates your cleaning supplies into one portable, leak-resistant unit. The capacity, zipper closure, and rigid base justify the price.

**Skip it if** you need a shoulder-carry option or only store a minimal number of products.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A well-executed utility product with one notable ergonomic flaw. For most shoppers, the pros far outweigh the handle issue.
