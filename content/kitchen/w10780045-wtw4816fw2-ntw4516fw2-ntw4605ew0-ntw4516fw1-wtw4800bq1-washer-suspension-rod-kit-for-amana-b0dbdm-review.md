It's not a hard job — typically under 30 minutes — but you need to know the order of disassembly (remove the cabinet top, then the front panel, then the four rods).

The second issue is packaging-related. A few buyers received rods with scuffed dampeners due to the flimsy poly bag. The rods still functioned, but it's a quality control concern worth noting.

Finally, this kit doesn't address other potential causes of shaking — like an unbalanced load, worn tub bearings, or a broken spider arm. If your washer still shakes after replacing the rods, the problem lies elsewhere.
