If there’s one chore that makes people abandon their air fryer, it’s scrubbing caked-on grease out of the basket. These pre-cut parchment liners for Ninja air fryers are designed to eliminate that pain point entirely. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, they’re clearly doing something right. But are they a perfect fit for your specific Ninja model? Let’s dig into the details.

**What You Actually Get**

The package contains square, pre-cut parchment paper liners sized for Ninja air fryer baskets in the 5-8 quart range. That covers the popular Ninja AF101, AF161, AF300, and similar models. Each liner is flat with raised edges — they’re not bowl-shaped, which is actually a plus. The flat design means they sit flush in the basket without obstructing airflow or needing to be weighed down. As shown in the product image, they’re designed to cover the basket floor completely, with the edges curving slightly up the sides.

**Real-World Performance**

The material is genuine parchment paper, not the cheaper coated paper some budget liners use. That distinction matters. This paper is rated to 428°F, which covers the max temperature on virtually every Ninja model. It doesn’t smoke, doesn’t burn, and doesn’t impart a papery taste to your food. The non-stick surface works exactly as advertised — chicken wings, french fries, and breaded shrimp release cleanly without tearing the liner.

The greaseproof backing is the real hero here. It catches all the drips that would normally bake onto your basket’s surface. After cooking, you lift out the liner, toss it, and the basket needs nothing more than a quick wipe. One reviewer noted, *“I was skeptical, but these caught every drop of bacon grease. My basket looks brand new after a month of daily use.”* That sentiment echoes across the reviews — the primary value proposition of these liners is the time saved on cleanup.

**Where They Fall Short**

The square shape is a double-edged sword. While it fits the rectangular Ninja baskets well, the corners of the liner don’t conform to the rounded interior corners of the basket. Food can sneak into those gaps and touch the basket directly, which means a small area still needs scrubbing. It’s a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

Fit is another consideration. These are sized for 5-8 quart models. If you own a smaller Ninja like the 2-quart or 4-quart versions, these liners will be oversized and flop around, reducing airflow and cooking efficiency. Measure your basket before ordering — don’t assume one size fits all.

Finally, they’re single-use. If you air fry daily, you’ll go through a pack quickly. That’s a recurring cost, though the convenience usually outweighs the expense for most users.

**Who Should Buy These**

This is a no-brainer buy for anyone who uses their Ninja air fryer more than twice a week. The cleanup savings are immediate and tangible. Busy parents, meal-preppers, and anyone who hates scrubbing will find these liners worth every penny. If you’re a light user who air fries once a month, you can skip these — your basket won’t get dirty enough to justify the recurring cost.
