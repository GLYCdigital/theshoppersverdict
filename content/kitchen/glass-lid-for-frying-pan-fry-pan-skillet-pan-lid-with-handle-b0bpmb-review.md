**Where It Falls Short**

Here's the honest part. This lid doesn't seal. If you're trying to braise short ribs or trap steam for rice, the gap between the glass and pan allows vapor to escape. It's a cover, not a pressure seal. For most frying pan uses — keeping splatter down, melting cheese, steaming vegetables — it's fine. But don't expect it to replace a Dutch oven lid.

The handle also gets warm. It's stainless steel, which conducts heat. At medium-high heat for longer than 10 minutes, you'll want a mitt. It's not dangerous, but it's worth knowing before you grab it bare-handed.
