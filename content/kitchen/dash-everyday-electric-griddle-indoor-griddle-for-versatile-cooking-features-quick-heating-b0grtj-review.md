## The Breakfast Machine That Earns Its Counter Space

Let me be direct: most electric griddles under $50 are flimsy, uneven-heating disappointments. The Dash Everyday Electric Griddle isn't one of them. With 235 ratings averaging 4.7 stars, this compact 20-inch cooking surface has become a staple in small kitchens, dorm rooms, and busy households. Here's why it deserves your attention — and where it falls short.

## What You Actually Get

The Dash Everyday comes with the griddle base, a detachable temperature control probe, and a nonstick cooking surface. That's it — no frills, no unnecessary accessories. The cooking surface measures roughly 20 inches by 10.5 inches, which sounds modest but handles a surprising amount of food. You can fit eight standard pancakes, six eggs, or four grilled cheese sandwiches simultaneously. The unit stands about 2.5 inches tall with a flat, stable base that won't slide around your counter.

## Real-World Performance

The standout feature here is heat distribution. I've tested griddles that scorch the center while leaving edges lukewarm — this one doesn't. The aluminum cooking plate heats evenly across the entire surface, and the adjustable thermostat gives you genuine control. The dial ranges from a low warm setting (perfect for keeping tortillas soft) up to roughly 400°F for searing burgers or getting that crispy edge on hash browns.

The indicator light is a small but meaningful touch. It cycles on and off as the griddle maintains temperature, so you're never guessing whether it's ready. From cold start, the surface reaches cooking temperature in about 3-4 minutes — not instant, but faster than most competitors in this price range.

Cleanup is straightforward thanks to the nonstick surface. Most food wipes off with a paper towel, and the smooth cooking plate means no crevices for grease to hide. Just be aware there's no drip tray — excess oil collects at the edges, so keep a paper towel nearby.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.7-star rating isn't accidental. Reviewers repeatedly highlight the same strengths:

*"I've used this every weekend for three months and it still works like day one. Cooks bacon, eggs, and pancakes without any sticking."*

*"Perfect size for a small apartment. I can cook breakfast for my family of four in one batch instead of doing multiple rounds in a skillet."*

The compact footprint gets frequent mentions — it stores upright and takes up less cabinet space than a standard frying pan. Multiple reviewers also note it's replaced their stovetop griddle entirely for family breakfasts.

## Where It Falls Short — Be Honest With Yourself

The short power cord (roughly 2 feet) is the most common complaint. You'll need to position this near an outlet or use an extension cord. That's a real limitation if your kitchen layout doesn't cooperate.

The nonstick surface is decent but not indestructible. Metal utensils will scratch it — several reviewers learned this the hard way. Use silicone, wood, or plastic tools exclusively. The surface also isn't dishwasher safe, which isn't unusual but worth noting.

One more thing: this is a 20-inch griddle, not a 24-inch one. If you regularly cook for six or more people, you'll likely need to work in batches. The Dash Everyday is optimized for 2-4 person households.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a breakfast enthusiast who wants to cook multiple items at once without juggling pans. You live in a small apartment or dorm with limited counter space. You want a reliable, budget-friendly griddle for weekend pancakes, weekday egg sandwiches, or grilled sandwiches.

**Skip it if:** You routinely cook for large gatherings. You need a drip tray for fatty foods like bacon. You prefer appliances with longer cords that offer more placement flexibility.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use metal utensils on the nonstick surface?**
A: You shouldn't. The nonstick coating scratches easily with metal. Use silicone, nylon, or wood utensils to protect the surface.

**Q: How long does it take to heat up?**
A: Roughly 3-4 minutes to reach cooking temperature. The indicator light tells you when it's ready.

**Q: Does it smoke when cooking at high heat?**
A: At maximum temperature (around 400°F), you may see some light smoke, especially with oil. This is normal for electric griddles without advanced smoke suppression.

## The Verdict

The Dash Everyday Electric Griddle earns its 4.7-star rating through consistent, even heating and a genuinely useful size for everyday cooking. It's not perfect — the short cord and lack of drip tray are real drawbacks — but at this price point, it outperforms most alternatives in its class.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Buy it if you want a reliable, compact griddle for daily breakfast cooking or small-batch meal prep. Skip it only if you need a larger surface or drip tray for high-fat cooking.
