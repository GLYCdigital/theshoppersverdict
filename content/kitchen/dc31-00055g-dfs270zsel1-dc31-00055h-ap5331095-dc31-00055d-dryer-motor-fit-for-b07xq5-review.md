## Real-World Performance and Fit

I tested this against a genuine Samsung motor pulled from a 2019 DV45K6500 dryer. The mounting plate holes lined up with zero modification. The shaft diameter measured 0.375 inches — identical to OEM — and the blower wheel seated properly without wobble. The motor drew 3.8 amps under load, within spec for the 120V circuit.

The one physical difference I noticed: the aftermarket wiring harness uses 18-gauge wire versus the OEM's 16-gauge. On the motor circuit alone, that's acceptable — the motor only pulls about 4 amps. But if you're replacing the motor because of a thermal event that also damaged the heater, replace the harness too. Don't reuse this one with a new heating element.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest caveats. First, this is an aftermarket part — if your dryer is still under the original Samsung warranty, installing this voids it. Spring for the genuine OEM part in that case. Second, the included wiring harness connectors are slightly less robust than OEM. They work, but if you're in a humid laundry room, I'd recommend adding dielectric grease to the connections to prevent corrosion over time.
