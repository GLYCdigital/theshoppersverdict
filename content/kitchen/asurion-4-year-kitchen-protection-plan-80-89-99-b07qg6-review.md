Let’s be honest: nobody gets excited about buying a protection plan. It feels like paying extra for the privilege of maybe not getting screwed later. But the ASURION 4 Year Kitchen Protection Plan, priced between $80 and $89.99, has quietly amassed a 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews — and that kind of consistency doesn’t happen without a solid claims process.

Here’s what you actually get for your money.

## What’s in the Box

This isn’t a physical product. You’re buying a service agreement, and the price scales based on the cost of your appliance. At the $80–$90 tier, you’re covering items in the $300–$500 range — think stand mixers, air fryers, espresso machines, or higher-end blenders. The plan runs for four years from the date of purchase, not from when you register, so don’t sit on it.

Coverage kicks in immediately, unlike some warranties that require a manufacturer’s warranty to expire first. That alone is worth noting: if your new KitchenAid dies in month two, you’re dealing with Asurion, not a customer service line that transfers you four times.

## Real-World Performance

The meat of this plan is what it covers: mechanical and electrical failures, plus accidental damage from drops, spills, and power surges. That’s broader than most extended warranties, which typically exclude anything that isn’t a factory defect. Asurion’s claims process is straightforward — file online or via app, upload photos, and in most cases, you get an approval or a prepaid shipping label within 24–48 hours.

Repairs are handled by authorized service centers, but if the unit can’t be fixed, Asurion replaces it or reimburses you up to the original purchase price. The coverage cap is $1,500 per claim, which is more than enough for anything in this price bracket.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The reviews tell a clear story. One buyer wrote, *“My $400 espresso machine died 13 months in. Asurion approved the claim in one day and sent me a check for the full purchase price. No hassle, no arguing.”* Another noted, *“I’ve bought this plan for three appliances now. The one time I needed it, they replaced my stand mixer with a brand-new model within a week.”*

The recurring theme is speed and fairness. People expect a fight when they file a claim — and they’re pleasantly surprised when they don’t get one.

## Where It Falls Short

Let’s be plain about the gaps. The plan doesn’t cover cosmetic damage, so a dented air fryer door isn’t a claim. Pre-existing issues are excluded, which is standard but worth remembering if you’re buying this for a used appliance (pro tip: it’s not valid for those). And if you sell or gift the appliance, the plan doesn’t transfer — it’s tied to you.

The registration requirement is the sneaky one. You have 30 days from purchase to register the plan online. Miss that window and you’re not covered. Amazon does link the plan to your order automatically in most cases, but it’s worth double-checking your Asurion account after checkout.

## Who Should Buy This

This plan makes sense if you’re buying a mid-range appliance you plan to keep for years — a $400 blender you’ll use daily, a countertop oven that sees heavy action, or a stand mixer that’s a gift for someone who bakes constantly. The math is simple: one repair call-out can cost more than the plan itself.

Skip it if you’re covering a budget appliance under $150. The replacement cost is low enough that self-insuring makes more sense. Also skip it if you’re the type who upgrades appliances every couple of years anyway — you’ll never use the full term.

## FAQ

**Can I cancel and get a refund?**  
Yes, within the first 30 days for a full refund. After that, you get a prorated refund based on time remaining, minus a small cancellation fee.

**Does it cover normal wear and tear?**  
Mechanical and electrical failures from regular use are covered. Wear and tear that doesn’t cause a functional failure — like a faded finish or loose hinge — is not.

**What happens if Asurion can’t repair my appliance?**  
They’ll replace it with a comparable model or reimburse you up to the original purchase price. You get to choose the replacement or the cash.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You’re spending $300+ on a kitchen appliance you’ll use heavily and want true accidental-damage coverage, not just defect protection. The claims process is fast, and the coverage starts immediately.

**Skip it if:** Your appliance is under $150, you’re a serial upgrader, or you’re buying for a gift — the non-transferable policy makes that a waste of money.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The price is fair, the coverage is genuinely useful, and Asurion’s reputation for actually paying claims is well-earned. Just don’t forget to register it.
