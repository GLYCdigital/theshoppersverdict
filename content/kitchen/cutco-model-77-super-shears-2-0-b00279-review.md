The serrated blade is the star. It grips — truly grips — wet poultry skin, cartilage, and even lobster shells without sliding sideways. Beyond poultry, they handled: trimming thick herbs, cutting parchment paper, snipping flower stems, and even slicing through a thin plastic takeout container. **Where they fall short**  

Honest downsides? First, the price — these run $80–$100 depending on sales. That’s overkill if you only need shears for occasional herb snipping. Third, the serrated edge makes fine, precise cuts (like trimming a pie crust) less clean than straight-edge shears. You’ll want a dedicated pair for delicate work.

**Who should buy these**  

Buy them if you: roast whole poultry weekly, break down large cuts of meat, or want a forever tool that outlasts your kitchen remodel. Skip them if you: only need shears for opening packages, have a tight budget, or prefer minimalist tools that do one job adequately.

**Q: Can these really be sharpened for free forever?**  
Yes — CUTCO’s Forever Guarantee covers free sharpening for life. You pay shipping to their factory, they sharpen and return them. Most users report a 2-week turnaround.

**Q: Are they truly dishwasher-safe?**  
The manufacturer says yes (top rack). But the serrated edge can dull faster with dishwasher detergent. Hand-washing is recommended if you want to maximize sharpness.

**Q: Will they cut through frozen meat?**  
They’ll handle partially frozen — think cutting a frozen pork chop in half — but don’t try to hack through a fully frozen roast. The serrations grip but the blades will dull prematurely.
