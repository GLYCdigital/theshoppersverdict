When your refrigerator ice maker breaks down and the repair quote makes your eyes water, the DOQAUS 4-Pack Ice Cube Tray Set is the sensible emergency kit that turns into a permanent upgrade. With over 12,600 reviews and a 4.5-star average, it's the most complete solution in its category.

## The System

This isn't just a set of trays — it's a system. Four silicone trays stack neatly in the freezer, a dedicated storage bin holds the finished cubes, and a scoop keeps things sanitary. The trays twist flexibly to release cubes with minimal effort, no banging on the counter or running under hot water.

The design is well thought out. The stackable trays take up surprisingly little freezer space for the volume of ice they produce. The storage bin sits neatly on top of the stack, so your workflow becomes: twist, dump into bin, refill trays, stack, and repeat.

## Real-World Experience

Users who ordered these after their ice makers failed are the most enthusiastic. One reviewer who was facing expensive appliance repairs says these trays "work better than the ice maker fridges in my opinion" and now prefers them. Another who uses a 36-oz Yeti daily for iced coffee says the cube size is perfect — big enough to cool drinks effectively but small enough to fit into water bottles.

The silicone material makes a real difference compared to cheap plastic trays. Cubes release easily without cracking or sticking. The 4-tray system produces enough ice for a household of 2-4 people, and with a quick rotation you'll never run out.

## The Downsides

Silicone can absorb odors. If your freezer has strong-smelling foods (fish, garlic, curry), the trays may pick up those notes. Occasional baking soda washes help. Filling the trays without spilling takes a light touch — the silicone is flexible, so overfilling can be messy.

The storage bin lid could be more secure — some users note it doesn't snap on as tightly as they'd like. And if you're looking for oversized cocktail cubes or perfect spheres, this isn't the set for you. These produce standard ice cubes in a sensible size.
