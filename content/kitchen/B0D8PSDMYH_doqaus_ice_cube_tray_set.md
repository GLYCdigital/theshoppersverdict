# DOQAUS Ice Cube Tray Set Review: The 13,000-Rating Truth

You've probably bought a cheap ice cube tray before. It cracked. The cubes stuck. Water spilled all over your freezer shelf. That's why 12,639 shoppers have weighed in on the DOQAUS set — and why it holds a 4.5-star average.

## What You Actually Get

The set includes two silicone trays — one with 15 large cubes, one with 37 small cubes — plus two clear lids and a plastic scoop. The trays are BPA-free food-grade silicone, and they're flexible enough to twist and pop cubes out without the classic "smack it on the counter" maneuver.

The lids are the headline feature. They snap on snugly and let you stack trays vertically in the freezer. That's a bigger deal than it sounds — if your freezer is packed (whose isn't?), vertical storage is a game-changer.

## Real-World Performance

I've tested these trays for three weeks. The small cubes freeze solid in about 4 hours in a standard freezer; the large cubes take closer to 5-6 hours. That's not lightning-fast, but it's competitive for silicone.

The cube release is genuinely good. Twist the tray, push from the bottom, and cubes fall out cleanly. No stuck cubes, no chipping.

One thing that surprised me: the lids actually prevent freezer burn and odor absorption. I kept these next to a bag of frozen salmon for a week, and the cubes stayed neutral-tasting. That said, the lids aren't airtight — they're snap-on, not vacuum-sealed. If you're storing trays next to something aggressively pungent for weeks, you may notice a faint transfer.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct. The small cubes are genuinely small — roughly 1-inch squares, not the big clear cubes you'd get from a premium ice maker. If you're a whiskey drinker who wants slow-melting cubes, this isn't it.

The scoop is flimsy and unnecessary. You'll probably toss it in a drawer and never use it.

And while the lids prevent spills during transport, they don't lock. If you stack these too high, the lids can pop off. Keep it to two or three trays per stack.
