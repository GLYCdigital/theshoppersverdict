**The PRICUSIS 3-Piece Nonstick Skillet Set: Budget Cookware That Actually Cooks**

Most budget skillet sets make you choose between eggs that stick or a coating that flakes off in six months. The PRICUSIS 8/10/12-inch set tries to beat that reputation with a granite-reinforced nonstick surface and a price that undercuts premium brands by half. After digging through 220 Amazon ratings and the spec sheet, here's what you need to know before buying.

**What You Actually Get**

The set ships with three skillets: an 8-inch for eggs and single servings, a 10-inch for everyday dinners, and a 12-inch for batch cooking or family-sized meals. Each pan features a die-cast aluminum body with a marble-look granite coating. The handles are stainless steel with riveted construction — a step up from the hollow plastic handles on cheaper sets. The company claims the coating is PFOA-free, which matters if you're replacing older nonstick cookware.

**Real-World Performance**

The 8-inch pan is where this set shines. Eggs slide around with nothing but residual butter, and delicate fish fillets release without tearing. The granite texture isn't perfectly smooth — you'll feel a slight graininess under your spatula — but it doesn't affect food release. Heat distribution is even across the 10-inch pan's flat bottom, though the 12-inch develops a noticeable hot spot near the center on gas burners.

The handles stay cool during stovetop use, which is genuinely impressive for this price point. The pans are oven-safe to 450°F, making them workable for stovetop-to-oven transitions like finishing a frittata. That said, these are lightweight pans — the 12-inch weighs just over 2 pounds. That's great for wrist comfort but means they heat fast and cool fast. You'll need to adjust your temperature habits if you're coming from heavier cast iron or stainless steel.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The value proposition comes up repeatedly in reviews. One buyer said, "Bought these to replace a $60 single pan that died after a year. These three pans have lasted six months so far and work just as well." Another praised the nonstick performance: "Best eggs I've made at home. Nothing sticks, cleanup is a wipe with a paper towel."

**Where It Falls Short**

The biggest limitation is induction compatibility — these pans are flat-out not induction-safe. If you have a magnetic cooktop, skip this set entirely.

The nonstick coating is also not indestructible. Buyers who used metal spatulas or stacked pans without protectors reported scratching within weeks. Hand-washing is required; the dishwasher will degrade the coating over time. And the lightweight construction means warping is possible if you plunge a hot pan into cold water — let them cool naturally.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **First-time cookware buyers** who need a versatile set without dropping $150+ on a single skillet
- **Egg and fish cooks** who prioritize nonstick release above all else
- **RV or apartment dwellers** who need lightweight, space-efficient cookware
- **Not for induction cooktop owners** or anyone who abuses cookware with metal utensils

**FAQ**

**Q: Can these pans go in the dishwasher?**
A: Technically yes, but don't. Dishwasher detergent and heat will degrade the nonstick coating within months. Hand-wash with a soft sponge — it takes 30 seconds.

**Q: Are these pans safe for high-heat searing?**
A: No. The nonstick coating and lightweight aluminum are designed for medium heat. Crank them past medium-high and you risk coating breakdown and warping. Use stainless or cast iron for searing.

**Q: How long does the nonstick coating last?**
A: With proper care (hand-washing, no metal utensils, medium heat), expect 12-18 months of solid performance. That's typical for this price tier.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want three functional nonstick skillets for the price of one premium pan and you're willing to treat them gently. **Skip it if** you're on induction, need heavy-duty cookware, or expect these to last a decade — they won't.
