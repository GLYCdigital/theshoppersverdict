## What You Actually Get

The woric roll measures 17.5 inches wide by 15 feet long — enough for roughly 4-5 medium-sized gifts depending on how generously you cut. The paper is 80gsm, which puts it in the heavier, more premium range compared to the flimsy 60gsm stuff you find in dollar stores.

The headline feature is the reversible design. One side features bold, colorful patterns with birthday themes — balloons, "Happy Birthday" lettering, confetti. As shown in the product image, this means you’re essentially getting two wrapping paper designs for the price of one roll.

Here’s where the woric paper earns its keep. The heavier weight makes a noticeable difference when you’re wrapping. It holds sharp creases at the folds, doesn’t tear when you pull it tight around corners, and resists the dreaded "poke through" when wrapping pointy items.

The reversible aspect is genuinely useful, not a marketing trick. ## Where It Falls Short

Let’s be honest about the downsides. At 15 feet, this roll is shorter than standard 20-25 foot rolls. If you’re wrapping large gifts — bikes, big boxes, oversized stuffed animals — you’ll burn through it fast. You might need two rolls for a big haul.

The lighter-colored side is also unforgiving. Any fingerprint smudges or minor scuffs show up clearly on the white background. Keep a clean surface and dry hands nearby, or you’ll see every mark.

One more note: the designs are more "generic birthday" than "kids-specific." If you’re looking for licensed characters or a specific theme like dinosaurs or unicorns, this isn’t that. It’s clean, colorful, and age-neutral — which is the point — but it won’t wow a kid obsessed with a particular franchise.
