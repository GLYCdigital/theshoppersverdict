**How it performs**

The 240V output is the spec that matters. This element delivers the full rated wattage — typically around 3,500 watts depending on your model — which means it heats to broiling temperature in about 90 seconds and maintains a steady bake cycle. The coil geometry matches OEM, so heat distribution across the oven floor is even. No hot spots in the back corner, no undercooked edge near the door.

Installation is straightforward for anyone who's replaced an oven element before. Unplug the range, pull the old element forward, disconnect the spade terminals, and reverse the process.
