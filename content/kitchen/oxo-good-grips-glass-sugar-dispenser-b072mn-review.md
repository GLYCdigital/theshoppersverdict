**FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask**

**Q: Can I put this in the dishwasher?**
A: The glass body is dishwasher safe, but hand-wash the lid. The silicone gasket can warp or lose its seal in high heat over time.

**Q: Will it keep brown sugar soft?**
A: It'll keep it from getting harder, but brown sugar needs a moisture source (like a terra cotta disc) to stay soft. This dispenser is best for granulated sugar.

**Q: Does the spout leak if it tips over?**
A: The flip-top seals tightly when closed, and the gasket prevents leaks. If you knock it over, you might get a small spill from the spout opening, but it won't pour out.
