Let me be blunt: most colanders are flimsy afterthoughts that flex, wobble, and shed their finish within months. The POPGRADE Colander With Handle is not that. It’s earned a 4.6-star average across 229 Amazon ratings, and after digging through the feedback and specs, the praise is mostly justified.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the colander itself — no accessories, no gimmicks. It’s a stainless steel basket with a long ergonomic handle and a wide base. The mesh is fine enough to strain rice and quinoa but open enough that water rushes through quickly. As shown in the product image, the handle extends well past the basket edge, which matters more than you’d think — it keeps your knuckles clear of steam when you’re draining boiling water.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The first thing you notice handling this colander is the rigidity. It doesn’t flex when you grip it, and the base sits flat without rocking. That’s rare at this price point. The handle is welded, not spot-welded or glued, so there’s no joint that’s going to fail after a year of heavy use.

Drainage speed is genuinely impressive. The fine mesh pattern covers the entire basket — including the bottom — so water exits from every direction. One Amazon reviewer noted, “Drains faster than my old colander and doesn’t leave water pooling in the bottom.” That tracks with the design: the bottom isn’t a flat disc with holes punched in it, it’s fully meshed, so nothing traps water.

The handle is the standout feature. It’s long enough to give real leverage, and the grip is comfortable even with wet hands. For anyone with arthritis or grip issues, this is a meaningful upgrade over the classic two-handled colander design.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the reviews, three themes dominate. First, durability — multiple reviewers mention dropping it or banging it against sinks with no damage. Second, the drainage speed, which I’ve already covered. Third, the size. It’s large enough for a full box of pasta or a big batch of washed vegetables, but it doesn’t dominate cabinet space.

One reviewer summed it up: “I’ve bought three colanders in the last two years. This is the first one I don’t hate. Solid, easy to hold, and cleans up fast.”

**Where it falls short**

The metal handle conducts heat. If you rest the colander against a hot pot while draining, the handle absorbs that heat quickly. It’s not a dealbreaker — you just need to be mindful — but it’s worth knowing.

Also, the manufacturer recommends hand washing. The finish holds up fine with soap and water, but if you’re someone who throws everything in the dishwasher, this will eventually show wear. That’s a tradeoff, not a flaw.

Finally, the fine mesh means small bits of food can occasionally get lodged in the holes. A quick rinse under running water clears them, but it’s slightly more maintenance than a coarse-hole colander.

**Who should buy this**

Get the POPGRADE if you cook pasta, rice, or grains regularly and are tired of colanders that flex, tip, or let quinoa escape. It’s also a strong pick for older cooks or anyone with hand weakness who needs a secure, comfortable grip. Skip it if you’re strictly a dishwasher-everything household or you need a colander that fits inside a specific cabinet slot without measuring first.

**FAQ**

**Is it really dishwasher safe?**
The manufacturer says no. Hand washing is recommended to preserve the finish. It’s quick to clean — a rinse and a wipe does it — but if dishwasher safety is non-negotiable for you, look elsewhere.

**Will it drain quinoa or other small grains?**
Yes. The fine mesh catches small grains effectively. Multiple reviewers confirm no quinoa escapes during rinsing.

**How big is it actually?**
It comfortably holds a full pound of pasta with room to shake. The wide base means it sits stable in most sinks without needing to be propped against the side.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a colander that will outlast your current one and makes draining hot pasta genuinely easier. **Skip it if** you need dishwasher-safe convenience or prefer a compact colander for tight storage.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Solid, well-built, and thoughtfully designed. The hand-wash requirement and heat-conducting handle keep it from a perfect score, but for most home cooks, this is the last colander you’ll need to buy.
