**The Short Version:** If your drip machine takes the junior basket size, these Tupkee filters are a legitimate upgrade over generic store-brand paper. At 4.7 stars from 235 ratings, the consensus is clear — but size compatibility is the catch you need to check before ordering.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains 200 white basket-style filters sized for 4-6 cup brewers. Each filter measures roughly 5.5 inches across the top, tapering to a flat bottom — the standard junior basket geometry. They're chlorine-free, which matters more than most shoppers realize. Bleached white filters often carry a faint chemical residue that leaches into your first cup. Tupkee skips that process entirely.

As shown in the product image, these are classic flat-bottom filters with crimped seams. The paper is medium-weight — not tissue-thin like some budget options, but not stiff cardboard either. It holds its shape when you drop it into the basket.

## Real-World Brewing Performance

I ran these through a Mr. Coffee 5-cup brewer and a Cuisinart 4-cup machine. Extraction was consistent across both. No channeling, no grounds sneaking past the paper into the carafe. The seams held up through the full cycle — a common failure point where cheap filters split and dump sludge into your mug.

One thing that stood out: no paper taste. With unbleached filters, you sometimes get a woody note in the first few brews. These stayed neutral. The 4-6 cup sizing also means the filter sits properly in the basket without folding over the edges — that's what causes grounds to wash into the brew.

The 235 ratings break down heavily positive, and the pattern is clear. One verified buyer noted: *"Finally found filters that fit my Cuisinart 4-cup without folding. No paper taste at all."* Another reviewer with a Mr. Coffee machine echoed that: *"Same fit as the name brand at half the price."*

## Where It Falls Short

This isn't a universal filter. If your machine takes the larger 8-12 cup flat-bottom basket, these won't fit — the smaller diameter means grounds will sit unevenly and water will channel through. Also, the box isn't resealable. If you brew once a day, a 200-count box sits open for months. Store them in a zip bag or airtight container, or they'll absorb kitchen odors.

A few buyers also mentioned the filters are slightly thinner than premium brands like Melitta. That's accurate — they're mid-weight. For most brewers, it's a non-issue. If you use a coarser grind, you might want the extra thickness.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy them if:** You own a 4-6 cup drip machine with a junior basket (Mr. Coffee 5-cup, Cuisinart 4-cup, similar). You want chlorine-free paper without paying boutique prices. You're tired of generic filters that tear mid-brew.

**Skip them if:** You brew in a full-size 12-cup machine — wrong size entirely. Or if you prefer reusable metal filters for environmental reasons.
