**FAQ**

*Does the non-stick coating contain PFOA or PTFE?*
No. The coating is PFOA-free and the pan is marketed as PTFE-free as well. It's a ceramic-based non-stick layer.

*Can I use metal utensils?*
You shouldn't. Even though the coating is durable, wood, silicone, or plastic utensils will extend its lifespan. Metal will eventually scratch it.

*Is it oven-safe?*
Up to 350°F for short periods. It's not built for high-heat roasting or broiling.
