Here’s where Amcrate separates itself from the dollar-store pack. The plastic is noticeably thicker—somewhere in the 10-12 mil range, which is sturdier than the typical 8-mil party cup. ## Where They Fall Short

No product is perfect, and these cups have a few honest downsides.

- **Not fully rigid.** While thicker than average, they’re still plastic. A hard squeeze near the rim can cause a permanent crease. They’re sturdy, not indestructible.
- **No lids or straws included.** If you’re planning for outdoor events with wind or bugs, you’ll need to buy those separately.
- **Color variety is hit-or-miss.** The multi-color packs sometimes arrive with uneven distribution—you might get more blue than red. One buyer mentioned, *“I wish the pack had an even mix, but it was mostly teal and purple.”*

Also, these are not compostable or biodegradable. If eco-friendliness is a hard requirement, look elsewhere. These are traditional petroleum-based plastic.

## Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Are these cups microwave-safe?**
A: No. They’re plastic, and while they’re food-grade, microwaving them can warp the material and potentially leach chemicals. Use them for cold or room-temperature drinks only.

**Q: Can I write on these with a marker?**
A: Yes, but use a permanent marker. Standard dry-erase markers will smear. Sharpies work well and hold up reasonably well against condensation.

**Q: How many come in a pack?**
