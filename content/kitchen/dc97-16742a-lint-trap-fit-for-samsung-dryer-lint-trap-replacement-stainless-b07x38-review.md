## Where It Falls Short

The mesh is a double-edged sword. Because it's finer than OEM, it traps more lint—but that means it clogs faster. If you dry towels, fleece, or pet bedding regularly, you'll be cleaning this filter more often. Some reviewers found they had to brush it after every load, whereas the original could go two or three loads.

Compatibility is the bigger risk. Samsung has used several lint trap designs across their dryer lineup, and this one doesn't fit everything. Check your dryer's model number against the compatibility list before ordering. A handful of negative reviews are from buyers who ordered without checking and got a part that didn't fit their model. That's not a defect—it's a spec you need to verify.
