Let's cut through the noise: you don't need 10 crumpet rings. You need one that works. The RICHSC-PAN set gives you ten, and after testing them for a week straight — crumpets, eggs, pancakes, even mini frittatas — I can tell you exactly what you're getting.

## What's Actually in the Box

Ten stainless steel rings, each measuring 4 inches across and standing 1 inch tall. The non-stick coating is applied inside and out, which matters more than you'd think. The edges are rolled smooth — no sharp burrs that'll slice your fingers when you're flipping eggs. They stack neatly, though there's no storage case included, so you'll need to find a drawer or hang them.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where this set earns its keep. The non-stick surface isn't marketing fluff. I cooked eggs with just a spritz of oil — no sticking, no scraping, no mangled breakfast. The rings release cleanly with a gentle twist, and food slides right out. For crumpets, the perforated bottom isn't there (these are solid rings, not the hole-punched kind), so you'll get a denser texture than traditional crumpets. That's a tradeoff worth knowing before you buy.

The 4-inch diameter is genuinely useful. It fits standard English muffin recipes, holds a large egg perfectly, and makes pancakes that actually fit on a sandwich bun. The 1-inch height limits you though — you won't get those tall, airy crumpets you see in fancy cafes. For eggs and pancakes, that's fine. For authentic crumpets, it's a compromise.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. The 1-inch height is the biggest one. If you're making authentic crumpets with that signature spongy texture and deep holes, these rings won't deliver. You need 1.5-inch or taller rings for that. Also, the handles are riveted on and do get warm near the base — nothing that'll burn you, but you'll want a towel for extended cooking.

There's also no recipe guide or storage solution included. For a set at this price point, a simple tin or box would've been a nice touch.

## FAQ

**Can these go in the dishwasher?**
Yes, but hand-washing extends the non-stick coating. The manufacturer says dishwasher-safe, but I noticed slightly better release performance when I hand-washed.

**Will eggs stick without oil?**
They'll release cleanly with minimal oil, but I wouldn't go zero-oil. A light spritz or butter swipe gives the best results.

**What else can I use these for besides crumpets?**
Eggs, pancakes, mini frittatas, hash brown patties, even cookie molds. The 4-inch size is versatile. I've used mine for single-serve egg bites with great results.
