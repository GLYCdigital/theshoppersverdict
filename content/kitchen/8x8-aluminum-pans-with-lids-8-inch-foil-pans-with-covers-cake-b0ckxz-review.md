**FAQ**

**Can I put these directly in the oven?**
Yes, up to 400°F. Remove the plastic lid first. For recipes requiring higher heat, use a glass or ceramic dish instead.

**Are the lids reusable?**
Yes, if you hand-wash them gently. They're sturdy enough for several uses, though they'll eventually lose their snap after repeated dishwashing.

**Do these leak?**
The pans themselves are solid, but the corner seams can weep if you puncture them with a knife or fork. Use a spatula to cut portions in the pan.
