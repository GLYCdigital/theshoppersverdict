**What You're Actually Getting**

The set includes multiple stackable bins in two sizes (typically large and medium, though check the listing for your specific configuration). They're made from clear, food-grade PET plastic—not the flimsy acrylic you see on cheaper sets. The clear material matters: you can see what's inside without pulling everything out, which is the whole point of fridge organization.

The stacking lip is the key design feature. Each bin has a recessed top rim that lets the next bin lock in place without sliding off. This is what separates these from a pile of generic plastic tubs. The bins are also designed to sit on standard refrigerator wire shelves or glass shelves without wobbling.

**Where They Fall Short**

No product is perfect, and the KEMETHY bins have real tradeoffs:

- **The large bins are big.** If you have a small fridge or narrow shelves, the larger size may be too deep and waste space.
- **PET plastic scratches.** Unlike acrylic or glass, this material shows scuffs over time. If you're obsessive about aesthetics, this will bug you.
- **The stacking lip collects crumbs.** The recessed rim that makes stacking possible also traps debris. You'll need to wipe the rims during cleaning.

A few buyers also mentioned the bins are not fully airtight. If you're storing strong-smelling foods like onions or fish, odors can transfer between stacked bins. Keep that in mind.

**Who Should Buy This**

**Buy it if:** You have a chaotic fridge, you drink canned beverages or bottled condiments regularly, or you're tired of losing items in the back of shelves. This is also a solid pick for meal-preppers who want grab-and-go access to prepped containers.

**FAQ: What Shoppers Ask**

**Q: Will these fit in a standard refrigerator with glass shelves?**
A: Yes. The bins are designed with a flat base that sits securely on both wire and glass shelves. Just measure your shelf depth first—you want at least 12 inches.

**Q: Can I wash these in the dishwasher?**
A: They're listed as dishwasher-safe on the top rack, but hand-washing is gentler on the stacking lip. If you use the dishwasher, skip the heated dry cycle to prevent warping.

**Q: How many cans fit in the large bin?**
A: Roughly 12-16 standard 12-ounce cans per large bin, depending on brand and can diameter. Stack two bins and you've got a 30-can capacity in the same footprint.
