## The $80 Fix That Saves You a $500 Service Call

When your dryer stops tumbling but the drum still turns by hand, it's almost always the drive motor. A repair technician will quote you $300–$500 for a part that costs a fraction of that. The 0EM 279827 Dryer Drive Motor with Pulley is the replacement part that keeps showing up in appliance forums and Amazon carts — and for good reason.

This is the motor that powers the drum rotation and drives the blower wheel on countless Whirlpool, Maytag, and Kenmore dryers. The "upgraded blower shaft length" in the title isn't marketing fluff — it's a genuine engineering fix for the most common failure point on the original part.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the complete motor assembly: the motor itself, the pulley pre-installed on the shaft, and the mounting hardware. As shown in the product image, the upgraded blower shaft is visibly longer than the OEM version — that extra length is what prevents the blower wheel from slipping off and shredding the shaft over time.

The motor body matches OEM dimensions, so it bolts into the same mounting bracket without modification. The electrical connector is the standard spade-terminal setup, which means you'll need basic wiring knowledge, but nothing exotic.

This is where the 0EM 279827 earns its keep. The motor runs quieter than the OEM part on most installations — several buyers note their dryers are noticeably less noisy after the swap. The upgraded shaft addresses the root cause of premature failure, and the motor itself is wound with heavier gauge wire than the original, which translates to better heat tolerance during long drying cycles.

One buyer put it plainly: *"My Whirlpool dryer stopped mid-cycle, error code F01. I replaced the motor with this and it's been running perfect for 6 months now. The longer shaft is the real deal — no more wobble on the blower wheel."*

## Where It Falls Short

The most consistent complaint across the 220 ratings is fitment variability. While the motor is designed as a direct replacement for a wide range of models, some buyers report the included pulley sits slightly proud of the shaft, requiring them to swap in the old pulley. That's a 10-minute inconvenience, not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you start.

There's also the model-matching issue. This is *not* universal — it's built for specific Whirlpool, Maytag, and Kenmore dryer families. If you have an LG, Samsung, or older GE model, this won't fit. Check your dryer's model number against the compatibility list before ordering. A wrong guess means a return and a week without a working dryer.

A: The 279827 motor is designed for the standard Whirlpool/Maytag/Kenmore belt-drive platform, which includes many Duet models. Verify your dryer's model number against the compatibility list in the product description. If your dryer uses a direct-drive motor (no belt), this won't fit.
