## Performance: Where this pin shines (and where it doesn’t)

The rotating handles are the headline feature, and they deliver. When you roll out pie dough, the barrel spins while your hands stay planted. You’re not bearing weight on your palms—you’re just guiding the pin. That’s a genuine ergonomic improvement for anyone who’s dealt with sore wrists or carpal tunnel flare-ups.

The weight is the tradeoff. This pin is **heavy**—around 2.5 pounds. That heft is great for flattening thick pizza dough or puff pastry without much downward pressure. It also means the pin glides smoothly across the counter instead of bouncing. But if you’re rolling out 40 wonton wrappers or delicate French pastry, you might find yourself fighting the weight. One reviewer put it plainly: *“Great for bread and pizza, but I switched to a lighter pin for croissants.”*

The barrel doesn’t have tapered ends, which is actually a plus for even thickness. You get uniform sheets, not the thin-center/thick-edge problem of tapered pins. For pie crusts, that’s a noticeable improvement.
