Most plastic storage bowls lie about being leakproof. The Oggi Prep, Store & Serve set doesn't. With a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, this four-piece set has earned its place in kitchens that prioritize function over flash. Here's the full breakdown before you spend your money.

## What's in the Box

You get four bowls with matching see-through lids. The set includes one 4-cup, two 2-cup, and one 1-cup bowl — a practical range for meal prep, leftovers, and lunch packing. The clear plastic construction means you can spot what's inside without lifting a single lid. ## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The first thing you'll notice is the lid. It's not a flimsy press-on disc — it's a genuine snap-lock design that requires a firm push to seal and a deliberate pull to release. Users consistently report carrying these bowls in bags without leakage. One reviewer noted, "I've shaken vinaigrette in the 2-cup size with the lid on, and not a drop escaped." That's the kind of endorsement that matters.

The BPA-free plastic is see-through enough to read labels through, and the material holds up to repeated washing. That said, this is lightweight plastic, not glass. It won't shatter if dropped, but it also won't feel substantial in your hand. The tradeoff is cabinet space — these stack into a fraction of the footprint that comparable glass containers occupy.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The near-perfect rating isn't accidental. Recurring themes in verified reviews:

- **The seal works.** Multiple reviewers describe tipping bowls upside down with confidence. This is the single most repeated positive.
- **Fridge organization improves.** Clear sides and uniform sizing make leftovers visible and stackable.
- **Versatile sizing.** The 4-cup bowl handles salads and pasta; the 1-cup works for dips, dressings, or single servings.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this set has three honest limitations:

**Not microwave-safe.** This is the biggest inconvenience. You'll need to transfer food to a microwavable dish for reheating, which adds a dish to your sink. If reheating in the same container is non-negotiable for you, look elsewhere.

**Hand-washing recommended.** While some users report success in the top rack of a dishwasher, the manufacturer recommends hand-washing to prevent warping. That's an extra chore for a plastic product.

**Lighter feel.** Compared to glass or heavy-duty plastic, these feel less durable at first touch. They won't crack easily, but the lightweight construction may bother anyone expecting a premium heft.

## FAQ

**Are these lids truly leakproof?**
Yes, when properly snapped down. The seal is a positive lock, not a friction fit. Multiple users confirm no leaks when shaking or transporting liquids. Just make sure the lid clicks fully into place on all four corners.

**Can these go in the freezer?**
Yes. The BPA-free plastic is freezer-safe, making them suitable for batch-cooking and freezing portions. Thaw in the refrigerator before reheating elsewhere.

**What sizes are included?**
The set includes one 4-cup bowl, two 2-cup bowls, and one 1-cup bowl. Lids are included for all four, and they nest for compact storage.
