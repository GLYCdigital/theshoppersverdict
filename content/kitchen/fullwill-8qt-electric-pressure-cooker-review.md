The Fullwill 8QT Electric Pressure Cooker Max brings big capacity and big features at a budget-friendly price. With 12-in-1 functionality and a ceramic non-stick pot, it's designed to replace half a dozen appliances without taking up half your kitchen.

## What Makes It Great

The 8-quart capacity is the headline feature. It's big enough for a whole chicken, a large batch of chili, or — as one reviewer uses it — "ten pounds of chicken breast to make our dog food for the week." At $76.49, that's a lot of cooking power per dollar.

The ceramic non-stick pot is a standout. Unlike standard non-stick coatings, ceramic is PTFE- and PFOA-free, making it a healthier choice. Reviewers consistently praise how easy it is to clean.

Japanese technology is reflected in the build quality. "Best pressure cooker I have experienced. It's quick, easy, and efficient," wrote one five-star reviewer.

## Where It Falls Short

With only 30 reviews at the time of this analysis, the Fullwill Max is still a relatively new product. Long-term reliability data is thin. While early reviews are overwhelmingly positive, there's less data on how it holds up after a year of heavy use.

It also lacks some advanced features like sous vide that competitors offer. And the 8-quart size is great for capacity but takes up significant counter space.

## The Bottom Line

The Fullwill 8QT Electric Pressure Cooker Max delivers exceptional value for anyone who needs big cooking capacity on a budget. The ceramic non-stick pot, 12-in-1 versatility, and 1200W performance make it a strong contender in the budget pressure cooker category.
