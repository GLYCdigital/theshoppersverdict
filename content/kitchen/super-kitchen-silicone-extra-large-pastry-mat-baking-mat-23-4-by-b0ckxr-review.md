If you bake more than once a week, you already know the pain: flour dust everywhere, dough sticking to a warped cutting board, and measurements you have to guess at. The Super Kitchen Silicone Extra Large Pastry Mat solves the mess part, but does it hold up where it counts? I tested it for a month of pie dough, bread rolls, and sugar cookies. Here’s the honest breakdown.

**What you actually get**  
This is a 23.6 x 15.7-inch mat — that’s the rolling surface, not the total with borders. It’s genuinely large enough for a full 9-inch pie crust or a 12-inch pizza base without the dough crawling off the edge. The silicone is food-grade and FDA-compliant, and it arrived flat with zero chemical odor. Also included: a set of suction cups (two corner pieces) and a small hanging loop. No rolling pin, no scraper — just the mat, which is fine at this price point.

**Performance and build quality**  
The surface has a slightly textured matte finish. Dough releases without sticking — I tested it with a high-hydration brioche (75% water) and had zero tears. The non-slip backing grips a granite countertop and a butcher block equally well. I did notice the suction cups help a lot on smooth surfaces; without them, the corners can lift on a vigorous fold-and-turn.  

Heat resistance is rated to 480°F — I used it as a trivet for a cast-iron skillet straight from the oven. No warping, no melted edges. The measurement markings are printed *under* the silicone layer, so they’re not going to scratch off. After 10 dishwasher cycles, they still read crisp.

**What buyers consistently praise**  
At 4.7 stars from 235 ratings, the pattern is clear. One verified buyer wrote: *“I’ve bought three cheaper mats that all curled at the edges within a month. This one stays flat and the size is actually accurate — my previous ‘large’ mat was a full three inches shorter.”* Another noted: *“Finally a mat that doesn’t smell like a tire factory. It’s thin but perfect for cookies and fondant work.”* Multiple reviewers mention the size as the deciding factor — it fits under a standard upper cabinet and rolls up tight.

**Where it falls short**  
Be honest with yourself about how you bake. This mat is thin — 0.6mm — and while that aids flexibility, it means you can feel the counter seam underneath if you’re rolling on a tiled or uneven surface. It’s also not ideal for heavy bread kneading; the silicone flexes under vigorous pressure, which can tire your wrists. One reviewer griped: *“The suction cups are fiddly to attach and pop off if you’re not careful.”* True — they take a firm press. And if you’re left-handed, note the printed markings favor right-to-left orientation, though it’s a minor annoyance.

**Who should buy this**  
- **Home bakers** who want a dedicated rolling surface that doesn’t take up drawer space.  
- **Cookie and pastry makers** who need accurate measurements and easy cleanup.  
- **Apartment dwellers** with limited counter space — the mat rolls into a cylinder the size of a rolled-up poster.  

**Skip it if** you’re a professional baker working with stiff doughs all day, or if you have a rough stone countertop — you’ll want a thicker, cushioned mat (look for 1.5mm+).  

**FAQ**  
**Q: Is it really dishwasher safe?**  
Yes, top rack or bottom. I’ve run it 10+ times with no warping or peeling. Let it air dry flat afterward to avoid creases.  

**Q: Will it scratch my countertop?**  
No — the silicone is soft. But clean the underside with a damp cloth occasionally; trapped flour can leave a residue on porous stone.  

**Q: Can I cut directly on it?**  
Technically yes, but the surface will scar. Use it for rolling, not chopping.  

**The Verdict**  
**Buy it if** you want a genuinely large, accurate, non-stick surface for under $20. **Skip it if** you need a thick mat for heavy-duty kneading.  

**Rating: 4.6/5** — it’s the best value in its size class, with minor tradeoffs on thickness that most home bakers won’t notice.
