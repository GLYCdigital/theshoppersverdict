**What you're actually getting**

**FAQ**

*Will these work with my FoodSaver?*
Yes, as long as you own a standard FoodSaver machine (not the handheld models). They're cut to size and have the textured channel that the machine's vacuum sensor needs. If you're using a generic vacuum sealer, check that it accepts pre-cut bags rather than rolls only.

*Can I use them for liquids?*
Not directly. Freeze liquid items first, then vacuum-seal. The 3-mil material is sturdy enough for frozen liquids, but attempting to vacuum-seal thawed liquids will make a mess and can damage your machine.

*How long do they last in the freezer?*
Expect 12-18 months of quality preservation, assuming you get a proper seal and the bag isn't punctured. That matches the performance of name-brand bags at a fraction of the cost.
