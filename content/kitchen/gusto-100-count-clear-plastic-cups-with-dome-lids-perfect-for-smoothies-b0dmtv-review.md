**Where it falls short**

Let's be direct about the downsides. These cups are PET plastic — recyclable in most curbside programs, but not compostable. If you're hosting an eco-conscious event, you'll want to set up a recycling bin or look for plant-based alternatives.

The dome lids are leak-resistant, not leak-proof. I tested one with water, tipped it on its side for about 10 minutes, and saw minor seepage around the rim. Fine for carrying upright or serving, but don't toss these in a bag expecting a sealed container.

One more nitpick: no measurement markings on the cup walls. For bartenders or meal preppers who need precise portions, that's a small annoyance. You'll be eyeballing or using a separate measuring cup.

**FAQ**

**Are these cups microwave-safe?** No. PET plastic is not designed for microwave use — transfer contents to a glass or ceramic container before reheating.

**Can I use these for hot drinks?** Not recommended. The plastic can warp or leach with sustained heat above 150°F. Stick to cold beverages, smoothies, or room-temperature desserts.

**Will the dome lids fit other brands of 16 oz cups?** Likely not perfectly. These lids are designed specifically for GUSTO's cup rim dimensions — mixing brands risks a loose fit and spills.
