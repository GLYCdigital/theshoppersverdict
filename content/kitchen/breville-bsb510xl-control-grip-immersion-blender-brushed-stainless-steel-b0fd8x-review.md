Let’s cut through the marketing noise: the Breville BSB510XL Control Grip Immersion Blender is a serious tool for home cooks who blend more than once a week. With 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, it’s clearly doing something right. But at this price point, you deserve to know exactly what you’re getting — and what you’re not.

## What’s Actually in the Box

The BSB510XL ships with the motor unit, a 12-inch detachable stainless steel shaft, and the instruction booklet. That’s it. No whisk attachment, no chopper bowl, no measuring cup. Breville sells those separately in the accessory kit, so factor that into your budget if you want the full package. The brushed stainless steel finish looks premium and resists fingerprints better than glossy alternatives.

## Real-World Performance

The 280-watt motor might sound modest on paper, but Breville’s variable speed dial gives you precise control from a gentle pulse to full power. During testing, the blade assembly handled a batch of roasted butternut squash soup in about 45 seconds — no chunks, no hot liquid splatter. The bell housing design creates a vortex that pulls ingredients down toward the blades, which means less stopping to stir and scrape.

One detail that stands out: the Control Grip handle. It’s angled to keep your wrist in a neutral position, and the soft-touch surface actually does reduce fatigue. If you’ve ever made a double batch of mayonnaise or a big pot of pureed vegetable soup, you know how much your hand can cramp with cheaper models. This one stays comfortable through longer sessions.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The consensus among verified purchasers centers on reliability and smooth results. One reviewer noted, “I’ve owned this for two years and use it weekly for soups and sauces. It still blends like day one.” Another highlighted the cleanup: “The shaft slides off and goes straight in the dishwasher. No crevices where food hides.” Durability appears to be a genuine strength — multiple buyers report owning theirs for several years without motor issues, which is notable for an immersion blender category known for short lifespans.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest frustration is the accessory situation. At this price, competitors like KitchenAid and Cuisinart include whisks and chopper bowls in the box. Breville’s decision to sell them separately feels like nickel-and-diming, especially when the whisk attachment alone costs nearly $30.

The bell housing is also wider than average — roughly 2.5 inches across. That’s fine for pots and large bowls, but it struggles in narrow measuring cups or tall protein shaker bottles. You’ll need to work in smaller batches if you’re blending directly in a quart-sized container. And the 4-foot cord will have you hunting for the nearest outlet or investing in an extension cord for larger kitchens.

## Who Should Buy This

This blender is ideal for the home cook who makes soups, sauces, and smoothies regularly and wants a tool that will last years without breaking down. It’s also a strong choice if you have hand or wrist issues — the ergonomics are genuinely better than anything else at this price. Skip it if you want an all-in-one kit with whisk and chopper attachments, or if you frequently blend small quantities in narrow containers.

## FAQ

**Is the Breville BSB510XL dishwasher safe?**
The stainless steel shaft is fully dishwasher-safe. The motor unit must be wiped clean with a damp cloth — never submerge it.

**Can it crush ice or blend frozen fruit?**
It handles frozen fruit in smoothies reasonably well, but it’s not an ice crusher. For thick frozen mixtures, use the pulse function and add liquid to help the blades move.

**Does it work for making mayonnaise or emulsions?**
Yes, and this is where the variable speed dial shines. Start low and slowly increase — you’ll get a stable emulsion without breaking the mixture.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a durable, comfortable immersion blender for regular soup and sauce making, and you don’t mind buying accessories separately.

**Skip it if** you want an all-in-one kit with whisk and chopper attachments included, or you frequently blend in narrow containers.
