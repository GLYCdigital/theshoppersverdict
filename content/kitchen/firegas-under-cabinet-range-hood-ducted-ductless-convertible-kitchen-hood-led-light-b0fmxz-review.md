**Is the FIREGAS Under Cabinet Range Hood worth your money?** At 4.3 stars from 215 Amazon buyers, this budget-friendly hood has clearly found an audience. But the 4.2 verdict score tells a slightly more nuanced story. Let's dig into what you're actually getting.

## What's in the Box

The FIREGAS arrives with the hood unit, a 6-inch round damper for ducted installation, two charcoal filters (critical for ductless mode), a hardware mounting kit, and a clearly written manual. No surprises, no missing pieces — which is more than I can say for some hoods in this price bracket. The unit itself measures roughly 30 inches wide, sized for standard cooktops, with a stainless steel body that matches most appliance suites.

## Real-World Performance

Here's the thing about range hoods: they're simple machines, but execution matters. The FIREGAS delivers a respectable 400 CFM airflow that handles everyday cooking — sautéing, boiling pasta, searing chicken — without fogging up your kitchen. The three-speed push-button controls are refreshingly old-school. No touchscreens that glitch, no Wi-Fi that disconnects. Press a button, get airflow. That's it.

The convertible feature is the real selling point. If you have existing ductwork, attach the included damper and vent outside. If you're in an apartment or older home without venting, switch to recirculating mode with the charcoal filters. That flexibility genuinely expands who can use this hood. One buyer noted, "I was worried about installation since my kitchen had no duct — the ductless option worked perfectly, and the filters actually kill the grease smell."

The LED light is a pleasant surprise. Many budget hoods use dim, yellow bulbs that make your food look unappetizing. The FIREGAS's 2W LED casts a clean, bright white light that genuinely helps you see browning and color changes while cooking.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 215 ratings, a few themes emerge. Installation ease tops the list — multiple reviewers mention completing it solo in under an hour with basic tools. The quiet operation at low and medium speeds also gets consistent shout-outs. One reviewer summarized: "The low setting is whisper-quiet. I run it while simmering and forget it's on. At high, it's loud, but it's also powerful enough to clear smoke fast."

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The stainless steel finish is fingerprint magnet — expect to wipe it down regularly or invest in a stainless cleaner. The high-speed fan produces a noticeable whoosh that makes conversation awkward if you're standing nearby. In ductless mode, the charcoal filters need replacing every 3-6 months depending on cooking frequency, adding an ongoing cost many shoppers overlook.

A few reviewers also mentioned that the included hardware feels a bit flimsy, though it's sufficient for standard under-cabinet mounting. If you have a particularly heavy-duty installation, consider upgrading the screws.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy the FIREGAS if:** You're a renter or homeowner without ductwork who wants real ventilation without major renovation. You're a home cook who needs solid performance without paying premium prices. You value simple, reliable controls over flashy features.

**Skip it if:** You do heavy wok cooking or frequent deep-frying that demands serious extraction power. You're a stickler for fingerprint-free finishes. You need whisper-quiet operation at maximum power.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this really be installed both ducted and ductless?**
A: Yes. The unit ships with a damper for ducted installation and charcoal filters for recirculating mode. You choose during installation — no parts to buy separately.

**Q: Is it loud?**
A: Low and medium speeds are genuinely quiet. High speed is loud but effective — think "powerful bathroom fan" rather than "jet engine." It's a fair tradeoff for the airflow.

**Q: How often do charcoal filters need replacing?**
A: With regular cooking (3-5 meals weekly), plan on every 3-6 months. Replacement filters are inexpensive and widely available on Amazon.
