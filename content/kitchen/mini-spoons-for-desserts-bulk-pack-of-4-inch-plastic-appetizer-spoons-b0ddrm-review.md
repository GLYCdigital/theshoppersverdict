Here's where these spoons separate themselves from the dollar-store variety. ## Where They Fall Short

The tradeoff for that durability is a shallower bowl than you might expect. If you're serving thick, dense desserts like mousse or peanut butter pie, you'll need a scooping motion rather than a simple dip. One reviewer noted: "Great for yogurt and ice cream, but I had to scrape twice for thick pudding." Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

The bigger limitation: these are hand-wash only. For a disposable spoon, that's an odd constraint. If you're buying these with the intention of washing and reusing for a family party, you'll be disappointed. They're really designed for single-use events where the durability prevents mid-event failures, not for building a reusable utensil collection.
