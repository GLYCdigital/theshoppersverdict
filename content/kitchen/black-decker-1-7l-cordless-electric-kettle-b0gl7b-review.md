Let me be direct about the BLACK+DECKER 1.7L Cordless Electric Kettle: it's not trying to be a $150 temperature-precise gooseneck. It's a workhorse that boils water fast and gets out of your way. And for the vast majority of households, that's exactly what you need.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the 1.7L stainless steel kettle, the 360° cordless power base, and a short manual. No frills, no extra filters or accessories. The kettle itself has a concealed heating element — that's important because it means no limescale buildup on an exposed coil, and it's dramatically easier to clean. The water window on the side is genuinely useful; you can see exactly how much water you're boiling without opening the lid.

**Real-world performance**

Filled to the 1.7L max line with tap water, this thing boils in about four and a half minutes. That's not a claim from the marketing sheet — that's consistent with what buyers report. If you're making tea for a family or filling a French press, that speed matters. The 1500W element is concealed under the stainless floor, which also cuts down on the metallic taste some cheaper kettles impart.

The cordless base is the standout feature. The kettle lifts off freely and sits back down at any angle — no fumbling to align a connector. It pours cleanly, though here's the honest tradeoff: the lid doesn't lock. If you tilt it aggressively for the last few drops, you'll need a finger on the lid. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Looking through the 229 ratings (4.6 stars), the patterns are clear. One buyer wrote, "Finally a kettle that boils fast and doesn't sound like a jet engine taking off." That's a recurring theme — it's noticeably quieter than many budget rivals. Others emphasize reliability: "Had mine for 8 months, used daily. Still boils as fast as day one. No rust, no scale issues."

The auto shut-off gets consistent appreciation. The kettle cuts power the moment it hits a rolling boil, and the boil-dry protection shuts it down if you somehow run it empty. For anyone who's ever melted a stovetop kettle, that's genuine reassurance.

**Where it falls short**

The lack of temperature presets is the biggest limitation. If you're a serious green tea drinker who needs 175°F, or a pour-over enthusiast chasing 195°F, this kettle isn't for you. It boils to 212°F, and that's it. You'll be waiting for the water to cool, which defeats the purpose for precision brewing.

The exterior gets hot — not scalding, but noticeably warm to the touch. The handle stays cool, so it's safe in practice, but if you have curious toddlers around, treat it like any other hot appliance. And as mentioned, the non-locking lid is a small design quirk that shows up in steep-angle pours.

**Who should buy this**

Get this kettle if you're a tea-and-coffee household that wants fast boiling without paying for smart features you'll never use. It's ideal for families, dorm rooms, offices, or anyone replacing a stovetop whistler. Skip it if you need precision temperature control for specialty coffee or delicate teas — in that case, look at a variable-temp model like the Fellow Stagg or a budget gooseneck.

**FAQ**

**Is the kettle body 100% stainless steel?**
The exterior is stainless steel, but the lid and some internal components are food-grade plastic. It doesn't affect water taste, and the plastic parts don't contact the water during boiling.

**Does it have a removable filter?**
No. The spout is a simple stainless design without a mesh filter. If you have hard water, you'll want to descale monthly with vinegar or citric acid. The concealed element makes this easier than exposed-coil models.

**How long does the warranty last?**
BLACK+DECKER backs it with a 2-year limited warranty. In practice, multiple buyers report replacements being shipped quickly when issues arose — though most report years of trouble-free use.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a fast, reliable, no-nonsense kettle that's quiet, safe, and easy to clean. **Skip it if** you need temperature control or insist on an all-metal interior.

At 4.6/5 across 229 ratings, this is one of the most dependable budget kettles on Amazon. It's not flashy, but it does the one job it's designed for exceptionally well.
