**The $50 toaster oven that actually toasts evenly — and does a few other tricks too**

Most budget toaster ovens share a dirty secret: they promise "4-slice capacity" but burn the two slices closest to the heating elements while leaving the middle pair pale and doughy. The BLACK+DECKER TO1745SSG takes a different approach with its Even Toast system, and after looking at what 225 buyers have to say, this compact unit might be the best value under $60 in the category.

**What you actually get**

The TO1745SSG ships with the toaster oven itself, a wire rack, a bake pan, and a removable crumb tray. No surprises, no useless accessories. The stainless steel body resists fingerprints better than most budget units, and the front glass door gives you a clear view of your food. Physical dials control the timer, temperature (up to 450°F), and toast shade — there's no digital display, which keeps things simple and repairable.

**How it performs in real use**

The headline feature is the Even Toast system, which uses a specially designed heating element layout to distribute heat more uniformly across the entire cooking surface. Multiple buyers confirmed this works as advertised. One reviewer noted, "I've had toaster ovens that cost twice as much that couldn't produce consistent toast — this one nails it every time." Another mentioned they've "used it daily for 8 months and toast comes out the same shade every single time."

Beyond toasting, you get four functions: toast, bake, broil, and keep warm. The bake function handles small casseroles, frozen pizzas (up to 9 inches), and reheating leftovers without making them soggy. Broiling gets hot enough to melt cheese and crisp the tops of dishes, though I'd recommend keeping the door slightly ajar for better browning. The 30-minute timer with automatic shut-off provides a basic safety net, though it does limit longer slow-cooking tasks.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The word "even" appears repeatedly in customer feedback. Beyond the toasting performance, users appreciate the compact design — it fits under most kitchen cabinets with room to spare. Multiple reviews mention this is "perfect for a small kitchen" or "great for a dorm room or office." The simple dial controls also earn consistent praise from older users who find digital touchscreens fiddly. As one reviewer put it, "I don't need WiFi in my toaster. I need it to make toast. This does that."

**Where it falls short**

The lack of an interior light is the biggest annoyance. You'll have to crack the door open to check browning progress, which lets heat escape and can extend cooking times. The crumb tray is also thinner than I'd like — let it cool completely before cleaning, or you risk warping it. And while the 450°F max temperature is fine for most tasks, it's not enough for properly roasting vegetables or baking artisan bread. One buyer noted the exterior gets "noticeably warm to the touch" during longer baking sessions, so keep it away from flammable materials.

**Who should buy this**

This toaster oven is ideal for singles, couples, or small families who want reliable toast and basic baking without spending $150+ on a convection model. It's also a strong choice for dorm rooms, small apartments, offices, or anyone with limited counter space. If you're replacing a dedicated toaster and want an oven that can handle frozen pizza, reheating leftovers, and broiling fish, this fits the bill. Skip it if you need convection baking, air frying, or precise temperature control for serious baking projects.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the Even Toast feature actually make a difference?**
A: Yes. The heating elements are positioned to distribute heat more evenly across all four slots. Buyers consistently report uniform browning across all slices, which is genuinely rare at this price point.

**Q: Can I fit a full-size frozen pizza inside?**
A: No. The interior accommodates pizzas up to about 9 inches in diameter. Larger pies need to be cut or baked elsewhere.

**Q: How loud is the timer when it goes off?**
A: The bell-style timer is audible but not obnoxious. You'll hear it from another room, but it won't startle you or wake a sleeping baby.

**The Verdict**

**Skip it if:** You require convection cooking, a window light, or precise temperature control for serious baking.
