The straight-sided design is a genuine advantage for canning. You can pack the jars to the absolute rim without spillage, and cleaning out the last bit of jam is far easier than with curved jars. **FAQ**

**Q: Can I reuse the lids for refrigerator pickles?**
Yes. If you’re not processing in a hot water bath — just storing in the fridge — the lids can be reused multiple times. The seal compound only degrades with heat processing.

**Q: Are these jars freezer-safe?**
Yes, but leave at least ½ inch of headspace for expansion. The thick glass handles freezing fine, though I wouldn’t fill them to the absolute rim.

**Q: Do they fit standard canning accessories?**
Regular-mouth lids and bands fit perfectly, and the jars work with standard canning racks, tongs, and funnel sets.
