Also, don't expect these to replace your regular kitchen spatulas. At 6.7 inches, they're designed for detail work—not flipping eggs or folding batter. Buy them for their intended purpose (scraping small containers) and you'll be happy. Buy them expecting full-size kitchen performance and you'll be disappointed.

**Can I put them in the dishwasher?**
Not recommended. The plastic handles can warp or degrade over time. Hand wash with soap and water—a quick rinse is all they need.

**Are they truly food-grade silicone?**
Yes, the heads are food-grade and BPA-free. Multiple buyers report using them for both cosmetics and cooking without issue.
