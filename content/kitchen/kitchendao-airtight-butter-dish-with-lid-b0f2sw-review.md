Butter picks up the smell of last night's salmon, goes rancid faster than it should, and turns into a rock-hard brick that shreds your toast. No butter knife included. Some competing dishes at this price point throw one in. KITCHENDAO doesn't. Not a dealbreaker, but it's a missing nicety.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the seal keep butter at room temperature from melting?**
No. The airtight seal prevents odor and moisture exchange, but it doesn't insulate. On a warm counter, butter will soften and eventually melt just like any other dish.

**Q: Will it fit a European-style butter block?**
European butters (like Kerrygold) come in 8-ounce blocks that are slightly taller than US sticks. The KITCHENDAO base is deep enough to accommodate them, but the lid sits closer to the butter. If your block is particularly tall, it may touch the glass. Test before committing.

**Q: Can the gasket be replaced if it wears out?**
Yes, the gasket is removable and KITCHENDAO sells replacements separately. I'd expect 12-18 months of daily use before needing a swap, depending on how often you run it through the dishwasher.
