Let’s be honest: most spoon rests are afterthoughts. You buy a $8 piece of ceramic, it slides around, collects grease, and the pot lid ends up dripping condensation all over your stovetop anyway. The OXO Good Grips Stainless Steel Spoon Rest with Lid Holder actually tries to solve that second problem — and it mostly succeeds.

**What you’re actually getting**

This is a single-piece stainless steel rest, roughly 9.5 inches long and 4 inches wide. The front has the standard spoon cradle with a slight upward lip to keep utensils from sliding off. The rear features a raised, slotted channel designed to hold a pot lid vertically — the edge of the lid rests in the groove, keeping it off the counter. The base has a silicone strip underneath, which does two things: stops the rest from scratching your countertop and keeps it from sliding around when you set a heavy spoon down.

The stainless is brushed, not polished, so it won’t show every fingerprint. It’s also dishwasher-safe, which matters because hand-washing a greasy spoon rest is a chore nobody wants.

**How it performs in real kitchens**

The lid holder is the headline feature, and it works better than I expected. I tested it with a 10-inch sauté pan lid and a 4-quart stockpot lid. Both seated securely in the groove without wobbling. The condensation drips down into the rest’s basin rather than running onto the counter — that’s the design win here. If you’re making pasta sauce or braising, this genuinely keeps your stovetop cleaner.

The spoon cradle itself is wide enough for a standard wooden spoon or silicone spatula, but it’s not oversized. A ladle with a thick handle might not sit flush. The silicone base strip is a nice touch — on my granite countertop, the rest stayed put even when I dropped a heavy metal spoon into it.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.7-star rating (235 ratings) isn’t inflated. Reviewers repeatedly mention the stability and the lid-holding functionality. One top reviewer wrote: *“Finally a spoon rest that actually holds my pot lid without it falling over. The silicone bottom keeps it from sliding. I bought a second one for my mother.”* Another noted: *“I was skeptical about the lid holder but it works exactly as shown in the product image. No more puddle of water on my stove.”* The dishwasher-safe claim also gets confirmed frequently — multiple buyers mention it comes out clean with no water spots.

**Where it falls short**

Honestly, this is a well-executed product, but it’s not perfect. First, the lid holder only accommodates lids up to about 11 inches in diameter. Larger stockpot lids will wobble. Second, the spoon cradle is shallow — if you set a heavily loaded spoon down at an angle, it can tip. Third, the stainless steel surface will develop minor scratches over time, especially if you use metal utensils. It’s cosmetic, not functional, but worth knowing if you’re particular about your kitchen gear looking pristine.

One more thing: the price is higher than a basic ceramic rest. You’re paying for the dual functionality and the silicone base. If you only need a place to park a spoon, spend less. If you regularly cook with lids, this justifies the premium.

**Who should buy this**

- **Home cooks who make sauces, soups, or braises** — the lid holder is a genuine quality-of-life upgrade.
- **People with limited counter space** — this replaces two items (spoon rest + lid stand) with one.
- **Anyone who hates wiping up condensation puddles** — that’s the real value proposition here.

**Skip it if:** you only cook simple meals, use lids rarely, or you prefer a ceramic rest to match your kitchen aesthetic. The stainless look is modern but won’t suit every decor.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this hold a heavy cast-iron lid?**
A: It can, but the groove is designed for standard stainless or glass lids. A heavy cast-iron lid may be too thick to seat properly. Stick to lighter lids.

**Q: Does it scratch the countertop?**
A: No. The silicone strip on the bottom prevents scratching and sliding. That’s one of the best features.

**Q: Can it go in the dishwasher?**
A: Yes, the entire piece is dishwasher-safe. Buyers confirm it comes out clean without rusting or warping.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** you cook with lids regularly and want to eliminate stovetop condensation mess. The lid holder works as advertised, the silicone base keeps it stable, and it’s built to last.

**Skip it if:** you need a rest for large lids (over 11 inches) or you prefer a ceramic look. Also skip if you rarely use pot lids — the premium price isn’t justified for spoon duty alone.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Docked slightly for the shallow spoon cradle and the lid-size limitation. For most home cooks, this is the best spoon rest design on the market right now.
