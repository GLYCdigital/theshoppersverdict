## Where It Falls Short

Honest downsides? First, there's no thermostat built into the kettle itself. Your machine's existing heat switch controls temperature, so if your machine's thermostat is drifting, this won't fix that. You might still get uneven popping if the root cause is the machine, not the kettle.

Second, the crank handle is functional but not beefy. It's adequate for normal use, but if you're popping multiple batches back-to-back and cranking aggressively, you'll feel some flex in the shaft. It won't break under normal use, but it's not industrial-grade.

Third, and this is the big one: **you must verify your machine's kettle size before ordering.** This is specifically for 8-ounce models. If you have a 6-ounce or 12-ounce machine, this won't fit. The mounting bolt pattern is different. Measure your existing kettle's diameter and check your model number before you commit.
