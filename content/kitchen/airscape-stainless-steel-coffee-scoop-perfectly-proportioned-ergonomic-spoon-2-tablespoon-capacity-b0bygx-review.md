**What you actually get**

The Airscape scoop is a solid, one-piece stainless steel construction — no seams, no welds, no plastic anywhere. It holds exactly 2 tablespoons when level, which is the standard Specialty Coffee Association recommendation for a 6-ounce cup. The scoop end is deep enough to plunge into a full bag of beans without spilling, and the handle is roughly 5 inches long, giving you clearance to reach the bottom of standard coffee bags.

The satin finish is worth calling out. It doesn't show fingerprints like polished stainless, and coffee oils wipe off with a dry cloth. The edge is flat and sharp enough to level off a scoop cleanly against the bag's rim — no tapping, no shaking, no guessing.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this scoop really 2 tablespoons, or is that the "heaping" measurement?**
A: Level measurement. The edge is flat and designed to be scraped clean against the bag. A heaping scoop will hold closer to 3 tablespoons, so level it for consistency.

**Q: Can it go in the dishwasher?**
A: Yes. It's solid stainless with no coating or plastic parts. Multiple long-term owners report daily dishwasher cycles with no rust or degradation.

**Q: Does it work for ground coffee or just whole beans?**
A: Both. Ground coffee scoops the same way, though you may get slightly more grams per scoop with fine grinds due to settling. The volume measurement stays accurate.
