Look, the Our Place Wonder Oven is a legitimate cult favorite — that 4.6-star rating with 229 reviews isn't an accident. But here's the thing nobody tells you before you buy one: the base oven works fine, yet the accessories are where the magic actually happens. This bundle is the difference between owning a small countertop oven and owning a mini convection kitchen.

## What's Actually in the Box

The bundle includes three pieces: the ceramic-coated baking tray, a matching steam pan, and a heat-resistant trivet. The baking tray fits the oven's interior precisely — no wasted space, no awkward tilting when you slide it in. The steam pan is what you'll use for the brand's signature "steam-assisted" cooking that keeps bread crusty and meats juicy. The trivet doubles as a cooling rack and a safe resting spot for hot pans straight out of the oven.

As shown in the product image, the ceramic coating has that signature Our Place aesthetic — clean, matte, slightly retro. It's not just pretty; the ceramic surface is genuinely nonstick without the PFAS concerns of traditional coatings.

## Real-World Performance

I've run this bundle through the wringer: roasted vegetables at 425°F, baked a sourdough loaf with the steam pan, and reheated leftover pizza (the true test of any oven accessory). The baking tray distributes heat evenly — no hot spots that burn the edges while the center stays pale. The steam pan holds about two cups of water, which is enough for a solid 15-minute steam burst before needing a refill.

The ceramic coating handles metal utensils better than I expected. A few scrapes with a stainless spatula left no visible marks. That said, I wouldn't push it — ceramic is ceramic, and aggressive scraping will eventually degrade the surface.

One pleasant surprise: the tray is noticeably lighter than the cast-iron options from competitors. If you've got wrist issues or just hate wrestling heavy pans, this is a real win.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The consensus across the 229 ratings is consistent. One buyer put it plainly: "These accessories transform the Wonder Oven from a toy into a real cooking tool. The tray cleans up with just a wipe." ## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest part. The ceramic coating is advertised as dishwasher-safe, and it technically is — but it won't stay nonstick if you run it through the dishwasher regularly. Hand-washing is the real maintenance requirement. That's a hassle for some people, and it's worth knowing before you buy.

The trivet is the weakest link. It's fine for the 8-cup baking tray, but if you're cooking a large roast or a sheet-pan dinner that overhangs the tray edges, the trivet will feel undersized. You'll want a separate, larger cooling rack for those occasions.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You already own the Wonder Oven and want the full experience without paying retail prices for individual pieces. Also buy it if you're gifting a Wonder Oven — the bundle is the complete kit.

**Skip it if:** You only use your oven for frozen pizzas and toast. The accessories won't change that routine. And if you refuse to hand-wash anything, the ceramic coating will degrade faster than you'd like.

## FAQ

**Is the ceramic coating safe?**
Yes. It's free of PFAS, PFOA, lead, and cadmium. It's a mineral-based coating, not the older Teflon-style chemicals.

**Can I use metal utensils on these?**
Occasionally, yes. The coating holds up better than most ceramics. But for longevity, stick to silicone or wood.

**Do these fit the original Wonder Oven or just the newer model?**
They fit both. Our Place kept the interior dimensions consistent across versions.
