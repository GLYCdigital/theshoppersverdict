## Hamilton Beach Electric Stand Mixer Review

The **Hamilton Beach Electric Stand Mixer** is the most popular budget-friendly stand mixer on Amazon. With over **4,400 ratings and a 4.4-star average**, it's the go-to choice for bakers who want stand mixer functionality without KitchenAid pricing.

### Design and Build

The Hamilton Beach stand mixer has a classic tilt-head design with a 4-quart stainless steel bowl. Available colors include **rose**, black, silver, and white.

**Key specs:**
- **Motor:** 300 watts
- **Bowl:** 4-quart stainless steel
- **Speeds:** 7 + slow start
- **Weight:** 12 lbs
- **Attachments included:** Dough hook, flat beater, whisk, splash guard

The **rose color** option is particularly popular — a soft pink that adds a decorative touch to countertops. The build quality is solid for the price, with a die-cast metal base and chrome-plated attachments.

| Task | Performance |

### FAQ

#### Can the Hamilton Beach stand mixer be used for large batches?
The 4-quart bowl handles standard recipes well — typically 3-4 dozen cookies, a single loaf of bread, or a standard cake recipe. For larger batches, you'll need to work in multiple batches.

#### Is the mixer loud during operation?
It's comparable to other stand mixers in its class — noticeable but not disruptive. The noise level is around the same as a hand mixer or blender. The slow-start feature helps reduce the initial noise spike.

#### How does the Hamilton Beach compare to the Cuisinart stand mixer?
At similar price points ($120-$150), both offer comparable performance. The Hamilton Beach has a larger user review base (4,400+ vs. Cuisinart's smaller pool), which gives more confidence in the long-term data. The Cuisinart may have slight edge on build quality, but both are excellent value options.

#### Does the Hamilton Beach stand mixer come with a warranty?
Yes, it includes a 1-year limited warranty from Hamilton Beach. Extended warranties are available through Amazon and other retailers.
