## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. The 9-cup capacity is measured in dry ingredients. If you're making soup or a large batch of batter, you'll fill it only about two-thirds before liquid sloshes up the center tube. For a family of four, that's fine. For batch cooking or big holiday prep, you'll work in smaller rounds.

The feed tube is narrower than I'd like. A full Roma tomato fits, but a beefsteak or a large onion requires a chop first. It's a minor friction point, but if you're processing whole vegetables frequently, it adds time.

And there's no blade storage built into the base or lid. The extra discs and dough blade need a drawer or a small caddy. It's a common omission at this price, but worth knowing before you buy.
