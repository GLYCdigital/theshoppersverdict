Let's be honest about extended warranties: most are overpriced insurance policies designed to profit from your anxiety. The ASURION 3 Year Kitchen Protection Plan for appliances in the $900-$999.99 range is different — and that's why it holds a 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews.

## What You're Actually Buying

This plan covers your kitchen appliance for three years from your date of purchase, layering on top of the manufacturer's warranty rather than replacing it. The coverage window matters: most built-in and freestanding kitchen appliances (think refrigerators, ranges, built-in microwaves, and dishwashers in that $900-$1,000 sweet spot) come with a one-year manufacturer warranty. The ASURION plan extends protection for two additional years — exactly when mechanical failures tend to surface.

The plan triggers after the original warranty expires and covers mechanical and electrical failures, including parts and labor. You're not paying for a repair person to come out — ASURION coordinates everything through their network. If the unit can't be repaired, you receive a replacement or an Amazon gift card for the purchase price.

## How Claims Actually Work

The process is refreshingly simple compared to traditional warranty companies. File a claim through your Amazon account, schedule a service visit, and the technician handles the rest. One reviewer noted: "My refrigerator compressor died at 14 months. ASURION had a tech out in three days, and when the part was backordered, they replaced the whole unit. No hassle, no runaround."

Another consistent theme in positive reviews: the 24/7 technical support line. When your $1,000 range stops heating the day before Thanksgiving, having a real person answer at 9 PM matters.

## Where It Falls Short

The exclusions are where extended warranties hide their teeth, and ASURION is no exception. Accidental damage, power surges, and cosmetic issues (dents, scratches, rust) aren't covered. If you live in an area with unreliable power, that surge exclusion is significant — a $30 surge protector would've been a smarter investment.

The Amazon gift card reimbursement is another honest tradeoff. If ASURION can't fix your appliance and opts to replace it, you're getting store credit, not cash. If you bought the appliance on Amazon anyway, this is likely fine. If you were planning to switch retailers for the replacement, it's a limitation worth noting.

There's also a 30-day registration window from purchase date. Miss it, and you're out of luck — and out of the plan's cost.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:**
- You're purchasing a major kitchen appliance with complex electronics (smart refrigerators, induction ranges, built-in convection ovens) — these have more failure points than basic models
- You're not handy and would pay $150-$300 per service call anyway
- You plan to keep the appliance beyond the manufacturer's warranty period

**Skip it if:**
- You're buying a simple, mechanical appliance (basic dishwasher, standard microwave) with a proven reliability record
- You have a home warranty that already covers appliance breakdowns
- Your credit card doubles the manufacturer's warranty (many premium cards do — check before paying for this)
