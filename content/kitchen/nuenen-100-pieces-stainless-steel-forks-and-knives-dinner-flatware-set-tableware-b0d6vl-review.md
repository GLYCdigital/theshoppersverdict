## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

This is where the Nuenen set surprises. Most budget flatware uses 18/0 stainless steel — it's magnetic, prone to rust, and feels like tin. The Nuenen set specs out at 18/10 stainless, which means 18% chromium and 10% nickel. That's the same grade used by brands like Oneida and Liberty Tabletop. In practice, this translates to better corrosion resistance and a brighter, longer-lasting shine.

The handles measure roughly 3mm thick — noticeably more substantial than the flimsy 1.5mm stuff you'll find at dollar stores. One reviewer noted, *"I was expecting cheap and lightweight. These have actual weight to them, like restaurant-grade silverware."* That's the consensus: the weight feels right, the balance is decent, and the mirror finish holds up.

I've seen complaints about the knife performance in multiple reviews. The serrated edge cuts through chicken and fish fine, but it's not a steak knife. Dense cuts of beef will require more sawing than slicing. If you regularly serve thick steaks, budget for a separate steak knife set.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about tradeoffs. First, there's no storage container. The set arrives in a plain cardboard box. If you don't have a drawer organizer or a caddy, you'll have a loose pile of 100 utensils. That's a minor annoyance, but worth knowing.

Second, the knife dullness issue is real. Multiple reviewers mentioned the same thing — the serration is shallow and won't handle heavy-duty cutting. Third, the finish is polished but not coated. It will show fingerprints and water spots. Nothing a quick wipe won't fix, but if you're obsessive about pristine tableware, keep a microfiber cloth handy.

## FAQ

**Will this rust in the dishwasher?**
The 18/10 stainless content makes it highly resistant to rust and pitting. Multiple reviewers report running it through the dishwasher daily for months with no issues. That said, don't leave it soaking in saltwater or acidic foods overnight — that'll stress any stainless.

**Is the set actually 100 pieces?**
Yes. 20 of each: dinner forks, dinner knives, soup spoons, teaspoons, and salad forks. Verified buyers confirm the count matches the listing.

**Are the knives sharp enough for steak?**
No. They're serrated but shallow-toothed. Fine for chicken, fish, and most cooked vegetables. For thick cuts of beef, you'll want dedicated steak knives.
