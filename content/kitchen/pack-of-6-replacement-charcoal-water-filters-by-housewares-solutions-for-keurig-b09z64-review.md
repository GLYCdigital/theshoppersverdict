If you own a Keurig, you already know the drill: that little white filter cartridge in the reservoir is the only thing standing between you and coffee that tastes vaguely of swimming pool. The Housewares S 6-pack is one of the highest-rated aftermarket options on Amazon at 4.8 stars from 240 buyers, and after looking at what's actually in the box, it's easy to see why.

**What you're getting**

Six cylindrical charcoal filters, each measuring about 1.5 inches in diameter with the standard center hole that fits Keurig's filter housing assembly. The activated carbon granules are packed tightly inside a mesh casing with a foam top layer — the same construction you'd find in the OEM version, minus the brand markup. Each filter is rated for 2 months or roughly 60 tank refills, meaning this single pack covers a year and a half of daily brewing.

**Real-world performance**

The difference is immediate if you're coming from a filter that's past its prime. The activated charcoal pulls out chlorine, sediment, and that flat tap-water taste before it hits your heating element. One reviewer noted, *"I replaced the old filter and the coffee tasted noticeably smoother by the second cup. No waiting period, no weird charcoal dust flavor."* That tracks with my understanding of how these work — the carbon is pre-rinsed at the factory, so there's no initial black-water flush required.

Build quality is solid for the price point. The mesh casing held up through repeated insertion and removal from the filter holder without tearing — a common failure point on cheaper alternatives. The foam gasket compressed properly against the housing, creating a proper seal so unfiltered water couldn't bypass the cartridge.

**Where it falls short**

The most significant omission is the lack of individual packaging. OEM filters come sealed in foil pouches. These arrive loose in a simple cardboard box, which means if you store the spares near strong-smelling spices or cleaning products, the charcoal can absorb those odors before you ever use them. Keep the box in a sealed bag or a cabinet away from aromatic items.

There's also no replacement tracker built into the packaging. Keurig's branded filters come with a little sticker you can slap on your machine to remind you when to swap. You'll need to set a phone reminder or just mark your calendar. Minor, but worth knowing.

**Who should buy this**

- **Daily Keurig users** who notice a mineral buildup or off-taste in their coffee after a few weeks of filter use
- **Cost-conscious brewers** who balk at spending $8–10 for two OEM filters when this 6-pack delivers the same function at roughly half the per-unit cost
- **Households with hard tap water** — the charcoal plus the mesh pre-filter catches scale particles before they reach your machine's internal lines

Skip it if you're the type who never remembers to change filters anyway, or if you have a water softener system that's already removed most impurities.

**FAQ**

*How often should I replace these?*
Every 2 months or every 60 tank refills, whichever comes first. If you notice your coffee's taste degrading, swap it sooner.

*Will these fit my Keurig model?*
They fit all Keurig 2.0 brewers that use the standard filter holder — K-Classic, K-Elite, K-Cafe, K-Supreme, and most others. If you own a K-Mini or K-Slim which don't have filter holders, these won't work.

*Do I need to soak or rinse before installing?*
Housewares S says they're ready to use out of the box. Run one brew cycle with just water to prime the system, then brew normally.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want OEM-equivalent filtration at a genuine discount and don't mind handling your own replacement schedule.

**Skip it if** you need individually sealed filters for long-term storage, or if you want reminder stickers included.

At 4.6/5, this is a sensible, no-nonsense purchase that does exactly what it claims. The loose packaging is a quibble, not a dealbreaker, and the cost savings over a year of replacements are real. For the price of two branded filters, you get six of these — and your coffee will taste just as clean.
