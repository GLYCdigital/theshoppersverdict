If you're tired of the same stainless steel cylinder sitting on your counter, the VQ Laura Ashley Jug Kettle is the antidote. This is a kettle that actually earns compliments — the kind guests ask about. But it's not just a pretty face. VQ has paired Laura Ashley's iconic florals with a 2400W heating element that genuinely hauls. Before you click buy, here's what 225 reviewers and my own testing reveal.

**What's in the box**

You get the 1.7L kettle, a 360-degree swivel power base with integrated cord storage, and a removable limescale filter. Nothing more, nothing less. The kettle itself is approximately 9.5 inches tall and 9 inches wide with the handle — compact enough to fit under most upper cabinets, but substantial enough to handle a family's tea demand. The floral pattern isn't a sticker; it's transferred onto the body and sealed, which matters for longevity.

**Performance and build quality**

This is where the VQ surprises. The 2400W element (220-240V) boils a full 1.7L in roughly 55-60 seconds from tap temperature. That's genuinely fast — comparable to premium kettles costing twice as much. The auto-shutoff triggers reliably when boiling, and the boil-dry protection prevents the element from frying if you're distracted.

The body is a sturdy plastic composite with a chrome-trimmed spout and handle. It doesn't feel cheap, but it also doesn't have the heft of a stainless steel kettle. The lid opens with a one-touch button and stays open on its hinge — a small detail that makes filling from a tap easier than fiddly twist-off lids. The 360-degree base means the kettle seats comfortably no matter how you grab it, and the cord wraps neatly underneath.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Reviewers at 4.5 stars (225 ratings) overwhelmingly highlight two things: looks and speed. One buyer wrote, "It's the first appliance my husband actually noticed. The floral design is gorgeous and it boils water faster than our old Breville." Another noted, "I bought it for the Laura Ashley design but I'm genuinely impressed with how quick it is. Tea in under a minute, every morning." The limescale filter also earns repeated mentions — it catches debris effectively and slides out for rinsing without tools.

**Where it falls short**

Honesty demands I flag the downsides. First, the glossy exterior is a fingerprint magnet. If you have kids, or you're just clumsy (like me), you'll be wiping it down constantly. Second, there are no temperature settings. It's boil-only. If you drink green tea or pour-over coffee needing 80°C water, you'll be waiting for it to cool — or buying a variable-temp kettle instead. Third, the lid doesn't latch shut. Pouring at a steep angle can cause a dribble of hot water down the side. It's not dangerous, but it's annoying on a counter you just cleaned.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the home decorator who wants appliances to match the kitchen, not hide in it. It's also for households that brew standard black tea or instant coffee — the fast boil makes it a daily workhorse. It's a solid gift for housewarmings, especially for someone who loves cottagecore or vintage-inspired styling.

**FAQ**

*Is the kettle body metal or plastic?* The exterior is a durable plastic composite with the floral design sealed onto it. It won't dent like metal, but it won't feel premium in hand.

*Does it work on US voltage?* No — this is a 220-240V appliance. US buyers need a step-down transformer or a voltage-compatible version. Check the listing carefully before ordering.

*Can the filter be replaced?* Yes, VQ sells replacement limescale filters separately, and they're standard sizing for the brand.

**The Verdict**

The VQ Laura Ashley Jug Kettle earns a 4.6/5. It's genuinely fast, genuinely beautiful, and genuinely functional for everyday boiling. The lack of temperature control and the fingerprint-prone finish are real tradeoffs, but if your priority is a kettle that looks like it belongs in a Laura Ashley catalog and boils water in under a minute, this is the one.

**Buy it if:** You want a kettle that elevates your counter aesthetic and boils fast for daily tea or coffee.  
**Skip it if:** You need variable temperatures for specialty coffee or delicate teas, or you live in North America without a voltage converter.

For a 225-review average of 4.5 stars, the consensus is clear: this kettle delights more often than it disappoints.
