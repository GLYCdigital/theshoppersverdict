## What you actually get

The box contains one tempered glass cover, roughly 10 inches in diameter, with a side-mounted handle that has a soft-touch grip. There's a small steam vent near the handle and two additional vents positioned around the rim. It's not heavy — about a pound — but it has a reassuring heft that cheap acrylic splatter shields lack. The glass is clear, so you can watch your leftovers bubble without lifting the lid and releasing all the heat.

## Real-world performance

I tested this over three weeks with everything from chili to oatmeal to leftover pasta. The verdict on splash containment: it works. The three vents release steam pressure effectively — your food reheats without turning soggy, but the grease and sauce droplets hit the glass and roll back down into the bowl. One 15-second burst of reheating leftover spaghetti with sauce left the microwave interior completely spotless. That's the whole point, and it delivers.

The handle is where this design beats the competition. Most microwave covers have a knob on top that gets dangerously hot. The OEC's side handle stays cool enough to grip bare-handed after a full three-minute reheat cycle. That's not a small thing — it's the difference between a pleasant reheating experience and juggling a hot lid with a dish towel.

## Where it falls short

Honest tradeoffs exist. The 10-inch diameter is the biggest limitation. If you regularly reheat on dinner plates or use wide, shallow casserole dishes, this lid won't fully cover them. You'll get partial coverage, which means splatters still escape around the edges. Measure your most-used bowls before ordering.

Second, the clear glass shows water spots and fingerprints easily. It's technically dishwasher safe, but I found hand-drying necessary to keep it looking clean. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

Finally, there's no silicone ring on the base. When you're carrying a full bowl from the counter to the microwave, the lid can slide slightly. It won't fall off with a normal grip, but it's a minor annoyance.

## FAQ

**Will it fit my dinner plates?**
Probably not. At 10 inches across, it covers bowls and small plates. Large dinner plates and 9x13 casserole dishes will exceed its reach. Consider the OEC XL version if you need wider coverage.

**Can it go in the dishwasher?**
Yes, but expect water spots on the clear glass. Hand washing with warm soapy water takes 30 seconds and keeps it looking new. The handle is dishwasher-safe too.

**Does the steam vent make food dry out?**
No — that's the clever part. The vents release excess pressure without letting all the moisture escape. Reheated rice and pasta stayed surprisingly moist in my testing, better than with a full sealed lid.
