**Where It Falls Short (Be Honest)**

No product is perfect, and this set has genuine limitations. The plastic blades won't handle rock-hard butter straight from the fridge—you'll need a metal bench scraper for that. If you regularly make laminated doughs or work with very cold ingredients, this set isn't your primary tool.

The bowl scraper is also thinner than some competitors. On thick, stiff batters (like a dense banana bread), it can flex more than you'd like, making it slightly less effective at pushing rather than scraping. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

One more thing: the plastic edge will dull over time. Unlike metal scrapers that can be sharpened, these are essentially disposable after a year or two of heavy use. For the price, that's acceptable—but it's worth knowing.
