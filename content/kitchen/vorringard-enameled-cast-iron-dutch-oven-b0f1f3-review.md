## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs.

The **weight** is real. At 13.2 pounds empty, the 6-quart is a two-hand job to move from stovetop to sink. If you have arthritis or grip strength issues, look at the 4-quart instead (9.8 lbs) or skip cast iron entirely.

The **cream interior** is gorgeous but impractical. Turmeric, tomato paste, and saffron will leave faint yellow-orange ghosts that resist regular dish soap. Baking soda paste removes them, but it's an extra step. Dark enamel interiors hide these sins better.

One owner noted the **lid fit**: *"It's not vacuum-sealed like my friend's Le Creuset. A tiny bit of steam escapes around the rim during a 4-hour braise."* For most recipes this means adding a splash more liquid. For purists chasing that self-basting seal, it's a minor knock.

## FAQ

**Is the VORRINGARD Dutch oven safe for induction cooktops?**
Yes. The flat, smooth base works on induction, gas, electric, and ceramic. It's also oven-safe to 500°F with the stainless knob.

**Can I use metal utensils in it?**
Technically yes — the enamel is tough. But metal will gradually scratch the surface over time. Silicone or wood preserves the finish longer.

**How do I clean stubborn stains from the cream interior?**
Make a paste of baking soda and water, apply it to the stain, let it sit for 15 minutes, then scrub gently with a soft sponge. Avoid harsh abrasives and never use the dishwasher.
