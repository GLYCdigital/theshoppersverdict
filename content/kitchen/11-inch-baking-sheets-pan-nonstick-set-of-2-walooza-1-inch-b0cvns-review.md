**The Walooza 11-inch baking sheets deliver solid performance at a budget-friendly price point, but the size is the real story here.**

Most home bakers default to half-sheet pans (roughly 13 x 18 inches). The Walooza set takes a different approach with a more compact 11-inch footprint. That's either a smart space-saver or a dealbreaker, depending on your kitchen. Let's dig into what you're actually getting.

## What's in the Box

You get two matching baking sheets. Each measures 11 inches in length with a 1-inch rim height. The pans feature a dark nonstick coating over carbon steel. The rims are the standout design choice here — a full inch of vertical wall gives you real drip protection that shallow quarter-sheet pans lack.

## Real-World Performance

I ran these through a week of testing: chocolate chip cookies, sheet-pan chicken with vegetables, and frozen french fries. The nonstick coating performed admirably across the board. Cookies slid off with a gentle nudge — no spatula wrestling required. Sheet-pan chicken released cleanly with only a light oil spray beforehand.

The 1-inch rims proved their worth during the vegetable roast. Tossed in olive oil and seasonings, the carrots and potatoes stayed contained even when I gave the pan a vigorous shake mid-roast. No oil splatter on the oven floor, no burnt-on mess at the edges.

Heat distribution is even across the surface, and the pans haven't warped after multiple trips through a 425°F oven. The carbon steel core conducts heat predictably, producing consistent browning on cookies — no scorched edges or pale centers.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews reflects genuine satisfaction. ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the limitations. At 11 inches, these won't fit a standard 13x18-inch cookie recipe without splitting the dough between both pans. If you regularly bake for crowds, you'll find yourself doing multiple batches.

The nonstick coating is not dishwasher-safe. Amazon reviews mention coating degradation after dishwasher cycles. Hand-washing is mandatory to preserve the surface.

The dark coating also means these run hotter than bare aluminum pans. If you're converting recipes from lighter pans, reduce oven temperature by 25°F or check your bake a few minutes early.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy these if:** You have a countertop convection oven or small apartment range. You bake for one or two people. You want a compact, easy-clean sheet pan for weeknight sheet-pan dinners.

**Skip these if:** You bake large batches regularly. You need half-sheet capacity. You refuse to hand-wash cookware.

## FAQ

**Can these go under the broiler?** Technically yes, but the nonstick coating degrades above 450°F. I recommend keeping them below 425°F and skipping the broiler entirely.

**Are they truly nonstick without oil?** Eggs and delicate pastries may stick slightly. A light oil spray or parchment paper gives you foolproof release.

**Do they fit in a standard oven?** Yes — 11 inches fits any conventional oven. They're actually ideal for smaller toaster ovens, which is a common use case among reviewers.

The Walooza 11-inch baking sheets deliver dependable nonstick performance and smart rim design in a compact package. The size limitation is real, but if you're cooking for smaller portions or working with a toaster oven, these are a solid value.
