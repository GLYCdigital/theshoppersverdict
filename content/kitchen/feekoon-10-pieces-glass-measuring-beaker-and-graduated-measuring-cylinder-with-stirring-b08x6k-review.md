# Feekoon 10-Piece Glass Beaker & Graduated Cylinder Set Review: Lab-Grade Accuracy for Your Kitchen

Most measuring cups in your kitchen are lying to you. The cheap polypropylene ones with faded markings? They're accurate to maybe ±10%. If you're making coffee precisely, mixing resin, or doing any serious kitchen chemistry, that's not good enough. The Feekoon 10-piece glass measuring set brings actual laboratory borosilicate glass to your countertop — and for most home users, it's a genuine upgrade.

## What's Actually in the Box

Feekoon packs this set with real utility. You get **five Griffin-style beakers** (50mL, 100mL, 250mL, 600mL, 2000mL) and **four graduated cylinders** (10mL, 50mL, 100mL, 500mL), plus two glass stirring rods. The 2000mL beaker is the workhorse — roughly 6.5 inches tall and wide enough to handle a full batch of soup stock. The 10mL cylinder is your precision tool, marked in 0.2mL increments for when you need exact drops.

Everything is made from **borosilicate glass 3.3**, which is the same material used in Pyrex labware — not the soda-lime glass of cheap kitchen measuring cups. That matters because borosilicate handles rapid temperature changes. You can pour boiling water into a cold beaker and it won't shatter.

## Real-World Performance

I tested the 600mL beaker by boiling water directly over a gas flame (yes, it's flame-safe) then immediately plunging it into cold water. No cracks, no stress fractures. The dual-scale markings — milliliters on one side, ounces on the other — are baked-in white enamel that doesn't scratch off with steel wool.

The graduated cylinders are the real differentiator here. The **500mL cylinder** is accurate to roughly ±2mL, which is genuinely useful for measuring cooking oil for a big batch of soap or calibrating a coffee brewer. The narrow 10mL cylinder has a pour spout that's well-formed — liquid flows cleanly without running down the side.

One thing customers consistently mention: **the markings stay legible**. Multiple reviewers note that after months of dishwasher cycles, the white enamel hasn't faded or chipped. That's not something you can say about most plastic measuring sets.

## Honest Shortcomings

No product is perfect, and Feekoon has tradeoffs. **The pour spouts on the beakers are shallow** — when you're transferring honey or a thick syrup, expect it to dribble down the side. You'll want a silicone spatula on standby. 

The **narrow cylinders (10mL and 50mL) are too tight for standard stirring rods**. The included rods fit the beakers fine, but if you need to mix inside a cylinder, you'll have to use a pipette or transfer to a beaker first. 

And there's **no storage case**. The 2000mL beaker is large and glass-on-glass contact in a drawer is a recipe for chipped rims. Buy a foam-lined container or store them individually wrapped.

## FAQ

**Is this glass safe for a microwave?**
Yes. Borosilicate glass 3.3 is microwave-safe for heating liquids. Avoid sudden temperature changes — don't go from freezer to microwave directly.

**Can I put these in the dishwasher?**
The glass and markings are dishwasher-safe. However, hand-washing extends the life of the glass and prevents clouding over years of use.

**Are the measurements accurate for baking?**
The beakers are accurate to about ±5%, which is fine for baking. For critical precision, use the graduated cylinders — they're calibrated to ±1-2%.
