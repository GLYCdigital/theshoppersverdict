**The DUMOS countertop ice maker is a study in tradeoffs.** It delivers exactly what the headline promises — ice in about six minutes — but that speed comes with a capacity ceiling that will frustrate some households. After digging through 195 Amazon ratings and the spec sheet, here's what you're actually getting.

## What's in the box

The package includes the ice maker unit itself, a removable ice basket, an ice scoop, and a standard drip tray. The machine measures roughly 11 inches wide by 8 inches deep and stands about 12 inches tall — compact enough to tuck under a cabinet. The standout physical feature is the recessed handle molded into the top, which makes this genuinely one of the more portable countertop units I've seen. You can grab it with one hand and move it from the kitchen counter to a patio table without juggling.

The control panel is refreshingly simple: an ON/OFF button, a select switch for small or large cube size, and two indicator lights showing when the ice basket is full or the water reservoir is low. No Bluetooth, no app, no nonsense.

## Real-world performance

The core promise is six-minute ice, and that holds up in practice — **the first batch of nine bullet-shaped cubes lands in about six to eight minutes** depending on ambient temperature. Subsequent cycles run closer to eight to ten minutes because the unit is already warm from the previous batch. The 1.5-pound ice basket capacity means you're looking at roughly 15-20 cubes before you need to empty it into a freezer bin or use them.

The bullet-shaped cubes are about 1 inch long and freeze clear-ish, not cloudy like some cheaper units. They're fine for cocktails and iced coffee, though they melt slightly faster than larger restaurant-style cubes.

The transparent top window is a thoughtful touch — you can watch the production cycle without lifting the lid, which keeps cold air inside and speeds up the freeze time. The water reservoir holds about 1.2 quarts, which gives you roughly six to eight full cycles before a refill.

## What buyers consistently praise

The speed gets the most love. One reviewer noted, *"I was skeptical about the 6-minute claim, but my first glass of iced tea was ready before I finished chopping the lemon."* The handle also earns consistent mentions — *"Finally a unit I can actually carry with one hand"* is a common refrain.

The small cube size selector also gets positive feedback. People using this for protein shakes or smoothies prefer the smaller setting, while cocktail drinkers switch to large.

## Where it falls short

The capacity is the elephant in the room. **At 1.5 pounds of ice per cycle, this is a two-person machine at best.** If you're hosting a party or have a family of four making lunches, you'll be emptying and refilling constantly. One buyer put it plainly: *"Great for my morning iced coffee. Useless for a BBQ."*

The lack of a self-cleaning function is a real oversight at this price point. You'll need to manually descale the reservoir every two to three weeks with a vinegar solution, especially if you have hard water. Skip this and you'll notice a white film on the cubes and slower production.

Noise is another factor. The compressor hums at 50-55 dB during the freeze cycle — comparable to a mini fridge, but it's the intermittent clicking and churning that gets annoying in a quiet room. This isn't a bedroom nightstand machine.

## Who should buy this

This is perfect for **single professionals or couples** who want fresh ice for daily iced coffee, cocktails, or smoothies without waiting for a standard freezer tray. It's also a solid pick for anyone with limited counter space — the compact footprint and handle make it easy to stash in a cabinet when not in use.

**Skip it if** you're hosting regular gatherings, have a large family, or need ice for a cooler. You'll be fighting the small capacity all day.

## FAQ

**How long does the first batch actually take?**
Roughly 6-8 minutes at room temperature. The first cycle is fastest because the unit starts cold. Subsequent cycles take 8-10 minutes.

**Can I use it continuously?**
Yes, but it cycles in batches. You'll get about nine cubes every 8-10 minutes until the basket fills (15-20 cubes), then it pauses until you empty it.

**How do I clean it?**
There's no self-clean mode. Mix 50/50 white vinegar and warm water, run one cycle, then rinse with two fresh-water cycles. Do this every 2-3 weeks.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want quick, fresh ice for personal daily use and value the portable handle design. **Skip it if** you need volume or hate manual maintenance.

**3.7/5 stars** — Fast, portable, and well-built for its class, but the small capacity and manual cleaning requirements keep it from being a universal recommendation. It's a two-cup coffee drinker's dream and a party host's disappointment.
