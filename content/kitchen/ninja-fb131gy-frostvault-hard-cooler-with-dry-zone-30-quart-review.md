## FAQ

### Is the Ninja FrostVault bear-resistant?

The cooler is lockable and can be made bear-resistant when used with Ninja FrostVault Perfect-Fit Padlocks. Without padlocks, it's not certified bear-resistant. For car camping in bear country, use the padlock option or store in a bear locker.

### How do I maximize ice retention?

Pre-chill the cooler before use (fill with ice 24 hours ahead, then drain and reload with fresh ice and contents). Use block ice rather than cubes when possible. Keep the cooler in the shade, minimize opening, and maintain a high ice-to-contents ratio. Properly prepared, many users report 2-3 days of solid ice retention.

### Can I put the Dry Zone drawer in the freezer?

The drawer is designed to be used inside the cooler at food-safe temperatures (under 40°F). It's not designed for standalone freezer use. The cold is transferred from the main ice compartment through the FrostVault system.

### Is the Ninja FrostVault good for saltwater use?

The cooler's heavy-duty construction holds up well in saltwater environments, though rinsing with fresh water after beach use is recommended to prevent salt buildup on the latch and handles.
