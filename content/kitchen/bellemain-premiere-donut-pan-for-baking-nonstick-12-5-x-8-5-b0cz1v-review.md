## Where It Falls Short

This pan is hand-wash only. The dishwasher will degrade the nonstick coating over time, and several negative reviews trace their issues back to machine washing. That's on the user, but it's worth knowing before you commit.

The six-cavity design is also a limitation. If you're feeding a family of four, you'll bake two batches for a full dozen. For occasional weekend bakes, it's perfect. For serious batch bakers, buy two — they stack neatly.

One more honest note: the coating isn't indestructible. Use silicone or wooden utensils, not metal, and avoid stacking heavy pans on top of it during storage. A few reviewers reported scratches after aggressive use, which is typical of any nonstick product.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Ask

**Q: Do I need to grease the pan if it's nonstick?**
A: A light coating of butter or baking spray still helps, especially for the first few uses. After that, the nonstick gets more effective. Don't use aerosol sprays with lecithin — they can leave a sticky residue on the coating.

**Q: Can I use this for other things besides donuts?**
A: Yes. The cavities work for small bundt cakes, egg bites, or mini quick breads. Some reviewers use it for cinnamon roll rings. Get creative.

**Q: Is the pan oven-safe at high temperatures?**
A: It's rated to 450°F, which covers virtually all baking recipes. Don't put it under a broiler.
