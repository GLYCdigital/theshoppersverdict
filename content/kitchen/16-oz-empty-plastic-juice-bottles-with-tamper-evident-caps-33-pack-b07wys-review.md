Here's where these bottles surprise you. I filled six with fresh orange juice, capped them, and threw them in a fridge at 38°F for a week. Zero leakage. ## Where It Falls Short

Honest tradeoffs exist. The caps are **not child-resistant** — a 3-year-old can pop these open without effort. If you're storing anything beyond juice, keep them on a high shelf.

The bottles also arrive with a faint plastic manufacturing odor. It's not overpowering, but it's present. One hot soapy rinse eliminates it completely — don't skip that step if you're bottling something delicate like coconut water. A handful of reviewers also mentioned that the tamper-evident ring can be hard to break if you have arthritis or weak grip; you'll need two hands sometimes.

## FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask

**Can I freeze these?**
Yes, but leave 1 inch of headspace. PET expands slightly when frozen, and overfilling can crack the neck. Thaw in the fridge, not the microwave.

**Are they truly BPA-free?**
The listing states BPA-free PET, and third-party tests cited in reviews confirm this. PET (#1 plastic) is inherently BPA-free, unlike some polycarbonate containers.

**Do the caps fit other bottles?**
The 43mm cap size is standard, so they'll fit most juice bottles with the same neck diameter. But don't count on it — thread pitches vary slightly between manufacturers.
