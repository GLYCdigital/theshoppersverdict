## Where It Falls Short

The flexibility trade-off is real. For dense batters or stiff mixtures, you'll work harder than with a rigid metal whisk. Some users also report that the silicone coating can pick up stains from turmeric or tomato-based sauces — cosmetic, not functional, but worth noting.

The other honest caveat: hand-washing is recommended. While the set is technically dishwasher-safe, the heat and detergent cycles will eventually degrade the silicone. Buy these knowing you'll need to rinse them by hand for maximum lifespan.
