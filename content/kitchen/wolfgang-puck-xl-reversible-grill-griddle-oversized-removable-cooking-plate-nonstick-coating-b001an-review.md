# Wolfgang Puck XL Reversible Grill Griddle Review: Is This Oversized 2-in-1 Worth Your Counter Space?

Most griddles are glorified frying pans — too small to cook a family breakfast, too flimsy to sear a steak. The Wolfgang Puck XL Reversible Grill Griddle takes a different approach: it's genuinely oversized, genuinely reversible, and genuinely built for feeding a crowd. After digging through 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, here's what you need to know before spending your money.

## What You Actually Get

This isn't a single slab. The package includes a heavy-duty aluminum frame plus two separate cooking plates: a flat griddle side and a ridged grill side. Each plate measures roughly 20 inches by 10 inches — big enough to span two burners on a standard stove. The plates lock into the frame with a simple mechanism and release with a push, which is the standout design choice here.

The nonstick surface is PTFE-based, and the whole unit is oven-safe up to 350°F (though honestly, at this size, you're not sliding it into a standard oven anyway). It's compatible with gas, electric, and induction cooktops — just check your burner layout first.

## How It Performs in Real Kitchens

The griddle side absolutely shines for breakfast. You can lay out a dozen eggs, a full pound of bacon, and a stack of pancakes simultaneously — no staggering batches, no cold food. The heat distribution is remarkably even across the flat surface, with no hot spots near the center where the burners sit.

Flip it over, and the ridged grill side handles steaks, chicken breasts, and vegetables with legitimate sear marks. The ridges are deep enough to keep meat above rendered fat, so you get grill-like char without the smoke and splatter of an outdoor grill. One reviewer noted, "I've cooked smash burgers for a family of six in one batch. The surface stayed hot even after adding cold patties."

The removable plates are the real winner. After cooking, you pop both plates off, rinse them under the tap, and toss them in the dishwasher. No crevices, no trapped grease, no awkward scrubbing around fixed rivets like cheaper reversible griddles.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The size dominates the conversation. Over and over, reviewers mention cooking for 6-10 people without breaking a sweat. One 4-star review put it well: "I bought this for camping and it replaced both my skillet and grill grate. Two burners, one pan, everything done at once."

The reversible design also earns consistent credit. Unlike flimsy double-sided pans that warp or wobble, this one sits flat on the stovetop thanks to the reinforced frame. The plates lock in securely with no rattling during cooking.

## Where It Falls Short

At nearly 10 pounds, this is a heavy piece of cookware. It's awkward to lift with one hand, and if your storage is an overhead cabinet, you'll think twice every time. It also requires a wide stovetop — if your burners are round and closely spaced, the rectangular footprint won't sit flush, and you'll lose heat on the ends.

The nonstick coating is functional but not premium. Multiple reviewers mentioned surface scratches appearing within months, especially if they used metal spatulas. Stick to silicone or wood utensils, and hand-wash the plates when you can — even though they're dishwasher-safe, the detergent is harsh on the coating over time.

One more thing: there's no grease tray. On the grill side, rendered fat collects in the ridges and can pool near the edges. You'll want paper towels on standby during cookout sessions.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You regularly cook for 4+ people, you're tired of batch-cooking pancakes or burgers, or you want one tool that replaces both a griddle and a grill pan. It's also a solid choice for RV and camp cooking since it's flat and stores easily.

**Skip it if:** You have a small kitchen with limited storage, you only cook for 1-2 people, or you're expecting a premium nonstick surface that lasts a decade. This is a workhorse, not a heirloom piece.

## FAQ

**Q: Does it fit all stovetops?**
A: It needs about 20 inches of horizontal space and two burners that can align under the rectangular footprint. Round burners spaced far apart will leave cold zones. Measure before buying.

**Q: Is the nonstick coating safe?**
A: It's PTFE-based (Teflon-type), not ceramic. It's fine at normal cooking temperatures under 500°F, but don't preheat empty on high or use metal utensils.

**Q: Can I use it in the oven?**
A: The manufacturer says oven-safe to 350°F, but at 20 inches long, it won't fit most home ovens. Treat it as a stovetop-only tool.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The Wolfgang Puck XL Reversible Grill Griddle earns its 4.6-star rating through sheer practicality. If you cook for a crowd and have the stovetop space, this is the one to buy. If you're a solo cook with a tiny kitchen, it's overkill — look for a smaller reversible griddle instead.
