If you own a Ninja Smart XL air fryer, you already know the cleanup is the worst part. The nonstick basket works — until oils bake into the corners and the mesh bottom turns into a crusty archaeology site. These pre-cut parchment liners are the simplest fix I've tested, and at 125 sheets per pack, you're looking at months of zero-scrub cooking.

Here's the real story after digging through 229 ratings and the specs sheet.

**What you're actually getting**

This is a 125-count pack of pre-shaped parchment liners cut specifically for the Ninja Smart XL's rectangular basket (roughly 9.5 x 8.5 inches at the base). They're not the generic round discs you'll find from no-name brands — these follow the basket's contours with raised edges that keep oils and crumbs contained. The paper is food-grade, unbleached, and rated to 428°F, which gives you headroom above the Smart XL's 400°F max.

No adhesive, no clips, no silicone frame. Just drop one in, set your food on top, and cook. When you're done, lift the liner out and toss it. The basket underneath stays essentially untouched.

**How they actually perform**

In real use, the fit is the make-or-break detail. Buyers consistently report the liner sits flush against the Ninja Smart XL's walls without bunching or folding over the rim — which matters because a curled edge can block airflow and leave you with unevenly cooked food. One reviewer put it simply: "Fits my Ninja Smart XL perfectly. No more scrubbing the basket every single night."

The raised lip does double duty. It catches grease drippings and keeps them off the basket floor, but it also creates a slight barrier that helps with airflow circulation. Food still crisps properly — chicken wings, frozen fries, even breaded items come out with the same texture as without a liner. You're not sacrificing performance for convenience.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest limitation is the specific sizing. These are cut for the Ninja Smart XL rectangular basket only. If you have a Ninja with a round basket — the AF101, AF161, or the older models — these won't fit properly. The corners will bunch up and the liner will sit unevenly. Read your model number before buying.

A smaller annoyance: loose liners can lift and flutter if you preheat the air fryer empty. A couple of buyers noted the paper shifted when they turned the unit on with nothing inside. The fix is simple — add food first, or press the liner down with a spatula — but it's worth knowing.

Also, the 125-sheet count is generous, but these are single-use. If you're looking for a reusable silicone option, this isn't it. For the cost per sheet, though, most people find the disposability worth every penny.

**Who should buy this**

- **The everyday air fryer user** — if you cook in the Smart XL 4-5 times a week, this pack saves you hours of scrubbing over the next few months.
- **The meal-prep cook** — batch-cooking proteins or roasted vegetables? Layer a fresh liner per batch and keep the basket clean between rounds.
- **Anyone who's ruined a nonstick coating** — aggressive scrubbing with metal brushes is the #1 way to kill an air fryer basket. Liners prevent that entirely.

Skip it if you have a round-basket Ninja, or if you're uncomfortable with single-use kitchen disposables.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I reuse these liners?**
Technically, if you're cooking something dry like toast or a single layer of fries, you can get two uses. But once they're soaked with grease or food particles, toss them. They're designed for one-time use.

**Q: Will the liner block airflow and make food less crispy?**
The raised edges sit against the basket walls, so the bulk of the liner is flat on the bottom. Air circulates over the top and through the mesh sides. Most reviewers report no difference in crispness.

**Q: Do they fit the Ninja Foodi Smart XL air fryer oven?**
No. This is specifically for the air fryer basket model — the one with the pull-out basket. The Foodi oven-style units with sheet pans need different liners.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you own a Ninja Smart XL with a rectangular basket and want to eliminate air fryer cleanup entirely. It's a small price per use for what you save in time and elbow grease.

**Skip it if** you have a round-basket Ninja, prefer reusable alternatives, or only air fry once a month.
