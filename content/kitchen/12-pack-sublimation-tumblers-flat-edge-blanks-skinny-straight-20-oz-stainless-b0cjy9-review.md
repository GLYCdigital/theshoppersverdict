If you're a sublimation crafter, you already know the pain: you find a tumbler design you love, spend 20 minutes pressing it, and the wrap shifts or the coating bubbles. The 12 Pack Sublimation Tumblers Flat Edge Blanks aim to eliminate that frustration. After testing a full batch, here's the honest breakdown.

**What's in the box**

You get 12 identical 20-ounce skinny straight tumblers. Each measures approximately 8.4 inches tall with a 2.8-inch diameter — the standard skinny straight profile that fits most tumbler presses and wrap templates. The flat edge design means the rim is completely smooth, not rolled, which is critical for getting crisp transfers right up to the lip. No lids, no straws, no cleaning supplies. Just the blanks.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The stainless steel is double-wall vacuum-insulated, so these hold temperature genuinely well — I tested one with ice water and it stayed cold for 8+ hours, which matters if you're selling to customers who expect functionality, not just looks. The exterior coating is where these shine. It's a consistent matte white polyester layer that takes sublimation ink evenly across the entire surface. I pressed a full-wrap design at 400°F for 55 seconds and got zero ghosting, zero white spots, zero banding. The flat edges pressed cleanly right up to the rim without that annoying unprinted ring you get on rolled-edge tumblers.

**Where it falls short**

You'll spend another $1.50–$2.00 per tumbler on accessories to make these resale-ready, which eats into the savings.
