## What You Actually Get

## Performance and Build Quality

I tested these with a few scenarios: dry goods, liquid, and the dishwasher. The seal holds. I filled one with water, shook it vigorously, and flipped it upside down — zero leakage. That's the result of the gasket-lined lid, which many jars in this price range skip entirely. The square shape is the real selling point. In a standard 12-inch cabinet, I fit 16 jars in the same footprint that held 12 round ones. That 20% density gain matters when counter space is precious. The glass is tempered enough to handle boiling water for sterilizing — I ran a full batch through a water bath canner and none cracked.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest complaints. First, the metal lids are not dishwasher-safe long-term. Repeated cycles will eventually cause surface rust on the rim. Hand-wash the lids; the glass is fine in the machine. Second, the size is deceptive in photos. At 150ml, these are ideal for spices, cosmetics, or sample-sized portions — not for storing a pound of flour. If you need bigger, look at the 250ml or 500ml versions of this line.

There's also the oddity of the brand name — HHMJSM isn't a household name, and the product listing is sparse on spec details. That's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, given the build quality.
