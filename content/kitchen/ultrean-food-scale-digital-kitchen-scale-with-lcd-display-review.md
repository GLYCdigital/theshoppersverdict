If you're baking, meal-prepping, or just trying to nail your morning coffee ratio, a kitchen scale is non-negotiable. The **Ultrean Digital Kitchen Scale** promises accuracy, compact storage, and six measurement units — all for $6.98. We analyzed 21,051 Amazon reviews to see if it delivers.

## The Good

### Accuracy You Can Trust

For a budget scale, the Ultrean punches above its weight on precision. Users who bake regularly — where grams matter — report consistent, repeatable readings. The tare function (zeroing out a container's weight) works smoothly: place your bowl, press tare, add ingredients, repeat. One reviewer who uses it daily for three years called it their "best friend" in the kitchen.

### Small Footprint, Big Utility

This scale is genuinely compact. Multiple reviews highlight how easy it is to tuck into a cabinet on its side or slide into a drawer. It takes up about the space of a small paperback book. If counter real estate is precious, the Ultrean respects that.

### Six Units, Zero Confusion

Grams, ounces, pounds, kilograms, milliliters, fluid ounces — the Ultrean covers every common cooking unit. The LCD display is clear and the unit button cycles through options without requiring a manual consult. One reviewer said, "I cook a lot but many recipes call for grams and other measurements I don't have a clue about. This makes it easy."

### Stainless Steel Surface

The weighing platform is 304 stainless steel — fingerprint-resistant, easy to wipe down, and doesn't absorb odors. It's a small upgrade over all-plastic scales that elevates the day-to-day experience.

## The Not-So-Good

### Battery Roulette

Batteries are included, but they're not always fresh. Several users reported their scale arrived with dead or near-dead CR2032 batteries. It's a $2 fix, but an annoying one for a product that markets "batteries included." Keep a spare coin cell on hand.

### Button Feel Is Inconsistent

The touch-sensitive buttons get mixed reviews. Some units have crisp, responsive buttons; others require a firmer press or a second tap to register. It's not a dealbreaker — you're adjusting units maybe twice per cooking session — but it's a reminder of the $6.98 price point.

### Platform Size Tradeoffs

The compact design means a compact platform. If you're using a large mixing bowl, it can overhang the scale and partially block the LCD. Workaround: place the bowl, tare, then read the display before it's obscured. Annoying, but manageable.

### 11-Pound Ceiling

For most home cooking, 11 pounds is plenty. But if you're batch-prepping massive quantities, canning, or weighing bulk ingredients, you'll hit the limit. This is a personal-use scale, not a commercial one.

## Who Should Buy It

The Ultrean is ideal for **home cooks, bakers, meal-preppers, and coffee enthusiasts** who want accuracy without spending $30+. It's also great as a second scale — keep one in the kitchen and one in the pantry. If you're new to cooking by weight, this is the lowest-risk entry point.

## Who Should Skip It

- Large-batch cooks who regularly weigh 10+ pounds
- Anyone who needs a large, unshaded platform for oversized bowls
- Premium buyers who expect flawless button responsiveness
- Commercial kitchen use (look at Escali or OXO for heavy-duty options)

## Bottom Line

At $6.98, the Ultrean Digital Kitchen Scale is **the best value in kitchen scales** — period. It's accurate, compact, easy to clean, and covers every measurement unit you'll ever need. The battery situation and button quirks are minor annoyances at this price. With a 4.6/5 rating from 21,000+ reviews, the message is clear: this is the kitchen scale most home cooks should own.
