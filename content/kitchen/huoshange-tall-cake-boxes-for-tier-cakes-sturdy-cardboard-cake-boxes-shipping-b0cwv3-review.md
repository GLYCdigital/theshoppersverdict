## Where It Falls Short

No box is perfect, and this one has two honest flaws. First, **there are no side handles**. For a tall box holding a heavy tiered cake, you have to grip from the bottom and carry it like a pizza box. That's awkward when you're also holding a car key or a delivery bag. Second, the bottom seam isn't a leak-proof seal. If you pack a very moist cake with a loose buttercream and let it sit for more than an hour, you'll get a small grease spot seeping through the seam. It won't collapse, but it will mark your car seat or delivery bag. For a quick transport, it's fine; for long-haul deliveries in a hot car, you need a box liner.
