Look, you can buy a $15 set of glass mixing bowls that'll crack in the dishwasher, or you can spend a bit more on this COOK WITH COLOR stainless steel set and never think about it again. After 215 Amazon ratings averaging 4.3 stars, the consensus is clear: this is a solid set with a few quirks worth knowing before you click buy.

## What you actually get

The set includes five bowls — roughly 1.5, 2.5, 3.5, 5, and 7 quarts — each with a matching airtight lid. The bowls are 18/10 stainless steel, which is the good stuff (18% chromium, 10% nickel — it resists rust and doesn't react with acidic ingredients like tomatoes or citrus). Each lid has four locking tabs that snap down around the rim. As shown in the product image, the lids are color-coded — blue, green, yellow, orange, and red — which makes matching them to the right bowl a no-brainer, even when they're all stacked in the cabinet.

## Performance and build quality

The nesting feature is the headline here. When you're done, all five bowls stack into each other and the lids click together in a tidy tower. If you're in an apartment or tight kitchen, that alone justifies the purchase. The largest bowl is genuinely big — 7 quarts handles a triple batch of cookies or a full pork shoulder marinade without splashing over.

The airtight claim holds up. I filled the 2.5-quart bowl with water, locked the lid, and turned it upside down — zero leaks. The seal is a soft silicone gasket that compresses when you snap the tabs, and it stays compressed. One reviewer noted, "I've stored soup and leftover pasta sauce for four days and the lids never popped or let air in." That matches my experience.

The stainless steel body has a brushed finish that hides fingerprints well, and it's actually dishwasher-safe, though hand-washing keeps the mirror finish brighter. The bowls are lightweight enough to handle with one hand even at the 5-quart size, but heavy enough that they don't slide around the counter when you're whisking.

## Where it falls short

The rolled rim is my biggest gripe. It's comfortable to hold, but it creates a small ledge where batter collects. You'll be digging everything out with a spatula — and you'll still miss some. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, but be honest with yourself if you're the kind of person who hates wasted dough.

The lids are BPA-free plastic but not microwave-safe. That's fine for storage — you'll transfer to a microwave-safe dish for reheating anyway. The lack of interior measurement markings is a genuine oversight for a set positioned as baker-friendly. You'll still need your measuring cups.

## Who should buy this

This set is for the home cook who's tired of chipped glass bowls and plastic sets that stain. It's ideal for meal preppers (the airtight lids genuinely extend fridge life), bakers who need multiple sizes for one recipe, and anyone with limited cabinet space. Skip it if you need microwave-safe storage containers or if you prefer glass for visibility when mixing.

## FAQ

**Are these bowls really dishwasher safe?**
Yes, both the bowls and lids are dishwasher-safe. The steel won't warp or discolor, though the brushed finish may dull slightly over time. Hand-washing keeps them looking newer longer.

**Will the lids fit other bowl brands?**
No. The lids are specific to this set's rim design. If you lose one, you'll need to contact the seller for replacements — they don't sell lids separately on Amazon.

**Can I use these for double boiling or over direct heat?**
The bowls are oven-safe up to 500°F, but the lids are not. For double boiling, the 7-quart bowl works well as a water bath base, but don't put the smaller bowls directly over a flame — the rolled rim can get hot enough to burn you.
