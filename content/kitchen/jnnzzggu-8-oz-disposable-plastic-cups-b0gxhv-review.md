**The Real-World Test**

I ran these through a week of actual use: hot coffee (briefly), ice water, soda at a backyard party, and even a few rounds in the freezer for dessert shots. The standout feature is the sidewall rigidity. Where cheaper cups flex and crack when you squeeze them, these hold firm—one reviewer noted, "I've double-stacked these for espresso shots at my brunch pop-up and not a single one split." The leak-proof base is another genuine win. I filled one with ice water and let it sit for 30 minutes on a paper towel. Zero weeping. **Where It Falls Short**

Honest tradeoffs: the rim is noticeably sharper than a rolled-edge Solo cup. It's not a safety hazard for adults, but if you're handing these to toddlers, watch them. One buyer commented, "Great cups but the lip feels like it could cut if you're not careful." That's fair feedback.

Second, despite the sturdy construction, these are NOT microwave-safe. The plastic is PET-based, which means reheating leftovers in these will warp them. Several reviewers made this mistake and ended up with melted cups. Use them for cold or room-temperature drinks only.
