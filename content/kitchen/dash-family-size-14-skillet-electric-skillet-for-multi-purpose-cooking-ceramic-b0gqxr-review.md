**The 14-inch electric skillet that actually earns counter space**

Most electric skillets are either too small to cook for a family or too flimsy to last past a few months. The Dash Family Size 14" Skillet tries to solve both problems at once. After digging through 220 ratings and the real customer feedback behind that 4.4-star average, here's what you need to know before buying.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the 14-inch skillet base with integrated heating element, the ceramic-coated pan insert, a tempered glass lid with steam vent, and a detachable temperature probe/dial. The pan itself is substantial — not featherweight — and the ceramic surface has a smooth, almost matte finish that feels different from standard non-stick. The glass lid fits snugly and lets you monitor food without lifting it.

**Real-world performance**

Here's where this skillet separates itself from budget competition. That ceramic surface is the star. One buyer noted, *"I've used it daily for three months and eggs still slide right off with zero oil."* Another praised its versatility: *"I've made pancakes, stir-fry, and even a full lasagna in this thing. It's replaced my stovetop for most weeknight meals."*

The 14-inch surface is genuinely family-sized. You can fit six chicken breasts, a dozen eggs, or a full pound of bacon in a single batch. The heating element reaches temperature quickly — roughly 3-4 minutes to medium — and maintains it reasonably well during cooking.

**Where it falls short**

No product is perfect, and this skillet has honest flaws. The temperature dial is imprecise. You get numbered settings, not actual degrees, so dialing in "350°F" for frying is guesswork. Several reviewers mentioned this: *"I wish the dial had actual temperature markings instead of just numbers."*

The short cord (approximately 24 inches) is another real annoyance. Unless your counter outlet is conveniently placed, you'll be using an extension cord or rearranging your kitchen. Finally, the heating element runs slightly hotter at the edges — a quirk several long-term users flagged. You'll need to rotate food occasionally for uniform browning.

**Who should buy this**

This skillet is ideal for three types of shoppers:

- **Family cooks** who need batch capacity without firing up the oven and heating the whole house
- **Small kitchen/dorm dwellers** who want one appliance that replaces a frying pan, griddle, and slow cooker
- **Health-conscious cooks** who want non-stick cooking without PTFE/PFOA concerns — the ceramic coating delivers here

Skip it if you're a precision cook who needs exact temperature control, or if you regularly cook for crowds larger than six people. The 14-inch surface handles a family fine, but it's not catering equipment.

**Three questions shoppers actually ask**

*Is the ceramic coating really durable?* Based on multi-month usage reports, yes — it resists scratching better than standard non-stick. Use wooden or silicone utensils and it should last years.

*Can the pan go in the dishwasher?* Yes, the insert is dishwasher-safe. Hand-washing is gentler, but the convenience is real.

*Does it work for searing meat?* It gets hot enough for decent browning, but it won't match cast iron for high-heat searing. It's a versatile everyday cooker, not a steakhouse tool.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely family-sized electric skillet with a safe, durable cooking surface and easy cleanup. **Skip it if** precise temperature control is non-negotiable, or you need a longer cord.

At 4.4 stars from 220+ buyers, the Dash Family Size 14" Skillet earns its reputation. It's not perfect — the imprecise dial and short cord are real drawbacks — but for the price, it delivers dependable, versatile cooking that replaces multiple appliances. For most family kitchens, that's a trade worth making. **4.2/5**
