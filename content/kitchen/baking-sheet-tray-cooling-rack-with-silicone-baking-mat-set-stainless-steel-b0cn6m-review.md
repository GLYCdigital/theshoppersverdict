If you've ever pulled a tray of cookies out of the oven only to watch them continue baking on the hot sheet, you already know why a cooling rack matters. This set bundles the rack with a silicone mat and a baking tray, and at 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, it's clearly earning its keep in real kitchens. But is it the all-in-one solution it claims to be, or just another Amazon kitchen gadget with good marketing? Here's what I found.

**What You Actually Get**

The set includes three pieces: a stainless steel cooling rack, a silicone baking mat, and a baking sheet tray. The tray itself is standard quarter-sheet size (roughly 13 x 9 inches), which fits most home ovens without crowding. The cooling rack sits flush on top of the tray, and the silicone mat lays flat inside — no curling at the edges, which is a common complaint with cheaper mats.

The rack is the standout component. It's made from heavy-gauge stainless steel, not the flimsy wire you see in budget sets. The grid pattern is tight enough to support small items like cherry tomatoes or biscuit rounds without them slipping through, but open enough to let heat escape. As shown in the product image, the rack has raised feet that lift it about three-quarters of an inch off the tray — enough clearance for real air circulation, which is the whole point of a cooling rack.

**Performance and Build Quality**

I tested this set with three scenarios: a batch of chocolate chip cookies, a sheet of roasted vegetables, and a glazed lemon loaf that I let cool completely on the rack. The cookies came off clean — the mat's non-stick surface worked as advertised, and the rack allowed even cooling without soggy bottoms. The roasted vegetables were fine, though the mat's edges did darken slightly from the high heat (it's rated to 450°F, and I pushed it close to that).

The glaze test is where things got interesting. The silicone mat is non-stick for most things, but sugary glazes will still leave a residue if you let them cool completely on the surface. That's not a defect — it's physics. If you're baking glazed donuts or caramel-heavy desserts, transfer them to a separate surface after they set. The mat is also a bit of a lint magnet out of the box; a quick rinse before first use fixes that.

The tray itself is decent but not exceptional. It's lightweight aluminum, which means it heats quickly but can warp if you subject it to sudden temperature changes. I didn't have that issue in testing, but several Amazon reviewers noted it. One reviewer wrote, "The rack is solid and the mat is great, but the tray is just okay — I've had sturdier ones." That's a fair assessment.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Across the 235 ratings, three themes dominate. First, the rack's durability. Multiple reviewers mention it doesn't bend or flex, even with heavy loads. One wrote, "I've gone through three cooling racks in two years. This one actually feels like it will last." Second, the mat's measurement markings — it has printed circles and lines that help with consistent dough spacing, which bakers genuinely appreciate. Third, the set's value as a gift or starter kit for new bakers. It's a complete package that covers most basic baking needs.

**Where It Falls Short**

The tray is the weakest link. It's functional but not premium, and if you already own a good half-sheet pan, you might find this redundant. The tight wire spacing on the rack, while great for small items, makes hand-washing slightly tedious — bits of food get stuck between the wires. Also, the mat is only available in one size, so if you need a half-sheet mat for bigger batches, this won't cover that.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **New bakers** who need a complete starter kit — this covers your bases without buying pieces separately.
- **Cookie and pastry bakers** who bake frequently and need a rack that won't warp.
- **Home cooks who want a non-stick surface** for roasting vegetables or baking fish without parchment waste.

**Skip It If**

- You already own a quality half-sheet pan and just need a cooling rack — buy a standalone rack instead.
- You bake exclusively in large batches — the quarter-sheet size will feel limiting.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Is the silicone mat safe for high-heat roasting?**
Yes, it's rated to 450°F, which covers most roasting and baking recipes. Don't use it under a broiler.

**Q: Can the cooling rack go in the dishwasher?**
Technically yes, but hand-washing is gentler on the wire finish. The tight grid traps food, so a quick soak helps.

**Q: Does the mat fit the tray perfectly?**
Yes, it's designed to fit this specific tray size. It won't fit a standard half-sheet pan.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're looking for a reliable, complete baking starter set that won't fall apart after a few months. The rack is genuinely well-made, and the mat adds real utility. **Skip it if** you only need one piece or already have a stash of baking sheets.
