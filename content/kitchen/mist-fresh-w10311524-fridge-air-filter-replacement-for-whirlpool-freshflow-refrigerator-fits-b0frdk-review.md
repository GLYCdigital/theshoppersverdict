**FAQ**

**Q: How do I know if this fits my refrigerator?**
Check your fridge's model number (usually on a sticker inside the fresh food compartment or on the side wall). If it calls for part number W10311524, this will fit. When in doubt, measure your current filter — it should be roughly 5.5" x 3".

**Q: How often should I replace it?**
Every six months is the standard recommendation. If you notice odors returning sooner, replace it earlier. The carbon saturates faster in warm, humid climates.

**Q: Does it actually reduce odors, or just mask them?**
It absorbs odors via activated carbon. It doesn't release fragrance to cover smells — it traps them. That's a meaningful difference from "fridge deodorizers" that just scent the air.
