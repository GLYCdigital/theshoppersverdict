The OXO Good Grips 5-Piece POP set is the storage container equivalent of a Swiss Army knife — every piece serves a purpose, but the whole is greater than the sum of its parts. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, this set has earned its place in serious kitchens. But is it worth your cabinet space? Let's break down what you're actually getting.

**What's in the Box**

This set includes five rectangular containers with the signature one-touch POP lids: one 4.4-quart (perfect for a 5-pound bag of flour), one 2.6-quart (ideal for a standard 16-ounce pasta box), and three 1.1-quart sizes that handle rice, sugar, coffee, or snacks. The acrylic bodies are crystal-clear, and the black lids feature the soft, non-slip grip OXO is known for. The dimensions are designed for modular stacking — the 1.1-quart containers nest perfectly on the larger ones.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The push-button mechanism is the headline feature, and it delivers. Press the center button to seal, press again to release — it's satisfyingly tactile and creates a vacuum-like seal. I tested this with a bag of tortilla chips and a half-used bag of brown sugar, two of the worst offenders for going stale. The chips stayed crisp for three weeks. The brown sugar remained scoopable after a month.

The square footprint is a silent hero here. Round canisters waste corner space in cabinets; these maximize every inch. As one verified buyer noted, "I replaced four mismatched containers with this set and gained an entire shelf of usable space." Another praised the visibility: "Being able to see exactly how much flour I have left without opening the lid is a small luxury I didn't know I needed."

**Where It Falls Short**

Here's the honest truth: these aren't indestructible. The acrylic scratches noticeably if you scrub with abrasive pads or run them through the dishwasher, despite the "top-rack safe" label. Multiple reviewers mention clouding after several dishwasher cycles. Hand-washing is the way to go.

The other quirk: overfilling. If you pack rice or beans right to the brim, the lid can pop off when you press to seal, breaking the airtight closure. Leave a half-inch of headroom and this isn't an issue, but it's a learning curve worth knowing.

**Who Should Buy This**

This set is ideal for:
- **Home bakers** who buy flour and sugar in bulk and need airtight, visible storage
- **Meal preppers** who want uniform containers that stack efficiently in the fridge or pantry
- **Kitchen organizers** replacing mismatched, non-sealing canisters

**Skip it if:**
- You rely on dishwasher-safe everything — these demand hand-washing for longevity
- You need to store huge quantities of a single item (the largest container holds about 5 pounds, not 10)

**FAQ**

*Can these containers freeze safely?* Yes, but leave room for expansion — the lids can crack if contents expand against a full seal.

*Do the lids stay attached when stacking?* The containers stack on each other's flat lids, but the lids themselves have no locking mechanism. They stay in place when stacked, but don't toss the stack in a moving box.

*Are the containers really airtight?* Yes, when closed properly and not overfilled. The seal is strong enough that I've flipped a filled container upside down with no leaks.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want clear, stackable, genuinely airtight storage for dry goods and you're willing to hand-wash to keep them looking new.

**Skip it if:** You need dishwater-safe durability or you're looking for single, large-volume bulk storage.

This set earns a solid 4.6 out of 5. The OXO Good Grips 5-Piece POP isn't perfect, but for the price, it's the best combination of function, visibility, and space efficiency I've tested in this category. Your pantry will thank you.
