The Original Salbree Steamer Basket for 6qt Instant Pot holds a remarkable position: with 7,808 reviews and a 4.7-star average, it's one of the highest-rated Instant Pot accessories on Amazon. At $19.99, it's also one of the most affordable upgrades you can make.

## The Perfect Accessory

The Salbree steamer basket transforms your Instant Pot from a pressure cooker into a versatile steaming station. The stainless steel basket fits perfectly inside the 6qt inner pot, with a raised design that keeps food above the water line for genuine steaming rather than boiling.

Reviewers consistently praise the build quality. The stainless steel is sturdy, and the silicone-covered handle stays cool during operation — a crucial safety feature that makes retrieval easy without needing separate tools.

## Versatile Performance

What makes this basket so popular is its versatility:

- **Vegetables:** Steam broccoli, carrots, green beans, and asparagus to perfect tenderness
- **Eggs:** The basket simplifies hard-boiled eggs — place eggs inside, steam, and lift the entire basket out for quick cooling
- **Seafood:** Steam fish fillets, shrimp, and shellfish without direct water contact
- **Reheating:** Perfect for reheating leftovers without sogginess

One experienced reviewer captured it well: "Great fit, helps a lot picking up hard boiled eggs and veggies. I'm a novice, so I'm still exploring what I can cook using this accessory."

## The Little Details

The silicone handle deserves special mention. It folds flat for storage inside the pot but stays cool to the touch when lifting hot food. If it feels loose, it can be tightened by removing, stretching slightly, and re-seating it.

Some users note that the bail handle can be stiff to release from its storage position initially. One reviewer mentioned it "was very difficult to release from its position inside the basket" but managed to work it loose.

## Storage-Friendly Design

Perhaps the best feature of the Salbree basket is where it lives: inside your Instant Pot. The collapsible handle allows it to sit flat inside the pot, taking up zero additional cabinet space. It's always there when you need it.

At $19.99 with a 4.7-star average from nearly 8,000 reviews, the Salbree Steamer Basket is the definitive Instant Pot accessory. It's well-made, versatile, and practically pays for itself in the first week of expanded cooking options. If you own a pressure cooker, this is the first accessory you should buy.
