You can buy a single 9-inch cake pan for $12, or you can buy this E-far set and never think about pan sizes again. For the price of one or two decent pans, you're getting nine graduated round pans from 3 inches up to 9 inches. That's not just a bargain — it's a different way to bake.

**What you actually get**

The set includes nine round pans: 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9-inch (the listing title says 3–9 but you're getting seven distinct sizes plus two spares — the 4 and 6 are duplicated in some configurations, so check the exact listing before ordering). Each pan is about 2 inches deep, which is standard for layer cakes and cheesecakes. The stainless steel exterior is brushed, the interior has a dark nonstick coating that's PFOA-free. As shown in the product image, they nest neatly for storage, though there's no included organizer — you'll want to stack them with a paper towel or cloth between to prevent scratching.

**How it performs**

I tested these with a classic vanilla layer cake, a dense pound cake, and a batch of mini cheesecakes. The nonstick surface is the real deal. I greased lightly with butter and flour — and honestly, the butter alone probably would've been enough. Cakes released cleanly with a simple run of a spatula around the edge and a flip. No crumbling, no stuck patches.

Heat distribution is where stainless steel shines. The pans heated evenly in my oven, with no dark rings or burnt edges on the cake bottoms. One buyer noted, "I've used these for six months and my cakes come out perfectly level every time." Another mentioned, "The smaller pans are perfect for individual desserts — my kids love making their own mini cakes."

The pans are lightweight — about half the weight of a comparable Wilton steel pan. That's good for handling, but it's also the source of my main complaint.

**Where it falls short**

These pans are thin. Not flimsy, but noticeably lighter than premium options. If you're baking at high heat (above 375°F) for a long time, there's a risk of warping. I noticed a slight bow in the 9-inch pan after a particularly long bake, though it flattened out once cooled. The manufacturer recommends hand washing and avoiding thermal shock — do not run cold water over a hot pan, or you'll risk permanent warping.

The nonstick coating is good but not indestructible. Metal utensils will scratch it, so stick to silicone or wood. One reviewer complained, "The coating started peeling after I put them in the dishwasher." That's user error, but it's worth flagging: these are hand-wash only, and the listing doesn't make that clear enough.

**Who should buy this**

This set is ideal for home bakers who want variety without buying individual pans. If you're just starting out, the 8 and 9-inch sizes will cover 90% of standard recipes. If you're more advanced, the smaller sizes are perfect for mini desserts, test batches, or multi-tier wedding cakes. It's also a great gift for someone who's recently discovered baking — they'll never outgrow this set.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Can these go in the dishwasher?*  
Technically yes, but they shouldn't. Dishwasher detergent and heat will degrade the nonstick coating over time. Hand wash with warm soapy water and dry immediately.

*Are they truly nonstick, or do I still need to grease?*  
They're nonstick, but a light greasing still helps with even browning and ensures clean release, especially for sticky cakes like pound cake or banana bread. A quick spray of baking spray is enough.

*Do the sizes match standard cake recipes?*  
Yes. The 8 and 9-inch pans fit standard boxed mixes and most from-scratch recipes. The 6-inch is perfect for smaller layer cakes.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a versatile set that covers every recipe from mini cheesecakes to tall layer cakes without spending a fortune. The nonstick release is reliable, heat distribution is even, and the size range is genuinely useful.

**Skip it if** you need heavy-duty pans for high-heat baking, prefer dishwasher-safe cookware, or you're a professional baker who needs thick, commercial-grade steel.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The E-far set delivers excellent value and solid performance. Just remember to hand wash, avoid thermal shock, and use silicone utensils. At this price point, it's hard to beat.
