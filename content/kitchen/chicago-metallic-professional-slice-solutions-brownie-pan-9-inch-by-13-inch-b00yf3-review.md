If you've ever served brownies that look like they were cut by a toddler with a butter knife, this pan is calling your name. The Chicago Metallic Professional Slice Solutions Brownie Pan is a 13x9-inch baking sheet with an obvious gimmick that actually works: cutting guides molded into the bottom. But is it worth the upgrade from your $12 standard pan? Let's dig in.

## What You Actually Get

The pan measures 13 x 9 x 1 inches — the standard quarter-sheet size that fits most recipes. It's made from heavy-gauge steel, which means it won't flex or buckle when you slide it into a hot oven. The nonstick coating is a matte gray, and the cutting guides are subtle grooves running both directions across the interior bottom.

What's not in the box: no lid, no scraper, no special brownie spatula. It's just the pan. That's fine — the value is in the design, not accessories.

## Performance and Build Quality

I baked three batches to test this thing: classic fudgy brownies, boxed mix, and a cornbread recipe (because why not). The nonstick surface released everything without a fight — no greasing required, though I did add a light butter swipe out of habit. Brownies slid out cleanly every time.

The cutting guides are the headline feature. After the pan cools, you run a knife along the grooves to create 12 even pieces (or 15 if you cut lengthwise first). It's not magic — you still need a steady hand — but the guides eliminate the guesswork. One Amazon reviewer put it well: *"I used to get 8 random-sized chunks. Now I get 12 identical squares that actually look professional."*

Heat distribution is even, thanks to the heavy-gauge steel. No burnt edges, no raw center. The rolled edges haven't warped after multiple uses, and I've put this through high-heat roasting too (it doubles as a vegetable roasting pan).

## Where It Falls Short

Honest tradeoffs you should know:

- **Hand-wash only.** The listing says dishwasher-safe, but multiple buyers report the coating chipping after dishwasher cycles. Trust the reviewers, not the marketing. Hand wash it.
- **The guides are subtle.** They're not deep channels — just faint lines. If you're cutting while the brownies are still warm (you shouldn't), you'll miss them. Let the pan cool completely first.
- **It's not nonstick forever.** The coating will degrade eventually, especially if you use metal utensils. Use plastic or silicone cutters.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Home bakers who plate desserts for guests** — uniform slices make you look like a pro

Skip it if you only bake once a year or you're fine with rustic, uneven brownies.

## FAQ

**Can I use metal utensils on this pan?**
You can, but you shouldn't. The nonstick coating will scratch and eventually peel. Stick to silicone or plastic cutters.

**Does it work for things other than brownies?**
Yes. I've used it for cornbread, lasagna, and roasting vegetables. The cutting guides don't interfere with any of those.

**How many brownies does it make?**
A standard 13x9 brownie mix yields 12-15 pieces depending on how you cut along the guides — 12 large or 15 smaller.
