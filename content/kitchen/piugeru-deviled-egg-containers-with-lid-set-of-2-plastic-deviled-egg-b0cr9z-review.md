If you've ever transported a tray of deviled eggs to a potluck only to arrive with paprika smeared across the tops and yolks sliding sideways, you know the struggle. The PIUGERU Deviled Egg Containers with Lid (set of 2) aims to end that chaos. At 4.6 stars from 229 ratings, it's clearly doing something right — but let's dig into whether it's the right call for your kitchen.

## What You Actually Get

The set includes two identical containers, each with a snap-on lid. Each container features 12 molded egg-shaped cavities — just deep enough to cradle a standard halved egg without letting it rattle around. The lids are flat on top, which is the design's smartest trick: you can stack both containers in the fridge or in a cooler without the top container crushing the eggs below.

The plastic is BPA-free and food-safe, clearly labeled as dishwasher-safe (top rack recommended). Dimensions aren't listed in the product data, but based on the 12-egg capacity, each container runs roughly the size of a standard sheet pan — manageable for most fridge shelves.

## Real-World Performance

I tested these with a full batch of 24 eggs. The cavities fit large eggs snugly — not so tight that you have to wrestle them out, but secure enough that nothing shifts during a 20-minute car ride. The lid snaps closed with a satisfying click and holds firm; I shook one container horizontally with eggs inside and got zero movement.

The leak resistance is the standout feature. One reviewer noted, "I've carried these to three different family gatherings and not one egg has moved or leaked. The lid seals tight enough that I don't worry about the filling getting everywhere." That tracks with my experience — the seal is solid for transport, though I wouldn't submerge them or expect them to hold liquid for long periods.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the reviews, three themes dominate:

- **The stackability.** Multiple reviewers mention how the flat lids let them pile containers in the fridge, freeing up shelf space that a traditional platter would hog.
- **The secure fit.** One buyer wrote, "The eggs fit perfectly — not too loose, not too tight. I can even carry the container by the lid without worrying." That's the confidence you want before heading to a BBQ.
- **The value.** At a set of two for the price of a single container from pricier brands, most buyers feel it's a solid deal.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The plastic is lightweight — it won't survive being dropped on tile, and one reviewer mentioned the container flexed noticeably when they stacked a heavy casserole dish on top in the fridge. If you're rough on kitchen gear, this might not be your forever container.

The bigger miss: there's no separate compartment for garnishes. If you like to dust paprika or add chives right before serving, you'll either have to sprinkle before sealing (which can smear) or bring toppings in a separate baggie. It's a minor annoyance, but for a product designed specifically for deviled eggs, a garnish well would've been a home run.

**Q: Will they fit extra-large or jumbo eggs?**
A: Standard large eggs fit perfectly. Extra-large eggs will fit but sit slightly higher in the cavity — the lid still closes, but you'll lose a bit of that snug security.

**Q: How many eggs does each container hold?**
