## Cuisinart DFP-14BKSY 14-Cup Food Processor Review

With over **21,000 ratings and a 4.6-star average**, the Cuisinart DFP-14BKSY is one of the most trusted food processors on Amazon. It's the sweet spot in Cuisinart's lineup: large enough for batch cooking but compact enough for everyday use.

We examined the feedback from thousands of real users to determine whether this food processor deserves its legendary reputation.

### Design and Build

The **black stainless steel finish** gives the DFP-14BKSY a professional look that blends seamlessly into any kitchen. The work bowl is clear Tritan plastic — shatter-resistant and BPA-free.

**Key specs:**
- **Capacity:** 14 cups (3.3 liters)
- **Motor:** 720 watts peak power
- **Bowl material:** Tritan copolyester
- **Blade material:** Stainless steel
- **Weight:** 12.5 lbs
- **Dishwasher safe:** Bowl and lid (top rack), but hand washing is recommended for blade longevity

The stainless steel blade assembly and discs feel substantial. The S-blade design creates a vortex that pulls ingredients down into the blade path for even processing.

### Performance

The DFP-14BKSY's motor is genuinely powerful. Almost every 5-star review mentions how quickly and evenly it processes food.

**Performance highlights from user reviews:**
- **Chopping:** Even dicing of onions, peppers, carrots in 5–10 seconds
- **Slicing:** Consistent, uniform slices — perfect for gratin dishes and salads
- **Shredding:** Fine shreds of cheese, cabbage, potatoes without mushing
- **Dough:** Handles pizza dough, pie crust, and bread dough up to 3-4 cups flour
- **Pureeing:** Smooth hummus, pesto, nut butters, and baby food

> *"Super fast and powerful with great results and very little wasted food."* — Verified 5-star review

The **quiet operation** is another commonly praised feature. Compared to other food processors in this class, the DFP-14BKSY runs with less whine and vibration.

### Ease of Use

The one significant learning curve: the instructions. Multiple reviewers note that the included manual is dense and the locking mechanism requires attention.

**Tips for first-time users:**
1. **Read the PDF manual** — it's available in the product description
2. **The bowl locks into the base** — must hear the click; it won't run otherwise
3. **Blades are sharp** — handle by the plastic hub only
4. **Don't overload** — work in batches for best results

Once you're familiar with the assembly, operation is straightforward: lock the bowl, insert the blade/disc, add ingredients, and pulse or run continuously.

### FAQ

#### Can the Cuisinart DFP-14BKSY handle ice?
The blade can crush ice for frozen drinks and cocktails. Pulse rather than running continuously to avoid overheating the motor.

#### Is the Cuisinart DFP-14BKSY dishwasher safe?
The bowl and lid are top-rack dishwasher safe, but hand washing the blades and discs is strongly recommended to maintain sharpness. The base is not dishwasher safe — wipe clean with a damp cloth.

#### How loud is this food processor?
Users consistently describe it as quieter than expected for this class. It produces a smooth hum rather than the high-pitched whine of smaller processors.

#### What is the difference between the DFP-14BKSY and the older DFP-14BC series?
The DFP-14BKSY (Black Stainless) has an updated finish and slightly improved sealing gasket. The mechanical operation, motor, and blade system are essentially the same proven design.
