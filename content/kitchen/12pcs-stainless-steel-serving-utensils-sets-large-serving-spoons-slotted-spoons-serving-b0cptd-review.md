**Where it falls short**

Honest talk: these aren't commercial-grade tools. Each piece weighs roughly 3.5 ounces, which feels light compared to a restaurant supply store's heavy-gauge utensils. If you're serving dense lasagna or carving a turkey, the slotted turner flexes more than I'd like. Use a dedicated carving fork for heavy lifting.

There are also no hanging loops on the handles. If your kitchen relies on a hook rack for utensil storage, you'll need to find drawer space instead. Several reviewers mentioned this as a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

The mirror finish, while attractive, shows fingerprints easily. You'll be wiping them down before guests arrive if presentation matters to you.
