## Performance and Real-World Use

I tested these in three scenarios: mixing two-part epoxy resin, stirring paint for a touch-up job, and measuring out concrete patch for a small driveway repair. Here's what stood out:

**The measurement markings matter.** Unlike many disposable cups that print flimsy labels that dissolve on contact, the LILYMICKY markings are molded directly into the plastic. They stayed legible even after aggressive stirring. The 20-ounce capacity hits a sweet spot—big enough for substantial resin pours or paint batches, compact enough to not waste material.

**The flexibility is a feature, not a flaw.** When you're mixing epoxy, that last 10% of material always clings to the bottom corners. Because these cups flex, you can squeeze and scrape every drop out. That's a small thing that saves real money when you're working with expensive resin.

**They hold up to chemical stress.** No softening, warping, or leaking during my epoxy test. The plastic handled the exothermic heat of curing resin without issue. Paint and concrete patch also behaved fine.

## Where It Falls Short

**No pour spout.** This is the biggest design miss. Pouring from a flat-rimmed cup gets messy, especially with thicker materials like resin or joint compound. You'll want a stir stick or spatula to guide the flow, or you'll be wiping up drips.

**Single-use environmental cost.** These are disposable, and 20 cups in one order means 20 cups in the landfill. For occasional DIYers, that's manageable. For daily users, look into reusable silicone options instead—though those cost 10x more.

**They're not food-grade.** Don't use these for cooking. The plastic isn't rated for food contact, and the smell during my tests (slight chemical odor, especially with warm materials) confirms they're for workshop use only.

**Q: Are these cups microwave-safe?**
A: No. They're plastic, not food-grade, and will warp or melt. Use them for paints, resins, and compounds only.

**Q: Can I reuse them after cleaning?**
A: Technically yes—they're sturdy enough for multiple uses if you wipe them out quickly. But they're designed for single-use, and repeated chemical exposure will degrade the plastic.

**Q: Do the measurement lines wash off?**
A: No. They're molded into the plastic, not printed. They won't fade or dissolve.
