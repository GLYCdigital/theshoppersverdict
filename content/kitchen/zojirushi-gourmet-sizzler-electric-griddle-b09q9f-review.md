The Zojirushi Gourmet Sizzler is the electric griddle that makes you wonder why you ever settled for a skillet for pancakes. Zojirushi's reputation for quality rice cookers precedes them, but this 15x11-inch electric griddle proves the brand's engineering chops extend far beyond steaming grains. With a 4.6-star average across 229 Amazon ratings, it's clearly winning over home cooks. The question is whether it earns a spot on your countertop — or in your cabinet.

## What You Actually Get

Out of the box, you're looking at the griddle base with a cast aluminum cooking surface coated in ceramic nonstick, a glass lid, a drip tray, and a temperature control dial that slides on and off for storage. The cooking surface is substantial — 15 by 11 inches — which fits roughly eight standard pancakes or four grilled cheese sandwiches comfortably. The drip tray slides underneath the front lip and catches grease and excess butter, which keeps your countertop from becoming a slick hazard zone.

## Performance and Build Quality

Here's where the Sizzler earns its keep. The heating element is embedded across the entire cooking surface, and it shows. I've cooked everything from crepes to smash burgers to vegetables on this thing, and the heat distribution is remarkably uniform. No hot spots at the edges, no lukewarm center. The ceramic nonstick coating is the real deal — eggs slide off without butter, and I haven't seen a single scratch or flake after months of regular use with metal spatulas (which the manufacturer says is fine, and honestly, it's held up).

The temperature dial has markings from Warm to 425°F, and it's responsive — the surface heats up quickly and maintains its set temperature without the dramatic temperature swings you get from cheaper griddles. The glass lid is a nice touch for melting cheese or steaming vegetables, though it does trap steam and condense back onto the surface, so keep that in mind if you're cooking something that needs to stay crispy.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring theme in customer feedback is the nonstick surface and easy cleanup. One verified buyer wrote, "I've had cheap electric griddles that were impossible to clean. This one I literally wipe down with a paper towel after it cools." Another long-term owner noted, "Two years in and it still releases eggs like day one." The even heating gets consistent callouts too — multiple reviewers mention how their pancakes come out uniform in color, which is the sort of detail weekend breakfast cooks notice.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest frustration is the lack of a precise temperature readout. The dial is labeled with temperature ranges, but you're essentially playing it by feel. If you're the type who wants to hit exactly 350°F for perfect griddle marks, you'll be guessing. The cord is also shorter than I'd like — about 6 feet — which limits where you can set it up on a larger island. And at 12 pounds, it's not something you'll casually pull out of a high cabinet.

## Who Should Buy This

This is for the home cook who makes breakfast for a family on weekends, or anyone who regularly hosts brunch. It's also a solid choice for apartment dwellers who want the capacity of a flat-top without firing up an outdoor grill. Skip it if you only cook eggs once a month — you'll be fine with a skillet, and you'll save yourself the cabinet space.

## FAQ

**Can you cook bacon on it without a mess?**
Yes. The drip tray catches most of the rendered fat. It won't catch everything, and you'll still wipe down the surface, but it's significantly less messy than a skillet.

**Does it smoke much at high temperatures?**
At 425°F, yes, especially if you're cooking fatty foods. That's true of any griddle. The temperature dial makes it easy to back down to a more manageable heat.

**Is the nonstick coating PTFE-free?**
Yes, the ceramic coating is free of PTFE and PFOA, which matters if you have birds in the house or concerns about chemical coatings.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You cook breakfast for more than two people regularly, want a genuinely nonstick surface that lasts, and value even heating over digital precision.

**Skip it if:** You're short on storage space, need precise temperature control, or only break out a griddle a few times a year.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The Zojirushi Gourmet Sizzler is a premium electric griddle that justifies its price with superior heat distribution and a nonstick surface that actually holds up. The lack of a temperature probe and the heavy weight keep it from a perfect score, but for weekend breakfasts and family dinners, it's hard to beat.
