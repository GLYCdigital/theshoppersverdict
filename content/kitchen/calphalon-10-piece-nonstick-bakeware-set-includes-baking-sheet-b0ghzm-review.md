# Calphalon 10-Piece Nonstick Bakeware Set Review: Is This the Last Bakeware Set You'll Buy?

If you're tired of prying stuck muffins out of warped pans with a butter knife, the Calphalon 10-Piece Nonstick Bakeware Set deserves a hard look. With a 4.7-star average across 235 Amazon ratings, this set has clearly earned its reputation. But here's the real question: is it actually worth upgrading your current mismatched collection, or is this just another shiny set that'll end up scratched and sad in a year?

I tested this set across multiple baking sessions — cookies, sheet cakes, banana bread, and a full batch of muffins — to give you the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The set includes 10 pieces: two baking sheets (one large, one medium), two round cake pans (8-inch and 9-inch), a 9x5-inch loaf pan, a 12-cup muffin pan, an 8x8-inch square pan, a 9x13-inch rectangular pan, and two cooling racks. That's the full rotation for most home bakers — you'll rarely reach for anything else.

The construction is the standout feature. These pans use heavy-gauge carbon steel, which is noticeably thicker than the flimsy stuff you'll find at discount stores. When you hold one, it has real heft — the loaf pan weighs about a pound, and the sheet pans don't flex when you pick them up with one hand.

## Performance: Where It Shines

The three-layer nonstick coating is genuinely effective. I baked chocolate chip cookies without any greasing — they slid off the sheet pan with zero residue. The muffin pan released a full batch of blueberry muffins cleanly. No scraping, no torn tops, no chunks left behind.

The dark coating does absorb heat faster than lighter pans. That's great for browning cookies and bread crusts, but it means you should drop your oven temperature by about 25°F compared to what recipes specify for shiny pans. One reviewer noted, *"I burned my first batch of cookies because I forgot to adjust the temperature — after that, perfect results every time."* That's a real learning curve, but one you'll adapt to quickly.

The cooling racks are a thoughtful inclusion. They're sturdy and elevate baked goods properly so air circulates underneath — no soggy bottoms.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 235 ratings, several themes emerge. The durability gets repeated shout-outs — one buyer wrote, *"I've had cheap bakeware warp in the first month. These have been through six months of weekly use and they're still flat as the day I got them."* Another praised the release factor: *"I don't even grease my pans anymore. Everything just pops out."*

The value angle comes up frequently too. Buyers note that buying these pieces individually would cost significantly more, and the fact that they all match and nest together is a bonus for organized kitchens.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest — there are tradeoffs. The hand-wash requirement is real. The care instructions explicitly state dishwasher use will degrade the nonstick coating over time. If you're someone who throws everything in the dishwasher, this set will disappoint you within a year. Several reviewers confirmed this: *"I ignored the hand-wash warning and now my muffin pan is sticking after three months."*

The pans don't have rolled edges — they're flat with squared corners. That looks clean but makes them slightly harder to grip with oven mitts, especially the larger sheet pans.

Also, there's no storage solution included. The pans nest together, but there's no divider or organizer, so you'll need cabinet space or a dedicated drawer.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a regular home baker who wants one reliable set that covers all the basics. If you bake weekly — cookies, bread, cakes, brownies — this set replaces your entire collection with quality pieces that'll last years with proper care.

**Skip it if:** You refuse to hand-wash cookware, or you bake so rarely that a $20 basic set from the grocery store covers your needs. Also skip if you need specialty sizes — there's no 9-inch springform or individual tart pans here.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the nonstick coating contain PFOA?**
No. Calphalon's nonstick coating is PFOA-free and lead-free, so it's safe for everyday cooking and baking.

**Q: Can these go in the oven at high temperatures?**
Yes, up to 450°F. They're safe for most baking recipes, though you'll rarely need to push that limit.

**Q: How do I clean them properly?**
Hand-wash with warm, soapy water and a soft sponge. Avoid metal utensils and abrasive scrubbers — use silicone, wood, or nylon tools to protect the coating.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The Calphalon 10-Piece Nonstick Bakeware Set is the rare kitchen purchase that delivers exactly what it promises. The heavy-gauge build resists warping, the nonstick coating genuinely releases baked goods, and you get everything you need in one cohesive set. The hand-wash requirement and heat-absorption quirk are the only real drawbacks, and both are manageable.

**Buy it if** you bake regularly and want to stop fighting your pans. **Skip it if** you can't commit to hand-washing or you rarely use your oven. For everyone else, this is the set that'll make you wonder why you waited so long to upgrade.
