## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**Weight.** At over 11 lbs. empty, this is a two-hand operation. The pumpkin ridges don't provide as secure a grip as traditional loop handles, especially when you're wearing oven mitts. If you have arthritis or wrist issues, this is a genuine concern.

**Price premium.** You're paying a significant markup over a comparable 4 qt. round cocotte for the figural design. There's no performance advantage — you're buying the aesthetic, and you should know that going in.

**Storage challenges.** The pumpkin shape doesn't nest with other pots. It takes up disproportionate cabinet space, and the lid doesn't stack flat. Plan for dedicated shelf space.
