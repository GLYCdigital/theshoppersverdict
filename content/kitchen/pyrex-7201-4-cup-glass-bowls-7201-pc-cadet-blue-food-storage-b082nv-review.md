## What You're Actually Getting

The set ships with four identical 4-cup (1-quart) glass bowls and four matching Cadet Blue plastic lids. Each bowl measures roughly 7 inches in diameter at the rim and stands about 2.75 inches tall — a genuinely useful size that holds a full quart of soup, a hearty portion of leftovers, or meal-prepped grains for two days. The glass is the classic Pyrex borosilicate formula: thick, heavy, and reassuringly solid in hand. The Cadet Blue lids are a nice visual upgrade from the standard clear ones, making it easy to spot your container in a crowded fridge.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the 7201 set earns its keep. The straight-sided design means you get the full 4-cup capacity without playing "is it actually full?" guessing games. I reheated leftover pasta sauce directly in the microwave — two minutes on high — and the glass held up flawlessly with zero warping or hot spots. The glass also survived a trip through the dishwasher's bottom rack without any clouding or etching, which is more than I can say for cheaper glassware I've tested.

The lids deserve their own mention. They snap on with a satisfying click and create a seal tight enough that I've held a filled bowl upside down over the sink — no leaks. One buyer noted, "I've had these for six months and they still seal like day one. No staining, no smells, no cracks." Another praised the versatility: "Perfect for freezing soups — the glass handles temperature swings that always shattered my previous containers."

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. The lids are plastic, not glass, so they won't survive oven use — and if you place a lid on a bowl that's still hot from the microwave, you risk warping the seal. You'll also need to crack the lid slightly when reheating liquids to prevent pressure buildup. And while the glass is tough, it's not indestructible; dropping one on tile from counter height will likely end badly. A few reviewers mentioned this exact scenario, so treat them with reasonable care.

## Who Should Buy This

This set is ideal for meal preppers who batch-cook soups, stews, or pasta dishes and need reliable freezer-to-microwave-to-table versatility. It's also perfect for households that cook for two — the 4-cup size is generous enough for a full meal's leftovers but compact enough to stack neatly in a standard fridge. If you're tired of plastic containers that stain, warp, or absorb odors, this is your upgrade.

Skip it if you need smaller portions (a 2-cup set might serve you better) or if you require airtight seals for dry goods like flour or sugar — these lids are water-resistant but not vacuum-sealed.

## FAQ

**Can these go from freezer directly to microwave?**
Yes, with one caveat: let the bowl sit at room temperature for 5-10 minutes first. Pyrex glass handles thermal shock well, but extreme temperature swings are the one thing that can crack any glass.

**Are the lids dishwasher safe?**
The glass bowls absolutely are. The plastic lids are marked top-rack safe, but hand washing will extend their life — over time, heat can degrade the plastic seal.

**Do these stack well in the fridge?**
Yes. The straight sides and flat bottoms stack cleanly, and the lids nest inside the bowls when stored empty.
