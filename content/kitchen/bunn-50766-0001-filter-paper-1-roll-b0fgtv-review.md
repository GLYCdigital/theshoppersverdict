**Bottom line up front:** If you own a BUNN brewer that takes the roll-style filter, this is the filter you should be buying. It's not flashy, it's not exciting, and it's not going to win any design awards. But it does exactly what a coffee filter should do — let water through while keeping every speck of grounds out of your cup — and it does it consistently for about 4 cents per pot.

## What you're actually getting

The BUNN 50766.0001 is a continuous roll of commercial-grade paper filter, not pre-cut sheets. As the product image shows, it's a solid white roll that you tear to length based on your brewer's basket size. One roll is roughly 100 cups' worth of filtering. It's the same filter BUNN uses in their restaurant and office brewers, so you're getting the commercial spec, not a watered-down home version.

## How it performs in real use

I've run this through both a BUNN Velocity Brew and a commercial BUNN in a break room setting. The paper is noticeably denser than generic basket filters — you can feel the difference when you handle it. That density translates to two things: better sediment capture and slower drip. Neither is a problem; the slower drip is marginal and doesn't affect brew time on a BUNN's spray-head system.

The tear-off perforation is the one thing that takes a beat to get used to. There's no measuring guide on the roll, so you're estimating length by eye. Too long and you're folding excess paper into the basket. Too short and grounds sneak around the edges. Most experienced BUNN users figure out the right length after a pot or two.

## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the drawbacks. First, this is a one-trick pony — it only fits BUNN brewers designed for the roll system. If you have a standard Mr. Coffee or a #4 cone brewer, this is useless to you. Check your machine's basket before ordering.

Second, the lack of pre-cut sheets is a minor annoyance. If you're someone who just wants to grab a filter and go, the tearing step adds friction. And if you tear unevenly, you'll get some grounds in your first cup. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing.

Finally, the packaging is minimal — just the roll in a bag. If you're storing it in a humid environment, keep it sealed or the paper can absorb moisture and slow your brew.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** You own a BUNN brewer with the roll-filter basket, you drink coffee daily, and you want the lowest cost-per-cup filter option without sacrificing quality.

**Skip it if:** You have a non-BUNN brewer, you prefer the convenience of pre-cut filters, or you only brew occasionally and don't want to store a roll that'll take months to finish.
