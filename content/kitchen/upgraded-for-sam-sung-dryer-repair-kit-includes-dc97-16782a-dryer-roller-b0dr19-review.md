## What's actually in the box

This kit is built around the DC97-16782A drum roller — the exact OEM part number for a huge range of Samsung dryers (think DV45H, DV50K, DV42H series and similar front-load electric and gas models). You get the roller itself, a new axle sleeve, and all the mounting hardware: screws, washers, and clips. No belt, no idler pulley, no felt seals — just the roller and what's needed to bolt it in.

The "upgraded" claim refers to the axle sleeve and bearing material. Samsung's original roller wears out because the plastic sleeve degrades under heat and friction. This kit uses a reinforced sleeve that's designed to hold up longer. As shown in the product image, the roller has a solid steel core with a thick rubber outer ring — that's what actually touches the drum and keeps it spinning smoothly.

## Real-world performance

Buyers consistently report that this kit fixes the problem. One reviewer wrote, "My dryer sounded like a jet engine taking off. Replaced this roller in 20 minutes and it's whisper quiet now." Another noted, "Fit perfectly on my DV45H7000EW. No shaving, no forcing — just bolted right up."

## Where it falls short

The tighter bearing sleeve can be a minor frustration. The axle needs a firm, straight push to seat fully — if you're gentle, it'll feel like it's not going in. Use a rubber mallet or a block of wood and tap it home. It's a 30-second annoyance, not a dealbreaker.
