## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and these have two honest weaknesses:

**The lids are a pain to fully clean.** The silicone gasket is removable, but it's a tight fit. If you're not diligent about popping it out and washing separately, you'll get grime buildup in the groove. It's manageable, but it's extra effort.

**No graduated markings.** The 8oz and 16oz sizes are clearly labeled on the bottom, but there are no measurement lines on the sides. If you meal-prep and need precise portion control, you'll still need a measuring cup.

**Price transparency issue.** The listing price fluctuates. Some buyers reported paying $15, others $22. It's a quality product, but don't pay above $20 — wait for a drop.
