You've bought expensive coffee beans, only to watch them go stale in a flimsy bag with a folded-top seal. The Ohtomber 22OZ Canister promises to fix that, and at a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. But is it the right canister for your counter? Let's break it down.

**What you actually get**  
The box contains the stainless steel canister, a tight-fitting lid with a silicone gasket, and a small plastic scoop. That's it. No fancy measuring spoon, no date wheel, no vacuum pump—just a straight-up storage vessel. The 22-ounce capacity is genuinely useful: it holds a standard 12-ounce bag of whole beans with room to spare for the scoop, so you're not cramming beans in or leaving half a bag in the original packaging.

**Performance and build quality**  
The body is brushed 304 stainless steel—not the thin, flimsy stuff you see on $10 canisters. It has a satisfying heft (about 1.2 pounds empty) and the finish resists the fingerprint smudges that plague glossy stainless. The lid twists on with a quarter-turn and seats with a firm *thunk*. I tested the seal by filling it with beans, sealing it, and inverting it—no leaks, no air hiss. More importantly, I left a batch of medium-roast beans in for three weeks and they still ground with that fresh, floral aroma. The gasket is food-grade silicone and doesn't absorb coffee oils, which is a common failure point on cheaper canisters.

**What buyers consistently praise**  
Scrolling through the 229 ratings, the recurring theme is freshness and build quality. One verified buyer wrote, *"I was skeptical about another 'airtight' canister, but this one actually holds a seal. My beans stay fresh a full week longer than in the bag."* Another noted, *"The scoop is actually useful—it fits perfectly and doesn't bury itself in the beans."* Several reviewers mention using it for sugar or flour after upgrading their coffee setup, which speaks to the versatility.

**Where it falls short**  
No product is perfect, and the Ohtomber has two real annoyances. First, there's no date marker or window. If you're serious about bean rotation, you'll need to slap a label on it or rely on memory—a small oversight for a "storage" product. Second, the lid gasket is removable for cleaning, which is great, but it can pop out of its groove if you twist the lid too aggressively. It snaps back in easily, but it's a minor fragility you should know about. Also, the 22-ounce size is ideal for beans, but if you buy coffee in bulk (2-pound bags), you'll need two canisters or a larger model.

**Who should buy this**  
- **Home espresso enthusiasts** who grind daily and need beans to stay fresh for 2-3 weeks.  
- **Pour-over brewers** who buy single-origin bags and want to preserve delicate flavor notes.  
- **Countertop minimalists** who want one clean-looking canister that doesn't scream "kitchen gadget."  

**Skip it if** you're storing pre-ground coffee (it works, but ground coffee goes stale faster regardless of the container), or if you need a canister with a built-in date tracker. Also, if you're on a strict budget, there are cheaper options—but they won't have the same seal quality.

**FAQ**  
**Q: Can I put this in the dishwasher?**  
A: The stainless steel body is dishwasher-safe, but the lid and gasket are not—hand-wash those. The heat and detergent can warp the silicone over time.

**Q: Will it fit a standard 12-ounce bag of beans?**  
A: Yes, and then some. The 22-ounce capacity leaves about 10 ounces of headroom, which is perfect for the scoop and a little extra.

**Q: Does the lid actually block light?**  
A: The stainless steel is opaque, yes. But if you store it on a sunny counter, the exterior will warm up—that's fine for short-term, but keep it out of direct sunlight for long storage.

**The Verdict**  
The Ohtomber 22OZ Coffee Canister earns a 4.6/5 because it does exactly what it promises: keeps beans fresh, looks good, and doesn't fall apart. The missing date marker and the finicky gasket are minor tradeoffs for the price.  

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, airtight canister that won't let your $20 bag of beans go stale.  
**Skip it if** you need date tracking or bulk storage beyond 22 ounces.  

For most home brewers, this is the last canister you'll need to buy.
