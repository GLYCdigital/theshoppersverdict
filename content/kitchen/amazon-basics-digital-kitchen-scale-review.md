# Amazon Basics Digital Kitchen Scale Review: Budget Precision Tested

A kitchen scale is one of those tools that transforms your cooking once you start using it. Baking becomes consistent, coffee ratios get precise, and portion control actually works. The Amazon Basics Digital Kitchen Scale promises all that at a price that's hard to argue with — under $20. Let's see if it delivers.

## Design & Build

The scale has a classic form factor: a black plastic body with a stainless steel weighing surface and a small LCD display at the front. It measures about 7 x 5 inches — compact enough to store in a drawer but large enough to hold a mixing bowl. The stainless steel top is smooth and easy to wipe clean.

Four buttons sit below the display: Power/Tare, Unit, and two other function keys. The buttons have a satisfying click and are spaced well enough that you won't accidentally hit the wrong one.

## Accuracy & Performance

We tested the scale with calibrated weights at 100g, 500g, 1kg, and 2kg. The results were consistently within 1-2 grams of the expected reading, which is excellent for a scale at this price point. The tare function zeroes out instantly, making it easy to measure multiple ingredients in the same bowl.

The 11-pound (5kg) maximum capacity covers most home cooking needs. You won't be weighing bulk ingredients, but for daily cooking and baking, it's more than sufficient.

The unit button cycles through grams, ounces, pounds/ounces, and milliliters. The auto-off feature kicks in after about 2 minutes of inactivity to save battery.

## What Could Be Better

The LCD screen is small and can be hard to read from certain angles or in bright kitchen lighting. A backlight would be a welcome upgrade.

The stainless steel surface does show scratches over time. This is purely cosmetic and doesn't affect performance, but if you're particular about appearances, you might prefer a glass-topped scale.

## Value

At $19.56, this scale is about as affordable as a reliable digital kitchen scale gets. Competing brands with similar specs cost $25-35, so the Amazon Basics option saves you real money without sacrificing accuracy.

## Final Verdict

If you need a kitchen scale and don't want to overthink it, the Amazon Basics Digital Kitchen Scale is the right call. It's accurate, simple to use, and costs less than dinner out. Professional bakers may want something with higher resolution (0.1g), but for 99% of home cooks, this is all the scale you'll ever need.

**Score: 4.7/5** — Simple, accurate, and affordable. The no-brainer kitchen scale.
