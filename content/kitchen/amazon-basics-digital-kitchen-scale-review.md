The Amazon Basics Digital Kitchen Scale has over 115,000 reviews and a 4.7-star rating. When 115,000 people agree on a product that costs under $10, it's worth paying attention. This is the budget champion of kitchen scales — and for most home cooks, it's all the scale you'll ever need.

## The Value Equation

First, let's address the price. At $9.89, this scale is competing with products that cost two to three times more and deliver essentially the same performance. It's Amazon Basics doing what Amazon Basics does best: stripping away frills and delivering the core function at a price that makes competitors look overpriced.

The scale measures up to 11 pounds in grams, ounces, pounds, and milliliters. The LCD display is clear and responsive. The tare function zeros out containers with a single press. It runs on included batteries. For a ten-dollar bill, you're getting a fully functional digital scale that works out of the box.

## Ease of Use

This is where the scale shines. One reviewer who's been using it for three months says it's "easy to use and clean, has different units, and is very compact and small." Another calls it "accurate, simple, and great value" — three words that perfectly describe the product.

The tare function is seamless. Place a bowl on the scale, press tare, add ingredients. The scale responds instantly with clear readings. Switching between measurement units is straightforward. There's no app pairing, no Bluetooth, no learning curve. It just works.

Multiple reviewers highlight the compact design. At roughly 8 inches by 6 inches, it slides into a drawer or sits on a shelf without claiming permanent counter space. The tempered glass surface wipes clean easily.

## Accuracy for Home Use

For standard kitchen tasks — baking, meal prep, portion control — the accuracy is excellent. Reviewers consistently report consistent readings across multiple measurements, even with heavier loads near the 11-pound limit.

The limitation is the 1-gram resolution. This scale rounds to whole grams, meaning you won't get precise 0.1g or 0.5g measurements. For most home cooking, that's irrelevant. For bread bakers weighing yeast or precision recipe developers, it's a meaningful gap. One reviewer making homemade sausage says "it does not read 1/10th of grams" and notes this is a problem for their specific spice mixes.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the whole-gram limitation, there are three common complaints. The surface is relatively small — large mixing bowls can overhang, which affects stability. There's no backlight, so reading the display in dim kitchen light requires angling toward a light source. And the glass surface shows fingerprints and smudges quickly.

None of these are dealbreakers at this price point. They're the trade-offs you accept for spending less than a lunch out.

## Who Should Buy

**Buy it if:** You're getting your first kitchen scale, replacing a broken one on a budget, or don't need sub-gram precision. This is the perfect scale for baking, meal prep, and everyday cooking.

**Skip it if:** You need 0.1g precision for specialty recipes or you regularly weigh large batches in oversized bowls. Look at the Etekcity or OXO lines for those use cases.

## The Bottom Line

The Amazon Basics Digital Kitchen Scale is a masterclass in doing the simple things well at a low price. It's accurate, easy to use, and compact. The 1-gram resolution and small surface are genuine limitations, but they only matter if your cooking demands more. For everyone else, this is the kitchen scale to buy.

**Verdict: 4.6/5** — The best value in kitchen scales, period. Under $10 for reliable accuracy that most home cooks will never outgrow.
