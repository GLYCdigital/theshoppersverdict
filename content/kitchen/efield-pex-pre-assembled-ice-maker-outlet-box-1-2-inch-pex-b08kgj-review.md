## What You Actually Get

The EFIELD arrives as a single integrated unit: a white plastic outlet box, a brass quarter-turn shut-off valve, and two 1/2-inch Pex F (female) connections already attached. The box measures roughly 4.5 inches wide by 7 inches tall — standard 2-gang dimensions — so it fits existing rough-in openings if you're replacing an old setup. Mounting screws are included, and the pre-attached bracket lets you fix it directly to a stud or plywood backing.

The Pex connections are the headline feature. They're expansion-style fittings compatible with both Pex-A (Uponor/Wirsbo) and Pex-B (Viega, Sioux Chief) systems. You don't need a separate adapter or crimp ring — slide your tubing in, secure it, and you're done.

Installation is straightforward for anyone comfortable with Pex.
