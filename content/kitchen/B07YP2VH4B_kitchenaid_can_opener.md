The KitchenAid Classic Can Opener is one of those products where the reviews tell an almost unbelievable story: thousands of users reporting a decade of flawless service from a $13 kitchen tool. With over 90,000 reviews and a 4.6-star average, the consensus is overwhelming.

## Why It Lasts

The cutting wheel is the key. The stainless steel blade is razor-sharp and cuts through lids with minimal effort, leaving a smooth edge rather than the jagged, dangerous lip you get from cheap openers. The large turning knob is comfortable to grip, and the ergonomic handles provide excellent leverage. One reviewer whose previous can opener stopped cutting after two years came back to this one because their original lasted about ten years.

The double-duty bottle opener is a nice bonus — it's built into the handles and works well for the occasional beer or soda.

## Daily Use

Users consistently praise how smoothly this opener works. It attaches snugly to the can top, cuts cleanly all the way around, and disengages without fuss. The larger size means it's easy to grip and turn, even for people with arthritis or limited hand strength.

One reviewer who bought it on a friend's recommendation says it "cuts the top all the way off every can" and "looks so rugged that I think my grandchildren will be using it someday."

## The Only Real Weakness

There's one consistent complaint, and it's entirely user-dependent: it will rust if you put it in the dishwasher. The instructions are clear — hand wash and dry immediately — but people ignore them and then leave one-star reviews. One reviewer admits their unit broke within a year because they "got lazy and just threw it in the dishwasher." Follow the instructions and this opener will likely outlast your other kitchen tools.

## The Bottom Line

At $13.69, the KitchenAid Classic Can Opener is the definition of buy-it-for-life value. The cutting performance is excellent from day one and stays that way, the ergonomics are comfortable, and the build quality is genuinely durable. Just hand wash it and it'll be the last can opener you buy for a very long time.

**Verdict: 4.5/5** — A kitchen classic that earns every one of its 90,000+ positive reviews. Sharp, comfortable, and built to last. Just keep it out of the dishwasher.
