If you've ever replaced a range hood, you know the pain: you buy a backsplash panel, and it doesn't line up with the mounting holes. Or the included hardware is garbage. Or the finish looks nothing like the product photo. This 24" x 30" reversible metal backsplash from a third-party manufacturer aims to eliminate all three headaches. Here's what I found after digging through 215 ratings and the spec sheet.

**What's in the box**

You get the metal panel itself, the matching mounting screws, and — critically — a design that's reversible. That means one side has one finish, the other side has a different finish. Flip it around and you've got a completely different look without buying a second panel. Most buyers report the two finishes are distinct enough to matter: one is typically a brushed or matte metal, the other a polished or patterned option. As one verified buyer put it, *"I wasn't sure which finish I wanted, so I just put the one I liked less facing out first, tried it for a week, then flipped it. Two looks, one price."* That's a genuinely practical feature, not a gimmick.

The screws are a small detail that deserves mention. Anyone who's mounted a backsplash knows that the wrong screw length or thread pitch means stripped holes and a wobbly panel. These match the Broan-NuTone mounting pattern precisely. That's the "Comp" in the title — compatibility — and it's the core selling point.

**Performance and build quality**

The panel is 24 inches wide by 30 inches tall, which covers the standard space behind a range. It's made of a metal that's rigid enough not to flex when you're pressing it against the wall, but thin enough to cut with tin snips if you absolutely must modify it. However, I'd advise against that unless you have no other option — the edges won't be finished after you cut, and you'll be left with a potentially sharp lip.

In real-world use, the finish holds up well against heat and grease splatter. Wipe it down with a damp cloth and it comes clean. The flip side is that it's a fingerprint magnet. Darker finishes especially will show every touch. One reviewer noted, *"I love how it looks, but I'm wiping it every other day. If you hate cleaning, buy a different finish."* That's honest feedback worth heeding.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest limitation is the size and compatibility. It's made for Broan-NuTone models specifically. If you own a different brand, you're gambling that the 24" x 30" dimensions happen to match your hood's spacing. Measure twice, order once. Also, while the reversible feature is smart, you can't see both finishes before you install it — you're committing to whichever side you mount first. If you hate both, you're returning it.

There's also no mention of a protective coating on the metal. Over time, especially in humid kitchens, some buyers have reported minor discoloration around the edges. It's not universal, but it's a risk with an uncoated metal surface.

**Who should buy this**

If you're replacing a Broan-NuTone range hood or installing one fresh, this is a no-brainer. The included screws alone save you a trip to the hardware store. If you're on the fence about finish, the reversible design gives you a way to test both looks without buying two panels. If you're a renter who wants a temporary upgrade without drilling new holes, the pre-aligned mounting points make installation clean and easy to reverse.

Skip it if you own a non-Broan-NuTone hood and aren't confident about measurements, or if you have a high-traffic kitchen where a fingerprint-averse surface matters more than price.

**FAQ**

**Can I paint this backsplash if I don't like either finish?**
Technically yes, but you'll need to scuff the surface and use a bonding primer designed for metal. It adds work and the paint may chip near heat sources. Better to pick a finish you like from the start.

**Will this fit a standard 30-inch range?**
The panel is 24 inches wide, which covers the gap between the range and the hood. For a 30-inch range, you'll have about 3 inches of exposed wall on each side. That's normal for this style of backsplash.

**Is the installation really screw-only, or do I need adhesive too?**
The screws are designed to hold it securely. Some buyers add a thin bead of silicone adhesive along the top edge to prevent any rattling, but it's not required. Clean the wall first, and the screws alone should hold it flat.
