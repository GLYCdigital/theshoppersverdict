# Zojirushi Maker607 Coffee Maker Review: The Understated Workhorse

Most coffee makers in this price range scream for attention with touchscreens, app connectivity, and more knobs than a cockpit. The Zojirushi Maker607 takes the opposite approach — and honestly, that's why it's worth your attention. This is a machine that focuses on the fundamentals: consistent extraction, durable construction, and zero plastic in the brew path.

Zojirushi built its reputation on rice cookers, but they've applied the same engineering philosophy here. The result is a 4.3-star rated brewer (215 ratings) that does exactly what it promises — no more, no less.

## What You Actually Get

Open the box and you'll find the 10-cup brewer, a stainless steel thermal carafe, a stainless steel brew basket, and a permanent filter. That last part is a genuine money-saver — you'll never buy paper filters again.

The unit measures roughly 10 inches wide by 9 inches deep, which puts it well under most upper cabinets. The body is brushed stainless steel with minimal branding. It looks like a professional piece of equipment, not a plastic gadget.

## Performance and Build Quality

The Maker607 uses a drip-shower head that distributes water evenly over the coffee grounds. In practice, this means you get a more balanced extraction than you'd expect from a machine at this level. The water heats to a proper 195-205°F range — critical for pulling out the good flavors without the bitter over-extraction you get from cheaper brewers.

The thermal carafe deserves special mention. It's double-walled stainless steel, which keeps coffee hot for hours without a heating plate. That's a huge advantage over glass carafes — no burnt, bitter coffee sitting on a burner, and no risk of shattering a glass pot in the sink.

One buyer put it this way: *"This is the first coffee maker I've owned that doesn't make my coffee taste like plastic. The stainless steel brew basket and carafe make a real difference."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides.

First, there's no programmable timer. If you want coffee waiting for you when you stumble into the kitchen at 6 AM, you'll need to set your alarm earlier and hit the button yourself. For a machine at this price point, that omission is noticeable.

Second, the carafe lid is a two-hand operation. You need one hand on the pot and one on the lid while pouring. When the carafe is full, attempting a one-handed pour risks a hot coffee spill. It's a small design flaw, but one you'll encounter every single morning.

Third, the "10-cup" capacity is measured in Zojirushi's 5-ounce cups, not the standard 8-ounce coffee cup. You're realistically getting about 6 regular mugs per full brew. This is common in the industry, but worth knowing before you buy.

## Who Should Buy This

The Maker607 is perfect for the home brewer who values clean taste and durability over convenience features. If you drink coffee black, you'll appreciate the lack of plastic in the brew path. If you've gone through three cheap coffee makers in five years, the Zojirushi's build quality will likely be your last purchase for a decade.

Skip this if you need programmable brewing, brew single cups, or prefer a glass carafe you can see through. Also skip it if you're left-handed — the pour spout and lid design favor right-hand use.

## FAQs

**Does the Maker607 use paper filters?**
No. It comes with a permanent stainless steel filter. You can use paper filters if you prefer, but the permanent one works well and saves money long-term.

**How long does the thermal carafe keep coffee hot?**
Expect 4-6 hours of genuinely hot coffee. After that, it's warm rather than hot. That's typical for insulated carafes.

**Is this machine easy to clean?**
The brew basket and carafe are dishwasher safe. The reservoir is accessible and can be wiped out, though it's not removable.

## The Verdict: 4.2/5

**Buy it if:** You want a durable, plastic-free brewer with excellent temperature control and don't need programmable features.

**Skip it if:** You require a timer, want a see-through carafe, or pour one-handed with a full pot.

The Zojirushi Maker607 isn't flashy. But it brews excellent coffee, holds temperature better than almost anything at this price, and will likely outlast two or three cheaper machines. For the right buyer, that's a solid trade.
