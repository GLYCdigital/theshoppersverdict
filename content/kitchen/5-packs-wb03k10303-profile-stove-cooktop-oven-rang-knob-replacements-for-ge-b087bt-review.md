## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the flaws. The white indicator line is painted, not molded into the plastic. On a heavily used cooktop knob, that line will start fading after 6–12 months. It won't disappear entirely, but it'll look worn. Cosmetic issue, not functional.

The bigger complaint is fitment variability on older models. Several reviewers report that the inner sleeve is slightly too tight on ranges manufactured before 2012. The fix is simple — file the sleeve opening with a round file for 30 seconds — but it's friction you shouldn't have to deal with on a brand-new part.
