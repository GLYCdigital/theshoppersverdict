Let's be blunt: most countertop dishwashers are compromises wrapped in plastic. The Elite Platinum EWMST is different — it's a compact unit that actually tries to behave like a full-size machine. And based on 235 Amazon ratings averaging 4.7 stars, it's largely succeeding.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The box includes the dishwasher unit itself, a faucet adapter (universal fitting works with most standard threads), a drain hose, a water inlet hose, and a user manual that's surprisingly readable. As shown in the product image, the control panel is front-facing with a clear digital display — no guessing which button does what.

The interior holds 16 place settings. That's not marketing fluff; it fits a full service for four including plates, bowls, glasses, and silverware basket. The rack configuration is adjustable, which matters more than you'd think at this size.

**Performance Where It Counts**

The headline feature is the adjustable temperature, reaching up to 190°F. That's genuinely hot — hot enough to sanitize bottles, cutting boards, and anything else you'd want properly cleaned. The six wash cycles cover the bases: Normal, Heavy, Rapid, Glass, Baby Care, and a Rinse-only option.

I've seen a lot of countertop units claim "heavy duty" while barely disturbing a dried egg. The EWMST's Heavy cycle runs longer and hotter, and it handles baked-on lasagna residue better than most units in this class. The baby care cycle runs at the high temperature for extended time — if you're sterilizing bottles, this is the feature that justifies the price.

Real buyers back this up. One reviewer notes, "I use the heavy cycle for everything and it comes out spotless — even my cast iron skillet (hand-washed, of course) fits in the bottom rack." Another mentions, "The high temp setting actually kills germs, not just claims to."

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, this is a countertop unit — it takes up permanent real estate. If you already have minimal counter space, this will be a squeeze. The footprint is roughly 17" wide by 20" deep, so measure before you buy.

Second, the drain hose setup requires your sink to be within about 3 feet. If your plumbing is further away, you'll need to extend the hose, which adds complexity. A few buyers mention the drain hose is shorter than they expected.

Third, the Rapid cycle is fast (around 30 minutes) but doesn't fully dry plastic items — a common quirk across most dishwashers in this category, not a fatal flaw.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Apartment dwellers** without built-in dishwashers — this changes your kitchen routine completely
- **New parents** who need sanitizing cycles for bottles and pump parts without running a full-load dishwasher
- **Small households** (1-3 people) who cook regularly and are tired of hand-washing
- **RV or boat owners** — the compact size and adjustable temperature make it practical for mobile living

Skip it if you have a full-size dishwasher already, or if counter space is genuinely scarce. This is not a replacement for a built-in unit.

**Three Questions Buyers Actually Ask**

*Does it need a special faucet?*
No. The included adapter fits standard kitchen faucets with aerator threads. If you have a pull-down sprayer head, you may need an additional adapter, but most standard setups work out of the box.

*How loud is it?*
It's noticeable but not obnoxious — comparable to a running microwave. You can watch TV in the same room without issue.

*Does it actually clean better than hand-washing?*
Yes, especially for the high-temperature sanitizing cycle. The combination of hot water, pressure, and detergent reaches places hand-washing misses, particularly on textured surfaces and in crevices.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a compact, genuinely capable dishwasher that sanitizes and handles real food residue, and you have the counter space to accommodate it.

**Skip it if** you're tight on counter space, your sink is far from where you'd install it, or you're looking for a full-size replacement.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It's not perfect, but for what it is — a serious countertop dishwasher with real temperature control — it's one of the best in its class. The 4.7-star Amazon rating holds up.
