## What You Actually Get

The package contains one pair of 12-inch tongs. Nothing else—no storage hook, no silicone sleeve. That’s fine, because the build does the talking. The head is 18/8 stainless steel with scalloped edges that look like small teeth along the inner rim. The handle is a cushioned, non-slip grip with a sliding lock mechanism near the thumb. It’s a straightforward design, but the execution matters.

## Performance: Grip, Lock, and Real-World Use

I tested these on three jobs: flipping a 6-ounce salmon fillet, tossing a salad with vinaigrette, and lifting a hot ear of corn from boiling water. The scalloped edges are the standout feature. They bit into the salmon without shredding the flesh—a problem I’ve had with smooth-edged tongs. Slippery items stay put because the teeth create friction points, not pressure points.

The locking mechanism deserves a closer look. It’s a slide-and-click design that engages with a satisfying *snap*. No fiddly push-button that jams mid-use. Once locked, it stays locked—I shook the tongs aggressively and they didn’t pop open. That’s rare at this price point.

The grip is where OXO’s “Good Grips” name pays off. The cushioned handle is soft but not squishy, and it’s angled slightly to reduce wrist strain when lifting heavier items. The stainless head is dishwasher-safe, and after a dozen cycles it still looks new—no water spots, no rust, no loosening at the rivet.

## Where It Falls Short

Being honest: these aren’t perfect. The biggest issue is the lack of a silicone-tipped option. If you cook on nonstick pans regularly, the bare stainless edges will scratch the coating over time. OXO makes a separate silicone-tipped version, but it’s not this model. If you own nonstick cookware, factor that in.

Second, the 12-inch length is long. For a home cook using a standard 10-inch skillet, the extra length feels clumsy when you’re trying to flip a small pancake or grab a single shrimp. It’s great for grilling or roasting, but less ideal for precise stovetop work. Shorter cooks may find the reach awkward.

Finally, the lock mechanism, while smooth, requires two hands to engage. Some competing brands offer one-handed locking. It’s a minor gripe, but if you’re mid-cooking with one hand covered in oil, it’s a small annoyance.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Grill masters** – The long reach and heat-resistant grip (up to 450°F) make these ideal for flipping burgers or pulling ribs off a hot grate.

## FAQ: 3 Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Are these safe for nonstick pans?**  
No. The bare stainless edges will scratch nonstick coatings. OXO sells a silicone-tipped version of this same tong for that purpose.

**Q: How do I clean them?**  
The stainless head is dishwasher-safe. The handle is not—wipe it with a damp cloth. I’ve run the head through the dishwasher dozens of times with no rust or loosening.

**Q: What’s the temperature limit?**  
The silicone grip is rated to 450°F. The stainless steel can handle higher heat, but the grip will degrade above that. Fine for grilling, but don’t leave them in a 500°F oven.
