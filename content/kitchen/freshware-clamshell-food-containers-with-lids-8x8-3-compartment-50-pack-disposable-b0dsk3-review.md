The Freshware 8x8 3-compartment clamshells are different — mostly in good ways, with a couple of caveats worth knowing before you commit to a 50-pack.

**What you actually get**

The box contains 50 containers with attached lids — they're the classic clamshell style, not separate tops that get lost in your drawer. Each measures 8x8 inches with three divided compartments. The base is made from rigid PET plastic that feels noticeably sturdier than the flimsy foam or thin polypropylene you find in most grocery store takeout sections. As shown in the product image, the dividers rise nearly flush with the rim, which matters more than you'd think — it actually keeps sauces and juices in their own lane instead of pooling into your rice or salad.

**Performance and real-world use**

I've tested these across the scenarios that typically kill cheaper containers: microwaving leftovers at 70% power for two minutes, stacking them in a loaded fridge, and tossing them in a lunch bag with an ice pack. The PET holds up. It doesn't soften or bow in the microwave the way thin PP trays do, and the snap-lock lid stays seated through a commute without popping open. The seal is snug — not vacuum-tight, but it won't leak if you're transporting something like pasta with a modest amount of sauce.

One thing I appreciate: the compartments are genuinely proportioned for real meals. The largest section fits a decent chicken breast or a cup of stew, the two smaller sections handle a vegetable side and a starch comfortably. This isn't a gimmick tray where one compartment is uselessly tiny.

**Where it falls short**

It fits in most fridges, but if your shelves are shallow or you're stacking these in a compact lunch box, measure first.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these microwave-safe?**
Yes, the PET base is microwave-safe for reheating. Use 70% power and avoid heating empty containers. The lid should be vented or slightly open.

**Q: Can you freeze these?**
Yes, they're freezer-safe. Let food cool completely before freezing to prevent condensation and cracking. Thaw in the fridge, not the microwave, for best results.

**Q: Are the lids dishwasher-safe?**
Technically yes, but they're disposable — washing them reduces lifespan. Most buyers use them once and recycle. If you're looking for reusable, these aren't it.
