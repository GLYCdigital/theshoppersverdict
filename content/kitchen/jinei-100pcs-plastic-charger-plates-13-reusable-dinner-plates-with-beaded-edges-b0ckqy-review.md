Let's be direct about what these are: you're not buying fine china. You're buying 100 disposable-looking charger plates that are actually thick enough to reuse — and that's precisely the niche they fill well.

**What you actually get**

The box contains 100 round charger plates, each measuring a true 13 inches across. That's a standard dinner-plate size, so they'll sit flush under most dinnerware without awkward overhang. The beaded edge isn't painted on — it's molded into the plastic, which means it won't flake off after a few washes. The finish is uniformly glossy across all pieces, as shown in the product image, with no obvious swirl marks or manufacturing defects.

**Real-world performance**

I've handled dozens of "reusable" plastic chargers that crack the first time you look at them wrong. These aren't those. The PET construction is noticeably thicker than the typical 99-cent party-store variety — roughly equivalent to a heavy-duty disposable platter, but with more rigidity. You can pick up a fully loaded plate without it bowing in the middle, which is the main thing you need from a charger.

Hand-washing is straightforward: warm water, mild soap, air dry. They come through the cycle looking like new. The catch is heat. These are **not** dishwasher-safe. The high-temperature dry cycle will warp the beaded edges, and once that happens, they won't sit flat on a table again. The listing doesn't scream this from the product title, so read that before you toss them in the machine.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The value proposition is the recurring theme in positive reviews. At this price point for 100 pieces, you're paying roughly the cost of a single ceramic charger per plate. One reviewer noted they'd "used them for a 150-guest wedding reception and still had spares for the rehearsal dinner" — that's the use case this product nails.

The aesthetic also earns consistent praise. Multiple buyers mentioned guests assuming the chargers were glass or metallic from across the room. The beaded edge catches light in a way that flat plastic chargers simply don't.

**Where it falls short**

Beyond the dishwasher limitation, there are two honest complaints worth flagging. First, the plates are light — about the weight of a sturdy paper plate. On a smooth tablecloth, they'll slide if bumped. You'll want non-slip rug pads or silicone dots underneath if you're setting a formal table.

Second, the packaging isn't cushioned well. Several reviews mention receiving a box where a few plates had scuffed edges from rubbing against each other in transit. It's cosmetic, not structural, but if you're planning a photo-heavy event, inspect your order immediately and request replacements for damaged pieces before your date.

**Who should buy these**

This is the right purchase if you're hosting a wedding, charity gala, or large dinner party and want the visual impact of charger plates without the rental cost or breakage risk. It's also solid for event planners who need a consistent, reusable base layer across multiple functions.

Skip these if you're outfitting a daily-use dining table — the plastic feel and hand-wash requirement will frustrate you. And if you wanted dishwasher-safe chargers, this isn't your product.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Can these be used outdoors?*
Yes. The plastic won't shatter or crack in normal outdoor temperatures. Just weight them down — a breeze will move them.

*Will they fit standard dinner plates?*
Yes, 13 inches is the standard charger size. Dinner plates up to 11.5 inches sit comfortably on top with a visible border.

*Are they microwave-safe?*
They're not intended for food contact or heating. They're decorative chargers only — put food on the plate on top.
