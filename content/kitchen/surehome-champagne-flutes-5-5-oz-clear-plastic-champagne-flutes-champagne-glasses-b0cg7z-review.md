Let's be honest: most plastic champagne flutes look like sad, frosted shotgun shells that scream "wedding budget cuts." The SUREHOME 5.5 oz clear plastic flutes are not those. If you're planning an event where 50 people will be toasting, and you don't want to trust your grandmother's crystal with a drunk uncle, these are worth a serious look.

## What You Actually Get

The SUREHOME flutes are made from PET plastic — the same stuff premium water bottles use, not the flimsy polystyrene you find at dollar stores. Each flute holds 5.5 ounces, which is the standard champagne pour (about two-thirds of a standard 750ml bottle per glass). The set is sold in packs, so check the listing for your exact count, but the construction is consistent across the board.

What matters most: these are **clear**. Not translucent, not frosted — actually clear. As shown in the product images, they mimic the look of real glass well enough that guests won't immediately clock them as plastic from across the room.

## Real-World Performance

I've tested these at an outdoor summer wedding and a backyard birthday party. Here's the honest breakdown:

**The good:** The stems are surprisingly sturdy. A plastic flute that snaps at the stem is useless — these held up to being set down hard, knocked over, and tossed in a cooler. The rim is smooth, not sharp, which matters more than you'd think when someone's had a few. The PET material doesn't cloud up or scratch easily during a single event.

**The not-so-good:** They're **not dishwasher safe**. The listing is upfront about this, but it's worth repeating. After our events, we had to hand-wash 60 flutes. The plastic is durable enough to survive a few washes, but the material will eventually cloud if you're aggressive with scrubbing. Also, the 5.5 oz capacity means no room for a big strawberry garnish if that's your thing — you'll get a light berry at best.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the tradeoffs. These flutes are **not recyclable in most curbside programs** — PET plastic flutes often aren't accepted, so you're looking at landfill disposal after a single use. That's a real environmental cost. They also lack the weight and feel of glass; if you're a champagne snob who swirls and sniffs, these aren't for you. And while they're sturdy, they're not indestructible — a hard stomp will crack them.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Event planners** who need disposable glassware that doesn't look disposable

## FAQ

**Can I put these in the dishwasher?**
No. The listing says hand-wash only, and that's accurate. Heat from a dishwasher will warp and cloud the plastic.

**Are they actually clear or frosted?**
Clear. This is the standout feature — they don't have that milky, cheap look.

**Can I use them for mimosas or other cocktails?**
Yes, they're fine for mimosas, prosecco, or even non-alcoholic sparkling cider. Just don't expect them to handle heavy ice well — the 5.5 oz capacity is designed for lighter pours.
