**The COSORI Pro Gen 2 is the air fryer you'll actually use daily — if you can get past its one annoying habit.**

I've tested a lot of air fryers, and most fall into two camps: cheap ones that cook unevenly and expensive ones that cost more than a countertop oven. The COSORI Pro Gen 2 sits in a sweet spot — it's not the cheapest, but it delivers results that rival units costing twice as much. Here's the full breakdown after two weeks of heavy use.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the 5.8QT air fryer itself, a square crisper basket with a non-stick coating, a removable tray, and a rather thick recipe booklet (100+ recipes, mostly useful). The footprint is roughly 11 x 12 inches — it's not compact, so measure your counter space before ordering. The matte black finish looks premium and resists fingerprints better than the glossy models.

## Performance That Justifies the Hype

The Pro Gen 2's headline feature is EvenCrisp 360° technology, and honestly, it works. I cooked a batch of frozen french fries at 400°F for 18 minutes with zero shaking — every single fry came out golden and crispy, with no pale spots or burnt edges. That's a genuine improvement over older COSORI models I've tested.

The 5.8QT capacity is the real star. It's a square basket, not round, which means you can fit a whole 4-pound chicken or 18 chicken wings in a single layer. For a family of four, this is the difference between cooking dinner in one batch or two. The square design also makes cleanup easier — no food getting trapped in rounded corners.

The touchscreen is responsive and the 12 presets are genuinely useful. The shake reminder isn't just a buzz — it's a loud, clear beep that you can actually hear from across the kitchen. One quirk: the preheat function adds 3-4 minutes to every cycle, and there's no way to disable it. You'll learn to account for it, but it's annoying when you're in a hurry.

## What Buyers Are Saying

The 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews holds up. One verified buyer noted: *"I've owned three air fryers before this one. The COSORI is the first one that doesn't require me to open it and shake everything halfway through. The EvenCrisp is not a marketing gimmick."* Another reviewer praised the build quality: *"The basket slides in and out smoothly, the tray doesn't warp, and the non-stick actually holds up after three months of daily use."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the preheat cycle — it's non-negotiable and adds time to every cook. Second, the top vent gets hot. I measured 180°F on the exterior near the vent, which is concerning if you have low-hanging cabinets. Third, the non-stick coating is durable, but the manual explicitly says not to put the basket in the dishwasher. If you hate hand-washing, this is a dealbreaker.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're cooking for 3-5 people, you want even results without babysitting the basket, and you're willing to hand-wash the basket to protect the coating.

**Skip it if:** You're on a tight budget (look at the COSORI Lite), you have limited counter space, or you absolutely refuse to hand-wash anything.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Does the 5.8QT hold a whole chicken?**
A: Yes, up to 5 pounds. I fit a 4.5-pound roaster with room to spare. The square basket makes a real difference here.

**Q: Is it loud?**
A: It's about 50-55 dB — comparable to a microwave. You can have a conversation over it, but it's not silent.

**Q: Can I skip the preheat?**
A: No, but you can add 3-4 minutes to your cook time and just put food in cold. It'll take longer to crisp, but it works in a pinch.

## The Verdict

The COSORI Pro Gen 2 is the air fryer I'd recommend to most people. It cooks evenly, holds enough for a family, and the build quality justifies the price. The preheat quirk and hot exterior are minor annoyances, not dealbreakers. At 4.6/5, this is a solid buy.

**Buy it if** you want consistent, even results and actually have the counter space. **Skip it if** you need dishwasher-safe parts or a compact footprint. For everyone else — this is the one.
