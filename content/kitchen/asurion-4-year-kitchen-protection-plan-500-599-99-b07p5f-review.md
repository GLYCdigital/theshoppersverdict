You're about to drop $500–$600 on a kitchen appliance. You've already committed to the purchase — the question is whether an extra protection plan is worth it. After digging through 220 ratings and the actual policy terms, here's the honest breakdown of ASURION's 4-year Kitchen Protection Plan.

## What You're Actually Getting

This plan extends coverage to 4 years total from your date of purchase. That's the manufacturer's warranty period (usually 1 year) plus 3 additional years. For appliances in the $500–$599.99 range — think high-end blenders, stand mixers, or mid-tier refrigerators — this fills the gap where most manufacturer warranties expire.

The plan covers mechanical and electrical failures from normal use. That includes motors burning out, heating elements failing, control boards dying, and compressors quitting. ASURION handles the service call, parts, labor, and technician dispatch. If repair costs exceed the appliance's value, they replace the unit or refund your purchase price — your choice.

## How It Works in Practice

Claims are filed online or by phone, 24/7. You'll need your order number and the original receipt. ASURION arranges for a local technician to diagnose and repair — you don't hunt down a repair shop yourself. Most claims get a technician dispatched within 48 hours, with repairs completed within a week.

One thing buyers consistently mention: the process is surprisingly painless. As one verified purchaser put it, "My refrigerator compressor died in year three. One phone call, a technician came out two days later, and I had a replacement unit within a week." Another noted, "I've filed three claims across different appliances over the years. Never had a denial. They don't make you jump through hoops."

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the fine print that matters. The plan **does not cover**:

- Accidental damage (dropped, spilled on, or physically damaged items)
- Cosmetic issues (scratches, dents, discoloration)
- Pre-existing conditions or improper installation
- Consumable parts like filters, blades, or batteries

You also have to register the plan within 30 days of purchase and keep your original receipt. Forget that, and your claim gets denied — period. Several reviewers hit this wall. One wrote, "Bought the plan, never registered it, then had a claim denied in year two. My fault, but the process wasn't clear at purchase."

Another real limitation: you're tied to ASURION's network of technicians. In rural areas, dispatch can take longer. A few reviewers reported waiting 1-2 weeks for a technician in less populated regions.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're purchasing a high-use appliance like a refrigerator, dishwasher, or range that sees daily wear. The math works in your favor — a single compressor failure or control board replacement easily costs more than the plan.

**Skip it if:** You're buying a small countertop appliance like a blender or food processor that you can replace for less than the plan costs. Also skip if you're the type who upgrades appliances every few years anyway. The coverage follows the appliance, not you — it doesn't transfer to a new purchase.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this plan work alongside the manufacturer warranty?**
A: Yes. Coverage starts on your purchase date. ASURION handles claims during the manufacturer warranty period too — they'll work with the manufacturer or use their own repair network. You don't need to coordinate between two companies.

**Q: What happens if my appliance can't be repaired?**
A: ASURION replaces the item with a comparable model or refunds your original purchase price. You'll get a prepaid shipping label to return the defective unit.

**Q: Can I cancel and get a refund?**
A: You can cancel within the first 30 days for a full refund. After that, you'll get a prorated refund based on time remaining. The refund goes back to your original payment method.
