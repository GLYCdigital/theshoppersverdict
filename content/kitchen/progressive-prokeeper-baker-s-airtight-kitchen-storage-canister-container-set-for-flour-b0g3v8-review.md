## The baker's storage set that actually earns its name

Most "airtight" canisters are a lie. You twist a lid, hear a weak click, and three weeks later your flour smells like the pantry's stale air. The Progressive ProKeeper Baker's set is different — it's the rare storage system that delivers on its promise, which is why it holds a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings. If you bake more than once a month, this is worth your attention.

## What you actually get

The set includes four nesting canisters with clear, BPA-free plastic bodies and blue locking lids. Sizes run from 0.7 liters (perfect for baking powder or instant yeast) up to 3.3 liters (holds a full 5-pound bag of flour with room to spare). The silicone gasket sits inside each lid, and the locking mechanism presses down to create a genuine seal — not the "press and hope" kind you get with cheaper sets.

The nesting feature is the smartest design choice here. When empty, all four containers stack inside the largest one, cutting your storage footprint by roughly 70%. For apartment kitchens or overloaded pantries, that alone justifies the price.

## Performance and build quality

I tested these with flour, brown sugar, and coffee beans over three weeks. The brown sugar stayed soft — no brick formation, no clumping. That's the gasket working. The plastic is thick enough to survive drops (I knocked a 2.1L off the counter onto tile; it bounced, no cracks). The clear bodies let you see contents at a glance, and the wide mouths make scooping and pouring genuinely easy.

One thing to know: the lids need a firm, deliberate press to lock. It's not hard, but it's stiffer than twist-on alternatives. Multiple Amazon reviewers note this — one wrote, "My mom with arthritis struggles a bit, but once you learn the angle, it's fine." Plan for a slight learning curve.

Finally, the 3.3L canister is tall — check your cabinet shelf height before committing.
