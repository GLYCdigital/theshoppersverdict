**Performance and real-world use**

The shift actuator's job is to move the mode cam inside your washer's transmission, switching between wash, drain, and spin modes. When it fails, the machine loses track of where the cam is positioned — hence the F5 E2 error. This replacement does that job correctly. The metal gear engages smoothly with the transmission, and the plastic housing has enough rigidity to handle the torque without flexing or cracking.

**Q: Will this fit my specific Maytag or Whirlpool model?**

**Q: How do I know if my shift actuator is actually the problem?**
A: The classic symptoms are an F5 E2 error code, a washer that spins violently during wash cycles, or a machine stuck in one mode. You can also test it by manually rotating the actuator gear — it should click smoothly through positions. If it feels gritty or binds, replace it.

**Q: Do I need to reset anything after installation?**
A: Yes — after replacing the actuator, unplug the washer for 60 seconds, then run a spin-only cycle. This resets the control board and lets it re-learn the cam position. Skip this and you might get a phantom error code.
