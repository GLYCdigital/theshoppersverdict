### The Not-So-Good

**Reliability is hit or miss.** The single biggest complaint: the spray mechanism stops working properly over time. "Quality Issues" was the top complaint category by a wide margin.

**There's a learning curve.** You can't just pump and spray — you need the right angle, the right pressure, and the right distance from the food. One reviewer said it's "Really handy, but takes a little practice" — and they mean it. First-time users should expect some trial and error.

**Not all oils are equal.** Thinner oils like avocado or grapeseed spray beautifully. Extra virgin olive oil, which is thicker, tends to clog more quickly. If you use EVOO exclusively, you'll be cleaning the spray tip frequently.

The YARRAMATE 16oz Olive Oil Sprayer delivers excellent results when it's working, and at $8.79 the value proposition is strong — your own oil, no aerosols, fine mist control.
