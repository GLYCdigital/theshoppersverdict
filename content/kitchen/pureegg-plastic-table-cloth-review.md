## Material Quality

The Pureegg tablecloths are made from **thickened PEVA plastic** — a step up from the ultra-thin, translucent plastic tablecloths you find at dollar stores. PEVA (polyethylene vinyl acetate) is a chlorine-free vinyl alternative that's more environmentally friendly than traditional PVC.

The "thickened" claim is relative. These are thicker than the sheer-est disposable covers, but they're still single-use plastic. The difference is that they feel substantial enough to handle a full party without tearing under normal use.

One reviewer who used the 84-inch round version described them as *"made from thickened PEVA plastic, which helps protect the table from spills and stains."* The material is **water-resistant** — liquid spills bead up rather than soaking through, which is the entire point of a disposable tablecloth.

## Size and Fit

The **54x108 inch** size covers standard **6-foot and 8-foot rectangular tables** with about 12-15 inches of overhang on each side. The 54-inch width is enough for standard 30-inch wide tables with some drape.

For **round tables**, Pureegg also sells an 84-inch round version. The rectangular version can be used on round tables but won't drape evenly.

The tablecloths are folded in the package and may have creases. A quick pass with a warm iron on low heat (or hanging them for a few hours before the event) will release most wrinkles.
