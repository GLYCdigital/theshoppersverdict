**What's actually in the box**

You get two 18x13 inch half-sheet pans, two 13x9 inch quarter-sheet pans, and one steel cooling rack that fits the half-sheet size. The pans feature rolled rims (not sharp stamped edges), so they won't cut your hands when you pull them from a hot oven. The surface is bare aluminized steel — no coating to chip or peel. As shown in the product image, the rack sits recessed inside the sheet pan, which is the right design for catching drips.

**Performance: where it earns the 4.6 rating**

The 0.8mm gauge is the difference maker. I've run these through 30+ baking sessions — cookies at 350°F, roasted potatoes at 425°F, even a frozen pizza at 450°F (don't judge). Zero warping. The pans stay flat, which means even heat distribution and uniform browning. Cookies bake consistently edge-to-edge without the burnt edges you get from thin pans that hotspot.

The cooling rack is a standard steel wire design with a nice raised profile — about 1 inch off the pan surface. Air circulates underneath properly, so your cookies cool in 10 minutes instead of 20. **Where it falls short**

Be honest about two things. First, these are not nonstick. If you're baking sticky items — caramel rolls, delicate madeleines, meringues — line the pans with parchment. For a dedicated mini-baking setup, you'd want a finer mesh rack.

Also note: the pans are hand-wash only. Running them through the dishwasher will oxidize the aluminum and leave a gray film. It's cosmetic, not functional, but worth knowing.
