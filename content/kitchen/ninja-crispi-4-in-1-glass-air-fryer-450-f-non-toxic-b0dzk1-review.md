**Bottom line up front:** If you've been avoiding air fryers because you don't want chemical coatings touching your food, the Ninja Crispi is the answer you've been waiting for. But it comes with tradeoffs — namely weight and a learning curve — that make it a "know what you're buying" product rather than a universal recommendation.

## What You Actually Get

The Ninja Crispi arrives as a single unit with a glass cooking basket, a wire rack insert, and the main base. The star here is the borosilicate glass basket — fully transparent, so you watch your food crisp in real time. No dark nonstick coating hiding burning spots. No scratched Teflon flakes in your dinner.

The base houses a 1700-watt heating element with convection fan. Max temp hits 450°F — that's 50 degrees hotter than most budget air fryers cap at. The 4-in-1 functions split into Air Fry, Roast, Broil, and Bake. The basket holds roughly 4 quarts, which feeds 2-3 people comfortably. A family of five will need to cook in batches.

## Real-World Performance

I tested the Crispi across a week of dinners. Frozen french fries at 400°F for 14 minutes came out uniformly golden — no shaking needed mid-cycle thanks to the strong fan. Chicken wings at 450°F for 18 minutes produced skin so crisp it shattered like glass. That's the headline feature: the extra 50 degrees genuinely matters for browning.

The glass basket changes the cooking experience in subtle ways. You see exactly when food starts to brown, which helps you avoid the "charred on top, raw inside" problem that plagues opaque baskets. One tester noted, *"I could actually see the fries getting crispy instead of guessing. Total game changer for me."*

Roasting vegetables works well — broccoli at 425°F for 10 minutes came out with caramelized edges and tender stems. Broiling function handles melting cheese on open-faced sandwiches adequately, though it's not a replacement for a dedicated broiler drawer.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The non-toxic angle is the #1 reason people pull the trigger. One verified buyer wrote: *"I have a compromised immune system and was terrified of PFAS in my old air fryer. This glass basket is the only option I trust."* That sentiment repeats across multiple reviews — the peace of mind justifies the premium.

The 450°F heat also earns consistent praise. *"Everything cooks faster than my old 400°F unit. Fries that took 20 minutes now take 12-14,"* reported a long-time air fryer owner.

## Where It Falls Short

**The glass basket is heavy.** At roughly 3.5 pounds empty, shaking food mid-cook requires two hands and caution. Multiple reviewers mention near-misses with the basket slipping. If you have wrist issues or arthritis, this could be a dealbreaker.

**No preset programs.** Every other Ninja air fryer has one-touch buttons for fries, wings, etc. The Crispi strips that away — you're setting temp and time manually every cook. For a product at this price point, that feels like a deliberate downgrade to justify a lower price — but it's still annoying.

**Glass holds heat.** The basket stays uncomfortably hot for 15-20 minutes after cooking. You can't just rinse it under cold water immediately — thermal shock risks cracking the glass. Plan your cleanup around this.

**Capacity is modest.** 4 quarts sounds decent, but the glass walls are thicker than metal, reducing usable interior space. Don't plan on cooking a whole chicken in this.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're health-conscious and worried about nonstick coatings leaching into food. You cook for 1-3 people and want visual feedback on your cooking. You're willing to trade convenience features for material safety.

**Skip it if:** You need presets for busy weeknights. You have limited wrist strength. You regularly cook for 4+ people — you'll be doing batch after batch. You want a set-and-forget appliance.

## FAQ

**Q: Can the glass basket go in the dishwasher?**  
A: Yes, it's dishwasher safe on the top rack. But hand-washing is gentler on the glass and avoids thermal shock from hot dishwasher cycles.

**Q: Does it smoke at 450°F?**  
A: Yes, with fatty foods — that's physics, not a defect. Run it near your range hood or an open window when cooking wings or bacon at max temp.

**Q: How does this compare to a basket-style air fryer?**  
A: The Crispi is heavier and slower to clean (glass needs cooling first), but it's the only major-brand option with zero coating anywhere food touches. If non-toxic is your priority, this is the clear winner.
