## Where it falls short

The honesty section. First, the "24oz" rating is optimistic. The cup holds 24 ounces to the absolute brim, but the printed MAX line sits at roughly 20 ounces. That's a real constraint if you're making large smoothies or protein shakes — you'll need to leave headroom or risk overflow. Know that going in.

Third — and this is worth repeating — these are cups only. If your blade base is worn out or the gasket is shot, this kit won't help. You need to verify your existing blade assembly is in good shape before ordering.
