If you’ve ever scraped burnt cheese off a basket with a sponge and a grudge, you already know why the Ctizne Air Fryer Liner exists. This is a 100-pack of pre-cut parchment squares designed to sit flat in your basket, catch the drips, and make cleanup a 10-second toss-and-go. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it’s clearly working for most people — but let’s talk about who it’s *not* for.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains 100 pre-cut parchment sheets, each about 8 inches square. That’s enough for roughly three months of daily use at one liner per meal. The material is food-grade parchment with a light silicone coating — that’s what gives it the non-stick properties without needing oil sprays. The squares are designed to sit flat in the basket rather than bunching up like rolled parchment, which is the main reason people switch to these.

As shown in the product image, the liners have slightly raised edges that help contain juices and crumbs. They’re not deep like a bowl — think of them as a protective floor mat for your basket, not a container.

## Real-World Performance

The non-stick claim holds up. Multiple buyers report that chicken wings, frozen fries, and even breaded fish come off the liner cleanly — no sticking, no torn paper, no residue left behind. One reviewer put it simply: *“I used to scrub my basket for 10 minutes after every meal. Now I just throw the liner away and wipe it down.”* That’s the core value proposition, and it delivers.

Heat resistance is another strong point. The liners are rated to 450°F, and in practice, they handle standard air fryer cooking without burning, smoking, or curling at the edges. Even at higher temperatures for crispy finishes, buyers report the paper holds its shape.

One genuine concern: the liners are on the lighter side. In air fryers with aggressive fan circulation — particularly those with a center post or high-velocity airflow — the paper can flutter or lift if there’s not enough food weight holding it down. This isn’t a safety issue, but it can mean the liner shifts and exposes part of the basket. Heavier food like chicken thighs or burger patties anchors them fine; lightweight items like kale chips or shredded cheese might not.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

- **Cleanup time**: The overwhelming majority of positive reviews center on how much faster cleanup is. This is the product’s reason to exist, and it doesn’t miss.
- **Fit**: The 8-inch square works with most standard 4-6 quart baskets (Cosori, Instant Pot, Ninja, Gourmia). Buyers with these sizes report a snug fit with minimal folding needed.
- **No chemical taste or smell**: Several reviewers specifically noted the absence of that “papery” or chemical odor you sometimes get with cheaper liners, even at high heat.

## Where It Falls Short

The flimsiness factor is real. If you’ve used heavier-duty liners from brands like Tovolo or IFANCIFAN, you’ll notice the Ctizne sheets feel thinner. They work, but they don’t have that premium stiffness that makes them sit perfectly flat without help.

The single size is also limiting. If you own a compact 2-3 quart air fryer, these squares are too large and will require trimming. If you own an 8+ quart basket, they’ll leave exposed edges. This is squarely a mid-size basket product.

## Who Should Buy This

- **High-frequency air fryer users** who cook 3+ times a week and are tired of scrubbing baskets
- **Anyone cooking messy foods** — marinated meats, breaded items, cheese-topped dishes that always leave residue
- **Meal preppers** who use the air fryer daily and value the 30-second cleanup

**Skip it if** you have a compact air fryer, prefer heavy-duty reusable silicone mats, or only use your air fryer occasionally.

## FAQ

**Are these safe at high temperatures?**
Yes. The parchment is rated to 450°F, and no buyers report burning, smoking, or off-gassing. Keep it below that limit and you’re fine.

**Can I reuse them?**
Technically, yes, if they’re clean and not torn. In practice, most buyers treat them as single-use. The non-stick coating degrades after one use, so reuse is possible but not ideal.

**Do they fit my air fryer?**
If you have a 4-6 quart basket-style fryer, almost certainly. The 8-inch square fits most standard models. Measure your basket floor before ordering — if it’s within 7-8 inches, you’re good. Compact or XL models need different sizing.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The Ctizne Air Fryer Liner does exactly what it promises: eliminates the worst part of air fryer ownership. The thinness and single size are minor annoyances, not dealbreakers. For anyone with a standard basket fryer who cooks regularly, this is a genuinely useful purchase.

**Buy it if** you want to cut cleanup time from minutes to seconds and don’t mind a slightly thinner liner.

**Skip it if** you have a compact fryer, want maximum durability, or rarely cook messy foods.
