# Our Place Cookware Duo: The Instagram-Famous Set, Tested

You've seen the ads. The pastel ceramic pans that promise to replace every pot and pan in your kitchen. The Our Place Cookware Duo — the Always Pan 2.0 paired with the Perfect Pot — is the brand's answer to "what if two pieces could do everything?" After 229 Amazon ratings averaging 4.6 stars, the hype is real, but so are the tradeoffs. Here's what 30 days of daily cooking actually taught me.

## What's Actually in the Box

The Duo bundles two pieces: the 10.5-inch Always Pan 2.0 and the 4.5-quart Perfect Pot. Both come with their nesting lids — the pan's lid doubles as a steamer basket when flipped, and the pot's lid fits the pan too. You also get a wooden spatula, a steamer insert, and silicone handle covers. That's $295 worth of cookware that stacks into roughly the footprint of a single large skillet.

The ceramic nonstick coating is the headline feature. It's free of PFAS, PFOA, and PTFE — the chemicals that made traditional nonstick pans so effective but potentially problematic. The aluminum core is designed for even heat distribution, and both pieces work on induction, gas, and electric stoves.

## Real-World Performance

Let's talk about the cooking. I ran the Always Pan through eggs, seared chicken thighs, and a full batch of caramelized onions. The nonstick surface is genuinely excellent — eggs slide around like they're on ice, and a single paper towel wipes the pan clean. The 10.5-inch cooking surface handles a family-sized frittata without crowding.

The Perfect Pot is the workhorse here. I made a 2-hour tomato sauce, a whole chicken soup, and steamed broccoli using the included basket. The pot's tall sides and 4.5-quart capacity mean you can brown meat in batches without splattering your stovetop. Heat distribution was consistent across both pieces on my induction cooktop — no scorching in corners, no cold spots in the center.

One thing the marketing doesn't emphasize: the lids are genius. The pot's lid sits flush on the pan, so you can cook a one-pot meal in the pan and cover it with the larger lid. The steamer basket nests inside the pan when not in use. For small kitchens, this space efficiency is a genuine win.

## What Buyers Are Saying

The Amazon reviews skew heavily positive. One verified buyer wrote, "I've owned expensive French cookware and cheap nonstick sets — these are the first pans I reach for daily. The cleanup is almost nonexistent." Another noted, "The Perfect Pot replaced my Dutch oven for 90% of my cooking. It's lighter, easier to clean, and heats faster."

But the criticisms are consistent too. A 3-star review warned, "The ceramic coating is not indestructible. My partner used a metal whisk and now there are scratches. Follow the care instructions or you'll be disappointed." That's the honest truth: this is not a set you can abuse.

## Where It Falls Short

The ceramic nonstick coating is the double-edged sword. It's safer than traditional Teflon, but it's also less durable. Metal utensils will scratch it — I tested this inadvertently with a fork, and the mark is permanent. The handles also conduct heat on gas stoves; the silicone covers help, but they're easy to misplace.

The price is the other issue. At $295, you're paying for design and brand as much as function. A $150 stainless steel set will last longer, though it won't be as easy to clean. This is a lifestyle purchase, not a budget one.

One more quibble: the Perfect Pot has no pour spouts. Draining pasta requires a colander, which feels like an oversight for a pot designed to replace your entire cookware collection.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Small kitchen dwellers** who need maximum function in minimal space
- **Home cooks who hate scrubbing** and want one-pan cleanup
- **Design-conscious shoppers** who want their cookware to match their aesthetic
- **Anyone replacing nonstick pans** who wants to avoid PFAS chemicals

Skip it if you use metal utensils regularly, cook over high heat constantly, or need a set that will survive a decade of abuse.

## FAQ

**Is the ceramic coating truly non-toxic?**
Yes. The coating is free of PFAS, PFOA, lead, and cadmium. It's one of the safer nonstick options on the market.

**Can I use it on high heat?**
Technically yes, but you shouldn't. The coating degrades faster above medium-high. Use medium heat for best results.

**Does the Perfect Pot replace a Dutch oven?**
For most home cooking, yes. It's lighter and heats faster. But you won't get the same browning or oven-safe temps (it's rated to 450°F).

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a beautiful, functional two-piece set that handles 90% of daily cooking with minimal cleanup — and you're willing to follow the care rules.

**Skip it if** you're rough on cookware, cook with metal utensils, or need a set that will last through heavy daily abuse without babying.

At 4.6 stars with 229 ratings, the Our Place Cookware Duo earns its reputation. It's not perfect — the coating needs care, and the price is premium — but for the right buyer, it's genuinely the last cookware set you'll want to own.
