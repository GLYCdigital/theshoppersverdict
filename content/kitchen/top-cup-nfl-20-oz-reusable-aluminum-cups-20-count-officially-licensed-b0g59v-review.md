**Where It Falls Short**

Here's the tradeoff. These cups are **not dishwasher-safe**. The manufacturer recommends hand washing only, which is a pain when you've got 20 dirty cups after a party. Also, the 20 oz size is smaller than it sounds. A standard 16 oz can fits fine, but if you're pouring from a 24 oz tallboy, you're doing two pours per can. Some reviewers found this annoying for long games.
