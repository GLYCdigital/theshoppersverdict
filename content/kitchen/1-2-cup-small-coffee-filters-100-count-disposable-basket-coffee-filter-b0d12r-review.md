Let's be honest about small coffee filters: they're the most annoying thing to shop for online. You can't see the actual size, the packaging rarely lists dimensions clearly, and if you guess wrong, you're stuck with a box of useless paper. That's exactly why these 1-2 cup basket filters from an off-brand seller have racked up 235 ratings at 4.7 stars — they solved a specific, frustrating problem for a lot of people.

## What you actually get

A box of 100 flat-bottom basket filters sized for 1-2 cup coffee makers. The key spec is the basket diameter — these measure roughly 4.5 inches across the top, which makes them compatible with Mr. Coffee 4-cup models, certain Cuisinart mini brewers, and the handful of other machines that take a small flat-bottom basket rather than a #4 cone. The paper is a medium-weight natural brown (unbleached), crimped on both sides with a reinforced seam.

One thing worth flagging right now: **measure your existing filter before you order.** The product listing doesn't print dimensions on the box, and multiple reviewers mention they ordered blind. If your basket is smaller (some 2-cup brewers run closer to 4 inches), these will overhang and cause grounds to spill into the carafe.

## How they actually perform

I tested these against the store-brand equivalents from a major grocery chain. The difference is the paper density. These filters are noticeably thicker — not in a "slow drip" way, but enough that they hold their shape when wet. The crimped seams held up when I grabbed the spent filter by the edges to toss it; the thinner store-brand version tore on me about half the time. That's a small quality-of-life win that adds up over 100 brews.

Brew time was consistent at roughly 3.5 minutes for a full 12-ounce pot on a Mr. Coffee 4-cup. No clogging, no stalling, and no paper taste in the finished cup. The unbleached paper does release a bit of sediment into the first few ounces of brew — if you're picky, give the filter a quick rinse under the tap before adding grounds. That's a 10-second fix and not a dealbreaker.

## What buyers are saying

## Where it falls short

There's also no count printed on the individual filter stacks — you'll be counting manually if you're tracking usage. And the lack of explicit size dimensions on the packaging is a genuine oversight for a product where fit is everything.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these fit a standard Mr. Coffee 4-cup?**
A: Yes — that's the most common use case in the reviews. The top diameter is approximately 4.5 inches, which matches the basket on most Mr. Coffee 4-cup models. But measure yours first; there are variations between model years.

**Q: Are these the same as #4 cone filters?**
A: No. #4 cones are pointed and fit cone-shaped brewers. These are flat-bottom basket filters — a completely different shape. Verify your brewer type before ordering.

**Q: Can I reuse these?**
A: Technically you could rinse and dry them, but they're designed for single use. The paper degrades after one brew and will tear on the second use. Just compost them.
