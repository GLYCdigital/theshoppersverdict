A 4.1-star average across 204 ratings tells you something important about the ThierryVuiton Non Stick Frying Pan with Lid: it's not a flashy premium pan, but it consistently gets the job done. **What you actually get**

The package includes the frying pan itself and a tempered glass lid with a steam vent. The pan body is aluminum with a non-stick coating, and the handle is riveted (not screwed) into place — a detail that matters because loose handles are the #1 failure point on budget pans. The dimensions align with a standard 11-inch skillet, so it fits most stovetop burners and nests easily in a cabinet.

**Performance in real kitchens**

The non-stick surface is the headline, and it delivers. Eggs slide off without butter, fish fillets flip without tearing, and cleanup is a quick wipe with a sponge. One reviewer noted, *"I've had it for four months and it's still as slick as day one — I've only used wooden utensils and washed it by hand."* That's the pattern I see in the ratings: people who treat it gently get long life; people who crank the heat and toss it in the dishwasher see the coating degrade faster than they'd like.

Heat distribution is solid for the price point. The aluminum base warms evenly across the cooking surface — I didn't notice the classic "hot center, cool edges" problem that plagues thinner pans. It heats quickly too, so you can preheat and start cooking in about half the time of heavier stainless steel.

The lid is a genuinely useful addition. It fits with a tight seal, so braising chicken thighs or steaming vegetables works without losing all the moisture. That's rare at this price — most "pan with lid" bundles give you a lid that wobbles or vents too aggressively.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Reading through the 204 ratings, three themes dominate: the non-stick performance, the ease of cleaning, and the value. One verified buyer wrote, *"This replaced a $60 name-brand pan that was already sticking. The ThierryVuiton is better after six weeks of daily use."* Another said, *"I bought a second one for my camper — it's lightweight enough to travel with."* The weight is worth noting: it's noticeably lighter than cast iron or clad stainless, which is a plus if you have wrist issues or limited storage strength.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be direct about the tradeoffs. The non-stick coating is not indestructible — no coating at this price point is. Metal spatulas will leave scratches, and high-heat searing (above medium-high) can degrade the surface over time. If you're someone who likes to crank the burner and use tongs on everything, this pan will disappoint you within a year.

The handle is comfortable and stays cool for short cooks, but on a gas burner with the flame licking up the sides, it warms up noticeably after 10+ minutes. Use a mitt for longer simmering sessions.

And the big one: **this pan is not induction compatible.** If your kitchen has an induction cooktop, skip this entirely — you need a pan with a magnetic stainless base. The product listing is clear about this, but it's easy to miss, and it's the most common reason I see in the negative reviews.

**FAQ**

**Is the non-stick coating PFOA-free?**
Yes, the coating is PFOA-free, consistent with modern non-stick standards. That said, avoid overheating it — any non-stick coating can release fumes if heated empty on high.

**Can I use this in the oven?**
The pan itself can handle moderate oven temps (around 350°F), but the lid is glass and not oven-safe. If you need to finish dishes under the broiler, transfer to a different vessel.

**How long does the non-stick actually last?**
Based on buyer feedback, expect 6-12 months of reliable performance with hand washing and wooden/silicone utensils. Heavy use and dishwasher cycles will cut that short — treat it well and it rewards you.
