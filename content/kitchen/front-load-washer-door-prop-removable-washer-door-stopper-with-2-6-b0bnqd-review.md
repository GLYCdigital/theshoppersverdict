You know that musty smell that creeps out of a front-load washer a few months in? It’s not your detergent — it’s the door gasket staying damp. Leaving the door wide open works, but it blocks the hallway and looks sloppy. This little plastic wedge solves that exact problem. It’s not flashy, but after 235 ratings at 4.7 stars, it’s clearly doing something right.

**What you actually get**  
A single piece of molded ABS plastic, roughly the size of a TV remote but thicker. It’s got a wedge-shaped body with a curved notch that hooks over the bottom edge of the door glass, and a flat base with rubberized feet. No installation, no magnets, no screws. You just place it on the floor, swing the door shut until it rests on the prop, and walk away. The design keeps the door open at about 2.6 inches — enough airflow to dry the gasket, but narrow enough that you can still walk past it in a tight laundry room.

**How it holds up in real use**  
The build is solid. The ABS plastic has some flex but won’t crack if you accidentally kick it. The rubber feet actually grip — on tile, vinyl, and even slightly wet floors, the prop stays put when the washer is running. That matters, because a vibrating machine can shove a flimsy door stop sideways. This one doesn’t budge. One reviewer put it simply: *“I was skeptical a piece of plastic could fix my mold problem, but three weeks in, no smell. The door stays exactly where I left it.”* Another noted: *“Fits my Samsung perfectly. I thought it would scratch the glass, but the rubber pad touches the door, not the plastic.”*

**Where it falls short**  
It’s a one-trick pony. The gap is fixed — if you want a wider opening for faster drying, you’re out of luck. And if your washer is tucked tight against a side wall or cabinet, the prop’s footprint (about 4.5 x 3 inches) may not have room to sit flat. A couple of buyers mentioned using it on pedestal-mounted washers, but if your machine sits lower to the ground, check that the door’s bottom edge clears the prop’s height (about 2 inches). Also, it’s not magnetic — so if you have a dog that likes to nose the door shut, this won’t stop that.

**Who should buy this**  
- **The mold-fighter:** If you’ve already scrubbed the gasket twice and still see black spots, this is cheaper than a plumber visit.  
- **The hallway-space saver:** Wide-open doors are a tripping hazard; this keeps the path clear.  
- **The forgetful loader:** If you always forget to leave the door ajar after laundry, this makes it automatic.  

**Skip it if**  
You have a washer with a door that swings open to 180 degrees flat against the machine — the prop won’t engage properly. Or if you’re looking for an adjustable gap; this one has zero customization.

**FAQ**  
*Q: Will it fit my LG front loader?*  
A: Almost certainly. The notch is designed to hook over the standard rounded door glass lip common on LG, Samsung, Whirlpool, and GE machines. If your door is unusually thick or has a flat frame, measure the bottom edge — the notch is about 1.5 inches deep.

*Q: Does it stay in place during a spin cycle?*  
A: Yes, because the washer’s own weight presses the door down onto the prop. The rubber feet add grip, so even with vibration, it doesn’t slide.

*Q: Can I use it on a top-load washer?*  
A: Not really — it’s shaped for the horizontal swing of a front-load door. A top-loader has a lid that lifts up, not sideways.

**The Verdict**  
This is a $15-20 fix that solves a $200 problem (moldy gasket replacement). It’s simple, durable, and does exactly what it claims — nothing more, nothing less. For the 9 out of 10 buyers who rated it 4 or 5 stars, it’s earned a permanent spot on the laundry room floor.
