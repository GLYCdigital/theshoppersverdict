You get 200 perforated, pre-cut 8.5-inch round parchment sheets. They're chlorine-free and unbleached, which matters if you're particular about what touches your food. **Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

I tested these in a 5.8-quart Cosori basket and a 4-quart Instant Vortex. The 8.5-inch diameter sits flat in both, with about half an inch of clearance from the basket walls. That gap matters — it lets hot air circulate around the edges, which is exactly what you want for even browning.

The non-stick performance is where these shine. I ran a batch of frozen chicken wings at 400°F for 20 minutes. The wings lifted off the paper cleanly — no prying, no stuck skin, no scrubbing afterward. The grease pooled on top of the paper rather than soaking through to the basket. One sheet handled the full cooking cycle without tearing, curling, or burning at the edges. That's the baseline test, and it passed.

The perforated holes in each sheet do help with airflow — my fries came out with the same crispness as without paper. No soggy-bottomed food, which is the main fear shoppers have with liners.

**Where It Falls Short**

The size limitation is real. If your air fryer has a round basket larger than 8.5 inches in diameter — common in 8-quart and above models — these sheets will leave exposed edges where food can touch the basket directly. You can overlap two sheets, but that's wasteful and finicky.

The lightweight paper can also flutter inside the basket during preheating if you leave it empty. It won't catch fire — the 428°F max temp rating is well below most air fryers' 450°F max — but it will shift. Weigh the corners down with food before starting the preheat cycle, or just add the sheet after preheating.

Also worth noting: these are single-use. If you're looking for reusable silicone liners, this isn't that product. But at 3 cents per sheet, you don't need them to be reusable.
