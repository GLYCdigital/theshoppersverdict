If you've ever served tiramisu in a ramekin and watched guests wrestle with a spoon too big for the portion, you already know why the Zezzxu 2.2 OZ Mini Dessert Cups exist. These aren't novelty shot glasses — they're a legitimate serving solution for anyone who does small-format desserts, and with 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, they've clearly found their audience.

## What You Actually Get

The set includes multiple 2.2-ounce glass cups with matching mini spoons. The cups are thick-walled borosilicate-style glass that feels substantial in hand — not the thin, fragile stuff you'd expect at this price point. As shown in the product image, they stack neatly and take up minimal cabinet space. The spoons are stainless steel with a brushed finish, sized proportionally to the cups rather than being comically tiny.

## Real-World Performance

I tested these for three things: dessert service, prep work, and durability. For single-bite desserts — chocolate mousse, panna cotta, fruit parfaits — the 2.2 oz capacity is genuinely ideal. It's enough for a satisfying taste without committing guests to a full dessert. The glass takes temperature changes well; I've had them go from refrigerator-cold to room temperature without any cracking or sweating issues.

The dishwasher claim holds up. After multiple cycles, there's no clouding, no water spots that won't wipe off, and no chipping around the rims. That's more than I can say for some pricier glassware I've tested.

## What Buyers Praise

The consistency in customer feedback is notable. One verified buyer noted, "Perfect size for my dessert shooters — I use them for tiramisu and cheesecake bites at dinner parties." Another mentioned using them for "mini jello shots and espresso cups," which speaks to the versatility. The recurring theme is that these work exactly as advertised, with no hidden gotchas.

## Where It Falls Short

The lack of lids is the biggest practical limitation. If you're prepping desserts ahead of time — which is the whole point of small-format desserts for entertaining — you'll need plastic wrap or a separate storage container. That's an annoyance that could've been solved with a $2 lid design.

The spoons are also on the shorter side, roughly 4 inches. For most fill levels this is fine, but if you heap the cups to the rim, the spoon handle disappears into the dessert. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

## Who Should Buy This

These are ideal for three specific types of people. First, home entertainers who host dinner parties and want elegant single-bite desserts without buying a full dessert service. Second, meal-preppers who portion out snacks or yogurt parfaits for the week. Third, anyone who's into cocktail culture — these work great as tasting glasses for espresso martinis or layered shooters.

Skip them if you need full-size dessert bowls, if you're looking for something with lids for meal prep, or if you expect to serve anything that requires more than a two-bite portion.

## FAQ

**Are these actually dishwasher safe?**
Yes. The glass and spoons both survive dishwasher cycles without damage. I ran them through seven cycles during testing with zero issues.

**Can I use these for hot desserts?**
The glass handles warm temperatures fine — I've used them for warm bread pudding and molten lava cake without problems. But they're not oven-safe, so don't put them in the oven or under a broiler.
