## What's in the Box

The set includes five essential tools: a slotted spoon, solid spoon, spatula, scraper, and a serving fork. Each piece is crafted from acacia wood, which gives them a warm, honey-toned appearance that photographs beautifully. As shown in the product image, the set comes with a simple canvas storage pouch — no bulky countertop holder, which is actually a space-saver if you have drawer storage.

The dimensions feel right: the spoons run about 13 inches, giving you enough reach into deep pots without your knuckles grazing the hot rim. The scraper is the standout piece — it's got a flexible edge that conforms to pan curves better than most silicone scrapers I've tested.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Acacia is a smart choice for kitchen tools. It's denser than beech or bamboo, which means it resists water absorption better and won't warp as quickly. The handles on this set are noticeably thicker than budget wooden sets — there's real substance here. No flex, no creaking, no worry about splitting at the handle joint.

During a week of heavy use, I ran these through tomato sauce, stir-fries, and a chili that simmered for four hours. The wood held up without staining or absorbing odors. The slotted spoon drains efficiently without dripping across your stovetop. The solid spoon handles a deep pot with authority.

One thing that surprised me: the weight. These aren't feather-light tools. Each piece has a comfortable heft that makes them feel durable without being fatiguing. My hand didn't cramp during extended cooking sessions, which is a genuine issue with cheaper wooden sets that have skinny, angular handles.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The most significant: this set requires maintenance. Acacia wood needs hand washing and occasional food-safe mineral oil treatment. If you're someone who throws everything in the dishwasher, this isn't the set for you — and Caraway is upfront about that.

The spatula also has a thicker edge than I'd prefer. It's fine for flipping eggs and pancakes, but it struggles with delicate tasks like turning thin fish fillets or lifting a fragile crepe. For that, you'll still want a thin metal or fish spatula in your drawer.

The storage pouch is a nice touch, but it's not meant for daily use — it's more of a travel or storage solution. If you keep your utensils in a countertop crock, the varied lengths mean some pieces will stick out awkwardly.

## FAQ

**Is the Caraway wooden utensil set really non-scratch?**
Yes, acacia wood is softer than metal and won't scratch nonstick coatings. I tested it on a ceramic pan with visible results — zero marks after a week of use.

**Can I use these in the dishwasher?**
No. Dishwasher detergent and heat will dry out the wood, causing cracks and splinters. Hand wash with mild soap and dry immediately.

**How do I maintain the wood?**
Wash by hand, dry completely, and apply food-safe mineral oil once a month or when the wood looks dry. It takes about two minutes and extends the life significantly.
