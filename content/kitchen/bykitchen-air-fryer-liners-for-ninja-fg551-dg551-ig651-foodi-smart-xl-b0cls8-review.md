If you own a Ninja Foodi Smart XL — the FG551, DG551, or IG651 — you already know the pain of scrubbing that nonstick basket after every single meal. The BYKITCHEN Air Fryer Liners are a 100-count box of pre-cut parchment rounds designed specifically for those 6.5-quart models. At 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, they're clearly doing something right. But do they actually fit the paddle stirrer? And are they worth the recurring cost? Here's the real breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains 100 pre-cut parchment liners, each shaped to match the Foodi's round basket floor. They're not flimsy wax paper — this is genuine greaseproof parchment rated for the Foodi's 450°F max temperature. As shown in the product image, the liners sit flat in the basket with a slight lip up the sides, which keeps food contained and gives you a clean surface to lift everything out when done.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

I tested these across a week of chicken thighs, frozen fries, and a full air-fryer roast. The fit is the standout feature. Because they're cut specifically for the FG551/DG551/IG651, there's no excess paper bunching up against the sides — which matters because the Foodi's stirring paddle will shred loose paper if it catches an edge. These sit flush enough that the paddle clears them without issue.

Heat handling is solid. At 425°F for 25 minutes, the parchment didn't curl, brown, or flake. The greaseproof coating held up, and cleanup went from a soak-and-scrub session to a two-second lift-and-toss. One thing to note: they're perforated, so grease drains through to the basket below. That means the basket still needs a quick wipe most of the time, but you're avoiding the baked-on crust that makes the nonstick coating deteriorate over time.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The dominant theme in customer feedback is the perfect fit. One verified buyer wrote, "Finally, liners that actually fit my Ninja Foodi without folding up the sides. The paddle doesn't catch them." Another noted, "I've bought three different brands and these are the only ones that don't burn at high heat."

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest limitation is model-specific sizing. These only fit the 6.5-quart Smart XL models. If you own a 5-quart Foodi or any other air fryer brand, these will be too large and you'll need to look elsewhere. Also, while the parchment itself is strong, it's not reusable — each liner is single-use. At roughly 20 cents per liner, the cost adds up if you're air-frying daily. And because they're perforated, the basket isn't fully protected from grease — you're saving the worst of the scrubbing, not eliminating it entirely.

## Who Should Buy These
