If you've ever wrestled with a wobbly springform pan while water leaks into your cheesecake crust, you know the frustration. The Tellshun 4-inch mini springform pan aims to fix that exact problem. After digging through 229 ratings and examining the construction, here's the real story — including where it falls short.

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes two 4-inch pans (the listing photo confirms the two-pack), each with a three-piece design: the ring, the base plate, and a separate bottom disc. The pans measure 4 inches across and about 2.2 inches deep. That's enough for a personal cheesecake, a single-layer mousse cake, or a mini quiche. The carbon steel construction with a nonstick coating feels solid in hand — not flimsy like some budget options at this size.

The key feature is the leakproof claim. Unlike traditional springform pans where the latch can fail under pressure, Tellshun uses a slip-on ring that fits snugly over the base plate. This is a genuinely different mechanism, and it's the reason this pan works for water baths.

**Performance in Real Use**

I've seen consistent feedback across the ratings on three points. First, the leakproof claim holds up. One reviewer noted, "I was skeptical about the leakproof promise, but I used it in a water bath for a New York cheesecake and not a drop seeped through." That's the experience most buyers report.

Second, the nonstick coating is legitimately effective. Cheesecakes slide out after the ring is removed, and the bottom disc releases cleanly. The coating also means you can skip the greasing step for most recipes, though a light butter touch doesn't hurt.

Third, the size is the selling point. At 4 inches, you get a dessert that's satisfying but not excessive. It's been a hit for meal prepping — one reviewer said they "make three flavors of cheesecake on Sunday and have dessert all week without overindulging."

**Where It Falls Short**

The carbon steel construction is the main tradeoff. These pans are not dishwasher safe — the coating will degrade, and the steel can rust. You must hand-wash and dry immediately. That's a dealbreaker for some buyers, and it's worth knowing before purchase.

The shallow depth is another limitation. At 2.2 inches, you're not making layered cakes or anything with significant height. This is a single-layer dessert pan, full stop. Also, the slip-on ring design, while leakproof, requires a bit more care when removing — you can't just pop the latch like a traditional springform. It's not difficult, but it's a different motion.

**Who Should Buy This**

The Tellshun mini pans are ideal for three types of buyers. First, home bakers who want individual cheesecakes without the hassle of a full-size pan and water bath. Second, portion-control folks who want dessert without a whole cake staring at them from the fridge. Third, anyone who's been burned by cheap mini springform pans that leak or warp.

Skip it if you're a dishwasher-dependent cook, need taller cakes, or bake for crowds regularly — you'd need multiple batches given the 4-inch size.

**FAQ**

*Can I use these in an air fryer?* Yes, if your air fryer has a 5-inch or larger basket. The pan fits in most basket-style models, and reviewers report good results for mini cheesecakes in air fryers.

*Do I need to grease the pan if it's nonstick?* Light greasing helps with clean release, especially for crusts with butter. The coating works well, but a quick brush of butter or a spray of baking release ensures no sticking.

*Will the coating scratch easily?* The nonstick surface is durable but not indestructible. Use silicone or wooden utensils, avoid metal tools, and hand-wash with a soft sponge. Following that, the coating holds up well over many uses.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want reliable, leakproof mini springform pans for personal desserts and don't mind hand-washing. **Skip it if** you need dishwasher-safe bakeware or plan to make tall, layered cakes.
