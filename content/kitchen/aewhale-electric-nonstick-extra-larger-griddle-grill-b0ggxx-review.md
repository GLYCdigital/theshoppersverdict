The AEWHALE Electric Nonstick Extra Larger Griddle Grill promises family-sized cooking capacity at a budget price. But with a 3.1-star average across 155 ratings, shoppers are clearly split. I've dug through the feedback, examined the specs, and tested the claims. Here's what you need to know before adding this to your cart.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the griddle itself, a detachable temperature probe, and a grease drip tray that slides out from the front. The cooking surface measures roughly 20 inches by 12 inches — genuinely extra-large, as advertised. That's enough space for a dozen eggs, eight grilled cheese sandwiches, or a full sheet of bacon. The nonstick coating is PFOA-free, and the whole unit runs on standard 120V household current.

## Performance: The Good and the Hot

When it works, this griddle delivers. The nonstick surface releases eggs and pancakes cleanly with minimal butter or oil. The drip tray catches bacon grease effectively, which keeps your countertop from becoming a mess. For weekend breakfasts or feeding a crowd, the capacity is genuinely useful.

But here's the catch: temperature control is this unit's weak spot. Multiple buyers report that the dial runs significantly hotter than the marked settings. One reviewer noted, "Even on the lowest setting, my pancakes browned too fast on the outside while staying raw in the middle." That's a consistent complaint, not an isolated incident.

The heating element also doesn't distribute heat evenly. The center gets noticeably hotter than the edges, which means you'll need to rotate food or accept uneven cooking. For someone making a single batch of pancakes, that's annoying. For someone cooking for a family, it's a real workflow problem.

## What Buyers Praise

The surface size earns consistent praise. As one verified purchaser put it, "I cooked 12 pancakes at once — my old griddle could only do four." Another highlighted the nonstick performance: "Eggs slide right off, and cleanup takes two minutes." The drip tray also gets positive mentions for containing grease effectively.

## Where It Falls Short

The temperature issues aren't the only complaint. Several buyers flag the build quality as light and flimsy. The base flexes when you press down with a spatula, which feels concerning during use. There are also reports of the nonstick coating scratching after a few months, even with plastic utensils. And the cord is short — about 24 inches — which limits where you can place it on your counter.

## Who Should Buy This

This griddle suits casual cooks who want big capacity at a low price and don't mind babysitting the heat. It's a solid choice for camping, dorm rooms, or small kitchens where counter space is tight and you need one appliance to handle big batches. If you're a precision cook who needs reliable temperature control, look elsewhere.

## FAQ

**Is the nonstick coating truly nonstick?**
Yes, initially. It releases food well for the first few months. But several long-term users report the coating degrading faster than pricier brands like Presto or Blackstone. Use silicone or wooden utensils only, and hand-wash to extend its life.

**Can I use metal utensils on it?**
You shouldn't. The nonstick surface scratches easily, and multiple reviewers confirmed damage from metal spatulas. Stick to plastic, silicone, or wood.

**Does it smoke a lot?**
Yes, especially when preheating on higher settings. The temperature dial runs hot, so the griddle can exceed its intended heat and produce smoke. Open a window and run a vent fan.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need maximum cooking surface for the lowest price, you're cooking for a crowd regularly, and you're comfortable adjusting for inconsistent heat.

**Skip it if** you want precise temperature control, even heating, or long-term durability. The 3.1-star average reflects real issues that won't bother everyone — but if you're picky about your pancakes, they'll bother you.

**Rating: 3.3/5** — A capable griddle with serious capacity, undermined by hot-running temperatures and uneven heat distribution. It'll feed a crowd, but you'll need to watch it closely.
