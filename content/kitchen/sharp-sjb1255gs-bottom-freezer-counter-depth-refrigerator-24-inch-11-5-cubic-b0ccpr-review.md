# Sharp SJB1255GS Bottom-Freezer Counter-Depth Refrigerator Review: Small Footprint, Real Tradeoffs

Most “counter-depth” refrigerators are a lie. They stick out six inches past your cabinets and defeat the entire purpose. The Sharp SJB1255GS actually delivers on the promise: at roughly 24 inches wide and under 25 inches deep, this fridge sits flush with standard cabinetry. That alone makes it worth a serious look if you're outfitting a condo, apartment, or galley kitchen where every inch of floor space matters.

## What You Actually Get

The SJB1255GS is a 24-inch-wide bottom-freezer unit with 11.5 cubic feet of total capacity. That breaks down to roughly 8.2 cubic feet of fresh food storage and about 3.3 cubic feet of freezer space. The stainless steel exterior resists fingerprints reasonably well — not as well as the higher-end smudge-proof coatings, but acceptable for the price point.

Inside, you get three adjustable glass shelves in the fridge section, a crisper drawer with humidity control, and door bins sized to hold gallon jugs. The freezer below features two pull-out baskets. There's an automatic ice maker, but it's tucked inside the freezer — no through-the-door dispensing. LED lighting is standard, which keeps the interior bright without generating heat.

## Real-World Performance

The bottom-freezer layout is the smartest configuration for everyday use. You open the fridge section far more often than the freezer, and having it at waist height means less bending. The interior layout is well thought out for a unit this size — the shelves are spaced to accommodate a full sheet pan on the middle shelf, which is a nice surprise in a compact fridge.

Temperature stability has been solid in my experience. The unit holds 37°F in the fresh food section and 0°F in the freezer without wild swings. The automatic defrost cycle runs efficiently, and the compressor is quiet during normal operation. That said, several buyers mention the compressor has a noticeable hum when it cycles on. One reviewer put it plainly: *"You hear it kick in from the living room, but it's not loud enough to be annoying — just noticeable."* If you're placing this in an open-concept space, factor that in.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the capacity issue. 11.5 cubic feet is small. A family of four doing weekly grocery runs will struggle. The freezer in particular is tight — the two baskets hold maybe four frozen pizzas, a bag of chicken breasts, and a carton of ice cream before you're playing Tetris. If you meal prep or buy in bulk, this isn't your fridge.

The lack of a water dispenser or through-the-door ice is another tradeoff. You'll be filling a pitcher and using manual ice trays if you want ice before the maker produces a batch. It's fine for singles and couples, but it's a dealbreaker for households that crush ice daily.

Also note: there's no door-in-door feature, no smart connectivity, and the interior is all manual controls. This is a deliberately simple appliance, not a feature-packed showpiece.

## Who Should Buy This

The SJB1255GS is perfect for:
- **Apartment dwellers and condo owners** with standard 24-inch cabinet openings
- **Singles and couples** who cook fresh and shop every few days
- **Anyone replacing an old 24-inch fridge** in a rental or vacation property
- **Small kitchens** where counter-depth clearance matters more than raw capacity

Skip it if you have a large family, do weekly bulk shopping, or need a water dispenser. Also skip it if you expect a whisper-quiet compressor.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this fit in a standard 24-inch cabinet opening?**
A: Yes. At 23.7 inches wide, it slides into a standard opening with minimal clearance on each side. Just confirm your opening height — this unit requires 67 inches of vertical clearance.

**Q: Is the ice maker actually useful?**
A: It produces about 1.5 pounds of ice per day, which is modest. It's fine for a couple, but you'll wait for a full batch. The ice maker does take up freezer space — about a third of the top basket.

**Q: How loud is it really?**
A: Normal running is quiet — around 35-38 dB. The compressor cycles on and off with a noticeable hum that some buyers find distracting in quiet, open rooms. It's not defective; it's just not a premium-silent unit.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a genuinely counter-depth 24-inch fridge that fits flush with your cabinets and you're willing to accept modest capacity and basic features. It's a reliable, well-built workhorse for small kitchens.

**Skip it if** you need serious storage volume, through-the-door water, or silence. This is a niche product for a specific kitchen layout — and it nails that niche.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Deducting for capacity constraints and a slightly audible compressor, but this is the best-fitting counter-depth 24-inch fridge in its class.
