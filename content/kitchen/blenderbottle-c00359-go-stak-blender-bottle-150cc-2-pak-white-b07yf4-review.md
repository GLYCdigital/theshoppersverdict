**If you’ve ever watched a scoop of protein powder go airborne mid-shake, you know the struggle.** The BlenderBottle GO STAK 150cc 2-Pak solves that specific problem — and a few others — without adding bulk to your gym bag. As shown in the product image, these are two small, white twist-on containers that attach directly to the bottom of your BlenderBottle shaker cup. They’re not meant to replace your main bottle; they’re meant to make it smarter.

Here’s what you actually get: two 150cc (about 5 fluid ounces) containers with threaded bases that screw onto the bottom of compatible BlenderBottle cups (the classic, SportMixer, and ProStak lines). Each container has a tight-fitting lid, so you can pre-measure your powder, stash a few almonds, or keep your keys dry at the pool. The twist-on mechanism is the key feature — it locks into the cup’s base with a satisfying click, and the seal held up in my testing even with thin pre-workout liquid sloshing around.

**Build quality is genuinely solid.** The plastic feels thick and durable, not flimsy like some cheap supplement containers. The threads are precise, and the lid snaps shut with a firm click. I’ve been using one for three weeks — dropped it on a tile floor, tossed it in a gym bag, and left it in a hot car. No cracks, no warping, no leaks. The 150cc size is perfect for a single scoop of most protein powders (about 30g), and it’s small enough that you won’t notice it’s there when attached to your bottle.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The convenience factor is the headline. One Amazon reviewer wrote, *“I used to carry a separate baggie for my powder. Now it just twists on. Game changer.”* Another noted, *“The seal is tight — I’ve left it in my backpack with water in the bottle and nothing leaked.”* That tracks with the 4.6-star average across 229 ratings. The twist-on base works with standard wide-mouth jars too, so you can use it with a mason jar or a non-BlenderBottle cup if you’re not married to the brand.

**Where it falls short:** The threads are stiff when new. You’ll need to give it a firm twist to seat it properly, and if you don’t, it can feel loose. It’s not a dealbreaker, but it’s the most common complaint in the review data. Second, these are **hand-wash only** — the manufacturer explicitly warns against the dishwasher because the heat can warp the threads. If you’re a “everything goes in the dishwasher” person, this will annoy you. Finally, the 150cc size is a bit small for anyone using a double scoop or stacking more than one layer of dry goods. If you need bulk storage, look at the 200cc version.

**Who should buy this:**  
- **Gym-goers who pre-portion supplements** — you’ll never carry a separate baggie again.  
- **Meal-preppers** who want to pack dry spices, nuts, or trail mix without extra containers.  
- **Travelers** who need a leak-proof way to carry powdered electrolytes or coffee grounds.  

**Who should skip it:**  
- **Dishwasher-only households** — hand washing is non-negotiable.  
- **Anyone needing large-capacity storage** — 150cc is small by design.  
- **Non-BlenderBottle users** — the twist-on base only fits compatible cups (though it does fit standard wide-mouth jars, so it’s not a total loss).

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit my BlenderBottle Classic or SportMixer?**  
A: Yes — both the Classic and SportMixer lines have the compatible base threads. If you have a ProStak, it also works. For non-BlenderBottle cups, check if the mouth is standard wide-mouth (about 2.5 inches); it will thread on.

**Q: Can I put liquid in the GO STAK container?**  
A: Technically yes, but it’s not designed for it. The seal is liquid-tight, so you *could* carry a small amount of sauce or dressing, but the 150cc size is better suited for dry goods. I wouldn’t risk a full liquid load in a gym bag.

**Q: Is it BPA-free?**  
A: Yes, the plastic is BPA-free, and the lid is food-grade. No plastic taste in my testing.
