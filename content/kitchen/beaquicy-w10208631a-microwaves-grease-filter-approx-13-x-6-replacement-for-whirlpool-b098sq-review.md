## FAQ

**How often should I replace my microwave grease filter?**
Every 6–12 months depending on cooking frequency. If you cook with oil daily, check monthly. When the mesh looks darkened and washing doesn't restore it, replace it.

**Is this filter washable and reusable?**
Yes. Hand-wash with hot water and dish soap, let it dry completely, and reinstall. It's not dishwasher-safe — the aluminum frame will warp.

**How do I verify this fits my microwave?**
Check the model number on the label inside your microwave door or on the back panel. Cross-reference it with the compatibility list on the product page. When in doubt, measure your current filter — it should be roughly 13" x 6".
