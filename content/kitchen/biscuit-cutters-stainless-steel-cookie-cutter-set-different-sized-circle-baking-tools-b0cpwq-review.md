I've used these for buttermilk biscuits, sugar cookies, and even homemade English muffins. The cutting edges are sharp enough to slice cleanly through dough without dragging or tearing the edges. **Who should buy this**

Get this set if you bake biscuits or cookies more than once a month, or if you're tired of plastic cutters that crack and stain. It's also a smart pick if you make donuts or bagels and need consistent rounds without buying a specialty cutter.

Skip it if you only bake a few times a year — a single cutter in your most-used size might be enough. And if you need novelty shapes like stars or hearts, this obviously won't cover that.

**FAQ**

*Can these go in the dishwasher?*
Yes, the stainless steel is dishwasher-safe. Hand washing is gentler on the edges, but they won't rust or corrode with machine washing.

*Are the sizes labeled or marked on the cutters?*
No, there are no engravings or size markings. You'll identify them by diameter, which is easy once you see the progression.

*Can these cut through thick dough?*
Yes, up to about half an inch of chilled dough. For thicker stacks, press straight down without twisting — twisting can seal the edges and prevent rising.
