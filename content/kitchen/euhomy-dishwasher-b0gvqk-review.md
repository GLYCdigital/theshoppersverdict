The EUHOMY countertop dishwasher sits in a crowded category of compact washers all promising the same thing: freedom from hand-washing. After digging through the specs and 200 Amazon ratings sitting at a steady 4.0, here's the honest breakdown of whether this one earns its counter space.

## What You Actually Get

The box includes the dishwasher unit, a faucet adapter kit with multiple fittings, a drain hose, a water supply hose, and a fruit basket accessory. The unit itself measures roughly 17 x 17 x 18 inches — compact enough for most kitchen counters but not so tiny that you're limited to washing coffee mugs. The interior is stainless steel, which matters more than you'd think for odor control and durability.

The standout feature is the dual water intake: you can either hook it up to your faucet with the included adapter or fill the built-in 5-liter water tank manually. That tank option is rare at this price point and genuinely useful if your kitchen has non-standard faucets or you want to run it in a garage, RV, or office breakroom.

## Performance and Real-World Use

I'll cut to the chase: it cleans well, but it's not fast. The heavy cycle runs about 95 minutes, the normal cycle around 75, and even the quick mode takes roughly 30 minutes. That's typical for countertop units — the tradeoff for compact size is longer cycles since the spray arms need time to hit everything with less water pressure than full-size machines.

When it comes to actual cleaning, the EUHOMY holds its own against pricier competitors. The top spray arm and bottom spray arm work together to tackle baked-on food reasonably well, though heavily crusted casseroles still benefit from a quick pre-rinse. The fruit wash cycle is a legitimately nice touch — it uses cool water and gentle spray patterns to clean produce without detergent residue.

One buyer noted: *"I was skeptical about the fruit wash feature but it actually removed the dirt from my strawberries and grapes without turning them to mush. That alone justifies the purchase for me."*

Another consistent point of praise: *"The installation took me 15 minutes and I didn't need a plumber. The faucet adapter fit my standard kitchen sink without issues."*

## Where It Falls Short

The drying function is the biggest complaint, and it's valid. The air-dry system relies on residual heat, which means glass and ceramic come out mostly dry but plastics stay wet with pooled water in concave surfaces. If you wash a lot of Tupperware, budget for a microfiber towel in your routine.

Noise is another consideration. It's not loud — roughly dishwashing-machine quiet, around 52-55 dB — but the water pump has a distinct hum that's noticeable in open-concept kitchens. It won't wake anyone in the next room, but you'll hear it running.

The 4.0 average rating reflects these tradeoffs. Happy owners are mostly renters, small households, or people who simply hate hand-washing. Unhappy reviewers typically expected full-size performance in a countertop footprint — which no unit in this category delivers.

## Who Should Buy This

This is ideal for singles or couples who cook at home but hate the sink pile-up. It's also a strong pick for apartment dwellers with no dishwasher hookup, RV owners, or anyone who wants an office kitchen upgrade without a major installation project.

Skip it if you have a family of four-plus, if you exclusively use plastic storage containers, or if you expect sparkling-dry dishes straight out of the cycle.

## FAQ

**Can this hook up to a standard kitchen faucet?**
Yes, the included adapter kit fits most standard aerator threads. If you have a pull-down faucet or a non-standard spray head, you may need a separate adapter — check your faucet type before ordering.

**Does it need a dedicated electrical outlet?**
Yes, it plugs into a standard 120V outlet and draws about 1450W. It needs its own circuit, so don't run it on the same outlet as your microwave or toaster.

**Will my dinner plates fit?**
Standard 10-inch dinner plates fit, but 12-inch plates don't. The interior basket has a flat rack design that accommodates plates, bowls, glasses, and small cookware — just not oversized items.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're tired of hand-washing and want an affordable, flexible countertop solution that installs without a plumber and actually gets dishes clean. **Skip it if** you demand fast cycles, dry plastics, or full-size capacity.

The EUHOMY isn't perfect — the drying shortfall and long cycle times are real. But for the price point and the dual water-intake flexibility, it's a solid 4.2-star value that solves the "I hate the dish pile" problem for small households.
