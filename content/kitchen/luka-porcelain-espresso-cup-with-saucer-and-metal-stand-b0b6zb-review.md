# LUKA Porcelain Espresso Cup with Saucer and Metal Stand: The Real Story

Most espresso cups are an afterthought. You grab whatever's in the cabinet, pour your shot, and move on. The LUKA Porcelain Espresso Cup with Saucer and Metal Stand makes a case for caring about the vessel — and after testing it daily for two weeks, I get it.

## What You Actually Get

The set includes two porcelain cups, two matching saucers, and a metal stand. The cups hold approximately 2.5 ounces — right in the traditional espresso range, though slightly under the 3-ounce standard for a double shot with crema. The porcelain is noticeably thick-walled, which isn't an accident. That heft is what keeps your espresso hot longer than the thin ceramic cups most people use.

The metal stand is the differentiator here. It's not flimsy — it has real weight to it, and the cups sit in it securely without rattling. It works as both storage and a countertop display piece. If you're someone who leaves the espresso machine out all day, this keeps the cups accessible and looking intentional.

## Real-World Performance

Heat retention is where this cup earns its keep. I poured a shot at 165°F and measured the temperature drop over five minutes. The LUKA cup lost about 12 degrees in that window. For comparison, a standard restaurant-supply espresso cup loses nearly double that. The thick porcelain acts as a thermal buffer, which matters if you sip slowly or tend to get distracted mid-morning.

The saucer deserves a shoutout. Most espresso saucers are cosmetic — too shallow to catch anything, and the cup rocks on them. This one has a recessed center that the cup seats into. No wobble, and it actually catches drips from a sloppy pour-over transfer. Small detail, but it's the kind of thing you notice every morning.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The 229 ratings average out to 4.6 stars, and the praise is consistent. One reviewer put it plainly: "Finally a cup that doesn't cool my espresso in 30 seconds. The stand is a nice bonus." Another noted, "I bought these for my home office and they look way more expensive than they are."

## Where It Falls Short

Be honest about the size before you buy. These are traditional espresso cups, not mugs. If you drink cortados or long blacks, you'll need something bigger. The 2.5-ounce capacity means a double shot fills it nearly to the brim — no room for milk.

The metal stand also requires hand washing. The finish is a black coating that doesn't hold up to dishwasher cycles. Buyers who tossed it in the machine reported dulling and spotting. Hand wash only, and dry it promptly.

One more thing: the cups themselves are dishwasher safe, but the gold rim detail on some colorways will fade over time. If you're buying for daily use, choose the plain white or matte black versions.

## FAQ

**Do these fit under a standard espresso machine spout?**
Yes, the cups are 2.5 inches tall at the rim, which clears most portafilters and spouts. If you have a commercial machine with low clearance, measure first.

**Can I microwave these cups?**
Yes, the porcelain is microwave-safe. The metal stand is not — keep it out of the microwave entirely.

**Is the stand stable with both cups loaded?**
Yes. The base is wide enough that it doesn't tip, even if you bump it pulling a cup out. The cups sit in recessed rings, so they don't slide.
