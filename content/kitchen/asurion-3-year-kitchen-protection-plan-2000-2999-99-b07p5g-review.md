Here's the thing about extended warranties: most are overpriced insurance for people who never file a claim. The ASURION 3 Year Kitchen Protection Plan for appliances in the $2,000–$2,999.99 range is different — not because it's perfect, but because it covers the exact window when expensive kitchen gear tends to fail. If you're dropping two and a half grand on a refrigerator, range, or built-in microwave, the $200–$400 you'll pay for this plan is a calculated bet. Here's whether it pays off.

**What you actually get**  
This is a service contract, not a physical product. You're buying three years of coverage that kicks in *after* your manufacturer's warranty expires (typically year two through year five). The plan covers mechanical and electrical failures — think a compressor that dies, a control board that fries, or a heating element that stops working. Claims are filed directly through your Amazon account, and ASURION handles the rest: they send a technician, or in some cases, ship you a replacement part if it's user-serviceable.

What it doesn't cover matters more. No accidental damage (dropped cast-iron skillet on the glass cooktop? That's on you). No cosmetic defects (scratches, dents, rust). No food spoilage if your fridge dies — ASURION explicitly excludes that. And you must register the plan within 30 days of your appliance's delivery date. Miss that window, and you've paid for nothing.

The 4.2-star rating across 210 reviews tracks with what you'd expect from a warranty: people who used it are generally satisfied, people who didn't are neutral, and a vocal minority got burned on technicalities. **Where it falls short**  
The exclusions are the real gotcha. If your $2,500 range's glass top cracks from a dropped pot, you're paying full replacement cost. And the 30-day registration deadline is stricter than most competitors (SquareTrade gives you 90 days). If you're the type who buys an appliance and forgets the paperwork, set a calendar reminder.
