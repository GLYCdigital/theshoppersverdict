### The Not-So-Good

**They don't come apart for cleaning.** This is the biggest functional limitation. The shears are riveted, not screwed, meaning you can't separate the blades to clean between them. Food particles — especially poultry fat and herb residue — can accumulate at the pivot over time. You'll need to run them through the dishwasher or use hot water and a brush, but you'll never get them completely clean inside the joint.

**Inconsistent blade alignment.** A small number of reviewers report receiving units where the blades don't meet cleanly at the tip, leaving a gap that makes the last quarter-inch of cut ineffective. It's not widespread, but it's a quality control miss worth checking on arrival.

**Micro-serrations wear down.** The grip-enhancing teeth on the blades are effective initially, but some long-term users report they smooth out after months of heavy use, particularly if the shears are washed in the dishwasher.
