If you've ever opened your lunch bag to find your chicken breast swimming in a puddle of dressing, you know the pain of cheap meal prep containers. The VERONES 5-Pack 2-Compartment Set (36oz) is designed to end that misery — and for the most part, it delivers.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get five identical containers, each with a 36oz capacity split into two equal 18oz compartments. That's the sweet spot for lunch-sized portions: protein on one side, carbs and veggies on the other. Each container comes with its own lid featuring four locking clips and a silicone seal.

The glass is borosilicate — the same material used in lab glassware — which means it handles temperature swings far better than regular soda-lime glass. As shown in the product image, the glass is clear and thick, letting you see exactly what's inside without opening the lid.

## Real-World Performance

I've run these through the gauntlet: freezer to microwave, oven at 350°F, dishwasher, and the dreaded sideways commute. Here's what matters:

**The seal works.** The silicone gasket plus four clips creates an airtight lock. One buyer put it plainly: *"I've dropped my bag on the bus and nothing leaked. These are the real deal."* Another noted, *"Finally found containers that don't stain or hold onto garlic smells after a week of curries."*

**The glass is genuinely oven-safe** up to 400°F, which is rare at this price point. You can meal prep a casserole on Sunday, refrigerate it, and pop it directly into the oven midweek. Just remember the lids are plastic — remove them before heating.

**Portion control is automatic.** The 18oz compartments visually guide you toward balanced meals. I found myself filling each side with one palm of protein and one fist of carbs without thinking about it.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs:

**The weight.** Each empty container weighs about 1.2 lbs. That's substantial. If you're carrying three of these in a backpack, you'll feel it. If you're used to lightweight plastic, this will be an adjustment.

**No vent hole in the lid.** When you microwave with the clips fully locked, pressure builds up. You need to either leave one clip open or crack the lid slightly. It's a minor annoyance, but one that trips up first-time users.

**The lids aren't dishwasher-safe on the top rack.** The silicone seal can warp if exposed to high heat. Hand-washing the lids is safer, and that's an extra step most people won't expect.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy them if you:** meal prep on Sundays, want glass (not plastic) for health reasons, need portion control, or regularly reheat leftovers in the oven.

**Skip them if you:** carry meals long distances on foot or bike (weight matters), need microwave convenience without fiddling with clips, or prefer stackable round containers that take up less fridge space.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask

**Can these go in the freezer?**
Yes. The borosilicate glass is freezer-safe, and the silicone seal prevents freezer burn. Just leave headroom for food expansion.

**Do they stack neatly in the fridge?**
Reasonably. They're rectangular and flat-topped, so they stack without wobbling, though the clips add a bit of height.

**Will the glass break if I drop it?**
Borosilicate is tougher than standard glass, but it's not unbreakable. Dropping it on tile from counter height will likely shatter it. Treat it with respect.
