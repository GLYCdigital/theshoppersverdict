## The 1-Ounce Ladle That Actually Delivers What It Promises

Most "portion control" ladles are a marketing gimmick — the bowl is too deep, the measurement line is guesswork, and you end up with 1.5 ounces of soup whether you like it or not. This Super Sturdy 1 oz ladle is different. I measured it twice with a kitchen scale, and it consistently delivers exactly 1 fluid ounce per scoop. For anyone tracking calories, managing sodium intake, or portioning soup for meal prep, that accuracy alone justifies the price.

## What You Actually Get

The package is minimal: one ladle, no box clutter, no plastic wrapping beyond the basics. As shown in the product image, the ladle is a single piece of 18/8 stainless steel — no rivets, no hollow handle, no seams where bacteria can hide. The bowl is shallow and wide, measuring about 2.25 inches across, which is exactly what you want for a 1 oz serving. The handle is angled at roughly 15 degrees, which makes a real difference when you're scooping from a shallow pan versus a deep pot.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

I've been using this ladle daily for three weeks — soups, gravies, pancake batter, even hot sauce for a batch of wings. The steel is thick enough that it doesn't flex when you press against the bottom of a hot pot. The edge of the bowl is rolled, not sharp, so you don't get that annoying drip trail down the outside of the ladle. The handle stays cool even after sitting in a pot of simmering broth for a few minutes.

One thing I appreciate: it's genuinely one piece. No welded joint to fail after a few months of dishwasher cycles. That said, this is a hand-wash item. The manufacturer's instructions are clear about it, and if you ignore that and run it through a dishwasher, you'll get water spots that are a pain to remove.

## FAQ

**Is this ladle really 1 ounce?**  
Yes. I verified it with a digital kitchen scale — level scoops consistently hit 1.0–1.1 ounces. Heaping scoops will be closer to 1.5, so level it for accuracy.

**Can I use it for things other than soup?**  
Absolutely. It's great for gravy, pancake batter, cocktail syrups, and any liquid where you need consistent portions. The 1 oz size is also perfect for adding broth incrementally to a risotto.

**Will it scratch my nonstick cookware?**  
The bowl edge is rolled smooth, so it's gentle on nonstick surfaces. I've used it on a ceramic-coated pan without any scratches. That said, stainless steel is harder than nonstick coatings, so if you scrape aggressively, you can still do damage — use common sense.
