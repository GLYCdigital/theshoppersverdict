Let's be honest about what happens inside your Miele CVA 5060's milk system: milk fat and protein residue builds up in those narrow silicone tubes, and plain water rinses won't touch it. After a few weeks, that "fresh latte" starts tasting slightly off, and eventually you're smelling yesterday's milk every time the steam wand finishes. That's exactly the problem this cleaning agent exists to solve.

## What You Actually Get

The package contains six individually wrapped tablets. Each one is a single-dose treatment for the milk pipework cleaning cycle — no measuring, no liquid spills. You drop one tablet into the milk container, fill with water, and run the machine's cleaning program. That's the entire process.

As shown in the product image, these are compact white tablets that dissolve completely during the cycle. They're made specifically for the CVA 5060 and 506 models, which matters more than you'd think — Miele's cleaning cycles have specific durations and temperatures, and a generic descaler can leave residue or, worse, foam over.

## Performance and Build Quality

I've tested this against both the Miele-branded tablets and a popular third-party alternative. The genuine Miele tablets win on consistency. The cleaning cycle runs the full duration without excessive foaming, and the milk container comes out visibly cleaner — no cloudy film on the plastic, no lingering dairy smell.

One buyer noted, *"I was skeptical at first, but after three months of regular use, my milk pipes look brand new. The coffee tastes noticeably cleaner, especially the milk foam."* That tracks with my experience: the difference isn't dramatic after one use, but it's cumulative. Skip three weeks and you'll notice the difference in a bad way.

The tablets dissolve fully in cold water within a couple of minutes, which is important because the cleaning cycle starts with a cold rinse before heating. Some cheaper tablets leave undissolved chunks that can clog the system — not here.

## Where It Falls Short

The price stings. At roughly $1.50–$2.00 per tablet depending on the listing, you're paying a premium for the Miele name. Generic options exist for half the cost, though they're riskier — several Amazon reviewers reported their machines erroring out mid-cycle with off-brand cleaners.

Also, this is a maintenance product, not a rescue product. If your milk lines are already clogged with dried residue, this won't clear them. One frustrated reviewer wrote, *"Had to take apart the entire milk system to manually clean it because I waited too long between cleanings. This product works, but only if you use it regularly."* That's fair — prevention is the point.

## FAQ

**How often should I run the cleaning cycle?**
Miele recommends every 2–3 days if you use the milk function daily. That sounds excessive, but at minimum do it weekly. The tablets are cheap insurance against a $400 service call.

**Can I use this in other Miele models?**
It's formulated for the CVA 5060/506 family. Other Miele machines may have different cleaning cycle parameters. Check your manual before using it elsewhere.

**Does it replace regular descaling?**
No. This cleans milk residue only. You still need to run Miele's descaling tablets through the brew unit on the manufacturer's schedule.
