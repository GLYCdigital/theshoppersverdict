The foil is noticeably thicker than the dollar-store variety. It's not heavy-gauge like a commercial steam table pan, but it won't crumple when you lift it with one hand mid-bake. **FAQ**

**Can I freeze these with the lids on?**
Yes, but leave headroom. Bake, cool completely, then cover and freeze. The lids prevent freezer burn. Thaw in the fridge overnight before serving.

**Are they truly reusable?**
Hand-wash with mild soap and a soft sponge. Dishwashers will warp them. Treated carefully, expect 5-8 bakes per pan before the foil thins at the folds.

**What recipes work best in a 10oz pan?**
Anything that fits a standard muffin tin but with more height. Brownies, mini pound cakes, individual pot pies, and single-serve cobblers all work well. Stick to batters that rise moderately — overly airy sponge cakes can overflow.
