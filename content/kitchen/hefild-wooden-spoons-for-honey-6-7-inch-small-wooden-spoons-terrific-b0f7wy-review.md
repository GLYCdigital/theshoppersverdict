## Where They Fall Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. The unfinished wood is a double-edged sword. It's food-safe and natural, but it requires maintenance. Over time, wood absorbs moisture and can crack if not treated. You'll want to apply food-grade mineral oil every few months to keep them in good condition. That's not a dealbreaker, but it's a responsibility some buyers won't expect.

The 6.7-inch length is also a constraint. If you're using extra-large honey jars or bulk-sized condiment containers, these spoons won't reach the bottom. They're designed for standard jars, and that's it. One reviewer noted this directly: "Great for small jars, but I had to buy longer spoons for the big containers I use for bulk honey."

## FAQ

**Are these spoons dishwasher safe?**
No. Hand wash them with mild soap and warm water, then air dry. The dishwasher's heat and moisture will cause the wood to crack.

**Will they stain or absorb honey over time?**
They'll darken slightly with use, which is normal for wood. Regular application of mineral oil will keep them looking clean and prevent deep staining.

**Are they safe for children?**
Yes, the smooth finish and lack of sharp edges make them safe for older kids. The 6.7-inch length is actually a good size for little hands.
