Let’s be honest: extended warranties are usually a racket. But when you’re dropping $800–$2,000 on a refrigerator, range, or dishwasher, a $125–$149 protection plan starts to look like cheap insurance. The ASURION 2 Year Major Appliance Protection Plan sits in that awkward middle ground — and after digging through 220 Amazon ratings and the actual policy terms, here’s what you need to know before you click “Add to Cart.”

**What you actually get**

The plan covers mechanical and electrical failures for two years from your purchase date. That includes parts, labor, and technician visits — no deductibles, no per-visit fees. You’re covered for things like compressor failures, control board malfunctions, heating element burnout, and motor failures. The plan explicitly excludes cosmetic damage, pre-existing conditions, and damage from improper installation or misuse, which is standard but worth reading.

The claim process runs through ASURION’s 24/7 phone line or online portal. Most claims get a repair-or-replace decision within 48 hours. If the repair costs more than the appliance is worth, they cut you a check or replace the unit. For a major kitchen appliance, that’s the outcome you actually want.

**How it performs in the real world**

The 4.4-star average across 220 ratings is genuinely solid for this product category. Buyers consistently praise how painless the claims process is. One verified purchaser wrote: *“My refrigerator compressor died 14 months in. One phone call, a technician came out two days later, and it was fixed under the plan. Cost me nothing out of pocket.”* Another noted: *“I was skeptical, but when my dishwasher control board failed at 18 months, ASURION replaced the whole unit. Worth every penny.”*

That said, the plan’s value depends heavily on your appliance’s reliability window. Most major appliances from brands like LG, Samsung, Whirlpool, and GE have a manufacturer warranty covering the first year. This plan effectively extends coverage into years two and three — which is exactly when compressors and control boards tend to fail. The math works if you’re buying a high-end unit or a notoriously repair-prone brand.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest complaint in the reviews is registration friction. You have to register the plan within 30 days of your appliance delivery, and if you miss that window, coverage gets complicated. Several buyers reported delays because their appliance was delivered before they purchased the plan, creating a paperwork headache.

The second issue is the repair network. ASURION uses third-party technicians, and in rural areas, some users reported waiting 5–7 days for a service visit. That’s fine for a dishwasher, less fine for a refrigerator full of food. Also note: this plan covers appliances purchased in the last 30 days — it’s not a retroactive protection for your existing gear.

**Who should buy this**

Buy it if you’re purchasing a major appliance from a brand with known reliability issues (looking at you, Samsung refrigerators), or if you’re buying a premium model where a single repair could cost more than the plan itself. Skip it if you’re buying a budget appliance where replacement costs roughly what the plan does — in that case, bank the $149 and self-insure.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this cover labor costs?**  
Yes. Parts, labor, and technician visit fees are all included. No deductibles.

**Q: Can I use it with an appliance still under manufacturer warranty?**  
Yes. The plan runs concurrently with the manufacturer warranty and takes over seamlessly when it expires. You don’t need to wait to file a claim.

**Q: What happens if the appliance can’t be repaired?**  
ASURION will replace the unit or refund the purchase price, minus any applicable depreciation. In practice, buyers report full replacement or a check for the original purchase amount.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You’re investing in a major appliance over $700, especially from a brand with known compressor or control board failure rates. The 48-hour claims decision and no-deductible structure make this one of the better protection plans on Amazon.

**Skip it if:** You’re buying a budget appliance under $400, or you’re the type who reads the fine print and actually remembers registration deadlines. For everyone else, this is a reasonable hedge — not a scam, not a must-buy, but a legitimate safety net that pays for itself with one solid claim.
