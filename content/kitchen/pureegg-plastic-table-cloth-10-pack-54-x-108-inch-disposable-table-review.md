The Pureegg Plastic Table Cloth 10 Pack solves a specific problem with brutal efficiency: you need clean table covers for an event, you don't want to wash fabric afterward, and you want them cheap. At $12.83 for ten 54x108 inch white tablecloths, that's about $1.28 per table. Here's what 6,500+ Amazon reviewers have to say.

## The Great Value Proposition

The overwhelming consensus is that this pack delivers exactly what it promises: disposable plastic tablecloths that look presentable and make cleanup trivial. The 54x108 size fits standard 8-foot folding tables with a decent overhang, and since they're rectangular, they work on smaller tables, buffets, and picnic tables too.

> "Great Value and Perfect for Parties — The Pureegg Plastic Table Cloth Disposable 10 Pack sized 84'' Round Table Cloths worked perfectly for my event. The size fit our tables well and gave them a clean, neat look. These disposable tablecloths are made from thickened PEVA plastic, which helps them resist spills and stains while protecting the table underneath. I was happy with the quality — the plastic is lightweight but still sturdy enough for a party or gathering without tearing easily."

The key phrase from nearly every positive review: "lightweight but still sturdy enough." The material is PEVA plastic, which is thicker than the ultra-cheap poly tablecloths you find at dollar stores. It drapes reasonably well and doesn't have that papery crinkle sound.

## Where It Shows Its Price

These are not restaurant-grade tablecloths. They are thin by design — single-use disposables that trade durability for cost savings. Multiple reviewers note that you need to handle them carefully:

> "Great product I used it for my baby shower! Good product for the price. My only thing was it was a little see through which was fine. It's definitely a thin plastic. Need to be careful not to rip it. But it worked great for my party, lasted all day."

> "As described. They are good for 1 time use table covers. They are thin but held up well to the wind at an outdoor open house."

The see-through factor matters if you're putting white covers over dark tables — the color will show through slightly. For most party settings with food and decorations on top, it's invisible.

## The Single-Use Reality

One reviewer summarized it perfectly:

> "If you are looking for some quick tablecloths that are disposable and cost-efficient, then these tablecloths will work for you. They are very durable, meaning they will last through whatever function you're using them for but you definitely will not be able to use them again. So these are single use, but for the price, the versatility, the fact that they stay in place and they are lightweight. It's worth the money."

You cannot fold and save these for another party. They crease, they thin at the fold lines, and they're designed for one event. But at $1.28 each, the economics still work in your favor when you compare against fabric rental or dry cleaning.

## What Buyers Use Them For

The versatility is a recurring theme: baby showers, birthday parties, soccer team gatherings, wedding receptions, church functions, outdoor picnics, craft tables, buffet lines. The white color is neutral enough for any occasion, and the material cleans up with a simple wipe if you do want to salvage one during the event.

## The Verdict

The Pureegg Plastic Table Cloth 10 Pack is the smart choice for anyone hosting an event who doesn't want to wash tablecloths afterward. At $12.83 for ten, it's a logistics saver and a budget win. The material is thin but functional, and the white finish looks clean and intentional. For the price point, it's hard to beat.
