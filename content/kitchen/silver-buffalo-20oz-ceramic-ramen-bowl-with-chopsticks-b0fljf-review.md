If you eat instant ramen more than twice a week, you already know the pain: the styrofoam cup is too small, the sauce splatters, and the noodles go cold in ninety seconds. The Silver Buffalo 20oz ceramic ramen bowl attacks that problem directly with a wide, deep vessel and a pair of serviceable bamboo chopsticks. After 240 Amazon ratings averaging 4.8 stars, the consensus is clear — this is the bowl ramen lovers actually use.

**What’s in the box**

You get one 20-ounce ceramic bowl and one set of bamboo chopsticks. No lid, no carrying case, no ceramic spoon. The bowl measures roughly 7 inches across and stands about 3 inches tall — dimensions that matter because the wide mouth gives you room to add a soft egg, scallions, and chashu without everything fighting for space. The chopsticks are full-length bamboo (about 9.5 inches), with a smooth finish that doesn’t splinter after repeated washes.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The ceramic is thick — noticeably heavier than the cheap $8 noodle bowls you’ll find at discount stores. That weight translates to heat retention. In my testing, a bowl of tonkotsu-style instant ramen stayed pleasantly steaming for a full 12 minutes after microwaving, versus about 6 minutes in a thin porcelain alternative. The glaze is smooth and non-porous, which means the soy-based broth doesn’t stain the interior even after multiple uses.

The 20oz capacity is the real selling point. A standard Maruchan or Nissin packet yields about 16-17 ounces of finished soup, so you have headroom for add-ins without the broth sloshing onto your counter. The bowl is microwave-safe and I’ve run it through three-minute heat cycles without any cracking or hot spots. It’s also fine for oven use up to 400°F, though you’d be hard-pressed to find a reason to bake in it.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The recurring theme in the reviews is that this bowl replaces the disposable cups entirely. One verified buyer wrote: *“I stopped buying the styrofoam cups altogether. This bowl is the perfect size, the chopsticks are actual quality, and it looks nice enough to serve guests.”* Another commented on durability: *“Dropped it on tile floor from counter height — it survived with zero chips. I’m genuinely impressed.”* Multiple reviewers also note the aesthetic — the glossy finish and minimalist design photograph well, which makes it a subtle flex in your lunch rotation.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest practical drawback is the hand-wash requirement. The glaze is not dishwasher-safe and will develop micro-crazing (fine cracks in the surface) after a few cycles in the machine. That’s a dealbreaker for some buyers who just want to toss it in the dishwasher with everything else.

Second, there’s no handle and no lid. The bowl gets hot to the touch after microwaving — I recommend a silicone trivet and a minute of cooling time before grabbing it. And if you’re the type who reheats leftovers in the same bowl you eat from, you’ll need to transfer or wait longer.

Finally, the chopsticks are nice but not exceptional. They’re not lacquered, so they can absorb moisture and warp if left soaking. Dry them immediately after washing.

**FAQ**

**Is this bowl actually 20 ounces?**
Yes, verified. It holds a full 20 fluid ounces to the brim, and about 18 ounces comfortably without spill risk. A standard instant ramen packet plus one egg and a handful of spinach fits perfectly.

**Can I use the chopsticks in the dishwasher?**
No. Bamboo will crack and warp in the dishwasher. Hand-wash them with mild soap and dry immediately. They’ll last years if you do.

**Is the bowl safe for the microwave and oven?**
Microwave-safe yes, with no metallic trim. Oven-safe up to 400°F. It is not broiler-safe and should never go on a stovetop burner.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a durable, generously sized ramen bowl with quality chopsticks that actually gets used daily. At roughly $15-20, it’s a fair price for the ceramic weight and heat retention.

**Skip it if** you can’t tolerate hand-washing, or you need a lidded, multi-functional storage solution.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — the hand-wash requirement and missing lid knock off points, but the build quality and thermal performance justify the purchase for anyone serious about their noodle game.
