**What's Actually in the Kit**

You get two rear drum support rollers with their axles. That's it — no brackets, no fasteners, no grease packet. The rollers are the critical wear items here. They're molded with a smooth, dense polymer surface that rides against the dryer drum's outer rim. The axles are steel, machined to the same tolerances as the OEM W10314173 part. If your dryer is throwing a squeak every rotation, these are the culprits 80% of the time.

**Real-World Performance**

The install is straightforward if you're even mildly handy. Unplug the dryer, remove the front panel or top (depending on your model), pull the drum forward, and swap the rollers. Plan on 30–60 minutes total. Several reviewers noted the rollers fit snugly with zero play on the axle shaft — that's the tolerance you want. Loose-fitting aftermarket rollers cause the drum to wobble and accelerate wear on the bearing surfaces.

One recurring theme in the feedback: this kit fixes the noise problem permanently, not just temporarily. Multiple buyers mentioned their dryers had been squeaking for months before they finally replaced the rollers. One reviewer wrote, "Quiet as new after install. Should have done this six months ago." Another noted, "The old rollers were flat on one side — these are perfectly round and the drum spins smooth."

**Where It Falls Short**

The fitment is the catch. This kit is designed for specific Whirlpool, Maytag, and Kenmore dryers that use the W10314173/W10314171 roller assembly. It is not universal. Check your model number against the compatibility list before ordering — returns are easy but wasted time isn't. Also, the kit doesn't include the mounting screws or clips. Most people reuse the originals without issue, but if yours are corroded or stripped, you'll need a separate trip to the hardware store.

A handful of reviewers reported the axle fit was too tight on certain models and required light sanding. That's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker — but worth knowing if you're not comfortable with light modification.
