## Small Size, Big Accuracy

The Ultrean scale is compact — roughly the size of a smartphone — and that's by design. It's meant to tuck away when not in use and pull out when you need precise measurements. The stainless steel surface is easy to wipe clean, and the LCD display is clear and readable.

> "Perfect for our needs. I cook a lot but many recipes call for grams and other measurements I don't have a clue about. This makes it easy. It's small and easy to store (we tuck it in a cabinet on its side). It's very accurate and I use it almost daily," writes one long-term user.

> "Small compact and easy to use. The LED readout can be adjusted to show measurements in your preferred measurement system (i.e., lbs, ounces, or grams, Kilos)."

The six measurement units cover everything a home cook or baker needs: grams, ounces, pounds, kilograms, milliliters, and fluid ounces. The tare function zeros out the weight of any container you place on top, making it easy to measure ingredients directly into a mixing bowl.

## The Battery Annoyance

The most consistent complaint across the reviews is the included batteries. The scale uses a CR2032 coin cell, and the batteries that come in the box are often dead or nearly dead:

> "Very nice scales. Works good. Only (minor) gripe was it came with cheap, dead batteries."

This isn't a dealbreaker — replacement CR2032 batteries cost pennies — but it's a frustrating first experience. Factor in the cost of fresh batteries when you buy this scale.

## The Capacity Question

The Ultrean scale handles up to 11 pounds (5 kg), which covers most home cooking and baking needs. Where it falls short is physical size: the platform is small enough that very large mixing bowls can be unstable. For everyday use — measuring flour, sugar, coffee beans, or ingredients in standard bowls — it's perfectly adequate.
