# Disney Mickey & Minnie Double Flip Waffle Maker by Select Brands: A Rotating Waffle Machine That Earns Its Keep

Let's be honest: most character-themed kitchen appliances are novelties that end up in a drawer after three uses. The Disney Mickey & Minnie Double Flip Waffle Maker by Select Brands is not that appliance. With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, this rotating waffle iron has real staying power — and after looking at what buyers actually say, it's clear why.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the waffle maker itself, a measuring cup for batter, and a small spatula. The unit is a double-flip design: two round nonstick plates, each imprinted with Mickey or Minnie's face, mounted on a central hinge that rotates 180 degrees. When you flip it, both waffles cook from both sides simultaneously — no opening the lid, no rotating each plate individually.

The exterior is white with subtle character detailing — tasteful enough that it won't scream "kids toy" on your counter. The plates are nonstick and the whole unit weighs just under 4 pounds, making it easy to move and store.

## How It Performs in Real Use

The rotating mechanism is the star here. Because both plates flip together, you get even browning across the entire surface — no pale spots on one side and burnt edges on the other. One buyer put it simply: *"The flip feature makes a huge difference. My old waffle iron always had uneven results, but these come out perfect every time."* Another noted, *"I was skeptical about the double flip, but it's genuinely easier than my Cuisinart."*

Batter distribution is straightforward: pour about 1/3 cup per plate, close, and flip. The nonstick coating holds up well — waffles release cleanly with just a light spray of oil. Cleanup is a quick wipe-down; the plates are removable, though most buyers say they don't bother taking them out since the nonstick surface wipes clean easily.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Beyond the flip mechanism, three themes dominate the positive reviews:

**The character detail is actually good.** The Mickey and Minnie face imprints are deep and crisp — waffles come out looking like the characters, not vague blobs. One parent said, *"My daughter actually gasped when she saw the waffle. It's that clear."*

**It's genuinely fast.** From plug-in to finished waffles is about 4-5 minutes, and since you're cooking two at once, family breakfasts go quicker than with a single-plate waffle iron.

**It handles multiple batters well.** Buyers report success with standard pancake mix, gluten-free blends, and even protein batter — the nonstick surface doesn't stick or scorch easily.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has two real drawbacks.

**No indicator light.** There's no "ready" light telling you when the plates are preheated or when the waffle is done. You'll gauge doneness by steam output and trial-and-error. Most buyers figure it out after 2-3 batches, but it's a learning curve that a $50+ appliance shouldn't require.

**Short cord.** At roughly 2 feet, the cord limits where you can use it. If your kitchen outlets are scarce, you'll need an extension cord or a different counter spot. It's a minor annoyance, but one that comes up repeatedly in reviews.

## Who Should Buy This

This waffle maker is perfect for:

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use any waffle batter, or does it need to be specific?**
A: Standard waffle or pancake batter works fine. Thicker batters (like Belgian-style) also work, but you may need to experiment with amount — too much batter will overflow the plate edges.

**Q: How do I know when the waffles are done without an indicator light?**
A: Watch for steam. When steam stops rising from the sides, the waffle is typically done — usually 4-5 minutes. The first batch is always a test batch; note the time and repeat it.

**Q: Are the plates dishwasher safe?**
A: The plates are nonstick and removable, but Select Brands recommends hand-washing. Dishwasher detergent can degrade nonstick coatings over time. A quick wipe with a damp cloth is usually all you need.
