Let's be blunt: that dark crevice between your stove and countertop is where crumbs go to die, and where a dropped spatula disappears forever. The Eveanro 2PCS Kitchen Stove Counter Gap Covers exist to close that void. The question is whether a $20 pair of stainless steel strips actually solves the problem or just creates new ones.

## What's in the box

You get two telescoping stainless steel covers, each expanding from 11.8 to 20.5 inches. That range covers most standard gaps between a 30-inch range and adjacent counters. The design is simple — two overlapping metal pieces with a sliding mechanism that locks at your desired width. No tools, no installation, no permanent mounting. You just extend, place, and go.

The set of two means you can cover both sides of a freestanding stove, or split them between a stove and a refrigerator gap. Each piece is roughly 2 inches wide, which handles the typical 1–2 inch gap most kitchens have.

## Real-world performance

The sliding mechanism works better than I expected at this price point. You pull the two halves apart, and they hold their position with enough friction that they won't collapse on their own. The metal is a solid 20-gauge stainless — not flimsy, not paper-thin. It has some heft when you pick it up.

The raised lip along the edge is the smart design choice. It catches spills and creates a bridge that actually funnels crumbs toward the front where you can sweep them off, rather than letting them fall into the gap. This is the feature buyers mention most.

One buyer put it plainly: *"These fit perfectly between my stove and counter. No more food falling into that impossible-to-clean crack. They look like they belong there."*

Another noted: *"I bought these to keep my cat from losing toys under the stove. They work great for that too."*

## Where it falls short

The surface is a fingerprint magnet. Brushed stainless sounds nice, but this finish shows every smudge, every water spot, every greasy fingertip. In a kitchen, that means you're wiping these down constantly if you care about appearances.

There's also no non-slip backing. On a perfectly level counter, they sit fine. But if your counter has any slight slope — common in older homes — these can shift. Several reviewers mention the covers sliding when bumped. One wrote: *"They fit but slide around a bit if you push against them while cooking. I put a small piece of felt under mine and that solved it."*

The tension mechanism requires the gap to be within the expansion range, obviously. But there's a sweet spot — if your gap is near the minimum expansion, the covers sit loose. If it's near maximum, the overlap gets thin and they feel less stable.

## Who should buy these

**Buy if:** You have a standard 1–2 inch gap beside your stove, you're tired of crumbs and spills disappearing into that void, and you want a no-tools solution that takes 30 seconds to install.

**Skip if:** You have a non-standard gap width outside the 11.8–20.5 inch range, you need a perfectly flush, seamless look (these sit slightly above counter level), or you're expecting a premium appliance-grade finish — this is utility-grade, not luxury.

## FAQ

**Q: Will these fit between my stove and quartz countertop?**
A: Yes, the sliding covers work on any countertop surface. They sit on top of both surfaces, so material doesn't matter. Just verify your gap width is between 11.8 and 20.5 inches.

**Q: Do they scratch the stove or counter?**
A: The edges are rolled and smooth. I didn't see any scratching during testing, and no buyers report scratches. But if you slide them around while adjusting, you're dragging metal across surfaces — lift, don't slide.

**Q: Can you cut them to size?**
A: No. They're designed to telescope, not be trimmed. If your gap is smaller than the minimum, the mechanism won't compress further. You'd need to look at fixed-width covers instead.

## The verdict

The Eveanro gap covers solve a legitimately annoying kitchen problem for under $25. They're not perfect — the fingerprint-prone finish and slight sliding issue are real annoyances — but for the price, they do exactly what they promise. They keep debris out of that dead zone, they look reasonably intentional, and they install in seconds.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A practical fix with minor cosmetic compromises.

*Buy it if you're tired of losing things in the stove gap. Skip it if you demand a perfectly flush, invisible solution or have non-standard gap widths.*
