## Where It Falls Short

**Size expectations.** The product images make these look bigger than they are. On a standard 24" deep fridge shelf, you'll fit about three across with a few inches to spare. If your fridge has narrower shelves, measure first. Returns are easy, but measuring saves the hassle.

**No dividers included.** The bins are one open compartment. For small items like spice packets or sauce sachets, you'll want to add your own small containers inside.
