### The Not-So-Good

**Narrow slots limit bread choice.** The slim design has a trade-off: the slots are narrower than standard toasters. Thick-cut sourdough, artisanal breads, and bagels cut in half can be a tight fit. You can often wedge them in, but it's not a smooth drop. If you're a bread snob who buys from the bakery counter, take note.

**Browning consistency varies.** Some reviewers report that one slot browns faster than the other, requiring you to swap bread positions mid-cycle for even results. It's not universal, but it's common enough to mention.

**Quality control is budget-tier.** A few reviewers received units with visible defects — scuffs on the exterior, a stuck lever, or inconsistent pop-up height. At this price, it's the reality of the category, but inspect yours on arrival.
