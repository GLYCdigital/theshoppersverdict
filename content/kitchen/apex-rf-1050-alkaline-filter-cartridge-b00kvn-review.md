# APEX RF-1050 Alkaline Filter Cartridge Review: The Right Way to Fix Flat RO Water

If you've got a reverse osmosis system, you know the problem: the water's clean, but it tastes flat. Lifeless. Like drinking rain. The APEX RF-1050 is designed to fix exactly that — by adding minerals back after the RO membrane strips them out.

Here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The RF-1050 is a standard 10-inch cartridge (9.75" x 2.5" to be exact) filled with a proprietary blend of calcium, magnesium, potassium, and other trace minerals. It's designed to be the *last* stage in an RO system — the water passes through the membrane, then through this cartridge, and comes out with a higher pH and better taste.

It's a universal fit. If your RO system uses standard 10-inch housings — which most do — this snaps right in. No adapters, no proprietary locking rings, no headache.

## Real-World Performance

Here's what actually matters: does it work?

Based on the 229 ratings at 4.6/5, yes — with some nuance. One reviewer put it plainly: *"Took my water from 6.5 pH to 8.5. You can feel the difference in how the water tastes."* Another noted: *"The minerals aren't just for taste — I could actually tell my body felt more hydrated."*

The media is dense. At 1 GPM flow rate, the contact time is enough to actually raise pH — which is the single biggest failure point for cheap alkaline cartridges. This one does what it claims.

The taste improvement is the headline. RO water goes from "flat" to "crisp" — that slight sweetness you get from natural spring water. If you're a water snob, you'll notice it immediately.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the gaps.

**No shutoff valve.** This cartridge doesn't stop water flow when spent. You need to track replacement by calendar (typically every 6-12 months or 1,500 gallons), not by taste. If you forget, you're drinking unfiltered RO water without realizing it.

**Initial flush is mandatory.** The first 5-10 gallons will have black carbon dust. If you skip the flush, your first glass looks like you're drinking from a mud puddle. Read the instructions.

**It's not a standalone filter.** This adds minerals — it doesn't remove contaminants. You need an RO membrane or carbon filter *upstream* for that. If you're buying this as your only filter, you're doing it wrong.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:**
- You have an RO system and hate the flat taste
- You want to raise your water's alkalinity without buying expensive bottled alkaline water
- You're replacing a spent alkaline cartridge and want a solid, consistent performer

**Skip it if:**
- You have municipal water that's already well-balanced (you won't notice as much benefit)
- You need a complete filtration solution — this is a finishing touch, not a foundation
- You're prone to forgetting maintenance schedules (no shutoff = no reminder)

## FAQ

**Q: How often should I replace the RF-1050?**
A: Every 6-12 months or 1,500 gallons, whichever comes first. If you've got a family of four, plan on 6-month replacements. Mark it on your calendar — there's no indicator.

**Q: Will this fit my system?**
A: If your RO system uses standard 10-inch x 2.5-inch cartridges — which covers 95% of residential systems — yes. Check your current cartridge dimensions before ordering.

**Q: Does it actually make water more alkaline?**
A: Yes. Multiple reviewers measured pH increases from 6.5 to 8.5-9.5 with test strips. It's not a gimmick — the mineral media is dense enough to do the job at normal flow rates.
