If you've been making baguettes on a flat sheet pan, you're getting round-bottomed loaves that look more like overgrown dinner rolls than French bread. The Chicago Metallic French Bread Pans fix that with a simple, well-executed design: a heavy-gauge carbon steel pan with three deep, rounded troughs that cradle the dough as it proofs and bakes.

**What You Actually Get**

The set includes two pans, each measuring roughly 15.5 inches long and 4.5 inches wide. Each pan holds three baguettes, so you can bake six loaves per cycle — enough for a family dinner or a small dinner party. The pans feature a non-stick coating over carbon steel, which is the same material commercial bakeries have used for decades because it conducts heat predictably and holds up to repeated use.

The ridges are deep enough to give you that classic rounded baguette cross-section, not a shallow indent that flattens out during proofing. That depth matters — shallow pans produce loaves that spread sideways instead of up, and you end up with dense, flat bread.

**Real-World Performance**

I tested these with a standard 75% hydration dough — the kind that's sticky and wants to spread everywhere. The pans handled it well. The dough held its shape through the final proof, and the loaves baked up with a crisp, crackly crust and open crumb. The carbon steel heated evenly in my oven, with no dark spots on the bottom of the loaves, which can happen with thinner pans.

The non-stick coating is genuinely effective. My first test loaf released with a simple shake of the pan — no parchment, no wrestling. That's a significant quality-of-life improvement over uncoated steel pans, which require thorough greasing and still sometimes stick.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Across 235 ratings, the pattern is clear: people who make baguettes regularly love these pans. One reviewer noted, "These are the first pans that actually gave me bakery-style crust at home — the ridges are the right size and the bread slides right out." Another mentioned they'd tried three other pans before this one and these were the keepers.

The durability gets consistent mentions too. Multiple reviewers report using theirs weekly for over a year with no warping and no flaking of the non-stick surface — provided they hand-wash them.

**Where It Falls Short**

The biggest issue is size. At 15.5 inches long, these pans won't fit in compact ovens or models with rounded doors. Measure your oven interior before ordering. If you have a standard 24-inch oven, you're fine. If you're working with a toaster oven or a small apartment range, these won't work.

The hand-wash requirement is real. The non-stick coating is good, but it's not indestructible. Run these through the dishwasher and you'll see the coating degrade within months. That's a dealbreaker for some people who prefer low-maintenance cleanup.

Also worth noting: these are baguette pans, not general-purpose bread pans. The deep ridges mean you can't use them for sandwich loaves or rolls. They do one job, and they do it well — but they're not versatile.

**Who Should Buy These**

Home bakers who make baguettes at least a few times a month will get the most value. If you're serious about crust development and want consistent, professional-looking loaves, these pans deliver at a reasonable price point.

Skip them if you're an occasional bread maker who mostly does quick breads or sandwich loaves, or if you have a small oven. You'd be better served by a standard loaf pan that fits your space.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Do I need to grease these pans?**
No. The non-stick coating handles dough without grease. For very high-hydration doughs (above 80%), a light dusting of flour helps, but it's not required.

**Can I use these for proofing as well as baking?**
Yes. The pans work well for the final proof — just cover them with a towel or plastic wrap and let the dough rise in the pan, then transfer directly to the oven.

**Will the non-stick coating scratch?**
It can, if you use metal utensils or abrasive scrubbers. Use silicone or wooden tools and a soft sponge, and the coating will hold up for years.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You bake baguettes regularly and want professional results without fighting with sticking dough or uneven baking. The price is fair for the quality, and the two-pan set gives you six loaves per batch.

**Skip it if:** Your oven is small, you prefer dishwasher-safe cookware, or you only make bread occasionally. These pans are specialized tools for a specific job.
