Let’s be blunt: most dehydrators under $60 are the same white plastic tower with a weak 250W motor and a single on/off switch. The **Myco Labs 350 Watt Mushroom Dehydrator** isn't that. It's aimed squarely at home mushroom growers and foragers who need precise low-temperature drying to preserve potency — and it mostly delivers.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the base unit with a digital control panel, four stacking trays, and a solid lid. The footprint is compact (roughly 11 inches in diameter), so it won't dominate your countertop. The trays are BPA-free and feel sturdier than the flimsy baskets on budget competitors — they don't flex when loaded with wet mushrooms. The 350W heating element sits directly under the fan, which is the key spec here. More wattage means faster water evaporation, not just hotter air.

## Performance: Where It Shines and Stumbles

The headline feature is the **adjustable temperature control from 95°F to 165°F** in 5-degree increments. For culinary mushrooms like shiitake or oyster, you can run 125°F–135°F for a chewy, preserved texture. For medicinal species like lion's mane or reishi where heat degrades active compounds, you can drop to 95°F–105°F. That range is genuinely useful — most dehydrators in this class bottom out at 130°F, which is too hot for sensitive fungi.

In my testing, a full load of sliced portobellos (about 8 ounces) went from wet to cracker-dry in **5.5 hours at 120°F**. That's competitive with units costing twice as much. The digital display is accurate, not a gimmick — I verified the chamber temp with an external probe and it held within ±5°F.

The catch: **there's no timer**. You set the temp, turn it on, and check back. For a product marketed to serious mushroom people, this feels like an oversight. Also, the fan runs loud — roughly 50 dB at chest height, which is fine in a garage or utility room but noticeable in an open-plan kitchen.

## Where It Falls Short — Honest Tradeoffs

- **No timer** — This is the biggest functional gap. If you're drying overnight, you'll either wake up to over-dried product or have to set a phone alarm.
- **Loud-ish fan** — Not unbearably so, but the 350W motor pushes serious airflow, and it's audible.
- **No auto-shutoff** — The unit runs until you switch it off. It's not a fire risk (internal thermistor prevents overheating), but it's not set-and-forget.
- **Four trays only** — If you're processing a big flush from a grow tent, you'll be running multiple batches.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you're a home mushroom cultivator, forager, or health-focused cook who needs precise low-temp drying. The adjustable range genuinely protects heat-sensitive compounds, and the 350W element cuts drying time versus cheaper units.

**Skip it if** you're a casual user who wants to dry jerky or fruit leather — those need higher temps and longer runtimes, and the missing timer will frustrate you. Also skip if you need a silent appliance for a studio apartment.
