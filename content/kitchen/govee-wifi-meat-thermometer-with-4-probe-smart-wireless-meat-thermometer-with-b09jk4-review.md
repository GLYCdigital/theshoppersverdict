Cooking a $90 brisket and guessing when it's done is a gamble. The Govee WiFi Meat Thermometer with 4 probes removes that gamble — mostly. With 215 ratings averaging 4.3 stars, this is one of the more popular multi-probe options under $70. Here's the real story after digging through specs and user feedback.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the main display unit, four stainless steel probe wires with color-coded silicone handles (blue, green, orange, white), and a USB-C cable for powering the base (though it also takes 3 AAA batteries). The probes are about 4.9 feet long — enough to reach from a smoker rack to the base sitting on a side table. The display itself is 3.5 inches with a backlight that shows all four temps simultaneously, plus target temps and a timer. As shown in the product image, the unit has a fold-out stand and a magnetic back, so you can stick it to a grill lid or fridge.

**Performance and real-world behavior**

The temperature range is 14°F to 572°F, which covers everything from cold-smoking salmon to searing steak. Accuracy claims are ±1.8°F, and in practice, users report it tracks within a couple degrees of instant-read thermometers like ThermoPop. The probes read fast — about 4-5 seconds to settle — and the WiFi connectivity is the standout feature. Once paired with the Govee Home app, you can walk inside, take a nap, and check temps from your phone. The app logs a temperature curve, which is genuinely useful for spotting the brisket stall around 160°F and knowing when to wrap.

The alarm system works: you can set high and low temp alerts per probe, and the base unit beeps loudly enough to hear from the next room. Push notifications from the app work reliably — one buyer noted, "I was at the grocery store and got the alert that my pork shoulder hit 203°F. That alone is worth the price."

**What buyers consistently praise**

The four-probe versatility is the top compliment. "Being able to track the flat and the point separately on my brisket was a game-changer," one review said. Others appreciate the preset temperature guides in the app for chicken, beef, pork, and fish — no math needed. The build quality of the probes gets positive mentions; the silicone handles are comfortable and the stainless steel tips are sturdy.

**Where it falls short**

The probes are not fully waterproof. The connection point where the wire meets the handle is a known weak spot — if you submerge that end, moisture can seep in and give false readings. Hand-wash only. Also, the app requires a 2.4GHz WiFi network. If your router is set to 5GHz-only or uses a combined SSID, you may need to reconfigure your network before setup. It's a one-time hurdle, but frustrating if you're not tech-savvy. Finally, the base uses AAA batteries when not plugged into USB-C — there's no internal rechargeable battery, so if you're using it wirelessly, keep spares handy.

**Who should buy this**

If you smoke meat regularly — brisket, pork butt, ribs — and want to monitor from inside the house, this is a solid pick. It's also great for Thanksgiving turkey, where you can track breast and thigh temps simultaneously. Skip it if you only grill burgers and steaks on a gas grill; a two-probe Bluetooth model will do the job for less money.

**FAQ**

*Can I use this in the oven?* Yes, the probes are rated to 572°F, so they're safe for oven use. Just don't let the silicone handles touch the heating element, and keep the base outside the oven.

*How far does the WiFi range reach?* As long as the base unit is within range of your home WiFi (typically 30-50 feet through walls), you can view temps from anywhere with internet access. Bluetooth range is about 260 feet line-of-sight if you're not using WiFi.

*Do the probes need calibration?* They come factory-calibrated, but you can do an ice-water test to verify. If a probe reads off by more than 2°F, contact Govee support — they're responsive, but the probes aren't user-replaceable.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're a home smoker or serious home cook who wants multi-point temperature tracking with remote monitoring. The four probes, WiFi connectivity, and app graphing make it a strong value for the price.

**Skip it if** you need waterproof probes for sous vide or frequent immersion cleaning, or if you can't easily switch to a 2.4GHz WiFi network.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Great features and reliable performance for the money, with a few durability caveats that keep it from being perfect.
