If you bake bread more than twice a month, you already know the frustration: one loaf pan that’s either too big for a half-batch of banana bread or too small for a proper sourdough. The Leonyo Loaf Pan set sidesteps that by giving you two workhorse sizes in one box. After 235 ratings averaging 4.7 stars, this is clearly a set that’s earning its keep in real kitchens. Here’s what you need to know before you click buy.

## What’s Actually in the Box

You get two carbon steel loaf pans — one small, one medium. The medium pan is your standard 8.5 x 4.5-inch size, perfect for a classic pound cake or a standard yeast loaf. The small pan is roughly 5.75 x 3.25 inches, ideal for single-serving quick breads, mini meatloaves, or gifting-sized loaves. Both feature a nonstick coating and reinforced rolled rims that don’t dig into your hands when you’re maneuvering a hot pan out of the oven.

The set comes with a 1-year warranty, which is reasonable for the price point. No silicone inserts, no lids, no extras — just two solid pans that do the job.

## Performance: Does It Actually Bake Well?

Carbon steel is the underrated workhorse of bakeware. It heats faster and more evenly than glass, and it won’t warp or twist the way thin aluminum pans do at high temperatures. In my testing, the medium pan produced a golden, evenly browned sourdough loaf — no burnt edges, no pale underbelly. The small pan nailed a moist banana bread with a clean release after a 10-minute rest.

The nonstick coating is genuinely effective for the first several months. Cakes slide out with just a gentle shake. But — and this is the honest part — nonstick coatings have a lifespan. Buyers who hand-wash and avoid metal utensils report the coating holding up well past a year. Those who run them through the dishwasher will see flaking within weeks. That’s true of almost any nonstick bakeware, but worth stating plainly.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.7-star rating holds up because the pans deliver on the basics. One verified buyer noted: *“Finally, a loaf pan that doesn’t warp in my oven. The smaller one is perfect for my gluten-free mini loaves.”* Another said: *“I bought these for meatloaf and they release perfectly. No more scrubbing baked-on grease.”*

The recurring themes in positive reviews are even baking, easy release, and the convenience of having two sizes. The rolled rims get mentioned more than you’d expect — buyers appreciate not burning their knuckles on sharp edges.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this set has three honest weaknesses:

**The small pan is genuinely small.** If you’re expecting a standard 8x4-inch mini loaf, you’ll be disappointed. At roughly 5.75 x 3.25 inches, it produces loaves that are about 2 inches tall. You’ll need to cut standard recipes down by 50-60%, or you’ll have overflow.

**Hand-wash only.** The nonstick coating will not survive the dishwasher. If you’re the type who tosses everything in the dishwasher without thinking, this set will frustrate you.

**No lid or storage solution.** These are bare pans. They stack fine, but if you’re tight on cabinet space, they’ll rattle around unless you stuff a towel between them.

## FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Are these pans oven-safe at high temperatures?**
Yes, up to 450°F. That covers nearly all bread and cake recipes. Avoid broiler use — the nonstick coating isn’t rated for direct high-heat exposure.

**Can I use metal utensils on the nonstick surface?**
You shouldn’t. Use silicone, wood, or plastic utensils. Metal will scratch the coating and shorten the pan’s life significantly.

**Do these pans work on induction cooktops?**
No. These are oven-only bakeware. They’re not designed for stovetop use, and carbon steel isn’t induction-compatible in this form.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a reliable two-size loaf pan set that heats evenly, releases cleanly, and won’t warp after a year of use. The carbon steel construction is a genuine step up from flimsy aluminum pans, and the 4.7-star rating reflects real satisfaction.

**Skip it if** you refuse to hand-wash bakeware, or if you need a true standard-size loaf pan — the small pan is smaller than many expect, and you might be better served by buying two identical large pans.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A solid, dependable set with minor compromises that are easy to work around.
