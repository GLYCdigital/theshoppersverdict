**What you actually get**

The rack measures roughly 10 x 16 inches, which fits standard half-sheet pans with room to spare. It's a one-piece welded grid construction — no hinges, no folding parts, no weak points. The legs are sturdy and elevate the surface about an inch off your counter or sheet pan. As shown in the product image, the wire is noticeably thicker than typical racks, and the tight grid spacing (about half-inch intervals) supports even delicate items like madeleines without sagging.

**Real-world performance**

I put this through a week of heavy use: three batches of sugar cookies, a full sheet-pan of roasted chicken thighs, and a lemon loaf that needed proper airflow. The rack didn't flex under the chicken — a problem I've had with cheaper racks that bow in the middle. The elevated design does its job with airflow; cookies cooled evenly without soggy bottoms, and the loaf released cleanly after about 10 minutes.

The stainless steel is the real selling point here. It's oven-safe up to 450°F, so you can use it as a roasting rack directly in the oven — something you can't do with coated racks. **Where it falls short**
