If you've ever wrestled with round skewers that spin food like a rotisserie every time you flip, you know the frustration. The OXO Good Grips Grilling Skewers (4.6/5 from 229 ratings) exist to solve exactly that problem — and they largely succeed.

**What you actually get:** A set of 6 skewers, each 16 inches long, crafted from stainless steel with a flat, squared profile rather than the typical round wire. The handles are molded with OXO's signature soft-grip overmold and feature a loop at the end for hanging. They're dishwasher-safe, though hand washing preserves the finish longer.

**The design that matters:** The flat shaft is the headline feature. When you slide on cubes of chicken, beef, or vegetables, the squared edges bite into the food and hold it in place. Flip the skewer, and the food flips with it — no more using tongs to individually rotate each piece. This isn't a gimmick; it's the difference between uneven char and properly cooked kebabs.

**Build quality and real-world performance:** The stainless steel is thick enough to resist bending under heavy loads — a full skewer of pork shoulder and pineapple won't bow in the middle. The pointed tip pierces raw meat cleanly without tearing, and the handle stays cool during normal use, though loading food all the way to the base will transfer heat quickly. At 16 inches, these skewers fit comfortably across a standard 22-inch kettle grill or a 30-inch gas model with room to spare.

**What buyers consistently praise:** In the review data, three themes dominate. First, the no-spin design: *"These are the first skewers I've used where the food actually stays put when I turn them — night and day difference from round ones."* Second, the length: *"I can fit a full meal on one skewer instead of juggling six short ones."* Third, the build quality: *"Held up through a full summer of grilling and still look new."*

**Where they fall short:** The pointed tips are sharp — obviously — but there's no protective sleeve or case included. If you store them loose in a drawer, they'll snag on other utensils. The handles, while comfortable, are also the weakest point for longevity; heavy dishwasher use can dull the soft-grip over time. And while the flat design prevents spinning, it doesn't prevent food from sliding off the tip when you lift the skewer vertically — load from the handle end first to keep everything in place.

**Who should buy these:** Weekend grillers who cook kebabs more than twice a season will get immediate value. Also, anyone who's ever cursed at a round skewer while trying to build an even char — this is the fix. If you're a camping minimalist who needs to pack light, the lack of a case might be a dealbreaker. And if you only grill a couple times a year, cheaper round skewers will do the job — but you'll still fight the spin.

**FAQ:**

*Are these skewers long enough for a standard grill?* Yes. At 16 inches, they span most home grills. For very large gas grills (30+ inches), you'll have a few inches of overhang on each side — that's fine.

*Can they go in the dishwasher?* The manufacturer says yes, but hand washing extends the grip lifespan. If you use the dishwasher, remove them promptly to prevent water spots.

*How many skewers do I need for a party?* For 8–10 guests, plan on 2 skewers per person. The set of 6 covers a small gathering; consider buying two sets for larger events.

**Skip it if:** You need something space-saving with a carrying case, or you're a once-a-year griller who can't justify the premium over basic skewers.
