Most coffee grinders in this price range are either blade-based (read: glorified choppers) or burr units with tiny 4-ounce hoppers that force you to refill constantly. The Hamilton Beach Electric Burr Grinder takes a different approach: a genuinely large 16-ounce hopper paired with a real burr mechanism and 18 grind settings. For anyone grinding for a household of multiple daily drinkers, that capacity alone changes the calculus.

## What You Actually Get

The unit ships with the grinder body, a removable 16oz bean hopper with lid, a clear plastic grind chamber, and the upper burr assembly. There's also a slide-out drawer-style catch bin that holds a solid amount of ground coffee. The controls are refreshingly simple: an on/off switch, a grind time dial (labeled in seconds up to 40), and a separate grind size selector. No digital displays, no smartphone app, no programming — just turn, press, grind.

## Performance and Build Quality

The burrs themselves are conical and do legitimate work. In testing, the fine settings produced espresso-appropriate powder with minimal boulders, and the coarse settings yielded uniform chunks suitable for French press. The 18 settings span a practical range — you won't find Turkish-level pulverizing here, but the vast majority of home brewers won't miss it.

The 16oz hopper is the standout feature. Most burr grinders at this price cap out around 8 ounces, forcing you to grind in batches. Here you can dump a full bag of beans and grind enough for a week of morning pots. The timed grind function is a clever addition — set it for the seconds you need, and it delivers consistent doses once you dial in your preferred output.

Build quality is typical Hamilton Beach: functional plastic that won't win design awards but feels solid enough for daily use. The motor handles the larger loads without bogging down, though it's not quiet — expect a noticeable hum that lasts the duration of the grind.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Amazon reviewers with verified purchases repeatedly highlight the capacity and grind consistency. One writes: "Finally a grinder that handles my family's coffee habit without grinding in batches. The 16oz hopper is a game changer." Another notes: "The grind settings actually make a difference — my pour-over went from bitter to smooth once I found the right number."

The ease of cleaning also earns consistent mention. The upper burr pulls out for rinsing, and the grind chamber wipes clean quickly. Multiple reviewers confirm the tool-free disassembly is a practical upgrade over sealed burr units.

## Where It Falls Short

Static cling is the most common complaint — and it's legitimate. Fine grounds stick to the clear plastic catch bin, and some reviewers note grounds escaping around the drawer edge. It's not a dealbreaker, but you'll want to tap the bin firmly before transferring grounds.

The grind size dial is smooth-turning without detents. During operation, vibration can nudge it slightly. It's worth double-checking your setting before each grind. A few reviewers also mention that the "fine" end of the spectrum doesn't go quite fine enough for true espresso machines requiring very fine powder — though it works fine for pressurized portafilter machines.

## Who Should Buy This

This grinder is ideal for households brewing 4+ cups daily who want consistent burr-ground coffee without spending $150+. It's also a strong pick for French press and drip coffee drinkers who value the large hopper for batch grinding. If you're a single-cup pour-over enthusiast making one serving at a time, a smaller unit with finer adjustment range would serve you better.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this grind for espresso?**
A: At its finest setting, it produces a fine grind suitable for pressurized basket espresso machines and moka pots. Dedicated espresso enthusiasts with non-pressurized portafilters will want more precision than this offers.

**Q: Is the hopper really big enough for a full bag of beans?**
A: Yes — 16 ounces holds a standard 12oz retail bag with room to spare. You can grind a week's worth for a typical household in one go.

**Q: Does it create a mess?**
A: Some static cling on fine settings, yes. The catch bin pulls out for transfer, but expect some stray grounds. It's manageable but not spotless.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want burr-grind consistency with the capacity to handle a whole bag of beans at once, and you primarily brew drip, French press, or cold brew. The 18 settings and timer give you real control without complexity.

**Skip it if:** You're a single-cup brewer needing precision espresso grinds, or you hate dealing with static cling and occasional grounds around the catch bin.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The Hamilton Beach Electric Burr Grinder with Large 16oz Hopper delivers genuine value for multi-cup households. It's not perfect, but at this price point, the combination of real burrs, massive capacity, and practical controls makes it a smart buy for most home brewers.
