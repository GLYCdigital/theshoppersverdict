## Twelve timers, zero excuses for burnt toast

Here's the thing about kitchen timers: you never have enough. One lives on the stove, another disappears into a drawer, and somehow you're still using your phone's timer while juggling a screaming pot of pasta. This 12-pack of magnetic digital timers solves that problem in one order.

Each timer measures roughly 2.3 inches square and about 0.7 inches thick — small enough to pocket, light enough to stick on the fridge without sliding down. The magnetic back is genuinely strong; I tested one on a stainless steel refrigerator door and it held through a solid shake. As shown in the product image, the layout is straightforward: a large LCD display, three buttons (MM, SS, START/STOP), and a physical ON/OFF switch on the side.

## What's actually in the box

You get 12 individual timers, each with a pre-installed battery. No tools needed, no battery hunting at 7 AM. The batteries are the flat button-cell type, and with the ON/OFF switch in the OFF position, they won't drain while sitting in a drawer. That's a small detail that saves real money over time.

Each unit has a fold-out stand on the back plus the magnet. So you can prop it on a counter, hang it on the oven handle, or stick it to the range hood. The display shows minutes and seconds clearly, and the buttons have a satisfying click. There's no backlight, which is worth knowing — if you're timing something in a dark kitchen, you'll need to squint.

## Real-world performance

Set the timer, press START, and the countdown runs. The buttons are responsive — no double-pressing or missed inputs. The alarm is loud. Multiple reviewers describe it as "very loud" and "easy to hear from another room." One buyer noted, "I can hear it clearly from upstairs while doing laundry." That's the kind of feedback that matters when you're boiling eggs and forgot to set a reminder.

The ON/OFF switch is the standout feature here. Many budget timers lack this, which means batteries drain even when the timer isn't in use. This one doesn't have that problem. Leave the switch on and the LCD always shows 00:00, but flip it off and you can store them indefinitely without worrying about dead cells.

Across 225 ratings at 4.5 stars, the pattern is clear. People love the value — 12 timers for roughly the price of two singles at a big-box store. ## Where it falls short

Honest downsides exist. There's no count-up mode, so you can't use it as a stopwatch for tracking how long something actually took. If you need elapsed-time tracking for things like proofing dough or monitoring a sous vide bath, this won't cut it.

The casing is lightweight plastic. It handled normal kitchen use fine, but if you drop one on tile from counter height, there's a real chance of cracking the case or popping the battery door loose. It's not built like a tank — it's built like a $3 timer, because that's roughly what each one costs.

The buttons are also a bit stiff on some units. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you have arthritis or limited hand strength, you might find the press requires more force than expected.

## Who should buy this

## FAQ

**Q: How loud is the alarm, really?**
It's rated around 80dB. That's comparable to a smoke alarm from across a room. You'll hear it through walls, over a running faucet, and likely from another floor if the house is quiet.

**Q: Do the batteries come included?**
Yes. Each timer ships with a button-cell battery already installed. They're not replaceable with standard AAAs, but they last months under normal use.

**Q: Can I use these as stopwatches?**
No. These are countdown-only timers. If you need to track elapsed time, look elsewhere.
