Let's be direct: most silicone baking cups are floppy, frustrating little things that tip over the moment you look at them. The OXO Good Grips version solves that with one smart design choice — a reinforced rim that holds its shape when empty. That alone makes this 12-pack worth a serious look.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains 12 cups in four cheerful colors (blue, green, orange, red — three of each). Each cup measures roughly 2.5 inches across the top and holds about 2 ounces of batter. That's slightly smaller than a standard muffin tin cup, so adjust your recipes accordingly.

The silicone is food-grade and BPA-free, with a smooth interior that doesn't require heavy greasing. OXO rates them for temperatures from -40°F to 450°F, which covers everything from frozen yogurt bites to standard baking.

## Real-World Performance

I tested these across three batches: classic blueberry muffins, chocolate cupcakes, and a batch of cornbread. The reinforced rim does exactly what it promises — the cups sat upright on the baking sheet without slouching, and I could fill them one-handed without wrestling the silicone.

Release was impressive. The muffins came out with only a light spritz of cooking spray, and the cupcakes didn't need any. The cornbread needed a butter knife run around the edge, but that's more about the batter than the cup.

Cleanup is straightforward. They're dishwasher-safe (top rack), but a quick hand wash with warm soapy water works fine. One honest note: after the chocolate batch, the cups picked up a slight brown tint that didn't fully wash out. It's cosmetic — doesn't affect performance — but if you're particular about appearance, know that staining happens.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews reflects real satisfaction. One recurring theme is durability — these don't warp or tear like thinner silicone competitors. As one reviewer put it: "These are thicker than other brands I've tried. They actually hold their shape instead of folding over when you pick them up."

## Where They Fall Short

The biggest tradeoff is size. These cups are shallower than standard paper liners. Your muffins will come out shorter and wider — more like a mini bundt shape than a tall bakery-style top. If you're chasing that classic domed muffin look, you'll be disappointed.

The staining issue I mentioned is worth repeating. Heavily pigmented batters (red velvet, chocolate, turmeric-spiced) will leave residue. It doesn't affect food, but it does make the cups look tired after a few months.

One more minor quibble: the colors are fun, but they're not colorfast forever. After repeated dishwasher cycles, the red cups fade noticeably faster than the others.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Eco-conscious bakers** who want to eliminate paper liner waste — these will outlast hundreds of disposable liners
- **Meal preppers** who freeze individual portions — the freezer rating and easy release are genuinely useful
- **Parents** baking with kids — the sturdy rim means less mess from spilled batter

**Skip them if** you're a professional baker needing consistent, tall muffin tops, or if you're looking for a one-size-fits-all replacement for every paper liner recipe.

## FAQ

**Do I need to grease these cups before baking?**
For most batters, no. A light spritz helps with very sticky recipes like cornbread or banana bread, but standard muffin and cupcake batters release cleanly without oil.

**Can I use these in a standard muffin tin?**
Yes, but note the size difference. They fit standard tins but will sit lower than the rim, producing shorter baked goods. They also work fine sitting directly on a baking sheet.

**Are these truly BPA-free?**
Yes. OXO uses food-grade silicone that's free from BPA, BPS, and phthalates. The cups are also FDA-compliant for food contact.
