**The Short Version:** If you're hosting a bridal shower, birthday party, or brunch and want a cake stand that looks like it cost three times the price, the Audab Metal Gold Dessert Table Display Set delivers. But it's not the towering centerpiece the photos suggest — this is a compact, practical display piece that shines on a buffet table, not a statement piece for a 50-person wedding.

---

**What's Actually in the Box**

The set includes a three-tier cake stand, a two-tier cupcake tower, and a small serving tray. All pieces are gold-finished metal — the product listing calls it "metal," and that's accurate: this is not plastic with a metallic coat. The finish is a warm champagne gold, not the brassy yellow you see on cheaper imports.

Assembly takes about two minutes. Each tier screws onto a central pole, and the whole thing stands about 16 inches tall when fully stacked. The cupcake tower is a separate piece — you get two usable display units, not one combined monstrosity.

---

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

I tested this with a 7-inch layer cake on the bottom tier, a dozen cupcakes spread across the top two tiers, and cookies on the side tray. The construction held up without any flexing or tilting — a genuine concern with budget-tier stands that use thin press-fit connections. The screw-in design is the smart choice here; it distributes weight evenly down the center pole.

One thing that surprised me: the gold finish held up to repeated washing. I ran the pieces through a gentle dishwasher cycle (the listing doesn't explicitly say dishwasher-safe, so hand-washing is safer), and there was zero fading or peeling. That's rare at this price point.

**Where It Falls Short — Read This Before Buying**

The dimensions are the elephant in the room. Product photos make the tiers look like they could hold a full 10-inch cake. They can't. The bottom tier maxes out around 8 inches, and the top tier is closer to 6. If you're baking a standard 9-inch round cake, it won't fit without overhanging the edge — which actually looks fine on the bottom tier, but the top tiers will look cramped.

There's also no handle. Moving the assembled stand across a room requires two hands and a careful shuffle. It's not heavy — maybe 3 pounds total — but the lack of a grab point makes transport awkward. Plan to assemble it in place.

**Who Should Buy This**

It's ideal for bridal showers, afternoon teas, and birthday parties where presentation matters but space is limited. **FAQ**

**Q: Is this safe for outdoor use?**
A: The metal construction handles heat well, but the gold finish can tarnish with prolonged sun exposure. Use it outdoors for an afternoon, but don't leave it out overnight.

**Q: Can I put hot items directly on the tiers?**
A: No. This is for room-temperature desserts. Hot items will damage the finish and could warp the metal. Let everything cool completely before displaying.

**Q: How do I clean it?**
A: Hand-wash with mild soap and warm water. Dry immediately with a soft cloth to prevent water spots. Avoid abrasive scrubbers — they'll scratch the gold finish.
