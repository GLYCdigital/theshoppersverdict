## What You Actually Get

This is a bamboo board, not plastic or acacia. Bamboo is harder than most domestic hardwoods, which means it resists deep scoring better than pine or rubberwood, but it also means it's tougher on your knife edges. The double-sided design is the headline feature: one side for raw proteins, flip it over for vegetables, fruit, or bread.

As shown in the product image, there's a juice groove running around the perimeter on at least one side — a small detail that matters a lot when you're carving a roast or slicing tomatoes. There's also a handle cut into the board, which makes it easy to lift off the counter and either hang it for storage or carry it to the sink without dragging juice across your kitchen.

## Real-World Performance

Bamboo boards hit a sweet spot for most home kitchens. They're lighter than end-grain maple, they don't absorb odors the way plastic can, and they're easier on knives than glass or stone. The IGOYE board follows that pattern. It's sturdy enough for daily chopping, and the double-sided feature genuinely reduces the "I just cut raw chicken on this, now I need to wash it before touching the lettuce" dance.

The juice groove deserves specific mention — it's deeper than what I've seen on many boards in this price range. That means less red liquid pooling on your counter when you're carving. The handle is a small touch, but it makes a real difference in handling.

## Where It Falls Short

No board is perfect, and this one has two notable tradeoffs. First, bamboo is hard — harder than acacia or walnut — and that means your knives will dull slightly faster than they would on a softer wood board. If you're using high-end Japanese knives, you'll notice this. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's real.

Second, the board doesn't have rubber feet on both sides — which makes sense given the double-sided design, but it means one side will sit flush against the counter and can slide during aggressive chopping. A damp paper towel underneath solves the problem, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

## FAQ

**Is this cutting board dishwasher safe?**
No. Bamboo will warp and crack in the dishwasher. Hand wash with warm soapy water and dry promptly. Occasionally treat with food-grade mineral oil to keep it from drying out.

**Does the juice groove work for carving large roasts?**
It catches runoff effectively, but for a large turkey or roast, you'll still want a carving board with a deeper trough. This is fine for everyday meat prep.

**How do I keep bamboo from cracking?**
Dry it standing on its edge, not flat. Apply mineral oil monthly or whenever the surface looks dry. Avoid soaking it in water.
