Here's the thing about portable washers: they're ugly. They sit on your counter or in a corner of your laundry room, collecting dust, splatters, and the kind of grime you don't want to think about. The Dustpro cover solves that problem with a simple, practical design that's earned a solid 4.6-star rating from 229 buyers. But is it worth your money? Let's dig in.

## What You Actually Get

The Dustpro is a fitted cover made from 600D Oxford cloth — the same material used in decent luggage and tool bags — with a waterproof coating on the interior. It's not a flimsy piece of plastic. The cover wraps around your portable washer with an elastic hem at the bottom that cinches it tight. The standout feature is the full-length zipper running down the front, giving you access to the control panel, detergent drawer, and lid without pulling the whole thing off.

It's designed for portable and compact washers up to roughly 1.5 cubic feet, which covers most countertop models from brands like BLACK+DECKER, hOmeLabs, and Giantex. The dimensions in the product listing are worth checking against your specific unit before ordering — this isn't a one-size-fits-all situation.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the Dustpro earns its keep. The waterproof backing actually works. One reviewer noted, "I spilled a full cup of water on the top while refilling the reservoir, and it beaded up and rolled right off. No soaking through." That's the kind of practical detail that matters when your washer sits next to a sink.

The zipper design is genuinely useful, not a gimmick. You can open the front panel, start a cycle, close it, and walk away — the cover stays in place the entire time. For anyone using their portable washer in a kitchen or bathroom where aesthetics matter, that's a significant upgrade over wrestling a generic cover on and off every time you do laundry.

The elastic hem does its job. It keeps the cover anchored even when the machine vibrates during the spin cycle. Multiple buyers mentioned the cover "stays put" and doesn't shift around, which was a common complaint with cheaper alternatives.

## Where It Falls Short

The sizing is the biggest issue. Several reviewers with smaller 0.9 cu ft units reported the cover was "roomy but not baggy" — fine — but a few found it too large, with excess material bunching at the bottom. Measure your washer's height and width before ordering, and if you're on the smaller end, expect some slack.

The zipper pull is small. It's functional, but if you have arthritis or limited hand strength, you'll find it fiddly. A larger tab or loop would have been a thoughtful addition.

One buyer flagged that the cover isn't machine washable — spot clean only. Given that it's designed to repel water and dust, that's acceptable, but worth knowing before you toss it in with your next load.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have a portable washer in a visible area — kitchen, bathroom, or laundry room — and want it to look intentional rather than like an appliance strapped to your counter. You're tired of wiping dust off the controls. You want a cover that doesn't require full removal every time you use the machine.

**Skip it if:** You have a larger portable unit (over 1.5 cu ft) or a full-size washer. You want a cover that doubles as a drying rack (this doesn't). You're on a tight budget and don't care about aesthetics — a basic tarp-style cover will be cheaper.

**Q: Will this fit my BLACK+DECKER portable washer?**
A: Most models under 1.5 cu ft fit, but check the dimensions. The cover is roughly 28" wide and 33" tall. If your unit is significantly smaller, expect bunching at the bottom.

**Q: Can I run the washer with the cover on?**
A: Yes — the zipper gives you access to the controls and detergent drawer. The cover doesn't interfere with ventilation. Just keep the top panel clear if your model vents upward.

**Q: Is it actually waterproof?**
A: The interior coating repels water well. One reviewer spilled a cup of water directly on top with zero absorption. It's not designed for full submersion, but for spills and splashes, it's more than sufficient.
