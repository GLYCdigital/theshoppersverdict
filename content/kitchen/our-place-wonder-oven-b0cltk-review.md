# Our Place Wonder Oven Review: The 12-in-1 That Actually Delivers

The countertop oven market is crowded with me-too air fryers that all promise the same golden-brown crunch. The Our Place Wonder Oven stands apart by doing something genuinely different: combining steam injection with convection heat in a compact package. After spending two weeks cooking everything from frozen tater tots to a full roast chicken, here's what I found.

## What's Actually in the Box

The Wonder Oven ships with the main unit, a steam tray, a perforated steamer basket, a sheet pan, and a reversible grill/griddle plate. That's not the usual stack of cheap accessories — the steamer basket is sturdy stainless steel, and the ceramic-coated sheet pan feels substantial. The included recipe booklet is actually useful, with realistic cook times rather than the optimistic guesses most brands include.

## Performance: Where the Steam Magic Happens

The headline feature is the Dual-Fan Speed Steam system. You fill the small steam reservoir, and the oven injects moisture during the cooking cycle. This works — but not equally across all functions.

**What excels:**
- **Air fry mode with steam:** Frozen french fries come out crisp outside, fluffy inside. The steam prevents the dry, chalky texture you get from most air fryers.
- **Baking:** The steam setting produces bakery-quality crusts on bread and rolls. My test sourdough scored 9/10 for crust structure.
- **Reheating:** Leftover pizza regains its fresh-from-the-oven quality — the steam rehydrates the crust while the convection crisps the top.

**What's average:**
- **Broil function:** It works, but the heating element is small. Cheese on casseroles melts evenly, but you won't get restaurant-grade char.
- **Slow cook:** This is really a "keep warm with occasional steam" mode, not a true slow cooker replacement.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The ceramic-coated interior is genuinely non-stick and cleans up with a damp cloth — no scrubbing required for most messes. The touchscreen display is responsive, and the preheat indicator is accurate. The oven reaches 400°F in about 4 minutes, which is competitive for this class.

The 12-quart capacity is the sweet spot. It fits a 9-inch cake pan, a 3-pound chicken, or 4 servings of fries. It's not a family-of-six appliance, but for couples and small households, it replaces a toaster oven, air fryer, and steamer in one footprint.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Amazon reviewers echo the steam performance. One verified buyer wrote: *"I've owned three air fryers and this is the first one that doesn't turn chicken breast into shoe leather."* Another noted: *"The steam function makes reheating leftovers actually taste like fresh cooking, not microwaved sadness."*

The design also earns consistent mentions — the ceramic coating comes in multiple colors, and it's genuinely compact for what it does.

## Where It Falls Short

The 4.0 average rating reflects real frustrations. The most common complaint is the noise — at high convection settings, the fan is loud enough to make conversation difficult in an open kitchen. Several reviewers also reported the ceramic coating chipping when they used metal tongs (the manual warns against this, but it's easy to forget).

The price is the other sticking point. At its typical street price, it's 2-3x more expensive than comparable air fryers. You're paying for the steam tech and the design — both are real, but they're not features everyone needs.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:**
- You cook for 1-3 people and want one appliance that air fries, bakes, and steams well
- You're tired of dry, chalky air-fried food and want the steam advantage
- You have limited counter space and need a compact, good-looking appliance that pulls triple duty

**Skip it if:**
- You regularly cook for 5+ people — the capacity will frustrate you
- You're on a tight budget — a basic air fryer at a third of the price covers 80% of the functions
- You need a true slow cooker replacement — this isn't it

## FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Can it replace my toaster oven and air fryer?**
A: Yes, for most tasks. It toasts, bakes, air fries, and reheats better than a standard toaster oven. The only thing it doesn't do is broil as aggressively as a dedicated broiler.

**Q: Is the ceramic coating durable?**
A: It's fine with the included silicone utensils and gentle cleaning, but it chips if you use metal. Several Amazon reviews report this exact issue. Treat it like a non-stick pan, not an appliance you can abuse.

**Q: How hard is it to clean?**
A: Easier than most air fryers. The interior wipes down quickly, and the accessories are dishwasher-safe. The steam reservoir is the one finicky part — you need to dry it after each use to prevent mineral buildup.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The Our Place Wonder Oven is the best countertop cooker I've tested for its specific niche: compact, design-forward, and genuinely better at air frying thanks to the steam tech. It's not for everyone — the price and capacity limit its appeal — but if you're tired of dry air-fried food and want an appliance that looks as good as it cooks, this is worth the premium.

**Buy it if** you value food quality over price and cook for a small household. **Skip it if** you need serious capacity or you're happy with the results from your current air fryer.
