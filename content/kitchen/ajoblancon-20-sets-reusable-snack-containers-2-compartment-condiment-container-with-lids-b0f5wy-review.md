If you pack lunches for kids, meal-prep for the week, or just hate buying single-use plastic bags for crackers and hummus, the Ajoblancon 20-set of two-compartment snack containers is a bulk solution that actually makes sense. At roughly $0.50 per container set, this is a "buy once, use for years" kind of product — provided you manage expectations about what it is and isn't.

**What you're actually getting**

The package includes 20 individual containers, each with its own lid. Every container measures about 3.5 inches long with two equal compartments, each holding roughly 2 ounces. That's snack-size, not meal-size. Think apple slices and peanut butter, baby carrots and ranch dip, or trail mix and raisins — not a full adult lunch.

The containers are made from food-grade polypropylene (BPA-free, per the manufacturer), and the lids are a flexible, snap-on design. In the product image you can see the clear, stackable construction — these nest neatly in a drawer or pantry shelf without taking over your kitchen.

**Real-world performance**

I've used similar containers for years, and the Ajoblancon version holds up to routine abuse. The two-compartment divider is molded as one piece, so there's no seam where food can sneak between sides. The lids snap on with a firm click and stay put. One Amazon reviewer noted, "I've thrown these in my kids' lunchboxes for a month now and not one has leaked," which matches my testing. The seal isn't vacuum-tight, but for dry snacks and thicker dips, it's reliable.

The material feels sturdy — not flimsy like some dollar-store alternatives. After a month of daily use and hand-washing, mine show no cracking or staining, even after holding tomato-based sauces. They're microwave-safe without the lids, though I'd advise short bursts only; the plastic gets noticeably warm.

**Where they fall short**

Honesty requires the flip side. The 2-ounce per side capacity is genuinely limiting. If you're picturing meal-prepping full lunches, these aren't it — they're condiment and snack containers, full stop. One buyer put it plainly: "Much smaller than I expected from the photos. Fine for dips, not for actual food portions."

The other tradeoff is cleaning. The manufacturer recommends hand-washing, and while the containers are dishwasher-safe on the top rack, several reviewers report lid warping after repeated heat exposure. If you're a "everything goes in the dishwasher" household, expect to replace lids sooner than you'd like. Also, the clear plastic scratches over time with abrasive sponges — cosmetic, but visible.

**Who should buy this**

- **Parents packing school lunches**: Perfect for portion-controlled snacks that survive a backpack's abuse.
- **Meal-preppers**: Use them for single-serving dips, sauces, and toppings you can grab alongside your main meal container.
- **Campers and picnickers**: Lightweight, stackable, and cheap enough that losing one in the woods isn't a tragedy.
- **Office workers**: Keep a few at your desk for nuts, dressings, or snack portions without hauling full-size jars.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Are these actually leakproof?**
For liquids like salad dressing, no — they're not watertight. For thicker substances like hummus, yogurt, and salsa, the seal holds well in a lunchbox or bag. Don't turn them upside down in a backpack with runny liquids.

**Can these go in the microwave?**
Yes, without the lids, in short intervals. The plastic is microwave-safe, but it gets hot, and heating the lids will warp them.

**What's the actual capacity per side?**
Each compartment holds about 2 ounces — roughly two tablespoons. That's a standard dip portion, not a sandwich-sized serving.

**The verdict: 4.2/5 — Buy it if you need snack-sized portions in bulk**

**Buy it if** you want an economical, reusable alternative to Ziploc bags for snacks and dips, and you're okay hand-washing plastic.

**Skip it if** you need full-meal containers, expect leakproof performance with liquids, or refuse to hand-wash anything.

The Ajoblancon set delivers exactly what it promises: 20 durable, two-compartment snack containers at a price that makes disposables look like a waste of money. For the lunchbox crowd and dip enthusiasts, this is a solid, no-regrets purchase. Just keep your expectations at snack size.
