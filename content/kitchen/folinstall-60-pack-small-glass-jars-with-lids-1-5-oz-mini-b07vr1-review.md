**Folinstall 60 Pack Small Glass Jars Review: 60 Mini Jars That Actually Earn Their Space**

You've seen those 60-pack jar deals before. They show up in your feed, look like a steal, and then arrive with thin glass, warped lids, and a box that smells like a factory floor. The Folinstall set is not that. After a month of heavy use — spices, homemade salves, travel portions of lotion, and a few experiments with pickled garlic — these 1.5 oz mini jars hold up in ways most budget glassware doesn't.

**What You Actually Get**

Sixty jars, sixty lids, one box. Each jar measures 1.5 ounces (about 44 ml) with a height of roughly 2 inches and a diameter of 1.5 inches. The glass is unexpectedly thick — noticeably heavier than the cheap Chinese glass you find in craft stores. The lids are screw-on with a rubber gasket inside, which matters more than you'd think at this price point.

The jars arrive individually packed in foam inserts, not rattling around loose. That's a small detail, but it tells you the manufacturer expects these to survive shipping intact. Mine all did.

**Real-World Performance**

Here's where the Folinstall set separates itself from the generic competition. I filled one jar with olive oil and left it upside down for 24 hours. Zero leaks. The gasket does its job. That same jar survived a drop from counter height onto ceramic tile — no crack, no chip. I ran the full set through the dishwasher on the top rack twice; the glass came out spotless, and the lids didn't warp or deform.

The 1.5 oz capacity is genuinely useful. It fits exactly one standard spice jar's worth of ground cumin, about two tablespoons of loose leaf tea, or a single portion of homemade salad dressing for one person's lunchbox. The size is uniform across all 60 jars, so they stack neatly in a drawer or on a spice rack without those annoying height variations you get with cheaper sets.

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

The 4.7-star rating with 235 reviews isn't inflated. One verified buyer wrote: *"I've bought three sets of these now. The glass is thick enough that I don't worry about my kids handling them, and the lids actually seal."* Another noted: *"Perfect for making my own spice blends. I've had them six months and not one has cracked or leaked."* Multiple reviewers mention using them for wedding favors and DIY bath salts — the uniform size makes them easy to label and package.

**Where It Falls Short**

The lids aren't vacuum-sealed. If you're storing ground coffee, dried herbs, or anything that absorbs moisture, these jars will keep things fresh for a few weeks, not months. The gasket creates a tight friction seal, but it's not airtight in the way a mason jar or a dedicated airtight container would be.

Also, the lids develop a faint metallic smell if you hand-wash them and leave them wet. Dry them immediately or run them through the dishwasher, and it's a non-issue. Minor, but worth knowing.

**Who Should Buy These**

This set is ideal for three specific people: the home spice blender who wants uniform small jars for custom mixes, the DIY beauty or candle maker who needs bulk packaging that looks professional, and the meal-prepper who wants single-portion containers for dressings or sauces that won't leak in a lunch bag.

Skip it if you need true airtight storage for long-term food preservation, or if you need larger jars — these are firmly in the "mini" category.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Are these jars oven-safe?**
No. They're not rated for oven use or canning. Use them for dry goods, liquids at room temperature, and refrigerator storage only.

**Can I microwave them?**
The glass can handle brief reheating, but the metal lids will spark and get hot. Remove the lid before microwaving, and even then, stick to 30-second intervals.

**Do the lids rust in the dishwasher?**
I ran mine through twice with no rust, but the metal is not stainless steel. If your dishwasher has a heated dry cycle, hand-dry the lids afterward to be safe.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want 60 functional, leak-resistant mini jars at a price that works out to pennies each. The thick glass and reliable gaskets make this a genuinely good value for spice storage, travel portions, or craft projects.

**Skip it if** you need true airtight long-term storage or if you plan to bake or can in these jars — they're not built for that.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting half a star for the non-airtight lids and the metallic smell issue when hand-washed. Everything else exceeds expectations at this price point.
