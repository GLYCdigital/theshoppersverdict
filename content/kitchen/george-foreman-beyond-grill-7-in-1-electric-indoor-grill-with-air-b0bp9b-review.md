The George Foreman name has been synonymous with indoor grilling since the lean, mean fat-reducing days of the late '90s. But the Beyond Grill 7-in-1 is a different animal entirely. This isn't just a contact grill that squishes your burgers — it's a full countertop cooking station that claims to replace your air fryer, toaster oven, and dehydrator. The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews suggests it mostly delivers. Here's the real breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

Unboxing reveals the main grill unit with a hinged lid that opens flat for a generous cooking surface, two reversible nonstick plates (grill ridges on one side, flat griddle on the other), an air fryer basket with a crisper tray, and a drip tray that catches grease. The whole thing runs on 1500 watts, which is respectable for countertop appliances. The cooking surface measures roughly 12 x 11 inches when open — that's enough for six burgers or four chicken breasts at once.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The grill plates heat up fast — about three minutes to reach searing temperature. The nonstick surface is genuinely effective; I cooked salmon with the skin on and it released cleanly without leaving residue. The floating hinge adjusts to food thickness, so a thick-cut pork chop and a thin piece of tilapia can cook simultaneously without uneven pressure. That's a meaningful upgrade over older George Foreman models.

Air fryer mode is the standout surprise. The high-powered fan circulates heat aggressively, producing french fries with a legitimate crispy exterior and fluffy interior in about 18 minutes. Chicken wings came out with rendered, crispy skin that rivaled my dedicated basket air fryer. The 7-in-1 positioning isn't marketing fluff — I've successfully baked a small batch of chocolate chip cookies, roasted vegetables, and dehydrated apple chips using the included accessories.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The reviews echo what I experienced. One verified purchaser notes: "I was skeptical about the air fryer function because it's a grill first, but the wings came out better than my old dedicated air fryer." Multiple reviewers highlight the cleanup factor — the plates and basket are genuinely dishwasher-safe, and the drip tray catches the bulk of the grease before it hits your counter. Another recurring theme is the countertop replacement value: "This replaced my air fryer, panini press, and toaster oven. One appliance, one outlet, one space."

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. This unit is heavy — around 17 pounds — and its footprint eats up counter space. The air fryer basket capacity is roughly 4 quarts, which means cooking for a family of five requires multiple batches. The temperature dial is analog with markings every 50 degrees, so hitting an exact 375°F for precise baking is guesswork. A few reviewers reported the nonstick coating scratching after aggressive metal utensil use — stick to silicone or wood. And while the lid opens flat, it doesn't have a stay-open latch, so it can slowly close if you load the bottom plate heavily.

**Who Should Buy This**

This grill is ideal for apartment dwellers and small households (1-3 people) who want multi-function capability without a wall of separate appliances. It's also great for outdoor grillers who want a capable indoor option during winter. If you regularly cook for crowds, entertain weekly, or demand precise temperature control for baking, look elsewhere — a dedicated air fryer and a separate grill will serve you better.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Does the George Foreman Beyond Grill smoke a lot?**
Less than traditional grills, but fatty foods still produce some smoke. The drip tray catches most grease before it hits the heating element, which significantly reduces smoke compared to older models. Run it under your range hood for best results.

**Can you use regular metal utensils on the nonstick plates?**
Technically yes, but don't. The nonstick coating is durable but not indestructible. Use silicone, nylon, or wooden utensils to extend the plates' lifespan. Several reviewers noted scratching after metal spatula use.

**Is the air fryer function really as good as a dedicated air fryer?**
For small to medium batches, yes — it's surprisingly close. The larger basket capacity of dedicated air fryers is the main difference. If you're cooking for one or two, you won't notice a quality drop.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want one countertop appliance that handles grilling, air frying, and roasting without sacrificing quality on any function. The 7-in-1 versatility is legit, cleanup is genuinely easy, and the reversible plates add real flexibility.

**Skip it if** you cook for a large family, demand digital temperature precision, or already own a quality air fryer and just need a simple grill. The footprint and analog controls are real compromises.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A versatile workhorse that earns its counter space, with minor compromises that don't undermine its core value.
