Let me save you 20 minutes of scrolling through Amazon reviews. This silicone grill mat is a **protection accessory**, not a cooking surface. Once you understand that, the 4.7-star rating makes perfect sense.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes the mat itself — a flexible, food-grade silicone sheet that drapes over your Blackstone griddle top. As shown in the product image, it's cut to fit the 22-inch and 28-inch models, with a raised lip that folds down over the front edge. There's a built-in drip channel that routes grease toward the front tray, plus a small tool slot if you want to keep your spatula nearby.

No clips, no magnets, no mounting hardware. It's a one-piece design that sits on the surface using its own weight and the slight lip over the edge.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this on a 28-inch Blackstone after a full cook session. Here's the honest breakdown:

**Heat handling:** Rated to 500°F, and it holds up fine at normal cooking temps. I pushed it to max output for 10 minutes — no melting, no off-gassing smell. The material stays pliable and doesn't warp.

**The drip tray actually works.** This is the standout feature. Grease from burgers and bacon runs toward the front channel instead of pooling on the griddle. That means less smoke, fewer flare-ups, and a significantly easier post-cook cleanup. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I used to spend 15 minutes scraping my Blackstone after every cook. Now I wipe it down and I'm done."*

**Fit and coverage:** It covers the main cooking surface well, with about a half-inch of play on each side. The lip over the front edge keeps it from sliding around during use.

## Where It Falls Short

**It's not a cooking surface.** You can't just throw this down and expect to sear steaks on it. The silicone doesn't conduct heat well enough for direct cooking, and food will stick without oil. It's strictly for covering and protecting between cooks.

**Corner curl is real.** If you leave the mat on a hot griddle and walk away, the corners can lift slightly as the silicone softens. It settles back down once cool, but it looks sloppy mid-session.

**One size doesn't fit all.** It's specifically cut for Blackstone 22" and 28" models. If you have a 36" griddle or a different brand with odd dimensions, it won't cover fully.
