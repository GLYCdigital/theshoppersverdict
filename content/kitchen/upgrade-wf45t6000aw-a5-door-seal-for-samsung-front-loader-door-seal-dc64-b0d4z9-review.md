If your Samsung front loader has started leaving puddles on the floor or you're spotting mold along the door boot, you're staring down a classic repair: the door seal. The Upgrade WF45T6000AW/A5 gasket is the replacement part that 229 Amazon shoppers have rated at 4.6 stars. Here's what that rating actually means when you're elbow-deep in a disassembled washer.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the rubber boot itself, a stainless spring clamp, and nothing else. That's the right approach — no junk screwdrivers or zip ties to inflate the price. The gasket is molded from a flexible EPDM rubber compound that feels denser than the factory Samsung part. The accordion folds are deep and evenly spaced, which matters because those folds are what allow the drum to flex during a spin cycle.

As shown in the product image, the boot has three pre-cut drain holes at the 5, 6, and 7 o'clock positions. These align with the drain ports on the WF45T6000AW and WF45T6000A5 tubs. The inner lip that seats against the drum is reinforced with a thicker bead, so it doesn't collapse when you tighten the spring clamp.

## Performance and Installation Reality

The fit is the headline here. This is an OEM-spec replacement, not a "universal" gasket that requires creative stretching. Owners report it seats properly on the first attempt. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I was dreading this repair but the seal lined up perfectly with the alignment arrows. No fighting it."* Another noted: *"The boot is actually thicker than the original Samsung part. Feels more durable."*

Installation runs 45 to 90 minutes depending on your comfort level. You'll remove the front panel, loosen the outer clamp, pull the old gasket, clean the tub lip, and reverse the process. The included spring clamp is a genuine plus — aftermarket seals often skip this, leaving you to reuse a corroded original. The rubber compresses well under the clamp, creating a watertight seal without needing excessive force.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest caveats. First, the rubber has a noticeable off-gassing smell when new. Most users say it fades after two or three hot washes, but if you're sensitive to chemical odors, run an empty cycle with vinegar before your first load of clothes.

Second, the boot is slightly stiffer than the factory rubber when cold. In a garage or basement laundry room during winter, the folds resist flexing until the washer warms up. This doesn't affect sealing, but it can make the door feel a bit harder to close for the first minute or two.

## FAQ

**Will this fit my Samsung WF45T6000AW?**  
Yes. It's specifically molded for the WF45T6000AW and WF45T6000A5 models. Double-check your model number on the door frame sticker — if it matches, this will fit without modification.

**How do I know when the seal needs replacing?**  
Water pooling under the washer, mold spots that won't clean off, or a visible tear in the boot. Also check the outer lip — if it's hardened or cracked, replace it before it leaks.

**Can I install this without removing the washer front?**  
No. You need access to the inner clamp, which sits behind the front panel. Plan to pull the washer out from the wall and lay it on its back or side.
