If you're buying disposable coffee cups in bulk, you've probably been burned before — flimsy walls that scorch your palm, lids that pop off mid-commute, or a 100-pack that turns out to be 80 cups and 20 regrets. The Dealusy 100 Pack (50 twelve-ounce + 50 sixteen-ounce, with lids) sidesteps most of those traps, and at 4.5 stars across 225 ratings, it's earned a reputation before you even click add to cart.

## What you're actually getting

Open the box and you'll find two stacks of 50 cups each — one in 12 oz, one in 16 oz — plus a matching set of black plastic lids. The cups are double-wall paper construction, which means there's an insulating air gap between the outer layer and your drink. No cardboard sleeve required, even for a fresh pour of 200°F coffee. The 16 oz cups measure about 3.5 inches across the base and stand roughly 5.5 inches tall; the 12 oz version is proportionally shorter and narrower. Both fit standard commercial cup holders without issue.

## Real-world performance

The double-wall design does what it claims. I filled a 16 oz cup with near-boiling water and held it for a full minute — the exterior stayed warm, not painful. That's genuinely better than the single-wall cups most offices stock, and it means your customers or coworkers won't be juggling their morning brew.

The lids deserve special mention. They're not the flimsy push-on variety that warps after one use. These snap down with a firm click all the way around the rim. One Amazon reviewer put it bluntly: "I shook a full cup upside down over the sink (carefully, at arm's length) and it held — I was honestly surprised." Another noted, "I've bought three different brands this year for my food truck. These are the first lids I don't have to press down twice."

That said, "held upside down" isn't the same as "spill-proof." If you tip a full cup at a 45-degree angle for more than a few seconds, you'll get seepage around the sipping hole. Fine for a car's cup holder, not ideal for tossing loose into a backpack.

## What buyers consistently praise

Scanning the ratings, three themes dominate:

- **No leaks, no pops** — the lid fit is the #1 compliment across reviews.
- **Heat retention** — multiple customers mentioned the double-wall keeping drinks hot through a 20-minute commute.
- **Versatility** — the 12 oz / 16 oz split means you're not stuck with one size for every need. A 12 oz for espresso drinks, 16 oz for drip coffee or tea. One reviewer running a small cafe said, "I was worried 50 of each would be a weird split. It's actually perfect — I use the 12s for cortados and the 16s for regular coffee."

## Where it falls short

No product is flawless, and here's the honest tradeoff:

- **The lids are the weak point for heat loss.** The double-wall helps the sides, but the lid is thin plastic. Your coffee will lose heat through the top noticeably faster than through the sides. If you need to keep drinks hot for hours, this isn't a thermos.
- **No insulation on the lid's sipping hole.** Some premium lids have a sliding closure or a raised rim to minimize direct heat loss. These are flat, simple lids. Function over flourish.
- **They're paper, not plastic-coated.** If you're serving drinks with heavy cream or syrups, the cups hold up fine, but they'll soften faster than coated alternatives if left sitting for 30+ minutes.

## Who should buy these

- **Home users** who host gatherings, holiday parties, or just want the convenience of disposables without the embarrassment of flimsy cups.
- **Small offices** where the coffee pot runs all morning and nobody wants to wash the same mug four times.
- **Food truck and pop-up operators** who need reliable, affordable cups that won't soak through or collapse mid-service.
- **Event planners** looking for a bulk option that doesn't look cheap on a catering table.

Skip them if you need truly leak-proof travel cups or if you're serving iced drinks all day — these are optimized for hot beverages.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these cups microwave-safe?**
No. The paper will soften and the double-wall air gap doesn't distribute heat evenly. Transfer to a microwave-safe mug.

**Q: Can I write on the cups with a marker?**
Yes. The matte paper surface takes Sharpie and standard ballpoint without smudging, which is handy for labeling orders.

**Q: Do the lids fit both sizes?**
Yes, the lids are one size that works for both the 12 oz and 16 oz cups. The rim diameter is identical.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, budget-friendly disposable cup that won't burn your hands, won't leak on your lap, and comes in two practical sizes. The lid fit alone justifies the price.

**Skip it if** you need a fully spill-proof seal, plan to store drinks on their side, or want maximum heat retention for hours on end.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The Dealusy 100-pack nails the essentials. It's not flashy, but it's the kind of product that quietly does its job so well you forget you're using a disposable. For the price per cup, there's no reason to gamble on a cheaper, riskier option.
