The Yedi Total Package 6-in-1 is the kitchen equivalent of a Swiss Army knife — and for once, the multi-tool approach actually works. With 215 ratings averaging 4.3 stars, this isn't a gimmick appliance that ends up in a garage sale. It's a legitimate countertop workhorse that swaps between six cooking functions: grill, griddle, waffle maker, sandwich press, Panini press, and a combination mode that lets you cook two things simultaneously.

**What you actually get**

Unboxing reveals the main unit with digital controls, a pair of reversible non-stick plates (grill ridges on one side, flat griddle on the other), a separate waffle plate set, a drip tray, and a recipe booklet. The plates click in and out with a simple release latch — no tools required. The unit measures roughly 12 x 13 inches when closed and stands upright for storage, though at 12 pounds, it's not something you'll be moving around casually.

**Performance and real-world use**

The digital temperature control is the standout feature. You can dial in anywhere from 200°F to 450°F in single-degree increments, which gives you genuine control over cooking. Grilling chicken breast at 375°F yields nice char marks without burning the exterior. The waffle plates produce evenly browned waffles in about five minutes at the recommended 400°F setting.

The 180-degree hinge is genuinely useful — you can lay both plates flat for a full griddle surface, doubling your cooking area. That's how the combo mode works: waffles on one side, bacon on the other, breakfast done in one pass.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Looking through verified reviews, three themes dominate. First, versatility: "I've used this every day since I got it — pancakes, grilled cheese, waffles, even seared salmon," one buyer notes. Second, the digital precision: "Finally a grill that doesn't burn everything. The temp control is accurate." Third, cleanup: the non-stick surfaces and removable drip tray get frequent shout-outs. One reviewer specifically mentions, "The drip tray catches everything — no more grease running all over my counter."

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the limitations. The waffle plates don't fully detach from their frame, which means the hinge area can trap batter over time. You'll want to wipe it down immediately after use. The griddle surface also has a slight hot spot on the left side — noticeable when cooking a large batch of pancakes. It's manageable, but you'll need to rotate your food occasionally. Finally, the unit takes up real estate. If your counter space is precious, this will dominate it.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for small kitchens, dorm rooms, RV owners, or anyone who cooks for one or two people and wants appliance consolidation. It's also perfect for apartment dwellers who crave grilled food but don't have outdoor space. It's *not* for serious home cooks with dedicated appliances — you'd find the individual functions underwhelming compared to a standalone waffle maker or a dedicated grill.

**FAQ**

*Q: Can the plates go in the dishwasher?*
A: The manufacturer says yes, and most buyers report success. However, hand-washing the waffle plates is gentler on the hinge mechanism and extends their life.

*Q: Does it smoke much when grilling?*
A: At temperatures above 400°F with fatty meats, yes — some smoke is normal. The drip tray catches most grease before it hits the heating element, but you'll still want ventilation.

*Q: How long does it take to preheat?*
A: About 3-4 minutes to reach 400°F. The digital display shows the current temperature as it climbs, so you'll know exactly when it's ready.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want one appliance that handles breakfast, lunch, and dinner without cluttering your counter with three separate machines. The digital control and six-function versatility deliver genuine value at this price point.

**Skip it if:** You cook for four or more people regularly, need a deep waffle maker for Belgian-style waffles, or expect restaurant-grade grill marks every time.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A versatile, well-built multi-cooker that earns its keep. Not perfect, but for the price, it's a genuinely smart buy for space-conscious cooks.
