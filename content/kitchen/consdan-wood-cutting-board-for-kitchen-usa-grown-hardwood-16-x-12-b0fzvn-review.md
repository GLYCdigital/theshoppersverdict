## FAQ

**Q: Is this board dishwasher safe?**
No. Never put wood cutting boards in the dishwasher. The heat and moisture will warp and crack the wood. Hand wash with warm soapy water, dry immediately, and stand it on edge to air dry.

**Q: What oil should I use?**
Food-grade mineral oil is the standard. Apply a generous coat, let it soak in for a few hours or overnight, then wipe off the excess. Do this monthly or whenever the surface looks dry.

**Q: Can I use this for raw meat?**
Yes, but understand the tradeoff. Wood has natural antibacterial properties, but you should hand wash thoroughly after raw meat use. Many cooks designate one side for meat and one for vegetables — this board is thick enough to make that practical.
