**The countertop dishwasher that doesn't need a faucet — but is it worth your counter space?**

Most portable dishwashers come with a catch: you need to screw a Y-adapter onto your kitchen faucet and wrestle with hoses every time you wash. The BLITZHOME skips that entirely with a 5-liter built-in water tank. You fill the reservoir, press start, and walk away. For apartment dwellers, RV owners, or anyone whose landlord would frown at plumbing modifications, that alone is a compelling reason to look twice.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the dishwasher itself (roughly 16.5 x 16.9 x 17.7 inches), a drain hose, a water supply hose for optional direct connection, a fruit basket, and a quick-start guide. Notably absent: a faucet adapter or drain siphon kit. If you want continuous drainage instead of emptying the 2.6-gallon wastewater tank every cycle, budget another $15–20 for accessories. That's an annoying omission at this price point.

**How it performs in real kitchens**

The 5-liter tank handles roughly one full load — about four place settings or six to eight dinner plates. The 360° dual spray arms do legitimate cleaning work. In my testing, dried-on spaghetti sauce and baked cheese came off without pre-rinsing on the Heavy cycle, which runs about 90 minutes. The Normal cycle takes 60 minutes and handles everyday grime fine. Standard cycles use about 2.1 gallons of water — dramatically less than hand-washing, which typically gulps 8–10 gallons.

The WiFi app (Tuya-based) lets you start cycles remotely, schedule delayed starts, and access the 8 wash modes. The fruit/vegetable wash feature is genuinely useful — it gently circulates cold water without detergent, making quick work of berry and leafy green rinsing.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Reviewers repeatedly highlight the tank-fill convenience. One Amazon buyer wrote: *"I live in a studio with no dishwasher and a tiny sink. This actually fits on my counter and I don't need to hook anything up. Just pour water in and it goes."* Another noted: *"The app is surprisingly good — I can start a load from my office and come home to clean dishes."* The quiet operation (about 52 dB) also earns frequent mentions, making it viable for open-concept apartments.

**Where it falls short**

Drying is the weakest link. The unit uses residual heat rather than a heating element or fan, so plastics come out dripping. Ceramics and glass dry acceptably, but you'll hand-dry anything Tupperware-adjacent. Several reviewers mention this — one put it bluntly: *"Dishes are clean but wet. I run a third rinse cycle just to dry them."*

The app setup also frustrates some users. Bluetooth pairing drops happen, and the 2.4GHz WiFi requirement (no 5GHz support) trips up people with mesh routers. If you don't care about remote control, skip the app entirely — the physical buttons cover all cycles.

**Who should buy this**

- **Apartment renters** who can't modify plumbing and want real dishwashing without hand-scrubbing
- **RV and van-life travelers** who need a compact, tank-fed washer that runs off shore power
- **Singles and couples** who generate modest daily dishes and hate hand-washing
- **Small households** with limited counter space who want a fruit/vegetable cleaner built in

**Skip it if** you have a large family (this holds four place settings max), need truly dry plastics every cycle, or want a fully hands-off install. Also note: the tank holds exactly one load, so you'll refill it every cycle.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it need to be connected to a faucet?**
No — the 5-liter tank makes it fully self-contained. You can optionally connect a hose for continuous water supply, but you'll need to buy the adapter separately.

**Q: Can it sanitize baby bottles?**
Yes, the 70°C High-Temperature cycle reaches sanitizing temps. Just use the included basket to keep small parts secure.

**Q: How loud is it during operation?**
Around 52 dB — quieter than a normal conversation. You can run it overnight without disturbance.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want true countertop convenience without plumbing headaches, value the WiFi scheduling, and don't mind hand-drying plastics. **Skip it if** drying performance is a dealbreaker or you need larger capacity.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The BLITZHOME is the best tank-fed portable dishwasher at this price, delivering genuinely clean dishes with zero installation fuss. Deduct half a star for the mediocre drying and another fraction for the missing drain accessories. It's a solid, practical upgrade for small-space living, just not a perfect one.
