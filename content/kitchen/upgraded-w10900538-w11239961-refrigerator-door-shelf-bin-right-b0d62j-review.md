Let's be blunt: when a refrigerator door bin cracks, your options used to be paying $60+ for an OEM part or living with a janky shelf held together by duct tape. This upgraded W10900538/W11239961 bin splits the difference — and at this price, it's worth a hard look.

**What You Actually Get**

The package contains one right-side door shelf bin, molded in clear polypropylene. It's designed to replace both the W10900538 (Whirlpool) and W11239961 (KitchenAid) part numbers, so it covers a wide swath of side-by-side and French-door fridges from those brands. The dimensions match the OEM part closely — roughly 10 inches wide, 4.5 inches deep, and 5 inches tall — so it slots into the door liner without any sanding or persuasion.

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

The plastic is noticeably thicker than some of the cheap knockoffs floating around. I loaded it with a half-gallon of milk, a jar of pickles, and a few condiment bottles, and there was zero flex in the base. The mounting hooks on the back are molded cleanly and click into the door rail with a satisfying snap. No wobble, no sagging after a week of daily use.

The clear material is a double-edged sword. On one hand, you can spot the ketchup bottle without cracking the door. On the other, every fingerprint and water smear shows up like a neon sign. If you're a neat freak, you'll be wiping this down weekly. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing going in.

**What Buyers Are Saying**

**Where It Falls Short**

The "upgraded" in the title is doing a lot of work. This is essentially an OEM-equivalent part, not a redesigned bin with extra capacity or a non-slip liner. If you're expecting a substantial improvement over the original, you'll be underwhelmed. It's a replacement, not an upgrade.

Also, the clear plastic is more brittle than the opaque white OEM parts in cold temperatures. If your fridge runs particularly cold, there's a small risk of cracking if you drop it while removing it for cleaning. Handle it gently.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for anyone with a Whirlpool, KitchenAid, or Maytag side-by-side or French-door refrigerator built between roughly 2015 and 2024 whose door bin has cracked, warped, or gone missing. If you're handy enough to remove a shelf, you can install this in under two minutes. It's also a smart buy for landlords or property managers who need a quick, affordable fix for a common rental repair.

**Skip It If:**

- You have a Samsung, LG, or GE fridge — the mounting rails are different.
