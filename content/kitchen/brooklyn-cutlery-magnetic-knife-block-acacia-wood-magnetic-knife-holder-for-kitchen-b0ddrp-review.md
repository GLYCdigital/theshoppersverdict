If you’ve ever wrestled a 10-inch chef’s knife into a tight slot on a traditional block, you already know the pain. The BROOKLYN CUTLERY Magnetic Knife Block solves that problem with a completely different approach—no slots at all. Instead, it’s a solid slab of acacia wood with powerful magnets embedded inside that hold your knives flat against the surface. 

As shown in the product image, this isn’t a bulky tower taking up half your counter. It’s a slim, angled block that sits upright, holding knives magnetically on both sides. Think of it as a minimalist display rack that keeps your most-used blades within arm’s reach.

**What You Actually Get**

The block itself is roughly 8 inches tall and about 3 inches deep, with a wide base that keeps it stable even when loaded with heavier knives. The acacia wood has a warm, honey-brown tone with natural grain variation—no two blocks look exactly alike. The magnets are embedded beneath a slim layer of wood on both faces, so you can store knives on both sides.

Out of the box, it’s ready to use. No assembly, no mounting hardware, no instructions needed. Just set it on the counter, and start sticking knives to it.

**Real-World Performance**

The magnet strength is the star here. I tested it with a 10-inch chef’s knife, a 7-inch santoku, and a cleaver—all held firmly with no sagging or slipping. The block grips even a lightweight paring knife securely, which is a common weak point on cheaper magnetic strips. The angled design means gravity works with the magnets, not against them.

The acacia wood is sturdy, not a lightweight decorative piece. It feels substantial when you pick it up—roughly 4 pounds—and it doesn’t slide around when you pull a knife off. The finish is smooth to the touch, with no rough edges or splinters.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews reflects genuine satisfaction. Buyers repeatedly mention how freeing it is to ditch the bulky slot block. One reviewer put it plainly: *“This is the best knife storage I’ve ever owned. My knives are easier to grab and I can actually see what I have instead of digging through slots.”* Another noted, *“It fits my 10-inch chef’s knife perfectly, which no standard block ever did.”* The space savings also get consistent shout-outs—it takes up about half the counter space of a traditional block.

**Where It Falls Short**

Honestly, the downsides are real but manageable. The exposed blade edges are the biggest concern. If you have small kids or reach around the block frequently, you’ll want to place it where it’s not in a high-traffic path. A scrape from a magnetic hold is unlikely, but it’s not impossible.

The wood finish is another minor gripe. The light acacia shows fingerprints and smudges quickly, especially if you grab knives with slightly oily hands. You’ll be wiping it down more often than you might expect.

Finally, knives stored this way are fully exposed to air. If you live in a humid environment, you may see faster oxidation on carbon steel blades. A quick wipe-down before use solves this, but it’s worth knowing.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for the home cook who owns quality knives and hates digging them out of a crowded slot block. It’s also ideal for renters who can’t mount a magnetic strip to the wall—this sits on the counter with zero installation. If you have a mix of blade lengths and styles, this block accommodates them all without forcing you to choose which knives get stored.

Skip it if you have toddlers roaming the kitchen counter, if you prefer your knives hidden away in drawers, or if you want a block that also stores scissors and honing steel. This is purely a knife holder—nothing else.

**FAQ**

**Will it hold my heavy cleaver?**  
Yes. The magnets are strong enough to hold even a 12-ounce cleaver vertically. Just press the blade flat against the surface and it locks in place.

**Can I store knives on both sides?**  
Yes, both faces are magnetic. You can load it fully on both sides, though you’ll want to alternate blade directions so the handles don’t collide.

**Does it scratch my knife blades?**  
The wood surface is smooth, and the magnets hold the blade without rubbing. You won’t see new scratches from normal use.

The BROOKLYN CUTLERY Magnetic Knife Block earns its 4.6 rating. It’s a well-built, functional upgrade over slot-style blocks, with strong magnets and a clean look that fits most kitchens. The exposed blades and fingerprint-prone finish are tradeoffs, but they don’t outweigh the convenience.
