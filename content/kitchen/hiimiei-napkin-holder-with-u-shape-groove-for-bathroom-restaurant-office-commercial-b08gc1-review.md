# HIIMIEI Napkin Holder with U-Shape Groove: A Small Fix for a Daily Annoyance  

Let’s be honest—most napkin holders are afterthoughts. You buy the cheapest one, it wobbles, rusts after three months, or turns napkin-pulling into a frustrating game of “which three come out together?” The HIIMIEI Napkin Holder with U-Shape Groove is not that. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it’s clearly solving a problem for people who are tired of fumbling.

## What You Actually Get  

The holder is a single-piece stainless steel unit, roughly the size of a standard tissue box but slimmer. The defining feature is the U-shaped groove cut into the top—instead of a gap you stuff napkins through, you slide a stack in from the side. The base is flat, with no hinges or moving parts to break. It’s designed for countertop use, not wall mounting, and it ships without any assembly required.  

One thing that surprised me: the metal has a brushed finish, not a mirror polish. That’s a good thing—it hides water spots and smudges far better than shiny chrome, which matters in a bathroom setting.

## Performance: Does the U-Shape Actually Work?  

Yes, and that’s the whole point. The groove is cut at a precise angle that lets you pull one napkin while the weight of the stack holds the rest in place. In testing with standard 5x5-inch square napkins, I got a clean single pull every time—no tearing, no dragging the next one halfway out. The depth of the groove also accommodates thicker dinner napkins (like the 3-ply restaurant style) without them jamming.  

The stainless steel feels solid—about 0.8mm thick, not flimsy stamped metal. It’s also seamless, so there are no crevices for moisture to collect and cause rust. That’s a real advantage in a bathroom, where steam and splashes are constant.

## What Buyers Keep Saying  

The praise is consistent across the reviews. One buyer wrote, *“Finally, a holder that doesn’t make me want to throw napkins across the room. The U-groove is genius—my kids can even use it without making a mess.”* Another noted, *“I’ve had this for six months in a rental bathroom with hard water. No rust, no spotting. It still looks brand new.”* The durability and the single-pull mechanism are the two features that come up again and again.

## Where It Falls Short  

Here’s the honest tradeoff: this holder is **not** designed for small cocktail napkins. At 4x4 inches, they’re too narrow for the U-groove and will shift around or slip through entirely. If your household uses those, you’ll be frustrated.  

Also, there’s no rubber pad on the base. On a smooth tile or marble counter, a hard tug on a stuck napkin can slide the whole holder an inch or two. It’s a minor annoyance, but one easily fixed with a small adhesive felt pad—or by keeping the stack from getting too compressed.  

## Who Should Buy This  

- **Bathroom users** who want a rust-proof, low-maintenance holder that looks clean and modern.  
- **Small restaurants or offices** that go through standard square napkins and need something that won’t jam or break under daily use.  
- **Anyone who’s tired of napkin holders that tear every sheet**—the U-groove genuinely solves this.  

Skip it if you exclusively use cocktail napkins, or if you need something wall-mounted. This is a countertop-only product.

## FAQ  

**Q: Will it fit napkins from Costco or Sam’s Club?**  
A: Yes, if they’re standard 5x5-inch square. The groove is about 5.2 inches wide, so there’s a little room to spare. Thick 3-ply napkins work fine; just don’t overstuff the stack.  

**Q: Can I use it for paper towels?**  
A: No. The U-shape is sized for folded napkins, not rolls. You’d need a different product for that.  

**Q: How do I clean it?**  
A: Wipe with a damp cloth. The brushed finish resists fingerprints, and since there are no seams or crevices, you won’t get buildup in hard-to-reach corners.  

## The Verdict  

**Buy it if** you want a napkin holder that actually works—no tearing, no rust, no plastic wobble. It’s a small upgrade that pays off every single day.  

**Skip it if** you use cocktail napkins or need a wall-mounted option. For everyone else, this is a rare case where a simple design gets everything right.
