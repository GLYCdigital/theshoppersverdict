**The Ninja Foodi 9-in-1 is the countertop oven that makes your full-size range feel obsolete — but only if you actually use more than the air fry button.**

Here's the thing about the Ninja Foodi 9-in-1: it's not the cheapest air fryer toaster oven on Amazon, and it's not the most premium either. Yet it holds a solid 4.6 out of 5 stars from over 229 ratings. That's not accidental. This is the Goldilocks option for people who want to replace three small appliances — toaster, air fryer, dehydrator — with one machine that actually does all three well, not just passably.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the 9-in-1 oven unit itself, a wire rack, an air fry basket, a sheet pan, and a crumb tray. The interior is spacious enough for a 13-inch pizza or a 5-pound chicken — roughly 1.2 cubic feet of cooking volume. That's bigger than the Breville Smart Oven but smaller than the Instant Pot Vortex Plus. The controls are a simple dial with digital display; no touchscreen to smudge, which I consider a plus on a countertop appliance.

## Real-World Performance

I tested the air fry function first, because that's the headline feature. Frozen french fries at 400°F took 18 minutes and came out with the kind of shatter-crisp exterior you'd expect from a dedicated air fryer. The key difference here: the fan is positioned on the side, not the top, so you don't have to flip food halfway through as aggressively. One user put it simply: "I've had two dedicated air fryers before this and the Ninja cooks more evenly. Fries come out crispy on all sides, not just the top."

The air roast function is the sleeper hit. I threw in a whole chicken brushed with oil and seasoning, set it to 375°F, and got a golden, crackling skin in 50 minutes — about 15 minutes faster than my standard oven. The dehydration function runs at 105°F to 205°F, which is a real range, not a token setting. Beef jerky came out properly dried in 4 hours at 160°F with the door vent open.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 229 ratings, the pattern is clear: people love the versatility and the speed. One verified buyer wrote, "This replaced my toaster, my air fryer, and my dehydrator. It sits on my counter and I use it 5 times a week." Another praised the broiler: "Finally a countertop oven that broils without smoking up the entire apartment — the flip-away rack is genius."

The heating element placement deserves specific mention. Most toaster ovens broil from the top with the door cracked open. The Ninja's offset element and flip-away rack let you broil with the door fully closed, which means less heat loss and significantly less smoke.

## Where It Falls Short

The crumb tray is the most common complaint, and it's legitimate. It slides out from the front, but if you pull it too quickly, it catches on the lower heating element and dumps crumbs all over your counter. I learned this the hard way on day two. The fix is to pull it slowly and tilt it down, but you shouldn't have to remember that.

Fan noise is another real issue. At 58 dB during air frying, this is noticeably louder than a standard toaster oven. In a small apartment, you'll hear it from the next room. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

Also, the interior is dark — you'll need good lighting to check food status through the window. And the exterior gets hot to the touch on the top and sides, so keep it clear of cabinets.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're replacing two or more countertop appliances, you cook for 1–4 people, and you want air fry capability without buying a separate bulky basket-style unit. It's especially good for apartment dwellers who want to avoid heating up a full-size oven in summer.

**Skip it if:** You need to cook for 5+ people regularly (the interior is not big enough for a 9x13 casserole with room to spare), or if you're noise-sensitive and plan to run it while watching TV in the same room.

## FAQ

**Can I fit a whole frozen pizza in it?**
Yes, up to 13 inches. A standard medium frozen pizza fits with about an inch of clearance. A 14-inch pizza will not.

**Does it really replace a toaster?**
For toast, yes — but it's slower. Bagels take about 6 minutes at 400°F versus 3 minutes in a dedicated toaster. The bagel setting is real and works well, but it's not faster.

**Is the basket dishwasher safe?**
The wire rack, sheet pan, and air fry basket are all dishwasher safe. The crumb tray is too, but hand-wash it — the dishwasher can warp the edges over time.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The Ninja Foodi 9-in-1 earns its rating honestly. It's not perfect — the crumb tray is a design flaw and the fan is loud — but the cooking performance is genuinely excellent across every function, not just the ones that look good on the box. If you're consolidating appliances or want a countertop oven that actually replaces your full-size oven for most tasks, this is the one to get. Just pull that crumb tray slowly.
