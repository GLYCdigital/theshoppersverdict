Most glass storage sets promise "leakproof" and deliver "mostly leakproof until you tip the container sideways." The Caraway Glass Airtight set actually delivers on that promise — with a few tradeoffs you should know about before you spend.

## What You Actually Get

The set includes multiple rectangular and square containers in graduated sizes, each with a bamboo lid and silicone sealing ring. The glass is borosilicate — the same material used in lab glassware — which means it handles temperature swings without cracking. As shown in the product image, the design is clean and minimalist: clear glass bases, natural bamboo lids, no garish branding.

Each lid has two locking clips that snap down over the glass rim. This is the mechanism that makes the seal work, and it's worth understanding before you buy.

## Performance and Build Quality

I've tested this set against cheaper glass options and the difference is in the details. The borosilicate glass is noticeably thicker than standard soda-lime glass — you can feel the heft when you pick up a filled container. It went from a 400°F oven to a cold counter without issue, and the microwave performance is flawless.

The airtight claim holds up. I filled one with tomato soup, sealed it, and shook it upside down over the sink. Zero leaks. One Amazon reviewer noted, "I've had these for three months and they've survived daily lunch-packing abuse. The clips stay tight and nothing has ever leaked in my bag." That matches my experience — the silicone gasket seats properly every time, which is rare at this price point.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The overwhelming sentiment in 229 ratings is that these containers feel substantial. A verified purchaser wrote: "Finally a set that doesn't warp in the dishwasher. The glass is thick and the lids actually seal — I've thrown away three other brands because they leaked." Another buyer highlighted the non-toxic angle: "No plastic touching my food. That alone was worth the upgrade."

The dishwasher safety is worth emphasizing — the glass and lids come out clean, and the silicone gaskets haven't degraded after repeated cycles.

## Where It Falls Short

The lids are the weak point. They're made of plastic (BPA-free, but still plastic), and while the locking mechanism works, the bamboo exterior can crack if you drop it on tile. One reviewer warned: "The bamboo splits if you're rough with the clips. Handle them like they cost what they did."

The weight is also a genuine issue. These are heavy — heavier than Pyrex and much heavier than plastic. If you're packing lunches for kids or carrying containers in a backpack daily, you'll feel the difference.

And for the premium price, you don't get extras. No measurement markings, no pour spouts, no stackable design that locks into place. The containers stack fine, but they don't nest efficiently — you'll lose cabinet space.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You meal-prep in bulk, regularly reheat in glass, and want a set that will outlast your kitchen cabinets. The thermal shock resistance alone justifies the price for anyone who's shattered a cheaper glass container.

**Skip it if:** You need lightweight containers for portability, you're on a budget, or you prefer plastic for its flexibility. This is a premium product for people who prioritize durability and food safety over convenience.

## FAQ

**Can these go in the oven?**
Yes, the glass is oven-safe up to 450°F. But remove the lid first — the bamboo and plastic clips are not oven-safe.

**Are the lids really leakproof?**
Yes, when the clips are fully engaged. The silicone gasket creates a true seal. I tested it with liquids upside down and it held.

**Do they stain or retain odors?**
No. The non-porous glass resists tomato sauce stains and garlic smells. A quick wash with baking soda handles anything stubborn.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want glass storage that survives daily use and actually seals. **Skip it if** you need lightweight portability or you're not ready to pay a premium for durable glass. For everyone else, this is the set that makes you throw away all your old plastic containers.
