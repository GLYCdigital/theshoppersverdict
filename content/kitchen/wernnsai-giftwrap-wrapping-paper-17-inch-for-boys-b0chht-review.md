That’s not ideal. Also, the designs skew young. Dinosaurs and trucks are perfect for a 5-year-old, but a 13-year-old might raise an eyebrow. If you’re wrapping for teens or adults, this isn’t the pack for you.

The per-roll cost runs higher than plain kraft paper from Target or Walmart. You’re paying for the prints and the thickness, and that math only works if you actually value the presentation.
