The Instant Pot Vortex Plus 6QT ClearCook Air Fryer arrives with the kind of recommendation that stops you from reading further: it's America's Test Kitchen's top-rated air fryer. With 7,887 reviews and a 4.6-star average at $89.99, it has the numbers to back up the endorsement.

## The Clear Window Advantage

The ClearCook viewing window is more than a gimmick. It genuinely changes how you use an air fryer. Instead of pulling the basket mid-cycle (losing heat, resetting timers), you simply glance through the window. The interior light illuminates your food so you can check browning progress without interrupting the cooking process.

For anyone who's ever opened an air fryer only to release all the hot air, this feature quickly feels essential.

## Cooking Performance

The Vortex Plus delivers consistently even results. "Browns nicely, we don't even have to flip the food, it cooks so perfectly," one user reports. Multiple reviewers mention that food comes out crispy without requiring the mid-cycle shake that many air fryers demand.

The 6-in-1 functionality includes air fry, broil, roast, dehydrate, bake, and reheat. In practice, most users settle on the air fry and reheat functions as their daily drivers, with baking and dehydrating used occasionally.

## Easy Cleaning

The non-stick interior cleans up with minimal effort. Food residue releases easily, and the basket is designed without hard-to-reach corners where grease accumulates. For an appliance that gets used multiple times per week, easy cleaning is the difference between regular use and gathering dust in a cabinet.

## Long-Term Durability

The reports from long-term users are encouraging. Several mention their previous Instant Pot Vortex lasted 5-6 years before needing replacement — and they bought the same model again. That kind of brand loyalty speaks volumes about the overall ownership experience.

The minority of negative reviews focus on units that failed prematurely. It's worth noting that Instant Pot's customer service gets mixed marks from the small percentage of users who need it.

## The Verdict

At $89.99 with 7,887 glowing reviews, the Instant Pot Vortex Plus 6QT ClearCook is the air fryer to beat. The ClearCook window, even cooking, and easy cleaning make it a genuinely useful kitchen appliance rather than another countertop gadget. It earned America's Test Kitchen's top spot for good reason.
