Let me be blunt: most air fryers are black boxes. You set the timer, pray, and open the basket to find either pale food or charcoal. The Instant Pot Vortex Plus 6QT ClearCook solves that with a literal window. That's the headline feature, and it's not a gimmick—it changes how you cook.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the 13.4 x 13.4 x 12.6-inch air fryer base, a 6-quart non-stick basket with a crisper tray, and a user manual that's surprisingly readable. The ClearCook window runs across roughly 60% of the basket front, and there's an interior LED light you toggle with one button. The digital display sits on top with touch controls for the six presets: Air Fry, Roast, Broil, Bake, Dehydrate, and Reheat. Temperature ranges from 95°F to 400°F, and the timer goes up to 60 minutes.

## Real-World Performance

I ran this unit through a full week of testing—frozen fries, fresh chicken thighs, a whole small chicken, and dehydrated apple chips. The 1750-watt heating element circulates air aggressively. Fries came out crispy in 18 minutes without shaking mid-cycle, which is genuinely impressive for a basket-style fryer.

The ClearCook window earns its keep during the last five minutes of any cook. You watch the browning happen in real time. Pull the basket when food hits your exact shade of golden—no guesswork. The interior light is bright enough to see through the window even in a dark kitchen, which sounds minor until you've used it.

The 6-quart capacity is the real sweet spot. It holds a 5-pound chicken flat, about 3 pounds of fries (enough for a family of five), or 12 chicken wings in a single layer. This isn't a single-person gadget.

## What 7,887 Buyers Consistently Say

The 4.6-star rating holds up across thousands of reviews. The recurring praise centers on ease of use and the window. One verified buyer wrote: *"I can finally see when my food is done without opening the drawer and stopping the cook. My chicken comes out perfect every single time."* Another noted: *"Replaced my toaster oven and microwave for reheat duty. The reheat function doesn't soggy-ify pizza like the microwave does."*

The most common complaint isn't about cooking—it's the noise. Multiple reviewers describe the beeps as "aggressive" and "loud enough to wake the kids." You cannot mute them. That's a real design oversight.

## Where It Falls Short

The basket is heavy. When loaded with a full chicken and crisper tray, it's a two-hand job to pull and shake. The non-stick coating works well initially, but reviewers who use metal tongs report scratching. Use silicone or wooden utensils only.

The "Plus" name suggests extra features, but there's no rotisserie, no dehydrator racks included (you'll need to buy them separately), and the cooking presets are just starting points—you'll still need to adjust times for your own recipes. The 60-minute max timer also limits low-and-slow dehydrating sessions; you'll need to reset it for longer runs.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Families (3-6 people):** The 6-quart capacity is the sweet spot between bulky 8-quart models and cramped 4-quart units.
- **Visual cooks:** If you hate opening the basket to check food, the ClearCook window is worth the price alone.
- **Countertop declutterers:** Replaces an air fryer, toaster oven, and dehydrator in one footprint.

Skip it if you need silent operation (this beeper is loud), if you're cooking for one (the 6-quart footprint is bulky), or if rotisserie chicken is a weekly staple—you'll need a different model.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the basket go in the dishwasher?**
A: Yes, the basket and crisper tray are dishwasher-safe. That said, hand-washing with warm soapy water extends the non-stick coating's life. I'd hand-wash.

**Q: How does it compare to a basket without a window?**
A: The window adds about $20 to the price, but it genuinely improves results. You can watch for doneness without opening the drawer, which means no heat loss mid-cook. That translates to more consistent browning.

**Q: Can you actually see clearly during cooking?**
A: Yes, especially with the interior light on. The window does steam up during the first few minutes, but it clears once the food releases moisture. The view is crisp and unobstructed during the critical browning phase.

**Buy it if:** You want a family-sized air fryer that removes the guesswork from crispy food. The ClearCook window is a genuine upgrade, not a marketing bullet point.

**Skip it if:** You need silent operation, want rotisserie capability, or have limited counter space.
