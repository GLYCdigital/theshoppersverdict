**What you actually get**

The set ships with three scoops — typically sized around 1.5 tablespoons, 2 tablespoons, and 3 tablespoons, though exact capacities vary slightly by batch. Each features a spring-loaded thumb trigger connected to a stainless steel sweeper blade. The handles are brushed 18/8 stainless (meaning 18% chromium, 8% nickel — the good stuff that resists corrosion). As shown in the product image, the heads are deep enough to hold a proper portion without spillover, and the trigger mechanism sits high enough that your thumb rests naturally.

What you don't get: a storage clip, hanging loop, or any kind of lock. They're bare-bones tools, which keeps the price reasonable.

**How it performs in real kitchens**

The core job — scooping cookie dough — these do exceptionally well. The sweeper blade wipes the bowl clean on each squeeze, so you get a full drop with no tearing or smearing. I tested chilled dough straight from the fridge (the toughest scenario) and the medium scoop handled it without bending or flexing. The large scoop doubles as a decent ice cream tool for quart tubs, though it won't muscle through rock-hard pints.

One buyer noted, "These are the best cookie scoops I've owned. The dough releases every single time and they clean up like new." Another mentioned using them for meatballs and melon balls with equal success. The 18/8 steel holds up to dishwasher cycles without pitting — a real advantage over cheaper plated scoops that develop rust spots within months.

**Where it trips up**

The lack of a trigger lock is the most common complaint. The lever swings freely, so if you toss these in a drawer, the trigger catches on other utensils and clicks open. Not a dealbreaker, but annoying. Store them on a hook or in a crock.

The smallest scoop's handle runs thin. Testers with larger hands reported mild discomfort after scooping 50+ cookies in one session. If you bake in massive batches, consider wrapping the handle with a grip or using the medium scoop exclusively.

**FAQ**

**Do these work for frozen cookie dough?**
Yes, but with effort. The sweeper blade handles cold dough fine, but extremely hard dough (straight from a deep freezer) can strain the spring. Let dough sit 5 minutes before scooping.

**Are they truly dishwasher safe?**
The stainless steel is. The spring mechanism may lose some tension over time with repeated hot cycles — hand-washing extends the lever's life, but they won't rust or discolor.

**What's the exact size of each scoop?**
Sizes vary slightly, but they approximate 1.5 tbsp, 2 tbsp, and 3 tbsp. Check the listing's size chart for your batch's specifics.
