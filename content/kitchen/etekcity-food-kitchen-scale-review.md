For under $14, the Etekcity Food Kitchen Scale delivers accuracy and ease of use that rivals scales costing three times as much. With a 4.6-star rating from over 174,000 reviews, this compact digital scale has become one of the most popular kitchen scales on Amazon — and for good reason.

## Verdict

The Etekcity food scale nails the fundamentals: it's accurate, responsive, and dead simple to use. Place it on the counter, press power, and you're weighing ingredients instantly. The digital display is clear and easy to read, and switching between grams and ounces is fast and intuitive. At $13.99, it's a no-brainer for anyone who weighs their food.

## Who Is This For

This scale is ideal for bakers who need precise flour and sugar measurements, meal preppers tracking portions, keto and weight loss dieters counting macros, and casual home cooks who want to improve their recipes. If you've been eyeballing ingredients or using measuring cups, this scale will tighten up your cooking immediately.

## What We Like

The auto-tare function is the standout feature. Place a bowl on the scale before turning it on, and it automatically zeros out — you measure exactly what goes in without calculating container weight. It's a small touch that reviewers consistently praise.

The scale switches quickly between measurement modes. One reviewer called it "indispensable" and noted it lives in a drawer, pulled out whenever needed. The stainless steel surface wipes clean easily, and the compact size means it won't clutter your counter.

Accuracy is consistently rated high. Multiple reviews mention relying on this scale for baking and meal prep without any issues.

## What We Don't

The main complaint is the unit button, which sometimes requires a double-click to register. One reviewer demonstrated this in a video and called it "the only thing" holding the product back. It's minor but can be annoying when you're mid-recipe.

The weighing surface is relatively small — large mixing bowls may overhang slightly. There's also no backlight, so reading the display in dim kitchen light requires angling toward a light source.

## Bottom Line

The Etekcity Food Kitchen Scale is one of the best values in kitchen gear. It's accurate, compact, and does everything a home cook or meal prepper needs. The minor button quibble doesn't overshadow the price-to-performance ratio.

**Verdict: 4.5/5** — Accurate, affordable, and popular for a reason. The best kitchen scale under $15 by a wide margin.
