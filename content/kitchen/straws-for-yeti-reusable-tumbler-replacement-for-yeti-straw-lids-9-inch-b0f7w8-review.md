Let's be real about the downsides.

**No flex point.** YETI's original straws have a slight angle at the top. These are straight steel tubes. When your tumbler gets down to the last inch of liquid, you'll be tilting your head further back than usual. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

**Temperature transfer.** Steel is a conductor. If you're drinking iced coffee, the exposed top of the straw gets cold against your lips. In summer that's actually pleasant. In winter with a hot drink, it's not. Worth knowing before you buy.

**No gasket included.** If you're buying these because your lid's seal is failing, this won't fix that. The straws fit the lid, but they don't repair it.
