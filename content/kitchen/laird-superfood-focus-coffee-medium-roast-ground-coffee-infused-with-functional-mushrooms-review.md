Laird Superfood Focus Coffee is the functional coffee for people who don't want their coffee to taste functional. With 336 Amazon reviews averaging 4.4/5, it blends Peruvian medium-roast beans with lion's mane extract, rhodiola rosea, and coffee cherry powder — and the most common thing buyers say is that it simply tastes like coffee.

> "No mushroom take, smooth coffee flavor." — Sabine, verified review

## What Buyers Love

The biggest surprise for most first-time buyers is that the adaptogens disappear into the cup. Pamela, who adds a scoop to her morning coffee, wrote that "it really helps with memory," while Sabine noted the mushroom blend is completely undetectable.

The energy profile gets almost universal praise. One long-time reviewer put it plainly: "I'm awake, but not jittery. That's all I can say." Jessica G. found it gentler on her stomach and better for afternoons — "it is not as acidic as normal coffee and I enjoy the taste. I like a cup in the afternoon and that I get a better boost of energy."

Wendy, who has been drinking it for years, describes the full effect: "When I make my morning coffee and drink it I don't think of tea, it tastes like a smooth cup of coffee that helps me to wake up — later I can get to sleep much easier."

## Where It Falls Short

Price is the loudest complaint. Boonesj27 was blunt: "The price on Amazon was way overpriced though compared to in stores. Won't buy off Amazon again." CaviKween agreed — "it costs too much" — even while praising the coffee cherry note she likes.

There's also a roast discrepancy worth knowing. Despite the medium-roast label, unccoda5 found it "definitely on the darker side and it simply just tastes bad," and several reviewers note the flavor is mild rather than bold. CaviKween also flagged that the organic claim "has none of the credentials that you can rely on" — only the non-GMO label is visible.

## Who Should Buy It

This is for the morning-coffee crowd who want a calmer, steadier lift — the kind of person who loves coffee but gets jittery or acidic-crashy on standard roasts. It's also a practical pick if you want lion's mane and rhodiola in your routine without buying separate supplements, and it brews exactly like normal ground coffee: drip, French press, or cold brew.

## Who Should Skip It

Skip it if you want a bold, punchy roast — this is smooth and mild by design. And if you're price-sensitive, check your local grocery or Laird's own site first; several buyers found better deals off Amazon.

## Bottom Line

With **4.4/5** from 336+ Amazon reviews, Laird Superfood Focus Coffee delivers on its promise: a smooth, functional cup that energizes without the jitters and tastes like regular coffee. Just don't pay Amazon's premium if you can find it cheaper in stores — the coffee itself earns the rating.
