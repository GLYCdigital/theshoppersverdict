Let's be direct: most disposable portion cups are flimsy junk that crack under light pressure or leak dressing all over your lunch bag. This set from the B09NJG listing is not that — mostly. After 235 ratings averaging 4.7 stars, these little containers have earned a reputation, and I've dug through the feedback to give you the real story.

**What you actually get**

The listing ships a stack of small plastic cups with matching lids — typically 50 to 100 pairs depending on the option you select. These are the classic 2-ounce deli-style containers, roughly 2 inches across and about 1.5 inches tall. The plastic is clear, letting you spot what's inside without popping every lid. They're built for single use, though plenty of buyers report washing and reusing them several times before they give up.

**Where these cups shine**

The lid seal is the headline feature here. I've seen multiple verified buyer reviews calling out the snap closure specifically. One reviewer wrote, "I packed ranch dressing for a week of salads and not one leaked." That echoes the dominant feedback pattern — people use these for everything from paint mixing to baby food to meal prep sauces, and the lids hold.

The clarity matters more than you'd think. When you've got three identical containers in the fridge, being able to see the yogurt versus the hummus versus the leftover marinara saves real time. The plastic is also stiffer than the bargain-bin alternatives — it won't crumple when you squeeze it, and the walls don't flex when you're scooping out the last bit of apple sauce.

The 2-ounce size hits a sweet spot. It's big enough for a proper snack portion or a side of dressing, but small enough that you're not wasting space in a packed cooler or lunch box. Parents specifically call these out as ideal for toddler portions — one reviewer noted they're "perfect for daycare snack prep without the waste of full-size containers."

**The honest downsides**

These cups are not microwave-safe. If your meal prep routine relies on reheating portions, you'll need to transfer contents to another dish first. That's a genuine limitation for a product marketed partly toward meal prep.

The lids need a firm, even press to seat properly. If you rush it, you'll get a partial seal — and a few buyers reported cracked rims on the cups when they forced the lid down at an angle. The solution is simple: press the center first, then work your way around the edges. But it's a quirk worth knowing before you buy 100 of these and get frustrated on the first use.

Also, while some people get multiple uses out of each cup, these are fundamentally disposable. The plastic thins out after a wash or two, and the lids lose their snap over time. If you want something that'll last a year, buy reusable glass or hard plastic instead.

**Who should buy these**

This product fits three specific buyer profiles. First, the meal prepper who makes sauces, dressings, or single-serve sides in bulk. Second, parents packing school lunches or managing toddler portions — the throwaway nature is actually a feature here, since you're not washing tiny containers every night. Third, anyone doing DIY projects that need small amounts of paint, glue, or other materials in cheap, disposable vessels.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Are these cups truly leak-proof?*
The lids seal tightly when pressed correctly, and most buyers report zero leakage with liquids like dressing or yogurt. But they're not vacuum-sealed — aggressive shaking or upside-down storage in a hot car can force liquid past the seal. Treat them as leak-resistant, not leak-proof.

*Can I freeze food in these?*
Yes, these handle freezing fine. Just leave a little headroom for expansion and let them thaw before you try to pop the lid off — the seal gets stubborn when contents are frozen solid.

*Are the cups BPA-free?*
The manufacturer states these are BPA-free, and multiple reviewers have used them for baby food and kids' snacks without concerns. If you're extra cautious, look for the specific BPA-free labeling on the packaging when your order arrives.

**The verdict**

Buy these if you want a reliable, affordable disposable portioning solution that seals properly and handles daily abuse. Skip them if you need microwave-safe containers or plan to reuse them dozens of times — this product is built for convenience, not permanence.
