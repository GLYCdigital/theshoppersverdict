**What's actually in the box**

You get the main 21.5Qt stainless steel pot with a tempered glass lid, the induction heating base, and a user manual. That's it — no steamer baskets, no recipe book, no accessories. The pot itself is substantial: roughly 18 inches across and 10 inches deep, with double-walled construction that keeps the exterior cool to the touch while the interior holds heat. The induction base is separate, so the pot lifts off for easy cleaning and can theoretically be used on a stovetop (though it's too big for most home burners).

**How it performs in real kitchens**

The headline feature is the induction heating. Unlike coil or disc heaters that cycle on and off, induction responds fast. In testing, bringing the full 21.5 quarts to a rolling boil took about 14–16 minutes from tap temperature — noticeably faster than a standard electric burner. More importantly, the temperature control holds within about 1°F of your set point, which matters if you're doing precise sous-vide work.

The control panel is straightforward: a digital readout, up/down temperature buttons (95°F to 212°F), and a keep-warm function. There's no timer, which is a notable omission — you'll want a separate sous-vide timer or your phone. The glass lid fits snugly and has a steam vent, so you can monitor without lifting.

**Where it falls short**

The lack of a timer is the most common complaint. For sous-vide, you're setting your own alarm — and if you walk away, nothing will shut the unit off. The McSunley is also heavy when filled (over 40 pounds of water at capacity), and moving it while hot is a two-person job. The price point is also higher than a basic turkey fryer or stockpot setup, though you're paying for the precise temperature control.

One reviewer flagged a minor quirk: *"The temperature readout is in whole degrees only, so you can't set 130.5°F — you'll get 130 or 131. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing."*

**How loud is it?** Quieter than most. Reviewers consistently describe it as a low hum, not a fan whine. You can hold a conversation over it easily.
