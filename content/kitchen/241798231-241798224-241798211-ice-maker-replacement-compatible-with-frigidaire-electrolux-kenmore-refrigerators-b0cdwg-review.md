The second issue is packaging. This ships in a plain box with minimal protection. It's not a fragile part, but the arm issue above suggests it's getting knocked around in transit.

And one thing to know: this is a generic replacement, not genuine Frigidaire. The plastic molding is slightly different in texture — a bit glossier — and the internal components aren't branded. If you're a stickler for OEM parts, this isn't for you. But functionally, it's identical.
