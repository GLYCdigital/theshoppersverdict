**Performance & Build Quality**  

I filled it with ice water at 8 AM, left it in a hot car for six hours, and the ice was still floating at 2 PM. The double-wall vacuum insulation is legit — this matches bottles twice the price. The spout lid is the real workhorse: it's 100% leak-proof (I shook it upside down for 30 seconds, dry). The straw lid is where things get nuanced.  

The flip-up straw is convenient for driving or gym use, but it's not sealed. It's splash-resistant. Tilt it too far, and you'll get a dribble. One Amazon reviewer put it bluntly: *"Straw lid leaks if it falls over in my bag — I only use the spout lid for travel."* That's accurate. The spout lid is your go-to for commuting; the straw is for desk or car use where the bottle stays upright.
