**Six jars, three sizes, one countertop solution — but are these worth your shelf space?**

Most cookie jar sets force you into one-size-fits-all thinking. The Nuogo 6-piece set takes a smarter approach: two jars each at 1L, 1.5L, and 2L capacities. That's enough room for a full 13-ounce bag of flour in the small ones, a standard box of cereal in the mediums, and a Costco-sized cookie stash in the large. For $30-ish (price fluctuates), you're getting roughly $5 per container — reasonable for the feature set.

**What you actually get**

The box contains six clear plastic jars with matching lids. Each lid has a silicone gasket ring around the rim. The jars are square-ish with rounded corners, so they pack tightly on a shelf without wasting the awkward gaps round jars create. The plastic is BPA-free and surprisingly clear — not the hazy, cheap-looking acrylic you see on dollar-store knockoffs.

One detail worth noting: the lids have a slight lip that makes them easy to grip, even with wet hands. That matters more than you'd think when you're mid-recipe and grabbing for sugar.

**Real-world performance**

I tested these with a 2-pound bag of chocolate chip cookies, a box of Cheerios, and loose candy. The gaskets do their job — cookies stayed soft for 12 days before I finished them (theoretically; my family helped). Cereal kept its snap for three weeks. The airtight seal is the standout feature here, and it's what justifies the price over a plain glass jar.

The stacking design works better than expected. The jars have a slight indentation on top that lets the next jar sit securely — I stacked all six in a pyramid on my pantry shelf without wobble. That's a space-saver if you're in an apartment or have shallow cabinets.

**Where it falls short**

The plastic is the weak point. It's food-grade and clear, but it's not thick. One reviewer reported: *"Dropped the 2L jar from counter height — it cracked on impact."* That's not unusual for plastic at this price, but glass would have survived better. Another consistent complaint: the lids warp in the dishwasher. The product says top-rack safe, but multiple reviews say the gaskets loosen after a few cycles. Hand-washing is the safe play.

The lids also don't nest when you're storing empty jars, so if you rotate pantry items seasonally, you'll lose cabinet space.

**Who should buy this**

- **Baking households** — the airtight seal genuinely preserves cookies, brownies, and crackers

**Skip it if** you need something drop-proof (get glass), you hate hand-washing, or you're storing liquids — these are dry-goods jars only.

**FAQ**

**Are these really airtight?**
Yes. The silicone gasket creates a proper seal. I tested by turning a filled jar upside down — no leaks. For moisture, they held up for weeks in testing.

**Can I put these in the dishwasher?**
Technically yes (top rack), but don't. Multiple reviews report warped lids and loosened gaskets after dishwasher use. Hand-wash in warm soapy water.

**Do the lids come in different sizes?**
Each jar's lid matches its size. They're not interchangeable — the 1L lid won't fit the 2L jar. Keep sets together.
