If you've ever tried to toss a family-size salad in a bowl that's barely big enough for a single head of lettuce, you know the struggle. Lettuce flies everywhere, dressing splatters the counter, and you end up serving from a mixing bowl that looks like it belongs in a test kitchen, not on your dining table. The KITEISCAT Extra Large Glass Salad Bowl Set is designed to solve exactly that problem — and for the most part, it does.

## What You Actually Get

This is a 5-piece set, and it's more generous than the price suggests. The main bowl holds 2.6 gallons — that's roughly 10 quarts, enough for a party-sized salad feeding 15-20 people. There's also a 1.5-quart secondary bowl for smaller sides or serving dressings separately. Both are made from borosilicate glass, which is the same thermal-shock-resistant material used in lab glassware. The set includes two acacia wood serving spoons and a bamboo lid that fits the large bowl.

As shown in the product image, the design is clean and modern — clear glass with warm wood accents. It looks like something you'd find at a high-end kitchen store for twice the price.

## Performance and Build Quality

I tested this set with a cold Caesar salad, a warm pasta salad, and a fruit medley. The borosilicate glass handled the temperature swing from refrigerator-cold to room temperature without any cracking or clouding. The 12mm-thick walls feel genuinely substantial — this isn't one of those thin glass bowls that shatters if you look at it wrong.

The main bowl is deep enough (about 6 inches) to actually toss ingredients without launching them across the room. The acacia wood servers are 12 inches long, which means you can reach the bottom of the bowl without scraping your knuckles. They're smooth, well-sanded, and comfortable to grip.

One thing I appreciate: the bowl has a slightly flared rim, which makes pouring out excess liquid or transferring contents much easier than a straight-sided bowl would be.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews holds up. The most common praise centers on size and versatility. One reviewer noted, "I've used it for salads, popcorn for movie night, and even as a punch bowl — it's become my most-used kitchen item." Another mentioned, "The glass is thick enough that I'm not terrified of breaking it, but it's still crystal clear and beautiful on the table."

The included lid is also a hit for transporting dishes to potlucks — though it's worth noting it's a bamboo cover, not an airtight seal. It keeps dust off and prevents splashing in the car, but don't expect it to keep greens crisp overnight.

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty time. The bamboo lid is decorative more than functional. It sits on top without any locking mechanism, so if you're carrying the bowl by its handles (yes, there are side handles, which is a nice touch), the lid can slide off if you tilt the bowl. For storage, you'll want to wrap the top in plastic wrap or transfer leftovers to a separate container.

The glass also shows every fingerprint, water spot, and smudge. If you're the type who likes a pristine table for guests, budget time to polish it before serving. And when the main bowl is full, it's heavy — around 12 pounds. It's manageable, but it's a two-hand job.

## Who Should Buy This

This set is ideal for:

Skip it if you're cooking for one or two, or if you have limited cabinet space — the main bowl is 10 inches in diameter and takes up real estate.

## FAQ

**Is the glass oven-safe?**
No. Borosilicate glass handles thermal shock from hot to cold, but it's not designed for direct oven or stovetop use. Keep it to serving, mixing, and refrigerator storage.

**Can the bowls go in the dishwasher?**
Yes, both glass bowls are dishwasher safe. The wood servers and bamboo lid are hand-wash only — wash them quickly and dry immediately to prevent cracking.

**How many people does the large bowl serve?**
Realistically, 15-20 people for a side salad, or about 10-12 as a main-dish salad. It's genuinely party-sized.
