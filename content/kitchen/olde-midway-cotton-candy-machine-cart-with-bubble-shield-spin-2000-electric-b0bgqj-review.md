## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the flaws. The bubble shield is functional but flimsy — it rattles audibly at full speed and requires a firm push to seat properly. It's a minor annoyance, but at this price point, you'd expect a tighter fit.

The lack of a power switch is a genuine design miss. You're plugging and unplugging the cord every time you want to start or stop, which is awkward when you're juggling sugar and cones. It's a small thing, but it adds friction to every session.

Cleanup is the real test of any cotton candy machine, and this one demands attention. Sugar residue builds up around the heating element quickly, and you'll need to wipe the head down after every 2-3 batches to prevent burning. The stainless steel head is dishwasher-safe, but the base and shield need hand-washing. Budget 10-15 minutes for proper cleanup after each use.

## FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: How long does a bag of sugar last?**
Each 8-ounce bag makes roughly 15-20 full-sized cones, depending on how generous you stack them. That's about 30-40 cones per machine session with both included bags.

**Q: Can you use regular sugar instead of floss sugar?**
No. Regular granulated sugar won't melt properly — you need floss sugar, which contains corn syrup solids and food coloring that facilitate the melting and spinning process. The included bags are floss sugar, so you're set for your first few batches.

**Q: Is the bubble shield necessary?**
Functionally, yes. The shield contains the sugar spray and keeps floss from flying around your kitchen. Without it, you'll have sticky sugar residue on everything within three feet of the machine.
