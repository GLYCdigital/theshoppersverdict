## What's Actually in the Box

This is the genuine Cuisinart replacement kit for the DFP-14 series (and compatible models like the DLC-2014). You get three pieces: the clear work bowl, the lid with the feed tube, and the pusher assembly. It's the full top half of your food processor, which matters because the lid's interlock mechanism is what tells the motor it's safe to run.

The bowl is the same heavy-duty clear plastic that shipped with your original unit — not a flimsy aftermarket knockoff. The measurement markings are molded in, and the handle is integrated into the body rather than glued on. As shown in the product image, the lid has the standard two-piece feed tube that lets you drizzle oil while the machine runs.

## Fit and Real-World Performance

The critical question with any replacement part is whether it actually seats correctly on your base. Based on buyer feedback and my own assessment, this bowl locks onto DFP-14 bases without the wobble or gap that plagues cheaper third-party bowls. The bowl handles everything you'd throw at a 14-cup processor: pie dough, shredding blocks of cheese, emulsifying mayonnaise. It's dishwasher-safe on the top rack, and the plastic doesn't cloud or crack from hot water cycles the way some generics do.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the price — this replacement kit runs roughly half the cost of an entire new Cuisinart food processor. If your base unit is old or showing signs of motor wear, you might be better off putting that money toward a full machine with a warranty.

Second, there's no gasket in the box. The DFP-14 series uses a rubber ring between the bowl and the blade assembly. If yours is worn or lost, you'll need to hunt that down separately — and it's annoyingly easy to overlook when ordering.

Third, the lid latch stiffness I mentioned. It's minor, but if you have arthritis or grip issues, the first few weeks of use will require more force than you're used to.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** Your Cuisinart base is in good working condition and you just need to replace a cracked or shattered bowl. It's also a smart purchase if you use your processor daily and want a spare bowl to keep prep flowing — one for wet ingredients, one for dry.

**Skip it if:** Your machine is more than a decade old with a motor that sounds strained, or if you've already replaced other parts. At this price point, you're close enough to a new unit that the warranty and fresh motor make more sense.

## FAQ

**Will this fit my Cuisinart model?**
It's designed for the DFP-14 series (DFP-14BK, DFP-14CH, DFP-14N, DFP-14PCN) and the DLC-2014. Check your base model number before ordering — this won't fit the older DLC-7 or the newer Custom 14 with a different latch design.

**Is it actually dishwasher safe?**
Yes, top rack only. The heat element on the bottom rack can warp the bowl over time. Hand washing extends the life of the plastic and keeps the lid seal tight.

**Can I use my old blade with this bowl?**
Yes. The blade stem and drive spline are unchanged. Your existing S-blade, shredding disc, and dough blade will all work without modification.
