## What You Actually Get

The set includes multiple borosilicate glass measuring cups in graduated sizes, all featuring the signature V-shaped spout design shown in the product image. The measurement markings are etched directly into the glass — not printed on like cheaper options — which means they survive dishwasher cycles, hot liquids, and years of use without fading into illegibility.

The borosilicate construction is the headline feature here. Unlike soda-lime glass (what most budget measuring cups use), borosilicate has a much lower thermal expansion coefficient. That's the technical way of saying it can go from your freezer to a simmering pot without shattering. For anyone who's ever melted butter in a glass cup straight from the fridge, this matters.

## Real-World Performance

The V-shaped spout isn't just a design flourish. It creates a narrow, controlled stream that pours cleanly into small-mouth containers — think oils into bottles, pancake batter into a hot skillet, or stock into a narrow saucepan. I've tested enough measuring cups to know most spouts dribble down the side when you tilt past a certain angle. This one holds the stream until you're nearly vertical.

The etched markings are genuinely easy to read at a glance, with both metric and US measurements included. That's a small thing that makes a big difference when you're mid-recipe and don't want to squint.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The larger cups in this set don't have handles. For a 1000ml (roughly 4-cup) measuring cup, that's a real ergonomic issue — especially when it's full of hot liquid. You'll need two hands, and even then, the pour can feel unstable.

The glass is also thinner than traditional Pyrex. That's a tradeoff of borosilicate — it's more thermal-shock resistant but less impact-resistant. It won't shatter from temperature change, but I wouldn't go throwing it in a sink full of dishes.

Finally, lids aren't included. If you want to use these for storage rather than just measuring, you'll need to buy separate covers. Minor, but worth knowing.
