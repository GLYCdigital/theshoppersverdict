## Where It Falls Short

Also, the mounting brackets are thin gauge. Not a problem once installed, but they bend easily during handling. Handle the assembly by the ceramic block, not the flanges.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I know if my heating element is actually the problem?**
A: Unplug the dryer, access the element, and test the two coil terminals with a multimeter. A good element reads 10-20 ohms. Infinite resistance means a broken coil — replace it. Also test the thermal fuse (should read near 0 ohms) since a blown fuse is often the root cause.

**Q: Do I need to replace the thermostats too?**
A: Not necessarily. If the dryer was running hot before it died, you may have damaged thermostats as well. Test them with the multimeter. If they read open at room temperature, replace them. Otherwise, the element alone is sufficient.

**Q: Will this work in a gas dryer?**
A: No. This is an electric heating element for 240V electric dryers only. Gas dryers use a burner assembly — completely different part.
