## Carori Dual Pot Electric Pressure Cooker Review

The Carori Dual Pot Electric Pressure Cooker is an ambitious contender in the multi-cooker market. With **223 ratings and a 4.6-star average**, it's already building a strong reputation as a budget-friendly alternative to the dominant Instant Pot.

At **$89.99**, this 9-in-1 cooker undercuts the competition while adding a feature the Instant Pot Duo doesn't offer: **two inner pots**.

### Design and Features

The Carori is a 6-quart electric pressure cooker with a digital control panel, 12 preset programs, and the signature dual-pot system.

**Key specs:**
- **Size:** 6 quarts (enough for 4-6 servings)
- **Programs:** 12 presets including Pressure Cook, Slow Cook, Rice, Steam, Saute, Keep Warm
- **Pots:** Stainless steel + nonstick (both 6 qt)
- **Controls:** Digital panel with clear button labels
- **Safety:** Automatic pressure release valve, locking lid, overheat protection

The control panel is straightforward — no complicated menus or multi-button sequences. Users consistently mention how easy it is to operate compared to other digital pressure cookers.

### FAQ

#### How loud is the Carori pressure cooker during operation?
It's comparable to other electric pressure cookers — a quiet hum during pressure building, with a gentle hiss during natural pressure release. The beeps for program completion are audible but not overly loud.

#### Can I use Instant Pot accessories with the Carori?
The 6-quart Carori is similar in size to the Instant Pot Duo 6-quart. Steamer baskets, trivets, and silicone accessories designed for 6-quart Instant Pots should be compatible. Custom-fit sealing rings and specialized parts specific to Instant Pot may not fit.

#### What safety features does the Carori have?
Standard pressure cooker safety features include: lid-lock mechanism (can't open under pressure), automatic pressure regulation, overheat protection, and a quick-release valve option. Multiple redundancy systems prevent pressure buildup past safe levels.

#### How long does the Carori take to come to pressure?
Approximately 8-12 minutes to reach pressure, depending on the volume of food and liquid inside. This is typical for electric pressure cookers in this size class. Pre-heating the pot on saute mode can speed this up.
