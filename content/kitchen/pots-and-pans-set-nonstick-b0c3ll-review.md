## Where It Falls Short

The dishwasher warning isn't a suggestion — it's a requirement. Multiple reviewers report the coating degrades after dishwasher cycles, so this is a hand-wash set, period. If you hate washing pans by hand, this will become a source of friction.

The lack of induction compatibility is another limitation. Roughly 30% of new cooktops are induction, and this set won't work on them. Check your stove before buying.

Finally, the 8-inch skillet is small. It's perfect for one egg or a single grilled cheese, but it won't handle a two-egg omelet comfortably. You'll likely reach for the 10.5-inch more often.

## Who Should Buy This

This set is ideal for first-time apartment dwellers, college graduates outfitting their first kitchen, or anyone replacing a worn-out nonstick set on a budget. It's also a smart buy for RV owners — the nesting design saves serious cabinet space. Skip it if you have an induction cooktop, if you refuse to hand-wash cookware, or if you need a full 12-piece set with specialty pieces like a griddle or wok.
