Let's be blunt: most air fryer accessories are gimmicks that take up drawer space. The BYKITCHEN Stackable Square Air Fryer Rack is not one of them — provided your air fryer is square. This two-tier stainless steel rack genuinely doubles your cooking surface, and with 220 ratings averaging 4.4 stars, the consensus backs it up. But it has a critical compatibility catch you need to know before clicking buy.

**What you actually get**

The package contains two stackable racks, each measuring roughly 8.5 x 8.5 inches with a 1.5-inch gap between tiers when stacked. The construction is 304 stainless steel — the same grade used in commercial kitchens — with a silicone coating on the feet. That silicone matters: it grips the basket floor, so the whole assembly doesn't slide around when you shake your fries mid-cycle. The racks nest flat for storage, which keeps your cabinet from turning into a cluttered mess.

**Real-world performance**

The design does something surprisingly smart: it elevates food off the basket floor entirely. This means air circulates under and around every piece, not just on top. In practice, that translates to crispier undersides on wings and more even browning on vegetables. One verified buyer put it plainly: *"I can cook a full pound of chicken wings in one batch instead of doing two separate runs. They came out crispy on all sides, not just the top."*

The stacking mechanism is simple — each rack has four legs that slot into the corners of the one below. It holds steady even when you're jostling the basket mid-cook. Multiple reviewers noted the racks don't wobble or collapse, even with heavier loads like pork chops or fish fillets.

**Where it falls short**

Here's the honest part. This rack is square-specific. If you own a round basket air fryer, this won't fit — period. Even among square models, you need to verify your basket is at least 8.5 inches wide. It fits most 5-8 quart models from Ninja, Cosori, and Instant Pot, but it's not universal.

There's also a height limitation. Stacking two tiers means the top rack sits close to the heating element. Tall items like bone-in chicken thighs on the bottom tier can block airflow to the top layer, leaving the upper rack's food undercooked. One reviewer noted: *"Works great for flat stuff like bacon and shrimp. But I tried stacking chicken drumsticks and the top rack came out less crisp."* Keep the bottom tier to low-profile foods when stacking fully.

Cleanup is straightforward — both racks are dishwasher-safe, though the silicone feet can trap water if you don't angle them properly when loading.

**Who should buy this**

- **Batch cookers**: If you're feeding a family and hate doing multiple air fryer rounds, this cuts cook time in half.
- **Meal preppers**: Cook a full tray of chicken breast or salmon fillets in one cycle, then portion out for the week.
- **Square air fryer owners with 5-8 quart capacity**: This is your sweet spot. Measure your basket before ordering.

**Skip it if** you have a round basket, a compact air fryer under 5 quarts, or you mostly cook tall, bulky items that need vertical clearance.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit my Cosori 5.8 quart air fryer?**
A: Yes, if your Cosori has a square basket. The 5.8 quart models typically measure 8.8 x 8.8 inches inside, which accommodates these racks. Measure your basket's interior width to confirm before ordering.

**Q: Can I use both tiers at once?**
A: Yes, but keep the bottom tier to flat items like bacon, shrimp, or sliced vegetables. Save the top tier for smaller pieces that don't need much clearance from the heating element.

**Q: Does the silicone coating on the feet withstand high heat?**
A: Yes. Food-grade silicone is rated up to 450°F, which exceeds what most air fryers reach. Reviewers report no melting or odor issues after months of use.
