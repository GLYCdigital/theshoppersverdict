**Is a 20-pack of metal straws actually worth your money, or are you just accumulating drawer clutter?** I've tested enough reusable straws to tell you the difference between a thoughtful set and a landfill-delaying gimmick. This one from the 10.5"/8.5" stainless category sits firmly in the first camp — with a few caveats worth knowing before you click buy.

**What's Actually in the Box**

You get 20 straight straws: 10 long (10.5") and 10 short (8.5"). Alongside them are two cylindrical cleaning brushes — and this matters more than you'd think. Many budget straw sets ship brushes that are too short or too flimsy to do the job. These bristles run the full length of the longest straw, which is the difference between a 30-second rinse and a five-minute wrestling match with a sponge.

The straws themselves are 304 food-grade stainless steel. That's the same grade used in quality cookware — it won't leach metallic taste into your drink and resists corrosion from acidic beverages like lemon water or iced coffee. The finish is brushed, not mirror-polished, which means fewer visible fingerprints and less scratching over time.

**Real-World Performance**

I put these through a week of daily use: iced coffee in a Yeti-style tumbler, smoothies with frozen berries, carbonated water, and hot tea. The long straws reach the bottom of a 30-ounce tumbler without your lips touching the lid — a small but significant comfort win. The short ones handle standard drinking glasses and mason jars without looking absurdly long.

The 6mm diameter is the sweet spot for most drinks. Thick smoothies pass through without requiring a superhuman suck, but it's not so wide that you're getting a mouthful of liquid per sip. Several Amazon reviewers echo this: *"The width is perfect for smoothies — not too thick, not too skinny. My kids actually prefer these over plastic now."*

Cleaning is straightforward: the brush slides through, a quick soapy rinse, done. Dishwasher-safe, though I'd recommend the top rack to preserve the finish. One buyer noted, *"I've run these through the dishwasher at least 50 times and they still look brand new — no rust, no pitting."*

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, every straw in this pack is straight. No bent option. That means with certain travel mugs or cups with narrow lids, you'll need to tilt the cup further than you might like. If you primarily drink from car cup holders, consider a set with angled straws instead.

Second, stainless steel is a thermal conductor. With hot drinks — especially straight from a kettle — the straw itself gets warm. Not scalding, but noticeably uncomfortable on the lips after a few minutes. This isn't a defect; it's physics. If hot beverages are your main use case, bamboo or silicone-tipped straws are better.

Third, the metal is on the thinner side. It's not flimsy — I bent one slightly by accident and it straightened back without breaking — but heavy biters will notice some flex. The included cleaning brush handles the interior fine, but the exterior can show minor dents if you're rough with them in a drawer.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The household replacing plastic straws** — 20 straws means you can keep a few in the kitchen, a few in the car, and still have spares for guests. The price-per-straw is genuinely hard to beat.
- **The iced-coffee enthusiast** — the 10.5" length is perfect for tall tumblers, and the 6mm bore handles cold brew with ice without clogging.
- **The eco-conscious gift-giver** — this set pairs well with a cleaning brush and makes a practical housewarming gift.

**Skip it if** you exclusively drink hot beverages (the thermal issue will annoy you), you need bent straws for travel mugs, or you prefer heavier-gauge metal that feels more substantial in hand.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these straws safe for kids?**
A: Yes, but with supervision. The edges are smooth and rounded, not sharp, but stainless steel is rigid — a child running with one could be a hazard. The 8.5" length is better suited for smaller hands.

**Q: Will these scratch or dent easily?**
A: The brushed finish hides minor scratches well, and the steel is resistant to normal use. Dropping one on tile could leave a small dent, but it won't affect function.

**Q: Do they work with bubble tea or thick smoothies?**
A: The 6mm diameter handles most smoothies fine. For boba pearls or very thick blends, you'll want a wider 10-12mm straw — these aren't designed for that.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a no-nonsense, budget-friendly way to ditch single-use plastic straws without sacrificing durability or cleaning ease. The two-length system covers most drinking vessels, and the included brushes make maintenance genuinely painless.

**Skip it if** you need angled straws, primarily drink hot beverages, or demand the heft of premium single-straw brands. For everyone else, this is a solid 4.6/5 — a practical, well-executed set that delivers exactly what it promises, with no gimmicks.
