## What's Actually in the Box

You get two components: the 4392067 dryer belt (a 92.5-inch, 4-rib belt) and the W10837240 idler pulley assembly. That's it — no rollers, no glides, no instruction sheet. ## Where It Falls Short

The main frustration: there's no installation guide. If you've never opened up a dryer before, you'll be lost. The good news is that this kit is popular enough that a quick search for "4392067 dryer repair" pulls up dozens of walkthrough videos.

The belt also ships tightly coiled. If you install it immediately without letting it relax, it can twist and cause the drum to bind. Give it 30 minutes to straighten out before you start.

One more consideration — this kit doesn't include the drum rollers or the felt seal. If your dryer is also thumping (not just squealing), you'll want to order those separately. The $20–30 you save doing it yourself is real, but only if you're comfortable with a screwdriver and a couple of hours of your Saturday.
