The Chefman 4 QT Compact Air Fryer proves you don't need to spend a fortune for excellent air frying. At under $60, it delivers crisp, delicious food with minimal counter space and minimal hassle.

## What Makes It Great

The easy-view window is a surprisingly useful feature. Most budget air fryers require you to pull the basket to check on food, losing precious heat. The Chefman lets you peek without interrupting the cook cycle.

It's also PFAS-free. "I was careful to buy one that was PFAS-free," noted one first-time air fryer buyer. The non-stick coating performs well, and reviewers consistently praise how easy it is to clean.

First-time air fryer users are especially enthusiastic. "This is my new best friend in the kitchen," one reviewer wrote. Another who "resisted jumping on the air fryer bandwagon for years" now uses it almost daily for bacon, veggies, and pork chops.

The 4-quart size hits a sweet spot for 1-2 person households. It's compact enough to live on the counter, yet large enough for a full meal.

## Where It Falls Short

The 4-quart capacity is ideal for singles and couples, but families of four or more may find it limiting. You can cook in batches, but that defeats some of the convenience.

The touch controls work well when dry but can be less responsive with wet fingers — a common issue across touch-controlled kitchen appliances.

## The Bottom Line

The Chefman 4 QT Compact Air Fryer delivers outstanding value at $60. PFAS-free construction, the easy-view window, and excellent performance make it a top choice for anyone new to air frying or looking for a compact, affordable option. With 4.6 stars from nearly 1,800 reviewers, the verdict is clear: this is a winner.
