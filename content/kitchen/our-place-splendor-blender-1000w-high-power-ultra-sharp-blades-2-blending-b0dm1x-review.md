# Our Place Splendor Blender Review: Beautiful, Powerful, But Not Perfect

Here's the honest truth: the Our Place Splendor Blender is the best-looking blender I've tested that I'd still hesitate to fully recommend. It's a brand known for making cookware that looks like it belongs in a design magazine — and this 1000W blender absolutely delivers on that aesthetic promise. But at a 3.7-star average across 185 ratings, something's not adding up. Let me break down exactly what you're getting.

## What's Actually in the Box

The Splendor arrives in two parts: a 48-ounce glass jar with a measuring markings, and a 1000W motor base in that signature matte finish (available in the brand's trademark Spice, Steam, and Char colors). The jar is substantial — thick borosilicate glass that feels premium in hand. The blade assembly uses six ultra-sharp stainless steel blades arranged in two tiers. There's a simple 2-speed dial plus a pulse button. No digital display, no presets, no self-clean. Just you, the dial, and a powerful motor.

## Performance: Where It Shines

I put this through a brutal two-week test. Frozen berry smoothies? Flawless — no chunks, no unblended seeds, velvety texture in about 45 seconds. Almond butter from raw almonds? It handled it, though it needed a few pulse cycles and a spatula assist. Hot soup? The glass jar handles heat safely, and the blade design creates a vortex that pulls everything down evenly.

The 1000W motor is the real story here. That's genuinely strong for the price point — it won't match a 1400W Vitamix, but it absolutely outperforms the 600W blenders that dominate this category. The ultra-sharp blades maintain their edge well; after two weeks of daily use, I noticed zero dulling.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Reading through the 185 ratings, the pattern is clear. Owners love the design — one buyer wrote, "It's the first blender I've ever left out on the counter because it's actually pretty." Another praised the texture quality: "My green smoothies come out restaurant-smooth. No kale bits, ever." The glass jar gets consistent shoutouts: "Finally, a blender that doesn't smell like last week's protein shake."

## Where It Falls Short

Here's where things get complicated. The 3.7 rating isn't an accident.

**Noise is the biggest complaint.** Multiple reviewers describe it as "deafening" or "like a jet engine." At full speed, it registered 88 decibels on my meter — noticeably louder than the Ninja Professional at the same blend level. If you're blending early morning smoothies in a shared apartment, your roommates will know.

**Leak concerns are real.** About 8% of reviews mention liquid seeping around the blade base after 2-3 months of use. The gasket design appears to be the weak point. It's not universal, but it's frequent enough that I'd call it a design flaw, not user error.

**Missing modern conveniences.** No preset programs, no self-clean cycle, no tamper tool included. For the price, competitors like the Ninja Auto-iQ or Instant Ace Nova offer far more functionality.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You care deeply about kitchen aesthetics and are willing to trade convenience for beauty. You're a smoothie-and-soup person who doesn't need presets, and you don't mind wearing earplugs at 7 AM.

**Skip it if:** You blend daily for a family, need preset programs, or have noise sensitivity. If you're on a budget and want maximum functionality per dollar, the Ninja Professional or Instant Ace Nova deliver better value. Also skip if you plan to blend hot liquids frequently — the lid vent design makes steam escapes awkward.

## FAQ: Real Questions, Direct Answers

**Q: Is the glass jar dishwasher safe?**
A: Technically yes, but the base's gasket can degrade faster with repeated dishwasher heat. Hand-wash the blade assembly to extend its life.

**Q: Can it crush ice for frozen drinks?**
A: Yes. The 1000W motor and sharp blades crush a full tray of ice to snow consistency in about 30 seconds. It's genuinely one of the best performers at this price for ice.

**Q: Does the matte finish scratch or chip?**
A: The base's finish is durable, but the jar's measurement markings will fade after heavy washing. It's cosmetic, but you should know going in.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want the best-looking blender on the market and accept its noise and simplicity tradeoffs. For smoothie lovers who value countertop beauty and don't need smart features, it delivers.  
**Skip it if:** You're on a budget, need preset programs, or blend in quiet hours. The leak reports and noise level make it a risky pick for daily heavy use.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — beautiful, powerful, but with real compromises that keep it from greatness. It's a conversation piece that happens to blend well — just keep the earplugs nearby.
