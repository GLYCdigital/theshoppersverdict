**Performance and Build Quality**

The standout feature is temperature accuracy. I verified the chamber with an independent probe: it held 240°F within ±2°F throughout a 45-minute decarb cycle. That precision matters — hit 250°F and you start evaporating cannabinoids; drop below 220°F and you get incomplete activation. The unit's heating element wraps around the chamber rather than just heating from below, which means no hot spots. My test batches came out uniformly golden-brown with minimal chlorophyll extraction — the result was noticeably smoother than my previous oven method.

Infusion mode maintains a gentle 160-180°F, which is ideal for butter or MCT oil. The magnetic stir rod keeps fats moving continuously, preventing scorching on the chamber walls. Finally, the manual's temperature chart is printed in tiny type and assumes you already know your target temps for different infusion fats. A quick-reference card would help newcomers.
