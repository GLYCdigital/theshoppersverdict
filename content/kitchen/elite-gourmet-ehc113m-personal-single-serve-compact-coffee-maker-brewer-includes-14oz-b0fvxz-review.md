If you live alone, work from a dorm room, or just want one decent cup without firing up a 12-cup machine, the Elite Gourmet EHC113M is the kind of no-nonsense appliance that quietly earns its counter space. It's not flashy, but at roughly the size of a large travel mug, it solves a very specific problem: brewing a single 14-ounce cup directly into the included mug, using whatever ground coffee you already own.

**What's actually in the box**

The EHC113M ships with the brewer base, a 14oz stainless steel travel-style mug, a reusable mesh filter basket, and a permanent filter. There are no pods, no cartridges, no proprietary anything—just pour in water, add a scoop of grounds, and flip the switch. The mug itself is double-walled enough to hold comfortably, though the brewer's heating element is where the real work happens.

**How it performs in the real world**

Brew time lands around 4-5 minutes for a full 14oz cup, which is slower than a Keurig but faster than a pour-over if you factor in the fuss. The water heats via a simple drip system—no pump, no pressure—so you're getting a straightforward, no-frills extraction. The included mesh filter lets more oils through than paper filters do, which means a slightly fuller-bodied cup. If you prefer a cleaner taste, you can drop a #4 paper filter into the basket, but the mesh works fine for most daily drinkers.

One thing that surprised me: the brewer shuts off automatically when the water reservoir empties, which is a nice safety touch. But there's no timer or auto-off after brewing completes—you flip the switch manually. Forgetting it once means the heating element stays warm until you remember.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the 215 ratings, the recurring theme is simplicity and value. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I just wanted a cup of coffee without pods or a giant machine. This does exactly that."* Another noted the portability—*"Took it camping in the RV, works great off the inverter."* The reusable filter is a consistent highlight, with buyers appreciating that there's zero ongoing cost beyond the coffee itself.

**Where it falls short**

The honest downsides are real but manageable. First, the mug is single-wall stainless steel, so it doesn't retain heat well. Your coffee goes from hot to lukewarm in about 20 minutes. Pre-warming the mug with hot water before brewing helps significantly. Second, the cord is short—about 24 inches—so counter placement matters. Third, the on/off switch has no indicator light, which sounds minor until you're squinting to see if it's on.

**Who should buy this**

- **Single coffee drinkers** who want one fresh cup without wasting a full pot
- **Dorm residents** with limited counter space and no interest in pod waste
- **RV or camper owners** who need something compact and 12V-friendly
- **Budget-conscious buyers** who already own ground coffee and refuse to pay per-pod prices

**Skip it if** you need a thermal carafe, programmable timer, or the ability to brew more than 14 ounces at once. This is strictly a one-cup machine.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it work with K-Cups?**  
A: No. It's designed for ground coffee with the included reusable filter. K-Cups require a separate adapter that isn't included.

**Q: Can I brew less than 14 ounces?**  
A: Yes—just add less water. The brew cycle ends when the reservoir empties, so a 10oz cup works fine.

**Q: Is the mug dishwasher safe?**  
A: The mug is hand-wash recommended. The filter basket can go on the top rack, but the mug's stainless finish will dull in the dishwasher.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a compact, pod-free, low-cost single-serve brewer that just works. **Skip it if** you need programmability, hotter coffee without preheating, or more capacity. At 4.3 stars, the EHC113M earns its rating through sheer practicality—it does one thing well, costs almost nothing to run, and doesn't pretend to be more than it is. For the right buyer, that's exactly the point.
