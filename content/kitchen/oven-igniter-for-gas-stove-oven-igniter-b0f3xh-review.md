## FAQ

**Q: How do I know if my igniter is actually the problem?**
If the oven clicks but won't light, and the igniter doesn't glow orange after 30 seconds, it's dead. You can also test resistance with a multimeter — a good igniter reads 40-80 ohms; an open circuit means it's gone.

**Q: Will this work on a bake and broil igniter?**
Yes, most buyers use it for both. The bake and broil igniters are typically the same part on major brands. Just check your model's parts diagram to confirm the wire length works for both locations.

**Q: Do I need to replace the gas valve at the same time?**
No. Unless the valve is visibly corroded or you're getting gas smell without ignition, the igniter is the failure point. Replace just the igniter first, and test before spending more money.
