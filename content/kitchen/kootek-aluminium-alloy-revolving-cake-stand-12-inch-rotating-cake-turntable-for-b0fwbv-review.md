Here's where the Kootek earns its rating. The rotation is the star: it spins on sealed ball bearings with a smoothness that feels almost hydraulic. **FAQ**

**Q: Will this scratch my cake boards or plates?**
The platter is smooth and anodized, but if you're sliding ceramic plates directly on it, you can scratch both surfaces. Use a cardboard cake board or a silicone mat underneath. The anodized finish resists scratches better than bare aluminum, but it's not scratch-proof.

**Q: Can it handle a heavy wedding cake?**
Yes — the ball-bearing mechanism and rigid aluminium construction handle a fully stacked 3-tier cake (roughly 10-12 pounds) without stuttering. The silicone base keeps it grounded. Just don't expect to spin a 4-tier cake with a heavy structure on it; that's beyond consumer-grade territory.

**Q: Is the platter food-safe?**
The anodized aluminium is food-safe and won't react with acidic ingredients like buttercream or fruit fillings. However, it's not meant for direct food contact for extended periods — use it as a decorating surface, not a serving platter for fresh fruit.
