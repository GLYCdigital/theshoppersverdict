# Extra Support Anti Fatigue Comfort Mat: The Kitchen Floor Upgrade Your Back Will Thank You For

You’ve heard the pitch before: "Anti-fatigue mat!" "Ergonomic support!" "Stand in comfort!" Most of those promises evaporate after two weeks when the foam pancakes out to a sad, flat shadow of its former self. The Extra Support Anti Fatigue Comfort Mat for Standing Desks, W — despite the clunky name — is not that mat. At 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, it’s earning its keep in kitchens, laundry rooms, and home offices. Here’s what you’re actually paying for.

## What Actually Shows Up

The mat itself arrives rolled tightly in a box. Unroll it, lay it flat for a few hours, and the edges settle down. You get the mat — that’s it. No adhesives, no anti-slip spray, no gimmicks. The textured top surface is slightly rubberized, which is immediately noticeable in a good way: it doesn't feel like cheap squishy foam. The underside has a honeycomb grip pattern that does real work keeping the mat planted.

## Real-World Performance in the Kitchen

I’m reviewing this as a kitchen mat because that’s where most shoppers will use it. Standing at a counter prepping vegetables for 45 minutes is a different beast than standing at a desk. The 3/4-inch thickness is the sweet spot — substantial enough to absorb shock through your heels and knees, but not so plush that you feel unstable while chopping.

The waterproof claim is legitimate. I spilled a half-full glass of red wine on it during testing (accidentally, but it made for good science). It beaded up, wiped off with a paper towel, and left zero stain. The surface texture also gives your bare feet some traction when things get greasy near the stove. One Amazon reviewer noted, *“I stand on this 8 hours a day at work and my feet don't ache anymore. The kitchen one I bought for home is holding up even better than the office one.”* That tracks with what I found.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

Reading through the 235 ratings, three themes dominate. First, durability — multiple reviewers mention owning it for months with no visible wear patterns. Second, the grip — the mat doesn’t slide across tile floors, which is a constant complaint with cheaper mats. Third, the easy cleanup. As one verified buyer put it: *“I was skeptical about the waterproof claim but my kid dumped a bowl of soup on it and it wiped right up. My old mat would have absorbed that and smelled forever.”* That’s the real-world scenario you need this for.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has two honest weak points. The edge beveling is shallow — more of a slope than a ramp. If you’re walking barefoot and catch your toe on the edge, it can trip you. It’s a minor annoyance, not a safety hazard, but worth knowing. Also, the mat is not machine washable. Surface spills wipe clean, but if something like turmeric or berry juice sets into the texture, you’ll need to scrub with a cloth and soap. A deep clean means bending over with a sponge, not tossing it in the washing machine.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You stand at a kitchen counter or desk for more than two hours a day and your current mat has lost its bounce. You have tile or hardwood floors and need something that won’t slide. You cook often and want peace of mind against spills and splatters.

**Skip it if:** You’re looking for something to fill a tiny 2x2-foot space — this mat’s footprint benefits from a larger area. Or if you prefer plush, carpet-like comfort over firmer support. This is a supportive mat, not a squishy one.

**Q: Is it actually waterproof or just water-resistant?**
