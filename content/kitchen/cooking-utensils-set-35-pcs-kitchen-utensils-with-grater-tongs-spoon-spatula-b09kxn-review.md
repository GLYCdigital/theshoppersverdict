## Where It Falls Short

The honest tradeoffs:

- **No storage solution.** 35 pieces need a home, and the set doesn't include a caddy or hanging rack. You'll want a drawer organizer or a countertop crock.
- **Nylon heads stain.** Tomato sauce and turmeric will leave light discoloration on the white nylon heads over time. It doesn't affect function, but it's noticeable if you're picky about appearances.
- **The potato masher is basic.** It works, but it's not built for heavy-duty mashing — think soft boiled potatoes, not root vegetables.

## FAQ

**Q: Are the handles really heat-resistant to 450°F?**
Yes, the stainless steel handles are rated for that temperature. The nylon heads have a lower threshold — around 390°F — so don't leave them resting in a hot pan.

**Q: Can the nylon heads go in the dishwasher?**
Yes, the heads are dishwasher-safe. The handles are also dishwasher-safe, though hand-washing keeps the stainless finish looking better longer.

**Q: Do the handles and heads stay connected securely?**
The snap-lock mechanism is solid. Multiple reviewers confirmed no heads have come loose during use. The connection is firm enough that you won't accidentally separate them mid-stir.
