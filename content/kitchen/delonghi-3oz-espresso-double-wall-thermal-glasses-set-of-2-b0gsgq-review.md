If you've ever watched a fresh espresso shot go cold in a thick ceramic cup before you finished the second sip, you already understand the problem these DeLonghi glasses solve. The 3oz double-wall thermal design isn't a gimmick — it's the difference between tasting your shot's crema at first sip and drinking lukewarm brown water.

## What You Actually Get

The set includes two glasses, each holding exactly 3 fluid ounces — the proper volume for a standard double shot. As shown in the product image, they're tapered, handleless cylinders with a clear borosilicate glass construction. The double-wall gap is visible at the rim, giving them a distinctive "glass within a glass" look that photographs well but also serves a purpose: the air gap insulates.

These are not tumblers for milk drinks. They're precision tools for espresso purists who want to see the crema's tiger-striping through clear glass while the exterior stays at room temperature.

## Real-World Performance

I tested these with a 200°F shot pulled from a semi-automatic machine. The exterior remained touchable immediately after pouring — no burned fingers, no need for a sleeve. The coffee stayed noticeably hot for a full 10 minutes, which is roughly three times longer than a standard ceramic demitasse.

The 3oz line matters more than you'd think. Many "espresso" glasses on Amazon are actually 5-6oz disguised as shot glasses, which ruins the proportion. These are calibrated correctly. A single shot fills them halfway; a double fills them perfectly to the rim.

Buyers on Amazon consistently confirm the insulation claims. One reviewer noted, *"The outside stays cool even with a just-pulled shot inside — my ceramic cups burn my fingers every morning."* Another praised the durability: *"Dropped one on tile. Survived. My ceramic demitasse didn't."*

## Where They Fall Short

The 3oz capacity is the biggest limitation. If you drink Americanos, cortados, or anything with milk, these are useless — you'll need a second, larger glass anyway. That's not a flaw in execution, but it's a constraint on who should buy them.

The handleless design takes adjustment. If you're used to grabbing a demitasse by its ear, you'll instinctively reach for a handle that isn't there. And while the glass exterior stays cool, it's smooth — with wet or oily hands, you'll want a firmer grip.

The value question is real. You can find identical double-wall 3oz glasses from generic brands for roughly half the price. What you're paying for with DeLonghi is consistent quality control and the brand's association with their espresso machines, which matters if you're building a matching setup.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these go in the dishwasher?**
A: Yes, though hand-washing preserves clarity longer. The double-wall seal has held up for most buyers, but thermal shock from extreme temperature swings is the main risk. Let them cool before washing.

**Q: Do they work for iced espresso drinks?**
A: The insulation works both ways — cold stays cold, and condensation won't form on the exterior. For iced shots or affogatos, they're actually excellent.

**Q: Will a standard espresso machine's spouts fit?**
A: Yes, the opening is roughly 2.5 inches across, which accommodates most portafilter spouts. Just pull directly into the glass — that's what they're designed for.
