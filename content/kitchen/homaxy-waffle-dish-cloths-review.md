# Homaxy Waffle Dish Cloths Review: 6-Pack Kitchen Towels Tested

Kitchen towels are one of those things you don't think about until you're stuck with a set that smears water instead of absorbing it. The Homaxy 100% Cotton Waffle Weave Dish Cloths have amassed over 15,000 reviews on Amazon — so we wanted to see if the hype matches reality.

## First Impressions

The 6-pack arrives neatly folded in a simple package. The dark grey color is a welcome departure from the usual white or beige kitchen cloths, and it does a great job of hiding stains. Each cloth measures 12 x 12 inches, which is compact compared to standard dish towels — think more of a bar mop size.

The waffle weave texture is soft to the touch right out of the package. A quick wash before first use is recommended (common for 100% cotton textiles), and after that initial wash, they hit their stride.

## Absorbency Test

We tested these against a standard flat-weave cotton towel by spilling 100ml of water on a counter. The Homaxy cloth absorbed the entire puddle in two passes with no streaks. The waffle weave structure creates tiny pockets that trap water efficiently, and the 100% cotton construction means it wicks moisture away from surfaces rather than pushing it around.

Drying time is where these really shine. After use, the waffle weave air-dries in about half the time of a standard terry cloth towel. That means less musty smell and less bacterial growth between uses.

## Durability

After 30 days of daily use and weekly machine washing, the Homaxy cloths show minimal wear. No fraying at the edges, no pilling on the surface. The dark grey has faded slightly but still looks far better than white cloths would after the same period.

## Value

At roughly $11.50 for six cloths (under $2 each), this is exceptional value. For context, a single high-end Swedish dishcloth can cost $5-6. The Homaxy 6-pack costs less than two of those.

## Final Verdict

There's a reason 15,000+ people reviewed these cloths. They absorb well, dry fast, last through countless washes, and cost pennies per cloth. If your kitchen needs an upgrade in the towel department, this is an easy buy.
