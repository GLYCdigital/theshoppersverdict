Most measuring sets fail in one of two ways: they're cheap plastic that stains and warps, or they're decent metal but scatter across three drawers. The Accmor 11-piece set tackles both problems at once. After 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, it's clear this set has found its audience — and for good reason.

**What's actually in the box**

You get 11 pieces total: 6 measuring cups (1 cup, 1/2 cup, 1/3 cup, 1/4 cup, 1/8 cup, and 3/4 cup) plus 5 measuring spoons (1 tbsp, 1 tsp, 1/2 tsp, 1/4 tsp, and 1/8 tsp). That 3/4 cup is a thoughtful addition — most sets skip it, forcing you to do math mid-recipe. Everything nests together via magnets in the handles, so the whole collection occupies roughly the footprint of a single large spoon.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The stainless steel body is the headline here. Unlike plastic sets that absorb turmeric stains or nylon ones that melt near hot pots, this metal construction handles heat and aggressive ingredients without complaint. The etched measurement markings are a genuine upgrade — they're stamped into the metal rather than painted on, so they won't fade after months of dishwasher cycles.

The magnetic system works as advertised. Each piece has a magnet embedded in the handle, and they click together satisfyingly. In practice, this means you can grab the entire set in one hand and pull out exactly what you need. One buyer noted, "I love that they stick together — no more rummaging through the drawer for the half teaspoon." That's the real convenience win.

**Where it falls short**

The handles are comfortable but not ergonomic — they're straightforward metal loops, nothing more. If you have arthritis or grip issues, you might find them less comfortable than rubberized alternatives.

The magnets are strong enough to keep the set nested but won't support hanging the cups on a magnetic strip on your wall. If you wanted to display them, you're out of luck — they'll hold together but not support their own weight hanging vertically.

Finally, there's no leveling tool. For precise baking, you'll need a straight edge to scrape off excess dry ingredients. It's a minor omission, but worth noting if you're particular about accuracy.

**Who should buy this**

This set suits three types of shoppers: home bakers who want durable metal over plastic, small-kitchen dwellers who need compact storage, and anyone tired of replacing cheap sets every year. It's also a solid gift for newlyweds or first apartments — the stainless finish looks sharp and professional.

Skip it if you need magnetic wall-mounting capability, prefer color-coded measurements for quick identification, or want a set with a built-in leveler.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Are these dishwasher safe?* Yes, the stainless steel handles heat and detergent fine. The etched markings won't fade. However, hand-washing will keep the magnetic components in better shape long-term.

*Can I use them for both wet and dry ingredients?* Absolutely. The cups handle liquids without leaking, and the steel won't react with acidic ingredients like tomatoes or citrus.

*How accurate are the measurements?* They're consistent with standard US measurements, though like most stamped metal sets, they're not laboratory-grade. For everyday cooking and baking, they'll serve you well.

**The Verdict**

The Accmor 11-piece set earns its 4.6-star rating through straightforward competence. It's not flashy, but it does exactly what a measuring set should: accurate measurements, durable materials, and smart storage. The magnetic nesting genuinely improves daily kitchen workflow, and the etched markings mean this set will still look new in five years.

**Skip it if** you need wall-mountable magnets or prefer color-coded tools for quick identification.
