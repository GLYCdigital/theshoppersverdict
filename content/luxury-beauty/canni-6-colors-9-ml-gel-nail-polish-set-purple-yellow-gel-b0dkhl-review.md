**The CANNI 6-Color Gel Polish Set: A Surprisingly Curated Mini Collection**

Most gel polish sets under $30 are a gamble — you get six random shades, three of which look like muddy versions of each other. The CANNI 6 Colors 9 ML Set (Purple Yellow collection) sidesteps that trap. This is a tightly edited palette of warm purples, soft lilacs, and buttery yellows that feel less like a starter kit and more like a capsule wardrobe for your nails.

**What's Actually in the Box**

Six 9ml bottles, each with a standard gel polish brush. No lamp, no base or top coat, no files. That's it. The shades lean spring/summer — think lavender, grape, buttercup, and a muted mustard. The bottles are compact and squat, which is fine for storage but means you'll get roughly 8-10 manicures per bottle, not the 15+ you'd expect from a 15ml drugstore polish.

**Performance and Build Quality**

Here's where this set earns its 4.7 rating. The formula is self-leveling in a way that surprises at this price point. It flows on smoothly, doesn't pull or drag, and two thin coats give full opacity even on the lightest lilac — that's rare in budget gels, which usually need three or four coats to avoid visible streaks.

The brush deserves a specific mention. It's flat, about 8mm wide, with a rounded tip. It covers the entire nail bed in one pass for most standard-width nails. No flooding of cuticles, no wasted product from brush-splitting.

Cure time is standard: 60 seconds under a 36W UV/LED lamp (not included). Wear time in my testing held up two weeks with minimal chipping at the tips, though I used a separate top coat. Without a top coat, expect wear to drop to about 10 days with visible dulling by day seven.

**What Buyers Keep Saying**

The Amazon reviews (235 ratings, 4.7 average) consistently highlight two things. As one verified buyer put it: *"The colors are exactly as pictured — no filter games here."* Color accuracy is a real issue with cheap gel sets, so this matters. Another reviewer noted: *"I've bought $50+ brands that streak more than this. Two coats and done."*

**Where It Falls Short**

- **No top coat included.** This is the biggest gap. You need a separate top coat for the glossy finish these shades deserve. A matte finish on a lavender just looks flat.
- **9ml bottles.** Smaller than standard. If you're doing weekly manicures, you'll repurchase sooner than you'd like.
- **Shade range is narrow.** It's all purples and yellows — if you want a nude or red in the same set, look elsewhere. The curation is intentional, but it's not versatile.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Home manicure hobbyists** who want a cohesive palette for spring/summer without paying $12+ per shade.
- **Beginners** — the forgiving formula and wide brush make it easier to get clean application on the first try.
- **Travelers** — the compact bottles take up half the space of standard gel jars.

**Skip it if** you need a versatile all-season set, or if you're allergic to buying a top coat separately. Also skip if you prefer larger 15ml bottles to justify the cost per use.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do I need a UV/LED lamp?**  
A: Yes. This is genuine gel polish — it won't air-dry. Any 36W+ lamp works. Don't try to skip this or you'll have sticky, smearing polish for days.

**Q: How many manicures per bottle?**  
A: Realistically, 8-10 if you use thin coats (which you should). The 9ml size is the tradeoff for the lower price.

**Q: Will these work on natural nails?**  
A: Yes, but they're not soak-off. You'll need to file them off or let them grow out. If you want removal without filing, look for a soak-off formula.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if** you want a color-coordinated set with premium-level pigmentation at a budget price, and you already own a lamp and top coat.

**Skip it if** you're starting from zero — you'll spend extra on accessories, and the small bottles won't feel like a bargain then.

For what it is — a focused, well-formulated mini collection — this is one of the better gel sets under $25. The color accuracy alone makes it worth the purchase. Just budget for the top coat.
