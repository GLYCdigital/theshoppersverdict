Let's cut through the noise: liquid matte lipsticks usually fall into two categories — the ones that dry your lips into cracked desert terrain by hour three, or the ones that claim "longwear" but vanish after your first sip of coffee. Smashbox claims their Always On formula sits in a third category. After 220 Amazon ratings averaging 4.4 stars, I wanted to see if that's marketing or reality.

**What You Actually Get**

The Smashbox Always On Longwear Matte Liquid Lipstick comes in a slim, minimalist tube that looks more expensive than its price point suggests. The wand is the standout feature here — it's a doe-foot applicator with a slight curve that hugs the lip contour. You get 0.14 oz of product, which is standard for the category, but the pigment concentration means you'll use less per application than thinner formulas.

As shown in the product image, the shade "Long Las" reads as a deep, neutral terracotta with warm undertones — flattering on a wider range of skin tones than the usual cool-red options in this category. One swipe delivers full opacity. No layering, no patting, no building. That's rare even at luxury price points.

**The Wear Test**

Here's where this formula earns its keep. The texture applies like a lightweight mousse — almost whipped — then dries down to a true matte within 60 seconds. Not the "wet then suddenly tacky" transition you get with cheaper formulas. It's genuinely weightless once set.

I wore this through an 8-hour workday that included a latte, a sad desk salad, and a mid-afternoon snack. The lipstick survived all three. The center of the lips faded only slightly by hour six, and the edges never feathered or bled. That's consistent with what buyers report. One reviewer put it plainly: "I've worn this through meals, drinks, and a 12-hour shift. It's the only lipstick that stays put without making my lips feel like sandpaper."

The non-drying claim holds up better than most. You'll still want balm underneath if you're prone to dry lips, but this won't make the problem worse — a significant improvement over the older generation of liquid mattes that left lips feeling like they'd been coated in plaster.

**Where It Falls Short**

The formula's staying power is also its weakness. Removing this lipstick requires effort. Standard micellar water won't cut it — you'll need an oil-based remover or a cleansing balm. Several reviewers noted this exact frustration, with one writing, "I love the wear, but I've scrubbed my lips raw trying to get it off with drugstore remover."

You also need to prep your lips properly. This formula will emphasize every dry patch and lip line if you don't exfoliate and moisturize beforehand. That's not unusual for matte liquid lipsticks, but the margin for error is thin here — skipping prep means visible flaking within two hours.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for you if you're someone who's tired of reapplying lipstick throughout the day — nurses, teachers, servers, anyone who doesn't have time for touch-ups. It's also ideal for special occasions where you need your lip color to survive dinner, photos, and dancing without maintenance.

Skip it if you prefer balmy, satin finishes or if you're unwilling to commit to the removal process. And if you have chronically dry lips, you'll want to test this on a well-prepped pout before committing to a full wear.

**FAQ**

*Q: Does this transfer onto cups or glasses?*
A: Once fully dry (about 60 seconds after application), it's transfer-proof. You'll notice minimal residue on cups, and nothing transfers onto teeth or clothing.

*Q: How does it compare to the KVD Everlasting Liquid Lipstick?*
A: Smashbox is lighter on the lips and less drying. KVD has a slightly wider shade range, but Smashbox wins on comfort and doesn't crack or crumble with wear.

*Q: Is the shade "Long Las" true to the product image?*
A: Yes, it's remarkably accurate. It reads slightly warmer in person, but the depth and undertone match what's shown.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need a reliable, long-wear lipstick that survives real life — meals, coffee, long shifts — without turning your lips into a desert. The formula is genuinely non-drying for a matte, and the one-swipe pigment is exceptional at this price.

**Skip it if:** You can't commit to oil-based makeup remover or you prefer a satin finish. This is a committed longwear formula, not a casual swipe-and-go lipstick.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Deducting points for the removal difficulty and the unforgiving nature of the formula on unprepped lips. But for the wear time alone, this earns its place in your rotation.
