**The 4-pack that solves the "which scent do I pick" problem**

Most dry shampoo shoppers face the same dilemma: you find one that works, but you're stuck with that single fragrance for weeks. Batiste's Variety Mix solves that by giving you four different scent profiles in one order — Original, Classic, and two others that rotate depending on stock. It's a smart way to test which one you actually like before committing to a full-size can of a single scent.

**What's actually in the box**

You get four 6.73-ounce aerosol cans. That's roughly 27 ounces of dry shampoo total — enough for three months of near-daily use if you're refreshing between washes. The formula uses rice starch as the primary oil absorber, which is a step up from the cornstarch base that many budget brands rely on. Rice starch particles are finer, which means less visible residue and faster absorption.

**Performance: the real-world test**

The application process is standard: shake well, hold six to eight inches from your roots, spray, wait 30 seconds, then brush or massage through. Where Batiste separates itself from the pack is in the absorption speed. The powder grabs oil almost immediately — you can see the difference at the roots within a minute. One shopper put it plainly: "I have dark, fine hair and this is the only dry shampoo that doesn't leave me looking gray. The rice starch actually disappears after I work it in."

The finish is matte, not stiff. Your hair moves naturally rather than feeling like you've sprayed it with hairspray. That's a genuine advantage if you're using dry shampoo on day two or three hair and want it to look like you just styled it, not like you're hiding a grease problem.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the 235 ratings, several themes emerge. The variety factor gets constant mention — people appreciate having options without buying four separate cans. The scent longevity is another winner. The fragrances aren't just initial bursts; they hold for several hours, which is rare in this category. And the value math works in its favor: per-ounce, this 4-pack costs less than buying individual cans at retail.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. The aerosol spray has a chemical propellant smell that hits your nose for the first few minutes after application. It fades, but if you're sensitive to synthetic fragrances, that initial blast might be off-putting. One reviewer noted, "I love the results but have to spray outside or in the bathroom with the fan on because the initial scent is strong."

Also, this is not a volumizing product. It absorbs oil and refreshes — that's it. If you're looking for something that adds body and lift at the roots, you'll need a separate texturizing spray. And the scent intensity varies across the four cans in the pack. Original is fairly mild; some of the others are noticeably stronger and can clash with perfume or other hair products.

**Who should buy this**

This 4-pack is ideal for three specific profiles: the gym-goer who needs a quick refresh between workouts, the traveler who wants a compact option for carry-on bags (all four cans are under the TSA aerosol limit), and the indecisive shopper who wants to sample multiple scents before committing. It's less ideal for those with extremely sensitive skin or noses — the fragrance load is real.

**FAQ**

**How often can I use this?**
Daily use is fine, but your scalp will thank you if you alternate with actual washes. The rice starch can build up over time if you never shampoo it out.

**Does it work on all hair colors?**
Yes, but dark hair requires a bit more brushing to work the powder through completely. The formula is white, though it disappears when massaged in.

**Is the scent strong enough to replace perfume?**
No — and it shouldn't be. The fragrance is meant to refresh hair, not serve as body fragrance.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a reliable, no-fuss dry shampoo with scent variety and strong oil absorption at a reasonable per-can price.

**Skip it if:** You have a sensitive nose, need volumizing properties, or prefer unscented hair products.

This 4-pack earns a 4.6 out of 5. It's not perfect — the initial chemical scent and inconsistent fragrance intensity across cans keep it from a perfect score — but for the price and performance, it's one of the best value options in the dry shampoo category.
