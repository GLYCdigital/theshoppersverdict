You've heard about the legendary fragrances of the 1980s — Giorgio, Obsession, Poison. Givenchy Ysatis belongs in that same conversation, but it's the one people tend to forget. That's a mistake. This is a bold, uncompromising floral chypre that doesn't apologize for its intensity, and for the right person, it's absolutely worth your attention.

## What You're Getting

The 3.3 fluid ounce (100ml) EDT comes in Givenchy's classic rectangular glass bottle with a substantial gold-toned cap. It's a handsome, weighty presentation that feels premium on a vanity. The sprayer itself is functional but not precise — it releases a wide mist that covers a lot of skin quickly, which is actually ideal for a fragrance this dense. One thing to note: this is a large bottle, roughly 5 inches tall, so it's not a travel companion.

## The Scent Story

Ysatis opens with a blast of aldehydes — that sharp, slightly sparkling sensation that immediately tells you this isn't a modern designer release. Within twenty minutes, the heart emerges: a rich bouquet of orange blossom, jasmine, and ylang-ylang that feels opulent and slightly honeyed. The base is where Ysatis truly shines, with sandalwood, vetiver, and a touch of civet-like warmth that lingers for hours.

This is not a subtle fragrance. Two sprays are sufficient for a full day. Three is pushing it. On skin, I consistently got 8-10 hours of noticeable presence. On a wool coat, it survived three days and a rain shower. If you want a fragrance that announces itself, this delivers.

## Honest Drawbacks

The biggest issue for most modern shoppers is the style. Ysatis is unapologetically old-school. If you're used to fresh aquatics, gourmands, or clean musks, this will feel heavy and potentially dated. The aldehyde opening genuinely borders on harsh for the first few minutes.

The sprayer also dispenses more product than necessary — you'll go through the bottle faster than expected if you're not careful. And while the 3.3 oz size offers good value per milliliter, it's a commitment. There's no travel-friendly option here.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're over 40 and nostalgic for classic fragrances, or you're a younger enthusiast who genuinely appreciates vintage compositions. You want a signature scent that lasts through a full workday plus evening plans. You prefer fragrances that project and make a statement.

**Skip it if:** You prefer light, close-to-skin scents. You're sensitive to strong florals or aldehydes. You're looking for something safe for a shared office environment. This is not a crowd-pleaser in the modern sense.

## FAQ

**Is Ysatis appropriate for daytime wear?**
It's heavy for casual daytime use. Save it for evenings, special occasions, or cooler weather. In summer heat, it becomes overwhelming.

**How does the EDT compare to the parfum version?**
The EDT is brighter and slightly sharper in the opening. The parfum is richer and more rounded but significantly more expensive. The EDT offers the best balance of value and performance.

**Does this smell like anything currently available?**
It's frequently compared to vintage Givenchy Amarige and some classic Chanel chypres, but Ysatis is more floral-forward and less powdery. It's genuinely unique in the current market.

## The Verdict

**4.2/5 stars**

Givenchy Ysatis is a masterpiece of a bygone era that still performs admirably today. It's not for everyone — the modern fragrance market has moved toward subtlety and approachability. But if you want a bold, long-lasting, complex scent that earns compliments and stands apart from everything on Sephora's shelves, this delivers in ways few current releases can match.

**Buy it if** you appreciate classic perfumery and want exceptional longevity. **Skip it if** you prefer modern minimalism or need something office-safe. At roughly $70-90 for 3.3 ounces, it's a reasonable investment in a piece of fragrance history that still works beautifully today.
