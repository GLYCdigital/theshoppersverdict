## What's Actually in the Box

This set includes a **1.6 oz (50ml) Eau de Parfum** and a **3.3 oz (100ml) matching body lotion**. That's significant — the lotion alone typically runs $50+ when purchased separately. As shown in the product image, everything arrives in Gucci's signature red floral-print gift box, which genuinely feels substantial. No gift wrap needed; it's ready to hand over.

The fragrance itself opens with a **bright pear and white gardenia** top note — not the heavy, syrupy floral you might expect from a designer house. The heart brings jasmine and tuberose, while the base settles into a soft, warm patchouli and brown sugar accord. It's youthful without being juvenile, floral without being powdery.

## Performance and Wear

Let's be honest about longevity: **this is not an 8-hour scent.** On most skin types, you're getting 4 to 5 hours of noticeable wear before it fades to a close-to-skin skin scent. That's actually typical for this category — fresh florals trade staying power for wearability. The body lotion helps extend it; layering both adds roughly an extra hour and a half.

Projection sits at moderate. You'll get a pleasant scent bubble within arm's reach, but don't expect to walk into a room and announce your arrival. If you prefer loud, sillage-heavy fragrances, this isn't it.
