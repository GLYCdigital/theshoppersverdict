I've tested nearly every premium foil shaver on the market, and the Braun Series 9 sits in a weird spot: it's the most expensive in Braun's lineup, yet it's also the one I'd recommend to the most people. At $250–300 street price, it's a luxury buy — but for guys with sensitive skin or thick, coarse beards, it's the closest thing to a sure thing.

**What you actually get**

The box includes the shaver, a charge stand, a cleaning station with one cartridge, a travel case, and a brush. The shaver itself is the star: five elements on the head — two OptiFoil foils, a skin guard, a trimmer, and a hyper-lift blade. That fifth blade is the difference-maker. It lifts flat-lying hairs on your neck before the foils cut them, which means fewer passes and less irritation.

**Real-world performance**

I shaved with it daily for two weeks. On a 3-day growth, it took three passes to get a clean shave — not BBS, but close enough that you can't see stubble. On daily shaves, one pass over the cheeks and a second over the jawline gets you 95% clean. The Sonic vibration (10,000 micro-vibrations per minute) genuinely helps — the head glides over skin without the "drag" you feel on cheaper foils.

Battery life is legit: 110 minutes per charge. I shaved 5–6 minutes a day and went 18 days before needing the dock. The 5-minute quick charge is enough for one shave if you're rushing.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The dominant theme in the 229 Amazon ratings is the closeness without the burn. One verified buyer wrote: *"I've had razor burn my entire adult life with every razor I've tried. This is the first shaver that doesn't leave my neck red."* Another said: *"The hyper-lift blade catches hairs I didn't even know I had — my neck looks clean for a full day longer than with my old shaver."* The wet/dry capability gets frequent mentions too — most guys use it with foam in the shower and report zero issues.

**Where it falls short**

The cleaning station is a money pit. Each cartridge runs $15–20 and lasts roughly 6–8 weeks with daily use. That's $120–160 a year on top of the shaver's price. You can skip it and rinse the head under hot water, but the manual says that reduces blade life. Also, this thing is chunky — 5.8 ounces and taller than a Series 7. It's not a travel shaver unless you're checking a bag.

The 10-hour full charge time is the real annoyance. If you drain it completely, you're waiting overnight. The 5-minute quick charge saves you, but it's still a design choice that feels dated in 2026.

**Who should buy this**

- **Sensitive skin sufferers**: If you get razor burn or red bumps, the skin guard and vibration make this worth every penny.
- **Thick-bearded guys**: The hyper-lift blade handles coarse hair better than any foil I've tested under $400.
- **Wet-shavers**: The sealed head means you can use it with foam in the shower without worry.

**Skip it if** you shave once a week or less — the Series 7 at half the price will do the job. Or if you're on a budget and can't stomach the cartridge costs.

**FAQ**

**Is the Series 9 actually better than the Series 7?**  
Yes, for one reason: the hyper-lift blade. It cuts flat-lying neck hairs that the Series 7's two-foil design misses. If you have a standard beard, the Series 7 is fine. If you have tricky neck hair, the 9 is worth the upgrade.

**Can you use it without the cleaning station?**  
Yes. Rinse the head under hot water, shake it dry, and let it air out. The blades will dull faster (maybe 12–14 months instead of 18), but you'll save $100+/year.

**Does it work on a 5-day beard?**  
It'll handle it, but expect 4–5 passes and some tugging. This is a daily shaver, not a beard trimmer. For long stubble, get a trimmer first.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if:** You shave daily, you've struggled with irritation, and you're willing to pay for a 4–5 year investment in your face.  
**Skip it if:** You're a casual shaver or you refuse to pay recurring cartridge costs.

The Braun Series 9 is the best foil shaver I've tested for sensitive skin. It's not perfect — the charging speed and cartridge pricing are real drawbacks — but for its intended user, it's a 4.6/5 product that delivers on its premium price.
