If you do your own acrylics at home, you already know the pain of cheap nail files that go dull after one set. The 80/80 Grit Nail Files (25-pack) are built to handle serious material removal — not just light shaping. These are the workhorse files you reach for when you're grinding down thick acrylic overlays, not the delicate ones you use for a quick buff.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains 25 individual emery boards, each measuring 7.5 inches long. That's a full-size file — not those stubby travel versions. The 80/80 grit designation means both sides are coarse, which is exactly what you want for acrylic and hard gel. Each board has a sturdy 2.5mm paperboard core wrapped in emery material that runs edge to edge, so you get maximum usable surface area.

## Real-World Performance

I tested these on a fresh set of acrylics that needed significant shaping, and the difference was immediate. The 80-grit surface grabs material aggressively — you can feel it cutting with each pass rather than just skimming the surface. One buyer notes, "I was genuinely surprised how fast these worked on my acrylics. My old files would slide and leave shiny spots, but these actually bite."

The key thing here is durability. Cheap emery boards lose their grit after a few uses, but these held up across multiple sets. The emery material doesn't separate from the core easily, which is a common failure point on budget files. That said, they're still disposable — which is the point. At this price, you're meant to use them a few times and toss them.

## Where This Product Falls Short

Let's be straight about the tradeoffs. First, 80/80 grit is aggressive — too aggressive for natural nails. If you're using these on your bare nail plate, you'll over-file fast. You need to pair these with a fine-grit buffer for finishing. Second, the paperboard core doesn't love moisture. If you're using acetone or soaking off products, keep these away from the liquid or they'll soften and warp. One reviewer mentions, "They work great until they get wet — then they're garbage. Just don't let them touch your acetone."

## Who Should Buy These

- **DIY acrylic users** who shape and file their own sets at home and need reliable, coarse files in bulk
- **Nail techs** who go through files quickly and want a cost-effective option for prep work
- **Anyone who does hard gel sculpting** and needs serious grit to cut through tough materials

Skip these if you only do natural nail care or light buffing — you'll find the 80-grit too harsh and you'll burn through your nail plate. You want a finer grit for that.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does one file last?**
A: With acrylic work, expect 3-5 full manicures per file before the grit noticeably dulls. For heavy shaping, maybe 2-3 uses. That's normal for this grit level.

**Q: Are these safe for natural nails?**
A: No. At 80 grit, these are for artificial nails only. They'll thin and damage natural nails if applied with pressure. Use a 180-grit or finer for natural nails.

**Q: Can I sanitize and reuse these?**
A: You can wipe them with alcohol between clients, but don't soak them. The paperboard core absorbs moisture and will lose structural integrity. Heat sterilization will also ruin the adhesive bond.
