**Real-World Performance**

I tested the 18-inch bundles against my usual brand, and the differences showed up fast. The cuticles run in one direction — confirmed by running my fingers down the shaft and feeling zero snags. That means less tangling at the nape and crown, even after sleeping on satin-less pillowcases.

Washing matters more than any other factor with human hair. After wash number one with sulfate-free shampoo, the hair retained its bounce without that weird straw texture cheap bundles develop. By wash three, I noticed minimal color fade — it stayed true to the natural black shade rather than pulling brassy or reddish.

One buyer put it plainly: *"This is the second time I've ordered these bundles. They blend seamlessly with my natural 4C texture after a light press, and I've gotten six weeks of wear without major shedding."* Another noted: *"I've worn these for a full month, washing twice weekly, and the wefts are still intact — no major matting at the base."*

**Where It Falls Short**

The shedding issue is real, just not universal. Some reviewers with coarse, kinky textures reported visible shedding at the weft after week four. That's a 50-50 gamble depending on your hair type and how often you manipulate the install.

The smell is the other complaint I can't dismiss. The hair carries a chemical processing odor — think hair dye left in the box too long. It dissipates after a couple of days with airing and a light conditioning treatment, but if you're installing immediately after opening, that scent will be noticeable.

And be clear on what you're buying: this is bundles only. No closure, no frontal. Factor another $40-80 if you need one for a complete sew-in.
