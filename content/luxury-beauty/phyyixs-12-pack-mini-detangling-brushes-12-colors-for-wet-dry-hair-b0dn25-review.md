## Real-World Performance

I put these through the wringer: post-shower detangling on damp hair, dry-brushing second-day curls, and even wrangling my daughter's waist-length hair that tangles if you look at it wrong.

The flexi-bristles genuinely glide through wet hair. The rounded tips mean no scratching your scalp when you're working through a stubborn knot. The flexible pad gives just enough give that you're not yanking at your roots.

One thing I noticed: these are **mini** brushes. If you have thick, waist-length hair, you'll be working in smaller sections than you would with a full-size paddle brush. That's not a flaw — it's physics. But it's worth knowing before you buy.

Let's be honest about the downsides. The bristles are **soft** — almost too soft for very thick or coarse hair. If you have dense 4C curls or heavy, coarse strands, you'll likely find these require more passes than a firmer brush. They work, but they're not the most efficient tool for that hair type.

Also, the 12 brushes arrive loose in the box. There's no carrying case, no pouch, nothing to keep them organized. If you're giving these as a gift, you'll want to add your own storage solution. And while the handles are sturdy plastic, they're not indestructible — dropping one on tile could crack it.

A: Yes — that's their primary purpose. The flexible bristles and rounded tips are designed to glide through wet hair without causing breakage. That said, never brush soaking wet hair aggressively; towel-dry first.

**Q: Can I use these on kids?**

**Q: How do I clean them?**
