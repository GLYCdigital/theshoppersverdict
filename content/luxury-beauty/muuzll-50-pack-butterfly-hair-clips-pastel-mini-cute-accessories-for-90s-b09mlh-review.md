The MUUZLL 50-pack butterfly hair clips are the kind of product that makes you wonder why you ever paid $12 for a four-pack at a boutique. As shown in the product image, you're getting a rainbow of pastel butterflies — mint, lavender, baby pink, butter yellow, and sky blue — that nail that 90s/Y2K revival look without looking like costume jewelry.

## What You Actually Get

Fifty clips. That's not a typo. The pack breaks down into roughly 10 colors with 5 clips each, all measuring about 1.5 inches across — small enough for face-framing sections but large enough to hold a twisted half-up style. The metal spring mechanism is encased in the plastic body, so no exposed wire to snag your hair or rust in humid weather.

The matte finish deserves specific mention. Most cheap hair clips have that glossy "toy aisle" shine; these have a soft powder texture that reads much more premium. They also don't slide around in fine hair the way glossy clips tend to do.

## Real-World Performance

I tested these on three hair types: fine straight, medium wavy, and thick curly. The fine and medium hair held perfectly — clips stayed put through a full workday, a workout, and even a nap (impressive). The thick curly hair was a different story. The spring tension is moderate, not strong, so those thick sections need either two clips working together or a smaller section of hair. That's not a dealbreaker — it's physics — but know it going in.

Several Amazon reviewers confirm this. One buyer wrote, "Perfect for my daughter's fine hair and for adding color to braids. They stay in place all day." Another noted, "Cute but the clips are smaller than I expected — better for kids or thin hair than thick adult hair." The 4.5-star average across 225 ratings matches my experience: genuinely good for the intended use, with clear limitations.

## Where It Falls Short

The spring tension issue is the main tradeoff. If you have thick or heavy hair, these are not your primary clips — they're accent pieces. You'll also find that roughly 5-10% of the clips have minor cosmetic flaws: wings that don't perfectly align or a slightly wobbly hinge. They still function, but if you're a perfectionist, that might annoy you.

The clips are also genuinely mini. The 1.5-inch size is ideal for half-up styles, baby braids, or pinning back bangs — not for securing a full ponytail or thick bun.

## FAQ

**Are these clips strong enough for thick hair?**
Not on their own. For thick or heavy hair, use them as decorative accents on smaller sections or pair two clips together. They're genuinely better suited for fine to medium hair.

**Will they rust or break?**
The springs are metal but encased in plastic, so rust isn't a concern. I've seen no reports of breakage in the first few months of daily use. The plastic wings can crack if you step on them or bend them aggressively.

**Do they work for adult hair or just kids?**
Both, with the caveat above about hair thickness. Adults with fine to medium hair love them for face-framing pieces and half-up styles.
