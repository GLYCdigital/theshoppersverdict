## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the flaking issue is real if you over-apply. This is a gel with actual hold — it's not a leave-in conditioner. Use more than a quarter-size amount and you'll see white flecks once it dries. Second, the fragrance is stronger than most curl products. It's a floral-citrus scent that's pleasant but persistent. If you're sensitive to fragrance or prefer unscented hair care, this might not be your match.

Finally, if your hair is fine or loose waves (2A-2B), this gel might be too heavy. It's optimized for defined curls and coils — the hold that works beautifully on 3A hair can weigh down looser waves.
