Let's be honest about what "moisture" means in a conditioner. Most brands slap the word on a bottle and call it a day. MONDAY Haircare's MOISTURE Conditioner with Hyaluronic Acid actually delivers — but with a few caveats worth knowing before you add it to your cart.

This is the pink bottle in MONDAY's lineup, formulated with hyaluronic acid, coconut oil, and shea butter. At 4.7 stars from 235 Amazon ratings, it's clearly working for most people. Here's the full breakdown.

**What you actually get**

One 16.9 fl oz (500 mL) bottle of conditioner in a recyclable pink plastic container with a pump. The pump is a genuinely thoughtful touch — you can dispense exactly the amount you need without the flailing squeeze-bottle dance. The formula itself is thick but spreadable, with a sweet, candy-like scent that's distinctly MONDAY.

**How it performs**

The star ingredient is hyaluronic acid — the same humectant that's taken over skincare. It draws moisture into the hair shaft rather than just coating the outside. In practice, that means hydration that lasts beyond wash day. One reviewer noted, "My ends stayed soft for three days after using this. I usually need to re-wet and re-apply something by day two."

The coconut oil and shea butter are present but not dominant. That's deliberate — it keeps the formula from turning into a heavy mask. Fine hair won't get greasy, and wavy or curly textures get definition without crunch. For thick or coarse hair, you may need more product than you'd expect; the formula is lightweight by design.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The pump dispenser gets repeated shout-outs. "Finally a conditioner that doesn't make me fight the bottle in the shower," one reviewer wrote. Another praised the value: "A little goes a long way — one pump covers my shoulder-length hair completely."

Multiple reviewers with color-treated hair reported the formula didn't strip their color or cause brassiness. The hyaluronic acid seems to genuinely help with the dry, brittle feeling that follows bleaching.

**Where it falls short**

The fragrance is the biggest complaint — it's strong and persistent. If you're sensitive to scents or prefer unscented haircare, this will be a problem. One reviewer mentioned, "The smell is nice but it stays in my hair for hours. I could smell it while I was at work."

It's also not a rescue treatment. Severely damaged hair — think over-bleached, heat-fried, or chemically processed — will need a weekly deep conditioning mask in addition to this. The lightweight formula simply isn't dense enough for that level of repair.

Finally, the pump can jam in cold bathrooms. The conditioner thickens when cool, and the mechanism struggles. Store it upright and give the bottle a shake before use to avoid frustration.

**Who should buy this**

- **Fine to medium hair types** looking for daily moisture without buildup
- **Color-treated hair** that needs hydration without stripping pigment
- **Wavy or loose curly textures** wanting softness and definition
- **People who hate squeeze bottles** — the pump is genuinely better

**Skip it if** you have very coarse or extremely damaged hair (you need a heavier formula), or if strong fragrance bothers you.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this safe for color-treated hair?**
A: Yes. Multiple reviewers with dyed or bleached hair reported no color fade or brassiness. The formula is sulfate-free and uses humectants rather than harsh cleansers.

**Q: How often should I use it?**
A: Every wash is fine for most hair types. If you have fine hair, you might alternate with a lighter conditioner every other wash to avoid any buildup.

**Q: Does it help with frizz?**
A: Yes, particularly in humid conditions. The hyaluronic acid balances moisture levels, which reduces the frizz that comes from dehydrated hair. One reviewer noted, "My hair was noticeably smoother on humid days after switching to this."

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you have normal-to-dry hair that needs dependable daily hydration, especially if you color-treat or have wavy textures. The hyaluronic acid formula delivers real results, the pump is practical, and the price point is fair for the quality.

**Skip it if** you need deep repair for severely damaged hair, or if you're sensitive to strong fragrance.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A solid, well-formulated daily conditioner that earns its high rating, held back only by the heavy scent and lightweight formula that won't fix serious damage.
