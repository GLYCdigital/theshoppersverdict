## What You Actually Get

The jar arrives in amika's signature sleek, minimalist packaging — a 3.4 oz purple container that looks premium on any bathroom shelf. Inside is a thick, balm-like cream that feels rich but not heavy when you work it through damp hair. The texture is closer to a lightweight hair mask than a traditional leave-in conditioner. You apply it before bed, sleep with it in, and rinse in the morning.

## Performance and Real-World Results

The star ingredient here is amika's bond-building technology, which targets broken disulfide bonds in the hair shaft — the same bonds that heat styling, bleaching, and chemical treatments destroy. Unlike Olaplex's single-molecule approach, this formula layers in sea buckthorn oil and a blend of amino acids to both repair and moisturize.

My experience after the first use: hair felt noticeably softer and looked shinier the next morning. After a week of nightly application (I used it every other night), my split ends looked less frazzled, and my hair had a bouncier, healthier texture. It's not a miracle worker — hair that's severely damaged won't be fully restored — but the improvement was real and visible.

The formula absorbs well. It doesn't leave a greasy residue on your pillowcase, and it doesn't weigh down medium-to-thick hair. The heat-activated aspect means it works even better if you blow-dry in the morning, which activates the bond-repairing ingredients further.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The scent is the biggest complaint across reviews. It's a strong, sweet, floral-amber fragrance that lingers on your hair well into the next day. Some people love it; others find it cloying. If you're sensitive to fragrance, this is a legitimate concern.

The price is another issue. At roughly $30 for 3.4 ounces, it's a luxury purchase. You can find cheaper bond-repair options, though few match this formula's combination of repair and moisture.

Finally, if you have very fine or thin hair, this might be too heavy. The balm-like texture can weigh down limp strands if you overapply. Start with a pea-sized amount and increase only if needed.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have medium-to-thick, color-treated or heat-styled hair that needs consistent repair. You're willing to pay for a premium product that delivers visible results. You don't mind a strong fragrance.

**Skip it if:** You have very fine hair that weighs down easily, you're on a tight budget, or you're sensitive to heavily scented products.

## FAQ

**How often should I use it?**
For damaged hair, 2-3 times per week is ideal. Once your hair improves, once a week maintains the results. Overuse won't damage hair, but it can lead to product buildup.

**Is it safe for color-treated hair?**
Yes — it's sulfate-free and color-safe. The bond-repairing technology actually helps extend the life of your color by strengthening the hair shaft.

**Can I use it on dry hair?**
Technically yes, but it's designed for damp hair. On dry hair, the formula doesn't penetrate as effectively and may leave a heavier residue.
