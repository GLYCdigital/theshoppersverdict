Let's be honest about eye primers: most are either too greasy to hold shadow or so drying they crease your lids by lunch. The Smashbox Photo Finish Intensify 24 Hour EyeShadow Primer sits in a rare middle ground, and after testing it across humid afternoons, sweaty gym sessions, and one very long wedding day, I understand why it holds a 4.5-star rating from 225 Amazon reviewers.

This is the "Nourishing" variant of Smashbox's intensifying primer line, meaning it swaps the original's mattifying focus for a vitamin-infused formula (E and B5) designed to prevent that tight, cracked feeling you get with other long-wear primers. The texture is a lightweight cream that blends to a soft, velvety finish—not sticky, not slippery. A pea-sized amount covers both lids, and the sheer neutral tint does real work neutralizing redness and veining before shadow ever touches skin.

**The 24-hour claim is more than marketing.** I applied a matte shadow at 7 AM, hit the gym at 6 PM, and the shadow hadn't budged or creased by midnight. Oily-lid reviewers echo this: "I have the oiliest eyelids on earth and this is the only primer that keeps shadow in place past hour five," one verified buyer notes. Another says, "My hooded eyes crease everything—this held my crease shadow for 14 hours straight." That's the consistency across feedback: this primer performs where others fail.

**But it's not perfect.** The formula sets quickly—within 30–45 seconds—so you must blend shadow on immediately after application. Hesitate, and you'll feel the drag. The 0.3 oz tube is also smaller than competitors (Urban Decay's Primer Potion is 0.33 oz, Too Faced's Shadow Insurance is 0.35 oz), and daily users report repurchasing every two months. At this price point, that's a real cost consideration.

**Where it genuinely excels:** the nourishing angle isn't gimmick. After eight hours, my lids didn't feel parched or tight—a first for a 24-hour primer. The finish also plays well with both powder and cream shadows, and it doesn't pill under glitter formulas. One reviewer sums it up: "Finally a primer that doesn't make my lids feel like cardboard by 3 PM."

**Who should buy this:** If you have dry-to-normal lids, hooded eyes, or routinely need shadow to survive 12+ hour days, this is your primer. It's also excellent for mature skin where drying primers accentuate fine lines. If you have extremely oily lids, the original Smashbox 24 Hour (not the nourishing version) may serve you better—though several oily-lid reviewers report success with this one, just with a light dusting of powder over the primer before shadow.

**Who should skip it:** If you're on a budget and only need a primer for occasional nights out, cheaper drugstore options (Milani, e.l.f.) deliver 80% of the performance for a fraction of the cost. Also skip if you prefer a tacky, grippy base—this one's smooth finish doesn't grab loose pigments as aggressively.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

*Does it work without eyeshadow?* Yes—worn alone, it evens out lid tone and keeps concealer from creasing. Several reviewers use it as a base for concealer on bare lids.

*Is it waterproof?* Not marketed as waterproof, but it survives sweat and tears reasonably well. One reviewer wore it through a humid outdoor wedding and reported "zero melting."

*Does it work with cream shadows?* Yes, but apply cream shadow immediately after the primer sets—don't wait more than a minute.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need serious all-day wear, hate the dry feeling of long-wear primers, or have problematic hooded or oily lids that defeat other options. **Skip it if:** You're a casual makeup user who won't finish a tube before it expires, or you're strictly budget-limited.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Premium performance, genuine 24-hour staying power, and a nourishing formula that respects your skin. The small tube and fast-setting texture cost it a perfect score, but for anyone who's fought creased shadow for years, this is worth every penny.
