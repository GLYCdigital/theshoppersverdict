**The Camila Paris AD66/2 French Side Combs: Big, Beautiful, and Almost Perfect**

If you've ever wrestled with flimsy plastic combs that snap under pressure or bend awkwardly mid-twist, the Camila Paris AD66/2 set is the upgrade you've been looking for. These aren't the dollar-store variety — they're substantial, curved tools that actually hold your hair in place. At roughly 4 inches long, they're noticeably larger than standard side combs, and that size makes a real difference when you're working with thick or lengthy hair.

**What You Get**

The 2-pack includes two identical tortoise-shell combs with a glossy, polished finish. The acetate material is dense and weighty — these feel like quality tools in your hand, not disposable accessories. The curved shape is designed to follow the natural contour of your skull, which is what allows them to grip and hold French twists, chignons, and side-swept styles without constant readjustment. As shown in the product image, the teeth are widely spaced and smoothly rounded at the tips, which minimizes pulling and breakage.

**Real-World Performance**

I tested these on both thick, wavy hair and fine, straight hair. On the thick hair, the combs held a French twist securely for a full workday with only minor slippage after several hours. The smooth teeth glide through without catching, and the curved back sits flush against the head — no awkward bulges under a scarf or hat. For fine hair, the experience was slightly different. The polished finish is so slick that the comb tends to migrate out of position, especially in freshly washed hair that lacks natural grip. A quick spritz of texturizing spray solves this, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

**What Buyers Praise**

Customer feedback at 4.0 stars out of 200 ratings reflects a product that mostly delivers. One reviewer noted, "These are the first combs that actually stay in my thick hair all day. The curve makes all the difference." Another mentioned the aesthetic appeal: "They look far more expensive than they are — the tortoise shell pattern is beautiful and matches everything." Durability also earns consistent praise, with several buyers reporting months of daily use without cracking or losing their shape.

**Where It Falls Short**

The slick finish is the most common complaint — one reviewer wrote, "Beautiful combs but they slide right out of my fine hair unless I use product." The large size is also a factor for some: at 4 inches, these can overwhelm petite features or short bob-length hair. And while the acetate is sturdy, it's not indestructible — dropping one on tile flooring can cause surface scratches, so treat them with care.

**Who Should Buy These**

These combs are ideal for anyone with medium to thick hair who regularly styles updos or side-swept looks. They're also a smart pick for brides or bridesmaids who want elegant, functional accessories for a special occasion. If you have very fine hair, very short hair, or simply prefer smaller tools, consider the standard-size version instead.

**FAQ**

**Will these work for a French twist?**
Yes, the curve and length are specifically designed for this style. They grip the rolled hair securely, though fine hair may need a texturizing product for extra hold.

**Are they comfortable to wear for hours?**
Generally, yes. The smooth edges don't dig into the scalp. Some users with sensitive scalps report mild pressure after 6+ hours, but most find them comfortable.

**Can I use them on wet hair?**
You can, but the acetate may become slippery. They're better suited for dry, styled hair.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You have thick or medium hair, love polished vintage-inspired accessories, and want combs that actually stay put through a full day.

**Skip it if:** Your hair is very fine, you prefer minimal tools, or you're looking for something subtle — these make a statement.
