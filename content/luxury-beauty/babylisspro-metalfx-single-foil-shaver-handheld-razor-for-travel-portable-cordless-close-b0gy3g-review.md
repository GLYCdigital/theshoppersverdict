# BaBylissPRO METALFX Single Foil Shaver Review: Luxury Price, Barber-Grade Results?

The BaBylissPRO METALFX Single Foil Shaver is not your drugstore electric razor. At a premium price point, it promises the kind of shave you'd expect from a $40 barbershop visit—without the mirror, the towel, or the small talk. But does a single foil actually deliver? After digging through 200 ratings and testing the engineering specs, here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the shaver itself, a USB-C charging cable, a protective cap, and a cleaning brush. No charging stand, no travel pouch, no blade replacements included—that's an extra $20-25 when you need them (roughly every 6-8 months with daily use). The metal body is reassuringly solid, not the hollow plastic you'd expect at this price point's cheaper competitors. It weighs 5.2 ounces, which feels substantial but not fatiguing.

## Real-World Shaving Performance

The headline feature is the titanium-coated foil, and it earns its place. On 1-2 day growth, this thing gets *close*—closer than most foil shavers I've tested at half the price. The single foil head flexes independently, which helps track jawlines and under-chin contours without pressing hard. You'll still need to go over trouble spots twice, but the result is smooth enough that you could skip the post-shave blade pass on non-important days.

The cordless runtime is genuinely impressive: 60+ minutes per charge. That's over a week of daily shaves for most guys, and the USB-C charging means you can top up from a laptop or power bank on the road. It charges in about 90 minutes flat.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The close shave is the recurring theme in positive reviews. One buyer wrote, *"First foil shaver that actually gets my neck smooth without razor burn. I've used Braun and Panasonic—this beats them on closeness."* Another praised the build quality: *"Feels like a professional tool, not a toy. The metal body makes a difference."* Travelers specifically call out the size and USB-C charging as game-changers for carry-on grooming.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be straight about the downsides. The single foil is slower—plan 8-10 minutes for a full face versus 5-6 with a multi-head razor. If you're a "shave in 3 minutes before work" guy, this will frustrate you. The motor is also louder than average; it has a noticeable whine that's fine in a bathroom but obnoxious in a hotel room at 6 AM. Some users report hand fatigue from the vibration during longer sessions. And there's no pop-up trimmer, so sideburns and mustache edges require a separate tool.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a travel-heavy professional who wants a close shave without dragging a full razor kit. You value build quality over shave speed. You have normal-to-coarse facial hair and don't need daily BBS-level smoothness.

**Skip it if:** You have a heavy beard that needs multiple passes daily, you're on a tight budget, or you want an all-in-one groomer. The METALFX is a specialist tool, not a multitasker.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work on long hair (3+ days growth)?**
A: No. Foil shavers need short stubble. Trim first if you're past 3 days—this will pull and tug on longer hair.

**Q: Can you use it wet with shaving cream?**
A: Technically yes, but it's not designed for it. The foil clogs quickly, and you'll get a better result dry. Stick to dry shaving.

**Q: How long do replacement foils last?**
A: With daily use, expect 6-8 months. You'll notice a drop in closeness when it's time to swap.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The BaBylissPRO METALFX is a premium tool that delivers where it counts: shave quality. The titanium foil genuinely outperforms competitors at this price, and the cordless USB-C design is a travel winner. But the single foil's slower pace and loud motor are real tradeoffs. If you prioritize closeness and build quality over speed, this is worth the investment. If you need a quick, versatile groomer, look elsewhere.

*Buy it if you want a professional-grade close shave in a travel-friendly package. Skip it if you're a speed-shave minimalist or need multi-function grooming.*
