# Braun Series 7 70 Shaver: The $200 Question

Most electric shavers under $150 make you choose between closeness and comfort. The Braun Series 7 70 tries to give you both — and mostly succeeds. But at this price point, "mostly" needs scrutiny.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the shaver itself, a smart cleaning dock, a charging stand, and a travel pouch. Notably absent: the cleaning cartridge refill, which you'll need to reorder every 4–6 weeks if you use the dock's cleaning cycle regularly. That's an ongoing cost many shoppers overlook.

The unit itself is brushed stainless steel with a rubberized grip. It's substantial in hand — 6.1 inches and about 7 ounces — and feels premium, not plasticky like cheaper Braun models.

## Real-World Performance

The headline feature is SonicTech: 10,000 micro-vibrations per minute that lift hair before the foil cuts it. In practice, this means the shaver does the work — you don't press. Glide it over your face and the floating head follows contours without digging in.

The AutoSense system reads hair density 13 times per second and adjusts power. On a 3-day beard, it kicks into high gear. On daily stubble, it dials back. This works well, but it's not magic — thick, coarse beards still need multiple passes. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"It's the best electric shave I've had, but it's not a blade. I still need 2–3 passes on my jawline."*

Battery life lands at 50 minutes — about 2 weeks of daily shaves. The 5-minute quick charge gives you one full shave, which is genuinely useful for travel. Charging takes a full hour.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The waterproof design gets frequent mention. You can shave in the shower, and cleaning is simply rinsing under the tap. The 4.3-star average across 215 ratings reflects genuine satisfaction, not hype.

Another common theme: the shaver is *quiet*. Compared to the whine of older Braun models, this runs at a muted hum that won't wake your partner or annoy you in a hotel room.

## Where It Falls Short

The foil is the weak point. It's thin and requires a light touch — press too hard and you'll get razor burn on the neck, especially if you have sensitive skin. The floating head mitigates this, but it takes a few shaves to retrain your muscle memory if you're coming from a cartridge razor.

The cleaning station, while convenient, is effectively mandatory if you want the shaver to perform at its best over time. Without it, manual rinsing works, but the foil clogs faster and the shave quality degrades within a few weeks. That's an added cost and a countertop space commitment.

Finally, the price. This sits firmly in premium territory. If you're a casual shaver who uses an electric razor twice a week, you're paying for features you won't fully use.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You shave daily or every other day, have normal-to-coarse facial hair, and want the closest electric shave possible without going full wet-shave with a blade. Also ideal for frequent travelers who need the quick charge and waterproof design for shower shaves.

**Skip it if:** You have very sensitive skin that reacts to any foil shaver, you're on a budget, or you prefer the flexibility of a rotary shaver for longer facial hair. Also skip if you won't commit to using the cleaning station — you're leaving performance on the table.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this with shaving cream or gel?**
A: Yes. It's fully waterproof and works wet or dry. Using it wet with gel actually reduces irritation and produces a closer shave on sensitive areas.

**Q: How often do I need to replace the foil and cutter?**
A: Braun recommends every 18 months. At roughly $40–$50 for the replacement combo, that's a reasonable cost for maintaining performance.

**Q: Does it work on a full beard or long stubble?**
A: No. This is designed for daily to 3-day growth. For anything longer, trim first with a beard trimmer — the foil will clog and pull on longer hair.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The Braun Series 7 70 is a genuine step up from mid-range electric shavers. The SonicTech vibration system, AutoSense power adjustment, and waterproof design combine for a shave that's close, fast, and relatively comfortable. The build quality is excellent, and the 50-minute battery covers most users' needs.

But it's not perfect. The thin foil demands a careful touch, the cleaning station is an effective money-grab if you factor in cartridge refills, and the battery life trails competitors. At this price, you're paying for refinement, not revolution.

**Buy it if** you want one of the best foil shavers available and will use it daily. **Skip it if** you're a casual shaver or expect blade-level closeness without compromise. It's a strong 4-star product that earns its rating — with a few asterisks.
