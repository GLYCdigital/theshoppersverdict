The curl market is crowded with products that promise definition and deliver crunch, or promise moisture and deliver grease. Creme of Nature's Pure Honey and Coconut Oil Curl Activator sits in a rare middle ground—it actually does what the label claims. With 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, this isn't a viral TikTok hype product; it's a steady performer with a loyal following. Here's the real breakdown.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The jar contains a thick, butter-like cream that melts on contact with body heat. As shown in the product image, the texture is more whipped than gel-like—this matters because it means the product isn't water-based. The formula leads with pure honey (a humectant) and coconut oil (an emollient), backed by shea butter for sealing. No sulfates, no parabens, and importantly, no mineral oil or petroleum, which many curl products rely on for shine at the cost of real moisture.

**Performance and Build Quality**

I tested this on freshly washed, soaking-wet hair (the recommended method) and on damp, day-old curls. On wet hair, it distributed evenly without clumping or leaving white patches—a common issue with honey-based products. Curls formed defined spirals with a soft, touchable finish. The cast is minimal, which is good for those who hate crunch but bad for anyone expecting gel-level hold.

The real test came at hour 30. Curls held their shape, and the frizz halo that usually appears by day two on my 3B hair was noticeably reduced. The coconut oil does the heavy lifting here—it seals the cuticle without weighing hair down. That said, the hold is light-to-medium. In humid conditions (think summer in the South), expect some droop by mid-day.

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

The pattern across reviews is consistent. One buyer noted, "My 4C hair has never looked this defined without a gel. I just scrunch and go." Another praised the ingredient list: "Finally a product that doesn't have that weird plasticky smell. This actually smells like coconut oil, not a candle." The honey factor gets specific attention—one reviewer said it "tamed my edges without flaking," which is a problem with many curl activators that dry stiff.

**Where It Falls Short**

The hold is the main tradeoff. If you sleep on your curls or live somewhere humid, this won't survive a full day without a refresher or a stronger hold product on top. The jar packaging is also a minor annoyance—you're digging product out with your fingers, and the cream's thickness means you'll likely use more than you need at first. It's also not ideal for travel; the jar is glass, which adds weight and breakage risk.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The wash-and-go minimalist**: If you want one product that moisturizes, defines, and seals without layering three things, this is it.
- **The fragrance-sensitive**: The scent is mild and natural. If you hate strong perfumes in hair products, this works.
- **The low-porosity curler**: Honey and coconut oil in this ratio absorb well without sitting on the strand. High-porosity hair may need a heavier sealer on top.

**Skip it if** you need all-day hold in extreme humidity or prefer gel-based products with a hard cast. Also skip if you hate jar packaging—this won't change your mind.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this work on relaxed or transitioning hair?**
A: Yes, but use less. Start with a dime-sized amount on damp hair. The honey can over-moisturize fine, relaxed strands, so build up gradually.

**Q: How much should I use for shoulder-length hair?**
A: About a quarter-sized amount, worked through in sections. The cream spreads far—most users over-apply on the first try and end up with greasy roots.

**Q: Can I use this as a leave-in conditioner?**
A: It's thicker than a leave-in. It works as one in a pinch, but you'll get better results using it as a styler on damp hair, then sealing with a lightweight oil.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want defined, moisturized curls without the crunch, and you're willing to accept a light-to-medium hold. It's particularly strong for wash-and-go routines and anyone with 3A–4C textures looking to simplify their product stack.

**Skip it if** you need maximum hold, live in a high-humidity climate, or prefer pump/tube packaging for hygiene or travel reasons.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A solid, honest curl activator that earns its rating. Not perfect, but for the price point and ingredient quality, it's a strong contender in a crowded category.
