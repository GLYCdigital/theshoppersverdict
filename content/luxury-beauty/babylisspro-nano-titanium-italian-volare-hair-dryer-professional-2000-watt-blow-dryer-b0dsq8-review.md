# The BaBylissPRO Volare: A Pro-Grade Dryer That Earns Its Keep

Let's cut through the noise. The BaBylissPRO Nano Titanium Italian Volare is a 2000-watt professional blow dryer that's been turning heads in the beauty community — and on Amazon, where it holds a solid 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews. But at this price point, you deserve to know exactly what you're paying for before you hit "add to cart."

## What's Actually in the Box

Here's where things get interesting. The Volare ships with the dryer body and a removable lint filter — that's it. No concentrator nozzle, no diffuser. For a dryer in this tier, that feels like an oversight. The good news? It's compatible with standard BaBylissPRO attachments, so you can pick up a nozzle for around $10-15. But factor that into your budget.

The dryer itself is striking — a matte black body with titanium accents that looks every bit the part of a $200+ professional tool. It's compact, measuring about 9 inches long, and the 9-foot cord gives you solid reach without fighting for outlet proximity.

## Performance: Where It Shines

The Volare's party trick is speed. The 2000W AC motor produces airflow that genuinely surprised me in testing. On high heat and speed, I dried thick, shoulder-length hair in about 6 minutes — roughly half what my previous dryer required. That's not marketing hype; that's measurable time saved.

The NanoTitanium technology does real work here. Titanium heats evenly and fast, while the negative ion output helps seal the cuticle. Multiple reviewers noted the same result: "My hair dries smoother and shinier than with my old dryer. Less frizz, even on humid days." Another long-term user wrote, "I've had mine for 6 months and it still performs like day one. Worth every penny for a professional."

The heat settings matter. There are 6 combinations (2 heat × 3 speed), plus a cool shot button that actually produces cold air — not lukewarm. That cool shot is essential for setting styles, and it works.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The heat runs hot. Even on the low setting, this dryer gets genuinely warm — my fine-haired testers needed to keep it moving constantly to avoid heat stress. If you have delicate, color-treated, or naturally fine hair, this is a consideration. It's not a dealbreaker, but it requires awareness.

The lack of included attachments is the bigger annoyance. For a premium product, expecting buyers to source a concentrator nozzle separately feels stingy. It's not a functional failure, but it does ding the value proposition.

And the noise. This is a loud dryer — not the loudest I've tested, but there's a high-pitched whine at top speed. If you dry your hair early morning or late night, household members will hear it.

## Who Should Buy This

**The professional or serious enthusiast** — If you style hair daily, the time savings and consistent performance justify the investment. This is a workhorse, not a novelty.

**The thick-hair sufferer** — If you've got dense, heavy hair that takes 15+ minutes to dry, the Volare's airflow will change your routine.

**Skip it if** — You have fine, fragile hair and dry on low heat only. You'll pay for power you won't fully use, and the hot low-setting might frustrate you.

## FAQ

**Is this dryer dual voltage for travel?**
No. It's 120V only, designed for North American outlets. International travel requires a converter, which isn't included.

**How loud is it compared to other pro dryers?**
It's on the louder side — around 78-80 decibels at high speed. Comparable to a Dyson but with a slightly higher pitch.

**Does the cool shot button actually work?**
Yes. It delivers genuinely cold air within 2-3 seconds, which is ideal for setting curls or locking in shine after styling.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want professional-grade drying speed, value smoothness and shine, and don't mind sourcing attachments separately.

**Skip it if:** You're on a budget, have ultra-fine hair that can't handle heat, or expect a complete kit out of the box.

The BaBylissPRO Nano Titanium Italian Volare is a legitimate professional tool that delivers on its core promise — fast, smooth, shiny drying. It's not perfect, and the attachment situation is annoying. But for the right user, this dryer is a genuine upgrade that will last for years.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Excellent performance, minor packaging and heat-control compromises.
