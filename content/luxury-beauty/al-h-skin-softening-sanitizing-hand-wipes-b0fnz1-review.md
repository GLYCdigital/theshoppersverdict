**What You Actually Get**

The pack contains 30 individually wrapped towelettes — not a pull-and-reseal tub, but separate foil packets you can toss in a purse, gym bag, or car console. **Where It Falls Short**

The price is the elephant in the room. At roughly $3 per wipe when you do the math, this is a luxury item, not an everyday essential. You can buy a 500-count bottle of generic sanitizer for what 30 of these cost. That's a real tradeoff.
