## Where It Falls Short

The honest downsides: this case weighs 11.5 pounds empty. Packed with a full kit, you're looking at 25-30 pounds. That's fine for wheeled transit but brutal if you're navigating stairs or cobblestones.

The top tray is shallow — about 2.5 inches deep. Tall serums, dropper bottles, or full-size setting sprays won't stand upright in the top compartment. You'll need to lay them flat or keep them in the bottom well.

The latch mechanism, while secure, requires a firm press to close. It's not a one-hand operation, and I've seen reviews from users with limited hand strength struggle with it.
