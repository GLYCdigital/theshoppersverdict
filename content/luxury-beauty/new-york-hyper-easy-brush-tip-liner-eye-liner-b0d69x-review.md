Let’s be honest about eyeliner: most brush tips either drag like a dull pencil or dump too much product mid-wing. The **New York Hyper Easy Brush Tip Liner** claims to thread that needle. At 4.4 stars from 220 Amazon ratings, it’s clearly doing something right. But is it *your* next everyday liner, or just another hyped tube? After digging through the specs and real buyer experiences, here’s the breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The packaging is sleek — a slim, matte-black tube that won’t roll off your vanity. Inside is a 0.4 fl oz (12 ml) reservoir of liquid liner. That’s slightly smaller than the typical 0.5 oz drugstore standard, which matters for the price-per-use math. The applicator is the star: a tapered brush tip with individual bristles, not a felt sponge. This distinction is huge — felt tips dry out and fray, while brush tips hold their shape and deliver more control.

## Performance and Build Quality

The pigment is genuinely black — not charcoal, not faded gray. It hits that deep, ink-like saturation that makes winged liner pop. The formula is buildable, so you can layer for intensity without clumping or flaking. Drying time is about 20-30 seconds, which is fast enough to avoid smudging but gives you a brief window to correct mistakes.

The brush bristles are firm enough to give you control but flexible enough to glide smoothly. The 0.01mm tip lets you draw hair-thin lines at the inner corner and then thicken the wing with a second pass. Several reviewers noted this precision: *“I have shaky hands and this liner is forgiving. The tip doesn’t skip or tug, even over eyeshadow.”*

Wear time is solidly 10-12 hours on most skin types. It holds through workdays and gym sessions without migrating into your crease or crumbling. The formula is also impressively smudge-resistant once dry — you can rub your eyes lightly without disaster.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The precision is the recurring theme in positive reviews. Users with hooded eyes, mature lids, and oily skin all report that this liner stays put. One reviewer wrote: *“I’ve tried every high-end liner from Tom Ford to Charlotte Tilbury. This performs as well as liners four times the price. The tip is perfection.”* Another noted: *“The brush doesn’t dry out between uses like felt tips do. Three months in, it’s still sharp.”*

## Where It Falls Short

Honest tradeoffs exist. First, this is **not** waterproof. It’s water-resistant — sweat and light rain are fine — but a crying session or swimming will cause fading around the edges. If you need a liner for events that might involve tears or pool parties, look elsewhere.

Second, the 0.4 oz bottle is on the smaller side. Daily users report needing a replacement every 2-3 months, which adds up. Some reviewers also mentioned that the pigment can look slightly gray if you don’t shake the tube before each use — the formula separates a bit in storage. It’s a minor nuisance, but worth knowing.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The precision seeker**: If you’ve struggled with unsteady hands or thick applicators, this brush tip gives you forgiveness and control.
- **The hooded-eye wearer**: The fast-dry, smudge-resistant formula means your liner won’t transfer onto your upper lid when you open your eyes.
- **The oily-lid sufferer**: This holds up where many liners crease within hours.

**Skip it if** you need a waterproof formula for extreme conditions, or if you prefer a felt-tip applicator and want maximum product volume per dollar.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this liner flake during the day?**
A: No — the film-forming formula stays flexible, not brittle. Most users report zero flaking even at the 10-hour mark.

**Q: How does it compare to the Stila Stay All Day liner?**
A: The Stila has a slightly more felt-like tip and a wider pigment range. The New York liner has a finer, more precise brush tip and comparable wear time at a lower price point.

**Q: Is this good for tightlining (lining the waterline)?**
A: Yes, the fine brush tip allows for controlled application on the lower waterline. It won’t smudge into your lower lashes the way pencils often do.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if** you want a high-precision, smudge-resistant liner that performs like a luxury product at a more accessible price. The brush tip is genuinely superior to most felt pens, and the pigment depth rivals far pricier options.

This is the liner you reach for when you have 90 seconds to get your wings sharp before running out the door — and it won’t let you down.
