Some fragrances fade into nostalgia. Wings by Giorgio Beverly Hills refuses to. This 1993 release—a chypre-floral that defined an era of shoulder pads and power suits—has quietly built a 4.7-star rating across 235 Amazon reviews. The question isn't whether it's a classic. It's whether it still earns a spot on your dresser in 2026. After testing it properly, here's my honest take.

**What's in the box**

You get the standard Giorgio Beverly Hills presentation: a 3.0 oz EDT spray in that unmistakable winged glass bottle. No box theatrics, no over-engineered cap. The bottle itself is heavy, substantial, with that angular Art Deco-inspired silhouette. The gold-tone cap twists off cleanly. It's not luxury packaging by modern standards—think 90s department store counter rather than niche boutique—but it feels solid and sits well on a vanity.

**The scent itself**

The opening is a blast of aldehydes and green notes—sharp, almost soapy, with a pronounced bergamot bite. Give it ten minutes and the heart emerges: rose, jasmine, and a surprisingly prominent carnation. The dry-down is where Wings earns its keep. Sandalwood, amber, and a whisper of musk create a warm, slightly powdery base that lingers elegantly.

This is not a subtle fragrance. It announces itself. One spray on the wrist filled a room during my testing. Two sprays was almost too much for office wear. The sillage is strong for the first two hours, then settles into a comfortable skin scent that persists for 6–8 hours on most skin types. On clothing, it easily lasts through a full workday.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Looking through the Amazon reviews, a pattern emerges. Longevity comes up repeatedly, with multiple reviewers noting they still catch whiffs 8+ hours later. One reviewer put it simply: *"Lasts all day, no need to reapply."* Another praised the value: *"Smells like a $100 fragrance, costs half that."*

The compliments factor is real too. Several reviewers mention strangers asking what they're wearing—the kind of unsolicited feedback that confirms projection.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. The opening is aggressive. If you're accustomed to sweet, gourmand, or fresh aquatic scents, the aldehydic sharpness in the first fifteen minutes can feel jarring. It takes patience to reach the beautiful dry-down.

The sprayer is genuinely mediocre. It delivers a narrow stream rather than a fine mist, which means you'll need two or three sprays to get even coverage. The winged bottle, while iconic, is also prone to tipping—the flat base is narrower than the shoulders.

And the scent profile itself leans mature. This is a fragrance for someone who knows what they like, not someone experimenting. Younger shoppers or those new to chypres may find it heavy or dated.

**Who should buy this**

Buy Wings if you're a fan of classic 90s fragrances, if you want a confident daytime scent that projects without being cloying, or if you're looking for an affordable designer option with genuine character. It's particularly suited to those who appreciate chypre structures—the green-floral-woody accord that modern perfumery has largely abandoned.

Skip it if you prefer fresh, clean, or sweet profiles, or if you need something office-safe in small spaces. This is not a quiet fragrance.

**FAQ**

**How long does Wings last on skin?**
Expect 6–8 hours on skin with the first two hours showing strong projection. On clothing, it can last through a full day. Heat and humidity extend the sillage.

**Is this a masculine or feminine fragrance?**
Officially marketed to women, but the green-woody base gives it an androgynous quality. Several male reviewers note wearing it successfully.

**Does it smell like anything currently on the market?**
The closest modern comparison is a lighter take on Paloma Picasso or an earthier version of Chanel Cristalle. It's genuinely unique today—nothing mainstream smells quite like it.

**The verdict**

Wings by Giorgio Beverly Hills is a flawed classic that still delivers. The dated packaging and sharp opening demand patience, but the dry-down is beautiful, the longevity is impressive, and the price is undeniably fair. At 4.6/5, it earns its rating—not for everyone, but for its intended audience, it's a steal.

**Buy it if** you want a confident, long-lasting chypre with real presence and don't mind a vintage character. **Skip it if** you prefer modern, sweet, or understated scents.
