## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has two real drawbacks. First, the thickness is a double-edged sword. If you have fine, straight hair, you need to rinse longer than you think — leave any residue and you'll get that "product-y" heaviness by day two. Second, the fragrance is strong. It's a warm, salon-like scent that lingers for hours, which is lovely if you like that, but if you're sensitive to fragrance or prefer unscented products, this will bother you.

Also, manage expectations: this is a cosmetic repair, not a structural one. It smooths and coats the hair shaft beautifully, but it won't rebuild broken bonds like a true protein treatment would. If your hair is severely damaged, rotate this with a protein mask.
