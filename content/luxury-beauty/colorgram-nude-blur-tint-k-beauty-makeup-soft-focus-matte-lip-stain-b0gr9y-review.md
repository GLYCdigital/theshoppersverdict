**The 3.6-star reality check: K-beauty blur tints are everywhere, but does COLORGRAM actually deliver?**

The COLORGRAM NUDE BLUR TINT sits in that crowded middle ground of K-beauty lip products — promising soft-focus matte perfection without the dryness that usually comes with it. With 180 ratings averaging 3.6 stars, this isn't a runaway hit. But the split between raves and frustrations tells a clear story: this tint is excellent for specific skin tones and habits, and genuinely wrong for others.

**What's in the tube**

You get a standard 3g/0.1 fl oz doe-foot applicator tube. The wand is angled with a precision tip, which helps for lining the cupid's bow. The formula is a mousse-like gel that applies sheer, then builds to a blurred matte finish within 30 seconds. The "nude blur" concept means the pigment is intentionally muted — these aren't bright K-pop reds. As shown in the product image, the shades read as soft, MLBB (my lips but better) tones with grayish undertones that diffuse the natural lip line.

**Performance and real-world wear**

Here's where the 3.6 rating makes sense. The blurring effect is legitimately impressive. One reviewer noted, *"It smooths the texture of my lips like a filter — no flaking, no dry patches, just a soft velvet finish."* The weight is nearly imperceptible, and it layers well over lip balm without sliding off.

But longevity is the tradeoff. Expect about 4-5 hours of even wear. After that, it fades from the inner lip first, creating that telltale "ring" effect that looks like you ate a popsicle. Touch-ups are fiddly — the product doesn't re-layer cleanly once it's set. You'll want to wipe off residue before reapplying.

**The shade problem you need to know about**

This is the most consistent complaint across buyer feedback. The current shade range is heavily skewed toward light-medium nudes. One verified purchase put it bluntly: *"I'm a medium-deep skin tone and three of the five shades look like concealer on me. The 'deep' shade isn't deep at all."* If you have deeper skin, this is likely a skip — you'll find better options from brands with more inclusive ranges.

**Build quality and packaging**

Minor durability gripe: the tube seam can leak if stored horizontally. Keep it upright and it's fine, but it's a design flaw that shouldn't exist at this price point. The cap clicks securely, and the wand doesn't bend, so the main packaging risk is user storage habits.

**Who should buy this**

- **The matte-averse minimalist**: If you've avoided matte lip products because they feel like sandpaper, this blur formula is genuinely comfortable.
- **The "my lips but better" devotee**: The muted nude shades are perfect for everyday wear, no mirror required.
- **Fair to light-medium skin tones**: This is your sweet spot. The shades are designed for you.

**Skip it if**

- **You have deeper skin**: The shade range won't serve you.
- **You need 8-hour wear**: This fades too quickly for all-day commitments without touch-up logistics.
- **You prefer glossy or satin finishes**: The matte blur effect is the entire point; if that's not your aesthetic, this won't convert you.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this dry out your lips?**
A: No — the mousse formula has a humectant base that keeps lips comfortable. It won't hydrate, but it won't crack or flake like traditional matte tints.

**Q: How many coats for full opacity?**
A: Two to three coats. The first layer is a translucent wash; building to full coverage requires layering within 60 seconds before the formula sets.

**Q: Is this transfer-proof?**
A: Mostly. It's smear-proof and survives coffee, but it will transfer slightly to masks, cups, and collars. It's not a long-wear liquid lipstick.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're fair to medium-toned, want a comfortable blur-matte for daily wear, and don't mind touch-ups. **Skip it if** you have deeper skin or need all-day longevity.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — A genuinely good formula held back by a narrow shade range and average wear time. For the right skin tone and expectations, this is a solid everyday pick. For everyone else, it's a miss.
