**The $6.70 lash that makes people ask if you got extensions**

You know the look. The one where someone leans in and says, "Wait, are those your real lashes?" That's the exact reaction the KISS Lash Couture Faux Mink 3D Matte lashes are engineered to produce. And at $6.70 a pair — versus the $150+ you'd drop on a lash extension appointment — they're the kind of affordable luxury that actually delivers.

**What's in the box**

You get one pair of lashes and a tiny tube of KISS's strip adhesive. That's it. The lashes themselves are the star: a 3D matte black fiber construction that mimics the texture of real mink lashes. The band is remarkably thin and flexible — about 0.3mm — which is the first thing I check, because a thick band is what separates "obviously fake" from "naturally enhanced." The matte finish matters more than you'd think; it kills the plastic shine that cheap lashes have, so these catch light the way natural hair does, not like a strip of vinyl.

**Performance and real-world wear**

I wore these for a 12-hour wedding day, from 8 AM setup through the last dance. No lifting at the inner corners, no drooping, no stabbing sensation at the lash line. The weight is the differentiator here — at roughly 0.02 ounces per pair, they're nearly imperceptible. You forget you're wearing them, which is exactly what you want. The 3D construction means the fibers are layered at different lengths, creating that wispy, fluttery effect that reads as "expensive" rather than "costume."

The matte finish does something subtle but important: it makes the lashes look like they're growing out of your own lash line. No shine, no glare, no "I'm wearing falsies" tell. If your makeup style is matte eyeshadow, matte skin, and you're going for that editorial, velvet-finish look — these pair perfectly.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews isn't an accident. The most repeated compliment is the natural look. One reviewer put it plainly: "I've tried every drugstore lash and these are the first ones that don't look like I'm wearing falsies. My coworkers thought I got extensions." Another noted the durability: "I've worn these five times and they're still holding their curl. No fraying, no falling apart."

The comfort factor gets consistent love too. Multiple reviewers mention forgetting they're wearing them, which is the highest compliment a lash can receive. The band flexibility seems to be the reason — it conforms to your eye shape rather than fighting it.

**Where they fall short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. The included glue is mediocre — it works, but it's not the long-wear, waterproof adhesive you'll want for all-day events. I'd recommend grabbing a dedicated lash glue like Duo or Kiss's own Strip Lash Adhesive in clear or black. That's an extra $5, which still keeps your total under $12.

The matte finish, while beautiful, isn't for everyone. If you like a glossy, dramatic, "lash extension" look with visible shine, these will disappoint. They're designed to blend in, not stand out. And while they're reusable, the 3D fibers are delicate — aggressive cleaning will shorten their lifespan. Use a gentle oil-free remover and they'll last 8-10 wears; treat them rough and you'll get half that.

**Who should buy these**

- **The natural-makeup minimalist** who wants definition without drama
- **The lash extension refugee** tired of monthly appointments and looking for a $6.70 alternative that doesn't scream "fake"
- **The wedding/bridesmaid** who needs comfortable, photograph-proof lashes that won't have you pawing at your eyes mid-ceremony
- **The budget-conscious beauty lover** who wants luxury results without the luxury markup

**Skip them if** you're after dramatic, glossy, full-glam lashes, or if you refuse to buy separate glue.

**FAQ**

**Q: How many times can you reuse these lashes?**
A: With proper care — gently peeling off glue residue and storing in the original tray — most users get 8-10 wears. Clean them after every use with oil-free makeup remover.

**Q: Do these work for hooded eyes?**
A: Yes, and this is a real advantage. The flexible band curves with your natural eye shape, so there's less of the "pushing down" problem that stiff lashes cause on hooded lids. Trim the outer edge slightly if needed.

**Q: Are they actually "faux mink" or just marketing?**
A: They're synthetic fibers engineered to replicate the softness and matte texture of real mink. No animal products, no cruelty, and honestly — the quality is comparable to premium faux mink lashes that cost three times as much.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want natural-looking, featherlight, reusable lashes that deliver a "no-makeup makeup" lash look for under $7.

**Skip it if** you need dramatic, glossy volume or want an all-in-one kit with quality glue included.
