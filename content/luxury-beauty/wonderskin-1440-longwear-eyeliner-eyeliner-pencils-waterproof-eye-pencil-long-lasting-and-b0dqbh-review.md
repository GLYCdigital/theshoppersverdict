**The 3.7-star reality check: This eyeliner nails longevity but stumbles on execution.**

You've seen the ads. "24-hour wear," "waterproof," "smudge-proof." Wonderskin's 1440 Longwear Eyeliner makes bold promises, and at this price point, you deserve to know if it delivers. After digging through 185 ratings and real user experiences, here's the unfiltered truth.

**What you actually get**

One eyeliner pencil. That's it. No sharpener included — annoying, since the pencil requires frequent sharpening. The packaging feels premium: sleek matte black with a weighted metal cap. The pencil itself has a traditional wooden casing, not the twist-up mechanism you might expect at this price.

**Performance: Where it shines**

The formula is genuinely impressive where it matters. The color payoff is intense — one pass delivers a rich, opaque line that rivals liquid liners. It glides on smoothly, no tugging or skipping, even on the tricky waterline. That's rare for a waterproof pencil.

The staying power is legit. Multiple reviewers mentioned wearing it through workouts, long shifts, and even emotional moments. One user noted, "I forgot I was wearing it and rubbed my eyes hard — it didn't move. My regular liner would've been halfway down my face."

For oily eyelids, this is a game-changer. The formula sets quickly and refuses to transfer. If you're used to pencils that smear by lunch, this will be a revelation.

**Where it falls short**

Let's talk about that "1440" — that's 24 hours, and it's marketing fluff. Most users report the liner starting to fade or crack by hour 10-12, especially on the waterline. It's still better than average, but don't expect a literal full day.

The removal process is a real chore. Standard makeup wipes won't cut it. You'll need an oil-based cleanser or balm, and even then, you might need two passes. If you're someone who falls asleep with makeup on, you'll regret it in the morning — this stuff does not come off on its own.

Sharpening is another frustration. The pencil is soft enough to break during sharpening if you're not careful, and the tip dulls quickly. You'll go through this pencil faster than you'd like, which makes the price harder to stomach.

**Who should buy this**

- **The sweaty gym-goer**: If your makeup needs to survive Spin class, this is your liner.
- **The oily-lid sufferer**: Finally, a pencil that won't migrate to your crease by noon.
- **The all-day commuter**: If you're out the door at 7 AM and home at 9 PM, this holds up better than most.

**Skip it if**: You prefer a softer, blendable smudge look (this sets too fast for that), you hate stubborn removal, or you're on a budget — there are solid drugstore waterproof options for a fraction of the price.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this actually waterproof or just water-resistant?**
It's genuinely waterproof. Multiple reviewers reported wearing it in the shower and swimming without losing the line. The waterline application held up better than any pencil I've seen tested.

**Q: Does it work for tightlining?**
Yes, and it's actually one of the better pencils for this. The creamy formula deposits pigment right at the lash line without the skipping you get from stiffer pencils. Just work quickly — it sets within about 30 seconds.

**Q: How many shades are available?**
The product listing shows several options, but the black shade gets the most positive feedback. The brown shades reportedly have less intensity and need layering for full opacity.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you prioritize staying power above all else and don't mind the extra removal effort. **Skip it if** you want versatility, easy clean-up, or value for money.

The Wonderskin 1440 Longwear Eyeliner is a specialized tool, not an everyday essential. It does one thing — long-lasting, waterproof eyeliner — exceptionally well, and compromises on everything else. At 3.7 stars, the rating reflects that tradeoff accurately. It's not a bad product; it's just a product with very specific strengths and very specific frustrations.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — Great for the right user, frustrating for everyone else.
