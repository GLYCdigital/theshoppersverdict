**What You Actually Get**

One pump bottle, 8.4 fluid ounces of translucent, gel-like liquid. The pump delivers a light, airy foam that spreads easily — no need to lather in your palms first. The formula is oil-free, hypoallergenic, and fragrance-free. No dyes, no parabens, no phthalates. It's about as no-nonsense as skincare gets.

---

**Performance & Real-World Use**

The star ingredient here is glycerin, a humectant that pulls water into your skin while you cleanse. That's why your face doesn't feel like a drumhead afterward. The foam is gentle enough for daily use — morning and night — yet it actually removes sunscreen and light makeup. Heavy waterproof mascara? You'll need a dedicated remover. That's not this product's job.

It's especially good for people with sensitive, reactive skin. The fragrance-free formula means no stinging around the eyes or on broken-out areas. A little goes a long way — one pump covers your whole face. A bottle lasts roughly two months with twice-daily use.

**Where It Falls Short**

Honest downsides? The foam is light and airy, not a thick, creamy lather. If you prefer a dense, luxurious foam, this might feel thin. It also won't remove heavy makeup on its own — you'll need a first-step cleanser or makeup remover for full-face makeup days. And while glycerin is great for hydration, if you have extremely oily skin that likes a squeaky-clean finish, you might find this too gentle. It leaves a slightly soft, moisturized feel, not a bone-dry one.
