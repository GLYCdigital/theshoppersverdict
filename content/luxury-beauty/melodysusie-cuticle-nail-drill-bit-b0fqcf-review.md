## Real-World Performance

I tested these on a mix of natural nails and gel-overlay removal. The standout is the small carbide cuticle bit. With an e-file speed around 8,000 to 10,000 RPM, it lifts and removes dead cuticle tissue cleanly in one pass — no dragging, no burning sensation. The diamond bits run hotter, so you'll want lower speeds and lighter pressure, but they leave a smooth, polished surface that makes gel polish adhere noticeably better.

One thing to note: these bits are surgical-grade, which means they're sharp. If you've never used a drill before, practice on a silicone hand first. ## FAQ

**Will this fit my MelodySusie drill?**
Yes. All MelodySusie drills use the standard 3/32-inch (2.35mm) chuck, and these bits fit that size. They also fit Makartt, LONOVE, and most generic e-files.

**How do I clean these bits?**
Remove debris with a soft brush after each use, then soak in 70% isopropyl alcohol for 10 minutes. For full sterilization between clients, use an autoclave or a hospital-grade disinfectant like Barbicide.

**How long do they last?**
With regular home use (once or twice a week), expect six months or more. Carbide bits dull faster if you push hard — let the drill do the work.
