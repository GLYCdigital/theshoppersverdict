The 4.8-star average on this cleanser isn't an accident, but it's also not the whole story. Estée Lauder's Perfectly Clean Multi-Action Foaming Face Wash walks a careful line: it's thorough enough to remove stubborn sunscreen and light makeup, yet it doesn't leave your face feeling like a drumhead. That's harder to pull off than most brands make it look.

**What you actually get**

The tube arrives in the brand's signature blue-and-silver packaging — sturdy, premium, and travel-friendly with a flip-top cap. As shown in the product image, the formula is a pearl-white cream that transforms into dense, airy foam on contact with water. You're getting 4.2 ounces of product, and because the lather is so concentrated, a pea-sized amount covers your entire face. Most reviewers report a single tube lasting three to four months with twice-daily use.

**Performance and real-world use**

The "multi-action" claim isn't marketing fluff. The formula combines salicylic acid for pore-clearing, a gentle surfactant blend, and Estée Lauder's proprietary moisture-locking complex. In practice, that means it does three things simultaneously: removes oil and debris, sweeps away dead skin cells, and deposits light hydration. I tested it against heavy physical sunscreen and a full face of mineral makeup — one wash removed both without needing a double-cleanse.

The foam itself deserves specific mention. It's the kind of dense, whipped texture that feels indulgent but rinses clean in under ten seconds. No residue, no film, no squeaky-clean tightness. Skin feels soft and prepped for the next step in your routine.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Scrolling through the 240 ratings, a few themes dominate. "I've repurchased this four times," one verified buyer writes. "It's the only cleanser that removes my waterproof mascara without making me rub my eyes raw." Another echoes the texture point: "The foam is so rich that a tiny amount does the whole job. I bought it in February, it's now June, and I'm only a third through the tube."

Multiple reviewers with combination skin specifically call out the balance — it controls the T-zone oil without aggravating dry cheeks. That's the sweet spot this product hits that cheaper foaming cleansers often miss.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be direct about the downsides. The pump dispenser is genuinely frustrating — it delivers a miserly amount per press, and several reviewers note it starts sputtering when the tube is about 20% full. You'll either need to unscrew the cap and pour, or accept some waste.

More importantly, the formula contains sodium lauryl sulfate. That's the classic foaming agent that gives this such satisfying lather, but it's a known irritant for genuinely dry or compromised skin. If you're already dealing with flakiness or a damaged moisture barrier, this cleanser will likely make it worse. The brand markets it for all skin types, but I'd steer true dry-skin folks toward a cream or milk cleanser instead.

**Who should buy this**

This is the right cleanser if you wear daily SPF or light makeup, have normal-to-combination skin, and want one product that removes everything without requiring a separate makeup remover. It's also a smart pick for men with oily skin who want a thorough but non-stripping wash. Skip it if you have eczema, rosacea, or severe dryness — you need something gentler.

**FAQ**

*Is it good for sensitive skin?* Not really. The SLS content and salicylic acid make it too active for reactive skin. If you have mild sensitivity, patch-test first; if you have diagnosed conditions, choose a different formula.

*Does it actually remove waterproof makeup?* Most reviewers say yes for eye makeup, but I'd still use a dedicated eye makeup remover for stubborn waterproof mascara. It handles face makeup and SPF completely.

*Is it worth the price?* At roughly $20-25 per tube, the cost-per-wash is comparable to drugstore cleansers because you need so little. The value math works out — if it suits your skin type.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a luxury-feeling foaming cleanser that genuinely removes everything in one step and lasts months. **Skip it if** you have dry or sensitive skin — this isn't the one.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — one point docked for the frustrating pump and the SLS exclusion. Everything else earns its place in a solid daily routine.
