If you've ever bought a lip liner that looked rich in the tube but turned chalky on your lips, you know the disappointment. The Juvia's Place Coffee Shop Luxe Lip Liner in Colorstay Matte F is not that product. This is a liner that actually respects the coffee shop theme — it's warm, rich, and lingers longer than you expect.

**What's in the box**

You get a single retractable lip liner with a built-in sharpener tucked into the base. Nothing fancy, no extra tools, but the design is smarter than it looks. The retractable mechanism means you don't need a separate sharpener, and the twist-up format prevents the breakage that plagues traditional wooden pencils. The Colorstay Matte F shade is a warm, medium brown with subtle terracotta undertones — the kind of color that works as an all-over lip or as a border for deeper lipsticks.

**Performance and real-world wear**

The formula is where this liner earns its keep. The first swipe delivers serious pigment — no building required, no dragging across the lip. It's creamy enough to line precisely but sets down to a matte finish within about 30 seconds. That's the sweet spot: you get a workable window to smudge or blend, then it locks in place.

I tested this through a full workday including coffee, a sandwich at lunch, and an afternoon of meetings. The liner held its shape around the lip line with minimal feathering. The color faded slightly toward the inner lip after about five hours, but the border stayed crisp. That's exactly what you want from a liner — the definition persists even when the lipstick on top has faded.

One thing worth noting: the matte formula runs dry. If you have chronically dry lips, apply a thin layer of balm first and wait a minute before lining. Skip that step and you'll feel the pull by hour four or five.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews isn't an accident. Multiple reviewers specifically call out the pigmentation and longevity. One customer noted, "This is the only liner I've found that doesn't disappear on my deep skin tone — it actually shows up." Another praised the retractable design: "No more broken pencils in my makeup bag. This thing survives everything."

Repeat purchases are common. Several reviews mention buying multiple shades after trying this one, which tells you the formula delivers consistently across the range.

**Where it falls short**

The shade range leans warm. If your undertones are cool or you have very fair skin, you'll find most of the Coffee Shop collection pulls orange or brown on you. The Colorstay Matte F shade specifically is gorgeous on medium to deep skin tones but will look muddy on pale, cool complexions.

The packaging also gives you zero information. No shade name printed on the barrel, no ingredient list, nothing. You'll need to reference the Amazon listing or Juvia's website to know what you've purchased. Minor annoyance, but for a product at this price point, it feels like an oversight.

**Who should buy this**

This liner is for three specific shoppers: the person with medium-to-deep skin who's tired of "nude" liners that look ashy; the matte-lipstick wearer who needs a border that won't budge through meals; and anyone who hates the waste and breakage of traditional wooden pencils.

Skip it if you have very dry lips and refuse to layer balm, or if you're exclusively looking for cool-toned liners.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Is this liner waterproof?* It's water-resistant, not fully waterproof. It survives normal wear, sweat, and minor moisture, but a full swim or heavy tears will break it down.

*Does it work as a full lipstick?* Yes. It's pigmented enough to wear alone as a matte lip, though it will feel drier than a dedicated liquid lipstick.

*How does it compare to MAC or Charlotte Tilbury liners?* At roughly half the price, it performs comparably on pigmentation and staying power. The shade range isn't as broad, but the formula holds its own.

**The verdict**

Buy it if you want a dependable, richly pigmented liner that survives real life. Skip it if you're cool-toned or refuse to prep your lips. For the price, this is one of the better matte liners on Amazon right now.
