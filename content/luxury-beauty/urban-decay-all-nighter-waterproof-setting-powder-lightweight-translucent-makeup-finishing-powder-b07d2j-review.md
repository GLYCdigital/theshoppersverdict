Let me be direct: most "waterproof" setting powders are a marketing lie. They'll survive a light drizzle but crumble the second you actually sweat. The Urban Decay All Nighter Waterproof Setting Powder is not most setting powders. After testing this against my usual rotation for three weeks, I'm genuinely impressed — and I'm also annoyed by a few things you should know before you buy.

**What's actually in the compact**

You get 0.28 ounces of loose powder in a screw-top jar with a sifter. The shade I tested is "Lightweight Translucent," which Urban Decay markets as a universal match. The powder itself is micronized to a consistency that feels closer to a fine mist than traditional loose powder — it's almost silky to the touch.

**Real-world performance testing**

I wore this through a 90-degree day at an outdoor festival, a gym session, and a full workday with a commute. Here's what happened:

The powder sets foundation without altering its finish. My T-zone stayed matte for about six hours before a natural glow appeared — not an oil slick, just skin. The waterproof claim held up. I literally splashed water on my face after applying, and the makeup underneath didn't budge. No white cast, no flashback in photos, and zero creasing under my eyes after eight hours. That last point matters because most powders settle into fine lines by hour four.

The formula uses a silica-based blend that absorbs oil while reflecting light — which is why it doesn't look flat or cakey. One buyer described it perfectly: "This is the only powder that doesn't make me look like I have a chalk mask on by noon. It just disappears into my skin and keeps everything in place."

**What buyers consistently praise**

With 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, the consensus is clear. The most repeated compliments in the reviews:

- Longevity: "I have oily skin and this kept my makeup intact for 12 hours at a wedding. I didn't even need to blot."
- Finish: "Finally a translucent powder that doesn't darken my foundation or leave a ghostly residue."
- Waterproof legit: "Wore this swimming and my makeup survived. I was genuinely shocked."

**Where it falls short**

The packaging is the biggest offender. The sifter holes are generous — too generous. One tap releases enough powder for your whole face, and half of it ends up on your counter. If you're clumsy like me, you'll lose product every single use.

The price is also a sticking point. At roughly $38 per 0.28 ounces, you're paying a premium. The drugstore competition from NYX or Maybelline costs half as much per ounce. You're paying for the All Nighter technology and the brand name. Whether that's worth it depends on how much you value the waterproof performance.

One more thing: the "translucent" shade runs slightly warm. On very fair, cool-toned skin, it can read a touch yellow in direct sunlight. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing if you're extremely pale with pink undertones.

**Who should buy this**

- **The sweaty commuter**: If you walk or bike to work and need makeup to survive, this is your powder.
- **The event-goer**: Weddings, outdoor ceremonies, long receptions — this will outlast your outfit.
- **The oily-skinned minimalist**: If you want one powder that sets, mattifies, and doesn't need touch-ups, this simplifies your routine.

Skip it if you're on a tight budget, prefer pressed powders for portability, or have very dry skin — the oil-absorbing properties can emphasize dry patches.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Q: Does this work for all skin tones?**
A: The translucent formula won't add color, so it generally works across the spectrum. Deeper skin tones won't get a white cast. Fair, cool-toned skin might notice a slight warmth, as I mentioned.

**Q: Should I apply this before or after foundation?**
A: After. Use a fluffy brush, tap off excess, and press it into the skin. Wait two minutes after foundation application so everything sets first — this prevents the powder from clinging to damp spots.

**Q: How does this compare to Laura Mercier's setting powder?**
A: Urban Decay is more waterproof and oil-controlling. Laura Mercier gives a softer, more luminous finish. If you're oily, go Urban Decay. If you're dry, Laura Mercier is gentler.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need makeup that survives sweat, humidity, or long days without touch-ups. The waterproof performance is legit, the finish is natural, and it doesn't crease.

**Skip it if:** You're budget-conscious, prefer pressed powders, or have dry skin that doesn't need oil control.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The formula is genuinely excellent and delivers on the waterproof promise. The packaging wastes product and the price is steep, but for anyone who's fought the mid-day shine battle, this powder is worth every penny.
