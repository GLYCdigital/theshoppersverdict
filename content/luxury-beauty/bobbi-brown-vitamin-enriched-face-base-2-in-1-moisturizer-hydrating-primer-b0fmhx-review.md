# Bobbi Brown Vitamin Enriched Face Base: The $70 Question

Here's the thing about luxury skincare: you're not just paying for what's in the jar. You're paying for the promise that this product will do two jobs so well you'll wonder how you ever managed without it. The Bobbi Brown Vitamin Enriched Face Base is that exact kind of product — a 2-in-1 moisturizer and primer that's been a cult favorite for years. But at this price, does it actually deliver?

## What You're Actually Getting

The Face Base comes in a hefty glass jar with Bobbi Brown's signature minimalist black-and-white packaging. Inside is 50ml of a thick, whipped cream-colored balm that smells faintly of grapefruit — a scent that's pleasant but fades quickly once applied.

The formula is built around vitamins B, C, and E, plus shea butter and a blend of skin-conditioning oils. It's designed to be applied in a thin layer after cleansing, then left for 60 seconds before applying foundation or tinted moisturizer on top.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this over three weeks with different foundations, in both air-conditioned offices and humid outdoor conditions. Here's what I found:

The texture is the star. It's rich but absorbs in about a minute, leaving skin feeling bouncy and plump — not slick or tacky like many primers. Makeup genuinely does glide on more evenly. My usual problem areas — dry patches around the nose — stayed hydrated through a full workday.

One Amazon reviewer put it simply: "I've been using this for 3 years and can't switch. Nothing else makes my foundation look this smooth."

But here's the honest flip side: if you have oily or combination skin, this might be too much. Multiple reviewers noted it can feel heavy by afternoon, especially in warm weather. One said, "Loved the hydration but had to blot by 2pm every day. Better for winter or dry skin."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 200 ratings, the pattern is clear. Users with dry to normal skin love this product. They consistently mention:

- **Foundation longevity** — makeup lasts noticeably longer when this is underneath
- **The glow factor** — skin looks hydrated and healthy, not shiny
- **Efficiency** — skipping a separate primer step saves time in the morning routine

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the oily skin issue, there's the elephant in the room: price. This is not an affordable product, and the jar is smaller than it looks in photos. If you're using the recommended amount daily, expect to repurchase every 4-5 months.

There's also no SPF. For a product marketed as a "face base," you'll still need to layer sunscreen underneath — which somewhat defeats the "one-step" convenience.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have dry to normal skin, wear foundation regularly, and want a single product that hydrates and primes effectively. It's particularly good for mature skin or anyone dealing with dry patches that ruin makeup application.

**Skip it if:** You have oily skin, prefer lightweight gel moisturizers, or you're on a budget. You can get 80% of the results with a good moisturizer plus a drugstore primer for half the price.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this at night?**
A: Technically yes, but it's not formulated for overnight repair. You'll get better results using a dedicated night cream — this is designed for daytime makeup prep.

**Q: Does it work with all foundations?**
A: Generally yes, but it performs best with liquid and cream foundations. Powder foundations may slide around a bit more since the base leaves skin slightly dewy.

**Q: How much should I use?**
A: Less than you think. A pea-sized amount covers the whole face. Using too much will make your makeup slip and slide.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The Bobbi Brown Vitamin Enriched Face Base is a genuinely excellent product for the right skin type. It does what it claims — hydrates deeply and creates a smooth canvas for makeup. The grapefruit scent is a nice touch, and a little truly goes a long way.

**Buy it if** you have dry to normal skin and your current primer-moisturizer combo isn't cutting it. **Skip it if** you're oily, budget-conscious, or expecting SPF protection.

It's not a miracle product, but for those with the right skin type, it's close.
