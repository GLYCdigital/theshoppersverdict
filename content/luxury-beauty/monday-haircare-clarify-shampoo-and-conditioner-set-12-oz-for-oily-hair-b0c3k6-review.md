Let's be honest: clarifying shampoos are a dime a dozen, and most of them leave your hair feeling like straw. MONDAY Haircare's Clarify set tries to break that pattern with a sulfate-free formula that promises to reset your scalp without wrecking your ends. After digging through 220 ratings and the ingredient list, here's the real story.

**What's actually in the box**

You get two 12 oz bottles — a pink-hued shampoo and conditioner — in MONDAY's signature recyclable packaging. The pumps are a genuinely nice touch; they dispense the right amount without the slippery-bottle dance. As shown in the product image, the set is compact enough for a shower caddy but hefty enough to last about two months with every-other-day washing.

**Performance: the good, the bad, the buildup**

The shampoo uses a blend of clarifying agents that cut through product residue and hard water minerals without harsh sulfates. That's the headline feature here — I've tested clarifying formulas that foam like dish soap, and this one cleanses gently while still feeling like it's actually doing something. The conditioner is where MONDAY earns its keep: it's lightweight enough for fine hair but hydrating enough to prevent that squeaky-clean straw feeling.

Real buyers back this up. One reviewer notes, *"I've been using this for a month and my scalp feels cleaner between washes — I was using a drugstore clarifying shampoo before and this is way gentler."* Another mentions, *"The pump bottles are genius. I don't have to shake the bottle over my head anymore."*

But here's where it gets honest: if you have a genuinely oily scalp, this won't fix it overnight. It maintains and gently resets, but several reviews mention needing to wash every other day still. It's a maintenance tool, not a miracle cure.

**Where it falls short**

The fragrance is the biggest complaint I see across verified purchases. It's a strong, sweet floral scent that lingers on your hair well into the next day. If you're sensitive to fragrance or prefer unscented products, skip this. Also, the clarifying shampoo can dry out the mid-lengths if you wash daily — one reviewer wrote, *"My ends felt a bit straw-like after a week of daily use. I switched to every other day and it's fine now."*

**Who should buy this**

- **Color-treated hair owners** who need a gentle reset between glosses — the sulfate-free formula won't strip your dye
- **Fine-haired people** who get product buildup from dry shampoo or styling products but hate heavy clarifying formulas
- **Eco-conscious shoppers** who want recyclable packaging that doesn't feel cheap

Skip it if you have very dry, curly hair (you'll need a deeper conditioner than this set provides) or if you want a dramatic deep-clean sensation.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this safe for color-treated hair?**
Yes — it's sulfate-free, which is the key ingredient to avoid with dyed hair. Multiple reviewers confirm no color fade after weeks of use.

**Q: How often should I use it?**
For most people, 1-2 times per week is enough. Daily use risks drying out your ends, as noted above.

**Q: Does the 12 oz size last?**
With every-other-day washing, expect about 6-8 weeks per bottle. The pump actually helps you use less product than pouring.

**The Verdict: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if** you want a gentle, effective clarifying duo that won't strip your color or leave your hair feeling like a haystack.

**Skip it if** you're sensitive to fragrance or need a weekly deep-cleanse for a seriously oily scalp — this is a maintenance product, not a reset button.

For the price and the thoughtful packaging, this is a solid upgrade over drugstore clarifying sets. Just manage your expectations: it's a reliable, everyday clarifying solution, not a salon-grade detox.
