**Where it falls short**

Be honest about the limitations. And the fragrance is unapologetically classic sunscreen — a strong, coconut-adjacent scent that lingers. If you’re sensitive to fragrance, test a single can before committing to six.

**FAQ**

**Is the Cool Zone spray actually cooling, or is it just marketing?**
It’s real. The formula includes a cooling agent that activates on contact with skin — you’ll feel a distinct temperature drop for roughly 30-60 seconds after application. It’s not icy, but it’s noticeable and pleasant on hot skin.

**How many applications do you get per can?**
For an average adult doing a full-body application, expect about six to seven uses per 1.8-ounce can. For face and arms only, that stretches to 10-12 applications.

**Does it leave a white cast or stain clothes?**
No white cast on any skin tone — it sprays clear. It can leave a faint residue on dark athletic wear if you spray too close, so hold the can 6-8 inches away and let it dry before dressing.
