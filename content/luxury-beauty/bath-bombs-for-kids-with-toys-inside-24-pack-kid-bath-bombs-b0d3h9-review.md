Let's be honest: you're not buying this for the bath bomb. You're buying it because you want five peaceful minutes to yourself while your kid splashes around hunting for a surprise. The question is whether this 24-pack delivers on that promise — or just leaves you scrubbing blue residue off the tub.

Here's what actually matters, based on the 210 ratings and 4.2-star average.

**What's in the box**

You get 24 individually wrapped bath bombs, each roughly the size of a golf ball (about 1.5 inches across). Each one contains a small toy — think plastic animals, rings, or mini figurines. The bombs come in bright colors: pink, blue, purple, green, and orange. The packaging is sturdy enough to survive shipping, and the individual wrapping means you can stash a few for later without them absorbing moisture from the bathroom air.

**Real-world performance**

Drop one in warm water and the fizz starts immediately. Most parents report the fizz lasts a solid 3 to 5 minutes — long enough to build anticipation before the toy surfaces. The scents are fruit-forward (strawberry, grape, lemon) and reasonably strong, but not cloying. One reviewer noted, "My daughter has sensitive skin and we had zero issues with irritation. The smell was lovely without being overpowering."

The bath water colors are vivid, which kids love and parents tolerate. The good news: the color doesn't linger on skin. The less good news: if you have a white or light-colored tub, don't let the water sit after draining. A quick rinse handles it, but letting it dry can leave faint staining. That's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

**What buyers praise**

Parents consistently mention three things. First, the value — 24 bombs at this price point beats what you'd pay per unit at a big-box store. Second, the entertainment factor: "My 4-year-old actually asks to take baths now. The toy reveal is the highlight of her night." Third, the packaging — several reviewers mention using the individual wraps as party favors, which is a smart touch.

**Where it falls short**

The toys are the weak point. Multiple reviewers report receiving cheap plastic pieces that break within a day or two. Some duplicates too — nobody wants three identical rubber ducks. It's worth noting that most toys are small enough to be a choking hazard for kids under 3, so keep that in mind. Also, if your child is sensitive to artificial colors, this isn't the product for you. The dyes are standard for the category, but they're not natural.

**Who should buy this**

- **Parents of kids aged 3-8** who need a bath-time bribe that actually works
- **Party hosts** looking for affordable favors or activity-bag fillers
- **Grandparents** who want an easy, fun gift that doesn't require assembly

Skip it if you're looking for premium, natural bath products — this is squarely in the "fun for kids" category, not the "luxury spa" one.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are the toys safe for toddlers?**
A: Most toys are small (1-2 inches) and pose a choking hazard for kids under 3. If your child still mouths objects, keep a close eye or skip it.

**Q: How long does one bath bomb last?**
A: The fizz runs 3-5 minutes. The scent lingers in the water but fades once drained.

**Q: Do the bombs stain skin or tubs?**
A: Skin, no. Tub, yes — if you let the colored water sit and dry. Rinse right after draining and you're fine.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a low-cost way to make bath time fun without overthinking it. The value is solid, the scents are pleasant, and the toy reveal genuinely delights kids.

**Skip it if** toy durability matters more than the bath experience, or if you prefer natural ingredients. This is a fun, disposable product — not a long-term investment.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid value and real entertainment, held back by inconsistent toy quality. For the price, it's worth trying one box.
