## Performance: Does It Actually Protect Color?

The short answer: yes, and the customer feedback backs it up. One reviewer noted, "I color my hair every 6-8 weeks, and this got me to week 10 without my red fading to orange." That's the kind of specific result that matters. The formula uses a blend of UV filters and antioxidant-rich fruit extracts that create a protective barrier around the hair shaft, sealing the cuticle and preventing the color molecules from washing out.

In real-world use, the conditioner detangles impressively well. A little goes a long way — most users report a quarter-sized amount is enough for shoulder-length hair. The texture is on the thicker side, which means it coats each strand thoroughly, but it also means you need to rinse longer than you might with a lighter formula. One tradeoff worth noting: if you have fine hair, use less than you think you need, or you'll feel a slight residue.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the drawbacks. First, the price per ounce is steep compared to drugstore alternatives like L'Oréal or OGX. You're paying for salon-grade quality, and it shows — but budget shoppers will feel the difference. Second, this is a maintenance product, not a repair product. If your hair is already dry, damaged, or over-processed, this won't fix it. You'll need a deep conditioning mask in rotation. Finally, the thicker formula can leave fine hair feeling weighed down if you over-apply — a learning curve, not a dealbreaker.

## Who Should Buy This

This is for you if you're already investing in professional color and want to protect that investment. Ideal for:
