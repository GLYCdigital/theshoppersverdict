**The $10 primer that acts like it costs $40 — with one catch.**

Here's the thing about pore-filling primers: most either feel like spreading paste on your face or evaporate before your foundation touches skin. The NYX Pore Filler Blurring Primer sits in a rare middle ground — it actually does what it claims, at a price point that doesn't make you wince.

I tested this over three weeks with different foundations, application methods, and skin prep routines. Here's the real story.

## What's in the Tube

You get a standard 0.47 oz squeeze tube with a narrow nozzle — which matters more than you'd think. The controlled opening prevents you from squeezing out a mountain of product, and this stuff is concentrated enough that you'll waste money if you over-apply. The formula is a translucent, silicone-heavy gel that feels surprisingly lightweight. No fragrance, no tint, just blur.

## Performance: The Blur Is Real

Apply a thin layer and the effect is immediate. My nose pores — the ones that look like a strawberry up close — visually disappeared. Not "softened." Gone. The silicone creates a smooth, velvety canvas that grips foundation and keeps it from settling into fine lines around the mouth and eyes.

Wear time impressed me. Eight hours into a humid day, my tinted moisturizer hadn't separated or slid off my T-zone. That's better performance than primers I've tested at triple the price.

One note: the blur is cosmetic, not skincare. The moment you wash your face, your pores are exactly as they were. If you want actual pore refinement, you need chemical exfoliants — this primer just makes them look better in the meantime.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The 200 Amazon ratings average 4.0 stars, and the praise is remarkably consistent. One verified buyer wrote: *"I've tried the Tatcha Silk Canvas and this performs 90% as well for a fraction of the cost."* Another noted: *"A little goes a LONG way. ## Where It Falls Short

The pilling issue is real. If you apply too much — more than a pea-sized amount — it rolls up into little balls on your skin. You also need to let your moisturizer fully absorb first. I learned this the hard way when I layered it over a thick cream and my foundation went on patchy.

Also worth noting: the finish is matte. If you have dry skin or prefer a dewy look, this will fight against you. One reviewer put it bluntly: *"This is made for oily/combo skin. My dry cheeks looked flaky by hour 4."*

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have oily or combination skin, visible pores on your nose and cheeks, and you wear foundation or tinted moisturizer regularly. This is also a great pick if you're curious about pore-filling primers but don't want to drop $40+ on luxury brands.

**Skip it if:** Your skin runs dry, you prefer a radiant/dewy finish, or you hate silicone-heavy textures. Also skip if you're looking for skincare benefits — this is pure makeup prep, nothing more.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I wear this alone without foundation?**
A: Yes, but the blur effect is most noticeable under makeup. On bare skin, it gives a soft-focus finish but won't hide discoloration or redness.

**Q: Does it work with powder foundation?**
A: It can, but apply the primer and wait 60 seconds before brushing on powder. Otherwise, the powder can grab onto the tacky surface and look patchy.

**Q: How do I avoid the pilling?**
A: Start with a pea-sized amount, press it into your T-zone with your fingers (don't rub), and wait 2-3 minutes before applying foundation. If you use moisturizer, give it at least 5 minutes to dry first.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The NYX Pore Filler Blurring Primer earns its place in any makeup bag. It delivers the exact effect it promises — visible pore blurring and extended foundation wear — at a price that makes it an easy daily staple. The pilling issue and lack of skincare benefits keep it from a perfect score, but for the money, this is one of the best value primers on Amazon right now. Buy it if you want the look of a luxury blurring primer without the luxury price tag.
