Let me cut through the hype: The Dior Addict Lip Glow #001 Pink isn't a lipstick, isn't a gloss, and isn't just a balm. It's a color-adapting hybrid that's been a cult favorite for over a decade, and after testing it against cheaper alternatives, I understand why it still holds the throne — and also why some shoppers feel let down.

**What You Actually Get**

The package is classic Dior: a sleek silver tube with the iconic CD logo, weighing just 3.2 grams. That's the first thing to note — this is a *small* product. At roughly the size of a standard lipstick but with less product inside, you're paying for the formula and the name, not volume. It arrives in a sealed box with the balm bullet intact — no applicator, no mirror, nothing fancy. Just the product and the promise.

**The Formula: Where the Magic (and the Catch) Lives**

The core technology here is the pH-reactive pigment. Applied to bare lips, it starts as a faint pink and deepens to a custom rose shade based on your skin's acidity. On my neutral-toned skin, it settled into a soft, "my lips but better" pink that reads natural — not neon, not washed out. On warmer skin tones, reviewers consistently report it pulls more coral; on cooler tones, it leans berry.

The texture is where this shines. It glides on like a melting shea butter blend (which it is — shea butter and mango seed oil are the second and third ingredients). Unlike many tinted balms that sit on the surface, this sinks in within 60 seconds, leaving zero tackiness. You can layer it three times for more color without it getting gummy — that's rare in this category.

**What 229 Buyers Keep Saying**

The 4.6-star rating holds up when you read the actual reviews. One verified buyer wrote: *"I've repurchased this four times. It's the only lip product that doesn't dry me out, and the color is perfect for every season."* Another noted: *"The shade genuinely changes on my lips — it's pinker in winter and more berry in summer. I don't know how they do it."*

The praise consistently centers on three things: the unique color adaptation, the non-drying formula, and the "expensive feeling" you get from the packaging and texture.

**Where It Falls Short**

Be honest with yourself before buying: this is a *tinted balm*, not a statement lip. If you want visible, opaque color, you'll be disappointed. The shade settles into a subtle wash that reads "glossy nude" more than "pink lipstick" in most lighting.

The longevity is also a tradeoff. While hydration lasts 4–6 hours, the visible tint fades within 2–3 hours of eating or drinking. And yes, the 3.2 g size is genuinely small — daily users report finishing it in 6–8 weeks. At this price point, that's a real consideration.

**Who Should Buy This**

Get this if you're a "minimal makeup, maximal polish" person — someone who wants hydrated, naturally flushed lips without fussing over a mirror. It's ideal for work, travel, or days when you want to look put-together without looking made-up. Skip it if you prefer bold, high-pigment lipsticks or want a budget-friendly everyday product that you can replace without thinking.

**FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask**

**Does the color actually adapt to your pH?**
Yes, but not dramatically. The shift is subtle — expect a variation of pink/rose/coral depending on your skin chemistry, not a dramatic transformation from clear to red.

**Can I wear this under lipstick?**
Absolutely. It creates a smooth, hydrated base that prevents matte lipsticks from clinging to dry patches. Just wait 60 seconds for it to absorb before applying.

**Is it worth the price for 3.2 g?**
If you value the formula and use it sparingly (2–3 swipes daily), it lasts about two months. That's roughly $1.50 per day for a luxury product. If that feels steep, consider a drugstore color-adapting balm — but know the texture and hydration won't match.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a foolproof, natural-looking lip color that hydrates all day, and you're willing to pay for the luxury experience.

**Skip it if:** You need bold pigment, long-lasting visible color, or a budget-friendly daily staple.

The Dior Addict Lip Glow #001 Pink earns its 4.6 stars — not because it does everything, but because what it does, it does flawlessly. It's a refined, wearable product that delivers exactly what it promises: a personalized wash of color with serious hydration. Just know what you're buying before you hit checkout.
