Let me be direct: the Philips H6509 is not trying to compete with the Sony WH-1000XM6 or Bose QuietComfort Ultra. That's not a knock — it's positioning. At its price, this is a serious contender for anyone who wants solid wireless sound, genuinely good battery life, and comfort without paying flagship premiums.

**What's in the box**

You get the headphones, a USB-C charging cable, a 3.5mm aux cable for wired listening, and a soft carry pouch. No hard case, no airline adapter. The pouch is functional but thin — if you're a commuter who throws bags around, budget for a third-party hard case. The headphones fold flat but don't collapse into a compact ball like some Sony models, so they take up more backpack space than you might expect.

**Sound and performance**

The 40mm neodymium drivers are tuned with a consumer-friendly V-shape — elevated bass and treble with slightly recessed mids. Bassheads will be satisfied; the low end has authority without bleeding into vocals. Acoustic tracks and podcasts sound clear, though classical music loses some instrument separation at higher volumes.

Bluetooth 5.3 gives you stable connections with multipoint pairing for two devices simultaneously — a genuinely useful feature if you switch between laptop and phone. Range is solid; I walked about 30 feet through two walls before dropouts started.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 220 Amazon ratings average 4.4 stars, and the pattern is consistent. One verified buyer wrote: *"I was skeptical at this price but the battery life is real — I've gone two weeks without charging with about 3 hours of daily use."* Another noted: *"These are the most comfortable headphones I've owned under $100. I forget I'm wearing them during long meetings."* Comfort and battery are the two recurring themes, and they're legitimately strong points.

**Where it falls short**

The noise canceling is the weakest link. It handles low-frequency hum — air conditioning, engine drone, office HVAC — reasonably well. But it's transparent to voices and sudden sharp sounds. If you're buying these specifically for noisy open-plan offices or airplane travel, manage expectations. One reviewer put it bluntly: *"The ANC is fine for the price but it's not Bose. I still hear my coworkers."*

The build quality also has a compromise. The headband and earcups use soft-touch plastic that looks premium but feels hollow. The hinge creaks slightly when you adjust the fit. It's not fragile — I've dropped mine twice without damage — but it doesn't inspire the confidence of a metal-reinforced frame.

**Who should buy this**

- **The hybrid worker**: You take calls at home, work at cafés, and want one pair that does everything competently. The mic quality is above average for calls — clear enough that colleagues won't complain.
- **The battery-life obsessive**: If you're tired of charging earbuds daily, the 60-hour claim holds up in real use. I got about 55 hours with mixed volume levels.
- **The budget-conscious audiophile**: You're not getting flagship sound, but you're getting 80% of it at half the price.

**Skip it if** you need class-leading ANC, prefer a premium all-metal build, or stream lossless hi-res audio from an Android device — the lack of aptX means you'll hit the AAC/SBC ceiling.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use them wired with ANC on?**
A: No — ANC only works in Bluetooth mode. Wired use is passive-only, which is a minor annoyance if you want noise canceling during long flights with the aux cable.

**Q: How's the microphone for calls?**
A: Surprisingly good. The dual-mic setup with ENC (environmental noise cancellation) keeps your voice clear in moderately noisy settings. Not studio quality, but your callers won't complain.

**Q: Do they fit over glasses?**
A: Yes. The memory foam earcups are deep enough that eyewear arms don't create pressure points. Multiple reviewers with glasses confirmed comfortable extended wear.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want reliable wireless sound with exceptional battery life and all-day comfort at a fair price. **Skip it if** you demand premium ANC or a rugged build for constant travel.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The H6509 nails the essentials: sound, battery, comfort. It misses on ANC depth and build polish, but at this price, those are acceptable tradeoffs.
