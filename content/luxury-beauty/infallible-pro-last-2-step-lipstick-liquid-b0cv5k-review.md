The L'Oréal Infallible Pro Last 2 Step Lipstick Liquid has been sitting at 4.4 stars across 220 Amazon ratings, which tells you something: this isn't a viral hype product. ## What You Actually Get

The box contains two components: a pigmented liquid lipstick wand and a separate clear balm applicator. The color step goes on like a gloss but sets within 60 seconds into a matte, transfer-resistant finish. The balm is designed to be applied over the top once the color dries, adding moisture without lifting the pigment. As shown in the product image, the dual-chamber design is straightforward — no mixing, no waiting period longer than a minute.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where this product earns its keep. The color layer genuinely stays put. Buyers consistently report surviving meals — one reviewer noted, "I wore this through a full Thanksgiving dinner and only needed to reapply the balm, not the color." That aligns with the 12-hour claims, though I'd say 8-10 hours is more realistic for most wearers before edges start fading.

The balm step is the differentiator. Most long-wear lipsticks dry your lips into raisins by hour three. This one doesn't. The balm adds genuine moisture, and because it's a separate step, you can reapply it throughout the day without disturbing the color beneath. Smart design.

**What buyers consistently praise:**
- "Stays on through everything — coffee, tacos, even a nap."
- "The balm actually helps, not like those other kits where the top coat makes it pill."
- "One coat is enough. This is the most pigmented lipstick I own."

## Where It Falls Short

The balm is a double-edged sword. Apply too much and it reactivates the color, turning your perfectly matte lip into a slippery mess that transfers everywhere. The learning curve is real — you need a thin, even layer, which takes practice.

Removal is also a commitment. Standard micellar water won't cut it. You'll need an oil-based cleanser or makeup remover balm, and even then, darker shades leave a faint stain for a day. Not ideal if you like changing lip colors throughout the day.

Finally, lip prep matters more than with any other lipstick I've reviewed. Skip exfoliation and this formula will find every dry patch and lip line. That's not a flaw unique to this product, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a professional who needs color that survives long days, meals, and meetings without bathroom touch-up breaks. You prefer matte finishes but hate the dry, cracked feeling of traditional liquid lipsticks. You're willing to spend 30 seconds prepping your lips before application.

**Skip it if:** You change lip colors multiple times a day, prefer glossy or satin finishes, or don't want to deal with oil-based removal. Also skip if you're prone to dry, flaky lips and won't commit to exfoliating.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this actually last 12 hours?**
A: In ideal conditions — no eating, minimal talking — yes. With normal eating and drinking, expect 8-10 hours before the center starts fading. The edges hold longest.

**Q: Which shades are most popular?**
A: The reds and berries get the most positive reviews. Nudes are less forgiving of lip texture, so if you're trying this formula for the first time, go with a saturated shade.

**Q: How does this compare to other long-wear lipsticks?**
A: It's more comfortable than traditional liquid mattes like the Maybelline SuperStay, but slightly less bulletproof. The balm step makes it the better all-day option, especially for dry lips.
