# L'Oréal Paris Colour Riche Eye La Palette: Solid, But Know What You're Buying

Here's the thing about drugstore eyeshadow palettes: most are either packed with chalky, low-pigment pans that frustrate you, or they cost nearly as much as a mid-range brand without the quality to back it up. The L'Oréal Colour Riche Eye La Palette sits somewhere more interesting — it's genuinely good, but it has quirks you need to know about before you hit "add to cart."

## What You Actually Get

This is a 10-pan palette measuring roughly 8 x 3 inches — compact enough for a makeup bag, though the lack of a mirror means you'll want one nearby anyway. The shade range spans warm neutrals: cream, taupe, bronze, a deep espresso, plus several shimmer finishes and a few mattes. It's not an adventurous palette, but it covers the basics well.

The dual-ended applicator is a pleasant surprise. One side is a dense sponge for packing color, the other is a softer brush for blending. It's not replacing your favorite brushes, but it's genuinely functional for travel.

## Performance: The Real Story

The shimmers are the stars here. They're richly pigmented, apply smoothly, and have that wet-look finish that makes people ask what you're wearing. The bronze and gold shades in particular are stunning — one swipe delivers serious color payoff.

The mattes are more of a mixed bag. The deeper shades like the espresso and warm brown blend beautifully and hold up well on the lid. But the lighter mattes — the cream and taupe shades — are frustratingly sheer. On fair skin they work okay, but if you have medium or deeper skin tones, expect to build them up significantly. That's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing going in.

Longevity is respectable but not exceptional. On normal-to-dry lids, you'll get six to seven hours before any noticeable creasing. If you're oily, budget for a primer — several reviewers noted creasing by the five-hour mark without one. One Amazon reviewer put it well: *"The shimmer shades are gorgeous and last all day, but I need a primer for the mattes or they disappear by lunch."*

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 204 ratings, a few themes stand out:

- **Value for money.** Multiple reviewers called it the best drugstore palette they've tried, with one saying, *"I've spent four times as much on palettes that perform worse."*
- **Shimmer quality.** Nearly every positive review mentions the metallic shades specifically.
- **Wearability.** The shade selection is practical — no neon shades you'll never touch.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the sheer light mattes and the mirror omission, there are a few other honest criticisms:

- **Fallout on the shimmers.** You'll want to do your eyes before your foundation, or tap off your brush thoroughly. The glittery shades deposit powder onto your cheeks if you're not careful.
- **The packaging is basic.** It's functional plastic, but it doesn't feel luxurious — despite the "Colour Riche" name suggesting otherwise.
- **Limited shade range.** If you're looking for cool-toned or colorful options, this isn't it. It's strictly warm neutrals.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want a reliable, everyday neutral palette that performs well for the price. It's ideal for work-appropriate looks, travel, or as a first "real" palette for someone learning to apply eyeshadow. If you're fair to medium-toned and love shimmer, this will quickly become a staple.

**Skip it if:** You have deeper skin and need pigmented mattes, you're strictly an all-matte user, or you need a mirror in your palette. Also skip if you're oily and don't want to commit to using a primer every time.

## FAQ

**Is this palette good for beginners?**
Yes, mostly. The shimmers are forgiving and easy to apply, and the neutral shades make it hard to mess up. The sheer light mattes might frustrate beginners who expect immediate payoff, but the darker shades blend easily enough that you can create a decent look even with limited skill.

**How does it compare to higher-end palettes?**
The shimmer shades genuinely rival mid-range brands like Urban Decay. The mattes fall short of that comparison — they're more like a good drugstore matte. Overall, you're getting about 80% of the performance of a $50 palette for significantly less.

**Does it work for all skin tones?**
The deeper shades will work beautifully on deep skin tones, but the lighter mattes will require serious building. If you're medium-to-deep, you'll get the most use from the shimmers and darker mattes.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The L'Oréal Colour Riche Eye La Palette is one of the better drugstore eyeshadow options available. The shimmer shades are genuinely impressive, the shade selection is practical, and the price point makes it an easy impulse buy. But the sheer light mattes, missing mirror, and creasing on oily lids keep it from being perfect.

**Buy it if** you want a versatile neutral palette with standout shimmers and don't need mirror-included convenience. **Skip it if** you're all-matte, have deep skin that needs pigmented light shades, or you're oily and hate using primer. For everyone else — at this price, it's worth the splurge on your next Target run.
