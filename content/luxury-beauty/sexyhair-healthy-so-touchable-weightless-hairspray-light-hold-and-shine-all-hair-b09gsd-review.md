Let’s be honest about hairspray: most either glue your hair into a helmet or evaporate the second you step outside. The SexyHair Healthy So Touchable Weightless Hairspray in Light Hold tries to split the difference. Does it work? After digging through 225 ratings and the actual formulation, here’s the real story.

**What’s Actually in the Can**

You’re getting a 10-ounce aerosol that markets itself as a "weightless" formula with a light hold and added shine. The key ingredients matter here: it uses a flexible polymer blend rather than the stiff resin you find in traditional high-hold sprays. That’s why it behaves differently. It also includes a touch of conditioning agents — nothing heavy, just enough to avoid that parched, straw-like feeling most sprays leave behind. The scent is pleasant but noticeable for the first few minutes; it fades to almost nothing within an hour, which is a plus if you’re sensitive to fragrance.

**How It Performs in the Real World**

I tested this on medium-length, slightly wavy hair and also had a friend with fine, straight hair try it. The standout feature is the finish. As the name promises, hair stays touchable — you can run your fingers through it without resistance. There’s no crunch, no white flakes, no buildup even after two days of use. The hold is flexible: it keeps loose styles in place, tames flyaways, and holds a soft curl for several hours, but it won’t survive a windy commute or a high-humidity day if you need serious staying power.

Where it surprised me was as a pre-style primer. Spray it on damp hair before blow-drying, and it adds a subtle grip that makes styling easier without weighing hair down. That’s a genuinely useful dual-purpose trick that most sprays in this price range don’t offer.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The Amazon reviews are overwhelmingly positive, and the recurring theme is the texture. One reviewer put it simply: *"Finally a hairspray that doesn't make my hair feel like straw. I can brush through it at the end of the day."* Another noted, *"I've bought this three times now. It's the only spray that works for my fine hair without making it flat."* That tracks with what I found — the weightless claim is legit, not marketing fluff.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let’s be direct about the limitations. The light hold is exactly that — light. If you have thick, coarse, or naturally curly hair, this will not hold a style on its own. You’ll need a stronger product for the base and use this only for finishing. The shine is also subtle; it gives a healthy gloss, not a wet-look or high-shine effect. And there’s a practical gripe: several reviewers mentioned the nozzle clogging or sputtering when the can is about a quarter full. It’s not universal, but it’s common enough to mention.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is made for people with fine to medium hair who want control without sacrifice. It’s ideal if you wear your hair down, need to tame flyaways, or want a spray that won’t ruin second-day hair. It also works well for anyone who prefers a natural, "I didn't try too hard" aesthetic over a polished, stiff finish. Skip it if you need serious all-day hold, have very thick hair, or expect a high-gloss shine.

**FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask**

*Does this spray make hair feel sticky or greasy?* No. The formula dries down clean with zero tackiness. It feels like nothing is there, which is the point.

*Can I use it on colored or treated hair?* Yes. It doesn't contain harsh alcohols that strip color, and it's gentle enough for daily use on processed hair.

*How does it compare to a medium or strong hold spray?* It's fundamentally different. This is a finishing spray for movement and softness. If you need structure, look elsewhere. If you need a light touch, this is the one.

**The Verdict**

The SexyHair Healthy So Touchable Weightless Hairspray earns its 4.5-star rating honestly. It delivers exactly what it promises — flexible, invisible hold that keeps hair looking and feeling natural. It's not a miracle worker for thick or unruly hair, and the nozzle issue is a real annoyance, but for its target audience, it's a reliable, repeat-purchase winner.

**Buy it if** you have fine to medium hair, want a no-crunch finish, or need a versatile spray for both styling and finishing.

**Skip it if** you need serious hold, have very thick hair, or want a high-gloss shine.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a genuinely good product with a clear purpose, held back only by its light-hold limitations and minor packaging quirks.
