You're looking at a gift set that solves the two biggest problems with buying fragrance online: guessing if the scent works on your skin chemistry, and overpaying for a single bottle that might miss. The COACH Floral 2-Piece Eau de Parfum Gift Set bundles a 3.3 oz bottle of the signature EDP with a 2.6 oz body lotion in the same scent. At the typical $80-90 street price, you're effectively getting the lotion free — not a bad deal when the perfume alone normally runs $75+.

## What's Actually in the Box

- **COACH Floral Eau de Parfum** — 3.3 oz (100 ml) spray, the full-size bottle
- **COACH Floral Body Lotion** — 2.6 oz (75 ml) pump bottle

Both come in the signature Coach packaging — the perfume bottle has that chunky glass horseshoe-shaped cap and the lotion mirrors it in miniature. The gift box is sturdy enough that you could wrap it as-is without an extra box.

## Performance and Real-World Wear

The opening is undeniably pretty: pear and pink peony hit first, with that characteristic Coach brightness. It's not a wallflower scent — you'll get a solid arm's-length projection for the first hour. But here's where I need to be straight with you: this is not a beast-mode fragrance. On my skin, it settled to a skin-scent within 3 hours and was barely detectable by hour 5. If you're someone who wants to spritz once at 7 AM and still smell it at dinner, this will disappoint you. It's designed as an everyday, office-friendly floral, not a statement perfume.

The lotion is genuinely better than most gift-set fillers. It's got a shea butter base that absorbs in about 60 seconds without leaving that greasy film, and it actually boosts the fragrance's staying power when you layer it. Spray the perfume over the lotion and you'll squeeze another hour or two out of the scent.

## What Buyers Consistently Say

The 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews holds up when you dig into the feedback. One reviewer noted: *"I bought this as a gift for my sister and ended up buying a second set for myself. The scent is light and fresh, not overpowering like some floral perfumes."* Another long-term user mentioned: *"I've worn COACH Floral for years and this set is the best value I've found. The lotion is a nice bonus, not a throwaway."*

The most common complaint isn't about the scent itself — it's about the longevity. Multiple reviews describe the same experience I had: *"Smells beautiful but fades faster than I'd like."* If you're used to heavier designer scents like YSL or Tom Ford, this will feel lighter and shorter-lived.

## Where It Falls Short

The scent is linear. It doesn't have that dramatic arc from citrus top notes to woody base — it opens floral, stays floral, and fades floral. For the price, that's a tradeoff you're making for the value and the brand name. Also, if you're buying this as a gift for someone who prefers warm, amber-heavy scents, this will miss the mark. It's decisively feminine and decisively fresh.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this the same scent as the original COACH Floral?**
A: Yes — it's the same Eau de Parfum formulation, just bundled with a matching lotion. The fragrance notes are identical: pear, pink peony, and raspberry with a base of musk and blonde woods.

**Q: How long does a 3.3 oz bottle last?**
A: With daily use (2-3 sprays), expect 4-6 months. If you're layering with the lotion, you'll use slightly more but get better scent retention.

**Q: Is this suitable for sensitive skin?**
A: The perfume is alcohol-based and can irritate sensitive skin — test on a small patch first. The lotion is gentler with shea butter, but both contain fragrance so they're not ideal for highly reactive skin.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, office-appropriate floral with a free matching lotion that actually works. This is a smart value play for a designer fragrance.

**Skip it if** you need all-day projection, prefer complex evolving scents, or are shopping for someone who likes heavier, warmer profiles.
