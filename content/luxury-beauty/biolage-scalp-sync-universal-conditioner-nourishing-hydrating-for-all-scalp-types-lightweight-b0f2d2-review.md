Here is the review:

Most conditioners force you to pick a side: hydrate your scalp or nourish your strands. The Biolage Scalp Sync Universal Conditioner is one of the few formulas that actually tries to do both — and for the most part, it succeeds. With 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, this product has clearly found its audience. But "universal" is a bold claim, and I'm here to tell you where it holds up and where it falls short.

**What You Actually Get**

You're paying for a 13.5 fl oz bottle of conditioner formulated around a scalp-first philosophy. The key ingredients do the heavy lifting: salicylic acid (0.5%) works to exfoliate buildup on the scalp, zinc PCA helps regulate oil production, and panthenol (pro-vitamin B5) plus glycerin provide the humectant moisture that your actual hair strands need. It's a smart combination — most scalp-focused products either ignore strand health or use ingredients that irritate sensitive skin. This one avoids both traps.

**Real-World Performance**

Here's the honest breakdown: this conditioner delivers exactly what it promises for normal-to-oily hair types. It rinses clean without that slippery, "did I get all the product out?" feeling. Your scalp feels genuinely cleaner after use — not stripped, but balanced. One Amazon reviewer noted, *"My scalp used to get itchy and flaky by day two. With this, I can go three days without washing and my scalp still feels fresh."* That tracks with the salicylic acid working as a gentle chemical exfoliant.

The hydration story is more nuanced. The formula is lightweight by design, which is excellent for fine hair. You won't get that heavy, coated feeling that many moisturizing conditioners leave behind. However, if your hair is thick, coarse, or curly, this may not be enough. Multiple reviewers with texture mention needing to pair it with a leave-in or deep conditioner. One put it plainly: *"Great for my scalp, but my ends still feel thirsty."*

**Where It Falls Short**

The "universal" positioning is both the product's strength and its weakness. It's a jack-of-all-trades that excels at scalp health but doesn't go deep enough for hair that genuinely needs heavy moisture. The 13.5 oz bottle is also smaller than typical drugstore conditioners (usually 16-20 oz), and the price reflects that premium positioning. The pump dispenser is my other gripe — it's not locked, so it can leak in transit, and it tends to dispense a generous dollop that's more than needed.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The oily-scalp sufferer**: If you deal with buildup, itchiness, or that "need to wash every day" feeling, this is worth your money.
- **The fine-hair hydration seeker**: You want moisture without the heavy, flattening residue. This delivers.
- **The sensitive-scalp user**: Fragrance is present but not overwhelming, and there are no sulfates or parabens to trigger irritation.

**Who Should Skip It**

- **Curly or coily hair types** needing serious slip and moisture retention — look elsewhere.
- **Anyone on a strict budget** — drugstore conditioners will do the job for a fraction of the cost, though with less scalp benefit.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this conditioner contain sulfates?**
A: No. It's sulfate-free, which is crucial since sulfates can strip the scalp's natural moisture barrier and worsen dryness.

**Q: How often should I use it?**
A: Daily if needed — it's gentle enough. But most users find 2-3 times per week sufficient, especially if your scalp is already balanced.

**Q: Will it weigh down thin hair?**
A: No. This is one of its biggest strengths. The lightweight formula rinses clean and won't flatten fine hair.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** your scalp is the source of your hair frustration — buildup, itchiness, or excess oil. This is a genuinely effective scalp-health conditioner that won't sacrifice strand hydration.

**Skip it if** your primary concern is deep moisture for dry, textured, or damaged hair. You'll need something richer.

At 4.6/5, the Biolage Scalp Sync Universal Conditioner earns its rating. It's not perfect for every hair type, but for its target audience — anyone whose scalp needs attention — it's a standout product that delivers on its promises.
