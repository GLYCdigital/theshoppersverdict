Let's be blunt: most "3-in-1" products are one good formula diluted into three mediocre ones. The Everyone brand actually pulls it off — and with a 4.6-star average across 229 Amazon ratings, buyers agree. But this isn't a miracle product, and there are a few things you should know before adding it to your cart.

## What You're Actually Getting

The bottle pictured is the 32-ounce size — that's a full quart of product. For a family using it daily as body wash, shampoo, and bubble bath, expect two to three months of use per bottle. The formula is plant-based, biodegradable, and free of sulfates, parabens, phthalates, and synthetic dyes. It's also Leaping Bunny certified cruelty-free, which matters if that's part of your buying criteria. The pump top dispenses a reasonable amount — roughly a quarter-sized dollop for body wash, a bit more for shampoo.

## How It Performs in Real Use

**As a body wash:** This is where it shines. The lather is rich and creamy, not thin or watery like some plant-based cleansers. It rinses clean without that squeaky, stripped feeling. Buyers with sensitive skin and conditions like eczema consistently report no irritation — one reviewer noted, "My dermatologist recommended this for my son's dry patches, and it's the only thing that doesn't make them worse."

**As a shampoo:** It works, with caveats. The coconut-derived surfactants clean effectively without stripping natural oils. For normal-to-dry hair, it's genuinely good. But if you have very oily hair or use heavy styling products, you'll likely need to shampoo twice — or follow with a conditioner, which this is not. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: "Great for my daily wash, but my wife with color-treated hair found it too drying on its own."

**As a bubble bath:** Pour a generous capful under running water and you'll get a legitimate layer of foam, not just sudsy water. It dissipates after 15-20 minutes, which is normal for a plant-based formula without synthetic foaming agents. Just remember — this is not tear-free, so keep it away from little faces.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The versatility is the headline. Reviewers repeatedly mention using one bottle for the whole family — baby's bath, dad's shower, mom's shampoo. The lack of artificial fragrance is another recurring theme. As one five-star review put it: "Finally a product that doesn't smell like a chemical factory. The coconut and lemon is natural and light — I can actually breathe in the shower."

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty requires addressing the scent. The coconut and lemon fragrance is pleasant but underwhelming — it's gone within minutes of rinsing. If you want a product that leaves you smelling fresh throughout the day, this isn't it. You'll need a separate cologne, lotion, or deodorant.

The shampoo function also has limits. Fine or oily hair types may find it leaves hair feeling slightly heavy, especially with daily use. It's a body wash first, shampoo second — adjust expectations accordingly.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Families** looking to consolidate bathroom clutter into one multi-purpose bottle
- **Sensitive skin sufferers** who react to synthetic fragrances and harsh sulfates
- **Campers, travelers, and gym-goers** who want one product that covers everything
- **Eco-conscious shoppers** who prioritize biodegradable, cruelty-free formulas

Skip it if you demand long-lasting fragrance, have very oily hair requiring targeted shampoo, or want a tear-free option for young kids.

## FAQ

**Is this safe for babies and toddlers?**
The formula is gentle and plant-based, but it's not tear-free. For infants under six months, consult your pediatrician first. For older kids, it works well as bubble bath — just supervise and avoid getting it in their eyes.

**Does it work as a daily shampoo?**
Yes, for most hair types. If your hair is oily, you may need to shampoo twice or alternate with a clarifying shampoo. If you have color-treated or very dry hair, pair it with a conditioner.

**Is the scent strong?**
No. The coconut and lemon is subtle and natural, smelling more like a light spa product than a perfumed body wash. It fades quickly after rinsing.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want an honest, effective multi-purpose cleanser that handles body, hair, and bath duties without harsh chemicals — and you value simplicity over fragrance longevity.

**Skip it if** you need a strong, lasting scent, have demanding hair care needs, or require a tear-free formula for young children.

At 4.6 stars with 229 ratings, the consensus is clear: this is one of the better 3-in-1 formulas on the market. It doesn't excel at everything, but it does everything well enough to earn a permanent spot in your shower.
