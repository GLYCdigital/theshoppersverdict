## Where It Falls Short

No product is flawless, and this one has three honest gaps.

First, there's no ingredient list printed on individual items. The box insert lists the main components, but if you or the recipient has sensitive skin and needs to verify a specific allergen (say, almond oil or fragrance type), you'll need to visit the Lovi website. Annoying for a gift where you can't ask the recipient to check.

Second, the bath salts come in a resealable mylar pouch, not a glass jar. It's functional but visually the weakest item in the box. If you're gifting to someone who values aesthetics, expect them to transfer the salts to their own container.

Third, the candle needs a wick trim before first burn. A few reviewers reported uneven melting and soot when they let it burn past three hours without trimming. That's standard candle care, but for a "self-care" product, it should be noted in the instructions.
