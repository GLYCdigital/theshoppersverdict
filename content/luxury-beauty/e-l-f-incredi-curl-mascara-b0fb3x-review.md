Let's cut through the noise: e.l.f. calls the Incredi-Curl Mascara their "first-ever Curl-Lock Mascara," and at a drugstore price point, that's a bold claim. I tested it for two weeks on uncurled, stick-straight lashes — the ultimate stress test. Here's what actually happened.

**What's in the tube**

You get the standard 0.24 oz mascara tube with a curved, hourglass-shaped wand. The wand is the key component here — the curve is designed to hug the lash line and physically lift while the Curl-Lock polymer dries down and holds the shape. The formula is jet black, slightly creamy rather than wet, which matters for curl retention. Wet formulas drag lashes down; this one sets quickly.

**The real-world performance**

The first thing you'll notice is the hold. I applied one coat, no lash curler, and my lashes stayed visibly lifted for a full workday — roughly nine hours including a commute. The second coat adds noticeable volume without spider legs. The formula builds well because it doesn't flash-dry mid-application; you have maybe 20 seconds of working time per eye before it sets.

Smudge-wise, it survived a rainy afternoon and an accidental eye rub without disaster. This is a waterproof-leaning formula, so it's tenacious. That's a pro for all-day wear, but a con for removal — you'll need an oil-based cleanser or a dedicated eye makeup remover. Regular micellar water will leave you scrubbing.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The Amazon reviews at 4.1 stars across 204 ratings tell you what you need to know. The common thread: this mascara performs like a prestige product at a fraction of the price. One reviewer noted, "I have short, straight Asian lashes that refuse to hold a curl, and this is the first drugstore mascara that gave me visible lift without a curler." Another said, "Three coats and my lashes look like I have extensions — no clumps, no flakes."

The curl-lock technology is the standout feature. It genuinely works — the polymer creates a memory effect that sets the lash shape as it dries. If you've tried curling mascaras before and been disappointed, this one is different.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the limitations. The packaging feels cheap — thin plastic, minimal design. For a product positioned as "Incredi-Curl" luxury, the tube doesn't match the performance inside. More importantly, the curl hold isn't universal. If your lashes are extremely stubborn or point straight down, you'll need to use a heated lash curler first. The mascara holds a curl; it doesn't create one from nothing.

Also, the wand can be tricky on lower lashes. The curved design is great for the upper lash line but makes precision application on the bottom lashes awkward. You'll want a separate tool for that.

**Who should buy this**

- **The curl-challenged**: If your lashes droop by noon with every other mascara, this is worth trying.
- **The budget-conscious beauty lover**: You're getting performance that rivals $25+ mascaras at a drugstore price.
- **The all-day wearer**: If you need mascara that survives work, workouts, and everything between, this holds up.

**Skip it if** you prefer lightweight, barely-there formulas — this has a noticeable presence on the lashes. Or if you hate waterproof-adjacent formulas and don't want to deal with stubborn removal.

**Three questions shoppers ask**

*Does it smudge on oily eyelids?* I tested on combo skin with no primer. Minor transfer after 8+ hours, but nothing a light powder under the lower lash line won't fix.

*How many coats before clumping?* Three is the sweet spot. Beyond that, you're pushing it. The formula is buildable but not infinite.

*Does it work on short lashes?* Yes, primarily because the wand curve lets you get right at the root and wiggle upward. That's where the curl starts.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want salon-worthy curl without the salon appointment or the prestige price tag. **Skip it if** your lashes are beyond stubborn or you hate tenacious formulas.

This is a 4.2/5 product. The curl technology is genuinely impressive, the price is right, and the performance rivals far more expensive options. The packaging is unremarkable, and the curl hold isn't universal — but for most people, this is a winner.
