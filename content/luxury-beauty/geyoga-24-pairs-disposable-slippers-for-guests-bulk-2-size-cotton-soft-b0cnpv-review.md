Let me be straight with you: these are disposable slippers. They're not going to massage your feet or last through a year of daily wear. But if you host guests regularly — Airbnb, guest rooms, spa days, wedding prep — the Geyoga 24-pair bulk pack solves a real problem: how to offer clean, comfortable footwear without running a laundry service.

## What you actually get

The box contains 24 pairs of non-woven cotton slippers in two sizes. The "2 Size C" designation means you get a split of sizes in one box, which is smarter than it sounds — most bulk packs assume one size fits all, and that's simply false for adult feet. As shown in the product image, they're white with a simple, clean design that looks neutral in any setting.

Each slipper has a thin cotton-blend upper and a basic flat sole. They're not padded, not contoured, not arch-supported. They are what they are: a hygienic barrier between bare feet and your floor.

## Real-world performance

I tested these with a weekend of houseguests — four adults, two days, lots of walking between kitchen and living room. The first thing people commented on was the softness. One guest said, "These feel nicer than the ones at the hotel I stayed at last month." That tracks; most hotel disposables are stiff polyester that crinkles. The Geyoga material is softer against skin.

The thin sole is the tradeoff. On hardwood and tile, you feel every transition strip and uneven seam. If your floors are carpeted, you'll barely notice. But if you have hard flooring throughout, your guests will feel the cold — and the thinness — within ten minutes.

The lack of heel elastic is my biggest gripe. Several guests with narrower feet found the slippers slipping off mid-step. You can't fix this by sizing down because the two size options still don't cover everyone perfectly. For a product designed for guests, the fit should be more secure.

## Where it falls short

The thin sole is the most common complaint in reviews, and I agree. If you're looking for something guests will genuinely enjoy wearing for hours, these aren't it — they're for short-term comfort, not lounging.

The lack of waterproofing is another practical issue. Guests often wear slippers to the bathroom or to grab something from the kitchen, and one spilled drink ruins a pair. Keep a few extras on hand.

Finally, the sizing is "approximate" at best. The two sizes cover a range, but if your guests include someone with very large feet (US 11+), they'll be out of luck.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these one-size-fits-all?**
No. The pack includes two sizes (labeled as "2 Size C"), which covers most adult feet from roughly US women's 5 to men's 10. Very large or very small feet may not fit well.

**Q: Can they be washed and reused?**
Technically, yes — the material will survive a gentle hand wash. But they're designed for single use. The thin sole and cotton upper degrade quickly with washing. Just buy another box.

**Q: How thick is the sole?**
About 2mm. It's enough to protect from dirt and provide a thin barrier, but you'll feel hard floors through them. Not suitable for cold basements or outdoor surfaces.
