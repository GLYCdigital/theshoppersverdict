**The mascara that promises "BANG" — and mostly delivers.**

Benefit's Badgal BANG has been a Sephora staple for years, but the Amazon version (B0B8MT8MP2) is where smart shoppers grab it with Prime shipping. At 4.4 stars from 220+ ratings, this isn't a runaway favorite — it's a solid performer with a few quirks worth knowing before you pull the trigger.

**What's in the box**

You get the standard 0.3 oz travel-size tube (the one most reviews reference) with Benefit's signature oversized hourglass brush. The wand is noticeably bigger than drugstore options — 15 bristle zones that grab evenly without the spiky clumping you get from cheaper rubber wands. The formula itself is a gel-based buildable texture that starts wet and sets within 60 seconds.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this on a 14-hour day that included a commute, client meeting, and gym session. First coat: impressive length with natural separation. Second coat: the volume kicked in — lashes looked visibly thicker without spider-leg clumping. The "36-hour wear" claim is marketing hype for most people, but I got a solid 12 hours before any flaking appeared near the outer corners.

The brush is the real hero here. That hourglass shape curves to match your lash line, so you're not fighting the wand to reach inner lashes. One pass does what most mascaras need three coats to achieve.

**What buyers consistently praise**

"I've tried every high-end mascara — this is the first that doesn't smudge on my oily eyelids," one verified reviewer notes. Another echoes the volume focus: "My lashes are practically invisible without mascara, and this makes them look like I'm wearing falsies."

The gel formula is the common thread. Unlike wax-based mascaras that flake by lunch, this stays flexible through the day. Several reviewers with sensitive eyes reported zero irritation, which aligns with the ophthalmologist-tested claim on the packaging.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest complaint across reviews: this isn't waterproof. Heavy tears, rain, or steamy weather cause raccoon eyes. Benefit markets a separate waterproof version, but the standard Badgal BANG isn't it. If you need something for weddings or pool days, look elsewhere.

Second issue: the formula dries stiff. By hour 10, lashes feel crunchy — not painful, but noticeable if you rub your eyes. Layering more than two coats amplifies this. One reviewer sums it up: "Great for volume, but I wouldn't wear it for an overnight shift."

**Who should buy this**

- **The volume seeker**: If your lashes are thin or straight, this gives dramatic payoff without extensions.
- **The all-day worker**: Nurses, teachers, retail — anyone who needs mascara that survives a shift without flaking into their eyes.
- **The makeup minimalists**: One coat replaces the "primer + mascara" routine. It's a time-saver.

Skip it if you need waterproof performance, prefer a natural "my lashes but better" look, or hate stiff-feeling formulas.

**FAQ**

*Does this work on short, straight lashes?* Yes — the hourglass brush lifts and curls at the base. Pair with an eyelash curler for maximum effect.

*How does this compare to Too Faced Better Than Sex?* Badgal BANG gives more length and less volume. BTS is thicker and heavier. Choose based on your priority.

*Is the travel size worth it vs. full size?* Yes. The 0.3 oz tube lasts 3-4 months with daily use. Mascara has a 6-month shelf life anyway, so you're not losing value.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want dramatic, clump-free volume that survives a full workday. **Skip it if** you need waterproof protection or prefer featherlight formulas. At this price point, it competes with prestige mascaras twice the cost — and wins on performance per dollar.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — a solid daily driver with one significant flaw (the waterproof issue). Worth it for the volume payoff alone.
