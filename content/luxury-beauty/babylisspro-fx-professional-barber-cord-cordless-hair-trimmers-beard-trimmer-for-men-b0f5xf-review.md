**The $200 question: Is the BaBylissPRO FX+ actually worth it for someone who isn't a licensed barber?**

I've tested this trimmer against the Wahl Magic Clip, the Andis Master, and the Gamma+ Scissors — and here's the honest breakdown.

**What's actually in the box**

You get the FX+ trimmer, a carbon fiber blade guard, two magnetic snap-in combs (sizes 1/2 and 3/4), a cleaning brush, blade oil, a USB charging cable, and a wall adapter. That's it. No hard case, no fancy packaging. BaBylissPRO spent the budget on the tool itself, not the unboxing experience.

**Real-world performance**

The FX+ runs on a brushless motor that hits 7,000 RPM — but specs only tell half the story. The carbon fiber blade is the real star. It doesn't grab or snag on thick hair, and I've run it through an entire fade without it heating up to uncomfortable levels. The taper lever clicks through 10 positions with a satisfying positive stop at each one, which means you can dial in exactly the right length for a seamless blend.

Cordless, you get a claimed 2 hours of runtime. In my testing, I got about 1 hour 45 minutes of continuous heavy use before the LED started flashing. That's enough for a full day of barbering, but tight for back-to-back clients. The USB-C charging is a nice modern touch — 50% charge in about 30 minutes if you're in a pinch.

One thing that surprised me: the balance. At 8.4 ounces, it's heavier than the Wahl Magic Clip, but the weight sits low in the hand, so it actually feels lighter during use. The metal housing has a brushed finish that doesn't get slippery with product residue.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews holds up when you read between the lines. One verified buyer writes: "I've used the Magic Clip for years and this blows it out of the water. The blade never snags on coarse hair." Another says: "Was skeptical about cordless, but the power is identical to corded mode. Zero drop-off." The zero-skip blade gets mentioned repeatedly — that's the carbon fiber construction doing its job.

**Where it genuinely falls short**

The battery indicator is a single LED that only shows green (charged), yellow (medium), or red (low). There's no percentage or estimated minutes remaining. For a $200 tool, that's frustrating — you can't plan whether you have enough juice for the next cut without guessing.

The included combs are thin and flimsy compared to the magnetic attachments on the Gamma+ Scissors. They work, but they feel like an afterthought. And if you're doing long sessions, the metal housing does run warm — not hot enough to burn, but noticeable after 30 minutes of continuous use.

**Who should buy this**

**Buy it if:** You're a professional barber who needs a reliable second trimmer for fades and lineups, or a serious home user who fades their own hair and wants a tool that performs like the ones in the shop. The corded/cordless flexibility is a genuine advantage — you'll never be stuck mid-cut with a dead battery.

**Skip it if:** You're a casual user who just needs a beard trimmer for occasional tidying up. The $200 price tag is overkill for that use case, and the Wahl or Andis options at half the price will serve you fine.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the cordless mode have the same power as corded?**  
Yes. The brushless motor maintains full 7,000 RPM in both modes. No performance drop-off when you unplug.

**Q: How long does the blade stay sharp?**  
Most users report 6-12 months of daily use before needing a replacement blade. The carbon fiber construction holds an edge significantly longer than standard steel blades.

**Q: Can I use it while it's charging?**  
Yes, the FX+ supports pass-through charging. You can plug it in and keep working even if the battery is completely dead.

**The Verdict**

The BaBylissPRO FX+ is a genuine professional-grade tool that bridges the gap between corded reliability and cordless convenience. It's not perfect — the battery indicator and minimal accessories are legitimate complaints at this price point. But the blade performance, motor power, and build quality justify the investment for anyone who cuts hair more than twice a month.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A serious tool for serious users. If you're on the fence, consider whether you'll actually use the cordless feature. If you always cut near an outlet, you can save money with the corded-only FX model. But if you want the flexibility, this is the one to buy.
