**The DKNY Be Delicious Eau de Parfum is the grown-up version of a classic.** If you remember the original green apple EDT from the early 2000s, this EDP iteration answers the biggest complaint fans had: it actually lasts. But "grown-up" also means the sharp, juicy apple bite mellows faster than you might expect. Here's the full breakdown.

**What's in the box:** You get the perfume spray in DKNY's signature apple-shaped bottle. The EDP comes in a clear glass apple with a green cap that mimics a stem and leaf. It's heavier than it looks in product photos — the base is substantial, so it won't tip over on your vanity. The box includes the standard 100ml (3.4 fl oz) size, which at daily use should run you 6-8 months. No travel atomizer or extras included, which is typical for this price tier.

**Performance and scent profile:** This is a fresh floral with a fruity opening. Top notes are green apple, cucumber, and grapefruit. The heart brings in rose, lily of the valley, and magnolia. Base notes are sandalwood, musk, and white amber.

That first spray is *glorious* — crisp, tart apple with a green, almost stemmy quality that cuts through summer heat. It genuinely smells like biting into a cold Honeycrisp. Within 30 minutes, the apple softens into the floral heart. That's where the scent shifts from "unique" to "pleasant but familiar" — the rose and musk combination is well-blended but doesn't break new ground.

Longevity is the headline improvement. I got 7 hours on skin (pulse points, no reapplication), and it lingered on a wool coat for two days. That's a significant upgrade from the EDT's 3-4 hour lifespan. Projection is moderate — you'll get compliments in conversation range, but it won't announce your entrance.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The freshness factor comes up constantly. One reviewer noted, *"This is my third bottle — it's the only perfume that doesn't give me a headache, and I get asked what I'm wearing every single time."* Another echoed the value angle: *"I was worried the EDP would be too heavy, but it's still light and clean. Lasts all day at work."* Multiple reviews mention the bottle surviving drops — that heavy glass construction is doing real work.

**Where it falls short:** The sillage disappointment is real for some. If you're used to heavy-hitting orientals like Flowerbomb or La Vie Est Belle, this will feel whisper-quiet. One verified purchase put it plainly: *"Smells amazing but I wish it projected more. After two hours, I have to put my wrist to my nose."* Also, the cucumber note — which sounds refreshing — can read slightly watery on certain skin chemistries. That's a body chemistry gamble, not a formula flaw.

**Who should buy this:**
- **The office professional** who needs a clean, non-offensive scent that won't trigger coworker allergies
- **The summer fragrance hoarder** looking for a fresh daily driver that outperforms citrus colognes
- **The DKNY loyalist** who loved the EDT but wanted more staying power

**Skip it if:** You prefer sweet gourmands, need beast-mode projection, or want a scent that evolves dramatically over the day — this stays in one lane.

**FAQ:**
**Q: Is this the same as the original DKNY Be Delicious?**
A: No. Same DNA, but the EDP concentration means more fragrance oils. It lasts roughly double the EDT and sits slightly warmer on skin.

**Q: How many sprays should I use?**
A: Two to three. One on each wrist and one on the chest. It's not a heavy scent, but overspraying won't fix the projection — it'll just waste juice.

**Q: Does it work for evening wear?**
A: It's better as daytime or spring/summer wear. For a date night, you'd want something with more depth — this leans fresh and clean, not sensual.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5 — Buy it if you want a signature fresh scent that actually lasts. Skip it if you need heavy projection or dramatic dry-down evolution.**

As shown in the product image, the bottle's apple shape is iconic — it looks great on a shelf and even better in your hand. At this price point, you're paying for the upgraded concentration, not just the name. It's a safe blind buy if you enjoy fresh, floral, or fruity scents. Just don't expect the apple to stick around all day — it's the opening act, not the headline.
