## Where It Falls Short

Two real downsides, and neither is a dealbreaker, but both are worth knowing.

**First: sizes are travel-friendly, not full-size.** This is a discovery set, not a replacement routine. Most pieces will last two to four weeks with daily use. If you’re buying this as a primary skincare regimen, you’ll be reordering sooner than you’d like. As a gift or travel companion, it’s perfect.

**Second: fragrance is present in several formulas.** It’s light and spa-like — think cucumber and white tea, not grandma’s perfume — but if the recipient has reactive skin, they should patch test before going all-in. One reviewer mentioned: *“I had mild redness around my cheeks for the first two days. It settled down, but I’d tell sensitive-skinned friends to start slow.”*
