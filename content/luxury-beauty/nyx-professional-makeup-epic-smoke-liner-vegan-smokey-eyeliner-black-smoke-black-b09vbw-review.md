## The smoke eye shortcut that actually works

Most "smokey eye" products promise drama and deliver smudged disappointment. The NYX Epic Smoke Liner takes a different route: it's a dual-ended pen where one side lays down a rich, gel-like line and the other blends it into that lived-in, smoldering look. As shown in the product image, the black side has a felt tip and the opposite end houses a sponge applicator — simple, functional, and refreshingly low-tech.

## What you actually get

The package contains one 0.04 oz pen in Black Smoke, which is a deep, neutral black without any blue or green undertones. The felt tip is firm enough to get a sharp wing but has enough give to glide along the lash line without tugging. The smudger side is a dense foam sponge, not the flimsy brush you see on cheaper dual-enders.

There's no primer included, no special remover — just the pen. That's fine, because the formula does the heavy lifting.

## Performance and real-world wear

Here's where the Epic Smoke Liner separates itself from the drugstore pack. The gel-cream texture applies wet, giving you roughly 30–45 seconds of blend time on the sponge side. That's the window to create your gradient. After about a minute, it sets into a transfer-proof film that stays put through humidity, tears, and the kind of day that makes you rub your eyes.

I tested this on a 10-hour shift plus a workout. The line didn't budge, didn't flake, and didn't fade into a gray shadow of itself. The smudged side held its diffused look without turning into a raccoon-eye situation. This is a genuine 24-hour formula that doesn't require a primer underneath.

## Where it falls short

The smudger sponge is the weak point. If you press too hard, it soaks up the product and deposits it unevenly, leaving patchy spots. The fix is to work in light, patting motions — but that's a technique curve you'll need to climb.

Second, the set time is a double-edged sword. That 60-second window is generous for blending, but if you're a slow worker or make a mistake, you're not wiping this off cleanly. It requires an oil-based remover; water-based wipes will smear it into a mess.

Finally, the felt tip isn't the finest on the market. If you're looking for a hair-thin precision line for graphic liner looks, this isn't your pen. It's built for smoke, not calligraphy.

## Who should buy this

- **The smokey eye novice** — this removes the guesswork and the need for three separate brushes.
- **The all-day wearer** — this survives what most liners can't.
- **The clean-beauty shopper** — vegan, cruelty-free, and doesn't compromise on performance.

Skip it if you want precise, intricate liner work or if you're impatient with a learning curve. And if you hate the idea of a sponge that needs gentle handling, you'll find that frustrating.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this really waterproof?**  
A: It's water-resistant, not fully waterproof. It survives sweat, tears, and rain, but heavy rubbing or a swim will break it down. For most daily wear, it's effectively permanent.

**Q: How do I clean the smudger sponge?**  
A: Wipe it on a tissue after each use. The product dries and can build up, so a quick wipe keeps it functional. Don't wash it with soap — that breaks down the sponge density.

**Q: Does it work on the waterline?**  
A: Technically yes, but the felt tip is a bit thick for the lower waterline. It's better on the upper lash line and outer corner. The smudger works well on the lower lash line for that smoked-out underside.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a foolproof, all-day smokey eye in one tool. **Skip it if** you need precision liner or hate working with a learning curve.

The NYX Epic Smoke Liner earns its 4.6/5 score. It's not perfect, but it solves a problem — the messy, time-consuming smokey eye — with an elegant, affordable solution. The pros far outweigh the cons, and it's a staple worth having in your kit.
