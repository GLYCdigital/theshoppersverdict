If you've been scrolling past jelly nail sets because they look like a gimmick, let me stop you: this is not the watery, streaky mess you're imagining. The beetles 6 Jelly Colors Gel Nail Polish Set delivers exactly what the category promises — translucent, glossy color that looks like stained glass on your nails. But this is a gel polish, which means you're signing up for a specific routine, not just a swipe-and-go experience.

## What You Actually Get

The set ships with six 0.5 fl oz bottles: pink, peach, purple, blue, green, and a clear-ish white. That's roughly 15-20 manicures per bottle if you're doing standard two-to-three coat applications. No base coat, no top coat, no lamp — just the six colors. As shown in the product image, the shades are genuinely translucent, not just pastel versions of opaque colors. The packaging is standard beetles fare: sturdy square bottles with the color-coded cap, and the brush is flat with a rounded tip that covers a medium-width nail in one pass.

## Performance and Real-World Use

I tested these with a 48W LED lamp, curing each coat for 60 seconds as directed. The formula is noticeably thinner than beetles' cream shades, which is expected for jelly polishes. The self-leveling is genuinely impressive — brush strokes disappear within seconds, and even my non-dominant hand application looked smooth after curing.

The catch: you need three thin coats minimum. Two coats leaves a visible nail line that reads as "dirty" rather than "sheer," especially on the pink and peach shades. The purple, blue, and green build to a more opaque jelly in two coats, but the lighter shades need the full three. If you're someone who hates waiting between coats, this will test your patience.

Wear time was solid — I got 12 days without chipping, and removal was standard gel soak-off (10 minutes in acetone with foil wraps). No staining on my natural nails after removal, which surprised me given the intensity of the purple and blue.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews reflects a consistent pattern. One reviewer noted, "The jelly effect is real — these look like glass beads on your nails, not like regular polish." Another mentioned, "I was worried the colors would look cheap, but they're actually sophisticated in person." The most common praise centers on the color depth and the lack of that "cloudy" look cheaper jelly polishes get after curing.

## Where It Falls Short

Honestly, the biggest issue is the shade inconsistency. The pink and peach are significantly sheerer than the purple and blue. If you're planning a multi-color jelly manicure, you'll need to adjust coat counts per nail to get uniform opacity — that's annoying, and it's a real design flaw. Also, no top coat included is a genuine omission. Jelly polishes are sticky and prone to scuffing without a proper top coat, and standard drugstore top coats don't play well with this formula. Budget for beetles' own top coat if you don't already have one.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The self-care enthusiast** who enjoys the ritual of gel application and has an LED lamp already
- **The trend-driven nail artist** who wants jelly finishes for accent nails or full manicures without salon prices
- **The undecided beginner** — this is a forgiving formula for learning gel application, since the sheer nature hides imperfections better than opaque polishes

Skip it if you're looking for one-coat coverage or if you don't own a curing lamp. Gel polish is a commitment, and this set won't change that.

## FAQ

**Do I need a special lamp for this polish?**
Yes. Any LED lamp with 36W or higher works. Curing time is 60 seconds per coat. UV-only lamps take longer — around 2 minutes — and the results are less reliable.

**Can I use this without a base coat?**
You can, but I wouldn't. Jelly polishes are prone to staining lighter colors on natural nails, and a base coat extends wear time by several days. It's a $10 addition that makes this set perform closer to salon quality.

**How do I remove this without damaging my nails?**
File the top coat to break the seal, then use acetone-soaked cotton wrapped in foil for 10-12 minutes. The jelly formula actually lifts easier than beetles' cream shades — I didn't need any scraping, just gentle pushing with a cuticle stick.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want genuine jelly effects with good staying power, you have a lamp, and you're willing to layer three coats for the lighter shades. The blue and purple alone are worth the purchase.

**Skip it if:** You want quick, single-coat application or you're expecting uniform opacity across all six shades. The shade inconsistency is real, and it will frustrate perfectionists.

At a 4.2/5, this is a strong entry in the jelly gel category — just go in knowing the lighter colors demand more patience. For the price per bottle, it's a reasonable experiment for anyone curious about the jelly trend.
