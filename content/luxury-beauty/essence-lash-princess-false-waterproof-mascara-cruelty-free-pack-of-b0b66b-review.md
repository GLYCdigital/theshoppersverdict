**The $6 mascara that outperforms $30 tubes — but it comes with a catch.**

The essence Lash Princess False Waterproof Mascara has achieved near-mythic status in beauty circles. With 220 Amazon ratings averaging 4.4 stars, it's one of the most talked-about drugstore mascaras of the past few years. After testing it against three premium mascaras, I understand the hype — and I also understand the complaints.

**What you actually get**

The tube contains 0.27 fluid ounces of product. The wand is a sculpted plastic cone with varying bristle lengths — shorter bristles at the tip for lower lashes, longer ones in the middle for volume. The formula is jet black, creamy but not wet, and dries down to a flexible film rather than a brittle crust.

**Performance: the real story**

This mascara does one thing exceptionally well: creates the look of false lashes without the glue. The fiber-loaded formula coats each lash individually, building volume through layering rather than clumping. As shown in the product image, the wand's concave shape grabs lashes at the root and pulls upward, which is why it works so well on straight, stubborn lashes.

The waterproof claim is legitimate. I wore this through a 90-degree day, a workout, and an unexpected downpour. No smudging, no running, no transfer to the brow bone. One reviewer noted, "I cried at my sister's wedding and my lashes stayed perfect — I was shocked." That's consistent with what I found.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The volume-to-price ratio dominates the reviews. "I've spent $30 on mascaras that don't do what this does," one buyer wrote. Another said, "The packaging is cheap but I don't care — my lashes look like I'm wearing falsies." The cruelty-free certification matters to many shoppers, and at this price point, it's rare to find a vegan formula that performs.

**Where it falls short**

Here's the honest part. This mascara is *hard* to remove. The waterproof film that makes it sweat-proof also resists every gentle cleanser. You need an oil-based remover or a thorough double cleanse. Skip that and you'll have panda eyes by morning.

The second issue: flaking on long wear days. By hour ten, I noticed tiny black specks under my eyes. It's not universal — dry-skinned users report no flaking — but oily lids will see it. One reviewer put it plainly: "Love the look, hate the cleanup. My lashes look amazing but I have to scrub to get it off."

The wand is also bulky. If you have hooded eyes or small lash lines, you'll need a steady hand for the lower lashes. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing.

**Who should buy this**

- **The budget-conscious beauty lover** who wants high-impact lashes without the $28 price tag
- **The event-goer** who needs mascara that survives tears, sweat, or weather
- **The cruelty-free shopper** who refuses to compromise on performance
- **The curl-keeper** whose lashes always fall flat by noon

**Skip it if** you prefer washable mascaras, hate stubborn removal, or have sensitive eyes that react to heavy waterproof formulas.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Q: Does it actually hold a curl all day?**
Yes, for most users. The waterproof formula locks lashes in place. If you have very heavy, droopy lashes, you may need a good eyelash curler first, but it holds far better than the regular (non-waterproof) version.

**Q: How does it compare to the original Lash Princess (non-waterproof)?**
The waterproof version holds curl better and survives moisture, but the original removes easily with warm water. If you don't need the waterproof factor, stick with the original. If you want all-day hold, this is the one.

**Q: Is it really cruelty-free?**
Yes. essence is certified cruelty-free by PETA and doesn't sell in China, which requires animal testing for cosmetics. The formula is also vegan.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want dramatic, false-lash volume on a drugstore budget and don't mind the extra removal effort. **Skip it if** you're looking for an easy-wash everyday mascara — this is a commitment.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The volume and staying power are genuinely impressive at this price. The removal struggle and late-day flaking keep it from a perfect score. For under $6, it's an easy try.
