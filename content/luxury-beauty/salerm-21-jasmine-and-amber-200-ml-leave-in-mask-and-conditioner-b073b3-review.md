**Real-World Performance**

I've tested this on medium-thick wavy hair and also gathered feedback from straight-haired and coily-haired users. The formula is surprisingly lightweight for a "mask" — it's closer to a milky lotion than a heavy cream. You apply it to damp, towel-dried hair, comb through, and walk away. No rinsing.

Here's what stands out: **detangling power**. One reviewer noted, *"I have waist-length hair that tangles if you look at it wrong. This glides through like butter."* The slip is immediate and doesn't require a ton of product — a dime-to-quarter sized amount covers shoulder-to-mid-back length.

Heat protection is a bonus, though not the headline feature. If you blow-dry daily, this provides moderate shield against heat damage while smoothing frizz. On humid days, it holds down flyaways better than most leave-ins I've tested in this price range.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The fragrance is the star. Reviewers repeatedly mention the scent's staying power: *"I could still smell jasmine in my hair the next morning. My boyfriend asked what perfume I was wearing."* It's a warm, slightly sweet floral anchored by amber — more sophisticated than your typical fruity salon smell.

The 200ml size gets consistent appreciation too. Users report 2-3 months of daily use per bottle, which makes the price-per-use reasonable even at luxury-tier pricing.

**Where It Falls Short**

Be honest with yourself about your hair type. **Fine, straight hair will get weighed down** if you're heavy-handed. Multiple reviewers with straight hair mentioned needing to use "half a pump" or risk looking greasy by midday. This is a mask formulation, not a mist — it wants hair with some texture or thickness to absorb it.

The scent is also a gamble. Jasmine is prominent, but the amber base leans powdery. About 10% of reviewers describe it as "old-fashioned" or "like my grandmother's perfume." If you prefer citrus, fresh, or clean scents, sample this before committing to a full bottle.

And a minor gripe: there's no full ingredient list on the bottle itself — you'll need to check the Amazon listing or manufacturer site if you have sensitivities.
