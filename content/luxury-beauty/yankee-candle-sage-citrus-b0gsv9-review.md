Some candles promise a fresh home but deliver a headache in a jar. The Yankee Candle Sage & Citrus walks that line carefully — and mostly lands on the right side. After burning through two of these over the past month, here's what you're actually getting for your money.

## What's in the Box

You get the standard Yankee Candle large jar: 22 ounces of wax, roughly 4.5 inches tall, with the classic glass container and a metal lid that seals well between burns. The wick is cotton-core and straight out of the box, it's ready to light without any initial trimming. No surprises here — if you've bought a Yankee before, you know the packaging drill.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The scent profile is where this candle earns its keep. The opening is bright, zesty citrus — think grapefruit and lemon zest rather than orange candy sweetness. Underneath sits an herbal sage note that keeps things grounded and green. The combination reads more expensive than it is, closer to a niche home fragrance than a grocery-store candle.

Scent throw measures solidly medium. In a 12x14-foot living room, I noticed the fragrance within 15-20 minutes of lighting. It's noticeable but not overpowering — you won't be coughing when you walk in the door, but you'll know someone's burning a candle. In larger open-concept spaces, you'll need to be closer to the candle to catch it, which is worth keeping in mind.

Burn quality is consistent. My first candle lasted 48 hours across roughly 30 burns, with even pooling across the top and minimal tunneling. The second one hit 52 hours. That's in line with Yankee's stated 45-55 hour range. Soot buildup on the jar lip was minimal — I trimmed the wick to a quarter-inch before each burn, which makes a real difference. Skip that, and you'll see black residue within a few hours.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The Amazon reviews at 4.0 stars across 200 ratings tell a clear story. The most common praise centers on the scent itself. One reviewer put it plainly: "This is the first citrus candle that doesn't smell like a bathroom cleaner. The sage makes it feel sophisticated." Another noted, "My husband hates most candles but he actually asked me to light this one again." The longevity of the fragrance — both on first burn and the scent lingering after extinguishing — comes up repeatedly.

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest part. The sage note is the interesting bit, and it fades within the first hour of each burn. What's left is a pleasant but fairly straightforward citrus scent. If you're buying this specifically for the herbal complexity, you'll be disappointed by hour two.

The jar also runs hot. The lower third of the glass becomes uncomfortable to touch after about 90 minutes of burning. That's not unusual for large jar candles, but it means you can't set it on untreated wood or plastic surfaces without a barrier. Yankee's own instructions mention this, but it's worth flagging.

At roughly $25-28 per candle depending on where you buy, you're paying a premium over comparable large jars from Bath & Body Works or DW Home. The quality justifies some of that, but not all of it.

## Who Should Buy This

This candle fits three specific buyers:

- **The citrus skeptic** who normally avoids lemon-scented anything but wants something fresh without smelling like a cleaning aisle
- **The living room host** who wants a welcoming, neutral scent that won't clash with food smells during dinner parties
- **The home office worker** who wants a subtle, non-distracting background fragrance that doesn't demand attention

Skip it if you want a long-lasting herbal or woodsy candle — the sage simply doesn't carry the whole burn. Also skip if you're on a tight budget; the price-to-wax ratio is steep.

## FAQ

**How long does the scent actually last?**
Each burn gives you a solid 3-4 hours of noticeable fragrance. After extinguishing, the scent lingers in a small room for 1-2 hours. The citrus lasts longer than the sage, which fades first.

**Is this candle safe for bedrooms or small spaces?**
Yes, but it's strong for tiny rooms. A bathroom or small bedroom will feel heavily scented within 30 minutes. You might want to burn it in shorter sessions — 30-45 minutes is plenty for small areas.

**Does it have a clean burn?**
It burns cleanly with proper wick maintenance. Trim the wick to 1/4 inch before every lighting. Doing that, I saw no significant soot or black smoke. Skip trimming, and you'll get soot streaks on the jar within a few burns.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5 — Buy it if you want a fresh, sophisticated citrus scent that doesn't scream "lemon cleaner" and you're willing to pay for Yankee's reliable burn quality. Skip it if you're after the herbal sage note as the star — it's a supporting player here, not the lead.** The candle delivers exactly what it promises for the first hour and a solid, pleasant citrus experience after that. It's not revolutionary, but it's dependable, and sometimes that's exactly what you want in a home fragrance.
