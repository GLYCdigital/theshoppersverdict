**Where It Falls Short**

The honesty section. At 3mm thick, these are visibly chunkier than your standard elastic. If you're going for a sleek, polished chignon or a barely-there ponytail, they'll show. You'll need an extra twist to get a snug fit, which slightly defeats the "effortless" promise for that hair type. And unlike fabric-covered ties, these don't have any texture, so if your hair is very slick, you may need to wrap twice to get the same security.
