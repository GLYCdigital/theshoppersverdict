**What you're actually getting:** A single 50 fluid ounce bottle of Softsoap Antibacterial Liquid Hand Soap in White Tea and Berry. It's a refill, so there's no pump — you'll need an existing dispenser. The bottle itself is functional but nothing special; it's designed to be poured, not displayed. The active ingredient is benzalkonium chloride (0.13%), which kills 99.9% of common bacteria — that's the FDA-compliant standard for OTC antibacterial soaps since triclosan was phased out.

**Real-world performance:** The formula lathers well — not overly thick, not watery. It rinses clean without that squeaky, stripped feeling some antibacterial soaps leave behind. **Where it falls short — honestly:** First, the antibacterial ingredient switch. If you're expecting the old triclosan formula, you'll be disappointed. Skip it if you're a scent purist who wants fragrance-free, or if you don't already own a dispenser and want all-in-one convenience.
