**The Erinde 10 Color Eyeshadow Palette: A $20 Palette That Thinks It's Luxury**

Here's the thing about budget eyeshadow palettes: most are either chalky disappointments or glitter-bomb disasters. The Erinde 10 Color Eyeshadow Palette sits in a rare middle ground — it's genuinely good, with a few honest caveats. With a 4.2-star average across 210 ratings, it's clearly earning its keep in makeup bags. But is it *your* next purchase? Let's break it down.

**What You Actually Get**

The palette arrives in a slim, rose-gold compact that's surprisingly sturdy for the price point. No cheap creaking hinges here. Inside, you get 10 shades — a mix of six mattes and four shimmers — that run warm-neutral: think soft taupes, rose golds, bronze, and a deep espresso for definition. Each pan holds a solid 1g of product, totaling 10g (0.35 oz) for the palette. That's comparable to higher-end options at a fraction of the cost.

As shown in the product image, the shade arrangement is logical: light-to-dark mattes on the left, shimmers on the right. It's a beginner-friendly layout that makes building a look intuitive.

**Performance: Where It Shines (and Where It Doesn't)**

I tested this palette three ways: bare lids, over primer, and with a damp brush for the shimmers. Here's the honest breakdown.

The mattes are the stars. They're soft, finely-milled, and buildable without turning patchy. The shade "Cocoa" — a warm matte brown — blended out effortlessly in the crease with zero fallout. That's rare at this price. Pigmentation is consistent across all six mattes; no duds hiding in the pan.

The shimmers are where opinions will split. The formula is more "satin with sparkle" than full-on metallic. Applied with a dry brush, you'll get a subtle sheen. Apply with a finger or a damp brush, and the intensity jumps notably. This isn't a flaw — it's a preference. If you like foil-level shimmer, you'll need to work for it. If you prefer everyday wearable shine, you're set.

One thing that surprised me: longevity. I wore this for 10 hours without primer, and there was minimal creasing. Even the shimmers stayed put without migrating into my crease.

**What Real Buyers Are Saying**

The 210 ratings tell a consistent story. One verified buyer wrote, *"I was skeptical at this price, but the mattes blend like my $50 palettes. The shimmer needs a finger, but the color payoff is gorgeous."* Another noted, *"Perfect for travel — compact and everything I need in one place."*

The criticisms are equally consistent. Multiple reviewers flagged the lack of a mirror as a real inconvenience. One put it bluntly: *"Great palette, but I have to bring a separate mirror when I travel. That's annoying."* Another mentioned, *"The lighter shimmer shades barely show on deeper skin tones — they're better as inner-corner highlights."*

**Where It Falls Short**

Honesty requires naming the tradeoffs. First, no mirror — for a palette marketed toward travel, that's a miss. Second, the shade range is limited. If you're cool-toned or prefer colorful looks, this isn't your palette. It's unapologetically warm-neutral. Third, the shimmer formula's need for a finger or damp brush might frustrate beginners who expect one-and-done application.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The minimalist traveler**: One compact palette that covers matte base, transition, crease, and shimmer — this is your all-in-one.
- **The warm-neutral lover**: If you live in bronze, rose gold, and taupe, this palette will serve you daily.
- **The budget-conscious beginner**: The mattes are forgiving, the shades are foolproof, and you won't cry if you drop it.

**Skip it if**: You need a mirror in the palette, you prefer cool-toned or colorful shadows, or you demand maximum metallic intensity from shimmers without extra effort.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is the Erinde palette cruelty-free?**
A: The brand states its products are not tested on animals. That said, they don't carry Leaping Bunny certification, so if that's a hard requirement, verify directly with the seller before ordering.

**Q: How does it compare to higher-end palettes like Urban Decay or Too Faced?**
A: The mattes are surprisingly comparable — blendability and pigmentation are close. The shimmers are where you'll notice the price difference; they're softer and less intense. For the price difference, it's a fair trade.

**Q: Will these shades work on deep skin tones?**
A: The deeper mattes (espresso and dark bronze) will absolutely show up. The lighter shimmers work best as highlights or lid shades under a deeper crease color. If you're very deep-toned, the two lightest mattes may read as wash-out on their own.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, warm-neutral palette with genuinely good mattes and don't mind using a finger for shimmers. **Skip it if** you need a mirror, prefer cool tones, or expect high-impact metallic from a dry brush.

The Erinde 10 Color Eyeshadow Palette delivers 80% of what luxury palettes offer at 20% of the price. It earns its 4.2 stars — not perfect, but a smart buy for the right shopper.
