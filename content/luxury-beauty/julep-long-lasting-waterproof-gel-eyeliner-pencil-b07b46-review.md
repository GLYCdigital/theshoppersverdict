## The eyeliner that actually stays put — mostly

You know the drill. You spend ten minutes perfecting a wing, step outside, and by lunch it's raccoon city. The Julep Long-Lasting Waterproof Gel Eyeliner Pencil exists to end that cycle. With a 4.1-star average across 204 ratings, it's not perfect — but for anyone with oily lids or a tendency to rub their eyes, this is a serious contender.

## What you actually get

The pencil itself is a twist-up gel formula with a built-in sharpener hidden in the cap — a small detail that matters more than you'd think. No separate sharpener to lose, no wasted product from shaving off half the pencil. The tip starts blunt, but one pass through the cap sharpener gives you a precise point for tightlining or winged looks.

As shown in the product image, the shade range skews classic: deep blacks and browns. If you're looking for jewel tones or pastels, this isn't your pencil.

## Performance: the good, the bad, the blink

The formula is genuinely creamy — not waxy or stiff like many drugstore gel pencils. It glides across the lash line with zero tugging, which makes it forgiving on mature eyelids or sensitive skin. The pigment payoff is immediate and buildable; one pass gives a soft definition, two passes deliver a true black.

Here's the catch: it needs time to set. For about 30 to 60 seconds after application, the gel is still moveable. Blink too fast, and you'll transfer product onto your crease. It's a minor adjustment, but if you're rushing out the door, it can bite you.

Once set, though, it's a different story. Multiple reviewers confirm this survives workouts, rain, and long shifts. One buyer wrote: *"I wore this through a 14-hour hospital shift and a shower mishap. Still intact. My old liner would've been gone by lunch."* Another noted: *"Finally, an eyeliner that doesn't betray me on oily lids."*

## Where it falls short

The twist-up mechanism deserves a warning. It's stiff initially, and several buyers report the pencil breaking if they twist too aggressively. Go slow, twist only a millimeter at a time. This isn't a product that rewards haste.

The shade range is the other weakness. It's essentially black and brown — practical, but uninspired. If you want a pop of color, look elsewhere.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** You have oily eyelids, live in humid climates, or need eyeliner that survives long days. It's also excellent for contact lens wearers who deal with watery eyes.

**Skip it if:** You're a "set it and forget it" applicator who doesn't want to wait 60 seconds before blinking. Or if you want colorful, creative eyeliner options.

## FAQ

**Is this actually waterproof?**
Yes, it's water-resistant and smudge-proof once set. It withstands tears, sweat, and rain. What it won't survive is vigorous rubbing — no eyeliner should be expected to.

**How do I remove it?**
You'll need an oil-based remover or cleansing balm. Water-based makeup remover won't cut it. A double cleanse is non-negotiable.

**Will it work for tightlining?**
Yes, especially after sharpening. The creamy formula deposits pigment right at the waterline without dragging. Just give it those 60 seconds before you blink fully.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you've been betrayed by smudged liner one too many times. **Skip it if** you can't be bothered to wait for the setting time or need vivid colors.

At a 4.2/5, this is a solid, workhorse eyeliner that delivers on its core promise: it stays. The learning curve is minimal, the payoff is real, and for anyone who's tired of touching up by noon — that's worth the price alone.
