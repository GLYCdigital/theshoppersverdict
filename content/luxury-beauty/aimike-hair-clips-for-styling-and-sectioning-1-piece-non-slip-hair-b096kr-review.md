**What You Actually Get**

One clip. That's it. AIMIKE sells this as a single piece, not a multi-pack. What you get for that price is a molded, one-piece design — meaning no metal springs, no hinges, no moving parts that can rust, snap, or catch your hair. The material is a matte-finish polymer with a curved jaw profile that's wider than your average duckbill clip. As shown in the product image, the interior has a series of interlocking teeth that create that aggressive non-slip bite.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

This is where the AIMIKE earns its keep. The grip is genuinely serious. I've tested clips that claim "non-slip" and then slide right out of thick, freshly washed hair. This one doesn't. The teeth bite into the section and hold it — even under the heat of a blow dryer or while you're wrestling with a round brush. Buyers consistently mention this. One reviewer noted, "I have very thick, heavy hair and this is the only clip that actually stays put." Another said, "Used it for a full highlight session — sections stayed exactly where I put them for 45 minutes."

The single-piece construction means no hinge to loosen. A traditional jaw clip's spring weakens after months of use; this design can't. It's durable in a way that cheap clips simply aren't.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

- **Grip strength**: The #1 reason people buy this. It holds.
- **No hair snagging**: The smooth, molded edges don't catch or pull when you remove it.
- **Versatility**: Works for sectioning during cuts, colors, and also as a finishing clip for sleek styles.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the price-per-clip is steep. For a professional cutting or coloring service, you typically need four to six clips to section a full head. At this price per single clip, that adds up fast — you're better off buying a budget multi-pack for heavy-duty salon work.

Second, the grip is almost *too* strong. On fine or delicate hair, leaving this clip in for extended periods can leave a temporary kink or crease. If you're using it on freshly curled or styled hair, keep it in only briefly.

Third, the size is fixed. There's one size — it's great for medium to thick hair, but if you have very fine, thin hair, the jaw might feel overly large and cumbersome for small sections.

**Who Should Buy This**
