## Honest Downsides You Should Know

No product is perfect, and this one has two real limitations. First, these are dry-use only. The velvet material doesn't respond well to water — it clumps, and the texture degrades. If you're someone who likes to dampen sponges for foundation application, look elsewhere. These are for powder, period.

Second, cleaning requires patience. Machine washing will ruin the velvet pile and leave you with a flattened, useless puff. Hand-washing in lukewarm water with a gentle cleanser, then air-drying flat, is the only way to maintain the texture. It's not difficult, but it's a step some buyers might skip — and they'll notice the difference after a few weeks.

## FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask

**How often should I wash these?**
Every 5–7 days if used daily. Use a mild soap or brush cleanser, lukewarm water, and squeeze gently — don't wring. Air-dry flat on a towel. With eight in the set, you can rotate and wash in batches.

**Will these work with cream products?**
Technically yes, but poorly. The velvet absorbs cream and becomes matted. Stick to powders for best results and longest lifespan.

**Are these true to the color in photos?**
