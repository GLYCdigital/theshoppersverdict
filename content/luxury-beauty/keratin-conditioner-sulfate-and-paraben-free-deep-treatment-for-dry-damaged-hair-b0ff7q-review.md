With hydrolyzed keratin sitting near the top of the formula — not relegated to the bottom like a marketing afterthought — this is a legitimate deep treatment masquerading as a daily conditioner. ## What You’re Actually Getting

The 16.9-ounce pump bottle delivers a thick, rich cream that feels more like a mask than a rinse-out conditioner. As shown in the product image, the packaging is clean and functional — no fussy jars, no tubes that split at the seam. The pump is the unsung hero here: it meters out a generous dollop without the “one more pump, oh no, too much” dance you get with squeeze bottles.

The formula skips sulfates and parabens entirely, which matters if you've invested in keratin treatments or color. Sulfates strip both, and this conditioner won't undo your salon work. What you get instead is a blend of keratin protein, argan oil, and shea butter — the holy trinity for dry, brittle strands.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this on two very different hair types: my own fine, wavy hair, and a friend’s thick, coarse, bleach-damaged mane. On my hair, a quarter-sized amount was plenty. It detangled instantly, and after blow-drying, my frizz was noticeably dialed down. Not sleek-salon smooth, but a solid 30% improvement over my usual drugstore conditioner.

On the coarse hair, the difference was dramatic. After three washes, the strands felt denser, less straw-like, and the breakage during brushing dropped significantly. This is where the keratin protein does its work — it fills in gaps in the hair shaft, smoothing the cuticle from the inside out.

One caveat: this is a *treatment*, not a lightweight daily conditioner. If you have baby-fine, flat hair, go easy. More than a quarter-sized amount and you'll be doing a midday re-wash. The product's own description warns against overuse, so follow it.

## Where It Falls Short

The scent is the biggest complaint, and it's legitimate. It's a strong, powdery-floral fragrance that lingers well into the next day. Some describe it as "old-fashioned" or "perfume-heavy." If you're sensitive to fragrance or prefer unscented hair care, this will be a dealbreaker.

The price point also sits above drugstore territory. You're paying for the keratin concentration and clean formula, but budget shoppers might balk. And as mentioned, fine hair needs careful portion control to avoid that weighed-down, almost-greasy feel by day two.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Color-treated or chemically processed hair**: The sulfate-free formula protects your investment while the keratin rebuilds what bleach and dye broke down.
- **Coarse, curly, or thick hair**: This is your sweet spot. The rich texture and protein content will smooth and define without fighting your natural texture.
- **Frizz fighters**: If humidity turns your hair into a halo of static, this will help. Consistently.

**Skip it if**: You have very fine, limp hair and hate any product that adds weight. Or if you're fragrance-averse. Either way, look elsewhere.

## FAQ

**Can I use this daily?**
Yes, but if your hair is fine, alternate with a lightweight conditioner every other day. The keratin can build up if you overdo it.

**Does it actually repair split ends?**
No conditioner can fuse split ends — that's a myth. What this does is smooth the cuticle so existing splits are less visible and future damage is prevented. Get a trim for the real fix.

**Is it safe for keratin-treated hair?**
Absolutely. It's sulfate-free, which is the #1 rule for maintaining a keratin treatment. The extra keratin protein is a bonus, not a conflict.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you have dry, damaged, color-treated, or frizz-prone hair and want a legitimate protein treatment without a salon appointment. **Skip it if** you have fine hair, hate strong scents, or are on a strict budget.
