Let’s cut the fluff: you don’t need a $30 toothbrush sanitizer. You need these $6 covers. I’ve tested dozens of oral-care accessories, and this 6-pack from a generic Amazon brand does exactly what it claims — keeps your Oral-B brush head clean between uses. But there’s a catch, and I’ll get to it.

## What You Actually Get

Open the package and you’ll find six slim, rounded caps — each about 2 inches long and 0.8 inches wide, sized to fit Oral-B’s cross-action, floss-action, and sensitive heads. The plastic is translucent (you can see the bristles through it, which is nice for tracking wear) and comes in assorted colors: blue, green, pink, and clear. No branding, no manual, no nonsense.

The design is simple: a snap-on hinge at the base and a small ventilation slit near the top. You press the cover over the brush head until it clicks, and the bristles are fully enclosed. The vent hole is the smart part — it lets air circulate so the brush dries faster, which matters because a damp brush head is a bacteria magnet.

## Real-World Performance

I used these on a daily-driver Oral-B Pro 1000 for three weeks. The fit is genuinely snug — the cover clicks into place with a satisfying snap and stays put, even when tossed into a toiletry bag or gym duffel. No rattling, no accidental pops. The vent hole does its job: after brushing, I shake the head, snap the cover on, and by the next morning the bristles are dry to the touch.

One thing I appreciated: the covers don’t trap water. Some cheap caps create a sealed, humid environment that actually *promotes* bacterial growth. These let the head breathe, which is the entire point of a dustproof cover.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest issue isn’t the build — it’s the compatibility claim. The listing title says “Compatible with Oral” (vague), but the fine print clarifies it only fits Oral-B replacement heads. If you have a Philips Sonicare, Braun, or any generic head, these won’t fit. I tested one on a Sonicare DiamondClean head and it didn’t click — it just sat loosely. So if you’re not an Oral-B user, skip this.

Second, the price-to-quantity ratio is fine, but you’re paying for plastic. There’s no antimicrobial coating, no UV feature, no travel clip. It’s a dust shield, not a sanitizer. If you expected more, you’ll be disappointed.

## FAQ

**Q: Will these fit Oral-B iO heads?**  
A: Yes, they fit iO, Pro, Smart, and Vitality heads. They won’t fit the older Triumph or cross-action *plus* heads with the wider base — check your model.

**Q: Can I use them with a manual toothbrush?**  
A: No. The opening is sized for the narrow neck of an electric brush head. A manual brush will be too thick.

**Q: How do I clean the covers?**  
A: Rinse with warm water and dish soap, then air dry. Don’t boil them — the plastic will warp.
