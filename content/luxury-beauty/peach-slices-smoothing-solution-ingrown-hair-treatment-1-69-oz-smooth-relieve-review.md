Let's be blunt: most "ingrown hair treatments" are overpriced alcohol in a fancy bottle. The Peach Slices Smoothing Solution is different — it's a legitimate Korean exfoliating serum dressed up with a clever delivery system. After 1,821 ratings averaging 4.4 stars, this $17.88 product has earned its place in the skincare conversation. Here's the breakdown.

**What you actually get**

The 1.69 oz bottle arrives with a 360-degree roller ball applicator — the same style used in luxury under-eye serums. You roll it directly onto affected areas, which means no cotton pads, no messy hands, and no wasted product. The serum itself is a clear, lightweight liquid that absorbs quickly without leaving that greasy film most body treatments leave behind.

The ingredient list reads like a chemistry lesson in exfoliation: BHA (salicylic acid) penetrates deep into hair follicles to break up the keratin plugs causing ingrowns, AHA works on surface texture, and PHA — the gentle cousin — makes it tolerable for sensitive skin. Niacinamide handles the red marks left behind after bumps heal, and licorice extract is there to brighten the dark spots that linger for weeks.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this on my legs after a particularly aggressive shave session, and on my boyfriend's neck where razor bumps have lived rent-free for a decade. Results were not instant — anyone promising overnight fixes is lying. But after four days of twice-daily application, the angry red bumps on his neck had visibly flattened. My legs, which typically look like a dotted swiss pattern post-shave, were smooth by day five.

The roller applicator deserves real credit here. As one verified buyer put it, "The 360 roller makes it so easy to apply on my bikini line without getting product all over my hands. I've tried every ingrown treatment out there and this is the only one I've repurchased." Another noted, "I was skeptical about the price but it cleared up my strawberry legs in about a week. The texture is so much better."

**Where it falls short**

The roller ball has a learning curve. On completely dry skin, it drags and tugs instead of gliding smoothly. You'll want to apply this right after showering while skin is still slightly damp — that's when the roller performs best. It's a minor workflow adjustment, but worth knowing before you buy.

More concerning: the bottle is smaller than it looks in photos. If you're treating both full legs, you'll burn through this in 3-4 weeks. At $17.88, that's roughly $5 per week for a targeted treatment. For larger body areas, that adds up. The licorice extract also gives it a faint herbal scent that isn't unpleasant, but it's not the fresh, clean fragrance most American body care products have.

**Who should buy this**

This is perfect for anyone who shaves, waxes, or epilates regularly and deals with recurring ingrowns, razor burn, or those rough "strawberry legs" bumps. It's also genuinely effective for men with sensitive neck skin — that's a demographic most Korean beauty products ignore. If you have sensitive skin, the PHA inclusion makes this gentler than straight BHA treatments, though you should still patch test first.

Skip it if you're looking for a full-body exfoliant or if you're prone to allergic reactions from botanical extracts. And if you're treating a large surface area daily, budget accordingly.

**Frequently asked questions**

*How often should I use this?* Start with once daily for the first week to let your skin adjust. If you tolerate it well, bump to twice daily. Don't use it on broken skin or active razor burns — wait until those heal first.

*Can I use this on my face?* Technically yes, but it's formulated for body skin which is thicker. If you're dealing with ingrowns from facial hair (common for men), use it sparingly and watch for irritation.

*Does it work on KP (keratosis pilaris)?* Multiple reviewers report improvement in chicken skin bumps on arms and thighs within two weeks. The BHA/PHA combination helps dissolve the keratin plugs that cause KP, but it's not a cure — you'll need consistent use.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You shave regularly, deal with ingrowns or razor bumps, and want a clean, targeted treatment that actually works without harsh chemicals.

**Skip it if:** You need a budget full-body exfoliant, have extremely sensitive skin that reacts to botanical extracts, or expect instant results.
