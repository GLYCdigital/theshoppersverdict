## Real-World Performance

The formula uses a combination of thermal polymers that form a protective barrier around each strand. In practice, this means your hair doesn't feel crispy or coated after application. ## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The weightless feel comes up repeatedly in positive reviews. Users with fine hair — the group that typically struggles with heat protectants — report no flattening or buildup. One verified purchase stated: "Finally a protectant that doesn't make my hair look like I haven't washed it in a week."

Color-treated hair users also report no fading, which is significant. Many heat protectants accelerate color loss, but the ghd formula appears gentle enough to avoid that issue.

The scent is the most common complaint. It's a strong floral fragrance that lingers after styling. Some users love it; others find it overwhelming. One reviewer wrote, "The smell is beautiful but very strong. If you're sensitive to fragrance, this might not be for you."

The price is the other sticking point. At $30+, this is firmly in luxury territory. You can find adequate heat protection for under $10, though you'll sacrifice the weightless feel and high-temperature protection.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have fine or easily weighed-down hair, use high-heat tools regularly, or have color-treated hair you want to protect. The lightweight formula genuinely outperforms budget alternatives for these specific needs.

**Skip it if:** You're sensitive to strong fragrances, style your hair rarely, or need a budget-friendly option. The protection is excellent, but you'll pay a premium for it.

## FAQ

**How much should I spray?**
Two to three pumps for shoulder-length hair, applied evenly through damp or dry hair before heat styling. More isn't better — oversaturating leaves hair damp and wastes product.

**Can I use it on dry hair?**
Yes. It works on both damp and dry hair, though dry application gives a lighter hold and faster styling time.

**Does it prevent all heat damage?**
No product completely eliminates damage. It reduces it significantly by raising the temperature threshold before damage occurs, but you should still avoid excessive heat passes.
