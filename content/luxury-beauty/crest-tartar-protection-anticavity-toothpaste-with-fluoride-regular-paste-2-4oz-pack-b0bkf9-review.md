Let's be honest: tartar control toothpaste is not the sexiest category in the oral care aisle. But if you're staring down a dental cleaning bill and your hygienist keeps muttering about "calcified deposits," this Crest formula deserves your attention.

I tested the 2.4oz tube over three weeks, alternating with my usual sensitivity paste, to see if the hype holds up. Here's the real story.

## What You're Actually Getting

The box contains one 2.4oz tube of Crest Tartar Protection in the "Regular Paste" formulation — not the gel, not the whitening variant. The active ingredient is sodium fluoride at 0.243% (0.15% w/v fluoride ion), which is the standard anticavity dose. The real differentiator is sodium hexametaphosphate, the ingredient that binds to tooth surfaces and prevents tartar crystals from forming.

As shown in the product image, it's a clean, no-nonsense tube design — white base, blue accents, clearly labeled ADA seal. No misleading "advanced whitening" claims plastered on the front.

## Performance: Does It Actually Work?

After three weeks of twice-daily brushing, I noticed two things immediately: my teeth felt smoother (less plaque grab) and my gum line looked less inflamed. The hexametaphosphate does what it claims — it doesn't dissolve existing tartar overnight, but it demonstrably slows new buildup.

The texture is a standard paste — not too foamy, not too gritty. It spreads well and doesn't dry out mid-brush like some budget pastes. The mint flavor is noticeably fresher than Crest's basic Cavity Protection but lacks the aggressive burn of their whitening line. It leaves a clean, neutral finish — no lingering chemical aftertaste.

One thing that surprised me: the fluoride uptake. My teeth felt less sensitive to cold air by week two, which aligns with the anticavity claims. That said, if you have exposed roots or existing sensitivity issues, this won't replace a dedicated sensitivity formula.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be real about the downsides.

**Size matters**: At 2.4oz, this is a travel or trial size. A typical household of two adults brushing twice daily will burn through this in three to four weeks. You'll need the larger 6oz or family-size tube for ongoing use — check the price per ounce before committing.

**No sensitivity relief**: The regular paste formula does nothing for tooth sensitivity. If you wince at cold drinks or have receding gums, you'll need to alternate with a potassium nitrate formula. This isn't a flaw per se, but it's a limitation worth noting.

**Tartar isn't instant**: Some buyers expect a miracle. This prevents new buildup but won't remove existing tartar — that requires a professional cleaning. If you're looking to skip the dentist entirely, you'll be disappointed.
