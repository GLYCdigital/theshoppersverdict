Let's be honest: $57 for a hair mask is a serious commitment. But TRUSS Net isn't your average drugstore deep conditioner, and the 4.7-star average across 235 ratings suggests something worth investigating. ## What You're Actually Getting

The TRUSS Net Hair Mask arrives as a substantial tub of dense, pearlized cream. This is a concentrated professional treatment — you only need a coin-sized amount for shoulder-length hair, so the container stretches further than you'd expect. It's a protein-repair formula built around the brand's "Net" technology, which claims to rebuild damaged hair bonds from the inside out. Think of it as the professional cousin to drugstore bond-builder masks.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where this mask earns its reputation. The texture is thick without being greasy — it spreads easily through damp hair and doesn't leave that heavy "product coat" feeling. In my testing, the most dramatic results showed up after the second application: noticeably smoother cuticles, less frizz on humid days, and hair that catches light rather than looking dull.

The protein repair component is the standout. If you have highlighted, bleached, or heat-damaged hair, this genuinely helps. One reviewer put it simply: *"I've used Olaplex for years. This works just as well at half the price per use."* Another noted, *"My colorist asked what I changed because my ends looked healthier than she'd ever seen them."*

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.7-star consensus isn't accidental. Recurring themes across the 235 reviews:

- **Visible repair on damaged ends** — the most common praise, with multiple reviewers noting improvement after 2-3 uses
- **Longevity** — because it's concentrated, most users report months of use from one tub
- **Versatility** — works for curly, straight, fine, and coarse hair types when used correctly
- **Salon results at home** — numerous reviewers compare it favorably to $80+ salon treatments

## Where It Falls Short

This is a treatment, not a maintenance conditioner. If your hair is already healthy, you're paying for repair capabilities you don't need — and the protein content can actually make fine or low-porosity hair feel stiff or straw-like if you use it weekly without rotating in a moisturizing mask. Several reviewers mentioned this exact issue. The price is the other obvious hurdle: at $57, it's competing with Olaplex and K18 territory, and for casual users, a $15 drugstore mask will do 80% of the job.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have chemically processed, heat-damaged, or otherwise compromised hair and want professional-grade repair without salon visits. Also great if you're transitioning from bond-building treatments and want a more concentrated option.

**Skip it if:** Your hair is naturally healthy or fine, you're on a tight budget, or you prefer lightweight conditioners over richer treatments. If you only wash your hair once a week, the value proposition gets harder to justify.

## FAQ

**How often should I use this mask?**
For damaged hair, start with weekly applications for 3-4 weeks, then drop to every other week. If your hair feels stiff or brittle afterward, you're overdoing it — cut back and add a moisturizing mask to alternate.

**Is this safe for color-treated hair?**
Yes, and most reviewers use it specifically to extend color vibrancy. The protein helps rebuild the cuticle, which prevents color from fading as quickly. Just don't mix it with direct-dye colors without patch testing first.

**How does this compare to Olaplex No. 8 or K18?**
TRUSS Net sits between them. It's more reparative than Olaplex No. 8 for major damage but less "instant fix" than K18. The value advantage is real: you get more applications per dollar with TRUSS.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The TRUSS Net Hair Mask delivers exactly what it promises: serious repair for damaged hair at a fair price per use. It's not for everyone — healthy hair doesn't need this level of intervention, and the protein content demands careful usage — but for anyone fighting breakage, split ends, or post-bleach straw texture, this is one of the most effective treatments in its price range. Skip it if you're a casual conditioner user; buy it if your hair needs actual repairs.
