**The Chairman™ PRO isn’t just another foil shaver with a fancy name.** MANSCAPED took their best-selling face trimmer, added a dual-foil cutting system, and aimed it squarely at guys who want a razor-close shave without the razor burn. At 4.1 stars from 204 ratings, it’s not universally adored — but the pattern of praise is clear: this thing shaves *closely* when you use it right.

## What’s in the box

You get the shaver itself, a magnetic charging dock, USB-C cable, a cleaning brush, and a surprisingly sturdy travel pouch. The unit weighs 4.9 ounces — heavy enough to feel premium, light enough for one-hand control. The matte black zinc-alloy body has a knurled grip that works even with wet, soapy hands. The foil head is the star: a flexible, spring-loaded dual-foil assembly that pivots independently.

## Performance: the good, the bad, the blade-close

On a 1–2 day stubble, this is genuinely impressive. The foils sit at a 30-degree angle to the skin, which lets you shave against the grain without the tugging you get from cheaper foils. One verified buyer put it plainly: *“I’ve used Braun and Panasonic foils for a decade. This is the first one that matches my Braun Series 7 in closeness, for half the price.”*

Battery life holds up too. A full charge gives about 90 minutes — that’s 20+ shaves for most guys. The USB-C port is on the bottom, and the magnetic dock snaps the shaver into place with a satisfying click. In practice, the 15-minute quick charge is a lifesaver when you’re rushing out the door.

But here’s the honest tradeoff: the foil width is 1.6 inches, roughly 20% narrower than a Braun Series 9. That means more passes, especially on a full beard. One reviewer summed it up: *“Great shave, but I need 5 minutes instead of 3. If you’re a daily shaver with light growth, it’s perfect. If you’re clearing a month of beard, bring a trimmer first.”*

## What buyers consistently praise

The waterproofing is a genuine differentiator. You can use it in the shower with shave cream, and the foils rinse clean under running water in seconds — no brush needed for daily maintenance. The skin comfort is also a recurring theme. The foils are coated in a titanium-nitride layer that minimizes friction, and several reviewers with sensitive skin reported zero razor burn, even with daily use.

## Where it falls short

The narrow foil is the main complaint, but there are two quieter issues worth flagging. First, there’s no travel lock — the power button sits flush on the front, and a few buyers reported the shaver turning on in their bag, draining the battery. Second, replacement foils aren’t cheap. MANSCAPED sells a 2-pack for around $30, and you’ll need them every 6–8 months with regular use. That’s not a dealbreaker, but it’s a cost to budget for.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** You shave daily or every other day, want a close shave without the blade, and value a waterproof shaver you can use in the shower. It’s also a solid pick for travel — the USB-C charging and compact size beat any corded shaver.

**Skip it if:** You have a heavy, thick beard that needs a wider foil head, or you’re on a tight budget. A Braun Series 5 or 7 will cover more surface area per pass, and you can find those for similar money on sale.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this with shaving cream?**  
Yes — it’s IPX7 waterproof. Use it wet with gel, or dry. The foils rinse clean under the tap. Just don’t submerge it for more than 30 minutes.

**Q: How does it compare to a Braun Series 7?**  
The Chairman PRO is close in shave quality, but the Braun has a wider foil and a more aggressive cutting angle for dense beards. The MANSCAPED wins on price, USB-C convenience, and the waterproof design.

**Q: How long do the foils last?**  
With 3–4 shaves per week, expect 6–8 months before you notice a drop in closeness. Replacement foils are ~$30 for a 2-pack.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Buy it if you want a close, comfortable daily shave with modern charging and full waterproofing. Skip it if you have a dense beard that needs a wider cutting surface. It’s not perfect, but at this price, it’s one of the best foil shavers under $100 — just budget for replacement foils down the road.
