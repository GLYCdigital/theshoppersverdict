**What you actually get**

The box contains 24 individually wrapped bath bombs, each roughly 2.5 inches in diameter. That's important because some budget packs ship smaller bombs that barely cover the bottom of a tub. These are a solid size. Every bomb has a plastic capsule embedded in the center, sealed separately from the fizzing material, so the toy inside stays dry and clean. As shown in the product image, the bombs come in a rainbow of colors — pinks, blues, purples, greens — which adds visual appeal for kids picking their nightly bomb.

The toys themselves are small plastic figures — think tiny animals, dinosaurs, and vehicles. They're not high-quality collectibles, but they're not garbage-grade either. They're the kind of thing that ends up in the toy bin and gets played with for a week before being forgotten. That's about right for the price point.

---

**Real-world performance**

Drop one in a warm bath and you get an immediate fizz. The color disperses nicely and tints the water without staining the tub — I tested one in a white porcelain tub and it rinsed clean with no residue. That's a legitimate concern with colored bath products, so it's worth calling out.

The scent is where things get modest. It's there, but it's light and unmistakably synthetic. Think bubblegum and fruit, not spa-grade lavender. For kids, that's fine. For parents who want aromatherapy benefits, this isn't the product.

The fizz lasts about two to three minutes. That's on the shorter end. Some premium kids' bath bombs hold four to five minutes of active fizzing. If your child is the type who loves watching the show, they might be left wanting more. The tradeoff is that the shorter fizz means you're not waiting long for the toy reveal, which is really the main event here.

Skin irritation? I tested one on a 4-year-old with mildly sensitive skin — no redness, no itching. The ingredients are generally gentle, but they do include synthetic fragrance and colorants, so kids with known sensitivities should get a patch test first.

**FAQ**

**Are the toys safe for kids under 3?**
No. The toys are small and pose a choking hazard. The manufacturer recommends age 3 and up, and I'd stick to that. Also, the plastic capsule inside is rigid — not a hazard once removed, but not something a toddler should handle.

**Will the colors stain my tub or my child's skin?**
In my testing, no. The tub rinsed clean, and no staining occurred on skin. However, if you have an older tub with porous porcelain or natural stone, test one bomb in a small area first. The dyes are water-soluble but can linger on porous surfaces.

**How long does the fizz actually last?**
Two to three minutes in a standard-size tub. If your child drags out the reveal, the bomb will be fully dissolved before they find the toy. That's by design — the capsule floats to the surface once the surrounding material dissolves, so you don't have to dig for it.
