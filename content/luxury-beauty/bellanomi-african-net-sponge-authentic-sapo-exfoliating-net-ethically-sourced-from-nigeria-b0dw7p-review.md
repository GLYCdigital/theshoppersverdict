**FAQ**

*How often should I use the African Net Sponge?*
Start with twice a week for the first two weeks. Your skin needs to adjust. After that, most users settle into every-other-day use. Daily use is fine for most people once you've built tolerance, but listen to your skin.

*How do I clean the net sponge?*
Rinse thoroughly after each use and hang to dry. Once a week, wash it in the washing machine with your towels, or soak in a diluted vinegar solution for five minutes. The nylon weave doesn't absorb bacteria like a loofah, but regular cleaning extends its life.

*Will this work with any body wash?*
Yes, but it works best with liquid soaps. Bar soap tends to get caught in the weave and leave residue. A small amount of body wash goes a long way — you only need a dime-sized drop for your whole body.
