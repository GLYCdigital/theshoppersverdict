# Bath Spa Gift Basket Set for Women: Does the Enchanted Orchid Set Deliver a Real Spa Night?

## Performance and Real-World Testing

I used this set for a week of evening soaks. The **bath salts** are the star. They're coarse-ground Epsom salt, so they take about 30 seconds to fully dissolve in hot water — not instant, but acceptable. The magnesium content is legit for muscle relaxation; I noticed genuinely less tension after a 20-minute soak.

The **body wash** lathers well. A quarter-sized amount produces enough foam for a full body wash. It rinses clean without that squeaky, stripped feeling. The **lotion** is the weak link — it's hydrating but lightweight. If your skin is dry or flaky in winter, you'll want a heavier cream on top.

The scent deserves its own paragraph. Enchanted Orchid is a soft, white-floral fragrance with a hint of vanilla underneath. It's not soapy or powdery like many affordable orchid scents. It lingers on the skin for about two hours post-shower — noticeable but not cloying. Multiple reviewers described it as "calming" and "not too strong," which matches my experience.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has a few tradeoffs worth knowing:

1. **Fragrance intensity is moderate**. If you want a bath bomb-level scent explosion, this won't do it. The orchid is present but subtle. Some buyers specifically wanted stronger scent throw.
2. **The box is bulky**. It's gift-ready, which is great, but the shipping box is larger than the product. If you're mailing this, factor in shipping costs.
3. **Lotion is thin**. For very dry skin, you'll need supplemental moisturizer. It works fine for normal-to-combination skin.
