Most floss is an afterthought. You grab whatever's on sale, tolerate the waxed tape that snaps between tight teeth, and move on. Dr. Emma's woven, coconut oil-infused floss makes you reconsider that routine. It's priced like a luxury item, but the question is whether the experience justifies the premium. After testing it against the standard options, here's the honest breakdown.

**What you actually get**

The package contains a single 30-yard spool in a compact dispenser roughly the size of a matchbox. The floss itself is the star: a woven, expanding texture that starts flat and fans out as it slides between teeth. The coconut oil infusion is subtle, not flavored-sweet like mint floss—you get a faint, natural scent and a smooth glide. It's also PFAS-free and coated with natural candelilla wax, which matters if you're avoiding traditional petroleum-based wax coatings.

**How it performs in real use**

The expanding action is the differentiator. Standard floss slides through tight contacts, but this one grips plaque as it expands, pulling debris out rather than just pushing past it. On posterior molars—where most people struggle—it slid through without the "snap" you get with flat tape. The woven texture also means it doesn't shred on rough fillings or crowded teeth. One reviewer noted, "My teeth feel cleaner than after a professional cleaning," and while that's hyperbolic, the difference is tangible. The coconut oil layer means you don't need to saw aggressively; a gentle rock and glide does the job. I found it especially effective at removing the film that builds up along the gumline before brushing.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the 229 ratings, three themes dominate. First, the smooth glide: multiple reviewers mention it "slides like silk" and doesn't leave waxy residue behind. Second, durability: the woven construction resists breaking mid-floss, which is the most common complaint with cheap floss. Third, the clean feel: "I didn't realize my old floss was leaving residue until I tried this," is a recurring sentiment. The coconut oil infusion also gets credit for not triggering the gag reflex that mint-flavored floss causes for some users.

**Where it falls short**

The price is the biggest hurdle. At roughly $10–12 per spool, it's three to four times the cost of a comparable drugstore option. If you floss once daily, a spool lasts about two months—so it's not outrageous, but it's a deliberate splurge. The dispenser is also a weak point. The lid isn't hinged, and if you drop it on a tile floor, it pops off, spilling the spool. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you toss it in a gym bag. Finally, the coconut oil flavor is subtle to the point of being nonexistent—if you want fresh-mint breath after flossing, this won't deliver it.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the person who flosses daily and wants the ritual to feel less like a chore. It's also a smart choice if you have tight contacts or bridgework that shreds standard floss. Sensitive gums benefit from the smooth glide, and anyone who hates the waxy taste of traditional floss will appreciate the clean, neutral profile. Skip it if you're on a strict budget or floss occasionally—the premium won't justify itself for sporadic use.

**FAQ**

*Does the coconut oil actually do anything, or is it marketing?*  
The oil acts as a natural lubricant, which reduces friction and makes the glide smoother. It's not a whitening or antibacterial treatment, but the glide benefit is real.

*Is this floss safe for braces or permanent retainers?*  
Yes, the woven texture threads through orthodontic hardware better than flat tape. Just be gentle—the expanding action can catch on brackets if you pull too hard.

*How long does one spool last?*  
At 30 yards with standard daily use, expect 6–8 weeks. If you floss twice daily or have wide spacing, plan for closer to a month.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want floss that actually removes plaque rather than just sweeping between teeth, and you're willing to pay for the upgrade. The expanding woven texture and coconut oil infusion deliver a noticeably better clean.

**Skip it if** you're satisfied with your current floss or want maximum value per dollar—this is a luxury, not a necessity.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It earns the score. The performance justifies the price for daily flossers, but the flimsy dispenser and premium cost keep it from a perfect five.
