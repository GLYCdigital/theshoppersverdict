If you've ever watched a lash tech hand-fan volume lashes and thought "I could never do that," the TDANCE Easy Fan line was made for you. These pre-made fans bloom open the moment they hit adhesive — no crimping, no pinching, no 45-minute learning curve. But at 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, is it actually a salon-grade shortcut or just another gimmick? I tested them to find out.

## What's in the Box

Each tray holds 16 rows of pre-made volume fans, roughly 160-192 lashes depending on the diameter you choose. You're getting C and CC curls — the two most universally flattering curl types for natural-to-glam looks. The 0.05 and 0.07 diameters are your everyday wear; the 0.10 is for clients who want drama. As shown in the product image, the fans arrive neatly aligned, though the tray itself is utilitarian rather than pretty — this is a working tool, not a display piece.

## Performance and Build Quality

The "rapid blooming" claim is legit. When you dip these fans into adhesive, they open up like a flower — no manual separation needed. During my test, a full set of 0.07 C-curl fans took about 35 minutes, compared to 70+ minutes with traditional hand-fanning. The bases are thin enough to sit flush against the natural lash line, which is critical for retention.

Bonding time is genuinely fast. The fans grab the natural lash within 1-2 seconds, which means less adhesive evaporation and fewer "floaters" — those lashes that lift off the natural lash and fall out within days. Retention held strong through week two in my testing, with only normal shedding afterward.

The C curl gives a lifted, doll-like effect; the CC curl adds extra curl at the tip for clients with downward-pointing natural lashes. Both hold their shape through the blooming process, which isn't always the case with cheaper pre-made fans that collapse when wet.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Reviewers who've used this product highlight the same thing I noticed: the time savings. My full sets went from 2 hours to 1 hour 15 minutes."* Another mentioned, *"The mix of C and CC in one tray is genius — I can do the outer corners in CC and the rest in C without buying two separate trays."*

## Where It Falls Short

The tray packaging is the weak point. The fans aren't secured tightly, so they can shift during transit. You'll need to carefully realign them before your first set — not a dealbreaker, but annoying when you're prepping for a client.

More importantly, these fans perform best with the TDANCE-recommended adhesive (or a similar low-viscosity glue). If you use a thicker adhesive, the blooming effect is less dramatic, and you'll find yourself manually separating fans anyway — defeating the purpose. Stick with their suggested glue pairing.

One more thing: the 0.10 diameter is chunky. It's great for photo shoots or stage makeup, but for everyday clients, it can look heavy and uncomfortable. Most users will live happily in the 0.05-0.07 range.

## Who Should Buy This

**Lash techs** who want to cut set time without sacrificing quality are the primary audience — these will pay for themselves in saved labor within a few sets. **DIY enthusiasts** with steady hands and patience will also do well, especially if you've already mastered basic isolation. If you're brand new to lash extensions entirely, start with individual lashes first; pre-made fans still require clean isolation and proper adhesive technique.

**Skip this** if you're a tech who prefers building custom fans for every client — the pre-made look is consistent but not infinitely customizable. Also skip if you refuse to use anything but your current adhesive; the blooming behavior depends on it.

## FAQ

**Q: How many sets will one tray do?**
A: A full set typically uses 5-8 fans per eye (10-16 total), so one tray of 16 rows gives you 1-2 full sets depending on your client's natural lash density.

**Q: Can I use these with any lash glue?**
A: Technically yes, but the rapid-bloom effect works best with low-viscosity adhesives. Thicker glues won't trigger the same opening action, so you'll be back to manual fanning.

**Q: Do these feel heavy on natural lashes?**
A: The 0.05 and 0.07 diameters are lightweight and comfortable. The 0.10 is noticeably heavier — reserve it for clients who want maximum volume and have strong natural lashes.
