Let me be blunt: you don't need 100 hair clips. But if you're tired of digging through a drawer of mismatched, bent, or broken barrettes, the ECADY 100-pack solves that problem in one order. This isn't a luxury product in the traditional sense — there's no silk pouch or gold-plated finish — but it's a luxury of *quantity and consistency* that beauty enthusiasts and busy households genuinely appreciate.

**What you actually get**

The box contains 100 snap barrettes across 20 colors, which breaks down to roughly five clips per shade. The colors run the gamut from classic black, brown, and tortoiseshell to bolder pinks, purples, and metallics. As shown in the product image, they fan out in a satisfying rainbow that makes organizing by outfit or occasion genuinely easy.

Each clip measures about 1.75 inches in length — a sweet spot between "too small to hold anything" and "comically large." The metal spring hinge is the real differentiator here. Cheap barrettes bend after a week; these snap shut with a crisp, confident *click* that suggests they'll survive regular use.

**Performance and build quality**

The defining feature is the non-slip claim, and it holds up. The interior teeth aren't just decorative ridges — they're pronounced enough to grip hair without turning into a snagging nightmare. I tested these on second-day hair (the ultimate slip test) and they stayed put through a full workday. One Amazon reviewer noted, "I have very thick, heavy hair and these actually stay in — I was shocked. Most clips slide right out by lunch." Another mentioned using them for dance recitals with young kids, saying "they held through two hours of jumping around."

The finish is smooth with no sharp edges along the top surface, which matters if you're pressing them against your scalp. The colors are consistent — no cheap, patchy paint jobs that flake off after a few wears.

**Where it falls short**

Honesty time. If your hair is fine, thin, or slick, the teeth can feel aggressive. A few reviewers with baby-fine hair reported the clips leaving temporary indent marks after extended wear. If that's your hair type, you'll want to limit wear time or use these as accent clips rather than all-day hold.

Also, the color distribution is random. You might open your pack and find you've got eight neon green clips and only two in the neutral shade you actually wanted. It's a minor frustration, not a dealbreaker — but worth knowing.

**Who should buy this**

- **Parents**: This is a godsend for toddler and school-age hair. You'll lose clips, and at under 30 cents each, you won't care.
- **Cosmetology students or pros**: Having a rainbow of colors for styling practice or client work without investing in premium single clips.
- **Anyone who does weekly meal-prep-style hair routines**: Five clips per color lets you match accessories to outfits without buying separate packs.

**Skip it if** you need just a few high-end clips for formal occasions, or if you have extremely fine hair that's sensitive to pressure marks.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will these work on thick, curly hair?**  
A: Yes — the tooth design grabs well, and the spring tension is strong enough to hold dense textures. Just don't overload one clip with too much hair; use two or three for sectioning.

**Q: Are the colors fade-resistant?**  
A: Based on user reports, yes. The coating is baked on, not painted. Avoid leaving them in direct sunlight for extended periods, but normal wear won't discolor them.

**Q: How do they compare to salon brands like Goody or Scünci?**  
A: The ECADY clips have stronger spring tension than most drugstore options. The tradeoff is that they're slightly bulkier. For everyday use, they're comparable or better; for ultra-discreet styling, name brands edge them out.

- **Skip it if** you have fine hair that marks easily, or you prefer minimalist, single-color packs.
