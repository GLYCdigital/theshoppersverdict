Here's the honest truth about the Philips Norelco OneBlade 360: it's the closest thing to a "does everything" grooming tool that actually delivers. This isn't a razor, it's not a beard trimmer, and it's not a body groomer — it's all three, and it does each job respectably. With 229 Amazon ratings averaging 4.6 stars, the consensus is clear, but let's dig into whether it earns a spot in your routine.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the OneBlade 360 handle, one dual-sided blade (the 360 version, which Philips says lasts up to 4 months), three fixed-length combs (1mm, 3mm, 5mm), and a USB-A charging cable. No charging stand, no travel case — that's reserved for the pricier Pro model. The unit itself is lightweight at 4.2 ounces and IPX7 waterproof, so it rinses clean under the tap without worry.

## Real-World Performance

The headline feature is the 360-degree blade that pivots independently. In practice, this matters most on body grooming. Shaving your jawline feels like a standard OneBlade — close but not razor-close, typically a "5 o'clock shadow" look that's actually the point of this device. But run it over your knees, armpits, or chest, and the pivoting head follows contours that flat razors miss. One buyer put it simply: *"I've used it everywhere — face, chest, back, and even down there — and haven't nicked myself once. That's never happened with any other trimmer."*

The dual-sided blade cuts in both directions, which means you're not fighting against the grain as aggressively. For face use, it's genuinely irritation-free — I have sensitive skin and got zero razor burn, which is rare for electric trimmers. The 5-minute face setting is quick enough for a morning routine, though it won't give you a baby-smooth shave. That's by design; the OneBlade is meant to trim, not shave clean.

The combs snap on magnetically, which is satisfying and secure. The 1mm setting gives a clean stubble look, 3mm is a short beard, and 5mm is a medium stubble — you won't get a longer beard length than that, so beard growers need a separate trimmer.

## What Buyers Love

The versatility is the recurring theme in reviews. One verified purchaser wrote: *"Replaced my $200 beard trimmer and my $30 body groomer. This does both jobs fine, and I can use it in the shower."* Another praised the longevity: *"Had my first OneBlade for 3 years before upgrading. The blade lasted 6 months before I felt it dull, and replacement blades are cheap."*

The wet/dry capability also gets consistent praise. Using it with shaving cream in the shower cuts irritation further and makes cleanup effortless.

## Where It Falls Short

Battery life is the biggest compromise. You'll get about 60 minutes of runtime — good for a week of daily use, but the charging time is 60 minutes too. There's no quick-charge feature, so if you forget to charge it, you're waiting an hour. The Pro model includes a charging case that doubles as a travel pouch; that's a $20 premium you may want to consider if you travel frequently.

The fixed combs are another minor frustration. If you want a 2mm or 4mm length, you're out of luck — you're limited to 1, 3, or 5. It's a deliberate cost-cutting choice, but adjustable dials exist on cheaper competitors.

## Who Should Buy This

This is ideal for three types of shoppers:
- **The minimalist groomer** who wants one tool for face, chest, and nether regions without maintaining a drawer of devices
- **The sensitive-skin sufferer** who gets razor burn from standard electrics — the 360 blade genuinely reduces irritation
- **The shower shaver** who wants a fully waterproof unit that works with cream

Skip it if you need a clean, BBS (baby-butt-smooth) shave — this trims, it doesn't shave. Also skip if you maintain a longer beard (3+ inches); the combs top out at 5mm.

## FAQ

**Does the OneBlade 360 actually shave close?** No. It trims to a stubble length (about 0.4mm). It's designed to look like a controlled 5 o'clock shadow, not a clean shave. If you want smooth skin, use a traditional razor afterward.

**How often do blades need replacing?** Philips rates the dual-sided blade at 4 months of use. In practice, buyers report 3–6 months depending on frequency and hair coarseness. Replacement blades run about $15–20 for a two-pack.

**Can I use it in the shower?** Yes. It's IPX7 waterproof, and using it with shaving gel or cream in the shower is actually recommended for reducing friction. Just rinse it under the faucet after.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5 — Buy it if** you want one reliable, nick-free tool that handles face and body grooming without a cabinet full of gadgets. **Skip it if** you need a truly close shave or maintain a long beard.

The OneBlade 360 earns its high rating by doing three jobs competently with zero irritation, backed by cheap replacement blades and a waterproof build. It's not perfect — the battery life and fixed combs are genuine compromises — but for most men, this replaces three devices with one that works better than each of them individually. At its typical street price, that's a solid deal.
