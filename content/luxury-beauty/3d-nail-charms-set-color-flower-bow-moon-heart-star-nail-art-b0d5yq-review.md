As shown in the product image, you're getting a substantial assortment: 400+ individual charms in six distinct shapes. The breakdown matters more than the total count. The finishes vary within the kit — some pieces are glossy, some matte, and a handful have a metallic sheen. That variety is genuinely useful. A glossy red flower works for a night out; a matte pastel moon suits an everyday manicure.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The flat-back design is the standout feature. Many 3D charms have rounded bottoms that create a gap between the charm and your nail plate — that gap catches on hair, snags sweaters, and invites lifting. These charms sit flush, which means better adhesion and longer wear.

I tested these with standard gel topcoat and a no-wipe topcoat. With gel, they stayed put for nine days before the first one loosened. With regular polish and a quick-dry topcoat, expect four to five days of solid wear. That's respectable for 3D embellishments.

One thing worth noting: the charms are lightweight acrylic, not resin. That's a plus for comfort — you won't feel them catching on everything — but they're not as durable as resin. If you're hard on your hands (gardening, constant typing, gym workouts), some thinner pieces like the small stars may flex or crack under pressure.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these work with regular nail polish, or do I need gel?**
A: They work with both, but adhesion is significantly better with gel. With regular polish, apply a thick topcoat, press the charm in, then seal with a second topcoat layer. Expect 3-5 days of wear versus 7-10 with gel.

**Q: Are these safe for natural nails?**
A: Yes. They're acrylic, so they add minimal weight. Removal is straightforward — soak in acetone for 5-10 minutes and they slide off with an orange stick. No filing required.

**Q: How many charms actually arrive intact?**
