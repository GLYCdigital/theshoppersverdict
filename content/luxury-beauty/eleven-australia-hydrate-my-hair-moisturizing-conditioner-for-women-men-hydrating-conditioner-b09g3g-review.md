Let's be blunt: most conditioners in the salon-adjacent price range are 80% marketing and 20% silicones that coat your hair like plastic wrap. The ELEVEN Australia Hydrate My Hair Conditioner actually does what the label promises — but it's not magic in a bottle, and you should know exactly what you're buying before you hit checkout.

**What You Actually Get**

The bottle (300ml or 500ml, depending on your order) is professional-grade packaging — a sturdy pump dispenser that doesn't crack or leak in the shower. The formula is a light cream texture, not a thick butter. It spreads easily through mid-lengths and ends, and it rinses clean without that squeaky, stripped feeling.

Key ingredients worth noting: coconut-derived conditioning agents, vitamin E for antioxidant protection, and a plant-based complex that targets dehydrated strands. What's *not* in here matters too — no parabens, no phthalates, and no sulfates. This is a clean-ish formula that respects color-treated hair.

**Performance in Real Life**

I tested this on medium-thick, wavy hair that gets dry at the ends and oily at the roots by day two. Results: immediate slip. You can run a wide-tooth comb through conditioner-coated hair without tugging. After rinsing, your hair feels soft but not mushy — there's still body and movement.

The real test is day-three hair. This conditioner held up better than drugstore options I've compared it against. My ends stayed hydrated without the roots looking greasy. Heat styling also seemed to cause less frizz afterward, which suggests the moisture barrier actually sticks around.

One thing to note: if your hair is *severely* damaged — think bleach-blonde, over-processed, or heat-fried — this won't be enough on its own. You'll want to pair it with a weekly deep conditioning mask. This is a maintenance product, not a rescue product.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Scrolling through the 225 ratings, a few themes pop up repeatedly. "Finally a conditioner that doesn't weigh down my fine hair but still hydrates my dry ends" is a common refrain. Multiple reviewers mention the pump bottle as a genuine convenience — no fumbling with caps mid-shower.

One verified buyer wrote: *"I've spent hundreds on high-end conditioners and this beats most of them. My hair feels like I just left the salon after every wash."* Another noted: *"The scent is gorgeous — like a spa treatment — and it lasts all day without being cloying."*

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The fragrance is strong — a floral, coconut-forward scent that some reviewers describe as "luxurious" and others as "overwhelming." If you're sensitive to fragrance or prefer unscented haircare, this is a real issue.

The price point is also worth flagging. At roughly $20-25 for the 300ml size, it's positioned as a premium product. You're paying for the brand and the formulation, not just the ingredients. If your budget is tight, drugstore options like OGX or Maui Moisture will get you 70% of the results for half the price.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Color-treated hair**: The sulfate-free formula is gentle on color deposits
- **Wavy or curly textures**: The moisture balance defines curls without crunchy build-up
- **Fine-to-medium hair**: Surprisingly lightweight for a "hydrating" formula
- **Anyone who hates heavy conditioners**: This rinses clean, no residue

**Skip It If**

- You prefer fragrance-free haircare
- Your hair is severely damaged and needs intensive repair
- You're on a strict budget — there are cheaper conditioners that work fine

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this safe for daily use?**
A: Yes. The formula is gentle enough for daily washing, though most reviewers use it 3-4 times per week. If you have fine hair, daily use might be too much moisture — start with every other wash and adjust.

**Q: Does it work on curly hair?**
A: Generally yes, especially for wavy-to-curly textures (2A-3B). It provides slip for detangling without deflating curl pattern. Tight coils (4C) may need a heavier leave-in or cream afterward.

**Q: How does it compare to Olaplex or Kérastase?**
A: Olaplex is a bond-builder — it repairs broken bonds, not moisture. This is purely hydrating. Kérastase has more specialized formulas but costs 30-40% more. ELEVEN sits in a sensible middle ground: better than drugstore, less expensive than luxury French brands.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want salon-quality hydration without the salon price tag, especially for color-treated or wavy hair. **Skip it if** you need heavy-duty repair or prefer unscented products.

At 4.6/5 stars, this is a legitimate contender in the mid-range conditioner market. It's not a miracle worker, but it's honest, effective, and delivers consistent results. For most people with normal-to-dry hair, this is a solid upgrade from the drugstore aisle — just be prepared for that fragrance.
