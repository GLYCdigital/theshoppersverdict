Let's be honest: most cosmetic sharpeners are an afterthought. You grab the freebie from the beauty counter, it destroys your favorite eyeliner pencil, and you're back online ordering a replacement within a month. The Palladio Double Barrel 3-in-1 aims to be the last sharpener you buy. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it's clearly doing something right — but is it actually worth the space in your makeup bag?

## What You're Actually Getting

The Palladio is a dual-port sharpener with one opening for standard-size pencils (your typical eyeliner, lipliner, or brow pencil) and a second, larger port for jumbo pencils and gel liners. The body is metal with a stainless steel blade assembly that you can remove for cleaning — a detail that matters more than you'd think. The whole thing measures about 2.5 inches tall, roughly the size of a lipstick tube, and comes with a snap-on cap that keeps the blades covered when it's rattling around in your drawer.

## Performance: Where It Earns Its Keep

The stainless steel blades are the real selling point here. They're sharp enough to handle softer formulas — the kind that normally crumble and break apart in cheaper sharpeners — without gouging chunks out of your pencil. The dual barrels mean you're not forced to choose between your jumbo shadow stick and your everyday eyeliner. One sharpener does both, which is genuinely convenient.

The removable blade assembly is the feature I wasn't expecting to care about. With most sharpeners, wax and pigment buildup becomes a permanent part of the mechanism after a few weeks. You're left with a dull, sticky mess that shreds your pencils. Here, you twist off the top, pull the blade unit out, and rinse it under warm water. Two minutes of maintenance and it performs like new. That's a real upgrade over the disposable sharpeners most of us are used to.

## What Buyers Are Saying

The praise in the reviews is consistently about the blades. One reviewer notes, "I've tried every sharpener at Ulta and Sephora, and this is the only one that doesn't chew up my NYX pencils." Another mentions using it for three years without the blade dulling — a claim that seems plausible given the stainless steel construction.

The jumbo port gets specific callouts too. "Finally something that handles my jumbo eye pencils without shaving off half the product," writes one buyer. That's the exact problem most sharpeners have with oversized pencils — they either don't fit at all or they eat the pencil whole.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and the Palladio has two honest drawbacks. First, there's no grip texture on the body. If your hands are the slightest bit damp — which is common when you're sharpening a liner in the bathroom right after washing your face — the metal body can slip. You'll need to dry your hands first or brace the sharpener against a counter.

Second, the cover is a snap-fit, not a seal. Pencil shavings and pigment dust can work their way out during travel. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, but if you're throwing this loose into a leather makeup bag, be prepared for some dark flecks at the bottom.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The jumbo pencil user**: If you own any thick gel or shadow sticks, you already know the struggle. This handles them without wasting product.
- **The "buy it once" shopper**: The stainless blades and cleanable assembly mean this should outlast several drugstore sharpeners.
- **The makeup artist or enthusiast**: If you sharpen pencils daily, the durability justifies the price.

Skip it if you only use twist-up pencils or if you're happy with the free sharpeners from beauty retailers. This isn't a miracle tool — it's just a well-made version of a basic necessity.

## FAQ

**Will this fit my NYX Jumbo Eye Pencil?**
Yes. The larger port is designed for jumbo pencils up to roughly 12mm in diameter. NYX Jumbo, Urban Decay 24/7 Glide-On, and similar formats fit without issue.

**Can I clean it without taking it apart?**
You can wipe the openings with a cotton swab, but the removable blade assembly makes proper cleaning much easier. Twist off the top, pull the blade cartridge out, rinse, and dry.

**Is it safe for soft gel pencils?**
Yes — the sharp blades create a clean cut rather than dragging through the formula. Soft pencils that crumble in cheap sharpeners come out intact here.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're tired of sharpeners that destroy expensive pencils, or if you own jumbo-format liners that nothing else fits. **Skip it if** you exclusively use twist-up pencils or you're happy with disposable options.

This is a straightforward, well-executed tool that does its job without fuss. The 4.6-star rating holds up. It's not glamorous, but neither is sharpening a pencil — and this one makes the whole task less annoying.
