**The $6 perfume oil that outperforms $80 sprays (with one catch)**

Al Rehab is a Saudi Arabian fragrance house that's been quietly dominating the budget perfume oil market for decades. Their Soft rollerball — a 6ml concentrated oil — has become a cult favorite among fragrance enthusiasts, and at roughly $6, it's cheaper than most fast-food lunches. But is it actually good, or just cheap?

Here's what I found after wearing it for two weeks straight.

---

**What you actually get**

The package is minimal: a slim glass rollerball bottle with a gold cap, containing 6ml (0.20 oz) of concentrated perfume oil. That's small — about the size of a lip gloss — but this is an oil, not a spray. You only need a few drops, and 6ml typically lasts 2-3 months with daily use.

The rollerball mechanism is smooth and precise. As shown in the product image, the ball sits flush in the neck, and a single swipe deposits a thin, even layer of oil. No spritzing into the void, no overspray on your clothes.

**Performance: the real story**

Perfume oil works differently than alcohol sprays. There's no alcohol flash-off, so the scent develops slowly on your skin over about 15 minutes. The opening is a soft, clean musk with a hint of powdery floral — think freshly laundered sheets with a subtle sweetness underneath.

The real surprise is longevity. Most budget sprays vanish within two hours. This oil held steady for six hours on my wrist and pushed past eight on my collar. It's not a projection monster — someone standing next to you will smell it, but they won't smell you from across the room. That's actually a feature for office wear.

**Where it falls short**

Be honest about the limitations. The scent is subtle — if you want people to notice your fragrance before they notice your shoes, this isn't it. It's a skin scent, not a statement.

The rollerball also requires a light hand. Press too firmly and you'll get a thick pool of oil that takes forever to absorb and can stain light fabrics. Apply, wait a minute, then dress.

One more thing: Al Rehab doesn't publish an official note breakdown for Soft. It's often described as a powdery musk with floral undertones, but if you're someone who needs to know exactly what you're wearing, that lack of transparency might frustrate you.

**Who should buy this**

- **Office workers**: The subtle projection won't overwhelm a cubicle or meeting room.
- **Travelers**: TSA-friendly size, no leaks, no glass spray bottle to break in your bag.
- **Fragrance layering enthusiasts**: It's a fantastic base that pairs well with vanilla, amber, or citrus scents.
- **Budget shoppers**: You get genuine performance for less than the cost of a coffee.

**Skip it if** you want strong sillage, prefer alcohol-based sprays, or need a fragrance that lasts through a 12-hour shift without reapplication.

**FAQ**

**Q: How many applications do you get from a 6ml rollerball?**
A: Roughly 150-200 swipes. With one application per wrist and neck, that's about 2-3 months of daily wear.

**Q: Does the scent change over time on skin?**
A: Yes. It opens as a soft musk, then develops a powdery floral note after about 15 minutes. It stays close to the skin the entire time.

**Q: Is this a feminine or masculine scent?**
A: It leans feminine due to the floral undertone, but the musk base makes it wearable for anyone. If you prefer something more neutral, Al Rehab's Choco Musk or Silver are better options.
