**ORIBE Travel Cote d'Azur Eau de Parfum, 0.33 fl. oz.**

Luxury beauty brands love to sell you "travel sizes" that are really just overpriced samples. The ORIBE Cote d'Azur travel spray, however, is a different animal — this is a legitimate 10ml (0.33 fl oz) eau de parfum that fits the actual purpose it claims. But whether it's *worth* it depends entirely on how you shop.

**What you actually get**

The bottle is a sleek glass mini with ORIBE's signature gold-tone cap, standing about 2.5 inches tall. It's an eau de parfum concentration (15-20% fragrance oils), not a weak eau de toilette. That matters because 10ml of EdP delivers roughly 80-100 sprays — expect 3-4 weeks of daily wear, not a weekend sample. The scent itself is the full Cote d'Azur experience: bright Calabrian bergamot and blackcurrant up top, a soft heart of neroli and jasmine, and a dry-down of white musk, driftwood, and amber. It's unisex-leaning-fresh, the kind of clean, Mediterranean scent that works year-round.

**Performance and real-world use**

I wore this for a week straight. Two sprays to the wrist and one to the neck gave me solid 6-hour longevity on skin, and the scent lingered on clothing into the next day. The sillage is polite, not loud — you'll get a compliment from someone in your personal bubble, not across the room. That's consistent with ORIBE's positioning: this is an intimate, skin-close fragrance, not a club banger.

The travel angle is legit. At 10ml, it clears TSA 3-1-1 rules with room to spare, and the glass bottle feels substantial — no flimsy plastic atomizer here. One gripe: the spray head has no protective cap, so it can depress and leak if you toss it loose into a bag. Keep it in the box or a zip pouch.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across 220 ratings, the 4.4-star average holds up. Owners repeatedly mention the portability and the scent authenticity. One reviewer wrote, *"Same scent as the full size — I didn't feel like I was getting a watered-down version."* Another noted, *"Perfect for my carry-on, and the spray actually atomizes well instead of dribbling."* The consistency with the full-size formulation is the most common compliment, which is the #1 thing that can go wrong with travel sprays.

**Where it falls short**

The price-per-ounce math is brutal. The full-size 75ml bottle runs roughly $180; this 10ml travel typically lands around $30-$40. That's roughly double the cost per ounce. You're paying for the convenience and the ORIBE name, not value. Also, if you're expecting the kind of projection you get from a niche house like Byredo or Le Labo, you'll be underwhelmed — this is a refined, office-safe scent, not a statement piece. And the lack of a cap is a genuine design oversight for a product literally named "travel."

**Who should buy this**

- **The frequent flyer** who already owns or loves the full-size Cote d'Azur and wants TSA-friendly backup
- **The scent-curious** who wants to test ORIBE's signature fragrance without committing $180
- **The minimalista** who prefers owning a few 10ml bottles to rotate rather than one giant flacon

**Skip it if** you're looking for a budget-friendly daily fragrance — this is a luxury product with a luxury price tag, and there are better-performing fresh scents at half the cost.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does the 0.33 oz bottle last?**
A: With 2-3 sprays daily, expect 3-4 weeks. It's a travel companion, not a monthly supply.

**Q: Is this the same scent as ORIBE's hair products?**
A: Yes — it's a complementary fragrance to the Cote d'Azur hair care line, sharing the signature bergamot, white musk, and driftwood notes.

**Q: Will it leak in my bag?**
A: The spray head can depress without a cap. Store it in the box or a small zip pouch to be safe.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want the authentic Cote d'Azur experience in a genuinely portable format and understand you're paying a premium for that convenience. **Skip it if** the price-per-ounce sting outweighs the portability benefit.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — An excellent travel-size rendition of a beautiful scent, docked only by the premium pricing and the missing spray cap.
