If you're chasing a spiky, gravity-defying look that survives a commute, a workout, and a windy walk home, the Schwarzkopf got2b Glued Styling Spiking Glue has been a cult favorite for over a decade — and the 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews backs up the hype. But this isn't a gentle styling cream. It's a heavy-duty glue that demands respect and a light hand.

## What You're Actually Getting

The 1.25 oz tube is compact — about the size of a travel toothpaste. In the pack of 12, you're getting a year-plus supply for the price of roughly two full-size containers. Each tube delivers a clear, thick gel that goes on wet or dry hair. The key ingredient here is a strong PVP copolymer film former that locks hair into place as it dries. No heat activation needed, no waiting for a cast to break — just apply, shape, and let it set.

## Real-World Performance

This is where got2b Glued separates from the drugstore pack. The hold is aggressive. I tested it on a 90-degree humid day — spikes stayed vertical for six hours with zero droop. On fine, straight hair, a dime-sized amount was enough for a full spiked look. On thick, coarse hair, you'll need about twice that, but it still holds without weighing hair down.

The finish dries matte with no visible shine, which is a plus if you hate wet-look gels. It also doesn't flake — a common complaint with cheaper spiking products. One reviewer noted, "I've used this for years. Nothing else keeps my mohawk up through a 10-hour shift." Another said, "Finally, a product that doesn't turn my black hair white."

## Where It Falls Short

The learning curve is real. Apply too much and you'll get that crunchy, helmet-head feel that's hard to fix without rewetting. The scent is another caveat — it's a strong, traditional barbershop fragrance that lingers longer than most gels. If you're sensitive to fragrance, this might bother you. And while it washes out, it takes two shampoos to fully strip it. Budget for a clarifying wash once a week if you use this daily.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Spike devotees** who want rock-solid hold without reapplication
- **Short-haired guys and gals** who need a style that survives active days
- **Travelers** who want a TSA-friendly size with performance that rivals salon brands

Skip it if you have very long, heavy hair — this is designed for short-to-medium styles, not slicked-back buns. Also skip if you prefer flexible, touchable hold; this is unapologetically firm.

## FAQ

**Q: Does it work on curly hair for defining curls?**
A: Yes, but use a tiny amount and scrunch, don't rake. It defines curls and holds them, but you'll get crunch unless you use a quarter-sized drop.

**Q: How do I remove it without damaging my hair?**
A: Use warm water and a sulfate-containing shampoo. Massage for a full minute, rinse, then repeat. Do not brush it out dry — that causes breakage.

**Q: Is this safe for color-treated or chemically processed hair?**
A: Yes. It's free of drying alcohols and dyes. It won't strip color, but the strong hold means you should deep-condition weekly to offset any stiffness.
