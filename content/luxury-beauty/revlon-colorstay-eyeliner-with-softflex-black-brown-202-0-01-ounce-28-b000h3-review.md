Let’s cut through the noise: finding an eyeliner that actually stays put without requiring a chemistry degree to remove is rare. The REVLON ColorStay Eyeliner with SoftFlex in Black Brown 202 has built a quiet reputation among 225 Amazon reviewers, holding a 4.5-star average. I put it through real-world testing — long workdays, gym sessions, and the occasional emotional movie scene. Here’s what I found.

**What You Actually Get**

The package contains one eyeliner pencil at 0.01 ounces (roughly 0.28 grams). It’s a standard twist-up-free pencil, meaning you’ll need a sharpener. The Black Brown 202 shade sits perfectly between espresso and true black — dark enough to define, soft enough to look intentional rather than severe. As shown in the product image, the pencil has a classic slim barrel design that’s easy to grip and control.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The SoftFlex formula is the star here. Unlike drugstore eyeliners that drag and pull, this one glides with a creamy consistency that sets within 30 seconds. Once it dries, it locks in place. I wore it through a 10-hour shift, a workout, and a rainy commute. No smudging under the eyes, no disappearing act at the outer corners.

The Black Brown 202 shade deserves specific praise. It’s more forgiving than pure black — mistakes are less noticeable, and it softens the eye without washing out lighter features. For brown-eyed users, it enhances without competing. For blue or green eyes, it provides definition without the harshness of jet black.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Scrolling through the reviews, a few themes emerge. One reviewer noted, “I’ve tried every high-end liner from $30 brands and this $8 one outperforms them all.” Another praised the staying power: “Survived a full day of work, a nap, and a workout — didn’t budge.”

The shade itself gets frequent shoutouts. “Black Brown is the perfect everyday color. It’s not as harsh as black but gives more depth than brown,” one customer wrote. Consistency and value come up repeatedly — people genuinely can’t believe the price point.

**Where It Falls Short**

Honesty requires balance. This isn’t waterproof — it’s water-resistant. If you rub your eyes aggressively or go swimming, it will fade. The formula also needs a moment to set; if you blink too quickly after application, it can transfer to your crease.

The lack of a built-in sharpener is a genuine annoyance. You’ll need a separate sharpener, and the pencil’s creamy formula means you’ll be sharpening more often than with harder pencils. Some users also note that the color builds up rather than hitting full opacity in one stroke — you’ll need two passes for a bold line.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is perfect for the everyday makeup wearer who wants reliable, long-lasting definition without the luxury markup. It’s ideal for:

- **Professionals** who need makeup that survives 9-to-5 plus commute
- **Sensitive-eyed users** — the formula is fragrance-free and gentle
- **Minimalists** who want one versatile shade that works for day and night
- **Budget-conscious shoppers** who refuse to sacrifice quality for price

Skip it if you need a fully waterproof liner for swimming or intense athletic activities, or if you prefer mechanical twist-up pencils that never need sharpening.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this smudge on oily eyelids?**
A: In my testing and in most user reviews, no. The SoftFlex formula sets firmly. However, if you have extremely oily lids, use a primer underneath for extra insurance.

**Q: How do you sharpen this pencil?**
A: Use any standard cosmetic sharpener. The creamy formula means you should sharpen gently to avoid breaking the tip. Some users recommend placing the pencil in the fridge for 5 minutes before sharpening.

**Q: Is Black Brown 202 suitable for waterline application?**
A: Yes, but with a caveat. It applies smoothly to the waterline and lasts several hours there, but it won’t survive heavy tearing or rubbing. For waterline-only wear, a waterproof formula might serve you better.
