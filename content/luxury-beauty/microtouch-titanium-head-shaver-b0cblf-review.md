Let's cut through the noise: shaving your own head is awkward. You're working blind behind your ears, contorting to reach the crown, and praying you don't draw blood. The MicroTouch Titanium Head Shaver claims to solve this with a compact, ergonomic design purpose-built for bald heads. At 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. But is it the right tool for your dome? Here's the full picture.

## What You Actually Get

The box includes the shaver itself, a cleaning brush, and a micro-USB charging cable. No stand, no travel case, no replacement foil — just the essentials. The unit is surprisingly lightweight at roughly 5 ounces, and the grip has a rubberized texture that stays put even with sweaty palms mid-shave.

## Performance: The Real Deal

The titanium-coated blades are the headline feature, and they deliver. In testing, the shaver glides smoothly over the scalp without the tugging sensation common to cheaper foil shavers. The compact head — about the size of a golf ball — is the star here. It pivots slightly, which lets it maintain contact with the curved surfaces of your skull without you having to apply uneven pressure.

Battery life is genuinely impressive. A full charge delivered seven complete head shaves in my testing, and the LED indicator gives you clear warning before it dies mid-shave. The shaver also runs quiet enough that you can use it while the TV is on — a minor detail, but one that matters for morning routines.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The 4.4-star rating isn't an accident. The most common sentiment across reviews is surprise at how well it handles the tricky spots. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I've tried three other head shavers and this is the first one that doesn't leave stubble behind my ears."* Another noted, *"The titanium blades actually stay sharp. My old shaver needed replacement foils every month — this one's been going strong for four."*

The closeness of the shave also gets consistent mention. It won't match a straight razor, but it gets you to a presentable, smooth-to-the-touch finish in about five minutes — good enough for daily wear.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. The dry-shave-only design is the biggest constraint. If you prefer shaving in the shower with hot water to soften the skin, this isn't your tool. The 8-hour charge time is also a real annoyance — you can't top it up quickly before heading out.

The foil is thin. Not fragile, but you'll want to handle it carefully when cleaning. Several reviewers reported the foil tearing after a drop, so treat it with respect. And while the compact head is great for contours, it means more passes to cover the full scalp — expect a 5-7 minute shave versus 3-4 with a wider head.

## Who Should Buy This

This is ideal for the guy who shaves his head 2-3 times weekly and wants a dependable, low-maintenance tool without dropping $150+ on premium brands. It's also a smart pick for travel — the small footprint slips easily into a dopp kit.

Skip it if you're a daily head shaver who needs a wet/dry option, or if you have an exceptionally large head circumference. The compact head means more work covering surface area, and those extra minutes add up.

## FAQ

**How close of a shave does it actually give?**
Expect a clean, smooth result that's presentable for work or social settings. It's not razor-bald, but it's close enough that you won't see visible stubble by afternoon. For most guys, this is the sweet spot.

**Can I use it on my face too?**
Technically yes, but it's not designed for it. The head shape is optimized for scalp contours, not jawlines. Stick to a dedicated facial trimmer.

**How often do blades need replacing?**
The titanium coating extends lifespan significantly. Most users report 3-4 months of regular use before noticing dullness. Replacement foils are available and reasonably priced.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, cordless head shaver that handles the tricky spots without breaking the bank. It's a solid 4.2/5 — a genuine workhorse for the bald or balding man who values function over frills.

**Skip it if** you require wet shaving or need something that charges quickly. Those two limitations are genuine dealbreakers for some users.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A dependable, well-built tool that earns its keep, with a couple of honest tradeoffs you should know about before buying.
