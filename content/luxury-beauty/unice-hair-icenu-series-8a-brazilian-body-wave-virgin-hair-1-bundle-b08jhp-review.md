## The $100 Question: Is This “8A” Grade Actually Worth It?

The UNice Icenu Series 8A Brazilian Body Wave is one of the most talked-about virgin hair bundles in the mid-tier price range. With 200 ratings hovering at 4.0 stars, it sits in that frustrating gray zone — good enough to recommend, but with enough mixed reviews to make you pause. After digging through the customer feedback and analyzing what this bundle actually delivers, here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains a single 8A-grade Brazilian virgin hair weft. Per the listing, you're getting 100% unprocessed human hair with intact cuticles, double-drawn ends, and a natural body wave pattern. The hair comes in a standard 3.5-4oz weft, machine-sewn with reinforced wefting at the top.

One thing to note immediately: this is a **single bundle**, not a full install kit. You'll need to purchase multiple bundles (usually 2-3 for a sew-in, 3-4 for a frontal install) plus a closure or frontal separately. The product images show the hair in its natural, unstyled state — a loose, flowing body wave with a subtle sheen, not the high-gloss look of cheaper synthetic blends.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The standout feature here is the double-drawn weft. That means the hair is thick from root to tip — no see-through ends or wispy tail-offs. Buyers consistently note that the density holds up well even after multiple washes. One reviewer wrote, *"I've had this installed for 6 weeks and the ends still look full. My last bundle from [a cheaper brand] was stringy by week 3."*

The texture is where this hair shines. The body wave pattern is loose enough to blend with natural hair but defined enough to hold a curl with flexi-rods. It also flat-irons straight without frizzing, which gives you two distinct styling options from one bundle. The cuticle alignment is proper — hair flows downward from root to tip, which minimizes tangling during the first few days of wear.

**However**, there are real consistency issues. Multiple verified buyers report receiving wefts shorter than the advertised length. One customer noted, *"Ordered 20 inches, received what measured closer to 17-18 inches once I flat-laid it on the table."* This is a known complaint across UNice's budget lines, and it's worth factoring into your ordering decision — size up if you need exact length.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The longevity is the most common thread in positive reviews. With proper care — sulfate-free shampoo, minimal heat, and nightly wrapping — several buyers report the hair lasting 8-12 months with regular wear. The lack of chemical processing means the hair accepts color well if you ever want to dye it.

Shedding is another bright spot. While all virgin hair sheds some, buyers report this weft loses notably less than competitors in the same price bracket. After the initial wash, most report minimal shedding — roughly a pinch of hair per wash, which is normal for double-drawn wefts.

## Where It Falls Short

The **factory smell** is the most frequent complaint. The hair arrives with a strong chemical odor that some describe as "perfume mixed with dye." It requires multiple vinegar-based soaks and air-drying sessions to remove. Not a dealbreaker, but plan for 2-3 days of prep before install.

The **inconsistent length** issue mentioned earlier is the bigger problem. When you're paying for specific lengths, receiving 2-3 inches short is frustrating. This seems to be a quality-control issue rather than intentional deception — some bundles are spot-on, others aren't.

Finally, the **4.0 star rating** reflects a split: roughly 60% of buyers are thrilled, 25% are satisfied but note the issues above, and 15% report significant tangling or shedding. That last group is concerning, but it's worth noting that many of those negative reviews involve heat styling at high temperatures or improper washing — which damages any virgin hair.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this really "virgin" hair?**
A: Yes, in the sense that it's unprocessed human hair with intact cuticles. However, it's been colored to a uniform black (which is standard for "virgin" Brazilian hair). It won't lighten dramatically with bleach, but it accepts semi-permanent dyes well.

**Q: How many bundles do I need for a full sew-in?**
A: For a standard sew-in with leave-out, 2 bundles (one for the base, one for volume). For a full install with a closure, 3 bundles. For a frontal, 3-4 bundles depending on your head size and desired fullness.

**Q: Will this tangle at the nape after a few weeks?**
A: It can, especially if you use heavy products or sleep without wrapping. The key is keeping it moisturized and braiding it down at night. Buyers who report minimal tangling follow a strict night-time routine.
