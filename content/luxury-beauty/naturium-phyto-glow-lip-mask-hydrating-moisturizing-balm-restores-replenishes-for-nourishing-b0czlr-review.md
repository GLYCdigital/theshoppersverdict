**The 4.7-star Naturium Phyto-Glow Lip Mask has 235 Amazon ratings for a reason — but it's not the lip product you think it is.**

If you're expecting a TikTok-famous, glassy, plumping lip gloss with a candy scent, scroll past. If you want a hardworking overnight treatment that actually repairs dry, cracked lips, keep reading. This is the skincare equivalent of a night cream — not a flashy serum.

## What You Actually Get

The 0.85 oz (24g) tube is larger than most lip masks (Laneige's is 0.7 oz) and uses a slanted metal applicator that feels cool and precise. The balm itself is a thick, opaque, pale-yellow cream — not a jelly, not a wax. As shown in the product image, it's a no-nonsense squeeze tube that's travel-friendly and hygienic (no finger-dipping).

The ingredient list is where Naturium earns its "phyto-glow" name: shea butter, jojoba oil, squalane, and a peptide complex. There's also bakuchiol, a plant-based retinol alternative, which explains the "restores and replenishes" claims on the label.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this for three weeks as a nightly treatment. The texture is dense — you feel it on your lips, which is the point. Within 15 minutes, the initial tackiness fades as the oils absorb. By morning, my lips felt soft without a greasy film that transfers onto pillows (a common complaint with cheaper balms).

What impressed me most was the cumulative effect. After week one, the chronic dry patches on my lower lip — the ones that catch on lipstick — were gone. After three weeks, my lips looked naturally fuller because the skin was hydrated, not because of any plumping ingredient. That's the "glow" in the name: it's internal hydration showing through, not a shimmer.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, the **scent is polarizing**. It smells faintly like herbs and shea — natural and earthy, but if you're used to vanilla-scented masks, it might take getting used to. One reviewer called it "slightly grassy," which is accurate.

Second, **this is not a daytime gloss**. The thick texture means it takes time to absorb. If you apply it and immediately put on lipstick, it'll slide around. It's a treatment, not a makeup product.

Third, the **bakuchiol** — while great for anti-aging — means this isn't ideal for anyone with sensitive skin who reacts to botanical actives. Patch test if you're prone to reactions.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The overnight treatment seeker**: You want to wake up with soft lips, not reapply gloss every hour. This delivers.
- **The chronic chapper**: Cold climates, Accutane users, or anyone on medications that dry out lips will see real improvement.
- **The ingredient-conscious shopper**: Clean, plant-based actives without synthetic fragrance or dyes. No parabens, no sulfates.

**Skip it if** you want instant shine, a sweet scent, or a lightweight balm for daytime wear. This is a night-only product for most people.

**Q: Can I wear this under lipstick?**
A: Not immediately. Wait at least 15-20 minutes for absorption, then blot. It works better as a pre-makeup treatment the night before.
