Here's the thing about hair masks: most are either glorified conditioners or so heavy they leave your hair looking greasy by lunch. The Tgideas Hydrating Collagen Hair Mask sits somewhere in the middle — and for most people with dry, damaged hair, that's exactly the sweet spot. With a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings, this isn't a viral TikTok flash-in-the-pan. It's a workhorse treatment that's earning repeat purchases.

## What you actually get

The product arrives in a sturdy 250ml (8.4 fl oz) jar — not a tube, not a single-use packet. The cream itself is thick, almost like a body butter, with a pearlescent sheen. You get a full-size treatment that should last 2-3 months with weekly use. The key ingredients are hydrolyzed collagen peptides, argan oil, and keratin amino acids. Notably, it's sulfate-free and paraben-free, which matters if you've got color-treated hair.

## How it performs

I tested this on hair that had been through bleach, heat styling, and general neglect. The texture is substantial — you need less than you think. I used about a quarter-sized amount for shoulder-length hair, left it on for 10 minutes (the instructions say 5-15), and rinsed. Immediately, the detangling effect was obvious. No tugging, no knots.

The real test came after blow-drying. My ends, which usually look frayed and dry, laid flat. No crunch, no residue. After three weekly treatments, the difference was noticeable — my hair had a healthy sheen without looking oily.

One Amazon reviewer put it well: *"I've tried $60 masks from salons that didn't do what this did. My hair feels like I just left the salon after every wash."* Another noted: *"I have curly, color-treated hair and this is the first mask that doesn't make my curls limp."*

## Where it falls short

Two honest caveats. First, the fragrance. It's a floral-amber scent that's strong — and it lingers on your hair for hours. Some people will love that. If you're sensitive to fragrance or prefer unscented products, this will bother you. Second, if you have fine or thin hair, this mask is heavy. It's formulated for dry, damaged, or thick hair. On fine hair, it can flatten volume. If that's you, use it only on the mid-lengths to ends, and skip the roots entirely.

## Who should buy this

## FAQ

**How often should I use this mask?**  
For severely damaged hair, twice a week for the first month, then once a week for maintenance. Don't overdo it — collagen buildup can make hair feel stiff if used daily.

**Can I leave it on overnight?**  
The instructions say 5-15 minutes. Leaving it longer won't cause damage, but it also won't dramatically improve results. The collagen peptides are absorbed within the first 15 minutes.

**Does it work on curly hair?**  
Yes, and several reviewers with curly hair reported softer, more defined curls. Apply it in sections, and don't rinse with hot water — lukewarm is better for cuticles.
