# Curaprox Hydrosonic Electric Toothbrush Review: Swiss Precision Meets Real Brushing Power

Here's the thing about luxury electric toothbrushes: most of them are overpriced Sonicare clones trading on brand names. The Curaprox Hydrosonic is different—it's a genuinely engineered piece of Swiss dental tech that does things most competitors can't touch. But it also carries a premium price tag and some quirks you should know about before you commit.

## What's Actually in the Box

The package includes the Hydrosonic handpiece, one Curen brush head with those distinctive ultra-soft bristles, a charging base, and a hard-shell travel case. Notably absent: replacement heads. That's a real oversight for a luxury product at this price point. You'll want to order spares immediately—the CS5460 heads are the softest Curaprox makes, and if you're used to medium-bristle brushes, the adjustment period is real.

## Performance That Justifies the Price

The Hydrosonic's headline number is 62,000 brush strokes per minute. To put that in context, most Sonicare models run around 31,000. That's double the cleaning action, and it shows in the first week of use. The five modes—Daily Clean, Sensitive, Gum Care, Whitening, and Deep Clean—aren't marketing filler; they genuinely change the vibration pattern and intensity. Gum Care mode pulses at a slower rhythm that massages rather than scrubs, which makes a noticeable difference if you have recession issues.

Battery life is where this thing really shines. Three weeks on a single charge is the claim, and multiple reviews confirm it holds up in real-world use. One reviewer noted, "I charged it once when it arrived and didn't think about it again for almost a month." That's a genuinely useful feature for travelers who forget chargers.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The consensus across the 215 Amazon ratings centers on two things: the brush head quality and the gum health results. Curaprox's Curen bristles are clinically studied and noticeably gentler than anything from Oral-B or Philips. One verified buyer wrote, "My hygienist asked what I'd changed because my gum pockets measured better at my last cleaning." Another called the soft bristles "a revelation if you've always found electric toothbrushes too aggressive."

The build quality also gets consistent love. The handpiece has a satisfying heft, the buttons are tactile, and the Swiss manufacturing shows in the fit and finish. This isn't a plastic toy—it feels like a precision instrument.

## Where It Falls Short

The proprietary charging base is the biggest frustration. There's no USB-C option, so you're tied to the included base. If you travel frequently, that's another charger to pack. And while the travel case is well-made, it doesn't accommodate the charging base—just the handpiece and one head.

The price is the other sticking point. At this premium level, not including a second brush head feels stingy. The CS5460 replacement heads cost roughly $12-15 each, so factor that into your total cost of ownership.

## Who Should Buy This

This is for the person who's serious about gum health and wants a brush that feels like an investment, not a gadget. It's also excellent for anyone with sensitive teeth or gums who's found standard sonic brushes too harsh. If you're a frequent traveler who forgets to charge devices, the three-week battery life is a genuine selling point.

Skip it if you're on a budget or you're happy with your current Sonicare—the upgrade is real but not life-changing if you already brush well.

## FAQ

**Is the Curaprox Hydrosonic better than Sonicare?**
For gum health and gentleness, yes. The ultra-soft Curen bristles are clinically proven to be less abrasive, and the 62,000 strokes-per-minute motor is significantly more powerful. Sonicare wins on ecosystem (more head options) and price.

**How often do I need to replace the brush head?**
Every three months, same as any electric toothbrush. The CS5460 heads are available on Amazon in multi-packs, and the color-fading bristles make it obvious when it's time to swap.

**Does the travel case hold the charger?**
No. The case fits the handpiece and one brush head only. Pack the charging base separately if you're traveling longer than three weeks.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You prioritize gum health, want the gentlest effective cleaning available, and don't mind paying for Swiss engineering.

**Skip it if:** You're happy with your current sonic brush, need USB-C charging, or expect replacement heads included at this price.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A genuinely excellent toothbrush that earns its premium price through superior performance and battery life, held back only by the proprietary charger and missing spare head.
