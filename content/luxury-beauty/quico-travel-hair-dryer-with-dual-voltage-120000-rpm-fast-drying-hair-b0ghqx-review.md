# Quico Travel Hair Dryer with Dual Voltage, 120,000 RPM Fast Drying

**The short version:** If you travel with nice hair, this is the dryer that keeps you from fighting hotel wall-mounts that feel like they were installed in 1992. The Quico isn't perfect — the missing diffuser is a genuine oversight — but for most travelers, the drying speed and voltage flexibility make it an easy purchase.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes the dryer body, a concentrator nozzle, and a drawstring travel pouch. That's it. No diffuser, no brush attachments. The dryer itself is compact — 8.7 inches fully extended, 5.9 inches folded. It's built from a matte nylon composite with a metallic accent ring that photographs better than it feels in hand; the texture reads premium without being slippery.

The motor is the story here. A 120,000 RPM brushless unit that pulls air through a rear grille and out the barrel at a claimed 21 m/s. In practice, it flattened my medium-thickness shoulder-length hair in 2 minutes 40 seconds on high heat. That's Dyson Supersonic territory at a fraction of the price.

## Real-World Performance

**Drying speed is the headline, and it delivers.** The high-airflow design means you're not relying on heat to do the work — you're blasting water off the shaft. For thick or dense hair, this is the difference between a 10-minute ordeal and a 4-minute routine. Multiple reviewers echo this: *"I have very thick, coarse hair and this dried it faster than my full-size dryer at home."* That's consistent with what I found.

**Dual voltage works flawlessly.** The auto-switching 100-240V circuit means you plug in anywhere and it just works. No red switch to forget, no fried motor at a hotel in London. This is the feature that justifies the "travel" label — it's genuinely international without accessories.

**Heat control is the weak point.** There are three heat settings and two speeds, plus a cold-shot button. The "low" heat still runs warm enough that fine or damaged hair will feel the stress. I'd recommend counting on the cold shot to finish — it's responsive, dropping temperature within a second of pressing. But if you're someone who needs gentle heat, this isn't it.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring themes in positive reviews center on portability and power. One verified buyer wrote: *"I've taken this to 11 countries in 8 months. Never once thought about the voltage. Dries my waist-length hair in 6 minutes."* Another noted the build quality: *"Feels like a $200 dryer, costs a fraction. The motor whine is higher pitched than I expected but it's not annoying."*

## Where It Falls Short

The missing diffuser is the biggest miss. For a $90+ luxury travel dryer, including a diffuser would cost maybe $4 in manufacturing. Curly and coily hair buyers should factor in the separate attachment bundle — which costs extra and adds bulk to the travel case.

Noise is real but tolerable. At 78dB on max, it's louder than the Dyson's 74dB. In a hotel bathroom at 6 AM, you'll hear it through the door. It's a high-pitched whine, not a deep roar, which some people find more irritating.

Finally, the power cord is 6 feet, not the 9 feet found on most full-size dryers. In hotel bathrooms where outlets are poorly placed, this can mean awkward positioning.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You travel internationally more than four times a year, you have medium-to-thick hair, and you want one device that eliminates the "will my dryer work here" anxiety forever.

**Skip it if:** You have very fine hair that needs gentle heat, you're strictly curly and need a diffuser daily, or you're buying this for home use only — a full-size dryer with longer cord and more attachments serves better at the same price.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the dual voltage really work, or do I need a converter?**
A: It truly auto-switches. The internal circuit detects input voltage and adjusts. Plug it into any outlet from 100V (US/Japan) to 240V (Europe/UK/Australia) without any adapter beyond the physical plug shape.

**Q: How loud is it actually?**
A: Measured 78dB at max speed. That's about the same as a food processor. Conversation-level is tough, but it's not "damage your hearing" loud. The pitch is higher than most dryers, which some find more noticeable.

**Q: Is the 120,000 RPM just marketing?**
A: No, but it matters less than you think. The motor speed enables high airflow at lower heat, which is why it dries fast without baking your scalp. What actually matters is the nozzle design that accelerates the air — and that works.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The Quico Travel Hair Dryer earns its rating through genuine engineering where it counts — motor speed, voltage flexibility, and compact build. It loses points on the missing diffuser and the heat control that runs hot for sensitive scalps. But for the international traveler with normal-to-thick hair who wants to stop renting cheap hotel dryers, this is an easy recommendation.

**Buy it if** you travel and value your hair routine. **Skip it if** you need gentle heat or a diffuser included. The core product is excellent; the accessories just need to catch up.
