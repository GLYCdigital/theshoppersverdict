Let me be blunt: most acrylic tip kits under $30 are a gamble. You either get flimsy plastic that snaps mid-week, or glue that refuses to bond. The MORGLES Clear Acrylic Tips kit is different — not perfect, but genuinely better than the price point suggests.

## What's Actually in the Box

This isn't a starter kit. You get 500 clear acrylic tips across 10 sizes (50 per size), two 5g bottles of nail glue, a stainless steel clipper, and a double-sided file. As shown in the product image, everything arrives in a compact organizer box that keeps sizes separated — trust me, you'll appreciate this when you're hunting for size 7 at 11pm.

The tips themselves are the star. They're made from a thicker ABS acrylic than most drugstore options, with a natural C-curve that mimics the shape of a healthy nail. The cuticle end is pre-shaped to reduce lifting, though you'll still want to file the edges for a flush fit.

## Performance and Real-World Use

I tested these on both natural nails and over a gel manicure. The glue is the real deal — it's cyanoacrylate-based, so it grabs fast. You get about 10-15 seconds of adjustment time before it sets. Press firmly for 30 seconds and you're good to go.

Here's where these tips genuinely surprised me: durability. I wore a set for 12 days straight — dishes, typing, gardening, the works. Two tips lifted on day 9 (my fault, I didn't roughen the natural nail enough). The rest held firm until I removed them.

The clipper is functional but basic. It cuts cleanly through the acrylic without cracking, though you'll want to file the edge afterward. The included double-sided file works — one side is coarse (180 grit), one is finer (240 grit). My honest advice: replace it with a glass file for the final shaping.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**Sizing runs small.** If you have medium-to-wide nail beds, you'll likely skip the smaller sizes entirely and find yourself using the largest ones. The size chart printed on the box is accurate to the millimeter, but the actual fit depends on your nail shape. Order a size up if you're between sizes.

**The files are mediocre.** The coarse side is aggressive enough to scuff the acrylic surface if you're not careful. Use light pressure and finish with the fine side.

**The glue bottle nozzle clogs.** I had to clear the tip with a pin after a few uses. Not a dealbreaker, but annoying.

## FAQ

**Q: How long do these tips last?**
A: With proper prep (buffing the natural nail, applying glue to both surfaces), expect 10-14 days. Longer if you're gentle and use cuticle oil daily.

**Q: Can I reuse the tips?**
A: Technically yes, but it's not worth it. Removing them with acetone warps the acrylic slightly. At roughly 4 cents per tip, just use fresh ones.

**Q: Does the glue damage natural nails?**
A: It's cyanoacrylate, so it's strong. Soak in acetone for 10-15 minutes to dissolve — don't pry them off. Use a cuticle oil afterward to rehydrate.
