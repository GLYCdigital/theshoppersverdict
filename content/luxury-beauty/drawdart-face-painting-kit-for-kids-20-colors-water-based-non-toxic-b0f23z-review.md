# Drawdart Face Painting Kit for Kids: A Parent-Approved 20-Color Set That Actually Delivers

Let's be honest — most kids' face paint kits are garbage. You get chalky colors that crack within an hour, brushes that shed bristles into your child's eye, and packaging that falls apart before Halloween. The Drawdart 20-color set is not that. At 4.6 stars from 229 ratings, this kit has earned its place in the rotation. Here's the real breakdown.

## What's Actually in the Box

The kit arrives with 20 individual pans of water-based face paint in a compact folding case. You're getting the expected rainbow — reds, blues, yellows, greens — plus the less common but essential shades: a true black, a decent white, and several pastels. Alongside the paints, Drawdart includes three fine-tip brushes (two thin, one slightly thicker) and two wedge sponges. For a kit in this price range, having the applicators included means you don't need to buy anything else to get started.

## Performance and Build Quality

The paint pans are reasonably sized — about the diameter of a quarter and roughly half an inch deep. That's enough for dozens of uses per color. The formula is water-based with a creamy texture that applies smoothly. The standout feature here is the pigmentation. Unlike cheaper kits where you're essentially painting with tinted water, Drawdart's colors hold their intensity on skin. Red, blue, and green deliver full opacity in a single pass.

As shown in the product image, the case is compact and portable — roughly the size of a large smartphone but thicker. The pans are removable, which makes cleaning easier. The brushes are a weak point — they're serviceable but not professional grade. They'll do fine for butterflies and superhero masks, but if you're planning intricate line work for a school play, upgrade to better brushes.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Looking through the 229 ratings, several themes emerge. Parents repeatedly mention how easily this washes off. One reviewer wrote, "Used it for a birthday party with 12 kids. Every costume was clean within five minutes of warm water and soap. No stained clothes, no scrubbing, no tears." That's a recurring sentiment — the removal process is genuinely painless.

Another consistent plus is that it doesn't irritate sensitive skin. Multiple reviewers note their children have eczema or general sensitivity, and this kit didn't cause reactions. The formula is certified non-toxic and complies with ASTM D-4236 and CE standards, which is the safety baseline you want for face products.

The color range also gets frequent mention. Having 20 shades means you're not mixing constantly — you can find a close match for most designs without custom blending.

## Where It Falls Short

The white and light pink are the weakest performers. They're translucent on first application and need multiple layers to achieve proper opacity. This is especially noticeable on darker skin tones, where pastels can look washed out. If your design requires strong white coverage, budget extra time for layering.

The paint also dries quickly on the palette. During longer sessions, the pans can develop a crusty surface. A quick spritz of water fixes this, but it's an extra step you'll need to remember mid-project. One reviewer noted, "By the time I finished the third kid at the party, the red pan was getting hard. I had to keep adding water drops to keep it workable."

The included brushes are the other weak link. They're acceptable for basic work but shed slightly and don't hold a fine point for detailed designs.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this safe for toddlers?**
A: Yes, for children 3 and up. The formula is non-toxic and hypoallergenic, but always patch test a small area first. Avoid use around the eyes and mouth.

**Q: How long does it last on skin?**
A: It holds up for 3-5 hours without smudging, assuming it's not rubbed. It's water-based, so sweat and tears will cause running. For active kids, apply a setting spray designed for face paint.

**Q: Will it stain clothing?**
A: Generally no. It washes out of fabric with standard laundry detergent. The darker colors (black, navy, red) can leave faint residue if left to set for over 24 hours, so wash promptly.
