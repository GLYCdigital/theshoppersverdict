Finding a nude gel polish set that doesn't turn your nails into a chalky, washed-out mess is harder than it should be. Most "skin tone" collections assume every hand is the same shade of almond latte. The RARJSM set of six actually acknowledges that diversity, which is the first reason it earned its 4.5-star average from 225 Amazon shoppers. Here's the full breakdown.

**What's actually in the box**

You get six 10ml bottles of gel polish, each labeled with a shade name that maps to a specific undertone rather than vague marketing like "nude." The range moves from a pale porcelain pink through warm caramel, true taupe, a mauve-leaning neutral, rich chestnut, and a deep espresso-brown. As shown in the product image, the bottles are squat, matte-finish, and clearly labeled — no guessing which color is which when you're mid-manicure.

There's no base coat, top coat, or lamp included. This is polish only, so factor that into your total cost if you're new to gel.

**Performance and real-world wear**

I applied these over a standard gel base and cured each coat for 60 seconds under a 48W UV/LED lamp. The consistency is on the thinner side — almost like a hybrid between traditional polish and gel. That's a double-edged sword: thin viscosity means easier self-leveling, but it also means you have to resist the urge to load the brush. One reviewer noted, "The first coat looks streaky, but the second coat evens out completely." That tracks with my testing. When you apply the second layer, the pigment snaps into full opacity.

The brush deserves specific mention. It's narrow, flat, and tapered at the tip, so you can get within a millimeter of the cuticle without flooding. If you've wrestled with wide, floppy brushes that paint half your finger, this is a genuine upgrade.

Wear time: with a proper top coat, I got 12 days before any tip wear, and removal was clean with standard wrap-and-soak method. No staining on the nail plate, even with the darker shades.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Digging through the 225 ratings, a few themes repeat. Color accuracy is the big one — "The colors look exactly like the photos, which never happens" shows up in multiple reviews. Another recurring note is the fair-to-deep range. One buyer wrote, "I'm dark-skinned and these are the first nudes that don't look gray on me." That's the kind of specificity that tells you the brand actually tested these on diverse skin tones.

**Where it falls short**

The thin formula punishes rushing. If you apply thick coats to save time, you'll get pooling at the sidewalls and a lumpy finish that requires filing. Also, the set lacks any sort of matte option — all six shades cure to the standard glossy gel shine. If you wanted a matte nude manicure, you'll need to buy a separate matte top coat. And while the price is reasonable for six colors, you're still paying for shades you might not use; there's no option to buy singles.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do I need to use a base coat with these?**
A: Yes. Gel polish needs a base coat to adhere properly, and you'll need a top coat to seal the color. This set only includes the six color bottles.

**Q: How long does it take to cure?**
A: Under a 36W or higher UV/LED lamp, each coat cures in 30-60 seconds. The darker shades may need the full 60 seconds to cure through completely.

**Q: Will these work on natural nails or just extensions?**
A: Both. They perform well on natural nails, but you need to prep properly — buff the nail plate and wipe with alcohol or cleanser before applying the base coat.

**The Verdict**
