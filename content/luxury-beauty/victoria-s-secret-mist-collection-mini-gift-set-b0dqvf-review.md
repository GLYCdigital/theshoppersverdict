## Performance and Real-World Use

Let's talk about what these mists actually do. The scent profiles are classic VS — Bombshell delivers that fruity-floral burst with notes of purple passion fruit, Shangri-la peony, and vanilla orchid. Love is softer, more intimate, with pear, peony, and white musk. Pure Seduction leans into plum and freesia for a sweeter finish.

The spray mechanism on these minis is consistent with the full-size versions — a fine mist that distributes evenly without soaking your clothes. The formula itself is alcohol-based, which means it evaporates quickly and leaves a clean, non-sticky finish. No residue, no staining on light fabrics.

Here's the honest caveat: longevity. These are body mists, not eau de parfum. On skin, you're looking at 2-4 hours before the scent fades to a skin-scent level. On clothes, it lasts longer — up to 6 hours in my testing. If you're expecting all-day projection, adjust your expectations. This is a refresh-and-reapply product, and that's fine for the price point.
