If you have curly, coily, or transitioning hair, you already know the struggle: conditioner either slips through your strands like water or sits on top like paste. Mixed Chicks Detangling Deep Conditioner claims to split the difference. After digging through 240 ratings and multiple rounds of real-world testing, here's the honest breakdown.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The 16-ounce squeeze tube delivers a thick, honey-like cream that's clearly concentrated. This isn't a watery rinse-out — it's a treatment. Key ingredients include aloe vera juice (first on the list), chamomile extract, and silk amino acids. Notably absent: sulfates, parabens, and phthalates. The brand markets it for "multi-textured" hair, which translates to anything from loose waves to tightly coiled 4C.

**How It Performs in Real Life**

The detangling power is genuinely impressive. One reviewer with waist-length 4C hair noted: *"I left it on for 15 minutes with a shower cap, and my Denman brush glided through like I had a relaxer — no breakage, no tears."* Another praised the slip: *"A little goes further than I expected, but the slip is unreal. I can finger-detangle first, then finish with a wide-tooth comb."*

For deep conditioning, it delivers on both timelines. A 5-minute application softens and smooths; pushing to 30 minutes under heat gives serious elasticity and shine. Curl definition improves noticeably without that crunchy, product-laden feel. It's also effective on color-treated and heat-damaged strands — several reviewers confirmed it helps revive over-processed sections.

**Where It Falls Short**

The thickness cuts both ways. On very long or dense hair, you'll need more than you'd like — one reviewer with hip-length hair went through a third of the tube in a single session. The scent is another split: the herbal-citrus blend (think lemongrass with a floral undertone) is strong on application and lingers. Some find it refreshing; others find it overwhelming. A few reviewers also mentioned that on low-porosity hair, it benefits from heat to truly penetrate — a cold application may sit on the surface.

One more honest note: if you're looking for a leave-in or daily conditioner, this isn't it. It's a treatment, and it performs best in that role.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for you if:
- You have type 3-4 curls that tangle easily and need serious slip
- You're transitioning from heat-styled or relaxed hair
- You want a multi-purpose deep treatment that works quickly or slowly
- You have a sensitive scalp — the aloe and chamomile are genuinely calming

Skip it if you have fine, loose waves that get weighed down easily, or if you're sensitive to strongly scented products.

**FAQ**

*Can I use it on kids?* Yes, but dilute it slightly and start with a 5-minute application. It's gentle enough for children's hair, though the scent may be intense for sensitive noses.

*How often should I use it?* Once a week for most hair types. If your hair is very dry or damaged, twice a week is fine. Don't exceed that — over-conditioning can lead to mushy, limp strands.

*Is it safe for color-treated hair?* Yes, and it actually helps prolong color by keeping the cuticle sealed and hydrated. Just avoid using it immediately before a color treatment.

**The Verdict**

- **Rating:** 4.6/5
- **Buy it if:** You want a hard-working detangler that doubles as a genuine deep treatment, and you don't mind a strong scent.
- **Skip it if:** You have fine or low-density curls that need lightweight moisture, or you prefer fragrance-free formulas.

Mixed Chicks delivers on its core promise: tangles gone, hydration restored, curls defined. The 4.8-star average across 240 ratings isn't inflated — this is a legitimate workhorse for textured hair. Just budget for the product usage and sniff-test the scent first if you're picky.
