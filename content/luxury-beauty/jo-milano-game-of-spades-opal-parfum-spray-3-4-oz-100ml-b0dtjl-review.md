## Performance and Scent Profile

The opening is a warm burst of bergamot and saffron that quickly settles into the heart: jasmine and orange blossom with a creamy vanilla base. The dry-down leans heavily amber, with a soft powdery musk that lingers close to the skin.

**Longevity:** This is the headline feature. As an extract de parfum, you're getting a high concentration of fragrance oils — and it shows. On skin, expect 8–10 hours before it fades to a skin scent. On clothing, I've caught whiffs 24 hours after application. One reviewer noted: *"Sprayed at 7am, could still smell it on my scarf at midnight. Unbelievable for the price."*

**Projection:** Moderate. You'll get a comfortable 2–3 foot bubble for the first 3–4 hours, then it settles intimate. If you're looking for a fragrance that announces your arrival before you enter a room, this isn't it. But for office wear or dinner dates, it's perfectly tuned.

## Where It Falls Short

**The linearity is the biggest flaw.** Don't expect a journey with this fragrance. What you smell at application is what you'll smell eight hours later — just softer. Fragrance enthusiasts who crave evolving compositions will find it one-dimensional.

**The inspiration is obvious.** If you own MFK Grand Soir or Initio Side Effect, you'll recognize the DNA immediately. It's not a clone, but it's clearly in the same olfactory family. If originality matters to you, that's a mark against it.

**Scent-free zones beware:** The vanilla-amber base can skew slightly sweet in high heat. In summer humidity, it can feel a bit syrupy. This is a cooler-weather fragrance, full stop.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this a clone of another fragrance?**
A: It's inspired by the popular "vanilla amber" genre — think MFK Grand Soir or PDM Oajan. Not a 1:1 clone, but the same family. If you like that style, you'll like this.

**Q: How many sprays should I use?**
A: 3–4 sprays max. The concentration is high, and overspraying can get cloying. One on each wrist, one on the chest, one on the neck.

**Q: How long does the bottle last?**
A: At 3–4 sprays daily, a 100ml bottle lasts roughly 6–8 months. The concentrated formula means you use less per application.
