# As I Am Curling Jelly Review: The Alcohol-Free Gel That Actually Respects Your Curls

Here's the thing about curly hair products: most styling gels are basically hairspray in a jar. They promise definition, deliver a crunchy helmet, and leave your curls looking like they've been shellacked. As I Am's Curling Jelly takes a different approach—and 225 Amazon reviewers have given it 4.5 stars for it. But is it worth adding to your rotation? Let's break it down.

## What You're Actually Getting

The As I Am Curling Jelly comes in a straightforward 8-ounce white tub with a flip-top lid. The gel itself is clear, thick, and has a slippery, almost lotion-like consistency rather than the sticky glue texture you get from drugstore gels. It's part of the brand's signature line that includes their famous leave-in conditioner, so it's designed to work in tandem with other As I Am products—though it plays fine with other brands too.

The formula is where this product earns its keep. It's alcohol-free, which is the single biggest reason it doesn't dry your curls out like straw. It also skips the sulfates, parabens, and phthalates. The star ingredients are aloe vera, which provides moisture and slip, and a blend of botanical extracts that help with curl memory without weighing hair down.

## Performance: The Real-World Test

I've used this jelly on everything from loose beach waves to tight 4C coils, and here's what I found: application method matters more than curl type. Apply it to **soaking wet hair**—I'm talking dripping, just-out-of-the-shower wet—and you'll get defined, bouncy curls that hold their shape. Apply it to damp hair, and you'll get a cast that's harder to break and less even distribution.

The hold is medium-to-firm. It won't give you that stiff, all-day helmet effect, but it also won't let your curls fall flat by noon. Multiple reviewers mention using it for special occasions where they need reliable hold. One reviewer noted, *"I used this for my sister's wedding in July—95 degrees and humid—and my curls still had definition at the reception."* That's a pretty solid endorsement for humidity resistance.

The cast issue is real, though. This product does form a slightly crunchy shell as it dries. That's not a flaw—it's how the gel locks in definition. But you absolutely need to **scrunch out the crunch** once your hair is fully dry. Use a few drops of oil or just your bare hands, and the cast breaks down into soft, touchable curls. Skip that step and your hair will feel crispy.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.5-star rating isn't accidental. Across the reviews, three themes keep coming up:

1. **Frizz control that lasts.** The combination of aloe vera and the alcohol-free formula means this doesn't just style—it conditions. One reviewer wrote, *"I've tried every gel from DevaCurl to Ouidad, and this is the only one that keeps my frizz at bay past hour three."*

2. **Curl definition without the sticky residue.** No flakes, no white residue, no tacky feel. It dries clean and leaves curls looking polished, not product-laden.

3. **Works on multiple hair types.** From wavy 2B to coily 4C, reviewers with different textures report success. The key is adjusting how much you use—a nickel-sized amount for fine hair, a palm full for thick coils.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The 8-ounce size is smaller than it looks, and if you have thick, long hair, you'll burn through this tub in three to four weeks with regular use. At the price point, that adds up.

The learning curve is also real. If you've never used a styling gel that requires scrunching out a cast, you might initially think you've made a mistake. It takes two or three washes to figure out the right amount and technique for your hair.

And if you're looking for a **strong, all-day firm hold**—the kind that survives a workout or a windy commute—this isn't it. It's a styling product, not a structural one.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Curly and coily hair types** (2B through 4C) who want definition without crunch
- **Frizz-prone individuals** who live in humid climates and need reliable hold
- **Clean-beauty shoppers** who specifically avoid alcohol, sulfates, and parabens in their haircare
- **Anyone frustrated with gel flaking** — this genuinely doesn't flake

**Skip it if** you have very fine, straight-to-wavy hair that doesn't need heavy definition, or if you want a strong-hold gel that doesn't require any post-dry styling work.

## FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Does As I Am Curling Jelly work on relaxed or color-treated hair?**
Yes. The alcohol-free formula is gentle enough for processed hair, and the aloe base actually adds moisture that color-treated curls often lack. Just be aware that relaxed hair may not form as tight a curl pattern, so start with a small amount.

**Q: Can I mix this with other styling products?**
Absolutely. Many reviewers layer it over a leave-in conditioner or mix it with a curl cream for extra moisture. It plays well with oils too—just add a drop of oil to your hands when scrunching out the cast.

**Q: How much should I use?**
For shoulder-length hair, start with a quarter-sized amount. Work it through wet hair with your fingers or a wide-tooth comb, then scrunch. Add more only if you need extra hold. Using too much is the most common mistake—it leads to heavy, weighed-down curls.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if** you want defined, frizz-resistant curls without the crunchy, flaky aftermath of traditional gels. It's a solid choice for anyone serious about their curl routine who's willing to master the wet-application, scrunch-out-the-cast technique.

**Skip it if** you want a grab-and-go product with zero learning curve, or you need industrial-strength hold for all-day styling.

At this price point with this performance, the As I Am Curling Jelly earns its place in the rotation. It's not the cheapest option, but it's one of the few gels that treats your curls like hair instead of a styling project.
