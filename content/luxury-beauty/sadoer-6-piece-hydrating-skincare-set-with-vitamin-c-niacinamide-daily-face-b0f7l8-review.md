## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, the serum and eye cream are the smallest bottles in the set, and they're the ones you'll run through fastest. At 30ml and 20ml respectively, daily use means repurchasing the set before the cleanser and toner are even half-empty. That's a real cost consideration.

Second, there's no SPF anywhere in this set. Vitamin C makes your skin more photosensitive, so skipping sunscreen actively undermines the brightening benefits. Factor in a separate daily SPF 30+ product.

Third, the packaging — while clean and functional — doesn't feel premium. The frosted plastic bottles are lightweight and practical, but if you're buying this as a luxury self-care gift, the unboxing experience won't match the price.
