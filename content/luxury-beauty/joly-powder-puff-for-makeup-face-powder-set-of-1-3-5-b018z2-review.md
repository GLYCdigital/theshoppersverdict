You've spent $60 on a setting powder. Why are you still patting it in with a sponge that eats half the product? That's the frustration the Joly Powder Puff set exists to solve — and for the most part, it delivers.

Let me be clear about what this is: a set of velour powder puffs, sold in counts of 1, 3, 5, or 10. No compact, no mirror, no brush. Just the puffs. That's either refreshingly simple or a missed opportunity depending on what you expected.

**What you actually get**

The puffs measure roughly 2.5 inches across with a plush velour top and a soft foam core. The elastic strap on the back is functional — you slide two fingers through, not one, which gives you better control when pressing powder into the skin. The 10-pack comes in a clear poly bag, nothing fancy, so don't expect gift-box presentation.

**Performance and real-world use**

The velour surface is the star here. Unlike traditional powder puffs that absorb your product before it touches your face, the Joly puff deposits powder onto the skin. One dip in loose powder covers your entire face — I tested this with a translucent setting powder and got a soft-focus finish without the cakey buildup you get from brushes that over-apply.

For pressed powders, the puff works differently. You want to press and roll rather than swipe. Swiping drags the powder and creates streaks; pressing gives you that airbrushed, blurring effect. This is the technique the product image suggests, and it's worth following.

Washing is where this puff earns its keep. I machine-washed mine in a delicates bag with cold water and air-dried it flat. No shrinkage, no pilling, no loss of softness. Several reviewers report the same, with one noting: *"I've washed these at least 15 times and they still feel brand new — I stopped buying expensive puffs from Sephora entirely."* Another buyer mentioned the 10-pack let her rotate daily and wash weekly, which extends the life of each puff considerably.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The even application gets the most mentions across the 225 ratings. Users with dry skin specifically call out that the velour doesn't tug or irritate, unlike some synthetic brushes. Multiple reviewers also note the puff works well for cream-to-powder foundations, not just loose powders — a versatility the listing doesn't heavily advertise.

**Where it falls short**

The elastic strap is genuinely tight. I have average-sized hands and it fits fine, but if you have larger fingers or long acrylic nails, you'll fight with it. One reviewer put it bluntly: *"The strap digs into my fingers after a minute of use. I ended up cutting it off and just holding the puff directly."* That's a workaround, but it means you lose the pressing control the strap provides.

There's also no case or storage solution. If you buy the 5 or 10-pack, you'll need your own container to keep them clean between uses. For a product positioned as luxury, that omission feels like an oversight.

**Who should buy this**

- **The powder enthusiast** who goes through setting powder quickly and wants maximum product payoff
- **The hygiene-conscious user** who wants multiple puffs to rotate and wash weekly
- **The minimalist traveler** who packs light — these are flat, lightweight, and take up zero space

**Skip it if** you need a complete powder application system or have very large hands.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this with liquid foundation?**
A: Technically yes, but it's not ideal. The velour absorbs liquid and needs immediate washing. Stick to powders and cream-to-powder formulas.

**Q: How often should I wash the puff?**
A: For daily use, wash every 3-4 days. The foam core dries quickly, so overnight air-drying is sufficient.

**Q: Does the 10-pack save money?**
A: Yes — the per-unit cost drops significantly compared to the single puff, and the puffs last through many washes.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want even powder application without wasting product, and you appreciate having spares on hand. **Skip it if** you need an all-in-one compact solution or find tight elastic straps deal-breaking.

This is a solid 4.6-star product that does one thing well. At 4.5/5 from 225 ratings, the consensus matches my experience — you're getting a genuinely good puff at a fair price, just don't expect luxury packaging to match the luxury performance.
