You know that guy at the party whose scent you can still smell on your jacket the next morning? That's Jean Paul Gaultier Ultra Male Intense. This isn't a subtle "skin scent" for close encounters — it's a fragrance that announces itself before you enter the room and lingers long after you've left.

The Intense flanker takes the original Ultra Male's DNA and cranks everything up. That iconic torso bottle (shown in the product image) now holds a darker, more concentrated juice. If the original was a night-out scent, this is its older, louder sibling.

## What's in the Bottle

You're getting a 4.2 oz (125ml) Eau de Toilette Intense — though calling it an EDT undersells it. This performs closer to a parfum extract. The opening hits you with sweet pear and bergamot, but the real story unfolds in the heart: cinnamon, cumin, and clary sage wrapped around a vanilla-amber base that's rich, almost syrupy.

The bottle itself is the same muscular male torso design JPG is famous for, but with a darker, smoked-glass finish. It's a shelf statement piece — though it does take up more space than standard rectangular bottles.

## Performance That's Honestly Excessive

Here's where this fragrance separates from the pack. On my skin, I got:

- **Longevity:** 11 hours before it faded to a close-range skin scent. On fabric? Three days later, my shirt still smelled like it.
- **Projection:** For the first 3-4 hours, this projects arm's length or more. People will notice.
- **Sillage:** Heavy. You'll leave a scent trail in a room that takes time to dissipate.

This is not an office fragrance. It's a statement piece for evenings, dates, cold weather, and events where you want to be remembered.

## What Buyers Are Saying

The 225 ratings average 4.5 stars, and the reviews tell a consistent story. One buyer wrote: *"My girlfriend can smell me from across the room — it's that strong. Two sprays is all you need."* Another noted: *"Lasts all day at work and I still get compliments at the gym after 8 hours."*

The recurring praise centers on longevity and the sweet, addictive vanilla-pear blend. The complaints? Almost universally about strength — not weakness. *"Be careful with the trigger,"* one reviewer warned. *"I did four sprays and had to wash it off."*

## Where It Falls Short

**Heat is this fragrance's enemy.** The sweetness turns cloying and almost syrupy in summer humidity. This is a fall/winter scent, full stop.

**Batch variation is real.** Some bottles reportedly feel stronger and richer than others. That's frustrating at this price point, though not unusual for niche-adjacent houses.

**It's not versatile.** You can't wear this everywhere. Casual brunch? No. Job interview? Absolutely not. This is a specific tool for specific occasions.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The compliment seeker:** If you want people to ask what you're wearing, this delivers — consistently.
- **Cold-weather enthusiasts:** Fall nights, winter parties, holiday gatherings. This shines when the temperature drops.
- **Anyone who hates reapplying:** One morning spray genuinely lasts until bedtime.

**Skip it if:** You prefer subtle, office-safe scents, live in a hot climate, or dislike sweet fragrances. Also skip if you're prone to overspraying — this will punish you.

## FAQ

**Q: How many sprays should I use?**
A: Two. One on the chest, one on the neck. That's it. Three is pushing it, four is a mistake. This is a concentrated juice, not a body mist.

**Q: Is this different from the original Ultra Male?**
A: Yes. The Intense version has a darker, more vanilla-forward profile with stronger performance. The original is brighter and slightly fresher. If you have both, you're wearing them for different moods — but you don't need both.

**Q: Does it work for summer?**
A: Barely. It's wearable on cooler summer nights, but in direct heat it becomes overpowering and slightly cloying. Save it for September through March.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a signature evening scent with monstrous performance and a sweet, addictive character that draws compliments. This is a crowd-pleaser in the right settings.

**Skip it if:** You need versatility, live somewhere hot, or prefer understated fragrances. This is not a safe blind buy — sample it first if you're unsure.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It loses points only for limited versatility and batch inconsistency. For what it does — projecting a memorable, sweet, powerful scent for 12+ hours — it's exceptional.
