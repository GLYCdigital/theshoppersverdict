**The 20-pack banana clip you'll either lose or lose patience with — here's the honest breakdown.**

Banana clips are having a moment again, and ROYHOO's 20-piece set is positioned as the budget-friendly way to jump on the trend. But with a 4.0-star average across 200 ratings, the real question isn't whether these are cheap — it's whether they're *good enough* to actually use daily.

**What you're actually getting**

Open the package and you'll find 20 banana clips in a mix of solid colors and patterns — think black, tortoiseshell, clear, pastel pinks, and some metallic finishes. Each clip measures roughly 4 inches long with the classic curved comb design. The plastic is rigid but not brittle, and the hinge mechanism feels smooth when you open and close each clip.

The comb teeth are spaced closely enough to grip hair without that painful raking sensation you get from cheap versions. I'll give ROYHOO credit here: the molding is clean, no sharp edges or burrs that catch on strands.

**How they hold up in real use**

The defining test for any banana clip is whether it stays put. For thick, wavy, or curly hair, these perform admirably. The teeth bite into the hair and the curved shape conforms to the back of the head naturally. One buyer noted, *"I have very thick hair and these actually hold everything up without slipping — I've bought three packs already."*

For fine or slippery hair, the story changes. Multiple reviewers report clips sliding out within an hour, especially with smooth, freshly-washed hair. One frustrated customer wrote: *"Cute but useless for my thin hair. Falls out just walking around the house."* That's not a manufacturing flaw — it's physics. Banana clips rely on tension and friction, and if your hair doesn't provide enough of either, no clip will save you.

**Where this set falls short**

The random color assortment is the biggest practical gripe. You're not choosing your palette — you're getting whatever's in the bag. Several buyers mention receiving four or five identical clips in colors they'd never wear. If you're buying these as gifts or for a specific aesthetic, that's a gamble.

Durability is mixed. Most clips survive regular use, but a handful of reviewers report hinges cracking within weeks, particularly in colder climates where plastic gets more brittle. At roughly $1 per clip, that's acceptable — but don't expect heirloom quality.

**Who should buy these**

This set makes sense for three specific people: the person with thick hair who loses accessories constantly, the parent tired of buying single clips for kids who scatter them like confetti, and anyone who wants to experiment with banana clips without committing to premium pricing. Skip them if you have fine hair, if color coordination matters to you, or if you want clips that'll last years rather than months.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Do these work for all hair types?**
No. They grip best on medium to thick textures. Fine and straight hair will likely need additional pinning or a different clip design.

**Are they safe for sleep?**
The plastic is smooth and teeth aren't sharp, but the clips are bulky enough that sleeping on them is uncomfortable. Use for daytime styling.

**Can I return them if the colors don't match?**
Amazon's return policy covers this, but the random assortment is disclosed in the listing. Don't expect a replacement based on color preference.
