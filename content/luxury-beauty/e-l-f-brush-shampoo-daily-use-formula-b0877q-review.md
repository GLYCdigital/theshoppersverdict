Let's be honest about what "daily use" means in the beauty world: most of us are lucky to deep-clean our brushes once a month. But if you're someone who actually does wash your tools regularly — or you're trying to break the habit of swirling dirty bristles across your face — the e.l.f. Brush Shampoo Daily Use Formula is one of the most practical cleansers you'll find at this price point.

## What You Actually Get

A 4-fluid-ounce bottle of clear, lightly viscous liquid soap. No fancy pump, no brush mat, no frills. The packaging is straightforward e.l.f.: clean white label, minimal design, and a flip-top cap that dispenses just the right amount — roughly a dime-sized drop per brush. As shown in the product image, it's a no-nonsense bottle that won't take up much room in your beauty drawer.

## Performance and Real-World Results

I tested this against three categories of brushes: dense synthetic foundation brushes, fluffy powder brushes, and natural-hair eyeshadow brushes. The results were consistent. A single wash with warm water and a few drops of the shampoo lifted caked-on liquid foundation and cream blush that had been sitting for days. No pre-soaking needed, no repeated scrubbing.

What impressed me most was the rinse-out. Many cleansers leave a slippery film that requires multiple passes to remove — this one rinses clean after about 20 seconds of agitation. That matters because leftover soap residue is what causes brushes to feel stiff and pick up product unevenly. The e.l.f. formula leaves bristles soft, and they dry within two hours on a flat surface.

The sulfate-free formulation is worth calling out. Harsh detergents can eat away at the glue holding bristles in place, causing shedding. I didn't lose a single bristle during testing, even on older brushes that had seen better days.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews reflects a pattern of satisfaction. One reviewer noted, *"I've tried the expensive stuff and this works just as well. My brushes feel brand new after every wash."* Another echoed a common sentiment: *"Finally a brush cleaner that doesn't leave my brushes smelling like chemicals for days."* The consensus is clear — this performs on par with cleansers costing three to four times as much.

## Where It Falls Short

The scent is the weakest link. It's not offensive — more like a faint, clean, slightly clinical aroma — but if you're accustomed to lavender-infused or citrus-scented cleansers, you'll notice the difference. It dissipates once brushes dry, so it's a minor gripe.

The bottle size is the bigger practical issue. At 4 ounces, you'll go through it quickly if you're cleaning a full set of brushes weekly. One Amazon reviewer summed it up: *"Love the product, hate how fast I run out. Wish they sold a bigger bottle."* Stock up or pair it with a larger-volume alternative if you're a heavy user.

## Who Should Buy This

This is ideal for makeup enthusiasts who clean brushes weekly or biweekly, makeup artists who need a reliable, budget-friendly option for between clients, and anyone with sensitive skin who wants a gentle formula that won't irritate. Skip it if you prefer heavily scented products or need a deep-cleaning treatment for heavily soiled professional brushes — you'll want something with stronger degreasing power for that. The product is also cruelty-free and vegan, which aligns with e.l.f.'s broader brand ethos.

## FAQ

**How often should I clean my brushes with this?**
Daily if you use cream or liquid products, every few days for powder-only application. The formula is gentle enough for frequent use without damaging bristles.

**Can I use this on beauty sponges?**
Yes. It works well on latex-free sponges — squeeze, rinse, repeat until the water runs clear. It's actually one of the better sponge cleansers I've tried.

**Does it work on synthetic and natural brushes equally?**
Both, but natural-hair brushes benefit most from the sulfate-free formula since they're more prone to drying out and shedding than synthetics.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want an affordable, effective daily brush cleanser that won't wreck your tools or your skin. It's a legitimate workhorse product.

**Skip it if:** You need a deep-cleaning treatment for heavily soiled pro brushes, want a larger bottle, or prefer scented formulas.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The small bottle and faint scent keep it from perfection, but at this price, the performance is genuinely hard to beat.
