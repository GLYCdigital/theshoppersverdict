Let's be direct about what 200 ratings at 4.0 stars actually tells you: this product delivers for most people, but it's not flawless. Here's what you need to know before handing over your money.

## What's Actually in the Pack

Full Shine sends you three or four wefts depending on the bundle size you choose—each measuring roughly 3.5 inches wide and running 20-24 inches long in the standard offering. The wefts are machine-sewn with a double-stitched track, which is the first thing I checked because that's where cheap extensions fail. Each bundle weighs around 95-100 grams, which is adequate for a full sew-in but won't give you that massive, influencer-level volume without buying extra.

The hair arrives in a plastic wrap with a satin tie—nothing fancy, but the packaging is functional and the hair isn't crushed or tangled on arrival. As shown in the product image, the wefts have a clean, straight edge and the hair lays flat without that telltale "bumpy" weft ridge you see on budget brands.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The cuticle alignment is genuine. I say that because multiple reviewers report the hair doesn't matte or tangle aggressively after washing—the #1 indicator of unaligned cuticles. One buyer noted, "I've worn these for three months with biweekly washes and they still brush through like day one." That's consistent with what I've seen from mid-tier Remy hair.

The texture is slightly coarse compared to premium Brazilian or Peruvian virgin hair. It's not rough, but it lacks that buttery softness you get at the $150+ per bundle price point. For daily wear, this is fine—the hair holds curls well and straightens crisply with a flat iron at 350°F.

Color performance deserves a mention: this is not true jet black. The "natural black" shade has a warm undertone that reads brown-black in natural light. If you have cool-toned hair, budget for a color correction. The upside? The cuticle alignment means it takes dye evenly. One reviewer successfully went from black to a honey blonde using bleach and toner in a single session without the hair turning brassy or mushy.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The longevity story is the strongest selling point. Multiple verified purchasers report 3-4 months of wear with proper maintenance—unusual at this price tier. The double weft construction means the tracks don't thin out at the sewing points, which is where cheaper single-weft extensions typically fail around week six.

Another consistent positive: the lack of chemical smell. Many budget human hair bundles arrive with that perm-like odor from processing. Full Shine's hair arrives neutral-smelling, which suggests minimal chemical processing.

## Where It Falls Short

Shedding is the big one—and it's inconsistent. Some buyers report minimal shedding, while others describe "handfuls of hair" coming out during the first brush. This variance suggests quality control issues between batches. If you get a good batch, you're set. If you don't, you're dealing with thinning wefts by week two.

The hair is also not as thick as advertised. The "full volume" claim is optimistic—you'll want to order an extra bundle for a full sew-in if you have medium-to-thick natural hair. Several reviewers mentioned needing to supplement with a second order.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're an experienced sew-in wearer who knows how to maintain extensions and wants reliable Remy hair without paying salon prices. The color versatility and longevity make it a solid value for someone who styles their hair regularly.

**Skip it if:** You're a first-time user looking for zero-maintenance hair, or you need true jet-black color. The shedding inconsistency is a gamble, and beginners won't know how to handle a bad batch.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I bleach these extensions?**
A: Yes, but with caution. The cuticle-aligned Remy hair processes evenly, but go 10-20 volume developer max and monitor closely. One session of lightening is fine; two is risky.

**Q: How long do these actually last?**
A: With proper care—washing every 7-10 days, avoiding heat above 350°F, and sealing the wefts—expect 3-4 months. Without maintenance, you'll see significant shedding by week 6.

**Q: How many bundles do I need for a full sew-in?**
A: For a standard install with medium-thickness natural hair, order 3-4 bundles. If you want dense volume or have thick hair, add a fifth.
