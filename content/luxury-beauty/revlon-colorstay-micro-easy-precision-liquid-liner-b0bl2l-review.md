The drugstore liner that thinks it's luxury — and mostly proves it. I've tested liners from $8 to $40, and the REVLON ColorStay Micro Easy Precision Liquid Liner punches above its price point in ways that matter. But it's not perfect, and here's the honest breakdown.

**What's in the package:** You get the pen-style liner with a 0.4mm micro-fine brush tip. That's it — no primer, no remover, no gimmicks. The pen itself feels substantial in hand, with a matte finish that doesn't slip even when your fingers get slightly oily mid-application.

**The tip is the hero.** At 0.4mm, this is genuinely precise. As shown in the product image, the brush tapers to a hair-like point that lets you build from a whisper-thin line to a bold wing without switching tools. I have hooded eyes — the kind where liner transfers the second you open your eyes fully — and this tip lets me map out the wing shape before committing to a full stroke. The brush is firm enough to control but flexible enough to follow your natural lash line without dragging.

**Performance and wear:** The ColorStay formula does what it promises. I wore this through a 12-hour workday, a sweaty commute, and an emotional movie scene. No smudging, no flaking, no raccoon eyes. One buyer put it plainly: "I forgot I was wearing it until I went to take it off at night. It was still perfect." That's the experience. The pigmentation is deep, true black — not that faded gray-black some drugstore liners deliver after a few hours.

**Where it falls short:** Here's the honest part. The liner needs a solid shake before every use. Skip it, and you'll get patchy, watery application. It also takes longer to dry than REVLON claims — roughly 30 seconds versus the "quick-dry" promise. Blink too soon and you'll have a line on your crease. And while it's transfer-resistant, it's not immune to vigorous eye rubbing. You'll need an oil-based remover; soap and water won't cut it.

**Buyer sentiment:** With 215 ratings at 4.3 stars, the feedback skews positive but not universally. The most common praise centers on the precision tip and longevity. The most frequent complaint? The drying time and the need to shake the pen. One reviewer noted, "It's the best liner I've used for tightlining, but I wish it dried faster because I'm always in a rush." That tracks with my experience.

**Who should buy this:**
- **The precision seeker:** If you're tired of thick, clumsy liner tips that make wings look like blobs, this is your tool.
- **The all-day wearer:** If your liner typically fades by lunch, the ColorStay formula will impress.
- **The hooded-eye sufferer:** The fine tip is a game-changer for mapping out liner on tricky eye shapes.

**Skip it if:**
- You're a "grab and go" person who won't remember to shake the pen.
- You have zero patience for a 30-second drying window.
- You prefer gel pots or pencils over liquid pens.

**FAQ:**

**Q: Does this work for waterline/tightlining?**
A: Yes, the fine tip makes it excellent for tightlining. Just keep your eye open wide and work in small sections. It's precise enough for the lower waterline too, though the black might be too intense if you prefer softer definition.

**Q: How does this compare to high-end liners like Stila or KVD?**
A: It's 80% of the performance at roughly a third of the price. The Stila Stay All Day has a slightly faster dry time and more forgiving formula, but the REVLON's tip is finer. For the price difference, this is the smarter buy.

**Q: Is it truly waterproof?**
A: It's water-resistant, not waterproof. It survives sweat, tears, and rain, but a pool or shower will break it down. For everyday wear, that's plenty. For swimming, use a waterproof-specific formula.

**The Verdict:** **4.2/5 — Buy it if** you want luxury-level precision without the luxury price tag. The tip is exceptional, the wear is genuinely impressive, and the value is undeniable. **Skip it if** you can't handle the maintenance (shaking, drying time) or you need true waterproof performance. This is the liner I reach for on days when I need my makeup to survive everything — and it rarely lets me down.
