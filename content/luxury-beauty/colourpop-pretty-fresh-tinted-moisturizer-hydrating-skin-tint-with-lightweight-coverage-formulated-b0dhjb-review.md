**The $14 tinted moisturizer that punches above its weight — but knows its lane**

Let me be blunt: most tinted moisturizers under $20 are either chalky, greasy, or evaporate by noon. ColourPop's Pretty Fresh doesn't. It's the rare drugstore-adjacent product that understands its job — **sheer, hydrating, skin-like color** — and executes it without pretending to be a full-coverage foundation.

---

**What's in the tube**

You get 1.0 fl oz of product in a **squeeze tube with a flip-top cap** — a smart choice, because pumps on lightweight formulas always clog. The texture is a fluid gel-cream that blends like a skincare product, not a makeup one. It contains **hyaluronic acid, coconut water, and a peptide complex**, which explains why it feels like you're applying a moisturizer, not paint.

The shade range is genuinely impressive: **24 options** spanning warm, neutral, and olive undertones. The "Pretty Fresh" line has always been ColourPop's most inclusive, and this formulation carries that forward.

---

**Performance and real-world wear**

I tested this over three weeks in humid summer weather. The finish is **dewy but not wet** — think "lit from within" rather than "sweaty." A pea-sized amount covers the entire face with a wash of color that evens out redness without masking your skin's natural texture.

**The hydration claim holds up.** By hour eight, my skin felt comfortable — no tightness, no dry patches flaking through. Multiple Amazon reviewers echo this. One wrote, *"This is the only tinted moisturizer that doesn't settle into my dry patches. My skin looks like skin, just better."* Another noted, *"I forget I'm wearing it. It's like my face but with a filter."*

**Where it falls short — and you need to hear this**

This is not a foundation. If you have acne, hyperpigmentation, or rosacea, **this will not cover it**. The coverage is maybe 30-40% at best. You'll need a separate concealer for blemishes and dark circles. That's not a flaw — it's the product's design — but shoppers expecting "lightweight coverage" to mean "hides my spots" will be disappointed.

Oily skin types should also proceed with caution. The dewy finish amplifies shine by midday. One reviewer summed it up: *"I have to powder my T-zone by noon or I look greasy. But my dry cheeks love it."* If you're oily, this needs a mattifying primer and setting powder to work.

**Also worth noting:** the SPF is only 15. Don't skip your dedicated sunscreen. This is a hydrating base, not sun protection.

---

**Who should buy this**

- **Dry or normal skin folks** who want a no-makeup makeup look for daily wear
- **Skincare-first minimalists** who hate the feel of foundation but want a touch of color
- **Anyone with olive or neutral undertones** who's tired of everything pulling pink or orange
- **Makeup beginners** who want something forgiving that won't look cakey

**Skip it if:**

- You need actual coverage for acne or discoloration
- You have oily skin and hate powdering
- You want a matte finish — this is dewy, period.

---

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this work for dark skin tones?**
A: Yes, mostly. The 24-shade range includes deep shades with red and golden undertones, not just "dark" shades that all look the same. However, the deepest shades sell out fast on Amazon — check availability.

**Q: Can I build it up to medium coverage?**
A: Not really. Layering more product just makes it look dewy and heavy, not more opaque. Use a concealer for targeted coverage.

**Q: How does it compare to Glossier's Skin Tint?**
A: It's more hydrating and has better shade depth. Glossier is thinner and even more sheer. ColourPop gives slightly more coverage and costs half the price.

---

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a comfortable, hydrating daily tint that makes your skin look like better skin — and you're not expecting to hide imperfections.

**Skip it if** you need coverage or have oily skin without a powdering habit.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — loses points for the dewy finish limitations and the SPF being too low to rely on. But for the price, this is one of the best lightweight tinted moisturizers you can buy, and the shade range alone puts it ahead of competitors at triple the cost.
