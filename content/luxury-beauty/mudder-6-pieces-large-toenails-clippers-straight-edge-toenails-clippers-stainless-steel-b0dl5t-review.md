## Where It Falls Short

The large head is a double-edged sword. If you have small feet or need to trim the corners of a pinky toenail, these feel clumsy. This is a brute-force tool for big toenails and thick nails — not a precision instrument.

There's also no nail catcher. You'll be chasing clippings across the bathroom floor unless you clip over a trash can. Minor, but worth knowing.
