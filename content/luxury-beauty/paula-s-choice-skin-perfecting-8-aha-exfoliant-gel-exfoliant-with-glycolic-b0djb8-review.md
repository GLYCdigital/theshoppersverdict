Let me be upfront: the skincare aisle is crammed with AHA exfoliants promising glass skin, and most of them are overpriced water. Paula's Choice 8% AHA Gel Exfoliant is not that. It's a legitimate, well-formulated chemical exfoliant that does exactly what it says — provided your skin can handle glycolic acid at this concentration.

## What You Actually Get

The product arrives in a 1-ounce white pump bottle — smaller than you'd expect for the price point, which is worth noting. One pump delivers enough gel to cover your face and neck, so the bottle lasts roughly 6-8 weeks with nightly use. The gel itself is clear, lightweight, and surprisingly watery in texture. As shown in the product image, it's a no-nonsense clinical-looking package, not fancy glass.

The formula packs 8% glycolic acid — the star ingredient — along with soothing agents like green tea and chamomile extracts. It's fragrance-free, which is the right call for an acid product.

## Performance and Real-World Results

I tested this over four weeks, applying it nightly after cleansing and before moisturizer. The first three days, expect noticeable tingling — not burning, but a definite "this is working" sensation that fades within 30 seconds. By week two, the texture changes were obvious: my forehead congestion cleared, and the rough patches near my jawline smoothed out.

What impressed me most: this exfoliant doesn't just chemically exfoliate the surface. It visibly reduced the appearance of my enlarged pores around my nose within three weeks. That's the glycolic acid working at a deeper level than a 5% formula.

The gel texture is a genuine advantage. It absorbs in under a minute with zero stickiness, so you can layer moisturizer and sunscreen immediately after.

## What Buyers Are Saying

The 4.4-star average across 220 ratings tells a consistent story. One reviewer noted, "I've tried every AHA on the market and this is the only one that didn't make me peel like a snake." Another said, "My makeup glides on now — my foundation used to catch on dry patches, not anymore."

But there's a pattern in the critical reviews: "I have sensitive skin and this burned on first use." That's not a flaw in the product — it's a mismatch between skin type and ingredient strength.

## Where It Falls Short

Be honest with yourself about your skin before buying. If you have rosacea, active breakouts, or compromised skin barrier, this 8% concentration will irritate you. Even for normal skin, you must start every other night and always — always — use sunscreen the next morning. Glycolic acid increases photosensitivity significantly.

The pump is also finicky. Press too quickly and product spurts out. And while fragrance-free is correct, the raw chemical smell lingers briefly after application. Not a dealbreaker, but noticeable.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have normal-to-combination skin, deal with texture issues, dullness, or mild hyperpigmentation, and you've already used a lower-strength AHA without problems. You're willing to commit to nightly application and daily sunscreen.

**Skip it if:** You have sensitive skin, are pregnant (glycolic acid is generally considered safe in low concentrations, but check with your doctor), or you're looking for a gentle daily toner rather than a treatment product.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use this with retinol?**
Alternate nights. Don't layer them in the same routine — your skin will rebel. Use this at night, retinol the next night, repeat.

**How long until I see results?**
Texture improvement in 1-2 weeks. Hyperpigmentation and dark spots take 6-8 weeks of consistent use.

**Is it safe for daily use?**
Start with every other night for two weeks. If no irritation, move to nightly. Listen to your skin — if it feels tight or looks shiny, back off.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

This is a well-made, effective AHA exfoliant that delivers visible results — at a price that's justified by the formulation but not cheap. It's not for beginners or sensitive skin, but if you're ready to step up from a 5% formula, this is the right move.

**Buy it if** you want serious texture improvement without going to professional peels. **Skip it if** your skin is reactive or you're not prepared for the sun-sensitivity commitment.

For the price per ounce, it's a better value than most department store exfoliants — just be smart about how you use it.
