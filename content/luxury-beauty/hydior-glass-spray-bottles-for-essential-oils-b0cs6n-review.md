**FAQ**

**Q: Can these handle pure essential oils without degrading?**

**Q: How do I clean these between different liquids?**
A: Rinse with warm water, then fill halfway with white vinegar, shake, and let sit for 10 minutes. Rinse thoroughly. The narrow neck makes cleaning a bit tedious, but the funnel helps with refilling.

**Q: Will the sprayer fit other standard bottles if the glass breaks?**
