### Where It Falls Short

Every product has tradeoffs, and these are honest ones:

- **No individual wrapping.** If you're using these for professional clients, the communal jar means every wand is exposed to air and potential dust between uses. You'll need to keep the lid firmly closed, and even then, it's not sterile.
- **The lid is tight.** Genuinely snug — removing it with damp hands requires effort. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.
- **Bristle density varies slightly** between batches. Most wands are densely packed, but occasional ones feel a bit looser. Not enough to affect performance, but noticeable if you're obsessive.
