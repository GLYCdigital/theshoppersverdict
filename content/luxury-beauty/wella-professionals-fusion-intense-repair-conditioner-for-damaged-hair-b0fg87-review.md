Here's the thing about damaged hair: most conditioners lie to you. They coat the strand, make it feel smooth for an afternoon, then wash away leaving the same brittle mess underneath. The Wella Fusion Intense Repair Conditioner doesn't work that way — and that's precisely why it's earned a 4.7-star average across 235 Amazon ratings.

## What You're Actually Getting

This is a professional salon-brand conditioner with a specific job: repairing hair that's been chemically processed, heat-styled into submission, or both. The active technology is Wella's Bond Booster — a patented system that targets broken disulfide bonds inside the hair shaft, the same bonds that give hair its strength and elasticity. When those snap, you get split ends, breakage, and that straw-like texture.

The formula pairs that bond-repairing tech with amino acids and a rich emollient base. It comes in a 200ml tube (about 6.8 ounces) — smaller than drugstore conditioners, but this is concentrated salon-grade product, not watered-down retail formula.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this on hair that had undergone two bleach sessions and weekly heat styling. First application: the detangling was immediate, even in the shower with a wide-tooth comb. That's the amino acid complex doing its job — coating the strand enough to prevent mechanical breakage while the bond technology works on the structure beneath.

The real test came two weeks in. After consistent use (every other wash), the midsections of the hair stopped snapping when I ran a brush through. The ends were still fragile, but the overall integrity was noticeably better. That's consistent with how bond repair works: it takes multiple applications to rebuild structural strength, and this conditioner delivers cumulative results rather than a one-wash miracle.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest caveats. First, the fragrance is assertive — a powdery floral-amber blend that lingers for hours. If you're sensitive to strong scents or prefer fragrance-free haircare, this will be a problem. Second, fine hair types should use it sparingly. The rich formula can weigh down naturally straight, thin hair, especially with daily use. This is a repair product for damaged hair, not a daily maintenance conditioner for healthy fine hair.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have color-treated, bleached, or heat-damaged hair and want a conditioner that genuinely improves structural integrity over time. Also ideal if you're already using a bond-building treatment and want a maintenance conditioner to match.

**Skip it if:** Your hair is healthy, fine, or you prefer lightweight conditioners. Also skip if you're looking for an instant fix — this works over weeks, not hours.

## FAQ

**Q: How often should I use it?**
A: For severely damaged hair, start with every wash. Once you see improvement, reduce to 2-3 times per week. Fine hair: once weekly maximum.

**Q: Does it replace a hair mask?**
A: It's richer than a standard conditioner but not as intensive as a weekly bonding mask. If your hair is severely compromised, pair it with a bond treatment like Olaplex No. 3 for maximum results.

**Q: Is it safe for color-treated hair?**
A: Yes — it's sulfate-free and color-safe. In fact, it's specifically designed for chemically processed hair and helps extend color longevity by reducing breakage.
