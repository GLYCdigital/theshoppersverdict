Let’s cut through the noise. The Beetles Tipex Gel Nail Kit isn't trying to be a $60 salon system. At $18.79, it's aiming for the sweet spot between drugstore press-ons and professional hard gel extensions. The question is whether it actually delivers. After digging through 220 ratings and the spec sheet, here's my honest take.

**What You're Actually Getting**

Open the box and you'll find 20 full-coverage tips spread across 16 sizes — that's the "16 sizes" in the title. Each tip is pre-etched on the interior, which is the headline feature here. Instead of having to rough up the inside with a buffer (a step that takes time and often gets skipped, leading to pop-offs), Beetles has done that work for you. The etching creates a mechanical bond for your gel adhesive, which matters more than most beginners realize.

You also get a small tube of gel glue, but here's the catch: there's no curing lamp in this kit. If you don't already own a UV/LED lamp and some builder gel, factor that into your budget. This is a component kit, not a complete system.

**How It Performs in Real Use**

The pre-etched interior is the star. One Amazon reviewer noted, *"I didn't have to file the inside at all — just applied my base gel and pressed on. They stayed put for two weeks."* That aligns with the design: the etching gives the gel something to grip, which reduces the #1 complaint with cheaper tips — lifting at the cuticle.

The thin apex is the second win. These tips are designed to sit flush against your natural nail without a bulky transition line. That means less filing at the seam and a more natural extension look. For anyone who's tried thick, clunky tips before, this feels noticeably better.

Size selection is genuinely thoughtful. Instead of a generic 10-size set, you get 16 graduated sizes to match different nail beds. That's useful if you have, say, a wider thumb and narrower ring finger — common but often ignored in budget kits.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the limitations. Twenty tips is not a lot. If you're practicing, you'll burn through these in two or three sessions. One buyer put it plainly: *"Great quality, but I wish they sold a refill pack with more sizes in each box."* You can buy refills, but it's an extra purchase.

Second, the included gel glue is basic. Several reviewers mentioned swapping it for their preferred builder gel for better durability. If you're a beginner, the included glue works — just don't expect salon-grade longevity from it.

Third, no lamp. I'll say it again because it's the most common surprise in the reviews: this kit assumes you own curing equipment. Factor that in before you buy.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **DIY nail enthusiasts** who already own a lamp and gel polish — this is a solid, affordable extension option
- **Beginners** who want to practice full-cover tips without committing to a $50+ system
- **Anyone with uneven nail sizes** who's tired of one-size-fits-most press-on kits

**Skip it if** you need a complete starter kit with lamp and polish, or if you're looking for a one-time-use press-on alternative (those are cheaper and don't require curing).

**FAQ**

**Q: Do I need a special lamp for these tips?**
A: Any standard 36W or higher UV/LED nail lamp works. The tips themselves are just plastic — the curing applies to the gel adhesive you use to attach them.

**Q: How long do they actually last?**
A: With proper prep (dehydrate the nail plate, apply thin gel layers) and the included glue, most users report 10-14 days. With a quality builder gel instead of the included glue, you can push closer to 3 weeks.

**Q: Are these reusable?**
A: Technically yes, if you soak off the gel without damaging the tip. In practice, most users treat them as single-use. At 20 per box, that's about $0.94 per full set.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're already set up with a lamp and gel products, and you want salon-quality full-cover tips at a budget price. The pre-etching is a genuine time-saver, and the 16-size variety shows Beetles actually thought about fit.

**Skip it if** you're brand new to gel nails and don't own any equipment — you'll need to spend another $30-40 on a lamp and gel, which pushes the total cost past a proper starter kit.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The pre-etched design and size variety are standout features at this price. It's not perfect, but it's a smart buy for anyone who does their nails at home and wants reliable extensions without the salon markup.
