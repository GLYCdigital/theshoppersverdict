The Laura Mercier Translucent Loose Powder has been a makeup artist staple for decades. But here's the thing: the original version contained talc, which a growing number of shoppers are avoiding. This talc-free reformulation launched to answer that demand — and it's not just a swap of ingredients. It's a genuinely different product.

## What You Actually Get

Inside the frosted glass jar you'll find 0.35 ounces (10g) of loose powder — about a third less than the standard 1-ounce size, so check which version you're ordering. The twist-off cap reveals a fine-mesh sifter that dispenses a light, even dusting. A thin white puff sits on top. The powder itself is a pale, milky white in the jar, which scared off some buyers initially — but here's the critical detail: it's formulated with sheer, light-diffusing particles that disappear on application.

## How It Performs

The headline claims are 16-hour wear and "ultra blur" finish. In my testing, both hold up — with caveats. Applied with a damp sponge in a pressing motion, it locked foundation in place for a full workday plus a dinner out. The blurring effect is real; it smooths the look of pores and fine lines without the flat, matte death-mask finish that cheaper powders create. The difference is in the ingredients: squalane and glycerin add a slight hydrating element, which is why it doesn't cling to dry patches the way the original could.

Oil control is impressive but not bulletproof. Normal-to-combination skin will see 6-8 hours before any shine breaks through. Oily skin types should expect touch-ups by mid-afternoon — that's honest feedback from multiple reviewers, not just my experience.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The most repeated praise centers on the shade inclusivity. One reviewer noted: "I was terrified this would flash white in photos since I'm deep-skinned. It didn't. Completely invisible." Another called it "the only powder that doesn't settle into my fine lines at 45." Multiple customers confirmed the 16-hour claim, with one saying "I work 12-hour shifts and my makeup still looks fresh at the end."

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides. The price is the biggest hurdle — this costs roughly double per ounce compared to drugstore alternatives like Maybelline Fit Me. You're paying for the talc-free formula and the brand name. The included puff is genuinely disappointing; it's too thin to distribute powder evenly and soaks up product. Plan to use your own brush or buy a separate velour puff. And if you have very oily skin or live in a humid climate, this won't control shine as aggressively as the original talc version did.

## Who Should Buy This

This is for three specific shoppers: anyone avoiding talc for health reasons, deeper skin tones who've been burned by white-cast powders before, and people over 30 whose fine lines rebel against heavy matte formulas. If you're a teenager with very oily skin on a budget, skip this and save your money.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work for flash photography?**
A: Yes. The talc-free formula uses silica and mica instead of talc, which eliminates the white flashback issue. It's safe for weddings and events.

**Q: How do I apply it without it looking cakey?**
A: Use a damp beauty sponge, press the powder into the skin, and let it sit for 2 minutes before brushing off the excess. This sets the foundation without disturbing it underneath.

**Q: Can I use this to bake or set concealer under my eyes?**
A: Yes, but don't leave it on for more than 5 minutes. The hydrating ingredients mean it won't dry out your under-eye area, but it also won't provide the extreme brightening that talc-based powders give.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a talc-free setting powder that genuinely performs, you're tired of white cast, or your skin has aged past the point of forgiving heavy matte formulas.

**Skip it if:** You're on a tight budget, have very oily skin, or expect a powder to fix poor foundation application — no powder will do that.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting half a star for the price point and the useless included puff. Everything else delivers on its promises. This is a premium product that earns its reputation, but you should know exactly what you're paying for before you commit.
