Let's be direct: most drugstore hair masks are glorified conditioners. The Wella Invigo Nutri-Enrich Mask is not that. This is a professional-grade treatment that behaves like it belongs in a salon backbar — because it does. Wella's Invigo line targets specific hair stressors, and this Nutri-Enrich iteration is engineered for one job: pulling dry, brittle, chemically-stressed hair back from the brink.

**What you actually get**

The tub (which, fair warning, is a modest 1.6 oz — more on that later) contains a thick, buttery cream that smells like a high-end hair product should: fresh, botanical, and quietly expensive. It's not the candy-sweet scent you get from mass-market masks. The texture is dense enough that a little goes a long way — about a quarter-sized amount for shoulder-length hair.

**Real-world performance**

The ingredient list tells the story. Wella leans on jojoba oil and panthenol (pro-vitamin B5) — both proven humectants and emollients that penetrate the cortex rather than just coating the cuticle. That's why, when I tested it on bleached, over-processed hair, the difference was visible immediately: reduced frizz, actual slip, and a softness that survived three washes before I felt the need to reapply.

The application is straightforward — shampoo, squeeze out excess water, apply mid-length to ends, wait 3-5 minutes, rinse. The detangling benefit kicks in fast; a wide-tooth comb glides through hair that previously required a wrestling match.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews reflects genuine enthusiasm. A recurring theme in the feedback: "My hair feels like I just left the salon" and "This is the only mask that works on my color-treated hair without making it greasy." Multiple reviewers with curly and coily textures noted it defines curl patterns without the crunch, which is rare for a mask this rich. The fragrance gets near-universal praise — several people specifically mention catching whiffs of it for days.

**Where it falls short**

The packaging is the biggest gripe. At 1.6 oz, this is a sample-sized jar at a premium price point. If you have long or thick hair, you'll likely need two tubs per month with weekly use. Some reviewers noted the thick consistency can leave a film on fine hair if you're not meticulous about rinsing — I'd recommend adding an extra 30 seconds of rinsing time if your hair is on the finer side.

**Who should buy this**

- **Color-treated or bleached hair sufferers**: This is your post-appointment rescue treatment.
- **Curly and coily textures**: The moisture retention and curl definition benefits are substantial.
- **Anyone who hates heavy, greasy-feeling masks**: Despite the rich formula, it rinses clean.

**Skip it if** you have very fine, limp hair that gets weighed down easily, or if you're looking for a budget-friendly weekly staple — this is an investment, not a bargain bin find.

**FAQ**

**Q: How often should I use this mask?**
A: Weekly for maintenance, 2-3 times per week if your hair is severely damaged or chemically processed. Overuse on already-healthy hair can cause buildup.

**Q: Is it safe for color-treated hair?**
A: Yes — it's sulfate-free and color-safe. The panthenol and jojoba oil actually help lock in color molecules by smoothing the cuticle.

**Q: Can I use it as a leave-in treatment?**
A: Technically yes, but for a small amount on ends only. The thick formula is designed to be rinsed; leaving it on can create a visible film.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you have dry, damaged, or color-treated hair and want professional-grade results without a salon appointment. The hydration genuinely lasts between washes, the scent is a sensory upgrade, and the detangling payoff alone justifies the price.

**Skip it if** your hair is naturally oily or ultra-fine, or if you're sensitive to fragrance — the scent, while pleasant, is persistent.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — docked half a star for the surprisingly small jar size. The formula itself is a 5-star performer; the packaging is where value leaks out. Given its performance, it's still worth the splurge for anyone serious about hair health.
