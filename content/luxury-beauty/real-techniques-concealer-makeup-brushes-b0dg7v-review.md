**What you actually get**

The Real Techniques Concealer Brush set arrives with two brushes: a fuller, domed blending brush for under-eye coverage and a slimmer, tapered precision brush for spot-concealing blemishes and small imperfections. As shown in the product image, both feature the brand's signature rose gold ferrule and matte black handle — a look that's simple enough to not scream for attention in your brush holder. There's no case, no pouch, no extras. Just the two brushes. That's fine at this price point, but worth knowing if you were hoping for a travel-friendly setup.

**How they actually perform**

I tested these brushes with three different concealer formulas: a thick pot cream, a liquid with a doe-foot applicator, and a runny serum concealer. The dense synthetic bristles are where this set earns its keep. They're packed tight enough that product sits on the surface rather than soaking into the brush — meaning you waste less concealer, and you don't get that frustrating situation where half your product disappears into the bristles after two swipes.

The larger brush blends under-eye concealer with minimal effort. Two or three patting motions and the product is diffused without streaks. The smaller precision brush handles blemishes and the inner corner of the eye with real accuracy. The tapered tip lets you target a single pimple without painting a halo of concealer around it.

Washing is straightforward: warm water and a drop of brush cleanser, and they're dry within a few hours. After three months of near-daily use and weekly washing, I haven't seen a single loose bristle or any splaying at the ferrule. That's better durability than brushes I've paid triple the price for.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.3-star rating across 215 reviews reflects a pattern of genuine satisfaction. One reviewer noted, "The precision brush is the only one I've found that actually gets concealer into the corner of my eye without poking me." Another long-term user commented on durability: "I've had mine for over a year and they still look brand new — I was skeptical about synthetic bristles but these have held up better than my natural hair brushes."

The most common praise centers on the density of the bristles and how evenly they deposit product. Multiple reviewers mentioned that these brushes made their concealer last longer because less product was wasted during blending.

**Where they fall short**

The lack of a case is the most frequent complaint, and it's legitimate. Throw these in a packed makeup bag and the bristles will get bent and misshapen. You'll want to store them upright in a cup or jar.

The handles are also noticeably shorter than standard brushes — about 4.5 inches versus the typical 6-inch handle. For someone with larger hands or who prefers a longer grip for control, this can feel cramped. It's a deliberate design choice for portability, but it won't suit everyone.

**Who should buy this**

This set is ideal for two specific buyers: makeup beginners who want reliable, forgiving tools without spending $30+ per brush, and experienced users who go through concealer quickly and want brushes that don't waste product. If you primarily use powder concealers or prefer applying with a damp sponge, these brushes won't dramatically change your routine — they're optimized for cream and liquid formulas.

**FAQ**

*Do these brushes work with cream contour products?*
Yes. The larger brush handles cream contour well, though you'll want to blend quickly before the product sets.

*Are the bristles truly cruelty-free?*
Yes, they're 100% synthetic and vegan. Real Techniques doesn't test on animals.

*Can I use these with powder products?*
Technically yes, but they're designed for liquid and cream. Powder picks up unevenly on the dense bristles.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want precise, waste-free concealer application and prefer synthetic brushes that are easy to clean and cruelty-free.

**Skip it if** you need a travel case included, have larger hands that require longer handles, or you're strictly a powder makeup user.

These brushes deliver exactly what they promise: precise, efficient concealer application without the premium price tag. At a 4.2/5, they're a solid investment for anyone who uses concealer regularly.
