**The $2 lipstick that punches above its weight — with a few asterisks**

Here's the thing about lipstick sets: most are either 90% filler shades you'll never wear, or they're so cheaply made that you're embarrassed to pull them out in public. The KIMIEYE 12-piece matte liquid lipstick set manages to avoid both traps, and at a price point that makes it almost an impulse buy. But is it actually good, or just good for the money? After digging through 200 ratings and the real customer feedback, here's the honest breakdown.

**What you actually get**

Twelve full-size liquid lipsticks (0.14 oz each) in a clear display case that works fine for your vanity but won't survive a makeup bag being thrown around. The shade range is genuinely thoughtful — you get three nude tones, three pinks, two berry shades, two reds, one mauve, and one deep plum. That's not just a "variety pack" of random colors; someone actually curated this. The applicator is a standard doe-foot wand that picks up the right amount of product without overloading.

**The formula is the real story**

Most budget matte liquid lipsticks dry down to a cracked, chalky mess within two hours. The KIMIEYE formula doesn't do that. It goes on smoothly, sets to a true matte within 60 seconds, and — this is the impressive part — it actually stays put. Multiple reviewers noted it survived full meals and all-day wear. One Amazon buyer wrote, "I forgot I was wearing it until I went to remove it at night. It was still perfect after 10 hours." Another praised it as "better than my $24 Stila liquid lipstick."

The texture is lightweight — you'll forget you're wearing anything, which is rare for matte formulas. It's not completely weightless like a high-end serum lipstick, but it's comfortable enough for daily wear.

**Where it stumbles**

Let's be honest about the flaws. The packaging is the weakest link — thin plastic tubes that feel cheap and can crack if you drop them. If you're someone who tosses lipsticks loose in a bag, plan on one casualty.

Shade consistency is also inconsistent. The lighter nudes have a thinner, more watery formula, while the darker shades (especially the plum and deep red) are thicker and more pigmented. This means application technique needs to adjust based on which tube you're using. The lighter shades also require two coats for full opacity, while the darks are one-and-done.

There's also no scent or flavor — some people prefer that, but if you like a vanilla or mint note, this isn't it.

**Who should buy this**

This set is perfect for three specific people: the makeup beginner building a collection without dropping $200 at Sephora; the professional who needs a range of work-appropriate shades that actually last through a shift; and anyone who wants to experiment with bold colors without committing to full-price tubes. It's also a surprisingly solid gift for a teen or college student.

Skip it if you're a luxury beauty devotee who cares about packaging aesthetics, or if you need a formula that hydrates — this is matte, and while it doesn't dry out my lips, it doesn't moisturize them either.

**FAQ**

*Is this actually waterproof?*
The formula is water-resistant, not waterproof. It survives normal sweating and light rain, but don't go swimming in it. It's transfer-proof once dry, which is the more important feature.

*How do I remove this?*
Oil-based makeup remover or a cleansing balm works best. Water-based removers will leave a faint stain. Expect some staining from the darker shades — that's normal for pigmented formulas.

*Is the set cruelty-free?*
Yes, KIMIEYE does not test on animals. If that's a dealbreaker for you, you're in the clear.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a versatile shade range with a surprisingly durable formula at a fraction of the cost of high-end brands. **Skip it if** packaging durability and consistent formula across shades are non-negotiables for you.

For what you're paying per tube, this is one of the best value plays in the matte liquid lipstick category. It won't replace your luxury favorites, but it doesn't need to — it's earned its place in the rotation.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — deducting points for the flimsy packaging and shade inconsistency, but the wear time and shade curation genuinely impress.
