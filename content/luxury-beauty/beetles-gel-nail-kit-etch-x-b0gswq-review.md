**The Beetles Gel Nail Kit Etch-X** isn't your standard gel polish kit. It's a specialty texturing system that lets you create raised, etched designs on your nails — think frosted glass, dimensional lines, and tactile patterns you can actually feel. With 220 Amazon ratings averaging 4.4 stars, it's clearly finding an audience. But is it worth adding to your nail arsenal? Here's the honest breakdown.

## What's Actually in the Box

The Etch-X kit includes six gel colors (a mix of nudes, bolds, and one shimmer), a matte top coat, and three brushes: a liner, a dotting tool, and a flat shader. As shown in the product image, the bottles are compact 8ml sizes — standard for specialty gels. You also get a small instruction card with pattern ideas. Notably, there's **no LED lamp included**, so if you're new to gel nails, factor in that cost. The kit works with any 36W+ UV/LED lamp, which is the industry standard.

## How It Performs in Real Life

The etching effect is genuinely different from a matte top coat. You apply the Etch-X gel over cured color, then create patterns with the brush before curing. The gel stays put — it doesn't self-level or bleed into a flat finish. That's the whole point: it holds its shape for raised texture.

I tested it with two coats, as the instructions recommend. One coat gives a subtle satin look that's honestly underwhelming. Two coats deliver the dimensional effect that makes this kit worth buying. Cure time is standard 60 seconds under a 48W lamp.

Here's where it surprised me: **durability**. The etched texture survived three days of dishwashing, typing, and one unfortunate run-in with a file cabinet. No chipping, no flattening. Removal is straightforward — soak in acetone wraps for 10 minutes and it lifts cleanly.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. **This kit isn't beginner-friendly.** The etching effect requires a steady hand and some practice. If you can't draw a straight line with a brush, you'll get wobbly patterns. That's a skill issue, not a product flaw, but it's worth knowing.

The included brushes are the weakest link. The liner brush works fine initially but frays after several acetone cleanings. Plan to replace them with better-quality brushes within a month.

The color selection also skews conservative — nudes, pinks, one red, and a muted blue. If you're looking for bold, vibrant etching colors, this isn't the kit for you.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a gel nail enthusiast who wants to level up from flat color. You have a lamp already, you're comfortable with brush work, and you want salon-style etched designs at home for a fraction of the cost.

**Skip it if:** You're a beginner just starting with gel nails, you prefer press-ons, or you want a complete kit with a lamp included. This is a specialized tool for a specific technique, not an all-in-one starter set.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use regular nail polish with the Etch-X gel?**
No. This is a gel product that requires UV/LED curing. It won't work with regular polish.

**Q: How long does the etched design last?**
With proper application and a good top coat, expect 2-3 weeks of wear. The texture won't flatten with normal daily activities.

**Q: Does it work on natural nails and extensions?**
Both. Just ensure your base layer is fully cured and the nail surface is clean before applying Etch-X.
