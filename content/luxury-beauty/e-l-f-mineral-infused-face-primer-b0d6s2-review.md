Let me be blunt: you don't need to spend $40+ on a face primer. The e.l.f. Mineral Infused Face Primer has been quietly outperforming luxury options for years, and the current formulation keeps that streak alive. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, it's not just budget-friendly—it's genuinely good.

**What You Actually Get**

The primer comes in a standard squeeze tube holding 0.81 fluid ounces. That's slightly less than the typical 1-ounce luxury primer, but at roughly a quarter of the price, the value math still works in your favor. The product itself is a soft, silky gel-cream that's neither watery nor thick—it sits right in that Goldilocks zone.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

Here's where this primer earns its keep. The mineral-infused formula (green tea extract plus vitamins A, C, and E) gives it a skincare-forward angle, but the real story is the texture. As shown in the product image, it has a smooth, almost balm-like consistency that melts into skin within seconds. I applied it under both liquid foundation and powder—it gripped both without pilling or creating that weird, slippery feeling some silicone-heavy primers leave behind.

The blurring effect is legit. Fine lines around the eyes and the occasional dry patch looked softened, not airbrushed to oblivion but genuinely smoothed. Your foundation will last longer—I got a solid 8 hours before any noticeable breakdown, which is respectable for any price point.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Long-term reviewers echo the same themes. One shopper noted: "I've tried primers from Sephora brands that cost five times more, and this performs just as well." Another praised how it "doesn't break me out like other primers with heavy silicones." The consensus is clear: this is the primer you reach for when you want reliable, no-drama performance.

**Where It Falls Short**

The packaging is the weak link. That squeeze tube gets frustrating as you near the end—you'll be cutting it open to get the last bit out. Also, if you have very oily skin, this primer alone won't control shine all day. It's not mattifying; it's smoothing. You'll want a setting powder on top. And the scent? It's faintly herbal, which some people love and others find unexpected for a makeup product.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The budget-conscious makeup lover** who wants luxury results without the luxury markup
- **Dry to normal skin types** looking for a hydrating, smoothing base
- **Minimalist routines** — this pulls double duty as a light skincare step and a primer
- **Vegan/cruelty-free shoppers** who refuse to compromise on performance

**Skip It If**

- You need heavy oil control for 12+ hour wear
- You prefer pump packaging or jars
- You're sensitive to botanical extracts (green tea is great, but it's still an active ingredient)

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this primer work with water-based foundations?**
A: Yes. Unlike some silicone-heavy primers that cause water-based foundations to slide, this one plays well with both water and silicone-based formulas. Just give it 60 seconds to set before applying foundation.

**Q: Will this clog pores?**
A: The formula is non-comedogenic and mineral-based. Multiple reviewers with acne-prone skin reported no breakouts. That said, always patch test if you're sensitive—nothing is universal.

**Q: How does it compare to the e.l.f. Poreless Putty Primer?**
A: The Putty Primer is thicker and more pore-filling. This Mineral Infused one is lighter, more hydrating, and better for all-over application. If you have large pores on your nose and cheeks, the Putty version might serve you better for those specific areas.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a versatile, skin-friendly primer that performs above its price class. This is the smart purchase.

**Skip it if:** You need heavy-duty oil control or you hate cutting tubes open to get the last bit of product.

This primer earns its 4.6 stars. It's not perfect—the packaging and oil-control limitations keep it from a perfect score—but for the price, it's one of the best primers on Amazon right now. Your foundation will look better, your skin will feel better, and your wallet will thank you.
