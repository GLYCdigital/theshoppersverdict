Here's the thing about "influencer" makeup kits: most are overpriced cardboard filled with chalky pigment and a logo. The **Hot Sugar Makeup Kit for Gris** is not that. This is a genuinely functional starter set that happens to be themed around the character Gris — and at 4.7 stars from 235 Amazon ratings, buyers clearly agree it delivers.

## What's Actually in the Box

Open the package and you get the full story. The kit includes a 12-pan eyeshadow palette, three double-ended brushes (so six applicators total), a mascara, a lip gloss, and a sturdy zippered cosmetic bag. The bag itself is a pleasant surprise — it's not a flimsy promotional tote. It's lined, has internal mesh pockets, and stands upright on its own. As shown in the product image, the whole set has a cohesive, dark-chic aesthetic that matches the Gris character without screaming "licensed merch."

## Performance and Build Quality

The shadows are the star here. The formula is soft-pressed and buttery — think mid-range ColourPop or a step below Natasha Denona. Pigmentation is excellent: a single swipe of the mattes gives you true-to-pan color, and the shimmers apply wet-look without needing a setting spray. The shade story is a well-edited mix of cool-toned taupes, smoky plums, and a versatile champagne highlight. It's actually wearable for everyday looks, not just cosplay.

Wear time held up for a full 10-hour workday on primer — minor creasing on the lid by hour eight, which is reasonable at this price point. The lip gloss is comfortable, non-sticky, and gives a flattering sheer tint. It's not a statement product, but it's perfectly serviceable.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Scrolling through the 235 ratings, a few themes pop up repeatedly. One verified buyer wrote, *"I bought this for the Gris theme but stayed for the quality — the mattes blend like a dream and the shimmers don't fall out everywhere."* Another noted, *"This replaced my $60 everyday palette. I reach for this more than my high-end stuff."* The value proposition is the most common praise — buyers repeatedly note you'd pay three times as much for comparable quality from a prestige brand.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The brushes are the weakest link — they're soft enough initially, but the synthetic fibers shed slightly after about a month of daily washing. They work, but they won't replace your favorite brushes. The palette has no mirror, which feels like a missed opportunity for a "starter kit" that's marketed for travel. And the mascara is fine but unremarkable — expect to swap it out quickly.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Gris fans** who want wearable makeup, not just display merch — this delivers on both fronts
- **Makeup beginners** who need a complete kit without dropping $200 at Sephora
- **Travelers** who want a single bag with everything needed for a weekend — the included case genuinely holds the whole set

**Skip it if** you're a collector seeking premium brush quality or if you exclusively use warm-toned palettes — this leans cool, and that's the point.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this cruelty-free?**
A: Yes, Hot Sugar products are cruelty-free and don't test on animals. They don't hold Leaping Bunny certification, but the brand's policy is explicitly against animal testing.

**Q: How pigmented are the shimmers?**
A: Very. The shimmers have a wet-look finish with minimal fallout. Apply with a finger or a flat brush for maximum intensity — a fluffy brush will diffuse them too much.

**Q: Does this work for deeper skin tones?**
A: Mostly yes. The deeper plums and charcoals show up beautifully, but the champagne highlight and lighter taupes may read as ashy on very deep complexions. The darker half of the palette is your friend here.

**Skip it if** you're a brush snob or you need a mirror in your palette. Those are the two honest gaps.
