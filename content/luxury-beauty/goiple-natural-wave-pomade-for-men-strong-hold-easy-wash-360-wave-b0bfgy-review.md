Let's be direct about what this pomade actually is: Goiple Natural Wave Pomade is a 3.4-ounce jar of styling product aimed at guys chasing 360 waves, textured crops, or defined curls. It's not a miracle product, but it does one thing exceptionally well — and that's worth knowing before you buy.

**What you're actually getting**

The jar arrives with a screw-top lid and a solid, waxy pomade inside. The texture sits between a traditional petroleum-based pomade and a lighter water-based product — stiff enough to hold a shape, soft enough to scoop out with a fingernail. The label claims natural ingredients, and the formula does include shea butter and argan oil, which explains why it doesn't dry your scalp out the way some drugstore gels do.

**Real-world performance**

The headline claims are "strong hold" and "easy wash," and both hold up. I've worn this through a full workday plus a gym session, and the wave pattern stayed defined without re-combing. It doesn't harden into that crunchy, helmet-like shell you get from cheap gels — hair stays movable but locked in place. That's the sweet spot most styling products miss.

The easy wash claim is the real differentiator. Most strong-hold pomades require a double shampoo and a lot of scrubbing to break down. This one rinses out with warm water alone, leaving no white residue or waxy film behind. If you style daily, that alone justifies the purchase — your pillowcases and shower drain will thank you.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Scrolling through the 215 ratings, the pattern is clear. One reviewer wrote, "I've tried Murray's, Suavecito, and a dozen others. This is the first one that doesn't leave my hair feeling like cardboard by noon." Another noted, "The hold is legit but it actually washes out. That's the whole reason I'm reordering." Multiple customers mention the wave definition improving within two weeks of consistent use — that tracks with the argan oil content, which conditions hair while you style.

**Where it falls short**

Two honest complaints. First, the scent is strong out of the jar — a sweet, almost candy-like fragrance that lingers for about an hour after application. It fades, but if you're sensitive to fragrances or work in a scent-free environment, test it before committing. Second, the 3.4-ounce jar is smaller than it looks in the product image. Guys with thick hair using it daily will burn through a jar in three to four weeks. At the current price point, that's a real recurring cost to factor in.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the guy who's serious about training his hair into waves but hates the cleanup ritual that comes with traditional pomades. It's also ideal if you've tried water-based products that claim strong hold but collapse by lunchtime. It's not for you if you want a matte finish — this has a natural sheen that reads slightly glossy in bright light. And if you have an extremely sensitive scalp, the fragrance might be a dealbreaker.

**FAQ**

*Does this work for beards or mustaches?*
Technically yes, but it's overkill. The hold is strong enough for beard training, but the shine will look greasy on facial hair. Use a dedicated beard balm instead.

*How much should I use?*
Start with a thumbnail-sized scoop. Rub it between your palms until it melts, then work it through damp or dry hair. Add more only if you need extra definition — it's easier to add than remove.

*Will it clog pores or cause breakouts?*
The argan oil and shea butter are non-comedogenic, but if you're acne-prone, wash your hairline thoroughly. The product itself doesn't cause breakouts — leaving it on overnight might.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a strong-hold pomade that washes out effortlessly and actually conditions your hair while styling. **Skip it if** you can't tolerate strong fragrances or need a larger container for daily thick-hair use.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A genuinely good product with one significant flaw (the scent) and one practical limitation (the jar size). The easy-wash formula alone puts it ahead of most competitors in this category.
