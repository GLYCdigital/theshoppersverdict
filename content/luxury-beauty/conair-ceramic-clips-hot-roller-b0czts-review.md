**FAQ**

*Can I use these on damp hair?* No. Always start with completely dry hair. Using them on damp hair causes uneven heating and potential damage.

*How hot do they get?* Hot enough to style effectively but the ceramic coating distributes heat evenly, so they're gentler than traditional metal rollers. Still, always test on a small section first.
