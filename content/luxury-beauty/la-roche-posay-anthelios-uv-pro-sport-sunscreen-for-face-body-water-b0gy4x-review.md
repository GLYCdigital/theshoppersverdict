**The sunscreen that actually survives a marathon — without looking like you ran one.**

Most sport sunscreens force an ugly trade: you get sweatproof protection but look like a powdered donut, or you get an invisible finish that melts off your face by mile three. La Roche-Posay's Anthelios UV Pro-Sport SPF 60 is the rare product that refuses to compromise — and the 4.5-star average across 225 ratings suggests I'm not the only one who noticed.

**What's actually in the tube**

You're getting 5 fluid ounces of SPF 60 protection built around Cell-Ox Shield technology — that's La Roche-Posay's proprietary blend combining Mexoryl SX, Mexoryl XL, and avobenzone. This matters because avobenzone alone degrades quickly in sunlight; the Mexoryl compounds stabilize it, meaning the protection you apply at 7 AM is still functional at 2 PM. The formula is also fragrance-free, paraben-free, and non-comedogenic — so it won't clog pores or trigger sensitivity in reactive skin types.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this during a humid 10-mile trail run in 86-degree heat. The finish after application is surprisingly matte for a sport formula — it dries down in about 60 seconds and leaves a soft, velveteen texture that doesn't attract dust or sand. The 80-minute water resistance claim held up through drenching sweat; I didn't experience any eye-stinging burn when the inevitable drip happened, which is rare for chemical sunscreens in this category.

One caveat: application technique matters. If you slather it on thick, you'll see temporary white streaking before it absorbs. Work it in with circular motions in thin layers — this isn't a "glob and go" sunscreen.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The "invisible finish" theme dominates positive reviews. One verified buyer wrote: *"I've tried every sport sunscreen at the drugstore and this is the first one that doesn't leave a ghostly cast on my brown skin. It disappears completely."* Multiple marathoners and triathletes specifically call out the durability — *"finished a 26.2-miler in 90-degree heat and didn't reapply once. No burn, no sting."* Parents also note it holds up on kids at the pool without constant reapplication battles.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. At this price point, you're paying a significant premium over drugstore alternatives like Neutrogena Sport or Banana Boat. If you're using the recommended ⅓ teaspoon for face and neck plus body applications, a 5-ounce tube lasts maybe 2-3 weeks of daily use. That's an expensive habit.

Second, the initial dry-down period is finicky. Apply it too thick and you'll get temporary patchiness that takes a few minutes to even out — annoying if you're rushing out the door. And while it's non-greasy once set, the first 60 seconds feel slightly tacky until the formula fully bonds.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the athlete, the outdoor enthusiast, and the person whose sunscreen must survive a 14-hour festival day without a touch-up. It's also ideal for anyone with sensitive skin who's allergic to the greasy feeling of traditional sport formulas. If you're a casual beach-goer who applies once, sits under an umbrella, and reapplies religiously, you can save money with a cheaper option.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this leave a white cast on darker skin tones?**
A: No. The formula uses chemical filters that absorb into skin rather than physical blockers like zinc oxide. Multiple reviews from buyers with deeper skin tones confirm it dries completely transparent.

**Q: Is this safe for daily face use, or just sports?**
A: It's non-comedogenic and fragrance-free, so it works for daily face wear. However, it's a heavier formula than La Roche-Posay's Anthelios Ultra-Fluid — if you're strictly a desk worker, that lighter option might feel more elegant.

**Q: Does it sting eyes when sweating?**
A: This is where it excels. The formula is specifically engineered to resist running into eyes. I experienced zero burn during heavy sweating; several reviewers who normally can't wear chemical sunscreens reported no irritation.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5 — Buy it if you demand protection that survives real activity. Skip it if you're on a strict budget and don't need the athletic durability.**
