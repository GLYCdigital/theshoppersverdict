# Davines Liquid Spell Reinforcing Bodifying Fluid: The $55 Volumizing Gamble

Let's be blunt: $55 for 4.2 ounces of hair product is a commitment. The Davines Liquid Spell Bodifying Fluid isn't a casual purchase — it's an investment in how your hair looks and feels every day. The question is whether it delivers enough to justify the price tag. After digging through 215 Amazon ratings (4.3 stars), here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The product arrives in Davines' signature minimalist white bottle — the kind that looks intentional on a bathroom shelf. It's a liquid-to-foam formula, which means it dispenses as a lightweight fluid that transforms into a mousse-like foam when worked between your palms. The texture is genuinely unique: unlike traditional mousses that feel heavy and wet, this one has a dry, airy consistency that spreads easily through damp hair.

The formula features what Davines calls "Biodynamic" ingredients — sourced from their regenerative agriculture project in Italy. You'll find sweet orange, lemon, and rosemary extracts, which give it a clean, spa-like scent that fades within minutes of application. No lingering perfume smell, which is a plus for fragrance-sensitive users.

## How It Performs in Real Use

As shown in the product image, the pump dispenses a precise amount — and precision matters here. Two to three pumps worked into damp roots before blow-drying gives fine hair noticeable lift without the crunch or stiffness that plagues most volumizing products. The liquid-to-foam transition is where this product shines: it distributes evenly, so you don't get patchy spots of product buildup.

One Amazon reviewer summed it up well: *"I have baby-fine hair and this is the only product that gives me actual volume at the roots without making my hair feel like straw. I use three pumps, blow dry upside down, and my hair looks twice as thick."* Another noted: *"It's expensive, but a little goes a long way. One bottle lasted me almost three months with daily use."*

The "reinforcing" aspect is real, too. Over several weeks, the product genuinely improves hair's density and resilience — this isn't a temporary styling fix, it's a treatment that strengthens the hair shaft. That's a meaningful distinction for anyone dealing with thinning hair.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's talk about the tradeoffs. First, the price. At $55, this is competing with professional salon brands, and while the quality is there, it's a tough pill to swallow for a styling product. Drugstore volumizers at $10-15 will give you similar root lift — they just won't condition or strengthen your hair over time.

Second, dosing is finicky. Use more than three pumps and you'll get a slightly tacky feel that's hard to brush out once dry. This isn't a product you can slather on carelessly. You need to learn the right amount for your hair length and density.

Third — and this is important — it's not for everyone. Multiple reviewers with thick or coarse hair reported it did nothing for them. This is specifically formulated for fine-to-medium textures that need a boost. If your hair is naturally dense, save your money.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have fine, limp, or thinning hair and want a product that provides both immediate volume and long-term strengthening. You appreciate clean, botanical formulations and don't mind paying for quality ingredients. You're tired of sticky mousses that leave residue.

**Skip it if:** Your hair is thick or coarse. You're on a tight budget and volume is your only concern — a drugstore mousse will do the job. You prefer products with strong fragrance.

## FAQ

A: Daily use is fine for most people. It's gentle enough for everyday application, and the conditioning benefits build over time. One bottle typically lasts 2-3 months with daily use.

**Q: Does it work on dry hair?**
A: No — apply it to damp, towel-dried hair before blow-drying. That's how the liquid-to-foam transformation activates properly. Using it on dry hair will just leave a sticky film.

**Q: Is it safe for color-treated or chemically processed hair?**
A: Yes. The formula is sulfate-free and gentle enough for colored hair. In fact, the strengthening benefits can help minimize breakage on damaged, processed strands.
