**Performance and Real-World Use**

Here's where this product earns its keep. The powder formula absorbs oil at the roots while simultaneously adding grip and volume. I've used it two ways: as a finishing spray on clean hair for a blowout, and as a dry shampoo on day-two hair. Both times it delivered.

The texture it creates is grippy but not sticky — you can still run your fingers through your hair without it feeling like straw. On day-two hair, it eliminates the greasy sheen and revives the style in about 60 seconds.

The powder is color-neutral, which matters if you have dark hair. I've seen cheaper dry powders leave a telltale white cast; this one disappears almost immediately when worked in.

Let's be honest about the downsides. The price per ounce is rough — you're paying a significant premium for the Kevin.Murphy brand. If you have thick or coarse hair, the volume effect will be less dramatic; this is really optimized for fine to medium textures.
