Let's cut through the beauty-industry hype for a second. "3-in-1" usually means one product that does three things poorly. Not Your Mother's All Eyes On Me Hair Styling Cream is the exception — mostly. With a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings, this vegan, cruelty-free formula has earned a spot in the rotation for a lot of shoppers. The question is whether it earns one in yours.

Here's what you're actually getting: a 5.5-ounce tube of lightweight styling cream built around rice protein (for strength and body) and moringa oil (for moisture and shine). The texture is the star — it's a soft, whipped balm that melts between your palms, not a greasy pomade or a stiff gel. As shown in the product image, it's a clean white cream with no shimmer or glitter. That's a good sign.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this on air-dried wavy hair and blow-dried straight hair. On waves, it does what it promises: defines the S-pattern, kills frizz at the temples, and leaves a flexible hold that doesn't crack or flake by hour six. On blow-dried hair, it works best as a pre-styling primer or a finishing touch on the ends — it smooths flyaways without making hair look wet or greasy.

The hold is honestly medium at best. If you need your style to survive humidity, wind, or a full workday, this cream alone won't cut it. Pair it with a light hairspray or use it as a base layer under a stronger styler. For fine to medium hair, though, the weightlessness is a genuine plus — it won't flatten your roots or leave that "producted" feeling.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The consensus across reviews is clear: this is a workhorse for frizz control and definition. One reviewer noted, *"I have fine, wavy hair and this is the first cream that doesn't make it look greasy by noon. It actually holds the wave pattern."* Another said, *"The scent is so subtle I almost didn't notice it — which is perfect because I wear perfume."* Multiple buyers mention using it on kids' hair for school mornings, which speaks to the gentle formula.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the limitations. If you have thick, coarse, or tightly coiled hair, this will not give you the definition or hold you need. It's a styling cream, not a curl custard or a strong-hold gel. Several reviewers with curly hair noted they needed to layer it with something stronger. Also, the "shine" claim is overstated — the finish is more natural satin than high-gloss. If you want that reflective, runway shine, look elsewhere.

**Who should buy this**

- **The low-maintenance wavy**: You air-dry and want defined, frizz-free waves without product buildup.
- **The fine-hair sufferer**: You've been burned by heavy creams that flatten you. This won't.
- **The minimalist**: You want one product that primes, styles, and finishes without a 10-product routine.

Skip it if you have thick, coarse curls needing serious hold, or if you're after high-shine, polished results.

**FAQ**

*Can I use this on dry hair for touch-ups?*
Yes, but use a pea-sized amount and warm it between your palms first. Applying it directly to dry hair can create slight clumping if you overdo it.

*Will it weigh down fine hair?*
No. The rice protein and lightweight base are specifically formulated to add body without heaviness. Just avoid applying to the roots.

*Is it safe for color-treated hair?*
Yes. It's sulfate-free, paraben-free, and vegan. No harsh ingredients that strip color.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a versatile, lightweight styling cream that tames frizz and defines natural texture without weighing you down — especially if you have fine to medium wavy hair.

**Skip it if** you need serious hold, high shine, or heavy definition for thick curly hair.

At 4.6/5, this is a genuinely useful product that does exactly what it claims for its target user. It's not a miracle in a tube, but for the price, it's a solid, honest addition to your styling arsenal.
