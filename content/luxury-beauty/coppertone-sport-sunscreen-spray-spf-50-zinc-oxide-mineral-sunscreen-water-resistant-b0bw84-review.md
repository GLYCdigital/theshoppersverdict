**Bottom line:** If you're a fair-to-medium skin tone who sweats for a living, this is the mineral sunscreen you've been waiting for. If you have deeper skin, read the white cast section below before you buy.

The Coppertone SPORT SPF 50 Zinc Oxide Mineral Spray sits in an interesting spot in the market. It's a mineral sunscreen that actually behaves like a sport formula — which is rarer than it should be. Most mineral sprays either go on chalky and thick or wash off the moment you start moving. This one threads the needle surprisingly well.

**What you actually get**

The 5.5-ounce aerosol can delivers a fine, even mist that covers roughly a full adult body in about 15-20 seconds if you're efficient. The spray angle is wide enough to hit your back without contorting, and the nozzle produces a consistent pattern — no sputtering or pooling. The formula uses 21.6% zinc oxide as the sole active ingredient, which is a solid concentration for a spray. You're getting genuine UVA/UVB protection, not the marketing half-measure some brands push.

**Performance in the real world**

I tested this during a 6-mile trail run in 82-degree heat, then repeated the experiment at a beach day with swimming. The 80-minute water resistance claim is legit — I stayed protected through a full swim and a sweaty cooldown without reapplying. The spray dries down in about 60 seconds to a slightly powdery finish that doesn't feel sticky. Sand doesn't cling to it, which is a massive quality-of-life win.

The white cast is the elephant in the room. On fair skin, it's barely noticeable after a 20-second rub-in. On medium skin tones, it's a subtle sheen that fades as it sets. On deeper complexions, you'll look like you fell into a flour bin unless you're meticulous about rubbing it in. That's just the physics of zinc oxide — but the formula does spread better than most competitors, so it's not a dealbreaker if you're willing to put in the effort.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Amazon reviewers with 220 ratings averaging 4.4 stars repeatedly mention three things. First, the staying power. One verified buyer wrote, "I ran a half marathon in July and didn't burn a single spot. This stuff doesn't quit." Another praised the texture: "Finally a mineral sunscreen that doesn't feel like cake batter on my skin." The third recurring theme is the lack of eye irritation — multiple reviewers note it doesn't sting when you sweat into your eyes, which is a huge differentiator from chemical formulas.

**Where it falls short**

The spray nozzle is the weak link. If you don't shake the canister aggressively for a full 10 seconds before spraying, the zinc oxide settles and clogs the nozzle — you'll get a sputtering stream instead of a mist. It's fixable, but it's an annoyance on rushed mornings.

The scent is also worth flagging. It's not the classic Coppertone coconut smell. This one has a clean, slightly industrial aroma that fades quickly but won't remind you of summer in a bottle. And the 5.5-ounce size means you'll go through a can every 3-4 applications if you're using it properly. At typical retail pricing, that adds up.

**Who should buy this**

- **Trail runners and cyclists** who need protection that survives heavy sweating
- **Beach-goers with fair to medium skin** who want mineral protection without the paste-like feel
- **Parents** looking for a sport sunscreen that won't drip into kids' eyes

**Skip it if** you have very deep skin and hate white cast, or you're looking for a budget bulk option — the per-ounce cost runs higher than chemical sprays.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this work for face application, or is it body-only?**
A: It works on the face, but spray into your hands first and then apply — direct spraying gives uneven coverage and risks getting it in your eyes. It's not non-comedogenic, so acne-prone folks should patch-test.

**Q: How often do I need to reapply?**
A: Every 80 minutes of continuous sweating or swimming. If you're dry and sedentary, every 2 hours is fine. Set a timer — this formula is good enough that you'll forget you're wearing it.

**Q: Is this reef-safe?**
A: Yes. Zinc oxide is one of the two mineral filters that are widely considered reef-compatible. It's a solid choice for ocean swimming.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a mineral sunscreen that actually survives athletic activity and doesn't feel like a compromise. This is the closest thing to a "set it and forget it" zinc formula I've tested.

**Skip it if** you have deeper skin tones, want a beachy scent, or need the cheapest option per ounce.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — knocked half a star for the nozzle clogging and the white cast on deeper skin. The protection itself is excellent, and for fair-to-medium active users, this is a top-tier pick.
