## What you actually get

Each pack contains 8 strands of pre-twisted, pre-looped passion twist hair. The twists arrive perfectly formed — no twisting, no braiding, no sealing the ends. You open the package, part your hair, and loop. That’s it.

The texture is where BOHOBABE separates itself. As shown in the product image, the twists have a defined, springy coil pattern that mimics a fresh salon install. The hair is made from a low-sheen synthetic that doesn’t scream "plastic" under bathroom lighting. At roughly 20 inches per strand, you get enough length for a mid-back install, though you’ll want 6–8 packs for a full, voluminous head depending on your natural hair density.

## Real-world performance

I installed these on a Friday evening. Full head, 7 packs, standard crochet method. Total time: just under two hours. Compare that to loose passion twist packs, which typically run 3–4 hours with twisting and sealing — the pre-looped design is a genuine time-saver.

The weight is the sleeper feature here. These twists are light at the roots, which means no tension headaches by day two. I wore them through a humid 90-degree weekend, a sweaty gym session, and two nights of sleep without a bonnet (I know, I know). By day five, the curl pattern was still intact. No major frizz, no unraveling at the loop.

## Where it falls short

Let’s be real about the downsides.

**Shedding is noticeable for the first few days.** The cut ends will release loose fibers, especially when you’re separating and styling. It’s not catastrophic — you’re not losing entire twists — but you’ll be wiping stray strands off your shoulders. A light mist of setting spray or mousse on the ends helps.

**The color runs warm.** If you order "natural black," expect a dark espresso brown in direct sunlight, not a true jet black. It’s flattering, but if you’re trying to match a cool-toned root touch-up, you’ll notice the difference.

**The pre-looped ends are delicate.** If you’re rough during installation — yanking the loop tight or pulling too hard — the loop can stretch out, making the twist harder to secure. Go gentle at the root.

## FAQ

**How many packs do I need for a full head?**
Most buyers report 6–8 packs for a full, voluminous install. If you have thin hair, 5 might suffice; for thick or long hair, err on the side of 8.

**Can I reuse these after taking them out?**
Technically yes, but they won’t look fresh. The pre-looped ends stretch and the curl pattern loosens. Treat them as a single-install product.

**How do I reduce shedding?**
Before installation, gently run your fingers through each twist to release loose fibers. After installing, seal the ends with a small amount of mousse or gel. Avoid excessive combing.
