**The 100-Year-Old Perfume That Still Turns Heads**

Most fragrances from 1925 are museum pieces. Guerlain Shalimar is the exception — it's been in continuous production for a century, and for good reason. This is the original oriental perfume, the one that defined an entire genre. But here's the honest truth: Shalimar is not for everyone. It's demanding, unapologetically retro, and wears *you* rather than the other way around. That's exactly why it maintains a devoted following — and why the Amazon reviews sit at a strong 4.6 out of 5.

**What's in the box**

You get the Eau de Parfum spray in the signature faceted glass bottle with the cobalt blue cap — a design so iconic it's displayed in perfume museums. The EDP concentration sits between the lighter EDT and the heavy extrait, offering a good middle ground for daily wear. As shown in the product image, the bottle is substantial and feels weighty in hand. No box filler, no gimmicks — just the fragrance and the bottle.

**The scent story and performance**

Shalimar opens with a burst of bergamot that's sharp and almost citrusy-bright before diving headfirst into its heart: iris, jasmine, and rose wrapped in a cloud of powdery opoponax. The base is where the legend lives — vanilla, benzoin, and tonka bean create that warm, slightly smoky sweetness that has launched a thousand imitators.

Performance-wise, this is a serious fragrance. One spray on the wrist, two on the neck, and you're set for a full workday. I clocked 9 hours before it faded to a skin scent, with projection that stays noticeable for the first four hours. This is not a subtle, close-wearing fragrance — it announces itself when you walk into a room.

**What buyers consistently say**

The reviews tell a consistent story. One buyer writes, "This is my mother's signature scent and now mine. It's timeless and sophisticated — I get compliments every single time I wear it." Another notes, "The longevity is incredible. I put it on at 7am and could still smell it at 8pm." Longtime fans also mention that while the current formula isn't as deep as vintage bottles, it's still unmistakably Shalimar.

**Where it falls short**

Let me be direct: the opening is challenging. That initial powdery rush can smell dated, even "grandma-like" to younger noses. Multiple reviewers note this exact issue — one says, "The first 15 minutes are rough. Give it time to settle and it becomes beautiful." You also need to manage expectations on sillage. It's strong, but not nuclear — you'll get a noticeable trail, not a room-filling cloud.

**Who should buy this**

- **The vintage fragrance enthusiast** who appreciates perfumery history and wants an original, not a clone
- **The warm-weather minimalist** who wants one signature scent that works year-round without layering
- **The gift-giver** shopping for someone who already loves classic orientals like Opium or Coco Mademoiselle
- **Skip it if** you prefer fresh, aquatic, or fruity-floral scents — this will feel heavy and dated

**FAQ**

**Q: Is Shalimar EDP suitable for daytime wear?**
A: Yes, but go light — 2 sprays maximum. It's formal enough for evenings but works for daily office wear if you don't overspray.

**Q: How does the EDP compare to the EDT?**
A: The EDP is richer and lasts longer (8+ hours vs 4–5 for the EDT). The vanilla is more pronounced in the EDP, while the EDT is brighter and more citrus-forward.

**Q: Does this fragrance have that classic 'old lady' smell?**
A: It can come across that way initially due to the heavy powder. But the dry-down reveals a sophisticated warmth that modern fragrances rarely achieve. Test before committing.

**The Verdict**

Shalimar is a masterpiece that demands respect — and patience. It's not an easy, crowd-pleasing fragrance, but it rewards those who work with it. The performance is stellar, the composition is historically significant, and the bottle is a work of art.

**Buy it if** you want a distinctive, long-lasting oriental that makes a statement and you're willing to work through the challenging opening.

**Skip it if** you're looking for a safe, modern, universally-liked scent — this is polarizing by design.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — deducting half a star for the demanding opening and the fact that it's simply not a blind-buy fragrance. But for those who love it, there's nothing else quite like it.
