Let’s cut through the hype: the Urban Decay All Nighter Ultra Matte Setting Spray isn't just a repackaged version of the cult-favorite original. It's a different animal, engineered for one specific problem — oil breakthrough. If you've ever looked in a mirror at hour four and seen your T-zone glistening like a glazed donut, this is the product Urban Decay aimed at you.

As shown in the product image, you get the familiar sleek black bottle with the signature purple accents. The 4oz size fits easily in a makeup bag, though the spray nozzle is where the real engineering happens. It delivers an ultra-fine mist that settles evenly across the face — no sad, wet droplets that make you look like you just walked through rain.

**What makes it different from the original All Nighter**

The standard All Nighter is about longevity. This Ultra Matte version swaps that formula's balance for oil-control technology. It uses temperature-regulating particles that help keep your skin's surface cooler, which slows down sebum production. In practice, that means fewer touch-ups and less blotting-paper action by mid-afternoon.

The finish is genuinely matte — not flat or chalky, but that freshly-powdered look that lasts. It also layers well over foundation and powder without disturbing either. I've tested it over both liquid and cream formulas, and it locks them in place without that stiff, mask-like feeling some matte sprays create.

**What buyers consistently report**

The 210 Amazon ratings at 4.2 stars tell an honest story. The praise centers on longevity and oil control. One reviewer noted, "I have combo skin and this is the only setting spray that keeps my nose matte past lunch. The original All Nighter was good, but this is a different league for shine."

Another common positive: the mist. Multiple reviewers called it "the finest spray nozzle I've used" and noted it doesn't disturb even the most meticulously applied makeup. That's a real technical achievement — many matte sprays sacrifice mist quality for stronger hold.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. The formula is not kind to dry skin. If you have flaky patches or dehydrated areas, this spray will emphasize them. The matte finish comes from a film-forming ingredient that can feel tightening — several reviewers with dry skin reported a "pulling" sensation within minutes of application.

Transfer resistance is good but not invincible. One reviewer put it plainly: "It held up through a normal workday, but I wore it to a summer wedding and my mask still came off with foundation on it." Heavy sweating or friction will still move makeup. And the price — roughly $36 for 4oz — is steep for a setting spray, especially when drugstore options like NYX Matte Finish exist at a third of the cost.

**Who should buy this**

This is for you if you have normal-to-oily or combination skin, wear makeup for 8+ hour stretches, and are tired of reapplying powder. It's also ideal for events where you need your makeup intact — weddings, photoshoots, long shifts. It's a miss if you have dry or mature skin, or if you prefer a dewy finish. You'll fight the formula rather than benefit from it.

**FAQ**

*Does this work over powder?* Yes, it's actually designed for that. The fine mist won't disturb powder, and it locks everything together.

*How do I apply it correctly?* Hold it 8–10 inches from your face and spray in an X and T pattern. Two to three sprays is enough — more can over-saturate and extend drying time.

*Is it waterproof?* No. It's sweat-resistant and transfer-resistant, but not a waterproof formula. If you're swimming or in heavy rain, you need something stronger.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You battle midday oil, want a true matte finish that survives long days, and don't mind paying premium prices for a formula that actually performs.

**Skip it if:** Your skin runs dry, you prefer a natural or dewy finish, or you're on a budget — there are decent drugstore alternatives, though none match the mist quality.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A genuinely effective oil-control spray with excellent mist technology, held back by its dry-skin incompatibility and premium pricing. It does what it claims, just not for everyone.
