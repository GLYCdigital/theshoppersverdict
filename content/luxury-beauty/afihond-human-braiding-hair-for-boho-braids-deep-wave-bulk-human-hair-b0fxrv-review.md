## Where it falls short

Let’s be honest about the downsides because they’ll affect your experience.

**Shedding on the first install.** You will lose some strands while braiding. It’s not catastrophic — think “noticeable but manageable” — but don’t braid over a white carpet. The shedding stops after the initial braid once the loose hairs are worked out.

**Color accuracy.** The photos show a true jet black, but several buyers reported the actual hair leans slightly warmer or softer black. If you need an exact match for a specific look, buy a smaller pack first to test.

**Length on the ends.** After a few weeks of wear, the ends can look slightly thinner if you’re constantly heat-styling them. Trim the ends lightly before your second install to refresh the wave pattern.

## FAQ

**How many packs do I need for a full head of boho braids?**
For medium-sized knotless braids, plan on 4–5 packs for shoulder-length and 6–7 packs for mid-back length. For jumbo braids, 3–4 packs will do. Buy one extra pack if you want fuller ends.

**Can I reuse this hair?**
Yes. If you remove the braids carefully and wash with a sulfate-free shampoo, you can get a second install. The wave pattern will soften slightly, but it still looks good. Most reviewers report 2–3 uses before the hair needs replacing.

**Does it tangle or matte easily?**
In normal wear, no. The deep wave pattern resists matting better than loose curl textures. However, if you sleep without a satin scarf or bonnet, expect tangling by week two. Wrap it at night and you’re fine.
