## The $10 Eyeliner That Thinks It's Luxury

Most drugstore eyeliners force a compromise: you get pigment or you get blendability, but rarely both. The **L'Oreal Paris Voluminous Smoldering Eyeliner** sidesteps that tradeoff with a formula that genuinely performs above its price point. With a 4.6/5 rating across 229 Amazon reviews, this pencil has clearly won over a lot of makeup bags. Here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The product is a twist-up pencil (no sharpening needed for the core itself) with a chiseled tip designed for both precision and smudging. The attached cap houses a **custom sharpener** — and unlike most built-in sharpeners that feel like an afterthought, this one actually works. The tip shape matters here: one edge gives you a fine line for tightlining, the flat side lays down color for smoky blending. You're not getting a brush or smudger tool in the box, so plan to use a fingertip or your own brush for that blended effect.

## Performance & Real-World Wear

The formula is the standout. It's **creamy without being waxy** — glides across the lash line in a single stroke, no tugging or patchiness. The "smoldering" descriptor is accurate: the black is deep and rich, but it's designed to be smoked out before it sets. You get roughly 30–45 seconds of working time before it locks down, which is generous enough to blend but short enough that it won't slide around all day.

The chiseled tip earns its keep. The narrow edge nails a tight, precise line along the upper lash line in one pass. The flat edge deposits enough product to create a soft smoky look without needing to layer five times.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The praise in the Amazon reviews clusters around three consistent themes. One buyer noted, *"This is the first liner that doesn't make my eyes water — I've repurchased three times."* That's a meaningful data point, since sensitive eyes are the #1 complaint across eyeliner reviews generally. Another reviewer called it *"the perfect gray between pencil and gel"* — a useful framing, because the texture really does split the difference. Multiple reviewers also specifically called out the sharpener cap, with one saying *"I actually use the sharpener — it fits perfectly and I haven't lost it."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct: **this is not a waterproof formula.** If you cry, sweat heavily, or get caught in rain, it will break down and migrate. Several reviews mention needing a touch-up after a long day, especially for those with oily lids. It's also not ideal for precise graphic liner — the smoldering quality that makes it great for soft looks works against you if you want sharp, architectural wings. And while the pencil doesn't need sharpening, the tip will eventually dull; you'll need to reshape it with the built-in sharpener to maintain precision.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a smoky-eye devotee, you have sensitive eyes, or you want a single pencil that handles both tightlining and blended looks. It's also a smart pick for makeup beginners — the forgiving formula gives you time to correct mistakes.

**Skip it if:** You need all-day, tear-proof wear for events, you prefer matte, bone-dry formulas, or your eyeliner must survive a 12-hour shift without attention.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work on the waterline?**
A: Yes, the creamy formula applies well on the upper waterline. It won't last as long there as on the lid — expect 4–6 hours before needing a touch-up.

**Q: How does the custom sharpener work?**
A: The cap has a built-in sharpener that you insert the pencil into and twist. It reshapes the tip when it dulls. It works fine, though you do need to clean it occasionally to prevent buildup.

**Q: Is this better than the classic L'Oreal pencil liners?**
A: Yes, if you want a softer, more blendable finish. The Voluminous Smoldering formula is noticeably creamier than L'Oreal's older pencil lines, which tend to be harder and drier.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5 — Buy it.**

This is the rare drugstore eyeliner that delivers luxury-level performance without the luxury price tag. The creamy formula, versatile tip, and genuinely useful sharpener make it a standout in the $8–12 range. It's not waterproof and won't give you razor-sharp wings, but for everyday smoky definition and sensitive-eye comfort, it's hard to beat. If you're torn between a high-end pencil and something budget-friendly, start here — you'll likely save yourself $30.
