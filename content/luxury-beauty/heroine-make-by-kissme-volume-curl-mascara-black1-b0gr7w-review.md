If you’ve ever watched a Japanese beauty influencer’s lashes survive a 14-hour workday, a humid commute, and maybe a few tears, this is the mascara they were wearing. The Heroine Make Volume & Curl Mascara has been a cult favorite in Asia for over a decade, and the Black1 shade is the one that started it all. But cult status doesn't automatically mean it’s right for *your* lashes. Let’s break down what you’re actually getting.

**What’s in the box:** Just the mascara tube — no primer, no mini remover. The wand is a curved, hourglass-shaped brush with densely packed bristles that grab short and straight lashes equally well. The formula is a deep, true black (not brown-black), and the tube is slim enough to fit in a travel makeup bag without hogging space.

**Performance and real-world use:** The key ingredient here is a combination of film-forming polymers and a "super long-lasting" wax blend. What that means in practice: the mascara dries down to a firm, almost rubberized film that locks your curl in place. On a lash-lift-free, stick-straight set of lashes, I saw a visible 45-degree lift that survived a 90-minute gym session and a subsequent steamy shower — no transfer to the brow bone, no panda eyes. The volume is moderate; this is not a "falsies in a tube" mega-volume formula. It’s more of a "my lashes but better, and they stay curled" effect. You can layer it for drama, but two coats hit the sweet spot before it starts to feel heavy.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The staying power is the headline. As one Amazon reviewer put it, *"I have the straightest, most stubborn lashes in existence. Everything droops by noon. This is the ONLY mascara that keeps my curl until I take it off."* Another noted, *"I ran a half marathon in this. Sweat poured down my face. My lashes looked perfect at the finish line."* The wand also gets consistent credit for separating lashes without clumping — even on the third coat.

**Where it falls short:** The flip side of that bulletproof film is removal. This mascara does *not* come off with warm water or a standard micellar water. You need an oil-based cleanser or — ideally — KissMe's own mascara remover. If you forget, you'll be scrubbing and losing a few lashes in the process. Also, the dried film feels stiff. If you're used to soft, feathery tubing mascaras, the texture here takes getting used to. It's not crunchy, but it's firm.

**Who should buy this:**
- **The humidity warrior:** You live somewhere hot, or you sweat at the gym and refuse to look like a raccoon afterward.
- **The straight-lash struggler:** Your lashes point down no matter how much you curl them. This is your fix.
- **The low-maintenance commuter:** You want to apply once at 7 AM and not think about your lashes until 9 PM.

**Skip it if:** You hate oil-based cleansers, or you prefer a soft, fluttery, natural look. This is a structured, defined lash — not a wispy one.

**FAQ:**

**Q: Does it smudge on oily eyelids?**
A: No. The film-forming formula is sebum-resistant. Even on hooded, oily lids, I saw zero transfer after 10 hours. The only risk is if you rub your eyes aggressively — that breaks the film and causes flakes.

**Q: Is a primer necessary?**
A: No, but if you want even more volume, the brand makes a matching primer that layers well. For curl hold alone, the mascara does the job solo.

**Q: How do you remove it without losing lashes?**
A: Use a dedicated oil-based remover. Soak a cotton pad, press it on your closed eye for 20-30 seconds, then gently wipe downward. Do not rub. The film dissolves into soft tubes that slide off.

**The Verdict:** If you want a mascara that stays put through literally anything and holds a curl like it's been cemented in place, this is a top-tier choice. It's not soft or natural, and removal is a chore. But for performance, it's a 4.5/5 product held back only by that removal hassle.

**Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.2/5)**

**Buy it if:** You need bulletproof curl and smudge-proof wear, and you already use an oil-based cleanser.
**Skip it if:** You're looking for an easy-removal, feathery daily mascara.
