# KEVIN MURPHY HEATED.DEFENSE 150ml Review: Luxury Heat Protection That Actually Works

You've spent $300 on a Dyson or GHD straightener. You're about to put 230°C of direct heat on hair that costs hundreds more to color and maintain. Skimping on thermal protection at this point is like buying a Ferrari and filling it with regular gas. The KEVIN MURPHY HEATED.DEFENSE spray is designed for exactly this scenario — and after looking at 220 ratings averaging 4.4 stars, it's clear this isn't just expensive salon-brand marketing.

## What You're Actually Getting

The 150ml bottle arrives in Kevin Murphy's signature minimalist black packaging — clean, professional, and appropriately premium for the price point. As shown in the product image, it's a dual-phase liquid that requires a shake before each use. The formula combines hydrolyzed keratin (for strength), pracaxi oil (for shine), and murumuru butter (for moisture retention). This thermal defense system claims protection up to 230°C/450°F, which covers essentially every consumer heat tool on the market.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where this product separates itself from the $8 drugstore options. The dual-phase formula is the key differentiator — the oil phase seals the cuticle while the water phase delivers the keratin deep into the cortex. Spray it on damp hair before blow-drying, and you'll notice the difference immediately: the hair dries faster, feels smoother at the root, and holds a blowout noticeably longer.

One buyer noted, "I've used every heat protectant from Oribe to Redken. This is the first one that lets my straightener glide without that crispy, fried feeling afterward." Another mentioned, "My color-treated hair finally stops snapping when I hit it with my flat iron at 400°F."

For those with fine hair, the murumuru butter is light enough that it won't plaster strands together — a common complaint with oil-heavy protectants. It also has genuine humidity resistance, which means your curls or waves stay defined rather than frizzing out by midday.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. At roughly twice the cost-per-ounce of solid competitors like Oribe's Royal Blowout or even drugstore options like Tresemmé Thermal Creations, this isn't a budget-friendly choice. The 150ml bottle is also smaller than you'd expect — if you have thick, waist-length hair, you'll burn through this in under two months with daily styling.

The fragrance is the second real issue. It's a distinct herbal-citrus blend with strong grapefruit notes that some users find overwhelming. One reviewer wrote, "The smell is beautiful but intense — it lingers on your pillowcase for days. If you're sensitive to fragrance, this might be a dealbreaker." It's not unpleasant, but it's assertive.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You use high-heat tools (400°F+) regularly, have color-treated or chemically processed hair, and want a protectant that doubles as a styling primer. It's also ideal for fine-to-medium hair that needs protection without weight.

**Skip it if:** You're on a strict haircare budget, prefer unscented products, or only heat-style occasionally. For occasional use, a mid-range protectant will serve you adequately.

## FAQ

**Q: Can you use this on dry hair for touch-ups?**
A: Yes, but sparingly. One or two light sprays on dry hair before re-heating works well. Over-spraying creates buildup, so keep it minimal.

**Q: Does it replace a heat protectant cream or serum?**
A: It can. The dual-phase formula covers all the bases — moisture, heat barrier, and styling hold. You don't need a separate cream underneath unless your hair is extremely dry or damaged.

**Q: How often should you use it?**
A: Every time you heat style — there's no such thing as "heat memory" protection. With daily use, expect the 150ml bottle to last 6-8 weeks for shoulder-length hair.
