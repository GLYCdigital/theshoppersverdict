If you've dropped $300 on dimensional balayage, the last thing you want is a hairspray that strips your toner or leaves your ends feeling like straw. Pureology's Lock It Down promises flexible, color-safe hold — and after digging through 225 Amazon ratings, the consensus is clear: this is a serious contender for anyone treating their hair color like an investment.

## What's Actually in the Can

The 10-fluid-ounce aerosol delivers what Pureology calls a "flexible, touchable hold" — level 3 of 5 on their strength scale. That's not your grandmother's shellac. The formula is built around their AntiFade Complex (a blend of antioxidants and UV filters) plus camelina oil, which explains why color-treated hair users report less fading between salon visits.

As shown in the product image, it's a sleek, professional-looking matte black can — nothing flashy, which fits the brand's salon-first positioning. It's also vegan and Leaping Bunny certified cruelty-free, which matters to a growing segment of beauty shoppers.

## Performance: Where It Shines

The real test for any hairspray is whether it holds without crunching. Pureology threads that needle better than most. Multiple reviewers note they can brush through their hair at the end of the day without the dreaded "sweep of broken strands" — the formula flexes with your hair rather than fighting it.

Humidity resistance is where this earns its keep. One reviewer in Florida mentioned their curls held shape through an outdoor wedding in July, while others note it controls flyaways without that stiff, lacquered look. The aerosol sprays fine and evenly, avoiding the wet spots that plague cheaper cans.

The scent deserves a mention: it's a light, clean fragrance — think salon, not grandma's perfume. It fades within minutes, so it won't compete with your perfume.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. At this price point, you're paying for the Pureology name and the color-care technology. A 10-ounce can runs significantly more than drugstore options, and if you're a heavy user, you'll be reordering often.

Some reviewers with fine, straight hair report it can feel slightly heavy if you overspray. The fix is simple — hold the can farther back and use less — but it's worth knowing before you buy. A few users also mentioned the nozzle can clog if you don't wipe it after use, which is a minor annoyance for the price.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have color-treated hair and want a hairspray that protects your investment. This is ideal for anyone with balayage, highlights, or fashion colors who's tired of their styling products stripping tone. It's also great for humid climates where you need reliable hold without stiffness.

**Skip it if:** You're on a strict budget and use hairspray daily in heavy amounts. Or if you prefer maximum-strength, rock-hard hold — this is medium hold, and it's honest about that.
