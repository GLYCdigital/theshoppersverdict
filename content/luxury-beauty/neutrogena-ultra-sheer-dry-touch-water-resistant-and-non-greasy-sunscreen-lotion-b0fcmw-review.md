**The sunscreen that actually feels like nothing — mostly**

Let's be blunt: most sunscreens feel like a penalty for going outside. Neutrogena's Ultra Sheer Dry-Touch has been the default recommendation for years, and this SPF 70 version with Helioplex technology is why. It's the rare sunscreen that disappears into skin instead of sitting on top of it.

**What you're actually getting**

This is a 3-fluid-ounce bottle of SPF 70 broad-spectrum protection. The star is Helioplex, Neutrogena's stabilized photostabilization system that keeps avobenzone from breaking down in sunlight. That matters — regular avobenzone degrades within 30 minutes of sun exposure. Helioplex keeps it effective for the full two-hour wear window. The formula uses avobenzone (3%), homosalate (10%), octocrylene (8%), and oxybenzone (4%) for chemical protection.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this on a 90-degree day with a 3-mile run followed by a pool session. Application is genuinely impressive: it spreads thin, absorbs fast, and leaves a matte finish. No greasy film, no dewy shine, no white cast. The dry-touch claim isn't marketing — skin feels like bare skin within a minute. It survived sweat and 80 minutes of water exposure without burning my eyes or running into them.

One thing to note: the finish is matte but not completely invisible. On very deep skin tones, there can be a faint sheen under direct sunlight, though nowhere near the chalky cast of mineral sunscreens.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews reflects a pattern. One reviewer wrote, "I've tried every high-end sunscreen from La Roche-Posay to Supergoop — this beats them all for everyday wear. No breakouts, no greasiness, and it sits perfectly under makeup." Another noted, "The only sunscreen my husband will wear. He hates the feeling of sunscreen, but he forgets he has this on." Multiple reviewers mention repurchasing for years, which says more than any single review.

**Where it falls short**

Two honest complaints. First, the fragrance. It's a light, clean scent, but if you have sensitive skin or rosacea, the combination of fragrance and denatured alcohol (second ingredient) can sting or trigger irritation. Second, pilling. If you layer this over a thick moisturizer or silicone-based serum, it can ball up. Apply to bare skin or wait five minutes after moisturizer. Also, the 3-ounce bottle is small for daily full-body use — expect to repurchase monthly if you apply properly.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the person who hates sunscreen but knows they need it. If you have normal to combination skin, wear makeup, or spend time outdoors, this is a strong daily option. It's also excellent for active people — runners, hikers, swimmers — thanks to the 80-minute water resistance.

Skip it if you have reactive skin, prefer mineral (zinc-based) formulas, or want a moisturizing sunscreen. Those with dry skin may find it slightly tightening by end of day.

**FAQ**

*Does it leave a white cast on dark skin?*
No significant white cast. The chemical filters are invisible once absorbed. Some deeper skin tones may notice a slight sheen in direct light, but nothing compared to physical sunscreens.

*Can I wear it under makeup?*
Yes, and it works well. Wait 2-3 minutes for it to set, then apply foundation. The matte finish actually helps makeup stay put. Just avoid heavy moisturizers underneath to prevent pilling.

*Is SPF 70 worth it over SPF 50?*
Marginal difference — SPF 50 blocks 98% of UVB, SPF 70 blocks about 98.3%. The real benefit is the Helioplex stabilization, which maintains efficacy longer. If you're in direct sun for hours, the higher SPF with stabilized filters is worth it.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want reliable, invisible sun protection that doesn't feel like sunscreen, works under makeup, and survives sweat and water.

**Skip it if:** You have sensitive skin prone to irritation from fragrance or alcohol, or you prefer mineral sunscreen for environmental or skin-sensitivity reasons.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deduct half a star for the fragrance and alcohol content that excludes sensitive-skin users. For everyone else, this is the sunscreen that finally makes daily SPF a habit, not a chore.
