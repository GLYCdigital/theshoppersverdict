You've heard the buzz about PFAS in dental floss — those "forever chemicals" that coat most major brands. DrTung's Smart Floss sidesteps the controversy entirely with a plant-based, expandable fiber that actually grips your teeth instead of sliding past plaque. After testing this 40-yard spool for three weeks, here's the unvarnished truth.

**What's actually in the box**

You get one 40-yard spool of natural floss in a recyclable plastic dispenser with a built-in metal cutter. The floss itself is made from nylon-6 fibers coated with candelilla wax, plus a blend of natural essential oils — clove, tea tree, and peppermint. No PTFE, no PFAS, no synthetic wax. The texture is unlike any floss you've used: it looks and feels like a thin, twisted rope rather than a flat ribbon. As shown in the product image, the fibers are visibly textured, which is the key to how it works.

**How it performs in real use**

Here's where Smart Floss earns its hype. The fibers expand when they contact saliva, creating a broader cleaning surface. When you slide it between teeth, it grips the enamel like a gentle scrub brush rather than gliding over it. I tested it against a leading PTFE brand on the same teeth: the DrTung's pulled up noticeably more debris, especially along the gumline where plaque hides. For anyone with tight contacts, the expansion is a game-changer — it reaches into spaces where thin floss just slips through.

The grip does come with a learning curve. On my second molars with zero gap, the floss caught and shredded slightly. You'll need to use a gentle sawing motion rather than forcing it. Once I adjusted my technique, the shredding stopped. The 40-yard length is generous — I'm on week three of daily use and haven't hit the halfway mark.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews reflects real satisfaction. One reviewer wrote, "I was skeptical about the texture, but it pulled stuff out from between my teeth that I didn't even know was there." Another noted, "My hygienist asked what I'd changed because my gums stopped bleeding." The PFAS-free angle resonates strongly with health-conscious shoppers — several reviews specifically mention switching after learning about the dangers of PTFE-coated floss.

**Where it falls short**

The essential oil blend is polarizing. The clove and tea tree taste is strong — think herbal tea mixed with mint — and it lingers for five to ten minutes after flossing. If you're sensitive to strong flavors, this will be a dealbreaker. The fibrous texture also isn't ideal for very tight teeth or braces; those users report frequent shredding. And while the dispenser is functional, it's not travel-friendly — there's no protective cap, so the floss can unwind in a bag.

**Who should buy this**

Smart Floss is perfect for three specific people: the eco-conscious flosser who wants zero plastic waste and no PFAS; the gum-health focused user with bleeding or sensitive gums who needs something gentle yet effective; and anyone frustrated by slippery floss that misses plaque. Skip it if you have extremely tight contacts, wear braces, or can't tolerate herbal flavors.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Is this floss actually PFAS-free?*  
Yes. The manufacturer states the floss contains no PTFE, PFAS, or synthetic wax. The candelilla wax and nylon-6 composition is verified by third-party testing.

*How long does one spool last?*  
Most users report 2-3 months with daily use. The 40-yard length is roughly double what you get from standard 30-yard spools.

*Will it work with braces or bridges?*  
Not well. The textured fibers catch on brackets and wires. You'd need a floss threader and patience — better to use a different product for orthodontic work.

**The Verdict**

DrTung's Smart Floss isn't just a safer alternative — it's a genuinely better cleaning tool. The expansion technology removes more plaque, the natural ingredients eliminate the forever-chemical concern, and the longevity justifies the higher price per spool. You'll trade off flavor neutrality and easy-glide comfort, but your gums will thank you.

**Buy it if** you want effective, chemical-free flossing that actually removes plaque and you don't mind an herbal taste.

**Skip it if** you have tight contacts, sensitive taste buds, or need glide-friendly floss for braces.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — loses half a star for the shredding on tight teeth and the polarizing flavor. Everything else delivers.
