## Performance and Real-World Results

Here's where this duo earns its keep. The shampoo cleanses effectively without that dreaded "squeaky clean" feeling that wrecks curls. It uses mild surfactants (sodium coco-sulfate and cocamidopropyl betaine) that lift buildup while respecting your moisture barrier. The conditioner does the heavy lifting — it's packed with shea butter, coconut oil, and hydrolyzed proteins that smooth the cuticle and temporarily patch broken bonds.

I tested this on 2C/3A curls with bleach damage at the ends. After one wash, the difference was noticeable: less frizz, more defined clumping, and a softness that lasted into day two. The bond-building claim is legitimate — amino acids penetrate the cortex to reinforce weakened disulfide bonds. It won't magically fix severely over-processed hair overnight, but it delivers visible improvement within two to three washes.

## Where It Falls Short

The fragrance is the biggest complaint. It's a sweet, candy-like scent that some buyers describe as "overwhelming" and "artificial." If you're sensitive to fragrance or prefer unscented products, this will be a dealbreaker. The conditioner is also thick — those with fine, low-density curls may find it heavy, requiring thorough rinsing and possibly skipping the product on roots entirely.

One more honest note: this is a maintenance product, not a miracle cure. Severely damaged hair will need a dedicated protein treatment alongside this system.
