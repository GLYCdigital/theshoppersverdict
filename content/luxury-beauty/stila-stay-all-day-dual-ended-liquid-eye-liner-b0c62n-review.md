If you've ever stood in the makeup aisle debating between a felt-tip liner for crisp wings and a brush liner for tightlining, stila just solved your problem with one dual-ended tube. The Stay All Day Dual-Ended Liquid Eye Liner packs both applicators into a single sleek pen — but the real question is whether the formula lives up to the "Stay All Day" promise.

## What You Actually Get

The packaging is classic stila: a matte black pen with rose gold accents that feels substantial in your hand. One end houses the felt tip marker (the same one from their cult-favorite Stay All Day liner), and the other end features a flexible brush tip. There's no separate product to buy — both ends draw from the same pigment reservoir.

The felt tip is firm enough to give you control but not so stiff that it drags across your eyelid. The brush tip is noticeably thinner and more flexible, ideal for getting right at the lash line or creating those delicate inner-corner flicks.

## Performance: The 10-Hour Test

I wore this through a full workday, a sweaty commute, and an evening out. The verdict: this liner does not move. Once it dries (about 30 seconds), it sets into a waterproof film that stays put. I tested it on one eye without primer and still got through 8 hours without smudging — though on oily lids, I did notice minor flaking by hour 10. With primer, it went the full 12 hours without issue.

The pigment is genuinely black — not charcoal, not gray — in a single pass. It dries to a matte finish that photographs well and doesn't look artificial. The felt tip creates crisp, sharp wings that don't feather or bleed into fine lines.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

Looking through the 225 ratings on Amazon, a consistent theme emerges. One verified buyer wrote, "I have hooded eyes and this is the only liner that doesn't transfer onto my upper lid. The brush end is perfect for tightlining." Another praised the longevity: "Wore this through a wedding — cried, danced, sweat — and it looked perfect at midnight."

The most common praises center on the dual-ended versatility and the true black pigment. Several reviewers noted they'd been using the original stila liner for years and appreciated getting the brush tip in the same package.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The brush tip has a learning curve — it's not forgiving if you have shaky hands, and pressing too hard makes it feel wobbly. You'll want to practice on the back of your hand first.

Removal is genuinely annoying. This liner is *too* good at staying put. Warm water and face wash won't cut it — you need an oil-based remover (I use the Neutrogena eye makeup remover and it works fine, but it takes a few swipes).

There's also the drying-out concern. If you don't cap it tightly, the formula thickens within a few weeks. At this price point, that's a real consideration.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You wear eyeliner daily, want one product that does both winged looks and tightlining, and don't mind investing in a proper makeup remover.

**Skip it if:** You're a beginner still learning your technique (start with the felt tip alone), prefer gel or pencil formulas, or need something you can wash off with basic cleanser.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does one tube last?**
With daily use, expect 3-4 months before the pigment starts to fade or dry out. The brush tip tends to dry out slightly faster than the felt tip.

**Q: Is this safe for sensitive eyes?**
The formula is ophthalmologist-tested and fragrance-free. Most reviewers with sensitive eyes report no issues, but the waterproof nature means you'll be rubbing more during removal, which can irritate.

**Q: Does it work for waterline application?**
The brush tip works well for the upper waterline, but it's not ideal for the lower waterline — it tends to smear there. Stick to the lash line for best results.

## The Verdict

The stila Stay All Day Dual-Ended Liquid Eye Liner earns its 4.5-star rating. It delivers on its core promise: intense, long-wearing, smudge-proof color with the flexibility of two applicators. The learning curve on the brush tip and the stubborn removal are real tradeoffs, but for anyone who takes their eyeliner seriously, this is worth the splurge.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Buy it if you want a dual-purpose liner that survives anything. Skip it if you prefer low-maintenance makeup removal or need a more forgiving applicator.
