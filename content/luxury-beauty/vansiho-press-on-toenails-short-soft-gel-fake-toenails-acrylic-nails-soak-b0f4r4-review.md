Let me be direct: you're probably here because you're tired of paying $50 for a pedicure that chips in a week. The Vansiho press-on toenails promise a salon look for a fraction of the cost. Here's what you actually get.

**What's in the box**

The Vansiho set ships with 24 nails — that's two full sets of 10 plus spares for the big toes, which are the ones you'll lose if you're hard on your feet. Each nail is labeled by size (0 through 9), and the sizing chart printed on the back of the package is accurate, which is rarer than it should be in this category. You also get a mini file, a cuticle stick, and 24 adhesive tabs.

What's *not* included: brush-on glue. You'll want to buy some separately.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

These are soft gel, not hard acrylic. That distinction matters. Hard acrylic press-ons on toenails feel like wearing tiny ceramic tiles — they catch on socks and pop off when you bump your foot. The Vansiho nails have a slight flex, which means they move with your toe when you walk. That's the single biggest reason they stay on.

As shown in the product image, the finish is a natural, low-shine pink that reads as "healthy bare nail" rather than "obviously fake." The short, rounded shape is conservative enough for work or sandals without looking costumey.

For application, the tabs work fine for casual wear — think beach vacation or a weekend event. If you're planning to wear these for a full week or more, skip the tabs and use a brush-on nail glue. With glue, several long-term users report 10-14 days of wear with minimal lifting at the corners.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The sizing is the standout feature. One reviewer wrote: "I've bought three other brands and always had to file down the big toe. These fit straight out of the box." Another said: "They look like I got a $60 pedicure." Multiple reviewers noted the spares for big toes are a thoughtful touch, since those are the ones that get knocked off.

**Where it falls short**

The included adhesive tabs are honestly underwhelming. They hold for a day or two, maybe three if you're careful. If you're planning to do anything more active than walking on sand, you'll need brush-on glue — factor that into your total cost.

The color is also slightly lighter in person than in most product photos. It's still flattering, but if you're expecting the deeper rose shown in the listing, you'll be mildly disappointed. And there's no prep tool included, so if you don't already own a cuticle pusher, you'll need to add one to your cart.

**Who should buy this**

- **The vacationer** who wants nice feet for a week without scheduling a pre-trip pedicure
- **The pedicure refusenik** who hates sitting in the salon chair but wants polished-looking feet
- **The bridesmaid** who needs coordinated toes for photos without spending $70 on a salon appointment

**Skip it if** you're a runner or hiker — the flex that makes these comfortable also means they'll eventually work loose under sustained pounding. Your money is better spent on a regular pedicure.

**FAQ**

*How long do they actually last?* With the included tabs, 2-4 days. With brush-on glue, most users get 7-14 days. Removal with glue requires soaking in warm soapy water for 10-15 minutes.

*Will they fit wide feet?* The size range covers most adults, but if your big toe is particularly wide, you may need to file the inside edge slightly. The gel material files easily without cracking.

*Can you reuse them?* Yes, if you use tabs. With glue, reuse is hit-or-miss — the nail surface gets rough when you pry them off. The kit includes enough nails for two full applications anyway.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a natural-looking, short pedicure alternative that doesn't require a salon visit and fits properly out of the box. **Skip it if** you're expecting the included tabs to survive a week of activity — buy glue separately and you'll be satisfied.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — loses points for the weak adhesive tabs and slight color variance, but the fit, comfort, and natural look are genuinely better than the category average.
