### The Overnight Lip Treatment That Actually Earns Its Name

Most "sleeping masks" for lips are just thick balms rebranded with a fancy name. The Burt’s Bees Lavender Vanilla Lip Sleeping Mask is different — it's a legitimately occlusive overnight treatment that stays put. If you wake up with cracked, peeling lips every winter, this is worth your attention.

---

### What You Actually Get

The product comes in a 0.6 oz (17g) jar with a screw-top lid. As shown in the product image, it's a soft, buttery balm with a pale lavender tint. The texture is thicker than a standard lip balm but softer than a solid stick — think whipped shea butter consistency.

The ingredient list is where Burt's Bees earns its reputation: **beeswax** forms the protective barrier, **coconut oil** and **sunflower seed oil** provide deep moisturizing, and **lavender oil** delivers the signature scent. No petroleum jelly, no parabens, no phthalates. This is a clean formula that doesn't compromise on performance.

---

### Real-World Performance

I tested this for two weeks on chronically dry lips — the kind that peel in winter and crack at the corners. The difference was noticeable by night three.

The texture is thick, so you'll feel it on your lips for the first 10-15 minutes after application. That's intentional. It's creating a seal that locks in moisture while you sleep. By morning, most of it has absorbed, and my lips felt soft — not greasy, not coated.

The lavender vanilla scent is genuinely calming. It's not the sharp, synthetic lavender you find in cheap drugstore products. This is a softer, more botanical aroma that pairs well with a bedtime routine.

### Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs:

1. **The jar packaging.** You'll need to use a finger or spatula to apply. If you're prone to forgetting hygiene routines, this is a minor annoyance. It's also bulkier than a tube for travel.

2. **The scent is not subtle.** If you dislike lavender — even high-quality lavender — this product won't change your mind. The aroma is present throughout the night, though it fades by morning.

3. **The residue factor.** For the first 15 minutes after application, your lips will feel coated. If you can't stand any texture on your lips, this will bother you. It's the price of an occlusive formula that actually works.

### FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: How is this different from regular lip balm?**
Regular balm is designed for immediate relief and frequent reapplication. This mask is formulated with a higher concentration of occlusive ingredients (beeswax, oils) to create a longer-lasting barrier. It's thicker and designed for nighttime use specifically.

**Q: Can I use this during the day?**
Technically yes, but the texture is too heavy for comfortable daytime wear under lipstick or gloss. Stick to nighttime use.

**Q: Is this safe for sensitive skin?**
The formula is free of common irritants like parabens and phthalates. However, if you're allergic to bee products or sensitive to essential oils, patch test first. Lavender oil is a potential allergen for a small percentage of people.
