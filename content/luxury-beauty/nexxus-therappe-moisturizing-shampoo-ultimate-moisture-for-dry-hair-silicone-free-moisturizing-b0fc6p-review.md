Let’s be honest: most "moisturizing" shampoos are just watered-down conditioners that leave your scalp greasy by noon. The Nexxus Therappe Ultimate Moisture formula isn't that. This is a legitimate cleanser with a protein backbone, and it shows.

## What You're Actually Getting

The bottle holds 13.5 fluid ounces of a pearlescent, honey-thick liquid. The texture is noticeably more concentrated than typical drugstore shampoos — you genuinely only need a quarter-sized dollop for shoulder-length hair. It lathers into a dense, creamy foam that feels more like a salon treatment than a daily wash.

The formula centers on **Elastin Protein** and collagen, a combination Nexxus has used for decades. Unlike the silicone-heavy "smoothing" shampoos that coat the strand temporarily, Therappe works by reinforcing the hair's internal structure. For those with color-treated or heat-damaged hair, this makes a real difference over time: strands feel denser, less cotton-candy fragile when wet.

## Performance in the Real World

I tested this over three weeks on medium-thickness, color-treated hair (highlighted, so porous). First wash: immediate slip, but not the artificial silicone slip — this is more of a "hair is actually moisturized" feeling. Second day: scalp stayed clean, no grease at the roots, no crunchy ends. By week three, I noticed less hair in the shower drain — not a dramatic change, but measurable.

One 13.5 oz bottle lasted me exactly six weeks with three washes per week. That's roughly $0.60 per wash at typical street pricing — comparable to mid-tier salon brands but much cheaper per use than anything from Aveda or Oribe.

## Where It Falls Short

**The fragrance is strong.** It's a clean, floral-citrus scent that lingers for hours. If you're sensitive to fragrance or use scented styling products, this can clash. Several reviewers noted this specifically — it's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing.

**Fine, low-porosity hair needs a lighter conditioner.** Therappe's protein content can feel over-moisturizing (ironic, given the name) for very fine hair if used daily. The solution is simple: pair it with a lightweight conditioner and skip the co-washing. But if your hair is ultra-fine and you wash every day, this might not be your daily driver.

**The price point.** At $12-$15 per bottle, it's 2-3x the cost of Pantene or Herbal Essences. The concentrated formula offsets this, but if you're on a strict budget, it's a real consideration.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Color-treated or highlighted hair**: The protein reinforcement genuinely helps prevent breakage between salon visits.
- **Dry, thick, or curly hair**: This is a match made in heaven. The moisture retention is real.
- **Heat-styling daily users**: If your hair sees a flat iron or blow dryer more than twice a week, this is the protective base you need.

**Skip it if:** your hair is baby-fine and you wash daily, you're fragrance-averse, or you're strictly budget-driven.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this silicone-free?**
A: Yes. The current formula is silicone-free, which is why it doesn't provide that instant "slip" — but it also won't build up on your scalp over time. That's a tradeoff worth making.

**Q: How does it compare to Nexxus Humectress?**
A: Humectress is a conditioner designed to pair with Therappe. If you use Therappe alone, expect clean, moisturized hair but not heavy-duty conditioning. For best results, use them together — that's the classic Nexxus duo.

**Q: Can I use this on keratin-treated hair?**
A: Yes, but be cautious. The protein can temporarily stiffen keratin-treated strands if used excessively. Alternate with a sulfate-free, protein-free shampoo every other wash.
