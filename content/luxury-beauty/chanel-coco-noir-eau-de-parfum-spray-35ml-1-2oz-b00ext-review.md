# Chanel Coco Noir Eau De Parfum Spray 35ml/1.2oz Review

Let's be honest about what's happening here. You're looking at a Chanel perfume — the 35ml travel size, specifically — and you're wondering if it's worth the premium. The 3.9-star rating on Amazon tells you something important: half the people love it, and enough people are frustrated that it's not a slam dunk. Here's the real breakdown.

## What You're Actually Getting

The 35ml/1.2oz bottle is the smallest Chanel offers in Coco Noir. As shown in the product image, it's the classic rectangular Chanel flacon — heavy glass, clean lines, magnetic cap that snaps shut with a satisfying click. The box includes the standard Chanel leaflet, but no sample vials or extras. This is the "I want to try it without committing to 100ml" size, and at roughly $95-110 depending on seller, you're paying a premium per ounce compared to the larger bottles.

## The Scent Itself

Coco Noir is a dark, oriental-floral that opens with grapefruit and Calabrian bergamot — but don't let the citrus fool you. Within minutes, the heart emerges: rose, jasmine, and geranium wrapped in a heavy blanket of patchouli, tonka bean, and vanilla. The dry-down is where this fragrance lives. It's warm, slightly powdery, and unmistakably grown-up. This is not a fruity-floral for a 20-year-old's first perfume. It's a "I have my life together" scent.

## Where It Shines

The composition is genuinely sophisticated. The patchouli here isn't the dirty, earthy kind — it's polished, almost creamy, blended with sandalwood and white musk that leaves a soft, close-to-skin trail. Multiple reviewers on Amazon describe it as "elegant" and "sexy without being loud." One buyer noted: *"I get compliments every single time I wear this. It's mysterious and deep — not a teenager's scent at all."* If you like incense, amber, and dark florals, this hits that nerve beautifully.

The 35ml size is a practical win. It's about 3 inches tall, fits in a small purse, and the sprayer delivers a fine, even mist. For travel or testing, this is the right call.

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the elephant in the room: longevity. For an Eau de Parfum (which should give you 6-8 hours), Coco Noir fades fast on many people. A significant portion of the 195 Amazon ratings complain about this. One frustrated review said: *"Gone in 3 hours. I reapplied twice before dinner was over. For this price, I expect more."* Your mileage will vary — skin chemistry matters — but if you're a one-spray-and-forget person, you'll be disappointed.

The opening is also a problem for some. The first spray can smell sharp and almost alcoholic for 15 minutes before the dry-down kicks in. If you're testing in a store, don't judge it in the first 10 minutes.

And finally — this scent is not universally flattering. The patchouli and tonka bean combo leans mature and heavy. If you prefer fresh, aquatic, or sweet gourmand scents, you will not like this. Several reviewers mentioned it reminded them of "a rich aunt" or "a classic department store scent from the 80s."

## Who Should Buy This

- **The Chanel collector** who wants the full lineup in travel sizes — this fits that shelf perfectly
- **The evening event attendee** who needs a dark, sophisticated scent for dinners, galas, or date nights — and doesn't mind reapplying
- **The patchouli lover** who enjoys earthy, warm bases and wants something refined rather than bohemian

## Who Should Skip It

- **The longevity maximist** — if you want one application to last a full workday, look at Tom Ford or Dior's stronger offerings
- **The fresh scent fan** — this will feel cloying and heavy in summer or daytime wear
- **The budget-conscious buyer** — the 35ml price per ounce is the worst value in the lineup; the 50ml is cheaper per ml

## FAQ

**Q: Does Coco Noir smell like Coco Mademoiselle?**
A: No. Mademoiselle is brighter, with more citrus and a lighter patchouli. Noir is darker, spicier, and heavier on the tonka bean and vanilla. If you own Mademoiselle and love it, Noir may feel like a sibling — but a moodier, more serious one.

**Q: How long does the 35ml bottle last with daily use?**
A: With 2-3 sprays per wear and daily application, expect 4-6 weeks. If you're reapplying due to the longevity issue, faster. This is a travel size, not a daily driver.

**Q: Is this a good gift for someone who likes Flowerbomb or La Vie Est Belle?**
A: Possibly. Those are sweeter, but all three share a patchouli-vanilla base. If they prefer heavy, sweet scents, Noir's darker profile might surprise them — in a good or bad way. I'd recommend a sample first.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a sophisticated, dark oriental for special occasions, appreciate the travel-friendly 35ml format, and don't mind reapplying every 3-4 hours.

**Skip it if:** You need all-day performance, prefer light/fresh scents, or want the best cost-per-ounce value — in which case, go for the 50ml.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — A beautiful, complex fragrance held back by inconsistent longevity and a polarizing profile. The scent itself is a 5-star experience for the right person; the execution is a 3-star reality for many.
