# Viktor&Rolf Flowerbomb & Flowerbomb Extreme: The Complete Guide

Here's the thing about Flowerbomb: it's been a bestseller since 2005, and for good reason. The original Eau de Parfum is a masterclass in floral-gourmand blending — jasmine, freesia, and rose wrapped in a warm vanilla-patchouli base. But if you've worn it for years, you know its flaw: it sits close to the skin. That's where the Extreme flanker comes in. This duo set gives you both sides of the Flowerbomb personality — and at roughly $60 for the pair, it's one of the smarter fragrance buys on Amazon right now.

## What's Actually in the Box

This set includes two full-size bottles: the classic Flowerbomb Eau de Parfum (1.7 oz) and Flowerbomb Extreme (1.7 oz). Both come in their signature grenade-shaped flacons — the original in that iconic ruby-red glass, Extreme in a deeper, almost blackened red that matches its darker juice. You're getting a complete signature scent and its evening counterpart for less than you'd pay for the original alone at department store prices.

## Performance: The Original vs. Extreme

Let's talk real-world wear. The original Flowerbomb opens with a burst of sweet bergamot and tea notes before settling into its floral heart. It's beautiful, feminine, and undeniably pleasant — but it's not a projector. On most skin, you'll get 4–6 hours before it becomes a skin scent. That's fine for office wear or daytime errands, but don't expect it to announce your arrival.

Extreme is a different animal entirely. It amps up the patchouli and adds a smoky, almost boozy depth with honey and tobacco accents. The projection is significantly stronger — you'll get a solid 3–4 hours of noticeable sillage before it settles into a warm, spicy base that lasts 10+ hours. This is a cold-weather, evening-out, date-night fragrance. It's the one people will ask you about.

## Where It Falls Short

The original's longevity is the most common complaint — and it's legitimate. If you're someone who wants one spray in the morning to last through dinner, the original will disappoint. You'll need to reapply. And Extreme, while impressive, isn't for everyone. The patchouli is prominent, and in hot, humid weather it can feel heavy. If you dislike gourmand or oriental scents, this isn't a "maybe" — it's a hard pass.

## FAQ

**Q: Is the Extreme version worth it if I already own the original?**
A: If you like deeper, spicier fragrances, yes. It's a completely different mood — think evening vs. daytime. If you only wear one signature scent, you might find Extreme redundant.

**Q: How long does each bottle last with daily use?**
A: With 3–4 sprays daily, expect 3–4 months per bottle. Extreme lasts longer per spray due to its concentration, so you'll likely use the original faster.

**Q: Is this suitable for gifting?**
A: Absolutely. The presentation is elegant, and the two-bottle concept gives the recipient options. Just confirm they enjoy floral gourmands first.
