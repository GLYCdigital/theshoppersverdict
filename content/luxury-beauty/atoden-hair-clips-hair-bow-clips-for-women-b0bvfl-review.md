Here's where these clips separate themselves. The metal clip has genuine tension — it grips. I've tested these on second-day hair, thick ponytails, and half-up styles, and they stay put. No mid-day readjusting, no sliding down the nape of your neck.

Also, color accuracy varies between sets. The "neutral" pack runs warmer in person than the product photos suggest — more caramel, less taupe. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you're buying specifically to match an outfit, keep that in mind.
