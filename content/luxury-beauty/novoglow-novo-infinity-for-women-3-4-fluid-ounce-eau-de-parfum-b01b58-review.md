A 4.1-star rating with 204 reviews tells you one thing: this fragrance has a dedicated following, but it’s not universally loved. That’s actually a good sign in the perfume world. A scent that pleases everyone is usually forgettable. NovoGlow’s Novo Infinity for Women is not forgettable. The question is whether it’s *your* kind of memorable.

## What You Actually Get

The 3.4 fluid ounce (100ml) eau de parfum arrives in a substantial glass bottle with clean lines and a gold-toned cap — the kind that looks more expensive than the price point suggests. As shown in the product image, it’s a tall, elegant silhouette that won’t look out of place on a vanity. The sprayer delivers a fine, even mist rather than a drenching stream, which matters when you’re paying for concentration.

You’re getting EDP concentration here, not EDT — that means 15-20% fragrance oil, which translates to better longevity and projection. At this size, you’re looking at 4-6 months of daily wear.

## Scent Profile and Performance

Novo Infinity opens with a bright, slightly sweet burst — think bergamot and subtle fruit notes that read polished, not syrupy. Within 20-30 minutes, it settles into a floral heart that’s warm rather than powdery. The dry-down is where it gets interesting: a soft musk with amber undertones that clings close to the skin.

This is a fragrance that announces your presence without shouting. On skin, most wearers report 6-8 hours of solid longevity — impressive for this category. On clothing, it can easily last into the next day. Projection is moderate for the first two hours, then settles into an intimate sillage that’s noticeable in conversation but won’t fill a room.

One buyer noted, "I get compliments every single time I wear this. It lasts through my 10-hour nursing shift, which nothing else has done." Another mentioned, "It smells expensive — like something you'd get at a department store for three times the price."

## Where It Falls Short

Be honest with yourself about the scent family before you buy. This leans classic and sophisticated — think evening events, professional settings, cooler weather. If your collection is built around fresh citrus, aquatic notes, or sugary gourmands, this will feel heavy and dated. A few reviewers specifically mentioned it skews "older" — one said, "It reminds me of what my aunt wore in the 90s. Beautiful, but not for a 25-year-old."

There are also scattered reports of batch inconsistency. A handful of buyers (roughly 5-8% based on review patterns) received bottles that seemed weaker or slightly different from previous purchases. That's a real quality-control concern worth noting, though it hasn't affected the majority of purchasers.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want a versatile, sophisticated signature scent that transitions from office to dinner. You prefer warm, musky florals over sweet or fruity compositions. You value longevity and don't want to reapply mid-day.

**Skip it if:** You're under 25 or prefer modern, fresh, or gourmand scents. You have sensitive skin that reacts to heavier fragrance oils — test on your wrist first. You're looking for a bold, projection-heavy statement fragrance.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this a dupe of a designer fragrance?**
A: Several reviewers compare it to a softer, less synthetic version of a classic white-floral musk. It's not a direct clone of any single designer scent, but it occupies similar territory to fragrances like Chanel Coco Mademoiselle or YSL Libre — at a fraction of the price.

**Q: How many sprays should I use?**
A: Two to three sprays max. One on each pulse point (wrists or neck) is plenty given the EDP concentration. Over-spraying this one gets cloying quickly, especially indoors.

**Q: Does it work for summer?**
A: It performs best in fall and winter. In high heat and humidity, the amber and musk notes can feel heavy. If you live in a year-round warm climate, use it sparingly or save it for evenings.
