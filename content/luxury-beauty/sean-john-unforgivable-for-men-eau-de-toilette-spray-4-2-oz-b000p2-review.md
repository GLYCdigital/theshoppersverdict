**Performance and Scent Profile**

Unforgivable opens with a bright burst of bergamot, mandarin, and grapefruit — a zesty, almost effervescent citrus that immediately grabs attention. Within 15 minutes, the mid notes emerge: lavender, nutmeg, and a subtle aquatic accord that keeps it from smelling like a generic citrus cleaner. The dry-down is where it gets interesting — a warm base of vetiver, musk, and amber that lingers close to the skin.

Here’s the honest truth about performance: this is an EDT, not a parfum. You’ll get 4–6 hours of solid projection, then it fades to a skin scent. That’s normal for the concentration and price point. But the sillage is strong for the first two hours — you’ll be noticed entering a room, which is exactly what the name promises.

**Where It Falls Short**

No product is perfect, and Unforgivable has clear tradeoffs:

- **Longevity is average.** If you want a scent that lasts 10+ hours without reapplying, this isn’t it. Expect to refresh it after lunch if you’re wearing it to work.
- **It’s not unique.** The DNA is clearly inspired by Creed’s Millésime Impérial — a much more expensive fragrance. If you know that scent, you’ll recognize the similarity immediately. Some buyers love this; others feel it’s derivative.
- **The bottle is bulky.** The heavy glass is attractive, but it takes up noticeable shelf space and isn’t travel-friendly. You won’t be tossing this in a carry-on without planning.
- **Scent is polarizing.** A handful of reviewers describe it as “too sharp” or “soapy” in the opening. If you prefer sweet or woody fragrances, test this on skin first — the citrus is assertive.
