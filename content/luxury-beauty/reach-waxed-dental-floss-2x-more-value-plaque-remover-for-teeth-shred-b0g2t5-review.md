**Where it falls short**

Honesty time. If you have severely crowded teeth or bridgework, this floss can shred or snap if you're not careful. The flat tape catches on rough edges more readily than round floss. The solution is technique — use a gentle sawing motion rather than forcing it through — but it's a real friction point for some users.

The dispenser also has a learning curve. The dial that advances the floss is stiff when new, and you'll likely need both hands to pull fresh floss initially. It loosens up after a week or two, but it's not the smoothest mechanism on the market.
