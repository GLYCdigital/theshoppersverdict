## Real-World Performance

I tested this on moderately heat-damaged, color-treated hair. Apply a generous amount to damp, towel-dried hair, leave it for 5–10 minutes (I pushed it to 15 with a shower cap for deeper penetration), and rinse. The immediate difference is real: hair detangles effortlessly, and you can feel the slip even while the product is still in.

After blow-drying, the shine is noticeable — not greasy, but that "just left the salon" reflection. The softening effect lasted through three washes before I felt the need to reapply. On severely damaged or over-processed hair, you'll want to use it weekly; on healthy hair, bi-weekly is sufficient.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest caveats. First, the fragrance is strong and persistent. If you're sensitive to scents, this could be a dealbreaker — one reviewer mentioned getting a headache from the smell that lingered on her pillow for two nights. Second, the silicone content is real. This mask uses dimethicone and related compounds to deliver that instant slip and shine. If you follow a strict no-silicone regimen (common with certain curly-girl methods), this product will conflict with your routine. The silicones are water-soluble, so they won't cause that dreaded buildup, but purists will want to look elsewhere.

## FAQ

**How often should I use it?**
For damaged hair, once a week. For maintenance, every other week. Overuse can lead to protein overload, which makes hair stiff and brittle.

**Can I use it as a leave-in treatment?**
Technically yes, but I don't recommend it. It's too heavy and will leave residue. Rinse it thoroughly — that's where the slip comes from.

**Is it safe for color-treated hair?**
Yes, and it actually helps preserve color by smoothing the cuticle, which reduces fading. Just don't use it within 48 hours of a fresh dye job.
