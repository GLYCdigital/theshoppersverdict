**The 3.8-star crowd is split on these $30-ish eye patches — here's who should actually buy them**

You've seen the TikTok clips: hydrogel crescents melting into your under-eyes, supposedly erasing decade-old dark circles in 30 minutes. SUNGBOON EDITOR's Deep Collagen Melting Eye Patch is one of the most-hyped K-beauty launches of the last year, and at roughly $30 for 30 pairs, it sits firmly in the "treat yourself" category. But with 190+ ratings averaging 3.8 stars, the real-world results are more complicated than the algorithm suggests.

**What's actually in the jar**

You get 60 crescent-shaped hydrogel patches (30 pairs) packed in a clear plastic tub. No applicator tool, no sealed inner lid — just the patches floating in essence. The formula leads with collagen, but the real heavy hitters are glutathione (for brightening) and niacinamide (for texture). The patches themselves are thick, gelatinous, and cool on contact. As shown in the product image, they're generously sized — they cover from the inner corner of your eye to just past the outer crease.

**The melting factor is real**

Here's where the product earns its name: these patches genuinely dissolve into the skin. After 20–30 minutes, they've thinned noticeably, and the remaining essence absorbs without that sticky film most hydrogel masks leave behind. One verified buyer wrote: *"I fell asleep with them on and woke up with zero residue — my under-eyes looked airbrushed for the whole morning."* That plumping effect isn't permanent, but it does last several hours, which is better than the standard 10-minute post-mask glow.

**Where it stumbles**

The complaints cluster around two issues. First, adhesion. Several reviewers note the patches slide down if you're not lying perfectly still — one person said they *"ended up on my cheeks after 15 minutes of reading."* That's a dealbreaker if you planned to wear them while working or doing chores. Second, the fragrance. It's a floral, almost soapy scent that lingers long after removal. If you have sensitive eyes, this will sting. It's not disclosed prominently on the listing, which feels like an oversight at this price point.

**Who should buy this**

This is for someone who wants a visible, temporary brightening effect before an event, or who enjoys a meditative 30-minute skincare ritual. It's also a decent introduction to glutathione-based products if you're curious about that ingredient. Skip it if you have reactive skin, zero patience for slipping patches, or if you're expecting permanent dark-circle reversal — no topical product delivers that, and this one doesn't pretend to.

**The verdict: 3.7/5**

**Buy it if** you want a luxurious, effective brightening treatment for occasional use and don't mind lying down for half an hour. **Skip it if** you need something you can wear while moving around, or if fragrance sensitivity is a concern. There are cheaper alternatives that stick better (the Cosrx Snail Peptide patches come to mind), but none melt quite like these.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use these daily?**
A: Yes, but at 30 pairs per jar, daily use gets expensive fast. Most users report best results with 2–3 times weekly application.

**Q: Do they actually reduce dark circles?**
A: They brighten temporarily and improve pigmentation with consistent use over 3–4 weeks. They won't fix vascular or structural dark circles.

**Q: How long does one jar last?**
A: With 30 pairs, a twice-weekly routine lasts about 4 months. Without a resealable lid, the essence evaporates faster in dry climates — use within 3 months for best results.
