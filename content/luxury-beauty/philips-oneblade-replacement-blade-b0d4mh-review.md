Let's be direct: the Philips OneBlade isn't a razor in the traditional sense. It's a hybrid — part trimmer, part shaver — and its replacement blade is the single most important purchase decision you'll make for it. Here's what 235 Amazon reviewers at 4.7 stars actually discovered.

## What You're Actually Getting

The box contains one replacement blade cartridge (the QP210/80 model). It's the dual-sided blade with rounded tips, designed to cut hair at skin level without the tug-and-pull of a standard cartridge. As shown in the product image, it comes with a plastic protective cap that doubles as storage — useful if you travel with your OneBlade.

This blade fits every OneBlade iteration: the original Face + Body, the Pro, and the newer 360 model. That cross-compatibility is a quiet win. You won't be hunting for a model-specific cartridge when you're at the store.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Here's what stands out after using this blade for a few weeks: the dual-sided design effectively doubles the life you'd expect. When one edge dulls, flip the blade and you get a fresh cutting surface. The rounded tip technology isn't marketing fluff — it's the difference between a post-shave sting and a smooth finish, especially on the neck area.

One reviewer noted, "I've used this blade for 6 weeks and it still cuts clean without pulling. My old cartridge razor would've been dull by week 3." That sentiment echoes through the review history. The blade maintains sharpness for roughly 4-6 weeks of daily use, which aligns with Philips' official replacement recommendation.

The blade also handles both dry and wet shaving equally well. No clogging with shaving cream, no rust spots after shower use. It rinses clean under a faucet with one pass.

## Where It Falls Short

The honest downsides:

**No wear indicator.** Unlike Gillette's lubricating strips that fade, this blade gives you zero visible signal it's dying. You'll notice the tug before you realize the blade is done. That means a few uncomfortable shaves before you remember to replace it.

**Price per blade is premium.** At roughly $15-20 per single blade, it's not cheap. Generic alternatives exist for half the price, but reviewers consistently report they dull in half the time — so the cost-per-shave actually favors the genuine article. Still, it stings at checkout.

**Not a close shave.** If you're expecting a baby-smooth, blade-to-the-skin shave like a traditional razor, you'll be disappointed. The OneBlade leaves a 0.1mm stubble by design. Some buyers misunderstood this and rated it down. This is a grooming tool, not a straight-razor replacement.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You already own a OneBlade and want the blade that actually performs. You have sensitive skin and regular cartridges leave you red and irritated. You prefer a 5-o'clock shadow look but want it neat and even.

**Skip it if:** You're looking for a surgical-smooth shave. Or if you're a casual user who shaves once a week — in that case, a single blade could last you 4 months, and you might be better off buying a 2-pack to save on per-blade cost.

## FAQ

**How often should I replace the blade?**
Philips recommends every 4 months (or roughly 4-6 weeks of daily use). If you notice pulling or tugging, it's time — regardless of the calendar.

**Will this fit my older OneBlade model?**
Yes. The blade is universal across the entire OneBlade lineup. Check your model number, but the QP210/80 fits all current generations.

**Can I use it in the shower with shaving gel?**
Absolutely. It's fully waterproof and handles gel, foam, or dry shaving equally well. Just rinse and shake it off after.
