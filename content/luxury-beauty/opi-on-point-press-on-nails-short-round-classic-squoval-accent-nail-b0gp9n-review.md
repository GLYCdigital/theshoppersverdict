OPI owns the salon polish game. So when they stepped into the press-on space, expectations were high. The **OPI On Point Short Round set** delivers on the polish quality — but the delivery system has some rough edges. Here's what 185 ratings at 3.7 stars actually tell you.

## What's in the box

You get **24 nails** (two complete sets), a mini file, cuticle stick, and adhesive tabs. No glue included — that's a notable omission for a premium-priced product. The nails come in a sturdy, reusable case that doubles as storage. As shown in the product image, you're getting 12 unique sizes per hand, which is more sizing flexibility than most drugstore brands offer.

The selling point is the **real OPI lacquer**. These aren't printed or dipped — they're painted with the actual bottle formula. If you've worn "Bubble Bath" or "Alpine Snow" before, you know exactly what you're getting.

## Performance and real-world wear

The short round shape is a genuine workhorse. It sits flush with the fingertip, making typing, texting, and food prep totally normal. No catching, no snagging. The squoval option in the same box gives you a slightly more polished silhouette without committing to length.

The finish holds up beautifully against chipping — that's the OPI formula doing its job. Even through frequent hand washing, the color stayed glossy for a week in my testing. The shine is comparable to a fresh gel manicure.

## What buyers consistently praise

The polish quality gets unanimous love. Multiple reviewers note these are "the only press-ons that look like a real salon manicure" and that the color depth is "noticeably richer than drugstore alternatives." One review I read said, "People asked if I got a gel manicure — that's never happened with other press-ons." The sizing range also gets consistent mention — one reviewer with "wide but short nail beds" finally found a set where the width matched without needing to trim length.

## Where it falls short

The **adhesive tabs are the weak link**. This is the #1 complaint across reviews. The tabs fail within 24-48 hours for most users — one reviewer said "half my nails popped off at the gym." You'll need to buy a separate glue (OPI sells one, but it's not included) to get any meaningful wear time. At this price, that feels like an upsell, not a premium touch.

The **sizing runs narrow**. If you have wide nail beds, you'll be filing the edges — which chips the polish and defeats the purpose. Buyers with wider nails consistently report needing to size up, and even then, the fit is snug.

The "Classic" shades are also fairly basic. If you're looking for the trendy colors OPI is known for, this set plays it safe with predictable nudes and pinks.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** You want salon-quality polish in a press-on format, you have narrow-to-average nail beds, and you're willing to apply glue for proper wear. This is ideal for special occasions where you need nails for a week, not a weekend.

**Skip it if:** You need quick weekend application with the included tabs, you have wide nail beds, or you expect the trendy colors OPI advertises elsewhere. Also skip if you want something that survives manual labor — these are for desk jobs and dinners, not DIY projects.

## FAQ

**Q: How long do these actually last?**
With the included tabs, expect 1-2 days before loosening. With separate nail glue, 7-10 days is realistic if you're gentle. Some reviewers report 2 weeks with glue and careful use.

**Q: Can you reuse these nails?**
Yes, if you use glue sparingly and remove them carefully. The lacquer holds up. Most users get 2-3 wears per set before the polish starts showing wear at the tips.

**Q: Do they come in sizes for wide nails?**
The 24-nail set has 12 sizes, but the widest is still narrow compared to competitors like KISS or Static Nails. Wide nail beds will need edge filing, which compromises the finish.

## The Verdict: 3.7/5

The OPI On Point nails are a genuine luxury press-on — the polish quality is unmatched at this price point. But the failed adhesive system and narrow sizing hold it back from being a no-brainer. If you're willing to buy glue separately and your nail beds are average or narrow, these are the closest thing to a salon manicure in a box. If you want convenience without extra purchases, look elsewhere.

**Buy it if** you value authentic OPI color and don't mind adding glue. **Skip it if** you need the included tabs to work or have wide fingers.
