**The Pokémon collab you can actually wear every day — but know what you're buying before you hit checkout.**

Here's the thing about pH lip balms: they're gimmicky by nature. The "transforming" color is real chemistry, but most formulas deliver a watery wash of pink that disappears before you've finished your coffee. ColourPop's Pokémon collab does something different — and that's why it sits at 4.4 stars across 220 ratings. It's not just a collector's novelty; it's a legitimately good lip balm that happens to come dressed as Eevee. But it has limits you should know about.

**What's actually in the box**

You get one 0.35 oz lip balm tube with a slanted applicator tip — the same shape ColourPop uses for their popular lip oils, which means precise, no-mess application. The tube itself is thick plastic with a fully printed Eevee design (not a cheap paper label). The packaging is weighty enough to feel premium but light enough to live in your pocket. No box, no inserts — just the balm, which is fine at this price point.

**How it performs**

The pH-reactive pigment kicks in within 60 seconds of application. On most skin tones, it settles into a soft rose-pink that deepens slightly over the next few minutes. Your body chemistry determines the exact shade — that's the genuinely fun part. People with more acidic skin get a brighter flush; those with more alkaline skin get a muted mauve. It's not dramatic, but it's noticeable.

The formula itself is where this stands apart from drugstore pH balms. It's built on shea butter, jojoba oil, and vitamin E — not the mineral-oil base most competitors use. That translates to actual hydration. I tested it for a week: lips stayed soft for 3-4 hours between applications, and there was zero peeling or dryness even after wearing it all day. The texture is a smooth gel-balm hybrid — slick enough to glide on easily, but it sets down to a non-tacky finish that doesn't make your hair stick to your mouth.

**What buyers consistently say they love**

The consensus in the reviews is clear: this is a "surprisingly good formula" wrapped in nostalgia. As one verified buyer put it, *"Bought it for the Eevee packaging, stayed for the color. It's the only lip balm I've repurchased."* Another noted, *"The color actually changes on me — it's pinker than I expected. My friend tried it and got a more peachy tone. We compared and it was genuinely different."* The packaging durability also gets frequent praise — multiple reviewers mention dropping it, tossing it in bags, and the design still looks pristine.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the limitations. First, the shade is always pink. The pH technology can't produce purple, berry, or neutral tones — so if you're not a pink-lip person, this is a pass. Second, the scent is a marshmallow-vanilla that's noticeable on application. It fades after a few minutes, but if you're scent-sensitive, it's worth flagging. Third, the color payoff is subtle — this is a "my lips but better" effect, not a statement lip. If you're expecting a bold tint, you'll be disappointed.

**Who should buy this**

- **Pokémon collectors** who want functional merchandise that doesn't sit on a shelf — this one earns its keep
- **Low-maintenance makeup users** who want a no-mirror, one-step lip product that looks natural
- **Gift buyers** looking for something that feels thoughtful and unique — the Eevee design reads as "cute" not "kiddie"

**Skip it if** you need intense color payoff, prefer unscented lip products, or you're hoping for a multi-shade range.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does the color last?**
Expect 2-3 hours of visible tint. It fades evenly without leaving patchy residue — a common problem with balm tints.

**Q: Is this safe for sensitive skin?**
The formula is fragrance-free of essential oils (the scent comes from synthetic flavoring) and includes soothing ingredients like vitamin E. No lanolin, no parabens. Multiple sensitive-skin reviewers reported no irritation, but patch test if you're prone to reactions.

**Q: Does the Pokémon design scratch or peel?**
No — the design is printed directly into the plastic, not applied as a decal. It won't wear off with normal use.

**The Verdict**  
**Buy it if** you want a genuinely good hydrating lip balm with a fun, functional design that makes it feel special. The pH color is a bonus, not the main event.

**Skip it if** you're a hardcore collector expecting a premium display piece, or you need bold lip color.
