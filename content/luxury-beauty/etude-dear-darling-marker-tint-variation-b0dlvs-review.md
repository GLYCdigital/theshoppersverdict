If you've ever tried to nail that Korean gradient lip with a regular bullet lipstick and failed, the ETUDE Dear Darling Marker Tint is designed for exactly your struggle. This cult-favorite K-beauty product swaps the traditional doe-foot or bullet applicator for a felt-tip marker that lets you draw color precisely where you want it — no smudging, no bleeding, no guesswork.

**What's in the box:** You get a single 1.7g marker-style tint. That's it. No liner, no gloss, no fancy packaging. The marker itself is slim and lightweight — think a chunky highlighter pen. The felt tip is firm enough for precise outlining but flexible enough to fill in the inner lip. The cap clicks shut securely, which matters because this formula evaporates fast if left open.

**Real-world performance:** The pigmentation is the standout feature here. As shown in the product image, one pass delivers a vivid, juicy wash of color — I tested PK001 (a rosy pink) and OR02 (a coral-red). The first swipe gives you that "just bit a cherry" flush; layering two to three swipes builds to near-full opacity. The stain sets in about 60 seconds, and once it's set, it's not going anywhere. I wore it through a coffee and a turkey sandwich — the color faded to a soft, even tint rather than disappearing in patches.

But here's the catch. The formula is *fast-drying* — almost too fast. You have roughly 10-15 seconds to blend or adjust before it locks in place. On smooth, exfoliated lips, this is fine. On any dryness or flakiness, it clings to those rough patches and leaves an uneven, streaky result. One Amazon reviewer put it bluntly: "Lovely color but it highlighted every single dry line on my lips. Exfoliate first or skip it."

**What buyers consistently praise:** The precision applicator and staying power get the most love. As one verified buyer wrote: "The marker tip changed my gradient game completely. I can draw the exact shape I want in seconds, then smudge the edges with my finger. Nothing else I've tried does this." Another noted: "The color is shockingly vibrant for the price — it looks like a $30 lip stain, not a $10 one."

**Where it falls short:** Beyond the drying issue, the shade range is the biggest limitation. The Variation line offers only four colors — all pink-to-red territory. If you're looking for mauve, berry, or anything brown-toned, this isn't your product. And on deeper skin tones, several shades read more sheer than the swatches suggest. One reviewer noted: "I'm NC42 and PK001 barely showed up — I had to layer it five times to get visible color."

**Who should buy this:** 
- **K-beauty enthusiasts** who want an authentic gradient lip without the technique barrier
- **Low-maintenance makeup users** who want a lip product that survives meals
- **Anyone with naturally smooth, hydrated lips** who hates re-applying throughout the day

**Skip it if:** You have chronically dry lips, want a full-coverage bullet-style tint, or need shades beyond pink/red.

**FAQ:**

*Does it dry out your lips over time?* Yes, somewhat. The stain itself isn't moisturizing, and the alcohol-based formula can leave lips feeling tight by hour three. Layer a balm underneath (let it absorb first) or on top once the stain sets.

*How do you remove it?* Oil-based cleanser or micellar water. Water alone won't budge it — that's a feature, not a bug, but it means rubbing your lips raw with a makeup wipe is the alternative.

*Is it actually a marker?* It's a felt-tip applicator, not actual ink. The product is a liquid tint dispensed through the porous tip. It works like a marker, but it's definitely cosmetic.

**The Verdict:** The ETUDE Dear Darling Marker Tint Variation is a clever, genuinely useful product for a specific purpose — precise gradient lips with serious staying power. It's not a universal lip product, and the demanding formula requires a bit of prep work. But if your lips are smooth and you want that trendy Korean look without the fuss, this delivers exactly what it promises.

**Buy it if** you want precision lips and don't mind exfoliating. **Skip it if** you have dry lips or want a versatile shade range.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — A well-executed niche product that does one thing brilliantly, but its limitations keep it from being a must-have.
