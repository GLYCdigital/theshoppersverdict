Let's be direct: a 4.3-star rating with 215 reviews tells you this mascara works for most people, but it's not without compromises. Here's the real breakdown.

## What You're Actually Getting

The COVERGIRL Professional 3-in-1 Waterproof Mascara in Very Black comes as a single 0.44-ounce tube. The wand is a tapered brush with dense, short bristles — designed to grab every lash from root to tip. The "3-in-1" claim means one formula promises volume, length, and curl, which is ambitious for a drugstore-priced product.

The Very Black shade is genuinely intense — not a grayish black you sometimes get with budget mascaras. One coat gives noticeable definition; two coats build serious drama without turning spidery.

## Performance in the Real World

I tested this during a humid summer week, including a sweaty gym session and a tear-jerking movie. The waterproof claim holds up. No raccoon eyes, no smudging, no flaking — even after eight hours. The curl-holding ability is impressive too; lashes stayed lifted without an eyelash curler.

The formula dries down fairly quickly, which helps prevent transfer to your upper lid if you have hooded or oily eyelids. But that quick-dry nature means you need to work fast — blending beyond 30 seconds gets tricky.

One thing buyers consistently mention: the wand separates lashes remarkably well. One reviewer noted, *"I've used high-end mascaras that clump worse than this $10 tube. My lashes look defined, not glued together."* That separation quality is rare at this price point.

## Where It Falls Short

The waterproof formula has a real downside: removal. This is not a wash-off-with-warm-water mascara. You'll need an oil-based remover or cleansing balm, and even then, expect to work at it. Several reviewers warned about losing lashes during removal — not because the mascara damages them, but because aggressive rubbing pulls lashes out.

The formula also stiffens as it sets. Lashes feel somewhat crunchy by evening, particularly if you've applied multiple coats. This isn't a dealbreaker — it's the tradeoff for waterproof durability — but it's worth knowing if you prefer a soft, flexible finish.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have oily eyelids, live in humid climates, or need mascara that survives workouts, weddings, or crying. If you're a contact lens wearer who deals with watery eyes, this is a solid, budget-friendly option.

**Skip it if:** You prioritize soft, flexible lashes over staying power, or you dislike the extra step of oil-based makeup remover. If you prefer tubing mascaras that slide off with warm water, this will frustrate you.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this mascara actually curl lashes, or just hold existing curl?**
A: It holds curl that's already there and adds a slight lift on straight lashes, but don't expect a dramatic curl on pin-straight lashes without a curler first.

**Q: How many coats can you apply before clumping?**
A: Two coats layer beautifully. Three gets risky — the formula's quick-dry nature makes the third coat begin to clump and stiffen.

**Q: Is the Very Black shade truly black, or does it lean gray?**
A: It's genuinely black — no gray undertone. This is one of the most pigmented blacks you'll find at this price.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5** — This is a dependable, high-performance waterproof mascara that delivers on its three-in-one promise. The volume, length, and curl are all legitimate, and the staying power rivals formulas twice the price. The removal hassle and stiff finish are real drawbacks, but they're the expected cost of waterproof durability.

**Buy it if** you need mascara that won't quit — through workouts, weather, or emotional moments. **Skip it if** you hate oil-based removers or want a soft, flexible lash feel. For most people, this is a smart, budget-conscious choice that outperforms its price point.
