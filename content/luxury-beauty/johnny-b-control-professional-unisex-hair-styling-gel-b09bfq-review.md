The Johnny B Control gel comes in a hefty 16.9-ounce white jar — this isn't a travel-size afterthought. **Performance and Real-World Testing**

I tested this gel on both freshly washed hair and second-day hair, and here's what matters: it delivers. The hold is genuinely firm — not the crunchy, helmet-head stiffness you get from drugstore gels. It dries down to a flexible finish that keeps your style in place but still lets hair move naturally. One application in the morning held a side part through an 8-hour workday, a gym session, and a commute home.

The scent is a sleeper hit. It's got that classic barbershop aroma — think talc, spice, and just a hint of sweetness — but it dials back significantly within 30 minutes. You won't smell like a salon at hour three.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be direct about the downsides. The jar packaging is the biggest annoyance. You have to scoop product out with your fingers, and this gel is thick — it gets under your nails and takes two washes to fully remove. For a "professional" product, a pump or squeeze tube would be a significant upgrade.

Application requires speed. The gel starts setting within seconds, so you need to work it through quickly or you'll end up with tacky patches that are hard to redistribute. This isn't a beginner-friendly product if you're still figuring out your styling routine.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this gel work on wet or dry hair?**  
Apply to damp hair — towel-dried, not soaking. It distributes more evenly and gives you that extra 30 seconds before it sets. On dry hair, it's harder to work through and can leave visible streaks.

**Q: Will it leave flakes or white residue?**  
No, provided you use the right amount. Start with a dime-sized portion and add more if needed. Over-application is the only way to get visible residue, and even then it's minimal compared to most gels.

**Q: How does it compare to pomade or wax?**  
This is a gel, so it dries with hold rather than staying wet or greasy. If you want a slicker, more malleable finish that lasts all day, pomade is your better bet. If you want something that locks in place and doesn't move, this gel wins.
