The GAMMA+ Cyborg isn't just another cordless clipper trying to look futuristic. The full metal body, digital display, and brushless motor suggest serious intent. But at this price, you're paying for performance — not just aesthetics. Here's what 220 buyers at 4.4 stars are actually getting.

## What's in the Box

You get the clipper itself, four guide combs (sizes 1–4), a charging cable, brush, and blade oil. Missing from the package: a storage case or stand. For a premium tool, that's a notable omission — you'll want to budget for something to keep it in.

## Build and Ergonomics

The Cyborg's metal chassis is the first thing you notice. It's substantial — roughly 15 ounces — and the weight distribution feels deliberate. The tapered neck lets you grip it low for precision work around ears and hairlines without hand fatigue. The rubberized grip sections actually work, even with damp hands.

The blade is a standard detachable design, compatible with most Wahl-style snap-on blades. It comes with a #0000 (zero-gapped) blade installed, which is a nice touch for fade work right out of the box.

## Performance: The Brushless Difference

The star here is the digital brushless motor. It's quieter than most cordless clippers — think a low hum rather than a high-pitched whine. More importantly, it holds speed under load. Cutting through thick, dense hair didn't cause the stutter or bog-down you get with cheaper motors.

The 5-speed dial (3,000–5,500 RPM) gives real control. Low speeds for detailed outline work, high speeds for bulk removal. The digital display shows your current speed and battery percentage — genuinely useful, not gimmicky.

One real-world note: the battery claim says 4 hours, but multiple buyers report closer to 3 hours under continuous heavy use. One reviewer wrote: "Great clipper, but the battery drains faster than advertised when you're doing full head fades back-to-back." Factor that into your workflow if you're a working barber.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The dominant theme in reviews is the motor's power and consistency. As one verified buyer put it: "This thing cuts through thick hair like butter. My old clipper would stall — this doesn't even slow down." Another praised the ergonomics: "I've had carpal tunnel issues with other clippers. The Cyborg's shape lets me work for hours without pain."

Several reviewers also noted the blade quality out of the box — sharp, aligned, and zero-gapped properly. That's rare at this price point.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the battery reality check, the blade adjustment system is a minor annoyance. You need a small screwdriver to adjust the blade gap — not a dealbreaker, but competitors like the StyleCraft Instinct offer tool-free adjustment.

The lack of a charging stand also feels cheap for a "professional" tool. You're paying for a premium product; a basic cradle shouldn't be an extra.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Working barbers** who use clippers 6+ hours daily and need consistent torque without hand fatigue
- **Home users cutting thick curly or coarse hair** that stalls cheaper clippers
- **Fade specialists** who want the 5-speed control for precise blending work

Skip it if you're a casual home user who cuts hair once a month — you can get 80% of the performance for half the price with a mid-range corded clipper.

**Q: How loud is it compared to other cordless clippers?**

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
