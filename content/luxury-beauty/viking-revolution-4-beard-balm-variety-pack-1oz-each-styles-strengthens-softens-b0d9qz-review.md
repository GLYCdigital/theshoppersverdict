**The 1oz tins are the smartest thing Viking Revolution has done in years.** Most beard balm variety packs force you into 2oz jars you'll never finish. This four-pack of travel-sized tins solves that — you get four distinct scents for roughly the price of one full-size jar elsewhere, and you can actually test which formula works for your beard before committing to a larger purchase.

## What's Actually in the Box

Four 1oz aluminum tins: Cedarwood, Sandalwood, Bay Rum, and Unscented. Each is a classic balm base — shea butter, beeswax, argan oil, and carrier oils — whipped to a firm-but-workable consistency. As shown in the product image, the tins are compact and stackable, with a screw-top lid that seals well enough to survive a gym bag without leaking. The unscented tin is the sleeper hit here; it's rare to find a fragrance-free option in a variety pack, and it's genuinely useful for guys with sensitive skin or who wear cologne.

## Performance: What 225 Buyers Actually Reported

The 4.5-star average holds up. The balm warms quickly between palms and distributes evenly through a medium-length beard. The beeswax content provides a light-to-medium hold that keeps flyaways pinned down for 4-6 hours, though it won't survive a windy commute or hold a dramatic sculpted shape. The conditioning layer is where this earns its keep — shea butter and argan oil soften coarse hair noticeably. Multiple reviewers noted visible improvement in beard texture within the first week of daily use. One buyer put it plainly: *"My beard feels like a different animal after a week. Softer, less itchy, and the scents are all winners."* Another praised the value: *"Bought this to test scents before buying full size. Glad I did — the cedar is my favorite, bay rum is not for me."*

## Where It Falls Short

The hold is the biggest limitation. If your beard is thick, curly, or longer than 3 inches, this won't tame it — you'll need a stronger wax or butter. Scent strength is also uneven across the four tins. The sandalwood is subtle and pleasant; the cedar is bold and can be overpowering if you're sensitive to smoke-forward woods. The 1oz size is a double-edged sword too — at daily use, each tin lasts 2-3 weeks, so you'll reorder frequently if you find a favorite. And the tins are smaller than they look in photos; check the dimensions before buying if you expected a full-size jar.

## FAQ

**How long does one 1oz tin last?** With daily use (about a pea-sized amount), expect 2-3 weeks per tin. The pack covers roughly two months of consistent use.

**Does the balm work for short stubble?** Yes — apply a smaller amount (half a pea) to clean, damp skin and beard. It conditions the skin beneath and softens stubble without greasiness.

**Which scent is most popular?** Cedarwood consistently gets the most praise in reviews, followed closely by sandalwood. Bay Rum is the most polarizing — some love the spiced profile, others find it too sweet.
