If you've got flat, fine hair that collapses by noon, you've probably tried every mousse, spray, and root-lift product claiming to fix it. Most fail. The Schwarzkopf got2b Powder'ful Volumizing Styling Powder is different — it's the rare product that actually delivers on its promise of instant, lasting volume. But it's not without its quirks, and the 10g tube costs more per gram than most prestige serums. Here's the real breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The product comes in a small, squeezable tube — think travel-sized toothpaste. Inside is a fine, white silica-based powder that you sprinkle directly onto your roots. The packaging is functional but unremarkable; there's no applicator nozzle, just a small opening that makes it easy to dispense too much if you're not careful. Don't let the size fool you — this thing is concentrated. I've used mine three times a week for two months and the tube still feels half full.

**How It Performs**

The application method is the key to success. You part your hair, sprinkle a pinch of powder along the roots, massage it in with your fingertips, and style as usual. The effect is immediate — your roots get noticeable lift and grip, almost like you've backcombed without the teasing damage. The texture is matte, not shiny or greasy, and it holds through humidity and wind.

I tested it on a typical workday: applied at 8 AM, by 6 PM my roots still had visible volume. That's genuinely impressive. The powder also works well as a texturizing base for updos or braids — it gives hair enough grip that pins stay put.

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

Across 220 Amazon ratings, the pattern is consistent. One reviewer wrote, "I have baby-fine hair that falls flat minutes after blow-drying. This is the only product I've found that actually gives me volume that lasts." Another noted, "A tiny pinch is plenty. I thought the tube was too small for the price, but it's lasted me four months already." The value-per-use argument is real — despite the premium price per gram, the product's longevity makes it cost-effective.

**Where It Falls Short**

The biggest issue is the learning curve. Use too much, and your hair feels sticky, looks dull, and gets that "crunchy" texture nobody wants. One frustrated reviewer complained, "I used it like the instructions said and my hair felt like straw. Took two washes to get it out." That's not a manufacturing flaw — it's a dosage problem — but the packaging doesn't make it easy to control.

The powder also isn't suited for every hair type. If your hair is already dry, brittle, or chemically processed, the silica can worsen that texture. And if you're hoping for soft, flowing volume a la hair commercial models, this isn't it — this gives you gritty, textured lift, not silky bounce.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Fine-haired folks** whose volume disappears by lunchtime — this is your fix
- **Short-to-medium hair wearers** who want quick root lift without heat tools
- **Updo and braid enthusiasts** who need grip and hold that hairspray can't match

**Skip it if** you have dry, fragile hair, or if you're looking for a subtle, natural-look product rather than obvious, dramatic volume.

**FAQ**

**Q: How do I apply it without making a mess?**
Part hair in small sections, sprinkle a tiny pinch (less than you think you need) directly onto the roots, then massage with fingertips. Start with a quarter of what seems reasonable — you can always add more.

**Q: Will it work on thin hair?**
Yes, this is actually the target user. Fine, limp hair responds best. Just don't apply to the mid-lengths or ends — roots only.

**Q: How do I get it out?**
A thorough shampoo with a cleansing or clarifying formula works. If you've over-applied, you may need two washes. Don't use a co-wash or conditioner-only cleanse — it won't fully remove the powder.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** your hair is fine, your volume vanishes by afternoon, and you're willing to learn a quick application technique. It's one of the most effective root-lift products on the market — just respect the dosage.

**Skip it if** you have dry, fragile hair or want a natural, low-maintenance volume fix. This is a tool, not a miracle — it requires a bit of practice and a little extra effort at wash time.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Points off for the steep learning curve and the risk of over-application, but for the right user, this is a game-changer.
