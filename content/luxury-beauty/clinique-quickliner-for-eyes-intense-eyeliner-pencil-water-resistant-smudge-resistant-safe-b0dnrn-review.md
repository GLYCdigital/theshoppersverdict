Let's cut to the chase: Clinique's Quickliner For Eyes Intense has been a beauty counter staple for years, and the reformulated "Intense" version earns its keep. But before you hit "add to cart," here's what 225 Amazon reviewers (averaging 4.5 stars) actually think — and where this pencil falls short.

**The Twist-Up Reality Check**

This is a self-sharpening pencil, which means no sharpener, no broken tips, no wasted product. The twist mechanism is smooth — you get about 0.01 oz of product, which sounds tiny but lasts 3-4 months with daily use. The pencil itself is 5 inches long, comfortable to grip, and the tip comes to a fine point that's precise enough for tightlining.

**Performance That Survives Real Life**

The formula is the star here. It's a gel-pencil hybrid that glides on like butter — no dragging, no skipping, even on the sensitive waterline. It sets in about 30 seconds, and once it does, it's not going anywhere. One reviewer put it perfectly: *"I cried at my sister's wedding and my liner didn't budge. This stuff is legit waterproof."* Another noted: *"I'm a nurse working 12-hour shifts and this is the only liner that doesn't end up under my eyes by hour 8."*

The black shade (really more of a rich charcoal) is intensely pigmented — one pass gives you full opacity. It's also ophthalmologist-tested and safe for contact lens wearers, which is a genuine plus for sensitive eyes.

**Where It Stumbles**

Here's the honest part. This isn't a one-and-done for everyone. On oily eyelids, you'll notice some transfer by hour 6 — nothing catastrophic, but if you're someone who needs 14-hour wear, budget time for a touch-up or pair it with an eyeshadow primer. The shade range is also conservative: six colors total, mostly neutrals and one navy. No deep greens, no burgundies, no fun.

One reviewer summed it up: *"Great pencil, but I wish they'd add more colors. The formula is amazing, the options are boring."*

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The waterline loyalist**: If tightlining is your daily move, this pencil's precision tip and waterproof formula are worth the price.
- **The all-day worker**: Nurses, teachers, anyone who needs makeup to survive a full shift without mirror checks.
- **The sensitive eye user**: If most eyeliners irritate your eyes, Clinique's allergy-tested, fragrance-free formula is a safe bet.

**Who Should Skip It**

- **The bold color seeker**: If you want a purple wing or emerald liner, look elsewhere.
- **The oily lid warrior**: Without primer, you'll see some smudging by mid-afternoon. If that sounds like you, consider a liquid liner instead.

**FAQ: Real Questions, Straight Answers**

**Q: Does this actually stay on the waterline?**
A: Yes, but give it 30 seconds to set before blinking normally. The gel formula needs a moment to dry down. Once set, it stays put through tears, sweat, and humidity.

**Q: How do I remove it?**
A: This is waterproof, so your basic micellar water won't cut it. Use an oil-based remover or a cleansing balm. A cotton pad soaked in oil cleanser, pressed on the eye for 10 seconds, wipes it all off.

**Q: Is the "Intense" version different from the original?**
A: Yes — the Intense formula has 30% more pigment and a slightly softer, more gel-like texture. The original was stiffer and required more pressure.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, waterproof daily liner that won't irritate your eyes and requires zero sharpening. It's a solid 4.5-star purchase for most people, especially if you're a waterline user.

**Skip it if** you have very oily lids (you'll need to pair it with primer) or want creative color options. It's not the most exciting product in the world — but it's dependable, which for an eyeliner is often worth more than flashy.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — deducting half a star for the limited shade range and less-than-perfect performance on oily skin. Everything else? This pencil earns its spot in your makeup bag.
