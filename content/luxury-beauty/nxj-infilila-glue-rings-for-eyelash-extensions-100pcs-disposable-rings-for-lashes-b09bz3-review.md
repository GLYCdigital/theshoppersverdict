## What you actually get

The package contains 100 disposable rings, each about the diameter of a US quarter. They’re molded from a soft, flexible silicone — not the brittle plastic you sometimes find in cheap packs. The center well is deep enough to hold a generous drop of adhesive without it beading over the edge, which matters when you’re working with fast-setting glues. As shown in the product image, they’re uniformly shaped, with a subtle lip that helps keep the glue pooled rather than spreading thin.

No storage case, no applicator tool — just the rings. That’s honestly fine for the price, but it’s worth noting if you’re the type who loses small things.

## Performance and real-world use

The standout feature is the grip. That soft silicone surface holds your tweezers far better than the hard plastic rings I’ve used in the past. When you’re isolating a lash and dipping into the adhesive, the last thing you need is the ring spinning or shifting. These stay put. One reviewer noted, "I've been through three other brands and these are the only ones that don't flip over when I'm picking up a lash." That matches my experience — the base is stable enough that even a heavy-handed dip won’t tip it.

The deep well also solves a specific annoyance: glue drying out too fast. Because the adhesive sits in a contained reservoir rather than spreading across a flat surface, it stays workable longer. Another 5-star review mentioned, "The well is deep enough that I can work a full set without refilling every 10 minutes." For volume sets, that’s a genuine time-saver.

## Where it falls short

These rings are thin — noticeably thinner than metal or acrylic alternatives. That’s not a problem during normal use, but if you press down firmly to pick up a ring from the table, you can bend the edge. It doesn’t ruin the ring, but it can make the well slightly shallower. Handle them with a light touch.

Second, no lid. If you’re a professional who stocks up, you’ll need to transfer unused rings to a sealed container. They’re silicone, so they won’t degrade, but dust and lint from your workstation will stick to them. A zip bag solves it, but it’s an extra step.

## FAQ

**Can these be reused?**
Technically yes, if you scrape off the dried glue. But at roughly 10 cents per ring, cleaning them isn’t worth your time. They’re designed as disposables.

**Do they fit all glue types?**
Yes. The silicone doesn’t react with cyanoacrylate-based adhesives (the standard for lash extensions). Just avoid letting the glue sit on the ring overnight — it will bond to the surface.

**Are they the same size as the metal rings I’m used to?**
Slightly larger in diameter, which is actually helpful if you have bigger hands. The well depth is comparable to standard metal rings.
