Let me be upfront: when I saw "28 pieces for this price," I assumed I'd be unboxing a set of tiny 5ml sample bottles. That's not what this is. The Double Rhythm 28Pcs Gel Polish Set delivers 28 full 0.5oz (15ml) bottles — the same size you'd buy individually from a salon supply store. That alone changes the value equation dramatically.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get 28 gel polishes, all in the "Same Color Sam" collection — meaning these are coordinated shades that share a consistent undertone family rather than a random rainbow grab bag. The set skews toward wearable neutrals, dusty roses, mauves, and a few deeper berries and wines. As shown in the product image, the bottles are uniform, cleanly labeled, and packed in a sturdy foam insert that survives shipping without rattling.

Each bottle is 0.5oz with a standard gel polish brush. No light is included, no base or top coat — this is purely the color collection. If you're new to gel nails, factor in the cost of a UV/LED lamp and base/top coats separately.

## Performance and Real-World Wear

I tested these over a proper base coat with a 60-second LED cure at 36W. The formula is noticeably thinner than drugstore gel polish — which sounds like a downside but actually isn't. Thin viscosity means it self-levels beautifully. Brush strokes disappear within seconds of application, and you don't need to fight with thick, goopy layers that pool at the cuticles.

Pigmentation is the real story here. Most gel sets have one or two weak shades that need three or four coats. Not this one. The lightest pink in the collection reached full opacity in two thin coats. The deepest wine shade? One coat was nearly sufficient. That consistency across a 28-shade range is rare at any price point.

Wear test: I ran these for 14 days with a good top coat. No chipping, minimal tip wear. The darker shades did show slight shrinkage at the cuticle line by day 10, but that's a common gel polish behavior when the free edge isn't capped properly — not a defect in this specific set.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest practical annoyance: no swatch chart or color identification system beyond the bottle labels. With 28 nearly identical-looking bottles in shades that vary subtly, you'll spend time hunting for the exact mauve you want. I solved this by painting a small dot on each bottle cap, but it's a miss for a set marketed to serious users.

The brush is functional but narrow. On my average-width nail beds, I needed three passes per coat. For anyone with wide nails, plan on either working slower or investing in replacement brushes.

Finally, the darker shades in the set demand discipline with capping the free edge. If you don't seal the nail tip properly, you'll see shrinkage within a week. This isn't unique to Double Rhythm, but it's worth knowing before you commit to the deep reds.

A: No. It's 28 color bottles only. You'll need a UV/LED lamp (36W or higher recommended) plus separate base and top coats.
