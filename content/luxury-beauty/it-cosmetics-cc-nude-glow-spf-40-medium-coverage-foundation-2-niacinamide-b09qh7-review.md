**The "skin but better" product that actually delivers—if you pick the right formula.**

Let me be blunt: most CC creams are overhyped tinted moisturizers with a marketing budget. But the IT Cosmetics CC+ Nude Glow has earned its 4.5-star rating across 225 Amazon reviews for a reason. This isn't just a foundation—it's a hybrid that genuinely functions as skincare first, makeup second.

**What you're actually getting**

The box contains a single 1 fl oz tube with a pump dispenser—that's it. No sponge, no brush. But here's the thing: you only need one pump for your entire face. The formula is surprisingly concentrated for something marketed as "nude glow." At this density, a single tube lasts most users 10-12 weeks of daily wear. That math matters at this price point.

**The formula breakdown**

The star ingredients here aren't pigments—they're niacinamide (vitamin B3) and hyaluronic acid. The niacinamide works on redness and texture over time, while the hyaluronic acid pulls moisture into the skin. This is why so many reviewers mention their skin looks better *after* removing the product, not just during wear.

The SPF 40 comes from chemical filters (not zinc oxide), which is a smart choice. It means no white cast—a recurring pain point with mineral sunscreens, especially on deeper skin tones. Multiple reviewers with medium-deep complexions specifically noted this didn't leave that grayish tint.

**Real-world performance**

Here's where the "Nude Glow" name matters. This is a dewy finish, no question. If you're dry or normal skin, it gives that lit-from-within look that photographs beautifully. But if you're oily—especially in the T-zone—you'll need setting powder by mid-morning. One reviewer put it plainly: "I blot by 2 PM, but the glow is so pretty I don't care."

Coverage sits at a true medium. It evens out redness and mild hyperpigmentation, but it won't hide active acne or deep scars. This is a "your skin but better" product, not a full-coverage shield.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The skincare-meets-makeup hybrid approach is the recurring theme in positive reviews. One verified buyer wrote: "I've worn this daily for three weeks and my texture has genuinely improved. It's the first foundation that doesn't feel like I'm wearing anything." Another praised the pump: "No wasted product, no messy cap, just controlled dispensing."

The shade matching also earns consistent kudos—the undertones are actually accurate, not just "warm" or "cool" slapped on a label.

**Where it falls short**

The shade range tops out at 14 options, and the deepest shades are notably limited. If you have deep or rich skin tones, you may struggle to find a match—a legitimate frustration echoed in several reviews.

The silicone-heavy base means pilling is a real risk over water-based serums. If you layer this over a hydrating toner or watery essence, give it 60 seconds to absorb fully before applying. Otherwise, you'll get little balls of product rolling off your face.

And if you're expecting the coverage of the original CC+ Cream, adjust expectations. Nude Glow is intentionally lighter—it's for the no-makeup makeup days, not full glam.

**Who should buy this**

- **Dry or normal skin types** wanting a glow finish and real skincare benefits
- **Minimalists** who want one product that covers SPF, hydration, and light coverage
- **Sensitive skin**—fragrance-free and non-comedogenic, per the brand

**Skip it if** you have oily skin and hate powdering, need full coverage, or have deeper skin tones (check the shade range carefully before ordering).

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this work as a sunscreen replacement?**
A: SPF 40 with chemical filters is solid for daily commuter wear, but if you're outdoors for extended periods, you'll want a dedicated sunscreen underneath. One pump is about 1/4 teaspoon—you'd need 4 pumps for the full SPF amount.

**Q: How does this compare to the original CC+ Cream?**
A: Nude Glow is lighter in coverage and finish. The original is fuller coverage with a more matte result. This one feels like nothing on the skin; the original has more presence.

**Q: Will it settle into fine lines?**
A: The hyaluronic acid helps plump skin, and the dewy finish actually minimizes the look of texture. Most reviewers over 40 report it sits well in wrinkles—just avoid powdering heavily, which can emphasize them.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a glow-finish foundation that doubles as skincare and don't mind powdering mid-day. This is a genuine hybrid that earns its 4.5 stars.

**Skip it if** you need full coverage, have oily skin, or fall outside the light-to-medium shade range.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A legitimate skincare-makeup hybrid that delivers on its promises, with only minor downsides around finish and shade range. The niacinamide benefit and SPF 40 make it one of the smarter formulations in this category.
