## What You Actually Get

Six caps in assorted colors. Each measures roughly 22 inches in diameter when laid flat — enough to cover most hair lengths and volumes. The packaging is straightforward: a simple polybag, nothing fancy. Given the price point, that's fine.

The real story is the construction. Each cap has a soft cotton interior lining and a waterproof exterior. Yes — and this is where the cap earns its keep. The elastic band is sewn into a channel that creates a decent seal around your hairline. ## What Buyers Consistently Praise

## Where It Falls Short

The elastic band is the weak point. On head circumferences over 22 inches, the cap fits but feels tight. One reviewer with a larger head size said: *"I had to stretch the elastic out over a bowl for a day before it was comfortable."* Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

Also, if you use shower caps for deep conditioning treatments, this isn't your product. The cotton interior absorbs product instead of trapping heat against your hair. For that purpose, a disposable plastic cap works better.
