Let's be honest: most pressed powders with "included brushes" are a trap. The brush is useless, the powder is chalky, and you end up tossing the whole thing in a drawer. The Revlon PhotoReady Pressed Face Powder with Brush is not that product. At $9.99, it's one of the few drugstore compacts that actually delivers on its promises — and with a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, I'm not the only one who thinks so.

## What You're Actually Getting

The compact contains 0.38 oz of pressed powder — a standard amount for the price point. The standout feature is the retractable brush built into the base of the compact. Twist the bottom, and a domed brush emerges. It's soft enough for daily use, picks up product well, and actually fits the contours of the face. As shown in the product image, the brush retracts fully when not in use, keeping the compact pocket-friendly.

The formula is described as "PhotoReady" — Revlon's light-diffusing technology that scatters light rather than reflecting it. That translates to a soft-focus effect on skin that blurs imperfections without that heavy, matte-mask look. It's oil-free and fragrance-free, which matters if you have reactive skin.

## Performance and Build Quality

I tested this powder over both liquid foundation and bare skin. Over foundation, it sets makeup without disturbing the base underneath. It controls shine for roughly 6-8 hours before I need a touch-up — impressive for a drugstore powder, especially in summer humidity.

The finish is the real selling point. This isn't a flat, matte powder. It leaves a natural, slightly luminous finish that photographs well (hence the "PhotoReady" name). If you've tried powders that make your face look like a chalkboard flashback in photos, this one won't do that. The texture is finely milled — no visible grain or patchiness on application.

One buyer noted: *"I've been using this for months and it's the only powder that doesn't settle into my fine lines. The brush makes it easy to reapply during the day."* That tracks with my experience — the formula is light enough to layer without caking.

The compact itself is sturdy. The hinge hasn't loosened after daily use, and the mirror inside is clear and distortion-free. The brush, however, requires maintenance. Product builds up on the bristles over time, so you'll need to wash it every couple of weeks. Several reviewers mentioned this as a minor annoyance — one wrote, *"The brush collects powder and gets stiff if you don't clean it. I just rinse it with soap and it's fine."*

## Where It Falls Short

The shade range is the biggest issue. There are only six shades, and they skew light to medium. If you have deep or rich skin tones, you likely won't find a match. That's a genuine limitation that excludes a significant portion of shoppers.

The brush, while functional, isn't ideal for precise application. It works well for all-over setting, but you'll want a separate brush for targeted areas like under the eyes or around the nose. Also, the powder can look slightly powdery if you apply too much — a light hand is necessary, especially in the first week of use when the powder is fresh and picks up easily.

## Who Should Buy This

This is for you if:
- You want a reliable setting powder that won't break the bank
- You need something for on-the-go touch-ups (the built-in brush is a lifesaver for your bag)
- You have normal to combination skin and want a natural, not-matte finish
- You're a beginner looking for an affordable, forgiving powder

Skip it if:
- You have deep skin tones — the shade selection won't serve you
- You prefer heavy, full-coverage powder foundation (this is a setting powder, not a foundation substitute)
- You hate cleaning brushes — this one requires regular washing

## FAQ

**Q: Does this powder cause flashback in photos?**
A: No. The light-diffusing formula is specifically designed to prevent the white cast that many powders create in flash photography. That said, apply sparingly — heavy application of any powder can still cause issues.

**Q: Is the brush replaceable if it wears out?**
A: No, the brush is built into the compact. If it deteriorates, you'd need to buy a new compact. In practice, with regular washing, the brush should last as long as the powder (several months of daily use).

**Q: Can I use this powder alone without foundation?**
A: Yes, but it won't provide coverage. On bare skin, it blurs pores and controls shine while leaving your natural skin visible. If you're looking for coverage, pair it with a tinted moisturizer or foundation.

## The Verdict

The Revlon PhotoReady Pressed Face Powder earns its 4.6/5 rating. It delivers a genuinely flattering finish, the built-in brush is a functional addition rather than a marketing gimmick, and the price is right. The limited shade range is a real drawback, but if you fall within the available shades, this is one of the best-value powders at the drugstore.

**Buy it if** you want an affordable, dependable setting powder that looks natural in person and in photos. **Skip it if** you're outside the shade range or need heavy coverage from your powder.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A solid, honest product that punches above its price point.
