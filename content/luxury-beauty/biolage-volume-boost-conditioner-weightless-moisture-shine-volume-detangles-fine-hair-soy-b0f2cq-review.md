**What's in the Bottle**

The 33.8 fl oz pump bottle delivers the full Volume Boost system: a sulfate-free, silicone-free conditioner built around soy protein, biotin, and a botanical complex. The texture is noticeably lighter than standard conditioners — almost a milk consistency, not a heavy cream.

**Real-World Performance**

Here's where this conditioner separates itself from drugstore volumizing formulas. Applied from mid-length to ends (skip the roots — it's not a 2-in-1), it detangles immediately. The soy protein does double duty: it coats the hair shaft to smooth the cuticle, but it's light enough to rinse clean without residue.

The results after a few weeks of consistent use: noticeably thicker-feeling hair, fewer broken strands in your brush, and a genuine shine that doesn't look greasy. The biotin component isn't a gimmick — multiple long-term reviewers report less shedding at the 30-day mark.

**Where It Falls Short**

Two honest limitations. First, the scent. It's not the fresh botanical aroma the green bottle suggests — it's more of a classic salon-chemical fragrance that lingers. If you're sensitive to fragrance, this could be a dealbreaker.

Second, this is a fine-hair formula, full stop. If you've got coarse, thick, or curly hair, this won't provide enough slip or moisture. You'll need to layer a leave-in or a heavier mask. And on hard water, you'll use more product to get the same slip — factor that into the cost-per-use calculation.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this conditioner silicone-free?**
A: Yes, it's silicone-free and sulfate-free. That's why it doesn't flatten fine hair — no heavy polymers coating the strand. The flip side: it won't give that instant "glass hair" smoothness that silicones provide.

**Q: How often should I use it?**
A: For fine hair, every wash is fine. But if you notice buildup, alternate with a clarifying shampoo once a week. The formula is lightweight enough for daily use without weighing hair down.

**Q: Does it work on hard water?**
A: It works, but you'll need 1.5x more product than normal. The sulfate-free formula doesn't lather as aggressively, and hard water minerals compete for the conditioning agents. Consider a chelating shampoo once a month if your water is very hard.
