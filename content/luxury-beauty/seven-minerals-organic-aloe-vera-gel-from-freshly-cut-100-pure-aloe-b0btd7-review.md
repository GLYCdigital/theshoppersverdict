## What's In The Bottle

You get a 12-ounce pump bottle of gel that's harvested and cold-processed within hours of cutting. ## Where It Falls Short

The lack of preservatives is a double-edged sword. Once opened, you have about 6 months before it starts to degrade — and that's with refrigeration. And the pump nozzle can clog with dried gel between uses, requiring a quick rinse.
