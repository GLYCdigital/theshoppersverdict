## What You Actually Get

Inside the box you'll find 12-16 individual cluster trays (depending on the set you order), each holding multiple pre-made fans in the Fluffy V style. The "240D" refers to the density — these are thick, voluminous fans that taper to a fine point, mimicking the handmade Russian volume technique. The clusters come in varying lengths, typically ranging from 8mm to 14mm, so you can layer them for a gradient effect. As shown in the product image, they arrive neatly organized in a clear tray with a protective lid — no tangled, crushed lashes out of the box.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The standout feature here is the weight. For a 240D cluster, these are remarkably light. The thin, flexible band sits flush against your lash line and becomes nearly invisible once your natural lashes blend with the fan. The Fluffy V shape gives a wispy, spiky texture rather than the solid "wall of lash" look that cheaper dense clusters produce. That's the difference between looking like you've had extensions done and looking like you're wearing costume lashes.

Wear time is genuinely impressive. Most buyers report 5-7 days of continuous wear with proper application and removal. One reviewer noted: *"I slept in these for four nights and they didn't budge. ## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The customization angle comes up repeatedly. Because these are clusters, not a full strip, you control the placement. You can apply three clusters per eye for a natural daily look, or stack five-plus for full glam. One verified buyer summed it up: *"I've tried every brand from Amazon — these are the first ones that don't look like spiders. ## Where They Fall Short

Let's be honest about the application curve. If you've never worked with lash clusters, your first attempt will likely test your patience. The fans are delicate — pulling them off the tray too aggressively can bend the base. It takes a few tries to get the placement angle right so they don't feel poking or look misaligned.

The bigger caveat: this is just the lashes. Kmilro doesn't include adhesive, sealant, or a tweezers in the box. You'll need to buy a bond and sealant system separately, which adds $10-15 to your total investment. That's a common practice in the cluster space, but it's worth knowing before you check out.

## Who Should Buy This

**The DIY lash convert** — someone who's done individual lashes before and wants faster application with bigger volume. **The salon refugee** — tired of dropping $150+ every three weeks and willing to invest an hour learning a new skill. **The volume lover** — if you've tried strip lashes and found them too heavy or obvious, these give you that full, fluffy look with a fraction of the weight.

Skip them if you want a 5-minute routine with zero learning curve, or if you prefer a natural, barely-there lash look — these are unapologetically dramatic.

## FAQ

**Q: How long do these actually last with daily wear?**
A: With proper application and a good bond/sealant, most users get 5-7 days. Oil-based cleansers will break down the adhesive, so use an oil-free remover and avoid touching your eyes.

**Q: Are these safe for sensitive eyes?**
A: The lashes themselves are lightweight and shouldn't irritate — but the adhesive you pair them with is the variable. Test your bond/sealant on your wrist first, and if you have a known lash adhesive sensitivity, proceed with caution.

**Q: Can I reuse the clusters after removal?**
A: Yes, if you remove them gently with a lash remover and store them in the tray. Expect 2-3 reuses before the base loses its shape and the fan starts to look ragged.
