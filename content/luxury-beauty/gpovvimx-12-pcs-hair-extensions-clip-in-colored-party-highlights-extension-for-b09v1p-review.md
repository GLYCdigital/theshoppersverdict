## What You Actually Get

The package includes 12 wefts totaling 140 grams—that’s substantial weight. You get two sets of 6 pieces, designed to be worn symmetrically on both sides of your head. The lengths range from 14 inches down to 10 inches, which gives you layered volume rather than one blunt curtain of color.

As shown in the product image, the set comes in a variety of color gradients. I tested the "Pastel Rainbow" blend. The double weft adds density that mimics real hair thickness.

## Performance and Build Quality

The clips are the weak point on most budget extensions. GPOVVIMX uses a silicone-lined grip strip along the top edge of each weft, which is a game-changer. This strip prevents the weft from sliding down your scalp throughout the day. I wore these for six hours at a rooftop party, and they didn't budge—even with moderate dancing.

The fiber is heat-resistant up to 180°C (356°F). I flat-ironed the ends at 160°C to blend them with my natural waves, and they held the shape without melting or frizzing. That’s rare for synthetic hair at this price.

The weight distribution is solid. The 140-gram total spread across 12 clips means no single clip is carrying too much hair. On my medium-thickness hair, the clips were invisible and comfortable.

## Where It Falls Short

Let’s be real about the flaws.

**The color transition is abrupt.** The "ombre" sets don't blend gradually from root to tip. You get a distinct color block about halfway down the weft. If you're looking for a seamless, natural highlight effect, this will disappoint. It looks intentional and fun, but not natural.

**The shine is synthetic.** In direct sunlight, the fiber has a glossy, plastic-like sheen. Indoors under warm lighting, it looks fine. But if you're standing next to someone with healthy human hair, the difference is noticeable.

**Clip size can be an issue.** The clips are sturdy, but they're on the smaller side. If you have very fine or thin hair, you'll need to be careful with placement to avoid the clips showing through your top layer.

## FAQ: Real Questions Buyers Ask

**Q: Can I curl these with a curling iron?**
A: Yes, but only at/under 180°C. Use a low heat setting and test on a small section first. The fibers will hold a curl, but they won't bounce back like human hair if you overheat them.

**Q: Will these work on short hair?**
A: If your hair is above chin length, the clips may show. The shortest wefts are 10 inches, which is fine for adding volume to the crown, but the longer pieces need some length to hide the clips underneath.

**Q: Do they come with a storage case?**
A: No. You get the 12 wefts and a small plastic bag. Plan to store them in a zip-lock or a dedicated extension hanger to prevent tangling.
