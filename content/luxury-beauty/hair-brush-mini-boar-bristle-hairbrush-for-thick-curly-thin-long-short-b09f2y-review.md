**Performance and real-world use**

For a mini brush, the bristle density is impressive. On thick, wavy hair, it distributes natural oils from root to mid-length without aggressively yanking through knots. Curly hair is more complicated. Loose curls and wavy textures handle this brush well for smoothing the crown and distributing product. But if you have tight coils, this is not a detangling tool. You'll need to finger-detangle or use a wide-tooth comb first. Once hair is tangle-free, the mini boar brush works for polishing the outer layer. Several Amazon reviewers note using it specifically for their edges and baby hairs — that's a smart application.

**FAQ**

**Does this work on wet hair?**
No. Boar bristles are designed for dry or damp hair only. Using it on soaking wet hair will cause breakage and the bristles will splay permanently.

**How do I clean it?**
Remove loose hair with a comb or your fingers after each use. Once a week, wipe bristles with a slightly damp cloth and let air dry bristle-side down. Never submerge the wooden handle.

**Is this cruelty-free boar bristle?**
The listing doesn't specify sourcing. Most boar brushes use natural shed bristles collected during grooming, but if that matters to you, contact the seller before purchasing.
