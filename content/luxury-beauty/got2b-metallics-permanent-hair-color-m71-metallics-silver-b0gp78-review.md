**Opening Hook: The Silver Standard or a Silicon Gamble?**

Most drugstore metallics are a lie. They promise chrome and deliver dishwater. Got2b's M71 Metallics Silver is the exception — it actually looks like metal, not gray hair. But that payoff comes with a price: it demands near-white hair, tolerates zero shortcuts, and fades fast if you neglect maintenance. At 3.9 stars across 195 ratings, the consensus is clear: this is a high-reward, high-effort product for people who already know their way around bleach.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains a 60ml color creme, 60ml developer lotion, a pair of gloves, and an applicator bottle. That's enough for shoulder-length hair — if you've got hair past your bra strap, buy two. The formula includes an "anti-breakage complex" that functions similarly to bond repair additives, which is a meaningful upgrade over the $8 box dyes that leave your hair feeling like straw. There's also a small after-color treatment sachet, but it's not enough for a full head — budget for a deep conditioner anyway.

**Real-World Performance**

As shown in the product image, the M71 delivers a genuinely reflective silver with a cool, blue-based undertone. On properly pre-lightened hair (level 9 or lighter), the color develops in about 30 minutes and covers evenly. One reviewer noted: "I've used every silver dye at the drugstore and this is the first one that didn't come out looking like old man gray." The bond repair additive makes a noticeable difference — hair retains elasticity and doesn't feel like brittle wire after processing.

But here's the catch: this is not a forgiving formula. Multiple verified buyers report patchy results on hair that was "blonde" but actually sat at level 7 or 8. One frustrated reviewer wrote: "My roots came out silver, my lengths came out greenish-gray. I had to bleach again just to fix it." That's the reality of metallic dyes — they don't blend, they amplify what's underneath.

**The Praise**

What buyers consistently love:
- The metallic finish is unique — it looks like foil, not flat gray
- The bond repair component genuinely reduces breakage versus other permanent dyes
- The kit is complete and the developer is thick enough not to drip everywhere

**Where It Falls Short**

Fade rate is the biggest complaint. Without a purple shampoo used every other wash, the silver shifts to a greenish-brass by week three. One buyer said: "By day 20 I looked like I had swamp water highlights." The ammonia smell is also potent — it's not the worst on the market, but it's noticeable and lingers. And if your hair isn't already light enough, this product will not lift it effectively; the included developer is 20 volume, not enough to bleach dark hair.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The committed silver chaser**: You're already bleaching your hair to platinum and want a metallic finish that stands out.
- **The DIY colorist with experience**: You've done fashion colors before and know your hair's porosity and level.
- **Skip it if**: Your hair is darker than a level 8, you hate maintenance routines, or you're looking for a low-commitment gray coverage.

**FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Ask**

**Q: Does this work on dark blonde or light brown hair?**
A: No. You need level 9-10 (very pale blonde) for the silver to show true. On darker hair, you'll get murky, uneven coverage that looks more green than metallic. Bleach first if you're serious about this color.

**Q: How long does the color last?**
A: With purple shampoo used every 2-3 washes, expect 4-6 weeks of decent silver. Without it, the color shifts to a greenish-brass around week three. It's permanent dye, but the metallic pigments fade faster than natural-looking shades.

**Q: How damaging is it compared to regular box dye?**
A: Less damaging than standard permanent color due to the bond repair additive, but it's still an ammonia-based permanent dye. If your hair is already compromised from bleach, this will not fix it. Deep condition weekly during the first month.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You have pre-lightened hair and want a true metallic silver without salon prices. The bond repair formula and reflective finish genuinely outperform other drugstore metallics.

**Skip it if:** Your hair isn't already nearly white, you're not willing to commit to purple shampoo maintenance, or you want a low-maintenance gray. This product rewards preparation and punishes laziness.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — Excellent payoff for the right candidate, but the demanding application requirements and fast fade hold it back from full marks.
