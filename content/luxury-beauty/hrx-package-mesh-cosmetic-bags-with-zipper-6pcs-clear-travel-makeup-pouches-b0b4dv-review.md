Let's be honest: most cosmetic bag sets are junk. The zippers catch, the mesh rips after three trips, and you end up with a drawer full of useless pouches you never touch. The HRX 6-piece set doesn't fall into that trap — and with a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, buyers clearly agree. But is it worth your money? Here's the breakdown after examining the construction, sizing, and real-world feedback.

## What's Actually in the Set

You get six bags in graduated sizes, which is the smartest thing about this system. The smallest is roughly 5.5 x 4 inches — perfect for rings, earrings, or pill organizers. The largest hits about 10 x 7.5 inches, big enough for a full-size shampoo bottle or a week's worth of skincare minis. The middle sizes handle makeup brushes, hair ties, chargers, and cables.

Each bag features a clear mesh front panel with a solid polyester back. The mesh isn't flimsy window-screen material — it's a denser PET weave that holds its structure. The zippers are the standout component: smooth-gliding with sturdy metal pulls that don't feel like they'll snap off in your hand. As one verified buyer noted, "I've bought three sets of these from different brands and returned them all because of zipper issues. These actually work."

## Real-World Performance

**The good:** The transparency is genuinely useful. You'll never mix up your shampoo with your body wash again, and TSA agents can see what's inside without you unpacking. The variety of sizes means you can create a dedicated system — one bag for liquids, one for brushes, one for jewelry, one for electronics. That's organization you'll actually maintain because it's zero-effort.

**The not-so-good:** The mesh is not waterproof. If a bottle leaks (and bottles always leak), the liquid seeps through the mesh and stains whatever's next to it in your suitcase. Several reviewers mentioned this exact issue. Also, the two largest bags don't have reinforced bottoms — load them with heavy items and they sag into a lumpy shape that doesn't pack flat.

## Where It Falls Short

The lack of waterproofing is the biggest miss, especially for a product marketed toward travel. A simple PVC lining on the interior would fix this, but you don't get it at this price point. The smaller bags also run tight — the 5.5-inch option fits a travel toothbrush and paste, but don't expect to cram a full skincare routine into it.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a frequent traveler who needs to see everything at a glance. You're someone who likes dedicated homes for different categories. You're tired of fighting with cheap zippers and want something that'll survive a year of regular use.

**Skip it if:** You carry mostly liquids and need leak-proof protection. You prefer opaque bags for privacy. You need one large bag rather than a multi-pouch system.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these fit TSA 3-1-1 liquids?**
Yes — the medium and large bags easily hold quart-size toiletry bottles, and the clear mesh makes security checks faster.

**Q: Will the zippers break if I overstuff them?**
The zippers are reinforced with a double-stitched track. They handle moderate overstuffing well, but like any zipper, extreme force will eventually cause failure.

**Q: Are these machine washable?**
Hand wash only. Tossing them in a washing machine will warp the mesh and damage the zipper pulls.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5** — This is one of the better value organizer sets on Amazon. The build quality punches above its price point, the size variety covers nearly every travel need, and the zipper reliability is genuinely impressive. The waterproofing gap and flimsy bottom panels on larger bags keep it from perfection, but for most travelers, this set solves more problems than it creates.

**Buy it if** you want a durable, transparent organization system for travel or daily use. **Skip it if** you're packing mostly liquids or need a single heavy-duty toiletry bag. For everyone else, this is a solid, honest purchase that won't leave you disappointed.
