**The $37 keratin treatment that actually delivers — with one important catch.**

If you've priced a salon keratin treatment lately, you know the sticker shock: $200–$400 for something that lasts three months. So when KERAGEN's Brazilian Smoothing Treatment shows up at $37.24 with 4.4 stars from 220 reviewers, it's worth a hard look. But here's the honest question: does it actually work, or is it another at-home disappointment?

I dug into the formula, the chemistry, and what real buyers are saying. Here's the straight story.

## What's in the Box

You get one 16.9 oz (500ml) bottle with a pump dispenser — shown clearly in the product image. That's it. No gloves, no applicator brush, no heat protectant (you'll need your own flat iron, ideally with temperature control). The formula is a keratin-based smoothing complex with hydrolyzed proteins and conditioning agents designed to coat the hair cuticle and seal in moisture.

One bottle is realistically enough for shoulder-length hair across two full treatments, or one treatment on thick, waist-length hair. Budget to buy a second bottle if you have dense or very long hair.

## The Real-World Results

The application process is time-consuming — budget 90 minutes to two hours. You'll shampoo with a clarifying wash (not included), towel-dry, apply the treatment section by section, let it sit for 15–20 minutes, blow-dry completely, then flat iron each section at 400°F or higher. It's not a quick process, but it's also not complicated.

Here's what buyers consistently report: hair becomes noticeably smoother, shinier, and less frizzy — especially in humid conditions. One reviewer with naturally curly hair noted, "My hair was softer and easier to manage for about 5 weeks. It didn't fully straighten my curls but took out most of the frizz." Another buyer with color-treated hair called it "the best at-home smoothing treatment I've tried — no weird smell like some others."

The "no smell" point is worth emphasizing. Many budget keratin treatments use formaldehyde-releasing chemicals that smell like a chemistry lab. KERAGEN's formula is noticeably milder, which several reviewers flagged as a deciding factor.

**Where it falls short:** This is a *smoothing* treatment, not a straightening one. If you have tight curls or kinky hair and expect bone-straight results, you'll be disappointed. It relaxes curl pattern by maybe 50–70%, and the effect fades faster than salon treatments — most reviewers report 4–6 weeks of visible results, not the 12 weeks salons promise.

Also worth noting: a handful of reviewers reported the treatment left hair feeling slightly heavy or greasy if they over-applied. A little goes a long way — start with half the amount you think you need.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have wavy or frizzy-prone hair, you're tired of spending 30 minutes blow-drying every wash day, and you want a middle-ground option between a $400 salon treatment and a $15 drugstore serum. Also great if you're sensitive to formaldehyde smells.

**Skip it if:** You have very tight curls and want true straightening, you wash your hair every single day (results will fade in 3–4 weeks), or you're not prepared to commit 90 minutes to the application process.

## Common Questions, Straight Answers

**Q: How long does it actually last?**
A: Most buyers report 4–6 weeks before frizz starts creeping back. Washing less frequently, using sulfate-free shampoo, and avoiding chlorine extends the results.

**Q: Can I use this on color-treated hair?**
A: Yes — the formula doesn't strip color. In fact, several reviewers noted it helped *maintain* their color by reducing washing frequency. Just avoid applying it to freshly bleached hair within 2 weeks.

**Q: Is it safe for pregnancy?**
A: The formula is low-formaldehyde, but it's not zero-formaldehyde. The manufacturer doesn't explicitly state it's pregnancy-safe. If you're pregnant, talk to your doctor or stick to a truly formaldehyde-free option.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

KERAGEN's Brazilian Smoothing Treatment is a legitimate at-home alternative to salon keratin — not a perfect substitute, but a strong value at $37.24. It delivers on the core promises: smoother, shinier, easier-to-manage hair with significantly less frizz and faster blow-dry time.

**Buy it if** you want salon-adjacent results without the salon price tag and understand it's a smoothing treatment, not a chemical straightener.

**Skip it if** you need dramatic curl relaxation or can't commit to the two-hour application process. And if you wash daily, budget for it to fade in under a month.
