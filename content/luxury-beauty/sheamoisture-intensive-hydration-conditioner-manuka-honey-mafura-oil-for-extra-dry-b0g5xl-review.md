**If your hair drinks moisture like a desert plant and laughs at regular conditioners, this is the product that finally makes it shut up and absorb.**

I've tested a lot of "intensive hydration" claims over the years. Most are marketing fluff — a thin lotion that smells nice and does nothing by lunchtime. SheaMoisture's Intensive Hydration Conditioner with Manuka Honey and Mafura Oil is not that. This is the real deal for anyone with extra-dry, curly, coily, or chemically processed hair that's been crying for help.

## What You're Actually Getting

The 12-ounce pump bottle delivers a thick, almost buttery conditioner that feels more like a hair mask than a daily rinse-out. The star ingredients are what you'd expect from this brand: raw Manuka honey (a humectant that pulls moisture in), Mafura oil (a deep-penetrating emollient from East Africa), and Shea butter as the base. There's also a hit of Baobab oil and marshmallow root extract.

Notably absent: sulfates, silicones, and mineral oil. That's a big deal if you've invested in color or keratin treatments — this won't strip them out.

## Performance and Real-World Use

Here's where this shines: it actually penetrates. I applied it to soaking-wet hair, left it for five minutes, and rinsed. The slip was immediate — my fingers glided through tangles that usually require a fight. After drying, my hair didn't just feel soft; it felt *heavy with moisture* in the good way. The hydration lasted through day two and into day three, which is remarkable for a conditioner that costs less than a salon deep treatment.

The thickness is both a strength and a consideration. You need less than you think — about a quarter-sized dollop for shoulder-length hair. That makes the bottle last roughly twice as long as a typical conditioner.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The fragrance is *strong*. If you're sensitive to scents or prefer fragrance-free products, this will overwhelm you. It's not subtle — it's a sweet, honey-forward profile that stays on your hair until the next wash.

The formula's richness also means it's not ideal for fine or thin hair. If your hair is loose-textured and gets greasy easily, this will weigh it down. It works best as a weekly deep-conditioning treatment for those hair types, not an everyday rinse-out.

Finally, the pump can be finicky. If you store the bottle on its side or in a cold bathroom, the mechanism can jam. Keep it upright and it's fine, but it's worth knowing.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this every day?**
A: You can, but you shouldn't need to. For most hair types, 1–2 times per week is plenty. If you have very coarse or extremely damaged hair, daily use is fine — just use a small amount.

**Q: Does it work for low-porosity hair?**
A: Yes, with a caveat. Low-porosity hair (where moisture sits on top) needs heat to help the ingredients absorb. Apply to damp hair and use a shower cap or steamy bathroom to help it penetrate.

**Q: Is this safe for color-treated hair?**
A: Absolutely. No sulfates or harsh detergents here. It's actually beneficial — the honey and oils help maintain moisture balance, which prevents color from looking dull or ashy.
