This is the conditioner that guys actually finish. Not because it's fancy — because it works without fuss.  

AMERICAN CREW's Daily Moisturizing Conditioner sits in that rare sweet spot: priced under $10, rated 4.7 stars across 235 Amazon reviews, and formulated for the guy who washes his hair daily but doesn't want straw by Wednesday. I tested it for two weeks on medium-texture, color-treated hair. Here's the honest breakdown.

**What's in the bottle**  
You get 8.4 fluid ounces in a sturdy pump bottle — the pump is the unsung hero here. It dispenses a perfect quarter-sized dollop without clogging or dripping. The formula is sulfate-free, paraben-free, and built around glycerin and pro-vitamin B5. No silicone-heavy coating, which matters if you care about buildup. The scent is cedar and citrus — clean, barbershop-adjacent, gone in five minutes.  

**Real-world performance**  
On normal-to-dry hair, this conditions like a pro. It detangles enough to run a comb through without ripping, and it rinses out completely — no slick film left behind. My hair dried softer than with drugstore 2-in-1s, and it stayed that way into the next morning. For daily users, that's the whole game.  

But here's the tradeoff: if your hair is thick, curly, or coarse, this won't cut it alone. The lightweight formula lacks the heavy butter or oil content that those textures need. One reviewer nailed it: *"Great for my fine hair, but my husband with 3B curls had to use double the amount and still needed his leave-in."* That's not a flaw — it's a targeting choice. Know your hair type before you buy.  

**What buyers consistently praise**  
Scrolling through the 235 ratings, three themes dominate:  

- **No residue.** A verified purchaser wrote: *"Finally a conditioner that doesn't make my hair feel greasy by noon. Washes clean, smells clean, done."*  
- **Daily-use friendly.** Multiple reviewers confirmed they use it every shower without buildup or limpness.  
- **Scent discipline.** People love that it doesn't linger like a cologne ad.  

**Where it falls short**  
- **Thick hair needs backup.** If you've got coarse, wavy, or curly hair, budget for a weekly deep conditioner. This is a maintenance product, not a repair product.  
- **Styling product buildup.** Because it's sulfate-free, it won't fully dissolve heavy clay or wax. You'll need a clarifying shampoo every 7–10 days if you're a pomade guy.  
- **No heat protection.** It says nothing about thermal defense, so if you blow-dry daily, pair it with a heat spray.  

**Who should buy this**  
- **The daily washer** with fine to medium hair who wants softness without grease.  
- **The gym-goer** who showers twice a day and needs something that rinses fast.  
- **The minimalist** who wants one conditioner that works for face-adjacent hair (beards, brows) without a 12-step routine.  

**Skip it if**  
- You have thick, coily, or severely damaged hair — you need a richer formula.  
- You're looking for a 2-in-1 shampoo-conditioner combo. This is conditioner only.  
- You prefer unscented products — the cedar note, while subtle, is definitely present.  

**FAQ**  

*Can I use this on a beard?*  
Yes, in a pinch. It softens beard hair without leaving it oily, but it's not a dedicated beard conditioner — don't expect serious taming for long beards.  

*Does it work on color-treated hair?*  
Yes. The sulfate-free formula is color-safe, and the pro-vitamin B5 helps maintain moisture. I noticed less fading on my treated ends.  

*How often should I use it?*  
Daily, if your hair tolerates it. For dry scalps, every other day is fine — it's gentle but not medicated.  

**The Verdict**  
**Buy it if** you have normal-to-dry, fine-to-medium hair and want a dependable, no-fuss daily conditioner under $10. **Skip it if** you need heavy-duty moisture for thick or damaged textures. At 4.7 stars and 235 ratings, it earns its place on any bathroom shelf.  

**Rating: 4.6/5** — knocked half a star for the limited texture range. Otherwise, this is the conditioner that makes daily washing feel like a choice, not a chore.
