Let’s be straight about this modelones 12-color set: it’s a mixed bag, but the good shades are *really* good. At a 4.1-star average across 204 ratings, buyers are mostly satisfied — and after examining the set closely, I get why. But you need to know which colors work before you buy, because not all twelve are created equal.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains twelve 8 mL bottles — smaller than the standard 15 mL you’re used to from OPI or Essie. That’s fine for trying new shades, but if you fall in love with a color, you’ll pan it fast. The set spans nudes, pinks, reds, a lavender, a sage green, a navy, and two shimmers. As shown in the product image, the range is thoughtfully curated — no random oranges or neon yellows cluttering the lineup.

## Performance and Build Quality

Here’s where the set splits. Six shades — the reds, navy, sage, and darker pinks — are outstanding. Two coats give full, even coverage. The formula is self-leveling, which means brush strokes disappear as it dries. That’s a premium behavior at a budget price.

The brush deserves special mention. It’s a wide, flat paddle with rounded edges that fans out nicely. One buyer noted, *“I could paint my entire nail in three strokes without flooding the cuticle — that never happens with cheap polishes.”* That tracks with my experience. The cap is ergonomic and gives decent control.

Wear time is respectable. With a top coat, expect 5–7 days before tip wear shows on natural nails. Without one, you’ll see chips around day 3–4. That’s standard for non-gel polish, but the darker shades wear better than the light ones.

## Where It Falls Short

The pale pink, lavender, white, and one shimmer are the problem children. They’re streaky and sheer. One reviewer put it plainly: *“The light pink needs three coats and still looks patchy. I was disappointed because the darker shades are beautiful.”* That’s the exact right summary.

The bottle size is the other real downside. At 8 mL, you’re getting 40% less product than standard bottles. If you’re someone who paints nails weekly, you’ll replace your favorite shades quickly.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you’re building your first polish collection, you love dark and saturated colors, or you want a budget-friendly set to test which families of shades suit your skin tone. The darker six shades alone justify the price.

**Skip it if** you primarily wear light, pastel, or nude shades — you’ll be frustrated by the patchy coverage. Also skip if you’re a heavy user who wants full-size bottles.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this gel polish or regular polish?**
A: Regular lacquer. It air-dries in 3–5 minutes per coat. No UV lamp needed.

**Q: How long does it last without chipping?**
A: With a top coat, 5–7 days on natural nails. Without, expect 3–4 days before chips appear.

**Q: Are the colors true to the product images?**
A: The darker shades match well. The light pink and lavender are slightly more muted in person than the swatch photos suggest.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

This is a 4.2-star set — not a 5-star one — because half the shades are excellent and half are mediocre. The good news? The excellent ones are the ones most people reach for daily. Just know what you’re getting before you add it to your cart.
