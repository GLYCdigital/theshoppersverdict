**What you're actually paying for**

Let's cut through the "luxury beauty" noise. At its price point, L'Oreal's Lineur Intense competes with prestige brands costing three times as much — and in some ways, it beats them. The Carbon Black shade is genuinely black, not the washed-out gray you get from cheaper felt tips. The pen delivers consistent pigment from the first stroke to the last, and the felt tip holds its shape remarkably well over weeks of daily use.

**What's in the box**

You get one eyeliner pen. That's it — no primer, no remover, no gimmicks. The pen itself is slim, lightweight, and comfortable to grip. The cap clicks securely, which matters more than you'd think; a loose cap dries out felt tips faster than anything else. The tip is a medium-fine felt nib — not as razor-thin as a brush liner, but precise enough for everyday wings and tightlining.

**Real-world performance**

Here's where this liner earns its keep. The pigment is intense enough that you don't need to layer strokes. One pass gives you full opacity, which means less tugging on delicate eyelid skin. The formula dries to a semi-matte finish that stays put through an 8-hour workday without flaking or crumbling into your eyes.

I tested this during a humid summer evening — the kind where most drugstore liners migrate by hour three. The Lineur Intense held its line. It's not waterproof (more on that below), but it's genuinely smudge-resistant once fully set.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across 225 Amazon ratings, the recurring themes are precision and value. One reviewer noted: *"I've used KVD and Stila, and honestly this performs just as well for a fraction of the price."* Another said: *"The tip doesn't dry out or fray like the last three liners I tried. I've had this for two months and it's still going strong."* Durability of the applicator is a common refrain — felt tips that soften or split are the #1 complaint in this category, and L'Oreal seems to have solved it.

**Where it falls short**

The honest tradeoffs: First, this is not a waterproof formula. If you cry, sweat heavily, or get caught in rain, it will run. Second, the set time is longer than advertised. You need about 60-90 seconds before opening your eyes fully, or you'll get transfer marks on your crease. Finally, the tip — while precise — isn't ultra-fine. If you prefer hair-thin micro-liners for graphic liner looks, this might feel slightly thick at the point.

**Who should buy this**

- **The everyday liner wearer** who wants reliable, opaque color without spending $30+ per tube
- **Beginners** who need a forgiving felt tip that doesn't drag or skip
- **Oily-lid sufferers** who've watched cheaper liners disappear by lunch

**Skip it if** you need a truly waterproof formula for swimming or tears, or if you demand surgical-precision micro tips for avant-garde looks.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does the tip last before drying out?**
A: With daily use and proper cap closure, most users report 2-3 months of consistent performance. The felt holds up better than sponge applicators, which tend to harden within weeks.

**Q: Does it work for tightlining (upper waterline)?**
A: Yes, but carefully. The tip is firm enough for precise placement, but it's not a gel pencil — you'll need a steady hand and a few practice sessions.

**Q: How does it compare to Stila Stay All Day?**
A: Stila has a slightly finer tip and faster set time. The Lineur Intense matches it on pigment intensity and longevity, but costs roughly half as much. For most users, the tradeoff is worth it.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want high-impact, long-wearing eyeliner without the prestige markup.

**Skip it if** you're a swimmer, a crier, or someone who needs a true waterproof formula.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — An exceptional value that punches well above its price class. The minor set-time quirk and lack of waterproofing are the only things keeping it from a perfect score.
