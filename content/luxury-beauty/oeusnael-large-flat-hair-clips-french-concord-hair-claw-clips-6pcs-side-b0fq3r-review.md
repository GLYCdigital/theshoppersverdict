## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The glossy surface finish, while attractive, is slick on fine or freshly-washed hair. If your hair is silky and prone to slipping, you may need to re-position the clip a few times before it settles. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing.

The medium clips are smaller than the product photos suggest. At roughly 2.5 inches across, they're better suited for half-up styles or securing sections, not full updos. If you have very long or voluminous hair, stick to the large ones and treat the mediums as backups.

One more note: the spring tension is strong. If you have arthritis or hand weakness, the larger clips require a firm squeeze to open fully. It's a tradeoff for the secure hold.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy if:** You have medium-to-thick hair and want clips that actually hold all day. You value low-profile design that works under helmets, hats, or just looks clean from the side. You want a versatile set with both sizes and neutral colors.

**Skip if:** You have extremely fine, baby-smooth hair that causes every clip to slide. You need one specific size — the mixed set means some clips may go unused. Your hands lack grip strength to operate high-tension springs.
