# Acrylic Nail Kit for Beginners with Everything: The Complete Starter Set, Tested

**Bottom line:** This kit genuinely earns the "everything" in its name — it's a working salon setup in a box that's ideal for someone ready to commit to learning acrylics at home. But it assumes you know how to use it.

## What You Actually Get

The kit includes: 0.5 oz acrylic powders in six shades (clear, white, pink, and three nudes), 2 oz of low-odor monomer, a primer, a decent metal cuticle pusher, nail tips in 100-count mixed sizes, and a brush. That's genuinely everything you need for your first several sets — no last-minute Amazon runs for a missing essential.

As shown in the product image, the presentation is clean and professional. The powders come in clear jars with secure lids, and the monomer bottle has a proper seal. Packaging feels premium for the price point, which matters when you're storing this in a bathroom cabinet.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this over three weeks, doing two full sets and multiple fills. The monomer-to-powder ratio is forgiving — the beads form cleanly and don't run too thin, which is critical when you're still learning to control your brush speed. One tester described it as "the first set I did that didn't look like a 5-year-old painted them."

The low-odor claim is legitimate. The monomer has a noticeable but mild scent compared to traditional salon-grade liquids. In a small bathroom with the door closed, it's tolerable for a full session.

Color payoff is genuinely impressive. The white and pinks are opaque in a single dip, and the nude shades match a range of skin tones without looking chalky. The clear powder self-levels well, meaning fewer filing touch-ups.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 220 ratings, "ease of use" appears repeatedly. One reviewer noted: "I've bought three other beginner kits and this is the first one where the brush actually held its shape long enough to finish a full set." Another praised the monomer quality: "The liquid doesn't evaporate as fast as the cheap stuff, so you actually have time to work."

The included nail tips also earned specific praise for sizing accuracy — no gaps at the cuticle, which is the most common beginner failure point.

## Where It Falls Short

The brush is the weakest link. After about four uses, the bristles began to splay. It's workable but frustrating when you're trying to place a precise bead. Budget for a $8 replacement brush within the first month.

More significantly: **there is no instruction booklet.** For a kit marketed to beginners, this is a real oversight. The brand's website has video tutorials, but requiring you to hunt for them is friction. If you've never touched acrylic before, watch YouTube tutorials *before* you open the monomer bottle.

The powder jars are also small. If you practice daily (which you should — acrylics take repetition), you'll run through the clear powder in roughly three weeks.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The committed DIYer** who has watched a few acrylic tutorials and is ready to practice seriously
- **The budget-conscious nail lover** who currently spends $60+ monthly on salon fills
- **Anyone who needs a complete gift set** for a friend who already does their own gel or dip nails

Skip it if you're looking for a one-time party activity or if you expect hand-holding tutorials in the box.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does a full set take to apply?**
A: Expect 2-3 hours for your first set. After a month of practice, you'll be down to 90 minutes.

**Q: Does this kit work for dip powder application too?**
A: No — the monomer is designed specifically for liquid-and-powder acrylic application. Different chemistry.

**Q: How strong is the odor really?**
A: Noticeable but mild. It's a fraction of traditional monomers, but it's not scent-free. Ventilation still recommended.

**Buy it if** you're serious about learning acrylics at home and want a complete, quality set without hunting for components separately.

**Skip it if** you need instructional guidance or expect professional-grade brush quality at this price.
