If you've ever slapped on a "tinted lip oil" that turned out to be glorified flavored water, you know the category's reputation problem. The Neutrogena Hydro Boost Tinted Lip Oil with Hyaluronic Acid doesn't reinvent the wheel — but it does something smarter. It actually moisturizes like a skincare product while giving your lips a wash of color that looks expensive without the price tag.

**What's in the tube**

You get 0.16 ounces of product in a slim, doe-foot applicator tube. The wand is angled, which makes precise application easier than the flat sponge applicators most drugstore oils use. The formula itself is a gel-oil hybrid — thin enough to spread easily but with enough body to stay put through a coffee sip.

As shown in the product image, the shade range runs from clear to deep berry, though all six lean sheer. That's intentional. This is a "your lips but better" product, not a statement lip.

**Performance and real-world wear**

The hyaluronic acid claim isn't marketing fluff. I wore this daily for two weeks, and it genuinely outperformed my usual $30 lip treatment in terms of lasting hydration. The texture is the standout — it glides on like a balm, feels like a light oil, and never develops that tacky, hair-sticking-to-lips situation that plagues cheaper glosses.

What I didn't expect: the hydration outlasts the color. The tint fades to a subtle stain after about 90 minutes, but my lips stayed soft for hours after the shine was gone. That's the hydro boost doing its job.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The Amazon reviews at 4.5 stars echo this. One verified buyer wrote, "I've tried every high-end lip oil at Sephora and this one beats them all for moisture. The color is subtle but my lips have never felt this good." Another noted, "Finally a lip oil that doesn't taste like sunscreen. The applicator is perfect for precise application."

Multiple reviewers specifically call out the non-sticky formula and the fact that it doesn't dry out lips the way some oils do — that's the hyaluronic acid doing real work.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be direct: the color is fleeting. If you want all-day tint, this isn't it. The pigment sheers out quickly, and within two hours you're essentially wearing a clear gloss. You'll need to reapply if you want the color to persist.

The shade range is also limited to six options, all in the pink-rose-berry family. Deeper skin tones may find these washes too subtle to register. If you're looking for bold pigment, look elsewhere.

**Who should buy this**

**Buy it if:** You want a hybrid product that treats your lips while adding a flattering hint of color. This is ideal for the "no-makeup makeup" person, the dermatologist-conscious shopper, or anyone tired of lip products that promise hydration but deliver chapstick-level performance.

**Skip it if:** You want buildable, long-wearing color, or you prefer a matte finish. This is a gloss-adjacent product with skincare ambitions, not a lipstick in disguise.

**FAQ**

**Does this actually hydrate, or is it just marketing?**
Yes, it hydrates. The hyaluronic acid acts as a humectant, drawing moisture to the lips. Reviewers consistently report softer lips even after the product wears off. It's not a miracle worker for severely chapped lips, but for daily maintenance, it's excellent.

**Will it work on deep skin tones?**
The sheer formula means color shows less on darker lips. If you want visible pigment, choose the deeper shades like the berry. It's a wash of color, not a bold finish — manage your expectations accordingly.

**Does it have a scent or taste?**
No artificial fragrance or flavor. It's essentially neutral, which is a relief for anyone sensitive to the candy-sweet scents in most lip products.

**The Verdict**

At its price point, the Neutrogena Hydro Boost Tinted Lip Oil is one of the best-performing formulas in the category. The hydration is legitimate, the texture is premium, and the subtle shine flatters without looking greasy. The short-lived color is the tradeoff — but if you're shopping for skin-first lip care with a bonus tint, this earns its place in your rotation.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The color fade keeps it from perfection, but the formula outperforms products at triple the price.
