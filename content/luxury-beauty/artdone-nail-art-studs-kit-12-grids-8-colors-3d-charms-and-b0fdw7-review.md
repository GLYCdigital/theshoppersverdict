If you've ever bought a "sampler" nail charm set and found 90% of it unusable, you know the pain. The Artdone Nail Art Studs Kit takes a different approach: instead of one jumbled bag, you get 12 individual grid cases, each holding a distinct color family of 3D charms, studs, and rhinestones. The 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews suggests it's doing something right — and after digging into the specifics, most of that praise is earned.

**What you actually get**

Twelve clear, hinged grid cases. Each one contains a specific color story — golds, silvers, iridescent pearls, rose golds, blacks, neons, and several multi-toned mixes. Within each grid, you'll find a blend of shapes: round rhinestones, pointed studs, teardrops, heart shapes, and small 3D charms. The cases themselves are the standout feature — they snap shut securely, stack neatly, and let you see everything at a glance. No more dumping a hundred tiny pieces onto your desk and praying.

As shown in the product image, the color separation is deliberate. You're not getting a rainbow dump — you're getting coordinated sets. That matters when you're matching a specific manicure.

**Performance and real-world use**

The charms are small — mostly 2-3mm — which is perfect for subtle accent nails or filling gaps in a gradient design. Application is straightforward with nail glue or gel topcoat; the flat backs sit flush against the nail surface. Several reviewers noted the adhesion surprised them. One wrote, *"I used these under a gel topcoat and they didn't budge for two weeks. I was honestly expecting a few to pop off by day three."* Another confirmed, *"The gold ones look expensive — not cheap plastic-y at all."*

The 3D charms — the slightly raised pieces — do require a thicker topcoat layer to fully encase, otherwise they can catch on hair and clothing. That's true of any 3D nail art, but worth knowing before you apply.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest complaint across reviews: the assortment isn't truly random. One buyer noted, *"I got a grid that was almost all teardrops in different shades. I wanted variety, not 30 of the same shape."* This seems to vary by batch, so you're rolling the dice on shape distribution.

The size is also a limitation. If you want a single large statement charm for an accent nail, this kit won't deliver. These are accent and filler pieces, not centerpieces.

**Who should buy this**

- **Home manicurists** who want a versatile stash without committing to 12 full-size packs
- **Nail techs** who need a portable, organized option for client touch-ups — the grid cases are genuinely travel-friendly
- **Beginners** learning placement and adhesion — you won't waste money on a huge set while you're still practicing
- **Anyone who hates disorganized craft supplies** — this is the most organized charm kit I've seen at this price point

**Skip it if** you need large, dramatic charms, or if you're particular about having exact quantities of specific shapes. The unpredictability of the assortment will frustrate you.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do these work with regular nail polish, or only gel?**
A: Both, but gel is better. With regular polish, apply a topcoat, let it dry slightly tacky, press the charm in, then seal with another topcoat. Gel gives you a stronger bond and smoother finish.

**Q: How many total pieces are in the kit?**
A: Artdone lists it as "12 grids, 8 colors" — most reviewers estimate 300-400 pieces total. Exact counts vary by batch, so don't expect a precise number.

**Q: Are the charms reusable?**
A: No. Once you set them in topcoat, they're done. Trying to remove and reapply them usually damages the charm's finish.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want an organized, versatile charm assortment that actually looks professional on the nail. The grid cases alone justify the purchase — the color coordination and adhesion quality are bonuses.

**Skip it if** you need large statement charms or exact shape quantities. The unpredictable assortment and small sizing will leave you wanting.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a genuinely useful kit for its intended purpose, held back only by inconsistent shape distribution and small piece sizes.
