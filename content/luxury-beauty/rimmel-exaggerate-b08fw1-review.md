## The $5 Lip Liner That Thinks It's Luxury

Here's the thing about drugstore lip liners: most are either chalky dry messes or greasy pencils that bleed into every fine line within an hour. The Rimmel Exaggerate sits in a rare middle ground — a budget pencil that performs like something triple the price. With 225 Amazon ratings averaging 4.5 stars, it's clearly earning its keep in makeup bags. But is it actually worth the hype? I dug into the formula, the shade range, and real customer feedback to give you the straight story.

## What You Actually Get

The Exaggerate is a classic wooden pencil liner — not a twist-up — with a small sharpener built into the base cap. That's a genuinely thoughtful touch because wooden pencils are notorious for becoming useless once the tip dulls. You get 0.01 ounces of product, which sounds tiny but lasts a surprisingly long time since you're only lining lips. The pencil itself has a slightly waxy, creamy texture that applies smoothly without tugging at the delicate skin around your mouth.

## Performance & Real-World Wear

The formula is where Exaggerate separates itself from the drugstore pack. It glides on with serious pigment payoff in a single pass — no building required. The finish dries down to a matte, skin-like texture that doesn't emphasize lip lines or settle into cracks. I tested it through coffee, lunch, and a full workday: it held up for about six hours before needing a touch-up, with zero feathering or bleeding into the skin around the lips.

That said, it's not bulletproof. Oily meals will break it down faster than dry snacks, and you'll definitely want to reapply after eating. But for a liner at this price point, the staying power is genuinely impressive.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

Scrolling through the reviews, a few themes come up again and again. One buyer put it simply: "This is the only lip liner I've repurchased five times. The shade 'East End Snob' is my perfect nude." Multiple reviewers mention the creamy formula as the standout feature — it's forgiving enough for shaky hands but precise enough for a sharp cupid's bow.

The shade range also gets consistent love. With 12 options from barely-there nudes to deep berries, there's something for most skin tones. The warm neutrals like "Tiramisu" and "Addiction" are particular favorites for everyday wear.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the shade range skews noticeably warm. If you have cool or neutral undertones, you'll find most options pull orange or yellow on your skin. The deeper shades also lean warm, so fair-to-medium cool-toned users may struggle to find a true match.

Second, the wooden pencil design means you're sharpening constantly — and each sharpening eats product. The built-in sharpener helps, but anyone who's used a twist-up liner will find the waste annoying. One reviewer noted, "I love the formula but I'm going through pencils twice as fast as my old twist-up liner."

Finally, the matte finish, while flattering, isn't hydrating. If you have chronically dry lips, you'll want to layer a balm underneath or skip this liner entirely.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want a high-pigment, long-wearing liner that won't break the bank. You prefer a true matte finish and don't mind the occasional sharpening. You have warm or neutral undertones and want a reliable nude or berry shade.

**Skip it if:** You're cool-toned and need a liner that matches your undertone. You hate wooden pencils and prefer twist-up convenience. You have very dry lips that need constant hydration.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this liner actually stay put through eating?**
A: Through drinks and light snacks, yes — expect 4-6 hours of wear. Oily meals will break it down faster, so plan for a touch-up after lunch.

**Q: Is the built-in sharpener any good?**
A: It works, but it's basic. It does the job without destroying the pencil, just don't expect precision sharpening. For a truly fine tip, use a high-quality separate sharpener.

**Q: Can I use this as a full lip color?**
A: Technically yes, but it's not ideal. The formula is designed for lining and will feel drying if applied all over. For full coverage, layer a gloss or balm on top.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The Rimmel Exaggerate delivers luxury-level pigment and staying power at a drugstore price. It's not perfect — the warm-leaning shade range and constant sharpening are real drawbacks — but the formula genuinely outperforms liners costing three times as much.

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, long-wearing liner with serious pigmentation and don't mind the maintenance. **Skip it if** you're cool-toned, hate wooden pencils, or need a hydrating formula.

For the price, this is one of the best budget lip liners on Amazon. Just sharpen wisely and pick your shade carefully.
