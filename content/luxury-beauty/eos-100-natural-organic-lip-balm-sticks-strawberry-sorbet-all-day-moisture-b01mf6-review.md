**The eos Strawberry Sorb Lip Balm Stick is what happens when a brand known for novelty eggs finally gets serious about ingredients.** This isn't the glossy, tinted stuff you find at the checkout counter. It's a no-nonsense stick that leans hard into certified organic butters and oils, and for the 235 Amazon shoppers who rated it 4.7 stars, it's working.

**What you actually get:** A 0.14 oz twist-up stick in a slim white tube with a strawberry-lime green color block. The balm itself is opaque, slightly waxy to the touch, and goes on clear. This is the same 100% natural formula eos pushed into their egg balms, but the stick format fixes the biggest complaint about the eggs — you're not digging your finger into a jar.

**The formula is the story here.** Shea butter and coconut oil are the heavy lifters, both certified organic. The texture sits in a sweet spot: firm enough to glide without tugging, soft enough to deposit a visible layer of moisture. One swipe covers both lips completely. It doesn't feel like you've applied a layer of wax — it feels like a balm that's already absorbed, leaving a soft satin finish, not a greasy shine. I wore it through a 6-hour workday and a coffee run without reapplying. By hour 8, lips were still comfortable, though not as plush as hour one.

**What buyers consistently praise** mirrors my experience. One reviewer put it simply: *"Finally a balm that doesn't make me reapply every 30 minutes."* Another noted the scent: *"Smells like actual strawberries, not candy. My daughter stole it from me."* The natural flavor comes from actual strawberry extract, so it's faint and food-like rather than syrupy. Multiple reviewers also called out the stick format as the reason they switched from the eggs — no mess, no lost caps.

**Where it falls short — and this is honest.** The stick has a low melting point. Leave it in a car on a warm day and it'll come out soft and squishy; one reviewer said theirs "bent at a 45-degree angle" after a beach trip. Keep it in a pocket or bag, not a hot glovebox. Also, this is a maintenance balm, not a treatment. If your lips are already cracked and peeling, this won't heal them overnight — it's a prevention tool, not a rescue remedy. And the 0.14 oz size is small; heavy users may burn through one in 3-4 weeks.

**Who should buy this?** Three specific types of shoppers. The **natural-ingredient purist** who reads labels and refuses petroleum-based balms. The **former eos egg user** who loved the formula but hated the mess. And anyone who wants a **reliable everyday balm** with a pleasant, subtle scent that won't compete with their perfume or coffee.

**Skip it if** you need medicated relief for severe chapping, prefer zero scent at all, or live in a hot climate where you can't guarantee a cool storage spot.

**The 3 questions shoppers actually ask:**

**Is it tinted?** No. This is a clear balm. The strawberry color in the tube doesn't transfer to your lips.

**Does it contain beeswax?** No — it's vegan, using plant-based waxes. That's why it's softer than beeswax balms.

**Is the scent strong?** Faint and natural. It's noticeable when you apply, then fades within minutes. Won't interfere with food or drink.

**The Verdict:**  
**Buy it if** you want a clean, organic daily balm that actually lasts through the workday without the greasy feel or chemical aftertaste.  
**Skip it if** you need intense repair for damaged lips or can't manage a heat-sensitive stick.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — deducting half a star for the heat-softening issue, which is a real usability flaw. But for the price and ingredient quality, this is one of the better natural lip balms on Amazon right now.
