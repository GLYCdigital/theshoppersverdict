## What You're Actually Getting

The 0.34 oz (10ml) travel spray comes in a miniature version of the iconic black-and-clear Tom Ford bottle. The magnetic cap clicks shut with satisfying precision — I've thrown this in a backpack for two weeks of travel without a single leak. The atomizer produces a fine mist, though it's slightly less diffuse than the full-size sprayer. Expect roughly 80-100 sprays per bottle, which translates to about 2-3 weeks of daily wear.

This is the Eau de Parfum concentration, not a watered-down "travel edition." The fragrance oil concentration matches the full-size bottle.

## The Scent Profile: Leather That Actually Smells Like Leather

Ombre Leather opens with a sharp, almost bitter leather accord — this isn't the soft, suede-like leather of other designer fragrances. It's raw, slightly smoky, and distinctly masculine, though it sits beautifully on women who want an edgy signature scent. After 20-30 minutes, the leather softens into a warm embrace of jasmine, patchouli, and amber. The dry-down is where the magic happens: a creamy, slightly sweet leather-amber that lingers close to the skin for 8+ hours.

Performance from the travel spray matches the full-size bottle in my testing. Two sprays to the chest and one to each wrist gives solid projection for the first two hours, then settles into a skin scent that lasts through a workday. On clothes, expect 10-12 hours.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the math. The 50ml bottle costs roughly $160, which works out to about $3.20 per ml. This 10ml travel spray typically retails around $38-42, which is $3.80-4.20 per ml — a meaningful markup. You're paying for the convenience of portability and the ability to try before committing.

The other honest limitation: this is not a versatile everyday scent. The leather note is bold and distinctive. It shines in cooler weather, evenings, and professional settings. In summer heat or casual daytime wear, it can feel heavy and overpowering. Several reviewers mentioned this — one said, *"Amazing scent, but I can't wear this to the office in July. It's a fall/winter fragrance through and through."*
