The Braun M60 Mobile Shaver is the kind of product you don't think about until you desperately need it. That 6 AM flight, the overnight bag you packed in a hurry, the desk drawer at work where you keep emergency supplies. It's the grooming equivalent of a Swiss Army knife — not your primary tool, but the one that saves you when nothing else works.

As shown in the product image, this is a strikingly minimal piece of hardware. The stainless steel casing gives it a premium weight that immediately separates it from the plasticky disposable travel shavers crowding the market. It's roughly the size of a thick marker — small enough to disappear into a glove compartment, substantial enough that it doesn't feel like a toy.

**What's actually in the box**

This is where you need to pay attention. Braun ships the M60 without a battery. You'll need to source a single AAA before first use. That's a minor annoyance, but once you've got one installed, you're set for months — the motor is surprisingly efficient, and most users report 2-3 months of daily touch-ups on one cell.

The shaver itself features a single foil head with Braun's signature ultra-thin foil. There's no trimmer, no attachments, no cleaning station. Just the shaver, a protective cap, and a promise that less is more. For the price point, that's acceptable — but only if you know what you're getting.

**Performance and build quality**

The stainless steel construction isn't just for looks. It's genuinely rugged. One Amazon reviewer noted, "I've dropped this thing on tile floors at least five times and it still works perfectly. My full-size shaver wouldn't survive that." That durability matters for a product designed to live in bags and get knocked around.

In terms of actual shaving, the M60 excels at what it's designed for: quick maintenance on 1-3 day growth. The foil head glides smoothly over skin without the tugging you'd expect from something this small. It's not going to give you a barber-grade close shave — that's simply not what this is built for — but it will keep you presentable through a business trip or a weekend away.

Where it falls short is longer hair. Without a trimmer attachment, anything past three days of growth will clog the foil and require frequent cleaning. And because the head is compact, covering a full face takes patience. One reviewer put it plainly: "It's fine for a touch-up, but I wouldn't want to shave my entire face with this every day."

**Consistent praise and honest criticism**

Buyers consistently highlight three things: the premium feel of the stainless steel body, the genuine portability (no cords, no charging dock), and the reliable performance for quick touch-ups. The 4.2-star average across 210 ratings reflects a product that does exactly what it promises.

The criticisms are equally consistent. The missing battery is the top complaint — several reviewers were caught off guard. The lack of a trimmer attachment limits versatility. And the small head means it's not a replacement for your primary shaver, no matter how much you might want it to be.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the frequent traveler who needs a reliable backup. The desk worker who wants a discreet touch-up option. The minimalist who values a single-purpose tool that does one thing exceptionally well. It's also a thoughtful gift for someone who's always on the move.

Skip it if you're looking for a primary shaver, or if you maintain a beard that needs regular trimming. This is a specialist tool, not a generalist.

**FAQ**

*Can you use this in the shower?* No. The M60 is not waterproof. Keep it dry.

*How long does the battery last?* Most users report 2-3 months of daily touch-ups on a single AAA battery, depending on usage.

*Does it come with a cleaning brush?* Yes, a small brush is included for maintaining the foil head.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need a rugged, dependable travel shaver that disappears into any bag and delivers consistent touch-up performance.

**Skip it if:** You expect a full shave on the go, or you don't want to deal with sourcing batteries.

The Braun M60 earns its 4.2 stars. It's not flashy, not feature-packed, and not a full-face solution — but for its intended purpose, it's one of the best-engineered compact shavers available. Pack one and forget about it. That's exactly what it's designed for.
