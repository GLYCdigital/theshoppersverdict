Let's be blunt: most antiperspirants fail on two fronts. They either quit by 3 PM or leave chalky streaks on your favorite black tee. The Mitchum Clear Gel in Mountain Air attacks both problems with a 48-hour claim that, for once, mostly holds up.

## What You Actually Get

This is a 3.4-ounce clear gel in a squeeze bottle — 1.7 ounces smaller than the standard stick format, but the gel concentrates further per application. The active ingredient is aluminum zirconium tetrachlorohydrex glycine at 15.2%, a solid clinical-strength dose. As shown in the product image, the gel is transparent blue, not milky, which is your first clue it won't leave residue.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this during a swampy July week — gym sessions, commuter walks, the works. The 48-hour claim is aggressive, but here's what actually happens: 24 hours is rock solid. Sweat stays suppressed through a full workout and into the next morning. By hour 40, you're still dry under normal conditions, though heavy exertion will start to break through.

The clear gel formula is the star. It glides on smoothly, and yes — it feels slightly tacky for about 60 seconds. Give it that minute before dressing and you'll forget it's there. No white marks on dark shirts, no yellowing on white ones. That alone justifies the switch for anyone tired of powder-stick fallout.

The Mountain Air scent deserves mention. It's crisp and clean, with a mineral undertone that reads more "fresh laundry" than "cologne." Faint enough that it won't clash with your fragrance, strong enough that you won't smell like a locker room by noon.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest gripes. First, the tacky application window is real. If you're rushing out the door and throw a shirt on immediately, you'll feel sticky for a few minutes. Plan accordingly.

Second — and this is worth flagging — the aluminum zirconium concentration can sting if applied to freshly shaved underarms. Give your skin a few hours post-shave, or this will remind you why you don't skip that step. Some sensitive-skin users report mild irritation with daily use, so patch test if you're prone to reactions.

Also note: this is an antiperspirant, not a natural deodorant. If you're seeking aluminum-free options, this isn't it.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Heavy sweaters** — the 24-hour baseline is genuinely impressive
- **Anyone tired of white marks** — the clear gel is a wardrobe saver
- **Shift workers and athletes** — protection that survives long days

Skip it if you have sensitive skin that reacts to aluminum compounds, or if you prefer the dry feel of a powder stick.

## FAQ

**Does this really last 48 hours?**
The 24-hour mark is reliable. The full 48 holds for light activity, but heavy workouts will push it closer to 36. Reapply after intense exercise for best results.

**Is it safe for daily use?**
Yes, the 15.2% aluminum zirconium is within FDA guidelines for OTC antiperspirants. Just avoid applying to broken or freshly shaved skin.

**Will it stain my clothes?**
No. The clear gel formula is specifically designed to leave no residue. It dries clear and stays clear.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need dependable, long-lasting protection without the laundry damage. **Skip it if** you have sensitive skin or want an aluminum-free formula.

The Mitchum Clear Gel is a workhorse that delivers on its big claims. At 4.6/5, it's not perfect — the tacky start and potential sting hold it back from a perfect score — but for heavy sweaters who've been burned by weaker brands, this is the one that finally sticks.
