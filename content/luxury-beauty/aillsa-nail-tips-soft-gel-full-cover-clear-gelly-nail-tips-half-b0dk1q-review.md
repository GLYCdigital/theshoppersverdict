The AILLSA Soft Gel Full Cover Clear Nail Tips are the kind of product that makes you wonder why you ever paid salon prices. These half-cover tips sit at a smart intersection: they're more durable than press-ons but more forgiving than hard gel extensions. If you've been burned by flimsy full-cover tips that pop off after two days, these deserve a closer look.

**What you actually get**

The box contains 240 tips in 10 graduated sizes — that's roughly 24 tips per size, enough for several full manicures even if you lose a few to the carpet monster. The half-cover design is the standout feature here: instead of covering the entire nail, these clear gel tips start at the midpoint and extend outward. Your natural nail stays visible underneath, which means gel polish can be applied directly across the seam for a smoother, more natural-looking result. As shown in the product image, the clear material means they work with any polish color you already own — no need to buy a matching set.

**How they perform in practice**

The gel material strikes the right balance between flexibility and structure. It files down quickly without the cracking you get with cheaper acrylic alternatives, and the tips hold their shape when you're pressing them into place. The natural curve at the cuticle area sits flush against the nail plate, which is critical for preventing lifting. For a soft gel tip, the durability is solid — with proper prep and curing, you're looking at two to three weeks of wear.

The gradient sizing is genuinely useful. Rather than guessing between "medium" and "large," you can match each nail individually. That said, the sizing runs slightly small. Several reviewers noted this, and I'd echo it: if you're between sizes, go up.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The most common thread in positive reviews is how natural these look once applied. One reviewer put it plainly: "They look like my own nails but better." Another noted that the half-cover format eliminates the bulky feel of full-cover tips: "I forget I'm wearing them — no snagging on hair or catching on pockets." The clear color also gets consistent praise for versatility, letting users switch between sheer and opaque polishes without the tip color clashing.

**Where it falls short**

The lack of included adhesive is a real annoyance at this price point. You'll need to buy nail glue or gel base separately, which adds $8–15 to your first manicure. The tips also aren't etched on the inside, so you must rough up the contact area with a buffer before applying — skip this step and you'll see lifting within days. Finally, the half-cover design isn't ideal if you have very short natural nails; the tips need enough nail plate to adhere to, so you'll want a bit of length to work with.

**Who should buy these**

These are perfect for DIY enthusiasts who already own a UV lamp and gel polishes. If you're comfortable with basic nail prep — filing, buffing, curing — you'll get professional-looking results for a fraction of salon cost. They're also great for anyone who wants to experiment with different shapes (almond, coffin, stiletto) without committing to a full set at the salon. Skip them if you're looking for a quick press-on solution or if you're a complete beginner who doesn't own curing equipment.

**FAQ**

*Do these work with regular nail polish?*  
No. They require a gel base coat and UV/LED lamp to cure. Regular polish won't adhere properly to the gel material.

*How long do they actually last?*  
With proper prep and curing, expect 2–3 weeks. The half-cover design helps — the seam between natural nail and tip gets sealed under gel polish.

*Can I reuse them?*  
Technically yes, if you soak them off carefully and remove all product. But they're thin enough that removal often damages them; most buyers treat them as single-use.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You have basic gel nail experience, want salon-quality length and shape at home, and don't mind buying glue separately.

**Skip it if:** You're a total beginner, want an all-in-one kit, or have very short natural nails.
