## Where It Falls Short

Be honest with yourself about your hair type. If your hair is fine, thin, or slippery, the strong spring tension might be overwhelming — these claws can pull or feel heavy on delicate hair. That's not a defect; it's a mismatch between product design and hair type.

The enamel coating is another consideration. It's durable for daily wear, but it *will* chip if you drop these on tile or concrete. Treat them like accessories, not indestructible tools, and they'll last.

Finally, the "cute" factor is subjective. The tulip design is charming, but it's also specific. If you prefer minimalist or sleek hair accessories, these might feel too ornamental for your everyday rotation.
