## Performance: Does It Actually Work?

Yes — and this is where Gellen earns its keep. The formula is pure acetone with added moisturizers (glycerin and aloe extract), which is the right approach for gel removal. In testing, I found it breaks down Gellen's own cured polish in about 10-12 minutes when wrapped properly. For tougher brands like Gelish or CND Shellac, expect 15 minutes.

The key is technique. Soak a cotton pad thoroughly, press it against the nail, wrap in foil, and wait. The acetone does the heavy lifting — you shouldn't need to scrape aggressively. Also, don't expect this to work miracles on builder gel or polygel — those require filing down first regardless of remover strength. The product is designed for standard gel polish, and it sticks to that lane.
