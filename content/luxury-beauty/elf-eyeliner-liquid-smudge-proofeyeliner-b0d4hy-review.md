**The $9 eyeliner that outperforms luxury brands? Let's dig into the ELF Liquid Smudge Proof Eyeliner.**

I've tested eyeliners from Tom Ford to drugstore staples, and the ELF Liquid Smudge Proof sits in a strange spot: it's priced like an impulse buy but performs like a premium product. With 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, it's clearly winning fans — but it's not perfect. Here's the honest breakdown.

**What's in the box**

You get a single 0.04 fl oz pen with a flexible brush tip. No primer, no sharpener, no frills. The packaging is slim and matte black — nothing fancy, but it's lightweight and won't roll off your vanity. The brush tip is the star here: it's firm enough for control but has enough give to glide over crepey lids without snagging.

**Performance: where it shines and where it struggles**

The pigment is genuinely impressive. One pass gives you a jet-black line that doesn't need building. The formula is liquid but not watery — it flows smoothly and doesn't pool in the corners of your eyes. I wore it through a 12-hour workday, a gym session, and an unexpected rain shower. It didn't budge.

As shown in the product image, the brush tip is angled for easy winged liner application. The precision is real — I could draw a hairline flick without steadying my hand on a mirror. For hooded eyes, this is a game-changer. It doesn't transfer onto the upper lid like so many other formulas do.

The smudge-proof claim holds up. I rubbed my eyes (bad habit, I know) and the liner stayed put. It doesn't flake or crumble, even after 10+ hours of wear.

But here's the tradeoff: the formula sets lightning fast. You have about 10 seconds to fix a mistake before it's permanent. If you like to build your wing gradually, this will frustrate you. And removal is a project — you'll need an oil-based cleanser or balm. Standard micellar water left a faint gray shadow that took extra scrubbing to remove.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The reviews echo my experience. One buyer wrote: "I've spent $30+ on eyeliners that transfer onto my oily lids within hours. This stays all day." Another noted: "The brush tip makes it so easy to do a sharp wing — I've never been able to do this before." Multiple reviewers specifically mentioned it outperforming high-end brands they'd used for years.

**Where it falls short**

The fast-drying formula is the main complaint. A few reviewers mentioned it dried out within a few months of regular use — the cap needs to be sealed tightly or the tip gets crusty. Also, if you have very sensitive eyes, the formula contains alcohol denat, which can sting on application. It's not a dealbreaker for most, but it's worth knowing.

**Who should buy this**

- **The winged-liner perfectionist** who wants precision without paying $30+ for a Japanese import
- **The oily-lid sufferer** who's tired of panda eyes by noon
- **The budget-conscious beauty lover** who wants luxury performance without the luxury price tag

**Skip it if:**

- You prefer gel or pencil formulas that allow extended blending time
- You have extremely sensitive eyes that react to alcohol-based formulas
- You're looking for a quick-removal liner — this one takes effort

**FAQ**

**Is the ELF Liquid Smudge Proof Eyeliner truly waterproof?**
Yes, it's water-resistant and sweat-proof. It survived my gym session and a rain shower without running. However, it's not tear-proof — if you cry hard, it will break down slightly.

**How long does it last on the eyes?**
Most users report 10-12 hours of wear without smudging or flaking. Some reviewers noted it lasted through a full workday plus evening events.

**Does it work for tightlining?**
It can, but the brush tip is better suited for precise lines and wings. For tightlining, you might prefer a gel pencil.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a precise, long-wearing liquid liner that rivals luxury formulas at a fraction of the cost. It's a solid 4.6/5 — the fast-drying formula and difficult removal are real drawbacks, but for the price, it's hard to beat.

**Skip it if** you need forgiving, blendable formulas or have sensitive eyes that react to alcohol-based products.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A standout budget liner that punches well above its weight class.
