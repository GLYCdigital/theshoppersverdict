**The $8 primer that makes foundation actually stay put — with a hydration kick that surprised me**

Let me be upfront: I've tested grip primers from Milk Makeup, NYX, and e.l.f., and I bought the essence Jelly Grip Hydrating Primer expecting another "just okay" budget option. What I found instead is a genuinely effective hydrating grip that outperforms primers at triple the price. It's not perfect — nothing at this price point is — but for most skin types, this is the one to grab.

**What's actually in the tube**

You get 1.01 fluid ounces of translucent, jelly-textured primer with a light gel consistency that spreads like a dream. The star ingredients are glycerin and aloe vera leaf juice — both humectants that pull moisture into the skin rather than sitting on top. There's no silicone in the formula, which is a big deal if your skin breaks out from dimethicone-heavy primers. It's also fragrance-free and vegan, which the brand certifies.

The product image shows exactly what you're getting: a clear gel that looks like aloe gel but feels silkier. A pea-sized amount covers your entire face — this tube will last you 4-5 months with daily use.

**How it performs in real life**

I wore this under a matte foundation during a humid 90-degree day, and here's the honest breakdown:

- **Grip:** My foundation didn't budge for 8 hours. No sliding, no patchiness around my nose. One reviewer said it best: *"This is the first primer that actually keeps my makeup on during my 12-hour nursing shifts. I was skeptical because of the price, but it outperforms my $30 primer."*
- **Hydration:** My skin felt bouncy and plump when I applied it, and my foundation didn't look cakey by hour six. If you have dry patches, this helps — it doesn't cure them, but it smooths them enough that foundation won't cling.
- **Texture:** It dries down slightly tacky, which is exactly what you want from a grip primer. But give it 60 seconds before applying foundation, or you'll drag your makeup instead of laying it down.

**Where it falls short**

The jelly texture is a double-edged sword. Apply too much — more than a pea-sized amount — and it pills into little white balls under foundation. You also can't layer it over a thick, heavy moisturizer. Lightweight skincare only.

It's also not a pore-blurring primer. If you're looking to fill in large pores or control oil all day, this isn't it. This is a hydration-plus-grip product, not a mattifier. Oily skin types will need a setting powder on top.

One buyer noted: *"It's great, but I have to be careful not to use too much or my sunscreen pills up. Once I figured out the right amount, it's perfect."*

The tube packaging is my biggest annoyance. The last 10% of product is impossible to squeeze out, and the cap has a thin hinge that can crack if you drop it. Keep it upright and you'll be fine.

**Who should buy this**

- **Dry or normal skin types** who struggle with foundation lifting or looking cakey by midday
- **Budget-conscious beauty lovers** who want a solid grip primer without spending $25+
- **Sensitive skin** — no fragrance, no silicones, no common irritants

**Skip it if** you have very oily skin and need oil control, or if you prefer a silicone-based blurring primer.

**The three questions shoppers actually ask**

**Q: Does this work for oily skin?**
It grips makeup well, but it won't control oil. You'll need a mattifying powder and blotting papers. If your skin produces a lot of oil in the first two hours, look at a mattifying primer instead.

**Q: How much should I use?**
Less than you think. A pea-sized drop for your entire face. Warm it between your fingers first, then pat it into the skin. Wait 60 seconds before foundation.

**Q: Is this a dupe for the Milk Hydro Grip primer?**
It's in the same family — both use humectants and a tacky finish — but the Milk version has more slip and a stronger grip. The essence version is about 60-70% of the performance at a fraction of the price. For most people, that's a fair trade.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely hydrating primer that locks in your foundation for 8+ hours without breaking the bank. **Skip it if** you need serious oil control or pore-minimizing effects — this does one thing (hydration + grip) and does it well.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Knocking off points only for the finicky texture and annoying packaging. The performance itself is a 4.5.
