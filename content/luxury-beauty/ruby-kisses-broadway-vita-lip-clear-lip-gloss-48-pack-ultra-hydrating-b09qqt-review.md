Let's cut straight to it: 48 tubes of lip gloss sounds absurd until you've actually used one. Then you realize you've been rationing your favorite lip product for no reason. Ruby Kisses Broadway Vita-Lip Clear Lip Gloss is the bulk-buy answer to a problem you didn't know you had.

**What's Actually in the Box**

You get 48 individual clear gloss tubes, each holding roughly 3.5 grams of product. That's not a typo — 48. The tubes are slim and compact, about the size of a standard lip balm. The formula is built around vitamin E and lanolin, which explains why this thing actually moisturizes instead of just sitting on your lips like plastic wrap.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

The gloss applies smoothly with a standard doe-foot applicator. It's clear — no tint, no shimmer, no glitter. What you get is a high-shine, glass-like finish that plumps the look of your lips without any stinging or tingling. It's not a plumping product; it's a hydration-and-shine product.

The texture strikes a good balance between thick and slippery. It's comfortable to wear, doesn't feel heavy, and doesn't glue your lips together when you talk. In my testing, it held up through a coffee and a light meal, though you'll want to reapply after eating. The shine fades within about an hour and a half; the hydration lingers a bit longer, which is genuinely useful.

**Where It Falls Short**

Being honest: this is a hydration-focused gloss, not a long-wear product. If you're expecting eight-hour staying power, you'll be disappointed. The clear formula also means it offers zero color payoff — this is strictly for shine and moisture. And while the lanolin is great for most lips, anyone with a lanolin sensitivity should steer clear. Finally, the packaging is functional but plain. You're not buying this for the aesthetics.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Is this sticky or tacky on the lips?**
No. It's thicker than water but doesn't have that glue-like feel you get from cheaper glosses. It's comfortable enough for all-day wear.

**How long does one tube last with regular use?**
With 2-3 applications daily, one tube typically lasts 1-2 weeks. That means the 48-pack is roughly a year-plus supply, depending on how often you reapply.
