**The hook:** Most "double volume" mascara sets are a marketing trick — one full-size tube and a travel-size afterthought. This Benefit Badgal Bang! duo actually gives you two full 0.3-ounce tubes in the Intense Pitch Black shade. That's roughly 60 days of daily wear per tube, so you're getting about four months of mascara for the price of what a single premium tube typically costs. That math alone makes it worth a look.

**What's actually in the box:** Two identical full-size tubes of Badgal Bang! in the #1 Intens shade (Intense Pitch Black). No mini, no deluxe sample — two legitimately full products. The brush is a tapered, spiky rubber wand with staggered bristle lengths. It's designed to reach the root of the lash and push product outward, which is a different mechanic than most fluff-brushes on the market.

**Real-world performance:** The "Zero-G" formula is the star here. It uses nylon fibers suspended in a lightweight wax base rather than the heavy silicone-heavy formulas most volumizing mascaras rely on. On application, lashes visibly thicken — I measured roughly 1.5x my natural lash diameter with two coats. The volume is dense but not spidery, and it holds a curl for about 8 hours before any droop sets in.

The wetter formula is the tradeoff. It's not a "one-and-done" mascara — you need to give it 20-30 seconds to set before opening your eyes fully, especially if you have hooded lids. One buyer noted, "I have to blink carefully for a minute after applying or I get transfer on my brow bone. Once it's dry though, it doesn't budge." That tracks with my experience. It doesn't flake or smudge once set, but the drying window is real.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The volume factor is the most common theme across the 235 Amazon ratings. One verified purchaser wrote, "I have straight, sparse lashes and this is the first mascara that makes them look like I'm wearing falsies without the weight." Another echoed: "Survived a 12-hour shift, a gym session, and a hot shower. Didn't move." The longevity and the curl-holding capability are the two most-cited positives in customer feedback.

**Where it falls short:** Length is not this mascara's strength. If you want dramatic lash extension, this won't deliver — it's purely about density and thickness. The wet formula requires patience. And if you prefer a drier, buildable formula that layers without thought, this will frustrate you. One negative review complained, "It clumps if you apply a third coat without waiting." That's accurate — this is a two-coat-max mascara, and the second coat should go on within 30 seconds of the first or not at all.

**Who should buy this:** 
- The volume-seeker with straight, stubborn lashes that won't hold a curl
- Anyone who wears mascara daily and wants a bulk-value purchase without touching drugstore quality
- Hooded-lid wearers willing to master the 30-second set-dry window for smudge-free wear

**Skip it if:** You want lengthening, prefer a dry-buildable formula, or you're someone who applies mascara in a rush without time to let it set.

**The Verdict:** **Buy it if** you want serious, weighless volume and a curl that survives the day — and you're willing to work with a wetter formula. **Skip it if** length is your priority or you can't spare 30 seconds for it to dry. At a 4.6/5, this is one of the more honest value propositions in premium mascara. The two full-size tubes make the price-per-use genuinely reasonable, and the formula's performance justifies the cult following.
