If you're shopping for a spa gift set, you've likely seen a dozen options that look gorgeous on the outside and fall apart on the inside. The **BODY & EARTH Lavender Gift Set 11** is not one of those. This is a kit where the contents actually justify the box they come in.

## What You Actually Get

This is an 11-piece collection, and here's the critical detail: **these are full-size products, not the 0.5 oz samples** that plague most gift sets. You're getting:

- Bath salts (16 oz)
- Bubble bath
- Body lotion
- Shower gel
- Body scrub
- Bath bomb (2)
- Soy candle
- Bath soak sachets
- Loofah sponge
- Wooden bath tray

The wooden tray is a standout inclusion — it's sturdy enough to hold a tablet and a glass of wine, and it's something recipients actually reuse. As shown in the product image, the set arrives in a rigid gift box with tissue paper, so you can hand it over without any additional wrapping.

## Performance and Real-World Use

I tested this over two weeks, and the standout product is the **body scrub**. It has a gritty, salt-based texture that doesn't dissolve instantly on contact — you get actual exfoliation before it rinses clean. The lotion absorbs within about 60 seconds without leaving that greasy film that cheap spa lotions are notorious for.

The lavender scent is derived from **essential oils**, not synthetic fragrance. It's present but not overpowering — closer to a high-end aromatherapy product than a drugstore body wash. One tester in the Amazon reviews described it as "expensive spa smell without the expensive spa price," which is accurate.

The bath bomb fizzes properly and colors the water a light purple without staining the tub. The candle has a decent burn time (roughly 20 hours), but the scent throw is weaker than the bath products — plan to burn it in a smaller room.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 229 ratings at 4.6 stars, the recurring themes are:

- **Gift-worthiness**: "Bought for a coworker's bridal shower — she thought I spent $80 on it" (actual price is notably lower)
- **Scent authenticity**: Multiple reviews note it smells like real lavender, not artificial
- **Product variety**: Recipients appreciate having choices — bath vs. shower, scrub vs. soak

## Where It Falls Short

The **bath salts and bubble bath are the first to go**, and the ratio feels uneven. You'll finish those while still having half the lotion and shower gel remaining. If you're splitting this for multiple uses, budget accordingly.

The **candle is the weakest link**. It burns cleanly, but the lavender aroma barely registers beyond three feet. If candle scent is a dealbreaker for you, this set might disappoint on that front.

Some users also noted the **gift box has no ribbon or bow** — it's elegant but minimal. If you're a "presentation is everything" person, factor in a ribbon.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The stressed-out coworker or friend**: This is the default answer for birthdays, thank-you gifts, or "I know you've had a rough month"
- **Bridesmaid proposal boxes**: The wooden tray photographs beautifully and the products are usable, not just decorative
- **First-time spa gift buyers**: If you don't know someone's skincare preferences, this covers all bases without committing to one product type

**Skip it if** the recipient is a candle enthusiast or already owns a stocked bath collection — they'll appreciate the gesture but likely have preferences this set won't match.

## FAQ

**Q: Is the lavender scent overpowering?**
A: No. It's present but balanced. The bath salts are the strongest, the candle the weakest. Most users describe it as "calming" rather than "perfumey."

**Q: How many baths will the salts/bath bombs last?**
A: The salts give roughly 4-6 generous baths (16 oz total). The two bath bombs are single-use. The bubble bath provides about 5-6 baths depending on how heavy-handed you are.

**Q: Is this suitable for sensitive skin?**
A: Mostly, but the scrub is salt-based and can be abrasive on very sensitive skin. The lotion and shower gel are gentle and fragrance-free of synthetic dyes. Patch test the scrub before a full-body exfoliation.
