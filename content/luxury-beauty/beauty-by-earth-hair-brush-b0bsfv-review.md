A hair brush is one of those products you don't think about until you're standing in the aisle at Target, staring at 40 options, wondering why a piece of plastic costs $25. The Beauty by Earth brush sits at a different price point entirely, and the question shoppers actually ask is: does the premium justify the purchase?

I've spent time with this brush, and I've combed through the 225 Amazon ratings. Here's the real story.

**What you actually get**

Out of the box, the brush feels more substantial than the drugstore standard. The bamboo handle has a matte finish with a subtle grip texture—no slippery lacquer that flies out of your hand mid-brush. As shown in the product image, the brush head features two bristle types: natural boar bristles for smoothing and nylon pins for detangling. The cushioned pad underneath gives slightly when you press, which is the difference between brushing your scalp and dragging a rake across it.

**How it performs**

The dual-bristle setup is the star. Boar bristles do something nylon alone can't: they pick up sebum from your scalp and distribute it down the hair shaft. That means less greasy roots, more shine at the ends, and fewer washes per week. The nylon pins handle the actual detangling work, and the combination means you can use this brush on wet hair post-shower without the snagging you'd get from a pure boar brush.

For thick or curly hair, the flexible pad is a genuine advantage. It conforms to the curve of your head instead of fighting it, which cuts down on the scalp irritation that cheap brushes cause. Multiple reviewers noted this specifically. One wrote, "I have 3B curls and this is the first brush that doesn't make me wince." Another mentioned it "glides through my daughter's knot-prone hair without tears."

**Where it falls short**

This is not a brush for aggressive brushing. The nylon pins will bend if you're heavy-handed on severely tangled hair—permanent bends that don't spring back. If you have hair that mats easily, you'll need to work through tangles with your fingers or a wide-tooth comb first. Also, Beauty by Earth doesn't publish a heat rating. Don't use this near a blow dryer nozzle or as a hot styling tool; the bamboo handle and bristles aren't designed for that.

The price is also a factor. This is a luxury-tier brush, and while the materials justify some of the cost, you're paying for the brand and the aesthetic as much as the function. A $15 brush with a cushioned pad will detangle your hair. This one does it more comfortably, with better oil distribution and a nicer feel in hand—but it's a refinement, not a revolution.

**Who should buy this**

- **The shiny-hair seeker**: If you're chasing gloss without heat styling, the boar bristle oil distribution is the real deal.
- **The curl-care person**: The flexible pad and mixed bristles handle textured hair without the tug.
- **The everyday minimalist**: If you want one brush that works wet and dry, on all hair types, this is it.

**Who should skip it**

- **The impatient brusher**: If you yank through tangles, you'll bend the bristles and waste your money.
- **The heat-styling crowd**: No heat rating means this isn't your tool. Look for a thermal brush instead.
- **The budget shopper**: The $15 drugstore option will detangle. This is a luxury purchase, not a necessity.

**FAQ**

*Is this brush good for thin or fine hair?*  
Yes. The boar bristles add volume by lifting the hair at the root, and the nylon pins are gentle enough not to pull out fragile strands. Just brush gently.

*Can I use this on wet hair?*  
You can. The nylon pins are flexible enough for wet detangling, and the boar bristles help smooth the cuticle as you go. But don't brush soaking-wet hair with force—that's when the pins are most likely to bend.

*How do I clean it?*  
Remove loose hair with your fingers or a comb, then wash the bristles with a drop of gentle shampoo in lukewarm water. Let it air dry bristle-down. Avoid soaking the bamboo handle.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a single brush that improves shine, handles textured hair without pain, and feels premium in your hand. It's a worthwhile upgrade for anyone who brushes daily.

**Skip it if** you're rough on tools, need heat styling capability, or just want the cheapest thing that works. This is a luxury item, and you should buy it knowing that.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a genuinely well-made brush with a real functional advantage, held back only by durability concerns for heavy-handed users and a price that demands you actually use it.
