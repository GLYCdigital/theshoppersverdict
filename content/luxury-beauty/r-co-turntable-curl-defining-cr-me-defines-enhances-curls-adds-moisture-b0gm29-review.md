# R+Co TURNTABLE Curl Defining Crème: The $38 Question

Let's be honest — curl products are a graveyard of broken promises. Half-empty jars of crunchy gels, greasy butters, and "definition" that evaporates by noon. So when R+Co, the cult-favorite salon brand, dropped TURNTABLE, the beauty world took notice. But does this luxury crème actually earn its price tag, or is it just another pretty tube? I tested it for three weeks on 2B-3C hair. Here's the real story.

## What You're Actually Getting

The product ships in R+Co's signature minimalist tube — think high-end skincare, not drugstore hair goo. You get 5.5 fluid ounces of product, which is generous for the luxury category. The crème itself is a pearly white, medium-weight lotion that smells like a high-end fragrance: bergamot, jasmine, and a hint of warm amber. It's pleasant, but it's *loud*. If you're sensitive to scent, this could be a dealbreaker — one Amazon reviewer noted, "The smell is gorgeous but lingers all day, even after drying."

## Real-World Performance

Here's where TURNTABLE earns its keep. The formula uses a blend of shea butter, coconut oil, and a proprietary "Curl Memory Complex" that actually does what it claims. Applied to soaking wet hair, it creates soft, defined spirals that dry without the infamous "crunch." I'm talking touchable, bouncy curls that survive a 10-hour workday and a commute through New York humidity.

The key is application. Work it through with your head flipped over — a generous pump for shoulder-length hair, two for longer locks. Then scrunch, diffuse or air-dry, and don't touch it until fully dry. The hold is medium — think "effortless beach wave" rather than "pin-straight to spiral perm." One reviewer nailed it: "This is the first curl cream that gives me definition AND movement. My curls look alive, not shellacked."

## Where It Falls Short

Let's talk tradeoffs. First, the price. At $38, this is squarely in luxury territory. You can get comparable results from drugstore brands like Not Your Mother's or SheaMoisture for a third of the cost — though you'll sacrifice the refined texture and fragrance. Second, the scent is genuinely polarizing. I've seen reviews ranging from "heavenly" to "gave me a headache." Test it in-store before committing.

Third — and this is important — if you have very coarse, tightly coiled hair (4B-4C), this alone won't cut it. It's a *defining* crème, not a deep moisturizer. You'll need a leave-in conditioner underneath. As one 4C reviewer put it: "Lovely definition, but my hair needed extra hydration first."

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have wavy-to-curly hair (2A-3C), want soft, defined curls without crunch, and appreciate a luxurious, salon-grade experience. It's perfect for the "wash and go" girl who wants to look effortless but secretly spent 20 minutes on her hair.

**Skip it if:** You're on a strict budget, hate strong fragrances, or have hair that needs heavy, buttery moisture. Also skip if you want maximum hold — this won't survive a rainstorm or a sweaty gym session.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How much should I use?**
A: Start with one pump for shoulder-length hair, two for longer. You can always add more — this formula doesn't get crunchy even if you over-apply, but it can get heavy on fine hair.

**Q: Does this work for wavy hair (2A-2B)?**
A: Yes, but use less product and apply to soaking wet hair. It'll enhance your natural wave without weighing it down. Skip it if you want loose, beachy texture — this defines more than it tousles.

**Q: Is it curly-girl method approved?**
A: Yes — no sulfates, silicones, or parabens. It's also cruelty-free and vegan, which is a bonus for ethically-conscious shoppers.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

R+Co TURNTABLE is a genuinely excellent curl crème that delivers on its promises: definition, moisture, and soft, touchable hold. It's not a miracle worker — coarse hair needs more, and the scent is a gamble — but for the wavy-to-curly crowd who wants a premium, effective product, this is a worthy splurge.

**Buy it if** you want salon-quality curls without the salon appointment. **Skip it if** you need deep moisture or maximum hold, or if $38 feels like too much for hair product.

Bottom line: It's expensive, but it works. And in the world of curl products, that's rarer than you'd think.
