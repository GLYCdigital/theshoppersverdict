Here's the thing about drugstore matte lipsticks: most are either patchy, drying, or both. So when L'Oreal claims their Colour Riche Intense Volume Matte delivers "intense volume" with a matte finish, it deserves scrutiny. After testing this across multiple wear scenarios, here's my honest take.

**What You Actually Get**

The lipstick comes in a weighty gold-and-black tube that feels more premium than its price point suggests. The twist-up bullet is angled with a sculpting tip — not the standard flat cut — which makes lining your cupid's bow noticeably easier. As shown in the product image, the formula has a creamy, almost mousse-like texture that glides on without dragging.

The line includes 20 shades ranging from nude beiges to deep burgundies. The star of the collection is arguably "Le Nude Intense," a versatile pinky-brown that works on most skin tones.

**Performance and Wear**

Here's where this lipstick separates from the drugstore pack. The pigment load is exceptional — one swipe delivers full, even coverage. No layering needed, no patchiness on dry patches. The texture is genuinely comfortable for a matte. L'Oreal infused this with argan oil, and you can feel it. It wears like a satin for the first hour before settling into a true matte that doesn't shrink-wrap your lips.

I tested it through a three-hour dinner plus coffee. It survived the meal with only minor fading in the center. But here's the honest caveat: this is not transfer-proof. You'll leave marks on glasses, and it will shift onto masks. The color that remains after a meal is still presentable — more of a soft stain than a mess — but don't expect the staying power of a liquid lipstick.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.3-star rating across 215 reviews reflects real satisfaction. One reviewer writes, "I've tried $30 mattes that aren't this comfortable. The color is gorgeous and doesn't settle into lines." Another notes, "Finally a matte that doesn't make my lips look like the Sahara." The consistent themes are color payoff and comfort — two things that rarely coexist in this category.

**Where It Falls Short**

The shade range has a clear warm undertone bias. If you're looking for cool-toned mauves or blue-based reds, options are thin. One verified buyer complained, "The 'nude' shades all pull orange on my fair, cool skin." Also, the formula is creamy enough that it can feather slightly on those with deep lip lines — use a liner if that's a concern.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The matte skeptic**: You want matte color without the dry, tight feeling. This converts you.
- **The one-swipe minimalist**: You're over building layers. This delivers full color instantly.
- **The luxury-curious on a budget**: If you love the look of high-end mattes but balk at $35+ price tags, this delivers close to 90% of the experience.

**Skip it if**: You need a truly transfer-proof formula for all-day wear under masks, or you specifically need cool-toned shades.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this drying like other matte lipsticks?**
A: No. The argan oil keeps it comfortable for hours. It's not hydrating like a balm, but it won't crack or flake.

**Q: How long does it actually last?**
A: Plan for 4-5 hours with light eating. Through a meal, expect center fading. It leaves a flattering stain behind, not a patchy mess.

**Q: Is one swipe really enough?**
A: For most shades, yes. The pigment concentration is genuinely high. Darker shades might need a quick second pass at the inner lip.

**The Verdict: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if** you want department-store quality at a fraction of the price and prioritize comfort alongside color payoff. **Skip it if** transfer-proof wear is non-negotiable or you need a specific cool undertone.

This is one of the few drugstore mattes that doesn't feel like a compromise. It's a smart buy — just keep a mirror handy for touch-ups.
