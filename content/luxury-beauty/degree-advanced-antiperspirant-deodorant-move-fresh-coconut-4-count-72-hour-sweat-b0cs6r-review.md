You know the drill: you buy a deodorant, it works for a week, then your body builds a tolerance and you're back to pit stains by noon. Degree Advanced claims to break that cycle with 72-hour protection and something called Motionsense — a technology that releases fresh scent when you move. After putting the Move Fresh Coconut formula through real-world testing, here's what I can tell you.

## What's in the Box

This listing gets you a 4-count pack of 2.7-ounce sticks. That's roughly four months of daily use for most people, which is where the value math starts making sense. The stick itself has a smooth, gliding application — no drag, no chalky buildup on the skin. As shown in the product image, it's a standard white stick format, not a gel or clear formula.

## Performance and Build Quality

Here's where Degree separates itself from the drugstore pack. The Motionsense technology isn't marketing fluff — the coconut fragrance genuinely intensifies as you move throughout the day. I noticed it most during a midday workout; the scent refreshed itself without needing to reapply. The 72-hour claim is optimistic for heavy sweaters, but the 24-hour protection is rock solid. Even on a 90-degree day with a commute and a gym session, there was no breakthrough wetness.

The active ingredient is aluminum zirconium tetrachlorohydrex gly — the standard heavy-duty antiperspirant compound. It works by temporarily blocking sweat ducts, so expect a significant reduction, not elimination, of sweating. That's a good thing; your body still needs to cool itself.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.8-star rating across 240 reviews isn't accidental. Repeated themes in customer feedback include:

- **Scent longevity**: Multiple reviewers note the coconut scent lasts longer than comparable products from Secret or Dove.
- **No residue on skin**: Several users mention it "disappears into the skin" rather than leaving a film.
- **Sensitivity tolerance**: One reviewer wrote, *"I have sensitive skin and most deodorants cause a rash. This one doesn't. I've been using it for three weeks with zero issues."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The strong formula can sting if you apply immediately after shaving — give it a few minutes or use it on alternate days if you're prone to razor burn. The white stick also requires a moment to dry before dressing; otherwise, you'll see chalky transfer on dark shirts. One reviewer noted, *"Love the protection but I have to wait 30 seconds before putting on my black work shirts."* It's a minor inconvenience, not a dealbreaker.

## Who Should Buy This

This deodorant is a strong match for:

- **Commuters and desk workers** who need reliable all-day protection without midday reapplication
- **Fitness enthusiasts** who want a product that works during and after workouts
- **Coconut fragrance fans** who prefer tropical scents over floral or powdery alternatives
- **Value-conscious shoppers** who appreciate the 4-pack economy

It's not ideal for those with truly hypersensitive skin, people who prefer unscented formulas, or anyone who needs a clinical-strength prescription product.

## FAQ

**Does the 72-hour claim actually hold up?**
In my testing, 72 hours is achievable if you're not exercising heavily. For most people, you'll still want to reapply daily — but the formula gives you a buffer if you skip a day.

**Will this stain my clothes?**
Clear gels stain less than white sticks. If you let it dry for 30 seconds before dressing, you'll avoid visible transfer. It doesn't leave yellow armpit stains like some formulas.

**Is the coconut scent overpowering?**
No, it's well-balanced. You'll notice it on application and when moving, but it fades to a subtle base note rather than competing with your perfume or cologne.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if** you want dependable long-lasting sweat protection with a genuinely pleasant scent that doesn't quit by lunchtime. The 4-pack value is excellent for regular users.

**Skip it if** you have extremely sensitive skin, prefer invisible gel formulas, or want a fragrance-free option. This is a solid, performance-focused antiperspirant with one notable quirk — the application timing — that's easily managed.

For the price per stick, the Degree Advanced Move Fresh Coconut delivers serious sweat-fighting power with a scent profile that actually makes you look forward to applying it. That's a rare combination at any price point.
