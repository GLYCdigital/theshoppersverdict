**The cult-favorite K-beauty powder that refuses to die — now in a "Variation" formula. Here's the honest breakdown.**

If you've been anywhere near K-beauty TikTok or Reddit's AsianBeauty forum in the past five years, you know the original ETUDE Zero Sebum powder has a near-religious following. The new "Variation" iteration keeps the same mission — annihilate shine — but tweaks the formula and packaging. I've been testing it for two weeks across humid outdoor days, 10-hour office stints, and one sweaty gym session. Here's what real buyers (and my own face) say.

**What you actually get**

The compact contains 5g of powder — roughly the size of a large coin. It comes with a thin, flocked puff tucked under a flip-top lid. The powder itself is white with a faint, clean scent that disappears within seconds. As shown in the product image, it's a simple, functional compact that's light enough for a pocket or small bag. No mirror inside, which is a minor miss for touch-ups on the go.

**Performance: the good, the bad, the greasy**

The Variation formula goes on translucent — no white cast, even on deeper skin tones. One light sweep over my T-zone after sunscreen killed shine for a solid four hours, which is respectable. By hour six, I needed a blotting paper, but that's typical for a powder, not a primer.

The blurring effect is the real star. As one verified buyer put it: *"I have large pores on my nose and this makes them disappear without looking cakey."* That tracks with my experience — it smooths texture better than the original formula, which could look chalky if over-applied.

Where it stumbles: the included puff. It's thin and deposits powder unevenly. Use a fluffy brush instead — you'll get a lighter, more controlled application. Also, if your skin is dehydrated or flaky, this will emphasize those patches. It's an oil-control product, not a skincare treatment.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across 215 ratings (4.3 stars average), the recurring themes are clear:

- **Versatility:** People use it as a makeup setter, a midday blotter, and even a dry shampoo substitute in a pinch.
- **Value:** A little goes a long way. One reviewer noted, *"I've had mine for six months with almost daily use and it's not even half empty."*
- **No flashback:** Unlike many loose powders, this doesn't ghost in photos — a critical test for event makeup.

**Where it falls short**

- **The puff is a weak point.** Multiple reviews mention it's too thin and picks up too much powder. Budget for a separate brush.
- **Not for dry skin.** If your skin leans dry or you use strong actives (retinol, acids), this can cling to dry patches. It's strictly for oily or combination skin.
- **The "Variation" name is vague.** It's not dramatically different from the original. If you already own the classic formula, you don't need this upgrade unless you want the new packaging.

**Who should buy this**

- **The oily-T-zone sufferer** who's tired of midday shine and wants a reliable, portable fix.
- **The minimal-makeup person** who wants to set sunscreen or tinted moisturizer without adding foundation.
- **The budget-conscious shopper** who wants a proven formula under $15 (price varies by seller, so check current listings).

**Skip it if** you have dry or flaky skin, or if you're expecting a dramatic reformulation — this is an evolution, not a revolution.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this over liquid foundation?**
A: Yes, but tap off excess powder on the puff or brush first. It sets makeup beautifully without disturbing it. For best results, press, don't swipe.

**Q: Is the white powder visible on dark skin?**
A: No. The Variation formula is truly translucent once blended. Multiple reviewers with deeper skin tones confirmed no white cast — just a matte finish.

**Q: How long does one compact last?**
A: With daily use, expect 4-6 months. The 5g size is small, but the powder is dense and requires minimal product per application.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need dependable, no-nonsense oil control that also blurs pores. **Skip it if** your skin is dry or you already own the original formula.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, affordable upgrade for oily skin, held back only by a weak applicator and a slight learning curve for even application. For the price and performance, it's hard to beat for everyday shine control.
