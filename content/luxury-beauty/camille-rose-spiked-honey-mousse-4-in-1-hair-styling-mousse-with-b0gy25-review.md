Let's be straight about the Camille Rose Spiked Honey Mousse: it's not a miracle in a jar, but it's genuinely close for the right hair type. With 215 ratings averaging 4.3 stars, this 4-in-1 styling mousse has earned a loyal following — and some legitimate criticism. Here's what you actually need to know before buying.

**What's in the jar**

You're getting a 240ml whipped mousse that pulls quadruple duty: styling, defining curls, moisturizing, and adding shine. The formula leans on honey (hence the name) and marshmallow root extract, both of which are genuinely humectant — they draw moisture into the hair shaft rather than just coating it. As shown in the product image, the texture is airy and light, closer to a whipped cream than a traditional gel-mousse hybrid.

**Performance and real-world use**

I tested this on freshly washed, damp hair with a twist-out. The application is forgiving — a little goes further than you'd expect, and it spreads evenly without that tacky, glue-like feeling some mousses have. The honey does real work here: my curls dried with definition and a soft, glassy shine that lasted into day two. No crunch, no flaking, no white residue on darker strands — a common complaint with cheaper styling foams.

But here's the tradeoff: the hold is medium, not maximum. On loose waves or fine-to-medium curly hair, you'll get beautiful, touchable definition that holds for a full day. On thick, coily, or very dense hair, expect to refresh or pair it with a stronger gel for extended hold. The marketing says "spiked" — the reality is softer, more flexible hold than that name implies.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The shine and moisture balance comes up repeatedly in customer feedback. One verified buyer noted, "Finally a mousse that doesn't leave my 4C hair feeling like straw. The honey actually sinks in." Another praised the versatility: "I use it for wash-and-gos, twist-outs, and even slicking down my edges — it does all three without buildup." The lightweight formula earns consistent mentions, with multiple reviewers noting they can restyle without re-wetting.

**Where it falls short**

The scent is the most common complaint. It's strongly honey-forward with a slightly medicinal undertone that some find cloying. If you're sensitive to fragrance, this could be a dealbreaker. Several reviewers also flagged the jar size — at roughly 8 ounces, it's smaller than typical styling mousse containers, and heavy users may blow through it in a month. And as noted, the hold ceiling is real. One reviewer put it plainly: "Great for definition, but if you need all-day hold on thick hair, you'll need backup."

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for those with wavy, curly, or coily hair who want moisture and definition without stiffness — especially if you prioritize hair health over maximum hold. It's a strong pick for wash-and-go routines, twist-outs, and braid-outs. Skip it if you need serious, sculpted hold for elaborate styles, have scent sensitivities, or prefer heavy-duty stylers for tightly coiled hair.

**FAQ**

*Can I use this on dry hair for refresh days?*
Yes, but go light. A pea-sized amount warmed between palms works best for reviving curls without buildup or crunch.

*Does it work on relaxed or color-treated hair?*
Generally yes — the honey and marshmallow root are gentle and moisturizing. But test a small section first, as the fragrance could interact with sensitive scalps.

*How does it compare to the Camille Rose Curl Maker?*
Curl Maker is a heavier, gel-like styler with stronger hold. This mousse is lighter, shinier, and better for soft, touchable definition — choose based on whether you want flexible or firm hold.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a versatile, moisturizing styler that defines curls and adds shine without crunch, and you prefer soft, natural-looking hold over sculpted stiffness.

**Skip it if** you need maximum hold on thick, coily hair, dislike strong honey scents, or want a larger container per dollar.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The Spiked Honey Mousse delivers on its 4-in-1 promise with genuine quality ingredients and beautiful results on the right hair type. The medium hold and fragrance are honest limitations, not dealbreakers.
