## The Pomade That Actually Respects Your Hair

Most men's pomades fall into two miserable categories: grease bombs that coat your hair like a deep fryer, or gel-like glue that cracks by lunch. BASED Pomade sits in a third camp — a water-soluble, medium-hold formula that behaves like a premium product should. After three weeks of daily use, here's the unvarnished truth.

## What's Actually in the Jar

You get a 3.4 oz (100g) amber glass jar with a matte black lid — the kind of packaging that doesn't look embarrassed sitting on your bathroom counter. The pomade itself is a light beige balm with a firm but scoopable texture. The ingredient list is refreshingly short: beeswax, shea butter, castor oil, and a few essential oils. No parabens, no sulfates, no mystery polymers you need a chemistry degree to pronounce.

The scent is bergamot and cedar — noticeable when you scoop it out, gone within half an hour. If you're sensitive to fragrances, this won't trigger you.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where BASED separates itself from the pack. The medium hold is genuinely medium — not "medium" like most brands mean (rock-hard), but actual flexibility. Your hair stays put through a workday, but you can still run your fingers through it at 5 PM without fighting for your life.

Application is straightforward: warm it between your palms (about 10 seconds), work it through towel-dried or dry hair. It spreads evenly without tugging or pulling. The finish is low-shine — think "healthy hair" rather than "greased back for a wedding."

The standout feature? **It washes out completely with one shampoo pass.** No residue, no scalp buildup, no pillowcase stains. That alone justifies the premium price for anyone who's dealt with the waxy aftermath of cheaper pomades.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

With 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, the feedback pattern is remarkably consistent. One reviewer noted: *"Finally a pomade that doesn't make my hair feel like plastic. I can restyle it midday if needed and it doesn't flake."* Another echoed: *"The hold is perfect for my fine hair — my head doesn't feel heavy or greasy."*

The most repeated compliment? The lack of residue. Multiple reviewers specifically called out how easily it washes out compared to brands like Murray's or Suavecito.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. At roughly $22–28 per jar, you're paying a significant premium over drugstore options. The 3.4 oz jar lasts about 6–8 weeks with daily use — that's a real cost consideration.

The low-shine finish is also a dealbreaker for some. If you want that glossy, slicked-back look, this isn't it. And while the scent is pleasant, it's also subtle — don't expect a fragrance that lingers.

One practical warning: **start with half the amount you think you need.** This pomade is concentrated. A dime-sized scoop covers most of a head of short-to-medium hair. Over-apply and you'll look greasy, not styled.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have fine to medium hair, want a natural-looking hold that doesn't announce itself, or you're tired of waxy buildup from cheaper pomades. It's ideal for office workers, guys with thinning hair (it doesn't weigh hair down), or anyone who hates the crunchy feeling of gel.

**Skip it if:** You want a high-shine, wet-look finish, you're on a strict budget, or you have very thick, coarse hair that needs a heavy hold. For those cases, look at clay-based products instead.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work for long hair?**
A: It's best for short to medium styles (up to 4–5 inches). Longer hair will need a stronger hold product.

**Q: Can I use it on wet hair?**
A: Yes, but it spreads more easily on dry or towel-dried hair. Wet application dilutes the hold.

**Q: How long does one jar last?**
A: With daily use (dime-sized amount), expect 6–8 weeks. Less frequent use stretches it to 3 months.
