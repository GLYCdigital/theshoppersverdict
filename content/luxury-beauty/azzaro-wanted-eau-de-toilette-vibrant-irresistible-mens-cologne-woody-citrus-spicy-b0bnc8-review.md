**Azzaro Wanted EDT Review: The Ginger Bomb That Earns Its Hype**

Some fragrances whisper. Azzaro Wanted shouts — but in a way that makes people lean closer, not back away. This is the rare designer release that actually delivers on its "irresistible" tagline. After wearing it through summer heat, office days, and date nights, I can tell you exactly where it shines and where it stumbles.

**What's in the Bottle**

You're getting a 100ml Eau de Toilette in that distinctive hexagonal amber glass bottle that looks like a whiskey decanter designed by an architect. The magnetic cap snaps shut with a satisfying click — small detail, but it tells you Azzaro didn't cheap out on presentation. The juice inside is a woody-citrus-spicy composition built around three stars: ginger, lemon, and cedarwood. Vetiver and amber round out the base.

**Performance That Punches Above Its Class**

The opening is unapologetically loud — sharp ginger and lemon that bite for the first 15 minutes. This isn't a shy scent. But give it an hour and it settles into something warmer: the cedar and tonka bean emerge, softening the edges while keeping that energetic core. On my skin, I got a solid 8 hours, with a faint skin scent lingering past 10. That's exceptional for an EDT, especially one that doesn't cost niche money.

Projection is the real story here. For the first two hours, you're announcing your presence from arm's length. After that, it settles to a comfortable bubble that invites compliments without suffocating anyone. This is a scent that works in an office if you stick to 2-3 sprays. More than that and you'll clear a conference room.

**What Buyers Are Saying**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 ratings reflects real consensus. One reviewer put it simply: *"I've gotten more compliments in two weeks with this than I did in a year with my previous cologne."* Another noted the versatility: *"Wore it to work, wore it to a wedding — works for both."* The most consistent praise centers on longevity, with multiple buyers reporting it outperforming pricier designer fragrances that vanish by lunch.

**Where It Falls Short**

Honesty time. The ginger opening is polarizing. If you're used to sweeter, more mass-appealing scents like Sauvage or Bleu de Chanel, this will initially smell sharp — almost harsh. It takes about 30 minutes to hit its stride, so don't judge from the first spray. Also, the loud projection means this isn't a "safe" choice for close-quarters environments. You need to calibrate your spray count or you'll be that guy.

The bottle itself is a fingerprint magnet — gorgeous to look at, but it picks up smudges constantly. Minor, but worth noting if presentation matters to you.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The office worker** who wants one scent that transitions from desk to after-hours without reapplication
- **The compliment-chaser** who's tired of paying $150+ for fragrances that fade by noon
- **The warm-weather wearer** who needs something that survives humidity without turning cloying

**Skip It If** you prefer subtle, skin-hugging scents, or if you're sensitive to ginger-forward openings. Also skip if you're looking for a formal/black-tie fragrance — this leans casual-to-smart-casual.

**FAQ**

**Q: How many sprays should I use?**
A: Start with 2 — one on each side of the neck. This scent projects hard. If you're going out, add one to your chest. Resist the urge to overspray; you'll thank yourself in an hour.

**Q: Is this suitable for summer?**
A: Yes, surprisingly. The ginger and lemon keep it fresh in heat, and the cedar provides just enough weight to avoid feeling thin. It's one of the better warm-weather club scents under $100.

**Q: How does it compare to the original Wanted by Night?**
A: This is fresher and more versatile for daytime wear. By Night is spicier and darker — better for evenings and cooler months. If you can only buy one, this EDT is the safer daily driver.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a versatile, long-lasting scent that generates compliments without requiring a mortgage payment. **Skip it if** you're allergic to bold openings or need something whisper-quiet.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The ginger-forward opening and loud projection cost it half a star, but the performance, versatility, and price-to-quality ratio make this one of the smartest buys in men's designer fragrance right now.
