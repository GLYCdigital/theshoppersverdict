Let's be honest about the tradeoffs:

It's not a problem if you're new to the brand, but seasoned buyers might feel they're paying for products they already own.

**No full-size hero.** The calendar intentionally includes trial sizes to drive full-size purchases. That's smart marketing, but it means you won't get a single fully-stocked full-size product. If you're looking to replace your daily moisturizer, this isn't the way.
