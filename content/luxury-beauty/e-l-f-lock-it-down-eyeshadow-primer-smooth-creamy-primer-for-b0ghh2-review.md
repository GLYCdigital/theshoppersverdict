Let’s be honest about eyeshadow primers: most either feel like glue on your lids or do nothing at all. The e.l.f. Lock It Down Eyeshadow Primer sits somewhere in the middle — and for under $10, that’s a genuinely impressive place to be.

I’ve tested this against primers three times its price, and here’s the unfiltered breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The product comes in a slim squeeze tube with a precision tip — roughly 0.47 oz of product. The primer itself is a soft, creamy white that dries down to a barely-there finish. No wand, no doe-foot applicator, no mess. You squeeze, dab, blend. That’s it.

## Performance: The Real Test

I wore this through a 10-hour day that included humidity, a commute, and a regrettable amount of eye-rubbing. The result? My crease-prone lids stayed crease-free. The formula grips powder shadows aggressively — shimmers and metallics in particular held their intensity without migrating into my creases.

The texture is where this primer shines. It’s smooth and spreadable without being slippery. You get about 30 seconds of working time before it sets, which is plenty for blending out the edges. Once it sets, it locks shadow down. I tested this with a cheap loose pigment that normally falls out within hours — zero fallout.

One thing worth noting: the creamy formula is forgiving on mature lids. It doesn’t cake or settle into fine lines the way some silicone-heavy primers do.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this primer has two honest flaws.

First, if your eyelids have dry patches or flakiness, this can emphasize them. The creamy formula grips texture, so you’ll want to moisturize your lids a few minutes before applying. This isn’t a dealbreaker, but it’s worth knowing if you have chronically dry skin.

Second, darker matte shades — think deep burgundies, navy, black — need slightly more blending time before the primer sets. If you’re too slow, you’ll get patchy color placement. It’s a minor learning curve, not a fatal flaw.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Budget-conscious beauty lovers** who want high-end performance without the price tag
- **Oily-lid folks** who fight creasing and fallout all day
- **Makeup minimalists** who want one reliable primer that works for both everyday and special occasions

Skip it if you have severely dry, flaky eyelids that need a hydrating primer, or if you prefer a tacky, glue-like grip that grabs even the most stubborn pigments instantly.

## FAQ: Real Questions, Direct Answers

**Q: Does this work with cream eyeshadows?**
A: Yes, but with a caveat. It works best underneath powder shadows. Cream shadows can slide around on the primer’s surface if you apply them too thick. For best results, use a thin layer of cream and blend quickly.

**Q: How much product do you need per application?**
A: A rice-grain-sized amount covers both lids. The tube lasted me about 4 months with daily use.

**Q: Is this suitable for sensitive eyes?**
A: Generally, yes. The formula is fragrance-free and didn’t irritate my sensitive eyes. That said, if you have allergies or contact dermatitis, patch test on your inner arm before using near your eyes.
