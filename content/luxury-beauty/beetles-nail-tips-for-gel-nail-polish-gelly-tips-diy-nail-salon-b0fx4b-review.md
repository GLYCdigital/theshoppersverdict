**What's actually in the box**

This isn't a bare-bones starter pack. As shown in the product image, you get a full system: 240 soft gel tips across 24 sizes (10 of each), a 40W LED lamp, gel base coat, top coat, two shades of gel polish, primer, a nail file, buffer block, cuticle pusher, and a wooden cuticle stick. The tips themselves are full-cover soft gels — meaning they cover your entire nail, unlike the half-tip press-ons of yesteryear. Everything packs into a sturdy clear case that doubles as storage.

**Performance and real-world use**

The game-changer here is the tip design. These aren't flimsy plastic press-ons; they're made of semi-cured gel that flexes with your natural nail. The cuticle area is thinner than the free edge, which creates a seamless blend once you file the transition zone. I tested these on a client with flat, wide nail beds — the natural curve of the tips accommodated her shape without the pinching you get from cheaper options.

Application takes about 90 minutes for a full set your first time. You'll brush the adhesive gel onto your natural nail, press the tip down, cure for 60 seconds, then file the seam. The learning curve is real but manageable — most reviewers report nailing it by the second attempt. One verified buyer put it plainly: *"I've spent years getting acrylics, and these honestly look better. My nails lasted 3 weeks with zero lifting."*

**What buyers consistently praise**

The longevity is the headline. Multiple reviewers report 2–3 weeks of wear with proper prep — wiping nails with alcohol, buffing the shine off, and being careful with the adhesive layer. The size variety also earns consistent praise: *"I have small thumbs and wide index fingers — the 24 sizes meant I actually found a perfect fit for every nail."* The included lamp, though compact, cures the polish and tips evenly without hot spots.

**Where it falls short**

The claimed 30-second cure time is optimistic. The 40W lamp requires a full 60 seconds per layer, and the top coat needs two passes. Budget your time accordingly.

The brush-on adhesive is thin. It flows toward your cuticles if you hesitate, which causes lifting. A thicker gel would be more forgiving for beginners. Several reviewers mention this exact issue: *"The glue is runny — work in small sections or it gets messy fast."*

Also note: the two included polish shades are basic. You'll likely want to buy additional Beetles colors (they're compatible with any gel polish).

**Who should buy this**

Skip it if you want instant results with zero learning curve, or if you have severely damaged, peeling nails — these need a healthy base to adhere to.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does the full set take?**
A: Plan 90 minutes the first time. You're doing 10 nails twice (adhesive + polish layers), with filing between steps. Experienced users get down to 60.

**Q: Will these damage my natural nails?**
A: No more than any gel system. The removal process matters more than the wear — soak in pure acetone for 10–15 minutes, then gently push off. Never peel or pry.

**Q: Do I need a separate lamp?**
A: No. The included 40W LED lamp fully cures the tips and polishes. It's small — fits one hand at a time — but it works.
