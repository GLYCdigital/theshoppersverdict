**Performance and Real-World Use**

I've tested enough replacement heads to know that cheap foils often shave close but irritate like sandpaper. The OXSITO Pro 4 heads don't have that problem. The foil sits flush against the skin with no sharp edges, and the cutter runs smoothly without the high-pitched whine you get from poorly balanced blades.

The real test is how it handles a week of growth. **Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The most commonly cited complaint in the reviews is the lack of a travel cover. The OEM heads typically ship with a plastic cap that protects the foil during storage and travel. OXSITO doesn't include one, which means if you toss your shaver in a gym bag, the foil is exposed to denting. A dented foil will tear your face up — so protect this head or buy a separate cover.

A smaller issue: a handful of buyers noted a slight color mismatch between the replacement's plastic frame and their shaver's housing. It's cosmetic only and doesn't affect function, but if you're particular about your grooming tools looking uniform, that's worth knowing.
