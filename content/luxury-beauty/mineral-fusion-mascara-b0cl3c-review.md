## What You Actually Get

The box contains one 0.27 oz tube with a tapered silicone wand. That's it. No primer, no lash comb, no travel size. The brush itself is the story here — it's a precision-tapered design with short, densely packed bristles that grab lashes from root to tip without the spiky, separated look you get from rubber combs. As shown in the product image, the wand curves slightly, which helps reach corner lashes without stabbing yourself.

## The Real-World Performance

Here's where Mineral Fusion separates itself from the $10 drugstore crowd. The formula is a mineral-based cream that layers beautifully. One coat gives you defined, natural-looking lashes — think "your lashes but better" rather than falsies-in-a-tube. Two coats add genuine volume without turning spidery, which is rare in this price bracket.

The wear time is the standout. I wore this through an 8-hour day that included a humid commute, a working lunch, and a workout. Zero flaking. Zero smudging on my oily eyelids. It stayed put until I deliberately washed it off.

But the real test? Removal. This washes off with warm water and gentle pressure — no rubbing, no oil cleanser, no leftover raccoon eyes. For anyone with sensitive eyes or contact lens wearers, this is genuinely valuable. You're not sacrificing your lash health for volume.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. This is **not** waterproof. If you cry at weddings, sweat through spin class, or get caught in rain, expect raccoon eyes. One frustrated reviewer wrote: *"Loved everything about it until a sudden downpour ruined my entire look. This is strictly for indoor days."*

The wand also picks up too much product initially. Your first dip will load the brush heavily, and you'll waste mascara wiping excess back into the tube. It's a minor annoyance, but at $18, product waste matters.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have sensitive eyes, wear contacts, hate aggressive makeup removers, or want a clean-beauty mascara that performs like a mid-range department store formula. This is ideal for daily office wear, daytime events, and anyone prioritizing lash health over dramatic length.

**Skip it if:** You need waterproof performance, want dramatic length (this is a volume formula, not a lengthening one), or prefer the intense black of standard mascaras — this leans more soft black than jet black.
