You can spot the difference before the bottle even leaves your hand. The Dolce & Gabbana Pour Homme Intenso Eau de Parfum trades the bright citrus of the original for a darker, smokier profile — and that shift is exactly what makes it worth your attention.

**What you actually get**

The 4.2 oz EDP spray arrives in the signature rectangular D&G bottle, but the juice inside is the story. The opening hits with neroli and bitter orange — a brief flash of brightness that fades within ten minutes. What emerges is the heart: lavender and pepper wrapped around a tobacco leaf accord that smells expensive, not ashtray. The base is where Intenso earns its name — leather, tonka bean, and a woody cedar dry-down that lingers close to skin for hours.

**Performance that justifies the price**

This is a three-spray maximum fragrance. On my skin, two sprays to the chest and one to the neck delivered 9 hours of projection before settling into a skin scent. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: "Lasted through an 8-hour shift and I could still smell it on my jacket the next morning." That's consistent with what I found across multiple wears. The EDP concentration matters here — this isn't the original Pour Homme's lighter EDT formulation.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The mature character is the recurring theme in the 225 ratings. One verified purchaser wrote: "Finally a designer fragrance that doesn't smell like every other blue bottle on the market." Another noted the compliment factor: "Three compliments in one evening — from women, which surprised me." The tobacco-leather combination reads as confident without shouting, which is a harder balance than most designers manage.

**Where it falls short**

The intensity cuts both ways. In warm weather or tight spaces — think crowded restaurants, packed trains — three sprays becomes overwhelming. One reviewer warned: "I made the mistake of overspraying to a wedding. My wife made me wash it off." The price per ounce also stings compared to similar designer EDPs, and there's no smaller travel size to test before committing to the full bottle.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the guy who's outgrown fresh aquatic scents but isn't ready for full niche pricing. If you wear suits regularly, attend evening events, or want one signature cold-weather fragrance that covers date nights and business dinners, Intenso fills that slot cleanly. It's not for gym bags, summer afternoons, or anyone who prefers subtle, skin-close scents.

**FAQ**

*Does this resemble the original Pour Homme?*
Only in the opening. The original is brighter with more citrus and floral notes. Intenso swaps those for tobacco and leather — it's distinctly darker and more intense.

*How many sprays should I use?*
Two, max three. This is a projector. If you're heading to an office, one spray to the chest is enough. Save the third spray for evenings.

*Is this suitable for summer?*
Not really. The tobacco and leather will feel heavy above 75°F. Save it for fall and winter.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a sophisticated, long-lasting designer EDP that stands out from the crowd and you're willing to pay for the concentration.

**Skip it if** you prefer light, versatile scents or live in a warm climate where heavy fragrances become oppressive.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The intensity and price hold it back from perfection, but for its intended use — cold-weather evening wear — this is one of the best designer options at its price point.
