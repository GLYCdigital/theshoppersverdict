# EltaMD UV Clear Face Sunscreen SPF, Oil-Free Sunscreen with

## The cult-favorite sunscreen that dermatologists actually recommend — but does it live up to the hype?

If you've ever asked a dermatologist for a sunscreen recommendation, you've heard the name EltaMD UV Clear. It's the sunscreen that dominates "best of" lists year after year, and with 220 Amazon ratings averaging 4.4 stars, it's clearly doing something right. But at a premium price point, is this the sunscreen you should actually be spending your money on? Let's break it down.

## What's in the box

You get one 1.7-ounce tube of EltaMD UV Clear SPF 46. The packaging is clinical and straightforward — a white tube with clean typography that looks like it belongs in a dermatologist's office. There's no fancy box or unnecessary frills, which is appropriate given this is a medical-grade product. The pump dispenser is a thoughtful touch that helps you control how much product you use, though you'll need to be careful toward the end of the tube to get every last drop.

## The formula that changed the sunscreen game

The magic here is in the ingredients. EltaMD UV Clear uses 9% zinc oxide as its primary UV filter, which provides broad-spectrum protection without the chemical sunscreen irritation that plagues sensitive skin. But what really sets this apart is the 5% niacinamide (vitamin B3) — the same ingredient found in high-end serums — which helps calm inflammation, fade dark spots, and regulate oil production.

The texture is where this sunscreen truly shines. As shown in the product image, it's a lightweight lotion that applies almost like a moisturizer. It's not watery, not thick — just the right consistency to spread evenly across your face. Within 30 seconds, it absorbs completely with zero white cast, even on deeper skin tones. That's a claim most mineral sunscreens can't make.

## Performance in the real world

I wore this daily for two weeks under makeup and alone. Under foundation, it performs beautifully — no pilling, no separation, and it actually helps makeup stay put longer. On bare skin, it leaves a natural, slightly dewy finish that isn't greasy or shiny. Oily skin types will appreciate that it doesn't add excess shine, and dry skin types won't feel tight or stripped.

One reviewer noted, "I've tried every sunscreen on the market for my acne-prone skin and this is the only one that doesn't break me out or make me look like a ghost." That sentiment echoes across hundreds of reviews from people with sensitive, reactive skin who've found their holy grail.

## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the price — this is a luxury sunscreen at around $40 for 1.7 ounces. You'll go through it in about 6-8 weeks with daily use, which adds up. Second, it's not water-resistant, so if you're sweating at the gym or swimming, this isn't your sunscreen. And third, some users report pilling when using it over thick moisturizers or certain serums — you'll want to give your skincare a few minutes to absorb before applying this.

## Who should buy this

- **Acne sufferers**: If you break out from every sunscreen you try, the niacinamide and oil-free formula makes this worth every penny
- **Rosacea warriors**: The calming ingredients and gentle zinc won't trigger flare-ups
- **Makeup wearers**: It's one of the few sunscreens that works flawlessly under foundation
- **Those with hyperpigmentation**: Niacinamide helps fade dark spots while you protect your skin

Skip it if you're on a strict budget, need a sport or beach sunscreen, or prefer a matte finish.

## FAQ

**Q: Is EltaMD UV Clear good for oily skin?**
A: Yes — it's oil-free and contains niacinamide, which helps regulate sebum production. Many users with oily skin report it doesn't make them greasier throughout the day.

**Q: Does it leave a white cast on dark skin?**
A: No. The zinc oxide is micronized finely enough that it disappears completely on all skin tones. This is one of the major reasons it's so popular.

**Q: Can I wear this under makeup?**
A: Absolutely. It acts as a great primer — just wait 2-3 minutes after applying before layering foundation.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you have sensitive, acne-prone skin and haven't found a sunscreen that works without breaking you out or leaving a white cast. It's expensive, but it's genuinely the best option for this specific skin type.

**Skip it if** you need a budget option, want a water-resistant formula, or prefer a completely matte finish.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — loses points for the price and lack of water resistance, but gains them back for being the most cosmetically elegant mineral sunscreen on the market.
