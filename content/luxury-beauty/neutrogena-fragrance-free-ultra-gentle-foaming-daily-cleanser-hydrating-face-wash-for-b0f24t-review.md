**The 4.6-star cleanser that actually respects your skin barrier — most of the time.**

Here's the thing about foaming cleansers: they're usually either too harsh (that squeaky-clean feeling is your skin screaming) or too creamy (leaving a film that makes you wonder if you actually washed your face). The Neutrogena Fragrance Free Ultra Gentle Foaming Daily Cleanse aims for the middle ground, and for most skin types, it lands there. With 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, it's clearly working for a lot of people. But is it the right cleanser for *you*? Let's break it down.

**What you actually get**

The product arrives as a 6.7-ounce pump bottle — no box frills, no spatula, no nonsense. The pump is the star here: it dispenses a light, airy foam that's already activated, so you don't need to work it up in your hands. One pump yields about a nickel-sized dollop of foam; two pumps give you full-face coverage. The texture is genuinely pleasant — think mousse, not shaving cream. As shown in the product image, the formula is a clean, translucent white foam with zero tint.

**Performance and real-world use**

This cleanser is formulated with glycerin as a key humectant, which explains why it doesn't leave that tight, stripped feeling. In my testing, it removed everyday dirt, sweat, and light SPF well. Rinsing is quick — no residue, no film, no "did I get it all?" uncertainty. Your skin feels clean but supple afterward, and you can go a few minutes before needing moisturizer without that uncomfortable pull.

The fragrance-free claim is legit — there's no perfume, and importantly, no masking scent to cover up chemical odors. For anyone with reactive skin or fragrance sensitivities, this is a meaningful differentiator. Several reviewers noted this specifically, with one writing: *"Finally a foaming cleanser that doesn't make my rosacea flare. I've repurchased three times."*

**Where it falls short — be honest with yourself**

The foam formula has inherent limitations. It will not remove heavy makeup, waterproof mascara, or water-resistant zinc-based sunscreens. If you wear any of these, you need a double-cleanse approach — oil or balm first, then this. That's not a flaw unique to Neutrogena, but it's worth stating plainly.

The other consideration: the foam is airy, which means some users find it doesn't "grip" the skin the way a gel or cream cleanser does. If you're used to a denser texture, this might feel insubstantial at first. A couple of reviewers mentioned needing extra pumps to feel adequately cleansed — a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

**Who should buy this**

- **Sensitive-skin sufferers**: If your face reacts to everything, this is a safe bet. Dermatologist-tested, fragrance-free, non-comedogenic.
- **Morning washers**: Light enough for AM cleansing without over-stripping overnight oils.
- **Second-cleansers**: If you already use an oil cleanser at night, this is an excellent follow-up.
- **Eczema-prone adults**: The glycerin base is gentle on compromised barriers.

**Skip it if** you wear heavy makeup daily (you'll need a dedicated remover anyway), or if you prefer a cream cleanser texture that feels more substantial on the skin.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this good for acne-prone skin?**
A: Yes, generally. It's non-comedogenic and won't clog pores. However, it doesn't contain active acne ingredients like salicylic acid — it's a cleanser, not a treatment. Pair it with a separate acne treatment if needed.

**Q: How does this compare to the original Neutrogena Ultra Gentle (creamy) formula?**
A: The foaming version feels lighter and rinses cleaner. The creamy version is better for very dry skin; the foam works better for normal-to-combination skin or as a second cleanse.

**Q: Can I use this on my eyes?**
A: It's gentle enough that it won't sting if it gets in your eyes, but it's not an eye makeup remover. Remove eye makeup separately first.

**Skip it if** you need a one-step makeup remover or prefer a richer cream texture.
