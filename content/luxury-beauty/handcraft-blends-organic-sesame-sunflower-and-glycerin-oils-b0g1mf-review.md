Let's be honest: most "organic oil" sets on Amazon are one decent oil padded with two bottles of filler. This Handcraft Blends trio isn't that. You get three genuinely different products — organic sesame oil, organic sunflower oil, and vegetable glycerin — each in its own 8-ounce amber glass bottle. That matters more than you'd think.

## What's Actually in the Box

Three 8 oz amber glass bottles (which protect the oils from light degradation — a detail cheap plastic brands skip). The sesame and sunflower oils are cold-pressed and unrefined. The glycerin is vegetable-derived, USP-grade, and kosher. Everything is USDA-certified organic, and there are no added fragrances, preservatives, or synthetic additives. As shown in the product image, the labels are clean and minimal, and the amber glass feels substantial — not like something that'll shatter in your shower caddy.

## How They Perform

**Sesame oil** is the heaviest of the three. It's rich and slightly warming — traditional Ayurvedic massage oil territory. It absorbs in about 10-15 minutes and leaves a protective layer rather than a greasy mess. If you do self-massage or use oil pulling, this is your workhorse.

**Sunflower oil** is the surprise standout. It's surprisingly light — thinner than jojoba, faster-absorbing than coconut. It's high in linoleic acid, which makes it excellent for acne-prone or combination skin that reacts badly to heavier oils. I'd argue this alone is worth the price of the set.

**Glycerin** is where people get confused. It's a humectant, not a moisturizer. Applied alone, it's thick, sticky, and pulls moisture *from* your skin rather than adding it. You must dilute it — mix a few drops with water or add it to your lotion. Used correctly, it's one of the most effective hydrating ingredients available. Used incorrectly, you'll wonder why anyone buys this stuff.

## Where It Falls Short

The glycerin bottle has no pump or dropper — just a wide-mouth opening. Given how viscous glycerin is, you'll likely over-pour and waste product. That's a design flaw, not a dealbreaker.

The sesame oil's aroma is potent. If you're sensitive to smells, it's not going to fade fast. It's earthy, slightly nutty, and unmistakably sesame. Some reviewers love it; others found it overwhelming.

And because there's no pump on the glycerin, the three-bottle system isn't as cohesive as it could be — different caps, different dispensing methods, different viscosities. It's a minor annoyance, but worth knowing.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use the glycerin directly on my face?**
No. It must be diluted. Mix 1 part glycerin with 3-4 parts water or add a few drops to your moisturizer. Applied alone, it can actually dehydrate skin.

**Q: Is the sesame oil refined or will it smell strong?**
It's unrefined and cold-pressed, so it retains a strong, nutty sesame scent. Some people love it for that reason; if you hate sesame aroma, this isn't for you.

**Q: Are these oils safe for oil pulling?**
Yes. The sesame oil is food-grade and unrefined, which is exactly what you want for oil pulling. The sunflower oil works too, though it's thinner.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a versatile, high-quality oil system for skincare, massage, and oil pulling — the sunflower oil alone justifies the price.

**Skip it if** you're sensitive to strong scents, or you want a single simple moisturizer rather than three products that require learning how to use them.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — the quality is undeniable, and the minor packaging gripes don't outweigh the value of three genuinely organic, cold-pressed oils in one purchase.
