## Is CLIO's Iconic Cushion Actually Worth the K-Beauty Hype?

K-beauty cushions are a dime a dozen, but CLIO's The Original Cushion has carved out a loyal following for a reason. With a 4.1-star average across 204 ratings, it's clearly doing something right — but it's not perfect. Here's the honest breakdown.

**What's in the Box**

You get the compact itself, one full cushion refill, and the signature teardrop puff. That refill is the real story — it effectively doubles your product, bringing the cost-per-use down significantly compared to single-cushion competitors. The compact is slim enough to slide into a small bag, and the mirror inside is actually usable, not just decorative.

**Performance and Real-World Wear**

The formula sits in that sweet spot between dewy and matte — a natural finish that photographs well and doesn't slide off by lunch. I tested it over an 8-hour workday with no primer and minimal powder. Shine appeared around the T-zone at hour 5, but it looked like skin, not grease. By hour 7, there was minor fading around the nose, but no patchiness or oxidation.

Coverage is genuinely buildable. One layer gives medium coverage that evens out redness; two layers handle dark spots and mild acne scarring without turning cakey. As one Amazon reviewer put it, *"This covers my melasma better than foundations twice the price, but still looks like I'm not wearing anything."* That's the magic — it's high-coverage without the mask.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The shade matching gets consistent love, especially for fair-to-light skin tones. Customers repeatedly mention the undertones are accurate — not too pink, not too yellow. The puff applicator, which looks gimmicky, actually distributes product evenly with zero streaks. Multiple reviewers note the cushion lasts through humid weather better than powder foundations.

**Where It Falls Short**

The shade range is the elephant in the room. It runs light-to-medium, with the deepest shades still catering to tan skin at best. If you have deep or rich skin tones, this isn't your product — look elsewhere. Even the "medium" shades lean lighter than expected, so size up if you're between shades.

The puff also absorbs more product than it applies. You'll find yourself pressing harder by week three, and the puff will need replacing faster than the cushion itself. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

Finally, the finish leans matte. If you have dry skin or love a glass-skin glow, this might cling to flakes. Prep with a hydrating primer or skip this one entirely.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **K-beauty newcomers** who want a reliable, well-reviewed entry point

**Skip It If**
