Let’s be honest: clear brow gel is one of those products where drugstore options usually make you choose between hold and comfort. NYX Control Freak claims to deliver both without the $24 price tag of the prestige brands. After testing it against three competitors and combing through 215 Amazon ratings, here’s the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The product arrives in a slim 0.27-fluid-ounce tube with a clear, dual-sided wand. One side has longer, spaced-out bristles for combing through the bulk of your brow; the flip side is shorter and dense, which is where the precision work happens. The gel itself is completely transparent in the tube and stays that way on skin. No tint, no fibers, no filler — just hold.

## Performance and Build Quality

This is where Control Freak earns its name. The formula sets fairly quickly — about 30 to 45 seconds — so you need to work fast. Once dry, your brows are locked. I tested it through a humid afternoon, a workout, and a full workday. My brows stayed groomed for roughly eight hours before any noticeable droop. 

The texture is worth discussing honestly. One coat gives a flexible, natural-looking hold. Two coats — or a heavy hand — and you’ll feel stiffness. A few Amazon reviewers echoed this. One wrote, *“Great hold but if you layer it, it gets crunchy. One pass is all you need.”* That matches my experience exactly.

The wand design is genuinely thoughtful. The longer side combs through unruly brow hairs without dragging the skin, and the shorter side tames the finer hairs at the head of the brow. That level of control is rare at this price point.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.3-star average (215 ratings) reflects a mostly satisfied crowd. The most frequent praise in customer feedback centers on three things:

- **No flakes.** Unlike many clear gels, this doesn't crumble into white specks as it wears. Multiple reviewers specifically noted wearing it overnight without residue.
- **Budget value.** At roughly $7 to $8, it costs less than a third of popular prestige options while delivering comparable hold.
- **Works on all brow types.** From sparse, fine brows to thick, unruly ones, reviewers report success across the board.

## Where It Falls Short

The stiffness issue is the main tradeoff. If you prefer a soft, brushed-up "laminated" look, this might feel too rigid on one coat — and two coats pushes into helmet territory. 

The tube size is also modest. At 0.27 ounces, daily users report running out in six to eight weeks. Compare that to similar-priced competitors offering 0.34 ounces, and the cost-per-use starts to equalize. 

Finally, the formula doesn't play well with powders. If you apply brow powder first, the gel can create a slightly tacky film that pulls color when you brush through. Apply the gel first, then powder, and you'll avoid this entirely.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The minimal-routine minimalist** who wants one product to groom and hold without fuss.
- **The oily-skin sufferer** who needs a gel that won't slide off by 2 PM.
- **The budget-conscious brow enthusiast** who refuses to pay $20+ for what is essentially clear glue.

Skip it if you prefer a featherlight, touchable finish or if you always layer products over your brow gel.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work for unruly, thick brows?**
A: Yes — the longer bristles on the wand are designed to flatten and direct stubborn hairs. One coat holds even coarse hairs in place for most of the day.

**Q: Is it truly clear, or does it leave residue?**
A: It dries completely transparent with a slight shine. No white cast, even on darker skin tones. Just don't layer it — that's when it can look tacky.

**Q: How do I remove it?**
A: Warm water and gentle cleanser take it off easily. No oil-based remover needed, though a micellar water works well if you're wearing waterproof makeup.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, flake-free clear brow gel that holds all day without breaking the bank. It's a genuinely well-designed product that outperforms its price point.

**Skip it if** you need a soft, flexible finish or want a larger tube for the money — or if you consistently wear brow powder and don't want to change your application order.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Impressive hold, smart wand design, and honest pricing. Just use a light hand and manage your expectations on tube longevity.
