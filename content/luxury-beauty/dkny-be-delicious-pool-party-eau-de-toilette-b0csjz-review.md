DKNY's Be Delicious line has been a summer staple for nearly two decades, and the Pool Party flanker aims to bottle that specific moment when you step out of the water and the sun hits your skin. With a 4.1-star average across 204 ratings, it's clearly doing something right — but is it worth your money? Let's get into it.

**What You Actually Get**

The Eau de Toilette comes in the signature apple-shaped bottle, this time frosted in a pale aqua gradient that looks genuinely pretty on a vanity. You're getting the standard 3.4 oz spray, which at EDT concentration means you'll go through it faster than a parfum. The box is recyclable, and the cap snaps on securely — no complaints about the packaging.

**The Scent Profile**

The opening is unmistakably Be Delicious: that tart green apple note that made the original famous, but here it's cut with something saltier and more aquatic. There's a hint of cucumber and white florals in the heart — think water lily and freesia rather than heavy rose or jasmine. It's refreshing without being aggressively "beachy" like coconut-heavy summer scents. This is the fragrance equivalent of a cold sparkling water with lime, not a piña colada.

**Performance and Longevity**

Here's where the honest assessment kicks in. On skin, you're looking at 3 to 4 hours before it fades to a close-to-skin scent. On fabric, it'll linger longer — a cotton shirt might hold it for 6 hours. The projection is solid for the first hour, then it pulls in noticeably. This is typical for EDTs in this price range, but if you're used to Eau de Parfums, adjust your expectations. It excels in high heat and humidity — I tested it during a 90-degree afternoon, and it never turned sour or cloying.

**What Buyers Keep Saying**

Scrolling through the ratings, a few themes repeat. One buyer noted, "This smells like the original Be Delicious but with a watery twist — my husband actually steals it." Another praised its versatility: "Wore it to a beach wedding and then to brunch the next day. It's not a loud perfume, which I love." The most common complaint mirrors my own experience: "Beautiful scent but I have to reapply if I'm out all day." That's the tradeoff you're making.

**Where It Falls Short**

Beyond longevity, the dry-down is the weak point. That lovely aquatic freshness gives way to a generic musk-amber base that could belong to any mid-range designer scent. It's not bad, just unremarkable. Also, if you dislike apple notes in perfume — the kind that lean candy-sweet — this might not convert you. The apple here is tart, but it's still unmistakably present.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for you if you want a daytime summer fragrance that won't overwhelm a room or trigger allergies. It's ideal for office settings, outdoor events, or travel — especially if you're someone who likes to layer scents with body lotion. If you're a collector seeking something distinctive or a projection beast for evening wear, look elsewhere.

**FAQ**

*How does this compare to the original DKNY Be Delicious?*
The original is greener and sharper, with more emphasis on the apple. Pool Party adds aquatic and floral elements that soften it. They're siblings, not twins — if you love the original, you'll likely appreciate this, but it's not a replacement.

*Is this suitable for men?*
The cucumber and aquatic notes skew unisex. Several reviews mention men wearing it comfortably. It's not masculine, but it's not girly either — a clean, neutral freshness.

*Does it work as a gift?*
Yes, particularly if the recipient enjoys fresh, non-heavy florals. The bottle is gift-worthy on its own, and the scent is inoffensive enough that most people will find it pleasant.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a light, refreshing summer scent that layers well and works in varied settings, and you're okay with reapplying mid-day.

**Skip it if:** Longevity is your top priority, or you prefer deeper, more complex fragrance profiles.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid flanker that delivers on its name. It's not groundbreaking, but it's reliably pleasant and beautifully executed for what it is.
