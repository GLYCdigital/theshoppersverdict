At $6.42, you'd expect a grab bag of misshapen plastic. The BELLEBOOST jelly rhinestones are not that. This 12-color set of flat-backed gems has earned a 4.7-star average from 235 buyers — and the consistency of sizing is the first thing you'll notice. Each stone in the batch I examined matched its color-mates within fractions of a millimeter. That matters when you're laying a gradient down a nail bed or framing a resin coaster.

## What's actually in the box

You get 12 individual color pots, each holding roughly 120 stones — about 1,440 total. The colors run from clear crystal and light pink through deeper sapphire and emerald jelly tones. These are flat-back, not round cabochons, so they're designed for adhesive application on flat or slightly curved surfaces. The "jelly" descriptor is accurate: they're translucent with a colored tint, closer to gummy candy than standard glass rhinestones.

The case is a clear plastic organizer with flip-top lids per compartment. It's functional, not fancy — the hinges feel light, but the separate compartments mean you're not dumping all colors together to search for one shade.

## How they perform in real use

**On nails:** These sit flush. The flat back eliminates the rolling problem that round-backed stones have during placement. With nail glue or gel topcoat, they held through a week of dishwashing and typing in my testing. The jelly finish refracts light in a softer, more diffused way than standard crystal — it reads expensive, not costume-y.

**On crafts:** I tested them on cardstock with craft glue and on a resin bookmark. They adhered cleanly and the uniform height creates an even surface when layering. The translucent nature means they pick up the color underneath — white or light backgrounds make them pop; dark backgrounds mute them significantly.

## What buyers consistently praise

The phrase that keeps appearing in verified reviews is "exactly as pictured." One buyer wrote, *"I've ordered cheap rhinestones before and gotten half-empty bags with broken stones. These were full, organized, and every single stone was usable."* Another noted the color accuracy: *"The sapphire is a true deep blue, not a muddy purple like some sets."* Durability gets called out too — stones don't crack when applied with pressure, which is rare at this price point.

## Where it falls short

Two honest limitations. First, no applicator tool. At this price, you're supplying your own tweezers or wax pencil — fine if you have them, annoying if you're a first-timer who hasn't built a toolkit yet. Second, the lighter jelly shades (clear, light pink, pale yellow) nearly disappear on dark polish or dark paper. They need a light base to show their color. If you work mostly on black or navy, prioritize the deeper shades in this set.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** You do nail art regularly and want a reliable flat-back stone that won't roll during placement. You craft with light or medium-colored materials. You want multiple colors without committing to full-size containers of each.

**Skip it if:** You need a single color in bulk — this is a variety set, not a refill pack. You work exclusively on dark backgrounds. You expect professional-grade Swarovski sparkle; these are good, but they're not Austrian crystal.

## FAQ

**Q: How many stones are actually in each color pot?**
Roughly 120 per compartment, though smaller shades like clear and pink tend to run slightly higher. Total is around 1,400-1,500 pieces.

**Q: Will these work with gel nail polish?**
Yes — apply them into the tacky gel layer before curing, then seal with topcoat. They hold firmly and won't shift during curing.

**Q: Are they safe for kids' crafts?**
They're small enough to be a choking hazard under age 5. For older kids doing supervised projects, the flat backs make them easier to handle than round beads.

The BELLEBOOST jelly rhinestones deliver what the listing promises: consistent size, accurate colors, and a finish that looks more premium than the price suggests.
