## The $10 Primer That Thinks It's Luxury

Here's the thing about the **e.l.f. Power Grip Primer**: it's not trying to be the most elegant product you'll ever touch. It's trying to be the most *effective* one. And at 4.6 stars from 229 Amazon ratings, the crowd seems to agree. This is the primer that made "grippy" a mainstream beauty term—and for good reason.

The concept is simple: instead of smoothing and blurring like a silicone-heavy primer, Power Grip uses a jelly-like, water-based formula that creates a **tacky surface** for makeup to literally stick to. Think of it as double-sided tape for your foundation.

## What You Actually Get

The 1.35 oz tube is straightforward—no fancy packaging, no mirror, no drama. What you do get is a **hygienic pump dispenser** (a genuine win over the pot-style primers that collect bacteria and dry out). One pump delivers roughly a pea-sized amount, which is exactly what you need for your T-zone. The formula itself is a clear, thick gel with a slight bounce to it—like a less-sweet gummy bear.

## Performance: The Real Story

Here's where this primer earns its keep. Applied to **damp skin** (this matters—more on that below), the Power Grip creates an immediate, almost uncomfortable tackiness. That's not a bug; it's the feature. Your foundation will not move.

I tested this with a lightweight tinted moisturizer and a full-coverage matte foundation. Both locked in place for a solid 8-hour workday. The tackiness fades after about 2-3 minutes as the gel dries down, leaving a slightly hydrated, grippy surface.

The formula also includes **niacinamide**, a skincare ingredient that helps with redness and pores. It's not a skincare treatment—don't expect miracles—but it's a nice bonus in a $10-12 product.

## What Buyers Are Saying

The 229 ratings at 4.6/5 tell a consistent story. One verified buyer wrote: *"I have oily skin and this is the ONLY primer that keeps my makeup on through a 12-hour shift. It feels weird at first but the payoff is real."*

Another echoed the sticky-then-smooth transition: *"The first 60 seconds feel like I glued my face, but once it sets, my makeup looks airbrushed."*

The criticism is equally consistent—and legitimate.

## Where It Falls Short

**The tacky phase is a real adjustment.** If you're someone who applies makeup in under 5 minutes, the initial stickiness will test your patience. You need to wait for it to set, or you'll be dragging your foundation across a gummy surface.

**Pilling is a genuine risk.** This primer does not play well with certain skincare. If you layer it over a water-based moisturizer that hasn't fully absorbed, you'll get little balls of product rolling off your face. The fix is easy—wait 60 seconds after moisturizer, or use a gel-based moisturizer—but it's a compatibility issue worth knowing.

**It's not for dry skin.** If you have flaky patches, this primer will emphasize them. The grip works best on smooth, hydrated skin. Dry-skinned users report the tackiness never quite settles, and foundation can look patchy.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Oily and combination skin types**—this is your holy grail for long-wear makeup
- **Gym-goers and commuters**—makeup stays put through sweat and humidity

## Skip It If...

- You have **dry, flaky skin** and prefer a hydrating, smoothing primer
- You're looking for **pore-filling or blurring** effects—this doesn't do that
- You hate waiting 2-3 minutes between skincare and makeup

## FAQ

**Q: Does the e.l.f. Power Grip Primer work with all foundations?**
A: It works best with water-based and liquid foundations. Powder or cream-to-powder formulas may not benefit as much from the grip.

**Q: How much should I use?**
A: One pump for your T-zone, two for full face. More than that and you'll feel like your face is in a spider web.

**Q: Is this primer cruelty-free?**
