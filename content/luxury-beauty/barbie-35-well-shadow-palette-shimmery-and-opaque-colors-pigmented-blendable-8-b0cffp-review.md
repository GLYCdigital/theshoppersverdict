You've seen "35-color palettes" before. Usually that means 30 shades of muddy brown and three chalky pinks that all blend into the same gray smear. The Barbie 35-Well Shadow Palette is not that. This one actually earns its 4.8-star rating across 240 Amazon reviews — and after breaking down what's inside, I get why.

**What you're actually getting**

The palette measures roughly 8 inches across and holds 35 individual wells of pressed shadow. The finishes split into three distinct groups: a row of true mattes (including a workable transition shade, not just black and white), a center section of high-shimmer metallics, and a set of opaque satin shades in the Barbie signature pinks and purples. The color story leans warm — think rose gold, coral, mauve, and bronze — with a few cool-toned pops for contrast.

As shown in the product image, the layout groups shades by finish rather than scattering them randomly. That matters more than you'd think. You can plan a look without playing a matching game across the case.

**Performance and real-world use**

I tested the pigmentation on bare skin first. Most shades delivered opaque color in one pass. The mattes are the standout — they're soft without being crumbly, and they blend without turning patchy. The shimmers have a wet-look finish that reads more premium than the price suggests. One buyer noted, "I was shocked at how creamy these are. I've paid triple for palettes that perform worse."

The caveat comes with the lightest shimmer shades. On bare lids, expect some fallout within a few hours. It's manageable — a primer fixes it — but it's worth knowing before you buy. The darker mattes and metallics held up through a full workday without creasing on my test.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The shade range is the recurring theme in positive reviews. One verified purchase wrote, "Finally a palette where every single color is actually usable. No fillers." Another highlighted the versatility: "I've done five completely different looks in a week and haven't repeated a shade."

The mirror also gets consistent mentions. It's large enough to do a full face, not just a touch-up, and the case hinges open flat so you can set it on a counter without holding it.

**Where it falls short**

No brush included. At this price point, that's a real miss — you're spending money on the product, not the tools, but you'll need to own brushes already. Also, the plastic case feels sturdy but not luxurious. It won't crack in transit, but it won't win any awards on your vanity either.

**Who should buy this**

- Makeup beginners who want one palette that covers every occasion — work, weekend, night out
- Barbie collectors who want a functional piece, not just a shelf display
- Travelers who want a single compact with a mirror and enough shades to skip packing a second palette

**Skip it if** you prefer curated, smaller palettes with a specific color story, or if you exclusively use high-end packaging and don't mind paying for it.

**FAQ**

*Is this palette cruelty-free?* The brand doesn't make explicit cruelty-free claims on the packaging. If that's a hard requirement, check the current listing details before purchasing.

*How does it compare to the Morphe 35-shade palettes?* The Barbie palette has better pigment density in the mattes and a more cohesive color story. Morphe offers more variety in undertones, but the Barbie formula is more consistently blendable across all 35 shades.

*Will the shimmer shades work on deep skin tones?* The metallics and deeper pinks yes — they have strong opacity. The three lightest shimmers will read as highlight shades rather than lid colors on deeper complexions.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a versatile, high-performing palette with genuine shade variety and you already own brushes. **Skip it if** you need included tools or prefer a smaller, more curated edit.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — the missing brush and minor fallout on light shimmers keep it from perfection, but the formula quality and shade range make this a genuinely smart buy.
