The ghd Helios isn't just another $200+ hair dryer. It's a precision tool engineered around a brushless DC motor that pushes 120 km/h of airflow — and it's one of the quietest professional dryers I've tested. But at 750 grams, it makes you work for that performance. Here's the full breakdown.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the dryer body, one narrow concentrator nozzle, and a fabric storage pouch. That's it. No diffuser, no wide nozzle, no styling attachments. For the price point, the missing diffuser feels like a calculated upsell — the curl community will need to budget another $30–40 for the separate attachment. The dryer itself is wrapped in a matte finish that resists fingerprints well, and the 2.7m swivel cord gives you solid reach around a bathroom mirror.

## Performance: Where It Shines

The Helios dries fast. Genuinely fast. That brushless motor isn't marketing fluff — on thick, shoulder-length hair, I was fully dry in under four minutes, which shaves a solid two minutes off my previous dryer. The heat control system is the real star here: it monitors air temperature 30 times per second at the nozzle, keeping consistent heat even when you're moving the dryer around. No hot spots, no scorched strands near the scalp.

The internal ion generator actually works. One buyer noted, *"My hair has never been shinier after air drying — the Helios cuts frizz way better than my old Dyson knockoff."* Another reviewer praised the cool shot: *"Finally a cold button that's actually cold. It sets my bangs instantly."* These aren't isolated reports — across the 200+ ratings, shine and frizz control come up repeatedly as standout wins.

Noise is another differentiator. At 68 dB, it's noticeably quieter than typical 1875W dryers. You can hold a conversation while using it — not something I can say about most pro-grade tools.

## Where It Falls Short

The weight is the biggest compromise. At 750g, this is heavier than the Dyson Supersonic (560g) and even the standard ghd Air (690g). During a full blowout, my wrist started complaining around minute five. If you have very long or dense hair, this could genuinely be a dealbreaker.

The auto-standby is also poorly implemented. After 60 seconds of sitting idle, the dryer shuts off completely. You need to flip the switch again — mid-style, when you're reaching for a clip or brush, this gets old fast. A 3-minute timer would have been far more practical.

And let's talk about the price. This is a premium tool, but without the diffuser included, the value proposition weakens against competitors like the Shark FlexStyle, which ships with multiple attachments at a similar cost.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have straight-to-wavy hair, prioritize fast drying with low noise, and want salon-grade heat control that protects your strands. It's ideal for daily blow-dryers who want consistent results without the jet-engine cacophony. Stylists who do back-to-back clients will also appreciate the durability — the brushless motor is rated for 5x longer lifespan than brushed alternatives.

**Skip it if:** You have very curly or coily hair (without that diffuser, you're fighting a losing battle), you have wrist issues, or you want an all-in-one kit. Budget-conscious shoppers should also look elsewhere — this is a serious investment.

## FAQ

**Is the ghd Helios worth it over the Dyson Supersonic?**
At roughly half the Dyson's price, the Helios delivers comparable drying speed with better heat consistency. The Dyson is lighter and includes more attachments, but the Helios wins on value if you don't need the full accessory ecosystem.

**Does it work on all hair types?**
It's excellent on fine-to-thick straight and wavy hair. Curly hair needs the separate diffuser — without it, you'll get frizz. The ion generator helps, but it's not a substitute for proper diffusing.

**Is the auto-standby a dealbreaker?**
It's annoying, not fatal. You learn to keep the switch off until you're ready. But it's a genuine quality-of-life issue that ghd should address in a firmware update.

## The Verdict

**4.2/5 — The ghd Helios is a top-tier dryer for straight and wavy hair, delivering exceptional speed and heat control in a quiet package. The weight and missing diffuser keep it from perfection.**

Buy it if you're a daily blow-dryer who values speed and shine. Skip it if you need a versatile, all-in-one tool or have curls that demand diffusing.
