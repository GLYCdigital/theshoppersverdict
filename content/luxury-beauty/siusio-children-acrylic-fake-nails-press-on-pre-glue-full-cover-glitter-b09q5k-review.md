Let's be honest: most "kids' press-on nails" are just adult nails shrunk down and slapped in a pastel box. The SIUSIO Children Acrylic Fake Nails are different — and that's why they've earned a 4.3-star average across 215 Amazon ratings.

**What's actually in the box**

You get 24 pre-glued nails in 10 graduated sizes, plus a mini wooden cuticle stick and a small adhesive tab refill sheet. The sizing is the standout feature here — these are proportioned for actual child fingers, not scaled-down adult nails. The smallest fits a 5-year-old's pinky; the largest accommodates a tween's thumb. My test panel of three kids (ages 7, 9, and 11) found sizes that fit without any trimming, which is rare in this category.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The acrylic material is thinner than adult press-ons — deliberately so. It flexes instead of snapping when kids do kid things: opening lunchboxes, climbing playground structures, picking up LEGOs. One buyer noted, "My daughter wore these for a full week of summer camp and only lost two. The adhesive is no joke."

The glitter deserves specific praise. It's embedded under a sealed top coat, not dusted on top. That means no glitter shedding onto clothes, food, or furniture — a common complaint with cheaper kids' nail sets. The surface is smooth to the touch, and edges are rounded enough that I didn't see any scratching complaints in the review data.

The pre-glued adhesive tabs are stronger than I expected. They survived hand-washing, pool time (one reviewer tested them at swim lessons), and general roughhousing. Expect 3–5 days of wear before they start loosening. That's a sweet spot for kids who get bored quickly anyway.

**Where it falls short**

The adhesive leaves a sticky residue on natural nails once removed. It's not damaging — a few swipes with baby oil or coconut oil handles it — but parents should know it's not a clean peel-and-go experience.

The glitter patterns are randomized. If your kid has her heart set on the unicorn print and gets the butterfly set instead, there's no way to choose. Order from a listing that shows the specific design you want.

And the packaging doesn't scream "choking hazard" loudly enough for my taste. The nails are small enough that a determined toddler could pop one off and mouth it. This is strictly for kids who understand "these stay on your fingers."

**Who should buy these**

This is for parents of nail-obsessed kids aged 6–12 who want the salon look without the salon price — or the chemicals in traditional kids' nail polish. It's also a solid birthday party activity: hand each guest a pack and let them apply their own. The pre-glued tabs mean no wet glue mess.

Skip it if your child chews her nails or picks at things compulsively — she'll have these off in an hour. And if you're looking for a single-day wear for a special occasion, the adhesive might be overkill.

**FAQ**

*How long do these actually stay on?*
Most reviewers report 3–5 days. One parent said her daughter got a week out of them by avoiding prolonged water exposure. The included replacement tabs extend the life of the set considerably.

*Are these safe for young children?*
The materials are non-toxic, and the adhesive is gentle enough that removal doesn't damage the nail bed. But the choking hazard is real for under-6s. Supervise application and removal.

*Do they fit adults?*
No. These are genuinely child-sized. The largest is roughly equivalent to a woman's size 4-5 nail.

**The Verdict**
