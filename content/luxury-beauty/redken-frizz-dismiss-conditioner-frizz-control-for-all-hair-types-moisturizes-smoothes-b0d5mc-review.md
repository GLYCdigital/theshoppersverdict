**This conditioner earns its keep — if you can handle the perfume.**

Frizz is the great equalizer. Whether you've got 2A waves or 4C coils, humidity turns polished hair into a puffball. Redken's Frizz Dismiss line has been a salon staple for years, and this conditioner is the step most people skip — which is a mistake. Here's the real talk after digging through 225 ratings and the actual formulation.

**What you're actually getting**

The bottle is 8.5 fluid ounces of thick, pearl-white conditioner. The star ingredients are a smoothing complex built around babassu oil and a cationic polymer system that lays down the cuticle rather than just coating it. There's no sulfate in the formula, which matters if you've got color or keratin treatments. As shown in the product image, it's a straightforward pump-top bottle — nothing fancy, but the cap locks for travel.

**How it performs in the real world**

The texture is denser than your average conditioner — think hair mask territory. You need less than you think; a quarter-sized amount covers shoulder-length hair. Work it through from mid-length to ends, leave it for two minutes, and rinse thoroughly. That last part is non-negotiable.

The results are genuinely impressive. Multiple buyers note the smoothing effect holds. One reviewer wrote, *"I live in Florida and this is the only conditioner that keeps my hair from turning into a Brillo pad by noon."* Another said, *"My stylist asked if I got a glaze — nope, just switched conditioners."* That tracks with the polymer technology; it's actually sealing the cuticle, not just masking frizz with silicones.

For curly hair, it defines without crunch. For fine, straight hair, it adds slip without flattening roots — as long as you don't over-apply. Color-treated hair? The absence of sulfates means your dye job stays put longer.

**Where it disappoints**

The fragrance is the biggest complaint — and it's a legitimate one. It's a strong, powdery-floral scent that lingers on your hair for hours. Several reviewers compared it to *"grandma's perfume"* or noted it triggered headaches. If you're scent-sensitive, this is a real problem, not a nitpick.

Second, the price. At roughly $1.50 per ounce, you're paying salon-brand markup. There are cheaper frizz fighters, but few with this level of cuticle-smoothing technology. Finally, the thick formula means buildup if you're lazy about rinsing — fine hair especially needs that extra 30 seconds in the shower.

**Who should buy this**

- **The humidity warrior**: Anyone in a high-moisture climate who's tired of reapplying serums by lunchtime
- **The color-treated:** If you've spent money on highlights or balayage, this protects that investment

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this daily?**
Yes, but if your hair is fine, alternate with a lighter conditioner every other wash to prevent buildup.

**Q: Does it work on dry, damaged hair too?**

**Q: Is it safe for keratin-treated hair?**
Yes — no sulfates or sodium chloride, so it won't strip your treatment.
