## The Good Stuff

### Mouse Tracking

This is where the YSAGi pad really shines for daily use. Using a mouse directly on a bare desk — especially a glass or glossy laminate surface — can be a frustrating experience. Tracking gets jittery, precision feels off, and you end up compensating with higher DPI settings or awkward arm positioning.

The YSAGi pad gives you a massive, consistent mouse surface. Optical and laser sensors track smoothly across the entire area. Whether I was doing precision graphic work or just browsing casually, the mouse felt accurate and responsive. If you're used to a tiny 8×10 mouse pad, switching to this is like upgrading from a compact car to an SUV — you immediately wonder how you managed with less space.

### Desk Protection

The PU leather surface creates a barrier between your desk and everything you put on it. Coffee mug condensation, accidental pen marks, laptop feet imprints — all of it lands on the pad instead of your desk surface. For anyone with a nice wood desk or a fragile laminate surface, this is genuinely useful.

I tested it with a sweating glass of iced water. Left it for an hour. Wiped it off with a paper towel — no stains, no residue, no warping. The waterproof claim holds up in real-world use.

### Non-Slip Performance

The underside of the pad has a textured grip layer. Once you lay it down and press it flat, it stays put. I tried nudging it with my elbow, sliding my laptop across it, even deliberately pushing my mouse off the edge — the pad didn't budge. If you're someone who gets annoyed when your desk mat slowly migrates across your desk over a day of work, this is a welcome feature.

### Cleanup

This is a minor but recurring joy. Spilled some crumbs from lunch? Wipe it off. Pen scuff? A damp cloth handles it. Coffee ring? Same deal. The PU leather doesn't absorb liquids, so cleanup is almost effortless. After two weeks of daily use, mine still looks brand new after a quick wipe.

## The Not-So-Good Stuff

### It Can Dent

The most consistent negative feedback across reviews — and my own experience confirms it — is that the PU leather surface dents relatively easily. If you press down hard with a pen, leave a heavy object sitting in one spot for a long time, or rest your wrists heavily at the same position every day, you'll see indentations.

Now here's the thing: these aren't permanent. Most dents and creases work themselves out over time, and the pad flattens back. But if you're the type who obsesses over every imperfection on your desk, this might bug you. I found that the slight wear actually gave the pad some character, but your mileage may vary.

### The Scent

As mentioned, there's a noticeable plastic/chemical smell when you first open it. It fades, but for the first day or two it's present. Not a dealbreaker — every budget PU leather product I've ever bought has the same thing — but worth noting if you're setting up a workspace where you'll be sitting right over it.

### Size Considerations

23.6×13.8 inches works great for a laptop setup, but if you have a full tower monitor on an arm, a tenkeyless keyboard, plus a mouse, you might find the pad a bit small. The YSAGi is designed as a desk pad / large mouse pad hybrid, not a full desk mat. Measure your desk and think about what goes on top before buying.

### Edge Lifting (Minor)

On perfectly flat desks, the pad lies completely flat after an initial break-in period. But if your desk has any texture or slight unevenness, you might notice the corners lifting slightly. This resolved itself for me within a day, but a few users mentioned it persisted on heavily textured surfaces. A heavy book on the corners overnight usually solves it.
