**What's in the box**

You get two fully assembled lamps, two fabric shades, and a small Allen wrench for tightening the finial. **Build quality and real-world performance**

The wood is the star here. It's solid, not hollow-sounding, and the finish is consistent across both lamps — a common issue with budget 2-packs is mismatched staining, but these match well. The 20.25-inch height is well-judged: tall enough to cast light over a reading chair or pour light across a king bed, but not so tall it feels like a floor lamp pretending to be a table lamp.

The linen-look shades diffuse light nicely. With a 60-watt equivalent LED bulb, you get warm, even illumination without harsh hotspots. The pull chain is smooth, not that cheap tinny feel you get on some budget lamps. One smart detail: a small groove in the base routes the cord flat against the surface, so the lamp sits flush instead of wobbling on a cord bump.

**Where it falls short**

The shades are the weakest link. They're fabric over a thin cardboard cylinder, and a firm bump can leave a dent that's hard to push back out. If you have kids or pets that knock things around, budget for replacement shades eventually. The sockets are standard E26, which is fine, but there's no built-in dimming. You'll need a smart bulb or inline dimmer if you want adjustable brightness.

Also note: the finish is matte, not sealed glossy. Water rings or dust show more easily, so use coasters or a mat if you're placing these near drinks.
