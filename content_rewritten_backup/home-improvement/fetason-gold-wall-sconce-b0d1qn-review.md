**Fetason Gold Wall Sconce Review: Does This Budget Fixture Deliver Designer Looks?**

Let's be real — most wall sconces under $50 look exactly like what they cost. The Fetason Gold Wall Sconce tries to break that pattern, and based on 220 Amazon ratings averaging 4.4 stars, it's largely succeeding. But is it actually worth your money, or are buyers just impressed by the photos? I dug into the specs, the feedback, and the installation reality to give you the straight story.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains two sconces (each sized at roughly 11.8 inches tall with a 5.9-inch backplate), mounting brackets, screws, wire connectors, and a simple instruction sheet. The shade is a tapered cylinder with an open bottom — that's important because it means light spills downward in a cone pattern rather than washing the whole wall. The gold finish is a brushed metallic tone, not that shiny, reflective gold you see on cheap fixtures. As shown in the product image, the design is clean and minimal, making it work in both modern and transitional spaces.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The metal body has a reassuring weight — nothing flimsy here. The finish resists fingerprints reasonably well, though it will show smudges if you touch it during installation (wear gloves or wipe it down afterward). The fixture takes a standard E12 candelabra bulb, and buyers consistently report that the included 3000K LED bulbs (if you get the bundle version) produce a warm, inviting glow that doesn't feel sterile.

One Amazon reviewer put it well: *"I was skeptical at this price point, but the finish looks like the $150 sconces I was eyeing at a local lighting store. The light is soft and warm — perfect for reading in bed without blinding you."*

Another noted: *"Installation took me about 15 minutes per sconce. The brackets align easily, and the wire nuts included are decent quality, not the flimsy ones you usually get."*

**Where It Falls Short**

Here's the honest stuff. First, **these are hardwired only** — there's no plug-in or battery option. If you're replacing a fixture, you're fine. If you're adding sconces where no wiring exists, budget for an electrician. That's not a flaw in the product itself, but it's a real consideration.

Second, **the shade angle is fixed**. You can't tilt the shade up or down, so if your ceiling is unusually high or low, the light pattern might not hit where you want it. One buyer mentioned: *"I wish the shade could angle downward slightly — mounted at 6 feet, the light pool sits a bit high on the wall."*

Third, the gold finish is consistent, but it's not a perfect match across all batches — a few reviewers noted slight tonal variation between pairs. Check yours immediately upon arrival and exchange if they don't match.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Bedroom readers** who want warm, directed light without a lampshade hogging nightstand space
- **Renters and homeowners** replacing outdated fixtures with something modern on a budget
- **DIY installers** comfortable with basic wiring (or willing to pay an electrician $100–150 for two sconces)

**Skip it if** you need adjustable light direction, want a plug-in option, or are matching a very specific gold tone to existing fixtures.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do I need a dimmer switch for these?**
A: No — they work with standard switches. If you want dimming, pair them with dimmable LED bulbs and a compatible dimmer switch.

**Q: What bulb size does it take?**
A: Standard E12 candelabra base. The included bulbs (if ordered with the bundle) are 3000K warm white, which is the sweet spot for bedrooms and living spaces.

**Q: Can I install these outdoors?**
A: No. This fixture is rated for indoor dry locations only. Use it in covered, weather-protected spaces at most.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want affordable, genuinely attractive wall sconces with warm, functional light and don't mind hardwiring. **Skip it if** you need adjustable angles or a plug-in design.

At 4.2/5, the Fetason Gold Wall Sconce delivers strong value for the price. It's not perfect — the fixed shade and hardwire requirement are real limitations — but for the majority of buyers looking to upgrade a bedroom, hallway, or reading nook, this is a solid, good-looking pick that won't embarrass you in front of guests.
