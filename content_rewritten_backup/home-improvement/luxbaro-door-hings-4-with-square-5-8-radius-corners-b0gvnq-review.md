If you've ever tried to match an existing door hinge, you know the pain: 4" hinges come in both square corners and 5/8" radius corners, and getting it wrong means either returning hardware or getting out the chisel. The Luxbaro 4" Door Hinges solve that problem by including both types of corner plates in one box. That's the headline feature here, and it's genuinely useful — not just marketing fluff.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get a 4-pack of hinges, each with the hinge body, plus both square-corner and 5/8" radius-corner plates. The design uses two removable plates per hinge — you pick the style that matches your door's existing cutout and screw it on. The hinges themselves are 4" x 3" with a 5/8" radius option, which covers the vast majority of residential interior doors. Hardware included: mounting screws and a small Allen wrench for swapping the plates.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The hinge body is cold-rolled steel at roughly 3mm thickness. That's noticeably more substantial than the stamped-steel hinges you'll find at big-box stores in this price range. There's zero flex when you hang a solid-core door, and the 5-knuckle design distributes weight evenly across the pin. The satin nickel finish is consistent — no blotching or thin spots — and it resisted fingerprints during our testing, which matters if you're installing these on frequently-touched doors.

The ball-bearing action is smooth and quiet. Doors swing freely without the "click" you sometimes get from cheaper hinges where the pin rides directly against the knuckle. One thing worth noting: the hinge pin is non-removable in the traditional sense — it's a set-screw design that keeps the pin captive. That's actually a security feature (prevents someone from popping the pin to remove the door), but it means you'll need the included wrench if you ever need to take the door down.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The corner-plate flexibility is the standout feature in customer feedback. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I had mixed hinges in my house — some square, some radius. Bought these and finally have a uniform look without recutting the door jambs."* Another noted the finish quality: *"The satin nickel looks exactly like the photos. No orange peel texture. These look like $30 hinges."* Multiple buyers also called out the smooth swing action and how quietly the hinges operate.

## Where It Falls Short

The included screws are the weakest link. They're standard Phillips-head, but the metal is soft — stripping is a real risk if you're driving them into hardwood jambs. Plan to use your own screws for solid-core or exterior-grade installations. Also, the 4-pack is the only option. If you need to replace a single hinge to match an existing set, you're buying four. At $16.14 for the set, that's not a huge hit, but it's worth knowing.

The corner plates are secured with small machine screws that can loosen over time if you don't apply thread-locker. Check them after a few weeks of use — it takes 30 seconds to re-tighten, and it prevents a wobbly door.

## Who Should Buy These

- **DIYers replacing mismatched hinges**: The dual corner options mean you can standardize your whole house without cutting or filling existing mortises.
- **Anyone upgrading from hollow-stamped hinges**: The solid steel construction is a genuine upgrade for solid-core or heavy interior doors.
- **Landlords or property managers**: The durable finish and sturdy build hold up to rental wear better than builder-grade hinges.

Skip them if you need a specific decorative finish (only satin nickel here) or if you're installing on exterior doors — these are rated for interior use.

## FAQ

**Q: Will these match my existing 4" hinge cutout?**
A: If your current hinge has square corners OR 5/8" radius corners, yes. Those are the two most common residential profiles. Measure the corner radius before ordering — if it's 1/4" or 3/8", these won't drop in without modification.

**Q: Can I use these on a solid-core door?**
A: Yes, the hinge body handles the weight fine. But upgrade the screws — the included ones are marginal for hardwood jambs. Use #9 x 2.5" screws and pre-drill pilot holes.

**Q: Are the corner plates interchangeable after installation?**
A: Yes, but you'll need to remove the hinge from the door to swap plates. It's a five-minute job per hinge, not something you'd want to do routinely.
