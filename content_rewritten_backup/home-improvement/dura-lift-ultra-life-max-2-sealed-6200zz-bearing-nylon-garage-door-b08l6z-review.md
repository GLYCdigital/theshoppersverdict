If your garage door sounds like a freight train every time you open it, the culprit is usually cheap, worn-out steel rollers. **FAQ: Real Questions, Direct Answers**

**Q: Will these fit my standard garage door?**
A: If your door uses 2-inch track (the industry standard for the last 30 years), yes. Measure the width of your track's vertical channel. If it is 2 inches, these will fit. If it is 1 inch, they will not.

**Q: Do I need to grease these?**
A: No. The 6200ZZ bearings are sealed and pre-lubricated. Do not remove the shields to add grease—you will void the seal and introduce dirt. Just install and forget.

**Q: How many do I need?**
A: A standard 16-foot double door uses 10 rollers (5 per side). A 9-foot single door uses 6-8 depending on the track configuration. Count your existing rollers before ordering.
