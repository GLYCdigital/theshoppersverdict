If you're wiring a whole house, a rental flip, or a commercial buildout, the math on the Sunco 100-pack is hard to ignore. At roughly $2–3 per fixture when you do the per-unit math, you're looking at contractor-grade pricing for a product with a legitimate 4.4-star average across 220+ ratings. But bulk pricing only matters if the fixture itself doesn't drive you crazy during install. Here's the real breakdown.

**What's actually in the box**

You get 100 slim canless LED fixtures, each with a junction box, integrated driver, and spring clips. The units are 6-inch round with a 0.5-inch trim profile — they sit nearly flush against the ceiling. The housing is aluminum with a polycarbonate diffuser, and each light has a CCT selector switch on the junction box that lets you pick 2700K (warm), 3000K (soft white), 4000K (cool white), or 5000K (daylight). That's a genuinely useful feature for contractors who don't want to sort separate boxes by color temperature.

**Install and performance**

The canless design means no housing to recess and no attic access required — you cut a 6.25-inch hole, push the spring clips through, and the fixture clamps to the drywall. The included junction box is rated for direct insulation contact (IC-rated), which is critical for insulated ceilings. Wiring is straightforward with the included push-in connectors. The 12-watt draw per fixture puts out roughly 900 lumens at 4000K, which is plenty for general living space. Color rendering comes in at 90+ CRI — colors look natural, not washed out.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the reviews, the recurring themes are consistency and ease. One contractor noted, "Installed 60 of these in a duplex over two days. Zero failures, and the color match between fixtures is perfect." Another homeowner mentioned the slim profile as the standout: "They disappear into the ceiling — you barely notice the trim." The damp-rated listing also gets credit from buyers who've used them in bathrooms and covered patios without issues.

**Where it falls short**

The 100-pack is the headline, but it's also the catch. If you're doing a single 2,000-square-foot house, you'll likely use 30–40 fixtures and then have 60 sitting in your garage. There's no 25-pack option listed, so you're committing to bulk whether you need it or not. Also, several reviewers report that the IC rating doesn't excuse sloppy insulation work — if you pack insulation tightly around the junction box, you can get heat buildup and premature failure. Finally, these are not dimmable out of the box in the sense that you can pair them with any switch. You need a compatible LED dimmer (Sunco recommends their own or a TRIAC-compatible model) or you'll see flicker at low levels.

**Who should buy this**

- **Contractors and flippers**: The per-unit cost and the CCT selector make this a no-brainer for multi-unit jobs where you want one SKU to cover every room.
- **DIYers with a large project**: If you're rewiring a whole house and have attic access, the volume pricing justifies the bulk buy — just plan your layout before you cut holes.
- **Anyone with a small job**: Skip it. Buy a 6-pack of a quality brand like Lithonia or Halo instead. You'll pay more per fixture but waste less.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can these be used in a shower or directly above a tub?**  
A: They're damp-rated, not wet-rated. That means they can handle steam and humidity but not direct water spray. Install outside the direct spray zone in a shower or skip them for a wet-rated fixture.

**Q: What size hole do I cut?**  
A: 6.25 inches. The spring clips accommodate drywall up to 1 inch thick. Measure twice — a hole that's too big will leave a gap between the trim and the ceiling.

**Q: Do these work with standard dimmers?**  
A: Only with compatible LED dimmers. Standard incandescent dimmers will cause buzzing and flicker. Check Sunco's compatibility list or use their recommended dimmer models.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're outfitting multiple rooms or units and want a reliable, color-tunable fixture at a price that makes sense in volume. The 90+ CRI, damp rating, and zero-clearance install are legitimately useful features.

**Skip it if** you need fewer than 20 fixtures, or if you're not prepared to verify dimmer compatibility before you wire everything in.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — a strong value play for bulk buyers, with minor compromises on dimming flexibility and a packaging model that doesn't serve small projects.
