Let me be blunt: most LED corn bulbs you'll find on Amazon are cheap, hot-running, and dead within a year. The TSEXES LED Corn Bulb is not that. But it's also not perfect — and knowing the difference before you buy saves you a return trip.

## What you're actually getting

The TSEXES is a corn-cob style LED bulb with an E26 medium base, designed for high-bay applications — think garages, workshops, barns, and utility rooms. The "corn" refers to the 360-degree arrangement of exposed LED chips around a central column, which gives you even light distribution rather than a single downward cone.

Physically, this thing is a unit. At roughly 6.3 inches long and 2.8 inches in diameter, it's significantly larger than a standard A19 bulb. The aluminum heat sink housing is the real deal — not the thin stamped metal you see on budget competitors. That matters because heat is the #1 killer of LED bulbs, and the TSEXES actually manages it.

## Performance: Does it light up a room?

The short answer: yes, dramatically. The 10,000-lumen claim (in the higher wattage variants) checks out in real-world use. Multiple Amazon reviewers describe replacing old 100W metal halide or 150W incandescent bulbs and seeing their garage or shop "brighter than it's ever been." One reviewer noted: *"I put two of these in my 2-car garage and it's like operating room lighting. I can actually see dirt on my workbench now."*

Color temperature options matter here. The 5000K daylight version is crisp and slightly clinical — great for detail work but harsh for a cozy living space. The 4000K neutral white is the better all-rounder for most people.

The 360-degree beam pattern is genuinely useful in dome fixtures or open sockets mounted on ceilings. You won't get the spotlight effect of a directional bulb; instead, light washes evenly across the space.

## Where it falls short

Let me be honest about the tradeoffs.

**Size is the big one.** Because of the heat sink and the corn-style layout, this bulb won't fit inside most enclosed globe fixtures or small flush-mount ceiling lights. Check your fixture depth before ordering. Several reviewers hit this wall and had to return units.

**Glare is real.** Those exposed LED chips are blinding if you look at them directly. This bulb is designed for fixtures where the bulb is above eye level or shielded. Install it in a table lamp at eye level and you'll regret it.

**Dimmer compatibility is spotty.** The TSEXES works with some dimmers but not others, and a handful of reviewers report audible buzzing at lower settings. If you need dimming, buy one and test it before committing to a whole case.

**Heat output is still noticeable.** While the aluminum body runs cooler than plastic alternatives, the higher-wattage versions (100W+ equivalent) still throw a surprising amount of heat. In a small, poorly ventilated fixture, that's something to watch.

## Who should buy this

- **Garage and workshop owners** who need serious, overhead light without rewiring for hardwired fixtures
- **Barn and outbuilding owners** replacing old high-wattage incandescents or fluorescent tubes
- **DIYers** with existing E26 sockets and open or dome-style fixtures

Skip it if you need a bulb for enclosed living-room fixtures, if you require reliable dimming, or if your sockets are in tight, enclosed spaces.

## FAQs from real shoppers

**Q: Will this work in a standard lamp socket?**
Technically yes, but practically no — the bulb is too long and heavy for most table lamps. It's designed for ceiling sockets, garage door openers, and utility fixtures.

**Q: How long does it actually last?**
TSEXES claims 50,000 hours. Realistically, with the aluminum heat sink, you should get several years of heavy use. Just don't put it in an enclosed fixture where heat can't escape.

**Q: Does it work with motion sensor sockets?**
Most reviewers report yes, but the bulb draws enough standby power that some cheap motion sensors may not detect it. Test with your specific sensor.
