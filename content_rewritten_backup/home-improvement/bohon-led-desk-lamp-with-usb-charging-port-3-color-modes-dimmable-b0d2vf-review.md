You can buy a desk lamp that just lights your desk. Or you can buy one that also charges your phone, switches between three color temperatures, and bends to aim light exactly where you need it. The BOHON LED Desk Lamp sits firmly in the second category — and with 225 Amazon ratings averaging 4.5 stars, it's earning its keep on plenty of desks already.

**What's actually in the box**

The lamp arrives fully assembled. You get the lamp head, flexible gooseneck arm, weighted base, and a DC power adapter. The base is substantial enough that the lamp won't tip over when you angle the head aggressively. The control panel sits on the base — three touch buttons for color modes, plus a touch slider for brightness. There's no assembly required beyond plugging in the power cord.

**Performance and build quality**

The gooseneck is the star here. It's a full metal flexible arm, not the flimsy plastic-coated wire you see on budget lamps. It holds its position even when you point the head straight down at a soldering job or up at a wall. The lamp head itself is compact but puts out surprisingly even light — no harsh hot spots or distracting shadows across your work surface.

The three color modes are genuinely useful. Warm (3000K) works for evening reading, neutral (4000K) handles general desk work, and cool (6000K) mimics daylight for detail-oriented tasks like drafting or electronics repair. The dimming slider on each mode gives you smooth, stepless control from a faint glow to full brightness — no annoying stepped jumps.

The USB port on the base delivers 5V/1A. That's enough to keep an e-reader or older phone topped up, but don't expect fast charging on a modern flagship. It's a convenience feature, not a replacement for your wall charger.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Reviewers repeatedly mention the lamp's flexibility and value. One buyer noted, "The arm holds exactly where I put it — I've had cheaper lamps that droop after a week." Another praised the color modes: "The cool white setting is perfect for my detail work; the warm setting makes a great reading light in the evenings." Multiple reviews highlight the build quality at this price point feeling more premium than expected.

**Where it falls short**

The lack of a memory function is the most common complaint. Every time you switch the lamp off and back on, it resets to the default brightness and color. If you prefer a specific dimmed setting, you'll be re-adjusting it constantly. The USB port's slow charging speed is another limitation — it's fine for maintaining a charge, but don't plan on a quick top-up before heading out. And the base, while stable, takes up a fair amount of desk real estate — measure your space before buying.

**Who should buy this**

- **Students and remote workers** who need adjustable lighting for different tasks throughout the day
- **Hobbyists** — model builders, crafters, electronics tinkerers — who need to aim light precisely
- **Anyone with a cluttered desk** who wants to reduce power strip clutter by charging their phone at the lamp base

**Who should skip it**

- **Fast-charge enthusiasts** who need modern USB-PD speeds
- **Minimalists** who want a small-footprint lamp — this one has a substantial base
- **People who need consistent settings** — the reset-to-default behavior will drive you nuts

**FAQ**

**Q: Can the lamp run on battery power?**
A: No. It requires the included DC adapter. There's no built-in battery, so it's strictly a plugged-in desk lamp.

**Q: How long is the gooseneck arm?**
A: The flexible arm measures roughly 14 inches, giving you a good range of motion without the lamp towering over your workspace.

**Q: Does the USB port work when the lamp is off?**
A: Yes, the USB port remains powered as long as the lamp is plugged in. You can charge your device even with the light off.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a versatile, well-built desk lamp with flexible positioning and multiple light temperatures at a reasonable price. It's a solid workhorse that handles everything from reading to detailed tasks.

**Skip it if** you need fast device charging or can't tolerate resetting your preferred brightness every time you power on.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The BOHON delivers reliable performance and genuine utility for most users. The memory function omission and slow USB port keep it from a perfect score, but for the price, this lamp punches well above its weight.
