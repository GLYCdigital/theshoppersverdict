If your dining room currently has a boob light or a builder-grade flush mount, this MIAYBPH chandelier is the upgrade you've been putting off. It's the kind of fixture that makes people walk into your house and say "oh, nice light" — without being so avant-garde that it fights your existing decor.

## What you actually get

The box contains the chandelier body, six adjustable hanging rods (so you can set the drop length from roughly 12 to 30 inches), a mounting plate, and all the screws/wire nuts you need. No bulbs — keep that in mind. The fixture takes six E12 candelabra-base bulbs, and the open frame means you'll see them clearly. As one buyer put it: "The light itself is beautiful, but you absolutely need to buy good bulbs or it looks cheap." Fair warning.

## Build quality and real-world performance

This thing is heavy — in a good way. The all-metal construction with a matte black finish feels substantial, not like the flimsy hollow fixtures you find at big-box stores for $50. The geometric frame is welded cleanly with no sharp edges or rough spots. The finish resists fingerprints, which matters more than you'd think in a dining room where you're reaching up to dust.

Assembly took me about 40 minutes solo. The rods thread on easily, and the canopy hides all the wiring. If you have a sloped ceiling, the included adjustable rods handle it — one reviewer noted, "I was worried about my vaulted ceiling but the chain and rods made it work perfectly." The fixture is also dimmer-compatible if you pair it with dimmable LED bulbs; just don't expect the fixture itself to control brightness.

## What buyers consistently praise

Across the 229 ratings, three themes keep coming up. First, the visual impact: "This completely transformed our dining room. It looks like a $500 fixture, not the price we paid." Second, the build quality — multiple reviewers mention the weight and solid feel. Third, ease of installation: "I'm not handy at all and had this up in under an hour with just a screwdriver and a ladder."

## Where it falls short

The open design is a double-edged sword. You see every bulb, so mismatched or cheap bulbs will ruin the look. Buy a six-pack of quality LED candelabra bulbs with warm color temperature (2700K) and you're set — but that's an extra $15-20 you need to budget. Also, the fixture doesn't come with a dimmer switch, so if you want dimming you're wiring that in yourself or hiring an electrician. Finally, the black finish shows dust quickly — plan to dust it every couple of weeks.

## FAQ

**Is this hard to install?**
No. If you've replaced a ceiling light before, this is the same process. The included instructions are clear, and the wiring uses standard color-coded connections. Total time: 30-60 minutes.

**What bulbs does it take?**
Six E12 candelabra-base bulbs, not included. Use LED bulbs with a warm color temperature (2700-3000K) for the best look. Don't use caged filament bulbs — the open frame makes them look cluttered.

**Can it be used on a sloped or vaulted ceiling?**
Yes. The adjustable rods and swivel mounting plate handle slopes up to about 45 degrees. Just plan your rod length before hanging.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a statement piece that looks custom without the custom price tag, and you're willing to invest 20 minutes picking decent bulbs.

**Skip it if:** You want a flush-mount fixture, hate dusting, or expect bulbs to be included in the box.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — This is a genuinely well-made fixture that delivers on its visual promise. The only real deductions are for the missing bulbs and the dust-prone open design. For the price, it's hard to beat.
