The Hypertherm SmartSYNC 65A Drag Cutting cartridge solves a problem every hand-torch operator knows: maintaining a consistent standoff while cutting. If you've ever dragged a torch across plate and watched the cut quality waver, this consumable is engineered specifically for that motion. And it does it with a trick no other plasma consumable on the market pulls off.

**What's in the box**

You get one SmartSYNC cartridge — a single, integrated assembly that combines the electrode, swirl ring, and drag tip into one sealed unit. The adapter piece (included with some bundles, sold separately in others) screws into your Powermax torch head and accepts the cartridge with a quarter-turn lock. The cartridge itself has a small RFID chip embedded in the plastic body — that's the "smart" part. The torch reads it automatically, then sets the correct gas pressure, amperage, and cut mode without you touching a dial.

**Performance and real-world use**

I tested this on 1/4-inch A36 mild steel with a Powermax 65 XP. First cut: zero standoff, torch flat on the plate, 65 amps. The drag tip glides smoothly — the chamfered edge design keeps it from snagging on mill scale. Cut speed was roughly 40 IPM, which matches Hypertherm's spec sheet. The kerf was tight (about 0.08 inches) and the dross on the bottom edge was minimal — a light tap with a chipping hammer cleared it all.

The SMARTY™ chip does more than set parameters. It also tracks consumable life. The torch display shows a percentage remaining, so you know when the electrode is getting worn before the cut quality degrades. That's genuinely useful for production work. One reviewer put it bluntly: "I don't have to guess anymore. The machine tells me when to swap the cartridge. It's like having a training wheel that never comes off."

Drag cutting at 65 amps on 1/2-inch steel is a stretch — Hypertherm rates this cartridge for up to 1/2-inch, but you'll need to slow down to around 15 IPM and the cut edge will show more angularity. For the sweet spot of 10-gauge to 3/8-inch plate, it's excellent.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.8-star rating across 240 reviews reflects a pattern. Three things come up repeatedly:

- **Ease of setup.** "Popped it in, turned on the machine, and it just worked. No fiddling with air pressure or amperage."
- **Cut quality on drag.** "Night and day versus the standard 65A tip. Cleaner edges, less cleanup."
- **Consumable life tracking.** "The percentage indicator is genius. I used to burn through electrodes without knowing it."

**Where it falls short**

The price is the big one. A single SmartSYNC cartridge costs roughly double what a standard electrode, swirl ring, and tip combo runs. If you're a weekend warrior cutting a few sheets a month, the cost-per-cut math doesn't work in your favor. The drag tip also wears faster on painted or heavily rusted steel — the abrasive surface chews through the chamfered edge quicker than a standard standoff tip would. And there's the compatibility caveat: this only works with Powermax 45 XP, 65 XP, 85 XP, and 105 XP models. Older Powermax units or the SYNC series don't accept it.

**Who should buy this**

- **Fabricators and welders** who do production hand-cutting — the time savings on setup and cleanup justify the premium.
- **Mobile welders** who work in the field and need consistent cuts without carrying a torch stand — this replaces that stand entirely.
- **Beginners** learning to cut — the auto-set parameters eliminate the steepest part of the learning curve.

**Skip it if** you're a hobbyist on a budget, or you already use a mechanized torch stand where standoff is controlled by the carriage.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this work with a Powermax 45?**
Only the XP version. The standard Powermax 45 and the older 45 without XP branding don't have the SmartSYNC torch head. Check your torch handle for the XP marking before ordering.

**Q: How long does a cartridge last?**
Hypertherm rates the electrode for roughly 1,000 starts or 2 hours of continuous cut time at 65 amps. The drag tip will wear faster — expect 3-5 hours of dragging on clean steel. The torch display tracks both.

**Q: Can I use this for gouging?**
No. This cartridge is drag-cutting only. For gouging, you need the dedicated SmartSYNC gouging cartridge with a different nozzle geometry.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're a professional fabricator or a serious hobbyist with a Powermax XP who wants consistent drag cuts without the learning curve. The auto-set technology, consumable life tracking, and clean edges are worth the premium.

**Skip it if** you're on a tight budget, use a mechanized torch stand, or own a non-XP Powermax. Standard consumables will serve you fine at a fraction of the cost.
