Let’s cut through the noise: this is a $20-ish USB lamp that does exactly three things, and does them well. No app, no smart home integration, no RGB disco mode. Just a pull chain and three color temperatures that actually correspond to real lighting needs.

**What you get**

The box contains the lamp base, a fabric shade (it arrives flat, you pop it into the wire frame — takes 30 seconds), and a USB cable hardwired into the base. No wall adapter included, which is honestly fine because everyone owns a dozen phone chargers. The shade measures about 8 inches across and the whole thing stands roughly 14 inches tall — compact enough for a nightstand but substantial enough to feel like a real lamp, not a toy.

The pull chain is the standout feature here. It clicks through four positions: off, 2700K warm, 4000K neutral, 5000K daylight. Each pull cycles to the next setting. This sounds simple, but it's genuinely refreshing compared to the touch-sensor lamps that require fumbling for a specific spot at 3 AM. Buyers consistently mention this — one reviewer noted, "I replaced a touch lamp with this because I kept waking up my husband trying to find the sensor. The chain is a lifesaver."

**Performance and build quality**

The base is metal with a slightly weighted feel, though it's not as heavy as the photos suggest. The shade is a decent fabric that diffuses light well — no harsh hotspots or glare when reading. The 2700K setting is genuinely warm, closer to candlelight than the harsh yellow-orange you get on cheap lamps. The 4000K is the sweet spot for most evening activities, and the 5000K is bright enough for paperwork or doing your taxes in bed (though why you'd do that is another question).

Light output is respectable for a USB lamp — roughly equivalent to a 60-watt equivalent bulb in the daylight setting. It won't light up a whole room, but for a bedside task light, it's plenty.

**Where it falls short**

No dimming. This is the biggest complaint in the reviews. The brightness is fixed per color temperature. If the 2700K setting is too bright for your 2 AM scrolling, you're out of luck — you can't adjust it. About 10% of the 225 ratings mention this as a frustration point.

The cable length is also stingy at 3 feet. You'll likely need an extension cord or a charging station positioned close to the lamp. And the base, while stable on a flat surface, isn't tip-proof. If you have a thick duvet that overlaps the nightstand, pulling the chain can drag the lamp toward the edge. I'd recommend a small adhesive pad under the base if this is a concern.

**Who should buy this**

This lamp is perfect for:
- **Side-sleepers and chain-pull traditionalists** — people who want the tactile reliability of a physical switch
- **Dorm dwellers or renters** — USB power means it works anywhere without hardwiring
- **Anyone tired of app-controlled lighting** — if you just want light and don't need it to sync with your Spotify playlist, this is your lamp

Skip it if you need adjustable brightness, want a heavier base for a crowded nightstand, or absolutely require a wall plug rather than USB power.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, no-fuss bedside lamp with genuinely useful color temperature options. **Skip it if** fixed brightness is a dealbreaker.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The pull chain and three-temp design are thoughtful, but the lack of dimming and short cable hold it back from a perfect score. At this price point, it's a solid upgrade over a bare bulb.
