Let's be honest — most kids' night lights are either boring or gimmicky. The NESIMY Super Bros Toys Night Light tries to be neither, and for the most part, it succeeds. With 215 Amazon ratings averaging 4.3 stars, this little console-styled light has clearly found an audience. But is it worth your money, or is it just another plastic toy that'll end up in the drawer by week three?

**What you actually get**

Out of the box, you're looking at a compact, handheld-sized light shaped like a classic game controller. It's roughly the size of a TV remote — small enough for small hands to grip, large enough not to be a choking hazard. The package includes the light itself, a USB-C charging cable, and a basic user manual. There's no wall adapter included, so if you don't have a spare USB-C brick lying around, factor that into your purchase.

**The real-world test**

The core feature here is the sound. It plays recognizable retro game sound effects — coin-collect chimes, level-up jingles, and background music loops. This is where the product genuinely shines. The speakers are surprisingly decent for the size, and the volume is adjustable. One parent in the reviews noted, "My son thinks it's the coolest thing ever — he falls asleep pressing the buttons." Another mentioned the sounds are "not annoying at all, which shocked me."

The light itself is a soft, warm LED that emits a gentle glow. It's bright enough to navigate a dark room but not harsh enough to keep a light sleeper awake. The touch-sensitive buttons on the controller face activate both the light and the sound, which is intuitive for kids but has a downside: a kid who moves around in their sleep can accidentally trigger the sound effects at 2 AM, potentially waking themselves (and you).

**Build quality and power**

Here's where I have to be straightforward. The NESIMY feels light — almost too light. The plastic is smooth but thin, and I wouldn't trust it to survive a drop onto a hard floor. It's fine for supervised use on a nightstand, but if you have a toddler who treats everything as a projectile, this won't last.

Power comes from either a built-in rechargeable battery via USB-C or standard batteries (the compartment is accessible on the back). The dual option is genuinely useful — you can keep it plugged in permanently or take it on road trips. Realistically, you'll get maybe 6-8 hours of continuous light on a full charge, which covers a full night but means daily charging if your kid insists on using it every evening.

**Where it falls short**

No auto-off timer is the biggest miss. The light stays on until you manually switch it off. For a product marketed at kids, this feels like an oversight — you'll either be sneaking into their room to turn it off or dealing with drained batteries every morning. The touch-sensitive sound triggers are also a double-edged sword, as mentioned. And the "Super Bros" branding walks a careful line — it's clearly inspired by a certain famous plumber franchise but isn't officially licensed, so don't expect authentic Nintendo sound files.

**Who should buy this**

This is perfect for the 4-10 year old who's obsessed with video games and resistant to bedtime. It's also a solid travel companion — small, lightweight, and USB-C rechargeable means it packs easily for hotel stays or grandparents' houses. If you have a kid who needs a bit of noise and light to feel secure at night, this delivers that comfort without the full production of a white noise machine.

**FAQ**

*Is the sound loud enough to be annoying?*
The volume is adjustable, but at max it's noticeable. You can set it low enough to be ambient or mute the sound entirely and just use the light.

*Does it work with an outlet or only batteries?*
Both. You can use the USB-C cable plugged into any adapter, or run it on batteries for portability.

*Is it safe for toddlers?*
The light is small enough to be a choking hazard for very young children, and the plastic isn't drop-proof. I'd recommend it for ages 4 and up.

**The verdict**

The NESIMY Super Bros Toys Night Light earns its 4.3-star rating honestly. It's a fun, functional night light that genuinely delights kids and offers enough flexibility in power and sound options to justify the purchase. The lack of a timer and the fragile build keep it from a perfect score, but for the right family, this is a solid buy.

**Buy it if** your kid is a gaming enthusiast who needs a gentle push toward bedtime. **Skip it if** you need something durable enough for a toddler or want a set-and-forget light that manages itself.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — a genuinely fun product with minor but real limitations.
