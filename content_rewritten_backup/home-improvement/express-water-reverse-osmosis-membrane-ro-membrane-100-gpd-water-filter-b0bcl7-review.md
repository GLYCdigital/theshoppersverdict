## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. First, this is a membrane only — if your system's pre-filters are overdue, you'll waste this membrane's lifespan because sediment will clog the RO layer quickly. Budget for a full filter set if it's been more than six months.

Second, performance suffers below 40 PSI incoming water pressure. If you're on a well with weak pressure, expect slower production and lower rejection rates. You'll need a booster pump — that's not this product's fault, but it's a real consideration.

Third, the wet-shipped membrane needs a 24-hour flush before drinking. Some buyers didn't read that step and got a slight plastic taste initially. It clears, but it's worth knowing.

## FAQ

**How often should I replace the RO membrane?**
Every 2-3 years, depending on your feed water quality and usage. If your TDS readings creep up or water tastes flat, it's time.

**Will this fit my iSpring or APEC system?**
Almost certainly. It's the standard 1812-100 size. Measure your current membrane's length — if it's 11.75 inches, this will fit.

**Do I need to replace pre-filters at the same time?**
Yes. Always replace sediment and carbon filters before installing a new membrane. Running a new membrane through dirty pre-filters wastes it.
