# SMONET Keyless Entry Door Lock with Handle: A Solid Smart Lock That Won't Break the Bank

Let's be honest — the smart lock market is crowded with options that either cost a fortune or feel like cheap toys. The SMONET Keyless Entry Door Lock with Handle sits in that sweet spot: a full lockset with integrated handle, keypad, and multiple unlock methods at a price that won't make you wince. But is it actually worth mounting on your front door? After digging through 215 ratings and the spec sheet, here's the real picture.

## What's Actually in the Box

SMONET ships this as a complete door lockset — you're getting the exterior keypad handle, interior thumb-turn handle, deadbolt assembly, two physical keys (for emergency access), and all mounting hardware. The finish is a matte black that looks significantly more expensive than the price tag suggests. The keypad has backlit numbers that are visible in low light but won't blind you at 2 AM.

The unit measures roughly 6.3 x 2.9 x 2.2 inches for the exterior assembly — slightly bulkier than a standard deadbolt but slimmer than many competing smart locks. It's designed for doors 1.38 to 2.17 inches thick, which covers most standard exterior doors.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The SMONET uses a fingerprint sensor as its primary unlock method, with PIN codes, RFID cards, physical keys, and a smartphone app as backups. The fingerprint reader is capacitive — not optical — which is the good kind. It reads reliably even with slightly sweaty or dry fingers, a common complaint on cheaper units.

Setup takes about 15 minutes if you're moderately handy. You'll need a Phillips screwdriver and about 20 minutes of patience. The included instructions are actually legible, which is rarer than it should be in this category.

Battery life sits at roughly 6-8 months with normal use (about 10-15 unlocks daily), running on 4 AA batteries. There's a low-battery warning that flashes on the keypad, and you can use a 9V battery as an emergency external power source on the bottom of the unit — a genuinely useful fail-safe that many competitors skip.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The fingerprint sensor speed is the standout feature across reviews. One buyer noted, *"My kids can get in the door before I even get my keys out of my pocket — it's that fast."* Another reviewer highlighted the build quality: *"The metal feels substantial, not like the plastic junk I had before. Install was straightforward, and the fingerprint reader works every time."*

Multiple reviewers also mention the auto-lock feature, which can be programmed to engage the deadbolt 10-120 seconds after closing the door. For anyone who's ever driven away wondering if they locked up, this is a genuine peace-of-mind feature.

## Where It Falls Short

The smartphone app is functional but nothing special. It handles user management, access schedules, and activity logs, but the Bluetooth connection can be finicky — several buyers reported needing to be within 10-15 feet for reliable connection. If you're expecting full Wi-Fi remote access, you'll need to buy the separate Wi-Fi bridge, which is an annoying upsell.

The fingerprint sensor occasionally struggles with very dry skin in winter months — a few reviewers noted this, and it's worth knowing if you live in a dry climate. A PIN code still works, so it's not a lockout situation, but it's a minor friction point.

The physical keyhole is hidden behind a sliding cover on the bottom of the keypad. It's a security feature, but it's a bit awkward to access — you'll need to slide the cover and insert the key at an angle. Fine for emergencies, not something you'll want to do daily.

- **Families with kids who keep losing keys** — the fingerprint reader solves that problem instantly
- **Airbnb hosts** — the app lets you issue temporary codes and revoke them remotely (with the Wi-Fi bridge)

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work with Alexa or Google Home?**
A: Not directly — you'll need the separate Wi-Fi bridge (sold separately) and even then, it's limited to lock status and remote lock/unlock commands. No IFTTT or advanced automations.

**Q: How many fingerprint profiles and PIN codes can I store?**
A: Up to 100 fingerprints and 100 PIN codes. More than enough for almost any residential use.

**Q: Is installation difficult if I've never replaced a deadbolt?**
A: No — if you can use a screwdriver and follow simple instructions, you can handle it. The hardest part is making sure your door's bore holes match the required dimensions (2-1/8 inch bore, 1-inch latch hole).

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if:** You want a reliable, fingerprint-first smart lock with solid build quality and don't need full smart home integration. This is a genuinely good value for the price.

**Skip it if:** You need Wi-Fi remote access out of the box, have very dry skin that might trip the sensor, or you're looking for something with a more polished app experience. Also skip if your door isn't a standard thickness — this won't fit everything.

The SMONET Keyless Entry Door Lock isn't flashy, but it does the job reliably. For the price, it's hard to beat the combination of fingerprint speed, build quality, and backup options. Just budget for that Wi-Fi bridge if you want remote control — and keep a PIN code handy for winter.
