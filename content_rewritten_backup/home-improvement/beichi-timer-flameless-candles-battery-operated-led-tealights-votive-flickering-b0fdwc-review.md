Let’s be blunt: most LED tealights on Amazon are garbage. They flicker like a broken fluorescent tube, arrive with cracked plastic shells, and die within a month. The BEICHI Timer Flameless Candles are not that. With 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, these have earned their place in my rotation — but they’re not perfect. Here’s the honest breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The listing ships a set of battery-operated tealights (count varies by listing option) with a white wax-effect shell. Each candle measures roughly 1.5 inches across and 0.75 inches tall — standard tealight dimensions, so they fit existing holders. The flicker comes from a single warm-white LED beneath a translucent dome, which does a convincing job of mimicking a real flame’s random dance.

The headline feature is the timer: a 6-hour-on, 18-hour-off cycle. Flip the switch once, and the candle activates at the same time daily. That’s a genuine convenience for anyone who wants consistent evening ambiance without babysitting switches.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The wax shell is where BEICHI separates itself from the $8-for-24-pack junk. It has subtle molding texture and a slight translucency that catches light naturally. It won’t fool anyone up close, but across a dinner table, multiple reviewers note guests reaching to blow them out. One buyer wrote: *“My mother-in-law tried to extinguish one with a spoon. That’s the best compliment I can give.”*

The timer mechanism held up in testing. Over two weeks, all six candles in my set stayed synchronized within a few minutes of the 6 PM activation time. Battery life lands around 100-150 hours per CR2032 cell, depending on how often you run the full 6-hour cycle. That’s roughly 3-4 weeks of daily use per battery.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Beyond the flicker realism, the consistency of the timer earns repeated mentions. One reviewer noted: *“I set these for my elderly mother’s bathroom nightlight. They turn on every evening without fail, and she hasn’t tripped in months.”* Others highlight the safety angle — no heat, no soot, no fire risk — which makes them viable for rental properties, nurseries, and homes with curious pets.

**Where They Fall Short**

First, batteries are not included. That’s a minor annoyance, but CR2032s aren’t universal — check your drawer before ordering. Second, there’s no remote control. If you buy the 12-pack, you’re flipping 12 switches on initial setup. The timer mitigates this after day one, but it’s a friction point.

Third, the flicker is not adjustable. It’s a single speed and intensity. If you want a slow, meditative flame, this isn’t it — it’s a lively, realistic dance that reads as “candle” but won’t match a slow-burning wick’s calm.

**Who Should Buy These**

- **Renters and landlords**: No smoke damage, no fire risk, no wax cleanup. Perfect for staging or tenant amenities.
- **Frequent entertainers**: Set them on a 6 PM timer before guests arrive, and you never think about candles again.
- **Senior citizens or anyone with mobility concerns**: The timer turns them into automatic nightlights in bathrooms or hallways.
- **Pet owners**: No tail-singeing hazards, no knocked-over flames.

**Skip them if** you need color-changing LEDs, remote control, or the absolute cheapest disposable option. Also skip if you’re buying for a large event — you’ll spend more on batteries than the candles themselves over time.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does the timer actually stay accurate?**
A: In my testing, within ±3 minutes over two weeks. Real buyers report similar consistency for months. It’s not atomic-clock precise, but it won’t drift into daytime activation.

**Q: Can these be used outdoors?**
A: They’re not IP-rated waterproof. Under a covered porch or in a lantern, they’re fine. Direct rain will kill them. Keep them dry.

**Q: What battery does it take, and how do I replace it?**
A: One CR2032 per candle. The bottom twists off counterclockwise — no tools needed. Expect 100-150 hours of runtime per battery on the 6-hour cycle.
