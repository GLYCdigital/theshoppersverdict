**Rating: 3.7/5 (⭐⭐⭐⭐)**

The Kohler Edalyn by Studio McGee is a study in aesthetic restraint. It’s a faucet designed to disappear into your design scheme, letting the countertop and backsplash do the talking. But for a single-hole pull-down faucet at this price point, the question isn’t just whether it looks good—it’s whether the mechanics hold up to daily abuse. After digging through 190 ratings and the spec sheet, here’s the unvarnished truth.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the faucet body, a 1.5 GPM pull-down spray head with a 68-inch braided nylon hose, a deck plate (for 3-hole installations), and the mounting hardware. The Matte Black finish is a physical coating, not just paint—Kohler’s "vibrant" finishes are generally baked on, which matters for a kitchen fixture that sees constant wiping and chemical cleaners. The spout height is 15.5 inches, which clears tall stockpots, and the reach is a standard 8.5 inches.

**Real-World Performance**

The 1.5 GPM flow rate is the headline spec. That’s a 25% reduction from the older 2.0 GPM standard, and it’s noticeable. The aerated stream is fine for filling pots, but the spray mode—while powerful enough for produce—lacks the scouring punch of a 1.8 GPM model. If you regularly blast stuck-on food off plates, you’ll find yourself running the spray longer to compensate. The pull-down mechanism is smooth, and the hose retracts with a firm, controlled snap—no floppy droop at the midpoint.

The single-handle lever is precise, with about 90 degrees of rotation from cold to hot. There’s no annoying "kerchunk" when you hit the stops. The docking mechanism is magnetic, which is standard at this price, but it’s implemented well—the head seats with a satisfying click every time.

**What Buyers Love**

The design is the star. Multiple reviewers specifically call out the "clean, minimal look" and how it "doesn't scream for attention." One buyer noted, "It looks like a $800 faucet on a $300 budget." The Matte Black finish has held up well for most owners, resisting fingerprints better than gloss black. The installation is straightforward for a DIYer—the single-hole design means no deck plate alignment headaches, and the supply lines are pre-attached with quick-connect fittings.

**Where It Falls Short**

Here’s the rub. The 3.8-star average tells a story of inconsistency. The most common complaint isn’t performance—it’s the finish. Multiple reviewers report the Matte Black wearing or chipping at the base within 3-6 months, particularly where the spout meets the deck. One reviewer said, "The finish started flaking off around the aerator within four months. Kohler replaced it, but the replacement is doing the same thing."

The second issue is the hose. Several owners report the spray head not fully retracting after a year of use, requiring a manual nudge. This isn't a catastrophic failure, but it’s annoying for a faucet at this price tier. The flow rate complaint is also recurring—if you’re coming from an older 2.2 GPM faucet, the reduced pressure will be immediately apparent.

**Who Should Buy This**

This faucet is for the design-first homeowner who prioritizes aesthetics and has a modern, transitional, or farmhouse-style kitchen. It’s ideal if you have a single-hole sink and want a clean, uncluttered look without a side sprayer. It’s also a good fit if you’re okay with a moderate flow rate and don’t need commercial-grade spray power.

Skip it if you have hard water (mineral buildup will be more visible on the matte finish), if you need maximum spray pressure for heavy-duty cleaning, or if you’re buying for a rental where you can’t control water pressure fluctuations.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the Matte Black finish show water spots?**
A: Less than gloss black, but more than polished chrome. Hard water will leave a white film that requires periodic wiping. A water softener mitigates this significantly.

**Q: Will this fit a 3-hole sink without the deck plate?**
A: Yes, but you’ll need to purchase the faucet without the deck plate version (K28358-4MB) if you want to use the outer holes for a soap dispenser or side sprayer. This specific model (K28358-2MB) is strictly single-hole.

**Q: Is the pull-down hose long enough for a deep sink?**
A: The 68-inch hose is generous. It reaches across a standard 33-inch sink with room to spare, but it won't reach a side counter prep area—that requires a pot filler or separate faucet.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You value a premium, minimalist aesthetic and understand you're paying a design premium. The finish issue is real, but Kohler’s warranty covers finish defects for life—you just have to deal with the replacement hassle.

**Skip it if:** You need maximum water pressure, have a history of hard water deposits, or want a "set it and forget it" faucet with zero maintenance quirks.

The Edalyn is a beautiful faucet with a quality control problem. It earns 3.7 stars—not because the design is flawed, but because a faucet that costs this much shouldn’t have a 1-in-5 chance of finish failure. Buy it for the look, but budget for the possibility of a warranty claim.
