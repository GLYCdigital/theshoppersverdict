If your bathroom is more "closet with plumbing" than "spa retreat," you already know the struggle of finding a vanity that fits without swallowing the room. The YITAHOME Bathroom Vanity with Sink targets exactly that problem — a compact cabinet designed for small spaces, half-baths, and rental bathrooms. At 4.4 stars from 220 ratings, it's clearly working for many people. But is it the right vanity for *your* specific situation? Let's dig in.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the vanity cabinet (with body pre-assembled), a ceramic sink top, and the hardware kit — screws, brackets, and mounting anchors. One notable detail: the sink and countertop arrive as a single molded piece, which means fewer seams to caulk and fewer places for water to hide. The cabinet itself measures in at roughly 17.7 inches deep by 15.7 inches wide — dimensions that matter when you're squeezing into a 3-foot-wide bathroom alcove.

The sink basin is deep enough to wash your face without splashing your shirt, and the glazed ceramic finish holds up well against toothpaste and hard-water residue. The cabinet interior offers one adjustable shelf, which is decent for a vanity this size.

## Real-World Performance

The pre-assembled cabinet body is the standout feature here. Several buyers noted they had it installed within the hour — one reviewer wrote, *"I'm not handy at all and had this fully mounted in about 45 minutes. The hardest part was hauling the box upstairs."* That's a meaningful data point for anyone who dreads flat-pack furniture night.

The soft-close hinges on the cabinet door are a pleasant surprise at this price point — no slamming, no jarring, just a quiet click. The drawer, however, is a standard ball-bearing glide. It works fine, but don't expect the buttery-smooth action of a $500 vanity. It's functional, not luxurious.

Water resistance is solid for the cabinet itself. The interior is sealed, and the sink top overhangs slightly to keep drips from running down the front face. Where you *will* want to be careful is the back panel — several customers mentioned it's thin and can crack if you overtighten mounting screws. Use the included anchors, not just screws, and you'll be fine.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring themes in positive reviews are **fit** and **finish**. Over and over, customers mention how well this vanity fits spaces that standard 24-inch units simply can't. One reviewer put it well: *"This was the only vanity I found that didn't require me to rip out my hallway wall to fit a bathroom sink."* Another praised the clean, modern look: *"Looks way more expensive than it is. Guests always comment on it."*

The ceramic top also gets consistent shout-outs for being easy to clean — a quick wipe handles soap scum and splashes, no scrubbing required.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this vanity has two honest weaknesses worth flagging.

First, the **faucet hole spacing is fixed at 8 inches**. If you have a single-hole or 4-inch-spread faucet, you'll need to buy an adapter plate or get creative. Several reviewers mentioned this as an unexpected snag in their installation. Check your faucet's spread *before* you order, not after.

Second, the **storage capacity is modest** — one shelf plus a small drawer. If you're expecting to store towels, toiletries, and cleaning supplies all in this cabinet, you'll be disappointed. This is a vanity, not a linen closet. Budget for a separate storage solution if your bathroom lacks one.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this vanity pre-assembled or flat-pack?**
A: The cabinet body arrives pre-assembled. You'll attach the legs, sink top, and hardware — approximately 45–60 minutes of work total.

**Q: Does it come with a faucet?**
A: No. The vanity includes the sink, cabinet, and mounting hardware, but you'll need to purchase a faucet separately. Match your faucet's hole spread to the 8-inch pre-drilled spacing.

**Q: Can this be wall-mounted, or does it need legs?**
A: It comes with legs, but the back panel can support wall mounting if you use the included anchors properly. The cabinet is light enough for a stud-mounted installation.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You need a compact, attractive vanity for a small bathroom, and your faucet fits the standard 8-inch spread. The pre-assembled body and solid ceramic sink make this an excellent value for the price.
