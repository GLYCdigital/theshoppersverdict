**FAQ**

*How often should I replace these filters?*
Every 12 months for the sediment and carbon block. If you have heavy sediment in your water supply, check the pre-filter at 6 months — it's clear so you can visually inspect it.

*Will these fit my Home Master system?*
They're designed for the HydroPerfection and HydroGuard series. Check your model number against the compatibility list on the product page before ordering. If you have a different brand's RO system, these won't work.

*Do I need to change the RO membrane at the same time?*
No — the membrane lasts 2-3 years. This set covers the annual filter change. If your TDS readings start creeping up between filter changes, that's your signal the membrane needs attention.
