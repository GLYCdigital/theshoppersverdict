Let's be straight about this thermostat: the Honeywell TH8321WF1001 Vision Pro 8000 isn't the flashiest smart thermostat on the shelf, and it won't win awards for app design. But if you have a complex HVAC system — heat pump with auxiliary heat, dual-fuel, or multi-stage equipment — this is one of the few WiFi thermostats that actually handles the job without needing a $200 professional-grade model.

**What's in the box**

You get the thermostat body, a wall plate, screws and anchors, a quick-start guide, and the standard Honeywell decal for marking wires during removal. No c-wire adapter is included — that's the biggest gotcha. The TH8321WF1001 requires a common wire for power. If your old thermostat only has two wires running to it, you'll need to run new wiring or buy a 24V transformer kit separately. Check your existing wiring before ordering; this is the #1 reason people return this unit.

**Real-world performance**

The 4.2-inch color touchscreen is bright and legible from across the room. The display shows current temp, setpoint, and system status (heat/cool/fan) without needing to touch it. The screen wakes on proximity — a nice touch you don't get on budget models.

Setup is the pain point. Wiring is straightforward if you're handy (labeled terminals, clear instructions), but the WiFi configuration is a chore. You navigate through a submenu, enter your network password using an on-screen keyboard that's cramped, and then wait 30 seconds for connection. It works — reviewers confirm it — but expect 20 minutes of fiddling, not the 5-minute setup Honeywell claims.

Once online, the Total Connect Comfort app gives you remote temp changes, scheduling, and system mode switching. It's reliable but basic. One reviewer put it plainly: *"The app does what it needs to — I can bump the heat from my couch — but don't expect fancy graphs or usage analytics."*

**What buyers consistently praise**

The scheduling flexibility gets consistent shoutouts. Four time periods per day, seven days a week, with independent weekday/weekend programming. One Amazon reviewer noted: *"Finally a thermostat that lets me set different schedules for Monday through Friday versus Saturday and Sunday without jumping through hoops."*

The Adaptive Intelligent Recovery feature also earns respect. It learns how quickly your house heats and cools, starting your HVAC early so the temperature hits your setpoint exactly when the schedule says — not 20 minutes late.

**Where it falls short**

The lack of geofencing is the most significant omission. If you want the thermostat to automatically drop the temp when you leave the house, this isn't the unit. You're manually changing it via the app or relying on your schedule.

Also, the screen shows a big "Change Filter" reminder that's annoying to clear — you have to dig through installer settings, and it resets every 90 days by default. Several reviewers mention this as a minor but persistent irritation.

**Who should buy this**

This is for homeowners with multi-stage HVAC equipment — heat pumps, dual-fuel systems, or furnaces with two-stage burners. It's also a solid pick if your local utility offers rebates; the model qualifies for most programs, effectively cutting your cost.

Skip it if you have a basic single-stage system and want modern smart features like geofencing, room sensors, or energy tracking. You'd be paying for capability you don't need and missing features you'd actually use.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this thermostat work without a C-wire?**  
A: No. The TH8321WF1001 requires a common wire for power. If you only have two wires, you'll need to run new thermostat wire or purchase a plug-in transformer kit.

**Q: Can it control a heat pump with auxiliary heat?**  
A: Yes — that's its strength. It supports up to 3 heat stages and 2 cool stages, including heat pumps with emergency heat and dual-fuel systems.

**Q: Is the app free?**  
A: Yes, Total Connect Comfort is free for iOS and Android. There's no subscription fee for basic remote access.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You have multi-stage heating or a heat pump and need reliable WiFi control without paying for a pro-installed model. The scheduling flexibility and adaptive recovery make it worth the setup hassle.

**Skip it if:** You want modern smart features like geofencing, room sensors, or detailed energy reports — or if your wiring is minimal and you're not comfortable running new thermostat cable.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A specialized workhorse that nails the basics for complex HVAC systems but shows its age next to newer smart thermostats.
