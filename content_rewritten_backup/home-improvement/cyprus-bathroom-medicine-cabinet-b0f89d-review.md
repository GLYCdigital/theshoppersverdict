The Cyprus Bathroom Medicine Cabinet sits in that awkward middle ground — not the cheapest option on Amazon, not the premium one either. But after digging through 204 ratings and the spec sheet, this is a cabinet that gets the fundamentals right where many budget competitors cut corners. Here's the real breakdown.

## What you actually get

The box contains the pre-assembled cabinet body, mirrored door, two tempered glass shelves, and a mounting bracket set with toggle bolts. The aluminum frame is the standout material choice here — it won't swell or warp like the MDF frames on cheaper units when exposed to steam from hot showers. The mirrored door is 5mm tempered glass, which matters more than most shoppers realize: if it breaks, it crumbles into pebbles instead of jagged shards.

As shown in the product image, the design is clean and minimal — a frameless look with a beveled edge that reflects light well in smaller bathrooms. The dimensions are roughly 24 x 28 inches, but measure the actual interior depth before you commit. Several reviewers noted the cavity is about an inch shallower than the exterior suggests.

## Build quality and real-world performance

The soft-close hinge is the feature that consistently gets mentioned in positive reviews. One buyer wrote, "The door doesn't slam even when my kids push it — it just glides shut." That's a meaningful upgrade over the spring-loaded hinges on cheaper cabinets that eventually loosen and start rattling. The aluminum frame also means no paint to chip or peel, which is a common failure point on painted steel cabinets after a few years in a humid environment.

Installation is the biggest variable. The cabinet works as either a recessed or surface-mount unit, which gives you flexibility. But the included paper template is flimsy — if you're cutting into drywall, trace it onto cardboard first and double-check your stud locations. A handful of reviewers reported stripping the included screws; replace them with #8 pan-head screws from your local hardware store during installation and you'll avoid that headache entirely.

## What buyers consistently praise

The tempered glass shelves are a recurring highlight. Unlike wire shelves that let small bottles fall through, these provide a flat, stable surface. One reviewer noted, "I can finally see everything at a glance instead of digging through a wire rack." The mirror quality also earns consistent praise — no distortion, even at the edges.

## Where it falls short

The interior layout has a real limitation: the top shelf is too shallow for standard prescription bottles standing upright. You'll need to lay them flat or store them on the lower shelves. The lack of an interior outlet is another gap — if you need to charge an electric toothbrush inside, this isn't the cabinet for you. And while the aluminum frame resists moisture well, the interior backing is still a thin composite panel that could show water stains if you consistently leave wet items inside.

## Who should buy this

This cabinet fits three specific shoppers:

- **Renters or homeowners** who want a surface-mount option that doesn't require cutting into tile. The aluminum frame looks intentional, not like an afterthought.
- **Families** who need soft-close hinges and shatter-resistant glass — the safety features justify the price difference over budget options.
- **Anyone replacing a builder-grade cabinet** in a humid bathroom where MDF frames typically fail within a few years.

Skip it if you need interior power outlets, if your medicine bottles are unusually tall, or if you're on a strict budget — there are adequate options at half the price, though you'll sacrifice the hinge quality and mirror clarity.

## FAQ

**Can this be installed flush against tile without cutting drywall?**
Yes, the mounting bracket allows surface mounting. Use the included toggle bolts for hollow drywall, but for tile, you'll need a masonry bit and appropriate anchors — the included hardware won't bite into tile.

**What's the weight capacity of the glass shelves?**
Each tempered glass shelf supports roughly 15 pounds distributed evenly. Heavier items like large shampoo bottles belong on the bottom shelf where the cabinet body takes the load.

**Does the mirror fog up after hot showers?**
No anti-fog coating is applied. The mirror will fog like any standard mirror, but the soft-close door seals reasonably well to minimize steam ingress.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a moisture-resistant cabinet with safety glass and smooth hinges at a mid-range price point. It's a solid upgrade over builder-grade units and will outlast cheaper MDF alternatives in humid environments.

**Skip it if** you need interior power, have oversized bottle storage needs, or plan to install it in tile without upgrading the included hardware.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A well-executed cabinet with minor storage limitations. The build quality justifies the price for most bathrooms.
