The Moen Weymouth S73204BL is the rare kitchen faucet that actually delivers on the promise of form meeting function. At 4.7 stars from 235 ratings, it's earned its reputation — but it's not perfect. Here's the unvarnished truth before you spend your money.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the Weymouth bridge faucet in matte black, a pulldown spray wand with a 68-inch nylon hose, mounting hardware, and Moen's signature Duralock quick-connect system. The faucet stands 16.5 inches tall with a 9.5-inch spout reach — substantial presence without overwhelming a standard sink. The two lever handles operate independently for precise hot/cold mixing, and the bridge design connects them visually and functionally to the spout.

## Performance and Build Quality

The spot-resistant matte black finish is the headline feature, and it earns the hype. After three weeks of daily use — coffee stains, tomato sauce, greasy hands — the finish still looks factory-fresh. Moen's powder-coating process is noticeably more durable than cheaper matte finishes that chip or fade.

The pulldown wand is where this faucet shines. The dual-function spray head toggles between a steady stream and a powerful spray with a simple thumb press. The pause button on the wand head lets you stop flow without shutting off the handles — genuinely useful when you're scrubbing a stubborn pan. The 68-inch hose gives you full sink access without yanking, and the wand retracts smoothly with no sputtering.

Water pressure is excellent at 1.5 GPM. The spray actually cleans — it blasts stuck-on food residue without requiring you to hold the wand inches from the surface. The lever handles have a satisfying, mechanical feel with no wobble.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring theme in buyer feedback is the finish. One reviewer put it simply: "The matte black is gorgeous and doesn't show water spots like my old stainless faucet did." Another noted the wand's reach: "The hose is long enough to fill a stockpot sitting on the counter next to the sink — huge convenience."

The Duralock installation system gets consistent shoutouts. Multiple reviewers mention a DIY install in under an hour with no plumber required. The quick-connect fittings genuinely are tool-free for the water lines.

## Where It Falls Short

The bridge design has a hidden catch: your sink must have three holes spaced exactly 8 inches apart. If your sink has four holes or non-standard spacing, you'll need a deck plate — and the Weymouth's styling doesn't accommodate one cleanly. Measure your sink before ordering.

The matte black finish, while spot-resistant, shows hard water mineral buildup more visibly than chrome or stainless. If your water is mineral-heavy, you'll need to wipe down the faucet more frequently. One reviewer noted: "Looks amazing but I have to dry it after every use or I get white spots."

The pull-down wand's pause button requires deliberate pressure — it's not a hair-trigger, which is good for avoiding accidental pauses, but takes a beat to get used to.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The style-focused renovator** updating a farmhouse or transitional kitchen — the Weymouth's bridge silhouette is a statement piece
- **The weekend DIYer** who wants a quality faucet they can install in an afternoon without calling a plumber
- **The practical household** that values a strong spray for daily cleaning and a finish that doesn't demand constant polishing

Skip it if you have a non-standard sink hole configuration, or if you have extremely hard water and don't want to wipe down faucets regularly.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my existing sink?**
A: Only if you have three holes spaced 8 inches apart. Measure center-to-center before buying. Four-hole sinks won't work without modifications.

**Q: How hard is installation really?**
A: Manageable for a competent DIYer — expect 45-60 minutes. The Duralock fittings are genuinely tool-free for water lines, though you'll need a basin wrench for the mounting nuts.

**Q: Does the matte black finish scratch easily?**
A: It's more scratch-resistant than standard powder-coat, but not indestructible. Avoid abrasive scrubbers on the body — a soft cloth and mild soap is all it needs.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a durable, good-looking bridge faucet with real spray power and you have standard 8-inch three-hole sink spacing.

**Skip it if:** Your sink configuration is non-standard, or you have hard water and don't want to maintain the finish.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — This is one of the better bridge faucets at this price point. The finish durability and spray performance are genuinely impressive. The sink compatibility requirement is the main reason it's not a perfect score.
