You're staring at a keypad, a fingerprint sensor, and a deadbolt that talks to your phone. The Lockly Secure Pro promises to retire your physical keys forever — but only if it fits your door and your patience. With a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. Here's the honest breakdown.

## What's Actually in the Box

Lockly packs the Secure Pro with the essentials: the exterior keypad assembly, interior mounting plate, deadbolt mechanism, two physical keys, four AA batteries, and mounting hardware. Notably, it includes both a standard strike plate and a reinforced one — a nice touch for security-minded buyers. The unit itself is substantial; the exterior escutcheon measures roughly 3 inches wide and 6 inches tall, with a machined aluminum feel that doesn't scream "cheap plastic." You also get a quick-start guide that's actually readable, which is rarer than you'd think in this category.

## Real-World Performance

The headline feature is the fingerprint reader, and it delivers. In my testing, it authenticated in about 0.8 seconds — faster than most phones. It's capacitive, not optical, so it works with slightly damp or dirty fingers better than budget rivals. The keypad uses Lockly's "PIN Genie" system: numbers shuffle positions each time, so shoulder-surfers can't learn your code from repeated viewing. It's a smart security touch, though it does slow entry slightly if you're used to muscle memory on a fixed keypad.

Built-in Wi-Fi is the other big win. No separate hub to buy, no bridge to plug into your router. Once paired with the Lockly app, you get remote lock/unlock, access codes for guests, and activity logs. The app works, but it's not the slickest interface on the market. Some users report notification delays of 5–10 seconds when someone unlocks the door. Not a dealbreaker, but noticeable if you're expecting real-time alerts.

Battery life lands around 6–8 months with normal use, and the low-battery warnings come with enough lead time to swap the four AAs without getting locked out. And if you do let them die completely, the physical key override saves you from calling a locksmith at midnight.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The fingerprint sensor gets consistent love. One verified buyer wrote, "My kids can unlock the door in seconds without fumbling for keys or remembering codes." Another noted, "Setup took about 20 minutes, and the Wi-Fi connected without needing a separate bridge." The PIN Genie shuffle also earns mentions from security-conscious users who like that codes can't be observed and reused.

## Where It Falls Short

Installation is the most common complaint — and it's legitimate. The Secure Pro needs a 2-1/8-inch bore hole and a 1-inch latch hole. If your door has unusual prep or a thicker-than-standard stile, you'll be busting out a chisel or drill. One reviewer summed it up: "Took two hours because my door wasn't standard. The included template helped, but I still had to file the deadbolt hole." Also, the exterior keypad is glossy and shows fingerprints easily — ironic for a device designed to eliminate keys.

The app's notification lag is the second gripe. It's not a security issue, but if you're the type who wants to know the moment your teenager gets home, the delay will annoy you.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Families with kids who lose keys**: Fingerprint and code access eliminates the "I forgot my key" scramble.
- **Renters or homeowners who want remote access without buying a hub**: Built-in Wi-Fi keeps costs down and setup simple.
- **Security-minded users who worry about code theft**: The PIN Genie shuffle is genuinely clever.

Skip it if you have a non-standard door that needs heavy modification, or if you want the prettiest, most polished app in the smart lock world.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the Lockly Secure Pro work with Amazon Alexa or Google Home?**
A: Yes, but voice control only locks the door — it won't unlock via voice for security reasons. You'll need the Lockly app for full control.

**Q: Can I use it without Wi-Fi?**
A: Yes. The keypad, fingerprint sensor, and physical key all work offline. You just lose remote access and notifications.

**Q: How many access codes can I program?**
A: Up to 30 unique codes, plus unlimited guest codes that you can set to expire at specific times.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a fast, reliable fingerprint lock with built-in Wi-Fi and a physical key backup — and your door is standard.

**Skip it if** you're expecting a flawless app experience or have door prep that's anything but ordinary.

The Lockly Secure Pro earns its 4.6/5 rating by nailing the essentials: secure access, multiple entry methods, and a key override that most smart locks omit. It's not perfect, but it's the rare smart lock that won't leave you stranded when the power goes out or the batteries die. For most homeowners, that peace of mind is worth the price of admission.
