**WOSHITU Bathroom Vanity Light Fixtures Review: The $60 Upgrade That Transforms a Bathroom**

Most bathroom lighting falls into two categories: harsh overhead glare that makes you look like a crime scene witness, or dim, yellowed fixtures from 2008 that buzz when you flip the switch. The WOSHITU vanity light sits somewhere better — and at a price point that doesn't require a second mortgage.

I spent three weeks living with this fixture, installed it in a guest bath, and tore through the Amazon review data (all 225 ratings) to give you the unfiltered truth. Here's what matters.

**What's Actually in the Box**

The WOSHITU arrives as a complete kit: the fixture body, three frosted glass shades, three integrated 3000K LED bulbs, mounting hardware, and wire nuts. The brushed nickel finish is consistent across the aluminum housing — no cheap plastic trim hiding anywhere. The backplate measures roughly 20 inches wide for the 3-light version, which is the sweet spot for a standard 24–36 inch vanity mirror.

The LED bulbs are integrated, not replaceable. That's worth knowing before you buy — when these burn out in 15,000–25,000 hours (roughly 10–15 years of normal use), you're replacing the whole fixture, not just a bulb.

**Real-World Performance**

The 3000K color temperature is the warm-white sweet spot for bathrooms — bright enough to apply makeup or shave without washing out your face like 5000K daylight bulbs do. Dimmability is smooth down to about 10% with compatible dimmer switches, and there's no audible hum or flicker at any level.

The frosted glass shades do real work here. They diffuse the light across a wide 120-degree spread, eliminating hot spots and shadowing on your face. If you've ever applied concealer in a bathroom where the light casts a shadow under your chin, you know exactly why this matters. The fixture is ETL listed for damp locations, so it's certified safe for the humidity of a bathroom — not just a marketing claim.

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

Across the Amazon reviews, three themes dominate. First, installation ease. One buyer wrote, "Took me 15 minutes to swap out my old fixture, and I'm not handy at all." The mounting bracket is pre-attached, and the wire connections are straightforward.

Second, the aesthetic. Multiple reviewers note it looks far more expensive than it is. "I've gotten three compliments from guests asking where I got it," one verified purchase reads.

Third, the brightness. "It's actually bright — not like those decorative fixtures that look pretty but light nothing," another reviewer said. The 3-light version produces roughly 2400 lumens total, which is genuinely sufficient for a single vanity.

**Where It Falls Short**

The hardwired-only design is the biggest limitation. If you're renting or don't have a junction box in the right spot, you're hiring an electrician — that adds $150–$250 to the real cost.

The included mounting screws are a known gripe. Several reviewers report the screws are barely long enough for standard drywall, and if you're mounting over tile or thick backsplash, you'll need to source longer ones. That's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

One more honest note: the integrated LEDs mean no color temperature options. You get 3000K, period. If you prefer cooler daylight tones, this isn't the fixture for you.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **DIY homeowners** replacing a dated fixture — this is a 30-minute weekend project
- **Bathroom renovators** on a budget who want the look of a $150–$200 designer fixture
- **Anyone with a dimmer switch** already installed — it pairs beautifully

**Skip it if** you need a plug-in option, want color temperature adjustability, or have a very wide double vanity (look at the 4-light version instead).

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this with a dimmer switch?**
A: Yes, but only with a compatible LED dimmer. Standard incandescent dimmers may cause flicker or a minimum brightness floor. Look for a switch rated for LED loads.

**Q: Is it truly safe for bathroom humidity?**
A: Yes — it's ETL listed for damp locations, which covers bathrooms, covered porches, and similar areas. It's not rated for direct water exposure like a shower fixture, but steam and humidity won't damage it.

**Q: What if I need more or fewer lights?**
A: WOSHITU offers this same design in 2-light, 3-light, and 4-light versions. The 3-light is the most popular for standard vanities; go 4-light for double sinks.
