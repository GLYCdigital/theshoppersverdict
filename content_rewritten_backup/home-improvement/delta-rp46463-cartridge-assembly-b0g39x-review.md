A dripping faucet isn't just annoying — it's literally money going down the drain. A single leaky hot water handle can waste thousands of gallons a year. That's where the Delta RP46463 cartridge assembly comes in. This is the replacement part for virtually every two-handle Delta faucet made since the 1990s, and at roughly $20, it's the cheapest fix you'll find short of calling a plumber (who'll charge you $150 minimum).

## What's in the Box

You get one cartridge assembly — the complete stem, brass valve, and nylon housing unit that slides into your faucet body. That's it. No o-rings, no grease, no instruction manual. The packaging is minimal and functional. The cartridge itself is well-machined with a solid brass stem that threads cleanly into the handle. The nylon body has the distinct shape Delta has used for decades, so it's instantly recognizable if you've done this before.

## Performance and Build Quality

The RP46463 is a genuine Delta OEM part, not an aftermarket knockoff. That matters. The tolerances are tight — it seats firmly in the faucet body without wobble, and the stem travels smoothly through its rotation with a satisfying, precise click at each stop. The internal rubber seals are properly vulcanized, not just glued in place like some cheaper third-party versions.

Installation is straightforward if you've got basic DIY skills: shut off the water, remove the handle screw, pull the old cartridge with pliers, and push the new one in. The whole job takes 15 minutes. One buyer noted, *"Took longer to find my hex wrench than to actually swap the cartridge. Drip stopped immediately."* Another said, *"I'd never done plumbing before and this was embarrassingly easy."*

What buyers consistently praise is the longevity. Multiple reviewers report the cartridge performing flawlessly for two-plus years with no degradation in flow rate or temperature control. The nylon housing doesn't corrode, and the brass stem shows no signs of wear even in hard-water homes.

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest truth: this cartridge only works on Delta faucets. If you've got a Moen, Kohler, or Pfister fixture, this won't fit — and you'll be returning it. Check your faucet brand before ordering.

The other gripe is that Delta doesn't include o-rings or plumber's grease in the box. You'll need to buy a small tube of silicone faucet grease (about $5) to properly lubricate the new o-rings during installation. It's a minor annoyance, but it's an extra trip to the hardware store if you don't have any on hand.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I know if my faucet takes this cartridge?**
A: Measure the stem — the RP46463 fits Delta two-handle faucets with a 1.5-inch cartridge. If your faucet says "Delta" on the base or handle, there's roughly a 90% chance this is the right part.

**Q: Can I install this without shutting off the main water supply?**
A: No. You must shut off the water — either the shutoff valves under the sink or the main supply. Skipping this step means a mini flood in your cabinet.

**Q: Will this fix low water pressure in one handle?**
A: Yes, if the low pressure is caused by a worn cartridge. If the issue is sediment in your pipes, you'll need to flush the lines first.
