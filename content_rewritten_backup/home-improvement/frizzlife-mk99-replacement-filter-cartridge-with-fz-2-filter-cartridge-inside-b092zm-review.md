If you own a Frizzlife MK99 water filtration system, you already know the catch: the filters are proprietary. That means you're locked into buying replacements from Frizzlife — and the MK99 cartridge with the FZ-2 filter is the one you'll be ordering every six months. The good news? It's actually a solid filter. The less-good news? You don't have a choice in brands.

## What you're actually getting

The box contains one complete replacement cartridge assembly. This isn't just a filter element — it's the full housing with the FZ-2 filter already sealed inside. The FZ-2 uses a two-stage filtration process: a sediment and activated carbon layer that catches particulates and chlorine, followed by an ion-exchange resin stage that targets heavy metals and reduces scale buildup. That second stage matters if you have hard water; it's the difference between a filter that just makes water taste better and one that actually extends the life of your coffee maker or kettle.

The cartridge twists into the MK99 base with a quarter-turn locking mechanism. No wrenches, no plumber's tape, no leaking connections to wrestle with. It's genuinely a two-minute swap.

## How it performs in real use

The filtration rate stays consistent at around 0.5 gallons per minute — not blazing fast, but you're not standing there waiting for a glass of water either. The carbon stage does its job removing the chloramine taste that plagues municipal water supplies. One reviewer put it plainly: *"Tastes like bottled water without the plastic waste. I can't go back to tap."* Another noted, *"Noticed the scale in my electric kettle dropped dramatically after the second week."*

The 320-gallon rated capacity translates to roughly six months for a typical household of two to three people. The housing has a printed replacement date window, which is a thoughtful touch — you won't have to guess when it's time to swap.

## What buyers consistently praise

The 4.6-star average across 229 ratings holds up when you dig into the feedback. The dominant themes are ease of installation and taste improvement. Multiple reviewers mention the cartridge being "the right replacement" and confirm it fits perfectly without the finicky alignment issues that plague cheaper knockoff filters. ## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, there's a break-in period. Several reviewers report a faint plastic or carbon taste for the first few gallons. It fades, but if you're replacing an old filter, flush it thoroughly before drinking. Run at least two full tanks through before your first glass.

Second, the lack of NSF certification is a legitimate concern for some shoppers. Frizzlife doesn't publicly list NSF/ANSI Standard 42 or 53 certifications for the FZ-2. The company makes claims about contaminant reduction, but without third-party verification, you're taking their word for it. If independent certification is a dealbreaker for you, this filter won't satisfy that requirement.

Third, and this is the structural issue: you cannot use this cartridge with any other system. It's engineered specifically for the MK99 countertop unit and a few compatible Frizzlife models. If Frizzlife ever discontinues the line, you're stuck. That's the price of a proprietary ecosystem.

## FAQ

**How often do I need to replace this filter?**
Frizzlife recommends every 6 months or 320 gallons, whichever comes first. If you have particularly hard or sediment-heavy water, you may need to replace it sooner — watch for slower flow or a return of the chlorine taste.

**Does the FZ-2 filter remove fluoride?**
No. The FZ-2 targets chlorine, sediment, heavy metals, and scale. It does not use reverse osmosis or specialized fluoride-removal media. If fluoride reduction is a priority, look at a dedicated RO system instead.

**Will this work with my Frizzlife under-sink system?**
Only if your unit is specifically listed as MK99-compatible. The cartridge uses a proprietary connector that won't fit standard 10-inch housings or other Frizzlife under-sink models. Check your system's model number before ordering.
