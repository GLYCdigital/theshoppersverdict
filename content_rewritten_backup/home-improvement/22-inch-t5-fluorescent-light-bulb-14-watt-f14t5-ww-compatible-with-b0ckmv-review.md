## Where It Falls Short

The biggest gotcha is compatibility. This is a standard T5 tube designed for fixtures with electronic ballasts. If you have an older magnetic ballast setup, this won't work — you'll need to upgrade or convert to LED. Check your fixture's ballast type before ordering.

The other common complaint is packaging. The tube ships in a thin cardboard sleeve with minimal internal cushioning. Most arrive fine, but there are scattered reports of cracked glass in transit. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing if you're ordering multiple.

Also note: no end caps included. This tripped up a few buyers who assumed replacement tubes came with everything needed. If your fixture's end caps are cracked or missing, budget for those separately.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this work in my 24-inch T5 fixture?**  
No. This is a 22-inch tube. It will not fit a 24-inch fixture — the G5 pins won't align. Measure your existing tube before ordering.

**Q: Does it need a starter?**  
No. Modern T5 fixtures use electronic ballasts that don't require separate starters. If your fixture has a starter, it's likely an older T8 or T12 system, and this tube won't be compatible.

**Q: Is the warm white noticeably different from cool white?**  
Yes, noticeably. 3000K is a soft, warm glow similar to incandescent lighting. If you're used to the stark white of 5000K+ tubes, this will look distinctly yellower — which most buyers find more comfortable for living spaces.
