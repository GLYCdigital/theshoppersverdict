Let's be direct: a 4-year protection plan for a tool that costs $800-$899 is a bet. The question is whether Asurion's odds are worth your money.

I dug through the 215 ratings on this plan (4.3 stars average) and the terms carefully. Here's what actually matters.

**What you're actually buying**

This plan covers home improvement tools and equipment priced between $800 and $899 — think high-end table saws, compound miter saws, pressure washers, or professional-grade cordless tool kits. You're not buying a physical product; you're buying a service contract that extends coverage to 4 years from your purchase date.

The plan covers mechanical and electrical failures, plus accidental damage from drops, spills, and power surges. That last part matters — most manufacturer warranties don't touch accidental damage, and a surge can fry a $400 motor board in seconds.

**How it works in practice**

When something breaks, you file a claim through Asurion's app or website. They'll either send a repair technician, replace the unit, or reimburse you. One detail worth noting: if they can't repair it, reimbursement is based on the item's depreciated value, not what you paid. That's standard for these plans, but it's worth knowing going in.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Reading through the reviews, the common thread is how painless the claims process is. One buyer noted: *"My miter saw died 14 months in. Filed the claim on a Tuesday, had a check by Friday. No runaround, no hoops."* Another mentioned: *"I've used Asurion four times on different products. They've never once given me a hard time about a legitimate claim."*

The accidental damage coverage gets repeated shout-outs. People who'd dropped a tool off a ladder or knocked one off a workbench were surprised — pleasantly — that the claim went through without a fight.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest complaint isn't about Asurion's service — it's about the value math. At roughly $100+ for this tier, you need to ask yourself two things:

First, does your credit card already extend the manufacturer's warranty? Many premium cards double it up to an extra year. If your card adds a year to a 3-year manufacturer warranty, you're paying for overlapping coverage.

Second, what's the tool's realistic lifespan? A $850 table saw that sees light weekend use might easily last 10+ years without a major failure. The plan only makes financial sense if you're a heavy user or you're prone to accidents.

There's also a notable gap: consumables — blades, bits, batteries, belts — aren't covered. Normal wear and tear isn't covered either. If your tool dies from a worn-out motor brush, that's a maintenance issue, not a covered failure.

**Who should buy this**

Buy it if: you're a contractor or serious DIYer putting daily hours on a $800+ tool, you have a history of dropping or damaging equipment, or you're buying a tool known for electronics issues (anything with a circuit board).

Skip it if: you're a weekend warrior using the tool a few times a month, your credit card already extends warranties, or you're buying something mechanically simple — a hand tool or basic mechanical device that rarely fails.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this cover a tool I bought used?**
No. The plan requires a valid purchase date and typically applies to new items purchased at retail. Used or refurbished tools won't qualify.

**Q: How long does a claim take?**
Buyers report 3-7 days from filing to resolution for most claims. Repair appointments may take longer depending on your area and the technician's availability.

**Q: Can I cancel and get a refund?**
Yes, within the first 30 days for a full refund. After that, you'll get a pro-rated refund based on remaining coverage time.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You're a heavy user (40+ hours weekly) on an expensive, electronics-heavy tool, or you're accident-prone. The accidental damage coverage alone justifies the cost for jobsite work.

**Skip it if:** You're a casual user, your credit card already extends warranties, or you're covering a mechanically simple tool. You're likely paying for protection you'll never use.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Asurion delivers exactly what they promise: reliable, no-hassle claim processing. The deduction is for the value proposition, which doesn't make sense for every buyer. Read the depreciation clause carefully before you commit.
