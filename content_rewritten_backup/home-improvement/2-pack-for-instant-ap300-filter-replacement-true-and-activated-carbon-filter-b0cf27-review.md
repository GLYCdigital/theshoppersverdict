## FAQ

**How often should I replace these filters?**
Instant recommends every 6 months for normal use. If you run the purifier on high continuously or live in a high-particulate environment (wildfire season, heavy dust), plan on 4-5 months.

**Will these work in the Instant AP300 Core model?**
No. These are specifically for the AP300 True and AP300 Activa. The Core model uses a different filter assembly — check your model number before ordering.

**Is there a washable pre-filter I can extend the life with?**
No. The pre-filter is bonded to the HEPA assembly. Vacuuming the outer surface can remove surface dust, but you can't rinse it without damaging the carbon layer.
