Most bath fans are an afterthought — noisy, weak, and forgotten until they rattle. The Broan-NuTone QTXE110 flips that script. With a 1.5-sone noise rating (roughly the sound of a quiet library) and 110 CFM of actual airflow, this is the fan you install once and never think about again. And that's exactly the point.

## What You Actually Get

Open the box and you'll find the fan housing, the blower assembly (already wired to the motor), a white polymeric grille, and the standout feature: adjustable hanger bars. These bars extend up to 24 inches and use a sliding bracket system that mounts directly to your joists. No cutting, no shimming, no improvisation with scrap wood. For anyone who's wrestled a traditional fan housing into place while balancing on a ladder, this is a genuine upgrade.

The housing itself is 13.5 x 10.75 inches — sized for standard 14-inch joist spacing. The included 6-inch duct connector is dampered to prevent backdrafting. One thing to note: this unit requires a 6-inch duct run. If you're replacing an older fan with 4-inch ducting, budget for an adapter or a short section of flex duct.

## Real-World Performance

The QTXE110 uses a brushless DC motor, and you can feel (and hear) the difference. At 1.5 sones, it's whisper-quiet — measured, not marketing-speak. One reviewer put it plainly: "I installed this in our master bath and I literally have to check that it's on. It moves air better than the 150 CFM builder-grade fan it replaced."

The 110 CFM rating covers bathrooms up to 100 square feet with standard 8-foot ceilings. If your bathroom is larger, or you have a soaking tub that generates serious steam, consider stepping up to the QTXE150. But for a typical master or hall bath, this is the sweet spot between power and noise.

Energy Star certification means the DC motor draws about 30% less power than comparable AC units. Over a decade of daily use, that's real money — and it runs continuously without the motor overheating, a common failure point on cheaper fans.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

Across 225 ratings (4.5 stars), the praise is consistent. The hanger bars get repeated mentions: "The included mounting bars saved me an hour versus my last fan install. It was genuinely plug and play." Another buyer highlighted the sound: "I was skeptical about the 1.5 sones claim. It's actually quieter than my refrigerator."

## Where It Falls Short

The grille is functional but plain. It's the standard white plastic that matches everything and nothing. If you're building a spa-like bathroom, you'll want to buy a separate decorative grille — they're available, but it's an extra cost.

The 6-inch duct requirement is the other sticking point. Many older homes have 4-inch runs, and adapting means reducing airflow. Measure your existing duct before ordering. Also, the included mounting screws are adequate but not great — grab a few better ones from your local hardware store if you're mounting to metal studs.

## Who Should Buy This

- **DIY homeowners** replacing a noisy builder-grade fan — the hanger bars make this a genuine weekend project
- **Anyone with a bathroom under 100 square feet** who wants quiet, effective ventilation without paying premium for a brand name
- **Energy-conscious buyers** who run fans frequently and want the lower power draw of a DC motor

Skip it if you have 4-inch ducting you can't easily replace, or if you need a light fixture built in — this is a fan-only unit.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this fan loud in a small bathroom?**
A: No. At 1.5 sones, it's one of the quietest fans in its class. You'll hear the airflow more than the motor itself. In a small powder room, it's nearly imperceptible.

**Q: Can I install this myself without experience?**
A: Yes, if you're comfortable with basic electrical (wiring a hot, neutral, and ground). The hanger bars handle the structural part. If you've ever swapped a light fixture, you can handle this.

**Q: Does it come with a humidity sensor?**
A: No — this model is a manual switch operation. If you want automatic humidity-triggered operation, look at the Broan-NuTone SensorSeries or add a separate humidistat switch.
