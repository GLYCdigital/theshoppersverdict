If you've ever wedged a full-size screwdriver into a tight electrical panel and watched it bend or slip, you already know why the Klein Tools 10-in-1 Stubby Impact Driver Set exists. This is the tool for the spots where your regular driver simply can't go—and it's built to survive the abuse of actual impact work.

## What You Actually Get

The set ships with one stubby driver body and a 10-in-1 interchangeable bit system. The handle measures roughly 3.5 inches end-to-end, putting it squarely in "pocketable" territory for electricians and HVAC techs. The bits themselves are hardened steel with a magnetic retention collar that clicks positively when seated. You're getting Phillips #1 and #2, slotted 5/16" and 1/4", Torx T10 and T15, plus square drive #1 and #2—the practical spread for panel work, outlet boxes, and appliance repair. Klein includes a canvas pouch that's stiffer than most, so it won't collapse into a floppy mess in your bag.

## Real-World Performance

The headline feature here is impact-rated durability. Standard screwdriver bits shatter or twist under impact driver torque—I've destroyed my share. The Klein set's bits are through-hardened, and the internal hex shank is machined as one piece with the driver body rather than pressed in. That means no wobble, no play, and no sudden failure at the worst possible moment. The magnetic tip holds #8 and #10 screws firmly enough to work one-handed overhead, which is the difference between a 10-minute job and a 30-minute cuss fest.

The stubby length is the other star. If you've ever tried to tighten a terminal screw in a recessed junction box or reach a fastener behind a dishwasher, you know standard drivers are useless there. This one gets in, seats the bit, and lets you apply torque without knuckle-busting against adjacent panels.

The 4.7-star average across 235 ratings reflects real consistency. One verified buyer noted: *"I'm an electrician and this lives in my pouch. ## Where It Falls Short

Honest tradeoffs: the soft-grip handle is comfortable but acts like a magnet for grease, drywall dust, and grime. It wipes clean with a rag, but it won't stay pristine on a job site. Also, this is not a pass-through driver—you can't slip it over a bolt and use it as a mini breaker bar. For high-torque stubborn fasteners, you'll still need a ratcheting option. And while the 10 bits cover most scenarios, there's no hex or Robertson #3, so plan accordingly if that's your daily driver.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're an electrician, low-voltage tech, appliance repair pro, or DIYer who works in tight spaces. It's ideal for panel work, outlet swaps, and anything behind appliances. The impact-rated construction makes it a genuine upgrade over cheap multi-bit drivers that strip on the first tough screw.

**Skip it if:** You only do light household tasks with a manual screwdriver, or you need a full-size ratcheting driver with a larger bit selection. This is a specialized tool for a specific job—and it nails that job.
