If you're tired of solar lights that look like glowing jellyfish after two weeks, the EXCMARK 10-pack deserves a closer look. At a 4.0-star average across 200 ratings, this set has clearly found its audience — but it's not without tradeoffs. Here's the honest breakdown before you spend.

**What's in the Box**  
You get 10 complete units: each light has a stake, a separate solar panel top, and a built-in rechargeable AA NiMH battery (200mAh). Assembly is tool-free — twist the head onto the stake, push into soil, done. The panel is roughly 1.5" x 2", which is slightly larger than the competition at this price point, and that matters for charging efficiency. Each unit stands about 15 inches tall, which is a good height for edging along walkways or flower beds.

**Real-World Performance**  
The lights automatically turn on at dusk and run 6–8 hours on a full charge. In my testing, they held up well through a week of overcast Pacific Northwest weather — they dimmed noticeably by 10 PM but never died completely. The light output is best described as "decorative accent" rather than "path illumination." You'll see the outline of your walkway, not trip-hazard-level brightness. That's fine for ambiance, but don't expect to read a book by these.

Build quality is the standout here. The ABS plastic housing feels denser than the cheapo translucent stuff you often get at this price. The IP65 rating means they shrug off rain, and the seams are sealed well enough that I didn't see any fogging after a storm. One buyer noted: *"I've had these out for two winters and they still work. The plastic hasn't yellowed or cracked."* That matches my experience — the dark bronze finish holds up better than glossy black alternatives.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**  
Across the 200 ratings, three themes keep coming up:  
- **Battery replacement**: Unlike sealed units, you can pop out the AA and swap in a fresh one. One reviewer said: *"My old lights died after a year. These I just replaced the batteries and they're good as new."* That's a real cost saver.  
- **Consistency**: Solar lights often come with one dud in the pack. Multiple buyers noted all 10 units worked out of the box.  
- **Aesthetics**: The "flickering flame" mode (switchable on the panel) is a nice touch that adds warmth without looking gimmicky.

**Where It Falls Short**  
Let's be blunt about the weaknesses. First, the light output is genuinely low — around 15 lumens per unit. If you're lighting a dark driveway for safety, skip these. Second, the plastic stakes are the weak point. They're hollow and can crack if you hammer them into hard soil. I'd recommend watering the ground first or using a pilot hole. Third, the auto on/off sensor can be fooled by nearby porch lights — a few buyers mentioned their units stayed off until late evening if mounted too close to a bright source.

**Who Should Buy This**  
- **Garden decorators** who want to outline a flower bed or patio edge without hardwiring.  
- **Renters** who need temporary, non-destructive lighting solutions.  
- **Budget shoppers** who want 10 functional units for less than the cost of three premium brands.  

**Skip it if** you need security-grade lighting, live in an area with brutal winters (the stakes may loosen in freeze-thaw cycles), or expect "wow" brightness.

**FAQ**  

*Q: How long do the batteries last before needing replacement?*  
A: With daily charge/discharge cycles, expect 1.5–2 years. They're standard AA NiMH cells, so replacements cost about $10 for a 4-pack.  

*Q: Can these be mounted on walls or fences?*  
A: Not without modification — the stake is the only mounting option. The panel is detachable, but there's no screw hole or bracket included.  

*Q: Do they work in shade?*  
A: Poorly. They need 4–6 hours of direct sun to fully charge. In partial shade, you'll get 2–3 hours of dim light. This is true of all solar lights in this price range, not just these.

**The Verdict**  
**Buy it if** you want an affordable, reliable way to add soft evening ambiance to your yard, and you value the ability to replace batteries instead of tossing the whole unit.  

**Skip it if** you need bright task lighting, plan to install in rocky soil, or live in a region with long stretches of overcast weather.  

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid value with honest limitations. For the price per unit, this is one of the smarter solar lighting purchases you can make. The 10-pack format alone justifies the buy for most homeowners.
