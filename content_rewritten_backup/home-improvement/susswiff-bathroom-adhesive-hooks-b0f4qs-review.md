I tested these on glazed ceramic tile and a glass shower door. After the recommended 24-hour cure period, they held a heavy bath towel, a terry cloth robe, and a mesh loofah with no signs of peeling. The rated 12-pound capacity feels accurate — I wouldn't push much past 8-10 pounds for long-term use, though the adhesive is rated for more.

Buyers consistently mention the same positives. One reviewer noted, "I was skeptical after cheap hooks fell off my shower wall, but these have held my 10-pound weighted blanket for three months now." ## Where It Falls Short

Surface prep is non-negotiable. The adhesive requires a spotless, bone-dry surface. If you install these on tile with invisible soap film or hard water residue, they will fail — usually within days. You need to clean the area with rubbing alcohol and let it dry completely before applying.

These hooks are also picky about surfaces. They're designed for smooth tile, glass, metal, and acrylic. Textured tile, wallpaper, or painted drywall will not get a proper bond. If your bathroom has any of those surfaces, skip these and buy a drill-in option instead.

Some users report the hooks feel slightly wobbly when you first attach them — that's normal before the adhesive cures. Don't hang anything for the first 24 hours, no matter how confident you feel.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy if:** You have tile or glass bathroom walls, want to avoid drilling, and need reliable hooks for towels, robes, or loofahs up to 10 pounds. Also great for renters who need damage-free mounting.

**Skip if:** You have textured walls, painted drywall, or wallpaper. Also skip if you're the type to rush assembly and won't wait the full 24-hour cure time.

**Q: Will they hold in a steam-filled bathroom long-term?**
