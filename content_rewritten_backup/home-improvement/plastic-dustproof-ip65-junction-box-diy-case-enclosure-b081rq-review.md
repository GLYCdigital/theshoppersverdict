**What you actually get**  
The box arrives with the main body, a gasketed lid (choose clear or solid), and four Phillips screws. That's it — no cable glands, no mounting hardware, no pre-drilled holes. The ABS plastic walls measure roughly 2.5mm thick, which is noticeably beefier than the 1mm-thin boxes you'll find at big-box hardware stores. As shown in the product image, the lid has a recessed channel where the rubber gasket sits — that's what gives it the genuine IP65 seal rather than the "IP65-ish" claims you see on cheaper enclosures.

**Performance in the real world**  
I've seen these used for everything from outdoor Wi-Fi bridges to marine bilge pump controllers. Another used it for a year in a Florida downpour with zero condensation issues. **Where it falls short**  
No knockouts is the big one. If you're not comfortable drilling, this box will frustrate you. Also, the included screws are standard Phillips — fine for indoor use, but they'll corrode if you're mounting in a coastal or high-humidity environment. Swap them for stainless #6 screws. And here's the gotcha: the listed dimensions are external. The usable internal depth is about 1.3 inches (33mm), so a thick terminal block or a Raspberry Pi with a tall heatsink won't fit. Measure your components *before* ordering.

**FAQ: What shoppers actually ask**  

*Can I mount this directly to a wall?*

*Does the clear lid yellow over time?*  
It's UV-stabilized ABS, so it holds up better than polycarbonate alternatives. Expect slight ambering after 2-3 years of direct sun, but it won't become brittle.

*Will it fit a standard outlet?*  
No. This is a junction box, not a receptacle box. It's about 4.7" x 3.7" externally — think small electronics, not electrical work.
