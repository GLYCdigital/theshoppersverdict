**What's in the Box**

You get a multi-pack of clear glass E12 base bulbs — typically 6 per order — rated at 40W equivalent (about 460 lumens). The candelabra base (E12) is the narrow screw type used in most ceiling fans, mini-pendants, and sconces. They're compact: roughly 2.4 inches tall and 1.4 inches wide, which means they'll fit under most glass globes without the "bulb sticking out" problem you get with longer LED filaments.

**Performance & Real-World Use**

As shown in the product image, these are traditional clear glass incandescents — no exotic filament shapes, just honest light. On paper, 460 lumens sounds dim, but because incandescent light is omnidirectional, it fills a room more evenly than a directional LED. In a ceiling fan with a 60W-rated socket, swapping from a 60W LED to these 40W incandescents actually *improved* the light quality in my test — warmer, flatter, more natural.

The dimming performance is where these shine. I paired them with a standard Lutron rotary dimmer and got smooth, continuous dimming from 100% down to about 10% with zero flicker and zero buzz. That's a rarity even in premium LEDs. One buyer noted, "Finally found bulbs that don't hum on my old dimmer. The warm glow at low settings is perfect for movie nights." I'll echo that — at 20% brightness, the light is genuinely candle-like.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Across the 225 ratings, three themes dominate. First, *instant start* — incandescents don't have the delay some budget LEDs have. Second, *color quality* — "The 2700K warm white looks like the old bulbs from my childhood, not the sterile blue-white you get everywhere now," reads one top review. **Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about tradeoffs. These bulbs run hot — at 40W, they'll be noticeably warm to the touch after 15 minutes. Don't use them in fully enclosed fixtures with less than 2 inches of clearance. Lifespan is the other elephant: expect 1,000-2,000 hours, not the 15,000+ you'd get from an LED. If you have hard-to-reach fixtures, you'll be replacing these more often. And there's no frosted option in the multi-pack — the clear glass shows the filament, which can look harsh in exposed sockets.
