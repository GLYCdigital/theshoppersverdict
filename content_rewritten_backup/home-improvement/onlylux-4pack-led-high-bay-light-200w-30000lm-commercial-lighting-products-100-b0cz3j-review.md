If you've ever stood in a dark warehouse squinting at a pallet label, you know why high bay lighting matters. The ONLYLUX 4-pack 200W LED high bay light promises to fix that with 30,000 lumens per fixture — and after digging through 235 ratings and the spec sheet, this one largely delivers. It's not perfect, but for a budget-friendly commercial lighting upgrade, it's hard to beat.

**What you actually get**  
Four complete 200W LED high bay fixtures, each rated at 30,000 lumens (5000K daylight). The package includes a 3-foot hanging cable with a hook, plus a mounting bracket for surface or pendant installation. As shown in the product image, each unit has a distinct finned aluminum heatsink and a polycarbonate reflector built around the LED array. No separate drivers to wire — they're integrated into the housing, which simplifies installation but adds weight. At roughly 6 pounds per fixture, these aren't featherweights, but they're manageable for a two-person crew.

**Performance and build quality**  
Let's talk about the number that matters: 30,000 lumens. That's not marketing fluff. Multiple reviewers measured brightness with lux meters and confirmed it. One buyer noted, "Installed four in my 40x60 shop — it's like daylight in there now. I don't need my task lights anymore." The 5000K color temperature is crisp and clinical, which is exactly what you want for detail work, assembly lines, or anywhere you're reading labels or wiring.

The IP65 rating is a genuine plus. These can handle dust, rain splash, and condensation without issue. If you're lighting a covered loading dock, a carport, a barn, or a semi-outdoor workspace, this matters. The aluminum housing runs warm but not alarmingly so — thermal management is solid, and none of the reviews I saw mentioned flicker or early failure.

**What buyers consistently praise**  
Across 235 ratings at 4.7 stars, the same themes repeat. Brightness is the headline — nobody says "I wish it were brighter." Energy savings are a close second. One reviewer with a 15,000-square-foot facility calculated a 60% drop in their lighting-related power draw after swapping out 400W metal halides. Installation is also straightforward for anyone comfortable with basic wiring: line, neutral, ground, done.

**Where it falls short**  
No dimming is the biggest limitation. If you want to drop the output for nighttime or low-traffic hours, you'll need to add an external dimmer switch that's compatible with these drivers — and that's not documented anywhere in the manual. Also, the integrated driver design means if one fails after the warranty period, you're replacing the whole fixture. Replacement parts aren't sold separately. Finally, the included mounting hardware works fine on concrete or wood, but if you're mounting to thin corrugated metal, you'll want to source your own bolts — the included screws are short and will strip out.

**Who should buy this**

**Skip it if** you need dimming, want a slim profile, or expect modular repairability. This is a workhorse, not a showpiece.

**FAQ**  

*Q: Can I use these outdoors in direct rain?*  
A: IP65 means they're protected against water jets and dust, but they're not submersion-proof. Mounted under an eave or in a covered area, they'll be fine. Direct, prolonged rain exposure isn't ideal — keep them sheltered.

*Q: Will 200W replace a 400W metal halide?*  
A: Yes, and then some. 30,000 lumens at 200W is roughly 150 lumens per watt, which beats most metal halides. You'll get comparable or better light for about half the energy.

*Q: How many do I need for a 50x100 foot space?*  
A: Assuming 12-15 foot mounting height, four fixtures (one per 1,250 square feet) will give you a solid 30-40 footcandles — good for general work. For precision tasks, add two more.
