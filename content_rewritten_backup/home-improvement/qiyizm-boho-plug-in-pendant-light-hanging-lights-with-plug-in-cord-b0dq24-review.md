## What You Actually Get

The box contains the pendant light fixture with an integrated plug-in cord, a ceiling hook mount, and a few mounting screws. The cord is substantial — roughly 10 feet of wrapped fabric-style cable in a neutral black or white (depending on the variant you choose). The shade itself is a handwoven rattan/wicker dome, roughly 10 inches in diameter, giving it a substantial presence without overwhelming smaller spaces. As shown in the product image, the natural fiber weave creates a beautiful light pattern on walls and ceilings when illuminated.

One critical thing to know upfront: **the bulb is not included.** You'll need an E12 candelabra-base bulb. A standard A19 bulb won't fit. Budget an extra $8–12 for a quality LED candelabra bulb.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The rattan shade is the star here. It's tightly woven — not the flimsy, gap-filled construction you sometimes see at this price point. Several reviewers specifically mention the craftsmanship. One buyer noted: *"The weave is tight and even — my last rattan light from another brand looked cheap up close, but this one looks genuinely handmade."* Another praised the cord quality: *"The fabric cord is thick and feels durable, not like the flimsy plastic cords on cheaper plug-in fixtures."*

The ceiling hook holds the weight securely. At roughly 3 pounds total, you don't need heavy-duty anchors unless you're mounting into plaster — standard drywall screws included in the kit will handle it. The cord routes through the hook and down to your wall outlet, so you'll want to plan your path to keep it tidy. Some buyers use adhesive cord clips (not included) to run it along the ceiling and down the wall cleanly.

## Where It Falls Short

Honest tradeoffs exist. The **lack of an inline dimmer** is the most common frustration. The cord runs straight from fixture to plug — no switch, no dimmer control. You're either at full brightness or off unless you invest in a smart bulb or a dimmer plug adapter. That's an extra purchase and an extra step.

The **bulb requirement** catches people off guard. The E12 candelabra base is less common than standard bulbs, and if you grab the wrong one from your closet, you'll be making a second trip to the store.

Finally, the **cord management** is on you. With 10 feet of cord, you'll have excess to deal with unless your outlet is conveniently positioned. The hook only supports the fixture — you'll want clips or ties to keep the slack organized. The included instructions suggest wrapping excess cord around the hook, which works but looks slightly less polished.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I cut the cord to make it shorter?**
A: Technically yes, but you'd need to rewire the plug. If you're not comfortable with basic electrical work, don't. Instead, use the included hook or cord clips to manage excess length. Most buyers find the full cord useful for routing.

**Q: What bulb should I buy?**
A: Look for an E12 candelabra-base LED bulb. A 4–6 watt warm white (2700K) LED gives the best boho glow. Avoid anything over 60-watt equivalent — the rattan shade concentrates heat and light, and you don't want to cook the weave or create glare.

**Q: Does it work with smart home setups?**
A: Yes, as long as you use a smart E12 bulb (like Philips Hue or Wyze candelabra bulbs) or plug the cord into a smart outlet. The fixture itself has no smart features, but it plays perfectly with both approaches.
