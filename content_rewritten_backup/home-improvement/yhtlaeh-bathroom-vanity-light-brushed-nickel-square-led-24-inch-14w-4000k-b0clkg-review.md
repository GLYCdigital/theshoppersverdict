Let's cut through the listing photos. The YHTlaeh 24-inch vanity light is a square-edged LED bar that promises modern bathroom lighting without the bulb-replacement hassle. At 4.4 stars from 220 raters, it's clearly doing something right. Here's what you're actually getting.

**What's in the box:** The fixture itself, a brushed nickel mounting plate, short mounting screws, wire nuts, and a basic instruction sheet. No bulbs — because there aren't any. The LEDs are integrated into the housing. That's the tradeoff you're signing up for.

**The real-world performance:** The 14W output delivers 4000K neutral white light that sits between warm and daylight. For a 24-inch bar over a standard 30-inch vanity, this gives you even coverage across your face when you're standing at the sink. No hot spots, no shadowing under your chin. The diffuser panel does its job — the light spreads evenly rather than creating that spotted LED look you see on cheaper fixtures.

The brushed nickel finish is worth noting. It's not the shiny chrome you'll see on builder-grade fixtures; it's a muted, matte-adjacent metal that hides water spots and toothpaste splatter. The square profile is genuinely modern without being aggressively angular. It works in transitional bathrooms, not just stark contemporary ones.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The installation is the headline. Multiple reviewers mention it took under 20 minutes. One verified buyer wrote: "Took down the old fixture, wired this in, done. The base covers the old junction box holes perfectly." That's not a trivial thing — most vanity bars force you to patch drywall or install a new box. The 4.25-inch round base plate is forgiving.

The brightness-to-wattage ratio also gets consistent love. At 14W, this replaces a 60W incandescent equivalent. One reviewer noted: "My bathroom went from dim and dingy to bright enough for my wife to do her makeup without a separate mirror light."

**Where it falls short:** The integrated LED issue is the elephant in the room. When these LEDs hit end-of-life — typically 25,000-30,000 hours, so roughly 7-8 years at normal use — you're replacing the entire fixture. That's not unique to YHTlaeh; it's the industry trend. But if you're the type who keeps fixtures for two decades, this isn't your light.

Dimmer compatibility is another gotcha. The listing doesn't scream about it, but several reviewers report flickering when paired with older dimmer switches. If you want dimmable light, budget for a modern LED-compatible dimmer or skip this fixture.

The mounting screws are the most common complaint. They're adequate for drywall, but if you're mounting over tile or thick plaster, you'll want longer screws and proper anchors. One reviewer put it plainly: "The included screws are too short for anything but bare drywall. I used my own."

**Who should buy this:** 
- **The weekend remodeler** who wants a modern update without calling an electrician
- **The renter** who needs a fixture that covers old junction box holes without damaging walls
- **Anyone replacing a dated 2-bulb vanity bar** and wants brighter, even light without bulb maintenance

**Who should skip it:**
- **Dimmer enthusiasts** — you'll fight compatibility issues
- **Long-haul homeowners** who despise fixture replacements
- **Anyone with thick tile walls** who doesn't want to make a hardware store run

**FAQ:**

*Is it really 24 inches wide?* The housing is 23.6 inches, with the mounting base extending to about 24.5 inches total. Measure your space and add an inch on each side for clearance.

*Does it work with a standard wall switch?* Yes, it's hardwired to a standard junction box and works with a basic toggle or rocker switch. Just not dimmers — unless you get a compatible one.

*Can I install it myself?* If you're comfortable wiring a ceiling fan, you can handle this. Two wires (black, white) plus a ground. Turn off the breaker first.

**The Verdict:**

**Buy it if** you want a modern, bright, no-maintenance vanity light and your walls are standard drywall. The 4000K color temperature is the sweet spot for bathrooms — bright enough for tasks, warm enough to not look like an operating room.

**Skip it if** you need dimming, hate throwing away fixtures, or your installation surface is anything but standard drywall.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, well-executed fixture that nails the modern aesthetic and installation ease. The integrated LED lifespan and dimmer quirks keep it from a perfect score, but for the price, it's a smart upgrade for most bathrooms.
