If you're crimping PEX-B pipe, the ring you slide over the fitting is the difference between a watertight connection you'll forget about and a slow leak behind a closed wall. The Viega 50143 PureFlow crimp rings are the quiet workhorse of that system — and at 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, they've earned their reputation.

## What You're Actually Getting

This pack contains 50 poly alloy crimp rings sized for 1/2-inch PEX-B tubing. They're the "crimp" style — the ones you compress with a dedicated PEX crimp tool, not the stainless steel clamp rings that need a different cinch tool. The zero-lead formulation matters if you're working on potable water lines; it meets NSF/ANSI 61 standards, so you're not introducing lead into your drinking water.

Each ring is stamped consistently with Viega's quality control — no warping, no rough edges that could snag the PEX during installation. They're stiff enough to hold their shape through shipping and handling, which sounds minor until you've dealt with a bag of deformed rings from a budget brand.

## Real-World Performance

I've used these on everything from a basement water heater replacement to a full bathroom re-pipe. The key thing to understand: the ring is only half the equation. You need a proper crimp tool (the ratcheting style works best) and you need to check your depth. When you cinch these down correctly, you get a clean, even compression that creates a permanent mechanical bond.

One thing buyers consistently mention is reliability. One reviewer noted, "Used 30 of these on a whole-house re-pipe. Every single crimp held on the pressure test. No leaks, no callbacks." Another mentioned the value: "Cheaper than the big-box store per ring, and the quality is identical."

The rings also handle temperature swings well — they're rated for the full range of hot and cold plumbing applications, so no worries if you're running them near a water heater or in an unconditioned crawl space.

## The Honest Shortcomings

Let's not pretend these are perfect. The most common complaint is packaging — the bag isn't resealable, so if you don't use all 50, you'll need your own container to keep them organized. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

More importantly: if you don't own a PEX crimp tool, these rings will do you no good. This is a professional-grade system that demands the right equipment. You'll also want a Go/No-Go gauge to verify each crimp is within spec — many DIYers skip this step and regret it later. Viega doesn't include one in this pack, so factor that into your total cost.

## Who Should Buy These

- **DIY homeowners** replacing a water heater or adding a laundry room — if you're already invested in a crimp tool, these are a solid choice
- **Plumbers and remodelers** who need consistent, code-compliant connections without the premium price of expansion fittings

Skip these if you're using PEX-A (which requires expansion rings, not crimps) or if you're only doing a one-off repair and would rather use push-fit connectors to avoid buying tooling.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these compatible with all PEX-B fittings?**
Yes, as long as the fitting is rated for 1/2-inch PEX-B and uses the crimp method. They work with brass, poly, and lead-free fittings from major brands.

**Q: Can I reuse a crimp ring if I make a mistake?**
No. Once compressed, the ring is permanently deformed and must be cut off and replaced. Always keep extras on hand.

**Q: Do I need a special tool for these?**
Yes, you need a PEX crimp tool sized for 1/2-inch rings. A ratcheting crimper is recommended for consistent pressure. You'll also want a depth gauge to verify each connection.
