I've tested enough solar lights to be skeptical of the word "waterproof." Most last one season before the seal fails and the battery corrodes. The ELUTENG 4-pack, though, is a genuine outlier in the $20-30 range.

**What you actually get**

The box contains four round lanterns, each measuring roughly 2.76 inches in diameter. They're built from ABS plastic with a frosted lens that diffuses the light into a soft, even glow. Each unit has a small solar panel on top — no visible wiring, no mounting brackets needed. You just hang them from the built-in loop or set them on a flat surface.

The IP65 rating is the headline feature here, and it's earned. The seam where the lens meets the housing is gasket-sealed, and the solar panel sits flush with no exposed gaps. I've seen these survive a week of Pacific Northwest drizzle without a hint of moisture inside.

**Performance in the real world**

The LED output is rated at 3000K warm white — noticeably warmer than the harsh blue-white you get from cheaper solar lights. It's genuinely pleasant for a patio or garden path. In full sun, you'll get 8-10 hours of light on a full charge. Partial shade cuts that roughly in half.

The automatic sensor is reliable. It kicks on at dusk and shuts off at dawn without the flickering you see on cheaper units. That said, there's no manual override. If you want them off, you're covering them or waiting for sunrise.

One thing buyers consistently note: the light output is modest. We're talking ambiance, not security lighting. One reviewer put it well: *"These are perfect for marking a walkway or adding a soft glow to a deck, but don't expect to read by them."* That's the right expectation.

**Where they fall short**

The biggest long-term complaint isn't the battery — it's the plastic. After 8-10 months of direct sun, the lens can develop micro-cracks and yellowing. The waterproofing holds, but the light diffusion gets cloudy. It's cosmetic, not functional, but it's worth knowing.

Replacing the AA battery requires unscrewing the base plate. It's doable, but the screws are small and easy to strip if you're not careful. Budget five minutes per light.

**Who should buy this**

This 4-pack is ideal for renters or homeowners who want reliable, no-wiring accent lighting without committing to hardwired fixtures. It's also a solid choice for decorative lighting on a fence, pergola, or tree branch where you don't want to run extension cords.

Skip it if you need task lighting or if you live in an area with extreme UV exposure — the plastic degradation will accelerate there.

**FAQ**

*Q: How long do these actually last on a full charge?*
A: 8-10 hours in summer with direct sun. In winter or overcast conditions, expect 4-6 hours. The battery degrades about 20% per year.

*Q: Can these be used indoors?*
A: Technically, but the solar panel needs direct sunlight. Placing them on a windowsill works, but you'll get maybe 3-4 hours of light.

*Q: Do they come with mounting hardware?*
A: No, just the hanging loop. You'll need a small hook or nail for hanging.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want affordable, genuinely waterproof accent lighting with a warm, pleasant glow and zero wiring hassle.

**Skip it if** you need bright task lighting or you're somewhere with brutal year-round sun exposure.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid value for the price, with a few durability caveats that come with the territory at this price point.
