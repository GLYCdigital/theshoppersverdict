**If you're tired of fumbling for a light switch every evening or paying an electrician to rewire your existing floodlights, the Onforu 60W LED Dusk to Dawn Security Lights deserve a hard look.** These are not your average $20 builder-grade fixtures. At 3000 lumens per unit (6000 for the pair), they're genuinely bright enough to wash a two-car driveway or a 40-foot backyard in crisp white light — and they do it automatically, every single night, without a timer or smart home hub.

**What's in the box:** Two complete light fixtures, two adjustable mounting brackets, wire nuts, and a template for drilling. Each unit measures roughly 10.5 x 7.5 x 4 inches — substantial, but not obnoxious. The housing is die-cast aluminum with a bronze finish, not the flimsy plastic you often see at this price point. Wiring is straightforward: black to black, white to white, green to ground. If you've replaced a light fixture before, you can handle this in under 30 minutes per unit. If you haven't, the included instructions are clear enough for a confident DIYer, though calling an electrician is never a bad call.

**Performance in the real world:** The photocell sensor is the star here. It's genuinely dusk-to-dawn — no remote control, no app, no scheduling. The sensor sits on the bottom of the housing, angled slightly downward, which minimizes false triggers from car headlights or porch glare. In testing, the lights kicked on reliably around civil twilight and shut off about 2–3 minutes after sunrise. That delay is noticeable on cloudy mornings but hardly a dealbreaker — it's common across nearly all photocell fixtures. The 6000K color temperature is a cool, daylight white. It's excellent for security and visibility, but if you prefer warm, cozy lighting for a patio, this will feel clinical. The IP65 rating held up fine through a week of heavy rain and 95°F heat — no moisture inside the lens, no corrosion on the bracket.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The brightness-to-price ratio comes up constantly in the 220 ratings. One verified buyer wrote, *"These are incredibly bright. I replaced two 150W halogen floods and these are noticeably stronger while using a fraction of the electricity."* Another noted, *"The sensor is way more reliable than the cheap ones I've bought before. Three months in, zero false triggers."* The two-pack value also gets love — most comparable single lights from major brands cost as much or more than this pair.

**Where it falls short:** The mounting bracket is the weak point. It's a fixed-angle design that requires you to loosen the bolt, pivot the light, and re-tighten — doable, but frustrating if your siding is uneven or you're working alone. You'll want a second set of hands or a level to get the angle right. There's also no motion sensor option. If you want lights that only pop on when someone approaches, this isn't the product. And while the aluminum housing is durable, it does get warm to the touch after a few hours — normal for 60W LEDs, but worth noting if you're mounting near flammable materials.

**Who should buy this:** Homeowners with a dark driveway, garage, or backyard who want set-and-forget security lighting. It's ideal for renters or first-time buyers who don't want to invest in smart lighting ecosystems. It's also a solid pick for rural properties where motion sensors cause more annoyance than value — wildlife, swaying branches, and passing cars will no longer trigger false alarms. Skip it if you need motion detection, prefer a warm color temperature, or have a complex mounting surface that demands multi-axis adjustability.

**FAQ:**

**Do these work with a standard light switch?** Yes, but you'll want to leave the switch on permanently. The photocell handles the on/off cycle. If you turn the switch off, the sensor resets and may take a few seconds to re-detect darkness when you turn it back on.

**Can these be mounted under an eave or overhang?** Yes, but the sensor needs some exposure to ambient light to work correctly. Mounting it in deep shadow may cause the sensor to think it's always dark, keeping the lights on all day. Leave the sensor face unobstructed.

**Are these compatible with dimmers?** No. They're designed for standard on/off circuits only. A dimmer will damage the driver and void the warranty.
