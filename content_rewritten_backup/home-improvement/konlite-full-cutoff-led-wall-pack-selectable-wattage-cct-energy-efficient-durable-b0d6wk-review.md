If you've ever installed a wall pack only to realize the color temperature is a cold, alien blue when you wanted warm, or the light blasts upward into your neighbor's bedroom window, you know the pain. The Konlite Full-Cutoff LED Wall Pack aims to end that frustration with a simple, practical design — and for the most part, it delivers.

I tested this fixture for two weeks on a detached garage, and here's what 240 other Amazon buyers and I learned the hard way.

**What's Actually in the Box**

You get the fixture itself (die-cast aluminum housing, tempered glass lens), a mounting bracket, and the hardware needed for standard junction box installation. No bulbs, no extra drivers, no surprises. The unit is pre-wired for 120-277V, which covers both residential and light commercial setups.

The standout feature is on the side: two small switches. One adjusts wattage (10W/20W/30W), the other adjusts color temperature (3000K/4000K/5000K). You can change both before mounting — or after, since they're accessible from outside the housing. That's genuinely useful if you're not sure what brightness you need until you see it at night.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The housing feels substantial — not the flimsy stamped steel you get with some $30 competitors. The gasket seals tight, and the IP65 rating means it's fully protected against dust and water jets. I hosed mine down after a dusty week and had zero issues.

Light output is where this thing earns its keep. The full-cutoff design means zero uplight escapes — every photon goes down where you need it. The beam spread is wide and even, covering a 20-foot expanse of wall and driveway without the hot spots and dark patches you get from cheaper reflectors.

At 30W, you're getting roughly 3,000 lumens, which is plenty for a residential garage, side entrance, or back alley. The 5000K setting is crisp and clean; 3000K is warm and less bug-attracting. I settled on 4000K — a neutral middle ground that feels security-grade without the hospital vibe.

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

The "selectable" theme dominates the positive reviews. One verified buyer put it simply: *“I set it to 20W/4000K and it's perfect — no more guessing or buying three different fixtures.”* Another noted the install was “surprisingly simple — took 15 minutes with basic tools.”

The full-cutoff optics get frequent shoutouts too. *“No more light pollution into my upstairs window,”* one reviewer wrote. *“My HOA actually approved this one.”*

**Where It Falls Short**

The mounting bracket is fixed — there's no swivel. You need to aim the fixture during installation because you can't adjust it afterward. Measure twice, tighten once.

At 30W, the housing gets noticeably warm to the touch after a few hours. It's within spec and shouldn't affect longevity, but it's worth noting if you're mounting it somewhere enclosed.

And while the 10W setting is great for a narrow walkway, it's dim for larger areas. This isn't a floodlight replacement — it's a focused security wall pack.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Homeowners** replacing an old dusk-to-dawn fixture with something energy-efficient and dark-sky compliant
- **Landlords** who need a reliable, low-maintenance light for rental properties
- **Small business owners** illuminating a rear entrance or loading dock without light trespass

**Skip it if** you need a motion-sensing fixture (this one is always-on or photocell controlled) or you want a 360-degree floodlight for a large yard.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Does it work with a photocell or motion sensor?**
A: Not built-in. You'll need to wire an external photocell or sensor into the circuit. The fixture itself is always-on when powered.

**Q: Can I change the wattage and color temperature after installation?**
A: Yes — the switches are accessible from the outside of the housing. No need to remove the fixture from the wall.

**Q: Is it really full-cutoff?**
A: Yes. The lens is flat and flush with the housing, directing 100% of light downward. No uplight escapes.
