**RYOBI P135 18V One+ 6 Port Lithium Ion Battery Supercharger: Can One Charger Really Handle Your Whole Fleet?**

If you’re three tools deep into the RYOBI One+ ecosystem, you already know the pain: a single charger bottlenecking your workflow. The P135 Supercharger promises to end that by juicing up to six batteries at once. But is it a genuine shop upgrade or just a plastic brick that takes up bench space? After digging through user experiences and the specs, here’s the honest breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The P135 is a horizontal, six-slot charging dock that works with all RYOBI One+ 18V lithium batteries (P100–P192 series). It’s not a rapid charger—it’s a maintenance and top-off station. You plug it into a standard wall outlet, slide batteries into the individual ports, and it charges them sequentially, not simultaneously. The unit is compact (roughly 12 inches wide) and has a low-profile design with a built-in LED indicator per slot.

Key spec to note: This is a **1-hour charger**, not a 30-minute supercharger. The “Supercharger” name refers to capacity, not speed. It pulls about 60W from the wall, which means it’s best for topping off a fleet overnight or between heavy use sessions.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The housing is solid ABS plastic with a non-slip base. It feels like a RYOBI product—durable enough for a garage, not rugged enough for a job site. Users report the spring-loaded slots hold batteries firmly, and the charging contacts are recessed to avoid shorts.

Performance-wise, the sequential charging logic is the big story. It prioritizes the battery with the highest charge level first, getting you back to work fastest. However, there’s a catch: it charges one battery at a time, and if you slap in six depleted 4Ah batteries, you’re looking at a 6+ hour cycle. That’s fine for overnight, frustrating if you need two batteries ready in 20 minutes.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The convenience factor is the headline. “I have 14 RYOBI batteries and this thing is a lifesaver,” one verified buyer notes. “No more swapping chargers on the wall. I just drop them in before bed and wake up to a full rack.” Another reviewer highlights the space savings: “It replaces three individual chargers on my shelf. Way cleaner setup.”

The LED status lights are also a hit—green for charged, red for charging, and a blinking pattern for errors. Users say it’s easy to glance at from across the room and tell which batteries are ready.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let’s be blunt about the limitations. First, **no rapid charging**. If you’re used to the P118 dual-chemistry fast charger, this will feel slow. Second, the sequential charging means if you plug in a dead 6Ah battery, it might sit for an hour before it even starts charging. Third, there’s no USB port for charging your phone—a missed opportunity given the “supercharger” branding.

One more real-world gripe: the footprint. It’s wider than it looks in photos. Make sure you have a dedicated 12-inch strip of bench space, or it’ll crowd your work area.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The Weekend DIYer** with 3–5 batteries: Perfect for keeping everything topped off without a wall of chargers.
- **The One+ Collector** with 10+ batteries: This is the organizational hub you didn’t know you needed.
- **Skip it if** you’re a professional using 4Ah+ batteries back-to-back all day. You’ll want the P118 fast charger for speed, and this as a secondary overnight top-off unit.

**FAQ: Real Shopper Questions**

**Q: Does it charge 18V and 40V batteries?**  
A: No. The P135 is strictly for 18V One+ lithium batteries. For 40V, you need the P197 charger.

**Q: Can I use it while charging?**  
A: No. It’s a passive charger. You can’t draw power from a battery while it’s seated.

**Q: How do I know when all batteries are done?**  
A: Each slot has its own LED. When all six show solid green, you’re fully charged.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you own multiple RYOBI tools and want a single, tidy charging station that handles your whole fleet overnight. It’s a smart investment for organization and battery longevity (it prevents overcharging).  

**Skip it if** you need fast turnaround on a single battery during active work—this won’t replace your rapid charger.  

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It does exactly what it promises, just not as fast as the name suggests.
