## What's in the Box

## FAQ

**Q: Is this cylinder DOT certified for transport?**
Yes, it's stamped with DOT-3AA specifications, which is the standard for high-pressure gas cylinders in the U.S. You can legally transport it in your personal vehicle.

**Q: Can I use this for 100% CO2 instead of 75/25 mix?**
The CGA 580 valve is correct for both, but confirm with your supplier. Some shops won't swap a cylinder that's designated for mixed gas into pure CO2 service due to safety and purity standards.

**Q: How long does a fill last?**
At 15-20 CFH (cubic feet per hour) on a typical hobbyist MIG setup, you're looking at 6-8 hours of actual arc time. For most people, that's 3-6 months of regular weekend welding.
