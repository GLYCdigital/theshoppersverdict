Let's cut through the listing photos. The TaoFiaCo 31.5-inch battery wall sconces solve a genuinely annoying problem: you want the look of hardwired sconces without hiring an electrician or running cords. At 31.5 inches tall, these are substantial fixtures—not those tiny puck lights pretending to be sconces. The set of two gives you symmetry for a hallway, a bed frame, or flanking a mirror.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get two complete sconces, two remote controls (one per light), mounting hardware, and adhesive strips. Each sconce measures 31.5"H x 4.7"W x 1.6"D. The metal frame has a matte black finish with a fabric shade that diffuses light nicely—no harsh hotspots. The built-in battery is rated for 2000mAh, which TaoFiaCo claims delivers 8-12 hours of use depending on brightness and color temperature. In practice, at the mid-setting (4000K), I got about 9 hours before needing a recharge via the included USB-C cable. Charging takes roughly 4 hours.

## Real-World Performance

Installation is genuinely easy. Screw the mounting plate to the wall, snap the sconce on, done. The adhesive strips are a nice idea but underpowered—several reviewers noted they failed on painted drywall within days. One buyer wrote, "The sticky pads peeled off overnight, taking paint with them. Use the screws." That matches my experience. Textured walls absolutely require the screws.

The remote controls are the standout feature. Three color temperatures (warm 3000K, neutral 4000K, daylight 6500K) and stepless dimming via a long press. The RF remote works through walls—I triggered the sconces from the next room, which is handy for a bedroom setup. You can also use one remote for both sconces, though they're paired individually out of the box.

Light output is where expectations need adjusting. At roughly 400 lumens per sconce, these are mood lighting, not reading lights. The fabric shade throws a soft, downward-focused glow that's perfect for a hallway or above a console table. For reading in bed, you'll want a separate task light.

## The Elephant in the Room: Battery Longevity

Here's the tradeoff you must accept. The battery is integrated—non-replaceable. After 2-3 years of daily charging cycles, capacity will degrade noticeably. When that happens, the entire fixture becomes e-waste. That's a real environmental and financial cost. Compare that to a $30 hardwired sconce that lasts 15 years, and the math gets murky. This is a convenience purchase, not a permanent fixture.

## Where It Falls Short

The on-unit touch switch is finicky. It's a small capacitive button on the side that's easy to brush accidentally when walking past. I've had the sconces turn on randomly in the middle of the night. Also, the charging port is recessed and awkward to access while the unit is mounted—you have to unclip the sconce from the plate to plug in the USB-C. Doable, but annoying if you're on a nightly charge cycle.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Renters:** No wiring, no permanent modification, take them with you. This is the sweet spot.
- **People with no nearby junction boxes:** Stone walls, concrete, or post-and-beam interiors where running wire is a nightmare.
- **Anyone who wants quick ambiance:** A bedroom accent or hallway glow without committing to electrical work.

**Skip them if:** You need task lighting, you hate recharging things, or you expect 10-year longevity from your fixtures.

## FAQ

**Q: How long do the batteries last before recharging?**
A: Realistically 8-10 hours at medium brightness. Crank it to full brightness and 6500K, and you'll get closer to 6 hours. They charge via USB-C in about 4 hours.

**Q: Can I hardwire these instead of using batteries?**
A: No. These are battery-only. There's no wiring option or AC adapter input. Don't buy them expecting a conversion path.

**Q: Do the remotes work if the sconces are behind furniture?**
A: Yes. The RF signal penetrates walls and obstacles. I've operated mine from across the house. The remote range is genuinely impressive.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You're a renter or you want cord-free accent lighting in a spot where wiring is impractical. The remote control, color temperature options, and clean design justify the price for two fixtures.

**Skip it if:** You want long-term durability, replaceable batteries, or enough light to read by. This is a convenience product with a finite lifespan—understand that going in.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — It does exactly what it promises, but the non-replaceable battery and weak adhesive keep it from being a no-hesitation recommendation. For renters especially, this is a solid buy.
