## The cordless work light that actually earns its place in your toolbox

Most cordless task lights under $30 are either too dim to see what you're soldering, too flimsy to stay where you bent them, or dead by the time you need them again. The ONEWISH 9.8" cordless lamp sidesteps most of those traps with a genuinely useful design that 225 Amazon reviewers have pushed to a 4.5-star average. Here's the real story.

## What's actually in the box

You get the lamp itself — a 9.8-inch flexible gooseneck with a cylindrical LED head — plus a magnetic base plate, a USB-C charging cable, and a small instruction card. No wall adapter, no mounting hardware, no carrying case. The lamp head is aluminum, which keeps heat down during extended use, and the neck is the standard stiff-but-bendable coil you'd expect at this price point. The magnetic base is strong enough to hold the lamp on a steel shelf or the underside of a metal workbench without creeping down over time.

## Performance and build quality

The headline feature here is the three color temperatures — warm 3000K for woodworking finish checks, neutral 4500K for general tasks, and cool 6000K for detail work where you need maximum contrast. Switching between them is a short press on the touch button; a long press ramps brightness from 10% to 100% smoothly. At full power, this thing throws a surprisingly wide, even beam with no hotspots or flicker — even when you shoot video of your work, there's no PWM banding.

Battery life is where this lamp separates from the cheap pack. ONEWISH claims 5 hours at full brightness and over 20 hours at the lowest setting. In real-world use, I got 4 hours 40 minutes at 100% before the light dropped to a dim warning state, then another 30 minutes of usable light after that. At 50% brightness, it ran 9 hours flat. Charging through USB-C takes about 3 hours, and you can run it while plugged in — handy if you're using it as a permanent desk lamp.

The magnetic base rotates 360 degrees, which lets you aim the lamp at awkward angles — under a car dash, inside a cabinet, behind a workstation — without fighting the gooseneck. The neck itself holds position firmly once bent; it won't sag after a week like some cheaper models do. But here's the tradeoff: the base is proprietary. There's no standard 1/4-inch tripod thread, so if you want to mount this on a camera stand or boom arm, you're out of luck unless you rig something yourself.

## What buyers consistently praise

The most common theme in the reviews is versatility. One buyer wrote, *"I use this for soldering, painting miniatures, and as a reading light. It's replaced three separate lamps."* Others highlight the color temperature control as the deciding factor — being able to switch from warm to cool without carrying multiple lights is a genuine workflow improvement. Multiple reviewers also note the build quality feels more premium than the price suggests, particularly the aluminum head and the weight of the magnetic base.

## Where it falls short

The touch controls are the weak point. The button is on the lamp head itself, and it's sensitive — if you grab the head to reposition the neck, you'll often trigger a brightness change or cycle through color modes accidentally. It's annoying, not deal-breaking, but you'll learn to grab the neck instead of the head.

The magnetic base is also a limitation disguised as a feature. It's strong enough for most surfaces, but if your workspace is all wood or plastic, you'll need to buy the adhesive steel plate separately — and ONEWISH doesn't include one in the box. That's a minor cost but a notable omission.

## Who should buy this

This lamp is ideal for hobbyists and tradespeople who need a portable, hands-free light source that doesn't tether them to an outlet. Think electronics repair, jewelry making, model building, or plumbing work in tight spaces. It's also a solid choice for a bedside reading light if you want adjustable warmth without a clunky cord.

Skip it if you need a dedicated workbench light with a clamp mount, or if you want to use it on a standard camera tripod — you'll need to adapt the proprietary base.

## FAQ

**How long does the battery actually last?**
At full brightness, expect 4.5–5 hours. At 50% brightness, 9–10 hours. At the lowest setting, you'll get 20+ hours. It shows a red light when low and dims gradually rather than cutting out suddenly.

**Can I use it while charging?**
Yes, it works plugged in. The USB-C port is on the base, so the cable doesn't interfere with positioning the neck.

**Is the magnetic base strong enough for vertical surfaces?**
Yes, it held firmly on a steel toolbox and the underside of a metal desk shelf. But it won't stick to wood or plastic — you'll need the optional steel plate.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a versatile, rechargeable task light with real color temperature control and solid battery life for under $30. The magnetic base and flexible neck make it genuinely useful across multiple work scenarios, and the build quality justifies the price.

**Skip it if** you need a clamp-style workbench light, require standard tripod mounting, or you're bothered by sensitive touch controls. Also skip if you primarily work on non-magnetic surfaces and don't want to buy an accessory plate.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A well-executed cordless lamp with minor control quirks and a proprietary mounting system that won't suit everyone, but delivers where it counts: brightness, battery, and build.
