**Joyunleo Wall Sconces Review: The 22.8-Inch Tube Glass Lighting That Actually Looks Expensive**

Walk into most big-box stores and you'll find wall sconces that look like they were stamped out of tin foil. The Joyunleo Tube Glass Sconce is not that. At 22.8 inches long, this chrome-finished fixture punches well above its price class, and with 235 Amazon ratings averaging 4.7 stars, it's clearly striking a chord. But is it right for *your* wall? Let's dig into the details.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains two sconces (yes, a pair — that's the smart move), each with a metal backplate, an angled arm, and a clear glass tube shade. The glass is seeded — that subtle textured look that scatters light — and it's real glass, not acrylic. The chrome finish is polished and reflective, not brushed or matte. You also get mounting hardware and clear instructions. What you don't get: bulbs. These take E12 candelabra base bulbs, max 40W. Budget for two LED bulbs per fixture.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The first thing you'll notice is the weight. These aren't hollow shells. The metal arm feels substantial, and the backplate sits flush against the wall with no gap — a common complaint with cheaper sconces. Hardwiring is straightforward: standard black/white/ground wires, and the included mounting screws are long enough for drywall but you'll want anchors for plaster.

Light output depends entirely on your bulb choice. With a 40W equivalent LED (about 450 lumens), you get warm, even illumination perfect for a hallway or reading nook. The seeded glass does its job — there's no hot spot, no harsh shadow. One buyer noted, *"The glass diffuses the light beautifully. My hallway looks like a boutique hotel now."* Another said, *"I was worried the chrome would look cheap, but it's genuinely reflective and well-made."*

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Across the reviews, three themes dominate. First, the aesthetics: the mid-century modern vibe works in both farmhouse and contemporary settings. Second, the size — 22.8 inches is long enough to make a statement above a bathroom mirror or flanking a bed, but not so long it overwhelms a standard 8-foot ceiling. Third, the value. Multiple reviewers compared it to fixtures costing three times as much. One wrote, *"I ordered these for my rental makeover and got compliments from every guest."*

**Where It Falls Short**

No product is perfect, and this one has two real caveats. First, the hardwire-only design is a hassle for renters. There's no plug-in version, so if you can't cut into your walls, these aren't for you. Second, the glass tubes are delicate. Two reviewers reported breakage during shipping — the packaging is decent but not bulletproof. Check the box immediately upon arrival and file a claim if the glass is cracked. Also, the chrome finish will show fingerprints; keep a microfiber cloth handy.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Homeowners updating a hallway or stairwell** — the length and light diffusion are ideal for narrow spaces.
- **Bathroom vanity lighting** — install two above a 48-inch mirror and you'll have shadow-free task lighting.
- **Mid-century modern enthusiasts** — the seeded glass and chrome are period-correct without being kitschy.

**Skip it if** you're renting (no plug-in option), or if you need directional light for reading — these cast ambient light, not focused beams.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use these with a dimmer switch?**  
A: Yes, but only if you use dimmable LED bulbs. The fixture itself doesn't have a built-in dimmer, but it's fully compatible with standard wall dimmers.

**Q: How far from the wall do they project?**  
A: About 6.5 inches from the wall to the front of the glass. That's shallow enough for hallways but gives enough clearance for the light to spread.

**Q: Are they easy to install for a beginner?**  
A: If you've wired a ceiling light before, yes. The wires are clearly labeled, and the backplate has four screw holes for alignment. If you've never touched wiring, hire an electrician — it's not worth the risk.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want high-end looks without the high-end price, and you're comfortable with a basic hardwire install. **Skip it if** you're renting or need focused task lighting. At 4.6/5, this is one of the best-value sconces in its size class — just buy bulbs with it and you're set.
