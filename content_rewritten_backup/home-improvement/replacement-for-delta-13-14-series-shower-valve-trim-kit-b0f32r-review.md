If your Delta 13/14 series shower valve works fine but looks like it survived a decade of hard water and aggressive scrubbing, you've got two options: pay Delta's OEM premium or grab this aftermarket trim kit. At roughly half the price of the branded equivalent, this replacement kit is the kind of product that makes you wonder why you didn't swap that ugly handle years ago.

**What's actually in the box**

The kit includes the handle, escutcheon (that's the round wall plate), and the mounting hardware you'll need — set screw, Allen wrench, and the adapter if your valve configuration requires it. As shown in the product image, it's a clean, minimalist design that fits the standard Delta 13/14 series rough-in valve. Nothing more, nothing less. No instructions, though — so if you're not the type who can figure out a trim swap by looking at it, you'll want to watch a quick YouTube video first.

**The real-world install**

The swap took me about 15 minutes, and that included hunting for my Allen wrench set. The old trim comes off by removing the handle screw and unscrewing the escutcheon. New one goes on in reverse. The critical part is making sure the adapter seats properly on the valve stem — if it's not fully engaged, the handle will feel loose or won't turn the valve at all. Several buyers noted this specifically:

*"Had to fiddle with the adapter to get it seated right, but once it clicked into place, it works perfectly. Looks like the $80 Delta version my buddy has."*

That's the consistent story across the 220 ratings. Most people get it right, but a handful of reviewers mentioned the adapter fitting snugly — not a defect, just a tolerance thing. If you're replacing trim that's been on the wall for 15+ years, the old stem might have mineral buildup. Clean it with vinegar before installing.

**Build quality and finish**

The handle is zinc die-cast with a brushed nickel finish. It feels solid, not hollow or cheap. The escutcheon plate is the same material — it's not a flimsy stamped metal disc that flexes when you push on it. Water spots wipe off easily, which is more than I can say for some cheap chrome trims I've dealt with.

The one honest knock against this kit: the finish won't be an exact match to OEM Delta parts if you're only replacing the handle and keeping the old showerhead or other fixtures. It's close — most people won't notice — but if you're a stickler about matching finishes across your bathroom, buy the whole trim set at once. One reviewer put it bluntly:

*"The brushed nickel is slightly warmer than my Delta faucet. Not a big deal unless you're OCD about it."*

**Where it falls short**

Beyond the finish variance, the set screw is genuinely small. If you crank it down with force, you'll strip the hex head and then you're dealing with a handle that won't stay tight. Use a light touch — snug is enough.

Also, depending on which version you receive, some buyers reported no temperature limit stop adjustment. That's the little mechanism that lets you cap how hot the water gets — important if you have kids or elderly family members. If that matters to you, verify the listing details before ordering.

**Who should buy this**

- **DIY homeowners** with a Delta 13/14 series valve and a dated-looking handle. This is a 15-minute project that transforms your shower's look for less than a dinner out.
- **Landlords** refreshing rental bathrooms on a budget. It's a solid, functional upgrade that doesn't look cheap.
- **Skip it if** you need an exact OEM finish match or you're not comfortable with basic hand-tool work — though honestly, this is about as beginner-friendly as plumbing repairs get.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Will this fit my Delta 13/14 series valve?**
If your valve is a Delta 13 or 14 series (check the rough-in valve body for the model number stamped on it), this kit is designed to fit. The adapter accommodates both series. If you have a different series, it won't work — verify before buying.

**Do I need to shut off the water supply to install this?**
No. The trim kit is entirely on the outside of the wall — you're not touching the valve body or any water-carrying parts. Just remove the old handle and plate, install the new one. No water involved.

**Is this the same quality as the OEM Delta part?**
It's comparable in construction (zinc, not plastic), but the finish isn't an exact match and there's no temperature limit stop on some versions. For the price difference, most buyers find it acceptable.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want to refresh your shower's look without paying OEM prices or calling a plumber. It's a well-built, straightforward replacement that the majority of 220+ reviewers found easy to install and good-looking on the wall.

**Skip it if** you need a perfect finish match with existing Delta fixtures, or you require the anti-scald temperature limit adjustment. Those are legitimate reasons to go OEM.

At 4.4 stars with consistent praise for the value and fit, this kit earns a solid **4.2/5**. It's not flashy — it's a shower handle. But it does exactly what it promises, and for most people, that's more than enough.
