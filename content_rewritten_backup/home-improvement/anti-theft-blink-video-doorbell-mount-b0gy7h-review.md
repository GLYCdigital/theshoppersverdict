Let's cut straight to it: a $35–$50 video doorbell is a tempting target for thieves, especially in apartment buildings or homes where the doorbell sits within arm's reach of the sidewalk. The Anti-Theft Blink Video Doorbell Mount exists to solve that exact problem — but does it actually work, or is it just a chunk of metal with a scary name?

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes the aluminum mounting bracket, two tamper-resistant Torx security screws with a unique head pattern, and a mounting plate spacer. The bracket is CNC-machined from a single piece of aluminum — no welded joints, no stamped sheet metal. The powder-coated finish is consistent and feels durable, not like the cheap spray-paint jobs you see on budget mounts.

Installation takes about five minutes. The bracket uses the existing screw holes on the back of your Blink doorbell, so you're not modifying the device itself. You mount the bracket to your wall or door frame with the included anchors, then secure the doorbell to the bracket with the Torx screws. Once those are torqued down, the doorbell is essentially locked in place.

**The Security Reality**

Here's what the marketing doesn't tell you: there is no such thing as a truly theft-proof mount. What this bracket does is raise the difficulty threshold. A thief with a Torx bit set and a few minutes can still remove it. But the kind of opportunist who grabs a doorbell and runs — which is the vast majority of doorbell thefts — won't have the right bit. That's the practical value here.

One buyer put it well: *"Someone tried to rip it off my door frame while I was at work. They got the doorbell partially loose but couldn't get the security screws out. The scratches on the bracket prove they tried. Doorbell was still there when I got home."*

That's the real-world outcome. It converts a 30-second theft into a 10-minute project requiring specialized tools. Most thieves won't bother.

**Where It Falls Short**

The depth issue is real. The bracket adds noticeable thickness, and if you have a narrow door frame or a pre-existing angled mount for the Blink doorbell, you might find the fit awkward. The offset design helps, but it's not universal.

The missing Torx bit is the bigger annoyance. For a product whose entire purpose is security, making buyers source the security bit separately is a head-scratcher. It's a $5 part that would've made the package complete. As another reviewer noted: *"Great mount, but I had to order a Torx security bit set from Amazon before I could even install it. Should come with the bit."*

Also worth noting: this only fits the standard Blink Video Doorbell, not the Blink Video Doorbell Plus or the newer Blink Mini-based setups. Check your model before ordering.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Apartment dwellers** whose doorbell is visible from a public walkway — this is your primary market
- **Renters** who can't make permanent modifications and need a mount that uses existing holes
- **Anyone** who's already had a doorbell stolen and doesn't want to repeat the experience

Skip it if you live in a low-crime area, have a doorbell mounted high enough to be out of reach, or own a Blink model other than the standard Video Doorbell.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a practical deterrent that makes doorbell theft genuinely difficult for the average opportunist. The aluminum construction is solid, the fit is precise, and the security screws do their job.

**Skip it if** you're expecting Fort Knox-level security, don't want to buy a separate Torx bit set, or your mounting situation is unusual.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — loses points only for the missing security bit and the compatibility limitations. For what it does — protecting a $40 device from a $5 theft — it earns its place on your door frame.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit my Blink Video Doorbell Plus?**
A: No. This mount is specifically designed for the standard Blink Video Doorbell. The Plus model has a different rear housing and screw pattern. Check your model number before purchasing.

**Q: Can I still use the doorbell's chime functionality?**
A: Yes. The mount doesn't interfere with the wiring or the internal chime mechanism. The doorbell functions exactly as it would without the mount.

**Q: How do I remove the doorbell if I need to charge it?**
A: You'll need the Torx security bit. Once you have it, removal takes about 30 seconds. If you lose the bit, you'll have to drill out the screws — which is exactly what a thief would face, but it's also what you'll face if you're not careful with the bit.
