If you've ever had a "heavy duty" magnetic hook drop your tools onto a concrete floor, you know the pain. The MIKEDE Magnetic Hooks claim to solve that with a swivel swing design and serious neodymium magnets. With 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, the consensus is clear: these are genuinely strong hooks. But they're not magic, and there are real limits you need to understand before buying.

**What you actually get**

The package includes multiple hooks (depending on your chosen pack size), each with a neodymium magnet base wrapped in a rubber coating, a swivel joint, and a metal hook. The magnet surface is roughly 1.5 inches in diameter, and the overall length runs about 3 inches from base to hook tip. The rubber coating isn't just for looks — it prevents scratching on painted surfaces and adds grip so the hook doesn't slide around on smooth metal.

**Real-world performance**

The headline feature here is the swivel swing mechanism. Unlike fixed hooks where you have to awkwardly twist whatever you're hanging, these let the hook rotate 360 degrees. That matters more than you'd think. One buyer noted, "I use these on my garage pegboard and the swivel lets me access tools from any angle without unhooking them." The joint is sturdy — no wobble or sagging once locked into position.

The magnet strength is genuinely impressive for the size. Multiple buyers report hanging heavy items without failure. One review specifically mentioned: "Holds my 10-pound fire extinguisher on the wall of my RV with zero issues." That matches the product's rated capacity, which is notable — many brands overstate their specs.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Reviewers across the board highlight three things: the strength-to-size ratio, the rubber coating, and the swivel action. A recurring theme in positive reviews is cruise ship use — passengers hanging lanyards, towels, and hats on metal cabin walls. The rubber base keeps them from marking the surfaces, which matters when you're in a rented cabin.

**Where it falls short**

Here's the honest part. The rated holding capacity assumes a clean, bare metal surface. If you're mounting these on a painted garage wall or a powder-coated fridge, expect the effective strength to drop by half. One reviewer discovered this the hard way: "Great on bare steel, but on my painted metal shed door, my 5-pound wrench set pulled it off."

The other limitation is directional. These magnets hold best with straight-down (vertical) loads. Apply a sideways or pulling-away force, and they'll release much sooner than the rating suggests. That's physics — but it means don't use these to hold things that get yanked, like a shop vac hose being pulled across a garage.

**Who should buy these**

- **Cruise travelers and RV owners**: Perfect for metal cabin walls and RV interiors — strong enough for real items, gentle enough for surfaces.
- **Garage organizers**: Hang tools, extension cords, and small equipment on metal pegboards or steel shelving.
- **Kitchen or laundry room users**: Mount on the side of a metal fridge for pot holders, measuring cups, or clothespins.

**Skip them if** you need to hang something on drywall, wood, or any non-metal surface. These are magnets — they need steel to work.

**FAQ**

*Q: Will these hold on a stainless steel fridge?*
A: Yes, but stainless steel varies in magnetic properties. Most fridge-grade stainless is magnetic enough. Test with the hook first — if it sticks firmly, you're good. If it slides, the panel may be too thin or non-ferrous.

*Q: How much weight can they actually hold?*
A: On bare steel with a straight-down pull, the rated capacity is realistic. On painted or coated surfaces, cut that number in half. Never use these for overhead applications where a failure could cause injury.

*Q: Do the magnets rust or lose strength over time?*
A: The rubber coating protects the magnet from moisture. Buyers report no degradation after months of use. However, avoid submerging them or leaving them in standing water — the swivel joint has exposed metal parts.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need versatile, genuinely strong magnetic hooks for metal surfaces and appreciate the swivel convenience. At 4.6 stars from 229 buyers, the MIKEDE hooks deliver what they promise for most real-world uses.

**Skip it if** your surfaces are painted or coated, or if you need a directional hold that resists sideways force. For bare-metal hanging with straight-down loads, these are excellent. Just respect their limits and they'll serve you well.
