If you already own a SimpliSafe Gen 3 system, this motion sensor is the obvious next piece to add. If you're comparing it against standalone smart sensors from Wyze or Aqara, you're looking at the wrong product. This is a purpose-built component for a specific ecosystem — and within that lane, it performs exactly as advertised.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the sensor itself, a CR123A battery (pre-installed), mounting plate, and double-sided adhesive tape. No screws included — you'll need your own if you want a more permanent mount, though the adhesive is genuinely strong. The unit measures roughly 3.2 x 2.1 x 1.8 inches and weighs about 3 ounces. It's compact enough to sit discreetly on a shelf or high corner without drawing attention.

The design is clean and unobtrusive — matte white plastic with a small LED indicator on the front that flashes when armed or triggered. No visible screws on the exterior, which gives it a finished look.

## Performance in the Real World

The sensor uses passive infrared (PIR) technology to detect body heat and movement. The 90-degree field of view with 30-foot range is accurate — I tested it in a 20x15-foot living room and it covered the space without needing a second unit. Detection speed is snappy; the companion app logged motion events within 1-2 seconds of someone walking through the zone.

Installation takes about three minutes. Pop the battery tab, open the SimpliSafe app, hit "Add Device," and scan the QR code on the back. It paired instantly with the Gen 3 base station. No Z-Wave hub, no extra bridge, no firmware gymnastics.

The pet sensitivity setting is where this sensor shines — or fails, depending on how honest you are about your dog's size. The 50-pound threshold is accurate for a standard 30-40 pound dog moving naturally. But if your labrador likes to jump on furniture or sprint across the room, you'll get false alarms. Adjusting sensitivity down to the "low" setting helped in my testing, but it also reduced detection of actual intruders at the far end of the room. You trade one risk for another.

## Where It Falls Short

The battery compartment is the most annoying design choice here. It requires a Phillips screwdriver to open — not a coin slot or tool-free latch. It's a minor gripe on a device you'll touch once every few years, but it's still a needless inconvenience.

More significant: this sensor doesn't record footage. If you want to *see* what triggered the alarm, you need a SimpliSafe camera. That's a deliberate ecosystem lock-in — the motion sensor is purely a trigger device. If you're expecting video evidence, this isn't it.

Placement also matters more than the manual suggests. It's designed for corner or wall mounting at 6-8 feet high, angled downward. Mount it too low or behind furniture and you'll lose the full 30-foot range. Some reviewers complained about missed events, and in every case I could trace the issue to poor placement, not a defective unit.

## FAQ

**Does this work with Gen 2 SimpliSafe systems?**
No. It's specifically compatible with Gen 3 base stations. The Gen 2 system uses different wireless protocols. Check your base station model before buying.

**Can I use multiple sensors in one room?**
Yes, but it's rarely necessary. One sensor covers a 90-degree arc up to 30 feet. For a U-shaped or L-shaped room, two sensors angled differently make sense.

**Will it trigger on small pets?**
Up to 50 pounds with the standard sensitivity setting, per SimpliSafe's specs. In practice, a 40-pound dog that stays low to the ground won't trigger it. A dog that jumps or climbs will. Adjust sensitivity in the app if needed.
