### Where It Falls Short

Honesty time. No product is perfect, and this one has two specific tradeoffs you need to know about.

First, **the screws are soft.** Several reviewers mention stripping the Phillips head screws during installation if you use a drill without a clutch. Use a hand screwdriver or a driver set to a low torque setting, and you'll be fine. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

Second, **the privacy lock is not a true bathroom lock.** It's a privacy function, not a security function. The emergency release on the exterior is a hole, not a key. If you need a lockable bathroom door that keeps out determined kids or nosy guests, this isn't it. It's designed for bedrooms and home offices where you want a simple "do not disturb" signal.
