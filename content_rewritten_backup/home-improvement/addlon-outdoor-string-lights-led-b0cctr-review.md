If you've ever squinted at a $15 string light set from a big-box store, you know the drill: plastic sockets that crack by October, bulbs that flicker in the first rain, and a warm glow that's really just a dull yellow haze. The addlon Outdoor String Lights LED is not that product. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, this is the set people buy once and forget about — in the best way.

## What's actually in the box

You get a 48-foot string with 24 weatherproof S14 LED bulbs spaced 24 inches apart, plus a 24-foot lead cord from the grounded plug to the first socket. That 72-foot total reach matters more than you'd think — it clears most patio perimeters without needing an extension cord daisy-chain. The bulbs are 1 watt each (2700K warm white), which means the whole string draws about as much power as a single old-fashioned nightlight.

The build is where addlon separates itself. The sockets are thick, UV-stabilized plastic with IP65-rated gaskets that actually seal. The bulbs themselves are glass, not that thin polycarbonate that yellows after a season. As shown in the product image, the ST38-style bulb shape gives that classic Edison-bulb silhouette without the energy suck.

## Performance: The glow test

Turn them on and the first thing you notice is the color — it's a true warm white, not that sickly green-white cheap LEDs throw. Dimming is smooth if you pair it with a compatible dimmer switch. One buyer noted, "I've had these up for 8 months through a Michigan winter and two thunderstorms. Not a single flicker or dead bulb." That matches the general sentiment across the reviews: these hold up.

## Where it falls short

Be honest with yourself about the lead wire. That 24-foot unlit cord from plug to first bulb is great for reaching a distant outlet but useless if you wanted lights starting right at the wall. Cutting and splicing voids the warranty, so plan your layout before you mount.

There's also the quality-control lottery. A handful of reviewers report a dead bulb on arrival — it's an easy swap since the bulbs are standard E26 base and replaceable, but it's a minor inconvenience on day one. And while the plastic is tough, it's not indestructible; don't run over the cord with a lawnmower and expect forgiveness.

## Who should buy this

- **Patio and deck owners** who want permanent-feeling lighting without hiring an electrician
- **Renters** who need something they can take down and reinstall without leaving holes
- **Small business owners** lighting a café patio or restaurant exterior who need reliable, weatherproof illumination

Skip it if you need a 100-foot run in one continuous set, or if you're on a tight budget and only need lights for a single summer party — the cheaper stuff will survive one season.

## FAQ

**Can these be left outside all year?**
Yes. The IP65 rating covers rain, snow, and UV exposure. The one caveat: don't submerge the sockets in standing water, and check the gaskets after extreme temperature swings.

**Are the bulbs replaceable?**
Yes, they're standard E26 base S14 bulbs. You can buy replacements or upgrade to different color temperatures later.

**Do they work with smart plugs or dimmers?**
The string itself works with most standard dimmer switches, but check your smart plug's compatibility — some don't support dimming and will just turn them on/off.
