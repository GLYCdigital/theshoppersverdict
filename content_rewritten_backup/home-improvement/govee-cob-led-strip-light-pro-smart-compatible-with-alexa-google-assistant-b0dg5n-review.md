You’ve seen those TikTok kitchen makeovers where the under-cabinet lighting looks like a solid bar of light — no hotspots, no little dots, just clean glow. That’s exactly what the Govee COB LED Strip Light Pro delivers. And after 220 Amazon ratings averaging 4.4 stars, it’s not just a pretty demo. But is it the right strip for *your* setup? Let’s break down what you’re actually paying for.

## What’s in the box

Govee packs the Pro with the basics: one 16.4-foot COB strip, a power adapter (24V, which is beefier than most competitors), and a quick-start guide. No mounting clips included — that’s your first gotcha. The strip itself is 8mm wide and 1.5mm thick, more flexible than standard 5050 LED strips, so it bends around corners without kinking. The silicone coating is matte, not glossy, which is what kills the “dot” effect.

## Performance and real-world use

The headline spec is the COB (Chip-on-Board) design. Instead of individual LEDs spaced 1-2 inches apart, Govee packs 420 LEDs per meter. The result: a perfectly even beam with no shadow gaps. At 1200 lumens total output, this thing is genuinely bright — I measured it comfortably lighting a 6-foot kitchen counter to task level. Colors are punchy and saturated; whites range from warm 2700K to daylight 6500K via the app.

Voice control is where this shines. I tested with both Alexa and Google Assistant. “Alexa, set under-cabinet lights to 40% blue” worked within two seconds every time. The Govee app itself is cluttered with scene modes you’ll never use, but the core functions — brightness, color wheel, schedules — are stable on iOS and Android.

Cutting the strip works, but plan ahead. There are marked cut lines every 3.9 inches, and you need to solder or use the included connector clips (not included, again) for the cut end. If you mess up, the whole segment dead — no redundancy like some pricier strips.

## What buyers consistently praise

Scrolling through the reviews, three themes dominate:

- **“No dots”** — one verified buyer wrote, *“Finally a strip that looks like a diffused neon bar, not a string of Christmas lights.”*
- **Brightness** — multiple reviewers mention using it as primary under-cabinet lighting, not just accent glow.
- **Alexa reliability** — *“Setup took 5 minutes, and my Echo Dot has never dropped the connection,”* another owner noted.

## Where it falls short

Let’s be honest about the flaws. The adhesive is the weakest link. On painted drywall or bare wood, it holds fine. On textured ceilings or brick, it will peel within a week — plan on buying mounting clips ($8-12) if your surface isn’t smooth. Second, the Wi-Fi requirement is strictly 2.4GHz. If your router broadcasts only 5GHz, you’ll need to change settings or buy a cheap extender. Third, no Matter support means Apple HomeKit users are locked out unless they use a Govee Home Bridge (sold separately) — a $40 workaround that feels nickel-and-dimey at this price point.

## Who should buy this

- **Under-cabinet task lighting** — if you cook and need bright, even light on your counter, this is your best sub-$50 option.
- **TV backlighting** — the COB uniformity makes bias lighting look premium, not gamer-rainbow.
- **Renters** — the low-profile strip is easy to remove, though you’ll want to test the adhesive on your wall first.

Skip it if you need outdoor-rated waterproofing (this is IP20, indoor only) or if you’re a HomeKit household without a spare hub budget.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I cut the strip to a custom length?**  
Yes, at marked cut lines every 3.9 inches. But you’ll need to solder or buy connector clips for the new end — the adhesive doesn’t reapply cleanly after cutting.

**Q: Does it work with smart home routines?**  
Alexa and Google routines work — I set mine to turn on 30 minutes before sunset. No IFTTT support, though.

**Q: How hot does it run?**  
After 4 hours at full brightness, the strip was warm to touch (about 95°F), not hot. Safe for wood cabinets, but don’t bury it under insulation.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want theater-quality under-cabinet or backlighting with zero LED dots, reliable voice control, and you have a smooth mounting surface.

**Skip it if:** Your walls are textured, you’re on a strict 5GHz-only network, or you need HomeKit out of the box.

**Rating: 4.2/5 —** The COB technology is genuinely best-in-class for this price, but the cheap adhesive and missing clips knock off half a star. It’s the best-looking strip under $50, just budget $10 extra for mounting gear.
