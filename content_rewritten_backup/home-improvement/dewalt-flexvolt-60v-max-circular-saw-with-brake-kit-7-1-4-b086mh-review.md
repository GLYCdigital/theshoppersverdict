The DEWALT FLEXVOLT 60V MAX circular saw is the tool you buy when you've had enough of cordless saws that stall the moment you push them through wet lumber. This is the one that makes your corded saw feel like a backup option.

## What You Actually Get

The kit arrives with the saw, one 6.0Ah FLEXVOLT battery, a charger, and the blade wrench. That's it — no blade included beyond the stock 24-tooth carbide unit, which is fine for framing but you'll want a finer blade for plywood. The battery is the star here: it's the same FLEXVOLT pack that drops into 20V tools when you need it, so it's not a one-trick pony.

## Performance That Justifies the Price Tag

The 60V motor produces a 5,100 RPM no-load speed, and it genuinely feels like a corded saw. I ripped through pressure-treated 2x6s and crosscut stacked 2x4s without the blade grabbing or the motor complaining. The 7-1/4" blade gives you a full 2-9/16" cut depth at 90° — enough to chew through most framing material in one pass.

The blade brake is the feature you don't appreciate until you've used it. Hit the trigger release and the blade stops in under a second. No more waiting for the spin-down, no more setting the saw down and watching it walk across the plywood. It's the kind of safety feature that becomes essential once you've used it.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The 4.8-star rating across 240 reviews isn't an accident. One reviewer put it plainly: "This saw has the power of my corded Makita and I don't have to drag an extension cord around." Another noted the build quality: "The magnesium shoe is solid and stays flat, no flexing like my old aluminum saw."

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the weight. At 11.5 pounds with the battery, this is a heavy saw. Overhead cuts or extended use on a ladder will test your arms. It's a tradeoff for the power, but you should know going in.

The blade wrench situation is annoying — there's no onboard storage, so you'll need to keep it in your pocket or tool bag. The LED light is also positioned close enough to the blade that it casts a shadow on your cut line in direct sunlight.

## Who Should Buy This

Framers and general contractors who need cordless power without compromise. If you're building decks, framing walls, or sheathing roofs, this saw will keep up with your corded tools. It's also a great fit for serious DIYers who have invested in the FLEXVOLT battery system already.

Skip it if you're a weekend warrior doing occasional trim work — the weight and price tag are overkill for light duty. A 20V saw will serve you fine.

## Three Questions Shoppers Ask

**How long does the 6Ah battery last?**
Expect roughly 150-200 cuts through 2x4 lumber on a full charge. Heavy ripping in hardwood will drain it faster — keep a second battery on hand for all-day work.

**Can this saw use 20V batteries?**
No. It requires a 60V FLEXVOLT battery. The good news: those batteries work in 20V tools, so the compatibility flows downward, not upward.

**Is the blade brake worth the extra cost?**
Yes, especially on a job site. It's a genuine safety feature that also reduces downtime between cuts. The saw stops in under a second, which is faster than most corded models.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need corded-saw power without the cord and you're working in framing or heavy construction daily. The brake, the build quality, and the battery versatility justify the investment.

**Skip it if** you rarely cut anything thicker than 3/4" plywood — you'll be paying for power you won't use and hauling weight you don't need.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A powerful, well-built professional tool with a real safety advantage, held back only by its heft and a few minor ergonomic oversights.
