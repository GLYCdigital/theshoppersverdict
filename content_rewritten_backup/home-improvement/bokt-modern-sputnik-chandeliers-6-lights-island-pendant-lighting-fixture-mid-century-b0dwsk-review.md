Sputnik chandeliers are having a moment, and the BOKT 6-light version is one of the more popular options on Amazon — 220 ratings, 4.4 stars. But popularity alone doesn't tell you whether this thing will look cheap in your dining room or hold up after a year of dusting. Here's the real breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the chandelier body, six individual arms, a mounting plate, canopy, and hardware. No bulbs — that's important. The fixture measures roughly 23.5 inches in diameter and 12 inches tall, with a 59-inch chain that can be shortened. It's a substantial piece, not a flimsy accent light.

The construction is steel with a matte black finish. The arms screw into the central hub, and each ends in an E26 socket with a small gold accent ring around the base. It's a nice touch that elevates the design beyond a plain black starburst.

## Installation Reality

Here's where the 4.4-star rating starts to make sense. The wiring is straightforward — black to black, white to white, ground to the mounting plate. Standard stuff for anyone who's changed a fixture before.

But the arms? Plan for 30–45 minutes of fiddling. Each arm threads into the hub, and you need to position them evenly while not over-tightening. Several reviewers noted the same thing: "The arms are a little tricky to align — one person needs to hold the hub while the other screws in the arms." Do this before mounting the fixture to the ceiling. Trust me.

The canopy covers a standard 4-inch junction box cleanly. If you have a sloped ceiling, the chain and swivel mount handle up to about 45 degrees, which is better than many comparably priced fixtures.

## Performance and Light Output

With six E26 sockets, you control the brightness entirely through your bulb choice. Use six 60-watt equivalent LED bulbs and you'll get roughly 4,500–5,000 lumens — plenty for a 6–8 foot island. The fixture works with dimmable switches provided your bulbs are dimmable, which is a plus for evening ambiance.

One thing to note: this is a statement light, not a task light. The bulbs point outward in different directions, so you'll get ambient glow rather than focused downlighting. If you need direct task lighting for food prep, pair it with under-cabinet fixtures.

## What Buyers Praise (and What They Don't)

The design is the star. Reviewers consistently call it "modern" and "sleek," with many noting it looks far more expensive than the price tag suggests. One buyer said, "The matte black finish is perfect — no fingerprints, no glare, just clean lines." Another mentioned the gold accents "add just enough warmth without being gaudy."

The complaints are mostly about assembly and missing bulbs. A few reviewers received units with slightly bent arms — likely shipping damage, not a manufacturing defect, but inspect carefully on arrival. The gold ring on the socket base has also drawn some criticism for looking "brassier" than the product photos suggest. It's subtle, but if you're pairing this with brushed nickel or chrome fixtures, it might clash.

## Who Should Buy This

This chandelier is ideal for:
- **Mid-century modern enthusiasts** who want an authentic Sputnik look without paying boutique prices
- **Kitchen island owners** with 6–8 foot islands who want a central statement piece
- **DIY installers** comfortable with basic wiring and willing to spend 30–45 minutes on assembly

Skip it if you need task lighting, have a very low ceiling (under 8 feet), or want a fixture with included bulbs. Also skip if you're expecting a polished, high-end finish — this is good for the price, but it's not a $400 designer piece.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this be installed on a sloped ceiling?**
A: Yes, the swivel mount accommodates slopes up to approximately 45 degrees. The chain can also be angled slightly for most standard vaulted ceilings.

**Q: What bulbs should I use?**
A: Use E26 base LED bulbs. For a warm, cozy feel, pick 2700K–3000K. For brighter task-adjacent lighting, go 4000K. Avoid incandescent — six bulbs generate significant heat and the open design won't hide them well.

**Q: How do I clean it?**
A: The matte black finish wipes down with a dry microfiber cloth. Use a damp cloth with mild soap for stubborn dust, then dry immediately. The arms are exposed, so dust will accumulate — plan to wipe it down every 2–3 weeks.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a genuine mid-century Sputnik look at a budget price, you're comfortable with basic wiring, and you don't mind buying bulbs separately.

**Skip it if:** You need a quick 15-minute install, want a fixture with included bulbs, or require focused task lighting over your workspace.

The BOKT 6-light chandelier delivers strong design value at a competitive price point. The 4.4-star rating is earned — most buyers are happy with the look and performance. Deduct half a star for the assembly fiddliness and the bulb situation, and you land at a solid 4.2. For the price, it's one of the better Sputnik options on Amazon right now.
