If you've been scrolling past a dozen "farmhouse" pendants that all look identical, the BOKT Vintage Sphere stops the thumb. This isn't a revolutionary design — it's a 6.3-inch steel sphere with an antique brass finish and a glass diffuser that sits inside the shade. What makes it notable is execution: the materials feel denser than the price suggests, and the reversible mounting genuinely changes the character of the light. It's the kind of fixture that looks like it costs $150, not whatever the current listing price happens to be.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the pendant canopy, a 59-inch adjustable chain, the sphere shade, and mounting hardware. No bulbs — that's important. The fixture uses a standard E26 base, so any medium-base bulb works, but you'll want vintage-style Edison bulbs to match the aesthetic. One buyer noted, "The glass is a warm amber tone that filters light beautifully, but you have to buy your own bulbs to get that glow."

The chain is genuinely adjustable. You can hang it as low as 12 inches from the ceiling or extend it to nearly 6 feet, which covers most island heights and vaulted ceilings. The canopy is 4.7 inches across — standard size that covers most junction boxes without needing a gap plate.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The shade is steel, not the thin aluminum you find on budget fixtures. It has a slight weight to it — roughly 3 pounds total — which tells you it won't warp or dent during installation. The antique brass finish is brushed, not shiny, which means fewer visible smudges and a more muted, period-appropriate look.

Light output depends entirely on your bulb choice. With a standard 60W equivalent LED, you get comfortable task lighting for a 4–6 foot island. With a dimmer switch and dimmable bulbs, buyers consistently report smooth adjustment. One reviewer wrote: "I installed two over a 6-foot island and the light is warm and even — no harsh shadows. The dimmer range is better than my previous $200 fixture."

The glass diffuser inside the sphere softens the bulb's glare. This is worth emphasizing: without that inner glass, you'd get a harsh hotspot with Edison bulbs. The design handles this correctly.

**Where it falls short**

The reversible mounting is advertised as a feature, but it's fiddly. You have to loosen the set screw, flip the shade, and re-tighten — not difficult, but the instructions are sparse. Plan for 10 minutes of trial and error.

Hardwiring only. There's no plug-in cord option, no battery, no solar. If you're a renter or uncomfortable with basic electrical work, you'll need a licensed electrician, which can add $100–200 to your total cost. Several buyers mentioned this in reviews: "Installation is straightforward if you've done a light before, but don't attempt it if you're not confident with wiring."

The 59-inch chain is generous, but if your ceiling is over 10 feet, you'll need to buy an extension chain separately.

**Who should buy this**

- **Kitchen island owners** with a 4–8 foot island who want warm, dimmable task lighting with a vintage-industrial look.
- **Dining room and foyer installations** where a single pendant makes a statement — this fixture reads well in both settings.
- **DIYers comfortable with basic hardwiring** who want a quality fixture without paying boutique prices.
- **Anyone tired of glass-globe pendants** that show every fingerprint — the brushed finish is genuinely low-maintenance.

**Skip it if** you're a renter needing a plug-in solution, you want a fixture with bulbs included, or you prefer a polished, shiny finish over the muted antique look.

**FAQ**

**Q: What bulb type does this use?**
A: Standard E26 base. Any LED, incandescent, or Edison-style bulb works. For the advertised look, buy vintage-style LED Edison bulbs (around $12–20 for a 2-pack). For dimming, ensure both the bulb and your switch are dimmable.

**Q: Can this be installed on a sloped or vaulted ceiling?**
A: Yes, the chain mounting allows it to hang plumb on sloped ceilings up to roughly 45 degrees. The canopy swivels at the ceiling plate.

**Q: Is the glass removable for cleaning?**
A: Yes. The inner glass diffuser lifts out without tools, and the outer shade wipes clean with a dry cloth. The brushed finish doesn't show dust as much as polished alternatives.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a well-built, dimmable pendant with a genuinely vintage character that won't break the bank. The reversible mounting is a real bonus — you can change the room's mood without buying a new fixture.

**Skip it if** you need plug-in installation, want bulbs in the box, or prefer a bright, modern look over the warm, amber-toned vintage aesthetic.
