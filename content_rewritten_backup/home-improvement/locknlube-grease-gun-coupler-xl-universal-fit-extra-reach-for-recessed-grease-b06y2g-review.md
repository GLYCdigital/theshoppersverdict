## What You Actually Get

The XL version builds on LockNLube's proven locking coupler design with a roughly 2-inch longer neck and a 45° angled head. The package includes the coupler itself, a spare rubber seal, and a small instruction card. No grease gun included — this replaces the stock coupler on your existing gun (standard 1/8" NPT threads fit most models). The coupler body is machined steel with a brass core and a replaceable rubber tip that creates the seal against the zerk fitting.

## Performance: Where It Shines

The headline feature is the 360° swivel collar combined with that angled head. In practice, this means you can approach a recessed fitting from an angle that keeps your hand clear of hot manifolds or spinning driveline components. The locking mechanism is the real differentiator: push it onto the zerk, twist the collar, and it's locked on with a satisfying click. You can literally let go of the coupler and it stays seated while you pump the gun with both hands.

One buyer with a fleet of farm tractors put it plainly: "I've thrown away three cheap couplers this year. This one has survived a full season of greasing balers and loaders without leaking a drop." That durability claim tracks — the brass core resists the galling that kills cheaper zinc couplers, and the replaceable seal means you don't toss the whole unit when the rubber finally wears.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The most repeated compliment in the Amazon reviews is the lock-on feature. One verified purchaser wrote: "The locking collar is a game-changer. The extra reach also earns consistent shout-outs. Owners of heavy trucks, tractors, and industrial equipment specifically mention reaching fittings they previously skipped or had to disassemble to access.

## Where It Falls Short

The XL's length is a double-edged sword. At roughly 5 inches from base to tip, it's noticeably bulkier than a standard coupler. If you're greasing tight spaces where a standard coupler barely fits — think tie rod ends on compact cars or zero-turn mower spindles — the XL can be too long to maneuver. This is a tradeoff, not a flaw, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

The locking collar also requires a deliberate twist to release. With greasy hands or heavy work gloves, that twist can feel stiff at first. It loosens up with use, but it's not as quick to detach as a simple push-on coupler. A handful of reviewers also mentioned that the collar can be finicky to align perfectly with the zerk on the first try — it takes a few uses to build muscle memory.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You grease tractors, trailers, heavy trucks, or any equipment with recessed or hard-to-reach fittings. The lock-on feature is a genuine safety upgrade — it keeps your hands away from moving parts and hot surfaces. DIYers with a single car will also appreciate it for those once-a-year chassis lubes.

**Skip it if:** You only grease easy-access fittings on small vehicles and want the smallest profile possible. A standard coupler is lighter, shorter, and cheaper. Also skip it if you're on a tight budget — this costs notably more than a basic brass coupler, though you're paying for the locking mechanism and reach.

## FAQ

**Will this fit my grease gun?**
Yes, if your gun uses a standard 1/8" NPT thread — that covers virtually all manual and cordless grease guns on the market. If you have an oddball European or industrial gun, check the thread pitch before ordering.

**Does it work with all zerk fittings?**
It fits standard 1/4" and 1/8" grease fittings. If you have metric or unusual fittings, you'll need an adapter. The locking collar works best with a clean, undamaged zerk — a burred or rusty fitting will compromise the seal.

**Is the seal replaceable?**
Yes, LockNLube sells replacement seals separately. The coupler includes one spare in the box. Most users report the original seal lasting a year or more with regular use.
