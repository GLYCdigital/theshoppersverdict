## Where it falls short

The pre-filter fabric is the weak point. It's slightly thinner than Blueair's, which means larger pet hair tends to rest on the surface rather than embedding in the fibers. You'll need to vacuum the outer sleeve every two weeks instead of monthly if you have heavy-shedding animals.

Second, there's no RFID chip. Your 211+ will still flash the "replace filter" indicator on its original schedule, and this filter won't auto-reset it. You'll need a long press on the reset button (check your manual — it's the same button you'd use for the OEM filter). Minor, but if you're tech-averse, it's a small annoyance.

Third, at the time of writing, there's no multi-pack discount. If you want to stock up, you're buying singles.

**Q: How often should I replace it?**
A: Blueair recommends every 6 months for the OEM filter. In typical home use, you can follow the same schedule. If you run the unit 24/7 in a dusty environment, lean toward 4-5 months.

**Q: Does it void my warranty?**
A: No. Under the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act, using a third-party consumable doesn't void your appliance warranty unless the manufacturer can prove the part caused the damage — which won't happen with a filter.
