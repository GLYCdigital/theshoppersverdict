**The $2 Detail That Makes Your Kitchen Look Expensive — or Like a Rental**

Walk into any model home and you'll notice it: the hardware. Not the cabinets, not the countertops — the small ceramic knobs that pull the whole room together. The Biaungdo Ceramic Kitchen Cabinet Knobs attempt to deliver that high-end look without the designer markup. But do they hold up to daily abuse from greasy fingers, slammed drawers, and the occasional toddler yank? I tested them for three weeks and read through dozens of verified buyer experiences to give you the straight answer.

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes 10 knobs, each measuring a substantial 1.25 inches in diameter. That's larger than the typical 1-inch builder-grade knob, which makes a visual difference — they read as intentional, not an afterthought. The porcelain body is glazed with a smooth, high-shine finish that mimics the look of expensive Italian ceramic. The base is genuine brass with a matte antique finish, not the sprayed-on gold paint that flakes off after six months.

As shown in the product image, the two-piece design (knob cap + mounting screw) assembles in under two minutes per knob. The included screws are standard 8-32 thread, so if you need longer ones for thick drawer fronts, any hardware store carries replacements.

**Performance and Build Quality**

Here's where this product separates itself from the $8-per-pack plastic alternatives. The porcelain has real weight — each knob feels dense and substantial in your hand. One buyer noted, "I've had these for eight months now and they still look brand new. No chips, no fading, and the finish hasn't worn off where my hand grips them daily." That's consistent with my testing: the glaze is genuinely durable, surviving the drop test onto tile without a scratch.

The matte brass base is the standout feature. It's not the brassy yellow of cheap hardware — it's a muted, slightly darkened bronze-gold that looks expensive against white shaker cabinets. Multiple reviewers echoed this, with one saying, "The color is more like a soft brushed gold, not tacky at all. Elevated my whole kitchen."

**The Honest Shortcomings**

The porcelain shade isn't pure white. It's a warm cream, which means if you have stark white cabinets (think bright white or glacier white), the knobs will look slightly off. One reviewer captured the frustration: "Loved the quality, but the ceramic is definitely more ivory than the photos suggest. Had to return because it clashed with my bright white cabinets."

The included screws are only 1 inch long. Standard cabinet doors are fine, but if you have overlay drawers with thick fronts or you're adding a backplate, you'll need to buy longer screws. Minor, but worth knowing.

No rubber washers are included. On soft wood cabinets, the brass base can leave compression marks over time. Thirty seconds with a felt pad solves this, but it's an extra step.
