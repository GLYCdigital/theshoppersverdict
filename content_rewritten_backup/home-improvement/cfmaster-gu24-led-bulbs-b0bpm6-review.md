## What You Actually Get

Each package contains the GU24 base bulbs you order (single, 2-pack, or 4-pack depending on listing). The base is the standard twist-and-lock style—no adapters, no rewiring, no E26 screw-base converters. The bulbs themselves are compact, with a frosted dome that diffuses light evenly.

What matters most: the specs. These are available in multiple color temperatures, from warm 2700K (that soft, incandescent-like glow for living rooms) to cool 5000K–6500K daylight for garages or workspaces. Lumens run 800–1100 depending on the variant, putting them on par with a 60W–75W incandescent replacement. Power draw sits around 9–12W, so you’re cutting energy use by roughly 80% versus old bulbs.

I’ll cut to the chase: these bulbs perform exactly as advertised. The GU24 base locks into place with a clean quarter-turn—no wobble, no loose connection. ## Where They Fall Short

Let’s be honest about the tradeoffs. **These bulbs are not dimmable.** If your GU24 fixture is connected to a dimmer switch, you’ll get flicker or a low hum at certain settings. That’s a dealbreaker for some, so check your setup before ordering.

Also, the physical bulb diameter is slightly wider than some older GU24 fluorescent tubes. If you have a tight recessed housing or a narrow decorative globe fixture, measure the opening first. A few reviewers mentioned having to return them because they didn’t fit their enclosed fixtures—worth 30 seconds to verify.

Finally, there's no smart-home integration. These are straightforward on/off bulbs. If you want app control or color-changing scenes, look elsewhere.
