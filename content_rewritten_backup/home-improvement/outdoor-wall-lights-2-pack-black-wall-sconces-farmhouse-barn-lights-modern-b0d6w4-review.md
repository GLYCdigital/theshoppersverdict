The seeded glass shade does more than look charming — it diffuses the light from a standard A19 bulb into a warm, even glow that doesn't blind you when you walk past. ## Where It Falls Short

Be direct about the limitations. First, **bulbs are not included**. You'll need two standard E26-base bulbs rated up to 60W each. LED A19 bulbs work great and keep energy costs minimal, but factor that $10–15 into your total.

Second, the **light pattern is fixed downward**. If you're mounting these on a tall wall and need to wash light upward or outward, this isn't the fixture for you. The barn-style shade directs all illumination downward, which is ideal for task lighting but not for ambient flood coverage.

Finally, a few reviewers mentioned the mounting screws are slightly soft — use a proper screwdriver, not a drill on high torque, to avoid stripping the heads.

**Q: Can I use these with a dimmer switch?**
