Let me cut to the chase: if you're remodeling a laundry room, workshop, or small commercial space, the Phivan wall-mount faucet is a surprisingly robust piece of hardware at a price that undercuts most big-box competitors. I've seen enough contractor-grade fixtures to know when something punches above its weight class — this one does.

**What's Actually in the Box**

The package ships with the faucet body, both lever handles, the spout assembly, and the necessary mounting nuts for the wall plate. That's it. No supply lines, no mounting bolts for the wall, no plumber's tape. As shown in the product image, the design is clean and utilitarian — brushed chrome throughout, with a compact profile that sits tight against the wall. The 4-inch center configuration means the two handle stems are spaced exactly 4 inches apart, which is the standard for US residential wall-mount installations.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

This is where the Phivan earns its keep. The body is solid brass — you can feel the weight when you unbox it. The chrome finish is applied thick enough that it won't flake off the first time you scrub it with a rough sponge. For a commercial application where the faucet gets used fifty times a day, that matters.

The quarter-turn ceramic cartridge valves operate smoothly right out of the box. One buyer noted, "Installation was straightforward, the valves turn with a satisfying precision, and there's no drip after months of use in our shop sink." Another reviewer in a restaurant setting confirmed, "Handles are smooth and the water pressure is excellent."

Temperature control is genuinely precise with the two independent lever handles. In a commercial setting where someone might walk up with greasy hands, the levers are easier to operate than cross handles — you can nudge them with a wrist or elbow.

**Where It Falls Short**

The spout reach is the biggest limitation. At about 5 inches from the wall plate to the outlet, this faucet is designed for hand-washing sinks, not deep utility sinks. If you're installing it over a 10-inch-deep commercial basin, the water stream will land closer to the back wall than the center. That's by design — the product name says "hand sink" — but it's worth knowing before you buy.

The missing mounting hardware is a genuine annoyance. The faucet uses a standard wall plate with two bolt holes, but you'll need to source your own lag bolts or masonry anchors depending on your wall construction. One reviewer flagged this: "Had to make a separate trip to the hardware store for bolts — would have been nice to include them."

Supply lines aren't included either. You'll need 1/2-inch IPS connections to tie into your existing plumbing. For a DIY installer, that's an extra trip to the store. For a plumber, it's Tuesday.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Homeowners upgrading a laundry room or mudroom sink**: The wall-mount design gets the faucet out of the way and makes countertop cleaning trivial.
- **Small business owners with a back-of-house hand sink**: It's commercial-grade without the commercial-grade price tag.
- **DIYers comfortable with basic plumbing**: If you've replaced a faucet before, this install is manageable — just budget for the extra parts.

**Skip it if** you need a deep-reaching spout for a utility sink, or if you want a single-handle design. This is strictly a two-handle, hand-washing fixture.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit a standard 4-inch center wall-mount faucet setup?**
A: Yes. The 4-inch center spacing is the US standard for wall-mount faucets. If your existing faucet has handles spaced 4 inches apart, this will bolt right up.

**Q: Is the chrome finish durable enough for commercial use?**
A: Based on buyer feedback and the brass construction, yes. Multiple reviewers report no corrosion or peeling after months of daily use in restaurant and workshop environments.

**Q: What additional parts do I need for installation?**
A: Plan on buying mounting bolts suitable for your wall material, two supply lines with 1/2-inch IPS connections, and plumber's tape. None of these are included.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely commercial-grade wall-mount faucet for a hand-washing sink without paying contractor markup. The brass construction, smooth valves, and clean finish justify the price.

**Skip it if** you need a deep spout for a utility sink, or if you'd rather avoid sourcing extra parts — this isn't a true all-in-one package.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting half a star for the missing hardware and supply lines. The faucet itself performs flawlessly; the nickel-and-diming on accessories is the only real complaint. For the price, this is a solid investment that should outlast the sink it's mounted on.
