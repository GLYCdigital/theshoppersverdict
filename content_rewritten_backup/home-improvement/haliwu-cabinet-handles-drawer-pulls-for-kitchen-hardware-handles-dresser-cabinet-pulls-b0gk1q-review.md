**The Short Version: Haliwu's cabinet pulls deliver exactly what budget hardware shoppers need — decent zinc alloy construction, a clean matte black finish, and enough size options to outfit a whole kitchen for less than the price of a single premium pull from big-box stores. The 4.2-star rating holds up, with a few caveats around finish durability and installation precision.**

Let's be honest about what you're getting here. These are Chinese-manufactured zinc alloy pulls priced to move. They're not heirloom-grade solid brass from a specialty maker. But for a kitchen refresh, bathroom vanity upgrade, or furniture flip, they punch well above their price point.

## What's Actually in the Box

Each pull ships individually wrapped with two mounting screws. The screws are zinc-plated and match the pull finish well enough that they don't look out of place. You're getting the pull, the screws, and nothing else — no paper template, no drill bit guide, no Allen key.

The product images show the full size range: 3-inch pulls for small drawers, 5-inch and 6.3-inch handles for standard cabinet fronts, and 10.2-inch pulls for large drawers or pantry doors. Hole spacing is standard — 64mm for the 3-inch, 128mm for the 5-inch, and 160mm for the 6.3-inch versions. That means they'll fit most pre-drilled cabinets without modification.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The zinc alloy body has a decent heft — roughly 0.15 pounds per small pull. The matte black finish is applied consistently across the surface, including the undersides where cheap hardware often shows bare metal. The texture is smooth but not glossy, which means it won't show every fingerprint the way polished chrome does.

Installation is straightforward if you're replacing existing hardware with matching hole spacing. The included screws are the right length for standard 3/4-inch cabinet doors. For thicker panels, you'll want to grab longer screws from your local hardware store.

One thing worth noting: the finish is baked-on powder coat, not anodized. That means it can chip if you drop a pull on tile or crank a screw down with an impact driver. Several reviewers mentioned this, and one noted that "the black finish scuffed when I dropped one on the concrete floor during installation." Handle them carefully and use a hand screwdriver for the final tightening.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 210 ratings break down favorably — roughly 70% five-star and four-star reviews. The most common praise centers on value. As one verified buyer put it: "Bought 30 of these for a full kitchen redo. Paid less than $60 total. They look way more expensive than they are."

Another recurring theme is the consistent finish across the entire set. Unlike some budget options where pulls from the same order arrive with slightly different shades of black, buyers report these match well. That's a bigger deal than it sounds when you're mounting 25+ pulls across a kitchen.

## Where They Fall Short

The finish durability is the main complaint, though it's worth contextualizing. If you're careful during installation, you won't have issues. But these won't survive a move or a teardown-and-reinstall cycle as well as pricier options.

The lack of a hole-spacing template is a legitimate inconvenience for anyone mounting these on un-drilled cabinet fronts. You'll need to measure carefully — a 1mm error across a 5-inch pull means the screw won't seat properly. A paper template costs pennies to include; its absence is a minor frustration.

A few reviewers also flagged that the longer 10.2-inch pulls can feel slightly flexy in the middle if not screwed into solid wood. On hollow-core doors, you'll want to add a backing plate or use toggle bolts.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy them if:** You're refreshing a kitchen or bathroom on a budget, you're flipping furniture, or you need to outfit a rental property without sinking real money into hardware.

**Skip them if:** You're building a high-end custom kitchen where every detail matters, you need hardware that survives frequent removal, or you're installing on un-drilled doors without the patience for precise measuring.

## Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Do the screws match the pull finish?**
Yes, the included screws have the same matte black coating. They're not invisible, but they blend well enough that you won't notice them at arm's length.

**Q: Will these fit standard pre-drilled cabinets?**
If your cabinets have 64mm (3-inch), 128mm (5-inch), or 160mm (6.3-inch) hole spacing, yes. These are the most common standard spacings, so odds are good.

**Q: How many pulls should I order for a typical kitchen?**
Count your existing hardware first. A standard 10-cabinet kitchen with four drawers typically needs 14 to 18 pulls. Order 10-15% extra to cover defects or installation mistakes.
