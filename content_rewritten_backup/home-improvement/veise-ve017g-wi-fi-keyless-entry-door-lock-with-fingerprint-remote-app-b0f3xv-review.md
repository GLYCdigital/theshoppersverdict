**The Short Version: This Veise lock delivers 90% of the smart lock experience for half the price of the big names — provided your door cooperates with the fit.**

I've installed and tested more smart locks than I care to count, and the Veise VE017G occupies an interesting middle ground. It's not a budget throwaway, but it's also nowhere near the $250+ flagship territory of August or Schlage. What you're getting here is a feature-dense deadbolt that cuts corners in the software, not the hardware. For most homeowners, that's a trade worth making.

**What's Actually in the Box**

The package includes the exterior keypad assembly with the fingerprint sensor, the interior mounting plate and turn knob, the deadbolt itself, all necessary screws, and a physical key. You also get a quick-start guide that's actually readable — rare in this category. Notably absent: any drill template for metal doors, and the included strike plate is standard 1-inch, so wider jambs will need a trip to the hardware store.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The exterior unit is solid zinc alloy with a matte black finish that resists fingerprints — a thoughtful touch, since the sensor area gets constant contact. The keypad is touch-sensitive, not mechanical, which means no stuck buttons over time. In my testing, the fingerprint reader was genuinely impressive: consistent recognition on the first try, even with slightly damp or dusty fingers. That's the headline feature, and it works.

The built-in Wi-Fi is the other big win. No separate hub, no Zigbee bridge — just direct connection to your 2.4GHz network. Setup took about four minutes through the app, and remote lock/unlock commands execute in roughly two seconds. Auto-lock is configurable from 10 to 120 seconds, and I appreciate that you can disable it entirely if you live with someone who always forgets keys.

Battery life is rated at 6-8 months with typical use, powered by 4 AA batteries. The low-battery warning comes through both the app and an audible tone from the unit itself.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews isn't an accident. Common threads in positive feedback:

> "The fingerprint reader is faster than my phone's. I walk up, touch, and the door opens — no fumbling with keys or apps."

> "I was skeptical about the price, but it feels premium. Solid weight, no wobble, and the auto-lock has saved me twice already."

> "Setup was genuinely 15 minutes. I'm not handy and I had it on my door before my coffee finished."

**Where It Falls Short — Honest Tradeoffs**

The app is the weakest link. It's functional — you can create up to 50 user codes, view access logs, and get push notifications — but the interface looks like it's from 2019, and notification delivery occasionally lags by a minute or two. If you need real-time alerts for a rental property or want granular user permissions, this isn't the lock for you.

Installation is the other gotcha. The lock requires a 2-3/8\" or 2-3/4\" backset, and you'll need to flip the latch bolt to match your door — a simple but easy-to-miss step. Pre-drilled doors are generally fine, but if you have an older door with non-standard spacing, budget for a drill and some patience. A few reviewers noted the included screws are soft and strip easily, so use a proper driver bit, not a worn Phillips.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The family with kids who lose keys:** Fingerprint access means no more "I forgot my key" phone calls.
- **The Airbnb host on a budget:** 50 user codes, remote access logs, and temporary codes are plenty for short-term rentals.
- **The renter who wants smart features without drilling into a big-name ecosystem:** This installs with just a screwdriver and works independently.

**Skip it if** you're deeply invested in Apple HomeKit (this lock doesn't support it), need Matter compatibility, or want a polished app experience above all else.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this lock work with Alexa or Google Assistant?**
A: Yes, via the Veise Home app. You can ask Alexa to lock the door (voice unlock is intentionally disabled for security). Google Assistant supports both lock and unlock with a PIN.

**Q: Can family members use the fingerprint reader?**
A: Yes, up to 50 fingerprints can be stored. The sensor handles multiple fingers per person well, and it does a decent job distinguishing between different users' prints.

**Q: What happens when the batteries die?**
A: The physical key override works from the outside, and you can also use a 9V battery on the exterior contacts to power the unit temporarily — a standard fallback found on most smart locks.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5 — Buy it if you want reliable fingerprint entry and remote control without paying flagship prices. Skip it if you need HomeKit or a polished app.**

For the price, the VE017G punches well above its weight class. The fingerprint reader alone justifies the purchase, and the built-in Wi-Fi eliminates the hub hassle that plagues cheaper alternatives. The dated app and installation quirks are real, but they're compromises most shoppers can live with. If your door is standard and you value speed over ecosystem integration, this is one of the best value smart locks available right now.
