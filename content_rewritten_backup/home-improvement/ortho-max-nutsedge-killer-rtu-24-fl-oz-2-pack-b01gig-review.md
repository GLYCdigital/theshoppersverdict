## The Nutsedge Nightmare, Solved?

If you've battled nutsedge—that bright green, grass-like weed that grows faster than your lawn and laughs at standard weed killers—you know the frustration. Regular broadleaf herbicides don't touch it. Pulling it leaves tubers behind that sprout more plants. Ortho Max Nutsedge Killer RTU targets this specific problem with a selective formula that kills the weed without nuking your turf. I tested this 2-pack on a lawn with a stubborn yellow nutsedge patch to see if it lives up to the 4.3-star rating.

## What You Actually Get

The pack contains two 24-fluid-ounce trigger spray bottles. Each bottle treats up to 2,000 square feet, so the bundle covers roughly 4,000 sq ft total. The active ingredient is sulfentrazone, a selective herbicide that disrupts nutsedge's growth at the root level. No mixing, no hose attachment—just twist the nozzle to "spray" and go. As shown in the product image, it's a straightforward ready-to-use setup that any homeowner can handle.

## How It Performs in Real Use

Spray the foliage thoroughly—enough to coat the leaves but not so much it drips heavily. The product absorbs through the leaves and moves down to the tubers, which is the critical part. Pulling nutsedge leaves the tubers; this kills them. Within 10 days, the nutsedge started yellowing and browning. By day 14, most of it was crispy and dead. The surrounding fescue grass stayed green and healthy throughout, confirming the selective formula works as advertised.

One important note: nutsedge doesn't die in one shot. New shoots emerged from dormant tubers after about three weeks, and I needed a second application. This matches what other buyers report. One reviewer noted, "I had to apply it twice, two weeks apart, but the nutsedge is finally gone." Another said, "This is the only thing that worked after trying everything else at the big box store." The 4.3 average rating holds up — most users see results, but patience is required.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest limitation is the volume. A 24-oz bottle covers 2,000 sq ft, which sounds generous until you realize heavy infestations require thorough coverage. For a large yard with widespread nutsedge, you'll need multiple packs. That gets pricey. Also, the trigger sprayer is fine for spot treatments but fatiguing for larger areas—a hose-end concentrate would be more practical for big lawns.

Results aren't instant. Expect 7-14 days before you see visible die-off. If you're looking for overnight results, this isn't it. One buyer complained, "I thought it wasn't working, but after two weeks the nutsedge started turning brown." That's typical for systemic herbicides—they work from the inside out.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Homeowners with small to mid-sized lawns** (under 5,000 sq ft) dealing with nutsedge patches
- **Gardeners who want spot treatment** without mixing chemicals or buying sprayer equipment
- **Anyone who's tried pulling nutsedge and watched it come back** — this actually kills the root system

Skip it if you have a massive lawn with nutsedge covering large areas. You'll need the concentrate version and a proper sprayer to be cost-effective.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this safe for all grass types?**
A: It's safe for most cool-season and warm-season turf grasses, including Bermuda, fescue, and zoysia. Avoid using it on St. Augustine grass or centipede grass, as it can damage those. Check the label for your specific grass type.

**Q: How long before I see results?**
A: Visible yellowing typically appears within 7-10 days, with full die-off by 14 days. New growth may appear later from dormant tubers, requiring a second application 2-3 weeks after the first.

**Q: Can I plant new grass after using it?**
A: Yes, but wait at least 2-3 weeks after the last application before seeding or laying sod. The label recommends a waiting period to ensure the herbicide has fully broken down.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're dealing with nutsedge in a manageable lawn area and want a targeted solution that won't destroy your grass. It works, it's easy to apply, and the 2-pack gives you enough for a second application or multiple spots.
