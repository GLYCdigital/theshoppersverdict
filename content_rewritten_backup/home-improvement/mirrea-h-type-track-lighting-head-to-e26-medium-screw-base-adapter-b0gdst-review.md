**FAQ**

**Q: Will this work with smart bulbs?**
A: Yes. The E26 socket accepts standard smart bulbs (Philips Hue, Wyze, etc.). Just ensure your track provides continuous power — it will, since this adapter has no switch to interrupt the circuit.

**Q: How do I know if my track is H-type?**
A: H-type tracks have two parallel copper conductors inside a slot roughly 1 inch wide, common in Halo and Juno systems. J-type (2-wire) and L-type (3-wire) have different connector shapes. When in doubt, measure the slot and compare photos of H-type connectors before ordering.

**Q: Can I use this with a dimmer switch?**
A: Yes, but the dimming capability depends entirely on the bulb. Pair it with dimmable LED bulbs and a compatible wall dimmer, and you're good. Non-dimmable bulbs will flicker or hum on a dimmer circuit.
