If you've ever stared at a standard RGB LED strip and wondered why the individual dots ruin the effect, this BTF-LIGHTING FCOB strip is the answer. It's not perfect, but for DIY under-cabinet lighting, TV bias lighting, or custom accent work, it's a serious step up from the pixelated look most strips deliver.

Here's what you actually get: a 5-meter roll of flexible PCB measuring 10mm wide, packed with 144 LEDs per meter. The FCOB (Flip Chip on Board) design means the red, green, and blue dies sit directly on the board without individual lenses or reflectors, creating one continuous light-emitting surface. The WS2811 ICs are integrated every 12mm, which is the secret sauce — unlike dumb COB strips that change color all at once, this one gives you individual addressable control at 25mm cut intervals.

**Real-world performance**

I ran this strip through standard DIY scenarios: under a desk, behind a monitor, and along a shelf edge. The light output is genuinely smooth. No hot spots, no visible diode pattern, just even illumination that looks closer to a neon tube than an LED strip. Color accuracy is solid for a WS2811 product — reds are deep, blues are rich, and the whites (when mixing RGB) lean slightly cool, so don't expect a true warm white.

The 5V operating voltage is worth noting. You'll need a decent 5V power supply — at full brightness, 5 meters can pull up to 7.5 amps. Undersize the PSU and you'll get voltage drop and inconsistent colors past the 3-meter mark. I'd recommend injecting power at both ends for runs over 4 meters.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The seamless look is the headline. One reviewer put it simply: *"Finally, a strip that doesn't look like a strip. The light is perfect for my kitchen under-cabinet install."* Another noted, *"These are the only COB strips I've found that actually do per-segment effects. Most just do whole-strip color changes."* The build quality also gets consistent props — the PCB is flexible without feeling flimsy, and the solder pads at the cut points are clearly marked.

**Where it falls short**

Heat is the tradeoff. At full brightness, this strip runs warm — not dangerously hot, but warm enough that I wouldn't stick it to anything heat-sensitive without a heatsink or aluminum channel. The included 3M adhesive is decent on clean, smooth surfaces, but if your wall or cabinet has any texture, it will peel within weeks. Budget for mounting clips or an aluminum extrusion.

The cut points are every 25mm, which is precise but unforgiving. Measure twice, cut once — there's no recovery if you're off by a few millimeters. And because it's 5V, you can't just daisy-chain long runs the way you can with 12V strips.

**Who should buy this**

- **DIY smart-home builders** who want addressable effects without the pixelated look
- **Under-cabinet lighting fans** who want even, continuous light for task areas
- **PC modders** doing ambient lighting who care about clean aesthetics

Skip it if you need warm white, if your install surface is textured, or if you're looking for a simple plug-and-play strip without controller setup.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this work with WLED or Home Assistant?**
Yes. Since it's a standard WS2811 protocol on 5V, it works with WLED, ESP32/ESP8266 controllers, and any WS2811-compatible controller. BTF even sells a compatible controller if you want a proprietary setup.

**Q: How many amps does 5 meters draw at full brightness?**
Roughly 7.5 amps (1.5A per meter). A 40W or larger 5V PSU is safe. For runs over 4 meters, power inject at the far end to avoid color shift.

**Q: Can I cut it and still use the remaining pieces?**
Yes, cut only at the marked solder pads every 25mm. Each cut piece remains addressable and functional as long as you solder on proper connections.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want seamless, addressable RGB lighting without the visible diode pattern that plagues standard strips. The BTF-LIGHTING FCOB WS2811 delivers exactly what it promises — smooth, bright, individually controllable light — at a fair price for the feature set.

**Skip it if** you need simple white light, don't want to manage a 5V power supply, or you're not comfortable with basic soldering and measurement. This is a maker's product, not a plug-and-play fixture.
