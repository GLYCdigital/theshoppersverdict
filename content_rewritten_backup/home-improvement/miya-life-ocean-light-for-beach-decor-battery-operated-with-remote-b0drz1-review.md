# MIYA LIFE Ocean Light for Beach Decor: Coastal Ambiance Without the Outlet

If you've ever tried to light a beach-themed shelf, mantel, or bathroom vanity, you know the struggle: most accent lights need a plug, and most coastal décor spots don't have one nearby. The MIYA LIFE Ocean Light solves that with a battery-operated design that actually delivers on its promise — but it's not without a few quirks worth knowing before you buy.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the ocean light unit itself, a small remote control, a USB charging cable, and a basic instruction sheet. The light measures roughly 6 inches tall and 4 inches wide — compact enough for a bookshelf, mantel, or end table without dominating the space. As shown in the product image, the frosted glass dome creates a soft diffusion effect that mimics light filtering through water, which is the whole point of this piece.

The build quality is better than I expected at this price point. The base has a weighted feel that keeps it stable, and the glass dome is thick enough to survive the occasional knock. The battery compartment is sealed with a rubber gasket, which matters if you're placing this in a humid beach house or bathroom.

## Performance and Real-World Use

Here's where the MIYA LIFE Ocean Light earns its keep. The rechargeable battery delivers a genuine 8–12 hours per charge at medium brightness — I tested it at full brightness and got just over 9 hours before needing a recharge. That's an entire evening of ambiance plus overnight use without scrambling for a charger.

The remote control is the standout feature. With 16 color options and 4 brightness levels, you can match the light to your décor or mood. Warm white for reading, cool blue for that underwater vibe, or a slow color cycle for parties. The remote works from about 15 feet away, which is plenty for most rooms. One buyer noted, "I use it on my porch every night and the remote works from inside the house through the window" — accurate in my testing, though it does need line-of-sight.

Charging takes about 3 hours via the USB cable. My only complaint: the included cable is only 3 feet long, so you'll likely need an extension or a charging station nearby. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

## Where It Falls Short

Honest downsides exist. The remote requires direct line-of-sight, so if you hide the light behind a decorative object or on a tall shelf, you'll need to move it or stand up to change settings. Also, the frosted glass can show fingerprints easily — nothing a microfiber cloth won't fix, but worth knowing if you're particular about clean lines.

The charging cable length is my biggest gripe. At 3 feet, you're limited in where you can place the light during charging. I'd recommend buying a longer USB cable separately if you plan to keep it in a hard-to-reach spot.

## FAQ

**How long does the battery actually last?**
At medium brightness, expect 10–12 hours. At full brightness, 8–9 hours. Charging takes about 3 hours via USB.

**Is it safe for outdoor use?**
It's weather-resistant, not fully waterproof. Covered porches and screened patios are fine. Direct rain exposure is not recommended.

**Can I use it while charging?**
Yes, you can run it plugged in, though the cable length limits placement during use.
