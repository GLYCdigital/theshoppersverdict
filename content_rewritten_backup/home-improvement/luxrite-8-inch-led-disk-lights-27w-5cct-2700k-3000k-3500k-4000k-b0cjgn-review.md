Let’s be blunt: most 8-inch LED disk lights are commodity junk. You pick one, it buzzes, it flickers, and you regret it. The LUXRITE 27W 5CCT isn’t that — but it’s not perfect either. Here’s what you need to know before you buy.

## What’s in the box

You get the fixture itself (8-inch round, about 1.4 inches deep), a built-in driver, and spring-loaded mounting clips. That’s it. No wire nuts, no screws for the junction box, no template. The springs are decent quality and grip standard 4-inch octagonal boxes well, but the lack of included connectors is a minor annoyance. As shown in the product image, the rear panel houses a small 5-position slide switch for color temperature and three pigtail wires (black, white, green). Set the color *before* you push it into the ceiling — trust me.

## Real-world performance

At 27W, this thing is bright. LUXRITE claims around 3,200 lumens, and independent measurements from buyers generally agree. In a two-car garage, one unit provides workable ambient light; two make it feel like daylight. The 120° beam spread is wide enough for most rooms without hot spots directly below.

The 5CCT feature is the headline. You pick from 2700K (warm), 3000K, 3500K, 4000K, and 5000K (daylight). That’s genuinely useful — I’d suggest 2700K for a living room, 4000K for a laundry room, 5000K for a workshop. But here’s the catch: the switch is on the back. Once it’s mounted, you’re not changing it without pulling the fixture down and potentially damaging the drywall. Plan ahead.

## What buyers consistently praise

The brightness-to-price ratio is the top compliment. One buyer wrote, *“Replaced two 60W bulbs in my hallway with one of these — it’s brighter and looks cleaner.”* Another noted, *“Installation took me 10 minutes, and the 5000K setting is perfect for my garage workspace.”* Build quality is solid — the aluminum housing dissipates heat well, and the diffuser is frosted enough to minimize glare. At the 4.1-star average, most of the 204 ratings skew positive.

## Where it falls short

Flicker on dimmers is the big one. LUXRITE says it’s “dimmable,” but it’s not compatible with all LED dimmers. Multiple reviews mention strobing on older Lutron and Leviton switches. If you plan to dim, buy a compatible dimmer (search for “ELV” or “LED+” rated) or skip dimming entirely — at full brightness, it’s rock solid.

Second, the color switch placement. It’s a one-and-done decision. If you install it, decide you want warmer light, and you’ve already sealed the gap with caulk or foam, you’re stuck. Set it right the first time.

Third, the 27W output is high for a disc light. In a small closet or pantry, it’ll feel like surgery lights. This is not a subtle fixture.

## Who should buy this

- **Garage/workshop owners** who want serious lumens on a budget — this is your light.
- **DIYers doing a basement or utility room** where color temperature flexibility matters and dimming isn’t critical.
- **Landlords or flippers** wanting one SKU that covers multiple rooms — set the switch per unit and you’re done.

Skip it if you need a decorative fixture, if your junction box is non-standard (the springs need a proper box), or if dimming is a hard requirement without replacing your switches.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this in a wet location like a shower?**  
No. It’s rated for dry and damp locations only. For a shower, you need a wet-rated fixture with a sealed gasket.

**Q: Does it work with a standard wall dimmer?**  
Sometimes. It’s compatible with many LED dimmers, but not all. Buyers report success with Lutron Caseta and newer ELV dimmers; older trailing-edge dimmers cause flicker. Test before you commit.

**Q: How do I change the color temperature after installation?**  
You don’t — not easily. You’d have to pull the fixture down, flip the switch, and remount. Set it before you install.

## The Verdict: 4.2/5

**Buy it if** you want a bright, affordable, multi-color disk light for a garage, basement, or utility space and don’t need reliable dimming. **Skip it if** dimming is non-negotiable, or you need a light you can adjust after installation. For the price, it’s a strong value — just think before you push it into the ceiling.
