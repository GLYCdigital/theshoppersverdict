## What You're Actually Getting

The box contains one 3-in-1 filter cartridge: a washable pre-filter layer, an activated carbon sheet, and a true HEPA element rated for 99.97% particle capture at 0.3 microns. The dimensions match the OEM HPA100 filter footprint — about 8.5 x 8.5 x 1.5 inches — and the sealed foam gasket around the frame is a thoughtful touch that prevents air from sneaking around the filter edges.

## Performance: Where It Shines and Where It Doesn't

I ran this filter in a Honeywell HPA100 for three weeks in a 200-square-foot bedroom with two shedding dogs. Particle reduction on the unit's built-in sensor dropped from "red" to "green" within 15 minutes of installation — that's genuinely good for a third-party filter.

The HEPA layer is the star here. It's dense, properly pleated, and captures the same stuff the OEM filter catches: pollen, dust mites, pet dander, mold spores. For allergy sufferers, this filter will do the job.

The activated carbon layer is where you'll notice the compromise. It's roughly half the thickness of the OEM Honeywell filter's carbon bed. Light cooking odors and bathroom smells? Handled fine. A litter box in the same room or a heavy smoker? You'll still smell it. If odor control is your primary concern, spend the extra money on the genuine Honeywell filter or add a standalone carbon filter to your setup.

## Fit and Installation: The Honest Truth

Here's the thing about third-party filters: they're never perfect clones. This one slides into the HPA100's filter slot with a snug fit, but it's not the "drop-in and done" experience you get with OEM. You'll need to press firmly to seat it fully — several users reported that the filter sits slightly proud of the housing if you don't push hard enough. Once seated, it holds fine and doesn't rattle or vibrate during operation.

One thing to note: this filter doesn't come with a reset reminder tab like some competitors. You'll need to track your 6-12 month replacement schedule manually or rely on the air purifier's own timer function.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a budget-conscious Honeywell HPA100/HPA094/HPA104 owner who changes filters on a strict schedule and cares primarily about particle filtration (dust, pollen, dander).

**Skip it if:** You need serious odor control (cigarette smoke, industrial smells, persistent pet odors) or you're someone who wants zero-tolerance fitment perfection. Also skip if you're using this in a high-humidity environment — the cardboard frame is less moisture-resistant than OEM.

## Three Questions Shoppers Ask

**How often should I replace this filter?** Every 6 months for average use, every 3-4 months if you run your purifier 24/7 or live with pets. The HEPA layer will clog faster than the pre-filter, so vacuum the pre-filter monthly to extend overall life.

**Will this work in my HPA094 or HPA104?** Yes — all three models share the same filter slot dimensions. The filter fits all of them identically.

**Is this a genuine Honeywell product?** No, this is a compatible third-party replacement. It's not certified by Honeywell, but it meets basic HEPA standards and fits correctly.
