**The $30 Trick That Turns a Bucket Into a Real Wet/Dry Vac**

If you’ve ever stared at a pile of sawdust or a flooded corner and thought, “I’m not hauling my full-size shop vac out for this,” this conversion kit is your answer. The Shop-Vac 9700606 is essentially a motorized lid that snaps onto any standard 5-gallon bucket, turning a $5 bucket into a surprisingly capable wet/dry vacuum. It’s not a gimmick — it’s a legit space-saver with real suction.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the power head assembly with a 2.5 HP motor, a 7-foot vacuum hose, a standard cartridge filter, a foam sleeve for wet pickups, and the lid itself. That’s it. No bucket included — you supply that. The lid fits snugly on standard 5-gallon buckets (the orange Home Depot style works perfectly). Assembly takes under a minute: snap the lid on, attach the hose, plug it in. The hose connects to the port with a friction fit that holds securely without being a wrestling match to remove.

**Performance: Small Footprint, Serious Suction**

Rated at 80 CFM of airflow, this little unit pulls hard. In testing, it lifted drywall dust, wood shavings, and even small screws without hesitation. For wet pickups, the foam sleeve swaps in quickly, and the motor handled a half-inch of standing water in a utility sink without bogging down. The 7-foot hose is shorter than full-size models, but for a fixed workstation — a garage bench, a workshop corner, or under a miter saw station — it’s plenty.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The recurring theme in customer feedback is the value-to-performance ratio. One reviewer put it plainly: “I’ve used it for months on my job site. It’s cheaper and more portable than my old 5-gallon shop vac.” Another noted, “I mounted it to a bucket, added a cyclone separator, and it’s replaced my $150 vacuum for dry dust.” The compactness is a major draw — several buyers mention hanging it on a pegboard or storing it in a car trunk for emergency spills.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let’s be honest about the flaws. First, it’s **loud**. There’s no sound-dampening insulation, so it runs at a sharp, high-pitched whine — wear ear protection for extended use. Second, the included cartridge filter clogs quickly with fine dust (drywall, joint compound). You’ll be tapping it out frequently, and replacement filters cost about $15. Third, there’s **no accessory storage** — the hose, nozzles, and filter are loose pieces. If you’re not organized, you’ll lose the crevice tool within a month. Finally, the power cord is a modest 6 feet, so you’ll likely need an extension cord for most jobs.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Workshop hobbyists** who need a dedicated dust collector at a specific station without dedicating floor space.
- **DIYers on a budget** who want wet/dry capability without dropping $100+ on a full unit.
- **Contractors** who need a lightweight backup vac for quick cleanups on job sites.
- **Car owners** who want a portable emergency wet vac for spills in the trunk.

**Skip it if** you need heavy continuous use, long hose reach, or quiet operation. This is a specialized tool for specific scenarios, not a full-time replacement for a conventional shop vac.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it fit any 5-gallon bucket?**
A: It fits standard 5-gallon buckets with a 11.8-inch top diameter. The orange Home Depot/Lowes buckets work perfectly. Some tapered buckets may fit loosely — test the seal before wet use.

**Q: Can I use this for both wet and dry without changing anything?**
A: No. Use the foam sleeve for wet, the paper cartridge for dry. Running wet without the foam sleeve will ruin the motor. The swap takes about 10 seconds.

**Q: Will it pick up large debris like nails?**
A: Yes, up to about a 1/2-inch diameter. Anything larger will clog the hose port. For big debris, sweep first.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a cheap, portable, surprisingly powerful wet/dry vacuum for light-to-moderate jobs and don’t mind the noise.

**Skip it if** you need quiet operation, long hose reach, or a machine that handles fine dust all day without filter maintenance.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It does exactly what it claims, at a price that’s hard to argue with. Just know what you’re signing up for.
