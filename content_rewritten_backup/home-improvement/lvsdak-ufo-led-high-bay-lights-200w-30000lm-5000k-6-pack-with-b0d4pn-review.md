If you're lighting a warehouse, workshop, or commercial garage, you already know the drill: old metal halide fixtures burn hot, buzz loudly, and take five minutes to reach full brightness. The lvsdak UFO LED High Bay 200W promises to fix all that with modern LED efficiency. After digging through 235 ratings and the technical specs, here's what actually matters before you drop money on a 6-pack.

**What's in the box**

Each of the six boxes contains the UFO fixture itself, a 3-foot hanging hook with V-bracket, and a short pigtail wire for hardwiring. That's it — no remote, no sensor, no fancy mounting plate. The fixture measures roughly 12 inches in diameter and 5 inches tall, weighing about 6 pounds. It's a standard round UFO form factor, so it'll look familiar if you've swapped high bays before. As shown in the product image, the aluminum housing has deep cooling fins that do real work keeping the driver cool.

**Performance and build quality**

The headline spec is 30,000 lumens at 5000K, and this is one of the few budget high bays that actually delivers close to its claim. Independent testing from buyers with lux meters confirms you're getting somewhere in the 28,000-30,000 lumen range — well within acceptable tolerance. The 5000K daylight color is crisp and clean, ideal for task work where color rendering matters. One reviewer noted, "Replaced 6 old 400W metal halides in my auto shop and the difference is night and day. Instant on, no hum, and my electric bill dropped noticeably the first month."

The die-cast aluminum body is solid, not flimsy sheet metal. The finned heat sink design pulls heat away effectively — critical for LED lifespan. Multiple long-term reviews mention zero flicker or buzzing even after six months of 12-hour daily use. That's a good sign for driver quality, which is usually the first thing to fail on cheap LED fixtures.

**Where it falls short**

There's no dimming capability. If your space has variable occupancy, you're running full brightness whenever the switch is on. That's a missed opportunity for energy savings, especially in warehouses with intermittent foot traffic.

The mounting hardware is genuinely basic. The included hook works fine for hanging at 15-20 feet, but if you need a flush mount against a ceiling, you'll need to buy the sliding bolt kit separately. It's a $10-15 add-on, but it's annoying when you're buying a 6-pack expecting everything included.

And it's 5000K only. No switchable color temperature. If you're lighting a retail space where warmer light flatters merchandise, this fixture will feel clinical and harsh.

**Who should buy this**

This 6-pack is perfect for the garage owner, workshop operator, or farm building manager who wants to replace old high bays with modern LED and doesn't need fancy controls. It's also a strong pick for anyone wiring a new shop or barn where you want reliable, bright light without paying premium brand prices. The cost-per-lumen math works strongly in your favor — you're getting commercial-grade output at roughly half the price of name-brand equivalents.

Skip it if you need dimming, smart controls, or a color temperature below 5000K. Also skip if you need flush mounting without a trip to the hardware store.

**FAQ**

*Can these be used outdoors?* Not directly — they're rated for dry locations only. You'd need a weatherproof enclosure for outdoor use, which defeats the cost advantage.

*What's the actual power draw?* Around 200W per fixture at full brightness. That's roughly 2.5 amps per fixture on a 120V circuit, so plan your breaker sizing accordingly.

*How high can they be mounted?* The beam angle is about 90 degrees, so they work best at 15-25 feet mounting height. Below 12 feet, the light will feel concentrated and potentially glaring.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need cost-effective, reliable high bay lighting with verified brightness and don't need smart features. **Skip it if** you require dimming, color temperature flexibility, or flush mounting without extra parts.
