# GE Disposall Continuous Feed 1/2 HP Garbage Disposal Review

**Is this budget-friendly disposer worth your kitchen remodel dollars, or will it leave you with a sink full of regrets?**

Let's cut to it — the GE Disposall Continuous Feed 1/2 Horsepower garbage disposal is the kind of product that doesn't get people excited, but it's the kind that makes or breaks daily kitchen life. With a 4.4-star average across 220 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. Here's who it's actually for and who should walk away.

---

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the disposer unit itself (pre-assembled), a mounting gasket, a sink flange, a snap ring, and a wiring harness. Installation is straightforward if you're decently handy — plan for about 60–90 minutes if you're swapping out an older unit with the same 3-bolt mounting pattern. The unit measures roughly 12 inches tall with a 5.5-inch diameter body, so it fits comfortably under standard kitchen sinks.

The 1/2 HP permanent magnet motor is the workhorse here. It's not the strongest in GE's lineup — that's fine, because it's not priced like it either. What matters is that it's a continuous feed model, meaning you can run water and feed scraps while it's grinding. No fussing with stoppers or batch loading.

---

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the GE Disposall earns its keep. The grind chamber is stainless steel, and the swivel impellers are corrosion-resistant. In practice, that means it chews through typical daily scraps — veggie peels, coffee grounds, small bones, leftover pasta — without jamming or complaining. The 2600 RPM motor clears the chamber quickly, which is exactly what you want when you're mid-meal-prep and just need stuff gone.

The sound level is noticeably quieter than older disposers, though it's no whisper-quiet model either. Think "garbage disposal hum" rather than "industrial woodchipper."

**What owners consistently praise:**

- *"Installation was a breeze — took me about an hour with basic tools."*
- *"Handles everything I throw at it. I've had it for 8 months and zero jams."*
- *"Much quieter than the builder-grade unit it replaced."*

---

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. At 1/2 HP, this is not the disposer for heavy-duty users. If you cook large meals regularly, process lots of fibrous vegetables, or have a family of five-plus generating serious food waste, the motor will struggle. I'd recommend stepping up to at least a 3/4 HP model for that kind of workload.

The splash guard is a bit thin and can pop out if you're aggressive with utensils while scraping plates. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

Some users have noted the power cord is short — you'll almost certainly need an existing outlet under your sink or an electrician visit if you're doing a fresh install.

---

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:**
- You're replacing a builder-grade disposer on a budget
- You have a 1–2 person household or cook lightly
- You want something reliable that won't break the bank
- You're handy enough to install it yourself

**Skip it if:**
- You have a large family or cook heavy meals daily
- You regularly dispose of fibrous or starchy waste in bulk
- You need the quietest possible operation
- You want a longer warranty (GE offers 2 years; competitors like Moen and InSinkErator offer more)

---

## FAQs

**Q: Will this fit my existing sink and plumbing?**
A: It uses the standard 3-bolt mounting system, so it'll fit most sinks with a 3.5-inch drain opening. The discharge tube is 1.5 inches, standard for US plumbing.

**Q: Can I install this myself?**
A: Yes, if you're comfortable with basic plumbing and electrical. You'll need a screwdriver, pliers, and plumber's putty. If you're not wired for an outlet under the sink, budget for an electrician.

**Q: How loud is it compared to other 1/2 HP models?**
A: Quieter than most budget units, but louder than premium models from InSinkErator. It's a noticeable hum, not a roar.

---

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐**

The GE Disposall 1/2 HP is a solid, no-nonsense garbage disposal that does exactly what it should for the price. It's not flashy, it's not the most powerful, and it's not the quietest — but it's dependable, easy to install, and backed by a brand people trust.

**Buy it if** you need a reliable budget disposer for light-to-moderate use. **Skip it if** you're a heavy-duty kitchen warrior — spend the extra $40–60 for more horsepower.

For the right buyer, this is a smart, honest purchase that'll serve you well for years.
