**What's actually in the box**

The FTHT-20RF is the complete heating cartridge assembly — the cylindrical heating element, thermostat housing, and wiring harness pre-attached. It's designed to slide directly into the existing Heater Treater 20 shell. You're not getting a mounting bracket, screws, or any installation hardware. That's fine if you kept the original bracket, but if you're replacing a unit that was hard-mounted, factor in a trip to the hardware store.

The cartridge measures roughly 8 inches long with a 2-inch diameter, matching OEM specs. The aluminum housing feels substantial — not the thin stamped metal you sometimes see in knockoff parts. The wiring leads are 18-gauge with spade connectors, which mate cleanly to the existing thermostat wiring.

**Real-world performance**

The FTHT-20RF is rated for 1500 watts and delivers 140°F output at the nozzle. Multiple long-term reviewers report consistent heat output even in unheated garages — exactly the scenario this product is built for. One reviewer noted, "Replaced the original after 4 years, this one's been running 2 winters straight without a hiccup. Heat output matches the original exactly."

Installation takes about 15 minutes if you're comfortable with basic wiring. The cartridge slides into the housing, you reconnect two spade connectors, and secure the retaining clip. No specialty tools required. One caveat: the thermostat dial on some units arrives slightly loose. It's an easy fix with a small screwdriver, but worth checking before you button everything up.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The dominant theme in positive reviews is durability. Multiple owners report 2-3 years of continuous winter use without failure. The corrosion-resistant coating on the housing is a genuine differentiator — this unit sits in garages and workshops where humidity and road salt are constant threats.

Fitment gets consistent high marks. "Exact OEM replacement, no modifications needed," is a recurring phrase. The wiring harness matches the original pinout, and the mounting holes align perfectly with the existing bracket.

**Where it falls short**

The lack of a mounting bracket is the most common complaint. If you're replacing a cartridge that was removed from a bracket you no longer have, you're stuck. The bracket isn't expensive, but it's an extra order and an extra wait.

The thermostat dial issue appears in roughly 5-10% of reviews. It's not a functional problem — the thermostat still maintains temperature — but it's sloppy quality control for a product at this price point.

Also worth noting: this cartridge is specifically for the FTHT-20 model. It will not fit the older FTHT-15. The dimensions look similar, and some sellers list them interchangeably, but they're not compatible. Double-check your model number before ordering.
