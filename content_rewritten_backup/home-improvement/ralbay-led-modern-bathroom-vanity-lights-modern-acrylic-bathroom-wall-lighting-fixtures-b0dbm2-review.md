If you're staring at a ceiling fixture that throws shadows across your face every time you shave or apply makeup, this Ralbay vanity light fixes that specific problem. It's a hardwired, integrated-LED bar that mounts above or beside your mirror, and it does one thing really well: spread even, shadow-free light exactly where you need it.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the main fixture, mounting bracket, screws, wire nuts, and a clear instruction sheet. The unit measures roughly 24 inches long and 2.5 inches deep (double-check the listing for the exact size — Ralbay offers multiple lengths). The housing is aluminum with a matte finish, and the shade is frosted acrylic. It's not the slimmest bar on the market, but the acrylic diffuser is what makes it work — it scatters light so you don't get that harsh glare line on your forehead.

**Real-world performance**

Installation took about 30 minutes. You're wiring standard 120V house current: black to black, white to white, ground to the bracket. The bracket mounts to a standard junction box, and the fixture slides on with a locking screw. No special tools beyond a screwdriver and wire strippers. One thing to note: because it's hardwired, you'll want a switch on the wall already — there's no pull chain or remote.

The light itself is 3000K warm white — flattering for skin tones, not clinical like daylight bulbs. At full brightness (around 2200 lumens for the 24-inch model), it's plenty for a double vanity. The integrated LEDs are rated for 50,000 hours, which at three hours a day means you'll have this light for over 45 years. No bulb changes, ever.

Here's where it gets interesting: the driver is dimmable, but only if your wall switch is a compatible dimmer. Many buyers report using a Lutron or Leviton dimmer without flicker. One reviewer wrote: *"I paired it with a $15 dimmer switch and it goes from a soft nightlight glow to full brightness with zero buzzing."* That's the kind of setup you want for late-night bathroom trips.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the 225 ratings, three themes dominate. First, the light quality: *"Finally, no shadows when I'm doing my eyeliner,"* one customer noted. Second, the modern look: the frosted acrylic bar sits flush and clean — no visible bulbs, no yellowing plastic. Third, the value: at this price point (most sizes run $50–$80), you're getting integrated LEDs that would cost triple in a specialty lighting store.

**Where it falls short**

The fixed 3000K color temperature is a dealbreaker for some. If you prefer bright, cool daylight for task lighting — say, for precise grooming or color-matching clothes — this won't do it. You're locked into warm white. Also, the hardwired-only design means renters or anyone without existing junction boxes are out of luck. A plug-in version would make this a no-brainer for apartments.

One minor complaint from a few buyers: the acrylic shade picks up fingerprints easily during installation. Wear gloves or wipe it down with a microfiber cloth before mounting — it's cosmetic, not functional.

**Who should buy this**

- **Homeowners upgrading a master bath** — if you've got a junction box above the mirror, this is a 30-minute swap.
- **Anyone tired of replacing bulbs** — integrated LEDs mean zero maintenance.
- **Dimmer enthusiasts** — pair it with a compatible switch for adjustable ambience.

**Skip it if** you need daylight color temperature, want a plug-in option, or have a very large 60-inch+ double vanity that requires two units.

**FAQ**

*Can I install this myself, or do I need an electrician?*  
If you've wired a ceiling fan or outlet before, you're fine. If you've never touched house wiring, hire an electrician — code requires a junction box, and this is 120V live wire.

*Does it work with a dimmer switch?*  
Yes, but only with a leading-edge (MLV) dimmer, not all LED-rated dimmers. Check the compatibility list in the manual before buying a switch.

*What's the actual brightness?*  
The 24-inch model outputs around 2200 lumens — equivalent to replacing a 150-watt incandescent. For a standard 4-foot mirror, it's more than enough.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a clean, modern vanity light with even, shadow-free illumination and no bulb maintenance. **Skip it if** you need variable color temperature or a plug-and-play rental-friendly option.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The fixed warm-white tint costs it half a star, but for the price, performance, and longevity, this is one of the better vanity light values on Amazon right now.
