Most buyers report it fits fine with a gentle push, but if your purifier has a tightly-toleranced filter compartment, you may need to persuade it. That's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing before you order.

**No washable pre-filter layer.** Some competing filters include a washable coarse filter on the intake side to extend HEPA life. This one skips that. If your purifier doesn't already have a pre-filter built in, budget for one or expect to replace this HEPA every 4-6 months instead of 6-8.

**Replacement reminder:** The 4.4-star average isn't perfect, and a handful of reviews mention filters arriving slightly compressed from shipping. If yours looks deformed out of the box, return it — a compressed pleat section will create a permanent bypass channel.
