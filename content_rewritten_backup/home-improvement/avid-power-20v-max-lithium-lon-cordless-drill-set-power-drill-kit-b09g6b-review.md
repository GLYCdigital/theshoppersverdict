If you're staring down a weekend of furniture assembly, curtain rod installs, or light shelving, the AVID POWER 20V MAX Cordless Drill Set is the kind of tool that makes you wonder why you'd spend three times as much on a name brand. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, this kit has earned its reputation as a budget-friendly workhorse. But let's be clear about what you're getting — and what you're not.

## What's Actually in the Box

The kit includes the drill/driver, two 20V MAX lithium-ion batteries, a rapid charger, and a double-ended bit set. The inclusion of two batteries is the headline feature here — it's rare at this price point, and it means you can keep one charging while the other is in use. As one buyer put it, "Having the spare battery saved me when I forgot to charge the first one overnight." The charger itself is compact and gets a depleted cell back to full in roughly an hour, which is respectable for a non-premium brand.

## Performance and Build Quality

The drill delivers up to 450 in-lbs of torque across 18 clutch positions plus a drill mode. For everyday household tasks — driving 2-inch deck screws into softwood, drilling pilot holes in drywall, or boring through 1/2-inch plywood — it handles the work without bogging down. The variable-speed trigger gives you enough finesse to seat screws flush without stripping the head, something reviewers consistently mention. One user noted, "I hung 12 curtain rods and drove about 80 screws without the battery dropping below half."

The 3/8-inch keyed chuck is the tradeoff. It holds bits securely — no slipping, even under load — but you'll need the included chuck key to change bits. That's a minor annoyance when you're switching between drill and driver bits repeatedly. If you're used to keyless chucks on pricier drills, this feels like a step back in convenience, though not in function.

The body is compact at under 8.5 inches, and the rubberized grip keeps things comfortable during extended use. The integrated LED light above the trigger does a decent job illuminating dark corners under sinks or inside cabinets, though it's not the widest beam I've seen.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the reviews, three themes dominate. First, value: "This replaced a $120 drill my ex took, and honestly I don't miss it." Second, battery life: multiple users report completing full projects on a single charge, citing the dual-battery setup as a genuine lifesaver. Third, ease of use — several first-time drill owners specifically mention that the clutch settings made it nearly impossible to strip screws once they read the manual.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the keyed chuck, there's no belt clip and no case — just the bare tool and charger. If you're hauling this to job sites or storing it in a truck, you'll want to buy a bag separately. The batteries also lack fuel-gauge style indicators; you get a simple three-light readout that's more "rough estimate" than precise. And while it's fine for light framing, don't expect to drive 3-inch lag bolts into hardwood all day — this is a 20V drill, not a hammer drill, and it knows its limits.

## Who Should Buy This

This is ideal for apartment dwellers, new homeowners, and DIYers who need a reliable drill for occasional projects without the sticker shock of premium brands. It's also a smart pick for a first drill — the learning curve is forgiving. Skip it if you're a contractor doing daily heavy-duty work, or if you need keyless bit changes for rapid-fire tasks.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this drill come with a carrying case?**
A: No — the base kit includes the drill, two batteries, charger, and bits only. You'll need to source your own bag or case.

**Q: Can it drill into concrete or masonry?**
A: Not effectively. This is a standard drill, not a hammer drill. For brick or concrete, you'll need a dedicated hammer drill or masonry bits with patience for light tasks like anchor holes.

**Q: How long does the battery take to charge?**
A: Roughly 60 minutes for a full charge from empty, based on user reports. The included charger is a standard wall plug, not a USB unit.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a dependable, affordable drill for home projects and value the peace of mind of a spare battery. **Skip it if:** You need a keyless chuck, professional-grade torque, or a tool you can drop-kick across a job site.

At 4.6 stars, this kit delivers exactly what most homeowners need — and then some. It's not flashy, but it's honest work, and that's worth your money.
