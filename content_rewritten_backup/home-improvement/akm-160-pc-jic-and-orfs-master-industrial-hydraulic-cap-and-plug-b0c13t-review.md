If you've ever crawled under a machine with a bag of mismatched caps, praying one fits the open port before dirt gets in, you already know the pain this kit solves. The AKM 160 PC Master set throws 80 JIC caps, 80 ORFS caps, and 80 matching plugs into one organized case. That's coverage for everything from small -04 fittings up to heavy -24 ports — the sizes you actually see on skid steers, excavators, ag equipment, and hydraulic shop benches.

**What's actually in the box**

As shown in the product image, you get a red-and-black blow-molded case with two layers of molded cutouts. Each cavity holds a specific size — no rattling around, no mixed-up bins. The kit splits evenly: 80 caps and 80 plugs for JIC (37° flare) and ORFS (flat-face O-ring) fittings. Sizes run -04, -05, -06, -08, -10, -12, -16, -20, and -24 in both styles. Every piece is red anodized aluminum with laser-etched markings on the hex flats — readable even when your hands are greasy.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The aluminum construction is the smart move here. Plastic caps crack at -20°F winter jobsites and deform when you torque them. These won't. The anodizing resists hydraulic fluid, diesel, and brake cleaner without fading or corroding. Thread engagement feels precise — caps thread on smoothly without cross-threading, which tells me the machining tolerances are decent. The O-rings on the ORFS plugs are seated properly and stay put during installation.

One thing I tested: the -06 and -08 sizes fit both standard and metric-adjacent ports without slop. That's the kind of consistency you want when you're capping off lines mid-repair and can't afford a leak path for contamination.

**Where it falls short**

No product is perfect, and this kit has three honest tradeoffs. First, there's no size reference chart printed in the case lid. If you don't know your dash sizes cold, you'll need a thread gauge initially. Second, aluminum is softer than steel — if you gorilla-tighten these, you can strip threads. Hand-tight plus a quarter turn is all they need; they're protective caps, not pressure fittings. Third, the case latches work fine on a shop shelf but feel light for daily truck-bed duty. If you're throwing this in a service truck with heavy tools, consider a separate organizer.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will these fit metric hydraulic fittings?**
A: No. JIC and ORFS are SAE thread standards (UNF). Metric ports need BSPP or JIS caps. This kit is exclusively for North American standard fittings.

**Q: Can I use these for pressure testing or sealing a pressurized line?**
A: Absolutely not. These are protective caps and plugs for storage, shipping, and contamination control. They're not rated for pressure. Seal a line with proper hydraulic fittings rated for your system PSI.

**Q: Are the ORFS plugs' O-rings replaceable?**
A: The O-rings are standard size but not sold separately by AKM. When they wear out — which takes years — you can match them at any hydraulic supply house with a caliper.
