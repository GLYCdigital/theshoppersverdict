Let's be honest: there are dozens of fixed-base routers under $200, and most of them feel like toys the first time you hog out a 3/4-inch dado in white oak. The Bosch 1617EVS is not one of them. It's been a workshop staple for over a decade, and with a 4.8-star average across 240 Amazon ratings, it's earned that reputation through sheer performance. But is it still the right buy in 2026? Yes — with a couple of caveats.

## What's in the Box

Bosch ships the 1617EVS with the fixed base, a 1/4-inch and 1/2-inch collet, a wrench, and a narrow edge guide. That's it. Notably absent is the plunge base — that's sold separately as the Bosch 1617EVSP kit, which bundles both bases for roughly $60–80 more. If you plan to do dovetails or any plunge work, spring for the kit. Buying the base later costs more than buying it bundled.

## Performance and Build Quality

The headline feature here is the 2.25 HP motor with electronic speed control. In practice, this means you can dial in between 8,000 and 25,000 RPM, and the motor holds that speed under load. Run a 1/2-inch straight bit through a 4/4 maple board, and you'll feel the difference versus a cheaper router that bogs down and burns the wood. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: *"I've used this on everything from pine to hickory, and it never stalls. My old Craftsman would struggle, this just eats through."*

The fixed base itself is machined aluminum with a micro-fine depth adjustment ring. It's precise — you can dial in a full turn for 1/16-inch adjustments or use the fine ring for hair-thin tweaks. The base also has threaded inserts on all four sides, so mounting it in a router table is a five-minute job. The spindle lock for bit changes is genuinely tool-free: pull the lever, swap the bit, release. No wrenches.

Dust collection is another strong point. The clear plastic shroud connects to a standard 1-1/4-inch or 2-1/2-inch vacuum hose and captures a surprising amount of debris. It's not perfect — you'll still get fine dust on your hands — but it's far better than most routers in this class.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the reviews, three themes dominate. First, the smoothness: *"Variable speed is so smooth — no vibration even at full RPM."* Second, the build: *"Handles like a precision tool, not a toy. The depth adjustment is dead accurate."* Third, the versatility: owners use it for edge profiling, joinery, and table-mounted work with equal success. The 12-amp motor delivers real torque without overheating, even in extended runs.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and the 1617EVS has three honest weaknesses. First, the fixed base only — if you need plunge capability, the separate kit is the better value. Second, the 8-foot cord is annoyingly short. You'll be reaching for an extension cord on anything larger than a shelf board. Third, the above-table height adjustment is not included — if you mount this under a router table, you'll need to buy the RA1160 or RA1161 adjustment mechanism separately. That's an extra $30–40 some competitors include.

Also note: the dust collection shroud can make bit visibility slightly worse in tight corners. It's a tradeoff between visibility and cleanup.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Cabinet makers and serious hobbyists** who need consistent power in hardwoods — this is your tool.
- **Router table users** who want a reliable motor without paying Festool prices.
- **Anyone upgrading from a trim router or cheap combo kit** — the precision here is night and day.

Skip it if you're a casual DIYer doing occasional edge rounding on pine. A $60 trim router will do that job, and the 1617EVS is overkill for your needs.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use 1/2-inch shank bits with this router?**
Yes. It comes with both 1/4-inch and 1/2-inch collets, and the 2.25 HP motor handles large bits comfortably.

**Q: Does this router work well in a router table?**
Yes, but plan on extra cost. It mounts easily via the four threaded base holes, but you'll need the separate RA1160 above-table adjuster for convenient bit height changes.

**Q: Is the variable speed really necessary?**
For bits over 1 inch in diameter, yes. Large bits generate too much heat at full RPM and can burn wood. The electronic speed control lets you match RPM to bit size, which protects both your workpiece and the bit.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a workhorse fixed-base router that won't bog down, holds precise depth settings, and will last years. **Skip it if** you need plunge capability (buy the 1617EVSP kit instead) or you're only doing light occasional work.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — deducting half a star for the short cord and the separate plunge base cost. The performance itself is flawless; the accessories are where Bosch pinches pennies.

**Final advice:** If you're on the fence, check both the fixed-base-only version and the kit with the plunge base. The price difference is small relative to the added utility, and you'll kick yourself later if you need plunge capability you didn't buy.
