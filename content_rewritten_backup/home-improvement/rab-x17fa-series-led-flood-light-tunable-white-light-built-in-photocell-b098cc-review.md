# RAB X17FA Series LED Flood Light Review: Tunable White, Built-In Photocell

**The Short Version:** If you need a commercial-grade flood light that doesn't require an electrician to rewire your junction box, the RAB X17FA delivers serious output with a genuinely useful feature: a built-in photocell that you can override. It's not cheap, but it's built like a tank and should outlast most of the fixtures you've owned.

---

## What You Actually Get

The RAB X17FA is a 17-watt LED flood light with a die-cast aluminum housing and a tempered glass lens. It's rated for wet locations (IP65), which means it can take direct rain and snow without flinching. The unit comes with a swivel bracket mount that lets you aim it in just about any direction, and the photocell is pre-installed — no separate sensor to wire up.

One thing that stands out immediately: this fixture is **UL Listed for wet locations**, and RAB backs it with a 5-year limited warranty. That's meaningful for a fixture in this class.

---

## Performance and Build Quality

Let's talk about the tunable white feature, because that's the headline. The X17FA lets you select between 3000K (warm), 4000K (neutral), and 5000K (daylight) — but here's the catch: **you make the selection with a switch on the fixture itself, before installation.** It's not a smart-light feature you can adjust from your phone. For most buyers, that's fine — you pick your color temperature once and forget it. But if you were hoping to change it later, plan ahead.

The photocell is the real win here. It's built into the fixture and uses a simple twist-lock or slide mechanism (depending on the exact X17FA variant) to set it to **on, off, or auto**. In auto mode, the light turns on at dusk and off at dawn. This is a huge upgrade over wiring a separate photocell into your circuit, and it's the feature buyers consistently mention in their reviews.

Output-wise, 17 watts doesn't sound like much, but the X17FA produces roughly 1,800 lumens — comparable to a 150-watt halogen floodlight. The beam spread is wide and even, with no hot spots or dark rings. That's typical of RAB's optics, which are consistently solid.

## Where It Falls Short

Honestly, the downsides are few, but they're worth knowing:

1. **The color temperature switch is pre-install only.** Once it's mounted and wired, changing from 4000K to 3000K means taking it down. If you're indecisive about tone, this could be a hassle.
2. **17W is modest for large areas.** This is perfect for a porch, garage, or small yard. If you're lighting a full driveway or a large backyard, you'll want two — or look at RAB's higher-wattage options.
3. **No motion sensor.** The photocell handles dusk-to-dawn, but there's no built-in motion detection. If that's a priority, you'll need to pair it with a separate motion sensor or pick a different fixture.

## FAQ: Three Questions Buyers Ask

**Q: Does the photocell work if the fixture is in a shaded area?**
A: Generally yes — the sensor is designed to respond to ambient light levels, not direct sunlight. But if it's under heavy tree cover, it may turn on a bit early. You can adjust the built-in delay (on some variants) or just use the manual switch.

**Q: Can I wire this to a regular light switch?**
A: Yes. The photocell can be set to "on" mode, which bypasses the sensor entirely and gives you full manual control via your wall switch. This is a great option if you want to use it on a timer instead.

**Q: Is the color temperature truly adjustable after installation?**
A: No — the selector switch must be set before you mount the fixture. Pick your color temperature during install. We recommend testing the setting before final mounting if you're unsure.
