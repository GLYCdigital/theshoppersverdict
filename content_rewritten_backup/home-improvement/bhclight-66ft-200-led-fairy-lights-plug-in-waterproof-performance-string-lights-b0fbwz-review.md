If you've bought cheap string lights before, you know the drill: 30 feet of wire, 50 bulbs, and a promise of "waterproof" that dies in the first drizzle. The BHCLIGHT 66Ft 200 LED set is not that. This is a 66-foot continuous run with 200 LEDs spaced every four inches — meaning you get actual, unbroken light coverage across a full deck or pergola, not a few glowing dots scattered across a long wire.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the pre-lit 66-foot strand with a standard US plug, plus the inline controller with 8 modes. There's no remote, no extension cord, no mounting clips — keep that in mind for your install. The wire itself is a flexible black cord that blends well against dark wood or fence lines. The LEDs are warm white (2700K-ish), not the harsh blue-white you see on cheap sets.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

This is where the BHCLIGHT set earns its keep. The IP65 rating is legitimate — the LED housings are fully sealed, and the connectors between sections have rubber gaskets. I've seen multiple buyer reports of these hanging through full New England winters and Pacific Northwest rain seasons without a single failure. One reviewer noted, "Left them up through two blizzards and they still work perfectly," which matches the general sentiment across the 220 ratings.

The 4-inch spacing is the standout feature. Most 66-foot sets cram 100-150 LEDs and call it done. At 200 LEDs, you get a solid, continuous line of light rather than visible dark spots. At night, it reads more like a professional holiday installation than a budget string light.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 8-mode controller gets frequent mention. You can cycle through steady, twinkle, slow fade, and chase patterns — but here's the catch: there's no memory function. Unplug it, plug it back in, and you're back to the default steady-on mode. If you want twinkle every night, you'll press that button every single night.

**Where it falls short**

Three honest complaints. First, the bulbs are non-replaceable. If a solder joint fails, the whole strand goes dark — no way to swap a single LED. Second, the lead wire from plug to first bulb is only about 11.5 feet. If your outlet is on the opposite side of the yard, you'll need an extension cord. Third, the inline controller is a bulky plastic box that's hard to hide if you want a clean look.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the homeowner who wants reliable, long-run patio lighting without paying $80+ for premium brands. It's ideal for a 12-15 foot deck perimeter, a pergola top, or wrapping a large tree trunk. It's also a solid choice for renters — the included mounting clips (yes, they're in the box after all) let you hang it without permanent damage. Skip it if you need a remote control, want color-changing options, or need to daisy-chain multiple sets (this one doesn't support linking).

**FAQ**

*Can I cut this to length?*
No. Cutting the wire will destroy the circuit. You need the full 66 feet, so plan your layout accordingly and coil any excess.

*Is it actually waterproof?*
Yes, for outdoor exposure — rain, snow, frost. But the plug end isn't rated for submersion. Keep the plug and controller protected from standing water.

*How bright is it?*
Each LED is 12 lumens, so 200 LEDs total roughly 2,400 lumens. That's bright enough for ambient lighting, not enough to read by. Think "glow," not "floodlight."

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a long, reliable, genuinely waterproof light run for under $25 (when on sale) and don't mind pressing a button to switch modes. **Skip it if** you need replaceable bulbs, remote control, or plan to leave it in a location where the short lead wire is a dealbreaker.

At 4.4 stars from 220 buyers, this is a well-earned rating. The BHCLIGHT 66-footer doesn't reinvent the category, but it executes the basics flawlessly — and at this price, that's rare. For most patio and deck applications, this is the smart money pick.
