If you're a homeowner who's tired of corded tools or a pro looking to standardize on one platform, the DEWALT DCK594P2 5-tool combo kit deserves a hard look. This is not the entry-level set DEWALT sells to beginners — it's the XR (eXtreme Runtime) line, which means brushless motors, better electronics, and batteries that actually last. And with 235 Amazon ratings averaging 4.7 stars, buyers are clearly getting what they paid for.

**What's actually in the box**

You get five tools: the DCD791 20V XR compact drill/driver, DCF887 impact driver, DCS570 7-1/4" circular saw, DCS369 reciprocating saw, and DCL040 worklight. DEWALT includes two 5Ah XR batteries, a fast charger, and a contractor bag. That's the full story — no surprises, no hidden "requires separate purchase" fine print.

**Performance and build quality**

The DCD791 drill is the standout. It's compact — 6.3 inches nose-to-tail — but delivers 460 unit watts out (UWO) of torque. That's enough to drive 3-inch deck screws without pre-drilling, and the all-metal chuck grips bits without the wobble you see on cheaper drills. The DCF887 impact driver is similarly impressive: 1,825 in-lbs of torque, three speed settings, and a LED that actually illuminates your work area.

The DCS570 circular saw cuts 2x4s cleanly at full depth. It's not the lightest saw — 7.5 pounds with the battery — but the magnesium shoe stays flat and the 24-tooth carbide blade that ships with it is usable, not disposable. The DCS369 reciprocating saw has a 1-1/8" stroke and tool-free blade change. It vibrates more than the corded version, but for occasional demo work or PVC/copper cuts, it's plenty.

Battery life is where this kit justifies its price. Two 5Ah batteries mean you're not constantly swapping. In real-world testing, buyers report getting through a full day of framing with one battery on the drill and one on the saw. The charger tops a depleted 5Ah pack in about 60 minutes.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Reading through the reviews, a few themes repeat. One buyer wrote, "I've used this set professionally for 6 months — framing, trim, and electrical. The drill never stalls and the batteries hold charge better than my old 3Ah ones." Another noted, "The compact drill fits in tight spaces where my old drill wouldn't. This kit replaced three corded tools."

The common thread: buyers are surprised by how much power DEWALT packed into tools this compact. The XR brushless motors run cool even under sustained load, and the battery indicator on each pack removes the guesswork.

**Where it falls short**

No product is perfect, and this kit has honest tradeoffs. The contractor bag is thin — it won't survive falling off a truck bed. Several reviewers mention buying a separate TSTAK case for real job-site durability. The circular saw's blade wrench storage is awkward: it clips to the shoe and tends to fall off in the bag. And the worklight, while bright at 450 lumens, drains a 5Ah battery in about 90 minutes. It's fine for quick inspections, not for lighting a room while you work.

One more thing: this kit doesn't include a charger that handles the 20V Max batteries at their fastest rate. The included charger is the standard 4-amp version, not the 8-amp fast charger DEWALT sells separately.

**Who should buy this**

Buy it if you're a serious DIYer tackling projects beyond hanging shelves — decks, fences, furniture, or basic remodeling. It's also a solid choice for tradespeople who want one kit for service calls without hauling a full rolling toolbox. Skip it if you only need a drill and driver for occasional repairs; the 2-tool kits from DEWALT will save you money. And if you already own 20V Max tools, you can buy these bare — but the battery savings here make the full kit the better deal.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is the circular saw powerful enough for framing?**
A: Yes, for occasional framing. It cuts through 2x6 pressure-treated lumber without bogging down, though it's not a replacement for a corded saw on a full-day framing job.

**Q: Do the batteries fit other DEWALT 20V tools?**
A: Yes, all 20V Max batteries and tools are cross-compatible. These 5Ah batteries work with any DEWALT 20V tool, including the FlexVolt line (at reduced voltage).

**Q: What's the warranty?**
A: Three-year limited warranty on tools, plus a 90-day money-back guarantee. Batteries and chargers are covered for three years too.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want one kit to handle everything from drywall screws to ripping plywood, with batteries that last and a brand that stands behind its products.

**Skip it if** you're on a tight budget, only need a drill and driver, or need a job-site storage solution — plan to spend another $40–60 on a hard case.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The DCK594P2 delivers genuinely pro-level performance at a price that undercuts buying tools individually. The battery capacity alone makes this kit worth the investment. Just budget for a proper case and keep expectations realistic about the worklight.
