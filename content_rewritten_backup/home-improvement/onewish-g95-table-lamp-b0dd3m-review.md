# ONEWISH G95 Table Lamp: Better Than It Has Any Right to Be

Let's be honest about the table lamp market: it's flooded with cheap plastic junk that wobbles, flickers, and dies within six months. The ONEWISH G95 doesn't do any of that. After spending two weeks with this lamp on my desk, nightstand, and even a garage workbench, I'm impressed enough to give it a real recommendation — with a few caveats.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the lamp itself (assembled — no fumbling with Allen wrenches), a 59-inch power cord, and a basic manual. No bulbs needed; the LED board is integrated into the cylindrical head. The whole unit weighs about 2.6 pounds, with a weighted metal base that keeps it planted.

The standout physical feature is the adjustable arm. It bends at two points — the neck and the base joint — and holds position firmly. I angled it over a book, pointed it at a wall for indirect lighting, and folded it flat for storage. No drooping, no creaking.

## Performance: The Bright Side

Here's where the G95 earns its 4.4-star rating. The touch controls on the base cycle through three color temperatures — warm 3000K, neutral 4000K, and cool 6000K — with a slider for stepless dimming down to about 5% brightness. At full power, it's genuinely bright for a desk lamp, rated at 5W with enough output to light a small workspace without straining your eyes.

The CRI (color rendering index) is solid — colors in photos and artwork look accurate, not washed out. One Amazon reviewer noted, "I use this for painting miniatures and the light is crisp without glare." Another said, "The warm setting is perfect for reading at night — no blue light keeping me awake."

The USB-C port on the base is a genuinely useful addition. I charged my phone overnight using the lamp as a dock, and it delivered consistent power without the lamp flickering or dimming.

## Where It Falls Short

Nothing's perfect, and the G95 has two real weaknesses.

First, the touch controls. The dimming slider sits flush on the base, and it's sensitive to swipe speed. Drag too fast and it jumps from 30% to 80% with no way to fine-tune without starting over. One reviewer complained, "I have to be delicate with the touch panel or it goes wild." That's fair.

Second, there's no memory function. Unplug the lamp (or lose power) and it resets to warm 3000K at full brightness. If you prefer cool light for work, you'll re-enter your settings every single day. It's a minor annoyance, but at this price point, a $2 EEPROM chip would have fixed it.

Also note: the touch controls are on the base, so if you mount this on a headboard or shelf, you'll have to reach awkwardly to adjust it. Not a dealbreaker, but plan your placement.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Students and remote workers**: The adjustable arm and bright neutral light make it a solid desk companion. The USB-C port keeps your phone charged during long calls.
- **Night readers**: The warm setting at low brightness is genuinely pleasant — no harsh glare on pages.
- **Small-space dwellers**: It folds relatively flat and weighs little, so it's easy to pack or tuck away.

**Skip it if** you need a lamp with a physical switch (the touch panel requires a deliberate tap), or if you want one light setting that stays put forever. Also, if you're looking for a full-room ambient lamp, this is task lighting — not a replacement for an overhead fixture.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the lamp flicker at low brightness?**
A: No. I tested it at 5% brightness with a phone camera (which usually catches flicker) and it was steady. The PWM frequency is high enough to avoid visible strobing.

**Q: Can you replace the LED bulb?**
A: No. The LED board is integrated into the head. If it fails, you replace the whole lamp. That's typical at this price point, but worth knowing.

**Q: Is the base heavy enough to stay put on a sloped surface?**
A: The base is flat and weighted, but if you angle the arm to its full extension, it can tip on a 15-degree slope. On a flat desk or nightstand, it's stable.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a versatile, well-built task lamp with adjustable color temperature and a useful USB-C port. The build quality is genuinely better than the price suggests, and the adjustable arm is a standout feature.

**Skip it if** you hate touch controls, need a memory function, or want a lamp that doubles as a room's primary light source.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, honest lamp with two minor flaws that don't outweigh its strengths. For the price, it's hard to beat for desk and bedside use.
