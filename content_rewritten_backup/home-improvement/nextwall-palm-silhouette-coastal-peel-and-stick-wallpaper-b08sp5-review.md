# NextWall Palm Silhouette Coastal Peel and Stick Wallpaper Review

This wallpaper is having a moment. The palm silhouette pattern is all over Pinterest boards and rental-friendly makeover blogs, and at 4.2 stars from 210+ Amazon shoppers, it's clearly connecting with people. But here's the thing about peel-and-stick wallpaper: the marketing photos always look flawless, and the reality is often a sticky, bubbled, misaligned mess. So I dug through the buyer feedback, checked the specs, and compared it against the other coastal-print options in this category. Here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

One roll measures 20.5 inches wide by 18 feet long — that's 30.6 square feet of coverage. It's a standard size for this category, enough for a small accent wall (roughly 8x10 feet) or a half-bath. The material is a vinyl-based film with a paper backing, not the fabric-backed stuff you see at big-box stores. That matters because it means the adhesive is more aggressive but also more forgiving to reposition.

The pattern repeat is 24 inches, which is generous. A larger repeat means you're not wasting material trying to match a busy print every 12 inches. The palm fronds are printed in a dark, almost charcoal-green tone against a warm off-white background. As shown in the product image, the silhouette style gives it a graphic, modern feel — not the faded, tropical-print look you might expect.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where this product surprises. The adhesive is genuinely repositionable. Multiple buyers noted they could peel it back and re-stick it several times without losing tack. One reviewer put it simply: "I messed up the first panel badly, pulled it off, and reapplied it. You can't tell." That's rare in this price tier.

The surface is also more durable than the typical peel-and-stick offering. It's rated for wipe-clean maintenance, and several reviewers confirmed it held up in bathrooms with steam and occasional splashes. The material is 0.008 inches thick — slightly beefier than the ultra-thin options from competing brands, which means it hides minor texture better and resists tearing along the edges during installation.

But there's a catch. Because the background is light, this wallpaper is merciless about what's underneath it. If your wall has old paint texture, nail holes, or even slight joint compound ridges, they'll telegraph right through. One buyer warned, "Prep your walls like you're painting a showroom. This shows everything." That's not a defect — it's physics — but it's worth knowing before you buy.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The pattern itself gets the most love. Buyers describe it as "sophisticated" and "not your grandma's palm print." Multiple people used it in entryways and powder rooms specifically because it reads more like modern art than tropical kitsch. The 24-inch repeat also earned frequent shout-outs for making alignment painless.

The removability factor is the other big win. Renters especially noted it came off cleanly without damaging paint — as long as they followed the instructions and pulled at a 180-degree angle, not straight out.

## Where It Falls Short

The color discrepancy is the most common complaint. The photos show a soft, muted print. In reality, the palm silhouettes are darker and the contrast is sharper. About a dozen reviewers mentioned being surprised by the boldness. If you want a subtle background texture, this isn't it.

The other issue is application on textured walls. This is not the wallpaper for knockdown or orange-peel surfaces. The film won't conform to heavy texture, and you'll end up with visible air pockets that won't smooth out. Smooth, primed drywall only — no exceptions.

## Who Should Buy This

This is for the renter who wants a dramatic accent wall without a security deposit battle. It's for the homeowner who wants a coastal vibe without committing to paint that'll be a pain to cover later. It's also for first-time wallpaper installers — the forgiving adhesive and larger pattern repeat make it one of the easier peel-and-stick products to get right on the first try.

Skip it if your walls are textured, if you want a subtle print, or if you're covering a large room on a budget — at roughly $30 per roll, a full room will add up fast.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I reuse it after removal?**
A: No. The adhesive loses its grip once fully removed. It's removable, not reusable.

**Q: Will it work in a steamy bathroom?**
A: Yes, but keep it away from direct water contact. Steam is fine; splashes on the surface are fine to wipe off. Don't install it inside a shower enclosure.

**Q: How do I cut it for outlets and corners?**
A: Use a sharp utility knife and a straight edge. The vinyl cuts cleanly without tearing. Score lightly first, then finish the cut.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if** you have smooth, primed walls and want a bold, modern coastal pattern that's genuinely forgiving to install and cleanly removable.

**Skip it if** your walls have texture, you want a subtle print, or you're covering more than one accent wall.

The NextWall Palm Silhouette delivers exactly what it promises — a high-impact, renter-friendly wall covering with better-than-average adhesive and durability. Just prep your walls properly and expect the pattern to be bolder than the photos. For the price, it's a solid buy.
