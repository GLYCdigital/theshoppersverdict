If your bathroom renovation budget is tight but you refuse to install builder-grade junk, the Moen Voss 6901ORB deserves a hard look. This is a classic two-handle centerset faucet that nails the traditional aesthetic without feeling dated — and it's held up well enough in real homes to earn a 4.6-star average across 229 Amazon ratings.

**What's in the box**

Moen ships the Voss with the faucet body, two lever handles, a matching drain assembly with pop-up stopper, and mounting hardware. You'll also get supply lines with quick-connect fittings — though they're standard 3/8-inch compression, so measure your existing shut-off valve distance before assuming they'll reach. The drain is the real deal: solid brass with a metal pop-up mechanism, not the cheap plastic stopper you see on many sub-$100 faucets.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

This faucet is heavy. The all-metal body has a substantial feel that immediately separates it from the hollow, lightweight units at big-box stores. The oil-rubbed bronze finish is applied over a brushed nickel base layer — that's why it doesn't chip off in sheets like some cheaper ORB finishes. Instead, it develops a subtle patina where you touch it most. That's the look you're paying for, but it's worth knowing: ORB is a living finish. Harsh chemical cleaners will accelerate wear, so stick to mild soap and water or a dedicated ORB polish.

The two-handle operation is straightforward — quarter-turn ceramic disc cartridges (Moen's 1224 series) give smooth, precise flow control. Hot and cold handles are clearly marked, and the lever design is easy to grip even with wet hands. The high-arc spout provides decent clearance for washing hands but doesn't swivel, so if your sink has two basins, expect to pick one side.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The most repeated positive theme in customer feedback is the installation ease. Multiple reviewers call out that it "fit perfectly" into existing 4-inch centerset holes with no countertop modification needed. One buyer noted, "Took 20 minutes to install and looks like a $300 faucet." Others emphasize the finish quality — several mention getting compliments from guests, and a few say they've had theirs installed for over a year with zero tarnishing or spotting.

**Where it falls short**

The fixed spout is the biggest functional limitation. If you have a deep rectangular vessel sink, the non-swiveling design makes rinsing the far corners awkward. A few reviewers also report the pop-up drain linkage can be finicky to adjust — the lift rod sometimes requires precise alignment to close fully. And while the oil-rubbed bronze looks stunning, it's not the most durable finish on the market; expect to see wear at contact points within a few years if you're hard on fixtures.

**Who should buy this**

- **The DIY renovator** replacing an old centerset faucet — this drops right in.
- **Anyone matching a traditional/timeless bathroom** with chrome, nickel, or bronze accents.
- **Buyers who want Moen's warranty** without paying premium for a brand-name showroom model.

**Skip it if** you need a swiveling spout, prefer a modern single-handle look, or want a finish that'll stay pristine with minimal care.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the drain assembly match the faucet finish?**
A: Yes, the pop-up drain is finished in the same oil-rubbed bronze. It's a matching set, not a generic chrome drain.

**Q: Will this fit a 3-hole sink with 4-inch centers?**
A: Yes, that's exactly what a centerset design is for. The faucet base covers all three holes, and the handles sit on the outer two.

**Q: Can I install this without a plumber?**
A: Most homeowners can handle it. You'll need a basin wrench and some patience with the supply line connections. Allow 30–45 minutes for a straightforward install.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a handsome, well-built centerset faucet that won't embarrass you when guests use the powder room — and you're comfortable with the living finish of ORB.

**Skip it if** you need spout swivel or want a low-maintenance chrome finish.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It's not perfect, but at this price point, the Voss delivers exceptional value and Moen's dependable backing. For most shoppers, that's a winning combination.
