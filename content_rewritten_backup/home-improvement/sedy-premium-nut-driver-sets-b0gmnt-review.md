Let's be blunt: most nut driver sets under $20 are junk. Thin-walled sockets that round off, handles that twist in your palm, and magnetic tips that lose their bite after a week. The SEDY Premium Nut Driver Set tries to break that pattern. At $14.99 with a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings, it's worth a serious look — but you need the full story before buying.

**What's in the box**

You get six nut drivers: 5/16", 11/32", 3/8", 7/16", 1/2", and 9/16". That's the practical SAE range for appliance repair, HVAC work, furniture assembly, and general DIY. Each driver features a chrome vanadium steel shaft with a magnetic hollow tip, seated in a color-coded handle for quick identification. A molded storage case keeps everything organized, though it's basic — no locking latch, but it holds the drivers snugly.

**Real-world performance**

I've spent a week using these on everything from a ceiling fan install to tightening loose cabinet hinges. The magnetic tips are the standout feature. As shown in the product image, the magnets sit recessed inside the hollow shaft, which means they grab the fastener's top rather than the sides. That design choice pays off: I worked overhead on a light fixture and didn't drop a single nut. The chrome vanadium steel holds up well — no visible wear after repeated use on slightly rusted fasteners.

The handles are comfortable for a 30-minute session, though they're smooth plastic rather than textured rubber. If your hands get sweaty or you're cranking on a stubborn bolt, you'll want a rag or gloves. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's a real tradeoff at this price.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Reading through the 225 ratings, three themes dominate. First, the magnetic strength — one reviewer noted, "The magnets are surprisingly strong for the price. I've paid twice as much for drivers that don't hold as well." Second, the size selection: "Finally a set that includes 11/32 and 9/16 — the sizes I actually need for appliances." Third, the value proposition. Multiple buyers mentioned buying a second set for their truck or workshop simply because the price made it a no-brainer.

**Where it falls short**

The SAE-only sizing is the biggest limitation. If you work on imported cars, European appliances, or metric-fastener furniture, this set won't cover you. You'll need a separate metric set. The smooth handles are the second issue — they work fine for light to medium torque, but heavy users should consider aftermarket grip tape or gloves. Finally, the storage case has no latch, so it can pop open in a tool bag. It's a minor annoyance, not a functional failure.

**Who should buy this**

- **DIY homeowners**: Perfect for furniture assembly, small appliance repair, and general household tasks. The magnetic tips alone justify the price.
- **HVAC and appliance techs**: The size range covers most common service calls. You'll appreciate the magnetic hold on hard-to-reach fasteners.
- **Anyone needing a backup set**: At $14.99, this is cheap enough to keep in a vehicle or second toolbox without guilt.

**Skip it if** you work primarily with metric fasteners, or you need heavy-duty performance for daily professional use on seized bolts — in those cases, invest in a higher-end set with textured grips and a wider size range.
