## Sixteen strands. One smart play.

Most fairy light sets give you one 10-foot strand and charge you $15 for the privilege. This 16-pack flips that math — you're getting 160 feet of lighting total, which changes how you think about decorating a room, a patio, or a wedding venue.

Here's what actually arrives in the box: 16 separate 10-foot strands, each with its own battery pack and switch. No master controller, no remote, no smartphone app. Just straightforward, independent light strings that each run on 2 AA batteries. The wire is thin gauge copper with warm white LEDs spaced evenly along the length — the classic fairy light look, not the harsh bluish tint of cheap LEDs.

## The build quality reality check

Let me be direct about the wire: it's thin. That's not a flaw — it's physics. You can't fit 16 strands into a compact package with thick gauge wire. But it means you need to handle these with respect. Gentle bends for shaping around mirrors or headboards? Fine. Yanking them tight across a room or wrapping them aggressively around railings? You're asking for a dead strand.

The battery packs are the weak point. Each one has a small slide switch, and on a few units, the switch feels slightly loose. The included wire ties work for bundling, but the packs themselves have no mounting holes — you'll need tape or command strips to hide them, which buyers consistently mention.

## Three questions shoppers ask

**Q: How long do the batteries last?**
With standard AA alkalines, expect 8-12 hours of continuous bright light, then gradual dimming. Use lithium AAs for roughly double that. The LEDs themselves are rated for thousands of hours — batteries are your limiting factor.

**Q: Can these be used outdoors?**
The battery packs are not weather-sealed. Under a covered porch or inside a sealed lantern, fine. Direct rain or sprinkler exposure will kill them. Don't risk it.

**Q: Are the LEDs replaceable if one burns out?**
No. The LEDs are embedded in the wire with sealed connections. A dead LED means that section is dark permanently. With 16 strands, this is rarely fatal to your setup — swap in a spare.
