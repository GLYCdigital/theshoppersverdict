# Appliance Buddy/Mover Review: The $30 Floor-Saving Hack Worth Knowing

You're staring at a 350-pound refrigerator that needs to move across your new hardwood floors. The furniture sliders you already own are too small. The cardboard trick leaves black marks. Your back hurts just thinking about it.

This is exactly the scenario the Appliance Buddy/Mover was built for. And after digging through 220 ratings and real buyer experiences, here's the honest breakdown of whether it delivers.

## What You Actually Get

The Appliance Buddy is a single mat, not a set. It measures 28 inches by 20 inches — large enough to slide under a full-size fridge or washing machine. The key feature is the material: a 5mm thick felt-like pad on the bottom that grips the floor, paired with a slick surface on top that lets the appliance glide.

The design is simple. You tip the appliance slightly, slide the mat underneath, and push. The mat stays put while the appliance moves across it. When you need to reposition, you lift and shift the mat to the next spot.

## How It Performs in Real-World Use

The physics here are sound. The felt bottom creates enough friction to stay in place on hard floors, while the top surface allows the appliance to slide with surprisingly little effort. Buyers consistently report moving refrigerators, stoves, and washing machines solo — something that would normally require a second person and a lot of swearing.

One verified buyer put it plainly: "I moved my full-size refrigerator across my entire kitchen and into the dining room by myself. I'm a 52-year-old woman and I did it in about 10 minutes." That's the kind of testimonial that matters.

The mat handles the weight too. Multiple reviewers mention using it for 400+ pound appliances without tearing or compressing the material. The felt doesn't shed fibers onto floors, and it doesn't leave scuff marks on tile or hardwood.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Three themes dominate the positive feedback:

**Floor protection that's real, not marketing.** The 5mm thickness is the difference-maker. Thinner mats let debris get trapped underneath and scratch floors. This one creates enough of a buffer that even slightly rough floors are safe.

**One-person operation.** The whole point of this tool is eliminating the need for a helper. Time and again, reviewers mention moving appliances alone that they previously needed two people for.

**Versatility across floor types.** It works on hardwood, laminate, tile, vinyl, and even low-pile carpet. You don't need to buy different mats for different rooms.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations.

**Carpet is the weak point.** On plush or thick carpet, the felt bottom grabs and the mat doesn't slide as smoothly. You'll still move the appliance, but it takes more effort and the mat tends to bunch up. If your entire path is thick carpet, this isn't the tool for you.

**Inclines remain a problem.** On any slope, even a slight one, a heavy appliance will want to run away from you. The mat reduces friction but doesn't add braking power. Several reviewers noted this as a safety concern on ramps or thresholds.

**Storage is awkward.** At 28 inches long and 3.5 pounds, this isn't something you toss in a drawer. It's a garage or closet item.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:**

- You're moving a refrigerator, washer, dryer, or stove and you're doing it solo
- You have hardwood, laminate, tile, or vinyl floors you care about
- You move appliances more than once a year — for a single move, renting a dolly might be cheaper

**Skip it if:**

- Your entire path is thick, plush carpet
- You need to move appliances up or down stairs (this mat won't help with stairs)
- You're on a tight budget and only need it once — consider renting instead

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit under my refrigerator without scratching the bottom?**
A: Yes. The top surface is smooth and won't scratch the appliance base. You'll need to tip the fridge slightly to get the mat underneath — use a pry bar or have someone lift the front edge.

**Q: Can I use it on carpet?**
A: Low-pile carpet works fine. Thick, plush carpet is a struggle — the felt bottom grabs and the mat won't slide smoothly.

**Q: How many mats do I need?**
A: One is usually enough for a straight move. For L-shaped paths or tight corners, two mats make repositioning easier — slide the appliance onto the second mat, then move the first mat ahead.
