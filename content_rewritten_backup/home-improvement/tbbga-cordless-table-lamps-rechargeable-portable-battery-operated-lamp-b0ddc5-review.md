If you've ever tripped over an extension cord running across a patio, or wrestled with a lamp that needs an outlet within arm's reach, you already know why cordless lighting makes sense. The TBBGA Cordless Table Lamp is a battery-powered light that promises to kill the cord problem entirely. After digging through the specs and 235 verified buyer ratings (4.7 stars), here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the lamp base, a shade, and a USB-C charging cable. That's it — no wall adapter, which is worth knowing before you buy. The lamp itself stands about 11 inches tall with a fabric shade that softens the LED glow. The base houses a 2000mAh lithium battery, and the whole unit weighs just over 2 pounds. It's genuinely portable — you can carry it one-handed from dining table to nightstand without thinking about it.

## Performance and Build Quality

The headline feature is the stepless dimming. A touch-sensitive panel on the base lets you sweep from 5% brightness up to full output, plus three color temperatures: warm white (3000K), neutral (4000K), and daylight (6000K). That's not just marketing — the range between dim and bright is genuinely useful. At 5%, it's a subtle nightlight. At 100%, it's bright enough to read by, though not to illuminate a whole room.

Battery life tracks with brightness. At full output, expect around 8 hours. Crank it down to 50% and you'll get closer to 16-18 hours. At the lowest setting, the manufacturer claims up to 30 hours — and multiple buyers confirm this is roughly accurate. One reviewer noted, "I've had it on my nightstand for a week at 30% brightness and haven't charged it once." The USB-C port is a welcome modern touch; any phone charger will top it up in about 3 hours.

The touch controls respond well, though there's a slight learning curve. Tap to turn on, hold to dim, tap the color icon to cycle temperatures. It's intuitive enough that most buyers figure it out without opening the manual.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The portability is the #1 reason people buy this lamp, and it delivers. "We use it for our deck dinners and it's perfect — no cords, just light," wrote one verified buyer. Another mentioned using it for power outages: "Kept our kitchen usable during a 5-hour blackout. Worth every penny."

The build quality also gets consistent credit. The fabric shade feels substantial rather than flimsy, and the metal base has enough heft to feel stable — on flat surfaces.

## Where It Falls Short

The lightweight base is a double-edged sword. On an uneven surface — a wobbly patio table or a couch armrest — the lamp can tip over if bumped. It won't shatter, but it's annoying. Several reviewers mentioned this specifically.

There's also no remote control or smart home compatibility. At this price point, that's not a dealbreaker, but if you want to dim from the couch, this isn't the lamp for you. And because the base is the only control point, you're always walking over to adjust it.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Patio and deck owners** who want ambient lighting without running cords across walkways
- **Campers and tailgaters** who need reliable light without a generator
- **Anyone who's dealt with frequent power outages** and wants a dependable emergency light
- **Renters** who can't hardwire lighting and want something portable

Skip it if you need smart home integration, want a lamp heavy enough to never tip, or need to light a large room.

## FAQ

**How long does the battery actually last?**
At 100% brightness, roughly 8 hours. At 50%, 16-18 hours. At minimum brightness, up to 30 hours. Real-world results depend on which color temperature you're using — daylight (6000K) drains faster than warm white.

**Can you charge it while using it?**
Yes. The USB-C port allows pass-through charging, so you can keep it plugged in and use it indefinitely. This makes it viable as a permanent desk lamp if you want.

**Is the light bright enough for reading?**
At full brightness, yes. The 4000K neutral setting is best for reading. The 6000K daylight setting works too, but it's harsher on the eyes in a dark room.
