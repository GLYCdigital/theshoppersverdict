The headline feature is the shatterproof construction, and it's not marketing fluff. One Amazon buyer put it plainly: "Dropped one from a 12-foot ladder onto concrete. Didn't even scratch. ## Where They Fall Short

Fitment is the biggest variable. The screw base is a standard E26, but a handful of reviewers reported loose connections on certain socket brands. One wrote, "They screwed in fine but wobbled slightly in my Sunlite sockets. A gentle pinch of the base tabs fixed it." It's not a dealbreaker, but if you're installing on a long run, test one socket first before committing all bulbs.

Brightness is the other tradeoff. At approximately 1.5 watts, each bulb produces roughly 80-100 lumens — noticeably dimmer than a 7-watt glass S14 that puts out 200+ lumens. For ambiance, this is perfect. If you're trying to light a workspace or a commercial patio that needs real illumination, you'll be underwhelmed.
