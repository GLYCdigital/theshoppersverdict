Let's be blunt: most drawer slides under $40 a pair are junk. The Knobonly Soft Close under-mount set isn't junk — but it's also not the forgiving, foolproof upgrade some product listings suggest. After digging through 210 ratings and the spec sheet, here's what actually matters.

**What you're actually getting**

The box contains two slides, mounting brackets, and the screws you'll need for a standard install. These are full-extension slides, meaning the drawer pulls out completely — no wasted space in the back of your cabinet. The soft-close mechanism is built into the slide body itself, not a separate dampener you have to align. That's a meaningful design choice: fewer parts to misalign, fewer failure points.

The load rating matters here. Knobonly specs these for heavy-duty use, and the zinc-alloy construction backs that up. If you're building out a workshop bench or kitchen base cabinets with cast-iron cookware, these won't flex the way lighter steel slides do. I've seen the 100-pound claim hold up in practice based on buyer feedback — the slides don't sag under dense loads.

**Real-world performance**

The soft-close action is the headline feature, and it works — but with a caveat. The dampening engages in the final couple inches of travel, pulling the drawer shut gently rather than letting it slam. Buyers consistently describe the action as "smooth" and "buttery." One reviewer put it simply: *"These make my kitchen feel twice as expensive."* That's the experience you're paying for.

However — and this is the part listings don't advertise — the soft-close only works if your drawer box is perfectly square and your cabinet opening has proper clearance. These slides need about a half-inch of side clearance per side. If your existing drawers are slightly warped or your cabinet frame isn't level, the dampener can bind or fail to engage fully. More than a few reviewers mentioned needing to re-drill holes or add shims to get the geometry right.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest frustration: there's no vertical or horizontal adjustment after installation. Higher-end slides (Blum, Hettich) include adjustment screws so you can fine-tune alignment without removing the drawer. The Knobonly slides don't have that. Once they're screwed in, alignment issues mean unscrewing, shimming, and re-securing. For a first-time installer, that's a real pain point.

Also worth noting: these aren't side-mount slides. Under-mount means you need a drawer box with a solid bottom and enough depth for the mechanism. If you're retrofitting an existing cabinet with shallow drawers, measure twice before you buy. The slides themselves take up vertical space inside the drawer.

**Who should buy these**

This is the right purchase if you're building new cabinets or replacing slides in drawers that are structurally sound. If you're a woodworker or a confident DIYer who measures carefully, the value is hard to beat — you're getting a smooth, full-extension, soft-close experience at a fraction of Blum's price.

Skip them if you're working with old, warped drawers, or if you don't have the patience for precise installation. This isn't a "slap it in and hope" product. It rewards careful work.

**FAQ**

*Do these fit standard kitchen cabinets?*  
Mostly, yes — if you have a standard 3/4-inch overlay cabinet with a full-height drawer box. But always measure the clear opening width first. The slides need roughly 12mm (about 1/2 inch) of clearance on each side of the drawer.

*Can I install these in an existing cabinet without removing the drawer?*  
No. This is a full under-mount install. You'll need to remove the drawer, attach the slides to the drawer box and the cabinet frame, and then reinstall. Budget about an hour per drawer if you're new to this.

*What's the actual weight capacity?*  
The manufacturer rates these for heavy loads, and buyer feedback supports that — people are using them for tool chests and heavy cookware without issue. Realistically, you can load them well past 75 pounds per pair without noticeable sag.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're building new cabinetry or replacing slides in square, solid drawers, and you want soft-close luxury without the high-end price tag. **Skip it if** you need tool-free adjustability or you're working with imperfect existing cabinet boxes.

The Knobonly Soft Close slides deliver 90% of the premium hardware experience at roughly half the cost. The missing adjustment mechanism is the tradeoff — but at 4.2 stars from 210 buyers, most people find the trade worthwhile.
