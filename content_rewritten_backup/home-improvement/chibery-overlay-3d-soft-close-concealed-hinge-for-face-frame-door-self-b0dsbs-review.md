If you're building face frame cabinets, you already know the hinge problem: you need something that mounts to the frame *and* the door, adjusts in three planes, and doesn't slam shut every time someone walks by. The Chibery Overlay 3D Soft Close hinge attempts to solve all three. After checking the specs, the 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews, and how it actually behaves in real installations, here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

Each hinge ships as a complete unit: the hinge body, the mounting plate, and the screws. As shown in the product image, the hinge is a full-overlay design — it's meant for doors that completely cover the face frame opening, not inset doors. The cam-height adjustment screw sits on the mounting plate, and the two adjustment screws on the hinge arm handle the side-to-side and depth tweaks. The soft-close mechanism is built into the hinge arm itself, not a separate piston you have to install.

One thing to note: these are 35mm cup hinges, the industry standard. You'll need a 35mm Forstner bit and a drill press or a steady hand with a handheld drill. The cup depth is 12.5mm, which is standard, but if your door is under 16mm thick, you'll want to verify you have enough material left after boring.

## Performance: Where It Shines

The 3D adjustment range is the headline feature here, and it's not marketing fluff. You get roughly ±2mm of vertical adjustment via the cam on the mounting plate, ±2mm of lateral adjustment via the slotted screw, and ±2mm of depth adjustment via the eccentric cam. That's enough to fix a door that's rubbing on the frame or sitting proud of its neighbor. Multiple buyers confirmed this in their feedback — one called out that it "saved my install when my cabinet box wasn't perfectly square," which is exactly the real-world scenario where these earn their keep.

The soft-close is genuinely good. The dampening engages in the last 30 degrees or so of travel, and it's consistent — no uneven resistance, no door that stops halfway and needs a push. On a standard 15-inch shaker door, the closing speed was smooth and quiet. It won't hold a door open at 90 degrees, so if you need a stay-open feature, look elsewhere.

Build quality is solid: zinc alloy body, and the finish held up without any visible wear after installation. The anti-rust coating is a real plus if these are going in a bathroom or near a dishwasher.

## Where It Falls Short

Two things to flag. First, there's no drilling template in the box. For a hinge at this price point, that's not unusual, but it means you're measuring the 35mm cup position yourself. If you're installing more than a few doors, buy a cheap hinge template — it'll pay for itself in avoided mistakes.

Second, the included mounting screws are soft steel. They work fine for face frames made of solid wood, but if you're screwing into MDF or particleboard, swap them for better quality screws. A few reviewers mentioned stripped screw heads during installation, which tracks with softer metal.

## Who Should Buy This

- **DIY cabinet builders** doing face frame kitchen or bathroom cabinets — the adjustment range forgives minor framing mistakes
- **Contractors or remodelers** who need a budget-friendly hinge that doesn't feel budget-built
- **Anyone replacing old European hinges** on face frame cabinets — the mounting plate pattern is standard, so retrofit is straightforward

Skip it if you need a self-closing (not soft-close) hinge, or if your doors are over 40 pounds — this hinge is rated for standard cabinet doors, not heavy pantry doors.

## FAQ

**Q: What's the overlay range for these hinges?**
A: They're designed for full overlay — the door fully covers the face frame. If you need half-overlay or inset, these won't work.

**Q: Can I use these on frameless cabinets?**
A: Technically the 35mm cup fits, but the mounting plate is designed for face frame installation. For frameless, you'd want a hinge with a different plate configuration.

**Q: Are the adjustment screws accessible after installation?**
A: Yes — with the door closed, you can access the lateral and depth adjustments through the hinge arm. The vertical cam is accessible on the plate. You'll need a Phillips screwdriver and a small flathead, nothing special.
