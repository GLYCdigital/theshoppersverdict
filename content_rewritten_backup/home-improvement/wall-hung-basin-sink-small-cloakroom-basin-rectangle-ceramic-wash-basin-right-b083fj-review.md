If your bathroom is the size of a phone booth, you already know the struggle: standard sinks eat floor space, and pedestal models make the room feel like a maze. This wall-hung basin is a purpose-built solution for exactly that problem — a compact ceramic rectangle that mounts directly to the wall, clearing the floor entirely.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains just the ceramic basin itself — no mounting bracket, no tap, no waste kit. That's worth knowing before you commit. The basin measures roughly 40cm wide by 36cm deep, with a depth of about 12cm. It's a genuine small-cloakroom size, not a scaled-down version of a full sink. The ceramic is white, glossy, and thick enough to feel solid — not flimsy like some budget imports.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The ceramic finish is the standout feature. It's smooth, non-porous, and resists hard water staining better than matte finishes. A quick wipe with a cloth after use keeps it looking new. The wall-hung design is a genuine win for small spaces — you can slide a mop or toilet brush underneath without contortions.

That said, the shallow basin depth is a real consideration. At roughly 12cm deep, water splashes back onto the counter and your shirt if you run the tap at full pressure. Pair it with a low-profile faucet and you'll minimize this, but it's an inherent tradeoff of the compact form factor.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Reviews on this unit run positive — 4.3 stars across 215 ratings — and the recurring theme is the size-to-value ratio. One reviewer noted, "Finally a sink that doesn't dominate my tiny bathroom. The ceramic is thick and the finish is flawless." Another commented on installation: "Took about an hour with a wall-mounted tap. Looks expensive, cost less than dinner out."

**Where It Falls Short**

The lack of included hardware is the most common complaint. You'll need to source a hidden mounting bracket (often sold separately), a wall-mounted faucet, and a waste kit. That adds cost and complexity to what looks like a straightforward install. If you're not comfortable drilling into tile or drywall, budget for a plumber.

Also, the rectangular design has sharp-ish corners — nothing dangerous, but worth noting if you have small children who might bump into it.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this sink come with a faucet?**
A: No. You'll need a separate wall-mounted or deck-mounted faucet. Many buyers pair it with a wall-mounted tap to maximize the space-saving benefit.

**Q: What is the exact width of this basin?**
A: Approximately 40cm (about 15.7 inches). Double-check the product listing for the specific model dimensions, as variations exist.

**Q: Can I install this on drywall?**
A: Yes, but you'll need to anchor into wall studs or use heavy-duty wall anchors rated for the sink's weight plus water. For tile-over-drywall, a masonry bit and proper anchors are essential.
