The O’Bright Cedric isn’t your grandfather’s pharmacy lamp. It’s a modern LED floor lamp that borrows the classic adjustable-arm silhouette but swaps the incandescent bulb for a 12W LED array pushing 1100 lumens. That’s roughly equivalent to a 75W incandescent—bright enough to read fine print, detail a model car, or inspect a solder joint. At 4.3 stars across 215 ratings, it’s clearly earning its keep in most homes. But let’s dig into whether it earns a spot in yours.

**What you actually get**  
The box contains the lamp (base, pole, arm, and LED head pre-assembled—no tools needed), a 6-foot power cord, and a small instruction card. Assembly takes under two minutes: screw the pole into the base, click the arm into the pole, done. The head measures roughly 8 inches across, and the arm extends to about 42 inches from the base. It’s not a compact lamp—plan for a footprint of about 15 inches square on the floor.

**Performance and build quality**  
The Cedric’s party trick is its three color temperatures: 3000K (warm white), 4000K (neutral), and 5000K (daylight). Toggle via the touch sensor on the arm—tap once for on, again to cycle through temps, and a long press to dim (10% to 100%). In practice, the 5000K setting is genuinely useful for detail work—it renders colors accurately without the blue-heavy glare of cheaper LEDs. The 3000K mode is warm enough for evening reading without feeling clinical.

Build quality is solid for the price point. The arm uses spring-loaded tension joints—they hold position firmly without drooping, even when extended fully over a workbench. The base is heavy enough (about 5 pounds) to keep the lamp stable at full extension. One quibble: the base has a smooth plastic underside. On hardwood or tile, a stray foot or a heavy dog tail will slide it a few inches. A rubber pad would have fixed that.

**What buyers consistently praise**  
Scrolling through the 215 ratings, three themes keep coming up. First, brightness range: *“I use it at 20% for watching TV and 100% for sewing—it actually covers both without blinding anyone,”* one reviewer notes. Second, the arm’s flexibility: *“I’ve got it angled over my drafting table and then swung it over my reading chair—it’s like having two lamps.”* Third, the USB-C port on the base—a small touch, but reviewers like charging their phone while working, keeping cords off the desk.

**Where it falls short**  
The touch controls are the weakest link. The sensor sits on the arm, and it’s easy to accidentally brush it while repositioning the head—suddenly your light cycles to a different color temp. A few reviewers also report the sensor being unresponsive when fingers are slightly damp. Not a dealbreaker, but if you’re used to a physical switch, expect an adjustment period.

The other tradeoff: no remote control and no smart-home integration. For a lamp in this category, that’s fine, but if you wanted to turn it off from bed, you’ll be walking to the base.

**Who should buy this**  
- **Detail workers**: Jewelers, model builders, painters, or anyone doing close-up work who needs directional, color-accurate light.  
- **Readers who want options**: The 3000K setting is genuinely relaxing; the 5000K is for reading prescriptions or fine print.  
- **Apartment dwellers with limited floor space**: The vertical footprint is small, and it replaces a table lamp plus a task light.  

**Skip it if** you need a lamp you can drag around (no carry handle, and the cord is fixed), or if you hate touch controls with a passion—look for a switch-based alternative.

**FAQ**  
**Q: Can the head angle straight down for overhead lighting?**  
A: Yes, the head pivots 90 degrees vertically and swivels 240 degrees horizontally. You can point it straight down for a focused pool of light.

**Q: Does the USB-C port charge fast?**  
A: It outputs 5V/2A (10W max). Enough for overnight charging, but not for fast-charging a modern phone or tablet.

**Q: How tall is the lamp fully extended?**  
A: About 62 inches from floor to the top of the head. The arm itself reaches roughly 42 inches horizontally from the pole.

**The Verdict**  
**Buy it if** you want one lamp that does triple duty—bright task lighting, soft ambient glow, and a phone charger—without cluttering your desk. **Skip it if** touch controls or a slippery base will drive you nuts.

**Rating: 4.2/5** (⭐⭐⭐⭐). It’s not perfect, but for the price, it punches above its weight. The O’Bright Cedric is a workhorse that most buyers will use daily for years—just keep a rubber mat under the base if you’ve got hardwood.
