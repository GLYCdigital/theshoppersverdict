## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**Warm-up time is real.** In a heated room, these reach full brightness in about 20 seconds. In a cold garage during winter, expect 60-90 seconds of dim, dingy light before they ramp up. If you need instant-on lighting, this is a dealbreaker—go LED.

**Physical size is a constraint.** The spiral design is roughly 1.9 inches in diameter at its widest point. That won't fit many enclosed bathroom vanity globes, small ceiling fan light kits, or tight recessed can housings. Measure your fixture before ordering.

**Disposal is a hassle.** CFLs contain trace mercury. You can't just toss them in the trash in most municipalities. That's an environmental consideration you can't ignore, even though SLEEKLIGHTING does package them safely.
