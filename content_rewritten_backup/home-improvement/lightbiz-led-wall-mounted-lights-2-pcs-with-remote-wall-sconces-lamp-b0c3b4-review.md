# Lightbiz LED Wall Mounted Lights: A Decent Sconce Set With a Catch

Let's be honest about what you're getting with the Lightbiz LED Wall Mounted Lights 2-pack: a budget-friendly pair of hardwired sconces that look far more expensive than they are. At roughly $40–$50 for two fixtures with remote controls, this is squarely in "try it and see" territory. And with a 3.8-star average across 190 ratings, buyers are split — but mostly leaning positive.

## What's Actually in the Box

Two complete sconce fixtures, each measuring about 10 inches tall with a 4.5-inch backplate. You get two remotes (one per light, though they're interchangeable), mounting hardware, wire connectors, and a basic instruction sheet. The fixtures are aluminum with a matte black finish — no glass shades to break, which is a genuine plus for hallways and stairwells.

Installation is straightforward for anyone comfortable with basic wiring. The fixtures require a standard junction box, and the included wire nuts are decent quality. Expect 20–30 minutes per light if you know what you're doing. If you don't, budget for an electrician — this is hardwired, not plug-in.

## Performance and Real-World Use

Here's where the Lightbiz set earns its keep. Each fixture outputs 600 lumens — that's genuinely enough to light a hallway or reading nook, not just create ambiance. The remote gives you three color temperatures (3000K warm, 4000K neutral, 6000K daylight) and stepless dimming from 10% to 100%. That's real versatility for the price.

The remote is IR-based, which is the biggest functional limitation. One reviewer noted: "The remote only works when pointed directly at the light. If you mount these behind a chair or around a corner, forget it." That's accurate. IR requires line-of-sight, so plan your placement accordingly.

The timer function (30/60/90 minutes) works as advertised, and the lights do dim smoothly without visible flicker at any level. Build quality is acceptable — the aluminum housing feels solid, and the finish resists fingerprints.

## What Buyers Praise

The most common positive thread in reviews is the look-to-price ratio. One buyer wrote: "These look like $150 fixtures. My guest bathroom remodel came in under budget and these are the star." Another noted: "The warm light setting is perfect for our bedroom — no harsh glare, just soft illumination."

The remote dimming gets consistent praise, especially from people who wanted adjustable lighting without installing a wall dimmer switch. Multiple reviewers confirm the dimming range is smooth and doesn't buzz at low levels.

## Where It Falls Short

The honest negatives matter here. The IR remote limitation is the biggest complaint — roughly 15% of critical reviews mention it. If you're mounting these in an open hallway, fine. If they're going in a recessed shelf or behind any obstruction, the remote becomes useless and you're stuck with the wall switch only.

There are also scattered reports of quality control issues. A few buyers mention flickering after a few weeks, typically traced to loose internal connections. One reviewer stated: "The left light started strobing after three weeks. Tightening the wire nuts inside fixed it, but I shouldn't have to do that."

The lack of power-outage memory is a minor annoyance. After any power loss, both lights reset to warm white at full brightness. If you prefer daylight temperature, you'll be fiddling with the remote every time the power blips.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want modern, hardwired sconces with remote dimming and color control without spending $100+ per fixture. Perfect for hallways, bedrooms, stairwells, or any open area where line-of-sight to the remote isn't an issue.

**Skip it if:** You need the remote to work around corners or through walls, or if you're installing in recessed alcoves. Also skip if you're not comfortable doing basic electrical work — this isn't a plug-and-play product.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these be used with a standard dimmer switch instead of the remote?**
A: No. The fixtures are not TRIAC-dimmable. Use the included remote only, or you'll get flickering and potential damage.

**Q: What's the actual color temperature range?**
A: Three presets: 3000K (warm), 4000K (neutral), 6000K (daylight). Dimming is stepless from 10% to 100% within each preset.

**Q: Are these suitable for damp locations like a bathroom?**
A: They're rated for dry locations only. Avoid direct steam exposure in showers or right above bathtubs.
