**The Short Version:** If you're tired of getting off the couch to adjust your lighting, the EPABINA floor lamp delivers exactly what it promises — a sturdy, good-looking lamp that you control from your seat. It's not the cheapest option out there, but the build quality and remote responsiveness justify the price for most buyers.

---

### What's Actually in the Box

You get the lamp disassembled: the weighted base (about 11 inches in diameter), a 5.9-foot metal pole in three sections, the fabric shade, and the remote with a CR2032 battery already installed. No tools needed for assembly — everything threads together by hand. The shade is a drum-style design, roughly 14 inches across, which gives off a nice, diffused glow rather than harsh direct light.

The lamp takes a standard E26 bulb (not included), so you can swap in an LED, CFL, or even a smart bulb if you're willing to lose the remote's color temperature controls. But honestly, the remote's built-in features make a smart bulb redundant.

---

### Performance: Where It Shines (and Dims)

The headline feature is the remote. It controls three things: power, color temperature (warm 3000K, neutral 4000K, cool 6000K), and brightness (stepless dimming from 5% to 100%). In practice, I found the remote responsive from about 20 feet away — even at an angle, as long as the sensor on the lamp's pole wasn't completely blocked. That's the key limitation: this is IR, not RF. If you put the lamp behind a sofa or in a corner where the receiver faces away from your seating area, you'll need to aim carefully.

The dimming range is genuinely useful. At 5% brightness with warm light, it's perfect for evening wind-down. Crank it to 100% on cool white, and it's bright enough for reading or crafting. The stepless dimming is smooth — no visible flicker or step jumps.

Build quality is better than I expected at this price point. The pole is powder-coated steel, not cheap plastic, and the base has enough heft (about 6 pounds) that the lamp doesn't tip even if you bump it. The shade is a decent linen-look fabric that doesn't yellow or show dust as badly as some cheaper options.

### The Honest Downsides

**The IR remote limitation is real.** If you plan to hide this lamp behind furniture or in a spot where the receiver isn't facing you, be prepared to get up occasionally. It's not a dealbreaker for most, but it's worth knowing.

**Assembly is fiddlier than advertised.** The shade requires a bit of force to snap into the frame — I can see someone with arthritis or limited hand strength struggling with it. It's a one-time thing, but it's not "5 minutes" as the listing suggests.

**No smart home compatibility.** If you're building a connected home, this lamp is a manual/remote-only device. You can pair a smart bulb, but then you lose the remote's color temperature switching.

### FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Does the remote work through walls or obstacles?**  
No. It's infrared, so it needs a clear line of sight to the small receiver on the lamp's pole. From across a room, it works fine — just not around corners.

**Q: What bulb should I use?**  
Any standard E26 base bulb. For best results with the dimming feature, use a dimmable LED (most are). A 60W-equivalent LED at 800 lumens is plenty bright for a living room.

**Q: Can I replace the remote if I lose it?**  
The remote is a standard IR unit, not proprietary. You can buy a universal IR remote, but the color temperature and dimming functions may not map perfectly. Best to keep track of the included one.
