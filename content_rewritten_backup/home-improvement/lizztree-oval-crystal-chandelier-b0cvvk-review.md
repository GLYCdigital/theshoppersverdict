# LIZZTREE Oval Crystal Chandelier Review: Does This Budget Crystal Fixture Actually Deliver?

Most chandeliers under $300 make a compromise: either the crystals are sparse plastic, or the frame looks like it came from a discount warehouse. The LIZZTREE Oval Crystal Chandelier sits in an interesting middle ground — it's a genuine K9 crystal fixture with enough visual weight to anchor a formal dining room, but it comes with real tradeoffs you should know before hitting "add to cart."

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the chandelier frame, roughly 200+ individual crystal strands, a mounting bracket, and canopy. **Bulbs are not included** — you'll need six E12 base candelabra bulbs. The fixture measures 23.6 inches wide by 11.8 inches high for the standard version, with a larger 31.5-inch option for bigger tables. The drop length is adjustable via included chain, giving you roughly 12 to 40 inches of hang room depending on ceiling height.

The crystals themselves are the star here. They're K9 glass (the same optical-grade crystal used in higher-end fixtures), and each strand is individually hung — no pre-assembled panels to cheat the density. As one reviewer put it: *"I was honestly shocked at how full it looks. Every single strand is there, nothing sparse."*

## Build Quality and Real-World Installation

Let's be direct: **assembly will test your patience.** Hanging each crystal strand one by one takes most buyers 2–3 hours. Several reviews mention this explicitly — one called it *"the most tedious assembly process I've ever done with lighting."* The good news is that the frame itself is solid steel with a welded seam that won't separate under the weight of the crystals. The finish is a brushed gold-brass tone, not a mirror-polished chrome-gold. If you're matching existing polished gold hardware, order samples first.

Wiring is straightforward — standard black/white/ground connections. But the included mounting bracket is basic. If you have a sloped or vaulted ceiling, you'll likely need to source your own angled adapter. Multiple reviewers noted the hardware feels "budget" compared to the fixture itself.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

- **Light output:** With six bulbs, this thing throws serious light. One reviewer said: *"It completely transformed my dining room — brighter than I expected, and the crystals scatter light beautifully."*
- **Presence:** The oval shape is the differentiator. It visually extends along the table rather than bunching up in a round blob.
- **Value:** At typical pricing under $250, buyers consistently say it looks "like a $600 fixture."

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond assembly time, the crystals arrive in plastic sleeves that produce **significant static** — they'll attract dust immediately. Plan to wipe each strand before hanging. Also, the chain link adjustment requires pliers to open and close, which isn't mentioned in the instructions. And while the fixture is dimmable, it depends entirely on the bulbs you choose — cheap non-dimmable LEDs will defeat the purpose.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Formal dining room owners** with rectangular tables (6+ feet) who want a statement piece without spending $800+
- **DIY-ers who enjoy assembly projects** — this is not a 30-minute install
- **Anyone with a standard flat ceiling** (sloped ceilings complicate installation significantly)
- **Buyers who prefer warm, ambient lighting** and will pair it with dimmable LED filament bulbs

## FAQ

**Q: Is this chandelier compatible with smart bulbs?**
A: Yes, any standard E12 candelabra smart bulb will work. Just ensure your wall switch stays on and control via app or voice assistant.

**Q: Can I shorten the chain without cutting it?**
A: Yes — open the chain links with pliers, remove excess links, and reattach. No cutting required. Allow 2–3 feet of chain for typical 8–9 foot ceilings.

**Q: How do I clean it without damaging the crystals?**
A: Use a microfiber cloth with a slightly dampened second pass. Avoid glass cleaners with ammonia — they can dull the K9 finish over time.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want real crystal density and an oval shape that flatters a rectangular dining table, and you're willing to invest 2–3 hours in assembly for a fixture that looks triple its price.

**Skip it if:** You expect plug-and-play installation, have a sloped ceiling, or want a polished gold finish — this is brushed brass-gold, and assembly is genuinely labor-intensive.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The LIZZTREE Oval Crystal Chandelier earns its stars through visual impact and material quality, but docks points for tedious assembly and minimal hardware. For the price, it's the best-looking crystal oval under $300 we've tested.
