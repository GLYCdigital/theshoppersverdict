## Performance: Melting Wax the Right Way

The core job here is candle warming, and these bulbs deliver. The 2800K color temperature sits firmly in the "candlelight adjacent" range — it's warmer than the 3000K you'd find in most residential LEDs, and that extra warmth matters. The heat output is consistent and even across the top of the candle, liquefying the wax pool without creating hot spots that burn the wick or darken the wax. Users consistently report this in reviews, with one buyer noting, "I've used these for three months on my Scentsy warmer and the scent throw is actually stronger than with the bulb that came with it."

The dimming is where these bulbs shine — literally. They work with most standard leading-edge and trailing-edge dimmers, and the range is genuinely useful. No hum, no stutter, just smooth control.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and these have two honest flaws. First, the 2800K temperature is *noticeably* more yellow than standard warm white. If you're using these in track lighting for general illumination rather than just candle warming, the color might feel dated or overly amber. Second, dimmer compatibility isn't universal. A handful of reviewers with older rotary dimmers reported a slight buzz at mid-range settings. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you have a dimmer that's over a decade old, budget for a replacement.
