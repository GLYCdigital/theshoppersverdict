# TROND Multi Plug Outlet Extender: The Desk-Saver That Earns Its 4.7 Stars

Most outlet extenders are cheap plastic afterthoughts that sag under the weight of a single laptop charger. The TROND Multi Plug Outlet Extender is not that. This compact cube solves the most frustrating problem in any home office: you have four devices that need power but only one wall socket. After two weeks of daily use, here's the honest breakdown.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the extender itself — a matte white ABS plastic block measuring roughly 2.5 inches square — plus a basic instruction card. No mounting hardware, no screws, no adhesive strip. It's designed to sit flush against the wall or sit on your desk. The front face hosts two USB-C ports (one rated at 20W, one at 12W) and a single USB-A port. The bottom edge has two standard AC outlets, and the side has three more. Total capacity: five grounded outlets plus three USB ports.

## Real-World Performance

The standout feature is the 45mm outlet spacing. I plugged in three chunky laptop adapters — a 65W Dell brick, a 96W MacBook charger, and a Nintendo Switch power supply — and every single one fit without blocking its neighbor. That's rare at this price point. The 20W USB-C port charged my iPhone 15 Pro from 20% to 65% in about 40 minutes, which is genuinely fast. The USB-A port is slower but fine for earbuds or a Kindle.

The extender is rated for 125V/15A (1875W max), which covers virtually all home office gear. The internal surge protection is basic — no joule rating is printed on the unit, so don't treat this as serious protection for a $2,000 workstation. It's a power distributor, not a full surge protector.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The 4.7-star average across 235 ratings isn't accidental. One verified buyer wrote: *"Finally an extender where my big charger bricks don't cover the other outlets. This thing is solid, no wobble."* Another noted: *"The USB-C port is the reason I bought this. My phone charges fast, and the build quality feels premium, not cheap."* The consensus is clear: people buy this specifically for the wide spacing and the fast USB-C, and both deliver.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has two real drawbacks. First, there's no power switch. If you want to cut standby power to your monitor or speakers, you're pulling plugs manually. Second, the shared USB power budget means plugging in two devices at once drops charging speed noticeably. One buyer put it plainly: *"Wish it had a switch, but for the price I'm not complaining."*

Also note the 15A rating. This is a home office device, not a workshop tool. Don't run a space heater or a circular saw through it.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you're a remote worker with a laptop, monitor, phone, and desk lamp fighting over one wall outlet. It's also perfect for nightstands, kitchen counters, or dorm rooms where outlet access is tight. The compact footprint and wall-hugging design mean it won't topple or sag.

**Skip it if** you need a full surge protector with a joule rating for expensive electronics, or if you want individual switches per outlet. Also skip if you're powering 240V appliances — this is strictly a North American 120V device.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I mount this to a wall or desk?**
A: No. It sits on a surface or plugs directly into a wall outlet. There are no screw holes or adhesive backing. Some users report it stays put well enough on a desk, but it's not designed for permanent mounting.

**Q: Will my large laptop charger block the other outlets?**
A: No. The 45mm spacing between outlets is the whole point. Even oversized chargers leave adjacent sockets accessible. This is the most common praise in the reviews.

**Q: Is the USB-C port fast enough for a laptop?**
A: No. The max output is 20W, which is fine for phones and tablets but won't charge a MacBook or Windows ultrabook. Use the AC outlets for laptops and USB-C for smaller devices.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a compact, well-spaced outlet extender for home office gear and want fast USB-C charging without a bulky power strip.

**Skip it if** you need surge protection with a published joule rating, individual switches, or 240V compatibility.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The TROND Multi Plug Outlet Extender nails the essentials: smart spacing, fast USB-C, and a sturdy build that doesn't sag. The missing switch and shared USB budget keep it from a perfect score, but for the typical home office setup, it's the best $20-ish you'll spend this year.
