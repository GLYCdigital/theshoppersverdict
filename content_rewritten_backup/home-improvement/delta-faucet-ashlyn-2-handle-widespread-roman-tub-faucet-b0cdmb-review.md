Let’s be blunt about what you’re looking at here. The Delta Ashlyn is a **widespread Roman tub faucet** — that’s the classic three-hole configuration with a centered spout and two lever handles sitting on the rim of your tub. It’s a style that says “traditional bathroom” without screaming “dated,” and at 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it’s clearly doing something right. But before you hand over your card, there are a few things about this faucet that the product photos don’t tell you.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the spout, two handle assemblies, and mounting hardware. That’s it. As shown in the product image, it’s a clean chrome finish (also available in brushed nickel and oil-rubbed bronze). The absence of a hand shower is worth repeating — many buyers assume a Roman tub faucet includes one, and it doesn’t. You’re buying a standalone tub filler.

The **widespread fitting** accommodates 8 to 16-inch centers, which covers most standard tub decks. If you’re remodeling and haven’t drilled holes yet, this flexibility is a genuine advantage.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Delta uses **solid brass construction** here, not the zinc alloy you’ll find in cheaper alternatives. Pick it up and you feel the weight difference immediately. The lever handles move with a smooth, damped action — no cheap plastic internals, no wobble.

The standout feature is Delta’s **Diamond Seal technology**. Instead of a rubber washer that degrades over time, the valve uses a diamond-coated ceramic disc that’s rated for a million cycles. In practical terms, that means you’re unlikely to ever need to replace the cartridge. One buyer noted, *“I’ve installed hundreds of faucets and this is the smoothest handle action I’ve felt in this price range.”* That sentiment tracks with the overall rating — buyers consistently praise the build quality and finish durability.

Flow rate is a solid **7 gallons per minute** — enough to fill a soaking tub in a reasonable time, though not the thunderous fill you’d get from a dedicated freestanding tub filler.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

- **Finish durability**: Multiple reviewers mention the chrome doesn’t spot or tarnish easily, even in hard-water areas.
- **Handle ergonomics**: The levers are easy to grip, even with soapy hands. One reviewer with arthritis specifically noted this as a deciding factor.
- **Installation clarity**: Delta’s instructions are genuinely better than most, with clear diagrams that make the three-hole mounting straightforward for a competent DIYer.

## Where It Falls Short

The **pop-up drain is not included**. That’s roughly $50-100 extra depending on finish, and it’s a common complaint in the reviews — buyers expect a complete kit at this price point. Factor that into your total cost.

Installation is **not a beginner project**. You need access behind the tub wall to connect the supply lines, and if your tub is already in place, that could mean cutting an access panel or hiring a plumber. Several reviewers mentioned this as the single biggest hurdle.

The **spout reach is modest** — about 6 inches from center. If you have a wide tub, the water stream lands closer to the back edge than you might prefer. Check your tub depth before committing.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Traditional bathroom renovations** where the three-hole setup fits your existing deck
- **Buyers who prioritize longevity** — Diamond Seal valves genuinely last
- **DIYers with moderate plumbing experience** who can handle supply line connections

## Skip It If

- You want a hand shower included
- Your tub is already installed without rear access
- You prefer a modern, minimal single-handle design

## FAQ

**Q: Does this fit a 3-hole tub deck?**
Yes, the widespread design accommodates 8-16 inch centers, which covers virtually all standard 3-hole configurations.

**Q: Is the drain included?**
No. Delta sells the pop-up drain assembly separately. Budget $50-100 for a matching finish.

**Q: Can I install this myself?**
If you have access behind the tub wall and basic plumbing tools, yes. Without rear access, you’ll need a professional.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a solid brass, traditional Roman tub faucet that will outlast cheaper alternatives and you’re prepared for the extra cost of the drain and potential installation complexity.

**Skip it if** you need a hand shower, lack wall access for installation, or expect a complete package at the listed price.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The Delta Ashlyn earns its stars through genuine build quality and Delta’s reliable warranty support (lifetime limited, and they actually honor it). The missing drain and hand shower keep it from a perfect score, but for the core function — filling your tub with style and reliability — it’s hard to beat at this price point.
