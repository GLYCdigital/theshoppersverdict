If you've ever wired a traditional recessed can, you know the drill: housing, separate trim, bulb, then balancing the spring clips while lying on your back. TORCHSTAR's 3-inch gimbal recessed light kills that whole song-and-dance — each unit has the LED module, gimbal, and junction box fused into one 3-inch package. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it's not just convenient; it's genuinely well-reviewed. Here's the real breakdown.

## What's actually in the box

You get 12 complete fixtures. Each one includes:

- A 3-inch gimbal trim with integrated 9W LED (650 lumens, 3000K warm white)
- A sealed junction box with 6-inch leads and push-in wire connectors
- 12 removable trim rings (some batches include frosted vs. clear — check the listing photos)
- No separate driver, no bulb, no can — everything pre-assembled

The junction box is the headline feature. It's UL-listed for direct ceiling contact (IC-rated), so you can bury it in insulation without fire concerns. The push-in connectors accept 14-gauge and 12-gauge Romex — no wire nuts needed. As shown in the product image, the box has knockouts on multiple sides, so you can feed from any direction.

## Real-world performance

I tested one in a hallway and one in a kitchen accent wall. The 650-lumen output at 9W is honest — it's noticeably brighter than the average 4-inch retrofit that claims 600 lumens but delivers 500. The 3000K color is a warm, neutral white (not yellow, not sterile). The 90+ CRI makes a real difference: wood grain, fabric, and paint colors look like they do in daylight.

The gimbal is the selling point. It rotates 40 degrees side-to-side and tilts 90 degrees forward. That's not marketing math — I aimed one at a picture frame on a wall directly below the ceiling, and the beam hit it square without any glare in the eyes. The beam angle is tight (about 40°), so it works as an accent light, not a floodlight. For general room lighting, you'll want these spaced 3–4 feet apart, not 6 feet.

Installation took me 8 minutes per fixture — and I'm not a pro. Cut a 3-inch hole, pull the Romex through, wire the push-connectors, then push the whole unit up until the spring clips bite. The integrated driver is in the junction box, so there's no separate transformer to hide.

## What buyers consistently praise

The most common thread in reviews is the convenience factor. One buyer wrote: *"Installed 24 of these in a basement remodel. No cans, no trims, no fuss — just cut holes and wire. Saved me a full day."* Another noted: *"The brightness surprised me. I expected these tiny 3-inchers to be dim, but they light up my laundry room completely."* Multiple reviewers specifically called out the uniform light output — no hot spots or flicker.

## Where it falls short

The fixed 3000K is the biggest gotcha. If your home has 2700K or 4000K fixtures elsewhere, these will clash. There's no switchable CCT, so plan accordingly. Also, the standard SKU is non-dimmable. If you want dimming, you have to find the separate dimmable version — don't assume this pack includes it. Finally, the 3-inch size means you need more units per room than a 4-inch or 6-inch can. For a 10x12 bedroom, you'll likely need 6–8 of these for even coverage.

## Who should buy this

- **DIY homeowners** replacing old, ugly cans — the integrated junction box removes the scariest part of electrical work.
- **Basement or garage remodelers** who want clean, low-profile lighting without buying 12 separate housings.
- **Accent lighters** aiming at shelves, art, or countertops — the gimbal is genuinely useful, not a gimmick.

Skip it if you need a single 6-inch flood for a large open room, or if your existing wiring uses outdated connectors — you'll have to swap the leads yourself.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these be used in insulation contact?**  
A: Yes, the junction box is IC-rated for direct contact with insulation — no clearance gaps needed.

**Q: What's the minimum hole size required?**  
A: You need a 3-inch diameter hole. Use a 3-inch hole saw; a 2.5-inch will be too tight.

**Q: Are they compatible with standard dimmers?**  
A: Only if you specifically order the dimmable SKU. This standard pack is not dimmable — check the product page carefully.
