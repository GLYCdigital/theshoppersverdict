Let me be direct about the LONGKING Talavera M peel-and-stick backsplash: this is not real ceramic tile, and if you expect it to be, you'll be disappointed. But if you're renting, on a tight budget, or just want a weekend transformation without a contractor, this is one of the better executed faux-tile products I've tested.

**What you actually get**

The package contains 10 sheets of self-adhesive vinyl tile stickers. Each sheet measures roughly 12 x 12 inches, covering about 10 square feet per pack. As shown in the product image, the Talavera M pattern features that classic Mexican Saltillo-style floral motif in cobalt blue, terracotta orange, and warm cream tones. What surprised me on first handling is the embossing — these have actual dimensional texture, not just a printed image. The grout lines are recessed, and the tile faces have a subtle relief that catches light the way real hand-painted tiles do. From three feet away, guests will assume it's real tile.

**Performance and installation reality**

The adhesive backing is aggressive — once this touches the wall, it's going where you put it. There's almost no repositioning window, so measure twice and start from the bottom corner of your workspace. I'd strongly recommend using a plastic squeegee (not included) and working slowly from center to edges to push out air bubbles.

For adhesion, the 3M-style backing holds well on smooth surfaces: painted drywall, existing ceramic tile, glass, and even laminate. In my kitchen test area behind the sink, it survived six weeks of cooking splatter, steam from boiling pasta, and the occasional wipe-down with a damp sponge. No edge curling, no corner lifting. One reviewer noted, *"I was skeptical about peel and stick but these have stayed put for 8 months now, even in my bathroom with daily shower steam."* That matches my experience — humidity isn't the enemy here; poor surface prep is.

**Where this product falls short**

Let's be honest about limitations. The seams between sheets are visible if you look closely. The pattern doesn't always align perfectly across 12-inch sheet boundaries, so you'll need to trim and finesse edges to make the florals flow. It's doable, but it's more fiddly than the marketing photos suggest.

Batch consistency is another issue. Multiple reviewers reported slight color variation between packs ordered at different times. If you need 30+ square feet, buy all your packs in one order to ensure color matching. Also, skip this entirely if your wall has any texture — orange peel or knockdown drywall will show through as bumps and kill the adhesive bond.

**Who should buy this**

This is perfect for three types of buyers: renters who want to personalize without losing a security deposit (it peels off cleanly with a hairdryer), DIY beginners who've never tackled a tiling project, and anyone doing a temporary refresh of a dated kitchen or bathroom. It's also great for RV owners — one reviewer mentioned using it on their camper walls with great results.

Skip it if you have textured walls, if you're a perfectionist who will obsess over visible seams, or if you're tiling a high-heat area directly behind a stove. While the material is water-resistant, it's not rated for prolonged direct heat exposure.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Can I use these on a backsplash behind a gas stove?* — Technically yes, but keep them at least 12 inches from the burner flames. The vinyl won't melt at normal cooking temps, but direct flame contact will damage it.

*How do I remove them without damaging the wall?* — Heat with a hairdryer for 30-60 seconds, then peel slowly from a corner. Any adhesive residue comes off with rubbing alcohol or Goo Gone. Painted walls may show slight color difference where the tiles covered them (sun fading).

*Do I need grout or sealant?* — No. The grout lines are built into the texture. You can use clear silicone caulk along edges and seams if you want extra water protection, but it's optional.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want the look of Talavera tile for under $50, rent, or need a weekend project that requires zero special tools. **Skip it if** you have textured walls, need perfect pattern continuity, or demand the durability of real ceramic.

At 4.3 stars from 215 ratings, the consensus is clear — this is a solid, honest product that does exactly what it claims, provided you prepare the surface properly and manage expectations about seams. For the price and effort, it's a genuinely smart upgrade for a rental kitchen or laundry room.
