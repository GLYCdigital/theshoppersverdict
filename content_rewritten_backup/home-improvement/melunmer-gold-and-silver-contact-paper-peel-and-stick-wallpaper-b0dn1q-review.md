Let me be blunt: most metallic contact papers look like a 1990s wrapping paper explosion. The MelunMer Gold and Silver Peel and Stick Wallpaper is not that. As shown in the product image, this stuff has a brushed, dimensional sheen that catches light like actual metal sheeting. At 4.4 stars from 220 ratings, it's earned a spot in the upper tier of peel-and-stick products — but it's not without quirks you need to know about before checkout.

## What You Actually Get

The roll measures 17.7 inches wide by 118 inches long (roughly 14.5 square feet per roll). That's enough to cover a standard bathroom vanity door or a single kitchen cabinet face, but plan on buying two rolls for a full cabinet bank. The backing paper has printed grid lines every inch — a small detail that saves you from digging out a ruler for every cut. The foil layer is 0.12mm thick, which is noticeably sturdier than dollar-store contact paper.

## Real-World Performance

Installing this stuff requires patience but not skill. The grid lines help, and the thickness means it doesn't crumple into a sticky ball the moment you peel the backing. You'll want a plastic squeegee and a hair dryer for corners — the metallic layer holds a crease once heat-set, which is a nice touch for wrapping edges.

The finish is the star. It genuinely mimics brushed gold or silver depending on which side you bought. The color is consistent across the roll, no banding or streaks. That's rare in this price tier.

But here's the tradeoff: glossy metallic surfaces are fingerprint magnets. If you're applying this to a high-touch area like a kitchen backsplash or cabinet pulls, you'll see every smudge. One buyer noted: *"Looks stunning for about three days, then my kids' hands turned it into a crime scene."* A clear matte topcoat solves it, but that's an extra step and cost.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 220 ratings, three themes dominate:

- **Ease of application** — the thickness and grid lines get frequent shout-outs. One reviewer said: *"I've used five different contact papers this year. This is the first one I didn't want to throw across the room."*
- **Visual quality** — people repeatedly use words like "expensive," "high-end," and "doesn't look like a sticker."
- **Adhesion strength** — it sticks firmly to laminate, wood, and even lightly textured walls without bubbling.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond fingerprints, there are two honest downsides.

First, the adhesive is *strong* — almost too strong. Removal is not the clean peel-and-stick promise. If you apply this to painted drywall and later want it off, expect to repaint. One reviewer reported: *"Tried to remove it after a redesign and it took the paint with it. Should've used a heat gun from the start."*

Second, the metallic surface scratches. Not deeply, but fine surface scratches show up on the silver version especially. A soft cloth and gentle cleaner only, no abrasive pads.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're refreshing furniture, cabinet fronts, or shelves and want a metallic look without paying for real metal laminate. You're comfortable with a one-way commitment — this is a "set it and forget it" product, not a temporary rental decoration.

**Skip it if:** You rent and need something removable, or you're covering a high-traffic area where fingerprints will drive you insane. Also skip if you're covering anything larger than a small cabinet — the 17.7-inch width means seams on bigger surfaces, and seams are visible on metallic finishes.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this go on textured walls?**
A: Light texture, yes. Heavy orange-peel or knockdown texture, no — the adhesive won't bridge the gaps and you'll get air pockets.

**Q: Does it need a topcoat to prevent scratches?**
A: Not for light use, but a water-based polyurethane or clear matte sealer helps if it's on a frequently touched surface. Test on a scrap piece first — some topcoats can yellow or dull the metallic finish.

**Q: How many rolls do I need for a standard kitchen cabinet door?**
A: One roll covers roughly one standard 30-inch-wide upper cabinet door. Measure your surface area and add 10% for waste and seam matching.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a legit metallic upgrade for furniture or cabinets and don't plan on removing it anytime soon. **Skip it if** you need something temporary or you're covering a high-touch surface where every fingerprint will mock you.
