If you own a Yamiry YR01 or YR05 smart lock with handle, you already know the frustration: Bluetooth works great when you're standing at the door, but the moment you're across town, you're locked out of your own lock's features. That's exactly the gap the Yamiry WiFi Gateway fills. This small white hub ($25–35 depending on current pricing) bridges your lock's Bluetooth signal to your home network, unlocking remote access via the Yamiry Home app. At 4.3 stars across 215 ratings, it's clearly doing something right — but it's not without quirks.

## What's Actually in the Box

The package is refreshingly minimal: the gateway hub itself, a USB power cable, a wall adapter, and a quick-start guide. No mounting hardware, no screws — this is a "plug it in and forget it" device. The hub measures about 2.5 inches square and weighs next to nothing, so it won't bulge awkwardly from an outlet. As shown in the product image, there's a single status LED on the front that flashes amber during pairing and glows solid green when connected.

## Real-World Performance

Setup is genuinely painless. You plug the gateway into an outlet within roughly 30 feet of your smart lock, open the Yamiry Home app, and scan the QR code on the device's underside. Most users report being fully operational in under five minutes. One reviewer noted, *"I'm not tech-savvy and had it working before my coffee finished brewing."*

The gateway's real value shows up in daily use. Remote lock/unlock commands typically execute within 2–3 seconds, and you get push notifications whenever someone uses the keypad, fob, or app. The range is solid for a typical home — the Bluetooth-to-Wi-Fi bridge handles distances up to about 50 feet through standard drywall, which covers most single-story layouts.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Three themes dominate positive reviews. First, reliability: *"Haven't had a single dropped connection in two months of daily use."* Second, the app integration — the Yamiry Home app is surprisingly polished, with activity logs, temporary access codes for guests, and a clear lock/unlock toggle. Third, the peace of mind: *"Now I can check if I actually locked the door from my office. Worth every penny."*

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest complaint across reviews is Wi-Fi dependency. The gateway only supports 2.4GHz networks — if your router broadcasts both bands under one SSID, you may need to split them in your router settings. Several buyers reported losing connectivity after firmware updates or router changes, requiring a re-pair. Another recurring gripe: no battery backup. If your power goes out, the gateway goes dead, and you're back to Bluetooth-only until the lights come back on.

There's also the compatibility ceiling. This is a single-purpose device for Yamiry's YR01/YR05 locks only. It won't talk to Alexa, Google Home, or SmartThings, and there's no Matter support. If you're building a broader smart home ecosystem, this gateway is a dead end — it's strictly a Yamiry-to-Yamiry bridge.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Rental property owners or Airbnb hosts** who manage multiple Yamiry locks and need remote code management for guests — this is a near-essential add-on.
- **Homeowners with the YR01/YR05** who frequently second-guess whether they locked the door and want push-notification confirmation.
- **Anyone who's already invested in the Yamiry app** and wants to avoid replacing their lock with a pricier native-Wi-Fi model.

Skip it if you only ever unlock your door from inside arm's reach, or if your home network is unstable enough that you can't trust a Wi-Fi-dependent device for access control.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this gateway work with multiple Yamiry locks at once?**
A: Yes, a single gateway can pair with up to 10 Yamiry locks within range, though each lock needs to be added individually in the app. This makes it cost-effective for multi-door setups.

**Q: Will it work with my dual-band router?**
A: Only if the 2.4GHz network is accessible. Many dual-band routers broadcast both bands under one name, which can cause pairing issues. You may need to create a separate 2.4GHz SSID in your router settings before pairing.

**Q: What happens to remote access if the gateway loses power?**
A: Remote features go offline completely. The lock itself still functions via keypad, fob, physical key, and Bluetooth from your phone within range — but you lose app-based control from anywhere else.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you own a Yamiry YR01/YR05 and want remote access, guest codes, or lock-status notifications without upgrading your entire lock. It's an inexpensive, effective bridge that does exactly what it promises.

**Skip it if** you're expecting a universal smart-home hub, need offline reliability, or have a network that's prone to outages. For the price, the Yamiry WiFi Gateway is a solid 4.2/5 — a practical add-on with clear limitations, but not deal-breakers for its intended audience.
