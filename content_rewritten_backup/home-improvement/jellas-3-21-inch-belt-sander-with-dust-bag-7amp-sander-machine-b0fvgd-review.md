**Is the Jellas 3×21 belt sander a budget-friendly win or a compromise you'll regret? Let's break it down.**

Most budget belt sanders in this class make you choose between power and control. The Jellas 3×21 tries to give you both with a variable speed dial and a 7-amp motor. At 4.4 stars from 220 buyers, it's clearly doing something right — but it's not without tradeoffs. Here's the honest picture.

**What's in the box**

You get the sander itself, one 3×21-inch belt (80-grit), a dust bag, and a manual. That's it. No extra belts, no carrying case. The sander weighs about 6.5 pounds, which is middle-of-the-road for this class — heavy enough to feel solid, light enough for overhead work on cabinets. The 6-foot power cord is adequate for most garage workshops but you'll likely want an extension cord.

**Performance and build quality**

The standout feature here is the variable speed dial with six positions. Most sanders in this price range run at one speed — full blast. The Jellas lets you drop down to around 1,200 SFPM (surface feet per minute) for softer woods or finishing passes, and crank up to roughly 1,600 SFPM for aggressive stock removal. This matters more than you'd think. One reviewer noted, *"I was skeptical about the speed control but it saved me from burning through a pine tabletop — slowed it down and it finished beautifully."*

The 7-amp motor delivers enough torque for edge jointing and flattening boards, but it's not a powerhouse. Under heavy pressure on hardwoods, it'll slow down. That's actually the built-in safety mechanism — you have to let the sander do the work. Keep that in mind if you're planning to hog off material from oak or maple.

The dust collection system is genuinely better than average for this price point. The bag captures most of the fine dust, and the 1.5-inch port connects to a standard shop vac hose, which cuts airborne dust dramatically. Speaking of dust, the bag is easy to empty — just unzip and dump.

Belt changes take about 10 seconds thanks to the quick-release lever. No tools needed, no alignment issues. That's a small thing that becomes huge when you're switching grits mid-project.

**Where it falls short**

The motor is the main limitation. While fine for furniture, trim work, and general DIY, it's not going to keep up with a professional who sands all day. Also, the tool comes with only one belt — you'll want to order a variety pack of grits immediately, because that 80-grit won't last long.

The dust bag, while effective, fills up quickly on larger projects. Plan to empty it often. And there's no soft-start feature, so the sander kicks to full speed when you pull the trigger — a minor jolt you'll get used to.

**Who should buy this**

If you're a DIYer tackling furniture refinishing, deck sanding, or small woodworking projects, this is a solid pick. The variable speed is a genuine upgrade over anything in its price range. It's also a good choice for hobbyists who want one tool that handles both rough stock removal and finer finish work without switching machines.

Skip it if you're a professional or semi-pro who sands daily. You'll want the extra horsepower and durability of a DeWalt or Bosch.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this sander work with any 3×21-inch belt?**
Yes. It uses standard 3×21 belts, so you can use any brand — Diablo, Norton, whatever you prefer. That's a big plus versus proprietary systems.

**Q: How loud is it?**
It's a belt sander — it's loud. But the variable speed means you can run it slower for less noise. Expect around 90 decibels at full speed. Wear hearing protection.

**Q: Does the dust bag actually work?**
Reasonably well. It captures maybe 70-80% of dust. For the rest, connect a shop vac to the port and you'll get near zero dust in the air.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a versatile 3×21 sander with variable speed control for less than comparable big-name models. It's ideal for furniture refinishing, edge work, and general home projects.

**Skip it if:** You need serious torque for heavy stock removal on hardwoods daily, or you want a carrying case included.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The Jellas 3×21 belt sander delivers strong value with its variable speed dial and effective dust collection. It's not a pro-grade tool, but for the price, it punches above its weight class.
