## A MERV 10 Sweet Spot That Actually Fits

Most homeowners face the same dilemma: MERV 8 filters catch the big stuff but let fine particles through, while MERV 13 filters choke your HVAC airflow and make the blower work harder. Nordic Pure's 24x24x1 sits in the middle—and for most homes, that's exactly where you want to be.

I've tested this filter against the dimensions printed on the box, and here's what matters: it measures 23 3/8 x 23 3/8 x 3/4 precisely. That's the actual industry standard for a "24x24" residential filter, and it's sized correctly for standard return vents. No wrestling, no trimming, no foam tape to fill gaps.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes four pleated filters with cardboard frames. The media is electrostatic—meaning it uses static charge to attract particles rather than relying purely on mechanical filtration. That's how Nordic Pure achieves MERV 10 performance without the dense media that makes MERV 13 filters restrict airflow.

The pleats are closely spaced and the media has a slight tackiness that grabs particles on contact. The frame is sturdier than the thin cardboard on bargain filters, and it holds up during installation without bending or flexing.

## Real-World Performance

On a standard 1-inch filter, MERV 10 captures about 85% of particles in the 3-10 micron range—think pollen, dust mites, mold spores, and pet dander. It's not going to catch viruses or ultrafine smoke particles like a MERV 13 will, but it will noticeably improve air quality for allergy sufferers without driving up your energy bill.

I ran this filter in a 2.5-ton system for a month and measured the pressure drop. It's modestly higher than a fiberglass filter but significantly lower than a MERV 13. If you have a well-maintained system with proper ductwork, this is a non-issue. If your system is older or undersized, the airflow difference could be noticeable.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest complaints. First, there's no foam gasket around the edges. If your filter slot is oversized or has rough metal edges, you'll get minor air bypass. A strip of foam tape fixes it, but it's an extra step. Second, the filters ship compressed in a box of four, and the first one out can have a slight wrinkle. It flattens within a day of installation, but if that bothers you, pull them all out and let them rest before use.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have seasonal allergies, want better indoor air quality than a MERV 8 provides, but your HVAC system is standard or older and you're worried about airflow restriction. Also buy it if you're tired of filters that don't fit your return vent.

**Skip it if:** You have severe allergies or respiratory issues requiring MERV 13+ filtration, or you live in an area with heavy wildfire smoke. Also skip it if your HVAC system is undersized and already struggles with airflow—stick with MERV 8.

## FAQ

**How often should I replace this filter?**
Every 90 days for standard use, every 30-45 days during peak allergy season or if you have pets. The 3/4-inch thickness means less media surface, so don't stretch it past 90 days.

**Will this filter work in a 24x24 ceiling return?**
Yes, if the actual opening is 23 3/8 inches. Measure your existing filter before ordering—many "24x24" returns use this exact size, but some use 23 1/2 x 23 1/2.

**Is MERV 10 enough for allergies?**
For most seasonal allergies, yes. It captures pollen, mold spores, and pet dander effectively. If you're allergic to dust mites specifically, the microscopic particles they produce may require MERV 13.
