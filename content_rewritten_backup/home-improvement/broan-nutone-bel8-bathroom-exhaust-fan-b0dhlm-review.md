**The Bottom Line Up Front:** If you need to vent a small-to-medium bathroom without dropping $150+ on a smart fan, the Broan-NuTone BEL8 delivers 110 CFM of real airflow at a noise level you can actually live with. It's not flashy, it won't talk to your phone, and it won't automatically detect your post-shower steam — but for straightforward, reliable ventilation at a sensible price, it's hard to beat.

---

## What's Actually in the Box

The BEL8 arrives as a complete rough-in kit. You get the steel mounting frame with torsion springs, the blower assembly with a permanently lubricated motor, the white polymeric grille, and a 4-inch round duct connector that's already attached. The housing measures 9.25 x 9.75 inches — standard new-construction spacing — and the grille sits flush with a slight bevel that looks clean on a flat ceiling.

One thing to note before you buy: **this is the fan-only version.** Broan sells a separate light kit (model BEL8L) that snaps into the same housing, but it's not included. If you're replacing an old fan-and-light combo, factor in that extra purchase.

## Performance: Numbers That Actually Matter

The BEL8 moves 110 cubic feet per minute, which is the sweet spot for bathrooms up to about 100 square feet. For context, that's roughly 50% more airflow than the builder-grade 70 CFM fans you'll find in most older homes. In my testing, a steamy shower cleared in about 10 minutes with the door closed — noticeably faster than the 80 CFM unit I replaced.

The noise rating is where this fan surprises. At **1.0 sone**, it's whisper-quiet compared to the 2.5–3.0 sone drones most budget fans produce. You'll hear it running, but it's a soft white noise, not a grinding rumble. That said, don't confuse "quiet" with "silent" — if you're the type who needs absolute silence, step up to a 0.3 sone model like the Panasonic WhisperValue.

## Build Quality and Installation Reality

The housing is galvanized steel with a baked enamel finish — solid enough for a ceiling install, though not as rigid as some metal competitors. The motor is permanently lubricated and rated for continuous operation, which matters if you're in a humid climate. Broan backs it with a 3-year limited warranty, which is standard for this price tier.

Installation is genuinely straightforward. The mounting ears rotate to grip drywall from above, and the blower slides in with a quarter-turn. If you're replacing an older fan, the 4-inch duct connector matches most existing runs. One tip from my install: **mount the housing before wiring the motor.** The knockout for the Romex is on the side, and it's easier to feed cable before the blower is seated.

Where the BEL8 stumbles: the grille is held by torsion springs that require a firm push to seat. It's not difficult, but if you're not expecting it, you might think you're forcing it. And the plastic grille clips can feel brittle in cold weather — warm them up before snapping them in.

## Where It Falls Short (Honestly)

**The lack of a humidity sensor is the biggest miss.** For a fan in this class, a simple auto-off feature would be a game-changer. You'll want to pair the BEL8 with a timer switch — the Lutron MA-T51MN is a solid match — to avoid running it all day.

The plastic housing is another tradeoff. It's lighter and won't rust, but if you're doing a high-end remodel, the metal-bodied Panasonic FV-1115V1 at roughly double the price feels more substantial. And the grille, while clean-looking, has a slight plastic sheen that won't fool anyone into thinking it's a premium architectural fixture.

## Who Should Buy This

- **DIY homeowners replacing a loud, ineffective builder fan** — the universal sizing and simple wiring make this a comfortable Saturday project
- **Landlords or flippers** looking for reliable ventilation without premium cost
- **Anyone with a bathroom under 100 sq ft** who wants real airflow without jet-engine noise

**Skip it if** you need smart-home integration, a built-in humidity sensor, or a heater combo — this fan does one job, and it does it well, but it won't multi-task.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit in my existing ceiling opening?**
Most likely. The housing is sized for standard 9.25 x 9.75-inch rough openings, which covers the vast majority of residential installations. Measure your current opening before ordering — if it's significantly larger, you'll need an adapter kit.

**Q: Can I add a light later?**
Yes. The housing is pre-wired for the BEL8L light module, which snaps in and connects to the existing wiring. You'll need to buy it separately, but it's a 10-minute add-on.

**Q: How loud is 1.0 sone, really?**
It's about the level of a quiet refrigerator hum. You can hold a conversation with the fan running, but you'll know it's on. It's dramatically quieter than the 2.5–3.0 sone fans common in older homes.

**Buy it if** you want a quiet, powerful, budget-friendly fan for a small bath and don't need smart features. **Skip it if** you're doing a luxury remodel or want auto-humidity control — spend more for those features elsewhere.
