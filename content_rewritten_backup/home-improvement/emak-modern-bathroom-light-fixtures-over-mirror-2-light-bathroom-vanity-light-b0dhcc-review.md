If your bathroom still has that builder-grade fluorescent bar from 2009, this Emak 2-light vanity fixture is the quickest upgrade you'll make this weekend. It's not flashy, not expensive, and not complicated — and that's exactly why 235 Amazon shoppers have pushed it to a 4.7-star average. But before you hit buy, there are a few things about the bulb spacing and shade design you need to know.

## What you actually get

The box contains the fixture body, two clear glass shades, a mounting plate, wire nuts, and mounting screws. No bulbs — Emak expects you to supply your own standard E26 base bulbs. The fixture itself measures roughly 18 inches wide with shades, and the backplate is a compact 4.7 inches, which covers a standard US junction box without leaving ugly gaps. The frame is aluminum with a matte black finish, and the interior of the sockets has a subtle brass tone that shows through the glass.

## Build quality and real-world performance

For the price point, the construction is solid. The matte black finish is baked on, not painted over cheaply — no chipping or peeling reported in the reviews I combed through. The glass shades are thick enough to survive a bump from a towel bar, and the sockets are ceramic, not the flimsy plastic you sometimes get at this price.

Here's the critical thing: this fixture is **fully dimmable** as long as you use dimmable LED bulbs. That's a genuine advantage over integrated-LED fixtures that lock you into one brightness. One buyer put it well: *"I put Philips warm glow dimmable bulbs in and it's perfect for late-night bathroom trips without blinding myself."* Multiple reviewers confirm the dimming works smoothly with standard Lutron dimmers — no flicker complaints.

The light distribution is decent for a 2-light bar, but don't expect it to flood a large bathroom. With two 60-watt equivalent LEDs, you'll get comfortable task lighting over a 24–30 inch mirror. If you have a double vanity, buy two of these and wire them to separate switches — that's a common setup in the review photos and it looks sharp.

## What buyers consistently praise

The mid-century modern aesthetic is the star. The brass socket detail visible through the clear glass shades gives it a warm, designer look that punches well above its price. One reviewer summed it up: *"Looks way more expensive than it is. I've gotten compliments from every guest who's used the bathroom."*

Installation is the other big win. The mounting plate is slotted, so you can level the fixture after it's attached to the box — a small detail that saves 20 minutes of frustration. Wiring is standard black/white/ground, and the included instructions are actually legible, which is rarer than it should be.

## Where it falls short

The clear glass shades are the biggest caveat. Without frosted or seeded glass, bare LED bulbs are fully visible. If you install standard frosted bulbs, the look is fine, but the fixture's design intent is clearly to show filament-style bulbs. Budget an extra $15–25 for decorative Edison bulbs if you want the intended aesthetic.

The fixed 10-inch spacing between the two sockets means this fixture is really best for a 24–36 inch mirror. If your mirror is wider, the light won't reach the edges evenly, and there's no way to adjust it. Measure before you buy.

Also note: the fixture is rated for damp locations only, not wet. Install it over a mirror in a dry vanity area, not directly inside a shower stall.

## Who should buy this

- **DIY homeowners** replacing a dated fixture — the install is a 30-minute job with basic tools
- **Small bathroom owners** with a single 24–30 inch mirror who want a design-forward look without paying designer prices
- **Renters and apartment dwellers** who want a fixture that's easy to remove and take with them

Skip it if you have a wide double vanity, need integrated LED with no bulb replacement, or want a completely glare-free frosted look.

## FAQ

**Is this fixture compatible with smart bulbs?**
Yes. Since it uses standard E26 sockets, any smart bulb (Philips Hue, Wyze, etc.) works. Just make sure the fixture switch stays on and control via your smart app or voice assistant.

**Can I install this on a vaulted or sloped ceiling?**
It's designed for wall mounting over a mirror. For ceiling use, you'd need an angled adapter, but that's not recommended — the shades are open-topped and not meant for overhead installation.

**What's the return policy if it doesn't fit my space?**
Amazon's standard 30-day return applies. Given the 10-inch socket spacing, measure your mirror width first — the fixture is 18 inches overall, so it needs at least that much horizontal clearance.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a clean, modern vanity light at a fair price with genuine dimming flexibility and a look that doesn't scream "budget." **Skip it if** you need adjustable spacing, want integrated LEDs, or can't live with seeing your bulbs through clear glass.
