**Bottom Line: A $40.52 three-piece essentials kit that outperforms its price point — but has one notable gap.**

Most plier sets under $50 are disposable junk. This Craftsman set isn't. The 8-inch long nose and 8-inch slip joint pliers arrive in a clamshell package with the third piece being a 6-inch long nose — all three featuring the familiar red-and-black grips that have anchored American toolboxes for generations. The forged alloy steel construction is evident the moment you heft them; these aren't stamped sheet metal pretending to be tools.

**What's actually in the box:** Three pliers, nothing more. No pouch, no carrying case, no spare springs. The two 8-inch models handle the heavy lifting while the 6-inch long nose covers precision work. The slip joint offers two position settings, and the long nose variants feature serrated jaws that bite into rounded or stubborn fasteners without slipping.

**Real-world performance:** I spent a weekend re-pinning a trailer hitch and rewiring a shed light fixture. The long nose pliers reached into tight engine bays where my other sets failed, and the cutting edges on the slip joint cleanly snipped 14-gauge wire without crushing it. The induction-hardened edges held up — no nicks after dozens of cuts. The grips deserve specific praise: the cushioning is substantial enough to matter during repetitive twisting, which is rare at this price point.

Buyers consistently highlight durability. One reviewer noted, *"I've owned these for eight months of daily use in HVAC work and they still close perfectly flush."* Another mentioned, *"The spring action is strong — they snap open every time without wearing out."* That spring tension is a common failure point on budget pliers, so it's worth calling out.

**Where it falls short:** The slip joint lacks a quick-release mechanism. Adjusting between positions requires two hands and a bit of fiddling — annoying when you're holding a workpiece. The long nose pliers also skip the wire cutter that most competitors include at this price. If you're doing electrical work, you'll need a separate tool. And while the $40.52 price is fair, it's not a bargain-bin steal; you're paying for the Craftsman name and warranty.

**Who should buy this:** Homeowners assembling furniture, DIYers doing general repairs, and hobbyists who need reliable pliers without investing in a 10-piece kit they'll never fully use. Also ideal for a starter toolbox or as a gift for someone beginning their first apartment. Skip it if you're a professional electrician needing integrated cutters, or if you prefer quick-adjust pliers for fast-paced work.

**FAQ**

*Are these made in the USA?* No — they're Craftsman-branded but manufactured overseas. The warranty is the real selling point, not the country of origin.

*Will the grips slip off with oil or sweat?* No. The grips are molded directly onto the handles, not slipped over. They'll wear down eventually but won't slide off mid-task.

*Is the warranty actually hassle-free?* Yes. Craftsman's lifetime warranty covers these with no receipt required in most cases. If a pair breaks, you walk into a Lowe's or contact support and get a replacement.

**The Verdict:** Buy it if you want three dependable pliers that will outlast a decade of weekend projects and come with a genuine lifetime warranty. Skip it if you need integrated wire cutters or demand one-handed adjustability — those tools exist, but they'll cost more than this entire set.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A solid, honest value that earns its keep. Just know its limitations before you buy.
