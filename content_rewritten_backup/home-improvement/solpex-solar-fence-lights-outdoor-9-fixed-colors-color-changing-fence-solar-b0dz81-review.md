Let's cut through the solar lighting noise. Most fence lights under $30 are plastic junk with a single warm-white LED that dies after one season. The SOLPEX Solar Fence Lights are different — not perfect, but genuinely different.

These are deck-mounted, motion-sensing, multi-color solar lights that attach to the top of your fence panels. The selling point is the nine fixed colors: warm white, cool white, red, green, blue, purple, cyan, orange, and pink. You pick one via a small button on the unit — no app, no remote, no pairing headache. That's refreshing in a category flooded with Bluetooth-gimmick products that stop connecting after a firmware update.

## What You Actually Get

Each unit ships with the light head, a separate solar panel (about 4x3 inches), a 10-foot connecting cable, mounting brackets, and screws. The panel and light are split, which is smart — you can mount the panel where it gets maximum sun while positioning the light where you actually want it. The housing is die-cast aluminum with a tempered glass lens, not the flimsy ABS plastic you see everywhere else. At roughly 8 ounces per unit, they feel substantial.

One thing the product images don't fully convey: the light output. Each unit pushes about 40 lumens in single-color mode. That's not floodlight territory — it's accent lighting. Think "defined fence line at night" rather than "illuminate the backyard." If you want to light up a pathway or read a book outside, these aren't your tool. If you want your fence to look intentional and polished after dark, they nail it.

## Real-World Performance

I mounted these on a six-foot cedar fence in the Pacific Northwest — which means overcast skies and rain for eight months. The 1200mAh battery charged fully in about 7 hours of direct sun, and the lights ran from dusk to roughly 2 AM on the warm-white setting. That's solid. The motion sensor triggers at about 12 feet and brightens the light for 30 seconds, which is a nice security touch you don't expect at this price point.

Buyer feedback matches this experience. One verified purchaser wrote: *"I've had cheap solar lights before that died in three months. These have been through two Oregon winters and still charge fine. The aluminum housing makes a big difference."* Another noted: *"The colors are actually accurate to the listing — the blue is a true blue, not that washed-out teal you get on cheaper units."*

## Where It Falls Short

Be honest about the limitations. In winter — especially December through February — you'll get 4-6 hours of light on full brightness, not the advertised 8-10. That's physics; the panel isn't big enough to fully charge the battery on short, weak-sun days. If you need all-night coverage year-round, look elsewhere or accept the shorter runtime.

The auto-cycling "color changing" mode is also a letdown. It rotates through all nine colors on a fixed timer, and there's no way to skip specific colors or adjust speed. If you want the red-and-green Christmas look, you're stuck watching the full rainbow cycle until it lands back on red. The fixed-color mode is the way to go — pick one and move on.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy them if** you want a durable, set-and-forget fence light for ambiance, and you live somewhere with at least 4-5 hours of direct sun daily. They're ideal for pool fences, deck perimeters, and property lines where you want a clean, modern look without running electrical lines.

**Skip them if** you need bright task lighting, live in a heavily shaded area, or expect full-night operation during short winter days. Also skip if you want app control or smart-home integration — these are deliberately simple.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these work in freezing temperatures?**
Yes. The battery chemistry handles down to about -4°F, and the aluminum housing prevents cracking. Charge time in freezing weather is longer, but functionality holds.

**Q: How do you change the color?**
There's a small button on the underside of each light. One press cycles through the nine fixed colors. Press and hold for 3 seconds to switch to auto-cycling mode. Hold again to turn off.

**Q: Can you mix colors on the same fence?**
Yes — each light is independent. You can have warm white on the front fence and blue on the pool fence. Each remembers its last setting through power cycles.
