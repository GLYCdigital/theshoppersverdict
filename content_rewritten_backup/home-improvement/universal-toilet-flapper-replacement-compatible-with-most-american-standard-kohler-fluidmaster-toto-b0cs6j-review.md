## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my Kohler with a 3-inch flush valve?**
A: Yes, the adjustable collar expands to handle 3-inch openings. Just make sure you set the chain length so the flapper closes completely — too much slack causes a weak seal.

**Q: How do I know if the chain is the right length?**
A: Install the flapper, then check that the chain has about 1/4 inch of slack when the flapper is closed. If it's taut, the flapper won't seal; if it's draped loosely, it can catch under the flapper and cause leaks.

**Q: Is this better than the OEM flapper from my toilet brand?**
A: For most standard toilets, yes — the silicone seal is more durable than OEM rubber, and it's significantly cheaper. For unusual or very old models, stick with OEM.
