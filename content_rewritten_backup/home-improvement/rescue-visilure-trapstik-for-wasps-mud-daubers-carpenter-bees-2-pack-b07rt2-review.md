**If you've got wasps buzzing your deck or carpenter bees drilling into your fascia, you need a solution that works *now* — not a spray that kills one bug at a time.** The RESCUE! VisiLure TrapStik is a mechanical trap that doesn't mess around with poisons. It's a simple, brutal, and effective design: a bright yellow sticky panel that pests can't resist landing on, and once they're on, they're done.

## What You Actually Get

The 2-pack includes two traps, each about 8.5 inches long with a foldable dual-sided adhesive panel. There's a built-in hanger and a wire twist-tie for mounting. The bright yellow color isn't cosmetic — it's the VisiLure technology that mimics the UV-reflective properties of flowers that stinging insects naturally seek out. Each trap is pre-baited with a non-toxic scent lure that's specifically formulated to attract wasps, mud daubers, and carpenter bees.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the TrapStik earns its keep. Set it up near active flight paths — eaves, porch corners, garage door frames — and you'll see results within hours, not days. The adhesive is genuinely industrial-grade. One Amazon reviewer put it bluntly: *"Hung two of these near my back door where wasps kept building nests. Caught 15+ wasps in the first day. They don't stand a chance."*

The dual-sided design is smarter than it looks. You can fold it into an L-shape to fit right against a corner, which is exactly where wasps like to travel. The adhesive stays tacky for weeks, even in humid conditions.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The most common theme in positive reviews is the sheer catch volume. People are shocked at how many pests one small trap collects. Another recurring compliment: no chemicals. If you have kids, pets, or edible plants nearby, this is a genuinely safer alternative to spraying insecticides around your home.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. **This trap catches foraging adults — it does not eliminate nests.** If you have an active wasp nest under your eaves, you'll catch workers but the colony keeps producing. You'll need to pair this with nest removal for a complete solution.

The adhesive is a double-edged sword. It's incredibly effective on insects, but it's also merciless on anything else. One reviewer warned: *"My dog walked into one and it took 20 minutes and vegetable oil to get it off her fur. Keep these out of reach of pets."* Wear gloves when handling, and absolutely do not hang these at nose height where someone could brush against them.

Finally, they're single-use. Once the adhesive is full or loses tackiness, you toss it and buy another. For heavy infestations, that's a recurring cost through the season.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does one trap stay effective?**
A: Most users report 4-6 weeks of solid catching before the adhesive loses its tack. Direct sun and rain reduce lifespan; mounting under an eave helps.

**Q: Will this attract more wasps to my yard?**
A: The lure is potent, but it only attracts pests within about 20 feet. If you place it near a problem area, you'll catch what's already there — you won't draw new colonies from across the neighborhood.

**Q: Does it work on yellow jackets?**
A: Yes, though it's less targeted for them than for paper wasps and mud daubers. For heavy yellow jacket pressure, consider a dedicated liquid bait trap.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need immediate, chemical-free relief from wasps, mud daubers, or carpenter bees around your home. **Skip it if** you're dealing with an active nest that needs physical removal, or if you have curious pets that might investigate the sticky panels.
