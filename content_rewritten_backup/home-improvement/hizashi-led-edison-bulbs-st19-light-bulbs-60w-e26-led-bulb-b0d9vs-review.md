## What's Actually in the Box

You get the bulbs themselves, typically in a 2-pack or 4-pack depending on listing. Each is an ST19-shaped globe — that's the classic elongated Edison silhouette, measuring roughly 5.3 inches tall with a 2.4-inch diameter. The E26 medium base is standard for US fixtures, so these screw into any regular lamp, ceiling fan, or pendant socket you already own.

The construction is solid: glass is frosted, not clear, which softens the light output while still showing off the carbon filament design inside. At 9 watts actual draw, they produce 800 lumens — the equivalent of a 60-watt incandescent. That's a legitimate 85% energy reduction.

## Performance and Build Quality

Here's where these bulbs surprise. The 2700K color temperature gives that warm, honey-toned glow people chase with Edison bulbs, but without the amber tint that makes some LED retrofits look orange. It's closer to a traditional soft white — think cozy, not sepia.

Heat management is handled well. The base stays cool to the touch after hours of use, which matters if these are going in enclosed fixtures or tight spaces. Flicker-free operation is consistent across the board — no annoying 60Hz hum or strobe effect that plagues cheap LED options.

Dimming is where you need to pay attention. These bulbs are **not** dimmable. If your current setup has a dimmer switch, you'll get either full brightness or nothing. That's a genuine limitation for anyone wanting to create layered lighting scenes.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The consensus from buyers centers on the light quality and appearance. One reviewer noted, "These bulbs look exactly like the expensive Restoration Hardware ones at a fraction of the price." ## Where They Fall Short

Beyond the dimming limitation, the ST19 shape is bigger than standard A19 bulbs. If you're using these in flush-mount ceiling fixtures, the globe may stick out beyond the shade. Measure your fixture depth before committing.

Color consistency across the batch can also vary slightly — one buyer reported a "barely noticeable difference" between bulbs in the same pack. It's minor, but perfectionists pairing these side-by-side might notice.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy if:** You're outfitting exposed-bulb fixtures, pendants, or lamps where the filament design is visible. You want the Edison look with modern efficiency, and you don't need dimming.

**Skip if:** You rely on dimmer switches for mood lighting, or your fixtures have shallow shades that won't accommodate the larger globe.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these bulbs safe for enclosed fixtures?**
Yes — the low heat output and cool-running base make them suitable for enclosed glass shades, which traditional Edison bulbs couldn't handle.

**Q: Can I use these outdoors?**
