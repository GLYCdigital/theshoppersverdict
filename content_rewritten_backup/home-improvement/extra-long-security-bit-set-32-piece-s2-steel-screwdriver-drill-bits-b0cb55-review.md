If you've ever stared at a recessed tamper-proof screw and realized your 2-inch bit won’t reach, this set is the answer you’ve been looking for. The **Extra Long Security Bit Set** from D—branded simply as a 32-piece S2 steel kit—solves a very specific, very annoying problem: fasteners buried deep inside housings, door assemblies, or automotive components that standard security bits can’t touch. At 4.4 stars from 220 ratings, it’s clearly earning its keep in toolboxes. But it’s not perfect, and here’s what you need to know before you buy.

**What you actually get:** 32 bits, all with extra-long shafts (roughly 6 inches, give or take a few millimeters depending on the profile), seated in a hard plastic case with a clear lid. The set covers the essentials: Torx security (TR8 through TR40), hex with center pin, spanner heads, tri-wing, and pentalobe—the oddballs that show up on gaming consoles, bathroom fixtures, and certain European appliances. Every bit is marked with its size, which is more than some premium sets bother to do. There’s also a magnetic bit holder with a standard 1/4-inch hex shank, so it works with any drill or impact driver.

**Performance and build:** The S2 steel is the headline here. It’s a step above the CR-V (chrome vanadium) steel you find in budget sets—harder, more resistant to twisting under load. In practice, that means the Torx TR25 bit on a security screw on a door hinge won’t round out after three turns. One Amazon reviewer put it bluntly: “Used these on some seriously corroded tamper-proof screws on an old truck. The bit didn’t strip, the screw came out.” The extra length isn’t just for show—it gives you leverage on deep-set fasteners and keeps your driver clear of obstructions like trim panels.

The tradeoff: there’s no quick-change chuck on the included handle. You’re locking each bit in manually with a collar, which is fine for one-off jobs but slows you down if you’re swapping bits across a dozen screws. Also, the case hinge is the weak link. Multiple reviewers note it feels cheap—one said the latch “gave up after six months in a work van.” It’s not a dealbreaker, but don’t expect a Pelican case.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The reach is the recurring theme. “Finally, a set that reaches the screws on my dishwasher control panel,” one wrote. Another highlighted the variety: “Has every weird bit I’ve needed for appliances, electronics, and even my car’s throttle body.” The magnetic holder gets love too—it holds bits snugly enough for vertical work without dropping them.

**Where it falls short:** Beyond the case and the chuck, the set lacks a pass-through ratcheting driver option, which would make those deep fasteners even easier. And if you’re a professional doing this all day, you’ll want to buy a spare case or organize these in a socket rail—the included case won’t survive heavy daily abuse. There’s also no metric sizing chart printed inside, so you’ll want to memorize or label which bit is which.

**Who should buy this:** Homeowners tackling appliance repairs (dishwashers, washers, dryers), DIYers working on automotive interior or engine bay fasteners, and anyone who’s ever been stopped cold by a pentalobe screw on a phone or console. Skip it if you only work with standard Phillips and flathead—you’re paying for security profiles you won’t use.

**FAQ:**

*Does the 6-inch length fit in a standard drill chuck?* Yes, the holder shank is 1/4-inch hex, so it fits any drill or impact driver with a standard chuck. The bit itself is long, but the holder adds another inch or so—keep that in mind for tight clearance spots.

*Will these strip on high-torque impact drivers?* S2 steel is impact-rated for most consumer use, but back off the clutch on seized screws. Several reviewers used them on impacts without breakage, but no bit survives repeated abuse at full torque.

*Are the bits really security/tamper-proof?* Yes—the hollow-point Torx, hex pin, spanner, and pentalobe profiles are designed for the drive shapes you can’t get from a standard bit set.

**The Verdict: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if:** You need deep reach on security fasteners and want a single case that covers every oddball profile. The S2 steel and length justify the price.

**Skip it if:** You’re a pro who needs a bombproof case or you rarely touch tamper-proof screws—a shorter, cheaper kit will do.

It’s a specialized tool that does its job well, with one clear weak spot (the case). For the price, you’re getting genuine utility, not gimmick.
