If you've ever been burned by a "3-way" LED that actually just blinks or only hits two of three brightness levels, the Zorykn A19 is a pleasant surprise. This isn't a novelty bulb with a marketing label — it's a legitimately functional 3-way LED that pairs with any standard 3-way lamp socket. With 225 Amazon ratings and a 4.5-star average, it's clearly earned its spot in the conversation. Here's the real story.

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes the A19 bulb itself — one bulb per box, so check your quantities. It's a standard A19 shape, meaning it fits most table lamps, floor lamps, and pendant fixtures. The bulb offers three light output levels (roughly 50W, 100W, and 150W incandescent equivalents) and dual color temperatures: 3000K warm white and 5000K daylight. A small toggle switch on the bulb's neck lets you pick your color temperature before installation. As shown in the product image, the switch is clearly labeled, which is more than many competitors offer.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The headline feature works. When you click your 3-way lamp through its settings, the Zorykn responds with three distinct, genuinely different brightness levels. No strobing, no skipped settings, no "third click = off." It's the kind of functionality that sounds basic but is surprisingly rare in budget LED 3-ways.

Color temperature accuracy is solid. The 3000K setting has that warm, amber-heavy glow that works in living rooms and bedrooms. The 5000K is crisp and clinical without veering into harsh blue territory. The switch on the bulb's neck is decisive — you won't accidentally bump it during installation, though it does require a quick decision before you screw the bulb in. Once installed under a tight lampshade, you're not reaching it again without a dance.

Brightness at the highest setting is genuinely impressive. At 150W equivalent, this thing puts out serious light — we're talking "read a book from across the room" territory. Flicker is minimal even at lower dim settings, which matters if you're sensitive to PWM flicker in LED bulbs.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 3-way function is the star. One reviewer noted, *"Finally a 3-way LED that actually works with my lamp. The other ones I tried would only do two levels or would buzz. This one is silent and smooth."* Another praised the dual color temps: *"Being able to switch between warm and daylight without buying two different bulbs is genius for my home office."* Build quality also gets consistent nods — the base fits snugly in standard sockets without wobble, and the plastic housing doesn't feel brittle.

**Where It Falls Short**

Honest tradeoffs exist. The color temperature switch placement is the biggest one — if you're installing this in a ceiling fixture or a lamp with a narrow shade, you're committing to one color temperature until you take the bulb out. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

Heat is the second issue. At the 150W equivalent setting, the bulb runs noticeably warm to the touch after an hour. It's within safe operating parameters, but I'd avoid fully enclosed fixtures where heat can't dissipate. The manual (or lack thereof — it's a slim insert) won't tell you this, but the physics will.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **3-way lamp owners** who are tired of buying bulbs that don't actually deliver three brightness levels

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this work in a regular lamp socket?**
A: No. It needs a 3-way socket (the kind with three click settings). In a standard socket, it will light at one fixed brightness — likely the middle setting.

**Q: Can I dim it with a dimmer switch?**
A: No. This is designed for 3-way sockets only. Using it with a wall dimmer may cause flickering or buzzing.

**Q: How long does the color temperature switch take to change?**
A: It's instant — flip the switch and the next time the bulb turns on, it's the new color. No warm-up delay.
