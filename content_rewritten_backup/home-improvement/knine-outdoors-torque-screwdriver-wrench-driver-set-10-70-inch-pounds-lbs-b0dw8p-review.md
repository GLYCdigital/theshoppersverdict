**How it performs in real use**

The click mechanism is the heart of this tool, and it works as advertised. That feedback is critical when you're torquing carbon fiber bike parts or aluminum scope mounts — overtightening those is an expensive mistake. I tested it against a beam-style torque wrench I own, and it consistently clicked within a couple inch-pounds of the target. Good enough for 95% of hobbyist and enthusiast work.
