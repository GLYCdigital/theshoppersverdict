**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this with a dimmer switch?**
A: No. Dimmer switches control voltage, which can damage the adapter and whatever you plug in. Use only with standard on/off switches.

**Q: Will it work in a pendant light fixture?**
A: Usually, but check the socket orientation. If the pendant hangs down, the adapter and plug will dangle awkwardly. Better suited for ceiling or wall fixtures.

**Q: Is it safe to leave plugged in 24/7?**
A: Yes, as long as you're not exceeding the 15A rating and the socket is in good condition. It's no different than leaving a lamp plugged in.
