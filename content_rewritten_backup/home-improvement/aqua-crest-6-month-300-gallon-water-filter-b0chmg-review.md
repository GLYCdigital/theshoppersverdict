## What You Actually Get

The package contains a single replacement filter cartridge, pre-wrapped and sealed. No mounting brackets, no adapters, no instruction booklet beyond a basic insert. The filter itself measures roughly 9 inches tall with a standard twist-lock cap — the same form factor used by most major refrigerator brands including Samsung, LG, and Whirlpool. The housing is food-grade polypropylene, and the internal media is a coconut-shell carbon block, which is the good stuff — cheaper filters use granulated carbon that channels and loses effectiveness.

## Performance and Build Quality

The filter uses a dual-stage design: a sediment pre-filter catches rust and particulates, then the carbon block handles chemical reduction. The certification list is genuinely impressive for the price point — NSF/ANSI 42 and 53 certified for chlorine taste and odor, lead, mercury, VOCs, and 70+ other contaminants. The carbon block is denser than what you'll find in most OEM filters, which is why AQUA CREST can claim the 300-gallon rating.

Real-world performance holds up. Multiple users report a noticeable difference in water taste within the first glass. ## Where It Falls Short

The biggest complaint is the priming requirement. You MUST flush 3-5 gallons before drinking. Some buyers skipped this and got black carbon dust in their ice maker for a week. This isn't a defect — it's standard for carbon block filters — but the packaging doesn't make it prominent enough.

The other issue is compatibility. While it fits most standard housings, it doesn't fit every model. A handful of buyers discovered this after purchase and had to return it. Check your fridge's filter housing dimensions against the product listing before ordering — the manufacturer's compatibility list is accurate but not exhaustive.
