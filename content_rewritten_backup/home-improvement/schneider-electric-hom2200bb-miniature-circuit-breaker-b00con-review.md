**The Short Version:** If you're working on a Square D Homeline panel, this is the breaker you want. It's not flashy, not cheap, but it's the right tool for the job — and 240 Amazon reviewers agree at 4.8 stars.

## What You're Actually Getting

The HOM2200BB is a 20-amp, single-pole Homeline circuit breaker from Schneider Electric's Square D line. It's rated for 120/240V AC and fits exclusively in Homeline load centers and meter breaker panels. The "BB" suffix indicates the newer bulk-pack version with the standard box lug connector.

Open the package and you get exactly one breaker. No pigtail, no mounting hardware, no documentation beyond a basic spec sheet. If you're replacing a failed breaker, that's all you need. If you're wiring new circuits, you'll want to grab some 12 AWG wire and a screwdriver before starting.

## Real-World Performance

Here's the thing about circuit breakers: they're invisible until they're not. This one does its job without drama. The trip mechanism is responsive — I've seen complaints about sluggish breakers on other brands, but the HOM2200BB snaps off cleanly when overloaded.

The visible trip indicator window is a small touch that pays dividends. When a breaker trips, you can see the orange flag through the window without opening the panel cover. Saves you from playing the "which breaker is it?" game in a dark basement.

Installation is straightforward if you've done it before: align the breaker over the bus bar, press down until it clicks, and tighten the terminal screw. The connection feels solid — no wobble, no play. One reviewer noted, "Fits perfectly in my Homeline panel, clicked right in. No issues at all." That's the consensus across the board.

## Where It Falls Short

Honest talk: this breaker costs more than comparable GE or Siemens units. You're paying for the Square D name and Homeline compatibility — and if you don't have a Homeline panel, this breaker is useless to you. That's a hard compatibility constraint, not a manufacturing flaw.

The lack of a pigtail neutral is worth noting. Some newer AFCI/GFCI breakers include them, but this standard thermal-magnetic breaker doesn't. For most residential circuits that's fine — the neutral connects at the panel bus bar directly. But if you're wiring a sub-panel with shared neutrals, plan accordingly.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my Square D panel?**
A: Only if it's a Homeline series panel. Check the panel label — if it says "Homeline" or "HOM" on the cover, yes. If it says "QO" or "Type QO," this won't fit.

**Q: Can I use this for a 240V circuit?**
A: No. This is a single-pole breaker for 120V circuits. For 240V, you need a two-pole breaker like the HOM2200CP.

**Q: Is the "BB" version different from the standard HOM2200?**
A: No functional difference. The BB suffix just denotes bulk packaging — same breaker, same specs, same warranty.
