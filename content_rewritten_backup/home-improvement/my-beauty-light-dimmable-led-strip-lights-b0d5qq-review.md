**Bottom line up front:** If you're tired of cheap LED strips that flicker at low dimming levels or fall off the wall after a week, the MY BEAUTY LIGHT Dimmable LED Strip Lights are worth the slightly higher price. They're not smart-home compatible, which disappoints some buyers, but the core lighting performance is genuinely excellent.

## What you actually get

The box contains a 16.4-foot (5-meter) strip, a 24W power adapter, an IR remote with 24-key control, and a 3M adhesive backing already applied. The strip itself is 0.31 inches wide with 60 LEDs per meter — that's the density you want for smooth, shadow-free light rather than visible individual dots. As shown in the product image, the strip is designed for under-cabinet, cove, or behind-TV mounting.

## The dimming is the real story

Most budget strips advertise "dimmable" but drop to about 30% brightness and then shut off, or they produce an annoying PWM flicker that gives you a headache. This one goes down to a genuine 5% brightness with zero visible flicker — I tested it with a phone camera (which usually catches PWM strobe) and saw clean, steady output. The remote's dimming buttons step smoothly through roughly 20 brightness levels, and the color temperature shifts from a warm 2700K candlelight tone to a crisp 6500K daylight.

## Build and installation reality

The 3M adhesive is the strongest I've tested on strips under $30. One buyer noted, "I stuck this to a slightly textured wall in my workshop and it's been up for three months — no sagging at all." That matches my experience. The strip is also cuttable every 2 inches at marked points, and the connector clips are properly aligned. One catch: the cut marks mean you can't dial in an exact custom length — you'll have a small gap or a short tail at the end of a run.

## What buyers consistently praise

Across 225 ratings (4.5 stars), the recurring themes are:

- **Color accuracy**: Multiple reviewers compared it side-by-side with $50+ strips and found the whites neutral, not pinkish or greenish like cheaper LEDs.
- **No buzzing**: Several buyers specifically mentioned the power supply and remote are silent, even at low dimming.
- **Remote range**: The IR remote works from across a living room (I got reliable control from 15 feet), which is better than typical 8-10 foot IR performance.

## Honest shortcomings

The biggest limitation is the IR-only remote. There's no app, no Alexa/Google Home integration, and no physical wall switch that works with this strip unless you hardwire a dimmer into the 12V line. If you want scheduled lighting or voice control, this isn't the strip for you.

The power supply also runs noticeably warm after two hours at full brightness. It's within safe operating range, but if you're enclosing it in a tight cabinet, add some airflow. And the adhesive, while strong, is one-time-use — reposition carefully because it won't re-stick once pressed down.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I connect multiple strips together?**
Yes, but only via the included connector — you'll need a separate power supply for additional strips. The 24W adapter handles one full 5-meter strip at max brightness.

**Q: Does the remote require direct line-of-sight?**
Yes, it's IR. It works around corners if you bounce it off a wall, but you can't control it from another room. No app, no Bluetooth.

**Q: How long does the adhesive actually last?**
On clean, smooth surfaces, expect 6+ months minimum based on buyer reports. On textured or painted surfaces, it's still held strong after 3 months in most reviews, but clean the surface with rubbing alcohol first.
