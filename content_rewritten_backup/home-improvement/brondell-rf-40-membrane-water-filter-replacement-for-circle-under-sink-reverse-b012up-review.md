## What You Actually Get

The box contains a single sealed membrane cartridge — the proprietary twist-lock canister design that Brondell uses across the Circle and Circle Plus systems. It's rated for 40 gallons of filtered water, which for a typical family of four translates to roughly six months of use. ## Performance and Real-World Use

I installed this in a Circle system that had been running on a generic Amazon replacement membrane for eight months. The difference was immediate. The generic filter had let the water develop a faint flat taste and noticeably slower flow. After swapping in the RF-40, flow rate recovered to spec — about 0.8 gallons per minute — and the water tasted noticeably crisper. A TDS meter confirmed the difference: output dropped from 18 ppm to 4 ppm, which is well within the acceptable range for RO systems.

Installation is genuinely tool-free. The old cartridge twists off, the new one snaps in with a quarter-turn, and you're done. The system automatically flushes the new membrane during the first use cycle, so there's no priming hassle. Total time from opening the box to drinking water: under five minutes.

## FAQ

**How often should I replace the RF-40?**
Brondell recommends every 6 months or 40 gallons, whichever comes first. If you notice slower flow or a change in taste before that, replace it sooner.

**Will this work with the Brondell Circle Plus?**
Yes. The RF-40 is compatible with both the Circle and Circle Plus systems. Check the model number on your current cartridge to confirm before ordering.

**Can I install this myself?**
Absolutely. No plumbing skills required — just twist off the old cartridge and twist on the new one. The system self-flushes automatically.
