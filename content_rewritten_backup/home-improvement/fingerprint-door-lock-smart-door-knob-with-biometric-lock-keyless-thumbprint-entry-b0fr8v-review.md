**The Short Version:** If you want keyless entry without turning your door into a smart-home project, this fingerprint knob delivers. It’s not flashy, it doesn’t connect to your phone, and that’s exactly why some shoppers will love it. But it’s not perfect, and there are a few quirks worth knowing before you buy.

---

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the fingerprint door knob assembly, a keyed override (because yes, it still comes with two physical keys), mounting hardware, and a user manual that’s actually readable. No Wi-Fi bridge, no hub, no app to download. As shown in the product image, the unit is compact — the exterior knob houses the fingerprint sensor in the center, and the interior side has a simple thumb-turn lock. It’s designed for standard residential doors with 2-3/8\" or 2-3/4\" backsets, and it fits both left- and right-hinged doors with a quick reversal of the latch.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The zinc alloy body gives it a reassuring heft. This isn’t one of those hollow-feeling locks that makes you wonder if a strong push would break it. The fingerprint sensor is responsive — most buyers report unlocking in under a second. One reviewer noted, *“I programmed three fingers and it reads them every time, even in the dark.”* That’s the experience for the majority: set it up, scan your thumb, and you’re in.

You can store up to 20 fingerprints, which covers a family plus a couple of trusted neighbors. The lock also accepts a 4-8 digit PIN via the keypad, which is a solid backup when your hands are full of groceries or your thumb is wet from rain. The keyed cylinder is there for emergencies, but it’s a standard pin-tumbler — don’t expect high-security features.

**Where It Falls Short**

The biggest limitation is the lack of connectivity. There’s no app, no remote access, no activity log. If you want to know who came and went, or if you want to unlock the door from your phone, this isn’t the product for you. That’s a deliberate design choice — it keeps the price down and the setup simple — but it’s worth stating plainly.

Wet or dirty fingers can cause false rejects. A few reviewers mentioned this specifically: *“After gardening, my thumb wouldn’t read no matter how I positioned it.”* The keypad saves you in that situation, but it’s an extra step. Battery life sits around 4-6 months depending on usage. The low-battery warning beeps when you open the door, but if you’re not paying attention, you could find yourself at the door with a dead lock and a key in your pocket. Keep the physical keys somewhere accessible.

**Who Should Buy This**

This lock is ideal for:

- **Renters** — it replaces the existing knob without drilling new holes, and you can swap it back before moving out.
- **Busy parents** — no more digging for keys while holding a toddler and a backpack.
- **Anyone who wants simple keyless entry** without dealing with Wi-Fi, subscriptions, or app updates.

Skip it if you need remote access, if you live in a climate with heavy rain and often have wet hands, or if you want a single smart system that ties into your existing home automation.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this lock on a metal door?**
A: Yes, but you may need to use the included longer screws and ensure the door frame is reinforced. Metal doors work fine, though the installation is slightly more involved. If your door is pre-drilled for a standard knob, it should fit.

**Q: How many fingerprints can I store?**
A: Up to 20. That’s enough for a family of four with multiple fingers each, plus a few extras. You can delete individual fingerprints without wiping the whole list.

**Q: What happens if the battery dies?**
A: The lock has a physical key override — two keys come in the box. There’s also a low-battery beep warning when you unlock the door. If you ignore that for two weeks, you’ll be using the key. The battery compartment is on the interior side and takes 4 AA batteries.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want reliable fingerprint entry at a fair price and don’t need app connectivity. The build quality is solid, the sensor is fast, and installation is genuinely easy.

**Skip it if:** You need remote locking/unlocking, real-time notifications, or you frequently deal with wet hands. This is a standalone lock, and that’s both its strength and its limitation.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A dependable, no-frills biometric lock that does exactly what it promises. Just don’t expect it to be more than that.
