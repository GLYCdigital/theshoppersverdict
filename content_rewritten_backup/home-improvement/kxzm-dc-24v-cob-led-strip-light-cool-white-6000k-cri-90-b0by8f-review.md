If you’ve ever squinted at a dark workbench trying to trace a wire or match a paint chip, you already know why the KXZM DC 24V COB LED Strip Light deserves a hard look. This isn’t the cheap 12V tape you find in the bargain bin — it’s a serious task-light solution hiding in a spool.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the 16.4-foot (5-meter) strip on a reel, plus basic mounting tape pre-applied. No power supply, no controller, no connectors. That’s either a dealbreaker or a blessing, depending on your wiring comfort. If you’re already running 24V shop lighting or have a spare Mean Well driver, this is ideal. If you expected a plug-and-play kit, budget another $15–25 for a matching 24V driver.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

The headline spec here is 450 LEDs per meter. That’s not marketing math — it means the COB (chip-on-board) architecture produces a continuous, dot-free light line. As shown in the product images, the density is immediately visible; there’s no perceptible spacing between emitters, even at six inches away. For under-cabinet shop lighting or inside tool chests, this is the difference between usable and premium.

At 6000K with a CRI of 90+, colors render accurately. I tested this against a 5000K standard strip side-by-side, and the KXZM’s output looked cleaner, with less yellow shift at the edges. The 24V design pulls roughly half the current of a comparable 12V strip, which means you can run longer lengths without the far end dimming — a real advantage for a 16-foot garage or workshop perimeter.

Heat is worth noting. COB strips run cooler than SMD equivalents at the same brightness, but this still gets warm after 30 minutes of continuous use. Aluminum channel is not optional for permanent installs; the adhesive will soften over time on bare wood or drywall.

**Where It Falls Short**

And the 2.36-inch cut intervals mean you can’t always trim to an exact fit without wasting segments.

**Who Should Buy This**

Skip it if you want an all-in-one kit with remote dimming and plug-and-play installation — this isn’t that product.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use a 12V power supply with this strip?**  
No. It’s 24V only. Using 12V will result in dramatically reduced brightness; using 24V on a 12V-rated strip will damage it. Match the driver to the strip.

**Q: How do I cut and reconnect this strip?**  
Cut only at the marked copper pads every 2.36 inches. Use solder or clip-on connectors rated for 24V. Do not overlap cut ends.

**Q: Is this safe for outdoor use?**  
No. It’s not IP-rated for moisture. Keep it indoors or inside a sealed aluminum channel with a diffuser cover.
