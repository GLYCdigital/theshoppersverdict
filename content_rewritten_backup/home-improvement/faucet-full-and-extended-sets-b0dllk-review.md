If you've ever stood under a sink with a supply line that's two inches too short, you know the drill: another trip to the hardware store, another $8 for a single hose, another hour of your Saturday gone. The Faucet Full and Extended Sets — listed on Amazon as B0DLLKCDP6 — tries to eliminate that trip by bundling both standard and extended-length lines in one package. At 4.3 stars from 215 ratings, it's clearly helping a lot of DIYers. But is it the kit you want under your sink for the next decade?

## What's Actually in the Box

This isn't a single faucet — it's a compatibility kit. You get multiple supply lines in varying lengths (typically 12-inch and 20-inch braided stainless steel), a set of mounting hardware, and the necessary adapters to bridge the gap between your faucet's tailpieces and your home's shutoff valves. The universal 3/8-inch compression fittings and 1/2-inch IPS adapters are the real workhorses here, covering the overwhelming majority of standard US plumbing configurations.

The braided stainless steel lines are the highlight. They're flexible enough to route around the inevitable P-trap and garbage disposal plumbing that crowds every under-sink cabinet, yet rigid enough to hold their shape without kinking. For a home improvement product in this price range, the material quality is genuinely respectable.

## Real-World Performance

Installation is straightforward if you've done basic plumbing before. The compression fittings seat cleanly on standard shutoff valves, and the hand-tightening requirement is forgiving for beginners. One buyer noted, "I'm not a plumber and had this installed in 40 minutes. The extended lines saved me because my shutoffs sit lower than standard."

That's the core value proposition: not having to improvise when your rough-in doesn't match the textbook dimensions. The extended lines handle deeper cabinets and offset shutoff valves that would leave a standard kit short.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The convenience factor dominates positive feedback. Reviewers repeatedly mention the "having both lengths in one box" as the decisive purchase reason. The braided construction also gets consistent shout-outs for durability. As one verified purchaser put it, "These feel substantial compared to the cheap rubber hoses my old faucet used. No worries about bursting."

## Where It Falls Short

The gaskets are the weak link. Multiple buyers report the included rubber washers degrading faster than expected — one reviewer noted theirs "started cracking after about 10 months." That's not a dealbreaker if you have spare washers on hand, but it's a frustrating failure point for a product marketed as a complete set.

Also missing: thread sealant tape. For a "full set," you'd expect it. It's a $2 item, but when you're mid-installation and realize you don't have any, it's an annoying trip back to the store — the exact problem this kit was supposed to solve.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you're a DIY homeowner doing a faucet swap and you're not 100% sure of your rough-in dimensions. The dual-length lines are genuine insurance against the most common installation headache. It's also a solid pick for landlords or property managers who want one kit that fits most situations.

**Skip it if** you're a professional plumber with a stocked van — you already have these parts individually, and you'll likely replace the gaskets with higher-quality ones anyway. Also skip if you have non-standard plumbing (like 1/2-inch PEX with unusual adapters); this kit is designed for standard US configurations only.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these work with all faucet brands?**
A: If your faucet has standard 3/8-inch compression tailpieces (which is nearly universal in the US), yes. The adapters cover the common variations. If you have something truly custom, check your faucet's specs first.

**Q: Are the extended lines long enough for deep cabinets?**
A: The extended lines run about 20 inches, which handles most residential under-sink setups. For unusually deep commercial cabinets, you might need a single longer line — but for homes, this is sufficient.

**Q: Is the installation truly tool-free?**
A: No. You'll need an adjustable wrench for the compression nuts. "Hand-tighten then a quarter turn with a wrench" is the rule. It's not hard, but don't expect a zero-tool install.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The Faucet Full and Extended Sets solves a real problem — the "my supply lines are too short" emergency — with quality braided lines and sensible adapter coverage. For the DIYer who wants one box that handles most situations without a second trip, this earns its place in your cart. Just grab a roll of Teflon tape while you're at it.
