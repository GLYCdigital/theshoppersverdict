**The lock that disappears into your door — but does it work as well as it looks?**

Most smart locks scream "I'm a gadget" from across the street. Bulky keypads, glowing rings, visible screws. The Level Lock+ takes the opposite approach: it hides *everything* inside your deadbolt. From the outside, it looks like a normal, high-end lockset. That's the magic — and the challenge.

With 204 ratings averaging 4.1 stars, the Level Lock+ has a solid but not flawless track record. Here's what you need to know before dropping serious cash on this thing.

---

**What's in the box**

You get the complete interior and exterior escutcheons, the deadbolt assembly (which houses the motor, battery, and electronics), a Matter-over-Thread module, mounting hardware, and a deadbolt key. Notably absent: any kind of keypad. If you want to punch in a code, you'll need to buy the separate Level Keypad or rely entirely on your phone.

The entire smart mechanism — motor, Thread radio, and the included CR2 battery — lives inside the deadbolt cylinder itself. As shown in the product images, the interior escutcheon is barely thicker than a standard lock plate. It's a genuinely impressive piece of industrial design.

---

**Performance and build quality**

The lock's party trick is Apple Home Key. Tap your iPhone or Apple Watch near the lock, and it unlocks — no app, no fingerprint, no code. It works even if your phone's battery is dead, thanks to the power reserve in Apple's wallet. That's a level of convenience you don't get with most competitors.

Matter-over-Thread means it plays nicely across Apple Home, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa. In practice, setup is smooth *if* you have a Thread border router (HomePod mini, Apple TV 4K, or newer Echo devices). Without one, you'll be stuck with Bluetooth-only, which is sluggish and range-limited.

Build quality is excellent. The metal components feel substantial, and the finishes — satin nickel and matte black — hold up well against scratching. The motor is quiet, and the deadbolt throws smoothly once properly aligned.

---

**What buyers consistently praise**

The invisible design is the runaway favorite. One reviewer wrote, *"My guests have no idea it's a smart lock until I tell them. It looks like a normal high-end deadbolt."* Another noted, *"Home Key is flawless — I just tap my watch and the door opens. No fumbling with apps."* The Matter integration also gets love from multi-ecosystem households.

---

**Where it falls short**

Installation is the biggest complaint. The lock demands near-perfect deadbolt alignment — if your door frame is slightly off, the bolt binds and the motor struggles. Several reviewers report spending over an hour on installation, and some needed to file down their strike plate. This is not a five-minute swap.

Battery changes are another sore spot. You must remove the entire interior chassis, pop out the old CR2, and slide in a new one. It's not hard, but it's far more involved than the slide-out battery tray on a Schlage or August lock. Expect to do this every 6–8 months with normal use.

And there's no keypad. If you want code entry for guests or contractors, that's another $80+ for the Level Keypad. At this price point, that omission stings.

---

**Who should buy this**

- **Apple ecosystem loyalists** who want the seamless Home Key experience
- **Design-conscious homeowners** who refuse to mount a bulky gadget on their door
- **Renters or condo owners** with standard doors who want something that looks like a normal lockset

**Skip it if** you're on a budget, don't have a Thread border router, or want keypad access without buying accessories.

---

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it work with HomeKit Secure Video or automations?**
Yes — via Matter, it's fully exposed to Apple Home, so you can build automations like "unlock when arrival is detected." No native camera, obviously, but it integrates with existing HomeKit cameras.

**Q: Can guests unlock it without an iPhone?**
They can if you share access through Apple Home, but they'll need an iPhone or Apple Watch for Home Key. Android users get app-based control only, and it's clunkier. Guests without either will need a physical key.

**Q: How does it compare to the August Wi-Fi Smart Lock?**
The August is easier to install and has a lower price, but it's bulkier and lacks Home Key. The Level wins on design and Apple integration; August wins on price and compatibility with older doors.

---

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're deep in the Apple ecosystem, value aesthetics above all, and have a standard door you're willing to fiddle with during install.

**Skip it if** you want plug-and-play setup, keypad entry, or a budget-friendly option.

The Level Lock+ is the best-looking smart lock on the market, and for Apple users, it's genuinely delightful. But it demands patience during installation and a premium budget. If you can live with those tradeoffs, it's a 4.2-star product that will make your door the envy of the neighborhood — while looking like it's doing absolutely nothing at all.
