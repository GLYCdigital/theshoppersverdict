The Woods L1707SVBLK doesn't try to be a design centerpiece. It's a workhorse outdoor light built for decades of service, and that's exactly why it holds a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings. If you need dependable illumination over a garage door, back porch, or workshop entrance without paying boutique lighting prices, this is the fixture to beat.

## What's in the Box

You get the complete wall-mount fixture in hammered black, a mounting plate with pre-attached screws, wire nuts, and a rubber gasket for sealing against the wall surface. The glass shade is a clear, seedy style that throws light efficiently rather than diffusing it into a dim glow. You'll need to supply your own bulb — a standard 100W A19 or an equivalent LED — and a 4-inch round junction box if your home isn't already wired for one.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The housing is die-cast aluminum, not stamped sheet metal. That distinction matters outdoors. Stamped fixtures flex, collect moisture at the seams, and eventually corrode. The Woods unit feels solid in hand, and the hammered texture isn't just cosmetic — it helps shed water and hides mineral deposits from hard-water sprinklers.

The weather seal is genuinely effective. Buyers consistently mention leaving it up through coastal storms and freezing winters:

> "Installed this on the beach side of our house three years ago. Still looks brand new — no rust, no pitting, no fading."

Another reviewer notes: "The gasket and sealed backplate keep bugs and moisture out. No spider webs inside the glass after a full season."

Light output is excellent with a standard 100W bulb. The clear glass and open-bottom design cast a wide, even pool of light that covers a typical 8-foot porch or walkway without harsh glare. If you want to cut energy use, an LED A19 drops in with zero modification and produces the same brightness at a fraction of the wattage.

## Where It Falls Short

The mounting requirement is the most common complaint. This fixture needs a 4-inch round junction box. If your home was wired with 3-inch octagon boxes — common in pre-1960s construction — you'll need to swap the box or use an adapter plate. That's a manageable DIY task, but worth knowing before you order.

The finish, while durable, isn't scratch-proof. The hammered texture does a good job hiding minor marks, but heavy impacts will leave visible dings in the aluminum. Also, the fixture has no dusk-to-dawn sensor or motion detection. It's a straightforward light with a switch — no smart features, no automation. If you want that, you'll need to add a compatible bulb or switch separately.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Homeowners replacing a worn-out porch light** — this is a direct, no-fuss swap if your junction box is 4 inches.
- **Anyone in coastal or high-humidity regions** — the aluminum body and sealed backplate are built for corrosive environments.
- **DIYers who want a fixture that won't need replacing in five years** — at this price point, the build quality punches above its class.

Skip it if you need a motion-sensing security light or if you're outfitting a modern interior space where aesthetics matter more than durability.

## FAQ

**Can I use an LED bulb in this fixture?**
Yes. It's rated for 100W incandescent, but standard A19 LED bulbs work fine and produce comparable light at a fraction of the energy use. Just avoid enclosed-rated bulbs if you're using the fixture with the glass fully sealed.

**Is the glass shade removable for cleaning?**
Yes, the shade lifts off after loosening the two small screws at the bottom. It takes about a minute and doesn't require any special tools.

**Will this fit over an existing round box?**
Only if the box is 4 inches in diameter. The mounting plate is designed specifically for 4-inch round junction boxes. Measure your existing box before ordering.
