*Q: Will it work on sliding glass doors?*
A: No. It’s designed for hinged doors that swing inward. For sliding doors, you need a dedicated sliding door lock or a dowel in the track.

*Q: Can I use it on a door with a lever handle instead of a knob?*
A: Yes, as long as the door has a standard latch bolt and a strike plate. The handle type is irrelevant — the lock engages the latch, not the handle.

*Q: Does it damage the door or floor?*
