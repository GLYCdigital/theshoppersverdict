Your bathroom fan starts humming like a trapped bee, then stops spinning entirely. You pull the cover off, and the motor is seized. If your fan housing has a label reading "BP28" or your fan is a Broan/NuTone model from the last 20 years, there's a strong chance this fits. The kit includes the motor, the blower wheel (squirrel cage), and the mounting bracket — everything you need except a screwdriver.

**What you actually get**

The package is refreshingly complete. **Where it falls short**

The honest downsides are worth knowing before you buy. First, the pre-wired connector pigtail is short — maybe 6 inches. If your fan housing has the junction box positioned awkwardly, you may struggle to plug it in without contorting your hand. It's workable, but not ideal.

Second, there's no real instruction manual. You get a basic exploded diagram, but if you're not comfortable with small appliance wiring, you'll want to watch a YouTube video first. The wiring itself is a simple plug-and-play connector — no wire nuts required — but knowing which screws to remove helps.

Third, this is a motor kit, not a full fan assembly. If your housing itself is cracked, rusted, or the damper is failing, this won't fix that. You need a working housing for this to solve your problem.
