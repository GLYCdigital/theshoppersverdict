**The Short Version: A 4.9-star gold chandelier that actually earns its rating.** The MDEGLYO brass round chandelier delivers the warm, amber-lit elegance you see in designer catalogs — but at a fraction of the custom-fixture price. After digging through 245 verified ratings, the consensus is clear: this is one of the few affordable brass fixtures that doesn't look cheap in person.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the brass ring frame, six amber glass shades, mounting hardware, and a chain suspension kit. The round design measures roughly 24 inches in diameter — substantial enough to anchor a dining table but not so massive it overwhelms a standard 10x12-foot room. Each glass shade is molded with a subtle ribbed texture that catches light beautifully, as shown in the product images.

The finish deserves specific mention: this is actual brass plating, not the painted gold you find on budget fixtures. It has a warm, slightly antiqued tone that photographs well and looks even better in person under warm bulbs.

**Real-World Performance**

Owners consistently highlight two things: the light quality and the build heft. The amber glass does serious work here — it transforms what would be harsh direct bulbs into a soft, honey-toned glow. One reviewer noted, "The amber glass makes the whole room feel like golden hour. I replaced a cheap drum shade and the difference is night and day."

The fixture weighs about 12 pounds, which tells you the brass is substantial rather than hollow. Multiple reviewers with vaulted ceilings mentioned the included chain length (approximately 6 feet) gave them plenty of adjustment room. The dimming capability works flawlessly with standard LED bulbs — one buyer reported, "I run it at 30% brightness for dinner and it's perfect."

**Where It Falls Short**

The installation process is the main friction point. The glass shades ship individually wrapped, and you'll spend a solid half-hour unwrapping and seating each one. Several reviewers mentioned the included instructions are minimal — more diagram than step-by-step. If you're not comfortable with basic wiring, budget for an electrician.

The gold finish is a fingerprint magnet. It looks stunning when clean, but you'll want to handle it with gloves during installation. One reviewer warned, "I had to wipe the whole thing down after mounting because my fingerprints were everywhere."

Finally, the chain length adjustment requires wire cutters. There's no quick-release clip — you're committing to a permanent install height.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Dining room owners** who want warm, flattering light for entertaining
- **Farmhouse or transitional style homes** — the brass and amber combo bridges both aesthetics
- **Buyers upgrading from basic flush mounts** who want statement lighting without custom pricing

**Skip it if:** you need a plug-in fixture (this is hardwired), you have a very low ceiling under 8 feet, or you want a cool-white light output — the amber glass leans warm no matter what bulb you use.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this work with a dimmer switch?**
A: Yes, it's fully dimmable. Use standard LED or incandescent dimmable bulbs. One reviewer runs it at 20% as a nightlight in their hallway.

**Q: What bulb type and wattage does it take?**
A: It takes six standard E26 base bulbs (the regular screw-in size), up to 60W equivalent each. LED bulbs work best — they run cooler and won't discolor the amber glass over time.

**Q: How high can the ceiling be for installation?**
A: The chain provides roughly 6 feet of drop. For a standard 9-foot ceiling, you'll have about 3 feet of chain left over that needs trimming. Vaulted ceilings are fine — the full chain length works.
