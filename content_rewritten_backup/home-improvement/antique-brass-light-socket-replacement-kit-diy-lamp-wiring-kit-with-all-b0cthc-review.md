Let me be direct: most lamp socket kits on Amazon are flimsy plastic garbage that feels like it'll crumble the moment you tighten the shade. This antique brass kit is not that. It's a proper vintage-style replacement that feels like it belongs on a lamp your grandmother actually used — in the best way.

**What's in the box**

You get a complete keyless socket assembly (shell, cap, and interior), a two-prong polarized plug, and the necessary mounting hardware. The socket takes standard E26 bulbs, which means you're not locked into some proprietary bulb size. The brass finish is the headline here — it's an antique patina, not shiny polished brass, so it matches older fixtures rather than glaring against them. The shell is thick enough that you won't bend it while tightening, and the interior is a heat-resistant phenolic, not the thin bakelite you see on cheaper kits.

**Real-world performance**

I rewired a thrift-store floor lamp with this kit, and the whole job took under twenty minutes. The screw terminals on the socket are clearly marked, which matters more than you'd think — older sockets often have faded or missing markings that turn a simple job into a guessing game. The plug included is polarized (one blade wider than the other), which is a small safety win that tells you the manufacturer didn't cut corners.

The keyless design means you control the lamp from a wall switch or inline cord switch, not from the socket itself. That's standard for this type of kit, but worth knowing if you were expecting a pull-chain.

The reviews at 4.6 stars out of 229 ratings are telling. One buyer put it plainly: "Perfect for restoring old lamps. The brass finish matched my antique fixtures exactly." **Where it falls short**

The lacquer on the brass will scratch if you're rough with it, exposing the raw metal underneath. If you're restoring a lamp you plan to handle frequently, be gentle during installation. Also, the kit doesn't include wire strippers — a minor omission, but it forces a trip to the toolbox if you're not already equipped. The cord included is a standard lamp cord, not a heavy-duty option, so don't plan on using this for anything beyond a table or floor lamp.

**Who should buy this**

- The vintage lamp restorer who needs a socket that actually matches the era of the piece
- The DIY homeowner replacing a cracked or melted socket on a lamp they'd rather fix than replace
- Anyone tired of buying plastic sockets that crack after a year

**Skip it if** you need a pull-chain socket, want a modern sleek look, or you're wiring something with a higher wattage than a standard household lamp.

**FAQ**

**Is this socket compatible with LED bulbs?** Yes. The E26 base fits standard LED, incandescent, and CFL bulbs. The heat-resistant interior handles the lower heat output of LEDs without issue.

**Do I need any special tools for installation?** Just a flathead screwdriver and wire strippers. The screw terminals are accessible and the wiring diagram is printed on the socket interior.

**Can I use this on a ceiling fixture?** Technically yes, but it's designed for lamps. For ceiling use, you'd want a socket with a mounting strap or canopy kit, not this one.
