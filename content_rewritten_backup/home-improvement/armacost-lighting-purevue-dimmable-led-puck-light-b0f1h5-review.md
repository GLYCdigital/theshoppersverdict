I've installed a lot of under-cabinet lighting over the years, and most puck lights fall into two camps: cheapo battery-powered units that die in six months, or expensive architectural fixtures that require an electrician. The Armacost PureVue sits in a third category — a pro-grade puck light at a price that won't make you wince. Here's the honest breakdown after putting it through its paces.

**What's Actually in the Box**

You get the puck light itself — a surprisingly substantial unit despite its 3.35-inch diameter and half-inch thickness. The housing is machined aluminum with a frosted lens that hides the LED array completely. As shown in the product image, the back is flat with a keyhole slot for screw mounting plus adhesive tape for tool-free installs. What you don't get: a power supply. You'll need Armacost's 24V driver (sold separately), which is a common gotcha I'll unpack below. The integrated 6-inch lead wire has a quick-connect plug that locks into the daisy-chain cable system.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The PureVue's headline feature is genuine 0-10V dimming. That's not marketing talk — this thing dims smoothly down to 5% brightness with zero flicker. I tested it with a Lutron dimmer and a cheap Leviton, and both produced clean output across the full range. The 3000K color temperature is warm and inviting, similar to a halogen under-cabinet look but without the heat output. At full brightness, each puck pushes 350 lumens — enough to light a standard 36-inch section of countertop without shadows.

The build quality matches Armacost's reputation. The aluminum housing acts as a heatsink, so these run cool even after hours of use. The frosted lens does an excellent job diffusing light — no hot spots, no LED "dots" visible even when you're staring directly at it.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews reflects real-world satisfaction. One reviewer noted: *"These are the third set of puck lights I've tried and the first ones that don't buzz when dimmed. The difference is night and day."* Another long-term owner mentioned: *"Installed 8 of these under kitchen cabinets 18 months ago. Not a single failure, and they look as good as the day I put them in."* Multiple reviewers also praised the daisy-chain system — you wire one cable run and connect each puck in sequence, which makes installation dramatically cleaner than running individual wires.

**Where It Falls Short**

The biggest knock: the power supply is sold separately. That's an extra $30-$50 depending on which driver you choose, and if you're comparing against all-in-one kits, the PureVue doesn't look as price-competitive initially. The fixed 3000K color temperature is another limitation — if you prefer daylight or cool white, this isn't your fixture. Finally, the adhesive tape is mediocre. I'd recommend using the screws, especially in humid environments like kitchens where heat and steam can weaken the adhesive bond over time.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Kitchen remodelers** who want under-cabinet lighting that looks custom and dims properly
- **DIYers with basic wiring skills** — the daisy-chain system is genuinely plug-and-play
- **Homeowners replacing failed cheap pucks** who are tired of flicker and buzzing
- **Anyone with dark cabinets or display shelves** needing puck lights that don't throw harsh shadows

Skip it if you need a plug-and-play solution with included power supply, or if you want switchable color temperatures.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use any 24V driver with these, or does it have to be Armacost's?**
A: Any quality 24V constant-voltage driver will work, but Armacost's own drivers are matched to the dimming curve and include the right connectors. Third-party drivers may require adapter cables.

**Q: How many pucks can I daisy-chain on one driver?**
A: Up to 12 fixtures per 60W driver. Each puck draws about 5W, so calculate your total wattage — a 40W driver handles 8 pucks safely.

**Q: Are these suitable for wet locations like bathrooms?**
A: They're rated for damp locations, which covers bathroom vanities and kitchens. Don't submerge them or install in direct steam exposure like a shower.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want under-cabinet lighting that dims like a high-end fixture, looks custom, and will outlast the cabinet hardware. The separate driver cost is annoying, but the performance justifies it.

**Skip it if** you need an all-in-one budget solution, prefer cooler color temperatures, or want plug-and-play convenience without wiring.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting half a point for the separate driver requirement. Everything else about the PureVue is exactly what a discerning homeowner wants: solid construction, flawless dimming, and clean installation.
