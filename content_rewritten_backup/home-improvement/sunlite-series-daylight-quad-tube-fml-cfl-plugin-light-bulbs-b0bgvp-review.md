If you're still running fluorescent troffer lights in a garage, workshop, or commercial space, you already know the pain of hunting for the right plug-in bulb. The Sunlite Series Daylight Quad Tube FML CFL is the rare replacement that actually matches what your fixture expects — no adapters, no wrestling with mismatched pins. With a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. But is it the right call for your setup? Let's dig in.

## What You're Actually Getting

This is a 4-pin GX24q-4 base quad-tube CFL rated at 26 watts, pushing 2850 lumens in a 6500K daylight color temperature. That's roughly equivalent to a 100-watt incandescent in brightness but with a crisp, blue-white cast that makes fine detail work easier. The four-tube configuration is designed to maximize light output from a compact footprint — important when your fixture housing is tight.

The box contains just the bulb itself. No ballast, no starter, no instructions worth reading. If you're replacing an existing quad-tube, it's a literal one-for-one swap.

## Real-World Performance

In a standard 2x2 troffer, these bulbs produce an immediate, even light with no visible flicker once warmed up. The 6500K temperature is the standout feature here — it's genuinely daylight, not the sickly greenish tint some budget CFLs throw. For anyone doing color-critical work, that's worth the price alone.

The warm-up period is the tradeoff. You'll get maybe 60% brightness instantly, but full output takes a solid two to three minutes. In spaces where lights are constantly switched on and off, that gets old fast. Leave them on for longer stretches and they're fine.

One buyer noted: *"Replaced four bulbs in our shop fixture. Brighter than the old 4100K ones by a noticeable margin. No humming, no flicker after warm-up."* That matches the consensus across the 225 ratings — the daylight color temperature is the most-cited reason for purchase.

## Where It Falls Short

The warm-up lag is the biggest practical issue. Motion-sensor fixtures will cycle these bulbs frequently, which also shortens their lifespan despite the 13,000-hour rating. You'll also want to avoid enclosed fixtures — the quad-tube design traps heat, and these run noticeably warm even in open housings.

Mercury content is worth mentioning plainly. If one breaks, you're dealing with a hazardous cleanup, not just sweeping up glass. And these are not dimmable under any circumstances — don't try.

One reviewer flagged the pin alignment on their first unit: *"The pins were slightly bent on arrival. Straightened them with pliers and it worked, but worth checking before you install."* It's a minor QC issue, but worth knowing.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you're maintaining troffer fixtures in a garage, warehouse, workshop, or under-cabinet task lighting where daylight color temperature improves visibility. It's also a solid choice if you're tired of hunting for GX24q-4 replacements — these are widely stocked and consistently in stock.

**Skip it if** your space relies on motion sensors, you need instant full brightness, or you're dealing with fully enclosed fixtures. LED retrofit kits are a better long-term investment if you're replacing more than a few bulbs.

## FAQ

**Will these work in my existing fixture?**
Check your fixture's base type. If it's GX24q-4, yes. If it's GX24q-1, 2, or 3, no — those require different wattage bulbs. The pins are not interchangeable between the q variants.

**Are these worth switching to from LED?**
If your fixture still has a working ballast, yes — it's a $10 fix versus a $50+ retrofit kit. But if you're starting fresh or your ballast is failing, LEDs are more efficient and instant-on.

**How long do they actually last?**
Rated at 13,000 hours, but real-world life depends on cycle frequency. On a typical 8-hour daily schedule, expect 3–4 years. Frequent switching will cut that significantly.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need reliable, bright, daylight-temperature light in a GX24q-4 fixture and don't mind the warm-up lag. **Skip it if** you're in a motion-sensor setup or want instant-on performance — that's LED territory. At 4.6/5, this is a solid, honest performer that does exactly what it promises. Just check your base type before ordering.
