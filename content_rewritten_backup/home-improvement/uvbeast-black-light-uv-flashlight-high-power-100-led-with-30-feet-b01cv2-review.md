## FAQ

**Q: Does this detect bed bugs?**
A: No. Bed bugs don't reliably fluoresce. This light is for finding their fecal spots (tiny dark smears) and shed skins — which do show up. It's a detection aid, not a magic wand.

**Q: Will it damage my eyes?**
A: 365nm UV-A is relatively safe in short bursts, but don't stare into the beam or shine it directly in anyone's eyes. If you're using it for hours, UV-blocking glasses are cheap insurance.

**Q: Can I recharge it?**
A: The light itself isn't rechargeable, but any rechargeable AAA batteries (NiMH) will work fine. The LED array draws about 1.5 watts, so standard 900mAh rechargeables give roughly an hour per pair.
