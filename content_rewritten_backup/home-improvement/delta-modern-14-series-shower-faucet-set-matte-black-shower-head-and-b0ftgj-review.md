# Delta Modern 14 Series Shower Faucet Set, Matte Black: The $262 Question

Let’s skip the pleasantries. At $262, the Delta Modern 14 Series isn't a budget buy — it's a mid-tier upgrade that promises to make your bathroom look like you actually thought about it. The real question is whether it performs as good as it photographs.

As shown in the product image, this is a complete trim kit: an 8-inch round shower head, a handheld sprayer, a 60-inch hose, a diverter knob, and the pressure-balancing valve trim plate. It's designed to pair with Delta's R10000 rough-in valve, which is sold separately.

## What You Actually Get

Open the box and the first thing you notice is the weight. The matte black finish isn't a cheap spray-on coating — it's an electroplated finish that feels dense and substantial in hand. The shower head swivels at the ball joint with a reassuring click, not a floppy wobble. The handheld has a rubberized grip that actually helps when your hands are soapy.

The trim plate covers a standard 1/2-inch IPS rough-in, and the included mounting hardware is solid brass. No plastic threads. That matters because plastic threads strip, and then you're making a second trip to the hardware store.

## Performance Under Real Conditions

Installation is straightforward if you're comfortable with basic plumbing. The trim plate mounts flush, and the diverter knob — which switches between overhead and handheld — is clearly marked. One buyer noted, *"Took about 45 minutes to swap out my old setup. The instructions were clear enough that I didn't have to YouTube anything."*

The spray performance is where Delta earns its reputation. The H2Okinetic technology creates a spray that feels fuller than it looks — it's not a needle-fine mist, it's a satisfying, drenching stream that rinses shampoo out in one pass. The handheld has three settings: full spray, massage, and a pause function. The pause is genuinely useful if you're trying to conserve water while conditioning.

The pressure-balancing valve (again, sold separately) does its job. If someone flushes a toilet while you're mid-shower, the temperature doesn't swing. It holds steady. That's not a luxury feature — in a household with more than one person, it's a sanity feature.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

Scrolling through the 225 ratings, a few consistent themes emerge. The finish is the star. One reviewer wrote, *"I've had this for six months. No water spots, no fingerprints, no peeling. My old brushed nickel fixture looked terrible after a year."* Another echoed: *"The matte black is exactly as pictured — not gray, not shiny, just a deep, consistent black."*

The spray quality gets consistent love too. Multiple buyers mention the overhead shower head feels more expensive than it is — one called it *"a hotel shower experience at home."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, the valve body not being included is annoying. Delta does this across their Modern line, and it pushes the true cost closer to $350 before you factor in any supply lines you might need. If you're replacing an existing shower, you might be able to reuse your current valve — but if you're doing a full remodel, budget for the R10000.

The 60-inch hose is adequate but not generous. For a standard 30-inch-deep shower, it's fine. If you have a larger walk-in shower or you're tall and want to reach your lower legs comfortably, you'll want to swap in a 72-inch hose. That's a $20 fix, but it's an extra purchase.

A handful of reviews mention the diverter toggle needs a firm push to switch between shower head and handheld. It's not a smooth slide like Delta's pricier multi-function diverter. It works, but it's a two-finger push, not a one-finger tap.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're doing a bathroom remodel and want a modern, matte black fixture that looks expensive without hitting luxury pricing. You're replacing an older Delta setup and can reuse the existing rough-in valve. You value temperature stability when other people use water in the house.

**Skip it if:** You're on a strict budget and can't absorb the extra valve cost. You prefer a rainfall-style shower head (this is a standard 8-inch round, not a 12-inch overhead). You want a smooth, premium-feeling diverter — this one is functional but not buttery.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this fit a standard shower arm?**
A: Yes, it connects to a standard 1/2-inch NPT shower arm. If you're replacing an existing shower head, it will thread right on. The trim plate covers up to a 6-inch rough-in opening.

**Q: Is the matte black finish prone to chipping?**
A: No. The electroplated finish is applied at the factory and is significantly more durable than painted finishes. It resists scratches and doesn't show fingerprints. Just avoid abrasive cleaners — use a soft cloth and mild soap.

**Q: Can I install this myself?**
A: If you're comfortable with basic plumbing tools (wrench, plumber's tape, channel locks), yes. The trim kit installs in under an hour. If you're also installing the R10000 valve body, that requires cutting into existing copper or PEX lines — call a plumber if that's outside your comfort zone.
