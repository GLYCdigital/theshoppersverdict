**The short version:** These are the lamps you buy when you want the farmhouse look without paying boutique prices. Two solid ceramic lamps, decent linen-look shades, and a footprint small enough for cramped nightstands. At 4.4 stars from 220 raters, they're doing something right — but they're not perfect.

---

**What's actually in the box**

You get two complete lamps — ceramic base, harp, finial, and a fabric shade each. The bases are heavy (around 3 pounds per lamp), which is a good sign; they won't tip over when you bump the nightstand at 2 a.m. The shade sits on a standard harp, so replacing it with a custom shade is easy if you want a different look.

The ceramic finish is the star here. It's smooth, matte, and slightly textured — reads as handmade even though it's clearly factory-produced. The farmhouse vibe comes from the neutral tone and simple silhouette, not from gimmicky distressing. As one verified buyer put it: *"They look way more expensive than they are. I've gotten compliments from three different guests already."*

**Real-world performance**

The on/off switch is on the cord, not the base — which is actually a plus for bedside use. You can reach it without knocking the lamp. The cord is 5 feet, standard length, and the socket accepts any standard bulb.

Here's the catch: the harp is short. A standard A19 bulb (the common 60-watt equivalent) will stick up above the shade line. You'll want to use a shorter bulb — an A15 or a globe bulb — to get the clean, fully-covered look shown in the product image. This isn't a dealbreaker, but it's a detail the product photos don't make obvious.

Light output is warm and even with the right bulb. The shade diffuses reasonably well, but it's not a thick linen shade — it's more of a textured polyester. With a 60-watt equivalent, you'll see a slight hotspot on the shade. A 40-watt or dimmer-rated LED solves this instantly.

**Where they fall short**

- **No dimmer, no smart features.** You're getting a basic lamp. If you want app control or dimming, look elsewhere.
- **Shade quality is "good enough," not great.** The stitching on the shade seam is visible up close. Again — fine for the price, but know what you're buying.
- **The base's matte finish smudges with oily hands.** A quick wipe fixes it, but it's worth noting if you have kids.

**Who should buy these**

- **Apartment dwellers** — small footprint, warm light, doesn't dominate a room.
- **Farmhouse-style decorators on a budget** — you get the look of a $80-per-lamp boutique piece for a fraction of that.
- **Guest bedroom setups** — buying two at once means matching lamps without hunting for a second one later.

**Skip them if:** you need tall lamps for a high headboard, you want dimming built in, or you're a stickler for premium shade fabric — you'll end up swapping the shades anyway, which adds cost.

**FAQ**

**1. Can I use smart bulbs in these?**
Yes, any E26-base smart bulb works. Just keep it short — an A15 or BR30 shape — so it doesn't peek above the shade.

**2. How tall are they exactly?**
About 10.5 inches to the top of the shade, and the base diameter is roughly 5.5 inches. They're genuinely small — measure your nightstand before buying.

**3. Do the shades come in other colors?**
The set ships with the neutral/tan shade shown. The base color is fixed too. No customization options at this price point.
