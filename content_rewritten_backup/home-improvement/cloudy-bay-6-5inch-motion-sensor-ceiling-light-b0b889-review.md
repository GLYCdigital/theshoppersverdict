Most "motion sensor" ceiling lights are really just glorified nightlights with a passive infrared sensor bolted on. The CLOUDY BAY 6.5inch is different — it's a full 900-lumen fixture that actually replaces your existing ceiling light, not just supplements it. After digging through 215 ratings and the technical specs, here's what you need to know before buying.

**What's Actually in the Box**

You get the fixture itself, a mounting bracket, wire nuts, and screws. It's a 6.5-inch round disc that sits nearly flush against the ceiling — about 2.5 inches of projection, so it won't look like a flying saucer in a hallway. The housing is aluminum with a frosted polycarbonate diffuser, which keeps the LED array evenly spread without hot spots. It connects to standard 120V house wiring with three wires: line, neutral, and ground. If you've swapped a light fixture before, this is a 20-minute job.

**Performance Where It Matters**

The 900-lumen output genuinely lights up a walk-in closet or laundry room. The 3000K color temperature is warm but not dim — think quality restaurant lighting, not a parking garage. The motion sensor has a 10-foot range and a 120-degree detection arc, which covers a standard hallway approach well. You can set the shutoff delay between 15 seconds and 3 minutes using a small switch on the sensor housing.

Here's the part that matters: the sensor doesn't have a photocell. That means it fires whenever it detects motion, regardless of ambient light. In a hallway with windows, it will light up at noon. Some buyers love this — one reviewer noted, "I installed it in my garage and it turns on every time I walk in, day or night. Exactly what I wanted." Others found it annoying. If you need daylight-only operation, this isn't the fixture for you.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The hands-free convenience tops nearly every positive review. One verified purchase wrote: "My hands are always full carrying laundry. This light comes on before I even reach the door." Another buyer praised the build quality: "The aluminum housing feels solid, not like the cheap plastic fixtures from the big box store." The 50,000-hour LED rating also gets mentioned — at 10 uses a day, that's over 13 years of service.

**Where It Falls Short**

The lack of a photocell is the biggest miss, and it's a real one for anyone installing this in a naturally lit space. Some buyers also reported the sensor being *too* sensitive, triggering from pets or from motion in adjacent rooms through open doorways. The 3000K color temperature is locked in — no switch to jump to 4000K or 5000K for those who prefer cooler, more clinical light in utility areas. And while it's rated for damp locations, a few buyers installing it in unconditioned garages noted the electronics can be finicky in extreme temperatures.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Homeowners with dark closets, pantries, or laundry rooms** — this is the ideal application where hands-free lighting actually saves you frustration.
- **Anyone with mobility issues** who struggles with traditional wall switches.
- **Garage or workshop users** who want instant light when carrying tools or boxes.
- **Renters or DIYers** replacing old builder-grade fixtures — the wiring is straightforward and the flush profile fits anywhere.

**Skip it if** you need daylight-only operation, prefer cool white light, or are installing in a space above 110°F consistently.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this work in a bathroom with a shower?**
A: Yes, it's rated for damp locations. Steam won't damage the sealed LED driver or sensor. Just avoid direct water spray.

**Q: How do I adjust the sensor sensitivity?**
A: There's a small dial on the sensor lens. Turn it clockwise for maximum range (10 feet), counterclockwise to reduce it to about 5 feet. The time delay switch is next to it.

**Q: Does it require a neutral wire?**
A: Yes. This is a standard 3-wire connection — line, neutral, and ground. Homes built before the 1980s may not have neutrals in ceiling boxes, so check before buying.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want reliable, hands-free lighting in a dark, frequently-used space and don't mind it firing during daylight hours.

**Skip it if** you need light-sensing capabilities or prefer a cooler color temperature.

At 4.3 stars across 215 ratings, the CLOUDY BAY 6.5inch earns its score. It's not perfect — the missing photocell and fixed 3000K are genuine limitations — but for the primary use case of closets, pantries, and hallways, it performs exactly as advertised. The build quality justifies the price, and the 50,000-hour lifespan means you'll likely forget you ever installed it. That's the highest compliment a utility light can get.
