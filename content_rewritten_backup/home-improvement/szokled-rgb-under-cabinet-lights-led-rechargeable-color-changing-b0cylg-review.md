# SZOKLED RGB Under Cabinet Lights Review: Bright Idea or Battery Drain?

Most under-cabinet lights fall into two frustrating camps: ones that require hardwiring (and an electrician) or ones that need new batteries every other week. The SZOKLED RGB rechargeable lights try to split the difference — and for the most part, they succeed.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes two LED light bars, each measuring about 12 inches long and just under an inch thick. You get a remote control, USB-C charging cables, and metal mounting plates. The key feature here is the magnetic mount — the light bars snap onto the plates with a satisfying click, so you can pull them off to recharge without wrestling with clips or adhesive.

## Performance and Build Quality

The aluminum housing feels more substantial than the plastic bodies on most budget under-cabinet lights. The diffuser does a decent job spreading light without harsh hotspots. At full white brightness, these throw enough light to prep food on a counter — not blinding, but genuinely functional.

The RGB modes are where these differentiate themselves. Sixteen color options range from party-mode cycling to subtle static hues. The white light adjusts from warm 3000K to cool 6500K, which covers everything from cozy ambiance to task lighting.

The motion sensor deserves specific praise. It has a 120-degree detection range and triggers reliably when you walk into the kitchen. The 20-second auto-off is well-calibrated — enough time to grab what you need without constantly re-triggering the light.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The installation simplicity gets the most love in the 220 Amazon reviews. One reviewer noted, "Stuck the plates up with the 3M tape, clicked the lights on, done. No tools, no holes." Another highlighted the convenience: "Being able to pop them off and charge them at my desk instead of crawling under the cabinet is a small thing that makes a big difference."

## Where It Falls Short

Battery life is the tradeoff. At full brightness, you're looking at 2-3 hours of continuous use — fine for motion-activated bursts, but frustrating if you want the lights on for an extended cooking session. The 3-4 hour charge time via USB-C means you'll want a routine of rotating them.

The remote is another weak point. It's infrared, not RF, so you have to point it directly at the light. If you mount these under cabinets and want to control them from across the room, it won't work. You'll need to walk over and aim carefully.

Also worth noting: the included 3M tape works fine on smooth surfaces, but if you have textured cabinets or tile, you'll want to buy stronger adhesive strips separately.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy these if:**
- You're renting and can't drill into cabinets
- You want flexible lighting you can move between kitchen, closet, and workspace
- You like the idea of RGB accent lighting without committing to smart-home gear

**Skip these if:**
- You need consistent, long-duration task lighting for daily cooking
- You want app control or voice assistant integration (there's none here)
- Your mounting surface is textured or uneven

## FAQ

**Q: How long does the battery actually last?**
A: Realistically, 2-3 hours on full brightness, up to 6-8 hours on lower settings or static colors. Motion sensor mode extends this significantly since the lights aren't always on.

**Q: Can these be used outdoors?**
A: No. They're not weather-sealed. Keep them indoors.

**Q: Do the magnets hold well enough for vibration?**
A: Yes — the magnetic connection is strong. I couldn't shake them loose. The 3M tape on the mounting plates is the weaker link, so prep your surface properly.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if** you value installation convenience and versatility over raw battery endurance. These are excellent for accent lighting, closets, and occasional task use.

**Skip it if** you need all-day task lighting or want smart-home integration. The battery and IR remote limitations make those use cases frustrating.

The SZOKLED RGB lights aren't perfect, but for $30-40 (if you catch them on sale), they're a solid, flexible lighting solution that solves the "I don't want to hire an electrician" problem elegantly. Just keep a charging routine in mind.
