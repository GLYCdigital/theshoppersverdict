Let me be direct: most battery-powered sconces are cheap plastic gimmicks that die in a month. The camuucci set of two is not that. With 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, this is one of the better cordless lighting options I’ve seen in this category. But it’s not perfect, and you need to know exactly where it fits before buying.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains two complete sconce fixtures, two rechargeable battery packs (USB-C charging), adhesive mounting plates, screws/anchors for hardwired-style mounting, and a user manual. The fixtures themselves measure roughly 11 inches tall with a 4.5-inch backplate—substantial enough to look like real hardwired sconces, not flimsy stick-on pucks. The metal body has a matte black finish that hides fingerprints well.

## Real-World Performance

The headline feature is the rechargeable battery system. Instead of buying AA batteries every few weeks, you pop out the internal pack, charge via USB-C (cable included), and get anywhere from 20 to 60 hours depending on brightness. At medium brightness with 2–3 hours of evening use, you’re charging roughly once every two weeks. That’s genuinely convenient.

The light quality surprised me. You get three color temperatures—3000K warm, 4000K neutral, 6500K daylight—and stepless dimming from 100% down to about 10%. The touch sensor on the fixture cycles through modes with a tap-and-hold for dimming. At full brightness, these throw enough light to read by if mounted at bedside height. The diffuser panel spreads light evenly without harsh hotspots.

**Installation is the real selling point.** The adhesive plate uses 3M-grade tape that, in customer feedback, holds firmly on painted drywall, tile, and even textured walls. One reviewer noted: *“I was skeptical about the adhesive holding above my bathroom mirror—it’s been 4 months and hasn’t budged.”* Screws are provided for drywall or wood if you prefer permanent mounting. No electrician, no wiring, no junction boxes. Total install time: about 10 minutes.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Looking through the 229 ratings, three themes dominate:

- **Ease of setup**: Multiple reviewers mention having them installed in under 15 minutes without tools.
- **Battery life**: Several users report 3–4 weeks between charges with evening-only use.
- **Looks expensive**: The metal construction and modern design fool guests into thinking they’re hardwired.

## Where It Falls Short

Here’s the honest tradeoff. The touch controls are on the front of the fixture body. If you mount these high—say, above a tall headboard or at standard 66-inch sconce height—you’ll be stretching to reach the sensor. This isn’t a dealbreaker, but it’s a daily annoyance if you want to dim without getting up.

Also, there’s no remote control and no motion sensor. These are strictly touch-operated. If you wanted smart-home integration or app control, this isn’t the product for you.

The lowest dim setting has a slightly amber tint rather than a pure warm white. It’s cozy, but if you’re chasing a precise candlelight aesthetic, you might be disappointed.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy these if** you’re a renter who can’t hardwire, you want reading lights beside a bed without nightstand lamps, or you need accent lighting in a hallway, gallery wall, or alcove where running wire is impractical. The 6500K daylight mode makes them genuinely useful as task lighting in a closet or pantry.

**Skip these if** you need smart controls, want motion-activated lighting for a stairwell, or plan to mount them higher than 6 feet—the touch controls become impractical.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does the battery actually last?**
A: Realistically, 3–4 weeks with 2–3 hours of evening use at medium brightness. Crank it to full daylight mode and you’ll get closer to 10–12 days.

**Q: Can these be mounted on wallpaper or textured walls?**
A: The adhesive works on smooth painted surfaces and tile. For heavily textured or wallpaper surfaces, use the included screws—the adhesive won’t grip reliably on bumps.

**Q: Do they charge while mounted?**
A: No. You remove the battery pack from the fixture and charge it separately. It takes about 3 hours for a full charge.
