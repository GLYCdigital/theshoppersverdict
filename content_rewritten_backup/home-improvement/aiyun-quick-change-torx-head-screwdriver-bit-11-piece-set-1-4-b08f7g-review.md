You're mid-project, staring at a stubborn T25 Torx screw buried in a door hinge, and your cheap single bit just rounded off. Again. The aiyun 11-piece Quick Change Torx set is the kind of purchase that solves that problem before it starts — but let's be clear about what you're actually getting.

## What's In the Package

The set covers T10, T15, T20, T25, T27, T30, T35, and T40 — the full spectrum you'll actually encounter on modern vehicles, electronics, and appliances. You also get a 50mm power bit (T25) for those recessed fasteners where a standard 25mm bit won't reach. Every bit uses a standard 1/4-inch hex shank, so it drops into any impact driver, drill, or ratcheting screwdriver you already own.

The bits come in a plastic storage case with a clear lid. Nothing fancy, but it keeps them organized in a drawer or toolbox.

## Performance and Build Quality

Here's where the aiyun set earns its keep. The bits are forged from S2 alloy steel with a heat-treatment process that gives them genuine hardness. In real-world use, that translates to bite — the star-shaped profile engages Torx screws with minimal cam-out, which is the primary failure point on cheaper bits. With Torx fasteners, fit is everything, and these tolerances are tight enough that you won't feel that annoying "wobble" when the bit is seated.

The quick-change collar is a machined steel sleeve, not a cheap spring clip. It locks bits firmly with a satisfying click, and there's zero play once engaged. I ran these through an impact driver on automotive interior trim screws and rusted exterior fasteners — no rounding, no twisting, no chrome peeling off the tips.

One note: the 50mm power bit is a genuinely useful addition. Reaching that T30 bolt behind the alternator bracket or the T25 screws on a recessed door striker becomes a one-hand operation instead of a contortionist act.

## Where It Falls Short

The case is the weak link. There's no molded bit holder inside — just slots that let the bits shift around. Open the case upside down and you'll be fishing T-tools out of your toolbox. The hinge mechanism also feels thin; treat it gently or expect cracks within a year of regular use.

Also, there's no T8 or T9. If you work on Apple products or small electronics, you'll need a separate precision set. This is aimed at automotive and general construction, not micro-electronics.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these fit standard impact drivers?**
A: Yes. The 1/4-inch hex shank is universally compatible with impact drivers, drill chucks, and bit holders.

**Q: Will the T40 handle lug bolt Torx fasteners?**
A: For light to medium duty, yes. For high-torque applications like suspension components, use a dedicated socket-style Torx bit instead — these are screwdriver bits, not impact sockets.

**Q: Are these magnetic?**
A: No. You'll want a magnetic bit holder or a dab of thread-holding compound for overhead work or hard-to-reach screws.
