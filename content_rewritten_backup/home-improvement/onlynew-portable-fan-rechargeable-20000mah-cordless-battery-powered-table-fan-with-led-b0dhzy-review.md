If you've ever sweated through a power outage or baked inside a tent in July, you know the problem with most portable fans: they die before you do. The ONLYNEW Portable Fan takes a different approach — it stuffs a 20000mAh battery into a cordless unit that claims to run for up to 60 hours. That's not marketing math; that's real staying power. Here's what 225 Amazon reviewers at 4.5 stars actually think after using it.

## What's actually in the box

You get the fan unit with a built-in handle, a detachable base that screws on for tabletop use, a USB-C charging cable, and a small instruction manual. No wall adapter included — plan to use your phone charger. The fan measures about 12 inches tall on the stand, with a 7-inch blade assembly. The LED light ring sits around the fan housing, which is a thoughtful touch for camping or workspace use.

## Real-world performance

The headline number is the battery. At the lowest speed, ONLYNEW claims up to 60 hours. Reviewers consistently report getting multiple full nights of use between charges. One buyer noted: *"Used it for a 3-day camping trip with the fan on low all night — still had 40% battery left."* On high speed, expect closer to 12–15 hours, which is still respectable for a unit this size.

Airflow is the tradeoff. At max speed, it's a solid desk fan — enough to keep you comfortable at arm's length, but it won't cool a room like a 20-inch box fan. The three speed settings are distinct, and the lowest setting is genuinely quiet — around a whisper level that won't disturb sleep. The highest setting has a noticeable whir, but nothing that drowns out conversation or TV.

The LED light is a genuinely useful addition. It has three brightness levels and casts a warm, wide beam. For tent camping or a workbench, it replaces a separate lantern.

## Build quality and quirks

The housing is ABS plastic with a matte finish that resists fingerprints. The handle is integrated into the top and feels solid. The base screws on with a thumb-screw — it's secure but takes 10 seconds to attach or remove. The USB-C charging port is on the back, and a full charge takes about 5–6 hours from empty.

Here's where it gets honest: at 3.5 pounds, this isn't a "grab and go" fan. It's designed for stationary use — a campsite table, a workshop bench, a bedroom nightstand. The lack of a remote is also a real gap. If the fan is across the room, you're getting up to adjust it. That's a minor annoyance at home, but a significant one if you're using it as a tent fan and don't want to unzip your sleeping bag at 2 a.m.

## What buyers consistently praise

The battery life dominates the positive reviews, and for good reason. Multiple reviewers highlight using it for hurricane prep, off-grid work, and long camping trips without recharging. One electrician commented: *"Keep it in my truck for outdoor jobs — the LED helps in crawl spaces and the fan keeps me cool. Charged it twice in three months."* The build quality also gets consistent praise for feeling more premium than the price suggests.

## Where it falls short

Beyond the weight and missing remote, a few buyers mentioned the fan doesn't oscillate. The head tilts manually, but there's no auto-swing. For a room fan, that's a limitation — you get a fixed column of airflow. The battery indicator is a simple four-LED display that's accurate but basic. And if you're expecting a whisper-quiet high speed, adjust expectations — it's audible.

## Who should buy this

- **Campers and RV owners** who need reliable airflow all night without shore power
- **Workshop users** who want a cordless fan plus a work light in one tool
- **Emergency-prep households** building a kit for power outages
- **Anyone with a hot bedroom** who wants a quiet, long-lasting sleep fan

Skip it if you need a room-cooling fan with oscillation, or if you're backpacking and need something under a pound. This is a "base camp" fan, not a trail fan.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does the 20000mAh battery actually last?**
A: On low speed with the LED off, expect 50–60 hours. On high with the LED on, figure 10–14 hours. Real-world results vary with temperature, but reviewers consistently report multi-day use between charges.

**Q: Can it charge my phone?**
A: Yes — there's a USB-A output port. It's a pass-through, not a fast charger, but it'll top off a phone in a pinch. Note that using it to charge your phone drains the fan's battery faster.

**Q: Is it quiet enough for a bedroom?**
A: On low, yes — it's roughly 30 dB, quieter than a whisper. On high, it's closer to 50 dB, which is noticeable but not disruptive. The LED has a dimmer setting that works well as a night light.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a durable, long-lasting cordless fan for camping, emergencies, or workshop use and don't mind the 3.5-pound weight. The battery life is genuinely exceptional, and the LED adds real value.

**Skip it if** you need oscillation, a remote, or a featherweight fan for backpacking. This is a workhorse, not a convenience gadget.

For the price, the ONLYNEW delivers category-leading battery life in a well-built package. It earns its 4.5-star rating — with the caveat that it's a specialist tool, not a universal solution.
