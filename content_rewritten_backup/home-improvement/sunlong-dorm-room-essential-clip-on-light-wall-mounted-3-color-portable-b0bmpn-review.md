**The $15 dorm lamp that actually earns its spot on your headboard**

If you've ever tried studying in a dorm room with only that overhead fluorescent fixture, you know the struggle: harsh shadows, that weird green tint, and zero flexibility. The SUNLONG Clip on Light is the budget fix that keeps showing up in dorm packing lists — and with 215 ratings averaging 4.3 stars, it's not just hype. Here's the honest breakdown after digging into what real buyers say.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the lamp itself — a clip base, gooseneck arm, and LED head — plus a permanently attached 5-foot USB cable. No wall adapter included, so you'll need any standard USB port (your laptop, a phone charger block, or a power strip with USB ports all work). The clip opens to about 2 inches, which handles standard headboards, desk edges, and shelf lips. The head itself is compact — roughly 4 inches long — so it doesn't cast a giant footprint on your workspace.

**Real-world performance**

The headline feature is the 3-color cycle: warm white (~3000K), natural white (~4500K), and cool white (~6000K). One button tap cycles through them; another tap turns it off. That's it — no dimming, no memory function. In practice, this is fine for most dorm scenarios. Warm light works for winding down, cool light helps you power through reading, natural sits in the middle.

The gooseneck arm is the surprise standout. It's fully flexible and holds its position — buyers consistently note that it doesn't droop, which is the #1 complaint in this price class. One verified buyer put it simply: *"I've had cheaper clip lights that sagged after a week. This one stays where I bend it."* The clip itself has decent spring tension; it won't fall off if you bump your headboard.

**What buyers consistently praise**

- **Versatility**: People mount it everywhere — headboards, desk corners, kitchen cabinets, even treadmill handles. The clip design means zero permanent installation.
- **Light quality**: Multiple reviewers mention the cool white setting is genuinely bright enough for close-up reading or crafting without being harsh.
- **Sturdiness**: The build feels more solid than the price suggests. The head is aluminum, not cheap plastic, which helps with heat dissipation.

**Where it falls short**

The lack of dimming is the biggest miss. At full brightness in cool white, it can be *too* bright for late-night scrolling when your roommate's asleep. You'll either deal with it or cover the light with a shirt — a workaround that shows up in more than a few reviews.

The permanently attached 5-foot USB cable is also limiting. If your outlets are across the room, you'll need an extension cord. And the clip pads are bare plastic — one reviewer noted scratches on a painted desk after overtightening. A strip of felt tape solves this, but it's an extra step you shouldn't have to take.

**Who should buy this**

- **Dorm residents**: This is the target use case, and it nails it. Compact, clip-on, no tools needed.
- **Apartment dwellers with limited outlets**: If you're renting and can't install fixtures, this gives you task lighting anywhere.
- **Crafters and hobbyists**: The cool white setting is excellent for detail work on a desk.

**Skip it if** you need dimmable light for nighttime use, or if your mounting surface is more than 2 inches thick.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can you use this while it's charging?**  
A: Yes, it's wired-only — no battery. It runs directly from USB power, so as long as it's plugged in, it's on.

**Q: Does it get hot after hours of use?**  
A: Warm to the touch but not hot. The aluminum head helps dissipate heat. No burn risk.

**Q: Will the clip damage my headboard?**  
A: The plastic pads can leave marks if you crank the spring hard. Add a thin felt strip to the pads if you're worried.

**The Verdict**

The SUNLONG Clip on Light isn't perfect — the missing dimmer and short cable are real annoyances. But at this price point, it delivers exactly what dorm shoppers need: reliable, flexible, decent-quality task lighting that mounts anywhere without tools. With 4.3 stars from 215 ratings, the consensus is clear.

**Buy it if** you want a cheap, versatile clip light that won't sag or die on you mid-semester.  
**Skip it if** dimming is non-negotiable or your mounting surface exceeds 2 inches.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — a solid pick that does the basics exceptionally well, with minor tradeoffs you can live with.
