## The Under-Cabinet Light That Finally Fits Right

If you've ever installed standard under-cabinet lights on cabinets with bullnose (rounded) front edges, you know the problem: the fixture hangs proud, light spills out the front, and it looks like an afterthought. The ASD LED Under Cabinet Bullnose fixture solves that specific headache with a housing shaped to match the curve of your cabinet's leading edge. It's a niche solution, but for kitchens with bullnose cabinets, it's the right one.

## What You Actually Get

This is a hardwired, 120V fixture—not a plug-in strip or battery puck. You get the light bar itself, a mounting bracket, wire nuts, and screws. The unit is linkable (up to 12 fixtures on a single run, per the specs), which matters if you're lighting a full kitchen run. The housing is aluminum, which helps with heat dissipation—something cheaper plastic fixtures struggle with. It's also wet-rated for damp locations, so it'll survive under a range hood or near a sink.

## Performance and Build Quality

The standout feature is the CCT selector: three color temperatures—2700K (warm), 3000K (soft white), and 3500K (neutral)—controlled by a small switch on the backplate. This is genuinely useful because it lets you match the light to your existing recessed cans or pendant bulbs. No more buying three different fixtures and hoping one matches.

Dimmability is the second win. It works with standard ELV and LED-compatible wall dimmers. One reviewer noted, "I paired it with a Lutron dimmer and it goes from a nice warm glow to full brightness with zero flicker." That's the experience you want—no buzzing, no strobing.

The light output is solid: 1200 lumens at 3500K from a 15-watt draw. That's roughly equivalent to a 75-watt incandescent. It's plenty for task lighting over a countertop or workspace. The beam spread is wide and even, with no hot spots or dark bands, which is often the complaint with cheaper LED strips.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The fit on bullnose cabinets is the #1 compliment. As one buyer put it: "Finally, a light that actually sits flush on my rounded cabinet edges. No gap, no light bleeding out the front. It looks custom." Another mentioned the install: "The bracket is straightforward—took me 20 minutes for two fixtures."

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest quibble: there's no switch on the fixture itself. You *must* wire it to a wall switch or dimmer, or it's always on. If you're retrofitting a kitchen without existing switched wiring, you'll need an electrician or a wireless switch kit. That's an added cost and hassle.

Second, the CCT selector switch is on the backplate. To change from 3000K to 3500K, you have to loosen the fixture and pull it down. Do your color temperature choosing before you mount it permanently.

Some buyers also noted the included wire nuts are small—fine for standard 14-gauge, but if you're daisy-chaining multiple fixtures, you'll want to grab a pack of larger connectors.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Kitchen remodelers with bullnose cabinets**: This is the exact fixture you've been hunting for.
- **DIYers comfortable with basic electrical work**: If you can wire a light switch, you can install this.
- **Anyone tired of plug-in puck lights**: Hardwired is cleaner, no adapter bricks dangling behind cabinets.

Who should skip it: If your cabinets have a square front edge, a standard flat under-cabinet light will be cheaper and just as effective. And if you're not prepared to deal with hardwiring, look at plug-in options instead.

## FAQ

**Can I use this with a smart dimmer switch?**
Yes, if the dimmer is rated for LED loads and is ELV or trailing-edge compatible. Standard leading-edge dimmers may cause flicker.

**How many fixtures can I link together?**
Up to 12 per run, but check your circuit load—each draws 15 watts, so you're looking at 180 watts max.

**Is the color temperature adjustable after installation?**
Technically yes, but you'll need to unclip the fixture from the mounting bracket to access the switch. Easiest to set it before final mounting.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you have bullnose cabinets and want a clean, flush, dimmable under-cabinet light with flexible color temperature. It's well-built, performs as advertised, and solves a real fit problem.

**Skip it if** you need a plug-in solution or have standard square-edged cabinets—you'll pay extra for the bullnose profile without needing it.
