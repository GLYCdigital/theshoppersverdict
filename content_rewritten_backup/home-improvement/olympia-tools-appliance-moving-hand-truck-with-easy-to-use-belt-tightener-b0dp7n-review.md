## FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Can one person safely move a washing machine with this?**
A: Yes, with the belt tightener. The strap secures the machine vertically, so you control the tilt. The trick is to keep the machine upright and use the dolly as a lever, not a wheelbarrow. For stairs, angle the dolly into the stairs and let the wheels take the weight.

**Q: Does the belt system work on oddly-shaped items like a stackable washer/dryer?**
A: The strap wraps around the frame and the appliance, not the appliance alone. As long as the item is flat against the nose plate, it will secure. For top-heavy units, add a second strap (not included) for peace of mind.

**Q: Will the pneumatic wheels go flat if I store it in a hot garage?**
A: They can lose air over time. Inflate to 40 PSI before each use. If you store it for months, consider placing it on a board to keep the tires off the concrete and prevent flat spots.
