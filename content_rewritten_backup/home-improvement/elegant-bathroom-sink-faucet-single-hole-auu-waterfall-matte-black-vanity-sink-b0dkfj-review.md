The AUU Waterfall Matte faucet is one of those products that looks dramatically more expensive than it actually is. In the product image, you see a sleek, minimalist silhouette with a wide, flat spout that promises a cinematic water flow. The real question is whether it delivers that experience without driving you crazy during installation or daily use. After digging through 225 ratings and the specifics of this single-hole design, here's what actually matters.

## What you get

The box contains the faucet body, a single lever handle, mounting hardware, and supply lines. That's it. No pop-up drain, no deck plate — this is strictly a single-hole installation. If your vanity has a three-hole setup, you'll need to buy a deck plate separately or choose a different faucet. The matte black finish is applied consistently across all surfaces, including the underside edges, which tells you the coating process was done right rather than just sprayed on the visible parts.

## Real-world performance

The waterfall spout is the headline feature, and it genuinely works. The stream comes out wide and smooth — roughly two inches across — and drops into the basin with minimal noise. One buyer noted, "The water flows like a sheet of glass, not a messy spray like I expected." That's accurate. The flow rate is adequate for handwashing, though it's not going to fill a basin quickly. It's a form-over-function tradeoff, but one that makes sense for a bathroom where aesthetics matter.

The ceramic cartridge is smooth and precise. There's no grinding, no resistance, no drip after shutoff. The handle has a solid, damped feel that you typically only find on faucets twice this price. Matte black finishes can be hit-or-miss with hard water, but this one holds up — several reviewers mentioned using it for months without visible spotting or fading.

## What buyers consistently praise

The aesthetic impact is the most common theme. A verified purchase reviewer wrote, "Everyone who visits asks where I got this faucet. It completely transformed my small powder room." Another noted, "The matte finish hides toothpaste splatter and water marks way better than my old chrome faucet." The installation process is also frequently called out as straightforward — the supply lines are pre-attached, and the mounting nut is accessible even in tight spaces.

## Where it falls short

The waterfall design has a genuine limitation: it needs a sink with enough depth to handle the wide stream. With a shallow vessel sink or a small rectangular basin, water can bounce off the bottom and splash onto the countertop. One reviewer warned, "Beautiful faucet, but I had to adjust my sink's water pressure down to avoid splashing." If your sink is less than four inches deep, measure carefully before committing.

The missing pop-up drain is also a real annoyance. You're spending on a premium-looking faucet, and then you have to spend another $15–30 on a drain assembly that matches the matte black finish. It's a cost and a convenience issue that shouldn't exist at this price point.

## Who should buy this

This faucet is for the homeowner updating a powder room or guest bath where visual impact matters most. It's perfect for a modern or transitional vanity with a single-hole setup and a deep enough basin. It's also a strong choice if you're tired of chrome showing every fingerprint. Skip it if you have a three-hole sink without a deck plate, a shallow vessel sink, or if you prioritize fast water flow over aesthetics.

## FAQ

**Does the matte black finish chip or scratch easily?**
The finish is powder-coated and appears durable, but no matte finish is scratch-proof. Avoid abrasive cleaners — a soft cloth and mild soap is all you need. Several long-term reviewers report no issues after six months of daily use.

**Will this fit my existing sink?**
It requires a single hole with a standard 1.375-inch diameter. If your sink has three holes, you'll need a deck plate. The faucet is also designed for 3/8-inch compression supply lines, which are included.

**Is the waterfall flow adjustable?**
The flow rate is controlled by the single lever handle, but even at full pressure, the waterfall spreads out. If your home has very high water pressure, consider installing an angle stop valve to regulate flow and prevent splashing.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a statement piece for your bathroom, have a single-hole sink with decent depth, and value a finish that actually stays clean-looking.

**Skip it if:** You need a pop-up drain included, have a shallow vessel sink, or want maximum water flow for utility over aesthetics.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The AUU Waterfall Matte delivers on its promise with excellent build quality and a genuinely striking design. The missing drain and splash potential in shallow sinks keep it from perfection, but for the right setup, this is a fantastic upgrade that looks like it cost twice as much.
