Let's be blunt: most bathroom fans under $50 are noisy, weak, and look like they belong in a gas station restroom. The Akicon 110CFM exhaust fan with light isn't that — but it's not a premium Panasonic either. Here's where it actually lands after digging through 215 verified ratings and the spec sheet.

**What you're actually getting**

The box contains the fan housing with a pre-mounted blower, the white polycarbonate grille, an integrated 18W LED light module, and standard mounting hardware. The unit is designed for 4-inch ducting — check your existing rough-in before ordering. The housing is 13 x 13 inches, which fits standard ceiling joist spacing without modification. One thing worth noting from the product images: the grille is a clean, modern square design that sits flush — no bulky dome sticking down into your bathroom.

**Performance and real-world use**

At 110 CFM, this fan is rated for bathrooms up to roughly 105 square feet. That's the sweet spot for a typical full bath with a shower — it'll cycle the air adequately to keep mirrors from fogging and stop moisture from lingering on walls. The 2.0 sones rating translates to roughly a quiet library hum. It's audible but not intrusive; you can hold a conversation or watch your phone without cranking the volume.

The LED light is genuinely useful — 18 watts pushing out light equivalent to a 100W incandescent. It's bright enough to shave by, and the color temperature is a neutral daylight white, not that sickly yellow or harsh blue you get from cheap fixtures.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The noise level is the headline across verified reviews. One buyer noted, "I was expecting the typical loud rattling fan. This is actually quiet — I forget it's running." Another recurring theme is installation ease: "Direct replacement for the old builder-grade fan. Wired up in about 30 minutes with basic tools." The light brightness also gets consistent mentions, with multiple buyers saying it replaced a separate light fixture entirely.

**Where it falls short**

No humidity sensor is the biggest miss. At this price point, you can't expect it, but it's worth knowing — you'll either run it manually or wire in a timer switch (a $15 upgrade that's worth it). The plastic grille is another tradeoff. It's sturdy enough and won't yellow like cheaper polycarbonates, but if you're used to brushed metal fixtures, it'll feel a step down. A few buyers also mentioned the fan isn't completely silent on the highest setting — the 2.0 sones is real, but it's not the near-silent 0.3-0.5 sones you'd get from premium units costing 3x as much.

**Who should buy this**

- **DIY homeowners** replacing a builder-grade fan in a standard-size bath — this is a direct, painless swap
- **Rental property owners** who want something quiet and reliable without overspending
- **Anyone with a 4-inch duct** already in place and a bathroom under 100 sq ft

Skip it if you need a humidity-sensing auto fan, have a 6-inch duct requiring an adapter, or want commercial-grade build quality for a high-end renovation.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this fan quiet enough for a nursery-adjacent bathroom?**
A: At 2.0 sones, it's quiet enough for normal conversation but not silent. If the fan is directly above a sleeping baby's room, consider a 0.5 sone model. Otherwise, it's fine.

**Q: Does the LED light need a special dimmer switch?**
A: No — it's a standard integrated LED. But it's not dimmable unless you have a compatible LED dimmer, and the fan and light are typically on separate circuits if wired correctly.

**Q: Will this fit in an existing 8-inch round ceiling hole?**
A: The housing is designed for new construction or retrofit between 16-inch joists. If you have a round hole, you'll need a retrofit bracket kit — this doesn't include one.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a quiet, bright, reliable bathroom fan for a standard-size bath without paying premium prices. It does exactly what it claims, installs easily, and the light is a genuine bonus.

**Skip it if** you need smart features like humidity sensing, have non-standard ducting, or demand commercial-grade metal construction.
