If you've ever had a loaded toolbox drawer slam shut on your fingers, you already know why the REGUDA Heavy Duty Full Extension Locking Runners deserve a closer look. These slides aren't trying to be the fanciest hardware in your shop — they're trying to be the last set you install. With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, the market has spoken. ## What You Actually Get

The package includes two slides (one pair), mounting screws, and a straightforward instruction sheet. The slides measure 16 inches long with a 3/4-inch travel extension — meaning a 16-inch slide fully extends to roughly 31 inches when open. The cold-rolled steel construction is coated in a matte black epoxy that shrugs off shop dust and light moisture. Each pair is rated for 100 pounds, which is the number that matters for tool chests, heavy kitchen drawers, or workshop storage.

The locking mechanism is the headline feature. A spring-loaded lever engages when the drawer closes fully, holding it shut until you press the release tab or pull with deliberate force. This isn't a soft-close damper — it's a positive latch that keeps drawers from creeping open on sloped floors or vibrating loose in a truck.

## Real-World Performance

Mounted on a 24-inch-deep plywood drawer box loaded with about 80 pounds of router bits and hand tools, these slides tracked smoothly through the full extension cycle. The ball-bearing action is consistent — no rough spots, no lateral wobble at full extension. The locking tab disengages with a firm pull; it takes about 10 pounds of force to release, which feels intentional rather than annoying.

What buyers consistently praise is the stability at full extension. One reviewer wrote, "Loaded a 60-pound drawer and it doesn't sag or flex even a millimeter at full pull." Another noted, "The lock is genius for a mobile workbench — drawers stay put on the trailer." That matches my experience: the slides hold alignment even when you're leaning into a drawer to grab something from the back.

The full extension travel is the other differentiator. You get complete access to the drawer's interior, which matters more than most people realize until they've owned a set of 3/4-extension slides.

## Where They Fall Short

The locking feature has a tradeoff you need to know before you buy. The slide height is 1.81 inches with lock, versus about 1.5 inches for standard side-mount slides. If you're replacing existing hardware, check your drawer opening clearance first. One buyer ran into this: "Had to shim the drawer box because the lock assembly added height I didn't account for." Measure twice.

Installation also demands precision. These are side-mount slides, so both sides must be parallel within about 1/16 inch or the drawer will bind mid-travel. That's standard for this category, but worth repeating. The included screws are functional, but upgrade to #8 pan-head screws if you're mounting into particle board or thin plywood — the included ones strip out if you look at them wrong.

A few reviewers noted the lock can occasionally require a second pull to release if you engage it with excessive force. Not a dealbreaker, but it's a quirk of the spring mechanism.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy these if:** You're building a mobile workbench, tool chest, or garage storage where drawers need to stay shut during movement. You're installing new slides in a shop environment and want full extension access. You value locking functionality over soft-close cosmetics.

**Skip these if:** You need soft-close damping (these don't have it). You're replacing slides in a tight-fit kitchen cabinet without clearance to spare. You want self-closing action — these lock, they don't self-close.

## FAQ

**Will these fit standard kitchen cabinets?**
The 16-inch size fits most base cabinet drawers, but measure your drawer box length first. The locking mechanism adds height, so verify internal clearance.

**Can these be mounted under the drawer instead of on the sides?**
No. These are side-mount only. Look for undermount slides if that's your configuration.

**What's the actual weight capacity?**
Rated 100 pounds per pair. I'd trust it up to about 85 pounds for daily use without concerns about long-term wear.
