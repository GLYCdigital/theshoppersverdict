# EDISHINE Wall Sconces Review: Farmhouse Vanity Lights That Actually Deliver

You've probably seen a dozen farmhouse sconces on Amazon that look identical in the product photos but arrive feeling like tin cans. The EDISHINE Wall Sconces Set of 2 is not that product. With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, these fixtures have earned their reputation through solid construction and thoughtful design details that matter during installation and daily use.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains two complete sconces — no assembly required. Each unit comes pre-wired with the backplate, arm, and glass shade already fitted together. You also get mounting screws, wire nuts, and a small level. That's it. No surprises, no missing pieces, no "you'll need to buy this separately" hardware trap.

The fixture itself measures roughly 11 inches tall with a 6.5-inch projection from the wall — substantial enough to make a statement above a 30-inch vanity without overwhelming the space. The seeded glass shade is the standout feature; it's not the thin, fragile stuff you see on budget fixtures. It has actual texture that diffuses light nicely and hides water spots.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The metal body feels dense and properly coated. I've handled enough cheap sconces to know the difference between powder coating that chips when you look at it wrong and the EDISHINE's finish, which held up fine during installation. The matte black version has a slight texture that resists fingerprints — genuinely useful in a bathroom.

Installation is straightforward if you're comfortable with basic wiring. The pre-assembled design means you're not juggling loose arms while trying to connect wires. One reviewer noted, "I installed both in about 20 minutes. The included hardware was actually sufficient quality — didn't have to run to the hardware store." That matches the general consensus: this is a DIY-friendly fixture.

The E26 socket accepts standard bulbs, which is more important than it sounds. Many competing sconces use candelabra bases or proprietary LED modules that limit your options. Here, you can use anything from vintage Edison bulbs to LED A19s. The fixture is also dimmer-compatible, provided you use dimmable bulbs.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the reviews, three themes dominate. First, the aesthetic: "These look way more expensive than they are. The glass is gorgeous and the finish is flawless." Second, the light quality — the seeded glass creates a warm, diffused glow that flatters the space without harsh shadows. Third, the value: buyers repeatedly mention that comparable fixtures at big-box stores cost double or triple for similar quality.

## Where It Falls Short

The backplate is 4.7 inches in diameter. Standard junction boxes are fine, but if you're covering a larger box or patching drywall from a previous fixture, you'll need to address that gap. Also, bulbs are not included. It's a minor annoyance, but with the price of vintage-style Edison bulbs, factor in an extra $10–15 for the complete look.

One reviewer mentioned the arm doesn't swivel — the sconce is fixed in position. If you need adjustable lighting direction, this isn't the fixture for you.

## Who Should Buy These

These sconces are ideal for three buyer profiles: **DIY homeowners** tackling a bathroom refresh with basic electrical skills, **renters** who want a high-end look they can take with them (installation is reversible), and **anyone matching farmhouse or transitional decor** who wants the look of high-end fixtures without the boutique price tag. If you need adjustable arms or a larger backplate for covering patched drywall, look elsewhere.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these be installed pointing down instead of up?**
A: Yes. The glass shade can be oriented either direction, though the arm's curve is fixed. Most buyers install them pointing down for vanity lighting.

**Q: What bulb brightness is recommended?**
A: For a bathroom vanity, go with 2700K–3000K LEDs in the 60-watt equivalent range. The seeded glass cuts roughly 15% of light output, so don't use anything dimmer than 60W equivalent.

**Q: Do these work with smart bulbs?**
A: Yes, standard E26 smart bulbs fit fine. Just ensure the smart bulb isn't physically too large — some chunky ones may not clear the glass shade.
