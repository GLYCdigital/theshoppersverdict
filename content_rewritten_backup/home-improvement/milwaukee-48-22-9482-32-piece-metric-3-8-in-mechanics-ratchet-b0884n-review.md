---

If you're a weekend wrench or a pro who's tired of digging through a cluttered drawer for a 13mm, the Milwaukee 48-22-9482 deserves a hard look. This 32-piece metric set covers 8mm through 24mm sockets (both shallow and deep), plus a 3/8-inch ratchet, two extensions, a universal joint, and a spark plug socket. That's a genuinely useful spread for most Japanese and European vehicles.

The star here is the ratchet. Milwaukee's 72-tooth gear gives you a 5-degree swing arc, which means you can turn a fastener in tight quarters without flipping the tool repeatedly. The head is compact, the handle is knurled for grip even with oily hands, and the quick-release button works smoothly. As one Amazon buyer put it: *"The ratchet feels like it costs twice what the whole set does."* That's not hyperbole—the mechanism has a crisp, positive engagement with no slop.

Sockets are broached properly and slide onto fasteners without wobble. The chrome finish is thick and polished—it doesn't just look good, it sheds dirt and grime with a quick wipe. Size markings are laser-etched on both sides and are large enough to read at a glance. Many reviewers specifically noted the markings stay legible: *"After a year of daily use, I can still read every size without squinting."*

**Where it falls short**

Honesty time. This set is metric-only. If you work on older American trucks or classic cars, you're buying a second set anyway. That's not a flaw in execution, but it's a critical buying consideration.

The case is the weak point. It's a standard blow-molded insert that holds everything securely, but the latch is thin and has been known to pop open if dropped from a workbench. Several reviewers mentioned this. It won't hurt the tools, but you'll be chasing sockets around the garage floor.

Also, 3/8-inch drive is a jack-of-all-trades size. It's perfect for interior work, brake calipers, and engine accessories. But if you're torquing lug nuts or breaking loose rusted suspension bolts, you'll need a 1/2-inch breaker bar. This set won't do everything.

**Who should buy this**

- **DIY home mechanics** working on modern cars, motorcycles, or lawn equipment—the metric range covers 90% of what you'll touch.
- **Professional techs** who want a durable, compact set for a service truck or toolbox drawer.
- **Anyone upgrading from cheap socket sets**—the ratchet feel alone justifies the upgrade.

**Who should skip it**

- **Classic car owners** who need SAE sizes more than metric.
- **Heavy-duty users** who regularly torque over 100 ft-lbs—step up to 1/2-inch drive.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Q: Does this set include a spark plug socket?**
Yes, there's a 16mm spark plug socket with a rubber insert included. It covers most modern engines.

**Q: Are the sockets impact-rated?**
No. This is a hand-tool set. Don't use these with an impact driver or you'll risk cracking the chrome and rounding fasteners.

**Q: Does Milwaukee warranty this set?**
Yes, it falls under Milwaukee's lifetime warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. The ratchet mechanism is covered, though normal wear on the chrome isn't.

**The Verdict**

The Milwaukee 48-22-9482 earns a **4.6 out of 5**. It's not the cheapest metric set on Amazon, but the ratchet quality and socket durability justify the price for anyone who uses tools more than twice a year. The case is the only real letdown, and the lack of SAE sizes is a predictable limitation, not a flaw.

**Buy it if:** You want a reliable, well-finished metric set with a ratchet that actually feels good in your hand, and you don't want to replace cheap sockets every 18 months.

**Skip it if:** You need SAE sizes, or you're a professional who needs a beefier 1/2-inch drive for heavy torque work.
