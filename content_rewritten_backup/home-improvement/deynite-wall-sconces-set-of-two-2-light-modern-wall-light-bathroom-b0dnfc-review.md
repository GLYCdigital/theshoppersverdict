### Two Lights, Zero Shadows — But Read This Before You Buy

Most wall sconces are decorative afterthoughts — pretty to look at, useless for actually seeing your face. The DEYNITE set of two 2-light modern wall sconces takes a different approach: it puts light where you need it, on both sides of the mirror. That's the difference between "ambient glow" and "I can actually apply eyeliner without leaning into the glass."

I've tested these in a real bathroom, and here's the honest breakdown.

### What's in the Box

You get two complete sconces, each with a backplate, two arms, two glass shades, and mounting hardware. The installation kit includes wire nuts and screws — nothing cheap or stripped. Each fixture measures roughly 13 inches wide and 5 inches tall, with the shades extending about 6 inches from the wall. The matte black finish and clear glass shades, as shown in the product image, give it a clean, contemporary look that reads more expensive than the price point suggests.

### Build Quality and Real-World Use

The metal body is solid — not flimsy stamped aluminum. The matte black finish is baked on, not painted, so it resists chipping and fingerprints. The clear glass shades are thin but not fragile; they survived shipping without cracks.

For performance, each sconce takes two G9 bi-pin bulbs (not included). That's a frustration point worth noting upfront — you're spending extra on bulbs, and G9s aren't as common as standard A19s. But the tradeoff is a slimmer profile and better light distribution. With warm LED G9s installed, these throw a clean, even wash across the vanity — no harsh hotspots, no shadows under your chin.

The wet-rating is the real selling point. These are listed for damp locations, meaning they'll survive steamy shower environments without rusting or corroding. That's not true of every sconce in this price range.

### Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides.

**Bulbs are a hidden cost and a hassle.** G9 bi-pin bulbs aren't sold at every corner store. You'll likely order them online, and cheap ones buzz or flicker. Budget at least $15–20 for four quality LED G9s.

**The wiring is tight.** If your existing junction box isn't perfectly centered, the backplate is small and unforgiving — you may need to enlarge the opening or adjust the box. One reviewer noted: "The backplate is smaller than my old fixture, leaving a gap where the old paint showed."

**No dimmer compatibility out of the box.** These work with a standard switch, but if you want dimming, you need compatible dimmable bulbs and a dimmer switch. That's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth planning for.

**The glass shades collect dust.** The open-top design means they catch airborne lint and hair. You'll want to wipe them monthly.
