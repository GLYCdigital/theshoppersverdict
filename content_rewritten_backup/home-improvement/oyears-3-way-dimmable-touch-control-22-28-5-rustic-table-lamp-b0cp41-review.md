Let’s cut through the product page fluff. The OYEARS 3-Way Dimmable Touch Control lamp solves a real problem: you want warm, adjustable light without a wall of switches, smart-home apps, or a pocketful of remote controls. At 4.5 stars from 225 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. Here's what that something is, and where it trips.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the lamp head, a wooden base, two metal pole sections, and a shade. Assembly takes about two minutes — you twist the poles together, screw the base on, and slip the shade over the socket. No tools required. The height adjustment is the headline feature: the pole telescopes from 22 inches up to 28.5 inches, which means it works as a bedside lamp on a nightstand or as a taller reading light next to a chair. As shown in the product image, the rustic finish is a mid-tone brown with visible grain texture — it reads more "modern farmhouse" than "antique," which is fine for most living rooms.

**Performance and build quality**

The touch controls sit on the metal pole, not the base. Tap once for on, cycle through brightness levels, or hold to dim continuously. The 3-way dimming is genuinely smooth — no jarring brightness jumps. A 100% to 10% sweep takes about three seconds, which is fast enough to be useful but slow enough to find your sweet spot. The light output depends entirely on the bulb you install (not included), but with a 9W dimmable LED, it comfortably lights up a 10x10 reading nook. The metal poles feel rigid, and the base has a decent heft — around 3 pounds — so it won't tip over during normal use. The fabric shade diffuses light well, cutting glare even at full brightness.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The adjustable height gets the most love. One reviewer wrote, *"I needed something that could work on my desk during the day and beside the couch at night. This does both without looking cheap."* Another noted the touch dimming was more reliable than expected: *"I've had touch lamps that were finicky. This one responds every time, even with slightly damp hands."* The aesthetic also scores points — multiple buyers mention that the rustic wood finish looks far more expensive than the price suggests.

**Where it falls short**

The touch sensor's placement is the main complaint. It's on the pole, which looks clean, but if you're reaching across the lamp to grab something, your forearm can brush it and cycle the light. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker. Also, the height adjustment uses a twist-lock collar that requires two hands to loosen and tighten. If you plan to change the height frequently, expect a few seconds of fiddling each time. And because the bulb isn't included, you'll need to buy a dimmable LED — a standard non-dimmable bulb will either flicker or only work at full power.

**Who should buy this**

- **Small-space dwellers** who need one lamp to serve multiple positions
- **Renters** who can't install hardwired lighting and want something portable
- **Anyone tired of losing lamp remotes** — the touch control is always there
- **Farmhouse/industrial decor fans** who want warm wood tones without the "big box store" look

**Three questions shoppers ask**

*Is the touch control reliable on the lowest setting?* Yes — the dimmer holds the low setting without flicker if you use a quality dimmable LED. Cheap bulbs may buzz at 10% brightness.

*Does the shade come in other colors?* As of this writing, the lamp ships with the beige fabric shade shown in the main product image. The base finish options are the only real variation.

*Can I use it with a smart plug?* Yes, but the touch control stays on the lamp — a smart plug will only cut power, not adjust brightness. You'll still use the touch sensor for dimming.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a versatile, good-looking lamp that adapts to different rooms and tasks, and you prefer tactile controls over app-based lighting.

**Skip it if** you need a lamp with a built-in USB port, want to preset specific brightness levels, or plan to move the lamp frequently — the twist-lock collar gets old if you're adjusting height daily.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A well-executed design with one minor ergonomic flaw. The adjustable height and smooth dimming are worth the price. Just buy a dimmable LED bulb alongside it.
