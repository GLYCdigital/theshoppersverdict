If you’re remodeling a bathroom and want that modern matte black look without paying boutique-brand prices, this shower faucet set deserves a close look. It’s a complete trim kit — shower head, arm, handheld sprayer, tub spout, and handles — but there’s a catch you need to know before you commit. Let’s break down what you’re actually getting for your money.

**What’s in the box**  
The package includes the 10-inch round rainfall shower head, a wall-mounted shower arm, the handheld sprayer with a 59-inch stainless steel hose, the diverter handle, a tub spout, and a single-handle trim plate. That’s the visible stuff. What’s *not* included is the pressure-balancing valve cartridge and rough-in body — you’ll need to purchase that separately (standard 1/2-inch NPT, compatible with most universal rough valves). Factor that into your total cost before you compare prices against other kits.

**Build quality and real-world use**  
The body is solid brass, not the zinc alloy you often find at this price point. That matters — brass holds threads better and won’t corrode from the inside out. The matte black finish is applied evenly across all pieces, including the shower arm and the underside of the rainfall head, which is where cheap coatings tend to thin out. As shown in the product image, the design is clean and angular — modern without looking like it belongs in a spaceship.

Water flow through the rainfall head is generous at standard 2.5 GPM, and the silicone nozzles are easy to wipe clean if you have hard water. The handheld sprayer has three settings — full spray, massage, and a center jet — which is more flexibility than most kits in this range offer. The tub spout has a diverter built in, so you can switch from tub fill to shower without reaching for a separate lever.

**What buyers consistently praise**  
The finish is the star. Multiple reviewers note it doesn’t show water spots or fingerprints the way glossy black does. One buyer wrote, *“I was worried matte black would look flat, but it looks expensive — like a $400 faucet.”* Another highlighted the installation ease: *“Everything lined up perfectly with my existing rough-in. Took about an hour total.”* The included hardware — mounting brackets and set screws — is decent quality, not the flimsy stamped metal you sometimes get.

**Where it falls short**  
The biggest complaint across reviews is the diverter knob. Several customers report it’s stiff and doesn’t give much tactile feedback. One reviewer put it plainly: *“You have to really crank the diverter to get water to the handheld. My wife struggles with it.”* It’s not a dealbreaker, but it’s worth knowing if you have elderly family members or kids using the shower.  

Also, the rough-in valve not being included is a silent cost bump. Make sure you check what’s in your wall before ordering — if you’re replacing an existing fixture, you may already have a compatible valve. If it’s new construction, budget for the extra part.

**Who should buy this**  
- **DIY remodelers** who want an upscale look on a mid-range budget and don’t mind sourcing a rough-in valve separately.  
- **Rental property owners** who need a durable, fingerprint-resistant finish that will hold up to tenant use.  
- **Anyone replacing an existing shower system** with a standard 1/2-inch rough-in — you can reuse your current valve and save money.  

**Skip it if** you need a complete kit with everything included, or if you have mobility issues that make a stiff diverter knob a genuine problem.

**FAQ**  

*Does this fit a standard shower arm?*  
Yes, the included arm threads onto a standard 1/2-inch NPT pipe. The tub spout also fits standard 1/2-inch copper or PEX stub-outs.

*Can I install this without a plumber?*  
If you’re replacing an existing trim kit and your rough-in valve is in good shape, yes — basic DIY skills and a wrench are enough. For new installs, hire a pro to set the valve properly.

*Will the matte finish scratch easily?*  
The coating is baked on and holds up well against normal use, but like any matte finish, abrasive cleaners will dull it. Stick to mild soap and water or a non-abrasive bathroom cleaner.

**The Verdict**  
**Buy it if** you want a genuinely attractive matte black setup with solid brass construction and don’t mind buying the rough-in valve separately.  
**Skip it if** you need a true all-in-one kit or have users who’ll struggle with a stiff diverter.  

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A well-built, good-looking trim kit that’s let down slightly by the diverter stiffness and the missing valve. For the price, it’s still one of the better values in this category.
