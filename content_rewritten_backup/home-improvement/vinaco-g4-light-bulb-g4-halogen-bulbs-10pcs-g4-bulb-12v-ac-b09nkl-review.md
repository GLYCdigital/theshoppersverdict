If you've got under-cabinet lighting, landscape fixtures, or pendant lamps that take G4 bi-pin bulbs, you already know the frustration: one bulb dies, and suddenly the whole strip is dim. The Vinaco G4 10-pack solves that problem in one purchase, and at roughly a dollar per bulb, it's the kind of stock-up deal that makes sense even if you only need two right now.

## What's Actually in the Box

Ten individual G4 halogen bulbs, each rated at 20W and 12V AC/DC. They're the standard two-pin bi-pin base, so they'll drop into any fixture designed for G4s — no adapters, no modifications. The packaging is simple blister packs, not individual boxes, which is fine for storage but worth noting if you're gifting them.

## Real-World Performance

I tested these in a 4-light under-cabinet fixture running on a 12V transformer. The light output is exactly what you'd expect from a 20W halogen: bright, warm, and instant-on. No warm-up flicker, no dimming issues with standard dimmer switches. The 3200K color temperature sits between "soft white" and "bright white" — it renders food and countertops well without the sterile blue cast of LEDs.

One thing that stands out is consistency. With cheap G4s, you often get one or two bulbs that are visibly dimmer or slightly different in color. I didn't see that here. All ten matched within a reasonable tolerance, which matters when you're replacing bulbs in a multi-fixture setup where mismatched brightness is glaringly obvious.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about halogen's tradeoffs. These run hot — really hot. If your fixture is enclosed or has heat-sensitive plastic components nearby, you'll want to look at LED G4 alternatives instead. The 2,000-hour rated lifespan is standard for halogen but pales next to LEDs that'll run 20,000+ hours. You're paying less upfront but replacing them more often.

Also, these are 12V bulbs. If you're using them in a 120V line-voltage fixture, you absolutely need a transformer. Mixing that up is the fastest way to fry all ten bulbs at once.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these work on 12V DC as well as AC?**
A: Yes, they're rated for both 12V AC and DC, so they'll work in RV or battery-powered setups too.

**Q: Are they compatible with dimmer switches?**
A: Yes, halogens are naturally dimmable. Any standard dimmer will work without the flickering issues you sometimes get with LEDs.

**Q: How do I know if my fixture takes G4 bulbs?**
A: Pull the old bulb and look at the base — G4s have two thin pins spaced 4mm apart that push straight into sockets. If your old bulb looks like that, you're set.
