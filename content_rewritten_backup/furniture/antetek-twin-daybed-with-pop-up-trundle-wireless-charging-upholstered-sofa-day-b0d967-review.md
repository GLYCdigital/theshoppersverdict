If you're shopping for a daybed that pulls double (or triple) duty — guest bed, living room seating, kids' sleepover station — the Antetek Twin Daybed with Pop Up Trundle is worth a hard look. The headline feature here isn't just the trundle; it's that this trundle pops *up* to match the main bed's height, so you're not putting a guest on a mattress that sits six inches off the floor like most trundles. That single design choice separates this from a dozen cheaper competitors.

## What You Actually Get

The box delivers the main twin daybed frame, the pop-up trundle mechanism, a beige upholstered headboard/backrest, and the armrests — one of which houses the wireless charging pad and two USB ports. You're not getting mattresses, which is worth noting upfront. The frame itself is steel with a fabric upholstery that has a soft, linen-like texture. As shown in the product image, the clean lines and neutral beige make it easy to blend into most rooms without looking like a dorm-room afterthought.

## Real-World Performance

Setting this up is the first hurdle. Budget a solid hour and have a second person for the trundle mechanism — it's heavy and awkward solo. The instructions are basic line drawings, so if you're not handy, expect some head-scratching. Once assembled, the build quality justifies the effort. The frame doesn't creak when you sit on the edge, and the pop-up trundle locks into place securely with a simple lift-and-pull motion. No pinch points, which matters if kids will be operating it.

The wireless charging is the surprise hit. It's integrated into the armrest, so you just drop your phone there and it charges — no fumbling with cables. It worked reliably with both iPhone and Android devices in testing, and the USB ports are handy for tablets or e-readers. Just know the charger needs its own AC adapter plugged into a wall outlet, which limits where you can position the bed if you want that feature live.

## What Buyers Praise

The 4.2-star average across 210 ratings reflects genuine satisfaction. Owners consistently mention the trundle height as the deciding factor. One buyer wrote, "My mom can actually get in and out of this trundle without feeling like she's sleeping on the floor." Another liked the dual-purpose aspect: "It's a couch by day, and when the grandkids visit, both beds are ready in under a minute." The charging station gets frequent shout-outs too, with several noting it's the feature that sold them.

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty time. The upholstery is a lint and pet-hair magnet — if you have a shedding dog, you'll be vacuuming this weekly. The fabric also shows water spots if someone sets a wet glass down, so consider a throw blanket for the seating area. The assembly instructions are thin, and the hardware comes in unlabeled bags, so sorting takes patience. And while the frame is rated for 600 pounds combined, the mattresses you'll buy separately need to be 6–8 inches thick for the trundle to sit flush — thinner ones leave a visible gap.

## Who Should Buy This

This is ideal for three specific buyers: parents setting up a kids' room that needs to host sleepovers, homeowners furnishing a home office that occasionally becomes a guest room, and anyone with older relatives who can't manage a floor-level trundle. If you're a minimalist who wants a pure sofa with no bed function, you're paying for features you won't use — look elsewhere.

## FAQ

**Do I need to buy mattresses separately?**
Yes. The frame requires two twin mattresses (main + trundle). Go with 6-inch minimum thickness for the trundle to sit flush with the main bed.

**Can the trundle stay popped up permanently?**
It can, but the trundle mattress will be exposed. It's designed for temporary use — pop it up for sleep, drop it for seating space.

**Is the wireless charger compatible with MagSafe?**
It charges any Qi-compatible phone, but it's not MagSafe-certified, so iPhones won't magnetically snap into place — they'll just sit on the pad.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a space-saving two-sleeper that doesn't treat guests like they're camping. The pop-up trundle, integrated charging, and sturdy frame make it a smart multi-purpose purchase.

**Skip it if** you can't handle a 60-minute assembly with vague instructions, or if you have heavy-shedding pets that will make the upholstery a constant chore.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A genuinely useful daybed that solves the trundle-height problem, with minor compromises in fabric maintenance and assembly friction that keep it from perfection.
