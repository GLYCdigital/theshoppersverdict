## The mobile file cabinet that actually earns its wheels

Most "rolling" storage cabinets treat wheels as an afterthought — four cheap casters that wobble and catch on every seam. The Fesbos Metal Storage Cabinet takes a different approach, and that's why it's sitting at 4.6 stars across 229 ratings. This is a genuinely mobile steel cabinet that doesn't sacrifice security or build quality for the convenience of wheels.

## What you actually get

The Fesbos arrives as a flat-pack kit. Inside the box you'll find the steel body panels, two height-adjustable shelves, four caster wheels, a locking door with a two-key set, and the necessary hardware. The cabinet measures roughly 31.5 inches tall, 15.7 inches wide, and 15.7 inches deep — compact enough to tuck under a desk or beside a filing cabinet, but substantial enough to hold letter-size hanging files or a small tower of office supplies.

The all-steel construction is the headline here. The powder-coated finish feels durable, not like the thin, chalky paint you see on bargain furniture. As shown in the product image, the clean lines and matte finish let it blend into a home office without screaming "industrial storage."

## Build quality and real-world performance

Let's talk about what 229 buyers actually experienced. The dominant theme in positive reviews is the wheel system. Four locking casters — two with brakes — let you roll this cabinet exactly where you need it, then lock it in place. One buyer noted, "I move this between my home office and the living room when I need to declutter. The brakes actually hold on hardwood." That's the kind of specific, practical feedback that tells you the design works.

The locking door is another strong point. It uses a cam lock with two stamped keys, and the door closes flush with no gaps. For storing sensitive documents or tools, it's genuinely secure for a cabinet in this price tier. The adjustable shelves — you can set them at two different heights — give you flexibility for everything from hanging files to a compact printer.

## Where it falls short

The assembly is the most common complaint, and it's legitimate. Expect 30–45 minutes of work. The included hex wrench is borderline useless — it strips under pressure. Buyers consistently recommend having your own Allen key set on hand. One reviewer put it plainly: "The cabinet is great, but the included tools are garbage. Use your own and it's a 20-minute build."

The keys themselves feel like an afterthought. They're thin stamped metal, the kind you'd get with a cheap lockbox. They work fine, but they won't survive being tossed in a drawer with loose change. And the wheels, while functional, are on the smaller side — about two inches in diameter. They roll smoothly on hard floors, but thick carpet or a raised door threshold will require a firm push.

## Who should buy this

This cabinet fits three specific buyers well:

- **Home office workers** who need secure, rolling storage that can tuck under a desk during the day and roll out for evening cleanup.
- **Garage or workshop owners** who want lockable tool storage that moves with the work — no more running back to a fixed cabinet.
- **Apartment dwellers** with limited floor space who need one piece of furniture that can serve as a side table, file cabinet, and lockable storage in one.

## FAQ

**Can it hold hanging file folders?**
Yes. The interior depth is sufficient for letter-size hanging files, and the adjustable shelves let you create a dedicated file section. Just note the cabinet is 15.7 inches deep, so legal-size files won't fit.

**How much weight can it handle?**
The steel frame and shelves handle a reasonable load — think a full box of paper or a small power drill set. It's not built for heavy machinery, but for office and light workshop duty, it's solid.

**Does the door lock securely?**
Yes, the cam lock engages firmly. It's not a high-security safe, but it will keep kids, curious visitors, and casual snooping out.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a genuinely mobile, lockable storage cabinet for a home office, workshop, or dorm room, and you're comfortable with a 30-minute assembly session.

**Skip it if** you're looking for a heavy-duty garage cabinet for power tools, or if you want zero assembly — order something pre-built and pay more for the privilege.

The Fesbos Metal Storage Cabinet delivers on its core promise: secure, mobile, steel storage at a price that makes sense. The assembly tools are weak, and the keys are flimsy, but the cabinet itself is built to last. At 4.6 stars with consistent buyer satisfaction, this is a solid pick.
