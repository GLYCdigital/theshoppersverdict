# BTHFST LED TV Stand: Power Where You Need It, But Bring Patience

Most TV stands in this price range have one job: hold the TV without collapsing. The BTHFST LED TV Stand tries to do more — and mostly succeeds. With a 4.2-star average across 210 ratings, it's clearly working for most buyers. But the assembly process will test your patience, and the finish demands more upkeep than you'd expect.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes the stand, two side shelves, a center console with two cabinet doors, and a back panel with integrated power. The power unit is the headline feature: two AC outlets and two USB ports built into the rear panel. No visible wiring, no separate brick, no ugly power strip taped to the back.

The unit measures 60 inches wide, 23.6 inches high, and 15.7 inches deep — sized for 65-70 inch TVs, though it works fine with smaller sets. Weight capacity is rated at 132 pounds for the top surface, which is genuinely enough for most modern TVs. The assembled footprint is stable, though the tempered glass shelves feel thinner than the photos suggest.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The power outlets work as advertised. One buyer noted, "Finally no more extension cords snaking behind my media cabinet. The USB ports charge my phone and controller without an adapter." The cable management holes are positioned at the back corners, and the rear panel has generous cutouts — enough to run HDMI, optical, and power cables without cramming.

The cabinet doors close smoothly with soft-close hinges, though the magnetic catch is stronger than necessary. You'll need a firm pull to open them. The center shelf inside the cabinet is adjustable, but there are only two height positions — not five like some competitors offer. If you're planning to store a cable box and a game console stacked, you'll want to measure carefully.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The power integration is the most-cited reason for purchase. As one verified buyer put it: "The built-in outlets are a game changer. I mounted my soundbar on the shelf below and everything stays hidden." Another common theme is the modern, low-profile design — it doesn't dominate the room.

The 132-pound capacity is also mentioned frequently as a reason for confidence. Several reviewers with 65-inch OLEDs (which run heavy) reported no sagging or wobble after months of use.

## Where It Falls Short

Assembly is the biggest complaint. The instructions are adequate but not generous — some steps reference parts by letter codes that aren't printed clearly on the hardware. Plan for 45-60 minutes if you're methodical, longer if you're not. The included hex wrench is the standard cheap L-shaped tool that will strip if you over-tighten. A real Allen key set will save you grief.

The glossy black finish is a dust magnet. One reviewer wrote, "It looks premium for about 20 minutes after I clean it." If you have kids or pets, expect to wipe it down weekly.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Cord-haters**: If your entertainment setup is a spaghetti factory behind the TV, the built-in power and cable routing will change your life.
- **Soundbar users**: The center shelf is sized to fit most soundbars, and the cable holes line up well for a clean install.
- **First-time TV stand buyers**: The price-to-feature ratio is solid, provided you're patient with assembly.

**Skip it if** you want a wood-look finish (this is glossy black only), need adjustable-height shelves beyond two positions, or you're replacing a stand that already has built-in power — the extra outlets won't be worth the assembly effort.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this stand fit a 65-inch TV with a center-mounted base?**
A: The top surface is 60 inches wide, so yes for most center-base TVs. The included mounting bracket works with VESA patterns up to 600x400, but some buyers report misalignment with 400x400 patterns. Measure your TV's VESA holes before ordering.

**Q: Can the power outlets handle a TV and a soundbar at once?**
A: Yes. The two AC outlets are rated for standard household loads (15A total). A TV and soundbar draw well under that. The USB ports output 5V/2A combined — fine for phones, not for tablets under heavy load.

**Q: Is the tempered glass shelf safe for heavy components?**
A: The side shelves are rated for 22 pounds each. A receiver or game console is fine. A center-channel speaker heavier than 20 pounds should go on the top surface instead.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're tired of extension cords and want a clean, modern media setup with built-in power. The 4.2-star rating holds up — this is a genuinely useful product with two real flaws: assembly effort and finish maintenance.

**Skip it if** you want a wood-grain look, need flexible shelf heights, or you're not willing to spend an hour with a screwdriver.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid value for the features, held back by assembly friction and the glossy finish. If BTHFST offered a matte version, this would be a 4.6.
