Let me be direct about this table: it's solving one very specific problem — where to put your phone, coffee, and charging cables in a space that can't fit a normal table. And for that job, it does nearly everything right.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the table top, two side panels, a lower shelf, the hardware bag, and a 6-foot power cord with a grounded 3-prong plug. The surface has one standard AC outlet and two USB-A ports built into the side edge — not the top, which is smart. You won't spill coffee into the sockets.

Assembly is straightforward. The panels are pre-drilled, and the included Allen wrench and screwdriver work fine. Budget 15-20 minutes. One buyer noted, *"Took me longer to unbox it than to put it together — everything lined up perfectly."*

## Performance and Build Quality

The dimensions are the headline: 5.9 inches deep, 31.5 inches tall, and available in 40-, 48-, or 55-inch widths. That sub-6-inch depth is critical. Standard consoles are 12-14 inches deep, which means they stick out awkwardly in hallways or crowd a sofa. This one hugs the wall.

The charging setup is genuinely well-executed. The outlet sits flush on the right side panel, and the two USB ports sit right next to it. You can charge a phone and a tablet simultaneously while keeping the cord routed behind the table leg. The 6-foot cord is adequate for most rooms, but measure your outlet-to-table distance before ordering. Multiple reviewers mentioned using a short extension cord to bridge the gap.

Build quality is good for the price point, but it's not heirloom furniture. The MDF core with a walnut or black laminate finish looks convincing from three feet away. The lower shelf handles up to 15 pounds; the top takes about 25. Don't put a heavy monitor on it and expect stability — this is a decor and convenience piece, not a workhorse desk.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The rating breakdown (229 ratings at 4.6 stars) tells a consistent story. Reviewers repeatedly mention three things:

1. **The slim profile** — *"Finally a table that fits the 6-inch gap between my sofa and wall. I've been looking for months."*

3. **Assembly ease** — *"No stripped screws, no misaligned holes. It just works."*

## Where It Falls Short

The 31.5-inch height is the most common complaint. Standard console height is 36 inches, so this table sits lower. If your sofa back is tall (36+ inches), the table top will be hidden behind it. That's fine if you only need the surface for a lamp and charging, but it's a problem if you want to see items displayed on it.

The MDF construction means you need coasters. One reviewer wrote, *"Left a cold drink on it for an hour and there's a white ring now."* That's not a defect — it's the material. If you expect solid wood at this price, you'll be disappointed.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Apartment dwellers** with narrow hallways or limited floor space
- **Anyone who charges devices near a sofa** and wants cords off the floor
- **Renters** who need a lightweight, easy-to-move piece that won't damage walls

Skip it if you need a full-height console, plan to display heavy items, or want a piece that will survive a decade of heavy use.

## FAQ

**Q: Can the outlet handle a laptop charger?**
Yes. The AC outlet is rated for standard 15-amp household current. A laptop brick, phone charger, and lamp simultaneously are fine. Don't daisy-chain power strips or high-draw appliances.

**Q: Does it come in other widths?**
Yes — 40, 48, and 55 inches. The 48-inch width is the most popular based on the review breakdown.

**Q: Is the USB port fast-charging?**
No. It's standard 5V/2.1A output. It'll charge your phone overnight but won't deliver rapid-charge speeds. Use the AC outlet with your own adapter for faster charging.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a genuinely slim table with integrated power and don't mind MDF construction. At 4.6 stars, this is a well-executed solution to a real spatial problem.

**Skip it if** you require standard 36-inch height, prefer solid wood, or need fast-charging USB ports.
