# LONXIU Bar Stools Set of 4 Review: Does This Budget 4-Pack Actually Deliver?

If you're outfitting a breakfast bar or kitchen island, a set of four stools under $200 sounds like a steal. But budget furniture often means wobbly legs, peeling finishes, and cursing during assembly. The LONXIU 28-inch counter height stools have earned a respectable 4.2 out of 5 from 210 Amazon ratings — so I dug into the specs and customer feedback to see if these actually hold up or just look good in the product photos.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains four identical stools, each with a solid rubberwood seat, a matte black powder-coated steel frame, and pre-drilled holes. The seats measure approximately 15.7 inches in diameter — roomy enough for a standard adult but not oversized. The overall height is 28 inches, which puts the seat surface around 28 inches from the floor. That's the counter height sweet spot, not bar height — a critical distinction.

Hardware, an Allen wrench, and a printed instruction sheet are included. No need for extra tools. Each stool assembles in about 10–15 minutes once you get the rhythm down. The instructions are straightforward, and the parts are labeled. One Amazon reviewer noted, *"Put all four together in under an hour while watching TV. Everything lined up perfectly."*

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The rubberwood seat is the standout feature here. It's a genuine hardwood, not MDF or particleboard, and the clear lacquer finish holds up against coffee rings and occasional knife slips. The steel frame is 1.2mm thick — not flimsy tubing — and the X-brace adds genuine lateral stability. I've seen stools double this price wobble on uneven floors; the LONXIU's non-slip foot pads do a decent job of compensating for minor irregularities.

Weight capacity is listed at 300 pounds per stool, which is reasonable for a 4-leg design. The seat attaches to the frame with four bolts per corner, and once torqued down, there's no rocking motion. One buyer with a 250-pound husband reported, *"He sits on these daily and they haven't budged. No creaking, no loose joints after three months."*

The footrest is a single horizontal bar connecting the front two legs. It's positioned about 7 inches below the seat. That's fine for average-height adults, but if you're under 5'6", your feet may dangle above it — a minor ergonomic annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 210 ratings, three themes dominate the positive feedback:

1. **Sturdiness** — Multiple reviewers mention the lack of wobble compared to cheaper sets.
2. **Ease of assembly** — The pre-drilled holes align correctly, and the included wrench is sufficient. One reviewer said, *"I'm not handy at all, and I did these myself in 20 minutes each."*
3. **Finish quality** — The wood seat looks more expensive than the price point suggests. *"Guests assumed these were from a high-end store,"* one buyer noted.

## Where It Falls Short

The fixed 28-inch height is the biggest limitation. If your counter or bar overhang is higher than 30 inches, these will feel too low. Measure your setup before ordering. This is the most common complaint in negative reviews — *"Didn't fit under my bar. Returned them."*

The footrest position is the second issue. At 7 inches below the seat, it's not adjustable. Taller users (6'+) may find their knees hit the underside of the counter when seated comfortably. This is a geometry issue, not a defect, but it's worth noting.

Finally, the black powder coating on the frame is durable but will chip if you bang the stools against hard edges during moving. Touch-up paint is cheap, but it's an extra step.

## Who Should Buy These

- **First-time apartment dwellers** needing a complete seating set without breaking the bank
- **Homeowners with standard 36-inch kitchen counters** who want a matching 4-pack
- **Anyone who values wood seats** over the cheap faux-leather or plastic alternatives at this price

Skip them if you have a **bar-height counter** (over 30 inches), need **stackable storage**, or prefer **padded seating** for long meals.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these be used outdoors on a covered patio?**
A: The rubberwood seat has a lacquer finish that's water-resistant, but the steel frame will rust if exposed to rain. Keep them indoors or in a fully covered area.

**Q: Do the stools scratch hardwood floors?**
A: The non-slip foot pads are rubber and protect floors adequately. However, if you drag the stools rather than lift them, the wood seat edges can scuff walls or baseboards.

**Q: What's the actual seat height from floor to top of the seat?**
A: It's 28 inches exactly. For a standard 36-inch counter with a 1.5-inch overhang, you'll have about 6.5 inches of leg clearance — comfortable for most adults.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a solid, wood-seated 4-pack for a standard counter height and value stability over frills. **Skip it if** your bar is higher than 30 inches, you're over 6'2", or you need padded comfort for extended sitting.

At 4.2/5 stars, the LONXIU set delivers exactly what it promises: sturdy, attractive, easy-to-assemble stools at a price that undercuts most competitors. The fixed height is the only real gotcha — measure first, then buy with confidence.
