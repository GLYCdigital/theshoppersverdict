**The Purple+ mattress is one of the most polarizing beds on Amazon.** Some shoppers swear it changed their sleep forever; others return it within the 100-night trial. At 4.2 stars from 210 ratings, the truth sits somewhere in the middle. Here's what you actually need to know before spending your money.

## What you're actually getting

The Purple+ is a hybrid mattress with a 2-inch GelFlex Grid comfort layer over a dense polyurethane foam support core. Unlike traditional memory foam, the grid works like a series of collapsible channels — it buckles under pressure points (hips, shoulders) while staying firm in areas that need support (lower back). The cover is a soft, stretchy polyester blend that's removable and machine-washable, which is a nice touch at this price point.

The bed measures 12 inches thick, arriving compressed in a box like most bed-in-a-box options. Unboxing takes about 10 minutes, though you should budget 24 hours for full expansion. As shown in the product image, the signature purple grid is unmistakable — you'll know immediately if this is your aesthetic.

## Real-world performance

**Pressure relief is the headline.** The GelFlex Grid genuinely delivers on its promise of no-pressure sleep. One verified buyer noted: "I'm a side sleeper with chronic shoulder pain, and this is the first mattress that doesn't have me waking up numb." The grid collapses exactly where your body needs it most, which is a fundamentally different feel than foam contouring.

**Cooling is excellent.** The open-air channels in the grid promote airflow that memory foam simply can't match. Multiple reviewers mention sleeping "cooler than any bed I've owned," and the breathable cover doesn't trap heat. If you're a hot sleeper, this is a legitimate differentiator.

**Motion isolation is surprisingly strong.** Despite not being a foam-heavy design, the grid absorbs movement well. As one reviewer put it: "My husband gets up at 5 AM for work and I don't feel a thing. That's the difference between being woken up and sleeping through everything."

## Where it falls short

The grid texture is the elephant in the room. It feels like nothing else — some call it "weird," others "like sleeping on a giant ice cube tray." It's not unpleasant, but it's definitely not the plush, sink-in feel most people expect from a premium mattress. If you prefer memory foam's hugging sensation, this will disappoint.

**Edge support is genuinely weak.** Sit on the edge to tie your shoes, and you'll slide off. There's no reinforced perimeter coil system, which is a significant omission at this price. Couples who need usable edge space should look elsewhere.

At 110+ pounds for a queen, this mattress is a beast to move. Multiple reviewers mention needing two people just to navigate stairs. Plan accordingly.

## Who should buy this

- **Hot sleepers** who've tried everything and still wake up sweating
- **Side sleepers** with shoulder or hip pain who need targeted pressure relief
- **Couples** with different schedules who value motion isolation
- **People who hate memory foam** and want something structurally different

**Skip it if** you want a traditional plush feel, need strong edge support, or prefer a firm, traditional innerspring sensation.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does the Purple+ take to break in?**
A: Expect 30 days. The grid softens slightly as it adapts to your body temperature and sleeping position. Most reviewers who initially found it "too firm" changed their minds by week two.

**Q: Is the Purple+ good for stomach sleepers?**
A: It depends on your weight. The grid provides enough support for average-weight stomach sleepers, but heavier individuals (250+ lbs) may find the support core insufficient for proper spinal alignment.

**Q: Does it sleep hot like other foam mattresses?**
A: No. The open grid structure is specifically designed for airflow. This is one of the few beds where "cooling" isn't marketing fluff — it's a genuine advantage.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're a hot, pain-prone sleeper who wants something genuinely different and doesn't need edge support.

**Skip it if** you want a traditional mattress feel, need usable edges, or aren't willing to commit to a 30-day adjustment period.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A genuinely innovative mattress for the right sleeper, held back by weak edge support and a feel that isn't for everyone.
