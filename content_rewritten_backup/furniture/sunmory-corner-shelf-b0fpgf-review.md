**The corner shelf that finally makes dead space useful**

That awkward corner where two walls meet is prime real estate you're probably ignoring. Most homes have at least one — usually in the bathroom or kitchen — where nothing fits because it's too narrow for furniture and too awkward for standard shelving. The SUNMORY Corner Shelf exists specifically to fix that problem, and with 235 ratings averaging 4.7 stars, it's clearly doing something right.

**What you actually get**

The package contains three bamboo shelves, metal support brackets, adhesive mounting strips, and a small level for alignment. The shelves measure roughly 12 inches across each side, forming a triangle that tucks neatly into a corner. It's a floating design — no floor footprint, no legs to trip over. The bamboo has a smooth, sealed finish that's visibly more refined than the cheap pine options in this price range. Assembly takes about ten minutes: peel, stick, wait, load.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

Bamboo is the right material choice here. It handles humidity better than particleboard and won't warp like MDF when exposed to steam from a hot shower. The corners are rounded, which matters more than you'd think when you're reaching into a tight space with wet hands. The metal brackets attach flush to the wall, and the shelves slot in with a satisfying click — no wobble once seated.

One buyer noted: *"I was skeptical about the adhesive strips, but after 48 hours this thing hasn't budged. I've got shampoo bottles and a heavy soap dispenser on it."* That tracks with my assessment. The 3M-style strips grip aggressively, but they demand patience. You need to clean the wall with rubbing alcohol, press firmly for 60 seconds per strip, and wait a full day before putting anything on the shelf. Skip that and you'll be picking bamboo off the floor.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The space-saving aspect dominates positive feedback. Multiple reviewers mention reclaiming shower caddies and clearing off sink counters. The three-tier layout gives you room for everything — towels on top, toiletries in the middle, smaller items below. The 15-pound per-shelf capacity is generous for bathroom use; you'd need to store cast iron pans there to hit the limit.

**Where it falls short**

The open-slat design is the main complaint. Small items — razor heads, makeup brushes, loose earrings — can slip through the gaps. Solution: put small things in baskets or trays before placing them on the shelf. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

Rental dwellers should note that removing the adhesive strips requires care. Pull them straight down (not outward) to avoid damaging paint. Several reviewers reported peeling paint when they rushed the removal. Follow the instructions and you're fine — ignore them and you're repainting.

The bamboo color is a medium honey tone. It won't match stark white modern bathrooms perfectly, but it works with most neutral palettes. If you're going for an all-white minimalist look, this might visually clash.

**Who should buy this**

You're the target buyer if you have a rental apartment and can't drill holes in walls. You're also the target if you're tired of knocking over bottles in a cramped shower. It's ideal for anyone with a small bathroom, a kitchen with wasted corner space, or a dorm room needing vertical storage. Skip it if you need to store heavy items (over 15 lbs per shelf) or if your walls have textured wallpaper — the adhesive won't bond properly.

**FAQ**

*Q: Can I mount this on tile?*
A: Yes, but you'll need the included strips to bond to clean, smooth tile. Grout lines can interfere with adhesion, so position the strips on flat tile surfaces.

*Q: How much weight can each shelf hold?*
A: The manufacturer rates each shelf at 15 pounds. Realistically, plan for 10-12 pounds to maintain a safety margin.

*Q: Does it come with screws for permanent mounting?*
A: No, it's adhesive-only. If you want screw mounting, you'll need to source hardware separately — the brackets don't have pre-drilled screw holes.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want to reclaim dead corner space without damaging walls or committing to permanent installation. It's well-built, looks more expensive than it is, and handles bathroom humidity without issue.

**Skip it if** you're impatient with installation, need to store tiny loose items, or have heavily textured walls.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A genuinely useful space-saver that does exactly what it promises, with only minor design tradeoffs holding it back from perfection.
