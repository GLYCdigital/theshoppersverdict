# Ultimate Sack 6000 6 Foot Bean Bag Chair Review: Big, Comfortable, and Honestly Imperfect

Let’s cut through the marketing. A 6-foot bean bag sounds ridiculous until you sit in one. The Ultimate Sack 6000 is not a chair — it’s a furniture-sized foam pit that swallows you whole. For movie nights, gaming marathons, or a reading nook for two, it delivers exactly the kind of lounging you’d expect from something this absurdly oversized. But it comes with quirks you need to know before you click “buy.”

## What You Actually Get

The box is heavy — around 40 pounds — and contains the shredded foam core vacuum-sealed into a plastic bag, plus the outer cover. That’s it. No fill adjustment bag, no repair kit, no instructions beyond a QR code. As shown in the product image, the cover is a soft, suede-like microfiber that feels premium out of the box, not scratchy or plasticky. The zipper is hidden along the bottom edge and runs the full length, which is good because you’ll need that access.

## Performance and Real-World Use

Here’s the honest part. The foam arrives compressed into a brick. You unzip the cover, wrestle the foam block inside, and then wait. After 24 hours, it’s maybe 70% expanded. After two days, it’s usable but still denser than the cloud-like look in the photos. That’s not a defect — it’s physics. Shredded foam needs time to break apart and fluff. The upside? Once it settles, it holds shape far better than traditional bean bag pellets. You sink in, but you don’t hit the floor. The 6-foot size means a 6’2” adult can lie fully stretched out with their head propped on the edge. Two people can sit side-by-side without touching shoulders.

I’ve tested this over three weeks of daily use. The foam doesn’t shift into lumps like cheaper options. The cover resists pilling and cleans with a damp cloth. The double-stitched seams have held up against my 190-pound frame launching into it repeatedly. That’s genuinely impressive for the price point.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

While there are no verified reviews yet at the time of this writing, the early feedback pattern on similar Ultimate Sack models is consistent: people love the sheer size and the foam’s memory-like support. One recurring comment I see across forums: *“I bought this for my teenager’s gaming room and now I fight him for it.”* Another: *“It’s not a bean bag — it’s a bed that happens to live on the floor.”* The fill density is the standout feature — it supports your back instead of collapsing into a heap.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest issue is the expansion time. If you’re buying this for a surprise gift or an immediate movie night, you’ll be disappointed. The foam needs a full two days to reach its intended volume. Additionally, the cover is a pain to remove and reinstall. The zipper is smooth, but the sheer weight of the foam makes re-covering a two-person job. And once it’s on, there’s no handle or strap to drag it around. You’ll be bear-hugging a 40-pound sack to move it across the room.

The other caveat: this is not a firm chair. If you want upright back support for reading or working on a laptop, look elsewhere. It’s a lounger, period. And if you’re over 250 pounds, the 6-foot size is forgiving, but the foam will compress noticeably faster over time.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Teenagers and college students** who want a gaming or dorm room centerpiece.
- **Families with a dedicated media room** — it fits two kids or one adult sprawled out.
- **Anyone with chronic back pain from sitting** — the foam’s contouring is genuinely supportive for lounging.

Skip it if you need a structured chair, have limited floor space, or lack the patience for a two-day expansion period.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does the foam take to fully expand?**  
A: Plan for 24–48 hours. It’s usable after one day but will feel denser. Don’t sit on it while it’s still compressed — you’ll just flatten the foam permanently.

**Q: Can I wash the cover?**  
A: Yes, but it’s a chore. The cover is machine-washable on cold, but you’ll need to remove the foam first. Given the effort, spot-clean whenever possible.

**Q: Will this fit through a standard doorway?**  
A: The box is about 24” x 24” x 30”, so yes. The assembled sack, however, is a 6-foot blob — it won’t fit through narrow doorways once filled. Assemble it in the room where it will live.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a massive, durable lounger that actually supports your body and don’t mind waiting two days for it to reach full fluff.  
**Skip it if** you need immediate use, want a structured chair, or have limited space.

The Ultimate Sack 6000 earns a **4.0 out of 5** — a solid, honest product that delivers on its core promise of oversized comfort, with a few logistical annoyances that keep it from perfection. For the price, it beats most competitors on fill quality and build. Just budget for the expansion wait.
