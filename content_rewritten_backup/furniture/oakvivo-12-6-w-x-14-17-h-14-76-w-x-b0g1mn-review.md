Let me be straight with you: $88 for two round side tables sounds suspiciously cheap. But after digging through 240 Amazon ratings sitting at 4.8 stars, the oakvivo nesting set is one of those rare budget finds that actually delivers.

**What you're actually getting**

The set includes two round tables — a larger one at 14.76" diameter with a 17.32" height, and a smaller companion at 12.6" wide and 14.17" tall. The smaller nests completely underneath the larger, turning the pair into one compact footprint roughly the size of a single side table. That's the whole selling point, and it works exactly as shown in the product images.

Each table comes with four screw-in legs, a top, and a lower storage shelf. The boxes arrive with the legs separate, but assembly is genuinely painless — one buyer noted, "I put both tables together in about 8 minutes while watching TV." You'll need nothing more than the included Allen wrench.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The tops are made from MDF with a printed wood-grain finish. Let's not pretend this is solid oak — it's not. But the finish does a respectable job mimicking a warm walnut tone, and it's sealed well enough to resist water rings from sweating glasses. The lower shelf is a nice touch, giving you a spot for magazines or a basket without cluttering the top surface.

Stability is where budget tables usually fall apart, but oakvivo got this right. The legs are thick enough that there's no wobble on carpet, and the cross-bracing on the shelf adds rigidity. One reviewer said, "I was worried they'd feel cheap, but the weight surprised me — these are sturdy enough to hold my lamp and a stack of books."

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the reviews, three themes keep coming up:

- **Versatility**: The different heights work perfectly beside a sofa and armchair. Buyers frequently mention using the smaller one as a plant stand or nightstand.
- **Space-saving design**: "I live in a studio apartment and these are a godsend. When guests come over, I pull both out. When they leave, they nest into one."
- **Value for money**: At the price point, most reviewers feel the quality exceeds expectations. The finish photographs well and looks more expensive than it is in dimmer lighting.

**Where it falls short**

The most common complaint is the lack of felt pads on the leg bottoms. The bare metal feet can scratch hardwood or tile if you slide the tables around. A $3 pack of adhesive felt pads solves this — buy them at the same time you order.

Second, the printed grain finish won't pass a close-up inspection. If you run your hand across the surface, it feels smooth rather than textured like real wood. It's a minor thing at this price, but don't expect heirloom furniture.

Third, the smaller table's height (14.17") puts it slightly below standard sofa arm height. If you're using it as a dedicated drink table, it works fine. If you want it flush with your couch cushions, measure first.

**Who should buy this**

- **Apartment dwellers** who need flexible furniture that doesn't dominate a room
- **First-time furniture buyers** furnishing a living space on a budget
- **Anyone needing a temporary solution** — guest rooms, rental properties, dorm common areas

**Skip it if** you need a single statement piece with real wood construction, or if you're placing these on unprotected hardwood floors without adding felt pads.

**FAQ**

Yes — the design accounts for this. The smaller table tucks fully beneath the larger, and when nested, the combined footprint is about 15 inches wide.

**Q: Are these tables stable enough for a heavy lamp?**
Yes. The MDF tops handle 20-30 pounds without bowing. One reviewer mentioned using the larger table as a printer stand.

**Q: Do the shelves come pre-attached?**
No, everything requires assembly, but it's minimal — four legs per table plus the shelf screws. No power tools needed.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5**

The oakvivo nesting table set earns its strong rating through smart design, solid-enough construction, and a price that's hard to argue with. It's not fine furniture, and it won't fool a woodworker. But as a functional, good-looking pair of side tables that adapt to your space, it punches well above its $88 price tag.

**Buy it if** you need versatile, space-saving tables that look decent and hold up to daily use without breaking the bank.
