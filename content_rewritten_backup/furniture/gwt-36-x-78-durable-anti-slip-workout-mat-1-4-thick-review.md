## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and the GWT mat has a few honest drawbacks. First, it's heavy. At roughly 20 pounds, moving it once it's down is a chore. If you're planning to roll it up after each workout, look elsewhere — this is a "set it and forget it" mat.

Second, the surface shows scuffs. Dark-soled shoes leave marks that require wiping down. It's cosmetic, not functional, but if you're particular about appearances, factor that in.

Finally, this is not a yoga mat. The firmness and grip that make it great for equipment are wrong for yoga practice. You'd want a dedicated yoga mat for that — this one is strictly for protecting your floor from machines and heavy free weights.
