# Lxsxlome 64" Tall Bathroom Storage Cabinet: Real Talk on Whether This Slim Tower Earns Its Spot

Bathroom storage is a puzzle. You need vertical space, but most cabinets are either too wide for the gap next to your toilet or too short to actually clear the clutter. The Lxsxlome 64-inch tall cabinet attacks that problem head-on with a narrow footprint and a full six feet of height. At 4.3 stars from 215 ratings, it's clearly working for a lot of people. But is it the right solution for your bathroom? Let's dig into what you're actually getting.

## What's in the Box

You get the expected flat-pack experience: one main cabinet body, four adjustable shelves, a back panel, hardware bag, and the arched doors (two, if you ordered the double-door version). The dimensions are the headline here — roughly 64 inches tall, 15.7 inches wide, and 15.7 inches deep. That's a true space-saver footprint that slots into that awkward 16-inch gap most bathrooms have.

The finish options include a clean white and a wood-tone that'll match most vanities. The arched door detail is the design element that sets this apart from a dozen other tall cabinets at the same price point — it softens the look and makes it feel less like a utility closet and more like intentional furniture.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Here's where the Lxsxlome earns its keep. The four adjustable shelves are the standout feature. You're not locked into fixed heights, which means you can configure the interior to hold tall shampoo bottles, stacked towels, or a small basket system. One buyer noted, "I fit everything that was cluttering my countertop and still have room to spare." That's the practical reality most shoppers care about.

The cabinet comes with a wall-anchoring kit, and you should use it. At 64 inches tall with a narrow base, it can tip forward if you load the top shelf heavily with kids reaching for things. That's not a flaw — it's physics — but it's worth taking seriously.

The arched doors open smoothly with soft hinges, and the magnetic closure keeps them shut. The top surface is flat and sturdy enough to hold a basket of toiletries or a decorative plant.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Reading through the 215 ratings, a few themes stand out:

- **Space efficiency**: Multiple reviewers mention how much floor space they reclaimed. One wrote, "This fits perfectly between my toilet and wall — I didn't think anything would work there."
- **Visual appeal**: The arched doors get consistent compliments. "Looks way more expensive than it is," is a recurring sentiment.
- **Storage versatility**: The adjustable shelves are the feature people actually use, not just a spec-sheet checkbox.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. Assembly is the biggest complaint. The particle board construction means screw holes can strip if you over-tighten, and several reviewers note the back panel is fragile. Plan for 60-90 minutes of assembly, and have a drill with a clutch setting handy.

The material is the other consideration. This is engineered wood, not solid timber. It's fine for a bathroom, but you need to keep it away from direct water contact. Moisture pooling at the base will cause swelling over time. A small silicone mat under the feet or wiping up splashes promptly will extend its life significantly.

The depth is tight for some items — large shampoo bottles or bulk toilet paper rolls might not fit lying flat. Check your storage needs before committing.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Small bathroom owners**: If you're working with limited floor space and need vertical storage, this is a strong candidate.
- **Renters**: The neutral finishes and reasonable price make it a smart investment that you won't feel bad leaving behind.
- **Anyone drowning in counter clutter**: If your vanity is a disaster zone, this gives you a dedicated home for the overflow.

Skip it if you need heavy-duty, solid-wood furniture that can take a beating, or if you're not comfortable with a moderately challenging assembly project.

**Q: Does the cabinet come with wall anchors?**
