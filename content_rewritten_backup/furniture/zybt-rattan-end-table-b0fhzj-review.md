The ZYBT Rattan End Table is the kind of piece that looks great in the listing photos and mostly delivers in person — but with a few caveats worth knowing before you hit buy. At a 3.8-star average across 190 ratings, it's clear this table has found an audience. It's also clear that audience has some mixed feelings.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the tabletop with the rattan weave already set into a solid wood frame, four legs, and the mounting hardware. That's it. No tools, no felt pads, no assembly guide beyond a single instruction sheet. The table measures roughly 16 inches square with a 20-inch height — compact enough for a tight corner beside a sofa or bed, but not so small it disappears.

The rattan itself is the headline feature. This is genuine hand-woven material, not the printed vinyl or pressed-cane shortcut you see on cheaper knockoffs. The weave is tight and consistent across the surface, with a natural warm honey tone that fits boho, coastal, or Scandinavian interiors without clashing.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

Assembly genuinely takes about ten minutes. The legs screw into pre-drilled inserts with included Allen bolts, and the fit is precise across the units we've seen reported. One buyer noted, "Took me longer to unbox it than to put it together," which matches the general consensus.

The weight capacity is the practical concern here. The tabletop is sturdy enough for a lamp, a book, and a coffee mug — but don't treat it like a stool. Several reviewers report the frame feeling slightly wobbly under heavier loads, particularly if you lean on it. This is a side table, not a step stool, and it performs best when you respect that boundary.

The finish is where the 3.8 rating starts making sense. The legs carry a lacquer coat that looks nice in photos but feels slightly sticky for the first week or two. It does settle down with use, but expect to wipe fingerprints off regularly during that break-in period. More importantly, those bare wood feet will scratch hardwood floors. Budget for felt pads or plan to place it on carpet.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The rattan weave quality is the most-cited positive across reviews. "The weave looks expensive — I've had friends ask where I got it," one verified buyer wrote. Another noted the color accuracy: "Matches the photos exactly, not too yellow, not too orange."

The size also earns consistent compliments. It's small enough to fit awkward spaces without dominating, but the 16-inch surface handles a drinks coaster and a phone charger simultaneously. For apartments or rooms with limited floor space, that footprint is a real advantage.

## Where It Falls Short

Color consistency between batches is the most common complaint. Some units arrive with a lighter, almost blonde rattan while others trend darker honey. If you're ordering two for symmetry, that's a gamble — order them together and be prepared to return one if they don't match.

The lacquer issue deserves a mention too. Reviewers describe the legs as "looking wet" for the first week, and one noted the finish "sweated" in a humid room. It clears up, but it's not the polished experience you'd get from a $200+ table. At this price point, though, it's a fair trade.

## Who Should Buy This

This table suits three specific buyers: apartment dwellers needing a compact accent piece, anyone decorating on a boho or coastal theme who wants authentic texture without paying boutique prices, and renters who need furniture that's easy to move and assemble without tools.

Skip it if you need a heavy-duty surface, if you're pairing two tables and demand perfect color matching, or if you have unprotected hardwood floors and won't add felt pads.

## FAQ

**Is the rattan top real or synthetic?**
Real. It's hand-woven natural rattan over a solid wood frame. No vinyl or printed imitation here.

**Will this hold a heavy lamp or books?**
It handles normal side-table duty fine — a lamp, a stack of magazines, a drink. Don't lean on it or use it as seating. The frame is sturdy but not load-bearing.

**Does it work outdoors?**
The natural rattan and lacquered legs aren't rated for outdoor exposure. Keep it inside or on a covered porch only.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want authentic rattan texture at a budget-friendly price and don't mind adding your own floor protection. **Skip it if** you need a matching set with guaranteed color consistency or a table that can take heavier use.

This is a good table for the money, not a great one. The weave quality punches above the price point, but the finish quirks and batch variation keep it from being a slam-dunk recommendation. For a compact accent piece that looks twice its cost, it's worth the gamble — just know the tradeoffs going in.
