Let's be direct: most bar stools under $150 a pair are wobbly, uncomfortable, or both. The EALSON 90117 set avoids that trap, and with a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, buyers clearly agree. But there are a few things you need to know before you click buy.

## What You're Actually Getting

This is a set of two stools with a 29.5-inch seat height — designed for standard 41-42 inch kitchen counters or bar tops, not your 36-inch island. As shown in the product image, the design is clean and modern: a padded upholstered seat in a light gray fabric, supported by solid rubberwood legs with a dark espresso finish. The legs are connected by a full-width footrest bar, which matters more than you'd think.

Each stool measures 16.5 inches deep and 16.1 inches wide at the seat. That's not a skinny perch — it's a real seat. The cushion is 3 inches thick with high-density foam, and the backrest is curved at a slight angle that supports your lower back without forcing you into an upright position.

## Performance and Build Quality

The rubberwood legs are the standout feature here. Most stools in this price range use pine or even MDF, which flexes and eventually cracks. Rubberwood is significantly denser and more resistant to warping. One reviewer noted, "I've had these for six months and they still feel as solid as the day I assembled them. No creaking, no wobble."

Assembly takes about 15 minutes per stool. The pre-drilled holes align correctly — a small thing that saves you from the frustration of fighting misaligned screws. All necessary tools are included, so you won't need to dig through your toolbox.

The footrest is positioned at a comfortable height, which sounds minor until you've sat on stools where the footrest hits your shins awkwardly. At 16 inches from the floor, it accommodates most adults well.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 229 ratings, a few themes emerge:

- **Sturdiness**: Multiple reviewers mention these feel "solid" and "well-built" — words you don't often see in budget stool reviews.
- **Comfort**: The thick cushion gets consistent mention. One buyer said, "I can sit on these for hours working at my kitchen island. My back doesn't hurt like it did with my old stools."
- **Looks**: The combination of light gray fabric and dark wood legs photographs well and matches most kitchen aesthetics.

## Where It Falls Short

The light gray fabric is the biggest practical concern. It shows spills, pet hair, and everyday grime quickly. Several reviewers mentioned this specifically — one wrote, "The color is beautiful but I'm constantly spot-cleaning. I wish I'd bought a darker color or applied Scotchgard immediately." Take that advice.

There's also no height adjustment. If your counter is 40 inches rather than 42, these will feel slightly tall. Measure your counter height before ordering. This is a fixed-height stool, and there's no way to modify it.

Finally, the fabric is a polyester blend that's easy to clean but not stain-resistant. You'll want to treat it with a fabric protector before first use.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Kitchen island owners** with standard 41-42 inch counters who want comfortable seating for daily use
- **Anyone tired of wobbly stools** — the rubberwood construction genuinely solves this problem
- **Homeowners who want a modern look** without paying designer prices

Skip these if you have a 36-inch breakfast bar (you need counter-height stools, not bar-height) or if you have kids who will inevitably drop food on the light fabric.

## FAQ

**Q: What's the weight capacity?**
A: The manufacturer lists 300 pounds per stool. Given the rubberwood construction and solid joinery, this seems accurate, though I wouldn't push it past 280 to be safe.

**Q: Do the stools scratch hardwood floors?**
A: The feet have small plastic glides, but they're thin. Several reviewers recommend adding felt pads to protect hardwood. Do this before assembly.

**Q: Can I use these outdoors on a covered patio?**
A: Not recommended. The fabric will fade and mold in humidity, and the wood legs aren't sealed for outdoor conditions.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a sturdy, comfortable pair of bar stools that won't embarrass you when guests visit. The rubberwood construction and thick cushions make this a solid value at this price point.

**Skip it if** you need adjustable height, have a 36-inch counter, or can't handle light-colored fabric that requires maintenance.
