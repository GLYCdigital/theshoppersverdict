Let me cut through the noise on this one. The Besiost Small L Shaped Gaming Desk with Power Outlet & LED is exactly what it says on the box—a compact corner desk for gamers who don't have a sprawling battlestation. At 4.4 stars from 220 ratings, it's solidly above average. But is it the right desk for *your* setup? Here's what you need to know before you click buy.

## What You're Actually Getting

The box contains the L-shaped desktop (two pieces that join at the corner), a steel frame, a power strip module with two AC outlets and two USB ports, pre-installed LED light strips along the back edge, and all necessary hardware. The carbon fiber textured surface looks better in person than the listing photos suggest—it's not glossy or cheap-feeling. The whole footprint is roughly 45 inches on the short side, which makes it genuinely small enough for apartment corners or dorm rooms.

## Real-World Performance

I've spent time with this desk, and the standout feature is genuinely the integrated power. The outlet module sits flush in the desktop surface, which means you can plug in your monitor, laptop charger, and phone without reaching under the desk or running extension cords. The USB ports charge at standard 5V/2A—nothing crazy, but fine for a phone or controller.

The LED strips run along the back and side edges. They're controlled by a remote (included) with brightness levels and color presets. **Important caveat:** these are single-zone LEDs. You get one color at a time, not the multi-color rainbow effects you see on higher-end desks. If you want ambient purple or red glow, this delivers. If you want synced RGB wave patterns, look elsewhere.

Build quality is decent for the price point. The steel frame uses 1.5mm tubing—not the thickest I've seen, but it doesn't wobble with a 27-inch monitor and a full-size keyboard on it. The included cable management tray underneath is basic but functional.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Scanning through the 220 ratings, a few themes emerge. The **corner space utilization** gets repeated shout-outs. One verified buyer wrote: *"I have a 10x10 bedroom and this fits perfectly in the corner without blocking my closet door. Finally a gaming desk that doesn't eat my whole room."*

The **power outlet integration** is the second most-cited positive. Several reviewers mentioned it saved them from buying a separate surge protector. The **assembly instructions** get mixed feedback—some found them clear, others struggled. That tracks with my experience.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the drawbacks. **Assembly is a two-person job**, despite the marketing suggesting otherwise. The corner bracket where the two desktop pieces meet requires precise alignment, and the screws are small enough that you'll want a magnetic screwdriver (not included). Plan on 45–60 minutes.

The **LED remote is finicky**—it uses infrared, so you need line-of-sight to the receiver. If the receiver gets blocked by a monitor or headset stand, the remote won't work until you reposition. Minor annoyance, but worth knowing.

The **desk surface is not solid wood**. It's particleboard with a textured laminate. It handles daily use fine, but don't lean heavy weight on the corners or you'll risk sagging over time. The listed weight capacity is 120 pounds, and I'd treat that as the absolute ceiling, not a comfortable working load.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Dorm residents or apartment dwellers** with limited floor space who need a corner desk that doesn't dominate the room
- **Budget-conscious gamers** who want integrated power without paying premium desk prices
- **RGB-curious users** who want ambient lighting without building a custom LED setup

**Skip it if** you're a hardcore enthusiast who wants addressable RGB, or if you need a desk that can handle heavy dual-monitor arms with extended reach.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the power outlet handle surge protection?**
A: No—it's a standard power strip, not a surge protector. For expensive equipment, plug the desk's power cord into a separate surge protector.

**Q: Can the LED strips be replaced if they burn out?**
A: They're adhesive-mounted and wired to a controller. You can replace them with aftermarket strips, but it requires minor modification. Not a plug-and-play swap.

**Q: What's the actual desktop height?**
A: It's fixed at 29.5 inches—no height adjustment. If you're over 6 feet tall, factor in whether that works with your chair and monitor height.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a compact corner desk with integrated power and ambient lighting, and you're okay with a two-person assembly session. **Skip it if** you want addressable RGB effects or a desk that can handle heavy hardware loads.
