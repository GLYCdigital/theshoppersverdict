## Where It Falls Short

I'm not going to sugarcoat it—there are two legitimate gripes.

**1. The USB ports are outdated.** In 2026, most people are buying USB-C to USB-C cables. This unit has USB-A only. You'll need to use an adapter or an A-to-C cable, which adds clutter. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's a missed opportunity.

**2. The cord management hole is on the back, not the bottom.** This forces you to route cables up from the floor and over the back lip. If your outlet is directly behind the nightstand, you'll see a loop of cable. It's not a huge deal, but it requires a bit of planning.

A few reviewers also noted that the "oak" finish runs slightly yellow in direct sunlight. If you have a bright, sun-drenched room, consider the darker walnut option.
