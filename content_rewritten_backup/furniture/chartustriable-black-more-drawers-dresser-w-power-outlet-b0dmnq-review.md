## Nine drawers and a power outlet — but is this dresser actually worth your money?

Most dressers under $200 give you six drawers and zero thought about how you actually live. The chartustriable Black More Drawers Dresser w/Power Outlet takes a different angle: nine full-width drawers plus a built-in charging station. At 4.1 stars across 204 ratings, it's not setting the world on fire, but it's quietly doing something right. Let's dig into whether it earns a spot in your bedroom.

## What you actually get

The box delivers the dresser in flat-pack form — expect roughly 40 pieces including the frame panels, nine drawer boxes, cam locks, dowels, and the power strip assembly. The unit measures approximately 55 inches wide, 15.5 inches deep, and 31 inches tall. That footprint is wider than a standard 6-drawer dresser, which is exactly why the drawer count works — each drawer is full-width rather than split into narrow columns.

The power outlet is the headline feature: a 2-outlet AC plug plus two USB-A ports mounted on the top back edge. The cord routes down the back and out the bottom, so you can plug it into a wall outlet behind the dresser. It's a thoughtful touch for charging a phone overnight or keeping a lamp plugged in without running cords across the room.

## Build quality and real-world performance

The construction is solid particleboard with a matte black laminate finish. It's not hardwood — at this price point, that's expected — but the panels are thick enough that the dresser doesn't wobble when drawers are open. The finish itself is a genuine highlight: the matte texture hides fingerprints well, and it resists the smudging that plagues glossy black furniture.

Drawer capacity is the real selling point. Each drawer measures roughly 20 inches wide, 13 inches deep, and 6 inches tall. That's enough for folded jeans in two stacks, sweaters, or a serious t-shirt collection. The full-width design means no wasted space from divider posts — you're using every inch.

The power outlet works as advertised. It's rated for standard household current, and the USB ports deliver enough juice to charge a phone reasonably fast. One caveat: the outlet is on the top back edge, so if your dresser sits flush against the wall, you'll need to reach over the top to access it. Plan for that clearance.

## What buyers consistently praise

The recurring theme in positive reviews is storage capacity. One buyer noted, "I was skeptical about the drawer count, but everything I own finally has a home. My old 6-drawer dresser couldn't hold half of this." Another praised the finish: "The black is actually matte, not glossy — it doesn't show every dust particle like my old furniture did."

The power outlet gets consistent mentions too, particularly from people using it for bedside charging or a small TV. Multiple reviewers mentioned the dresser exceeding expectations for the price, calling it "surprisingly sturdy once assembled."

## Where it falls short

Assembly is the biggest complaint — and it's legitimate. The cam lock system requires patience, and the diagram-only instructions leave room for interpretation. Several reviewers reported misaligned holes or drawers that needed adjustment after assembly. Budget 2-3 hours with two people, and have a screwdriver and rubber mallet ready.

The drawer slides are plastic, not metal. They work fine for normal use, but they don't glide as smoothly as ball-bearing slides, and there's no soft-close mechanism. Let a drawer go and it slams. If you're used to higher-end furniture, this will feel noticeably less refined.

One more thing: the power outlet is a fixed component. If it ever fails, you can't easily replace it without disassembling the back panel. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing.

## Who should buy this

This dresser is ideal for:
- **Apartment dwellers** who need maximum storage in a compact footprint
- **Anyone with a serious clothing collection** — nine full-width drawers handle a lot
- **Bedroom setups needing a charging station** — the built-in outlet eliminates cable clutter
- **Budget-conscious shoppers** who want function over luxury materials

Skip it if you demand soft-close drawers, solid wood construction, or a quick 30-minute assembly.
