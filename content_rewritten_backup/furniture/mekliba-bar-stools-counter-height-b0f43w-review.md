## Where the MEKLIBA Stools Fall Short

No product is perfect, and these have a couple of legitimate quirks.

**The footrest height is the biggest issue.** It sits about 8 inches off the floor, which is fine for average-height adults but too high for anyone under 5'4". Verified reviews mention "my feet don't quite reach the footrest" and "a bit awkward for my shorter guests." If your household includes kids or petite adults, factor this in.

**The PU leather isn't breathable.** In a climate-controlled kitchen, it's comfortable. In a sunroom or garage bar that gets cold in winter, the seat will feel stiff and cold initially. It warms up within a minute, but it's worth knowing.

**The backrest is fixed.** There's no swivel and no height adjustment. It's a simple stool, and that's fine — but don't expect ergonomic flexibility.
