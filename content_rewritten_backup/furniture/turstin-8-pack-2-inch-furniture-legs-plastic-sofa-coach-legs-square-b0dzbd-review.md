**Where they fall short**

Let's be real about the limitations. These are plastic legs, and plastic has a fatigue life. They're fine for a standard sofa, loveseat, or occasional chair — but I would not put these under a heavy sectional, a sleeper sofa with a metal frame, or anything over roughly 300 pounds. If you have kids who jump on the furniture, the constant flexing stress will eventually cause cracking. The black-only finish is also a limitation; if your furniture has light oak or white legs, these will look like a patch job, not an upgrade. And while the 2-inch height is great for cleaning clearance, it's not enough to significantly change the seating angle or make a low-profile sofa feel taller.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will these fit my sofa?**
A: Check the mounting plate on your existing legs. The TURSTIN legs use a 2-inch square plate with standard screw spacing. If your sofa has a flat wooden rail underneath, they'll almost certainly fit. Curved or metal frames are less predictable — measure before you buy.

**Q: Can I use these on carpet?**
A: Yes. The flat, wide base distributes weight well and won't sink into plush carpet. On hardwood, the plastic base slides a bit, so consider adding felt pads if you have slick floors.

**Q: Are they sturdy enough for a recliner?**
A: Not for a heavy recliner with a motor and metal mechanism. They're best for stationary sofas, loveseats, and chairs under 300 pounds. For anything heavier, invest in metal legs.
