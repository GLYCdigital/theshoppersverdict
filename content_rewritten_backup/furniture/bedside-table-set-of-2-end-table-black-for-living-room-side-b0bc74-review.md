Let’s be upfront: a set of two nightstands for the price of one is either a steal or a trap. After digging through 220 ratings and the spec sheet, this black end table set lands firmly in the first camp — with a few caveats you should know before checkout.

**What’s actually in the box**

You get two identical tables. Each measures roughly 15.7" L x 15.7" W x 19.7" H — compact enough for tight corners but tall enough to sit flush with most standard bed heights. The top surface holds a lamp, phone, and a glass of water without feeling crowded. Below, an open shelf gives you about 7 inches of clearance, ideal for books, a small plant, or a basket of remotes.

The black finish is a matte laminate over particleboard. It looks more expensive than it is — the texture mimics wood grain subtly, and it doesn't collect fingerprints like glossy alternatives. All hardware, an Allen wrench, and a printed manual ship in the box. No power tools needed.

**Real-world performance**

The build is straightforward: four legs screw into pre-drilled brackets, the shelf slides in, and the top caps it off. Most buyers report assembly times between 15 and 25 minutes per table. One reviewer noted, *"I put both together while my coffee was still hot. The holes lined up perfectly, which never happens with furniture at this price."* That alignment matters — misaligned holes are the #1 frustration in flat-pack furniture, and this set avoids it.

Stability is decent for the category. The legs have small plastic floor glides that prevent wobble on carpet but slide slightly on hardwood. If you have a toddler or a clumsy pet, anchor the tables to the wall — they're light enough to tip with enough force. The particleboard is dense, not hollow, so it holds up to daily use without creaking. But water is the enemy. A sweaty glass left overnight will leave a permanent white ring. Use a coaster or a tray.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The value proposition is the headline. Multiple reviewers highlight that buying this set costs less than a single comparable nightstand from big-box stores. The open shelf is a recurring favorite — it keeps the surface clutter-free without the hassle of drawer alignment. The matte finish also earns repeated mentions for hiding dust between cleanings.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. There are no cable management holes, so if you charge your phone on the nightstand, the cord will snake visibly down the leg. The screws are soft metal — hand-tighten only; a drill will strip them. And while the particleboard is sturdy, it's not moisture-resistant. One buyer warned, *"I placed a diffuser on the top and the condensation ruined the finish within a month. Wish I'd used a tray."*

**Who should buy this**

- **First-time apartment dwellers** who need functional furniture without a big spend
- **Guest bedrooms** where the tables see light use but need to look intentional
- **Renters** who want a neutral black that won't clash when they move

**Skip it if** you need solid wood, drawer storage, or furniture that survives a humid climate without fuss.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does assembly actually take?**
A: Plan for 15–20 minutes per table. The instructions are clear, and all holes are pre-drilled. No tools beyond the included Allen wrench are required.

**Q: Can these fit in a small bedroom?**
A: Yes. At under 20 inches tall and 16 inches square, they work in tight spaces. The open shelf makes them feel less bulky than drawer units.

**Q: Are they stable on carpet?**
A: Generally yes — the plastic glides grip most carpet. On plush, high-pile carpet, you may notice slight wobble. Use felt pads or adjust the glides if needed.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a clean, budget-friendly set that assembles easily and looks decent from across the room. **Skip it if** you expect heirloom quality or need water-resistant surfaces.
