Let's be honest about folding chairs: most of them are torture devices disguised as seating. The KAOCHA Black Folding Chair with Padded Cushion is not that. It's the rare folding chair that doesn't make you regret every minute past the 30-minute mark — and with 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, plenty of buyers agree.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains two chairs (check the listing — the price point typically covers a pair), each measuring roughly 19.5 inches wide by 21.5 inches deep, with a seat height around 18 inches. That's standard dining height, so these work at most kitchen tables, card tables, or as overflow seating around a coffee table. The steel frame is powder-coated black — not glossy, not matte, just a functional finish that resists scratches better than I expected. Each chair weighs about 11 pounds, which feels substantial without being annoying to carry.

**The Build and the Sit**

Here's where this chair separates itself from the $25 specials at big-box stores. The steel frame uses cross-bracing under the seat, and it shows — I've seen reviews from buyers using these at 250+ pounds with zero wobble. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: "These are sturdier than chairs I paid three times as much for." The locking mechanism on the folding joint engages with a solid click, and the rubber feet keep the chair from sliding on hardwood or tile.

The padded cushion is the headline feature, and it's legit. You're getting roughly two inches of foam under a bonded leather-look cover. It's not memory foam, and it won't rival your living room recliner, but for a folding chair it's genuinely comfortable for a full dinner party or a poker night. The cushion is attached to the frame, so it won't slip off when you stand up — a small detail that matters.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The comfort-to-price ratio comes up constantly. One reviewer noted, "I bought these for Thanksgiving and ended up using them all week at my desk." Another mentioned using them for a backyard wedding and that guests commented on the seating. The fold-flat design also gets consistent love — at about 4 inches thick when folded, they tuck into narrow spaces without dominating a closet or garage corner.

**Where It Falls Short**

No armrests. If you're planning to sit for multi-hour stretches — think gaming sessions, long work-from-home days, or extended card games — your shoulders will feel it. This is a dining/event chair, not a task chair, and it doesn't pretend otherwise.

The cushion cover is also not removable. Spot-clean only. One reviewer mentioned a red wine spill at a party that left a faint stain despite quick action. If your household is hard on furniture, factor that in.

The padding is also on the firmer side initially — a few reviewers noted it takes a few sits to break in. It's not a dealbreaker, but don't expect plush on day one.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Hosts who need dependable overflow seating** — these look intentional, not like you raided the garage
- **Apartment dwellers with limited storage** — the flat-fold design is a genuine space saver
- **Anyone who's tired of wobbly, flimsy folding chairs** — the steel frame is the real deal
- **Outdoor event planners** — the powder coating handles light weather, though I wouldn't leave them in the rain

**Skip it if** you need armrests, you want washable covers, or you're looking for a chair to sit in for 8-hour workdays.

**FAQ**

**Q: What's the weight capacity?**
A: The frame is rated for 300 pounds. Multiple reviewers confirmed stability at heavier weights, though the cushion will compress more noticeably.

**Q: Do these chairs scratch hardwood floors?**
A: The rubber feet are effective. Reviewers on wood and tile floors report no scratching. The feet are also replaceable if they wear down.

**Q: Are the cushions replaceable?**
A: No. The cushion is attached to the frame. When it wears out, you'd need to replace the whole chair — though at this price point, that's a reasonable tradeoff.
