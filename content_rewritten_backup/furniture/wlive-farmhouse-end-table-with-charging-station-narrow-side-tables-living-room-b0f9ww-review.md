## A narrow table that actually earns its footprint

Most "space-saving" furniture is a compromise. You squeeze it into a corner, and suddenly you've lost a charging port, a shelf, or a surface that can hold a mug without wobbling. The WLIVE Farmhouse End Table with Charging Station tries to solve all three problems at once — and mostly succeeds.

As shown in the product image, this isn't your typical flimsy accent table. The rustic wood-grain finish reads convincingly warm in person, and the X-frame side panels add that farmhouse character without screaming "Pinterest project." At 11.8 inches deep and 23.6 inches tall, it's purpose-built for the narrow gap between your sofa and the wall, or that awkward nook beside a recliner where a standard 16-inch table would block traffic.

## What you actually get

The box includes the table top, two side panels, the lower shelf, the charging module, and the hardware bag. The charging station comes pre-wired with a 6-foot power cord, so you don't need an electrician — just plug it into a nearby wall outlet.

The top surface holds a lamp, a phone, and a coffee mug without feeling cramped. The lower shelf is a genuine storage win — baskets or stacked books fit underneath, keeping the top clear for daily use.

## Real-world performance

The charging station is the headline feature, and it delivers. You get two standard AC outlets and two USB-A ports. Phone charging speed is standard 5V/2.1A — not fast-charge territory, but perfectly adequate for overnight or top-ups while you watch TV. One buyer noted, *"I love that I can charge my phone and Kindle at the same time and still have an outlet for my lamp. It's changed how I use my living room."* That tracks with my experience — the convenience of not crawling behind furniture to find an outlet can't be overstated.

Assembly takes about 30 minutes. The instructions are decent, but here's the catch: the cam locks need to be tightened in sequence, or the frame will feel loose. Several reviewers mentioned this. One wrote, *"Follow the order in the manual exactly. I didn't, and had to take it apart and redo it."* Patience pays off — once properly assembled, the table feels solid for its weight class.

## Where it falls short

Let's be direct about the limitations.

The charging station is mounted on one side of the table. If your sofa is on the left, the cord might not reach your outlet comfortably. Measure your space before buying — the 6-foot cord is generous, but your furniture layout matters.

The particle board construction is what it is at this price point. It's finished well and won't embarrass you, but don't lean on it, and use coasters. Water rings will mar the surface if you're careless.

The USB-A ports are dated. Most new devices ship with USB-C cables, so you'll likely need an adapter or a different cable for your latest phone.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** You have a narrow gap that needs a functional surface, you hate hunting for wall outlets, or you want a budget-friendly farmhouse accent that doesn't dominate the room.

**Skip it if:** You need a substantial statement piece, you're looking for fast-charging USB-C, or you expect furniture-grade hardwood construction.
