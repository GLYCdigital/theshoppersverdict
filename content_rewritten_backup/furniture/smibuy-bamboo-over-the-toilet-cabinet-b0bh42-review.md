**The Short Version**

If your bathroom has wasted vertical space above the toilet, the SMIBUY bamboo over-the-toilet cabinet turns that dead zone into genuinely useful storage. At 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, it's a solid mid-tier pick — not perfect, but for the price, it punches above its weight class.

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes the cabinet itself, all hardware, and a tool that's technically a hex wrench but will make you wish you'd grabbed a real screwdriver. The unit measures roughly 23.6" wide, 9.4" deep, and 59" tall — narrow enough to clear most standard toilets but tall enough to hold serious bathroom clutter. The footprint is the selling point: you're using air, not floor space.

Construction is where SMIBUY earns its keep. This is actual bamboo, not the bamboo-strip particle board you see on cheaper units. The grain runs consistently, the edges are routed clean, and the finish has a warm honey tone that doesn't look plasticky. It's heavier than you'd expect — around 30 pounds — which gives it a stable, anchored feel once mounted.

**Real-World Performance**

The storage layout is smart. Two upper cabinets with doors hide the messy stuff — medications, backup toiletries, cleaning supplies. The middle shelf inside the right cabinet is adjustable, which matters more than you'd think. That tall shampoo bottle you've been shoving under the sink? It fits now. Two open shelves below give you display space for folded towels, decorative baskets, or the things you grab daily.

The doors close with a soft-close mechanism that actually works. No slamming, no rattling. The hinges feel substantial, not like the flimsy stamped metal you find on budget furniture.

Assembly is the price of admission. Plan for 45–60 minutes, and don't expect a pleasant afternoon. The instructions are mostly pictograms, and a few reviewers noted pre-drilled holes that didn't quite line up. One buyer put it plainly: "The assembly was a bit challenging but manageable with two people." Another said, "It took me about an hour to put together, but the end result looks great." Bring a drill, patience, and maybe a second pair of hands.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The recurring theme across reviews is the quality-to-price ratio. People are surprised it's not cheap-looking. One reviewer wrote: "Looks beautiful, good quality, easy to assemble, sturdy." Another noted, "Great purchase! Fits perfectly over the toilet and adds a lot of storage."

The space-saving aspect gets called out repeatedly. Bathrooms with limited square footage benefit most — you gain storage without sacrificing floor space. Multiple reviewers mentioned it replaced a cluttered shelf unit and made their bathroom feel more open.

**Where It Falls Short**

The open bottom shelf sits low enough that toilet contours can obscure smaller items. If you're storing short bottles or small baskets down there, they might visually disappear. Keep the bottom shelf for taller items only.

There's also occasional QC variance. A handful of reviews mention minor scratches or a slight bamboo smell that takes a few days to dissipate. Nothing deal-breaking, but worth noting if you're picky about finish.

The depth is another consideration. At 9.4 inches, it's deeper than some over-the-toilet units. If your toilet tank sits close to the wall or your bathroom is narrow, measure carefully — you don't want the cabinet mounting brackets hitting the tank lid.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Small bathroom owners** who need storage without floor space
- **Renters** who can't install permanent shelving — this mounts to the wall but leaves no lasting damage
- **Anyone tired of staring at toiletries on the back of the toilet tank**

**Skip it if** you hate assembly, have a toilet tank that sits flush against the wall, or need something wider than 24 inches.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can this hold a lot of weight?**
The open shelves handle 20–30 pounds each without issue. The closed cabinets are lighter-duty — keep them for toiletries and smaller items, not heavy towels.

**Q: Is it real bamboo or printed particle board?**
Real bamboo. It's a natural product, so grain varies slightly between units. That's a feature, not a flaw.

**Q: How hard is assembly really?**
On a scale of 1–10, it's a 6. The pieces are labeled, but the pictogram instructions are vague. Two people make it significantly easier, especially when mounting the cabinet to the wall.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need smart vertical storage and don't mind an hour of assembly. **Skip it if** you're expecting furniture-store precision or have a toilet setup that limits depth.

This is a genuinely good value that does exactly what it promises: it stores your bathroom clutter and looks decent doing it. The 4.4-star rating holds up. Just bring a drill and a patient attitude.
