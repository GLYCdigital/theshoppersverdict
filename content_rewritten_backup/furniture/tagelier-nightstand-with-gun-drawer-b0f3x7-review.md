Most nightstands just hold your phone, a book, and a glass of water. The TAGELIER Nightstand with Gun Drawer tries to do something more — secure quick-access storage for a firearm while still looking like a piece of furniture you'd actually want in your bedroom. After digging through 210 ratings and the product specs, here's the real story.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the nightstand (roughly 20" wide × 16" deep × 24" tall), the locking drawer assembly, a fingerprint scanner module, backup keys, and a USB-C charging cable for the lock mechanism. The locking drawer sits in the upper position, leaving a standard open shelf below for books or baskets. The padded top is upholstered in a leather-look material that's comfortable for setting down a drink or resting a phone.

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

The construction is solid MDF with a wood-grain veneer that doesn't scream "gun safe." The drawer glides on metal ball-bearing tracks, and the lock mechanism is the standout feature. You can program up to 20 fingerprints or a 4-8 digit PIN. In practice, the scanner reads reliably — most users report it unlocks in about 1.5 seconds, which is what you want at 2 AM when you hear a noise.

One buyer put it plainly: *"I tested it 50 times with my eyes closed in the dark. Every single time it opened. That's what I bought it for."*

The drawer interior is lined with a soft foam cutout that fits most compact and mid-size handguns. If you carry something bigger — a full-size 1911 or a revolver with a red dot — you'll need to remove the foam insert and use the drawer's bare floor. That's workable but worth knowing before you commit.

**Where It Falls Short**

Assembly is the biggest complaint across reviews. The cam locks require precise alignment, and the included Allen wrench bends under pressure. Plan for 45-60 minutes of assembly, and grab a proper hex driver from your toolbox before you start.

The lock beep is another gripe. You can toggle it to a lower volume, but you cannot disable it entirely. If you're sneaking out before dawn or need absolute silence, that single beep might bother you.

The fingerprint scanner needs occasional re-registration — some users report having to re-scan their print every few weeks, especially if their hands are dry or calloused. The backup key and PIN code always work, so it's an annoyance, not a security flaw.

The interior dimensions are tighter than the photos suggest. Measure your firearm's length and height before ordering. The drawer is roughly 12" × 9" × 3", which handles most handguns but nothing larger.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for anyone who wants a firearm accessible at night but secured from kids or visitors. It's also a solid pick for apartment dwellers who don't want a visible gun safe bolted to the floor. The nightstand works as a legitimate furniture piece — it doesn't scream "gun owner" the way a metal safe does.

Skip it if you need a full-size safe that can hold multiple firearms, or if you want a true biometric safe with silent operation. This is a single-handgun solution with furniture aesthetics.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can the drawer lock be damaged by repeated fingerprint use?**
A: No. The scanner is rated for hundreds of thousands of cycles. The more common issue is a dry-finger read failure, which the PIN code solves instantly.

**Q: Does it come with a mounting kit to secure it to the wall?**
A: Yes, a basic anti-tip bracket is included, along with screws. It's worth using if you have kids in the house — the nightstand is light enough to tip over if climbed on.

**Q: How long does the battery last?**
A: The lock runs on a rechargeable lithium battery that lasts roughly 3-4 months with normal daily use. It charges via USB-C and takes about 2 hours for a full charge. A low-battery warning beeps for several days before it dies.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want quick-access firearm storage that looks like furniture, and you're willing to spend an hour on assembly.

**Skip it if:** You need silent operation, have a large handgun with optics, or expect a tool-free setup.

The TAGELIER Nightstand with Gun Drawer earns a solid 4.2. It solves a real problem — secure, fast access — without turning your bedroom into a tactical command center. Just budget time for assembly and measure your firearm first.
