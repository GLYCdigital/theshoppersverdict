## The Standing Desk That Actually Comes With Storage

Most budget standing desks make you choose: either you get the motorized lift *or* you get drawers. The FEZIBO Height Adjustable Electric Standing Desk with Double tries to give you both — and mostly pulls it off. With 210 Amazon ratings averaging 4.2 stars, it's clearly finding an audience. But is it the right desk for your home office? Let's break down what you're actually getting.

## What's in the Box

You get the usual flat-pack setup: the desktop panel, dual motor legs, crossbar, controller, and the two drawer units. The frame is steel with a powder-coated finish, and the desktop comes in a wood-grain laminate that looks better in person than the product photos suggest. The dual drawers are pre-assembled as units — you attach them to the underside, which is the part that trips people up (more on that below). The controller includes three programmable height presets and a digital display. A cable management tray is included, though it's on the shallow side.

## Performance and Build Quality

The dual motor system is the real story here. It lifts up to 176 pounds, which covers a monitor arm, a heavy 27-inch display, and then some. At normal operating speed, it's quiet enough that you won't wake anyone in the next room — the motor hum is noticeable but not obtrusive. The height range spans roughly 28 to 47 inches, which accommodates seated work for most body types and standing work for users up to around 6'2".

The desktop itself is solidly constructed — it's not solid wood, but the laminate finish resists scratches and cleans up easily. The frame's crossbar adds stability at standing height, though you'll still get some wobble if you aggressively type on a mechanical keyboard while standing. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing if you're coming from a heavy fixed desk.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The drawers are the feature that keeps showing up in positive reviews. One buyer noted, "Finally a standing desk that doesn't force me to buy a separate filing cabinet — the drawers actually fit letter-size folders." Another appreciated the preset buttons: "I switch between sitting and standing 10+ times a day. The memory presets make it one-touch, no holding the button down."

Multiple reviewers also highlighted the value proposition, with one writing: "Looked at the big-name brands and couldn't justify double the price. This does the same job for less."

## Where It Falls Short

The drawer installation is the most cited complaint — and it's legitimate. The drawer slides need to align perfectly with the frame brackets, and several buyers report the holes not lining up cleanly. One reviewer put it plainly: "Took me an extra 45 minutes to get the drawers aligned. Doable, but frustrating." If you're not comfortable with basic tool work, budget extra assembly time.

The included cable tray is genuinely too shallow for a typical power strip with a brick-sized adapter. You'll want to plan your cable management separately — a small cable basket or heavy-duty zip ties will serve you better. The controller buttons also feel a bit cheap; they work fine now, but they don't inspire long-term confidence with daily use.

## Who Should Buy This

The FEZIBO is ideal for the home office worker who wants sit-stand flexibility *and* needs drawer storage without buying a separate piece of furniture. It's particularly well-suited for small spaces where a hutch or filing cabinet won't fit. Students setting up dorm desks and budget-conscious remote workers will get the most value here.

Skip it if you're a heavy user who needs rock-solid stability at maximum standing height, or if you hate assembly projects with finicky alignment steps. Premium options from brands like Uplift or Fully offer more stability and better build quality, but you'll pay 2-3x the price.

## FAQ

**Is the desk stable at standing height?**
Reasonably stable for normal typing and mouse work. You'll notice some wobble if you lean heavily on the desk or type aggressively, but it's within acceptable limits for the price point. A monitor arm will help isolate screen movement.

**What are the actual dimensions?**
The desktop measures approximately 48 x 24 inches, which fits a dual-monitor setup if you use monitor arms. The height adjusts from 28 to 47 inches via the dual motor system.

**Does the desk wobble when adjusting height?**
There's a brief sway during adjustment, which is normal for dual-motor desks. The crossbar helps, but all single-post or dual-motor desks in this class have some movement mid-lift.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a functional sit-stand desk with real drawer storage, don't need commercial-grade stability, and are willing to spend an extra 30-45 minutes on assembly. The dual motors, 176-pound capacity, and programmable presets deliver genuine value at a budget-friendly price point.

**Skip it if:** You're a tall user (over 6'2") who needs maximum standing height stability, or if you have zero patience for finicky drawer alignment. In those cases, spend more on a premium frame.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, feature-packed standing desk held back only by assembly frustrations and minor build compromises. It does the job, looks decent, and won't break the bank.
