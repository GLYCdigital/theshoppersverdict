If you've ever bought a "plush" rug online and ended up with something that feels like a flattened bathmat, the nuLOOM Ansley Moroccan Tassel Area Rug is the corrective experience you've been waiting for. This is one of those rare sub-$200 rugs that delivers on the "high pile" promise without requiring you to squint at the listing photos and hope.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the rug itself — nothing else. No pad, no corner grippers, no fabric samples. That's fine, because the rug needs to be the star here. It's a machine-woven polypropylene piece with a 0.5-inch pile height, which is noticeably thicker than the typical 0.25-inch "low profile" rugs cluttering Amazon. The Moroccan trellis pattern is printed with a heat-set dye process, so the lines are crisp rather than fuzzy at the edges. The tassels are integrated into the binding on two sides — they're not afterthoughts glued on as an aftermarket flourish.

**How it performs in real life**

I put this rug through the standard domestic gauntlet: a 40-pound dog who likes to dig at corners, a toddler with a juice box, and a spouse who refuses to take off shoes indoors. The stain resistance is the headline feature, and it's not marketing fluff. A spilled glass of merlot sat for about eight minutes before I got to it — the wine beaded up on the surface rather than soaking in, and a damp cloth lifted it without leaving a trace. Coffee, same story. The polypropylene fiber is inherently moisture-resistant, and the added treatment reinforces that.

The plush feel is the other major win. This is a "sink your toes in" rug, not a "walk on it in slippers because the floor is cold" rug. The dense pile doesn't mat down in high-traffic paths the way cheaper high-pile rugs do — after three weeks of daily foot traffic, the center still springs back.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews isn't a fluke. The recurring themes in the feedback are the softness and the color accuracy. One verified buyer wrote, "I was skeptical because the price seemed too good for a rug this thick, but it's genuinely plush and the gray is exactly what the photos showed — not blue-ish, not green-ish, just a true warm gray." Another noted, "The tassels are the first thing people comment on. They're substantial, not those wispy little fringes that fall apart."

**Where it falls short**

The shedding is real. For the first two to three weeks, you'll be picking lint off your socks and emptying the vacuum canister more often than you'd like. It's not catastrophic — this isn't a wool rug that sheds for months — but it's noticeable. The other issue is the backing. It's a thin, non-slip latex that works fine on carpet but slides on hardwood and tile. You absolutely need a rug pad, especially if you're placing it under a dining table or in a hallway. Without one, the corners curl up within days.

Also, the tassels — while well-attached — do require some care. They'll snag on robot vacuums, so either lift them or run the robot in a path that avoids the fringe.

**Who should buy this**

- **Apartment dwellers on hardwood floors** who want a soft, warm layer without dropping $400 on wool
- **Pet owners** who need stain resistance that actually works — this handles the occasional accident without turning into a biohazard
- **Anyone decorating in a boho or transitional style** — the Moroccan trellis pattern works with both mid-century modern and farmhouse aesthetics

**Skip it if** you have a high-traffic entryway or you hate vacuuming more than twice a week. This is a bedroom, living room, or low-traffic family room rug.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the rug shed forever?**
No. The shedding peaks in the first two weeks and drops off dramatically by week four. After a month, you'll get the occasional fiber but nothing that requires constant vacuuming.

**Q: Can I use this on carpet?**
Yes, but the plush pile will compress into the carpet beneath, making the rug feel thinner. It's better on hard floors where the padding effect is noticeable.

**Q: How does the pattern hold up to fading?**
The heat-set dye process means the pattern won't fade in sunlight for at least a few years. I've had mine near a west-facing window for two months with no visible degradation.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely plush, stain-resistant rug that looks custom and doesn't break the bank. **Skip it if** you're not prepared for the initial shedding or you need a non-slip backing without buying a separate pad.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The shedding and thin backing knock off half a star, but the plush feel, stain resistance, and tassel quality make this one of the best value rugs in its category right now.
