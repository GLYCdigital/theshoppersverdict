Let's cut through the noise. The Relbiety Swivel Bar Stools Set of 2-1035 has a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, which is genuinely impressive for a product category where wobbly legs and peeling upholstery are the norm. The real question is whether these stools earn that score in your kitchen, not just in the Amazon reviews. Here's what I found after digging through the data.

**What you actually get**

The box contains two identical bar stools, each with a padded faux leather seat, a chrome footrest, and a round base. The key spec that matters: these are bar-height stools, meaning they'll pair with a standard 40-42 inch counter or bar. The seats are roughly 15 inches in diameter — comfortable for most body types but not oversized. Assembly is genuinely minimal: the base screws into the seat column with a single bolt per stool. Buyers consistently report being done in under 15 minutes, with one reviewer noting, "Took me longer to get the box open than to put them together."

**Performance and build quality**

This is where the Relbiety stools separate themselves from the budget pack. The 360-degree swivel is buttery smooth — no grinding, no catching, no squeaking. That's rare at this price point. The gas lift mechanism (yes, these have gas lift height adjustment, not just a swivel) lets you raise or lower the seat about 5 inches, which makes them workable for both standard counter height and taller bar setups.

The base is weighted metal, not plastic. That matters more than you'd think. Several customers mention their stools survived a house with three kids who treat furniture like playground equipment. One review I saw put it simply: "These things are solid. My 220-pound brother sat on one and it didn't even creak."

**What buyers consistently praise**

The most repeated compliments cluster around three things: ease of assembly, the smooth swivel, and how easy the seats are to clean. That last one is a bigger deal than it sounds — bar stools live in the splash zone. Spilled coffee, dropped salsa, kids' sticky fingers — it all wipes off the faux leather with a damp cloth.

**Where it falls short**

I'm not going to pretend these are perfect. The faux leather is the obvious weak point. If you're using these for a busy family kitchen with daily breakfast bar meals, expect visible creasing and wear on the seat surface within 12-18 months. That's the tradeoff for a wipeable surface at this price.

The footrest is also fixed at one height. If you're under 5'4", you may find it hits your calf rather than your foot. And the chrome finish on the footrest will scuff — it's not a scratch magnet, but it's not indestructible either.

**Who should buy these**

These are ideal for anyone furnishing a home bar, kitchen island, or breakfast nook who wants the look and function of a $150-per-stool product at a fraction of the cost. They're a particularly good fit for:

- **First-time apartment dwellers** who need functional seating without the commitment of expensive furniture
- **Home entertainers** who want guests to be able to swivel and chat without scooting chairs around
- **Anyone with kids or pets** who needs surfaces that survive real life

**Skip them if** you want genuine leather, or if you're over 6'2" — the seat height tops out around 26 inches, which may feel low for taller users.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these stools actually bar height or counter height?**  
A: They're adjustable. The gas lift gives you roughly 21-26 inches of seat height, which covers both standard bar (40-42") and counter (36") setups.

**Q: How sturdy is the swivel over time?**  
A: Based on long-term reviews, the swivel holds up well. The mechanism uses ball bearings, and no one at the 6-month mark reports wobbling or loosening.

**Q: Does the set come with any tools for assembly?**  
A: Yes, there's an Allen wrench included. You'll need it for the single bolt that attaches the base. That's the entire assembly process.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a clean, functional, good-looking bar stool set that won't break your budget or your back during assembly. The 4.8-star rating is earned — these stools deliver on comfort, durability, and ease of use.

**Skip it if** you're a tall person, you demand genuine leather, or you need footrests positioned perfectly for petite users. Those are the three scenarios where these stools fall short.
