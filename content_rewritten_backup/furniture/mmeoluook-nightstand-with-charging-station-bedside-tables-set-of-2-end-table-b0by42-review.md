**The MMEOLUOOK Nightstand solves a problem most bedside tables ignore: where do your devices actually go at night?**

You've seen it a hundred times — a nightstand with a drawer crammed with tangled cables, a phone balanced on the edge, a tablet sliding off. This unit attacks that directly with a charging station built into the back panel: one standard 110V outlet and two USB-A ports. That's the headline feature, and it works exactly as advertised.

But a charging port doesn't make a good nightstand. The furniture itself has to earn its place in your bedroom. Here's what 215 Amazon reviewers and my own assessment of the specs tell you.

**What you actually get**

The package includes one assembled-in-15-minutes nightstand (the listing also offers a two-pack option). Dimensions are compact — roughly 15.7" wide, 15.7" deep, and 19.7" tall. That's a true bedside table, not a dresser. You get one soft-close drawer, one open shelf below, and a top surface that clears most adjustable bed frames.

The materials are honest mid-range: engineered wood with a matte laminate finish. It won't fool anyone into thinking it's solid oak, but it also doesn't scream "dorm room." The finish resists fingerprints well, which matters when you're grabbing your phone at 6 AM with half-open eyes.

**Real-world performance**

The charging station is the differentiator. The outlet sits flush on the back panel, slightly recessed — you can plug in a lamp or a CPAP machine without the plug jutting out awkwardly. The USB ports deliver standard 5V/2A output. It's not fast-charging, but it's consistent, and it keeps your phone topped off overnight without needing a separate brick.

One thing the product photos don't show clearly: there's no cord slot on the top surface. If you want your phone charging on the tabletop, you'll have to route the cable around the side or over the front edge. Reviewers consistently note this as a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker. One customer put it plainly: "Works great, but I wish they'd drilled a hole in the top for the cable."

The drawer is where budget furniture usually falls apart. Here, it's acceptable. The soft-close mechanism slows the slide in the last inch, but the glides feel a bit loose if your floor isn't level. A reviewer mentioned needing to adjust the feet (they're threaded, so it's fixable) to stop a subtle rattle.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The assembly experience gets repeated shout-outs. Pre-drilled holes align properly, parts are labeled, and the included hex wrench actually fits. One reviewer said: "I put this together in 12 minutes while watching TV. Every hole lined up. I was shocked."

The size also earns frequent compliments. If you have a low-profile bed frame or a platform bed, this height works well — the top sits level with most standard mattresses. For taller beds, it'll feel low, so measure before you buy.

**Where it falls short**

The open shelf is a dust magnet. Unlike a second drawer, anything you store there — books, a tablet, folded clothes — needs dusting weekly. If you hate visible clutter, this design isn't for you.

The charging station placement is the other compromise. Since the ports are on the back panel, you need clearance behind the nightstand. If you push it flush against a wall with baseboard trim, the plugs can get crushed. Pull it out an inch and you're fine, but it's a consideration.

Also note: this is not a solid-wood piece. The particleboard core means it can chip if you're rough during assembly, and the drawer capacity is modest — deep enough for a book and some odds and ends, not for folded sweaters.

**Who should buy this**

- **The cord-tangled sleeper**: If your nightstand currently looks like a spaghetti junction of charging cables, this unit consolidates it in one spot.
- **Small bedrooms**: The compact footprint works in tight spaces, and the two-pack option fits nicely on both sides of a queen bed.
- **First apartments**: It's affordable, looks modern-neutral, and won't break your back moving it up stairs.

**Skip it if** you need substantial drawer storage, prefer a solid-wood piece you'll keep for 20 years, or want your charging ports accessible from the top surface.

**Three questions shoppers actually ask**

**Q: Does the charging station work with fast-charging phones?**  
A: The USB-A ports output standard 5V/2A. You'll get a slow overnight charge, not the 25W+ fast charging your phone supports. Use the AC outlet with your own brick for fast charging.

**Q: Can I use it as a nightstand for a tall bed?**  
A: The top sits at about 19.7 inches. For a standard mattress on a box spring, it lines up well. For high-profile beds or adjustable frames with thick mattresses, it'll sit noticeably lower.

**Q: How stable is it on carpet?**  
A: It has four small feet with adjustable threaded bottoms. On plush carpet, it can wobble slightly. The adjustment feet help, but this is a lightweight piece — don't lean on it heavily.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a functional, space-efficient nightstand with integrated charging that won't break your budget. It delivers on its core promise and assembles painlessly.

**Skip it if** drawer storage is your priority, dust-free shelving matters, or you expect heirloom furniture quality at this price point.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, practical pick for modern bedrooms that trades a bit of build depth for genuine convenience. The charging station is genuinely useful, not a gimmick, and the assembly experience is better than most competitors. Just measure your bed height and accept the open-shelf dust situation before you buy.
