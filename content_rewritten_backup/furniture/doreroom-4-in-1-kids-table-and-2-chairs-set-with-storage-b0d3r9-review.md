### Where It Falls Short (Honest Tradeoffs)

1. **The storage tray is shallow.** It’s about 4 inches deep, so it holds flat items like paper, crayon boxes, and small cars. But tall toys (think Barbie dolls or chunky trucks) won’t fit under the table when closed.
2. **No chair back support for taller kids.** The chairs are sized for ages 2-5. A 6-year-old will fit, but their back will hover off the chair’s backrest. If your kid is on the taller side, measure their seated height before buying.
3. **The tabletop scratches.** Not badly, but a dropped fork or a toy with a rough edge will leave a visible mark. It’s not a showpiece—it’s a kid’s table, so this is expected.
4. **The tray has no locking mechanism.** As mentioned, a quick pull slides the entire tray out. It won’t dump on its own, but a toddler can definitely remove it.

### FAQ: 3 Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Is the storage tray removable for cleaning?**
Yes, it slides all the way out. It’s a solid plastic tray that you can wipe down or rinse. I recommend wiping it weekly—crayon bits and cracker crumbs love to collect in the corners.

**Q: Can the chairs be used separately?**
Absolutely. The chairs are not attached to the table. They’re lightweight (about 4 lbs each) and can be moved to a desk or used for story time on the floor.

**Q: What’s the weight limit?**
The table holds up to 50 lbs distributed, and each chair holds 50 lbs. That’s more than enough for a 5-year-old, but don’t let an adult sit on the chairs—they *will* flex under 150+ lbs.
