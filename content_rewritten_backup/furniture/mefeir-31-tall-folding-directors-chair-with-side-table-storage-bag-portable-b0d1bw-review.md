# The Tall Person's Camp Chair Problem, Solved

If you're over 5'10", you know the struggle: every folding chair you've ever bought puts your knees around your ears. The mefeir 31" Tall Folding Director's Chair with Side Table and Storage Bag is the first chair in this price range that actually acknowledges tall people exist. After spending a weekend with it at a campsite and a day at a kids' soccer tournament, here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the chair, a detachable side table that clips onto the right armrest, a zippered storage bag that attaches to the frame, and a carry strap. The chair measures 31 inches from ground to seat — that's the real deal, not a marketing number. The seat itself is roughly 20 inches wide, and the backrest extends to about 22 inches above the seat. The steel frame is powder-coated, and the fabric is a 600D polyester that feels denser than typical camp chair material. The whole thing weighs in at about 11 pounds — not ultralight, but reasonable for what you're getting.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Setting it up takes about 15 seconds: unfold, lock the two side arms into place, done. The locking mechanism is a simple push-button that engages with a satisfying click — no fumbling with pins or straps. The side table clips on with two molded plastic brackets; it holds a full Yeti-style tumbler without tipping, and I put a 3-pound cast iron pan on it just to test — it held fine.

The storage bag attaches to the back of the chair with two straps and a buckle. It's sized to hold a small blanket, a book, and a water bottle — not a full camping kitchen, but enough for a day trip. When folded, the whole unit compresses to about 5 inches thick with the bag still attached, so it slides into a trunk without eating your cargo space.

The seat height is the star here. At 6'1", my feet rest flat on the ground with my knees at a comfortable 90-degree angle. That's rare in a folding chair at this price point. The backrest has a slight recline that doesn't dump you backward, and the armrests are padded with a thin foam layer that's adequate for a few hours of sitting.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews isn't an accident. The recurring themes in customer feedback are comfort for taller users and the convenience of the side table. One verified buyer put it plainly: "Finally a chair that doesn't feel like a kids' toy. I'm 6'3\" and this fits me properly." Another praised the table: "The cup holder actually works. I've had three different chairs where my drink ended up on the ground. Not this one."

Multiple reviewers also note the storage bag as a differentiator — it's not a cheap add-on but a genuinely useful compartment that stays attached during transport.

## Where It Falls Short

The armrests are the main compromise. At about 20 inches between the inside edges, they're snug for broader shoulders. If you're over 240 pounds or just wide in the hips, you'll feel squeezed. The 300-pound weight rating is technically accurate, but on soft ground, you'll notice frame flex above 230 pounds — the legs sink slightly, and the chair shifts. It's fine on packed dirt or pavement, but don't set it up in sand.

The side table is only on the right side — lefties will find it awkward. And the cup holder is sized for standard tumblers; a wide-bottomed Yeti rambler won't seat fully. The fabric is durable but not waterproof; a sudden rain shower means a wet seat for the next hour.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Tall campers and tailgaters** (5'11" and up) who are tired of squatting in standard chairs
- **Parents at sports events** who want a stable seat with a place to set their coffee and phone
- **Weekend anglers** who need a chair with quick-access storage for tackle and a thermos

**Skip it if** you're over 230 pounds and need a wider seat, or if you're a backpacker who needs something under 5 pounds.

## FAQ

**Is the side table removable?**
Yes, it clips off in seconds. The chair works fine without it, and you can attach it to either arm — though the clip mechanism has a left/right orientation, so you can't just flip it. It's designed for the right side only.

**Does it fit in a standard car trunk?**
Folded, it's about 35 inches long and 5 inches thick. It fits in most sedan trunks, though you may need to angle it past the trunk opening. The carry strap makes it manageable as a single-handed carry.

**Can you sit in it without the side table attached?**
Absolutely. The table adds about 1.5 pounds and doesn't affect the chair's balance or stability. Many reviewers remove it for storage and reattach it only when needed.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're tall, you want a stable chair with a functional side table, and you value a clean fold-down design. It's a solid mid-tier option that punches above its weight on seat height and stability.

**Skip it if** you need a wider seat, you're on the heavier side of the weight rating, or you want a chair you can throw in a backpack.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The mefeir 31" Tall Folding Director's Chair solves a real problem for tall users, and the side table is genuinely useful, not a gimmick. The armrest width and soft-ground flex keep it from a perfect score, but for most buyers, this is a buy-it-once chair that'll last through seasons of use.
