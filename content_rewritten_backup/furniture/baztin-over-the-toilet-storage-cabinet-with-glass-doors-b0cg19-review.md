If your bathroom is drowning in towels, toilet paper, and the 47 half-used bottles of shampoo you swear you'll finish, the Baztin Over The Toilet Storage Cabinet is the vertical rescue mission you've been looking for. With 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, it's clearly earning its keep in real bathrooms — but let's talk about what that rating doesn't tell you before you pull the trigger.

**What Actually Shows Up at Your Door**

The box is heavy — around 38 pounds based on the shipping weight — and that's because you're getting real materials, not particleboard pretending to be wood. The frame is engineered wood with a glossy white laminate finish, and the two cabinet doors feature tempered glass panels. Tempered matters here: bathroom humidity and temperature swings will make regular glass crack over time. This won't.

Inside, each of the two cabinets has three adjustable shelves. That's six total shelving positions you can configure to fit tall bottles, stacked towels, or your ever-growing candle collection. The top of the unit is flat and solid, giving you a landing spot for decorative baskets or a small plant — though I'd skip anything that needs frequent watering.

**How It Performs in Real Bathrooms**

The selling point here is the soft-close hinge mechanism on both glass doors. It's smooth, quiet, and prevents the "slam and pray" moment when someone's reaching for a towel at 6 AM. The doors also have a magnetic catch that keeps them firmly shut — no gaping, no rattling.

Dimensionally, the cabinet measures roughly 24 inches wide, 60 inches tall, and 8 inches deep. It's designed to straddle a standard toilet with about 7 inches of clearance between the tank and the bottom shelf. If you have a tall toilet or a tank with a wide lid, measure first. A few buyers noted their elongated toilet bowls made the clearance tighter than expected, so grab a tape measure before ordering.

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

The consistent theme in positive reviews is the glass door durability. One buyer put it plainly: "I was nervous about glass in a bathroom with kids, but the tempered panels feel solid and the soft-close mechanism means little fingers can't slam them." Another reviewer noted, "The shelves hold my heaviest bottles without bowing — my previous cabinet sagged within a month." That shelf rigidity comes from the cabinet's backing being screwed in, not stapled, which is a detail cheap furniture skips.

**Where It Falls Short**

The glossy white finish is the biggest practical gripe. It looks sharp in product photos, but in a real bathroom with hard water or a steamy shower, it shows every water spot, fingerprint, and splatter. You'll be wiping it down regularly to keep it looking crisp. Plan for that.

Assembly is the second hurdle. It's not hard, but it is time-consuming — expect 90 minutes with two people, mostly because the glass panels are heavy and need steady hands to align. One reviewer summarized it well: "Doable, but don't start at 11 PM thinking you'll be done in 20 minutes." The instructions are clear, but the included Allen wrench is flimsy; grab your own hex set.

**Who Should Buy This**

This cabinet is ideal for small to medium bathrooms where floor space is precious and vertical storage is the only answer. It's also a strong pick for guest bathrooms where you want storage that doesn't look utilitarian — the glass doors elevate it beyond a basic wire shelving unit. Skip it if you have a tiny powder room with less than 24 inches of width above the toilet, or if you're not comfortable mounting furniture to the wall — the anti-tip kit is included and you should absolutely use it.

**FAQ**

**Will the glass doors fog up or warp in a steamy bathroom?** No. The tempered glass won't fog permanently, and the engineered wood frame has a moisture-resistant laminate. Wipe down any condensation that collects on the frame after hot showers.

**Can I fit a standard toilet paper pack inside?** Yes — a 12-pack of mega rolls fits flat on a shelf with room to spare. The top shelf easily handles a 24-pack if you stack it.

**Does it work with a bidet attachment?** Generally yes, if your bidet doesn't add more than 7 inches of height to the tank. Measure your setup first.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need serious vertical storage in a small bathroom and want something that looks intentional, not like a dorm room afterthought. The tempered glass doors, soft-close hinges, and six adjustable shelves justify the price point.

**Skip it if** you're not prepared for the 90-minute assembly or you hate wiping down glossy surfaces. This cabinet rewards patience and punishes neglect.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It's not perfect, but for the combination of durability, storage flexibility, and bathroom-safe materials, it earns a solid place in the over-the-toilet category.
