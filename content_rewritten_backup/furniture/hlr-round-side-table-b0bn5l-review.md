**The Short Version:** The HLR Round Side Table is the kind of piece that quietly solves a problem. It's compact, surprisingly sturdy for its price point, and looks far more expensive than it is. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, the consensus is clear: this is a dependable accent table that earns its place next to your sofa, bed, or reading chair. But it's not without a couple of small compromises worth knowing before you click "add to cart."

## What You Actually Get

The table arrives in a flat box with two main components: the round top and the tripod-style base. As shown in the product image, the design pairs a clean, circular surface with a minimalist black metal frame. The dimensions put it in the "small but useful" category — roughly the height of a standard end table but with a footprint that slides into corners or beside furniture without blocking walkways.

Assembly is genuinely tool-free. The legs twist into the central hub, and the X-cross brace locks everything into place with a satisfying click. Real talk: you'll spend more time pulling the packing materials out than actually building it. One reviewer summed it up: "Took me longer to find a pair of scissors than to put this together."

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The frame is welded steel with a matte powder-coat finish, and the top is a wood-grain laminate that passes the "is this real wood?" test from across the room. Up close, it's clearly not solid hardwood — but at this price, that's expected. The important thing is how it holds up.

The X-cross brace is the MVP here. It eliminates the sideways wobble that plagues most cheap side tables. Multiple buyers noted the stability, with one writing: "I was skeptical because it's so lightweight, but once assembled, it doesn't budge. My toddler has bumped into it multiple times and it hasn't tipped."

The adjustable leveling feet are a thoughtful touch — they let you compensate for uneven floors. That's rare at this price point and makes a real difference on carpet or older hardwood.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Scanning the reviews, three themes dominate:

1. **Sturdiness** — The X-brace design repeatedly gets called out as rock-solid.
2. **Aesthetics** — The clean, modern look photographs well and fits most decor styles.
3. **Value** — Shoppers consistently say it looks and feels more premium than the price tag suggests.

One five-star review captured the vibe: "I've bought three of these now — one for my bedroom, one for the living room, and one for my office. They're the perfect little tables and match everything."

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty time. The black metal frame is a fingerprint magnet. If you have kids or pets, you'll be wiping it down regularly. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's a maintenance consideration.

The laminate top is also prone to scratching if you drag heavy objects across it. A customer noted: "Scratched the top on the second day when I slid a laptop across it. Use a mat if you're clumsy like me."

Finally, this is a **small** table. The round top is roughly 16–18 inches in diameter. It fits a lamp, a book, and a mug — that's about it. If you need a surface for a large plant, a printer, or family-size snack bowls, keep looking.

## FAQ

**Q: Is the HLR Round Side Table stable on carpet?**
A: Yes, but the adjustable leveling feet help a lot. One leg will sink slightly into plush carpet, so take the 30 seconds to level it properly.

**Q: Can it support a heavy lamp or books?**
A: The listed weight capacity is around 20–25 pounds. A standard lamp and a couple of books are fine. A heavy printer or a stack of hardcovers? Not so much.

**Q: Is the top water-resistant?**
A: The laminate surface handles condensation rings from cold drinks, but standing water left for extended periods can cause swelling. Use coasters for anything that sweats.
