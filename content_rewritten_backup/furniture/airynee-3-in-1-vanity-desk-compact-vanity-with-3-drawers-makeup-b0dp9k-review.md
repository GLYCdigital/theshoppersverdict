## The 3-in-1 gimmick actually works — but check the fine print

Most "3-in-1" furniture pieces are marketing fluff. The Airynee Compact Vanity, however, genuinely pulls triple duty: it's a makeup station, a small writing desk, and a storage unit with three drawers. As shown in the product image, the medium size (roughly 31.5" W x 15.7" D x 29.5" H) fits neatly into bedroom corners or studio apartments. But before you click "buy," understand what you're not getting — and what buyers say fails after a few months.

## What's in the box

You get the tabletop, side panels, three drawers, drawer fronts, hardware, and an instruction booklet. No mirror, no stool, no decorative extras. The drawer runners arrive pre-installed on the drawer boxes, which cuts assembly time. Most buyers report finishing in 45–60 minutes with a Phillips screwdriver. The particleboard (MDF) pieces are pre-drilled, and the cam-lock fittings align well — a pleasant surprise at this price point.

## Performance and real-world use

The tabletop surface is smooth and easy to wipe down after foundation spills. The three drawers (each about 19.5" W x 11" D x 2.5" H) are shallow but fine for palettes, brushes, and small accessories — not for full-size hair dryers or thick binders. One buyer noted: *"The drawers slide okay when half-full, but if you cram them, they stick. I keep my heavy stuff on the bottom shelf."*

The "desk" function works best for a laptop or journaling, not for writing with pressure. The MDF top flexes slightly under heavy loads — a 30-pound monitor is pushing it. Several reviewers mentioned wobble: *"It's sturdy enough for daily use, but my cat jumping on it makes the whole thing shake. I anchored it to the wall and it's been fine."* The included anti-tip strap helps, but you'll need your own wall anchor screws.

## What buyers consistently praise

The space-saving design is the standout. Multiple reviewers called it *"perfect for small apartments"* and *"exactly what my cramped bedroom needed."* The three drawers hold a surprising amount — one buyer said they fit *"all my skincare plus my jewelry box with room to spare."* Assembly gets repeated kudos for clear labeling: *"I'm not handy and had it together in 40 minutes without swearing."* The clean, minimalist look (available in white, black, or oak) photographs well and doesn't scream "cheap" from across the room.

## Where it falls short

The mirror omission is the biggest head-scratcher. For a product marketed as a "vanity," you'd expect at least a basic mirror. You'll spend another $20–40 on a tabletop mirror or wall mount. The MDF construction is what brings the rating down to 3.6 stars. Over 15% of ratings are 1- or 2-star, with complaints clustering around: drawers that stop aligning after a month, the tabletop warping in humid climates, and the back panel being a thin fiberboard that tears if you over-tighten screws. The weight rating is also low — the manufacturer lists a 50-pound tabletop max, which limits heavy decor.

## Who should buy this

- **Studio apartment dwellers** who need a single piece to serve as vanity, desk, and storage
- **Teenagers or college students** who want a functional starter vanity without a big investment
- **Anyone with limited floor space** who prioritizes footprint over premium materials

Skip it if you need a dedicated makeup station with a mirror, if you're hard on furniture, or if you expect solid-wood durability. This is a budget-friendly, temporary-solution piece — not a heirloom.

## FAQ

**Q: Does it come with a mirror?**  
A: No. You'll need to buy a separate mirror. Many buyers mount one on the wall above it.

**Q: What's the maximum weight the drawers can hold?**  
A: The manufacturer doesn't specify per-drawer, but buyer experience suggests keeping each under 10 pounds. Heavier loads cause the runners to bind.

**Q: Can it fit a 27-inch monitor?**  
A: The desk depth is 15.7 inches, so a monitor arm is recommended. A standard monitor stand will work, but you'll have little room left for a keyboard.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a compact, multi-functional piece for a small space and accept that it's MDF, not hardwood. **Skip it if** you want a proper vanity with a mirror, or if you expect furniture that will survive multiple moves.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — Good value for the price, with honest limitations. The 3-in-1 versatility is real, but the missing mirror and wobbly build keep it from greatness.
