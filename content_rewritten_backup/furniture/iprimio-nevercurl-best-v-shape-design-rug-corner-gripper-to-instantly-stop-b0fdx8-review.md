Let me be blunt: if you've ever owned a rug that curls at the corners, you know the drill. You flatten it. It curls again. You put a book on it. It curls when you move the book. You consider gluing it to the floor. The iPrimio NeverCurl takes a different approach — and after looking at how it actually works, I can tell you it's not just another piece of adhesive tape in a fancy box.

## What You're Actually Getting

The package contains two V-shaped clear plastic grippers. That's it. No screws, no adhesive strips, no double-sided tape. The design is deceptively simple: each gripper forms a corner brace that slips under the rug's edge and pins it down using tension rather than glue.

As shown in the product image, the V-shape is the key differentiator here. Most rug corner solutions are straight strips that hold one edge. The V design grabs both sides of a corner simultaneously, which matters because curling typically starts at the point where two edges meet. One reviewer put it well: "I've tried tape, weights, and those sticky pads. This is the first thing that actually held my hallway runner flat after a week of daily foot traffic."

## Performance in Real-World Use

The grippers work on both carpet and hardwood — the mechanism is pure friction and pressure, so there's nothing to damage your floors. On standard low-to-medium pile rugs (think typical 8x10 living room rugs from big-box stores), they hold corners flat effectively. The clear plastic is unobtrusive; from three feet away you won't notice it unless you're looking.

What impressed me in the feedback is the longevity. Multiple buyers mention these surviving vacuuming, kids running past, and pets. One customer noted, "My dog does zoomies across this rug every morning. Corners stayed put for two months now." That's the real test — not whether it works on day one, but whether it holds after the novelty wears off.

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest tradeoff: you get two grippers per pack. A standard rectangular rug has four corners. If all four are curling — which is common with new rugs — you're buying two packs. At typical pricing that's not outrageous, but it's an extra purchase you should budget for.

More importantly, these won't work on every rug. If your rug has a thick, plush backing — the kind that's over half an inch of dense padding — the gripper can't get enough purchase to hold the corner down. It'll slip off. This is a friction-based solution, and friction needs surface contact. One reviewer found this the hard way: "Didn't work on my shag rug with the thick rubber backing. Returned them."

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have a standard-pile rug with corners that curl on hardwood, tile, or low carpet. You want a no-mess solution that doesn't involve adhesives you'll regret later. You're tired of re-flattening the same corner every time you walk past.

**Skip it if:** Your rug has plush high-pile backing, or you want to fix all four corners with a single purchase. Also skip if you need something invisible from two feet away — the grippers are discreet but not invisible.

## FAQ

**Will these damage my hardwood floors?**
No. The grippers use pressure, not adhesive. There's no sticky residue and no surface contact that could scratch. The bottom is smooth plastic.

**Can I use them on outdoor rugs?**
Technically yes, but they're not weatherproof. Rain and UV exposure will degrade the plastic over time. Keep them indoors.

**Do they work on thick berber carpet?**
Berber's tight weave is actually ideal — the gripper gets good purchase on the edges. Yes, they'll hold.
