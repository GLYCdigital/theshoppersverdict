Let me be straight with you: most end tables with charging ports are gimmicks. The ports are placed where you can't reach them, the cord is three inches long, and the whole thing wobbles after a month. The ZEXVIDA Small Round End Table with Charging Station isn't that — but it's not perfect either. Here's the real story after digging through 220 ratings and buyer feedback.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the table in three main pieces (top tier, middle shelf, bottom shelf), the pole assembly, and a hardware kit. Assembly takes roughly 15-20 minutes with the included Allen wrench. No power tools needed. The charging station — two USB-A ports and one standard AC outlet — is pre-wired into the top tier, so you don't have to fumble with any electrical work.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The round top measures about 15.5 inches in diameter, with the middle shelf at 13 inches and the bottom at 14 inches. Total height sits around 21 inches, which works well beside a standard sofa or recliner. The particleboard construction is what you'd expect at this price point — it's not heirloom furniture, but it's solid enough for a drink, a lamp, and your phone.

The charging station is the headline feature, and it works as advertised. Plug the attached cord into your wall outlet, and you've got two USB ports for your phone and earbuds plus a three-prong AC outlet for a lamp or laptop charger. The ports are located on the side of the top tier, which keeps cables tidy and out of the way.

Here's the catch: the power cord is only about 4 feet long. If your couch isn't within arm's reach of a wall outlet, you're buying an extension cord. This came up repeatedly in buyer feedback. One reviewer put it plainly: "The cord is too short to reach from the wall behind the couch. I had to buy a 6-foot extension cord."

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The three-tier design gets genuine love. It's the reason most people chose this over a standard end table. The vertical storage means you can keep books on the bottom, a basket of remotes on the middle, and your drink on top — all in a footprint small enough for tight corners.

Assembly ease is another recurring compliment. Multiple buyers mentioned having it together in under 20 minutes with no frustration. The hardware is labeled clearly, and the instructions are actually readable — rarer than it should be.

Sturdiness surprised a few people. One reviewer noted, "I was expecting it to wobble since it's a pole design, but it's rock solid. My cat jumps on it daily and it hasn't budged." That tracks with the design — the pole base threads into a weighted bottom plate, and the tiers lock in with set screws.

**Where It Falls Short**

The particleboard construction means water damage is a real risk. A sweaty glass or a spilled drink left unattended will stain the surface. Use coasters. This isn't optional.

The charging station is convenient but not fast. The USB ports output standard 5V/1A, which is fine for overnight charging but won't give you the rapid charge you get from a modern wall adapter. If you're expecting USB-C Power Delivery, this isn't it.

The small footprint cuts both ways. The top tier fits one standard coffee mug and a TV remote. If you're the type who keeps a stack of books, reading glasses, and a laptop on your end table, this will feel cramped.

**Who Should Buy This**

This table is ideal for small apartments, dorms, or anyone with a tight living room layout. The round shape and narrow profile let it fit where rectangular tables won't, and the three tiers give you storage without sacrificing floor space. It's also a solid pick for a bedside table if you need a charging station within arm's reach of your pillow.

Skip it if you need a large surface for daily work-from-home setup, or if you're looking for furniture that'll survive a move without scratches. This is a practical, budget-friendly piece, not a long-term investment.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Can the charging station be used without the USB ports?**
A: Yes. The AC outlet works independently of the USB ports. You can plug a lamp or charger into the AC outlet and still use both USB ports simultaneously.

**Q: How much weight can each tier hold?**
A: The top tier handles 15-20 pounds comfortably. The middle and bottom shelves hold about 10 pounds each. Don't use the middle shelf as a footrest — the set screws will loosen.

**Q: Does the table come with a warranty?**
A: ZEXVIDA offers a standard 12-month warranty covering manufacturing defects. Reach out to the seller through Amazon if you have issues with the charging station or hardware.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a compact, multi-tier end table with built-in charging for a small space. The assembly is painless, the design is practical, and the charging station actually works.

**Skip it if** you demand solid wood construction, need a larger surface area, or don't want to deal with the short power cord.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A genuinely useful small-space table with a functional charging station. The short cord and particleboard construction keep it from being a slam dunk, but at this price, it delivers real value.
