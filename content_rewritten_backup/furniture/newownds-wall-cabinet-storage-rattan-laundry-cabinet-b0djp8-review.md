If your laundry room looks like a chaotic pile of detergent bottles, dryer sheets, and stray socks, the NEWOWNDS Wall Cabinet Storage-Rattan Laundry Cabinet is trying to fix that with style. The pitch is simple: hang it on the wall, hide the mess, and add some warm boho texture while you're at it. With a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings, it's clearly working for a lot of people. But is it right for your space? Let's dig in.

**What you actually get**

The package includes the cabinet body, two rattan-front doors, a fixed interior shelf, mounting screws, and wall anchors. As shown in the product image, the design is clean: a rectangular MDF frame with woven rattan panels set into the door fronts. It mounts flush to the wall, so you don't lose any floor space — a significant win for cramped laundry closets. The dimensions are compact enough for above-the-washer or beside-the-dryer placement but generous enough to swallow a full load of folding supplies.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The MDF frame is solid, not flimsy — it holds its shape and doesn't wobble once properly anchored. The rattan is genuine woven material, not printed vinyl, which gives it a tactile warmth that cheap alternatives lack. The doors align well out of the box, and the magnetic catch keeps them snugly closed. The fixed shelf is sturdy and can handle a stack of folded towels or a box of detergent without sagging.

That said, the hinges are standard friction hinges, not soft-close. If you're the type to let a door drop, you'll hear it. And the included wall anchors are basic — fine for stud mounting, but if you're going into drywall, spend a few dollars on heavier toggle bolts for peace of mind. One buyer noted, "The cabinet is beautiful and well-made, but I replaced the anchors before hanging it because my wall is old plaster." That's the kind of practical insight worth heeding.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The aesthetic is the headline. Multiple reviewers describe it as "exactly the boho look I wanted" and "a beautiful upgrade from the plastic shelving I had before." The space-saving aspect also gets consistent love — especially from apartment dwellers and people with side-by-side washers who had zero usable floor storage. Several mention it's sturdy enough to hold more than just laundry supplies, with one using it in a bathroom for towels and toiletries.

**Where it falls short**

Beyond the hinge and anchor caveats, the fixed shelf is the biggest limitation. You can't adjust it to fit taller spray bottles or a bulk-size detergent jug — you'll have to lay those flat or store them elsewhere. It's a minor annoyance, but one worth knowing before you commit. The rattan also requires a bit of care; it's not waterproof, so keep it away from direct splashes and wipe spills promptly.

**Who should buy this**

- **Small-space dwellers** who need vertical storage without sacrificing floor area
- **Boho and farmhouse decor lovers** who want function that doesn't look utilitarian
- **DIYers comfortable with a drill** who can handle basic wall mounting (or have a stud where they want it)
- **Anyone tired of cluttered countertops** who wants a quick visual upgrade in a utility space

**FAQ**

**Q: Can this hold real weight, or is it just decorative?**
A: When mounted into studs or with quality anchors, it's genuinely functional. The shelf can handle a full stack of towels or several bottles of detergent. Just don't overload it — it's a wall cabinet, not a bookcase.

**Q: Is the rattan hard to clean?**
A: Not really. A quick wipe with a slightly damp cloth handles dust and minor splashes. Avoid soaking it, and don't use harsh chemical cleaners that could discolor the weave.

**Q: Does it require professional installation?**
A: No. If you can use a level and a drill, you can install this in under an hour. The pre-drilled holes align well, and the included template helps with placement.

The NEWOWNDS Wall Cabinet is a well-executed, attractive solution for a genuinely common problem. It's not perfect — the fixed shelf and basic hinges are honest compromises — but for the price and the look it delivers, it's a strong value.
