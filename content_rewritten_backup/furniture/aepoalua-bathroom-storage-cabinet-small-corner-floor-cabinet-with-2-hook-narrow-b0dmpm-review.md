If your bathroom has that awkward corner that swallows floor space and collects dust, the AEPOALUA Small Corner Floor Cabinet is aimed squarely at you. This narrow tower is designed to slip into the dead zone next to a toilet or beside a pedestal sink, giving you closed storage where you previously had nothing but wasted square footage.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes the cabinet body, two adjustable-ish shelves (more on that in a moment), and two side-mounted hooks. The cabinet measures about 11.8 inches wide, 23.6 inches deep, and 47.2 inches tall — dimensions that matter when you're measuring that tight corner. The surface is a matte white waterproof finish that resists the splashes and steam that wreck lesser furniture.

As shown in the product image, the design is clean and minimal — a single door with a simple handle, two hooks on the side, and a slim profile that doesn't overwhelm a small bathroom visually.

## Real-World Performance

The MDF construction feels solid for the price point. The waterproof coating isn't just cosmetic — users report it withstands daily humidity without swelling or peeling. One reviewer noted, *"I've had this in my bathroom for three months and there's no warping, even with hot showers every morning."* That's the kind of durability you want in a room where moisture is constant.

The two hooks are genuinely useful. They're positioned at a height that works for hanging a bathrobe or a hand towel, and they save you from drilling into tile. A customer mentioned, *"The hooks are sturdier than I expected — my heavy terry cloth robe hangs fine without sagging."*

Storage capacity is modest by design. The interior is roughly 10 inches deep and about 9.5 inches wide, which fits standard toiletries, cleaning supplies, or extra toilet paper rolls. It's not a linen closet substitute — it's a smart-use-the-corner solution.

## Where It Falls Short

Assembly is the biggest friction point. Several reviewers describe the process as "fiddly" and "time-consuming." The instructions are minimal, and some panels require you to align screws blind. Budget 45 minutes and have a screwdriver ready — a power drill with a clutch setting helps.

The fixed shelf positions are another limitation. You get two internal shelves, but they're not adjustable. Stacked bottles over 10 inches tall won't fit on the lower shelf. One buyer wrote, *"My tall shampoo bottles had to go on the top — the bottom shelf is too short for them."* Plan your storage around that constraint.

The back panel is thin. It's fine for stability once assembled, but it won't take heavy pressure. Don't shove the cabinet hard against a wall with items pressing against the back.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Small bathroom owners** — if you're working with under 50 square feet, this corner cabinet is a genuine space-saver.
- **Renters** — the hooks eliminate wall damage, and the cabinet itself is freestanding, so it moves with you.
- **Anyone with a toilet alcove** — the narrow width fits the gap beside most standard toilets.

**Skip it if** you need serious storage capacity, or if you have zero patience for multi-step assembly. Also skip if your bathroom has very uneven floors — the feet are not individually adjustable, so a slightly tilted floor will wobble.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this hold the weight of a small countertop sink?**
A: No. This is a storage cabinet, not a vanity. The MDF top is rated for lighter items like soap dispensers and small baskets, not plumbing fixtures.

**Q: Does the door open fully in a tight corner?**
A: Yes, the hinge allows about 100 degrees of opening, but account for the door swing when positioning. Leave at least 12 inches of clearance in front.

**Q: Is the waterproofing actually effective?**
A: For normal bathroom humidity and incidental splashes, yes. It's not rated for direct standing water or soaking wet floors, so keep it out of direct shower spray.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're fighting for floor space in a small bathroom and need closed storage that disappears into a corner. The design is practical, the hooks add real value, and the waterproof finish holds up in humid conditions.

**Skip it if** you're looking for adjustable shelving, heavy-duty construction, or a quick 10-minute assembly. This cabinet rewards patience and punishes rushing.
