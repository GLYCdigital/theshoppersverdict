The LOKKHAN Industrial Bar Table isn't trying to be a dining table. It's a hybrid — part standing desk, part bar counter, part accent piece. And for the price, it does something most tables in this category won't: it adjusts height without tools and swivels on its base. That's a genuinely useful combination, especially if your space does double duty as a home office by day and a cocktail corner by night.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the round tabletop (roughly 24 inches in diameter), the steel pedestal base, a footrest ring, and the pneumatic lift column. The finish is a matte dark wood grain on the top with a black powder-coated steel frame — the industrial look is consistent with the product photos. The base is heavy enough to keep things stable, which matters because the swivel mechanism only works if the table doesn't shift when you rotate it. One Amazon buyer put it simply: *"Feels solid, not wobbly like some of the cheaper ones I looked at."*

**How it performs in real use**

The headline feature is the height adjustment. A gas-lift mechanism lets you move the surface between 38.6 and 48.4 inches — that's roughly counter height to standing bar height. You press a lever and lift or lower; it's smooth, not jerky. That range makes it viable for both seated dining on standard bar stools and standing work with a laptop. The swivel top is the sleeper feature: you can rotate the surface toward a sofa or pivot it out of the way without dragging the entire table across the floor.

The 220-pound weight capacity is honest. I wouldn't lean on it with your full body weight, but for drinks, plates, a laptop, or a small spread of appetizers, it's more than adequate.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the 225+ ratings (4.5 stars), the recurring themes are stability and versatility. One verified purchase noted: *"I use it as a standing desk during the day and a bar table for game night. Takes five seconds to switch."* Another praised the footprint: *"Small enough for my apartment but doesn't look like a cheap folding table."* The matte finish hides fingerprints and smudges well, which is a small but real quality-of-life win.

**Where it falls short**

The particleboard top is the biggest compromise. It's not engineered for heavy abuse — dropping a cast-iron skillet on it will leave a dent. The edge banding is thin, so if you're careless with a cart or vacuum, you can chip it. Assembly is also a two-person job, primarily because the gas cylinder needs to be aligned perfectly to seat into the base. One reviewer said: *"Took us 40 minutes and some swearing. Not hard, just awkward."* Fair warning.

The footrest ring sits at roughly 12 inches off the floor. If you're 6'2" or taller, it'll feel too low to be useful. That's a minor complaint, but worth knowing if you plan to use it with tall stools.

**Who should buy this**

- **Apartment dwellers with limited square footage** — the adjustable height eliminates the need for separate desk and bar tables.
- **Home entertainers** who want a flexible serving surface that can move between kitchen and living room.
- **Remote workers** who alternate between sitting and standing and want something with more character than a standard standing desk.

**Who should skip it**

- **Serious home chefs** who need a durable prep surface — the particleboard won't survive knife work or hot pans.
- **Anyone expecting a traditional, heavy pub table** — this is lighter and more mobile than it looks.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is the height adjustment actually smooth?**  
A: Yes, the gas lift is the strongest part of the build. It moves evenly without sticking, and it locks firmly at your chosen height.

**Q: Can one person assemble it?**  
A: Technically, but you'll want help. The cylinder alignment is the tricky part — a second set of hands makes it 10x easier.

**Q: Will the swivel top scratch the base?**  
A: There's a bearing ring between the top and column, so it rotates without metal-on-metal contact. No scratching reported in reviews.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a compact, height-adjustable table that works for both work and entertaining. The swivel top and gas lift are features you'll actually use, not gimmicks.

**Skip it if** you need a heavy-duty work surface or you're over 6'2" and want a functional footrest.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It's not perfect, but at this price point, the LOKKHAN delivers more versatility than anything else in its class. The particleboard top is the only real compromise, and for most buyers, it's a fair trade for the adjustability.
