**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

The tempered glass is the standout feature here. It’s safety-rated, which matters if you’ve got kids or pets bumping into furniture. The versatility is the recurring theme. People are using this cabinet for everything from china collections to sneaker displays to bar setups. One verified purchase wrote, “I bought this for my wife’s crystal collection, and it looks like a million bucks in our dining room.” Another reviewer mentioned using it in a home office to display signed memorabilia.

**Where It Falls Short**

Here’s the honest part. The glass shows every fingerprint, every smudge, every dust particle. If you’re someone who hates cleaning glass, this cabinet will test your patience. You’ll want a microfiber cloth and glass cleaner within arm’s reach.

The back panel is thin fiberboard. It does its job — it holds the cabinet square and looks fine from the outside — but it’s not furniture-grade material. A few reviewers mentioned that the screw holes in the back panel can strip if you overtighten. Go easy with the drill.

Also worth noting: the cabinet is heavy once assembled. Plan where you want it before you build it, because moving it afterward is a two-person job.
