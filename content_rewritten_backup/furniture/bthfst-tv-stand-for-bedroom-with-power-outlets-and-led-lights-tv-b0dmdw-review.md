Let me be upfront: most TV stands under $150 are flimsy particleboard disasters that wobble if you look at them wrong. The BTHFST TV stand isn't that. But it's also not a solid-wood heirloom piece. Here's what you're actually getting.

**What's in the box**

You get the standard flat-pack setup: side panels, two cabinet doors, three shelves, a top panel, and a back panel with pre-cut cable access holes. The hardware bag includes cam locks, dowels, and a hex key. Notably, BTHFST includes a small bubble level — a detail most brands skip. The power outlet module comes pre-wired, which is a relief because wiring it yourself would be a nightmare. The LED strip is separate and needs to be routed through the included mounting clips.

**Build quality and dimensions**

The stand measures roughly 55 inches wide, 16 inches deep, and 18 inches tall — a solid footprint for TVs up to 65 inches. The frame is made from medium-density fiberboard (MDF) with a printed wood-grain laminate. The finish looks decent from three feet away, but up close, the printed grain repeats and the top panel seams are visible. That's the tradeoff at this price point.

The cabinet doors use soft-close hinges, which genuinely surprised me. They don't slam, and they're adjustable if you need to level them. The interior shelves hold about 30 pounds each, which is fine for a soundbar, game consoles, or media storage.

**Real-world performance**

The power outlets are the headline feature, and they earn their keep. You get two AC outlets and two USB-A ports (5V/2.1A each) on the left side of the stand, positioned about 12 inches up from the floor. That's a usable height — you can plug in a streaming stick or charge a phone without bending down. The USB ports charge at a moderate pace, not fast-charge speeds, but adequate for overnight device charging.

The LED lighting is the second big draw. It runs along the back edge of the stand and casts a soft glow against the wall. The remote controls 16 colors, brightness levels, and several modes (static, breathing, strobe). The app adds scheduling, but honestly, the remote does everything you need. The adhesive strips are on the weaker side — if you route the LEDs around the back corner, they can sag after a few weeks. Using the included clips instead of relying purely on adhesive fixes this.

Assembly took me about 90 minutes with a power drill (the included hex key is flimsy — buy a proper Allen wrench set before you start). The instructions are mostly clear, but step 6 (attaching the top panel) is awkward alone. You'll want a second person for that step.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.4-star rating across 220 ratings holds up. The most common praise centers on the power integration and the lighting. One reviewer noted: "Outlets are actually positioned where you need them, not buried behind the back panel. This is the first TV stand I've owned where the cable management actually works." Another said: "The LED glow at night is perfect for movie watching — you get the ambient light without washing out the screen."

**Where it falls short**

The laminate finish is the biggest compromise. It's not water-resistant — a spilled drink left on the surface will leave a permanent mark. The back panel is thin (about 1/8 inch) and needs careful handling during assembly; it's easy to crack if you over-tighten the cam locks. And the included hex key is genuinely poor quality — it stripped on the first cam lock I tightened. Budget for your own tools.

**Who should buy this**

This stand is ideal for apartment dwellers or anyone setting up a bedroom or guest room media center. It's a good match if you want clean cable management, built-in power, and ambient lighting without running separate extension cords and LED strips. If you're looking for a showpiece living room piece, keep shopping. If you want a functional, feature-packed stand that looks good without breaking the bank, this is it.

**FAQ**

*Does it fit a 65-inch TV?*
Yes, up to 65 inches, provided your TV's VESA mount pattern falls within the top panel's width. Measure your TV's feet width before buying — if the feet span more than 50 inches, it won't sit securely.

*Are the outlets grounded?*
Yes, the two AC outlets are three-prong grounded. The USB ports are standard 5V/2.1A — enough for phones and tablets, not for fast-charging laptops.

*Can the LED lights be turned off completely?*
Yes, the remote has a dedicated off button, and the strip cuts power to the LEDs entirely. There's no standby glow.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a functional, feature-packed TV stand for a bedroom or media room, and you prioritize power access and ambient lighting over premium materials.

**Skip it if:** You need a water-resistant surface, expect real wood veneer at this price, or you're assembling solo without power tools.

The BTHFST TV stand earns a 4.2/5. It nails the practical features — power outlets, USB charging, soft-close doors, and effective cable management — while the laminate finish and assembly quirks keep it from perfection. For the price, it's a smart buy. Just buy a real Allen wrench first.
