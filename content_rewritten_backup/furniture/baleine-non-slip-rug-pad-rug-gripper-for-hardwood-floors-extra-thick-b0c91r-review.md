A rug that slides on hardwood is more than an annoyance — it's a trip hazard. The BALEINE Non Slip Rug Pad is built to solve that specific problem, and based on 220 ratings averaging 4.4 stars, it's doing something right. Here's the honest breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The BALEINE pad arrives rolled tight in plastic. As shown in the product image, it's a dense, felt-like pad with a textured rubber backing — not the flimsy mesh you see with cheap grippers. The extra thick 8mm version is what most buyers opt for, and it makes a visible difference under thin rugs. You're getting a single pad sized to your rug dimensions (not a roll you cut to length), so measure your space before ordering. Scissors trim it easily if your rug isn't a perfect rectangle.

**Performance and Build Quality**

This pad uses a two-layer construction: a soft felt top that cushions the rug and a rubber underside that grips the floor. On hardwood, that rubber surface creates serious friction. One verified buyer noted, *"I have a 5x8 wool rug that used to slide every time my dog ran past. This pad stopped it completely — the rug doesn't move an inch."* That tracks with what the gripper does best: locking down medium-to-heavy rugs on smooth surfaces.

The 8mm thickness is the standout feature. It adds noticeable plushness under thin flatweaves, and it smooths over minor hardwood unevenness that can cause rugs to ripple. It also protects your floor from the abrasive backing some rugs have — a real concern if you've ever seen the black marks a cheap rug leaves behind.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The grip is the headline. Multiple reviewers mention placing the pad and immediately testing it with their feet — no sliding, no bunching. The carpet-like top surface also helps keep the rug from shifting against the pad itself, which is a problem with thinner grippers. Another recurring positive: the pad doesn't leave sticky residue on hardwood when removed, unlike adhesive-backed alternatives.

**Where It Falls Short**

The rubber smell is real. Several reviewers mention needing to air the pad out for a few days before installation, especially in warmer rooms. It's not overpowering, but it's noticeable if you're sensitive to odors.

The bigger issue: lightweight rugs still slide at the edges. The pad grips the center of the rug well, but if you have a thin cotton or low-pile rug with curled corners, those edges can work loose over time. One reviewer put it plainly: *"Works great for my medium-weight rug, but my thin hallway runner still creeps at the ends."* If your rug is featherweight, consider a rug with a thicker backing or pair this with corner grippers.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Hardwood floor owners** with medium-to-heavy rugs that slide or bunch — this is your fix
- **Anyone with thin rugs** who wants added padding and floor protection in one product
- **Renters** who need a non-damaging solution — no adhesive, no residue, no floor marks
- **People with odd-shaped rugs** who need something they can trim to fit

Skip it if your rug is extremely lightweight, if you're sensitive to rubber odors, or if you're looking for a permanent solution for curling edges (you'll want carpet tape for that).

**FAQ**

**Will this pad damage my hardwood floors?**
No. The rubber backing is non-abrasive and leaves no adhesive residue. The felt top also protects your rug's backing from wearing against the floor.

**Can I use this on carpet?**
Technically yes, but it's overkill. The gripper works best on smooth, hard surfaces. On carpet, the rubber backing won't get the same friction and the pad may shift underfoot.

**How do I clean it?**
Vacuum it regularly or shake it out. For spills, spot-clean with a damp cloth and let it air dry completely before putting the rug back. Don't machine wash it.

**The Verdict**

**Skip it if** your rug is ultra-thin (edge sliding persists), you can't tolerate rubber smell for a few days, or you need a solution for curled corners rather than general sliding.
