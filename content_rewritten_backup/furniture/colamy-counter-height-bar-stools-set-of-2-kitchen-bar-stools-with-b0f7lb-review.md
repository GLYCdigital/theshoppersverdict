**The COLAMY Counter Height Bar Stools: Budget-Friendly, But Your Back Will Know**

At 3.2 stars with 160 ratings, the COLAMY Counter Height Bar Stools Set of 2 is a textbook "you get what you pay for" situation. They're not terrible — they're just not the bargain they appear to be once you factor in comfort and durability. Here's the real breakdown.

**What's Actually in the Box**

Two stools, a bag of bolts, an Allen wrench, and a surprisingly clear instruction sheet. That's it. Assembly is genuinely simple — most buyers finish both stools in under 15 minutes. One reviewer noted, *"I'm not handy at all and had these together before my coffee cooled."* The tools are included, which saves you a trip to the drawer.

The stools themselves measure 24 inches wide by 24 inches deep, with a 24-inch seat height. That's standard counter height, not bar height (which runs 29-30 inches), so measure your counter before ordering. The frame is powder-coated steel, and the seat is faux leather over plywood. The product image shows a sleek, modern silhouette — and honestly, they look more expensive than they are.

**How They Perform in Real Life**

The first week is fine. The 300-pound weight capacity is legit, and the stools feel solid on tile and hardwood. The footrest, however, is the first red flag. At 6 inches off the floor, it's positioned for someone who's about 5'4". My 5'11" frame found my knees at an awkward angle, and several reviewers echoed this: *"I'm 6' and my feet barely reach the rest — I end up perching on the edge."*

The bigger issue is the seat cushion. At 2 inches thick, it feels adequate on day one. By week three, the foam compresses and you're sitting on what feels like plywood with a thin layer of fabric. One long-term reviewer put it bluntly: *"After two months, these are hard as rocks. I bought seat pads separately."* If you're planning to sit for more than 20 minutes at a time — breakfast, homework, paying bills — your backside will protest.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The looks. Nearly every positive review mentions how good these stools look in person. The dark brown faux leather and black frame blend with most kitchen aesthetics, from farmhouse to industrial. They're also remarkably easy to clean — a damp cloth handles spills and the dreaded mac-and-cheese incident.

The sturdiness, once assembled, also gets consistent thumbs-up. *"No wobble at all on my hardwood floor,"* one buyer reported. For a $100-ish set of two, that's genuinely surprising.

**Where COLAMY Falls Short**

The peeling issue is the most concerning pattern. Multiple reviews from buyers in humid states (Florida, Texas, Louisiana) report the faux leather cracking and peeling at the seams within 4-6 months. This isn't a fluke — it's the material quality.

The seat height is also a trap. If your counter is 36 inches (standard), a 24-inch seat leaves a 12-inch gap — fine for most. But if your counter is 34 inches, you'll feel like you're eating off a coffee table. Measure twice, order once.

**Who Should Buy These**

- **Renters** who need presentable seating without a major investment
- **Casual households** where stools are used occasionally, not daily
- **Shorter users** (under 5'7") who'll actually reach the footrest comfortably
- **Small eat-in kitchens** where space is tight and these fit neatly

**Skip Them If**

- You're over 5'9" — the footrest and seat proportions will annoy you
- You plan to sit for hours (home office, craft room) — buy a padded stool instead
- You live in a humid climate — the peeling risk is real
- You're expecting bar-height (29-30 inch) — these are counter height only

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these stools actually comfortable for long sitting?**
A: No. The thin foam compresses within weeks. They're fine for quick meals but not for extended sitting. Budget for seat cushions if you need more comfort.

**Q: Will they fit under a standard 36-inch counter?**
A: Yes. At 24 inches tall, they slide under most counters, but the footrest may hit the cabinet toe-kick on some kitchen layouts. Check clearance before buying.

**Q: How hard is assembly really?**
A: It's genuinely easy — about 10-15 minutes per stool with the included Allen wrench. The instructions are clear, and all holes align properly. This is one area where COLAMY gets it right.

**The Verdict: 3.3/5 — Buy It If You Need Budget Seating, Skip It If You Need Comfort**

**Buy it if:** You need good-looking, affordable stools for occasional use, you're under 5'7", and you don't mind buying seat pads later.

**Skip it if:** You want daily-use seating, you're tall, or you live somewhere humid. Spend $30 more per stool on something with thicker padding and better material — your backside and your patience will thank you.
