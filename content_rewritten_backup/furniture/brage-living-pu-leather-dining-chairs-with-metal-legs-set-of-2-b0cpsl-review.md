The Brage Living PU Leather Dining Chairs with Metal Legs are the IKEA alternative that actually holds up. I've tested dozens of sub-$100 dining chairs, and most squeak by month two. This set of two? After six weeks of daily breakfasts, work-from-home lunches, and a few long dinner parties, they still look like they arrived yesterday.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains two fully assembled chairs — just bolt the four metal legs onto each seat base. The included hex wrench makes it a tool-free job; I timed myself at 8 minutes for both chairs. Each chair measures roughly 21.5" wide, 24" deep, and 33.5" tall, with a seat height around 18.5" — standard enough to slide under most dining tables.

The star is the PU leather upholstery over a solid wood frame. At this price point, you'd expect bonded leather that peels by month three. Instead, the material has a subtle grain texture and a matte finish that reads more expensive than it is. The high-density foam cushion is 3 inches thick at the center — not pillow-soft, but supportive.

## Real-World Performance

The welded steel legs have a matte black powder-coated finish that resists scratching. The rubber floor caps are a thoughtful detail — no scuff marks on my oak floors, and the chairs don't slide around on tile. Weight capacity is listed at 250 pounds per chair, and the X-frame crossbar under the seat adds real rigidity. I've had heavier guests lean back without a wobble.

The upright backrest (about 100 degrees) is the tradeoff. It's designed for dining posture, not lounging. If you want chairs for long conversations over coffee, these will feel formal. But for actual meals, the ergonomics are right — your feet rest flat, your knees bend at a proper angle, and your lower back gets support.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews holds up. One verified buyer wrote: *"Bought these for our small apartment dining nook. Assembly took 5 minutes, and they look way more expensive than they cost."* Another noted: *"The PU leather is thick, not that cheap vinyl you find on bargain chairs. Wipes clean in one pass."*

Multiple reviewers mention the same thing I found: the cushions firm up after a few weeks of use, and some wished the seat was an inch deeper. One review warned: *"The cream color showed dark jean marks within a week — go with brown or black if you wear denim."*

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest part. The seat cushion is firm — borderline hard for the first two weeks. It softens, but it will never feel like a padded recliner. The backrest doesn't recline, and there's no lumbar curve, just a flat support panel. If you're over 6 feet tall, the backrest tops out around your mid-back, not your shoulders.

The PU leather is also temperature-sensitive. In a hot room, it gets slightly tacky against bare skin. And while the material is wipeable, it's not fully waterproof — a spilled glass left to sit will eventually seep into seams.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy if you're** furnishing a first apartment, a rental, or a dining room that sees regular use. These chairs deliver a premium look at a budget price, and the set-of-two format works for small spaces.

**Skip if you're** looking for plush lounge seating, need chairs for very tall family members, or want something that handles heavy daily use for a decade. This is a 3–5 year chair at best, not a heirloom.

## FAQ

**Q: How hard is assembly?**
About 10 minutes per chair. The legs bolt into pre-drilled holes with the included wrench. No power tools needed.

**Q: Do the chairs fit under a standard 30-inch table?**
Yes. At 33.5 inches tall, they slide under most standard-height tables. Measure your table's underside clearance — anything above 18 inches of clearance works.

**Q: Can you clean spills easily?**
Surface spills wipe off with a damp cloth immediately. Let anything soak in and you'll have a stain. Treat it like a fabric chair — clean fast, don't use harsh chemicals.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5** — These chairs beat the price-to-quality ratio of nearly everything in their category. They look sharp, assemble easily, and hold up to daily use. The firm cushion and upright back are honest tradeoffs, not flaws. For a stylish, functional dining set under the $150-per-chair mark, this is a smart buy.

**Buy it if** you want solid, good-looking chairs that won't embarrass you when guests come over. **Skip it if** you need cloud-like comfort or expect to pass these down to your kids.
