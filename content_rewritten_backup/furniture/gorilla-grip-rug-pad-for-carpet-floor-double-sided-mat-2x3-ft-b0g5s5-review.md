## The GORILLA GRIP Rug Pad: A Solid Grip, But Is It Built to Last?

Here's the thing about rug pads for carpeted floors: they're solving a problem most people don't realize they have until their rug is a wrinkled mess. The GORILLA GRIP 2x3 double-sided pad promises to fix that — and for many buyers, it does. But with a 3.5-star average across 175 ratings, there's clearly a split opinion on whether this pad earns its place under your rug.

## What You Actually Get

The package contains a single 2x3 foot rectangular pad, roughly the same footprint as a standard entryway mat or small kitchen runner. It's constructed as a double-sided grip surface — both faces have a textured, slightly rubberized feel designed to grab the rug above and the carpet below without using adhesives.

The material is noticeably thinner than premium felt-and-rubber hybrid pads, coming in around 1/8 inch thick. That's fine for keeping a rug from sliding, but it won't add meaningful cushioning or sound absorption. If you're looking for padding underfoot, this isn't it.

## Performance: The Good, The Bad, The Honest Truth

**The grip itself works.** On carpet, the pad's underside bites into the fibers and stays put. The top surface holds a standard rug firmly enough that you won't see shifting after vacuuming or foot traffic. Several reviewers noted this specifically:

> "Works exactly as described. My runner hasn't moved an inch since I put this underneath."

That's the core promise, and for the first few months, the GORILLA GRIP delivers consistently.

**But here's where it gets complicated.** The 3.5-star average isn't an accident. Multiple reviewers report the same issue: edge curling. After several weeks, the corners and edges of the pad start to bend upward, which creates visible bumps under your rug and actually defeats the purpose of the pad — the rug begins to wrinkle right above the curled edges.

The thin construction is the likely culprit. A thicker pad with a felt layer would hold its shape better; this one relies entirely on the rubberized coating for structure, and that coating can fatigue over time.

**One more practical note:** if you're placing this under a rug that takes heavy foot traffic near a desk or entryway, the compression marks are real. Chairs with casters will leave permanent indentations, and the pad won't spring back. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you commit.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

- **No adhesive mess** — Unlike sticky-backed pads, this one is repositionable and leaves no residue on your carpet. Multiple reviewers highlighted this as a major plus.
- **Immediate grip** — Most users report the pad works right out of the box, no break-in period needed.
- **Easy to trim** — If your rug isn't exactly 2x3, the pad cuts cleanly with scissors, which a few reviewers mentioned as a practical bonus.

## Where It Falls Short

The durability concern is the elephant in the room. For a product carrying the GORILLA GRIP name (which implies rugged, long-lasting construction), the edge curling reports are disappointing. If you're placing this in a low-traffic area and don't plan to move it often, you'll likely be fine. But if you need a pad that will withstand daily use for years, this may not be your best option.

There's also the sizing limitation. At 2x3 feet, it's specifically for small rugs. If you need larger coverage, you'll have to buy multiple pads and deal with seams — not ideal.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have a small rug on carpet in a low-to-moderate traffic area, want a no-mess grip solution, and don't mind replacing the pad in a year or two if edges start curling.

**Skip it if:** You need cushioning underfoot, have heavy furniture on the rug, or want a pad that will last for years without maintenance. Also skip if you need a size larger than 2x3.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this pad damage my carpet?**
A: No. It's non-adhesive and leaves no residue. The grip comes from friction, not chemicals. You can reposition or remove it without leaving marks.

**Q: Can I use this on hard floors?**
A: The product is specifically designed for carpeted floors. The grip mechanism relies on carpet fibers to hold it in place — on hardwood or tile, it won't perform the same way.

**Q: How do I clean it?**
A: Spot clean with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid machine washing or submerging it in water, as that can degrade the grip coating.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 3.7/5**

The GORILLA GRIP Rug Pad does exactly what it says initially — it grips. For the first few months, it's a reliable, mess-free solution for keeping a small rug anchored on carpet. The problem is longevity. With a 3.5-star average and recurring reports of edge curling, this feels like a product that prioritizes immediate performance over long-term durability.

**Buy it if** you need a quick, no-fuss grip solution for a small rug and accept you might replace it down the road.

**Skip it if** you want a permanent, set-and-forget rug pad that will outlast your current rug. In that case, spend a few extra dollars on a thicker felt-and-rubber hybrid pad.

For the price point, it's a fair value — just don't expect Gorilla-level toughness.
