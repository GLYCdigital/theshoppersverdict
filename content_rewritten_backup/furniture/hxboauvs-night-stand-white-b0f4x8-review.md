If you've shopped for a white nightstand lately, you know the market is flooded with flimsy particleboard boxes that wobble the first time you lean on them. The HXBOAUVS Night Stand White is not that. With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, this one earns its place next to your bed — but not without a couple of caveats worth knowing before you click buy.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the nightstand (15.7" W x 15.7" D x 19.7" H — compact enough for tight corners but tall enough to sit level with most mattress tops), two drawers, one open shelf, and the hardware kit. You also get an instruction manual that's mostly diagrams, which is fine because assembly is straightforward.

As shown in the product image, the design is clean and minimal: two drawers on top, an open cubby below. No fake wood grain, no ornate handles — just a smooth white laminate with simple metal pulls. It's the kind of piece that disappears into a room, which is exactly what most people want from a nightstand.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Here's where this stand surprises. The frame uses a thicker engineered wood core than typical budget furniture — not the hollow honeycomb stuff you can dent with a fingernail. The drawers run on metal tracks with ball bearings, not plastic slides, so they open smoothly even when you've stuffed them with charging cables, glasses, and a Kindle.

One buyer noted, *"I was skeptical at $40, but this thing feels solid. The drawers don't sag, and the top doesn't flex when I put my lamp and water glass on it."* That matches the general sentiment across reviews — people repeatedly mention the sturdiness-to-price ratio as the main selling point.

The white finish is a matte laminate that resists scratches from keys and phone cases. It also handles cleaning wipes without dulling, which is more than you can say for cheaper glossy finishes that cloud over time.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

- **Easy assembly:** Most reviewers report 20-30 minutes to build, with one noting, *"The pre-drilled holes lined up perfectly. I didn't have to force anything."*
- **Stability:** The back panel is actually screwed in, not stapled like many competitors. This makes a noticeable difference in preventing racking (side-to-side wobble).
- **Versatile sizing:** At just under 20 inches tall, it fits under most standard beds and pairs well with both low-profile platform beds and traditional frames.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

First, the matte white surface shows fingerprints and smudges worse than you'd expect. If you're the type who touches the drawer fronts to close them (rather than using the pulls), you'll be wiping it down weekly. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's a maintenance reality.

Second, the included Allen wrench is soft metal. Several reviewers mentioned it stripping if you apply too much torque. Use your own Phillips screwdriver for the main screws and go easy on the hex bolts — hand-tight plus a quarter turn is plenty.

Third, the open shelf is 6.5 inches high. That fits most paperbacks and small baskets, but a stack of hardcover books or a tall water carafe won't fit. Plan your storage accordingly.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Small bedroom dwellers:** The compact footprint fits where a full-size dresser won't.
- **Renters:** It's light enough to move easily and neutral enough to match any decor.
- **First-time furniture buyers:** If you're outfitting a first apartment on a budget, this delivers more durability than the price suggests.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this hold a heavy lamp and a large phone charger?**
A: Yes. The top panel supports up to about 30 pounds without flexing. A lamp, alarm clock, and charging pad won't be an issue.

**Q: Does the white finish yellow over time?**
A: The laminate is UV-stabilized, which helps, but no white furniture is immune to sun exposure. Keep it out of direct, harsh sunlight and it'll stay bright.

**Q: Is it easy to move with the drawers loaded?**
A: The drawers have safety stops that prevent them from sliding out fully, so you can lift the whole unit without drawers dumping their contents. It's a thoughtful safety touch.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a sturdy, good-looking nightstand without paying boutique prices. The build quality punches above its weight class, and the drawer hardware is genuinely better than most competitors in this range.

**Skip it if** you hate wiping fingerprints off white surfaces, or if you need a taller nightstand for a high bed frame — this one sits low.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It's not perfect, but it's honest furniture that does its job without drama. At this price, that's a rare thing.
