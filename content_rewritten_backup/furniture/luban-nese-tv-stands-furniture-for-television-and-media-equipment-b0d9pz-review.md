**A TV stand that actually does its job — without looking like a cardboard box**  

The Luban Nese TV stand is the kind of furniture that makes you wonder why more manufacturers don’t just copy this formula. It’s affordable, it holds a big television, and it doesn’t scream “dorm room” from across the room. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it’s clear buyers agree — but let’s dig into what that rating actually means before you hit “add to cart.”

**What you actually get**  
The box includes the stand itself (top panel, two side panels, three shelves, and a back panel), hardware, and a hex key — no power tools required. As shown in the product image, the design is straightforward: two open shelves flank a center cabinet with a solid door. The dimensions are generous enough for a 65-inch TV (the top surface runs about 55 inches wide), and the total height sits around 23 inches — right in the sweet spot for eye-level viewing from a sofa.

**Build quality and real-world use**  
The frame is engineered wood with a matte finish that resists smudges — a small detail that matters when you have kids or a cat that likes to park on top. The shelves are rated for roughly 30 pounds each, which is enough for a cable box, game console, and a few decorative items. One buyer noted, “Assembly took me 40 minutes with zero frustration — the parts are labeled, and the instructions are actually readable.” That matches the general consensus: the pre-drilled holes align correctly, and the cam locks tighten without stripping.

The center cabinet closes with a soft-close hinge — a feature I didn’t expect at this price point. Inside, there’s a single adjustable shelf, so you can stack a Blu-ray player and a streaming box without cramming.

**What buyers consistently praise**  
The most repeated compliment is the balance between form and function. As one reviewer put it, “It looks way more expensive than it is — my wife thought I spent twice the price.” Another said, “The open shelves are great for hiding cables behind the TV, and the door hides the ugly router.” The clean lines and low-profile design fit modern and transitional living rooms without clashing.

**Where it falls short**  
Let’s be honest about the flaws. The back panel is thin — about 1/8 inch of fiberboard — and it can bow if you load the shelves with heavy gear like a vintage receiver or a multi-channel amp. It’s not a structural issue, but it looks wavy from the side. The cable management cutout in the back is also smaller than the photos suggest; a thick power strip or HDMI cable will need a bit of persuasion to fit through.

Another thing: the feet are fixed, not adjustable. On uneven floors, you’ll feel a slight wobble. A couple of felt pads under the corners solve that, but it’s worth noting if your living room floor isn’t perfectly level.

**Who should buy this**  
This stand is ideal for first-time apartment dwellers, anyone upgrading from a media tower, or couples who want a low-profile unit that doesn’t dominate the room. It’s also a strong pick for rental units — the matte finish is easy to wipe down, and the neutral tones (black and walnut) go with most decor. Skip it if you need heavy-duty storage for a full home theater setup with multiple amps or a massive center-channel speaker — you’ll want a solid wood stand with more weight capacity.

**FAQ**  
*Q: Does it fit a 65-inch TV?*  
Yes, the top surface is about 55 inches wide, and most 65-inch TVs with a standard stand will sit comfortably. Just check your TV’s VESA width — anything under 50 inches wide is fine.

*Q: How sturdy is it on carpet?*  
It’s stable on low-pile carpet, but the fixed feet mean it can tilt slightly on thick shag. Use a leveler or add felt pads for extra grip.

*Q: Can I mount a soundbar on the top shelf?*  
The top shelf is open, so you can place a soundbar directly in front of the TV. Just keep the total weight under 30 pounds.

**The verdict**  
Buy it if you want a clean, functional TV stand under $150 that assembles without cuss words and looks good enough for guests to compliment. Skip it if you need heavy-duty storage or have an uneven floor you can’t fix.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A solid, honest performer that earns its rating. Just don’t overload the back panel.
