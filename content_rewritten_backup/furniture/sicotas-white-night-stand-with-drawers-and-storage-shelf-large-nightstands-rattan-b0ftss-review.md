**The Shopper's Verdict: SICOTAS White Night Stand with Drawers and Storage Shelf – L**

Most nightstands under $150 are either particleboard junk or so minimalist they hold one lamp and a phone. The SICOTAS Large White Night Stand sits in the middle — and that's exactly why it's earned a 4.6-star rating from 229 buyers. It's not flashy, but it solves the two biggest bedroom problems: clutter and wobbly furniture. Here's the real story after digging through specs and owner feedback.

**What you actually get**

The box contains two pre-attached drawer fronts, the main carcass, the storage shelf, cam locks, dowels, and a hex key. No tools needed beyond what's included — that's rare at this price point. The finished dimensions are 19.7" wide by 15.7" deep by 23.6" high, which puts it in "large nightstand" territory without overwhelming a standard queen bed. The top surface is 0.6" thick MDF with a white laminate that's wipe-clean — coffee rings and water marks don't sink in.

**Performance and build quality**

The standout feature is the drawer system. Both drawers ride on metal ball-bearing runners, not the plastic tracks you see on $80 competitors. Owners report smooth operation even after months of daily use. One buyer noted: *"I've had cheaper stands where drawers sag after a season. These still close flush — the metal runners make the difference."* The drawer boxes themselves are dovetail-joined, which adds structural integrity where most budget pieces use butt joints and staples.

The open shelf below the drawers is 12.6" high — enough for a stack of hardbacks, a tissue box, or a small basket for remotes. The back panel is finished with the same white laminate, so you can place this perpendicular to the wall or in a walk-in closet without seeing raw particleboard.

**Where it falls short**

Assembly is the biggest complaint across reviews. The cam lock system is standard, but the instructions are mostly pictographs. If you've never built flat-pack furniture, budget a full hour and watch the included QR-code video before starting. A second person helps for aligning the top panel — it's heavy enough to be awkward solo.

The matte white finish is another consideration. It's not glossy, so it won't show fingerprints, but it does scuff. One reviewer wrote: *"My vacuum cleaner bumped the front and left a gray mark that wouldn't wipe off. I had to touch it up with white paint."* If you're rough on furniture or have kids, consider a clear matte sealant on the front panels before assembly.

**Who should buy this**

- **Small bedroom dwellers** – The 15.7" depth fits tighter spaces than most "large" nightstands, which often run 18" deep.
- **People who read in bed** – The shelf is perfect for a Kindle, reading glasses, and a water bottle within arm's reach.
- **Anyone replacing a wobbly IKEA MALM** – This has more storage (two drawers plus shelf) and better hardware for roughly the same footprint.

**Skip it if** you need a nightstand with a USB port or power outlet built in — this has none. Also skip if you want a wood grain finish; the white laminate is the only option.

**FAQ**

*Q: Can the drawers hold a laptop?*  
A: Each drawer interior is 16.5" wide by 12.2" deep by 4.3" high. A 14" laptop fits flat, but a 15.6" model won't close. Use the shelf for larger items.

*Q: Does the shelf support a printer or heavy decor?*  
A: Yes — the shelf is rated to 22 lbs. A small laser printer or a stack of photo albums is fine.

*Q: Is the white color bright white or off-white?*  
A: It's a true bright white (RGB ~245, 245, 245). It matches most white trim and will not look yellow next to a white bed frame.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a functional, clean-lined nightstand with genuinely useful storage and hardware that won't fail in a year. The 4.6-star rating is earned — this punches above its price class on drawer quality and depth of storage.

**Skip it if** you hate assembling furniture or need a power outlet built in. Also skip if you're hard on furniture and can't tolerate scuffs on a matte white surface.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A solid, honest piece of furniture that does exactly what it promises, with minor assembly friction and finish sensitivity holding it back from perfection.
