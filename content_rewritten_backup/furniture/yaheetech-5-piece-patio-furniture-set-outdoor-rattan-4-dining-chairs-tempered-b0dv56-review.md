**The Short Version**

If you're shopping for a compact patio dining set under $300, the Yaheetech 5-Piece Rattan Set is the one to beat. It's not luxury furniture, but it delivers exactly what most buyers need: a decent-looking, weather-resistant table and four chairs that don't fall apart after one season. At 4.5 stars across 225 ratings, the consensus is clear — this set overdelivers for the price.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains one rectangular table (about 43 inches long) with a 5mm tempered glass top, four armless chairs, and all necessary hardware. The table's raised edge is a thoughtful touch — it keeps plates and drinks from sliding off during breezy evenings. The chairs measure roughly 22.5 inches wide and 34 inches tall, which fits standard-size adults comfortably. As shown in the product image, the dark brown rattan weave is tight and uniform, not the cheap, gap-filled weave you see on budget sets.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The frame is powder-coated steel, which resists rust far better than the bare metal you'll find on competing sets in this price range. The PE rattan is UV-treated, so it won't fade to a sickly gray after two months of sun. One buyer put it plainly: *"We've had this set through two rainy seasons and it still looks brand new. Just hose it off and it's clean."*

The tempered glass top is the standout feature. It's genuinely strong — several reviewers mentioned leaning on it without any flex or creaking. The chairs support up to 265 pounds per the manufacturer, and user feedback confirms they hold up well beyond that.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the assembly. The manual claims 30 minutes; plan for 60-90. The bolts are small and the instructions are mostly diagrams with minimal text. Two people make it significantly easier, especially when attaching the table legs. A second frustration: no cushions included. The woven seats are firm — fine for a 30-minute coffee, uncomfortable for a two-hour dinner. Budget $40-60 for seat pads.

Some buyers also noted the set sits lower than standard dining height — it's more of a café-height table. If you're over 6 feet tall, you'll find it slightly cramped.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **First-time patio owners** who want a decent set without dropping $800+
- **Apartment or small-deck dwellers** — the 5-piece footprint fits tight spaces
- **Renters** who don't want to invest heavily in furniture they might move away from
- **Hosts who prioritize function over luxury** — this set handles daily use and occasional parties

Skip it if you need formal dining height, want cushions included, or expect heirloom quality. This is solid mid-tier furniture, not a lifetime purchase.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will the glass top shatter in extreme heat or cold?**
A: No. Tempered glass handles temperature swings from -20°F to 120°F without issue. The concern is impact — avoid dropping heavy objects directly on the surface.

**Q: How does the rattan hold up in heavy rain or snow?**
A: Well. The PE material is waterproof, and the powder-coated frame resists corrosion. Cover it during winter or store chairs indoors for maximum lifespan.

**Q: What's the actual seat height?**
A: Approximately 17.5 inches from floor to seat. That's standard for café-height sets, not full dining height (which is typically 19-20 inches).

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely good-looking, weather-resistant patio set at a budget price and don't mind an hour of assembly or buying separate cushions.

**Skip it if** you need full dining height, expect included padding, or want furniture that will outlast a decade of harsh winters.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The Yaheetech 5-Piece Set nails the essentials: sturdy construction, attractive design, and real durability. Deduct half a star for the misleading assembly time and firm seats. At this price, it's the smart buy for most backyard setups.
