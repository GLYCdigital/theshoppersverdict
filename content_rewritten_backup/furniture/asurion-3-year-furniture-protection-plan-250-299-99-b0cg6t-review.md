**Is paying $250+ to protect a couch actually smart money, or just an expensive peace-of-mind gamble?**

Here's the thing about furniture warranties: most manufacturer warranties only cover manufacturing defects for 1-2 years. They won't lift a finger when your kid spills red wine on the armrest or the recliner mechanism gives out after 14 months. That's exactly where the ASURION 3-Year Furniture Protection Plan steps in — and for the right buyer, it's genuinely worth the premium.

## What You're Actually Buying

This is a service contract, not a physical product. You're purchasing 36 months of coverage for furniture priced in the $250-$299.99 range — think accent chairs, ottomans, small tables, or lamp sets. The plan covers:

- **Accidental stains** (food, drink, ink, you name it)
- **Rips, tears, and punctures** in fabric or leather
- **Structural failures** like broken frames, springs, or joints
- **Mechanical failures** in recliners, swivels, or lift mechanisms

One important detail in the fine print: this plan covers *repair or replacement* up to the purchase price of the item. If the repair costs more than the furniture is worth, ASURION cuts you a check instead. That's a fair deal.

## How It Works in Real Life

Filing a claim is refreshingly simple. You call or submit online, describe the issue, upload photos, and ASURION either sends a technician or approves a payout — usually within a week. No shipping your sofa across the country, no fighting with customer service bots.

That said, the 30-day registration window is a real gotcha. Miss it, and your effective coverage shrinks to 35 months minus however long you waited. Set a reminder the day your furniture arrives.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations:

- **No pet damage coverage.** That clawed armrest? Not covered.
- **Normal wear-and-tear is excluded.** Fading, pilling, and gradual cushion compression are your problem.
- **The $250-$299.99 price point is steep** for budget furniture. If you bought a $300 accent chair, you're paying nearly 100% of its value for three years of coverage. That makes mathematical sense only if you're accident-prone or have kids.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have young children or host frequent gatherings where spills and bumps are inevitable. You're purchasing a recliner, power sofa, or piece with moving parts that can fail. You bought a moderately expensive item ($800+) where $250 is a reasonable insurance premium.

**Skip it if:** You're protecting a $200 budget end table. You live alone with no pets and are careful with your stuff. You prefer to self-insure — i.e., set aside $250 in a "furniture emergency" fund instead.

## 3 Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Can I transfer the plan if I sell the furniture?**
A: Yes, the plan is transferable if you sell the item within the coverage period. Contact Asurion to initiate the transfer — there's no fee.

**Q: Does it cover delivery damage?**
A: No. You need to inspect furniture upon delivery and file a damage claim with the seller or carrier. This plan covers post-delivery incidents only.

**Q: How do payouts work for a total loss?**
A: If the item can't be repaired, Asurion reimburses you the original purchase price (up to the plan's coverage limit) via check or Amazon gift card. You don't have to send the broken furniture back.
