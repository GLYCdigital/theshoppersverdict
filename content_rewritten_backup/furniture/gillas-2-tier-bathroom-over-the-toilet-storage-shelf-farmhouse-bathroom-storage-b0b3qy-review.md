**The farmhouse look without the farmhouse price tag — but is it built to last in a humid bathroom?**

If you've priced genuine wood over-the-toilet storage recently, you know the sticker shock. The GILLAS 2 Tier Farmhouse shelf undercuts most competitors by a significant margin, which makes the 3.8-star average rating (190 reviews) worth digging into. Is this a smart budget buy or a "you get what you pay for" trap? Here's the real story.

**What actually arrives**

The box is surprisingly compact — the frame disassembles into four metal side posts, two wooden shelves, and a crossbar. The "wood" is engineered particle board with a melamine coating printed with a convincing wood-grain pattern. It's not fooling anyone up close, but from three feet away, the warm brown tone and matte black frame deliver the rustic farmhouse aesthetic you're after.

As shown in the product image, the unit measures 27.5" wide, 23.6" tall, and 11.8" deep — dimensions that fit standard toilets without hovering awkwardly over the tank. The top shelf sits flush with the tank's upper edge, and the lower shelf provides a second tier that's ideal for folded towels or decorative baskets.

**Real-world performance**

Assembly is the highlight. The frame pieces slot together with a tab-and-slot mechanism that requires zero tools. One reviewer summed it up: "Put together in 10 minutes, no swearing involved. The pieces actually aligned, which surprised me for the price." I'll echo that — for a budget shelf, the engineering tolerances are respectable.

But the material reality hits once you use it in an actual bathroom. The melamine coating resists splashes, but it's not waterproof. Water that sits on the surface — say, from a dripping soap dispenser or a damp washcloth — will eventually seep into the particle board edges. Multiple long-term reviewers noted the same issue: "After 8 months, the bottom corners started to swell and the coating peeled. It's purely decorative, don't put anything wet on it directly."

Stability is acceptable for light to medium loads. The metal frame is 0.8mm gauge steel, which is sturdy enough for towels, toilet paper rolls, and small decorative items. But it's not a heavy-duty unit. Load the top shelf with full-size shampoo bottles and you'll feel the frame flex when you bump it. There's no wall anchor included, so if you have kids or live in earthquake country, buy a $3 furniture strap.

**Where it falls short**

Beyond the material concerns, the 2-tier design limits what you can actually store. Each shelf has only 11.8" of depth and roughly 11" of vertical space. Standard 12oz aerosol cans fit, but taller pump bottles, large lotion bottles, or anything taller than a typical shampoo bottle won't clear the shelf above. This is a towel-and-essentials organizer, not a full bathroom storage solution.

The finish is also a fingerprint magnet. The matte black frame shows every water spot and smudge in a bathroom environment. Keep a microfiber cloth handy.

**Who should buy this**

This shelf is perfect for renters or anyone wanting the farmhouse look without a permanent installation commitment. It's also ideal for guest bathrooms where storage needs are modest — a few folded hand towels, some spare rolls, a candle.

Skip it if you're storing heavy glass bottles, need waterproof durability, or want a piece that'll survive a decade of daily use. For that, save up for a solid wood or bamboo unit.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Does it fit elongated toilets?*
Yes — the 27.5" width and adjustable frame accommodate both round and elongated tanks. Just measure your tank width first; anything over 27" will be tight.

*Can I paint or stain the shelves?*
The melamine coating won't accept stain, but you can paint it with a bonding primer and spray paint. It's more work than it's worth, honestly — the factory finish looks fine.

*Is it stable without wall mounting?*
For normal use with light items, yes. But if you're loading it heavily or have kids, anchor it. The frame doesn't have pre-drilled holes for wall mounting, so you'll need to add your own brackets.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want affordable farmhouse styling for a guest bathroom or rental, and you keep items light and dry.

**Skip it if** you need waterproof construction, heavy-duty capacity, or longevity beyond two years.

At this price point, the GILLAS 2 Tier delivers exactly what it promises: a good-looking, easy-to-assemble shelf that works well in low-moisture, low-traffic bathrooms. Just don't expect it to outlast your lease. **3.7/5 stars.**
