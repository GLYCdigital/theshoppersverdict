## Where It Falls Short

Let's be blunt about the downsides.

**Zero reviews is a real concern.** The 4.0 rating shown on Amazon is likely a placeholder or based on a single early rating. You have no collective wisdom about whether the fabric pills, the joints loosen after six months, or the color matches the photos. With an established brand, you have a track record. Here, you're gambling.

**Upholstery is high-maintenance.** Fabric frames absorb dust, pet dander, and spills. If you have allergies or kids, this will need regular vacuuming with an upholstery attachment. And if the cover isn't removable — which the specs don't suggest it is — a stain is permanent.

**Heat retention.** The padding acts as insulation. Hot sleepers may find the headboard area uncomfortable to lean against in summer, and the frame itself can trap warmth in smaller bedrooms.

## FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Ask

**Does this bed require a box spring?**
No. The integrated slat system supports your mattress directly. Most modern mattresses work fine on slats, though memory foam models may need a bunkie board if the slat spacing is wide.

**How long does assembly take?**
Expect 45 minutes to 1.5 hours with two people. The headboard is the heaviest piece, and you'll need a Phillips head screwdriver. All hardware is typically included, but you'll want your own drill for the center support bolts.

**Is the fabric pet-friendly?**
No. Upholstered frames are magnets for pet hair and claws. If you have cats that scratch or dogs that shed heavily, a leather or wood frame is a more practical choice.
