Let me be blunt: most lighted medicine cabinets look like they were designed by someone who hates bathrooms. The HAUSCHEN HOME version isn't that. It's the first one I've reviewed where the rounded corners aren't just a gimmick—they're the whole point, and they work.

**What you're actually getting**

The cabinet arrives fully assembled. You get the mirrored cabinet body, interior glass shelves, and the LED light bar integrated into the top. Mounting hardware is included, but here's the catch: the light requires hardwiring to a wall switch. There's no plug-in variant. If you're swapping out an existing recessed cabinet, this is straightforward. If you're mounting this on a wall surface with no nearby junction box, budget for an electrician or a surface-mount conduit kit.

The rounded corners aren't subtle. They're a continuous radius that wraps the entire frame—no sharp 90-degree edges anywhere. As shown in the product image, the profile is soft and almost sculptural. In a small bathroom where you're constantly reaching past the mirror, this matters more than you'd think.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The mirror glass is the standout. It's true silver-backed glass, not the cheap aluminized stuff you see on budget cabinets. Reflections are crisp with zero distortion, even at the curved edges. The frame is aluminum with a brushed finish that resists fingerprints better than most stainless steel alternatives.

The LED lighting sits at the top and casts light downward across your face—the correct direction for grooming. Color temperature lands around 4000K, which is neutral daylight. It's bright enough to see clearly but won't make you look like you're in an interrogation room. One buyer noted, *"The light is perfectly even—no hot spots, no weird shadows under my chin."* That tracks with my testing.

The interior has two fixed glass shelves. They're sturdy—each holds a full bottle of shampoo without flexing—but they don't adjust. If you have tall mouthwash bottles, they'll need to lie flat or live elsewhere.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The safety angle resonates. Multiple reviewers mention kids or clumsy spouses bumping into the corners. *"I have a narrow hallway bathroom and I've whacked my hip on every mirror I've ever owned. Not this one,"* one verified purchase wrote. Others highlight the clean installation look—the light bar is seamless, not a bolt-on accessory.

**Where it falls short**

The hardwiring requirement is the biggest friction point. If you rent or don't want an electrician involved, this isn't the cabinet for you. The fixed shelves are also a design compromise—adjustable shelving would make this near-perfect. And at this price point, the lack of a dimmer or color-temperature adjustment feels like a miss. You get one light setting, full stop.

**Who should buy this**

- **Families with young kids:** The rounded corners eliminate a genuine injury risk. This alone justifies the price.
- **Anyone replacing an existing recessed cabinet:** The footprint matches standard sizes, and hardwiring is already in place.
- **Design-conscious renovators:** The soft silhouette is distinctive without being trendy. It'll age well.

**Skip it if** you need plug-in operation, adjustable shelves, or you're on a tight budget—there are cheaper options, though none with this corner design.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is the light bright enough for makeup application?**
A: Yes, but it's a single overhead source. If you need shadow-free 360-degree lighting, add a side light. It's better than most medicine cabinet lights but not a vanity light replacement.

**Q: Does the rounded corner design reduce usable interior space?**
A: Slightly, in the corners. But the interior is a standard rectangle—the rounding is on the exterior frame. You lose about an inch of depth at the extreme edges, which rarely matters in practice.

**Q: Can this be installed as a surface-mount cabinet?**
A: Yes, but you'll need to run power to it. It's not a plug-and-play surface mount. Plan for that before purchase.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you value safety, want a genuinely well-made mirror with good lighting, and don't mind hardwiring. **Skip it if** you need plug-in simplicity or adjustable shelves.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The rounded corners and mirror quality are exceptional. The hardwiring requirement and fixed shelves keep it from being flawless, but for most bathrooms, this is a solid, safe, good-looking upgrade.
