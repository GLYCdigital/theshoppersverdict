You don't need to replace your sofa to fix a wobble. That's the pitch behind the Wlrrcwdttc Metal Adjustable Height Furniture Legs, and after reading through the buyer feedback, the promise mostly holds up. With a 4.6 average across 229 ratings, these legs are earning their place under couches, beds, and dressers. But let's talk about what you're actually getting before you click buy.

**What's in the box**

Each set includes four metal legs with welded mounting plates, the adjustment screws, and basic mounting hardware. As shown in the product image, the legs have a clean cylindrical profile with a matte black finish — no decorative flourishes, just functional hardware. The height adjustment works via a threaded collar near the top of each leg, letting you dial in anywhere from about 4 to 6 inches of clearance.

**Performance and real-world use**

The steel construction is genuinely sturdy. Multiple buyers mention using these under heavy sectionals and platform beds without any bending or flexing. One reviewer noted, "I put these on a 300-pound sleeper sofa and they haven't budged in three months." The welding on the mounting plate is clean — no sharp edges or weak points that would fail under load.

The adjustability is the headline feature, and it works as advertised. The threaded collar gives you about two inches of travel, which is enough to level furniture on uneven floors or raise a low couch for easier cleaning underneath. The threads are coarse enough that you don't need tools to adjust — just twist by hand.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The universal fit gets frequent shout-outs. The mounting plate has pre-drilled holes spaced to match most standard furniture leg positions, so you can typically bolt these on without drilling new holes. One buyer said, "I was worried about compatibility with my IKEA sofa, but the holes lined up perfectly." Another praised the finish: "The matte black looks more expensive than what I paid."

**Where it falls short**

The most common complaint is the lack of floor protection. These legs come with bare metal feet — no rubber pads, no felt glides. On hardwood or tile, they'll scratch if you slide furniture. You'll want to add adhesive felt pads or rubber caps, which is an extra cost and an extra trip to the hardware store.

The adjustment mechanism can also bind. If you crank the collar too tight, it becomes difficult to turn — you'll need to loosen it slightly and retry. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's annoying when you're trying to fine-tune the height on all four corners.

**Who should buy this**

These legs are ideal for three specific situations:

- **Renters** who can't modify furniture permanently but need to level wobbly pieces on uneven floors
- **DIY furniture flippers** upgrading thrift store finds with clean, modern hardware
- **Anyone with low-profile furniture** who wants to raise it 1-2 inches for easier vacuuming or under-bed storage

Skip these if you need extreme height adjustment (more than 2 inches of travel) or if you're working with furniture that has non-standard leg mounting positions.

**FAQ**

*Can these support a heavy sofa?* Yes — the steel construction handles typical household loads. Multiple buyers report using them under full-size sofas and queen beds without issues.

*Will these fit my furniture?* The mounting plate uses standard hole spacing, but measure your existing leg positions before ordering. If your furniture has legs set unusually wide or narrow, you may need to drill new holes.

*Do I need tools to adjust the height?* No, the threaded collar turns by hand. But you'll want a screwdriver or drill for the initial installation of the mounting plate.
