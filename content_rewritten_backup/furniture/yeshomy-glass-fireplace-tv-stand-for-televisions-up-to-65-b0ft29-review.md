## Where It Falls Short

Assembly is the bigger complaint. At roughly 90 minutes with two people, it's not a quick build. If you're assembling solo, budget extra time and consider having a friend help.

The fireplace heat isn't enough for a large open-concept space. It's perfect for a bedroom or small living room, but don't expect it to replace a central furnace in a big area.
