# SAFAVIEH Cape Cod 9' Square Jute Rug: The Natural-Fiber Workhorse That Earns Its Keep

Most jute rugs under $300 look great in the product photos and fall apart within a year. The SAFAVIEH Cape Cod Collection is different — and the 4.6-star average across 229 ratings backs that up. This 9-foot square flatweave has become a staple in coastal and farmhouse living rooms for one simple reason: it does exactly what a natural-fiber rug should do, without pretending to be something it's not.

## What You Actually Get

The Cape Cod is a handwoven 100% jute rug in a natural, undyed finish. At 9' x 9', it's a true square — a size that's surprisingly hard to find. Most brands only offer rectangles or 8' rounds, so if you're furnishing a square dining area, a large round table, or a conversation seating group, this fills the space properly. The weave is dense and tight, with visible texture that adds visual warmth to any room. It ships rolled, and you'll want to let it flatten for a day or two before laying it out fully.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Here's where this rug separates itself from cheaper jute options. The weave is substantial — not the loose, scratchy construction you'll find on budget natural-fiber rugs. The fibers are tightly twisted and packed, which means it holds its shape and resists crushing even under heavy furniture legs. In high-traffic walkways, the surface will show some flattening over time, but it doesn't develop permanent divots or bald spots the way thinner jute rugs do.

The natural coloring is another strong point. Because it's undyed, the rug has subtle tonal variation across the surface — some fibers are lighter, some darker. This isn't a flaw; it's what gives the rug depth and makes it forgiving when dirt inevitably accumulates. Coffee spills? Blot them quickly and you're fine. Muddy shoes? The color hides it better than any light wool or synthetic rug will.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides, because they're real. First, shedding. Jute rugs shed — it's physics, not a defect — and this one sheds noticeably for the first month or two. Plan on vacuuming weekly (without a beater bar, which can pull fibers) and expect some loose strands on your hard floors. It tapers off, but it's not zero.

Second, the texture is rough. This is not a rug for lying on. It's scratchy under bare feet and uncomfortable for kneeling. If you want something soft and plush, this isn't it. It's a utility-and-aesthetic rug, not a comfort rug.

Third, there's no backing. On hardwood or tile, it will slide unless you add a rug pad. Buy a good non-slip pad with this — it's non-negotiable for safety and floor protection.

## Who Should Buy This

This rug is ideal for three specific buyers: the coastal-farmhouse decorator who wants authentic texture without paying boutique prices; the dining room owner with a large round table who needs a true square footprint; and the high-traffic household that wants a rug that hides dirt and won't need replacing in 18 months. Skip it if you want a soft, plush surface for a nursery, bedroom, or any spot where you'll spend time on the floor.

## FAQ

**Will this rug shed forever?**
No. Heavy shedding lasts 4-6 weeks with regular vacuuming. You'll see occasional loose fibers after that, but it's minimal and normal for jute.

**Does this work on carpet?**
Yes, it lays flat on low-pile carpet. On plush carpet, you may want a thin pad to keep the edges from curling.

**How do I clean spills?**
Blot immediately with a dry cloth. For deeper cleaning, use a mild soap solution and blot — never soak jute, as it can swell and distort.
