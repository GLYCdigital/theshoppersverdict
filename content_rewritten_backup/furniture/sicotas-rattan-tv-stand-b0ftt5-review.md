# SICOTAS Rattan TV Stand Review: Boho Style Without the Boho Price Tag

Most TV stands under $200 look like they were stamped out of a particleboard factory and shipped with a "some assembly required" warning that means three hours of cussing. The SICOTAS Rattan TV Stand is not that. At a glance, it reads like something from a boutique furniture store — the kind that charges $600 and calls it "artisanal." The woven rattan doors, the warm natural finish, the clean mid-century silhouette. It's genuinely surprising when you check the price.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the TV stand (two cabinet doors, one open center shelf), all hardware, and a hex key. That's it. No tools needed beyond what's included. The frame is MDF with a wood-grain laminate, and the door fronts feature hand-woven rattan panels. Dimensions are roughly 47 inches wide, 15.7 inches deep, and 19.7 inches tall — a solid fit for TVs up to 55 inches, though the manufacturer suggests 50 inches max to be safe.

Assembly is straightforward. Ten screws, four cam locks, and the doors hang on pre-installed hinges. Most buyers report finishing in 20–30 minutes. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: "Took me 25 minutes with a beer in one hand. Everything lined up perfectly."

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The MDF frame is sturdier than expected for the price. It doesn't wobble, and the legs have adjustable levelers — a small detail that matters on uneven floors. The rattan weave is tight and uniform, not the flimsy, gap-toothed weave you see on cheap imports. It's decorative, though. The cabinet doors are solid MDF with rattan on the front only; there's no ventilation behind them.

That's the biggest functional tradeoff. If you're planning to hide a cable box, a streaming device, or a game console inside those cabinets, heat buildup is a real concern. One buyer noted: "My PS5 runs noticeably warmer inside the cabinet. I ended up leaving the doors open during long sessions." For media components that generate serious heat, this stand is better used for storage — blankets, board games, vinyl — and letting electronics sit on the open center shelf.

The top surface holds roughly 50 pounds. That's fine for most modern flat-screens, but if you're mounting a TV on the wall and just need a console for decor, it's more than adequate. Just don't park a 65-inch behemoth on top and expect it to thrive.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The aesthetic is the star. Across 215 ratings at 4.3 stars, the recurring theme is that this stand punches way above its weight class visually. A verified purchase put it this way: "Everyone who walks into my living room asks where I got it. They assume it cost three times what I paid." The natural rattan pairs well with plants, macrame, and warm wood tones — it's a boho-chic anchor piece that doesn't scream "Amazon furniture."

Storage is the other highlight. Two cabinets with adjustable shelves inside plus the open center section give you real flexibility. The open shelf is perfect for a soundbar or a stack of books; the cabinets hide the messy stuff.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the ventilation issue, the natural rattan has batch-to-batch color variation. If you're buying two of these for a long media wall, they might not match perfectly. It's a natural material, so this is expected — but it's worth knowing before you order multiples.

The MDF construction also means it's not built for rough treatment. It's fine for normal household use, but if you have toddlers who treat furniture like climbing equipment or a dog that likes to bump into things, this isn't the stand for you. It's furniture for a tidy, intentional living space, not a war zone.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want a stylish, affordable TV stand for a modern flat-screen in a living room or bedroom, you're decorating with a boho or mid-century vibe, and you don't need to house heat-generating electronics behind closed doors.

**Skip it if:** You have a massive TV, you need enclosed, ventilated storage for a gaming console or cable box, or you're looking for solid-wood heirloom furniture. This is a good piece, but it's not that.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit a 55-inch TV?**
A: Yes, but just barely. The stand is 47 inches wide, so a 55-inch TV's legs will be near the edges. For safety, the manufacturer recommends a 50-inch TV or smaller. Wall-mounting the TV and using this as a console is the best combo.

**Q: Is the rattan real?**
A: Yes, the door panels are hand-woven natural rattan. The frame is MDF with a wood-grain laminate. It's a hybrid — real natural material on the visible surfaces, engineered wood underneath for stability and cost control.

**Q: How hard is assembly?**
A: Easy. Most people finish in under 30 minutes with the included hex key. All holes are pre-drilled, and the instructions are clear enough that you won't need to call for backup.
