If you've ever wrestled with a flimsy folding chair that wobbles, pinches, and collapses mid-meal, you know the pain. The Soohow Folding Dining Chair isn't that chair. It's a genuinely sturdy, comfortable space-saver that pulls double duty as extra seating and a storage-friendly solution for small apartments, frequent hosts, or anyone tired of tripping over bulky chairs.

**What you actually get**

The box contains two chairs (sold as a pair), each with a steel frame, padded PU leather seat and backrest, and the hardware to attach the backrest. Assembly is minimal — you're bolting the backrest to the seat frame, not building furniture from scratch. Expect roughly 10 minutes per chair with the included hex wrench. The chairs fold flat to about 4 inches thick, which is the headline feature. As shown in the product image, they tuck neatly beside a console table, behind a door, or under a bed.

**Real-world performance**

The frame is the differentiator here. The steel construction eliminates the side-to-side sway that plagues cheaper folding chairs. At 300+ pounds rated capacity, it handles a broad range of body types without flexing or creaking. The padding is legitimately comfortable — not plush like a dining room throne, but miles ahead of the hard plastic or thinly-padded metal seats you typically find. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I was expecting cheap folding chairs, but these feel solid. The padding is actually comfortable for a two-hour dinner."*

The PU leather surface wipes clean easily, which matters if kids or messy eaters are in your life. Spills don't soak in; they bead up and wipe off.

**Where it falls short**

No armrests. If you want to lean back and linger over coffee, you'll feel the absence. This is a dining chair, not a lounge chair — buy accordingly.

The PU leather isn't breathable. On a warm evening or during a long holiday meal, your back will get sweaty. It's the tradeoff for easy cleaning.

Assembly, while simple, is still assembly. You're not getting a fully assembled chair. If you're buying these as a last-minute solution for guests arriving in 20 minutes, budget time to bolt them together first.

**Who should buy this**

- **Apartment dwellers** who need extra seating for guests but don't have a dedicated dining room to store four bulky chairs
- **Frequent hosts** who want backup chairs that don't look like they came from a church basement
- **Small-space workers** needing a spare desk chair that disappears when not in use
- **Anyone with limited storage** — the flat-fold design is the whole point

**Who should skip it**

- **Lounge seekers** — armrests are non-negotiable for you
- **Anyone wanting zero assembly** — you'll spend 10 minutes per chair with a wrench
- **Hot climates** — the non-breathable seat surface gets uncomfortable in summer

**FAQ**

**Q: How much weight can these hold?**
A: The steel frame is rated for 300+ pounds. The structure is the strongest part of the chair; the padding and PU leather will wear before the frame fails.

**Q: Do they wobble on uneven floors?**
A: No. The four-point base is stable, and the folding mechanism locks securely. One reviewer noted, *"No wobble at all, even on my slightly uneven hardwood floors."*

**Q: Can these be used outdoors?**
A: Technically yes, but they're not weatherproof. The PU leather and steel frame will degrade with prolonged sun and rain exposure. Keep them indoors.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need sturdy, comfortable extra seating that disappears when you don't need it. The Soohow Folding Dining Chair solves the space problem without the flimsy compromise most folding chairs demand.

**Skip it if** you require armrests or zero assembly. Otherwise, at 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, this is one of the better folding chair values available.
