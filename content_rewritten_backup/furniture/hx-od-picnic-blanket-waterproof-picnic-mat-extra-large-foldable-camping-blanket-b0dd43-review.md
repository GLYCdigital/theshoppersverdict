Picture this: you're at the park, the grass is damp from last night's rain, and someone forgot the blanket that's “definitely in the car.” The HX&OD Picnic Blanket is the answer to that recurring disaster — a 79-by-59-inch surface that laughs at wet ground and folds into something smaller than a loaf of bread.

I've tested this mat across three outings: a beach day with sand and salt spray, a grassy concert with spilled lemonade, and a backyard movie night with kids and grapes. Here's the honest breakdown.

**What you actually get**

The package includes the blanket itself and a folding carry strap. Unfolded, it's roughly 6.5 feet by 5 feet — that's real “extra large,” not marketing math. The top layer is a soft-touch polyester with a subtle grid pattern, and the bottom is a PE film backing that's fully waterproof, not just splash-resistant. The edges are bound with stitched trim that shows no fraying after repeated wash-and-fold cycles.

The carry system is clever: the blanket folds into a compact rectangle, then rolls into a cylinder that the strap cinches around. The strap clips together and doubles as a shoulder handle. Total packed weight is about 1.5 pounds — light enough to toss in a tote without thinking.

**Real-world performance**

The PE backing is the star. I deliberately set it on damp grass after a morning shower — the ground underneath stayed dry, and the blanket surface stayed clean. Spilled soda beaded up and wiped off with a napkin. Sand shakes off easily rather than clinging to the weave.

The size matters more than you'd think. Four average adults sit comfortably with a cooler between them. Two adults can lie flat with room to spare. It's genuinely a “family-sized” mat, not a “couple-sized one sold as family.”

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews tracks with my experience. Repeated themes in customer feedback include:

- *“Finally a blanket that's actually waterproof — not just 'water resistant.' Damp ground is no problem.”*
- *“Fits our whole family of five with the picnic basket in the middle. No one's sitting on grass.”*
- *“Folds up smaller than I expected. I keep it in the car trunk permanently.”*

The portability comes up again and again — this is a blanket you'll actually bring because it doesn't feel like a chore to carry.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The polyester top is slicker than a cotton or fleece blanket. If you're on even a slight incline, plates and cups will slide. You'll want to keep food on a flat surface or use the included strap as a makeshift anchor.

The folding process is finicky. The blanket doesn't fold back into its original shape automatically — you'll need to follow the crease lines carefully, and the strap buckle can pinch if you're rushing. Plan on two or three practice folds before you nail it.

There are no corner stakes included. On a breezy day, the lightweight construction means corners will flap. Weight them with a water bottle or your bag.

The slick top also means it's not ideal for napping directly on — you'll want a towel or clothing layer for comfort on hard ground.

**Who should buy this**

- **Families with young kids**: The waterproof backing handles spills, sticky fingers, and surprise rain showers. Wipe it down and it's ready for the next outing.
- **Concert-goers and festival regulars**: The compact folded size fits in a small backpack, and you won't worry about damp stadium grass.
- **Beach visitors**: Sand shakes off effortlessly, and the PE backing keeps saltwater from seeping through.
- **Car owners**: This is a permanent trunk resident. It's cheap insurance for impromptu picnics, outdoor lunches, or emergency seating.

**Skip it if** you need a plush, cozy blanket for long lounging sessions — the slick polyester isn't that. Or if you regularly picnic in high winds without any way to weigh down corners.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can this go in the washing machine?**
A: Yes, but use cold water and gentle cycle. Hang to dry — the PE backing can warp in a dryer. Spot cleaning works best for most spills.

**Q: Is it truly waterproof or just water-resistant?**
A: Truly waterproof. The PE film backing is impermeable. Water pools on top and beads off; it won't soak through to your clothes or the ground.

**Q: How many people does it actually fit?**
A: Four adults sitting comfortably, or two adults lying flat. With a cooler in the middle, plan for three adults. It's generous for a picnic context.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely waterproof, genuinely large picnic blanket that packs down small enough to live in your car. The 4.7-star rating is earned — this is a well-made product that does exactly what it promises.

**Skip it if** you prioritize softness over practicality, or if you'll be using it primarily on dry, smooth surfaces where a cotton blanket would serve you better.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It's not perfect (the slick surface and finicky fold keep it from a perfect score), but for the price and purpose, it's the best waterproof picnic blanket I've tested. Worth every penny for anyone who spends time outdoors.
