**What you actually get**

The MetalMax Display Cabinet arrives in a surprisingly compact box. Inside: the steel frame pre-assembled into two halves, four tempered glass shelves, a top-mounted LED light strip, and the hardware bag. No surprises, no missing pieces — that's more than I can say for most flat-pack furniture in this category.

The dimensions are sensible for a curio cabinet: roughly 36 inches wide, 18 inches deep, and 72 inches tall. It's tall enough to anchor a room but narrow enough to fit an awkward corner. The carbon steel frame has a powder-coated matte black finish that doesn't show scratches easily, and the tempered glass is genuinely thick — not the cheap stuff that flexes when you load it.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

Here's where this cabinet separates itself from the $150 Amazon specials. The frame is welded, not bolted together with flimsy brackets. The shelves slide into adjustable tracks, so you can rearrange spacing without tools. Each tempered glass shelf is rated for 22 pounds — plenty for collectibles, books, or a heavy serving set.

The adjustable leveling feet deserve a shout-out. If you've ever wrestled with a wobbly display cabinet on hardwood or carpet, you'll appreciate how quickly these stabilize. One reviewer put it bluntly: "I've had this for six months on a slightly uneven floor, and it hasn't wobbled once. My old cabinet from another brand drove me insane."

The LED lighting is a single strip mounted under the top shelf. It illuminates your items reasonably well, but it's not a showpiece lighting setup — no dimmer, no remote, no color temperature adjustment. If you're displaying high-end collectibles that deserve dramatic lighting, plan to add your own strip lights.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the 225 ratings (4.5 average), a few themes dominate. Assembly is the big one — multiple reviewers mention finishing in under 30 minutes. The included hex wrench and pre-drilled holes make it genuinely tool-light. One customer noted: "I'm not handy at all and had this together before my coffee finished brewing."

The build quality gets consistent love too. "The steel frame feels premium, not hollow like I expected at this price," one verified purchase wrote. "The glass shelves are heavy and don't bow at all under my ceramic collection."

And the look — it reads more expensive than it is. The matte black against clear glass gives it a modern-industrial vibe that works in both a loft apartment and a traditional living room.

**Where it falls short**

The LED strip is the biggest compromise. It's bright but harsh — a single point of light that creates shadows rather than even illumination. There's no switch on the door; you're reaching behind to toggle it. Annoying, but not a dealbreaker.

The glass is a fingerprint magnet. Every touch leaves a mark, and dust settles visibly on the black frame. If you have kids who like to press their nose against displays, keep a microfiber cloth nearby.

One more thing: the cabinet has no backing panel. It's open to the wall, so you'll want to keep the back side flush against a wall. It's not designed as a room divider.

**Who should buy this**

- **Collectors** with moderate display needs — figurines, sports memorabilia, vinyl, books
- **Small-space dwellers** who need vertical storage without the visual weight of solid wood
- **First-time furniture buyers** who want something sturdy without a complicated assembly marathon

**Skip it if** you need museum-grade lighting, plan to display heavy items over 22 pounds per shelf, or want a furniture piece that hides clutter — the glass-and-steel design puts everything on display, for better or worse.

**FAQ**

**Is the MetalMax Display Cabinet easy to assemble?**
Yes. Two halves connect with bolts, shelves slide into tracks, and the whole thing takes most people 20–30 minutes. The instructions are clear with labeled parts.

**Can the shelves hold heavy items?**
Each tempered glass shelf is rated for 22 pounds. That's fine for most collectibles and dishware, but don't stack cast-iron cookware on it.

**Does the lighting come with the cabinet?**
Yes, a single LED strip is included and pre-wired to a switch. It plugs into a standard wall outlet. It's basic but functional.

**The verdict: 4.6/5**

Buy it if you want a sturdy, good-looking display cabinet that assembles fast and doesn't wobble. Skip it if you need premium lighting or plan to display heavy, valuable items that deserve a museum-grade setup. For the price, the MetalMax delivers solid value — it does the job without fuss, and the steel frame will outlast cheaper alternatives by years.
