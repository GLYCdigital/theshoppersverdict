**Furnulem Tall LED Dresser with Charging Station, Vertical 5-Drawer Storage Tower Review**

**The Short Version:** If your bedroom is drowning in clothes and you’re one outlet short of a functioning life, this vertical dresser is a legitimate space-saving solution. It’s not heirloom furniture, but for a compact, tech-enabled storage tower at this price point, it delivers more than you’d expect.

---

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the 5-drawer vertical tower, a charging station with two USB ports and one AC outlet, and the LED light strip (which runs along the top back edge). Assembly requires a screwdriver and patience—plan for 45–60 minutes. The unit measures roughly 23.6" W x 15.7" D x 47.2" H, so it fits snugly in a corner or closet without dominating the room. The "vertical 5" configuration means all five drawers are stacked (no wide bottom drawer), which is the whole point: maximum storage in a narrow footprint.

---

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

The composite wood construction is standard for this class—it’s sturdy enough for daily use but won’t survive a move unscathed. The drawer slides are the weak point here; they’re not soft-close, and heavier loads can make them stick. Keep the weight under 10–12 pounds per drawer and you’re fine.

The charging station is the standout feature. The 48-inch power cord reaches most outlets, and the integrated cable management hole keeps your phone charger from becoming a tangled mess. The LED light strip is a nice touch—it has multiple color modes and brightness levels, controlled by a button on the top edge. It’s not smart-home integrated, so don’t expect app control, but for ambient lighting it works well.

---

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The space efficiency is the runaway winner. One verified buyer wrote: *"I live in a studio and this holds everything without taking up floor space. The drawers are deeper than they look."* Another noted: *"The charging station is actually useful—I charge my phone and tablet on top every night."* The LED lighting gets consistent shout-outs for adding a modern feel to otherwise basic furniture.

---

**Where It Falls Short**

The particle board construction is the tradeoff. It’s not water-resistant—a spilled drink on top will warp the surface. The drawer runners are basic; they’ll hold up for a few years of normal use, but don’t expect smooth-gliding precision. Assembly instructions are mostly diagrams with minimal text—if you’re not handy, budget extra time. And note the lack of a back panel on the lower drawers; the wall is your backstop, which is fine unless you have uneven baseboards.

---

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Small-space dwellers**: Studio apartments, dorms, or anyone working with a tight footprint
- **Tech-heavy users**: The built-in charging station justifies the price alone
- **Renters**: It’s affordable enough to leave behind or replace without regret

**Skip it if** you need furniture that can survive a move or if you’re looking for solid-wood construction.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the LED strip require batteries or plugging in?**  
A: It plugs into the same power source as the charging station—no batteries needed. There’s an on/off button on the strip itself.

**Q: Can the drawers hold jeans and sweaters without sagging?**  
A: Yes, but keep the bottom three drawers for heavier items. The top two are shallower and best for smaller items.

**Q: Is the charging station safe for overnight charging?**  
A: The AC outlet and USB ports are standard—no overheating issues reported. That said, unplugging the unit when you leave for extended periods is good practice with any electronics.
