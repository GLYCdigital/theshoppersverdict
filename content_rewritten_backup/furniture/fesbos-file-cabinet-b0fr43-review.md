**Fesbos File Cabinet Review: The $80 Question**

Here's the thing about budget file cabinets: most are flimsy sheet metal that wobbles, jams, and makes you regret skipping the $200 Steelcase. The Fesbos File Cabinet doesn't fully escape that category — but it punches well above its price point in ways that matter.

**What You Actually Get**

The box includes the assembled cabinet body, three drawers, a drawer track set, and a hardware kit with cam locks. Assembly is genuinely straightforward — the slides come pre-attached to the drawers, so you're mostly aligning tracks and sliding drawers in. Most buyers report 20-30 minutes from box to functional. One Amazon reviewer noted, "The instructions are mostly pictures, but I didn't need them. Everything lined up perfectly."

The cabinet measures 18.5" deep, 15.4" wide, and 28.5" tall — sized for letter or legal hanging files. The top drawer holds letter-size, the bottom two are sized for legal. The powder-coated finish on the steel body is smooth and even, with no sharp edges on the drawer fronts.

**Real-World Performance**

The ball-bearing slides are the standout feature. Full-extension pulls let you see everything in the drawer, and they glide smoothly even with a full load of hanging files. The interlocking safety mechanism prevents more than one drawer from opening at a time — a nice touch that keeps the cabinet from tipping forward when you pull two drawers out simultaneously.

The locking system is a single key that turns a central bar, engaging all three drawers at once. It works reliably, though you can't secure just one drawer — a minor design compromise.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The recurring theme in positive reviews is the "surprisingly solid" construction. One verified buyer wrote: "I've owned $150 cabinets that felt cheaper than this. No wobble, drawers don't bind, and the lock actually lines up." Multiple reviewers mention the cabinet arrives well-packaged with no dents or scratches — a common failure point with budget furniture.

**Where It Falls Short**

The stated 40-pound per-drawer capacity is optimistic. At full legal-file loads, the slides still work, but you'll feel the strain. Around 30 pounds is the comfort zone. Also, there's no anti-tip bracket in the box — if you have kids or pets, order one separately. It's a $5 part that should be included.

The key is a bit stiff in the first week. It loosens up with use, but don't expect a smooth turn out of the box. And the drawer fronts are plain rectangles — no handles, just recessed pulls. That's fine for a utility cabinet, less ideal if you want something that looks like furniture rather than office equipment.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Home office workers** who need reliable file storage without spending furniture-store prices
- **Students** organizing coursework and important documents
- **Small business owners** who need a secondary unit that matches an existing budget setup

**Skip it if** you need a showpiece, plan to exceed 30 pounds per drawer regularly, or require separate locking per drawer.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it fit letter-size hanging files?**
A: Yes, the top drawer fits letter-size hanging files (12.5" wide). The bottom two drawers accommodate legal-size. All three accept standard hanging file rails.

**Q: Is assembly really tool-free?**
A: Not quite — you'll need a Phillips screwdriver for the drawer tracks and cam locks. But no power tools required, and everything is pre-drilled.

**Q: Will it tip over if I pull out two drawers?**
A: The interlock mechanism prevents opening two drawers at once, which is the main safety feature. But without the optional anti-tip bracket, a fully loaded top drawer plus an empty bottom could still pose a risk on carpet.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a functional, dependable filing cabinet that won't embarrass you or break your budget. The Fesbos delivers on the basics — smooth slides, solid build, reliable lock — with only minor compromises.

**Skip it if** you need heavy-duty capacity or furniture-grade aesthetics. This is a utility piece, and it knows it.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A genuinely good value that outperforms its price class, held back only by an optimistic weight rating and a missing safety bracket.
