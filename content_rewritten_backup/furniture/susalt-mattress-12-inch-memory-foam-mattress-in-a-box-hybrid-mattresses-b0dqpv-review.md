The "mattress in a box" category is crowded with foam slabs that all claim to feel like luxury hotels at budget prices. Most don't deliver. The Susalt 12-inch hybrid actually does something different: it pairs a coil support core with memory foam comfort layers, which solves the two biggest complaints about cheap bed-in-a-box options — sagging and heat retention.

**What you actually get**

The box arrives compressed and rolled. Inside: the 12-inch mattress, a small cutting tool, and minimal packaging. As shown in the product image, this is a true hybrid — pocketed coils underneath, 3 inches of gel-infused memory foam on top, wrapped in a knit cover. The construction is straightforward: you unbox it, cut the plastic, and wait. Full expansion takes about 24 hours, though it's sleepable after 4–6.

**How it performs in real use**

The coil base changes the feel significantly compared to all-foam competitors. You get responsiveness — you don't sink into a crater and struggle to roll over. The memory foam layer provides pressure relief for hips and shoulders, which is the main reason side sleepers buy this category of bed. The 12-inch height also means the edge doesn't collapse when you sit on it to put on socks, a common failure point on cheaper 8–10 inch models.

One buyer noted: *"I was skeptical about the hybrid thing, but the coil support makes a real difference. I don't wake up with lower back pain like I did on my old all-foam bed."* That tracks with the 4.3-star average across 215 ratings — most owners are getting what they paid for.

Motion isolation is genuinely good. The pocketed coils are individually wrapped, so a partner shifting at 3 AM won't create a ripple effect. If you sleep with someone who tosses, this is a strong point.

**Where it falls short**

The off-gassing is real. Multiple reviewers mention a chemical smell that lingers for a couple of days. It's not toxic — it's the VOCs from manufacturing — but if you're sensitive to smells, unbox it in a ventilated room and wait 72 hours.

Memory foam sleeps warm. The Susalt has gel infusion, but it's not a true cooling mattress. Hot sleepers will want a breathable mattress protector or a cooling pad. This is a common tradeoff at this price point, but worth knowing before you buy.

Setup is a workout. A queen weighs around 70 pounds compressed. You'll want a second person to maneuver it into the bedroom and position it on the frame. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's not a one-person job either.

**Who should buy this**

- **Side sleepers on a budget**: The 3-inch foam layer gives the pressure relief you need without the $2,000 price tag of premium hybrids.
- **Couples**: Motion isolation plus edge support means two people can share the bed without disturbing each other or feeling like they're rolling toward the center.
- **Back sleepers who want medium-firm support**: This sits around a 6 on the firmness scale — supportive but with enough give for comfort.

Skip it if you're a stomach sleeper who needs a very firm surface (this is too plush), or if you sleep extremely hot and refuse to use additional cooling gear.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the Susalt work on a box spring?**
Yes, but it's not necessary. The coil core provides enough support for slatted frames, platform beds, or even the floor temporarily. A box spring is fine if you already have one, but don't buy one specifically for this mattress.

**Q: How long does it take to fully expand?**
Most buyers report full expansion within 24 hours. The edges and corners fill out last. It's sleepable after about 4–6 hours, but you'll get the best feel after a full day.

**Q: Is there a trial period or warranty?**
Standard for the category — check the listing for current terms, but expect a 10-year warranty and a 100-night trial. Keep the original packaging for at least a month in case you need to return it.

**The Verdict**

The Susalt 12-inch hybrid hits the sweet spot for a bed-in-a-box: real coil support, decent memory foam comfort, and a price that undercuts traditional mattress stores. It's not a luxury mattress, but it doesn't pretend to be. It's a solid, honest bed that most sleepers will be happy with.

**Buy it if** you want hybrid support without the premium markup and can tolerate a few days of off-gassing.

**Skip it if** you're a strict stomach sleeper, need active cooling, or expect white-glove delivery service.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — This is a strong value purchase with a couple of predictable caveats. For the price, it outperforms most all-foam competitors in its class.
