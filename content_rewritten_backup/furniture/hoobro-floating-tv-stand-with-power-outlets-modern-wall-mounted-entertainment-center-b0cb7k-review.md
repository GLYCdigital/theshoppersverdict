# HOOBRO Floating TV Stand with Power Outlets Review: Wall-Mounted Style That Actually Works

**The Short Version:** If you're tired of your TV stand eating floor space and collecting dust, this HOOBRO floating unit delivers the sleek, modern look you're after — with built-in power outlets that make cable management genuinely painless. It's not perfect, but at this price point, it punches well above its weight.

---

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the main wall-mounted cabinet, a separate shelf unit, mounting hardware (including heavy-duty anchors), and a clearly written instruction manual. The unit measures roughly 47 inches wide — sized for TVs up to 55 inches, though it'll work with larger sets if you're not fussy about the proportions. The engineered wood construction has a matte finish that resists fingerprints, which matters more than you'd think in a living room.

The standout feature is the built-in power strip with two AC outlets and two USB ports, tucked into a cutout on the top surface. It's a small touch that eliminates the ugly cable-dangle problem most floating units create.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The cabinet uses a combination of particleboard and MDF with a laminated surface. That's not premium hardwood, but it's exactly what you'd expect at this price — and it's sturdier than the price suggests. The wall-mounting system uses a French cleat design, which distributes weight more evenly than simple keyhole brackets. Several buyers noted the installation was straightforward: *"Took me about 30 minutes with a stud finder and a drill. The included template made it foolproof."*

The soft-close hinges on the cabinet door are a genuinely nice touch — no slamming, no rattling. The floating shelf below provides space for a soundbar or cable box, and the open cubby design keeps everything accessible.

**Weight capacity** is listed at 50 pounds for the main unit and 22 pounds for the shelf. That's adequate for most setups, but don't plan on loading it with heavy books or a massive A/V receiver.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The customer feedback across 225+ ratings paints a clear picture:

- **The power outlet integration.** This is the feature people mention most. *"The outlets in the top are genius. No more crawling behind the TV to plug things in."*
- **The floating aesthetic.** *"It completely transformed my living room. Looks like a custom installation."*
- **Assembly difficulty.** Most reviewers found it manageable, with the included mounting template earning specific praise.

## Where It Falls Short

**The cable management channel is shallow.** If you have thick HDMI cables or multiple power adapters, you'll struggle to tuck everything neatly. Plan on using zip ties or velcro straps to keep things organized.

**The finish is prone to scratching.** The matte surface hides fingerprints but shows scuffs more easily than glossier alternatives. Use felt pads under anything you place on top.

**Stud placement can be an issue.** The 47-inch width means you'll need studs spaced 16 inches apart to hit two mounting points. If your wall has irregular stud spacing, you'll need to use the included drywall anchors — which reduces the weight capacity significantly.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Apartment dwellers** who want to maximize floor space without permanent modifications
- **Minimalist decorators** seeking a clean, floating look at a budget price
- **Cable-haters** who want power access without visible cords

**Skip it if** you have a massive soundbar setup, need to store heavy equipment, or prefer traditional floor-standing units with more storage capacity.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this hold a 65-inch TV?**
A: The listed capacity is 50 pounds, and a 65-inch TV typically weighs 40-60 pounds. It'll work, but you're pushing the limit. Stick with 55 inches or smaller for safety margin.

**Q: Does it come with a template for mounting?**
A: Yes, the included paper template is surprisingly accurate. Mark your holes, drill, and mount — most buyers reported no issues.

**Q: Are the power outlets grounded?**
A: Yes, the built-in power strip is grounded and includes surge protection adequate for basic electronics.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if** you want the floating, modern look without paying custom-installation prices. The power outlet integration is genuinely useful, assembly is straightforward, and the aesthetic payoff is significant.

**Skip it if** you need serious storage capacity, have heavy equipment to house, or your wall studs are unusually spaced.

For under $100, this is one of the better floating TV stands available — just keep a tube of touch-up paint handy for the inevitable scuffs.
