A 63-inch credenza that does double duty as a dining room buffet and entryway catch-all is a smart buy — if the build quality backs it up. With 220 Amazon ratings averaging 4.4 stars, the WILSHINE Sideboard Buffet Credenza is clearly earning its keep in real homes. Here's what you should actually know before you add it to your cart.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The box contains the credenza body, two cabinet doors, two soft-close drawers, an adjustable interior shelf, and all the cam lock hardware pre-bagged and labeled. No tools needed beyond a Phillips screwdriver — the cam locks do the heavy lifting. The 63-inch width fits standard dining room walls without overwhelming a smaller space, and the 30-inch height works well behind a sofa or against an entryway wall. The wood grain finish is printed, not real veneer, so don't expect heirloom-quality material at this price point. But it reads convincingly as wood from a few feet away.

**Real-World Performance**

The soft-close drawers are the standout feature. They glide smoothly and close without slamming — a detail buyers consistently mention. One reviewer noted, "The drawers feel more expensive than the price suggests, honestly." The cabinet interiors are deep enough for stacked plates, serving bowls, or a small appliance. The adjustable shelf lets you reconfigure for tall items like wine bottles or a stand mixer.

Assembly is straightforward but not instant. Most buyers report 45–60 minutes with two people. The instructions are mostly visual, and the parts are labeled A through H, which cuts down on guesswork. One reviewer said, "I've built a lot of flat-pack furniture — this was one of the easier ones."

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The storage flexibility tops the list. Owners use it as a dining buffet, a TV stand, an entryway shoe storage unit, and even a home office credenza. The neutral finish blends with most decor styles. The soft-close mechanism gets repeated shout-outs. And the footprint — 63 inches wide, about 15.5 inches deep — fits narrow spaces where a hutch or armoire won't.

**Where It Falls Short**

The back panel is thin MDF. Overloading the shelves can cause it to bow or separate from the frame. If you plan to store heavy cast iron or stacks of ceramic dishes, distribute weight evenly and consider adding a support brace. Several reviewers reported minor scuffs or chipped edges on arrival — shipping damage, not manufacturing defects. The printed grain finish will show scratches over time, especially in high-traffic areas. And while the cam lock assembly is solid, the unit is not designed to be disassembled and moved repeatedly. If you move apartments annually, this may not survive more than two relocations.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Dining room entertainers** who need extra serving space and storage for dishes and linens
- **Small apartment dwellers** who want one piece that handles entryway, dining, and living room duties
- **First-time furniture buyers** who want a solid, functional piece without the IKEA assembly headache

**Skip it if** you need a furniture-grade piece that will survive decades of heavy daily use, or if you need it to match a specific real-wood grain pattern.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the back panel really bow?**
Yes, if you overload it. The MDF back is thin — keep heavy items toward the front and center of shelves, and it holds up fine. Anchor to the wall if you have kids who might climb.

**Q: Can the doors open from either side?**
Yes. The hinges are reversible, so you can mount the doors to open left or right. It takes about 10 minutes to swap them during assembly.

**Q: Is the 63-inch version the only size available?**
WILSHINE offers this line in several widths. The 63-inch is the most popular for dining rooms and larger entryways, but smaller versions exist if you're tight on space.

**The Verdict**

The WILSHINE Sideboard Buffet Credenza delivers solid storage and clean looks at a price that undercuts most competitors. It's not heirloom furniture — the printed grain and thin back panel make that clear — but for a functional, attractive piece that handles daily use, it's a strong value.

**Buy it if** you need versatile, good-looking storage without paying boutique furniture prices. **Skip it if** you expect real wood construction or plan to move it multiple times.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Deducting a half-point for the flimsy back panel and potential shipping scuffs. Everything else delivers as advertised.
