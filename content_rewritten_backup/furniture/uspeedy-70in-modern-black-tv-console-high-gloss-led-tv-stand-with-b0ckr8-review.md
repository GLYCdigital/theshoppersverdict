Some TV stands are furniture. This one is a statement piece. The Uspeedy 70-inch Modern Black TV Console with its high-gloss finish and integrated LED lighting sits somewhere between a budget Amazon find and something you'd see in a showroom — and at 4.2 stars from 210 ratings, the consensus says it mostly delivers.

Here's what you need to know before you click buy.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the main console body, two glass shelves, the LED strip kit, hardware, and an instruction manual that's decent but not great. The unit measures 70 inches wide — designed for TVs up to around 65 inches, though some buyers report using it with 70-inch panels successfully. The high-gloss black finish is applied to MDF panels, not solid wood. That's worth knowing.

The LED lighting is the headline feature. As shown in the product image, the strips run along the bottom edge and cast a soft glow beneath the unit. The included remote controls color and brightness. Buyers consistently mention this as the standout feature — one reviewer noted, *"The lights look way more premium than I expected for this price point."*

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The MDF construction is what it is — this isn't heirloom furniture. But Uspeedy did the little things right. The high-gloss coating resists fingerprints far better than most black gloss furniture, which typically turns into a smudge magnet within days. The cable management holes in the back are positioned to line up with standard power strips, which saves you from the usual zip-tie mess behind the unit.

The glass shelves are tempered and feel substantial. They're rated to hold AV components, but don't overload them — a heavy receiver plus a center channel speaker on one shelf is pushing it. The center console area fits most soundbars up to 50 inches wide.

Assembly is where things get real. Plan for 60 to 90 minutes if you're solo. The glass shelves are heavy and awkward — you'll want a second person or a soft blanket on the floor. One buyer said, *"The instructions are mostly pictures, and a couple steps were confusing. Take your time and it's fine."* Another warned, *"The glass is heavy. Don't rush it."*

## Where It Falls Short

The high-gloss finish is a double-edged sword. While it resists fingerprints, it shows micro-scratches easily. If you slide a cable box or soundbar across the surface, you'll leave marks. Use felt pads religiously.

The LED remote is IR, not RF — meaning you have to point it directly at the unit. If the stand is behind a cabinet door or around a corner, you'll be reaching around to change colors. Minor, but annoying.

Some buyers reported the LED strips failing within a few months. It's not a widespread issue, but it's worth noting that replacement strips are cheap on Amazon if yours dies.

## Who Should Buy This

This stand is perfect for someone furnishing a media room or apartment who wants the modern, floating-console look without paying boutique furniture prices. It works especially well in rooms with dark floors or accent lighting — the LED glow genuinely elevates the space.

Skip it if you're looking for solid wood that'll survive a decade of moves. This is a style-forward piece built for the here and now.

## FAQ

**Does it fit a 70-inch TV?**
Yes, most buyers report success with 70-inch panels, though it's officially rated for up to 65 inches. Measure your TV's stand width — the console top is about 70 inches wide, so you have room.

**Are the LEDs worth it?**
Yes, for most people. They're bright enough to serve as bias lighting while watching movies, and the color options are fun for gaming setups. Just know the remote requires line of sight.

**How sturdy is it?**
Sturdier than expected. The weight is distributed well, and the unit doesn't wobble on carpet. The glass shelves are the weak point — don't overload them.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a modern, LED-accented TV console that looks twice its price, and you're willing to spend an hour assembling it carefully.

**Skip it if:** You need something that'll survive moves and heavy daily use without showing wear, or you want solid wood construction.

This is a solid 4.2/5. It delivers on looks, the LED feature genuinely adds value, and the finish holds up better than most gloss furniture. Just be gentle with it and use those felt pads.
