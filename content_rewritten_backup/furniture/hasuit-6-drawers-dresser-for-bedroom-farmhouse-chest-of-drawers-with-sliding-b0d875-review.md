**The Short Version**

A 6-drawer dresser that actually fits a standard bedroom without dominating it, the Hasuit Farmhouse Chest hits a sweet spot between budget and build quality. At 4.2 stars from 210 ratings, it's not perfect — but for the price, it outperforms most flat-pack alternatives from big-box stores.

---

**What You're Actually Getting**

The box arrives with 6 full drawers, a top panel, side panels, and the inevitable hardware bag. Dimensions run roughly 31.5 inches wide by 15.7 deep by 33.5 tall — compact enough for a guest room or apartment, but the drawer depth is the real story. Each drawer interior measures about 28 inches wide and 5.5 inches deep, which means folded sweaters stack two high without cramming.

The drawer glides deserve a callout. These are full-extension ball-bearing slides rated for 30 pounds each — not the nylon-on-plastic tracks you see on $80 dressers. One buyer noted, "The drawers slide smoother than my $400 dresser from a furniture store." That's not hyperbole; the glide mechanism is genuinely the best part of this unit.

---

**Build Quality: What Holds Up and What Doesn't**

The frame is engineered wood (MDF with a laminated finish), which is standard at this price. The farmhouse aesthetic comes from a distressed gray-wash over a faux wood grain — it photographs well and looks convincing from a few feet away. Up close, you'll see the laminate texture, but that's expected under $200.

The top panel is where Hasuit cut a corner. It's a thinner MDF piece that can flex if you lean on it heavily. Keep heavy decor off the center span. The drawers themselves are sturdier — dovetail-style joints (actually glued mechanical joints, not true dovetails) hold up fine for normal use.

The anti-tip kit is a genuine safety feature, not a legal checkbox. The included wall anchor is metal, not plastic, and the instructions make installing it a priority. If you have kids, use it. No exceptions.

---

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The dominant theme in positive reviews is the storage capacity-to-footprint ratio. Real quotes from verified buyers:

- *"I was shocked how much fits in this. Six drawers organized my entire bedroom closet overflow."*
- *"Assembly was tedious but the end result looks like a $500 piece."*
- *"The color is exactly as pictured — a warm gray, not too dark, not too light."*

The farmhouse style is polarizing but consistent. If you're matching a Joanna Gaines-inspired room, this fits. The black metal handles are substantial, not flimsy wire pulls.

---

**Where It Falls Short**

Assembly is the biggest complaint, and it's legitimate. The drawer fronts come detached from the drawer boxes — you have to align and screw each one onto its slide. Misalign one by 2 millimeters and the drawer binds. Budget 60-90 minutes and bring a second pair of hands for the panel alignment.

The finish is the second issue. Vinyl laminate chips if you bang furniture into it. One reviewer noted, "The corner chipped when my vacuum cleaner bumped it — white MDF showing through." Touch-up markers help but won't fully hide it.

The depth is also slightly shallower than some competitors. If you hang long dresses, this isn't your piece — it's optimized for folded clothes, not hanging storage.

---

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Apartment dwellers** who need vertical storage in a tight footprint
- **Guest room furnishers** who want style without hotel-furniture blandness
- **Anyone on a budget** who refuses to buy wobbly particleboard junk

Skip it if you need a real-wood heirloom piece, or if you're not handy with a screwdriver — the assembly isn't hard, but it's not 15 minutes either.

---

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the dresser wobble?**
A: Not if assembled correctly. The back panel is a stabilizing sheet that must be attached with all screws — skip it and you'll get lateral sway. With the anti-tip kit mounted, it's solid.

**Q: Will it fit in a narrow hallway?**
A: The box dimensions are about 34 x 20 x 5 inches — measure your doorways before ordering. The assembled dresser is under 32 inches wide, so it clears most standard doors, but the box is the bottleneck.

**Q: Can the drawers hold heavy items like jeans?**
A: Yes — the 30-pound rated glides handle a full drawer of denim without sagging. The drawer bottoms are reinforced with a center support bar, which is a thoughtful touch.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want farmhouse style on a flat-pack budget and don't mind an hour of assembly. The glide quality and storage efficiency punch above the price point.

**Skip it if** you need real wood, can't handle laminate finishes, or want something assembled in under 30 minutes.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid value with honest limitations. The assembly friction and laminate finish keep it from a perfect score, but for the price, this dresser outperforms expectations where it counts: storage, smooth drawers, and visual appeal.
