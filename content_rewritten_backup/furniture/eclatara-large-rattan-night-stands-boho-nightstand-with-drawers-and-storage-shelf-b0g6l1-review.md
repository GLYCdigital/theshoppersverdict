If you've been scrolling past a dozen "boho" nightstands that are really just gray MDF boxes with a sticker saying *rattan*, here's the one that actually delivers. The Eclatara Large Rattan Nightstand has earned a 4.6-star average from 229 Amazon ratings, and after digging through the feedback and the specs, I get why. This isn't a trend piece — it's a genuinely useful bedside table with a hand-woven texture that photographs as well in person as it does in the listing.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the nightstand (two stacked drawers plus an open bottom shelf), a full hardware kit, and an instruction manual that's mostly diagrams. The dimensions are generous for the category: 23.6 inches wide, 15.7 inches deep, and 23.6 inches tall. That top surface fits a 12-inch lamp, a stack of hardcovers, and your phone without a game of Jenga. The drawers run on metal slides — full extension, which is rare at this price point. The legs are solid rubberwood, not the hollow pine you see on cheaper units.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The frame is engineered wood with a warm teak-toned finish, and the drawer fronts feature actual woven rattan in a herringbone pattern. Up close, the weave is tight and even — no loose strands or gaps. One thing to note: rattan is a natural material, so expect subtle color variation between panels. That's character, not a defect, but if you're a perfectionist, order a set so the pieces are from the same batch.

Assembly takes about 30 to 45 minutes. The cam-lock bolts are standard, but a few reviewers noted that the drawer slides need precise alignment — don't overtighten until everything's seated. Put the back panel on last, and you'll avoid the most common complaint: drawers that rub.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the feedback, three themes dominate. First, the look: *"I was honestly shocked at how nice this looks for the price — the rattan is real, not printed."* Second, the size: *"Finally a nightstand tall enough to actually use with my platform bed."* Third, the storage split: two drawers for clutter, an open shelf for baskets or a stack of books you're actively reading. That combination is harder to find than you'd think — most competitors give you either drawers *or* a shelf, not both.

**Where it falls short**

The back panel is thin hardboard — fine for a nightstand, but don't shove heavy items against the rear. Direct sunlight will fade the rattan over months, so keep it out of a south-facing window. And if you're expecting furniture-store assembly quality out of the box, recalibrate: this is flat-pack, and it shows in the alignment step. One reviewer put it bluntly: *"Took me an hour, and I've built IKEA stuff for years. Worth it, but set aside time."*

**Who should buy this**

- **The boho-maximalist** who wants real texture, not printed vinyl grain
- **The platform-bed sleeper** whose current nightstand is too short to reach comfortably
- **The small-space dweller** who needs drawer *and* shelf storage in one footprint

Skip it if you need a fully assembled piece, or if your bedroom gets harsh afternoon sun right where the nightstand sits.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can this hold a 55-pound aquarium?**  
A: No. The frame is rated for typical bedside loads — lamps, books, phones, a small plant. The top is solid, but the hardboard back and cam-lock joints aren't built for that kind of concentrated weight.

**Q: Does the rattan snag or fray?**  
A: Not in normal use. The weave is sealed, and reviewers with kids and pets report no issues. Just don't drag sharp objects across the drawer fronts.

**Q: Is the color more yellow or brown in person?**  
A: It leans warm honey-brown, closer to natural cane than the dark walnut shown in some lifestyle photos. If you want a darker match, check the espresso variant.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want real rattan texture and two-drawer-plus-shelf storage without paying boutique prices. **Skip it if** you can't handle a 45-minute assembly or need a piece that shrugs off direct sunlight.

At 4.6 stars from 229 ratings, this is one of the few boho nightstands that earns its rating. The Eclatara delivers the aesthetic and the utility — just budget the assembly time.
