Let’s be honest: most clear acrylic furniture is a gamble. Either it looks cheap and scratches if you breathe on it, or it costs as much as a used car. The Flash Furniture Cheryl sits somewhere in the sensible middle — and with 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, it’s clearly doing something right. Here’s the real breakdown after digging through buyer feedback and the spec sheet.

## What You Actually Get

The Cheryl is a one-piece molded acrylic chair with a contoured seat and gently curved backrest. It’s roughly 22.5 inches wide, 23 inches deep, and stands about 32 inches tall with a seat height around 18 inches — standard dining proportions that also work as accent seating. The chair arrives fully assembled, so no wrestling with Allen wrenches. Weight capacity is listed at 300 pounds, which is respectable for a polycarbonate chair.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The acrylic has a clean, polished finish that mimics glass without the shatter risk. The contoured seat isn’t just decorative — it follows the curve of your legs, which makes a hard material noticeably more comfortable. Several buyers mention using these for multi-hour dinner parties without needing to grab a cushion.

For outdoor use, the Cheryl holds up better than most acrylic chairs. The material is UV-stabilized to resist yellowing, and the one-piece construction means no joints to loosen or rust. One buyer noted leaving them on a covered patio through two full summers: *“They still look brand new — no clouding, no cracks. I wipe them down with a damp cloth and they’re good.”* That matches the general sentiment from outdoor users.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

- **Versatility is the headline.** The clear design disappears into any décor. Buyers use them as dining chairs, makeup vanity seating, office accent chairs, and poolside seating.
- **Comfort surprises people.** The ergonomic contouring gets consistent shout-outs. “Way more comfortable than I expected from a clear chair,” is a recurring refrain in reviews.
- **Sturdiness.** Multiple reviewers note the chair feels solid, not flimsy like cheaper acrylic options. The 300-lb capacity isn’t marketing fluff — heavier users report no flexing or creaking.

## Where It Falls Short

**Fingerprints are your new roommate.** Every smudge, dust particle, and pet hair shows on the clear surface. If you have kids or live anywhere with dust, you’ll be wiping these down regularly. Microfiber cloths and a mild cleaner become part of your routine.

**No stacking.** Unlike many dining chairs that nest, the Cheryl’s contoured shape doesn’t allow stacking. If you’re buying four for a small apartment, they’ll take up meaningful floor space when not in use.

**Scratches are permanent.** Acrylic is soft relative to glass. Dragging the chair or catching it on a metal table leg will leave marks that can’t be buffed out easily. Use felt pads under the legs if you have hard floors.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Indoor/outdoor flexers:** If you want one chair that works on a covered porch in summer and moves inside for holiday dinners, this is your pick.
- **Small-space dwellers:** The visual lightness of clear chairs makes rooms feel bigger than wood or upholstered options.
- **Event hosts:** Buy six, use them for gatherings, and they won’t clash with any table style.

**Skip it if** you have young kids who drag chairs across floors, or if you’re the type who wants furniture to stay pristine without maintenance. Also skip if you need stackable storage.

## FAQ

**Q: Can these really stay outside year-round?**
A: They handle sun, rain, and temperature swings well. For full-year exposure in harsh climates (freezing winters, intense coastal salt), bring them inside or cover them. No chair survives everything forever.

**Q: How do you clean them without streaks?**
A: Use a microfiber cloth with a drop of dish soap in warm water, then dry immediately with a second clean microfiber cloth. Avoid ammonia-based glass cleaners — they can cloud acrylic over time.

**Q: Do they fit standard dining tables?**
A: Yes. At 18 inches seat height, they match most standard 28–30 inch dining tables. Measure your table height to confirm before buying.
