Let's be direct: if you don't already own a Lego City train, this set is useless to you. But if you do, the 60238 Switch Tracks is one of those small expansions that quietly transforms your entire layout. I've tested it with both the passenger and cargo train sets, and here's what 240 Amazon reviewers (4.8 stars) and I discovered.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get 42 pieces. That's it. But what matters is what those pieces do. The set includes one complete switch mechanism — two curved track sections that pivot to redirect trains onto a diverging path — plus straight track segments to connect it into your existing loop. As shown in the product image, the gray and dark-gray color scheme matches the standard Lego train track system perfectly, so it blends seamlessly into any layout you've already built.

The build takes about 8 minutes. My 6-year-old did most of it solo. It's genuinely simple — no hidden complexity, no fiddly bits that require adult fingers.

## Performance: Does the Switch Actually Work?

Here's where this set earns its keep. The switching mechanism is mechanical and satisfying. You flip a small lever on the side, and the track physically repositions to guide the train onto the new path. I've run trains through this switch dozens of times at various speeds, and it hasn't jammed or derailed once — provided the train is moving at a reasonable clip.

One thing to note: the switch is manual. You can't automate it with the Lego City train app or add a motor to it. Buyers have mentioned this in reviews, with one noting, "Works great, but I wish it was motorized like the older 9V switches." If you're building an automated layout, you'll need to factor that in.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.8-star rating isn't an accident. Across the 240 reviews, a few themes dominate:

**Compatibility** — This is the big one. Reviewers repeatedly confirm it works with all current Lego City train sets, both battery-powered and push-along. One parent wrote, "Bought this to expand my son's cargo train set. It connected perfectly with zero issues."

**Value for money** — Given that it's a small set, buyers feel the price is fair for what it adds. "Cheaper than buying a whole new train set just to get track variety," noted one reviewer.

**Simplicity** — Parents love that kids can build and use this independently. "My 5-year-old figured out the switch mechanism in minutes," one review reads.

## Where It Falls Short

Honest downsides? Three things.

**It's an expansion, not a standalone** — If you don't have a train set, this is a box of useless track. That's not a flaw in the product, but it's a reality you need to accept before buying.

**One switch per box** — For a figure-eight or more complex layout, you'll need two or three of these. That adds up quickly.

**Manual operation only** — The lever mechanism is fine for kids playing actively, but it limits what you can do with automated train operations. Some hobbyists will find this frustrating.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You own any Lego City train set (60197, 60336, or the older 60198) and want to add real routing capability to your layout. It's also perfect for parents whose kids have mastered the basic oval track and want to introduce switching logic.

**Skip it if:** You're just starting out and don't own a train yet, or you're building a fully automated layout that needs motorized switches.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this work with my older Lego train set?**
A: If your train uses the current plastic track system (introduced in 2012), yes. The 60238 is fully compatible with all current-generation Lego City train tracks. Older 9V metal-track systems won't work.

**Q: Can I power the switch with my train's battery box?**
A: No. The switch is purely mechanical. There's no power connector or motor mount on this piece.

**Q: How many do I need for a figure-eight layout?**
A: You'll need two — one for each crossover point. The set includes one switch, so plan to buy two boxes for that setup.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The Lego City Trains Switch Tracks 60238 is a niche product that does exactly what it promises. It's not flashy, it's not automated, and it requires existing infrastructure to be useful. But for what it is — a functional, durable, kid-friendly track expansion — it's nearly perfect. The 4.8-star Amazon rating reflects that: buyers know what they're getting, and it delivers.

Buy it if you have a train set and want your layout to feel bigger and more interesting. Skip it if you're expecting a standalone product or motorized automation. For everyone in between, this is a solid, worthwhile addition to your collection.
