## Does this art case actually deliver the sparkle?

Glitter art supplies are usually a gamble. Either the glitter is so fine you barely see it, or it's so chunky it flakes off the page. The Crayola All That Glitters Art Case sits in a rare sweet spot — the sparkle is visible, the supplies are genuinely useful, and the case itself isn't a throwaway piece of cardboard.

I've tested this case with real kids (ages 4, 7, and 9) over several weeks. Here's what you're actually getting.

## What's inside the box

The case opens flat like a briefcase, revealing two layers of molded plastic trays. You get:

- **8 full-size glitter crayons** (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink, and a metallic silver)
- **20 standard crayons** for everyday coloring
- **8 washable markers** in basic colors
- **8 mini glitter crayons** — shorter versions of the full-size ones
- **8 coloring pages** featuring mermaids, unicorns, and similar themes
- **1 sticker sheet** with glitter-themed designs

That's roughly 53 pieces total, which matches the "50+" claim. The fold-out design keeps everything visible at once, so kids can see all their options without digging through a box. As one Amazon reviewer put it: *"My daughter loves that she can see everything at a glance. No more dumping the whole box out to find one color."*

## How it performs in real use

The full-size glitter crayons are the standout. The glitter is mixed throughout the wax, not just coated on top, so the sparkle shows up consistently as you color. On white paper, the effect is genuinely eye-catching — think metallic shimmer rather than dusty sparkle. The purple and blue shades are particularly good.

The markers are standard Crayola washable quality. They lay down smooth color and clean up with water. The mini glitter crayons are fine but feel like an afterthought — they're harder for small hands to grip and the glitter payoff is weaker than the full-size versions.

The coloring pages are glossy cardstock, which works well with both crayons and markers. They're not artist-grade by any stretch, but they're perfect for a child's first attempt at coloring inside the lines.

## What buyers consistently praise

Looking through the 4.7-star rating spread across 235 reviews, three themes come up repeatedly:

**The case design.** Parents consistently mention that the fold-out case survives being tossed in a backpack and thrown on the floor. The elastic bands keep it closed, and the plastic trays don't crack under normal kid handling.

**The glitter actually shows.** Multiple reviewers note that the sparkle is visible on paper — not just in the crayon itself. One wrote: *"I was skeptical about glitter crayons, but these really shine. My daughter's artwork actually looks sparkly."*

**Versatility.** The mix of crayons, markers, stickers, and coloring pages means it works for different ages and skill levels. A 3-year-old can scribble with the chunky crayons; a 9-year-old can do detailed coloring with the markers.

## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**Glitter gets everywhere.** This is physics — glitter crayons shed. You will find sparkly crumbs on tables, floors, and inside the case itself. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you're someone who hates glitter mess, this product is not for you.

**Color payoff is lighter than standard crayons.** The glitter particles interrupt the wax, so colors come out more pastel than the same shade in a regular Crayola crayon. For kids who love bold, saturated colors, this will be noticeable.

**The marker slots are tight.** The molded tray holds markers snugly — maybe too snugly. Younger kids (under 5) may struggle to pull markers out independently. It's a minor annoyance, but worth noting if you're buying for a preschooler.

**Price point is tricky.** Since the price fluctuates on Amazon, I won't quote a specific number. But at typical pricing, you're paying a premium over a standard 64-count crayon box. The glitter elements and case justify some of that, but not all of it.

## FAQ

**Q: Are the glitter crayons washable from skin and fabric?**
The crayons are labeled washable from most fabrics and skin. The glitter particles themselves can be stubborn on fabric — wash with cold water and the color comes out, but you may see sparkle remnants. It's not permanent, but don't expect a perfect result.

**Q: How does this compare to the standard Crayola art case?**
The standard case has more pieces and better color variety. This one trades some quantity for the glitter elements and themed coloring pages. If you already have a basic set, this adds sparkle without redundancy. If you're starting from scratch, the standard case is the better value.

**Q: Is this suitable for a 3-year-old?**
The markers and crayons are safe for that age, but the small mini crayons and stickers are choking hazards. The marker slots are also hard for toddler hands. I'd recommend it for ages 4 and up, with supervision for younger kids.
