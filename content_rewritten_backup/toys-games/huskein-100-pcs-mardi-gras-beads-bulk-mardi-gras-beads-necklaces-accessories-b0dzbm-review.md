## Build Quality and Real-World Use

Here’s where you need to calibrate expectations. These are not premium beads. The plastic is lightweight, glossy, and hollow-ish in feel—exactly what you’d expect at this bulk price point. But that’s not a flaw; it’s a feature. Heavy glass beads would be dangerous to throw and would cost ten times more.

The elastic cord is the weak point. It’s thin, and if you stretch it aggressively, it will snap. In practice, that means you should handle them gently when distributing—don’t yank them off your arm or let kids tug hard. One Amazon reviewer noted, *“The string is a bit flimsy, but for the price, you can’t complain. We used them for a float and they held up fine.”* Another said, *“Colors are vibrant and true to the photos. No fading after a day in the sun.”*

The beads themselves are durable enough for a single event. They won’t crack if dropped, and the color coating is baked in, not painted on top—so no chipping or flaking off onto your hands or costumes.

## Where It Falls Short

Honest downsides, because every product has them:

- **The elastic stretches out.** If you leave them hanging on a hook for months, the cord will lose tension and the necklaces will sag. These are event-use items, not long-term keepsakes.
- **No customization.** You get whatever color mix is in the bag. If you specifically need all-purple or all-gold, this isn’t the pack for you. Check the listing photos carefully to see the current color distribution.
- **Some batches have quality variance.** A few reviewers reported occasional necklaces with a snapped cord right out of the bag—maybe 2–3 out of 100. Not a dealbreaker, but if you need every single one, order a small buffer.
