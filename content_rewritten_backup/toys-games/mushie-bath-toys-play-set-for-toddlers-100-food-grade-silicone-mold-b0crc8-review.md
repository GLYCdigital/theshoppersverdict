## What You Actually Get

The set includes three graduated stacking cups in soft sage and ivory tones, a spinning flower wheel, and a star-shaped shape-sorter piece — all made from 100% food-grade silicone. There's no plastic, no BPA, no phthalates. The entire thing weighs next to nothing and feels like a high-quality kitchen spatula, not a cheap bath toy.

As shown in the product image, the cups nest neatly for storage, and the whole set takes up about as much space as a coffee mug. That's a win for small bathrooms or travel bags. The silicone surface has a matte, slightly grippy texture that's pleasant to hold — even when wet.

## Where It Falls Short — Honest Tradeoffs

The most common complaint is the lack of suction cups. The cups float and drift, which makes "stacking" harder than with traditional suction-based sets. If your toddler is in the "everything must stick to the wall" phase, this could be a minor letdown.

The spinner is also smaller than it looks in marketing photos. At roughly two inches across, it's a fine motor challenge for younger babies — some reviewers noted their 8-month-olds couldn't grip it effectively. It's better suited for 12 months and up.

Finally, there's the price. You're paying a significant premium for the material quality and design philosophy. If budget is a concern, you can find cheaper silicone bath toys, but they won't match mushie's aesthetic or durability.
