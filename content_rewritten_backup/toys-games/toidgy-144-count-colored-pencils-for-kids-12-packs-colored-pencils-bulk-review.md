Let’s cut through the noise: you’re looking at 144 pencils for $15.29. That’s 10.6 cents per pencil. At that price, you’re not buying art supplies — you’re buying volume. And for that purpose, the Toidgy 144 Count Colored Pencils deliver exactly what they promise, with a few tradeoffs you should know about before you hit “add to cart.”

## What You Actually Get

The box contains 12 individual packs, each with 12 pre-sharpened pencils in a standard rainbow-plus palette: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink, brown, black, and a few variations in between. Each pack is cellophane-wrapped with a cardboard header — which matters more than you’d think. If you’re a teacher building a prize bin or a parent prepping birthday party favors, you don’t have to sort 144 loose pencils. Grab a pack, hand it out, done.

The pencils themselves are hexagonal (which keeps them from rolling off desks) and measure a standard 7 inches. The cores are pre-sharpened to a usable point — not razor-sharp, but fine for immediate coloring.

## Real-World Performance

I tested these on standard copy paper and cardstock. The lead lays down smoothly with moderate pressure, and the color payoff is surprisingly even across all 12 shades — no weak yellows or streaky blues, which is rare at this price point. The cores are pigmented enough for solid coverage without requiring multiple passes, though they’re not wax-heavy like Crayola, so they won’t blend as easily for gradient work.

The pre-sharpened points held up reasonably well — about 20 minutes of steady coloring before needing a touch-up. The wood casing is thinner than what you’ll find on premium brands like Prismacolor, so aggressive colorers or overly enthusiastic kindergarteners may snap a few shafts. It’s a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, especially at this price.

## Where It Falls Short

Let’s be honest about the downsides.

**No erasers.** If you’ve got kids who need to fix mistakes, these won’t help. You’ll need a separate eraser, which is an extra purchase and an extra item to manage in a classroom setting.

**Thinner wood casing.** As mentioned, these aren’t indestructible. One reviewer noted, “My 6-year-old broke two pencils within the first hour of coloring.” If you’re buying for very young children who press hard, expect some casualties. At 10 cents a pencil, it’s not a financial disaster — but it’s worth knowing.

**Color range is basic.** You’re getting 12 standard colors, not a rainbow of 144 unique shades. Each pack is identical. If you need a wide spectrum for art class, this isn’t it. If you need bulk basic colors, this is perfect.

## FAQ

**Are these pencils safe for young children?**
Yes — they’re non-toxic and conform to ASTM D-4236 standards. The pencils are standard size, so they’re not a choking hazard for kids 3 and up, but adult supervision is advised for the under-5 crowd.

**Will the tips break in shipping?**
The individual packaging and cardboard headers provide decent protection. Most reviewers report receiving intact points, though a few noted minor breakage in transit. At this price, it’s not worth returning over — you’ll still have 140+ usable pencils.

**Can these be sharpened with a standard sharpener?**
Yes, they fit any standard pencil sharpener. The wood is soft enough for hand-crank sharpeners, and electric sharpeners handle them without jamming.
