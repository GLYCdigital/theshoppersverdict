## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has tradeoffs worth noting.

First, the search-and-find checklist is a separate piece of paper. It’s not laminated or attached to the box, so it can get lost. Consider taking a photo of it or taping it to the back of the box before your child gets their hands on it.

Second, at 48 pieces, this is not a "sit down and do it once" puzzle. For very young children (under 3), the piece count will be overwhelming. This is squarely aimed at the 3-6 age range, and younger kids will need significant parental help.

Third, the finished puzzle is large, which means it needs floor space. It won’t fit on a standard kid’s table. That’s by design — it’s a "floor puzzle" — but it’s worth knowing before you buy.
