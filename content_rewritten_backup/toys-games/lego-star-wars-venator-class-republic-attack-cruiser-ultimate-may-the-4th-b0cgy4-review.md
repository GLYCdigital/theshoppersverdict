Let's be direct: if you've been waiting for a LEGO Star Wars set that truly captures the *Venator*-class Republic Attack Cruiser's aggressive wedge silhouette, this is it. The Ultimate Collector Series treatment finally does justice to the ship that led the Grand Army of the Republic into battle. With 5,374 pieces, it's the largest *Star Wars* ship LEGO has released in this scale — and it shows.

**What's in the box**

You get 26 numbered bags, a thick instruction manual with background on the ship's *Revenge of the Sith* appearances, a display stand with printed plaque, and 10 minifigures. The figure lineup is strong: Captain Rex (exclusive to this set with updated printing), Admiral Yularen, Phase II clone troopers, a clone gunner, and a couple of Jedi. The minifigures are a genuine draw for collectors, but the ship is the star here.

**The build experience**

This is a three-to-five-day project for most builders. LEGO has engineered some genuinely clever techniques to achieve the hull's slope — angled bracket assemblies and SNOT (studs not on top) work that creates the aggressive downward rake from bow to stern. The twin bridge towers stack up with greebling that looks fantastic from every angle.

The interior frame is rock-solid. I picked the finished model up by the midsection and it didn't flex or creak. That matters at this size. The landing bay doors on the underside open via a simple gear mechanism, and the hangar bay has enough room for the included Delta-7 Aethersprite (a small, swooshable build) and a Republic gunship microfighter.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The Amazon reviews (4.7 out of 5 stars across 235 ratings) repeatedly highlight two things: the screen accuracy and the display presence. One reviewer put it plainly: "This is the Venator I've wanted since I was a kid. The proportions are finally right." Another noted, "Photos don't do it justice — the scale is breathtaking in person."

The included display stand is also a smart addition. It angles the ship for viewing and keeps the underside visible without needing a custom acrylic case. The printed plaque is a nice touch, though at this price point, a brick-built nameplate would have felt more premium.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the drawbacks. The mid-section hull is repetitive — you'll build essentially the same panel structure multiple times, and it can drag. This isn't a set for casual builders.

The ship is 21 inches long and about 13 inches wide at the stern. It will not fit in standard IKEA Detolf display cases, and it's too heavy for wall mounting without serious reinforcement. Plan your shelf space before you buy.

There's no meaningful interior. The top deck is mostly solid plating, and the hangar is accessible only through the underside doors. This is a display model, not a playset. If you want something to swoosh around the living room, buy the smaller 75333 Obi-Wan's Jedi Starfighter instead.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the adult collector who wants a centerpiece display piece and has the shelf space to accommodate it. It's also ideal for anyone who's been building LEGO *Star Wars* for years and wants a flagship UCS-style set that isn't another X-wing or Millennium Falcon. The minifigure selection alone will tempt completionists.

**FAQ**

**How long does the build take?**
Expect 12–18 hours spread across multiple sessions. The repetitive hull sections are faster once you get the rhythm, but the bridge towers and hangar detailing require patience.

**Is this compatible with the 2012 Venator set?**
No. This is a completely new design with different internal framing and scale. It's roughly 20% larger than the 2012 version and uses modern building techniques throughout.

**Does it come with a stand?**
Yes, a clear display stand with a printed plaque is included. The stand is sturdy and holds the ship at a slight angle for viewing.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if:** You're a serious LEGO *Star Wars* collector who wants the definitive version of this ship, and you have both the budget and shelf space for it.

**Skip it if:** You're on a budget, short on display room, or prefer playset-style features over display accuracy. At this price point, it's a deliberate purchase.

The LEGO Star Wars Venator-Class Republic Attack Cruiser Ultima earns its 4.6/5 rating. It's not perfect — the repetitive build and size limitations are real — but as a centerpiece display piece, it's the best *Clone Wars*-era ship LEGO has ever produced. If this is your era of *Star Wars*, this is your set.
