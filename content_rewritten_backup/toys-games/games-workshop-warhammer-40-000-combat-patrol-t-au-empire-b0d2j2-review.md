## Opening: The Tau Empire's Big Plastic Gamble

The T'au Empire Combat Patrol is Games Workshop's one-box answer to starting a shooting army. At roughly 500 points, this box gives you a playable force straight out of the shrink wrap — but "playable" comes with caveats. With a 4.8-star average across 240 Amazon ratings, the community clearly likes it. But is it the right starting point for *you*? Let's break down what's actually in the box, how it builds, and where the value really lies.

## What's In the Box

You get 15 plastic miniatures across three sprues:

- **1x Ghostkeel Battlesuit** — the big centerpiece, a stealth-suit walker with twin fusion blasters or cyclic ion raker options
- **1x Pathfinder Team (10 models)** — your objective-scoring infantry with rail rifles and drone options
- **1x Stealth Battlesuits Team (3 models)** — forward-deploying unit with battlesuit support
- **2x Drones** (included with Pathfinders and Stealth suits)

No glue or paints included — this is a hobby kit, not a board game. You'll need plastic cement, clippers, and a mold-line scraper before you even start.

## Build Quality and Assembly

The plastic quality is typical modern GW: crisp detail, minimal flash, and parts that fit cleanly. The Ghostkeel is the star — a multi-part kit with poseable arms and a cockpit that actually opens. It's an impressive model once assembled, but that's the catch: *assembled* is doing heavy lifting. Expect 6-10 hours of build time if you're new, with the Ghostkeel alone taking 2-3 hours. The Pathfinders are simpler, but 10 models with drones means repetitive small-part work. Experienced hobbyists will breeze through; beginners should budget a weekend.

One thing buyers consistently mention: the instructions are clear, but the Ghostkeel's leg assembly is fiddly. Take your time dry-fitting before gluing.

## Real-World Performance on the Tabletop

As a Combat Patrol box, this is designed for the smaller 500-point game mode. The Tau play as you'd expect: shoot first, ask questions never. The Ghostkeel brings serious anti-armor punch, while Pathfinders mark targets for the rest of your force. It's a genuinely strong, synergistic list — arguably one of the better Combat Patrol boxes of the current edition.

But here's the honest tradeoff: Combat Patrol requires the separate *Combat Patrol* rulebook or the Leviathan core book. This box isn't a standalone starter. If you want to play full 40K, you'll need a second HQ — no Cadre Fireblade or Ethereal is included, which is a real oversight at this price point. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"Great value for the models, but I had to buy a Commander separately just to field a legal 1000-point army."*

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.8-star rating isn't accidental. Across the reviews, three themes dominate:

1. **Value for money** — *"Cheapest way to get a Ghostkeel, and it's a gorgeous model."*
2. **Quality of sculpts** — multiple buyers note the detail is above average even for GW.
3. **Playability out of the box** — the list works well in Combat Patrol games without heavy optimization.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the missing HQ, there are two genuine frustrations:

- **No transfer sheet** — for a box at this price, the absence of decals for the Tau sept markings is stingy.
- **Drones are push-fit but fragile** — the clear plastic flight stands snap easily during transport. Buy a carry case or magnetize early.

## Who Should Buy This

- **New Tau players** wanting a solid Combat Patrol force — this is your box.
- **Existing 40K players** who want a Ghostkeel at a discount — buying the model alone costs nearly as much.
- **Hobby-first buyers** who enjoy assembly and painting — the Ghostkeel is a rewarding project.

**Skip it if** you're a pure beginner with zero hobby tools, or if you specifically want a melee-oriented army. The Tau are pure shooting — there's not a close-combat option in this box.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this a complete army?**
A: For Combat Patrol (500 points), yes — it's a legal, competitive list. For full 40K, no. You'll need an HQ like a Cadre Fireblade or Commander, and eventually more troops.

**Q: Do I need glue and paints?**
A: Yes. This is a hobby kit, not a pre-assembled game. You'll need plastic cement, clippers, and paints. Budget an extra $50-80 for tools and paint.

**Q: Can I play out of the box with just the Combat Patrol rules?**
A: Yes, but you need the Combat Patrol rulebook. The box includes only the assembly instructions and unit datasheets.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want the best value entry into the Tau Empire, especially if you're drawn to the Ghostkeel. It's a well-designed, playable force with genuine tabletop punch.

**Skip it if** you're on a tight budget and don't want to buy a separate rulebook and HQ, or if you prefer armies with more melee options.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a strong box with minor gaps that keep it from perfection. The Ghostkeel alone makes it worth the purchase for most players.
