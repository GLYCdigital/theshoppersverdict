## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the drawbacks. The furniture pieces are small and some are fragile — the chair legs and table pieces can snap if handled roughly. This isn't ideal for children under 4 who still put things in their mouths or play aggressively. Also, the house has no lighting or sound features, which some kids expect from modern toys. And while the neutral design is beautiful, it may not appeal to kids who want bright colors and fantasy elements — this is a "grown-up" style dollhouse, so manage expectations accordingly. Finally, assembly time is longer than most toy parents anticipate; it's not a 10-minute setup.

## Who Should Buy This

This is perfect for parents who want a dollhouse that doubles as décor, and for kids aged 4–10 who prefer realistic, modern play over fantasy themes. It's also great for Montessori-style families who favor open-ended, aesthetically pleasing toys.
