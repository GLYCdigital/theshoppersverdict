If you've got two kids close in age, you already know the drill: one ride-on car, one argument every thirty seconds. The ARBELI 24V 2 Seater 4WD truck sidesteps that problem entirely, and it does so with enough torque to make you question whether it's actually a toy. With four 200W motors pushing independent wheels, this isn't your average mall-crawler battery car.

## What's Actually in the Box

ARBELI ships this thing mostly assembled. You're looking at attaching the wheels, bumpers, and side mirrors — roughly 20-30 minutes of work with the included tools. The box includes:

- The truck body (spoiler alert: it's big — about 55 inches long)
- Four EVA tires (already mounted on rims)
- 24V battery and charger
- Bluetooth remote control for parent override
- Two seatbelts, two cup holders, and a wireless charging pad for phones

The EVA tires are a meaningful upgrade over the hard plastic wheels you see on cheaper models. They're foam-filled, so no flats, but they provide actual grip on grass and gravel. More on that below.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where this truck separates itself from the $150 Amazon specials. The 4x200W motor setup means each wheel gets its own motor. That's not marketing fluff — it translates to real 4WD capability. On packed dirt and short grass, this thing moves without bogging down. The listed top speed of 7.5 mph feels faster when you're watching a 5-year-old at the wheel.

The 24V battery gives roughly 60-90 minutes of continuous runtime, depending on terrain and weight. Two kids plus the truck's 110-pound weight capacity means you're near the limit with two average 6-year-olds, but it handles it without straining.

The remote control is the killer feature for parents. You get full override — steering, speed, and braking — which is non-negotiable if you've got a daredevil kid. The remote works from about 50 feet, which is plenty for driveway supervision.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.4-star rating (220 ratings) reflects genuine enthusiasm. A common thread in positive reviews:

> "We have a hill in our backyard and I was skeptical, but this thing climbs it with both kids in it. My neighbor's kid has a 12V single-seater that can't even make it up the driveway."

Multiple reviewers highlight the build quality — the plastic body is thick, not the flimsy stuff you can dent with a finger. The paint finish holds up to scrapes and bumps. Parents also consistently mention the Bluetooth audio and wireless charging as features the kids actually use, not just spec-sheet padding.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**Weight: 85 pounds.** This is a beast to move around. If you don't have a garage or covered storage, hauling it inside nightly gets old fast. It's not a "pick up and carry" toy.

**Assembly instructions are mediocre.** Several reviewers note the manual has confusing diagrams, particularly for the steering linkage. It's doable, but budget an hour if you're not mechanically inclined.

**The battery connector is a weak point.** A handful of reviews mention the connector getting loose after a few months, requiring a zip-tie or replacement. It's not universal, but it's a known issue worth monitoring.

**No suspension.** Despite the truck styling, there's no shock absorption. On bumpy terrain, the ride is jolting. Fine on pavement and short grass, but don't expect a smooth ride on uneven ground.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have two kids under 7 who'll actually ride together. The 2-seater layout with independent seats (no bench squishing) is the real selling point. Also buy if you have uneven terrain — the 4WD and EVA tires genuinely handle it.

**Skip it if:** Your kid is an only child and you just want a basic cruiser — you'll pay for features you won't use. Also skip if you lack covered storage; this truck is too heavy for casual indoor/outdoor shuffling.

## FAQ

**Q: How fast does it actually go?**
A: 7.5 mph max, but you can lock it to 3.5 mph via the remote or the speed selector on the dashboard. The low-speed mode is perfect for younger kids.

**Q: Will it work on gravel or dirt?**
A: Yes, and that's the differentiator. EVA tires with 4WD handle loose surfaces that stall 2WD models. Steep hills are a different story — it climbs moderate grades, but don't expect rock-crawling.

**Q: How long does the battery take to charge?**
A: About 8-10 hours for a full charge. Plan for overnight charging. There's no fast-charge option, so keep that downtime in mind.

## The Verdict

The ARBELI 24V 2 Seater is a legitimate step up from the budget ride-on crowd. The 4x200W motor setup delivers real power, the EVA tires handle terrain most competitors can't, and the dual-seat design solves the "my turn" problem permanently. It's not perfect — the weight, assembly quirks, and occasional connector issues are real — but for families with two kids and uneven ground, this is one of the best options under $500.

**Buy it if** you need genuine 4WD capability and a true two-seater layout.  
**Skip it if** you're on flat pavement with one kid and want something lighter and cheaper.

The ARBELI earns its keep where it counts: keeping siblings happy and actually tackling the terrain you own.
