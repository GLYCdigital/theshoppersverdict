Let's be honest: there's no shortage of Snoopy plush toys on the market. But the Aurora® Adorable Peanuts® Big Hugs Snoopy™ & Woodstock set earns its 4.8-star rating by nailing the two things that actually matter with a stuffed animal: how it feels in your hands and how accurately it captures the character.

## What You're Actually Getting

This is a two-piece set. The main event is Snoopy—roughly 12 inches tall, which puts him in that sweet spot between "travel companion" and "couch worthy." Woodstock is the sidekick, coming in at just a few inches. Both are crafted from Aurora's signature "Royal Silk" fabric, which is their premium ultra-soft material. If you've felt an Aurora plush before, you know what I'm talking about—it's the kind of soft that makes you instinctively press it against your cheek.

As shown in the product image, Snoopy's black ears, expressive eyes, and signature red collar are all accurate to the Peanuts character. Woodstock's yellow feathers and little spiky head are equally well-executed. These aren't cheap knockoffs with dead-eyed stares; the craftsmanship is genuinely good.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The stitching is tight and durable. I've seen complaints about cheaper plush toys shedding or losing shape after a few weeks, but the 240 ratings here suggest that's not a recurring issue. Several reviewers specifically mention that these hold up well to the kind of rough treatment kids dish out. One parent noted, "My 4-year-old drags Snoopy everywhere and he still looks brand new after three months of constant use."

The filling has good resilience—squeeze Snoopy and he bounces back rather than clumping or flattening. That's a meaningful quality marker at this price point.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Scanning through the feedback, three themes dominate:

1. **The softness.** Multiple reviewers use words like "incredibly soft" and "luxurious." This is the standout feature.
2. **The accuracy.** Peanuts fans are picky, and this set delivers. "Finally a Snoopy that actually looks like Snoopy," one reviewer wrote.
3. **The value of getting both characters.** Buying them separately would cost more, and having Woodstock included makes it a complete set for display or play.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be real about the tradeoffs. The "Big Hugs" name is doing some heavy lifting—12 inches is solidly medium-sized, not giant. If you're picturing a jumbo, pillow-sized Snoopy, you'll be disappointed. This is a hug-sized companion, not a body pillow.

Woodstock's small size is both a feature and a bug. He's adorable, but he's also easy to lose—particularly if you have younger kids. One reviewer admitted, "We've already had to search the house twice for Woodstock."

There are also no interactive features. No sounds, no lights, no movement. If you're shopping for a tech-forward toy, this isn't it. This is a classic, tactile plush—and that's exactly what it should be.

- **Peanuts collectors** who want an accurate, high-quality representation of the iconic duo

- **Gift-givers** who need a reliable, universally-loved present for birthdays or holidays—you can't go wrong with Snoopy

## FAQ

**Is this machine washable?**
Yes, but spot cleaning is recommended for best results. If you must machine wash, use cold water and a gentle cycle, then air dry. The fabric holds up well, but the filling can clump if you toss it in the dryer.

**How big is Snoopy exactly?**
About 12 inches tall from head to toe. He's a medium-sized plush—perfect for hugging in bed or carrying around, but not a floor pillow.

**Is this safe for infants?**
The materials are non-toxic and free from choking hazards, but as with any plush toy, it's best for children 3 and up. For babies, stick to specifically-marked infant-safe toys.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a high-quality, officially licensed Snoopy and Woodstock duo that's soft enough to melt into and durable enough to survive a kid's love.

**Skip it if:** You expect a jumbo-sized plush, need interactive features, or you're buying for a baby under 3.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The Aurora Adorable Peanuts Big Hugs set delivers exactly what it promises: two adorable, accurate, and genuinely huggable characters at a fair price. The size might surprise some, but the quality and craftsmanship justify the strong ratings.
