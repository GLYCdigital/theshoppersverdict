The Shadow Pokemon finally gets his due. Funko's Gengar Pop is the kind of figure that makes you wonder why it took them this long — and whether the final product lives up to the hype. After handling one and cross-referencing buyer feedback across 240 ratings, here's what you need to know before you click add to cart.

**What you actually get**

The box is standard Funko fare: window display front, protective cardboard sleeve, and the figure seated inside. Gengar himself stands roughly 3.75 inches tall — which is worth noting before you buy. This isn't a full-size Pop. He's noticeably shorter than your average 4-inch figure, which makes sense given his in-game sprite proportions but might throw off collectors who line up their Pops by height.

The vinyl itself is solid. The signature Gengar purple is accurate — not too dark, not washed out. Paint application on the eyes and the jagged grin is clean, which is honestly hit-or-miss with Funko's production line. No sloppy edges here.

**The glow factor**

Here's where this figure separates itself from the standard Gengar release: this version glows in the dark. Charge it under a lamp or direct sunlight for a few minutes, and the entire body emits that eerie, sickly green glow that fits the Ghost/Poison type perfectly. Buyers consistently mention this feature as the selling point. One reviewer put it simply: "The glow effect is way better than I expected — it's actually bright, not that weak glow you get with some figures." Another noted it "charges fast and stays visible for hours."

**Build quality and real-world use**

The base peg fits snugly into the stand. No wobble, no leaning. The head is proportionate to the body, and the ears are angled upward in that classic mischievous pose. For a mass-produced vinyl figure, this is about as good as it gets. One thing to note: the figure's small size means the details on the back are minimal, but that's true of the source material anyway.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the size. If you're expecting a standard Pop, you'll notice the difference immediately. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's a real consideration if you're displaying him alongside other Pokemon Pops. Second, there's no chase variant or convention sticker. This is the standard retail release — which is great for affordability, but if you're hunting for a grail piece, this isn't it. Third, the glow works best in complete darkness. In a dimly lit room, it's subtle rather than dramatic.

**Who should buy this**

This is a no-brainer for three specific groups: Pokemon collectors filling out their Gen 1 roster, Funko Pop completionists who need every Ghost-type variant, and parents shopping for a kid who's obsessed with Gengar specifically. If you fall into any of those categories, you'll be happy. If you're a casual buyer hoping for a big, imposing display piece — look elsewhere.

**FAQ**

**Is this the same size as other Funko Pops?**
No. Gengar is roughly 3.75 inches tall, noticeably shorter than the standard 4-inch Pop. He fits the character's proportions, but he won't line up perfectly with taller figures.

**Does it actually glow in the dark?**
Yes, the entire body glows. Charge it under bright light for 5-10 minutes for best results. It holds the glow for several hours, though intensity fades gradually.

**Is this an official licensed product?**
Yes, it's officially licensed by The Pokemon Company International. It comes in standard Funko window packaging with the official logo.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You're a Pokemon collector who wants an accurate, glow-in-the-dark Gengar for under $15. The paint quality is above Funko's average, the glow effect genuinely works, and it's a solid desk or shelf piece.

**Skip it if:** You're particular about uniform Pop sizing, you're chasing exclusive variants, or you want a larger display piece. This one's small, standard, and uncomplicated — and that's exactly what makes it a reliable buy.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Great figure, small stature, honest glow. For the price, it's hard to argue with 240 buyers giving it 4.8 stars.
