**The Real Story Behind This 4.8-Star Set**

Spidey and His Amazing Friends is the gateway drug for many young LEGO fans, and the Mobile Headquarters (set 10791) is the line's flagship vehicle. With 240 ratings averaging 4.8 stars, it's clearly doing something right — but let's dig into what that something actually is, and whether it's right for your kid.

**What's in the Box**

You get 248 pieces, four minifigures (Spidey, Miles "Spin" Morales, Ghost-Spider, and a brick-built Rhino), and the HQ truck itself. The truck measures about 10 inches long when assembled — a good size for small hands. Included accessories are thoughtful: a web-line launcher, a jail cell with opening door, a lab station with a beaker, and a small catapult on the roof. There's also a separate mini-jet that docks on top.

**Build Quality and Real-World Play**

The transformation gimmick is the star here. The truck's side panels swing open to reveal a jail and lab, and the roof pops up to create a launch pad for the jet. It's not a complex mechanism, but it holds together well — a critical factor since this set is aimed at ages 4 and up. One Amazon reviewer noted, "My 4-year-old can open and close the panels himself without breaking anything. That's a win." The pieces are chunky and forgiving, and the included "starter brick" baseplates make assembly frustration-free for pre-readers who follow the picture instructions.

The wheels roll smoothly on carpet and hard floors, and the truck stays intact during aggressive play — something parents of rough-and-tumble kids will appreciate. The minifigures are the real highlight: Miles in his full Spidey suit is exclusive to this set, and Ghost-Spider is a rare figure in the Friends line. Rhino is the weakest link — he's a brick-built character, not a molded minifigure, so he looks blockier than the heroes.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The praise in reviews clusters around two things: the play value and the minifigure selection. One buyer wrote, "My son plays with this every single day. The jail cell is his favorite part — he keeps putting Rhino in there." Another said, "Great set for the price. The truck is sturdy and the figures are adorable." Multiple reviewers mention that it's a "perfect first LEGO set" for toddlers transitioning from DUPLO, thanks to the simplified pieces and clear instructions.

**Where It Falls Short**

Honesty time. At 248 pieces, this is a quick build — most adults will finish in 20-30 minutes, and even a patient 5-year-old will be done in under an hour. If your child is 7 or older and already into LEGO, they'll likely outgrow this quickly. The interior is also tight: once you have Spidey and Miles inside the HQ, there's barely room to maneuver them, so the "headquarters" aspect feels more like a diorama than a fully interactive playset. And while the price is competitive for a licensed LEGO set, the piece count-to-price ratio is on the higher side compared to non-licensed sets of similar size.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is ideal for:
- **Parents of 4-6 year olds** who want a first "real" LEGO set that's actually playable, not just displayable
- **Fans of the Disney Jr. show** — the set accurately reflects the cartoon's characters and style
- **Collectors** who need Miles and Ghost-Spider minifigures for their Marvel collection

Skip it if your kid is 8+, already deep into LEGO City or Technic, or if you're hoping for a complex build experience.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this compatible with other LEGO sets?**
A: Yes, it uses standard LEGO bricks throughout. The truck's panels and accessories can be integrated into any other LEGO build, and the minifigures work across all Marvel sets.

**Q: What age is this actually suitable for?**
A: The box says 4+, and that's accurate. The pieces are larger and the instructions are picture-based. A 3-year-old might manage with heavy supervision, but 4-6 is the sweet spot. Older kids will find it too simple.

**Q: Does it come with stickers or printed pieces?**
A: Mostly printed pieces, which is great — only a couple of small stickers for the truck's side details. Printed pieces last longer and are less frustrating for young kids than applying stickers.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You have a preschooler obsessed with Spidey who needs a sturdy, playable LEGO set. This is the best value in the Spidey and His Amazing Friends line for the price.

**Skip it if:** Your kid is 8+ or already building 500+ piece sets independently — they'll be bored in 20 minutes.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A near-perfect first LEGO set for the preschool crowd, held back only by its quick build time and cramped interior. The exclusive minifigures and durable design make it an easy gift choice.
