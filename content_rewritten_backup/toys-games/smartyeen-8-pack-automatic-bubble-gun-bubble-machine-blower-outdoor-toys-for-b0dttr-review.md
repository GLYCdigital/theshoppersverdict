## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**Battery drain is real.** These guns chew through batteries faster than you'd expect, especially with enthusiastic use. Budget for rechargeable AAs if this will see regular play.

**The solution bottles leak if tipped.** The caps aren't incredibly tight, and kids will absolutely tip them sideways or upside down. Expect sticky hands and possibly a sticky car seat or picnic blanket.

**Build quality is budget-tier.** The plastic feels thin, and the bubble wand ring is the most vulnerable part. Rough play will eventually crack or bend them. They're not fragile, but they're not indestructible either.

**No off switch.** The trigger is the only control — there's no lock or power button. This means accidental activation in a toy box or backpack is a real possibility, which wastes batteries and creates a mess.
