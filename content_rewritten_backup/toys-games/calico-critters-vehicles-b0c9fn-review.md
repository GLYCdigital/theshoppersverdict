## The Calico Critters Vehicles Set: Finally, a Car That Actually Works

If you've got a Calico Critters collection at home, you already know the struggle: the houses are wonderful, but getting your critters *somewhere* has always been awkward. The Calico Critters Vehicles set — which includes a red family car and a yellow camper van — is the brand's answer to that problem. And honestly? It's a good one.

This isn't a gimmick purchase. It's a legitimately well-designed playset that expands what your kids can do with their existing Calico Critters figures.

---

## What's Actually in the Box

You get two vehicles: the Family Car (red) and the Camper Van (yellow). Both are sized to fit standard Calico Critters figures — the 3-inch tall ones, not the babies.

The Family Car seats two critters in front and two in back. The Camper Van has room for two up front and a full camper interior in the back, complete with a fold-down table, a small bed, and a tiny stove. Both vehicles have doors that open, functioning trunks, and rolling wheels.

No figures are included in this set — you're buying the vehicles only. That's worth knowing if you're shopping for a gift and don't already own Calico Critters.

---

## Build Quality and Real-World Play

Here's where this set earns its keep. The older Calico Critters vehicles had a notorious problem: figures didn't stay seated. Bump a car and your critter would launch out like a stunt driver. This set fixes that with proper seat pegs. Each seat has a small peg that the figure's foot hole clicks onto, keeping critters firmly in place during play.

As one verified buyer put it: *"My daughter has the older school bus and the critters would always fall out. This is so much better — they actually stay in the seats now. Worth the upgrade."*

The wheels roll smoothly on carpet, tile, and hardwood. The doors open and close with a satisfying click. The paint is clean with no overspray, and the details — headlights, taillights, even a tiny spare tire mounted on the camper's back — are genuinely impressive at this scale.

The camper's interior is the highlight. The table folds down, the bed is a real sleeping spot for a critter, and there's enough room for two figures to sit inside and "camp." It's the kind of detail that makes imaginative play easy.

---

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**Storage is a problem.** These are large toys. The camper van is roughly 8 inches long and the car about 6 inches. There's no bag, no storage box, nothing. They'll sit on a shelf or in a toy bin, taking up real estate. If you're tight on space, factor that in.

**The small parts are a hazard.** The spare tire, the picnic basket, the tiny stove pieces — all of these are choking hazards. The box says 3+, and that's accurate. If you have a toddler who puts things in their mouth, keep this on a high shelf.

**Hard plastic wheels.** They roll fine, but they're not rubberized. On wood floors, enthusiastic play can leave scuff marks. A rug or play mat solves this, but it's worth knowing.

---

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:**
- You already own Calico Critters figures and houses
- Your child is 4-10 and into imaginative, dollhouse-style play
- You want a vehicle set that won't break after a week of use

**Skip it if:**
- You're buying your first Calico Critters product (get a house or figure set first)
- You need something portable for travel — there's no carrying case
- Your child is under 3 — the small parts are a genuine risk

## FAQ

**Q: Do these vehicles fit all Calico Critters figures?**
A: Yes, they fit standard-size critters. The baby figures (the tiny ones) fit too, but they sit in the seats without the peg system — they can slide around more.

**Q: Can you remove the camper van's roof?**
A: No, the roof is fixed. But the doors open wide and the interior is accessible from the front and back, so play isn't restricted.

**Q: Are the wheels removable or do they lock?**
A: They're fixed and roll freely. There's no steering function or locking mechanism — it's a simple push-and-roll toy.
