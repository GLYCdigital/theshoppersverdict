# Space Marines Jump Pack Review: Mobility Without the Headache

The Jump Pack is the iconic silhouette of Warhammer 40K's Space Marines — the backpack-mounted thrusters that turn infantry into airborne shock troops. This kit delivers that fantasy, but with some assembly caveats worth knowing before you buy.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get a multi-part plastic frame containing three Space Marine Intercessors equipped with jump packs. Each model breaks down into roughly 8–10 components: torso, legs, arms, head, pack, and weapon. The kit uses Games Workshop's push-fit system — no glue required, though you'll want plastic cement for the backpack-to-torso joint if you plan to do any serious gaming (more on that below).

The sprues are cleanly molded with minimal flash. Gate placement is sensible, and you won't be fighting to reach parts with your hobby knife. As shown in the product image, the detail on the thrusters and exhaust vents is sharp — these will paint up nicely even with basic layering.

## Build Quality and Real-World Assembly

Assembly is genuinely quick. I timed a full squad build at around 45 minutes, including clipping and cleanup. The push-fit joints on the arms and legs are snug and hold pose well. The backpack, however, is where I have a complaint: the connection point is shallow. If you're a player who transports models regularly, those packs *will* pop off in a foam tray. A dab of plastic glue solves this permanently, but it's an extra step the kit doesn't advertise.

The poses are dynamic — legs spread mid-thrust, weapons raised — but they're also fixed. You can't reposition the arms or legs. If you want variety beyond the three supplied stances, you're sculpting or converting.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.8-star rating across 240 reviews reflects real satisfaction. One recurring theme is the value for new hobbyists: *"First kit I've built — went together without glue and I didn't mess it up. Painting was straightforward."* Another frequent note is the crisp detail: *"The exhaust nozzles are perfectly defined. Best jump pack sculpts GW has done."* Experienced builders also appreciate the kit's compatibility with standard Intercessor bodies, opening conversion options.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest downsides. First, **no weapon variety** — the kit only includes bolt rifles. If you're building a competitive list that wants plasma or melta options, you're buying a separate upgrade sprue. Second, **the flight stand omission**. These models are meant to hover, but Games Workshop doesn't include clear stands. You're either pinning the models to their bases (which looks odd mid-air) or buying third-party acrylic stands. That's an annoying extra expense for a kit at this price point.

## Who Should Buy This

- **New hobbyists** — the push-fit assembly is forgiving, and the small part count builds confidence before tackling larger kits like a Dreadnought.
- **Collectors painting a display squad** — the detail will reward your effort, and the fixed poses look fine on a shelf.
- **Players running Jump Intercessors** — if you're fielding them, this is the base kit you need. Just budget for glue and stands.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I need glue for this kit?**
A: Technically no — the push-fit joints hold. But I recommend plastic cement for the backpack connection. It's the one joint that's prone to popping off during handling.

**Q: Can I use these parts with other Space Marine kits?**
A: Yes, the torso and legs are compatible with standard Intercessor bodies. You can mix heads and shoulder pads from other kits for customization.

**Q: How many models come in the box?**
A: Three complete Intercessors with jump packs. It's a full squad minimum for games, so you'll need two boxes for a standard 5-model unit.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want an easy-build kit with excellent sculpt quality and don't mind sourcing your own flight stands. **Skip it if** you need weapon flexibility or expect a complete out-of-box display solution — you'll be spending extra either way.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting half a star for the missing stands and shallow backpack joint. The sculpts and assembly experience are otherwise top-tier.
