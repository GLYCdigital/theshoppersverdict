**WHAT'S IN THE BOX**  

This is a five-piece set: a tiara, a wand, a necklace, a pair of gloves, and a ring. The tiara is the centerpiece—it's made from a flexible plastic rather than rigid metal, which matters more than you'd think. Rigid tiaras dig into temples and end up abandoned by minute three. This one bends without breaking. The wand is roughly 12 inches long with a star-shaped topper, and the gloves are the stretchy "one size fits most" style that'll work for ages 3 through 8 without drama.  

The necklace is the weakest link physically—it's a thin chain with a small pendant. For a toddler who yanks on things, it's fine. For a 7-year-old who plays like a feral cat, expect it to snap eventually.

**PERFORMANCE AND BUILD QUALITY**  

The "Glo" in the name refers to the glow-in-the-dark elements, which are primarily on the tiara and wand. In practice, these glow faintly—not the radioactive-green of a glow stick, but a soft luminescence that fades after 20 minutes in darkness. Kids who've seen *Frozen* will expect more. Parents who've bought glow-in-the-dark anything will know better.  

The plastic has a decent weight to it—not the featherlight hollow stuff that feels like it'll crush if you look at it wrong. The rhinestones are glued on (not sewn), so they will come off with aggressive play. Multiple reviewers note losing a few gems within the first week. That's expected at this price point, but worth knowing before you buy.

**Q: What age range is this really for?**

**Q: Will the gems fall off?**
