## What You Actually Get

The box contains 12 plastic eggs, each measuring about 2.5 inches long — a solid, chunky size that's easy for small hands to grip and open. The eggs themselves are two-piece snap-together shells in pastel pink, purple, and blue. They're sturdier than the flimsy dollar-store variety; the hinge mechanism holds up to repeated opening and closing, which matters if you plan to refill them for future hunts.

Inside each egg is a mix of princess-themed items: plastic rings with faux gems, bracelets, tiaras, hair clips, and sticker sheets. As shown in the product image, the contents are visible through a clear window on the box, so you know exactly what you're buying — no mystery meat. The pieces are made of smooth, flexible plastic with no sharp edges, which is reassuring for younger kids.

## Real-World Performance

The selling point here is convenience, and Uiseba delivers. Each egg comes pre-filled, so there's zero prep work. The toys are genuinely age-appropriate for the stated 3+ range — nothing is small enough to be a choking hazard, and the jewelry is sized for preschool-to-early-elementary fingers. Parents in the reviews consistently mention this as a time-saver. One buyer noted, "I didn't have to buy anything else — just threw these in the basket and was done." Another praised the quality: "The eggs are much thicker than the ones I usually buy at the dollar store. They didn't crack when my 4-year-old threw them around."

The princess theme is cohesive, which is rarer than you'd think for prefilled eggs. Many competing products mix random dinosaurs and farm animals into a "princess" set; this one sticks to crowns, tiaras, and jewelry throughout. That consistency makes it feel more intentional and gift-worthy.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the 12 eggs don't contain 12 unique items. Several Amazon reviews mention receiving duplicate rings or bracelets across multiple eggs. If you have two kids splitting the set, they may both get the same pink ring — which can trigger a meltdown. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

Second, the jewelry is small. The rings fit adult pinky fingers at best, and kids around 7-8 years old may find the bracelets too tight. This is firmly a toddler-to-kindergarten product. Third, there's no candy inside — which is fine if you're avoiding sugar, but if your kids expect chocolate with their eggs, you'll need to supplement.

## Who Should Buy This

This is ideal for parents of 2- to 5-year-old girls who want a no-fuss Easter basket filler. It's also a solid choice for grandparents who want to ship something festive without assembling a care package, or for classroom egg hunts in preschool settings where you need uniform, non-edible treats. The resealable eggs mean you can reuse them year after year with your own fillings.

**How big are the eggs?**
Each is about 2.5 inches long — slightly larger than a standard Cadbury Creme Egg. They're easy to hide but not tiny enough to lose under furniture instantly.
