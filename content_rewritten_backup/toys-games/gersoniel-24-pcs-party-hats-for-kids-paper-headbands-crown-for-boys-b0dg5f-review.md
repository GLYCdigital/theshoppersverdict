## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. The one-size-fits-most approach excludes tweens and adults. If you're planning a family party where grandparents will wear hats too, these will likely be tight or uncomfortable on adult heads. Buy a separate adult pack if that matters.

The color variety is also more pastel-leaning. If you're throwing a bold-themed party — think bright superheroes or neon rave — these might feel muted. They're perfect for birthdays, Easter, and general celebrations, but less ideal for high-energy themed events.

Finally, while the assembly is easy, the hats are still paper. They're single-use. If you're looking for reusable party supplies, these aren't that product.
