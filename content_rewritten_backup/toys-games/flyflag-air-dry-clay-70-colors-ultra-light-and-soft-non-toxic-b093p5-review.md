Seventy colors in one box sounds like a craft-store impulse buy that'll gather dust. But the FlyFlag Air Dry Clay kit has quietly earned 225 ratings at 4.5 stars, and there's a reason beyond the rainbow presentation. Let's dig into whether this is the clay set your shelf needs.

## What You Actually Get

The headline feature is 70 individual containers, each holding 10 grams of clay. That's 700 grams total — roughly 1.5 pounds of material. As shown in the product image, the set arrives as a compact storage case with each color in its own small tub, which beats the typical multi-color block where you're guessing which shade is which.

The clay is described as "ultra light" and that's accurate — it's noticeably airier than standard modeling clay. It's a foam-clay hybrid that feels similar to a dense marshmallow. This isn't the heavy, oily clay you remember from art class.

## Real-World Performance

The texture is where this set wins. It comes out of the container soft and workable immediately — no kneading, no warming, no frustrated kids waiting for clay to soften. It's pliable enough for small hands but holds shape well enough for detailed sculpting.

Drying takes 24-48 hours depending on thickness. The lightweight formula dries without the cracking that plagues cheaper air-dry clays, even on thin pieces like flower petals or animal ears. One Amazon reviewer noted: "My 7-year-old made a tiny unicorn and it didn't crack at all — that never happens with the stuff from the craft store."

The 70 colors are the real selling point. You get gradient ranges — multiple blues, pinks, greens — that let kids match specific shades without mixing. That's a genuine time-saver and frustration-reducer for younger crafters.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Parents repeatedly mention the organization. The color-coded containers keep everything tidy, and the clear lids mean kids can find the exact color they want without dumping out the whole box.

The softness gets consistent shoutouts. From the reviews: "My 3-year-old can actually mold this — other clays are too stiff for her hands." That's a meaningful accessibility point for younger kids or those with motor skill challenges.

## Where It Falls Short

The 10-gram portions are genuinely small. A single egg-sized sculpture will use 2-3 containers of one color. Kids who like making big creations will blow through the set quickly, and there's no refill pack for individual colors — you'd need to buy the whole set again.

The finished pieces are lightweight and somewhat brittle. They're perfect for display or play, but don't expect to make functional items like bowls or pen holders that need structural integrity. This is decorative craft clay, not durable material.

Finally, the container lids can be tricky for small hands to reseal — several reviewers mentioned needing to help kids close them properly to prevent drying out.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have kids ages 3-10 who enjoy crafting and want a mess-free, color-organized option. The soft texture is ideal for beginners, and the color variety keeps projects interesting.

**Skip it if:** You're a serious sculptor or need clay for large, functional pieces. The small portions and lightweight finish will frustrate advanced users.

## FAQ

**Is this clay really non-toxic?** Yes — it's listed as non-toxic and safe for children. That said, it's not edible, so supervision for kids under 3 who still mouth objects is wise.

**How long does it take to dry?** Thin pieces dry in 24 hours; thicker sculptures need 48 hours. Drying time depends on room humidity.

**Can you paint the dried clay?** Yes, acrylic paints work well on the dried surface. It's a nice way to extend the set once colors run out.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The FlyFlag Air Dry Clay delivers exactly what it promises: 70 colors in a soft, ultra-light formula that's genuinely easy for kids to use.
