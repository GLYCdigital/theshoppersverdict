Four pounds of slime. That's not a typo. The FunKidz Milky Butter Slime Bucket is a serious commitment to the slime lifestyle, and for parents of kids who can't get enough of the stretchy stuff, it's a value play that's hard to ignore.

## What You Actually Get

The bucket arrives filled to the brim with 64 fluid ounces of milky-white butter slime. The container itself is a sturdy plastic tub roughly the size of a large paint bucket — think 8 inches tall and 6 inches wide. Inside you'll find the slime, a small bottle of activator solution, and a plastic scoop. No charms, no glitter, no frills. Just a mountain of buttery, cloud-like slime.

## The Real-World Test

Butter slime gets its signature spreadable texture from soft clay mixed into clear glue base. FunKidz gets the ratio right. Out of the bucket, it's pillowy and spreadable — you can literally "butter" it across a table like frosting a cake. It stretches about 12 inches before snapping, which is respectable for a butter slime (these are never going to be as stretchy as clear or jelly slimes).

The scent is worth mentioning: it's a soft, clean vanilla-milk smell that's noticeable but not cloying. Multiple reviewers have noted it's a "very pleasant smell" that doesn't linger on hands after washing — a big win for parents who've suffered through chemical-smelling slimes.

The texture does require some work. Fresh out of the bucket, it's slightly tacky. The included activator fixes that — knead a few drops in for 2-3 minutes and it transforms into that signature buttery, non-stick feel. One reviewer noted, "It's a bit sticky at first, but after adding a little of the included activator it becomes the perfect consistency."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The sheer quantity is the headline. Overwhelmingly, customers emphasize the value: "This is a LOT of slime. I was shocked at how full the bucket was." Parents of younger kids (5-8) report it surviving months of weekly play sessions. The durability is real — the clay-based formula doesn't dry out as fast as glue-only slimes, and it can be rehydrated with a few drops of water if it starts to stiffen.

## Where It Falls Short

The bucket lid is friction-fit, not airtight. If your kid doesn't seal it properly (and they won't), the surface layer dries out within 3-5 days. You'll need to store it in a ziplock bag or transfer it to an airtight container if you want it to last. That's a real design flaw for a product marketed as a gift.

There's also no variety. One color, one scent, no add-ins. For the price point, some kids will find it monotonous after a few sessions — especially compared to slime kits that include multiple textures and mix-ins.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Parents of kids 5-10 who are obsessed with slime** — this replaces three or four smaller containers at a fraction of the cost
- **Teachers or camp counselors** — enough slime for a group activity without individual containers
- **Gift-givers looking for a big-impact present** — the bucket presentation is impressive and the value is undeniable

**Skip it if** you're buying for a slime connoisseur who wants variety, or if you can't tolerate the initial stickiness and the storage hassle.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this slime stain hands or furniture?**
A: No. The butter slime uses a clay-based formula that's non-staining on skin. On fabric, it can leave a greasy residue if left to dry, but it washes out with warm soapy water.

**Q: How long does it last?**
A: With proper storage (airtight container), 2-3 months. In the original bucket with the lid on, 3-4 weeks. If left open overnight, the top layer will crust over but the interior stays usable.

**Q: Is it safe for younger kids?**
A: It's non-toxic and labeled for ages 3+. That said, the texture is very appealing to put in mouths, so I'd keep it away from kids under 5 who still explore with their mouths. The included activator is a skin irritant if left on, so supervise younger kids during play.
