### Where It Falls Short (The Honest Truth)

The biggest complaint is the dustpan design. The handle is short, and the pan itself is shallow, making it awkward for little hands to hold the pan and sweep into it simultaneously. It’s a minor design flaw, but it does frustrate kids who are trying to master the mechanics of sweeping.

Second, the mop handle is a bit long relative to the broom. For a tall 3-year-old, the broom is perfect, but the mop can feel unwieldy. Finally, the set is lightweight by design—so if your child is prone to throwing toys, the wooden pieces can be a minor hazard if they catch you in the shin. It’s not a dealbreaker, but it’s worth noting.
