If your toddler has watched *Moana* roughly 47 times (no judgment — our house is the same), this Little People playset is about to become the MVP of your toy rotation. But here's the thing: it's not just a licensed cash-in. It's a genuinely well-designed toy that respects how toddlers actually play.

## What's in the Boat

You get the canoe itself — a chunky, molded plastic boat with a removable sail and a small seat — plus two Little People figures: Moana in her signature red outfit and Pua the pig. That's it. No lights, no sounds, no accessories. And honestly? That's part of the appeal.

The figures are the classic Little People size — roughly 2.5 inches tall — which means they fit perfectly in a 12-18 month old's grip but aren't small enough to be a choking hazard. The canoe measures about 6 inches long, making it a substantial toy that's easy to grab and carry.

## Real-World Play Performance

Here's where this toy surprised me. The canoe actually floats. Drop it in the bathtub and it bobs along nicely — Moana stays seated in her little slot while the boat drifts. That's a huge win for bath time, which is where this toy genuinely shines.

On land, the play value is solid but straightforward. The canoe rocks side to side, the figures pop in and out of their seats easily (which toddlers love — popping things out is apparently peak entertainment), and the sail rotates. It's designed for open-ended imaginative play, not flashy features.

One thing parents consistently mention in the 245 Amazon reviews is how well the figures fit small hands. One reviewer put it simply: "My 2-year-old carries Moana everywhere. She's the perfect size for him to hold while he toddles around." That tracks with our experience — the figures are chunky enough to grip but light enough to carry without dropping.

## What Buyers Love

The 4.9-star rating isn't inflated. Across the reviews, three themes dominate:

**Durability.** Fisher-Price Little People toys are practically indestructible, and this set is no exception. Multiple reviewers noted their kids have dropped, thrown, and chewed on these figures with zero damage.

**Bath time success.** The floating feature gets called out repeatedly. One parent wrote: "I bought this for the boat, but the fact that it floats in the tub is the real winner. My daughter asks for bath time now just to play with it."

**The Pua factor.** Pua the pig is a genuine crowd-pleaser. Several reviews mention that the pig is their child's favorite figure — he's cute, small, and easy to grip.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. **Maui is missing.** For a set called "Moana & Maui's Canoe," the demigod is nowhere to be found. If your kid is obsessed with Maui (and what toddler isn't?), they'll notice. You can buy Maui separately, but that's an extra purchase.

The lack of sounds or lights is also worth noting. Some toddlers — especially those used to more interactive toys — may lose interest faster. If your child needs auditory feedback to stay engaged, this might not hold their attention as long.

Finally, the figures are small enough to vanish into the toy abyss. They're not tiny, but they're definitely "check under the couch" material. If you're gifting this, pair it with a small storage bin.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have a toddler (12-36 months) who loves Moana, needs a bath-safe toy, or you're looking for a screen-free, open-ended play option. It's also a fantastic gift for a first or second birthday — it's age-appropriate, durable, and doesn't require batteries or assembly.

**Skip it if:** Your child is over 3 and expects more complex play features, or if they're specifically asking for Maui — you'll need to buy him separately, which adds to the cost.

## FAQ

**Is this toy safe for a 1-year-old?** Yes. The figures are too large to be choking hazards, and the canoe has no small detachable parts. The recommended age is 18 months and up, but many parents report giving it to younger toddlers under supervision.

**Does the canoe actually float?** Yes. It's designed for water play and floats well in a bathtub or pool. Just dry it off after bath time to prevent mildew.

**Can you buy additional figures separately?** Yes. Fisher-Price sells Maui and other Moana characters separately, so you can expand the set over time.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5 stars**

This is a genuinely good toddler toy that earns its place in your toy bin. The floating canoe, durable figures, and open-ended play value make it a solid investment for the 1-3 age range. The missing Maui and lack of interactive features keep it from a perfect score, but for what it is — a simple, sturdy, imaginative playset — it delivers exactly what it promises.

**Buy it if** you want a durable, bath-friendly Moana toy that will survive toddler chaos. **Skip it if** you need interactive features or want the full Moana character roster in one box.
