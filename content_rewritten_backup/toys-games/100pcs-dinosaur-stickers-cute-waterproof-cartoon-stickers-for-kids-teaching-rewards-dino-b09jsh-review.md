## Where It Falls Short

Being honest, there are three real drawbacks:

**Size.** Most stickers hover around 1.5 inches. The product images make some look larger than they actually are. If you're expecting big statement decals, you'll be disappointed. These are accent stickers, not centerpieces.

**Sticker sheets can be tricky.** The die-cutting is clean, but the backing paper is thin. A few reviewers mentioned the sheet folding during shipping, causing some stickers to crease. It's not widespread, but it happens.

**Not repositionable.** As mentioned, the strong adhesive means no do-overs. For perfectionists or young kids who misplace stickers, this could cause frustration.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these stickers safe for a 3-year-old?**
The stickers themselves are non-toxic, but some of the smaller ones (around 1 inch) could be a choking hazard for kids who put things in their mouths. Supervision recommended for toddlers.

**Q: Do they leave residue when removed?**
On smooth surfaces like plastic or glass, they peel cleanly with minimal residue. On paper or painted walls, you'll likely damage the surface — these are meant for hard surfaces.

**Q: Can I use them on a car window or bumper?**
Technically yes, but they're not rated for outdoor UV exposure long-term. They'll survive a few months, but will fade faster than purpose-made car decals.
