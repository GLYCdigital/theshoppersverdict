Two iconic brands collide in this Care Bears x Wicked 2-pack, and honestly, it's a match made in crossover heaven. The set pairs Love-a-Lot Bear as Glinda with Share Bear as Elphaba, and the design team clearly had fun with the concept. Love-a-Lot's signature heart belly badge gets a sparkly pink makeover with a tiny crown, while Share Bear rocks Elphaba's black hat and cape. The product images don't do the details justice — in person, the costume elements are sewn in, not printed on, which makes a huge difference in how premium these feel.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains two plush bears, each roughly 10 inches tall. That's slightly smaller than the standard 12-inch Care Bears, which caught a few reviewers off guard. One buyer noted, "I was expecting the classic size, but honestly these are perfect for display on a shelf." The set also includes small tags identifying each character's crossover role, which collectors will appreciate. No batteries, no sound, no accessories — just the two bears.

**Build Quality and Real-World Handling**

The plush material is soft without being flimsy — that dense, huggable quality Care Bears are known for. The embroidered facial features and belly badges are stitched securely; after several weeks of handling, there's zero fraying or loose threads. The costumes are attached at key points (crown to head, cape to shoulders) with hidden stitching that holds firm even when kids tug at them. I'd trust these to survive the average child's playroom, though they're clearly designed with collectors and display in mind.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.9-star rating across 245 reviews isn't hype. The crossover concept resonates strongly — one reviewer called it "the collab I didn't know I needed," while another mentioned buying a second set "because my daughter claimed the first one immediately." The quality of the costume details gets frequent shout-outs, particularly Love-a-Lot's Glinda gown, which includes subtle shimmer fabric that catches light nicely. Parents also appreciate that these work as a gift without requiring additional purchases.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about limitations. At 10 inches, these are display pieces first, cuddle toys second. If you're buying for a toddler who expects a full-size plush, the size difference matters. There's also zero interactivity — no sounds, no movement, no hidden features. For the price point, some buyers might expect more, though the two-pack value partially offsets that. Finally, if you're not a Wicked fan, the costume elements might feel random — this is very much a crossover product designed for fans of both properties.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Wicked fans** who want subtle (or not-so-subtle) merchandise beyond the standard merch
- **Care Bears collectors** completing their crossover lineup
- **Gift-givers** looking for something unique for a musical theater kid or pop culture enthusiast
- **Display-oriented buyers** who want a conversation piece that doesn't scream "toy"

Skip it if you're after a classic, full-size Care Bear, or if the recipient doesn't know or care about Wicked.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these the same size as regular Care Bears?**
No — these are about 10 inches tall versus the standard 12-inch size. It's noticeable when placed side by side, but they're still substantial plush toys.

**Q: Can the costumes be removed?**
The costume elements are sewn in, not removable. You can't swap them between bears or take them off for washing.

**Q: Are they suitable for young children?**
Yes, for supervised play. The stitching is secure and there are no small detachable parts, but the smaller size and lack of interactive features make them better suited for ages 4+ who can appreciate the design.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're a fan of either franchise and want a high-quality, visually charming crossover piece that shows genuine design thought. The two-bear set delivers value, the craftsmanship is solid, and the novelty factor is high.

**Skip it if** you need a standard-size Care Bear for a young child, or if you're not invested in the Wicked connection — the costumes are the entire point here.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a delightful, well-executed crossover with minor size and feature tradeoffs that won't matter to its target audience.
