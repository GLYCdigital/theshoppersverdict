If you've been watching the POP MART x Powerpuff Girls collab from afar, here's the honest breakdown: this isn't just another sticker-on-a-product cash grab. The Crybaby aesthetic — those perpetually sad, tear-streaked eyes — actually works surprisingly well with Blossom, Bubbles, and Buttercup. The melancholy twist on the famously upbeat trio gives collectors something genuinely fresh.

**What's in the box**

You're getting one sealed blind box containing a single vinyl face plush. The base series has six characters (Blossom, Bubbles, Buttercup, plus three secret variants), and there's a chase version with a 1:72 pull rate. Each figure stands about 4 inches tall — noticeably smaller than the standard Crybaby vinyl figures, which run closer to 5-6 inches. If you're a scale collector mixing lines, keep that in mind.

The construction is the standout feature. The face is a rigid vinyl mask with that signature Crybaby teardrop detail, while the body is a soft, huggable plush. It's a hybrid approach that feels premium in hand — the vinyl has a smooth, slightly matte finish, and the plush is dense enough to hold its shape on a shelf but soft enough to actually squeeze.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.7-star average across 235 ratings reflects genuine enthusiasm. One reviewer noted, "The quality is way better than I expected for a blind box — the stitching is clean and the vinyl face doesn't wobble or detach." Another collector highlighted the character design: "They actually made Bubbles look cute-sad instead of just slapping Crybaby eyes on the same molds. The color gradients on the dresses are subtle but noticeable."

The chase figure hunt is a real draw. Buyers report the 1:72 odds feel fair — rare enough to be exciting, but not so absurd you'll never see one in person. Several reviews mention buying multiple boxes specifically to chase the full set, and the packaging does include a character checklist that makes tracking your collection easy.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be direct about the downsides. The blind box format is the biggest frustration — if you want a specific character, you're gambling. Buying a full case (6 boxes) doesn't guarantee a complete set because the chase replaces one random figure. Expect duplicates; it happens to nearly everyone.

The plush body is also a dust magnet. The soft fabric picks up lint and pet hair quickly, and spot-cleaning is tricky because the vinyl face can't get wet. Keep these displayed behind glass or in a cabinet if you want them to stay pristine.

Finally, the price-per-inch math is rough. At this size, you're paying a premium for the brand collab, not the physical volume of material. If you're not a POP MART or Powerpuff Girls fan specifically, this won't convert you.

**Who should buy this**

- **POP MART completionists** building out their Crybaby collection
- **Powerpuff Girls nostalgia fans** who want a quirky, adult-oriented take on the characters
- **Blind box thrill-seekers** who enjoy the chase and don't mind duplicates

Skip it if you're buying for a young child — the vinyl face is a choking hazard for kids under 3, and the collectible pricing doesn't match hard play.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I tell which character is inside before opening?**
A: No. The boxes are sealed and unmarked. Weighing them doesn't reliably work either — the plush bodies vary slightly but not enough to identify characters with confidence.

**Q: How does the chase version differ from the base figures?**
A: The chase has a metallic or iridescent finish on the vinyl face and slightly different eye detailing. It's a 1:72 pull rate, meaning roughly one in every 72 boxes.

**Q: Are these safe for kids?**
A: The vinyl face is a separate piece from the plush body. For children under 3, the risk of detaching and choking is real. For older kids, they're fine but fragile — not rough-play toys.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You're a collector who appreciates the clever design crossover and the hybrid plush-vinyl construction. The quality justifies the price for the right buyer.

**Skip it if:** You hate blind box gambling, want a larger display piece, or are shopping for a child's everyday toy.
