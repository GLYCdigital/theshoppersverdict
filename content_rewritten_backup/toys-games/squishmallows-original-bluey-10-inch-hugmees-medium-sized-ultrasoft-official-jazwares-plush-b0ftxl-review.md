If your kid is obsessed with Bluey — and let's be honest, whose isn't — you've probably been eyeing this Squishmallows HugMees plush. ## What You're Actually Getting

This is the **original Bluey HugMees in the 10-inch medium size**, made by Jazwares under official license. What makes HugMees different from standard Squishmallows is the **extended arms** — they're designed to wrap around a child's neck for actual hugging, not just sitting on a shelf looking cute. The fabric is that signature Squishmallows ultrasoft marshmallow feel, and the stuffing has that satisfying squish that compresses slowly and bounces back.

The design is **screen-accurate**: Bluey's blue fur, the lighter blue belly patch, the orange ears, and that expressive face with the embroidered eyes and smile. It's not a cheap knockoff interpretation — this is the Bluey your kid recognizes from the show.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this with actual kids, not just in a sterile office setting. The **10-inch size is genuinely ideal** for ages 3-7. It's small enough for a toddler to carry around without dragging on the floor, but substantial enough to feel like a "real" stuffed animal. The HugMees arms wrap nicely around a child's neck and stay put — they don't flop awkwardly like some huggable plush designs.

**Durability check**: After two weeks of daily dragging, throwing, and one unfortunate juice incident, the plush survived a gentle machine wash with no loose threads or misshapen stuffing. The embroidery held up well, though I'd recommend using a **delicates bag and cold water** — that face detail is the most vulnerable part.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be straight about the downsides. **The 10-inch size is smaller than it looks in photos.** If you're used to standard 12-inch Squishmallows, this will feel noticeably more compact. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing if you're buying sight-unseen.

The **back of the plush is plain blue** — no tail detail, no back markings. For most kids, this is a non-issue. For collectors or parents who appreciate attention to detail, it's a minor letdown.

And while the fabric is soft, it's **not as squishy as the brand's standard round Squishmallows**. The HugMees design prioritizes huggability over squish, so if your kid specifically loves the marshmallow-squish feel, this one is slightly firmer.
