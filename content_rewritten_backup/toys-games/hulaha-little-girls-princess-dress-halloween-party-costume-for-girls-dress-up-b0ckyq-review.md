## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Parents consistently report this dress surviving what kids actually do: running, twirling, jumping, and the occasional face-plant. The tulle holds its shape — no sad, flattened skirt by 7 PM. ## Where It Falls Short

The sleeves are the main complaint. They run snug — several parents mention sizing up specifically for arm room. If your daughter has broader shoulders or falls between sizes, go up. A dress that fits everywhere but binds at the arms will ruin the night.

The skirt is also not machine-washable in the conventional sense. Tulle and sequins don't play well with a washing machine. Spot cleaning is the realistic approach, which means this is a costume first, not a durable everyday dress.
