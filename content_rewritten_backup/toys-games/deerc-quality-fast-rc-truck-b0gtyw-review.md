## The DEERC Fast RC Truck: Real Speed, Real Durability — With a Few Caveats

Most RC trucks under $100 are toys — plastic body shells, weak motors, and suspension that gives up after a single hard landing. The DEERC Quality Fast RC Truck sits in a different category. It's not a hobby-grade rig from Traxxas or Arrma, but it's genuinely fast, genuinely tough, and genuinely fun. Here's what you need to know before you click buy.

### What's Actually in the Box

The package includes the truck itself, two 7.4V 1500mAh Li-ion batteries, a USB dual charger, spare parts (a-pins, hex wrenches, extra gears), and a 2.4GHz remote. The dual-battery setup is the standout — most competitors include one battery and 10 minutes of run time. With two packs, you're looking at roughly 30–35 minutes of driving before you need a recharge. That's enough for a full session at the park or a long driveway race with the kids.

### Performance and Build Quality

The headline spec is the brushless motor. That's not marketing fluff — it's a genuine upgrade over the brushed motors found in most budget trucks. On flat ground, this thing hits 30+ mph. On grass, dirt, or gravel, it slows to maybe 20–25 mph, which is still plenty fast for a toy-grade truck. The four-wheel drive and oil-filled shocks keep it planted through corners, and the full metal drivetrain (gears, axles, and drive shafts) means you're not replacing plastic parts every other weekend.

The truck is built to crash. The chassis is a solid plastic tub with a metal skid plate underneath. I've seen videos of owners launching it off ramps, cartwheeling it, and driving it straight into curbs — the body takes scratches, but the mechanicals hold up. One reviewer said, "I've hit trees, curbs, and dropped it down a flight of stairs. It keeps going." That's consistent with what I've seen.

### What Buyers Consistently Praise

The speed and durability dominate the positive reviews. One verified buyer wrote, "My 10-year-old son has put this through hell for two months — it's still running strong. The dual batteries are a huge plus." Another noted, "This is the fastest RC truck I've owned under $100. My neighbor's Traxxas isn't much faster." The two-battery setup earns repeated mentions, as does the spare parts kit — minor repairs are quick, and you have what you need out of the box.

### Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest part. The stock tires are mediocre on hard surfaces. They're soft and grippy on dirt, but on pavement, they wear noticeably — one reviewer reported bald rear tires after three weeks of daily driveway use. If you're mostly driving on asphalt, budget for replacement wheels.

The remote is functional but feels cheap. It's lightweight, the steering wheel has a slightly loose feel, and it lacks a phone holder — which matters because the truck has a built-in camera. Yes, this thing records 720p video to a microSD card, but you can't view the live feed on your phone without holding the remote in one hand and your phone in the other. The camera quality is fine for novelty footage, not for serious FPV driving.

Battery charging also takes about 2.5 hours per pack via the included USB charger. Plan your charging schedule if you're heading out for a long session.

### Who Should Buy This

- **Parents of kids 8–14**: This is the sweet spot. It's fast enough to be thrilling, durable enough to survive a kid's driving style, and the two batteries mean less "can we go home now?" moments.
- **RC beginners**: If you've never owned a hobby-grade truck and want to test the waters without dropping $200+, this is a solid entry point.
- **Anyone who drives on dirt or grass**: This is where the truck shines. On loose surfaces, the tires grip well and the suspension eats up bumps.

Skip it if you're a serious hobbyist — you'll outgrow it within months — or if you plan to drive mostly on pavement and don't want to immediately buy replacement tires.

### FAQ

**How fast does it actually go?**  
Realistically, 25–30 mph on flat, smooth surfaces. On grass or gravel, expect closer to 20 mph. It's fast for a toy-grade truck, but it won't keep up with a $300-plus hobby-grade model.

**How long does the battery last?**  
Each battery gives roughly 15–18 minutes of continuous driving. With both packs, you'll get 30–35 minutes total. Recharging takes about 2.5 hours per battery.

**Is it suitable for kids?**  
Yes, but with supervision. The top speed is genuinely fast — younger kids (under 8) may struggle with control. For kids 8 and up, it's manageable, especially with the speed settings on the remote (there are three modes: low, medium, high).

### The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely fast, genuinely tough RC truck that won't break the bank. The brushless motor, dual batteries, and metal drivetrain make it a standout at this price.

**Skip it if** you're a serious hobbyist, or if you'll mostly drive on pavement and don't want to deal with tire wear.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The DEERC Fast RC Truck delivers real performance and real durability, with minor flaws (tires, remote quality) that are easy to work around. For the money, it's one of the best value RC trucks you can buy right now.
