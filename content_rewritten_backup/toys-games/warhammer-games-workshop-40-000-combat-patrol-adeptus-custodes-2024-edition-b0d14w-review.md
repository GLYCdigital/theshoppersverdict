**The Verdict: A Golden Price Tag for the Emperor’s Finest**

You are looking at the Adeptus Custodes Combat Patrol, and here is the unvarnished truth: this box is the single best value entry point into Warhammer 40,000 that Games Workshop currently sells. If you have been eyeing the golden boys of the Imperium, this kit is the definitive starting line. But "value" in the Warhammer world is relative, and this box has a few sharp edges you need to know about before you swipe your card.

**What’s in the Box (The Golden Haul)**

You are not getting a horde of plastic; you are getting a surgical strike force. The box contains five multi-part plastic Adeptus Custodes Custodian Guard, a single Shield-Captain, and the star of the show: a Venerable Contemptor Dreadnought. That is roughly 600 points of elite infantry, which is a massive chunk of a standard 1,000-point army.

As shown in the product image, the sprues are packed with ornate detail—filigree, aquilas, and power weapon options. You also get a transfer sheet with the various shield host markings. Do not expect a rulebook or dice; this is a pure model kit. You get the plastic, the bases, and the decals. That’s it.

**Build Quality and Assembly**

This is where your patience is tested. The Custodes are gorgeous, but they are not beginner-friendly. The fit is tight, and the mold lines are visible on the curved surfaces of the power armor. You will need a hobby knife, plastic glue, and a steady hand. The Dreadnought is a chunky, satisfying build with a lot of poseable joints, but the legs can be tricky to align perfectly with the torso.

Real talk: this kit is a test of your assembly skills. If you are a veteran hobbyist, you will enjoy the detail. If you are new to the hobby, budget a solid weekend to build this without rushing. The payoff is a stunning centerpiece unit, but the process is not instant gratification.

**Performance on the Tabletop**

In the current edition, the Custodes are a "elite glass cannon" army. They hit like a truck but die to concentrated fire. The Combat Patrol box gives you a surprisingly balanced force: the Shield-Captain is a beatstick leader, the Guard can be built with shields for durability or spears for damage, and the Dreadnought provides the heavy firepower you desperately need.

The 4.7/5 rating (with 235 ratings) reflects that players consistently praise the tactical flexibility. One buyer noted, *"The Dreadnought alone is worth the price; it wrecks face in smaller games."* That sentiment is echoed across the reviews—the box is seen as a "must-buy" for starting a Custodes army.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let’s be blunt about the downsides. First, you only get five infantry models. If you want a full 2,000-point army, you will need to buy two or three of these boxes, which gets expensive fast. Second, the instructions can be vague on the wargear options. The kit allows you to build different weapon loadouts, but the manual doesn't clearly explain the points cost of each choice, which can trip up new players.

Third, the transfer sheet is fiddly. The decals are small and require Micro Sol/Set solution to look good. If you skip that step, they will peel off and look terrible. Finally, the price per model is high. You are paying for quality, not quantity. If you are on a strict budget, this is a luxury purchase.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The Competitive Player:** You need the Dreadnought for your list. This box is a cost-effective way to get one, even if you already have infantry.
- **The Lore Buff:** If you love the "10,000" and their tragic backstory, this kit is a love letter to the faction. The detail is unmatched.
- **The Collector:** Even if you never play, this is a display piece that will look incredible on a shelf.

**Skip It If:**

- You are a complete beginner looking for an easy first build. Start with a Space Marine or Ork box to learn the basics.
- You are on a tight budget and want the most models for your money. This is the opposite of a horde box.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this box enough to play a game of Combat Patrol?**
A: Yes. The Combat Patrol game mode is designed to be played with just this box. It is legal and balanced for that format.

**Q: Can I use the Dreadnought in a full 40k army?**
A: Absolutely. The Venerable Contemptor Dreadnought is a valid Heavy Support choice for a standard Adeptus Custodes army.

**Q: Is this the 10th Edition version?**
A: Yes, this box contains the current 10th Edition datasheet cards and is compatible with the latest rules index.

**The Final Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You are committed to the Custodes and want a strong, flexible core for a new army. The Dreadnought is a centerpiece, and the infantry are versatile.

**Skip it if:** You are a budget-conscious gamer or a novice builder. The cost-per-model and assembly difficulty are real hurdles.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting half a star for the finicky assembly and the lack of a proper instruction booklet for wargear options. Otherwise, this is a golden masterpiece.
