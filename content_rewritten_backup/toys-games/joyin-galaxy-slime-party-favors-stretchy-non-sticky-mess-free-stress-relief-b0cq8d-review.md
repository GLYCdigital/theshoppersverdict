**Performance and build quality**  

The formula is the star. It stretches easily to about three times its original length without snapping, and it holds its shape when you roll it into a ball. The "non-sticky" claim is legitimate — after five minutes of play, I pressed it against a paper towel and got no residue. On a clean counter, it left a faint shimmer from the glitter but wiped off with a dry cloth. No oil, no stickiness, no film.

The slime is soft and squishy, not the dense, rubbery kind that tires little hands. It also recovers well from being flattened — a quick knead brings it back to life. One thing to note: the glitter does transfer slightly to fingers, but it washes off with soap and water. Expect some sparkle dust on the playmat.

**Where it falls short**  

The containers are small. At 2 ounces each, a heavy-handed 8-year-old can work through one in two 20-minute sessions before it starts to dry out. The scent is mild — a faint fruity aroma — but it's clearly artificial. If you or your kid are sensitive to synthetic fragrances, this could be a dealbreaker. The glitter, while pretty, is the one mess factor that persists; it gets on hands and requires a quick rinse. Also, if you're buying for under-3s, skip it — the container lid is a choking hazard and the slime is not taste-safe.
