Let's be honest: most mirror cubes are either cheap trash that locks up or overpriced display pieces you're afraid to touch. The HELLOCUBE Cyclone Boys Magnetic Reflective Mirror sits in a rare middle ground — it's a functional puzzle that also happens to look stunning on a shelf. After 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, the consensus is clear: this is the mirror cube to buy if you want one that actually performs.

## What's in the Box

You get the cube itself, factory-lubed and tensioned. No tools, no extra stickers, no nonsense. The mirrored surface is applied as a proper reflective coating on the plastic itself, not a peel-and-stick overlay. That matters — cheap mirror cubes develop peeling edges within weeks. This one's built to last.

## Performance and Build Quality

The magnetic system is the star here. Each turn snaps into place with a satisfying click that tells you exactly when a layer is aligned. This isn't just gimmick — it genuinely helps with the shape-shifting nature of mirror cubes, where you're solving by piece height and width rather than color.

Turning quality is smooth but not buttery-loose. It has a controlled, slightly damp feel that suits the puzzle's character. Corner cutting is decent for a mirror cube — about 30-40 degrees — which is more than adequate given the irregular shapes. You won't be setting speed records, but you'll never fight the cube either.

The 3x3x3 mechanism underneath is standard Cyclone Boys quality, which means reliable and consistent. It's not a flagship speed cube, but it doesn't need to be. This is a puzzle you solve with patience and spatial reasoning, not finger tricks.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The reflective finish dominates the feedback. One reviewer noted it's "the only cube I own that gets compliments from non-cubers," and that tracks — the mirror effect is genuinely striking, catching light from every angle. Multiple buyers mention it arrived "perfectly lubed and ready to solve" with zero break-in period.

The magnetic feel gets consistent love too. "The magnets make it feel premium," wrote one buyer, "like a much more expensive cube." For the price point, the build quality punches well above its weight class.

## Where It Falls Short

The mirror finish is a fingerprint magnet. You'll be polishing it after every few solves, and the reflective surface shows every smudge, dust particle, and skin oil. Keep a microfiber cloth nearby.

The magnets, while satisfying, are set to a medium strength that won't satisfy hardcore speedcubers. If you're looking for a cube to push your TPS limits, this isn't it. The shape-shifting nature of mirror cubes inherently limits speed, and this one doesn't break that rule.

A few buyers noted the stickers (or rather, the reflective coating) can show minor scratches if you're rough with it. It's not fragile, but it's not indestructible either. Treat it with reasonable care.
