Let's be blunt: the PS5 is a massive console. When Sony shipped it, the included plastic base did the bare minimum to keep it upright — and plenty of owners have watched their $500 machine wobble dangerously close to a desk edge. The **PlayStation Vertical Stand for PS5** is Sony's official fix. At 4.7 stars from 235 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. But is it worth your money when a $15 third-party stand exists? Let's dig in.

## What You Actually Get

Sony keeps it simple here. The box contains the stand itself — a sturdy, curved ABS plastic base that matches the PS5's design language — plus one metal screw and a small rubber cap for the screw hole when not in use. No tools needed beyond your fingers. The stand measures roughly 10.5 inches deep and 6.5 inches wide, giving the console a stable footprint that's wider than the console itself. That extra width is exactly what prevents the top-heavy PS5 from tipping.

The installation takes about 30 seconds. You line up the stand's center post with the screw hole on the console's underside, hand-tighten the screw, and you're done. The screw is the key difference here — it physically locks the console to the stand, so there's zero play even if you bump the desk.

## Performance and Build Quality

The stand is made of the same matte-finish ABS plastic as the console itself, so it blends in seamlessly. It's not rubber-coated, but the bottom has four small rubber feet that grip your surface well. On a hardwood or laminate desk, the PS5 feels rock-solid in this stand. On carpet, the rubber feet still bite reasonably well.

The real benefit is airflow. Laying the PS5 flat puts the intake vents closer to whatever surface it's on. This stand lifts the console about an inch, creating a clean gap underneath. One reviewer summed it up: *"My PS5 fan noise dropped noticeably after I switched to this vertical stand. It's quieter and runs cooler, especially during long sessions of demanding games."* Another noted, *"The screw makes all the difference. I had a cheap third-party stand before and the console slid around. This one doesn't move at all."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. First, this **only fits the original PS5** — both the disc and digital editions. If you own the PS5 Slim or the Pro, this won't fit. Sony made it specific to the launch model's dimensions, and the screw hole positioning is different on newer hardware.

Second, the screw is easy to misplace. It's small, and if you're someone who moves their console between rooms, you'll be unscrewing and re-screwing each time. Lose it, and the stand becomes a friction-fit tray that's far less secure. A few Amazon reviewers have mentioned this exact issue, with one writing, *"Great stand, but I wish Sony included a spare screw. I dropped mine behind the desk and had to buy a replacement kit."*

Third, it's a one-trick pony. No extra USB ports, no headphone holder, no game storage. It's purely a stability and cooling accessory. If you want a multi-function stand, look elsewhere.

## Who Should Buy This

- **New PS5 owners** who want to set up their console vertically from day one and skip the included base entirely
- **People with pets or kids** — the screw-lock design prevents accidental knocks from tipping your console
- **Anyone with carpet** who wants better airflow and lower fan noise
- **Collectors** who want the official accessory that matches perfectly, not a third-party plastic knockoff

Skip it if you have a PS5 Slim or Pro, or if you're fine laying your console flat — there's genuinely no benefit to vertical orientation for those users.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this stand work with the PS5 Slim or PS5 Pro?**
No. It's designed only for the original PS5 models (both disc and digital). Sony sells separate stands for the Slim and Pro.

**Q: Will this damage my console over time?**
No. The screw threads into a metal insert on the console's underside — it's the same mounting point Sony designed for the included base. It's safe for long-term vertical use.

**Q: Does the stand reduce fan noise?**
For many users, yes. Vertical orientation creates better airflow around the intake vents, which can lower fan speed and noise during heavy gaming sessions. Results vary by room temperature and airflow.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if** you want the most stable, official way to stand your PS5 vertically — especially if you've dealt with a wobbly third-party stand or nervous moments with pets and kids nearby. The screw-lock design alone justifies the price over cheaper alternatives.

**Skip it if** you own a newer PS5 model, never plan to stand your console vertically, or just want a cheap stand — in that case, a basic friction-fit model under $15 will do the job, though you'll lose the security of the screw.

The PlayStation Vertical Stand is a simple, well-executed accessory that does exactly what it promises. It's not exciting, but it's the difference between a console that sits on your desk and one that stays there. For the peace of mind alone, it's worth the cost.
