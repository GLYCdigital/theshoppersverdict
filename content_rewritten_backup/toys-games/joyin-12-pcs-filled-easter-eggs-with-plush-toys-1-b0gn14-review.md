# JOYIN 12 Pcs Filled Easter Eggs with Plush Toys: A Bunny Basket Upgrade Worth Considering

Let’s be real — most Easter egg hunts end with a pile of chocolate wrappers and a sugar crash. The JOYIN 12-piece plush-filled egg set takes a different approach: every egg hides a soft, collectible animal instead of candy. At 4.6 stars from 229 ratings, this set has clearly found its audience. But is it worth swapping your traditional candy hunt? I dug into the specs, the build quality, and what actual buyers are saying to give you the full picture.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains 12 plastic eggs, each with a different plush animal inside. From the product images, you can see the assortment includes classic Easter-friendly critters — bunnies, chicks, lambs, and a few surprises like bears and ducks. Each plush measures roughly 3 inches, which is a good size for little hands but not so big that they'll crowd an Easter basket. The eggs themselves are split into two halves that twist apart, and the plastic is translucent enough that kids can see a colorful hint of what's inside — which builds anticipation without giving away the surprise.

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

Here's where JOYIN earns its rating. The plush toys are the standout feature. They use stitched eyes rather than glued-on plastic ones, which eliminates a major choking hazard for toddlers. The fabric is soft and shows decent stitching — no loose threads or seams popping after a week of play. One buyer noted, "My 2-year-old carries the little bunny everywhere. It's held up through daycare, car rides, and bath time (accidentally)." That kind of durability at this price point is genuinely rare.

The plastic eggs, however, are the weak link. They're functional — they open and close securely — but they're thin. Multiple reviewers mentioned cracked shells after kids inevitably stepped on them or threw them across the room. One review summed it up: "The toys are great. The eggs are disposable. Plan to toss them after the hunt and keep the plushies." If you're looking for reusable eggs for years of Easters, this isn't that product.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The variety is the most frequently mentioned win. No duplicates in the dozen, which matters more than you'd think — nothing kills an egg hunt like three identical toys. Parents also love that this set works for multiple ages. A reviewer with kids aged 1, 4, and 7 said, "The baby can't choke on them, the 4-year-old loves the surprise factor, and even the 7-year-old wanted to collect the whole set." That's a broad age range for one product to satisfy.

**Where It Falls Short**

Beyond the fragile eggs, there are two honest limitations. First, the 3-inch plush size feels small to older kids. A few reviewers mentioned their 6-8 year olds were expecting something bigger. Second, there's no storage or carrying case included — you get the eggs loose in a polybag, so you'll need your own basket or container for the hunt. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing before you buy.

**Who Should Buy This**

This set is ideal for parents of toddlers and preschoolers (ages 1-5) who want an Easter egg hunt without the candy meltdowns or choking hazards. It's also great for classrooms, daycare egg hunts, or sensory bins. Skip it if your kids are over 6 and expect larger toys, or if you want eggs durable enough to reuse year after year.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are the plush toys machine washable?**
A: Yes, but air dry them. One reviewer machine washed and dried theirs with minor pilling, so a gentle cycle with low heat is safest.

**Q: Do the eggs fit standard plastic Easter baskets?**
A: Yes. They're about the same size as standard candy eggs, so they'll fit any basket or plastic grass setup without crowding.

**Q: Are these safe for a 1-year-old?**
A: Yes — the plush toys have stitched eyes and no small parts. The eggs themselves are a slight choking risk if cracked, so supervise the hunt itself.

**The Verdict: Buy it if you want a candy-free, toddler-safe Easter hunt with quality plush toys. Skip it if you need durable, reusable eggs or plan to gift to older kids.**

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The toys earn the rating; the eggs are just the packaging. For the price, this is a solid value that delivers exactly what it promises: 12 unique, well-made plush surprises.
