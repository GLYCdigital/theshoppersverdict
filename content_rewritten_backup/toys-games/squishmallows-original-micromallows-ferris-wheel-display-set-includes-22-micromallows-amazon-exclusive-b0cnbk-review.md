Let's be blunt: if you're buying this for a child who just wants *more* Squishmallows to cuddle, you might be missing the point. This set is a collector's display piece first, a toy second — and that's exactly what makes it brilliant.

**What's Actually in the Box**

The Amazon-exclusive Squishmallows Original Micromallows Ferris Wheel Display Set delivers 22 individually wrapped 5-inch Micromallows plush — that's a full collection, not blind bags. You know exactly what you're getting, which eliminates the disappointment of duplicates. The set includes a mix of fan favorites and newer characters, all in the classic Squishmallow squishy texture.

The Ferris wheel display itself is the centerpiece. It stands roughly 15 inches tall when assembled and features 12 gondola seats. Each seat holds two Micromallows snugly — the plush are sized to fit without falling out even when the wheel spins. The base is weighted enough that the whole thing doesn't tip over during play, though it's not what I'd call heavy-duty plastic.

**Building the Thing: Real Talk**

Assembly is the biggest hurdle. The wheel spokes and gondola attachments require careful alignment — I'd budget 20-30 minutes with a child helping, less if you're doing it solo. The instructions are mostly diagrams with minimal text, so adults will need to supervise. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"My 8-year-old couldn't do it alone. We did it together in about 15 minutes — not bad, but don't expect instant gratification."*

Once assembled, the wheel spins smoothly on its axle. It's not motorized — you rotate it manually — but for display purposes, that's fine. The whole unit sits on a flat, stable base, and the gondolas stay upright as the wheel turns, which is a nice engineering touch for a toy at this price point.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The value proposition is the standout. Getting 22 full-size Micromallows (these aren't the tiny 2-inch ones) for roughly the price of 8-10 individual blind bags makes this set a smart buy for collectors completing their lineup. Parents also love that it doubles as storage — the wheel keeps the plush organized and off the floor.

The character selection gets consistent praise too. One verified purchase noted: *"Perfect variety — my daughter got her favorite (Cam the Cat) plus 21 others she didn't have. No repeats in our box."* The 5-inch size is ideal for small hands and fits on standard bookshelves without overwhelming the space.

**Where It Falls Short**

The plastic display feels a bit thin in places, especially the gondola clips. If a child yanks a plush out roughly, the clip can stress-crack. It's not fragile — but it's not indestructible either. Treat it as a display piece, not a rough-play toy.

Also, if you already collect Squishmallows, you'll likely own some of these characters from other sets. The 22 included aren't all exclusive — several are common in other Micromallows packs. For a first-time buyer, this is a non-issue. For veteran collectors, check the character list before ordering to avoid duplicates.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Collectors** completing their Micromallows lineup — this is the most efficient way to bulk up your stash.
- **Parents of Squishmallows-obsessed kids** (ages 5-10) who want a display solution that keeps the collection contained.
- **Gift-givers** looking for a memorable, visually impressive present — the assembled wheel looks fantastic on a shelf.

Skip it if you're looking for a cuddly, throw-around toy. These are display pieces first. The plush are soft, but the wheel is meant to be seen, not roughhoused.

A: Yes — same polyester fill, same signature squishiness. The 5-inch size is just scaled down. They're machine washable on gentle cycle, though the wheel display is not.

A: Not currently. If a gondola clip breaks, you'd need to contact Kellytoy customer service directly. The base and wheel structure are sturdy enough that this shouldn't be a common issue, but it's worth noting.

**Q: How many are actually exclusive to this set?**
A: Based on community reports, roughly half the 22 characters are exclusive or hard to find elsewhere. The rest are common in other Micromallows collections.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a complete Micromallows collection with a functional display piece that looks great on a shelf. The 4.7-star rating from 235 buyers holds up — this is a well-executed collector's set.
