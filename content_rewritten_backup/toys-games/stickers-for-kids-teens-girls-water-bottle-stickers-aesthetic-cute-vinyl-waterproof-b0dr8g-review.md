Third, the vinyl is durable but not indestructible. A reviewer mentioned that one sticker started showing wear after aggressive scrubbing with a rough sponge. Use a soft cloth and you'll be fine.

**Q: Will they stick to textured bottles?**
A: No. Smooth surfaces only. Textured or rubberized bottles will cause peeling within days. Clean the surface with rubbing alcohol before applying for best results.
