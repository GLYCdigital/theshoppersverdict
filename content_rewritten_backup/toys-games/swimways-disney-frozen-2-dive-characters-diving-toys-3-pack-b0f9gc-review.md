These aren't just Frozen-branded pool junk. The SwimWays Disney Frozen 2 Dive Characters (3-pack) actually function as proper diving toys — weighted, sinkable, and designed to stand upright on the pool floor. If you're tired of cheap dive rings that float sideways or bob back up mid-game, these Arendelle characters solve a real problem.

**What's in the package**

You get three sealed vinyl figures — Elsa, Anna, and Olaf — each standing about 5 inches tall. They arrive with a small hang tag for retail display, but no storage bag or mesh carry pouch. That's a minor miss, since three loose figures will rattle around in a pool bag. The characters are pre-weighted at the base, so there's no assembly, no sand to add, no inflation needed. Open the package, toss them in the water, done.

**How they actually perform**

Drop one from poolside and it sinks fast — no slow flutter, no drifting mid-descent. The weight distribution is the standout engineering here. Elsa and Anna land base-first and stay upright, which makes them feel like actual "characters" underwater rather than lumps of plastic. Olaf, being rounder, tends to roll slightly on contact but still stays put.

The vinyl material is thicker than the dollar-store dive toys I've tested. The glossy print survived a full summer of weekly pool use — no peeling at the seams, no color bleeding, no chlorine clouding. The seal around the weighted base is solid; none of the three developed leaks after prolonged submersion.

**What buyers consistently love**

The 4.7-star average across 235 ratings is backed by genuine enthusiasm. One parent noted, "My daughter spends an hour a day diving for Olaf — it's the only pool toy that keeps her actually swimming instead of splashing." Another reviewer highlighted the durability angle: "We've had these for two summers and they still look brand new. The print hasn't faded at all." Multiple buyers mentioned the "stand upright" feature as the differentiator that makes retrieval a real game rather than a free-for-all.

**Where they fall short**

The size is the honest tradeoff. At 5 inches, these are compact. In a murky or oversized pool, kids under six will struggle to spot them on the bottom. Several reviews mention losing track of Elsa for days at a time. Also, the sealed design is a double-edged sword — if the vinyl ever punctures (pool edges, rough decking), water gets inside and there's no drain plug. It becomes a permanent sinker. No repair path.

**Who should buy these**

- **Parents of kids 6-12** who want a pool toy that teaches breath control and underwater comfort without feeling like a lesson.
- **Frozen fans** who are past bath toys but still want themed play in the pool — the character recognition is strong enough to keep them engaged.
- **Families with small, clear pools** where a 5-inch target is easy to spot.

**Skip them if** your child is under 5 (too small to track), you have a huge cloudy pool, or you need a toy that can handle being thrown onto concrete.

**FAQ**

**Q: How deep do they sink?**
They drop to the bottom of any standard residential pool (up to 8-10 feet) and stay there. No buoyancy issues.

**Q: Are these safe for toddlers?**
The figures are solid vinyl with no small parts, but the 5-inch size is a choking hazard for children under 3. Recommended for ages 4+.

**Q: Do they float at all?**
No. They're designed to sink immediately. If you want floating Frozen toys, this isn't the set.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a durable, properly weighted diving toy that holds a kid's attention for an entire pool season. **Skip it if** you need something bigger, or you're shopping for a toddler.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — the size is the only real knock. Performance and build quality punch well above the price point.
