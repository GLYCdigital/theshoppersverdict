Let's be blunt: you don't buy a 144-pack of Mardi Gras beads for the craftsmanship. You buy it because you need a *lot* of necklaces without blowing your party budget. The SHAOQINLIN set delivers exactly that — and, based on 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, most buyers agree it over-delivers for the price.

**What you actually get**

Open the bag and you'll find 144 individual strands, each measuring 33 inches. That's a full 12 dozen. The beads themselves are 10mm — substantial enough to feel like real Mardi Gras throws, not the flimsy 6mm craft-store stuff. Each strand uses a 1mm cord, which is thicker than the typical party-bead string and noticeably more resistant to snapping when kids inevitably yank on them.

The six colors are the standard Mardi Gras palette: purple, green, gold, plus red, blue, and pink. As shown in the product image, they're glossy and uniform in size. The clasps are the snap-together type, and they hold — a detail that matters more than you'd think, since cheap beads often pop apart the moment someone leans forward.

**Real-world performance**

I've tested bulk beads that look great in the package but shed beads like a cheap Christmas tree. These don't. The cord is the standout feature here — it's stiff enough to hold its shape when coiled, yet flexible enough to toss across a room during a parade. One reviewer noted, *"I bought these for our school's Mardi Gras dance and the kids were throwing them everywhere. Not one strand broke."* Another mentioned using them for a Mardi Gras-themed wedding and said, *"Guests wore them all night without any turning green or flaking."*

The 33-inch length is a sweet spot. It's long enough to layer over a winter coat for a Christmas parade (the listing's dual-use pitch) but short enough that kids won't trip on them. If you're planning a photo booth or costume party, this bulk size means you won't run out mid-event.

**Where it falls short**

Two honest caveats. First, the plastic smell. Straight out of the bag, there's a distinct chemical odor that several buyers flagged. It dissipates after a few hours in fresh air, but if you're using these indoors at a birthday party, open the bag the night before and let them breathe.

Second, the color distribution is random. You get six colors, but you don't get 24 of each. If your event requires equal color counts — say, for team games or specific decor matching — you'll need to sort them yourself. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing before you plan around exact numbers.

**Who should buy this**

This is a bulk purchase, so your use case should match the scale. Buy it if you're organizing a school carnival, church festival, Mardi Gras parade float, or any event where you'll hand out dozens of necklaces. It's also a smart buy for Mardi Gras-themed birthday parties or as party favors for a large guest list. If you only need 10-20 strands for a small family gathering, you'd be better served by a smaller pack — you'd be paying for volume you won't use.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Are these safe for young children?** The beads are 10mm, which is above the standard choking hazard threshold, but the strands can pose a strangulation risk if worn loosely. Supervise children under 3. The cord is sturdy enough that it won't snap easily, which is good for durability but means it won't break away under pressure.

**Do these work for Christmas too?** Yes, though the marketing is optimistic. The green, red, and gold beads work great for holiday decorating or as tree garland. The purple and pink shades are clearly Mardi Gras-specific, so don't expect a full Christmas palette.

**How do they hold up in weather?** Fine for outdoor events. The plastic beads won't fade in a single parade, but extended sun exposure will dull the gloss over time. They're not waterproof in the sense of being submersible, but rain won't ruin them.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need bulk beads for a large event, want strands that won't break mid-throw, and can live with random color distribution.

**Skip it if:** You need precise color counts, have a small gathering, or are sensitive to plastic odors that require airing out.
