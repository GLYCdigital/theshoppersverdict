## What You're Actually Getting

This is a bulk pack, so don't expect a fancy tin. You get 240 individually wrapped colored pencils in 12 assorted colors — that's 20 pencils per color. Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink, brown, black, and a few others cover the standard classroom palette. Each pencil is pre-sharpened, which sounds minor until you've spent 15 minutes on the first day of school with a manual sharpener and a line of impatient six-year-olds.

The pencils are standard 7-inch length with hexagonal barrels — they won't roll off desks, which is a small but meaningful design choice. The cores are described as break-resistant, and based on the material specs, they use a reinforced lead formula rather than the fragile chalky cores you find in dollar-store brands.

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty requires noting the tradeoffs. The wood casing is standard cedar but not premium — if you use an electric sharpener set to aggressive, you may see some splintering. Manual sharpeners work fine, but tell kids not to crank it.

The pigment, while vibrant, isn't blendable. If you're looking for art-class quality where students learn shading and gradient techniques, these aren't the tool. They're coloring pencils, not drawing pencils — an important distinction.

Also, there's no storage container. The 240 pencils arrive in a bulk box, and once you distribute them, you need your own solution for the extras. A simple plastic bin or pencil case works, but it's an additional purchase to factor in.

## Who Should Buy These

This is a teacher's supply purchase, plain and simple. If you're running a classroom, Sunday school, or scout troop that needs a reliable, affordable pencil for every kid, this is the right call.
