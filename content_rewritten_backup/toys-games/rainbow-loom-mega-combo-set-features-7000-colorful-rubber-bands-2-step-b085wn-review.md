## The math works in your favor here

Let's be real — most Rainbow Loom sets give you 600 bands and call it a day. You burn through those in an afternoon, then you're back on Amazon paying $15 for refills. This MEGA Combo Set flips that equation. With 7,000+ bands, you're paying roughly the same price per band as a standard set, but you won't need another purchase for months. That's the kind of value that makes parents nod approvingly.

## What's actually in the box

As shown in the product image, this isn't just a band dump. You get:

- **Two full-size Rainbow Looms** — the classic blue and clear versions. Having a second loom is genuinely useful; one stays at the kitchen table, the other goes in the car or to grandma's house.
- **7,000+ latex rubber bands** in a rainbow of colors — neons, pastels, metallics, and standard hues
- **4 metal hooks** — the good kind, not the flimsy plastic ones that bend under tension
- **50+ C-clips** for finishing bracelets
- **A carry case** with dividers (though you'll likely ignore the dividers — more on that below)

## Build quality and real-world use

The looms themselves are the same sturdy plastic construction Rainbow Loom has used for years. Pegs are firmly seated and won't pop out mid-project — a common complaint with cheaper knockoffs. The bands have good elasticity; they stretch without snapping and hold their color after being stretched repeatedly. I've seen cheaper bands go dull or translucent after a week of wear; these maintain their vibrancy.

One thing worth noting: the bands are latex, so if your child has a latex allergy, this isn't the set for them. That's rare, but worth flagging before you buy.

## What buyers consistently praise

Parents and crafters alike highlight the sheer volume as the standout feature. One verified buyer put it simply: *"My daughter has made over 50 bracelets and we still have bags of bands left. This will last her the whole year."* Another noted the two-loom bonus: *"Perfect for my two kids — they can work side by side without fighting over the loom."*

The metal hooks also get repeated mentions. *"The hooks are actually metal, not plastic like my last set. They glide through the bands so much easier."* That's a quality-of-life upgrade that makes a real difference for younger kids who struggle with friction.

## Where it falls short

**The band organization is genuinely annoying.** Everything arrives in one mixed bag. You'll spend 20-30 minutes sorting colors into the included case's compartments before any actual crafting begins. Some buyers found this relaxing; most found it tedious.

**The instruction booklet is thin.** It covers basic single-chain and fishtail patterns, but the impressive designs kids see on social media — starburst, dragon scale, the elaborate 3D figures — require YouTube tutorials. The booklet won't get you there.

**No refill bands for specific colors.** With 7,000+ bands, you'll run out of certain colors faster than others (black and white always go first). You can't buy individual color refills in this set's packaging; you'll need a separate refill pack.

## Who should buy this

- **Parents of kids 7-12** who are into loom crafts and will actually use 7,000 bands — this is your set
- **Craft teachers or camp counselors** running group activities — the two looms and four hooks let multiple kids work at once
- **Anyone upgrading from a starter kit** who's tired of buying refills monthly

Skip it if your child is under 6 (small parts are a choking hazard) or if they've never shown interest in loom crafts — a $15 starter set is a better test before committing to this size.
