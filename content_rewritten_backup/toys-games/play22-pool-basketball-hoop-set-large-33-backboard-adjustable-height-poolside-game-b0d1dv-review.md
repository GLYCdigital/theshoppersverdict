**Is this the pool toy that actually gets used, or the one that rusts in the shed by July?**

Here's the thing about pool basketball hoops: most are flimsy plastic junk that wobble the moment a wet 10-year-old throws a shoulder into them. The Play22 set, with its oversized 33-inch backboard and adjustable height pole, is trying to be the exception. After digging through 200 ratings and the design specs, here's what actually matters.

**What you're actually getting**

The package contains the backboard, rim, net, the two-piece adjustable pole, and all mounting hardware. The backboard is a full 33 inches across — that's meaningfully bigger than the 24-inch boards on most competitors. The pole adjusts from roughly 4 feet up to about 6.5 feet, which covers both a 6-year-old's layup line and a grown adult's dunk attempt.

Mounting is where you need to pay attention. This is not a clamp-on unit. You're drilling the base plate into your pool deck with the included screws. That's a permanent commitment — or at least a "you'll have deck holes if you remove it" commitment. For above-ground pools, the mounting bracket slides over the pool wall, which works but requires a second person to hold things steady while you tighten.

**How it performs in the water**

The large backboard is the standout feature here. As one buyer put it, "The bigger board makes a huge difference — shots that would bounce off a smaller one just drop in." That's the physics working in your favor: more surface area means more accidental makes, which means less arguing about who's winning.

The adjustable height actually works smoothly. The twist-lock collar holds firm during play — one reviewer noted "it didn't slip once even with three kids hanging on the rim." That's the kind of real-world stress test that matters.

**Where it falls short**

The ball situation is annoying. There's no ball included, and the one Play22 sells separately is a mini basketball that's too light for proper shots. Buyers consistently recommend grabbing a standard pool basketball separately — factor that into your total cost.

Stability on uneven surfaces is also a concern. If your pool deck isn't perfectly level, the base plate can rock slightly. Several reviews mentioned needing shims or washers to get it flush. It's fixable, but it's an extra trip to the hardware store you shouldn't have to make.

**Who should buy this**

- **Above-ground pool owners** who want a hoop that actually mounts securely to the pool frame
- **Families with kids of different ages** — the adjustable height means the 5-year-old and the 13-year-old both get a fair shot
- **Anyone who's tired of cheap hoops that tip over** — the drilled base isn't portable, but it's rock solid

**Skip it if** you have an in-ground pool with a tile deck you don't want to drill into, or if you want something you can take down and store each winter.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this fit all above-ground pools?**
A: The mounting bracket works with pools up to 54-inch walls. If your pool has a wider top rail (some oval pools do), check the bracket width before ordering.

**Q: Can I adjust the height without tools?**
A: Yes, the twist-lock collar loosens by hand. It takes about 30 seconds to change heights.

**Q: Will the screws rust?**
A: The included hardware is galvanized, but one reviewer reported surface rust after two seasons in a saltwater pool. Rinse the base after use if you're in a chlorine-heavy pool.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a substantial pool basketball setup that won't tip over during a heated game of HORSE. The large backboard is a genuine upgrade, and the adjustable height extends its usefulness across a wide age range.

**Skip it if** you're not willing to commit to drilling into your deck or if you want a ready-to-play set without buying a ball separately.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The size and stability make it a standout, but the missing ball and mounting commitment keep it from being a no-brainer purchase.
