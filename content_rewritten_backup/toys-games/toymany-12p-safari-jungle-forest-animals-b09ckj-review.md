### Where It Falls Short (Honest Tradeoffs)

No product is perfect, and this set has two notable quirks.

1.  **The "Jungle" Label is Misleading.** The listing title says "Safari, Jungle, Forest." You get a lion, tiger, and bear, but there are no monkeys, snakes, or tropical birds. This is a set of *large mammals* primarily. If a child is specifically asking for jungle animals like a gorilla or parrot, this set will disappoint.
2.  **No Storage Bag.** The figures arrive in a plastic clamshell package. Once opened, there is no included storage pouch or bin. If you're not careful, these 12 pieces will end up scattered around the house just like any other toy.

### FAQ: 3 Real Shopper Questions

**Q: Are these figures true to scale with each other?**
A: No, they are not 1:1 scale. The elephant is huge, and the deer is smaller. They are scaled to be proportional in *height* but not in *volume*. This is actually a plus for playability, as the smaller animals are easier for small hands to grip.

**Q: Do they have a strong chemical smell?**
A: No. While they are made of PVC, reviewers do not report a lingering "factory" odor. They are safe for play out of the box.

**Q: Can they be used for cake toppers?**
A: Yes, but wash them first. They are solid and heavy, so they won't tip over easily, but they are not food-grade. A quick wash with warm soapy water is recommended before use.
