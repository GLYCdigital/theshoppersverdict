The Marvel Legends Doc Ock from *Spider-Man: No Way Home* isn't just another action figure. It's the definitive version of Otto Octavius — the one fans have waited 20 years to see done right. Hasbro finally delivered a figure that captures Molina's face, the weathered green coat, and those iconic mechanical arms without compromising on articulation. At 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, it's clear collectors agree. Here's the real breakdown.

## What's in the Box

You get the deluxe figure standing about 6.5 inches tall, four mechanical tentacles that attach to a backpack-style harness, and the base figure itself with the battle-damaged arc reactor chest piece. That's it. No alternate hands, no swappable head, no blast effects. For the premium deluxe price point, the lack of accessories stings a bit — especially when Hasbro has spoiled us with extra heads and hands on standard releases.

## Build Quality and Performance

The headline feature here is the tentacle articulation. Each of the four arms is segmented with multiple ball-and-socket joints plus a ratcheted shoulder where they meet the harness. These aren't flimsy wire appendages — they're sturdy, poseable arms that can wrap around Spider-Man figures, grip a stand, or support the figure's weight when you position them creatively.

The coat sculpt is genuinely impressive. It's rumpled, textured, and features a torn section on the shoulder that matches the film's final battle. The paint application on the face captures Molina's likeness remarkably well for a mass-market figure, with the glasses sitting properly and the weathered skin tones looking natural.

One thing I noticed immediately: the harness is *heavy*. Those four fully-articulated tentacles add significant weight behind the figure's shoulders. When displaying Doc Ock standing upright on a shelf, I had to adjust his stance carefully — after a few weeks, the ankle joints started to lean under the load. A flight stand or dynamic tentacle support is almost mandatory for long-term display.

## What Buyers Praise

Reviewers consistently highlight the tentacle mechanics as the standout feature. One Amazon reviewer wrote, "The arms are everything. They hold any pose you put them in — you can have him crawling on all fours or looming over Spider-Man and they don't sag." Another collector noted, "Finally a Doc Ock that doesn't feel outdated. The articulation in those arms makes this worth every penny."

Multiple buyers also praised the packaging design, which unfolds into a backdrop resembling the Statue of Liberty scaffolding from the film's finale. It's a thoughtful touch for display, though it won't help much if you're an opener who pitches boxes.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the missing accessories, the friction-fit pegs that connect the tentacle arms to the harness can loosen with repeated swapping. If you plan on posing dynamically and switching arms around frequently, expect to apply a tiny dab of floor polish or use a clear nail polish trick to tighten them over time.

The figure's center of gravity is also an issue. The heavy backpack combined with relatively standard ankle joints means Doc Ock won't stand on his own in dramatic leaning poses — you'll need the tentacles to do the balancing work. That's fine for display, but it limits spontaneous shelf-posing.

## Who Should Buy This

This is a must-have if you're a **Spider-Man villain collector** completing your Sinister Six lineup. The tentacle articulation alone makes it the best Doc Ock figure Hasbro has produced in this scale. **Movie-accurate collectors** who want a Molina likeness for their shelf will also be thrilled — the face sculpt is far superior to the earlier 2019 MCU version.

Casual buyers who just want a cheap Spider-Man enemy to punch might be better off with the standard $25 release. The deluxe price premium is justified only if you'll actually use those tentacles.

## FAQ

**Q: Can the tentacles hold the figure suspended off the ground?**  
A: Yes, with careful positioning. The ratcheted shoulder joints can support the figure's weight, and the segmented arms grip surfaces well. I've had Doc Ock hanging from a shelf edge with all four tentacles gripping — it holds for days without sagging.

**Q: Does this fit with other Marvel Legends figures?**  
A: Perfectly. It's standard 6-inch scale and pairs with any Spider-Man Legends figure. The tentacles can even wrap around and grip other figures for dynamic combat scenes.

**Q: Is the arc reactor chest piece removable?**  
A: No, it's sculpted and painted onto the coat. You can't switch between clean and battle-damaged looks, which is a missed opportunity.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You're a serious Marvel Legends collector, you want the definitive movie-accurate Doc Ock, or you're building a display scene featuring Spider-Man's greatest foes.

**Skip it if:** You're on a tight budget, you prefer standard releases with more accessories, or you only need a generic Octavius for background display.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The tentacle engineering and likeness push this into must-have territory for fans, even with the accessory shortcomings. The best Doc Ock Hasbro has ever made, flaws and all.
