If you just dropped $450 on a Nintendo Switch 2, the last thing you want is to toss it loose into a backpack. The ProCase Carrying Case for Nintendo Switch 2 (2025) is purpose-built for the new hardware's larger dimensions — and with 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, it's clearly doing something right. The question is whether it earns a spot in your daily carry. I've dug through the specs, the customer feedback, and the real-world tradeoffs so you don't have to.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the hard-shell case itself — nothing more, nothing less. No screen protector, no thumb grips, no bonus cables. Inside, there's a molded cradle that grips the Switch 2 console snugly, a mesh pocket on the lid for cables or earbuds, and a flap with **24 game card slots**. That's the headline feature: a full library of physical games, organized and protected. There's also a small zippered pouch under the card flap for microSD cards, spare Joy-Con straps, or a charging cable.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The shell is a rigid EVA material — the same stuff used in most quality camera bags — with a smooth exterior that resists scuffs. The interior is lined with a soft microfiber that won't scratch the console's screen or Joy-Cons. As shown in the product image, the Switch 2 sits in a precise recessed cradle; the cutouts align with the analog sticks and shoulder buttons, meaning the console isn't pressing against anything when the case is closed and under pressure in a bag.

The zipper is the weak point on many budget cases, but ProCase uses a chunky, double-slider zipper that feels sturdy. It glides smoothly and doesn't catch on the interior fabric. The exterior strap is functional — you can slip it over a suitcase handle — but it's on the thin side. If you're planning to carry this case by the strap for extended periods, you'll feel the weight of a fully loaded Switch 2 digging into your hand.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The dominant theme in the 229 reviews is fit and finish. One verified buyer puts it plainly: *"Finally a case that actually fits the Switch 2 — not a stretched-out Switch 1 case. The console clicks in perfectly and the 24 game slots are a lifesaver for a physical collector."* Another reviewer notes, *"The hard shell gives me actual confidence throwing this in my carry-on. The interior is soft enough that I don't even use a screen protector."*

The 24-game capacity gets consistent shoutouts. Physical game collectors are the core audience here, and the card slots are snug — cards won't rattle around or fall out when you open the flap.

## Where It Falls Short

Honest assessment: this is a **console-and-cartridge case**, not a travel hub. There's no room for the Switch 2 dock, the AC adapter, or a Pro Controller. If you're planning to take your Switch 2 to a friend's house and hook it up to their TV, you'll need a separate bag for the dock and cables. That's not a flaw — it's a design choice — but it's worth knowing before you buy.

The strap, as mentioned, is thin. And the case is slightly bulky for a handheld-only solution. If you're the type who wants to slip a case into a jacket pocket, this isn't it. It's a proper protective shell, not a sleeve.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you're a physical game collector who wants your library on the go, or if you're looking for a robust, drop-proof case for daily commutes and travel. It's also a solid choice for parents who want a tough case that can survive being tossed into a kid's backpack.

**Skip it if** you're all-digital (the 24 game slots are wasted on you) or if you need a single case that carries the dock and accessories for TV play. For those needs, look at a larger travel organizer instead.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this fit the original Nintendo Switch or Switch OLED?**
A: No. ProCase designed this specifically for the Switch 2's larger dimensions. The original Switch and OLED are smaller and will slide around inside the cradle. Don't buy this for older models.

**Q: Can the Switch 2 stay in the case while playing?**
A: No. The console sits in a recessed cradle, so you need to remove it to play. This is a carry case, not a play-through shell.

**Q: Are the 24 game card slots actually usable, or are they too tight?**
A: They're snug but functional. Multiple reviewers confirm the cards fit securely and don't fall out when the flap is opened upside down.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want reliable, everyday protection for your Switch 2 with generous game storage. **Skip it if** you're digital-only or need a full travel kit with dock space. For the price and the 4.6-star consensus, this is the case to beat for the Switch 2 launch window.
