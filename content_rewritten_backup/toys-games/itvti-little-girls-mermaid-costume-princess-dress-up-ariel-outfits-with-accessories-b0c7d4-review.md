Let me be upfront: I've tested dozens of dress-up costumes over the years, and most are junk. Thin polyester, scratchy embellishments, accessories that break before the Halloween candy runs out. The iTVTi mermaid costume isn't that. But it's also not a $150 custom piece. Here's where it lands.

**What's actually in the box**

The set arrives with the main dress, a matching hair tiara, a glitter wand, and a jeweled necklace. The dress itself features a sequined shell bodice with an attached skirt of layered tulle in that classic Ariel teal-green gradient. The back closes with a hidden zipper — a genuinely thoughtful touch at this price point, since most competitors use flimsy hook-and-loop that fails within weeks.

The fabric blend is a polyester-spandex mix, which means the bodice stretches. That matters, because kids don't stand still for measurements. The tulle has a slight iridescent shimmer that photographs well — worth noting if you're buying for a birthday party or themed photo shoot.

**How it holds up in real use**

I put this through the wringer: daily wear for a week with a particularly energetic 6-year-old. The bodice kept its shape. No sequins fell off during normal play, though a few shed during the first two wears — normal for this style, and it stops after the initial break-in. The zipper functioned smoothly throughout, which surprised me given how many costumes in this range jam or catch fabric.

The skirt volume is the standout feature. It flares properly when she twirls, which — if you know any 4-to-8-year-olds — is essentially the entire point of a dress-up costume. The length hits around mid-calf on an average 6-year-old, giving enough coverage for active play without tripping hazards.

**What buyers consistently say**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews holds up when you dig into the feedback. One parent wrote, "My daughter refuses to take it off. The quality surprised me for the price — it's held up through three washings so far." Another noted, "The shell top looks way more expensive than it is. Strangers at the store asked where we got it."

The most common praise centers on the accessories actually being usable — the wand doesn't bend on first swing, the tiara stays on smaller heads without constant adjustment. That's rarer than it should be in this category.

**Where it falls short**

Sizing is the biggest complaint, and it's legitimate. Multiple reviews note the costume runs small. If your child is at the top of a size range, size up. A 5T fits more like a 4T; the 7-8 size fits closer to a slim 7. This isn't a dealbreaker, but it's the difference between a gift that fits and a return.

The tulle snags. Velcro from other costumes, rough playground equipment, even some car seats — it catches and pulls. Keep fabric scissors handy. And the sequins do shed lightly at first, which matters if you have a toddler who puts everything in her mouth or a carpet you're precious about.

**Who should buy this**

- Parents of girls aged 4-8 who want a quality Ariel costume without paying boutique prices
- Anyone buying for dress-up, not just Halloween — this holds up for daily play
- Gift-givers who want accessories included so they don't have to piece together a set

Skip it if your child is between sizes and you're not willing to size up, or if you need a costume for a one-time event and want something disposable-cheap — this is built for repeated use, and you'll pay slightly more for that.

**FAQ**

**Is this costume machine washable?**
Yes, but use cold water and air dry. The tulle will survive gentle cycles, but heat damages the sequins. Hand washing is safest if you want maximum longevity.

**What ages fit the size chart?**
The 3-4T runs small — think average 3-year-old. The 5-6 fits slim-to-average 5-year-olds. The 7-8 tends to fit through most of 7 but runs snug by 8. When in doubt, size up.

**Does the skirt have a built-in crinoline or is it just tulle?**
It's layered tulle only — no separate crinoline. The volume comes from multiple overlapping layers, which actually works better for washing and storage.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** your daughter wants a mermaid costume she'll wear for months, not one afternoon. The construction quality exceeds the price point, the accessories are genuinely usable, and the twirl factor is off the charts.

**Skip it if** you need something for a single event and want to spend less, or if your child is solidly at the top of a size range — the sizing quirk will frustrate you.

This is a solid 4.6/5. It's not perfect, but for the money, it's one of the better dress-up costumes I've tested. Your little mermaid will be happy.
