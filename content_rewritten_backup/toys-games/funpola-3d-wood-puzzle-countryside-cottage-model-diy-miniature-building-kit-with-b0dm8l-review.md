**What’s Actually in the Box**

You get 5 laser-cut plywood sheets, a fabric dust cover, sandpaper, and a small bag of hardware (screws, bolts, and a screwdriver). No glue is required—everything snaps or screws together. The finished cottage measures roughly 8.5 x 7 x 7 inches, which makes it a solid desk or shelf piece rather than a tiny trinket.

The wood is pre-cut with precision—tabs fit snugly, and the grain is clean. The kit includes a small LED light kit (batteries not included) that tucks into the attic, which is a nice touch for evening display.

---

**Build Experience: The Good, The Frustrating, The Sharp**

I’ll be straight: this is not a beginner’s kit. The instructions are icon-based diagrams, not step-by-step photos. If you’ve built IKEA furniture without crying, you’ll manage. If you haven’t, budget 4–6 hours and keep a pair of tweezers nearby.

The screw-together design is a genuine improvement over glue-based kits. You can disassemble and reposition pieces without ruining them—a lifesaver when you misalign a wall. The fabric dust cover is a thoughtful addition; it keeps the interior clean and adds a soft, homey look.

Where it falls short: the wood splinters at the edges if you’re too forceful when separating pieces. Sandpaper is included, but you’ll want a small file for tight corners. Also, the LED wiring is fiddly—routing the wire through the pre-drilled holes takes patience, and the switch is a bit stiff.

3. **No glue means some gaps.** The snap-fit design leaves hairline seams on the roof. If you’re a perfectionist, keep wood filler on hand.
4. **Price-to-size ratio.** Given the build time and detail, it’s fair value—but if you’re expecting a large statement piece, this is more of a cozy accent.
