## What's Actually in the Box

This isn't a single replacement part. It's a full refresh kit. Inside you get the replacement tires, wheel bearings, and swivel assembly — the three components that take the most abuse in any pressure-side cleaner. The kit is designed to fit the Polaris 280 and 380 models specifically, and because these are genuine Polaris parts (not aftermarket), the fitment is exact. No shims, no forcing, no returning mismatched parts.

## Performance: What 240 Buyers Are Actually Saying

The consensus from verified purchasers is consistent: this kit brings a tired cleaner back to life. One reviewer put it plainly: *"My Polaris 280 was barely moving across the pool floor. After replacing the tires and bearings, it's like having a new cleaner. Tracks straight, climbs walls, and actually picks up debris again."*

What's driving that improvement is the bearing replacement. Worn bearings create drag, which robs the cleaner of the water pressure it needs to climb and traverse. Fresh bearings plus new tires mean the cleaner's weight is distributed properly and the drive train isn't fighting itself. Another buyer noted the swivel replacement eliminated a persistent tangling issue with the hose — a common complaint on older units.

The build quality of these OEM parts is noticeably better than generic alternatives. The tires feel denser, the bearing housings seat firmly, and the swivel rotates smoothly right out of the box. You're paying a small premium over no-name kits, but you're eliminating the risk of premature wear or a poor fit that costs you more time in the long run.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest drawbacks. First, there are no installation instructions in the kit. If you've never disassembled a Polaris 280/380, you'll need to pull up the owner's manual online or find a teardown video. It's not a difficult job — maybe 30-40 minutes for a first-timer — but the kit assumes a baseline of mechanical comfort.

Second, this is not a universal fit across all model years. The listing specifies compatibility, and a few reviewers noted they needed to verify their specific model year before ordering. Double-check your cleaner's serial number against the compatibility list before you buy. It's an easy thing to get wrong, and returns on pool parts are tedious.

## Who Should Buy This

This kit is for you if: you own a Polaris 280 or 380 that's 2+ seasons old, you're seeing reduced suction or erratic movement, or you want to proactively refresh wear items before a failure mid-season. It's also a smart purchase if you just bought a used cleaner and want to baseline its condition.

Skip it if: your cleaner is less than a year old and performing well, or if you're not comfortable with basic disassembly. In those cases, you can defer this purchase.

## FAQ

**How often should I replace these parts?**
Most owners get 2-3 seasons from the tires and bearings with normal use. If you have a plaster pool (rougher surface), plan on the shorter end of that range.

**Will this fit my Polaris 380?**
Yes, the kit is designed for both the 280 and 380 models. Just verify your specific year against the compatibility list on the product page.

**Do I need special tools?**
No. A flathead screwdriver and possibly a pair of pliers are all you need. The parts are designed for tool-free replacement in most cases.
