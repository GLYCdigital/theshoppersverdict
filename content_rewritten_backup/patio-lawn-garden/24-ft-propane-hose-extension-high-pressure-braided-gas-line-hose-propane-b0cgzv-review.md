### Performance and Build Quality

The first thing you'll notice is the heft. This isn't a flimsy, kink-prone line. The braided steel jacket gives it serious rigidity while still allowing enough flex to route around corners. In real-world use, it handles the 20-pound tank standard pressure without any swelling or hissing. The brass fittings thread on smoothly and seat tightly—no Teflon tape needed, though adding a bit doesn't hurt.

One thing to check before buying: this is a **high-pressure** hose, not a low-pressure regulator hose. That means it's designed to carry gas *before* the regulator, not after. If you're replacing the short pigtail between your tank and your grill's regulator, you're in the right place. If you're trying to extend the low-pressure side between a regulator and a burner, this is the wrong tool—you'll need a low-pressure rated hose instead.

### Where It Falls Short

The braided exterior is durable, but it's also **stiff**. If you need tight 90-degree bends or plan to coil it up between uses, it will fight you. Expect to give it some slack and route it with generous curves. A few reviewers mention the fittings are slightly bulky—if your grill has a recessed connection point, you might struggle to get the wrench in there.

Also, this is a bare hose—no quick-disconnect, no regulator, no adapters. If you need those, factor in the extra cost. And while the brass fittings are quality, the hose itself doesn't carry any visible certification stamping. For a critical component like a gas line, some buyers may want to see a clear UL or CSA mark. That's a fair concern, though the 4.7-star average suggests most users aren't troubled by it.

### FAQ

**Will this fit my Weber or other standard grill?**
If your grill uses a standard 1/4" NPT connection at the tank and regulator, yes. Most major brands (Weber, Char-Broil, Dyna-Glo) use this thread size. When in doubt, check your current hose's fitting diameter.

**Can I use this with a 100-pound tank?**
Yes, the pressure rating handles standard outdoor propane service. Just make sure your regulator is rated for the larger tank's flow rate.

**Is this hose safe for permanent outdoor installation?**
It's weather-resistant, but not designed for burial or direct, constant UV exposure. If it'll sit in direct sun year-round, cover it or expect the outer jacket to fade and stiffen over time.
