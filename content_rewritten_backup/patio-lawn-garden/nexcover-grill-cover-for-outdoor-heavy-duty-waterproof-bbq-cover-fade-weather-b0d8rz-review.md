## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has two honest drawbacks. First, the black color is the only option available. You'll need to spot-clean it, and if you're dealing with bird droppings or sap, that means elbow grease with a mild soap solution.

There's also a legitimate fit question. While the cover accommodates most standard gas grills up to roughly 60 inches, owners of extra-large models or grills with side shelves may find the fit snug. Measure your grill before ordering—don't guess.
