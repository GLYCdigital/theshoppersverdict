Let’s be honest: most kayak handles are afterthoughts. The stock plastic loops that ship with your boat are fine for lifting an empty hull but sketchy when you’re dragging a fully rigged fishing kayak up a rocky bank. That’s where the Occult Kayak Handles (2 Pack) step in — and for the price of a decent six-pack, they solve a real problem.

## What’s Actually in the Package

You get two pre-assembled handles, each with a 10-inch section of 8mm bungee cord, a molded rubber grip, and stainless steel mounting hardware (bolts, washers, and nuts). The bungee is the star here — it’s roughly 2mm thicker than the standard 6mm cord you’ll find on most factory kayaks. As shown in the product image, the stainless hardware is substantial, not the cheap zinc-plated stuff that corrodes after one saltwater trip. No tools included, so have a Phillips screwdriver and a knife ready.

## Real-World Performance

I tested these on a 12-foot sit-on-top fishing kayak, replacing the factory bow handle that had stretched to the point of uselessness. The 8mm cord holds its tension — after a season of hauling the boat up a steep launch ramp, the handles show zero sag. The rubber grip has a nice tacky feel even when wet, which matters more than you’d think when your hands are covered in sunscreen and fish slime.

The mounting hardware is the sleeper feature. The included stainless bolts and washers fit standard kayak scupper holes and threaded inserts. One buyer noted, “The bolts are beefier than the ones that came with my Hobie — no stripping issues.” That tracks. The assembly took me about 15 minutes per handle, including drilling pilot holes.

## What Owners Consistently Praise

Across the 220 ratings (4.4 stars), the recurring theme is durability. A verified buyer wrote: “These are overkill for a kayak, which is exactly what I wanted. The bungee doesn’t stretch out like the stock ones.” Another paddler mentioned using them to replace the worn-out carry handles on a 14-foot canoe: “The grip is comfortable even with a heavy load.”

## Where They Fall Short

Two honest gripes. First, there’s no cutting tool in the box — the bungee is pre-cut to length, but if you need a custom fit for a thicker hull, you’ll need your own blade. Second, the handles only come in black. For a safety-conscious paddler, a hi-vis orange option would be a genuine improvement for spotting your boat in low light or after a capsize. Not a dealbreaker, but worth noting.

- **Kayak anglers** who drag their rigs across sand, gravel, or concrete — the thick bungee and stainless hardware are built for abuse.

## FAQ

**Will these fit my kayak?**
Most sit-on-top and sit-inside kayaks have standard 1-inch mounting holes. Measure your existing handle spacing first — the included bolts are 1.5 inches long, which covers hull thicknesses up to about 1 inch. If you have a roto-molded fishing kayak with thicker walls, you may need longer bolts.

**Are these saltwater safe?**
Yes. The stainless steel hardware won’t corrode, and the bungee is UV-resistant. Rinse them with fresh water after salt trips like you would the rest of your gear.

**Can I use these for something other than a kayak?**
Absolutely. Owners have used them on dock carts, jon boats, and even as heavy-duty tie-down points in truck beds. The 8mm bungee is strong enough for light lashing tasks.
