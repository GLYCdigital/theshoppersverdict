**Where It Falls Short**

First, the cable is stiff. At 12AWG with thick insulation, it doesn't bend gracefully around tight corners. If you're routing it through a camper window or a narrow gap, you'll need to give it a generous radius or it will fight you.

Second, the MC4 connectors are the standard male/female pair, but they're not the locking variety with the metal collar. They seat fine, but if you're in a high-wind area and the panel is vibrating, there's a small chance of disconnection. A zip tie or electrical tape around the connection solves this.

Third—and this is niche—if your solar panel already comes with an XT60 output (some newer portable panels do), this cable is useless to you. Check your panel's output connector before buying.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this cable to connect two solar panels in parallel?**  
A: No. This is a single pair of MC4 connectors on one end. For parallel connection, you need a Y-branch adapter. This cable is strictly for connecting one panel (or a series string) to a power station.

**Q: Is 12AWG overkill for a 100W panel?**  
A: It's not overkill—it's future-proofing. A 100W panel at 12V pulls about 8 amps. 12AWG handles that with minimal loss, but it also handles a 200W panel comfortably if you upgrade later.

**Q: Will this work with my Jackery Explorer?**  
A: Most Jackery models use an 8mm barrel connector, not XT60. You'd need an XT60-to-8mm adapter (sold separately). Check your power station's input port before ordering.
