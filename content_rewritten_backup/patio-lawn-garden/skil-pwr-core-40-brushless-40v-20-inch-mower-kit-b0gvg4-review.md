## The Cordless Mower That Actually Feels Like a Gas Mower

Most electric mowers under $400 feel like toys. The SKIL PWR CORE 40 isn't one of them. With a brushless motor pushing 40V through a 20-inch deck, this mower cuts through damp, thick St. Augustine like it's annoyed at the grass for existing. If you're stuck between hating gas maintenance and hating weak battery tools, this is the bridge you've been looking for.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the mower deck with a 5.0Ah PWR CORE 40 battery, a standard charger, and a 50-liter soft bag that catches clippings without turning into a clogged mess. The self-propelled rear-wheel drive is built in — no separate kit to assemble. Assembly takes about 10 minutes if you've got a screwdriver and half a brain cell. The handles fold flat for storage, which is nice if your garage looks like a Tetris game.

## Performance: Where It Earns the 4.6 Stars

The brushless motor is the star here. It's not just marketing jargon — brushless means fewer moving parts, less heat, and more torque. In real terms: it chews through wet grass without stalling, which is the #1 complaint I see on rival models like the Ryobi 40V. The self-propelled drive is genuinely useful on inclines. One buyer put it plainly: *"I have a steep backyard and this mower climbs it without me pushing. My old corded mower made me want to cry."*

Battery life is honest. On a standard suburban lawn (about 1/4 acre), you'll get through the whole cut on one charge if the grass isn't jungle-tall. If you let it go two weeks, expect to swap batteries mid-lawn. The 5.0Ah pack is decent, but the charger is slow — 120 minutes from dead to full. That's the tradeoff for the price point.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across 229 ratings, three themes keep coming up:

1. **Cut quality** — The 20-inch deck with a rear bagger leaves a clean, even strip. No clumping, no stragglers. One verified buyer said: *"Cuts better than my old gas Toro. I'm not kidding."*
2. **Build feel** — The deck is composite, not stamped steel, but it's rigid and doesn't flex. The wheels are 7-inch front, 8-inch rear — big enough to roll over small roots without bouncing.
3. **Ease of use** — Push-button start, no primer, no oil. One reviewer summed it up: *"I spent more time reading the manual than I did mowing."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct. **The charger is the weak link.** Two hours to recharge a 5.0Ah battery is sluggish. If you've got a big lawn, you'll want a second battery, and SKIL's PWR CORE 40 packs aren't cheap. 

**No mulch plug included.** For a mower this capable, that's a real oversight. You can buy one separately, but it's an extra trip and an extra $20-$30. 

**The bag is small-ish.** 50 liters sounds fine, but thick, wet grass fills it fast. You'll be emptying it more often than you'd like on a big lawn.

Also worth noting: the handle is height-adjustable but not telescoping. If you're over 6 feet tall, you'll be leaning slightly. Not a dealbreaker, but it's there.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have a 1/4-acre or smaller lawn, you're tired of gas mower maintenance, or you want a self-propelled mower without spending $600+. It's also a great pick for anyone with a sloped yard — the rear-wheel drive is the real deal.

**Skip it if:** You have more than 1/3 acre, you absolutely need mulching without buying extra parts, or you can't tolerate a 2-hour charge time without a spare battery.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does the battery really last?**  
A: On a normal cut — 30-45 minutes of actual mowing. If you're bagging wet grass or going slow through thick patches, expect closer to 25-30 minutes. Dry, short grass will stretch it.

**Q: Can I use this for mulching?**  
A: Not out of the box. You need the SKIL mulching plug (sold separately). The mower will cut and bag fine, but without the plug, clippings will just dump out the side.

**Q: Is the self-propelled drive worth it?**  
A: If your yard is flat, you can save money and get the push version. If you have any slope — even a gentle one — the self-propelled rear-wheel drive is worth every penny. It's responsive and doesn't feel jerky like some budget models.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The SKIL PWR CORE 40 is the best cordless mower under $400, period. It cuts like a gas mower, starts instantly, and folds up small enough to hang on a garage wall. The slow charger and missing mulch plug are real annoyances, but they don't kill the value. 

**Buy it if** you want a low-maintenance, self-propelled mower for a standard suburban yard. **Skip it if** you've got a big lawn and no patience for battery swaps. For everyone else, this is the one.
