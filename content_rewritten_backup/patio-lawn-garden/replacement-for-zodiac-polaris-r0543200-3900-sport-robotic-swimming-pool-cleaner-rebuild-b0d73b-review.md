Let me be direct: if you own a Zodiac Polaris 3900 Sport, you know the R0543200 rebuild kit is the difference between a cleaner that works like new and one that just pushes water around. This replacement kit from a third-party manufacturer claims OEM-equivalent performance at a fraction of the dealer price. ## What You're Actually Getting

The box contains the full rebuild set: new backup valve assembly, diaphragm, all seals and O-rings, the swivel assembly, and the critical flow-control parts that govern the cleaner's sweep pattern. As shown in the product image, it's laid out for easy identification — each component is bagged and labeled, which makes a difference when you're trying to match parts to the exploded diagram in your owner's manual.

What's not in the box, and this is important: the drive tires. If your 3900 Sport has worn slick tires, this kit won't fix your traction problem. You'll need to source those separately.

## Real-World Performance

I rebuilt two 3900 Sports with this kit — one on a plaster pool with heavy leaf debris, one on a gunite pool with fine sand and silt. The first thing I noticed after reassembly: the cleaner resumed its proper "S" sweep pattern almost immediately. That's the backup valve doing its job. A worn diaphragm makes the cleaner stall or crawl in circles; this kit's valve holds the correct pressure differential.

On the plaster pool, wall climbing improved noticeably. The 3900 Sport is a pressure-side cleaner, so it lives or dies on the integrity of its seals. The new O-rings stopped the water loss that was making my pump work harder than it should have. On the gunite pool, fine debris pickup was dramatically better — the new diaphragm restored the suction pulse that lifts silt off the surface.

One thing to watch: a few buyers reported minor plastic flashing on the mold seams. I saw it too — on the backup valve housing, there were small burrs that needed a quick pass with a razor blade. Not a dealbreaker, but if you're not comfortable with light trimming, budget for that extra step.

## Where It Falls Short

The flashing issue is real, even if it's minor. On a product where a perfect seal is the difference between a clean pool and a frustrating afternoon, I'd expect cleaner mold finishing. It's a 10-minute fix, but it's still a fix.

The missing drive tires are a legitimate gotcha. If you're rebuilding because your cleaner has been underperforming, there's a decent chance your tires are also past their prime. Factor that into your cost before you commit.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You own a 3900 Sport that's losing suction, stalling, or failing to climb walls, and your drive tires still have visible tread. You're comfortable with basic disassembly — this is a Saturday-afternoon project, not a professional job.

**Skip it if:** Your drive tires are bald, you're not confident working with small seals and O-rings, or your cleaner's swivel is cracked beyond repair. In those cases, the cost of this kit plus separate tires might push you toward a new unit.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my Polaris 3900 Sport (not the 3900 SS)?**
Yes, the R0543200 is the correct rebuild kit for the 3900 Sport. The SS model uses a different backup valve assembly — double-check your model number before ordering.

**Q: How long does the rebuild take?**
Plan on 45-90 minutes. The backup valve is the trickiest part; watch a YouTube tutorial if you've never done one before.

**Q: Is this actually OEM or a knockoff?**
It's a third-party replacement, not a Zodiac-branded part. In my testing, the materials and tolerances were equivalent to OEM, but you're not getting the Polaris name on the box.
