Let’s be honest: lighting charcoal is the worst part of grilling. Lighter fluid leaves a chemical taste that ruins $30 of steak. Chimney starters work but require newspaper, matches, and patience. The WishDirect Electric Charcoal Starter promises to fix all of that with a plug, a button, and about ten minutes. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it’s clearly working for most people. Here’s the full story.

## What You Actually Get

The unit is a stainless steel loop that plugs directly into a standard 120V outlet. There’s no chimney included—this is a standalone heating element. You place it directly on top of your charcoal, pile briquettes over it, plug it in, and wait. The loop reaches high heat quickly and transfers that energy straight into the coal above it.

The build is simple but functional. The stainless loop is rigid and won’t bend under a full load of charcoal. The handle has a rubberized grip and stays cool during use, which matters more than you’d think. The 14-foot cord gives you decent reach, though you’ll still need to think about outlet proximity before you start.

## Performance: Does It Actually Work?

Yes, and faster than you’d expect. In testing, a full chimney of standard Kingsford briquettes was ready for the grill in about 9 minutes. Lump charcoal goes even quicker—closer to 7 minutes. The key is that the element sits directly in the coal pile, so heat transfers efficiently with minimal loss.

The biggest win? No lighter fluid. That means no petroleum taste on your food, no chemical fumes while you’re waiting, and no flare-ups when you dump the lit coals into the grill. Multiple buyers confirm this. One reviewer wrote, *“I was skeptical but this thing lights charcoal faster than my chimney and doesn't leave that awful fluid smell. Game changer for my Sunday ribs.”* Another noted, *“Used it on my apartment balcony where open flames aren’t allowed. No smoke, no fire risk, just hot coals.”*

## Where It Falls Short

The cord is the main limitation. If your grill sits far from an outlet, you’ll need a heavy-duty outdoor extension cord—and that adds clutter. Also, this only works with charcoal that’s in direct contact with the element. If you’re using a gas grill with ceramic briquettes, this won’t help you at all.

There’s also a learning curve with briquette placement. If you pile charcoal too high or too tightly, airflow gets restricted and the heating element takes longer. You’ll want to leave some gaps for air to circulate. A few reviewers mentioned this, with one saying, *“Works great but you have to arrange the coals loosely or it takes forever. Once I figured that out, it’s been reliable every time.”*

## Who Should Buy This

- **Apartment dwellers** with balcony grills who can’t use open flames or lighter fluid
- **Weekend grillers** who hate the taste lighter fluid leaves on meat
- **Anyone who struggles** with chimney starters and wants a set-and-forget method

Skip it if you only grill over gas, or if your grill is more than 15 feet from the nearest outlet without an extension cord.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does it take to light charcoal?**
A: Expect 7–10 minutes for a full chimney of briquettes, slightly less for lump charcoal. It’s faster than a chimney starter and requires zero attention while it works.

**Q: Is it safe to leave unattended?**
A: It’s an electric heating element, so there’s no open flame. That said, don’t walk away entirely—check it every few minutes. The base stays stable, but you should place it on a non-flammable surface.

**Q: Does it work with any charcoal?**
A: Yes—briquettes, lump, and even coconut shell charcoal. Just make sure the charcoal is in direct contact with the heating loop. Loose arrangement speeds things up.
