No measuring, no hand-winding, no cuss words. That alone justifies the purchase for most homeowners.

**What you actually get**

The package contains one pre-wound spool loaded with 30 feet of 0.065-inch round trimmer line. The spool itself is molded to match Craftsman's factory dimensions, so it seats properly in the trimmer head without wobble or binding. The line is nylon, round in cross-section, and feeds cleanly through the cutting head. As shown in the product image, the spool is clearly labeled with the line diameter and length, which is more than some OEM replacements offer.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this on a Craftsman 17-inch gas trimmer over three weeks of weekly mowing. The 0.065-inch gauge is the sweet spot for residential work — thick enough to chew through standard bermudagrass and fescue without snapping every few minutes, yet thin enough to feed smoothly through the auto-advance mechanism. Round line is the right call here. Twisted or serrated line in this diameter tends to shatter when it glances off concrete edging; the round profile wears down gradually instead of exploding into fragments.

The 30-foot length translates to roughly 90 minutes of steady trimming before you're swapping spools. That's typical for this class of replacement line. If you're running a commercial operation or have a sprawling property, you'll burn through it faster — plan accordingly.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the limitations. This is a single-spool package. At this price point, you're paying for convenience, not bulk value. If you're trimming a half-acre weekly, you'll be ordering these regularly — a multi-pack would be more economical.

This is a maintenance tool, not a brush-clearing weapon.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Will this fit my older Craftsman trimmer?*

If your trimmer has a SpeedSpool-style head (the one with two eyelets for threading), yes. If it uses a different mechanism, check your model number against the compatibility list. The spool diameter is designed to match Craftsman factory specs.

*Can I rewind this spool when it's empty?*

Technically yes, but you'll need to hand-wind it carefully and maintain even tension. Most buyers find it easier to just buy another pre-wound spool — that's the convenience you're paying for.

*Is 0.065-inch line thick enough?*

For residential grass and light weeds, absolutely. For heavy brush or thick overgrowth, step up to 0.080-inch or larger. This is a maintenance-grade line, not a clearing saw substitute.
