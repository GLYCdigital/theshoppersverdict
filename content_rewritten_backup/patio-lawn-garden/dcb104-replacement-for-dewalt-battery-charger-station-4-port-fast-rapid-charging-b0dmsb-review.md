If you run more than two DeWalt cordless tools in rotation—say a leaf blower, string trimmer, and hedge clipper for weekend yard work—you already know the pain of waiting on a single charger. This four-port station aims to end that bottleneck. Here's the honest breakdown after digging through specs, user reports, and real-world testing chatter.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the charging station itself and, depending on the listing variant, a power cord. The unit measures roughly 8 x 6 x 4 inches and weighs about 2.5 pounds. It accepts both 20V MAX and 60V FlexVolt batteries, sliding them into four independent bays. Each bay has its own LED indicator: red for charging, green for complete. No wall adapter is included if you grab the bare-tool version, so check the listing carefully—that's a common source of frustration.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The charging logic here is straightforward: each bay draws power independently, so you can mix battery sizes without one starving another. In practice, a 20V 5Ah battery goes from dead to full in roughly 60–75 minutes—comparable to the factory single-bay charger. The 60V FlexVolt packs take longer, closer to two hours, which lines up with user reports. One reviewer noted, “It charges my 60V blower battery but definitely slower than the DeWalt-branded one I had before. Not a dealbreaker for the price.”

The plastic housing is denser than the OEM unit, which gives it a solid feel on a workbench but makes it heavier. The charging contacts are spring-loaded and grip batteries firmly—no wobble or intermittent connections reported. Heat dissipation is adequate; the unit runs warm but not hot during multi-bay charging sessions.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The most repeated plus in the 220 ratings is convenience. One verified purchase wrote, “I keep three batteries rotating between my mower and trimmer. This charges all of them overnight, and I'm ready for Saturday morning. Worth every penny.” Another highlighted the space savings: “Replaced three separate chargers on my garage wall. One unit, one outlet, done.”

The LED indicators also earn consistent mentions. Multiple reviewers note the per-bay status lights remove the guesswork—you can glance from across the garage and know which battery is ready.

**Where It Falls Short**

The biggest tradeoff is third-party status. It's not a genuine DeWalt product, so don't expect identical charge curves. The 60V FlexVolt charging is slower than the OEM DCB104—anywhere from 15 to 30 minutes longer per full charge based on user feedback. If you're running heavy 60V equipment daily, that lag matters.

The power cord situation is also inconsistent. Some listings include it, others don't. If you order the cheaper variant without reading the fine print, you'll wait an extra delivery cycle for a cord. And while the dense plastic feels robust, it adds bulk—this isn't a wall-mountable unit out of the box, so plan bench space.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Weekend landscapers** with 3–5 batteries across 20V tools: this eliminates the single-charger bottleneck entirely.
- **Pros with mixed fleets**: if you own both 20V and 60V DeWalt tools, the four bays handle both formats without separate chargers.
- **Anyone tired of wall clutter**: consolidating multiple OEM chargers into one unit saves outlet space and shelf real estate.

**Skip it if** you're a daily-driver contractor relying on 60V FlexVolt batteries for income. The slower charge times will cost you productivity, and the OEM unit is worth the premium there.

Yes, but it takes longer than the genuine DeWalt DCB104—expect roughly 2 hours for a 6Ah FlexVolt pack versus about 1.5 hours on the OEM unit.

**Q: Can I charge different battery sizes at the same time?**
Absolutely. Each bay operates independently, so a 20V 2Ah and a 60V 9Ah can charge simultaneously without issue.

**Q: Does it come with a power cord?**
Check the listing. Some variants include the AC cord, others are bare units. The product image shows the station without a cord in some listings—verify before you buy.
