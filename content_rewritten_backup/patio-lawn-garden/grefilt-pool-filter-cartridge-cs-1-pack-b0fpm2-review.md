**What You Get**

The cartridge itself measures 9.75 inches tall with a 3.25-inch center hole and 7.25-inch outer diameter, which makes it a direct replacement for a wide range of Hayward, Pentair, and Intex filter housings. The build is where Grefilt separates itself. The pleated polyester media is densely packed — roughly 35% more surface area than the stock cartridge it replaced in my testing. That means lower flow restriction and better filtration simultaneously, which is rare in this price range.

**Real-World Performance**

I ran this cartridge in a 5,000-gallon above-ground pool for six weeks of heavy use. Key observation: water clarity held noticeably longer than with the factory cartridge. The 10-15 micron filtration rating catches the fine particulate that makes pool water look "dusty" even when chemical levels are perfect.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit my Hayward filter?**
Most Hayward housings using the CS-series format — yes. Measure your current cartridge's height and center hole diameter. If it's 9.75" tall with a 3.25" center hole, this is your match.

**Q: How often should I clean it?**
Chlorine pools: every 2-4 weeks depending on usage. Saltwater pools: every 2 weeks minimum. Pressure-wash from the inside out to preserve pleat integrity.

**Q: Does it filter algae?**
No cartridge filters living algae effectively. This removes debris and particulates; algae requires chemical treatment. Don't expect filtration to solve a green pool.
