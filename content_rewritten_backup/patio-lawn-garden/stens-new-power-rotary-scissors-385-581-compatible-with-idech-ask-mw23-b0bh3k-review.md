The durability caveat is real. Several reviewers reported the blades dull faster than factory originals when they accidentally hit gravel, concrete, or fence posts. Also note the compatibility limit: this is strictly for Idec-style rotary heads. If you own a standard trimmer that uses round nylon line, this won't help you. Verify your trimmer model and head type before ordering.
