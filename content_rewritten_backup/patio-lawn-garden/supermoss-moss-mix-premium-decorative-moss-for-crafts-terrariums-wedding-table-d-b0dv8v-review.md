## Where It Falls Short

Let's talk about the honest tradeoffs.

**Color inconsistency is real.** The "green" in the product name isn't a guarantee. Some batches lean toward olive or yellow-green, especially if you get a bag with more mood moss than sheet moss. If your project requires a specific green tone, order ahead of time and inspect before your craft day.

**Debris at the bottom.** You'll lose maybe 10% of the bag to fine particles and tiny broken pieces. Not a dealbreaker, but don't plan your project expecting to use every gram.

**Not for outdoor use.** This is preserved, not living. Rain and direct sun will fade it within weeks. If you need moss for an outdoor planter, buy live moss instead.
