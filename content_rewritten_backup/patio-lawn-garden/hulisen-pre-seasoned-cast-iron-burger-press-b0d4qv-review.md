**FAQ: 3 Real Questions Shoppers Ask**

**1. Can I use this on a gas grill?**
Yes, but you’ll need a flat surface. It works best on a griddle or a cast iron skillet placed on the grill grates. Directly on round grates, the press won’t sit flat enough for even smashing.

**2. Does it come with a cover or storage bag?**
No. It arrives bare (just the press and handle). You’ll need to store it in a dry place, preferably with a light oil coating to protect the seasoning.

**3. Is the handle really cool to the touch?**
Yes, the beechwood handle stays cool during normal use. But if you leave the press on a hot griddle for 10+ minutes, the metal collar around the handle can get warm. Use a mitt if you’re preheating it for extended periods.
