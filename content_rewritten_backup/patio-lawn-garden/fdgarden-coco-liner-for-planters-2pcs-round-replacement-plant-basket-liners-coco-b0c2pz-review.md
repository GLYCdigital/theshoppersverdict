That's not a flaw; it's the deal you make for a biodegradable liner. But if you want something that lasts five years, you need plastic or metal. **FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask**

**Q: Can I use these with plastic hanging baskets instead of wire?**
Yes, but they work best with open-weave wire baskets. In solid plastic, the coir holds excess moisture against the roots, which can lead to rot. If you're using plastic, cut extra drainage holes and consider a lighter mix.

**Q: How many seasons will one liner last?**
One full growing season is realistic, sometimes two if you're in a mild climate and don't flood them. The natural decomposition is slow but steady — you'll see thinning at the bottom by month ten.

**Q: Do I need to soak the liner before planting?**
It helps. A quick soak in a bucket of water makes the fiber more pliable and reduces initial shedding. Five minutes is plenty; don't oversoak or it gets heavy and drippy.
