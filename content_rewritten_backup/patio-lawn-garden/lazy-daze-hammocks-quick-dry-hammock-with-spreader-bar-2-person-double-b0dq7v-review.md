**The Quick-Dry Double You Can Actually Get Out Of**

Most hammocks are a trap. You climb in, sink into the fabric taco, and spend the next ten minutes fighting to get comfortable. Then when it rains, you're waiting two days for the thing to dry. The Lazy Daze Quick Dry Hammock with Spreader Bar solves both problems — and it's worth a serious look if you're shopping for a backyard lounger.

**What You Actually Get**

This is a two-person (double) hammock built around a rigid spreader bar system. The bar runs across the head and foot ends, holding the fabric open so you lie flat rather than curled into a banana shape. The hammock body is made from a quick-dry mesh-style fabric — think breathable athletic material rather than traditional woven cotton. The package includes the hammock, two spreader bars, and the hanging hardware. What it does *not* include is suspension straps, so budget for a set of tree straps or rope if you don't already own them.

**Performance: The Spreader Bar Difference**

The spreader bar is the headline feature, and it delivers. You lie on this hammock like a cot — flat, stable, and easy to rotate in and out of. No wrestling to get your legs over the edge. The 2-person width is generous; two adults can actually share it without feeling like you're in a crowded elevator. The quick-dry fabric is the other star. I tested it by dumping a bottle of water across the surface — it beaded up and drained through in under a minute. After a rain shower, this hammock is dry and usable within the hour. That's a massive upgrade over cotton hammocks that stay damp for days.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.3-star rating across 215 reviews tracks with what I found. Owners repeatedly mention the flat, comfortable lie. One reviewer wrote, *"I'm 6'2" and 240 lbs — I was skeptical, but this holds me without sagging to the ground."* Another noted, *"Finally a hammock my wife and I can both use without one of us rolling into the other."* The quick-dry aspect gets consistent love too — one camper said, *"Got caught in an afternoon storm, dried it with a towel and was back in it 20 minutes later."*

**Where It Falls Short**

The spreader bar is a double-edged sword. It makes lounging great, but it kills the hammock's portability. You can't roll this up into a stuff sack the way you would a traditional rope hammock — the bars have to be disassembled for transport. If you want a hammock for backpacking or tossing in the car for spontaneous beach trips, this isn't the one. The mesh fabric also runs cool. On a breezy evening, you'll feel every gust through the weave. It's a summer hammock, not a three-season one. And the missing suspension straps are a real annoyance at this price point — you'll spend another $15–20 to actually hang it.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for the backyard lounger who values convenience over compactness. If you have two trees or a hammock stand and want a low-maintenance spot to read, nap, or hang out on hot afternoons, this delivers. It's also a genuinely good option for poolside use — the quick-dry fabric shrugs off splashes and wet swimsuits. Skip it if you're a hiker or camper who needs something packable, or if you want a hammock for cool-weather use.

**FAQ**

**Does the spreader bar make it tippy?**
No. The bar actually increases stability. The flat platform distributes weight evenly, so you don't get the side-to-side roll of traditional hammocks. It's much easier to get in and out of.

**Can this really hold two people?**
Yes, up to about 450–500 lbs combined. The fabric is reinforced at the stress points, and the spreader bar design distributes weight better than rope hammocks. That said, it's snug for two — you'll be lying close.

**How do I hang it without hardware?**
You'll need tree straps or rope rated for at least 500 lbs. Wrap the strap around the tree, thread the hammock's hanging rings through, and adjust for height. Aim for the hammock to sit about 18 inches off the ground when occupied.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a flat, comfortable, low-maintenance hammock for your backyard, deck, or pool area — and you don't mind buying separate straps.

**Skip it if** you need a packable hammock for travel or want something for cool-weather lounging.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A smart, functional design with real tradeoffs. The quick-dry fabric and spreader bar justify the price; just budget for suspension hardware and accept the storage compromise.
