If you're shopping for a chaise lounge that won't disintegrate after one humid summer, the Outsunny Wicker Outdoor Chaise Lounge deserves a hard look. With a 4.2-star average across 210 Amazon ratings, it's not the flashiest option out there — but it's quietly earning its place on patios and pool decks.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the chaise frame, the 5-level adjustable backrest mechanism, all necessary hardware, and a small wrench. No cushion, no side table, no umbrella hole — this is a bare-bones lounge chair. The dimensions are generous: roughly 75 inches long, 24 inches wide, and 19 inches high at the seat. That's enough length for most adults to stretch out fully without your feet hanging off the edge.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The headline feature is the PE rattan weave. Unlike natural wicker that cracks and fades, this synthetic version is UV-resistant and won't splinter. The weave is tight and consistent across the frame — no loose strands or gaps that suggest cheap manufacturing. Underneath, the powder-coated steel frame provides the structural backbone. One buyer noted, *"I've left it out through two rainy seasons and it still looks brand new. The frame doesn't wobble when I sit down."*

The 5-level backrest is the star. It locks into position with a satisfying click, and the mechanism doesn't slip even when you shift your weight. At full recline, it lays nearly flat — good for sunbathing. At the most upright position, it's comfortable for reading or checking your phone. The transition between positions is smooth, not jerky.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The adjustability gets steady love. One reviewer wrote, *"My husband likes it fully reclined, I prefer it upright. We both get what we want without arguing over settings."* Another mentioned, *"The backrest holds firm even when I lean forward to grab my drink — no sudden snapping back."* The weather resistance also earns repeated mentions, with several buyers reporting the chaise survived snow, rain, and intense summer sun without visible wear.

**Where it falls short**

The lack of a cushion is the most common complaint. The bare wicker surface is firm — some would say hard — and in direct sun, it absorbs heat. One buyer put it bluntly: *"You will absolutely want a pad. The wicker gets uncomfortable after 20 minutes without one."* A 2-inch outdoor cushion will run you another $30–50, so factor that into your total cost.

The weight is also a consideration. At around 30 pounds, it's light enough to move easily but not heavy enough to resist strong gusts. One reviewer reported, *"A windstorm knocked mine over and it landed on the armrest — no damage, but I had to chase it across the yard."* If you live in a windy area, plan to weigh it down or store it during storms.

**Who should buy this**

This chaise is ideal for:
- **Budget-conscious shoppers** who want a solid, functional lounge without paying $400+ for a designer brand
- **Pool or patio owners** in sunny climates who need UV-resistant furniture that won't fade
- **Anyone who values adjustability** — the 5-level backrest genuinely accommodates different lounging positions

It's less ideal for:
- **People who want a cushioned, plush experience** right out of the box
- **Those in consistently windy locations** without a sheltered patio or deck

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the backrest actually hold at all 5 positions?**
A: Yes. The locking mechanism uses a metal pin that seats firmly into the frame. It won't slip during normal use, though you'll need to lift slightly to change positions — it's not a one-hand adjustment.

**Q: Can I leave it outside all year?**
A: The PE rattan and steel frame handle rain, snow, and sun well. However, in freezing temperatures, the frame can become brittle. If you live somewhere with harsh winters, storing it indoors or under a cover will extend its lifespan.

**Q: What's the weight capacity?**
A: Outsunny lists 265 pounds. Several buyers over 200 pounds confirmed it holds steady without creaking or flexing.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a durable, adjustable chaise that survives real weather and won't break the bank. It's a workhorse, not a showpiece.

**Skip it if** you require plush cushioning included, or if your patio is a wind tunnel.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid construction, genuinely useful adjustability, and honest weatherproofing make this a smart value. Just budget for a pad and keep an eye on the forecast.
