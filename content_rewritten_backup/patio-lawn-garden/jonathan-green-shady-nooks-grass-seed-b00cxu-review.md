# Jonathan Green Shady Nooks Grass Seed Review: The Real Fix for Bare Spots Under Trees?

If you've got that patch of dirt under a mature maple where nothing grows no matter what you throw down, you know the frustration. Most "shade mixes" from big-box stores are 50% annual ryegrass — they sprout fast, look green for six weeks, then die off and leave you right back where you started. The Jonathan Green Shady Nooks blend takes a different approach, and at a premium price, it needs to deliver.

## What You're Actually Getting

This isn't a filler-heavy mix. The bag contains a blend of **turf-type tall fescue and chewings/creeping red fescue** — both are true shade-tolerant perennials. The seed is coated with a moisture-absorbing polymer that pulls water from the soil into the seed, which matters in the dry, root-competed soil under trees.

Coverage is straightforward: a 7-pound bag covers roughly 2,000 square feet for new lawns or 4,000 for overseeding. As shown in the product image, the bag clearly labels the mix percentages and coverage area — no mystery about what's inside.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where this product separates itself from the $12 bag at the hardware store. **Germination takes 14-21 days** — this is not instant gratification. But what comes up is a dense, fine-bladed turf that actually persists in shade. Buyers consistently mention the same outcome:

> "I've tried every shade seed at Lowe's and Home Depot. This is the first one that actually filled in under my oak tree after two seasons."

That's the key insight: this is a long-game product. The fescues establish deep root systems over the first season, and by year two, you have a real lawn, not a temporary green patch.

The moisture-coating technology is a genuine advantage. Multiple reviews note that the seed survived dry spells during germination that would have killed uncoated seed. One buyer reported success with just light watering every other day — important if you don't have an irrigation system in those hard-to-reach areas.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides:

**The price stings.** A 7-pound bag runs noticeably more than generic shade mixes. If you're covering a large area, the cost adds up quickly.

**It's slow.** If you need quick erosion control or a fast green-up before an event, this isn't the product. You'll be waiting three weeks for visible results.

**It's shade-only.** This blend will not thrive in full sun. If your "shady spot" gets more than 6 hours of direct sun, you're better off with a sun-and-shade mix. Several reviewers made this mistake and blamed the product, but the label is clear.

**Prep matters more than usual.** This seed needs good soil contact. Raking, aerating, or topdressing with compost dramatically improves results. Tossing it on hard-packed dirt under a tree is a waste of money — and a few negative reviews reflect exactly that scenario.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have established trees or structures creating dappled-to-moderate shade, you're willing to prep the soil properly, and you want a permanent solution rather than a temporary green fix. It's ideal for homeowners in the Northeast, Mid-Atlantic, and Pacific Northwest where cool-season grasses thrive.

**Skip it if:** You're in the deep South or Southwest (this is a cool-season blend), you need fast results, or you're looking for a budget option. Also skip if your "shade" is actually full sun — you'll be disappointed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use this in full sun areas of my lawn?**
No. This blend is specifically formulated for shade. In full sun, the fescues will thin out and struggle during summer heat. Use a sun-tolerant mix for open areas.

**Q: When is the best time to plant?**
Late summer to early fall (August-October) is ideal — soil temperatures are warm enough for germination, but the cooler air reduces stress on young seedlings. Spring planting works but requires more diligent watering through summer.

**Q: How does this compare to Jonathan Green's Black Beauty Ultra?**
Black Beauty Ultra is their all-purpose premium mix that handles sun and partial shade. Shady Nooks is exclusively for low-light conditions. If your yard is mixed, you'll need two different products — or use Black Beauty Ultra everywhere and accept slower growth in the deepest shade.
