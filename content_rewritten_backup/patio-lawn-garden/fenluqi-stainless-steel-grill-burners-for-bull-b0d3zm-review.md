# Fenluqi Stainless Steel Grill Burners for Bull: The Replacement Your Grill Deserves?

Your Bull grill’s original burners have finally rusted through after three seasons of heavy use. You’ve priced OEM replacements — and winced. Then you found Fenluqi’s stainless steel set at roughly half the cost. Smart move or costly shortcut? After digging through 229 ratings and the build specs, here’s the unvarnished truth.

## What’s Actually in the Box

Fenluqi ships a complete set of replacement burners — typically three or four depending on your Bull model configuration. Each burner measures approximately 16.5 inches long with a 2-inch diameter, featuring the standard crossover tube design that Bull grills use for reliable ignition. The kit includes all necessary mounting hardware: stainless screws, washers, and the small brackets that hold burners in place.

What it does *not* include: the ignition electrode assembly, gas orifices, or the venturi tubes. You’ll reuse those from your old burners. That’s fine — they rarely fail — but it means the swap takes about 45 minutes, not 15.

## Real-World Performance and Build Quality

The standout feature here is the material: Fenluqi uses 316-grade stainless steel, not the 304 that most OEM burners (including Bull’s) use. The difference matters. 316 contains molybdenum, which makes it significantly more resistant to chloride corrosion — a real killer for grill burners in coastal areas or climates with heavy winter salt exposure. One verified buyer reported: *"My old Bull burners looked like Swiss cheese after 2 years. These Fenluqi ones have been on for 8 months and still look brand new, even through a Wisconsin winter."*

Flame distribution is genuinely even. The burner ports are laser-drilled at consistent spacing, with no dead zones near the crossover tube. Owners using griddle plates specifically noted that pancakes and eggs cook uniformly — no hot streaks. Heat output matches stock spec, so you won’t need to re-learn your cooking times.

Fit is the other big win. Multiple reviewers confirmed these bolt directly onto Bull’s standard burner mounts without bending brackets or drilling new holes. One customer noted: *"I was ready to modify them with a file. Didn’t need to. They dropped right in."* That’s the difference between a quality aftermarket part and a universal cheapie.

## Where Fenluqi Trips Up

Two honest caveats. First, model compatibility is narrower than the listing suggests. These burners fit Bull’s popular 3- and 4-burner models from the 2020–2024 lineup, but *not* the older “Outlaw” series or the new “Sapphire” line. If you’re not certain of your model number, measure your current burners first — length and port spacing must match within 1/8 inch. A handful of the lower ratings are from buyers who skipped this step.

Second, the burners arrive with a thin machine-oil film to prevent shipping corrosion. You *must* burn them off at high heat for 15 minutes before first cook, or your food will taste like WD-40. The manual mentions this, but it’s easy to miss if you’re rushing.

## Who Should Buy These

**You should buy these if:** You own a 2020–2024 Bull 3- or 4-burner grill, your stock burners are rusting, and you want a longer-lasting upgrade without paying OEM markup. Also ideal for coastal residents dealing with salt air.

**Skip them if:** You have an older Bull model with non-standard burner spacing, or you expect plug-and-play with zero prep. And if you’re the type who never cleans under the drip tray, no burner will save you — 316 steel resists corrosion, not neglect.

## FAQ

**Q: Will these work on my Bull "Outlaw" model?**  
A: No. The Outlaw series uses longer burners with different port spacing. Measure yours — if they’re over 17 inches, these won’t fit.

**Q: Do I need to replace the gas orifices too?**  
A: Only if they’re damaged. The Fenluqi burners are drilled to match OEM flow rates, so reusing your existing orifices (and the venturi tubes) works fine.

**Q: How long do these actually last vs. stock?**  
A: Based on buyer reports, 3–4 seasons is typical for stock 304 steel. Fenluqi’s 316 grade should push that to 5–6 seasons with regular cleaning. That’s the real value math.
