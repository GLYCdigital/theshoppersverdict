Let's be blunt: most patio sets under $700 look great in the product photos and fall apart by August. The Rattaner 5-piece set has managed to hold a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings, which suggests it's doing something right. After digging through buyer feedback and examining the specs, here's the real story.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The set includes a 3-seat sofa (roughly 80 inches wide), two single armchairs, a tempered glass coffee table, and enough cushions for everything. The frame is powder-coated steel wrapped in all-weather PE rattan — not the cheap plastic wicker that cracks in direct sun. The woven texture looks convincingly like natural rattan up close, which is the main reason buyers consistently say it "looks way more expensive than it is."

One detail that matters: the glass tabletop has a safety edge and sits in a recessed frame, so glasses don't slide off when someone bumps the table. That's a small thing that makes a big difference with kids around.

**How It Holds Up in the Real World**

The PE rattan is the star here. Multiple reviewers mention leaving it out through summer storms and fall weather without visible damage. One buyer noted: *"We've had this set for two seasons now, and the wicker hasn't faded or cracked. The cushions are a different story — they're not waterproof, so we bring them in when rain is forecast."*

That's the honest tradeoff. The cushions are comfortable but firm — think supportive rather than sink-in soft. They're covered in a fade-resistant fabric, but they're not outdoor-rated for constant moisture. If you live somewhere with frequent rain, plan to cover them or store them. The set doesn't include a cover, so factor that into your budget.

The steel frame is properly welded and coated — no sharp edges or flaking paint reported in the feedback I reviewed. Assembly is the biggest complaint. Expect 2-3 hours with two adults. The instructions are adequate but not great, and a few buyers noted the pre-drilled holes on the chair arms don't line up perfectly. It's a frustration, not a dealbreaker, and most people got through it with a bit of patience.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Three themes dominate the positive reviews:

1. **Sturdiness** — People are genuinely surprised at how solid it feels. One reviewer wrote: *"I'm 230 pounds and this holds me without any wobble. The set feels much more expensive than the price tag."*

2. **Looks** — The brown rattan finish photographs well but looks even better in person. It reads as a premium set from a distance.

3. **Seating capacity** — The sofa genuinely fits three adults, and the chairs are full-size, not the narrow, cramped versions you get with cheaper sets. This is a real 5-person setup.

**Where It Falls Short**

The cushion situation is the main weakness. They're not waterproof, they're firm, and they don't have ties or straps to keep them in place. On a windy day, expect to chase them around the yard. Also, there's no built-in storage, so winterizing means finding space for six cushions somewhere indoors.

Assembly quality control is inconsistent. Most buyers reported no issues, but a minority dealt with misaligned holes and one or two missing screws. Check the box contents before you start — the hardware bag is small and easy to misplace.

One more thing: the set is heavy. The sofa alone is around 40 pounds. That's good for stability, but moving it around the patio is a two-person job.

**Who Should Buy This**

This set is ideal for **first-time patio buyers** who want a complete, cohesive setup without spending $1,500+ at a big-box store. It also works well for **renters or homeowners with covered patios** who want furniture that looks custom without the permanent commitment.

Skip it if you need **truly weatherproof cushions** (look for Sunbrella fabric sets instead) or if you want something lightweight you can rearrange solo.

**Three Questions Shoppers Ask**

**Q: Can this stay outside all year?**
The frame and wicker can. The cushions cannot. In freezing climates, bring the cushions indoors or store them in a deck box. The frame itself will survive snow and ice fine.

**Q: How long does assembly actually take?**
Budget 2-3 hours with two people. Have a power drill with a Phillips bit handy — doing it by hand will take twice as long. Don't tighten everything fully until all pieces are aligned.

**Q: Are the cushions replaceable?**
Yes, and that's a plus. They're standard sizes, so you can order replacement cushions from multiple retailers if needed. You're not locked into the manufacturer.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a complete, attractive 5-piece set that looks premium, seats real adults comfortably, and won't fall apart after one season. The frame and wicker construction is genuinely solid for the price.

**Skip it if:** You need a maintenance-free setup, want plush, sink-in cushions, or live in a high-rain area without covered storage. This set requires minor upkeep to stay in top shape.

The Rattaner 5-piece set earns its 4.5-star rating through solid construction, honest sizing, and a look that punches above its weight class. Just plan for assembly time and cushion care, and you'll get years of use out of it.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A strong value for the price, docked slightly for cushion durability and assembly frustrations.
