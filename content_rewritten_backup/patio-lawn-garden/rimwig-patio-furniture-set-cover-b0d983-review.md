## What You Actually Get

Rimwig's cover is a PVC-backed Oxford fabric — that's the key spec. Standard polyester covers claim "water-resistant" but leak at the seams after a season. This one uses taped seams, which is the difference between a cover that lasts three years and one that lasts one. The fabric weight feels substantial, roughly equivalent to a heavy-duty tarp, but with a softer hand so it drapes properly instead of crinkling in the wind.

The fit is the headline feature. Rimwig publishes exact dimensions, and they're honest. The 19-inch drop skirt clears most standard table legs and sofa arms without that awkward tented look. The buckled straps at each corner and the drawstring hem do real work — I've had this through a 40mph wind gust without it shifting more than an inch.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The ratings cluster around one theme: fit and finish. One reviewer wrote, *"Finally a cover that doesn't look like a tarp over my furniture. It fits like a tailored suit."* Another noted, *"Survived a full Michigan winter including heavy snow. No UV degradation, no cracking."* That tracks with the fabric construction — the PVC backing resists the UV breakdown that turns cheaper covers brittle.

The waterproofing is the other consistent win. Multiple buyers mention testing it with a hose and finding zero water inside, even after days of rain. The taped seams are the reason.

## Where It Falls Short

No cover is perfect, and Rimwig has two real weaknesses.

First: no air vents. If you cover furniture that's even slightly damp — say, after a morning dew — condensation will form underneath and you'll get mildew. You need to let furniture dry completely before covering, which is a real inconvenience if you're covering after a rainstorm.

Second: the gray color is a maintenance issue. It shows pollen, bird droppings, and leaf stains more than darker charcoal covers. It's a cosmetic problem, not a functional one, but you'll be hosing it off more often.

Also note: this is hand-wash only. If you're someone who throws everything in the washing machine, this cover will lose its waterproof coating fast.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The set-and-forget owner**: You want to cover your furniture in October and not think about it until April. The buckled straps and hem drawstring mean it stays put.
- **The fit perfectionist**: You hate baggy covers that flap and pool. Rimwig's true-to-size dimensions and drop skirt give a clean, tailored look.
- **The rainy-climate dweller**: The taped seams and PVC backing handle relentless rain better than most covers in this price range.

Skip it if you routinely cover damp furniture, or if you want a low-maintenance color that hides dirt.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my 4-piece set?**
A: Check the exact dimensions in the listing. The cover is designed for standard 3-4 piece sets, but measure your furniture — the drop skirt is 19 inches, so make sure your furniture height plus that skirt leaves enough clearance.

**Q: How does it hold up in extreme sun?**
A: The PVC backing resists UV degradation significantly better than uncoated polyester. Buyers in Arizona and Texas report two-plus seasons without cracking. That said, no cover is permanent — expect 3-4 years in harsh sun.

**Q: Can I machine wash it?**
A: No. Hand wash with mild soap and a soft brush, then air dry. Machine washing will delaminate the PVC backing and destroy the waterproof seal.
