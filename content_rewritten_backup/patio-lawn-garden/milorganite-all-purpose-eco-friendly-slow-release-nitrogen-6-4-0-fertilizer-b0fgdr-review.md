Let's cut through the marketing. Milorganite has been around since 1926 — that's nearly a century of lawns fed by Milwaukee's wastewater treatment byproduct. The 6-4-0 formulation is their flagship, and it's earned a 4.6-star average across 229 Amazon ratings. Here's what you need to know before you spread a single pellet.

**What you're actually getting**

The bag contains heat-dried, granulated biosolids — microbially digested sewage sludge processed at high temperatures to kill pathogens. Each granule delivers 6% nitrogen, 4% phosphate, and 0% potassium. The nitrogen is slow-release, meaning it breaks down via soil microbes over 8-10 weeks rather than dumping everything at once.

As shown in the product image, the dark granules are uniform and easy to spread with a rotary spreader. A 32-pound bag covers roughly 2,500 square feet at the standard rate of 1 pound per 100 square feet. No mixing, no spraying — just dry application.

**How it performs in the real world**

The slow-release mechanism is the star here. Synthetic fertilizers like Scott's Turf Builder hit hard and fade fast; Milorganite builds gradually. You won't see dramatic greening in 48 hours — you'll see steady, even color over several weeks. Multiple Amazon reviewers note this exact pattern: "Takes about two weeks to see results, but the green lasts much longer than chemical fertilizers."

The non-burning guarantee is legit. Because nitrogen is released only as microbes digest the granules, you can't overdose your lawn into yellow stripes. This makes it forgiving for new homeowners who aren't sure about spreader settings.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The soil-building aspect gets repeated mention. One reviewer wrote: "After two seasons, my clay soil is noticeably darker and earthworms are everywhere." That's the organic matter working — something synthetic fertilizers simply don't do.

The iron content (2.5%) also gets love for deepening green color without the flush of growth that requires more mowing. Several reviewers with dogs specifically mention it's safe after drying — though you should still keep pets off until the granules are watered in.

**Where it falls short**

The smell is real. It's not overpowering, but it's distinctly earthy — some describe it as "mulch mixed with manure." It dissipates within a day or two, but if you have neighbors close by and plan a backyard gathering, time your application accordingly.

The zero potassium is a genuine limitation. Potassium drives root development, disease resistance, and winter hardiness. If you're in a region with harsh winters or heavy foot traffic, you'll need a separate potassium source — which adds cost and complexity.

**Who should buy this**

- **New lawn owners** who want a forgiving product that won't burn mistakes
- **Organic-minded gardeners** who want to build soil biology without synthetic chemicals
- **Low-maintenance types** who prefer one application every 6-8 weeks over weekly feeding schedules

**Skip it if** you need quick results for an event, want a complete NPK formula in one bag, or can't tolerate any odor near your living space.

**FAQ**

*Can I use this on vegetables?* Technically yes, but the heat treatment doesn't eliminate all heavy metal concerns. For edibles, stick to compost or specially labeled organic fertilizers. Milorganite is best for lawns and ornamentals.

*How often should I apply?* Every 8-10 weeks during the growing season — typically 3-4 applications per year. Over-application is hard to do, but don't exceed the bag rate.

*Is it safe for pets?* Once watered in and dried, yes. The granules themselves are not toxic, but keep pets off freshly applied lawns for 24 hours to avoid ingestion of concentrated pellets.

**The Verdict**

Milorganite 6-4-0 earns its reputation through steady, soil-building performance and near-zero risk of user error. It's not the fastest or the most complete fertilizer on the shelf, but it's one of the most forgiving and environmentally sound options at this price point.

**Buy it if** you want a low-maintenance, non-burning fertilizer that improves soil health over time. **Skip it if** you need fast results or want a single complete NPK product.
