## FAQ

**Q: How much should I add to lower pH by 0.2?**
A: For a 10,000-gallon pool, start with 1 pint (16 oz) and retest after 4–6 hours of circulation. Always add in small increments — it's easier to add more than to neutralize an over-correction.

**Q: Can I use this to clean pool tile and stains?**
A: Yes, but dilute it 1:10 with water first. Full-strength acid can etch plaster and eat grout. For scale removal, apply diluted solution, wait 5 minutes, scrub, and rinse thoroughly.

**Q: Will this expire?**
A: Muriatic acid doesn't spoil, but it can lose strength if the seal is broken and it's exposed to air. Keep the caps tightly closed and store in a cool, dry place. A properly sealed jug stays effective for years.
