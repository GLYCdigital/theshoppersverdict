## FAQ: What Shoppers Ask

**Q: Will this fit a regular (non-built-in) Blackstone 36-inch griddle?**
No. The mounting brackets are sized specifically for the built-in Omnivore models. The standalone units have different frame geometry. Don't force it—you'll strip the bolts.

**Q: Can I use this with the hood closed for baking?**
Yes, with caveats. The internal temp can reach 400°F+, but the vent gap prevents a perfect seal. You can bake small items like cornbread or cookies, but you won't get the same results as a dedicated oven. It's best for melting, steaming, and holding heat.

**Q: How do I clean the hood?**
Wipe it down with warm soapy water after it cools. The interior has a non-stick coating that resists baked-on grease, but don't use abrasive scrubbers—they'll scratch the finish. The exterior needs occasional oiling to prevent surface rust, just like the griddle itself.
