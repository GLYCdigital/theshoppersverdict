You don’t need to hear that pool filters are important. You need to know if this $40-ish cartridge actually replaces a $90 Hayward part without headaches. After digging through 229 ratings and the technical specs, here’s the straight story.

**What you actually get**  
The box contains one cartridge. That’s it. No gaskets, no O-rings, no instructions (you won’t need them). The filter measures 9.5 inches in diameter by 23.5 inches tall — identical to the OEM Hayward C500 replacement. The pleated polyester media is wrapped around a reinforced ABS core with molded end caps. As shown in the product image, the pleats are tight and uniform across the entire surface — a good sign for even water distribution.

**Real-world performance**  
The 50-square-foot filtration area is the same as the factory part. In practice, that means you’ll get the same flow rate — around 80 GPM at typical pump speeds — without the pressure spike you sometimes see with cheap knockoffs. One reviewer noted: *“Pressure gauge stayed at 12 PSI after install, same as the Hayward original that lasted 3 seasons.”* That’s the metric that matters.

The polyester media catches algae spores and fine sand down to about 10-15 microns. It won’t filter as finely as DE, but for a cartridge system, it’s the standard. Buyers consistently report it clearing green water within 24-48 hours when paired with a proper shock treatment.

**Build quality and longevity**  
The end caps are sonically welded, not glued — that’s a real difference. Glued caps fail at the seam after a year. Welded caps hold. The center core is thick-walled ABS that won’t collapse if you accidentally run the pump dry for a few minutes. Multiple reviewers report getting 2 full seasons before replacement, with one stating: *“Second year using this, still holds pressure, no channeling.”*

The one consistent complaint: the filter can arrive slightly oval-shaped from being compressed in shipping. It doesn’t affect fit, but you’ll want to let it sit for a few hours before installing so the pleats fully expand. If you install immediately, you might get slightly reduced flow for the first day.

**Where it falls short**  
No gasket included. The Hayward C500 housing uses a large rubber O-ring that seals the lid. POOLPURE assumes you’ll keep your old one. If yours is cracked, you’ll need to order one separately — that’s an extra $8-10 and a trip to the pool store. Also, this is a cartridge-only replacement. It does NOT include the internal manifold or the top/bottom grids. That’s fine for most people, but if your housing internals are worn, this won’t fix that.

**FAQ**  

*How often should I replace this filter?*  
With normal use (daily pumping, 4-6 hours), replace every 12-18 months. If you run your pump 24/7 or have heavy tree debris, plan on annual replacement. You’ll know it’s time when pressure rises 8-10 PSI above clean baseline and hosing off doesn’t bring it down.

*Will this fit my Hayward C500 housing exactly?*  
Yes. The diameter, height, and center hole all match the OEM C500 cartridge. No adapters or cutting needed. It also fits the CX500-RE and Pentair PA50, which use the same cartridge.

*Can I clean and reuse this filter?*  
Yes, but only 2-3 times. Hose it down gently from top to bottom, let it dry, and reinstall. After the third cleaning, pleats start to lose shape and filtration drops. Don’t use a pressure washer — it will damage the media.
