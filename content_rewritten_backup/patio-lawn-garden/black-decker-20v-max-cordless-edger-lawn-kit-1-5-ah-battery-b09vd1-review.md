Let me be blunt: a 3.6-star average on 180 ratings means this edger has a split personality. Some owners swear by it; others want to throw it in a dumpster. After digging through buyer feedback and the spec sheet, here's what you actually need to know about the BLACK+DECKER 20V MAX Cordless Edger.

**What's in the Box**

You get the edger head, a 1.5 Ah 20V MAX battery, a charger, and a blade wrench. That's it. No spare blade, no second battery, no gloves. The edger itself is lightweight—around 5.5 pounds—so it's easy to maneuver with one hand while you guide the cord with the other. The wheel is 7 inches, and the blade is 7.5 inches wide, which is enough to cut a clean line along most standard walkways.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The motor is a 20V MAX brushed unit that spins the blade at a decent clip. On edging duties—cutting that grass overhang along driveways and sidewalks—it does the job. The blade cuts cleanly, and the design throws the clippings away from the lawn, which is a thoughtful touch. The depth adjustment is tool-less: a simple lever lets you set how deep you want to cut, from a light trim to a deeper trench.

The build is typical BLACK+DECKER: plastic housing, no-nonsense ergonomics, and a handle that's comfortable enough for 20 minutes of work. The wheel rolls smoothly on flat surfaces, and the edger tracks reasonably straight. It's not a professional-grade unit—this is a homeowner tool through and through.

**Where It Shines**

Owners consistently praise the cordless convenience. One buyer noted, "No extension cord dragging around, no fighting with the cord while edging the driveway." Another highlighted the clean cut: "It leaves a crisp edge that makes my yard look professionally maintained." For small to medium lawns, this thing is genuinely useful. It's also quiet enough to use early morning without waking the neighbors.

**Where It Falls Short**

The battery is the elephant in the room. The included 1.5 Ah pack gives you roughly 15-20 minutes of continuous edging. If your yard is anything bigger than a postage stamp, you'll be recharging mid-job. Multiple reviewers flagged this: "Battery died before I finished the front yard." A few also noted the motor bogs down in thick, wet grass—you'll need to slow your pace or take lighter passes.

The plastic blade guard is also a weak point. It does its job, but it can crack if you whack a curb or rock hard enough. Replacement guards are cheap, but it's an annoyance.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for homeowners with small to medium lawns who want a lightweight, cordless edger for weekly maintenance. If you have a ranch-style house with a modest sidewalk, this will save you time and effort. It's also a good fit if you already own BLACK+DECKER 20V tools—the battery is cross-compatible with the rest of their lineup.

Skip it if you have a large property, thick Bermuda or St. Augustine grass, or you're expecting commercial-grade durability. This is not that tool.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does the battery actually last?**
A: Expect 15-20 minutes of active edging. If your edges total less than 100 linear feet, you're fine. Anything more, budget for a second battery.

**Q: Can I use my existing BLACK+DECKER 20V batteries?**
A: Yes. Any 20V MAX battery works. Buy a 4 Ah pack for longer runtime—it's worth the upgrade.

**Q: Is it hard to change the blade?**
A: No. The blade wrench included in the kit fits the nut, and the whole process takes about 3 minutes. No special skills required.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you have a small-to-medium lawn, want cordless convenience, and don't mind swapping batteries mid-job. It's a solid entry-level edger that gets the job done.

**Skip it if** you have a large yard, thick grass, or you need something that'll survive years of heavy use. For those cases, look at a corded edger or a gas-powered unit.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — It does what it promises, but the short battery life and plastic build keep it from greatness. A spare battery and a slightly bigger motor would make this a 4.5-star tool. As it stands, it's a decent pick for the right buyer.
