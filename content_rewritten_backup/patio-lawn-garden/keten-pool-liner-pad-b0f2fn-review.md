## Is the Keten Pool Liner Pad Actually Worth Your Money?

Here's the thing about above-ground pool setups: most people skimp on the underlayment, then wonder why their liner looks like a golf ball after one season. The Keten Pool Liner Pad is designed to fix that specific problem. At 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. But is it the right pad for *your* installation? Let's dig into the details.

## What You Actually Get

The Keten pad is a closed-cell polyethylene foam sheet, roughly 7mm thick. That thickness matters — cheap pads are often 3-4mm and might as well be paper. The closed-cell structure means it won't absorb groundwater, which is critical because a waterlogged underlayment becomes a breeding ground for mold and accelerates liner breakdown.

The material cuts cleanly with scissors or a box cutter. If you're working around a round pool, you'll need to trim it to fit; if you have an oval or rectangular frame, plan to lay multiple strips side by side. It arrives tightly compressed in a roll, and in my experience, it takes a few hours in direct sunlight to relax flat. Don't unroll it at 7 AM and expect it to lay perfectly by noon.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The primary job of any pool pad is to create a smooth barrier between the ground and your liner. The Keten pad does that effectively. The 7mm thickness absorbs minor ground imperfections — think small pebbles, grass stubble, or slightly uneven dirt. It won't fix a yard with significant grade changes, but it handles the typical backyard prep job.

One thing buyers consistently mention is how much easier installation feels with this pad underfoot. As one reviewer put it: *"Made a huge difference. I had rocks and roots I thought I'd cleared, but this pad saved my liner. No punctures after the first season."* That's the exact scenario this product is built for.

Temperature-wise, the closed-cell foam doesn't conduct cold as aggressively as bare ground, so the pool floor feels marginally warmer in early summer. Not a game-changer, but a nice side effect.

## What Buyers Praise

The consensus among the 220 ratings is that this pad delivers on its core promise. People appreciate:

- **Puncture resistance** — Multiple reviews confirm it prevents liner damage from small debris that survives ground prep.
- **Easy trimming** — The foam cuts cleanly without tearing or crumbling, even with household scissors.
- **Fast flattening** — Unlike some competitors that stay curled for days, this pad rebounds reasonably quickly in warmth.

## Where It Falls Short

Honest feedback time. First, the dimensions run slightly conservative. If your pool footprint is right at the listed size, you'll likely need to order up or buy two rolls to get proper coverage with overlap. That's an annoying surprise when you're mid-installation.

Second, the surface is slick. When you're smoothing the liner over it, the pad can shift slightly underfoot. Take your time and weigh down the edges while you work.

Third, there's no seam tape or adhesive included. For larger pools, you'll need to secure overlapping panels yourself — duct tape works, but it's an extra cost and step nobody mentions in the product photos.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're installing a new above-ground pool on bare ground, grass, or sand. You want genuine puncture protection without paying premium custom-pad prices. You're comfortable trimming foam to fit your pool's shape.

**Skip it if:** You have a heavily uneven yard that needs actual leveling first (no pad fixes that). You're installing a permanent in-ground pool — this isn't designed for that application. Or if you're on a tight budget, consider that you may need two rolls plus seam tape, which adds up.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this pad directly on grass?**
Yes, but you should still clear the area of visible debris first. The pad protects against small stuff, not branches or construction debris.

**Q: Will this pad prevent liner wrinkles?**
It helps, but wrinkles are mostly a function of installation technique — you need to smooth the liner carefully and fill slowly. The pad provides a flat base, which reduces wrinkle risk.

**Q: How long does it last?**
Closed-cell foam doesn't degrade from moisture, so it should outlast several liner replacements. Most users report it's still intact after 3+ seasons.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5 — Buy it.**

The Keten Pool Liner Pad is a solid, no-nonsense underlayment that does exactly what it claims. It's not the cheapest option, and the sizing quirks and missing seam tape are genuine annoyances. But if you want to avoid the heartbreak of a punctured liner — and the $200+ replacement cost that comes with it — this pad is a smart investment. Just measure twice, order slightly large, and bring your own tape.

*Buy it if you want real liner protection without paying boutique prices. Skip it only if your ground prep is absolutely perfect and you're willing to gamble on a bare-ground install.*
