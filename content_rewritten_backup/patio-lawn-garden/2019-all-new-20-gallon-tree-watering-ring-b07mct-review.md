**Build Quality and the Durability Question**

The PVC material feels substantial at first — roughly 20-25 mils thick. But here's the honest truth: this is a UV-exposed product that lives outside year-round. Multiple long-term reviewers report cracking and splitting after 12-18 months of direct sun exposure. The included repair kit helps, but it's a temporary fix, not a permanent solution.

The fill cap is a weak point. It's a threaded plastic fitting that can strip if you overtighten it. The drain plugs work well for winterizing, but you absolutely must remember to use them before freezing temperatures hit. Water left inside will expand and split the seams.
