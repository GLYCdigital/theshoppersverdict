## Honest Shortcomings

Here's where I push back on the marketing.

**No gasket included.** The CL58 housing uses a large O-ring between the cartridge and the manifold. You'll need to reuse your old one or buy a replacement separately. If your O-ring is cracked, the cartridge will still "fit" but you'll have a slow bypass leak. Factor in $8–12 for a new O-ring if you're replacing everything.

**Pleat density is slightly lower** than the genuine Jandy cartridge — approximately 110 pleats versus 120. In practical terms, this means the CV580 may need cleaning slightly more often if you have heavy debris (trees, pollen, heavy swimmer load). For most pool owners, this is a non-issue. For those with constant leaf drop, it's worth knowing.

**No 1-year warranty language on the packaging.** Some competitors offer explicit 12-month coverage. This one relies on Amazon's return window. If you're buying mid-season and the cartridge fails in month 8, you're likely out of luck.
