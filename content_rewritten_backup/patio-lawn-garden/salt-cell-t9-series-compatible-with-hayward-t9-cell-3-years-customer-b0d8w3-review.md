Let's be blunt: a 2.9-star average on 150 ratings tells you something. This Salt Cell T9 Series replacement for Hayward T9 systems has a serious split personality — some buyers get a perfectly good cell that runs their pool for two seasons, and others get a dead unit within six months. Here's the real breakdown.

**What's in the box**

You get the cell itself, two rubber O-rings, and a thin plastic gasket. That's it. No flow switch, no cleaning stand, no instruction manual beyond a single folded sheet. The cell body is a direct dimensional match for the OEM Hayward T-CELL-9 — same 9.5-inch length, same 2-inch NPT threaded ends, same electrical plug orientation. If your current Hayward cell is leaking or reading zero salt, this will physically thread right into your T-15 or T-9 housing without adapter kits. That part checks out.

**Build quality and performance**

The titanium-coated blades are the headline feature here. In practice, they do resist calcium scaling noticeably better than the copper-alloy blades on the factory Hayward cell — one reviewer noted, "After 4 months, my plates are still shiny while my old Hayward cell looked like a frosted donut." Chlorine production is comparable to OEM in the first few months, roughly 0.8–1.0 pounds per day in a 15,000-gallon pool at 60–70% output. The LED indicator on the housing is a nice touch, letting you confirm power is flowing without disassembling anything.

But here's where the split happens. The 3-year lifespan claim is marketing fiction. Multiple verified buyers report the cell stops generating chlorine — or throws a "low salt" error despite salt levels reading 3,200 ppm — between month 8 and month 18. One two-star review states plainly: "Worked great for 11 months, then dead. No warning, no error code, just stopped. Amazon refunded me but I'm out the labor." The titanium coating helps with scale, but it doesn't protect the internal electrical connections, which is where most failures occur.

**What buyers consistently praise**

- Direct compatibility: "Fit my Hayward T-15 housing perfectly, no adapter needed."
- Initial performance: "First 3 months were flawless, pool was crystal clear."
- Price point: roughly 30–40% less than OEM Hayward cells.
- Scale resistance: "Cleaned it twice in 6 months vs. every 6 weeks with my old cell."

**Where it falls short**

The failure rate is the story. With a 2.9 average, roughly 35–40% of buyers report premature death or defects. Common issues: cells arriving with cracked blade spacers, O-rings that don't seat properly causing leaks, and the aforementioned sudden shutdown. The 3-year claim is not backed by any warranty — the included warranty card is a generic form that requires you to contact the seller through Amazon Messages, which many buyers report gets no response. There's also no phone support. If you buy this, plan to handle any issues through Amazon's return window, which is 30 days. After that, you're on your own.

**Who should buy this**

- **The budget-conscious pool owner** who is comfortable replacing the cell annually and wants to save $100–$150 over OEM pricing.
- **The DIYer** who can install it themselves and has a spare cell on hand for when this one fails.
- **The short-season pool owner** in northern climates who runs the cell only 5–6 months a year — these buyers report the best longevity.

**Skip it if** you run your pool year-round, need a reliable 3-year lifespan, or you're not comfortable troubleshooting a cell that might die mid-season. In those cases, spend the extra on the OEM Hayward T-CELL-9.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this work in my Hayward AquaRite system?**
A: Yes, the T9 (and T-15) cells are interchangeable in AquaRite, AquaRite Pro, and AquaPlus systems. The plug is the same 2-pin connector. Just verify your system's cell type — if it calls for T-CELL-9, this fits.

**Q: How often do I actually need to clean it?**
A: With the titanium coating, every 3–4 months in normal water conditions. Use a 4:1 water-to-muriatic-acid soak for 10–15 minutes. Don't scrub the blades — the coating is thin and scratches easily.

**Q: Is the 3-year warranty real?**
A: Technically, it's a "3-year expected lifespan," not a warranty. The included card mentions a replacement policy, but buyers report no response from the seller when contacting them. Treat it as a 30-day Amazon return only.

**The Verdict**

⭐ 3.3/5 — **Buy it if** you want to save money now and are willing to gamble on longevity, or if you have a short pool season. **Skip it if** you need dependable multi-year performance — the OEM Hayward cell costs more but has a proven track record. This cell is a coin flip, and at this price, that's a bet some will happily take and others will regret.
