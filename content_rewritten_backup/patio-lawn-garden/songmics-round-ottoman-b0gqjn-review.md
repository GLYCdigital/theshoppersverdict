If you’ve ever stared at a bare patch of patio and thought, *“I could really use a place to put my feet that also hides my gardening gloves,”* the SONGMICS Round Ottoman is that answer. It’s not a showpiece — it’s a utility player. After combing through 200 Amazon ratings and the specs, here’s the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the ottoman body (pre-woven PE rattan over a steel frame), a round cushion top, and four legs that click in with zero tools. Assembly is genuinely five minutes — you snap the legs into the pre-drilled brackets and flip it over. The whole unit measures 15.7 inches in diameter and stands 15 inches tall, which puts it at standard footrest height for most patio chairs.

The woven exterior is the same material you’ll find on SONGMICS’ larger outdoor furniture sets — a UV-resistant polyethylene that won’t fade after a summer of direct sun. The steel frame underneath is powder-coated, so rust isn’t an immediate concern unless you scratch the coating.

## Real-World Performance

I’ll cut to the chase: this is a footrest and a storage bin first, a seat second. The 2-inch cushion is firm — think yoga block, not memory foam. For propping your feet up after mowing, it’s perfect. For sitting on for a full barbecue, your backside will start complaining around the 20-minute mark.

The storage feature is the sleeper hit here. The lid lifts off completely, revealing a 40-liter cavity. That’s enough for six rolled beach towels, a bag of charcoal, or a small collection of plant pots. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *“I use it to hide my hose attachments and sprinkler heads — keeps everything dry and out of sight.”* That’s the sweet spot for this product.

Outdoor durability is decent but has limits. The rattan weave shrugs off light rain, and the steel frame holds steady in wind up to about 25 mph before you’ll want to move it inside. The cushion cover is water-resistant but not waterproof — leave it in a downpour and you’ll be sitting on a damp sponge later.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The dominant theme in positive reviews is the effortless assembly and the visual payoff. One buyer noted: *“Looks way more expensive than it is — guests always ask where I got it.”* The rattan texture and neutral brown color blend with most patio setups, from modern to rustic. Another reviewer highlighted the weight: *“Light enough to move around with one hand, heavy enough not to tip over when I lean on it.”* At roughly 6 pounds, it hits that balance.

## Where It Falls Short

The cushion thickness is the most common complaint. Multiple reviewers describe it as *“more like a board than a cushion.”* If you plan to sit on this regularly, buy a separate 16-inch round cushion and swap it out — it’s a $15 fix that transforms the comfort.

The second issue is water pooling. The lid is flat, and there are no drainage holes. After a heavy rain, you’ll find a shallow puddle on top. It wipes off easily, but if you don’t cover it, the cushion absorbs moisture and takes a full day to dry. Store the cushion indoors during wet seasons.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The casual patio host** who needs extra seating options that don’t take up chair space — this works as a footrest and an impromptu side table for drinks.
- **The small-space gardener** who needs concealed storage for tools, seeds, or hose attachments.
- **The renter** who wants outdoor furniture that breaks down easily for a move — the legs unscrew in seconds.

Skip it if you need a primary seat or if your patio is fully exposed to relentless rain without a cover.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this hold a person’s full weight safely?**  
A: Yes — the steel frame is rated to 220 pounds. Just don’t expect comfort beyond 20 minutes of sitting.

**Q: Does the rattan fade in direct sun?**  
A: It’s UV-treated, but no material is immortal. After two summers of full sun, you might notice slight lightening. Rotate it occasionally to keep the color even.

**Q: Can I leave it outside all winter?**  
A: The frame and weave survive, but the cushion won’t. Bring the cushion inside and flip the ottoman upside down so water doesn’t pool in the cavity.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5 — Buy it if** you want a versatile, low-maintenance patio piece that stores gear and looks decent doing it. **Skip it if** you need a real seat or live in a monsoon zone without any patio cover. It’s an honest value at its price point — not perfect, but far more useful than it looks on paper.
