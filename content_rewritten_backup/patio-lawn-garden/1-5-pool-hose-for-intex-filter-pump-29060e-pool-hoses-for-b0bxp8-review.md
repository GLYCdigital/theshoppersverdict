## Where It Falls Short

The **3-foot length is limiting**. If your pump sits more than a few feet from your pool's wall, you'll need to extend these, which requires additional connectors or a longer single hose. This is fine for standard above-ground setups where the pump mounts right at the pool edge, but it's a real constraint for anything unconventional.

The **plastic collars are the weak point**. They'll hold up fine under normal hand-tightening, but I've seen reports of users cracking them by using pliers to force a tighter seal. Treat them like hand-tight-only fittings. Also, while the hose is flexible, it's not soft — in cold weather (below 50°F), it stiffens noticeably and becomes harder to route around tight corners.
