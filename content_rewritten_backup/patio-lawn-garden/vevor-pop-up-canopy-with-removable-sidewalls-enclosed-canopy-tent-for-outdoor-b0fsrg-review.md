# VEVOR Pop up Canopy with Removable Sidewalls Enclosed Canopy Review

**The Short Version:** A budget-friendly enclosed canopy that delivers solid weather protection for the price—if you're willing to wrestle with assembly and treat the frame with care.

---

## What You're Actually Getting

The VEVOR Pop Up Canopy arrives as a complete package: the canopy frame, a full top cover, and four removable sidewalls with zippered door and window panels. As shown in the product image, this is a true enclosed structure—not just a canopy with a couple of flaps. The frame is aluminum with steel connectors, and the fabric is a polyester weave with a PU coating rated for water resistance.

Here's what matters dimensionally: this is a 10x10-foot footprint with roughly 9.5 feet of center height. That's enough to cover a 6-foot table with chairs on all sides, or a decent-sized vendor booth.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The frame uses a standard push-button locking mechanism on the telescoping legs. It's the same system you'll find on canopies in the $300+ range, but the tolerances here are looser. The connectors feel slightly thinner than premium brands, and several buyers reported bent joints after moderate wind exposure.

That said, the fabric is genuinely good for the price point. The PU coating sheds water effectively—multiple reviewers confirmed staying dry through steady rain. The sidewalls attach via a combination of hook-and-loop strips and bungee cords. The zippers on the door and window panels are functional but not heavy-duty; they'll hold up for seasonal use but won't survive daily commercial abuse.

Setup takes two people and about 20 minutes the first time. The frame goes up quickly, but attaching all four sidewalls adds time. One person can technically do it, but you'll fight with the fabric. The included stakes are basic—upgrade them if you're using this on grass.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The value proposition is the headline. One reviewer put it plainly: "For the price, this is hard to beat. The sidewalls are substantial and the canopy held up through a summer of weekend use." Another noted the enclosed design was perfect for their craft fair setup: "The zippered door is a game-changer. I can lock up my booth overnight without breaking everything down."

The waterproofing also gets consistent shout-outs. Multiple reviewers confirmed no leaks after several hours of rain exposure.

## Where It Falls Short

The frame is the weak point. It's not flimsy, but it's not confidence-inspiring either. Several reviewers reported leg connectors bending or the crossbars bowing after moderate wind. This is a fair-weather canopy—don't leave it up unattended if there's any chance of a storm.

The carrying bag is also undersized. Once the sidewalls are packed with the frame, you'll be fighting the zipper. Several reviewers mentioned this frustration.

Finally, the assembly instructions are minimal. There's no video included, and the printed diagrams are small and hard to read. You'll likely need to watch a YouTube video or figure it out through trial and error.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You need an enclosed canopy for occasional weekend use—backyard parties, kids' sports events, craft fairs, or camping. You want four-season coverage with the sidewalls included without paying premium prices.

**Skip it if:** You're a professional vendor using this daily, you live in a windy area, or you need something that can survive being left up for extended periods. Also skip if you want quick solo setup—the sidewalls make this a two-person job.

## FAQ

**Q: Is it truly waterproof or just water-resistant?**
A: The PU coating handles moderate rain well. Several reviewers confirmed staying dry through steady showers. But it's not designed for prolonged downpours—water can pool on the top if it's not pitched with proper tension.

**Q: Can I use the canopy without the sidewalls?**
A: Yes. The sidewalls are fully removable, and the canopy works fine as an open-air shelter. The frame has the same stability either way.

**Q: How does it hold up in wind?**
A: It's rated for light wind only. Use the included stakes and consider additional sandbags or weights if there's any breeze. The sidewalls actually help by reducing wind flow through the structure, but don't push it.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 3.7/5**

The VEVOR Pop Up Canopy with Removable Sidewalls is a solid budget option that punches above its weight on waterproofing and enclosed coverage. The frame limits its long-term durability, and setup requires patience, but for the price, you're getting a functional enclosed shelter that handles most outdoor needs.

**Buy it if:** You want an affordable enclosed canopy for occasional use and understand the frame's limitations.

**Skip it if:** You need daily commercial durability or you're in a consistently windy environment.

For most backyard and recreational users, this is a smart purchase that delivers on its promises—just don't expect it to last a decade.
