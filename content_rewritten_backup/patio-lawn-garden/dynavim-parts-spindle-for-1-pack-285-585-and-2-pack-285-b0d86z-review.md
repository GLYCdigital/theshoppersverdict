## Where It Falls Short

The break-in period on the bearings is worth noting. If you're expecting a buttery-smooth spin out of the box, you'll be mildly surprised. It's not a problem — it's fresh seals and grease — but it's worth knowing before you install.

Finally, this is not a commercial-duty part. If you're running a landscape crew mowing eight hours a day, spend the extra money on OEM or heavy-duty aftermarket. This is built for residential and light commercial use, and it performs accordingly.
