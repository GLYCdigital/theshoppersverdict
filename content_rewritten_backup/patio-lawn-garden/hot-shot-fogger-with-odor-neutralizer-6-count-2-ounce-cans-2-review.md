## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about limitations. The chemical smell during fogging is strong — you absolutely must vacate for 4+ hours, including pets and plants (cover fish tanks). Some users with respiratory sensitivities reported needing longer air-out time.

More importantly, **this is not a one-and-done treatment**. Permethrin kills adult and larval stages but doesn't affect eggs. Flea pupae, roach egg casings, and bed bug eggs can survive. Expect to reapply in 2-3 weeks to catch the next generation. If you're looking for a single-treatment solution, you'll be disappointed.

Also worth noting: the aerosol cans can't be used in spaces with pilot lights or open flames. And if you have electronics you're worried about, cover them — while non-staining, the mist can settle on surfaces.

## FAQ

**Q: Is it safe around pets?**
A: No. Cats are especially sensitive to permethrin. Remove all pets, birds, and fish (cover tanks) before fogging and keep them out for the full 4-hour treatment plus air-out period.

**Q: How many cans do I need for a standard bedroom?**
A: One can covers 2,000 cubic feet — roughly a 12x12x8-foot room. For larger or open-concept spaces, use multiple cans simultaneously, placing one per 2,000 cubic feet.

**Q: Does it leave residue on furniture?**
A: The formula is non-staining, but the mist settles on surfaces. Wipe down food-contact surfaces and countertops after treatment. Wash bedding and linens that were exposed.
