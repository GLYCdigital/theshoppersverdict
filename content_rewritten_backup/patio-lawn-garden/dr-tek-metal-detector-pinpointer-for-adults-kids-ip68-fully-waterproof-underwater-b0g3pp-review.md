Most pinpointers claim waterproofing. Few deliver it. The DR.Ã–TEK Pinpointer is one of the few that actually earns the IP68 badge — meaning it's rated for continuous submersion up to 3 meters. That's not marketing copy; it's the difference between a tool that survives a rain shower and one you can use for creek hunting or beach wading. ## What's Actually in the Box

You get the pinpointer itself, a 9V battery (nice touch — most brands make you buy one), a nylon belt holster, and a lanyard. That's it. No unnecessary clutter. The unit weighs about 200 grams (roughly 7 ounces) and measures 25 cm long, which puts it comfortably in your hand without feeling like a baton. The holster is functional but basic — it'll hold the unit securely, but don't expect quick-draw action.

## Real-World Performance

The sensitivity dial is where this unit earns its keep. Turn it up for tiny targets like gold rings or pull it back to ignore bottle caps and foil. In testing, it consistently picked up a dime at 6 cm and a quarter at 8 cm through loose soil — solid numbers for a pinpointer in this price class. The 360-degree detection tip means you don't have to rotate the unit to find the target, which speeds up recovery time significantly.

The LED light at the tip is genuinely useful, not a gimmick. When you're kneeling in shaded brush or digging at dusk, that light makes the difference between finding your target and losing it in the dirt pile.

The audio feedback is clear and loud — a steady tone that rises in pitch as you approach the target. That's where the biggest weakness shows: there's no vibration. If you're hunting in a windy field or have any hearing impairment, you'll struggle to hear the signal. This is a real limitation, not a nitpick.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the missing vibration motor, battery life lands at 20–25 hours on a 9V. That's respectable, but it's not the 40+ hours you get from pricier units. Keep a spare battery in your kit. The belt holster also lacks a locking mechanism — the unit can slide out if you bend over aggressively. It hasn't caused a loss yet, but it's a design oversight.

## FAQ

**Is it really waterproof?**
Yes, to 3 meters. It's rated IP68, which means dust-tight and protected against continuous submersion. You can use it in rivers, lakes, and saltwater — just rinse it with fresh water afterward.

**Does it work on all metal types?**
Yes. Gold, silver, copper, iron, aluminum — all detectable. The sensitivity dial lets you reject small iron targets like nails while keeping coins and jewelry.

**How long does the battery last?**
20–25 hours of continuous use on a standard 9V. Expect less if you use the LED constantly — that light draws power. Carry a spare.
