Let me save you 30 minutes of reading forum threads: the Intex 1500 GPH Easy Set pump is the default answer for most above-ground pools up to 16 feet. That's not because it's perfect — it's because at this price point, nothing else matches the combination of flow, simplicity, and parts availability. I've tested it against two competitors in the same class, and here's what actually matters.

**What's in the box**

You get the pump unit itself, two 1.25-inch hoses (one intake, one return), a pair of strainer connectors, the 10-inch filter cartridges (two of them, pre-installed), and a flow diverter. There's no ground fault circuit interrupter plug — that's a notable omission for a product that sits near water. You'll want to use a GFCI outlet or add an inline adapter. The hoses are the flimsy corrugated style that kink if you bend them sharply, so plan your routing before you commit.

**Real-world performance**

The 1,500 GPH rating is honest at zero head height. Once you connect the hoses and the filter is slightly dirty, expect more like 1,100–1,200 GPH actual throughput. That's still enough to turn over a 5,000-gallon pool in about four hours, which is the standard recommendation. As one verified buyer put it: *"My 12-foot pool went from green to clear in about 36 hours with this pump running continuously."* That matches my testing — the twin cartridges do a solid job catching debris down to about 10 microns, which is fine for pollen and dust but won't catch algae spores.

**Build quality and longevity**

The motor housing is ABS plastic, and the impeller is molded rather than machined. That's typical at this price. What surprised me is the bearing quality — several long-term reviewers report 2+ seasons of daily use without noise or wobble. One buyer noted: *"Second summer with this pump, still runs like day one. I do rinse the cartridges every other day during peak algae season."* The strainer basket catches leaves before they hit the impeller, which protects the motor. The weak point is the hose threads — cross-thread them once and you'll be fighting leaks forever. Go slow when connecting.

**Where it falls short**

Three honest criticisms. First, the flow rate collapses if you add the Intex saltwater system or heater inline — you'll lose 30–40% of throughput, and the motor runs hotter. If you plan to add those later, buy the 2,500 GPH model instead. Second, the 1.25-inch hoses are proprietary. Standard 1.5-inch pool hose won't connect without an adapter, and the adapters are annoyingly hard to find locally. Third, the pump is not self-priming. If the intake hose loses prime (air gets in), you'll need to manually refill the strainer basket with water. This happens more often than Intex admits, especially if your pool water level drops below the intake.

**Who should buy this**

This is the right pump for a first-time pool owner with an Easy Set or Metal Frame pool up to 16 feet, or a seasonal pool you set up and tear down each year. It's also a good fit for someone who wants a spare pump for emergencies — it's light enough to store easily and cheap enough to replace without tears. Skip it if you have a permanent above-ground pool with 1.5-inch plumbing, if you plan to run a heater or salt system, or if you want a quiet pump for night operation — this one has a noticeable hum that carries through the house if the pool is near a bedroom window.

**FAQ**

*How often do I need to clean the filter cartridges?*
During normal use, every 3–5 days. During peak algae season, every 1–2 days. Rinse with a garden hose; replace every 4–6 weeks or when the material starts to fray.

*Can I bury the hoses?*
No. The corrugated hoses will collapse under soil pressure, cutting flow to near zero. Keep them above ground.

*Does this work with a 20-foot pool?*
Technically yes, but you'll be running it 24/7 and still fighting clarity. Better to step up to the 2,500 GPH model for anything over 16 feet.

**The Verdict**

This pump does exactly what the box promises for pools it's sized for — nothing more, nothing less. The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews is earned; it's reliable, easy to use, and the replacement parts are cheap and available at most big-box stores. The downsides are real but predictable, and every one of them can be worked around or planned for.

**Buy it if** you have an above-ground pool up to 16 feet and want a set-and-forget pump that's simple to maintain.

**Skip it if** you're building a permanent setup, adding accessories that restrict flow, or need whisper-quiet operation at night.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — a solid workhorse with a few engineering compromises you should know about before you buy.
