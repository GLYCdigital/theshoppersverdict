The pack includes multiple clear nursery pots in what Vesici calls "large sizes" — think 5-inch through 7-inch diameters, though the exact mix varies by listing. Each pot has pre-drilled drainage holes in the base, which is non-negotiable for healthy roots. You're not getting flimsy disposable cups here; these have real structural integrity.

## Performance: Seeing Is Believing

The clear walls are the headline feature, and they deliver. You can spot root development at the edges, check for soggy soil, and even catch early signs of root rot before it becomes a plant funeral. ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the clear plastic tradeoff. In bright light, you'll get algae growth on the interior walls where moisture collects. It's harmless to the plant but looks ugly. The fix is simple — use these as cachepots inside decorative containers or keep them in lower light — but it's a real quirk you should know about.

Second, no saucers are included. The drainage holes work great, which means water will run out onto your shelf or windowsill. Budget for trays if you don't already have them.

Finally, the "variety" aspect cuts both ways. Some packs include a wider size range than others, and if you have a specific pot size in mind, you might get more of one size than you wanted. Read the listing dimensions carefully before purchase.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these pots reusable after repotting?**
Yes, and that's a strength. The thick plastic holds up to repeated washing and reuse. Just scrub off any algae and they're good for another round.

**Q: Will the clear plastic harm my plant's roots?**
No, but roots that prefer darkness (like some orchids) may need the pot wrapped or placed inside an opaque cachepot. Standard houseplants are completely fine.

**Q: Do these work for succulents?**
They work, but the clear walls make it easy to see when the soil is fully dry — which is exactly what succulents need. Just ensure you're using a gritty, fast-draining mix.
