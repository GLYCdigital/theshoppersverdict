**Where It Falls Short**

No product is perfect, and this one has two honest drawbacks.

First, the hulls. Broccoli seeds have notoriously stubborn seed coats that cling to the cotyledons of young microgreens. You'll need to spend a few extra minutes rinsing and picking hulls off your harvest, especially if you're growing them to the microgreen stage. Sprouts are less affected, but for microgreens, budget some cleanup time.

Second, the packaging. The stand-up pouch doesn't have a resealable zipper. Once you open it, you'll want to transfer the remaining seeds to an airtight jar or bag. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's an annoying oversight that could affect seed viability if you're a slow grower. Store them in a cool, dark place and they'll stay viable for a year or more.
