If you need to feed a crowd of 15+ or keep food cold overnight, you'll want something bigger. The price also sits higher than generic soft coolers — you're paying for the Stanley name and the build quality that comes with it, but budget shoppers may balk.

A few users mentioned the exterior fabric shows lighter stains (think sandy or dusty environments), though it wipes clean easily enough.

## FAQ

**Does it actually hold 20 cans?** Yes, with room to spare if you're using cans. If you add ice packs or loose ice, expect closer to 14-16 cans plus food.

**Is the liner really leakproof?** Yes. The interior lining is welded and sealed — no stitches for water to seep through. Melted ice stays inside until you dump it out.

**Can I fit this as a carry-on?** It's a bit bulky for overhead bins on most airlines. Better suited for road trips and outdoor use than air travel.
