If you've ever balanced on a step ladder with a pressure washer wand in one hand, praying the hose doesn't yank you off — this 60-inch extension from RIDGE WASHER is the tool that gets you back on solid ground. It's not flashy, but it solves a genuinely annoying problem: how to clean high surfaces without risking your neck.

## What You Actually Get

The package is refreshingly simple: one 60-inch aluminum wand, a brass quick-connect fitting at the gun end, and a standard 1/4-inch male plug at the nozzle end. No adapters, no proprietary nonsense. The wand itself is thick-wall aluminum with a knurled grip section about six inches from the gun connection — a small detail that matters more than you'd think when your hands are wet and soapy.

As shown in the product image, this isn't a flimsy telescoping wand that wobbles at full extension. It's a rigid one-piece tube. That rigidity is the whole point — telescoping wands save storage space but introduce play that makes precision cleaning miserable. This thing is solid.

## How It Performs in Real Use

I tested this against a 2024 model of a major brand's pressure washer (the universal fitting clicked right in, no adapter needed). The first thing you notice is the reach — standing flat-footed, I could clean second-story soffits without a ladder. That's the headline feature, and it delivers.

The tradeoff is physics. At full 60-inch extension with a 0-degree nozzle cranked, there's noticeable flex at the tip. You won't snap it — the aluminum is genuinely thick — but you'll want a two-handed grip for anything beyond a 25-degree nozzle. One-handed operation at full pressure is doable but sketchy.

Down low, it shines differently. Washing car roofs, cleaning under deck railings, or reaching behind bushes to rinse siding — the length means you're not crouching or contorting. Multiple Amazon reviewers note the same thing; one wrote, "I'm 5'6\" and can now wash my truck's roof without a step stool." Another mentioned using it for gutter cleaning with a gutter attachment, which is a smart pairing.

Across the 229 ratings, three themes dominate:

## Where It Falls Short

The two-hand requirement at full pressure is the biggest practical limitation. If you have arthritis or limited grip strength, this wand will tire you out faster than a standard 24-inch lance.

Also, there's no swivel at the gun connection. The hose will twist if you're working in tight spaces, forcing you to stop and untangle. A swivel would've made this a no-brainer 5-star product.

Finally, at 60 inches, it's awkward to store. It won't fit in most pressure washer onboard storage clips. You'll need wall hooks or a garage corner.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my pressure washer?**
Almost certainly. If your gun accepts a standard 1/4-inch quick-connect (which includes Ryobi, Craftsman, Sun Joe, Simpson, Karcher with an adapter, and most others), it snaps right in. No tools needed.

**Q: Can I use this with an electric pressure washer?**
Yes, and it pairs especially well with electric units since their lower flow rates mean less wand whip. Gas units with 2.5+ GPM will have more flex at the tip.

**Q: Does it come with nozzles?**
No. It's just the wand and fittings. You'll use your existing nozzles from your current pressure washer.
