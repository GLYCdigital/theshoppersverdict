## Where It Falls Short

Honesty requires pointing out the tradeoffs. First, **the stainless steel grade is not marine-grade 304.** It’s a lower-cost alloy that will resist rust better than standard steel but will discolor over time with heavy use. If you grill year-round in a humid climate, expect some surface pitting eventually.

Second, **the kit is not universal.** While it fits most Performance models, Charbroil has released multiple iterations. You must verify your specific model number before ordering. A few negative reviews mention receiving the kit and finding the burner length off by an inch—this was a model mismatch, not a defect, but it’s a frustrating reality.

Finally, the hardware included is functional but basic. The screws and clips are zinc-plated, not stainless, meaning they’ll corrode faster than the main components. It’s a minor annoyance, but worth noting.
