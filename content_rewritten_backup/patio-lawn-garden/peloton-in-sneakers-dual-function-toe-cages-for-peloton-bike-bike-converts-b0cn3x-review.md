## Performance and Build Quality

The cage is molded from reinforced nylon with a metal hinge bracket, which is the right material choice here. Nylon flexes slightly under pressure instead of cracking, and the metal bracket handles the stress of repeated engagement. ## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the width issue, there are two real downsides. First, the straps can stretch slightly over months of use. It's not a dealbreaker — you just re-tighten them — but it's worth knowing if you're someone who hates ongoing maintenance. Second, the cage doesn't work well with very thick-soled shoes like hiking boots or platform sneakers; the strap doesn't get enough purchase to hold them securely.

One more thing: if you're a serious cyclist who's used to proper clip-in pedals, these won't match that level of power transfer. They're a massive upgrade over bare pedals, but there's still some flex compared to a rigid cleat system. For casual riders and fitness-focused users, that tradeoff is absolutely worth it.
