## What You Actually Get

The package contains the steel panels, corner connectors, and all necessary hardware — no tools required. You're getting a modular system that assembles by locking panels together at the corners. As shown in the product image, the bed sits directly on the ground with an open bottom, which is a significant design choice. The galvanized coating is the key feature here; it's the same material used in commercial agricultural applications, meaning this bed is built to handle years of watering, freezing, and UV exposure without rotting or warping.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The steel panels are rigid and substantial — this isn't a thin, foil-like product that flexes when filled with soil. Owners consistently mention how sturdy the bed feels once assembled. One Amazon reviewer noted, *"I filled mine with 12 cubic feet of soil and it didn't budge an inch. The metal is thick enough that I'm not worried about it bending."* That's the kind of structural confidence you want when you're investing in a permanent garden fixture.

The open bottom design is a genuine advantage over container-style beds. It allows plant roots to penetrate into the native soil below, which means healthier plants and better drought tolerance. It also preserves earthworm activity, which is critical for soil health. If you're placing this on a patio or deck, you'll want to add a landscape fabric liner underneath to prevent soil from staining the surface — that's an extra cost but a minor one.

Assembly is genuinely tool-free. The panels lock together with connectors, and most owners report completing setup in 10–15 minutes. One reviewer mentioned, *"I put this together by myself while holding a cup of coffee in the other hand. It's that easy."*

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and the ANLEOLIFE bed has a couple of honest drawbacks. First, the corners have rounded edges — which is great for safety — but if a panel gets bent during shipping, those edges can become sharp. A few reviewers noted receiving panels with minor dings that required careful bending back into place. It's not a dealbreaker, but inspect the panels immediately upon arrival.

Second, there's no included liner. For a product in this price range, that's a minor omission, but it's worth knowing before you buy. You'll want to budget for landscape fabric if you're placing the bed on a hard surface.
