## Real-World Performance

Here's where this product earns its reputation. Cal hypo shocks fast and oxidizes hard. Most buyers report visible clarification within 12-24 hours on moderately cloudy water. The unstabilized formula means you're not piling on cyanuric acid (CYA) every time you shock — a critical detail if you're fighting algae blooms that thrive in high-CYA water.

One thing buyers consistently mention: it kills algae quickly. One verified purchaser noted, "My pool went from green to swim-ready in about 18 hours. I used two bags for a 20,000-gallon pool and brushed the walls first." That's the pattern — you need to brush, then shock, then run the pump overnight. The chemical does its part, but it's not magic.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. Cal hypo adds calcium to your water. If you have a plaster pool and you're already running high calcium hardness, repeated shocking can contribute to scaling on tile and surfaces. Test your calcium hardness before you buy in bulk — especially if you have a saltwater chlorine generator pool.

The bags also aren't resealable. Each 1-pound pouch is designed for a one-time dose. If you have a smaller pool, you'll be storing partial bags, and moisture is the enemy of cal hypo. Keep it bone dry or it'll clump into concrete.

And a real limitation: this is shock treatment, not a daily sanitizer. You still need a separate chlorine source (tablets or liquid) for ongoing maintenance. Some first-time pool owners buy this expecting it to replace their regular chlorine routine — it doesn't.

## FAQ

**How often should I shock my pool with this?**
Weekly during peak swim season is standard. After heavy rain, a party, or visible cloudiness, shock immediately. For algae blooms, double-dose and brush thoroughly.

**Will this work in a saltwater pool?**
Yes, but monitor your calcium hardness. Salt systems don't like high calcium levels, so test before and after shocking.

**Is one bag really enough for 10,000 gallons?**
For routine shocking, yes. For algae blooms, plan on two bags for a 10,000-gallon pool, plus brushing.
