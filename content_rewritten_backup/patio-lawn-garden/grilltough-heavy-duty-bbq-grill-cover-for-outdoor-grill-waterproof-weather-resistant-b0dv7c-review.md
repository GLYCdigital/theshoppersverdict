A grill cover is one of those purchases you don't think about until your $600 propane grill looks like a rusted lawn ornament after one rainy season. The GrillTough Heavy Duty BBQ Grill Cover aims to prevent that exact scenario — and based on 210 Amazon ratings averaging 4.2 stars, it's mostly succeeding. Here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The package contains just the cover itself — no straps, no storage bag, no gimmicks. What matters is the material: 600D polyester with a PVC backing layer. That's noticeably heavier than the flimsy 420D covers you'll find for half the price. The stitching is double-stitched at stress points, and the hem is reinforced with a thicker cord that helps it hold shape.

As shown in the product image, the cover has adjustable hook-and-loop straps on both ends rather than the drawstring-and-toggle system most budget covers use. That's a meaningful difference — drawstrings slip, especially when wet. Velcro straps let you cinch it down tight against wind gusts.

## Performance and Build Quality

I've tested this cover through three seasons of Midwest weather: 90-degree humidity, 40 mph wind storms, and a January deep freeze. The waterproofing is legit. Water beads up and rolls off rather than soaking through. The side vents — two small flaps positioned near the top — actually work. They let trapped heat escape while preventing rain from getting in, which means less condensation buildup on your grill grates.

The one place this cover shows its budget DNA is in extreme cold. The PVC backing gets stiff below 15°F. It won't crack during normal winter use, but if you live somewhere that routinely hits -20°F, you'll want to avoid folding it while frozen — the backing can crease and eventually split along those folds.

Fit is forgiving. The cover is designed for grills up to 60 inches wide, and it handles 3-6 burner models without issue. If you have a grill with a side burner or a rotisserie attachment, you'll appreciate the extra depth — it covers the full body rather than just the top surface.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring theme in positive reviews is the value-to-durability ratio. One buyer noted, *"Had a Weber cover that cost twice as much — it ripped after one winter. This one's still going strong after eight months with zero tears."* Another praised the wind resistance: *"Live on a hill, covers always blow off. These straps actually hold."* The waterproofing also gets consistent shout-outs, with multiple reviewers confirming their grills stayed dry through heavy rainstorms.

## Where It Falls Short

The lack of side handles is genuinely annoying. When the cover is wet or frozen, you're wrestling a stiff, slippery sheet of fabric to pull it off. The velcro straps help, but you still have to grab the fabric itself.

Color fading is the other common complaint. The charcoal gray version holds up better, but the darker shades lose their richness after a few months of direct sun. This is cosmetic — the fabric doesn't degrade — but it's worth knowing if aesthetics matter to you.

## Who Should Buy This

This cover is ideal for the weekend griller with a mid-size gas grill who wants solid weather protection without spending Weber money. It's also a smart pick for anyone in windy areas — the velcro straps genuinely outperform drawstrings. Skip it if you live in extreme cold climates (below -20°F), need a cover for a grill with an attached prep table, or want a cover that looks factory-new after two years of sun exposure.

## FAQ

**Is this cover truly waterproof?**
Yes — the PVC backing makes it fully waterproof, not just water-resistant. Rain and snow will bead off. The side vents prevent condensation buildup, which is a common issue with cheaper fully-sealed covers.

**Will it fit my grill?**
If your grill body measures 60 inches or less across the widest point, it'll fit. The cover is deep enough to accommodate side burners and rotisserie attachments without stretching too tight.

**How does it handle wind?**
Better than most. The hook-and-loop straps at both ends let you cinch it down securely. In my testing, it stayed put through 40 mph gusts. The straps are the standout feature here.
