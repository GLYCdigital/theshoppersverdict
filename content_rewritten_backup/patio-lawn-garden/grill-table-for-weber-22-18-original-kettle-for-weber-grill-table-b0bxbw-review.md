If you own a Weber Kettle, you already know the struggle: you've got tongs in one hand, a plate of raw chicken in the other, and nowhere to set anything down. This grill table from the aftermarket solves that problem without modifying your grill. Here's the honest breakdown after digging through 229 ratings and the specs.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes the steel side table, a clamping bracket set, and the hardware needed to mount it. No tools required for installation — you clamp it onto the kettle's rim and tighten two knobs by hand. The table measures roughly 20 inches wide by 12 inches deep, which gives you a solid prep zone without overhanging awkwardly past the grill's footprint.

The build uses heavy-gauge steel with a matte black powder coat. It's not flimsy sheet metal — this thing has real weight to it. The mounting bracket is designed to grip the rolled rim of both the 22-inch and 18-inch Original Kettles, and it does so without scratching the porcelain finish, based on buyer reports.

## Real-World Performance

I've seen a lot of aftermarket grill accessories, and most fail on one of two fronts: flimsy construction or poor fit. This one gets both right. The clamp bites firmly — reviewers consistently mention that once it's tightened, the table doesn't wobble, even when leaning on it to flip burgers.

The fold-down feature is genuinely useful. When you're done cooking, the table pivots down flat against the kettle side, so it doesn't stick out and catch your hip as you walk past. That's a thoughtful touch that a lot of cheaper options skip.

One thing to note: the table surface has a raised lip around the edges, but it's shallow. A reviewer noted that "small items like a skewer or a bottle cap can roll off if you bump the table hard." It's not a dealbreaker, but don't expect a deep tray for corralling loose items.

## What Buyers Praise

The dominant theme in the reviews is how stable and solid it feels. One buyer put it plainly: "Feels like it came from Weber themselves. No wobble, no sag, and the powder coat matches my grill perfectly." Another mentioned the installation specifically: "Took me two minutes to clamp on. My wife thought I'd drilled into the kettle — nope, just the clamp."

Fitment across both kettle sizes gets consistent approval. Multiple owners of the 18-inch model confirmed it fits without any modification, which is worth calling out because that's the size that usually gets shortchanged by aftermarket accessories.

## Where It Falls Short

The shallow lip is the main complaint, and I'd add another: there's no accessory rail or hook system built in. If you want to hang your tongs or a grill brush on the side, you'll need to buy a separate hook attachment. The table is also not compatible with kettles that have a built-in thermometer probe on the side — the clamp would interfere.

Price-wise, it's positioned in the mid-range for aftermarket tables. You're paying a bit more than the flimsy plastic options, but less than the premium stainless steel units. For the build quality, it's a fair trade.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Weber Kettle owners who grill frequently** — if you're out there 2+ times a week, this pays for itself in convenience

## FAQ

**Will this fit my Weber 22-inch Master-Touch?**
Yes, as long as your kettle has the standard rolled rim. The Master-Touch, Original, and Premium models all use the same rim profile. If you have a Performer with the built-in table, you don't need this.

**Can I leave it on the grill in the rain?**
The powder coat holds up fine, but the clamp bolts are not stainless steel. If you live in a coastal area or leave it out year-round, apply a light coat of grease to the threads to prevent rust.

**Does it interfere with the vents on the bottom of the kettle?**
No. The clamp attaches to the top rim, and the table sits above the ash catcher level. The bottom vents remain fully accessible.
