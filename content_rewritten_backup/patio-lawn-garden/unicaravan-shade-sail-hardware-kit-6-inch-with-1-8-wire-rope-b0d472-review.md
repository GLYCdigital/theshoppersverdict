# Unicaravan Shade Sail Hardware Kit 6 inch: The Complete Rigging Solution

**The short version:** If you're hanging a shade sail and want everything in one box — turnbuckles, wire rope, thimbles, shackles — this Unicaravan kit delivers. It's the difference between a clean install and a frustrating afternoon of mismatched parts from three different stores.

## What's Actually in the Box

This kit is built around the **6-inch turnbuckles** — the workhorses of shade sail tensioning. You get:

- **Two 6" stainless steel turnbuckles** with jaw-and-eye ends (the jaw connects to your mounting point, the eye to the thimble)
- **20 feet of 1/8" galvanized wire rope** — enough for most standard shade sail corners
- **Wire rope thimbles** to protect the loop where cable meets turnbuckle
- **Shackles** for connecting to D-rings or existing hardware
- **Cable clips** to secure the wire rope ends

What's notably *not* included: mounting screws, bolts, or concrete anchors. You're buying the rigging, not the anchors.

## Real-World Performance

The **316 stainless steel** on the turnbuckles is the right call — it's marine-grade, meaning it won't pit or corrode when exposed to rain, sprinklers, or coastal humidity. The threads engage smoothly, and the 6-inch length gives you about 3 inches of adjustment range, which is plenty for dialing in tension on a typical 12x16 sail.

The **1/8" wire rope** is adequate for sails up to about 200 square feet. It's galvanized, which is fine for most backyard applications, but if you live near the ocean, expect surface rust within a year or two. One buyer noted: *"The galvanized cable started showing brown spots after six months by the beach — the turnbuckles stayed perfect, but I wish the cable was stainless too."*

Installation is straightforward *if* you've done this before. The kit assumes you know how to create a thimble loop and terminate cable with clips. First-timers should budget 30 minutes to watch a tutorial — the included instructions are one page of basic diagrams.

## Where It Falls Short

- **Galvanized cable, not stainless.** If you want a fully rust-proof setup, buy a separate stainless cable — this kit's weak link.
- **No mounting hardware.** You'll spend another $10-20 on lag bolts or concrete anchors depending on your posts.
- **Minimal instructions.** This is a DIY kit for people who know basic rigging. Beginners, bring your phone.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Backyard DIYers** mounting a shade sail over a patio or deck — this saves you three store trips
- **RV owners** rigging a sun shade at camp — the stainless turnbuckles survive travel vibration
- **Anyone replacing worn-out hardware** on an existing sail — the 6-inch turnbuckles fit standard setups

**Skip it if** you need a fully stainless system for coastal exposure, or if you want a turnkey kit with anchors included.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this kit with a 20-foot sail?**  
A: The 20 feet of cable is enough for two corners of a typical sail. For a 4-corner install, you'll need a second kit or extra cable.

**Q: Does the wire rope fit through standard sail grommets?**  
A: Yes — 1/8" cable passes through most shade sail eyelets. For tight grommets, the included thimbles keep the loop from binding.

**Q: How much tension can the turnbuckles handle?**  
A: These are rated for residential sails up to 200 sq ft. For commercial or high-wind areas, step up to a heavy-duty 3/16" system.
