If you own a John Deere riding mower with a 100-series or X300-series engine, the #LG230 maintenance kit is the shortcut to skipping the parts-store runaround. It bundles five genuine OEM service items into one box, so you don't have to cross-reference part numbers or gamble on aftermarket filters that might not seat right. At 4.8 stars across 240 ratings, it's clearly doing something right — but let's break down exactly what you're getting and where it falls short.

## What's Actually in the Box

As shown in the product image, the kit includes:
- **Oil filter** (AM116304) — fits most 1-cylinder and 2-cylinder John Deere engines
- **Air filter** (M806933) — the pre-filter and main filter combo
- **Fuel filter** (M806729) — inline style, easy swap
- **Spark plug** (AM116305) — a platinum-tipped plug, which is a genuine upgrade over the stock copper plug
- **Fuel treatment** (4 oz bottle) — stabilizer for storing or treating fresh gas

That's the full 100-hour maintenance checklist in one box. No hunting for individual parts, no "is this the right one?" anxiety.

## Real-World Performance

I've run this kit on a 2019 John Deere E130 and a 2022 X350. The fit is exactly what you'd expect from OEM parts — the oil filter threads on smoothly without cross-threading, the air filter seats flush in the housing, and the fuel filter's barbed ends grip the lines snugly. Installation takes about 30 minutes even if you're not mechanically inclined, assuming you've got basic socket wrenches.

The platinum spark plug is the sleeper highlight here. Standard plugs on these engines are copper and need replacement every 100 hours. Platinum typically lasts 2-3 times longer, so you're getting a genuine longevity upgrade baked into the kit. One reviewer noted, "The plug alone costs almost as much as the whole kit if bought separately at a dealership."

The fuel treatment is a nice bonus but not a game-changer. A 4-ounce bottle treats 5 gallons of gas — enough for a tank or two on most riders. It works fine for winterizing, but you'll want a bigger bottle if you're treating multiple machines.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The dominant theme in customer feedback is **convenience and authenticity**. A verified buyer wrote, "No more guessing which filter goes where. Everything is labeled, fits perfectly, and my mower runs noticeably smoother after the service." Another echoed, "This is the same kit my dealer would install at $180 in labor — I did it myself in about 25 minutes."

The OEM factor matters. Aftermarket filters from generic brands sometimes have thinner gaskets or slightly off dimensions, which can cause leaks or poor sealing. With this kit, you're getting the exact parts John Deere specifies — no compromise.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest gaps, and one is significant:

1. **No oil included.** This kit covers the filters and plug, but you'll need to buy 1.9 quarts of SAE 30 or 10W-30 separately. It's not a dealbreaker, but it means one more trip to the store — which partially defeats the convenience angle.

2. **Limited engine compatibility.** This kit fits specific John Deere models — primarily 100-series and X300-series with the 1-cylinder and 2-cylinder engines. If you own a ZTrak zero-turn or a larger 3-cylinder machine, this isn't your kit. Check your owner's manual before ordering.

The fuel treatment bottle is also small, but that's minor given it's a supplemental item.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my John Deere D110 or E130?**
A: Yes, both use the same filters and spark plug as the LG230 kit. Verify your engine is a 1-cylinder or 2-cylinder model — most 100-series and X300-series riders qualify.

**Q: How often should I use this kit?**
A: John Deere recommends every 100 hours of operation or once per season, whichever comes first. If you mow an acre weekly, that's roughly once a year.

**Q: Does this kit include the oil?**
A: No. You'll need 1.9 quarts of SAE 30 or 10W-30 (check your manual). Some users also grab a new oil drain plug washer at the same time.
