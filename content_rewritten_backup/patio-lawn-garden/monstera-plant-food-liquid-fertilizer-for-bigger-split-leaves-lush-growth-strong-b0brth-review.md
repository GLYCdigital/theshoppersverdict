## FAQ

**Q: How often should I fertilize my monstera?**
A: Every 2-4 weeks during spring and summer. Stop completely in winter when growth slows. Over-fertilizing causes brown leaf tips.

**Q: Can I use this on my other houseplants?**
A: Yes, but dilute it further—half strength—for smaller plants like pothos or spider plants. The 3-2-4 ratio is safe for most tropical houseplants.

**Q: Will this fix my monstera’s yellow leaves?**
A: Only if the yellowing is from nutrient deficiency. If it’s from overwatering or root rot, fertilizer will make it worse. Check your watering habits first.
