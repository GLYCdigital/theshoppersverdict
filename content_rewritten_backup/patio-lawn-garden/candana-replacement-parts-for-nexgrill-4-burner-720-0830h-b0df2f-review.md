If your Nexgrill 720-0830H is four or five years old, you already know the drill: the burners corrode, the heat tents rust through, and suddenly your $400 grill is a sad metal box that won't hold even heat. The CANDANA replacement kit is designed to fix exactly that — and based on 229 Amazon ratings averaging 4.6 stars, it's doing the job for a lot of people.

## What's Actually in the Box

The kit arrives with four stainless steel burners, four heat tents (flavorizer bars), and the drip shields — essentially everything you need to rebuild the firebox of your 720-0830H. The burners are the H-style loop design matching the OEM geometry, with the same length and port spacing. The heat tents are V-shaped stainless, slightly thicker than the originals.

One thing I verified: these are not universal "fits many models" parts with flimsy adapters. They're drilled and bent specifically for the 720-0830H frame. The mounting tabs line up with the existing screws, and the gas valve orifices align with the burner inlets without shims.

## Real-World Performance

The most common failure point on Nexgrill burners is the crossover — the narrow section between the loops where flame travels from the igniter to light the full burner. CANDANA reinforced this area with a heavier gauge than stock, which directly addresses the #1 complaint in the reviews.

One buyer put it simply: *"These are actually better built than the factory parts. The metal is thicker and the welds are cleaner."* Another noted, *"Fit was perfect. Took about 30 minutes to swap everything out and my grill gets way hotter than it has in years."*

Flame distribution is even across all four burners, and the heat tents are doing their job — no hot spots or warping after several cooks. The stainless is 430-grade, which is what you'll find on most grills in this price class. It's not magnetic 304, but it's a clear upgrade over the painted cold-rolled steel Nexgrill uses on some components.

## Where It Falls Short

The connectors where the burner slides onto the gas valve are tight — a few reviewers mentioned needing to tap them with a rubber mallet. That's not a defect; it's actually good news because it means no gas leaks. But if you're not handy, budget 45–60 minutes for the swap rather than 20.

Also, this kit does **not** include the ignition wires or electrode. If your igniter is dead (a common simultaneous failure on older grills), you'll need to order that separately. And while the drip shields are included, the grease tray pan itself is not.

## Who Should Buy This

- **DIY grill owners** with a 720-0830H showing uneven flames or rusted burners — this is your most cost-effective fix.
- **Anyone quoted $150+** for a repair shop to replace OEM parts. This kit runs a fraction of that and installs with basic hand tools.

Skip it if your grill frame is also rusted through or the gas valves themselves are failing — you need a new grill, not parts.

## FAQ

**Q: Will these fit my Nexgrill 720-0830H if I bought it in a different year?**
A: Yes, the 720-0830H model number reflects the frame design, which hasn't changed across production years. Confirm your model number is stamped on the lid or the grease tray before ordering.

**Q: Do I need to replace all four burners at once?**
A: You should. If one corroded, the others are close behind, and mixing old and new burners creates uneven heat distribution.

**Q: Are these compatible with natural gas conversion?**
A: Only if you already have the NG conversion orifices from Nexgrill. CANDANA's burners are drilled for propane flow rates, so you can't just bolt them onto an NG setup without changing orifices.
