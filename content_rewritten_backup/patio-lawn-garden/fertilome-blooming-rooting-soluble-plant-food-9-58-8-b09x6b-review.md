### Where It Falls Short (Honestly)

**It will burn your plants if you guess at the dose.** This is 58% phosphorus — that’s not a typo. If you use a heaping scoop instead of a level tablespoon, you’ll see leaf tip burn within 48 hours. Measure carefully.

**It’s not a complete fertilizer.** The nitrogen (9%) and potassium (8%) are low by design. This is a *supplement*, not a standalone diet. If you feed exclusively with this, your plants will eventually show nitrogen deficiency (yellowing lower leaves). Use it *alongside* a balanced feed, not instead of one.

**The bag isn’t resealable.** The foil pouch is sturdy but once opened, moisture will clump the powder. Transfer it to an airtight jar immediately or you’ll be chiseling chunks out by month two.

### FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Can I use this on all my plants?**
A: No. Use it on flowering and fruiting plants (roses, tomatoes, peppers, orchids, hibiscus). Do *not* use it on seedlings, lettuce, spinach, or any plant where you harvest the leaves — the low nitrogen will stunt vegetative growth.

**Q: How often should I apply it?**
A: Every 2-3 weeks during the active growing/blooming season. Do not apply during winter dormancy. For container plants, alternate with a balanced 20-20-20 feed to prevent nitrogen lockout.

**Q: Is this safe for hydroponics?**
A: Yes, but it’s not ideal. It's designed for soil and soilless mixes. In a hydro system, it will drop pH quickly. If you must use it, check pH daily and expect to adjust with pH Up.
