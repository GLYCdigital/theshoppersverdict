If you're already invested in DEWALT's 20V MAX ecosystem — the yellow-and-black drill, impact driver, or circular saw sitting in your garage — this trimmer and blower kit is the logical next step. But if you're starting from scratch with a half-acre lot, keep reading, because there's a catch.

**What's Actually in the Box**

DEWALT ships this as a complete kit (model DCST972X1 + DCBL720P1): the 13-inch string trimmer with a 5-in-1 head, the handheld leaf blower, two 5Ah 20V MAX batteries, a dual-port charger, and the shoulder strap. No hidden accessories to buy — the trimmer includes the edger guide, and the blower has its concentrator nozzle pre-installed. The trimmer weighs 8.2 pounds with the battery, which is manageable but not featherlight. The blower is a svelte 4.5 pounds.

**Real-World Performance**

The brushless motor on the trimmer is the headline feature. It spins the .080-inch line fast enough to chew through standard Bermuda and fescue without bogging down — as long as the grass is dry. One Amazon reviewer put it bluntly: *"Cuts great until the grass is wet, then it's a fight."* That tracks. This is a 20V tool, not a 60V FlexVolt, so it's built for weekly maintenance, not jungle reclamation.

The bump-feed head is genuinely well-designed. You tap it on the ground while running, and line advances in small increments — no more yanking out six inches of line at once. The 5-in-1 head also rotates 90 degrees for edging, which works but requires you to flip the trimmer sideways and hold it at an awkward angle. It's functional, not ergonomic perfection.

The blower moves 90 CFM at 110 MPH. That's enough for dry leaves on a driveway, grass clippings off a walkway, or dusting out the garage. It's not enough for wet, matted autumn leaves — you'll be there all afternoon. The lack of a variable-speed trigger is the bigger annoyance. It's full blast or nothing, which makes precision work like clearing flower beds unnecessarily aggressive.

**Battery Life: The Honest Numbers**

DEWALT claims 40+ minutes of runtime per 5Ah battery. Real-world testing and buyer feedback consistently land closer to 25-30 minutes of continuous trimming. The blower drains a battery in about 20 minutes at full throttle. With two batteries in the kit, you can cycle through roughly an hour of combined work before everything's dead. For a quarter-acre lot, that's cutting it close. For anything larger, budget for additional batteries or a faster charger.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The build quality gets near-universal approval. One reviewer summed it up: *"Typical DEWALT toughness. I've dropped it, banged it against fences, and it keeps going."* The brushless motors are also a favorite — no brushes to replace, more torque, and they run cooler than brushed alternatives. The dual-battery charger is another win, letting you top off both packs simultaneously.

**Where It Falls Short**

Beyond battery life, the trimmer's cutting width is a modest 13 inches — narrower than many competitors' 15-16 inch decks. That means more passes to finish the same yard. The blower's noise level (75dB) approaches gas-powered territory, so ear protection isn't optional. And the edger function, while included, feels like an afterthought. The handle doesn't rotate, so you're twisting your wrist the entire time.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **DEWALT tool owners** with a quarter-acre or smaller lawn who want one battery platform for everything
- **DIYers** who need a trimmer for fence lines and a blower for patios — not commercial-grade work
- **Cordless converts** who hate gas engines and don't want to deal with fuel stabilizer or carburetor cleaning

**Skip It If**

- You have a large property with thick, wet grass — get the 60V FlexVolt version instead
- You need a blower for serious fall cleanup — this won't handle heavy leaf loads
- You're not already in the DEWALT ecosystem — comparable Ryobi or Craftsman kits cost less

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use my existing DEWALT 20V batteries from other tools?**
Yes, any 20V MAX battery works. The included 5Ah packs are the sweet spot — 2Ah batteries will drain in 10 minutes.

**Q: How long does it take to charge the batteries?**
About 60-90 minutes per battery with the included dual-port charger. A faster 6A charger is sold separately.

**Q: Does the trimmer use standard replacement line?**
Yes, any .080-inch line works. The bump feed head is compatible with universal replacement spools.

**The Verdict: 4.2/5 — Buy It If You're In the Ecosystem**

**Buy it if:** You already own DEWALT 20V tools and want a competent, well-built trimmer and blower for routine yard maintenance. The brushless motors and included batteries make this a solid value for suburban lots.

**Skip it if:** You need serious power for a large property or heavy leaf cleanup. This kit is a dependable lightweight, not a heavyweight. For that, look at the 60V FlexVolt line or a gas model.

At 4.4 stars from 220 ratings, buyers are mostly satisfied — the gripes center on battery life and wet-grass performance, both of which are inherent to the 20V platform. It's a good kit, honestly priced, and it'll serve you well for years of weekly trimming. Just know its limits before you buy.
