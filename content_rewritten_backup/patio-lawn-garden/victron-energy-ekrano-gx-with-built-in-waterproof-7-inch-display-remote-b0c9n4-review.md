Let's be direct about what the Victron Energy Ekrano GX actually is: a 7-inch waterproof touchscreen that turns your Victron power system into something you can actually see and control. It's not cheap, it's not a toy, and it's not for everyone. But if you've already invested in Victron's ecosystem, this display might be the missing piece.

## What You Actually Get

The Ekrano GX ships with the display unit itself, a mounting bracket kit, and the necessary cables. The 7-inch IPS panel runs at 1024×600 resolution — sharp enough for reading voltage numbers from across a cockpit. The IP67 rating is the headline feature here; the entire unit is sealed against dust and water ingress. You can mount this on an open helm, spray it with a hose, or leave it in a downpour without a cover.

## Real-World Performance

The touchscreen responds well, even with slightly wet fingers — a genuine concern with cheaper marine displays. The interface mirrors the familiar Victron Remote Console layout, so if you've used the VRM portal or the phone app, you'll find your way around immediately. You get live data from every connected device: battery state of charge, solar input, inverter load, generator status, and shore power draw.

One thing that impressed me: the screen brightness. At full brightness, this thing is readable in direct sunlight. That's not a given in this price range — many competitors wash out by noon.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews reflects real satisfaction. One reviewer put it simply: *"Finally a display that doesn't need a bimini top to read."* Another noted the setup time: *"Plugged it into my Cerbo GX and it just worked — no configuration nightmare."*

Multiple buyers mention using it as a full system replacement for the older Color Control GX, and the speed improvement is noticeable. The Ekrano GX boots faster and navigates more smoothly.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The price is the big one. You're paying a premium for the marine-grade waterproofing and Victron branding. A ruggedized Android tablet running the Victron Remote Console app would do 80% of this job for half the money — though it wouldn't survive the same punishment.

The Wi-Fi-only connectivity is another limitation. If you want remote monitoring on the boat when you're away, you'll still need a separate LTE router or hotspot. For a device at this price point, that feels like a miss.

Also, the mounting hardware is functional but not elegant. The included bracket works, but the unit sticks out about an inch from the mounting surface. Flush-mounting requires additional parts.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're running a Victron system on a boat, RV, or off-grid setup and want a permanent, weatherproof display at your primary control station. You'll get the full GX ecosystem — remote VRM access, inverter control, generator autostart, and solar tracking — in a package that won't die from a wave splash.

**Skip it if:** You're on a tight budget, you don't need a permanent display (your phone works fine), or you're running a non-Victron power system. This is an ecosystem product — it doesn't make sense outside the Victron world.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work without a Cerbo GX?**
A: No. The Ekrano GX is a display for the GX ecosystem — it needs a Cerbo GX, Venus GX, or compatible Victron device to talk to your batteries and inverters.

**Q: Can I mount this outside on an open boat?**
A: Yes, that's the whole point of the IP67 rating. Direct spray and rain won't hurt it. Just don't intentionally dunk it.

**Q: Does it have Bluetooth?**
A: No. Network connection is Wi-Fi only, with an Ethernet port on the back for a wired connection. Bluetooth requires an additional Victron dongle.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The Ekrano GX is the best Victron display Victron has made — full stop. It's rugged, bright, and deeply integrated with the ecosystem. The price is steep, but if you're already running a Victron system, this is the display you've been waiting for.

**Buy it if:** You want a permanent, waterproof command center for your Victron setup and value the peace of mind a sealed marine display provides.

**Skip it if:** You're cost-sensitive, or you're fine using your phone and the free VRM app. The Ekrano GX is a luxury, not a necessity — but it's a very good luxury.
