**Title: BUBLUE Bubot 800P Robotic Pool Cleaner Review: 2.9-Star Reality Check**

**Meta Description:** BUBLUE Bubot 800P robotic pool cleaner review: 2.9/5 rating. Does the suction match the hype? Real buyer feedback on performance, build, and value.

---

I'll be straight with you: a 2.9-star average across 150 ratings is a red flag for any product, let alone a $300+ robotic pool cleaner. But numbers alone don't tell the whole story. Some shoppers love the Bubot 800P. Others have had it fail within weeks. Here's what you actually need to know before spending your money.

**What You Get**

The Bubot 800P is a cordless robotic pool vacuum with a claimed 8,000 Pa of suction power. In the box you get the unit itself, a charging dock, a power adapter, and a filter basket. There's no wall-mounted caddy or extra filter included — that's $30-40 in accessories you'll likely want to budget for.

The unit is compact, weighing just over 8 pounds, which makes it easy to lift out of the water. The charging dock is straightforward: drop the bot on it, wait 3-4 hours, and you get roughly 90 minutes of cleaning time per the specs.

**Performance: The Good and the Ugly**

When it works, it works. The 8,000 Pa suction is genuinely strong — several buyers report it picking up fine sand, small pebbles, and debris that their previous pool vacs left behind. One reviewer noted: "It cleaned my 20-foot above-ground pool floor in about 45 minutes and the filter was packed with dirt." That's the experience Bubue is selling.

The climbing capability is also real. It scales pool walls and handles the waterline with reasonable competence, though it's not as aggressive as a Dolphin or Hayward unit at three times the price.

**Where It Falls Short**

Here's where the 2.9-star rating makes sense. The most common complaints cluster around reliability and navigation:

- **Random navigation pattern.** This thing does not clean systematically. It bounces around like a Roomba from 2012. Multiple reviewers mention it spending 10 minutes in one corner while ignoring the rest of the pool. You'll often need two cycles to get full coverage.
- **Filter clogging.** The filter basket is small and clogs quickly with fine debris. One buyer said: "I had to stop and clean the filter three times during a single 40-minute run." That's a dealbreaker if you have a lot of leaves or fine silt.
- **Build quality concerns.** Several buyers report units dying within 1-3 months — charging issues, motors stopping, or the unit simply refusing to turn on. Customer service response is described as slow and unhelpful in multiple reviews.
- **Charging time vs. run time.** At 3-4 hours to charge and only ~90 minutes of run time, you'll be waiting around if you have a larger pool.

**Who Should Buy This**

The Bubot 800P is for the budget-conscious above-ground pool owner who's willing to babysit their cleaner. If you have a small pool (under 20 feet), don't mind occasional manual spot-cleaning, and want to save $200-400 versus a premium robotic cleaner, this can work.

**Who Should Skip It**

If you have a large in-ground pool, expect set-it-and-forget-it operation, or need something that'll last more than a season, skip this. The 2.9-star average exists for a reason. A corded suction-side cleaner like the Pentair Kreepy Krauly will be more reliable at a similar price point.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it work on in-ground pools?**
A: Technically yes, but the random navigation and short run time make it impractical for pools larger than 30 feet. Stick with above-ground or small in-ground pools.

**Q: How often do you need to clean the filter?**
A: Expect to rinse the filter every 30-45 minutes of operation, depending on debris load. It's quick to clean, but it's a real interruption.

**Q: What's the warranty situation?**
A: BUBLUE offers a 1-year warranty, but multiple buyers report difficulty getting replacements. Keep your receipt and document any issues early.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You have a small above-ground pool, a tight budget, and you're willing to babysit the cleaning cycle. It does deliver strong suction when it's working.

**Skip it if:** You want reliability, systematic cleaning, or a product that'll last multiple seasons. The random navigation and reported failure rates are too risky at this price point.

**Rating: 3.3/5** — The suction is genuinely impressive and the price is attractive, but the navigation flaws, filter maintenance demands, and reliability concerns keep this from being a confident recommendation. You're gambling on getting a good unit.
