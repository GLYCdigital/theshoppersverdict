If you're staring at a backyard that's become a kiddie-pool graveyard of punctured vinyl and warped frames, the INTEX 26700EP is the upgrade that actually holds up. This 10-foot by 30-inch Prism Frame pool sits in INTEX's premium line, and the difference isn't just cosmetic — though the grey basket-weave exterior is a welcome departure from the neon blue of cheaper models. With 215 ratings averaging 4.3 stars, it's clearly doing something right. But let's talk about what that rating doesn't tell you.

**What's in the box**

You get the pool body itself, the powder-coated steel frame with T-joints, a 1,000 GPH cartridge filter pump, the ground cloth, a pool cover, and a ladder — though the ladder is the compact A-frame style that works fine for kids but feels flimsy for any adult over 150 pounds. The pump requires two connections and about ten minutes to set up. Missing from the box: any sand, leveling equipment, or a pump timer. Budget for those separately.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The Prism Frame line uses a 3-ply sidewall construction with a laminated interior that resists punctures better than the single-layer pools INTEX sells at the low end. The steel frame is powder-coated to resist rust, and the T-joint connectors snap together without tools. In practice, this means the pool holds its shape even when partially drained — a common failure point on cheaper frames that start bowing after a couple of weeks.

Water capacity is around 2,000 gallons, which takes roughly 4-6 hours to fill depending on your hose pressure. The included pump circulates the water adequately for light use, but here's the honest truth: if you have more than two kids splashing around daily, the cartridge filter will need cleaning every 3-4 days, and you'll still see surface debris. Several reviewers mention upgrading to the larger INTEX pump almost immediately. One owner wrote: *"The pump that came with it kept the water clear for about a week, then I was cleaning the cartridge every other day. Swapped in the 2,500 GPH model and it's been set-and-forget since."*

**What buyers consistently praise**

The most common positive thread in reviews is the build quality relative to price. Owners repeatedly note the frame feels solid — not like a toy. The grey exterior also gets frequent mentions for looking intentional in a backyard rather than like a plastic eyesore. Ease of assembly is another recurring theme: most people report 45-60 minutes for two people, and one reviewer noted, *"I put this together solo while my wife watched. The instructions are actually clear, which is rare."*

**Where it falls short**

Ground prep will make or break your experience. The included ground cloth is essentially a tarp — it won't protect against roots or rocks. You need a perfectly level surface, and INTEX's own manual recommends sand. That's an extra $30-50 and a few hours of labor that the listing photos don't show. Also, be aware the pool cover is a basic solar-style cover that won't survive a full winter if you leave it out. And the ladder, as mentioned, is functional but not built for adults.

**Who should buy this**

This pool is ideal for families with kids aged 3-10 who want a real swimming pool experience without the permanence (or cost) of an in-ground installation. It's also a strong choice for renters — it disassembles and moves without leaving damage. Skip it if you're looking for a lap pool, expect to host adults, or live somewhere with harsh winters where you'd need to fully disassemble and store it annually.

**FAQ**

*Do I need a pump with this, or is it included?*  
Included — a 1,000 GPH cartridge pump. It's sufficient for basic use but upgrade if you plan heavy daily use.

*Can I leave it up year-round?*  
The frame and liner tolerate a mild winter, but the water must be drained below the skimmer, and you'll want a more robust cover. Freeze-thaw cycles will eventually degrade the liner.

*What's the actual setup time?*  
Expect 1-2 hours total including ground prep. The pool assembly itself is under an hour, but leveling the ground takes most people the rest of the time.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a durable, good-looking above-ground pool for kids that assembles in an afternoon and will survive multiple seasons with basic care.

**Skip it if:** You're on a tight budget (the add-ons — sand, a better pump, a sturdier ladder — push the real cost up 20-30%) or you expect adult-sized lounging capacity.

At 4.2/5, this is a solid, honest product that delivers exactly what it promises: a premium-feeling backyard pool at a mid-range price. Just budget for the upgrades.
