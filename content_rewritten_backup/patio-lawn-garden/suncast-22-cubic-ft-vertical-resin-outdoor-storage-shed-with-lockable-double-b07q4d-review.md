If you've got a pile of garden tools leaning against the garage wall and a hose that's perpetually tangled, the Suncast 22 Cubic Ft. Vertical Resin Shed is the kind of product that makes you wonder why you waited so long. But it's not perfect. Here's the real story after digging through 220 ratings and buyer experiences.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the pre-formed resin wall panels, a steel-reinforced double door, the roof cap, and all the plastic hardware clips. There's no floor included — you'll want to set it on pavers, concrete, or gravel. Assembled, it stands about 77 inches tall and measures roughly 44 inches wide by 21.5 inches deep. That footprint is compact enough for a narrow side yard or balcony corner.

The standout feature in the product images is the side pass-through slot for a garden hose. You coil the hose inside, feed the end through the opening, and close the door. No more dragging a tangled hose out of a full shed.

**Performance in the real world**

The resin is thick and UV-stabilized, so it won't fade, crack, or warp the way cheaper plastic sheds do. Owners in rainy climates report the interior stays dry *if* you seal the seams. One reviewer noted: *"After the first rain, I found water pooling inside near the door. A tube of silicone caulk fixed it permanently."* That's the single most common complaint, and it's worth knowing before assembly.

The lockable doors are a genuine security upgrade. The hasp accepts a standard padlock, and the doors themselves are steel-reinforced, so they don't flex or bow like hollow resin doors. That said, this is a 22-cubic-foot shed — it'll secure a chainsaw or fertilizer, not a $4,000 lawn tractor.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The hose pass-through is the runaway favorite feature. Multiple reviewers call it "genius" and say it's the reason they chose this model over cheaper alternatives. The vertical design also earns consistent praise for maximizing storage in small spaces — you can hang rakes and shovels on the interior walls and still have floor space for a leaf blower and potting soil.

Assembly time averages 2-3 hours for two people. One buyer cautioned: *"The instructions are mostly pictures, and some hole alignment is off by a quarter inch. It's manageable, but don't expect a 20-minute snap-together job."*

**Where it falls short**

The depth is the biggest limitation. At 21.5 inches deep, you can't store a standard wheelbarrow or a bulk bag of topsoil laid flat. It's a vertical shed, so think "tall and skinny" storage, not "garage in a box." The plastic hinge pins also feel less durable than the rest of the construction — handle the doors gently during assembly to avoid snapping them.

**Who should buy this**

- **Small-yard homeowners** with no garage or a full garage who need organized tool storage
- **Renters** who want a portable, no-permit storage solution (it's lightweight enough to move)
- **Gardening enthusiasts** who want the hose pass-through convenience and lockable security for pricier equipment

**Skip it if** you need to store wide or bulky items, or if you expect it to be 100% waterproof without any DIY sealing.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I leave this outside year-round in snow?**
A: Yes, the resin is cold-resistant, but clear snow off the flat roof to prevent ice buildup. The roof is sloped enough for rain runoff.

**Q: Does it come with a floor?**
A: No. You'll need to level the ground and place it on pavers, a concrete pad, or treated lumber. This also helps with drainage.

**Q: Will a standard garden hose fit through the pass-through?**
A: Yes, a standard 5/8-inch hose fits easily. A 3/4-inch commercial hose will be tight but workable.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need compact, lockable, low-maintenance storage for hand tools, hoses, and lawn chemicals, and you're comfortable spending an afternoon assembling it with a tube of caulk handy.

**Skip it if** you need to store wide equipment or want a truly weatherproof structure right out of the box.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A feature-forward shed with real utility, docked half a star for assembly frustrations and seam sealing requirements. For the price, it's a solid investment for organized outdoor storage.
