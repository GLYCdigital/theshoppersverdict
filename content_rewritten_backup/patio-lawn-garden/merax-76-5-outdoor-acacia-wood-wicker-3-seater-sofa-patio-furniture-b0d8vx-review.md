**Opening: What you're actually getting for the money**

The Merax 76.5" Outdoor Acacia Wood Wicker 3-Seater Sofa sits in that awkward middle zone of patio furniture: priced below premium brands like Polywood or Hanamint, but well above the flimsy $200 sets that fall apart after one season. The question is whether it delivers enough durability to justify the jump. After digging through 210 ratings and the spec sheet, here's the honest picture.

**What's in the box**

You get the three-seat sofa frame, three back cushions, three seat cushions, and all necessary hardware. The frame combines a solid acacia wood sub-structure with a hand-woven resin wicker exterior — a smart pairing that gives you the natural stability of wood with the weather resistance of synthetic wicker. The cushions come in a neutral beige tone that'll work with most outdoor decor schemes. As shown in the product image, the silhouette is clean and modern, with slightly angled arms that make it feel more contemporary than a standard boxy patio sofa.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

Here's where Merax earns its keep. The acacia wood frame isn't just decorative — it's the load-bearing structure, and acacia is naturally dense and rot-resistant, which matters if you live somewhere with humidity or regular rain. The resin wicker is UV-stabilized, so it won't fade into that sad gray color within two summers like cheaper polyethylene blends.

Seat depth measures 24 inches, which is genuinely generous. I'm 6'1", and my knees don't jut out awkwardly. The 5-inch thick cushions are firmer than they look — supportive for sitting upright with a drink, but soft enough that you won't feel the frame through them after an hour.

Assembly is manageable. The frame comes in two halves that bolt together, and the legs screw on with pre-drilled holes. Plan for 45–60 minutes with a second person holding pieces steady. One buyer noted: *"Took about 40 minutes with my husband. The instructions were actually clear, which surprised me."*

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.2-star rating reflects genuine satisfaction. Common themes in positive reviews:

- **Sturdiness**: Multiple buyers mention it feels more solid than expected for the price. One wrote: *"I was worried it would be flimsy like other wicker sets I've owned. This thing doesn't budge when my 200-pound uncle sits down."*
- **Comfort**: The deep seats and cushion thickness get consistent shout-outs. *"Finally a patio sofa I can actually nap on,"* said one reviewer.
- **Looks**: The acacia-and-wicker combo photographs well and looks more expensive than it is.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be direct about the tradeoffs.

First, the cushions are not waterproof. The covers have zippers and are machine-washable, but the foam core will soak up moisture if you leave the sofa uncovered through a rainstorm. You'll need to bring cushions inside or invest in a cover. This is standard at this price point, but it's not disclosed clearly in the listing.

Second, the exposed metal joining brackets aren't rust-proofed. In coastal climates or areas with heavy winter salt, these will develop surface rust within a year. A quick coat of rust-resistant spray paint before assembly solves this cheaply, but it's an extra step you shouldn't have to take.

Third, at approximately 80 pounds, it's on the lighter side for a three-seater. It won't tip over, but on uneven patio stones you may get a slight wobble. Level the surface or use shims.

**Who should buy this**

- **First-time patio owners** who want a solid, good-looking sofa without dropping $1,500+ on premium brands
- **Renters** who need something substantial but portable enough to move
- **Entertainers** who host outdoor gatherings and need comfortable seating for multiple adults
- **Skip it if** you live in a heavy-rain climate without covered patio space, or if you want a zero-maintenance set that can stay out year-round

**FAQ**

**Q: Are the cushions really removable for washing?**
A: Yes, all six cushions have zippered covers that are machine-washable on a gentle cycle. Air dry only — machine drying will shrink them.

**Q: Can this sofa stay outside all winter?**
A: The frame can, but not the cushions. If you don't have covered storage, take the cushions inside or wrap the entire sofa in a waterproof cover rated for outdoor furniture.

**Q: How much weight can it hold?**
A: The frame is rated for roughly 300 pounds per seat position, but realistically you'll seat three average adults comfortably. The acacia wood handles weight well; the concern is more about cushion compression over time.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a well-built, comfortable three-seater that looks far more expensive than it is, and you're willing to manage the cushions during rain.

**Skip it if:** You need a true leave-it-outdoors-year-round solution or you're shopping for a coastal environment without being prepared to apply rust protection.

At 4.2 stars across 210 ratings, the Merax delivers exactly what it promises: solid value, respectable durability, and real comfort. It's not a heirloom piece, but it's a smart purchase for the price.
