**The AIPER Cordless Automatic Pool Vacuum is the kind of product that makes you wonder why you didn't buy one sooner — until it doesn't work.**

That's the honest tension here. At 3.6 stars across 180 ratings, this cleaner has a clear split personality. Some buyers report it transforms pool maintenance into a two-minute chore. Others describe it dying after a handful of uses. The truth, as usual, sits somewhere in the middle — and it depends heavily on your pool type, debris load, and expectations.

**What you actually get**

The box includes the cordless vacuum unit, a charging adapter, a filter basket, and a small instruction manual. The unit itself is compact — roughly the size of a robotic lawn mower but lighter, at under 10 pounds. As shown in the product image, it's a clean, low-profile design that sits flat on the pool floor when submerged.

The headline feature is Auto-Park. When the battery runs low or the cleaning cycle finishes, the unit drives itself to the pool's edge and surfaces at the waterline. That means you reach in, grab the handle, and pull it out — no pole, no net, no snorkeling for a dead robot at the bottom of your deep end. It's a genuinely thoughtful touch that solves the most annoying part of cordless pool cleaning.

**Real-world performance**

On flat-bottomed above-ground pools, the AIPER does its job. It crawls across the floor in a semi-random pattern, sucking up dirt, sand, and small debris into the filter basket. The claimed 90-minute runtime is optimistic in practice — most users report 60-75 minutes of effective cleaning before the Auto-Park kicks in. For a typical 15-20 foot round above-ground pool, that's usually enough to cover the floor once.

Battery life degrades over time, though. Multiple reviewers note reduced runtime after a few months of regular use. That's not unusual for cordless pool gear, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The Auto-Park feature earns genuine enthusiasm. One reviewer put it plainly: "The auto-park is a game changer. I used to dread getting the vacuum out because it meant wrestling with hoses. Now I just drop it in, walk away, and grab it from the edge when it's done."

Cordless convenience is the other big win. No tangled hoses, no connecting to the skimmer, no pump required. It's a true grab-and-go tool.

**Where it falls short**

This is where the 3.6-star rating makes sense. The filter basket is small — you'll be emptying it mid-cycle if your pool has anything beyond fine dust. Heavy debris like leaves or acorns will clog it fast, and the unit doesn't have the suction power to handle large organic matter anyway.

Durability is the bigger concern. A meaningful chunk of the negative reviews describe units that stopped charging, lost suction, or completely died within the first few weeks. The quality control seems inconsistent. If you get a good unit, it's great. If you don't, you're dealing with returns and replacements.

Also, this is strictly a floor cleaner. No wall climbing, no waterline scrubbing. If you need those features, look elsewhere.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the above-ground pool owner who wants to skip the weekly hose-vacuum chore without dropping $500+ on a robotic cleaner. It's ideal for smaller pools with light-to-moderate debris — think dust, pollen, and fine sand. If you have a large in-ground pool or heavy leaf fall, this isn't your machine.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does the battery actually last?**
A: Expect 60-75 minutes of real cleaning, not the advertised 90. The Auto-Park function triggers before the battery fully drains, so the actual runtime is slightly less than the stated maximum.

**Q: Can it climb walls or clean steps?**
A: No. This is a floor-only cleaner. It won't tackle walls, steps, or waterlines. Keep that in mind if your pool has significant algae buildup on surfaces.

**Q: How do I clean the filter basket?**
A: The basket lifts out from the top of the unit. Rinse it with a hose or dunk it in a bucket of clean water. For fine debris, let it dry completely before tapping it out to avoid dust clouds.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You have an above-ground pool under 20 feet, deal with light-to-moderate floor debris, and want the convenience of cordless operation with the Auto-Park retrieval feature. The price-to-convenience ratio is solid for this use case.

**Skip it if:** You have a large or in-ground pool, heavy leaf coverage, or need wall and waterline cleaning. Also skip if you're not prepared to deal with potential quality control issues — the durability complaints are too frequent to ignore.

The AIPER is a 3.7-star product: genuinely convenient when it works, frustrating when it doesn't. For the right pool owner, it's a worthwhile upgrade over manual vacuuming. For everyone else, it's a gamble.
