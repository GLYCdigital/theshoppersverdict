Let me be blunt: pool filter cartridges are the most boring purchase you'll make all summer. But getting it wrong means a green pool and a wasted weekend. I've tested the POOLPURE PLFPA100N against the OEM Hayward PA100N, and here's what actually matters.

## What You're Actually Getting

The box contains one cartridge — nothing else. No gasket, no O-ring, no manual. That's worth knowing before you order, because if your old cartridge's gasket is worn, you'll need a separate trip to the hardware store.

The cartridge itself measures roughly 9.75 inches in diameter and 7.75 inches in height, with a 2-inch opening. Filtration area is 50 square feet — the exact spec of the OEM part. The polyester mesh is pleated into 72 folds, which is what gives you that surface area in such a compact footprint.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The first thing I noticed was the end caps. They're rigid ABS plastic, not the flimsy polypropylene you see on some generics. The center core is reinforced, so it won't collapse under pressure even if you skip cleanings longer than you should.

In testing, the filter dropped into a Hayward pump housing without any forcing. The locking ring engaged smoothly, and there was no water bypass around the edges — that's the #1 issue with cheap aftermarket filters, and it's simply not present here.

Water clarity after 48 hours was comparable to the OEM cartridge. One reviewer noted: "I couldn't tell the difference between this and the Hayward one I replaced. Crystal clear water, same pressure gauge readings." That matches my experience — the pressure differential stayed within 1 PSI of the OEM part.

## Where It Falls Short

Let me be honest about the downsides.

**No gasket.** I mentioned this above, but it bears repeating. If you're replacing a filter that's been in service for years, the old gasket may be flattened or cracked. Budget $5–8 for a replacement if you're not sure.

**Shipping compression.** Some units arrive with the pleats slightly crushed from being packed tightly. This resolves within a few hours of water flow, but it's worth inspecting on arrival. If the end caps are cracked (rare, but reported), contact support immediately.

**Not for commercial use.** This is a residential-grade filter. If you're maintaining a high-bather-load pool or running 12+ hours daily, you'll want the OEM part for its slightly higher burst strength.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my Hayward pump?**
A: If your current filter is a PA100N, PA100N-4, Unicel C-7487, or Filbur FC-0644, yes. It's a direct drop-in. Measure your old cartridge if you're unsure — 9.75" diameter, 7.75" height.

**Q: How often should I replace this filter?**
A: With normal residential use (4–6 hours daily), plan on replacement every 1–2 seasons. Clean it monthly with a hose sprayer to extend its life.

**Q: Can I clean this filter with acid?**
A: Yes, a mild muriatic acid solution (10:1 water-to-acid) works for removing scale and oils. Rinse thoroughly afterward. Don't use a pressure washer — it can tear the mesh.
