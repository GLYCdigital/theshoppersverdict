**Bottom line: The Macrout plastic storage shed delivers exactly what it promises — a weatherproof, compact storage solution for under $200 that won't rot, rust, or need painting. For the price, it's a solid value, but it's not without a few quirks.**

## What You Actually Get

The box includes the resin panels, a molded plastic floor, double doors, two interior shelves, and all necessary hardware. No tools beyond a Phillips screwdriver are required. The floor is a genuinely useful addition — most sheds in this price range skip it, forcing you to build a gravel base or lay pavers. Here, you get a complete unit that sits level on any flat surface.

Dimensions run roughly 51 inches wide, 28 inches deep, and 71 inches tall. That's enough for a two-wheeled trash can, gardening supplies, or patio cushions — not a riding mower. Know that going in.

## Real-World Performance

The waterproofing is the headline feature, and it holds up. The resin panels overlap with rubber gaskets between seams, and the sloped roof sheds water effectively. One reviewer noted: *"We've had torrential downpours and everything inside stayed bone dry. The floor is a game-changer — no more moisture wicking up from the ground."*

Assembly is genuinely manageable. Most buyers report 30–45 minutes solo. The panels snap together with a cam-lock system that's forgiving for beginners. A few reviewers mentioned needing to re-seat panels mid-build, but nobody called it frustrating. The double doors swing wide for easy access, and the magnetic catches keep them closed in wind.

The resin material is UV-stabilized, so it won't yellow or become brittle after a season of sun exposure. It's also essentially maintenance-free — a hose-down keeps it looking new.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The floor, the waterproofing, and the size-to-price ratio come up repeatedly in reviews. One buyer summed it up: *"Perfect for our narrow side yard. Fits between the house and fence with room to spare. Everything stays dry, even after a hurricane."* Another noted the value: *"Tried building a wooden shed — cost three times as much and took a weekend. This took an hour and looks better."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the weak points. There's no hasp for a padlock — just a small hole where you can add one yourself. If you're storing expensive equipment, plan on a trip to the hardware store.

The two included shelves are rated for light-to-moderate loads. They'll hold bags of soil or small hand tools, but don't stack heavy power tools on them. The resin itself is durable, but the shelf brackets are the limiting factor.

Finally, the panels feel slightly flexible before assembly is complete. This is normal for resin sheds, but if you're used to metal or wood construction, it might feel concerning. Once all screws are tightened, the structure firms up considerably.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You need a compact, weatherproof storage solution for patio furniture, gardening tools, or trash bins. You want something that installs in under an hour without foundation work. You're done with painting and maintaining wooden sheds.

**Skip it if:** You're storing a riding mower or large equipment — you need something bigger. You require security-grade locking. Or you prefer the rigidity of metal construction.

## FAQ

**Will this hold up in snow?**
The sloped roof sheds light snow, and the resin handles freezing temperatures. For heavy snow, clear it after major storms — the roof isn't designed for extreme accumulation.

**Can I assemble this alone?**
Yes, but having a second person helps for the first 10 minutes while attaching the floor to the back panel. After that, it's a solo job.

**Does it need a concrete base?**
No. The included floor sits directly on level ground. For maximum stability, you can anchor it through the pre-drilled holes in the floor.

## The Verdict

The Macrout plastic storage shed earns a **4.6/5**. It's not perfect — the missing lock hasp and moderate shelf capacity are real limitations. But for the price, you get a genuinely waterproof, low-maintenance storage solution with a floor that most competitors charge extra for. If your needs fit its compact footprint, this is one of the best value picks in the category.
