## Build Quality and Real-World Use

The standout feature is the drainage. Each pot has pre-drilled holes in the base, which sounds basic but is shockingly rare in budget planter sets. You won’t need to break out a drill or risk cracking the plastic — just add a saucer underneath and water freely.

The finish is a smooth matte texture, not the high-gloss shine that shows every fingerprint. It hides water spots and dust between cleanings. The pots are also lightweight enough to move around when empty, though they hold their shape well with damp soil inside — no bowing or bulging at the sides.

One design choice to note: **there are no attached saucers.** If you’re the type to water generously and walk away, you’ll need to buy drip trays separately. This is the single most common complaint in the reviews, and it’s worth planning around.
