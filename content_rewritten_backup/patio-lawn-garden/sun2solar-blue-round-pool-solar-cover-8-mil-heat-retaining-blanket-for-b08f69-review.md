# Sun2Solar 8 Mil Round Pool Solar Cover: Heat Without the Hype?

A solar cover is the cheapest upgrade you can make to an above-ground pool. The math is simple: every 10°F of extra water temperature buys you weeks of swim season. But not all bubble wraps are created equal — and the Sun2Solar 8 Mil sits in a crowded market of cheap 4-mil sheets that shred within weeks.

After digging through 210 verified owner ratings (4.2 stars), here's the real story on whether this blue blanket earns its keep.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get one circular sheet of blue, 8-mil, double-bubble polyethylene. Cut to fit standard above-ground pools from 12' to 24' diameter — measure your pool's width before ordering, because Sun2Solar sizes these by exact foot increments. The cover arrives folded in a thin cardboard box, so expect crease lines that take a day in the sun to puff back out. There's no reel, no tie-downs, no storage bag. It's just the blanket.

## Real-World Heat Performance

Here's where this cover earns its keep. The double-layer bubble design traps a cushion of air between the water surface and the sheet — that's what actually slows evaporative cooling. Owners consistently report water temps jumping 5-10°F within two days of full sun exposure. One reviewer summed it up: *"Pool went from 68°F to 78°F in three days. The kids stopped complaining."*

The 8-mil thickness matters more than most shoppers realize. Budget covers at 4-5 mils tear at the handles or split along creases within weeks. The Sun2Solar holds up better — though not forever. Multiple long-term owners note the perimeter seam (where the top and bottom layers are heat-sealed) can separate after a full season, especially if you drag it across concrete instead of lifting it.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

- **Chemical savings:** The cover blocks UV light, which destroys chlorine. Several owners report cutting their chemical additions by a third.
- **Debris control:** Leaves and grass clippings stay on top instead of sinking to the bottom. Skimming takes 30 seconds instead of 30 minutes.
- **Water retention:** Evaporation drops by 90%+ when the cover is on. That matters if you're on a well or paying for water.

## Where It Falls Short

Honest downsides, because there are always downsides:

1. **UV longevity is limited.** This is a *summer* cover, not a winter cover. Leave it on through fall and the sun will embrittle the plastic — expect cracking within 8-12 months of constant exposure. Plan to replace it every season or two.
2. **Bubble collapse.** Where the cover folds in storage, the bubbles flatten permanently. It still floats and retains heat, but those sections look wrinkled and transfer heat slightly less efficiently.
3. **No wind protection.** It's not weighted or strapped. A gusty day can flip it or blow it off the pool entirely. You'll want to weight the edges with pool noodles or clips.
4. **Handling is awkward.** A 24-foot round cover is heavy when wet. One person can manage it, but two make it far easier to fold and remove.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have an above-ground pool and want a cost-effective way to extend your swim season by 3-4 weeks. You're okay replacing it every 1-2 seasons and willing to handle it carefully.

**Skip it if:** You want a 5-year investment (buy a thicker, UV-stabilized cover instead). You have a large in-ground pool — the round sizing won't fit rectangles or ovals. Or you want a winter-grade cover that can survive snow load.

## FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: How long does it take to heat the pool?**
A: In full sun, expect a 5-10°F rise within 48-72 hours of leaving the cover on during daylight. Cloudy stretches slow that down significantly.

**Q: Does the blue side face up or down?**
A: Blue side up. The dark blue absorbs solar radiation; the bubble side down traps the heat against the water.

**Q: Can I cut it to fit an irregular pool?**
A: Yes, scissors cut it cleanly. Just leave a 2-inch border around the edges to account for water movement and keep the cover from riding up the walls.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5**

The Sun2Solar 8 Mil is a solid middle-tier solar cover that does exactly what it promises: retain heat, reduce evaporation, and cut chemical costs. It's not the toughest cover on the market, and you'll likely replace it after a season or two of hard use. But at this price point, the heat gain you get pays for itself in the first month of extended swimming.

**Buy it if** you want summer warmth without a heater. **Skip it if** you need something that survives years of abuse — this one's a season-to-two-season performer, not a forever solution.
