## Where It Falls Short

No tool is perfect, and the WOTOW has honest limitations:

- **The chain breaker is narrow.** It works fine on standard 9/10/11-speed chains, but wider e-bike chains or 12-speed setups can be tight. If you ride a modern 12-speed drivetrain, test this before relying on it.
- **The tool is heavy.** At 220 grams, it's not the lightest option. Weight-conscious riders might prefer a lighter, less complete tool for race day.
- **The case is basic.** It comes in a simple nylon pouch that won't survive years of abuse. Plan on replacing the storage or just carrying the tool loose in a bag.
- **The 8mm hex is short.** Removing crank arms or pedals requires significant leverage, and the short arm on this tool makes that a struggle. It's a backup option, not a shop-grade tool.
