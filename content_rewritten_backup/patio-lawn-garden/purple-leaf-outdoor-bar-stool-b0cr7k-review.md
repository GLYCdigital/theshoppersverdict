If you’re shopping for outdoor bar seating, you’ve probably seen the PURPLE LEAF name pop up. With 240 ratings and a 4.8-star average, this stool is clearly doing something right. But is it actually worth your money, or is it just another mid-priced patio product riding on good marketing? I dug into the specs, the build, and what real owners are saying. Here’s the straight story.

**What you actually get**

The box contains one fully assembled stool — no confusing hardware bags or multi-step puzzle. The frame is rust-resistant aluminum wrapped in a dark brown PE rattan weave. The seat height sits at 18.5 inches, which pairs correctly with standard 42-inch outdoor bar counters. The backrest curves gently, and there’s a footrest bar at a comfortable height (about 7 inches off the ground) that actually works for taller folks — not just a decorative afterthought.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The aluminum frame is the standout feature here. It’s lightweight (11 lbs) but doesn’t wobble when you shift your weight. The rattan weave is tight and uniform — no loose strands or gaps you’d expect at this price point. One owner noted: *“I left it out through a full Midwest winter and it looks exactly like it did on day one. No rust, no fading.”* That tracks with the powder-coated frame and UV-stabilized weave.

The seat cushion, though — that’s the weak link. It’s a 2-inch foam pad with a basic polyester cover. It’s fine for a 30-minute coffee or a casual drink, but don’t plan on lounging for hours. And despite the “outdoor” label, the cushion isn’t waterproof. If you leave it exposed to a heavy downpour, it will absorb moisture and stay damp for a day. Owners consistently recommend storing the cushions indoors or investing in a cover.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the reviews, three things keep coming up:  

- **Ease of assembly** — *“Took me 8 minutes with a single Allen wrench. Everything lined up perfectly.”*  
- **Stability** — *“I’m 6’2” and 230 lbs. This stool doesn’t creak or shift even when I lean back.”*  
- **Appearance** — *“Looks way more expensive than it is. Guests always ask where I got them.”*  

The 4.8 rating isn’t inflated. Most complaints are about the cushion, not the stool itself.

**Where it falls short**

The cushion is the obvious tradeoff — thin and not waterproof. Also, these don’t stack. If you’re buying four or six for a small balcony, you’ll need dedicated storage space during the off-season. And while the 18.5-inch height is standard, double-check your bar counter’s clearance. If you have a 40-inch counter, your knees will hit the underside — measure before you buy.

**Who should buy this**

- **Homeowners with a covered patio** — the rattan and frame will last, and you can keep cushions dry.  
- **Renters** — lightweight and easy to move; no permanent installation needed.  
- **Anyone replacing cheap plastic stools** — this is a clear upgrade in both looks and durability.  

Skip it if you need waterproof cushions, want stackable storage, or have a non-standard counter height.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can these be left outside year-round?**  
A: The frame and rattan can. The cushions cannot — they need to be stored or covered during rain and snow.

**Q: What’s the weight capacity?**  
A: The frame is rated for 300 lbs. The cushions will flatten sooner under heavier use, but the structure itself holds firm.

**Q: Do they work for a standard kitchen island?**  
A: Only if your island is 42 inches tall. If you have a 36-inch kitchen counter, these will feel too tall — measure first.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a solid, good-looking outdoor bar stool that assembles fast and won’t rust or fade. **Skip it if** you need waterproof cushions or stackable storage.

The PURPLE LEAF Outdoor Bar Stool earns a **4.6/5** — a genuinely reliable pick for the price, with one obvious flaw (the cushion) that’s easy to work around.
