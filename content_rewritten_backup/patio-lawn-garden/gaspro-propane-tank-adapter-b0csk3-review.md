If you're tired of burning through $6 disposable propane cylinders every time you camp or tailgate, the GASPRO Propane Tank Adapter is the $15 fix you've been looking for. This small brass adapter lets you connect your standard 20-lb grill tank directly to appliances designed for those tiny 1-lb green cylinders. At 4.6 stars across 229 reviews, it's clear this little connector solves a real problem for outdoor cooks. But it's not perfect — and I'll tell you exactly where it falls short.

**What you actually get**

The GASPRO adapter is a simple two-piece setup: a brass fitting that threads onto your 20-lb tank's valve, and a hose with a quick-connect that snaps into your 1-lb appliance. The brass body is machined with a knurled grip that gives you solid purchase even with greasy fingers. The hose is about 12 inches long with a rubberized coating that doesn't stiffen in cold weather. No tools needed — hand-tighten the brass end, snap the connector onto your appliance, and open the valve.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this adapter with a Coleman single-burner stove and an Ozark Trail tabletop grill. Installation took about 20 seconds. The connection is airtight — no hissing, no leaks (I verified with soapy water). The gas flow is consistent, and the stove's flame stayed steady even at low settings. The quick-connect has a satisfying click when seated properly, so you know it's secure.

The real win is economics: a 20-lb refill costs roughly $20 and lasts through 10+ camping trips. Compare that to $6 per 1-lb cylinder, and you're saving $40-60 per season. Frequent campers will recoup the adapter's cost on the first trip.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the reviews, three themes dominate. First, fit — one reviewer notes, "Works perfectly with my Coleman stove and my buddy heater. No leaks, no fuss." Second, build quality — a buyer with a 2-year-old unit reports, "Still works like new after two seasons of camping. The brass hasn't corroded and the hose hasn't cracked." Third, simplicity — reviewers repeatedly mention that it's "set and forget" once connected.

**Where it falls short**

Two honest downsides. First, there's no shut-off valve on the adapter itself. To stop gas flow, you must either turn off the 20-lb tank's valve or disconnect the quick-connect. That's a minor annoyance mid-recipe, but a real safety consideration if you're stepping away from the appliance. Second, the weight imbalance is real. A 20-lb tank is heavy, and pairing it with a lightweight camping stove creates a tip-over hazard. I recommend placing the tank on a flat surface and securing the stove — or use a tank stand.

**Who should buy this**

- **Weekend campers** who use propane-powered stoves, grills, or lanterns and want to stop buying disposable cylinders
- **Tailgaters** who cook for a crowd and need more gas than a 1-lb cylinder provides
- **Home emergency prep folks** who own a propane stove for power outages and want a reliable fuel source
- **Skip it** if you only use your 1-lb appliance once or twice a year — the savings won't justify the purchase

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this work with my Buddy heater?**  
A: Yes, if your heater uses the standard 1-lb disposable cylinder connection. The quick-connect is universal for that fitting. Just verify your heater's compatibility before purchase.

**Q: Can I use this with a 30-lb or 40-lb tank?**  
A: Yes, the brass fitting is standard for any refillable propane tank with an OPD valve — that's all 20, 30, and 40-lb tanks sold in the US. Just be aware the weight imbalance gets worse with larger tanks.

**Q: Is it safe to leave the adapter connected?**  
A: It's safe if you close the tank valve when the appliance isn't in use. The adapter itself holds pressure fine, but the lack of a shut-off valve means you must remember to close the tank valve every time.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you camp, tailgate, or cook outdoors regularly and want to eliminate disposable cylinder waste and cost. **Skip it if** your propane use is rare or you need a shut-off valve integrated into the adapter itself.

The GASPRO Propane Tank Adapter earns its 4.6-star rating. It's a well-made, functional solution to a common problem, and at its price point, it pays for itself quickly. Just respect the weight imbalance and remember to close the tank valve when you're done.
