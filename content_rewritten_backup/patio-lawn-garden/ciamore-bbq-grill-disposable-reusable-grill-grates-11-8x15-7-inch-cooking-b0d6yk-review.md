Let's be blunt: most "disposable grill grates" are flimsy aluminum sheets that tear before your burgers hit medium-rare. The ciAmore BBQ Grill set takes a different angle — it bundles three reusable stainless steel grates with three disposable foil sheets in the same 11.8x15 inch footprint. That's six cooking surfaces for the price of a decent spatula. Here's who should actually buy them.

## What's actually in the box

You get six pieces total. Three reusable grates made from stainless steel with a raised 7-inch cooking surface — these sit above your existing charcoal or gas grill grates, creating a second tier. Three disposable foil grates match the same dimensions. The 11.8x15 inch size fits most standard charcoal kettles (like a 22-inch Weber) and many gas grills, but the real trick is that you can trim both materials with household scissors to custom-fit round or oddly shaped grills. No tools needed.

## Real-world performance

The reusable stainless grates are the standout. The raised design creates a temperature buffer zone — perfect for two-zone cooking where you want sear marks without the char. Because they sit 7 inches above your primary grates, you can use them for gentle indirect heat or as a warming rack that actually has surface area, not just thin bars. I tested them over lump charcoal and they held their shape, didn't warp, and cleaned up with a standard grill brush.

The disposable foil sheets are serviceable for what they are. They're great for camping, tailgating, or when you don't want to scrub anything after cooking fish or marinated chicken. They're NOT indestructible — one reviewer noted they "work fine but you have to use a plastic spatula or they tear." That tracks. The foil is thin enough to conform to your existing grates, which helps with heat distribution, but it's not a replacement for cast iron.

## What buyers consistently praise

The versatility is the recurring theme across the 229 ratings. One verified buyer wrote: "Used these on my Weber kettle and they fit perfectly after a quick trim. The reusable grates are now my go-to for vegetables that would fall through the regular grates." Another mentioned using the disposables for a beach cookout and "just threw them away after — zero cleanup." The trim-to-fit flexibility gets mentioned repeatedly, and the fact that you get both options in one purchase means you're not locked into one cooking style.

## Where it falls short

The foil disposables are the weak link. They're genuinely disposable — one-time use, and you'll want to layer them over a solid surface, not expect them to support a whole brisket. The stainless grates have no handles, which is annoying when they're hot — bring tongs or heat-resistant gloves. And there's no storage solution included; the reusables will rattle around loose in your garage unless you supply your own bag.

## Who should buy this

- **Weekend charcoal grillers** who want a second cooking tier without buying a dedicated warming rack
- **Campers and tailgaters** who want the option of zero-cleanup cooking without committing to pure disposables
- **Vegetable-heavy grillers** who lose asparagus and pepper strips through standard grates
- **Apartment dwellers** with portable grills who need a custom-fit solution

Skip it if you exclusively cook on a gas grill with porcelain-coated grates and never feel the need for a second level — you're paying for features you won't use.

## FAQ

**Will these fit my Weber 22-inch kettle?**
Yes, with a quick trim. The 11.8x15 inch sheets are slightly oversized for the kettle's round cooking surface, but both the steel and foil cut easily with scissors. Several reviewers confirmed this exact fit.

**Can I wash and reuse the disposable foil grates?**
Technically yes, the foil can be wiped clean and reused once or twice. But they're designed for single use — washing bends the edges and weakens the structure. The stainless ones are the reusable option.

**Are these safe on a gas grill?**
Yes. The stainless grates sit above your existing grates and won't interfere with gas burners. The foil disposables also work fine on gas, just keep the heat at medium to avoid the foil breaking down.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a flexible, two-tier cooking setup for your charcoal grill and appreciate having both disposable and reusable options in one package. **Skip it if** you need heavy-duty grates that can handle aggressive flipping with metal tools, or if you never cook on a round kettle grill.
