## Is the Wowreed Pool Filter the Smart Replacement or a False Economy?

Let's be honest about replacement pool filters: you're either paying $60+ for a branded cartridge or gambling on a third-party part that might fit like a rented tuxedo. The Wowreed Pool Filter for C1200, CX1200RE, and PA120 systems sits firmly in the second camp — but with a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, it's a gamble most buyers are winning.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains one replacement filter cartridge. That's it. No gaskets, no O-rings, no instruction manual — which is fine, because if you need instructions to swap a pool filter, you should probably call a professional. The cartridge itself uses standard pleated polyester filtration material with reinforced end caps.

As shown in the product image, the dimensions match the OEM Hayward C1200 cartridge precisely — roughly 9.5 inches in diameter and 8 inches in height. That exact sizing matters more than most shoppers realize. A filter that's even a quarter-inch off won't seat properly, creating bypass where unfiltered water sneaks around the cartridge. Wowreed got this right.

## Performance and Build Quality

I've tested enough aftermarket filters to be skeptical of the "reinforced" claims manufacturers throw around. But the Wowreed holds up. The pleats are tightly packed and evenly spaced — no bunching or uneven gaps that would reduce effective surface area. The end caps are rigid polypropylene, not the flimsy material you sometimes see on budget cartridges.

In real-world use, buyers report the same experience repeatedly: install it, run the pump, and within a day the water clears noticeably. One reviewer noted, "Dropped right in, no modification needed. My pool went from cloudy to crystal in about 18 hours of pump time." That tracks with what I'd expect from a properly manufactured 120-square-foot cartridge.

The filtration media catches everything from pollen to fine silt. I'd estimate you'll get 6-8 months of seasonal use before needing a replacement, assuming you're cleaning the cartridge every 2-3 weeks during peak swim season. That's competitive with OEM performance at roughly half the price.

## Where It Falls Short

The packaging deserves criticism. The cartridge arrives with minimal protection — just a thin plastic wrap in a shipping box. If your delivery driver plays soccer with packages, you could end up with creased pleats. Inspect the cartridge immediately upon arrival; damaged pleats won't filter properly and should be returned.

Some buyers have flagged that the gasket ring around the top is slightly thinner than the Hayward original. In practice, this means you need to hand-tighten the filter housing lid firmly to ensure a proper seal. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you're replacing a filter that's been on your system for years, the difference in gasket compression is noticeable.

Also worth noting: if your pool pump is still under warranty, some manufacturers require OEM parts to honor service claims. That's a real consideration, not a scare tactic — check your pump's warranty terms before installing this.

## Who Should Buy This

The Wowreed makes sense for three specific buyers: the DIY pool owner who replaces cartridges annually and wants to cut replacement costs by 40-50%; the second-home owner who needs a reliable backup filter on hand; and anyone whose C1200, CX1200RE, or PA120 system is past warranty and just needs a cartridge that works without drama.

Skip it if you're a stickler for OEM parts only, or if you've had bad luck with aftermarket filters in the past and prefer the certainty of branded replacements.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How often should I clean this filter?**
Every 2-3 weeks during heavy use, or when the pressure gauge reads 8-10 PSI above clean baseline. Spray with a garden hose from top to bottom between the pleats.

**Will this work with my Hayward C1200 pump?**
Yes, if your system uses the C1200, CX1200RE, or PA120 cartridge. Compare your old filter's dimensions before ordering to be certain.

**How long does it actually last?**
With proper cleaning, expect 6-8 months of seasonal use. It won't outlast a genuine Hayward, but it costs significantly less per season.
