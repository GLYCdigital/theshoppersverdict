# OHOME Christmas Wrapping Paper Review: Jumbo Sheets That Actually Cover Big Gifts

You know the Christmas Eve panic. The bike won't fit in a standard roll. The oversized storage bin defies every fold. You end up wrapping three separate pieces and hoping the recipient doesn't notice the tape seam running down the middle.

OHOME's jumbo wrapping paper sheets solve that specific problem. Each sheet measures 30 by 40 inches — roughly double the coverage of a standard roll's width — which means most awkward gifts get wrapped in one clean piece. No patchwork. No visible joints. Just a smooth, professional-looking finish on gifts that normally fight back.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes 12 jumbo sheets in six coordinating designs, so you get two of each pattern. That's 100 square feet of wrapping material total. The designs lean traditional — reds, greens, snowflakes, plaids — and each sheet is printed on both sides, effectively doubling your pattern options.

The paper itself is 80gsm stock. For reference, standard drugstore wrapping paper runs 40-50gsm. That extra thickness matters when you're wrestling a 30-inch sheet around a corner or pulling it taut over an irregular shape. It resists the dreaded corner-tear that ruins cheaper rolls.

## Real-World Performance

The double-sided printing is the standout feature here. Flip a sheet over and you get a completely different pattern without buying another pack. That's genuinely useful when you're wrapping multiple gifts and want visual variety on the tree.

Customer feedback backs up the quality claims. One verified buyer noted, "The paper is thick enough that you can't see through it, which is a problem I've had with cheaper brands." Another praised the size: "Finally a product that wraps my large canvas art prints without taping two pieces together."

## Where It Falls Short

Be honest about the tradeoffs before you buy.

**No cutting grid.** The back of each sheet is blank. On a standard roll, those grid lines guide your straight cuts. Here, you'll need a ruler, a cutting mat, and a steady hand — especially if you're wrapping multiple gifts and want consistent sizing.

**Shipping creases.** These sheets arrive folded, and heavy crease lines can be stubborn. On darker solid patterns, the creases show through after wrapping. Ironing on low heat helps, but that's an extra step you shouldn't have to take.

**Not ideal for small gifts.** If you're wrapping a book, a candle, or a gift card, this is overkill. You'll waste most of a 30x40 sheet. You're paying a premium for jumbo coverage you won't use on small items.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You wrap large, awkward gifts regularly — sporting equipment, home decor, framed art, kids' toys. You also appreciate the double-sided patterns for variety without buying multiple packs.

**Skip it if:** Your gifting is mostly small items, or you prefer the convenience of a rolled paper with cutting guides. The per-square-foot cost here runs higher than budget rolls, so it only makes sense if you actually need the jumbo size.

## FAQ

**Is this paper suitable for beginners?**
The thickness actually helps novices — it's forgiving when you pull it tight and won't tear as easily as thin paper. But the lack of grid lines means you'll need to measure manually. If freehand cutting isn't your strength, grab a ruler.

**Can I use these sheets for other crafts?**
Absolutely. The 80gsm stock works for table runners, gift tags, drawer liners, or even a quick tablecloth for a kids' crafting station. The double-sided patterns give you two looks for projects.

**How does it compare to standard wrapping paper rolls?**
It's thicker and wider, which means cleaner results on large gifts. It's also more expensive per square foot. For big items, it's worth the premium. For everyday small gifts, a standard roll is more economical.
