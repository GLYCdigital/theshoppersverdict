The under-sink cabinet is where good intentions go to die. You buy the organizer, swear you'll keep it tidy, and three weeks later you're spelunking for a bottle of drain cleaner behind a pile of old sponges. The HOMEBOSILY Under Sink Organizer attempts to fix this with a simple premise: two sliding drawers that pull out so you can actually see what you own.

**What you actually get**

The package contains two stackable wire drawers, four support poles, and the sliding track assembly. The frame measures roughly 15.7 inches wide by 11.8 inches deep, with each drawer standing about 5.5 inches tall. The whole unit sits on four adjustable feet that let you level it on uneven cabinet floors — a small touch that matters more than you'd think when you're dealing with a sink base that's never perfectly flat.

The wire construction is coated in a matte finish that feels more premium than the glossy chrome you see on cheaper racks. It's not going to win any design awards, but it won't look out of place under a modern kitchen sink either.

**Performance and real-world use**

The standout feature is the ball-bearing slides. They're genuinely smooth — even loaded with a full complement of cleaning supplies, the drawers roll out with one finger. That's the difference between an organizer you actually use and one you abandon after a week.

Assembly takes about eight minutes. The poles snap into the drawer frames with a satisfying click, and the whole thing feels solid once locked together. There's no wobble when the drawers are extended, which is a common failure point on cheaper units.

One thing to know: the two drawers are not the same height. The top drawer is shallower, designed for smaller items like sponges, scrub brushes, and dish soap. The bottom drawer is deeper, meant for taller bottles. If you have a lot of tall spray bottles, measure them before you buy. Anything over 10 inches tall won't fit standing up.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews reflects real satisfaction. One reviewer noted, "I was skeptical about the price, but the slides are shockingly smooth. My husband was impressed and he never notices organization products." Another said, "Finally, I can see everything under my sink. I bought a second one for the bathroom."

The most common compliment centers on the sliding mechanism and the space efficiency. Multiple reviewers mentioned that the vertical stacking freed up floor space they didn't realize they were wasting.

**Where it falls short**

The depth is the biggest limitation. At 11.8 inches, this won't work in every cabinet. If your sink's plumbing — the P-trap and the drain lines — protrudes more than a few inches from the back wall, the drawers won't clear it. Measure your cabinet depth and the plumbing obstruction before ordering.

Some users also reported that the bottom drawer can scrape against cabinet doors that swing inward. The adjustable feet help, but if your hinges push the doors into the cabinet opening, you may need to shim the organizer forward.

**Who should buy this**

This is for you if you have a standard 15-inch or wider under-sink cabinet, you're tired of digging for supplies, and you want something that installs without drilling or mounting. It's ideal for renters who can't modify cabinets. It's also a solid choice for bathroom vanities — the compact footprint works well in those tight spaces.

Skip it if you have a deep cabinet with lots of vertical space you'd rather use with a taller, multi-shelf unit. Or if you store mostly oversized bottles and jugs that won't fit in a 5.5-inch drawer.

**FAQ**

*Will it fit around my garbage disposal?*
Probably not. The disposal unit and its wiring take up significant space and typically require a dedicated cutout or a different organizer style. This works best in cabinets without a disposal, or where the disposal is offset to one side.

*Can I use it for non-cleaning items?*
Yes. The drawers work well for plastic wrap, foil, trash bags, and even small kitchen gadgets. Some reviewers use theirs for pet supplies and craft storage.

*Does it come with a warranty?*
HOMEBOSILY offers a standard 12-month warranty. Customer service responses are generally quick, though a few reviewers noted slight delays during peak seasons.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a tool-free, space-efficient organizer that actually slides smoothly and stands up to daily use. The build quality justifies the price, and the assembly is genuinely painless.

**Skip it if** your cabinet has significant plumbing obstructions, you have unusually tall bottles, or you need a unit that mounts to the cabinet wall.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A well-executed, practical solution for one of the most frustrating storage spots in the kitchen. Just measure your space before you commit.
