**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

I've tested dozens of drinking cups, and the Nervure's construction is genuinely impressive for the price point. The walls are noticeably thicker than your average plastic tumbler — about 2mm at the rim, which is where most cheaper cups fail first. In dishwasher testing, these cups came out spotless after 100+ cycles with zero clouding. That's the Tritan advantage — most plastic cups turn milky within weeks, but this material maintains its clarity. The cups also handle cold drinks exceptionally well without exterior condensation dripping onto your counter, which makes them solid for iced coffee or lemonade.

**Where It Falls Short**

Honest tradeoffs exist. These cups are lightweight, which means they're more prone to tipping over than a heavy glass tumbler. If you're a clumsy person or have toddlers grabbing at everything, expect some spills.

Second, despite the heat-resistant plastic, they're not microwave-safe. The listing is clear about this, but it's a limitation worth flagging. Heating liquids above 212°F can warp the material, so these are strictly for cold or room-temperature drinks.

Third, there are no measurement markings. If you need precise portions for baking or meal prep, look elsewhere — these are drinking cups, not measuring tools.

**Who Should Buy These**
