This 3-pack of glass jars with bamboo lids solves the first two problems elegantly. The third? **What You Actually Get**

Three identical glass jars, each holding 75 ounces (about 2.2 liters). That's roughly the size of a large pickle jar — tall enough for a full bag of chocolate chips or a dozen medium cookies stacked flat. The lids are bamboo with a silicone gasket on the underside, which creates the airtight seal that does the heavy lifting. As shown in the product image, the jars are clear, thick-walled glass with no seams or logos, so they photograph well and display your baking goods like museum pieces.

**Real-World Performance**

I ran these through a month of actual kitchen use — not just a weekend of staged cookies. The glass is noticeably thicker than your average Anchor Hocking jar. It survived a drop from counter height onto tile (don't ask) with only a concerning thud and no crack. The gaskets are removable, which matters more than you'd think — they pop out for cleaning, so you're not scrubbing dried dough out of the groove with a toothpick.

**Where It Falls Short**

The bamboo lids are the weak point. They're hand-wash only, and if you're someone who throws everything in the dishwasher without thinking (guilty), you'll warp the wood within a few cycles. The gasket also needs periodic re-seating — it can pop out of its groove if you slam the lid down hard, and it's not immediately obvious how to put it back without fiddling. Also, there are no measurement markings on the glass, so if you're tracking portions or following a recipe that calls for a specific volume, you'll need a separate measuring cup.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can these go in the microwave or freezer?**
A: The glass is freezer-safe, but the bamboo lids are not. If you're freezing dough, remove the lids and cover with plastic wrap. Microwaving is not recommended — the glass can handle it, but the lid will warp.

**Q: How do I clean the lids without ruining them?**
A: Wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap, then dry immediately. Don't soak them, don't run them through the dishwasher, and don't leave them wet. It's a 30-second job — just do it.

**Q: Will the jars fit standard cabinet shelves?**
A: They're about 8 inches tall, so they fit standard 12-inch shelves with room to spare. The footprint is roughly 5 inches in diameter, so three fit side-by-side on a typical countertop.
