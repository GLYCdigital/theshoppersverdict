Let's be direct: a 3.9-star average across 195 ratings tells you this Gourmia 8-inch unit isn't perfect. But it also tells you plenty of people are using it daily without complaint. The question is whether you'll land in the satisfied camp or the frustrated one. Here's what the data and buyer feedback actually say.

**What You're Getting**

The box contains the 8-inch cooking surface, a detachable temperature control probe, and a power cord. That's it — no spatula, no recipe booklet, no extras. As shown in the product image, the design is intentionally minimal: a rounded, compact base with a matte finish that won't show fingerprints as badly as glossy alternatives. The 8-inch diameter is the headline feature here, and it's genuinely smaller than most competing units in this category, which typically run 10-12 inches.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The non-stick surface performs admirably in real-world use. One verified buyer noted, "Eggs slide right off, and I've stopped using butter entirely." That tracks with the coating quality at this price point. The heating element reaches cooking temperature in roughly 90 seconds, which beats many pricier competitors by a solid 30 seconds.

The build feels sturdier than the price suggests. The base doesn't flex under pressure, and the exterior stays cool enough to touch near the edges during operation — a safety feature that matters if you have kids around. However, the temperature dial is where things get frustrating. Multiple reviewers flagged that the "low" setting still runs hot, making delicate tasks like melting chocolate or simmering sauces trickier than they should be. One customer put it plainly: "There's a learning curve. The dial isn't precise, but once you figure out your settings, it works."

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The portability factor dominates positive reviews. Owners repeatedly mention taking this to dorm rooms, offices, and camping trips. The compact size genuinely delivers on that promise. The non-stick durability also earns consistent praise — several reviewers reported six months of heavy use with no scratching or peeling, which is better than many budget non-stick surfaces manage.

**Where It Falls Short**

Beyond the imprecise temperature control, the cord length is a genuine annoyance. At roughly 24 inches, you'll need the outlet close by, which limits placement on larger counters or kitchen islands. The 8-inch surface also means you're cooking in small batches — a family of four will spend significant time waiting for round two of pancakes.

One recurring complaint involves the temperature probe connection. A few buyers reported loose-fitting connections after several months, causing intermittent power loss. It's not widespread, but it's worth knowing before you commit.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is ideal for students, singles, couples, or anyone with limited counter space who needs a versatile cooker for eggs, grilled sandwiches, or small stir-fries. It's also a solid choice for RV owners or office workers who want hot meals without a full kitchen setup. If you regularly cook for four or more people, or if you need precise temperature control for sauces and delicate items, look elsewhere.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Can I use metal utensils on the non-stick surface?**
No. Stick to silicone, wood, or plastic utensils. Metal will scratch the coating and shorten the pan's lifespan.

**Does it come with a warranty?**
Yes, Gourmia includes a one-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Register your purchase on their website to activate it.

**How long does it take to cool down after cooking?**
Plan for roughly 15-20 minutes before the surface is safe to touch. The unit retains heat longer than expected, which is good for keeping food warm but a minor inconvenience for immediate storage.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a compact, reliable cooker for small portions and don't mind learning the temperature dial's quirks.

**Skip it if** you cook for larger groups, need precise heat control, or require a longer cord for flexible placement.

This Gourmia 8-inch earns a solid 3.7 out of 5. It's not the best in its category, but for the right user, it's a genuinely useful tool that won't break the bank. The temperature imprecision and short cord are real drawbacks, yet the non-stick performance and portability make it a worthwhile consideration for small-space living.
