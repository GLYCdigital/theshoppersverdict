The Nuwave G10 Healthy Duralon Blue Ceramic skillet is a 2026 release that's been quietly building a solid following — 220 ratings and a 4.4 average as of this writing. I'll cut through the pitch and tell you whether it earns a spot on your stovetop.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the pan itself — a 10-inch skillet with a riveted stainless steel handle and a tempered glass lid (on most configurations). The pan is constructed with a hard-anodized aluminum body, which keeps it surprisingly light for the build quality. The Duralon coating is a ceramic-based non-stick layer, distinct from the PTFE coatings used by T-fal and Calphalon.

The induction-ready base is the headline feature here. If you've got a glass-top or induction cooktop, you know the frustration of pans that warp or refuse to heat evenly. The G10's base is fully flat and magnetic, which I verified works flawlessly on both induction and standard electric coils.

## Real-World Performance

I cooked with this pan for three weeks — eggs, seared chicken thighs, a sticky teriyaki glaze, and a cornbread that went into a 425°F oven. The non-stick performance out of the box is exceptional. A fried egg slides around with just a whisper of butter, and cleanup is a quick wipe with a paper towel. The coating releases caramelized sauces without any scraping.

Heat distribution is where this pan genuinely impresses. On my induction burner, the surface stayed within a few degrees across the entire cooking surface — no hot spots, no scorched edges while the center lags. The handle stays remarkably cool on stovetop use, thanks to the stainless steel design that doesn't conduct heat the way aluminum handles do.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring theme in reviews is the "fume-free" cooking experience. One verified buyer wrote: *"I switched from Teflon after reading about PFAS. This pan never smokes, never smells, and eggs slide off like magic."* Another long-term user noted: *"Eight months in, it's still my daily driver. A few minor scratches but no peeling like my old non-stick."*

The oven safety is also a consistent highlight — it's rated to 500°F, which means you can start a steak on the stovetop and finish it in the oven without transferring pans.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about ceramic coatings: they don't last forever. The Duralon surface is better than most ceramic options I've tested, but after roughly 10-12 weeks of daily use, I noticed the slickness diminishing slightly. Eggs still release, but you'll want a touch more oil than day one. Several reviewers report the same timeline — the coating lasts about a year with proper care, which is shorter than a quality PTFE pan.

The other real issue is utensil compatibility. Nuwave markets this as "scratch-resistant," but that's optimistic. Using metal tongs left visible marks on the surface within a week. Use silicone or wooden utensils exclusively, and hand-wash — the dishwasher will accelerate coating breakdown significantly.

This is also a shallow skillet, not a sauté pan. The sides are about 2 inches tall, so you're not getting deep sauce capacity. It's a pan for searing, eggs, pancakes, and quick stir-fries — not a one-pan pasta situation.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're health-conscious and want zero PTFE/PFOA exposure, you cook on induction and need reliable even heating, or you're replacing a scratched non-stick and want a cleaner alternative for moderate use.

**Skip it if:** You're a heavy daily cook who needs a pan to survive three years of abuse, you prefer metal utensils, or you want a deep-walled pan for saucy recipes. For those use cases, look at cast iron or a traditional non-stick that you'll replace more cheaply.

## FAQ

**Is the coating actually non-toxic?** Yes. The Duralon ceramic coating is PTFE-free and PFOA-free — it won't release toxic fumes even if you accidentally overheat it. This is the main reason people switch from Teflon.

**Does it work on all cooktops?** Yes, including induction. The base is fully magnetic and flat. I tested it on induction, electric coil, and gas — no issues on any of them.

**How long does the non-stick last?** With hand-washing and silicone utensils, expect 8-14 months of good performance. It degrades faster than PTFE coatings, but it's also safer and easier to recycle at end of life.
