## What You're Actually Getting

The kit ships with two sealed canisters: a 5-micron sediment filter (typically the first stage) and a carbon block filter (second stage). Both carry NSF 42 and NSF 372 certification — the former for aesthetic chlorine reduction, the latter for lead-free materials. That's not marketing fluff; that's a third-party verification that the filter media actually does what it claims.

Installation is straightforward if you've done it before. The quick-connect fittings mate directly with Avalon's standard housings. You'll twist off the old filters, swap in the new ones, and run water for 10 minutes to flush the system. That flush is critical — skip it and you'll get a burst of carbon fines in your first glass.

The lack of a wrench in the box is the bigger annoyance, especially for first-time buyers who don't have one on hand.

There's also the flush requirement. Ten minutes of running water is time-consuming, and if you're replacing filters on a schedule, you'll want to plan it for a morning when you won't need the cooler right away.
