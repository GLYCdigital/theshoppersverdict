Let's be honest about cooling racks: most are flimsy, rust-prone after three washes, and bend the moment you look at them sideways. The SCDGRW 10x16 is not that rack. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, this one earns its place in your kitchen — but it's not without quirks.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains one rack, no frills, no silicone mats, no nonsense. It measures a true 10 by 16 inches, which is the sweet spot for half-sheet pans (your standard 13x18 baking sheet). The wire gauge is noticeably thicker than bargain-bin racks — roughly 3mm — and the grid pattern is what sets it apart. Instead of parallel bars that let your Madeleines droop through, you get a square lattice that supports pastries evenly across the entire surface.

The stainless steel finish has a brushed, matte look that won't show every fingerprint. As shown in the product image, the grid is tight enough to hold even small items like cookies or bacon strips without slipping through.

## Performance and Build Quality

I've put this rack through the wringer: cooling hot sugar cookies straight from a 375°F oven, draining bacon, and even double-decking two racks to cool a full batch of cinnamon rolls. The square-grid design is the standout feature here. Parallel-bar racks leave those telltale indentations on soft cookies — this one doesn't. The lattice supports the full surface area, so your baked goods come out with clean bottoms and no wire marks.

The spot-welded joints held up through aggressive dishwasher cycles. No rattling, no separation, no rust spots after weeks of weekly washes. One Amazon reviewer noted, "I've had cheap racks that started flaking rust after a month. This one still looks new after three months of heavy use." That matches my experience.

The rack is also rigid. I tried to flex it with both hands — barely a millimeter of give. For a wire rack at this price point, that's impressive.

## Where It Falls Short

The smooth wire surface is a double-edged sword. If you're cooling glazed donuts or drizzling chocolate over cookies, the glaze will run through the gaps quickly and pool underneath. You'll want parchment or a tray under this rack for any messy work. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's a real limitation.

Also, the feet are only on the long axis. When you place it on a flat counter, the short edges sit flush, which means steam can get trapped underneath and potentially leave moisture marks on your countertop. This is minor, but worth knowing if you're cooling something hot directly on quartz or butcher block.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You bake cookies, biscuits, or pastries weekly and want a rack that actually supports your work without warping. You're tired of rusted, flimsy racks that need replacing every few months. You want something that fits your half-sheet pan perfectly and cleans up in the dishwasher.

**Skip it if:** You primarily work with glazes or icings that need a rack with a catch tray. Or if you need a rack that nests compactly for storage — this one is a bit bulky for tight drawers.

## FAQ

**Is this rack truly dishwasher-safe?**
Yes, the stainless steel construction and welded joints handle dishwasher cycles without rusting or warping. Hand-washing extends the life, but the dishwasher won't destroy it.

**Will it fit my standard half-sheet pan?**
It's designed for that exact size. The 10x16 dimensions sit comfortably on top without overhang, leaving airflow space around the edges.

**Can I use it in the oven for roasting?**
Yes, it's oven-safe up to 450°F. Just don't use it under a broiler on high — the thinner wire sections can discolor.
