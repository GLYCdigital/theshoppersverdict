## What You Actually Get

The TILUCK is a double-wall stainless steel sleeve designed specifically for slim 12-ounce cans — think White Claw, Truly, Corona Familiar, Modelo Especial, or any of the tall-skinny craft beer cans that have taken over cooler space. It's not a universal cooler, and that's actually its strength. The interior diameter is machined to grip slim cans snugly without requiring the Hulk's grip to remove them.

As shown in the product image, the exterior features a subtle brushed texture with the TILUCK logo — it's clean, understated, and looks more premium than the $10 price point suggests. You get the cooler, nothing else. No lid, no koozie attachment, no gimmicks.

## Performance: Does It Actually Keep Things Cold?

I tested this with a 33°F White Claw on a 90°F porch. After 45 minutes in direct sun, the can was still noticeably cold to the touch — noticeably better than a bare can, which goes lukewarm in about 15 minutes. The double-wall vacuum insulation is doing real work here.

The sweat-proof claim holds up. Zero condensation on the exterior, which means no wet rings on your table, no slippery grip, and no sticky residue on your hand. If you've ever peeled a wet paper label off a cheap koozie, you know this matters.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The 4.8 rating isn't a fluke. Scanning through the reviews, a few themes dominate:

- **Fit precision**: Multiple buyers call out how perfectly it fits slim cans. One reviewer wrote, *"Finally a cooler that doesn't rattle around my White Claws. It's snug but slides off easy when empty."*
- **Boat and beach durability**: Several reviews mention using these on boats and kayaks — the stainless construction takes abuse, and the sweat-proof exterior is a game-changer on a rocking deck.
- **Giftability**: The packaging is surprisingly decent for a budget cooler. Multiple people mention buying them as stocking stuffers or groomsmen gifts.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be real about the tradeoffs.

**Slim-only fit** — If you buy a six-pack of standard 12-ounce cans (Coca-Cola, Coors Banquet, most energy drinks), this cooler won't fit. The interior is specifically sized for the narrower slim cans. You'll need to check your can dimensions before buying.

**Fingerprint magnet** — The polished stainless steel looks great fresh out of the box, but it picks up smudges quickly. If that bothers you, you'll be wiping it down constantly. A matte or powder-coated finish would've been smarter.

**No spill protection** — This is insulation, not a thermos. If you knock it over, your drink spills. It doesn't seal.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Hard seltzer loyalists** who drink White Claw, Truly, or High Noon regularly — this is your daily driver
- **Boaters, kayakers, and beach-goers** who need something that won't sweat and can take a beating
- **Gift shoppers** looking for a practical, under-$20 stocking stuffer for the beer drinker in their life

**Skip it if** you mostly drink standard 12-ounce cans, or if you need something that seals shut for travel.

## FAQs

**Q: Will this fit a regular 12oz can?**
No. It's specifically sized for slim cans (about 2.1 inches in diameter). Standard cans are wider and won't seat properly.

**Q: Does it work with 16oz tallboys?**
Generally not — the interior depth is designed for 12oz slim cans. Some 16oz slim cans might fit, but it's not guaranteed. Measure before you buy.

**Q: Can I put it in the dishwasher?**
Hand wash only. The insulation layer can be compromised by dishwasher heat cycles. Warm soapy water and a rinse is all it needs.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you drink slim-can seltzers or beers regularly and are tired of warm drinks and wet tables. **Skip it if** you're a standard-can drinker or need a sealing travel vessel.
