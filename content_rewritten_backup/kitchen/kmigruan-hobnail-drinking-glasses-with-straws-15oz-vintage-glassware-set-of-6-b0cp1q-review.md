**What You Actually Get**

The set includes six 15-ounce glasses, six bamboo lids, six glass straws, and two cleaning brushes. The hobnail texture — those raised diamond bumps covering the exterior — is the defining feature. **Where It Falls Short**

The bamboo lids are not watertight. If you're looking for a travel cup or something to toss in a bag, this isn't it. They're decorative and functional for keeping debris out, but don't expect a leak-proof seal.

The glass straws are fragile. One buyer noted, "The straws are beautiful but I broke one within a week washing it." You'll need to handle them gently, which is a real drawback for households with kids or clumsy adults.

The hobnail texture, while great for grip, does trap residue. If you drink anything creamy or sugary, you'll need to pay extra attention to the crevices when cleaning. The included brushes help, but it's an added step.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Are these glasses dishwasher safe?**
Yes, they're dishwasher safe on the top rack. However, hand washing will preserve the hobnail texture's appearance longer, and you should always hand wash the bamboo lids and glass straws.

**Do the lids make them spill-proof?**
No. The bamboo lids fit snugly and keep out dust and insects, but they don't create a watertight seal. Don't put these in a bag or backpack expecting zero leaks.

**Can I use these for hot drinks?**
Technically yes, but I wouldn't. The thick glass handles heat, but the bamboo lids may warp over time with repeated exposure to steam. Stick to cold or room-temperature beverages.
