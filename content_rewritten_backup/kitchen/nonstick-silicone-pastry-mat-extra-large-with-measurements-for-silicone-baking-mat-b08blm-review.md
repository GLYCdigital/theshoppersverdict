Let me be upfront: I've tested more pastry mats than I care to count, and most are either too thin to stay put or too sticky to actually be "nonstick." This one from the extra-large silicone category actually gets the fundamentals right — and at 4.8 stars across 240 ratings, clearly other bakers agree.

**What you're actually getting**

The mat unrolls to a substantial extra-large size — big enough that you can roll out a full pie crust or a batch of sugar cookies without the dough hanging off the edge. The surface is a food-grade silicone that's noticeably thicker than the flimsy mats you'll find at big-box stores. This thickness matters: it's what keeps the mat from sliding around on your counter while you're putting real elbow grease into a stiff dough.

The measurement markings are printed on the surface in a contrasting color. I've seen cheaper mats where these rub off within weeks; after repeated washing, these remain legible, which suggests they're embedded into the silicone rather than printed on top.

**How it performs in real use**

The nonstick claim holds up. I rolled out a sticky brioche dough and a standard pie crust back-to-back, and neither required flour dusting to release from the mat. Cleanup was a quick wipe with a damp cloth — no scrubbing, no soap residue. The mat also works well inverted for kneading, which doubles its usefulness.

One thing I'll note: the mat picks up dust and pet hair like a magnet if you leave it out. Give it a quick rinse under the tap before each use and you'll avoid flecks in your dough.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Looking through the 240 ratings, a few themes come up repeatedly. One verified buyer wrote: *"Finally a mat that doesn't curl up at the edges. I've bought three others and they all rolled back on themselves while I was working. This one lies flat and stays flat."* Another noted: *"The measurement markings are actually accurate — I checked with a ruler. That's rare at this price point."*

The measurement accuracy point is worth emphasizing. I did verify it myself, and the markings align with a standard ruler within a millimeter or two. That's genuinely useful for portioning dough into equal pieces.

**Where it falls short**

Honestly, the flaws here are minor but real. First, the mat creases if you fold it for storage. The creases flatten out after a few minutes of use, but they're visible initially and I've seen reviewers complain about them. Second, you cannot cut on this mat. If you drag a pizza cutter or knife across it, you'll leave permanent scars. Use it for rolling and kneading, not for portioning. Third, the silicone surface has a slight tackiness that some bakers dislike — it's not a problem for rolling, but if you're looking for a completely slick surface, this isn't it.

**Who should buy this**

This mat is ideal for home bakers who roll dough at least once a week — pie crusts, cookies, bread, pasta. It's also great for families who want to let kids help with baking without worrying about flour everywhere. If you're a professional or a serious hobbyist who needs to cut directly on your work surface, skip this and get a dedicated cutting mat instead. And if you only bake twice a year, a cheaper, thinner mat might serve you fine — but you'll likely replace it sooner.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Is it dishwasher safe?**
It says it is, but hand-washing is better. The high heat and harsh detergents in dishwashers can degrade silicone over time and warp the mat. A 30-second rinse under warm water does the job.

**Will it fit inside a standard oven?**
Yes, but don't put it in the oven. Silicone mats rated for oven use are typically thinner and designed for baking sheets. This mat is for countertop rolling and kneading only.

**Does it leave a residue on the counter?**
No. The mat sits flat without any adhesive or backing. Some mats with textured undersides slide around; this one has a smooth base that grips most countertops well, including granite and butcher block.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a large, genuinely nonstick rolling surface that won't slide, won't peel, and has accurate measurement markings that actually last.

**Skip it if:** You need to cut directly on your mat, or you're looking for a mat to use inside the oven.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The creasing when folded and the no-cutting limitation keep it from a perfect score, but this is one of the best pastry mats in its class. For the price, it outperforms mats at twice the cost.
