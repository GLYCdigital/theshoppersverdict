## The 6.5-inch paper that saves your cake (and your sanity)

If you've ever pried a stuck cheesecake out of a springform pan, you know the feeling: crust crumbled, sides ripped, and half your dessert stays behind. BOYAN's precut parchment rounds exist to end that specific frustration. These are 6.5-inch flat circles designed for small pans and tortilla presses — not a universal solution, but a precise one.

## What you actually get

Open the box and you'll find 200 flat, pre-cut parchment rounds. No roll, no scissors, no guessing. Each circle measures 6.5 inches across, which makes them a drop-in fit for:

- 6-inch and 7-inch springform pans
- 6-inch cake pans
- Tortilla presses (the 6.5-inch size matches most manual presses)
- Small pizza pans or toaster oven trays

The paper itself is heavier than standard supermarket rolls. It's bleached white, smooth, and doesn't have that waxy coating smell some cheap brands carry.

## Real-world performance

I tested these in a 6-inch springform pan with a dense, wet cheesecake batter. The round sat flat on the bottom, and the paper didn't shift when I poured the batter. After a 90-minute bake at 325°F, the paper came out clean — no sticking, no grease soaking through, no tearing when I cut the first slice.

The heavyweight build matters more than it sounds. Thin parchment curls up when it hits wet batter, and that curl can poke through your crust. These BOYAN rounds stay flat. I also used them in a tortilla press for fresh corn tortillas — the paper released cleanly without sticking to the masa.

## Where it falls short

Be honest about the limitations. This is a single-size product. If you own an 8-inch or 9-inch springform pan, these rounds won't cover the bottom completely. You'd need to buy a different size or keep cutting your own.

Also, the 6.5-inch diameter is slightly smaller than the bottom of a standard 7-inch springform pan (which usually measures around 7 inches inside). It covers most of the surface, but there's a thin exposed ring around the edge. For most bakes, that's fine — the crust will still release. But if you're making a sticky caramel-based cake, that uncovered rim could cause a minor stick.

## Who should buy this

- **Cheesecake bakers** who use 6-inch or 7-inch pans and want foolproof release
- **Tortilla makers** who need a clean, non-stick press surface
- **Small-batch bakers** who prefer precise convenience over cutting from a roll

Skip it if you only bake with 8-inch or larger pans — you'd need to stack multiple rounds or cut them to fit, which defeats the purpose.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these reusable?**  
Not really. They're designed for single use. You could reuse one if it stays clean, but that's rare with wet batters.

**Q: Can I use them in an air fryer?**  
Yes. They fit small air fryer baskets that take 6-inch rounds. Just don't let them touch the heating element directly.

**Q: Do they have a silicone coating?**  
No. These are parchment paper (quilon-coated), not silicone mats. They're fine up to 425°F, but don't push them past that.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you bake small cheesecakes or use a tortilla press regularly and want zero-fuss release every time. **Skip it if** you need larger sizes or prefer reusable silicone mats.
