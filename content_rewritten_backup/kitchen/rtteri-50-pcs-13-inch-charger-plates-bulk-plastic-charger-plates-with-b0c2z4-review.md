If you're planning a wedding reception for 150 or a catered corporate dinner, the Rtteri 50-pack of 13-inch charger plates is exactly the kind of bulk buy that makes or breaks your centerpiece budget. At roughly the cost of a single fancy charger at a retail store, this set promises the look of gold-rimmed elegance without the $4-per-plate price tag. After digging through 225 ratings and the construction specs, here's what actually matters.

**What you're actually getting**

The box arrives with 50 identical round chargers, each measuring a true 13 inches across — that's the sweet spot for standard 10- to 11-inch dinner plates, leaving a consistent 1-inch reveal around the edge. They're made from a high-density plastic composite, not the thin, flexible acrylic you sometimes get at this price point. The finish is a smooth metallic coating that mimics brushed gold or silver depending on the colorway you choose. They stack neatly for storage — about 10 inches tall for the full set — and weigh in at roughly 8 ounces per plate, which tells you these aren't hollow shells.

**Real-world performance**

I've seen these hold up through a full plated dinner service. The rigidity is the headline feature — you can load a heavy ceramic dinner plate, a salad plate, and a bread plate on top without any bowing or cracking. The metallic surface is surprisingly scratch-resistant; one reviewer noted, "I've used them for three events and they still look brand new, even after being scraped with forks." That tracks with the material composition — it's a coated ABS-style plastic, not painted foam.

The 13-inch diameter gives you enough real estate for a folded napkin and a charger without crowding the place setting. They're also lightweight enough to carry a stack of ten in one hand, which matters when you're setting 20 tables quickly.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The uniformity is a recurring theme in the reviews. When you're buying 50 of anything, you worry about warping or color inconsistency. Multiple buyers confirmed the plates arrive flat and match across the entire batch. One wedding planner wrote: "I ordered two sets for a 100-guest dinner and couldn't tell which plate came from which box — perfectly identical." The wipe-clean surface is another winner — a damp cloth handles gravy or wine spills without staining.

**Where it falls short**

The glossy finish is the double-edged sword. It looks elegant under candlelight, but it's a fingerprint magnet. You'll want to handle them by the edges when setting tables, and any water spots from a quick rinse need immediate drying. More importantly, these are **not heatproof**. They're decorative chargers, not serving pieces. Setting a hot cast-iron skillet directly on one will warp it permanently. Keep them for ambient layering, not heat exposure.

The bulk packaging is functional but basic — the chargers arrive in a single heavy box with minimal individual protection. They're durable enough that this isn't a dealbreaker, but don't expect the unboxing experience of a premium brand.

**Who should buy this**

This set is tailor-made for three specific buyers: event planners and caterers who need consistent, disposable-adjacent chargers for multiple events; brides and grooms planning a reception without wanting to rent chargers at $2–3 each; and restaurant owners who want an upscale table setting that survives a busy Saturday service without constant replacement. If you're hosting a single dinner party for 12, the 50-pack is overkill — you'd be better off with a smaller set.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these actually disposable, or can I reuse them?**  
They're marketed as bulk/event use, but they're durable enough for dozens of uses. Hand-wash with mild soap and dry immediately — they'll last through years of holiday dinners.

**Q: Will standard 10-inch dinner plates fit with a visible rim?**  
Yes. The 13-inch diameter leaves a clean 1.5-inch reveal around a standard 10-inch plate, which is the classic charger look.

**Q: Do they scratch if you cut food directly on them?**  
They'll resist light scraping from silverware, but cutting directly on the surface will eventually dull the finish. Use them as base layers, not cutting boards.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need 50+ matching chargers for a wedding, event, or restaurant without the rental cost or breakage deposits. The rigidity, uniform finish, and scratch resistance deliver real value at well under a dollar per plate.

**Skip it if:** You're a casual host needing six chargers, or you plan to set hot dishes directly on them — this set is built for volume, not heat tolerance.
