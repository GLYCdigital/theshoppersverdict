The set includes both round and square borosilicate glass jars fitted with bamboo lids and silicone gaskets. **Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

Borosilicate glass matters more than most shoppers realize. It handles temperature swings without cracking, so you can take a jar from a cold pantry to a warm counter without drama. It also won't latch onto lingering smells — a genuine problem with plastic containers that mysteriously taste like last month's chili even after washing.

The bamboo lids seal via a silicone gasket that presses down snugly. One buyer noted, "I've had cereal go stale in the box within a week, but it stays crunchy in these jars for three weeks plus." That tracks with the design — the gaskets create enough compression for daily pantry use. Just don't expect vacuum-seal performance. **Where SHOWIN Falls Short**

Honesty requires flagging the bamboo lid maintenance. Wood and dishwashers don't mix — if you run these through a heated cycle, the lids will warp, crack, or split. Hand-wash them with mild soap and dry immediately. It's an extra step, but it's the tradeoff for lids that look this good.

Also, the lids aren't airtight enough for true long-term storage. If you're buying these to keep opened chips fresh for a month, you'll be disappointed. For weekly-to-biweekly pantry rotation — flour, sugar, pasta, cereals — they perform exactly as intended.

**FAQ**

**Can these go in the dishwasher?**
The glass jars are dishwasher-safe, but the bamboo lids are not. Hand-wash lids and dry them immediately to prevent warping. The glass itself handles machine washing fine.

**Are the jars microwave-safe?**
Yes, the borosilicate glass is microwave-safe. Remove the bamboo lid before heating, and be careful — the glass gets hot.
