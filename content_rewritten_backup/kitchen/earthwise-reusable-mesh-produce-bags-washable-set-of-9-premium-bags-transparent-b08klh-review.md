The Earthwise Reusable Mesh Produce Bags aren't the flashiest kitchen gadget you'll buy this year. But after 235 Amazon ratings averaging 4.7 stars, they might be the one you actually use every single week. Here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

Open the package and you'll find nine bags in three sizes — three small (roughly 8x10 inches), three medium (10x13 inches), and three large (13x16 inches). Each bag weighs 0.35 ounces, and that number is pre-printed on the fabric. That's a small detail that saves you from hunting for the tare weight at self-checkout.

The mesh is a transparent 90gsm polyester weave. It's see-through enough that cashiers can identify your kale without untwisting the drawstring, but dense enough that you won't lose a single green bean through the gaps.

## Performance and Build Quality

Here's where the Earthwise bags separate themselves from the $9 generic sets. The seams are double-stitched — I loaded a large bag with six pounds of potatoes and carried it by the handles across a parking lot. No stretching, no popped threads, no structural complaints.

The drawstring closure is functional, not fancy. It pulls tight and stays put, but the cords themselves are on the thin side. If you're the type who yanks drawstrings like you're starting a lawnmower, you'll want to ease up. Several reviewers mention the same thing — one noted, "The strings are a bit flimsy but the bags themselves are solid." That's a fair tradeoff at this price point.

Washing is straightforward. Toss them in a laundry bag and run a cold cycle, or hand-wash in the sink with dish soap. They dry in about 20 minutes draped over a dish rack. I've run mine through 30+ wash cycles with zero fading or fraying.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The transparency is the most-cited win. "I love that the cashier can see what's inside without me having to untie every bag," one verified buyer wrote. "Makes checkout so much faster."

Another recurring theme is durability. Multiple reviewers mention using these bags for months — even years — without any rips. One shopper reported, "I've been using these for 18 months and they still look brand new. The seams haven't budged."

## Where They Fall Short

Honest downsides? Two.

First, all nine bags are identical in color. If you want to sort by category — greens in one color, root veggies in another — you're out of luck. You'll either memorize which size you used for what or play "guess the bag" when you get home.

Second, the drawstring cord is thinner than I'd like. It works fine for normal use, but it's the most likely failure point long-term. If you're rough on closures, these won't be your forever bags.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy them if:** You're replacing single-use plastic produce bags and want something that'll survive a year of weekly grocery trips. You shop at stores with self-checkout and appreciate the pre-printed tare weight. You want to see your produce at a glance without opening every bag.

**Skip them if:** You need color-coded organization or plan to haul more than six pounds per bag regularly. If you're a die-hard zero-waster who wants mesh bags for bulk grains and frozen items too, these are too breathable for that — they're produce-specific.

## FAQ

**Are these bags really washable?**
Yes. Machine wash cold in a mesh laundry bag, or hand wash. Air dry. They hold up well over repeated washes.

**Will the mesh tear with sharp produce?**
No. The 90gsm weave is dense enough to resist punctures from stems and rough edges. I've stored carrots and broccoli without issues.

**Do these work at self-checkout scales?**
Yes. Each bag has its tare weight printed on it, so you can subtract it at the scale without guessing.
