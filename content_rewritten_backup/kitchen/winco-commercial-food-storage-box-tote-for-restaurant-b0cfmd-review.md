## Where It Falls Short

The lid fit, while secure, isn't airtight. If you're storing liquids and plan to shake or flip the container, you'll get leakage. Not a dealbreaker for most prep work, but worth knowing.

The size is a limitation for some home users. This is a fractional gastronorm container designed for commercial kitchens — if you're looking to store leftovers or meal prep for a family of four, you'll find it too small. This is a professional tool, not a household container.

The price is also higher per unit than standard deli containers. You're paying for commercial-grade durability, and if you don't need that level of abuse tolerance, cheaper options exist.

## FAQ

**Is this NSF-certified?**
Yes, Winco's polycarbonate food storage boxes meet NSF standards for commercial food contact. That's a meaningful distinction for health inspections.

**Can these go in the microwave?**
Yes, the polycarbonate is microwave-safe. Just remove the lid before heating, and be aware the container will get hot.

**Do the lids stay on securely in transit?**
They snap on firmly, but they're not leak-proof. If you're transporting liquids, wrap them or use a different container. For dry ingredients and solid prep items, the seal is plenty secure.
