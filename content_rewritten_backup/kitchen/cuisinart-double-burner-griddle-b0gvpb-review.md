### The breakfast game-changer you didn’t know you needed

Most "double burner" griddles are a lie. They claim to span two burners but leave a dead zone in the middle where pancakes come out pale and sad. The Cuisinart Double Burner Griddle actually solves this problem—and that's why it's sitting at 4.7 stars from 235 Amazon shoppers.

---

### What you actually get

The box contains the griddle itself—a 20-inch by 10.5-inch cooking surface made from heavy-gauge steel with a nonstick coating. There are no accessories, no spatulas, no nonsense. It's designed to sit across two burners (works on gas, electric, and ceramic tops), with a slick black exterior that looks purposeful on any stove.

The weight is noticeable but not unwieldy—about 5 pounds. You'll feel the quality when you pick it up. This isn't one of those flimsy sheet-metal griddles that flexes when you press down with a spatula.

---

### Real-world performance

I tested this griddle for the breakfast trifecta: pancakes, eggs, and bacon. Here's what matters.

**Heat distribution:** The standout feature. The griddle's construction uses a bonded aluminum core that spreads heat evenly across the entire 20-inch surface. Pancakes cooked on the far left and far right finished at the same time as the ones in the middle. No hot spots, no cold zones. That's rare at this price point.

**Nonstick surface:** Genuinely nonstick. Eggs slide around with just a pat of butter. The coating is PFOA-free and held up through multiple uses without scratching—though you should still use silicone or wood utensils to be safe.

**Cooking capacity:** You can fit six to eight pancakes at once, or four eggs plus a full pound of bacon. For a family of four, this replaces your entire breakfast cookware setup. One reviewer noted, *"I made breakfast for my family of five in about 15 minutes. Normally it takes me 45 with two pans."*

**Cleanup:** The surface wipes clean with a paper towel. For stuck-on residue, it's dishwasher-safe, though hand-washing is gentler on the nonstick coating.

### What buyers consistently praise

The 4.7-star rating isn't accidental. Across the 235 reviews, the same themes keep appearing:

- **Even heating:** Multiple reviewers specifically call out that pancakes and eggs cook uniformly—no rotating pans, no babysitting.
- **Size:** *"Finally a griddle that actually fits over two burners and doesn't hang off the edge awkwardly."*
- **Build quality:** People are impressed by the heft and durability. This isn't a disposable griddle you'll replace in six months.

### Where it falls short

**No grease management.** This is the biggest miss. There's no drip channel or grease tray around the edges. When you cook bacon, the rendered fat pools at the perimeter of the griddle. You'll need to carefully tilt it to pour off the grease—or keep a paper towel handy to soak it up mid-cook.

**Heat retention is a double-edged sword.** The griddle gets very hot, and it stays hot after you turn off the burners. You need a dedicated storage spot where it can cool safely. The handles stay cool to the touch, which helps, but the main body is a burn hazard for about 15 minutes after use.

**Not ideal for induction.** Works perfectly on gas and standard electric, but induction cooktops need a fully magnetic base—this one is only partially magnetic. Check your cooktop before buying.

---

### Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** You cook breakfast for multiple people regularly, you're tired of rotating pans to get even heat, or you want a griddle that doubles as a panini press and a quesadilla maker on weeknights.

**Skip it if:** You're on induction, you only cook for one or two, or you're looking for a griddle with built-in grease management (look at electric griddles instead).

### FAQ

**Can you use metal utensils on it?**
Technically yes, but you'll shorten the nonstick lifespan. Silicone or wood is the right call.

**Does it work on a glass-top stove?**
Yes. It sits flat and doesn't scratch, but lift it rather than sliding it to avoid any risk of scratching the glass.

**Is it truly dishwasher-safe?**
The manufacturer says yes, but hand-washing is gentler and will extend the nonstick coating's life. Most reviewers hand-wash.

### The Verdict

**4.6/5 stars**

The Cuisinart Double Burner Griddle earns its reputation. It delivers the rare combination of genuinely even heat, a generous cooking surface, and solid construction. The missing grease tray is annoying, but not a dealbreaker—especially at this price point.
