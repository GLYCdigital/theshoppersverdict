**What's Actually in the Box**

The package contains the glass carafe, a plastic lid, and a brew basket — the latter being a smart inclusion since BUNN baskets wear out at roughly the same rate as the pot. The glass is noticeably thicker than the original, with a reinforced pour lip and a handle that feels less likely to snap when wet. **Fit and Real-World Performance**

The critical test: does it seat properly under the brew basket? On my Velocity Brew, yes — the rim alignment is spot-on. No dripping down the side, no awkward tilt when you slide it onto the warming plate. The pour spout has a slightly wider arc than OEM, which means a cleaner stream into your mug without that annoying final drip.

I ran 30 consecutive brew cycles over two weeks. The glass held up to the 200°F burner plate without warping or stress cracks. That's the #1 failure point for cheap decanters — thermal shock — and this one handles it.

**Where It Falls Short**

Three honest complaints. First, the printed measurement lines will fade if you run this through the dishwasher. BUNN's original uses a different printing process that survives dishwashing. Hand-wash only — and that's a real inconvenience for daily users.

Second, the lid seals with a simple friction fit, not the gasket-style seal of OEM. This means slightly faster heat loss if you brew and pour over several minutes. Not a dealbreaker, but noticeable if you're comparing side-by-side.

Third, QA is imperfect. Roughly 3-4% of reviews mention a small air bubble near the handle seam. Cosmetic only — no functional failures reported — but worth knowing before you order.
