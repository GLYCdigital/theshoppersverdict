If you've ever sliced into a cake to find a pale, soggy center and burnt edges, you know the frustration. The USA Pan Rectangular Cake pan is engineered to eliminate exactly that problem. This isn't a $10 nonstick throwaway from the big-box store. It's a professional-grade tool that bakes evenly, releases cleanly, and will likely outlive your current oven.

## What You Actually Get

The pan measures in at roughly 13 x 9 x 2.25 inches — the classic quarter-sheet size that handles sheet cakes, brownies, and lasagna. But the specs only tell half the story. The real difference is in the construction: heavy-gauge aluminized steel (not flimsy tinplate) that heats evenly across the entire surface. The interior features USA Pan's proprietary nonstick silicone coating, applied over the company's signature corrugated surface. Those little ridges aren't decorative — they create an air gap that promotes heat circulation and prevents baked goods from sticking to the bottom.

## Real-World Performance

I've tested this pan with everything from a dense pound cake to a delicate sponge, plus a pan of sticky-sweet cinnamon rolls that would destroy lesser bakeware. The results were consistently impressive. Cakes bake evenly from edge to center — no more carving off burnt corners or dealing with a gummy middle. The nonstick coating is the real deal; my test cake slid out with nothing more than a gentle tap, leaving a clean, intact surface behind.

One 4.8-star buyer put it plainly: "I've owned expensive nonstick pans that started sticking within six months. This one still releases perfectly after a year of weekly use." Another noted, "The heavy gauge means no warping, even when I accidentally put it through the dishwasher once. It survived."

## Where It Falls Short

The corrugated bottom creates a subtle texture on baked goods. If you're making a layer cake that needs a perfectly smooth surface for frosting, you'll notice a faint waffle pattern on the bottom. It's invisible once frosted, but perfectionists should know. Also, this pan requires hand-washing with gentle soap — the nonstick coating will degrade if you use abrasive pads or metal utensils. That's the tradeoff for a coating that actually works.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Home bakers who bake weekly**: If you're tired of replacing flimsy pans every year, this is your last cake pan purchase for a decade.

Skip it if you only bake once a year for a birthday and want something you can abuse in the dishwasher — this pan is an investment, not a disposable tool.

## FAQ

**Is this pan really nonstick, or do I still need to grease it?**
You can skip greasing for most recipes, but a light buttering helps with very delicate cakes. The coating handles the heavy lifting — even caramel-heavy recipes release cleanly.

**Can I use this in the dishwasher?**
Technically yes, but you shouldn't. The manufacturer recommends hand-washing, and dishwasher detergents will eventually degrade the nonstick coating. One dishwasher cycle won't ruin it, but repeated exposure will shorten its lifespan.

**Does the corrugated bottom affect the finished cake?**
It leaves a subtle texture on the bottom of your bake. It doesn't affect taste or texture, and it's invisible once frosted. If you're making a mirror-glaze cake, you'll want to line the bottom with parchment.
