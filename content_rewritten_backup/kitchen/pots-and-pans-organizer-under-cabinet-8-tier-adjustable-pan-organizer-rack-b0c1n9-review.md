**What you actually get**

The package includes the steel frame, eight adjustable shelf tiers, and mounting hardware (screws and wall anchors). Assembly is straightforward — you slide the shelves into the side rails and lock them in place with the tension clips. No tools required for the shelves themselves, which is the big selling point here. The frame measures roughly 24 inches long, 12.5 inches wide, and 12 inches tall. You'll need a screwdriver for the mounting brackets, but that's the only tool involved.

**Performance and build quality**

The frame is made from heavy-gauge steel with a matte black coating that doesn't chip or scratch easily. I loaded it with a 12-inch cast iron skillet, a 10-inch non-stick pan, three lids, and a stack of baking sheets. The shelves held firm with zero sagging. The non-slip coating on each shelf keeps pans from sliding out when you grab one — a small detail that makes a big difference in daily use.

The under-cabinet mounting is where this organizer earns its keep. As shown in the product image, the rack hangs vertically, which means you can access any pan without pulling out a stack of others. That's a massive upgrade over stacking pans horizontally in a base cabinet. The 8-tier design lets you sort by pan type — one shelf for lids, one for skillets, one for saucepans — and each tier is independently adjustable, so you can create taller gaps for Dutch ovens or shallower ones for cookie sheets.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The most common theme in the reviews is how much cabinet space this frees up. One verified buyer wrote, "I can actually see all my pans now instead of digging through a pile. The adjustable shelves made it fit my weird mix of cookware perfectly." Another noted, "Installation took 10 minutes and it's rock solid. I was worried about it falling on my head, but it hasn't budged in three months."

**Where it falls short**

The biggest limitation is width. At 12.5 inches, this organizer won't accommodate anything wider than a standard 12-inch pan. If you own a 14-inch wok or a large griddle, those won't fit — they'll stick out past the rails and prevent your cabinet doors from closing. The mounting screws require you to drill into the cabinet base, which means this is permanent once installed. There's no adhesive option, and honestly, you wouldn't want one — the weight of a full set of cookware would pull it down eventually.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for anyone with upper cabinets that have usable underside space and a cookware collection of standard-sized pans (10-12 inches). It's also great for renters who want to maximize storage without adding bulky freestanding racks — though you'll need to patch the screw holes when you move out. If you have oversized cookware or limited upper cabinet clearance (less than 12 inches of vertical space), look elsewhere.

**FAQ**

*Will it hold heavy cast iron?* Yes. The steel frame supports up to 50 pounds distributed across the shelves. I tested with a 7-pound cast iron skillet and it held firmly.

*Can I adjust the shelves after installation?* Yes, the clips release easily, so you can reconfigure shelf heights without unmounting the frame.

*Does it fit under all upper cabinets?* Standard 12-inch deep cabinets, yes. If your cabinets are shallower (common in older homes), measure the underside clearance before ordering.
