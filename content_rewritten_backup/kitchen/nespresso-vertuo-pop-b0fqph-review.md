If you've got 6 inches of dead counter space and a serious coffee habit, the Nespresso Vertuo Pop+ might be the smallest upgrade that changes your mornings. This is the baby of the Vertuo line — deliberately compact, aggressively colorful, and designed for people who want café-quality coffee without dedicating their entire kitchen to a machine the size of a microwave. But "compact" always comes with compromises. Here's what you need to know before buying.

## What's Actually in the Box

The Pop+ ships with the machine itself, a starter kit of 12 assorted Vertuo pods, and the standard manuals. That's it. No milk frother, no descaling kit, no fancy extras. The machine measures roughly 5.5 inches wide and 9.5 inches deep — genuinely small, about the size of a large toaster standing upright. As shown in the product image, it's got that rounded, playful silhouette that reads more "modern decor piece" than "kitchen appliance." It comes in six colorways, from muted pastels to loud neon, so you can match it to your kitchen or just make it a statement piece.

## How It Actually Performs

The Pop+ uses the same Centrifusion technology as the larger Vertuo machines — the pod spins at up to 7,000 RPM while hot water is injected, creating a crema layer that's noticeably thicker than what you get from standard drip brewers. In practice, this means your espresso shots have that velvety golden foam on top, and your larger cups don't taste watery or over-extracted.

What sets the Pop+ apart from the older Vertuo Next is the brew size flexibility. You can pull a 1.35oz espresso, a 2.7oz double espresso, a 5oz gran lungo, a 7.7oz mug, or a full 12oz carafe-style pour. That's a genuinely wide range for a machine this small. The 12oz option is particularly impressive — it brews a full travel-mug serving in about a minute and a half, which is faster than most drip machines.

Heating time is roughly 25 seconds from cold start. That's not the fastest in the Nespresso lineup — the original line machines beat it by about 10 seconds — but it's fast enough that you won't notice the wait.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Looking through the 225 Amazon ratings (4.5 stars average), the praise clusters around two themes: size and simplicity. One reviewer put it plainly: *"Perfect for my studio apartment. It takes up less space than my toaster and makes better coffee than my old Keurig ever did."* Another noted, *"I'm not a coffee person — I'm a 'I need caffeine and I need it now' person. This thing is idiot-proof."*

The crema quality gets consistent mention too. Multiple buyers note the espresso shots rival their local coffee shops, which is high praise for a sub-$200 machine.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, you're locked into Nespresso's Vertuo pods — they're proprietary, they're RFID-tagged, and they cost roughly $0.90 to $1.25 each. There is no third-party alternative, and refillable Vertuo pods are notoriously unreliable because the barcode system rejects them. If you like buying bulk coffee from Costco, this is not your machine.

Second, the 24oz water tank is genuinely small. If you're brewing two 12oz carafes back-to-back, you're refilling mid-morning. This is fine for singles or couples, but it's a real friction point for households of three or more.

Third, the "Pop+" name suggests you get something extra, but there's no built-in frother. You'll need to buy the separate Aeroccino (another $100+) if you want lattes or cappuccinos.

## Who Should Buy This

The Pop+ is perfect for: apartment dwellers with limited counter space, students who want espresso without a full espresso machine's learning curve, and anyone who drinks coffee in small quantities throughout the day and values variety over bulk efficiency.

Skip it if: you're on a tight per-cup budget, you regularly brew for more than two people, or you want a machine that accepts any coffee brand.

## FAQ

**Can I use regular Nespresso OriginalLine pods in the Vertuo Pop+?**
No. The Vertuo system uses larger, dome-shaped pods with barcodes. OriginalLine pods physically won't fit. You're buying into the Vertuo ecosystem specifically.

**How often do I need to descale the machine?**
Nespresso recommends descaling every 3 months or after every 300 capsules, whichever comes first. The machine has an indicator light that tells you when it's due.

**Does it come with a warranty?**
Yes — 1 year standard manufacturer warranty, and Nespresso offers an extended warranty plan if you buy pods directly through their subscription program.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want genuine espresso and full-size coffee from a machine that fits in a shoebox, and you don't mind the proprietary pod cost.

**Skip it if:** You're a high-volume household, a budget-conscious coffee drinker, or you want milk-based drinks without buying a second appliance.

The Nespresso Vertuo Pop+ delivers exactly what it promises: compact, fast, versatile coffee with excellent crema. It's not the cheapest way to drink coffee, but it's one of the most convenient — and at 4.6/5, it earns its place on your counter.
