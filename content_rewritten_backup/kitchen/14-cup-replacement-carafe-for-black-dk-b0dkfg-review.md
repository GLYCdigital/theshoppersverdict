## Where It Falls Short

Here’s the honest part. The 4.5-star average isn’t perfect, and the complaints are worth heeding.

First, the **fit isn’t universal across all Black DK models**. A few reviewers report that the carafe sits slightly high in their machine, making it hard to close the lid when the carafe is in place. This appears to be a manufacturing tolerance issue—most units fit fine, but a small percentage don’t. If you have an older Black DK model, double-check the dimensions against your current carafe before ordering.

Second, **the handle is a bit slippery when wet**. Not a dealbreaker, but if you’re prone to dropping things first thing in the morning, be careful. A textured grip would have been a nice touch.

Third, **the markings on the side fade over time**. The measurement lines are printed, not embossed. After a few months of dishwasher cycles, they start to wear. Hand-washing will prolong them, but it’s a minor annoyance for a replacement part.
