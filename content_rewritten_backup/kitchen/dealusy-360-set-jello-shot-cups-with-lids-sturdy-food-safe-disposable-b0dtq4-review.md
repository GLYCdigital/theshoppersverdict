Let’s be blunt: buying 360 of anything sight-unseen is a gamble. But when you’re prepping for a wedding, a college tailgate, or a backyard BBQ where you’d rather mingle than pour shots, bulk is the only way to go. The Dealusy 360 Set Jello Shot Cups with Lids promises to be the last set you’ll ever need. I’ve dug into the specs and buyer feedback to see if these clear plastic cups earn their 4.8-star rating.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains 360 cups and 360 matching lids—no mixing and matching, which is rarer than you’d think in this category. The cups are the standard 2-ounce size, which is the sweet spot for a solid Jell-O shot (about 1.5 oz of liquid plus room for your fruit garnish). The plastic is a semi-rigid, crystal-clear PET-like material. They are technically disposable, but as I’ll get into, “disposable” does a disservice to how sturdy these feel in hand.

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

I’ve handled flimsy shot cups that buckle the moment you pour warm liquid in. These don’t. The walls are noticeably thicker than the cheap solo-cup alternatives you find at party stores. The base is flat and weighted enough that they don’t tip over when you set them on a tray to chill—a critical detail when you’re moving 50 cups from counter to fridge.

The lids are where this set shines. They snap on with a satisfying *click* and form a tight seal. Several verified buyers noted that they inverted the cups to test for leaks. One reviewer wrote, *“I filled them with liquid, snapped the top on, and shook them like a bartender. Not a drop came out.”* That seal matters because a leaky lid will ruin a fridge shelf and make transporting shots to a party a nightmare.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The durability is the headline. In a category filled with one-time-use flimsiness, buyers repeatedly mention that these cups feel reusable. One top review stated, *“I’ve washed and reused these three times now for different events. They still look new.”* That is a huge value add.

The clarity of the plastic is also a hit. When you’re making layered shots (think piña colada on the bottom, strawberry on top), you want the colors to pop. The clear walls make your work look professional. Many party hosts also used them for pudding shots and mini-desserts, which tells you the 2-oz size is versatile beyond just Jell-O.

**Where It Falls Short**

Nothing is perfect. The cups are BPA-free, but they are *not* microwave-safe. If you’re melting gelatin in the cup itself, you’ll need to mix in a separate bowl and pour. A few buyers also mentioned that the lids, while secure, can be a bit tough to remove if you stack them tightly after filling. You’ll want to give them a slight twist before pulling.

Also, while they are sturdy, they are still plastic. If you’re looking for an eco-friendly, compostable option for a high-end event, this isn’t it. These are designed for convenience and reusability in a pinch, not for sustainability.

2. **The Batch Prepper:** If you make boozy treats for holidays or game days, having 360 on hand means you never run out mid-project.

**FAQ: What Shoppers Ask**

**Q: Are these cups reusable or strictly one-time-use?**
A: They are marketed as disposable, but due to the thick walls, many buyers report washing and reusing them several times by hand. They are not dishwasher-safe (the heat can warp them), so hand-wash only if you plan to reuse.

**Q: Can I use these for hot liquids?**
A: No. The plastic is not heat-resistant. If you pour boiling water directly into them, they will warp. Always mix your gelatin in a heat-safe bowl first, then pour into the cups.

**Q: What is the exact capacity?**
A: They hold 2 ounces to the brim. For a classic shot, fill them to about 1.5 ounces to leave room for the lid to seal properly without overflow.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a bulk set that won’t crack, leak, or let you down on party day. The seal quality and durable plastic put this a cut above the generic alternatives.

**Skip it if:** You need microwave-safe cups or you’re looking for a biodegradable, eco-friendly option.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The only reason it’s not a perfect 5 is the lack of microwave safety and the tight lid removal, but for the price per unit, this is the best value in bulk shot cups on the market.
