## Where It Falls Short

Honesty time. The metal lid inserts aren't fully stainless. If you hand-wash the lids and leave them in the drying rack wet, a few buyers report small rust spots forming on the ring edges over time. The fix is simple — dry them immediately — but it's worth knowing before you buy.

Also, there are no measurement markings on the glass. For a canning or meal-prep jar, that's fine. For someone hoping to use these as measuring containers, you'll need to use a separate cup. Minor, but it affects how you'll use them.

**Are these lids compatible with Ball or Kerr jars?**
Yes. The threading is standard Mason jar sizing, so you can swap in any regular-mouth Ball or Kerr lid if you need to replace one. That's a huge plus — you're not locked into a proprietary system.

**Can these go in the dishwasher?**

**Are they safe for canning?**
