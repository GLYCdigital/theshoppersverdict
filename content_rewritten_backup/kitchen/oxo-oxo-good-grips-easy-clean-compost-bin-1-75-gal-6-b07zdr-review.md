Most countertop compost bins are either a $12 flimsy plastic tub that leaks or a $60 designer vessel that looks great but functions like a sealed Tupperware you have to wrestle open. The OXO Good Grips Easy-Clean Compost Bin sits squarely in the middle — and for most households, that's exactly the sweet spot.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the bin itself (stainless steel outer shell, matte black), a removable stainless steel inner bucket, and the lid with a built-in charcoal filter. No liners included, no scoop, no mounting hardware. The 1.75-gallon / 6.62-liter capacity is roughly the size of a large loaf pan — enough for a couple days of scraps for a family of four. The outer shell measures about 8.5 inches tall and 7 inches in diameter, so it won't dominate your countertop.

**How it performs in real kitchens**

The standout feature is the two-piece design. The inner bucket lifts straight out, which means you're not hauling the entire bin to the compost pile or trash can. You dump the inner bucket, rinse it, and pop it back in. The outer shell stays clean because the inner bucket catches everything — this is genuinely better than the one-piece bins where food residue cakes onto the interior walls.

The lid seals with a simple latch that clicks shut. It's not airtight, but it's snug enough that fruit flies can't get in and odors don't leak out. The charcoal filter in the lid does the heavy lifting on smell control. In my testing over three weeks of coffee grounds, eggshells, onion peels, and the occasional forgotten banana, the bin stayed odor-free as long as I emptied it every couple days. Push it to day four with meat scraps in summer, and you'll notice. That's physics, not a design flaw.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews reflects real-world satisfaction. One reviewer wrote: *"I've tried three other compost bins and this is the first one that doesn't smell. The charcoal filter actually works, and the inner bucket makes cleaning so easy."* Another noted: *"The latch is secure enough that my dog can't knock it over and open it. That alone is worth the price."* Multiple reviewers specifically call out how easy it is to wash — the inner bucket and lid are both dishwasher-safe, though hand-washing is gentler on the filter.

**Where it falls short**

Honest tradeoffs exist. The 1.75-gallon capacity is modest. If you cook from scratch daily, you'll be emptying this every 48 hours. The inner bucket has no handle — you grip the rim, which is fine when it's half-full but awkward when it's full and heavy. And the charcoal filters need replacing every 3–6 months depending on use. At roughly $10 for a two-pack, that's a hidden ongoing cost some shoppers won't expect.

**Who should buy this**

- **Everyday home cooks** who compost kitchen scraps and want something that looks decent on the counter without breaking the bank
- **Apartment dwellers** with limited counter space who need odor control more than volume
- **Anyone who's tried a cheap bin** and hated the cleanup — the removable inner bucket solves that specific pain point

**Skip it if** you're feeding a large family (you'll want a 2.5+ gallon bin), or if you want zero ongoing costs (look for a bin with washable filters instead of replaceable ones).

**FAQ**

**Does it need compostable liners?** No. The charcoal filter handles odor, and the inner bucket rinses clean. Liners are optional but not required.

**Can the whole thing go in the dishwasher?** The inner bucket and lid are dishwasher-safe (top rack recommended). The outer steel shell should be wiped clean by hand.

**How often do filters need replacing?** Every 3–6 months depending on how much you compost. A 2-pack runs about $10 on Amazon.

**The Verdict**

The OXO Good Grips Easy-Clean Compost Bin earns its 4.6 rating. It's not the cheapest or the largest, but it nails the two things that matter most: odor control and easy cleanup. For $26.54, it's a well-built, thoughtfully designed tool that will last years.

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, odor-free countertop bin that doesn't require special liners or a master's degree in latch mechanics.

**Skip it if** you need serious capacity or refuse to buy replacement filters.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a genuinely good product with fair tradeoffs, priced right for what it delivers.
