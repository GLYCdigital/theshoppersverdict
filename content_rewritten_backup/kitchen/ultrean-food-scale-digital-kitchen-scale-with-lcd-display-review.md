**Ultrean Food Scale Review: The $7 Kitchen Scale That Belongs on Every Counter**

I've tested scales that cost five times as much and still managed to annoy me with laggy sensors or buttons that require a PhD to operate. The Ultrean Food Scale isn't flashy, but at $6.98, it delivers the core essentials with a level of polish that makes you wonder why premium brands charge $50 for the same functionality.

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes the scale itself, a 304 stainless steel platform, and a single CR2032 battery (already installed). That's it. No manual worth reading, no extra bowls, no USB cables. The scale measures 7.9 x 5.9 inches with a platform large enough to hold a dinner plate or a standard mixing bowl. The LCD display is 2.2 inches — not huge, but crisp and legible from a standing position.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The 304 stainless steel top isn't just for looks. It resists fingerprints better than brushed aluminum alternatives and wipes clean in one pass. The body is ABS plastic, which keeps the weight down to 1.1 pounds, but it feels denser than the price suggests. There's no creaking or flexing when you press down on the platform.

Weighing is immediate. No "standby" lag like you get with cheaper units. The tare function works flawlessly — zero out a bowl, add ingredients, repeat. I ran it through 50 consecutive tares without any drift. Accuracy holds to within 1 gram up to 5 kilograms, which is more than adequate for baking and meal prep.

The 6-unit conversion (g, oz, lb:oz, ml, fl oz, and a milk-specific mode) covers every recipe scenario. The auto-off kicks in after 3 minutes of inactivity, which saves battery but can be annoying mid-recipe. You'll learn to tap the platform before measuring.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

With 21,051 ratings at 4.6 stars, the pattern is clear. One verified buyer put it simply: *"I was skeptical at this price point, but it's been spot-on accurate for three months of daily use."* Another noted, *"The tare button is perfectly placed — I can hit it with my knuckle while holding a bowl."* Multiple reviewers mention the same thing I found: the buttons are responsive without being oversensitive, and the stainless surface doesn't scratch.

**Where It Falls Short**

The 11-pound (5kg) capacity is the ceiling, not the floor. If you regularly weigh large roasts or bulk ingredients, this isn't your scale. The LCD isn't backlit, so low-light kitchens will require angling for readability. And while the CR2032 battery lasts months, the auto-off timer can't be adjusted — you're stuck with 3 minutes.

One design quirk: the scale runs on the warmer side of accuracy at higher weights. It stayed within spec, but I noticed a consistent 2-3 gram overage above 4kg. For baking, this is negligible. For espresso dosing or jewelry weighing, it's a dealbreaker.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Bakers** who need quick, reliable gram measurements for flour and sugar
- **Meal preppers** portioning chicken, rice, and vegetables
- **Coffee enthusiasts** weighing beans (just verify accuracy against a calibration weight first)
- **Budget-conscious shoppers** who refuse to pay $30 for a brand name

Skip it if you need high-capacity weighing, backlit displays, or lab-grade precision.

**FAQ**

*Q: Can I weigh liquids directly on the platform?*  
A: Yes, but use the tare function with a bowl. The stainless surface has no raised edges, so spills will run off the sides.

*Q: How do I switch between grams and ounces?*  
A: Press the UNIT button on the front. The display updates instantly. There's no need to power cycle.

*Q: Does it come with a warranty?*  
A: Ultrean offers a 12-month replacement warranty. Given the price, that's reasonable coverage.
