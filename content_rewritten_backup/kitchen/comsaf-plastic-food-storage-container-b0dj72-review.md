## Where It Falls Short

The locking tabs require genuine effort to close and open. If you have arthritis, carpal tunnel, or just weak grip strength, you'll wrestle with these lids daily. It's a tradeoff for the airtight seal — looser lids leak, tighter lids fight back.

Second, "leakproof" has limits. While the seal holds when upright, laying these on their side in a lunch bag with thin soup will result in a mess. The corners aren't liquid-tight under pressure. For thick sauces and dry goods, you're fine. For broth-heavy meals, keep them upright or buy a dedicated soup container.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You meal prep on Sundays, want to actually see what's in your fridge, and need containers that survive both the dishwasher and the microwave without degrading. **Skip it if:** You have limited hand strength, need genuinely leakproof containers for wet lunches, or you're someone who's already invested in a glass container system and doesn't need a second set.
