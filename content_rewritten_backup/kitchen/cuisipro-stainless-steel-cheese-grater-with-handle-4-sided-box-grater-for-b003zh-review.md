Most box graters are flimsy sheet metal that flexes the moment you apply real pressure to a block of aged cheddar. The Cuisipro is not that. This is the grater that feels like a kitchen tool, not a toy — and at 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, I'm not the only one who noticed.

## What You Actually Get

The Cuisipro measures roughly 10.5 x 5 x 4.5 inches and weighs about a pound. The entire body is 18/8 stainless steel — no plastic anywhere, including the base. That matters more than you'd think. The handle is a comfortable, slightly angled grip that keeps your palm away from the grating surface.

Four sides give you: a coarse grater for cheddar and mozzarella, a fine grater for Parmesan, a slicer for vegetables like carrots or zucchini, and a zester side for citrus peel or nutmeg. The top flips open so you can clean out trapped bits, though a brush helps for the fine side.

## Performance: Sharp Enough to Notice

The blades are stamped from the same stainless sheet, but they're sharper than most box graters I've tested. A block of Parmigiano-Reggiano goes through the fine side with minimal pressure — no rocking back and forth, no mashed cheese paste. The coarse side produces uniform shreds that melt evenly, which matters for pizza night.

One thing buyers consistently mention: the sharpness stays. A verified purchaser wrote, "I've had this grater for three years and it still cuts like the day I bought it." Another noted, "Finally a grater that doesn't make me dread grating cheese. The handle is actually comfortable." These aren't paid placements — they're the kind of feedback that shows up when a product just works.

## Where It Falls Short

The base is flat stainless steel with no rubber grip. On a smooth quartz or granite countertop, it will slide unless you hold it or put a damp towel underneath. That's a minor annoyance, but at this price point, a silicone strip would've been easy to add.

Also, the sharp edges are genuinely sharp. If you have kids helping in the kitchen, this isn't the grater to hand them unsupervised. And while the manufacturer says it's dishwasher-safe, the fine blades will dull faster with repeated machine washing. Hand-wash it. Your future self will thank you.

## FAQ

**Is this grater actually dishwasher safe?**
Technically yes, but the fine zester side will dull faster with detergent and heat cycles. Hand-washing preserves the blade edges for years longer.

**Will it rust?**
No — 18/8 stainless is the same grade used in quality cookware. Just dry it after washing to avoid water spots.

**Can it grate soft cheese like mozzarella?**
Yes, but chill the mozzarella first. Soft cheese will smear on any grater, not just this one.
