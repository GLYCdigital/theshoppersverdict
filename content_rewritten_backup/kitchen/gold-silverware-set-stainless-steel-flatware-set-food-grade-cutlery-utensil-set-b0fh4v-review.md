PVD bonds at the molecular level, so it's dramatically more durable.

### FAQ: What Shoppers Ask

**Q: Is this set dishwasher safe?**
Yes, the PVD finish is dishwasher safe. Most reviewers run it through daily cycles without issues. That said, to maximize the finish's lifespan, use the normal cycle (not heavy-duty) and avoid stacking items on top of the flatware basket.

**Q: Will the gold color fade or chip?**
With normal use, no. PVD coating is the same process used on high-end watch bands and knife blades. It won't flake or peel like painted finishes. Scratches from metal-on-metal contact are possible, but fading and peeling are not reported issues at this price point.

**Q: Is this real gold?**
No — and don't pay extra for anyone claiming it is. It's a PVD-applied gold-tone finish over 18/10 stainless steel. It looks like gold, wears like stainless, and won't cost you a fortune.
