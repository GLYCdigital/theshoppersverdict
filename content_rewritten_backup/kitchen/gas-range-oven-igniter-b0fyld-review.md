## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my Whirlpool/GE/Frigidaire oven?**
A: Likely yes. The 3-hole and 2-hole bracket options cover most American-made ranges from the past 15 years. Measure your existing igniter's bracket spacing to confirm. If it matches the pattern shown in the product images, you're good.

**Q: How do I know if my igniter is actually the problem?**
A: If your oven clicks repeatedly but never lights, or takes several minutes to ignite, it's almost certainly the igniter. You can verify with a multimeter — a good igniter should show 50–100 ohms of resistance. An open circuit (infinite resistance) means it's dead.

**Q: Is this a safety risk to install?**
A: Not if you follow basic precautions. Unplug the range or shut off the breaker before working. You're not touching the gas line — just the electrical connectors. The ceramic body insulates properly, and the spade connectors are standard automotive-style fittings.
