You buy it because your clothes are coming out damp, your dryer's throwing an error code, or you've already opened the back panel and seen the telltale break in the coil. ## What you actually get

Two complete heating element assemblies. Each includes the ceramic insulator blocks, the coiled nichrome wire, and the metal mounting bracket — the whole unit that slides into the heater housing. You don't get the thermal fuse or thermistor (the little sensors that clamp onto the housing), so if those are blown, you'll need to order them separately. That's not a knock on AMI PARTS — it's standard for aftermarket elements — but it's worth budgeting for if your dryer died from overheating rather than a simple coil break.

The bracket is stamped steel with the same hole spacing as the OEM part. As one verified buyer put it: "Bolted right in, no modification needed. Took me 20 minutes to swap out the old one."

## Performance: does it actually heat?

Yes. The 240V coil draws the same wattage as the original (around 5,400W), so dry times are on par with what you had before the failure. Multiple reviewers report their dryers "running like new" after the swap, with one noting: "First load came out bone dry in 45 minutes — same as when the dryer was new."

The one caveat: a few long-term reviews mention the coil looking slightly thinner than the genuine Samsung part. For the average household doing 5–8 loads a week, that's unlikely to matter. If you run a commercial laundry or dry heavy blankets daily, you might get less lifespan — but at this price for two, you're still ahead.

## Where it falls short

- **No safety components included.** If your element burned out because the thermal fuse failed, just swapping the element will kill the new one too. Check the fuse and thermostat with a multimeter first. It's a $10 fix if they're bad.
- **The 2-pack is only a deal if you need two.** If you're fixing one dryer, you'll have a spare — which is honestly smart, since elements fail at the worst times. But if you'd rather pay less upfront, a single element runs about $15–20 less.
- **Terminal connectors are slightly snug.** A handful of buyers mention needing to crimp or file the spade connectors slightly. Not a dealbreaker, but have pliers handy.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my dryer?**  
Check your model against the DC47-00019A compatibility list. If your old element has that part number stamped on it, this will fit. If you're unsure, pull your old element and compare the bracket shape to the photos.

**Q: Do I need to replace the thermal fuse at the same time?**  
If the fuse is blown (test it with a multimeter — should read near zero ohms), yes. If it's fine, reuse it. Don't skip this step; a dead fuse will burn out the new element fast.

**Q: Is the 2-pack worth it for one repair?**  
Yes, if you can stomach the upfront cost. Elements fail every 3–5 years. You'll have a spare ready when it does. If you're on a tight budget, buy a single — but you'll pay shipping twice.
