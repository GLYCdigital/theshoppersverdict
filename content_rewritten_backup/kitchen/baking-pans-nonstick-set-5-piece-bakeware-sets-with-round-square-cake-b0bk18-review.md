## What You're Actually Getting

This set includes two 8-inch round cake pans, one 8-inch square pan, one 9x5-inch loaf pan, and one 9x13-inch sheet pan. That covers the vast majority of home baking needs — birthday cakes, brownies, banana bread, sheet cakes, and roasting vegetables.

The pans are made from heavy-gauge carbon steel, which matters more than most shoppers realize. Thin pans create hot spots and burn edges while the center stays underdone. The heavier construction here distributes heat more evenly, and multiple reviewers noted their baked goods came out uniformly golden.

## Real-World Performance

The nonstick coating is the star. Multiple buyers described cakes "falling right out" with just a light buttering or even no greasing at all. One reviewer wrote: "I've baked three cakes and two batches of brownies — nothing sticks. Cleanup is a quick wipe with a sponge."

The pans also hold up to high heat. The coating is rated to 450°F, which covers virtually any baking recipe. The sheet pan handles roasting vegetables and even frozen pizzas without issue.

## Where It Falls Short

The honest downsides:

**Hand-wash only.** The nonstick coating will not survive the dishwasher. If you're someone who tosses everything in the dishwasher, this set will degrade quickly — peeling and flaking within months. Several reviewers who ignored this warning reported exactly that.

**No storage solution.** The flat pans stack awkwardly, and the nonstick surfaces can scratch each other when stored directly on top of one another. You'll want to add pan protectors or parchment between them.

**The loaf pan is on the smaller side.** At 9x5 inches, it's standard, but if you regularly bake large artisan loaves, you'll need a bigger option.

## FAQ

**Is the nonstick coating safe?**
Yes, the coating is PFOA-free and food-safe. It's a standard nonstick formulation similar to what you'd find on quality cookware from major brands.

**Can I use metal utensils with these pans?**
Technically yes, but you shouldn't. Metal utensils will scratch the coating over time. Use silicone, wood, or plastic tools to extend the life of the nonstick surface.

**Will these work on a glass-top stove?**
These are oven-safe bakeware, not stovetop pans. They're designed for oven use up to 450°F — don't use them on direct heat.
