Here's where this set earns its 4.4-star rating. Buyers consistently report that these plates hold up to daily use. ## What Buyers Praise

The durability theme dominates positive reviews. Multiple buyers mention these replacing previous sets that chipped within weeks. One parent wrote, "With three kids, I expected breakage. ## Where It Falls Short

The most common complaint: the white glaze shows wear. Utensil marks appear on the surface over time, especially if you cut food directly on the plate. Knife scratches are visible up close, though not from across the table. This is inherent to white porcelain, not a defect — but worth knowing before you buy.

The plates aren't labeled microwave-safe on the product itself. Most buyers report using them in microwaves without issues, but the lack of explicit labeling gives some pause. If you plan to use these primarily for microwave reheating, verify with the manufacturer first.

Finally, these plates are noisy when stacked. The porcelain-on-porcelain contact produces a clatter when you pull them from the cabinet. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.
