If your household goes through cereal like mine does — three different boxes open at once, half of them going stale before they're finished — the OXO Good Grips Countertop Cereal Dispenser solves a problem you didn't realize you had. This isn't a gimmick gadget. It's a genuinely functional piece of kitchen infrastructure that keeps cereal fresh and makes breakfast faster.

## What You Actually Get

The unit arrives fully assembled with three clear BPA-free containers, each holding 1.9 liters. That's enough for a standard 12-ounce box of cereal per bin, which means you can load a full variety pack without crushing anything. The design is straightforward: each container sits on a dispensing base with a lever on the front. Pull the lever, cereal drops into your bowl. Release it, the opening closes.

The build quality is typical OXO — thoughtful and durable. The containers lock securely into the base with a satisfying click. The levers are spring-loaded and feel sturdy, not flimsy like cheaper dispensers I've tested. The whole unit weighs about 4 pounds empty, so it stays put when you're dispensing.

## Performance and Real-World Use

Here's where this dispenser earns its keep. The dispensing mechanism actually controls portions. A single lever pull releases roughly one cup of cereal — enough for a modest bowl. Two pulls for a proper breakfast. Compare that to pouring from a box where you either get a trickle or a flood.

The airtight seals on the lids are the real differentiator. I loaded one bin with Rice Krispies and left it for three weeks. Still snap-crackle-popping. Another bin held granola for a month without going stale. That's the feature that justifies the price if you're a slow cereal eater.

The wide-mouth lids flip open easily, and the entire container lifts off the base for refilling. Cleanup is simple — everything except the base is top-rack dishwasher safe, though I recommend hand washing to avoid the plastic clouding over time.

## What Buyers Praise

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews reflects consistent satisfaction. One buyer noted, "My kids can finally serve themselves without dumping half the box into their bowl." Another reviewer highlighted the freshness factor: "Cereal that used to go stale in a week now lasts for weeks. This paid for itself." Multiple reviews mention the sturdy build and the fact that the levers don't jam or stick — a common failure point on cheaper dispensers.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. This thing is big. At 10.6 inches wide and 16 inches tall, it will not fit under most upper cabinets and will dominate your countertop. If your kitchen is tight, measure your space before ordering.

The included measuring cup holds 1.5 cups — that's a large bowl of cereal by most standards. If you're tracking portions for health reasons, ignore the cup and use your own.

Finally, the clear plastic scratches. Not immediately, but over time if you scrub it. Hand wash with a soft sponge and it'll stay clear for years. Use abrasive pads and it'll look foggy within months.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have kids who serve themselves cereal, you buy cereal in bulk, or you're tired of stale half-eaten boxes cluttering your pantry. Also great for households with multiple cereal preferences — everyone gets their own bin.

**Skip it if:** You have minimal counter space, you only eat cereal occasionally, or you prefer your cereal in the pantry rather than on display. This is a countertop appliance — it's meant to be seen.

## FAQ

**Can this dispense different cereal sizes without crushing them?**
Yes. The opening is wide enough that even large flakes like granola clusters dispense without breaking. The lever mechanism drops cereal straight down rather than grinding it through a chute like some cheaper models.

**Does it keep cereal fresh, or is it just a dispenser?**
The airtight lids genuinely keep cereal fresh. I tested it with three different cereals over several weeks and noticed no staleness. The seals are the same quality you'd find on premium storage containers.

**Is it easy to clean?**
The containers lift off the base and are dishwasher safe (top rack). The base should be wiped down with a damp cloth — cereal dust accumulates there. Total cleaning time is about five minutes.
