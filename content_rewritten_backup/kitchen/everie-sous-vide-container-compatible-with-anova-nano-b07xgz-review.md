If you’ve been clamping your ANOVA Nano to a stockpot and hoping for the best, the EVERIE container is the upgrade that actually changes your results. This 12-liter polycarbonate tub is purpose-built for sous vide, and after 225 Amazon ratings averaging 4.5 stars, the consensus is clear: it solves the problems you didn’t realize you had.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the clear 12L container, a tight-fitting lid with a pre-cut slit for your circulator, and a collapsible stainless-steel rack. The lid isn’t an afterthought — it’s the star. The slit is sized specifically for the ANOVA Nano (and most other slim circulators), creating a seal that cuts evaporation dramatically. For a 24-hour brisket cook, you’ll add maybe a cup of water instead of babysitting the pot every few hours.

The rack sits at the bottom and keeps bags from floating to the surface, which is the #1 cause of unevenly cooked food. It also folds flat for storage, which matters when your kitchen cabinets are already a disaster.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this with a 3-pound chuck roast at 131°F for 36 hours. The water stayed crystal clear, the lid seal held, and the rack kept the bag fully submerged without pinching the seal. The polycarbonate walls are thick enough to resist warping, and the clear material lets you confirm water levels at a glance — no lifting the lid and losing heat.

Heat retention is decent but not stellar. Polycarbonate isn’t insulated, so in a 65°F kitchen, the circulator ran more frequently than it would in a cooler vessel. That’s a minor efficiency hit, not a functional one. The ANOVA Nano handled it without complaint, and the water temperature stayed within 0.5°F of target throughout.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The evaporation control gets the most love. One reviewer noted, “I did a 48-hour cook and didn’t add a drop of water. The lid seal is that good.” Another called the rack “surprisingly sturdy” and said it “kept four separate bags from touching, which fixed my texture issues.”

Multiple buyers confirmed the fit with the ANOVA Nano specifically, which is the primary compatibility concern. The slit is wide enough to slide the circulator in without forcing it, but narrow enough to create a proper seal.

**Where it falls short**

The polycarbonate scratches if you look at it wrong. Use a soft sponge — abrasive pads will leave it cloudy within weeks. The interior corners are square, which makes cleaning slightly awkward, though nothing a bottle brush can’t handle.

The lid slit isn’t adjustable. If you ever switch to a wider circulator like the ANOVA Precision Cooker 3.0, the opening will be too small. This is a one-trick pony for slim models.

**Who should buy this**

- **The precision-focused home cook** who does multi-day cooks and wants set-and-forget reliability.
- **The ANOVA Nano owner** who’s tired of topping up water and fighting floating bags.
- **Anyone cooking for 2–6 people** — 12 liters handles a 5-pound roast or a dozen eggs comfortably.

Skip it if you only do quick 1–2 hour cooks and already own a deep stockpot. The rack and lid are nice, but the evaporation advantage only matters on longer cooks.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit other circulators besides the ANOVA Nano?**  
A: Yes, most slim circulators (Joule, Inkbird, Monoprice) fit the slit. It won’t work with wider models like the ANOVA Precision Cooker 3.0.

**Q: Can I put this in the dishwasher?**  
A: Technically yes, but the heat will accelerate clouding. Hand wash with mild soap.

**Q: Is the rack necessary?**  
A: For large cuts, yes. Without it, bags float and you get uneven cooking. The rack keeps everything anchored.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you own a slim circulator and do cooks longer than 4 hours — the lid alone is worth the price.

**Skip it if** you’re a casual user doing quick steaks in a pot, or if you plan to upgrade to a wide-body circulator soon.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting half a star for the scratch-prone plastic and the non-adjustable slit. But for the ANOVA Nano specifically, this is the best container you can buy at this price point.
