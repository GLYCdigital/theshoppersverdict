## What You Actually Get

The unit is a flat heating plate, roughly the size of a large coaster, with a short power cord and a temperature dial. As shown in the product image, it's designed to sit under your existing candle jar — no proprietary vessels required. The heating element warms from below, gently melting the wax and releasing the fragrance oils without ever igniting them.

There's no assembly. Plug it in, set your candle on top, dial in the heat, and walk away. The adjustable thermostat is the key feature here — it's not just an on/off switch. You can ramp it up for a quick scent throw in a large room or dial it down for subtle, prolonged fragrance.

## Performance and Build Quality

The standout feature is **even melting**. Because heat radiates from the bottom, the entire wax pool liquefies uniformly. This eliminates the classic tunneling problem where a burned candle leaves a thick wax ring around the glass. You'll get every last drop of fragrance out of each candle — and that's real money back in your pocket.

The temperature control actually works. At the low setting, the plate holds wax at a gentle melt; on high, it produces a visible melt pool within 15–20 minutes. The auto shut-off kicks in after several hours, which is a genuine safety feature for people who forget to unplug things.

Build quality is solid for the price point. The exterior stays cool to the touch on the sides, and the non-slip feet keep the unit planted. It's lightweight enough to move between rooms easily.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. **The plate gets genuinely hot** — not burn-your-hand-off hot, but hot enough to be a concern in homes with toddlers or curious pets. The auto shut-off helps, but it's not a childproof device.

Second, **very small candles don't perform well**. A 3oz tin or short votive may not make solid contact with the plate's surface, resulting in slow, weak scent throw. Stick to standard 8–16oz jars and you'll be fine.

Finally, the scent throw is **never as aggressive as a lit candle**. If you like your room *saturated* in fragrance within five minutes, a warmer won't deliver that. It's a gentler, longer-lasting scent experience.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this work with any candle brand?**
A: Most standard 8–16oz candles in glass jars or tins work great. The plate is about 5 inches across, so wider jars sit fine. Extremely narrow or unusually tall jars may be unstable.

**Q: How long does a candle last on the warmer?**
A: Expect 2–3 times longer than burning. A 12oz candle that burns for 40 hours might give you 80–100 hours of scent on low-to-medium heat.

**Q: Does it melt wax evenly or just the bottom?**
A: The entire wax pool melts uniformly — that's the biggest advantage over top-down warmers. The heat distributes across the plate, so the whole candle liquifies.
