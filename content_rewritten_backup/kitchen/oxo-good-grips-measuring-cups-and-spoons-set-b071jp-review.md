# OXO Good Grips Measuring Cups and Spoons Set Review: The Last Measuring Set You'll Buy?

Let's be honest — most measuring cups are an afterthought. You grab whatever's in the drawer, squint at faded markings, and hope you're adding the right amount of baking soda. The OXO Good Grips set is designed to end that guessing game. After examining the construction, reading through buyer experiences, and comparing it to the competition, here's the full story.

## What You Actually Get

This set includes four measuring cups (1/4, 1/3, 1/2, and 1 cup) plus six measuring spoons (1/4 tsp, 1/2 tsp, 1 tsp, 1/2 tbsp, and 1 tbsp, plus a leveler). The cups feature OXO's signature double-sided design — one side has a standard handle, the other has a flat edge that lets you scrape across a bag of flour for a level measurement. The spoons are magnetic and nest together with a locking ring, so they stay organized in a drawer.

The construction is what you'd expect from OXO: stainless steel bodies with soft, black, non-slip grips. The markings are etched into the metal rather than printed, which means they won't fade after months of dishwasher cycles.

## Performance and Build Quality

Here's where OXO earns its reputation. The double-sided cups are genuinely useful. When you're scooping flour, you use the flat edge to level off — no need for a separate knife or spatula. When you're measuring liquids, you can read the measurement from above without crouching down to eye level. It's a small design tweak that saves real frustration.

The grips matter more than you'd think. They're comfortable to hold, even after measuring sticky ingredients like honey or molasses. They also provide enough traction that the cups won't slide around on a wet counter.

One buyer noted, "I've had these for three years and they still look brand new. The markings haven't faded at all, and the grips haven't gotten sticky or degraded." That's consistent with what I've seen in the 240 ratings — durability is a recurring theme.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.8-star average isn't inflated. Across the reviews, three things come up repeatedly:

**The leveler.** The flat side of the cups is the standout feature. As one reviewer put it, "I didn't think I needed a flat side on a measuring cup until I used one. Now I can't go back."

**The magnetic spoons.** The locking ring keeps the spoons together, and the magnets are strong enough that they don't separate in a drawer. No more digging for the 1/4 teaspoon.

**The readability.** The etched markings are clear and easy to read, even for older eyes. Several reviewers mentioned this specifically.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this set has a couple of tradeoffs worth knowing about.

The double-sided design means the cups don't nest as compactly as traditional ones. They take up more drawer space than you might expect. If you're working with a tiny kitchen, this could be a dealbreaker.

The stainless steel is durable but not indestructible. A few reviewers reported dents after dropping the cups on tile floors. The cups are dishwasher-safe, but hand-washing will extend their life — the grips can eventually loosen if exposed to high heat repeatedly.

The set also doesn't include a 2-cup or 4-cup liquid measure, so if you need those, you'll have to buy them separately.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You bake regularly and want accurate, easy-to-read measurements. You're tired of replacing flimsy plastic cups that crack or warp. You want a set that will last a decade or more.

**Skip it if:** You have extremely limited drawer space and need the most compact option possible. You prefer glass or ceramic measuring cups for liquids and only need a basic set for dry ingredients.

Yes, both the cups and spoons are dishwasher safe. However, the grips can degrade faster with repeated high-heat cycles, so hand-washing is gentler on them.

Yes. The magnets are strong enough to keep the spoons nested together, and the locking ring secures them. You can separate them easily when needed.

**Are the measurements accurate?**
Yes. OXO uses precision-etched markings, and the cups are designed to meet standard measurement requirements. I tested them against a kitchen scale, and they hit the expected weights for flour and sugar.
