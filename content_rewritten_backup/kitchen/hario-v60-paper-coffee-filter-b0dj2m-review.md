### What You Actually Get
This is a box of 40 cone-shaped paper filters, specifically designed for the V60 series (01 and 02 sizes). They are unbleached, which gives them that natural brown color, and they are crimped along the seam. The crimping isn't just aesthetic; it prevents the filter from collapsing and fusing to the brewer, which keeps the flow rate consistent. The paper is relatively thick, which is a double-edged sword we'll get to shortly.

### Performance and Real-World Use
The V60's entire design philosophy relies on a specific spiral ribbing inside the cone. These filters are engineered to sit flush against those ribs, creating channels for air to escape and water to flow. In practice, this means you get a very clean cup with zero sediment, which is the hallmark of pour-over. The 40-count box is a standard size; if you brew one pot a day, it lasts just over a month.

Here's the critical part: **The V60 requires a gooseneck kettle and a scale.** You cannot just dump water in and walk away. If you are looking for a set-and-forget drip machine, this is not that. But if you are chasing a specific flavor profile—bright, tea-like, and complex—this filter is the essential delivery mechanism for that.

### Where It Falls Short
Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. **The paper is thick.** This means your brew time will be longer compared to thinner filters. If you're using a medium-fine grind, you might find your drawdown time creeping toward the 4-minute mark, which can over-extract and introduce bitterness. You must dial in your grind size to compensate. If you use a coarse grind, you'll be fine; if you're on a fine grind, expect to adjust.

Also, the 40-count box is smaller than some competitors. If you're a heavy user, you'll be reordering frequently. And there's no avoiding the price-per-filter math; it's higher than a standard basket filter, but comparable to other specialty cone filters. Finally, the unbleached paper has a slight earthy smell right out of the box—it demands a thorough rinse or you'll taste it in your first sip.

### FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask
**1. Can I use these with a Chemex or other pour-over brewer?**
No. These are specifically designed for the V60's 60-degree cone angle. A Chemex uses a thicker, half-moon filter. Using these in another brewer will result in a poor seal and a weak, watery brew.

**2. Do I really need to rinse the filter before brewing?**
Yes. The unbleached paper has a slight papery taste. A quick rinse with hot water (about 30 seconds) wets the paper, removes loose fibers, and pre-heats your mug or carafe. Skipping this step will compromise the flavor.

**3. What's the difference between the 01 and 02 sizes?**
The 01 is for a single cup (1-2 cups). The 02 fits the larger V60-02 brewer (1-4 cups) and is the most common size. Make sure you're buying the correct size for your dripper; an 01 filter in an 02 brewer will drop too low and collapse.
