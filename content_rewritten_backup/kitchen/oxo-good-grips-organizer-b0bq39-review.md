# OXO Good Grips Organizer Review: The Kitchen Drawer Solution You Didn't Know You Needed

**The Short Version:** If your kitchen drawers are a chaotic mess of measuring spoons, whisks, and random spatulas, the OXO Good Grips Organizer is the $15-ish fix that will change how you cook. This isn't a flashy gadget — it's the boring, functional backbone your kitchen has been missing.

---

## What You're Actually Getting

The OXO Good Grips Organizer is a countertop or drawer organizer designed to hold your most-used cooking tools upright and within reach. It features a weighted, non-slip base and a divided interior that keeps utensils separated instead of tangled together. The dimensions are compact enough to fit in most standard drawers but substantial enough to hold a full set of cooking tools without tipping over.

The build is classic OXO: soft-touch grips, sturdy ABS plastic, and clean lines that don't look out of place on a counter. It's not going to win design awards, but it doesn't need to — it's built to work.

---

## Real-World Performance: Does It Actually Work?

Here's the thing about utensil organizers: they either hold your tools upright and accessible, or they're useless plastic bins. The OXO falls firmly in the first camp. The weighted base genuinely prevents tipping — even with heavier items like metal tongs or a potato masher leaning against one side. The divided slots keep similar tools grouped, so you're not digging through a pile to find your spatula mid-cook.

What buyers consistently mention is how it **cleans up their workspace**:

> *"I was skeptical about spending money on something so simple, but this cleared off my counter completely. Everything I use daily is right there, no digging through drawers."*

Another reviewer noted:

> *"The non-slip bottom is no joke. I've knocked it twice while grabbing for something behind it and it hasn't budged."*

That non-slip grip is the standout feature. Most organizers slide around when you pull tools out; this one stays planted.

---

## Where It Falls Short

Honest talk: this isn't a miracle worker. If you have more than eight to ten tools, you'll outgrow it quickly. The slots are generous but not unlimited, and oversized items like a rolling pin or immersion blender won't fit. 

Some users also noted that very short utensils (like small measuring spoons) can sink to the bottom and become slightly harder to grab than if they were in a drawer. That's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

The price point varies, but it's typically in the $12–$18 range. For that money, you're getting a well-built organizer — but you could also buy a drawer divider set for less if you prefer the drawer-storage route.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Will this fit in a standard drawer?**
A: Yes, most standard kitchen drawers are deep enough. Measure your drawer depth first — it's about 8 inches long and 5 inches wide, so it needs roughly that footprint.

**Q: Can it hold heavy items like cast iron?**
A: No. It's designed for standard kitchen utensils — plastic, silicone, and light metal tools. A cast iron skillet or heavy meat mallet will overwhelm it.

**Q: Is it dishwasher safe?**
A: The container itself can be hand-washed easily; OXO recommends hand washing to preserve the non-slip base. The base pad should not go in the dishwasher.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It's not exciting, but it's exactly what it promises: a sturdy, non-slip organizer that keeps your daily tools accessible. The build quality is consistent with OXO's reputation, the weighted base actually works, and it solves a real kitchen annoyance without adding clutter.

**Buy it if** you're tired of fishing for your favorite spatula. **Skip it if** you need industrial-grade storage for a serious cookware collection.

For the price, this is one of those rare "buy once, forget about it" kitchen items. That's about the highest compliment you can pay a gadget.
