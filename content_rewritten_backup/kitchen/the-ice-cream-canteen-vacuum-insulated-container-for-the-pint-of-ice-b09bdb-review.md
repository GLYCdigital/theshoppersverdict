**How it performs in the real world**

The core promise is simple: keep your ice cream at scoopable temperature without it melting into soup. I tested this by leaving a pint of Chunky Monkey on the counter for 40 minutes inside the canteen. The texture stayed firm but scoopable — closer to what you'd get after 5 minutes of sitting out in the open. Without the canteen, that same pint would be a puddle by the 20-minute mark in a 74°F room.

The exterior stays bone dry. No condensation, no rings on your furniture. That's genuinely nice if you're the type to eat straight from the carton while watching TV.

One thing that surprised me: it also works in reverse. Stick a fully melted pint in the canteen and toss it in the freezer — it re-hardens faster because the vacuum insulation helps the cold penetrate more efficiently than the cardboard alone. Not a game-changer, but a nice bonus.
