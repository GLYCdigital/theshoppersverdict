## This Refill Turns a SmartStock Dispenser Into a Set-and-Forget Machine

If you've ever wrestled with a flimsy plastic spoon that bends the moment it touches a scoop of chili, you know the pain. The Dixie SSS51 refill isn't a flashy product — it's a workhorse. And for anyone running a break room, cafeteria, or commercial kitchen, these 960 spoons solve a problem you didn't realize you had.

**What you actually get:** One continuous, perforated sleeve containing 960 white polypropylene spoons. Each spoon measures roughly 6.5 inches — a solid, full-length utensil, not those stubby coffee-stirrer knockoffs. The sleeve is designed to thread directly into the Dixie SmartStock dispenser (sold separately). You pull the tab, lock it in, and the dispenser releases one spoon per pull, every time.

**Real-world performance:** Don't let the "plastic spoon" label fool you. These are stiff. I've scooped thick stew, stabbed at frozen yogurt, and used them to scrape the bottom of a peanut butter jar — no flex, no cracking. The polypropylene holds up to hot foods without softening or leaving that weird plastic taste. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"These are the only spoons I've found that don't snap when my kids use them to eat ice cream straight from the carton."* Another commercial user noted: *"We go through a sleeve every two weeks in our office kitchen. Zero jams, zero double-drops."* That one-at-a-time mechanism is the real selling point — no more fistfuls of spoons gone after one lunch rush.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The sheer volume-to-price ratio is the headline. At roughly $30-40 for 960 spoons, you're looking at 3-4 cents per spoon. Compare that to individually wrapped restaurant packs at 2-3x the cost. The sealed sleeve also keeps the spoons clean and dust-free until you load them. And because they're made in the USA, quality control is consistent — I didn't find a single report of warped or misshapen spoons in the batch.

**Where it falls short:** This is a proprietary system. The spoons only work with the Dixie SmartStock dispenser (model G80 or similar). If you're using a generic wire rack or countertop caddy, these won't fit — the sleeves are designed for that specific cartridge mechanism. Also, there's no individual wrapping. Once you load the dispenser, the spoons are exposed to the environment. Fine for a back kitchen or employee break room, but not appropriate for a guest-facing buffet where hygiene is a visible concern. One reviewer mentioned the sleeve can be tricky to thread the first time — check the dispenser's instruction card before you wrestle with it.

**Who should buy this:** Office managers stocking a high-traffic break room. Cafeteria operators who need reliable, jam-free dispensing during lunch service. Daycare centers and schools where durability matters. Anyone with a SmartStock dispenser who's tired of refilling every 48 hours.

**Skip it if:** You don't already own the SmartStock dispenser, or you need individually wrapped spoons for takeout or guest service.
