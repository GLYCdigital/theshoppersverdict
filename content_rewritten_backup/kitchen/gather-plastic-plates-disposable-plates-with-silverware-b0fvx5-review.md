Second, the plates aren’t microwave-safe. I know that sounds like a minor point, but if you’re reheating leftovers at an event or catering setup, you’ll need to transfer food to a different plate first. That’s a genuine friction point that some buyers flagged.

Also worth noting: the silverware, while sturdy, is not the same weight as real flatware. It’s a premium disposable, not a metal replacement. If you’re planning a formal event, this isn’t the choice. But for casual-to-semi-formal gatherings, it’s spot on.
