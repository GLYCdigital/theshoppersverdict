**Where it falls short**  

Be honest about the maintenance. These are not dishwasher-safe. The heat and detergent will strip the oil finish and cause the wood to crack. You'll need to hand-wash with mild soap and dry immediately, then periodically rub them with food-grade mineral oil to keep the wood from drying out. If you're a "throw it all in the dishwasher" household, this set will frustrate you.

Second, acacia is porous. Acidic foods like tomato sauce or turmeric can leave faint yellowish stains. They're not harmful, but they're visible if you're picky about appearances. One customer mentioned: "The spatula has a slight orange tint from cooking with curry. No smell, just cosmetic." Oiling the wood monthly minimizes this, but it won't eliminate it.

**FAQ**  

**Q: Can I use these on cast iron?**  
Yes, but they're not ideal. Wood won't scratch cast iron, but the surface is too soft to scrape up stuck bits effectively. Use a metal spatula for that.

**Q: How often should I oil them?**  
Every 3-4 weeks, or whenever the wood looks dry or ashy. Rub on a thin layer of food-grade mineral oil, let it sit for an hour, then wipe off the excess.

**Q: Do they absorb odors?**  
Acacia is less porous than bamboo, but garlic and fish oils can linger if you don't wash thoroughly. A quick scrub with lemon juice and salt removes most smells.
