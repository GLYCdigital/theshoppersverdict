## What's Actually in the Box

You get four suspension rods, and here's the detail that matters: the set includes both the DC97-16350U and DC97-16350T part numbers. On Samsung washers like the WA50R5200AW, those two rods are not identical — they have different lengths and spring rates depending on their position in the chassis. Buying a mixed set like this removes the guesswork of trying to source each rod separately.

The rods themselves are steel with a coated finish, and the rubber dampening bushings are pre-installed. You also get the mounting clips and bolts, which is helpful because those small plastic retainers often crack during removal and aren't sold individually at most hardware stores.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I know if my suspension rods are actually bad?**
Unplug the washer, remove the top panel, and push down on the tub. If it feels loose, bounces excessively, or makes a clunking sound, the rods are worn. Also check for rust or visible fluid leaking from the rods themselves.

**Q: Can I replace just one rod instead of all four?**
Technically yes, but don't. The rods wear at similar rates, and replacing only one leaves the others partially worn, which can cause uneven dampening and future vibration issues. Replace all four at once.

**Q: How long do these upgraded rods last compared to OEM?**
Buyer reports are still early, but the thicker dampening material suggests they should match or exceed the OEM lifespan of roughly 3-5 years with normal use. Heavier loads (comforters, blankets) will accelerate wear on any rod.
