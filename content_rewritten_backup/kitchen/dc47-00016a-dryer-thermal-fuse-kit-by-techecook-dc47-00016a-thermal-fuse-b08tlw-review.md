**FAQ**

**Q: How do I know if my thermal fuse is actually blown?**
A: Unplug the dryer, locate the fuse (typically on the blower housing or exhaust duct), and test it with a multimeter set to continuity. If it reads infinite resistance, it's blown. This is a five-minute check that saves you from buying parts blindly.

**Q: Can I install this without any tools?**
A: No. You need a T20 Torx screwdriver to open the dryer panel, and a crimping tool or pliers for the wire connectors. A multimeter is strongly recommended for diagnosis.

**Q: Will this fix a dryer that's completely dead — no lights, no sounds?**
A: Probably not. A blown thermal fuse usually leaves the dryer with power (lights on, drum may turn) but no heat. If your dryer is fully dead, check the outlet, circuit breaker, and door switch first.
