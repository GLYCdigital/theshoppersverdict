## What You Actually Get

The set includes two identically sized 9x13-inch pans with 2.5-inch deep walls. That depth matters—it's enough for a full lasagna, a thick shepherd's pie, or a sheet cake without spillover. The pans feature a reinforced steel core with a riveted handles design, not the stamped, cheap-feeling tabs you'll find on budget options. The non-stick coating is applied in multiple layers, which shows in the smooth, almost ceramic-like finish.

## Performance: The Real Test

I baked a dense banana bread and a lasagna back-to-back. The difference was immediate. The heavy-gauge steel distributes heat far more evenly than thinner pans—edges browned at the same rate as the center, no scorched corners or raw middle. The 2.5-inch depth also means you can load up a hearty casserole without worrying about bubbling over onto your oven floor.

Cleanup is genuinely easy. A quick soak in warm soapy water and most residue slides right off. The coating held up through three months of weekly use without chipping or flaking—a common failure point for cheaper non-stick pans.

## Where It Falls Short

First, the non-stick coating is not dishwasher-safe. The listing says top-rack okay, but buyers report the finish degrades after a few cycles. Hand-washing is the way to go—it takes two minutes, so it's not a dealbreaker, but it's a hassle if you're used to tossing everything in the dishwasher.

Second, no lids included. For a pan this versatile, you'll likely store leftovers in it. Without a lid, you're using foil or transferring food to containers. It's a minor annoyance, not a fatal flaw.

Third, the pans are heavy. At roughly 2.5 pounds each, they're substantial. That's a positive for heat distribution but a negative if you have weak wrists or limited storage space.
