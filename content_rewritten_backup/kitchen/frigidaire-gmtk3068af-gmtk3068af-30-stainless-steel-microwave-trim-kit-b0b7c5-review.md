## What You Actually Get

The box contains the stainless steel trim frame, mounting brackets, screws, and a paper template for positioning. That’s it. No microwave — this is purely the cosmetic surround that makes a countertop model look built-in. The frame measures exactly 30 inches wide, matching standard upper cabinet openings, and the brushed stainless finish is designed to sit flush against the microwave face.

## Real-World Fit and Finish

The critical thing to understand: this kit is model-specific. It’s engineered for Frigidaire’s 1.6–2.0 cu. ft. over-the-range microwaves (check your model number against the compatibility list before ordering). ## Where It Falls Short

Two honest caveats. First, this is cosmetic only. If you’re hoping to vent cooking odors outside, you’ll need a separate ducting setup — the trim kit does nothing for airflow. Second, the brushed surface scratches if you look at it wrong. One reviewer noted: *“Micro-scratches showed up after wiping with a standard kitchen cloth. Use a soft microfiber only.”* That’s annoying for a part that costs what it costs.

Also, don’t expect this to fit random off-brand microwaves. Buyers who ignored the compatibility list ended up with gaps or a frame that wouldn’t seat properly. Measure your microwave’s width and depth before ordering.
