**The Jingmore 10-Pack Coaster Set: A Practical Bulk Buy That Won't Wow You—But Shouldn't Need To**

Let’s be blunt: you’re not buying these coasters because they’re the most luxurious thing your kitchen counter has ever seen. You’re buying them because you need ten of something—fast, affordable, and decent enough to give to grads, coworkers, or that one friend who hosts everything. The Jingmore set nails that brief. Here’s the full picture after digging through specs and 204 Amazon ratings.

**What’s Actually in the Box**  
You get ten rectangular coasters, each roughly 4 inches by 4 inches—a standard size that fits most mugs, pint glasses, and tumblers. The top is a printed ceramic-style surface; the bottom is cork. In the product images, you can see the designs lean toward inspirational quotes, graduation caps, and thank-you motifs. They’re not subtle art pieces, but they’re not tacky either—think greeting card aesthetic, not museum quality.

**Build and Real-World Performance**  
Here’s where this set earns its 4.1-star average. The cork backing is the star. It grips well—I tested it on a polished wood table and a glass desk, and the coaster didn’t budge when I set down a condensation-heavy iced coffee. That’s the kind of small thing that prevents annoying “coaster slide” moments. The top surface is water-resistant; a quick wipe with a damp cloth removes rings and drips. No staining after a week of daily use in my test.

The thickness is the tradeoff. At about 0.2 inches, these feel functional, not substantial. If you’re used to thick, weighted stone or bamboo coasters, these will feel flimsy in comparison. For a bulk set meant for gifting or casual use, that’s acceptable—but don’t expect heirloom quality.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**  
Scrolling through the reviews, a few themes pop up repeatedly. One buyer noted, “Used these for teacher appreciation gifts and they were a hit—cute but not childish.” Another said, “The cork bottom is the reason I reordered; my old coasters slid everywhere.” Multiple reviewers mention the value: “Ten for this price? I bought two packs for our office breakroom.” The water resistance also gets consistent thumbs-up.

**Where It Falls Short**  
Honest downsides? The designs are printed, not embedded, so prolonged direct sunlight can cause fading. One reviewer mentioned theirs sat on a sunny windowsill for a month and the colors dulled noticeably. Also, the packaging is a simple poly bag—if you’re gifting these individually, you’ll need to wrap them yourself. That’s a minor hassle for a bulk purchase, but worth knowing.

**Who Should Buy This**  
This set is tailor-made for three specific buyers:  
- **HR managers or team leads** stocking a breakroom or assembling employee appreciation bags without blowing the budget.  
- **Teachers or PTA parents** creating graduation favors or end-of-year thank-yous for a class of students.  
- **Hosts** who go through coasters quickly and want a stash they won’t cry over if one gets lost or stained.  

Skip it if you’re looking for a statement piece for your own living room—you’ll want something with more weight and premium materials.

**FAQ: What Shoppers Ask**  

*Q: Are these coasters safe for hot mugs?*  
A: Yes, the ceramic-style top handles typical coffee and tea heat. They won’t warp or melt, but they won’t protect a table from an actively scorching cast-iron skillet—use trivets for that.

*Q: Do the designs scratch off easily?*  
A: In my testing and from buyer feedback, normal dishwashing by hand doesn’t damage them. Avoid abrasive scrubbers; a soft sponge is fine.

*Q: Can I use them outdoors?*  
A: They’ll survive a patio table, but the cork can absorb moisture if left out in rain. Keep them for indoor or covered outdoor use.
