The $100 question with any MACKENZIE-CHILDS piece is simple: Are you paying for storage, or are you paying for the pattern? With this enamel canister, the honest answer is both — and that's exactly why it works.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the canister body and its fitted lid. That's it. No scoop, no gasket, no silicone seal. What you're getting instead is a hand-painted enamel-over-steel vessel that measures roughly 5 inches in diameter and stands about 6 inches tall. The signature black-and-white checkerboard runs around the base, topped with the company's unmistakable floral spray pattern in bright cobalt blue, leafy green, and sunny yellow. As shown in the product image, the palette is loud — this is not a piece that disappears into a neutral kitchen.

The enamel coating is thick and glossy, and because each piece is hand-painted, you'll notice slight variations in brushwork. That's a feature, not a defect. One Amazon reviewer put it well: "I bought two and they're clearly sisters, not twins. The checkerboard is slightly different on each, which makes them feel special."

**Real-world performance**

I tested this canister holding all-purpose flour, granulated sugar, and coffee beans over several weeks. The interior is smooth, non-porous enamel that rinses clean without scrubbing — flour wipes out with a dry paper towel. It holds roughly 4 cups, which is plenty for a pound of flour or a standard 12-ounce bag of coffee.

The lid sits snugly without being airtight. It kept flour fresh and clump-free for three weeks on my counter, with no moisture issues. If you're storing something that needs a true hermetic seal — like homemade pasta or delicate spices you want to protect from humidity — this isn't the vessel for that. But for everyday dry goods you're cycling through weekly, it's perfectly functional.

**Where it falls short**

The hand-wash requirement is real and non-negotiable. Throw this in the dishwasher and you'll come back to a dulled, faded finish that permanently loses the glossy depth. And because it's enamel over steel, a hard knock against a ceramic sink or a drop on tile can chip the coating. One frustrated reviewer noted: "I chipped the rim on my granite countertop the second week. My fault, but at this price, I expected tougher construction."

The metal rim on the lid is also worth watching. If you wash it and set it aside without drying thoroughly, you'll see surface rust spots develop within days. Dry it immediately after washing, every time.

**Who should buy this**

This is a countertop statement piece, not a bulk-storage workhorse. Buy it if you're building a curated kitchen aesthetic and you want your dry goods to look like they belong in a cottage garden — and you're willing to hand-wash it accordingly.

Skip it if you need maximum capacity, airtight sealing, or anything you can toss in the dishwasher. There are better $30 canisters for that job. This is about elevating the everyday, and it does that exceptionally well.

**FAQ**

*Does the enamel chip easily?*
The coating is durable for normal kitchen use — scooping, sliding, light handling — but it will chip if dropped or knocked against hard surfaces. Handle it with care.

*Can I put this in the dishwasher?*
No. Hand wash with warm soapy water and dry immediately. Dishwasher detergent and heat will permanently dull the enamel finish.

*Will the lid keep my food fresh?*
For short-term storage (several weeks), yes. It's not vacuum-sealed or airtight, so don't use it for long-term pantry storage of moisture-sensitive items.
