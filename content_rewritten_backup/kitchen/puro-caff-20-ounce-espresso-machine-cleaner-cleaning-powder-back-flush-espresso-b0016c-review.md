## What You're Actually Getting

The 20-ounce container of Puro Caff is a white, odorless powder that you dissolve in water for backflushing. As shown in the product image, it comes in a sturdy plastic jar with a small measuring scoop. The powder itself is a specialized detergent formulated to break down coffee oils, not a descaler — that distinction matters. You're buying this to clean the group head, portafilter, and internal lines of oil residue that degrades shot quality over time.

For reference, that 20 ounces translates to roughly 60-80 cleaning cycles at the recommended 1-2 teaspoons per backflush. Home users doing weekly maintenance will get six months to a year out of a single container. That's the real value proposition here compared to the single-dose packets that cost $1-2 each.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The process is straightforward: remove the basket, insert a blind filter, add the powder, and run your backflush cycle. On my Breville Barista Express, I ran two 10-second backflushes, let it sit for a few minutes, then flushed through with clean water. The first backflush produced water that looked like iced tea — that's the coffee oil releasing. After a full rinse cycle, the group head looked visually cleaner, and subsequent shots tasted noticeably brighter with less bitterness.

The powder dissolves quickly in hot water without clumping. It doesn't leave residue or a chemical aftertaste when rinsed properly. I've also used it on a Rancilio Silvia and a Gaggia Classic Pro, and it performed identically — no issues with group gaskets or brass components.

## Where It Falls Short

First, this is not a descaler. If your machine has hard water scale, you need a separate citric acid or commercial descaling product. Some buyers confused the two and were disappointed. Second, measuring is imprecise — using too much powder creates aggressive foam that can overflow the drip tray. The included scoop is small, and I recommend using a digital scale (about 3 grams per cycle) for consistency. Third, the powder can be messy if you're not careful with the scoop, especially when the jar gets low.

## FAQ

**How often should I backflush with Puro Caff?**
Weekly for home use if you pull 2-4 shots daily. If you're a lighter user, every two weeks is sufficient. You'll know it's time when the water runs slightly brown during a plain water backflush.

**Can I use this in a super-automatic machine?**
Generally yes, but check your manufacturer's manual. Some super-automatics have specific cleaning procedures. For semi-automatic machines, it's universally safe.

**Does it need to be rinsed completely?**
Yes — run at least two full reservoirs of clean water through the group head after cleaning. Residual detergent will ruin the crema and taste bitter.
