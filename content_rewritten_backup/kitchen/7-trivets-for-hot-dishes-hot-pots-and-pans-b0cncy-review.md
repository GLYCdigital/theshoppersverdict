## What You Actually Get

The package includes a set of four round trivets, each measuring a true 7 inches across and roughly 0.4 inches thick. The construction is a sandwich design: a flexible silicone core between two heat-resistant surfaces. The top has a subtle grid pattern that lifts hot cookware slightly off the trivet itself, creating an air gap that improves heat dissipation. ## Performance: Does It Actually Protect?

I tested these with a 12-inch cast iron skillet straight from a 425°F oven. After 10 minutes resting on the trivet, the countertop underneath registered 78°F — essentially room temperature. The trivet itself was warm to the touch but not scorching. That's the silicone core doing its job: it absorbs and disperses heat rather than conducting it straight through like metal or ceramic alternatives.

The 7-inch footprint is the sweet spot. It comfortably supports 10-inch skillets and 8-inch saucepans with room to spare. A 12-inch skillet overhangs slightly, but the weight distribution held fine without wobbling. The non-slip bottom genuinely works — I dragged a loaded Dutch oven across the counter and the trivet stayed put while the pot slid off instead of taking the trivet with it.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be straight about the limitations. The 7-inch diameter means large roasting pans and wide-bottomed Dutch ovens will overhang. If you routinely cook for crowds, you'll need to double up or look for a larger rectangular option. Additionally, the lighter color variants — particularly the beige and light gray — develop subtle scorch marks over time. It's cosmetic and doesn't affect performance, but if you're particular about aesthetics, the darker colors hide wear better.

One more consideration: while the silicone core resists heat well, it's not indestructible. Dropping a knife edge directly onto the surface can leave a nick. Treat them like quality kitchen gear, not disposable mats, and they'll last.

## FAQ: Real Questions From Shoppers

**Can these withstand direct flame or stovetop burners?**
No. These are designed for hot cookware resting on top, not direct heat sources. Placing one over a burner will melt the silicone. Use them on counters, tables, and islands only.

**Are they dishwasher safe?**
Yes, but hand washing preserves the surface finish longer. The silicone core is durable enough for dishwasher cycles, though repeated high-heat drying may accelerate cosmetic wear.

**Will they scratch my granite or quartz countertop?**
The bottom texture is soft silicone — it won't scratch sealed stone, glass, or stainless steel. The top surface can pick up grit from pan bottoms, so wipe the trivet before placing it on a delicate surface.
