The Progressive DKS-100 Prep Solutions earns its place in your kitchen not by reinventing the grater, but by executing the basics with uncommon competence. It’s a dual-sided prep tool that handles everything from hard cheese to citrus zest, and at 4.7 stars across 235 ratings, buyers are clearly getting something right. The question is whether it belongs in your drawer — and the honest answer is: probably, with one caveat.

## What You Actually Get

Unboxing reveals a single, integrated tool. No extra attachments, no confusing assembly. The unit measures roughly 12 inches long, with a stainless steel grating surface on one side and a finer rasp-style surface on the other. The handle is molded with a soft-touch grip, and the base has a rubberized strip that keeps it planted on your cutting board. As shown in the product image, the design is straightforward: two working surfaces, one handle, no moving parts.

## Real-World Performance

I spent a week using this as my primary prep tool. Coarse grating on cheddar and zucchini was effortless — the sharp teeth bite cleanly without shredding food into mush. The fine side handled Parmesan and ginger well, though it demanded noticeably more pressure on firmer produce. One buyer noted, "I've owned this for three months and use it almost daily for garlic and ginger. It hasn't dulled a bit," which matches my testing — after a dozen uses plus two dishwasher cycles, the edges remain sharp and the surface shows no staining.

The stability is the standout feature. The rubber base genuinely grips, even on a smooth marble counter, and the handle geometry lets you apply downward force without your hand slipping. That matters more than most shoppers realize — a grater that shifts mid-task is both frustrating and a safety hazard.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the reviews, three themes emerge. First, durability: multiple long-term owners report no rust after months of regular use, which is notable given how many graters fail this test. Second, the dual-sided versatility — one reviewer put it simply: "I replaced two separate graters with this one and reclaimed drawer space." Third, ease of cleaning. The single-piece design means no crevices where food hides, and it comes out of the dishwasher looking new.

## Where It Falls Short

No storage cover is included. That's the honest tradeoff. The exposed grating edges will scratch other utensils and, annoyingly, can catch on your fingers when you're digging for a spatula. It's not a dealbreaker — a silicone edge guard costs a few dollars — but at this price point, a cover would have made this a no-brainer. Additionally, the fine grating side is less efficient on dense vegetables. If you regularly grate large quantities of carrots or sweet potatoes, your forearm will notice.

## Who Should Buy This

This is ideal for home cooks who want one reliable tool that covers most grating needs — someone who zests citrus, shreds cheese, and minces garlic but doesn't need a drawer full of specialized graters. It's also a smart pick for small kitchens where storage space is at a premium. Skip it if you're a heavy-volume cook who processes pounds of produce at once; a box grater or food processor will serve you better.

## FAQ

**Q: Is it really dishwasher safe?**
Yes, but the manufacturer recommends placing it on the top rack. Buyers report no rust or warping after dozens of cycles, though hand-washing will extend the edge sharpness.

**Q: How sharp is the fine grating side?**
It's comparable to a Microplane for most applications. It's excellent for ginger, garlic, and hard cheese, but requires more effort on raw root vegetables.

**Q: Will it fit in a standard utensil drawer?**
At 12 inches, it fits in most drawers but takes up meaningful width. The lack of a cover means you'll want to position it carefully to avoid scratching other tools.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a durable, dual-purpose grater that handles daily prep without fuss and doesn't rust after a month. **Skip it if** you can't tolerate unprotected grating edges in your drawer or you need a heavy-duty tool for bulk vegetable prep.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a genuinely well-made tool with one design oversight that's easy to work around.
