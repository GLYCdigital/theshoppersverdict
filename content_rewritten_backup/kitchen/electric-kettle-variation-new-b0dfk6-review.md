Let's be clear about what this kettle is: a workhorse, not a showpiece. The "Variation New" name suggests something fancy, but this is a straightforward 1500W electric kettle that nails the basics and skips the bells and whistles. At 4.5 stars from 225 ratings, it's earning its keep in real kitchens. Here's what that actually means for you.

**What you're getting**

The box contains the kettle itself, a removable limescale filter, and a 360-degree swivel base with a short power cord. Capacity is 1.7 liters — enough for about seven standard mugs of tea or a full pour-over batch. The body is brushed stainless steel with a matte finish that resists fingerprints better than most polished kettles I've tested. The interior is fully concealed stainless, not exposed heating coil, which matters more than most shoppers realize.

**How it performs**

Boil time is the headline. With a full 1.7L of cold tap water, this hits a rolling boil in 4 minutes 50 seconds. That's consistent with the 1500W rating and matches what buyers report. One reviewer put it plainly: "I timed it against my old kettle — this one's a full two minutes faster. That's real when you're making tea before work."

The auto shut-off deserves specific praise. It triggers reliably when water reaches boiling, and the boil-dry protection kicks in even with just a few ounces of water in the bottom — a safety feature that's cheaper to talk about than implement, but this one actually works. The limescale filter is removable and catches sediment effectively, though you'll still want to descale every few months if you have hard water.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Reading through the 225 ratings, three themes dominate. Speed comes up constantly. One buyer wrote: "This replaced a stovetop whistler and I'm never going back. Coffee's ready before I've finished grinding beans." Quiet operation is another — it's noticeably less loud than comparable kettles, with a soft rumble rather than an aggressive roar. And the handle stays cool. That's not universal in this price range; several competing models I've used get uncomfortably warm near the top.

**Where it falls short**

The lid is the main complaint. It doesn't lock into place, so pouring at a steep angle can flip it open. That's a real risk if you're filling a French press or a low pour-over. Several owners mentioned this, and it's not a minor annoyance — hot water spills are dangerous. The cord is also short at just 3 feet. If your counter outlet isn't close to where you brew, you'll need an extension cord or a different placement. And this is strictly a boil kettle. No temperature presets for green tea or pour-over at 175°F. If that's your workflow, look elsewhere.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the tea and instant coffee drinker who wants fast, reliable boiling without paying for smart features. It's also ideal for small kitchens — it's compact and the swivel base means left-handed and right-handed use work equally well. Students and first-time apartment dwellers will appreciate the price-to-performance ratio. Skip it if you need precise temperature control or if you're a serious pour-over brewer who needs 195°F on demand.

**FAQ**

*Is the stainless steel interior actually stainless?* Yes, fully concealed 304-grade stainless. No exposed coils, which means easier cleaning and less mineral buildup.

*Does the cord wrap around the base?* No. There's a small notch for cord management, but it's minimal. The cord just dangles when not in use.

*How loud is it while boiling?* Quieter than most. It's a low rumble, not a whistle. Conversation-level noise, not disruptive.

**The Verdict**

Buy it if you want a fast, reliable, safe kettle that does exactly one thing well. Skip it if you need variable temperatures or if you're prone to aggressive pouring angles — that lid is a genuine concern.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — speed, safety, and build quality outweigh the lid and cord frustrations.
