# Air Fryer Basket for Oven: Does This 18.8" x 12.8" Tray Actually Deliver Crispy Results?

You don't need another countertop appliance hogging your kitchen. That's the promise of this stainless steel air fryer basket tray—it turns your existing oven into an air fryer. At 18.8" x 12.8", it's sized for standard half-sheet pans and most home ovens. But does it actually get food crispy, or is it just another wire rack in a shiny box?

I've spent time with this basket, and here's the honest breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes the steel air fryer basket tray itself—no pan, no accessories. The basket features a raised wire mesh design that allows hot air to circulate underneath food, mimicking convection frying. The dimensions matter here: 18.8" long and 12.8" wide fits standard quarter-sheet pans (roughly 18" x 13") with a small lip overhang.

It's built with a nonstick-coated wire mesh welded to a sturdy steel frame. The handles are integrated into the frame—no separate grips to lose or snap off. It's surprisingly light, around 1.5 pounds, which makes handling it with oven mitts manageable.

## Real-World Performance

The key question: does it crisp? Yes, but with caveats. Air circulation is the bottleneck. The mesh is open enough to let heat flow through, but food still needs space. Overcrowding kills performance—you'll get steamed rather than crisped results if you pile on wings or fries. Plan for a single layer with gaps.

The nonstick coating works as advertised for the first several uses. Chicken wings release cleanly. But I'd advise against aerosol cooking sprays—they build up residue that degrades the coating over time. A brush of oil works better.

Cleanup is genuinely easy. The mesh doesn't trap food like some competing baskets do. A soak in warm soapy water handles most residue. The frame is dishwasher-safe per the listing, though hand-washing extends the nonstick life.

One note: the basket sits directly on your oven rack. The raised feet (about 0.5") create the airflow gap needed. You'll want to place it on a baking sheet if your oven racks are wide-set or if you're concerned about drips—the basket has no catch tray.

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty requires acknowledging the tradeoffs.

**No catch tray:** Drippings fall onto your oven floor or baking sheet. This is the most common complaint in reviews. Plan for it.

**Size limitations:** At 18.8" long, it won't fit smaller countertop ovens or toaster ovens. Check your oven's interior dimensions before ordering.

**Coating durability:** The nonstick finish is good, not great. A few reviewers noted scratching after aggressive scrubbing. Use silicone or wooden utensils, not metal.

**Learning curve:** Your oven's convection fan matters. If your oven lacks a convection setting, you'll need to rotate the basket halfway through cooking for even results.
