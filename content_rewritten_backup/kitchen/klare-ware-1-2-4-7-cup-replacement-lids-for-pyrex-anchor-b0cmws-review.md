The kit includes four vented lids designed to snap over round glass containers with a 4.7", 5.9", 6.9", and 8.7" top opening. That’s the standard lineup for Pyrex Simply Store and Anchor Hocking storage bowls. They’re made from BPA-free polypropylene plastic—the same food-grade material most meal-prep lids use—and each lid has a steam vent that you can close for stacking or open for microwaving.

The "Ancho" in the title refers to Anchor Hocking compatibility, not a color. They come in clear, which is fine because you can see leftovers without lifting the lid.

## Fit and Real-World Performance

The most critical job of a replacement lid is sealing properly. Pyrex lids use a friction fit, not a screw thread, so if the plastic is too rigid or the lip is too shallow, you get gaps. Klare Ware’s lids have a slightly deeper lip than the OEM versions, which actually improves the seal on older Pyrex bowls that have been through the dishwasher a hundred times.

The vented design is a genuine upgrade over the solid lids Pyrex ships. When you microwave leftovers, the steam escapes through the small slit instead of building up and popping the lid off. That said, the vent isn't adjustable—it's just a slit in the plastic—so you can't fully seal it airtight for liquid storage. If you’re storing soup or marinades, keep the container upright.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest complaint is the lack of a truly airtight seal. Because the lid is vented, it won't lock out air completely. If you're using these for dry goods like flour or sugar, moisture can get in over time. This isn't a dealbreaker for most fridge use, but it's worth knowing.

A second issue is that the larger 7-cup lid (8.7" diameter) can be slightly loose on some Anchor Hocking bowls. Pyrex bowls are consistent, but Anchor Hocking has had minor variations in rim thickness over the years. If you own older Anchor Hocking pieces, test the fit before you toss the original lids.

Finally, these are hand-wash recommended. The manufacturer says they're top-rack dishwasher safe, but several reviewers reported warping after repeated dishwasher cycles. If you run your dishwasher on a high-heat sanitize cycle, hand wash these.

## FAQ

**Q: Will these fit vintage Pyrex bowls?**
A: Not reliably. Pyrex changed their rim design in the 2000s. These are designed for the current Simply Store line and similar modern bowls. Vintage pieces have a different lip profile.

**Q: Can I stack these in the fridge?**
A: Yes, the lids are flat on top, so you can stack filled containers. Just ensure the vent slit is closed (it slides shut) to prevent condensation from dripping onto the container below.

**Q: Are these microwave-safe?**
A: Yes, the polypropylene is microwave-safe, and the vent allows steam to escape. Open the vent slit before heating to prevent pressure buildup.
