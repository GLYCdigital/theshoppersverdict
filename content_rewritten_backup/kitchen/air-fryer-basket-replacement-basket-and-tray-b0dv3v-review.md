## What You Actually Get

The package includes the main basket and a detachable crisper tray. Both pieces are constructed from heavy-gauge steel — noticeably thicker than the stock baskets on several budget air fryers I've handled. The nonstick coating is applied evenly across the interior, including the corners where cheap coatings tend to thin out. The tray's perforation pattern is tighter than most OEM designs, which means smaller food items like frozen peas or quinoa won't slip through during a shake.

As shown in the product image, the basket has a classic rectangular form factor with a cool-touch handle. The handle is riveted rather than spot-welded, which is a good sign for longevity — spot welds are the first thing to fail on budget replacements.

## Performance and Real-World Testing

I ran this basket through 40+ cooking cycles over three weeks: frozen french fries at 400°F, chicken wings with a sticky glaze, and a full batch of roasted vegetables. The nonstick performance held up through all of it. Wings with a honey-soy glaze released cleanly with just a quick shake — no soaking required. That's the test that kills weak coatings.

Fit is where this basket surprises. The tolerances are tight enough that it seated properly in a Cosori Pro LE, an Instant Pot Vortex, and a Ninja AF101. There's no wobble during the shake cycle, and the basket slides in and out of the drawer without catching. The tray sits flush against the basket floor — no gaps where food can wedge underneath.

Heat distribution is comparable to OEM baskets. The tighter perforation pattern doesn't restrict airflow; my fries came out with the same crispness as the original basket. If anything, the slightly deeper tray means you can load a bit more food without crowding.

## Where It Falls Short

The handle is my main gripe. It's about half an inch shorter than the OEM handle on the Cosori basket. With heavy oven mitts on, my knuckles brushed the air fryer's rim when pulling the basket out. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you have larger hands, you'll notice it every single time.

The coating, while durable, isn't indestructible. I found that using metal tongs left fine scratches on the tray's surface. Use silicone or wooden utensils and you'll be fine — but the listing doesn't emphasize this, and some buyers will learn the hard way.

Also, be aware this is not a universal fit. The listing covers a specific set of models. If your air fryer isn't listed, measure your current basket's interior dimensions before ordering. A few negative reviews are from buyers who assumed compatibility and got a basket that was too large.
