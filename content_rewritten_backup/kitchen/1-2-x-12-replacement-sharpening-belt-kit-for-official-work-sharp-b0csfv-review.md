## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest part. This kit only fits Official Work brand sharpeners. If you own a different belt sharpener — even one that uses similar dimensions — these won't work. The mounting holes, wheel diameters, and tension specs are brand-specific.

Second, the individual belts aren't labeled with their grit. Each belt looks identical at a glance. If you mix them up in your drawer, you'll be guessing which is coarse and which is fine. Pro tip: mark them with a permanent marker the moment you open the kit.
