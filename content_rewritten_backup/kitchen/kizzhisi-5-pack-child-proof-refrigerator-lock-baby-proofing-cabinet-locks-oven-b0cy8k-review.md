If you've got a toddler who treats the refrigerator like a personal buffet, you already know the struggle. The KIZZHISI 5-pack childproof refrigerator lock tackles that problem with a simple, adhesive-based magnetic system that's earned a solid 4.5-star average across 225 Amazon ratings. But does it hold up when your little Houdini puts it to the test? Here's the real story.

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes five identical locks—each with an adhesive base plate, a locking mechanism, and a magnetic key. The locks themselves measure roughly 2.5 inches long and 1.5 inches wide, so they're unobtrusive on most appliance surfaces. You get enough hardware to cover a refrigerator, freezer drawer, oven, and two lower cabinets in one go. The installation requires zero drilling—just clean the surface, peel the 3M VHB backing, press firmly for 30 seconds, and wait 24 hours before testing.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The plastic housing feels sturdy, not flimsy. The magnetic key mechanism is the standout feature—it's genuinely difficult for small fingers to manipulate. Unlike strap-style locks that toddlers eventually figure out, this magnetic system has no accessible release that a curious kid can pry or pull. The adhesive is the real workhorse here. On clean, smooth surfaces like stainless steel, it bonds aggressively. I've seen reports of the adhesive holding strong for months on refrigerator doors, dishwasher panels, and wooden cabinets.

One thing worth noting: the 24-hour cure time is non-negotiable. If you install and immediately test, you'll likely pull the base plate right off. Several buyers made this mistake and initially thought the locks were defective. Give it a full day, and the bond becomes seriously strong.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The convenience of the magnetic key comes up repeatedly in customer feedback. As one verified purchaser put it, "The magnet key is so much easier than trying to squeeze those little buttons on other locks. I can open the fridge with one hand while holding my baby." Another reviewer highlighted the versatility: "Used two on the fridge, one on the freezer drawer, and two on lower cabinets. One pack covered everything."

The no-drill installation also earns consistent praise from renters—no holes to patch when you move out.

**Where It Falls Short**

The magnetic key is a double-edged sword. It's small—about the size of a large coin—and easy to lose. If you misplace it, you're fighting with the lock's release mechanism, which is intentionally hard to bypass. My advice: stick the key to your refrigerator door with a small magnet or keep it in a designated kitchen drawer. One reviewer noted, "Lost the key twice in the first week. Finally taped it to the side of the fridge."

The adhesive can also struggle on textured or uneven surfaces. If your fridge has a brushed metal finish with visible grain, the bond may weaken over time. Test on an inconspicuous spot first.

**Who Should Buy This**

This pack is ideal for parents of crawlers and early walkers—typically ages 8 months to 2 years—who need quick, affordable coverage across multiple appliances. It's also great for renters who can't drill into cabinets. If you have a determined preschooler (age 3+) who watches you unlock things, this may not hold them off indefinitely. Their fine motor skills and problem-solving are just too advanced by then.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will these locks damage my stainless steel fridge?**
A: No, if removed correctly. Use a hairdryer to warm the adhesive for 30-60 seconds, then gently pry with a plastic card. Goo Gone handles any residue.

**Q: Can I reposition a lock after installation?**
A: Not really. Once the adhesive cures, it's on. You get one shot per base plate. Order the 5-pack and test placement with painter's tape first.

**Q: Do these work on refrigerator drawers or just doors?**
A: Both, provided the surface is smooth and flat. They work well on freezer drawers and oven panels—just ensure the locking mechanism aligns properly with the drawer's motion.
