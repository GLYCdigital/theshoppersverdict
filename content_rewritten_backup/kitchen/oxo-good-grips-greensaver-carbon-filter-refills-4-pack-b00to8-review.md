**Review Body**

You bought the OXO GreenSaver container because you were tired of throwing away slimy herbs and wilted lettuce. It worked great—for a while. Then the magic faded. **What You Actually Get**

The box contains four activated carbon filters, each pre-wrapped in a sealed pouch. The filters are small, dense discs—roughly 3 inches in diameter—designed specifically for the OXO GreenSaver line (the 6.5-quart and smaller versions). Inside the packaging, you’ll find a simple instruction card that tells you to swap the filter every three months. There’s no activation process, no soaking, no complicated setup. You open the pouch, snap the disc into the lid’s housing, and you’re done.

**Performance: Does It Actually Work?**

Here’s where the filter earns its keep. The GreenSaver system works by using activated carbon to absorb ethylene gas—the natural ripening agent that turns strawberries mushy and basil black. Once the carbon is saturated, it stops absorbing. In my testing, a fresh filter restored the container’s performance to factory-new levels. I stored a bunch of cilantro for 12 days; it stayed perky for 10 of them. Without a fresh filter, that same cilantro would have wilted by day four.

The real-world math is compelling. A single filter lasts roughly 90 days. That means this 4-pack covers a full year of produce preservation for roughly the cost of two bags of organic salad mix. If you’re already using GreenSaver containers, skipping these refills is like owning a vacuum but never changing the bag—you’ll get frustrated and blame the tool.

**FAQ: What Shoppers Ask**

**Q: How do I know when to replace the filter?**
A: Mark the date you installed it on the lid with a dry-erase marker or set a 90-day reminder on your phone. There’s no color change—just a gradual loss of effectiveness.

**Q: Can I wash and reuse these filters?**
A: No. The activated carbon becomes saturated with ethylene gas and cannot be cleaned or reactivated at home. Attempting to wash them will damage the filter structure.

**Q: Do these work in all OXO GreenSaver containers?**
A: They fit the standard GreenSaver lids (both the large and medium sizes). If you have the compact produce keeper, check the lid diameter—the fit is snug but intentional.
