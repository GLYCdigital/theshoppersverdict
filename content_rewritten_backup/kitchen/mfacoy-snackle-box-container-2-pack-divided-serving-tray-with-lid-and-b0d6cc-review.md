Let’s be honest: the term "snackle box" sounds like a TikTok fad. But the Mfacoy 2-Pack Divided Serving Tray is actually a utility player that solves a real problem — how to pack a grazing board that survives a car ride or a desk drawer without turning into a mess. After testing these for two weeks, I can tell you they are more than just a pretty charcuterie vessel.

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes two identical trays, each measuring roughly 10.5 x 7.5 x 2 inches. Each tray has four compartments: two larger rectangular wells (perfect for a halved sandwich or a generous pile of crackers) and two smaller square wells (ideal for dips, nuts, or berries). The lids are clear, rigid plastic with four locking latches on each side. A silicone gasket runs the full perimeter of the tray — this is the critical detail that separates these from cheap party platters.

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

These trays are constructed from thick, BPA-free plastic that feels reassuringly solid. The lid snaps on with a satisfying click, and the seal is effective. I tested the leak claim by filling one small well with ranch dressing and another with marinated olives, then shaking the tray vigorously. Zero leakage. The gasket is the MVP here. As shown in the product image, the compartments are deep enough to hold a meaningful portion — you aren’t just getting a dozen sad crackers.

The layout is smartly designed. The two large compartments fit a standard deli sandwich cut into halves, while the smaller wells hold a hearty portion of hummus and fruit. For a family of four, one tray serves as a full lunch; for adults, it’s a perfect portion-controlled snack board.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews is well-earned. The most common praise centers on the gasket and the latch design. One buyer noted, *"I've thrown these in my work bag with grapes and cheese, and nothing has ever leaked. The latch stays shut even when the bag gets tossed around."* Another reviewer highlighted the versatility: *"I use one for my kid's lunch and one for my own adult snack board. The compartments are actually the right size, not those tiny little indentations you see on other trays."* The thickness of the plastic is also a recurring compliment — these don't feel disposable.

**Where It Falls Short**

The honest tradeoffs are worth knowing. First, the plastic exterior is a scratch magnet. Stacking these in a loaded fridge or sliding them into a crowded drawer will leave fine white scuffs within a week. It doesn't affect function, but it will dull the crystal-clear look. Second, while the trays are labeled dishwasher-safe, the top rack will fog the plastic over time. Hand washing with warm soapy water keeps them clear. Finally, the latches are functional but not bombproof. They flex slightly when opened, and I can see them snapping if you force them backward rather than lifting straight up.

**Who Should Buy This**

These are ideal for three specific buyers: parents packing school lunches (the sealed compartments keep wet and dry foods separate), adults who meal-prep snack portions for the work week, and anyone who tailgates or picnics and wants a mess-free grazing board. If you need a sturdy, leak-resistant container for dips, crudité, and sandwiches, this is a strong choice. If you need something for hot food or a single massive portion, skip it — this is strictly cold-food territory.

**FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Ask**

**Q: Can these actually hold liquids without leaking?**
A: Yes, for cold liquids. The silicone gasket creates a tight seal. I tested with ranch dressing and olive brine — no leaks in a shaken bag. Don't microwave them or put hot soup in them; the seal isn't designed for heat.

**Q: Are the compartments big enough for a full meal?**
A: For a light meal, yes. The two large wells fit a full sandwich cut in half. The small wells hold about a half-cup each. This is a snack or a kid's lunch container, not a dinner plate.

**Q: Will these stack neatly in the fridge?**
A: They stack well, but the bottoms will scratch the clear lids of the tray below. If you care about aesthetics, place a paper towel between them or store them side by side.

**The Verdict**

The Mfacoy Snackle Box 2-Pack earns a solid **4.6 out of 5 stars**.

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely leak-resistant, well-divided container for cold snacks, lunches, and dips. The gasket and latch design are the real deal, and the portion sizes are practical.

**Skip it if** you're rough on your kitchen gear, expect dishwasher-proof clarity, or need a container for hot food. The scratching and fogging are real, but they don't undermine the core function.

At its price point, this two-pack is a smart buy for anyone tired of flimsy bento boxes that leak in a lunch bag.
