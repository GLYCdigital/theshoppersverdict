Some kitchen tools are so forgettable you don't realize they're bad until you've lived with them for a month. The Flezoo 4-pack coffee scoop is the opposite — it's the kind of boring, functional purchase you make once and never think about again, which is exactly what you want from a $10 utensil.

## What you actually get

Four identical scoops, each stamped from a single piece of 304 stainless steel. The bowl holds a true 15 milliliters — that's one level tablespoon — and the handle stretches about 6.5 inches from tip to bowl. No plastic, no rivets, no seams where coffee oils can hide. They arrive nested together in a poly bag with a small instructional card you'll throw away.

The polish job is better than it has any right to be at this price. The mirror finish on the bowl reflects light like a decent cocktail jigger, and the handle has a subtle brushed texture that keeps it from sliding out of your grip when your hands are damp from washing dishes.

## How it performs in daily use

I tested these against a digital scale using freshly ground beans. A level scoop of medium-roast whole beans ground fine landed at 8.5 grams — right in the standard 1:15 to 1:16 coffee-to-water ratio ballpark for a 12-ounce mug. Heaped, it hits closer to 12 grams, which gives you useful flexibility if you prefer stronger brew.

The long handle is the standout feature. Standard coffee scoops from Folgers tubs have handles so short your knuckles scrape the rim. The Flezoo's extra reach means you can dig to the bottom of a deep canister without wearing the coffee grounds. It also doubles as a sugar scoop, protein powder measure, or baking spoon for dry ingredients.

Cleanup is a non-event. Dishwasher-safe, though I hand-washed to see if the finish would hold up — it does. No water spots, no dulling, no rust after two weeks of daily use and regular dishwasher cycles.

## What buyers consistently praise

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews holds up when you read the actual feedback. One verified buyer wrote: *"Finally a scoop that doesn't bend when I'm scooping frozen grounds. ## Where it falls short

The biggest miss: no measurement markings anywhere. The listing says "1 tablespoon 15ML" but the physical scoop itself is unmarked. If you grab the wrong one from a drawer, you have no way to verify size. It's a minor gripe, but for a product whose entire job is precision, an etched "15ML" would cost pennies to add.

Second, these are lightweight. At roughly 25 grams each, they feel insubstantial in hand. That's not a functional problem — the steel is thick enough to resist bending — but if you're used to weighted, premium-feel scoops, these will feel cheaper than they are.

## FAQ

**Is the 15ML measurement accurate?**
Yes, verified against a digital scale. A level scoop of ground coffee weighs 8.5 grams, which matches the standard 15ML volume for medium-fine grounds.

**Can these go in the dishwasher?**
Yes. 304 stainless handles heat and detergents fine. I ran mine through 10+ cycles with no discoloration or pitting.

**Are these safe for hot liquids?**
The steel conducts heat, so don't leave one resting in a hot pan. For scooping dry ingredients or stirring cold drinks, they're fine.
