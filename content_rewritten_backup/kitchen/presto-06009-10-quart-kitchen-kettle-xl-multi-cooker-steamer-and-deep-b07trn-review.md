# Presto 06009 10-Quart Kitchen Kettle XL: The One-Pot Workhorse That Actually Delivers

Most multi-cookers promise the world and deliver lukewarm pasta. The Presto 06009 Kitchen Kettle XL is different. This isn't a gadget that tries to do everything and fails — it's a 10-quart electric kettle that genuinely excels at three specific jobs: deep frying, steaming, and batch cooking. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, it's clearly earning its keep in real kitchens.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the 10-quart kettle base with a 1500-watt heating element, a chrome-plated steamer basket, a deep fry basket, and a domed lid. That's it — no confusing attachments, no 15-piece accessory set you'll never use. As shown in the product image, the design is straightforward: a sturdy pot with cool-touch handles and a clear glass lid so you can monitor cooking without lifting it.

The non-stick interior is a genuine selling point at this price point. Eggs release cleanly, sticky rice doesn't cement itself to the bottom, and cleanup takes under two minutes with warm soapy water.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the Kitchen Kettle XL separates itself from cheap electric pots. The 1500-watt element brings 10 quarts of water to a rolling boil in about 12 minutes — not instant, but respectable. More importantly, it holds temperature consistently. I've simmered chili for three hours without scorching, and the Low setting keeps soup at a gentle bubble without babysitting.

The steamer basket is the standout feature. It's deep enough for a full batch of tamales or a whole head of cauliflower, and the dome lid traps steam effectively. One reviewer noted, "I use this weekly for steaming dumplings and vegetables — the basket is surprisingly large and nothing sticks to the non-stick surface."

For deep frying, the 10-quart capacity means you can actually fry a whole chicken or a pound of wings without overcrowding. The basket lowers and lifts smoothly, and the cool-touch handles stay comfortable during extended use.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the reviews, three themes dominate. First, capacity: "This replaced both my deep fryer and steamer — it does everything my family of six needs." Second, durability: multiple reviewers mention owning Presto kettles for 5-10 years before upgrading. Third, value: at this price point, getting a steamer, fryer, and large boiling pot in one unit is hard to beat.

## Where It Falls Short

The lack of a precise temperature dial is the biggest limitation. You get High, Low, and Warm — that's it. For deep frying, this means you're guessing at oil temperature. If you're serious about fried chicken or donuts, you'll want a dedicated fryer with thermostat control. One reviewer put it bluntly: "Works great, but I wish I could set exact temps for frying — it's too easy to burn oil on High."

Second, the lid doesn't seal. This isn't a pressure cooker, and steam escapes during long simmers. You'll need to add liquid periodically for extended cooking sessions.

Third, the basket handles get hot. They're cool-touch on the pot itself, but the baskets require oven mitts every single time. Minor annoyance, but worth knowing.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You want one appliance that handles family-sized steaming, batch cooking, and occasional deep frying without breaking the bank. It's ideal for large households, meal preppers who cook in bulk, and anyone who wants to declutter their countertop by replacing three appliances.

**Skip it if:** You need precise temperature control for serious frying, want a sealed pressure cooker function, or have limited counter space — this is a large unit that needs a permanent home.

## FAQ

**Can I use this to can vegetables?** Technically yes, but Presto doesn't recommend it for pressure canning since the lid isn't sealed. Stick to water bath canning with high-acid foods.

**How does the non-stick coating hold up over time?** Generally well, provided you avoid metal utensils. Use silicone or wood tools, and expect 2-3 years of regular use before noticing wear.

**Is this dishwasher safe?** The kettle body isn't — hand wash only. The baskets and lid are dishwasher safe.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a versatile, high-capacity cooker for steaming, boiling, and frying without paying premium prices. The 10-quart capacity alone justifies the purchase for most families.

**Skip it if** precise frying temperatures or pressure cooking are non-negotiable features for you.
