# Ninja Foodi Pro 5-in-1 Indoor Integrated Smart Probe Review: Is the Smart Probe Worth It?

The Ninja Foodi Pro 5-in-1 isn't another me-too air fryer. It's the one that finally makes the "smart" label meaningful — with a built-in probe that actually changes how you cook, not just a companion app that blinks at you. At 4.7 stars across 235 Amazon ratings, it's earned its reputation. But the probe's fixed design and a few mode quirks mean it's not for everyone. Here's the breakdown after extensive testing.

## What's in the Box

You get the main unit with a 6.5-quart ceramic-coated basket and crisper plate, the integrated smart probe (stored in a slot on the unit), and a quick-start guide. That's it. No extra racks, no rotisserie spit. The basket is genuinely large — it fits a 5-pound chicken with room to spare, and you can lay a full pound of bacon flat without overlapping, which is rare at this size. The probe stores neatly on the unit's side, so you won't lose it in a drawer.

## Performance: Where the Smart Probe Shines

The headline feature is the integrated smart probe. You insert it into whatever you're cooking, set a target internal temperature, and the Foodi Pro monitors it continuously. The clever part: it automatically switches from a high-heat searing mode to a lower finishing mode when the meat hits a pre-set threshold. For a thick ribeye, it sears at 450°F, then drops to 325°F as the internal temp approaches your target. The result is a proper crust with a medium-rare center — no guesswork, no overcooked edges.

Air frying is strong too. The 6.5-quart basket circulates heat aggressively; frozen fries come out crispy in 18 minutes without shaking, and chicken wings get a genuinely shatter-crisp skin. Roasting and baking performed as expected — a whole chicken roasted at 375°F hit 165°F in the breast in about 55 minutes, with the probe alerting me precisely when it was done.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Amazon reviewers echo the same themes. One verified buyer wrote, *"The probe is a game-changer. I've cooked the perfect turkey breast three times in a row — no more dry meat."* Another noted, *"This replaced my air fryer, toaster oven, and dehydrator. Counter space is finally clear."* The ceramic-coated basket also gets consistent love for being genuinely non-stick and dishwasher-safe — eggs slide right off, and cleanup takes under a minute.

## Where It Falls Short

The probe is the main draw, but it's fixed to the unit. You can't detach it to check the temp of something on the stove or in a slow cooker — it only works inside the Foodi Pro. If you want a standalone instant-read thermometer, you'll need a separate one.

Dehydrate mode runs hot. The dial goes down to 105°F, but in practice it hovers around 120-125°F. Thin fruit leather and jerky can over-crisp if you set it and walk away. It's not a dealbreaker, but it requires checking more often than you'd expect.

There's also no rotisserie. Several competitors in this price range — like the Instant Pot Vortex Plus — offer one. If rotating chicken is your thing, this isn't it.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You cook meat regularly and want consistent, foolproof results. The smart probe genuinely removes the guesswork from roasts, steaks, and poultry. Also buy it if you're short on counter space and want one appliance that air fries, roasts, bakes, broils, and dehydrates.

**Skip it if:** You need a standalone thermometer for stovetop cooking, want rotisserie capability, or you're a dehydrating enthusiast who needs precise low-temp control.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the smart probe work with the dehydrate function?**
A: No. The probe is designed for meat cooking modes (air fry, roast, broil, bake). Dehydrate runs on a manual timer — you'll need to check doneness visually.

**Q: How big is the basket, really?**
A: 6.5 quarts. It holds a 5-pound chicken, about 3 pounds of fries, or a full sheet pan of vegetables. It's one of the larger baskets in this category.

**Q: Is the probe dishwasher safe?**
A: The probe itself is hand-wash only. The basket and crisper plate are both dishwasher safe, but keep the probe's cable and connector away from moisture.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The Ninja Foodi Pro 5-in-1 earns its price tag through the smart probe's genuine utility — it delivers consistently cooked meat with zero guesswork, which is worth a lot for home cooks. The 5-in-1 versatility replaces multiple appliances, and the large basket handles family-sized portions. The fixed probe and no rotisserie are real limitations, but they don't undermine what this machine does exceptionally well.

**Buy it if** you want foolproof, probe-guided cooking and multi-function versatility. **Skip it if** you need standalone thermometer flexibility or rotisserie capability. For most households, this is the air fryer that finally does it all — and does it right.
