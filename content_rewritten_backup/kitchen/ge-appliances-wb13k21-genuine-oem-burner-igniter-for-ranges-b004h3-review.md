## FAQ

**Q: How do I know if this fits my GE range?**
A: Check your range's model number (found on a sticker inside the oven door or drawer) and verify it against GE's compatibility list. This part fits most GE and Hotpoint gas ranges from the early 2000s onward.

**Q: Will this fix a burner that clicks but doesn't light?**
A: Yes — that's the classic symptom of a faulty igniter. The clicking sound means the module is sending power, but the electrode isn't producing a spark at the right position. Replacing the igniter resolves this in the vast majority of cases.
