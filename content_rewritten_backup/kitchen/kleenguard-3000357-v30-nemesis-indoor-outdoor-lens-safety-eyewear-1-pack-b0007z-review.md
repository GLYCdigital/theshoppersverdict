Let's be honest: most safety glasses feel like a punishment. They fog up the second you start sweating, pinch your temples, and slide down your nose every time you look down. The KleenGuard V30 Nemesis isn't perfect, but it's one of the few pairs that actually solves the fogging problem without feeling like you're wearing a plastic box on your face.

**What You Actually Get**

This is a single pair of ANSI Z87.1+ rated safety glasses. The headline feature is the "V30" anti-fog coating, which KleenGuard applies to both the interior and exterior of the lens. You get clear, distortion-free polycarbonate optics with 99.9% UVA/UVB protection. The frame is a lightweight, wraparound design with a rubberized brow guard and adjustable nose piece. It's not a "kit" — no case, no microfiber pouch, no extra lenses. Just the glasses.

**Build and Real-World Performance**

The polycarbonate lens is optically clear with zero noticeable distortion at the edges, which is rare at this price point. The wraparound design gives you solid peripheral coverage, and the rubberized brow guard does a decent job of catching sweat before it runs into your eyes. The frame flexes slightly, which helps it conform to different head shapes without feeling flimsy.

The anti-fog coating is the real story here. In humid conditions — think hot kitchens, steamy dish pits, or working outdoors in summer — these hold up noticeably better than standard coated lenses. The coating isn't magic; if you're doing high-intensity work in extreme humidity, you'll still get some fogging. But for normal kitchen prep, garage work, or yard work, the V30 coating buys you significantly more clear time than typical safety glasses.

**What Buyers Keep Saying**

The 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews is backed by consistent feedback. **Where It Falls Short**

No product is flawless, and the Nemesis has three notable tradeoffs:

- **The fit runs narrow.** If you have a wider face, the arms can press against your temples after extended wear. This is the most common complaint in negative reviews.
- **The anti-fog coating is fragile.** It scratches if you wipe the lenses with anything abrasive or dry cloth. You must rinse or use the included microfiber-style care, or you'll degrade the coating quickly.
- **No hard case included.** For a product with a delicate lens coating, the lack of a protective case is a real oversight. Toss these in a tool bag unprotected and you're risking scratches.

**Who Should Buy These**

- **Kitchen professionals** who need clear vision over hot steam and open flames — this is the primary use case.
- **Outdoor workers** in humid climates who've given up on anti-fog claims from cheaper brands.
- **DIY home cooks** who process large batches of chili peppers, onions, or work with hot oil and need eye protection that doesn't fog mid-task.

Skip these if you have a wide head shape or if you're the type to toss your safety glasses loose into a drawer. You'll be better served by a model with a wider frame and a storage case.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do these fog up when cooking over a boiling pot?**
A: The V30 coating resists fogging far better than standard lenses, but extreme steam exposure for several minutes will still cause some misting. For intermittent steam exposure, they stay clear.

**Q: Can I wear these over prescription glasses?**
A: No. The wraparound design sits too close to the face. You'd need a prescription insert or look at over-glasses models instead.

**Q: Are the lenses scratch-resistant?**
A: The polycarbonate lens itself is impact-resistant, but the anti-fog coating is soft and will scratch if handled roughly. Rinse with water and pat dry, never wipe dry with a shirt.
