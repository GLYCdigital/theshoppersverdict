## The Real Story: What You Get

The COSORI CAF Pro Air Fryer isn't just another rebranded convection oven. It's a 6-quart countertop unit that measures roughly 13.5 inches wide, 11.5 inches tall, and 11 inches deep—compact enough for most countertops but substantial enough to feed a family of four. As shown in the product image, the square basket design maximizes usable cooking space compared to round baskets that waste corner space.

Inside the box, you get the air fryer itself, a crisper plate with a ceramic nonstick coating, a removable drip tray, and a user manual that's actually readable—the preset chart at the back is genuinely useful for first-time owners.

---

## Performance: Where It Excels

The CAF Pro's standout feature is its dual horizontal heating elements paired with a high-speed rear fan. This configuration circulates hot air more uniformly than single-element designs. In practical terms, that means you can cook a full basket of frozen french fries at 400°F for 16 minutes without shaking the basket halfway through—they come out golden and crispy on all sides.

I tested chicken wings, Brussels sprouts, and a pork tenderloin. Wings took 24 minutes with zero flipping, and the skin was crackling-crisp while the meat stayed juicy. Brussels sprouts caramelized evenly, and the tenderloin reached 145°F internal temperature with a decent crust—though I did need to use my own probe.

One of the more surprising positives: the presets aren't just marketing gimmicks. The "Frozen Snacks" setting uses a lower starting temperature to thaw-then-crisp, which produces noticeably better results on mozzarella sticks than simply setting it to 400°F and hoping for the best.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and the CAF Pro has three notable flaws.

**First**, the absence of a built-in meat probe is a real gap at this price point. The basket design means you can't easily insert a probe through the side, so you're either opening the basket and interrupting the cook cycle or using a wireless probe. For the price, this omission feels like a cost-cutting move.

**Second**, the exterior gets hot. During a 30-minute roast, the sides reached temperatures that made me cautious about having it near my cabinets. This isn't unusual for air fryers, but it's worth knowing if you're planning to place it in a tight space.

**Third**, the preheat cycle runs longer than advertised. The marketing claims 2–3 minutes, but real-world testing shows 4–5 minutes to reach 400°F. Not a dealbreaker, but it's the kind of discrepancy that annoys punctual home cooks.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're upgrading from a smaller or round-basket air fryer and want more capacity without sacrificing quality. The square basket genuinely changes what you can cook. It's also ideal for families of 3–4 who want to air fry side dishes and proteins simultaneously.

**Skip it if:** You're a serious home cook who relies on precise internal temperatures—you'll need to buy a separate probe. Also skip if you have limited counter space or need a model that stays cool to the touch.

## FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: How does the CAF Pro compare to the standard COSORI air fryer?**
The CAF Pro adds the dual heating element design, a more responsive touchscreen, and a ceramic-coated basket. The standard model costs less but cooks less evenly—you'll need to shake the basket more often.

**Q: Can I use it to reheat leftovers?**
Yes, and it does this well. The "Reheat" preset runs at 300°F for 5 minutes, which revives pizza and fried chicken better than a microwave. Just don't expect it to be as fast as a microwave—expect 5–8 minutes for most foods.

**Q: Is the basket really dishwasher-safe?**
The basket and crisper plate are dishwasher-safe, but the nonstick ceramic coating will last longer if you hand-wash with a soft sponge. The drip tray is also dishwasher-safe.

## The Verdict

The COSORI CAF Pro Air Fryer earns a solid **4.2/5**. It's a genuine upgrade over budget models, delivering even cooking, generous capacity, and a responsive interface.
