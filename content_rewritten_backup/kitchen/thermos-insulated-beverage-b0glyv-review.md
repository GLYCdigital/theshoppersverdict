Here's where this bottle earns its keep. Multiple buyers report coffee staying "painfully hot" for a full workday — one reviewer noted their morning brew was still steaming at 6 PM. ## Where It Falls Short

The narrow 2.5-inch mouth is the most common complaint. Adding ice cubes requires awkward tilting and poking, and cleaning inside requires either a bottle brush or a lot of shaking with soapy water. If you primarily drink iced beverages, this is a genuine friction point.

There's also no handle or clip attachment point. For commuters who hang bottles from bags or hikers who need carabiner access, this is a miss. You're holding it or stashing it in a pocket — no other options.
