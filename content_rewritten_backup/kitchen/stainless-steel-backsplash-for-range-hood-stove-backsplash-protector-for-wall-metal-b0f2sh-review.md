## Where It Falls Short

Honesty requires naming the tradeoffs. The first is that this is a permanent installation. Once you drill those holes, you're committed. If you rent or change your kitchen layout, you'll have wall damage to repair. That's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

The second issue is the size coverage. The panel protects the wall directly behind the cooktop, but it won't cover the full width of a large range if you have a 30-inch or wider stove with side splatter zones. Some buyers mention they wished the panel was wider. Measure your cooktop width before ordering—don't assume one size fits all.

Third, the metal will show water spots and fingerprints if you don't dry it after cleaning. It's stainless steel, which means it's not completely maintenance-free. A quick wipe with a microfiber cloth after cleaning keeps it looking sharp.
