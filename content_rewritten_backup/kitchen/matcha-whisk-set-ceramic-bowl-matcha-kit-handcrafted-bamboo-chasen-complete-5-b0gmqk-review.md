## What's actually in the box

The set arrives as a complete five-piece kit. You get the 100% natural bamboo chasen whisk, a hand-glazed ceramic matcha bowl, a bamboo scoop (chashaku), a stainless steel sifter, and a whisk holder/stand. As shown in the product image, everything nests together neatly — nothing rattles around loose in shipping, and the presentation quality is good enough that you could gift this without a box.

The bowl itself has a wide 5-inch opening and a subtle ridged interior that helps break up clumps as you whisk. It's substantial without being bulky, and the glazed finish is smooth and consistent — no rough spots where glaze pools could harbor bacteria.

## The real test: does it make good matcha?

I put this through a full week of daily use — two servings a day, both ceremonial and culinary grade matcha. The chasen's 80+ tines do the heavy lifting, and the difference between this and a cheap electric frother is night and day. You get a dense, micro-foam layer on top with no gritty sediment, which is exactly what you want for a proper usucha (thin tea) preparation.

The sifter deserves special mention. It's fine-mesh enough to catch the clumps that plague cheaper matcha powders, and it fits over the bowl rim snugly. One less mess to deal with at 7 AM.

## Where it falls short

The bamboo chasen is a consumable item, and this kit doesn't change that. With daily use, the tines will gradually splay outward and lose their snap — expect to replace the whisk every 3–6 months. Replacement chasens run $8–12, which isn't a dealbreaker, but it's a recurring cost to budget for.

The ceramic bowl is also hand-wash only. It's not dishwasher-safe, and at 1.2 pounds, it's heavy enough that you won't want to pack it for travel. If you're looking for a throw-in-a-bag matcha solution, this isn't it.

## FAQ

**How many servings does the bowl hold?**
The bowl comfortably holds one standard 2–3 oz matcha serving for whisking. You can double the batch, but whisking efficiency drops with more liquid in the bowl. For two servings, whisk separately.

**Can I use this for other teas or coffee?**
Technically yes, but the chasen is specifically shaped for matcha. It'll work for other powdered teas like hojicha, but don't use it for loose-leaf tea — the tines will damage the leaves and the whisk itself.

**How do I clean and maintain the bamboo whisk?**
Rinse immediately after use with warm water only — no soap, which absorbs into the bamboo and ruins future batches. Gently shape the tines back together and let it air dry on the included stand. Store it away from humidity.
