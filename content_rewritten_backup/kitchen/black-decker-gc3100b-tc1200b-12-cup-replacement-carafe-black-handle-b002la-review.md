Let's be honest: nobody wakes up excited to buy a replacement carafe. You're here because you dropped yours, or the handle finally gave out after three years of daily use. The question is whether the Black+Decker GC3100B TC1200B is a worthy stand-in for the original that shipped with your coffeemaker.

**What You Actually Get**

This is a genuine Black+Decker OEM part, not a third-party knockoff. You receive the glass carafe with the black plastic handle and lid assembly attached. It holds a true 12 cups when filled to the top marking, and the measurement lines on the side are printed clearly—not embossed in a way that collects coffee residue. The handle is ergonomically shaped with a thumb rest, which sounds like a small thing but matters when you're carrying a full pot to the table.

**Fit and Compatibility**

The GC3100B is the most widely compatible replacement carafe Black+Decker makes. It fits nearly every 12-cup model they've produced in the last decade, including the DCM25 series, the CM5000 series, and most of the SpaceMaker line. The lid seats properly on the brew basket, and the carafe sits flush on the warming plate. One buyer noted, "It fit my 6-year-old coffeemaker like it was made for it—because it was." That's the real advantage of buying OEM: no guessing about whether the "universal" replacement from a third party will actually seat correctly.

**Real-World Performance**

The glass is borosilicate-style tempered, which handles thermal shock reasonably well. Pouring hot coffee into a cold carafe won't crack it, and it survives the dishwasher on the top rack—though hand-washing is gentler on the measurement markings. The pour is clean and controlled for normal use, with a decent spout design that gives you a steady stream.

**Where It Falls Short**

The glass is thinner than you might expect. One reviewer wrote, "It broke the second time I used it when I tapped it against the sink while rinsing." That's a real risk with this carafe—it's not built like a commercial-grade thermal pot. You need to handle it with reasonable care. The second issue is the lack of a drip guard. If you tip it aggressively toward the last few drops, coffee will run down the outside of the carafe and pool on the counter. It's a minor annoyance, but worth knowing.

**Who Should Buy This**

If your Black+Decker coffeemaker is otherwise in good shape and you just need a replacement glass, this is the right purchase. It's also ideal if you've bought a used coffeemaker that arrived without its carafe. If you're the type who breaks carafes every six months, consider buying two—or look at a thermal carafe model instead.

**Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask**

**Does this fit my model?**
It fits almost every Black+Decker 12-cup model made in the last 10-15 years. Check your model number, but the GC3100B is the standard replacement for the vast majority of their drip machines.

**Can it go in the dishwasher?**
Yes, top rack only. The heat from the bottom heating element can warp the plastic handle over time.

**Is the lid included?**
Yes, the lid assembly comes attached. It clicks into place securely and stays put when pouring.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You own a Black+Decker 12-cup coffeemaker and need a genuine replacement that fits without guesswork. The OEM quality, accurate markings, and comfortable handle make it a solid upgrade over generic alternatives.

**Skip it if:** You're rough on glass carafes, or you're looking for a thermal/stainless option that won't shatter. This is glass, and it will break if you treat it carelessly.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The thin glass at the base is the only real knock, but for a direct OEM fit at this price, it does exactly what it should. Most of the 225 reviewers agree, with 4.5 stars overall. If you need a replacement today, this is the one to get.
