Let’s be honest: most paper napkins are an afterthought. You grab whatever’s cheapest, deal with the flimsy tearing, and move on. The Party Solids 100-count pack aims to change that calculus — and with a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, it’s clearly doing something right. But is it worth your money, or just another disposable that disappoints mid-barbecue?

**What you actually get**

This is a straightforward package: 100 two-ply luncheon napkins in solid colors. Sized around 13 inches square when fully opened, they sit squarely in the "beverage and luncheon" category — big enough for a meal, not so big they feel like tablecloths. The 2-ply construction is the headline here; many budget napkins claim double layers but deliver tissue-thin sheets that disintegrate on contact with moisture. These don’t.

**Real-world performance**

I put these through the standard gauntlet: greasy pizza slices, barbecue sauce, coffee spills, and the dreaded "napkin under a sweating glass" test. The Party Solids held up admirably. The paper has a noticeable heft — you can feel the density when you pull one from the stack. It absorbs liquid without immediately wicking through to your hand, and it resists tearing when you need to scrub at a stubborn stain.

One buyer noted, *"I use these for everything from lunchboxes to dinner parties. They’re thicker than what you get at most restaurants."* That tracks with my experience. The dye job is also solid — the colors are saturated and consistent, and they don’t bleed when wet. I folded one around a damp paper towel for ten minutes; no transfer to the towel or my hands.

**The praise is consistent**

Scrolling through the ratings, three themes dominate. First, durability: multiple reviewers mention using them for heavy-duty tasks like wrapping silverware or cleaning up spills, not just dabbing lips. Second, aesthetics: the solid colors photograph well and don’t look cheap on a party table. Third, value: at roughly 10 cents per napkin or less depending on the color pack, they out-perform store brands at a comparable price point.

**Where they fall short**

No product is flawless, and this one has two genuine drawbacks. First, the surface is smooth — there’s no embossing or quilting pattern. That means less texture for gripping slippery foods like chicken wings or greasy burgers. You’ll still get the job done, but it’s not the most tactile experience. Second, color consistency across batches can drift slightly. If you buy a 100-pack now and another in six months, the "red" might not match perfectly. For most people this is irrelevant; for event planners obsessing over exact color matching, it’s worth ordering your full quantity at once.

**Who should buy this**

If you host regular gatherings, pack lunches daily, or just hate the flimsy napkins that come with takeout, this pack delivers. It’s also a smart pick for anyone who wants a more polished look at a backyard BBQ or picnic without paying linen-rental prices. On the flip side, if you need ultra-absorbent napkins for heavy-duty messy meals — think saucy ribs or juicy burgers — consider stepping up to a 3-ply option. The Party Solids will handle it, but you’ll use more of them.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Do these napkins work for holding greasy food?* Yes, but with limits. A single napkin wraps a chicken wing fine, but for anything dripping, double up or use two layers.

*Are the colors fade-resistant?* Yes, in practice. The dye is stable during normal use, including light moisture exposure.

*Are they lint-free?* Mostly. I didn’t notice residue on dark clothing, which is a common complaint with cheaper napkins.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want dependable, good-looking napkins for everyday meals and casual entertaining without overpaying. **Skip it if:** You need maximum absorbency for heavy-duty messy eating, or you require perfectly matched colors across separate orders.

At 4.6 out of 5, the Party Solids 100-pack earns its rating. It’s not revolutionary — it’s just a solid, honest product that does what it promises. For most households, that’s exactly what you want in a disposable.
