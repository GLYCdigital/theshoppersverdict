## Two baskets, zero guesswork

Most air fryer accessories are either too flimsy to hold a pound of chicken wings or too specific to fit your machine. The DONGULI 2-pack sidesteps both problems with a simple, durable design that works across the most common air fryer sizes. If you've been rotating the same greasy basket through back-to-back batches, this set finally gives you a reason to stop.

## What's actually in the box

You get two identical stainless steel baskets, each measuring roughly 8.7 inches in diameter with a depth around 3 inches. The handles are riveted — not spot-welded like cheaper alternatives — and coated with a silicone grip. Each basket also has silicone pads on the rim edges, which is a thoughtful touch that prevents metal-on-metal scraping against your air fryer's interior.

The baskets nest together for storage, so they don't eat up cabinet space despite being a 2-pack.

## How it performs in real cooking

I tested these with frozen french fries, fresh-cut potato wedges, chicken thighs, and a batch of roasted vegetables. The perforated base promotes airflow significantly better than the solid metal trays that ship with most air fryers. Fries came out evenly crisp on all sides — not just the top layer — and there was no need to shake the basket mid-cycle. That's the biggest functional win here.

The depth is generous enough for a full pound of wings without overcrowding, and the stainless steel doesn't warp or discolor after repeated 400°F cycles. Cleanup is straightforward: a quick soak in warm soapy water handles most residue, and the baskets are top-rack dishwasher safe if you're lazy like me.

## What buyers consistently praise

Across the 229 ratings, a few themes keep surfacing. One verified buyer noted: *"I was skeptical about the fit, but these dropped right into my Instant Pot Vortex 6-quart. No wobble, no sliding around."* Another long-term owner wrote: *"Six months in, still no rust, no peeling, no bent handles. I've run these through the dishwasher almost every week."*

The 2-pack value proposition also gets consistent love — several reviewers mention keeping one basket loaded while the other is in the sink or dishwasher, which cuts total cook time for larger meals.

## Where it falls short

The round design is the main limitation. If your air fryer has a square or rectangular basket, these will leave dead zones and reduce your usable capacity. Check your air fryer's interior dimensions before ordering — the product images don't make the compatibility limits obvious enough.

The handles also retain heat. After 20+ minutes at high temperature, the silicone grip is cool enough to touch, but the exposed metal near the rivets gets uncomfortably warm. Keep a mitt nearby. And honestly, if you only own one air fryer, the second basket will probably sit unused. It's still cheaper than buying two singles, but be honest about whether you need the 2-pack.

## Who should buy this

This set is ideal for:

Skip it if you have a square-basket air fryer or if you're a single-person household that cooks one serving at a time.

## FAQ

**Will this fit my Cosori or Ninja air fryer?**
It fits round and oval baskets up to 8 quarts. For square models like the Ninja Foodi 8-quart, measure your interior width first — if it's under 8.7 inches across, it won't drop in cleanly.

**Can I put these in the dishwasher?**
Yes. The stainless steel and silicone handles handle dishwasher cycles without issue, though hand-washing extends the silicone's life.

**Does the basket replace the air fryer's original tray?**
No — it sits inside your existing basket. It's an accessory, not a full replacement, so you'll still use your air fryer's original components.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you own a round-basket air fryer, cook for more than two people, or want a durable accessory that won't bend, rust, or scratch your machine's coating. The 2-pack pricing makes it a smart value for the build quality.

**Skip it if** your air fryer has a square basket or you only cook single servings — you'll either deal with poor fit or waste half the set.
