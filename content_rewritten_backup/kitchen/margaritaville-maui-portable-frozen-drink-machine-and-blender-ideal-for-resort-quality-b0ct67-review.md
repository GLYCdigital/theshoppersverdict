You've seen the ads: sun-drenched poolside, a frozen concoction swirling in a portable cup, no cord in sight. The Margaritaville Maui ($99-ish street price, often discounted) promises resort-grade frozen drinks anywhere — beach, tailgate, campsite. Here's the honest breakdown before you click buy.

**What's in the box:** The Maui ships with the 24-ounce blending cup with measurement markings, a locking lid with a drinking spout, the USB-C charging cable, and the base unit that doubles as the motor housing and charging dock. No wall adapter included — you'll use your phone charger. The whole thing weighs 3.2 pounds, so it's genuinely portable but not pocket-sized. As shown in the product image, the cup locks onto the base with a quarter-turn; get that alignment right or you'll get leakage.

**Real-world performance:** The Maui uses a 7.4V lithium-ion battery rated for roughly four 8-ounce frozen drinks per charge. That matches what buyers report. Blending time for a proper frozen margarita consistency runs 60–90 seconds — longer if your ice is straight from the freezer. The blade is a four-prong stainless steel design that handles crushed ice and frozen fruit well, but it chokes on full cubes. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: "I learned to crack the ice with a mallet first. The Maui finishes the job, but it's not a Vitamix." Another noted the motor pitch gets whiny on thick mixes — that's normal for a portable unit, but worth knowing.

**What buyers consistently praise:**
- "Took it on a pontoon boat weekend. Charged it twice, made 20+ drinks. It's the star of every cooler."
- "The dishwasher-safe cup is a game-changer. Rinses clean, no weird smells lingering."
- "It's not a showpiece. It's a workhorse. I've used it daily for two months."

**Where it falls short:** The "frozen" setting is optimistic. True slushie texture requires you to start with crushed or pebble ice — the manual says so, but most people miss it. The battery indicator is just a single LED that blinks when low; no percentage readout, so you'll guess. And the lack of a safety cutoff means if the cup isn't twisted fully into place, you get a puddle on your counter before you notice. One buyer summed up the frustration: "I wish it had a 'you're about to make a mess' warning."

**Who should buy this:**
- **Tailgaters and campers** who want frozen margaritas without a generator — the USB-C rechargeability is gold.
- **Small households** — 24 ounces is perfect for two drinks. It's not a party machine; it's a personal bartender.
- **RVers and boaters** — compact footprint, easy to store, and no fragile glass pitcher to break underway.

**Skip it if:** You need to blend full ice cubes from a bag of freezer ice, or you're hosting more than four people — you'll be babysitting recharges. Also skip if you expect margarita-machine consistency; this makes *good* frozen drinks, not *perfect* ones.

**FAQ:**

*Q: Can it crush regular ice cubes?*
A: Not effectively. The manual recommends crushed or pebble ice. If you only have full cubes, crack them first or blend in short bursts with liquid added — full cubes will stall the motor.

*Q: How long does a full charge take?*
A: About 2.5 hours via USB-C. You can use it while plugged in, but it drains the battery faster than it charges, so it's not a true pass-through.

*Q: Is the cup truly dishwasher-safe?*
A: Yes — the cup, lid, and blade assembly are all top-rack dishwasher safe. The base is not; wipe it clean with a damp cloth.

**The Verdict:** The Margaritaville Maui earns its 4.3-star rating honestly. It's a capable, portable frozen drink maker that nails the use case it's designed for — single or double servings on the go. The crushed-ice requirement and the occasional leak are real annoyances, but for the price, it's the most fun you can have with a blender that fits in a beach bag.

**Buy it if:** You want frozen drinks at the beach, the boat, or the campsite and don't mind pre-crushing ice.
**Skip it if:** You expect full-cube blending or party-sized batches.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A great buy for the right buyer, with two small compromises you should know about before you order.
