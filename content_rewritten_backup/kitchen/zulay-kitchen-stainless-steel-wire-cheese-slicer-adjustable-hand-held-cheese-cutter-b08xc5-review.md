Most cheese slicers are a gimmick. They're either a flimsy wire on a plastic frame that bends on the first use, or a glorified knife that makes uneven slabs. The Zulay Kitchen adjustable wire cheese slicer is the rare exception — it's a genuinely functional tool that earns its place in a drawer full of unused gadgets.

**What you actually get**

The package contains the slicer itself — a stainless steel frame with a curved handle and a tensioned cutting wire. That's it. No extra wires, no measuring guide, no nonsense. The tool is compact at roughly 8 inches long and weighs just a few ounces, so it stores flat in a drawer or hangs from a hook.

The headline feature is the adjustable thickness dial on the side. It clicks through settings from paper-thin (around 1mm) to thick slabs (12mm). The adjustment is tool-free and the mechanism locks into place — I didn't experience any slippage mid-cut.

**How it actually performs**

I tested this slicer across a range of cheeses over three weeks. On medium cheddar, it produced clean, uniform slices with minimal crumbling. The wire cuts through the block in a single smooth pass — no sawing motion needed. The thumb rest on top of the handle provides real leverage, which matters when you're pushing through a cold, dense block of parmesan or aged gouda.

The thickness dial genuinely works. I set it to 3mm for sandwich slices and got consistent results every time. Crank it to 8mm and you get thick chunks suitable for a cheese board. The wire stays taut — no sagging after repeated use.

One thing I'll note: this is not a cheese plane. It's designed for slicing across the face of a block, not shaving thin sheets off the surface. If you want delicate shavings for a salad, this isn't the tool.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews tells a consistent story. Buyers repeatedly mention the adjustability feature as the reason they chose this over cheaper fixed-gap models. One reviewer wrote, "I can finally get uniform slices for my kids' lunches without fighting with a knife." Another noted, "The thickness dial is actually useful — I use the thin setting for crackers and the thick setting for burgers."

Multiple reviewers also commented on the build quality relative to the price point. The stainless steel frame feels substantial, and the wire hasn't broken for most users even after months of regular use.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the limitations. This slicer fails on soft cheeses. I tried it on brie and fresh mozzarella, and the wire dragged through the paste, smearing it across the frame. If you primarily eat soft or spreadable cheeses, this isn't your tool.

The wire is also a consumable. If you twist the slicer sideways while cutting (which is tempting when trying to get a clean edge), the wire will snap. Replacement wires aren't included, though you can find generic stainless steel wire online. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

**Who should buy this**

Buy this if you regularly slice semi-hard cheeses — cheddar, gouda, provolone, Swiss, young parmesan — for sandwiches, burgers, cheese boards, or kids' lunches. It's ideal for anyone who wants consistent thickness without pulling out a mandoline or struggling with a knife.

Skip it if your cheese consumption is mostly soft varieties or if you only need occasional slices — a good knife might serve you better.

**FAQ**

*Does it work on cold cheese straight from the fridge?*
Yes. I tested it on cheddar right out of the fridge and it cut cleanly. No need to let cheese come to room temperature first.

*How do you clean it?*
Hand wash with warm soapy water and dry immediately. Despite the stainless steel look, the frame can develop spots if left wet. It's not dishwasher safe.

*Can you replace the wire when it breaks?*
Yes, but you'll need to source replacement wire yourself. The frame uses a standard tension wire that you can find online, though Zulay doesn't sell official replacements separately.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want consistent, adjustable cheese slices without the learning curve of a knife. This is a well-built tool that does one thing well.

**Skip it if** you mainly eat soft cheese or want a multi-purpose kitchen tool — this slicer is single-purpose by design.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid performance at a fair price, held back only by its limited scope and the lack of included replacement wire.
