# TOPZEE 11 Quart Large Air Fryer Review

**Meta Description:** TOPZEE 11 Quart Large Air Fryer review: Does this family-sized basket fryer deliver crispy results? We break down capacity, performance, and value.

---

## The Big Question: Is Bigger Actually Better?

Most air fryers top out at 5-6 quarts — enough for a couple, maybe a small family. The TOPZEE 11 Quart Large Air Fryer takes a different approach: go big or go home. At nearly double the capacity of standard models, this unit promises to handle a whole chicken, a full batch of fries, or meal prep for the week. But does the extra space translate to better results, or just a bigger footprint on your counter?

## What's in the Box

The TOPZEE 11 Quart arrives with the main unit, a large non-stick basket, a crisper tray, and a recipe booklet. The basket is substantial — roughly 11 inches across and 5 inches deep — giving you real vertical clearance for roasts or stacked food. The digital display sits on top with eight preset functions (fries, chicken, steak, fish, shrimp, pizza, bake, and dehydrate) plus manual temperature and time controls ranging from 180°F to 400°F.

## Performance and Build Quality

Here's where the TOPZEE earns its 4.2 verdict score. The 1700-watt heating element gets hot fast — preheating takes about 2-3 minutes — and the fan circulates air aggressively enough to crisp food evenly across the full basket. In testing, frozen french fries came out golden and crunchy in 18 minutes at 400°F with a single shake. Chicken wings needed 25 minutes and came out with that satisfying crackle.

The non-stick coating on both the basket and crisper tray held up well through multiple washes, and everything is dishwasher-safe (though hand-washing is recommended to preserve the coating). The exterior stays cool to the touch thanks to double-wall insulation, which matters when you've got kids around.

The "large" claim checks out. You can fit a 5-pound chicken vertically, a 9-inch pizza flat, or roughly 3 pounds of fries in a single batch. For families of 4-6, this genuinely replaces your oven for most cooking tasks.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The capacity is the star. One verified buyer wrote: *"I cooked a whole 5-pound chicken in 45 minutes and it was perfect. My old 5-quart could never do that."* Another noted: *"Finally don't have to cook in three batches for my family of five."*

The ease of cleaning gets consistent mentions — the non-stick surface genuinely releases food without scraping, and the lack of nooks and crannies (no exposed heating coil inside the basket) makes wiping it down quick.

## Where It Falls Short

Be honest about the tradeoffs. At roughly 14 inches tall and 12 inches deep, this is a large appliance. It will not fit under most upper cabinets — plan counter space accordingly. The basket, when loaded, is heavy. If you have wrist issues, lifting and shaking a full basket one-handed is genuinely difficult.

Temperature accuracy is decent but not lab-grade. Some users reported the displayed temperature runs about 15-20°F hot at lower settings. Not a dealbreaker, but you'll want to check doneness a few minutes early.

The presets are conservative — they tend to undercook slightly, so you'll likely add 2-4 minutes. And the loud beep when cooking finishes can't be muted, which is annoying during early morning meal prep.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Large families** — if you're cooking for 5+, this replaces batch-cooking frustration
- **Meal preppers** — cook a full week of chicken breasts or roasted vegetables in one go
- **Whole-chicken enthusiasts** — this is one of the few air fryers that handles a full bird
- **Anyone with limited oven time** — for small kitchens, this reduces oven dependency

Skip it if you're single, cooking for two, or have limited counter space. A 5-6 quart model will serve you better without the footprint.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I stack food in the basket, or does everything need to be in a single layer?**
For crispiest results, keep food in a single layer. That said, the tall basket allows you to stack wings or drumsticks — just shake halfway through. For fries or nuggets, single layer is best.

**Q: Does it make noise?**
Yes, around 55-60 dB — comparable to a microwave or blender on low. It's noticeable but not disruptive for conversation.

**Q: Is the basket really dishwasher-safe?**
Yes, but the non-stick coating will last longer with hand-washing. If you use the dishwasher, place the basket upside down on the top rack.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You cook for a crowd, want to replace your oven for most daily cooking, or need serious batch capacity without the heating time of a conventional oven.

**Skip it if:** Counter space is precious, you're cooking for one or two, or you prefer compact appliances you can tuck away after use.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The TOPZEE 11 Quart delivers on its core promise: genuine large-format air frying at a reasonable price. It's not perfect — the size is a commitment, and temperature accuracy needs a watchful eye — but for families who want crispy food without the oven, this is a solid investment that earns its counter space.
