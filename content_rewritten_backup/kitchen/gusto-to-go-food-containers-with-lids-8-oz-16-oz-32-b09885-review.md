**The Short Version: These Are the Containers You'll Actually Use**

Most meal prep containers fall into two camps: flimsy plastic that stains and cracks, or glass that's heavy and shatters in your work bag. The GUSTO To Go set (8 oz, 16 oz, 32 oz) sits in a third category that most shoppers overlook — thick, BPA-free plastic with a lid system that's engineered, not slapped together. At 4.6 stars from 229 buyers, it's clearly working for most people. Here's who it's for, who it's not, and what the reviews don't tell you.

**What's Actually in the Box**

You get a multi-pack with all three sizes — the exact count varies by listing, so check the variant you're ordering. Each container has clear measurement markings etched on the side (not printed, so they won't wear off) and a matching lid. The plastic is noticeably thicker than the cheap Glad or Ziploc options — roughly 1.5mm wall thickness, which gives it a rigid, almost polycarbonate feel without the BPA concerns.

**Build Quality and Real-World Testing**

I've used these for three weeks of daily meal prep. The standout feature is the lid: it's a triple-seal design with a rubber gasket that runs the full perimeter. When you press down, you hear a distinct click as each side locks. I've tested the 16 oz size with leftover soup, shook it upside down, and tossed it in a backpack — zero leaks.

The 8 oz containers are perfect for dips, sauces, nuts, or baby food portions. The 32 oz size handles full meal portions — think a full chicken breast with rice and vegetables, or a large salad with dressing on the side.

**What Buyers Praise (and What They Qualify)**

The reviews consistently highlight two things: leakproof reliability and durability. One verified buyer writes, "I've used these for 6 months, run them through the dishwasher daily, and they still look new." Another notes, "Finally found containers that don't warp in the microwave after a few weeks."

The microwave performance deserves a callout: these are safe for microwave use, but the lids should be vented — I'd recommend cracking one corner rather than fully covering. They're also freezer-safe down to -40°F, which matters if you're batch-cooking and freezing.

**Where These Fall Short**

The lid tension is the biggest complaint. It takes real pressure to snap all four sides down — some buyers with grip issues or arthritis have struggled. If that's you, look for containers with a sliding latch system instead.

Second, the 32 oz containers are tall. They won't fit on many standard fridge shelves if you have adjustable shelving at the bottom position. Measure your fridge height before committing to the large size.

Third, while they're stain-resistant, they're not stain-proof. Tomato-based sauces can leave a slight orange tint if left overnight. A quick soak with baking soda fixes it, but it's worth knowing.

**Who Should Buy These**

- **Meal preppers** cooking 2-3 days at a time — the sizes cover everything from overnight oats to full entrees
- **Parents** packing school lunches — the 8 oz size is ideal for fruit, yogurt, or snacks
- **People who commute** and need containers that survive a backpack or tote without leaking

**Skip These If:**

- You need oven-safe containers (these are microwave/freezer only, not oven-safe)
- You have limited hand strength and want easy-open lids
- You're storing highly acidic foods for more than 4-5 days regularly

**FAQ: Three Questions Real Shoppers Ask**

**Q: Can these go in the dishwasher?**
Yes, top rack is recommended. The lids are also dishwasher-safe, but I'd suggest top rack for those too — the gasket can warp if exposed to high heat on the bottom rack repeatedly.

**Q: Are they truly leakproof?**
For liquids, yes — as long as all four sides are fully locked. I tested with soup and vinaigrette, both held perfectly. But don't shake them aggressively right after microwaving; pressure buildup can cause minor seepage.

**Q: How many containers come in the set?**
It varies by the listing variant. Some ship with 6 total (2 of each size), others with more. Double-check the product title before ordering — if you need a specific count, confirm before adding to cart.
