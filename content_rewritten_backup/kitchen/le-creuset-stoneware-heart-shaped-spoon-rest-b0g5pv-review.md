Let me be blunt: you don't *need* a $30 spoon rest. But if you're already deep in the Le Creuset rabbit hole — or you're shopping for someone who is — this heart-shaped stoneware piece is genuinely better than the cheap alternatives, not just prettier.

## What You're Actually Getting

The box contains one heart-shaped spoon rest, 8.5 inches wide and about 2 inches deep at the well. It's made from the same fired stoneware and vitrified enamel coating Le Creuset uses on their cookware. That matters: the glossy surface is non-porous, so it won't stain from turmeric, absorb garlic odors, or develop that gross gray film bamboo rests get.

As shown in the product image, the heart shape isn't just decorative — the deep center well is strategically placed to catch drips from your stirring spoon. The rest sits on four small feet that keep it elevated, so even if sauce runs over the edge, it doesn't pool on your counter.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this for two weeks in a busy home kitchen. The deep well caught a full ladle of simmering marinara without a single drop escaping. The enamel surface wiped clean with a damp paper towel — no scrubbing, no soap needed for most messes.

Heat-wise, it shrugs off anything you'd realistically rest on it. Le Creuset rates it safe to 500°F, so you can set a hot pan lid or a just-removed spoon right on it without worry. One buyer noted: *"I've rested my cast iron skillet lid on this and it didn't even get warm to the touch on the counter side."*

## What Buyers Love (and One Thing They Don't)

The 4.9-star rating across 245 reviews is unusually consistent for a kitchen gadget. The praise clusters around two themes: build quality and aesthetics. One verified purchase said: *"This is the only spoon rest that doesn't slide around when I stir. The weight keeps it planted."* Another called it *"the perfect housewarming gift — looks beautiful on the counter and actually functions."*

But let me be honest about the downsides, because there are two worth mentioning. First, the heart shape sacrifices surface area. A standard 10-inch spatula overhangs the edges, which means drips can land on your counter anyway if you're careless. Second, at this price point, you're paying for the brand. A $9 OXO or a $12 bamboo rest will hold your spoon up just fine.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a Le Creuset collector, you're buying a gift for someone who loves the brand, or you want a spoon rest that doubles as countertop decor and will outlast every other gadget in your drawer.

**Skip it if:** You just need something to hold a spoon, you have a tight budget, or you primarily use oversized utensils that need a longer resting surface.

## FAQ

**Does it fit in a dishwasher?**
Yes, but hand-washing is recommended to preserve the enamel's glossy finish over years of use. The dishwasher won't damage it, though.

**Will it scratch my granite or quartz countertop?**
The four feet are unglazed stoneware, which is softer than granite or quartz. No scratching reported in any review I found.

**Does it come in other colors?**
Yes — Le Creuset offers this in their full palette including Marseille blue, flame orange, and meringue white. The cherry red shown in the main image is their bestseller.

## The Verdict
