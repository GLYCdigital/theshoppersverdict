## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the capacity is limited—about 1.5 ounces of peppercorns per fill. If you're cooking for a crowd or run a busy kitchen, you'll be refilling every few days. Second, the aluminum body transfers cold—if you leave it near the fridge, it'll feel chilly in your hand. Not a dealbreaker, but worth noting.

The biggest issue, though, is the grind adjustment mechanism. It works flawlessly now, but the internal components are plastic where they mate with the ceramic burrs. That's the classic failure point for mills in this price range—I've seen it fail after a year of heavy use on comparable models. It's too early to say definitively, but it's a risk you should know about.
