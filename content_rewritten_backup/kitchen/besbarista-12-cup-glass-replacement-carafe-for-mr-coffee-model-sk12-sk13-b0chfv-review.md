**What's Actually in the Box**

You get the glass carafe with the handle pre-attached, a plastic lid that fits snugly, and the internal brew basket that sits inside the carafe top. This one includes it, and buyers confirm it fits the Mr. Coffee SK12 and SK13 models without modification.

The pour spout is the make-or-break feature on any carafe. A bad one drips down the side, leaving brown streaks on your counter and burning your hand when you grab a towel to wipe it. The BESBARISTA spout is sharp and clean — buyers consistently mention no-drip pouring, which is the #1 reason people stick with it. **Where It Falls Short**

The glass is noticeably thinner than the original. It's not fragile — it'll survive normal use and dishwasher cycles — but if you're the type to clank the carafe against the warming plate or scrub aggressively with a metal brush, you'll want to adjust your habits. The lid gasket also needs a firm push to seat properly when new. It loosens up after a few uses, but the first couple of brews might have you wondering if it's seated right. Not a dealbreaker, just a heads-up.
