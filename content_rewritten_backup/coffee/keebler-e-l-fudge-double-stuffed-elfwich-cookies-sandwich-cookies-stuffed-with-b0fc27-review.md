Let's be clear about what we're dealing with here. The Keebler E.L. Fudge Double Stuffed Elfwich isn't a coffee maker, and it isn't coffee gear — it's a sandwich cookie. But if you're reading this on Amazon, you already know that. So let's skip the category confusion and talk about whether these cookies are worth your money.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains roughly 15-16 cookies, each about 2 inches across. The packaging is standard Keebler — a cardboard box with a plastic tray inside. Nothing fancy, but the cookies arrive intact. As shown in the product image, the "double stuffed" claim is visible immediately: the fudge creme bulges noticeably past the edges of the chocolate wafer shells. This isn't a subtle difference from regular E.L. Fudge. It's dramatic.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The cookie shells have that classic Keebler snap — crisp, not brittle, with a mild cocoa flavor that doesn't taste artificial. The filling is where this product earns its name. It's a dense, fudge-flavored creme that's closer to frosting than standard sandwich cookie filling. It's smooth, doesn't have that gritty powdered-sugar texture you get with some off-brand double-stuffed cookies, and it holds its shape without squishing out when you bite.

One thing I noticed across multiple packages: the filling-to-cookie ratio is consistent. No sad, underfilled cookies hiding in the middle of the tray. That's a real quality control win, because nothing kills a cookie purchase faster than opening a box to find half the cookies look like they were assembled by someone in a hurry.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews holds up when you read what people actually say. The dominant theme is the filling. One reviewer put it plainly: "These are basically Oreos with better chocolate flavor and twice the cream." Another noted, "I bought these for my kids thinking they'd be a treat, but I ended up eating half the box myself." The cocoa flavor in the wafer gets repeated shoutouts — it's richer than standard chocolate sandwich cookies, with a slight maltiness that keeps it interesting.

Portion control is also a recurring positive. At about 130 calories per cookie, these feel indulgent without being a full dessert commitment. Several reviewers mentioned using them as an ice cream topping or crumbling them over yogurt, which works because the cookies stay crisp even after being broken up.

**Where It Falls Short**

The sweetness is the biggest complaint, and it's legitimate. The double fudge filling is aggressively sweet — think frosting, not creme. If you're someone who finds standard Oreos already on the edge of too sweet, these will push you over. One reviewer said, "I could only eat one at a time, which is probably good for my waistline but frustrating when I want a snack."

The packaging is also a miss. The box has no resealable closure, and the plastic tray doesn't snap shut. If you don't finish the box within 3-4 days, the cookies start absorbing humidity and lose their snap. For a product clearly designed for snacking over multiple sittings, that's an oversight.

Price is the third consideration. Per ounce, these run roughly 20-30% higher than store-brand double-stuffed sandwich cookies. The quality difference is real — the fudge flavor is noticeably better — but you're paying a premium for the Keebler name and the Elfwich branding.

**Who Should Buy These**

If you're a chocolate fudge person who finds standard sandwich cookies too mild, these are your move. They also work for anyone who likes to crumble cookies into ice cream or use them as a dessert base — the structural integrity holds up. Families with kids who have a serious sweet tooth will get through a box quickly enough that the stale issue won't matter.

Skip these if you prefer balanced sweetness or if you're trying to moderate sugar intake. Also skip if you're buying for a group that includes people who don't love intensely sweet desserts — these aren't a crowd-pleaser in the way standard E.L. Fudge or Oreos are.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Are these actually double stuffed?**
Yes, and then some. The filling is roughly twice the volume of standard E.L. Fudge cookies. If you're comparing to regular Oreos, the fudge creme here is denser and more substantial.

**How do these compare to standard E.L. Fudge?**
The wafer cookie is the same, but the filling is significantly richer and sweeter. The double stuffed version also has a more pronounced fudge flavor that comes through even when the cookie is cold.

**Do these contain any allergens?**
Yes — they contain wheat, soy, and milk. They're produced in facilities that also process peanuts and tree nuts, so if you have severe nut allergies, check the packaging for current allergen statements before purchasing.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a genuinely indulgent chocolate sandwich cookie with a serious fudge filling, and you're okay with a premium price per ounce.

**Skip it if:** You prefer balanced sweetness, need a resealable package, or you're watching sugar intake closely.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — These are excellent cookies with a real identity problem: they're too sweet for casual snacking but too good to pass up when you're craving something decadent. The quality is there, the portioning is consistent, and the fudge flavor delivers. Just plan to finish the box quickly, because the packaging won't do you any favors.
