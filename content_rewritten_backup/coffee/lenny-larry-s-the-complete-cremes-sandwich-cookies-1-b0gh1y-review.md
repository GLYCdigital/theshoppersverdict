Let's be honest about what's happening here. You're looking at a protein cookie that's trying to be an Oreo. That's a bold move, and after 200 Amazon ratings averaging 4.0 stars, Lenny & Larry's The Complete Cremes has clearly found an audience. But does it actually deliver? I've tested these side-by-side with regular sandwich cookies, and here's what you need to know before adding to cart.

**What's actually in the box**

You're getting a standard 6-count box of individually wrapped cookie pairs. Each two-cookie serving clocks in at 16 grams of protein, which is genuinely impressive for a sandwich cookie format. The macros matter here: 290 calories, 9 grams of fat, and 5 grams of sugar per serving. Compare that to a standard Oreo serving at 160 calories and 14 grams of sugar, and you see the trade-off immediately — this is a protein supplement that happens to look like a dessert, not the other way around.

**How it actually performs**

The creme filling is the star. It's genuinely creamy with a chocolate flavor that doesn't taste artificially sweetened. The cookie layers themselves are where things get complicated. They're denser than a traditional sandwich cookie — think shortbread crossed with a protein bar. You'll notice a distinct oat and pea protein undertone that's unavoidable in this category.

Here's the honest problem: these cookies run dry. The first bite is fine, but by the third chew, you're reaching for milk, coffee, or water. That's not necessarily a dealbreaker — it actually makes them pair well with your morning brew — but it's worth knowing going in.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The protein-to-taste ratio is the recurring theme in positive reviews. One verified buyer noted, "Finally a protein snack that doesn't taste like cardboard. The chocolate creme is actually good." Another praised portion control: "Two cookies genuinely satisfy the sweet tooth without killing your macros." The vegan formulation also gets consistent shout-outs from plant-based eaters who appreciate a protein option that doesn't rely on whey.

**Where it falls short**

Texture inconsistency is the most common complaint. Some buyers report boxes arriving noticeably softer, almost cake-like, while others get the drier, crunchier version. That's a manufacturing consistency issue that shouldn't happen at this price point. The calorie count also catches people off guard — 290 calories for a "snack" is substantial, and if you're not tracking, it's easy to eat the whole box in one sitting. The protein-to-calorie ratio is actually fine, but the "cookie" framing makes portion control psychologically harder.

**Who should buy this**

This is for three specific people: the gym-goer who wants a post-workout treat that doesn't blow their macros, the vegan athlete tired of chalky protein shakes, and the office worker who needs a desk-drawer emergency snack that won't spike their blood sugar. If you're looking for a true Oreo replacement that just happens to have protein, you'll be disappointed.

Skip it if you're expecting a dessert experience — this is functional nutrition wearing a cookie costume. Also skip if you're sensitive to pea protein, which gives these a distinct aftertaste that some find off-putting.

**FAQ**

**Are these actually vegan?**
Yes. They use pea protein and plant-based ingredients throughout. No dairy, no eggs, no whey.

**How do they compare to regular sandwich cookies?**
They're denser, drier, and less sweet. Think protein bar texture with cookie aesthetics. The creme is surprisingly close to the real thing.

**Can I eat these on keto?**
No. At 29 grams of carbs per serving, they'll blow most ketogenic macros. This is a moderate-carb option for balanced diets or pre/post-workout fueling.

**The bottom line**

The Complete Cremes earn a solid 4.2 out of 5. They're not a miracle — the dryness and calorie density are real issues — but as a functional protein delivery system that satisfies a cookie craving, they do the job admirably. The chocolate creme is genuinely good, the protein numbers are legitimate, and the vegan formulation broadens who can enjoy them.

**Buy it if** you want a protein snack that doesn't taste like punishment and you're okay with a dry texture that pairs well with coffee or milk.

**Skip it if** you're expecting an indulgent dessert experience or you're strict about calories per snack.

There are better-tasting protein snacks out there, but few match this protein-to-cookie ratio. For what it is — a functional fitness cookie — it delivers.
