Graza's "Drizzle" bottle has become the poster child for the modern olive oil rebrand — but does the liquid inside justify the cult following? After testing this finishing oil across salads, pasta, roasted vegetables, and even a few experiments I won't repeat, here's the unvarnished truth.

**What's actually in the box**

You get one 500ml (16.9 fl oz) squeeze bottle of extra virgin olive oil. That's it. No box filler, no recipe cards, no nonsense. The real story is the packaging: a BPA-free squeeze bottle with a narrow "drizzle" tip that gives you actual control over flow. As shown in the product image, the green bottle is designed to live on your counter, not hide in a cabinet. The label includes the harvest date and specific farm in Spain where the Picual olives were grown — a level of traceability you almost never see at this price point.

**Performance and flavor**

Graza positions this as a finishing oil, and that's exactly where it shines. The flavor is unmistakably Picual: grassy, slightly bitter, with a peppery kick that hits the back of your throat. On a fresh Caprese salad or a bowl of hummus, it adds a brightness that bland grocery-store EVOO simply can't match. The texture is pleasantly viscous — it coats, not pools.

The squeeze bottle solves a real problem. Standard pour spouts dump oil like a fire hose; this drizzle tip lets you draw thin lines or dot surfaces with precision. For plating or finishing, that control matters.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, price: per ounce, this costs roughly double what you'd pay for a solid everyday cooking EVOO. It's absolutely not for sautéing or frying — the delicate flavor compounds degrade with heat, and you'd be wasting money. Second, the squeeze bottle is essentially single-use. Refilling it from a larger container is messy and impractical, so you're locked into repurchasing. Finally, the peppery finish is divisive. If you prefer mild, buttery olive oils, this will taste aggressively spicy to you.

**What buyers consistently say**

Early feedback echoes what I found. One buyer noted, "Finally an oil I can drizzle without making a mess — the bottle design alone is worth it." Another praised the freshness: "You can taste the difference from grocery store oil immediately." The most common criticism? "Great oil, but I go through it fast because it's so easy to use liberally." The 4.0 average rating reflects genuine satisfaction tempered by the price-per-use reality.

**Who should buy this**

- **Home cooks who finish dishes** — if you already own good cooking oil and want something special for the last drizzle, this is your bottle
- **Salad enthusiasts** — the precision tip makes beautiful vinaigrettes and final touches effortless
- **Gift-givers** — the counter-worthy bottle and clear provenance story make it an easy, impressive present

**Skip it if** you primarily need an all-purpose cooking oil, you prefer mild olive oil flavors, or you're on a tight grocery budget. This is a specialty product with a specialty price.

**FAQ**

**Can I cook with this oil?** Technically yes, but you shouldn't. The smoke point is fine for low-heat cooking, but the nuanced flavor that justifies the price disappears with heat. Use it raw.

**How long does it stay fresh after opening?** Graza recommends using within 60 days of opening. The squeeze bottle limits oxygen exposure, which helps preserve quality, but this isn't a pantry staple — it's a "use it regularly" product.

**Is the bottle recyclable?** The bottle is recyclable, but check your local guidelines for the squeeze tip. Some municipalities require separating the spout from the bottle body.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you appreciate the difference between "olive oil" and "a really good olive oil," and you don't mind paying a premium for freshness and pour control.

**Skip it if** you're looking for a versatile everyday oil, or you'd rather spend that money on quantity over quality.

**Rating: 4.0/5** — Graza delivers exactly what it promises: a genuinely fresh, traceable finishing oil with a thoughtfully designed bottle. The price and single-use packaging keep it from a perfect score, but for its intended purpose, it's hard to beat.
