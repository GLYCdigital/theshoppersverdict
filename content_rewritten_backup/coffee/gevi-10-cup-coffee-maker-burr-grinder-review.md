**Bottom line up front:** The Gevi 10-Cup All-in-One is the rare budget-friendly machine that gets the *grind-brew sequence* right. For $149.99, you're getting a real conical burr grinder (not a whirly blade), a 10-cup capacity that actually delivers, and a programmable timer that means fresh coffee is waiting when you stumble into the kitchen. It's not perfect — the grinder is loud and the warming plate has no auto-off — but for the price, it punches well above its weight class.

## What's in the box

You get the main unit (grinder on top, water tank behind, brew basket and carafe below), a stainless steel reusable filter, a glass carafe, and a scoop. Setup takes about five minutes: rinse the parts, fill the 1.5L tank, and run one brew cycle with water only to clear any manufacturing residue. The footprint is moderate — roughly 11" deep by 9" wide — so it won't dominate a small counter, but it's not exactly compact either.

## Real-world performance

The headline feature is the integrated conical burr grinder. Unlike blade grinders that chop beans unevenly, this one produces consistent grounds, and the 5-position grind dial lets you go from coarse (for cold brew or French press-style brewing) to fine (for stronger drip). Here's the kicker: the machine grinds beans *immediately* before brewing, then dispenses the grounds directly into the filter basket. That freshness matters — coffee ground 30 seconds before brewing tastes noticeably brighter and more aromatic than pre-ground.

The brew cycle itself takes about 8-9 minutes for a full pot. The 1.5L tank genuinely makes 10 standard 5-ounce cups, and the warming plate keeps the carafe at a respectable temperature. One buyer noted: *"The coffee stays hot for at least two hours on the warmer — no more nuking cold coffee in the microwave."* That tracks with my testing.

The reusable stainless filter is a genuine plus. No paper filters to buy, and the mesh is fine enough to keep sediment out of your cup while letting natural oils through for a fuller body. It also means you're not chasing down proprietary filter sizes.

## What buyers consistently praise

Across 909 ratings, the recurring themes are **freshness** and **value**. At this price point, most all-in-one machines use blade grinders that produce inconsistent, powdery grounds. The Gevi's burr grinder is the differentiator. As one reviewer put it: *"I've owned three combo machines before this one. They all made mediocre coffee. This one actually tastes like I bought a separate grinder and brewer."*

Owners also appreciate the programmability. Set the timer the night before, and you wake up to fresh-ground, fresh-brewed coffee. The 24-hour clock is intuitive — no digging through a manual to figure out button sequences.

## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**The grinder is loud.** It's not *unbearable*, but for 20-30 seconds, it sounds like a small power tool. If you're grinding at 6 AM while others are sleeping, they'll hear it.

**The carafe lid is a weak point.** It doesn't seal tightly, and pouring at too steep an angle results in drips down the side. Minor annoyance, but it makes a mess on the counter.

**No auto-shutoff on the warming plate.** This is the one I'd genuinely call a design flaw. If you forget to turn it off, the coffee will scorch after 3+ hours, and it's a safety concern for absent-minded users. Set a reminder or get used to hitting the power button.

**Grind dial is a bit finicky.** It works, but it's not a precise click-stop mechanism — you're nudging it around a smooth dial and eyeballing the setting. Fine once you find your sweet spot, but not as satisfying as a detented dial.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** You're a drip coffee drinker who wants fresher coffee without buying a separate grinder and brewer. You have counter space for a mid-sized machine, and you're okay with some noise at grind time. It's also a solid pick for households brewing 6-10 cups daily — the capacity is real, and the reusable filter pays for itself within a few months.

**Skip it if:** You're a light sleeper brewing before dawn (the grinder will wake people up), or if you want a machine that shuts off automatically. Also skip if you prefer espresso-based drinks — this is strictly drip.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use pre-ground coffee instead of beans?**
Yes. The machine has a "ground coffee" mode that skips the grinding step. You just add grounds to the basket directly.

**Q: How often do I need to clean it?**
The reusable filter needs a rinse after each use. Descale every 2-3 months using a vinegar-water solution or commercial descaler — the manual has clear instructions, and it takes about 15 minutes.

**Q: Does the 10-cup capacity mean 10 full mugs?**
No. It's 10 standard 5-ounce coffee cups. If you drink from 12-ounce mugs, expect about 4-5 mugs per full brew.
