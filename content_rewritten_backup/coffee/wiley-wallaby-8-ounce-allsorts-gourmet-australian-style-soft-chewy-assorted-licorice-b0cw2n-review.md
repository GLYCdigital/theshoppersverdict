Let’s cut to it: If you’ve only ever eaten Twizzlers or Red Vines, Wiley Wallaby’s Allsorts bag is going to feel like a different species of candy. This is soft, squishy Australian-style licorice — closer to a firm fruit snack than the tough, waxy ropes most Americans grew up with. The 8-ounce bag gives you an assorted mix of flavors, making it a low-commitment way to see what all the fuss is about.

**What’s actually in the bag**

You get four flavors in the Allsorts mix: black licorice, raspberry, green apple, and mango. Each piece is roughly the size of a thick french fry — about 2.5 inches long and a third of an inch wide. The bag is resealable, which matters because these candies are soft and will absolutely clump together if you leave them loose in a warm pantry. As shown in the product image, the pieces are uniformly coated and clearly differentiated by color, so there’s no guessing game.

**How it performs in the real world**

The texture is the headline act here. Wiley Wallaby uses a recipe that swaps wheat flour for a cornstarch-and-gelatin base, which is why these come out soft and pillowy rather than chewy and tough. Biting into a piece, you get an initial gentle resistance that gives way immediately — think a firm marshmallow crossed with a fruit snack. The black licorice flavor has real anise depth without the harsh bite of some European imports. The fruit varieties are sweet and clean, though the mango is the mildest of the bunch — some buyers have noted it tastes more like generic fruit punch than actual mango.

One thing to flag: these are gluten-free and made in the USA under license from the Australian brand. That’s not a knock on quality — the recipe is solid — but if you’re expecting an authentic imported product, adjust your expectations.

**What buyers consistently say**

The 4.2-star rating across 210 reviews holds up when you read the feedback. The texture praise is nearly universal — one reviewer called it "the only licorice my kids will eat because it's not like pulling taffy off your teeth." Another noted, "I ordered these for a road trip and they lasted about 45 minutes. Dangerously snackable."

The most common complaint, beyond the mango being weak, is that the soft pieces stick together in the bag during shipping. If you live in a warm climate, consider refrigerating the bag for 15 minutes before digging in — it firms them up just enough to separate cleanly.

**Where it falls short**

Beyond the sticking issue, the assorted format is a double-edged sword. If you love black licorice but can’t stand fruit flavors — or vice versa — you’ll have to pick around pieces you don’t want. There’s no way to filter the mix. Also, the soft texture means these don’t hold up as well as harder licorice for snacking on the go in hot weather; they get tacky fast.

**Who should buy this**

- **The licorice skeptic** who’s only tried American brands and wants to see what the softer style is about
- **The candy grazer** who likes variety and doesn’t mind picking through a mixed bag
- **Anyone needing a gluten-free candy option** that still delivers on flavor and texture

Skip it if you need a single-flavor fix in bulk, or if you prefer the aggressive chew of traditional licorice.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this actually from Australia?**  
No. It’s made in the USA under license. The recipe is Australian-style, but it’s not imported.

**Q: How should I store these?**  
Keep the bag sealed in a cool, dry place. In warm weather, refrigerate before opening to prevent pieces from fusing together.

**Q: Are these good for kids?**  
Yes — the soft texture is easy to chew, and the fruit flavors are mild and sweet. The black licorice might be too strong for younger palates, though.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely different licorice experience that’s soft, snackable, and gluten-free. The assorted bag is a smart way to find your favorite flavor before committing to a larger single-variety purchase.

**Skip it if** you’re a hardcore traditionalist who wants that tough, chewy bite, or if you need a single flavor without picking around the mix.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, well-executed candy that delivers on its promise, with only minor gripes about flavor consistency and bag clumping. Worth your money if you’re curious about the softer side of licorice.
