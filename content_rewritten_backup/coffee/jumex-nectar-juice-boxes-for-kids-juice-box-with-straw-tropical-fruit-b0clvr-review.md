## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**Price point.** Nectars cost more per ounce than standard juice boxes. You're paying for the real fruit content and the import factor. If you're buying these in bulk for a classroom, the cost adds up quickly.

**Texture can be polarizing.** Some kids don't like the thicker mouthfeel. One reviewer noted their child called it "too pulpy" and refused to finish it. If your kid is picky about texture, this might be a hard sell.

**Limited availability.** This isn't always in stock at mainstream grocery chains. You'll likely need to order online, which means you can't grab a box last-minute for a school event.

**Sugar content is still significant.** While it's lower than many competitors, nectar is still a sweet beverage. At roughly 20-25 grams of sugar per box (depending on flavor), it's not a low-sugar option. It's just *better* than the worst offenders.
