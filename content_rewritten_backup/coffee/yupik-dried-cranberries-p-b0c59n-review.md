Let's be direct: most dried cranberries on Amazon are either rock-hard, overly sugared, or taste like cardboard. The Yupik 1kg bag sits at 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, which in the dried fruit category is genuinely rare. Here's whether it deserves your money.

**What's actually in the bag**

You're getting a 2.2-pound (1kg) stand-up pouch of sweetened dried cranberries. The berries are medium-sized, deep red, and — this is the key differentiator — they arrive *moist*. Not sticky-wet, but soft enough that you can eat them straight from the bag without worrying about cracking a tooth. As one verified buyer put it, "These are the only dried cranberries I've found that don't turn into little rocks after a week." That's the core value proposition.

**How they actually perform**

Texture holds up remarkably well. I've had my bag open for three weeks now, and the berries on top are still pliable. That's because they're infused with sugar syrup rather than just coated, which locks in moisture. The sweetness level is moderate — noticeably less cloying than Ocean Spray's standard Craisins. The tart cranberry flavor comes through on the finish, which makes them work better in savory applications like salads or stuffing than the candy-sweet alternatives.

The bag itself is the weak point. It's a simple foil pouch with no zipper. Once opened, you're committed to either a chip clip or transferring to a mason jar. Several reviewers noted their berries arrived clumped into a solid mass — this happens during shipping in warm weather, and you'll need to whack the bag against the counter to break them apart. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

**Where they fall short**

Two honest complaints from the review pool. First, the clumping issue mentioned above. It's cosmetic, not a quality problem, but it's annoying when you're trying to grab a handful for oatmeal. Second, there's no unsweetened option in this listing. If you're managing blood sugar or on a strict low-carb plan, the added sugar (about 29g per 100g serving) is a hard stop. The ingredient list is clean — just cranberries and sugar — no sulfites, no artificial colors, no palm oil.

**Who should buy these**

Buy them if you're a baker, a trail-mix maker, or someone who goes through dried fruit quickly. The 1kg size is economical — you're paying roughly half per ounce what you'd spend on the 12-ounce bags at the grocery store. They're also excellent for holiday baking; the moisture content means they don't suck liquid out of cookie dough the way cheaper dried cranberries do.

Skip them if you only need a small quantity, want unsweetened fruit, or don't have a container to store them in after opening.

**Three questions shoppers actually ask**

**Are these the same as Craisins?** Not quite. They're less sweet, slightly softer, and about 40% cheaper per ounce in this bulk size. The flavor is more authentically cranberry — tart with a sugar finish rather than sugar with a hint of cranberry.

**How long do they last after opening?** Stored in an airtight container at room temperature, expect 2-3 months of good quality. In the fridge, push that to 6 months. They won't spoil — they'll just gradually dry out and harden.

**Can I use these in baking?** Yes, and they perform better than grocery-store brands because of the higher moisture content. Reduce the sugar in your recipe slightly to account for the sweetness.

**The bottom line**

At 4.6 stars from 229 buyers, this is a solid, honest product that delivers exactly what it promises: soft, flavorful dried cranberries in bulk at a fair price. The packaging is mediocre and the clumping is real, but neither affects the eating quality.

**Buy it if** you go through dried fruit regularly and want good value without sacrificing texture.

**Skip it if** you need a small quantity, want unsweetened, or can't be bothered to transfer the berries to a sealed container.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Docked half a star for the packaging shortcomings. The fruit itself is excellent.
