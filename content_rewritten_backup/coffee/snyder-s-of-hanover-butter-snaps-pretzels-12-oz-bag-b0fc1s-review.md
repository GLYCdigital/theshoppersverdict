Let’s be clear about what these are: Snyder’s of Hanover Butter Snaps aren’t your average pretzel. They’re thin, crispy, and engineered for snacking — not for dunking in thick dips or building soft-pretzel sandwiches. At 12 ounces, the bag is generous, but the real question is whether the flavor and texture justify the shelf space.

**What’s in the bag:** You get a resealable (technically) plastic pouch of small, twisted pretzel snaps. Each piece is about the size of a quarter, with a golden-brown sheen that hints at the butter coating. The ingredient list keeps it simple — enriched flour, butter, salt, yeast — no artificial preservatives screaming at you. As shown in the product image, they’re uniform and neat, which is nice for portioning.

**Real-world performance:** The first thing you notice is the crunch. It’s a sharp, clean snap — not the dense, doughy bite of a traditional rod. That’s either a win or a miss depending on what you want. I found them ideal for mindless snacking; you can crush a handful without feeling weighed down. The butter flavor is genuine but restrained — it coats the tongue lightly, without leaving that greasy film some flavored pretzels do. The salt level is moderate, though buyer reviews reveal a catch.

One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *“Great crunch, but one batch was salted like crazy and the next was almost bare. Inconsistent.”* That matches what I’ve seen — the seasoning distribution is the weak spot. Some snaps deliver a perfect salty-buttery hit; others taste flat. It’s not a dealbreaker, but it’s worth knowing.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The texture gets the most love. Multiple reviewers call them “dangerously easy to eat” and “the perfect party snack.” The thin profile also makes them excellent for pairing with hummus or ranch dip — they don’t break like thicker pretzels. One shopper noted, *“These are my go-to for soup — they add crunch without turning soggy instantly.”* That’s a fair point; the structure holds up better than expected.

**Where they fall short:** Beyond the salt inconsistency, the bag’s resealable zipper is weak. I’ve seen complaints about it popping open, leaving pretzels stale within days. Transfer them to a jar or clip the bag shut. Also, if you’re a fan of dense, chewy pretzels (think Philly soft pretzels), these will disappoint — they’re snacks, not pretzel bread.

**Who should buy these:**  
- **The volume snacker** — someone who wants a low-guilt, crunchy option to graze on during work or movie nights.  
- **Dip lovers** — the crisp texture handles creamy dips without crumbling.  
- **Lunchbox packers** — single servings are easy to portion, though you’ll want small bags to avoid staleness.  

**Skip them if:** You demand consistent seasoning batch-to-batch, or you prefer thick, hearty pretzels with a chewy bite.

**FAQ:**  
**1. Are these vegan?** The butter content means no — they contain dairy. Check the label for specifics.  
**2. Can I freeze them?** Yes, but they’ll lose some crunch. Better to store in an airtight container at room temperature.  
**3. How do they compare to regular Snyder’s rods?** These are thinner and crispier, with a lighter butter flavor. Rods are denser and better for heavy dips.

**The Verdict:**  
**Buy it if** you want a crunchy, buttery snack that disappears fast and plays well with dips.  
**Skip it if** you’re sensitive to inconsistent salting or need a sturdy pretzel for serious dunking.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid snack with a texture win, held back by seasoning variance and a flimsy bag. Worth trying, especially if you spot it on sale.
