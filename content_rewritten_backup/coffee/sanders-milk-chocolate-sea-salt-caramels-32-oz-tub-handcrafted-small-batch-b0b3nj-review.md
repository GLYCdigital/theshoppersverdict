**The 32-ounce tub that keeps giving — if you can stop at one.**

Let me be direct: Sanders has been making these caramels since 1879, and that longevity shows in the product. But what matters to you is whether a 32-ounce tub of milk chocolate sea salt caramels earns its pantry space. After digging through the specs, the 215 Amazon ratings (averaging 4.3 stars), and the real customer feedback, here's the honest picture.

**What you actually get**

The tub contains roughly 90–100 individually wrapped caramels. That's not a guess — the 32-ounce weight translates to about 2 pounds of chocolate-covered confection, and each piece runs approximately 0.3–0.35 ounces. You're getting a serious quantity here, not a sampler. The caramels arrive in a resealable plastic tub, which matters for freshness across weeks of snacking.

Each caramel is enrobed in milk chocolate and topped with flaky sea salt. The ingredient list is refreshingly short — cream, sugar, corn syrup, chocolate, butter, salt. No preservatives, no palm oil substitutes. That's the handcrafted small-batch claim backed up by the label.

**Performance and real-world use**

The texture is where Sanders earns its reputation. The caramel is genuinely chewy without being tough — it yields slowly, then melts. The milk chocolate coating is thin enough to snap cleanly but substantial enough to register. The sea salt isn't cosmetic; it hits your palate in distinct flakes, cutting through the sweetness in a way that makes you reach for another piece.

One buyer put it well: *"These are dangerously good. The salt is actually salty, not just for show, and the caramel is soft, not rock hard like some brands."* Another noted the value angle: *"I bought these for a wedding favor and ended up keeping half the tub. The individual wrapping makes them easy to grab and go."*

**Where it falls short**

The 4.3-star average isn't perfect, and the complaints cluster around two issues. First, temperature sensitivity: in warm conditions, the caramels can soften and stick to their wrappers. One reviewer mentioned, *"They arrived slightly melted in summer — still tasted fine, but the wrappers were a mess."* Store them in a cool spot, and this becomes manageable.

Second, salt coverage varies. Some pieces get a generous flake; others get a light dusting. It's a minor inconsistency, but if you're buying specifically for that salty-sweet balance, know that not every piece delivers it equally.

**Who should buy this**

This tub is ideal for three specific scenarios: office candy dishes that need refilling less often, holiday gift bags where individual wrapping saves you time, and anyone who genuinely prefers chewy caramels over hard ones. It's also a solid choice for baking — chopped caramels fold well into brownies or cookies.

Skip it if you're looking for a gift-ready presentation box (this is a bulk tub, not a gift set), if you live in a hot climate without climate-controlled storage, or if you prefer dark chocolate — this is unapologetically milk chocolate.

**FAQ**

**How long do these stay fresh after opening?** The tub is resealable, and with the individually wrapped pieces, they'll maintain quality for 2–3 months if stored away from heat and direct sunlight. The wrappers do the heavy lifting.

**Are these gluten-free?** Yes, the ingredient list contains no wheat or gluten-containing additives. They're produced in a facility that handles other products, so if you have severe celiac, exercise caution.

**Can I freeze them?** Yes. They freeze well for up to 3 months. Thaw in the refrigerator to prevent condensation, then bring to room temperature before serving.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a large quantity of genuinely good, chewy caramels with real sea salt character at a reasonable per-piece cost. The handcrafted quality justifies the price over mass-market options.

**Skip it if** you need consistent salt on every piece, live in a warm environment without cool storage, or prefer a smaller quantity that won't tempt you daily.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A well-executed classic with real value at volume. The minor salt inconsistency and temperature sensitivity keep it from perfection, but for the price per piece and the quality of the caramel itself, this is a strong buy.
