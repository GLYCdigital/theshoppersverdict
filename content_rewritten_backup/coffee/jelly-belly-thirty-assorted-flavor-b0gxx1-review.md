**What You Actually Get**

The "Thirty Assorted Flavor" bag delivers exactly what the name promises: 30 of Jelly Belly’s most iconic flavors in a single resealable pouch. You’re not getting the full 50-flavor lineup here—this is the greatest-hits collection. Think Very Cherry, Buttered Popcorn, Juicy Pear, Toasted Marshmallow, and Green Apple.

One thing worth noting: this is a mix of *assorted* flavors, not a curated box where you pick specific ones. You’re getting a random assortment of those 30, which matters if you’re the type who hates certain flavors. If you’re a purist who only wants the fruit flavors and despises the savory ones (looking at you, Buttered Popcorn), the randomness could be a mild frustration.

**Build Quality & Real-World Performance**

Let’s be honest about what "performance" means for candy. The texture is spot-on—that signature crisp shell with a soft, chewy interior that doesn’t stick to your teeth. The flavor intensity is where Jelly Belly separates itself from generic jelly beans. The flavors are remarkably accurate. The Very Cherry tastes like cherry candy, not vaguely fruit-flavored sugar.

The resealable bag is genuinely functional. It’s got a zipper-style closure that actually holds up to repeated openings, which matters if you’re not going to demolish the whole bag in one sitting (good luck with that).

**Where It Falls Short**

No product is perfect, and this one has a few honest tradeoffs:

- **No flavor list on the bag.** You know you’re getting 30 flavors, but the bag doesn’t tell you *which* 30. If you’re buying for someone with allergies or strong preferences, that’s a blind spot.
- **Random assortment.** You can’t guarantee you’ll get your favorites. If you hate Licorice and the mix is heavy on it, you’re picking around them.
- **Portion control is a myth.** The bag is large enough that you’ll eat more than you planned. That’s on you, but consider yourself warned.
