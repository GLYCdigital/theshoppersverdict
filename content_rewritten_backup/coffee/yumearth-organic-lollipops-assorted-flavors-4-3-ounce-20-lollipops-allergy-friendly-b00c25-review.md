**Is this actually a good lollipop, or just a "safe" one?** That's the question most shoppers ask when they see the YumEarth bag sitting next to the Dum Dums and Tootsie Pops. The short answer: yes, these are genuinely good. But they're not the same experience as a sugar-bomb candy, and you should know that before you buy.

## What's in the bag

You get 20 individually wrapped lollipops in assorted flavors — cherry, mango, pomegranate, grape, and a few others that rotate depending on the batch. Each pop is about 0.2 ounces, which is noticeably smaller than a standard Tootsie Pop. The wrappers are twist-tied clear cellophane, so it's easy to see the color of each pop before you unwrap it.

The ingredient list is where YumEarth separates itself. Certified organic cane sugar, brown rice syrup, and natural flavors. No corn syrup. No artificial colors — the vibrant reds and purples come from fruit and vegetable juices. No gelatin. And critically, the facility is free from the top 9 allergens: milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soy, and sesame.

## How they actually taste

Here's the honest part. These are not as sweet as conventional lollipops. The cane sugar gives a cleaner, lighter sweetness, and you'll notice a slight stevia aftertaste on certain flavors — grape is the worst offender, pomegranate is the best. The fruit flavors taste genuine rather than candy-like. The cherry is tart and bright, not medicinal. The mango is subtle, almost floral.

Texture-wise, they're hard and dense — they last longer than a Dum Dum but not as long as a Tootsie Pop. They don't crack or shatter easily, which matters if a kid is biting down.

## What buyers consistently say

The 4.7-star average holds up when you read the reviews. The most common praise is about allergen safety:

> *"Finally a lollipop my daughter with a peanut allergy can take to school. The teacher keeps a stash in her desk."*

Another recurring theme is taste approval from picky kids:

> *"My 4-year-old ate three in a row — I had to hide the bag. She asks for these over the grocery store brands."*

Parents also appreciate the individual wrapping — it makes portion control easier and keeps pops fresh for months.

## Where they fall short

Let's be direct about the downsides. The size is the biggest complaint. At 4.3 ounces total, you're getting small pops. If you're used to jumbo lollipops, these will feel skimpy. One reviewer put it plainly: *"They're tiny. My kid finishes one in five minutes."*

Shipping fragility is a real issue. The bag doesn't have protective packaging — just the pops inside a thin plastic pouch. Several reviews mention opening the bag to find a few pops broken into shards. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's annoying.

And the stevia aftertaste is real. YumEarth uses stevia leaf extract as a secondary sweetener. Some people don't notice it. Others find it off-putting. If you're sensitive to stevia, buy a single bag first before committing to the bulk size.

## Who should buy these

**Buy them if:** You have a child with food allergies and need a school-safe treat. You want a lollipop with recognizable ingredients rather than a chemistry lab list. You're packing lunchboxes and want something portion-controlled with no artificial dyes.

**Skip them if:** You want big, indulgent, candy-sweet lollipops. You hate stevia aftertaste. You're looking for a value pack — these cost more per pop than conventional brands.

## FAQ

**Are these actually organic?**
Yes. Certified organic by USDA. The cane sugar, brown rice syrup, and natural flavors all meet organic standards. No synthetic pesticides or GMOs.

**Do they contain any allergens?**
No. The top 9 allergens are absent, and the facility is allergen-free. They're also gluten-free and vegan. That said, always check the label — recipes can change.

**How long do they last?**
Unopened, about 6 months. Once opened, they'll stay fresh for months if you keep the bag sealed — the individual wrappers help. Store in a cool, dry place.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

YumEarth Organic Lollipops earn their rating not because they're the best-tasting lollipops on the shelf — they're not — but because they solve a real problem. For allergy-affected families, school classrooms, and anyone avoiding artificial ingredients, these are the safest, most reliable option that still tastes like a treat. The small size and stevia note keep it from a perfect score.

**Buy it if** you need allergen-free, organic candy that kids will actually eat. **Skip it if** you want big, sugary, conventional lollipops — you'll be disappointed by both the size and the sweetness. For the specific niche they serve, these are the gold standard.
