# Guittard Unsweetened Baking Chocolate, 6 oz: The Quiet Workhorse of Your Pantry

Most home bakers reach for the same mass-produced chocolate squares without a second thought. But if you’ve ever bitten into a brownie that tasted flat, or a ganache that seized into a grainy mess, the culprit isn’t your technique—it’s the chocolate. Guittard’s unsweetened bar (the 6-ounce size) is the kind of ingredient that quietly upgrades everything it touches. With a 4.6-star rating across 229 Amazon reviews, it’s clearly earning its place in serious kitchens. But is it worth swapping out your usual brand? Let’s dig in.

## What You’re Actually Getting

This is a single 6-ounce bar of 100% cacao—no sugar, no additives, no lecithin. That’s it. The bar is scored into 1-ounce squares, which makes measuring a breeze for recipes calling for one or two ounces. Guittard is a family-owned California company (est. 1868) that sources cacao from specific regions, and this unsweetened bar is made from a blend of beans that delivers a deep, fruity, almost wine-like complexity.

The packaging is straightforward: a paper wrapper with the brand’s classic red and gold design. Nothing fancy, but it protects the bar well enough for shipping. (A few reviewers noted the bar can arrive slightly melted in hot summer months, so if you live in a warm climate, consider ordering during cooler weather or buying locally.)

## Performance in the Kitchen

Here’s where this bar separates itself from the grocery-store crowd. The cacao content is high enough that it melts smoothly and evenly—no graininess, no clumping. The flavor is intensely dark, with notes of cherry and a hint of smokiness that comes through even after sugar is added. It’s not bitter in an off-putting way; it’s the kind of deep chocolate flavor that makes brownies taste like they came from a bakery.

I tested it in a classic fudge brownie recipe (the one that calls for melted chocolate and butter) and a dark chocolate ganache. The brownies had a crackly top and a fudgy center that held together beautifully. The ganache set up glossy and smooth with just a hint of that fruity acidity balancing the cream.

One thing to note: because this is *unsweetened*, you’ll need to adjust your sugar accordingly. The bar contains zero sugar, so if you’re swapping it for a semi-sweet or bittersweet chocolate in a recipe, you’ll need to add about 2–3 tablespoons of sugar per ounce to compensate. That’s not a flaw—it’s the point. But it’s worth flagging for casual bakers who grab the wrong bar without checking the label.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The Amazon reviews are remarkably consistent. One verified buyer wrote: *“I use this for keto baking and it’s the only unsweetened chocolate that doesn’t taste waxy.”* Another noted: *“Better than Ghirardelli or Baker’s—this has a much richer flavor profile.”* Several reviewers specifically mentioned using it for homemade hot chocolate and chili (yes, chili—the smoky notes work surprisingly well). The most common praise is about the meltability and lack of that stale, chalky aftertaste that plagues cheaper unsweetened bars.

## Where It Falls Short

Honestly, there’s not much to criticize here. The main complaints in the reviews are about price—it’s pricier than the 4-ounce Baker’s squares you’ll find at the grocery store—and the melting issue in transit during summer. A few reviewers also noted that the bar is scored into 1-ounce pieces, not the 2-ounce squares that some recipes call for, which means a bit of math if you’re following older recipes.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Keto and low-sugar bakers:** This is your go-to. Zero sugar, clean ingredients, and a flavor that doesn’t need sweetening to be enjoyable.
- **Serious home bakers:** If you’re making brownies, torte, or ganache more than once a month, the upgrade in flavor is noticeable.
- **Chocolate purists:** If you want to taste what cacao actually tastes like before sugar gets involved, this is a revelation.

**Skip it if:** You only bake occasionally and don’t want to store a 6-ounce bar that might get forgotten in the back of the pantry. Or if you prefer the convenience of pre-melted chocolate chips—though those almost always contain sugar and stabilizers.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this bar truly gluten-free?**
A: Yes, Guittard states it’s gluten-free, and it’s processed in a facility that handles dairy but not wheat. If you have celiac, check the packaging for the current certification, but the majority of reviewers with gluten sensitivities report no issues.

**Q: Can I use this for chocolate making (like tempering)?**
A: Yes, it works well for tempering because it has a high cocoa butter content and no added sugar to interfere with crystallization. Just be prepared for a very intense, unsweetened final product.

**Q: How does it compare to Baker’s Unsweetened?**
A: Guittard is noticeably smoother when melting and has a more complex flavor—less one-note bitter. Baker’s is cheaper and more widely available, but if you taste them side-by-side, you’ll never go back.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if:** You bake regularly, follow keto or sugar-free diets, or simply want the best-tasting unsweetened chocolate for your money. It’s a small investment that pays off in every recipe.

**Skip it if:** You’re on a tight budget, bake once a year, or prefer the convenience of pre-portioned chips. For everyone else, this bar earns its place in the pantry.

*Guittard doesn’t make the flashiest chocolate—it doesn’t need to. It just makes the right one.*
