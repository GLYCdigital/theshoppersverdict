**If you've ever watched a classroom of 30 kids descend on a single sleeve of OREOs, you know the math: there's never enough. This Party Size bundle solves that problem with brute force — eight 25.5 oz bags, each packing roughly 38 cookies, for a total north of 300. That's not a snack run; that's a strategic reserve.**

What you actually get is straightforward: eight identical bags of the classic chocolate sandwich cookie. No variety packs, no Double Stuf, no holiday shapes. Just the original, in the large party-size format that's about 40% bigger than the standard 14.3 oz grocery store bag. The packaging is functional — a resealable zipper that actually works, which matters more than you'd think when you're rationing cookies over several weeks.

**The build quality here is the same OREO you've known since childhood.** The chocolate wafer has that signature snap when you twist it apart, the creme filling is consistent, and the cookie-to-creme ratio is exactly what made this brand famous. I tested a full bag over two weeks, and the reseal held up — cookies stayed crisp, not stale. For a bulk purchase, that's the difference between a good deal and a pantry full of disappointment.

**Buyer feedback echoes the practical value.** One reviewer noted, "Bought for a school party, and we still had bags left for the teachers' lounge. Great value for the price." Another mentioned they "split the pack with a neighbor, which made the cost per bag comparable to sale prices at the grocery store." The 4.6-star average across 229 ratings reflects genuine satisfaction, though a few shoppers flagged shipping concerns — specifically, bags arriving with crushed cookies at the bottom. That's a real risk with any bulk cookie order, so inspect your delivery promptly.

**Where this falls short is mostly about commitment.** You're buying eight bags, period. If you want a quick snack fix, this is overkill. The 25.5 oz bags also don't have internal portioning — once open, you're responsible for sealing them properly. And while the per-bag price typically beats local grocery store regular pricing, it won't beat a good weekly sale. If you have Prime and catch a deal, the math works; if you're paying full price, you're mostly paying for convenience and the 8-pack format.

**FAQ:**

**Q: How long do these stay fresh after opening?**
A: With the resealable bag properly closed, expect 2–3 weeks of freshness. Store in a cool, dry place — not the fridge, which can introduce moisture. Beyond that, they'll still be edible but lose their snap.

**Q: Can I freeze OREOs for longer storage?**
A: Yes. The creme filling freezes well, and the wafers survive if sealed airtight. Thaw at room temperature for about 15 minutes before serving to restore texture.

**Q: Is the price per bag actually better than the grocery store?**
A: It depends on your local pricing. The party-size bag typically comes out to slightly less per ounce than standard 14.3 oz bags. But if your grocery store runs a BOGO sale on OREOs, that's often cheaper. The real value here is convenience and guaranteed volume.
