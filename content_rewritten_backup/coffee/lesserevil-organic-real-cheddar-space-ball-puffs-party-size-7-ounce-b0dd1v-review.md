Here's the thing about LesserEvil's Space Ball Puffs: they're not trying to be Cheetos. And that's exactly why they work.

This 7-ounce party-size bag has earned a 4.6-star average across 229 Amazon ratings, and the praise is remarkably consistent. But before you add it to your cart, understand what you're actually getting — because "organic real cheddar" isn't just a label here. It changes the snack fundamentally.

## What's Actually in the Bag

The ingredient list is refreshingly short: organic corn, organic sunflower oil, real cheddar cheese (organic milk, cheese cultures, salt, enzymes), salt, and annatto for color. That's it. No maltodextrin, no monosodium glutamate, no "cheese seasoning" that's 40% powdered cellulose.

The 7-ounce party size translates to roughly 8 cups of puffs — enough for a movie night for four or a week of desk snacking for one. As shown in the product image, the puffs are larger than typical cheese balls, closer to golf-ball-adjacent than marble-size, which gives them a satisfying heft.

## Taste and Texture: The Honest Breakdown

The cheddar comes through immediately — sharp, tangy, unmistakably real. This isn't the neon-orange dust that coats your fingers and tastes vaguely of cheese-adjacent salt. The flavor is cleaner, more pronounced, and lingers pleasantly rather than burning off after three puffs.

But the texture is where opinions split. These are crunchy — genuinely crunchy, with a snap that gives way to a dense, airy interior. They're closer in structure to a crispy corn puff than the melt-in-your-mouth Cheeto-style ball. One Amazon reviewer put it well: "These are not light and airy like normal cheese puffs. They're dense and crunchy, almost like a savory popcorn ball. Took me a minute to adjust, but now I prefer them." Another noted, "My kids call them 'grown-up cheese puffs' because they actually taste like cheese."

That density is a double-edged sword. It makes the puffs more satiating — you'll eat fewer and feel fuller — but if you're expecting that ephemeral, dissolve-on-the-tongue experience, you'll be disappointed.

## Where It Falls Short

The resealable bag is a legitimate issue. The zipper closure doesn't seal tightly, and within two or three days of opening, the puffs start taking on a stale, chewy quality. Transfer them to an airtight container if you're not finishing the bag in one sitting.

Salt content is also modest — about 170mg per serving. For reference, that's roughly 40% less than mainstream cheese puffs. If you're used to aggressively seasoned snacks, these will taste restrained. The cheddar carries the flavor, but some buyers will inevitably reach for the salt shaker.

And at the party-size price point, you're paying a premium for the organic label. You're not getting more snack per dollar than conventional brands — you're paying for cleaner ingredients and better sourcing. That's a tradeoff, not a flaw.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy them if:** You care about what's in your snack food, you prefer real cheese flavor over powdered artificial seasoning, or you're looking for a party snack that won't leave everyone's hands looking like they've been finger-painting with Cheeto dust.

**Skip them if:** You're a purist who wants light-and-airy cheese puffs, you need a resealable bag that actually works, or you're on a tight snack budget and prioritize quantity over ingredient quality.

## FAQ

**Are these actually spicy?**
No. Despite the "Space Ball" name, there's zero heat. They're straight cheddar — kid-friendly, no capsaicin.

**How do they compare to Pirate's Booty or Smartfood?**
They're crunchier and denser than Pirate's Booty, with a more pronounced cheddar tang than Smartfood's white cheddar. Closest analog would be a cheese puff crossed with a corn nut in texture.

**Is the 7-ounce bag a good value?**
If you're comparing per-ounce cost against conventional cheese puffs, no. But the real cheddar means you're getting more flavor per puff, so you'll eat less to feel satisfied. Most buyers report the bag lasting longer than expected.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want an organic snack that tastes like actual cheese and doesn't leave you feeling like you've eaten a chemistry experiment. **Skip it if** you're attached to the classic light-and-crunchy cheese puff texture or need a bag that truly reseals.

LesserEvil has made a genuinely better cheese puff — denser, more flavorful, and cleaner than anything in the conventional aisle. The storage issue and modest salt are minor quibbles against a snack that delivers what it promises. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, the consensus is clear: this is a party bag worth bringing.
