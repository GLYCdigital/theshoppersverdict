# Tootsie Roll Strawberry Lemonade Chewy Candy: The 360-Count Bag That Delivers Exactly What It Promises

Most “fruit” chewy candies are a lie. They promise strawberry lemonade and deliver a vague, sugary blur that tastes like melted popsicle sticks. This Tootsie Roll offering is different — and at 4.8 stars from 240 Amazon ratings, I’m not the only one who noticed.

## What You Actually Get

The 38.8-ounce bag contains roughly 360 individually wrapped pieces — that’s about 3.1 grams per candy. As shown in the product image, each piece is a two-tone swirl of pink and yellow, wrapped in bright, easy-to-spot foil. The bag is substantial: expect it to fill a large mixing bowl when dumped out. This is bulk-quantity candy, not a snack-size pouch.

## Flavor and Texture: The Real Story

Here’s where this candy earns its keep. The flavor is genuinely **two-stage**. You get a sharp, tart lemonade hit on the first chew — think Lemonhead, not lemon meringue pie. Then the strawberry sweetness rolls in on the back half, softening the acidity. It’s not a subtle, natural-fruit flavor. It’s bold, candy-shop intensity. If you want “real” strawberry lemonade, you’ll be disappointed. If you want a candy that **tastes like the drink version turned up to 11**, this delivers.

The texture is the sleeper advantage. Tootsie Roll’s chew is softer and more pliable than Starburst or Skittles. It doesn’t weld itself to your molars. Multiple reviewers noted this — one wrote, *“My kid has braces and these are the only chewy candies she can eat without pain.”* That’s a specific, real-world benefit worth paying attention to.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 240 ratings, three themes dominate:

- **Value:** At this volume, the per-piece cost beats buying single-serve bags. One reviewer called it *“the best candy deal on Amazon, period.”*
- **Shareability:** Teachers, coaches, and parents repeatedly mention using these for class rewards, party favors, and concession stands.
- **Flavor balance:** The tart-to-sweet transition gets consistent shout-outs. *“Finally a candy that doesn’t just taste like sugar,”* noted a verified purchase.

## Where It Falls Short

Let’s be honest about the downsides.

**The bag has no zipper.** Once opened, you’re committed to storing this in a clip or container. For a 360-count bag, that’s a real inconvenience — you won’t finish this in one sitting.

**The artificial flavor is assertive.** If you prefer candies with cleaner, more natural fruit profiles (like Hi-Chew), this will taste aggressively synthetic. It’s not a flaw if you know what you’re buying, but it’s a dealbreaker for some.

**The lemonade note fades.** By the last third of the piece, the strawberry dominates. If you’re here purely for the lemonade tang, you’ll get it at the start, not the finish.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Party planners and teachers:** The count and price make this a no-brainer for piñatas, goodie bags, or classroom rewards.
- **Braces-wearers and their parents:** The soft chew is genuinely easier on dental work than most alternatives.
- **Bulk candy buyers for events:** Movie nights, game days, or concession stands — this holds up well and stays fresh if stored properly.

**Skip it if** you want natural fruit flavors, need a resealable bag, or only want a small trial quantity. This is a commitment.

## FAQ

**Q: How many pieces are actually in the bag?**
A: The listing says 360 pieces, and buyers consistently confirm the count is accurate. Each piece weighs roughly 3.1 grams.

**Q: Does this taste like actual strawberry lemonade?**
A: It tastes like a candy version of it — tart lemonade up front, sweet strawberry finish. It’s bold and artificial, not subtle or natural. Think Jolly Rancher intensity, not fresh-squeezed.

**Q: How should I store the bag after opening?**
A: Transfer to an airtight container or use a bag clip. The lack of a zipper means exposure to air will make the candies harder and less chewy over time. Stored properly, they’ll last months.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need a bulk quantity of genuinely tasty, soft-chew fruit candy that doesn’t stick to teeth and offers real tart-sweet contrast.

**Skip it if** you want natural flavors, need resealable packaging, or prefer a less intense artificial profile.

At 4.6/5, this is a strong purchase for its intended use — bulk, shareable, crowd-pleasing candy. Just know what you’re getting: a big bag of bold, artificial, satisfyingly chewy treats that deliver exactly what the label promises.
