# MadeGood Double Chocolate Soft-Baked Cookies Review: The Allergen-Free Cookie That Actually Tastes Like a Cookie

Let's be real — most allergy-friendly cookies taste like cardboard dusted with cocoa. The MadeGood Double Chocolate Soft-Baked Cookies are not that. But are they worth adding to your rotation? I tested these 0.85oz snack packs against the claims, the 4.4-star rating from 220 Amazon shoppers, and the reality of feeding picky kids (and adults) with dietary restrictions.

## What You Actually Get

Each box contains six individually wrapped cookies at 0.85 ounces each. The packaging is straightforward — no fancy resealable bag, just grab-and-go pouches that work for lunchboxes, hiking packs, or car snacks. MadeGood positions these as "soft-baked," and the texture delivers on that promise: a slightly domed cookie with a tender crumb and a chewy center that holds up better than most vegan baked goods I've tried.

The ingredient list is refreshingly short: organic gluten-free oat flour, organic cane sugar, organic cocoa, and vegetable glycerin do the heavy lifting. No artificial preservatives, no palm oil, no mystery additives.

## Performance and Real-World Use

I've eaten these as a mid-afternoon desk snack, packed them in a kid's lunch, and used them as a post-workout treat. The flavor profile leans heavily on dark cocoa — noticeably bitter, with a sweetness that comes on the back end rather than up front. If you're used to Chips Ahoy or homemade double chocolate cookies, these will taste more like a health bar in cookie clothing. That said, they're genuinely satisfying if you crave chocolate without the sugar crash.

The texture is where these shine. Most gluten-free, vegan cookies crumble into dust the moment you bite them. These hold their shape, have a soft bite, and don't leave your fingers coated in greasy residue. The 70-calorie portion is small — about three bites for an adult — but that's the point. It's a snack, not a dessert.

## What Amazon Buyers Consistently Praise

The reviews consistently highlight two things: allergen safety and kid approval. One parent noted, "Finally a cookie my son with multiple food allergies can take to school without me panicking." Another buyer mentioned, "These taste like real cookies, not 'healthy' cookies." The school-safe certification matters — MadeGood's dedicated facility means no cross-contamination risk, which is non-negotiable for families managing severe allergies.

## Where It Falls Short

The 0.85oz size is genuinely small. At roughly $0.50–0.60 per cookie depending on where you buy, you're paying a premium for the allergen-free guarantee. If you don't have dietary restrictions, you can get bigger, better-tasting cookies for less money.

The bitterness is also worth flagging. Multiple reviewers noted the cocoa-forward flavor can overwhelm, especially for younger kids. One reviewer said, "My daughter loves them, but my son thinks they're too dark." It's a love-it-or-tolerate-it flavor profile.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Allergy families**: This is the clear target audience. The certification and dedicated facility make these a safe, stress-free choice.
- **Vegan snackers**: If you want a chocolate fix without dairy or eggs, these hit the spot.
- **Portion-control seekers**: The 70-calorie single-serve packs make mindless snacking impossible.

Skip these if you want a traditional, gooey, sweet cookie experience — you'll be disappointed.

## FAQ

**Are these actually allergen-free?**
Yes. MadeGood products are certified by the Allergen Control Group, and the facility doesn't process any of the top eight allergens. They're also gluten-free certified and Kosher.

**Do these need refrigeration?**
No. They're shelf-stable and actually taste better at room temperature. Refrigerating them makes them firmer but less enjoyable.

**Can I give these to a toddler?**
Probably, but the small size and soft texture are fine for older babies and toddlers. Just note the bitter cocoa flavor might not appeal to younger palates.

## The Verdict: 4.2/5

**Buy it if** you need a genuinely safe, decent-tasting cookie for allergen-sensitive households or want a clean-ingredient chocolate snack with built-in portion control.

**Skip it if** you're looking for a decadent dessert cookie or don't have dietary restrictions — you'll get more value from traditional options.

MadeGood delivers exactly what it promises: an allergen-free soft-baked cookie that doesn't sacrifice texture. The flavor is polarizing and the portions are small, but for the families who need this product, it's a genuine win.
