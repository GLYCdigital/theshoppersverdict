## What You Actually Get

The bottle is exactly what it claims: 64 fluid ounces of 100% Montmorency tart cherry juice. No added sugar, no water, no preservatives, no "from concentrate" games. The ingredient list is literally one item: tart cherries. As shown in the product image, it arrives in a sturdy glass bottle — which is heavier and more fragile than plastic, but it also means no plastic leaching concerns.

Each bottle pours about 8 servings (8 oz each), which puts it in a reasonable price range compared to other premium tart cherry juices. You're paying for the purity here, not the packaging.

## Taste and Real-World Performance

Here's the honest truth: this juice is *tart*. Not "oh, that's a bit tangy" tart — proper, lip-puckering, Montmorency tart. If you've only had sweet cherry juice from the grocery store, prepare yourself. That said, it's not harsh or bitter; it's clean and bright, with a genuine cherry flavor that actually tastes like the fruit, not candy.

For the sleep and recovery crowd — the main reason people buy tart cherry juice — the science is solid. Tart cherries are one of the few foods with naturally occurring melatonin, plus anti-inflammatory compounds. Multiple Amazon reviewers mention drinking it an hour before bed and noticing a difference. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I've tried several brands and this one is the least processed. I sleep deeper and my joint pain is noticeably reduced after about a week."*

The consistency is thin and watery — which is correct for 100% juice, not a syrupy concentrate. It mixes well into smoothies, works as a base for overnight oats, and makes a surprisingly good cocktail mixer if you're into that.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides:

**Shelf life is short.** Once opened, you have about 2-3 weeks to finish it. That's not a lot for a 64 oz bottle if you're drinking the recommended 2 oz daily for maintenance. You need to commit to roughly 4-8 oz per day to finish it in time, or freeze portions in ice cube trays.

**The tartness is genuinely polarizing.** If you're not used to unsweetened tart cherry juice, this will be a shock. Some reviewers found it too strong to drink straight and had to dilute it. That's not a defect — it's the product being honest — but know what you're getting.

**Glass bottle shipping.** It arrives well-packaged, but glass is glass. A few reviewers reported breakage in transit, though Cherry Bay's customer service appears responsive based on the reviews.
