Let me be straight with you: this isn't the trail mix you grab for a sugar rush. Second Nature's Wholesome Medley is the sensible, adult version — and that's exactly why it works.

**What's in the Box**

You get 10 individually sealed 1-ounce pouches (15 ounces total). Each pouch is roughly a quarter-cup serving — the actual recommended portion, not the "I'll just have a handful" portion that turns into half the bag. The mix includes almonds, walnuts, pepitas, sunflower seeds, dried cranberries, and raisins. No peanuts, which matters if you're shopping for nut-allergy-sensitive environments (though the facility does process tree nuts).

**Real-World Performance**

The ingredient list is refreshingly short: nuts, seeds, dried fruit. That's it. No added sugar, no palm oil, no "natural flavors" that are anything but. Each serving delivers 4 grams of protein and a solid dose of heart-healthy fats — enough to keep you satisfied between meals without the blood sugar spike that comes from candy-laden mixes.

The portion control is the real selling point here. One pouch is genuinely enough to kill a snack craving. As one Amazon reviewer noted, "I love that I can grab one and not have to think about how much I'm eating." Another long-time customer said, "These are my go-to for hiking and lunchboxes — the packaging survives being tossed around."

**Where It Falls Short**

The cost-per-ounce math isn't in your favor. You're paying for the convenience of pre-portioned packaging, and if you're someone who goes through a pouch daily, you'll be reordering more often than you'd like. Buyers have also mentioned that the cranberries can stick together in humid environments — a minor annoyance, not a quality issue.

And if you're used to trail mixes loaded with chocolate chips or yogurt-covered pretzels, this will taste almost savory by comparison. The sweetness comes only from the dried fruit, which is subtle.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Office workers** who need a desk-drawer snack that won't lead to a 3 PM sugar crash
- **Parents packing school lunches** who want something nut-free-ish (check with the school) and portion-controlled
- **Hikers and travelers** who want durable, mess-free snacks that won't melt or crush
- **Anyone on a weight management plan** who struggles with portion control on snack foods

**Should You Skip It?**

- If you want a sweet, indulgent trail mix with chocolate and candy pieces
- If you're feeding a family and need bulk value — a standard 2-pound bag will be more economical
- If you have a peanut allergy and need a completely nut-free facility

**Three Questions Shoppers Ask**

**Are these good for kids' lunches?** Yes, generally. The single-serve pouches are perfect for lunchboxes, and the lack of added sugar is a win. But check your school's nut policy first — while there are no peanuts, it does contain tree nuts.

**How long do they stay fresh?** The sealed pouches have a solid shelf life, roughly 6-8 months from production. Once opened, eat within a day — though honestly, one pouch rarely survives that long.

**Is this keto-friendly?** It's low-carb and fits most keto macros at around 7g net carbs per serving, thanks to the nuts and seeds. The dried fruit adds some carbs, but it's manageable for most.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a clean, portion-controlled snack that actually tastes like real food and keeps you full. **Skip it if** you're looking for a candy-adjacent treat or need maximum value per dollar.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The price per ounce keeps it from being perfect, but for what it does — honest ingredients, genuine portion control, and a satisfying crunch — it's one of the better trail mixes on Amazon. If you're tired of lying to yourself about "just one handful," this solves that problem.
