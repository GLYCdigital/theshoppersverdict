# Rishi Tea Lavender Mint Herbal Tea Review: A Calming Cup Worth Every Penny

Let's be honest—most herbal teas taste like hot grass water. Rishi Tea's Lavender Mint blend is not that. This is the tea that converts skeptics, the one you brew when you need to exhale after a brutal day, and the box you'll find yourself reordering before the last bag is gone.

## What You Actually Get

Rishi sends you 16 individually wrapped tea bags in a resealable foil pouch. Each bag holds 2.5 grams of whole-leaf tea—not the dusty fannings you'll find in grocery store bargain boxes. The blend features real French lavender buds, organic peppermint, and a touch of lemon verbena. The dry leaf aroma alone is worth the purchase: fragrant, floral, and unmistakably fresh.

## Real-World Performance

Brew this at 212°F (boiling water) and steep for 5-7 minutes. The lavender comes through first—sweet, slightly citrusy, not at all soapy. The mint follows with a clean, cooling finish that lingers pleasantly. Here's the thing that surprised me: the flavor holds up even if you forget your cup for 15 minutes. Unlike green or black teas that turn bitter when over-steeped, this blend gets *deeper* and more soothing. That's a practical win for anyone who brews and then gets distracted by email.

The tea bags themselves are unbleached, compostable, and made with a crimped seal—no staples, no plastic strings, no glue. If you're someone who cares about what goes into your compost bin, this matters.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has tradeoffs worth knowing:

- **The price point is steep.** You're paying roughly $0.80 per bag. That's triple what you'd pay for a drugstore mint tea. You're paying for whole-leaf quality—but if you're on a tight budget, this is a splurge.
- **The lavender flavor is assertive.** If you're looking for a predominantly minty tea with a whisper of lavender, this isn't it. The floral notes lead. Some reviewers noted they wanted more mint presence.
- **Caffeine-free but not for everyone.** Pregnant women and those on certain medications should check with their doctor about lavender consumption. It's worth flagging.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Ask

**Q: Is this actually caffeine-free?**
Yes. Peppermint, lavender, and lemon verbena are all naturally caffeine-free. Safe for evening consumption.

**Q: Can I cold-brew this?**
Absolutely. Drop one bag in a pitcher of cold water, refrigerate for 4-6 hours. You'll get a refreshing, lightly sweet iced tea with no bitterness.

**Q: How many steeps do you get per bag?**
One, maybe two if you're stretching it. The second steep will be noticeably weaker on the lavender but still pleasant.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a genuinely relaxing, high-quality herbal tea that tastes like a spa day in a mug, and you're willing to pay for real ingredients.

**Skip it if:** You're looking for a bargain-priced mint tea, or you prefer mint-forward blends where lavender plays a supporting role.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Rishi has crafted something exceptional here. The price stings a little, but the quality, sourcing, and consistency justify it. This is the tea I now keep in my desk drawer for 3 PM slumps and 11 PM wind-downs.
