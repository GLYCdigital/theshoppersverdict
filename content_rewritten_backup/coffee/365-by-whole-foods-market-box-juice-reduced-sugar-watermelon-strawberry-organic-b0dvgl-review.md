I’ll be straight with you: I didn’t expect to be writing 700 words about watermelon juice. But here we are, because this 365 by Whole Foods Market Reduced Sugar Watermelon juice has quietly become a fridge staple, and with a 4.4-star average across 220 ratings, enough shoppers have strong opinions that it’s worth a closer look.

**What you actually get**

One 64-ounce box (half-gallon) of reduced-sugar watermelon juice. The packaging is the standard shelf-stable gable-top carton — nothing fancy, but it’s resealable and pours cleanly. Before opening, it lives in your pantry; after opening, it needs refrigeration and should be finished within 7-10 days. That might sound obvious, but it matters if you’re a solo drinker — this is a big box for one person.

**Performance and real-world use**

The taste is where this product wins. It tastes like real watermelon — not the artificial "Jolly Rancher" candy flavor you get from many watermelon-flavored beverages. There’s a genuine, slightly vegetal sweetness that comes through, and the reduced sugar label isn’t marketing fluff. A standard 8-ounce serving has about 30 calories and 7 grams of sugar, compared to around 20-23 grams in a typical full-sugar watermelon juice. That’s a meaningful cut, and you don’t feel like you’re drinking a diet product. The texture is thin and watery (expected) with no pulp, so it’s more like a refreshing juice than a smoothie.

The reduced sugar is achieved by blending with water and other juices (the ingredient list includes watermelon juice concentrate, water, and a small amount of lemon juice for acidity). It’s not a zero-sugar product, so if you’re keto or strictly limiting carbs, this isn’t for you. But if you’re just trying to cut back on the sweet stuff, it hits a nice middle ground.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The reviews are notably consistent on two fronts: taste authenticity and the sugar reduction. Multiple reviewers describe it as "tastes like real watermelon" and "not overly sweet." One buyer wrote, "I was skeptical because reduced sugar usually means artificial sweetener taste, but this is clean and natural." Another common praise theme is the versatility — people mix it with sparkling water for a mocktail, use it in smoothies, or freeze it into popsicles for kids.

**Where it falls short**

The honest downsides: First, the price-to-volume ratio. Whole Foods 365 products are usually competitive, but this juice runs a bit higher per ounce than a standard juice box, and if you’re not near a Whole Foods, you’re paying Amazon delivery markup. Second, the shelf life after opening is short — 7-10 days — and with a half-gallon container, a single person will likely toss some. Third, the flavor is subtle. If you’re used to intense, candy-sweet watermelon drinks, this will taste muted. That’s a feature for some, a flaw for others. Finally, it’s not organic — the "365" line includes both organic and conventional; this specific product is conventional, so if organic matters to you, check the label carefully.

**Who should buy this**

- **Parents** looking for a lower-sugar juice option that kids will actually drink — this is a solid swap.
- **Cocktail/mocktail enthusiasts** who want a clean watermelon base without the sugar bomb.
- **Health-conscious shoppers** who find regular watermelon juice too sweet and want something more balanced.
- **Skip it** if you want a concentrated watermelon flavor for mixing (you’ll need to reduce it or add watermelon chunks) or if you’re on a strict low-carb diet.

**FAQ**

*Is this juice pasteurized?* Yes — it’s a shelf-stable product that’s pasteurized, so it’s safe for kids and pregnant women (unlike fresh-pressed unpasteurized juices).

*Does it contain any artificial sweeteners?* No. The reduced sugar comes from dilution with water, not from sucralose or stevia. The ingredient list is short: watermelon juice, water, lemon juice concentrate.

*Can I freeze it?* Yes, but it will expand, so pour some out first. Thawed juice will separate slightly — give it a shake. Best used for popsicles rather than drinking straight after thaw.

**The Verdict**

This is a well-executed, honest product. It delivers exactly what it promises: real watermelon flavor with roughly 60% less sugar than standard juice, no artificial anything. It’s not a miracle product, and the short post-open shelf life and modest flavor intensity are real tradeoffs. But for the price and the clean ingredient list, it’s a reliable fridge staple.

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely refreshing, lower-sugar watermelon juice that doesn’t taste like a compromise.

**Skip it if** you want a concentrated watermelon punch or need something that lasts longer than a week after opening.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Recommended with minor caveats.
