## What You're Actually Getting

The product image shows a resealable bag packed with whole cashews — and that's accurate. These are labeled "Whole Fancy" grade, which in the nut world means at least 90% whole kernels. In practice, I found maybe 10-15% broken pieces at the bottom of the bag, which is well within spec for this grade.

You get a choice between sea salt and unsalted versions. The sea salt version uses a light hand — flakes are visible on the nuts but not caked on. The roast is even, with a medium-brown color that suggests proper dry roasting rather than oil roasting.

## Performance and Real-World Use

Texture is where these shine. They're genuinely crunchy — not stale-soft like so many grocery store brands. The dry roasting process gives them a toasty, almost buttery depth without added oil. The sea salt provides a clean, salty finish that doesn't linger or overpower the nut's natural sweetness.

For coffee pairing specifically, these hold up well. The salt cuts through dark roast bitterness nicely, and the crunch provides satisfying contrast to a smooth latte. I also tested them in a trail mix and sprinkled over yogurt — both solid applications.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Looking through the 210 ratings, a few themes emerge. One reviewer noted, "These are the freshest cashews I've had from Amazon — no rancid aftertaste like other brands." Another echoed the texture point: "Finally, cashews that are actually crunchy, not chewy." The resealable bag also gets consistent mention for keeping them fresh over multiple weeks.

## Where It Falls Short

Being honest: this isn't a budget option. Per-ounce pricing runs higher than bulk grocery store cashews. The unsalted version is genuinely unsalted — if you're expecting even a hint of seasoning, go with the sea salt. Also, while the bag is resealable, the zipper isn't particularly robust; after a few weeks of daily opening, it started to lose its seal. Transfer to an airtight container if you're not going through these quickly.

## FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: How long do they stay fresh after opening?**
A: About 3-4 weeks in the resealable bag if stored in a cool, dry place. For longer storage, refrigerate — they'll keep for 2-3 months without losing crunch.

**Q: Are they truly dry roasted, not oil roasted?**
A: Yes. The ingredient list shows just cashews and sea salt. No oils, no additives. That's why the texture is crisp rather than greasy.

**Q: How much broken pieces should I expect?**
A: Around 10-15% by weight. "Whole Fancy" doesn't mean 100% intact — it allows for some breakage during shipping. The pieces are still perfectly good for cooking or snacking.
