**What You Actually Get**

The package is a 12-ounce plastic canister with a resealable lid. Inside, you get dry-roasted peanuts. That's it. No gimmicks, no flavors, no seasoning packets. The peanuts are uniform in size, which tells you they've been sorted properly, and the roast is even across the batch — no pale nuts or scorched ones.

**The Real-World Test**

I've gone through two canisters now, one for desk snacking and one for baking. The first thing you notice is the crunch. These peanuts snap cleanly when you bite them, not the soft, stale chew you get from some store-brand bags. The roast is medium-dark, giving them a toasty depth without any burnt bitterness.

The salt level is worth discussing. These are "lightly salted" — if you're used to ballpark peanuts or heavily seasoned brands, you might find these surprisingly mild. The salt is there, but it's measured. It lets the peanut flavor come through rather than masking it. For baking, that's ideal. For snacking straight from the canister, some buyers may want more punch.

**What Buyers Keep Saying**

The 235 ratings at 4.7 stars break down in a telling way. The praise clusters around freshness and reliability. **Where It Falls Short**

More importantly, the "lightly salted" designation means you should read the label before buying if you have a strong salt preference.

**FAQ**

**Are these peanuts good for baking?**
Yes. The even roast and low salt make them versatile for sweet recipes. Just chop them or grind them as needed — they hold up well to heat without turning bitter.

**How long do they stay fresh after opening?**
With the lid properly sealed, they'll stay crunchy for 3-4 weeks. After that, they start to soften. If you're a slow snacker, portion them into an airtight container.
