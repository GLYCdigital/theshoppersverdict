Here’s the thing about snacking: most people default to chips or pretzels, and they end up hungry twenty minutes later. Planters Heat Peanuts are the opposite. These aren’t just a salty distraction — they’re a protein-packed, spicy little punch that actually satisfies. And in the 1.75 oz packet format, you get portion control without the commitment of a giant jar going stale in your pantry.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains 18 individual 1.75-ounce packets. That’s roughly 49.5 grams of peanuts per pack, which is a decent single serving — enough to curb hunger without feeling like you’re grazing. The packets are resealable? No, they aren’t. That’s fine because the size is designed for one sitting. If you’re someone who nibbles over hours, you’ll want to portion these out or commit to finishing the bag.

**The Heat Factor: Real Spice, Not Gimmick**

The name says "Heat" and these deliver. This isn’t a mild barbecue dusting. The peanuts are coated in a spicy seasoning that hits you upfront with a cayenne-forward burn that lingers on the lips. It’s not ghost-pepper absurd, but it’s definitely above the average "spicy snack" threshold. If you’re someone who puts hot sauce on everything, you’ll appreciate the kick. If you’re sensitive to spice, these might be too much.

The texture holds up well. The coating is dry and adheres properly — no greasy fingers or clumpy residue. The peanuts themselves are crunchy and fresh, not stale or rubbery. That’s a common issue with pre-packaged nuts, and Planters avoids it.

**What Buyers Keep Saying**

The 4.7-star average across 235 ratings is earned. Here’s what keeps coming up in verified reviews:

- **"Perfect snack for work"** — the individual packets make portion control effortless for desk drawers or lunch bags.
- **"Actually spicy, not like other brands"** — multiple reviewers note this is one of the few mass-market spicy nuts that doesn’t wimp out on heat.

**Where It Falls Short**

The biggest downside? The heat can be inconsistent between batches. Some packets hit hard; others taste noticeably milder. It’s not a dealbreaker, but if you’re buying these specifically for a consistent burn, you might get occasional surprises.

Also, the 1.75 oz size is small. If you’re expecting a big handful to munch on during a road trip, you’ll be through the bag in about five minutes. These are more of a quick protein hit than a long-session snack.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The office snacker**: Keep a few packets in your drawer. They won’t go stale, and the heat is a nice break from sweet granola bars.
- **The keto/low-carb eater**: Peanuts are a solid fat and protein source, and these have zero added sugar.
- **The heat seeker**: If your baseline is jalapeño chips, these will probably feel mild. If you enjoy habanero-level spice, these are right in your lane.

Skip these if you prefer sweet or honey-roasted nuts, or if you need a snack that delivers a large volume for grazing.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Are these gluten-free?**
A: Yes, peanuts themselves are gluten-free, and the seasoning doesn’t contain wheat-based ingredients. However, if you have severe celiac, always check the label since processing facilities can vary.

**Q: How long do they stay fresh after opening?**
A: These packets are single-serving, so you should finish them in one sitting. If you don’t, they’ll stay crispy for about a day in a sealed container, but the seasoning can absorb moisture and soften over time.

**Q: Are these truly spicy or just mildly seasoned?**
A: They’re genuinely spicy. The cayenne kick is noticeable from the first bite and builds as you eat. It’s not painful, but it’s a legitimate burn — not a marketing trick.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a portable, high-protein snack with real heat and consistent quality. The packet format is perfect for portion control, and the value is solid for 18 servings.

**Skip it if** you prefer sweet nuts, need a bigger bag to graze on, or have a low spice tolerance.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It loses half a star for batch-to-batch heat inconsistency and the small packet size, but for what it is, this is a top-tier spicy snack option.
