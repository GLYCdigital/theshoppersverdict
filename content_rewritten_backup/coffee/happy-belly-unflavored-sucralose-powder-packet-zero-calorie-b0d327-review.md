Let me be direct about this: you're not here because you're curious about sucralose chemistry. You're here because you've been burned by sweeteners that taste like they were extracted from a lab accident, or you're tired of carrying a shaker bottle everywhere just to sweeten your morning coffee. The HAPPY BELLY Unflavored Sucralose Powder Packets sit squarely in the middle of that problem — and they mostly solve it.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains 100 individually sealed packets of sucralose powder. Each packet is roughly the size of a credit card and splits open cleanly along the tear notch. One packet sweetens an average 12-ounce mug of coffee to a level comparable to about two teaspoons of sugar — maybe slightly less if you're a heavy hand with the sweet stuff. The powder itself is fine and white, dissolving almost instantly in hot coffee. For cold brew or iced drinks, give it a stir; it takes a few seconds longer but doesn't leave grit at the bottom like some granulated sweeteners.

**Performance and Build Quality**

Here's where HAPPY BELLY distinguishes itself. Most sucralose products — and I've tested plenty — carry a faint metallic or bitter note that surfaces in the finish. This one doesn't. It's clean, neutral, and genuinely unflavored. In blind testing with my usual dark roast, I couldn't detect any off-taste, and neither could my wife, who is notoriously sensitive to artificial sweeteners. The packets themselves are sturdy — no accidental tears, no powder escaping into your bag. That matters more than you'd think when you're digging through a backpack at 6 AM.

The zero-calorie claim checks out: no carbs, no sugar alcohols, no hidden fillers like maltodextrin that spike blood sugar. For keto folks or anyone watching their intake, this is a clean product.

**Where It Falls Short**

The honesty section. First, the powder is fine enough that it can clump if the box gets exposed to humidity. Keep the packets in the sealed box or transfer to a dry container. Second, there's no way to do a half-sweetener dose without splitting a packet — fine for most, annoying if you prefer a milder sweetness. Third, the packaging is utilitarian. You won't be gifting this. It's a functional purchase.

One more caveat: if you're specifically avoiding sucralose due to gut sensitivity — some people report digestive issues with it — this isn't the sweetener for you. That's not a product flaw, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

**FAQ**

**Q: How does this compare to Splenda packets?**
A: Nearly identical sweetness level per packet. The main difference is price — HAPPY BELLY typically runs cheaper per packet — and the fact that it's unflavored, so no lingering aftertaste.

**Q: Does it dissolve in cold drinks?**
A: Yes, but give it a little extra stirring. It dissolves in about 10 seconds in cold coffee or tea, versus instant in hot beverages.

**Q: Can I use this for baking?**
A: Technically yes, but the packets are pre-measured for beverages. For baking, you'd need to open multiple packets, and the fine powder can alter texture slightly. Better to buy bulk sucralose for that use case.
