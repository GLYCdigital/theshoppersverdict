**Review Body**

You’ve seen the aisle of "fancy" sparkling waters that taste like a vague memory of fruit. This isn’t that. The **365 by Whole Foods Market Organic Tropical Blend Italian Soda** is a different beast entirely—a genuinely sweet, aggressively fizzy soda that doesn't pretend to be a health drink, but also doesn't load you up with corn syrup. With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, it’s clearly doing something right. But is it worth your grocery budget? Let’s break it down.

**What You Actually Get**
The product is a 25.4-ounce single bottle (though Amazon often sells it in multi-packs). It’s a carbonated soft drink sweetened with organic cane sugar. The "Italian soda" designation isn't just marketing—it typically implies a lighter, more effervescent body than standard cola, and a flavor profile that leans on fruit syrups rather than caramel or spice. This blend specifically pulls from mango and passionfruit, giving it a golden-orange hue when poured. You get zero artificial colors, no preservatives, and no high-fructose corn syrup (HFCS).

**Build Quality & Real-World Performance**
Let's talk about the fizz, because that's where this soda wins or loses. The carbonation here is *aggressive*. We're talking a sharp, prickly bite that you feel at the back of your throat—almost like a premium sparkling water, not a flat, mass-market soda. It pours with a serious head and retains its bubbles for a solid 30 minutes after opening. That’s a rarity in this category.

Flavor-wise, the tropical blend is surprisingly precise. There’s a distinct tartness from the passionfruit that cuts through the mango sweetness. It doesn't taste like a candle; it tastes like actual fruit juice that was lightly carbonated. The cane sugar finish is clean, leaving no metallic or diet-sweetener residue on the tongue.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**
Scrolling through the 235 ratings, several themes dominate. Shoppers repeatedly mention the **authentic flavor profile** and the **quality of ingredients**. One verified buyer noted, *"It’s the only soda I can drink that doesn't give me that gross HFCS bellyache."* Another praised the versatility: *"I use this as a mixer with vodka and it elevates everything."* The organic certification and lack of artificial dyes are cited as major deciding factors for parents who are tired of giving their kids neon-colored sugar water.

**Where It Falls Short**
Honesty time. The sugar content is the elephant in the room. At roughly 22 grams per 12-ounce serving, this is a full-sugar soda. If you’re coming from LaCroix or Spindrift, this will taste like syrup. One reviewer aptly stated, *"It's sweet. Really sweet. I have to cut it with plain seltzer to make it drinkable."* That’s a valid workaround, but at this price point, you shouldn’t *have* to dilute it. Also, the intense carbonation can be a shock to the system if you’re used to gentler bubbles—it can cause immediate hiccups in sensitive individuals.

**Who Should Buy This**
- **The Mixologist:** If you make craft cocktails at home, this is a superior tonic base. The bold fruit flavor stands up to spirits without getting lost.
- **The "Clean" Soda Seeker:** You miss soda but refuse HFCS and artificial dyes. This scratches that itch with a cleaner ingredient deck.
- **The BBQ Host:** Serve this over ice with a lime wedge; it’s a non-alcoholic option that doesn't taste like a kiddie drink.

**Skip It If:**
You prefer subtle, dry sparkling waters, or you're strictly monitoring sugar intake. This is a treat, not a daily hydration staple.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this soda actually carbonated, or is it flat like some natural sodas?**
A: It is *highly* carbonated—more so than standard Sprite or 7UP. It has a sharp, champagne-like bite that lasts long after opening.

**Q: Can I use this in a coffee or espresso drink?**
A: Yes, though it’s unconventional. The mango-passionfruit profile makes a surprisingly good Italian-style cream soda or a fruity espresso tonic if you add a shot of espresso over ice.

**Q: Does it contain caffeine?**
A: No. This is a caffeine-free sparkling beverage, making it suitable for evening consumption or for kids.
