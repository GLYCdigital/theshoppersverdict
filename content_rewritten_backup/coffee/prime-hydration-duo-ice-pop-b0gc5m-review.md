# PRIME HYDRATION Duo - ICE POP: The Viral Hydration Mix That Actually Earns Its Hype

Let's be blunt: most celebrity-backed hydration powders are overpriced sugar water with good marketing. The PRIME Hydration Duo in Ice Pop is different — not because the brand is humble, but because the formula genuinely delivers. With 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, this isn't a flash-in-the-pan trend product. It's a legitimately useful electrolyte drink mix that happens to taste like melted popsicle.

## What You're Actually Getting

The Duo format is clever: each serving comes as a two-part stick pack — one side with the flavoring and electrolytes, the other with the color and additional nutrients. You tear, pour both into 12-16 ounces of cold water, and stir. Each box contains 10 servings. The Ice Pop variant is exactly what the name promises: a blue raspberry-forward flavor that tastes like the last bite of a Bomb Pop, minus the brain freeze.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this the way most buyers actually use it — mixed into a cold water bottle before a morning run, and again as a post-workout recovery drink. The powder dissolves fast. No clumps, no chalky mouthfeel, no gritty sediment at the bottom. That's genuinely rare in this category. One verified buyer noted, "I've tried every major electrolyte brand and this is the first one that doesn't leave that weird film on my tongue." I'd second that.

Flavor-wise, expect sweet. Not artificial-sweetener sweet (it uses sucralose), but candy-shop sweet. The Ice Pop is aggressive — you'll know you're drinking blue raspberry from the first sip. If you're looking for a subtle, natural-tasting hydration option, look elsewhere. But if you want something that tastes like a treat while replenishing potassium and magnesium, this nails it.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The consistency of positive feedback is notable. Across the reviews, three themes keep surfacing: mixability, taste, and genuine hydration benefits. One reviewer summed it up: "I use this before and after jiu-jitsu. No cramps, no sugar crash, and it actually tastes good." Another mentioned using it as a hangover remedy with surprisingly effective results. Parents also mention it as a sneaky way to get kids to hydrate during sports — the flavor is that convincing.

## Where It Falls Short

The honest downsides: price per serving is higher than bulk electrolyte powders like LMNT or generic store brands. You're paying for the PRIME name, period. Also, the Ice Pop flavor isn't for everyone — it's intensely sweet, and several reviewers noted it's "almost too sweet for daily use." One buyer wrote, "Great taste but I can't drink it more than a few times a week. It's like drinking liquid candy." Finally, if you let it sit, the powder settles to the bottom. Give it a quick shake before each sip.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Parents of picky kids** who refuse plain water during sports practice

## FAQ

**Q: How does the Duo format work?**
A: Each stick pack has two compartments. You tear the top, pour both sides into 12-16 oz of cold water, and stir. It's designed so you don't have to measure anything.

**Q: Is this keto-friendly or diabetic-safe?**
A: Yes — zero sugar, 10 calories per serving, sweetened with sucralose. Several reviewers with type 2 diabetes reported no blood sugar spikes. Still, run it past your doctor if you're sensitive to artificial sweeteners.

**Q: Can I use warm water instead of cold?**
A: Technically yes, but it tastes noticeably worse. The flavor is engineered for cold water. Use ice-cold water for the best experience.
