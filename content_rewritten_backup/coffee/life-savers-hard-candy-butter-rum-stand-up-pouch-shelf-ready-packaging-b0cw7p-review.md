**What's Actually in the Bag**

This isn't the flimsy pillow bag you remember from checkout lanes. The stand-up pouch is a rigid, gusseted design that holds roughly a pound of candy (the exact weight varies by batch, but you're getting a serious stash). As shown in the product image, the bag features a clear window strip so you can see the signature red-and-white swirled candies before committing. It's shelf-ready in the truest sense: the flat bottom lets it stand upright on a counter, pantry shelf, or office desk without tipping over.

**Flavor and Texture: The Real Test**

Butter rum is a polarizing flavor—too often it tastes like artificial vanilla with a cough-syrup aftertaste. Not here. The first bite cracks open to a creamy, buttery sweetness that builds into a warm, spiced finish reminiscent of brown sugar and cinnamon. It's genuinely candy-forward, not boozy, which makes it family-safe and endlessly snackable.

The texture holds up too. These aren't the brittle, shatter-on-impact candies that leave shards in your teeth. They're dense and slow-dissolving—a single piece lasts 10–15 minutes of steady sucking, which makes the pouch last longer than you'd think.

**Where It Falls Short**

Here's the honest tradeoff: there are no individual wrappers inside. In humid climates (or if you leave the pouch in a hot car), the candies will stick together into a clump. You can break them apart, but you'll lose the perfect swirl aesthetic. Also, the pouch is designed for one-time use—once the zipper wears out (usually after 20–30 openings), you'll want to transfer the rest to a jar. That's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.
