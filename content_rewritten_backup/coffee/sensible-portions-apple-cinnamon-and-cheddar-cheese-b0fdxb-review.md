Let’s be clear about what this isn’t: a coffee maker. But if you’re grabbing a bag of something crunchy to pair with your morning brew or afternoon espresso, this review is for you. Sensible Portions has built a name on baked veggie snacks, and this two-flavor bundle is one of their most interesting offerings. With a 4.5-star average across 225 ratings, it’s clearly finding fans. I dug into the specs, the flavor profiles, and the customer feedback to see if this snack duo actually earns a permanent spot on your counter.

## What You Actually Get

The bundle includes two separate bags: one Apple Cinnamon and one Cheddar Cheese. Both are baked, not fried, and are made primarily from vegetable flour (think potato and corn, with a touch of apple puree in the sweet variety). The Apple Cinnamon crisps are a sweet-savory hybrid, dusted with a cinnamon-sugar blend that’s more "baked apple" than "candy apple." The Cheddar Cheese crisps are sharp, tangy, and deliver a surprisingly bold cheese flavor for a baked chip. You're getting about 4 to 5 ounces per bag, depending on the variant, which is standard for the category.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Let's talk texture because that's where baked snacks usually stumble. The Apple Cinnamon crisps are light and airy, with a satisfying crackle. They don't have the greasy heft of a fried chip, which is the point. The cinnamon coating is generous but not cloying; it clings well and doesn't turn into a powdery mess at the bottom of the bag. The Cheddar Cheese crisps are a bit sturdier. They hold up better for dipping—if you're brave enough to pair them with a French onion dip, they won't shatter on impact. That’s a real plus over thinner competitors.

One thing I noticed in customer feedback: the texture can be inconsistent between batches. A few reviewers noted that some bags arrived with more "broken bits" than others, which is a shipping and handling reality, not a manufacturing flaw. It doesn't affect taste, but if you're looking for perfect whole crisps for a charcuterie board, you might be disappointed.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The consensus is clear: the Cheddar Cheese crisps are the star of the show. One reviewer wrote, "I bought this for the apple cinnamon, but the cheddar is dangerously good. It tastes like a real cheese puff, not a health food." That sentiment echoes across multiple ratings. Another buyer noted, "Finally a sweet snack that doesn't taste like cardboard. The apple cinnamon is subtle, not like eating a candle."

The portion control aspect also gets love. Each serving is a reasonable handful, and the resealable bags actually work—they don't tear or lose their seal after a few opens, which is more than I can say for some premium chip brands.

## Where It Falls Short

Honestly, the Apple Cinnamon flavor is the weaker link. It's pleasant, but it lacks the "wow" factor. It leans more toward a mild, cinnamon-sugar toast flavor rather than a punchy apple experience. If you're expecting a cinnamon toast crunch-level of sweetness, you'll be underwhelmed. Also, at the price point (around $5–6 for the bundle), you're paying a premium for the "veggie" label. You can get twice the volume of conventional chips for less money. This is a "healthy-ish" snack tax, and you should know that going in.

Another minor gripe: the cheddar flavor, while bold, leaves a lingering artificial cheese powder aftertaste for some. It didn't bother me, but one reviewer said, "It's good, but I taste the 'baked' flavor underneath. It's not as clean as a Cheeto."

## Who Should Buy This

- **The "Snack While Working" Crowd:** If you want something crunchy that won't grease up your keyboard or leave orange dust on your mouse, these are perfect.
- **The Coffee Pairing Enthusiast:** The Apple Cinnamon crisps are surprisingly great with a black dark roast. The cheddar pairs well with a lighter, fruity pour-over.
- **The Calorie-Conscious Snacker:** At about 130-140 calories per serving, you can eat the whole bag without blowing your day. That's a win.

## Skip It If

- You want a serious, intense snack flavor. These are mellow, not loud.
- You're on a strict budget and value quantity over quality.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these gluten-free?**
A: Yes, both varieties are made with corn and potato flour and are certified gluten-free. They're also Non-GMO verified.

**Q: Can I use these for dips?**
A: The Cheddar Cheese crisps can handle a light dip (salsa, guacamole). The Apple Cinnamon crisps are too delicate—skip the dip and eat them straight.

**Q: How does the Apple Cinnamon taste compare to actual apple chips?**
A: It's not like a dried apple slice. It's more like a cinnamon-sugar puffed corn snack with a subtle apple essence. Manage your expectations accordingly.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You're looking for a reliable, better-for-you crunchy snack with one genuinely excellent flavor (cheddar) and one pleasant-but-mild sweet option.

**Skip it if:** You want bold, in-your-face snack flavors or you're trying to save money on munchies.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The cheddar alone justifies the purchase. The apple cinnamon is a respectable sidekick, not a lead singer. This is a solid, honest snack bundle that delivers on its "sensible" promise.
