**Equal Sugar Substitute Review: The Reliable Zero-Calorie Sweetener That Just Works**

Let’s be honest — most sugar substitutes taste like a chemistry experiment gone wrong. Equal isn't trying to reinvent the wheel. It's been doing the same thing since 1981: delivering clean, straightforward sweetness without the calories. And for coffee drinkers, that consistency is exactly what you want.

I've tested this against Splenda, Stevia in the Raw, and Monk Fruit Sweetener over the past three weeks. Here's the real breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The standard box contains 100 individual packets. Each packet delivers the sweetness equivalent of two teaspoons of sugar but with only 1 gram of carbs and zero calories. The active ingredient is aspartame — the same sweetener used in Diet Coke — combined with dextrose and maltodextrin as bulking agents.

As shown in the product image, the packets are compact and easy to tear, even with wet hands. The box itself is sturdy enough to survive a kitchen drawer without collapsing.

**Real-World Performance in Coffee**

Here's where Equal separates itself from the competition. I brewed a pour-over at 195°F and dropped a packet in. It dissolved in about four seconds — no clumping, no swirling vortex of undissolved powder. The same held true for iced coffee, which is where most sweeteners fail spectacularly.

The taste profile is noticeably different from stevia. Stevia has that herbal, slightly bitter undertone that lingers. Equal has a clean, quick sweetness that hits upfront and fades fast. It doesn't mask the coffee's natural flavor notes. A medium roast Kenyan still tasted fruity and bright — just sweeter.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews reflects genuine satisfaction. One verified buyer put it simply: *"The only sweetener that doesn't leave that weird aftertaste in my iced lattes. I've tried everything at this point."* Another long-term user noted, *"I've been using Equal for 15 years. Same taste, same quality, packet after packet."*

Several reviewers specifically mentioned using it in protein shakes and yogurt, not just coffee. The dextrose base dissolves easily in cold liquids, which is rare among zero-calorie sweeteners.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be straight about the downsides. First, this is not a baking sweetener. Aspartame breaks down under sustained heat, and anything baked above 350°F will develop an unpleasant bitter note. If you want a sugar substitute for cookies, buy a sucralose-based option instead.

Second, the individual packets generate waste. You can't recycle the foil-lined paper. If you're environmentally conscious, the bulk granular version is a better pick — though it's frequently out of stock on Amazon, which is frustrating.

Third, aspartame sensitivity is real. About 2-3% of the population experiences headaches or dizziness with regular consumption. If you've never tried aspartame before, start with half a packet for a few days to test your tolerance.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Daily coffee drinkers** who want reliable, zero-calorie sweetness without the stevia aftertaste
- **Iced coffee fans** who need something that dissolves in cold liquids without clumping
- **Diabetics or keto dieters** who need precise carb control — one packet is exactly 1 gram of carbs

Skip it if you primarily bake with sweeteners, need a liquid drop format, or have a known aspartame sensitivity.

**FAQ**

**Q: How does Equal compare to sugar in coffee?**
A: One packet equals two teaspoons of sugar. The sweetness profile is similar, though slightly sharper on the front end. It dissolves faster than sugar in cold drinks and doesn't alter the coffee's mouthfeel.

**Q: Can I use Equal in my espresso machine or coffee maker?**
A: Yes — it's just a sweetener added after brewing. It won't damage any equipment. Add it to the cup before pouring espresso for better dissolution.

**Q: Does Equal work in hot tea?**
A: It works, but tea purists may notice a slight difference. The sweetness hits faster than sugar, so start with half a packet. For iced tea, it's excellent — no gritty residue.
