## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, shipping a 5-pound bag of hard candy means breakage. Expect some cracked or chipped pieces — it's cosmetic and doesn't affect flavor, but if you're planning to gift them individually, sort through first. Second, the bag has no resealable closure. Once opened, you'll want a clip or a transfer to an airtight container to maintain freshness long-term. And if you're hoping for variety, this is strictly classic peppermint — no wintergreen, no spearmint, no sugar-free options.

## Who Should Buy This

Buy this if you're a peppermint purist who goes through mints like water, you're stocking for a wedding or holiday event, or you run a business that keeps a candy dish on the counter. Skip it if you only want a small seasonal tin, prefer sugar-free options, or need uniform, gift-ready pieces without any imperfections.
