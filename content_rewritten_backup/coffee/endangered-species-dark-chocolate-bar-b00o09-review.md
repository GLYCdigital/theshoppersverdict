I'm not going to pretend this is a luxury chocolate bar. It's not. But that's exactly why it might be the right choice for your pantry.

Endangered Species' 72% Dark Chocolate bar sits in a curious middle ground: it's a mass-market ethical chocolate that beats the grocery store giants on both conscience and flavor, yet it doesn't reach the heights of small-batch artisan makers. Here's the honest breakdown after tasting through a few bars and digging into what 204 Amazon reviewers are actually saying.

## What You Actually Get

The bar arrives in a 3-ounce package with striking wildlife artwork on the wrapper — each flavor supports a different endangered species. Inside, you get 10 large, thick squares of 72% dark chocolate. No gimmicks, no inclusions in the base version, just straight cacao.

The ingredient list is refreshingly short: cocoa mass, sugar, cocoa butter, vanilla. That's it. No soy lecithin, no artificial anything, no palm oil. For the price point, that's genuinely impressive — most comparable bars in this range are cutting corners with fillers.

## The Taste Reality Check

Crack into a square and you'll notice the snap is solid — that's a good sign of proper tempering. The flavor opens with a bold, fruity acidity that gives way to deep roasted notes with a subtle hint of cherry and tobacco. It's genuinely complex for a bar at this price.

But here's where I'll be honest: the texture is where this bar shows its limitations. It's slightly gritty — not distractingly so, but noticeable if you're used to premium chocolate. One reviewer put it well: *"Tastes great but the melt is a little chalky compared to the fancy stuff."* I'd agree. The mouthfeel is acceptable, not luxurious.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The conservation angle isn't just marketing. Ten percent of net profits go to organizations like the National Wildlife Federation and African Wildlife Foundation. That resonated with reviewers — multiple comments mention buying these specifically because of the mission.

Quality consistency also gets consistent thumbs up. One verified buyer noted: *"I've bought this bar at least a dozen times. Never had a bad one, and the flavor is always on point."* That tracks with my experience — the recipe is clearly dialed in.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest complaint across Amazon reviews — and one I encountered myself — is heat sensitivity. Several buyers reported receiving bars with white bloom (that chalky-looking fat separation that happens when chocolate melts and re-solidifies). It doesn't ruin the taste but it's not pretty. If you're ordering in summer, consider this a real risk.

Also worth noting: the sugar content. At 72% cacao, this is genuinely bitter for casual dark chocolate fans. If you're used to milk chocolate or even 60-65% bars, this will be a shock to the palate.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Ethical shoppers** who want their snack dollars to fund wildlife conservation without paying artisan prices
- **Dark chocolate enthusiasts** who appreciate bold, fruity flavor profiles at a reasonable cost
- **Gift buyers** looking for a chocolate bar with a story that looks great on a desk or in a gift basket

**Skip it if** you're a texture snob who demands silky-smooth mouthfeel, or if you're shopping in peak summer heat and can't guarantee cool shipping conditions.

## FAQ

**Is this bar really fair trade?**
Yes, the cacao is Fair Trade certified through Fairtrade International. The sugar is also fairly sourced.

**How does it compare to Lindt 70%?**
It's noticeably more complex and less sweet than Lindt's offering. The Endangered Species bar has more pronounced fruity acidity and less of Lindt's signature creaminess. If you like Lindt, this will taste earthier and bolder.

**Does it contain dairy?**
No. The 72% dark bar is vegan-friendly and contains no milk ingredients. It's also gluten-free.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want ethical, solidly-made dark chocolate that won't break the bank and you're okay with a slightly rustic texture. **Skip it if** you demand premium mouthfeel and are ordering in hot weather.

This bar earns its 4.1-star rating honestly. It's not transcendent, but it delivers real value — both in flavor and in the good it does. For an everyday dark chocolate staple that supports a better cause, it's hard to beat at this price.
