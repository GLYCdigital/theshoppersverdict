If you like your espresso like you like your mornings — strong, dark, and no-nonsense — the Nespresso Kazaar Intenso is about as good as it gets in a capsule.

Rated 4.8 out of 5 stars from over 3,600 Amazon reviews, this is Nespresso's highest-intensity offering in the OriginalLine at Level 13. And unlike some "ultra-strong" coffees that compensate with ashiness or burnt notes, Kazaar pulls off something rare: it's powerful without being punishing.

One reviewer sums it up perfectly: *"Espresso with cojones, delicious taste, and smooth, seductive crema. A perfect 10 for me."* Another calls it *"go-go juice"* — one strong cup replaces the need for multiples. That tracks.

The crema is consistently described as thick, blonde, and long-lasting — not the thin, bubbly foam you sometimes get from weaker pods. The flavor profile leans toward cocoa and vanilla, with enough body to stand up to milk if you're making a latte, but it truly shines as a straight shot.

At roughly $45 for a 50-count box (about $0.90 per pod), it's pricier than your average drip coffee. But for Nespresso drinkers who've tried the range and keep coming back to this one — and many do — it's worth the premium.

**Who should buy it:** Espresso drinkers who want café-quality intensity at home. Nespresso OriginalLine owners who find mild roasts too thin.

**Who should skip it:** Vertuo users (these won't fit). Anyone who prefers light, fruity espresso. Budget-focused coffee drinkers.
