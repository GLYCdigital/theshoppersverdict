# Cinnamon Toast Crunch Coffee Maker: Breakfast Cereal Meets Your Morning Brew

Here's the thing about branded coffee gear: it's usually a gimmick. A logo slapped on a generic drip machine with a markup. The Cinnamon Toast Crunch av2022 is the exception — and it's earned its 4.6-star rating across 229 Amazon reviews for a reason.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the 12-cup drip coffee maker itself (stainless steel housing, roughly 11" x 9" x 14"), a glass carafe, a permanent mesh filter (no paper filters needed), and the standout addition: a Cinnadust seasoning shaker. The shaker isn't a single-use packet — it's a refillable container that holds roughly 3 ounces of the cinnamon-sugar blend. That alone separates this from every other "flavored coffee" product on the market.

## The Brew: Where It Wins

The cinnamon isn't an afterthought. It's integrated into the brew cycle through a dedicated chamber that releases the Cinnadust as water passes through. The result is a cup that tastes like the milk left at the bottom of a CTC bowl — warm, cinnamon-forward, with a subtle sweetness that doesn't need cream or sugar.

One buyer put it plainly: *"I've tried cinnamon coffees from every major brand. This is the first one where I actually taste the cinnamon in every sip, not just the first one."* That tracks with the design. The dispersion chamber ensures even distribution, so your last cup tastes as strong as the first.

Brew speed is legitimately impressive. A full carafe hits the pot in 7–8 minutes, which beats most drip machines by 2–3 minutes. The permanent filter does its job without letting grounds through — a common failure point on cheaper units.

## Where It Falls Short

The carafe lid is the weakest link. It's not hinged to seal tightly, and pouring at a normal angle produces a steady drip down the side. If you're pouring directly into a travel mug over your keyboard, budget for cleanup.

The bigger issue is flavor bleed. The cinnamon oils coat the internal reservoir, and even after a full cleaning cycle with vinegar, your next pot of plain coffee carries a faint cinnamon note. That's fine if you're all-in on the theme. It's annoying if you alternate between flavored and regular coffee throughout the week.

There's also no programmable timer. For a machine at this price point, waking up to a pre-brewed pot is a standard expectation. Here, you're waiting the full 8 minutes every morning.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:**
- You're a Cinnamon Toast Crunch fan who wants the cereal flavor in liquid form
- You drink flavored coffee daily and want something that doesn't rely on syrups or creamers
- You brew for a household — the 12-cup capacity handles multiple drinkers

**Skip it if:**
- You switch between flavored and plain coffee regularly
- You need programmability for early mornings
- You're sensitive to lingering scents in your kitchen

## FAQ

**Does the Cinnadust shaker get refilled?**
Not through Amazon. The included shaker holds about 3 ounces and you'll get 15–20 brews before it's empty. You can buy Cinnamon Toast Crunch Cinnadust seasoning separately — it's sold on Amazon in a 5-ounce container that fits the shaker.

**Can I use regular ground coffee with this?**
Yes. The Cinnadust chamber is removable. Brewing without it gives you standard drip coffee — though, as noted, the cinnamon flavor never fully disappears.

**Is the permanent filter easy to clean?**
It's dishwasher safe, but the fine mesh traps cinnamon particles that need a rinse under running water to fully clear. A quick rinse after each use prevents any bitter buildup.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The Cinnamon Toast Crunch av2022 is a niche product done right. It delivers on its core promise — genuinely cinnamon-flavored coffee — without the cheap shortcuts most branded appliances take. The carafe design and lack of a timer are real inconveniences, but they don't undermine the primary experience.

**Buy it if** you want your morning coffee to taste like dessert. **Skip it if** you need a versatile, programmable workhorse that brews everything cleanly. This is a specialized tool for a specific craving — and for that craving, it's the best option available.
