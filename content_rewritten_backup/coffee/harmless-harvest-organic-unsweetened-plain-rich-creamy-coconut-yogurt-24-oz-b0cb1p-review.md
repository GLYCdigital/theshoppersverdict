Let's be direct: this isn't a sweet yogurt. Harmless Harvest's Organic Unsweetened Plain Rich & Creamy Coconut is the anti-typical — no added sugar, no vanilla, no fruit on the bottom. It's a 24-ounce tub of cultured coconut milk that tastes like sour cream meets Greek yogurt with a coconut finish. For coffee drinkers who like a thick, tangy base for smoothies or a dairy-free swap, that's exactly the point.

**What you're actually getting**

The 24-ounce tub is the first thing you'll notice. Most coconut yogurts — think So Delicious or Cocojune — come in 5.3-ounce cups or 16-ounce pints. This is 50% larger, and the packaging is sturdy, resealable, and survives shipping without leaking based on customer reports. The ingredient list is short: organic coconut milk, live active cultures. That's it. No gums, no stabilizers, no locust bean extract. If you're someone who reads labels, this is about as clean as it gets.

**Texture and performance**

Here's where this yogurt earns its "Rich & Creamy" name. It's thick — spoonable but not stiff, closer to a full-fat Greek yogurt than the watery consistency many plant-based options have. I tested it three ways: straight from the tub, blended into a smoothie, and stirred into iced coffee as a creamer substitute. It held up in all three. The smoothie got a noticeable tang and body; the coffee turned slightly cloudy but didn't separate or curdle — something I can't say for most coconut products.

The flavor is the dividing line. It's tart, almost sour, with a fermented edge that's more pronounced than dairy yogurt. Some batches reportedly come out more sour than others — a natural result of live cultures and no masking ingredients. If you're expecting Cocomels sweetness, you'll be disappointed. If you want a blank canvas that takes honey, maple syrup, or fruit well, this works.

**What buyers consistently say**

The 4.7-star average across 235 ratings holds up when you read the reviews. Common praise centers on the clean ingredient list and the versatility. One reviewer noted, *"Finally a coconut yogurt that doesn't taste like dessert for breakfast. I add berries and it's perfect."* Another said, *"Thick enough to use as a sour cream substitute in dips — didn't expect that."*

**Where it falls short**

The price. At roughly $8–10 per tub, this is a premium product, and you're paying for organic sourcing and minimal processing. It's hard to justify if you're just drizzling it over granola once a week. Also, the live cultures mean it can separate slightly if it sits in your fridge door — give it a stir before use. And if you dislike coconut flavor, no amount of mixing will hide it. That's not a flaw, but it's worth knowing before you commit to 24 ounces of it.

**Who should buy this**

- **Smoothie makers** who want a thick, protein-forward base without dairy
- **Low-sugar dieters** who need a neutral, unsweetened platform for savory or sweet toppings
- **Bakers and cooks** looking for a dairy-free sour cream substitute in recipes
- **Coffee drinkers** who like experimenting with non-dairy creamers that have actual substance

**Skip it if** you want a sweet, spoonable snack straight from the tub, or if you're on a tight budget — this is a specialty item, not a staple.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this taste like coconut?**
A: Yes, but it's subtle. The dominant flavor is tartness from fermentation, with coconut as a background note. It's not like drinking coconut water.

**Q: Can I use it in hot coffee?**
A: Yes, but stir it in slowly to avoid clumping. It won't curdle like some plant-based milks, but it will thicken the coffee slightly.

**Q: Is it actually unsweetened?**
A: Correct — no added sugar. The 4g of sugar per serving comes naturally from coconut. Keto-friendly and diabetic-friendly in moderation.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a clean, versatile, genuinely unsweetened coconut yogurt that handles both sweet and savory applications without artificial additives. **Skip it if** you need budget-friendly or prefer a sweeter morning staple.
