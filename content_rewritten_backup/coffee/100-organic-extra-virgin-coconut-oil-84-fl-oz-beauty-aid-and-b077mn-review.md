**FAQ — real questions shoppers ask:**

**Q: Does this oil taste strongly of coconut in coffee?**
A: Yes. It's unrefined, so the natural coconut flavor is intact. In black coffee, it's noticeable. If you're using it for bulletproof-style coffee with butter and MCT oil, the flavor blends but doesn't disappear. If you want neutral oil, look for refined coconut oil instead.

**Q: Can I use this for high-heat cooking like frying?**
A: Not ideal. Virgin coconut oil has a smoke point around 350°F. It's fine for sautéing and baking, but for deep frying or high-heat searing, you'll get smoke and off-flavors. Use a high-smoke-point oil like avocado for that.

**Q: How long does an 84 oz jar last?**
A: With daily use (one tablespoon in coffee plus occasional cooking), expect 3–4 months. Stored in a cool, dark place, it stays fresh well beyond that. The jar is large enough that you'll likely finish it before any quality degradation.
