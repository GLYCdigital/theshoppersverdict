**Q: Is this raw honey or pasteurized?**
A: It's raw and unpasteurized, meaning the beneficial enzymes and pollen are intact. It will crystallize over time—that's a good sign, not a defect.

**Q: How does MGO 100+ compare to UMF 10+?**
A: MGO 100+ is roughly equivalent to UMF 10+—both indicate a moderate antibacterial activity level. Good for daily wellness, not for treating serious wounds or infections (that requires MGO 400+/UMF 15+).

**Q: Can I use this for baking or cooking?**
