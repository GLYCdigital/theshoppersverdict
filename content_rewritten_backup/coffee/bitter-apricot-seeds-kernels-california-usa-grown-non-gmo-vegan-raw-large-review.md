## FAQ

**How many kernels should I eat per day?**
Most guidance suggests 1-2 kernels per 10 pounds of body weight, spread throughout the day. For a 150-pound adult, that's roughly 15-30 kernels daily. Start at the low end and assess tolerance.

**Can I eat these raw?**
Yes, that's the intended use. Chew them thoroughly. Do not blend an entire batch into a single smoothie — that concentrates the amygdalin dangerously.

**How should I store them?**
Keep the pouch sealed in a cool, dark place. Refrigeration extends freshness significantly, especially past the three-month mark.
