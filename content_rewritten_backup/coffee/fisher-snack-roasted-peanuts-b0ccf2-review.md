Let’s be honest: a peanut is a peanut, right? Not quite. Fisher Snack Roasted Peanuts has carved out a loyal following on Amazon, holding a steady 4.2 out of 5 stars across 210 ratings. But does it actually deliver, or is it just another jar of nuts riding on brand recognition? I dug into the specs, the reviews, and the real-world experience to give you the straight answer.

## What You Actually Get

The product image shows a classic clear plastic jar with Fisher’s familiar red-and-yellow branding — nothing flashy, and that’s fine. Inside is what matters: roasted peanuts packed in a resealable container that genuinely does its job. The jar seals tight, which matters more than you’d think; it’s the difference between fresh-crunchy peanuts on day 14 and sad, rubbery ones.

Each serving runs about 7 grams of protein and roughly 170 calories. The sodium sits around 95mg per serving — noticeable salt, but not the kind that leaves your lips puckering. It’s a snack you can eat by the handful without immediately regretting it.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The roasting is the star here. These nuts have a proper roasted depth — not raw, not burnt, but that golden-brown flavor that makes you reach for another handful. The texture holds up consistently: crunchy with a clean snap, not that soft, mealy texture you get from cheaper brands.

The salt distribution is surprisingly even. No clumps of salt crystals or bland patches. That’s harder to achieve than you’d think with oil-roasted peanuts, and Fisher gets it right.

One thing worth flagging: these are oil-roasted, not dry-roasted. That gives them a slightly richer mouthfeel, but it also means you’re getting a bit more fat per serving. If you’re tracking macros strictly, that’s a consideration.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

Reading through the 210 ratings, a clear pattern emerges. The word “fresh” comes up constantly. One reviewer put it simply: *“These are the freshest peanuts I’ve had from Amazon — crunchy every time.”* Another noted, *“Good value for the size, and the resealable jar is a nice touch.”*

The praise isn’t universal, though. The most common complaint is broken pieces. Several buyers mentioned that their jars arrived with a higher-than-expected percentage of split or crushed peanuts — not a dealbreaker, but noticeable if you’re particular about whole nuts.

## Where It Falls Short

The broken piece issue is the main recurring gripe. It’s not every jar, but it’s frequent enough to show up in the reviews. If you’re buying these for a party bowl where appearance matters, that’s a real concern.

Also, there’s no variety within this specific listing — it’s just the one size and one flavor profile. If you want unsalted or honey-roasted, you’re looking at a different product entirely.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The desk snacker** — someone who wants a reliable, protein-dense snack that doesn’t require refrigeration and survives in a drawer for weeks.
- **The lunchbox packer** — parents looking for a kid-friendly snack that’s not pure sugar. The resealable jar portion control is a win.
- **The party host** — as long as you don’t mind a few broken pieces mixed in, these work fine in a bowl.

Skip it if you’re a peanut purist who needs every single nut intact, or if you’re specifically looking for a low-fat dry-roasted option.

## FAQ

**Q: How long do these stay fresh after opening?**
A: With the resealable lid, they hold their crunch for 2-3 weeks. Beyond that, they start to soften. Storing them in a cool, dark place helps.

**Q: Are these gluten-free?**
A: Yes, peanuts are naturally gluten-free, and there’s no wheat-based coating here. If you have severe celiac, check the label for cross-contamination warnings, but no wheat ingredients are listed.

**Q: Can I use these for baking or cooking?**
A: Absolutely, but the salt content is a factor. If you’re adding them to a recipe that already calls for salt, you’ll want to adjust. For snacking, they’re ready to go as-is.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a dependable, crunchy, well-salted peanut that stays fresh in a resealable jar. It’s a solid everyday snack with good protein value.

**Skip it if** you need perfectly intact whole peanuts or a dry-roasted, lower-fat option.

Fisher Snack Roasted Peanuts earns its 4.2-star rating honestly. It’s not a revolutionary product — it’s a well-executed classic that does exactly what it promises. For the price, you’re getting a quality snack that outperforms most generic store brands. Just don’t be surprised if you find a few broken pieces in the mix; that’s the tradeoff for a product this widely distributed.
