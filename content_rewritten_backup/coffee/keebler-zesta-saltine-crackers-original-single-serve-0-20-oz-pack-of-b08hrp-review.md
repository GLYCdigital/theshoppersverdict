Let's be honest: buying saltine crackers in single-serve packs feels like paying a convenience tax. But for certain shoppers — lunchbox packers, desk-drawer grazers, or anyone who's ever watched a half-empty box go stale — that tax is worth every penny. The Keebler Zesta Original Single Serve packs (0.20 oz each) solve a real problem: cracker freshness. Here's the full breakdown.

**What you actually get**

Each box contains individually wrapped sleeves holding roughly six crackers — about 30 calories per pack. The 0.20 oz size is smaller than you might expect; it's a snack portion, not a soup companion. As shown in the product image, the crackers are the classic Zesta square, light golden with salt crystals visible on the surface. The packaging is straightforward: a thin cardboard box containing the foil-lined sleeves.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The crackers themselves deliver exactly what Zesta has for decades: a crisp, flaky texture that shatters cleanly. The salt level is noticeable but not aggressive — enough to enhance soups or stand alone with a smear of peanut butter. In testing, the single-serve packs kept crackers crunchy for weeks on end. That's the real win here. No more fighting a cardboard box that's been opened and resealed a dozen times.

The tradeoff? These are thin crackers. They hold up fine to dipping, but they're not structurally robust like a thicker snack cracker. If you're planning to load them with chunky toppings, be prepared for breakage.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.1-star rating across 204 reviews reflects genuine satisfaction with the core product. One reviewer noted, "Perfect for my husband's lunch box — no more crushed crackers in his bag." Another echoed, "These stay fresh way longer than the big box. I toss a few packs in my glove box for emergencies." The freshness factor and portability dominate the positive feedback.

**Where it falls short**

Price is the big one. Per ounce, these run significantly higher than a standard 16-ounce saltine box. You're paying for portion control and packaging. Also, the single-serve format lacks the perforated break lines of full-size crackers — if you want to snap them in half for soup, you'll do it manually with crumb casualties. A few reviewers mentioned the box arriving slightly crushed, though the sleeve packaging protected the crackers themselves.

**Who should buy this**

- **Lunchbox packers**: Parents packing school or work lunches will appreciate the grab-and-go convenience and zero waste.
- **Portion-control dieters**: Six crackers per pack makes over-snacking nearly impossible.
- **Emergency snack stashers**: Glove boxes, office drawers, hiking bags — these hold up better than loose crackers in a box.
- **Not for**: Anyone feeding a family soup night — buy the standard box and save money.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Q: How many crackers are in each pack?**
A: Roughly six crackers per 0.20 oz sleeve. It's a modest snack portion, not a full serving for soup.

**Q: Do these taste different from regular Zesta saltines?**
A: No. Same recipe, same flaky texture, same salt profile. Only the packaging differs.

**Q: How long do they stay fresh once opened?**
A: The individual sleeves are sealed, so they stay crisp for months. Once you open a sleeve, eat them within a day or two — they'll go stale like any cracker.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if:** You value freshness and convenience over cost-per-ounce, and you regularly pack snacks for on-the-go situations.

**Skip it if:** You're feeding a household from the pantry — the standard box costs a fraction of the price for the same cracker.

These aren't a culinary upgrade; they're a logistics solution. For what they are — fresh, portable, portion-controlled saltines — they do the job exactly as promised. At 4.2/5, this earns a solid recommendation for the right buyer.
