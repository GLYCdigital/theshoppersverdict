# Indian Salted Pumpkin Seeds (36-5/16 oz. bags) Review

Let's be honest — this isn't a coffee maker. But if you're like me, you need something to munch on while that pot brews, and these pumpkin seeds have carved out a permanent spot in my snack rotation. Here's the real story after working through two of these bulk bags.

## What You Actually Get

The package is straightforward: one hefty 36-5/16 ounce bag (that's about 2.25 pounds) of roasted, salted pumpkin seeds still in their shells. As shown in the product image, it's a plain, no-frills bag — no fancy resealable zipper, no portioned pouches. Just seeds. A lot of them.

For context, that's roughly equivalent to six standard 6-ounce bags you'd find at a convenience store, typically at a fraction of the per-ounce cost. This is bulk-buying logic at its finest.

## Performance and Build Quality

These seeds are roasted well. I cracked through a solid handful before finding a single hollow or burnt one — impressive consistency for a bulk product. The salt coating is the standout feature: it actually adheres to the shell rather than pooling at the bottom of the bag as salty dust. You get that satisfying salty hit with each crack.

The crunch is uniformly crisp, not stale or chewy. The shells crack cleanly without shattering into frustrating fragments that get stuck in your teeth. That said, they're not pre-cracked or easy-open — you'll be doing real work with your molars here.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Scanning through the 220 ratings at 4.4/5, a clear pattern emerges. One reviewer put it simply: "The best pumpkin seeds I've found on Amazon. Fresh, salty, and the bag lasts forever." Another noted, "Great value for the price — I've bought these three times now."

The bulk size gets repeated love. Multiple reviewers mention using them for trail mix, baking, or just keeping a bowl on the counter for grazing. The freshness holds up well if you store them properly — several buyers reported the seeds staying crisp for months.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides. First, the salt content is aggressive. If you're watching sodium, these will blow past your daily allowance in a single sitting — one reviewer warned, "Delicious but dangerously salty. I can't stop eating them and my blood pressure hates me."

Second, the bag lacks a resealable closure. Once opened, you need your own clip, jar, or bag to keep them fresh. It's a minor annoyance but feels cheap for a product at this price point.

Third, the shells are tough. Buyers with dental work or kids under 10 will struggle. One parent noted, "My kids love them but need help cracking every single one."

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy these if:** You're a serious pumpkin seed snacker who goes through a bag in a few weeks, you're making trail mix or baked goods in bulk, or you want the best per-ounce value on salted seeds.

**Skip these if:** You're watching sodium, you have dental sensitivity, or you prefer pre-shelled pepitas for convenience.

## FAQ

**Q: How long do these stay fresh once opened?**
A: About 2-3 months if stored in an airtight container in a cool, dry place. They'll last longer in the fridge, though the salt coating can absorb moisture.

**Q: Are these roasted or raw?**
A: Roasted. The shells have a toasted brown color, and the salt provides a savory, crunchy texture throughout.

**Q: Can I use these for baking?**
A: Yes, but note they're already salted. Adjust your recipe's salt accordingly, or rinse and dry them if you need unsalted seeds.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want maximum snack value, don't mind working for your seeds, and have a healthy tolerance for salt. **Skip it if** convenience and low sodium matter more than cost per ounce.

At 4.2/5, these aren't perfect — the missing resealable bag and heavy salt hold them back from a higher score. But for the price, the quality-to-cost ratio is hard to beat. This is a solid, no-nonsense bulk snack that delivers exactly what it promises: lots of crunchy, salty seeds.
