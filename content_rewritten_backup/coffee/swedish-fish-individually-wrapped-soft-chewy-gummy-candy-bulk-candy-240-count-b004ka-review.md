**The 240-Count Bulk Box That Actually Makes Sense**

Let's be honest: Swedish Fish are a nostalgia trip in candy form. That glossy red exterior, the faintly fruity aroma, the way the gummy yields just before your teeth sink in. But buying them in bulk usually means a giant bag of loose gummies that fuse into one sticky mass by the time you reach the bottom.

This individually wrapped version solves that problem. And it does it without screwing up the candy.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The box contains 240 individually wrapped pieces — about 2.5 pounds of candy total. Each fish comes in a sealed foil wrapper bearing the classic red-and-white branding. As shown in the product image, the wrappers are uniform and print quality is sharp, which matters if you're setting these out at a wedding or event where presentation counts.

The fish themselves measure roughly 1.5 inches long, bigger than the mini Swedish Fish but smaller than the giant ones. That's the sweet spot for snacking — you can eat two or three without feeling like you've committed to a sugar project.

**Taste and Texture: The Real Test**

Here's where this product surprises. The individually wrapped pieces are actually firmer than the loose bulk Swedish Fish you're used to. The outside has that classic thin shell, but the interior is denser, with more chew resistance. This isn't a flaw — it's honestly closer to the original Swedish recipe than what you get in the big red bags sold at Walmart.

The flavor is unmistakably Swedish Fish: that bright, slightly tart red berry profile that's neither cherry nor strawberry but somehow both. It's sweet without being syrupy, and the tang keeps you reaching for another piece.

One buyer put it well: *"I ordered these for my office and they're dangerous. The individual packaging means people take one without making a mess, but everyone keeps coming back for more. I've reordered three times."*

Another reviewer noted: *"My kids are in elementary school and these are the only candies I'll let them bring in their lunches. The wrappers keep them from getting sticky and they last forever."*

**Where It Falls Short**

The wrappers are the double-edged sword here. Yes, they preserve freshness — but you're generating 240 pieces of trash. If you're environmentally conscious, that's going to grate on you. The foil wrappers aren't recyclable in most curbside programs.

Also, if you're expecting the exact same texture as the loose bags, you'll notice the difference. These are slightly firmer, which some people love and others find off-putting. It's a subtle difference, but it's there.

Finally, the box is large. Not heavy — about 2.5 pounds — but the packaging takes up real shelf space. If you have limited pantry room, this might not be your best option.
