I'll be honest: I didn't expect much from a box of crackers. But after working through the RITZ Bits Cheese and Peanut Butter Sandwich Crackers variety pack, I understand why this thing holds a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings. It's not revolutionary — it's just done right.

## What you actually get

This is a straightforward variety pack. You get a mix of two flavors: RITZ Bits Cheese Sandwich Crackers and RITZ Bits Peanut Butter Sandwich Crackers. Each bag is the standard 1.38-ounce snack pack — the kind you'd toss in a lunchbox or keep in a desk drawer. The exact count of cheese vs. peanut butter bags varies by shipment, but the box I received was split fairly evenly.

As shown in the product image, the packaging is clean and functional — no fancy resealable stand-up pouches here, just the classic single-serve bags. That's fine for most uses, but if you're planning to pour these into a bowl at home, you'll be opening multiple bags.

## How they actually taste

The cracker base is where RITZ earns its reputation. Each bit-sized cracker has that signature buttery, slightly salty flavor with a satisfying crunch. The texture holds up well — even the peanut butter variety, which has a filling that could theoretically soften the cracker, stays crisp.

**Cheese flavor:** This is a mild, creamy cheddar-style filling. It's pleasant and crowd-pleasing, but don't expect anything sharp or aged. One reviewer put it well: *"Taste great, especially the cheese ones. My kids devour them."* That's the target audience — kids and anyone who prefers a gentle cheese note.

**Peanut butter flavor:** The PB filling is smooth and sweet, with a texture that's more like a frosting than a natural peanut butter. If you're used to Jif or Skippy, this will taste familiar. If you're a fan of crunchy or natural peanut butter, this will likely disappoint — it's firmly in the "sweet snack" category, not the "protein-packed savory" category.

## Where it falls short

Let's be direct about the limitations.

The **portion size** is the biggest practical issue. At 1.38 ounces per bag, these are snack-sized, not meal-sized. If you're packing these for a work lunch, you'll want two bags — which means more packaging waste.

The **peanut butter sweetness** is a genuine miss for some buyers. Multiple reviews mention it: *"The peanut butter ones were too sweet for my taste."* It's not a defect — it's a flavor profile choice — but it's worth knowing before you buy.

The **cheese flavor is subtle**. If you're looking for a bold cheddar punch, this isn't it. It's more of a background note that complements the buttery cracker.

## Who should buy this

This variety pack is ideal for:

- **Parents packing school lunches** — the individual bags are perfectly portioned, and the two-flavor mix keeps things from getting boring
- **Office snackers** — keep a box in your drawer for the 3 p.m. craving; the sealed bags stay fresh for weeks
- **Road trip snacks** — the bit-sized crackers are easy to eat one-handed while driving (though maybe keep your eyes on the road)
- **Kids' parties or playdates** — the small bags are easy to hand out, and the flavors are universally kid-approved

Skip it if you want a single-flavor bulk purchase, prefer savory peanut butter, or need a larger portion per serving.

**Q: Are these gluten-free?**
A: No. RITZ crackers are made with wheat flour, so this product contains gluten. Not suitable for celiacs or those on a strict gluten-free diet.

**Q: How many bags come in the box?**
A: The box contains 12 individual snack packs — a mix of cheese and peanut butter flavors. The exact split varies by shipment, but it's typically close to 6 and 6.

**Q: Do these stay fresh if I don't finish the box right away?**
A: Yes. Each bag is individually sealed, so unopened bags stay fresh for months. Check the "best by" date on the box for specific guidance. Once opened, finish within a few days — the crackers will lose their crunch.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5** — This is a solid, dependable snack that does exactly what it promises. The RITZ cracker quality is consistent, the two-flavor variety adds value, and the individual packaging makes it genuinely convenient.

**Buy it if** you need a reliable, kid-approved snack that works for lunches, travel, or desk drawers — and you're okay with a sweet peanut butter filling.

**Skip it if** you're looking for a bold cheese flavor, savory peanut butter, or a bulk bag for at-home snacking. This variety pack is optimized for portability, not pantry storage.

For the price point and the 4.7-star rating, this is a safe, practical purchase. It won't blow your mind, but it won't let you down either — and that's exactly what a snack cracker should be.
