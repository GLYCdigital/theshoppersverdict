# Philippine Brand Dried Mango, 20 Ounce (Pack of 1) — A Sweet Treat Worth the Hype?

Let's be clear about something upfront: this is **not** the tart, leathery dried mango you've bought in bulk bins at the grocery store. Philippine Brand Dried Mango is a different category of snack entirely — closer to a soft, plump confection than a chewy jerky. And for the 225+ shoppers who've rated it 4.5 stars, that distinction matters.

## What You Actually Get

The 20-ounce pouch is substantial. As shown in the product image, it comes in a gold-and-green resealable bag that's roughly the size of a large cereal bag. Open it and you'll find golden slices — not the pale, sulfur-treated strips from Thailand — but deep amber pieces that look like fresh Carabao mangoes were sliced and gently dehydrated.

You're getting real Philippine Carabao mangoes, widely considered among the sweetest in the world. Each slice is about 2-3 inches long, soft, and pliable. The bag holds roughly 3 cups of fruit once separated.

## Taste and Texture: The Real Story

Here's where this product separates itself. Most commercial dried mango is treated with sulfur dioxide to preserve color — it gives that bright orange look but leaves a faint chemical aftertaste. This one skips that process entirely. The slices are naturally darker, but the flavor payoff is immediate: intensely sweet with that distinctive honeyed mango note and just a whisper of tropical acidity at the finish.

The texture is the second differentiator. These slices are **soft and almost velvety** — they bend easily without cracking. Bite into one and it's like eating a concentrated fresh mango, not a chewy strip. That makes them dangerous for portion control. One reviewer noted, "I ordered these for a road trip and the bag was gone before we hit the state line. They're dangerously good."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the reviews, three themes dominate:

**Freshness:** Multiple buyers mention that these taste "just-picked" compared to other dried mango brands. One wrote, "I've tried every brand on Amazon. This is the only one that tastes like actual mango instead of sugar syrup."

**Versatility:** Bakers love these. The high moisture content means they chop easily and rehydrate quickly in recipes. Several reviewers mention using them in oatmeal, yogurt, and even mango sticky rice.

**Authenticity:** The Philippine origin matters to buyers who grew up with the real thing. As one reviewer put it, "This is what we ate as kids in Manila. Worth every penny."

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

First, **the stickiness factor**. Because these are less processed, the slices cling together in the pouch. You'll need to gently peel them apart, and if you're not careful, you'll end up with a few torn pieces. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

Second, **shelf life after opening**. The lack of preservatives means these won't last months in your pantry like commercial brands. Plan to finish the bag within 7-10 days of opening or store it in the fridge. The resealable closure helps, but it's not airtight enough for long-term storage.

Third, **the price**. This 20-ounce bag costs more per ounce than generic dried mango. You're paying for the sourcing, the lack of additives, and the superior texture. For some shoppers, that premium is justified. For others, it might feel steep.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you're a dried fruit purist who wants the real thing, a baker who needs high-quality mango for recipes, or someone looking for a gift that feels premium. It's also ideal for anyone who found traditional dried mango too tough or too tart.

**Skip it if** you're on a tight budget, prefer crispy dried fruit, or need something that will last months in your pantry. Also skip if you have a serious sweet tooth — this is naturally sweet but not sugar-coated, and some reviewers found it less candied than they expected.
