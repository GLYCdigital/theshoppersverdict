Here's a product I didn't know I needed until I used it: a stainless steel pour-over that doesn't burn your fingers, won't shatter in your camping bag, and makes coffee good enough to retire your Chemex.

The YETI Pour Over is exactly what you'd expect from the brand that made overbuilding cool. It's a double-wall stainless steel pour-over cone that fits on top of your mug. That's it. But the execution is where YETI earns its reputation.

**The thermal engineering matters.** Most pour-over cones are glass or ceramic. They get hot. You need a handle or a careful grip. This YETI stays cool on the outside because of the double-wall vacuum insulation. One reviewer notes: *"It stays cool enough on the outside that it's easy to set aside after brewing."* Meanwhile, the coffee inside stays hot because the stainless steel doesn't suck heat out of your brew like ceramic does.

**It's practically indestructible.** Glass Chemex? One knock away from tragedy. Ceramic Hario? Chip it once and it's done. This YETI can go in a backpack, a camping gear box, or checked luggage without a second thought. Reviewers consistently mention travel use: *"We travel a lot and I didn't want to take my glass carafe so I purchased this. Strong, sturdy, and easy to clean."* Another uses it for camping and hiking.

**But it does have size limitations.** The YETI Pour Over is designed for standard mug sizes. If you have an oversized coffee cup — those massive 20 oz diner mugs — this won't sit on top securely. As one reviewer discovered: *"Almost the perfect pour over...if you have an oversized coffee cup, this is not the pour over for you."*

**The brewing experience is solid.** It uses standard V60 cone filters (no proprietary nonsense), and the pour-over process is straightforward. The double-wall construction means your coffee stays at brewing temperature longer, which gives you a more consistent extraction. One reviewer notes: *"Reviews about learning the process are reliable. Have a bit of patience, but it will deliver a great cup of coffee."*

**Who is this for?** Campers, travelers, and anyone who's broken their last glass pour-over. YETI loyalists who want the full Rambler ecosystem. People who want pour-over quality without the fragility of ceramic.

At $30, this isn't the cheapest pour-over on the market. But it's probably the last one you'll buy. It's the pour-over that survives your life.
