Let's be direct: most coconut milk powders are either loaded with maltodextrin fillers or they clump into sad, chalky blobs in your mug. The Micro Ingredients Organic Coconut Milk Powder avoids both traps — mostly. Here's what 220 Amazon reviewers (averaging 4.4 stars) are actually saying, and where this 2-pound bag earns its keep.

**What's in the bag**

You get one 2 lb (32 oz) stand-up pouch. That's roughly 90 tablespoons — about 90 servings if you use the standard 1-tablespoon dose. The ingredient list is exactly one item: organic coconut milk powder. No silicon dioxide, no maltodextrin, no sugar. That's increasingly rare in this category. The powder itself is fine and off-white, closer to flour than to the gritty granules you see in cheaper brands.

**How it performs in coffee**

This is where the rubber meets the road. In hot coffee, it dissolves surprisingly well. Drop a tablespoon into a fresh mug, stir for about 10 seconds, and you get a smooth, milky texture without the clumping that plagues many competitors. The mouthfeel is genuinely creamy — closer to full-fat dairy than to almond or oat milk.

The flavor is subtle. You get a gentle coconut note that plays nicely with medium roasts but disappears entirely in dark, smoky beans. If you're chasing a bold coconut-forward latte, this isn't it. If you want a clean, dairy-free creaminess without an overwhelming flavor, this delivers.

**Where it stumbles**

Cold drinks are the weak point. In iced coffee, the powder separates and settles at the bottom unless you blend it or shake it hard in a jar. Several reviewers noted this exact issue. One wrote, "Tastes great in hot coffee but I couldn't get it to dissolve in my iced lattes no matter how much I stirred." That tracks — the fat content (about 60% coconut oil) means it needs heat or mechanical agitation to emulsify properly.

Also, the bag isn't resealable in the traditional sense. It has a zip closure, but the top tends to curl and doesn't always seal flush. If you don't transfer it to an airtight container, you risk clumping from humidity. Minor, but worth knowing.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The clean ingredient list gets the most love. Reviewers repeatedly mention using it for keto, paleo, and dairy-free diets without worrying about hidden additives. One verified purchase wrote: "Finally a coconut milk powder that doesn't have a bunch of junk in it. I use it in my coffee every morning and it's perfect for my keto macros."

The value also earns consistent praise. At this size, you're paying a fraction of what single-serving sachets cost, and it lasts a solid three months with daily use.

**Who should buy this**

- **Keto and dairy-free coffee drinkers** who want a clean, single-ingredient creamer
- **Hot coffee loyalists** who don't need iced latte capability
- **Bakers and smoothie makers** who want a versatile coconut milk powder for recipes

**Who should skip it**

- **Iced coffee devotees** who won't tolerate the separation issue
- **Anyone wanting a strong coconut flavor** — this is subtle, not punchy
- **Budget shoppers** who don't need organic and are fine with fillers

**FAQ**

**Does it contain added sugar?** No. It's 100% organic coconut milk powder with no sweeteners. If you want sweetness, add your own.

**How does it compare to canned coconut milk?** It's less rich and less coconut-forward than canned full-fat. But it's shelf-stable, doesn't need refrigeration, and won't separate in your pantry. For coffee, it's actually more convenient.

**Can I use it in baking?** Yes, but expect a lighter texture than canned coconut milk. It works well in curries, smoothies, and baked goods where you want coconut flavor without extra liquid.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a clean, single-ingredient coconut creamer for hot coffee and value a 90-serving bag over pricey single-serve options.

**Skip it if** you're an iced coffee person or you want a strong coconut punch in every sip.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — This is a solid, honest coconut milk powder that nails the basics. The cold-beverage weakness and subtle flavor keep it from a perfect score, but for hot coffee and general kitchen use, it's a reliable, clean-label choice that earns its place in your pantry.
