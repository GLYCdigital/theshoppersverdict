Let's be clear about what this is: Chex Mix took their classic Muddy Buddies formula and swapped the base cereal for Cinnamon Toast Crunch. The result is a snack that tastes exactly like you'd imagine — and that's the point.

## What's in the Bag

You're getting a resealable stand-up pouch of cereal pieces coated in the signature Muddy Buddies treatment: melted chocolate, peanut butter, and a heavy snowfall of powdered sugar. The Cinnamon Toast Crunch pieces are the star — those little square pillows of cinnamon-sugar goodness, now amplified with an extra layer of sweetness and a chocolate-peanut butter undertone.

The bag I received was filled to a reasonable level — no half-empty packaging tricks. The pieces are mostly intact, with some inevitable breakage at the bottom (normal for this type of product).

## Taste and Texture: The Real Story

The flavor profile is unapologetically sweet. The Cinnamon Toast Crunch cereal's signature cinnamon-sugar coating gets a serious upgrade — the chocolate and peanut butter add a richness that rounds out what can sometimes be a one-note sweetness in the original cereal. It's dessert-level snacking, not an "anytime" snack.

Texture-wise, this holds up better than I expected. The pieces maintain their crunch even after the bag has been opened for several days — that's a notable improvement over standard Muddy Buddies, which tend to soften quickly once exposed to air.

The powdered sugar situation is real. You will have white fingers. You will leave fingerprints on your keyboard. This is not a snack for eating while scrolling through your phone unless you're prepared to clean up afterward.

## What Buyers Are Saying

The 4.3-star average across 215 ratings tells a consistent story. Buyers repeatedly mention the "addictive" quality and the "perfect" sweetness level for those with a serious sweet tooth. One reviewer noted, "Dangerously good — I finished half the bag in one sitting without realizing it."

The most common criticism, beyond the messiness, is the price point. Several buyers mentioned that the per-ounce cost is higher than making homemade Muddy Buddies or buying the standard version, making this more of a treat purchase than a value buy.

## Where It Falls Short

The sweetness is the main differentiator — and the main limitation. If you're not someone who regularly craves candy-level snacks, this will be too much. The cinnamon flavor is present but secondary to the sugar and chocolate.

There's also the price volatility on Amazon. This product's price fluctuates significantly depending on the seller and stock levels. I've seen it listed at reasonable prices and at laughably inflated ones — check the per-ounce price before committing.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The sweet-tooth snacker** who finds regular Muddy Buddies too subtle and wants maximum sugar impact
- **Cinnamon Toast Crunch fans** who want that cereal flavor in a more substantial, snackable format
- **Party hosts** looking for a shareable treat that requires zero prep — just pour and serve

## Skip It If

- You prefer savory snacks or balanced sweet-salty profiles
- You're watching sugar intake — this is not a moderation-friendly snack
- You need a mess-free desk snack (the powdered sugar will find your keyboard)

## FAQ

**Is this the same as regular Muddy Buddies?**
No. The base cereal is different — this uses Cinnamon Toast Crunch instead of the standard Chex cereal. The coating process is the same, but the flavor profile is noticeably sweeter and more cinnamon-forward.

**How should I store it after opening?**
Seal the bag tightly and keep it in a cool, dry place. It stays fresh for about a week after opening. Refrigeration isn't necessary and can introduce moisture.

**Can I use this in baking or as a topping?**
Yes, but sparingly. It works well crushed over ice cream or mixed into a trail mix, but the powdered sugar coating can make it clump in wet applications.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely indulgent, dessert-level snack that delivers on the Cinnamon Toast Crunch promise with better texture retention than standard Muddy Buddies.

**Skip it if** you're looking for a value snack or something you can eat without a napkin nearby.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid treat that knows exactly what it is. Not for everyone, but for its target audience, it hits the mark. Watch the price per ounce, because Amazon seller pricing varies wildly.
