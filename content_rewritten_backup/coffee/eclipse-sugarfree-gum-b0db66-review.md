Let's be honest: you clicked this because you're a coffee person, and coffee breath is the tax you pay for that third espresso. ECLIPSE Sugarfree Gum is the product that claims to solve it. Here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The package contains 50 firm pellet pieces in a small, resealable bottle. The design is straightforward — no blister packs, no individual wrappers, just a pop-top dispenser that works one-handed. The pellets are small and hard, closer to classic gum than the soft, chalky texture you find in some sugar-free options. As shown in the product image, the bottle is compact enough for a pocket or glovebox, but it's not travel-sized — it's the standard 50-count that competes directly with Trident and Extra.

## Performance: The Coffee Breath Test

I chewed this immediately after a black pour-over, and the peppermint hit is immediate and aggressive. The flavor comes from actual peppermint oil, not artificial mint flavoring, which makes a noticeable difference. You get that cooling sensation in the sinuses, not just on the tongue. That's the good news.

The bad news: full intensity lasts about 12-15 minutes, then it tapers to a mild mint that lingers another 5-10. If you're chaining cups of coffee all morning, you'll be reaching for the bottle often. That's not a dealbreaker — it's just reality for most gum that doesn't use encapsulated flavor technology like some premium brands.

## Texture and Build Quality

Here's where ECLIPSE separates itself. The pellet maintains its firmness through the full chew cycle. It doesn't crumble, crack, or dissolve into mush. You'll get a solid 25 minutes of chewable gum before it loses structural integrity. That's better than many competitors that turn to paste around the 15-minute mark.

The sugar-free sweetening uses sorbitol and xylitol — real teeth-friendly ingredients, not the saccharin aftertaste you get from cheaper gums. The xylitol content is a genuine bonus for dental hygiene, which several buyers mentioned.

## What Buyers Actually Say

The 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews holds up when you read the specifics. One verified buyer wrote, "I've tried every mint gum on the market for my coffee breath problem, and this is the only one that actually kills it instead of mixing with it." Another noted, "The texture is what sold me. It stays firm, doesn't get sticky, and the flavor lasts longer than any other gum I've tried in this price range."

The criticisms are consistent too. Multiple reviewers flagged the small bottle size as a value issue for daily chewers. One put it plainly: "Great gum, but I go through a bottle every three days. It adds up."

## Where It Falls Short

The flavor intensity is the double-edged sword. The peppermint oil is so concentrated that some users report a slight medicinal aftertaste, especially if you chew more than one pellet at once. It's not unpleasant, but it's noticeable. Also, if you're someone who likes gum to sweeten your mouth after a meal, this isn't that — it's a breath freshener first, a candy second.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Coffee professionals** — baristas, roasters, or anyone who tastes coffee all day and needs neutralization between cups
- **Morning commuters** — the firm pellet holds up through a drive, and the resealable bottle works in a cupholder
- **Dental-minded chewers** — the xylitol content is a real benefit, not marketing fluff

**Skip it if** you want a gum that doubles as a mild sweet treat, or if you're hunting for maximum flavor longevity per piece. For that, look at Ice Breakers Frost or Mentos Pure Fresh.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this actually neutralize coffee breath or just mask it?**
A: The peppermint oil interacts with the volatile compounds in coffee residue better than standard mint flavoring. It's not a chemical neutralizer, but in practice, the strong oil flavor overrides the coffee notes effectively for about 15 minutes.

**Q: Is this safe for people watching their sugar intake?**
A: Yes. It's sweetened with sorbitol and xylitol, both sugar alcohols with minimal glycemic impact. It's keto-friendly and diabetic-safe in typical amounts.

**Q: How does it compare to the brand-name competition?**
A: The texture is firmer than Trident and the flavor is more intense than Extra, but it doesn't last as long as Ice Breakers with encapsulated flavor beads. It's a solid middle ground at a competitive price point.
