**These aren't crackers that pretend to be bread. They're something better — if you know what you're getting into.**

ELLA’S FLATS All Seed Savory Crisps sit in that crowded "healthy cracker" aisle where most products taste like compressed cardboard. These don't. They've carved out a legitimate 4.2-star rating across 210 Amazon reviews, and after digging into what buyers actually say, the praise centers on one thing: texture that actually works.

**What's in the box**

You get a bag of thin, flat seed crisps — think a denser, sturdier version of a flax cracker. The base is a blend of sunflower seeds, sesame seeds, and flax, baked into a crisp that's got real structural integrity. As shown in the product image, they're uniform rounds, about the size of a standard cracker, with visible seed speckling throughout. One bag runs around 4 ounces, which matters when you're comparing price per serving against conventional crackers.

**How they perform in real use**

The defining feature is crunch. Multiple reviewers specifically note these hold up to thick dips, spreads, and toppings without disintegrating mid-bite. One buyer put it plainly: *"Finally a seed cracker that doesn't crumble the second you look at it."* That's the core value proposition — you can load these with hummus, tuna salad, or soft cheese and they'll stay intact.

The flavor profile is savory but restrained. There's a subtle roasted nuttiness from the seeds, a light salt finish, and no lingering aftertaste. If you're expecting bold garlic or herb punch, you'll be disappointed. These are a delivery vehicle, not a flavor bomb. The texture is where the satisfaction lives.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The ingredient list gets frequent shoutouts. No gluten, no added sugar, no weird gums or starches. For anyone navigating keto, paleo, or gluten-free restrictions, these check every box without tasting like a compromise. One long-term reviewer mentioned they've repurchased multiple times specifically because *"I can eat them with anything and they don't upset my stomach."*

Shelf stability also earns mentions — they stay crisp for weeks after opening, which isn't guaranteed in the seed-cracker category.

**Where they fall short**

Let's be direct about the tradeoffs. The flavor is mild — if you're used to aggressively seasoned snacks, these will feel bare. You'll want a dip or topping to make them sing. That's not a flaw, but it's a reality.

The price is the bigger sticking point. You're paying a premium for the seed base, and the per-ounce cost runs noticeably higher than conventional crackers. For a family snack rotation, that adds up fast.

Packaging is the final frustration. The crisps are thin and fragile, and multiple reviewers report receiving a bag with a fair amount of broken pieces. The taste is unaffected, but if you care about presentation, expect some casualties in transit.

**Who should buy these**

- **Keto and low-carb dieters** who need a crunchy vehicle for dips and toppings without the carb load
- **Gluten-free eaters** tired of rice-based crackers that taste like styrofoam
- **Clean-label shoppers** who read ingredients and want nothing but seeds and seasoning
- **Charcuterie hosts** who want a sturdy, attractive cracker that won't embarrass them under a smear of brie

**Skip them if** you want bold snack flavor straight from the bag, or you're on a tight grocery budget.

**FAQ**

**Are these actually crunchy or do they get stale fast?**
They're genuinely crisp with a hard snap, not a soft crunch. Buyers report they stay fresh for weeks after opening when stored in a sealed container. The texture is closer to a baked cracker than a raw seed chip.

**Can I eat these on a keto diet?**
Yes. The net carb count is low, driven by the seed base rather than flour or starch. Multiple reviewers specifically mention using them for keto snacking and dips.

**Do they taste strongly of flax?**
No. The sunflower and sesame seeds dominate the flavor, with flax playing a background role. The overall profile is nutty and mildly savory, not grassy or earthy.

**The Verdict**

**Skip it if** you need bold flavor straight from the bag or you're watching per-ounce grocery costs closely.
