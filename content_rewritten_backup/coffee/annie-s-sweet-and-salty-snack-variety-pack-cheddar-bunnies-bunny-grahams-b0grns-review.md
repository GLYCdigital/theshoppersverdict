Let’s be honest: most snack variety packs are a graveyard of stale, sad crackers. Annie's Sweet and Salty Snack Variety Pack, featuring Cheddar Bunnies and Chocolate Chip Bunny Grahams, is the exception. With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, this isn't just another lunchbox filler — it's the pack that actually gets finished.

**What you're actually getting**

The pack contains a mix of Annie's classic Cheddar Bunnies and Chocolate Chip Bunny Grahams, each in individually sealed 1-ounce pouches. That's roughly 12-14 pouches per box depending on the mix ratio. The packaging is straightforward — no fancy resealable bag, just grab-and-go pouches that work for lunchboxes, diaper bags, or desk drawers. As shown in the product image, the boxes feature Annie's signature bunny mascot and clean, cheerful branding that stands out from the neon chaos of typical kids' snacks.

**Performance and real-world use**

The Cheddar Bunnies deliver that familiar, slightly buttery cheese cracker crunch. They're not as sharp or salty as Cheez-Its — they're gentler, more rounded in flavor. The Chocolate Chip Bunny Grahams are the standout: sweet without being cloying, with actual chocolate chips that don't disintegrate into the graham base. Both hold up well in transit; I didn't encounter a single crushed pouch, which is rare for boxed snacks.

Portion size is the real win here. At 1 ounce per pouch, these are properly single-serving. No more handing a kid a 400-calorie "snack" and calling it portion control. The pouches are easy for small hands to open, though the tear notch can be finicky — a pair of scissors helps for younger kids.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Parents overwhelmingly cite the organic certification and clean ingredient list as the primary draw. One verified buyer noted, "Finally a snack I don't feel guilty throwing in the lunchbox. The ingredients are things I can actually pronounce." Another wrote, "My daughter is obsessed with the bunny shapes — she actually eats these instead of trading them at school." The resealable individual pouches also get frequent shoutouts for keeping portions honest.

**Where it falls short**

The two-flavor limitation is the most common complaint. You're getting cheddar or chocolate chip — that's it. For families with multiple kids who have different preferences, the mix ratio can also feel lopsided. Several reviewers noted receiving mostly Bunny Grahams with fewer Cheddar Bunnies, which is frustrating if your kid only wants the cheese variety.

The flavor intensity is another consideration. If your family is used to bold, heavily seasoned snacks, the Cheddar Bunnies will taste muted by comparison. This is a feature for parents seeking milder flavors, but a genuine drawback for kids transitioning from mainstream cheese crackers.

**Who should buy this**

This pack is ideal for parents of 2-8 year olds who prioritize organic ingredients and sensible portion control. It's also solid for adults who want a clean, nostalgic snack for road trips or desk drawers. Skip it if your household prefers spicy, bold-flavored snacks or if you need more than two flavor options to keep everyone happy.

**FAQ**

*Are these gluten-free?*  
No. Both the Cheddar Bunnies and Bunny Grahams contain wheat flour. Annie's does make gluten-free versions of some crackers, but this variety pack is not among them.

*How many pouches come in the box?*  
The pack typically contains 12 to 14 individual 1-ounce pouches, though the exact count can vary slightly by batch.

*Are these suitable for school nut-free policies?*  
Yes. These are produced in facilities that handle wheat and milk, but there are no nut ingredients in either flavor. Always check the packaging for the most current allergen statement.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a clean, organic snack that keeps portions honest and kids happy. **Skip it if** you need bold flavors or more than two varieties to sustain interest.

This is a solid 4.6-star product that delivers exactly what it promises: wholesome, tasty snacks in sensible portions. The two-flavor limit and milder cheddar profile keep it from perfection, but for most families, this will become a lunchbox staple. At this price point, it's a worthwhile buy.
