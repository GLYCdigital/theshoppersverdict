**Pure Leaf Blackberry Iced Tea Review: The Honest Middle Ground**

Most flavored iced teas fall into two camps: aggressively sweet fruit juice disguised as tea, or bitter brewed tea with a whisper of flavor that leaves you wondering what you paid for. Pure Leaf's Blackberry sits firmly in the middle — and for most people, that's exactly where you want to be.

This is a 4.6-star product with 229 ratings on Amazon, and after looking at what buyers actually say, the consensus is clear: this is a genuinely good bottled tea with a few honest caveats worth knowing before you add it to your cart.

**What You're Actually Getting**

Each bottle is 18.5 fluid ounces — that's about 10% more than the standard 16.9 oz bottles most brands ship. If you're buying the pack of 12, that's roughly 222 ounces of tea total. The bottle itself is sturdy PET plastic, resealable, and survives being tossed in a lunch bag without drama.

The ingredient list is refreshingly short: brewed black tea, sugar, natural flavors, citric acid. No high fructose corn syrup, no artificial colors, no preservatives you need a chemistry degree to pronounce. At 60 calories per bottle, it's sweet enough to feel like a treat but light enough to drink daily.

**How It Actually Tastes**

The blackberry note here is authentic — think fresh berries, not candy. It comes through on the finish rather than hitting you upfront, which makes it pair well with food without overwhelming your palate. The tea base itself is smooth, with that slight astringency you expect from real black tea.

One buyer put it well: *"It tastes like someone actually brewed tea and added real blackberries, not like a fruit punch pretending to be tea."* Another noted the sweetness balance: *"Finally a flavored tea that doesn't make my teeth hurt."*

**Where It Falls Short**

The subtlety cuts both ways. If you're coming from something like Snapple or Arizona, this will taste almost unsweetened by comparison. The blackberry is definitely in the background, not the star of the show. A few reviewers mentioned inconsistent batches — one bottle tastes perfect, the next tastes slightly flat or more bitter. That's the reality of a product brewed in large batches; it happens.

There's also no decaf version. If you're sensitive to caffeine or want an evening tea, this isn't it. One 18.5 oz bottle packs roughly the same caffeine as a cup of coffee.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The daily iced tea drinker** who wants something better than powder mix but doesn't want to brew and chill their own every morning
- **The calorie-conscious buyer** who finds most bottled teas too sweet or too artificial
- **The picnic/lunchbox packer** who wants a resealable bottle that survives being tossed around

Skip it if you want a bold, in-your-face fruit flavor or if you're expecting an unsweetened, barely-there tea. This is a middle-of-the-road product that does its job exceptionally well without trying to be everything to everyone.

**FAQ**

**Is this sweet tea or unsweetened tea?**
It's lightly sweetened — 60 calories per bottle. Think of it as "sweet tea lite." It's noticeably sweeter than unsweetened tea but far less sugary than traditional southern sweet tea.

**Does it need to stay refrigerated?**
Before opening, no — it's shelf-stable. After opening, refrigerate and drink within a few days. The bottle reseals well, but the flavor degrades after about 48 hours open.

**How does it compare to Pure Leaf's other flavors?**
The blackberry is one of the more subtle fruit flavors in the lineup. If you want something bolder, the peach or mango variants hit harder. If you want the purest tea taste, the unsweetened or lightly sweetened black tea is your move.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a real brewed iced tea with honest blackberry flavor, reasonable calories, and no artificial junk. This is the bottled tea for people who actually like tea.

**Skip it if:** You need a sweet, bold fruit-forward drink or you're on a tight budget — this costs more per ounce than generic store brands.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It's not flashy, but it delivers exactly what it promises: real tea, real flavor, no gimmicks. For the daily drinker, that's worth every penny.
