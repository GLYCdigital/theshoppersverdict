Most dried cranberry products on Amazon are basically candy wearing a health halo. Ocean Spray's original Craisins pack 29 grams of added sugar per half-cup. That's why My Berry's 3oz pouch stands out: the label says "unsweetened" and the nutrition panel backs it up with 1g of naturally occurring sugar per serving. That's not a marketing trick — that's the entire cranberry, dried, with nothing else in the ingredient list.

If you've only ever had sweetened dried cranberries, prepare for a shock. These are tart. Bracingly tart. The kind of tart that makes your jaw tingle on the first handful. But here's the thing: that's what cranberries actually taste like. The flavor is clean, bright, and unmistakably authentic. As one verified buyer put it, "These are so tart they make my mouth pucker, but I love that I can eat them without feeling like I'm eating dessert." Another noted, "Perfect for my sugar-free diet. I add them to homemade granola and they're incredible."

The 3oz pouch is a double-edged sword. On one hand, it's the perfect trial size — you're not dropping $20 on a pound of something you might hate. The resealable zipper works well and keeps the berries from turning to leather between uses. On the other hand, if you do love them, you'll be reordering frequently. At roughly 3-4 servings per pouch depending on how generously you pour, a daily oatmeal habit will burn through this in under a week.

**Build and texture** are where these show their limitations. The cranberries are small and slightly irregular — a natural product, not a factory-perfect pellet. The texture leans chewy-leathery, closer to a dried apricot than a soft Craisin. Some buyers reported drier batches than others, which seems to be a consistency issue rather than a defect. If you're using them for baking, the dryness actually works in your favor — they rehydrate beautifully in muffins and quick breads. For snacking straight from the bag, you'll want to be okay with a bit of chew.

**Where they shine:**
- **Baking** — The tartness cuts through sweet batters perfectly. Fold them into scones or banana bread for a sophisticated contrast.
- **Trail mix** — Pair with almonds and dark chocolate; the tartness balances the richness.
- **Savory applications** — These are excellent in wild rice pilaf or chopped into a spinach salad with goat cheese.
- **Sugar-free and keto diets** — At 1g sugar per serving, they fit cleanly into strict macros.

**Where they fall short:**
- **Direct snacking** — If you're expecting sweet-tart Craisins, you will be disappointed. These are for people who genuinely enjoy sour flavors.
- **Portion size** — 3oz is small. It's a trial size, plain and simple. Frequent users should look for the 8oz or 16oz options.
- **Texture variability** — Batch-to-batch moisture levels vary more than I'd like.

**Who should buy this:** Keto and diabetic shoppers who miss dried fruit. Bakers who want unsweetened cranberries for recipes where added sugar would throw off ratios. Anyone who genuinely loves sour candy and wants a natural alternative. If you fit any of these profiles, these are a solid value.

**Who should skip it:** If you want a sweet dried cranberry for snacking, buy Craisins. If you need bulk quantities, skip the 3oz and go bigger. And if you're expecting soft, plump berries, manage expectations — these are chewy, not juicy.

**FAQ:**

**Q: Are these actually unsweetened, or is that just marketing?**
A: The ingredient list is literally one item: cranberries. No sugar, no apple juice concentrate, no stevia, no oils. The 1g of sugar per serving is naturally occurring in the fruit itself.

**Q: How should I soften them for baking?**
A: Soak them in hot water or orange juice for 10 minutes before adding to batters. They'll plump up nicely and won't dry out your baked goods.

**Q: Can I eat these on a keto diet?**
A: Yes. At 1g sugar and roughly 30 calories per serving, they fit into even strict ketogenic macros. Just mind portion size — they're calorie-dense relative to fresh berries.

**The Verdict:** These are a niche product done exceptionally well. My Berry delivers exactly what they promise — unsweetened, naturally tart dried cranberries in a convenient trial-size pouch. The 4.0-star rating reflects that some buyers expected a sweet product and got a sour one. That's not a product flaw; that's a labeling success.

**Buy it if** you want honest, unsweetened cranberries for baking or clean eating. **Skip it if** you want a sweet snack.
