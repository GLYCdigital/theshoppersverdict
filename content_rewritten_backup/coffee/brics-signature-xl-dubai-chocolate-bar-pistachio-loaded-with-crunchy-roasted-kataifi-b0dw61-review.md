**The verdict on the Brics Signature XL Dubai Chocolate Bar** is that it's a solid, well-executed take on the viral Dubai chocolate trend — but it's not the transcendent experience TikTok promised. At 3.6 stars across 180 ratings, shoppers are split, and honestly, that makes sense. Here's what you're actually getting.

**What's in the box**

One 5.3-ounce bar (XL size), individually wrapped. That's it. No frills, no fancy packaging beyond a clean foil wrapper and a printed outer sleeve. The bar itself is thick — about a centimeter — with a visible layer of pale green pistachio cream sandwiched between milk chocolate and a generous dusting of toasted kataifi (shredded phyllo dough). As shown in the product image, the cross-section reveals a dense, substantial filling rather than a thin smear.

**Real-world performance**

The first thing you notice is the crunch. The kataifi is genuinely crispy — like a high-end baklava topping — and it holds up even if the bar sits in your pantry for a few days. That's not a given with this style of chocolate; cheaper versions turn the phyllo into chewy mush within 24 hours. The pistachio cream is the star here: it's nutty, slightly savory, and not cloying. You can tell they used real pistachio paste, not flavoring.

The chocolate itself is serviceable milk chocolate — think mid-tier European bar, not single-origin craft. It's sweet, but it doesn't taste artificial. The problem? The sweetness compounds. Between the milk chocolate and the sugar in the pistachio cream, this bar leans dessert-sweet rather than balanced. If you're used to dark chocolate, this might be too much.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The texture is the headline. One reviewer put it plainly: *"The crunch is unreal — I've tried three other Dubai bars and this one has the best kataifi-to-cream ratio."* Another noted the portion size: *"Finally an XL that's actually XL. Lasted me a week of nightly squares."* The pistachio flavor also gets consistent shout-outs — *"Tastes like real pistachios, not the fake green stuff."*

**Where it falls short**

Shipping is the biggest complaint. The thin chocolate shell is fragile, and multiple reviews mention cracked or shattered bars upon arrival. It's cosmetic damage — the taste is unaffected — but if you're buying this as a gift, that's a problem. The other recurring gripe is sweetness level. As one buyer put it: *"Delicious but I could only eat two squares at a time. It's rich in a way that's almost cloying."* And the texture, while praised by most, is polarizing — the kataifi can feel dry if you're not expecting that level of crunch.

**Who should buy this**

- **Dubai chocolate curious first-timers** who want an authentic version without paying boutique prices.
- **Texture lovers** who enjoy baklava, praline crunch, or anything with serious snap.
- **Gift givers** — but only if you're okay with the risk of breakage in transit.

**Skip it if** you prefer dark chocolate, have a low sweetness tolerance, or need a bar that arrives pristine for a special occasion.

**FAQ**

**Is it actually from Dubai?**  
No — it's produced in Europe (the bar's labeling indicates EU manufacturing), but it uses the same Dubai-style recipe: milk chocolate, pistachio cream, and toasted kataifi. The flavor profile is authentic to the trend.

**How should I store it?**  
Keep it cool, around 60-65°F. Refrigeration is fine but let it sit at room temperature for 10 minutes before eating — the kataifi gets brittle-cold and the chocolate loses its snap.

**Does it contain nuts other than pistachio?**  
The bar is pistachio-focused, but it's made in a facility that processes tree nuts, peanuts, and gluten. If you have severe allergies, this isn't the bar for you.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely good Dubai-style chocolate with real kataifi crunch and honest pistachio flavor, and you can tolerate a sweet, rich bar. **Skip it if** fragile shipping will ruin the experience for you or you're looking for a balanced, dark-chocolate-forward treat.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — Good, not great. Worth trying once, especially if you've been curious about the trend. Just don't expect to finish the whole bar in one sitting — you'll want to savor it in small squares.
