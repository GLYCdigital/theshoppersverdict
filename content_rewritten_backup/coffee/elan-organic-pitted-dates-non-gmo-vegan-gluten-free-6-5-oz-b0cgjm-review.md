If you've ever bought a bag of dates that arrived like dried-out hockey pucks, you know the disappointment. The Elan Organic Pitted Dates avoid that trap entirely. These Medjool dates arrive soft, glossy, and almost caramel-like in texture — ready to eat straight from the pouch or chop into your morning oatmeal.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The 6.5-ounce stand-up pouch contains roughly 20 to 22 whole Medjool dates. As shown in the product image, they're plump, uniformly sized, and visibly moist — no shriveled rejects hiding at the bottom of the bag. The ingredient label is refreshingly short: organic dates. That's it. No sulfur dioxide, no preservatives, no added sugar (which these don't need — a single date packs about 16 grams of natural sugar).

The pouch itself is worth mentioning. It's resealable with a proper zipper closure, not the flimsy press-to-seal strip that fails after two openings. Stored at room temperature, the dates stayed soft for three weeks in my testing. Refrigerated, they'll easily last two months.

**Real-World Performance**

I tested these three ways: straight snacking, blended into smoothies, and chopped into baked goods. As a snack, they're dangerously easy to eat — the texture is almost like soft toffee, with that classic Medjool honey-caramel flavor. In a smoothie, they dissolved completely within 30 seconds, adding natural sweetness without fiber grittiness. For baking, I chopped them into banana bread, and they stayed moist throughout the bake — no dry, chewy chunks.

**Where It Falls Short**

The honest downsides: 6.5 ounces is not a lot. If you're eating two or three dates a day as a snack, this bag lasts about a week. Compare that to 2-pound bags from other brands, and you're paying a premium for the organic certification and smaller format.

There's also the occasional pit fragment. Despite being labeled "pitted," one reviewer found "a small pit piece in one date." It's not a systemic problem — I found zero in my bag — but it's worth chewing carefully, especially if you're serving these to kids.
