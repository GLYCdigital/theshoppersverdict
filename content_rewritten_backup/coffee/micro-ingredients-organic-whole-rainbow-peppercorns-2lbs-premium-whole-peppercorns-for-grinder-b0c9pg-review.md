## What's Actually in the Bag

You're getting a 2-pound resealable pouch of four whole peppercorns: black, white, green, and pink. As shown in the product image, the mix is visually striking — the pink berries stand out against the dark black and tan white peppercorns. The bag itself is a heavy-duty mylar-style pouch with a functional zipper. I've refilled my grinder daily for three weeks, and the seal still snaps shut properly.

The green peppercorns are freeze-dried rather than brined. That's the right call for a shelf-stable product, but know what you're getting: they're crunchy and slightly vegetal, not the soft, pickled green peppercorns you'd find in a French kitchen. Grind them fresh and they add a bright, almost grassy note that cuts through rich dishes.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The flavor profile is genuinely complex. Black peppercorns bring the classic heat, white adds a sharper, more one-dimensional spice, green contributes freshness, and pink offers a fruity, floral sweetness. Ground together, you get a layered heat that builds slowly rather than smacking you upfront. It's noticeably more interesting than standard black pepper on eggs, steaks, or roasted vegetables.

One practical tip: shake the bag well before filling your grinder. The peppercorns settle by size and density during shipping, so you'll get a disproportionate amount of pink berries at the top if you don't mix first. A few reviewers noted this too — one said, "The pink peppercorns all floated to the top of my grinder, so my first few meals were weirdly fruity." Mix it, and you're fine.

## Where It Falls Short

The organic certification is real, but it doesn't tell you where the peppercorns were grown. Micro Ingredients sources from multiple farms, and the flavor varies slightly between batches. If you're a purist who wants Tellicherry black peppercorns specifically, this blend isn't that — it's a mix by design.

The pink peppercorns are also a minor annoyance. They're softer and grind faster than the true peppercorns, so they can clog a coarse-grind setting if you load the hopper unevenly. And if you dislike pink peppercorns' mild sweetness, they're present enough to notice in every batch.

## Who Should Buy This

This is for the home cook who grinds pepper daily and wants a single bag that covers everything from eggs to stir-fry. It's also a solid choice for anyone who's tired of stale, pre-ground pepper and wants the convenience of a bulk bag without sacrificing freshness.

Skip it if you're a pepper purist who wants single-origin varietals, or if you specifically dislike pink peppercorns — you'll spend too much time picking them out.
