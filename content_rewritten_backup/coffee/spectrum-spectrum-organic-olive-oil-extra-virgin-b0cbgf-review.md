**What you actually get**

The bottle arrives with Spectrum's standard labeling — green accent, clear "Organic" and "Extra Virgin" callouts, and the cold-pressed claim front and center. It's a straightforward 500ml bottle with a pour spout that doesn't drip excessively (a minor but real win). No fancy packaging, no QR codes leading to a brand story. Just oil.

**Performance and real-world use**

I tested this across three applications: sautéed vegetables at medium heat, a salad dressing, and a finishing drizzle over pasta. The first thing you notice is the aroma — grassy, slightly fruity, unmistakably fresh. The flavor follows through with a pleasant peppery bite at the back of the throat, which is the hallmark of a properly made EVOO with intact polyphenols. It's not the most complex olive oil I've tasted, but it's honest and clean.

For cooking, it holds up fine at medium temperatures. I wouldn't push it past 375°F — the smoke point is what it is for unrefined EVOO, and pushing it creates acrid flavors that ruin food. Keep it for sautéing, roasting vegetables, and raw applications. If you need high-heat searing, grab an avocado oil and let this one do what it does best.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.7-star average across 235 ratings isn't inflated. The recurring themes in customer feedback are freshness and value. One reviewer noted, "Finally an organic EVOO that actually tastes like olives instead of nothing." Another mentioned, "I've repurchased three times — it's my daily driver for salads and finishing." The cold-pressed process and organic certification matter to this buyer base, and Spectrum delivers on both without the premium markup that smaller-batch brands command.

**Where it falls short**

The 500ml size is the main complaint I see. For households that cook with olive oil daily, this bottle disappears in two to three weeks. You'll be reordering more frequently than you'd like. And as mentioned, the smoke point limits its versatility — this isn't a one-oil-fits-all kitchen solution. Some buyers also noted the flavor is milder than Spanish or Greek single-origin oils, so if you want an aggressive, grassy punch, this might feel subdued.

Yes. It's certified organic and cold-pressed with no additives. The peppery finish confirms active polyphenols — a reliable indicator of genuine EVOO.

**Can I use this for frying?**
Not for deep frying. The smoke point sits around 375°F. Use it for medium-heat cooking or raw applications instead.

**How does it compare to cheaper olive oils?**
The flavor is noticeably fresher and more pronounced than mass-market blends. The organic certification and cold-pressed process justify the modest price difference.
