## FAQ

**Q: Are these the same as organic red lentils?**
A: No. Organic certification covers the entire farming process. CLEAR CREEK only tests for glyphosate residue. They're a different standard—not better or worse, just different.

**Q: Do they need to be rinsed before cooking?**
A: Yes, rinse them regardless of brand. Even clean lentils have dust and starch on the surface. Rinsing also reduces foaming during cooking.

**Q: How long do they stay fresh?**
A: Stored in a cool, dry place in the resealable bag, expect 12–18 months. They'll last longer in an airtight glass jar. Watch for fading color—that's the first sign of age.
