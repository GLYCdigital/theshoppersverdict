**FAQ**

**Q: Are these roasted or raw?**
A: Raw and unsalted. They're natural, unroasted nuts — you get the pure, slightly sweet flavor of each variety. If you want roasted, you'll need to do it yourself (10 minutes in the oven at 350°F works well).

**Q: How long do they stay fresh?**
A: In a cool, dry pantry, about 4-6 weeks. For longer storage, refrigerate in an airtight container — they'll keep for 3-4 months, or up to a year in the freezer.

**Q: Do I need a special tool to crack them?**
A: A standard nutcracker works for almonds, hazelnuts, and pecans. Walnuts and Brazil nuts have harder shells — you may need a pair of pliers or a heavier-duty cracker for those.
