Let's be direct: you're not buying this for the nutrition facts, and you don't need me to pretend otherwise. The question is whether GHIRARDELLI's holiday assortment justifies the shelf space and the price tag — especially when every drugstore in America is pushing peppermint bark this time of year. After working through a full box (purely for research, obviously), here's the honest breakdown.

**What's actually in the box**

You're getting 24 pieces total, divided across three varieties. The peppermint bark is the headliner — dark and milk chocolate layered with crushed candy cane pieces, delivering that proper snap when you break it. The Dubai-style milk chocolate with pistachio is the wildcard: it's a nod to the viral "Dubai chocolate" trend, with green pistachio filling folded into milk chocolate. The third is a caramel square, which is the crowd-pleaser that disappears fastest at any gathering.

As shown in the product image, the box itself is genuinely handsome — dark green with gold accents, sturdy enough to survive shipping without crushed corners. It's a 10.6-ounce total weight, which works out to roughly 0.44 ounces per piece. That's slightly smaller than standard GHIRARDELLI squares, so adjust your expectations on serving size.

**How it actually tastes**

The peppermint bark is the standout. The mint flavor is assertive without tasting like toothpaste, and the candy cane pieces retain real crunch rather than dissolving into soggy bits. The chocolate-to-mint ratio is well-balanced — you taste the chocolate first, mint second, which is how it should be.

The Dubai-style pistachio square is interesting but imperfect. The pistachio is finely chopped rather than a smooth paste, which gives it a mealy texture against the milk chocolate. It's pleasant, but if you've had authentic Dubai chocolate with that stretchy pistachio cream filling, this won't scratch that itch. It's sweeter than the peppermint bark, too — noticeably so.

The caramel squares are the utility player here: reliable, buttery, no surprises. They round out the assortment nicely and give non-mint-lovers something to reach for.

**What buyers consistently say**

With 229 ratings averaging 4.6 out of 5, the consensus is clear. One reviewer put it well: "The peppermint bark alone is worth it — I've bought this three times this season." Another noted, "Great value for the variety, and the box doesn't look cheap like some holiday chocolates." The few negative reviews cluster around two themes: shipping in warm weather causing melting, and disappointment that the pistachio squares aren't as rich as the viral Dubai chocolate trend suggests.

**Where it falls short**

The Dubai-style squares won't satisfy anyone chasing that specific trend — it's a loose interpretation, not a faithful recreation. The peppermint bark pieces being smaller than the other squares is a minor annoyance, especially if you're trying to split the box evenly among guests. And if you're buying this as a gift in warm climates, note that chocolate ships poorly above 70°F — several reviewers reported melted pieces.

**Who should buy this**

This is an ideal pick for hostess gifts, office Secret Santa exchanges, or as a nicer-than-drugstore option on your own holiday table. It's also a smart choice if you're catering to mixed tastes — the three varieties cover mint lovers, nut lovers, and caramel traditionalists. Skip it if you're specifically hunting for authentic Dubai chocolate, or if you need individually labeled pieces for allergy-sensitive guests.

**FAQ**

*Does the box list allergen information?* Yes, on the outer packaging. Milk, soy, and tree nuts (pistachio) are present. Individual pieces aren't labeled, so keep the outer box if allergies are a concern.

*How should I store these?* Cool, dry place under 65°F. The peppermint bark will soften quickly at room temperature. Refrigeration is fine but let pieces sit out 5 minutes before eating.

*Is this a good value?* At roughly $1 per piece, it's priced above grocery-store chocolates but below premium artisanal brands. You're paying for GHIRARDELLI's consistent quality and the attractive packaging.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, giftable, crowd-pleasing holiday chocolate assortment with one genuinely excellent star (the peppermint bark) and two solid supporting players. **Skip it if** you're chasing the authentic Dubai chocolate trend, or if you need individually wrapped pieces for gifting.

The peppermint bark alone earns this box its keep — and at 4.6 stars, most shoppers agree it's a worthwhile seasonal splurge.
