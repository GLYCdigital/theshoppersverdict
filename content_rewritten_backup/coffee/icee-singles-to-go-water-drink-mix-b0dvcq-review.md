Let's be honest: most "make-your-own slushie" products are a gimmick. You end up with vaguely sweet ice cubes that taste nothing like the real thing. The ICEE Singles To Go Water Drink Mix is different — and that difference matters if you've got a serious frozen drink habit.

This is a single-serve powdered mix that turns an 8-ounce cup of water into an ICEE-style slushie after several hours in the freezer. It's not a drink mix you sip — it's a freeze-and-scrape situation. And based on 220 Amazon reviews averaging 4.4 stars, shoppers are mostly impressed. Here's the real breakdown.

**What's in the box**

Each order includes multiple single-serve packets (the count varies by listing, but you're getting individual portions, not a bulk canister). Each packet contains the dry mix — sugar, flavoring, and the proprietary "slushie magic" that prevents the water from freezing solid. You add cold water to a cup, stir in the powder, and freeze.

The packaging is straightforward: tear-open packets, no measuring required. The product image shows the classic ICEE branding, so you know exactly what you're getting. Available flavors include Blue Raspberry, Cherry, and a few rotating options.

**How it actually performs**

Here's the honest truth: this works, but it demands patience. The instructions call for 8+ hours in the freezer. If you're expecting a 20-minute slushie, you'll be disappointed. But if you prep it before bed or in the morning, you'll have a legitimately frosty, scoopable slushie by afternoon.

The texture is the standout feature. Unlike typical freeze-your-own drinks that turn into a solid block, the mix keeps the ice crystals suspended and loose. You end up with that signature ICEE graininess — the fine, snow-like texture rather than chunky ice. One reviewer noted, "It actually tastes like the ICEE from the movie theater, which I didn't expect from a powder packet."

The flavor is sweet and tart, matching the original ICEE taste closely. The Blue Raspberry is the crowd favorite, with Cherry running a close second. It's not a subtle drink — it's candy-sweet and full of artificial flavor, which is exactly what ICEE fans want.

**What shoppers consistently praise**

The nostalgia factor is huge. Multiple reviewers mentioned the taste brings back childhood memories of ballpark visits and mall food courts. The portion control also gets love — each packet makes one serving, so you're not tempted to overdo it.

One Amazon reviewer said, "My kids think I'm a wizard. They ask for these every single day after school." Another mentioned, "I've tried other slushie mixes and they all turn into ice blocks. This one genuinely stays slushy."

**Where it falls short**

First, the yield is small. Each packet makes about 8 ounces — that's a short glass, not a big ICEE cup. If you're used to the 32-ounce theater size, you'll need several packets per person. At the per-packet price, that adds up fast for a family.

Second, the texture is temperamental. If your freezer runs cold, you'll get a solid block instead of a scoopable slushie. The instructions suggest pulling it out and letting it sit for a few minutes if that happens, but it's still a minor annoyance. One reviewer complained, "I followed the directions exactly and it froze solid. My freezer is just too cold for this to work."

Third, the nutrition profile isn't great — these are essentially sugar with flavoring. Each serving packs a solid sugar hit, which is fine for an occasional treat but not something you want daily.

**Who should buy this**

This is perfect for parents of ICEE-obsessed kids who want a cheaper, at-home alternative to constant concession stand stops. It's also great for anyone who hosts parties or movie nights and wants a fun, novel drink option. If you're a slushie purist who loves the texture of real ICEE, this delivers.

Skip it if you're looking for an instant drink mix (this requires hours of freezing), if you're watching sugar intake, or if you need big batches for a crowd without spending on multiple packets.

**FAQ**

**How long does it actually take to freeze?**
Plan for 8 hours minimum. Overnight is ideal. Some reviewers reported success at 6 hours with a deep freezer set to 0°F, but the safe bet is overnight.

**Can I use sparkling water or juice instead of plain water?**
Stick with plain cold water. Carbonation loses its fizz during freezing, and juice changes the sugar balance, which affects the slushie texture.

**Is this a drink or a frozen treat?**
It's frozen. You eat it with a spoon or thick straw after freezing. It doesn't work as a regular beverage mix.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want authentic ICEE flavor at home and don't mind planning ahead. It's a fun, nostalgic treat that delivers on taste and texture when you follow the instructions.

**Skip it if** you need instant gratification, have an ultra-cold freezer, or want a budget option for frequent consumption — the per-serving cost is steep compared to standard drink mixes.
