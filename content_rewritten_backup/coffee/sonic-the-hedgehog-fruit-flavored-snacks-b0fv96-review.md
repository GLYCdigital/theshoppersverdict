If you’ve ever watched a kid tear open a fruit snack pouch, you know the ritual: shake, dump, sort by color, eat the blue ones first. Sonic the Hedgehog Fruit Flavored Snacks lean hard into that nostalgia — but they’re not just another licensed sugar bomb. After 240 Amazon ratings averaging 4.8 stars, these gummies have staying power. Here’s what’s actually in the pouch.

**What you get:** Five 0.9-ounce pouches per box, each packed with roughly 12-15 gummies. The shapes aren't generic bears — you get Sonic’s spiky head, Tails’ twin tails, and Knuckles’ fist, all molded with surprising detail. The product image doesn't exaggerate: the blue raspberry Sonic pieces actually look like him, right down to the eye cutout. The texture is firm but chewy, not the gummy-schmear you get from cheaper brands that melt in your pocket.

**Flavor breakdown:** The blue raspberry is the star — sharp, sweet, and unmistakably "blue." The green apple Tails pieces taste like a Jolly Rancher's softer cousin, while the orange Knuckles pieces are mild, almost like a Creamsicle that forgot its cream. One buyer noted, "My 6-year-old ate the Tails ones first because they taste like the green Jolly Ranchers, then traded the orange ones to his sister." That tracks. The fruit juice concentrate (apple, pear, and grape) is listed first, which explains why these don't have that artificial aftertaste cheap gummies leave behind.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The detail on the molds is the recurring theme. "I bought these for my 9-year-old who's Sonic-obsessed," one review reads, "and he refused to eat the Sonic ones for a week because they 'look too cool.'" Another parent appreciated that they're individually wrapped in pouches, not a single resealable bag — better for lunchboxes and portion control. The lack of high-fructose corn syrup also earned mentions from health-conscious shoppers.

**Where it falls short:** The price-to-quantity ratio stings. At roughly $3.99 per box, you're paying a licensing premium — about 20% more per ounce than a store-brand equivalent. And if you're expecting a variety of distinct flavors, adjust expectations. The blue raspberry is the loudest note; the green apple and orange are quieter, almost as if they're sharing a flavor profile. One critical review summed it up: "Great gummies, but my kid finished the box in two days. That's $2 a day for snacks."

**Who should buy this:** This is a gift purchase first, a snack purchase second. Buy it for the Sonic-obsessed 5-to-10-year-old who'll appreciate the character shapes more than the taste. Buy it for the adult collector who wants recognizable molds for a themed party. Skip it if you just need cheap fruit snacks for the pantry — you'll get more value from a generic brand.

**FAQ:**

*Are these gluten-free?* Yes. The packaging clearly states gluten-free, and the ingredient list shows no wheat or barley derivatives. They're also kosher-certified.

*Do they contain real fruit juice?* Yes — apple, pear, and grape juice concentrates are the first liquid ingredients. But don't expect the nutritional profile of actual fruit; these are still gummies with added sugar.

*How long do they stay fresh?* Sealed pouches hold up well past the best-by date. One buyer reported eating them 4 months past expiration with no texture change. Opened, they'll go stale in 2-3 days.

**The Verdict:** Buy it if you want a character-driven snack that actually tastes good and has cleaner ingredients than most licensed candies. Skip it if you're on a budget or expect flavor variety that goes beyond "blue raspberry plus two supporting players."

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The molds, texture, and honest fruit juice make this a standout in the licensed snack aisle. The price and flavor hierarchy keep it from perfection.
