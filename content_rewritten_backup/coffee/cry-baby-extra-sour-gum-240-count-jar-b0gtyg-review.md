# Cry Baby Extra Sour Gum: The 240-Count Jar That Packs a Punch

Let's be honest — you're not here because you're curious about gum. You're here because you or someone you love is a sour candy masochist who's tired of paying $1.50 for a tiny 15-piece box at the checkout lane. The Cry Baby Extra Sour Gum 240-count jar is the bulk-buy answer to that problem, and at roughly 11 standard boxes of gum in one container, the value proposition is immediately obvious.

## What You Actually Get

The jar contains 240 individually wrapped pieces of Cry Baby Extra Sour Gum — the classic neon-yellow, rectangular pieces that have been making kids cry (and adults wince) since the 1980s. Each piece is wrapped in the familiar yellow-and-black packaging, making this ideal for party favors, pinatas, or just keeping a steady supply in your desk drawer.

The jar itself is a standard plastic container that's about the size of a large peanut butter jar. It's not fancy, but it does the job — the pieces are packed in tightly, and the lid seals securely.

## The Sour Experience

Here's where Cry Baby earns its name. The initial bite hits you with a malic acid coating that's genuinely intense — this isn't the "sweet-then-slightly-sour" of Sour Patch Kids. This is a face-scrunching, eye-watering, "why am I doing this to myself" level of sour that lasts through the first few minutes of chewing.

What's impressive is that the sourness doesn't vanish after the first 10 seconds. The coating is generous enough that you get a solid 2-3 minutes of tartness before it settles into a sweet, fruity bubble gum flavor that's surprisingly pleasant. The chew itself is firm and holds up well — it's not the kind of gum that turns to mush in 30 seconds.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be real about the downsides. **The sugar content is the elephant in the room.** Each piece has 3g of sugar, which means a 10-piece binge session (not uncommon for sour candy fans) is 30g of added sugar — that's the American Heart Association's entire daily limit for adult men. This is not a health food, and it's not a gum you should be chewing constantly throughout the day.

The sour coating can also be physically irritating. If you chew more than a few pieces back-to-back, the malic acid can start to wear on the roof of your mouth and your tongue. Several reviews mention this — it's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth pacing yourself.

The jar lid is also child-proof, which is smart for safety but genuinely annoying when you're trying to grab a piece one-handed while driving or working.

## FAQ

**Is this gum actually extra sour, or is it just marketing?**
It's genuinely extra sour. The malic acid coating is intense on the first bite and lingers for several minutes. If you're someone who finds Warheads too mild, this is in the same league.

**How long does a jar last?**
With 240 pieces, you're looking at roughly 24 servings if you chew 10 pieces at a time (which is a lot). For most people, a jar lasts 2-3 months of regular chewing.

**Can I share this or is it too much for one person?**
It's perfect for sharing. The individual wrappers keep pieces fresh, and the jar is ideal for parties, classrooms, or office candy bowls.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're a genuine sour candy fan who wants the best per-piece value on the market and you're okay with the sugar content. The 240-piece count, intense sour kick, and fresh individually wrapped pieces make this a solid 4.6/5 purchase.
