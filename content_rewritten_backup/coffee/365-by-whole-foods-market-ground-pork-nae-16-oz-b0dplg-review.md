**FAQ**

**Is this the same as organic ground pork?**  
No. NAE means no antibiotics ever; organic covers that plus organic feed and other standards. This is NAE, not certified organic. Check the label if organic is your requirement.

**How long does it last in the fridge?**  
The sell-by date is usually 3–5 days out from delivery. If you won't cook it within two days, freeze it immediately — it holds for up to three months.

**Does it taste different from regular ground pork?**  
It's milder and cleaner. If you're used to stronger-flavored conventional pork, this will taste noticeably fresher, especially in simple preparations like patties.
