I'll be honest upfront: I didn't expect a store-brand cashew to nail Thai curry flavor. But 365 by Whole Foods Market's Thai Curry Cashews have earned their 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews — and after tasting them, I get it. This isn't a timid "hint of curry" snack. The seasoning hits you with coconut, lemongrass, and a gentle warmth that lingers.

**What you actually get**

The 12-ounce bag is generous — roughly three cups of whole cashews. As shown in the product image, these aren't the sad, broken pieces you find in bargain bins. The nuts are uniformly sized, lightly glazed with a golden-amber coating that clings evenly without leaving your fingers a sticky mess. The resealable zipper actually works, which matters when you're grazing over two weeks.

**Flavor and texture breakdown**

The first thing you notice is sweetness — palm sugar or similar — followed by coconut cream richness, then a slow-building curry spice that never crosses into heat territory. Lemongrass and kaffir lime come through on the finish, giving it that authentic Thai profile rather than generic "curry powder" dust. Texture-wise, the cashews maintain their snap. The glaze adds a slight tackiness that dissolves quickly, then gives way to the nut's natural buttery crunch.

Reviewers consistently mention the same thing. One shopper wrote, "These taste like they came from a fancy spice shop, not a grocery store brand." Another noted, "I've bought four bags already — dangerous to keep in the house." The consistency of whole nuts impressed multiple buyers, with one commenting, "Finally a brand that doesn't fill the bag with crumbs."

**Where it falls short**

The sweetness is the main divider. If you're expecting a savory, roasted-curry experience, the sugar-forward glaze might surprise you. It's closer to a Thai-inspired snack mix than a savory roasted nut. Several reviewers did flag this — one said, "Delicious but sweeter than I expected; more like candied curry." Also, the heat is genuinely mild. If you want fire, this isn't it. The spice is aromatic, not piquant. Finally, at roughly $8-10 depending on your region, it's pricier than plain cashews — you're paying for the seasoning, and it shows.

**Who should buy this**

- **Thai food lovers** who want that coconut-curry profile in a portable snack
- **Trail mix builders** looking for a flavored nut that isn't ranch or honey-roasted
- **Office snackers** who need something satisfying that won't leave greasy residue on keyboards
- **Gift-givers** assembling snack baskets — the bag looks premium enough to include

Skip it if you want spicy nuts, prefer savory-only seasonings, or are watching sugar intake. The glaze adds real sweetness per serving.

**FAQ**

**Are these actually spicy?**
No. The curry flavor is aromatic and warm, with a mild tingle at most. Think Thai-inspired seasoning, not chili-forward heat.

**Do they contain tree nut allergens beyond cashews?**
The packaging notes they're processed in a facility that handles other tree nuts and peanuts. If you have severe allergies, check the label before purchasing.

**How long do they stay fresh after opening?**
The resealable bag keeps them crunchy for 2-3 weeks if stored in a cool, dry place. Beyond that, the glaze can soften slightly.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely flavorful, high-quality flavored cashew with authentic Thai character and solid crunch. **Skip it if** sweetness in savory snacks is a dealbreaker or you need serious heat.

This is one of the better store-brand flavored nuts I've tried — the ingredient quality and seasoning balance justify the premium over generic curry cashews. At 4.6 stars with 229 ratings, the consensus holds: these are a worthy pantry staple for adventurous snackers.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A flavorful, well-executed snack with minor caveats around sweetness and heat level.
