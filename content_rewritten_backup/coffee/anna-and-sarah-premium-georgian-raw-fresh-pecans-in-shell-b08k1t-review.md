## FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Do these taste noticeably fresher than supermarket pecans?**
A: Yes. The raw, unroasted flavor is distinctly sweet with that characteristic buttery undertone. Supermarket pecans often taste flat or slightly stale by comparison.

**Q: How should I store them after opening?**
A: Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate. Raw nuts contain oils that go rancid at room temperature, especially in warmer climates. They'll keep 3-4 months refrigerated, longer frozen.

**Q: Are these suitable for baking?**
A: Absolutely. The clean cracking means you'll get usable halves for decorating, and the sweet flavor carries through baking well. Toast them first for deeper flavor in recipes.
