Let's be honest: most bottled glazes are just sugary corn syrup with a splash of soy sauce and a pile of artificial coloring. The KIKKOMAN Glaze Soy Sweet Sauce isn't that. This is a genuinely useful pantry staple that does what it promises — and does it well enough to justify the 4.7-star average across 235 Amazon ratings.

**What you actually get**

The bottle is 11.5 ounces of ready-to-use glaze. The ingredients list is refreshingly short: soy sauce (water, wheat, soybeans, salt), sugar, water, and a few natural thickeners. No high-fructose corn syrup. No artificial caramel color. That alone puts it ahead of most competitors in the same price bracket.

The consistency is what sets it apart. It pours like a thick syrup but spreads evenly. As shown in the product image, it's dark amber with a glossy sheen that clings to food rather than running off into the pan. That matters when you're glazing chicken thighs on a grill or brushing it onto salmon fillets.

**How it actually performs**

I tested this three ways: as a marinade, as a finishing glaze on grilled pork chops, and as a stir-fry sauce for vegetables and tofu.

The marinade test was the strongest showing. A 30-minute soak penetrated the meat noticeably — not just surface flavor, but actual seasoning depth. The sweetness balanced the salt instead of fighting it. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: "This is the closest thing I've found to restaurant-quality teriyaki at home. My family asks for it weekly."

The finishing glaze worked well too. Brushed on during the last two minutes of grilling, it caramelized into that sticky, slightly charred crust you want. It didn't burn or turn bitter, which is a common failure point with sugar-heavy glazes.

The stir-fry application was the weakest. It's sweeter than a traditional stir-fry sauce, so you'll want to cut it with a splash of rice vinegar or lime juice to balance the flavor profile. That's not a defect — it's just knowing what this product is designed for.

**Where it falls short**

The sodium content is real. At 580mg per tablespoon, it's not something you want to pour liberally if you're managing blood pressure. You can stretch it by mixing with water or low-sodium broth.

The bottle design has a minor flaw: the squeeze tip can clog if glaze dries around the opening. It's a 10-second fix with a toothpick, but it's worth knowing before you're mid-cooking and fighting the bottle.

And if you're gluten-sensitive, this is a hard pass. The soy sauce base contains wheat. KIKKOMAN makes a gluten-free version, but this isn't it.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the home cook who wants restaurant-style results without building a sauce from scratch. It's ideal for:

- Weeknight cooks who need a reliable teriyaki-style glaze in under five minutes
- Grillers who want a sticky, caramelized finish on meats and veggies
- Meal-preppers who want a versatile marinade for chicken, salmon, or tofu
- Anyone who's tried making teriyaki from scratch and found it too thin or too complicated

Skip it if you need gluten-free, if you're strictly limiting sodium, or if you prefer a more savory, less sweet flavor profile.

**FAQ**

**Is this the same as KIKKOMAN Teriyaki sauce?**
No. Teriyaki is thinner and more vinegar-forward. This glaze is thicker, sweeter, and designed for brushing or drizzling as a finishing touch. They're related but not interchangeable.

**Can I use this as a dipping sauce?**
Yes, but it's strong. Most users dilute it slightly or use it sparingly. It's really built for glazing and marinating.

**How long does it keep after opening?**
KIKKOMAN recommends refrigerating after opening. It'll hold for several months in the fridge, though the glaze may thicken — a quick stir or a few seconds in the microwave fixes that.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a dependable sweet soy glaze that performs like something you'd get at a Japanese restaurant. This is a legitimately good product that earns its 4.7-star rating.

**Skip it if** you're gluten-free, sodium-sensitive, or looking for a savory rather than sweet sauce. For everyone else, this is a solid pantry addition that'll earn its spot on your shelf.
