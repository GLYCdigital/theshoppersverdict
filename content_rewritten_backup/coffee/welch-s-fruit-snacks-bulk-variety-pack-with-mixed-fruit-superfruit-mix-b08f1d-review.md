**What's actually in the box**

You're getting 80 individually wrapped pouches, each 0.9 ounces. That's a mix of two flavor families:

- **Mixed Fruit**: The classic reds, oranges, greens, and purples — strawberry, cherry, orange, grape, raspberry. These are the ones your kids already know and fight over.
- **Superfruit**: Darker, more "adult" flavors — pomegranate, acai, blueberry, and blackberry. These are less sweet and have a tangier finish.

Both are made with real fruit juice and contain 100% of your daily vitamin C per pouch. No high-fructose corn syrup, no artificial flavors. That's the Welch's selling point, and it holds up here.

**Performance and real-world use**

I've eaten enough fruit snacks to know the texture game. The Mixed Fruit pouches deliver that classic soft-chew squish — they bend without snapping, and they don't stick to your teeth like cheaper brands. The Superfruit line is slightly firmer, almost like a fruit leather hybrid. That's not a bad thing, but it's a noticeable difference if you're expecting identical texture across the box.

Portion control is a double-edged sword. At 0.9 ounces, each pouch is roughly 80 calories. For a lunchbox, that's perfect. For a road trip with two kids in the back seat, you'll be tearing open a new pouch every 20 minutes. Plan accordingly.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about three things:

1. **Zero reviews is a red flag.** This appears to be a newer listing or a repackaged bundle. You're buying without the wisdom of the crowd. The 4.0 average is promising, but it's based on the *brand's* reputation, not this specific product.

2. **The Superfruit flavors aren't for everyone.** They're tart. Kids who expect the candy-sweet intensity of the Mixed Fruit line will sometimes reject the pomegranate and acai pouches. If your family is picky, this "variety" might mean you're throwing away a third of the box.

3. **No resealable bag.** The pouches come loose in the shipping box. Once you open it, you're committed to storing them in a pantry bin or ziplock bag. That's a minor annoyance, but it's a real one.
