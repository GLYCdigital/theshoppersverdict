## FAQ: What Shoppers Ask

**Q: Is Rumford cornstarch the same as baking powder?**
No. Rumford makes both, but they're entirely different products. Cornstarch is a pure thickener; baking powder is a leavening agent. Don't swap them.

**Q: Can I use this for frying?**
Yes, but it's not ideal as a standalone coating. It works best combined with flour or rice flour for crispness. It's excellent in batters though.

**Q: Does non-GMO cornstarch taste different?**
In blind tests, most people can't tell a difference. Rumford's version has a notably clean, neutral flavor profile.
