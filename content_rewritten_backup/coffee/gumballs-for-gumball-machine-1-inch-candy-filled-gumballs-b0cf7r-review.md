### FAQ: What Shoppers Ask

**Q: Will these fit my vintage gumball machine?**  
Yes, if your machine takes standard 1-inch gumballs. Measure the opening—if it’s a modern machine, you’re fine. Antique machines sometimes use 5/8-inch or 27mm balls, so verify first.

**Q: How long do they stay fresh in the machine?**  
In a sealed globe, about 2-3 months before they start to harden. In the resealable bag, up to a year in a cool, dry place.

**Q: Are they gluten-free or allergen-safe?**  
The hard shell and gum base are typically gluten-free, but they’re processed in facilities that handle nuts and soy. Not suitable for severe allergies.
