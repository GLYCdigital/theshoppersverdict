**What You Actually Get**

The package contains the classic Now and Later chews—those dense, fruity, surprisingly tough little rectangles that have been around since 1962. You’re getting the standard assortment of flavors (cherry, strawberry, grape, green apple, and lemon-lime), each individually wrapped. The texture is the whole point: these are *chewy* in the way taffy wishes it was. They start firm, soften with heat or patience, and deliver a long-lasting flavor punch that doesn’t fade after two seconds like most gummy candies.

**Performance and Build Quality (Yes, Really)**

Here’s where it gets interesting for the coffee crowd. Now and Laters are not just candy—they’re a functional tool for anyone who drinks bitter, black coffee. The dense chew gives your jaw a workout, and the fruit acidity cuts through coffee’s bitterness better than any sugar packet. One Amazon reviewer put it succinctly: *“I keep these on my desk next to my French press. **What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.6-star rating isn’t an accident. Across the reviews, three themes dominate:

1. **Longevity per piece** – Buyers repeatedly mention how long one chew lasts, making the bag go further than expected.

**Where It Falls Short**

Be honest with yourself before buying. These are *hard* chews. If you have dental work, a sensitive jaw, or TMJ issues, this is not the candy for you. Multiple reviewers noted that the first few minutes of chewing are genuinely strenuous. Also, the wrapper situation is annoying—the paper sticks to the candy, especially if it’s been sitting in a warm car. You’ll need to peel carefully or you’ll lose half the piece. Finally, if you prefer soft, gummy-textured candy, this will disappoint. It’s not a beginner chew.
