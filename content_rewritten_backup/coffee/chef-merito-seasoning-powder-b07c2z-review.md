## FAQ

**Q: Is this just taco seasoning?**
No. Taco seasoning typically includes oregano and more chili powder. This is more of an all-purpose Mexican table salt-style seasoning. It works on tacos, but also eggs, rice, and grilled vegetables.

**Q: How long does it stay fresh?**
Stored in a cool, dry place, dry spices like this typically last 6-12 months. The shaker lid helps keep moisture out.

**Q: Can I use it as a dry rub?**
Yes, but go light. Because of the salt content, it can easily overpower a dry rub. Mix it with other spices if you're doing a full rub.
