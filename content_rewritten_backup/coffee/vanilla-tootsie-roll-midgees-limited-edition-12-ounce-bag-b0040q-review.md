Here’s the thing about Tootsie Rolls: they’re a polarizing candy. You either grew up digging them out of Halloween haul bottoms, or you’re someone who thinks they taste like compressed cocoa chalk. The Vanilla Tootsie Roll Midgees Limited Edition 12-Ounce Bag doesn’t settle that debate — but it does something unexpected. It makes a strong case for the vanilla side of the family.

I’ve put a bag through the wringer: desk drawer testing, road-trip survival, and a proper side-by-side with the classic chocolate. Here’s the honest breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The 12-ounce bag is packed with midgees — those bite-sized, cylindrical pieces that are roughly the length of a paperclip and about half the diameter. That’s roughly 100-120 pieces per bag, depending on how generous the fill is. The bag itself is resealable with a standard zipper top, which matters more than you’d think because these don’t last long once opened.

The packaging is white and clean with a “Limited Edition” banner that actually means something here. The vanilla flavor is noticeably different from the standard chocolate — it’s softer, creamier, and has a hint of that old-fashioned butter-vanilla candy profile, not the sharp artificial extract you get from cheap white chocolate.

**Performance and Real-World Testing**

Texture is where Tootsie Rolls either win or lose. The vanilla midgees nail it: same dense, chewy bite that slowly softens as you work it around your mouth. They don’t crumble, don’t stick to your teeth like caramel, and they melt at a pace that makes a single piece last a satisfying two to three minutes.

Flavor-wise, the vanilla is genuinely distinct. It’s lighter than chocolate and brings a subtle caramelized sugar note in the finish. The sweetness level is the one real adjustment — it’s a solid step sweeter than the original. That’s fine in small doses, but eating a handful at once gets cloying.

**What Buyers Are Saying**

The 4.7-star average across 235 ratings holds up. Repeated praise centers on the flavor being a pleasant surprise. One reviewer wrote, “I didn’t expect to like these more than the original, but the vanilla is so smooth and creamy.” Another noted, “These are dangerously easy to eat. I finished half the bag in one sitting.” The common thread: people who were skeptical about a vanilla version ended up genuinely enjoying it.

**Where It Falls Short**

Honestly, the biggest drawback is the limited edition status. If you find you love these, that’s it — you can’t reliably stock up. Stockpiling is your only option, and the 12-ounce bag isn’t a bulk size.

The sweetness is also a genuine consideration. If you have a low tolerance for sugary candy, these will hit your limit fast. And while the resealable bag works well, the midgees are small enough that crumbs accumulate at the bottom quickly.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Vanilla candy enthusiasts** — anyone who prefers white chocolate, vanilla caramels, or butterscotch will likely love these.
- **Tootsie Roll fans wanting a twist** — if you’re bored with the classic, this is a worthwhile change of pace.
- **Gift-givers** — the limited edition angle makes this a fun stocking stuffer or candy dish filler.

**Skip it if:** you’re strictly a chocolate purist, or you’re looking for a sugar-free option (there isn’t one).

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these the same size as regular Tootsie Roll Midgees?**
A: Yes, identical dimensions. The only difference is the vanilla flavor.

**Q: Do they taste like vanilla extract or something artificial?**
A: Closer to real vanilla — think vanilla bean ice cream, not imitation extract.

**Q: How long do they stay fresh after opening?**
A: With the bag sealed properly, about 3-4 weeks before they start getting slightly harder and less chewy.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely good vanilla candy that respects the original’s texture. **Skip it if** you’re sensitive to sweetness or prefer the classic chocolate profile.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — loses a fraction for the limited availability and elevated sweetness, but earns full marks for executing a flavor variant that actually justifies its existence. If you see it in stock, grab a bag. You’ll either love it or learn something about your candy preferences.
