## FAQ

**Q: Is 60,000 SHU actually hot?**
A: Yes. For reference, common grocery store cayenne runs 30,000–40,000 SHU. Jalapeños are 2,500–8,000. This is genuinely spicy — start with less than you normally use.

**Q: How does this compare to red pepper flakes?**
A: Red pepper flakes are usually 15,000–30,000 SHU, so this cayenne is 2–4 times hotter. It also has a finer grind, so it integrates into dishes rather than sitting on top.

**Q: Does the resealable lid keep it fresh?**
A: Yes, and that matters. Cayenne loses potency over time. The tight seal protects it from moisture and air. Store it away from your stove's steam and it'll hold its heat for a year.
