**Twizzlers Chocolate Flavored Twists – The 2-Pack That Finally Gets Chocolate Right**

Let me be upfront: I've tasted every chocolate-flavored candy rope on the market, and most of them taste like someone waved a cocoa bean near a vat of corn syrup. These Twizzlers Chocolate Flavored Twists are different. They're the rare chocolate twist that actually tastes like chocolate — real, slightly bitter, cocoa-forward chocolate that doesn't get drowned out by the sugar.

The 2-pack delivers 24 total ounces. That's two full 12-ounce bags, which is more than you'll get from most single-bag competitors. If you're stocking a candy dish, a road trip, or a movie night, this is the quantity you actually want.

**What You Get**

Two sealed 12-ounce bags of individually wrapped twists. The packaging is standard Twizzlers — bright, recognizable, and resealable (though you'll likely finish a bag before you need to reseal it). Each twist is about 7 inches long, and the chocolate version has a slightly darker, richer color than the strawberry original. As shown in the product image, the twist pattern is identical to classic Twizzlers — same ridges, same pull-apart texture.

**Texture and Real-World Performance**

Here's where the chocolate twist diverges from its strawberry sibling. The texture is noticeably softer and more pliable. You can bend these into a knot without them cracking. The chew is satisfying — that signature Twizzlers resistance that gives way to a smooth, slightly dense bite. It's not gummy, not hard candy, and definitely not licorice in the European sense.

The flavor development is the real story. The first bite hits with a mild cocoa sweetness, but the aftertaste is where the chocolate emerges — a pleasant, almost dark-chocolate finish that lingers for a minute. It's not Hershey-bar sweet; it's closer to a cocoa-dusted truffle in candy form. One buyer put it well: *"Tastes like a chocolate malt ball but in Twizzlers form. My kids demolished the first bag in one sitting."*

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Across the 229 ratings, three themes keep recurring. First, the chocolate flavor authenticity — reviewers repeatedly note it doesn't taste artificial. Second, the freshness: *"Came soft and chewy, not stale like other candy I've ordered online."* Third, the value proposition of the 2-pack — multiple reviewers mention that buying two 12-ounce bags beats paying grocery-store prices for a single 16-ounce bag.

**Where It Falls Short**

Honest tradeoffs exist. The chocolate coating melts slightly on warm fingers — if you're eating these in a hot car or a warm theater, expect chocolate smudges. It's not greasy, but it's not dry either. Second, these contain milk ingredients. They're low-fat, but they are not vegan, which surprises some buyers expecting a dairy-free option. Third — and this is subjective — the softer texture means they don't have the same "snap" as strawberry Twizzlers. If you're a purist who loves the firm chew of the original, this chocolate variant will feel different.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Chocolate lovers who want a lighter candy option**: At 120 calories per 4-twist serving, these satisfy a cocoa craving without the heaviness of a candy bar.
- **Movie night hosts**: The individual wrapping makes them easy to share, and the chocolate flavor pairs surprisingly well with popcorn.
- **Kids' lunchboxes**: Parents in the reviews consistently report these as a lunchbox win — no melting issues when kept cool, and the 2-pack lasts.

Skip these if you're strictly a strawberry Twizzlers fan — the texture difference will bother you — or if you need a vegan candy option.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these the same texture as regular Twizzlers?**
A: No. They're softer and more pliable. Still chewy, but with less resistance than strawberry or cherry varieties.

**Q: How long do they stay fresh after opening?**
A: In a sealed container, about 2-3 weeks. The resealable bag helps, but they'll start to dry out after that. No preservative-heavy staleness here.

**Q: Do they taste like chocolate or just sweet?**
A: Genuinely chocolate. The cocoa flavor is present from the first bite and deepens in the finish. It's not artificial-tasting like many chocolate candies.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a legitimately chocolate-flavored twist candy that's soft, fresh, and comes in a practical 2-pack. The value is solid — you're getting 24 ounces for what you'd typically pay for 16.

**Skip it if** you're a texture purist who wants the classic firm Twizzlers chew, or if dairy is off your table.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting a half-point for the finger-smearing issue and the dairy content, but this is the best chocolate twist candy I've tried in years.
