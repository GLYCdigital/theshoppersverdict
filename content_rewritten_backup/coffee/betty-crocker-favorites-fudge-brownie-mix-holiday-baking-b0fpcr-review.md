**Betty Crocker Favorites Fudge Brownie Mix, Holiday Baking** is a surprising contender in the coffee-and-dessert pairing world. While it’s not a coffee maker itself, this brownie mix is the perfect companion to your morning brew — and it’s earned a stellar 4.8/5 rating from 240 Amazon shoppers. I’ve tested this mix twice: once as a straight bake, once with a twist. Here’s the honest breakdown.

## What’s actually in the box

You get one 18.3-ounce box of Betty Crocker Favorites Fudge Brownie Mix. That’s it — no eggs, oil, or water included. You’ll need those three pantry staples plus a bowl and a pan. The mix comes in a resealable pouch inside the festive holiday-printed box, which is a nice touch if you’re gifting it. The packaging shows a rich, dark brownie with fudge swirls — and honestly, the final product looks close to that image.

## Real-world performance

The prep is dead simple: dump the mix, add 1/2 cup oil, 1/4 cup water, and 2 eggs. Stir until combined — about 50 strokes — and bake at 350°F. I used a 9x13 metal pan and checked at 25 minutes. The center was set but not dry. Texture was fudgy in the middle with a slightly crackly top, which is exactly what you want.

Flavor-wise, this is noticeably darker and richer than standard box brownies. There’s a genuine cocoa depth that pairs well with black coffee or a latte. It’s not cloying — the sweetness level is balanced, which is rare for a mainstream mix.

## What buyers consistently praise

Amazon reviewers are overwhelmingly positive. One shopper wrote, "These are the best box brownies I've ever had — moist, chocolatey, and they don't taste artificial." Another noted, "I bought these for holiday baking and ended up making three batches in one week." The consistent theme is the fudge flavor and the easy cleanup — one bowl, one spatula, no mixer required.

The holiday packaging also gets frequent mentions. Several buyers said they grabbed extra boxes as gifts for neighbors and coworkers. It’s a small detail, but it matters when you’re baking for the season.

## Where it falls short

Honestly, the main complaint is yield. The box says it makes 20 brownies, but those are on the small side — about 2-inch squares if you cut a 9x13 into 5x4. If you’re feeding a crowd or want thick, bakery-style slabs, you’ll need two boxes. That pushes the cost up.

Also, the bake time is finicky. At 28 minutes, my edges were slightly crumbly and dry. The center was perfect, but the corners suffered. Set your timer for 22 minutes and check with a toothpick — don’t trust the full 30 minutes on the box.

## Who should buy this

- **Coffee lovers who want a quick dessert pairing** — this mix delivers a rich, mocha-friendly brownie.
- **Holiday bakers** who want a reliable, gifting-ready product with festive packaging.
- **Beginners** who’ve never made brownies from scratch — this is nearly impossible to mess up if you follow the time.

## FAQ

**Can I use this mix in a muffin tin?**  
Yes, but reduce bake time to 18-20 minutes. Fill liners 3/4 full for fudgy bites.

**Does it need eggs, or can I use a substitute?**  
It needs 2 eggs, but applesauce (1/4 cup per egg) works if you’re in a pinch. Texture will be slightly cakey.

**Is it suitable for a 9x13 pan or just an 8x8?**  
Both work. The 8x8 yields thicker, fudgier brownies; the 9x13 gives thinner, crispier edges. Adjust time accordingly.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, genuinely fudgy brownie mix that pairs beautifully with coffee and comes in gift-ready holiday packaging.

**Skip it if** you need to feed more than 10 people without buying a second box, or if you prefer cakey brownies — this is firmly in the fudge camp.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a solid, crowd-pleasing mix with minor yield and timing quirks. At this price point, it’s a worthy countertop companion for your coffee maker.
