If you've ever stood in an office break room staring at a can of stale, sad coffee, you already know the problem this product solves. Farmer Brothers has been supplying restaurants and diners for over a century, and their Breakfast Blend ground coffee comes in individually wrapped 2-ounce filter packs that make brewing a consistent pot almost foolproof.

## What you actually get

Each box contains pre-measured 2-ounce filter packs — enough for a standard 12-cup pot. The medium roast uses a blend of Central and South American beans, ground to a uniform size that works well in both drip machines and pour-over setups. As shown in the product image, the packs are sealed individually, which is the real selling point here: open one, brew, and the rest stay fresh.

## Performance and real-world use

I brewed this in a standard Mr. Coffee drip machine and a Chemex. In the drip machine, extraction was clean — no channeling, no weak first cup. The flavor profile hits classic breakfast blend notes: a mild nuttiness up front, a slight caramel sweetness in the middle, and a clean finish with zero of that acrid burn you get from over-roasted commodity beans.

The grind size deserves specific praise. It's fine enough to extract properly in a 12-cup brewer but coarse enough that it won't over-extract and turn bitter if you leave the pot on the warmer for twenty minutes. That's a real consideration for office settings where the pot sits.

One thing I noticed: the strength is consistent across multiple pots. Because each pack is pre-measured, you don't get the "heavier hand on Tuesday" problem. That consistency is worth something if you're brewing for a group.

## What buyers consistently praise

The 225 Amazon ratings at 4.5 stars reflect a pattern. One reviewer noted, "Finally a coffee that doesn't taste burnt — smooth and easy to drink black." Another mentioned, "The individual packs are perfect for our office. No more guessing how much to scoop." Several buyers in the hospitality space mentioned using this for hotel breakfast bars and catering events, citing the convenience of not weighing or scooping.

## Where it falls short

The biggest limitation is the lack of a whole-bean option. Once you buy this, you're committed to their grind, which is optimized for drip brewing. If you prefer a French press or espresso machine, this won't be your best choice — you'll want a coarser or finer grind respectively.

Packaging is the second issue. Each filter pack is individually wrapped, which means significantly more waste than a single bag. If you're environmentally conscious, that's a legitimate drawback. The packs also take up more shelf space than a comparable bag of beans.

## Who should buy this

This is ideal for three specific buyers:

- **Office managers** who need consistent, crowd-pleasing coffee without training staff on proper measurements
- **Campers and event caterers** who need portable, protected portions that survive transport
- **Home brewers who hate measuring** — pop a pack, add water, done

Skip it if you're a coffee snob chasing single-origin complexity or if you use brewing methods other than drip.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use these filter packs in a Keurig?**
A: No. These are designed for standard drip coffee makers. The 2-ounce packs are meant to be emptied into the filter basket, not used as K-cups.

**Q: How much coffee does one pack make?**
A: One 2-ounce pack is calibrated for a standard 12-cup pot. For smaller batches, use half a pack — though you'll want to fold the pack tightly to keep the grounds fresh.

**Q: Is this actually a medium roast?**
A: Yes, and it's a true medium, not a dark roast in disguise. There's a gentle caramel note but no oiliness on the beans and no smoky char in the cup.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want reliable, good-tasting coffee without measuring, especially for offices or events where consistency matters more than exotic flavor notes.

**Skip it if** you need whole beans, prefer French press or espresso, or want to minimize packaging waste.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Solid, dependable, and genuinely convenient. It won't win awards from coffee purists, but it will win the break room.
