**The Shopper's Verdict: Elite Gourmet EHC112 Single Serve Coffee Maker**

Look, you don't need a $400 espresso machine to get a decent cup of coffee at 6 AM. The Elite Gourmet EHC112 proves that point — but with a few caveats worth knowing before you hit "Add to Cart."

**What You Actually Get**

Out of the box you get the brewer itself, a 15-ounce thermal travel mug with lid, a reusable mesh filter basket, and a measuring scoop. That's it. No pod adapter, no carafe, no descaling kit. The machine measures about 12 inches tall and 10 inches deep, so it tucks neatly under standard cabinets without hogging your counter. The red accent trim on the black body gives it a slightly retro diner vibe — not fancy, but not ugly either.

**How It Actually Performs**

The EHC112 is dead simple: fill the water reservoir (which is actually the travel mug itself — you fill it, snap it into place, and the machine pumps water through the filter and back into the mug). Pop in your grounds, press the single button, and wait about 3-4 minutes for a full 15-ounce brew. The thermal mug keeps coffee hot for a solid 2 hours, which is genuinely useful if you sip slowly.

But here's the honest tradeoff: there's no strength selector, no temperature control, no programmable timer. You get one brew profile, and that's it. The coffee comes out hot and reasonably strong with a medium grind, but if you prefer lighter roasts or extra-bold extraction, you'll need to experiment with your own grind size. The reusable filter does a decent job — paper filters will give you a cleaner cup, but the mesh catches most fines without letting sludge through.

**What Buyers Keep Saying**

Across nearly 10,000 ratings, the consistent praise centers on value and simplicity. One reviewer put it plainly: *"I've had this for a year and it still brews like day one. For the price, I honestly didn't expect it to last this long."* Another noted, *"The mug is the best part — I take it to work every day and my coffee stays hot until lunch."*

**Where It Falls Short**

The 15-ounce capacity is the biggest limitation. If you drink a standard 8-ounce cup, that's fine — one brew gets you through the morning. But if you're sharing or want a second cup, you're re-brewing with a fresh cycle. That's not a dealbreaker at this price, but it's worth knowing going in.

There are also scattered reports of the lid hinge cracking after several months of daily use. It's not widespread — maybe 5% of reviews mention it — but it's consistent enough that I'd recommend handling the lid gently. And because the reservoir is the mug itself, you can't brew without it in place. Lose the mug and you've got a paperweight.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Dorm students or first-apartment renters** who need a functional brewer without a big investment
- **Single coffee drinkers** who want one solid cup without pod waste or recurring costs
- **Travelers or commuters** who'll actually use the thermal mug daily

**Who Should Skip It**

- **Couples or households** — you'll be brewing back-to-back every morning
- **Coffee purists** who need precise temperature or strength control
- **Anyone who prefers K-Cups** — this machine is ground coffee only

**FAQ**

**Can I use paper filters with the reusable basket?**
Yes, #4 cone filters fit, though you'll need to fold the edges slightly. The mesh filter works fine on its own.

**Does the mug leak?**
The lid seals well when closed. Some users report minor drip from the drinking spout if you tip it aggressively, but normal use is leak-free.

**How often should I descale?**
Every 4-6 weeks with white vinegar or citric acid, depending on your water hardness. The manual includes instructions, and it's a 15-minute process.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a no-frills, budget-friendly single-serve brewer that eliminates pod costs and comes with a genuinely useful thermal mug. At this price point, it's hard to beat for the solo coffee drinker.

**Skip it if** you need more than one cup at a time, want customization options, or expect appliance-grade durability. This is a value machine, and it performs exactly at that level.

**Rating: 8.2/10** — A solid budget choice with real limitations. It earns its 4.3-star average honestly: great for what it is, not pretending to be more.
