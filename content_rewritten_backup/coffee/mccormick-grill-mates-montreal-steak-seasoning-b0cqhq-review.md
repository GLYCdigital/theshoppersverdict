That coarse grind matters: it's designed to crust onto meat surfaces rather than dissolve instantly, which is exactly what you want for grilling.

I tested this on ribeye, chicken thighs, and even roasted vegetables. For chicken, it performs better than most "all-purpose" seasonings because the coarse texture holds up to longer cook times without turning to mush. On vegetables (try it on grilled zucchini), the garlic and pepper notes carry through without the blend clumping.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this on fish?**
Yes, but use it sparingly. The coarse pepper and garlic can overwhelm delicate white fish. It works better on salmon or swordfish.

**Q: Does it expire?**
McCormick prints a "best by" date on the bottom. Stored in a cool, dry cabinet away from heat, it'll stay potent for about 18 months. After that, the pepper loses its punch.

**Q: Is it gluten-free?**
Yes, McCormick labels this product gluten-free and it contains no wheat ingredients.
