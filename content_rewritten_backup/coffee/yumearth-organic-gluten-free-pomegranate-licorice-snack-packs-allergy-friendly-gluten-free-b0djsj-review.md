Let me be upfront: this is a candy review, not a coffee gear review, despite what the category metadata suggests. But if you're here, you're likely a parent or snack buyer hunting for allergy-safe treats, and that's a mission I respect. The YumEarth Organic Pomegranate Licorice Snack Pack sits at a 3.9-star average across 195 ratings — a score that tells me real shoppers are split. Here's the unvarnished truth after digging through the feedback.

**What you actually get**

The snack pack ships with multiple 1-ounce pouches of pomegranate-flavored licorice twists. Each pouch is roughly a palm-sized portion — enough for a lunchbox or a quick craving hit, not a movie-night bag. The licorice itself is a deep red, made with organic cane sugar and brown rice syrup instead of corn syrup. It's certified organic, gluten-free, and free from the top nine allergens, which is the entire reason this product exists.

**The texture debate is real**

Here's where the 3.9 rating starts making sense. Traditional licorice is soft and pulls apart easily. YumEarth's version is noticeably denser and chewier — closer to a fruit leather that's been twisted. One reviewer put it plainly: "My kids loved the flavor but complained it was too hard to chew." Another said, "It's like chewing on a rubber band at first, but it softens up after a few bites." That's consistent with what I see in the ratings. If you have dental work, braces, or young kids with developing molars, this texture is a genuine consideration.

**Flavor: sour first, pomegranate second**

The pomegranate flavor is authentic — you can taste the actual fruit, not a vague "red candy" flavor. But the citric acid coating hits first, and it's aggressive. These are more sour than sweet, which surprised several buyers expecting a milder licorice. If you like Warheads or sour gummies, this will land well. If you wanted a sweet, mellow licorice like a Twizzler, you'll be disappointed. One verified purchaser summed it up: "Great clean ingredients, but the sourness overpowers the pomegranate. My daughter still asks for them, so that says something."

**What buyers consistently praise**

The allergen-free angle is the star. Parents with kids who have celiac disease, nut allergies, or dairy sensitivities repeatedly mention this is one of the few candies their child can safely eat. The organic certification matters too — no artificial dyes, no high-fructose corn syrup, no hydrogenated oils. For the clean-ingredient crowd, this is a legit win. The portion control also gets love from parents who don't want to police a giant bag of candy.

**Where it falls short**

Beyond texture and sourness, the value proposition is weak. These snack packs run a premium per ounce compared to conventional licorice, and the small pouches go fast. Several reviewers noted the packs are "smaller than expected" and that the price-per-serving is hard to justify for an everyday snack. Also, if you're a licorice purist expecting that classic anise or strawberry profile, the pomegranate-sour combo is a departure that won't convert everyone.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the allergy-conscious parent, the gluten-free dieter who misses chewy candy, or anyone who prefers organic ingredients and doesn't mind a sour kick. It's also a decent option for stocking a school-safe treat bin. Skip it if you want traditional soft licorice, if you're on a budget, or if you have dental sensitivity.

**FAQ**

*Is this actually gluten-free and safe for celiacs?* Yes, it's certified gluten-free and made in a facility that avoids the top nine allergens. Many reviewers with celiac disease report no issues.

*Does it taste like real pomegranate?* The flavor is present but buried under a strong citric acid sourness. If you're expecting a sweet, fruity candy, adjust expectations.

*How many pouches come in the pack?* It varies by listing, but the snack pack format is designed for portion control — roughly 5-6 single-serve pouches per box. Check the listing for the exact count.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need an allergy-safe, organic candy that actually tastes like fruit, and you don't mind a chewy, sour profile. **Skip it if:** You want classic soft licorice or a better cost-per-ounce value.

I'm landing at 3.7 stars. The ingredients and allergen profile are genuinely excellent, but the texture and sour-forward flavor will polarize your household. It's a niche product done well — just know what you're getting before you buy.
