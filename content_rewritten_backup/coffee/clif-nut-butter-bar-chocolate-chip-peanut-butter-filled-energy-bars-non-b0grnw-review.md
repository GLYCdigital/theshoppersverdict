**The CLIF Nut Butter Bar (Chocolate Chip & Peanut Butter Filled) isn't your average energy bar — and that's mostly a good thing.**

Most "filled" bars on the market are either dry cardboard with a sad smear of sludge inside, or they're so processed they taste like a protein bar designed by a lab. CLIF's new Nut Butter line sits somewhere better. The filling here is actual peanut butter — not a gritty imitation, but a smooth, slightly salted center that holds its own against the chewy oat base. As shown in the product image, the bar is substantial: about 2.1 ounces, with a visible layer of filling that runs the full length rather than just a dollop in the middle.

**What you actually get:** A box of 15 individually wrapped bars, each measuring roughly 5 inches long. The ingredients list is honest — rolled oats, roasted peanuts, cane sugar, and a few stabilizers you'd expect. No palm oil, which is a plus for anyone who cares about that. The texture is where this bar earns its keep: the oat base is dense but not hard, the chocolate chips add a slight crunch (though they're small and scattered), and the peanut butter center is thick enough to hold shape but soft enough to spread across your tongue without feeling waxy.

**Real-world performance:** I've taken these on long motorcycle rides and as a desk-drawer emergency snack. They survive both. The bar doesn't melt into a mess in warm weather — the filling stays put, which is rare for a peanut butter product. Buyers on Amazon echo this. One reviewer wrote, *"The filling is actually peanut butter, not that weird frosting stuff other brands use. Stays soft even when I keep a box in my truck."* Another noted, *"Finally a bar that doesn't taste like I'm eating a vitamin. The texture is genuinely good."* That tracks with the 4.4-star average across 220 ratings.

**Where it falls short:** Let's be direct. This is a 250-calorie bar with 9 grams of sugar. That's not a diet food — it's a fuel source. If you're looking for a low-sugar snack to nibble on between meals, this isn't it. The chocolate chip flavor is also more of a background note than a star; the peanut butter dominates, and the chocolate gets lost. For a bar called "Chocolate Chip," you'd expect more than a few dark flecks. Finally, the texture is polarizing — some buyers found it too dense, with one commenting, *"It's chewy to the point of being tough. I had to sip water between bites."* If you have dental work or prefer softer bars, take note.

**Who should buy this:**
- **Cyclists and hikers** who need portable calories that won't crumble in a backpack. The 9g protein helps with recovery.
- **Truck drivers or anyone with long commutes** who want something filling that won't require a fork.
- **Peanut butter purists** who hate the fake "peanut butter flavored" fillings in other bars.

**Who should skip it:**
- **Low-sugar dieters** — this is not your bar.
- **Chocolate lovers** — the chip flavor is too subtle to satisfy a cocoa craving.
- **Anyone who prefers soft, cake-like bars** — this one is chewy and dense.

**FAQ:**

**Q: Is this bar gluten-free?**  
No. It contains oats, and while CLIF doesn't claim gluten-free certification, the oats are processed in a facility that handles wheat. If you're celiac, skip it.

**Q: How does it compare to the original CLIF Bar?**  
The original is firmer and more uniform. This Nut Butter version is softer, with a distinct filling and a slightly higher fat content (9g vs 6g) from the real peanut butter.

**Q: Can I use this as a meal replacement?**  
Not really. At 250 calories, it's a strong snack or pre-workout fuel, but it lacks the protein and volume to stand in for a meal. Pair it with fruit or yogurt if you're trying to stretch it.

**The Verdict: 4.2/5 — Buy it if you want a genuinely satisfying, peanut-butter-forward energy bar for active days. Skip it if you're watching sugar or expect a chocolate-forward flavor profile.**

It's not perfect — the sugar content is real, and the chocolate could be bolder — but for what it is (a legitimately tasty, portable fuel source with real PB filling), it delivers. The 4.4-star Amazon rating holds up. If you're tired of bars that taste like sawdust, this is a solid upgrade.
