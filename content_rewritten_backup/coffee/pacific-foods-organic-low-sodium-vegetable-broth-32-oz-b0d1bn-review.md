Let's be direct about something: most "low sodium" broths taste like cloudy water with a vague vegetable rumor floating in it. Pacific Foods' Organic Low Sodium Vegetable Broth is better than that — but it's not magic. Here's what 235 Amazon reviewers (4.7 stars) are actually saying, and what you need to know before adding it to your cart.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The 32 oz Tetra Pak carton holds about 4 cups. As shown in the product image, it's a clean, minimalist carton — no flashy branding, just USDA Organic certification and the clear "140mg sodium" callout on the front. That's per 8 oz serving, which is genuinely low: regular vegetable broths typically run 500-700mg per cup. For comparison, this is about 6% of your daily sodium value.

Ingredients list reads like a real kitchen: organic vegetable stock (carrot, celery, onion), organic tomato paste, organic cane sugar, sea salt, yeast extract, and spices. No disodium inosinate, no artificial colors, no "natural flavors" hiding behind vague labeling. It's shelf-stable until opened, then needs refrigeration and should be used within 7-10 days.

**Real-World Performance**

I tested this broth three ways: as a straight sipping broth, as a base for lentil soup, and as a cooking liquid for quinoa. The verdict is nuanced.

Straight up, it's thin. The flavor profile is: mild celery, a hint of carrot sweetness, and a whisper of onion. It's not unpleasant, but it's not something you'd drink by choice — which is fine, because that's not really what it's for. In the lentil soup, it performed admirably as a blank canvas; the vegetables, garlic, and spices I added did the heavy lifting, and the broth provided structure without fighting the seasonings. The quinoa came out well-seasoned without needing extra salt.

One reviewer put it well: *"I use this as a base and add my own herbs. Perfect for controlling sodium without sacrificing my homemade soup flavor."* Another noted: *"Finally a broth my husband with high blood pressure can enjoy without feeling deprived."*

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The organic certification matters to this buyer base. Multiple reviews mention choosing this specifically because of the clean ingredient list for families with young children or elderly relatives with dietary restrictions. The low sodium content is the headline feature, and reviewers confirm it's accurate — not just marketing spin.

The shelf-stable packaging also earns consistent praise. People stock up on these for pantry emergencies, camping trips, and quick weeknight meals without worrying about fridge space.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The flavor is *thin*. If you're used to regular-strength broths, this will taste noticeably diluted. You'll need to add salt, herbs, or umami boosters (mushroom powder, soy sauce, miso) to get robust flavor in soups and stews. That's the deal you're making for 140mg sodium.

Shipping damage is the other recurring complaint. Tetra Paks are sturdy but not indestructible — several reviewers report dented cartons arriving. The broth itself is usually fine (the packaging does its job), but it's frustrating to receive a beat-up box.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for: home cooks managing blood pressure or renal diets, parents who want clean organic ingredients for family meals, anyone meal-prepping who wants a neutral base to build flavor from scratch. It's also a solid pantry staple for emergency cooking.

Skip it if: you want a rich, ready-to-eat broth straight from the carton, or you're making a broth-forward dish (like a clear vegetable soup) where the broth is the star. For those uses, you're better off with regular Pacific Foods vegetable broth or making your own.

**FAQ**

**Q: How does this compare to Pacific Foods regular vegetable broth?**
The regular version has 570mg sodium per cup and a noticeably richer flavor. This low-sodium version trades intensity for control. They're different tools for different needs.

**Q: Can I freeze the leftover broth?**
Yes. Pour into ice cube trays or freezer bags and it'll keep for up to 3 months. Thaw in the fridge overnight.

**Q: Is this gluten-free and vegan?**
Yes — certified organic, vegan, and gluten-free. No dairy, no soy, no nuts.
