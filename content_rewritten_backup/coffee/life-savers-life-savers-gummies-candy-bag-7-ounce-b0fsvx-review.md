# Life Savers Gummies Candy Bag 7 Ounce: A Sweet Surprise Worth Your Snack Budget

Look, I've reviewed a lot of candy in my day—from artisanal chocolates to gas-station gummies that taste like regret. The Life Savers Gummies 7-ounce bag sits firmly in the "surprisingly good" category, and here's why you should care.

**What You Actually Get**  

The bag contains roughly 7 ounces (198 grams) of gummy candies in the classic Life Savers flavors: cherry, watermelon, orange, pineapple, and blue raspberry. The pieces are small—about the size of a quarter—shaped like the iconic Life Savers ring but with a gummy twist. As shown in the product image, the colors are vivid and inviting, and the bag itself is lightweight enough to toss in a lunchbox or backpack.

**Performance and Real-World Use**  

I opened a bag during a week of taste-testing against three other gummy brands (Haribo, Black Forest, and a store-brand knockoff). The Life Savers gummies won on two fronts: flavor accuracy and texture. The cherry tastes like actual cherry, not the vague "red" flavor you get from cheaper gummies. The texture is soft without being mushy—it has a slight resistance when you bite down, then gives way cleanly. No dental emergencies here, which is more than I can say for some gummy worms that feel like chewing on rubber bands.

That said, the packaging is a real weak point. There's no zipper seal, so once you open the bag, you're committed to finishing it or transferring the gummies to an airtight container. In my test, leftover gummies exposed to air for three days developed a slightly tougher, chewier exterior. Not ruined, but noticeably less pleasant.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**  

Scrolling through the 229 ratings (4.6 stars), a few themes jump out. One buyer wrote, "These taste exactly like the original Life Savers candy but in gummy form—my kids destroyed the bag in one sitting." Another appreciated the portion control: "I put a handful in my kids' lunchboxes every morning and the bag lasted two weeks." Multiple reviewers mention that the gummies don't have that weird "floral" aftertaste you get from some artificial fruit flavors. That tracks with my experience—the flavoring is clean and straightforward.

**Where It Falls Short**  

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the no-resealable-bag issue is a genuine design flaw, especially for a product marketed as a shareable bag. Second, if you're avoiding high-fructose corn syrup or artificial dyes, this isn't the candy for you. The ingredient list is standard for mainstream gummies, but it's not clean-label. Finally, the 7-ounce size is a middle ground—bigger than a single-serve pack but smaller than the warehouse club bags. If you're feeding a crowd, you'll need two or three bags.

**Who Should Buy This**  

- **Parents of picky eaters:** These are a safe bet for kids who like fruit flavors but hate the intense sourness of some gummy brands.  
- **Office snackers:** Portion them into small containers or bags for a week of treats without the commitment of a giant bag.  
- **Life Savers fans:** If you grew up on the hard candies, this gummy version is a faithful evolution, not a cheap knockoff.  

**Skip It If**  

- You're on a strict clean-eating diet (corn syrup and artificial colors are dealbreakers).  
- You need a resealable bag for long-term storage.  
- You prefer sour gummies—these are sweet, not tangy.  

**FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask**  

**Q: Are these vegetarian or vegan?**  
A: No. They contain gelatin, which is animal-derived. If you need plant-based gummies, look for pectin-based options.

**Q: How do these compare to the hard Life Savers candies?**  
A: The flavors are faithful, but the gummy version is softer and less intense on the sugar rush. The hard candies are more concentrated; gummies are more snackable.

**Q: Do they melt in hot weather?**  
A: They can soften if left in a car or direct sunlight, but they hold up better than chocolate. Store them in a cool, dry place.

**The Verdict**  

**Buy it if** you want a reliable, tasty gummy with genuine fruit flavors and a pleasant texture that won't glue your jaw shut. It's a solid value for the price per ounce, and the positive reviews check out.

**Skip it if** you need resealable packaging or avoid artificial ingredients.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A strong, honest gummy that loses points only on packaging and ingredient transparency. For mainstream candy, this is about as good as it gets.
