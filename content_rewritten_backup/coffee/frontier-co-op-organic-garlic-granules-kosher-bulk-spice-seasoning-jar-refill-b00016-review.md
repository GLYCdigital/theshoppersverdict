Most garlic powders sit on shelves for two years, tasting like dusty cardboard by month six. Frontier Co-op's Organic Garlic Granules avoid that fate — and the 4.8-star rating across 240 reviews backs it up. If you cook with garlic more than twice a week, this bulk bag belongs in your pantry.

## What you actually get

The 1-pound bag contains roughly 2.5 cups of granules — not powder, not flakes, but somewhere in between. The texture is consistent, about the size of fine sea salt. As shown in the product image, it's a straightforward kraft bag with a clear label. No scoop, no jar, no frills. The ingredient list is one word: garlic. That's it.

## How it performs in real cooking

Here's where this product separates itself from the grocery store stuff. The granules rehydrate in about 60 seconds when added to sauces, marinades, or dressings. No clumping, no bitter raw-garlic aftertaste that cheap powders leave behind. The aroma when you open the bag is noticeably fresh — sweet and pungent, not stale.

One reviewer put it simply: "This is the only garlic I use anymore. The flavor is so much cleaner than the powder from the supermarket." Another noted, "I've bought this three times now. It dissolves better than any brand I've tried."

The flavor is more robust than standard garlic powder but still milder than fresh. If a recipe calls for one clove of fresh garlic, use about 1/4 teaspoon of these granules. If you're substituting for garlic powder, use slightly more — the larger particle size means less surface area for flavor release.

## What buyers consistently praise

Across the reviews, three themes dominate:

**Value.** A 1-pound bag at this price point beats the per-ounce cost of any 3-ounce supermarket jar by a wide margin. One reviewer calculated: "I was paying $4 for 3 ounces at the store. This is a pound for less than I was paying for 10 ounces."

**Consistency.** The granules are uniform, which means even distribution in rubs and batters. No surprise pockets of intense garlic flavor.

**Clean sourcing.** Frontier Co-op is a certified B Corp with a longstanding reputation for ethical sourcing. For shoppers who care about where their spices come from, that matters.

## Where it falls short

Honest tradeoffs exist. The granules are too coarse for dry rubs where you want a fine, clingy texture — you'll need a separate garlic powder for that. The bag has no resealable zipper, so you'll want to transfer to an airtight jar immediately; moisture is the enemy here. And if you're used to the punch of fresh garlic, you'll need to adjust your quantities upward.

## Who should buy this

This is for the home cook who goes through garlic quickly — someone making marinades, stir-fries, soups, or spice blends weekly. It's also ideal for meal preppers who want consistent flavor without the hassle of peeling and mincing. If you bake your own bread or make your own seasoned salts, the bulk size makes sense.

Skip it if you use garlic only occasionally or prefer the finer texture of powder for specific applications.

## FAQ

**How long does it stay fresh?**
Stored in an airtight container away from heat and light, expect 2-3 years of solid flavor. It won't spoil, but it will slowly lose potency.

**Is this really organic and kosher?**
Yes. USDA Organic certified, kosher certified, and non-GMO verified. The full certification details are on Frontier's website.

**Can I use this in place of garlic powder in baking?**
Yes, but reduce the amount by about 25% and expect a slightly coarser texture in the final product.
