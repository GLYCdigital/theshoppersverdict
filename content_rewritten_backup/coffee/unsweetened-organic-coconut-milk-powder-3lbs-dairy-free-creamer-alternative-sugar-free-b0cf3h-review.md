Skip the refrigerated carton. This 3-pound bag of unsweetened organic coconut milk powder is the workhorse solution for anyone who takes their coffee seriously but can't do dairy. It's not a perfect dairy mimic—nothing is—but for the price per cup, it punches well above its weight.

## What you actually get

One hefty 3lb bag of fine, off-white powder. That's roughly 96 tablespoons, or about 90–100 cups of coffee if you use a heaping teaspoon per mug. The ingredients list is refreshingly short: organic coconut milk, organic acacia gum. No sugar, no maltodextrin, no silicon dioxide. If you're keto, paleo, or just avoiding fillers, this is the cleanest option in this category.

## Real-world performance in coffee

Here's where this powder earns its keep. Dropped into a fresh cup of black coffee, it dissolves faster than most powdered creamers. The acacia gum does the emulsifying work, so you get a smooth, uniform lightening without the oil slick that plagues cheap coconut powders. I tested it in both hot drip coffee and cold brew. Hot: flawless. Cold: requires a quick stir or a minute of patience—coconut fat wants to cling together in cold liquid.

The flavor is where you need to adjust expectations. This tastes like coconut, not like cream. It adds a subtle, nutty sweetness (from the coconut, not sugar) and a silky texture, but the mouthfeel is noticeably thinner than whole milk or half-and-half. If you're transitioning from dairy, give it a week. Your palate will recalibrate.

## Where it falls short

The honesty section. First, the bag itself: no zipper seal. Once opened, you need a chip clip or a transfer to a mason jar, or humidity will turn the powder into hard clumps. Second, the texture is not dairy creamer. It's lighter, almost like a very milky tea rather than a rich latte. Third, if you're using it for baking or smoothies, you'll need to blend it—stirring into cold liquid leaves tiny specks.

One more note: the coconut flavor is present in every cup. If you want a completely neutral creamer, this is not it. But if you like a hint of tropical nuance in your morning brew, you'll actually prefer this to bland powdered creamers.

## FAQ

**Does this dissolve in iced coffee?**
Yes, but it needs vigorous stirring or a quick shake. For best results, mix it with a splash of hot water first, then add to your cold drink.

**How much should I use per cup?**
Start with one teaspoon for a light creaminess, go up to one tablespoon for a richer cup. You can always add more, but you can't take it out.

**Is this actually organic and unsweetened?**
Yes. The label is verified organic, and the only ingredients are organic coconut milk and acacia gum. There is zero added sugar.
