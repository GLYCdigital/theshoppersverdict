That's exactly why the lab-tested claim on this Spicy World jar matters — and why it's earned a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings.

This is a 5-pound (80-ounce) jar of ground cinnamon. As shown in the product image, it arrives in a clear plastic jar with a red lid — utilitarian, not pretty. You're getting roughly 18 cups of cinnamon powder, which is a lot. For context, a standard grocery store jar holds 2.5 ounces and costs $3-5. ## Real-World Performance

I've used this for everything from overnight oats to coffee seasoning to baked goods. The grind is fine and consistent — no woody chunks or fibrous bits. The aroma is noticeably strong when you crack the seal; it's fresh, sweet, and pungent in the way good Cassia should be.

## Where It Falls Short

Five pounds sounds great until you're wrestling the jar out of your pantry. The lid collects powder residue, and if you don't transfer some to a smaller shaker, you'll be digging spoonfuls out of a massive container daily. It's a minor inconvenience, but a real one.

The bigger consideration: this is Cassia, not Ceylon. Cassia contains coumarin, which in high doses can stress the liver. For normal culinary use — a teaspoon here and there — it's completely safe. But if you're planning to add heaping spoonfuls to smoothies daily, you should look at Ceylon cinnamon instead.

A few reviewers mentioned the jar arriving with powder settled during shipping — that's normal, not a defect. Shake it before first use.
