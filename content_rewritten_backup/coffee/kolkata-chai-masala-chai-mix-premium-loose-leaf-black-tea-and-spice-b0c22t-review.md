Let’s be honest: most “chai mixes” on Amazon are ground dust with a vague cinnamon smell. The Kolkata Chai Masala Chai Mix is not that. As shown in the product image, you get a loose-leaf Assam black tea blended with visibly whole spices — cardamom pods, cloves, cinnamon bark, and dried ginger. That alone puts it ahead of 80% of the competition.

**What’s actually in the pouch**

You’re getting a 250g resealable foil pouch of loose-leaf tea and spice blend. That’s roughly 40–50 cups depending on how strong you brew it. No sugar, no milk powder, no artificial flavoring — just tea leaves and spices. The leaf grade is solid: small, dark CTC-style Assam leaves that release color fast and stand up to boiling with milk.

**Taste and performance**

This is a chai built for the traditional stovetop method. Bring 1 cup water to a boil, add 1 heaping teaspoon of the mix, simmer 3–4 minutes, then add ½ cup milk and sugar to taste. The CTC leaf base gives you that bold, malty Assam backbone that doesn’t go bitter when you boil it — a critical failure point for cheaper blends. The spice profile is warm and rounded, with cardamom leading and cinnamon close behind. Clove and ginger are present but restrained.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews reflects genuine enthusiasm. One reviewer put it simply: *"Finally a chai that tastes like the stuff I got from street vendors in Kolkata. The spices are real — you can see them in the tea."* Another noted, *"I've tried every boxed chai at the grocery store. This blows them away. **Where it falls short**

The spice balance leans sweet and cinnamon-forward. If you’re chasing that aggressive, ginger-and-black-pepper punch you get from some Indian households, this blend will disappoint. It’s aromatic and cozy, not fiery. Also, there are zero brewing instructions on the pouch. If you’re new to loose-leaf chai, you’ll need to experiment — oversteeping turns it astringent, and under-steeping leaves it flat. Finally, you absolutely need a strainer or infuser; the loose leaves and whole spices will make a mess in your mug otherwise.

**Who should buy this**

- **The chai-curious coffee drinker**: If you like bold, full-bodied drinks, this is an easy gateway. The Assam base has the same body as a dark roast.
- **The ex-pat or traveler**: If you’ve had roadside chai in India and miss it, this is the closest you’ll get from a pouch.
- **The batch brewer**: Make a big pot for guests — it scales beautifully and fills the house with a better smell than any candle.

**Skip it if** you want a 30-second instant mix, or if you prefer tea bags for convenience. This demands a stovetop and a strainer.

**FAQ**

**Q: How much chai does one pouch make?**  
A: At 1 teaspoon per 8-ounce cup, roughly 40–50 servings. If you brew it strong with milk, closer to 35–40.

**Q: Does it contain caffeine?**  
A: Yes — it’s a black tea base. Comparable to a standard cup of coffee, roughly 40–60mg per serving.

**Q: Can I make it without milk?**  
A: Technically, but the CTC Assam leaf is designed to be brewed with milk. Without it, the tannins will taste sharp. If you must, use a splash of cream or oat milk to smooth it out.
