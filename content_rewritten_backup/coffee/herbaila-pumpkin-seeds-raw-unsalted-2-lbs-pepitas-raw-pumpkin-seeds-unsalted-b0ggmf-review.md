Let's be blunt: most "raw" pumpkin seeds you find in grocery stores are roasted, salted, or sitting on a shelf for eleven months. Herbaila's 2-pound bag of raw, unsalted pepitas is the exception — and at 4.7 stars from 235 ratings, shoppers seem to agree. But is this bulk bag actually worth your money, or is it just another Amazon gamble? I dug into the specs, the feedback, and the real-world use cases to give you the straight answer.

**What you actually get**

The bag arrives as a 2-pound (32 oz) pouch of shelled green pumpkin seeds — technically *Cucurbita pepo* kernels, the variety grown specifically for snacking rather than carving. As shown in the product image, these are the vibrant, dark-green pepitas, not the white-shelled seeds you'd scoop out of a jack-o'-lantern. The ingredient list is exactly one item: pumpkin seeds. No salt, no oil, no preservatives. That's it.

**Performance and build quality**

Freshness is where this product either wins or loses, and Herbaila mostly wins. The seeds have a mild, slightly sweet, nutty flavor with a pleasant chew — not the bitter, rancid taste you get from old stock. Texture holds up well across the bag; I didn't find any soft or moldy specimens in my testing, which is a common complaint with bulk nut products.

The packaging, however, is a minor letdown. It's a foil-lined bag with a fold-over top, not a zip-lock. If you're the type who opens the bag and forgets about it for three weeks, you'll want to transfer these to an airtight jar. Several reviewers noted the same thing. One buyer wrote, *"Great seeds but the bag doesn't reseal properly, so I put them in a mason jar and they stayed fresh for months."* Fair point.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The "raw and unsalted" angle is the big draw. One reviewer summed it up: *"Finally, pumpkin seeds that taste like pumpkin seeds, not like a salt lick. I use these for my morning smoothies and homemade granola."* Another mentioned using them for sprouting — a use case that requires truly raw seeds, and these deliver. The value proposition is also strong: 2 pounds of organic-quality pepitas at this price point is competitive with bulk bins, especially when you factor in the convenience of home delivery.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the broken-seed ratio. You'll get some split kernels and hull fragments mixed in — not a dealbreaker, but noticeable if you're using these for presentation-heavy dishes like a salad garnish. Second, the lack of a proper resealable closure is a genuine annoyance. Third, if you're looking for *roasted* flavor, this isn't it. These are raw, so they're blander than what you're used to from a snack aisle. That's a feature, not a bug, but know what you're buying.

**Who should buy this**

- **Home bakers and granola makers** who want clean, additive-free seeds to fold into recipes
- **Health-focused snackers** who want portion-controlled, salt-free crunch without processed oils
- **Sprout enthusiasts** who need genuinely raw seeds for germination — these work
- **Budget-conscious bulk buyers** who go through a pound of seeds a month or more

Skip this if you want pre-roasted, seasoned snack seeds, or if you only use pumpkin seeds once a year in a holiday recipe — the 2-pound size will go stale before you finish it.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these really raw, or are they roasted?**
A: They're raw. No heat treatment, no oil, no salt. You can sprout them, which confirms they haven't been pasteurized or roasted.

**Q: How should I store them?**
A: Transfer to an airtight container in a cool, dark place. They'll keep for 3-6 months. For longer storage, freeze them — raw seeds freeze well.

**Q: Can I roast these myself?**
A: Absolutely. Toss with a little oil and salt, spread on a baking sheet, and roast at 325°F for 10-15 minutes. You'll get fresher results than most store-bought roasted seeds.

**The Verdict**

**Skip it if** you need pre-roasted flavor, prefer smaller packaging, or are sensitive to a few broken pieces in your bag.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Solid value, genuinely raw, and fresh. The packaging and the occasional broken kernel keep it from perfection, but for the price and quality, this is a reliable buy.
