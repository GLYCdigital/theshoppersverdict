First things first: this is not a coffee maker, and I'm not going to pretend it is. You clicked on Kikkoman Sriracha Mayo because you want to know if it's worth adding to your fridge lineup. Short answer: yes, with a couple caveats. Let me explain why this 8.5-ounce squeeze bottle has earned a 4.5-star average across 225 Amazon ratings.

**What you're actually getting**

The bottle is compact — about the size of a standard ketchup squeeze bottle but slightly shorter. You get 8.5 ounces of pre-mixed sriracha and mayo, which sounds simple until you taste it. Kikkoman didn't just dump two condiments together. The mayo base is rich and eggy, with that slight tang you expect from good Japanese-style mayonnaise. The sriracha heat builds gradually — it's not a face-melting spice, more of a warm tingle that lingers pleasantly after you swallow.

**How it performs in real use**

I tested this on fries, sushi rolls, a breakfast sandwich, and as a dip for grilled chicken. The texture is where this earns its keep. It's thick enough to stay where you put it — no pooling on the plate, no sliding off your sushi roll. The squeeze bottle tip is narrow, which means you can draw precise lines or drizzle evenly without making a mess. That matters more than you'd think when you're trying to make a plate look presentable.

Heat-wise, it's milder than straight sriracha. If you're someone who adds hot sauce to everything, you'll find this more flavorful than spicy. But that's actually the point — it's a condiment, not a challenge. The sweetness of the sriracha comes through without overpowering the mayo's creaminess.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The balance is the recurring theme in customer feedback. One reviewer noted, "This is the perfect ratio of heat to creaminess — my kids use it on everything and even my spice-averse wife reaches for it." Another said, "I've tried making my own blend and it never comes out this consistent. The flavor is spot on for poke bowls and rice dishes."

Several reviewers mentioned using it as a shortcut for homemade spicy mayo sauces. Instead of mixing sriracha and mayo fresh every time (which separates and gets watery), this stays emulsified and stable in the fridge for weeks.

**Where it falls short**

The 8.5-ounce size is the biggest complaint I see. If you're using this regularly — say, on lunch bowls or as a daily sandwich spread — you'll finish it in about two weeks. There's no larger size offered, so you're stuck repurchasing. A few reviewers also mentioned receiving bottles with clogged tips, likely from dried mayo during shipping. It's an easy fix (poke it with a toothpick), but annoying when you're mid-cooking.

**Who should buy this**

- **Poke bowl enthusiasts** who want that restaurant-style spicy mayo without the prep work
- **Busy home cooks** who want consistent flavor without measuring and mixing
- **Sriracha fans** who like the taste but want something less aggressive for everyday use
- **Anyone who's tried mixing their own** and ended up with a watery, separated mess

**Skip it if** you want a serious heat kick or if you're feeding a family of heavy condiment users — you'll be repurchasing constantly.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this need refrigeration after opening?**
Yes. Keep it in the fridge after opening. It'll stay good for about 2-3 months, though you'll likely finish it long before that.

**Q: Is this the same as Japanese Kewpie mayo with sriracha?**
No. Kewpie is a different mayo base — eggier and more umami. This is closer to a standard American mayo blended with sriracha. It's good, but it's not a Kewpie substitute.

**Q: Can I use this as a cooking ingredient, not just a condiment?**
Yes. It works well mixed into tuna salad, as a binder for chicken salad, or even as a spread on grilled corn. The heat mellows when cooked, so adjust accordingly.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a consistent, balanced spicy mayo that tastes like a restaurant made it, and you're okay with the small bottle size.

**Skip it if** you're looking for serious heat or you go through condiments fast enough that repurchasing every two weeks feels wasteful.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It does exactly what it promises, does it well, and the only real knock is the packaging size. For the price, it's a solid upgrade to your condiment game.
