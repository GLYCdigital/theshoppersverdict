**What You're Actually Getting**

This is a 2.6-ounce canister of certified organic curry powder. The ingredient list reads like a proper Indian spice box: turmeric, coriander, cumin, fenugreek, ginger, black pepper, cayenne, and fennel. No salt added as a cheap filler. No anti-caking agents. No artificial anything. The blend is ground fine enough to dissolve into sauces smoothly but coarse enough that you can see individual spice flecks — a sign it wasn't over-processed.

**Real-World Performance**

I tested this three ways: a coconut-based vegetable curry, a quick chicken marinade with yogurt, and roasted sweet potatoes. In each case, the spice bloomed nicely when toasted in oil for 30 seconds — that's when you smell the cumin and coriander wake up. The flavor is warming and aromatic rather than punishingly hot. The cayenne gives a gentle tingle at the back of the throat, but this isn't a blend that'll make you reach for water.

The color payoff is genuinely impressive. Your dishes turn that deep golden-yellow you associate with proper curry houses, and it doesn't fade during cooking. That's the turmeric doing real work — some cheaper blends use annatto or artificial coloring to fake it.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the limitations. The heat level is mild — if you're someone who adds extra cayenne to everything, this will feel timid. Some reviewers noted they had to supplement with additional chili powder or garam masala to get the depth they wanted.

The canister is also smaller than typical supermarket jars. At 2.6 ounces, you'll burn through this in a month if you cook curry weekly. That's fine for the price point, but be prepared to reorder.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for the home cook who wants a reliable, clean curry powder for weeknight dinners without maintaining a full spice cabinet. It's perfect for:

**FAQ**

**Is this curry powder spicy?**
Mild to medium at most. The cayenne is present but restrained. Kids can eat it, but adults wanting real heat should add fresh chilies or extra cayenne.

**Does it contain salt?**
No. The ingredient list is just spices. You control the salt, which is helpful if you're watching sodium intake.

**How should I store it?**
Keep it in a cool, dark place away from your stove. The airtight canister with a sifter lid helps, but like all ground spices, it'll lose potency after 6-8 months.
