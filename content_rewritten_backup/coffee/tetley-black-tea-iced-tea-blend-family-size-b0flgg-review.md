**Performance and Real-World Brewing**

This blend is specifically formulated for cold brewing or hot brewing followed by pouring over ice. The leaf cut is fine but not dusty, which means you get a strong, brisk extraction in about 5 minutes of steeping. I tested it both ways: hot-brewed and poured over ice, and cold-brewed overnight in the fridge. The hot method produced a deep amber color with a clean, astringent bite — that classic “sweet tea base” flavor. The cold method was smoother, less tannic, and slightly sweeter on the palate. Neither method produced the bitter, cloudy results you get from steeping standard orange pekoe too long.

**FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask**

**Q: Can I use these bags for hot tea?**
Yes, but expect a stronger cup than a standard tea bag. Steep for 2-3 minutes maximum, not 5, or it will be too astringent for hot drinking.

**Q: How long does brewed tea last in the fridge?**
3-4 days max. After that, the tea will oxidize and develop a stale, slightly sour taste. Don’t push it to a week.

**Q: Is this the same as Tetley’s regular black tea?**
No. This is a different leaf blend (usually a mix of Assam and African teas) specifically processed for cold brewing. It’s bolder and less floral than the standard orange pekoe.
