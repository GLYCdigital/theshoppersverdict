Let's be direct: this is a laxative tea. You're not buying it for the flavor profile. You're buying it because you're backed up and desperate. The question is whether 96 tea bags of organic senna leaf is the right answer for your money and your digestive system.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains 96 individually foil-wrapped tea bags. That's 8 weeks of nightly use if you're following the label, or closer to 6 months if you're using it as an occasional rescue (which is what I'd actually recommend). Each bag delivers 900mg of organic senna leaf — the active stimulant laxative — blended with fennel, ginger, orange peel, and licorice root. The foil wrappers matter more than you'd think: senna degrades with moisture and air exposure, so the individual packaging keeps each bag fresh until you're ready for it.

## How It Performs

Brew time is 10-15 minutes in hot water. The taste is... tolerable. The fennel and licorice do heavy lifting to mask the bitterness of senna, but this isn't an herbal tea you'll sip for pleasure. It's medicine with a pleasant enough disguise.

The real performance metric: does it work? Yes, predictably. Most users report results within 6-12 hours of drinking it at bedtime. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: "I drank it before bed and by morning, things were moving. Not explosive, just normal. It was the first normal bowel movement I'd had in a week." Another noted the dosage flexibility: "I cut the steep time to 5 minutes and it was gentler. You really can dial it in."

The supporting herbs aren't just for taste. Fennel and ginger are carminatives that reduce gas and bloating, which matters because senna alone can leave you feeling distended. The licorice root adds a natural sweetness that makes the whole cup more palatable than straight senna.

## Where It Falls Short

The cramping is the elephant in the room. Even at the recommended dose, senna is a stimulant laxative — it forcibly contracts your intestinal muscles. Some users describe the first hour as genuinely uncomfortable. One reviewer warned: "If you have a sensitive stomach, start with half a cup. I made the mistake of drinking a full cup and spent an hour curled up on the couch."

The other limitation: this isn't for chronic use. Your colon builds tolerance to stimulant laxatives, and relying on them daily can weaken natural bowel function. If you need this more than 2-3 times a week, you need a doctor, not more tea.
