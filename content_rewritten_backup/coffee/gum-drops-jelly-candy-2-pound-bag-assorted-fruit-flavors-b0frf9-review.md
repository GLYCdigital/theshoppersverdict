**Full Review Body:**

Most 2-pound candy bags are a lie. You know the drill — a giant pouch that's 40% air, with a sad handful of candy rattling at the bottom. That's not the case here. This gum drops bag from the assorted fruit flavors line is genuinely packed to the advertised weight, and multiple Amazon reviewers confirm it. One buyer put it plainly: *"I weighed it on my kitchen scale — 2 pounds, 1 ounce. That's rare for bulk candy."* So if you're buying for a party, a candy dish, or a movie night that's going to last a month, the quantity is legit.

**What you're actually getting**

Open the bag and you'll find classic gum drop shapes — the dome-style with the sugar crust, not the gummy bear texture. The color spectrum runs through cherry red, lemon yellow, orange, lime green, and grape purple. As shown in the product image, the pieces are uniform in size, about the diameter of a quarter, which makes them easy to grab by the handful. The bag itself is a heavy-duty Mylar-style pouch with a zip-lock top that seals properly — again, not the flimsy press-seal that fails after two opens. This matters more than you'd think for a 2-pound purchase.

**Performance and texture**

Gum drops live or die by their chew. These have the classic two-stage bite: a firm sugar shell that gives way to a denser, jelly-like interior. They're not overly soft like some cheap brands that squish into paste, and they're not rock-hard like stale candy. The chew holds up for about five or six bites before dissolving. Flavor-wise, the fruit profiles are distinct — the lemon has actual tartness, the cherry tastes like cherry, not generic "red." That's a pleasant surprise at this price point.

**Where it falls short — honestly**

Three issues keep this from being a 4.5-star product. First, the sugar coating is aggressive. Some pieces arrive with a thick crust that can be off-putting if you prefer a lighter dusting. Second, if you live in a warm climate or order during summer, the candy can arrive slightly clumped together from heat exposure during shipping. It's fixable — toss the bag in the fridge for 30 minutes and the pieces separate — but it's an annoyance you shouldn't have to deal with.

Third, and this is the bigger one: flavor degradation over time. The first two-thirds of the bag are great. By week three or four, the fruit flavors start tasting flat and overly sweet. This is a 2-pound bag — most people won't finish it in a week. If you're a slow snacker, buy the smaller size or share with a crowd.

**Who should buy this**

This is for the person who wants a classic candy that doesn't pretend to be gourmet. It works for office candy dishes, kids' birthday party favor bags, or as a bulk option for baking projects that call for chopped gum drops (cookies, fruitcake, holiday bars). It's also a solid choice if you're hosting a retro-themed party — the flavor profile is nostalgic, not modern-gummy.

Skip it if you need sugar-free options, if you're sensitive to heavy sugar coatings, or if you expect artisan-quality candy. This is bulk candy, and it knows it.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does the bag stay fresh after opening?**
A: With the resealable zip-lock stored in a cool, dry place, you'll get good flavor for about 3-4 weeks. After that, the fruit notes fade and the sweetness becomes cloying. Don't expect 3-month freshness from a bulk bag.

**Q: Are these the same as gumdrops from the bulk bins at the grocery store?**
A: Close, but slightly denser and with a firmer sugar shell. They're a step above generic store-brand gumdrops, a step below specialty candy shop versions.

**Q: Can I use these for baking?**
A: Yes, and this is actually one of their better uses. They hold their shape when folded into cookie dough or fruitcake batter. Chop them with a lightly oiled knife to prevent sticking.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a genuinely full 2-pound bag of classic gum drops at a reasonable per-ounce price, and you'll consume them within a month. **Skip it if** you're a slow snacker, need sugar-free, or want a premium candy experience.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — Solid value, honest weight, and good flavor in the short term. The sugar coating and mid-bag flavor fade knock it down a peg, but for a bulk candy purchase, it delivers what it promises.
