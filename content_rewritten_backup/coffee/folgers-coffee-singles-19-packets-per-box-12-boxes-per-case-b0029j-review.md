Let's cut to the chase: Folgers Coffee Singles solve a very specific problem. You want a decent cup of coffee without brewing a whole pot, without a Keurig machine, and without measuring grounds into a filter. These pre-portioned packets deliver exactly that — and with 228 packets per case, you're set for months.

**What you actually get**

Each box contains 19 foil-wrapped packets, and you're buying 12 boxes — 228 single servings total. Each packet holds 0.46 ounces of Folgers' Classic Roast medium-ground coffee. The math matters here: at one cup per day, that's roughly 7.5 months of coffee. As shown in the product image, the packets are compact and stack neatly in a pantry or desk drawer.

**How it performs in real use**

The brewing process is dead simple. Tear open the top, place the packet over your mug (it drapes over the rim like a miniature pour-over), and slowly pour 6-8 ounces of hot water through it. Let it drip, give the packet a gentle squeeze, and discard. Total time: about two minutes.

The resulting cup is classic Folgers — smooth, mild, and reliably consistent. It's not a third-wave single-origin experience, but it's exactly what millions of Americans grew up drinking. The strength lands between diner coffee and office brew, which makes it an easy crowd-pleaser.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The convenience factor dominates the reviews. One verified buyer notes, *"Perfect for my camper — no electricity needed, just hot water."* Another long-term customer writes, *"I've been buying these for years. The taste is always the same, and they're perfect for when I only want one cup."* The consistency angle comes up repeatedly — same flavor, same strength, every single time.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, the cost per cup is substantially higher than buying a can of ground coffee. You're paying for convenience and portion control. Second, the environmental footprint is real — every packet is individually wrapped in plastic, and the outer box adds more packaging. If you're eco-conscious, this will grate on you. Finally, the flavor ceiling is low. There's no depth, no brightness, no complexity. It's a dependable workhorse, not a destination.

**Who should buy this**

These are ideal for office workers who want decent coffee without a shared pot. Campers and RV travelers who lack brewing equipment. Emergency kits where shelf stability matters. Or anyone who lives alone and wastes half a pot of coffee each morning. If you're already a Folgers fan, this is a no-brainer purchase.

**Real questions shoppers ask**

*Can I use these with a Keurig?* Technically yes, but you shouldn't. The packet is designed for manual pouring over a mug. Using it in a Keurig's reusable cup creates a mess and a weak brew. Stick to the intended method.

*How much coffee is in each packet?* Each contains 0.46 ounces (about 13 grams), which is slightly less than a standard 2-tablespoon coffee scoop. That's why the resulting cup runs milder than a typical drip brew.

*Do these work with cold brew?* Not effectively. The grounds need hot water to extract properly in this format. For cold brew, use a dedicated coarse-ground product.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want reliable, no-fuss single cups without buying a machine. **Skip it if** you're chasing bold flavor, trying to minimize plastic waste, or watching your per-cup budget closely.
