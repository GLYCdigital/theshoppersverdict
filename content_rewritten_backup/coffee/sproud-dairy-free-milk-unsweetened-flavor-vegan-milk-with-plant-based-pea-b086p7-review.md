# SPROUD Dairy Free Milk UNSWEETENED: The Pea Protein Contender

Most dairy-free milks in the coffee world fall into two camps: oat milk that's basically dessert in a carton, or almond milk so thin it might as well be flavored water. SPROUD's unsweetened version takes a third path — pea protein — and it's genuinely worth your attention if you're tired of either extreme.

## What You Actually Get

SPROUD comes in a standard 33.8 fl oz (1L) carton. The unsweetened formula delivers 30 calories per serving, zero sugar, and 7 grams of pea protein. That protein content matters: it's what gives this milk body without relying on added fats or gums. The ingredient list is short — water, pea protein, sunflower oil, a few stabilizers — and there's no added sugar hiding under a different name. As shown in the product image, it pours like dairy milk, not like watery almond milk.

## Performance in Coffee

Here's where SPROUD earns its keep. The pea protein base holds up under heat better than most plant milks. In espresso, it steams without that curdled, split mess you get with cheap almond milk. One reviewer noted: "Finally a plant milk that doesn't break apart in my Americano. It holds the crema better than oat milk." Another said: "Froths well for lattes, no weird clumping."

That said, it's not a barista-blend oat milk. It's thinner when steamed, and it won't give you that velvety microfoam you'd get from Oatly Barista or Califia Farms. It's serviceable, not spectacular, for latte art.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The nutrition profile gets the most love. Multiple reviewers called out the macros specifically. "Only 30 calories and no sugar — my go-to for keto coffee," one wrote. Another appreciated the clean ingredient list: "No gums, no oils that separate, no added junk."

The flavor neutrality is also a recurring theme. Unlike oat milk's sweetness or soy's distinct taste, SPROUD is relatively blank — it lets your coffee's origin notes come through. For black-coffee drinkers who want a splash of something creamy without sugar, this hits a specific niche.

## Where It Falls Short

The honest downsides:

- **The pea aftertaste.** Some buyers detect a beany, slightly savory note, especially in cold brew or iced coffee. It's subtle, but it's there. If you're sensitive to plant-based protein flavors, this may not be your carton.
- **Frothing ceiling.** It works, but it doesn't reach oat milk's thick, glossy texture. If your priority is latte art, look elsewhere.
- **Packaging.** A few reviewers reported dented cartons on arrival. Amazon doesn't always pack liquids with extra padding, and SPROUD's thin carton shows it.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Keto or low-sugar coffee drinkers** who want creaminess without carb load
- **People who dislike oat milk's sweetness** but find almond milk too watery
- **Anyone trying to reduce dairy** but missing the mouthfeel of whole milk

Skip it if you're a latte-art enthusiast, or if you can't tolerate any beany notes in your beverages.

## FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Does this taste like peas?**
A: In hot coffee, minimally — the heat masks it. In cold applications, some people detect it. Try a single carton before committing to a multi-pack.

**Q: Can I use this in a milk frother?**
A: Yes, it froths fine in handheld and automatic frothers. Just don't expect ultra-thick microfoam.

**Q: Is this actually unsweetened?**
A: Yes. Zero grams of sugar, no sweeteners of any kind. The sweetness in the regular version comes from added sugar, not this one.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a low-calorie, no-sugar plant milk that doesn't fall apart in hot coffee and has real protein. It's a solid everyday dairy alternative, especially for those watching carbs.

**Skip it if:** You need barista-grade foam, or you're sensitive to pea protein's flavor profile.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — SPROUD unsweetened is a niche winner. It won't replace oat milk for everyone, but for clean-ingredient, low-sugar coffee drinkers, it's one of the better options on Amazon.
