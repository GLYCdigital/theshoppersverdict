**FAQ: Three Questions Buyers Actually Ask**

**1. Can I use this in hot coffee?**
Yes, but it dissolves best if you add it to the hot liquid *before* it sets. Stir a teaspoon into your brewed coffee while it’s still steaming, then add milk. It won’t thicken the coffee—it just adds flavor and sweetness.

**2. How long does the prepared gelatin last in the fridge?**
About 7-10 days, sealed. If you’re using it as a syrup, keep it in a squeeze bottle and it’ll stay liquid at room temperature for a few days, but refrigeration is safest.

**3. Does it work in cold brew?**
Absolutely—but you’ll want to make the gelatin first, then cut it into small cubes and stir them into your cold brew. It dissolves slowly and evenly, which is actually a plus for sipping.
