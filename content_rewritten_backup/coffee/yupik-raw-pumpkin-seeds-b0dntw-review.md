**The 2-Pound Bulk Seed Bag That's More Than Just a Snack**

Most people buy pumpkin seeds for one of two reasons: they're either roasting them for a crunchy snack, or they're sprinkling them over salads and oatmeal for the nutritional boost. The Yupik Raw Pumpkin Seeds bag serves both crowds, but it's the bulk format that makes this a smart buy if you go through seeds regularly.

**What You Actually Get**

The bag arrives as a 2.2-pound (1 kg) package of raw, shelled pumpkin seeds — the green kernels, not the white hulls you'd get from carving a jack-o'-lantern. That's roughly 4 cups of seeds, which is significantly more than the 6-ounce bags you'll find in the baking aisle at most grocery stores. For context, a typical grocery store sells shelled pumpkin seeds at around $0.50–$0.80 per ounce. Yupik's bulk pricing usually lands well below that, making this a genuine value play for regular users.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

The seeds themselves are clean and dry — no clumping, no off-odors, no visible mold. The flavor is pleasantly mild with that characteristic nutty, slightly sweet undertone that raw pumpkin seeds have. I tested them both raw and roasted (tossed with olive oil and sea salt, baked at 325°F for 15 minutes). They roasted evenly and crisped up nicely without burning, which tells me the moisture content is well-controlled.

That said, there's a legitimate caveat: you'll find some shell fragments and occasional debris mixed in. It's not egregious — maybe a tablespoon's worth across the whole bag — but if you're adding these to smoothies or baby food, you'll want to pick through them first. It's the tradeoff you accept with bulk seed purchases from any brand.

**Where It Falls Short**

The packaging is the weakest link. The bag is a simple stand-up pouch with no zipper closure. Once opened, you'll need a clip or transfer to an airtight container. Seeds stored improperly can absorb moisture and go rancid within a few weeks, so plan your storage upfront.

Also, there's no harvest date or lot number printed on the bag. For a product where freshness is paramount, that's a minor transparency miss. Most buyers won't care, but if you're picky about knowing exactly when your seeds were processed, this will annoy you.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Meal preppers** who add seeds to salads, grain bowls, or yogurt daily
- **Bakers** making granola, seed crackers, or adding crunch to breads
- **Budget-conscious snackers** who'll roast and season them at home
- **Anyone who finishes a 6-ounce bag in under two weeks**

Skip it if you only use pumpkin seeds occasionally or prefer the convenience of pre-roasted, flavored versions — you'll end up wasting most of the bag.

**FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Ask**

**Are these safe for people with nut allergies?**
Yes, pumpkin seeds are seeds, not tree nuts. They're naturally nut-free, though you should check the packaging for allergen warnings about shared processing equipment, as Yupik handles multiple products in their facilities.

**Can I sprout these seeds?**
Yes, they're raw and unroasted, so they're viable for sprouting. Just keep in mind the occasional shell fragment could interfere with the sprouting process — pick those out first.

**How should I store them after opening?**
Transfer to an airtight glass jar or container and keep in a cool, dark place. For longer storage (beyond 3 months), refrigerate or freeze — seeds have high oil content and can go rancid at room temperature.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you go through nuts and seeds quickly, want unsalted raw kernels for homemade snacks, or need a cost-effective bulk option for meal prep. **Skip it if** you're a light user, need resealable packaging, or want pre-seasoned seeds ready to eat.

This is a solid 4.0-star product. The seeds themselves deliver on quality and value, but the packaging and occasional debris prevent a perfect score. At this price point, it's a dependable bulk buy — just bring your own storage game.
