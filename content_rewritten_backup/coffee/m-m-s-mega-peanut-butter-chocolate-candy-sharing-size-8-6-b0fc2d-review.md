**Opening: The Bigger Question**

Let's be honest — when M&M'S announced "Mega" versions of their classic candies, the immediate reaction was skepticism. Bigger candy usually means more wax, less flavor, or a gimmick that wears off by the third piece. After a full bag of the Mega Peanut Butter variety, I'm happy to report this isn't that. But there are tradeoffs worth knowing before you add this 8.6-ounce sharing bag to your cart.

**What You Actually Get**

The 8.6-ounce bag delivers roughly 15 pieces per serving (about 1/2 cup), totaling around 10 servings per bag. Each candy is noticeably larger than the classic version — think a thick, substantial disc rather than a thin oval. The shell is still that iconic candy coating, but it's proportionally thinner relative to the filling. That matters.

**Performance: Where It Wins**

The peanut butter filling is the star here. It's dense, slightly salty, and has actual texture — not the overly sweet, pasty center you get in regular peanut butter M&M'S. The thinner shell means you taste chocolate first, then the peanut butter hits with real presence. It's closer to eating a Reese's piece than a standard M&M, which is a genuinely different experience.

Heat matters with these. At room temperature, the filling is firm but creamy. Pop them in the fridge for 15 minutes and they get a satisfying snap when you bite through. Leave them in a warm car and they'll get messy — the filling softens faster than regular M&M'S, so plan accordingly.

**What Buyers Keep Saying**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 ratings reflects real enthusiasm. One reviewer noted, *"I didn't think they could improve on the original, but the bigger size gives you more of the peanut butter flavor that gets lost in regular M&M'S."* Another said, *"Dangerously good. I finished half the bag in one sitting without realizing."* The consistent theme is that the larger format actually enhances the flavor balance rather than diluting it.

**Where It Falls Short**

The price per ounce is the biggest sticking point. You're paying a premium — often 20-30% more than regular peanut butter M&M'S for the same weight. That's a lot for what's essentially a format change, not a new recipe.

The portion control problem is real. A "serving" is only about 15 pieces, and they're so satisfying that stopping there takes genuine willpower. The resealable bag helps, but it's a false comfort — you'll likely finish this faster than you expect.

Finally, if you're someone who prefers the classic thin-shell-to-filling ratio, these might feel too soft. The mega format leans heavily into the peanut butter, so if you're a chocolate-first person, stick with the originals.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Peanut butter purists** who want the filling to be the main event
- **Snackers who find regular M&M'S too small** to actually taste the filling
- **Party hosts** looking for a conversation-piece candy that delivers on taste, not just novelty

**Skip it if** you're on a budget, strictly counting calories, or prefer the classic M&M'S texture profile.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these actually bigger than regular M&M'S, or is it just packaging?**  
A: Genuinely bigger — roughly 40% larger by volume. The difference is obvious when you see them side by side.

**Q: Do they taste like peanut butter M&M'S, just bigger?**  
A: Not exactly. The flavor balance shifts because the filling-to-shell ratio changes. You get more peanut butter flavor and a softer texture overall.

**Q: How long do they stay fresh after opening?**  
A: The bag has a solid resealable seal. Stored in a cool, dry place, they'll last 2-3 weeks without going stale. They won't last that long in your house, though.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5 — Buy it**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely upgraded M&M experience with a richer peanut butter center and don't mind paying a small premium for the larger format.

**Skip it if** you're watching your wallet per ounce or prefer the snappy, chocolate-forward bite of classic M&M'S.

These are the first "mega" candy I've tried that actually justifies the name. Just don't blame me when the bag disappears faster than you planned.
