**The Whole Foods Turkey Jerky That Actually Tastes Like Turkey**

Most turkey jerky tastes like cardboard seasoned with regret. This one doesn't. The 365 by Whole Foods Market Original Turkey Jerky has carved out a loyal following — 4.2 stars across 210 ratings — and after breaking into an 8 oz bag, I understand why. It's the jerky you grab when you want something protein-dense but don't want to feel like you're chewing a leather boot.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The 8 oz resealable bag delivers roughly eight 1-ounce servings. Each serving packs 9 grams of protein, 70 calories, and 3 grams of fat — respectable macros for a shelf-stable snack. As shown in the product image, the jerky comes in thin, flat strips with a matte finish. Don't expect the glossy, lacquered look of mass-market beef jerky; this looks like what it is — minimally processed turkey.

The ingredient panel is where this product earns its keep: turkey, cane sugar, sea salt, celery powder, spices, and natural flavoring. No sodium nitrite, no MSG, no artificial anything. That's the Whole Foods promise, and it delivers.

**Taste and Texture: The Real Story**

Here's the honest breakdown. The texture is the standout feature — genuinely tender. You can tear a strip with your fingers without a fight. It's not the stringy, fibrous mess that clings to your teeth like some beef jerkies. This makes it a legit option for older kids or anyone with dental work.

Flavor-wise, it's a sweet-forward teriyaki-adjacent profile. The cane sugar is unmistakable — second on the ingredient list — and it shows. There's a gentle smokiness underneath, but this is not a savory, peppery jerky. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: "Tastes like a healthier version of teriyaki turkey jerky — sweet, mild, and easy to snack on." Another noted, "My go-to jerky. Great texture, not too salty, and the flavor is clean without that chemical aftertaste."

**Where It Falls Short**

The sweetness is the main divider. If you're coming from traditional beef jerky — think Jack Link's or Old Trapper — this will taste closer to candy than jerky. The sugar content sits at 4 grams per serving, which is noticeable. Savory purists should look elsewhere.

The value proposition is also worth scrutinizing. The 8 oz bag runs at a premium per-ounce price compared to mainstream beef jerky, and the bag's resealable zipper is functional but not great — it tends to lose its seal grip after a few openings. Transfer to your own container if you don't finish it in a week.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for the health-conscious snacker who wants clean ingredients without sacrificing convenience. It's perfect for hiking, office snacks, or road trips where you want protein without refrigeration. It's also a solid fit for anyone easing off processed meats — the absence of nitrates is a genuine plus. Skip it if you want bold, smoky, savory jerky or if you're watching added sugars.

**FAQ**

**Is this jerky actually gluten-free?**
Yes. Whole Foods labels it gluten-free, and the ingredient list contains no wheat, barley, or rye derivatives. It's also dairy-free.

**How does it compare to beef jerky in protein?**
It's slightly lower — 9g vs. 10-12g per ounce for most beef jerkies. But it's also leaner with less saturated fat, making it a better everyday snacking option.

**Does it need refrigeration after opening?**
No, but it's a perishable product. Keep it in a cool, dry place and it'll stay good for up to a week once opened. The resealable bag helps, though as noted, the zipper isn't the strongest.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a clean, tender, protein-rich snack with honest ingredients and don't mind a sweet flavor profile.

**Skip it if:** You crave bold, savory jerky or need maximum protein per dollar.

This is a genuinely good product that does exactly what it claims — clean turkey jerky with a pleasant texture. It's not the cheapest, and it's not the most savory, but for what it is, it earns its 4.2 stars.
