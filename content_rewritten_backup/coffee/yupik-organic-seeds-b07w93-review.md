**FAQ**

**Are these seeds raw or roasted?**
They’re raw, which makes them more versatile for baking and cooking. You can roast them yourself if you prefer that flavor.

**How should I store them after opening?**
Keep them in the resealable bag, but for long-term storage, transfer to an airtight container in a cool, dark place. Refrigeration extends freshness significantly, especially in warmer climates.

**Can I sprout these seeds?**
The mix contains multiple seed types with different sprouting requirements, so it’s not ideal for sprouting. Buy single-variety sprouting seeds for that purpose.
