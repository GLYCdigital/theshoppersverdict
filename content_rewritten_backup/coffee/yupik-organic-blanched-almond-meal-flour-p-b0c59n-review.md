**FAQ**

**Q: Is this the same as almond flour, or is it meal?**  
A: It’s labeled "meal/flour" because it’s finer than coarse meal but not as powdery as super-fine flour. For 95% of recipes (cookies, breads, coatings), it works as a direct 1:1 substitute.

**Q: Does it taste bitter?**  
A: No. Blanching removes the skins, which is where the bitterness lives. You get a clean, sweet almond flavor.

**Q: How should I store it?**  
A: Fridge or freezer. Almond flour goes rancid in warm cupboards in about 2-3 months. The resealable bag is fine, but an airtight jar extends freshness significantly.
