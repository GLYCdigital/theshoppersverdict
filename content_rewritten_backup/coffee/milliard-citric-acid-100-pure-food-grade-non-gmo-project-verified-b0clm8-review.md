**Real-world performance**

I tested this in a standard drip machine and a single-serve pod brewer. The ratio that works consistently: 2 tablespoons per 32 ounces of hot water. Run it through a full brew cycle, let it sit 30 minutes, then run two plain water cycles. **Frequently asked questions**

**Q: Is this safe for my espresso machine?**
Yes, but use half the concentration for espresso machines — 1 tablespoon per 32 ounces. Espresso boilers are more sensitive to aggressive descaling. Rinse thoroughly with two full reservoir cycles afterward.

**Q: How does this compare to commercial descalers like Dezcal or Urnex?**
Those are typically 95-99% citric acid with a few additives. You're paying for convenience and packaging. This is the active ingredient at a fraction of the price. The tradeoff is you must measure it yourself.

**Q: Can I use this on a Keurig with a removable reservoir?**
