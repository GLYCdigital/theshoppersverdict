**The cracker that thinks it's a chip — and mostly pulls it off**

Here's the honest truth about Crunchmaster Multi Grain Crackers: they're the rare gluten-free cracker that doesn't taste like cardboard. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, this isn't a niche health-food cult following — it's a mainstream snack that earns its place in your pantry. But before you add it to your cart, let's talk about what you're actually getting.

**What's in the box**

The 21-ounce box is a serious commitment. Most cracker boxes top out around 9 to 12 ounces, so this roughly doubles what you'd typically get. Inside, you'll find the crackers sealed in a large plastic tray — not individual serving packs. That means once you open it, you're committed to eating them within a few weeks or transferring them to an airtight container.

As shown in the product image, these are thin, square crackers with a visible multi-grain texture. Brown rice flour, whole grain corn, sesame seeds, quinoa, and flaxseed make up the base. The ingredient list is refreshingly short — no weird preservatives or artificial flavors.

**Performance and real-world use**

These crackers deliver serious crunch. The texture is closer to a thick tortilla chip than a delicate table cracker, which means they hold up well under substantial dips. I'm talking hummus, spinach-artichoke dip, even chunky salsa — they don't snap in half on the first scoop.

The flavor is nutty and toasty with a subtle sesame undertone. They're lightly salted, so they work equally well with sweet toppings (peanut butter, cream cheese with jam) or savory ones. The whole grains give them a hearty, substantial mouthfeel that makes a small handful feel satisfying.

One thing to note: they're baked, not fried, so they're less greasy than standard snack crackers. At 130 calories and 5 grams of fat per serving (about 13 crackers), they're a solid choice if you're watching your intake but want something that actually tastes like a snack.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The gluten-free crowd is notoriously picky, and this product wins them over. As one reviewer put it, "Finally a gluten-free cracker that doesn't taste like sawdust. My kids eat these faster than regular crackers." Another mentioned, "Great crunch and flavor. I keep buying these every month."

The value angle gets consistent love too. Multiple reviewers note the larger box size makes this more economical than buying two smaller boxes. "The 21-ounce box is a much better deal. I've been buying these for years and this size is my go-to."

**Where it falls short**

Let's be direct about the downsides. First, shipping fragility is a real issue. The crackers are thin and brittle, and several reviewers report receiving boxes "with a lot of broken pieces." It's not a dealbreaker — crushed crackers still work in soups or as breading — but if you want pristine crackers for a charcuterie board, buy them in-store.

Second, the light salt level won't satisfy everyone. If you're used to flavored crackers like Cheez-Its or seasoned Wheat Thins, these will taste almost plain. You're getting a clean, wholesome flavor — not a flavor bomb.

Finally, the 21-ounce box is a storage commitment. It's tall and wide, and if you're in a small kitchen, it'll dominate your snack shelf.

**Who should buy this**

Get these if you're gluten-free and tired of sad, crumbly alternatives. They're ideal for parents packing school lunches with allergy restrictions, for anyone hosting a party with mixed dietary needs, or for snackers who want something crunchy that doesn't leave you feeling gross afterward.

Skip them if you want bold, aggressive flavor or if you're prone to eating an entire box in one sitting — the satisfying crunch makes portion control difficult.

**Buy it if:** You want a genuinely crunchy, gluten-free cracker that works with dips, cheese, or eaten straight from the box. The 21-ounce size delivers real value for families or frequent snackers.
