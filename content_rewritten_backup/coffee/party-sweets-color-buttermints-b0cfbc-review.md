Party Sweets Color Buttermints have carved out a loyal following among wedding planners and event hosts, and after testing them against the standard peppermint pillow crowd, I get why. ## What You Actually Get

The package delivers a generous bag of pastel-colored buttermints — think soft pink, mint green, lavender, and pale yellow. As shown in the product image, these are the classic rounded-square shape, about the size of a thumbnail. The count varies by bag size you order, but the consistency is what matters: each piece has that signature buttermint crumble — firm enough to hold shape, yet tender enough to dissolve on your tongue without aggressive sucking.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where these mints separate themselves from the cheap stuff. The flavor profile is a gentle vanilla-mint blend — the mint is present but doesn't smack you in the face like some commercial mints. The buttery undertone comes through clearly, which is the hallmark of a proper buttermint versus a generic sugar mint. Texture-wise, they melt slowly and evenly, leaving no chalky residue that plagues lesser brands.

I tested these at room temperature and chilled. Chilled, they're firmer and last longer — a solid choice for summer events. At room temp, they soften slightly but don't turn sticky or clump together in the bag, which is a common failure point for budget mints.

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty time. The most consistent complaint in the reviews is breakage. These mints are delicate by nature, and shipping can take a toll. Several buyers reported finding a noticeable percentage of broken or chipped pieces in their bags. If you're planning to use these as individual favors where presentation is critical, budget the time to sort through and discard damaged pieces.

The sweetness level is also worth flagging. These lean decidedly sweet — the buttermint profile is rich, not subtle. If you're serving these after a heavy meal or alongside other sugary treats, guests may find them cloying after one or two.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Wedding planners** who need bulk quantities in specific color schemes — the pastel variety is a major plus
