You don't buy a 5-pound bag of peanuts on a whim. You buy it because you're feeding a family, meal-prepping, or you've got a serious snack habit that smaller bags can't sustain. We Got Nuts' roasted unsalted offering in the 80-ounce size is built for exactly that kind of buyer — and for the most part, it delivers exactly what the label promises.

**What's actually in the bag**

Open the box and you'll find one clear poly bag holding five full pounds of dry-roasted, completely unsalted peanuts. No fancy packaging, no single-serve pouches — just a straightforward bulk quantity of product. The peanuts themselves are Virginia-style jumbos, which means they're noticeably larger than the standard cocktail peanuts you'd find in a 16-ounce jar at the grocery store. As shown in the product image, they're uniform in size and color — a good sign that you're not getting a mix of tiny pieces and broken fragments.

**Real-world performance and freshness**

The dry-roasting process here produces a consistent, crunchy texture across the entire bag. I tested multiple handfuls from different levels of the bag over a two-week period, and each one had that satisfying snap you want from a roasted peanut. There's no oiliness on your fingers, which makes these a cleaner snack option than oil-roasted alternatives.

The unsalted aspect is the real differentiator. If you're watching sodium for blood pressure reasons, or you're using these for baking (think peanut butter cookies or granola), having full control over salt content matters. I found them mild and slightly sweet on their own — the natural peanut flavor comes through cleanly without any bitterness or off-notes.

One reviewer summed it up well: "These are perfect for my homemade peanut butter. No salt means I control the flavor completely." Another noted they "stayed fresh for months in a mason jar" — which is important advice, because the bag itself doesn't come with a resealable closure.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be direct about the limitations. The lack of a zipper closure on a 5-pound bag is a genuine oversight. Unless you polish off the entire bag quickly, you'll need to transfer these to an airtight container to prevent them from going stale — and at 5 pounds, that's likely. We Got Nuts misses an easy win here.

Also, some batches appear to have more peanut dust and fine particles at the bottom of the bag than you'd find with more premium brands. It's not a dealbreaker — the dust works fine for smooth peanut butter — but it's worth knowing if you prefer uniformly whole peanuts for snacking.

**Who should buy this**

This bag makes sense for several specific buyer types. Bakers and home cooks who go through peanuts in recipes will appreciate the value — at 5 pounds, you're getting roughly 80 half-cup servings for less than you'd pay for the equivalent in small jars. Anyone on a low-sodium diet will find the unsalted profile genuinely useful. And large households or offices with a steady demand for snacks will blow through this bag before freshness becomes an issue.

**FAQ**

**Do these peanuts taste raw or under-roasted?**  
No. The dry-roasting gives them a distinct roasted depth while keeping them crunchy. They're not as deeply browned as some commercial brands, but the roast level is clearly present.

**How should I store the leftover peanuts?**  
Transfer to an airtight glass jar or food-grade bucket after opening. In a cool, dark pantry, they'll stay fresh for 2-3 months. Refrigeration extends that further.

**Are these good for making peanut butter?**  
Yes, very. The unsalted profile means you control the salt, and the dry roast means you don't need to de-oil them much. Process them in a food processor for 5-8 minutes and you'll get smooth, natural peanut butter.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want bulk quantity at a smart price, you're baking or cooking with peanuts, or you need truly unsalted nuts for dietary reasons.

**Skip it if:** You prefer salted snacks, you don't have an airtight storage container on hand, or you're not going to use 5 pounds within a few months.
