**The 11.7% Protein Secret: Why This Flour Bakes Differently**

Most shoppers grab whatever all-purpose flour is on sale without thinking about protein content. That's a mistake. Protein percentage determines gluten development, which dictates whether your cookies spread properly or your bread rises tall. King Arthur's organic all-purpose flour sits at 11.7% protein — notably higher than the 10-10.5% found in most national brands. This single number changes your baking outcomes more than any other variable, and it's the primary reason this flour has earned a 4.6-star average across 229 Amazon ratings.

**What You're Actually Getting**

The 5 lb paper bag arrives with no frills — just flour, a clear nutrition label, and the recognizable King Arthur branding. Expect a fine, pale cream-colored powder with a clean, slightly sweet aroma. It's unbleached, meaning it hasn't been treated with chlorine dioxide or benzoyl peroxide to whiten it artificially. It's also unbromated — no potassium bromate, a dough conditioner some commercial flours still use. For anyone sensitive to chemical aftertastes in baked goods, this matters. As one verified buyer noted, *"I can taste the difference in my pie crusts — cleaner, more buttery, not that faint chemical note I get from cheaper bleached flours."*

**Real-World Performance**

The higher protein content delivers tangible results. Cookies hold their structure without turning into flat puddles. Sandwich bread develops a proper crumb with enough chew to satisfy without requiring bread flour. Scones and biscuits come out tender but not crumbly. The flour hydrates predictably — doughs feel slightly more elastic than with lower-protein flours, so you may need to adjust liquid ratios by a tablespoon or two on your first batch. It's not a dramatic learning curve, but it's worth knowing before you swap it into a recipe written for bargain-bin flour.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be direct about the drawbacks. The 5 lb size is genuinely inconvenient for anyone baking weekly. At roughly 20 cups per bag, a single loaf of bread plus a batch of cookies will deplete it quickly. The paper bag has no resealable closure — you'll want to transfer the flour to a glass jar or plastic container immediately to keep out moisture and pantry moths. And the price premium is real. You're paying 30-50% more per pound than conventional all-purpose flour. Whether that's justified depends entirely on how much you value organic certification and consistent protein content.

**Who Should Buy This**

This flour suits three specific buyers: the home baker who has hit a plateau and wants consistent, reliable results batch after batch; the health-conscious cook who wants organic certification without sacrificing baking performance; and anyone who bakes for others — dinner party breads, birthday cakes, holiday cookies — where texture and flavor matter. It's a poor fit for occasional bakers who go through one bag every six months, or for high-volume bakers who need 25 lb bulk bags to keep costs reasonable.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

*Can I substitute this 1:1 for all-purpose flour in any recipe?*
Yes, but expect slightly chewier results in delicate items like angel food cake or shortbread. For yeast breads, you'll get better structure. For cakes, consider sifting twice to aerate.

*Is this flour bleached or treated with additives?*
No. It's unbleached, unbromated, and contains no chemical additives — just organic wheat.

*How should I store a 5 lb bag?*
Transfer to an airtight container immediately. For longer storage, freeze it for 48 hours to kill any potential pantry pests, then keep it in a cool, dark cabinet.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you bake regularly and want a single flour that handles both breads and pastries competently, with clean organic sourcing. **Skip it if** you're on a tight budget, bake infrequently, or need bulk quantities for cost efficiency.

The higher protein content, clean ingredient profile, and consistent performance justify the premium for serious home bakers. At 4.6 stars, this flour isn't a gimmick — it's a workhorse that delivers measurable improvements to your baking.
