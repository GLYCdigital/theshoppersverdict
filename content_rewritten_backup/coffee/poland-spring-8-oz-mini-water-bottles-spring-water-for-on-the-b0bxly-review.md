## Where It Falls Short

Honest downsides exist. First, the plastic waste. If you use one daily for coffee, that's 30 bottles a month hitting the recycling bin. A reusable bottle with a measuring cup is more eco-friendly, though less convenient.

Second, the lack of a sports cap. You must fully unscrew the lid. For a child or someone driving, that's a minor annoyance. A flip-top would make these perfect.

Third, the price per ounce is higher than buying larger bottles. You're paying for convenience and portion control, not value. If you just need hydration, a 24-pack of 16.9 oz bottles is cheaper per gallon.
