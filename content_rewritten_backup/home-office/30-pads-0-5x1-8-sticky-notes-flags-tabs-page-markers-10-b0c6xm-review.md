I've tested these flags on three surfaces: standard printer paper, a glossy magazine cover, and a paperback book page with a matte finish. The adhesive is the standout feature. **Where it falls short**

Here's the honest tradeoff. These are single-use. The adhesive is strong, but it's not repositionable. Once you peel a flag off a page, it won't stick to a second location — it curls and loses tack. If you're someone who likes to move tabs around as you revise notes, that's a dealbreaker. You'll burn through pads faster than you expect.

The 0.5-inch width is also a limitation. You won't be writing "Chapter 12 — key formula" on these. The space fits a tiny checkmark, a number, or a two-letter abbreviation at best. If you need writeable flags, look for a wider 1x2 inch option. At this size, these are for marking locations, not annotating.
