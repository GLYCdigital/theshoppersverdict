Most pencil reviews are about graphite, wood, and erasers. The ECOTREE #2 HB Pencil is really about a question: can an eco-conscious pencil actually perform like a workhorse, or is it just a feel-good purchase? After digging through the 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews, the answer is more nuanced than you'd expect.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains 100 pre-sharpened #2 HB pencils. Nothing fancy — no bonus eraser caps, no fancy tin. Just a solid stack of hexagonal barrels in a recyclable cardboard box. As shown in the product image, these pencils have a natural, unfinished wood look with a subtle grain pattern. There's zero paint, zero lacquer, and the branding is minimal.

## Performance and Build Quality

The lead is where ECOTREE earns its keep. Multiple buyers describe it as "smooth" and "consistent," with some noting it compares favorably to brands costing twice as much. One reviewer put it plainly: "These write as well as any pencil I've used, and I've been teaching for 20 years." The HB (No. 2) grade is standard for standardized tests, everyday writing, and most office work.

The hexagonal shape prevents rolling — a small but meaningful detail for desk workers. The pre-sharpened tips arrive intact, which isn't always the case with bulk pencil packs.

Here's the catch: the wood is softer than premium competitors. That's the tradeoff for using recycled materials. It sharpens fine, but you'll lose more wood per sharpening pass. For heavy users, that means a slightly shorter lifespan per pencil compared to a Ticonderoga or a Dixon.

The eraser is the weakest link. It works for light corrections but tends to smear graphite rather than lift it cleanly. If you're a heavy eraser user, bring a separate eraser. If you're a student who needs to fix mistakes mid-exam, that's worth knowing.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The eco-friendly angle isn't just marketing. ECOTREE uses recycled wood and a natural finish, which several reviewers specifically called out. One parent noted: "No weird chemical smell. I feel good letting my kids use these." Another buyer appreciated the value: "100 pencils for the price of what I'd pay for 20 at the store — and they write better."

The most common praise centers on the lead quality and value per pencil. For classrooms, offices, and standardized testing, this is a practical bulk buy.

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond the eraser and softer wood, there's a consistency issue. A handful of reviewers reported receiving pencils with slight variations in lead hardness — a few wrote darker than others. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you need absolute uniformity (like for Scantron forms), test a few before committing to a full box.

Also, the natural wood finish means these pick up dirt and oils quickly. They won't look pristine after a week in a pencil case. Function over form.

## Who Should Buy These

**Buy if you're:** a teacher stocking a classroom, a parent buying school supplies in bulk, an office manager equipping a shared workspace, or an eco-conscious writer who goes through pencils by the dozen.

**Skip if you're:** someone who needs a premium eraser, prefers a lacquered finish for aesthetic reasons, or requires perfectly uniform lead density for professional drafting work.

## FAQ

Close on lead smoothness, but the wood is softer and the eraser is worse. If you're a casual writer, you won't notice. If you're a daily pencil user, you'll see the difference within a week.

**Do these work for standardized tests?**
Yes. The HB grade is standard for Scantron and most state testing. Just bring your own eraser for corrections.

**Are they really eco-friendly?**
Yes — recycled wood, natural finish, minimal packaging. This is one of the few pencils where the environmental claim checks out.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

The ECOTREE #2 HB Pencil is a genuinely good bulk pencil that happens to be eco-friendly, not an eco-friendly pencil that happens to be mediocre. The lead is smooth, the value is exceptional at 100 pencils per box, and the environmental angle is legit.

**Buy it if:** you want quality pencils in bulk without paying premium prices, or you care about reducing plastic and chemical waste.
