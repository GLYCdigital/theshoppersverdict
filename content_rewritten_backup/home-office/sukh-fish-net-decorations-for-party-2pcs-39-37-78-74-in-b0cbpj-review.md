This is a 2-pack of actual rope netting, each panel measuring 39.37 inches wide by 78.74 inches tall — that's a genuine 6.5-foot drop, which means it covers a standard interior door or the back of a 6-foot banquet table without leaving an embarrassing gap at the bottom.

## What You Actually Get

Two separate net panels, not one long sheet you have to cut. Each is constructed from twisted polypropylene rope with roughly 1-inch diamond mesh openings. The material has real weight to it — this isn't the tissue-paper-thin stuff you find at dollar stores. The panels arrive folded, so expect crease lines that relax within a few hours of hanging. There's no hardware in the package, no adhesive strips, nothing but the nets themselves. Budget for command hooks or zip ties.

## Where It Falls Short

The shedding issue is real. On first unboxing, loose rope fibers come off — not a ton, but enough that you'll want to shake the panels outside before hanging them over food or white tablecloths. One reviewer mentioned, "The first hour it shed little bits of rope on the floor. After that, fine."

The color is also worth noting: this is a dark charcoal gray, not a pure black. Against bright white walls it reads as black, but if you're pairing it with true-black decor, the difference is visible. Finally, there's no included hanging solution. It's a minor annoyance, but worth knowing before you're scrambling for tape five minutes before guests arrive.

## FAQ

**Can I cut these nets to a different size?**
Yes. The rope construction cuts cleanly with sharp scissors, and the mesh structure means cut edges won't unravel significantly. Just trim carefully and consider folding the cut edge over before securing.

**How do I hang these on a wall?**
Command hooks or push pins work through the mesh openings without damaging the net. For a cleaner look, run fishing line or clear string through the top row of holes and secure to existing hooks or nails.

**Are these safe for outdoor use?**
The polypropylene rope resists water damage, but the netting will fade with prolonged direct sun exposure. Fine for a single outdoor event; don't expect years of outdoor display.
