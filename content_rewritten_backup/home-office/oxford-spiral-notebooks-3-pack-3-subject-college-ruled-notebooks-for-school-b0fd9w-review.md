Each notebook measures 10.5" x 8", which is standard size and fits in most binders or bags without awkward overhang.

The college ruling is standard, but if you have larger handwriting or prefer more space between lines, this won't be your favorite. Also, the covers, while durable, are flexible — not rigid like a hardcover. If you need something to write on without a desk, you'll want a clipboard underneath.
