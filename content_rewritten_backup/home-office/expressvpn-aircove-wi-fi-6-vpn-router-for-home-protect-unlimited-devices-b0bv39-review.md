If you've ever tried to put a VPN on your smart TV, gaming console, or that ancient printer that still works fine, you know the pain. The ExpressVPN Aircove exists to eliminate that hassle entirely — it's a Wi-Fi 6 router with ExpressVPN baked directly into the firmware. Instead of installing apps on every device, you install the VPN once, on the router, and every gadget on your network gets protected automatically.

As shown in the product image, the Aircove is a compact black router with two external antennas and a minimalist design that won't look out of place on a bookshelf. It's an AX3000 dual-band unit, meaning you're getting Wi-Fi 6 with theoretical speeds up to 3 Gbps across the 2.4GHz and 5GHz bands. For most homes, that's plenty.

**What's in the box:** The Aircove, a power adapter, and an Ethernet cable. No setup CD, no USB drive, no paper manual worth mentioning — everything happens through the ExpressVPN app on your phone. If you already have an ExpressVPN subscription (required; this isn't a standalone router), setup takes roughly 15 minutes. Scan a QR code, log in, and the router configures itself.

**Real-world performance** is where the Aircove earns its keep. The built-in VPN runs on the router's dedicated processor, so you're not sacrificing your main CPU to encryption overhead. In testing, users on 500 Mbps fiber connections report getting 300-400 Mbps through the VPN tunnel — enough for 4K streaming on multiple devices simultaneously. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"I was skeptical a router could handle VPN duties without slowing everything down. My download speeds dropped from 450 to about 340 Mbps, which is barely noticeable in practice."*

The per-device routing feature is the standout. You can assign specific devices to use the VPN and let others bypass it — useful when you need to access a local network device or a streaming service that blocks VPN IPs. One buyer noted: *"I keep my work laptop off the VPN so my employer's security software doesn't complain, but my streaming devices and phone stay protected. Takes 30 seconds to set up."*

**Where it falls short:** The Aircove's simplicity cuts both ways. Power users will find the app frustratingly basic — there's no advanced QoS, no per-device bandwidth limits, and no way to tweak Wi-Fi channel widths manually. The 2.4GHz and 5GHz bands are combined into a single SSID, which is convenient for most people but annoying if you need to force a device onto a specific band.

The bigger issue: you're locked into ExpressVPN. You can't configure the Aircove to work with NordVPN, Surfshark, or any other provider. If you ever switch VPN services, this router becomes an expensive paperweight unless you're willing to flash custom firmware (which voids the warranty and isn't officially supported).

**Who should buy this:**
- **Streaming households** with smart TVs, Fire Sticks, or consoles that can't run VPN apps natively
- **Privacy-conscious families** who want one-click VPN coverage for every device, including guests
- **Remote workers** with employer-provided laptops that can't have VPN software installed

**Skip it if:** You're a tinkerer who wants granular router controls, or you're not already committed to ExpressVPN as your provider.

**FAQ:**

**Q: Does the Aircove work with Ethernet-connected devices?**
Yes. Any device plugged into the four LAN ports gets the same VPN protection as Wi-Fi devices. It's ideal for desktop PCs or network-attached storage.

**Q: Can I use my own DNS or ad-blocking settings?**
Custom DNS is available in the app, but there's no built-in ad-blocking or parental control filtering beyond what ExpressVPN's Threat Manager offers (which blocks trackers and malicious sites on the VPN tunnel).

**Q: Will this replace my existing router entirely?**
Yes — the Aircove replaces your router, not just sits alongside it. You'll connect it directly to your modem. If you have a mesh system, you'll need to run the Aircove in front of it, which adds a layer of complexity.

**The Verdict: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if** you want whole-home VPN protection without fiddling with individual devices and you're already an ExpressVPN subscriber. **Skip it if** you need advanced router controls or want the flexibility to switch VPN providers later.

The Aircove isn't the fastest or most configurable router on the market, but it solves a genuinely annoying problem — protecting devices that can't run VPN software — with almost zero technical effort. At this price point, you're paying for that convenience. For the right user, it's worth every penny.
