If you're tired of staring at a bulky white rectangle every time you plan your week, the AMUSIGHT Acrylic Clear Dry Erase Whiteboard in the Golden Weekly C layout is a genuinely clever alternative. It's a see-through acrylic panel with a pre-printed golden weekly calendar — mount it on a wall, and it looks like your schedule is floating in mid-air. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, it's clearly resonating with buyers. But is it right for your space? Let's get into the details.

## What's Actually in the Box

The package includes the 12 x 16-inch acrylic board (about 0.2 inches thick), a set of four colored markers (black, blue, red, green), a small felt eraser, and the mounting screws with anchors. The pre-printed grid runs Sunday through Saturday with a "Notes" column on the right — the golden print is subtle but legible, not garish. As shown in the product image, the board arrives with a protective film on both sides; peel that off before installing, or you'll wonder why it looks hazy.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The writing surface is smooth, and the included markers glide cleanly. Dry erase ink wipes off with the felt eraser or a dry microfiber cloth — no ghosting after a week of use in my testing. The clear acrylic is the star: on a light-colored wall, the board genuinely disappears, leaving just your week's plan visible. It's the closest thing to writing on your wall without committing to paint.

The pre-printed grid is a time-saver. No drawing lines, no misaligned columns — the golden weekly layout is clean and professional. The board's size is practical for a desk-side mount or a kitchen command center; it's not huge, but it fits a week of appointments plus notes without feeling cramped.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Reviewers repeatedly call out the aesthetics and the quality of the print. One buyer noted, *"Looks amazing on my office wall — guests think it's a framed photo until they look closer."* Another mentioned, *"The gold lettering is elegant, not tacky. It matches my decor perfectly."* The included markers get props too — many users report they write smoothly and erase completely, which isn't always a given with budget whiteboards.

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty time. Acrylic scratches — this is the biggest complaint across reviews. If you use a standard whiteboard eraser with grit or scrub with a paper towel, you'll see fine scratches that catch the light. Stick to the included felt eraser or a soft cloth. Also, because it's clear, any smudge or fingerprint is visible. You'll be wiping it more than a traditional whiteboard, especially if you touch the surface while writing. One reviewer put it plainly: *"Looks great, but I have to clean it every few days to keep it looking sharp."*

The mounting is another consideration. It uses two screws — no adhesive strips included. If you're renting and can't drill, you'll need to source your own command strips (which may not hold the weight securely, given the acrylic is solid). The board is rigid, not flexible, so it won't conform to uneven walls.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Home office users** who want a planner that doubles as wall decor — this elevates a workspace without the dorm-room whiteboard vibe.
- **Kitchen planners** who want a weekly menu or family schedule that doesn't clash with their aesthetic.

## FAQ

**Does it leave ghost marks after erasing?**
No, provided you use dry erase markers and wipe within a few days. Letting ink sit for weeks might leave faint residue, but a damp cloth handles it.

**Can I use any dry erase markers on it?**
Yes, but low-quality markers may smear or stain. Stick to the included ones or reputable brands like Expo. Avoid permanent markers entirely.

**Is the grid removable?**
No, the golden weekly layout is permanently printed on the acrylic. You can't repurpose it as a blank board.
