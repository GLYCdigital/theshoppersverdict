Let me start with the honest bottom line: at 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, the HERKKA 25-pack isn't perfect — but it's one of the better values I've seen for bulk dry erase lapboards. The double-sided design gives you 50 writing surfaces for roughly the price of 25 single-sided boards. That math alone makes it worth a hard look for teachers, tutors, and anyone running workshops.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains 25 lapboards measuring 12 x 9 inches — that's the standard letter-size-ish format most classrooms use. Each board is double-sided, so you're really getting 50 usable surfaces. HERKKA also throws in 25 low-odor black markers and 25 small felt erasers. The markers are a nice touch, but the erasers are where this kit cuts corners — more on that below.

The plastic itself is roughly 2mm thick, which gives it enough rigidity to write on without a hard backing surface. It's not flimsy like some thinner boards I've tested that flex and crack under pressure. The surface has a matte finish that grabs dry erase ink well.

## Real-World Performance

I've been using these for six weeks now, both in a training room and at home with two kids. The writing surface handles repeated erasing without ghosting — that's the dealbreaker for most cheap lapboards, and the HERKKA boards pass. Even with red and blue markers left on for a full weekend, a dry wipe cleared them completely.

One thing that surprised me: the included markers are genuinely decent. They write smoothly with consistent ink flow and don't dry out between sessions. That's rare in budget kits where markers are usually an afterthought.

The double-sided feature is the real winner here. In a classroom setting, you can have students write on one side, flip for a fresh surface, and only wipe down when both sides are full. It cuts transition time significantly.

What buyers consistently say in their reviews: "I use these for my 4th grade class and they've held up all year." Another reviewer noted, "The boards are sturdy and the size is perfect for small hands." A third mentioned, "Great value for the price — I bought two sets for my Sunday school class."

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest truth — the erasers are the weak link. They're thin felt pads that shed fibers and lose their effectiveness after a few weeks of regular use. Plan to replace them with proper dry erase erasers. It's an added cost, but the boards themselves will last far longer than the accessories.

The edges also have a slight roughness where the boards were cut. It's not dangerous, but if you have younger kids who drag boards across their forearms, a quick pass with fine sandpaper makes them more comfortable. A few reviewers mentioned this too — one said, "The edges are a bit sharp, so I sanded them down for my kindergarteners."

## Who Should Buy This

- **Teachers and tutors** — the bulk quantity and double-sided design make this a no-brainer for classroom use. You'll have extras for replacements.
- **Homeschool parents** — 25 boards means you'll never run out, and they're durable enough for multiple kids over multiple years.
- **Training facilitators** — the lapboard size works well for small-group exercises and icebreakers.
- **Anyone running workshops** — keep a set in your car for impromptu activities.

**Skip it if** you need boards for professional client presentations. The matte finish and plastic construction look utilitarian, not premium. For that use case, spend more on a set of porcelain or glass boards.

## FAQ

**Q: Do the markers stain or ghost after extended use?**
A: No — I left red marker on for 72 hours and it wiped clean with a dry cloth. The matte surface resists staining better than glossier boards I've tested.

**Q: Can I use any dry erase marker on these?**
A: Yes, standard dry erase markers work fine. The included ones are decent, but you can swap in your favorite brand (like Expo) without issues.

**Q: Are these safe for young children?**
A: Yes, but sand the edges first if you have kids under 7. The plastic is non-toxic and BPA-free, but the cut edges can be slightly sharp.
