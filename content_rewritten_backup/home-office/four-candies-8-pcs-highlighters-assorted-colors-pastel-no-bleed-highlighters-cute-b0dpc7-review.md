**Four Candies 8 Pcs Highlighters Assorted Colors Pastel — The No-Bleed Claim Holds Up**

Most pastel highlighter sets look cute in the package and fall apart on page one. The Four Candies 8-pack doesn't. After three weeks of daily use in a planner, on legal pads, and through a 200-page textbook chapter, these markers earned their 4.5-star rating — and a permanent spot in my desk drawer.

**What You Get**

Eight pastel highlighters: pink, peach, yellow, green, mint, sky blue, lavender, and gray. Each pen has a chisel tip that's roughly 5mm wide. The barrels are hexagonal, so they won't roll off your desk mid-thought. No case included — just the pens in a clear plastic sleeve. At this price point, that's fine. You're paying for the ink, not packaging.

**The No-Bleed Test That Matters**

The product name throws "No Bleed" right in there, which is a bold claim for water-based highlighters. I tested these on 20-lb multipurpose paper, the cheap stuff most of us actually use. The ink sat on the surface. No feathering, no ghosting on the reverse side, no dark halos bleeding through to the next page. I also tried them on 24-lb resume paper and a Moleskine notebook — both handled perfectly.

One caveat: dry time. The ink takes about five seconds to set. If you immediately write over a fresh highlight with a ballpoint, you'll smear it. Use a gel pen or wait a beat. That's the tradeoff for the no-bleed formulation.

**Build and Feel**

The tips hold their shape. After heavy use, the chisel edge hasn't frayed or softened. The ink flow is consistent — no dry starts, no sudden pooling. The colors are genuinely pastel but not so pale they're useless. The lavender and mint are the standouts; they're soft without being washed out. The yellow is the most opaque, which makes it the best for dense text.
