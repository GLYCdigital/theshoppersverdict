**If your Toast TP200 has ever jammed mid-service, you know the pain of a cheap paper roll. The Vonlyst Thermal Paper Roll is engineered to make that a non-issue — and at 4.7 stars from 235 buyers, it's clearly working for most restaurants.**

## What You Actually Get

Each order ships with 50 rolls of 80mm x 80mm thermal paper, with a 12mm core diameter that matches the TP200's spindle requirements exactly. The rolls are individually wrapped to keep dust off, which matters more than you'd think — dust is a common cause of thermal printhead failure.

The paper weight comes in at a solid 65gsm (grams per square meter). That's noticeably thicker than bargain rolls you'll find at office supply stores, which typically run 55-58gsm. Thicker paper means fewer tears when the printer pulls it through, and less ink bleed-through on the back of the receipt.

## Real-World Performance

I tested these rolls in a busy cafe setting over two weeks. The print quality is the standout feature — the thermal coating produces a deep black print that's immediately legible. No gray, washed-out text like you get with some budget thermal papers.

The BPA-free coating is a genuine plus here. That's not just marketing speak — BPA in thermal paper has been linked to hormone disruption, and restaurant staff handle hundreds of receipts daily. Vonlyst's coating is both BPA-free and passes FDA food-contact standards, which is a meaningful health consideration for anyone running a food business.

One buyer noted: *"We've been through three different brands since switching to Toast. These are the only rolls that haven't jammed once in four months of breakfast service."* Another long-term reviewer added: *"Prints are still dark and readable on receipts I kept from six months ago. No fading issues at all."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct: 50 rolls won't last long if you're high-volume. A busy restaurant printing 150-200 receipts daily will go through these in about two to three weeks. At the current price point, that's roughly $0.12 per roll — reasonable, but not a bargain.

There's also no recycled-content option. If your business has sustainability commitments, you'll need to look elsewhere. Vonlyst doesn't offer an eco variant of this roll.
