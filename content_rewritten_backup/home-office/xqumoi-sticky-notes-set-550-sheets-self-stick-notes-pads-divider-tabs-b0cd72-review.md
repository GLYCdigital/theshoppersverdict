**If you've ever watched a sticky note peel off a document five minutes after you stuck it down, you know the frustration.** The Xqumoi Sticky Notes Set exists to solve that specific problem — and based on 240 ratings averaging 4.8 stars, it's doing a solid job. This 550-sheet set isn't trying to reinvent the office supply wheel. It's just making the wheel work properly.

**What's actually in the box:** Five pads of 110 sheets each, in distinct colors (think classic yellow, pink, blue, green, and orange — not muddy pastels). You also get two sets of divider tabs designed to work alongside the notes for indexing documents or textbooks. The sheets measure a standard 3x3 inches, which is the sweet spot for margin notes without dominating the page. Given the price point, you're looking at roughly 550 notes for what you'd typically pay for 200 sheets of a premium brand — that math works in your favor.

**Performance is where this set earns its keep.** The adhesive is the star here. It's aggressive enough to stay put on vertical surfaces — I tested it on a monitor bezel and a whiteboard over 48 hours — yet it releases cleanly when you need to move it. No residue, no torn paper corners. The paper itself is noticeably denser than the flimsy sheets you get in generic multi-packs. Ink doesn't bleed through, and gel pens write smoothly without snagging. The colors are saturated enough to catch your eye but not so loud they distract from the note content itself.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The staying power comes up repeatedly. One reviewer noted, "I've had these on my desk for three months and they still stick like day one — the last sheets in the pad work as well as the first." Another mentioned the value angle: "For the sheet count, this is the best deal I've found that doesn't sacrifice quality." The divider tabs also get love from students — they fit standard binder systems and hold up to repeated opening and closing without tearing at the punch holes.

**Where it falls short:** The lack of a single-color option is the most common gripe. If you're someone who color-codes by pad type — red for urgent, yellow for general — you're stuck with whatever mix ships. The divider tabs, while handy, lose their repositionable grip after a few uses; they're better treated as semi-permanent rather than endlessly movable. And in humid environments, a few users reported the pad edges curling slightly. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing if you live in a coastal climate without AC.

**Who should buy this:** Students juggling multiple subjects will get immediate value — five colors means a natural system for five classes. Home office workers who live by task lists will appreciate the bulk count. Teachers, project managers, and anyone who burns through notes weekly will find the 550-sheet supply lasts far longer than the two-week lifespan of cheaper alternatives.

**Skip it if** you need a uniform color scheme, or if you're the type who uses sticky notes once and throws them away — the stronger adhesive is wasted on single-use applications.
