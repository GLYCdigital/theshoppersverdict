You've seen those "fireproof" boxes that are really just painted steel with a latch. The Noiposi Fireproof Document Box is not that. This thing actually has layered insulation rated to keep interior temperatures below 350°F for 30 minutes even when exterior flames hit 2000°F — which is the difference between your birth certificate surviving as paper or becoming ash.

**What You Actually Get**

The box measures roughly 15 x 11 x 10 inches externally, with about 13 x 9 x 7 inches of usable interior space. That's enough for letter-size hanging files, passports, a few USB drives, and your emergency cash stash. It ships with a combination lock (three-digit, resettable), a silicone sealing gasket around the lid, and a carry handle that feels sturdy enough for the 11-pound empty weight. One thing that surprised me: the exterior has a soft-touch coating that actually grips well when you're carrying it, not the slippery painted metal most competitors use.

**Real-World Performance**

The fire rating is the headline, and it's backed by actual testing standards (UL 350 level for 30 minutes). But here's what matters in daily use: the lock mechanism is smooth, the lid seals tight enough that you can feel the resistance when closing, and the interior has a removable tray for organizing small items. Buyers consistently mention the build quality — one reviewer noted, *"The hinges feel solid, not like the cheap ones on my previous fireproof safe that sagged after a year."* Another said, *"I tested it with a blowtorch for 10 minutes and the inside stayed cool to the touch."*

Water resistance is a genuine bonus, not marketing fluff. The gasket creates a real seal, and several commenters have confirmed their documents stayed dry after a pipe burst or sprinkler activation. That's a scenario most fireproof boxes ignore entirely.

**Where It Falls Short**

The combination lock has no key override. If you forget the code, you're either drilling or contacting the manufacturer. That's a real risk if you buy this for elderly parents or as an emergency document vault you might access under stress. Also, at 11 pounds empty, you won't want to move it around casually. It's a "set it and forget it" box, not a portable organizer. A few reviewers mentioned the combination dial feels slightly stiff when cold — not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **New homeowners** consolidating deeds, mortgage papers, and insurance policies
- **Parents** storing birth certificates, social security cards, and vaccination records
- **Small business owners** protecting contracts, tax documents, and backup drives
- **Preppers** who want fire and water protection without paying $200+ for a full safe

Skip it if you need UL-rated 2-hour protection for firearms or irreplaceable heirlooms — for that, you need a true safe, not a document box.

**FAQ**

*Q: Can it hold hanging file folders?*  
A: Yes, letter-size hanging files fit without bending. The interior depth is just enough for standard folders.

*Q: How does the 30-minute rating work?*  
A: It means the interior stays under 350°F for 30 minutes in a 2000°F fire. That's enough for most home fires to be extinguished or for you to retrieve it. It's not a bank vault.

*Q: Is it truly waterproof or just resistant?*  
A: Water-resistant. The gasket handles sprinklers, pipe bursts, and light flooding. It is not submersible.
