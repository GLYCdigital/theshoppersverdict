If you're still rocking a 9.7-inch iPad from 2017 or 2018, you already know the struggle: most accessory makers have moved on to newer models. That's exactly why the BMOUO case deserves a hard look. It's built specifically for the iPad 6th and 5th generation — not a one-size-fits-all compromise — and with 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, it's clearly filling a real gap in the market.

**What you're actually getting**

The case arrives as a single-piece folio design. The back is a hard polycarbonate shell fused with shock-absorbing TPU edges — that dual-material approach matters because it gives you rigidity without scratching the aluminum frame during installation. The front cover is covered in a soft-touch polyurethane with a microfiber lining that rests against your screen. As shown in the product image, the cover folds into a triangular stand configuration, giving you two viewing angles: one for typing and one for media consumption.

The magnetic sleep/wake function works through the cover, so flipping it open turns your iPad on instantly. Cutouts line up properly with the camera, speakers, and charging port — no fighting to plug in your Lightning cable or fumbling with the volume buttons.

**Real-world performance and build quality**

I've handled dozens of iPad cases, and the BMOUO's key advantage is the fit. Because it's model-specific rather than a universal "fits 7-13 inch tablets" monstrosity, the iPad snaps in securely without sliding around. The raised bezels around the screen and camera lens are substantial — roughly 2-3mm — which means face-down placement won't scratch the glass or lens. Drop protection is adequate for daily bumps but not military-grade; this is a workplace companion, not a construction-site tool.

Buyers consistently mention the same positives. One reviewer noted, "Fits my 2018 iPad perfectly, all buttons work and it feels solid in hand." Another echoed, "The stand is surprisingly sturdy — I use it for video calls and typing daily." The auto wake/sleep feature gets consistent praise for battery preservation.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, this case has no Apple Pencil storage. If you use the 2018 iPad for note-taking or drawing, you'll need a separate holder — that's a genuine oversight. Second, the polyurethane exterior is a fingerprint magnet. It looks professional out of the box, but after a week of use, it'll show smudges that require regular wiping. Third, and this is critical: do not buy this case for any other iPad. It's designed exclusively for the 9.7-inch 2018/2017 models (A1893, A1954, A1822, A1823). If you have a newer iPad, this won't fit.

**Who should buy this**

- **Office workers** using a 2018 iPad for email, spreadsheets, and video conferencing — the stand angles are ideal for hands-free use
- **Students** with a 5th-gen iPad who need basic drop protection and a lightweight case for backpacks
- **Budget-conscious buyers** who want a functional, protective case without paying flagship prices

Skip it if you need Apple Pencil integration, want a premium leather aesthetic, or own any iPad newer than 2018.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Does the auto wake/sleep feature work reliably?*
Yes, the magnetic sensor aligns with the iPad's built-in sleep function. Reviewers report it activates consistently, and it saves battery by preventing accidental screen-on time in bags.

*Is the stand stable on uneven surfaces?*
The triangular fold is solid on flat desks and tables. On soft surfaces like beds or couches, it can tip, but that's typical for any folio-style case. For lap use, consider holding it rather than propping it.

*Will this fit an iPad with a screen protector?*
Yes, most standard glass screen protectors fit underneath the raised bezels without lifting. Thick privacy or matte protectors might add enough bulk to cause slight pressure — test fit before applying.

**The Verdict**

The BMOUO case is a practical, well-built solution for a specific device generation that's often ignored by accessory brands. It's not flashy, and it's missing Pencil support, but it nails the fundamentals: precise fit, solid protection, and a functional stand. At this price point, it's a smart purchase for anyone still using a 9.7-inch iPad in a work environment.

**Buy it if** you have a 2018/2017 9.7-inch iPad and want reliable protection with a usable stand at a fair price.

**Skip it if** you need Pencil storage, want a premium finish, or have a different iPad model.
