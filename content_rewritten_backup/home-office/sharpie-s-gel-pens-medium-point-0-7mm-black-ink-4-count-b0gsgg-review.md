**Will these bleed through thin paper?**
In my testing, they performed well on standard 20-pound printer paper with minimal ghosting. On ultra-thin Bible paper or cheap notebook sheets, you might see slight show-through, but it's nowhere near as bad as liquid ink pens.

Yes, Sharpie makes the S-Gel in blue, red, and blue-black, plus multi-color packs. This particular listing is black only, so check other variations if you need a rainbow.
