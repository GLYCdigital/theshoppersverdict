If you've ever bought a "universal" iPad case and found the camera cutout misaligned or the buttons mushy, you know the pain. The JETech Case ONLY for iPad Pro 11 Inch sidesteps that entirely — because it's designed to fit every generation of the 11-inch Pro released between 2018 and 2022. That's a smart move for a product category where compatibility is usually the first thing to go wrong.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains exactly one item: the case itself. No screen protector, no stand, no extras — just a slim, hard-backed shell with soft TPU edges. It's clearly positioned as a "case only" product, which is refreshingly honest. The back is a rigid polycarbonate panel with a subtle matte texture; the edges are flexible rubber that wraps around the tablet's frame.

As shown in the product image, the camera module has a precise rectangular cutout that sits flush against the bump. The buttons are covered with responsive TPU pads — they click rather than squish, which is rare at this price point. The bottom speaker grilles and USB-C port are fully exposed, so you won't fight with third-party cables.

## Performance and Build Quality

I've handled dozens of slim iPad cases, and JETech nails the basics. The fit is snug without being impossible to remove — you'll need to apply firm pressure to pop the corners, but it's not a pry-bar situation. The raised lip around the screen is about 2mm, enough to keep the glass off a table when face-down.

The matte back is the standout feature. It doesn't show fingerprints the way glossy cases do, and it adds enough friction that the iPad won't slide off a slanted desk during a meeting. One buyer noted, "The back texture is perfect — grippy but not rubbery sticky." Another praised the "exact fit on my 2021 Pro, camera cutout is spot on."

Drop protection is adequate for everyday bumps but not rugged. The corners are reinforced with slightly thicker TPU, but the open sides around the buttons mean a direct corner impact could transfer force to the frame. If you're a construction worker tossing your iPad into a toolbox, look elsewhere.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest drawback is Apple Pencil charging. The case wraps around the side where the Pencil magnetically attaches, so you can't charge the Pencil while the case is on. You'll need to pop the iPad out to top up the Pencil — annoying if you're mid-sketch and the battery dies.

Also, the case adds about 3mm to the overall thickness. It's not bulky, but if you dock your iPad in a keyboard folio or use a magnetic stand, the added girth might interfere with those accessories. Check your setup before buying.

## Who Should Buy This

This case is ideal for three specific buyers:

- **The multi-generational household**: If you have a 2018 Pro and your partner has a 2022 Pro, one case fits both — though you'll probably want two.
- **The note-taker**: The grippy back and precise cutouts make it comfortable for long Notability or GoodNotes sessions on a desk.
- **The minimalist**: You want basic scratch and drop protection without bulk, a built-in stand, or a keyboard slot.

Skip it if you need a case with an integrated stand for media viewing, or if you rely on wireless Pencil charging daily.

## FAQ

**Q: Will this fit my iPad Pro 11-inch 2022 (M2) model?**
Yes. JETech explicitly designed this for all four generations — 2018 (A12X), 2020 (A12Z), 2021 (M1), and 2022 (M2). The dimensions and button positions are identical across these models, so the fit is consistent.

**Q: Does the case work with a screen protector?**
Yes, but expect a tight fit. The raised lip is minimal, so a thicker tempered glass protector might push against the edges. A standard 0.3mm protector fits fine; if you use a privacy or paper-feel protector, test it right away.

**Q: Can I use the Apple Pencil while the case is on?**
The Pencil attaches magnetically to the side, but the case blocks the charging coil. You can still use the Pencil detached, but it won't charge wirelessly until you remove the case.
