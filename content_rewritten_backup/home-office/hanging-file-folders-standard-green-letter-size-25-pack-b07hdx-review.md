Let's be honest: hanging file folders are the least glamorous purchase you'll make all year. But if you've ever had a folder rip off its hooks mid-drawer, scattering documents like confetti, you know the pain of buying cheap. This 25-pack from a no-name brand (at least on the label) has quietly racked up a 4.6-star rating from 229 buyers. I've tested them against the big names — here's the real breakdown.

**What's in the box:** You get 25 letter-size folders in standard green, each with factory-installed metal hooks and a pre-attached tab insert (blank, so you can write or print your own labels). The folders measure the standard 12.5 x 9.75 inches — that's the full legal-width fit for letter paper. They're cut from 11-point stock, which is noticeably stiffer than the flimsy 9-point folders you'll find at dollar stores.

**Build quality and real-world use:** The first thing I checked was the hook attachment. On cheap folders, the hooks are crimped on with a weak metal tab that bends after a few months of drawer slides. These have a clean, welded-style joint — I pulled hard on both hooks and they held firm. The folder body itself doesn't tear at the stress point, which is the most common failure I see in a busy home office.

Sliding them into a standard metal frame rack was smooth. The hooks have a slightly rounded profile that glides without catching on the rails — a small detail that matters when you're yanking a drawer open with one hand and holding coffee in the other.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The feedback across those 229 ratings is remarkably consistent. One buyer noted, *"I've bought these twice now. They hold up better than the name-brand ones I used to get at the office supply store."* Another mentioned, *"The box says 25 but I counted 26 — not complaining."* Multiple reviewers specifically called out the stiffness: *"These don't flop over when you're flipping through them. They stand up straight in the drawer."*

**Where it falls short:** The 25-count is actually a mild annoyance. If you're setting up a full four-drawer cabinet, you need 100+ folders, which means buying four boxes. There's no bulk 100-pack option here. The green-only color is fine for utilitarian use, but if you color-code by client or project, you'll need to buy a separate pack of tabs or different colors elsewhere. And the tab positions are fixed — you can't move the tab from the front position to the center without cutting and re-attaching, which isn't practical.

**Who should buy this:** Home office users who need a solid, no-frills filing system without paying a premium for a brand name. Small business owners who go through folders monthly and want something that won't deteriorate after a year of heavy use. Anyone tired of folders that sag or tear at the hooks.

**Skip it if:** You need color-coded categories, want a bulk pack to save money per unit, or you're a legal office that requires a specific archival standard — those users should stick with Pendaflex or Smead at a higher price point.

**FAQ:**

*Q: Will these fit a standard letter-size filing cabinet drawer?*  
A: Yes. They're cut to the standard 12.5 x 9.75-inch letter size and fit both metal frame racks and plastic hanging systems common in home office cabinets.

*Q: Do the hooks scratch drawer rails?*  
A: No. The hooks are smooth with no sharp burrs. I slid them back and forth dozens of times on a metal frame — no scratching or snagging.

*Q: Are the tabs replaceable?*  
A: Yes, the clear plastic tab inserts pop out and you can slide new labels in. The tab itself is attached to the folder, but the inserts are reusable.

**The Verdict:** At 4.6 stars, this pack earns its rating. It's not the cheapest option per folder, but the build quality justifies the price. Buy it if you want folders that survive daily use without drama. Skip it if you need variety or bulk pricing. For most home and small office setups, this is the right call.
