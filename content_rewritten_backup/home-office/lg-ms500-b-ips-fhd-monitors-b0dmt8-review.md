## The office monitor that quietly gets everything right

Most office monitors are forgettable. You plug them in, they display spreadsheets, and you never think about them again. The LG MS500-B breaks that pattern — not with flashy features, but with the small improvements that make eight hours of desk time feel less draining.

I've spent time with this 27-inch IPS panel, and it's the 100Hz refresh rate that separates it from the budget-pack. If you've been staring at a 60Hz office display for years, the difference is immediate. Scrolling through long documents, dragging windows between apps, even moving your cursor — everything feels like it's gliding instead of stuttering. It's one of those upgrades you don't realize you needed until you've tried it.

## What's actually in the box

LG keeps things straightforward here. You get the monitor, a stand that attaches without tools, an HDMI cable, and a power cord. The stand snaps in with a satisfying click and feels solid once locked down.

The 27-inch IPS panel runs at 1920×1080, which is worth being honest about: at this size, you'll notice pixel density is looser than a 1440p panel. Text isn't razor-sharp like you'd get on a 4K display, but it's perfectly readable and most office work won't suffer. For the price, this is a common tradeoff.

The real story is the port selection. As shown in the product image, you're getting HDMI, DisplayPort, and — the standout — USB-C with 65W power delivery. That USB-C port changes how you use this thing. One cable from your laptop handles video, data, and charging. No more hunting for a second power brick on your desk.

## Build quality and daily use

The IPS panel delivers what you'd expect: wide viewing angles, consistent brightness, and colors that look accurate without any tweaking. At 250 nits, it's not a HDR monster, but it's fine for a well-lit office. The anti-glare coating is effective — no annoying reflections bouncing off the screen during afternoon sun.

What buyers consistently praise in their reviews is the slim bezel design. One reviewer noted, "The three-sided borderless design makes this feel much more premium than its price point suggests." Another mentioned, "Setup took under five minutes, and the 100Hz refresh rate was immediately noticeable." That matches my experience — this looks like a monitor that costs more than it does.

## Where it falls short

The stand is the weak point. Tilt-only adjustment means you're locked into whatever height the desk gives you. If you're tall, or you use a standing desk with varying positions, you'll want a VESA arm — the 100×100 mount is there, but that's another purchase to factor in.

The built-in speakers are also underwhelming. At 5W per channel, they handle system notifications and maybe a background video, but music sounds thin and voices lack warmth. You'll want headphones or external speakers for anything serious.

And I'll repeat the resolution caveat: 1080p at 27 inches isn't crisp. For pure text work — writing, coding, data entry — you'll see slight softness compared to higher-resolution panels. It's a compromise that keeps costs down, but worth knowing before you buy.

## Who should buy this

The MS500-B is for the home office worker who wants a solid daily driver without spending premium money. It's ideal for:

- **Laptop users** who want one-cable docking via USB-C and 65W charging
- **Anyone tired of 60Hz flicker** in spreadsheets and documents
- **Minimalists** who care about thin bezels and clean desk setups
- **Multi-monitor builders** who want consistent, slim-framed panels

Skip it if you need height-adjustable ergonomics out of the box, or if you're doing color-critical photo or video work that demands higher resolution.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work with MacBook?**
Yes, via USB-C. It delivers video and charges your MacBook through the same cable. Mac users report plug-and-play compatibility without drivers.

**Q: Is 1080p sharp enough at 27 inches?**
For general office work, yes. Text is readable and colors are accurate. But if you're coming from a 1440p display, you'll notice the difference. For spreadsheets and documents, most users are satisfied.

**Q: Can I mount this on an arm?**
Yes, it has a standard 100×100 VESA mount. Just note the included stand is tilt-only, so if you want full ergonomic adjustability, plan for a separate arm.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a smooth, reliable 27-inch office panel with modern USB-C connectivity and a premium look at a reasonable price. The 100Hz refresh rate alone justifies the upgrade from older budget monitors.

**Skip it if** you require ergonomic stand adjustments, need 1440p sharpness for text work, or expect usable built-in audio.
