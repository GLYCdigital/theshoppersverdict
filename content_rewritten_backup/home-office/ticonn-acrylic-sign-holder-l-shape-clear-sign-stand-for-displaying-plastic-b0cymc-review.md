I tested this with standard 8.5x11 paper, a laminated menu, and a thick cardstock flyer. The 2mm acrylic handles all of them without bowing. Also note the orientation. This is designed for portrait-oriented inserts. If your signage is landscape (like a wide menu or banner), you'll need to rotate the stand itself, which means the base sticks out sideways. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing before you buy.
