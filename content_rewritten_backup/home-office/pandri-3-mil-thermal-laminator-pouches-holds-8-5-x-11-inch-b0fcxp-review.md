**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be direct: 3 mil is entry-level protection. If you're laminating menus, outdoor signs, or anything that will face bending, folding, or rough handling, you need 5 mil pouches. These will protect against spills and smudges, but a hard crease will fold the document inside.

Also, the pouches come out of the machine noticeably warm. That's normal for thermal lamination, but these seem to retain heat slightly longer than premium brands like Scotch or Fellowes. You'll want to wait 10–15 seconds before trimming edges or stacking finished pieces to avoid warping. One reviewer noted, *"They work great, but give them a moment to cool before you cut — I rushed it once and the lamination peeled slightly at the cut line."*
