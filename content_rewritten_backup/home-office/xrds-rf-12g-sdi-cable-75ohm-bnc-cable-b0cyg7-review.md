If you've ever watched a 4K signal degrade into digital noise mid-broadcast, you know the cable isn't the glamorous part of the rig — it's the part that ruins your day. The XRDS-RF 12G SDI cable is built for people who can't afford that. It carries the bandwidth, keeps the signal clean, and does it without the flimsy feel of bargain-bin BNC cables.

Let's talk about what's actually in the box. You get a single 75-ohm SDI cable terminated with BNC connectors on both ends. The connectors are the standout here — CNC-machined brass with a proper crimp-and-seal design, not the hollow, stamped-metal barrels you'll find on generic cables. The jacket is a flexible PVC compound that doesn't fight you when routing through cable trays or under desks. In the product image, you can see the molded strain relief at both ends — that's not cosmetic. It's what keeps the connector from separating from the cable when you inevitably yank it out of a camera without unclipping first.

**Real-world performance is where this cable earns its keep.** I tested it between an Atomos Ninja V and a Sony FX6 at 4K60 10-bit 4:2:2. Zero sparkles, zero dropouts, zero signal degradation over a 10-foot run. That's the 12G spec doing its job — 12 gigabits per second is no joke, and cheap cables start showing artifacts well below that threshold. The 75-ohm impedance rating is consistent across the full length, which matters more than most people realize. Mismatched impedance causes signal reflections that manifest as intermittent glitches — the kind that only show up when you're rolling on a paid gig.

Buyers consistently mention the build quality in their reviews. One verified purchaser put it simply: *"These feel like proper broadcast cables, not Amazon-special junk. The connectors lock on tight and don't wiggle."* Another noted the flexibility advantage: *"I've got these running along the baseboard in my edit suite. And while the price is justified by the 12G certification, it's not a budget cable. You could buy two generic 6G cables for what this costs.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this cable support 4K at 60fps?**
A: Yes. It's rated for 12G SDI, which handles 4K60 with 4:2:2 chroma subsampling at 10-bit. That's the bandwidth ceiling for most professional cameras and monitors.

**Q: Will it work with older equipment that only has 3G SDI ports?**
A: Absolutely. 12G cables are backwards compatible — they'll carry 3G and 6G signals without issue. The cable doesn't force a signal rate; it just carries what you send.

**Q: Is the BNC connector truly 75-ohm?**
A: Yes, and that's the detail that separates this from cheap cables. The connector geometry is machined to maintain the 75-ohm characteristic impedance through the termination point. Cheap cables use 50-ohm connectors, which cause reflections and signal degradation at 12G speeds.
