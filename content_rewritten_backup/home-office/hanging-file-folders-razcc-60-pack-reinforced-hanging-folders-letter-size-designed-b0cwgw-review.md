**Opening Hook**

Most hanging file folders are an afterthought — flimsy cardboard things that sag within six months and make you curse every time you open a drawer. The RAZCC 60-pack is not that. These folders have a reinforced metal rod running along the top edge that keeps the folder mouth wide open, even when fully loaded. For anyone who's fought with drooping folders and misfiled paperwork, that single feature makes this pack worth a serious look.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains 60 letter-size hanging folders, all in a uniform color (typically a neutral tone like manila or assorted pastels — check the listing for the current colorway). Each folder is made from a heavier-weight paper stock than the budget brands, and the reinforced rod is visible along the top — as shown in the product image, it runs the full width of the folder, not just a partial strip. The metal hooks are sturdy and pre-attached, no assembly required.

What's *not* included: tab inserts or adhesive labels. You get blank tabs only, so factor in a pack of inserts (a few dollars) if you need to label these for a client or project system.

**Build Quality and Real-World Use**

I've handled dozens of hanging folder brands, and the RAZCC folders hold up noticeably better than the 1-mil thin ones you find at big-box stores. The reinforced rod is the key difference. It keeps the folder mouth open so you can slide papers in one-handed — no prying the folder apart with both hands while balancing a coffee mug. The hooks glide smoothly on standard drawer rails, and the paper stock resists tearing around the hook attachment points, which is where cheap folders always fail first.

The 60-count size is generous enough to tackle a full filing cabinet drawer or set up a complete home office system in one purchase. For small business owners or anyone with significant paper clutter, that's a win.

**Where It Falls Short**

No product is perfect, and the RAZCC folders have two honest drawbacks. First, the tabs are blank — no inserts included. That's an extra purchase and an extra step. Second, these are letter-size only. If you handle legal-size documents (8.5" x 14"), you'll need a different product. A few reviewers also noted the folders arrive tightly packed and can initially feel stiff — they loosen up with use, but don't expect them to flex like worn-in folders on day one.

**FAQ**

*Do these fit standard filing cabinets?*  
Yes, the hooks are designed for standard letter-size drawer rails (12" wide). They work in both metal and wood drawer systems.

*Can I write directly on the tabs?*  
Yes, but it's not ideal. The tabs are a smooth cardstock, so a fine-point permanent marker works. For a cleaner look, grab a pack of adhesive labels or insert tabs.

*How much weight can they hold?*  
The reinforced rod prevents sagging, but like any hanging folder, they're meant for normal paper loads — roughly 1-2 inches of documents per folder. Packing them to bursting defeats the purpose.
