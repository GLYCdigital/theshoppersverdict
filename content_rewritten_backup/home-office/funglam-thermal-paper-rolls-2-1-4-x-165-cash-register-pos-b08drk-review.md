If your business burns through receipt paper like a food truck through napkins, the FungLam Thermal Paper Rolls 2 1/4" x 165' deserve a hard look. This 50-pack case isn't glamorous, but it's the kind of purchase that quietly keeps your front counter running. Here's what 240 buyers at 4.8 stars already know.

**What's Actually in the Box**

You get 50 individually wrapped rolls, each measuring 2.25 inches wide by 165 feet long. The core is 0.5 inches, which fits the vast majority of standard POS terminals, mobile credit card readers, and small cash registers. The paper is 80gsm — slightly thicker than the flimsy 55gsm stuff you sometimes find in discount packs. That weight matters: thinner paper curls more, jams more, and tears mid-print.

**Real-World Performance**

I tested these in a standard Epson TM-T20III and a Star Micronics TSP143. Feed was smooth from first roll to last. No jams, no paper dust buildup on the thermal head, no annoying curling at the exit chute. Print darkness is set-and-forget — the thermal coating responds well at default printer settings, producing sharp, high-contrast text that remains readable after several months stuffed in a customer's wallet.

One detail that stands out: the rolls are perforated cleanly. Every sheet tears off straight without dragging the next sheet halfway out of the printer. That sounds minor until you've wrestled with a roll that tears at a 30-degree angle mid-transaction.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The volume-to-price ratio is the headline. As one verified buyer put it: *"Bought this for our retail shop in January. It's August and I still have a few rolls left. The print is just as dark as the name-brand stuff we used to pay double for."* Another reviewer noted the packaging survives shipping intact — no crushed rolls, no crushed cores, no wasted paper.

The BPA-free claim also gets consistent mentions. If you're in food service or retail, customers occasionally ask about receipt paper safety. Knowing these are BPA-free gives you a clean answer.

**Where It Falls Short**

Be honest about the 165-foot length. Some competitors (like the popular 230-foot rolls) give you roughly 40% more paper per roll. In a high-volume environment — think a busy pharmacy or a checkout lane that runs 200+ transactions daily — you'll change rolls more often. The 0.5-inch core is also a hard limitation. If your printer takes 1-inch cores, these simply won't fit. Check your equipment before ordering 50 rolls of anything.

A minor gripe: the cardboard core is slightly thinner than premium brands. It holds up fine in normal use, but if you're rough when loading rolls, you can crush the edge and cause feed issues.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Mobile vendors** using Square or Clover readers — these rolls fit compact devices and the 165-foot length is plenty for low-to-moderate volume

Skip it if you run a 24/7 high-volume operation that needs maximum paper per roll, or if your equipment requires 1-inch cores.

**FAQ**

*Does this fit a typical Square reader?* Yes — Square, Clover Flex, and most mobile POS devices take 2.25-inch paper with a 0.5-inch core. This matches those specs.

*Will the print fade over time?* Thermal paper fades with heat and sunlight exposure. Stored normally, receipts stay legible for 6–12 months. Keep them out of direct sun and car dashboards.

*How do these compare to name brands like Kooline or Uline?* They're comparable in print quality and paper weight, but roughly 30–40% cheaper per roll. The tradeoff is a slightly less rigid core.

**The Verdict**
