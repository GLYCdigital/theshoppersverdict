I'm going to be straight with you: you don't need 24 mesh zipper pouches. But once you have them, you'll wonder how you managed without them. The Sooez 24-pack solves a specific problem — the chaos of small items scattered across your desk, bag, or craft room — and it solves it thoroughly.

**What you actually get**

The package includes 24 pouches in six assorted colors (four of each). Each pouch measures roughly 9.8 x 7.5 inches — large enough for chargers, cables, makeup, stationery, or small tools, but compact enough to slide into a backpack or tote. The construction is straightforward: a clear vinyl front panel, 200D mesh backing, and a nylon zipper running along the top. As shown in the product image, the color-coding helps you sort by category at a glance — red for first aid, blue for tech cables, green for craft supplies, and so on.

**Build quality that surprises**

For a bulk pack at this price point, I expected flimsy material that would fall apart in a month. That's not what you get here. The mesh is thick enough to resist punctures, and the seams are reinforced with double stitching — something I verified by stress-testing one pouch with a heavy power bank and a handful of cables. No tears, no stretched mesh, no zipper failure. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: "I've bought these three times now. They hold up way better than the cheap ones from the dollar store, and the zippers don't break after two weeks." Another noted, "The clear front is the selling point — I can see exactly what's inside without opening every pouch."

**Where it falls short**

Here's the honest tradeoff. The mesh backing means these are not waterproof. Spill a drink in your bag and the contents of that pouch will get wet. If you're storing liquids — think travel-sized toiletries or paint — wrap them separately or choose a solid-sided organizer instead. Also, 24 pouches is an awkward quantity. You'll use maybe eight to twelve regularly; the rest will sit in a drawer. That's not a flaw in the product, but it's worth considering whether you need the full bulk pack or would be better served by a smaller count.

**Real-world performance**

I tested these across three scenarios: a work backpack, a craft tote, and a kitchen junk drawer. In the backpack, the pouches kept cables and chargers from tangling into a knot — something my previous "loose in the pocket" approach failed at spectacularly. In the craft tote, the clear fronts meant I could spot the exact color of embroidery floss without unzipping six pouches. In the kitchen drawer, they corralled batteries, twist ties, and random hardware into labeled groups. The zippers hold up to constant opening and closing — no jams, no stuck teeth — and the pouches lie flat when empty, which makes storage between uses simple.

**Who should buy this**

- **Teachers and tutors**: Sort flashcards, markers, and small manipulatives by subject. The color coding is a lifesaver.
- **Travelers**: Keep toiletries, chargers, and meds separated in your carry-on. TSA-friendly since everything's visible.
- **Crafters and artists**: Organize beads, threads, or small tools. The mesh lets you see contents at a glance.
- **Anyone with a junk drawer**: Stop throwing everything in loose. Give each category its own pouch.

**Skip it if** you need waterproof protection, want a single premium pouch with leather trim, or don't actually have enough small items to justify 24 organizers.

**FAQ**

*Are these pouches TSA-approved for carry-on liquids?* They're not specifically TSA-certified, but the clear front makes contents visible during screening. You'll still need to follow the 3-1-1 rule for liquids in your carry-on.

*Can I wash these pouches?* Yes, hand wash with mild soap and air dry. The mesh dries quickly, and the vinyl wipes clean. Avoid the washing machine — the zippers can catch on other items.

*Will the zippers break with heavy use?* Based on my testing and customer feedback, the zippers are the strongest part of the design. One reviewer mentioned using them daily for six months with no failures. Just avoid overstuffing past capacity, which stresses any zipper.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're drowning in small-item chaos and want a budget-friendly, color-coded organization system that actually works. **Skip it if** you need waterproofing or only have a handful of items to organize.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A solid, practical bulk purchase that delivers on its promises. The lack of waterproofing and the sheer quantity are the only reasons it doesn't earn a perfect score.
