If you've ever tried to write a legible drink menu with a fat-tipped chalk marker, you know the struggle. The Loddie Doddie 24-pack with 1mm tips is built to fix that specific problem — and for the most part, it does.

**What you actually get**

Twenty-four markers with fine 1mm bullet tips, spanning a full spectrum: white, black, reds, blues, greens, purples, pinks, oranges, and a few pastels. Each marker is roughly standard size — about 5.5 inches long — and the caps click shut with a satisfying seal that suggests they won't dry out between uses. There's no fancy packaging or bonus eraser cloth included; it's just the markers in a simple box. For the price point, that's acceptable.

**Real-world performance**

I tested these on glass storefront windows, a laminated cafe menu board, and a porous chalkboard wall. The difference was night and day. On glass and laminate, the 1mm tip lays down crisp, consistent lines. You can write small — think coffee cup labels or table numbers — without the ink bleeding into illegible blobs. The bullet tip holds its shape well; I didn't experience the tip mush that plagues cheaper markers after extended use.

The color payoff is solid. The white is opaque enough to read from across a room, which is the real test for any chalk marker. The jewel tones — particularly the teal and magenta — are genuinely vibrant. One reviewer noted, "The colors are richer than I expected for the price. My window display actually looks professional now."

But there's a catch with the ink consistency. The pigment settles fast. Every marker needs a solid 30-second shake before first use, and even then, the first several strokes can be watery. One Amazon reviewer summed it up: "Great colors, but be prepared to shake these like a martini before every single use." That's not hyperbolic — if you leave a marker sitting for two days, you'll see separation when you uncap it.

**Where they fall short**

The biggest limitation is surface compatibility. These are liquid chalk markers, which means they're made for non-porous surfaces only. On real chalkboard paint or any porous surface, the ink gets absorbed and becomes nearly impossible to wipe off completely. Several buyers learned this the hard way. One wrote, "Used on my chalkboard wall and now I need to repaint it. The box doesn't make it clear enough that these aren't for actual chalkboards."

The 1mm tip is also a double-edged sword. It's excellent for fine detail work, but if you're hoping to fill in large areas or make bold signage, you'll be there all day. This is a precision tool, not a poster marker.

**Who should buy these**

- **Cafe and bar owners** running daily specials on glass or acrylic boards — the fine tip and color range are perfect for legible, attractive menus
- **Wedding and event planners** creating seating charts or welcome signs on mirrors and glass
- **Teachers** using them on glass whiteboards or laminated charts
- **Bullet journalists** who want chalk-style accents in planners without the mess of real chalk

Skip these if you're working on actual chalkboard paint, or if you need bold, wide strokes for large signage.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do these work on real chalkboards?**
A: Technically they'll write, but the ink sinks into porous chalk paint and becomes extremely difficult to remove. Stick to glass, acrylic, mirrors, and laminated surfaces.

**Q: How do you remove the ink?**
A: A damp cloth or paper towel with light pressure works on non-porous surfaces. For stubborn residue, a glass cleaner or a bit of rubbing alcohol does the trick without damaging the surface.

**Q: Are the 1mm tips replaceable?**
A: No. When a tip wears out, the marker is done. That said, I've used these consistently for weeks without significant tip degradation, so they should last through normal use.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need precise, fine-line chalk lettering on glass or laminated surfaces and want a full color palette in one box. The 24-color range and 1mm tips make this genuinely useful for professional-looking signage and detailed decorative work.

**Skip it if** you're working on porous chalkboard surfaces, or if you need broad strokes for large displays — the fine tips and non-porous-only formula will frustrate you.

Rating: **4.2/5** — A solid, specialized tool with real strengths and one significant limitation. Know your surface before you buy.
