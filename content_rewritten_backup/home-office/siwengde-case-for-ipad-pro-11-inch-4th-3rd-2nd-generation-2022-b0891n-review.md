Look, most iPad cases under $30 are either flimsy plastic shells or generic folios that fall apart in three months. The SIWENGDE Case for iPad Pro 11 Inch (4th/3rd/2nd Gen) sits in a different lane. With 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, it's clearly doing something right — but it's not perfect. Here's the unvarnished truth for anyone considering it.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the case itself — nothing else. No screen protector, no bonus stylus, no fluff. That's fine; you're paying for the case, not junk accessories. The case is made from a hard polycarbonate back panel with a soft, textured TPU frame that wraps around the edges. The front is a tri-fold folio cover with a microfiber lining that cleans your screen every time you close it.

It's compatible with the 2022 4th gen, 2021 3rd gen, and 2020 2nd gen iPad Pro 11-inch models. All button covers, speaker grilles, and the camera bump have precise cutouts — nothing's blocked. The Apple Pencil holder is a cutout slot on the right edge with a flap that secures the pencil in place, not just a weak magnetic strip that loses grip in a bag.

## Performance and Build Quality

The tri-fold front cover folds into two angles: a steep one for writing or FaceTime and a flatter one for watching content. Both are stable — the cover doesn't collapse under normal typing pressure. I'll be direct here: the angles are fine, but they're not infinitely adjustable like some folios with elastic bands. You get two positions, and that's it.

The back panel is rigid enough to prevent flexing. Drop protection is decent for a folio — the raised bezels around the screen and camera bump offer real edge protection. The TPU frame is grippy, which helps when you're holding the tablet one-handed during a presentation or meeting.

Here's what buyers consistently mention. One user said: "The pencil stays put even when I toss it in my backpack — the flap actually works." Another noted: "Fits my 4th gen perfectly, all buttons click properly through the case." The consistency of that feedback — fit, button response, pencil security — tracks with what I found.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. This case is **bulky**. It adds maybe 40% more thickness to your iPad. If you're coming from a slim snap-on back case, this will feel chunky. It also doesn't support any keyboard attachments — the front cover won't fold into a typing wedge, so if you use a Bluetooth keyboard, you'll need a separate stand.

The TPU frame is a lint magnet. It picks up pocket fuzz and dust easily, and the dark colors show it clearly. Also, the front cover's magnetic closure is strong enough to stay shut, but it's not a "smart" cover that wakes/sleeps your iPad reliably. It does work, but occasionally the sleep function misses a beat — a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:**
- You need a rugged everyday case for commuting, school, or fieldwork
- You use an Apple Pencil and want secure storage, not just magnetic attraction
- You want a case that fits perfectly across the 2020–2022 iPad Pro 11 models
- You prioritize drop protection over slim profile

**Skip it if:**
- You want a minimal, ultra-thin case that adds zero bulk
- You plan to use a keyboard attachment regularly
- You need multiple adjustable viewing angles for varied tasks
