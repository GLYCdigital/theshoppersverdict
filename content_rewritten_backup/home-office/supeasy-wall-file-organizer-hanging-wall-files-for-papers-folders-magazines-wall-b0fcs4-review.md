**What You Actually Get**

The unit is a steel-framed, multi-pocket wall file. It arrives fully assembled—no screwdriver required. You get the organizer itself, mounting hardware, and a set of adhesive strips for renters or anyone who hates drilling into drywall. The steel frame is powder-coated, which matters because it resists the rust and chipping that plagues cheaper painted metal organizers. The file pockets are deep enough to hold standard letter-size folders, and the sloped design keeps papers from sliding out when you grab one from the middle.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

Here’s where SUPEASY separates itself from the $10 fabric options. The frame is rigid. When you mount it properly into a stud, it doesn’t flex when you pull a heavy three-ring binder out. The adhesive strips work—but with a caveat: they’re rated for smooth surfaces. On textured drywall or painted-over wallpaper, they will fail. Use the screws if your wall isn’t perfectly flat.

The pockets are slightly angled downward, which is a smart design choice. It means you can see the tabs on your hanging folders at a glance, and you can grab a file with one hand without the whole stack coming with it. For a busy desk-adjacent wall, this is the feature that makes it worth the money.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, the pockets are not oversized. If you're storing legal-size documents or thick catalogs, they'll stick out the top. The product is explicitly aimed at standard letter-size paper, and it doesn't pretend otherwise.

Second, the adhesive strips are a one-shot deal. If you misalign the organizer on your first attempt, you'll need to buy replacement strips. Plan your mounting carefully. Third, while the steel frame is sturdy, the backing is a thin hardboard panel. If you're prone to overstuffing, the bottom pockets can bow slightly under extreme weight—nothing structural, but noticeable.

**FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask**

**Q: Will the adhesive strips hold on painted drywall?**
A: Yes, if the paint is smooth and clean. Wipe the wall with rubbing alcohol first, let it dry, then press firmly for 30 seconds. On textured or popcorn walls, skip the adhesive and use the included screws.

**Q: Can I mount this vertically instead of horizontally?**
A: No. The pockets are designed for horizontal mounting only. Rotating it will cause papers to slide out.

**Q: How much weight can each pocket hold?**
A: Safely, about 10-12 pounds per pocket when mounted into a stud. With adhesive strips, keep it under 5 pounds per pocket to avoid a sudden gravity event.
