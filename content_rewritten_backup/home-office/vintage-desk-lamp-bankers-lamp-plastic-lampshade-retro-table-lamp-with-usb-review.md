Let's be honest: most lamps under $40 are disposable junk. This vintage-style bankers lamp, however, has earned a 4.3-star rating from 267 buyers for a reason. It's not perfect — the plastic shade is the obvious compromise — but for $37.97, it delivers a surprising amount of practical value for a home office desk.

**What's in the box:** You get the lamp itself (base, arm, and shade pre-assembled), the T45 bulb included, and a standard AC power cord. No tools needed. The metal base has a weighted feel that keeps it stable even if you bump it while reaching for a notebook. Assembly took me under a minute — it's genuinely plug-and-play.

**Build & performance:** The shade is the headline feature. It's that classic green banker's dome, but in heavy ABS plastic rather than metal. Up close, you'll notice the difference — it has a slight sheen that real enameled metal doesn't. That said, the plastic is thick and won't dent when you inevitably knock it over. The adjustable arm uses a spring-loaded mechanism that holds position well; I've had cheaper lamps sag after a week, and this one hasn't budged in a month of daily use.

The included T45 bulb gives off a warm, 2700K glow — think cozy library, not harsh office fluorescent. It's bright enough for reading and writing, but don't expect task-light levels for detailed drafting. You'll want to pair it with overhead lighting for precision work.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The integrated charging ports are the surprise MVP. As one verified buyer put it, *"I didn't buy this for the USB ports, but now I use them every day. My phone charges on the base while I work, and it keeps my desk clutter-free."* Another noted, *"The warm light is perfect for late-night reading without killing my eyes."* The ports output 5V/2A — enough to trickle-charge a smartphone or top up wireless earbuds, though not fast-charge a modern flagship.

**Where it falls short:** Three honest complaints. First, the plastic shade does look cheaper in person than the product photos suggest — if you're a materials snob, this will bother you. Second, and more annoyingly, the USB ports only work when the lamp is switched on. If you want to charge overnight, you're also lighting up the room. Third, there's no brightness adjustment. It's a simple toggle switch on the base. For some, that's charmingly retro; for others, it's limiting.

**Who should buy this:** The home-office worker who wants desk-adjacent charging without extra cables. The student on a budget who wants a reading lamp with a vintage vibe. The bedside-table user who wants a warm, dimmable-by-position light for winding down. Skip it if you need precision task lighting for detailed work, or if a metal shade is a dealbreaker for you.

**Frequently Asked Questions:**

**Q: Does the USB-C port support fast charging?**
A: No. Both the USB-A and USB-C ports output a standard 5V/2A (10W max). It'll charge your phone steadily but won't trigger fast-charge protocols on newer devices.

**Q: Can I replace the bulb with an LED?**
A: Yes, it takes a standard E26 base bulb. You can swap in a brighter or dimmer LED, but the included T45 gives the best aesthetic match for the vintage look.

**Q: Is the arm sturdy enough for daily adjustment?**
A: Yes. The spring mechanism is tensioned well and holds its position. I've repositioned mine dozens of times without any sagging.

**The Verdict:**  
The Vintage Bankers Lamp earns a solid 4.2 out of 5. It's not a premium heirloom piece, but it's a functional, charming, and genuinely useful desk companion that outperforms its price point. The USB ports are a thoughtful addition, the included bulb saves you a trip to the store, and the warm light creates a pleasant workspace atmosphere.
