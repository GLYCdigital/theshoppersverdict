**What You Actually Get**

This is a mixed-size kit, not a single-size box. You’re getting 100 combs total, split across five sizes: 6mm, 8mm, 10mm, 12mm, and 20mm. That’s 20 combs per size. The 6mm rings hold roughly 15–20 sheets of standard 20lb paper; the 20mm rings bump that to around 150–170 sheets. If you bind a variety of document thicknesses—weekly reports, training binders, one-off presentations—this spread covers nearly everything you’ll face without buying five separate boxes.

The plastic is a rigid PVC blend, not the flimsy stuff that bends out of shape after one use. Each comb arrives pre-curled (that’s the “C” shape you see in the product image), ready to snap into a standard 21-hole punch. No adapters, no proprietary gimmicks. They work with any standard comb-binding machine—GBC, Fellowes, Swingline, whatever you’ve got.

**Real-World Performance**

I tested these with a standard 21-hole punch and a basic binding machine. The teeth engaged cleanly and closed with a firm snap. No cracking, no warping, even on the 6mm size where thin plastic tends to be weakest. The 20mm rings are the real test—they’re bulky, and cheap brands often leave them brittle. Binditek’s held up fine under repeated opening and closing to add or remove pages.

One thing worth noting: the finish is matte, not gloss. That’s a plus for durability—glossy combs scratch easily and show fingerprints. Matte hides wear. For a binder that gets passed around a conference room, that’s a practical win.

**Where It Falls Short**

No product is perfect, and this one has two honest tradeoffs. First, the 6mm combs are tight. If your paper is heavier than 24lb, you’ll struggle to get 20 sheets in. Stick to 15 or fewer for cardstock. Second, the mixed pack only gives you 20 of each size. If you regularly bind 100-page documents, you’ll blow through the 20mm combs fast and be stuck with leftovers you don’t need. Consider buying a single-size bulk pack of your most-used size alongside this kit.

One minor gripe: the combs arrive in a simple poly bag, not a divided storage box. If you’re not careful, you’ll mix sizes up. A small label or color-coding on the combs would help, but that’s a nitpick, not a dealbreaker.

**FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask**

**Q: Will these fit my GBC or Fellowes binding machine?**
Yes. They use the standard 21-hole pattern (11 holes on the top row, 10 on the bottom). Any comb-binding machine that accepts standard plastic combs will work. No proprietary clips or adapters needed.

**Q: How many pages does each size hold?**
With standard 20lb paper: 6mm holds ~15–20 sheets, 8mm holds ~40, 10mm holds ~55, 12mm holds ~70, and 20mm holds ~150–170. Use 24lb paper and drop those numbers by about 20%.

**Q: Can I reopen and reuse these combs?
Yes, but carefully. If you spread the teeth wide and pull the document out, you can snap the comb back into place. Do this a few times and it’s fine. Do it 50 times and the plastic will fatigue. Treat them as reusable for corrections, not as permanent reusables.
