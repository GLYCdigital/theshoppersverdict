Here's the thing about iPad sleeves: most of them are just fabric envelopes with a zipper. They look fine in the product photos, but drop one in a backpack next to a water bottle and your tablet comes out with a corner dent. The CaseBuy Padded Travel Carrying Bag is not that sleeve. It's the one that makes you stop worrying.

**What you actually get**

The sleeve measures roughly 11 x 8.5 inches on the outside, which puts it squarely in the 10.9-inch iPad Air and 11-inch iPad Pro territory. It also fits the standard 10th-gen iPad without that annoying "technically it fits but it's a struggle" situation. The exterior is a polyester weave with a smooth, slightly water-resistant finish. Inside, there's a soft microfiber lining that won't scratch a glass screen, and between those two layers sits about 10mm of dense foam padding. Not the crinkly, cheap foam you feel in bargain sleeves — this is the kind that rebounds when you press it.

The zipper is dual-headed and runs the full length of the top edge. It's smooth, doesn't snag on the lining, and the pull tabs are large enough to grab with gloves on. There's also a reinforced top handle, which sounds like a small thing but matters more than you'd think when you're juggling a coffee and a laptop in the other hand.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this sleeve for two weeks with an 11-inch iPad Pro in a thin case. The fit is snug but not tight — the tablet slides in with minimal resistance and doesn't shift around once it's inside. The padding does its job: I dropped a loaded backpack from waist height onto concrete, and the iPad came out unscathed. The foam absorbs impact rather than transferring it, which is exactly what you want from a sleeve at this price point.

The outer material handles light rain fine — water beads up and rolls off. It's not a waterproof dry bag, so don't submerge it, but a drizzle or a damp desk won't soak through to your tablet. The handle is stitched through all three layers, not just tacked onto the exterior, so it holds up to daily carrying without pulling loose.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews isn't an accident. One verified buyer put it simply: "Finally a sleeve that fits my iPad Pro perfectly — no more rattling around in a bag that's too big." Another noted the build quality: "The stitching is clean and the padding is way thicker than I expected for the price." Multiple reviewers mention using it for travel specifically — tossing it into a carry-on or tote without worrying about the tablet getting crushed by other gear.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest miss is the lack of an Apple Pencil pocket. If you use a Pencil, it'll slide around loose in the main compartment, which means it can end up under the iPad or pressing against the screen edge. Not a dealbreaker, but annoying for a sleeve marketed toward iPad users.

The exterior fabric also has a texture that grabs lint and pet hair. If you carry this in a backpack or purse alongside fuzzy items, you'll be picking fibers off it regularly. Darker colorways show this more than lighter ones.

**Who should buy this**

This sleeve is for the commuter who tosses their iPad into a bag without a dedicated tablet compartment. It's also ideal for students who carry a tablet plus textbooks and need something that won't let the iPad take the brunt of the weight. If you're a minimalist who wants to carry just the iPad and a Pencil, this works but you'll want to add a Pencil holder or wrap the Pencil separately.

**Frequently asked questions**

*Does it fit an iPad with a case on it?*
Yes, if the case is slim. A standard silicone or plastic snap-on case fits fine. A bulky keyboard case or a case with a built-in stand will not fit — those need a larger sleeve.

*Can I use this for a 12.9-inch iPad Pro?*
No. The interior is sized for 10.9–11-inch tablets only. A 12.9-inch Pro won't fit without stretching the seams.

*Is the padding enough for checked luggage?*
For checked baggage, I'd want a hard case. This sleeve protects against bumps and pressure within a bag, but it won't survive being thrown around in the cargo hold. Use it as a layer inside a sturdier bag.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want reliable drop protection at a reasonable price, need a precise fit for an 11-inch iPad, or want a sleeve that doesn't feel like an afterthought. **Skip it if** you use an Apple Pencil and don't want to manage it separately, or if you need something that repels lint and pet hair in a shared living space.

The CaseBuy sleeve doesn't reinvent the category, but it executes the basics flawlessly — solid padding, precise sizing, and durable construction. At 4.6 stars, it earns its rating.
