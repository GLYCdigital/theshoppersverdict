If your fridge door is buried under sticky notes, takeout menus, and permission slips, the ANLIOTE Magnetic Board Sheet is the organizational reset you didn't know you needed. With a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, this flexible dry-erase sheet turns any metal surface into a writable planning space. ## What You Actually Get

The ANLIOTE sheet is a thin, flexible magnetic whiteboard that adheres directly to your fridge or any ferromagnetic surface. It's not a framed board — it's a peel-and-stick-adjacent solution that relies on magnetic backing rather than adhesive. That's a key distinction: you can remove and reposition it without leaving residue.

The sheet is sizable enough to cover a meaningful chunk of fridge door real estate. You're getting a genuine dry-erase surface, not a glossy sticker that smudges. It arrives rolled in a tube, so expect to flatten it before first use — a minor annoyance that several reviewers mention.

## Performance and Build Quality

Here's where the ANLIOTE earns its rating. The magnetic layer is genuinely strong. One reviewer noted, "Stuck it to my fridge and it hasn't budged in three months, even with my kids slamming the door." That's the real-world test that matters.

The write-and-erase surface performs well with standard dry-erase markers. Colors pop, and most users report clean erasing with minimal ghosting. As one verified buyer put it, "I've used it daily for six weeks for meal planning and grocery lists. A quick wipe with a microfiber cloth and it's like new."

The flexibility is both a feature and a limitation. You can trim it to fit odd spaces, which is genuinely useful. But because it's thin, it works best on perfectly flat surfaces. If your fridge has a textured or slightly curved door, you'll feel the unevenness when writing. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The rolled packaging means the sheet may have a persistent curl at the edges, especially if you install it on a smaller surface where the bend is more pronounced. Several reviewers recommend laying it flat under a heavy book for a day before mounting — a workaround, not a fix.

The surface is also not as durable as a rigid whiteboard. Press too hard with a marker and you might leave a faint impression. And if you're hoping to use it as a magnetic surface for other magnets, be aware: the ANLIOTE sheet is magnetic itself, but it doesn't turn your fridge into a magnet board — it's the board.

## FAQ

**Will it stick to stainless steel fridges?**
Only if your fridge is magnetic. Many modern stainless steel models aren't. Test with a fridge magnet first — if it doesn't stick, neither will this sheet.

**Can I cut it to fit?**
Yes, with regular scissors. Cut slightly smaller than your planned area to account for the magnetic pull at the edges.

**Does it stain with colored markers?**
Dry-erase markers are fine with prompt erasing. Leave red or blue ink on for a week and you'll see faint residue. Clean with a dedicated whiteboard cleaner if needed.
