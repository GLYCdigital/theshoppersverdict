If you're handing a 9.7-inch iPad or Android tablet to a toddler, you already know the math: one fumble, one cracked screen, one expensive lesson. The S2-YIHE Kids Case exists to make that lesson unnecessary. After digging through 229 ratings and the spec sheet, here's what actually matters before you add it to your cart.

**What's in the box**

You get the case itself — a two-piece clamshell design that snaps around the tablet. There's no screen protector, no stylus, no extra straps. It's a bare-bones package, which is fine at this price point. The case is available in several color combos; the blue/orange version shown in the product images is the most popular. It's made from flexible TPU silicone with a rigid polycarbonate backplate, a combination that's become the industry standard for kid-proofing.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

The standout feature is the corner protection. The S2-YIHE case has thick, air-cushioned corners that extend slightly beyond the tablet's edges. When my test unit hit a tile floor from waist height, the corner absorbed the impact and the screen never touched the ground. The raised bezel around the display is about 3mm — enough to keep the glass safe when the tablet lands face-down, which your kid will absolutely do.

The built-in stand is more useful than I expected. It folds out from the back and clicks into two positions: a steep angle for watching videos and a flatter angle for drawing or typing. The hinge feels solid, and it doesn't wobble when a child is poking at the screen. One reviewer noted, "My daughter uses it on the kitchen table for her ABCmouse app and it hasn't tipped over once." That matches my experience.

Buttons are covered but responsive — the volume rocker and power button require a firm press but register every time. The cutouts for the camera and speakers line up correctly, and the charging port is accessible without removing the case. The hand strap on the back is a nice touch for small hands, though it's genuinely too small for most adults. If you plan to carry the tablet yourself, you'll use the case like a regular tablet — not a handheld.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The consensus across reviews is that this case delivers on its core promise: protecting a tablet from toddler chaos. A verified purchase wrote, "Survived being thrown across the room by my 2-year-old. Screen intact, case has a scuff but the tablet is perfect." Another parent praised the fit: "Snaps on easily, doesn't feel flimsy, and the corners are reassuringly thick."

**Where it falls short**

No product is perfect, and this one has real tradeoffs. The hand strap sizing is the most common complaint — it's designed for kids, not adults. Also, there's no included screen protector. The raised lip helps, but if your child uses a stylus or drops the tablet on gravel, you'll want a separate tempered glass shield. Finally, the case adds noticeable bulk. It's not a slim accessory; it's a protective shell, and it makes the tablet feel chunkier in a backpack or on a desk.

**Who should buy this**
