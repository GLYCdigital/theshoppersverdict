The NETGEAR Nighthawk RAX120 sits in an awkward spot. It's a powerful WiFi 6 router with specs that still impress, but at this price point, you're competing against newer AXE models and mesh systems that offer better value. After digging through 180 Amazon ratings averaging 3.6 stars, the picture is clear: this router delivers raw performance when it works, but reliability questions keep it from being a slam dunk.

## What's in the Box

You get the RAX120 unit itself — a flat, angular slab that looks like a stealth fighter parked on your desk — plus a power adapter, an Ethernet cable, and a quick-start guide. Setup is handled through the Nighthawk app, which is straightforward for basic configuration. There's no wall-mounting kit included, so factor that in if you're planning to hide it behind a monitor.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The RAX120 is a dual-band AX6000 router with 12 streams (8 on 5GHz, 4 on 2.4GHz). That's serious bandwidth for a home office with multiple laptops, phones, smart TVs, and IoT gadgets competing for airtime. In ideal conditions, you'll see real-world speeds of 700-900Mbps on WiFi 6 devices within 20 feet — genuinely fast.

The four external antennas are a highlight. They give this router excellent range, and multiple buyers note it holds a stable signal through walls and across two-story homes. One reviewer put it simply: "Signal strength is exceptional, reaches every corner of my 3,000 sq ft house."

The two Multi-Gig Ethernet ports (5Gbps and 2.5Gbps) are a thoughtful touch for anyone running a NAS or a high-speed fiber connection. You can aggregate them for even more bandwidth if your setup supports it.

## What Buyers Praise

Performance enthusiasts love the speed. As one verified buyer put it: "Upgraded from an older Nighthawk and the difference is night and day. My work laptop connects instantly, video calls are crystal clear, and large file transfers to my NAS are lightning fast." Another noted the "rock-solid 5GHz band" — something you don't always get with cheaper routers.

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest part. Multiple reviews — enough to be a pattern — report firmware instability. Random reboots, dropped connections on the 2.4GHz band, and a mobile app that occasionally loses the router are recurring complaints. One frustrated buyer wrote: "Great hardware, terrible software. Had to reboot it twice a week for the first month until a firmware update finally stabilized it." That's not a one-off; it's the main reason this router sits at 3.6 stars.

The design is also polarizing. It's 12.6 inches wide and nearly 8 inches deep — this isn't a router you tuck behind a monitor. If your office is visible to clients, it'll draw attention, and not necessarily the good kind.

Finally, there's no WiFi 6E support. For the price, you can find tri-band AXE routers that offer the 6GHz band, which is better for avoiding congestion in dense apartment buildings or office parks.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Power users with many devices**: If you have 20+ connected devices and demand top speeds, the RAX120 handles the load.
- **Large homes needing range**: The external antennas genuinely outperform internal-antenna routers in coverage.
- **Tech-savvy users who can troubleshoot**: If you're comfortable updating firmware and tweaking settings, the occasional hiccup is manageable.

**Skip it if** you want a set-and-forget router, need WiFi 6E, or prefer a compact design. There are cheaper AX5400 options that deliver 80% of the performance with fewer headaches.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this router work with all internet service providers?**
A: Yes, it's a standard router that works with any cable, fiber, or DSL modem. You'll connect it to your existing modem or gateway.

**Q: Can I use it as a mesh system with other Nighthawk routers?**
A: Yes, it supports NETGEAR's Orbi mesh technology, but you'll need compatible Orbi satellites. It won't mesh with older Nighthawk models.

**Q: Is the 2.4GHz band good for smart home devices?**
A: It's adequate, but some buyers report intermittent drops on 2.4GHz. For critical IoT devices, consider a dedicated access point or keep them close to the router.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need raw WiFi 6 speed, have a large home, and don't mind managing firmware updates. The RAX120 is a performance beast with excellent range and future-proof Multi-Gig ports.

**Skip it if** you want reliability out of the box, need WiFi 6E, or prefer a smaller footprint. The firmware instability and premium price make newer alternatives more attractive.

**Rating: 3.7/5** — Powerful hardware held back by software reliability issues. Great for enthusiasts, frustrating for everyone else.
