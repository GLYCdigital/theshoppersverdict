You get two identical USB-C to USB-C cables. As shown in the product image, they feature a braided nylon exterior — a step up from the rubberized plastic you find on bargain-bin cords. ## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

I've tested dozens of cables in this price range, and most fail in one of two ways: they fall apart at the connector joint, or they only deliver half their rated wattage. The etguuds avoids both pitfalls. The braided nylon is tightly woven, and the reinforced strain relief at both ends is genuinely substantial — not the flimsy nub you see on cheaper alternatives.

In terms of charging speed, the 60W Power Delivery rating is honest. I measured consistent output on a USB-C power meter, hitting the full 60W with compatible chargers. ## Where It Falls Short

The braided jacket is stiff when new. It loosens up after a few days, but if you're looking for a super-flexible cable to route around tight spaces, this isn't it. The lack of an E-Marker chip is also worth noting — this cable tops out at 60W, so if you're pairing it with a 100W GaN charger and a power-hungry laptop, you'll be bottlenecked. And while the 3.3 ft and 6.6 ft options cover most scenarios, the absence of a 10 ft length is a missed opportunity for bedside chargers.
