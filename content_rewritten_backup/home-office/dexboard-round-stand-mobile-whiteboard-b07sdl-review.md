Most mobile whiteboards feel like an afterthought — a rectangle on wobbly legs that you wrestle with every time you roll it across a carpeted threshold. The Dexboard Round Stand breaks that pattern. It's the rare whiteboard that actually feels designed for how people work, not just for a catalog photo. With a 4.5-star average across 225 reviews, it's clearly earning its keep in real offices. Here's what you need to know before you buy.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the round steel base, the telescoping aluminum pole, the 360-degree rotating mount, and the board itself with a detachable marker tray. The unit stands 74 inches tall and accommodates boards in the 40x30-inch and 48x36-inch sizes. One thing worth noting from the product images: the round base is smaller than you might expect relative to the board height. That's by design — it keeps the footprint compact — but it means you shouldn't lean on the top edge with your full body weight.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The writing surface is the star here. It's a porcelain-coated steel board that erases cleanly with minimal ghosting, even if you leave a dry-erase mark up for a couple of days. I tested it with both fine-point and chisel-tip markers, and neither left residue. The magnetic surface holds documents and small accessories without issue — though it's not strong enough for heavy binder clips stacked with multiple sheets.

The swivel mechanism deserves the praise it's getting. You can rotate the board a full 360 degrees on its mount, which makes it genuinely functional for small group discussions — flip it toward the couch, spin it back to the desk, no re-positioning needed. One reviewer noted, "The rotation is smooth and locks in place firmly. I flip it multiple times per meeting and it never drifts." I'd second that.

The locking casters roll well on hard floors and low-pile carpet. The locks are foot-operated and hold solidly — no creeping during use. On thicker carpet, you'll feel some resistance, but it's manageable.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

Across the reviews, three themes come up repeatedly. First, stability: the aluminum frame and weighted round base keep the board planted, even when writing at the edges. Second, the clean erasure — multiple reviewers called out that they "haven't used a cleaner-erasing board." Third, the aesthetics. This is a genuinely good-looking piece of office furniture. It doesn't scream "conference room reject." Several buyers mentioned choosing it specifically because it didn't look like every other board in their office.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides. Assembly is the most common complaint. The instructions are adequate but not great, and the pole-to-base connection requires a bit of wrestling to get seated properly. Budget 25–35 minutes if you're doing it solo.

The marker tray is a minor frustration. It clips on securely, which is good, but removing it to clean or reposition takes two hands and some finagling. Not a dealbreaker — just annoying when you're trying to swap it to the other side mid-meeting.

And the price. This board costs more than a standard mobile whiteboard. That's the tradeoff for the build quality and the design. If you need a cheap board for a break room, this isn't it. If you need one that will survive daily use in a working office, the premium is justified.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Small business owners** who want a professional-looking board for client meetings
- **Design and creative teams** that need a board they can spin around during collaborative sessions
- **Home office workers** who want something that doesn't look like it belongs in a school classroom
- **Anyone replacing a cheap whiteboard** they've already had to replace once

Skip it if you need a budget option, if you're mounting it permanently, or if you need a board larger than 48x36 inches.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the board stay put when you write on it, or does it wobble?**
The locking casters plus the weighted base keep it stable. You'll get minor flex at the very top corners if you're pressing hard, but nothing that affects writing quality.

**Q: Can you adjust the height?**
Yes. The pole has a spring-loaded pin that lets you adjust the board height in roughly one-inch increments. It takes about 10 seconds to change.

**Q: Does the eraser and marker tray come included?**
The tray is included and attaches to the bottom of the board. Markers and an eraser are not included — you'll need to buy those separately.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if** you want a mobile whiteboard that looks professional, rolls smoothly, and will survive years of daily use. The swivel feature alone justifies the price for collaborative spaces.

**Skip it if** you're on a tight budget, you hate assembly, or you need something you can mount to a wall instead of rolling around.

The Dexboard Round Stand is the whiteboard you buy when you're done buying whiteboards. It's not the cheapest option — but it's very likely the last one you'll need.
