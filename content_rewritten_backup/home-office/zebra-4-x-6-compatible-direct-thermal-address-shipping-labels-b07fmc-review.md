**What You're Actually Getting**

This is a roll of 4x6 direct thermal labels — no ribbon, no ink, just heat-reactive paper that darkens in your printer. The package includes a single roll with a 1-inch core, which is the standard fit for most direct thermal printers like the Zebra ZP450, LP2844, and Rollo. The labels are perforated every 4 inches, so they tear cleanly without needing scissors.

What makes these different from the generic no-name rolls flooding Amazon is the thermal coating quality. The print contrast is noticeably darker than bargain-bin labels, which matters when USPS or FedEx scanners are reading that barcode at 3 AM.

**Real-World Performance**

I've tested these in two printers — a Zebra GK420d and a Rollo. The perforation is the unsung hero here. Many cheap labels have perforations that tear at weird angles or leave paper fibers that gum up the printhead. These tear straight across every time. No jams, no misfeeds, no wasted labels.

The adhesive is another standout. It's a permanent acrylic adhesive that grips corrugated cardboard aggressively. One reviewer put it simply: *"These labels are very sticky and stay on boxes well even in the rain."* That's a real-world endorsement — shipping labels that peel off in transit are a nightmare of lost packages and angry customers.

**Where It Falls Short**

Here's the honest part. These are *compatible* labels, not genuine Zebra. That means the dimensions can vary slightly. A few users with older printers reported a 1-2mm width difference that caused the label to sit slightly off-center. It didn't affect printing, but if you're a perfectionist about alignment, it might bug you.

Also, the core is 1 inch. If you own a budget printer with a smaller spindle, these won't fit without an adapter. Check your printer's core requirement before ordering.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Will these work in a Rollo printer?**
Yes. The 1-inch core and standard 4x6 dimensions fit Rollo printers perfectly. Many Rollo users report excellent print quality with these.

**Q: Are these waterproof?**
They're water-resistant, not waterproof. Direct thermal labels will smudge if soaked. For normal shipping conditions, they hold up fine — rain, humidity, and rough handling won't ruin them.

**Q: How should I store them?**
Keep them away from heat and direct sunlight. Direct thermal paper darkens when exposed to heat or UV light, so store the roll in a cool, dark place until use.
