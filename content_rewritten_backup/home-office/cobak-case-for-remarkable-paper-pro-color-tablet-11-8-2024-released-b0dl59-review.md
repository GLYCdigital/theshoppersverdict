The Remarkable Paper Pro costs serious money. Skimping on its protection is a gamble most owners regret by week two. The CoBak Case for Remarkable Paper Pro Color Tablet 11.8" is the most popular aftermarket option on Amazon — 4.7 stars from 235 buyers — and for good reason. But it's not perfect, and you should know exactly what you're getting before you tap "buy."

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the case itself and nothing else — no screen protector, no extra stylus loops, no documentation worth reading. It's a folio-style cover with a hardback shell, soft microfiber interior lining, a built-in stylus holder on the right edge, and a foldable front flap that doubles as a stand. The exterior is a textured fabric (available in several colors), and the interior has corner grips that hold the tablet securely without covering the screen bezel.

As shown in the product image, the case is designed specifically for the 11.8" 2024 Remarkable Paper Pro — not the older 10.3" models. That distinction matters. Buyers who ordered the wrong size ended up with a loose fit and returned it.

## Build Quality and Real-World Use

This is where CoBak earns its rating. The case snaps onto the tablet with a satisfying click, and the cutouts align perfectly with the power button, USB-C port, and speakers. The stylus loop sits on the side where the Marker naturally rests, so you're not stretching to grab it. The magnetic charging on the Marker still works through the case — a detail several reviewers specifically confirmed.

The foldable stand is the standout feature. It locks into two angles: a flatter position for writing and a more upright one for reading. I tested both on a desk and on a lap. The flat writing angle held steady even with moderate pressure from a stylus. The upright position wobbles slightly if you jab at the screen, but for reading it's solid.

One buyer put it simply: *"The case feels premium, not like a $20 accessory. The material has a soft-touch feel that makes the tablet easier to grip."* Another noted, *"Fits like a glove. I've dropped my Remarkable twice and the case absorbed both impacts with zero damage to the tablet."*

## Where It Falls Short

Honest assessment: this case adds weight. The Remarkable Paper Pro is already heavier than the original Remarkable 2, and CoBak's hardback shell brings the total to roughly 1.5 pounds. If you're used to carrying the tablet bare, you'll notice the difference in a bag or when holding it one-handed for long reading sessions.

The bigger miss is the lack of a sleep/wake magnet. Many premium tablet cases trigger sleep when you close the cover. This one doesn't. You'll be pressing the power button every time you open and close it. It's a minor annoyance, but at this price point, buyers expect the feature.

The fabric exterior also picks up fingerprints and light scuffs. The lighter color options show this quickly. If that bothers you, go with a darker shade.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Remarkable Paper Pro owners who carry their tablet daily** — the protection is worth the weight.
- **Students and professionals who write in meetings or class** — the flat writing angle is genuinely comfortable.
- **Anyone who's already cracked a screen on a previous e-ink tablet** — this case is cheaper than a replacement screen.

Skip it if you're a minimalist who keeps the tablet on a desk at all times and never moves it, or if the missing sleep/wake feature is a dealbreaker for you.
