Every parent knows the drill: you write your kid's name on 47 water bottles, 12 lunchboxes, and 8 jackets, and by Tuesday, half of it has washed off or been "borrowed" by a classmate. The Promot Personalized 2-Line Stamp is the no-nonsense fix for that chaos — a self-inking stamp that puts your name and phone number on anything your kid touches.

**What you actually get**

The package contains one stamp with your two lines of custom text — typically a name on line one and a phone number on line two, though you can use any text up to the character limit. The wooden handle feels solid in hand, not hollow or cheap. The stamp itself measures about 2.5 inches tall, which is compact enough to toss in a drawer or backpack pocket. You also get a small ink pad built into the base, which is how the self-inking mechanism works — press down, and it re-inks automatically.

**How it performs in the real world**

I tested this stamp across paper, fabric, plastic, and cardboard. On paper and cardboard, the impressions are crisp and dark — no fading at the edges, no uneven patches. The text stays legible even after repeated washings on fabric labels, which is what most parents actually need. The ink is permanent and waterproof, so a trip through the washing machine won't erase your kid's name from their school uniform.

One thing that surprised me: the stamp alignment is precise. The two lines stay straight and evenly spaced every time, which sounds minor but matters when you're stamping 20 items in a row. The self-inking mechanism holds up well — after hundreds of stamps, the impressions remained consistent without needing to re-ink manually.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The customization options get frequent mentions in reviews. Parents appreciate being able to choose the font and layout rather than being stuck with one generic style. One buyer noted, "I ordered these for my twin boys' school supplies and the stamp came out exactly as designed — clean, clear, and perfectly aligned." Another mentioned, "The ink doesn't smear on lunch bags or water bottles, which was my biggest worry."

**Where it falls short**

The biggest drawback is that the ink cartridge isn't replaceable. When it runs out — roughly 10,000 impressions, per the manufacturer — you have to buy a new stamp entirely. For a heavy-duty user stamping multiple kids' items daily, that could mean replacing it every year or two.

Also, the ink takes a few seconds to fully set on non-porous surfaces. If you stamp a plastic water bottle and immediately rub it, you'll smudge it. Let it air-dry for about 15 seconds and you're fine, but it's a step that's easy to forget when you're rushing out the door.

**Who should buy this**

- **School-age parents** who are tired of writing names on everything — this pays for itself in time saved within the first week.
- **Camp counselors and coaches** who need to mark gear for multiple kids.
- **Daycare providers** who want a fast way to label bottles, bibs, and blankets.

Skip it if you only need to label a handful of items — a permanent marker will do the job just fine. And if you're looking for a stamp that works on dark fabrics, note that this ink is dark-colored, so it won't show up well on black clothing.

**FAQ**

**Q: How long does the ink last?**
A: Roughly 10,000 impressions. For a typical parent stamping a few items daily, that's about a year or two of regular use.

**Q: Can I use this on clothing?**
A: Yes, the ink is permanent and wash-safe. Stamp the care label or a fabric patch for best results.

**Q: What's the character limit per line?**
A: Typically 20 characters per line, which is enough for a name plus a phone number. Check the product listing for the exact limit on your chosen font size.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you're labeling school supplies, sports gear, or daycare items on a weekly basis and want a clean, professional-looking solution that takes seconds. **Skip it if** you need a stamp for dark fabrics or you're only labeling a handful of items.

The Promot Personalized 2-Line Stamp does exactly what it promises — no more, no less. It's well-built, the customization is solid, and the ink quality holds up. The non-replaceable cartridge is the only real annoyance, but at this price point, it's a reasonable tradeoff for the convenience. **4.6/5 stars.**
