If you've bought a Samsung Galaxy Tab A9+ or A11+ and thought, "I wish this thing could pull double duty as a mini laptop," this keyboard case from the 7-color lineup is the accessory to make that happen. At $62.99 with a 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews, it sits in that sweet spot between budget garbage and overpriced first-party options. But is it actually worth your money? Let's dig in.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the folio case with the keyboard integrated into the bottom half, a USB-C charging cable for the keyboard, and a basic instruction card. The case itself is made from a textured PU leather exterior with a soft microfiber lining that protects the tablet's screen when closed. The keyboard connects via Bluetooth — there's no pogo-pin magic here, so you'll need to pair it once and charge it every few weeks depending on usage. The case weighs about 1.2 pounds, which brings the total tablet-plus-keyboard weight to around 2 pounds. Not ultraportable, but acceptable for a bag or backpack.

**Real-world performance and typing feel**

The keys have about 1.5mm of travel, which is decent for a case-mounted keyboard. They're not mechanical by any stretch, but they're not mushy either — you get a satisfying tactile bump when typing. Key spacing is near full-size, so my average-sized hands didn't feel cramped during longer writing sessions. The top row includes Android-specific shortcuts: home, back, recent apps, brightness controls, volume, and media keys. That's a thoughtful touch that makes the case feel designed for the Tab series rather than a generic Bluetooth keyboard slapped onto a folio.

The magnetic stand holds the tablet securely at two angles: a steeper 110° for typing and a more reclined 125° for media consumption. The magnet is strong enough that you won't accidentally knock the tablet over when tapping the screen. The Bluetooth connection has been reliable in my testing — no dropped keystrokes or reconnections needed mid-session.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The color options are the standout feature, and buyers notice. One reviewer noted, "Finally a keyboard case that isn't just black or gray. The sage green looks even better in person than the photos." Another common theme in the reviews: the value. Multiple buyers compared this favorably against Samsung's own book cover keyboard, which costs significantly more. One verified purchase put it simply: "Half the price of the Samsung brand one and works just as well for everyday typing."

**Where it falls short**

The trackpad is the weakest link. It's functional for scrolling and clicking, but it's small — about 3.5 inches diagonally — and the click mechanism requires more pressure than you'd expect. If you plan to do heavy cursor work, you'll want a separate mouse. Also, the color options in the product images are misleadingly vibrant. The "coral" and "mint" shades are noticeably more muted in person. And because this is a Bluetooth keyboard, you're managing two devices to charge — the tablet and the keyboard — which is a minor annoyance if you're used to pogo-pin cases that never need battery attention.

**Who should buy this**

This case is ideal for students who need to type notes in class without carrying a separate laptop, or professionals who want a lightweight secondary device for email and document review. It's also a great fit for anyone who's frustrated with the limited color choices of most tablet accessories. If you're a heavy tablet user who types more than 2,000 words a day, you might want to invest in a more substantial keyboard. But for casual to moderate typing, this hits the mark.

**FAQ**

*Does the keyboard work when the case is in tablet mode (keyboard folded behind)?*
No. The keyboard auto-locks when you fold it behind the tablet, which saves battery but means you can't use it as a standalone Bluetooth keyboard while the tablet is propped up separately.

*Will this case fit the Tab A9+ and the A11+?*
Yes, both the Galaxy Tab A9+ and A11+ have the same 11-inch chassis dimensions, so this case fits both models. Double-check your model number before ordering, but the compatibility is confirmed in most reviews.

*How long does the keyboard battery last?*
Most users report 3-4 weeks of regular use (a few hours daily) before needing a recharge. A full charge takes about 2 hours via the included USB-C cable.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want an affordable, colorful keyboard case that transforms your Tab A9+/A11+ into a productivity device for notes, email, and light document work. The typing experience is solid, the magnetic stand is secure, and the color options are a genuine differentiator in a sea of black accessories.

**Skip it if** you need a precise trackpad for heavy cursor work, want a keyboard that never needs charging, or expect the colors to match the product photos exactly. Also skip if you're a power typist who needs mechanical-switch-level feedback.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Good value, solid typing, and a welcome splash of color. The trackpad and muted colors keep it from being great, but for the price, it's a smart buy.
