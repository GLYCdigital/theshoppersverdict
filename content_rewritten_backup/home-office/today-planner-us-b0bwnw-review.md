Let's talk about the Today Planner US. At 4.8 stars with 240 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. But is it the right planner for *you*? I've dug through the specs, the customer feedback, and the real-world complaints to give you the honest breakdown before you spend your money.

**What You Actually Get**

This is an undated daily planner — 12 months of pages in a 6.5" x 8.5" format. The spiral binding is the standout feature here; it's not the flimsy wire you see on cheap notebooks. It's a thick, coated spiral that lays completely flat on your desk, which matters when you're actually writing in it. The cover is a soft-touch matte material that resists fingerprints and has held up well against daily tossing into bags.

The paper is the real star. At 120gsm, it's significantly thicker than the 80-90gsm you'll find in most planners at this price point. This means your gel pens, rollerballs, and even fountain pens won't bleed through or ghost onto the next page. If you're someone who likes to use colored pens or highlighters, this is a legit differentiator.

**Real-World Performance**

The layout is where this planner earns its keep. Each daily spread gives you an hourly breakdown from 6 AM to 9 PM, which is ideal for scheduled workdays. There's also a dedicated top-three priorities box, a habit tracker, and a notes section on every single page. It's not trying to be a full productivity system — it's a focused daily tool.

One thing buyers consistently mention is the build durability. A reviewer noted, "I've carried this in my work bag for six months and the binding hasn't loosened, the corners haven't frayed, and the pages haven't torn." That's the kind of real-world feedback that matters. Another buyer mentioned "the paper is thick enough that I can use my favorite fountain pen without worrying about wrecking the next page" — a huge plus if you're particular about your writing tools.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the drawbacks. The undated format is a double-edged sword. If you skip a day, you just leave it blank — no wasted pages. But if you're someone who needs the structure of pre-printed dates, this will annoy you. You'll spend a few minutes filling in dates for the week ahead.

The monthly overview page is also minimalist — it's not a full calendar spread with detailed boxes. It's more of a snapshot for the month. If you rely on a monthly view for planning appointments, you'll need a separate calendar or app for that. This is a daily planner, not an all-in-one system.

**Who Should Buy This**

This planner is ideal for professionals with structured days — think project managers, teachers, healthcare workers, or anyone whose calendar is appointment-driven. The hourly layout shines when you need to block out specific times. It's also great for students who want to track daily assignments and study sessions.

If you're a freeform journaler who needs open space for long-form notes, or someone who lives by monthly and weekly overviews, this probably isn't your match. You'd be better served by a bullet journal or a classic weekly/monthly planner.

**FAQ: Real Questions Buyers Ask**

**Is the paper really fountain pen friendly?**
Yes. At 120gsm, this is some of the thickest paper you'll find in a commercial planner. Fountain pen users in the reviews consistently report no bleed-through or ghosting.

**Does it work if I don't start on January 1st?**
Absolutely. The undated format means you can start any day, any month. Just fill in the dates yourself.

**Will the spiral binding bend or break in a bag?**
No. The binding is reinforced and thicker than standard wire. Multiple long-term users report no issues after months of daily carry.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a durable, fountain-pen-friendly daily planner with a practical hourly layout and don't mind writing in your own dates.

**Skip it if:** You need a true monthly calendar view or want a dated system that requires zero setup.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — This is a well-built, thoughtfully designed daily planner that nails the fundamentals. The paper quality and binding elevate it above the competition, and the hourly layout genuinely helps with time-blocking. It's not perfect — the monthly view is thin and the undated format isn't for everyone — but for its intended purpose, it delivers. If you're a daily planner person, this is a solid investment.
