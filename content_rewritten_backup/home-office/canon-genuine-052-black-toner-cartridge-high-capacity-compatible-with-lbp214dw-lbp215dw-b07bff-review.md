**FAQ**

**Q: Will this work in my Canon LBP213dw?**  
No. The 052 cartridge is specifically for the LBP214dw, LBP215dw, and compatible models like the i-SENSYS MF445dw / MF449dw. Using a 052 in a printer designed for a different model will cause a "wrong cartridge" error.

**Q: How do I check if my printer is low on toner?**  
The printer’s LCD display will show "Toner Low" when you’re roughly 10-15% from empty. You can also check via Canon’s Status Monitor software on your PC — it shows a percentage estimate.

**Q: Can I shake the cartridge to get more pages when it says low?**  
Yes, but only once. When the printer says "Toner Low," remove the cartridge and gently rock it side-to-side (not up and down) to redistribute toner. You’ll typically get 100-200 extra pages. Do this only once — after that, replace it.
