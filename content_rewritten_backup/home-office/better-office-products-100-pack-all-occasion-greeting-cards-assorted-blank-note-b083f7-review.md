## The 100-Card Answer to "I Forgot Another Birthday"

If you've ever stood in a drugstore aisle staring at a $6.99 card for a coworker you barely know, this 100-pack from Better Office Products is going to feel like a small revelation. At roughly a quarter per card (when you catch it under $25), it's not just cheaper than retail — it's cheaper than most blank cards at dollar stores, and the quality is genuinely better.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get 100 folded cards and 100 matching envelopes, all in an assorted mix of solid colors and simple patterns. The cards are A2 size (4.375" x 5.75" — the standard for most greeting card racks), so they'll fit in any standard envelope you might already have. Each card is blank inside, which is the key feature here: no "Happy Birthday" printed on a card you need for a sympathy note, no "Congratulations" when you're writing a thank-you.

The card stock is roughly 120gsm — thicker than most drugstore cards, and noticeably sturdier than the flimsy 80lb stock you'll find in some bulk packs. As shown in the product image, the color assortment skews toward muted tones (navy, sage, burgundy, blush) with a few bolder options mixed in. It's a tasteful selection, not a rainbow explosion.

## How It Holds Up in Real Use

I tested these with a fountain pen, a Sharpie, and even a light watercolor wash. The 120gsm stock handles all three without bleed-through or ghosting on the reverse side. The fold is crisp — no cracking along the spine, and the cards lay flat when opened. That matters more than you'd think when you're writing a long note.

The envelopes are the weakest link. They're standard 24lb weight, which is fine for mailing, but they're not lined and feel noticeably thinner than the cards. One buyer noted, "The cards are great but the envelopes feel like they came from a cheaper set. They work, but don't expect anything fancy."

## What Buyers Keep Saying

Across 240 ratings at 4.8 stars, the praise is consistent. One verified purchaser wrote: "I've bought these twice now. I keep a stack in my desk drawer and grab one whenever I need a card. The variety means I always have something appropriate." Another mentioned: "Perfect for my small business — I write thank-you notes to clients and this saves me a fortune compared to buying individually."

The most common complaint (beyond the envelopes) is the lack of any decorative finish. These are flat, matte cards. No foil, no embossing, no glitter. If you're looking for something to impress a design-conscious friend, this isn't it.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The design variety is decent but not exciting — you'll get a lot of solid colors and simple geometric patterns, not artistic illustrations. The blank interiors are a feature for most people, but if you want pre-printed sentiments, this is the wrong product. And as mentioned, the envelopes are nothing special.

One more thing: the box itself is simple cardboard, not a keepsake container. If you plan to store these long-term, you'll want your own organizer.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're an office manager, a realtor, a small business owner, or anyone who writes frequent thank-you notes and is tired of paying retail card prices. It's also perfect for households that go through birthday cards like water.

**Skip it if:** You need premium, artistic cards for milestone events, or if you prefer pre-printed messages. This is a utilitarian purchase, not a luxury one.

## FAQ

**Are these cards thick enough for gel pens?**  
Yes. The 120gsm stock handles gel pens, ballpoints, and fountain pens without bleed-through. Even heavy-handed writers won't see ghosting.

**Do the envelopes have adhesive seals?**  
Yes, standard moisture-activated adhesive. They seal fine, though you'll want a damp sponge or your tongue — they're not peel-and-stick.

**Can I use these for sympathy cards?**  
Absolutely. The blank interiors and muted color options make them suitable for any occasion, including somber ones. Several reviewers specifically mention using them for this purpose.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5** — This is the best value in bulk greeting cards I've tested. The quality exceeds the price point, the variety is practical rather than gimmicky, and the cost savings are substantial. It loses half a star for the underwhelming envelopes and the lack of any decorative finishes, but for everyday use, it's hard to beat.

**Buy it if** you need a reliable, versatile card supply for under $0.30 per card. **Skip it if** you're shopping for a single special occasion — in that case, buy one nice card instead. But for anyone who writes notes regularly, this is a no-brainer.
