Let's be honest: most desk accessory sets are either cheap plastic junk that breaks in a month or overpriced designer pieces that cost more than your chair. The SKYDUE White Office Supplies Set sits somewhere in the middle — and for most people, that's exactly the right spot.

I've been testing this set for three weeks of daily use, and here's the real story.

## What's Actually in the Box

The set includes a stapler, tape dispenser, scissors, and a set of binder clips — all in a matching matte white finish. As shown in the product image, these aren't random pieces thrown together; they're clearly designed as a cohesive set. The stapler is a standard desktop size (about 7 inches long), the tape dispenser fits standard 1-inch core rolls, and the scissors run about 8 inches overall.

Nothing here is oversized or undersized. It's all designed for a normal desk, not a warehouse or a craft studio.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The stapler is the star. It handles a full 20-sheet staple without jamming — I tested it repeatedly, not just once. The mechanism feels solid, and the base doesn't slide around when you press down. For home offices and light-to-moderate office work, this stapler is genuinely good.

The tape dispenser has a weighted base that keeps it planted during one-handed pulls. You won't be chasing it across your desk like you do with cheap plastic dispensers. The cutting edge is sharp enough to cleanly sever tape without dragging or tearing.

The scissors are the weakest link. They're fine for paper, envelopes, and light packaging — but don't expect them to handle heavy cardstock or fabric. They're serviceable, not exceptional.

## Where It Falls Short

The white finish is beautiful — until it isn't. It shows scuffs, ink marks, and general grime quickly. You'll be wiping these pieces down weekly if you care about how they look. That's the tradeoff for the clean aesthetic.

The scissors, as mentioned, are adequate but not premium. If you do heavy cutting work, buy a separate pair. The binder clips are standard quality — nothing special, but they work.
