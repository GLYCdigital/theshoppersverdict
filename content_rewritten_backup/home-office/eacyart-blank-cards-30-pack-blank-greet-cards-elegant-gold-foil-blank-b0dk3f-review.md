**The Full Review**

Most blank card packs treat "blank" as an excuse to skimp on quality. The EACYART 30-pack flips that assumption. These aren't flimsy cardstock rectangles you'd use for a last-minute thank-you note — they're substantial 5x7 cards with a gold foil border that makes recipients assume you spent more than you did. At 4.8 stars across 240 ratings, the consensus is clear: this is a thoughtfully made product for a niche that usually gets ignored.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains 30 cards and 30 matching A2 envelopes. The cardstock is 300gsm — noticeably thicker than the 250gsm you'll find in most comparable packs. The gold border is applied as a foil stamp along the outer edge, framing the blank white interior. As shown in the product image, the design is minimal: white field, gold frame, nothing else. That's it. No pre-printed greetings, no embossed patterns, no liner on the envelopes.

**Performance and Build Quality**

Here's where this pack earns its rating. The 300gsm weight means you can use fountain pens, brush markers, or even light watercolor washes without bleed-through or warping. One buyer noted, *"I make my own cards for clients and these hold up beautifully with alcohol markers — no ghosting on the other side."* Another wrote, *"The gold border photographs well. I've sent these for wedding thank-yous and everyone asked where I got them."*

The foil itself is matte rather than high-shine. It catches light subtly rather than flashing like metallic thread. That's a design choice that reads "elegant" in person but might disappoint if you're expecting the glossy foil found on premium retail cards.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Across the 240 ratings, three themes dominate:

1. **Versatility** — crafters, small business owners, and everyday users all report using these for everything from birthday cards to corporate thank-you notes.
2. **Envelope quality** — the included A2 envelopes are sturdy, with proper gummed seals that don't split when moistened.
3. **Value per card** — at roughly 50 cents per complete card-and-envelope setup, buyers repeatedly note this beats buying individual cards at retail.

One reviewer summarized it well: *"I've bought three packs already. The paper takes ink beautifully and the gold border gives it a finished look that plain white cards just don't have."*

**Where It Falls Short**

The matte foil is the most consistent complaint. If you're expecting the shiny, reflective gold of luxury stationery, you'll be underwhelmed. It's more of a brushed-gold tone — refined, but not flashy.

The interior is truly blank. No subtle watermark, no pre-printed "Thank You" or "Happy Birthday." If you're not confident in your handwriting or calligraphy, these cards will expose that.

The envelopes are plain white, not gold-lined or coordinated with the card's border. It's a minor aesthetic mismatch — the card says "elegant" while the envelope says "office supply."

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Small business owners** who send handwritten thank-you notes to clients and want a professional look without custom printing costs.
- **Crafters and card makers** who need a quality blank base for Cricut projects, embossing, or mixed-media work.
- **Wedding parties** creating DIY invitation suites or favor tags — the 30-count pack covers a typical guest list.
- **Anyone who writes letters regularly** and wants cards that feel substantial in hand without the boutique price tag.

Skip it if you need pre-printed messages, shiny metallic foil, or coordinating envelopes. This isn't that product.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do these fit standard A2 envelopes?**
Yes — the included envelopes are standard A2 size (4.375 x 5.75 inches) and hold the 5x7 cards folded perfectly. No trimming needed.

**Q: Can I use watercolor or alcohol markers on these?**
The 300gsm weight handles both well. Light watercolor washes dry flat without major warping. Alcohol markers won't bleed through, though heavy saturation may ghost slightly on the back.

**Q: Are the cards pre-scored?**
Yes, they come pre-folded with a clean, crisp crease. They fold flat and lay flat when opened — no curling at the edges.
