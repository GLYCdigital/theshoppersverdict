**The boring label that saves you from shipping disasters.**

Let's be honest: nobody gets excited about address labels. But when you run an Etsy shop, eBay store, or just send a lot of holiday packages, the difference between a label that peels clean and one that jams your printer feels like a big deal. The Avery Shipping Address Labels (product code 5160-compatible, 30 per sheet) have earned their 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews for a simple reason: they just work.

## What you actually get

This box contains 25 sheets, each with 30 labels — 750 total. Each label measures 1" x 2-5/8", the standard address-label size that fits most shipping templates in Word, Canva, and Avery's free Design & Print software. As shown in the product image, these are your classic white rectangular labels with a permanent adhesive backing. Nothing fancy. That's the point.

## Real-world performance

The two things that matter in a shipping label: does it stick, and does it print clean? On both counts, these deliver.

**Adhesion:** The permanent adhesive grabs corrugated cardboard, poly mailers, and even slightly textured paper envelopes. Multiple buyers report packages arriving with labels intact after cross-country trips. One reviewer noted, "I've shipped over 200 packages with these and never had one come back due to a label issue." That's the confidence you want when a lost package means a refund and an angry customer.

**Print quality:** The matte finish accepts inkjet and laser printing without smearing. Water-based ink dries fast and stays put — rain on your porch won't turn your label into a blurry mess. The surface is also smudge-resistant, so handling by postal workers won't degrade readability.

**The perforation actually works.** This is where Avery separates itself from generic store brands. The labels peel off cleanly with no tearing, no curling edges, no fighting to separate them from the carrier sheet. It sounds minor until you've spent ten minutes wrestling with cheap labels that split down the middle.

## What buyers consistently praise

The dominant theme across reviews is reliability. A verified purchase put it plainly: "These are the only labels I trust for my business. Never jammed, never wasted a sheet." Several users specifically mentioned the jam-free feeding, which matters if you've ever watched a cheap label sheet crumple inside your printer and destroy your afternoon.

## Where it falls short

Let's be direct about the downsides.

**Thirty labels per sheet is inefficient.** If you're shipping more than a few packages a day, you'll burn through sheets quickly. The larger 2-per-sheet format (Avery 5163) might serve higher-volume shippers better. And at this size, you're paying a premium for the Avery name — generic alternatives run cheaper, though you sacrifice the reliable perforation.

**White only.** This listing offers just the standard white finish. If you need clear labels for branding purposes or kraft-colored labels for aesthetic consistency, look elsewhere.

**No free template software included.** You'll need to download Avery's Design & Print (free, but requires an account) or use built-in Word templates. Slightly annoying if you want to just print and go.

## Who should buy this

- **Small business owners** shipping 5-50 packages weekly — the reliability justifies the price

A: Yes. The labels are rated for both inkjet and laser printers. Just make sure you select the correct paper type in your printer settings to avoid heat damage.

**Q: Can I print on these with a standard home printer?**

**Q: Are these waterproof?**
A: The labels are water-resistant, not waterproof. They'll survive rain and condensation, but don't submerge them. For extreme conditions, consider Avery's weatherproof line.
