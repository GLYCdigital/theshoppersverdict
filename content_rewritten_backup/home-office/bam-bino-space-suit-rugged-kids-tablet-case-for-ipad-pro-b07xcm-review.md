Let’s be blunt: handing an iPad Pro to a child is a gamble. One wrong toss and you’re out $300+ for a screen replacement. That’s exactly the problem the Bam Bino Space Suit was built to solve—and at 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, it’s clearly doing something right. But is it the right armor for *your* kid’s iPad Pro? Here’s the real breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The Space Suit is a two-piece clamshell case: a soft, shock-absorbing silicone inner layer that wraps the iPad’s edges, plus a rigid polycarbonate outer shell that locks it all together. It’s designed specifically for the 11-inch iPad Pro (1st, 2nd, and 3rd gen models), so make sure you’re not buying this for the 12.9-inch version—that’s a common mix-up.

The case adds noticeable bulk—roughly 0.5 inches to the thickness and about 8 ounces of weight. It also includes a built-in kickstand that folds flat against the back, plus a detachable shoulder strap. The strap loops through reinforced anchor points, not flimsy plastic clips, which is a thoughtful touch.

## Performance and Build Quality

The standout feature is the raised bezel. It sits a full 3mm above the screen, which means when your kid drops the iPad face-down (and they will), the bezel hits the floor first, not the glass. The corners are reinforced with air-cushion pockets—the same tech you see in OtterBox cases, just at a lower price point.

The port covers are the weak link in most rugged cases, but Bam Bino did these right. The charging port cover is a hinged flap that stays out of the way when open, and the speaker grilles are exposed rather than covered with mesh that muffles sound. The buttons are covered with raised silicone nubs that require a firm press—some kids find them stiff, but that’s better than accidental volume mashing.

One thing buyers consistently praise: the grip. The textured silicone backing makes the iPad significantly harder to drop in the first place. As one parent noted, *“My 4-year-old can carry it one-handed without it slipping. The texture is tacky enough that it stays put on car seats and couches.”*

## What Buyers Love

The drop protection is the headline, and it delivers. Multiple reviewers report surviving falls from toddler height onto tile and hardwood with zero damage. The kickstand is also a hit—it holds the iPad at two angles (typing and viewing) and locks into place with a satisfying click.

Parents also appreciate that the case is easy to clean. The silicone surface wipes down with a damp cloth, which matters when your kid eats lunch while watching *Bluey*.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has three real issues:

**1. Apple Pencil compatibility is awkward.** The case doesn’t have a dedicated slot. You can slide the Pencil into the side groove, but it’s not magnetic and will fall off if the iPad is jostled. You’ll want a separate holder.

**2. The kickstand is not adjustable.** It locks into two preset angles. If your child prefers a steeper viewing angle, they’re out of luck.

**3. Bulk is a tradeoff.** This is not a case you’ll want to remove for adult use. It’s 100% child-proofing, 0% sleek. If you share this iPad with your kid, expect to leave the case on permanently.

One reviewer summed it up: *“It’s clunky and ugly, but my toddler has thrown this thing across the room twice. The iPad is fine. That’s all I care about.”*

## Who Should Buy This

- **Parents of toddlers and preschoolers (ages 2-6):** This is your case. The grip and drop protection are exactly what you need.
- **Teachers or therapists** using iPads in a classroom setting where devices get handled roughly.
- **Anyone who already has AppleCare+** — this case will save you the deductible trips.

**Skip it if:** Your kid is 8+, handles electronics carefully, or you need the iPad to stay slim for travel.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this fit the iPad Pro 11-inch M2 or M4 models?**
A: It fits 1st, 2nd, and 3rd gen 11-inch iPad Pros (2018, 2020, 2021). The M2 (4th gen) and M4 (5th gen) have different dimensions and will **not** fit. Check your model number before ordering.

**Q: Can you charge while the case is on?**
A: Yes, the hinged port cover flips open and stays out of the way. You can use standard USB-C cables and most angled cables.

**Q: Does it interfere with the Face ID camera?**
A: No. The front camera cutout is precise, and the screen protector (if you use one) fits underneath the raised bezel without bubbling.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You need serious drop protection for a young kid’s iPad Pro and don’t care about looks. The grip alone is worth the price, and the corner protection is genuinely effective.

**Skip it if:** You need Apple Pencil storage, adjustable viewing angles, or a slim profile. This is a specialized tool, not a daily-driver case.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — loses points for the Pencil issue and limited kickstand angles, but it does exactly what it promises: keeps your iPad alive through toddler chaos.
