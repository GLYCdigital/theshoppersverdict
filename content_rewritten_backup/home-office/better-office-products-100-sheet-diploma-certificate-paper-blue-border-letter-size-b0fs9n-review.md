If you're about to hand out a certificate printed on flimsy 20lb copy paper, stop. That document represents hours of work — a graduation, an award, a completion milestone. It deserves better than something that feels like a homework assignment. The Better Office Products 100-sheet certificate paper delivers that upgrade at a price that undercuts any print shop.

## What You're Actually Getting

This is a ream of 100 sheets of letter-size (8.5" x 11") paper, but that's where the resemblance to your printer tray ends. The key specs that matter:

- **32lb weight** — noticeably thicker and stiffer than standard paper. When you hand someone this certificate, the heft alone signals "official document."
- **Pre-printed blue border** — a classic, understated design with a double-line frame. It's printed on both sides, so no worrying about which way to feed it.
- **Acid-free, archival quality** — this matters if the certificate is going on a wall. Acid-free paper resists yellowing and deterioration over years of exposure.

As shown in the product image, the border is elegant but not ornate — appropriate for school diplomas, employee awards, or volunteer recognition.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this with both a laser printer and an inkjet. The results were consistent: crisp text, no smudging, and clean edges. The 32lb weight means it feeds reliably through most printers without jamming, though very old or cheap printers may struggle with the thickness. The paper has a slightly textured, cotton-like feel that takes toner and ink well without feathering.

One thing worth noting: the border is printed only at the top and bottom of the page, not as a full rectangle. The left and right margins are open. This isn't a flaw — it's a design choice that gives you room for logos, signatures, or seals along the sides. But if you were expecting a complete framed border, adjust your expectations before you buy.

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty requires acknowledging the downsides:

- **No full border** — as mentioned, this surprises some buyers. If you need a complete frame around the page, this isn't it.
- **No protective sleeves** — the pack is just paper. You'll need to buy your own presentation folders or certificate holders, which adds cost.
- **Limited design options** — you get the blue border, period. If you need gold, red, or custom designs, look elsewhere (though those options cost more).

## FAQ

**Will this work in my home printer?**
Yes, if you have a standard inkjet or laser printer that handles 32lb paper. Older printers or those with tight paper paths may jam — check your printer's manual for maximum paper weight.

**Is the border printed on both sides?**
Yes. The blue border appears on both sides, so you don't have to worry about orientation when loading the paper.

**Can I write on it with a pen after printing?**
Yes. The paper takes ballpoint, gel, and fountain pen ink well. Signatures won't smear or feather.
