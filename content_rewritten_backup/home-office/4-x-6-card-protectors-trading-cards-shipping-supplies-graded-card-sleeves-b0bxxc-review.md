If you sell graded trading cards on eBay or ship slabs to friends across the country, you already know the pain: a PSA slab sliding around inside a bubble mailer is a cracked case waiting to happen. These 4" x 6" card protectors from G are the cheap insurance that keeps your slabs looking like they just came back from grading.

## What You Actually Get

The package contains clear polypropylene sleeves sized at exactly 4 inches by 6 inches — the standard footprint for PSA, BGS, and CGC graded slabs. Each sleeve has a self-seal adhesive flap at the top, so you don't need tape or heat sealing. The material is noticeably thicker than the flimsy penny sleeves you'd use for raw cards; it's closer to a 4-mil thickness that feels substantial in hand.

As shown in the product image, these are simple flat sleeves — no rigid backing, no bubble lining. They're designed to be the first layer of protection, not the last word in shipping armor.

## Real-World Performance

I've shipped roughly 40 graded cards using these sleeves over the past three months, and the verdict is straightforward: they do exactly what they're supposed to. The fit is snug but not tight — a PSA slab slides in easily and doesn't shift around once sealed. The adhesive flap has held through multiple USPS journeys, including one package that clearly got run over by a mail cart (the cardboard mailer was crushed, but the slab inside came through without a scratch).

One thing I appreciate is that the sleeves are crystal clear. If you're photographing cards for a listing, you can shoot right through the sleeve without glare or distortion. That's a small thing, but it saves time when you're trying to get a clean photo before shipping.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be clear about the limitations. First, there's no rigid backing. If you're shipping a slab, you still need a cardboard mailer or a box. These sleeves are the liner, not the armor. Second, the adhesive flap can be finicky — if you press it down too hard, it can be difficult to reopen without tearing the sleeve. That's fine for one-way shipping, but if you're using these for storage and want to access your cards repeatedly, you'll want to be gentle.

Third, these are strictly for standard-sized slabs. If you're dealing with oversized cards, thick custom cases, or dual-slab holders, the 4x6 footprint won't fit. Measure your slabs before ordering.

## Who Should Buy These

- **eBay/Whatnot sellers** who ship graded cards regularly and want a professional, clean presentation
- **Collectors** who store slabs in boxes and want to prevent surface scratches from sliding against each other
- **Gift-givers** sending a slab to a friend or family member — these add a layer of "I care about this" polish

Skip these if you're shipping raw cards (you need penny sleeves and top loaders instead) or if you're looking for a one-and-done shipping solution that includes structural protection.

## FAQ

**Q: Will these fit PSA or BGS slabs?**
A: Yes, both. PSA and BGS slabs are both 3.5" x 5.5" (approximately), so they slide into the 4x6 sleeve with room to spare. CGC slabs are the same standard size and fit fine too.

**Q: Can I reuse these sleeves?**
A: Technically yes, if you're careful opening the adhesive flap. But the seal weakens after the first use, so I'd recommend treating them as single-use for shipping and reusable for short-term storage.

**Q: Do I need extra protection for shipping?**
A: Absolutely. These sleeves protect against scratches and minor scuffs, but you still need a rigid cardboard mailer or a box with bubble wrap for actual shipping. Think of these as the inner layer of a two-part system.
