If you've ever tried writing on a flimsy clipboard balanced on your knee, you already know the struggle — the paper slides, the board bends, and your handwriting turns into illegible chicken scratch. The HERKKA hardboard dry erase clipboard aims to solve that with a rigid 3mm backing and a whiteboard surface that pulls double duty. After 220 ratings averaging 4.4 stars, it's clearly earning its keep in offices and job sites alike. Here's the full breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The package contains a single clipboard measuring approximately 9" x 12.5" (standard letter size) with a low-profile metal clip. The writing surface is a ceramic-steel whiteboard layer laminated over a dense hardboard core. That's it — no markers, no eraser, no mounting hardware. You'll need to supply your own dry erase supplies, which is worth noting if you're buying this as a gift or starting from scratch.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The standout feature here is the rigidity. In my testing, the board barely flexed even when I pressed down firmly with a ballpoint pen. That's a direct result of the 3mm hardboard construction — it's noticeably more substantial than the 1-2mm particle board you'll find on budget clipboards. The low-profile clip is another thoughtful touch; it holds a 40-page stack securely but sits flush enough to slide into a briefcase without snagging.

The whiteboard surface itself performs admirably. Dry erase markers glide smoothly, and even after leaving black ink on overnight, it wiped clean with minimal ghosting. One buyer noted, *"I use this on construction sites daily. The surface still looks new after three months of abuse."* Another praised the erasing specifically: *"Finally a clipboard that doesn't leave those annoying marker shadows. One wipe and it's clean."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The single black colorway is a missed opportunity — if you wanted to color-code clipboards for different teams or projects, you're out of luck. More notably, the whiteboard surface is a fingerprint magnet. Within minutes of handling, it looks smudged and slightly grubby. You'll want a microfiber cloth nearby if you care about presentation.

A few reviewers also mentioned that the clip, while sturdy, is not removable. If you planned to use this as a standalone whiteboard on a wall, you're stuck with the clip protruding. Minor, but worth knowing.

A: No. You'll need to purchase markers and an eraser separately. A standard dry erase marker works perfectly.

**Q: Can I use it as a wall-mounted whiteboard?**
A: Technically yes, but the clip is fixed and will protrude. It's designed as a handheld clipboard, not a wall display.

**Q: How does it hold up to heavy daily use?**
A: The ceramic-steel surface is scratch-resistant and the hardboard core holds up well. Multiple reviewers report months of daily use without warping or surface degradation.
