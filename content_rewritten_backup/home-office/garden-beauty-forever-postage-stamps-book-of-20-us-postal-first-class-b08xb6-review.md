## Where It Falls Short

Let's be straight about the tradeoffs. First, this is a booklet of 20, not a roll or sheet of 100. If you're a high-volume mailer — say, running a small business that sends invoices weekly — you'll be reordering frequently. The per-stamp cost is the same as any Forever stamp, but the convenience factor is lower than bulk options.

Second, the Garden Beauty design is decorative. If you're sending formal business correspondence, a florid botanical print might feel out of place. The stamps are lovely for personal mail, thank-you notes, and invitations, but they're not neutral.

Third, the booklet format means all 20 stamps live in one place. Lose the booklet, lose all 20. A roll or sheet distributes the risk a bit more.
