## What You Actually Get

The calendar covers 18 months, running from July 2026 through December 2027. Each monthly spread sits on a two-page layout with generous daily blocks, roughly 1.5 inches by 1.25 inches. That's real writing room, not the sliver you get on smaller desk calendars.

The paper is thicker than what you'll find on most budget calendars — 120gsm stock that holds up to gel pens and light marker without ghosting through to the next page. ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides. First, this is a month-at-a-glance only. If you need weekly or daily breakdowns for hourly scheduling, this isn't your calendar — you'll want a planner with more granular views.

Second, the physical footprint. At 17 inches wide when open, it will overhang a standard 12-inch-deep desk. It's called "medium" but that's relative to giant wall calendars, not to your actual workspace. Measure before you buy.

Third, the price point sits above basic desk calendars. You're paying for the paper quality and the 18-month span, but if you only need a single year and don't care about paper heft, you can find cheaper options.
