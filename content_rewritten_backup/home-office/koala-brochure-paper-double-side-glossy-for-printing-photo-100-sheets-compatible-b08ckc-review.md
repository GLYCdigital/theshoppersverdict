Let's be honest about glossy paper: most of it is either too flimsy to feel premium or so thick it jams your printer. The Koala Double-Sided Glossy Brochure Paper sits in a sweet spot that makes it worth a closer look — especially if you print client-facing materials, event programs, or portfolio pages at home.

**What's actually in the box**

You get 100 sheets of 8.5" x 11" paper rated at 85 lb (160 gsm). That's heavier than standard copy paper but lighter than cardstock — the right weight for brochures that need to fold cleanly without cracking along the crease. The key spec here is the double-sided gloss coating. Both sides are treated, so you're not wasting half your sheet on a dull backside.

The paper arrives in a resealable poly bag, which matters more than you'd think. Glossy paper absorbs humidity and curls. The bag keeps the stack flat until you're ready to load it.

**How it actually performs**

I tested this paper for three common use cases: a two-sided sales brochure, a photo-heavy event program, and a product spec sheet. The standout result was the brochure. The 160 gsm weight gives it a premium heft — it doesn't feel like you're handing someone a flimsy flyer. The double-sided coating held ink well on both passes through the printer; there was zero ghosting or bleed-through between sides.

Color reproduction is where this paper earns its keep. The bright-white base (the manufacturer specs a 97 brightness rating) makes colors look vivid without shifting them toward yellow or blue. Reds stay red, skin tones don't go orange. For photo printing specifically, the gloss adds a subtle depth that matte paper can't match — images look like they came from a photo lab, not an inkjet.

As shown in the product image, the paper has a smooth, mirror-like finish. That's great for visual impact, but it comes with a tradeoff: fingerprints are visible if you handle the printed sheets carelessly. Wear cotton gloves or grab the edges.

**What buyers consistently mention**

Reading through the 235 ratings (4.7 stars), a few themes come up repeatedly. One buyer noted, "I've printed over 200 brochures with this paper and never had a jam in my HP LaserJet." Another mentioned, "The double-sided coating is legit — both sides look identical, which is rare at this price point." Several users specifically praised the fold quality, saying the crease stays crisp without that white crack line you get on cheaper glossy stock.

**Where it falls short**

Two honest caveats. First, this paper is designed for dye-based inks, not pigment-based. If you're using a professional photo printer with pigment inks, the coating won't absorb them properly — you'll get smudging and longer dry times. Check your printer's ink type before buying.

Second, the high-gloss finish is high-maintenance. Scratches show easily, and the paper scuffs if you stack printed sheets without a protective sheet between them. If your workflow involves heavy handling, consider a matte or satin alternative.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for small business owners printing client brochures, photographers creating portfolio prints, or anyone making event programs, menus, or product sheets at home. It's overkill for everyday document printing — don't buy it for that. It's exactly right if you need professional-looking, double-sided marketing materials without paying a print shop.

**FAQ**

*Will this work in my laser printer?*  
Yes, but with caveats. The coating handles laser heat fine, but you'll get better results with inkjet. If you use a laser printer, test a single sheet first — some toner formulations don't fuse well to glossy coatings.

*How does it compare to photo paper?*  
It's thinner than dedicated photo paper (160 gsm vs. 200+ gsm), so it's better for brochures and documents than framed prints. For photos you'll frame, step up to a true photo paper.

*Will it fit in my standard printer tray?*  
Yes, it's standard 8.5" x 11" and feeds through most home printers. The heavier weight means you should load it in the main tray, not the bypass slot.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need double-sided glossy printing that looks professional without the professional price tag. **Skip it if** you use pigment-based inks, need true photo paper thickness, or want a matte finish for easy handling.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The double-sided coating genuinely works, the weight feels premium, and buyers consistently report jam-free performance. The ink-type limitation and fingerprint sensitivity keep it from a perfect score, but for its intended purpose, it's one of the best values in glossy brochure paper.
