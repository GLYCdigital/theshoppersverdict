The most common complaint is the color cartridges. Multiple reviewers mention that yellow and cyan run out faster than expected, especially if you print a lot of graphics or highlighted documents. If you print presentations with full-bleed color, buy two packs.

The second issue is the occasional dud. A handful of reviewers report one cartridge in the five-pack failing to register or leaking slightly. The math still works in your favor: one dud out of five is still cheaper than buying five OEM cartridges.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will these cartridges work in my Epson Expression or Workforce printer?**
A: Only if your printer uses the 410/412 cartridge series. Check your printer’s manual or the cartridge number printed on your current OEM cartridge. If it says “410” or “412,” you’re good.

**Q: Do these void my printer warranty?**
A: No. In the US, the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act prevents manufacturers from voiding warranties because you used third-party consumables. Epson cannot deny a repair claim solely because you used remanufactured ink.

**Q: How do these compare to Epson OEM cartridges in page yield?**
A: For black, you’ll get roughly 80–90% of OEM yield — about 400–450 pages per cartridge. Color cartridges yield slightly less, around 350–400 pages, because the ink volume is marginally lower.
