It’s genuinely thicker and better-finished than the generic options crowding Amazon — and with a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, it’s clear buyers notice the difference.

## What You Actually Get

This is a single-piece, L-shaped acrylic stand designed for standard 8.5x11 paper. It arrives wrapped in protective film on both sides — don’t skip peeling that off, or you’ll think the acrylic is cloudy. The base is flat and stable, and the back panel sits at a slight backward angle that’s comfortable for reading at eye level on a counter or desk.

The key spec to note: the acrylic thickness. At roughly 0.1 inches (about 2.5mm), this is noticeably beefier than the 1.5mm holders you’ll find for half the price. That thickness translates directly to stability — it won’t flex when you slide a flyer in or out.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this with standard printer paper, a laminated menu, and a thicker cardstock sign. All three slid in and out of the recessed lip without catching or bending. The 0.2-inch lip at the bottom holds the paper securely — you can carry the holder across a room without the insert sliding out, which is more than I can say for cheaper L-shaped stands.

The acrylic is crystal clear, not the slightly green-tinted plastic you see on budget models. Text underneath remains fully legible with no distortion. On a retail counter or office front desk, it looks professional — not like a temporary placeholder.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest caveats. First, this is not scratch-resistant. If you wipe it with a dry paper towel or any abrasive cloth, you’ll leave fine micro-scratches that show up under direct lighting. Use a microfiber cloth and spray cleaner. Second, there’s no wall-mount option — no pre-drilled holes or adhesive strips. This is strictly for horizontal surfaces. If you need to hang a sign on a wall or glass door, look elsewhere.

Also note: it’s sized for 8.5x11 only. If you regularly display half-sheet or legal-size documents, this isn’t your holder.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this with double-sided signs?**
Yes — the clear acrylic shows both sides, though the back side is slightly less legible when placed against a dark surface.

**Q: Does it fit thicker paper or laminated sheets?**
Laminated pages slide in fine. For cardstock thicker than 110 lb, you may need to trim the edges slightly to fit the recessed lip.

**Q: How should I clean it without scratching?**
Use a microfiber cloth with a mild glass cleaner. Avoid paper towels, which contain wood fibers that scratch acrylic.
