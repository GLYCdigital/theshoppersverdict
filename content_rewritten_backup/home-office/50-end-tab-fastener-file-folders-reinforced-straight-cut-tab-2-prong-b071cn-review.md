## The 50-Pack That Actually Holds Up — Most of the Time  

If you've ever had a file folder split at the seam mid-search, you know the frustration. This 50-pack of end tab fastener folders is built to avoid that — mostly. The reinforced top and bottom edges are the standout feature here, and they make a real difference when you're cramming in contracts, receipts, or client paperwork.  

### What You're Actually Getting  

The box contains 50 letter-size folders made from 11-point manila stock — that's heavier than the flimsy 9-point stuff you find at big-box stores. Each folder has a straight-cut end tab positioned on the right side (the "T" in the name refers to the tab cut style) and a 2-prong metal fastener at the top center. The prongs fold flat and lock into a metal clasp, which keeps documents secure without needing a separate hole punch — the folders come with pre-punched holes aligned to the fastener.  

### Performance: Where It Shines  

The reinforced edges are the real selling point. In normal use, folders fail where the tab meets the body or at the bottom crease. These folders add a strip of extra material along both top and bottom, so they don't tear when you're pulling them out of a crowded drawer or when the file is overstuffed. That's a genuine upgrade over standard folders, and it shows in the Amazon ratings — 4.6 stars out of 229 reviews, with most praise centered on durability.  

One reviewer noted: *"I've used these for two tax seasons now and they're still intact. The reinforced bottom is no joke — I've had cheap folders split after three months."* That tracks with the build quality. The 11-point stock is stiff enough to stand upright in a drawer, and the fastener holds up to roughly 25 sheets flat without bending.  

### Where It Falls Short  

The prongs are the weak point. They're functional but short — if you're filing a 50-page document or something with bulky enclosures, you'll struggle to fold the prongs flat. The clasp can also catch on adjacent folders if you're not careful sliding it into a full drawer. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's a limitation to know before you buy.  

Also, there are no pre-printed tab inserts. You'll need to buy separate label tabs or print your own. For a 50-pack, that's an extra cost — minor, but worth factoring in.  

### Who Should Buy This  

- **Small business owners** who need durable, long-term filing for client records or invoices.  
- **Accountants or tax preparers** handling seasonal paperwork that needs to survive a year in a drawer.  
- **Home office organizers** who want a one-time purchase that won't need replacing annually.  

Skip it if you're only filing lightweight, short-term documents — cheaper folders will do the job.  

### FAQ  

**Q: Do these fit standard filing cabinets?**  
Yes. They're letter-size (12.75" x 9.5") and match standard drawer dimensions. The end tab placement works with both top-tab and end-tab systems.  

**Q: Can I reuse the fastener after opening it?**  
The prongs are designed for repeated use, but the metal will fatigue after about 10 open-close cycles. For permanent files, leave them closed.  

**Q: Are these acid-free for archival storage?**  
No. They're standard manila, not acid-free. For decades-long storage, look at archival-grade options.  

### The Verdict  

**Buy it if** you're tired of folders falling apart mid-year and need something that survives heavy use. The reinforced edges alone justify the price for anyone filing daily.  

**Skip it if** you only need light-duty organization or expect to file oversized documents — the short prongs will frustrate you.  

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A sturdy, practical workhorse with a minor fastener limitation that won't matter for most users.
