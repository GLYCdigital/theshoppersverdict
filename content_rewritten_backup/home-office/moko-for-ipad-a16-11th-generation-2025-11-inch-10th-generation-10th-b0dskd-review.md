If you just dropped $349+ on the new iPad (A16) 11th generation, the last thing you want is a case that fights you every time you use it. The MoKo case for the 11th gen (and 10th gen) iPad is currently sitting at 4.8 stars from 240 buyers — and after looking at the specifics, it earns most of that praise. But it's not flawless, and you should know exactly what you're getting before you add it to cart.

## What's actually in the box

You get the case itself — a trifold front cover with a translucent back shell and an integrated pencil holder. There's no screen protector included, no extra accessories. What matters is the fit: this is molded specifically for the A16 11th gen (2025) and the 10th gen (2022), which share the same 11-inch form factor. The cutouts for the camera bump, speakers, and USB-C port are positioned correctly. The button covers are responsive — a common weak point on budget cases, but buyers report they click properly here.

## Build quality and daily use

The front cover is a hard-shell plastic with a soft microfiber lining that rests against the screen. The trifold design gives you two landscape viewing angles and a lower typing incline. The magnets holding the flap in place are strong enough that the case stays propped up during note-taking — one reviewer noted, "I've used it daily for two weeks and the stand hasn't collapsed once, even with my heavy-handed stylus tapping."

The back is a transparent polycarbonate that shows off your iPad's color. That's a nice touch if you opted for the blue or pink finish. The downside? It's a fingerprint magnet. If you're someone who notices smudges, you'll be wiping it down regularly.

The pencil holder runs along the side edge. It keeps the Apple Pencil Pro secure, but it's worth noting: if you charge your Pencil wirelessly by snapping it to the iPad's magnetic edge, this holder sits on the same side and can interfere with that magnetic connection. You'll need to remove the Pencil from the holder to charge it properly. That's a design tradeoff, not a dealbreaker.

## What buyers consistently praise

The standout theme in reviews is the fit. Multiple buyers called it "perfectly snug" — the corners don't peel away, and the iPad doesn't rattle inside the shell. The precise alignment of the camera cutout is another recurring compliment. One verified purchase said, "I've bought three different cases for this iPad and returned two. This one fits like it was made by Apple." That's the kind of feedback that matters.

The price point also draws consistent mention. For a case that offers drop protection, a stand, and a pencil holder, buyers feel it's a strong value compared to Apple's own $79 folio.

## Where it falls short

The fingerprint issue on the clear back is the most common complaint. It's cosmetic, but if you're particular about how your gear looks, it'll bother you.

The auto sleep/wake feature is another point of confusion. Some colorways include the magnetic sensor that wakes the iPad when you open the cover; others don't. Buyers have reported receiving versions without the feature even when the listing suggests it's included. If auto wake matters to you, check the product details carefully before ordering — or be prepared to live without it.

The side-mounted pencil holder, as mentioned, can block wireless charging for the Pencil Pro if that's your charging method.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** You want a reliable, well-fitting case under $25 that protects your new iPad and gives you a functional stand for watching content or typing. It's ideal for students taking notes in class and professionals who need a lightweight folio for meetings.

**Skip it if:** You need guaranteed auto wake/sleep, you hate visible smudges on clear plastic, or you primarily charge your Pencil Pro wirelessly and want a case that doesn't get in the way.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this case fit both the 10th gen and 11th gen iPad?**
A: Yes. The 11th gen (A16, 2025) and 10th gen (2022) share the same dimensions, so this case fits both. Just confirm you're ordering the 11-inch version, not the 10.9-inch Air.

**Q: Does the front cover support the iPad in portrait orientation?**
A: No. The trifold design only provides landscape viewing angles. For portrait use, you'll need to hold it or prop it against something.

**Q: Will the Apple Pencil Pro charge through the case?**
A: No. The pencil holder is on the side, and the iPad's magnetic charging edge is covered by the case material. You'll need to remove the Pencil to charge it.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5 (⭐⭐⭐⭐½)**

The MoKo case for iPad A16 11th gen is a solid, dependable choice for most buyers. The fit is excellent, the stand works, and the price undercuts Apple's official option significantly. The fingerprint-prone back and inconsistent auto wake feature keep it from being perfect, but at this price point, it's a smart buy for anyone who wants protection without overpaying. If you can live with those two quirks, this case will serve you well.
