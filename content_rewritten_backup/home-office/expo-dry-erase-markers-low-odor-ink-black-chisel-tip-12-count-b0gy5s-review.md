Let's be honest: when you search for dry erase markers, you're probably just tired of the cheap ones that skip, smear, and smell like a chemistry lab. The EXPO 12-pack of black chisel tip markers is the default choice for a reason — but "default" doesn't always mean "best." Here's the real story after putting these through actual board work, both in an office and on a whiteboard at home.

**What you actually get**

Twelve black markers, chisel tip, low odor ink formula. That's it — no eraser, no cleaner, no surprise extras. The packaging is standard blister plastic that's easy enough to open. Each marker has the classic EXPO barrel design: a black cap that clicks securely, a color-matched body, and a chisel nib that's roughly 5mm at its widest. The clip on each cap is functional and won't snap off with normal use.

**Performance where it matters**

The chisel tip is the star here. It's genuinely useful — hold it at an angle and you get a fat stroke for headers or emphasis; flip it perpendicular and you get a clean, thin line for writing notes or drawing diagrams. The low odor ink does what it claims: it has that faint chemical marker smell, but it's nowhere near the eye-watering intensity of cheaper brands. In a windowless conference room with six people and the door closed, it's tolerable. That alone justifies the price premium over generic store brands.

Ink flow is consistent across all 12 markers in my testing. No skips on vertical boards, no patchy lines when you write fast. The black is genuinely black — dense and opaque, not the grayish tint you get from economy markers. As shown in the product image, these are straightforward workhorses, nothing flashy, which is exactly what most offices need.

The 4.0 average across 200 ratings masks a pattern: people who use these daily for meetings and classrooms love them. One verified buyer noted, "These are the only markers I buy for my classroom now. **Where it falls short**

The honest downsides: these markers dry out faster than the premium EXPO variants if you're careless with caps. I left one uncapped for about 20 minutes during a brainstorming session and noticed reduced intensity afterward. Another common complaint in the reviews — several buyers mention receiving one or two markers in a pack that need aggressive shaking to get flowing. It's not widespread, but it happens. Also, the 12-pack being all black means you're locked into monochrome unless you buy the multi-color packs separately, which costs more per marker.

**FAQ**

**Q: Do these erase cleanly from whiteboards?**
A: Yes, with standard dry erase erasers or even a dry paper towel. Let the ink sit for a few seconds before erasing — if you erase immediately, you'll get smudging.

**Q: How long does one marker last?**
A: With daily use and proper capping, expect 1-2 weeks per marker for heavy users, or 2-3 months for occasional board writers. Leaving caps off cuts lifespan dramatically.

**Q: Are these safe for kids?**
A: The low odor formula is non-toxic and doesn't stain — safe for classroom use. That said, they're not marketed as children's art supplies and shouldn't be used on skin.
