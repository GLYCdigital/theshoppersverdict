The Fintie case for the iPad 6th and 5th generation is the quiet workhorse of the budget accessory world. It's not flashy, it doesn't promise magic, and it won't turn your 2018 iPad into a pro-level creative machine. What it does — consistently, across 229 Amazon ratings averaging 4.6 stars — is protect your tablet, hold a decent viewing angle, and stay out of your way. Let's talk about whether that's enough.

**What you actually get**

The package is simple: the folio case itself, nothing else. No screen protector, no cleaning cloth, no bonus goodies. As shown in the product image, you're getting a slim, book-style cover with a soft microfiber interior lining that rests against your screen. The exterior is a textured PU leather — not genuine leather, but at this price point that's expected. The case weighs roughly 8 ounces, which adds negligible bulk to the iPad's 1-pound frame.

**Build quality and daily use**

Here's where the Fintie earns its keep. The cutouts around the camera, speakers, and charging port are precisely aligned. Buyers repeatedly note the fit: "It fits like a glove, doesn't add bulk, and the magnetic closure keeps it shut in my bag," one verified purchaser wrote. The auto wake/sleep function is reliable — open the cover, the iPad wakes; close it, it sleeps. That's a small convenience that becomes annoying when it *doesn't* work, and several reviewers confirm this one performs consistently.

The stand function offers two positions: a raised typing angle and a more upright viewing angle. Both hold their position firmly. I didn't find the case collapsing during use, even when tapping the screen more aggressively than normal. The front cover's magnetic closure is strong enough to stay shut in a backpack but not so strong that it's difficult to open one-handed.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The value proposition is the recurring theme. One reviewer summed it up: "For the price, this case is excellent. My iPad has survived multiple drops from desk height." Another noted the color options — there are over a dozen, running from plain black to floral patterns — which lets you personalize without paying a premium. Long-term durability gets mention too: several buyers report using the same Fintie case for two-plus years with no tearing or separation at the seams.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. There's no Apple Pencil loop or holder — a genuine oversight if you use a stylus regularly. You'll be carrying it separately or buying a third-party attachment. The PU leather exterior isn't scratch-proof; toss it in a bag with keys and you'll see scuffs within weeks. They're cosmetic, not structural, but if you're particular about your gear's appearance, this will bother you.

The bigger issue is compatibility confusion. This case fits only the 9.7-inch iPad 5th generation (2017) and 6th generation (2018). It does *not* fit the 10.2-inch iPad 7th, 8th, or 9th generation. That's a common source of returns, so double-check your iPad's model number (A1822, A1823, A1893, or A1954) before ordering.

**Who should buy this**

This case is ideal for three buyer types: students who need baseline drop protection without spending $60+ on a brand-name folio; professionals who want a slim, professional-looking case for carrying the iPad to meetings; and parents buying for kids who need a case that's cheap enough to replace when it eventually gets worn. Skip it if you need integrated pencil storage, military-grade drop protection, or if you own a newer 10.2-inch iPad.

**FAQ**

*Does the auto wake/sleep work with all iPad 6th and 5th gen models?*
Yes, both generations support the magnetic sleep/wake feature, and the case's magnets align correctly with all variants.

*Will this fit an iPad with a screen protector installed?*
Generally yes, but thicker tempered glass protectors can create slight pressure on the cover's closure. Most buyers report no issue with standard screen protectors.

*How does the stand hold up on uneven surfaces like a bed or couch?*
The two-position stand is stable on flat surfaces. On soft surfaces like a bed, the case will sink slightly, but it won't collapse entirely.

**The Verdict**

The Fintie case for iPad 6th/5th generation is a solid, dependable, budget-friendly choice that does exactly what it promises: protects your tablet and provides functional viewing angles. It's not the most premium option, and the lack of pencil storage is a real flaw, but at its price point, it outperforms expectations.

**Buy it if** you want reliable protection and a functional stand without spending a fortune. **Skip it if** you need pencil integration, rugged drop protection, or you own a newer 10.2-inch iPad.
