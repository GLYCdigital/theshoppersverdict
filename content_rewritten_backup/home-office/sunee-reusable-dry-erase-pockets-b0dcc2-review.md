Let's be honest about dry erase pockets: most are flimsy, ghost-stained garbage that tear at the seam after three weeks. The SUNEE Reusable Dry Erase Pockets are not that. With a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, these have quietly become a classroom and home-office staple. Here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The set includes five pockets in assorted bright colors (red, blue, green, yellow, and orange), plus five low-odor dry erase markers and one felt eraser. The pockets measure 9.5 x 12.5 inches — slightly oversized so standard 8.5x11\" paper fits without bending corners. The front is crystal-clear PVC; the back is a matte finish that gives you a writing surface with some tooth to it.

This matters more than you'd think. The matte back means your dry erase marker doesn't skate around like it's on glass. You get actual control over your handwriting, which teachers will appreciate when modeling math problems or phonics drills.

## Build Quality That Survives Real Use

The difference between these and the $8 generic packs is the construction. The edges are reinforced with stitched binding rather than heat-sealed welds that split. The grommet at the top is metal, not plastic, so it won't crack when you hang them on hooks. One reviewer noted, "I've had mine for a full school year and they still look new — the stitching hasn't pulled at all."

The PVC is thick enough to resist creasing but flexible enough to bend without cracking. If a student folds one in half (they will), it springs back flat.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 240 ratings, three themes dominate:

- **No ghosting**: "I was skeptical because my last set stained within a week. These wipe completely clean even after sitting overnight with marker on them."
- **Versatility**: Teachers are using them for math centers, handwriting practice, and even as reusable menu covers at home.

## Where It Falls Short

The included eraser is one small felt square for five pockets. You'll want to buy a bulk pack of microfiber cloths or use an old sock. That's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

Second, the top opening is not sealed. If you're using these for flashcards or loose worksheets, small items can slide out when stored vertically. The solution is simple — fold the paper slightly or use a binder clip — but worth knowing before you buy.

Finally, the colors are not as vibrant as the product photos suggest. The blue is more of a muted navy, and the yellow leans mustard. Cosmetic only, but if you're buying these to color-code subjects, adjust your expectations.

## FAQ

**Q: Can you use any dry erase marker, or do you need specific ones?**
A: Any standard dry erase marker works. The included ones are fine, but you can swap in Expo or any other brand. Do NOT use permanent markers — they'll bond to the PVC and require rubbing alcohol to remove.

**Q: Do these work with wet erase markers?**
A: Yes, but wet erase ink sits on the surface longer and may require a damp cloth. Stick to dry erase for daily use.

**Q: How do you clean them if they do ghost?**
A: A bit of rubbing alcohol on a cloth removes stubborn residue. Avoid abrasive scrubbers — they'll scratch the surface and make ghosting worse.
