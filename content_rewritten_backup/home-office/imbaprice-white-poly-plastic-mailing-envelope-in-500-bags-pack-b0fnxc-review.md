## What You Actually Get

Open the box and you’ll find 500 flat poly bags, each measuring 10.5 x 16 inches. That’s a solid medium-large size—fits a hoodie, a pair of jeans, a small book, or several shirts folded flat. The bags are white, semi-translucent (you can see shapes through them, but not read text), and feature a self-seal adhesive strip running across the top flap. There’s also a perforated tear-away strip that lets you open the bag without scissors, plus a second adhesive strip underneath for returns or reshipment. That dual-strip design isn’t common at this price point.

The material is 1.5-mil polyethylene—not the thickest you can buy, but standard for e-commerce shipping. It’s water-resistant, not waterproof. A light drizzle won’t hurt your contents; submerging it in a puddle will.

## Real-World Performance

I’ve shipped with these for several weeks across different carriers. The self-seal adhesive is aggressive—press it down once and it’s not coming off without tearing the bag. That’s good for security, but it also means you can’t reposition a misaligned seal. Line it up carefully the first time.

The tear-away strip works as advertised. One clean pull and the bag opens evenly. I’d rate the durability at “solid for normal use but not indestructible.” If you’re shipping heavy items with sharp edges (tools, metal parts), wrap them first or choose a padded mailer. These are for soft goods, clothing, and lightweight products.

One thing that impressed me: the bags lie flat and don’t have that “stuck together” static-cling problem you get with cheaper poly mailers. They separate easily, which speeds up packing when you’re filling dozens of orders.

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty requires addressing the limitations. First, these are thin. At 1.5 mil, they won’t survive rough handling with heavy contents. If you ship books or anything with corners, expect possible punctures.

Second, the 10.5 x 16 size is your only option in this listing. If you need small mailers for jewelry or large ones for bulky coats, you’ll need to look at iMBAPrice’s other listings. There’s no size mix in this pack.

Third, the white color shows scuffs and dirt quickly. They don’t look beat-up when they arrive, but they don’t look pristine either. If brand presentation matters, consider the opaque black version instead.

## FAQ

**Can I write directly on these with a Sharpie?** Yes, but it’s not ideal. The poly material smudges if you rub it immediately. Let the ink dry for 30 seconds before stacking. Better option: use a shipping label or tape a paper label on top.

**Will these fit a USPS Priority Mail flat rate?** No. These are for sending via your own shipping labels (UPS, FedEx, USPS First Class or Parcel Select). They won’t work with flat-rate packaging requirements.

**Are they recyclable?** The bags are LDPE #4 plastic. Many big-box stores (Target, Walmart) have plastic film recycling bins that accept these. Check with your local drop-off—don’t toss them in curbside recycling.
