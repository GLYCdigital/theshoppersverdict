Door-to-door solicitors don't read flimsy paper signs. They ignore faded plastic. But a thick, laser-engraved acrylic plaque? That changes the conversation before it starts. The Maoerzai No Soliciting Sign is the rare home accessory that actually does its job without looking like a prison warning.

**What you actually get**

The package contains one 6-inch by 2-inch acrylic sign, roughly 4mm thick — substantially beefier than the 1-2mm acrylic competitors use. The lettering is laser-etched into the surface, not printed on top, which means it's physically part of the material. The back comes with pre-applied 3M adhesive strips covered by a red release liner. Peel, press, done. No screws, no mounting hardware, no level required.

**Build quality and real-world performance**

This thing feels premium in hand. The acrylic has a subtle beveled edge that catches light nicely, and the frosted etching contrasts cleanly against the clear background. As shown in the product image, it reads "No Soliciting" with a small "No Trespassing" line below — concise, professional, unambiguous.

The 3M adhesive is the star here. It's the same industrial-grade tape used on car emblems, and it bonds aggressively to wood, metal, glass, and most painted surfaces. One reviewer noted, "Stuck it on my steel front door two months ago and it hasn't budged through rain, snow, and 90-degree heat." That tracks — 3M VHB tape is rated for extreme temperature swings and outdoor exposure.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The durability and appearance dominate the feedback. Multiple reviewers mention neighbors asking where they got it. One wrote, "Finally a sign that doesn't look like it came from a dollar store. It's elegant enough for my office and clear enough for solicitors to actually read." Another praised the thickness specifically: "I've bought three other signs before this. They all warped or faded within months. This one looks brand new after a year."

**Where it falls short**

The permanent adhesive is a double-edged sword. If you rent, or if you ever want to reposition the sign, you're likely looking at damaged paint or a sticky residue that requires solvent and elbow grease to remove. Test a corner before committing.

Second, at 6 inches wide, it's compact. On a wide front door or a large office glass panel, it can get visually lost. If you want maximum deterrent effect, you might need something larger — though that means giving up the sleek aesthetic.

Finally, the design is fixed. You get the modern minimal look. No customization, no option for different text, and the "No Trespassing" line might feel redundant for some users.

**Who should buy this**

- **Homeowners with a front door problem**: If you're tired of solar salespeople and roofing scammers interrupting dinner, this is your first line of defense.
- **Small business owners**: Put one on your office door or reception glass. It looks professional while cutting down on unsolicited vendor pitches.
- **Anyone replacing a worn-out sign**: If your current sign is faded, cracked, or just ugly, this is a clear upgrade that will outlast your mortgage.

**Skip it if** you're a renter who can't risk surface damage, or if you need a sign visible from across the street — this is a close-range deterrent, not a billboard.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does it work on textured or brick surfaces?**
A: No. The adhesive needs a smooth, flat surface. Textured doors, brick, or stucco won't get full contact, and the sign will eventually fall off. Use it on painted doors, metal, glass, or smooth wood.

**Q: Can I remove it without damaging my door?**
A: Technically yes, but it's risky. Heat the adhesive with a hairdryer and use fishing line to gently work it loose. Expect some residue and possibly paint discoloration on cheaper finishes. It's designed to be permanent.

**Q: Will it hold up outdoors in extreme weather?**
A: Yes. The acrylic won't yellow from UV exposure, and the 3M tape handles temperatures from -20°F to 200°F. Rain, snow, and sun won't affect it. I've seen no complaints about weather damage in the reviews.
