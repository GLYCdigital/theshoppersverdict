**What You Actually Get**

This is a bulk buy—50 folders in one box. Each folder is a standard letter-size (9.5" x 11.5") with two interior pockets and a 3-prong metal fastener in the center. The prongs are designed to hold punched paper (up to roughly 20 sheets) securely, while the pockets catch unpunched handouts, receipts, or homework. The folders are made from a heavy-duty paper stock—around 11-12 pt thickness, which is noticeably sturdier than the flimsy folders you get at dollar stores. They come in assorted colors (typically blue, red, yellow, green, and purple), which helps with color-coding if you’re the organized type.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The first thing you’ll notice is the rigidity. These aren’t the kind of folders that flop over in a backpack. The paper stock has a slight gloss finish, which resists minor spills and smudges better than matte options. The prongs are sturdy—they don’t bend or snap off when you’re pushing them through a stack of papers. One reviewer noted, *“I’ve bought cheap folders before where the prongs tore through the paper. These held up for an entire semester.”* That tracks with the material quality.

The pockets are deep enough to hold 30-40 loose sheets without bulging. The folders lie flat when closed, which is a small but important detail if you’re filing them in a drawer or cabinet. The colors are consistent across the pack—no weird dye variations.

**Where It Falls Short**

No product is perfect, and these folders have two notable weaknesses. First, the prongs are positioned in the center, which means you can’t use them with top-bound or side-bound punched paper—only center-punched (which is standard for 3-hole paper, so this is a minor limitation). Second, the paper stock, while sturdy, is not waterproof. If you’re leaving these in a car or a damp basement, the corners will eventually warp. That’s a tradeoff at this price point—you’re not getting Tyvek durability.

A few reviewers mentioned that the colors are random per pack, so you can’t request a specific color distribution. If you need exactly 10 of each color for a filing system, you’ll be disappointed.
