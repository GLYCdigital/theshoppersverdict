Second, there’s no cable tie or clip included. For a product marketed as “workspace gear,” a simple Velcro strap would’ve been a thoughtful addition. Neither issue is a dealbreaker, but they’re worth knowing before you buy.

Also note: this is a micro-USB cable. If your device has moved to USB-C (newer Ring models and most 2025-era cameras), this won’t fit. Check your device’s port before ordering.
