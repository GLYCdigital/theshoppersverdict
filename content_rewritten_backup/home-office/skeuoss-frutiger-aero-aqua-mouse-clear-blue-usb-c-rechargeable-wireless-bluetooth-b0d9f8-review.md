**Where It Falls Short**

No product is perfect, and this one has a few honest tradeoffs:

1. **The glossy shell is a fingerprint magnet.** You will need to wipe it down weekly. The clear blue finish shows smudges easily.
2. **The mouse is on the smaller side.** If you have large hands (XL glove size or bigger), your palm will drag on the desk. It’s best for medium or smaller grips.
3. **No adjustable DPI button on the fly.** You’ll need to change sensitivity via system settings, not a physical button on the mouse.
4. **The Bluetooth connection drops occasionally** if you’re more than ~8 feet from the device. The 2.4GHz receiver is more reliable.
