**What's actually in the box**

You get two sealed cartridges: one PG-275XL black and one CL-276XL tri-color (cyan, magenta, yellow). For a home office printing a few documents daily plus occasional photos, that's roughly two to three months per set.

**Performance and build quality**

These are OEM Canon cartridges, not third-party refurbs. That matters because the print head nozzles on the Pixma line are sensitive — off-brand ink often leads to clogging or streaking within weeks.
