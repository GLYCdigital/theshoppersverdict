## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the limitations. If you're not someone who needs 15-minute granularity, this planner will feel overly structured. The daily page format means you're flipping pages constantly — there's no weekly overview to help you plan ahead. That's a significant tradeoff for anyone who thinks in terms of the week rather than the day.

The 7 AM to 8 PM time range is also a constraint. Night owls who work past 8 PM or early risers who start before 7 AM will find themselves writing in margins or skipping sections entirely. The notes area at the bottom helps, but it's not ideal.
