Four notepads, each measuring about 4.5 x 6 inches—small enough to fit between cabinet handles but large enough for a real grocery list. Each pad comes with a full magnetic back, not just a strip. **FAQ: What shoppers actually ask**  

*Do these work on stainless steel fridges?*  
Yes, the full-back magnet grips smooth and brushed stainless well. For textured or fingerprint-resistant finishes, they hold fine—just avoid extremely rough surfaces.

*Can I use dry-erase markers on the covers?*  
The covers are matte cardstock, not laminate. Dry-erase will stain them. Use the paper pages for writing and keep markers away from the covers.

*Are the pads refillable?*  
No. Each pad is disposable. You'll repurchase the set or find a compatible refill—though the specific size and adhesive backing make third-party refills hard to find.
