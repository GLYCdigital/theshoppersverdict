# Mount-It! Sit-Stand Monitor Arm Desk Mount with Keyboard Tray Review

## A Full Workspace Transformation in One Box

If you've been eyeing a standing desk conversion but don't want to replace your entire desk setup, the Mount-It! Sit-Stand Monitor Arm Desk Mount with Keyboard Tray might be the middle ground you're looking for. This single unit combines a gas-spring monitor arm with an articulating keyboard platform, turning any flat surface into a sit-stand workstation. At a 4.1-star average across 204 ratings, it's clearly working for many people — but it's not without compromises.

---

## What's Actually in the Box

The Mount-It! package includes the monitor arm assembly, a keyboard tray with a separate articulating arm, a C-clamp mounting bracket, a grommet mount option, and all necessary hardware. The monitor arm supports screens from 13 to 32 inches with a weight capacity of up to 19.8 pounds per VESA mount. The keyboard tray measures roughly 24 inches wide with a smaller mouse platform that attaches to either side — a nice touch for lefties or righties.

You also get a gas spring tension adjustment tool, cable management clips, and a hex wrench kit. Nothing feels cheaply machined here; the aluminum and steel construction has a solid, commercial-grade feel out of the box.

---

## Real-World Performance: Where It Shines

The headline feature — converting a standard desk into a sit-stand setup — works genuinely well. The gas spring mechanism lifts a 20-pound monitor with smooth, controlled motion. Adjusting tension requires a bit of trial and error (the instructions suggest starting with minimal tension and working up), but once dialed in, the arm holds position without sagging or drifting.

The keyboard tray is the standout. Its articulating arm tucks the keyboard completely under the desk when you want to stand, and pulls it into a comfortable ergonomic position when seated. The tilt mechanism lets you angle the tray slightly for better wrist alignment — a small detail that matters after hour three of typing.

Buyers consistently praise the build quality. One reviewer noted, *"This thing is rock solid — I've got a 27-inch monitor and a heavy mechanical keyboard on it, and there's zero wobble at standing height."* Another echoed, *"Feels like a $300 product for half the price."*

---

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the limitations.

**The keyboard tray is shallow.** At roughly 9 inches deep, it won't comfortably fit a full-size keyboard with a wrist rest, and the attached mouse pad area is small. Anyone using a numeric keypad plus a separate mouse will feel cramped.

**Setup is genuinely frustrating.** Multiple reviewers describe the assembly as "time-consuming" and "confusing." The manual's diagrams are small and occasionally ambiguous, particularly around the gas spring tension adjustment. Budget 45–60 minutes for installation, and keep your patience handy.

**Monitor arm range is limited.** The arm extends about 19 inches, which is fine for most desks but restricts how far forward or back you can position the screen. If you have a deep desk (30+ inches), you might find the monitor can't pull close enough for comfortable viewing.

**The gas spring can be finicky.** Several reviewers report the arm losing tension over time, requiring periodic re-adjustment. One wrote, *"Works great for a few weeks, then I have to tighten the spring again. Not a dealbreaker, but annoying."*

---

## Who Should Buy This

- **Apartment dwellers or renters** who can't install wall-mounted standing desk solutions
- **Budget-conscious shoppers** wanting a sit-stand setup without buying a full standing desk
- **Users with lighter monitors** (under 16 pounds) who prioritize stability over adjustability
- **People with standard 24–28 inch desks** who need a compact, integrated solution

**Skip it if** you have an ultrawide monitor (the 19.8-pound limit won't accommodate most 34+ inch ultrawides), need a deep keyboard platform, or expect tool-free installation.

---

## FAQ

**Q: Will this work with a dual-monitor setup?**
A: No — this is a single-monitor arm. Mount-It! sells separate dual-arm versions if you need two screens.

**Q: Can I use the keyboard tray without the monitor arm?**
A: No, they're integrated into a single mounting system. You install both together or not at all.

**Q: What's the minimum desk thickness for the C-clamp?**
A: The clamp fits desks from 0.4 to 2.4 inches thick. Anything thicker requires the grommet mount (which needs a pre-drilled hole).

---

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want an affordable, solid-feeling sit-stand conversion and don't mind a fiddly setup afternoon. The integrated keyboard tray and gas-spring monitor arm deliver genuine ergonomic value at a price well below full standing desks.

**Skip it if** you need extensive monitor adjustability, use a large keyboard setup, or want something you can install in 10 minutes. For those cases, consider separate monitor arm and keyboard tray units.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Strong value for the price, held back by setup complexity and a cramped keyboard platform.
