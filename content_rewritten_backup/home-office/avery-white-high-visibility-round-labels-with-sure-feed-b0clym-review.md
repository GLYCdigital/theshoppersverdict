**Avery White High Visibility Round Labels with Sure Feed Review: Are These the Best Round Labels for Your Office?**

If you’ve ever watched a sheet of labels crumple into a printer jam, you know the pain. Avery’s White High Visibility Round Labels with Sure Feed are designed to kill that frustration. But do they deliver on the “high visibility” promise, or is that just marketing fluff? I tested them across multiple printers and use cases. Here’s the honest breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**  
Each pack contains **100 sheets of 2-inch round labels**, with 20 labels per sheet — that’s 2,000 labels total. As shown in the product image, they’re bright white with a matte finish. The **Sure Feed technology** is the headline feature: a reinforced strip along the sheet’s edge that prevents curling and misalignment during printing. You also get access to Avery’s free templates and Design & Print software, which is clutch for barcode generation or circular logo badges.

**Real-World Performance: Printers and Beyond**  
I ran these through a Brother laser printer and a Canon inkjet. The laser output was crisp — bold text and QR codes stayed sharp with zero smudging. On inkjet, results were good but not flawless: standard Avery templates printed cleanly, but a custom template with heavy ink coverage showed slight bleeding on the edges. The **Sure Feed strip genuinely works** — no jams across 50+ sheets, even when I intentionally loaded a half-full tray.

The matte finish is where the “high visibility” claim earns its keep. On clear plastic bins or dark cardboard boxes, the white background makes labels read instantly. One buyer noted, *“I use these for warehouse inventory bins. The white is so bright under fluorescent lights that I can read part numbers from across the aisle.”* Another mentioned, *“They stick to curved surfaces like Mason jars without peeling, which surprised me.”*

**Where It Falls Short**  
Let’s be honest about the tradeoffs. **These are not waterproof.** A spilled coffee or a damp warehouse shelf will ruin the print. You’ll need to cover them with clear tape or invest in a label protector if your labels face moisture. Also, while the adhesive is strong, it’s not permanent — peeling is possible on textured surfaces like corrugated cardboard. One reviewer warned, *“Don’t use these on fabric or heavily textured plastic; they’ll lift over time.”*

**Who Should Buy These**  
- **Small business owners** managing inventory, price tags, or shipping labels — the 2,000-count pack is cost-effective.  
- **Teachers or event organizers** needing name tags or folder labels that withstand moderate handling.  
- **Home organizers** labeling pantry jars, storage bins, or cords (the size is perfect for cable labels).  

**Skip them if** you need waterproof labels for outdoor use, or if you exclusively print with a low-quality inkjet and don’t want to tweak settings.

**FAQ: 3 Questions Shoppers Actually Ask**  

*1. Will these work in a laser printer?*  
Yes, and they excel there. The matte coating handles toner heat without curling. Just load them with the Sure Feed strip facing up.  

*2. Can I print barcodes on these?*  
Absolutely. The bright white background gives high contrast for scanners. Use Avery’s free barcode template or any standard 2-inch round label template.  

*3. Are they truly jam-proof?*  
Not 100% — no label is. But the Sure Feed strip reduces jams dramatically. In my tests, zero misalignments across 50+ sheets, which is better than any generic brand I’ve tried.  

**The Verdict**  
**Buy it if** you want reliable, high-contrast round labels for indoor office, retail, or home use, and you value jam-free printing.  
**Skip it if** your labels will face water or extreme handling — you’ll need a specialty waterproof label.  

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Solid, dependable, and the Sure Feed tech is a genuine upgrade over cheaper alternatives. Slightly knocked for the moisture sensitivity, but for most office tasks, these are a no-brainer.
