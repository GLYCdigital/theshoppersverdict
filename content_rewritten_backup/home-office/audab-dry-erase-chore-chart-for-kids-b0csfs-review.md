Most chore charts for kids fall into two categories: flimsy paper printables that last a week, or over-engineered magnetic boards that cost more than a family pizza night. The Audab Dry Erase Chore Chart sits somewhere in the sensible middle. At 4.2 stars across 210 ratings, it's clearly working for most families — but not without some honest caveats worth knowing before you hit buy.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes one magnetic dry-erase board, a set of colorful markers, an eraser, and a collection of magnetic reward pieces. As shown in the product image, the board features a clean layout with space for multiple children, a weekly task grid, and a reward section. It's designed to mount on your refrigerator, which is where the magnetic backing comes into play.

One thing I appreciated immediately: the board feels substantial. It's not a flimsy sheet of laminated paper pretending to be a chart. The magnetic layer is strong enough that the board stays put even when a six-year-old yanks on it to reach the top row.

## Real-World Performance

This is where the Audab chart earns its keep. The dry-erase surface is genuinely good — it wipes clean without leaving those annoying ghost marks that plague cheaper boards. One reviewer noted, "Been using this for three months with two boys and the surface still looks new. The eraser works well, and even dried-on marker comes off with a damp cloth."

The reward system is simple enough for kids to understand: they complete chores, move magnetic stars or tokens, and work toward a reward. The visual feedback is immediate, which is exactly what you want for behavioral reinforcement. Kids can see their progress without needing to read.

The layout accommodates up to three children, which covers most families. Each child gets their own column with enough space to write age-appropriate tasks.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 210 ratings, several themes emerge. The sturdiness tops the list — parents repeatedly mention the board surviving daily use without bending or warping. The wipe-clean surface is the second most-cited win. One parent wrote, "I was skeptical about another 'magnetic' chart, but this one actually sticks. It hasn't fallen off once in two months, and my kids are not gentle with it."

The customizable nature of the chart gets consistent praise too. Because it's dry-erase, you can change tasks weekly as kids grow. It's not locked into a rigid system.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the flaws. The included markers are mediocre at best. They dry out quickly and the colors are dull. You'll want to replace them with quality dry-erase markers almost immediately — factor that into your budget.

The magnetic reward pieces are smaller than the product images suggest. They're roughly the size of a quarter, which means they're easy for small hands to lose under the fridge or in couch cushions. If you have a toddler in the house, count on replacing some of these.

There's also no storage solution for the markers and eraser. The board mounts flat on the fridge, and the accessories just sort of live on the counter or in a drawer. A simple attached tray would have solved this, but Audab didn't include one.

## Who Should Buy This

This chart is ideal for parents with children aged 3 to 10 who want a visual, tactile chore system without a complicated app or subscription. It's especially good if you have multiple kids — the multi-column layout makes sibling comparison (and friendly competition) easy.

Skip it if you're looking for a decorative piece to match your kitchen aesthetic. It's functional first, and it looks like a chore chart. Also skip it if you want something that works with a specific reward app or digital system — this is proudly analog.

## FAQ

**Can this chart be used on surfaces other than the fridge?**
Technically yes, but the magnetic backing needs a ferromagnetic surface. You could mount it on a magnetic whiteboard or a metal door, but the included mounting hardware is minimal. It's really designed for fridge use.

**How many chores can I list per child?**
The layout gives you roughly six task slots per child per week. That's enough for daily chores like making the bed, clearing dishes, and homework without feeling overwhelming.

**Does the surface stain with colored markers?**
If you leave colored marker on for weeks, you might see faint staining. But with normal weekly use and cleaning, the surface holds up well. Red and blue markers are the most prone to ghosting if ignored.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a durable, no-nonsense chore chart that survives real family life. The strong magnetic backing, wipe-clean surface, and multi-child layout make it a solid value.

**Skip it if** you can't tolerate replacing the cheap markers or you need built-in storage for accessories. Those are minor annoyances, but they're real ones.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A genuinely useful product with two fixable flaws. Replace the markers and you've got a chore system that'll last years.
