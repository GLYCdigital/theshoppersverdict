## What You Actually Get

The GOTOBY ships as a single black toner cartridge, factory-sealed in a foil pouch with a protective plastic cap over the drum unit. Installation is identical to the OEM Brother TN433: pull the orange tab, shake the cartridge side-to-side five or six times to distribute the toner, and click it into your printer's toner slot. The chip is pre-installed, so there's no separate chip swapping or tape to peel off the underside — a step that trips up many compatibles.

This works with the TN433, TN431, and TN-433 designations, covering Brother's HL-L3210CW, HL-L3230CDW, HL-L3270CDW, and MFC-L3710CW, MFC-L3750CW, and MFC-L3770CDW models. Make sure your printer matches that list before ordering; the cartridge shape is specific to Brother's compact color laser line.

## FAQ

**Will this void my Brother printer warranty?**
No. Under the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act, using compatible consumables doesn't void a printer warranty unless the manufacturer can prove the cartridge caused the damage. Brother cannot legally refuse warranty service solely because you used a compatible cartridge.

**How do I know this fits my specific Brother model?**
Check your printer's model number against the compatibility list: HL-L3210CW, HL-L3230CDW, HL-L3270CDW, MFC-L3710CW, MFC-L3750CW, MFC-L3770CDW. If your model isn't listed, it won't fit.

**Does it come with a reset chip or do I need to buy one separately?**
The chip is pre-installed on the cartridge. No separate purchase or manual reset is needed — just install and print.
