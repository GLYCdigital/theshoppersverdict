## FAQ

**Q: Is this compatible with my motherboard?**
Check if your board has DDR4 DIMM slots (not DDR5 — they're physically different and won't fit). Most Intel 12th-14th gen and AMD AM4 boards support DDR4. Verify your CPU's memory controller supports 3200MHz, though most do.

**Q: Do I need to enable XMP?**
The stick runs at 2133MHz by default. Enabling XMP/DOCP in BIOS unlocks the rated 3200MHz speed. It's a 30-second change and worth doing — you're leaving performance on the table otherwise.

**Q: Can I mix this with my existing RAM?**
Technically yes, but it's not recommended. If your current stick is a different speed or timing, the system runs at the slower module's specs. For best stability, use identical sticks.
