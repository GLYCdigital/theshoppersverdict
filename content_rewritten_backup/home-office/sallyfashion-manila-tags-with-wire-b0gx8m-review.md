## Where It Falls Short

The wire ends are the main gripe. If you don't twist them tightly flush against the tag, the exposed tip can snag on clothing, thin plastic, or paper. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker — but worth knowing if you're tagging fabric items or stacking tagged products tightly.

Also, these are blank tags. No pre-printed numbers, no lines, no reinforcement rings. If you need machine-readable or sequentially numbered tags, look elsewhere. For handwritten labeling, these are ideal.
