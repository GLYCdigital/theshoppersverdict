If you're buying lanyards in bulk for a cruise, an office, or a conference, you've probably been burned by the flimsy ones that twist, snap, or irritate your neck by day two. The Ollain Badge Lanyards Neck Bulk set aims to fix that with a straightforward design: a 0.9-inch woven polyester strap, a metal clip, and a breakaway clasp. At 4.6 stars from 229 ratings, most buyers agree it hits the mark. Here's the real story.

## What you actually get

This is a bulk pack — you're getting multiple lanyards, typically 10 or 20 depending on the listing you choose. Each one is the same: a 36-inch strap, a sturdy metal lobster-claw clip for your badge, and a plastic breakaway clasp near the neck. The strap is woven, not printed ribbon, which means the edges are sealed and it won't fray after a few tugs. The clip is metal, not the cheap nickel-plated stuff that bends the first time you attach a thick cruise card.

## Performance and build quality

I've tested these against the typical office-issued lanyard, and the difference is immediate. The woven polyester has a slight texture that grips your shirt collar — it stays put without sliding around. The width (0.9 inches) is wide enough to distribute weight, so a heavy badge or a keycard in a thick holder doesn't pull the strap into a painful line on your neck.

The breakaway clasp is the standout feature. It's not a cosmetic addition; it releases with a firm tug. For anyone working around machinery, or parents using these for kids' IDs, that's a genuine safety feature. The metal clip is equally solid — I've clipped and unclipped it dozens of times, and it hasn't lost tension or bent.

## What buyers consistently praise

The reviews echo the same themes. One buyer wrote, *"Bought these for a cruise with my family — the clips held our key cards securely through pools, dinners, and excursions. No lost cards, no broken clasps."* Another noted, *"I use these for my retail staff. ## Where it falls short

No product is perfect, and these have two notable tradeoffs. First, the 36-inch strap is fixed — there's no slider to adjust length. For someone under 5'4" or with a shorter torso, the lanyard hangs closer to the waistline than the chest, which can be awkward. Second, the colors aren't UV-stable. If these sit in a sunny car or get worn outdoors daily, the brighter shades (red, blue) will fade noticeably within a few weeks. The black and navy hold up better.

## Who should buy this

- **Cruise travelers**: The metal clip and breakaway clasp are ideal for key cards that need to survive pools, buffets, and shore excursions.
- **Office managers**: The woven strap looks more professional than ribbon lanyards, and the bulk pricing makes them a no-brainer for new hires or visitor badges.
- **Event coordinators**: If you need 20 lanyards for a conference, these are reliable enough to last multiple events.

Skip them if you need adjustable-length straps or if your lanyards will live in direct sunlight for months at a time. For those cases, look for a model with a slider and UV-resistant dye.
