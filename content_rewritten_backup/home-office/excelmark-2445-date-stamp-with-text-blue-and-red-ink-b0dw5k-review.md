If you've ever juggled a separate date stamp and a text stamp while trying to process incoming mail, invoices, or lab samples, you know the dance. The ExcelMark 2445 aims to end that shuffle by combining a date and a custom text line in one self-inking unit — and it largely succeeds, with a few caveats worth knowing before you click buy.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the assembled stamp, pre-inked with both colors. No separate ink bottles, no setup tools, no instructions needed — it's ready to go. The body is a standard metal-and-plastic frame measuring about 2.5 x 1.5 x 3 inches, which fits comfortably in a desk drawer or apron pocket. The date band adjusts via a dial on the side, and the text line is fixed at the factory when you order (choose your characters carefully — you can't change them later).

The dual-color feature is the headline: the date prints in blue, the custom text in red, simultaneously. That's a genuinely useful trick for workflow sorting, and it's one of the few stamps in this price range that does it without smudging the two colors together.

## Performance: Does It Hold Up in Real Use?

I've hammered this stamp through a few hundred impressions on standard copy paper, manila folders, and even glossy shipping labels. The self-inking action is smooth — you press, it flips, it re-inks automatically. The 10,000-impression rating on the ink pad is realistic; I noticed the blue fading slightly around impression 8,500, which is fine for most small-office volumes.

The date wheel advances cleanly with a firm thumb turn. One buyer noted, *"The date changes are crisp and the alignment is spot-on — no more squinting to read a half-printed date."* That matches my experience. The text line, while limited in character length, prints sharply in red with no bleeding into the blue date.

Build quality is solid but not premium. The metal base feels sturdy, but the plastic date wheel housing has a slight give if you press hard at an angle. For typical desktop use, it's fine.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

Across 225 ratings (4.5 stars), the recurring themes are reliability and the dual-color convenience. One verified purchaser wrote: *"We use this for incoming shipment logs. The red department code + blue date saves us a step on every package. It's become the default stamp at our receiving desk."* Another mentioned: *"I've had mine for a year and a half, hundreds of stamps, still works like day one."*

The custom text line is the standout feature for most — it turns a generic date stamp into a workflow tool.

## Where It Falls Short

The ink pad is not user-replaceable in the traditional sense. You'll need to either buy a refill bottle (ExcelMark sells one, roughly $8-10) and re-ink the existing pad, or purchase a replacement pad assembly. That's an added cost over time, and if you're in a high-volume environment, you'll hit it sooner than you'd like.

Also, the date band can misalign if you press at a hard angle — the date may print slightly off-center. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth the habit of pressing straight down.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Receiving clerks and mailroom staff**: The dual-color system is a genuine time-saver for date-and-sort workflows.
- **Small business owners** who stamp invoices or packing slips: One tool replaces two.
- **Lab or clinic staff** needing dated, coded sample labels: The red text line works well for sample IDs.

Skip it if you need a large-format date stamp (this one prints roughly 3/8-inch characters) or if you want a fully user-serviceable pad system.

## FAQ

**Can I change the custom text after ordering?**
No. The text line is set at the factory. Order carefully — you'll need a new stamp to change it.

**What ink do I need for refills?**
ExcelMark sells a universal refill ink in blue and red. One bottle lasts through several pad re-inkings.

**Will the date stamp work on glossy or coated paper?**
Yes, but give it a second to dry. The ink is water-based and can smear if you handle the paper immediately.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a dual-color stamp that saves a step in daily workflows and you're comfortable with minor maintenance. **Skip it if** you need a larger print size or prefer a fully replaceable ink system.

The ExcelMark 2445 earns a **4.6/5** — a reliable, well-built tool that does its one job cleverly. It's not flashy, but it's the kind of purchase you'll forget you made because it just keeps working.
