## Real-world performance

The primary job here is mouse tracking, and the YSAGi delivers. The PU surface is smooth with just enough texture that optical and laser sensors track precisely — no jitter, no skipping. It's not a hard gaming surface, but for office work, spreadsheets, and design software, it's more than adequate. You genuinely don't need a separate mousepad on top of this.

As a writing surface, it provides a firm, stable base for documents. The 3mm thickness is enough to smooth out minor desk imperfections (wood grain, scratches) but won't mask a significantly warped or damaged desk. I tested it on a laminate surface and a solid wood desk; on both, it stayed put during aggressive mouse movements.

One note: the PU surface does show fingerprints and smudges, especially in darker colors. A quick wipe with a damp cloth handles it, but if you're obsessive about a pristine look, factor that in.

## Where it falls short

Let's be direct: this is not genuine leather. It's PU (polyurethane), which is a synthetic material. For the price, that's expected — but if you're expecting the patina and durability of real leather, you'll be disappointed. PU can crack or peel over years of heavy use, especially in hot, humid environments.

The thin profile is another consideration. At 3mm, it provides no meaningful wrist cushioning. If you press hard when writing or rest your palm heavily on the surface, you'll feel the desk underneath. This is a desk protector, not an ergonomic wrist rest.

Finally, the surface is vulnerable to heat and sharp objects. A hot mug placed directly on it can leave a permanent ring. Scissors or box cutters will scratch it. It's a working surface, not an indestructible one.
