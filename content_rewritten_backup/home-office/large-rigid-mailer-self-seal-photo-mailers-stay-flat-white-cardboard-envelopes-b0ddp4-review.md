No product is perfect, and these have a few tradeoffs. First, the rigidity means they take up storage space. If you’re in a small home office, a stack of 50 of these will eat shelf space quickly. They’re not something you can tuck behind a monitor.

Third, the white exterior shows scuffs and dirt more readily than kraft brown. If your shipping process involves rough handling or dusty warehouses, these may look worn by the time they arrive. It’s cosmetic, not structural, but worth noting.
