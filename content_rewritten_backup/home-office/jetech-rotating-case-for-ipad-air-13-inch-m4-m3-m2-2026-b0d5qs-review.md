The front cover folds back completely, and the whole assembly rotates 360 degrees, letting you flip between portrait and landscape without taking the iPad out.

The material is a matte TPU back with a soft-touch finish. It's not slippery, which matters when you're holding a 13-inch slab one-handed. The front flap has a microfiber lining that does its job protecting the screen from scratches.

Here's where this case earns its keep. The rotating mechanism is smooth — a firm detent stops at each 90-degree increment, so it doesn't flop around when you're typing. The kickstand angles range from roughly 15 to 150 degrees, which covers everything from desk typing to couch viewing.

The 13-inch form factor with this case on it becomes a legitimate laptop replacement for note-taking and document review. ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. This case is thick. The rotating mechanism adds roughly 3-4mm to the back, pushing the total weight closer to 1.5 pounds with the iPad inside. If you're used to a slim folio, this will feel chunky in a bag.

The kickstand also has a quirk: on soft surfaces like a bed or couch cushion, the narrow foot can sink in and make the iPad wobble. It's rock-solid on a desk, but don't plan on balanced lap use without a hard surface underneath.

There's no keyboard integration, and no magnetic attachment for the Magic Keyboard. If you were hoping to switch between this case and Apple's keyboard accessory, you'll be removing the iPad every time — the back shell is not keyboard-compatible.
