## Small dots, big organizational payoff

Most dot stickers are an afterthought — you grab whatever's cheap, deal with flimsy paper that curls at the edges, and replace them when they fall off three days later. The TownStix 3/4" Round Dot Stickers are not that product. At 4.7 stars across 235 Amazon ratings, these have quietly become the default recommendation in organization and small-biz circles. Here's why they earn it.

## What's actually in the pack

You're getting a standard 3/4-inch round dot — roughly the diameter of a US quarter. The pack ships as full sheets of perforated labels, and the backing paper is stiffer than what you'll find on bargain-bin brands. That stiffness matters more than you'd think: it keeps the sheet flat in a drawer and prevents the dots from curling at the edges before you even peel them. As one verified buyer put it, "These are the only dot stickers I've used where the entire sheet comes out of the package flat, not warped from shipping."

## Real-world performance

The adhesive is the headline feature. TownStix uses a permanent acrylic adhesive that bonds aggressively to most surfaces — glass, plastic, cardboard, even slightly textured poly mailers. In testing, dots stayed put on curved mason jar lids and corrugated shipping boxes through a week of handling. They're not repositionable, so plan your placement carefully; once a dot touches down, it's committed.

The color saturation is another standout. The ink is dense and uniform, with no patchy coverage or see-through corners. The classic rainbow assortment gives you bright primaries plus pastel shades, and the neon pack delivers colors that genuinely pop under fluorescent office lighting. The 3/4" size hits a sweet spot too — big enough to read a number or letter written in fine-point Sharpie, small enough to use for color-coding inventory bins without overwhelming the label area.

## What buyers consistently praise

The recurring theme in customer feedback is reliability. "I use these for garage sale pricing and they stick to everything — paper bags, plastic containers, even the occasional damp bottle," one reviewer noted. Another mentioned using them for months on shipping supplies: "I've gone through three packs for my Etsy shop. They hold up in transit and don't leave residue when removed."

The clean perforation gets frequent shoutouts too. You can peel a single dot without tearing the backing or accidentally pulling up the adjacent sticker — a small thing that becomes a huge quality-of-life win when you're labeling 200 items in one sitting.

## Where it falls short

The single-size format is the biggest limitation. If you need 1-inch dots for pricing or 1/2-inch dots for planner work, you're buying a separate pack. Some users also noted that the fluorescent yellow and green shades lose their intensity after a few weeks in direct sunlight — fine for indoor use, less ideal for outdoor garage sale signage.

## Who should buy these

- **Small business owners** shipping products or managing inventory — the adhesive holds through transit
- **Teachers and classroom organizers** color-coding supplies or creating behavior charts
- **Event planners** using dots for RSVP tracking or seating charts
- **Anyone who's been burned by cheap stickers** that peel off within a day

Skip them if you need repositionable dots or a multi-size variety pack.

## FAQ

**Will these stick to fabric or clothing?**
They'll hold temporarily but aren't designed for fabric. Use them on tags or packaging instead.

**Do they leave residue when removed?**
No. The adhesive releases cleanly from glass and plastic, though it's permanent enough to stay put until you want it off.

**Can I write on them with a standard pen?**
Ballpoint and gel pens work fine. Sharpies dry quickly without smearing.
