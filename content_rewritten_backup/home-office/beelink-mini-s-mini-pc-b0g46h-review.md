Let's be direct: most mini PCs under $300 are a gamble. The Beelink MINI S is one of the few that actually earns its place on your desk. With a 4.2-star average across 210 Amazon ratings, it's not perfect — but for the price, this little box punches well above its weight for office work, streaming, and everyday computing.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the MINI S unit itself (measuring roughly 4.9 x 4.4 x 1.7 inches — about the size of a thick paperback), a 12V/3A power adapter, an HDMI cable, a VESA mount bracket, and a small user manual. The build is all-black plastic with a brushed metal top plate. It looks more premium than the price suggests. Ports are generous: two HDMI 2.0 ports, one DisplayPort, one USB-C (data-only, not power delivery), three USB 3.2 Gen 2 Type-A ports, one USB 2.0, and a 3.5mm headphone jack. Front-mounted USB ports are a small but appreciated touch for flash drives.

## Performance: Real-World Numbers

The AMD Ryzen 7 5825U with 16GB DDR4 RAM and a 512GB NVMe SSD handles everyday tasks without breaking a sweat. I ran 20+ browser tabs, a spreadsheet with heavy formulas, and a 1080p video stream simultaneously — no stuttering, no fan ramp-up. Boot times hover around 10 seconds from cold start. The integrated Radeon graphics are fine for 4K video playback (the unit supports triple 4K displays), but don't kid yourself about gaming — you're looking at esports titles at low settings, not AAA games.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

Scrolling through the reviews, three themes dominate. First, silence: "I honestly forget it's running — the fan is whisper-quiet," one verified buyer notes. Second, value: "Got this for my home office setup and it replaced a laptop that was dying. Way faster than I expected for the price." Third, the VESA mount — multiple buyers mention mounting it behind their monitor for a genuinely clean desk setup. That's a real workspace win.

## Where It Falls Short

The 5825U is a Zen 3 chip from 2021. It's efficient but not future-proof. If you're planning to run demanding creative software or heavy virtualization, look elsewhere. The Wi-Fi 6 antenna is external and finicky — one reviewer said, "Had to experiment with placement for two days before the signal stabilized." Also, no USB 4.0 means external NVMe enclosures will top out at 10Gbps, not the 40Gbps you'd get on newer systems. And while 16GB is fine now, it's soldered — no upgrade path later.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're setting up a home office, need a second workstation, want a quiet media center, or need to replace an aging desktop without spending big. It's also perfect for someone who wants a clean, minimal desk — the VESA mount is brilliantly simple.

**Skip it if:** You're a gamer, video editor, or someone who needs future upgradeability. Also skip if you rely heavily on Wi-Fi in a congested apartment — you might want to run ethernet to be safe.

## FAQ

**Q: Can this run dual monitors at different resolutions?**
A: Yes. The two HDMI ports support 4K@60Hz, and the DisplayPort handles 4K@60Hz as well. Mixed resolutions work fine — I ran a 1440p and 1080p setup without issue.

**Q: Does it come with Windows pre-installed?**
A: Yes, Windows 11 Pro is pre-installed and activated. There's also a Linux option listed on the product page if you prefer that route.

**Q: Is the RAM upgradeable?**
A: No. The 16GB is soldered to the board. Choose your configuration carefully at purchase — there's no aftermarket upgrade path.

## The Verdict

The Beelink MINI S is a smart buy for anyone needing a compact, quiet, capable office machine without paying premium prices. It's not a powerhouse, and it's not future-proof — but it does exactly what 90% of desktop users need, and it does it well.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Buy it if you want a dependable, silent workhorse for everyday computing. Skip it if you need raw performance or upgradeability. For the price, this is one of the better values in the mini PC space right now.
