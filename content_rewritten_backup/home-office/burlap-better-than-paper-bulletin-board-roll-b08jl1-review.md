### FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Can I write on it with dry-erase markers?**
A: No. The burlap texture is too porous for clean dry-erase use. If you need a writing surface, pair this with a separate whiteboard or use sticky notes.

**Q: Will push pins leave visible holes?**
A: The material is woven, not solid paper. Pins will leave a small hole, but the fibers tend to close up around the pin. When you remove the pin, the hole is largely invisible—far better than paper or cork, which show every puncture.

**Q: How does it handle humidity?**
A: Very well. One reviewer in the southeastern U.S. noted it didn't warp or sag in a humid classroom over the summer. The synthetic backing resists moisture better than natural burlap or paper.
