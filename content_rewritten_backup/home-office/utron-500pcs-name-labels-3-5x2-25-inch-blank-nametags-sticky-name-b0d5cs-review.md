Let's be honest: nobody gets excited about blank name labels. But if you've ever run a conference, taught a class of 30, or managed an office move, you know the pain of running out mid-event and paying $12 for a 20-pack at the nearest office supply store. This Utron 500-count box solves that math permanently.

## What You Actually Get

A flat box containing 500 individual 3.5 x 2.25 inch labels. That's the classic badge size — roughly the dimensions of a standard business card, but slightly wider. Each label has a peel-off backing, and the stack ships as full sheets that you cut apart yourself. The surface is a matte white finish designed to work with ballpoint pens, markers, and most laser printers.

At 500 units, you're looking at a buy-once, forget-for-years proposition for most personal use. For events, it's roughly 10 full conference days worth of badges.

## Real-World Performance

The adhesive is the standout feature here. Multiple verified buyers noted the labels stayed put through entire multi-day events. One reviewer wrote: *"Used these for a 3-day trade show. Wore the same badge all day, through meals and meetings, and it never curled at the edges."* That aligns with my own assessment — the glue is aggressive enough for fabric lanyards and polyester shirts without leaving residue when removed.

Print quality holds up too. The matte coating absorbs ink rather than letting it pool. Ballpoint dries instantly, and laser printer output comes out sharp without smearing. If you're using Sharpies or other alcohol-based markers, give them 10 seconds to dry before handling.

**The tradeoff:** these are not premium cardstock. The paper is noticeably thin — comparable to standard printer paper, not the heavy 110lb stock you'd get from specialty badge suppliers. For a one-day event, fine. For badges that need to survive multiple wears or get stuffed into pockets, they'll show wear.

## Where It Falls Short

The lack of perforation is the biggest practical annoyance. Each sheet contains multiple labels, and you'll need scissors or a guillotine cutter to separate them. If you're prepping 200 badges the night before an event, that's a genuine time sink.

The permanent adhesive also means zero margin for error. Stick it slightly crooked, and that label is wasted. Repositionable adhesive would have been nice, but at this price point, you can afford to toss a few.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Event planners** running conferences, workshops, or networking mixers — this is your bulk supply
- **Teachers** who cycle through name tags for new classes or substitute days
- **Office managers** who host frequent visitors or run training sessions
- **Small businesses** doing weekly inventory or shipping labels with handwritten addresses

Skip it if you need premium presentation badges for a high-end gala, or if you're printing full-color designs that demand heavier paper stock.

## FAQ

**Can these go through a laser printer?**
Yes, but cut the sheets to size first and feed them manually. The matte coating handles toner well, but the thin paper can jam if you try to print on full uncut sheets through an auto-feeder.

**Do they work on fabric lanyards?**
Well, actually — the adhesive grabs fabric securely and survives the day. Removal might pull a few fibers on delicate knits, so stick to lanyards or smooth shirt surfaces for reuse.

**What's the actual per-label cost?**
Depends on current pricing, but at typical rates for 500-packs, you're looking at pennies per label — roughly 80% cheaper than retail 20-packs.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You need reliable, cheap name labels in bulk and don't mind cutting them yourself. The adhesive quality alone justifies the purchase.

**Skip it if:** You need premium stock, perforated sheets, or repositionable adhesive for exact placement.
