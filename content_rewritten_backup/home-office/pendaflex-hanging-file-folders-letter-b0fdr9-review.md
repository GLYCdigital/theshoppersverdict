## Pendaflex Hanging File Folders, Letter: The Office Workhorse That Just Works

Let's be honest—nobody gets excited about file folders. But when your desk drawer looks like a paper avalanche hit it, the humble hanging folder becomes the difference between chaos and control. Pendaflex has been making these for over a century, and the 4.7-star rating across 235 reviews suggests they've figured something out. I've tested these against cheaper store-brand options, and the differences matter more than you'd think.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains 25 letter-size hanging folders with reinforced tabs—five each of red, yellow, green, blue, and orange. Each folder measures 11-3/4" x 9-1/4" with a 1/8-inch cut tab position, which means the tabs sit in a staggered pattern when you hang them. The rods are 12 inches wide, fitting standard letter-size drawer rails. You also get 25 blank insertable tab inserts, so you can label them with your own system or use the included A-Z inserts.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The 11-point stock is noticeably sturdier than the flimsy folders you find at dollar stores. The paper surface has a slight texture that grips documents—they don't slide around when you're rifling through a packed drawer. The rods are welded at both ends, not just crimped, which is the difference between a folder that lasts five years and one that pops apart after six months of heavy use.

I've loaded these with 100+ page reports, and the folder holds its shape without bowing. The color-coding system is genuinely useful if you run multiple projects—red for urgent, green for completed, that sort of thing. The tabs are scored cleanly and tear out without leaving jagged edges.

**What Buyers Keep Saying**

The reviews consistently mention durability and value. One reviewer noted, "These are the only folders I buy for my medical office. They hold up to constant use and the colors make filing by patient type easy." Another mentioned, "I've tried cheaper brands and they sag after a few months. **Where It Falls Short**

The tab inserts are thin and can curl if you write on them with standard ballpoint pens—use a fine-tip marker instead. The 25-count box is smaller than some competitors' 50-count options, so if you're outfitting a full office, you'll need multiple boxes. And while the colors are bright, they're not lightfast—prolonged direct sunlight will fade them over a year or two. Minor issues, but worth knowing.
