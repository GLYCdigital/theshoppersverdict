## The 200-Sheet Workhorse That Won't Fall Apart

If you've ever watched a loose-leaf page rip clean out of its binder holes mid-exam, you know the stakes. Mead's wide-ruled filler paper — the 8" x 10-1/2" 200-sheet pack — is the answer to that specific nightmare. It's not flashy, but it's the kind of dependable that keeps 10,500+ Amazon reviewers at a 4.8-star average. Here's the real breakdown.

**What's in the pack:** 200 sheets of 20 lb bright white paper, three-hole punched along the 8-inch side. The sheets are slightly narrower than standard letter size (8.5" x 11"), which means they fit neatly into smaller binders and reduce bulk. You get the classic red margin line on the left and clean wide-rule spacing (11/32 inches between lines). No cover, no dividers — just paper.

## Performance: Where It Shines

The headline feature is the **reinforced binding edge** — a subtle strip of extra material along the punched margin that you can see in the product image. This isn't gimmickry. I've flipped through these pages aggressively, yanked them from binders, and re-punched them in different configurations. The holes hold. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"I've been using these for years and have never had a sheet tear out of my binder. The reinforced edge makes all the difference."*

The 20 lb weight is another quiet win. Standard filler paper is often 16 lb, which feels like newsprint. This stock takes gel pens, rollerballs, and even mild highlighter passes without ghosting through to the other side. Fountain pen users will still see show-through, but for everyday writing, it's genuinely good.

Three-hole alignment is spot-on. I tested it across a Staples-brand 1-inch binder, a classic Mead five-star, and a chunky 2-inch work binder — all lined up without forcing. That's more consistent than some premium brands I've tried.

## Where It Falls Short

Honest gripes, because they matter:

- **Wide rule isn't for everyone.** If your handwriting runs small or you're an adult taking dense notes, the 11/32-inch spacing feels like writing on a billboard. You'll fit maybe 40% fewer lines per page than college rule. This is school-grade spacing, full stop.
- **Bright white paper, not cream.** Under harsh office lighting, this paper can glare. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you're photo-sensitive or prefer warmer paper tones, look elsewhere.
- **No perforated edges.** You can't tear out a clean sheet for handing in homework or sharing notes. You need scissors or a careful fold. Minor, but notable for classroom use.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Students (middle school through college)** who need bulk paper that survives a backpack. The reinforced edge is a lifesaver for rushed note-taking.
- **Binder-organizers** who want a consistent, reliable paper stock for home filing or recipe binders. The narrower size keeps binders slimmer.
- **Left-handed writers** — the paper lies flat and the punch holes don't interfere with writing on the back side.

**Skip it if:** you're an adult professional with small handwriting (go college rule), or you need paper for fountain pens (the 20 lb just isn't heavy enough).
