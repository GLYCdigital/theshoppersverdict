The braided exterior isn't just for looks. It resists kinking and tangling significantly better than the stock rubber cable JBL ships. Second, this is micro-USB, not USB-C. In 2026, that's a legacy connection. If you're buying this for a newer JBL model that uses USB-C, it won't fit. Double-check your speaker's port before purchasing.
