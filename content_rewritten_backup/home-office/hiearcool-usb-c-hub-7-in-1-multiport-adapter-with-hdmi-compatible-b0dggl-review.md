**FAQ**

**Q: Does this hub work with USB-C iPhones or Android phones?**  
A: Yes, it works with USB-C phones that support DisplayPort Alt Mode, like recent Samsung Galaxy and iPhone 15 Pro models. You can output video to a TV or monitor, but the hub will draw power from the phone unless you also connect a charger to the PD port.

**Q: Will it charge my 16-inch MacBook Pro at full speed?**  
A: The PD passthrough supports up to 100W input, but the hub itself uses about 5W. So your MacBook Pro will get roughly 95W of the 100W your charger provides. That's enough for full-speed charging on almost all laptops.

**Q: Can I use all seven ports at the same time?**  
A: Yes, all ports work simultaneously. However, if you're using the PD port for charging, the HDMI output drops to 4K@30Hz as mentioned. Also, connecting multiple high-power devices (like two portable SSDs) may cause the hub to distribute power less efficiently.
