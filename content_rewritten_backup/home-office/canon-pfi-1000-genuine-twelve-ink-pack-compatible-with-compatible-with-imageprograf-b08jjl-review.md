# Canon PFI-1000 Genuine Twelve Ink Pack Review: Premium Ink for Serious Print Work

Canon's imagePROGRAF PRO-1000 is a serious investment—roughly $1,000 for a professional-grade 17-inch pigment printer. But here's the catch that many photographers discover too late: the printer is only half the cost equation. The PFI-1000 twelve-ink set is the ongoing commitment that keeps that investment producing gallery-quality prints. With a 4.6/5 rating across 229 Amazon reviews, this genuine Canon ink set has earned its reputation—but it's worth understanding exactly what you're paying for.

## What's in the Box

This is a complete set of all twelve LUCIA PRO pigment ink cartridges designed specifically for the PRO-1000: the standard CMYK set, plus photo cyan, photo magenta, gray, photo gray, matte black, and the two specialty inks—Chromia Optimizer and CO (clear coating). Each cartridge holds 80ml, which is substantial for a desktop printer. You get one full set in the box, and when you install them, the printer's internal ink tank system also gets its initial fill.

## Performance and Real-World Results

The LUCIA PRO pigment ink system is Canon's professional-grade formulation, and the difference shows in the prints. Colors are exceptionally stable with a claimed archival life of over 100 years in dark storage and 60+ years under standard glass framing. The Chromia Optimizer is the unsung hero here—it's a colorless gloss enhancer that eliminates the bronzing and metamerism issues that plague less sophisticated pigment systems, especially on glossy papers.

Users consistently note that this genuine ink delivers accurate, repeatable color rendition across different paper types. As one verified buyer put it, "The quality of the prints from genuine Canon ink is unmatched. I tried third-party inks once to save money and immediately regretted it—the color shifts and clogging weren't worth the savings."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

- **Clog-free reliability**: The PRO-1000's ink system keeps the print head primed, and genuine ink maintains that integrity. Multiple reviewers report leaving the printer idle for weeks and returning to perfect prints.
- **True black depth**: The combination of matte and photo blacks, plus the gray inks, delivers tonal transitions that reviewers describe as "silky smooth" and "free of banding."
- **Genuine chip recognition**: No firmware update headaches or "non-genuine ink" warnings that plague third-party cartridges.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest: the price is the elephant in the room. This set runs roughly $300–$350, which breaks down to about $25–$30 per cartridge. That's steep, especially compared to compatible alternatives that can cost half as much.

There's also the **waste factor**. The PRO-1000 uses ink for print head cleaning and maintenance, and the printer's internal tank needs a full charge upon setup. That means your first set gets partially consumed before your first print even comes out. Some users noted that the printer consumes a surprising amount of ink during the initial setup cycle—something to budget for.

Finally, if you print infrequently (less than a few prints per month), the ink will still be used for maintenance cycles. That's the tradeoff of a pigment printer with a permanent print head.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Professional photographers** who need color-accurate, archival prints for clients
- **Fine art printers** selling limited editions where longevity claims matter
- **Serious hobbyists** who've already invested in the PRO-1000 and want predictable, clog-free performance
- **Not for**: Budget-conscious users who print casually or only occasionally—you'll spend more on maintenance than on actual output

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I use individual cartridges from this set, or must I buy the full twelve-pack?**
A: The printer requires all twelve cartridges installed simultaneously, but you can replace them individually as they run low. This pack gives you a full set upfront, which is ideal for initial setup or a complete refresh.

**Q: How many prints will this set produce?**
A: Realistically, expect 200–300 13x19-inch prints depending on coverage. Photo-heavy, full-bleed images with high ink coverage will yield fewer prints; documents with lighter coverage will go further.

**Q: Is third-party ink really that much worse?**
A: For casual prints, the difference may be subtle. But you risk color shifts, reduced archival life, and potential head clogs. The PRO-1000's print head is expensive to replace—genuine ink is essentially insurance for a $1,000 printer.

**Buy it if:** You're a working photographer or serious printmaker who needs predictable, archival-quality output and values the reliability that genuine ink provides.

**Skip it if:** You print occasionally, don't require archival longevity, and are comfortable troubleshooting potential issues with third-party alternatives to save roughly 40–50%.
