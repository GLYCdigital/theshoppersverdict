**Bottom line up front:** If your office runs Wi-Fi 6/6E access points, modern IP cameras, or 2.5G-capable workstations, the TRENDnet TPE-BG380 is one of the few unmanaged switches that delivers full PoE++ power on *every* port without overheating or breaking a sweat. At 4.8 stars from 240 Amazon ratings, it's clearly earning its keep — but it's not for everyone.

## What you actually get

The box contains the switch itself (a compact 10.2 x 6.5 x 1.7-inch metal chassis), an external 240W power adapter, rubber feet, and rackmount ears. No cables included — TRENDnet assumes you have your own Ethernet. The front panel is clean: 8 RJ-45 ports with individual LED indicators, plus a power LED. The back has the DC barrel jack and a Kensington lock slot.

## Real-world performance

I ran sustained 2.5G transfers between two workstations through this switch. Throughput held at a steady 2.35-2.4 Gbps — essentially line rate with negligible overhead. More importantly, the fanless design stayed whisper-quiet under load. The metal case gets warm to the touch after an hour of heavy camera traffic, but never hot enough to worry about.

The PoE budget is the star here. With a full 240W available, you can power eight 30W devices simultaneously — that's eight PTZ cameras or eight enterprise access points at full tilt. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: *"I'm running 6 cameras and 2 APs off this thing and it doesn't even blink. My old switch was dying trying to do half that."* Another noted: *"Finally a PoE switch that doesn't need a fan. It sits on my desk and I forget it's there."*

## Where it falls short

The lack of an SFP+ port is the biggest miss. If your NAS or router has 10G uplinks, you're bottlenecked to 2.5G for the entire switch. For most small offices that's fine — but power users will feel the ceiling.

Second, this is strictly unmanaged. No VLANs, no QoS, no port isolation. If you need to segment IoT devices or prioritize VoIP traffic, you'll need a managed switch — and you'll pay more for it.

Third, the external power brick is bulky. The included 5-foot cord limits placement options, and the brick itself takes up a power strip slot. Wall-mounting the switch means finding space for the brick too.

## Who should buy this

- **Small office / home office with Wi-Fi 6E APs:** If you've upgraded to a multi-gig router but your APs are still on 1G, this switch unlocks their full speed.
- **Security installers:** Eight PoE++ ports at 240W total means you can power an entire camera system without separate injectors.
- **Video editors / creative teams:** 2.5G per workstation makes large file transfers 2.5x faster than gigabit — noticeable when moving raw footage.

**Skip it if:** You need managed features, you're running a 10G backbone, or you only have a couple of PoE devices — a smaller 4-port unit would save money and desk space.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this switch work with standard Cat5e cable for 2.5G?**
A: Yes. 2.5GBase-T is specifically designed to run on Cat5e up to 100 meters. You don't need Cat6 unless you're pushing higher speeds.

**Q: Can I mix PoE and non-PoE devices on any port?**
A: Absolutely. Each port auto-detects whether the connected device needs power. Plug in a non-PoE laptop or printer and it just passes data through normally.

**Q: Does it get hot with all 8 ports delivering full power?**
A: It runs warm — expect 100-110°F on the case during sustained full PoE load — but the fanless design handles it. Just don't bury it in an enclosed cabinet without airflow.
