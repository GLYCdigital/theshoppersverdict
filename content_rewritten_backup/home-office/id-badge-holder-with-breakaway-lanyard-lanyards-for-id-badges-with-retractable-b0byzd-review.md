The package includes the lanyard, the badge holder, and a metal clip on the back of the holder. The lanyard itself is a flat, woven polyester strap—not the cheap, tubular kind that rolls up on itself. The breakaway buckle sits at the back of the neck, which is the correct placement for safety; it releases under sudden tension rather than snapping during normal movement. The badge holder is a vertical, clear PVC pouch that fits standard 3.375 x 2.125 inch ID cards snugly. There’s also a small metal grommet at the top of the holder where the clip attaches, which is a step up from the molded plastic loops that crack within a month.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

Let’s talk about the clip first, because that’s where most of these products fail. This one is a heavy-duty, swivel-style metal clip with a spring-loaded gate. It’s not the tiny, flimsy lobster claw you see on budget lanyards. The swivel means the badge doesn’t torque against the lanyard when you sit down or lean forward—a small detail that prevents the constant “badge facing backwards” annoyance. The PVC pouch is thick enough that it doesn’t crinkle or yellow immediately, and the edges are heat-sealed, not glued, so they won’t peel apart.

The breakaway mechanism works exactly as intended. I tested it by snagging the lanyard on a drawer handle and walking away; the buckle popped cleanly, and the badge dropped safely. It reattaches with a firm click. In daily use, it never released accidentally—no false triggers when I bent over or reached across a desk.

**FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask**

**Q: Will this fit a standard HID proximity card?**
Yes. The internal dimensions of the pouch are roughly 3.5 x 2.25 inches, which accommodates standard 3.375 x 2.125 inch cards with a little room to spare. Thick cards with embedded chips fit fine.

**Q: How hard does the breakaway need to be pulled to release?**
It takes a firm, sudden jerk—not a gentle tug. It won’t pop when you lean forward, but it will release cleanly if the lanyard gets caught and you keep moving. That’s the safety sweet spot.

**Q: Can you attach a retractable reel to this lanyard?**
The lanyard has a fixed metal clip, so you’d have to swap the clip for a reel attachment. It’s possible, but this product is designed to be a complete, standalone system.
