The MeLE Quieter 4C isn't trying to win a spec-sheet war. It's aiming for a different victory: disappearing into your workspace while quietly handling the daily grind. At 4.3 stars across 215 ratings, it's clearly hitting that mark for a lot of buyers. But "quiet" doesn't mean "powerful," and you need to know exactly where this little box fits before you pull the trigger.

## What's Actually in the Box

MeLE keeps the accessory list lean: the mini PC itself, a 12V/3A power adapter, an HDMI cable, a VESA mount bracket with screws, and a quick-start guide. No keyboard, no mouse, no surprises. The unit measures roughly 5.1 x 3.1 x 1.2 inches — about the size of a paperback novel — and weighs just over a pound. The aluminum chassis feels solid, not cheap, and the matte finish resists fingerprints.

## Real-World Performance

Here's the honest truth: the Intel N150 (4 cores, 4 threads, up to 3.6 GHz) is a 2024-era entry-level chip. It'll handle your browser with 15 tabs, Microsoft Office, email, and video calls simultaneously without breaking a sweat. It'll even play 4K YouTube or Netflix without stuttering. But push it into photo editing in Lightroom, heavy spreadsheet macros, or anything with 20+ browser tabs plus a video conference plus a streaming app, and you'll feel the lag.

The 16GB of DDR4 RAM and 512GB NVMe SSD (both standard on this model) help compensate. Boot times are snappy, apps open quickly, and the storage is genuinely fast. It's a machine designed for flow, not for heavy lifting.

The real hero here is the fanless design. The entire aluminum chassis acts as a heatsink, which means zero moving parts and zero noise. In a quiet office, you genuinely cannot tell this thing is running. It also draws under 15 watts at idle, so it'll barely register on your power bill.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The silence and the dual Ethernet ports are the two features that keep showing up in customer feedback. One buyer put it simply: "Perfect for a home office — completely silent, runs Windows 11 Pro without bloatware, and the 2.5G ports are a bonus I didn't expect at this price." Another noted, "I use mine as a Plex server and it handles 4K transcoding without overheating — the passive cooling is genuinely impressive."

Multiple reviewers also highlight the dual 2.5G LAN ports as a standout feature. That's rare at this price point and makes this box genuinely useful as a small-business router, firewall appliance, or NAS front-end. If you're into homelab tinkering, this is a bargain.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the limitations. The N150 has a single memory channel, which caps integrated graphics performance — you'll get occasional frame drops even in casual games like Minecraft. The single HDMI 2.0 port means you'll need a USB-C to HDMI adapter for a second monitor, which costs extra. And while the 512GB SSD is adequate, heavy media hoarders will want external storage.

One Amazon reviewer flagged a real issue: "The Wi-Fi antenna placement is a bit weak — I get about 60% signal strength compared to my laptop in the same room. Wired Ethernet solved it, but worth knowing." If you're planning to rely on Wi-Fi, budget for a USB Wi-Fi dongle or use wired connections.

Also note: this unit runs warm to the touch under sustained load. That's normal for fanless designs — the chassis is doing its job — but it might surprise you if you're used to plastic-bodied mini PCs.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if you're:** a remote worker with a dedicated desk, a small business owner needing a reliable office PC, a homelab enthusiast wanting a silent router/NAS, or anyone who hates fan noise.

**Skip it if you're:** a gamer, a video editor, someone running heavy virtual machines, or anyone who needs more than one native HDMI output without adapter hassle.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I upgrade the RAM or SSD?**
A: The 16GB is soldered, so no RAM upgrades. The NVMe SSD is replaceable — it's a standard M.2 2280 slot. You'll need to remove four bottom screws to access it.

**Q: Does it support dual monitors?**
A: Yes, but you'll need a USB-C to HDMI or DisplayPort adapter — there's only one HDMI 2.0 port built-in. The USB-C port does support video output.

**Q: Will this run Windows 11 updates smoothly?**
A: Yes. N150 fully supports Windows 11, and MeLE ships with the latest build pre-activated. Regular updates install without issues based on customer reports.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The MeLE Quieter 4C nails its niche: a silent, energy-efficient, dual-LAN office workhorse that's genuinely useful beyond basic desktop duties. It won't win benchmarks, but it will win over anyone who values a dead-quiet workspace.

**Buy it if** you need a dependable, fanless Windows PC for office work, light browsing, or homelab duties and want dual 2.5G networking without spending router-appliance money.

**Skip it if** your workflow demands serious processing power or you need native dual-HDMI output without adapter workarounds. For those needs, look toward N100/N150 units with more ports or step up to an AMD-based mini PC.
