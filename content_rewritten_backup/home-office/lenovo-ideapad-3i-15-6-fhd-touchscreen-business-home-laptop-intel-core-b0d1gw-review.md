**The 15.6-inch touchscreen laptop that actually earns its place on your desk**

Most budget-to-midrange laptops make you choose between a touchscreen and decent performance. The Lenovo Ideapad 3i 15.6" FHD tries to give you both — and mostly succeeds. After spending a week with this machine running everything from video calls to spreadsheet marathons, here's the honest picture.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the laptop itself, a 65W USB-C charger, and minimal paperwork. No stylus, no sleeve — keep that in mind if you plan to use the touchscreen for note-taking. The laptop measures 14.1 x 9.3 x 0.7 inches and weighs 3.9 pounds, which is reasonable for a 15.6-inch machine. It's not an ultraportable, but it slides into most backpack sleeves without drama.

**Performance that punches above its price**

The star here is the 12th-gen Intel Core i5-1235U processor. It's a 10-core chip (2 performance + 8 efficiency cores) with 12 threads, and it shows in daily use. I ran 25 Chrome tabs, a YouTube video, and a 50,000-row Excel sheet simultaneously — the fan spun up, but there was zero lag. The 8GB of DDR4 RAM is the bottleneck, not the CPU. If you're a tab hoarder like me, consider the 16GB configuration.

The 15.6-inch FHD (1920x1080) IPS display hits 250 nits of brightness. That's decent for indoor use and surprisingly workable near a window, though direct sunlight washes it out. The 10-point touchscreen responds accurately — I found myself reaching for it more than expected, especially for scrolling and zooming in documents. Colors are accurate enough for photo editing, though creatives will want something with wider sRGB coverage.

**Where it shines**

The keyboard is the unsung hero. It's a full-size layout with a dedicated numeric keypad — a genuine productivity boost if you work with numbers. Key travel is 1.5mm, which is comfortable for long typing sessions, and the keys have a satisfying tactile bump. The trackpad is equally solid, with smooth glass-like tracking and no dead zones.

Connectivity is generous for this class: 1x USB-C 3.2 Gen 1, 2x USB-A 3.2 Gen 1, HDMI 1.4b, an SD card reader, and a 3.5mm headphone jack. The USB-C port handles charging and data, but don't expect Thunderbolt — that's reserved for pricier models.

**Real buyer sentiment**

The 4.5-star average across 225 ratings tracks with my experience. One verified buyer noted, *"The touchscreen is surprisingly responsive for the price — I use it more than I thought I would."* Another praised the keyboard: *"I type 80 WPM and this feels as good as my previous $1,200 Dell."*

**The honest downsides**

Battery life is the biggest compromise. Lenovo advertises up to 8 hours, but real-world testing gives you 5–6 hours with moderate use (screen at 70%, Wi-Fi on, mixed browsing and document work). Heavy video streaming drops that to 4 hours. It'll get you through a workday if you're near an outlet, but not a full day of off-grid work.

The all-plastic chassis flexes noticeably around the palm rest when you press firmly. It won't break, but it undermines the premium feel. The 720p webcam is also dated — fine for Teams calls in good lighting, but grainy in dim rooms.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for students, home-office workers, and small business owners who need a reliable daily driver with a touchscreen and solid keyboard. It's also a strong choice for anyone who wants a second laptop for light productivity and entertainment. Skip it if you're a gamer (the integrated Iris Xe graphics handle Minecraft and light titles only), a creative professional needing color accuracy, or someone who needs all-day battery life.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Can I upgrade the RAM later?**
No. The 8GB is soldered to the motherboard. Buy the 16GB version upfront if you run multiple heavy apps.

**Does the touchscreen work with a stylus?**
It supports capacitive styluses, but there's no active pen support, so no pressure sensitivity. A basic stylus works fine for navigation.

**Is it compatible with Windows 11?**
Yes, it ships with Windows 11 Home and supports all current updates.

**The verdict: Buy it if you want a versatile daily driver**

**Buy it if:** You need a reliable laptop with a touchscreen for work or school, value a great keyboard, and can live with 5–6 hour battery life.

**Skip it if:** You need all-day battery, plan to upgrade RAM later, or want a premium metal chassis.

The Lenovo Ideapad 3i 15.6" FHD Touchscreen is a genuine value at its price point — a rare laptop that balances features, performance, and affordability without major compromises. It earns a solid **4.6/5** and a spot on your shortlist.
