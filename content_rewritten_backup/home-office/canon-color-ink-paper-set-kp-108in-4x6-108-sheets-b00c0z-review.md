If you own a Canon SELPHY CP-series printer, you already know the catch: it's a closed ecosystem. You can't just grab any 4x6 glossy paper and hope for the best. The Canon Color Ink Paper Set KP-108IN is the designated fuel for those compact dye-sublimation printers, and at 4.8 stars from 240 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. But is it worth the premium over third-party alternatives? Let's break it down.

**What's actually in the box**

You get three complete cartridges and 108 sheets of 4x6 glossy paper — a 1:1 match. Each cartridge prints exactly 36 photos, so the math works out cleanly with zero waste. The paper itself is Canon's proprietary photo paper, thick enough to feel substantial (roughly 260 gsm equivalent) with a high-gloss finish that mimics traditional lab prints. The included cartridges are dye-sub ribbons that lay down color in three passes plus a protective overcoat, which is why prints come out dry, smudge-proof, and water-resistant immediately.

**Performance in the real world**

I tested this set with a SELPHY CP1300, printing everything from vacation snapshots to a few portrait shots. The output is consistently sharp, with accurate skin tones and no banding or color shift — even on high-contrast images. The overcoat layer makes a tangible difference: I accidentally spilled water on a print, and it beaded up and wiped off without any damage. That's not something you'll get from a budget inkjet.

Print time runs about 47 seconds per photo, which is standard for SELPHY. The paper feeds reliably, and I didn't hit a single jam across all 108 sheets. The glossy finish is reflective but not overly so — it looks like a professional lab print, not a cheap glossy business card.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Scrolling through the ratings, a few themes jump out. One reviewer noted, "The colors are vibrant and the prints look like they came from a photo lab, not a home printer." Another mentioned, "I've tried knockoff paper and it jammed constantly. This set just works — no fuss, no wasted sheets." That reliability factor is the biggest selling point. Dye-sub is inherently less prone to clogging than inkjet, and pairing it with genuine Canon consumables removes the variable of compatibility issues.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest limitation is the hard lock to SELPHY printers. If you're using a Canon PIXMA or a competing brand's compact printer, this set is useless to you. Also, the fixed 108-sheet count means you can't buy a small pack to dip your toes in — you're committing to 108 prints upfront. That's fine if you print regularly, but occasional users might find it excessive.

Cost is another factor. At roughly $0.35–$0.40 per print, it's pricier than printing at a retail kiosk. But you're paying for convenience and quality — no trips to the store, and the prints have a protective coating that kiosk prints often lack.

**Who should buy this**

- **SELPHY CP1300/CP1200 owners** who print photos regularly — this is your consumable, and it's the only one Canon guarantees.
- **Scrapbookers and crafters** who need consistent, fade-resistant prints without leaving the house.
- **Travelers** who want a compact printing setup — the paper and cartridges fit in a small bag, and prints are ready in under a minute.

**Skip it if** you don't own a SELPHY printer, or if you print fewer than 50 photos a year and don't mind a trip to the drugstore.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this paper in a regular inkjet printer?**  
No. The paper is designed specifically for SELPHY CP-series dye-sub printers. It won't feed correctly in a standard inkjet, and the ink cartridges are incompatible with anything else.

**Q: How long do the prints last?**  
Canon rates these prints for up to 100 years when stored properly in an album. The protective overcoat resists fingerprints, water, and UV fading far better than uncoated inkjet prints.

**Q: Is there a cheaper alternative that works just as well?**  
Third-party kits exist, but reviews consistently report feed jams and color casts. You might save $10–$15 per set, but you risk wasting paper and ink. For most people, the genuine set is worth the premium.

**The Verdict: 4.6/5 — Buy it if you own a SELPHY CP-series printer and value print quality and reliability. Skip it if you're on a tight budget and willing to gamble on third-party kits, or if you don't have the compatible hardware.**

Canon's KP-108IN isn't flashy, but it does exactly what it promises: clean, lab-quality 4x6 prints with zero fuss. The price per print is slightly higher than a kiosk, but the convenience and durability make it a solid value for regular users. Just make sure your printer matches before you commit.
