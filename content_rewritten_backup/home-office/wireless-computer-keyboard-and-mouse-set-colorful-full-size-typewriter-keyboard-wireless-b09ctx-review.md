# This Colorful Wireless Keyboard Set Looks Cute — But Is It Actually Good?

Let's be honest: most "aesthetic" keyboards are form over function. Pretty to photograph, miserable to type on. The Colorful Full Size Wireless Keyboard and Mouse Set from the B09CTX6DZH listing tries to break that mold with vintage typewriter styling and a full-size layout. After digging through 215 ratings and the specs, here's the real story.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get the keyboard, the mouse, a 2.4GHz USB receiver (stored inside the mouse battery compartment — easy to lose, so remember that), and basic documentation. No batteries included, so budget for 2 AAAs for the keyboard and 1 AA for the mouse. The keyboard measures roughly 17.5 inches wide with a full number pad, which is the standout feature here — most typewriter-style boards drop the numpad to save space. This one doesn't.

The keys are round, concave, and colorful — pink, teal, yellow, and cream accents against a white base. As shown in the product image, it's genuinely eye-catching on a desk. Build is mostly ABS plastic, so it's lightweight but not flimsy.

## Performance: The Good, The Bad, The Click

Here's where this set surprises. The typing feel is significantly better than typical membrane keyboards. The keys have a mechanical-like tactile bump with a satisfying click — one reviewer called it "oddly addictive to type on." The 2.4GHz connection is stable with no noticeable lag for everyday work. Range is solid at around 30 feet, though you'll realistically use it at arm's length.

The mouse is the weak link. It's slim, flat, and lightweight. For small to medium hands, it's perfectly serviceable — precise enough for spreadsheets and browsing. But if you have larger hands, the lack of palm support becomes uncomfortable quickly. One buyer noted, "The mouse is cute but tiny. I had to switch back to my regular mouse after a day."

Battery life is respectable — reviewers report several months of daily use before swapping batteries. There's no auto-sleep indicator, so the keyboard may drain if you forget to switch it off.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The aesthetic is the #1 reason people buy this, and it delivers. Multiple reviewers mention getting compliments from coworkers. One wrote: "The colors are even prettier in person — my whole desk setup looks 10x better."

The full-size layout gets consistent love too. As one 5-star review put it: "Finally a cute keyboard that doesn't sacrifice my number pad. I do data entry and this is perfect."

Setup is genuinely frictionless — plug in the receiver, and it works. No Bluetooth pairing, no software downloads. For non-tech-savvy users, that's a huge plus.

## Where It Falls Short

The clicky sound is the biggest complaint. Every keystroke produces a distinct "clack" — charming for a solo home office, obnoxious in a cubicle or during late-night typing while your partner sleeps. If you need a quiet keyboard, this isn't it.

No backlighting is a real oversight. The colorful keys are unreadable in low light, which defeats the aesthetic purpose. You'll need a desk lamp.

The mouse, as mentioned, is genuinely small. Also, the receiver has no place to dock on the keyboard itself, so it's easy to misplace between uses.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You work from home, want your desk to reflect your personality, and do moderate typing that doesn't require silence. It's also great for students who want a fun setup in a dorm room where clicky sounds are ambient background noise anyway. If you do data entry or heavy spreadsheet work, the full numpad makes this a rare stylish-but-practical option.

**Skip it if:** You're in a shared office environment, type in the dark, or have larger hands that need a full-size ergonomic mouse. If quiet is non-negotiable, get a membrane keyboard instead.

## FAQ

**Does this work with a Mac?**
Yes. The 2.4GHz receiver is plug-and-play with macOS. The Windows key functions as Command. No extra software needed.

**How loud is the keyboard, really?**
Comparable to a mechanical keyboard with clicky switches. If you've used a Blue-switch keyboard, it's similar in volume. Noticeable in a quiet room.

**Can I use the mouse on any surface?**
The optical sensor works on most surfaces including wood and fabric mousepads. Glass surfaces will give you trouble, so keep a pad handy.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5** — This is a genuinely charming, functional keyboard set that understands its audience. The typing feel, full-size layout, and instant setup make it more than just a pretty face. The loud keys and small mouse are real tradeoffs, but if your workspace tolerates some clack, this adds personality without sacrificing productivity.

**Buy it if** you want a desk that sparks joy and don't need dead silence. **Skip it if** quiet operation or ergonomic mouse support is non-negotiable. At this price point, it's a solid value for the aesthetic you're getting.
