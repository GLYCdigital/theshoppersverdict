Let's be blunt: compatible ink is a gamble. You're betting your print head and your patience against saving 40-60% over Canon's OEM cartridges. The DAM Aila 1200XL set for Canon Maxify printers (PGI-1200 and CLI-1200 equivalents) mostly pays out that bet — but with a caveat or two you should know before clicking buy.

## What's actually in the box

You get four cartridges: one black (PGI-1200XL) and three color — cyan, magenta, yellow (CLI-1200XL). They're the standard pigmented ink formulation Canon Maxify printers require, which matters because Maxify heads are designed for pigment, not dye. Using dye ink in a Maxify is a fast way to clog nozzles. These are pigment-based, so they're chemically compatible.

The cartridges themselves feel solid. The plastic body is slightly lighter than Canon OEM, but the chip contacts are seated properly and the snap-in fit is identical to genuine cartridges. No adapters, no tape to peel, no fiddly alignment.

## Real-world performance

I tested these in a Maxify MB2120, running a mix of text documents, grayscale spreadsheets, and color presentation slides. Black text came out crisp — no feathering on standard copy paper. Color output was punchy and accurate enough for internal business documents. If you're doing photo printing, this isn't your ink, but that's not what Maxify printers are for anyway.

The yield claim is 2,400 pages for black and 1,500 per color cartridge. That's the same spec as Canon's XL versions. One buyer noted: *"I've run about 1,800 pages through the black so far and it's still going strong. No smudging, no streaks."* Another said: *"The install took 30 seconds. My printer recognized them instantly, no error messages."*

That instant recognition matters. Older compatibles often triggered "non-genuine ink" warnings or failed to report ink levels. These don't. The chip communicates correctly with the printer, and low-ink warnings appear at reasonable levels, not prematurely.

## Where it falls short

The biggest limitation is packaging. You must buy the full four-cartridge set. If you burn through black faster than color — which most offices do — you'll have surplus color cartridges sitting around while you re-order the whole set. There's no singles option. That's inconvenient, though the price per set is low enough that it's not a dealbreaker.

A few reviewers mentioned minor ink residue on the cartridge nozzles after the first install. It didn't affect print quality in their reports, but it's worth wiping the contacts with a lint-free cloth before inserting if you see any wetness. This is common with compatibles, not unique to DAM Aila.

One other note: these are not remanufactured cartridges. They're new builds. That's actually a plus — remanufactured units carry the risk of leftover ink mixing and head damage. New builds eliminate that variable.

## Who should buy this

This set is ideal for home offices and small businesses that run moderate-to-heavy print volumes on a Canon Maxify (MB2120, MB2320, MB2720, MB5120, MB5320, MB5420, or the iX/iB series). If you're printing 500+ pages a month of documents, invoices, or shipping labels, the savings versus OEM add up fast — you'll likely recoup the cost difference within two cartridge cycles.

Skip it if you print less than 100 pages a month. The ink may sit in the print head long enough to dry, and the minor risks of compatibles aren't worth it for occasional use. Also skip if you demand OEM reliability for client-facing color materials — stick with Canon branded ink there.

## FAQ

**Will these void my Canon warranty?**
In the US, no. The Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act prevents manufacturers from voiding warranties solely for using third-party consumables. If your print head fails, Canon must prove the ink caused it — which is rare with pigment-based compatibles.

**Do they track ink levels accurately?**
Yes. The chips report levels correctly in the printer's status monitor. Users report accurate low-ink warnings rather than sudden drops to empty.

**Can I mix these with a remaining OEM cartridge?**
Yes, but avoid mixing colors in the same channel. If your OEM magenta is low, finish it before swapping in the compatible. Mixing old and new ink in the same reservoir can cause clogs.

## The Verdict

The DAM Aila 1200XL set delivers solid, reliable printing at a fraction of OEM cost. It's not flawless — the all-or-nothing pack is annoying, and the plastic feels slightly cheaper than genuine Canon — but for sheer print volume value, it's one of the better compatibles on the market.

**Buy it if:** You run a high-volume Maxify and want to cut ink costs by half without sacrificing document quality.
