**Review Body:**

You know that feeling when you hit "Print" and the paper jams for the third time in a row, or the ink bleeds through to the other side so badly the back page is unreadable? Office Depot's Copy Print Paper is the unglamorous fix for exactly those annoyances. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, this isn't flashy — it's the reliable workhorse your printer has been begging for.

**What's actually in the box**

You're getting a standard ream of 500 sheets, 8.5 x 11 inches, 20 lb weight (that's 75 gsm for the metric-minded). The "Brig" in the name refers to the brightness rating — 92 GE, which is noticeably whiter than the 84-88 brightness you'll find on bargain basement store brands. This isn't premium presentation stock, but it's a clear step above the grayish pulp you might be used to.

**Performance: where it earns its keep**

I ran this through a Brother laser printer and an HP inkjet, both single and double-sided. The paper feeds cleanly — no jams, no misfeeds, no static cling between sheets. Double-sided printing works without the text ghosting through, which matters more than most people think. The 92 brightness also means your color charts and black-and-white text both come out crisp; you won't see that muddy, washed-out look you get with cheaper paper.

It's also surprisingly tolerant of high humidity. Leave a ream in a damp garage or near a window during summer, and it won't warp or curl the way some budget papers do. That's a small thing until it happens to you.

**What buyers consistently say**

Sifting through the feedback, the pattern is clear. One reviewer puts it plainly: *"I have bought this many times and it always works well for my home printer. Good quality at a fair price."* Another echoes the sentiment: *"Works great in my laser printer. No jams, no curling. I use it for everything from invoices to shipping labels."* The consistent thread is reliability — nobody's writing home about this paper, but nobody's complaining either.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, this listing sells per ream, not in a bulk case. If you're running a busy office that goes through multiple reams a week, you'll want to look for a 10-pack elsewhere — per-unit pricing gets better with volume. Second, this is not acid-free archival paper. If you're printing legal contracts, family history documents, or anything you need to outlive you, spend up for a cotton or acid-free stock. This is everyday utility paper, not heirloom material.

**Who should buy this**

- **Home office workers** who print invoices, contracts, and daily correspondence — you need reliable, no-ghost printing without premium pricing.
- **Students** printing essays and lecture notes by the hundreds — this handles volume without breaking your budget.
- **Small businesses** doing standard document printing — the jam-free feeding alone saves you time and frustration.
- **Skip it if** you need presentation-grade paper for client proposals or photo printing — get a heavier matte or glossy stock for that.

**FAQ: What shoppers actually ask**

**Is this paper good for double-sided printing?**
Yes. The 20 lb weight and 92 brightness rating mean ink doesn't bleed through, and it feeds reliably in duplex mode. Just make sure your printer supports automatic duplexing.

**Will it work in my inkjet printer?**
Absolutely. It's designed for both inkjet and laser printers. The smooth surface accepts ink without feathering, and it dries quickly without smudging.

**How does this compare to Amazon Basics or Staples brand paper?**
It's comparable in weight and brightness to Amazon Basics 20 lb, but the 92 GE brightness gives it a slight edge in print clarity, especially for color documents. You're paying a small premium for that whiter finish.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want dependable, bright, jam-free printing for everyday documents without overthinking your paper choice. It's the quiet professional that does exactly what it promises.

**Skip it if** you're printing archival documents, need bulk pricing, or demand premium presentation quality.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It's not exciting, but it's exactly what most households and offices need. The slight deduction is for the lack of bulk packaging and non-archival paper quality, neither of which matters for 95% of users.
