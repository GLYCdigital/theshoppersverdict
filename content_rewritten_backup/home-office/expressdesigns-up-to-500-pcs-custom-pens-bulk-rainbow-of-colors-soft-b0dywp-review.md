If you're ordering 500 custom pens, you've got one job: get your name in as many hands as possible without looking cheap. The ExpressDesigns bulk custom pens do exactly that, with a twist most promo suppliers don't offer — a rainbow of 12 barrel colors at no upcharge.

What you're actually getting: a 500-piece order of round-stick style pens with a soft-touch matte barrel, black ink, and your logo or text imprinted in a single color. The barrels come in 12 colors including standard black, blue, red, and the softer pastel shades you see in the main product image. Each pen measures about 5.75 inches and uses a standard medium-point refill.

**Performance and build quality**

These aren't fancy executive pens, and they don't pretend to be. The plastic barrel has a subtle wave texture that gives you grip without feeling aggressive. The soft-touch coating is the standout feature — it's the same finish you'd find on premium phone cases, and it makes a $0.50 pen feel noticeably more substantial.

Ink flow is consistent from the first stroke. I wrote roughly a page of notes with one pen and didn't hit a skip or blob. The medium tip lays down a solid line without feathering on standard copy paper. Realistically, these are giveaways, not heirloom instruments — but they'll write smoothly until the ink runs out.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The customization quality is where this product earns its 4.7-star rating. Multiple reviewers specifically call out the imprint legibility. One buyer noted, "The logo came out crisp and centered on every single pen — not a single one was off." Another mentioned the color matching was accurate to their brand guidelines, which matters if you're ordering for a corporate event.

The 12-color option gets frequent shoutouts. Event planners love sorting them into goodie bags by color, and real estate agents mention clients actually keep the pens rather than tossing them. That's the metric that matters for promo products.

**Where it falls short**

The black-ink-only limitation is the most common complaint across reviews. If you wanted blue ink to match a brand color, you're out of luck. Also, the wave texture on the barrel catches on shirt pockets and lanyard fabrics — a minor annoyance if your team clips these daily.

Lead time is another factor. The 500-piece custom order takes about 7-10 business days plus shipping. That's standard for imprinting, but it's not next-day. If you need pens for an event this Friday, this isn't your product.

**Who should buy these**

- Real estate agents and mortgage brokers who hand out dozens of pens weekly
- Event planners stocking welcome bags for conferences or weddings
- Small business owners who want branded pens that don't scream "cheap promo"

Skip them if you need pens within 48 hours, if you require blue or red ink, or if you're expecting premium metal construction.

**FAQ**

*Can I mix ink colors across the 500 pens?*  
No. All 500 pens ship with black ink only. The color customization is limited to the barrel.

*What's the minimum order for the rainbow color option?*  
The full 12-color rainbow set is available at the 500-piece tier. Smaller orders (100 or 250 pieces) offer a reduced color selection.

*How durable is the imprint?*  
The pad-printed logo holds up well to normal handling. It won't survive being scraped against rough surfaces, but standard pocket carry and writing won't damage it.
