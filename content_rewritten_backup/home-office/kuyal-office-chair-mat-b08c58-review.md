## Where It Falls Short

Be honest about your floors before you buy. This mat is built for **low-pile carpet or hard floors** — not plush residential carpet. The lip is aggressive and won't seat properly on thick pile, causing corners to lift and catch chair wheels. One frustrated buyer warned, "On my medium-pile carpet at home, this mat is a tripping hazard. The edges won't stay down."

Then there's the smell. Outgassing from the PVC is strong for the first week. You'll want to unbox it in a garage or ventilated room. It dissipates, but it's a real consideration if you're setting up a nursery or small home office.

## FAQ

**Will this work on high-pile carpet?**
No. The mat's studs need firm carpet to grip. On plush carpet, the mat will sit unevenly and the edges will curl.

**How do I flatten the curl when it arrives?**
Roll it the opposite direction, secure with a bungee cord, and let it sit overnight. Then place it stud-side down and weigh the corners for 24 hours. The curl returns slightly in cold rooms — keep it above 65°F.

**Can I use this on a hardwood floor?**
Yes, but the studs won't grip as firmly as on carpet. Consider flipping it smooth-side down on hard floors to avoid marking the finish.
