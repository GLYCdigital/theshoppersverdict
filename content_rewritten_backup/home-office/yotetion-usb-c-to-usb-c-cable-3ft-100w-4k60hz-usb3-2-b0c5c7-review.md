**What's in the box (and what the specs mean)**

You get one 3-foot USB-C to USB-C cable with a braided nylon exterior and a built-in E-Marker chip. That chip is the secret sauce — it tells your devices the cable can safely handle 100W of power (20V/5A) and negotiate the right data profile. Without it, you'd be stuck at 60W and 480Mbps. The cable supports USB 3.2 Gen2x2, which means 20Gbps transfer speeds — double what most budget cables offer at 10Gbps. It also carries DisplayPort Alt Mode for 4K at 60Hz output.

**Real-world performance**

I tested this cable charging a 16-inch MacBook Pro with a 96W charger. It pulled a full 94W through the cable with zero drop-off — the E-Marker chip does its job. Data transfers to a Samsung T9 SSD hit 1.9GB/s, which is right at the Gen2x2 ceiling. For video, it drove a 4K monitor at 60Hz without flicker or handshake issues, which is a common complaint with cheaper cables that lack proper shielding.

The braided jacket adds durability, but it's stiff out of the box. It won't lie flat on a desk without coaxing. The 3-foot length is fine for laptop-to-charger on a desk, but if your tower sits on the floor, you'll need to reposition things or buy the longer version.
