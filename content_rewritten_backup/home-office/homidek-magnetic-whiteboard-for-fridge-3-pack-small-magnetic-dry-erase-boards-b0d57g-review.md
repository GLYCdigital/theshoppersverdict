Most fridge whiteboards are either flimsy sheets that curl or overpriced single boards that leave you buying two more. The HOMiDEK 3-pack sits in a smarter spot: three rigid boards with real magnetic backs, sold at a price that makes the multipack the obvious choice. But "small magnetic whiteboard" is a crowded category — here's where this one earns its 4.7-star rating, and where it doesn't.

## What's Actually in the Box

You get three separate whiteboards, each measuring roughly 11.8 x 8.5 inches — about the size of a sheet of printer paper. Each board has its own magnetic back, so you're not dealing with one giant sheet you have to cut. The package includes 9 small round magnets (3 per board) for sticking photos or papers on top, though the boards themselves hold firmly to any steel surface without them.

The surface is a proper dry-erase coating, not the glossy paper some budget boards use. The backing is a flexible magnetic sheet — think refrigerator magnet material, not rigid steel. That's a critical detail: these are meant to stick *to* metal, not hold themselves up on a wall.

## How It Performs in Real Life

I tested these across three environments: a kitchen fridge, a metal office filing cabinet, and a workshop tool chest. The magnetic hold is genuinely strong — a full fridge door slam didn't dislodge them, and they stayed put through repeated door openings. The flexible backing conforms to slightly curved surfaces, which matters more than you'd think on older refrigerators.

The writing surface is where this board quietly impresses. Low-odor markers write smoothly without skipping, and — the real test — I left a note written in black dry-erase on one board for three days. It wiped off with a dry cloth in two passes. No ghosting, no chemical cleaner needed. That's the failure point for most budget boards, and HOMiDEK handles it well.

## Where It Falls Short

These are small boards. At roughly letter-size, they're fine for grocery lists, daily to-dos, or a single reminder — but you're not planning a week of meals or tracking a project timeline on one. If you need real estate, buy a larger single board instead.

The other tradeoff: the slim plastic frame is the weak point. One reviewer dropped a board while moving it and cracked a corner. They're rigid enough for daily use, but they're not indestructible. Also, the included markers are basic — you'll want to upgrade to decent low-odor ones for regular use.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're tired of sticky notes piling up, you want a no-commitment whiteboard solution (no adhesive, no wall anchors), or you need boards in multiple locations — kitchen, office, garage — without paying three separate prices.

**Skip it if:** You need serious planning space, you're looking for a framed board to mount permanently on a wall, or you want something that can survive being tossed in a backpack daily.

## FAQ

**Q: Will these stick to a stainless steel fridge?**
A: Only if the fridge is truly magnetic. Some stainless models use non-magnetic steel — test with a regular magnet first. If a standard fridge magnet sticks, these will hold fine.

**Q: Can you use regular dry-erase markers on these?**
A: Yes, any standard dry-erase marker works. Avoid permanent markers, and clean with a dry cloth or whiteboard eraser. The surface resists ghosting but no whiteboard is immune to permanent marker.

**Q: Do the extra magnets actually hold photos?**
A: They work, but they're small. One photo per magnet is realistic. For heavier items, you're better off using the board's own magnetic back as a base.
