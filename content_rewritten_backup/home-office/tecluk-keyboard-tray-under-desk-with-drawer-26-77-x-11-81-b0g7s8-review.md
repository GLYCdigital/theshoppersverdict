If your shoulders have been screaming after an hour of typing, you already know the problem: your keyboard is sitting too high. The TECLUK under-desk keyboard tray with drawer (26.77" x 11.81", $53.99) tackles that directly by pulling your input devices down to elbow level, where they belong. But is it worth drilling into your desk for? Let's get into it.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the steel mounting rails, the tray itself, the sliding drawer, mounting screws, and a basic instruction sheet. The tray surface is a textured black plastic composite — not wood, not metal. That's fine for a keyboard, but the built-in mouse surface is also plastic. If you're used to a cloth mousepad, you'll notice the difference in glide. Several reviewers mention this, and it's a fair critique.

The drawer is the standout feature here. It's about 11 inches deep and slides out smoothly on the same ball-bearing rails as the main tray. It's deep enough for a notepad, pens, earbuds, or your phone. It doesn't rattle when closed, which is more than I can say for cheaper alternatives.

**Build quality and real-world use**

The steel rails are the backbone of this thing, and they're solid. The ball-bearing slides move smoothly and lock in place when extended — no accidental sliding back while you're typing. The tray itself supports a full-size keyboard and mouse with room to spare. At 26.77 inches wide, it fits standard desks without overhang.

Mounting requires a drill. There's no clamp option, and you'll need to measure carefully before you commit. Once installed, the tray is fixed at whatever angle you set — there's no tilt adjustment after the fact. That's the biggest design limitation. If you mount it slightly off, you're living with it.

One specific gripe from multiple reviewers: the mounting holes are pre-drilled, but the included screws are short. If your desk is thicker than 1.5 inches, you'll need to buy longer screws. That's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The drawer is the most-cited positive. One reviewer wrote, "I didn't expect to use the drawer as much as I do — it holds my phone, glasses, and a few pens, and it keeps my desk clutter-free." Another praised the stability: "The rails are much beefier than I expected for the price. No wobble at all when typing."

The ergonomic benefit is the other recurring theme. People who installed this at the right height report immediate relief in shoulder and neck tension. That's the core value proposition, and the TECLUK delivers it.

**Where it falls short**

The lack of tilt adjustment is the biggest miss. Competing trays in this price range often include a simple angle dial. The TECLUK doesn't, so you're committing to a fixed position at install.

The plastic mouse surface is another compromise. For everyday office work, it's fine. For precision work in design or gaming, you'll want your own mousepad on top.

And installation is permanent. If you're renting, have a glass desk, or move frequently, this isn't the product for you.

**Who should buy this**

- **Ergonomics-focused office workers** who spend 6+ hours daily at a desk and need to drop their keyboard to elbow height
- **People with cluttered desks** who want a dedicated spot for keyboard, mouse, and small supplies
- **Home office setups** where you own your desk and don't plan to move it

**Skip it if** you're a renter (no clamps, only screws), you need adjustable tilt, or you want a genuine mousepad surface built in.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit a mechanical keyboard with wrist rest?**  
A: Yes, the tray is 26.77" wide. A full-size mechanical keyboard plus mouse fits without issue. A separate wrist rest will eat into the mouse space, so measure your setup first.

**Q: How much weight can the drawer hold?**  
A: The rails are rated for roughly 10 lbs, but realistically, keep it under 5 lbs. It's best for light items like pens, sticky notes, or a phone — not a tablet or heavy tools.

**Q: Can I install this on a standing desk?**  
A: Yes, but double-check the desk's underside for crossbars or structural ribs before drilling. The mounting plate is about 12 inches wide, so you need a clear, flat space.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a stable, well-built under-desk tray with a genuinely useful drawer and you're fine with a permanent install. **Skip it if** you need tilt adjustment, can't drill into your desk, or demand a cloth mouse surface.

At $53.99, the TECLUK delivers solid value for the money — a 4.2/5 from me. It's not perfect, but for the price, it does the job it promises: getting your keyboard off the desk and your shoulders out of your ears.
