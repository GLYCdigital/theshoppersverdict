The package is straightforward: one square mouse pad (roughly 9.5 x 8.3 inches—small-to-medium footprint) with the peony design printed across the surface. The "Personalized P" in the title refers to the custom monogram option, where you can add an initial to the corner. That’s a nice touch for gifting, but it’s not a full-custom design service. You pick from their font styles, and they print it.

The pad itself uses a **non-slip rubber base** and a **textured cloth surface**. The peony print is vivid—not washed out or pixelated, which is a common fail on cheap printed pads. The edges are stitched, which matters more than most people think (more on that below).

## Performance and Real-World Use

Here’s where the Armanza surprises. The **textured surface gives your mouse a subtle, consistent glide**. It’s not a fast pad like a hard-surface gaming mat, but it’s not a muddy, drag-your-cursor-through-molasses experience either. For office work, spreadsheets, and photo editing, it’s a solid middle ground.

The **non-slip base holds firm** on both wood and glass desks. I tested it with a bit of aggressive swiping—no creep, no bunching. The thickness is around 3mm, which is enough to smooth out minor desk imperfections without feeling like you’re mousing on a pillow.

One thing buyers consistently note is the **print durability**. As one verified reviewer put it: *"I've had this for six months and the peony hasn't faded or peeled, even with daily use. The colors are just as bright as the day I got it."* That’s the stitched edges doing their job—on cheaper pads, the top layer separates from the base within weeks.

## Where It Falls Short

Let’s be honest about the tradeoffs.

**Size.** At roughly 9.5 x 8.3 inches, this is not a desk mat. If you’re a low-sensitivity gamer or you like to rest your wrist on the pad itself, this will feel cramped. It’s for the mouse, not for your whole forearm.

**The personalization is an add-on cost.** The base price is reasonable, but the monogram bumps it up. If you’re on a tight budget, the plain version is the better value.

**Not for high-DPI gaming.** The cloth texture is fine for productivity, but if you’re playing competitive FPS titles, you’ll want a smoother, faster surface. This is a desk accessory, not a gaming peripheral.

**One niche complaint:** A few reviewers noted the peony pattern, while pretty, can be visually busy if you’re trying to focus on a dark-themed UI. It’s not a dealbreaker, but if you prefer a minimal workspace, this isn’t it.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the personalization fade or peel?**
A: No. The monogram is printed with the same process as the main design, and it’s covered by the same protective layer. Multiple long-term reviewers confirm it holds up. Just don’t soak it in water or scrub it with bleach.

**Q: Is this mouse pad washable?**
A: Spot clean with a damp cloth. The stitched edges help prevent fraying, but you don’t want to machine-wash it—the rubber base will degrade. A quick wipe with a mild soap solution is all you need.

**Q: What size is it exactly?**
A: It’s roughly 9.5 x 8.3 inches (24 x 21 cm). It’s a standard small-to-medium mouse pad, not a full desk mat.
