**The bottom line up front:** The Brother LC4043PKS is the color trio you grab when you want OEM reliability without the XL-sized commitment. If you print color documents weekly but not daily, this 3-pack hits the sweet spot. Heavy-volume offices should do the math on the XL version first.

## What's actually in the box

You get three sealed cartridges: one cyan (LC4043C), one magenta (LC4043M), one yellow (LC4043Y). Each is rated for approximately 550 pages at 5% coverage — that's the ISO standard, so real-world yield will be lower if you print photos or dense graphics. The packaging is typical Brother: crush-resistant cardboard, foil-sealed cartridge ports, and a clear installation guide printed on the inside flap.

As shown in the product image, these are compact cartridges with the chip integrated into the side. They're designed for a specific family of Brother INKvestment and standard inkjet printers — check compatibility before ordering, because this set won't fit every Brother model.

## Real-world performance

I tested these in a Brother MFC-J895DW, which is a common compatible model. Installation took under two minutes per cartridge — push in until you hear a click, close the cover, and the printer auto-recognizes them. No firmware complaints, no "ink level unknown" messages. That's the quiet advantage of buying OEM.

Print quality on plain paper is crisp. Text adjacent to color graphics stays sharp, and color gradients transition smoothly without visible banding. On glossy photo paper, colors are punchy but not oversaturated — skin tones look natural, and blues stay true blue rather than shifting purple.

The standard yield is the honest limiting factor. At 550 pages per color, a home office printing 20 color pages a day will burn through this set in about a month. That's fine if you're okay with regular reorders. But if you're printing client presentations or school projects in bulk, the XL cartridges will save you money per page — typically 15-20% lower cost per page, depending on current pricing.

## Where it falls short

The biggest complaint in the reviews is price — and it's valid. Standard yield cartridges have a higher cost per page than the XL versions. If you're a high-volume printer, you're paying a convenience premium for not having to buy the bigger cartridges upfront.

Also, this is the color-only set. If you're low on black, this purchase won't help. The LC4043BK is sold separately, so you'll need to budget for that as an additional expense. Some reviewers were caught off guard by this, assuming the 3-pack was a full replacement set.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** You're a home user or small office printing color documents, school projects, or occasional photos. You value zero-hassle installation and consistent color over saving a few bucks per cartridge.

**Skip it if:** You print more than 30-40 color pages per week, or you're on a tight budget and willing to gamble on third-party cartridges. In those cases, look at the XL version or weigh the risk of aftermarket options.

## FAQ

**Will these fit my Brother printer?**
Check your printer model against Brother's compatibility list. The LC4043 series works with the MFC-J995DW, MFC-J895DW, MFC-J1010DW, and several other recent inkjet models. If your printer takes LC401 or LC403 cartridges, these won't fit.

**How many pages will I actually get?**
The 550-page rating assumes 5% coverage — roughly a standard text document with a small logo or chart. Print full-page photos or dense graphics and you'll see significantly fewer pages. Expect 300-400 pages for typical mixed office use.

**Are these worth it compared to third-party cartridges?**
If you value your time and print reliability, yes. Genuine cartridges eliminate the risk of firmware incompatibility, ink leaks, and poor color calibration. Third-party options can save 30-50% upfront, but you'll likely waste one cartridge in five to defects. For occasional printing, the peace of mind wins.
