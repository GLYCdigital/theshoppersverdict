Let's be honest: most hanging file folders are flimsy afterthoughts you tolerate because you have to. The SUNEE Extra Capacity 25-pack is not that. With 229 Amazon ratings averaging 4.6 out of 5, this is the rare office supply that earns its praise — and has a couple of quirks worth knowing before you buy.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains 25 letter-size hanging folders with a genuine depth advantage. The "extra capacity" claim isn't marketing fluff — these measure roughly 12.5 inches deep versus the standard 9.5 inches you'll find in most office store packs. That translates to holding a solid 30% more paper per folder before the dreaded bowing starts. Each folder has a rigid plastic frame running through the top, with reinforced rod channels that grip standard drawer rails without catching.

The full-length access tabs on both ends are a thoughtful touch. You can label from either side of the drawer, which matters more than you'd think when you're standing at a filing cabinet and don't want to flip folders around to read them.

## Performance in Real Use

I loaded these with thick client files, old tax paperwork, and multi-year project binders. The deeper design genuinely prevents the "V-shape collapse" that plagues thinner folders — documents stay vertical and visible. The plastic-coated rods slide smoothly on both standard metal rails and the cheaper wire racks. After a month of daily use, none of the folders have torn at the stress points where the rod meets the frame, which is where cheap folders always fail.

One buyer noted, "These are noticeably thicker than the ones from the office store. I've been using them for three months and they still look new." Another mentioned the smooth sliding action: "Finally, folders that don't catch on the rails. My whole drawer glides now."

## Where It Falls Short

The folded corners are the main gripe. SUNEE ships these flat, which means the corners arrive creased and need a firm press to sit properly. It's a 30-second fix per folder, but if you're unpacking 25 at once, it adds up.

There are also no interior divider pockets. These are pure hanging folders — you'll need separate manila folders if you want to sub-categorize within each hanging file. For heavy organizers, that's an extra purchase. And while the plastic frame is sturdy, it's not indestructible; one reviewer reported a frame crack after aggressive overstuffing past what any folder should hold.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Small business owners** drowning in client files who need folders that survive daily use
- **Home office organizers** with deep drawers who hate the sagging of standard folders
- **Anyone replacing an entire filing system** — the 25-pack is the right quantity to do a full drawer in one go

Skip it if you're a light filer who touches files twice a year, or if you need built-in sub-dividers.

## FAQ

**Q: Do these fit standard filing cabinets?**
A: Yes, they work with both letter-size metal rails and the wire frames in most home office cabinets. The rods are standard width.

**Q: Can I use these without a filing cabinet?**
A: Technically yes, but they're designed for drawer rails. Without rails, they'll flop. Get a portable file box instead.

**Q: Are they acid-free for archival storage?**
A: The folders are made from durable stock but aren't marketed as acid-free or archival-grade. For long-term document preservation, use acid-free manila folders inside these hanging files.
