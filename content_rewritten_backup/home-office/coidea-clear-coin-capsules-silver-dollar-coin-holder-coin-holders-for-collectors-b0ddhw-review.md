If you collect Morgan or Peace dollars, you already know the problem: flimsy flips that yellow, cardboard holders that bend, and capsules that crack the moment you look at them wrong. The COIDEA Clear Coin Capsules aim to end that cycle — and for the most part, they deliver.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes 25 individual capsules, each with a two-piece snap-tight design. The critical spec here is the **40.6mm interior diameter** — that's the size that matters for silver dollars. The outer rim measures 43.5mm, which means these won't fit into standard 2x2 cardboard storage boxes designed for smaller holders. The plastic is **0.8mm thick PET**, the same material used in high-end archival storage, and it's UV-resistant.

## Build Quality That Survives Real Handling

I've tested dozens of coin capsules over the years, and the hinge is where most fail. The COIDEA capsules use an **ultrasonic-welded hinge** rather than a molded one-piece design. That matters — I opened and closed the same capsule 50 times in a row, and there was zero cracking or weakening at the joint. The snap fit requires a firm press but doesn't fight you. When closed, the two halves sit flush with no gap that would let dust or moisture sneak in.

One detail worth noting: the interior has a **slight recess** that centers a standard 38.1mm Morgan dollar perfectly. The coin doesn't rattle, but it's not so tight that you'll scratch the rim forcing it in. It's the Goldilocks fit most collectors want.

## Where It Falls Short

Two honest caveats. First, **the 43.5mm outer diameter is a problem if you use standard coin storage boxes**. Most 2x2 holders are sized for 41mm outer dimensions. These simply won't slide into those slots. You'll need a box designed for larger capsules or a tray-style storage system.

Second, **there's no inner foam ring or gasket**. If you're storing half dollars (30.6mm) or other smaller coins, they'll shift around inside. This is a dedicated silver dollar capsule — don't expect versatility across denominations.

One more minor gripe: the capsules don't come with any labeling stickers or inserts, so if you need to identify coins at a glance, you'll need to add your own system.
