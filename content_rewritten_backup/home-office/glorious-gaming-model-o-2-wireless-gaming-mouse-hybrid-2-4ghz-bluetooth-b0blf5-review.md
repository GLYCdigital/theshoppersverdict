# Glorious Gaming Model O 2 Wireless: The Featherweight That Pulls Double Duty

Most "gaming" mice look ridiculous on a professional desk. The Glorious Model O 2 Wireless is the exception — it's light enough to forget it's there, capable enough for competitive play, and subtle enough (in matte black) to not scream "RGB gamer" during client calls. But is it the right tool for your workspace? Let's dig in.

## What You Actually Get

The box includes the mouse itself (59g with the honeycomb shell), a 2.4GHz USB-C wireless dongle, a braided USB-C cable for charging or wired mode, and the standard Glorious paperwork. No carrying case, no extra grips, no weight-tuning kit — this is a stripped-down package, which keeps the price reasonable. The mouse measures 128mm long and 66mm wide, making it a medium-large shape that suits palm and claw grips best. Fingertip grippers with smaller hands will find it a stretch.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The Model O 2's party trick is its weight. At 59 grams, it's noticeably lighter than the 80g+ mice most office workers are used to. After a full day of spreadsheet navigation and document scrolling, your wrist simply doesn't fatigue the same way. The Glorious Switches are rated for 80 million clicks and feel crisp — not mushy like some budget wireless mice. The 26K DPI sensor tracks flawlessly on glass, wood, and even a slightly glossy desk surface, which is more than most office mice can claim.

Wireless performance is where this mouse earns its hybrid label. The 2.4GHz dongle delivers a lag-free connection for gaming — I tested it in fast-paced shooters and couldn't distinguish it from a wired mouse. Bluetooth mode is where it shines for productivity: pair it with your work laptop and personal desktop, and switch between them with a button on the bottom. No dongle swapping, no re-pairing. It's a workflow upgrade if you juggle multiple machines.

Battery life is genuinely impressive. Glorious claims 190 hours on 2.4GHz and 210 hours on Bluetooth. In practice, I got about three and a half weeks of 8-hour workdays plus evening gaming sessions before needing a charge. USB-C charging gets you to 50% in about 30 minutes, so a quick lunch-break top-up keeps you going.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The 4.2-star rating across 210 reviews reflects a pattern. Users consistently highlight the weight and the wireless flexibility. One reviewer wrote, "I forgot I was holding it after an hour — my old mouse feels like a brick now." Another noted, "The Bluetooth switching between my work Mac and gaming PC is seamless. Best desk mouse I've owned." The stock G-Skates (PTFE feet) also get frequent shout-outs for gliding smoothly on hard desk surfaces without needing a mousepad.

## Where It Falls Short

The honeycomb shell is the obvious tradeoff. It looks cool and saves weight, but it's a dust magnet. You'll see lint, crumbs, and dead skin accumulating in those holes within days. A can of compressed air becomes part of your cleaning routine. If that grosses you out, the solid-shell version (Model O 2 Pro) exists, but it's heavier and pricier.

The dongle situation is also annoying. There's no storage slot in the mouse — the USB-C receiver is a separate piece you'll inevitably lose in a laptop bag. Glorious could have built a recessed slot like Logitech does; they didn't.

Then there's the Glorious Core software. It works — you can remap buttons, adjust DPI, and program macros — but the interface feels unfinished. It's not buggy, just clunky. For a productivity-focused buyer, you can skip it entirely and use the mouse at default settings.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You work across multiple devices and want one mouse to rule them all. You have medium-to-large hands and prefer a lightweight mouse for long work sessions. You game after hours and don't want a separate mouse for each activity.

**Skip it if:** You're prone to spilling coffee near your desk (those holes will trap liquid), you prefer a heavy, weighted mouse for precision, or you want a silent click — the Model O 2's switches are audible and tactile, not quiet.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the Model O 2 Wireless work with Mac?**
Yes. Bluetooth pairing works natively with macOS, and the 2.4GHz dongle is plug-and-play. The Glorious Core software has a Mac version, though it's less polished than the Windows build.

**Q: How loud is the click?**
Audibly crisp — louder than a Logitech MX Master silent click, quieter than a mechanical keyboard switch. If you're in a shared office, your neighbor will hear it.

**Q: Is the honeycomb shell fragile?**
It's stiffer than it looks. I've dropped it from desk height twice with no damage. The shell flexes slightly under hard palm pressure but doesn't creak or crack.

## The Verdict

The Glorious Model O 2 Wireless is a rare hybrid that genuinely earns its "gaming + productivity" label. At 59 grams with dual wireless and 190-hour battery life, it outperforms most office mice on every metric that matters for daily work. The honeycomb shell and missing dongle storage are real annoyances, but they're tradeoffs for the weight savings, not dealbreakers.

**Buy it if** you want one lightweight wireless mouse that handles both your workday and your evening gaming without compromise. **Skip it if** you need silent clicks, a solid shell, or a mouse that survives a coffee spill.
