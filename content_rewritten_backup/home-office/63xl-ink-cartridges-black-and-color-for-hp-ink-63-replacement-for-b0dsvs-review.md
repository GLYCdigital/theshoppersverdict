**What's in the Box**

The package contains one black 63XL cartridge and one tricolor 63XL cartridge. Based on the product listing and buyer photos, these are remanufactured units — meaning they're recycled OEM shells refilled with third-party ink. The XL designation matters: you're getting roughly 480 black pages and 330 color pages, which is about double the standard 63 yield. Both cartridges come individually sealed, though as one reviewer noted, "the protective tape on the contacts was a little loose on the color cartridge — I just pressed it back down before installing."

**Real-World Performance**

I've seen enough third-party ink to be skeptical, but the consistent thread across positive reviews is reliability. Users report clean installation with no "ink cartridge problem" errors on Envy 5055, DeskJet 3755, and OfficeJet 3830 models. Print quality holds up for everyday documents — text is crisp down to 10-point font, and color photos are acceptable for school projects or shipping labels, though not gallery-grade.

One buyer put it plainly: "I've used these for six months across three different HP printers. The black runs out faster than I'd like if I'm printing full-page graphics, but for regular office work, it's been flawless." That's the honest tradeoff — this is a workhorse cartridge, not a precision instrument.

**Where It Falls Short**

The 4.3 average isn't perfect for a reason. A small but consistent subset of reviews mention two recurring issues. First, HP firmware updates can trigger "non-genuine HP cartridge" warnings. You can usually dismiss these, but it's an annoyance that doesn't exist with OEM. Second, yield varies by usage pattern — printers that run frequent head-cleaning cycles will burn through black ink faster than the 480-page estimate suggests.

One verified buyer wrote: "Worked great for two months, then the black cartridge stopped being recognized. My printer just wouldn't read it anymore. The color one is still going." That's the risk you accept at roughly half the OEM price.

**Who Should Buy This**

This two-pack makes sense for three specific people: the home office worker printing documents and the occasional label, the student burning through assignments and handouts, and the budget-conscious buyer who doesn't need photo-lab color quality. If you print less than 50 pages a month, you're better off with the standard-yield version — you'll save money upfront and avoid ink drying out in the cartridge.

Skip it if you're a professional photographer, print high-volume marketing materials, or have a printer that's historically finicky with third-party cartridges. For those cases, the $5–$10 savings per cartridge isn't worth the headache.

**FAQ**

**Will these work with my HP Envy 6000 series?**
Yes, the 63XL is cross-compatible with most HP printers that accept the standard 63 cartridge, including Envy, DeskJet, OfficeJet, and Tango models. Double-check your printer's cartridge compatibility in the manual before ordering.

**How many pages will I actually get?**
The XL black is rated for 480 pages and the color for 330 pages, but that's at 5% page coverage — basically standard text documents. Dense graphics or photos will drop that number significantly.

**Do these void my printer warranty?**
Under US law (Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act), using third-party cartridges doesn't void your printer warranty unless the cartridge specifically causes the damage. HP will still service your printer for defects unrelated to the ink.
