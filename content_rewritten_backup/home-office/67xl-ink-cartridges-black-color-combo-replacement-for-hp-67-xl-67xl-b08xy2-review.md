**What's in the Box**

You get two cartridges: one black (67XL) and one tri-color (cyan/magenta/yellow). Both are sealed in foil packaging. Installation is genuinely tool-free — you pop out the old cartridges, peel the orange tab, and snap these in. **Real-World Performance**

I tested these across a mix of text documents, color presentations, and 4×6 photo prints. Black text is crisp — no streaking or banding, even on standard copy paper. Color output leans slightly cool (blues and greens are a touch more vivid than HP originals), but skin tones and gradient-heavy graphics look perfectly acceptable for home office use.

The ink is dye-based, not pigment. That means prints are water-soluble — a spilled coffee will smear your documents. That's standard for HP 67 replacements, but worth knowing if you need archival-quality prints.

**Where It Falls Short**

Here's the tradeoff. Multiple reviewers report page yield runs closer to standard (about 200 pages black, 165 color) than the XL-rated 350/300. You're not getting ripped off — the math still favors this combo — but don't plan around XL capacity.

Second, the color cartridge has a shelf-life issue. If you primarily print black-and-white, the tri-color cartridge can dry out within 2–3 months of sitting idle. HP's own cartridges last longer because their packaging is more airtight. If you're a sporadic printer, this might cost you more in the long run.

Finally, there's no chip reset option — once the cartridge reports low, you can't force it to keep printing like you can with some competitors.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will these void my HP printer warranty?**
A: No. In the US, the Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act prevents manufacturers from voiding warranties for using third-party consumables, as long as the product doesn't cause the damage.

**Q: Do these work with HP Instant Ink?**
A: No. Instant Ink requires cartridges that are tied to your subscription plan. These are standalone cartridges — you'll need to cancel Instant Ink before using them.

**Q: How do I know which HP printers are compatible?**
A: These fit any printer that accepts genuine HP 67 or 67XL cartridges. That includes Deskjet 2700/2800, Envy 6000/6400, and several OfficeJet models. Check your printer's manual or look for the "67" cartridge designation.
