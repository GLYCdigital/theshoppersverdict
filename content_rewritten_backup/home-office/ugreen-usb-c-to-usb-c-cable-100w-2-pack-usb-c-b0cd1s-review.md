### What You Actually Get

The package includes two 3.3-foot USB-C to USB-C cables, a velcro strap for each, and a small instruction card. That's it. No charging brick, no carrying pouch. The cables themselves feature a braided nylon exterior that feels closer to paracord than the cheap plastic wrap you find on gas station cables. The connectors are aluminum with laser-etched UGREEN branding — a nice touch that prevents the "is this the cheap one?" guessing game when your drawer is a graveyard of identical-looking cords.

---

### Performance: Does 100W Actually Mean 100W?

I tested these with a USB-C power meter (because I don't trust specs). Through an Anker 737 power bank, the cable delivered a steady 96.7W to a MacBook Pro 16" — the small loss is normal and within spec. On the Dell XPS 13, it held 87W consistently. Data transfer tops out at 480Mbps (USB 2.0), so if you're planning to move large video files, look elsewhere. This is a charging cable first, a data cable as an afterthought.

The E-Marker chip inside each cable negotiates power properly with the device, which means no "this accessory may not be supported" popups — a problem I've hit with cheaper 100W cables from no-name brands.

---

### Build Quality and Real-World Abuse

I've wrapped these around my laptop charger brick, folded them into a cramped tech pouch, and accidentally yanked one out of a wall adapter while running to catch a train. The strain relief at both ends held up without fraying. The braided sleeve shows zero matting or loose threads. That said, the connector housing is slightly larger than standard — if you use a phone case with a narrow cutout or a tight port on a docking station, you may need to wiggle it a bit.

### Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about limitations. The 3.3-foot length is shorter than it sounds. If your wall outlet is behind a desk or your power bank sits on the floor, you'll feel the stretch. There's no 6.6-foot option in this 2-pack — you'd need to buy UGREEN's single-cable version for that. Also, USB 2.0 data speed is a real compromise. For a cable that costs what this does, I'd have liked at least USB 3.0 (5Gbps) support. It won't matter if you're just charging, but it's a spec-sheet shortfall worth noting.

### Who Should Buy This

- **Remote workers with multiple devices:** Two cables means one stays at the desk, one travels in your bag. You won't have to dig through luggage at a coffee shop.

### FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Will this work with my iPhone 15 or Samsung phone?**
Yes. USB-C is universal — it'll fast-charge phones that support Power Delivery up to 100W. For phones capped at 25W or 45W, it negotiates down automatically.

**Q: Can I use it with a 65W charger instead of a 100W one?**
Absolutely. The cable is rated for up to 100W, but it's backward compatible. It'll only draw what the charger provides.

**Q: Is this certified by USB-IF?**
Yes, UGREEN states these are USB-IF certified, which means they've passed official compliance testing for power delivery and safety. That's a meaningful difference from uncertified cables that can overheat.
