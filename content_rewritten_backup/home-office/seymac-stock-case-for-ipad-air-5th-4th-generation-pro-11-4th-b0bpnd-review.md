Let me save you a scroll: the SEYMAC stock case for iPad Air 5th/4th Gen and Pro 11 is a solid, no-nonsense protective shell that does exactly what it promises — but it's not the sleekest option on the shelf. With a 4.4-star average from 220 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. Here's the breakdown after digging through specs, buyer feedback, and real-world use cases.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the case itself — nothing more, nothing less. No screen protector, no bonus accessories. The case is constructed from a hard polycarbonate back shell fused with a soft TPU bumper around the edges. The front is a tri-fold cover that magnets shut. As shown in the product image, there's a molded slot on the right edge designed specifically for the Apple Pencil (2nd gen), which snaps into place magnetically and charges through the case. The cutouts for the camera, speakers, and charging port are precise — multiple buyers noted the alignment is "spot on," with one reviewer writing, "Every port lines up perfectly, no guessing which side is up."

**Real-world performance**

The dual-layer construction is the star here. The hard back resists flex, and the TPU bumper absorbs drops. I've seen this case survive a waist-height fall onto concrete in a buyer photo — the iPad came out unscathed. The tri-fold front cover converts into a kickstand, and it holds at two angles: roughly 30 degrees for typing and 60 degrees for viewing. It's stable enough for lap use, but don't expect to balance it on a car headrest — the hinge isn't that strong.

The sleep/wake function is conspicuously absent. The front cover does not trigger auto-lock when closed. That's a minor annoyance for a case in this price bracket, and a few 3-star reviews called it out specifically. One buyer put it plainly: "I assumed a magnetic cover meant auto sleep. It doesn't. Adjust your expectations."

**What buyers consistently praise**

The fit is the most common compliment. This case is designed for three devices — iPad Air 4th, Air 5th, and Pro 11 (1st–4th gen) — and it nails all of them. The camera bump sits flush, the buttons are responsive, and the pencil holder is a genuine differentiator. As one verified purchase put it: "Finally a case where my Apple Pencil doesn't fall off in my bag. It clicks in and stays." The drop protection also earned repeated shout-outs, with several reviewers mentioning they'd replaced cheaper cases after a single fall.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. This case is thick. It adds roughly 0.4 inches to the iPad's profile and noticeable weight — the back shell is rigid, not featherweight. If you're coming from a slim folio, you'll feel the difference in your bag. The "stock" texture on the back is a fingerprint magnet. In the dark gray color, it looks smudged within minutes of handling. Not a functional failure, but a cosmetic one.

The price point is competitive, but it's not a budget case. You're paying for the dual-layer build and the integrated pencil slot. If you don't use an Apple Pencil, you're overpaying for a feature you'll never touch.

**Who should buy this**

- **Students and commuters** who toss their iPad into a backpack without a second thought — the drop protection justifies the bulk.
- **Apple Pencil users** who've lost a stylus to a weak magnetic connection — this case locks it in place.

**Who should skip it**

**FAQ**

*Q: Will this case fit the iPad Pro 11 with a screen protector installed?*
A: Yes, most thin glass or PET screen protectors fit without lifting the case edges. Thicker tempered glass with rounded edges is fine; avoid bulky privacy filters.

*Q: Does the Apple Pencil charge while stored in the case?*
A: Yes, the pencil snaps to the magnetic charging strip through the case. It charges, but slightly slower than direct contact. Ensure the pencil is fully seated — off-center placement interrupts charging.

*Q: Can I use the kickstand while holding the iPad in portrait mode?*
A: No. The kickstand only works in landscape orientation. In portrait, the front cover just flaps open with no support.
