**Bottom line up front:** If you run a mid-size meeting room (6–12 people) and your current laptop mic makes remote coworkers feel like they're listening through a tin can, the Logitech Group bundle is a solid, dependable fix. It's not the flashiest or cheapest conference cam on the market, but it nails the fundamentals — clear video, reliable audio expansion, and zero-fuss setup. Just know its limitations before you buy.

## What's actually in the box

The bundle includes the Logitech Group conference cam (the main unit with integrated speaker), two expansion microphones, and all the cabling you need. As shown in the product image, the expansion mics are puck-shaped units that sit on the table and daisy-chain from the main unit. Each mic adds about 7.5 feet of pickup range, taking total coverage to roughly 15 feet from the camera.

The camera itself is a 1080p unit with a 90-degree field of view and 10x HD zoom. It's a fixed-focus lens with auto-light correction — no pan-tilt motors here, which keeps the price down but means you'll physically reposition the camera if your room layout changes.

## Real-world performance

Video quality is genuinely good. The 1080p sensor handles mixed lighting well, and the auto-exposure adjusts quickly when someone near a window washes out. The 90-degree FOV captures a standard 8-foot conference table without cropping anyone out. One experienced buyer put it simply: *"The picture is crisp and the camera doesn't struggle in our dimly lit boardroom."*

The expansion mics are the real selling point. Without them, the main unit's built-in mic tops out around 6–8 feet — fine for four people huddled close, but useless for a full room. With both expansion mics plugged in, far-end participants reported hearing everyone clearly, including people speaking from the far side of the table. One reviewer noted: *"We had eight people in the room and remote attendees said it sounded like we were all sitting right next to the mic."*

Setup is genuinely plug-and-play. USB connection to a laptop or PC, and it's recognized instantly on Zoom, Teams, and Webex. No proprietary software required, no driver downloads. That's a bigger deal than it sounds — I've tested conference cams that require a 20-minute setup ritual before they'll even show video.

## Where it falls short

The speaker is the weakest link. It's fine for a small room with four or five people, but push past that and audio gets thin and slightly echoey. If your meeting room is larger than about 15 feet across, plan to route audio through external speakers or a dedicated soundbar. This is a common complaint in the reviews, and it's a legitimate one.

Also, there's no wireless option. Everything runs through the USB cable, which means the camera needs to sit near your computer. That's fine for most setups, but it limits flexibility if you want to mount the camera across the room.

Finally, the price. This bundle costs meaningfully more than comparable third-party systems from brands like Poly or Jabra. You're paying a premium for Logitech's software reliability and brand trust — which has value, but only if that matters to you.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** You manage a mid-size meeting room (6–12 people), use standard platforms like Zoom or Teams, and want a dependable system that works without IT support. It's especially good for teams that have struggled with audio pickup from laptop mics.

**Skip it if:** You have a large conference room (15+ people), need pan-tilt-zoom camera control, or want a wireless setup. Also skip if you're on a tight budget — there are capable alternatives that cost less, even if they lack Logitech's polish.

## FAQ

**Can I use just one expansion mic?** Yes. The mics daisy-chain, but you can plug in one, two, or none. Each adds range, so start with one and add the second if your room needs it.

**Does it work with Mac?** Yes, it's USB class-compliant and works with both Mac and PC. No special drivers needed.

**Is the camera good for presentations or whiteboards?** The 90-degree lens captures a whiteboard if you position the camera 6–10 feet away, but the 10x zoom is digital, not optical — so don't expect to read fine print from across the room.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Logitech Group is a dependable workhorse for mid-size meeting rooms. It delivers clear video, solid audio pickup with the expansion mics, and effortless setup. The speaker is underwhelming, there's no wireless option, and you'll pay a premium for the brand. But if you need a conference system that just works — and works consistently — this is a safe, proven choice. Buy it if you prioritize reliability over cutting-edge features. Skip it if you need a large-room solution or a bargain.
