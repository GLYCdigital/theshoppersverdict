## What You Actually Get

The box contains 12 fine-tip markers in a rainbow of colors: black, blue, red, green, orange, purple, pink, and several shades in between. The "magnetic" in the name refers to the caps—each one has a small magnet embedded so you can stick them to a metal whiteboard or fridge when not in use. That's a genuinely useful feature if you're tired of markers rolling off your tray.

Each marker is about 5.5 inches long, with a standard barrel diameter that fits most desk organizers. The fine tip is a true 1-2mm line—good for writing notes, not just scribbling bold headlines.

## Performance and Build Quality

The ink flow is consistent out of the box. Colors are vibrant and saturated, especially the blues and purples. The low-odor formula is legit—you can use these in a small office or classroom without your eyes watering. I'd say the scent is barely perceptible unless you hold the marker right up to your nose.

Here's where it gets interesting: these markers are dry-erase, but they also work on glass, mirrors, and laminated surfaces. The ink wipes clean with a standard eraser or even a dry cloth in most cases. However—and this is important—if you let the ink sit for more than a few days, you'll need a damp cloth or some isopropyl alcohol to fully remove it.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the flaws. The fine tip is *fine*—not great. If you're doing detailed technical drawings or architectural sketches, you'll want a higher-end brand like Expo or Artline. The maxtek tips are serviceable but slightly stiff, and they don't produce ultra-crisp lines at the 1mm end.

The magnetic caps are a nice idea, but the magnets are weak. They'll hold the marker to a vertical whiteboard, but a slight bump or vibration will send them dropping. Don't expect to hang them from a metal shelf and have them stay put in a busy environment.

Ink life is moderate. Heavy daily use—like a classroom teacher writing multiple boards per day—will drain a marker in about a week. That's normal for this price point, but you'll need to restock more often than with premium brands.
