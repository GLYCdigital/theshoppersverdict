**What's actually in the box**

The set breaks down into five binder clip sizes (19mm, 25mm, 32mm, 41mm, and 51mm) plus jumbo paper clips in two sizes. You get roughly 120 binder clips and 220 paper clips, though exact counts vary slightly by batch. The binder clips come in classic black metal with the standard squeeze handles. The paper clips are nickel-plated steel, not the cheap colored coated kind that flakes off.

As shown in the product image, everything arrives in a clear plastic organizer case with snap-lock compartments. That's a bigger deal than it sounds — most clip sets in this price range ship loose in a poly bag, and you spend 20 minutes sorting sizes. Here, each size has its own slot, so you can grab what you need without a desk drawer explosion.

**Performance and build quality**

The 51mm jumbo clips are the standout. They clamp down on stacks of 100+ pages without the arms bowing outward. I tested them on a thick bound report (about 1.5 inches) and they held firm for three days with no slippage. The 41mm clips handle standard 500-sheet reams easily. Spring tension is consistent across all sizes, which is rare at this price point — cheaper sets often have one or two sizes that feel weak or gritty.

The paper clips are solid but nothing special. They're smooth, which means they hold standard copy paper well but can slide off high-gloss brochure paper. If you're clipping glossy marketing materials, you'll want the binder clips instead.

Fine for everyday use, but if you're clipping 200-page manuscripts daily, you'll want to upgrade those specifically.
