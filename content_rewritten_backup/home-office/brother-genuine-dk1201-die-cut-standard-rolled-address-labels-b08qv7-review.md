If you own a Brother QL label printer, you already know the frustration of a label roll that jams mid-run or peels apart in your hands. The DK1201 is Brother's answer to that — and with a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, it's clearly working for most people. But is it worth the premium over generic alternatives? Let's dig in.

## What You're Actually Getting

The DK1201 is a single roll of die-cut, standard address labels sized at 62mm × 29mm (about 2.4" × 1.1"). Each roll contains 400 labels, and these are genuine Brother consumables — meaning they're engineered specifically for QL-series printers like the QL-800, QL-820NWB, and QL-1100. The die-cut design means each label is individually cut with a backing that stays intact, unlike continuous rolls where you cut manually.

As shown in the product image, the roll is compact and clearly marked with Brother branding and specs. It's a straightforward consumable — no software, no adapters, just pop it in and print.

## Real-World Performance

The standout feature here is the die-cut perforation. Labels separate cleanly every time, with no ragged edges or backing paper that tears unpredictably. I've processed hundreds of these in a busy shipping office, and the consistent scoring makes a real difference when you're moving fast.

Adhesion is genuinely strong. Labels stick firmly to standard #10 envelopes, poly mailers, and even slightly textured corrugated boxes. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: *"These labels stick and stay stuck. I've never had one peel off in the mail."* That's the experience most users report — the adhesive holds through postal sorting machines without curling or lifting.

Print quality is crisp with Brother's thermal printing technology. Text comes out sharp and dark, and because there's no ink or toner to smudge, labels stay readable even after getting rained on during delivery.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

Across the 240 ratings, a few themes dominate:

- **Reliability**: Multiple reviewers note zero jams or misfeeds, even after months of daily use. One user mentioned: *"I've printed over 5,000 labels with these and never had a single issue."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs.

**Cost per label** is the biggest issue. Brother genuine labels run roughly 30-50% higher per label than third-party compatible rolls. If you're shipping hundreds of packages monthly, that adds up quickly.

**Limited sizing** is another constraint. At 62mm × 29mm, these are strictly for standard addresses. If you need larger shipping labels or custom dimensions for barcodes, you'll need a different SKU — which means keeping multiple roll types in stock.

**No refill option** — the cartridge is single-use. Once the 400 labels are gone, you're buying a new roll. Some competitors offer reloadable cartridges that reduce waste and cost.

## FAQ

**Will these fit my Brother QL-700?**
Yes. The DK1201 is compatible with all QL-series printers that accept DK rolls, including QL-700, QL-800, QL-810W, QL-820NWB, and QL-1100.

**How many labels are on each roll?**
Each DK1201 roll contains 400 labels. At standard address size, that's a solid month of shipping for most small businesses.

**Can I use these with a non-Brother printer?**
No. These are specifically designed for Brother QL thermal printers. They won't fit other label printers or standard inkjet/laser printers.
