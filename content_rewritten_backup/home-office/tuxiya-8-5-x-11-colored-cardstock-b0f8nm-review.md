A: Yes, but set the blade pressure to the “cardstock” setting (usually 4-5 on Cricut) and use a standard grip mat. The 65 lb weight cuts cleanly without tearing, though intricate designs under 1 inch may require a second pass.

**Q: Is it acid-free for scrapbooking?**
A: The listing doesn’t specify acid-free certification, which is a red flag for archival projects. For scrapbooks meant to last decades, spend more on a brand that explicitly states acid-free. For everyday crafts, it’s fine.

**Q: Will it work in a standard home printer?**
A: Yes, both inkjet and laser. Feed it through the manual bypass tray if your printer has one, and set the paper type to “cardstock” or “heavy weight” in your print settings. This prevents ink smudging and ensures proper fuser temperature.
