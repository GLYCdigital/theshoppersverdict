Let's be blunt: most bulk notebooks are junk. Thin covers that curl, spines that crack after three flips, and paper that bleeds through with a fountain pen. The feela 56-pack kraft spine notebooks aren't that. They're not luxury stationery, but they punch well above their price point for anyone who burns through notebooks like I do.

As shown in the product image, you're getting a full case of 56 notebooks with that classic brown kraft cover. The aesthetic is utilitarian in the best way — no glossy branding, no faux-leather nonsense. Just solid, functional notebooks that look good on a shelf and don't scream for attention in a meeting.

**What you actually get**

Open the box and you'll find 56 notebooks, each measuring roughly 8.5 x 5.5 inches — a compact size that fits in a back pocket, purse, or lab coat. The kraft covers are thicker than I expected, probably 12-14 point stock, and they resist bending and scuffing admirably. Each notebook has approximately 60 sheets (120 pages) of lined paper, which gives you a solid 6,720 total pages across the pack.

The "spine" design in the name refers to the glued binding that lets the notebook lay completely flat when open. This isn't a gimmick — it genuinely works. I tested one by opening it to the middle and setting a coffee mug on it. The pages stayed put without any creasing or spine damage.

**Performance and real-world use**

The paper is the weak point, and I'll get to that in a second. But for everyday writing — ballpoint, rollerball, standard gel pens — it performs fine. The lines are printed consistently, and the ruling is about 7mm, which gives you a comfortable writing area without feeling cramped.

I've been using a few of these for daily journaling and meeting notes for three weeks now. The covers still look fresh, the spines haven't cracked, and the pages aren't falling out. That's more than I can say for some $5 single notebooks I've bought from big-box stores.

**Where it falls short**

Here's the honest part. The paper is thin — roughly 60gsm, maybe less. If you use gel pens with heavy ink flow, you'll see ghosting on the back of the page. Markers and highlighters will bleed through. This is a bulk notebook, not a journaling-grade product, so adjust your expectations accordingly.

Second, there's no elastic closure, no ribbon bookmark, no pocket in the back. For a pack this size, that's a reasonable tradeoff, but if you need those features, look elsewhere.

Third, the lines are a standard blue-gray color. If you prefer dotted grid or blank pages, this isn't the pack for you — it's lined only.
