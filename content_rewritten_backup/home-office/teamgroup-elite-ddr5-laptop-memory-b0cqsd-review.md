## FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Will this work in my laptop?**
Check your laptop's spec sheet for "SO-DIMM DDR5" support and the maximum speed rating. Most 12th-gen Intel and Ryzen 6000/7000 series laptops support 4800MT/s. If your laptop shipped with DDR4, this won't fit—different physical slot.

**Q: Can I mix this with my existing RAM?**
Technically yes, but it's not recommended. Mixing different brands, speeds, or capacities can force both modules to run at the slower speed. For best results, buy a matched pair or use this as a single-stick upgrade.

**Q: Is 4800MT/s fast enough in 2026?**
For 95% of productivity tasks, absolutely. The bottleneck in most laptops is storage and CPU, not RAM speed. Unless you're doing heavy video editing or playing games that rely on integrated graphics, you won't notice the difference between 4800 and 5600MT/s.
