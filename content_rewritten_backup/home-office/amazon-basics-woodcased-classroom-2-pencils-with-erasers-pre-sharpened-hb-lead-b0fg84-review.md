**How it performs when you actually write**

I put these through a week of daily note-taking, sketching, and standardized test-style bubble filling. The HB lead is the sweet spot for classroom use: firm enough to hold a point for a reasonable stretch, soft enough to write without pressing through the paper. The erasers surprised me. Most bulk pencils come with erasers that smear lead across the page like butter on toast.
