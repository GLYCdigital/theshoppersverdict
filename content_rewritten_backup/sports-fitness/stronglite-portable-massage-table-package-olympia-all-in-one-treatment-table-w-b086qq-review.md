**The STRONGLITE Olympia Portable Massage Table: The 1,000-Pound Question**

Most portable massage tables under $300 make a promise they can't keep. They claim "heavy-duty" construction, then buckle under a 200-pound client. The STRONGLITE Olympia Package doesn't have that problem. Rated for 1,000 pounds of static weight, this is the rare table that actually over-delivers on its specs — and it shows in the 4.7-star average across 235 Amazon ratings.

**What's Actually in the Box**

This is the "All-In-One" package, so you're not buying a bare table. You get the Olympia table itself, a multi-density 5-inch foam cushion, a face cradle with two headrest options, a bolster, a fitted sheet set, and a padded carry bag. That's roughly $150 worth of accessories included — which makes the value proposition clear right out of the gate.

The table measures 73 inches long and 30 inches wide when fully assembled. The frame is aircraft-grade aluminum with a cross-brace design. It folds down to 33 x 19 x 9 inches — small enough to fit in most car trunks, though the included bag adds bulk.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The Olympia's foundation is its frame. The aluminum alloy legs lock into place with a spring-loaded mechanism that feels solid — no wobble, no creaking, even when a client shifts their weight during a deep-tissue session. The 5-inch cushion is the other standout. It's a layered foam construction (firm base, medium top layer) that provides enough give for comfort without sacrificing the support you need to work effectively.

One detail that impressed me: the upholstery is a heavy-duty vinyl that doesn't stretch or pill, even after months of oil-based massage products. That's a common failure point on cheaper tables, and STRONGLITE clearly invested here.

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

The stability is the recurring theme in customer feedback. One verified buyer wrote: "I'm 250 pounds and I've had clients close to 300 on this table. It doesn't budge." Another noted: "The carrying case actually fits everything, which is rare. My last table's bag was useless."

Portability is the second most-cited win. At 32 pounds, the Olympia is light enough for home-visit therapists, but heavy enough to feel substantial. It's a sweet spot that many tables miss.

**Where It Falls Short**

The face cradle cover is the weakest link. It's a thin PU material that doesn't breathe well and shows wear quickly. Plan to spend $20-30 on a replacement cover within your first six months — most serious buyers do.

Taller practitioners should note the height range: 25 to 34 inches. If you're over 6 feet, you'll likely want the leg extenders (sold separately) to get the table to a comfortable working height. That's an extra cost to factor in.

The included carry bag gets the job done, but it's not built for daily commuting. The single shoulder strap digs in on long walks, and there's no backpack-style option. For occasional transport, it's fine. For full-time mobile practice, budget for a better case.

**Who Should Buy This**

**The right fit:** New massage therapists building their first mobile kit. Home-based practitioners who need a reliable table for occasional clients. Students who want one table that'll last through school and into their first few years of practice. Anyone who values stability and doesn't want to upgrade in 18 months.

**Skip it if:** You're a full-time mobile therapist doing 15+ sessions weekly — you'll want a lighter table (under 25 lbs) with a better carry system. Or if you're over 6'2" and don't want to spend extra on leg extenders.
