**Title:** Abu Garcia C3 Species Special Baitcast Reel Review

**Meta Description:** Abu Garcia C3 Species Special baitcast reel review: Swedish-built round reel with dual-brake system, 4.6/5 rating. Real angler pros, cons & verdict.

---

The Abu Garcia C3 Species Special is the reel you buy when you're done chasing gimmicks and just want something that works. It's a Swedish-built round reel with a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, and it carries the DNA of the legendary 6500 C3 that's been hauling in fish for decades. But this isn't just a nostalgia play — the "Species Special" badge means it's tuned for multi-species versatility. Here's the honest breakdown.

## What's in the Box

You get the reel itself (weighing in at 10.8 ounces), a spare spool, and the standard Abu Garcia paperwork. No rod, no line, no frills. The 4.6:1 gear ratio is a deliberate choice — this is a cranking reel, not a speed demon. If you're throwing fast-moving lures that need quick retrieve, this isn't it. But for spinnerbaits, crankbaits, and live bait rigs, that low ratio translates to serious torque.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The frame is machined aluminum, not the cheaper graphite you'll find on most reels in this price bracket. The Swedish engineering shows in the tolerances — the spool spins true with zero wobble, and the Carbon Matrix drag system delivers smooth pressure from 1 pound all the way up to its 12-pound max. That's a wide enough window to fight a feisty bass on light line or put the brakes on a channel cat.

The dual-brake system is where this reel earns its keep. You get both centrifugal and magnetic brakes working together, which means you can dial in casting control for anything from a 1/4-ounce crankbait to a 3/4-ounce spinnerbait without swapping spools. In practice, backlash is rare even for anglers who haven't touched a round reel in years. The 5+1 bearing system is smooth — not "buttery" like a $300 Daiwa, but respectable for the price point.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The durability stories are what stand out across the reviews. One angler put it simply: **"I've owned Abu Garcia reels for 30 years and this one feels like the classic 6500 but with modern updates. It's built like a tank."** Another mentioned the spare spool as a deciding factor: **"Being able to swap spools with different line weights without re-spooling is a game-changer for river fishing."** The consensus is that this reel handles saltwater exposure better than most in its class, thanks to the corrosion-resistant bearings.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The 4.6:1 gear ratio feels sluggish if you're accustomed to modern high-speed reels — you'll be cranking more to keep up with fast-moving lures. The weight is also noticeable; at nearly 11 ounces, it'll tip the balance of lighter rods forward. And while the dual brakes are effective, the external magnetic dial is stiff to adjust on the fly, especially with cold, wet hands.

One reviewer noted: **"Great reel, but the handle doesn't fold down. That's fine for a dedicated casting setup, but it makes storage in a rod tube awkward."** That's a fair point — this isn't a reel you'll easily tuck away for travel.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Crankbait and spinnerbait anglers** who want torque and control over raw speed
- **Multi-species fishermen** who fish freshwater and light saltwater and need one reel to do it all
- **Anglers who appreciate durability** — this is a buy-it-once reel, not a replace-every-season budget option
- **Anyone who fishes from a boat or bank** with heavier rods that can balance the weight

**Skip it if** you're a finesse angler throwing weightless plastics all day, or if you need a fast-retrieve reel for burning topwater baits. This is a tool with a specific job.

## FAQ

**Is this reel good for beginners?**
Yes, with a learning curve. The dual-brake system is forgiving, but round reels sit differently in the hand than low-profile baitcasters. Expect a few birds' nests in your first session.

**Can it handle saltwater?**
It's rated for light saltwater use, and the corrosion-resistant bearings hold up. Just rinse it thoroughly after each trip and re-oil regularly.

**Does the spare spool come pre-loaded with line?**
No, it's empty. But having a second spool pre-spooled with different line weights (say, 12-pound mono and 20-pound braid) is the whole point.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a dependable, versatile round reel that'll outlast three budget reels. The 4.6-star rating isn't hype — it's earned.

**Skip it if** you need high-speed retrieve or ultralight weight, or if you prefer the compact profile of low-profile baitcasters.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It's not perfect, but it's honest, built to last, and performs exactly as advertised. For the angler who values reliability over flash, this is a solid investment.
