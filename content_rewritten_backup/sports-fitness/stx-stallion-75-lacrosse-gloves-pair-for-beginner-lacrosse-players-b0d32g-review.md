# STX Stallion 75 Lacrosse Gloves: The Smart Starter's Choice (With a Catch)

If you're outfitting a new youth or high school lacrosse player, the glove aisle is a minefield of $150 pro-stock models that look cool but are overkill for someone still learning to cradle. The STX Stallion 75 sits firmly in the budget-friendly tier, and with a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, it's clearly doing something right. But "beginner" doesn't have to mean "cheaply made"—and these gloves prove that point, with a few caveats worth knowing before you click buy.

## What's in the Box

You get a single pair of gloves (left and right) in standard team colors—black, white, or navy are the usual suspects. There are no liners, no extra pads, no ball. Just the gloves, ready to break in. Sizing runs from Youth Small up to Senior Large, so measure your player's hand from the base of the palm to the tip of the middle finger before ordering. STX's size chart is accurate, but if you're between sizes, size up—these run slightly snug out of the box.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The Stallion 75 uses a two-piece thumb and a single-layer backhand pad with strategic foam inserts. That's not pro-level protection, but it's genuinely sufficient for youth and beginner high school play. The palms are a synthetic suede material that offers solid stick feel without the premium price tag of leather. On the field, that means you can feel the ball in your pocket during ground balls and passing drills, but you won't get the tackiness of a $200 gamer glove.

The backhand flex is where these gloves surprised me. They're not stiff like some budget options; the articulated fingers allow for natural hand movement, which is critical for beginners learning to snap their wrists on passes. One buyer noted, *"My son moved from a cheaper brand to these and immediately noticed he could bend his fingers easier. His passing accuracy improved within a week."* That's the kind of feedback that tells you the design is working.

Ventilation is decent—mesh panels between the fingers keep hands from turning into sweat sponges during summer practices. Not elite-level breathability, but fine for the price point.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 235 ratings, a few themes dominate:

- **Value for money:** *"These are half the price of the gloves my older son wears, but they feel 80% as good. Perfect for a freshman just starting."*
- **Durability beyond expectations:** Multiple reviewers mention surviving a full season of youth play with minimal wear. The palm material holds up better than expected for synthetic.
- **Fit consistency:** Parents appreciate that the sizing is true to STX's chart, reducing the guesswork when buying online.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The protection is adequate, not exceptional. A hard shot to the back of the hand will sting more than it would in a pro-stock glove. The wrist strap is a basic hook-and-loop closure—functional but not the adjustable cinch system you'll find on higher-end models. If your player is already taking heavy checks in a competitive high school league, these are not the right gloves.

Some buyers also note the break-in period takes a few practices. They're not stiff to the point of being unusable, but they won't feel broken-in on day one. And the color options are limited—if your team uses a specific accent color, you may need to look elsewhere.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Youth players (ages 8–12)** just starting in rec or town leagues—the protection and fit are ideal for this level.
- **High school freshmen or JV players** who need a reliable pair without blowing the budget.
- **Parents buying for a player who's still growing**—you won't feel bad replacing these in a year or two.

## Who Should Skip

- **Varsity or elite club players** taking regular contact—you need more backhand protection.
- **Players with wider hands**—the snug fit may be uncomfortable; consider a different brand with a roomier cut.
- **Anyone needing specific team colors** beyond black, white, or navy.
