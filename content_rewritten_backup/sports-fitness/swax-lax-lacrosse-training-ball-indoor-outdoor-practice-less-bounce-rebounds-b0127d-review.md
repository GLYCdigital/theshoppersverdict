If you've ever tried to run wall ball drills in a shared space, you know the problem: one errant rebound and you're patching drywall or apologizing to a neighbor. The SWAX LAX training ball solves that with a simple, effective design tweak — it's a regulation-weight ball that just doesn't bounce much. That's the entire pitch, and honestly, it works.

**What you actually get**

The box contains a single ball. No pump, no accessories, no carrying bag. It's a vulcanized rubber ball that matches regulation dimensions — 7.75 inches around, right at 5 ounces. The surface has a slightly tacky, grippy texture that feels close to a standard game ball in your stick. The weight distribution is what matters most: it's not a lightweight foam trainer or a weighted overload ball. It's a deadened regulation ball, which means your stick skills transfer directly.

**Performance in the real world**

The reduced bounce is the headline feature, and it delivers. Drop it from chest height and it barely reaches your knees. This makes it excellent for basement wall ball sessions, hotel room warmups, or any tight space where a standard ball would ricochet dangerously. The ball tracks true off the wall — no weird wobble or dead spots — so your passing and catching mechanics stay honest.

The durability has held up well in testing and customer reports. One reviewer mentioned using it daily against a brick wall for months with only surface scuffing. The tacky texture does pick up dust and dirt quickly, especially outdoors, but a quick wipe restores the grip.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The most common thread in the 235 ratings is the "game-feel" factor. As one verified buyer put it, "It feels exactly like a real lacrosse ball in my stick, just without the bounce. Perfect for practicing in my garage without worrying about breaking anything." Another noted, "My son uses this every day in the basement. The lack of rebound means he actually has to work on his cradle and catch rather than just chasing the ball."

**Where it falls short**

The deadened feel cuts both ways. When you switch back to a regulation ball, there's an adjustment period — the standard ball pops off your stick faster and rebounds harder than you've trained for. Some players may find this transition frustrating. The ball also has a slightly "thuddy" feel on impact that takes a session or two to get used to.

It's also not a game-legal ball, so this is strictly a practice tool. And if you're looking for a weighted trainer to build arm strength, this isn't that — it's exactly regulation weight, just with reduced bounce.

**Who should buy this**

- **The garage warrior**: Anyone who wants to put in reps without destroying their space or annoying their household.
- **Youth players**: Parents who want their kids practicing wall ball without the constant ball-chasing that comes with standard rebounds.
- **Traveling players**: If you're in hotel rooms or unfamiliar spaces, this packs easily and won't cause damage.
- **Not for**: Players who need game-legal balls for scrimmages, or anyone wanting an overload/weighted training tool.

**FAQ**

*Q: Does this ball work outdoors on concrete?*
A: Yes. The vulcanized rubber handles concrete and asphalt well. You'll see surface scuffing, but the ball maintains its shape and bounce characteristics over months of use.

*Q: Will it damage my stick or mesh?*
A: No more than a regulation ball. The weight and surface feel are nearly identical, so pocket wear is comparable.

*Q: How different is the feel from a standard lacrosse ball?*
A: In your stick, it feels regulation. On impact, it's noticeably deader — roughly 75% less bounce. Your catch and cradle mechanics stay the same, but you'll need a short adjustment period when switching back.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want to practice anywhere, anytime, without worrying about rebounds destroying your space or wasting time chasing balls. It's a smart, focused training tool that does exactly what it claims.

**Skip it if** you only practice on open fields with room to roam, or if you need a game-legal ball for full-speed play.
