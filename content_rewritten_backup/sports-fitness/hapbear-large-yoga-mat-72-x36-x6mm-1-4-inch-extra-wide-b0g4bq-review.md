## What You Actually Get

The mat ships rolled with a simple carrying strap — no bag, no blocks, no gimmicks. Unrolled, it measures a full 6 feet long and 3 feet wide, with a 6mm (roughly 1/4-inch) thickness. The material is TPE (thermoplastic elastomer), which is lighter than rubber and free of the strong "new mat" smell that PVC mats often have. The top surface has a subtle crosshatch texture; the bottom uses a different, slightly grippier pattern to anchor it to the floor.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this mat on both a hardwood floor and a low-pile carpet. The bottom grip is the standout feature — it doesn't slide even during fast Vinyasa transitions. On the top, the texture provides enough traction for downward dog without grabbing your palms so aggressively that you can't adjust your hand position. The 6mm thickness is a sweet spot: your knees and spine feel protected in tabletop or prone poses, but you still get enough ground feedback for balancing poses like tree or half-moon. 

One pleasant surprise: the width genuinely changes your alignment. In wide-legged forward folds or crescent lunges, your back foot stays fully on the mat — no more micro-adjustments to keep your heel from kissing the floor. Buyers echo this. ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, it's heavy — about 6.6 lbs. That's roughly 2 lbs more than a standard 5mm PVC mat. If you're carrying it to a studio on foot or by bike, you'll feel it. The included strap helps, but it's not a bag.

Second, TPE is a softer material than rubber or PVC. After a few weeks of use, you may notice crease marks where the mat folds, and scuffs from shoes or props show more readily. It's cosmetic, not functional, but if you're obsessive about a pristine mat, this will bother you.

Third, the texture isn't for everyone. A handful of reviewers mentioned that the top surface feels slightly "sticky" during hot yoga when hands get sweaty — it grips *too* well and can make transitions feel sticky. If you practice hot yoga regularly, a towel is still recommended.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the 36-inch width fit in standard yoga mat straps or bags?**  
A: No. Most straps are designed for 24-inch mats. The included strap works, but if you want a bag, look for one labeled "extra wide" or "large" — standard bags will be too snug.

**Q: Is TPE safe for sensitive skin?**  
A: Generally, yes. TPE is latex-free and phthalate-free, which is why it lacks the chemical smell of PVC. If you have severe contact allergies, patch-test a corner first, but it's one of the more hypoallergenic mat materials.

**Q: How do I clean it?**  
A: Wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap, then air dry. Avoid machine washing or soaking — TPE degrades with prolonged water exposure. A spray of 1:3 vinegar-water works well for deep cleaning.
