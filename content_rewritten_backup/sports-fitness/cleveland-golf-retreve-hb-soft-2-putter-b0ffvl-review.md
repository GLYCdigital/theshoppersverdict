Let me be blunt: most putters are just sticks with a flat face. The Cleveland Retreve HB Soft 2 is different — it has a ball-retrieval slot built into the sole, which sounds gimmicky until you use it. And at 4.6 stars from 229 Amazon ratings, it's clearly not a novelty act.

**What You Actually Get**

Cleveland ships this as a complete putter — no headcover included (which annoys some buyers, so budget for one). The mallet head is milled from 304 stainless steel, with the signature HB Soft 2 insert in the face. That insert is a thermoplastic elastomer that Cleveland says is 20% softer than their previous generation. The shaft is a standard steel model with a pistol-style grip that has a subtle texture — not tacky, not slick.

The headline feature is the retrieval slot machined into the sole. You press the back of the putter against a ball in the cup or on the fringe, and it pops up enough to grab. It works. I tested it on a practice green and in the rough; the ball seats into the slot and lifts cleanly. For anyone with back issues or who just hates bending over, this is genuinely useful.

**How It Performs**

The HB Soft 2 insert is the star here. Impact feels muted and plush — almost like a premium insert putter from Odyssey or TaylorMade. Off-center hits don't produce that harsh "clack" you get from some milled faces. Distance control is predictable; the mallet head has a high MOI, so mishits toward the toe still roll out respectably.

The stroke-balanced design means the face stays square through the stroke — ideal for players with a straight-back, straight-through putting motion. If you have a strong arc, you might find it slightly resistant to rotation, but it's not a dealbreaker.

One thing to note: the retrieval slot adds mass to the back of the head. On fast greens, I found myself leaving lag putts short by a foot or two until I adjusted. It's not a flaw — it's a weighting tradeoff you need to adapt to.

**What Buyers Keep Saying**

The retrieval feature gets consistent love. One verified buyer wrote: "I'm 62 with a bad back — this putter saves me 20 bends per round. The feel is a bonus." Another noted: "The soft insert took a round to get used to, but now I can't miss inside 6 feet."

Criticism is mostly about the alignment aid. The single sightline is simple — some players said they'd prefer a more complex alignment system. A few also mentioned the lack of a headcover as a minor annoyance.

**Where It Falls Short**

- **Weight distribution:** The slot makes the head feel heavier than the listed 365g. If you prefer a lighter putter for feel, this will take adjustment.
- **Alignment options:** One sightline only. No plumber's neck, no multi-line setup.
- **Aesthetics:** The mallet shape is utilitarian, not flashy. If you want a showpiece, look elsewhere.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Golfers with back or knee issues:** The retrieval slot is a genuine ergonomic win. You'll feel the difference by the 12th hole.
- **Mid-to-high handicappers:** The high MOI and soft insert mask mishits and improve distance control.
- **Anyone who plays fast greens:** Once you adjust to the weight, the soft feel excels on slick surfaces.

**Skip it if:** You're a low handicapper who demands a specific head weight or arc, or you need complex alignment aids. This is a game-improvement putter, not a tour-precision tool.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the ball retrieval slot work on the fringe?**
A: Yes — press it against a ball on short grass and it pops up. On longer rough, it's less reliable; you may need to nudge the ball to a cleaner spot first.

**Q: Is the grip replaceable?**
A: It's a standard .580" diameter shaft, so yes — any pistol or oversize grip will fit without special tools.

**Q: How does it compare to the previous HB Soft model?**
A: The insert is softer in the 2.0 version, and the mallet shape is slightly more compact. The retrieval slot is unchanged. If you liked the old feel, you'll like this more.

**The Verdict**

The Cleveland Retreve HB Soft 2 is a niche putter that does its job exceptionally well. The ball retrieval feature is not a gimmick — it's a practical tool that improves the pace of play and saves your back. The soft insert and high MOI make it forgiving and pleasant to use.

**Buy it if** you want a comfortable, forgiving putter that also saves you from bending over 20 times a round.

**Skip it if** you're a purist who wants a traditional blade or needs a specific alignment system.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Recommended for the vast majority of recreational golfers. It's not perfect, but it's honest, functional, and well-built.
