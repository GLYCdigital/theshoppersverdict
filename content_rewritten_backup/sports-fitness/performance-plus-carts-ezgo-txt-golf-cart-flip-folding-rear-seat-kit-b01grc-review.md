**What You Actually Get**

Open the box and you'll find the pre-assembled flip seat frame, two full-width bench cushions, a backrest, and a hardware kit. The steel frame is powder-coated black — it's not glossy show chrome, but it's durable and matches most stock carts. The seat measures a genuine 37 inches wide, so two adults fit without shoulder bumping. As shown in the product image, the seat flips forward to reveal a flat cargo deck that measures roughly 18 inches deep — enough for a golf bag, a cooler, or a week's worth of groceries.

**Real-World Performance**

The flip mechanism is the star here. It's a simple spring-assisted hinge that locks into place with a solid click. Owners consistently mention how easy it is to operate one-handed while holding a putter. The frame itself is 16-gauge steel — noticeably thicker than the cheap imports that flex under weight. I've seen reports of 400+ pound combined passengers with zero frame deflection.

Installation is the tradeoff. The bracket bolts align with the existing EZGO TXT rear body holes, which is a genuine timesaver. But the hardware isn't labeled, and the instructions are basic line drawings. Plan for 2-3 hours if you're handy, longer if you're not. Several reviewers note that the seat back and seat base need simultaneous alignment — recruit a helper or use a jack to hold the frame while you thread bolts.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews is earned. The most repeated compliments:

- *"This thing is solid. My kids stand on the seat to climb over the back — zero flex."*

The powder coating also gets consistent shoutouts — carts live outside, and this seat holds up to sun and rain without chalking or rusting.

**Where It Falls Short**

The lack of a grab bar is the biggest miss. Rear-facing passengers on a moving cart have nothing to hold onto except the seat back or your shoulders. Performance Plus sells a separate grab bar kit, but at this price point, it should be included.

The cushions are also firmer than stock. They're fine for a 20-minute ride from hole 1 to hole 18, but if you're planning hour-long trail rides, you'll feel every bump. One reviewer summed it up: *"Great seat for golf. Terrible for a 2-hour beer run."*

And the hardware — bring your own zip ties and patience. The included bolts are adequate, but the lack of labeling means you'll spend 20 minutes sorting fasteners before you start.

**Who Should Buy This**

**Skip It If:**

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this fit my EZGO TXT with a lifted suspension?**
A: Yes. The seat mounts to the rear body, not the suspension. Lifts don't affect fit.

**Q: Can I install this by myself?**
A: Technically, yes. Realistically, no. The seat back and base need simultaneous bolt alignment. Use a floor jack to support the frame, or bribe a friend with a cold drink.

**Q: Does the flip seat affect the cart's weight capacity?**
A: The frame is rated for 500 pounds. The cart's stock rear suspension is the limiting factor, not this seat.
