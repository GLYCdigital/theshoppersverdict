Most scorebooks fall into one of two traps: they're either flimsy spiral notebooks that fall apart by midseason, or they're overbuilt binders with more tabs than a tax return. The CHAMPRO Baseball Score Book sits deliberately in the middle, and for most youth, high school, and travel ball coaches, that's exactly the right spot.

## What You Actually Get

The book measures 9 x 11 inches — slightly larger than standard letter size, which matters when you're jotting in cramped dugouts. It ships with 30 full scoring sheets, each with spaces for both teams, batting order, player positions, and a dedicated pitch count column per inning. That last feature is the quiet MVP here. Most scorebooks in this price range bury pitch counts in the margin; CHAMPRO gives you a proper column, which means you're not doing mental math in the third inning.

The spiral binding is the real deal — metal wire, not the plastic coil that snaps on cold mornings. It lays completely flat on a bench or clipboard. The paper is 100gsm, noticeably thicker than the tissue-thin pages on cheaper books, and it holds up to pen pressure without ghosting through to the next page.

## Real-World Performance

I've scored games with this book in direct sun, light drizzle, and the chaos of a 12U tournament doubleheader. The vinyl cover repels moisture well enough, though it's not waterproof — a hard rain will seep through the spiral holes. The layout is intuitive: each inning has clear boxes for runs, hits, errors, and left on base, plus that pitch count column. One reviewer noted, "Finally a book that doesn't make me flip pages to find where I am," and that tracks with my experience. The flow is logical, which matters more than most coaches admit.

## Where It Falls Short

Honest gripes first. There's no dedicated section for defensive substitutions or pinch-hitter tracking. If you coach a team where kids rotate positions every inning, you'll be scribbling in margins. The soft vinyl cover scuffs easily against other gear — it's not a hardcover, so treat it accordingly. And while 30 sheets sounds like plenty, tournament-heavy schedules burn through it fast; you'll likely need a second book by June.

## Who Should Buy This

This is squarely aimed at youth, high school, and recreational league coaches who score every game and need a reliable, no-fuss book. It's also a solid choice for parents who want to keep a season record. If you're a college or pro scout tracking advanced metrics like pitch type sequences or spray charts, you'll outgrow this quickly — look for a premium scouting book instead.

## FAQ

**Does it work for softball too?**
Yes. The layout is diamond-agnostic. You'll use the same boxes for runs, hits, and pitch counts. No softball-specific adjustments needed.

**How many innings per sheet?**
Each page covers one full game for both teams, with standard 9-inning grids. Extra inning boxes are in the margin.

**Is the binding durable enough for heavy use?**
The metal spiral is solid, but the vinyl cover is the weak point. Slip it in a plastic sleeve if you're rough on gear.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you're a coach who wants a clean, durable scorebook with a pitch count column and paper that won't bleed through. It's the best value in its category.

**Skip it if** you need advanced stat tracking, defensive substitution logs, or a hardcover that shrugs off a gear bag's abuse.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A legitimate workhorse that does exactly what it promises, with only minor compromises that won't bother most buyers.
