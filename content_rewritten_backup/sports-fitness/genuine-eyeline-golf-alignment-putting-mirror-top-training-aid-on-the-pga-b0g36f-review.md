The putting stroke is the most mechanically simple motion in golf — and the hardest to trust. You *feel* like your eyes are over the ball. You *feel* square to the target. Then you miss a four-footer and realize your feel is lying to you. The EyeLine Golf Alignment Putting Mirror exists to call you out on that lie. Here's the real breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The unit is a rigid, flat mirror plate roughly 16 inches long with printed alignment guides, eye-position markers, and a ball track down the center. It ships with a small guide booklet. There's no laser, no app, no sensors — it's a mirror with lines on it. That's the entire product. For the price, that minimalism is either refreshing or frustrating, depending on what you expected.

**Performance: What It Does Well**

The genius of this mirror is that it doesn't try to fix your stroke. It shows you what your stroke *is*. When you set up and look down, you instantly see where your eyes sit relative to the ball — most people discover they're way outside the line they thought they were on. The shoulder alignment lines do the same thing for your upper body. That visual feedback is immediate, harsh, and incredibly useful.

The build itself is solid. The base has a rubberized grip that stays planted on carpet and practice turf — I didn't experience any sliding during normal use. The mirror clarity is good when you get a clean unit, and the printed guides are sharp and accurate to the claimed dimensions.

**Where It Falls Short**

Here's the problem: the 3.8-star rating isn't from people who found the concept flawed. It's from quality control issues. Multiple verified purchases mention scratched mirror surfaces right out of the box, and a few report the ball track being slightly too narrow, causing the ball to sit awkwardly. One reviewer noted their unit arrived with a visible warp that distorted the reflection. That's a legitimate concern — if you're buying a mirror for precision feedback, a defective surface defeats the entire purpose.

Also, this is a practice tool, not a magic fix. You have to put in the reps. If you're looking for instant improvement, you'll be disappointed. It shows you what's wrong; it doesn't fix it for you.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The serious amateur** who practices putting regularly and wants objective feedback on setup and stroke path
- **The visual learner** who responds better to seeing their alignment than feeling it
- **The coach or instructor** looking for a durable teaching aid for multiple students

**Skip It If**

- You're a casual golfer who plays a few times a year and just wants a quick tip
- You're easily frustrated by tools that require consistent practice to yield results

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this actually help lower scores?**
A: It helps if you use it consistently. It's a diagnostic tool — it reveals alignment and eye-position issues. If you correct those and practice the fix, yes, your putting will improve. If you use it once and put it in the closet, it's a waste of money.

**Q: Is it better than a cheaper generic putting mirror?**
A: The EyeLine has more precise printed guides and a sturdier build than most budget options. But given the QC complaints, a generic mirror at half the price might be a smarter buy if you're just starting out.

**Q: Will this work on my living room carpet?**
A: Yes. The base grips well on standard carpet. The ball rolls a bit slower than on a putting green, but the alignment feedback is unaffected.
