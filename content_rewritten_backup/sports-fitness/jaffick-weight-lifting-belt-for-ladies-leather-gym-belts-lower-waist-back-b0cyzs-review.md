## What You Actually Get

The Jaffick belt is a 4mm genuine leather belt with a tapered profile — wider in the back (roughly 6 inches) and narrower in the front (4 inches). That taper is the secret. It supports your lumbar spine where you need it, then narrows so you can still hinge at the hips without the belt digging into your lower ribs.

The closure system uses a double-prong roller buckle with a leather keeper loop. You also get the standard sizing holes drilled through the leather, so there's room to adjust as your body changes.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The leather is the real deal — not that bonded junk that peels after three sessions. It's stiff initially. Like, *needs to be worn around the house while watching TV* stiff. But after a couple weeks of consistent use, it molds to your torso. That's the tradeoff with quality leather: break-in period for a custom-ish fit.

Under a heavy deadlift set, the belt holds its shape. No rolling, no buckling at the edges. The double-prong buckle is the right call here — single prongs can slip under 300+ pound pulls. This one locks in and stays.

The 4-inch front taper genuinely matters for women with shorter torsos. Standard 4-inch-everywhere belts push into the diaphragm when you're folded over in a deadlift setup. This one clears the ribs and lets you brace properly.

## Where It Falls Short

**Sizing is the biggest complaint.** Multiple reviewers mention ordering up. If you wear a size 28 jeans, don't order a small — you'll likely want a medium or even large depending on where you carry weight. The brand's size chart runs optimistic. Measure your waist at navel height and add 2-3 inches before picking.

**The break-in period is real.** If you need a belt for a competition next week, this isn't it. The stiff leather needs time to conform. Plan for 2-3 weeks of regular training before it feels truly comfortable.

**The leather keeper loop** is purely cosmetic. It slides around and doesn't hold the strap end flush against the belt. Minor annoyance, not a functional issue.
