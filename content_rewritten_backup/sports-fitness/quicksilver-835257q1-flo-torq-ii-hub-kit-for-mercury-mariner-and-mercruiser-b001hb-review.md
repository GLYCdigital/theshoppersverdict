# Quicksilver 835257Q1 Flo-Torq II Hub Kit Review: The Quiet Fix for Your Mercury Outboard

If you've ever sat at the dock with a Mercury outboard idling and heard that annoying *clack-clack-clack* coming from your prop, you already know the problem. That's your propeller hub rattling against the shaft, and it's not just annoying — it's a sign of wear that can lead to bigger issues. The Quicksilver 835257Q1 Flo-Torq II Hub Kit is the factory-engineered answer, and with a 4.7-star average across 235 ratings, it's clearly working for a lot of boaters.

## What You Actually Get

This is a complete replacement hub kit, not a band-aid. In the box you'll find the precision-machined aluminum hub body, a vulcanized rubber dampener insert, a stainless steel thrust washer, and the necessary retaining hardware. It's designed to replace the hub assembly on Mercury, Mariner, and MerCruiser props — specifically those with the Flo-Torq II system. The kit fits standard Mercury spline shafts (often called "large" spline), so if you're running a 75hp to 300hp outboard or a MerCruiser stern drive, this is likely your match.

## Performance and Build Quality

The Flo-Torq II design uses a rubber insert that absorbs torsional shock between the prop and the shaft. What that means in practice: smoother shifting, less drivetrain stress, and — the feature most buyers mention first — no more prop rattle at idle. One reviewer put it simply: *"The clatter I'd been hearing at the dock for two seasons disappeared completely. Quietest my old Mercury has been in years."*

The aluminum hub body is CNC-machined to tight tolerances, which matters because a sloppy fit causes the vibration you're trying to eliminate. It's a direct OEM replacement, so you're getting the same quality as the factory part without paying the dealer markup. Installation takes about 15 minutes if you have basic tools — remove the prop nut and washer, pull the old hub, slide this one on, torque to spec.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, this is not a universal part. If you're running a Yamaha, Suzuki, or Evinrude, this won't help you — it's strictly Mercury/Mariner/MerCruiser. Second, the rubber dampener is bonded into the hub. When it eventually wears out (typically after several seasons of hard use), you're replacing the whole kit, not just the insert. A few buyers noted that on older, slightly worn prop shafts, they had to do a light file pass on the hub's splines for a perfect slip-fit. That's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing if you're working with a high-hour lower unit.

## Who Should Buy This

This kit is for you if:

- **You own a Mercury, Mariner, or MerCruiser** with a prop rattle or vibration that's driving you crazy
- **You're replacing a worn or spun hub** and want the OEM-quality fix rather than a cheap universal
- **You run a high-horsepower outboard** (75hp+) where drivetrain shock can be brutal on cheap hubs

Skip it if you have a different brand of outboard or if you're running a small outboard (under 40hp) that uses the smaller Flo-Torq I hub — that's a different kit.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I know if my prop uses the Flo-Torq II hub?**
Check your prop's part number or the Mercury parts diagram for your model. The Flo-Torq II is standard on most Mercury props designed for 75hp and larger engines. If your current hub has a rubber ring visible between the prop and the thrust washer, it's likely Flo-Torq II.

**Q: Will this fix a spun prop hub?**
Yes, if the damage is limited to the rubber insert. If your prop blades are damaged or the splines on the prop itself are worn, you'll need a new prop — this kit won't fix that.

**Q: Can I install this myself?**
Absolutely, if you're comfortable with basic wrench work. You'll need a prop wrench and a torque wrench to hit the manufacturer's spec (typically 55 ft-lbs for the prop nut). It's a straightforward 15-minute job.
