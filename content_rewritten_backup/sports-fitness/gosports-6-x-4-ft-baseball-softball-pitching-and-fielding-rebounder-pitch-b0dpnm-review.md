It’s made of a thick, woven nylon that takes repeated 90 mph impacts without fraying or developing soft spots.

For pitching, the 6x4 size is generous. It gives you a realistic target area to work corners, and the included strike zone graphic helps reinforce location. It’s not a full-size L-screen replacement, but for a backyard setup, it’s the right balance between portability and usable surface area.

### Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has two notable tradeoffs.

First, the **frame is heavy**. At roughly 25 pounds, it’s not a "carry to the park" piece of gear. It lives in the backyard or garage. It’s portable in the sense that you can move it, but it’s not backpack-friendly.

Second, the **net is designed for use on grass only**. The ground stake is required for stability. If you plan to use this on a concrete driveway or a turf field where you can't stake it down, the frame will slide on hard impacts. This is a significant limitation for some buyers.
