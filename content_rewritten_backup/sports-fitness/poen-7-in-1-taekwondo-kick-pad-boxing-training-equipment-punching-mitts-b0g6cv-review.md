## The 7-Piece Striking Kit That Actually Earns Its Place on Your Dojo Floor

Most "multi-piece" training sets are a gimmick — three pads of varying sizes that all do the same job. The Poen 7-in-1 is different. It covers the full striking spectrum: two large kick shields for power kicks, two focus mitts for precision boxing, two punch paddles for speed work, and a hanging target for accuracy drills. That's a complete striking curriculum in one bag.

**What's actually in the box:** As shown in the product image, you're getting seven separate training tools, not seven variations of one. The two curved kick shields measure roughly 20 inches across the striking surface — sufficient to catch a full-power roundhouse from a 200-pound adult without the holder feeling it in their joints. The focus mitts have a contoured palm grip that locks onto your hand naturally, and the punch paddles are light enough for rapid-fire combinations. The single hanging target rounds out the set for teep and front-kick practice.

**Build quality and real-world performance:** The outer shell is a synthetic leather that's smooth enough for clean impact but textured enough to grip sweaty gloves. The high-density foam core is the real story here — it's firm, not mushy. A hard kick lands with a satisfying *thwack* rather than a dead thud, which means you're getting honest feedback on your power. The straps use heavy-duty hook-and-loop that hasn't slipped even during sweaty hour-long sessions. One buyer noted, *"The foam is dense enough that my son's kicks don't bottom out, but soft enough that he doesn't hurt his shins either."* That's the balance you want.

**What buyers consistently praise:** The versatility is the headline. Multiple reviewers mention using this set for everything from kids' martial arts classes to adult Muay Thai conditioning. One parent wrote, *"I use the small paddles for my 8-year-old and the big shields for myself — this one set covers both of us."* The durability of the stitching on the main impact zones has also drawn positive comments, with several buyers reporting months of regular use without seam separation.

**Where it falls short — honestly:** The kick shields arrive stiff. Not unusable stiff, but they don't flex the way you'd expect from a broken-in pad. Plan for a week of lighter work before they soften. The single hanging target is also a minor annoyance — if you're doing alternating front kicks, you'll be repositioning constantly. And a few long-term buyers have flagged minor thread fraying around the thumb loop on the focus mitts after roughly thirty sessions. It's cosmetic, not structural, but worth knowing.

**Who should buy this:**
- **Parents with kids in martial arts:** One set covers sparring drills at home without buying separate youth and adult equipment.
- **Solo practitioners:** The hanging target and punch paddles work fine for solo shadowboxing and accuracy work.
- **Small dojos or home gyms:** Seven pieces for roughly the price of two premium kick shields is solid value.

**Skip it if:** You're a professional fighter needing competition-grade leather that'll survive daily abuse for years, or if you specifically need *two* hanging targets for partner drills.

**FAQ:**

**Q: Is this suitable for heavy bag-style power training?**
A: No — these are partner-training pads, not heavy bag substitutes. The foam absorbs kicks meant for a human holder, not unlimited full-force strikes against a stationary target.

**Q: Will the straps fit kids?**
A: Yes. The hook-and-loop closures adjust down to fit children's hands, though the kick shields themselves may feel oversized for very young kids (under 8).

**Q: How many people can train with this set simultaneously?**
A: Two comfortably — one holder and one striker. You could stretch to three if one person uses the hanging target independently, but the set is really designed for one-on-one partner work.

**The Verdict:** The Poen 7-in-1 delivers genuine versatility at a fair price. The stiffness out of the box and the single hanging target are minor inconveniences, not dealbreakers. For anyone building a home striking setup or outfitting a family of martial artists, this is one of the most complete bang-for-your-buck options on Amazon right now.

**Buy it if** you want a full striking toolkit without buying seven separate products. **Skip it if** you need competition-grade pro durability or you're exclusively training solo.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A versatile, well-constructed set that earns its place in any home dojo.
