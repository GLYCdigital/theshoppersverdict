## Where It Falls Short

Honestly, this rope has two real weaknesses.

**The counter can't be turned off.** Once the rope starts spinning, it counts every revolution. That means if you're doing crossovers or jumping jacks with the rope, it adds phantom counts. For strict skipping, fine. For varied routines, the number will be inflated.

**The PVC cable is not for outdoor concrete.** It's a gym or indoor rope. Use it on rough surfaces and you'll chew through the coating in a few months. Buyers who used it on asphalt reported fraying within 6-8 weeks.

Also, the handles are on the heavier side (about 4.5 oz each). That's fine for beginners but feels sluggish for advanced speed work. Some competitive jumpers will want a lighter, competition-style handle.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I set the rope length?**
Stand on the middle of the cable with one foot. The handles should reach your armpits. Cut the excess cable, then re-tighten the screw inside the handle. Takes under 2 minutes.

**Q: Does the counter work for double-unders?**
It counts every rope revolution, so a double-under registers as two counts. It's accurate but inflates your total versus single skips.

**Q: Is this good for kids?**
Yes. The adjustable cable and lighter PVC make it suitable for kids 8+. Just supervise cutting the cable — the PVC can splinter if cut poorly.
