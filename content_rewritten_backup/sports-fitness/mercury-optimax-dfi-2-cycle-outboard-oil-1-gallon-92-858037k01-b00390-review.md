## What's Actually in the Jug

You're getting a full US gallon (128 oz) of Mercury's proprietary DFI blend. It's not just TC-W3 certified — it's formulated specifically for the combustion dynamics of direct fuel injection. The key difference: DFI engines run leaner and hotter at idle, which causes standard two-stroke oils to leave deposits that foul spark plugs and gum up injectors. This blend uses a low-ash synthetic base with an additive package designed to burn clean at those lower cylinder pressures.

As shown in the product images, the jug has a molded handle and clear graduated markings on the side. That's genuinely useful — no guesswork when you're mixing a 50:1 ratio at the dock.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this in a regular Mercury two-stroke (non-DFI)?**
A: Technically yes — it's TC-W3 certified and won't harm the engine. But you're overpaying for DFI-specific additives that a carbureted engine doesn't need. Use a standard TC-W3 oil instead.

**Q: What ratio should I mix with gas?**
A: Mercury specifies 50:1 for all Optimax and DFI models. That's 2.6 oz of oil per gallon of fuel. The graduated markings on the jug make this easy to measure.

**Q: Does it work in a Mercury 4-stroke?**
A: No. Four-stroke engines require different oil — using two-stroke oil will cause severe damage. Check your owner's manual.
