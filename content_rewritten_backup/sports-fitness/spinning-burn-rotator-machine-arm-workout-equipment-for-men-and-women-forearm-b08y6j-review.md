### Where It Falls Short

Honesty requires pointing out the flaws. First, **there is no adjustable resistance.** The only way to increase difficulty is by spinning faster or extending your arm further from your body. This limits progressive overload—you’ll plateau eventually.

Second, **it’s not a complete arm workout.** It only targets the forearms, wrists, and grip. It will not build your biceps or triceps. You’ll still need traditional curling and pressing movements for full arm development.

Third, **the learning curve is real.** Unlike a dumbbell, you have to master the circular wrist motion to keep the rotor spinning. Some users reported frustration in the first few sessions before the movement clicked.
