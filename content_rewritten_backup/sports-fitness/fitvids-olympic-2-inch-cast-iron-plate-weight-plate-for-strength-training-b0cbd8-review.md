Most budget weight plates have two problems: the center hole is drilled sloppily so they flop on the bar, and the weight is a wish — not a guarantee. The Fitvids Olympic 2-Inch Cast Iron Plates sidestep both pitfalls with a tighter 2-inch bore and a manufacturing tolerance that actually respects the number stamped on the side. At 4.6 stars across 229 ratings, these aren't a mystery box — they're a dependable workhorse.

## What You Actually Get

The listing varies by set size, but the individual plates are straightforward: solid cast iron, a 2-inch center hole sized for standard Olympic barbells, and a matte powder-coat finish. No chrome, no rubber coating, no gimmicks. The plates are relatively thin for their weight class, which means you can load more total iron on the bar before running out of sleeve space — a real advantage for deadlifts or rack pulls.

As shown in the product image, the finish is uniform and clean. The lettering is pressed, not painted, so it won't flake off after a few months of chalk and sweat.

## Where They Fall Short

No product is flawless, and these have two honest drawbacks. First, the bare cast iron edges will chew up softer barbell sleeves. If you own a chrome or zinc-coated bar, use plastic collars or accept some cosmetic wear. Second, they're loud — dropping these on a concrete floor without a deadlift platform will annoy your neighbors and risk cracking tiles. That's inherent to cast iron, not a defect.

Also, the plates ship with a light protective oil coating. Wipe each one down before your first session or the chalk-and-oil combo turns into a gritty mess.
