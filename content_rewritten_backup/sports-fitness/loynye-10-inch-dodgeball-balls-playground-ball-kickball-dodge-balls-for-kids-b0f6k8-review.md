## Real-World Performance: Soft Enough for Kids, Durable Enough for the Gym Floor

It's small enough for kids aged 6–12 to grip, but large enough that throwing hard doesn't turn it into a dangerous missile. ## Where It Falls Short (Honest Tradeoffs)

No product is perfect, and the LOYNYE set has three consistent complaints.

**1. Inflation is a hassle.** A few buyers reported that the valve stems are slightly recessed, making it tricky to get a good seal with standard pump needles. If you're inflating six balls before a birthday party, budget an extra 10 minutes. It's not a dealbreaker, but you'll want a quality pump, not a cheap plastic one.

**2. Durability under aggressive outdoor use.** On blacktop or gravel, the surface scuffs quickly. If you're playing on concrete, expect visible wear within a few sessions. The balls won't pop easily, but they'll look beat-up fast. That's fine for gym use, less fine for a schoolyard that doubles as a parking lot.

**3. The "10-inch" is a minimum, not exact.** A few customers noted the balls measure closer to 9.5 inches when fully inflated. For most games, that difference is negligible. But if you're using these for regulation kickball drills, the size inconsistency might annoy you.

**Q: Do these come inflated?**
A: No. They ship flat to reduce shipping costs. You'll need a hand pump or ball pump with a needle. Inflate to about 2 PSI — they should feel slightly squishy, not rock-hard.

**Q: Are these safe for 4-year-olds?**
A: They're soft enough that the risk of injury is low, but the 10-inch size might be hard for tiny hands to grip and throw. For ages 4 and under, look for an 8.5-inch or smaller playground ball. Ages 5+ are the sweet spot here.

**Q: Can I use these for kickball on grass?**
A: Yes, but they'll collect grass stains and dirt. The rubber is easy to wipe clean, but the surface will show wear over time. They're best on smooth surfaces like gym floors or short grass.
