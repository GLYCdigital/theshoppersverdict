The core selling point is the adjustable rebounder head. In practice, this works as advertised. The mid-angle is the sweet spot—it gives you a realistic pass back that forces you to control with both feet. Build quality is solid for backyard use. The frame holds up to hard shots, though the included stakes are only useful on grass.
