The WEKAPO Beach Blanket solves a specific problem that anyone who's ever been to the beach will recognize instantly: sand gets everywhere. It sticks to towels, blankets, bags, and follows you home. This blanket's claim is that sand slides right off. 14,673 Amazon reviews and a 4.7-star average suggest it delivers.

## The Sand Test

The defining test for any sandproof blanket is whether it actually works on a windy beach day. One reviewer who bought it skeptically said: *"I bought this beach blanket with a bit of a 'gadget mindset' hoping it would work, but accepting it might be just hype. I was so pleasantly surprised. This stayed perfectly in place and barely got any sand on it."*

Another owner who used it on a Florida beach reported: *"The sand literally shakes right off, keeping everything clean and sand-free. Waterproof and sandproof! It even held up in the heat really well."* Multiple reviewers confirm that a quick shake before folding removes almost all sand.

## Build and Practicality

At $32.26, the WEKAPO isn't a luxury blanket. The material is thin — more of a technical fabric than something you'd want to nap on directly. You'll feel small rocks and shells underneath. But that thinness is what makes it work: sand can't get trapped in thick fibers, and the blanket dries in minutes.

The stakes are a surprisingly important feature. Beach wind is relentless, and a blanket that won't stay down is useless. A family reviewer noted: *"It was large enough for our family of 4, and the stakes held the blanket down with no issues even on very windy days!"*

The WEKAPO Beach Blanket is a specialist tool, not a luxury blanket. If you want something soft to lie on at the beach, buy a thick towel. If you want something that won't turn your car, bag, and house into a sandbox, buy this. At $32, it's cheap enough to be worth trying.

The trade-off is comfort. You feel the ground. But for a day at the beach where sand management is the priority, it's the right choice.
