## FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: How do these compare to Winning gloves?**
Winning gloves offer a softer, more protective feel out of the box — many consider them the gold standard for sparring. Cleto Reyes provides more feedback and a more traditional Mexican-style fit. If you want maximum hand protection, go Winning. If you want authentic fight feel with better durability, go Reyes.

**Q: Do I need to buy lace converters?**
Not if you have a training partner to tie them. If you train solo, absolutely — the laces are the only closure system. Expect to spend an additional $10-20 for quality converters.

**Q: How long does the break-in period last?**
Most users report 2-4 weeks of regular training (3-4 sessions weekly) before the horsehair softens and molds properly. Your hands may feel sore during this period — that's normal and part of the process.
