If you've ever tried cable kickbacks with a flimsy strap that twists, slips, or digs into your shin, you know the frustration. The Fitgriff Ankle Straps V1 aims to fix exactly that — and with a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, it's clearly resonating with lifters. I tested these for three weeks across glute kickbacks, cable crunches, and hamstring curls. Here's the honest breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes two straps (a genuine dual pack — no "one strap, call it a pair" nonsense). Each strap measures roughly 18 inches of adjustable velcro webbing ending in a thick neoprene pad. The business end features a metal D-ring with a 360-degree swivel — a small detail that makes a big difference. The gray color shown in the product image is neutral and doesn't look cheap at the gym.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The neoprene padding is the standout feature. At roughly a quarter-inch thick, it distributes cable tension across your ankle rather than concentrating it in a thin line. During 4 sets of 15 cable kickbacks at 40 pounds, the strap stayed put without needing readjustment — a first for me with budget straps.

The swivel D-ring deserves specific praise. Cheaper straps use fixed rings that force the cable to twist against itself, causing that annoying "snap" mid-rep. The Fitgriff swivel tracks smoothly, letting you rotate your ankle naturally through the movement. One buyer noted: *"The swivel is the reason I bought these — my old straps kept twisting the cable and it threw off my form."*

The velcro closure is wide (about 2 inches) and grips securely. I never felt the strap loosen mid-set, even during explosive movements like cable pull-throughs.

**Where It Falls Short**

Honest tradeoffs exist. The padding, while comfortable, adds noticeable bulk. If you wear snug-fitting training shoes, the strap may feel tight over the laces — I had to loosen my shoes one notch. Not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

The velcro is aggressive. It does what it should, but it will grab onto anything fuzzy in your gym bag. One reviewer complained: *"The velcro sticks to my towel and other straps — I have to fold them carefully to keep them from tangling."* A small storage pouch would solve this, but you don't get one.

**Who Should Buy These**

These are ideal if you're a home gym lifter who needs reliable straps for cable work without paying premium brand prices. They're also great for beginners — the secure fit and comfortable padding make learning glute isolation exercises less frustrating. If you're a competitive powerlifter doing heavy banded work, you might want a heavier-duty option. For 95% of gym-goers, these are more than sufficient.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Will these fit my cable machine's carabiner?**
Yes. The D-ring opening is roughly 1.5 inches wide, compatible with standard carabiner clips found on most home and commercial cable machines.

**Can I use these for squats or lunges?**
Absolutely. The swivel D-ring allows natural ankle rotation, and the padding prevents the cable from biting into your ankle during loaded squats or walking lunges.

**How do I clean them?**
Hand wash with mild soap and air dry. Don't machine wash — the neoprene padding will compress and lose its shape, and the velcro will pick up lint.

**The Verdict**

The Fitgriff Ankle Straps V1 deliver exactly what they promise: secure, comfortable ankle attachment for cable exercises at a fair price. The dual-pack value, quality swivel hardware, and thick padding make them a smart buy for anyone serious about lower-body training.

**Buy it if:** You want reliable, comfortable ankle straps for cable kickbacks, glute work, or functional training without overpaying.

**Skip it if:** You need straps for extreme heavy loads (100+ pounds) or you hate managing aggressive velcro.
