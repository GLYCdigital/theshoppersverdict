## Where It Falls Short

Honesty requires addressing the weak points. Multiple reviewers report the internal sand bags shift after several months of frequent use, creating an uneven feel. It's not a defect that ruins the product, but you'll notice it during single-leg exercises.

The stitching around the strap loops is another durability concern. A handful of reviews mention fraying after six months of daily wear. These aren't lifetime products — expect 12-18 months of regular use before replacement.

Finally, the sizing runs slightly large. If you have slender ankles, the straps may feel bulky even at their tightest setting. That's a fit consideration, not a dealbreaker, but worth noting.
