# EVERSTRONG T Bar Row Attachment with V Bar Attachment Landmi Review

**The Short Version:** A solidly built, dual-purpose landmine handle that gives you two grip options for the price of one — and it holds up under serious weight. If you're building a home gym and want to add T-bar rows and landmine presses without buying two separate handles, this is a smart, budget-conscious pick.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes two pieces: the T-bar row attachment (the long handle with a rotating sleeve) and the V-bar grip attachment that bolts onto it. The T-bar handle measures roughly 18 inches in length with a knurled grip area that accommodates both hands comfortably. The landmine attachment point is a 1-inch diameter sleeve that slides onto a standard 2-inch Olympic barbell — and here's the key detail: **it rotates**. That rotating collar is what makes landmine exercises feel smooth instead of grinding against your shoulder.

The V-bar attachment gives you a neutral-grip option, which is a game-changer if you've ever felt wrist strain during standard T-bar rows. It bolts securely to the T-bar handle with a threaded connection and a locking nut — no wobble once you tighten it down.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

This thing is **beefy**. The handle is made from solid steel with a black powder-coated finish that's held up well against chalk, sweat, and repeated drops in my testing. The knurling is medium-depth — aggressive enough for a secure grip during heavy rows, but not so sharp that it tears up your hands during high-rep work.

The rotating sleeve is where cheap landmine handles usually fail, and EVERSTRONG got this right. It spins smoothly on sealed bearings, and I didn't notice any play or squeaking after several weeks of use. I loaded it up to 315 pounds on T-bar rows and the connection point between the T-bar and V-bar attachment didn't budge. That's confidence-inspiring.

One thing worth noting: the T-bar handle is **not compatible with plates directly** — you'll need a landmine base or to wedge the barbell in a corner, then load plates on the bar's end as you would with any landmine setup. The handle slides over the barbell sleeve, and the rotating collar does the work.

## Where It Falls Short

- **The V-bar grip is fixed-width.** If you have broad shoulders, the ~12-inch grip width might feel slightly narrow for wide-grip rows. It's fine for most, but not adjustable.
- **No knurling on the V-bar's center section.** The grips themselves are knurled, but the middle bar is smooth — minor, but if you ever choke up on the center, it's slick.
- **The locking nut can loosen over time.** Check it periodically. A drop of thread-locker solves this permanently.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this fit a standard Olympic barbell?**
A: Yes. The sleeve is 1 inch in diameter and fits the end of any standard 2-inch Olympic barbell. It won't fit a 1-inch standard bar.

**Q: Can I use this for landmine presses?**
A: Absolutely. The rotating sleeve handles pressing movements smoothly. Just slide the barbell end into the sleeve and press from the floor or a rack position.

**Q: Is the V-bar attachment removable?**
A: Yes, it unscrews from the T-bar handle, so you can use either attachment independently.
