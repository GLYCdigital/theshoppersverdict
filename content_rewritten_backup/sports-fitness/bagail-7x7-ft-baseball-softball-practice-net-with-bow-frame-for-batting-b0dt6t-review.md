## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the weaknesses.

The included ground stakes are adequate for grass but borderline useless on hard-packed dirt or concrete. If you're setting up on a driveway or a field with compacted soil, plan to add sandbags or weight the base. This is a known issue across budget-to-midrange nets, but it's worth flagging.

The carry bag is the other weak point. It's a thin nylon sleeve — functional but not built for years of abuse. If you're hauling this to the field multiple times per week, expect the seams to show wear within a season.

Also note: this is NOT a heavy-duty cage net. It's designed for practice swings and soft toss — not for someone throwing 100 mph from 20 feet away on a regular basis. The bow frame absorbs a lot, but it has limits.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you're a parent with a young athlete (8-14) who needs daily reps, a high school player working on mechanics at home, or a coach who needs a portable net for team practices.
