## FAQ

**Is this harness truly no-choke?**
Yes. The front clip redirects pulling force to the chest, not the neck. No pressure on the trachea whatsoever.

**How do I measure my dog for the right size?**
Measure the widest part of the chest, behind the front legs. Add 2 inches for breathing room. Compare to the size chart—if between sizes, go up.

**Can I leave it on all day?**
It's comfortable enough for extended wear, but remove it at night to let skin breathe. The mesh prevents most chafing, but no harness should be worn 24/7.
