Let’s cut through the noise: most dog playpens are either too flimsy to contain a determined chewer or too heavy to move from room to room. The HealSmart Plastic/Metal Dog Playpen tries to split that difference, and for the most part, it works. With 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, owners are mostly happy — but not everyone, and the complaints are worth hearing before you buy.

**What you actually get**

The box contains eight modular panels, each roughly 30 inches tall, that connect via a click-lock system. No tools, no screws, no swearing. The panels alternate between solid plastic sections and metal wire mesh — a hybrid design that gives you visibility while blocking the chew-through paths a determined puppy might exploit. The whole thing assembles in about four minutes on your first try, faster once you get the hang of the connector tabs. It folds down flat for storage, which is a genuine plus if you live in a small apartment.

**Real-world performance**

The hybrid build is the smartest thing about this pen. The plastic panels take the brunt of puppy teeth, while the wire mesh lets you see what’s happening inside. That’s a better tradeoff than all-plastic pens, which turn into saunas, or all-metal pens, which are heavy and cold. The 30-inch height works for small to medium breeds — think dachshunds, frenchies, corgis — but it’s not a barrier for athletes. One reviewer noted, “My 6-month-old beagle mix cleared it in one jump. I had to stack it against the couch to keep her in.” That’s not a defect; that’s physics. If you have a jumper, buy a taller pen.

The base has rubber feet that grip decently on hardwood and tile, but on carpet they’re less effective. If your dog likes to push the pen across the floor, you’ll want to weigh down a corner or two. The connectors are the weakest link — they’re plastic, and while they hold up well for everyday use, they’re the first thing to crack if you’re rough with them during setup or teardown. The metal frame itself feels solid, but the joints are where cost-cutting shows.

**What owners consistently praise**

The assembly ease comes up again and again. “I set this up while my coffee was still hot,” one buyer wrote. “Took longer to unbox than to build.” Portability is the second most-cited win — owners use it for travel, camping, and moving between rooms. The chew resistance gets solid marks too, with several owners confirming their puppies couldn’t destroy it the way they demolished fabric alternatives.

**Where it falls short**

Three consistent complaints surface across reviews. First, no top cover — escape artists will find their way out. Second, the plastic connectors feel cheaper than the product photos suggest; they work fine but don’t inspire confidence under heavy daily use. Third, the 30-inch height limits its usefulness for larger or more athletic breeds. If you have a golden retriever puppy, this is a temporary solution, not a long-term containment strategy.

**Who should buy this**

- **Apartment dwellers with small to medium dogs** who need a portable, storable pen for daytime containment
- **Puppy owners** who need a chew-resistant space for crate training and supervised play
- **Travelers** who want a pen that packs flat and sets up in minutes at a rental or campsite

Skip it if you have a large breed, a determined climber, or a dog that’s already destroyed a metal crate. You need something taller and heavier.

**FAQ**

**Is this safe for puppies?**
Yes, for supervised use. The panels are chew-resistant but not indestructible, and the 30-inch height is fine for most puppies under 20 pounds. Don’t leave a teething puppy unattended for hours.

**Can I connect two of these together?**
Yes, the panels are modular, and owners report connecting two sets for a larger enclosure. Just note that the connectors are the same plastic ones — more panels mean more stress on the joints.

**Does it work outdoors?**
It’s stable on grass and dirt, but the rubber feet work best on hard surfaces. It’s not weatherproof, so keep it under shade or bring it in during rain.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a lightweight, chew-resistant pen for a small or medium dog and value setup speed and portability. **Skip it if** you have a jumper, a large breed, or a dog that’s already proven it can break out of standard pens.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The HealSmart playpen nails the essentials for its intended audience. It’s not perfect, but for the price, it’s a solid, practical choice that most owners will use daily. The escape-artist caveat is real — know your dog before you buy.
