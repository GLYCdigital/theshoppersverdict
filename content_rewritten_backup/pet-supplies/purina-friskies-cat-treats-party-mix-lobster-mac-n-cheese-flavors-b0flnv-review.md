# Purina Friskies Cat Treats, Party Mix Lobster & Mac 'N' Chee Review

## What You're Actually Buying

Let’s be real: the name sounds like a joke. Lobster and mac 'n' cheese? For a cat? But here's the thing — your cat doesn't care about gourmet credibility. They care about crunch, smell, and the immediate gratification of a treat that tastes like something they'd never find in the wild. And based on the 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, plenty of cats are on board.

This is a 2-ounce resealable bag of Friskies Party Mix, and it sits in the "novelty flavor" lane of the cat treat aisle. The texture is the signature Party Mix crunch — small, jagged kibble-like pieces that are easy for cats to crunch without effort. The flavor is seafood-forward with a cheesy undertone, which sounds bizarre but apparently hits the spot for feline taste buds.

## What You Get in the Bag

The bag itself is standard Friskies fare: resealable zipper top, bright packaging, and about two weeks' worth of treats if you're doling out the recommended portion. The pieces are roughly the size of a pea, maybe slightly larger, and they're dense enough to satisfy the crunch instinct without turning into a crumb bomb in your pocket.

One thing to note: this is a *treat*, not a meal. The calorie count is modest, but it's not something you should free-feed. Cats who inhale treats will polish this bag off in a few days if you're not careful.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The dominant theme in customer feedback is that *cats who are picky eaters actually go for this flavor*. One buyer noted, "My cat is the pickiest creature on earth and she devours these. I don't know what sorcery this is, but it works." Another reviewer mentioned using them as a training reward: "I've been trying to get my cat to use the scratching post instead of the couch. These treats are the only thing that gets her attention."

The crunch factor also gets consistent shoutouts. Cats seem to prefer the texture over softer treats, and multiple reviewers mentioned that even senior cats with dental issues could manage these without difficulty.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's talk about the downsides honestly.

First, the flavor is *weird*. If you have a cat that's sensitive to strong seafood smells, the lobster note is noticeable. It's not overpowering, but it's there. Some reviewers mentioned that the smell lingers on fingers after feeding, so wash your hands unless you want to smell like a seafood boil.

Second, the resealable bag is convenient, but it's not airtight. If you live in a humid climate, these treats can go stale faster than you'd expect. Transfer them to an airtight container if you're not going through the bag quickly.

Third, the ingredient list isn't going to win any awards. The first ingredient is poultry by-product meal, followed by corn meal and soybean meal. This is a budget treat, and it's priced accordingly. If you're looking for grain-free, single-protein, or otherwise "clean" ingredients, this isn't it.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The owner of a picky cat** who has tried a dozen treats and watched their cat turn up their nose at all of them. This flavor seems to be a reliable crowd-pleaser.
- **The training-focused owner** who needs a high-value reward that cats actually care about. The crunchy texture makes it easy to break into smaller pieces.
- **The multi-cat household** looking for a treat that works across different ages and preferences. The crunch is universally appealing.

Skip it if you're looking for a grain-free option, a soft treat for a senior cat with no teeth, or a treat where you can pronounce every ingredient on the label.

## Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Can kittens eat these?**
A: Yes, but introduce them slowly. The pieces are small enough for kittens to chew, but wait until they're at least 8-10 weeks old and already eating solid food comfortably.

**Q: Are these treats good for cats with sensitive stomachs?**
A: It depends on the cat. The by-product meal and corn base are common allergens for some cats. Start with a small amount and watch for any digestive upset. If your cat has known food sensitivities, this probably isn't the safest choice.

**Q: How many treats should I give per day?**
A: Friskies recommends a small handful per 10 pounds of body weight per day. But honestly, if your cat is food-motivated, they'll convince you they're starving after two pieces. Stick to the serving size — treats should be no more than 10% of daily calories.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You have a picky cat, you're training and need a high-value reward, or you want a budget-friendly treat that cats actually enjoy.

**Skip it if:** You need grain-free ingredients, you're concerned about by-products, or your cat prefers soft treats.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The flavor is a hit with cats, the crunch is satisfying, and the price is right. It's not a premium product, but it doesn't pretend to be. For a fun, affordable treat that gets enthusiastic feline approval, this is hard to beat. Just keep the bag sealed tight and wash your hands after feeding.
