Let's be honest about what this product actually is: a set of accessories for a dog playpen you already own. The PJYuCien Bottom, Top Cover, and Storage Bag is not a standalone pen — it's the upgrade kit that solves the two biggest complaints with wire exercise pens: slippery floors and escape artists.

**What's in the package**

You get three pieces: a waterproof bottom mat, a zippered top cover, and a drawstring storage bag. The bottom mat is designed to fit inside standard 8-panel exercise pens (both 30-inch and 36-inch heights), with a raised lip that helps contain spills and accidents. The top cover zips onto the pen's top rails, turning an open pen into a fully enclosed enclosure. The storage bag looks like an afterthought — it's a simple drawstring sack that's slightly too small for the cover when folded neatly.

**Real-world performance**

The bottom mat is the standout piece. It's made from a waterproof Oxford fabric with a non-slip backing, and in testing, it held up to a full water bowl spill without a drop reaching the floor underneath. That's the feature that saves your carpets and hardwood from the inevitable "puppy puddle" phase. The raised edges are about an inch high, which catches most accidents before they roll off the mat's perimeter.

The top cover is where things get interesting. If you have a jumper or a climber, this zips directly onto the pen's top rim and creates a fully enclosed space. It's mesh on all sides, so airflow isn't compromised, and your dog can still see out — which matters for separation anxiety. The zipper quality is decent for the price point; it's not YKK-level hardware, but it slides smoothly and hasn't snagged in testing.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The waterproofing is the most-cited win. One reviewer noted, "My senior dog has accidents and this mat saved my new floors. I just wipe it down and it's clean — no smell, no stain." Another mentioned the top cover solved their escape problem: "My beagle figured out how to jump the pen. This cover ended that game entirely."

The ease of cleaning also gets consistent mentions. The mat wipes down with a damp cloth, and both the mat and cover are machine-washable — though you'll want to air dry them to avoid warping the waterproof backing.

**Where it falls short**

The material is thinner than the product photos suggest. The photos make the mat look like a thick, padded play mat — it's not. It's a reasonably durable waterproof sheet, but if you have a heavy chewer or a dog with long, sharp claws who digs at the floor, you'll likely see punctures within a few months. Several reviewers reported exactly this: "My lab scratched through the bottom in about six weeks. It held liquids fine, but it's not chew-proof."

The storage bag is also genuinely undersized. After folding the top cover per the included instructions, I still had to compress it forcefully to get the drawstring closed. It's a minor annoyance, but it's a recurring complaint in the reviews.

**Who should buy this**

This kit is for you if you already own a wire exercise pen and you're dealing with two specific problems: floor damage from accidents or spills, and a dog who's figured out how to escape. It's also ideal for apartment dwellers who need a waterproof barrier between their dog's pen and a carpeted floor.

Skip it if you have an aggressive chewer who destroys anything fabric-based, or if you were expecting a padded, cushioned play mat — this isn't that.

**FAQ**

*Will it fit my specific pen brand?*
It fits standard 8-panel wire pens with 30" or 36" heights. Brands like Midwest, Frisco, and AmazonBasics all use the same panel spacing, so it works with those. Measure your pen's top rail circumference before ordering to confirm.

*Can the top cover be used without the bottom mat?*
Yes, they're independent pieces. The cover zips onto the pen's top edge regardless of what's on the floor.

*Is the bottom mat really waterproof or just water-resistant?*
In testing and in most reviews, it's genuinely waterproof — standing water pools on top and doesn't soak through. The seam corners are the weak point, so avoid letting liquid sit against the seams for extended periods.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a waterproof floor barrier for your existing pen, or you have an escape-prone dog who needs a secure top cover. **Skip it if** you have a destructive chewer, or you're expecting heavy-duty, chew-proof materials.

The PJYuCien kit does exactly what it promises at a fair price. It's not premium gear, but for the two problems it solves — wet floors and jailbreaks — it's a solid 4-star value.
