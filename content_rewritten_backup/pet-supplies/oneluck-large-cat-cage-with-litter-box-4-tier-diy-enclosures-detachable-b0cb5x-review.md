Let me cut through the noise: if you're eyeing a cat cage, you're probably in one of three situations — you're introducing a new cat, you need a safe space for a recovering pet, or you're trying to contain a feline tornado while you're at work. The **Oneluck Large Cat Cage with Litter Box** promises to handle all three scenarios. After digging through 204 ratings and 4.1 stars of real customer feedback, here's what you actually need to know.

## What You're Actually Getting

This is a 4-tier enclosure that stands roughly 5.5 feet tall, and the footprint is generous enough that a large Maine Coon won't feel like a sardine. The key feature that sets it apart from cheaper cages: the **litter box is built into the bottom tier**. That's not a gimmick — it means your cat has 24/7 access to a bathroom without you having to wedge a separate box inside. The frame is metal with a powder-coated finish, and the shelves are solid plastic panels that are easy to wipe down.

The "DIY" in the name isn't marketing fluff. The panels are detachable, so you can reconfigure the layout, remove a tier, or expand it later if you add another unit. That flexibility is genuinely useful for people who want a cage that grows with their cat's needs. The ramps between tiers are a thoughtful touch — senior cats and kittens both use them without the awkward jumping you see in cheaper multi-level cages.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the Oneluck earns its 4.1 stars. Customers consistently report that the cage is **sturdier than expected** once assembled. The wire mesh is welded, not just crimped, and the frame doesn't wobble when a cat launches off a mid-tier shelf. One reviewer put it plainly: *"My 15-pound ragdoll can't budge this thing, and he's tried every day for a month."* That's the endorsement that matters.

The integrated litter box is the standout feature for most buyers. Because it slides out from the bottom, you can clean it without opening the main door — a quality-of-life improvement you'll appreciate at 7 AM. Multiple reviewers noted that the design actually contains litter scatter better than freestanding boxes, which is a pleasant surprise.

The 4.1 rating, though, tells you this isn't perfect. The most common complaint threads through nearly every negative review: **assembly is a puzzle**. The manual is diagram-only, and several customers reported spending 45–90 minutes figuring out which panel goes where. One frustrated buyer wrote, *"Great cage, but I almost returned it during setup. Clearer instructions would make this a 5-star product."*

## Where It Falls Short

Beyond assembly, the wire spacing is worth a hard look. If you have a kitten under 4 months or a particularly determined escape artist, the gaps may be wide enough for a small head to squeeze through. This isn't a flaw for adult cats, but it's a dealbreaker for fostering kittens. Also, the plastic shelves, while easy to clean, can scratch under aggressive scratching — plan to add a sisal mat if your cat is a dedicated claw artist.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You need a multi-cat or large-breed enclosure with a built-in litter solution, you want vertical space without a bulky catio, or you're recovering from surgery and need a safe confinement space that doesn't feel like a prison.

**Skip it if:** You're housing kittens under 4 months, you want something you can assemble in 10 minutes flat, or you need a fully enclosed outdoor run — this is strictly an indoor cage.

## FAQ

**Q: Can two adult cats share this cage comfortably?**
A: Yes, if they get along. The 4-tier layout gives each cat a private shelf, and the ramps reduce territorial conflicts. Just make sure the litter box is cleaned daily — two cats will fill it fast.

**Q: How hard is the assembly really?**
A: Plan for 45–90 minutes. The panels are labeled, but the diagram-only instructions require patience. Lay everything out first, and watch a video if you get stuck.

**Q: Does the litter box smell leak through the cage?**
A: No more than a standard litter box. The bottom tray is solid and seals reasonably well, but you'll still want to scoop daily and deep-clean weekly.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The Oneluck Large Cat Cage is a solid, well-built enclosure that solves a real problem: giving your cat vertical space and a bathroom without sacrificing your living room floor. It's not perfect — the assembly frustration and wire spacing for kittens are genuine drawbacks. But for adult cats, especially larger breeds, this is one of the better values in the category.

**Buy it if** you want a sturdy, expandable cage with a built-in litter solution and you're willing to invest an hour in setup. **Skip it if** you need something for kittens or you want a no-fuss, 15-minute assembly. For the right buyer, this cage will last for years.
