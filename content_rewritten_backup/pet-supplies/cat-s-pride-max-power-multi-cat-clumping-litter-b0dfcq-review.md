If you've got more than one cat, you already know the struggle: litter that clumps like wet sand, odor that punches through the lid, and dust that coats every surface in a three-foot radius. Cat's Pride Max Power Multi-Cat Clumping Litter claims to fix all three. After three weeks with a two-cat household (one of whom is a serial litter-box digger), here's the honest breakdown.

**What's in the box**

You get a 15-pound jug of unscented, lightweight clay litter. The jug has a resealable pour spout and a handle — small details that matter when you're hauling this monthly. The litter itself is fine-grained with a slightly textured feel, not the powdery dust you get with bargain brands. As shown in the product image, the packaging is straightforward: no gimmicks, just a sturdy plastic container that won't split when you're carrying it from the garage.

**Performance and build quality**

This is where Max Power earns its name. The clumping is genuinely fast — within 10 seconds of contact, urine forms a solid, scoopable mass that holds together when you lift it. No crumbling, no sticky residue left on the bottom of the box. For multi-cat households, that's the difference between a 30-second scoop and a five-minute scrape.

Odor control is the star feature. The unscented formula uses activated carbon to trap ammonia, and it works. Even with two cats sharing one large box (which experts recommend against, by the way — one box per cat plus one extra is the rule), the room stayed fresh for the full three weeks. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: "I've tried every brand from the cheap stuff to the $30 bags. This is the first one that actually kills the smell without covering it with perfume."

Dust levels are low but not zero. During daily scooping, there's minimal kick-up. But when you dump the entire box and refill, expect a fine haze. It settles quickly, but if you have a cat with respiratory issues, this isn't the dust-free option the marketing suggests.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.5-star rating across 225 reviews reflects genuine satisfaction. The most common themes:

- **Clump integrity** — "These clumps don't break apart. I can scoop without having to fish out pieces."
- **Value for multi-cat homes** — "I have four cats and this lasts longer than the expensive brands."
- **No scent masking** — "It just smells like nothing, which is exactly what I want."

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. First, the lightweight formula means it's prone to scattering. If your cat digs with enthusiasm (ours does), you'll find granules outside the box. A mat or tray underneath is non-negotiable.

Second, the "low dust" claim is relative. It's better than standard clay litters, but it's not in the same league as crystal or pine options. If dust is your primary concern, look elsewhere.

Third, the 15-pound jug lasts about three weeks with two cats. That's middle-of-the-road for cost per use — not a bargain, but not a ripoff either.

**Who should buy this**

- **Multi-cat households** (2–4 cats) that need reliable clumping and odor control without spending premium prices.
- **Owners who hate scented litters** — this one is genuinely unscented and stays that way.
- **Busy people** who want to scoop once a day without a wrestling match with the litter box.

**Skip it if:**

- You have a cat with asthma or severe dust sensitivity — look at pine or crystal litters instead.
- You're on a tight budget — there are cheaper clay options, though you'll sacrifice clump quality.
- Your cat is a flinger — the lightweight formula will end up on your floor.

**FAQ**

**Is this litter flushable?**
No. Cat's Pride Max Power is not flushable — it will clog pipes. Dispose of it in the trash.

**How often do I need to change the full box?**
With two cats, a full dump and clean every 2–3 weeks works well. The clumping keeps the clean litter fresh, but ammonia can still build up in the box's corners over time.

**Does the unscented formula really control odor?**
Yes, surprisingly well. The activated carbon traps ammonia molecules rather than masking them with fragrance. It's not a miracle worker — you still need to scoop daily — but it's the best unscented option in this price range.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You have multiple cats, hate scented litter, and want clumps that actually hold together. The odor control is legitimately impressive for an unscented formula.

**Skip it if:** Dust sensitivity is a dealbreaker, or your cat treats the litter box like a sandbox at the beach.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Cat's Pride Max Power is a solid, reliable choice that does exactly what it promises. It's not perfect, but for most multi-cat households, it's the best balance of performance and price you'll find.
