Let me be blunt: most dog car seats are overpriced bolsters with straps that shift the moment your dog leans. The Lealchum dog car seat for medium dogs is not that. After digging through 235 ratings and the construction details, this one earns its 4.7-star average — but it's not perfect.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the seat itself, a padded safety tether that clips to your dog's harness, and two adjustable straps. No tools. No confusing hardware. The seat measures roughly 20 inches wide, 18 inches deep, and 15 inches tall at the back — sized for dogs in the 15 to 50 pound range, though the sweet spot is clearly 25 to 40 pounds.

The build is where Lealchum separates itself from the cheap stuff. The base uses high-density foam rather than the flimsy polyester fill you see in budget seats. That matters. A 40-pound dog compresses cheap foam into a pancake within weeks. This base holds its shape, and the waterproof liner underneath the fleece cover means a wet dog or an accident doesn't soak into the foam core.

**Installation and real-world use**

Installation takes about two minutes. You thread the strap through the headrest posts, clip the lower straps around the seat belt receiver, and tighten. The four-point system is the difference-maker here — most seats use two straps and pivot forward on hard braking. The Lealchum's rear anchors keep it locked in place.

As shown in the product image, the seat includes a tether that clips to your dog's harness, not their collar. That's a genuine safety feature — a collar clip can injure a dog's neck in a sudden stop. The tether is adjustable and gives your dog enough slack to stand or turn around without allowing them to climb into the front seat.

The elevated design gives medium dogs a view out the window, which sounds cosmetic but matters for anxious travelers. Multiple reviewers note their dogs settle faster with this seat because they can see what's happening. One owner wrote, "My 35-pound beagle mix used to pace and whine. With this seat, he watches the road like a cop on patrol. Zero drama."

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the reviews, three themes dominate. First, stability — owners specifically mention the seat not shifting on turns, which is the #1 complaint across competing products. Second, the washable cover. It zips off in under a minute and survives repeated machine washing without pilling. Third, the tether quality — it's a real climbing-grade clip, not the flimsy carabiner you get with cheaper seats.

**Where it falls short**

The fabric is a genuine issue if you own a shedding breed. The fleece-like material grabs fur aggressively, and owners with German Shepherds and Labs report needing a lint roller after every trip. It's not a dealbreaker, but it's worth knowing.

The side bolsters are firm — intentionally so, for safety — but smaller medium dogs under 20 pounds will find themselves staring at the bolster rather than the window. If you have a smaller dog, measure them against the 15-inch back height before ordering.

**Who should buy this**

Get this seat if you have a 20-to-45-pound dog who rides at least weekly. It's ideal for commuters, weekend hikers, and anyone whose dog gets anxious in the car. The elevated view plus the stable base genuinely reduces motion sickness in some dogs because they can fix their gaze on the horizon.

Skip it if your dog is over 50 pounds — you need a seat with a wider base — or if you have a tiny dog under 15 pounds. Also skip it if you have a heavy shedder and no tolerance for fur cleanup.

**Frequently asked questions**

**Is this seat crash-tested?**
No. Lealchum does not claim crash-test certification. It's a travel comfort seat with a safety tether, not a crash-rated carrier. For maximum protection, pair it with a crash-tested harness.

**Does it fit bucket seats or only benches?**
It works with both, but it's more stable on bench seats. On bucket seats, the four-point strap system holds it in place, but the seat will sit at a slight angle.

**Can I use the seat belt with a dog in the seat?**
Yes, the seat is designed so the vehicle's seat belt can still be buckled, and the tether can also attach to the belt itself for added security.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if:** You have a 20-to-45-pound dog, you drive regularly, and you're tired of your dog sliding across the back seat or trying to climb into your lap. The stability alone justifies the price.

**Skip it if:** You have a heavy shedder who'll turn the fabric into a fur coat, or you need crash-test certification for peace of mind.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A well-engineered seat that solves the two biggest problems in this category: shifting and sagging. Deduct half a star for the fur-magnet fabric and the firm bolsters that don't suit every dog size.
