## Are These the Last Dog Pads You'll Need to Buy?

Let's be honest — most puppy pads are flimsy tissue paper that tear the moment your dog gets excited. The CROCI Super pads in extra large claim to be different, and after digging through 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, the consensus is clear: these are genuinely better than the drugstore basics, but they're not perfect.

## What You Actually Get

The CROCI line comes in three sizes — 23"x24", 28"x34", and the massive 30"x36" — so you can match the pad to your setup. The construction uses five layers: a soft top sheet, a distribution layer, super-absorbent polymer core, a leak-proof plastic backing, and rolled edges that prevent liquid from wicking sideways.

For a 70-pound dog or an x-pen setup, the 30"x36" is the one to grab. It covers roughly 7.5 square feet, which is enough for most senior dogs or puppies who like to spin before squatting.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the CROCI pads earn their keep. The absorbent core uses SAP (super-absorbent polymer) — the same stuff in quality diapers — which means liquid gets pulled down fast and locked away. One buyer noted: *"My 80-pound lab drinks like a fish and these hold up through two or three uses before I swap them out. No smell, no leaking through to my hardwood."*

The rolled edges matter more than you'd think. Cheap pads let urine pool at the edges and seep onto your floor. The CROCI's edge construction prevents that sideways wicking, which is the #1 complaint about budget pads.

The top sheet stays dry-ish between uses, which reduces the chance of urine scald on paws — a real concern for senior dogs or puppies with sensitive skin.

- **Absorption speed:** Liquid sinks fast rather than beading on top

## Where They Fall Short

No adhesive tabs. The CROCI pads rely on their own weight to stay put. On hardwood, tile, or laminate, a determined dog can push them around. You'll want a pad tray or double-sided tape underneath — especially for male dogs who lift a leg.

The scented version has a noticeable fragrance. Some buyers love it; others report their dog refused to use the pads entirely. If your dog is picky, order the unscented option. Also, the super-absorbent polymer can swell into a gel-like blob when saturated — that's normal, but it can feel odd underfoot if you don't change the pad promptly.
