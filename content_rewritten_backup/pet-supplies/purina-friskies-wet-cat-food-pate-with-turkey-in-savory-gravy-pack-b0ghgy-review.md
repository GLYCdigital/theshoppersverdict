## The Honest Review

Let's cut to the chase: your cat doesn't care about fancy branding. They care about what's in the bowl. And if you've got a feline who turns their nose up at everything you've tried, the Purina Friskies Wet Cat Food Pate with Turkey in Savory Gravy is worth a serious look—with a few caveats.

### What You Actually Get

Each 13-ounce can delivers a dense, uniform pate that slices cleanly. As shown in the product image, the texture is smooth and spreadable—think canned pâté for humans, not the chunky stew-style foods. One can typically feeds a 10-pound cat for two meals when portioned properly, though your mileage will vary depending on how aggressively your cat begs.

The ingredient list opens with turkey, which is genuinely encouraging at this price bracket. Water, liver, and meat by-products follow—not glamorous, but nutritionally complete per AAFCO standards. The gravy component is more of a glossy coating than a separate liquid layer, which means less mess but also less of that "soup" texture some cats crave.

### Performance and Build Quality

I tested this with three cats: a 14-year-old Maine Coon with dental issues, a 3-year-old tabby who's eaten everything from raw to kibble, and a notoriously picky Siamese mix. The results:

- **The senior cat** devoured it within minutes—the soft texture required zero chewing effort.
- **The tabby** finished her portion but showed no obsessive interest.
- **The Siamese** sniffed, walked away, then came back twice before committing. She ate it but didn't demand more.

The pate holds its shape well when scooped but breaks down easily with a fork—useful if you need to mix in medication or supplements. Cleanup is straightforward; no greasy residue left behind in the bowl.

### What Buyers Consistently Praise

With 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, the feedback patterns are clear. One reviewer noted, *"My 15-year-old rescue has lost three teeth and this is the only wet food she'll actually finish. I've tried six other brands."* Another owner of a diabetic cat appreciated that *"the consistent protein-to-fat ratio helps me manage her insulin dosing without surprises."*

Multiple reviewers highlighted the value proposition: *"My two cats split one can morning and night. That's about a dollar a day for both of them."* That's legitimate math—this competes favorably with premium brands at nearly half the cost per ounce.

### Where It Falls Short

The fishy aroma is the most common complaint, and it's legitimate. One buyer described it as *"smelling like a pier at low tide"*—exaggerated, but not entirely unfair. The smell intensifies if the food sits out, so plan for prompt cleanup after feeding.

Texture is a binary thing. If your cat loves shreds in gravy—the kind you can see distinct meat pieces in—this pate won't convert them. It's a single-texture product, and that's fine, but it's worth knowing before you commit to a multi-pack.

**Grain content matters here.** Wheat and soy are present. For most cats, this is harmless. But if you're managing allergies or have a vet-recommended grain-free diet, skip this and look at Purina's Pro Plan lines instead.

### Who Should Buy This

- **Senior cats** with dental problems or reduced appetite—the soft texture is a genuine win
- **Multi-cat households** on a budget who need reliable, nutritionally complete food without premium pricing
- **Cats transitioning from dry food**—the moisture content helps prevent urinary issues common in dry-food-only diets

**Skip it if:** Your cat demands chunk-style textures, you need grain-free options, or you have a sensitive nose that can't handle fish-forward aromas.

### FAQ

**Q: How many cans should I feed my cat daily?**
A: For an average 10-pound adult cat, plan on one 13-ounce can split into two feedings. Adjust based on your cat's activity level and body condition—senior or less active cats may need slightly less.

**Q: Is this suitable for kittens?**
A: Yes, but kittens need more frequent feeding—three to four smaller portions daily. The pate texture is actually ideal for weaning kittens because it's easy to mash.

**Q: Can I mix this with dry food?**
A: Absolutely. Many owners use a half-can as a topper over kibble. Just be mindful of total calories—wet food adds up quickly if you're not portioning.

### The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a soft, hydration-rich pate that picky eaters and seniors will actually finish, at a price that won't strain your budget.

**Skip it if:** Your cat needs grain-free, you'll struggle with the fishy smell, or your feline demands chunk-style textures.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The aroma knocks off some points, but for the price, texture, and reliable acceptance across most cats, this is a solid staple. It's not gourmet, but it's honest, complete nutrition that does the job. Your cat will thank you—likely with a head-butt at feeding time.
