# Tiki Cat Born Carnivore Broths Wet Cat Food Topper Review

## The Real Story Behind This 4.0-Star Topper

Finicky cats are the ultimate product testers. They don't care about marketing claims or pretty packaging—they either eat it or they don't. With 200 ratings averaging 4.0 stars, Tiki Cat's Born Carnivore Broths has clearly won over plenty of picky eaters. But is it worth adding to your cat's rotation? Let's dig into what you're actually getting.

## What's In the Box

Each package contains individual broth packets designed to be poured over dry food or mixed into wet food. The formula is built around the "born carnivore" philosophy—meaning it's heavy on animal-based ingredients and light on the fillers you'll find in many competing toppers. The texture is thin and soupy, not gelatinous like some bone broth products on the market.

## Real-World Performance

The primary job here is hydration and palatability. Cats are notoriously bad water drinkers, and this broth delivers moisture in a form most cats find irresistible. When poured over kibble, it softens the texture while adding a meaty aroma that seems to trigger even the most disinterested eaters.

The ingredient list is refreshingly straightforward—broth, meat, and minimal extras. There's no carrageenan, no artificial colors, and no vague "meat by-products" hiding in the formula. For cats with sensitive stomachs or allergies to common fillers, this clean profile makes a meaningful difference.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The most repeated compliment across reviews is picky cat acceptance. One reviewer noted their cat "who turns her nose up at everything" happily laps up the broth. Another mentioned it's become their "secret weapon" for getting medication mixed into food.

The variety pack approach also earns points. You get multiple protein options, which helps prevent flavor fatigue—a real issue with daily toppers.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. At this price point, you're paying for convenience and quality ingredients, not volume. Each packet is small, and if you're using it daily as directed, you'll go through a box quickly.

Some cats simply don't care for it. A subset of reviewers report their cats sniff and walk away—broth toppers are inherently hit-or-miss, and this one is no exception. The thin consistency also means it doesn't cling to kibble the way thicker gravies do; you'll need to stir thoroughly.

Packaging complaints appear occasionally, with some users finding the packets difficult to tear open without spilling. It's a minor annoyance, but worth noting if you're dealing with a hangry cat at mealtime.

## Who Should Buy This

This is ideal for:

- **The hydration-challenged cat**: If your cat barely touches their water bowl, this is a low-effort way to boost fluid intake
- **Senior cats with dental issues**: The soft texture makes eating easier for cats with sore gums or missing teeth
- **Picky eaters who've rejected other toppers**: The clean, meat-forward formula has a higher acceptance rate than heavily processed alternatives
- **Cats on restricted diets**: If your vet has banned fillers and additives, this fits the bill

Skip it if you're looking for a meal replacement—this is a supplement, not a complete food.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: How many servings come in a box?**
A: Each box contains multiple single-serving packets, with the exact count varying by pack size. Check the listing for the specific quantity before ordering.

**Q: Can I use this as a full meal?**
A: No. This is designed as a topper or hydration supplement. It lacks the complete nutritional profile cats need for balanced daily nutrition.

**Q: Does it need refrigeration after opening?**
A: Yes. Once a packet is opened, treat it like any fresh food product—refrigerate leftovers and use within a day or two.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** Your cat needs more hydration, turns up their nose at dry food, or you want a clean-ingredient topper without the junk. The convenience factor and picky-cat acceptance rate justify the cost for most households.

**Skip it if:** Your cat is already a good water drinker who inhales their meals without encouragement—you're paying for a solution to a problem you don't have.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A solid, well-formulated topper that delivers on its promises, with minor packaging and value caveats keeping it from perfection.
