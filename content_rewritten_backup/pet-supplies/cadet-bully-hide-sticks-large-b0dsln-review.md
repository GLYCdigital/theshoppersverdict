If you've ever tossed a traditional bully stick to a dog who's done with it in nine minutes flat, you already know the frustration — and the cost. The Cadet Bully Hide Sticks Large take a different approach: instead of a single dense muscle, they're made from braided beef hide that forces your dog to work for every bite. Whether that translates to lasting value or just a longer wait for the inevitable mess is what we're here to sort out.

## What You Actually Get

The Cadet Bully Hide Sticks Large are braided chews made from 100% beef hide — not rawhide, which is a critical distinction. Rawhide is typically treated with harsh chemicals during processing; these sticks skip that entirely. As shown in the product image, each stick is tightly braided, roughly 6 inches long, and dense enough that you can feel the weight when you pick one up. They come in a resealable bag, which matters because these do have a scent — more on that below.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The braided construction is the whole game here. A dog has to unravel the weave before they can swallow chunks, which naturally slows down consumption. For moderate chewers, owners report these lasting 45 minutes to a couple of hours per stick. That's dramatically better than the 10-minute meltdown you get with most standard bully sticks.

The texture softens as your dog works it, which is deliberate — it becomes pliable enough to avoid cracking teeth while still providing resistance. That's a thoughtful design choice, not an accident. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: "My lab is a shredder and these are the only chews that keep him busy without me worrying about him swallowing something dangerous."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 225 ratings, three themes dominate:

- **Low odor**: Multiple reviewers noted these have "almost no smell" compared to traditional bully sticks. That's a genuine win if you've ever gagged on the aroma of standard bully chews.
- **Digestibility**: Several customers with sensitive-stomach dogs reported no vomiting or diarrhea after use. That's the beef-hide-versus-rawhide difference showing up in practice.
- **Engagement**: Owners of high-energy breeds describe these as a "lifesaver" for redirecting destructive chewing.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides.

**Aggressive chewers will still win.** These are not for determined power chewers. Multiple buyers with pits and shepherds reported sticks gone in 20 minutes or less. If your dog has destroyed every "indestructible" toy you've bought, this is a good chew, not a miracle.

**Calorie load.** These are dense protein. For a 50-pound dog, one stick is a meaningful chunk of daily calories. If your dog is overweight or on a strict diet, you need to account for this or skip it entirely.

**Scent still exists.** While far milder than standard bully sticks, these aren't odorless. A few sensitive-nose reviewers said the smell was still noticeable in small rooms.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Moderate chewers**: Dogs who chew for enrichment rather than destruction will get the most value.
- **Indoor owners**: The reduced odor makes these viable for apartment living.
- **Sensitive-stomach dogs**: The simple beef hide formula works well for dogs who react badly to rawhide.

**Skip it if** your dog is a determined power chewer or if you need a zero-calorie chew option.

## FAQ

**How long does one stick last?**
For a moderate chewer, expect 30 minutes to 2 hours. Aggressive chewers may finish one in under 20 minutes.

**Is this safe for puppies?**
The 6-inch size and braided texture are appropriate for puppies over 6 months with adult teeth. Supervise the first use regardless.

**Can my dog eat the whole thing?**
Yes, the sticks are fully digestible beef hide. That said, always supervise chewing and remove any piece small enough to swallow whole.
