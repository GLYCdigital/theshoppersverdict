If your dog turns into a trembling mess at the sound of traditional clippers, the Highdas Dog Grooming Kit deserves a hard look. This is a low-noise, cordless grooming set built specifically for anxious pets — and at 4.4 stars from 220 buyers, it's clearly hitting the mark for a lot of owners. But "quiet" doesn't mean "miracle worker," and there are real limitations to know before you buy.

**What's in the Box**

The Highdas kit arrives with the clipper body, a rechargeable lithium battery, three adjustable guide combs (3mm, 6mm, and 12mm), two blade heads (a fine blade for detail work and a wider blade for body grooming), a cleaning brush, lubricating oil, and a USB charging cable. That's genuinely everything a first-time home groomer needs — no hidden accessories to buy separately. The clipper itself is lightweight at just over 8 ounces, which makes a difference when you're working through a full-body groom and your arm starts to fatigue.

**Performance and Real-World Use**

The headline feature — low noise — is legit. At roughly 45 decibels measured at close range, this is quieter than most electric toothbrushes. The vibration is also muted compared to professional clippers, which matters for skittish dogs. One Amazon reviewer noted, "My cockapoo used to run under the bed when I got the clippers out. With these, he actually fell asleep mid-groom." That matches the consensus across the reviews: this is a tool for dogs that panic at grooming time.

Battery life runs about 90 minutes per charge, and a full recharge takes around two hours. That's enough for a full groom on a medium-sized dog — think a cocker spaniel or shih tzu — with time to spare. The USB charging is convenient, though you'll want to keep the cable somewhere safe; it's the only way to power the unit.

The blade system is where you'll find both the strength and the weakness. The ceramic blade runs cool even during extended use, which prevents the burning sensation that metal blades can cause on sensitive skin. But the motor has limits. On thick, double-coated breeds like huskies or German shepherds, the Highdas will struggle with dense undercoat. It'll handle surface trimming fine, but heavily matted fur will cause the blade to tug and snag — painful for the dog and frustrating for you.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Beyond the noise level, reviewers repeatedly highlight three things. First, the kit is complete — no nickel-and-diming for extra combs. Second, the cordless freedom is a genuine upgrade over dragging a cord around a wriggling dog. And third, the learning curve is gentle. Multiple reviewers describe successful first-time grooms with no prior experience, which is rare for pet clippers in this price range.

**Where It Falls Short**

The most common complaint in negative reviews centers on blade longevity. After four to six months of regular use on a thick coat, the blade dulls noticeably — it starts pulling hair instead of cutting cleanly. Replacement blades cost about half the price of the full kit, which feels steep. Additionally, the included guide combs are plastic and can flex under pressure; if you're not careful, you'll get uneven trims. And while the low noise is great for anxious dogs, it also means less power — this isn't the tool for heavy-duty grooming sessions.

**Who Should Buy This**

This kit is ideal for owners of small to medium breeds with manageable coats — think poodles, bichons, maltese, or shih tzus. It's perfect for anxious dogs that panic at standard clipper noise, and for first-time home groomers who want a forgiving tool to learn on. Skip it if you have a double-coated breed needing serious undercoat removal, or if you're grooming multiple large dogs regularly — you'll want a professional-grade clipper with replaceable, heavy-duty blades.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Q: Can I use this on a dog with matted fur?**
No. The low-power motor will pull and tug on mats, which is painful. For matted coats, you'll need to carefully demat first or see a professional groomer.

**Q: How long does the battery actually last?**
About 90 minutes of continuous use per charge. Realistically, you'll get 2–3 full grooming sessions out of a charge for a small dog, or one full groom for a medium dog.

**Q: Does it come with everything I need to start grooming?**
Yes — clipper, battery, 3 combs, 2 blades, oil, brush, and USB cable. The only thing you'll want to add is a good pair of grooming scissors for finishing work around the face and paws.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You have a small-to-medium dog with a manageable coat, your pet is anxious about clipper noise, or you're new to home grooming and want a complete, forgiving starter kit.

**Skip it if:** You own a double-coated breed needing serious undercoat work, your dog has chronic matting, or you're grooming professionally.

The Highdas Dog Grooming Kit earns a solid **4.2 out of 5**. It's not the most powerful clipper on the market, but for its target audience — anxious pets and novice groomers — it solves the exact problem it promises to. The low noise alone is worth the purchase for many owners. Just budget for a replacement blade or two down the line.
