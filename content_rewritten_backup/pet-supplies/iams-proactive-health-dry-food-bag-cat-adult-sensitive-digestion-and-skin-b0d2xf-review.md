Let me be upfront: I’m not going to tell you this is the most premium cat food on Amazon. It isn’t. What I *will* tell you is that IAMS Proactive Health for Sensitive Digestion does exactly what it promises for the price, and that’s rarer than you’d think in this category.

**What’s actually in the bag**

You’re getting a 7-pound or 16-pound bag of dry kibble (check the listing for current sizing) formulated specifically for adult cats with delicate stomachs. The first ingredient is real chicken — not a by-product meal — followed by ground whole grain corn and chicken by-product meal. That’s not going to win awards from the raw-food crowd, but for a mass-market sensitive-stomach formula, the protein sourcing is respectable.

The functional ingredients matter more here: prebiotic fiber and beet pulp support healthy digestion, while omega-6 fatty acids and vitamin E target skin and coat health. This is a dual-purpose formula — it doesn’t just stop the runs, it’s also supposed to reduce itching and dander. The kibble itself is small and round, which multiple reviewers note is a blessing for senior cats with dental issues or kittens transitioning to adult food.

**Real-world performance**

Here’s where this food earns its 4.7-star rating. The most common thread across the 235+ reviews is that it stops chronic vomiting and loose stools — and it does it fast. One owner wrote, *"My 9-year-old tabby had diarrhea for two weeks. Switched to this and within three days, solid stools. She's been on it for six months now."* That’s the typical trajectory. Another reviewer with a Ragdoll prone to hairball-related vomiting reported a noticeable drop in episodes after two weeks.

The coat improvement is the sleeper benefit. Multiple reviewers mention their cats’ fur becoming noticeably softer and shinier within a month. That’s the omega-6 doing its job, and it’s a nice bonus when you’re primary goal is digestive peace.

**Where it falls short — and you need to hear this**

First, this is not grain-free. If your cat has a confirmed corn or wheat allergy, this food will make things worse, not better. It’s designed for *sensitive* stomachs, not *allergic* cats. Know the difference before you buy.

Second — and this is the complaint I see repeated most often — the bag has no resealable zipper. In a humid climate, this kibble can go stale within two weeks. Several reviewers specifically called this out, with one noting, *"Great food, terrible bag. I have to keep it in a Tupperware container or my cat won't touch it after day 10."* Budget for an airtight storage container if you buy this.

Third, the kibble is a bit high in carbohydrates, which is typical for this price point but worth knowing if your cat is overweight or diabetic.

**Who should buy this**

This is the right choice for multi-cat households on a budget where one cat has a sensitive stomach but the others eat anything. It’s also ideal for senior cats, first-time cat owners dealing with transition diarrhea, and anyone whose current food causes chronic hairball issues. It’s *not* the right choice for cats with diagnosed grain allergies, owners who insist on grain-free or novel-protein diets, or picky eaters who refuse anything they haven't seen before (though most cats take to this quickly due to the chicken-forward flavor).

**Frequently asked questions**

**Q: How fast will I see improvement in my cat's stool?**
Most owners report firmer stools within 3–5 days. Full digestive normalization typically takes 1–2 weeks. If you don't see improvement after 10 days, consult your vet.

**Q: Can I mix this with wet food?**
Yes. Many reviewers feed it alongside wet food. Just factor in the calorie adjustment — this is a complete and balanced food, so you'll need to reduce portions if adding wet food.

**Q: Is this good for kittens?**
The formula is labeled for adult cats (1+ years). For kittens, IAMS makes a separate Kitten formula with higher protein and calories. Don't substitute this one.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You need a proven, affordable sensitive-stomach formula that actually stops vomiting and diarrhea without a prescription diet, or you want a coat-improving kibble that won't break the bank.

**Skip it if:** Your cat has a confirmed grain allergy, you refuse to buy foods with corn or wheat, or you don't want to deal with storing kibble in a separate container.
