If your cat inhales food like it's their last meal and then hacks it back up ten minutes later, you've probably tried every "anti-vomit" bowl on Amazon. Most are gimmicks. This one, the Wellbro Ceramic Raised Cat Bowl, actually has the engineering and the reviews to back up its claims. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, it's not perfect — but for the right cat, it might be the last bowl you buy.

## What You Actually Get

The Wellbro is a single-piece ceramic bowl with a raised, tilted feeding surface. The angle is roughly 15 degrees, which promotes a more natural head position while eating. It's not adjustable — the tilt is baked into the design. The bowl itself is substantial; it's heavy enough that most cats won't tip it, and the ceramic is thick with a smooth, glossy glaze. No plastic, no metal aftertaste, no seams where bacteria can hide. It's also dishwasher-safe, which is a bigger deal than most people realize when you're washing pet dishes twice a day.

## Real-World Performance

The core claim here is "anti-vomit," and that's where the Wellbro earns its keep. The raised, angled position slows down gulping and reduces the amount of air swallowed with each bite. Multiple verified buyers report their cats stopped regurgitating within days. One reviewer noted, *"My ragdoll used to throw up almost daily. Three weeks with this bowl and we've had zero incidents. The angle makes her eat slower naturally."* Another owner of a senior cat with arthritis appreciated that the raised height meant less bending — a genuine benefit for older pets with joint issues.

The ceramic construction is a real plus. Unlike plastic bowls that scratch and trap bacteria (a known cause of feline acne), the glazed surface is non-porous and easy to sanitize. Owners who switched from plastic or metal reported fewer chin breakouts and less whisker fatigue.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the flaws. The most common complaint in the reviews: there's no rubber grip on the bottom. The bowl relies entirely on its weight to stay put. On hardwood or carpet, that's fine. On slick tile or laminate, a determined cat can still push it around. Several reviewers solved this with a silicone placemat underneath, but at this price point, a non-slip ring should be standard.

The fixed tilt is also a limitation. Some cats simply prefer eating from a flat surface and will push food out of the bowl to get it. If your cat is picky about bowl geometry, this may not be the solution. And while the ceramic is durable, it's still ceramic — drop it on a hard floor and it will chip. It's not indestructible.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** Your cat is a fast eater who regurgitates after meals, you have a senior cat with mobility issues, or you're tired of scrubbing bacteria out of plastic bowls.

**Skip it if:** Your cat is a slow, meticulous eater with no vomiting issues, you have slick tile floors and a cat that pushes bowls around, or you need an adjustable-height solution for a growing kitten.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this bowl really dishwasher-safe?**
A: Yes, the glaze holds up fine in the dishwasher. Several reviewers mention running it through daily cycles with no fading or cracking. Just avoid the sanitize cycle with extreme heat.

**Q: Will this help with my cat's vomiting?**
A: It helps with regurgitation caused by eating too fast. If your cat vomits due to hairballs, food allergies, or medical issues, this bowl won't fix that. It's a mechanical solution, not a medical one.

**Q: What's the capacity?**
A: It holds roughly 1.5 cups of food — sufficient for most cats' meals. If you free-feed multiple cats, you'll want a second bowl.

## The Verdict

The Wellbro Ceramic Raised Cat Bowl does exactly what it promises for the majority of buyers. It's well-made, easy to clean, and the tilted design genuinely reduces vomiting in fast-eating cats. The lack of a non-slip base is an annoying oversight, but not a dealbreaker for most households. At 4.6 stars, it's a solid investment for any cat parent dealing with the dreaded post-meal hairball sound.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Buy it if your cat eats too fast. Skip it if they're already fine with their current bowl.
