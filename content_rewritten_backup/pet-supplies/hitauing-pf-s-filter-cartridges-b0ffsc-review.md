**Buying replacement filters for a pet fountain is a chore — you want it to fit, work, and not cost more than the fountain itself.** The HiTauing PF-S cartridges claim to do all three. After digging through 229 ratings and testing compatibility across the most common PF-S fountains, here’s what actually matters.

**What you get**  
The box includes four rectangular filter cartridges, each about 3.5 x 2.5 x 0.5 inches. Each has the standard three layers: a coarse pre-filter sponge on top, a carbon-impregnated felt middle, and a fine non-woven bottom sheet. No tools, no adapters — they’re designed as a direct swap for the PetSafe Drinkwell, Veken, and a handful of other PF-S models that use the same tray shape.

**Real-world performance**  
I tested these in a 50oz PetSafe fountain with municipal tap water (moderate hardness). The fit is the headline: they drop in flush, no gaps, no wobble. The pump stayed quiet, and water flow didn’t drop off — a common complaint with off-brand filters that pack the carbon too tight.  

Filtration holds up for about two weeks of constant use. At day 7, there’s zero film or smell. By day 12, I noticed a faint chlorine hint if I sniffed the water stream directly — not enough for my cat to refuse it, but noticeable. By day 16, the carbon was spent. That’s slightly shorter than the 3–4 week lifespan of the OEM version, which matters if you’re forgetful about swaps.

**What buyers consistently praise**  
Scrolling through the reviews, three themes dominate. First, **fit reliability**: “Finally a filter that doesn’t float or let water bypass — my Veken fountain runs perfectly,” wrote one verified buyer. Second, **value**: a 4-pack at this price is roughly half the cost of two OEM cartridges. Third, **odor control**: multiple pet owners of both cats and dogs noted the fountain water smelled “cleaner than the sink” for the first week.  

**Where it falls short**  
The thinner foam layer is the weak point. If you’re aggressive when cleaning the pump and push the cartridge down hard, the foam edge can curl, letting unfiltered water slip around. It’s fixable — just align it before snapping the pump housing — but it’s a step you don’t need with the pricier OEM parts. Also, if your water is very hard (over 150 ppm), the ion-exchange resin will exhaust faster; you’ll see white scale on the filter surface by day 10. That’s a chemistry limit, not a defect.

**Who should buy this**  
Get the HiTauing PF-S if you own any PF-S-compatible fountain, change filters on a regular schedule, and don’t want to pay OEM markup. It’s especially good for multi-pet households where you’re replacing filters monthly — the 4-pack covers two months and saves you a reorder.  

Skip it if you have extremely hard water and need maximum carbon life, or if you’re the type who forgets swaps for a month — the cheaper build won’t forgive neglect like the OEM will.

**FAQ**  
*Q: Will these fit my PetSafe Drinkwell 360?*  
A: No. The PF-S designation fits the 50oz and 84oz models (like the Drinkwell 2.0 and Veken 84oz). The 360 uses a round filter. Double-check your fountain’s model number before ordering.

*Q: How often should I replace them?*  
A: Every 2–3 weeks for clear water and no odor. If you have one pet, stretch to 3 weeks. With two or more, stick to 2 weeks — the carbon depletes faster with higher throughput.

*Q: Can I rinse and reuse these?*  
A: Rinsing the surface foam is fine, but the carbon layer is non-rechargeable. Once it’s saturated, rinsing does nothing. Plan on discarding them.
