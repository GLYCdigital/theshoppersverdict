## Where It Falls Short

**No suet cake is truly melt-proof.** In direct, unshaded sun at 90°F+, these will soften and can sag. Mount them in shade or use a feeder with a roof. That's not a defect — it's physics.

**The "high energy" formula attracts squirrels.** If you have a determined squirrel population, they will chew through the mesh to get at these. Budget for a squirrel-proof feeder or accept the theft.

**Texture is crumbly at the edges.** The cakes hold together in the middle but the corners can flake off when you handle them. Use a feeder with a tight mesh, or you'll get small crumbs on the ground (which the birds will eat anyway, so it's minor).

**No cage or tray included.** This is a straight 10-pack of cakes. If you don't already own a suet feeder, factor that into your total cost.
