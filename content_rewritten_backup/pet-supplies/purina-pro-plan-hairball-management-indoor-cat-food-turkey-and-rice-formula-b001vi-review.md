## What You Actually Get

This is a 3.5-pound bag of dry kibble, though you'll also find it in larger sizes. The formula features real turkey as the first ingredient, followed by rice, corn gluten meal, and wheat gluten. It's designed specifically for indoor cats — which means it addresses the two biggest indoor cat issues: hairballs and weight management. The kibble size is modest, easy for most adult cats to crunch, and the turkey flavor is generally well-received.

## How It Works

The science here is straightforward. The formula contains a natural fiber blend — psyllium husk and oat fiber — that helps move swallowed hair through the digestive tract instead of letting it accumulate in the stomach. It also includes a moderate fat content (around 12% minimum) to keep calories in check for less active indoor cats.

In real-world use, this translates to fewer hairballs. One verified buyer noted, "My long-haired cat had hairballs every week. After two weeks on this food, we're down to maybe once a month." Another reviewer said, "My vet recommended this after my cat had a blockage. We've been using it for 6 months with zero issues."

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

First, **corn and wheat gluten are present.** Some cats don't tolerate grains well, and if yours has a sensitive stomach, this might not be the best choice. You'll want to watch for digestive upset during the transition period.

Second, **it's not a complete indoor cat solution.** While it handles hairballs and weight, it doesn't include urinary health support — a common issue in male indoor cats. If your cat has a history of urinary problems, you might need to look at Pro Plan's Urinary Health formula instead.

Third, **transition time matters.** Some reviewers noted initial vomiting or loose stools when switching too quickly. You need to mix it with the old food over 7-10 days.

## Who Should Buy This

**Q: How long until I see a difference in hairballs?**
A: Most owners report improvement within 2-3 weeks, with significant reduction by the end of the first bag. Full effect usually takes 4-6 weeks as the fiber builds up in the system.

**Q: Can I feed this to an outdoor cat?**
A: Yes, but the indoor formula has a specific calorie profile for less active cats. An active outdoor cat may need more food or a different formula.

**Q: Is this good for kittens?**
A: No — this is formulated for adult cats. Kittens need higher calories and different nutrient ratios for growth.
