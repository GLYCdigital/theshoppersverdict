**Is this the kibble that finally settles your dog's stomach? Or just another overpriced bag of promises?**

If you've spent months watching your dog scratch, gurgle, or turn up their nose at dinner, you know the drill: vet visits, elimination diets, and a cabinet full of half-eaten bags. Holistic Select's Chicken Meal & Rice Recipe positions itself as the middle-ground answer — a natural formula with digestive support built in, without the boutique $90-per-bag price tag. After digging through 229 ratings and the ingredient panel, here's the real story.

**What's actually in the bag**

This is a 13.5-ounce sample size or standard 25-pound bag depending on your order — check the listing carefully because the price varies wildly by size. The formula leads with chicken meal (a concentrated protein source, not the watery "chicken" that cooks down to nothing), followed by ground brown rice and ground white rice. You're getting a straightforward poultry-and-carb base with no corn, wheat, soy, or artificial preservatives.

The headline feature is the "ActivBiome+" blend: proprietary probiotics, prebiotics, and digestive enzymes. That's not marketing fluff — the inclusion of *Lactobacillus acidophilus* and *Bacillus subtilis* is genuinely rare in this price bracket. Most sub-$50 bags stop at "added vitamins," so this is a meaningful differentiator.

**Real-world performance**

The 4.6-star average holds up when you read between the lines. The most frequent praise isn't about shiny coats or energy levels — it's about digestion. One owner wrote, "My mini schnauzer had loose stools for two years. Switched to this and within a week, solid. I'm not exaggerating." Another noted, "Our vet recommended a prescription GI diet that cost triple. This has the same effect for us."

Owners of senior dogs and breeds prone to gas (looking at you, boxers and bulldogs) reported noticeably less flatulence and better stool quality within 10-14 days. That's a realistic timeline for the enzymes to colonize the gut — anyone promising overnight miracles is lying.

**Where it stumbles**

The most consistent complaint is kibble size. These are small, flat discs — fine for toy breeds and puppies, but a 70-pound lab will inhale them without chewing. If you have a gulper, you'll want a slow-feeder bowl or need to size up to a large-breed formula.

The second issue is palatability. A meaningful minority of dogs flat-out refused it. One reviewer said, "My golden retriever sniffed it and walked away. I had to mix in wet food for a week before she'd touch it." If your dog is already a picky eater, order the small size first rather than committing to a 25-pound bag.

**Who should actually buy this**

- **Sensitive-stomach dogs**: This is the sweet spot. If your vet has ruled out allergies but your dog still has intermittent loose stool, the probiotic blend is worth trying.
- **Budget-conscious natural-food buyers**: You're getting a clean ingredient panel and digestive support at roughly half the cost of comparable brands like Nutro or Wellness.
- **Puppies and small breeds**: The small kibble size is ideal, and the calcium-to-phosphorus ratio supports growth.

**Skip it if** your dog is a massive, fast-eating breed (the kibble's too small) or if they're already thriving on a fish-based or grain-free diet — there's no reason to switch a happy stomach.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this good for dogs with chicken allergies?**  
No. Chicken meal is the primary protein. If your dog reacts to chicken, look at the salmon or lamb variants instead.

**Q: How fast will I see digestive improvements?**  
Most owners report changes in 7-14 days. Transition slowly over a week to avoid shocking the system.

**Q: Is this actually "holistic"?**  
The term is unregulated, but the formula does include whole-food ingredients like pumpkin and papaya, plus the enzyme blend. It's a legitimate effort, not just a label.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** your dog needs digestive support without a prescription diet. **Skip it if** you have a picky eater or a giant breed that needs chunkier kibble.

At 4.6 stars, this is one of the most reliable mid-priced digestive-care kibbles on Amazon. It won't fix every dog's issues, but for the majority of sensitive-stomach cases, it delivers on its promises. Just grab the small bag first to test the waters.
