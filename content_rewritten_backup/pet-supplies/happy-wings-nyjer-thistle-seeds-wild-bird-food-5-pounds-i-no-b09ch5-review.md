## FAQ

**Q: Does this seed work in all thistle feeders?**
Yes. Nyjer seeds are uniformly small and work in mesh feeders, tube feeders with small ports, and even platform feeders with fine mesh bottoms. Just avoid mixing it with larger seeds in the same tube — the bigger seeds block access.

**Q: How long does the 5-pound bag stay fresh once opened?**
If you press the air out and seal the zipper fully, expect it to stay viable for 4-6 weeks. Keep it in a cool, dry spot away from direct sunlight. Don't store it in a hot garage — heat accelerates oil degradation.

**Q: Will this attract other birds besides finches?**
Occasionally you'll see pine siskins and the occasional chickadee. Dark-eyed juncos may forage underneath for fallen seeds. But the primary visitors will be goldfinches and house finches — that's exactly what this seed is designed for.
