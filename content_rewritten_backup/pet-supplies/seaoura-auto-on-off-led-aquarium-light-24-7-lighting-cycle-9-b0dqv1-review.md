**The Short Version:** If you're tired of manually flipping your aquarium light on and off every morning and night, the SEAOURA LED light with its 24/7 auto cycle actually delivers on that promise. It's not a powerhouse light for demanding planted tanks, but for community fish tanks and low-light setups, it's a genuinely convenient, well-built option that's earned its 4.2-star rating across 210 reviews.

---

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains the LED light bar itself, two mounting clips, a power adapter, and a basic instruction sheet. The unit is lightweight — roughly 12 ounces for the standard size — and the aluminum housing feels sturdier than the price point suggests. The gooseneck-style clips rotate to fit tank rims up to about 1.5 cm thick, and the light bar extends to cover tanks in the 12–18 inch range depending on the model you select. As shown in the product image, it's a slim profile that won't dominate your tank's aesthetics.

**The 24/7 Cycle — The Real Selling Point**

Here's what actually sets this apart from cheaper clip-on lights: the built-in 24-hour cycle mimics a natural day. Over a 30-minute window, the light gradually ramps up from a soft dawn glow to full brightness, holds steady through the day, then fades into a gentle dusk before turning off completely. Fish that bolt when lights suddenly click on — I'm looking at you, tetras and angelfish — adapt much faster to this gradual transition.

The auto on/off function is the headline feature, and it works. Once you set the current time, the light handles everything. Multiple buyers noted this convenience in their reviews, with one writing, "I set it up and haven't touched it in three weeks. It just works." That's the honest experience for most users.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The LED chips produce a clean white light with a slight blue accent that makes fish colors pop without looking artificial. Color rendering is decent — reds and blues on fish like guppies or neon tetras come through naturally, not washed out. Heat management is solid; the aluminum body stays warm to the touch but never hot, even after a full 10-hour cycle.

Coverage is even across the tank with no obvious dark spots or hot zones at the edges. For a standard 10-gallon community tank, this light provides adequate illumination for viewing and supports low-light plants like java fern and anubias. Just don't expect to grow a carpet of dwarf baby tears under this thing — it's simply not that powerful.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Beyond the auto cycle, reviewers frequently mention the minimalist design. One verified purchase noted, "Finally a light that doesn't look like a spaceship landed on my tank." The low-profile clips are genuinely unobtrusive, and the light sits flush against the rim.

Another recurring positive is the silent operation — no buzzing from the driver, no transformer hum. For a bedroom tank, that matters more than you'd think.

**Where It Falls Short**

The biggest limitation is the lack of intensity control. There's no dimmer, so if the full brightness is too much for your setup, you're stuck with it. Several reviewers mentioned this as a frustration, especially those with light-sensitive fish or tanks positioned near windows.

The timer reset issue is the other notable gripe. If you lose power — even briefly — the clock resets, and the light defaults back to whatever the factory time was. You'll need to reprogram it. For a product marketed on convenience, this is a genuine annoyance. One reviewer put it plainly: "Power blinked during a storm and suddenly my fish are getting 'sunrise' at 3 AM."

**Who Should Buy This**

This light is ideal for:
- **Beginner fish keepers** who want a set-and-forget solution
- **Community tank owners** with tetras, barbs, livebearers, or cichlids
- **Low-light planted tanks** with java fern, moss, or crypts
- **Anyone tired of remembering to turn lights on and off daily**

Skip it if you're running a high-tech planted tank with CO2 injection and demanding stem plants. You need a stronger, dimmable light with programmable intensity.

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this light support live plants?**
A: Low-light plants only. Java fern, anubias, and moss will survive and grow slowly. High-light carpeting plants or red stem plants will struggle.

**Q: How long does the light stay on each day?**
A: The built-in cycle runs roughly 10–12 hours of light total, with gradual dawn and dusk transitions built in. You can't customize the duration — it's a fixed cycle.

**Q: Does it work on rimmed tanks?**
A: Yes, the clips adjust to fit rims up to about 1.5 cm thick. Rimless tanks work too since the clips grip from the top edge.
