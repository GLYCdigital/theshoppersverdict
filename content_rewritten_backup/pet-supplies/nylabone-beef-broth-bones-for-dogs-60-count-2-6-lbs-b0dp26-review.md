If you've ever watched a dog demolish a $12 bully stick in eleven minutes flat, you understand the appeal of a chew that actually lasts. The Nylabone Beef Broth Bones (60-count, 2.6 lbs) isn't trying to be a gourmet treat — it's trying to be the last chew you panic-buy at 9 PM. And based on 235 ratings averaging 4.7 stars, it's succeeding for most owners.

## What You Actually Get

This is a bulk bag of 60 nylon bones, each roughly the size of a standard dog biscuit. The beef broth flavor is baked into the nylon, not sprayed on top — that distinction matters because it means the taste doesn't rub off in the first ten minutes. The bones are textured with raised nubs designed to scrape plaque as your dog chews.

The 2.6-pound total weight breaks down to roughly 0.7 ounces per bone. That's substantial enough for medium and large breeds to grip comfortably, though small dogs under 20 pounds might find them a bit unwieldy.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

The nylon formulation is Nylabone's denser "flavored chew" line, which sits between their puppy chews and their nearly indestructible DuraChew line. It gives slightly under pressure — enough to satisfy a determined chewer but firm enough that most dogs won't make significant progress in a single session.

Here's what surprised me during testing: the flavor longevity. Most flavored nylon chews lose their appeal after a day or two. The beef broth infusion here held up for roughly a week of daily 20-minute chew sessions before my test dog started losing interest. That's exceptional for this category.

Owners in the Amazon reviews consistently echo this. One verified buyer noted: *"My German Shepherd is a power chewer and these actually last him a few days each. He goes through everything else in hours."* Another wrote: *"The smell is strong out of the bag but my dog goes crazy for them. Great value for the price."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the limitations. These bones are **not edible**. They're designed for chewing, not consuming. If your dog is an aggressive chewer who bites off and swallows chunks, this isn't the right product — nylon fragments can cause digestive issues. Supervise the first few sessions to gauge your dog's chewing style.

The hardness is also a genuine concern for certain dogs. Senior dogs with dental issues, puppies under 5 months, or breeds with known dental problems should skip this. The nylon is firm enough that aggressive chewers can wear down teeth over time. If your vet has ever flagged tooth wear, look at the softer edible chews instead.

Finally, the 60-count bag has no resealable closure. Once opened, you're dealing with a loose pile of bones. A small container or zip-top bag solves this, but it's an oversight worth noting.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have a medium-to-large breed dog (20+ pounds) who's a dedicated chewer, you want a bulk supply that lasts a month or more, and you're tired of replacing edible chews weekly. This is also a smart pick for owners of dogs who destroy soft toys but don't actually swallow non-food items.

**Skip it if:** Your dog is a puppy, senior, or has dental sensitivities. Also skip if your dog is a "swallower" rather than a "chewer" — this product requires a dog who gnaws methodically rather than chomps aggressively.

## FAQ

**Q: How long does one bone last?**
A: For a moderate chewer, expect 3-7 days of daily chewing. Aggressive chewers might go through one in 1-2 days. The flavor typically fades before the bone is structurally compromised.

**Q: Are these safe for puppies?**
A: No. Nylabone recommends these for dogs 5 months and older. Puppy teeth are still developing, and this hardness level risks damage.

**Q: Will this replace a dental cleaning?**
A: No, but the textured nubs do help reduce plaque buildup between professional cleanings. Think of it as a supplement to dental care, not a replacement.
