## The Corner Solution That Actually Delivers Space

Most "jumbo" bird cages are just tall versions of the same rectangular box. The Flyline Parrot Escape Jumbo Corner Cage does something different — it wraps around a corner, giving your bird significantly more horizontal room without dominating your living space. As shown in the product image, the angled front panel and deep interior create a footprint that tucks neatly into a 90-degree wall corner. For anyone with a large parrot and a room that isn't a warehouse, that geometry matters.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes the cage itself, a removable stainless steel bottom tray, two food cups with covers, two acrylic perch cups, a wooden perch, and a play top with a ladder. The cage measures roughly 32 inches across the front, 24 inches deep at the sides, and stands 68 inches tall including the stand. Bar spacing is 5mm — tight enough for a conure but spacious enough for a cockatoo or Amazon.

The powder-coated finish is smooth and even, without the rough spots or drips you see on cheaper cages. The wire bars are welded properly, not just crimped, which is the difference between a cage that lasts five years and one that starts separating at the joints.

## Real-World Performance

This is a heavy cage — around 90 pounds fully assembled. That's a feature, not a bug. The weight keeps it stable even when an energetic cockatoo is doing acrobatics on the play top. The four locking casters roll smoothly on hardwood and low-pile carpet, and two of them lock securely.

The corner design does create one quirk: the interior is deeper at the sides than in the center. Your bird gets a more complex, interesting space, but you'll need to reach further to clean the back corners. The removable tray slides out from the front, which helps, but plan on using a hand vac for stray seed in the tray channels.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Owners of larger parrots are the most enthusiastic reviewers. One cockatoo owner put it plainly: *"My bird has finally stopped pacing. He actually flies across the cage — something he couldn't do in his old one."* Multiple reviewers mention the cage surviving enthusiastic chewing sessions without damage. The play top gets frequent shout-outs too — it's large enough for a bird to actually spread out, not just perch awkwardly.

## Where It Falls Short

Assembly is the biggest complaint, and it's legitimate. The instructions are minimal — mostly line drawings with no written steps. Plan on 90 minutes to two hours, and keep a second pair of hands available for aligning the panels. Several reviewers noted that the bolts aren't labeled, which means some trial and error.

The seed guards are functional but shallow. If your bird is a messy eater (and most large parrots are), expect seed hulls on the floor around the cage. The door latch is secure but not locking — if you have a clever escape artist, you'll want to add a small padlock.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if** you have a medium-to-large parrot (African grey, Amazon, cockatoo, large conure) and want maximum interior space without sacrificing your entire room. It's also a strong pick if you've had a smaller cage that your bird has outgrown — the difference in usable space is dramatic.

**Skip it if** you have a tiny apartment with narrow hallways (moving this through doorways is awkward), or if you're not comfortable with a moderately challenging assembly project. For a small bird like a budgie or cockatiel, this is overkill — you'd be paying for space they'll never use.

## FAQ

**Is the bar spacing safe for small parrots?**
Yes — 5mm spacing is fine for conures, senegals, and other mid-size birds. For tiny birds like parrotlets or finches, you'd want tighter spacing, but this cage isn't designed for them anyway.

**Does it fit through standard doorways?**
The cage is 32 inches wide at the front, but the depth at the sides is about 24 inches. Most standard interior doors are 30-32 inches, so it fits, but barely. You may need to angle it through.

**Can the play top support a heavy bird?**
Yes. The top is reinforced steel with a solid platform. Multiple reviewers with cockatoos and macaws report using it daily without any bending or flexing.
