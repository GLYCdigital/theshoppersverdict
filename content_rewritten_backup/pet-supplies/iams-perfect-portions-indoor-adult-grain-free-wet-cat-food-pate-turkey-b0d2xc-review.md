Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The twice-a-day feeding schedule is rigid — if your cat prefers to graze throughout the day, this format forces a routine on them (and on you). One reviewer noted, *"My cat ate the first tray but refused the second — she's just not a pate cat. I ended up mixing it with dry food to get her to finish it."*

There's also the calorie question. This is an indoor-specific formula, which means it's intentionally lower in fat and calories. If you have a cat that goes outside or is naturally high-energy, you may need to feed more than the recommended amount to maintain their weight — which eats into the value proposition.
