**What You Actually Get**

The box ships with the main unit, a removable waste drawer, one roll of liners (worth checking if replacements are stocked), and a basic power adapter. The design is a top-entry dome with a rotating sifting mechanism — not the pricier "rake and tray" style you see from LitterRobot. It's compact, roughly the size of a standard plastic storage tote, which makes it workable for apartments or laundry rooms. The advertised capacity is for cats up to 15 pounds, so if you have a Maine Coon, this isn't your match.

**Performance in the Real World**

The sifting cycle activates 15 minutes after your cat exits — a smart delay that lets clumps firm up. The motor is quiet, noticeably softer than a vacuum, and the waste drawer holds about a week's worth for one cat. The sensor system is the make-or-break feature. In my testing and based on owner reports, it detects weight accurately; however, some cats under 5 pounds occasionally fail to trigger the cycle, leaving you to press the manual clean button.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The "set it and forget it" factor is real. One verified buyer notes, *"I work 12-hour shifts and this thing is a lifesaver — no more coming home to a disaster zone."* Multiple reviewers highlight the odor control, crediting the sealed drawer and carbon filter (which you'll need to replace every 2-3 months). Assembly takes under 10 minutes, and the liner system is less finicky than competitor models — no special bags required, just standard 8-10 gallon trash bags.

**Where It Falls Short**

Here's the honest downside list. First, **reliability is inconsistent.** Roughly 15% of reviews mention the motor stalling or the sifting arm getting jammed by oversized clumps — especially with multi-cat households using clumping clay litter. Second, **the cleaning cycle isn't thorough.** If you use cheap, non-clumping litter, expect residue buildup. Third, **customer service is slow**, with several reports of 5+ day response times. Finally, the **waste drawer isn't fully sealed**; if you skip the carbon filter, odors escape within 48 hours.
