If your cat greets you at 5 AM like a furry alarm clock with claws, the Cat Mate C200 is designed to buy you a few extra hours of sleep. This isn't a smart feeder with Wi-Fi and an app — it's a mechanical, two-compartment device that does exactly one thing: opens a lid at a set time to reveal food. At 4.4 stars from 220 raters, it's clearly working for many households. But is it right for yours?

**What You Actually Get**

The C200 arrives fully assembled. You get the main feeder body with a clear plastic dome lid, two 1.5-cup capacity bowls, two reusable ice packs, and a manual. The unit measures roughly 15 x 10 x 5 inches — compact enough for a countertop or laundry room floor. Each compartment has its own rotating timer dial on top, letting you set the left side and right side to open at different times, up to 48 hours out. This makes it genuinely useful for weekend trips where you're gone overnight but don't want to leave a full bowl out to get stale.

**Real-World Performance**

The mechanism is refreshingly simple. You rotate the dial to the current time, then push in and turn to the desired feeding time. When the timer hits zero, the lid springs open. No batteries, no app setup, no firmware updates. That simplicity is both the strength and the weakness. Several reviewers noted the dial takes practice — one called it "fiddly but workable," while another said, "I set it 10 minutes fast to compensate for my clumsy setting." The lid springs open with enough force to startle a cat the first time, but most adapt within a day or two.

The included ice packs are a thoughtful touch. They slide into a tray beneath the bowls and genuinely help keep wet food cool for several hours. In summer, this is the difference between a happy cat and a bowl of room-temperature mush. For dry food, the hinged clear lid keeps kibble fresh and lets your cat see the meal waiting — which seems to reduce the "where's my food" caterwauling.

**What Owners Consistently Praise**

The dual-timer flexibility gets the most love. One verified buyer wrote: "I work 12-hour shifts and now my cat gets breakfast at 6 AM and dinner at 6 PM even when I'm not home. The two sides are perfect." Another noted, "My cat learned within a week that the lid opens at 7 AM. She sits and watches it now instead of screaming at me." Multiple reviewers with diabetic cats praised the consistent portion control for timed feedings.

**Where It Falls Short**

The plastic body is functional but not rugged. If you have a 15-pound cat who likes to push things around, the C200 can scoot across the floor. One reviewer reported their cat learned to wedge a paw under the lid and pry it open early — the latch isn't childproof, and determined cats can defeat it. The 1.5-cup capacity per side also means this won't work for large-breed dogs or multiple cats sharing one feeder. Cleanup is straightforward — bowls are dishwasher-safe top-rack — but the main unit is not.

**Who Should Buy This**

This feeder suits single-cat households or owners with predictable schedules who need one, maybe two, timed meals. It's ideal for wet food users who want freshness, thanks to the ice packs. It's also a smart choice for people who distrust smart-home gadgets and want something that works even during a power outage. If you need portion control across multiple days, a gravity feeder or programmable dispenser with larger capacity will serve you better.

**FAQ**

**Can I use this for dry food only?** Yes, and it works well. The ice packs are optional. Just leave them out if you're feeding kibble.

**How do I set the timer correctly?** Rotate the dial until the current time aligns with the arrow, then push the dial inward and turn to the desired feeding time. Release to lock. Practice once before trusting it with a real meal.

**Will this work for a dog?** It's designed for cats. Small dogs under 15 pounds might manage, but most dogs will knock it over or figure out the lid latch quickly.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you need a dependable, no-frills way to deliver two meals on a schedule, especially for wet food with the ice packs. **Skip it if** you have a determined escape-artist cat, need multi-day capacity, or want app-based control.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid, simple, and effective for its niche. Just don't expect it to outsmart a clever cat.
