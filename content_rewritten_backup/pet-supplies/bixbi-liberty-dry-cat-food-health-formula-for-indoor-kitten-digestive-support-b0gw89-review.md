## Is BIXBI Liberty Actually Worth It for Your Kitten?

Most kitten foods are either packed with filler or priced like they're made of gold dust. BIXBI Liberty sits in a sweet spot: a small-batch-style recipe with genuinely clean ingredients that doesn't require a second mortgage. But "clean" doesn't automatically mean "right for your cat." Here's what 225 Amazon reviewers and the ingredient panel actually tell you.

## What's in the Bag

You get a 5-pound or 10-pound resealable bag of small, dark-brown kibble. The first ingredient is deboned chicken — real meat, not chicken meal. That's followed by chicken meal (a concentrated protein source, which is fine), then brown rice, pearled barley, and chicken fat preserved with mixed tocopherols (vitamin E, not chemical preservatives).

The standout addition is pumpkin. It's a natural source of soluble fiber that helps firm up stools and ease digestion — a genuinely thoughtful choice for kittens, who are notorious for sensitive stomachs. There's also dried Bacillus subtilis fermentation product, a probiotic that supports gut health.

As shown in the product image, the kibble is appropriately sized for kitten mouths, though it's on the crunchier side.

## Performance and Real-World Use

Kittens need roughly twice the calories per pound of body weight compared to adult cats, and this formula delivers 429 kcal/cup — solid energy density. The crude protein sits at 36%, which meets AAFCO growth requirements without going overboard.

The most common praise in reviews centers on picky eaters. One buyer noted, *"My 4-month-old ragdoll turned her nose up at three other brands before I tried this. She finished her first bowl in under two minutes."* Another reviewer with a multi-cat household mentioned that even their adult cat tries to steal it — a good sign for palatability.

The digestive benefits also show up consistently. Several reviewers report firmer stools within a week of switching, and fewer hairball incidents. That's the pumpkin and probiotic working as intended.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has tradeoffs worth knowing.

**It's not grain-free.** If you're strictly avoiding grains, this contains brown rice and barley. That's a dealbreaker for some owners, even though these whole grains are actually digestible and provide energy.

**The transition matters.** Multiple reviewers mention loose stools when they switched cold turkey. You need a 7-10 day gradual mixing period — not ideal if you're in an emergency switch situation.

**Kibble hardness.** A few reviewers with very young kittens (under 10 weeks) noted the pieces are slightly hard, recommending soaking in warm water for the first few weeks.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:**
- You want a first ingredient of real meat without paying boutique prices
- Your kitten has a sensitive stomach or you're dealing with soft stools
- You prefer a US-sourced, independently manufactured food (BIXBI is a Colorado company, and their dry food is made in the US)
- You want a clean ingredient list without artificial colors, flavors, or preservatives

**Skip it if:**
- You require a grain-free recipe
- Your kitten has a confirmed chicken allergy (this is chicken-heavy)
- You're looking for a raw or freeze-dried topper-style diet — this is kibble only

## FAQ

**Q: How much should I feed my kitten?**
A: For kittens 8-12 weeks, start with about 1/3 cup per day split into 3-4 meals. At 4-6 months, move to 1/2 cup daily in 2-3 meals. Adjust based on body condition — you should be able to feel ribs without seeing them.

**Q: Is this good for adult cats too?**
A: It's formulated for growth, so the calcium and phosphorus levels are higher than adult maintenance. Adult cats can eat it temporarily, but long-term, switch to the adult formula.

**Q: My kitten has diarrhea on other foods. Will this help?**
A: The pumpkin and probiotic specifically target digestive health, and many reviewers report improvement. But if diarrhea persists beyond a week, see your vet — it could signal parasites or an underlying issue.
