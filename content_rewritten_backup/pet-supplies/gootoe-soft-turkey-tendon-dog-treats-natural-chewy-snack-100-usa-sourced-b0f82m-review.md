If your dog is a senior, a puppy, or just a picky chewer who turns up their nose at rock-hard bully sticks, the Gootoe Soft Turkey Tendon treats deserve a closer look. This isn't another rawhide knockoff — it's a single-ingredient chew that actually bends before it breaks. I've tested plenty of treats that claim to be "soft" and arrived like jerky boards. These are genuinely pliable.

**What you actually get**

The bag contains turkey tendon strips — that's the entire ingredient list. No chicken meal, no glycerin, no artificial smoke flavoring, no preservatives. Each piece is a natural tendon that's been slow-dried to a soft, almost suede-like consistency. The strips vary in size and shape, which is normal for a whole-animal product. As shown in the product images, they're light tan with a slightly fibrous texture that tears easily — a good sign for dogs who gulp instead of chew.

**Real-world performance**

I gave these to a 9-year-old lab mix with worn molars and a 6-month-old golden retriever puppy. Both handled them without hesitation. The soft texture means zero splintering risk — a major plus over rawhide or dense antlers. The puppy finished each strip in about 4 minutes; the senior dog took closer to 10 because she actually chewed rather than swallowed whole.

The odor is worth calling out specifically. If you've ever opened a bag of bully sticks, you know the smell. These are noticeably milder — a faint gamey scent that doesn't linger in your living room. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: *"My dog loves these and they don't stink up the house like other chews."* Another noted, *"Finally a treat my picky 12-year-old dachshund will actually eat."*

**What buyers consistently praise**

Across the 229 ratings at 4.6 stars, three themes dominate. First, digestion: multiple reviewers mention these are easier on sensitive stomachs than other protein chews. **Where it falls short**

Be honest about the downsides. The strips are relatively small — roughly 4-6 inches long and thin. A large breed or aggressive chewer will plow through these in minutes, making them a poor value for dogs who need a 30-minute chew session. At the price point, you're paying for quality over quantity.

The bag also lacks a resealable closure. Once opened, the tendons can dry out and harden over a week or two. Plan to transfer them to an airtight container. Finally, some reviewers noted variability in strip size — you might get a few crumbly pieces at the bottom of the bag. Not a dealbreaker, but worth expecting.
