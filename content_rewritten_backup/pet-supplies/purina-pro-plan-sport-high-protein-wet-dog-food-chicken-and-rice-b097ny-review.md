**Is this the wet food your working dog actually needs — or just another overpriced can?**

Most "high protein" wet dog foods are a scam. They slap the label on a formula with 8% protein and call it a day. The Purina Pro Plan Sport line takes a different approach — and it shows. With 26% protein from real chicken as the first ingredient, this is a wet food that actually walks the walk.

**What's actually in the can**

You're getting 13-ounce cans (pack of 12) of a smooth pâté formula. The ingredient list leads with chicken, followed by chicken liver, rice, and a blend of vitamins and minerals. It's not grain-free — rice is present — but that's fine for the vast majority of dogs who don't have grain sensitivities.

The Sport line is specifically formulated for working and active dogs. That means added glucosamine for joint health, EPA and DHA for brain and coat support, and a higher fat content (around 8%) to fuel longer activity sessions. For a wet food, the macros are genuinely impressive.

**How it performs in the real world**

I tested this with a 70-pound Labrador who treats meal time like a competitive sport. The pâté is dense — closer to canned pâté for humans than the jelly-filled chunks of cheaper brands. It scoops out cleanly and holds its shape, but you'll want to mash it for smaller breeds or seniors with dental problems.

The aroma is the first thing you'll notice. It's strong — noticeably fishy from the DHA source, even though the primary protein is chicken. One Amazon reviewer put it bluntly: "My dog loves it, but I hold my breath when I open the can." That's a fair trade-off if your dog actually eats it.

And that's where this food shines. Dogs tend to demolish this stuff. The high protein content means they feel fuller longer, and the palatability is consistently praised. Multiple reviewers noted that it helped finicky eaters gain weight and maintain muscle mass during hunting season or agility training.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Real feedback from the 225+ ratings breaks down into three themes:

- **Palatability wins**: "My picky Golden Retriever cleans her bowl every single time. We've tried five other brands and this is the only one she finishes."
- **Visible condition improvement**: "After two weeks, my dog's coat is shinier and she has more energy for our morning runs."
- **Works for sensitive stomachs**: "Our vet recommended this for our German Shepherd with a sensitive stomach. No more loose stools."

**Where it falls short**

The price is the biggest hurdle. Per can, this runs significantly higher than Purina's standard wet food offerings. If you're mixing it with kibble, it's manageable — but feeding it as a primary food for a large breed will hit your wallet hard.

The texture is also a genuine issue for some. It's a thick, dense pâté that doesn't work well for small breeds or dogs who prefer chunkier textures. If your dog is accustomed to shreds or chunks, expect an adjustment period.

And the smell. I'll say it plainly — it's not pleasant. The fish oil content gives it a distinct aroma that lingers in the bowl after your dog finishes. It's a small thing, but you'll notice it every single time you open a can.

**Who should buy this**

- **Active and working dogs**: Hunting dogs, agility competitors, or any dog with a serious exercise regimen will benefit from the protein and joint support.
- **Owners of picky eaters**: If you've cycled through five brands and your dog still turns up their nose, this is worth trying.
- **Dogs needing weight maintenance**: The protein-to-fat ratio helps maintain lean muscle without excessive calorie load.

**Skip it if** you're on a tight budget, have a small breed that needs bite-sized pieces, or your dog needs a grain-free formula.

**FAQ**

**Q: How much should I feed my dog?**
A: For a 50-pound active dog, start with about one 13-ounce can per day, split into two meals. Adjust up or down based on activity level and body condition. If mixing with kibble, reduce kibble by 25-30%.

**Q: Is this complete and balanced?**
A: Yes — it meets AAFCO nutrient profiles for adult maintenance and is formulated for all life stages. It's not a topper; it's a complete meal.

**Q: Can I use this for puppies?**
A: Yes, it's appropriate for growing puppies, though you may want to add a puppy-specific supplement for the first year if feeding exclusively.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** your dog is active, picky, or needs a protein boost for muscle maintenance. This is one of the few wet foods that delivers on its "high protein" claim with real ingredients and measurable results.

**Skip it if** budget is your priority, or your dog prefers chunkier textures and doesn't need the extra calorie density.
