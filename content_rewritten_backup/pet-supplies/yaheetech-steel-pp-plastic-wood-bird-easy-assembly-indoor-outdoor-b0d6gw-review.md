Let's be honest: most bird cages under $100 are flimsy wire boxes that wobble, rust, and make you question why you own a pet at all. The Yaheetech Steel PP Plastic Wood Bird Cage isn't that. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, it's quietly become one of the more reliable budget options for small-bird owners. But "reliable for the price" and "actually good" are different things — here's the real breakdown.

**What you actually get**

The box contains the cage body (steel wire with a powder-coated finish), a PP plastic base tray, two wooden dowel perches, two plastic food/water cups, and the hardware. Assembly is genuinely tool-free — the panels snap together with a locking mechanism that doesn't require a screwdriver. Most buyers report 10–15 minutes from unboxing to bird-in-cage. As one reviewer put it, "I'm not handy at all and had this together while my coffee was still hot."

**Build quality and real-world use**

The steel frame is the standout feature here. It's noticeably sturdier than the thin aluminum you find on comparably priced cages — no wobble when your bird hops from perch to perch. The PP plastic base pulls out as a full tray, which makes daily cleaning dramatically easier than cages with fixed bottoms. You can rinse it in the sink or wipe it down in under a minute.

The wire spacing sits around 0.5 inches, which is ideal for budgies, finches, canaries, and cockatiels. It keeps curious heads from getting stuck while still giving good visibility. The cage works fine indoors on its stand, and the design holds up in a sheltered outdoor aviary — though I'd caution against full sun exposure for hours at a time.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The ease of cleaning comes up constantly. "The removable tray is a lifesaver — I can clean this in two minutes versus twenty with my old cage." Multiple reviewers also note the value proposition: you're getting a cage that feels like it should cost 30-40% more. The assembly simplicity gets mentioned repeatedly, especially from first-time bird owners who expected a frustrating afternoon.

**Where it falls short**

The included perches are smooth, uniform dowels. Birds need varied perch diameters to exercise their feet and prevent bumblefoot — plan to swap in natural branch perches immediately. The PP plastic base, while easy to clean, can warp if the cage sits in direct sunlight for extended periods. And the bar spacing means this is strictly for small birds; if you have a conure or larger parrot, this cage is a no-go.

Some buyers noted the food cups sit a bit low, which means birds can scatter seed onto the tray more easily. Not a dealbreaker, but you'll be sweeping the floor around the cage a little more often.

**Who should buy this**

- **First-time budgie or finch owners** who want a cage that assembles without frustration
- **Cockatiel owners** upgrading from a starter cage who want easy daily cleaning
- **Multi-bird households** needing a secondary cage for quarantining or travel
- **Outdoor aviary enthusiasts** with a sheltered setup who want a durable, affordable option

**Skip it if** you own medium-to-large parrots, want a cage with a playtop or seed catcher, or need something that can handle full, direct outdoor sun year-round.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can this cage hold a cockatiel comfortably?**
Yes, for a single cockatiel. It's on the cozy side, so your bird will need daily out-of-cage time. Bar spacing prevents escape but doesn't feel cramped.

**Q: How do I clean the tray without removing the bird?**
You don't — the tray slides out the bottom while the bird stays in the cage. Slide it out, rinse it, dry it, slide it back. Takes under two minutes.

**Q: Does the powder-coated finish chip easily?**
Only if you're scraping it with metal tools. Normal use and regular cleaning won't damage it. Just avoid abrasive scrubbers.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a sturdy, easy-to-clean small bird cage at a fair price without sacrificing build quality.

**Skip it if** you own larger parrots or need a cage that survives full outdoor exposure.

**4.6/5** — This is the cage I'd recommend to anyone with a budgie, finch, or cockatiel who's tired of flimsy starter cages. It's not perfect, but at this price point, it's the right choice for most small-bird households.
