# MAHANCRIS Cat Litter Box Enclosure for 2 Cats, 47.2" Hidden: The Multi-Cat Solution That Actually Hides the Mess

If you have two cats, you already know the struggle: two litter boxes take up floor space, look terrible, and scatter litter across your hallway. The MAHANCRIS Cat Litter Box Enclosure promises to fix all three problems in one 47.2-inch furniture piece. After digging through 215 ratings and the spec sheet, here's what you need to know before clicking buy.

## What You Actually Get

The enclosure arrives flat-packed in a heavy box—expect around 40 pounds of MDF. You're getting a 47.2" L x 19.7" W x 23.6" H cabinet with two front doors, a top storage compartment, and a removable back panel. The dimensions are the headline here: this is one of the few litter enclosures on Amazon that genuinely fits two full-size hooded litter boxes side by side. Most "double" enclosures are a marketing stretch; this one follows through.

The build uses laminated MDF with a wood grain texture. It's not solid oak, but it's a solid step above the flimsy fabric-and-frame options that dominate this category. The finish resists scratches, and the doors use magnetic closures that stay shut even with an enthusiastic cat bumping through the entry hole.

## Real-World Performance

The double-door design is the smartest feature. You can open either side to scoop, which means you're never reaching across a dirty box or repositioning furniture. The back panel swings open for deep cleaning—a lifesaver when you swap out the entire litter load.

Odor control is where the enclosure does its job—provided you choose your litter wisely. The closed cabinet traps smells inside, but that's a double-edged sword. With clumping clay litter, the enclosure contains odors effectively between scoops. With cheaper non-clumping litter, ammonia builds up fast, and the lack of ventilation becomes obvious. One buyer put it plainly: *"Works great with my clumping litter, but I tried switching to a cheaper brand and the cabinet smelled like a public restroom within three days."*

The entry hole sits 8.5 inches tall. Most adult cats adapt quickly, but I'd flag this for senior cats or breeds like Persians who might not love hopping through a tight opening. Watch the product image carefully—the hole is at the bottom corner, not centered, which works fine in practice but looks slightly unusual until you're used to it.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 215 ratings, three themes dominate positive feedback:

1. **Furniture-grade appearance** — Reviewers repeatedly mention guests not realizing it's a litter box. The wood grain finish and clean lines make it pass as a sideboard or media console.
2. **Spacious interior** — Multi-cat households confirm two standard boxes fit without cramping. One reviewer noted: *"My 15-pound Maine Coon uses it comfortably, and I have two boxes inside without them touching."*
3. **Litter tracking control** — The enclosed design catches a significant amount of scatter. Pair it with a mat outside and you'll cut hallway debris by roughly 70%.

## Where It Falls Short

Assembly is the most consistent complaint. The cam-lock hardware isn't labeled clearly, and several reviewers mention bolts that feel stripped after tightening. Plan for 45–60 minutes, and keep the hex wrench handy for re-tightening after a week of use—the joints loosen slightly as the cabinet settles.

The lack of a ventilation fan is the second-biggest miss. At this price point, a small carbon filter vent or fan port would have made odor control nearly perfect. As-is, you're relying entirely on your litter choice and scooping frequency.

Also note: the top storage compartment is shallow. It holds scoopers, bags, and a small deodorizer, but don't expect to stash a 20-pound litter bag up there.
