**Is this the indoor potty solution that finally works — or just another plastic tray that'll end up in your garage?**

Let me cut through the noise. The Reusab Dog Grass Pad with Tray is a solid 4.2-star product for a reason: it solves the #1 problem with indoor potty training — dogs hate peeing on flat, squishy pads. Real grass is what they want. This gives them the texture without the mud.

**What's actually in the box**

You get three pieces: the artificial turf mat, a perforated drainage tray, and a solid base tray that catches the liquid. The turf is roughly 24 x 16 inches — check that dimension before you buy. The tray snaps together cleanly and the turf sits flush on top. No assembly tools needed. The whole unit weighs under 3 pounds, so you can move it from room to room or onto a balcony without breaking your back.

**How it performs in real life**

The drainage system is the real MVP here. When your dog pees, liquid passes through the turf's drainage holes into the bottom tray. You dump it, rinse it, and you're done. This is a massive upgrade over pee pads where urine pools on top and gets tracked across your floors.

The turf itself has a surprisingly convincing grass feel — not that scratchy fake-rug texture you find in cheaper pads. Dogs seem to genuinely not know the difference. One buyer wrote, *"My 9-month-old Lab mix took to this on day two. She'd been refusing pee pads for weeks, and this was the first thing she used without hesitation."* That matches what I've seen in training circles: surface texture is everything for stubborn pups.

Washing holds up too. Multiple 4-star reviews mention scrubbing the turf with soap and water weekly for months without the fibers matting down or the backing degrading. The drainage holes don't clog easily, even with thicker urine.

**Where it falls short**

Honesty time. This pad is **small**. At 24 x 16 inches, it's really designed for toy breeds, small dogs under 25 pounds, or puppies. A medium-sized dog will circle, turn, and end up peeing over the edge — which defeats the entire purpose. If you have a retriever, shepherd, or anything above 30 pounds, look for the larger version or a different product entirely.

The plastic tray corners are also fragile. Several reviewers noted cracks after accidentally dropping the unit during cleaning. It's not a rugged product — treat it gently.

And there's no odor control built in. Without the drainage tray working properly (or if you let urine sit too long), the turf will develop a smell. You'll need an enzyme-based cleaner, not just soap.

**Who should buy this**

This is for you if:
- You have a small dog, puppy, or senior dog that can't hold it overnight
- You live in an apartment without easy outdoor access
- You're tired of throwing away disposable pads every other day
- You want a balcony potty option that doesn't look like a trash bag

Skip it if you have a large breed, expect zero maintenance, or want something you can leave uncleaned for a week.

**FAQ**

**Q: How often do I need to clean it?**
A: Dump the liquid tray daily. Deep-clean the turf with enzyme cleaner every 3-5 days, depending on use. More often if you have multiple dogs.

**Q: Will my adult dog accept this if they're used to pee pads?**
A: Usually yes, within a week. Some dogs transition instantly because the grass texture is more natural. A few stubborn ones need you to place a used pee pad underneath on the turf for the first few days.

**Q: Does it work outdoors on a balcony?**
A: Yes, and it's actually better outdoors because rain rinses the tray. Just make sure it's on a flat surface and not in direct sunlight all day, or the plastic can warp over time.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you have a small dog or puppy and you're tired of disposable pads. The drainage system, realistic turf, and durability for the price make it a legitimate upgrade over the pee-pad life. It's not perfect — the size limits who it works for, and the corners need care — but for the right household, this is a genuinely useful tool.

**Skip it if** you have a medium or large breed, or you want something you can neglect for a week. This product demands a basic cleaning routine.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Good value for small-dog households. Serious buyers should measure their dog first and be honest about the size limitation.
