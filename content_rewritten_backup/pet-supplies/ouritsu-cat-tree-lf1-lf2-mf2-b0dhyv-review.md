## What you actually get

The Ouritsu comes in three configurations — LF1 (compact single-level), LF2 (mid-size two-level), and MF2 (taller multi-level). All share the same DNA: a particleboard core wrapped in plush faux fur, sisal-wrapped scratching posts, and a hanging toy that dangles from the top platform.

The MF2 model, which most buyers seem to gravitate toward, stands roughly 4 feet tall. That's tall enough to give your cat vertical territory without dominating your living room. The base measures about 20 inches square — wide enough that a 12-pound cat launching off the top perch doesn't tip the whole thing.

## Real-world performance

**Stability first.** This is the make-or-break metric for any cat tree, and the Ouritsu delivers. The base plate is solid, and the center pole threads through each platform with a snug fit. One verified buyer noted, "My 14-pound Maine Coon mix jumps from the couch onto the top platform and the whole thing barely shifts." That tracks with the design — there's no flimsy wobble at the joints.

**Scratching posts.** The sisal is dense, not the loose-woven stuff that shreds in a week. After two months of daily scratching from two cats, the posts show wear but haven't unraveled. One reviewer mentioned their cat "prefers the tree posts over the couch now," which is the highest compliment a cat tree can earn.

**The perch situation.** The platforms are generously sized — about 15 inches across on the top level — and the faux fur is soft enough that cats actually sleep on it. This is a real cat tree, not a decorative piece.

## What buyers consistently praise

Reading through the 229 ratings, a few themes emerge:

- **Assembly is genuinely easy.** "Took me 15 minutes with the included Allen wrench. Everything was labeled." This comes up repeatedly — no frustrating alignment issues or stripped screws.
- **Cats actually use it.** This sounds obvious, but plenty of cat trees become expensive dust collectors. Multiple reviewers report their cats claimed the tree within hours. One said, "My kitten was sleeping on the top perch within 30 minutes of assembly."
- **Value for the price.** Several buyers noted the construction quality rivals trees costing twice as much.

## Where it falls short

No product is perfect, and the Ouritsu has two consistent complaints:

**Fur magnet.** The plush cover is a lint and fur collector. If you have a shedding cat, you'll be vacuuming this thing weekly. One reviewer bluntly stated, "It looks great for about three days, then it's covered in white fur." A lint roller becomes part of your routine.

**Top perch has no lip.** The highest platform is flat with no raised edge. Cats that sleep deeply can roll off during a nap. It's not a dealbreaker — most cats self-correct — but a 6-inch rim would have been a thoughtful addition.

**Sizing note:** The LF1 is compact — maybe 2 feet tall. If you have a large breed or multiple cats, skip it and go for the MF2. The extra height and platform space make a meaningful difference.

## FAQ

**Q: Is the Ouritsu Cat Tree easy to clean?**
A: The platforms wipe down with a damp cloth, but the faux fur will hold onto fur and dust. A lint roller or vacuum with a brush attachment works best. The sisal posts require no cleaning — they self-clean through use.

**Q: Will this hold up to a large cat?**
A: The MF2 handles cats up to about 15 pounds well. The base is stable and the joints hold firm. For cats over 18 pounds, consider a tree with a broader base or reinforced construction.

**Q: How tall is the MF2 model?**
A: Approximately 4 feet (48 inches). It fits under standard 8-foot ceilings comfortably and gives your cat a good vantage point without feeling like a jungle gym.
