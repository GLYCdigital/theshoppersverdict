## What You’re Actually Getting

The 1-gallon jug is straightforward. You get a concentrated liquid clarifier that uses a polymer-based flocculant. When added to pond water, it binds suspended particles — the microscopic algae and organic debris that cause that murky green or brown haze — into clumps heavy enough to sink to the bottom or get caught in your filter. The gallon size is the smart buy here: it treats up to 11,000 gallons of pond water, which is roughly four to five times the pond volume most backyard hobbyists maintain.

The initial dose is 1 cup (8 oz) per 500 gallons, and maintenance is 1 cup per 1,000 gallons weekly. That means a single gallon can last an entire season for a moderate pond.

## How It Performs in Real-World Use

Buyer feedback consistently highlights speed. Multiple reviewers note visible improvement within 24 hours, with most achieving clear water by the second day. One long-time pond keeper wrote, *“I’ve used this for three seasons. It clears up my koi pond in about a day and a half. Just make sure you have your filter running.”* That last part matters — the clumped particles need somewhere to go. If you don’t run your pump and filter continuously, the sediment just settles on the bottom and decomposes, creating a new problem.

The product is safe for fish and plants at labeled doses, and it doesn’t harm your biological filter, which is a common fear. That’s a real advantage over algaecides that can crash your nitrogen cycle.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest complaint isn’t about the product failing — it’s about unrealistic expectations. ACCU-CLEAR treats the symptom, not the cause. If your pond has excessive nutrients from fish waste, decaying leaves, or runoff, the algae will return within a week or two without maintenance dosing. Several reviewers mention this frustration directly.

Also, dosing mistakes are punishing. Overdosing can cause a temporary milky-white cloud and, in rare cases, stress sensitive fish. If your pond is heavily stocked with koi, start with a half-dose and work up. And don’t use it with a UV clarifier simultaneously — the UV will break down the polymer before it can work.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Busy pond owners** who want quick results for a backyard gathering or just don’t want to wait weeks for a biological fix.
- **Koi and goldfish keepers** who need a fish-safe option that won’t disrupt their biofilter.
- **New pond owners** dealing with the classic “first-year green water” phase who need a temporary bridge while plants and bacteria establish.

Skip it if you’re looking for a permanent solution, or if you have a severe algae bloom caused by heavy nutrient loading — that requires a UV clarifier, water changes, and a filter upgrade.

## FAQ

**How long does it take to work?**
Most users see noticeable clearing in 24–48 hours. Full clarity can take up to 72 hours depending on how dirty the water is and your filter flow rate.

**Will it hurt my fish?**
At the labeled dose, no. It’s designed to be fish-safe. The risk comes only with overdosing or using it in a pond with very low oxygen levels — run aeration during treatment.

**How often do I need to add it?**
For maintenance, once a week. Many users find they can stretch it to every two weeks once the pond stabilizes, but that depends on your bio-load.
