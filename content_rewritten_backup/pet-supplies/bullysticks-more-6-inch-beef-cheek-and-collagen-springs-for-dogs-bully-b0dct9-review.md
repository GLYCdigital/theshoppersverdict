If you've ever peeled a slimy, chemical-smelling rawhide chew out of your dog's mouth and wondered what you're actually feeding them, this product is worth a look. BULLYSTICKS & MORE's 6-inch Beef Cheek and Collagen Springs combo delivers what the category promises: long-lasting, digestible chewing satisfaction without the rawhide baggage. At 4.3 stars across 215 ratings, it's clearly doing something right—but let's dig into whether it's right for *your* dog.

**What's in the bag**

You get a mixed pack of two chew types. The beef cheek strips are flat, dense pieces of slow-roasted beef cheek muscle—think jerky but tougher. The collagen springs are rolled, spiral-shaped chews made from beef collagen (the same protein found in tendons and ligaments). Both are single-ingredient, which means no rawhide, no glue, no artificial flavoring. As shown in the product image, they're substantial-looking 6-inch pieces—big enough for a Lab or Shepherd to get a good grip, but not so long that a small-to-medium dog feels overwhelmed.

**Real-world performance**

Here's where this product earns its keep. The beef cheek strips are genuinely tough. In my experience and in customer reports, these hold up for multiple sessions with aggressive chewers. One reviewer noted, "My GSD goes through bully sticks in 20 minutes but these beef cheeks lasted her two full days." That's the kind of longevity that justifies the price per chew.

The collagen springs are a different story. They're softer, more pliable, and shred progressively rather than snapping off in dangerous chunks. That's actually a safety plus—collagen breaks down in the stomach, unlike rawhide. But that softness means they won't last as long. Most buyers report 30-60 minutes of active chewing per spring, which is fine for a daily treat but not a marathon chew.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The most common thread in positive reviews is the digestibility. Dogs with sensitive stomachs that react to rawhide or commercial bully sticks (which often carry a strong odor) seem to tolerate these well. One verified purchase put it simply: "Finally a chew that doesn't give my dog the runs. The beef cheek is her new favorite." Another buyer highlighted the value: "Cheaper than buying bully sticks separately and my dog likes these more."

Another consistent positive is the lack of that overpowering bully-stick smell. While not odorless, the beef cheek strips have a milder, more natural aroma—a big deal if you've ever had to ban bully sticks from the living room.

**Where it falls short**

Be honest: the collagen springs aren't as durable as the marketing suggests. Multiple reviewers with power chewers reported the springs being destroyed in under an hour. If your dog is a "destroy, not chew" type, you'll get more value from the beef cheek strips alone.

There's also a texture consistency issue across batches. A few buyers noted that some beef cheek pieces were dry and brittle, breaking into shards rather than softening into chewable strips. That's a quality-control frustration, not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing.

**Who should buy this**

- **The owner of a medium-to-large dog** (25+ pounds) who needs a chew that lasts longer than a rawhide knot but is safer than a bone.
- **The owner of a sensitive-stomach dog** who can't handle rawhide or high-odor bully sticks.
- **The bargain shopper** who wants variety—two textures in one bag lets you figure out which one your dog prefers before committing to a bulk purchase.

**Skip it if** your dog is a light chewer (these may be too tough), or if you have a small breed under 15 pounds—the 6-inch size might be unwieldy for tiny mouths.

**FAQ**

**Q: Are these safe for puppies?**
A: For puppies over 6 months with adult teeth coming in, yes. Younger pups with baby teeth may struggle with the density. Always supervise first-time chewers.

**Q: How many chews come in a bag?**
A: Pack counts vary by listing—check the current product page for the specific quantity. Most buyers report receiving a mix of roughly half beef cheek strips and half collagen springs.

**Q: Will these stain my carpet?**
A: The beef cheek strips can leave a greasy residue as they soften, especially on light-colored fabrics. Chew on a towel or mat if that's a concern.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a digestible, long-lasting chew alternative to rawhide for a medium or large dog, and you appreciate having two textures to rotate.

**Skip it if** you need maximum durability above all else—the collagen springs won't survive a determined power chewer, and you might be better off with a 100% beef cheek pack.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid value, honest ingredients, and two useful textures. Just manage your expectations on the springs' longevity.
