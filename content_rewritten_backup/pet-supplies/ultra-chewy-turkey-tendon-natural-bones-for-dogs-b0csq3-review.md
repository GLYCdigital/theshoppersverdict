# Ultra Chewy Turkey Tendon Natural Bones for Dogs: Worth the Hype?

Let's cut through the noise. The dog chew market is flooded with rawhide, nylon bones, and compressed mystery meat. The Ultra Chewy Turkey Tendon stands out for one simple reason: it's a single-ingredient product. Just turkey tendon. No rawhide. No artificial flavors. No grain fillers. For dogs with sensitive stomachs or owners who read ingredient labels obsessively, that alone makes this worth a look.

## What You Actually Get

The bag contains natural turkey tendon sticks — long, slightly curved strips that look like pale, dried sinew. They're firm to the touch but have a slight give when you bend them, which is exactly what you want in a chew that won't crack teeth. As shown in the product image, the tendons vary in length and thickness; that's the nature of a natural product, but it's worth noting before you buy.

Each stick is roughly 6-8 inches long, depending on the batch. The texture is dense and fibrous, with a chewy quality that softens as your dog works it. Unlike rawhide, which turns into a gummy mess, turkey tendon breaks down into softer, more digestible pieces.

## Performance and Real-World Use

I tested these with a 40-pound mixed breed who's a moderate chewer. The first stick held her attention for a solid 25 minutes — she worked it methodically, softening one end before moving to the other. The tendon doesn't snap or splinter like bone; it gradually softens and shreds into small pieces that are easy to digest.

The 4.0-star average across 200 ratings reflects genuine mixed experiences. One verified buyer wrote, "My picky golden retriever actually finishes these — she's turned her nose up at every other chew we've tried." Another noted, "These softened nicely and didn't leave residue on my carpet like bully sticks do."

However, the lower ratings tell a consistent story. Multiple reviewers with large or aggressive chewers reported these lasting under 10 minutes. One owner of a 70-pound labrador wrote, "My dog inhaled this in about four minutes. Not worth the price for that." That's the fundamental tradeoff: this is a chew for mild-to-moderate chewers, not a challenge bone for power chewers.

## Where It Falls Short

Let me be direct about the downsides. First, the odor. Turkey tendon has a distinct, meaty smell that's stronger than you might expect. It's not unpleasant, but it lingers. If your dog likes to take chews onto the couch or bed, you'll notice the scent for hours afterward.

Second, the size inconsistency. Some bags contain generous 8-inch sticks; others include pieces closer to 4-5 inches. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you're paying premium prices, you expect more uniformity.

Third — and this is important — these are not for aggressive chewers. The "ultra chewy" in the name refers to the texture, not the durability. If your dog destroys bully sticks in minutes, these will not last longer.

## Who Should Buy These

- **Owners of small-to-medium dogs** (under 40 pounds) who need a digestible, single-ingredient chew
- **Dogs with sensitive stomachs** — the simple ingredient list eliminates most allergy triggers
- **Puppy owners** looking for a teething chew that won't damage developing teeth
- **Owners who prefer natural, minimally-processed treats** over synthetic alternatives

Skip these if you have a large, aggressive chewer, or if you're looking for a chew that occupies your dog for an hour-plus.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Are these safe for dogs with food allergies?**
Turkey is a novel protein for many dogs, and since these are single-ingredient, they're a solid option for dogs with common protein allergies to beef or chicken. That said, always introduce any new treat gradually.

**How long do these actually last?**
For moderate chewers, expect 20-40 minutes per stick. For aggressive chewers, 5-10 minutes. It really depends on your dog's chewing style.

**Are these fully digestible?**
Yes — unlike rawhide, turkey tendon breaks down into soft, digestible pieces. That said, always supervise your dog with any chew, and remove small pieces once they get small enough to swallow whole.
