If your couch is currently a fur-covered battleground and your dog treats every blanket like a personal burrow, the SORKUT Dog Blanket is worth a serious look. This isn't another flimsy throw that slides off the sofa the moment your pup stands up. With 225 ratings averaging 4.5 stars, it's quietly become a favorite among owners who need something that survives both muddy paws and machine washing.

**What you actually get**  
The blanket is a two-layer construction: a soft flannel top (both sides are the same plush fabric — no "wrong side" to argue with) bonded to a waterproof backing. It arrives folded, not vacuum-sealed, so there's no weird crease line that takes a week to flatten. Sizes range from small (roughly 30×40 inches) up to a generous 50×60 for large breeds. The backing feels like a thin, flexible tarp — not crinkly plastic, which is a relief if your dog is noise-sensitive.

**Real-world performance**  
I've tested this with a 70-pound Lab who treats every blanket as a nest-building project. The flannel holds up to serious digging and kneading without pilling after three weeks of daily use. The waterproof layer does its job — a spilled water bowl and one "accident" from a nervous rescue pup didn't soak through to the couch cushion underneath. After a hot-water wash and low-heat dry, the blanket came out intact with zero shrinkage. That's more than I can say for most pet blankets I've reviewed in this price range.

**What buyers consistently praise**  
Scrolling through the reviews, three themes keep coming up. First, the softness: "My golden retriever refuses to sleep anywhere else now — she drags this blanket from room to room." Second, the waterproof backing: "Finally, a blanket that protects my furniture from my incontinent senior dog without feeling like a shower curtain." Third, durability: "I've washed this at least 15 times and it looks brand new." Multiple reviewers mention buying a second one because the first became the dog's "security blanket."

**Where it falls short**  
Honest downsides: This is a *blanket*, not a pad. It's thin — roughly the weight of a standard fleece throw. If you're looking for joint support for an arthritic dog, this won't cut it. The flannel is a fur magnet; dark-colored blankets show every white hair, and you'll be lint-rolling constantly if your dog sheds heavily. Sizing also runs small. A 50×60 fits a large dog, but if your pup likes to stretch out, size up — one reviewer noted their 40-pound border collie needed the large size to fully sprawl.

**Who should buy this**  
- **Couch protectors**: If your dog owns the sofa and you're tired of washing throw pillows, this is your solution.  
- **Car travelers**: The waterproof backing makes it great for backseat protection on muddy hikes or rainy vet trips.  
- **Crate liners**: It folds thin enough to fit under a crate pad without bunching.  
- **Skip it if**: You need orthopedic cushioning, or your dog is a heavy chewer (the flannel won't survive determined teeth).

**FAQ**  
**Q: Is the waterproof backing noisy?**  
A: No — it's a soft, matte finish that doesn't crinkle like a raincoat. Your dog won't hear it moving.

**Q: Can I use it in a washing machine?**  
A: Yes, cold or warm water on a gentle cycle. Tumble dry low. Avoid fabric softener — it can break down the waterproof layer over time.

**Q: Does it slide off furniture?**  
A: The backing has a slight grip, but it's not non-slip. On leather or slick surfaces, you may need to tuck the edges under cushions.

**Skip it if** you need plush cushioning or your dog chews fabrics.
