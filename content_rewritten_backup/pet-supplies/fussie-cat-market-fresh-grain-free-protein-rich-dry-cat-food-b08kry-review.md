**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be direct about the downsides:

- **The price.** This is not a budget food. A 5 lb bag runs significantly more per ounce than mainstream brands. If you have a multi-cat household, you'll feel it monthly.
- **No weight management formula.** If your cat is prone to weight gain, the high protein and fat content (around 18-20% fat) might be too calorie-dense. You'll need to measure portions carefully.
- **Some cats reject the texture.** A few reviewers noted their cats wouldn't touch the kibble at all — it's not a universal winner. One buyer wrote: *"My cat ate it for a week then refused. Waste of money for us."*

**FAQ: Real Questions From Shoppers**

**Q: Is Fussie Cat Market Fresh suitable for kittens?**
A: It's formulated for all life stages, but kittens need higher calories and specific nutrients. Check with your vet — some prefer kitten-specific formulas for the first year.

**Q: Can I mix this with wet food?**
A: Absolutely. Many buyers mix a small amount of kibble with Fussie Cat's wet food line for added texture and hydration. Just adjust portions to prevent overfeeding.

**Q: How fast should I transition my cat?**
A: Over 7-10 days. Start with 25% new food, gradually increasing. Cats with sensitive stomachs may need a slower transition to avoid digestive upset.
