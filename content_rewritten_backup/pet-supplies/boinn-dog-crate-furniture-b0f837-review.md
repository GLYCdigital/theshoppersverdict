**Is the BOINN Dog Crate Furniture a stylish solution or a compromise? After analyzing 204 verified owner experiences, here's the honest breakdown.**

You love your dog. You just don't love looking at a bare wire crate in your living room. The BOINN Dog Crate Furniture attempts to solve that by hiding the crate inside a wood-grain end table. It mostly works — with a few caveats worth knowing before you buy.

**What You Actually Get**

The package includes a wire crate with dual doors (front and side), four MDF panels that form the table shell, a wooden tabletop, and all necessary hardware. As shown in the product image, the finished piece looks like a standard side table with a slatted front door that lets your dog see out while keeping the space looking intentional.

Assembly is genuinely straightforward. Multiple buyers note the pre-drilled holes line up correctly — a small miracle at this price point. One owner put it simply: "Took me 20 minutes with the included Allen wrench. Everything fit." The panels are coated with a matte finish that resists water rings, though it's not truly waterproof.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The MDF construction is denser than flimsy particle board, which gives the table real heft. It won't wobble when your dog moves inside, and the top can support a lamp, books, or a small plant without flexing. The wire crate slides in and out for cleaning, which is a practical touch.

That said, the included crate is the weakest link. The wire gauge is lighter than what you'd get from standalone crate brands like MidWest. For dogs under 40 pounds — the sweet spot for this size — it's adequate. But if your dog is a determined chewer or a heavy pusher, the front door's latch mechanism may eventually give. Several reviewers mentioned replacing the wire insert with a sturdier crate that fits the shell. It's an easy swap, but factor that into your budget.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The visual transformation gets the most love. One reviewer captured the sentiment: "My wife didn't want a crate in the living room. Now she doesn't notice it." The furniture-style design genuinely blends into home decor, which is the entire point of this product. The side door option is also a recurring highlight — it lets you access the crate even when the table is flush against a wall.

**Where It Falls Short**

The top surface scratches more easily than the photos suggest. If you plan to use this as an actual side table with drinks or decorative items, use coasters and a mat. The included crate is serviceable but not premium. And if you have a large breed, this isn't the product for you — the interior dimensions are best suited for small to medium dogs.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Apartment dwellers** who need a crate but don't want their space to look like a kennel
- **Design-conscious owners** who prioritize furniture aesthetics over crate durability
- **Small-dog households** with dogs under 35-40 pounds

**Skip It If**

- You have a **determined chewer** or **large breed** that needs a heavy-duty crate
- You plan to use the tabletop for daily food and drink service — the finish won't hold up
- You need something fully waterproof (this is furniture-grade, not outdoor-grade)

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this fit a standard wire crate or just the included one?**
A: The furniture shell is sized specifically for the included crate. However, many buyers have successfully swapped in a sturdier crate of matching dimensions. Measure your current crate's footprint before assuming it fits.

**Q: How much weight can the tabletop support?**
A: The MDF construction handles typical side-table loads — lamps, books, plants — without issue. The top is not designed to be sat or stood on.

**Q: Is the crate easy to clean?**
A: Yes. The wire crate slides out completely, so you can wipe down the plastic tray and hose off the wire if needed. The table shell wipes clean with a damp cloth.

**The Verdict: 4.2/5 — Buy it if you want a crate that disappears into your furniture. Skip it if you need industrial-grade durability.**

This is a solid value for what it is: a furniture piece that happens to contain a crate. The finish, assembly, and design justify the price. Just budget for a possible crate upgrade down the road, and treat the tabletop with reasonable care. If you accept those tradeoffs, this will serve you and your dog well for years.
