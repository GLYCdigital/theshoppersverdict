## Performance and Real-World Use

Here's where the MOFBEAWK separates itself from the $12 generic pans. The material has some flex to it, which is exactly why it survives chewers. ## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides. First, sizing. The dimensions don't match every crate manufacturer's specs exactly. Several reviewers report the pan being slightly smaller than their crate floor, leaving a gap that can trap debris. **Measure your crate floor before ordering**—don't assume the size name matches your crate brand.

Second, the surface isn't scratch-proof. Determined dogs who dig at the pan will leave claw marks over time. These scratches are cosmetic and don't affect structural integrity, but if you're obsessive about appearance, that's worth knowing.

Third, there's no non-slip backing. On smooth floors, the pan can slide around when your dog is actively moving inside the crate. A cheap rubber mat underneath solves this, but it's an extra step.
