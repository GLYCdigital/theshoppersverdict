If you've got a picky eater who turns their nose up at half the wet foods on the shelf, Cesar Simply Crafted deserves a closer look. This isn't the mushy, mystery-meat loaf your grandparents fed their beagle. The "Simply Crafted" line trades the usual gravy-heavy formulas for what looks and smells like actual food—shredded chicken and beef in a light broth, not a pâté brick.

**What's Actually in the Box**

You're getting 3.5-ounce single-serve trays, typically sold in multi-packs of 12. Each tray has an easy-peel foil lid—no can opener required, which matters more than you'd think when you're half-asleep at 6 AM. The ingredient list leads with real meat: chicken or beef as the first ingredient, followed by broth, then vegetables like carrots and peas. No artificial colors, no by-product meal, no corn or wheat fillers. For a mass-market brand, that's genuinely respectable.

**Real-World Performance**

The texture is where this product separates itself. It's shredded, not ground—think pulled chicken rather than pâté. That makes it easy to break apart with a fork, and it's particularly good for senior dogs with dental problems or small breeds that struggle with dense canned food. The broth-to-meat ratio is well-balanced: enough moisture to keep it appetizing without turning into soup.

One customer summed it up: "My 14-year-old shih tzu has refused every wet food I've tried for the last year. She cleaned this tray in under two minutes. I actually checked the packaging twice to make sure I hadn't accidentally bought something from a gourmet pet store."

Another reviewer noted the practical upside: "I keep two trays in my glovebox for emergencies. The peel-top lid means I can feed my dog without carrying a can opener, and the trays stack flat in my console."

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be direct about the tradeoffs. The portions are small—3.5 ounces per tray. A 25-pound dog needs two trays per meal, which makes the per-meal cost add up quickly. Compared to buying 13-ounce cans of comparable quality, you're paying a premium for the convenience of single servings.

Some dogs also aren't fans of the shreds. One reviewer reported: "My labrador is a grazer who prefers pâté-style food. She licked the broth off and left the meat chunks. If your dog is used to smooth textures, this might not convert them."

There's also the environmental angle: each tray is individually packaged in plastic. If you're eco-conscious, that's a meaningful drawback versus a single recyclable can.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Picky eaters and seniors**: The aroma and soft texture win over dogs who've gone on hunger strikes
- **Travelers**: Peel-top trays are infinitely easier than cans when you're on the road
- **Small-breed owners**: Portion control is built-in; no guessing how much to scoop
- **Not ideal for**: Large-breed households on a budget, or anyone trying to reduce plastic waste

**Frequently Asked Questions**

**Can this be used as a complete meal, or is it a topper?**
It's nutritionally complete and balanced for adult dogs, so yes, it works as a standalone meal. That said, many owners use it as a topper over kibble because the strong aroma encourages picky eaters to finish their dry food too.

**Is it suitable for puppies?**
Vets generally recommend puppy-specific formulas for dogs under 12 months. This is formulated for adult maintenance, so wait until your dog is fully grown.

**How long does an open tray stay fresh in the fridge?**
Covered and refrigerated, it's good for about 24 hours. Since the portions are single-serve, you'll rarely have leftovers—but if you do, don't push it past a day.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** your dog is a picky eater, a senior with dental challenges, or you want a high-quality wet food that's genuinely portable and convenient. The real-meat-first ingredient list and shredded texture justify the premium price for many owners.

**Skip it if** you have a large dog on a tight budget, or you're committed to minimizing plastic packaging. For those households, a bulk canned option makes more sense.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Cesar Simply Crafted delivers exactly what it promises: real ingredients, excellent palatability, and unmatched convenience. It's not the cheapest way to feed your dog, but for the dogs who need it most, it's worth every penny.
