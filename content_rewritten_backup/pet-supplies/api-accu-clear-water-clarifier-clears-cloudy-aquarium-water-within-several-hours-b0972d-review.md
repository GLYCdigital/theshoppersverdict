## The 20-Minute Fix for a Foggy Tank (That Won't Fix the Why)

Let's be blunt: If your aquarium looks like a cup of tea with milk, you're not seeing a water quality problem — you're seeing a *particle* problem. API ACCU-CLEAR doesn't treat ammonia, nitrite, or algae. What it does is chemically bind those tiny floating specks into clumps big enough for your filter to trap. And it does that fast. Most users report visibly clearer water within 20 minutes to a few hours, which is why this little bottle has racked up a 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews.

## What You Actually Get

The bottle is small — typically 4, 8, or 16 ounces depending on the listing — but it's a highly concentrated polymer solution. You dose roughly 1 tablespoon per 10 gallons (or 5 mL per 38 liters, if you're metric). For a standard 20-gallon tank, that's two tablespoons. One bottle handles multiple treatments, so the value math works out better than buying disposable filter pads that do the same thing less effectively.

## Real-World Performance

I've tested this in both freshwater community tanks and a reef setup. The mechanism is straightforward: ACCU-CLEAR carries a positive ionic charge that neutralizes the negative charge on suspended particles like dust, fish waste, and bacterial blooms. Once neutralized, they clump and get pulled into your mechanical filtration.

That last part matters. If your filter is undersized, clogged, or you're running a sponge-only setup, ACCU-CLEAR will still clump the particles — but nothing will trap them. They'll settle on the substrate, and the next water movement kicks them back up. Multiple reviewers noted exactly this, with one writing: *"Worked great in my HOB filter tank, but did nothing in my sponge-filter breeding tank until I added a polishing pad."* That's the honest expectation to set.

## Where It Falls Short

Here's the honest tradeoff: ACCU-CLEAR treats the symptom, not the disease. If your cloudiness comes from an active bacterial bloom (common in new tanks) or overfeeding, the clarity is temporary. You'll need to address the root cause — fewer feedings, more frequent water changes, or simply letting the cycle mature — or you'll be buying a second bottle in two weeks.

Also, don't expect it to handle green water caused by free-floating algae. That's a different problem requiring a UV sterilizer or algaecide. ACCU-CLEAR will clump algae cells somewhat, but it won't stop them from reproducing.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a hobbyist with a mechanically filtered tank (hang-on-back, canister, or sump) who needs a quick clarity fix after substrate disturbance, medication treatments, or a new tank bacterial bloom. It's also worth keeping on hand for emergency clarity before photos or tank inspections.

**Skip it if:** You run a sponge-filter-only setup, you're looking for a long-term water quality solution, or your cloudiness is green rather than white/gray.

## FAQ

**Q: How often can I use API ACCU-CLEAR?**
A: You can re-dose daily if needed, but if you're dosing more than twice a week, you're masking a filtration or feeding problem. Fix that instead.

**Q: Will it harm my beneficial bacteria?**
A: No. It only clumps suspended particles; the nitrifying bacteria living on surfaces in your filter and substrate remain untouched.

**Q: Does it work in saltwater tanks?**
A: Yes, it's safe for marine aquariums. Just dose slightly less — saltwater responds faster to polymer clarifiers.
