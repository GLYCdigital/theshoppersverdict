# Wellness Healthy Indulgence Gravies Variety Pack Review: The Picky-Eater Test

If your cat turns their nose up at pâté and treats every mealtime like a hostage negotiation, the Wellness Healthy Indulgence Gravies line deserves a closer look. This variety pack is specifically engineered for cats who crave that slick, savory gravy coating — think shredded chicken floating in broth, not a mystery loaf. After digging through 215 ratings averaging 4.3 stars, here's what you actually need to know before adding it to your cart.

## What You Get

The Variety Pack ships with 12 pouches (2.8 oz each) spanning four flavors: Chicken, Turkey, Beef, and Whitefish. Each pouch is easy-tear and single-serving, which beats wrestling with a can opener at 6 AM. As shown in the product image, the texture is the headline — visible shreds of meat suspended in a glossy gravy, not a uniform mush. The ingredient deck is clean: real chicken or fish is listed first, no by-product meal, no carrageenan, no artificial colors. That alone separates it from the budget shelf at your grocery store.

## Real-World Performance

The gravy-to-meat ratio is generous — arguably the selling point. When you squeeze a pouch into a bowl, it pours like a light broth with meat chunks, and most cats respond to that immediately. One buyer noted, *"My 14-year-old who refuses almost everything licked the bowl clean. I actually cried."* Another said, *"Finally something my ragdoll will eat without me adding water."* That tracks with the texture-first design; this is wet food for cats who want to *drink* their dinner.

But there are tradeoffs. The 2.8 oz size is smaller than it looks. For an average 10-pound cat, the feeding guide suggests roughly one pouch per meal, but multiple reviewers reported needing two pouches to satisfy their cats — which pushes the daily cost up quickly. Some also mentioned the gravy can separate when warmed in the microwave (which you shouldn't do anyway; serve at room temp or lightly warmed in warm water). A handful of reviews flagged inconsistent batches where one pouch was gravy-heavy and another was mostly liquid.

## What Buyers Praise

The strongest recurring themes in customer feedback are palatability and digestion. Cats who are notoriously finicky — or recovering from illness — tend to accept this food with minimal fuss. Multiple reviewers with senior cats or cats with kidney issues appreciated the high moisture content, which supports hydration. The variety pack format also earns points: rather than gambling on a 24-pack of one flavor, you can rotate proteins and see which ones your cat actually finishes.

## Where It Falls Short

Honest downsides: the price. At roughly $1.50–$2.00 per pouch depending on current pricing, this is a premium product — not a budget staple. If you have multiple cats, costs add up fast. The smaller pouch size feels like a deliberate choice to keep perceived price low, but it means bigger cats eat through a case quickly. Also, if your cat prefers pâté or chunkier textures, this won't convert them. It's a gravy-first product, and that's the whole point.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The picky-eater owner** — if you've cycled through five brands already, this is worth a shot
- **Senior cat parents** — high moisture content and soft shreds are gentle on aging teeth and kidneys
- **Rotation feeders** — the variety pack is perfect for keeping mealtime interesting

Skip it if you need a budget-friendly daily staple for multiple cats, or if your cat has a confirmed poultry allergy (the Chicken and Turkey flavors dominate).

## FAQ

**Is this food complete and balanced for everyday feeding?**
Yes. It meets AAFCO nutrient profiles for adult maintenance, so it's suitable as a full meal — not just a topper. That said, many owners use it as a topper anyway due to cost.

**How many pouches should I feed per day?**
For a 10-lb cat, the label suggests 2.5–3 pouches daily. In practice, most buyers report feeding 2–3 pouches, depending on whether their cat also eats dry food.

**Does the gravy cause loose stools?**
Occasionally, if your cat isn't used to high-moisture foods. Most reviewers report normal digestion, but transition gradually over 4–5 days if your cat is new to wet food.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Buy it if your cat demands gravy and you value clean ingredients. Skip it if you're on a tight budget or your cat prefers pâté.

**Buy it if:** You have a picky or senior cat who needs encouragement to eat and stay hydrated. The texture-first approach works, and the clean ingredient list gives you peace of mind.

**Skip it if:** You have multiple cats and need an affordable daily staple. The small pouches and premium price make this a treat-tier product for most households.

The Wellness Healthy Indulgence Gravies Variety Pack isn't the cheapest way to feed your cat, but it might be the most effective way to get a stubborn eater to finish a meal. For that alone, it earns a solid recommendation.
