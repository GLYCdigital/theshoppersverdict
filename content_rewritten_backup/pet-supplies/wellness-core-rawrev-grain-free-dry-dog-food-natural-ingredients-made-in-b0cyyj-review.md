**If your dog turns up their nose at ordinary kibble but you're not ready to commit to a full raw diet, Wellness CORE RawRev sits in a smart middle ground.** It's a high-protein grain-free kibble with freeze-dried raw turkey pieces mixed right in. The idea is simple: give dogs the convenience of dry food with the nutritional punch of raw. At 4.4 stars across 220 ratings, most owners report it delivers — but there are a few things worth knowing before you buy.

## What's Actually in the Bag

Open the bag and you'll see two textures: dark, dense kibble nuggets and lighter, crumbly freeze-dried raw pieces. The formula leads with deboned turkey, followed by chicken meal and salmon meal — these concentrated protein sources push crude protein to a minimum of 36%, which is genuinely high for commercial dry food.

The fat content sits at 16%, with omega-3 fatty acids included for skin and coat health. You're also getting glucosamine and chondroitin for joint support, which is a thoughtful addition for aging or active breeds. The freeze-dried raw bites are made from turkey, turkey liver, and turkey heart — single-source animal protein that's minimally processed.

## How It Performs in Real Life

Owner feedback consistently highlights three things. First, the protein boost is noticeable — multiple reviewers mention their dogs have more energy and better muscle tone. As one verified buyer put it, *"My picky golden retriever actually gets excited for mealtime now. She licks the bowl clean, which never happened with our old kibble."*

Second, the raw pieces do help with palatability. Dogs that ignore standard kibble often respond to the texture contrast. One customer noted, *"I was skeptical about the freeze-dried bits, but my 11-year-old lab mix who stopped eating her food now finishes every bowl."*

Third, digestion seems to improve for many dogs. The grain-free formula with probiotics and fiber from chicory root, flaxseed, and mixed berries appears to agree with sensitive stomachs. Several reviews report firmer stools and less gas within two weeks of switching.

## Where It Falls Short

The most common complaint is inconsistent distribution of the freeze-dried pieces. Because they're denser than the kibble, they settle during shipping. Multiple reviewers report the top half of the bag is mostly plain kibble, with the raw pieces concentrated at the bottom. It's not a dealbreaker, but worth knowing — you may need to shake the bag thoroughly before each serving.

The grain-free aspect also deserves honest consideration. While most dogs do fine, the FDA has investigated a potential link between grain-free diets and dilated cardiomyopathy (DCM) in certain breeds. If your dog is a golden retriever, or has any history of heart issues, have a conversation with your vet before switching.

Finally, there's the price. This isn't budget kibble — you're paying a premium for the raw inclusion and high-quality protein sources. For a large breed dog, expect to go through a bag every two to three weeks.

## Who Should Buy This

This food is best for **owners of active, athletic dogs** who need sustained energy, **picky eaters** who refuse standard kibble, and **dogs with grain sensitivities** that show up as itchy skin or digestive upset. It's also a solid choice if you're curious about raw feeding but want the convenience and safety of commercial dry food.

Skip it if you're on a tight budget, have a dog with known heart conditions, or your dog is a senior with significantly reduced activity levels — they may not need this much protein.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I feed this to my puppy?**
A: It's formulated for adult maintenance. Wellness makes a separate puppy formula — use that instead for growing dogs.

**Q: How should I store the freeze-dried raw pieces?**
A: Keep the bag tightly sealed in a cool, dry place. The raw pieces are shelf-stable until opened, but moisture is their enemy. Don't store in the refrigerator — condensation can introduce bacteria.

**Q: Is this food suitable for weight management?**
A: At 16% fat and 36% protein, it's calorie-dense. If your dog needs to lose weight, look for a lower-calorie formula. For maintaining weight on an active dog, portion control is key.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** Your dog is a picky eater, you want a high-protein food without going full raw, or your dog has grain sensitivities.

**Skip it if:** You're on a strict budget, your vet has concerns about grain-free diets for your breed, or your dog is sedentary.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Solid nutrition, great palatability, and a smart approach to raw inclusion. The price and the uneven distribution of raw pieces keep it from a perfect score, but for most dogs, this is a genuinely excellent food that delivers visible results.
