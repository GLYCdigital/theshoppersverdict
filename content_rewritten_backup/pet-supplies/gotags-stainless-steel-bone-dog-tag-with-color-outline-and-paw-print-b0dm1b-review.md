A dog tag is one of those purchases you hope you never actually need to rely on. But when your dog bolts after a squirrel or slips through a gap in the fence, that little piece of metal becomes the difference between a happy reunion and a frantic week of posting flyers. The GoTags Stainless Steel Bone Dog Tag with Color Outline and Paw Print is designed for exactly that scenario — and it does the job well.

## What You're Actually Getting

This is a bone-shaped ID tag made from stainless steel, not the cheap stamped aluminum you'll find at big-box pet stores. The tag measures roughly 1.5 inches across, which puts it in the medium range — appropriate for most dogs from 15 pounds up to large breeds. The front features a colored outline (you can choose from several colors at checkout) with a paw print accent. The back is where your dog's information gets laser-engraved.

The package includes the tag itself plus a split ring for attaching it to your dog's collar. That's it. No silencers, no extra hardware — though you can order those separately if your dog's tag clanks against their food bowl at 3 AM.

## Build Quality and Real-World Performance

Here's where the GoTags tag earns its keep. Stainless steel is genuinely more durable than the aluminum tags most pet stores stock. Aluminum bends, scratches, and fades within a year of daily wear. Stainless doesn't. The laser engraving is deep — you can feel the recessed lettering with your fingernail — which means it won't wear away like printed or embossed tags do.

One owner noted, "My dog swims in the lake every day and rolls in mud constantly. After six months, the tag still looks brand new and the engraving is perfectly legible." That tracks with what I'd expect from this material.

The split ring is sturdy enough to hold up to normal wear, though I'd recommend checking it monthly for any signs of fatigue — that's true of any tag, regardless of brand.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The bone shape — while classic and cute — spins on the collar ring. That means the engraved side occasionally faces backward, and you'll need to flip it around to read it. Not a functional problem for anyone scanning a QR code or microchip, but annoying if you're trying to read the info quickly.

For small breeds, this tag runs heavy. A 6-pound Chihuahua wearing a 1.5-inch stainless tag will feel the weight. GoTags makes smaller sizes, so consider sizing down if your dog is under 10 pounds.

Finally, the color outline is painted onto the metal. It's durable, but after a few years of heavy scratching against concrete or crates, it may show wear. The engraving will outlast the paint.

## Who Should Buy This
