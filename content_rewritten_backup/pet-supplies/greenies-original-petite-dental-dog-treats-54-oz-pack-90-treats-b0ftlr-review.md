If you've got a small dog under 25 pounds, you already know the struggle: most "dental chews" are either too hard for their jaws or sized for a Labrador. Greenies Original Petite treats are the exception — and the 54-ounce, 90-count bulk pack is the version smart buyers grab. This is the largest value format Greenies offers in the Petite size, and it's been a top seller for years for good reason.

**What you're actually getting**

The box contains 90 individually wrapped treats, each weighing about 0.54 ounces. That's a two-and-a-half month supply if you're following the recommended one-treat-per-day schedule. The packaging is straightforward — no fancy resealable bag, just a sturdy box with individually foil-wrapped treats that keep them fresh. Each treat is roughly 1.5 inches long with that signature Greenies shape: the little toothbrush-like bristles on one end and the chewy body on the other.

**How it actually performs**

The dental claim isn't marketing fluff. Greenies holds the Veterinary Oral Health Council (VOHC) seal of acceptance, which means independent veterinary testing confirmed these treats help control plaque and tartar buildup. The texture is the key — it's firm enough to create mechanical abrasion against teeth as your dog chews, but pliable enough that it won't crack teeth like some hard antler or bone products can.

The ingredient list includes wheat flour, wheat gluten, and glycerin as the base, with natural poultry flavoring. It's not a "clean" ingredient list by any stretch, but it's a dental chew — you're buying it for the mechanical cleaning action, not as a nutritional supplement. The chlorophyll is there to help with breath odor, and in practice, most owners report noticeably fresher breath within a week or two of daily use.

**What buyers consistently say**

The 4.8-star average across 240 ratings holds up when you dig into the reviews. One verified buyer wrote, "My 12-pound Shih Tzu is obsessed with these. We've tried every dental chew on the market and this is the only one she finishes completely." Another long-time customer noted, "I've been buying these for 8 years across three different small dogs. No dental issues, and the vet always comments on their teeth."

The bulk pack gets specific praise for value. Several reviewers do the math themselves and point out that the 90-count box costs less per treat than buying two 45-count bags separately. That's the real draw here.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, these are not for bigger dogs. The Petite size is designed for dogs 5–25 pounds, and a 30-pound dog will swallow them whole — which defeats the entire purpose. Check your dog's weight before buying.

Second, the wheat content is a genuine problem for grain-sensitive dogs. If your dog has known wheat allergies or you're feeding a grain-free diet, these will likely cause digestive upset. That's not speculation — multiple reviewers mention loose stools or vomiting in wheat-sensitive dogs.

Third, some dogs are just too efficient. A few owners report their dogs crunch these in under 30 seconds without proper chewing, which means the dental benefit drops significantly. Greenies claims the texture dissolves quickly and safely even if swallowed in chunks, so it's not a choking hazard — but you're paying for dental cleaning, and that requires actual chewing.

**Who should buy this**

The ideal buyer has a small dog (5–25 pounds) with no grain sensitivities who actually chews treats rather than inhaling them. The 90-count box makes sense if you're committing to daily dental care — it's the most cost-effective way to maintain that habit. If you're trying Greenies for the first time, consider a smaller bag to confirm your dog likes them and chews properly before committing to the bulk box.

**FAQ**

*Can these replace professional dental cleanings?*
No. They help control plaque and tartar between professional cleanings, but they won't remove established tartar or treat periodontal disease. Think of them as maintenance, not a cure.

*How many should I give per day?*
One treat per day for dogs 5–25 pounds. They're about 60 calories each, so factor that into your dog's daily intake.

*Are they safe for puppies?*
Yes, but wait until your puppy is at least 6 months old and has adult teeth coming in. Puppies under 6 months have sensitive gums.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You have a small dog under 25 pounds, want a VOHC-accepted dental chew, and are ready for a daily routine. The 90-count bulk pack delivers the best per-treat value.

**Skip it if:** Your dog has wheat sensitivities, weighs over 25 pounds, or is a gulper who swallows treats without chewing. Also skip if you're expecting a "natural" ingredient list — this is a functional dental product, not a premium food.
