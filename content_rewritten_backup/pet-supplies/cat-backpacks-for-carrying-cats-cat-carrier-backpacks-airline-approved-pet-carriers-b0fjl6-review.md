## FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Can I wash the entire backpack?**
No — spot clean the outer shell. The fleece liner is removable and machine washable, which is what actually gets dirty. Don't dunk the whole thing in water; you'll ruin the frame.

**Q: Will my cat escape from the mesh?**
The mesh is reinforced and the zippers are lockable. I found no credible reports of cats clawing through the mesh. The bigger risk is the front zipper being left partially open — that's on you, not the product.

**Q: Is it safe for a cat who's never been in a carrier?**
Start with short, positive sessions at home. Leave the carrier open with treats inside. The top-loading design helps because you can lower a nervous cat in rather than shoving from the front. Reviewers report success with previously carrier-averse cats, but it still requires patience.
