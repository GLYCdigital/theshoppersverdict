If you've ever watched your dog slide a plastic bowl across the kitchen floor while eating, you know the struggle. The MILIFUN Double Dog Cat Bowls set aims to solve that with a weighted, non-slip stand and stainless steel inserts. At 4.2 stars across 210 ratings, it's a solid mid-tier option — but it's not without compromises.

**What You Actually Get**

The set includes two 2-cup capacity stainless steel bowls nestled into a single raised plastic stand. The stand features a rubber base for grip and a center handle for easy carrying. As shown in the product image, the design is straightforward: two round wells, no dividers, no frills. It's compact enough for corner placement but substantial enough to hold a full day's water for a medium dog.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The stainless steel bowls are the standout feature here. Unlike plastic, they won't harbor scratches where bacteria multiply, and they're fully dishwasher-safe. The stand itself is molded plastic — not premium, but sturdy. The rubber base does its job; one reviewer noted, *"My 70-pound lab tries to push this around while eating and it doesn't budge."* That's the core promise delivered.

For cats and small-to-medium dogs, the raised height is a genuine ergonomic win. It reduces bending and keeps whiskers from brushing the bowl edges — a detail sensory-sensitive cats appreciate. The bowls hold enough for a 30–40 lb dog's meals without constant refilling.

**What Buyers Praise**

Recurring themes in positive reviews:
- *"Finally a bowl set that stays put on tile floors."* — multiple mentions of the non-slip base
- *"Easy to clean, no more plastic smell."* — the stainless steel wins loyal fans
- *"The raised height helped my senior dachshund eat more comfortably."* — joint-friendly design matters

**Where It Falls Short**

The most common complaint? The bowls aren't locked in place. They sit in recessed wells but can be lifted out by determined dogs — or dumped entirely by a paw-happy cat. One frustrated reviewer wrote, *"My German Shepherd flips the water bowl out every time he drinks."* That's not universal, but it's a real risk with heavy drinkers.

Second, the stand height is fixed. If you own a Great Dane or a Basset Hound with different needs, this won't adjust to fit. It's built for the average dog, not the extremes.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Cat owners** with whisker-sensitive felines who refuse to eat from deep bowls
- **Medium dog owners** (20–50 lbs) with tile or hardwood floors
- **Senior pet parents** whose dogs need a slight elevation to reduce joint strain
- **Multi-pet households** that want a single, stable station for both food and water

Skip it if you have a giant breed, a dog that deliberately tips bowls for entertainment, or if you need height adjustability.

**FAQ**

**Can the bowls go in the dishwasher?**
Yes, the stainless steel bowls are dishwasher-safe. The plastic stand should be hand-washed — heat from a dishwasher cycle can warp it over time.

**Is this suitable for a puppy?**
It works, but the raised height may be awkward for very small puppies. Consider a ground-level bowl until they're 5–6 months old.

**Will the rubber base damage hardwood floors?**
No. The base is soft rubber that grips without scratching. That said, debris trapped under the base can leave marks, so wipe the floor periodically.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a stable, easy-to-clean feeding station for a cat or medium dog without paying premium prices. The non-slip design genuinely works, and the stainless steel bowls justify the cost alone.

**Skip it if** your pet is an aggressive eater prone to flipping bowls, or you need a height-adjustable system for a very large breed.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — A dependable, well-executed basic that nails the essentials but stops short of being exceptional. For the price, it's a smart buy for most households — just know the bowl-securing limitations going in.
