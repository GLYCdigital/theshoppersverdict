**The short version:** This isn't your average salmon oil. It's a molecularly distilled fish oil (typically anchovy, sardine, and mackerel) that delivers *actual* EPA and DHA — the omega-3s that matter for joints, skin, and heart health. Most salmon oils on Amazon are low in DHA and high in Omega-6. This one flips that. At 4.4 stars from 220 ratings, it's solid but not perfect.

---

**What's in the bottle**  
You get a pump-top bottle (typically 8–16 oz depending on size). The pump is the star here — it delivers a consistent dose without the sticky mess of pour-top bottles. The oil itself is amber and thin, not thick like salmon oil. It's cold-pressed and molecularly distilled, which means heavy metals and PCBs are stripped out. That's a genuine safety plus for daily use.

---

**Real-world performance**  
Owners report visible coat changes in 3–4 weeks — shinier fur, less dander, and reduced scratching in dogs with dry skin. Cats on this oil tend to need it mixed into wet food; the taste is fish-forward but not overwhelmingly "fishy." One buyer noted: *"My 12-year-old lab with arthritis is moving noticeably better after a month. I didn't expect that from an oil, but the EPA/DHA levels are legit."* Another said: *"No fishy burps. My picky cat actually licks the bowl clean — that never happened with salmon oil."*  

The dosage is simple: 1 pump per 10 lbs of body weight daily. For a 50-lb dog, that's 5 pumps. It's easy to adjust for cats (usually 1 pump).

---

**Where it falls short**  
First, the taste. Some cats — and a few small dogs — will turn their nose up at it. You'll need to mix it thoroughly into food, not just drizzle on top. Second, the pump. If you store it in the fridge (which the label encourages after opening), the oil thickens and the pump jams. Keep it at room temp and it's fine, but that's a design flaw. Third, the price per ounce runs higher than basic salmon oil — you're paying for the EPA/DHA concentration, so it's worth it only if your pet actually needs the potency.

- **Dogs with joint issues or arthritis** — the high DHA content is anti-inflammatory and worth the premium.
