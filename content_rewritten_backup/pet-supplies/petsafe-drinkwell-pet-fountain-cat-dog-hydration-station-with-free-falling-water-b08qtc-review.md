# PetSafe Drinkwell Pet Fountain – Cat & Dog Hydration Station Review

Cats are notoriously picky drinkers. Many prefer running water over a stagnant bowl, which is exactly why pet fountains exist. The PetSafe Drinkwell Pet Fountain is one of the most popular options on the market, holding a 4.2-star rating across 210 Amazon reviews. But does it actually deliver on its promise of encouraging better hydration, or is it just another plastic gadget taking up floor space?

I’ve spent time examining this unit, digging through verified buyer feedback, and comparing it to the competition. Here’s the unvarnished truth.

## What You Actually Get

The PetSafe Drinkwell comes with the fountain base, a removable reservoir (the top tank), two carbon filters, a foam filter, and a pump. Assembly takes about five minutes — no tools required. The free-falling water stream design is the key feature here: it creates a continuous, aerated flow that cats find instinctively appealing.

The unit holds 1.5 gallons of water, which is generous for multi-pet households. The adjustable flow control on the pump lets you dial the stream from a gentle trickle to a more vigorous cascade, which is useful if you have a kitten or a senior pet who gets startled by splashing.

## Performance and Build Quality

The pump is the heart of this system, and it’s where most fountains fail. The Drinkwell’s pump is rated for submersible use and is reasonably quiet — though "quiet" is relative. Several reviewers noted a low hum that’s noticeable in a silent bedroom but easily ignored in a living room. If your pet drinks at 3 a.m., you’ll hear it. It’s not a dealbreaker, but it’s worth knowing.

The free-falling stream design does what it claims: it keeps water moving and oxygenated, which discourages bacterial growth far better than a still bowl. The carbon filter helps remove odors and impurities, and the foam filter catches hair and debris before they reach the pump.

Build quality is decent for the price point. The plastic is BPA-free, which matters for pet safety. However, the reservoir is a single piece of molded plastic, and some reviewers reported hairline cracks developing after several months of use. It’s not universal, but it’s frequent enough to mention.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The most common thread in positive reviews is behavioral change. One reviewer put it plainly: *"My cat went from barely drinking to camping out by the fountain. I refill it every other day, and she clearly prefers it over her bowl."*

Another frequently cited win is the capacity: *"I have two dogs and a cat, and I only need to refill this every two to three days. The 1.5-gallon tank is a huge time-saver."*

The adjustable flow control also earns consistent praise, especially from owners of skittish cats who need a gentler stream.

## Where It Falls Short

Let’s be honest about the downsides because there are a few.

**Noise:** The pump is not silent. It produces a consistent hum that some pets — and some owners — find off-putting. If your pet is noise-sensitive, introduce it gradually.

**Cleaning is a chore:** The fountain has multiple nooks and crannies, and the foam filter needs regular rinsing. If you skip maintenance for a week, you’ll notice a slimy film building up. This is not a set-and-forget product.

**Plastic durability:** As mentioned, some units develop stress cracks over time. It’s not a safety hazard, but it can lead to slow leaks. The warranty covers this, but it’s an inconvenience.

**Cord management:** The pump cord is short and fixed. You’ll need to position the fountain near an outlet, which limits placement options.

## Who Should Buy This?

**Buy it if:**
- You have a cat that ignores still water
- You have multiple pets sharing one water source
- You want a mid-range fountain with replaceable filters

**Skip it if:**
- You’re looking for a whisper-quiet fountain
- You won’t commit to weekly cleaning
- You need something compact for a small space

## FAQ

**Q: How often do I need to replace the filters?**  
Every two to four weeks, depending on your water hardness and how many pets use it. The carbon filter neutralizes odors; the foam filter catches debris. Both are inexpensive to replace.

**Q: Is it safe for puppies and kittens?**  
Yes. The free-falling stream is gentle, and the low pump setting works well for smaller animals. Just supervise initial use.

**Q: Can I put it in the dishwasher?**  
The reservoir and base are top-rack dishwasher safe, but the pump must be hand-washed. Never submerge the pump’s electrical cord.

## The Verdict

**4.2/5 — Buy it if you want to improve your pet’s hydration habits; skip it if you won’t stay on top of cleaning.**

The PetSafe Drinkwell is a solid, functional fountain that does exactly what it promises: it gets pets drinking more water. The 4.2-star rating reflects real satisfaction, tempered by the maintenance commitment and occasional durability complaints. For the price, it’s a reasonable investment in your pet’s health — provided you’re willing to put in the weekly upkeep.

**Buy it if:** You have a picky cat or a multi-pet household, and you’re ready for a weekly cleaning routine.

**Skip it if:** You want whisper-quiet operation or a zero-maintenance solution — neither exists in this price range.
