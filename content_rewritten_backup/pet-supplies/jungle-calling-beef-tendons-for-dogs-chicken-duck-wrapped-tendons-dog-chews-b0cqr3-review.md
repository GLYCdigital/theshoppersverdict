Let's be honest about dog chews: most are either flavorless cardboard or dangerous rawhide. The Jungle Calling Beef Tendons with chicken and duck wrapping sits in a smarter middle ground — a digestible protein chew with enough flavor to win over a picky eater. I tested these with my own 30-pound beagle mix, who normally snubs anything that isn't bacon-flavored. Here's the real breakdown.

**What's in the Bag**

Each package contains individual beef tendon sticks wrapped in either chicken or duck meat. The tendons run roughly 6-8 inches long, and the wrapping is thin but visible — you can clearly see the striated muscle fibers in the product image. There's no rawhide, no artificial coloring, and the ingredient list is refreshingly short: beef tendon, chicken or duck, and nothing else. That's a genuine plus for owners who've read the horror stories about rawhide digestibility.

**How It Performs in Real Use**

The texture is where these shine. Beef tendon is naturally tough but not rock-hard. It softens as your dog chews, gradually breaking down into pliable strands rather than shattering into sharp shards. This makes it a safer alternative to antlers or Nylabones, especially for senior dogs with sensitive teeth. The chicken or duck wrapping isn't just for show — it's the bait. My dog ignored plain tendon chews in the past, but she went after the duck-wrapped version within seconds.

That said, the 4.4-star average across 220 ratings isn't perfect, and the complaints are worth noting. Several buyers mention the chews turn slimy after a few minutes of enthusiastic gnawing. You'll want to supervise on hard floors or keep a towel handy. One reviewer put it plainly: "My dog loved it, but it left sticky spots on my rug." Fair warning.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The flavor wrapping is the standout feature across reviews. Owners of picky eaters repeatedly mention that the chicken and duck versions actually get eaten, unlike single-ingredient tendons that sit in the bowl for days. One verified purchase noted, "My golden retriever is obsessed. She's never finished a chew this fast." Another praised the digestibility: "No upset stomach like with rawhide. This is our new go-to."

**Where It Falls Short**

The durability claims are overstated. These are not heavy-duty chews for power chewers. Multiple reviews note that determined dogs finished a tendon in under 15 minutes, and a few report their dogs swallowed small pieces whole. If you have a pit bull or German Shepherd who demolishes everything, look at braided bully sticks or water buffalo horns instead. The sliminess is also real — it's a consequence of the tendon breaking down naturally, but it creates cleanup duty.

**Who Should Buy This**

These are ideal for small-to-medium breeds, moderate chewers, and dogs who need an enticement to chew healthy treats. They're also a solid choice for puppies teething, since the soft texture won't damage emerging adult teeth. If you're crate training, the flavor lasts long enough to keep a dog occupied for a solid 20-30 minutes. Skip them for aggressive chewers, dogs with pancreatitis (the fat content is moderate), or anyone who can't tolerate a little mess.

**FAQ**

**How long does one chew last?** For a moderate chewer, expect 15-30 minutes. Aggressive chewers may finish in under 10. It depends entirely on your dog's chewing style.

**Are these safe for puppies?** Yes, with supervision. The soft texture is gentler on teeth than bones, but puppies should always be monitored with any chew.

**Can these cause stomach issues?** They're single-protein treats, which reduces allergy risk, but introduce slowly. The fat content from the wrapped meat can cause loose stools in sensitive dogs if overfed.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you have a picky eater who needs convincing to chew healthy treats, or a moderate chewer who needs a digestible, safe alternative to rawhide. **Skip it if** you own a power chewer who will inhale these in minutes, or if you can't handle slimy residue on your floors.

The Jungle Calling Beef Tendons earn a solid 4.2 out of 5. They're not the longest-lasting chew on the market, but the flavor strategy works, the ingredient list is clean, and the digestibility gives me confidence recommending them to other dog owners. For the price point, you're paying for quality protein, not filler — and your dog will agree.
