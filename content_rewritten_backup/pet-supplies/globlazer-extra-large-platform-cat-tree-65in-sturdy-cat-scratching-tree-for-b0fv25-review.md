## The 65-Inch Cat Tree That Actually Handles Big Cats

Most cat trees are built for kittens and dainty 8-pound house cats. The Globlazer Extra Large Platform Cat Tree takes a different approach — it's engineered for the heavyweight division. At 65 inches tall with platforms nearly 20 inches wide, this is one of the few scratching trees that won't wobble when a 15-pound cat launches itself at the top perch.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains 14 pre-drilled parts, a hex key, and clearly labeled hardware bags. No tools beyond the included key are needed. The build sequence takes about 15-20 minutes — the instructions are pictorial, not paragraph-heavy, which is rare in this category. The base measures 18x18 inches, and the three main platforms are spaced so cats can hop between them without awkward stretching.

Sisal wrapping covers the three main posts from base to top. It's natural sisal, not the painted synthetic kind, and it's wrapped tightly — no loose strands or gaps where the cardboard core peeks through. The plush fabric on the platforms is a low-pile fleece that holds up to clawing better than the shaggy materials cheap trees use.

## Real-World Performance

The structural integrity is where this tree earns its price. The posts are 3 inches in diameter — noticeably thicker than the 2.2-inch posts on typical $60 trees. That extra girth translates to less flex when a cat stretches to scratch. One verified buyer noted, "My 18-pound Ragdoll uses the top perch daily and I've never seen it wobble, even when he jumps down hard."

The wide platforms are the standout feature. Most cat trees max out at 12-14 inches of perch width. The Globlazer's 19.7-inch platforms give big cats room to sprawl without hanging off the edges. That's a meaningful difference for owners of Maine Coons, Norwegian Forest Cats, or just overweight house cats who need more real estate.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 225 ratings, three themes dominate positive feedback:

- **Stability:** Multiple reviewers mention the tree staying put on hardwood floors without wall anchoring.
- **Scratching durability:** The sisal posts hold up to daily use for months without fraying into strings.
- **Easy assembly:** The labeled parts and straightforward design cut assembly time dramatically versus competitors.

One 5-star reviewer wrote: "Bought this after returning two other trees that collapsed under my 16-pounder. This one doesn't budge. The platforms are actually big enough for him to stretch out."

## Where It Falls Short

The base, at 18x18 inches, is functional but not generous. On thick carpet, a 25+ pound cat making a hard landing from the top perch can shift the tree slightly. Wall anchoring is recommended for extremely heavy cats or households with multiple cats racing around.

The top perch lacks a raised rim. Most cats adapt fine, but a rambunctious cat mid-play-chase can overshoot and fall. It's a 4-foot drop to the floor — not dangerous, but startling for the cat and potentially damaging to nearby furniture.

Assembly holes align well, but the included instructions don't specify which side of the base faces forward. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker — the asymmetry only matters for perfect placement.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:**

## FAQ

**Q: Will this hold two cats at once?**
A: Yes, if they're under 25 pounds combined. The 18-inch base and thick posts handle the weight, but two cats wrestling on the top platform may cause wobble.

**Q: Is the sisal safe for kittens?**
A: Yes. Natural sisal is safe if ingested in small amounts, and the tight wrapping means no loose strands for kittens to pull and swallow.

**Q: Does it come with a hanging toy?**
A: No. The tree is platform-and-post only. You'll need to attach your own toys if your cat likes to bat at dangling items.
