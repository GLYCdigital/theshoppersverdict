## What You Actually Get

The FORWH bowl is a **one-piece design** — no complicated assembly, no separate float mechanism to lose. The key feature is the **built-in floating disc** that rises as water level drops, minimizing the exposed water surface. This is what keeps splashing to a minimum.

**Dimensions:** Roughly 11 inches wide and 3.5 inches tall — wide enough for medium and large breeds without being so deep that whiskers get soaked. The 70oz/2L capacity means fewer refills throughout the day, especially for households with multiple pets.

The bowl is made from **BPA-free, food-grade plastic** with a **non-slip rubber base**. The base is the real workhorse here — it grips most floors well enough that even enthusiastic drinkers won't slide it across your kitchen.

## Real-World Performance

**The no-spill claim holds up — mostly.** The floating disc effectively reduces surface area, which cuts splashing by a significant margin. If your dog is a messy drinker who dunks their whole muzzle in, this bowl will save your floors from the worst of it.

**However, it's not 100% spill-proof.** Aggressive drinkers can still splash water over the rim if they're really going at it. The disc also means the bowl needs regular cleaning — water sits in the gap between the disc and the bowl wall, which can develop a slimy film if you don't wash it every few days.

**Cleaning is straightforward** — it's top-rack dishwasher safe, though hand washing is faster since the disc can trap debris. The plastic is sturdy, not flimsy, and shows no cracking or warping after several months of use.

## Where It Falls Short

**The floating disc can be a nuisance.** Some buyers report it makes a **clanking noise** as it bobs when the water level drops. It's not loud, but in a quiet room, you'll notice it.

**The plastic scratches easily.** After a few weeks, visible scuffs appear from regular washing — cosmetic, not functional, but worth noting if aesthetics matter to you.

**The disc doesn't fully prevent mess for every dog.** Breeds with long, floppy ears (think Labs, Spaniels) still drip water onto the floor when they drink — the bowl reduces splashing but won't dry their ears.

**One buyer's frustration:** *"The disc floats up and my dog can nudge it out of place. It doesn't stay perfectly centered."* This seems to be a manufacturing tolerance issue — most units work fine, but some discs sit slightly off-center.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this bowl safe for small animals like guinea pigs or rabbits?**  
A: Yes, the low profile and 2L capacity work well for small pets. The floating disc prevents them from getting their heads wet, though you may want a smaller version if you have just one small pet — 70oz is a lot of water for a guinea pig.

**Q: How often do I need to clean it?**  
A: Every 2-3 days. The gap between the floating disc and bowl wall traps debris and can develop a biofilm if neglected. Dishwasher-safe, but hand washing is quicker.

**Q: Will the floating disc break if my dog plays with it?**  
A: The disc is durable but not indestructible. If your dog is a chewer, this isn't the right bowl — they could dislodge or damage the disc.
