**Blue Buffalo True Chews Review: The Honest Take on This Popular Dog Treat**

When a dog treat hits 4.6 stars across 229 Amazon ratings, it earns a closer look. Blue Buffalo True Chews has built a loyal following, but is it worth your money? I dug into the ingredient list, the texture, and what real buyers are saying. Here's the straight story.

**What You Actually Get**

The bag contains soft, chewy strips made with real chicken as the first ingredient — not chicken meal, not by-product, actual deboned chicken. That's a meaningful distinction in the treat aisle. As shown in the product image, the pieces have a visible meaty texture, not the uniform processed look you see with cheaper brands. The resealable zipper closure is genuinely functional, which matters for soft treats that can dry out fast.

**Build Quality and Real-World Performance**

The texture is where True Chews shines. These are soft enough to break apart with your fingers, which makes them excellent for training sessions or for senior dogs with worn-down teeth. One Amazon reviewer summed it up: "My 12-year-old lab has trouble with hard treats but she goes crazy for these. She can chew them without any struggle."

The treats hold their moisture well. Several buyers mentioned that even halfway through the bag, the pieces stayed soft and fresh — a common failure point for competitors in this category. The chicken flavor is strong, which is likely why so many reviewers report their picky eaters devouring these without hesitation.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Across the reviews, three themes keep coming up:

- **Palatability**: Dogs that turn their nose up at other treats consistently accept these. One reviewer wrote, "I've tried four different brands and my fussy shih tzu refuses everything except these."
- **Ingredient transparency**: Owners appreciate that the label is straightforward. No artificial preservatives, no vague "meat derivatives" hiding in the list.
- **Digestibility**: Multiple reviewers with sensitive-stomach dogs reported no issues, which aligns with the simple ingredient profile.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the pieces run smaller than many buyers expect. Several reviews noted that for the price, the bag doesn't last long if you have a large breed dog. One frustrated buyer said, "These are tiny. My golden retriever swallows them in one bite, so I go through a bag in about a week."

Second, these contain barley and rice. If you're feeding a grain-free diet for medical reasons, this is a hard no. That's not a flaw in the product itself, but it's a compatibility issue you need to know before buying.

**Who Should Buy These**

- **Senior dog owners**: The soft texture is ideal for aging teeth and jaws.
- **Puppy trainers**: Small pieces and high palatability make for effective, quick training rewards.
- **Owners of picky eaters**: If your dog is a food snob, this is worth a shot.

Skip these if you need grain-free treats or if you have a large dog and budget matters — you'll go through bags quickly.

**How many treats come in a bag?** The count varies by bag size, but reviewers note the pieces are smaller than expected, so don't assume a large bag means a long-lasting supply.

**Do these contain any artificial ingredients?** No artificial preservatives, colors, or flavors are listed. The ingredient profile is clean and straightforward.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a soft, high-quality treat that most dogs genuinely love and that you feel good about feeding. The ingredient quality justifies the price for smaller breeds and moderate treat budgets.

**Skip it if** you need grain-free options or you're feeding a large dog on a tight budget — you'll burn through bags faster than you'd like.
