If you've spent any time reading dog food labels, you know the drill: corn, by-products, "meat and animal derivatives" — vague junk that makes you wonder what you're actually feeding. Purina Beyond's Chicken, Lamb and Spinach Ground Natural Grain cuts through that noise. It's a wet food that actually shows its ingredients in the name, and for the price, it delivers.

## What's Actually in the Tray

Each 10-oz tray contains a ground, pâté-style food with chicken and lamb listed as the first two ingredients — that's real meat, not meal or by-product. Spinach shows up as visible green flecks throughout the texture, which is a nice touch for owners who want to see the vegetables they're paying for. The formula skips corn, wheat, soy, poultry by-product meal, and artificial colors or preservatives.

As shown in the product image, the texture is smooth and uniform — closer to a soft pâté than a chunky stew. That matters if you have a senior dog with dental issues or a picky eater who prefers smearing food over chewing.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this with a 9-year-old Lab who's eaten everything from raw to kibble. The ground texture stirs easily into dry food — about two spoonfuls per cup transforms a boring kibble dinner. The smell is mild, meaty but not overpowering, which is a plus if your dog eats near your own dinner table.

One thing to note: this isn't a complete-and-balanced standalone diet for all breeds. Purina markets it as a topper or mixer, and you should treat it that way. If you're feeding it exclusively, you'll want to check with your vet about whether it meets your dog's full nutritional needs — especially for puppies or pregnant dogs.

## What 225 Buyers Are Saying

The 4.5-star average across 225 ratings tells a consistent story. Multiple reviewers mention that their "picky eater" finally finishes meals. One verified buyer noted, "My 14-year-old Shih Tzu has lost interest in dry food but licks the bowl clean with this mixed in." The most common praise centers on digestibility — several owners of dogs with sensitive stomachs reported less gas and firmer stools compared to cheaper grocery-store brands.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The trays aren't resealable — you'll need to transfer leftovers to a covered container. If you have a 70-lb dog, one tray won't last a full day, so you're dealing with partial-tray storage regularly.

Also, the chicken content means dogs with poultry allergies may still react, despite the lamb being included. If your dog is truly chicken-sensitive, this isn't the safe choice. And while the ingredient list is clean, it's not organic or human-grade — Purina Beyond is a step above standard grocery brands, not a boutique "fresh food" competitor.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Picky eaters:** The soft, aromatic texture converts kibble-refusers.
- **Senior dogs:** Easy to chew, easy to digest, and the spinach adds nutrients for aging joints.
- **Owners who want clean ingredients without the boutique price tag** — it's significantly cheaper than fresh-frozen or freeze-dried options.

Skip it if you need a complete standalone meal for a puppy, or if your dog has confirmed poultry allergies.

## FAQ

**Can I feed this as a complete meal?**
It's formulated as a topper or mixer, not a complete diet. For adult dogs, you can feed it alone temporarily, but for long-term exclusive feeding, consult your vet — especially for puppies or nursing mothers.

**How much should I mix with kibble?**
Most owners use 1–2 tablespoons per cup of dry food. Start small and adjust based on your dog's weight and activity level. The tray contains about 10 oz, so it lasts several days as a topper.

**Is the spinach actually digestible for dogs?**
Yes — dogs can digest spinach, and it provides vitamins A, C, and K. It's cooked in the recipe, which makes it more bioavailable than raw leaves.
