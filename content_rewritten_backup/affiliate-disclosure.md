The Shopper's Verdict is committed to full transparency. We want you to know exactly how we operate, how we earn revenue, and how that does — and doesn't — affect our content.

## Affiliate Links

Some of the links on this site are affiliate links. If you click on one of these links and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. These commissions help us cover the cost of running the site — research tools, hosting, and the time it takes to analyze thousands of reviews.

## Does this affect our verdicts?

No. Our verdict scores are based entirely on real Amazon customer reviews. We do not accept payment, free products, or any form of compensation in exchange for favorable coverage. A product earns its place on our site through the data — not through a marketing budget.

## Which products have affiliate links?

Any product page on our site may contain affiliate links to Amazon. We clearly label these links and disclose this relationship on every relevant page.

## Amazon Associates

The Shopper's Verdict is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.
