## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides:

- **Drying**—this is a stain, not a balm. If you already struggle with dry lips, you'll need a hydrating lip mask underneath. Several reviewers with chronically dry lips found it uncomfortable by hour four.
- **Uneven shade intensity**—the nude and mauve shades apply lighter than the tube color suggests. The red and berry are true to swatch, but the lighter shades need two coats.
- **Removal is a workout**—this stuff does *not* come off with a gentle wipe. You'll need an oil-based remover or a good micellar water and some patience.
