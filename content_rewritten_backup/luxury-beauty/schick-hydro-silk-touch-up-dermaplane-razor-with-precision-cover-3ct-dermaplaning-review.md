## Performance: Does It Actually Dermaplane?

Yes — but with technique requirements. The moisturizing strip helps the blade glide, but it's not a lubricant substitute. I found that washing my face and waiting 5 minutes for it to fully dry gave the best results — any dampness caused tugging.

On the eyebrow precision cover: it's genuinely useful. The guard narrows the blade exposure to about 1/4 inch, making it safe for shaping brows and cleaning up upper lip hair. This is rare — most drugstore facial razors don't include a precision attachment, and it elevates the value significantly.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the tradeoffs:

1. **Durability is limited.** Each razor realistically lasts 2–4 sessions before the blade feels tuggy. If you're dermaplaning weekly, that's a 3-week supply, not a 3-month one.
2. **Not for wet use.** Despite the "Hydro Silk" branding, this performs best on dry skin. Using it in the shower or on damp skin causes dragging and increases nick risk.
3. **No safety guard on the main blade.** The Precision Cover only fits the small portion. The full-width blade is exposed, so it's not ideal for beginners who are heavy-handed. Start with the eyebrow cover if you're nervous.
4. **Plastic construction.** It's lightweight, which is good for control, but it feels less premium than metal tools. If you want a reusable stainless steel handle, this isn't it.

## FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask

**Q: Can I use these to remove peach fuzz from my entire face?**
A: Yes. The full-width blade is designed for cheeks, forehead, and chin. Use the Precision Cover for upper lip, eyebrows, and jawline edges. Always on dry, clean skin with light pressure.

**Q: How often should I replace the razor?**
A: After 2–3 uses, or as soon as you feel any tugging. Dull blades cause micro-tears and irritation. At $3 per razor, there's no reason to push it.

**Q: Will dermaplaning make my hair grow back thicker or darker?**
A: No. This is a myth — shaving or dermaplaning does not change hair thickness, color, or growth rate. The blunt tip of the cut hair can feel slightly stubbly for a day or two, but it grows back exactly as it was before.
