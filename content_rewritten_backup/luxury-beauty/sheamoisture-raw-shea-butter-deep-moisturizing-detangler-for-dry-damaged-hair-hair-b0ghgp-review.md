## What's actually in the bottle

You get a 12-ounce pump bottle of what looks like a cross between a lotion and a light cream. The star ingredients are raw shea butter (the first listed), fair-trade shea oil, and a blend of hydration-focused extracts. Notably absent: silicones, sulfates, and parabens — which is why it plays well with curly girl method routines.

As shown in the product image, the pump dispenser is the unsung hero here. It meters out a clean dime-sized dollop without the crusty nozzle buildup that plagues most hair products. Small detail, but you'll appreciate it after the third use.

## How it actually performs

I tested this on my own 3B/3C hair that had gone four days without washing — the kind of matted, pillow-flattened mess that usually requires a full wet-down to detangle. With the SheaMoisture detangler, I worked a nickel-sized amount through each section *on dry hair*. The tangles didn't melt instantly, but after about 30 seconds of finger-detangling, the brush glided through without a single snag.

The slip factor is genuinely impressive. The shea butter coats each strand rather than just lubricating the surface, which means the detangling effect lasts through the next day — not just until the product dries.

## Where it falls short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. This is a **heavy** product. If you have fine, straight, or low-porosity hair, even a small amount can leave your hair looking greasy rather than moisturized. You'll need to experiment with how little you can get away with — and for some hair types, the answer might be "none at all."

The price is also a sticking point. At around $13 per bottle, you're paying a premium over mass-market detanglers. The ingredient quality justifies it, but budget shoppers may balk.

One more note: the pump can clog if you let product dry around the nozzle. Wipe it after each use or you'll be wrestling with a stuck pump by week three.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:**
- You have thick, coily, or tightly curled hair (3C–4C) that needs serious slip
- You detangle on dry hair and need something that doesn't require a full wash day
- You're sensitive to fragrances and want an effective detangler without the perfume
- You follow curly girl method and need silicone-free products

**Skip it if:**
- Your hair is fine, straight, or low-porosity — this will weigh it down
- You're on a tight budget and just need occasional detangling, not daily moisture

## FAQ

**Can I use this as a leave-in conditioner?**
Technically yes, but it's not ideal. The formula is thicker than a typical leave-in and works better as a pre-detangling step. Use a lighter leave-in after washing, then this only when you need serious slip.

**Does it work on kids' hair?**
Yes — several reviewers specifically mention using it on children's hair. The fragrance-free formula is a plus for sensitive young scalps. Just start with less than you think you need.

**Will it make my hair greasy?**
If you have high-porosity or coarse hair, no — it absorbs well. If your hair is fine or low-porosity, yes, very quickly. Start with a pea-sized amount and build up.
