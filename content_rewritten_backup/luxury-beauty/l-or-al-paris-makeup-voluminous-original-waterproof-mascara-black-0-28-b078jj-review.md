**The $10 mascara that refuses to budge — and that's both its glory and its curse.**

Let's be honest: waterproof mascara is a compromise. You trade easy removal for bulletproof wear. The L'Oréal Voluminous Original Waterproof makes that trade-off worth it — most of the time.

I've tested this against gym sessions, rainy commutes, and one particularly emotional movie scene. It held. Not a single smudge, flake, or raccoon eye. That's the headline here.

## What You Actually Get

The 0.28 fl oz tube delivers the same formula that's been a drugstore staple for decades. The wand is the star — a classic bristle brush with a slight taper that grabs lashes at the root and pulls through to the tip. The formula itself is on the drier side, which sounds bad but actually helps you layer coats without clumping.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where this mascara earns its 4.4-star rating. The waterproof formula creates a genuine seal around each lash. I wore this through a 45-minute spin class — dripped sweat, toweled my face, and my lashes looked exactly like they did at the start. That's not marketing talk; that's the "waterproof" promise actually delivered.

The volume build is respectable. One coat gives you "your lashes but better." Two coats deliver noticeable thickness. Three coats approaches falsie territory — though you'll want to work quickly since the formula sets fast. The black shade is deep and true, not gray-ish like some budget waterproofs.

But there's a cost. By hour eight, lashes feel stiff. Not uncomfortable, but you're aware you're wearing mascara. And if you're someone who rubs your eyes reflexively, this won't forgive you — the formula doesn't flake, but it also doesn't flex.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The Amazon reviews (220 ratings at 4.4/5) echo my experience. One reviewer put it simply: "I've worn this to funerals, weddings, and the gym. It's the only mascara I trust." Another noted the trade-off directly: "Takes forever to remove, but I've stopped caring because it never smudges."

The most common complaints cluster around removal — the same issue I hit. A reviewer warned: "Do not buy if you don't own a good eye makeup remover. This stuff is stubborn." Fair warning.

## Where It Falls Short

**Removal is genuinely difficult.** You need an oil-based remover, and even then, expect to spend 60 seconds per eye. This isn't a "wash and go" mascara.

**The formula dries out faster than standard versions.** The waterproof seal means the tube's lifespan is shorter — I noticed the formula getting tacky around the two-month mark.

**No lengthening to speak of.** This is a volume mascara. If you want dramatic length, layer it over a lash primer or look elsewhere.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:**
- You have oily eyelids or live somewhere humid
- You need mascara that survives workouts, swimming, or long days
- You prefer buildable volume over lengthening formulas
- You're okay investing in a quality remover

**Skip it if:**
- You hate the feeling of stiff lashes
- You want easy, no-fuss removal
- Your priority is length, not volume
- You're looking for a soft, feathery finish

## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually waterproof, or just water-resistant?**
A: Genuinely waterproof. I've worn it in a pool and through heavy rain. No smudging. The trade-off is that it's also resistant to your regular cleanser.

**Q: How does this compare to the non-waterproof version?**
A: The waterproof version gives slightly less volume but holds a curl better and lasts longer. It's also noticeably stiffer. If you don't specifically need waterproof wear, get the regular version.

**Q: Will this work for sensitive eyes?**
A: The formula is fragrance-free, which helps. But the difficulty of removal means more friction on the eye area. If you have sensitive eyes, be prepared to use a gentle, oil-based remover and be patient.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you need mascara that refuses to quit — whether that's for workouts, long shifts, or emotional events. It delivers on its core promise with zero compromise.

**Skip it if** you value easy removal or soft, flexible lashes more than bulletproof wear.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — It's not perfect, but for the price, this is one of the most reliable waterproof mascaras you can buy. The removal struggle is real, but so is the all-day confidence. Stock up on makeup remover and you're set.
