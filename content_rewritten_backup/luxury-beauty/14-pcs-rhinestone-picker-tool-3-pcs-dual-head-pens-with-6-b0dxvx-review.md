## What's Actually in the Box

The set breaks down to three full-size pens and six tip pairs. Each pen is dual-ended, meaning you get two working tips per pen — that's where the "14 pieces" math comes from: 3 pens × 2 ends + 6 additional tips + the case. The kit spans three tip categories:

- **Wax-tipped ends** — for flat-backed crystals and standard rhinestones
- **Metal claw ends** — for picking up beads with pre-drilled holes
- **Silicone suction ends** — for larger cabochons and smooth-backed gems

As shown in the product image, the pens are color-coded by tip type, which sounds minor until you're mid-manicure and grabbing the right tool without reading labels.

## Real-World Performance

I tested these on gel nail extensions, paper crafts, and a resin coaster project. The wax tips are the standout — they hold 3mm–5mm rhinestones with a satisfying grip that releases cleanly with a slight twist. No more sticky residue on your nail art. The silicone ends handle larger pieces better than anything else in this price range, and the suction actually holds a 10mm cabochon without dropping it mid-placement.

The metal claw tips are the trickiest. They work well for precision placement on flat surfaces, but they require a feather-light touch. One reviewer noted, "The metal tips scratched my practice nail when I pressed too hard — but once I got the hang of the pressure, they were perfect for tiny beads."

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has three honest gaps.

**Wax is consumable.** The included wax tips will last through roughly 15–20 moderate craft sessions. Replacement wax pencils aren't included, and you'll need to source them separately. It's a minor cost, but an annoyance when you run out mid-project.

**The case is basic.** It's a simple tin with a foam insert — no individual tip slots. Loose tips rattle around. You'll want to be careful opening it over a carpeted floor.

**Metal tips have a learning curve.** They're precision tools, not beginner-friendly. If you're new to rhinestone application, stick to the wax and silicone ends until you've built confidence.

## FAQ

**Q: Will the wax tips work with nail glue?**
Yes — the wax picks up the stone, you apply a dot of glue to your nail, then press the stone into place. The wax doesn't interfere with adhesion.

**Q: How do I clean the metal tips?**
Wipe them with rubbing alcohol between uses. Nail dust and dried glue build up quickly; a quick wipe keeps the grip precise.

**Q: Can I use these for Swarovski crystals?**
Absolutely. The silicone tips handle the larger crystal sizes (10mm–20mm) without scratching the facets, which is critical for high-end stones.
