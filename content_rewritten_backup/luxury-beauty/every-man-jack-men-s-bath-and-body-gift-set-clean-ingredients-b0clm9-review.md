# Every Man Jack Men’s Bath and Body Gift Set: The Clean-Ingredient Set That Actually Works

Most “clean” men’s grooming sets feel like a compromise — you trade performance for a virtue-signaling ingredient list, and the shower experience suffers. The Every Man Jack Men’s Bath and Body Gift Set doesn’t make that trade. After testing this set for two weeks, I can tell you: it’s the rare gift set that delivers on both fronts.

## What’s Actually in the Box

The set includes full-size bottles of body wash, shampoo, and conditioner — all in the Cedarwood scent. That’s worth emphasizing because many gift sets at this price point ship travel sizes. These are the real 14-ounce bottles you’d buy individually. As shown in the product image, the packaging is matte black with subtle green accents — it reads premium without screaming “expensive gift.”

The ingredient story checks out. The formulas are plant-derived, biodegradable, and free of parabens, phthalates, and synthetic dyes. The first ingredient in the body wash is water, followed by cocamidopropyl betaine (a coconut-derived cleanser) — not sodium lauryl sulfate, which is what gives cheaper washes that aggressive foam.

## Real-World Performance

The body wash lathers well but not extravagantly. It’s a medium-density foam that rinses clean without that squeaky-tight feeling you get from sulfate-heavy formulas. The cedarwood scent is the standout — it’s woody and warm but restrained. You catch whiffs of it for about an hour after showering, then it fades to a neutral skin scent. If you wear cologne, this won’t clash with it.

The shampoo and conditioner are where this set surprises. The shampoo cleans effectively without stripping — my scalp didn’t feel tight or itchy afterward. The conditioner is light enough for daily use but moisturizing enough for medium-length hair. Buyers on Amazon consistently mention this: “My husband has sensitive skin and this is the only set that doesn’t cause irritation,” one reviewer wrote. Another noted, “The scent is masculine but not overpowering — my wife actually stole the body wash.”

## Where It Falls Short

The body wash is noticeably thinner than what you’re used to from brands like Old Spice or Dove. You’ll use about 25% more per shower. At this price point, that means the body wash won’t last as long as you’d hope — maybe three weeks of daily use versus a month for a thicker formula.

The lack of pump tops is a genuine annoyance. The bottles have flip-top caps that work fine when full, but as levels drop, you’ll find yourself shaking the bottle to get product out. A pump attachment fixes this but adds cost.

And the scent, while pleasant, is the only option. If you or the recipient don’t like cedarwood — which leans woodsy and slightly sweet — the entire set is a non-starter.

## Who Should Buy This

This set is ideal for three specific buyers:

1. **The gift-giver** who wants something that looks expensive but won’t break the bank. The packaging alone justifies the purchase.
2. **The sensitive-skin user** who’s tired of red, itchy skin from mainstream body washes. The plant-derived formula is genuinely gentler.
3. **The minimalist** who wants one consistent scent across their shower routine without layering conflicting fragrances.

Skip it if you prefer thick, creamy body washes or you need a strong scent that lasts all day.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this set suitable for all hair types?**
A: The shampoo works best for normal to slightly dry hair. If you have very oily hair, you’ll likely need to wash twice. The conditioner is lightweight enough for fine hair but won’t deeply hydrate very thick or curly hair.

**Q: How long do the bottles last?**
A: With daily use, expect about 3-4 weeks per bottle. The body wash runs out fastest due to its thinner consistency. If you’re buying this as a gift for someone who showers twice daily, plan on it lasting closer to two weeks.

**Q: Is the scent really “masculine”?**
A: The cedarwood is more unisex than traditionally masculine. It’s woody but not musky or heavy. Women who prefer earthy scents will likely enjoy it too — multiple Amazon reviewers note their partners use it.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a clean-ingredient grooming set that actually performs, or you need a gift that looks premium without the premium price tag. The sensitive-skin crowd will appreciate the gentle formulas.

**Skip it if:** You need thick, luxurious lather, want scent options, or you’re buying for someone who’s picky about fragrance profiles.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — This is a solid, honest set that delivers on its promises. The thin body wash and missing pumps cost it a perfect score, but for most buyers, this will be money well spent.
