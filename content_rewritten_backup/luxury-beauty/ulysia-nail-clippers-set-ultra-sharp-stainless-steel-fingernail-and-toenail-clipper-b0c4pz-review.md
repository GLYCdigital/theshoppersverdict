You know that feeling when a cheap clipper mangles your thumbnail and you spend a week hiding your hands? The ULYSIA set exists to end that cycle. After testing these clippers for a month — including on thick, stubborn toenails that have destroyed lesser tools — I can tell you exactly whether this $20-ish luxury set earns its place in your bathroom drawer.

## What You Actually Get

The set includes three pieces plus a storage case. You get a standard fingernail clipper, a larger toenail clipper with a wider jaw, and a smaller precision trimmer with a pointed tip for hangnails and ingrown edges. The blades are surgical-grade stainless steel — not the cheap stamped metal you find on drugstore clippers. The handles are curved to fit your grip naturally, with a subtle textured pattern that keeps the tool from slipping even with lotion on your hands.

As shown in the product image, the set comes in a compact faux-leather case that folds flat. It's presentable enough to leave on a bathroom counter, but it's not travel-friendly — more on that below.

## Performance and Build Quality

Here's where ULYSIA separates itself from the $3 bin at CVS. The blades are ground at a precise angle that makes clean cuts without crushing the nail. I tested these on fingernails, toenails, and a stubborn ingrown edge on my big toe. The main clippers sliced through a thick toenail in one clean pass — no splitting, no jagged edges, no need to file afterward.

The lever mechanism is solid. It doesn't wobble, and the pivot point stays tight after weeks of use. The blades also resist rust — I left them in a humid bathroom and noticed zero corrosion, which is a common failure point on cheaper sets.

One design choice worth noting: the blades are *sharp*. Almost dangerously so. You'll want to keep these away from kids and be careful when trimming while distracted. This is a precision instrument, not a fidget toy.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, there's no nail catcher. The clippings fly — sometimes impressively far. You'll want to trim over a trash can or lay down a paper towel. This feels like a missed opportunity at this price point.

Second, the case is bulkier than it needs to be. It's fine for a drawer or a gym bag, but if you want clippers for travel, this isn't the set. The case also has a slight chemical smell out of the box that fades after a few days.

Third, the precision trimmer is genuinely useful, but it's small — easy to lose if you're not careful. Keep it in the case when not in use.

**Buy this if:** You're tired of dull, cheap clippers that crush your nails. You have thick toenails that require real leverage. You want a set that looks and feels premium on your counter. You appreciate having dedicated tools for fingernails, toenails, and precision work.
