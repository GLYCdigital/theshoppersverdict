**The Review**

In the world of prestige hair care, Davines is a name that carries weight. The MOMO line, specifically the Hydration And Detangling Conditioner, promises a dual-action punch: quenching thirsty strands while making the comb-out process painless. But at this price point, you deserve more than promises. Does it deliver, or is it just pretty packaging from a salon brand? I put it to the test.

**What You Actually Get**

This isn't a basic rinse-out. The formula is built around babassu oil, a lightweight yet deeply penetrating emollient that's been a staple in Brazilian hair rituals. Davines boasts that 98% of the ingredients are naturally derived, which is a significant pull for those trying to avoid the harsh sulfates and parabens found in cheaper options. The texture is a rich, creamy white that feels substantial in the hand, and the scent—a fresh, botanical mix of floral and green notes—is immediately recognizable as "professional grade."

**The Real-World Performance**

Here’s where the rubber meets the road. The defining feature of this conditioner is the "slip." You don't need a ton of it—a quarter-sized amount is enough for medium-length hair. It glides on and instantly feels like it's coating the cuticle. After three minutes, the detangling process is noticeably easier; I found that a wide-tooth comb went through my hair with zero resistance, even after air-drying.

The hydration aspect is balanced rather than heavy. My hair felt soft and pliable, but it didn't have that greasy or weighed-down look that often follows "moisturizing" formulas. This makes it an exceptional choice for those with fine-to-medium hair that gets easily flattened by heavy butters. It leaves a subtle, glossy finish that looks healthy without looking wet.

**What Buyers Are Saying**

With a 4.0-star average from 200 ratings, the feedback is overwhelmingly positive, though not without nuance. One reviewer noted, *"I have color-treated hair that tangles like a nightmare, and this is the only thing that lets me get a brush through it without ripping. The smell is incredible."* Another echoed the sentiment on texture, saying, *"It makes my hair feel like silk, but I can still style it without it falling flat."*

**Where It Falls Short**

I have to be honest about the shortcomings. For those with thick, coarse, or curly hair, this might not be the heavy hitter you need. The lightweight nature of babassu oil means it provides surface-level slip and light moisture, but it may not penetrate deeply enough to soften thick strands; you’ll likely need a mask or leave-in conditioner to follow up.

Also, the price is a sticking point. When you consider how quickly you can blow through conditioner, this is a "treat yourself" purchase, not a daily driver for everyone. The pump bottle is excellent for dispensing (no wasted product), but it’s a luxury line, and the price tag reflects that positioning.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The Fine-Hair Sufferer:** If your hair gets greasy at the roots but is dry at the ends, this is your sweet spot.
- **The Tangle-Prone:** If you dread the post-shower comb-out, the slip this provides is a game-changer.
- **The Ingredient-Conscious:** If you're transitioning away from silicones and sulfates, this is a clean-enough option that doesn't sacrifice performance.

**Skip It If:**

- You have extremely thick, coily hair that requires heavy butters.
- You're on a strict budget and prefer a $5 conditioner that does the bare minimum.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this conditioner safe for color-treated hair?**
A: Yes. Because it is free of sulfates and heavy salts, it won't strip color. In fact, the babassu oil helps protect the hair shaft, keeping color vibrant longer.

**Q: What does "Formula F" mean?**
A: This indicates it is the "Fine" hair variant. Davines uses this designation to signal that the formulation is lighter and won't weigh down fine or thin hair types, unlike their "R" (Rich) variants.

**Q: Does it contain silicone?**
A: No. The brand avoids silicones in this line, using natural oils instead to create that smooth, detangled effect.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a high-performance detangler that hydrates without sacrificing volume and you're willing to pay for the clean, salon-grade ingredients.

**Skip it if** you have heavy, dry hair that needs intense butter-based masks, or if the premium cost is a dealbreaker.

For a luxury product, the Davines MOMO Conditioner earns its 4.2/5 score. It solves the specific problem of tangling with brilliant efficiency, but its lightweight nature means it isn't the all-in-one moisture solution for every hair type.
