## What You Actually Get

Each pack contains pre-stretched, pre-braided jumbo goddess braids measuring roughly 0.5 inches wide. The 10-inch length is the selling point here — it hits that sweet spot between bob-length and shoulder-grazing without the weight of longer braids pulling at your edges. Each pack weighs about 3.5 ounces, and most reviewers report needing 6-8 packs for a full install depending on your head circumference and desired fullness.

The pre-braided ends are the real feature. You're not spending 20 minutes curling or dipping each braid tip — they arrive finished and ready to install. As one verified buyer put it: *"First time doing my own braids and the pre-done ends made it look like I went to a salon. No burnt ends, no frizz, just done."*

## Performance and Real-World Wear

The goddess curl pattern is the standout. The wavy texture at the roots transitions into defined curls that hold their shape remarkably well. Multiple reviewers noted the braids still looked fresh at the two-week mark, which is better than most budget crochet hair in this price range. The kanekalon blend is soft — not scratchy against the neck — and the pre-stretched nature means less bulk at the root, which translates to less tension on your edges.

Install time averages 3-4 hours for DIY users, which is faster than traditional box braids because the crochet method eliminates individual braiding. The jumbo width means fewer braids to install overall, and the hair feeds through the latch hook smoothly without excessive shedding.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides. The most consistent complaint across reviews is the color discrepancy — what looks like a rich honey brown in the product photos pulls noticeably darker in natural light. One reviewer warned: *"If you're between shades, size down. I ordered chocolate brown and it's basically black unless I'm standing in direct sunlight."*

The length is also slightly off. Several buyers measured the installed braids at 8.5 to 9 inches rather than the advertised 10. It's not a dealbreaker, but if you're specifically buying this length to hit a certain point on your body, factor in the shrinkage.

That initial chemical smell — typical of synthetic hair — lingers for about a day. A vinegar rinse or leaving the packs open for 24 hours before install helps significantly.

## FAQ

**How many packs do I need?**
Most buyers use 7-8 packs for a full head. If you have a smaller head or want less fullness, 6 packs will work.

**Does it shed a lot?**
Minimal shedding during install — maybe a few strands per braid. After the first wash, expect normal synthetic hair shedding, nothing excessive.

**Can I style it with heat?**
Yes, but keep it low. The synthetic fibers will melt at high temperatures. Use a low-heat setting or steam to refresh curls.
