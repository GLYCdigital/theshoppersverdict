## The Nude Pink That Finally Looks Nude

Finding a "nude" polish that doesn't pull too pink, too orange, or too gray is a genuine quest. The RARJSM Nude Pink Gel Polish solves that. It's the shade that makes people ask "what's on your nails?" because it looks like your nails, but better.

This is a jelly formula — sheer by design, meant to be built in thin layers. The glitter is fine-milled, suspended throughout the polish rather than dumped on top. In the bottle, it reads as a soft, pearlescent shimmer. On the nail, it catches light without screaming for attention.

## What You Actually Get

One 10ml bottle of gel polish. That's it. No base coat, no top coat, no lamp. If you're new to gel, factor in those costs — a decent LED lamp runs $25-40, and you'll need a base and top coat to make this last.

The brush is worth mentioning: it's flat with rounded edges, which makes getting clean cuticles significantly easier than with the narrow brushes many budget polishes ship with. The bristles lay down even, streak-free color — assuming you don't overload the brush.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this with a full gel routine: light buff, dehydrator, base coat, two coats of RARJSM, top coat, cured under a 48W LED lamp. Wear time hit 14 days with only minor tip wear — no chipping, no lifting at the cuticle line.

The formula self-levels well. You have maybe 10-15 seconds of working time before it starts to set, which is standard for gel. The first coat looks patchy and uneven — that's normal. The second coat is where the magic happens, and a third thin coat gets you to that fully opaque-but-still-sheer look.

One thing to watch: the glitter settles. Hard. If you skip the shake, you'll get a glitter-heavy first dip and a nearly clear second coat. Shake it for a full 30 seconds before every use.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

Across the 225 Amazon ratings, the consistent praise centers on the color. One reviewer put it plainly: *"Finally a nude that doesn't make my hands look washed out."* Another noted, *"I've bought this three times. It's the only gel polish I've repurchased."*

The complaints are equally consistent: the sheer formula demands patience, and if you rush it, you'll see streaks. A few buyers also mentioned the glitter clumping if the bottle wasn't shaken thoroughly.

## Where It Falls Short

This is not a one-coat polish. If you want quick, slap-it-on-and-go color, this will frustrate you. It needs three thin coats to reach the advertised look, and each coat needs proper curing time.

The glitter, while beautiful, is a commitment. Removal requires the standard gel soak-off — foil wraps or clips for 10-15 minutes. You cannot peel this off without damaging your nail plate, and the fine glitter makes acetone soak slightly more tedious than solid colors.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The gel enthusiasts** who already own a lamp and base/top coats and just want a reliable neutral

## FAQ

**How many coats do I need?**
Two coats give a sheer wash; three coats reach the full jelly look shown in the product images. Let each coat cure fully before adding the next.

**Does this work with regular nail polish?**
No. This is a UV/LED-cured gel polish. It requires a lamp and won't dry on its own.

**How long does it actually last?**
With proper prep and a quality top coat, expect 2-3 weeks. I got 14 days before tip wear became noticeable.
