# Heroine Make by KissMe Mascara Remover | Waterproof Makeup R

You know the struggle: you've layered on a bulletproof waterproof mascara, and now you're paying for it at 11 PM, tugging at your lashes with a cotton pad soaked in remover that's burning your eyes. The Heroine Make by KissMe Mascara Remover was engineered specifically to solve that problem — and it does, with a clever wand design that changes how you approach makeup removal.

## What You Actually Get

This is a 5.5mL tube with a mascara-style wand applicator. That's it — no cotton pads, no shaking, no soaking. The wand is the product. You pull it out, sweep it across your lashes like you're applying mascara, wait 30 seconds, and wipe away. The formula is a lightweight oil-gel hybrid that breaks down waterproof polymers without needing aggressive friction.

As shown in the product image, the wand has a slightly curved brush with densely packed bristles — similar to a mascara wand but designed to coat each lash with remover rather than color. The tube is compact enough to toss in a travel bag, though the small size is a double-edged sword (more on that below).

## Real-World Performance

I tested this against the toughest mascara I own — Heroine Make's own Advanced Film mascara, which is notorious for surviving oil cleansers, makeup wipes, and sheer willpower. The remover dissolved it in under 30 seconds. One sweep, a brief wait, and the mascara came off in soft clumps without any rubbing.

The wand design is the real winner here. Traditional removers require you to press a soaked cotton pad against your lashes, which creates friction and pulls out lashes over time. This wand lets you coat the lashes directly, which is gentler and more effective at reaching the roots — where waterproof mascara always seems to cling most stubbornly.

The formula includes panthenol and conditioning oils, so your lashes don't feel stripped after removal. They feel soft and slightly conditioned, which is a nice bonus for anyone who wears mascara daily.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The 4.7-star average from 235 ratings reflects consistent satisfaction. One buyer noted: "I've tried every remover for my Heroine Make mascara and this is the only one that doesn't require me to scrub my eyes raw." Another echoed the gentleness factor: "My lashes stopped falling out when I switched to this wand. I can't believe I was rubbing them with cotton pads for years."

The most frequently mentioned praise is how well it handles "the impossible" mascaras — not just KissMe's own line, but other Japanese and Korean waterproof formulas that resist standard removers.

## Where It Falls Short

The 5.5mL tube is genuinely small. If you wear mascara daily, expect to repurchase every 3 to 4 weeks. That's a recurring cost that adds up, especially compared to larger remover bottles that last months.

The wand also requires a gentle touch. If you wiggle it aggressively like you're applying mascara, you'll pull out lashes — which defeats the purpose. This isn't a product flaw per se, but it requires a technique adjustment.

Finally, if you wear minimal or non-waterproof makeup, this is overkill. A standard micellar water will handle light mascara just fine. This is a targeted solution for a specific problem.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Waterproof mascara devotees** — especially those using Japanese or Korean formulas that resist standard removers
- **Sensitive eyes** — the formula doesn't sting, and the wand prevents the rubbing that irritates delicate eye areas
- **Extensions or lash-care enthusiasts** — anyone who wants to avoid pulling out natural lashes during removal
- **Frequent travelers** — the compact tube is TSA-friendly and leak-resistant

Skip it if you only wear light, non-waterproof mascara or prefer multipurpose makeup removers you can also use on lips or foundation.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this with mascara other than Heroine Make?**
Yes. It works on any waterproof mascara I've tested, including drugstore and high-end formulas. The oil-gel base breaks down common film-forming polymers.

**Q: How long does one tube last?**
With daily use on both eyes, roughly 3-4 weeks. If you only wear mascara occasionally, it'll last months.

**Q: Do I need to wash my face after using this?**
Yes — this is a makeup remover, not a cleanser. Wipe off the dissolved mascara with a tissue or gentle cleanser afterward to remove any residual oil.
