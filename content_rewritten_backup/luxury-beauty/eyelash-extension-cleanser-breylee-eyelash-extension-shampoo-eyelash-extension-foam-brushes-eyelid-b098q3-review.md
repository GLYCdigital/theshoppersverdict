If you've dropped $150 on a full set of volume lashes, the last thing you want is for them to fall out in week two because you're scared to wash them. That's the exact problem BREYLEE's Eyelash Extension Cleanser solves — and it does it with a 4.5-star average across 225 Amazon ratings.

## What You Actually Get

The kit arrives with a 3.4 fl oz pump bottle of the cleanser plus a reusable dual-ended brush. One end has dense, fine bristles for scrubbing the lash line; the other is a soft silicone tip for more delicate areas. The product itself is a clear, lightly-scented gel that foams up when worked between your fingers. As shown in the product image, the packaging is clean, minimal, and looks intentional on a bathroom shelf — not like a clinical afterthought.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The formula is oil-free, which matters more than most people realize. Oil breaks down the cyanoacrylate adhesive that holds extensions on. BREYLEE's cleanser uses a surfactant blend that lifts makeup, sunscreen, and the day's grime without attacking the bond.

Here's how it actually works in practice: wet the brush, add a drop of the gel, and work it into a lather. Brush along the lash line in small circles — not the lashes themselves — then rinse with a gentle splash of water. You don't need to scrub hard. The foam does the work. After drying, your lashes look clean, separated, and feathery. No filmy residue, no chalky white cast.

One reviewer put it plainly: *"I was terrified to wash my lashes at all. This made it so easy — no stinging, and my lashes are lasting a full week longer than before."* That sentiment — extensions lasting longer because the base stays clean — shows up repeatedly in the feedback. Another buyer noted: *"I've tried the lash-shampoo bars and foams from salons. This is better, and half the price."*

## Where It Falls Short

It's not perfect. The 3.4 oz bottle is small. If you're cleansing daily, expect to reorder every month or so. That's manageable, but the price-per-ounce isn't a bargain — you're paying for a specialized, gentle formula, not bulk.

Also, the foam pump can be finicky. Pump it too fast and it sputters out watery product instead of a clean dollop. It's a minor annoyance, not a dealbreaker, but you'll learn to press slowly.

A few reviewers with sensitive eyes reported mild irritation if the product got directly into the eye. It's not a burn — more of a brief, soapy sensation. Keep your eyes closed when rinsing and you'll avoid it entirely.

## Who Should Buy This

This is for you if:

- **You get professional lash extensions regularly** and want them to last the full 3–4 weeks between fills.
- **You're prone to eye irritation** from makeup and want a gentle, non-stinging cleaner.
- **You like precise application tools** — the included brush makes this feel like a salon-grade product.

Skip it if you have **no extensions at all** (your regular facial cleanser works fine) or if you're on a tight budget and don't mind using a generic baby shampoo diluted with water.

## FAQ

**How often should I use this cleanser?**
Daily, if you wear eye makeup and sunscreen. Every other day is the minimum if you want to prevent adhesive breakdown from oil buildup. Skipping more than two days starts to compromise the bond.

**Will this work on lash lifts or tinted brows?**
Yes, but it's designed for extensions. For lash lifts, you don't need this level of gentleness — any oil-free cleanser works. For brows, it'll clean but won't do much else.

**Is it safe for sensitive eyes?**
Mostly. The formula is pH-balanced and fragrance-free. But keep eyes closed while rinsing. If you have chronic dry eye or allergies, patch-test on your inner arm first.
