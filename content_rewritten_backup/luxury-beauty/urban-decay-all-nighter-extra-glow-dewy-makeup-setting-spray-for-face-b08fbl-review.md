# Urban Decay All Nighter Extra Glow Dewy Makeup Setting Spray Review

You know the struggle: you've spent 20 minutes on your base, and by lunch it's sliding off your face. The original All Nighter has been the gold standard for makeup longevity for years. But what if you want your makeup to *stay* — and also look like you just came from a facial? That's where the Extra Glow version comes in. It's the same lock-down formula, but with a dewy finish that makes your skin look hydrated and alive, not flat and powdery.

## What's in the Bottle

You get the classic 4-ounce (118 mL) spray bottle with the fine-mist pump mechanism Urban Decay is known for. The product itself is a lightweight, non-oily liquid that feels almost like water when sprayed. The pump delivers a consistent, micro-fine mist — no giant droplets that leave your face looking like a wet mess. It dries down in about 30-45 seconds, which is faster than many competitors I've tested.

## Performance and Real-World Testing

I tested this over a full day — 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. — with a full coverage foundation, cream blush, and powder highlight. The verdict? My makeup didn't budge. No creasing around the nose, no patchiness on the chin, and the blush stayed where I put it. The "extra glow" is real but subtle: it's a luminous sheen, not a shimmer bomb. You can layer it with a matte foundation and still get that lit-from-within look without looking oily.

Where it shines (pun intended) is on normal to dry skin. If you have combination or oily skin, you'll want to be strategic — the glow can amplify mid-day shine on a T-zone if you skip powder. One reviewer noted, "I have dry skin and this is the only setting spray that doesn't make me look cakey by hour 5." Another said, "It kept my makeup on through a humid outdoor wedding — I was shocked."

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews is earned. The most common praise points:

- **The finish:** "It gives the perfect dewy glow without looking greasy. My skin looks like glass."
- **Longevity:** Many mention 10-12 hour wear with zero touch-ups needed.
- **Mist quality:** "The sprayer is the best I've used — it's so fine, it feels like a refreshing facial mist."

## Where It Falls Short

Honest talk: this isn't perfect for everyone. If you have dry, flaky patches, the dewy finish can cling to them and make them more noticeable — you *must* moisturize well before using. The fragrance is also present. It's pleasant (a light floral, clean scent), but if you're sensitive to fragrance or spraying anything scented near your eyes, this could be a dealbreaker. And while the 4-ounce bottle is standard, you'll go through it quickly if you use the recommended 4-6 sprays per application — budget for a repurchase every 6-8 weeks with daily use.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Dry or normal skin types** wanting a healthy, hydrated finish
- **Anyone with textured or mature skin** — the glow blurs the look of fine lines better than matte sprays
- **Event-goers** who need makeup to survive from morning through late night

Skip it if you have very oily skin and hate any hint of shine, or if you're sensitive to scented cosmetics.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this actually keep makeup on all day, or is it just a glow spray?**  
A: It does both. The same polymer technology from the original All Nighter is in here — it forms a flexible film over your makeup that resists sweat, humidity, and friction. You get the lock-down *and* the glow.

**Q: Can I use this over powder or only on bare skin?**  
A: Both. You can spray it over your whole finished face — powder, blush, everything. It actually helps melt powder into the skin so you lose that "dusty" look. You can also use it as a hydrating prep spray before makeup, though you'll get better longevity if you use it as the final step.

**Q: How does the glow compare to the original All Nighter?**  
A: The original dries completely matte. This one leaves a visible luminous sheen that lasts. If you want the same staying power but a skin-like, hydrated finish, Extra Glow is the upgrade. If you're oily, stick with the original.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want your makeup to survive a 12-hour day *and* look like you have naturally glowing, well-moisturized skin. It's a premium product, but it delivers on both promises — many budget sprays nail one or the other, rarely both.

**Skip it if** you're strictly matte, have very oily skin, or prefer zero fragrance in your beauty routine.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — One of the best dewy setting sprays on the market, held back only by fragrance sensitivity and dry-patch amplification for certain skin types. For most people, this is a holy grail.
