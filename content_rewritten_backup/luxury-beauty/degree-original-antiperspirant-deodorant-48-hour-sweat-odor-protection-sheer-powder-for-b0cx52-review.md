Let's be real about antiperspirant: most 48-hour claims are generous at best. So when Degree's Original formula with "MotionSENSE" technology hit my desk, I was skeptical. After three weeks of daily wear, gym sessions, and one genuinely stressful travel day, here's the honest breakdown.

**What you actually get**

The product is a standard 2.7-ounce twist-up stick. The Sheer Powder variant goes on white but dries completely invisible — that's rare. The active ingredient is aluminum zirconium tetrachlorohydrex glycine at 15%, which is right in the effective range for over-the-counter antiperspirants. No prescription needed.

**How it actually performs**

Here's where Degree differentiates itself. The MotionSENSE technology uses microcapsules that release fresh fragrance and active ingredients when you move. It's not gimmicky — I noticed that after a run, there was a subtle burst of scent and my pits stayed dry while my forehead was dripping. The 48-hour claim? In normal conditions, yes. Through a full day of meetings plus a workout, I stayed dry for roughly 30 hours. Pushing to 48 hours without reapplying left me feeling damp by hour 40, but odor remained neutral.

The Sheer Powder finish is the standout feature. As someone who wears dark dress shirts, I've ruined too many with chalky white marks. This one leaves nothing. No roll-on wetness, no flaking, no buildup on fabric.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Scrolling through the 229 ratings, one theme dominates: the invisible finish. One reviewer wrote, "Finally a white stick that doesn't show on my black scrubs. Been using for two weeks and no residue at all." Another noted, "I was skeptical about the 48-hour thing but it genuinely lasted through a 14-hour hospital shift." Multiple customers mention the scent as a plus — a clean, classic powder that doesn't clash with perfume or cologne.

**Where it falls short**

The fragrance is the main complaint. It's not subtle — the powder scent is strong on application and lingers for hours. If you're sensitive to fragrance or prefer unscented products, this isn't your match. Also worth noting: this isn't clinical strength. If you have hyperhidrosis or sweat through normal antiperspirants, this won't be the miracle you're looking for. A few reviewers mentioned needing to reapply on exceptionally hot days despite the 48-hour label.

The stick can also feel slightly waxy during application — it needs a couple of swipes to distribute evenly, especially right after a shower when skin is damp.

**Who should buy this**

- **The professional**: Anyone wearing dress shirts, scrubs, or uniforms who needs invisible protection that survives long shifts
- **The moderately active**: Gym-goers who sweat but don't have clinical hyperhidrosis
- **The fragrance-conscious**: People who want a clean, classic scent that won't clash with their usual fragrance routine

**Skip it if** you need clinical-strength protection, prefer completely unscented products, or have sensitive skin that reacts to fragrance-heavy formulas.

**FAQ**

*Q: Does it really last 48 hours?*
A: For most users, yes — but with caveats. Normal daily activity, yes. Intense exercise or extreme heat will shorten it to roughly 24-30 hours. The 48-hour claim is best for sleep-and-shower cycles, not consecutive active days.

*Q: Will it stain my clothes?*
A: No. The sheer powder formula dries clear and doesn't transfer to fabric. This is its biggest selling point and it holds up.

*Q: How does MotionSENSE actually work?*
A: Microcapsules in the formula break open when you move, releasing fresh fragrance and active ingredients on demand. It's not just marketing — you can feel a subtle cooling sensation when you start sweating.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want reliable all-day protection that won't ruin your shirts and you don't mind a classic powder fragrance.

**Skip it if** you need clinical strength or prefer fragrance-free formulas.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The best invisible-finish antiperspirant I've tested this year, held back only by the strong scent and the 48-hour claim being more like 30 in real-world conditions. At this price point, it outperforms comparable drugstore options from Dove and Secret.
