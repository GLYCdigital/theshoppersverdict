**Beauty of Joseon Daily Tinted Fluid Sunscreen 12 Shades for Face Moisturizing — In-Depth Review**

---

**The Bottom Line Up Front**

Korean skincare giant Beauty of Joseon has finally entered the tinted sunscreen arena with a 12-shade lineup—a bold move for a K-beauty brand, most of which still offer only 2-3 shades. The Daily Tinted Fluid promises broad-spectrum SPF 40 protection with a sheer, skin-like finish. But does it live up to the hype, or is it another case of "Asian sunscreen that doesn't play well with deeper skin tones"? Here's the honest breakdown.

---

**What You're Actually Getting**

The box contains a single 40ml tube (about 1.35 fl oz) of fluid sunscreen. That's standard for Korean sunscreens, but be aware—it's roughly half the size of a typical American sunscreen tube. The fluid texture is thin, almost serum-like, and dispenses easily from the small nozzle.

The 12-shade range is the headline feature here, spanning from very fair (shade names like "Light," "Fair") to deep ("Deep," "Rich"). That's a genuine milestone for a K-beauty brand. The formula uses chemical filters (Uvinul A Plus, Tinosorb S) alongside niacinamide and a small dose of hyaluronic acid for that "daily moisturizing" claim.

---

**Performance and Real-World Wear**

I tested this over two weeks, both alone and under makeup. The fluid applies best with fingers—a brush or sponge soaks up too much product. It dries down to a natural, satin finish rather than matte or dewy, which is a sweet spot for most skin types. The coverage is sheer-to-light; it evens out redness but won't hide blemishes. Think "my skin but better" rather than foundation.

Wear time is solid: 6-7 hours before noticeable fading on oily areas. It doesn't pill under makeup as long as you let it set for 60-90 seconds first. On dry skin, it's genuinely comfortable. On oily skin, you'll want powder on the T-zone by midday.

---

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The shade range is the runaway winner in customer feedback. One verified reviewer noted, *"I've been waiting for a Korean brand to finally get shades right. I'm shade 'Tan' and it's not orange or ashy—it actually blends."* Multiple reviewers echo this: the undertones are well-calibrated, avoiding the dreaded gray cast deeper skin often gets from tinted sunscreens.

The texture also earns frequent praise. *"It feels like a light moisturizer, not a sunscreen,"* wrote one buyer. The absence of white cast—even on deeper skin tones—is a recurring theme.

---

**Where It Falls Short (Honest Talk)**

At 4.0 stars with 200 ratings, this isn't a flawless product. The most consistent complaint? Shade matching. Even with 12 options, the jumps between shades are uneven—there's a noticeable gap between "Medium" and "Tan" that leaves some users without a true match.

The 40ml size is also a practical issue. At this price point, you're paying premium for a product that daily users will burn through in 4-6 weeks. Compare that to a standard 50ml American sunscreen at similar cost. The SPF 40 rating (not 50+) also feels slightly conservative for the luxury price tag.

Some users with sensitive skin reported mild stinging around the eyes—the chemical filters can be irritating if you're prone to sensitivity. Patch test if you have reactive skin.

---

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Shade-starved skincare fans**: If you've been locked out of K-beauty because of limited shade ranges, this is your entry point.
- **Minimalist makeup wearers**: If you want SPF + light coverage in one step, this replaces foundation on low-effort days.
- **Dry-to-normal skin**: The moisturizing finish is genuinely comfortable.

**Skip it if**: You have oily skin and want all-day matte finish, you need medium-or-higher coverage, or you're on a budget and want more product per dollar.

---

**FAQs From Actual Shoppers**

**Q: Is this a sunscreen or a foundation?**
A: It's a sunscreen first. The tint provides sheer, buildable coverage (think tinted moisturizer level), not foundation-level coverage. If you need to conceal blemishes, you'll still need concealer.

**Q: How do the 12 shades compare to MAC or Fenty?**
A: Roughly: Light = MAC NC10-15, Fair = NC20-25, Medium = NC30-35, Tan = NC40-42, Deep = NC45-50. The undertones lean neutral-to-warm. If you're between shades, buy the lighter one—the fluid sheerness means it blends more forgivingly than a foundation.

**Q: Can I use this under makeup?**
A: Yes, but wait 2-3 minutes for it to fully set. Apply it as your moisturizer-sunscreen step, then foundation. It layers well with both water- and silicone-based formulas.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if**: You want a genuinely inclusive K-beauty sunscreen with a natural finish, you're tired of white cast, or you want a 2-in-1 SPF + light coverage product for daily wear.

**Skip it if**: You need full coverage, have oily skin that demands matte, or you're sensitive to chemical sunscreen filters.

The price-per-ounce stings, and shade matching has gaps, but for the right user, this is a daily staple that finally makes "tinted Korean sunscreen" accessible across the spectrum.
