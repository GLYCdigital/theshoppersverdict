**The drugstore concealer that thinks it's luxury — and mostly pulls it off**

Let's be honest: most crayon concealers are gimmicks. They promise precision, deliver chalky streaks, and end up in a drawer by week two. The L'Oreal Paris True Match Super Blendable Crayon Concealer breaks that pattern — but it's not without quirks. Here's what 229 Amazon reviewers at 4.6 stars are actually saying, and what they're not.

**What's in the stick**

You get a single 0.05 oz crayon-style concealer with a precision tip that mimics a retractable lipstick mechanism. The twist-up design exposes about a centimeter of product — enough for targeted application without the mess of a wand. There's no sponge applicator, no doe-foot, no blending tool. You apply directly from the stick and work it in with a finger, brush, or sponge.

The formula is described as "super blendable" — and it mostly lives up to that. It's a creamy balm that warms up on contact with skin, moving from a slightly stiff texture to a workable cream within seconds. The finish leans natural: medium coverage that can be built to full with a second pass, but never looks mask-like if you blend properly.

**Real-world performance**

Here's where this concealer shines: precision. The narrow tip lets you place product exactly where you need it — a blemish, a red spot under your nostril, a shadow at the inner corner of your eye. Reviewers consistently praise this. One buyer wrote: *"The tip is everything. I can dot it on a spot without covering my whole face in product."*

The True Match shade system is the other standout. L'Oreal numbers shades by undertone — warm (W), neutral (N), cool (C) — with numbered depth levels. If you already know your True Match foundation shade, the concealer matches perfectly. That's a genuine advantage over brands that just label shades "light," "medium," and "dark" without nuance.

Wear time sits around 6-8 hours before noticeable creasing under the eyes. Set it with powder and you'll push that to 9-10 hours on normal-to-combination skin. It doesn't oxidize — a common complaint with drugstore concealers — and it plays well over both water-based and silicone-based foundations.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Beyond the precision tip, reviewers highlight the natural finish. One wrote: *"It looks like skin, not makeup. My coworkers thought I just slept well."* Another noted: *"I've tried $30 concealers that perform worse than this $10 one."* The blendability claim holds up — it works with fingers, brushes, and sponges without streaking or grabbing at dry patches.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest complaint: dryness. On mature or dry skin, the crayon formula can drag and skip before it warms up. Reviewers with dry under-eyes report patchiness unless they prep with moisturizer or eye cream. This isn't a "grab and go" product if you have flaky skin — you need to prep.

The shade range is also limited. Twelve shades cover fair through tan/deep, but the darkest few options lean red rather than golden, and there's no true deep-ebony shade. If you're in the deeper half of the spectrum, check swatches carefully before buying.

And the twist-up mechanism only goes one way. Twist too much and you can't retract — you'll be wiping excess product off the tip. It's wasteful and annoying, but not a dealbreaker.

**Who should buy this**

- **The precision user**: Someone who wants to spot-conceal without foundation across their whole face
- **The undertone nerd**: If you've already matched a True Match foundation, this is a no-brainer pairing
- **The minimalist**: One product that handles blemishes, dark circles, and redness — no need for three different concealers

**Skip it if** you have very dry skin without a solid prep routine, or if you need deep shades beyond what the 12-option range offers.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this crease under the eyes?**
A: On most skin, it creases after 6-7 hours without powder. With a light dusting of translucent powder, you'll get 9+ hours. On oily skin, creasing starts around hour 5 — set it.

**Q: Is this a dupe for NARS Radiant Creamy Concealer?**
A: Not exactly. The finish is more natural/matte than NARS' radiant glow. It's closer to a MAC Pro Longwear in texture, but at a third of the price.

**Q: How do I find my shade?**
A: Use L'Oreal's True Match shade finder online. If you wear a foundation in the same line, match the number. If not, check undertone: W for warm/yellow, N for neutral, C for cool/pink.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if**: You want precise, buildable coverage with a natural finish and already know your undertone. At this price point, it outperforms concealers costing three times as much.

**Skip it if**: Your skin runs dry, you need deeper shades than what's offered, or you prefer a dewy radiant finish.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A precision tool that delivers on its promises, with minor flaws that won't stop most people from repurchasing.
