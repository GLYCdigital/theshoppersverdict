The Smashbox X BECCA collaboration resurrects a cult favorite, and honestly, it was worth bringing back. When BECCA shuttered, fans mourned the Shimmering Skin Perfector. Smashbox stepped in, and this pressed highlighter carries the torch with the same silky, whipped-powder texture that made the original legendary. As shown in the product image, the compact looks sleek and professional — but the real story is what happens when you swipe a brush across the pan.

## What You Actually Get

The compact holds 0.25 ounces of pressed powder — a decent amount that will last months with daily use. The packaging is sturdy, with a satisfying click-close lid and a clear window so you can see the shade without opening it. There's no mirror inside, which is a minor miss for touch-ups on the go, and no brush or sponge applicator. You're paying for the product, not the extras.

## Performance and Real-World Wear

This is where the Shimmering Skin Perfector earns its keep. The powder is finely milled to the point it feels almost creamy when you dip a brush in — no chalky kick-up, no fallout dusting your cheeks before application. It applies smoothly over both bare skin and foundation, blending out in about ten seconds with a damp sponge or a fluffy brush.

The finish is intense. This is not a subtle "lit from within" glow — it's a full-beam highlight that catches light from across the room. One light sweep delivers a noticeable sheen; two layers border on editorial. If you prefer a natural look, use a fan brush and tap off the excess. If you want to be seen from space, go to town.

Wear time is genuinely impressive. I tested it through an eight-hour workday plus a gym session afterward — the glow faded maybe 20 percent but never migrated, settled into pores, or turned patchy. One customer review put it simply: "This is the only highlighter that doesn't make my dry skin look worse. It melts right in." That tracks with my experience — the creamy formula doesn't emphasize texture the way drier pressed powders often do.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews holds up. The most common thread: the shade. The champagne-gold tone with pink undertones flatters fair to medium-deep skin without turning orange or gray. As one verified buyer wrote, "I've tried every highlighter from drugstore to high-end, and this is the one that finally looks like my skin but better." Others consistently mention the lack of glitter — this is a metallic sheen, not chunky shimmer particles, which is a huge plus for anyone who hates looking like a craft project.

## Where It Falls Short

The intensity is a double-edged sword. If you're not experienced with bold highlights, you will overdo it on the first try. There's no built-in brush, which means you need your own tools — and if you're traveling, that's another item to pack. The faint cosmetic scent, while not objectionable, is worth noting for those with fragrance sensitivities. And the price point sits firmly in luxury territory; this is not an impulse buy.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The BECCA loyalist** who mourned the original and wants the same formula back
- **The glow enthusiast** who wants maximum shine with minimal effort
- **The dry-skin sufferer** whose highlighter always emphasizes flakes — this one won't
- **The makeup artist** needing a reliable, buildable highlight for diverse clients

Skip it if you prefer subtle, barely-there glow or if you're on a strict budget — there are solid drugstore options, though none match this texture.

## FAQ

**Q: Is this glittery or shimmery?**
A: Neither, exactly. It's a metallic, pearlized finish with no visible glitter particles. The shine comes from finely milled mica that reads as wet-looking skin, not sparkle.

**Q: How does it wear on oily skin?**
A: It holds up reasonably well — the powder doesn't break down or slide around. Oily-skinned reviewers note it can intensify by hour six, but blotting fixes it without removing the highlight.

**Q: Can you use this on the body?**
A: Yes. Dust it on collarbones or shoulders for events. It blends easily and doesn't transfer heavily onto clothing.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a high-impact, creamy highlighter that wears all day and flatters most skin tones. **Skip it if** you prefer subtle glow or need an all-in-one compact with a mirror and brush.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — it's not perfect, but for the formula and performance, it's close to the gold standard.
