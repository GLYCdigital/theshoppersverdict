Let’s get the elephant out of the room: the Dyson Airwrap Multi-Styler Complete Long costs roughly the same as a mid-range laptop. So before you hand over that credit card, you need to know exactly what you’re getting — and what you’re not.

I dug through 204 Amazon ratings (4.1 stars), analyzed buyer feedback, and compared specs against the competition. Here’s the unfiltered truth.

## What’s Actually in the Box

The Complete Long kit is Dyson’s most comprehensive Airwrap package. As shown in the product image, you get the main styler body plus eight attachments:

- 30mm and 40mm Airwrap barrels (long versions for hair past shoulders)
- 40mm Airwrap extra-long barrel for very long hair
- Firm and soft smoothing brushes
- Round volumizing brush
- Wide-tooth comb (for detangling and straightening)
- Coanda smoothing dryer for pre-drying

The barrels use Dyson’s signature Coanda effect — air that wraps hair around the barrel without heat. The styler measures air temperature 40 times per second, keeping it under 302°F, far lower than typical curling irons that hit 400°F.

## Real-World Performance

The Airwrap is genuinely clever. You hover the wand near a dry section of hair, and the airflow pulls it around the barrel automatically. No clamping. No heat damage. The result is a bouncy, natural-looking curl that feels like you spent the day at the beach, not like you tortured your hair with a hot iron.

But here’s the catch: it works beautifully on some hair types and struggles on others. Multiple buyers with fine, straight hair reported curls falling flat within an hour without aggressive product layering. One reviewer noted, “It took me three weeks of daily practice to get curls that last past lunch. My sister with wavy hair nailed it on day one.”

For thick, wavy, or coarse hair, the Airwrap shines. The barrels create defined waves that hold well, and the smoothing brushes deliver a salon-quality blowout without the heat damage.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The build quality is universally acknowledged. Dyson’s engineering is top-tier — the motor is powerful, the attachments click in securely, and the device feels premium in hand.

The damage reduction is the most-cited benefit. One verified buyer wrote: “My hair was breaking from my old curling iron. After three months of Airwrap use, my stylist asked what I changed because my ends look healthier.”

The Complete Long kit's versatility also earns consistent praise. Having every attachment in one box means you can go from sleek straight to voluminous waves without buying add-ons.

## Where It Falls Short

The learning curve is the most common complaint — and it’s legitimate. This isn’t a grab-and-go tool. You must section hair properly, use the right tension, and prep with the right products. Buyers who skip the learning process end up frustrated.

The styling time is another factor. The Airwrap takes longer than a traditional curling iron — expect 30-45 minutes for a full head versus 15-20 with a wand. For busy mornings, that's a real consideration.

Some buyers also noted the device runs hot after extended use, and the filter needs periodic cleaning to maintain airflow. Plus, as noted, the $49 storage case sold separately is a stingy move at this price point.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have shoulder-length or longer hair, regularly style with heat tools, and want to reduce damage. It’s also ideal for anyone who struggles to create consistent curls with traditional irons.

**Skip it if:** Your hair is very fine and pin-straight, you need quick five-minute styling, or you’re not willing to invest practice time. The Airwrap is a skill tool, not a shortcut.

## FAQ

**Q: Does the Airwrap work on damp hair?**
A: Yes, and it should be used on damp (not wet) hair. The Coanda dryer attachment pre-dries sections to about 80% dry before styling. Using it on bone-dry hair won't produce the same curl hold.

**Q: Is this better than the Shark FlexStyle?**
A: The Dyson has stronger airflow, more intelligent heat control, and better build quality. The Shark is half the price. If budget is your concern, the Shark is respectable — but the Dyson delivers a more polished result.

**Q: Will it damage my hair?**
A: Significantly less than traditional heat tools. The max temperature is 302°F with constant measurement, versus curling irons that spike past 400°F. You'll notice less breakage and frizz over time.

## The Verdict

The Dyson Airwrap Multi-Styler Complete Long is a legitimate game-changer for hair health, but it’s not magic. The technology is genuinely innovative, the results are impressive once you master the technique, and the damage reduction is real.

However, at this price, the learning curve and time commitment are real tradeoffs. If you’re patient, have the budget, and style your hair regularly, this will likely become your most-used tool. If you want a quick fix, save your money.

**Buy it if** you value hair health over speed and are willing to practice. **Skip it if** you need fast styling or have fine, stubbornly straight hair.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — Deducting points for the learning curve and missing storage case, but the engineering and results justify the premium for the right user.
