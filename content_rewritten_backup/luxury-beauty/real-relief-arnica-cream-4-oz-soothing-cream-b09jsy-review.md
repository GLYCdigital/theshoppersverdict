The 4 oz size also goes faster than you'd expect. If you're applying it to large areas like your back or both knees twice daily, you're looking at 2-3 weeks per tube. At this price point, that's not insignificant for regular users.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this with other pain medications?**
A: Generally yes, since arnica works topically and doesn't interact systemically like oral NSAIDs. However, never apply it to broken skin or open wounds, and consult your doctor if you're pregnant or nursing.

**Q: How often should I apply it?**
A: Most users find 2-3 times daily works well. Apply a thin layer to the affected area and massage until absorbed. Consistency matters more than quantity.

**Q: Is this safe for daily long-term use?**
A: Yes, for most people. Arnica has been used topically for centuries. The key is avoiding use on broken skin and giving your skin breaks if you notice any irritation.
