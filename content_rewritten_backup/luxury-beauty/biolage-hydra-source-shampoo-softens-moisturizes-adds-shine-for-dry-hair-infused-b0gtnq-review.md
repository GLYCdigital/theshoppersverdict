## What You Actually Get

You're getting a 33.8-fluid-ounce bottle of sulfate-free shampoo built around aloe vera — the plant extract that's been hydrating skin and hair for centuries. The formula also includes a blend of botanicals designed to lock moisture into the hair shaft rather than just coating it temporarily.

As shown in the product image, the packaging is classic Biolage — clean, professional, no gimmicks. The shampoo itself is a pearlescent gel that lathers up surprisingly well for a sulfate-free formula.

## Performance: Does It Actually Work?

Here's where this shampoo separates itself from the pack. The first wash delivers immediate slip — that slippery, detangled feeling you normally only get from conditioner. Your hair feels softer before you even apply anything else.

The aloe vera isn't just a token ingredient. It's listed prominently, and it shows in the results. Hair comes out of the shower feeling hydrated, not squeaky-clean-stripped. For anyone with dry, damaged, or color-treated hair, that's the difference between a shampoo that works and one that fights you.

One thing worth noting: the lather is rich for a sulfate-free product. You don't need to use half the bottle to feel like you're actually cleansing. A quarter-sized amount handles medium-length hair easily.

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty time. This shampoo has two notable downsides.

**The fragrance is potent.** It's a fresh, botanical scent — think aloe and green notes — but it lingers. If you're sensitive to fragrance or prefer unscented hair care, this will be a problem. One reviewer described it as *"lovely but strong — I could smell it on my pillow the next morning."*

**Thick, coarse hair may need backup.** If you have dense, curly, or extremely coarse hair, this shampoo cleanses well but won't single-handedly fix deep dryness. You'll need to pair it with a rich conditioner or a weekly deep conditioning mask. It's a moisturizing shampoo, not a miracle worker.

## FAQ

**Is this shampoo safe for keratin-treated hair?**
Yes, generally. It's sulfate-free, which is the main requirement. However, the aloe vera is hydrating, so it won't interfere with keratin treatments the way oil-based products can.

**How often should I use it?**
For most people, every other wash is ideal. If your hair is severely dry, daily use is fine — it's gentle enough. Just don't expect it to fix extreme damage overnight.

**Does it work on curly hair?**
It helps with hydration, which curls desperately need. But if you follow the Curly Girl Method strictly, check the full ingredient list — some curl purists find certain ingredients questionable.
