**What you're actually getting**

The Kirkland Signature Aller-Flo box contains five identical bottles, each holding 120 metered sprays of fluticasone propionate (50mcg per spray). That's 600 total doses — roughly an eight-month supply if you're using the standard two-sprays-per-nostril, once-daily regimen. For context, that's about three times what you'd get from a single Flonase pack at twice the price per unit. The active ingredient is chemically identical to the name brand; there's no proprietary delivery system or secret formulation here. It's the same corticosteroid that's been the over-the-counter allergy standard for years.

**Performance and real-world use**

The spray mechanism delivers a consistent, fine mist with each actuation. I've tested cheaper generics where the pump dribbles or sputters — this one doesn't. The dose is metered and reliable, which matters when you're relying on it daily during peak pollen season. As shown in the product image, each bottle is compact and travel-friendly, though the five-pack is obviously designed for home stocking.

One thing to understand: this is a preventative medication, not a rescue spray. It takes 3-5 days of consistent use to reach full effectiveness. If you're in the middle of a sinus infection or a severe allergy spike, this won't give you instant relief. You need to start it before symptoms peak. That's true of all fluticasone products, but it's worth stating plainly because many first-time users expect immediate results.

**FAQ**

**Is this exactly the same as Flonase?**
Yes, the active ingredient and concentration (50mcg/spray) are identical. The inactive ingredients differ slightly, but there's no clinical difference in effectiveness.

**How long does one bottle last?**
At the standard dose (2 sprays per nostril, once daily), one bottle lasts about 30 days. The five-pack covers roughly five months.

**Can I use this while pregnant or breastfeeding?**
Fluticasone propionate is generally considered safe during pregnancy, but you should always consult your OB-GYN before starting any medication. Don't rely on a product review for medical advice.
