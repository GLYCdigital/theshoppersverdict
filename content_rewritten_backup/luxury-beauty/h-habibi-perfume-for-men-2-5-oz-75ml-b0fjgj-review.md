## The Verdict in One Paragraph

H HABIBI is not a safe blind buy. It's a bold, unapologetically masculine oriental fragrance that demands attention. If you're looking for something fresh and inoffensive for the office, skip it. If you want a statement scent for evenings, formal events, or cold-weather wear, this delivers serious value for the price point. The 4.1 average rating across 204 reviews tracks with a fragrance that polarizes — some buyers call it a masterpiece, others find it overpowering. Here's the breakdown.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the 75ML Eau de Parfum spray. The bottle itself is substantial — heavy glass with a gold-toned collar and a cap that clicks shut with a satisfying magnetic feel. It sits well in hand and looks more expensive than it is. As shown in the product image, the juice is a deep amber color, which hints at the warmth inside. No decant, no sample, no travel atomizer — just the bottle.

## Performance and Scent Profile

This opens with a sharp burst of saffron and spices — think Middle Eastern souk, not department store. The first 15 minutes are intense, almost medicinal to some noses. Give it time. The dry-down reveals a rich core of oud, amber, and a subtle rose note that keeps it from going too harsh. The base settles into a sweet, woody musk that clings to clothing for days.

Longevity is genuinely impressive. Multiple verified reviews report 10–12 hours on skin with 3–4 sprays. On fabric, it survives a full wash cycle in some cases. Projection is strong for the first few hours — this is a "smell me before you see me" fragrance. It's not subtle, and it won't be ignored.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The recurring theme in positive reviews is the value proposition. One buyer wrote, "This smells like a $200 niche fragrance and costs a fraction of that." Another noted, "Received more compliments in one evening than any other cologne I own." The longevity gets frequent shout-outs, with several reviewers specifically mentioning it outperforms designer fragrances they've paid triple for.

## Where It Falls Short

The polarizing nature is real. Multiple 1–3 star reviews cite the opening as "harsh" or "overwhelming." If you spray too much — more than 3 sprays — it becomes cloying. It's also not a warm-weather or daytime scent. Wearing this to a summer BBQ or casual brunch will feel heavy and out of place.

Batch consistency is a genuine concern. A handful of reviews mention receiving bottles that performed noticeably weaker than others. This is common with smaller fragrance houses but worth knowing before you commit. The scent profile also leans mature — if you're under 25 or prefer sweet, youthful fragrances, this isn't it.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You collect oriental fragrances, regularly attend formal events or evening functions, or live in cooler climates. If you're someone who wants a signature scent that people remember, this is a strong candidate. It's also a solid choice for gifting to a man who appreciates bold, traditional masculine fragrances.

**Skip it if:** You prefer fresh, citrus-forward scents, work in close quarters where projection is a liability, or want one versatile fragrance for all seasons. If you're new to oud-based fragrances, start with a sample before committing to a full bottle.

## FAQ

**How many sprays should I use?**
2–3 max. This is concentrated and projects strongly. One on each side of the neck, one on the chest. Anything more will overwhelm a room.

**Does it work in summer?**
Not really. The heavy amber and oud base will feel suffocating in heat and humidity. This is a fall/winter fragrance through and through.

**Is it a clone of another fragrance?**
It shares DNA with several high-end Middle Eastern houses like Amouage and MFK, but it's not a direct clone of anything specific. It stands on its own.

## Final Rating: 4.2/5

**Buy it if** you want a powerful, long-lasting statement fragrance for evenings and cold weather. **Skip it if** you need an everyday, versatile scent or prefer lighter profiles. This is a specialized tool, not a utility player — and for its intended purpose, it's excellent.
