### Performance and Real-World Use

Drop one in a hot bath and you'll see the fizz action start within seconds. The bombs dissolve fully in about 5-7 minutes — fast enough that you're not sitting there waiting, slow enough that you get the full experience. The water turns a rich, saturated color (think deep rose, vivid lavender, bright citrus) and the scent lingers on your skin after you dry off.

The fragrance profile is where this set earns its "luxury" label. These aren't the harsh, synthetic-smelling bombs you find in drugstore bins. The scents are layered and complex — lavender and eucalyptus for relaxation, citrus and bergamot for morning baths, vanilla and sandalwood for evening soaks. One of the 215 Amazon reviewers put it well: *"The smell isn't overwhelming but it's definitely noticeable. My whole bathroom smelled like a spa for hours after."*

Skin feel is another standout. The bombs are formulated with moisturizing oils (shea butter and coconut oil appear in most blends), so your skin doesn't feel stripped or tight after a soak. You'll want to rinse the tub afterward, though — these leave a slick residue that needs a quick wipe-down.

### Where It Falls Short

Honesty time. "Handmade" means inconsistency. Some bombs in the same box will fizz more aggressively than others. A few reviewers reported bombs arriving slightly crumbly or cracked — not unusable, but not pristine either. If you're gifting these, inspect each one before wrapping.

The moisturizing oils also mean the bombs are greasier than commercial alternatives. If you hate the "just applied lotion" feeling after a bath, you might find this off-putting. And the slick tub residue is real — one reviewer noted, *"Loved the soak, hated cleaning the ring afterward."*

Packaging for shipping is adequate but not over-engineered. These are fragile; if the box gets tossed in transit, you might open it to find powder instead of bombs.

**Q: Do these stain the tub?**
