Let’s cut the preamble: if you’re on this page, you already know Kayali’s Yum line has a stranglehold on the gourmand-fragrance community. The question isn’t *whether* **Yum Boujee Marshmallow 81** smells good — it’s whether the “Intense” concentration justifies the premium over the original, and whether it earns a spot in a rotation that probably already has three other pink-juice perfumes. After testing this 50ml bottle for two weeks, here’s the honest breakdown.

## What’s Actually in the Box

You get a 50ml rectangular glass flacon with a magnetic cap — no sprayer issues, no cheap plastic. The juice itself is a light blush pink, which matches the brand’s aesthetic. The box includes the standard Kayali booklet with fragrance layering suggestions. Nothing else. If you’re expecting a travel atomizer or discovery set, that’s not here — you’re paying for the juice, not the extras.

## Performance: The “Intense” Is Not Marketing Fluff

Here’s where this version earns its name. On my skin, **Yum Boujee Marshmallow 81** delivered:

- **2–3 hours of strong projection** (arm’s-length sillage, easily noticed by others)
- **6–8 hours of skin longevity** before fading to a close-to-skin scent
- **12+ hours on clothing** — a cotton shirt still smelled distinctly sweet the next morning

The opening is a burst of tart strawberry and pink pepper that immediately reads “fruity candy” — but the magic is in the transition. Within 20 minutes, the marshmallow accord softens the edges, and a pistachio-cream note emerges that keeps it from smelling like a generic strawberry body spray. That’s the Kayali signature: gourmand without being cloying.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

Across the 225+ ratings (4.5 average), the praise consistently centers on two things: **compliments and longevity**. One verified buyer wrote, *“I sprayed this at 7 AM and could still smell it on my scarf at midnight. My coworker asked what I was wearing twice.”* Another noted, *“This is the first Kayali that doesn’t disappear after an hour on my skin. The intense version is worth the extra cost.”*

## Where It Falls Short

Let’s be honest about the tradeoffs.

**First, the initial sillage is LOUD.** If you spray more than three times, you will announce your presence before you enter a room. For an office setting, this is a one-spray fragrance — and even then, the first hour is borderline. This isn’t a flaw if you love projection, but it’s a real consideration for daily wear.

**Second, the strawberry note fades faster than the marketing suggests.** The first 30 minutes are undeniably strawberry-forward, but the dry-down settles into a powdery vanilla-marshmallow with a faint nuttiness. It’s beautiful, but if you’re buying specifically for the bright red-fruit opening, you’ll be disappointed by hour two.

**Third, the DNA is familiar.** If you own **Kayali Yum Pistachio Gelato 33** or have smelled **Burberry Her**, parts of this will feel like a remix. It’s not a copycat, but it’s also not a groundbreaking new direction — it’s a refinement of notes Kayali has been working with for years.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The gourmand collector** who wants a stronger, longer-lasting version of the marshmallow-pistachio profile
- **The layering enthusiast** — this pairs exceptionally well with vanilla-forward scents or even a touch of citrus to brighten the top notes
- **Anyone who found the original Yum Boujee too faint** — the “Intense” concentration fixes that complaint decisively

## Who Should Skip It

- **Office workers in close quarters** who can’t control their trigger finger — the sillage will get you noticed, and not always positively
- **Fans of the strawberry top note** who want that scent to last all day — it doesn’t survive past the first hour
- **Anyone expecting a completely new scent** — this is evolution, not revolution

## FAQ

**Q: How does this compare to the original Yum Boujee Marshmallow?**
The intense version has roughly 50–70% better longevity and a denser, creamier dry-down. The opening is similar, but the intense version pushes the marshmallow note forward and tones down the berry-sharpness earlier.

**Q: Is this unisex?**
It leans feminine but the pistachio-cream base keeps it from being saccharine. Anyone who enjoys sweet gourmands can wear it, but it’s definitely not a “clean” or “fresh” profile.

**Q: How many sprays should I use?**
Two sprays max for close settings — one on the chest, one on a wrist. This is a fragrance that rewards restraint. Three or more sprays and you’ll be scent-sitting in a room by yourself.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

**Buy it if:** You want a sweet, high-performance gourmand that gets compliments and survives a full workday. The intense concentration is a genuine upgrade, not a cash grab.

**Skip it if:** You prefer subtle, skin-scents, or you already own Yum Pistachio Gelato and don’t want a sibling scent in your collection.

At this price point, you’re paying for performance and polish — and Yum Boujee Marshmallow 81 delivers on both. Just respect the spray count.
