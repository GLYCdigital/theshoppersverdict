Let's be blunt: most press-on nail sets are disposable afterthoughts. The 087 square nail tips, however, are trying to be something more — a reusable, salon-adjacent option that doesn't crumble after one wear. With a 4.6 average across 229 ratings, they're clearly doing something right. The question is whether they're right for *you*.

## What's Actually in the Box

The set arrives in a clear, gradient-organized case that displays all 24 sizes at a glance — no digging through a jumbled bag to find your pinky. Each tip is full-coverage (no French-tip cutouts or half-nail designs here), which matters more than you'd think. Full coverage means the color you apply is the color you get, with zero translucent patches where the natural nail shows through.

You get 24 nails total, which breaks down to roughly 1.3 sets if you're doing standard 10-finger application. Sizes run from 0 to 23, with the smaller numbers fitting pinkies and the larger fitting thumbs. The gradient case isn't just pretty — it's functional, letting you grab the next size up without breaking your flow mid-manicure.

## How They Actually Perform

The material is a medium-thickness polymer that strikes a decent balance between durability and flexibility. It's not the flimsy, bendable plastic you get from drugstore sets — these have enough structure to hold their shape through typing, dishwashing, and general life. But they're also not so rigid that they'll snap the moment you catch one on a jacket zipper.

Application is straightforward: buff the natural nail, clean with alcohol, apply glue (or adhesive tabs if you're using your own), press and hold for 30 seconds. The undersides are smooth with a slight well at the cuticle area, which helps them sit flatter against the nail bed — a detail that prevents that annoying "lifted at the base" look.

What buyers consistently praise is the shaping. One reviewer noted, "The square cut is actually square — not rounded off like some sets that claim square but deliver squoval." Another mentioned they'd "gotten three full wears out of one set by removing with oil and storing back in the case," which tracks with the reusable design.

## Where They Fall Short

The square shape runs wide. If you have narrow, slender nail beds, you'll likely need to file the edges down — and the polymer doesn't file as cleanly as acrylic. You'll get dust, and you'll need to be patient to avoid rough edges.

More notably, the base set doesn't include adhesive tabs. You're buying nail tips, not a complete kit. That's fine if you already have glue, but it's an extra purchase for first-timers. The packaging doesn't shout this from the front, so it's easy to miss until you're mid-application.

Some users also reported that the tips are slightly thinner than luxury salon brands — they're not going to survive heavy manual labor, but they're fine for office work and social wear.

## Who Should Buy These

If you want a reusable set that looks polished with gel or regular polish, this is a smart pick. The 24-size range genuinely accommodates most hand shapes, and the gradient case makes organization painless.

Skip them if you have extremely wide or very narrow nail beds, or if you want a complete kit with adhesive tabs included. Also skip if you're a one-and-done user who doesn't want to fuss with removal oils and storage — there are cheaper disposable options that'll serve you fine.

## FAQ

**How long do these last with glue?**
With proper prep and a good nail glue, expect 7–10 days. With adhesive tabs, 3–5 days. Reuse is possible up to three times if you soak off gently and store them back in the case.

**Can I file these to a different shape?**
Yes, but carefully. They're polymer, not acrylic, so filing produces fine dust. Shape them before application, and use a fine-grit file to avoid chips.

**Do they work with gel polish?**
Yes, but you'll need a UV/LED lamp. The surface takes gel well without priming, and the full-coverage design prevents the polish from looking patchy.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want a reusable press-on set that looks professional, comes in a genuinely useful size range, and holds up through multiple wears.

**Skip it if:** You need a complete kit with adhesive tabs, or you have extremely narrow nail beds that require heavy reshaping.

At 4.6 out of 5, the 087 square nail tips earn their score. They're not flawless — the width issue and missing tabs are real — but for the price point and the durability they offer, they're one of the more sensible press-on options on Amazon right now.
