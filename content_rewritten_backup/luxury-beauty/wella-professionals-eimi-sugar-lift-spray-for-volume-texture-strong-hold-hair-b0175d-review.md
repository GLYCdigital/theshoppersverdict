Some hair sprays promise volume but deliver stiffness. The Wella Professionals EIMI Sugar Lift Spray is different — it gives you that gritty, touchable texture that makes fine hair look deliberately messy, not accidentally flat. It’s a cult favorite among stylists for a reason, and with 220 Amazon ratings averaging 4.4 stars, real users back that up.

## What’s in the bottle

You get a 5.1 oz aerosol can that sprays a fine, dry mist. The formula uses sugar-based polymers — hence the name — to create grip and memory in the hair. Unlike waxes or pomades, this isn’t greasy. It sprays on clean and dries down to a matte, slightly rough texture that holds shape without looking wet or shiny.

## Performance: where it shines

This is a multi-tasker. Use it on damp roots before blow-drying for serious lift at the crown — the kind that makes flat hair look like you just left a salon. Or spray it on dry hair, scrunch, and you’ve got instant piece-y texture for a bedhead look. The hold is strong but flexible: hair moves, then snaps back into place.

One buyer put it plainly: *“I have baby-fine hair and this is the only product that gives me volume that actually lasts through a workday. A little goes a long way.”* That sentiment echoes across reviews — users with fine, straight hair consistently report the best results.

Humidity resistance is another strong point. The sugar polymers create a barrier that keeps your style from collapsing when the air gets thick. It’s not waterproof, but it handles a sweaty commute better than most sprays in this price range.

## Where it falls short

Here’s the honest tradeoff: this is a texture product, not a hold-everything-in-place hairspray. If you’re looking for helmet-level stiffness, skip it. Over-application is the #1 complaint — spray too much and you’ll get a crunchy, stuck-together mess that’s hard to brush out. The fix is easy (two quick spritzes, not five), but it takes a learning curve.

Fine hair users should also note: applying to wet roots can make hair feel heavier once dry. The sweet spot is 70% dry hair, then spray and finish blow-drying. And if your hair is chemically treated or very dry, the sugar content can feel slightly tacky by day two — plan on washing it out.

## Who should buy this

- **Fine or flat hair**: You’ll get the most dramatic lift here.  
- **Short to medium cuts**: Pixies, bobs, and shags love the piecey definition.  
- **Stylists or home users who want salon-grade versatility**: One can replaces three products.

Skip it if you have thick, coarse hair that doesn’t need volume — the texture won’t add much, and you’ll just get grit without payoff.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work on second-day hair?**  
Yes, actually better than fresh-washed hair. The natural oils give the sugar spray something to grip, so you get a more lived-in, matte finish.

**Q: How do I avoid the crunchy look?**  
Spray from 8–10 inches away, use only 2–3 short bursts, and scrunch immediately. For a softer finish, apply to damp hair and blow-dry through with your fingers.

**Q: Is it safe for color-treated hair?**  
Yes. The formula is sulfate-free and doesn’t contain alcohol, so it won’t strip color or dry out the cuticle.
