If you’ve ever wrestled with a straight mascara wand trying to coat those tiny lashes in the inner corner, you know the struggle. L’Oréal’s Voluminous Panorama attacks that exact problem with a curved brush that rotates as you sweep — and at 4.5 stars across 225 ratings, it’s clearly resonating. But does the gimmick hold up, or is this just another drugstore mascara with a fancy wand? I tested it for two weeks straight to find out.

**What’s in the tube**

You get a single 0.27-fluid-ounce mascara in the signature Voluminous black tube. The star here is the wand: a curved, flexible brush with bristles that shorten at the tip and lengthen at the base. The design is meant to hug the natural curve of your lash line and catch every hair, including the ones you usually ignore. The formula itself is infused with panthenol (pro-vitamin B5), which explains why lashes feel conditioned rather than crispy by end of day.

**Real-world performance**

First application, I noticed the formula runs wetter than the original Voluminous. That’s a double-edged sword: it spreads easily and builds volume fast, but you need to give it about 30 seconds to set before you open your eyes fully or blink aggressively. One reviewer nailed the experience: “The curved brush is a game changer for my hooded eyes — it actually gets the lashes at the outer corner without stabbing myself.” I’d echo that.

The 360° design isn’t marketing fluff. It genuinely rotates through your lash line, catching hairs a standard straight wand skips. Two coats deliver a wide-eyed, dramatic look without clumping. Wear test: 10 hours at a desk job, no flaking, no raccoon eyes. It survives light humidity but won’t hold up through a workout — it’s not waterproof, so keep that expectation in check.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The curved wand is the headline. Across the 225 ratings, the most repeated compliments involve how well it works on hooded eyes, deep-set eyes, and straight lashes that typically point downward. Users with sparse lashes also report that the panthenol formula doesn’t weigh their natural lashes down or cause fallout. Multiple reviewers noted it remakes cleanly — warm water and gentle pressure, no oil-based remover required, which is a nice bonus for sensitive eyes.

**Where it falls short**

The wet formula is the biggest complaint I found. If you’re impatient and don’t let it dry between coats, you’ll get transfer onto your brow bone. Also, this is a volume-first mascara. It will not stretch your lashes into falsie territory. If length is your primary goal, you’ll want to pair this with a lash primer or choose a different formula entirely. Finally, the curved wand takes a few tries to master — the first application may feel awkward if you’re used to straight brushes.

**Who should buy this**

- **The hooded-eye crowd**: The curved wand is genuinely designed for you.
- **Volume seekers**: If you want bold, dramatic lashes without layering five products, this delivers.
- **Sensitive-eye users**: Panthenol is gentle, and the formula removes easily without harsh scrubbing.

**Skip it if** you’re all about length, if you hate waiting for mascara to dry, or if you need a waterproof formula for sweaty days or emotional events.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is this better than the original Voluminous?**
A: For volume and inner-corner coverage, yes. The curved wand is a real upgrade. But the original has a drier formula that sets faster — if you’re always in a rush, the classic is more forgiving.

**Q: Does it smudge on oily lids?**
A: In my testing, no — the formula sets down firmly after that initial dry-down window. But if you have very oily eyelids, dust a tiny bit of translucent powder under your lash line as a safeguard.

**Q: How many coats before it gets clumpy?**
A: You can comfortably do three coats if you let each layer dry for 30 seconds. Beyond that, you’ll start to see spider-leg clumps.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a drugstore mascara that actually addresses the lash-coverage problem with a thoughtful wand design. It’s a solid upgrade for anyone frustrated with missed lashes or looking for volume without the flake factor. **Skip it if** you need lengthening or waterproof wear.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The curved wand is a genuine innovation, and the panthenol formula keeps lashes healthy. The wet formula and lack of lengthening hold it back from perfection, but for most shoppers, this is a clear win at the price point.
