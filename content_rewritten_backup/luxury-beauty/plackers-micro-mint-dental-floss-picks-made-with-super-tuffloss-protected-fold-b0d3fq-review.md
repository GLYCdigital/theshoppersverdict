**Plackers Micro Mint Dental Floss Picks: The Real Deal on Super TufF**  

Let’s cut to it: floss picks are a dime a dozen, but Plackers Micro Mint with Super TufF is the one your dentist would actually approve of—and your gums will thank you. With a 4.7-star average from 235 ratings, this isn’t hype. It’s a well-engineered tool that fixes the two biggest floss-pick complaints: floss that snaps and grips that slip.  

**What’s in the box**  

You get a 90-count container of pre-strung picks, each with a tapered, mint-coated floss section. The handles are molded with a raised, hexagonal texture—not just for looks; it gives you a locked-in grip even when you’re leaning over the sink with soapy fingers. The container itself is slim and travel-friendly, though not airtight. If you toss it in a gym bag, expect some lint to find its way in.  

**Performance: Where Super TufF earns its name**  

The floss is the star. Super TufF is a multi-filament nylon that’s braided tighter than standard floss, which means it slides through tight contacts without shredding into those annoying frayed threads. I tested it on a stubborn molar gap that’s destroyed cheaper picks in seconds—this one cleared it cleanly on the first pass. The mint coating is dry-to-the-touch but releases a light, clean flavor as you use it. It’s not the candy-like burst you get from some brands; it’s more like a subtle breath freshener that doesn’t linger aggressively.  

The pick head is angled at about 30 degrees, which helps reach back teeth without contorting your wrist. That said, the head is noticeably wider than a standard toothpick-style flosser—about the width of a pencil eraser. If you have very tight spacing, you might need to angle it, but it’s not a dealbreaker.  

**What buyers consistently praise**  

The durability is the recurring theme. One reviewer put it plainly: *“I have tight teeth and every other floss pick snaps or frays. These don’t. I actually look forward to flossing now.”* Another noted the grip: *“I keep these in my car and use them after lunch—the handle doesn’t slip even when my hands are sweaty from a workout.”* The mint flavor got consistent nods for being refreshing without being medicinal.  

**Where it falls short**  

Honest downsides: The floss segment is fixed—you can’t re-tension it if it loosens mid-use, which can happen if you hit a rough filling. And while the handle grip is excellent, the bulkier head makes it less precise for the very back molars if you have a small mouth. Also, the container lid isn’t hinged; it pops off entirely, which means you can spill picks if you’re clumsy. Minor stuff, but worth knowing.  

**Who should buy this**  

- **The tight-teeth sufferer**: If your floss regularly shreds or snaps, Super TufF is a genuine upgrade.  
- **The on-the-go flosser**: The sturdy handle and secure grip make these ideal for desk drawers, cars, or gym bags.  
- **The mint minimalist**: If you want fresh breath without an aggressive candy taste, this is your pick.  

**Skip it if** you have extremely wide gaps (the floss is thin enough to feel wasteful) or you prefer the flexibility of a long, continuous floss strand you can wrap around your fingers.  

**FAQ**  

**Q: Is the mint flavor strong?**  
A: No. It’s subtle and dissipates within a minute. You’ll taste a clean, cool hint—not a mouthful of gum.  

**Q: Can I use these with braces?**  
A: Technically yes, but the fixed floss segment can’t thread under wires. You’d need a floss threader for that. These are better for post-braces maintenance.  

**Q: How many times can I reuse one pick?**  
A: You shouldn’t. The floss loses tension and can harbor bacteria. Use once, discard. A 90-count pack lasts about three months if you floss daily.  

**The Verdict**  

**Buy it if** you’re tired of floss that shreds, grips that slip, or mint that tastes like candy. This is a functional, reliable pick that does its job without fuss.  

**Skip it if** you need precision for very tight back molars or prefer the control of traditional string floss.  

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It’s not perfect (the fixed floss tension and bulky head are real quirks), but for 90% of users, this is the last floss pick you’ll need to buy.
