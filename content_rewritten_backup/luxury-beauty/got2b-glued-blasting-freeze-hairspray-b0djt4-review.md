## Real-World Performance

I tested this on a humid summer day with fine, straight hair that typically falls flat within hours. After curling and spraying with the Got2b Glued, my waves held their shape through a full workday, a gym session, and an evening out. One application was enough — no touch-ups needed. This is the kind of performance that justifies the "Blasting Freeze" name.

The hold is real, but it's not magical. You need to apply it correctly. Hold the can 10-12 inches from your head and spray in short bursts. Too close and you'll get wet spots that take forever to dry. Too much product and fine hair turns stiff. Start with a light mist, let it dry, and build up if needed.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has honest limitations.

**The scent is strong.** The initial spray smells like a mix of hairspray and alcohol — not unpleasant, but definitely noticeable. It fades within 15-20 minutes, but if you're sensitive to fragrance, this could be a dealbreaker.

**Overspray is real.** The aerosol nozzle releases a wide, forceful mist. If you're not careful, you'll get product on your face, mirrors, or countertops. Cover your eyes and shield nearby surfaces when applying.

**Fine hair needs a light touch.** The "freeze" hold is intense. If you have thin or fine hair and use too much, you'll get stiffness, not just hold. The learning curve is real, but manageable.
