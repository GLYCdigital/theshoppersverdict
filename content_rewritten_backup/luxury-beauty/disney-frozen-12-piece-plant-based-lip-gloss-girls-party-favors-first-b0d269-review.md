**Is this the Frozen lip gloss set that actually delivers, or just another licensed product coasting on the brand name?**

When you search for kids' makeup on Amazon, you wade through a swamp of gimmicky sets with glitter fallout and mystery ingredients. The Disney Frozen 12-Piece Plant Based Lip Gloss set stands apart — and the 4.8-star average across 240 ratings confirms parents have noticed.

**What's actually in the box**

You get 12 full-size glosses (0.10 oz each, which is generous for a party-favor price point). The shades span the full Frozen spectrum: icy blues, Elsa-inspired lavender, Anna's warm corals, and a few shimmery pinks. Each tube features character art, and the colors genuinely match the character on the label — a detail kids care about and parents appreciate.

The formula is the real story here. It's plant-based, which means no petroleum jelly or synthetic dyes. The ingredient list is short and readable: plant oils, natural waxes, and mineral pigments. No parabens, no phthalates, no gluten. For parents wary of what goes on their kids' lips, that's the selling point.

**How it performs in real life**

I tested these on a 6-year-old and an 11-year-old. The gloss applies smoothly with no tugging, and the color payoff is surprisingly strong — this isn't a clear gloss with a hint of tint. The icy blue shade looks genuinely frosty, and the corals show up without needing multiple coats.

Wear time is about two hours before needing reapplication, which is standard for any gloss. It's not sticky — hair doesn't get caught in it, and it doesn't leave that annoying film kids complain about. The vanilla scent is pleasant but uniform across all 12 tubes. If your kid wanted different flavors per shade, that's not happening here.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Parents in the reviews repeatedly mention two things: the safety factor and how well the colors match the packaging. One reviewer wrote, *"My daughter has sensitive skin and this didn't cause any reaction. The colors are actually beautiful, not cheap-looking."* Another noted, *"Perfect for my 5-year-old's Frozen birthday party favors — each girl got two and they were thrilled."* The plant-based angle is the recurring theme — parents feel good about handing these to kids.

**Where it falls short**

The packaging is the weak point. The tubes are lightweight plastic with printed labels that will scuff and peel with regular use. If your child throws this in a backpack without a pouch, expect scratches. The caps also aren't the most secure — one reviewer mentioned a leak in a purse, so keep them upright.

The shimmer shades also have a bit of fallout. It's not excessive, but if your kid applies heavy-handed, you'll see glitter on cheeks and chin. Easy to wipe off, but worth knowing.

**Who should buy this**

- **Party parents:** The 12-count format is ideal for goodie bags — you can give each guest two shades and still have spares.
- **Frozen-obsessed kids ages 4-10:** The character-matched colors will make them feel like Elsa and Anna.
- **Conscious shoppers:** If you avoid synthetic ingredients in kids' products, this formula fits the bill.

**Skip it if** your kid is over Frozen, or you need a gloss that survives a week in a school bag. The packaging won't hold up to heavy abuse.
