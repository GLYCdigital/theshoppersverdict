**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the downsides.

First, replacement heads. The 7AM2M heads aren't as widely available as Oral-B or Sonicare heads. You'll need to buy them from the same brand, and they're not sold at your local Target or CVS. That's a convenience cost you'll pay long-term.

Second, the handle is slightly slimmer than a Sonicare. If you have larger hands, it may feel a bit delicate. It's not fragile — it's just a different ergonomic profile.

Third, the pressure sensor is a binary threshold, not a graduated scale. It either buzzes or it doesn't. The higher-end brushed from Philips have a continuous light that fades as pressure increases. This is a minor gripe, but worth knowing.

Fourth, the charging case isn't wireless. It's a physical USB-C connection, which is fine, but if you're used to a glass charging cup, this is a different workflow.
