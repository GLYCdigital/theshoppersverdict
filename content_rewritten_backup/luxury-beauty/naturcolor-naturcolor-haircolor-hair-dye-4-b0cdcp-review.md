Let's cut through the noise: most "natural" hair dyes are glorified conditioners with a little pigment. They wash out in three weeks and leave your grays glowing like beacons. Naturcolor is different — it's a *permanent* dye that skips ammonia and uses MEA (monoethanolamine) instead. That distinction matters, because it means actual lift and lasting color, not just a tinted rinse.

I tested shade 4 (Dark Brown) over six weeks to see if the 4.2-star reputation holds up. Here's the unvarnished truth.

## What's in the Box

The kit includes the color tube, a separate developer bottle, gloves, and instructions. No conditioner sachet — that's a deliberate choice since this is a professional-style oxidative dye. You mix the tube into the developer, which comes in a squeeze bottle for easy application. The included gloves are decent quality, not the flimsy plastic you get with budget brands.

One thing to note upfront: the color tube is 60ml versus the 90ml you'll find in L'Oréal or Garnier. If your hair is past your shoulders, budget for two boxes. This is a real cost consideration, not a nitpick.

## Performance and Build Quality

The mixing process is straightforward — shake the developer, squeeze in the color, shake again. The consistency is thicker than drugstore foam dyes, closer to a professional cream. That's good for precision application but means you need a brush or careful sectioning for even coverage.

**Application experience:** The scent is where this product earns its premium positioning. There's a faint botanical note, but honestly, the standout is what you *don't* smell — no harsh ammonia punch that lingers for days. It's the closest thing to a salon experience you'll get at home.

**Color payoff:** Shade 4 deposits a rich, warm-leaning dark brown. On virgin hair, it's a true medium-dark brown with natural dimension. On porous or previously colored hair, expect it to pull warmer — think chestnut rather than espresso. If you're chasing cool, ash tones, this isn't your formula. The color has a softness that reads natural, not that hard, opaque box-dye look.

**Gray coverage:** This is the make-or-break metric. At 40 minutes with heat, coverage is excellent — 90-95% on stubborn grays. At the 30-minute mark, you'll see translucent coverage that looks washed out. The instructions say 30-40 minutes; trust the higher end. One reviewer noted, *"I've used every natural dye on the market and this is the first one that actually covered my grays without looking like shoe polish."*

**Fade rate:** Color held well through six weeks with sulfate-free shampoo. By week five, I noticed slight warmth creeping in at the roots, but no banding or patchiness. That's respectable for any permanent dye, let alone an ammonia-free one.

## Where It Falls Short

The quantity issue is real. At 60ml, you're getting 25% less product than competitors for a similar price point. For anyone with thick or long hair, the two-box requirement makes this significantly more expensive per use.

Processing time demands patience. This isn't a "color in 20 minutes while you scroll your phone" product. It requires commitment to the full 40 minutes for proper coverage. Rushing it produces mediocre results.

The warm undertone on porous hair is worth flagging. If you have previously colored hair that's grabbed red or copper tones, this will amplify that rather than neutralize it. Consider shade 3 if you need a cooler result.

## Who Should Buy This

**The sensitive-scalp grayer:** If your scalp reacts to ammonia with itching or burning, this formula is a genuine solution. The MEA base is significantly gentler.

**The natural-leaning minimalist:** You're already using clean skincare and want a hair dye that aligns with that philosophy without sacrificing permanence.

**The DIY salon client:** You know your hair, you're comfortable with a brush, and you want salon-quality results without the salon price tag.

**Skip it if:** You have very long, thick hair and don't want to manage two boxes. Or if you expect foam-dye convenience — this requires more effort and precision.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this damage hair like traditional permanent dye?**
A: It's gentler than ammonia-based formulas, but it's still an oxidative dye that opens the cuticle. Use a deep conditioner weekly and you'll be fine. It won't leave hair feeling like straw like some drugstore options.

**Q: How long does the color actually last?**
A: With color-safe shampoo and cool water, expect 5-6 weeks before noticeable fade. Warm-water washing will pull it faster. It outperforms semi-permanent "natural" dyes by a significant margin.

**Q: Can I use this on previously colored hair?**
A: Yes, but test a strand first. The color will pull warmer on porous hair. If you're coming from a dark shade, it will lighten slightly. If you're coming from black, it won't lift enough — do a color remover first.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if:** You want permanent, ammonia-free color with genuine gray coverage and don't mind investing the extra processing time. The low-odor formula and natural finish justify the premium positioning.

**Skip it if:** You need maximum product quantity per dollar, want a quick 20-minute dye job, or have porous hair that grabs warm tones — you'll fight this formula's natural warmth.
