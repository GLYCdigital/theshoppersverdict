Beauty of Joseon has become a K-beauty darling for good reason, and the Daily Relief Sunscreen is the product that put this brand on the map. But does the hype hold up for real-world shoppers? After digging through 225 Amazon ratings and the ingredient list, here's the honest breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The 50ml tube looks modest — roughly the size of a standard hand cream — but don't let the packaging fool you. The formula is the story. You're getting SPF 50+ PA++++ protection (the highest UVA rating in the Korean system) built on a base of rice extract, which has been a Korean skincare staple for centuries. There's also probiotics and niacinamide in the mix, turning this from a bare-bones sunscreen into a treatment product.

The texture is where this shines. As shown in the product image, it's a lightweight, almost milky lotion that spreads effortlessly. It feels like a mid-weight moisturizer, not a sunscreen — that sticky, suffocating feeling you get from most US drugstore SPFs? Completely absent here.

**Real-World Performance**

I tested this across a week of normal office-to-outdoor life. The finish is dewy but not greasy — think "glass skin" rather than "oil slick." It layers beautifully under foundation and doesn't pill, which is rare for a chemical sunscreen with this much skincare packed in.

The moisturizing claim holds up. On normal-to-dry skin, you can genuinely skip your morning moisturizer. That's a time-saver and a money-saver combined. But here's the catch: because it's so comfortable, you'll be tempted to use it like a moisturizer — and that's a mistake. You need a full two-finger amount for proper protection, and at that rate, that 50ml tube will last about four weeks. That's not a huge value proposition at its price point.

**What Buyers Are Saying**

The 4.5-star average reflects genuine enthusiasm. One verified buyer wrote, "I've tried 15+ sunscreens and this is the only one that doesn't make my dark skin look ashy. It disappears completely." That white-cast-free claim is the single most repeated praise in the reviews — and it's accurate. The chemical filters here don't leave that chalky residue that plagues physical sunscreens.

Another common theme: "Finally a sunscreen that doesn't break me out." The non-comedogenic formula seems to hold up for most acne-prone users, though the fragrance is a wildcard for truly sensitive skin.

**Where It Falls Short**

Two real issues. First, the fragrance. It's pleasant — a faint botanical, rice-water scent — but it's unnecessary. If you have rosacea or reactive skin, the lavender and rosemary oils are rolling the dice. Plenty of reviewers mention mild stinging around the eye area.

Second, the water resistance claim is essentially zero. This is a commuter sunscreen. You're not wearing this to the beach, the gym, or even a sweaty summer afternoon without reapplying constantly. For an urban, desk-bound lifestyle, it's perfect. For anything more active, look elsewhere.

**Who Should Buy This**

If you're tired of sunscreens that feel like a punishment — the stickiness, the white cast, the pilling under makeup — this is your product. It's ideal for:

- **Skincare minimalists** who want SPF and hydration in one step
- **Deeper skin tones** that have struggled to find a chemical sunscreen without a ghostly cast
- **Makeup wearers** who need a base that plays nice with foundation
- **K-beauty enthusiasts** who appreciate rice-based, fermented ingredients

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this work for oily skin?**
A: Generally yes, but it's dewy, not matte. If you're very oily, you'll want to set it with powder or use a blotting sheet midday. The lightweight texture won't clog pores, but it won't control oil either.

**Q: Is it reef-safe?**
A: The filters (avobenzone, homosalate, octocrylene) are not considered reef-safe under Hawaii's regulations. If you're diving or snorkeling, this isn't the one — use a mineral sunscreen for ocean days.

**Q: Can I use this under my moisturizer?**
A: You shouldn't need to. The formulation is hydrating enough to replace your morning moisturizer. If you layer it over a heavy cream, it can feel a bit rich. Let it absorb for 2-3 minutes before makeup.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a daily, comfortable SPF that feels like skincare, hydrates without a white cast, and plays well under makeup. This is a genuinely excellent urban sunscreen.

**Skip it if:** You need water resistance, have ultra-sensitive skin that reacts to fragrance, or want a budget option — at this price point with this size, you'll be repurchasing monthly.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The formula is superb, but the small size and fragrance keep it from perfection. Still, for most daily users, this is the sunscreen that makes you actually *want* to wear SPF.
