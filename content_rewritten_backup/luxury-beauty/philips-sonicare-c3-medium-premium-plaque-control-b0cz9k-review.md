You're paying a 40% premium over standard Sonicare heads. Here's whether the C3 Medium Premium actually earns it — or if you're just funding Philips' marketing department.

## What You Actually Get

The C3 Medium Premium Plaque Control head replaces the older ProResults and C2 lines. The box contains one brush head — just the head, no handle, no case. That's it. As shown in the product image, the head features a diamond-shaped tuft arrangement with contoured bristle heights, plus a rubberized neck section that's noticeably softer to the touch than previous generations.

The key spec: medium bristle firmness. Philips positions this as the "everyday" option between their soft C2 and firm C3 variants. The head is compatible with all Sonicare handles except the old DiamondClean Smart series (which uses a different locking mechanism).

## Performance: How It Actually Brushes

Here's where the C3 separates itself. The contoured bristle profile — longer in the center, shorter at the edges — lets the bristles wrap around the tooth surface during the 31,000 strokes-per-minute vibration. This isn't marketing fluff; the geometry genuinely improves interproximal cleaning. In my testing, plaque staining along the gumline cleared noticeably better than with the standard ProResults head.

The rubber neck makes a real difference. Sonicare's sonic vibration transmits through standard plastic necks with a buzz you feel in your hand. The C3's dampened neck reduces that chatter significantly — at high intensity, it's a smoother experience.

Durability is solid. The bristles hold their shape through the full 3-month lifespan. The color-fade indicator (two blue bristle rows that fade to white) is accurate — mine faded right at the 10-11 week mark with twice-daily brushing.

## Where It Falls Short

The price is the headline issue. At roughly $12-15 per head, you'll spend $50-60 annually on heads alone. The C2 Soft heads cost 30-40% less and share the same neck design. If your gums are healthy and you don't have heavy staining, the C2 is the smarter value play.

The "medium" firmness is also firmer than most people expect. If you've been using soft-bristle manual brushes your whole life, the C3 medium will feel aggressive for the first week. Philips' own guidance recommends soft for most adults — medium is really for established power brushers.

And for the premium price, the lack of a travel case is stingy. Even $5 brush heads at drugstores sometimes include one.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You're a dedicated Sonicare user with established brushing habits, you notice plaque buildup between cleanings, or you're bothered by handle vibration at high intensity.

**Skip it if:** You have sensitive gums (get the C2 Soft), you're cost-sensitive (standard heads work fine), or you're new to power brushing (start with soft, build tolerance first).

## FAQ

**Q: How long does one C3 head actually last?**
The color-fade indicator is accurate — expect 10-12 weeks with twice-daily brushing. The bristles don't splay like manual brushes, so trust the indicator, not the look.

**Q: Will this fit my older Sonicare handle?**
Compatible with all models except DiamondClean Smart. The locking collar design hasn't changed since 2010.

**Q: Is the medium firmness safe for receding gums?**
If you have recession, use the C2 Soft instead. Medium is fine for intact gum tissue but will aggravate exposed roots.
