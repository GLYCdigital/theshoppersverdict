Let’s cut through the marketing. The Oral-B iO brush head is the only replacement head that works with the iO electric toothbrush line — and that’s both its greatest strength and its biggest catch. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, buyers are mostly satisfied, but there are real tradeoffs worth knowing before you commit to this ecosystem.

## What You're Actually Getting

The box contains one brush head — that's it. No handle, no charger, no travel case. You're paying for the round, dentist-inspired design that Oral-B has refined for decades, but with a twist: the iO head uses a magnetic attachment system instead of the traditional snap-on collar. It clicks into place with a satisfying, secure feel. As shown in the product image, the bristles are densely packed in a circular pattern with a slight contour that cups each tooth individually.

The head itself is compact — roughly the size of a quarter — which makes it nimble inside the mouth. That's the whole point. The round shape allows the brush to tackle one tooth at a time, pivoting around curves and reaching behind molars where oval heads tend to miss.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the iO head earns its keep. The combination of the round shape and the iO handle's micro-vibrations (up to 48,000 movements per minute) creates what Oral-B calls "3D cleaning action." In practice, this means the bristles oscillate, rotate, and pulse simultaneously. Your teeth feel noticeably smoother after the first use — that's the plaque being physically disrupted, not just swept.

One buyer noted: *"I switched from a Sonicare to the iO system and the difference in how my teeth feel after brushing is night and day. My hygienist commented on how much cleaner my gums looked at my last visit."*

The bristles deserve specific mention. They're soft enough for sensitive gums but dense enough to feel like they're doing work. The indicator bristles fade from blue to white over roughly three months, giving you a visual cue for replacement — a nice touch that takes the guesswork out of maintenance.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the reviews, three themes dominate:

**Gum health improvement.** Multiple reviewers report less bleeding and reduced sensitivity after switching. The round head seems to massage gum tissue more effectively than flat, oval designs.

**Ease of use.** The magnetic attachment is genuinely tool-free. No twisting, no clicking at odd angles — it just snaps on and stays put. One reviewer put it simply: *"Why doesn't every toothbrush work like this?"*

**Cleaner feel.** The consensus is that the iO head delivers a "just left the dentist" sensation that persists between brushings.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the downsides.

**The cost.** Replacement heads for the iO run roughly $10–12 each, and you'll need 3–4 per year. That's $30–48 annually just for brush heads. Compare that to standard Oral-B refills at half the price, and the premium becomes obvious.

**Compatibility lock-in.** This head only fits iO handles. If you own an older Oral-B Pro or Vitality model, this won't work — you're tied to the iO ecosystem, which also requires a premium handle purchase upfront.

**No travel case.** At this price point, a small protective cap or storage case would be reasonable. You'll need to buy separately or use the handle's own cap if it came with one.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You already own an iO toothbrush and want the genuine replacement head (third-party knockoffs exist but have mixed reviews on fit and bristle quality). You have sensitive gums or struggle with plaque buildup in hard-to-reach areas. You value the visual wear indicator that takes the guesswork out of when to replace.

**Skip it if:** You're on a strict budget for oral care consumables. You own a non-iO Oral-B handle. You prefer the larger cleaning surface of oval brush heads — some users find the round head feels small and takes getting used to.

## FAQ

**Q: How often should I replace the Oral-B iO brush head?**
A: Every 3 months, or sooner if the bristles splay. The indicator bristles fade from blue to white — when they're fully white, it's time to swap.

**Q: Does this head work with my Oral-B Pro 1000?**
A: No. The iO head uses a magnetic attachment that is incompatible with older Oral-B handles. You need an iO series toothbrush.

**Q: Are third-party compatible heads a good alternative?**
A: They're cheaper, but reviews are mixed. Some fit loosely, and bristle quality varies. If you've invested in the iO handle, the genuine heads are worth the premium for consistent performance.

## The Verdict

The Oral-B iO brush head is a well-engineered component that delivers on its promises — better gum-line cleaning, secure attachment, and a genuinely smoother finish. The 4.6-star rating reflects real user satisfaction. But it's a premium product with a premium price, and the compatibility lock-in means you're committing to this ecosystem long-term.

**Buy it if** you're already in the iO family and want the best-performing replacement head available. **Skip it if** you're shopping for a standalone brush head without owning the iO handle — you're not the intended buyer.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Excellent performance, but the price and exclusivity keep it from perfection.
