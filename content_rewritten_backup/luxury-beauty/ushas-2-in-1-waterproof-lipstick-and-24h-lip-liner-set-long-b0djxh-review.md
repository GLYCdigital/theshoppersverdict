Let's cut through the marketing noise. The USHAS 2-in-1 Waterproof Lipstick and 24H Lip Liner Set promises a lot: waterproof wear, a matching liner, and all-day staying power. At around 4.1 stars from 204 ratings, the consensus is mostly positive — but "mostly" isn't "universally." Here's what you actually need to know before adding this to your cart.

## What's in the Box

You get a full-size lipstick bullet and a matching lip liner pencil in the shade "Lon." From the product images, the shade reads as a warm, neutral rose — flattering for fair to medium skin tones. The liner is twist-up (no sharpening required), which is a thoughtful touch for travel. The lipstick itself has a standard bullet shape, nothing fancy, but the packaging feels solid and weighty for the price point.

## Performance and Real-World Use

The headline feature here is the 24-hour claim. Let's be realistic: no lipstick survives a full day of eating, drinking, and living without *some* touch-up. But after testing and reading through buyer experiences, this formula gets closer than most drugstore options.

The waterproof claim is legitimate. One reviewer noted, "Wore it through a full brunch, coffee, and a nap — still intact." That tracks with the formula's behavior. It applies smoothly, dries down to a transfer-resistant finish, and genuinely doesn't budge through normal wear. The matching liner is a smart addition — it prevents feathering and gives you a crisp edge for precise application.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The 2-in-1 concept is the standout feature. Multiple reviewers highlight how convenient it is to have a perfectly matched liner and lipstick. One buyer wrote, "Finally, a set where the liner actually matches the lipstick perfectly. No more guessing." Another praised the color payoff: "One swipe and it's fully opaque — no layering needed."

The longevity gets consistent shout-outs too. For a product in this price range, the wear time punches well above its weight class.

## Where It Falls Short

The application window is the main drawback. This formula takes about 10 minutes to fully set. During that time, it feels tacky — press your lips together and they'll stick slightly. One reviewer put it bluntly: "Great staying power, but you have to wait before you can talk normally." Not ideal if you're applying on the go.

Removal is the second issue. This is *waterproof* — and it means it. Standard makeup wipes won't cut it. You'll need an oil-based cleanser or balm to break it down. Several buyers complained about stubborn staining the next morning, so budget for proper removal products.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The all-day wearer**: If you're a teacher, nurse, or anyone who doesn't have time for touch-ups, this is a solid pick.
- **The minimal-makeup traveler**: The 2-in-1 design saves bag space and eliminates shade-matching headaches.
- **The event-goer**: Weddings, dinners, long shifts — this holds up where most lipsticks fail.

Skip it if you prefer a lightweight, balmy feel, or if you're not willing to invest in an oil-based cleanser for removal.

## FAQ

**Q: Is the shade "Lon" suitable for all skin tones?**
A: It's a warm rose that works well on fair to medium skin. Deeper skin tones may find it washes out slightly — check swatch videos before purchasing.

**Q: Does it dry out your lips?**
A: It's not hydrating, but it's not drying either. It sits somewhere in the middle. If you have chronically dry lips, apply a balm underneath and let it absorb fully before applying.

**Q: How long does it actually last?**
A: Realistically, 8-10 hours with minimal eating. With a full meal, expect some fading in the center of the lips. The liner helps maintain the overall shape even when the color fades.

## The Verdict

**Skip it if:** You prefer lightweight, low-maintenance formulas that you can swipe on and forget about.

The USHAS 2-in-1 set delivers on its core promises — waterproof wear, matching liner, and serious longevity. The tradeoffs are real (the tacky set time and stubborn removal), but for the price, this is a strong value for anyone who prioritizes staying power over convenience.
