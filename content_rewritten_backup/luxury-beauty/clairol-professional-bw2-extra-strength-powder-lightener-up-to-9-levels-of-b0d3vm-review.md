**FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Actually Ask**

**Q: Can I use this with 10 volume developer for a gentler lift?**
A: Technically, yes, but you’ll get minimal lift—maybe one to two levels. This formula is designed for maximum lift, so you’re better off using 20 or 30 volume for noticeable results. If you need gentle lightening, look for a dedicated high-lift color instead.

**Q: Does this work on previously colored hair?**
A: It can, but results are unpredictable. Existing color creates a barrier. You may need to use a color remover first, or accept that the lift won’t be even. Professional colorists often use this on previously lightened hair for touch-ups, but for full color correction, consult a pro.

**Q: How long should I leave it on?**
A: This depends on your developer and starting level. With 20 volume, check at 30 minutes. With 30–40 volume, check every 10–15 minutes for up to 45 minutes max. Never exceed 50 minutes total, and do a strand test first. Your hair’s health matters more than speed.
