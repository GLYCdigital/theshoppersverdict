If you're still wrestling with foil wraps and pure acetone to get gel polish off, you're wasting time. ZOYA Remove Plus claims to be the one-bottle solution for gel, acrylic, and regular polish — and after digging through 235 ratings and the chemistry behind it, I can tell you it mostly delivers. But "mostly" comes with caveats worth knowing before you hit buy.

## What's Actually in the Bottle

This isn't your drugstore acetone. The 3-in-1 formula swaps harsh pure acetone for a blend that includes conditioning agents like aloe vera and keratin protein. The idea is simple: dissolve polish without stripping your nails to paper-thin shreds in the process. The 4-ounce bottle has a pump top, which sounds convenient — and mostly is, but more on that later.

## Real-World Performance: Where It Shines

Here's where this product earns its keep. For **regular polish**, it's genuinely fast — one soaked cotton ball per nail, a few seconds of pressure, and the color wipes away clean. No scrubbing, no pink residue staining your cuticles.

For **gel polish**, it's a different story. You'll need to soak each nail for 10-15 minutes. The bottle's instructions suggest this, and buyers who follow them report success. One verified buyer put it plainly: *"I was skeptical because gel usually needs pure acetone. I soaked for about 12 minutes and the gel lifted right off with a cuticle pusher. No filing, no damage."*

The conditioning angle isn't marketing fluff either. Multiple reviewers noted their nails felt noticeably less brittle after switching from traditional removers. *"My nails used to peel after every gel removal. Three months with this and they're actually growing,"* one long-term user reported.

## Where It Falls Short

The pump is the weak link. Several reviewers mention it dispenses in an awkward spray rather than a steady stream, wasting product. It's fixable — pump into a small dish and dip cotton balls — but it's an annoyance at this price point.

Gel removal also requires more patience than the marketing suggests. If you're expecting a quick wipe-off, you'll be disappointed. This is a soak-and-wait product for gels, and buyers who skip the soak time end up frustrated.

## Who Should Buy This

**The at-home gel enthusiast** — if you do your own gel manicures, this eliminates the need for pure acetone, foil wraps, and the resulting nail damage. It's your best one-bottle solution.

**The frequent color-changer** — if you switch polish weekly (or more), the conditioning formula protects your nails from the cumulative damage of regular removers.

**The sensitive-nail crowd** — if acetone leaves your nails splitting and peeling, the aloe and protein infusion genuinely helps.

**Skip it if** you only remove polish a few times a year (drugstore remover suffices) or if you demand instant gel removal without soaking. This isn't that product.

## Three Questions Buyers Actually Ask

**Q: Can I use this for dip powder nails?**
A: Yes, but it takes longer — expect 15-20 minutes of soaking. Some buyers report success; others found dip powder more stubborn. If dip is your primary nail style, look for a dedicated dip remover instead.

**Q: Does it have a strong smell?**
A: It's acetone-based, so yes, there's a scent. But it's noticeably milder than pure acetone, and the added conditioning agents cut some of that harsh chemical odor. Crack a window and you'll be fine.

**Q: How long does one bottle last?**
A: For regular polish users, 3-4 months of weekly changes. Gel users will go through it faster — the soak method consumes more product per session.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a single product that handles every polish type while keeping your nails healthy. It's a premium product with premium results, especially for gel users tired of damaged nails.

**Skip it if** you're on a tight budget, only remove polish occasionally, or expect instant gel removal without soaking. The pump quirk and price point make it a luxury, not a necessity.

At 4.7 stars from 235 ratings, the consensus is clear: this is one of the better polish removers on the market. It's not perfect — the pump design and gel soak time prove that — but for nail health and versatility, it's hard to beat. **4.6/5 stars.**
