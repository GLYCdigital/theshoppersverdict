I'm going to be straight with you: the hair care aisle is a minefield of overpriced, over-perfumed bottles that promise "revitalization" and deliver a greasy scalp. So when Sun Bum — a brand better known for sunscreen — dropped a shampoo and conditioner duo, I was skeptical. After digging through 225 ratings and testing the formulas myself, here's the honest breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The set includes two 12-ounce bottles: one Revitalizing Shampoo, one Revitalizing Conditioner. Both are vegan, cruelty-free, and formulated without sulfates, parabens, or phthalates. The star ingredients are banana extract and coconut oil — a classic combo for moisture and shine. The packaging is clean and beachy, with a pump dispenser that's functional if not fancy. As shown in the product image, the bottles are a cheerful yellow-and-teal design that'll look fine on a shower shelf but won't win design awards.

**Performance: Does It Live Up to "Revitalizing"?**

I have shoulder-length, wavy hair with a tendency toward dryness at the ends and oil at the roots. The shampoo lathers surprisingly well for a sulfate-free formula — that's the coconut-derived surfactant doing its job. It rinses clean without that "squeaky" stripped feeling, but I'll note: on my finer-haired friends, it left the roots a touch dry. Pair it with the conditioner, and that issue disappears.

The conditioner is where this duo shines. It's thick enough to coat strands without being heavy, and it detangled my post-beach tangles in under a minute. Rinsing leaves hair feeling soft, not slick. My waves dried with minimal frizz and a natural, non-greasy shine that lasted two full days. For a drugstore-adjacent price point, that's genuinely impressive performance.

**What Buyers Keep Saying**

The 4.5-star average tracks with what I found. The most consistent praise centers on the scent. One reviewer wrote, *"I bought this for the banana smell and it did not disappoint — it's like a tropical vacation in a bottle."* Another said, *"My hair has never felt this soft after a beach trip — it actually fixed the saltwater damage."* Multiple buyers with color-treated hair confirmed it didn't strip their color, which is a big win for a sulfate-free formula.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be real about the downsides. The scent — a banana-forward, coconut-sweet blend — is not universally loved. One reviewer described it as *"like a melted banana Popsicle,"* and if you prefer citrus or floral hair products, this will be too much. The pump dispensers are also a minor annoyance: they're stiff on first use and tend to leak a bit down the bottle if you're not careful. And if you have very fine, straight hair, the shampoo alone will leave it feeling slightly straw-like — you absolutely need the conditioner to balance it.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is for the beach-goer, the swimmer, or anyone whose hair takes a beating from sun, salt, and chlorine. It's also a solid choice for wavy or curly hair types that need moisture without heaviness. If you have color-treated hair, the sulfate-free formula makes it a safe bet. Skip it if you prefer unscented products, have extremely oily roots, or want a 2-in-1 that does both jobs — this needs to be used as a pair.

**FAQ: What Shoppers Actually Ask**

**Is this safe for color-treated hair?**
Yes. The formula is sulfate-free and paraben-free, and multiple buyers with colored hair reported no fading after weeks of use. That said, always do a patch test if you're switching products.

**Does it smell like sunscreen?**
No — it's banana and coconut, more like a piña colada than Coppertone. The scent lingers subtly in hair for a few hours but doesn't overwhelm.

**Will it weigh down fine hair?**
The shampoo alone can be slightly drying on fine hair, but the conditioner is light enough to use sparingly. Start with a dime-sized amount and adjust.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want a gentle, effective cleanser-conditioner pair that protects color, tames frizz, and leaves hair soft with a tropical scent. It's especially good for beach or pool season.

**Skip it if:** You hate sweet scents, have very fine hair that needs lightweight-only products, or you're looking for a single-bottle solution.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The performance punches above its price point, and the minor flaws (pump design, polarizing scent) don't outweigh the results. This is one of the better value hair care duos I've tested this year.
