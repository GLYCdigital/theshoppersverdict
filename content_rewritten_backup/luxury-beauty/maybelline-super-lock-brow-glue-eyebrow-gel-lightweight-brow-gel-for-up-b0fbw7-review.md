Brow gels are a dime a dozen, but finding one that actually locks your arches in place without turning them into crunchy caterpillars? That's rarer. The Maybelline Super Lock Brow Glue has racked up 225 ratings at 4.5 stars, and after digging through what buyers are saying, here's the real story.

**What you're actually getting**

The tube is compact — roughly the size of a standard mascara — and the wand is the star of the show. It's a precision spoolie with shorter bristles on one side that catch fine hairs along the brow edge and longer bristles on the other for the main body. The gel itself is clear, so it works whether you're going natural with just a brush-up or layering over a brow pencil for a fuller look.

**How it performs in the real world**

Here's where this product earns its keep. The formula dries down to a flexible, natural finish — no crunch, no white flakes. One reviewer put it plainly: "I've tried every high-end brow gel from ABH to Glossier, and this holds my stubborn downward-growing hairs better than any of them." That tracks with what I see in the ingredient profile: it uses a film-forming polymer blend that creates a physical lock on the hairs rather than relying on waxes that melt or stiffen.

The hold is genuinely impressive. Most buyers report 10-12 hours of staying power through normal wear — workdays, commutes, the occasional nap. But here's the catch: it's not waterproof. If you're sweating through a workout or get caught in rain, the hold breaks down. That's a tradeoff at this price point, not a dealbreaker.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The lightweight feel comes up constantly. Multiple reviewers note they "forget they're wearing anything" — that's the flex finish doing its job. The precision wand also gets love: "The small spoolie side is perfect for my sparse outer corners. I can actually style those tiny hairs instead of just brushing them flat." 

**Where it falls short**

The learning curve is real. If you apply too much, the gel stays tacky for several minutes and can look wet. You need a light hand — one swipe, maybe two if your brows are unruly. Some buyers also mention that if you rub your eyes or touch your brows throughout the day, the hold weakens faster than a standard wax-based gel. And again, zero waterproofing means this is a fair-weather product.

**Who should buy this**

- **The laminated-brow devotee**: If you've got brow lamination or naturally stubborn hairs that point every direction, this is your fix.
- **The makeup minimalist**: Clear gel, natural finish, no glitter or tint — just groomed brows.
- **The oily-skin sufferer**: Unlike wax-based gels that slide around on oily brows, this film-forming formula stays put.

**Skip it if** you need all-day sweatproof wear, prefer a tinted product, or have a tendency to over-apply (you'll waste product and hate the tacky feel).

**FAQ**

*Q: Can I use this over brow powder or pencil?*  
Yes, and most reviewers do. Let your makeup set for a minute, then apply the gel in short upward strokes. It won't smear or drag your product.

*Q: How do I remove it?*  
Warm water and your regular cleanser. It breaks down easily — no special remover needed. That's actually a plus compared to stubborn wax-based gels.

*Q: Does it work on thin brows?*  
Yes, but manage expectations. It styles the hairs you have for a fuller, brushed-up look. It won't create density where there's no hair.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want salon-style laminated brows without the salon price and don't mind a small learning curve. **Skip it if** you need waterproof performance or prefer a tinted brow product.

At 4.6/5, this is one of the best drugstore brow gels I've seen — and it quietly outperforms products at triple the price. Just start with a light hand.
