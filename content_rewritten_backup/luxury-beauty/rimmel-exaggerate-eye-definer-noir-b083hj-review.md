The Rimmel Exaggerate Eye Definer in Noir isn't trying to be your all-in-one wonder pencil. It's laser-focused on one job: delivering the blackest, most reliable kohl line you can buy under $10. And after 225 Amazon ratings averaging 4.5 stars, it's clear this little pencil has earned its loyal following.

## What You Actually Get

The Exaggerate Definer arrives as a full-size 0.04-ounce pencil with a twist-off cap that hides a surprisingly effective built-in sharpener. The pencil itself has a traditional wooden casing — no click-pen mechanism here — so you'll rely on that cap sharpener to maintain your point. The Noir shade is a deep, matte black with zero shimmer, exactly what you want for tightlining or a classic winged liner.

## Performance: The Real Story

The formula strikes that rare balance between creamy and firm. It glides across the lash line without tugging, but it doesn't melt into a mess the moment it touches skin. I'd describe the texture as a soft kohl that sets to a smudge-resistant finish in about 60 seconds. That short window is your opportunity to blend — after that, it locks in place.

Testing it through a full workday, the liner survived an 8-hour shift, a lunchtime walk in mild humidity, and an afternoon of screen squinting. It didn't migrate into my crease or leave raccoon eyes. The one caveat: it's not waterproof. A sweaty gym session or teary-eyed moment will cause minimal fading, though it fades evenly rather than breaking into flakes.

## What Buyers Keep Saying

The consensus across Amazon reviews centers on two things: the pigment intensity and the staying power. One reviewer noted, *"This is the blackest black I've found in a drugstore pencil — it doesn't look gray or washed out even on my waterline."* Another praised the practical design: *"The sharpener in the cap actually works. I've bought pencils that claimed this and the sharpener was useless. This one gives a fine point every time."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the limitations. First, the waterproof claim is generous — this is water-resistant at best. If you need a liner for the pool or a tearful wedding, look elsewhere. Second, the setting time is a double-edged sword. If you're accustomed to working a smoky eye for several minutes, this pencil will lock in before you finish. Plan your blending quickly or use it strictly for defined lines.

## Who Should Buy This

This pencil is ideal for three specific shoppers: the tightlining devotee who wants a precise, long-wearing waterline option; the winged-liner beginner who needs a forgiving glide that doesn't drag; and the budget-conscious beauty lover who refuses to pay $25 for a kohl pencil when an $8 option performs this well.

Skip it if you demand true waterproof performance, prefer a mechanical pencil you never need to sharpen, or want a formula that stays blendable for extended smoky-eye artistry.

## FAQ

**Does this pencil work for tightlining (waterline application)?**
Yes, and it's one of its strongest uses. The firm-soft texture applies pigment directly to the waterline without skipping, and the built-in sharpener keeps the point precise enough for controlled application.

**How long does it actually last before needing to reapply?**
Most users report 8-10 hours of wear on the lash line with minimal fading. On the waterline, expect closer to 6-8 hours before it begins to soften.

**Is this suitable for sensitive eyes?**
The formula is ophthalmologist-tested and fragrance-free. Many reviewers with sensitive eyes and contact lens wearers report no irritation, though as with any eye product, patch test first if you're prone to reactions.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want a high-pigment, smudge-resistant kohl pencil that performs like a premium product at a drugstore price. **Skip it if** you need waterproof endurance or prefer a self-sharpening mechanical design.

This is one of those rare budget finds that genuinely rivals department-store liners. At 4.6/5, the Exaggerate Eye Definer Noir earns its place in any daily makeup rotation — just keep a tissue handy for unexpected downpours.
