**Is the ColourPop Lippie Lip Pencil Vault the best bang-for-your-buck lip product on Amazon right now? At $29 for 24 full-size pencils, the math is compelling. But quantity doesn't always equal quality. Here's the honest breakdown after working through this set.**

## What You Actually Get

The vault contains 24 full-size Lippie Pencils — the same 0.04 oz formula ColourPop sells individually for $7-8 each. The shades span the spectrum: warm nudes like *BFF* and *Contour*, pinks like *Sike* and *Screenshot*, bold reds including *Trust Me* and *Creeper*, and deeper berries like *Bull Chic* and *Love Bug*. As shown in the product image, the pencils are arranged in a rainbow gradient, which makes shade hunting easy.

Each pencil is sharpenable (not twist-up), so you'll need a sharpener — the vault doesn't include one. That's a small oversight worth flagging.

## Performance and Wear

The formula is where ColourPop has always excelled, and this set doesn't disappoint. The texture is creamy but not slippery — it glides on smoothly without tugging at delicate lip skin. You can wear these alone for a matte, blotted look or use them as a base underneath lipstick to extend wear time. I tested *Creeper* under a gloss for six hours through coffee, lunch, and a long meeting. The liner held its color while the gloss faded — exactly what you want from a liner.

Pigmentation is genuinely high. Most shades deliver full opacity in one pass, which matters for darker colors where patchiness is common. The lighter nudes (*Contour*, *BFF*) need two passes for even coverage on medium-to-deep skin, but that's consistent with most liner formulas in this price range.

## What Buyers Are Saying

Across 225 Amazon ratings at 4.5 stars, the pattern is consistent. One verified buyer noted: *"I bought these for the shade range alone — 24 colors for the price of three singles. The formula is creamy and doesn't dry out my lips like other liners I've tried."* Another wrote: *"Great pigmentation, but bring your own sharpener. I didn't realize they weren't twist-ups."*

The most common criticisms center on packaging and shade selection. A few reviewers mentioned that the cardboard sleeve doesn't protect the pencils well during travel, and some lighter shades didn't work for deeper complexions.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the tradeoffs. First, no sharpener included — that's an extra $5-8 purchase. Second, the shade range leans toward lighter and medium tones. If you have deep skin, you'll find maybe 6-8 shades that work as liners; the rest are better for lip art or mixing. Third, the packaging is functional but not premium. This is a value set, not a luxury presentation.

One more thing: these are not transfer-proof. They're long-wearing, but they'll leave a trace on coffee cups and mask edges. That's expected for a pencil formula, but worth knowing if you're expecting budge-proof wear.

## Who Should Buy This

This vault is ideal for three types of shoppers:

- **Beginners** building out a lip collection without spending $100+ on individual liners
- **Makeup artists or content creators** who need a broad shade range for different clients and looks
- **Experimenters** who want to try bold colors without committing to full-price singles

Skip it if you exclusively use twist-up liners (the sharpening requirement will annoy you) or if you only reach for one or two signature shades — singles are cheaper in that case.

Absolutely. The formula is pigmented enough to wear alone as a matte lip. Add a balm or gloss on top for comfort if you have dry lips.
