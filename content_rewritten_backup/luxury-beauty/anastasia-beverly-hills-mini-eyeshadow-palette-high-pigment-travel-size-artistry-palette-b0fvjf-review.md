# Anastasia Beverly Hills Mini Eyeshadow Palette: Big Pigment, Small Package

Let's be honest — most mini palettes are a compromise. You trade performance for portability, and you usually end up with chalky shadows that barely show up. The Anastasia Beverly Hills Mini Eyeshadow Palette flips that expectation. With a 4.6-star average across 229 ratings, this compact delivers the same high-pigment formula that made ABH famous, just in a travel-friendly format.

## What You Actually Get

This is a curated selection of shades — typically five or six pans depending on the specific color story you choose — housed in a slim, magnetic-closure case. As shown in the product image, the palette is roughly palm-sized, making it substantially more compact than the brand's full-size palettes. The pans are smaller than standard ABH shadows, but they're not joke-sized. You're getting enough product for months of regular use.

The shade range varies by variant, but most options include a mix of warm neutrals, a shimmer or two, and a deeper crease shade. The formula is the same signature ABH blend — finely milled, highly pigmented, and incredibly blendable.

## Performance and Build Quality

The shadows perform like their full-size counterparts. One swipe delivers serious color payoff — no building needed for most shades. The mattes blend out smoothly without turning muddy, and the shimmers apply evenly without chunkiness. I tested fallout during application, and it's minimal compared to other high-pigment brands.

The palette itself feels sturdy. The magnetic closure snaps securely, and the case doesn't feel flimsy or cheap. That said, there's no mirror inside, which is a notable omission for a palette marketed for travel. You'll need to bring your own.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

Across the 229 ratings, a clear pattern emerges. One reviewer noted, "These shadows are so pigmented I barely need to dip my brush in — a little goes a long way." Another praised the blendability: "I've used high-end palettes that don't blend this easily. It's like butter."

Portability is a recurring theme too. Multiple reviewers mention taking it on flights, weekend trips, and in work bags. The compact size genuinely makes it a grab-and-go option without sacrificing quality.

## Where It Falls Short

The biggest limitation is the shade range. You're not getting a complete look in one palette — you'll likely need a transition shade from another palette or a single shadow to round things out. If you prefer doing your entire eye look from one compact, this isn't it.

The lack of a mirror is also a real miss. For a product clearly designed for travel, not having a mirror feels like an oversight. You'll need to pack one separately.

Finally, the price per ounce is higher than full-size palettes. You're paying a premium for the convenience of a smaller format, which is fine if portability matters to you — but know that going in.

## Who Should Buy This

This palette is ideal for three specific types of shoppers:

**The frequent traveler**: If you're on planes weekly and want quality shadows that won't hog your carry-on space, this is a solid pick.

**The minimalist**: If you want a curated selection of shades you'll actually use, without the overwhelm of a 14-pan palette, this delivers.

**The ABH formula loyalist**: If you love the brand's full-size palettes but don't need another giant compact, this gives you the same formula in a smarter format.

Skip it if you want an all-in-one palette for complete looks, or if you prefer a mirror in your compact.

## FAQ

**Q: Are these the same shadows as the full-size ABH palettes?**
A: Yes, the formula is identical. The pans are just smaller and the shade selection is curated differently.

**Q: How many shades are included?**
A: It varies by variant, but most versions include 5-6 shades with a mix of mattes and shimmers.

**Q: Does it work for oily eyelids?**
A: Yes, but you'll want a primer. The formula performs well, but no shadow holds up all day on oily lids without a base.

## The Verdict

**Buy it if** you want ABH's premium formula in a travel-friendly size and don't mind pairing it with other shadows for complete looks.

**Skip it if** you need an all-in-one palette with a mirror, or if you prefer larger pans for the price.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The missing mirror and limited shade range hold it back from perfection, but the formula quality alone justifies the price. This is a genuinely well-made mini palette that doesn't feel like a compromise.
