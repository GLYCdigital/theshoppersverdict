**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this on wet hair?**
Yes, but it’s better on damp or dry hair. Wet fine hair is fragile, and boar bristles can cause tugging. Towel-dry first, then brush.

**Q: How do I clean it?**
Remove loose hair with a comb after each use. Weekly, wash the bristles with mild shampoo, rinse bristle-side down, and air dry flat. Never soak the wooden handle.

**Q: Does it actually help with hair growth?**
