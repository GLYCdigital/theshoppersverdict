Let me be direct: the hair extensions market is a minefield of misleading labels and overpriced synthetic blends. So when a product claims "100% Brazilian unprocessed virgin" at this price point, my skepticism kicks in hard. After digging through 200 ratings and real customer experiences, here's the honest breakdown.

**What you actually get**

The package contains one bundle of hair, typically 3.5 ounces, in your chosen length — I tested the 10-inch and 14-inch versions. The hair arrives in a plastic bag with a simple paper band. No fancy packaging, no closure, no frontal. That's it. The texture is tight, defined kinky curls that mimic natural 4A/4B hair patterns. The weft is machine-sewn and dense — roughly 4 inches across with consistent thickness throughout.

**The real-world performance**

This is where Original Queen earns its keep. The unprocessed claim holds up: cuticle alignment is correct (hair flows downward from root to tip), and the curls respond beautifully to water and leave-in conditioner. One buyer noted, *"I've bought 'virgin' hair three times before that turned out to be processed within weeks. This one actually behaves like my own hair — it shrinks, it coils, it holds moisture."* That's the experience most reviewers echo.

Shedding is minimal — maybe 10-15 strands per wash session after the initial install. Compare that to cheaper options that shed fistfuls in the first week. The curl pattern holds through heat styling (though you shouldn't need it) and multiple wash cycles. Several reviewers report getting 6-8 months of wear with proper maintenance, including re-installation.

**Where it falls short**

Now the honest part. The most consistent complaint: a chemical odor on arrival. One reviewer described it bluntly: *"Smells like a perm solution mixed with barnyard. I had to soak it in vinegar water three times before I could install it."* This isn't a dealbreaker, but it's a real inconvenience — budget a day for deodorizing before your install appointment.

The second issue is length consistency. Multiple reviewers report receiving hair that's 1-2 inches shorter than labeled. One said, *"Ordered 16 inches, got maybe 14. Not the end of the world, but when you're paying for specific lengths, accuracy matters."* If your style depends on exact length, measure immediately upon arrival and be prepared to contact customer service.

Finally, the tangling. While shedding is low, the kinky texture does mat at the ends after 4-6 weeks of daily wear, especially if you're not diligent about moisture and detangling. This isn't a defect — it's the nature of unprocessed curly hair — but it requires commitment.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for women with natural 4A-4C texture who want a seamless blend without chemical processing. It's also solid for protective stylists who prioritize authenticity over convenience. If you're someone who wants a sleek, straight look or needs a pre-stretched curl pattern, look elsewhere — this hair wants to be curly.

Skip it if you're a beginner with extensions, if you need exact length precision, or if you're sensitive to odors. The maintenance curve is real.

**FAQ**

*Does this hair shed excessively?*
No, but expect minor shedding (10-15 strands per wash) after the initial install. If you're seeing clumps, that's a quality issue — return it.

*Can I dye this hair?*
Yes, since it's unprocessed, it takes color well. But go to a professional — home dye jobs on virgin hair can still damage the cuticle.

*How long does it last?*
With proper care, 6-8 months. Some reviewers report a year with rotation between two sets.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if:** You want authentic unprocessed texture that blends invisibly with natural curls and you're willing to handle the initial smell and maintenance routine.

**Skip it if:** You need exact length guarantees, want a low-maintenance option, or prefer pre-stretched textures.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The authenticity and quality are real, but the odor issue and length inconsistency keep it from a perfect score. At this price point for genuine virgin hair, it's a solid value — just go in with realistic expectations.
