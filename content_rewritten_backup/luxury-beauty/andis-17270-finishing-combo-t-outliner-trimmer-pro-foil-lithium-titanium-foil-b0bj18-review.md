## The barber-shop duo that actually earns its place on your counter

Most "combo" grooming kits are a bait-and-switch — one solid tool, one throwaway. The Andis 17270 Finishing Combo sidesteps that trap by pairing two legitimate workhorses: the legendary T-Outliner trimmer and the Pro Foil Li foil shaver. This isn't a starter set for someone who trims once a month. This is the kit you reach for when a clean neckline or a smooth, razor-free finish actually matters.

As shown in the product image, you're getting the classic black-and-chrome T-Outliner alongside the sleeker Pro Foil Li. Both are compact, weighing in around 8-10 ounces each — light enough for precision work, substantial enough to feel like they won't shatter when dropped on a bathroom tile.

## What's actually in the box

Open it up and you get the T-Outliner trimmer, the Pro Foil Li shaver, two charging adapters, and a set of four attachment combs for the outliner (sizes 000 through 1). That's it. No storage case, no blade oil, no cleaning brush. For a kit that positions itself as "finishing" equipment, the lack of a basic maintenance kit is a small but annoying omission.

The T-Outliner runs corded or cordless — a genuine flex, since the lithium-ion battery holds up for about 90 minutes of continuous use. The Pro Foil Li is fully cordless with a 60-minute runtime and charges in roughly two hours. Both are dual-voltage, so international travelers won't need a separate converter.

## Real-world performance

The T-Outliner's carbon-steel blades are the headline act. They're adjustable — you can zero-gap them for razor-close fades or back them off for a more forgiving trim. I've used it for crisp necklines, beard outlines, and even detailed work around ears. The motor is a rotary type with enough torque to power through thick hair without snagging or pulling. One reviewer put it bluntly: "These are the same trimmers my barber uses. I bought them for home touch-ups and they're identical in performance."

The Pro Foil Li is the sleeper hit. The titanium-coated foil glides smoothly and lifts flat-lying hair — the kind that clings to your neck after a haircut. It's not a full face shaver; you're not replacing your daily razor for a clean-shaven look. But for finishing edges, cleaning up stray hairs, and maintaining a sharp line between trims, it's excellent. The foil is gentle enough for sensitive skin, which several buyers confirmed.

One consistent praise point: the battery life. Multiple reviewers noted they get several weeks of use between charges with regular trimming. A 4.5-star average across 225 ratings tells you the consensus is strong — this isn't a polarizing product.

## Where it falls short

Heat is the most common complaint. The T-Outliner runs warm — noticeably warm after 10-15 minutes of continuous use. It's not burn-your-skin hot, but if you're doing a full beard sculpt in one sitting, you'll feel it. The Pro Foil Li runs cooler, but the foil can tug slightly on longer hair if you haven't trimmed first.

The missing accessories are a real oversight. No oil, no brush, no case. At this price point, you expect a basic maintenance kit. You'll need to spend $10-15 extra on blade care supplies within the first month.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if** you're a home groomer who wants barber-grade results without booking weekly appointments, or a traveling barber who needs a reliable backup kit. **Skip it if** you only need a quick beard trim once a month — a single corded trimmer will do the job for half the price.

## FAQ

**Is the T-Outliner good for sensitive skin?**
The carbon-steel blades are sharp, which actually helps reduce irritation — dull blades cause inflammation. But the heat buildup can bother very sensitive skin. Use a pre-trim oil if you're prone to razor burn.

**Can I use the Pro Foil Li on my head?**
Yes, for maintaining a shaved head or cleaning up patches, but it's not designed for full-head shaving of long hair. Trim first, then use the foil to finish.

**How often should I replace the blades?**
With regular use and proper oiling, expect 6-12 months before the T-Outliner blades need replacement. The Pro Foil's foil and cutter typically last 12-18 months.

## The Verdict

The Andis 17270 Finishing Combo is a rare thing: a grooming kit where both tools earn their keep. It's not cheap, and the missing maintenance supplies are a genuine gripe. But if you want precision line work and a smooth finish at home, this is the kit to beat.

**Buy it if** you take your grooming seriously and want professional-grade results. **Skip it if** you're a casual trimmer — you'll be paying for capability you'll never use.
