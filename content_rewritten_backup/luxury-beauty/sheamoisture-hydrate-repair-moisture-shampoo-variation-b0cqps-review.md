### What You're Actually Getting

This isn't a minimalist formula. The star players are **shea butter** (for deep moisture), **argon oil** (for shine and elasticity), and **sea kelp** (for strengthening). The texture is noticeably thicker than standard shampoos — almost a creamy lotion — which means a little goes a long way. A 13 oz bottle lasts roughly 6-8 weeks with 2-3 washes per week.

### Real-World Performance: The Good, The Bad, The Tangled

**The Good:** This shampoo delivers on its core promise. The lather is rich and creamy (it's sulfate-free, so don't expect a foamy explosion), and it rinses clean without that squeaky, stripped feeling. The immediate effect is noticeable: hair feels softer and looks shinier right after the first wash. The sea kelp is the unsung hero here — it genuinely reinforces the hair shaft, making strands feel less fragile when wet.

**The Bad:** The protein content is high. If you have low-porosity hair (which doesn't absorb moisture easily) or hair that's already protein-sensitive, this shampoo can leave your strands feeling stiff and brittle. You'll need to follow up with a heavy, hydrating conditioner — this is *not* a standalone product. It works best as a 1-2 punch with the matching Hydrate & Repair conditioner.

**The Honestly Frustrating:** The packaging is a squeeze bottle, but the thick formula makes it a workout to get out near the end. You'll be shaking it like a ketchup bottle. Minor, but annoying.

### Where It Falls Short (Honest Tradeoffs)

- **Not for Fine Hair:** If your hair is thin or delicate, this will weigh it down. It's formulated for thick, coarse, or heavily damaged textures.
- **Scent is Strong:** The shea butter and kelp blend has a distinct, earthy fragrance. It's not bad, but it's not subtle either. If you're sensitive to smells, test it before committing.
- **Clarifying Power:** It's a repair shampoo, not a clarifying one. If you have heavy product buildup, this won't fix that. Use a dedicated clarifying shampoo once a month.
