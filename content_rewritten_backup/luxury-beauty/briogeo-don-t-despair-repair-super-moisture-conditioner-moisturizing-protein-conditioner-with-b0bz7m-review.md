**Briogeo Don't Despair, Repair! Super Moisture Conditioner Review: Does This 4.5-Star Repair Treatment Actually Work?**

You've seen the hype. The clean-beauty crowd swears by Briogeo's "Don't Despair, Repair!" line, and this Super Moisture Conditioner holds a solid 4.5-star average across 225 Amazon ratings. But at a luxury price point, the question is simple: does it deliver results that justify the splurge? After digging into the ingredient list, customer feedback, and real-world performance, here's the honest breakdown.

**What You Actually Get**

The 8-ounce bottle arrives with a pump dispenser — a small but meaningful detail. You're not wrestling with a squeeze tube in a wet shower. The conditioner itself is a rich, opaque cream with a subtle, natural scent. No synthetic perfume here; it smells like the botanical extracts inside, which is refreshing.

The formula leans on algae extract and rosehip oil for moisture, plus a proprietary repair complex of amino acids and plant-derived proteins. Notably, it's free of sulfates, silicones, and phthalates. That matters if you're trying to avoid buildup on fine or color-treated hair.

**Real-World Performance**

Here's where this conditioner earns its keep. The texture is thick — almost like a deep treatment mask — and it coats strands thoroughly. You don't need much; a quarter-sized amount covers shoulder-length hair. After rinsing, hair feels soft immediately, not slippery with silicone residue.

The protein component is the star. Customers repeatedly mention reduced breakage and fewer split ends after consistent use. One reviewer wrote, "My color-treated hair has never felt this healthy. It's been three weeks and I've noticed way less shedding in the shower." Another noted, "I have 2C curls and this is one of the few conditioners that actually moisturizes without weighing my waves down."

That's the sweet spot: it delivers both moisture and structure, which is rare in a single conditioner. Most products do one or the other.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be direct about the downsides. First, the price. This is a luxury product, and you're paying for the clean ingredient list and brand reputation. If you go through conditioner quickly, the 8-ounce size will feel small for the cost.

Second, the thick texture can be finicky. If you have very thick or coarse hair, you'll need to add water to distribute it evenly — otherwise you'll end up with concentrated patches. A few reviewers mentioned this exact issue, noting they had to work harder to spread it through longer hair.

Third, the protein content means it's not ideal for daily use if your hair is already protein-sensitive. Overuse can lead to stiffness. Most users find 2-3 times per week is the sweet spot.

**Who Should Buy This**

This conditioner is built for specific hair profiles. Buy it if you have:
- Color-treated or heat-damaged hair that needs repair
- Fine to medium hair that gets weighed down by heavy conditioners
- Wavy or curly hair looking for moisture without silicone buildup

Skip it if you have very coarse, dense hair (you'll need something richer) or if you're protein-sensitive. Also skip if you're on a tight budget — there are solid drugstore options, though they won't match this ingredient quality.

**FAQ**

*Is this conditioner safe for color-treated hair?* Yes. It's sulfate-free and color-safe. Multiple reviewers with balayage and highlights reported no fading after weeks of use.

*Does it help with frizz?* Indirectly, yes. The moisture and protein balance smooths the cuticle, which reduces frizz over time. It's not a styling product, so don't expect instant sleekness.

*How often should I use it?* Most users get best results 2-3 times per week. Daily use may overshoot the protein balance for some hair types.

**The Verdict**

Briogeo's Super Moisture Conditioner is one of those rare products that delivers on its promises. The 4.5-star rating holds up — this is a genuinely effective repair conditioner with a clean formula that performs. It's not cheap, and it won't suit every hair type, but for the right buyer it's a legitimate upgrade.

**Buy it if** you have damaged, color-treated, or fine hair and want visible improvement in softness and strength. **Skip it if** you have coarse, dense hair or need a budget-friendly everyday conditioner.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — a premium product that earns its price tag for the right user.
