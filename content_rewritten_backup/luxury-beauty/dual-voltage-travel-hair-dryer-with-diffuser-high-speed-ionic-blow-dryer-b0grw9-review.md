## Where It Falls Short

Let’s be honest about the flaws. First, it’s louder than a full-size dryer—you’ll hear it through thin hotel walls. Second, the cool-shot button requires holding it down continuously; there’s no lock mechanism, which gets annoying when you’re styling. Third, the cord is only six feet, which feels short in hotel bathrooms where outlets are awkwardly placed. Finally, the heat settings are limited—high and low with a warm/cool toggle, but no true medium heat. For fine or heat-sensitive hair, the low setting might still feel too hot.

**Q: Is the diffuser big enough for thick curly hair?**  
The bowl is about 4 inches wide with flexible prongs. It works well for medium curls, but for very dense, long hair, you’ll need to section it—it’s not a salon-sized attachment.

**Q: How hot does the cool shot actually get?**  
It’s cool-ish, not truly cold. It’s fine for setting curls, but don’t expect an ice-cold blast like a pro dryer.
