**The $60 salon treatment in a bottle — or just expensive shampoo? Let's break it down.**

You've paid $450 for a Brazilian Blowout at the salon. Six weeks later, the frizz is creeping back. The stylist told you to use "professional products" — but did she mean the $28 shampoo at the front desk? Here's the honest truth about the Brazilian Blowout Anti-Frizz Shampoo & Conditioner set and whether it's worth your money.

**What you actually get**

This is a 2-count set: one 12oz shampoo and one 12oz conditioner. Both are sulfate-free, which isn't marketing fluff — sulfates are what strip keratin treatments from your hair. The shampoo uses a mild cocamidopropyl betaine base (the same gentle cleanser in baby shampoo) with acai and annatto extracts. The conditioner leans on keratin amino acids and murumuru butter, a Brazilian ingredient that's richer than shea butter.

**Performance and real-world use**

The shampoo lathers moderately — don't expect the thick foam of drugstore brands. That's normal for sulfate-free formulas. It gets hair clean without that "squeaky" feeling that signals your cuticle has been stripped. One pump covers a decent section.

The conditioner is where this set earns its keep. It's thick, almost buttery, and a quarter-size amount detangles even coarse, color-treated hair. Rinse it out and your hair feels smooth — not silicone-slick, but genuinely conditioned. When you blow-dry, frizz is noticeably reduced. Not eliminated, but tamed.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.5-star rating across 225 ratings is legit. One verified buyer wrote: *"I've tried every 'keratin-safe' shampoo at Target — this is the only one that keeps my blowout fresh for 2+ weeks past when I'd normally schedule my next appointment."* Another said: *"I have fine, wavy hair and this doesn't flatten it. **Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. First, the scent. It's a strong, floral-citrus fragrance that smells like a perm solution. It fades after a few hours, but if you're sensitive to fragrance, this will bother you. Second, the bottle size. At 12oz each, this set lasts about 5-6 weeks with daily washing. For $30+ per set, that's not cheap — though it's still less than one salon treatment.

Third, if you have very thick or curly hair, the shampoo alone might not feel cleansing enough. You may need to shampoo twice on heavy product days.

**FAQ: What shoppers actually ask**

**Q: Can I use this with color-treated hair?**
Yes. It's sulfate-free, which protects both keratin and color. Multiple reviewers with balayage and highlights report no fading.

**Q: Does it replace my regular shampoo for good?**
If you have a keratin treatment, yes — it's designed for that. If not, you can alternate with your regular shampoo on days you need deeper cleansing.

**Q: Is it safe for daily use?**
Yes, the mild surfactant base is gentle enough. But if your hair is fine, you might prefer every-other-day washing to avoid build-up.

**Buy it if:** You have a keratin treatment or deal with persistent frizz and want salon-quality maintenance without salon prices.

**Skip it if:** You're on a tight budget, dislike strong scents, or have very fine hair that gets weighed down easily.
