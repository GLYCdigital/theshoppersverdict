Let's be blunt: most "body butters" are glorified lotions with a fancy label. This one from Burt's Bees is different — but it's also not for everyone. Here's what 229 Amazon reviewers at a 4.6 average are actually saying, and what you need to know before you buy.

## What's in the Tube

You get 6.5 ounces of dense, whipped butter in a squeeze tube. As shown in the product image, the texture is thick and opaque — this is not a pump-lotion situation. The ingredient list is refreshingly honest: shea butter leads, followed by coconut oil and cocoa seed butter. The "tropical fruit" comes from natural mango and papaya extracts, and the scent is more subtle than the name suggests. No parabens, no phthalates, no synthetic fragrances.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this on dry winter skin and post-shower summer skin. The first thing you notice: it's rich. A dime-sized amount covers both hands, and a quarter-sized amount handles an entire arm. It takes about 45 seconds of rubbing to fully absorb, which is longer than a lotion but shorter than most body butters I've tried.

The hydration genuinely lasts. Eight hours after application, my elbows still felt moisturized — not just coated. One reviewer put it simply: *"This is the only thing that touches my husband's alligator-skin elbows. He actually asks for it now."* Another noted, *"I've tried every luxury body butter from Sephora and this beats them at half the price."*

The scent is the sleeper hit. It smells like a piña colada that grew up and got a job — tropical but not childish. It fades to a whisper within 10 minutes, so it won't clash with your perfume.

## Where It Falls Short

The texture is the main tradeoff. If you're looking for a quick-slide lotion you can apply and pull jeans on immediately, this isn't it. You need to wait a minute for full absorption. One reviewer warned: *"Great product but if you're impatient, you'll feel sticky for a few minutes. Worth the wait, but know that going in."*

The tube packaging is also a minor annoyance. Once you get product on your hands, the tube gets slippery. I lost a glob of expensive butter down the sink drain in week one. Squeeze from the bottom and keep a towel handy.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You have chronically dry skin, live in a dry climate, or want a natural alternative to petroleum-based lotions. It's excellent for post-shower full-body application, especially on legs, elbows, and feet. It also makes a thoughtful gift — the tropical scent and natural positioning give it a premium feel without the luxury price tag.

**Skip it if:** You hate thick textures, need a grab-and-go lotion for your desk, or are allergic to coconut-derived ingredients. Sensitive noses should also be cautious — the natural fruit extracts are mild, but they're present.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is this good for eczema-prone skin?**
A: Several reviewers with eczema reported positive results, but shea butter is a known trigger for some people with nut allergies. Patch test on your inner arm first. The formula is fragrance-free in the traditional sense (natural extracts only), which helps.

**Q: How does this compare to the classic Burt's Bees hand salve?**
A: The salve is heavier and more of a spot treatment. This butter is lighter, spreads over larger areas, and absorbs faster. If you use the salve on cracked hands, this is a good all-over companion.

**Q: Does it leave a greasy film on your phone or keyboard?**
A: Not after the initial absorption period. Wait 60 seconds after applying, and you're good to type, text, or handle doorknobs without leaving fingerprints.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5** — This is a legitimately good body butter that does what it promises. It's not for the impatient, and the packaging could be better, but the formula is honest, effective, and priced fairly for what you get.

**Buy it if** you want real, lasting hydration from natural ingredients and don't mind spending an extra minute rubbing it in. **Skip it if** you prefer lightweight lotions or need something you can apply and dress in 30 seconds flat.

For the price, this outperforms most department store body butters. Your elbows will know the difference by day three.
