Here's a hard truth about the "gel effect" nail polish category: most of it is just thick, gloopy lacquer with a glossy top coat. The Nails Inc It's Topless Gel Polish 4-in-1 is different — but not in the way the marketing suggests. This is a genuinely clever hybrid that replaces your base coat, color, top coat, and strengthening treatment with one bottle. Whether that's a miracle or a compromise depends entirely on how you do your nails.

**What's actually in the box**

You get one 10ml bottle of the "It's Topless" shade — a sheer, warm-toned nude with subtle pink undertones that reads like a polished, healthy natural nail. There's no separate top coat, no base coat, no lamp required. The brush is a standard flat paddle shape that fans out nicely, covering the full nail bed in two strokes. The bottle itself is compact and travel-friendly, though the minimalist white-and-clear design feels more pharmacy than luxury.

**Real-world performance**

The 4-in-1 claim is legitimate. The first coat acts as a base, gripping the nail plate without staining (even on my slightly yellowed nails from years of dark polish). The second and third coats build the color, and the final layer self-levels to a high-gloss finish comparable to a dedicated top coat.

Here's where it gets interesting: the formula dries relatively fast — about 90 seconds to touch-dry between coats — but the manufacturer's instruction to wait two minutes between layers isn't a suggestion. Skip it, and you'll get microbubbles in the finish and peeling by day two. I tested both ways. The patience method delivered 6 days of chip-free wear on my dominant hand; the rushed method gave me 48 hours before the middle finger started flaking.

The shade itself is the sleeper hit. "It's Topless" is deceptive — it looks like a milky white in the bottle but applies as a flattering sheer that lets your natural nail show through. If you have warm undertones, this will look like an expensive "my nails but better" manicure. Cool-toned skin may find it slightly too yellow.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.0-star rating over 200 reviews reflects genuine satisfaction with the concept. One reviewer noted: "This is my third bottle. I've stopped buying separate base and top coats entirely — this does both jobs and my nails have never been stronger." Another echoed the shade appeal: "Finally a nude that doesn't look chalky or fake. It's like a tinted moisturizer for nails."

The strengthening claim also holds up. The formula contains hydrolyzed keratin, and several long-term reviewers reported less peeling and breakage after a month of continuous use.

**Where it falls short**

The thin formula is the main tradeoff. You need three coats for full opacity, and at 10ml per bottle, that's roughly 15-18 manicures before you're buying again. At the luxury price point, that's not cheap per use.

The brush, while functional, is narrower than comparable luxury brands like Essie's gel line. It's workable but means more strokes per nail, which increases the risk of streaking if you're not careful.

And the "gel" descriptor is doing heavy lifting. This is not a gel polish — it won't give you that thick, glass-like, 14-day wear. It's a very good regular lacquer with a glossy finish. Set expectations accordingly.

**Who should buy this**

- **The minimal routine devotee** who wants one bottle that does everything, especially for travel
- **Natural nail enthusiasts** who want a subtle, professional-looking manicure without salon visits
- **Anyone prone to nail peeling** looking to strengthen while wearing color

Skip it if you prefer bold, opaque color in one coat, or if you're expecting true gel durability.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this actually work as a base coat?**
A: Yes, but it's a thinner base than dedicated primers. If your nails are naturally oily, use a dehydrator first or you'll get less wear.

**Q: How many coats for the shade to look opaque?**
A: Three coats minimum. The first two are distinctly sheer; the third brings it to a solid nude. Four if you want full coverage on longer nails.

**Q: Will this damage my nails like gel polish?**
A: No — it's regular lacquer that removes with standard nail polish remover. No soaking, no filing, no damage.

**The Verdict: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if** you want a genuinely versatile nude that simplifies your routine without sacrificing finish quality. **Skip it if** you're expecting true gel performance or prefer one-coat opacity.

The Nails Inc It's Topless Gel Polish isn't revolutionary, but it's a smart refinement of the all-in-one concept — and that shade is worth the price of admission alone.
