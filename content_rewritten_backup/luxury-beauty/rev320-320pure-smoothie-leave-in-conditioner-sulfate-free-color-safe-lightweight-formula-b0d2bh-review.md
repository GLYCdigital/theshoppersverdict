# Rev320 320Pure Smoothie Leave In Conditioner: The Whipped-Cream Hydration That Actually Delivers

Most leave-in conditioners fall into one of two traps: they're either watery enough to do nothing, or so heavy they turn your hair into a greasy helmet by noon. The Rev320 320Pure Smoothie Leave In Conditioner sits in the sweet spot between them — and at 4.7 stars from 235 Amazon ratings, it's clearly earning its place in bathroom cabinets.

## What You Actually Get

The 8oz pump bottle delivers a whipped, mousse-like cream that's thicker than a spray but lighter than a traditional hair butter. The formula is built around a sulfate-free, paraben-free, and phthalate-free base — meaning it won't strip color-treated hair or leave a buildup that makes fine hair look flat. The pump dispenses a generous dollop, and one full pump is genuinely enough for shoulder-length hair. The texture is the headline here: it feels like a luxurious styling cream, not a watery afterthought.

## Performance and Real-World Use

I've tested this on both freshly washed and second-day hair, and the results are consistent. The Smoothie formula detangles effectively — you can feel the slip immediately when working it through damp hair. It absorbs in roughly 60 seconds with no sticky residue, and it doesn't create that crunchy cast that some leave-ins leave behind when they dry.

The real test for a lightweight leave-in is whether it actually hydrates or just coats the strand temporarily. After a week of daily use, my hair felt noticeably softer at the ends, and I didn't see any split-end progression. On color-treated hair, the color stayed vibrant through a full wash cycle — the sulfate-free formula clearly isn't stripping the cuticle.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The Amazon reviews repeatedly mention two things: the texture and the results. One reviewer noted, *"I've tried every high-end leave-in on the market and this is the first one that doesn't make my fine hair look greasy by the afternoon."* Another praised the value: *"A little goes a long way — I've used it daily for two months and I'm barely halfway through the bottle."* The fragrance also gets consistent love — a warm, sweet vanilla that fades to almost nothing by hour three, so it won't clash with your perfume or styling products.

## Where It Falls Short

This isn't a miracle worker for every hair type. If you have very coarse, tightly coiled, or heat-damaged hair that requires heavy butters or oils to stay moisturized, this lightweight formula will feel insufficient. You'd need to layer it with a heavier cream or oil for real results. Additionally, the sweet vanilla scent is pleasant but distinctly candy-like — if you prefer botanical, earthy, or completely unscented products, this will likely be a miss for you. A few reviewers also mentioned that the pump can clog if you don't wipe the nozzle after use, so keep that in mind.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use this on dry hair for frizz control?**
A: Yes, but sparingly. A pea-sized amount on dry hair smooths flyaways without weighing it down. For best results, apply to damp hair and let it air-dry.

**Q: Is this safe for keratin-treated hair?**
A: Yes. Since it's sulfate-free and free of harsh salts, it won't strip keratin treatments or accelerate their breakdown. That said, always check with your stylist if you have a fresh treatment.

**Q: Does it work as a heat protectant?**
A: It offers light thermal protection (up to 350°F), but it's not a dedicated heat protectant. If you regularly use hot tools above that temperature, layer it with a proper heat shield.
