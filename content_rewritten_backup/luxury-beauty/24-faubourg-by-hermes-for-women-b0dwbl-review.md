# 24 Faubourg By Hermes For Women: A Luxury Review That Cuts Through the Hype

Some perfumes announce themselves with a whisper. 24 Faubourg announces itself with a standing ovation. This is Hermès' tribute to their flagship Paris boutique at 24 Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré — and it wears its heritage like a couture gown. But heritage doesn't automatically mean "worth your money." Here's the real story after digging through 220 Amazon ratings and testing notes.

## What You Actually Get

The box contains the classic 24 Faubourg Eau de Parfum in Hermès' signature rectangular flacon — a heavy glass block with a gold cap that feels substantial in hand. As shown in the product image, it's unmistakably luxury packaging; the kind that makes you feel like you've bought something, not just a bottle of liquid. You're getting 100ml of EDP concentration, which is the sweet spot for daily wear without reapplication anxiety.

## Performance and Build Quality

Let's talk chemistry. 24 Faubourg opens with a burst of neroli and orange blossom — bright, almost citrusy, with a green freshness that's immediately recognizable as old-school French perfumery. Within 30 minutes, the heart emerges: jasmine, tuberose, and ylang-ylang creating that signature white floral richness. The base is where it gets interesting — sandalwood, vanilla, and amber with a soft leather undertone that grounds the florals.

Longevity is genuinely impressive. Multiple verified buyers report 8–10 hours of wear. One reviewer noted: *"Put it on at 7 AM, could still smell it clearly at 5 PM. Nothing else in my collection lasts this long."* Sillage is moderate-to-high for the first two hours — this is a scent that fills a room when you enter it. It's not subtle, and that's either a feature or a bug depending on your workplace.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

The floral complexity is the runaway highlight. A recurring theme in positive reviews: *"Every hour it smells different — the dry-down is warm and creamy, nothing like the opening."* The packaging also earns consistent praise, with buyers calling it "gift-ready" and "a piece of art for the vanity."

## Where It Falls Short

Honesty time. This is not a modern fragrance. If you're used to the sugary vanilla bombs or aquatic freshies dominating current Sephora shelves, 24 Faubourg will feel like your grandmother's perfume — elegant, but unmistakably from another era. The powdery dry-down is the most divisive element; some find it sophisticated, others find it dusty.

Performance issue: the first two hours are loud. One reviewer complained: *"Sprayed once before a meeting. My colleague asked if I was wearing perfume — not a compliment, a question."* If you work in close quarters, go light on the trigger.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The white floral devotee** who appreciates jasmine, tuberose, and orange blossom done properly
- **The professional woman 35+** who wants a signature scent with authority and staying power
- **The fragrance collector** seeking a classic French house scent that's still in production

**Skip it** if you prefer fresh, aquatic, or gourmand profiles, or if you're looking for a versatile dumb-reach scent for every occasion.

## FAQ

**Is 24 Faubourg suitable for daytime wear?**
Yes, but apply sparingly. One spray to the chest and one to the wrist is enough. Two sprays on each pulse point will project too strongly for most daytime settings.

**How does this compare to other Hermès fragrances?**
It's warmer and more floral than Terre d'Hermès (masculine/woody) and richer than the Jardin series (light/green). It's the most "classic French" of the line.

**Does the bottle last?**
At 100ml with 2–3 sprays per wear, expect 6–8 months of daily use. The EDP concentration means you'll use less per application than EDT.

## The Verdict

**4.2/5 — Buy it if** you want a statement-making white floral with real longevity and don't mind a loud opening. **Skip it if** you prefer modern, subtle, or sweet profiles.

24 Faubourg is a beautiful anachronism — a perfume that refuses to apologize for being bold, floral, and unapologetically French. It's not for everyone, but for the right person, it's a signature scent that will turn heads for years. At its price point, you're paying for heritage, quality ingredients, and packaging that feels genuinely luxurious. Whether that's worth it depends entirely on whether you want to smell like a Parisian boutique — or just smell expensive.
