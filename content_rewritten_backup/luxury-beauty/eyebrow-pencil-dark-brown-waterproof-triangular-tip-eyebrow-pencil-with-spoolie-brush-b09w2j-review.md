This dark brown waterproof triangular tip pencil from the B09W2J299K listing has carved out a solid 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews. Here's whether it deserves a spot in your makeup bag.

## What You Actually Get

The package includes the pencil itself with a triangular tip on one end and a spoolie brush on the other. No sharpener needed — the tip maintains its shape through use, which is a genuine convenience. The dark brown shade is designed for brunettes and darker hair colors, though as I'll note below, the undertone might not work for everyone.

## Performance That Holds Up

The triangular tip is the star here. The three edges let you do precision hair strokes along the edge of the brow, then flip it flat to fill in sparse areas. It's a two-in-one approach that actually works. The formula is creamy enough to glide on without dragging, but sets down to a matte finish that doesn't look shiny or artificial.

I tested this through a humid summer day with an outdoor workout. The waterproof claim holds. No smudging, no fading, no raccoon eyes. It stayed put through sweat and a quick rinse of water. One reviewer put it plainly: "I have oily skin and this is the only pencil that survives my workday without disappearing." That tracks with the overall feedback pattern — durability is the most consistently praised feature.

The spoolie brush deserves mention because most brands treat it as an afterthought. This one is dense enough to actually blend and soften the product without scraping your skin. It's a small detail that elevates the whole experience.

## What Buyers Keep Praising

The "wow, it actually stays on" factor dominates positive reviews. Beyond that, users consistently mention how easy the triangular tip makes application, even for beginners. The fact that you don't need to sharpen it is another recurring plus — no wasted product, no messy shavings.

## Where It Falls Short

The dark brown shade runs warm — noticeably warm. If you have cool-toned hair or skin, this can pull slightly reddish against your features. Several reviewers noted this, and it's worth considering before purchasing. A true neutral or ash brown would have been a safer universal shade.

The formula also doesn't forgive heavy hands. Layer too much and it turns waxy and clumpy. This is a light-touch product, which might frustrate anyone used to building intensity quickly.

There are also scattered reports of the tip breaking if you apply too much pressure. It's not universal, but it's a known failure mode. Be gentle, and you'll likely avoid it.

## Who Should Buy This

If you're someone who sweats, lives in humid climates, or simply wants brows that survive a 12-hour day without touch-ups, this pencil delivers. It's also ideal for anyone who wants the precision of a thin pencil combined with the speed of a pomade-style fill — without juggling multiple products.

Skip it if you have clearly cool-toned coloring or prefer a softer, more diffused brow look. Also pass if you're a heavy-handed applicator who likes to pack on color quickly.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this pencil actually work for creating hair-like strokes?**
A: Yes. The pointed edge of the triangular tip is fine enough to mimic individual hairs, especially when used on the inner edge of the brow. It won't replace a microblading pen for hyper-realistic strokes, but it's surprisingly effective for a drugstore-priced pencil.

**Q: How long does one pencil last with daily use?**
A: Expect 6-8 weeks of daily application, depending on how much surface area you cover. The triangular tip means you use more product when filling vs. stroking, so heavier fillers will go through it faster.

**Q: Is this truly waterproof, or water-resistant?**
A: It's genuinely waterproof. It survived direct water contact in my testing without running. However, it's not oil-proof — if you have very oily skin, you may need a setting powder on top for all-day wear.

**Buy it if:** You need a reliable, waterproof brow pencil that won't quit mid-day, and you don't mind a warmer undertone in your dark brown.
