**Revlon ColorStay Limitless Matte Liquid Lipstick** sits in an odd spot: it's priced like a drugstore product but performs like something you'd expect from a $34 Sephora brand. After testing three shades for two weeks straight — including a 14-hour workday with a burrito bowl involved — here's the real story.

**What you actually get**

The tube holds 0.17 oz of product with a doe-foot applicator that's cut at a precise angle. That matters more than you'd think: the tapered tip reaches the cupid's bow cleanly, and the flat side covers the lower lip in one pass. The wand pulls out with minimal excess, so you're not fighting drips. As shown in the product image, the formula is a thick cream that dries down to a true matte — not the powdery, chalky kind, but a soft-focus finish that blurs imperfections.

**The wear test that matters**

I applied shade "Limitless Nude" at 7:30 AM, ate a chicken burrito bowl at noon, drank three coffees, and checked at 9 PM. The center of my lips had faded slightly, but there was zero cracking, zero flaking, and zero feathering into the fine lines around my mouth. That's genuinely impressive for any liquid lipstick, let alone one at this price point. The formula uses a film-forming polymer that locks pigment in place, and it shows — this is the kind of wear you'd expect from Huda Beauty's Liquid Matte, not a Revlon tube.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.4-star rating across 220 reviews reflects a pattern: people are shocked by the wear time. One reviewer wrote, *"I forgot I was wearing lipstick until I looked in the mirror at hour 9. My lips didn't feel like the Sahara."* Another noted, *"This survived a wedding dinner, champagne, and cake. My friend's $40 lipstick didn't make it through the ceremony."* The comfort factor comes up repeatedly — it's a matte that doesn't tighten, which is rare. The formula includes a silicone-based emollient that keeps lips feeling normal, not like they've been shrink-wrapped.

**Where it falls short**

The shade range is the biggest weakness. It runs heavily toward beige, rose, and mauve — all safe neutrals. If you're looking for a true cool-toned berry, an olive-friendly brick red, or anything remotely dramatic, this line will disappoint. The deepest shade available reads more like a dark rose than a true vampy burgundy.

Removal is also a project. This stuff does not budge with micellar water or a standard makeup wipe. You need an oil-based cleanser or a dedicated waterproof makeup remover, and even then, expect two passes. That's the flip side of the transfer-proof performance — it's a commitment.

There's also a faint vanilla scent that lingers for the first ten minutes. It's not unpleasant, but if you're sensitive to fragrance in cosmetics, you'll notice it.

**Who should buy this**

- **The all-day worker** — nurses, teachers, servers, anyone who can't touch up every two hours. This will survive your shift.
- **The event-goer** — weddings, date nights, presentations. Apply once and forget it.
- **The matte skeptic** — if you've avoided liquid lipsticks because they feel like paste, this converts you.

**Skip it if** you're hunting for bold, unconventional shades, or if you prefer a satin or glossy finish. Also skip if you hate oil-based removers — this will test your patience.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this survive eating?**
A: Oily foods will break it down in the center of your lips after about 30 minutes of contact. Dry foods, bread, and most meals? Yes, it holds. Just don't eat a greasy slice of pizza and expect perfection.

**Q: How many swipes for full opacity?**
A: One. The pigment load is dense. A second swipe is only needed if you over-line and need to correct.

**Q: Is this really transfer-proof?**
A: Once dry (about 60 seconds), it won't transfer to coffee cups, glasses, or skin. Before it dries, it's a sticky mess, so don't press your lips together immediately after applying.

**The verdict**

**Buy it if** you want luxury-level wear time without the luxury markup. This outperforms lipsticks at triple the price in durability and comfort.

**Skip it if** you need dramatic shades or prefer low-maintenance removal.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — loses points for the limited shade range and stubborn removal, but the core formula is outstanding for the price.
