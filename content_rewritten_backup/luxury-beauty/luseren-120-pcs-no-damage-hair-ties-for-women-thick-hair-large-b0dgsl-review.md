If you've got thick hair, you already know the struggle: every cute hair tie either snaps the moment you stretch it, digs into your scalp like a vice, or leaves a dent so deep it looks like you slept on a wrench. The LuSeren 120 Pcs No Damage Hair Ties claim to end that cycle. After digging through 235 ratings and the design specs, here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The package delivers 120 spiral hair ties in the "Large" size, explicitly engineered for thick, heavy, or curly hair. Each tie is roughly 3.2 inches in circumference when relaxed — noticeably bigger than standard elastics. The spiral construction isn't just aesthetic; it's a hollow, spring-like coil wrapped in a soft fabric covering. The set includes multiple colorways, so you're not stuck with 120 identical black ties.

## Performance and Build Quality

Here's where the "no damage" claim gets interesting. Traditional elastic bands concentrate pressure on a thin strip of hair, which is why you get those deep creases and eventual breakage. The LuSeren's spiral design spreads that tension across a much wider surface area. For thick hair, this genuinely matters — the tie holds a heavy ponytail or bun securely without the "tugging at the roots" sensation that thinner bands produce.

The fabric coating is soft to the touch and doesn't catch or pull at the hair shaft the way some rubber-core spirals do. During wear, they're comfortable enough to sleep in, which is a major plus for satin-bonnet-free nights or messy buns that last into the next morning.

## What Buyers Consistently Praise

One verified buyer noted, *"I have very thick, heavy hair and most ties break within a week. These actually hold a full day without slipping or snapping."* Another reviewer with 3C curls said the ties didn't create the "crunchy, dented look" that regular elastics leave behind. The durability factor also comes up repeatedly — the coil structure doesn't stretch out permanently like fabric bands, which means a single tie lasts significantly longer before losing its grip.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be honest about the tradeoffs. The spiral design that makes these gentle on thick hair can be a nuisance for finer textures. If you have thin or silky hair, the coil has a tendency to catch on individual strands during removal — you have to unwind it carefully rather than yank it out. That's not a dealbreaker, but it requires a learning curve.

The fabric covering is the weak point. Several long-term users reported fraying at the seam after roughly a month of heavy rotation. The ties still function when the fabric wears, but they look less polished. At 120 pieces, you can cycle through them, but if you were hoping for a forever product, that's not quite what this is.

## Who Should Buy This

## FAQ

**Q: Will these work for a high ponytail that needs to stay put all day?**
Yes, but with a caveat. The grip is secure for thick hair, though you may need to wrap twice for very heavy manes. They won't slip like fabric bands, but they're not a replacement for a firm elastic if you need military-grade hold.

**Q: How do you remove them without pulling hair?**
Unwind the spiral gently in the opposite direction of how you put it in, rather than sliding it out. It takes two extra seconds but completely eliminates snagging.

**Q: Are these truly "no damage"?**
They're dramatically gentler than standard elastics because they don't create a single high-pressure point. No tie is 100% damage-free, but these are about as close as it gets for thick hair.
