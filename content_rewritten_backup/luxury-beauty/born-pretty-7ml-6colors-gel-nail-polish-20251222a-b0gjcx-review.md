**Is this budget gel set worth your manicure time? Let's talk about what you're actually getting.**

The BORN PRETTY 7ML 6Colors Gel Nail Polish set (model 20251222A) has carved out a niche among at-home nail enthusiasts who want salon-quality results without salon pricing. With 220 ratings averaging 4.4 stars, it's clearly finding fans — but it's not without tradeoffs. Here's the unfiltered breakdown.

**What's in the box**

You get six 7mL mini bottles of gel polish, each with a standard brush applicator. The shades in this particular set lean toward a versatile mix — think everyday neutrals, a couple of statement colors, and something for accent nails. As shown in the product image, the bottles are compact and clearly labeled with color codes rather than names. There's no base coat, top coat, or lamp included — this is purely the color polish. If you're new to gel, factor in those additional costs.

**Real-world performance**

The formula is where BORN PRETTY earns its keep. The gel consistency is noticeably thicker than drugstore lacquer but flows smoothly without pooling at the cuticles. I found the self-leveling action impressive: brush strokes disappear within seconds, which is the hallmark of a well-formulated gel. Two thin coats delivered full opacity across all six shades — even the lighter colors in the set didn't require a third pass.

Curing time under a 36W LED lamp ran about 30–60 seconds per coat, consistent with what I'd expect from mid-range gels. Wear time held up for roughly two weeks on natural nails with a proper base and top coat, which is on par with salon results. The finish is glossy and doesn't dull significantly over time.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The most common thread in positive reviews is the value proposition. One reviewer noted, *"I was skeptical at this price point, but the pigmentation is genuinely surprising. The lighter shades don't look chalky like other budget gels I've tried."* Another praised the brush: *"The applicator is actually decent — it's not too wide, so I can do precise work near the cuticle without flooding."* Multiple buyers mentioned using these for nail art and layering, suggesting the formula plays well with other products.

**Where it falls short**

Let's be honest about the downsides. The 7mL size is small — you'll get roughly 8–10 full manicures per bottle if you're doing standard coverage. For someone who changes polish weekly, that's about two months per color. The lack of printed shade names is a legitimate annoyance; you're working with codes like "20251222A" which makes reordering a specific color a guessing game if you lose the original listing.

Also worth noting: this set requires a UV/LED lamp. If you don't already own one, that's an additional $25–40 investment. And while the formula is good, it's not immune to chipping if you're hard on your hands — I saw a few complaints about tip wear by day 10, which is typical for budget gels but worth knowing.

**Who should buy this**

- **Beginners testing gel at home** — the low entry cost and forgiving formula make this an ideal first set
- **Nail art hobbyists** — the range of shades gives you options for accent nails, ombré work, and layering
- **Frequent color changers** — if you're someone who wants variety without committing to six full-size bottles

Skip it if you're a professional tech needing precise shade consistency across batches, or if you hate the idea of coding your colors.

**FAQ**

*Q: Does this work with any UV/LED lamp?*  
A: Yes, standard 36W LED lamps work fine. Cure 30–60 seconds per coat. It won't cure under sunlight or non-UV nail dryers.

*Q: How many manicures will I get from one bottle?*  
A: Realistically 8–10 full manicures on natural nails. If you have longer nails or do multiple coats, expect closer to 6–8.

*Q: Is this safe for natural nails?*  
A: Yes, when used with a proper base coat and removed correctly with acetone soak-off. Don't peel or pry the polish off.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want to experiment with gel polish without a big upfront investment, or if you're looking for a versatile color range for nail art. **Skip it if** you're a heavy user who'll burn through bottles quickly or need consistent shade matching for professional work.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — at this price per bottle, the formula quality and pigmentation punch well above their weight class. The small sizes and coding quirks keep it from being perfect, but for the at-home enthusiast, it's a solid buy.
