**What's in the package**

That's every length from a skin-tight buzz to a scissor-over-comb alternative. The guards are stamped with their size on the side — molded into the plastic, not printed on, so they won't fade after a few washes.

**Build quality and fit**

These are designed for Wahl clippers, and the snap-on mechanism is the star here. The clips engage with a satisfying click and sit flush against the blade. I can't test these on my own head, but the buyer consensus is clear. Over 4.4 stars from 220 ratings, with consistent praise for smooth gliding across the scalp. Honesty time. The lack of a storage case is annoying — eight loose guards in a drawer is a recipe for losing the 5/8" you need most. And while they fit Wahl models well, "most Wahl clippers" isn't "all" — if you have an older or oddball model, check the compatibility notes before ordering.
