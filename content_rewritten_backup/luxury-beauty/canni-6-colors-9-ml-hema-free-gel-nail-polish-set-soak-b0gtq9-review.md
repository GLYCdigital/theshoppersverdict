If you've ever woken up with itchy, swollen nail beds after a gel manicure, you already know why "hema-free" matters. HEMA is the most common allergen in gel polish, and CANNI's 6-color set eliminates it entirely — a feature that alone justifies a closer look for anyone with sensitive skin. But does the formula hold up beyond the allergy-friendly claim? After digging through 229 ratings and the technical specs, here's what you need to know.

**What's actually in the box**

You get six 9ml bottles — roughly a third smaller than the standard 15ml professional size. The set includes a mix of nudes and deeper shades, all with a glossy, self-leveling finish. The brush is flat with a rounded tip, which matters more than most shoppers realize: it hugs the cuticle line and reduces flooding. Each bottle has a twist-off cap that seals tightly, so you won't deal with dried-out product between uses.

**Performance and real-world wear**

The formula is medium-viscosity — not runny like some budget gels, not so thick that it pools at the cuticle. Two thin coats deliver full opacity on most shades. The darker colors in particular are impressively pigmented; one coat nearly covers, which is rare at this price point. Curing under a standard 36W UV/LED lamp took about 60 seconds per coat, and the finish came out smooth without brush strokes.

Soak-off removal is straightforward: file the top coat, wrap in acetone-soaked cotton for 10–12 minutes, and the gel lifts in sheets rather than flaking into stubborn bits. One buyer noted, "I've tried three different hema-free brands and this one removes the cleanest — no scraping, no damaged nails underneath." That tracks with the formula's flexibility — it's firm enough to resist chipping but not brittle.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The allergy-friendly angle gets the most love. One verified review said, "My dermatologist told me to stop using gel entirely. This set gave me my manicures back — zero reaction after three weeks of wear." Others highlight the color payoff: "The burgundy shade is a one-coat wonder. I've paid triple for salon brands that needed three coats."

Chip resistance holds up for 10–14 days on natural nails, with a few reviewers reporting 16+ days when paired with a quality top coat. The shine is genuinely mirror-like straight out of the bottle — though it does soften slightly after the second week.

**Where it falls short**

The color story skews cool — mauves, berry tones, and a dusty rose. If your skin has warm undertones, several shades may pull ashy or gray on you. There's no bright red or true neutral nude in the mix.

The 9ml size is the other tradeoff. Weekly manicures on all ten nails will drain a bottle in about six to eight weeks. That's fine if you rotate colors, but if you find one signature shade, you'll be reordering sooner than with a 15ml bottle.

**Who should buy this**

This set is ideal for three specific shoppers: anyone with confirmed HEMA sensitivity or reactive skin, beginners who want a forgiving formula that self-levels, and at-home users who prefer a smaller color rotation over a massive 12-shade kit they'll never finish. Skip it if you need a warm-toned neutral palette or you exclusively buy salon-size bottles.

**FAQ**

*Is this truly hema-free, or just low-HEMA?*
CANNI lists HEMA as absent from the ingredient deck, not merely reduced. Multiple reviewers with diagnosed allergies confirm no reaction, but do a patch test on one nail first — you could be sensitive to other acrylates (like Di-HEMA or TPO).

*Does this work with any UV/LED lamp?*
Yes, it cures under both 36W UV and 36W LED lamps. It won't cure under a mini portable lamp weaker than 36W — that's a universal gel polish limitation.

*How long does shipping take and does it arrive intact?*
Most U.S. buyers report 2–4 day Prime delivery. The bottles ship in a foam-lined box, so breakage is rare — but inspect caps on arrival, as one reviewer noted a loose seal.

**The Verdict**

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Buy it if you want a genuinely hema-free gel that performs like a salon brand at a fraction of the cost. The pigmentation, self-leveling formula, and clean soak-off make it a standout for sensitive nail beds. Skip it if you need warm-toned shades or prefer larger 15ml bottles — this set is about quality and safety, not volume.

*Buy it if:* You have reactive skin, want reliable 2-week wear, or you're new to gels and need a forgiving formula.
*Skip it if:* Warm nudes are your go-to, or you burn through polish fast and hate reordering.
