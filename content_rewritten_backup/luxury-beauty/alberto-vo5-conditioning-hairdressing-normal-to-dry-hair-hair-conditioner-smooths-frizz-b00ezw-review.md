Let's be direct: most drugstore hair conditioners are watered-down lies. This one isn't. The Alberto VO5 Conditioning Hairdressing has been a barbershop staple since the 1950s for a reason—it actually works. With a 4.8-star average across 240 ratings, it's not hype. It's a dense, lanolin-rich cream that treats hair like fabric, not fiber.

**What's actually in the jar**

You get a 6-ounce tub of thick, opaque white cream. The texture is closer to a heavy hand balm than a typical rinse-out conditioner. Ingredients list mineral oil and lanolin as the heavy hitters—both are occlusive agents that seal moisture into the hair shaft. There's also a classic, masculine powder-and-almond fragrance that screams "old school barbershop." No pumps, no fancy packaging. Just a simple screw-top jar that'll last months because a little genuinely goes a long way.

**Performance: The honest breakdown**

I tested this three ways. As a pre-shampoo treatment, I massaged a quarter-sized amount into dry hair, let it sit 20 minutes, then shampooed twice. Result: noticeably softer, more manageable hair with zero residue. As a leave-in on damp hair, a pea-sized amount tamed frizz on a humid 80-degree day—impressive. As a daily styling cream on dry hair, it smoothed flyaways and added a subtle, healthy sheen without the wet-gel look.

The catch? This is a heavy product. If you have fine or thin hair, one mistake with the amount and you'll look like you forgot to rinse. Start with a rice-grain amount and build up. It's also not a heat protectant, so layer something else before styling with tools.

**What buyers consistently say**

The Amazon reviews paint a clear picture. One long-term user writes, *"I've used this for 40 years. Nothing else controls my coarse, dry hair like this does."* Another notes, *"My grandfather used this, my father used this, and now I do. **Where it falls short**

Honestly, the scent is the biggest hurdle. If you're not into retro, powdery fragrances, this will bother you. It's not subtle—one reviewer called it *"my grandfather's dresser drawer."* It also doesn't provide heat protection, so you'll need a separate product. And for those with oily scalps, the lanolin can feel heavy if applied to the roots. Keep it mid-length to ends.

**Who should buy this**

- **Curly and coily hair folks** fighting chronic dryness—this is your moisture insurance
- **Men with short, coarse hair** who want a classic groomer that controls frizz without product buildup
- **Budget-conscious shoppers** tired of $30 conditioners that don't deliver
- **Skip it if** you have fine, limp hair or absolutely hate powdery vintage scents

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I use this on color-treated hair?**
A: Yes. Mineral oil and lanolin are color-safe and won't strip dye. One caveat: it can slightly dull fresh highlights if over-applied, so use sparingly.

**Q: How often should I use it?**
A: For normal hair, 2-3 times weekly as a leave-in is plenty. For very dry or damaged hair, daily use is fine—just adjust the amount.

**Q: Does it cause buildup?**
A: Not if you shampoo regularly. The formula washes out cleanly with any standard shampoo. It's only a problem if you use it daily and skip cleansing for weeks.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you have dry, coarse, frizz-prone hair and want a versatile, affordable treatment that outperforms its price point. **Skip it if** you're sensitive to strong fragrances or have fine hair that weights easily.
