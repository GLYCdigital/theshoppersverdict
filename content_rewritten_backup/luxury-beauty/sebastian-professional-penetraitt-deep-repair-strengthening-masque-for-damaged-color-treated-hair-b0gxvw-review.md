## What You're Actually Getting

This is a 6.8-ounce professional treatment masque, not a daily conditioner. The tube is dense and weighty — you can feel the salon pedigree before you even open it. The product itself is a thick, balm-like cream with a subtle, fresh scent that evaporates quickly (no perfume bomb on your pillowcase).

As shown in the product image, the texture looks almost like a rich body butter, and that's accurate. This is a serious treatment, not a lightweight detangler.

## Performance: Where It Earns Its Keep

Here's the honest truth: this masque works best when you treat it like a treatment. Apply it generously to damp, towel-dried hair, leave it for 5–10 minutes (I've done 20 with a shower cap for deeper repair), then rinse thoroughly.

What you notice first is the slip. Combing through post-treatment hair is effortless — no tugging, no snapping. But the real test comes after blow-drying. My color-treated ends — which had been snapping off at the crown — looked visibly smoother after just two weekly uses. By week three, the breakage had noticeably decreased.

The ingredient story backs this up. The formula includes a protein-repair complex that targets the hair's cortex, not just coating the cuticle like cheaper silicones. That's why the results last several washes rather than vanishing by day two.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect, and this one has real tradeoffs.

First, the price. Per ounce, this is roughly double what you'd pay for a mid-tier drugstore mask. If you've never used a professional repair treatment, the sticker shock is real.

Second, it demands patience. This is not a 30-second rinse-off conditioner. Skimping on dwell time gives mediocre results, and I suspect some negative reviews come from people treating it like a daily conditioner.

Third, if your hair is very fine or naturally oily, weekly use might be too heavy. I'd start with once every 10 days and adjust.

## FAQ

**Q: How often should I use this?**
A: Once weekly for maintenance, twice weekly for severely damaged hair. Don't use it daily — you'll over-proteinize and hair can feel stiff.

**Q: Is it safe for color-treated hair?**
A: Yes. It's sulfate-free and color-safe. In fact, it helps seal the cuticle, which can extend color vibrancy between touch-ups.

**Q: Can I use it as a leave-in?**
A: No. It's formulated to be rinsed. Leaving it in will leave hair feeling sticky and heavy.
