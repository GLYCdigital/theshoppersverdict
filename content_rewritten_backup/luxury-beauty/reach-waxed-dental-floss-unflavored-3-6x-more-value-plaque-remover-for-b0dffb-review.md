Let's be honest: dental floss is about as exciting as a root canal. But if you actually use the stuff daily, the REACH Waxed Dental Floss in the 3.6X value pack is one of the smartest purchases you'll make this year. With 225 ratings holding a solid 4.5-star average, this isn't a flashy product — it's a workhorse. Here's the real story.

## What You Actually Get

The "3.6X More Value" claim isn't marketing fluff. The package contains roughly 200+ yards of waxed floss — that's three-plus standard refills in one box. For a daily flosser, that's about four to six months of supply depending on how generous you are with length. The dispenser itself is a compact white container with a clear window so you can see when you're running low — a small touch, but genuinely useful.

## Performance and Build Quality

The waxed coating does its job. It glides between teeth without the shredding or snapping that plagues cheaper unwaxed floss, especially if you have restorations or slightly rough edges on your molars. One Amazon reviewer put it plainly: "It slides between tight teeth without shredding. I have bridges and this is the only floss that doesn't catch." That's a consistent theme across the feedback.

The unflavored formula is worth calling out. If you've ever gagged on aggressively minty floss first thing in the morning, you know the struggle. This is clean, neutral, and doesn't leave a residue or aftertaste.

## Where It Falls Short

No product is perfect. The floss has a wider, tape-like profile — while that's great for distributing pressure and comfort, it's less forgiving if your teeth are packed tightly together. You'll need to use a gentle sawing motion to get it through, and some users report it can be a bit fiddly in the very back molars.

The dispenser is also basic. There's no smooth click-action cutter; you have to wrap the floss around the metal tab and snap it. It works, but it's not the most elegant mechanism on the market. One reviewer noted, "The cutter tab is a bit sharp and takes getting used to."

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You floss daily, hate mint flavoring, and want to stop reordering floss every few weeks. This is also a smart pick for families — one box covers multiple people for months.

**Skip it if:** You have extremely tight contacts and prefer a thin, round floss like Glide. The wider profile will frustrate you. Also skip if you need a dispenser with a one-touch cutter — you'll want a premium dispenser brand instead.

## FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: How long does the 3.6X pack actually last?**
A: For one person flossing daily with the recommended 18-inch strand, expect about five to six months. For a family of two, roughly three months.

**Q: Is this floss safe for braces or bridges?**
A: Yes — the waxed coating and flat profile work well around orthodontics and under bridges. That said, thin super-floss is still better for threading under fixed retainers.

**Q: Does unflavored mean no taste at all?**
A: Correct. No mint, no sweetness, just clean floss. Some people actually prefer this because it doesn't interfere with the taste of toothpaste or mouthwash.

## The Verdict

The REACH Waxed Dental Floss Unflavored 3.6X Value Pack is a practical, no-nonsense buy.
