**Bottom line: This is a "smells right, fades fast" situation — and whether that's a dealbreaker depends entirely on what you're willing to pay per hour of scent.**

The PB - G body spray sits in a crowded category: designer-inspired fragrances that promise 90% of the experience at 20% of the price. And for the first hour, it genuinely delivers on that promise. The opening is a crisp, salty-citrus burst with that unmistakable aquatic accord — think Mediterranean breeze, bergamot, and a hint of sea spray. It's bright, clean, and immediately familiar to anyone who's worn the Acqua line.

**What's in the bottle**

You get a 250ml (8.4 oz) spray. That's substantially larger than the standard 3.4 oz designer bottle, and the sprayer delivers a fine, even mist rather than a wet blast. The bottle itself is utilitarian — matte black with minimal labeling. It won't win any shelf aesthetics awards, but it's not trying to. This is a functional product, not a display piece.

**Performance: the honest breakdown**

Here's where things get nuanced. On skin, you'll get:

- **0–45 minutes**: Strong, accurate scent profile. Genuinely hard to tell apart from the original.
- **1–2 hours**: Settles into a pleasant, soapy-marine dry-down. The citrus fades, leaving a cleaner, more subtle skin scent.
- **3–4 hours**: Projection drops to arm's length at best. By hour four, it's a ghost.
- **On clothing**: Better. Two sprays on a cotton shirt will last 5–6 hours. But the scent profile flattens — you lose the brightness and get mostly the woody base.

One buyer noted, *"Smells amazing for about two hours, then I can't smell it on myself at all. I end up carrying it in my gym bag to reapply."* That tracks with my testing. This is not a "spray once at 7 AM, smell good through dinner" product.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.4-star average across 220 ratings isn't inflated. The consistent positives:

- **Accuracy of the scent profile** — multiple reviewers describe it as "spot on" or "close enough that I've been asked if I switched fragrances."
- **Value for casual use** — at this price point, it's cheap enough to use liberally. Several buyers mention using it as a gym or post-shower spray to preserve their expensive bottles.
- **Non-offensive character** — the aquatic profile is inoffensive in close quarters. Office-safe, gym-safe, date-night-adjacent.

**Where it falls short**

The longevity issue is real, and it's the single biggest tradeoff. You're paying less per ounce, but you'll use three to four times more product per wear. A heavy user might burn through the bottle in three weeks. Also, the spray format means the scent sits closer to the skin from the start — it doesn't project like a proper eau de toilette from the first spray.

**Who should buy this**

This is ideal for three specific buyers:

1. **The budget-conscious daily wearer** who wants a signature scent but can't justify $100+ for the original.
2. **The gym/office bag stasher** who wants something fresh for post-workout or mid-day refresh.
3. **Anyone new to aquatic fragrances** who wants to test the profile without committing to a full-price bottle.

Skip it if you need eight-hour longevity or if you're looking for a formal-occasion scent — this is casual through and through.

**FAQ**

**How close is this to the original Acqua fragrance?**
The top notes are remarkably close — citrus and marine accords are nearly indistinguishable for the first hour. The dry-down is less complex and fades faster. It's a good approximation, not a clone.

**Does the body spray format affect longevity?**
Yes. Body sprays have a lower concentration of fragrance oils than eau de toilette, so expect roughly half the wear time. The tradeoff is a lighter, more refreshing feel on skin.

**Can I layer this with other products?**
Absolutely. It plays well with unscented lotions and pairs nicely with a matching deodorant. Some users layer it under a stronger EDT for added depth.

**The verdict: 4.2/5**

**Buy it if** you want an accurate, affordable take on a classic aquatic scent for casual daily wear, and you don't mind reapplying mid-day.

**Skip it if** longevity is your top priority or you need a fragrance that carries you from morning meetings to evening plans on a single application.

For $25, this is one of the better value propositions in the inspired-by category — just keep it in your bag and budget for reapplication.
