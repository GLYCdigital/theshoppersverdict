## What You Actually Get

The XCOATTIPS French set arrives with 24 nail tips in 12 sizes, which means you get two full manicure options per box. That's roughly $0.50 per wear if you're getting two applications from one box—a significant value proposition compared to salon visits running $50+ per French manicure.

They're designed to be applied with gel or acrylic overlay, making them a hybrid between salon extensions and DIY press-ons. The "XCOAT" technology refers to the pre-thinned cuticle area that's supposed to blend seamlessly with your natural nail—no bulky edge where the tip meets your nail bed.

## Real-World Performance

Here's where the XCOATTIPS diverge from drugstore press-ons. These are **thicker, more substantial tips**—comparable to salon gel extensions. That's a double-edged sword.

**The good:** Once applied with gel, these nails genuinely last. Multiple reviewers report 2-3 weeks of wear with proper application. The white French tips don't yellow, and the surface holds polish and gel topcoat without chipping. One reviewer noted, *"I've tried every brand on Amazon and these are the only ones that don't pop off when I'm doing dishes."*

**The catch:** These are not "apply and go" nails. You need gel base coat, a curing lamp, and ideally some practice with gel application. If you're expecting the peel-and-stick experience of standard press-ons, you'll be disappointed. The learning curve is real—but the payoff is salon-quality results at home.

## Where It Falls Short

## FAQ

**How long do these actually last?**
With proper gel application, 2-3 weeks. Without gel—just using the included adhesive tabs—expect 3-5 days maximum. The tabs are a backup, not the primary system.

**Can I fill or reuse these?**
Yes, with care. If you remove them gently and clean off the gel, you can reuse them. Most users get 2-3 wears per set before the tips become too thin from filing.

**Do I need a gel lamp?**
Yes, if you want them to last. These are designed to be worn with gel overlay. A cheap UV/LED lamp works fine—you don't need a professional-grade device.
