If you've ever watched your foundation slide off your face by 2 PM, you already know the frustration. The Urban Decay All Nighter Waterproof Setting Spray — the natural finish version of the cult classic — is the product that's supposed to end that drama. After two weeks of wearing it through gym sessions, a humid outdoor wedding, and a 10-hour workday, here's the honest breakdown.

**What you actually get**

The box contains a single 4 fl oz (118 mL) bottle with a screw-on cap. That's it — no mister head to replace, no travel pouch. The spray itself is clear, fragrance-free, and dries down completely invisible. The "Na" in the name refers to the natural finish variant, which means it won't add the dewy glow of the original All Nighter or the matte flatness of the De-Slick version. It sits squarely in the middle.

**Performance under real conditions**

I tested this with a full face of medium-coverage foundation, concealer, and powder. The first thing you notice is the mist — it's fine enough to coat evenly, but you need to hold the bottle back. Get too close and you'll deposit droplets that take forever to dry. The brand claims a 16-hour hold, which I'd call optimistic but not delusional. I hit the 12-hour mark with only minor creasing around my smile lines and zero transfer onto a white collar.

The waterproof claim is the real differentiator. I wore this through an outdoor run in 78-degree humidity, and my makeup didn't budge. A few buyers on Amazon confirmed this, with one noting: *"I cried at my sister's wedding and my makeup stayed perfect. I actually had to use makeup remover to get it off."* That tracks with my experience — removal takes a double cleanse with oil-based remover.

**What buyers consistently praise**

The 4.6-star rating across 229 reviews isn't accidental. The most common praise centers on the smudge-proof performance, especially for people with oily skin who've struggled with other setting sprays. Multiple reviewers mention that it outperforms the original All Nighter for longevity. One buyer wrote: *"I've repurchased the original for years but this natural finish version holds better. My blush actually stays on my cheeks now."* I'd agree — the color payoff of cream products lasts noticeably longer with this formula.

**Where it falls short**

The nozzle is the biggest complaint, and I get it. On several sprays, the mist came out unevenly, depositing larger droplets that took 60+ seconds to dry. It's not a dealbreaker, but it requires technique. If you're in a rush, you'll likely get spots.

Second, the "waterproof" promise has limits. It resists sweat and rain, but if you're swimming or submerged, it will eventually break down. That's physics, not a flaw, but worth knowing.

Third — and this is specific — very oily skin types may still see breakthrough shine by hour 6. This isn't a mattifying spray; it's a locking spray. If your T-zone produces oil like a deep fryer, pair it with a mattifying primer underneath.

**Who should buy this**

- **The bride or event attendee** who needs makeup to survive tears, sweat, and a packed dance floor
- **The commute warrior** who lives through summer humidity or winter rain and wants zero makeup meltdowns
- **The "set it and forget it" person** who wants to apply makeup at 7 AM and not think about it until evening

**Skip it if** you have extremely dry skin (the natural finish won't add any hydration) or you prefer a dewy, glass-skin look — you'd be better served by the original All Nighter or a hydrating mist.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does the natural finish version look different from the original All Nighter?**
A: Yes — subtly. The natural finish leaves skin looking like skin, with no added glow or matte film. It's the most undetectable of the three All Nighter finishes.

**Q: How far should I hold the bottle when spraying?**
A: At least 10-12 inches from your face. Close the eyes and mouth, spray in an X and T motion, and wait 60 seconds before touching your face.

**Q: Is it worth the price over drugstore setting sprays?**
A: If you need actual waterproof performance, yes. Drugstore sprays hold makeup but rarely survive sweat or water. This is one of the few that does — the difference is measurable.

**The Verdict**

- **Buy it if:** you need bulletproof makeup that survives sweat, humidity, and long days without a touch-up. The natural finish is universally flattering.
- **Skip it if:** you have very dry skin, want a dewy glow, or hate finicky spray nozzles.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — the performance justifies the luxury price, but the nozzle could be better engineered. This is the setting spray to grab when the stakes are high.
