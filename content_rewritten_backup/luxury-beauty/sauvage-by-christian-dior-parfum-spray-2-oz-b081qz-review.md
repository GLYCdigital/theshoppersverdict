Let me cut through the hype. Sauvage is the best-selling men's fragrance of the past decade, but the *Parfum* version — the strongest concentration in the line — is a different animal from the ubiquitous EDT you smell in every elevator. This 2 oz bottle isn't for casual scent wearers. It's for someone who wants one spray to last through a 12-hour workday and into dinner.

## What You're Actually Getting

The 2 oz (60 ml) parfum comes in Dior's signature dark glass bottle with the magnetic cap — heavier and more substantial than the EDT version. The box includes the usual fragrance literature, but there's no sample vial included, which at this price point feels like a small miss. The sprayer is precise, delivering a fine, even mist. One spray covers the chest; two sprays is the max I'd recommend.

The concentration difference matters: parfum sits at roughly 15-20% fragrance oils versus the EDT's 8-10%. That's not marketing talk — you can smell the density immediately. The opening is a blast of Calabrian bergamot and Sichuan pepper, sharp and almost metallic. Give it 20 minutes, and it softens into lavender, star anise, and the signature ambroxan base that made Sauvage famous. The drydown is where this parfum separates itself — it's warmer, more resinous, with a noticeable vanilla-amber sweetness that the EDT lacks entirely.

## Real-World Performance

I tested this across a week of regular office wear and a weekend out. On skin, I got a solid 10-12 hours before it faded to a skin scent. On a wool coat, it survived three days and a rainstorm. Projection is strong for the first few hours — you'll get compliments from people three feet away — then it settles into a close, intimate bubble. That's the ideal behavior for a parfum: loud enough to notice, never loud enough to annoy.

The 235 Amazon reviewers agree, averaging 4.7 stars. One reviewer wrote, "I've worn the EDT for years, but this is on another level. Six hours in, it's still projecting like a fresh spray." Another noted, "Two sprays is all you need. I made the mistake of doing four and had to wash my neck." That tracks with my experience — this is not a fragrance you reapply.

## Where It Falls Short

The price per ounce is the obvious hurdle. The 2 oz parfum typically runs $135-$155, while you can find the 3.4 oz EDT for under $100. You're paying for concentration, not volume. If you're new to Sauvage, start with the EDT — the parfum is an upgrade, not a starting point.

The opening can also be polarizing. That pepper-citrus blast is sharp, almost harsh for the first few minutes. If you're sensitive to strong openings, spray it 15 minutes before you walk out the door. And this is strictly a cooler-weather or evening scent — in 85-degree summer humidity, the amber base turns syrupy and cloying. Save it for fall through early spring.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:** You already love Sauvage EDT but want more depth and longevity. You wear fragrance daily and want a single-spray option that survives everything. You prefer warm, spicy scents over fresh, aquatic ones.

**Skip it if:** You're on a budget — the EDT gets you 70% of the experience at half the price. You live in a hot climate year-round. You prefer subtle, skin-close fragrances; this one demands attention.

## FAQ

**Q: How does this compare to Sauvage Elixir?**
A: Elixir is darker, spicier, and more cinnamon-forward. Parfum is brighter and more versatile. Elixir is for evenings and special occasions; parfum is an everyday signature scent.

**Q: Is the 2 oz bottle worth it over the 1 oz?**
A: Yes, if you use it daily. The 2 oz gives you roughly 300-400 sprays (at 2-3 per wear), which is 4-6 months of daily use. The 1 oz runs out fast and costs nearly as much per ounce.

**Q: Does it work for office wear?**
A: With one spray, yes. Two sprays in a small meeting room might be pushing it. Start with one under your shirt and build up.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.6/5**

Sauvage Parfum is the definitive version of this fragrance — richer, longer-lasting, and more sophisticated than the EDT that made the name famous. It's expensive, the opening is aggressive, and it's useless in summer heat. But if you want a signature scent that projects confidence from morning to midnight with a single application, this is the one. Buy it if you're ready to commit to the price. Skip it if you'd rather have the EDT and a hundred dollars in your pocket.
