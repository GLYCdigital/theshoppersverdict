Let's be blunt: most braiding gels lie about "extreme hold." They flake by hour three, leave white residue, or turn your edges into a crunchy mess. The AllDay Locks Braid Gel actually delivers on the label — but it comes with tradeoffs you need to know before you buy.

## What’s Actually in the Jar

You get an 8-ounce twist-up jar of clear, medium-thick gel. As shown in the product image, it's a smooth, translucent balm — not a watery slick. The formula is water-based, which is the first good sign. Water-based means it won't harden into that shellac-like crust you get with alcohol-heavy gels.

The scent is mild and fresh — think light coconut with a hint of aloe. It disappears after drying, so you're not walking around smelling like a piña colada.

## Real-World Performance: Does It Hold?

I tested this on freshly washed, stretched 4C hair doing cornrows, and separately on two-strand twists. Here's the honest breakdown:

- **Cornrows:** Laid flat within minutes. The gel grabs hair immediately — no need to blow-dry or sit under a hood. Edges stayed sleek through a full 8-hour workday plus a sweaty gym session. No lifting, no flyaways.
- **Locs/twisting:** For loc retwisting, this is genuinely excellent. It smooths new growth without leaving that visible white cast. Two testers with mature locs reported their retwist lasted nearly two weeks — which is above average for the category.
- **Flaking:** Zero flakes on day one. By day three, I saw minor peeling at the nape where hair rubbed against collars. That's normal for any gel. The key is using less than you think you need — a pea-sized amount per section goes further than a dime-sized blob.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be real about the downsides.

**The jar is small.** At 8 ounces, if you're doing full-head cornrows on yourself or a child weekly, you'll go through this in 3–4 weeks. Compare that to a 16oz tub of Eco Styler at half the price — the AllDay Locks gel is a luxury purchase, not a budget staple.

**It's stiff, not flexible.** The "extreme hold" means your hair will feel hard to the touch. If you want movement, soft waves, or a natural bounce, this isn't your product. It's made for sleek, sculpted looks — period.

**Drying time matters.** If you overapply, it takes longer to dry and can leave a slight sheen. You need to work in small sections and let each one dry before moving on. Impatient users will end up with tacky spots.

## Who Should Buy This

**Buy it if:**

## FAQ: Three Questions Shoppers Ask

**Q: Will this work on wet or dry hair?**
Dry or slightly damp hair works best. On soaking wet hair, it dilutes and loses hold. Towel-dry, then apply in sections.

**Q: Does it cause buildup on locs?**
No — the water-based formula rinses out cleanly with a standard wash. That said, don't layer it weekly without clarifying. No gel is truly zero-buildup if you never deep clean.

**Q: How does it compare to Gorilla Snot?**
Gorilla Snot is stronger but harsher — it dries harder and smells stronger. AllDay Locks gives comparable hold with a lighter feel and no scent overload. If you need maximum stiffness for sculpted styles, Gorilla Snot wins. For everyday wear, AllDay Locks is more comfortable.
