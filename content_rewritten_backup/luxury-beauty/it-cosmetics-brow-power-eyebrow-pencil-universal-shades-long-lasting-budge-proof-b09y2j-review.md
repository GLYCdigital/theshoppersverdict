**IT Cosmetics Brow Power Eyebrow Pencil Review: The 3-in-1 That Earned Its 4.6 Stars**

I’ve tested dozens of brow pencils, and most fall into one of two camps: they either smudge by lunchtime or require a makeup artistry degree to apply. The IT Cosmetics Brow Power Eyebrow Pencil in Universal Taupe sits firmly in a third category—the one that actually delivers on its "brow gel, pencil, and highlighter in one" claim. With 229 ratings averaging 4.6 stars, this isn't a niche cult favorite; it's a mainstream workhorse. Here’s the unvarnished breakdown.

**What’s Actually in the Tube**

You get a three-in-one system: a twist-up retractable pencil on one end (no sharpening needed), a tinted brow gel on the other, and a built-in sponge-tip highlighter that pops out from the base. The pencil itself is ultra-fine—about 1.1mm—which is thin enough to sketch individual hair strokes for a natural, feathered look. The Universal Taupe shade is a neutral, cool-leaning brown that flatters both blonde and brunette brows without pulling orange or ash-gray. It’s genuinely universal, which is rare.

**Performance and Build Quality**

The star here is the wear time. The formula is infused with silk, biotin, and collagen (marketing aside, it does feel conditioning, not drying). I wore it through a 12-hour workday, a sweaty gym session, and a rain shower. It didn’t budge. The pencil glides on smoothly without tugging—no waxy drag, no skipping. The gel side sets the hairs in place with a flexible hold, not the crunchy stiffness you get from cheaper brow gels.

The highlighter is a subtle champagne shimmer, not glittery. It’s a nice bonus, but honestly, it’s the least impressive component. It works, but it won’t replace your dedicated highlighter for a full glam look. It’s more of a brow-bone brightener.

**What Buyers Keep Praising**

Reviewers consistently mention two things: the natural finish and the staying power. One buyer wrote, *"I've finally found a pencil that looks like real hair. The color is perfect for my light brown brows."* Another said, *"I've repurchased this four times. It lasts all day, even on my oily skin."* The universal shade is a recurring theme—multiple reviewers with different hair colors noted it matched without looking painted on.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let’s be honest. The price point is premium for a drugstore-adjacent product, but not outrageous for the IT Cosmetics brand. The real issue is the retractable mechanism. A few reviewers reported the pencil breaking inside the barrel if you twist out too much—it’s a soft formula, so use small twists. The highlighter sponge tip also gets dirty quickly and isn’t replaceable. And if you have very oily eyelids, the gel might need a touch-up by hour 8, though the pencil itself holds strong.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **The "no-makeup makeup" minimalist** who wants one product to do three jobs in a morning routine.
- **The sweaty commuter or gym-goer** who needs brows that survive a workout without reapplying.
- **The natural-brow devotee** who wants hair-like strokes, not blocky filled arches.

**Skip It If**

- You prefer a bold, Instagram-style carved brow (this is too subtle for that).
- You hate multi-step tools and want a simple pencil with a spoolie.
- You’re on a strict budget—this is a luxury price point.

**FAQ**

**Q: Is the Universal Taupe too dark for very blonde brows?**
A: It’s a medium taupe, so it will look like a soft brown on platinum blondes. If you have white-blonde hair, test it first—it may read too dark. For dishwater or strawberry blonde, it’s perfect.

**Q: Does it smudge or transfer?**
A: In my testing and in most reviews, no. It’s budge-proof through sweat and light rain. Oil-heavy skincare underneath can shorten wear, so keep the brow area dry.

**Q: How long does one pencil last?**
A: With daily use, about 2–3 months. The gel side runs out slightly faster than the pencil—you’ll likely use the gel up first.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a dependable, natural-looking brow pencil that won’t quit by dinner. **Skip it if** you need a bold, sculpted arch or want a budget option.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — It’s not perfect (the highlighter and price are minor gripes), but for the core job—flawless, natural, long-wearing brows—this is one of the best on the market. The 4.6-star average is deserved.
