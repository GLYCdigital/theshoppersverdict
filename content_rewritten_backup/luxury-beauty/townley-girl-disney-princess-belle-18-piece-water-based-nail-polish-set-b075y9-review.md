**Review Body**

If you've ever tried to remove traditional nail polish from a wiggly five-year-old, you know the struggle: the acetone fumes, the stained clothing, the tears when the cotton ball drags across a fresh cuticle. The Townley Girl Disney Princess Belle 18-Piece Water-Based Nail Set sidesteps that entire drama. And for parents, that alone might be worth the price of admission.

**What You Actually Get**

The box contains 12 mini nail polishes in Belle-inspired shades — golds, pinks, reds, and a few neutral tones — plus six sheets of Disney Princess nail stickers and a small nail file. The polishes are 0.17 fluid ounces each, so they're clearly sized for little hands rather than adult manicures. As shown in the product image, the set's packaging leans heavily into the *Beauty and the Beast* aesthetic with Belle's silhouette and rose motifs. It's gift-ready out of the box, which explains why so many buyers purchase it for birthday parties.

**Real-World Performance**

The water-based formula is the headline feature, and it delivers. Application is thin — noticeably thinner than conventional polish — so you'll want two to three coats for the lighter shades like the soft pink and the champagne gold. The darker red covers reasonably well in two coats. Drying time runs about 10 minutes between coats, which is reasonable for the category.

The removal process is where this set earns its keep. A warm soapy soak loosens the polish enough that it peels off in sheets. No acetone, no scrubbing, no ruined washcloths. One Amazon reviewer put it simply: "My daughter can change her nail color every day without me worrying about chemicals."

The stickers are a nice bonus. They're printed on thin adhesive sheets, apply easily over dry polish, and stay put for a day or two. Expect them to peel at the edges if your child is hard on her hands — swimming and frequent hand-washing will shorten their lifespan.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

Across the 225 ratings, three themes dominate:

1. **Safety and ease of removal** — Parents repeatedly mention the peace of mind that comes with a non-toxic, water-based formula. One reviewer noted, "My 3-year-old can paint her own nails and I don't have to supervise the cleanup."
2. **The Disney branding** — For Belle fans specifically, the packaging and included stickers are a hit. "The gold and rose details are beautiful," wrote one buyer. "It feels more special than the generic princess sets."
3. **Value for the price** — With 18 pieces included, the per-item cost is negligible, and the set has held up well for multiple playdates and sleepovers.

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be direct: this is not long-wear polish. On active kids, expect chipping within 24 to 48 hours. The thin formula means lighter colors require patience for full opacity, and the brush is small — fine for little nails but fiddly for adults helping out.

The biggest frustration in customer feedback is the polish's tendency to peel off in one piece if it's applied too thickly. One reviewer complained, "My daughter's entire nail color came off like a sticker when she scratched her head." The fix is simple — thin coats only — but it's a learning curve.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **Parents of kids aged 3-8** who want a mess-free, chemical-free introduction to nail polish
- **Disney Princess Belle fans** who will love the themed packaging and stickers
- **Gift-givers** looking for a birthday party or holiday present that's genuinely useful

Skip it if you're looking for salon-grade durability or if your child is older than 10 — the mini bottles and simple color palette will feel babyish.

**FAQ**

**Is this polish safe for toddlers?**
The formula is water-based and non-toxic, making it significantly safer than conventional polish. That said, it's still not meant for ingestion — supervise young children during application.

**How do you remove the polish?**
Soak nails in warm soapy water for 2-3 minutes, then gently peel or wipe away. No acetone needed.

**Does the set include a top coat?**
No. The 12 polishes are all color shades; there's no clear base or top coat included. A separate clear polish can extend wear if desired.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a stress-free, chemical-free nail polish experience for a young Disney fan. The water-based formula, generous 18-piece contents, and charming Belle packaging make this an easy recommendation at its price point.

**Skip it if** you need polish that survives a week of school, sports, and general kid chaos — this one won't.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — A thoughtful, well-executed product that prioritizes safety and fun over longevity. For its intended audience, it's nearly perfect.
