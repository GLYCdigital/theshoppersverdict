Let me be blunt: most budget gel polish kits are a gamble. You either get watery pigment that pools in your cuticles, or a thick sludge that won't cure evenly. The modelones 6 Colors Gel Polish Kit sits in a rare middle ground — it's genuinely good for the price, but it's not flawless. Here's what you need to know before checkout.

**What's in the Box**

You get six 0.25 fl oz bottles of soak-off gel polish, each with a narrow brush applicator. The set I evaluated included a neon coral, a hot pink, a true red, a peach, a pastel pink, and a deep berry. There's no lamp, no base coat, and no top coat included — this is strictly the color collection. If you're starting from zero, factor in buying a separate base/top set and a 36W+ LED lamp.

**Real-World Performance**

I tested these on natural nails with a standard rubber base coat and a no-wipe top coat. The standout shades were the red, berry, and hot pink — each delivered full opacity in a single coat. The brush is where modelones earns its keep: it's tapered and slim, so you can get within a millimeter of the cuticle without flooding. That alone saves you 10 minutes of cleanup per hand.

The cure time is standard: 60 seconds under a 48W LED lamp. No tacky layer remained after curing, which means no alcohol wipe needed. Wear time held up to two weeks with a proper top coat, including daily dishwashing and typing. One minor chip appeared on my dominant thumb at day 12 — acceptable for the price point.

**The Two Shades That Disappoint**

The peach and pastel pink are the weak links. Both require two, sometimes three, coats to avoid a streaky, translucent finish. They're not unusable, but they demand patience and a steady hand. If you're buying this kit specifically for those soft shades, you'll be frustrated.

**What Buyers Consistently Say**

Reading through the 215 ratings, a few patterns emerge. One reviewer noted, "The pigment is honestly better than my $15-per-bottle brand. The red is a one-coat wonder." Another praised the brush: "I have tiny nails and this brush is the first one that doesn't paint my fingers." On the downside, several buyers mentioned the small bottle size — one said, "I got maybe five full manicures out of the red before it ran low." That tracks with my testing.

**Where It Falls Short**

The bottle size is the biggest practical issue. At 0.25 fl oz, you're paying for convenience, not longevity. If you do your nails biweekly, a single bottle might last two months. Also, there's no color chart on the packaging — the bottles have plain labels, so you'll need to hold them up to see the shade. Minor, but annoying when you're digging through a drawer.

**Who Should Buy This**

This kit is ideal for three types of shoppers: beginners who want to practice without investing in $12-per-bottle polishes, anyone who does their own manicures monthly and wants a solid color rotation, and gift-givers looking for a starter set. Skip it if you're a professional tech needing precise color matching or if pastels are your go-to — you'll be disappointed by the two weak shades.

**FAQ**

*Is this gel polish truly soak-off?* Yes. With a proper acetone soak and foil wrap, it lifts in 10-12 minutes. No filing needed.

*Does it work with any LED lamp?* It cured under both my 48W lamp and a cheaper 36W model. Avoid UV-only lamps — cure times become unpredictable.

*Can I use this without a base coat?* Technically yes, but you'll risk staining your natural nails, especially with the red and berry shades. Use a base coat.

**The Verdict**

The modelones 6 Colors Gel Polish Kit earns a 4.2/5. It delivers professional-grade pigmentation on four of six shades, a genuinely excellent brush, and reliable curing — all at a budget price. The small bottles and weak pastels are real tradeoffs, but they don't outweigh the value.

**Buy it if** you want salon-quality color without salon prices and you're okay working with the two finicky shades.

**Skip it if** pastels are your primary palette or you need large bottle volumes for frequent use.

For most home manicurists, this kit is a smart addition to your polish drawer. Just know which shades to reach for first.
