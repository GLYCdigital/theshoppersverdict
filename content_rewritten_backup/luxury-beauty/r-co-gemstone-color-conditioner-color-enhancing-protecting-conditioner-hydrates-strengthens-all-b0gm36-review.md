# R+Co GEMSTONE Color Conditioner Review: The Color-Boosting Conditioner That Actually Delivers

Most color-depositing conditioners are a gamble. You either get one that stains your shower floor and leaves hair feeling like straw, or one so weak it does nothing at all. The R+Co GEMSTONE sits in a rare middle ground: it's a legitimately good daily conditioner that happens to also refresh your color.

Here's the honest breakdown after digging through the 229 ratings and customer feedback.

## What You're Actually Getting

The 8.5 oz pump bottle looks like something you'd find in a high-end salon — sleek, professional, no nonsense. The formula itself is where the magic happens. It uses a blend of murumuru butter for hydration, quinoa protein for strength, and a heat protectant that shields hair up to 450°F.

Unlike drugstore color-depositing conditioners that rely on cheap dyes, GEMSTONE uses actual pigment that bonds to the hair shaft. You're not just coating strands with temporary color; you're rebuilding the cuticle while depositing tone.

## Performance: Does It Work?

I'll cut to the chase — yes, but with caveats. The color-depositing effect is immediately visible. Red and copper shades get a serious vibrancy boost after one use. Brunettes see richer depth. The formula also genuinely conditions — hair comes out soft, detangled, and shiny, not stripped or crunchy like some tinted products.

The heat protection is a sleeper feature. If you blow-dry or curl your colored hair regularly, this replaces a separate spray, saving you money and time.

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be real about the downsides.

**The price stings.** At about $38 per bottle, this is a luxury purchase. If you wash daily, expect to replace it monthly. Budget-conscious shoppers should consider it a salon-treatment alternative, not a drugstore staple.

**Pigment transfer is real.** If you don't rinse thoroughly, your hands and towel will pick up color. It's not permanent, but it's annoying. A few reviewers noted this specifically.

**Blondes, beware.** This is formulated for richer tones. Several lighter-haired users reported their platinum or ash blonde turned slightly warm or brassy. If you're pastel or icy blonde, this isn't your product.

## Who Should Buy This

- **Vivid color enthusiasts** who want to stretch time between salon visits
- **Heat stylers** looking to consolidate products — the 450°F protection is a genuine bonus
- **Fine-haired folks** who need hydration without heavy buildup

**Skip it if** you're a light blonde, on a tight budget, or prefer a leave-in treatment over a rinse-out formula.

## FAQ

**How often should I use it?**
Start with 2-3 times per week. If your color is seriously faded, daily use for the first week, then maintain with 2-3 times weekly.

**Will it stain my shower?**
Generally no, but the pigment can transfer to hands and towels if not rinsed completely. Rinse until water runs clear.

**Does it replace my regular conditioner?**
Yes. This is a full conditioner, not a supplement. You don't need to layer another product underneath.
