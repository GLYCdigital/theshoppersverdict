**FAQ: Real Questions Shoppers Ask**

**Q: Can I use these on natural nails?**
Technically yes, but the coarse side is overkill for natural nails — you'll risk thinning them. Stick to the finer side for natural nail shaping, or use these exclusively for acrylics.

**Q: How long does one file last?**
With regular use (2-3 sets per week), expect 3-4 weeks per board. They hold up significantly better than drugstore alternatives.

**Q: Are they reusable after disinfecting?**
Yes. Wipe with alcohol or wash with soap and water, let them air dry completely, and they're good for the next session. Avoid soaking them — that breaks down the adhesive over time.
