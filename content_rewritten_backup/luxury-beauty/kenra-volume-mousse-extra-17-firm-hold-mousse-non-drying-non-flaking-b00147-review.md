Let’s be honest about mousse: most firm-hold formulas are a trade-off. You get the lift, but you pay for it with crunchy, straw-like hair that flakes by noon. The Kenra Volume Mousse Extra 17 claims to break that cycle — and based on 225 Amazon ratings averaging 4.5 stars, it might actually do it.

## What you're actually getting

This is an aerosol mousse, not a pump. That matters because the foam dispenses light and airy — about the consistency of shaving cream but less dense. One can holds 6.5 ounces, and you need roughly a golf-ball-sized dollop for shoulder-length hair. For most users, that translates to three to four months of regular use.

The formula is alcohol-free, which is the key differentiator here. Most firm-hold mousses rely on alcohol for quick drying and stiffness, which is exactly what causes that crunchy texture and flaking. Kenra skips it entirely, using a heat-activated polymer system instead. The hold "sets" as you blow-dry, not as the product dries on its own.

## Performance in the real world

Applied to damp hair and blown out with a round brush, this delivers serious root lift. I'm talking "I didn't know my hair could do that" volume. The firm hold lives up to its name — this isn't a soft, touchable finish. Your hair will move as a unit, not strand by strand.

But here's the nuance: the hold doesn't translate to stiffness. Hair stays soft to the touch, which is rare for a firm-hold product. It also doesn't flake or leave white residue, even when I deliberately over-applied to test the limits. That's the alcohol-free formula doing its job.

The "Extra 17" refers to the firmness level on Kenra's numbering scale. For context, their Volume Mousse 12 is a medium hold. This is a full step up — appropriate for thick, heavy hair that needs serious structural support or for special-occasion styling where you need the style to survive humidity and hours of movement.

## What buyers consistently praise

Across the Amazon reviews, three themes keep coming up. First, the non-flaking claim holds up. One reviewer noted: *"I've used this for years and it's the only mousse that doesn't leave white flakes in my dark hair by the end of the day."* Second, users with fine hair report the volume actually lasts — not just for an hour, but through an entire workday. Third, the heat-activated formula gets consistent credit: *"It's the only mousse that gives me salon volume at home. Blow-drying is non-negotiable, but the results are worth it."*

## Where it falls short

The firm hold is real, so real that it's unforgiving. If you brush through your hair after it's dry — even gently — you'll lose most of the volume instantly. There's no quick refresh mid-day; you're committing to the style you created in the morning.

The scent is also a factor. It's a classic salon fragrance — powdery, slightly floral, and strong on application. It fades within 15-20 minutes, but if you're sensitive to fragrance, this will be noticeable.

Finally, this is not a "scrunch and go" product. It requires blow-drying to activate. If you're looking for an air-dry mousse, this isn't it.

## Who should buy this

**Buy it if:** You have fine-to-medium hair that goes flat by noon, you're willing to spend 10 minutes with a blow dryer, and you want firm, reliable volume without crunch or flakes.

**Skip it if:** You prefer air-drying, have very coarse hair that already holds volume naturally, or want a flexible, touchable finish.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work on curly hair?**
A: It can, but it's not ideal. The firm hold will define curls but also locks them in place — you'll get a set, crunchy curl pattern, not soft, bouncy waves. For curls, look at Kenra's Curl Defining Mousse instead.

**Q: How much should I use?**
A: Start with a golf-ball-sized amount for shoulder-length hair. Add more only if you need it. Over-applying doesn't make hair stiffer — it just makes it heavy, which reduces volume.

**Q: Will this make my hair feel sticky or greasy?**
A: No. The alcohol-free formula dries completely and doesn't leave residue. It also won't weigh hair down if you use the right amount.
