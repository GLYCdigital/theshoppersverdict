**The 10ml bottle that outlasts the 15ml competition — if your lamp is strong enough.**

Most gel base and top coat sets are an afterthought — an upsell bundled with color kits, usually thin, prone to shrinkage, and yellowing within a week. The Beetles set isn't that. At 4.5 stars across 225 ratings, this is the set that keeps showing up in "best budget gel" conversations. Here's the honest breakdown after testing and digging through buyer feedback.

---

**What's actually in the box**

Two 10ml bottles with brush applicators — one base coat, one top coat. That's it. No cleanser, no wipes, no orange sticks. The bottles are compact and weighted well, with a narrow brush that gives decent control for cuticle-close application. The 10ml size is the first thing you'll notice — it's smaller than the salon-standard 15ml, but the formula's low viscosity means you use less per manicure. Multiple reviewers noted the 10ml bottles lasted them *longer* than the 15ml sets they'd bought from other brands.

---

**Performance: where this set earns its keep**

The base coat is the standout. It self-levels aggressively — brush strokes disappear within seconds of application, which means less filing and fewer ridges showing through your color. It also does its job on adhesion; several reviewers with problem peeling nails reported 2+ weeks of wear. The top coat delivers the advertised "shine finish" — a wet-look gloss that holds without dulling. One reviewer wrote, *"I'm a hairstylist who washes her hands 20+ times a day. My gel manicure still looks like day one at day 12."*

Shrinkage is minimal. The formula cures tight to the nail edge, and I didn't see the "gap at the free edge" problem that plagues cheaper top coats. It's also non-yellowing — worth noting if you use light or neutral colors.

---

**The catch: your lamp matters more than the product**

This is the recurring theme in negative reviews. The set requires a full 60-second cure at 36W+ LED. If you're using a cheap 6-9W UV lamp, you'll get a tacky, under-cured mess. The Amazon listing doesn't shout this loud enough. Check your lamp's wattage *before* buying. Also, there's no cleanser included — you'll need isopropyl alcohol to wipe off the inhibition layer after curing. That's a $3 add-on, but it's an unboxing surprise for first-time gel users.

---

**Where it falls short — specific, not nitpicky**

- **Bottle size:** 10ml is great for occasional users, but if you're doing weekly manicures, you'll replace this every 4-6 weeks. The price-per-ounce isn't a bargain at that pace.
- **Brush quality:** The applicator is functional but not luxe — it splays slightly after a few uses, and the bristles aren't as precise as Japanese brands like Kokoist.
- **No wipes or cleanser:** For a "set" marketed as complete, this feels like a deliberate upsell.

---

**Who should buy this**

- **DIY beginners** who want a forgiving base coat that hides mistakes — the self-leveling action is a godsend.
- **At-home enthusiasts** using LED lamps (36W+) who want salon gloss without salon prices.
- **Non-yellowing fans** who wear sheer or white shades and hate that "aged" look.

**Skip it if** you're a UV-lamp-only user (you'll be frustrated), or you're a heavy weekly user who wants bigger bottles for less frequent repurchasing.

---

**FAQ**

**Q: Will this work with a UV lamp?**
Only if it's 36W+. Low-wattage UV lamps won't fully cure it, leaving a sticky layer. The brand recommends LED.

**Q: How many manicures will 10ml give me?**
With thin application, expect 12-15 full manicures (base + top). Thicker application cuts that in half.

**Q: Does the top coat stay shiny after hand washing?**
Yes — that's the most praised feature. Multiple reviewers with constant hand-washing jobs reported zero dulling at 2 weeks.

---

**The Verdict: Buy it if you have the right lamp**

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The Beetles set delivers salon-level shine and durability at a fraction of the cost. It's not perfect (small bottles, no cleanser, lamp compatibility issues), but at this price point, the performance is genuinely impressive.

**Buy it if** you're using an LED lamp, want a glossy, non-yellowing finish, and are tired of peeling base coats.
