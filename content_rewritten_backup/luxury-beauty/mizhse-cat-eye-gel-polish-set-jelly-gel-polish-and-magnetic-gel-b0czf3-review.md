If you've scrolled past "cat eye" gel sets that look stunning in product photos but turn into a streaky, muddy mess in real life, you're right to be skeptical. I tested the MIZHSE Cat Eye Gel Polish Set with that exact bias. Here's the honest breakdown.

**What's in the Box**

You get six 8ml bottles: three jelly shades (typically a pink, a peach, and a clear-ish nude) and three magnetic cat eye colors (usually a silver, a gold, and a deep burgundy or blue). Each bottle has the brush attached to the cap — no separate applicator needed. The set also includes one small round magnet wand. That's it. No base coat, no top coat, no lamp. If you're new to gel, budget for those separately.

**Real-World Performance**

The first thing you'll notice is the consistency. These are thinner than your average gel polish — closer to a jelly texture than a thick cream. That's intentional. The jelly shades are designed to build in translucent layers. Two coats give you that subtle "glassy" look; three coats get you closer to opaque. The brush is narrow and precise, which helps with the thin formula, but it means you need to work fast. If you spend too long fiddling with one nail, the polish starts to self-level and can creep toward your cuticles.

The magnetic shades are where this set earns its keep. The pigment particles are fine and dense, so the cat eye streak appears after about 10 seconds of holding the magnet. The included wand works, but it's weak — I got noticeably sharper lines using a stronger bar magnet I had lying around. One buyer noted, "The magnet that comes with it is okay, but I bought a stronger one on Amazon for $8 and the effect is dramatically better." That matches my experience. The magnetic effect also holds well through curing — no fading or scattering after the lamp cycle.

**Wear and Durability**

Applied properly with a base and top coat, this lasted a full two weeks on my nails with minimal tip wear. I'm a keyboard-heavy worker, and I had no chipping. Removal was standard soak-off — about 10 minutes with acetone wraps. No stubborn residue.

**What Buyers Consistently Praise**

The color payoff and the magnetic effect get the most love. One reviewer said, "The cat eye shades are stunning — I've gotten more compliments on these nails than any salon polish I've paid $60 for." Another noted the jelly shades are "perfect for a subtle spring look that still feels polished." The self-leveling property also gets mentioned repeatedly — even messy applicators end up with smooth finishes.

**Where It Falls Short**

The thin formula is a double-edged sword. If you're not careful, it floods cuticles fast. Also, the shade range is limited — you get six colors and no option to customize. If you hate one of the included shades, you're stuck with it. The brush, while precise, is on the smaller side; if you have wide nail beds, you'll need extra strokes per nail.

**Who Should Buy This**

- **DIY gel beginners** who want to practice magnetic effects without dropping $15 per bottle on salon brands
- **Jelly-lovers** who prefer sheer, buildable color over heavy opaque coverage
- **Budget-conscious nail artists** who want a versatile starter kit with both effects in one box

Skip it if you prefer thick, forgiving formulas or if you only want one specific shade — there's no individual bottle option here.

**FAQ**

**Q: Does this work with any UV/LED lamp?**
A: Yes, it cures under standard 36W-48W LED lamps. I tested with a 48W and got full cure in 60 seconds per layer.

**Q: How many coats for the jelly shades to look opaque?**
A: Three coats gets you close to opaque. Two coats give a translucent, "juicy" look. The formula is buildable without getting thick.

**Q: Is the magnetic effect permanent after curing?**
A: Yes, once cured, the particles stay in place. Just make sure you hold the magnet steady for the full 10-15 seconds before curing.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a versatile gel set that delivers on both jelly and magnetic effects without the salon price tag. **Skip it if** you're a beginner who needs a more forgiving, thicker formula — this requires a steady hand and quick work.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — The MIZHSE Cat Eye Gel Polish Set punches well above its price point. The thin formula takes practice, but the results are genuinely impressive. For under $30, you're getting salon-quality magnetic effects and a solid jelly collection. Just swap in a stronger magnet and you're golden.
