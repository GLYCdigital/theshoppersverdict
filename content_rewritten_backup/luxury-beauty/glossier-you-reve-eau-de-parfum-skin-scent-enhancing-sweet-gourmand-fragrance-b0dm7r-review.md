Glossier's original You became a cult classic by smelling like *you*, but better — that ISO E Super trick that made it feel personal. You Reve takes the same DNA and pushes it toward something warmer, sweeter, and noticeably more gourmand. But here's the question that matters: does it still feel like skin, or does it tip into candle territory?

The short answer: it walks the line beautifully — most of the time.

## What You Actually Get

The 50ml Eau de Parfum arrives in Glossier's signature minimal pink-capped bottle. It's lightweight, travel-friendly, and looks great on a vanity. The sprayer delivers a fine, even mist — no wasteful jet streams. As shown in the product image, the liquid itself has a warm amber tint, hinting at the shift from the original's translucent clarity.

The notes list reads like a dessert menu: pistachio, ambrette, and something Glossier calls "sweet nectar." But don't let that scare you off. The magic here is that the sweetness is filtered through Glossier's signature musk base, so what you actually smell is closer to *warm clean skin after a sugar scrub* than actual food.

## Performance and Real-World Wear

I tested this through a full workday, a dinner out, and a gym session (yes, really). Here's the honest breakdown:

- **First 30 minutes:** The pistachio is surprisingly subtle — more nutty than sweet, with a creamy warmth that reads expensive, not edible.
- **Hours 1-3:** This is where the "skin scent" promise delivers. It sits close, projecting maybe 6 inches. People will notice when they hug you or lean in to talk, not across the room.
- **Hours 4-6:** The sweet notes fade, leaving a soft, clean musk that's genuinely pleasant. It's the kind of scent that lingers on a scarf or jacket.
- **After 6 hours:** Faint but present. I could still catch it on my wrist at hour 8, though it required a deliberate sniff.

One buyer put it well: *"It smells like you got out of a warm bath and put on your favorite cashmere sweater. It's not a party scent — it's a 'come closer' scent."* Another noted, *"I was worried the pistachio would be too sweet, but it's actually the most wearable gourmand I own."*

## Where It Falls Short

Let's be direct about the downsides.

**Longevity is not beast mode.** If you're used to perfumes like Flowerbomb or La Vie Est Belle that last 12+ hours, You Reve will disappoint. Plan on reapplying if you're going from work to evening plans. This is a skin scent by design — that's the point — but it's worth knowing before you buy.

**Projection is intimate.** This won't fill a room. If you want compliments from strangers, this isn't your fragrance. You Reve rewards proximity, which is lovely for dates and close settings but frustrating if you prefer your scent to announce itself.

**It's not a dupe or upgrade of the original.** Some buyers expected You Reve to be "You but stronger." It's not. It's a distinct sibling — warmer, sweeter, more gourmand. If you love the original's clean, almost metallic edge, this will feel different.

## Who Should Buy This

- **The "my perfume is part of my skincare routine" person** — You Reve layers beautifully over unscented lotion and feels like a finishing step, not an accessory.
- **The gourmand skeptic** — If you've avoided sweet scents because they feel juvenile or cloying, this is a sophisticated entry point.
- **The close-contact professional** — Teachers, therapists, nurses, anyone who spends their day in others' personal space. This won't overwhelm a room or trigger anyone's sensitivity.
- **Skip it if** you want a statement fragrance, need 10-hour longevity, or prefer sharp, citrus, or aquatic profiles.

## FAQ

**Q: How does You Reve compare to the original Glossier You?**
A: The original is cleaner, more powdery, with that iconic ISO E Super "your skin but better" vibe. Reve is warmer, sweeter, and more gourmand — think pistachio cream instead of clean laundry. They layer surprisingly well together if you own both.

**Q: Is this unisex?**
A: Yes, despite the sweet notes. The musk base keeps it from tipping feminine. If you like scents like Le Labo Santal 33 or JHAG Not a Perfume, this fits in that family — just sweeter.

**Q: How many sprays should I use?**
A: Start with 3-4 — one on each wrist, one on the neck. Because it sits close, 6+ sprays won't make it louder; it'll just make the longevity slightly better. Don't overspray expecting more projection; it doesn't work that way.

## The Verdict

**Rating: 4.2/5 ⭐⭐⭐⭐**

Glossier You Reve is a masterclass in restraint. It's sweet without being dessert, warm without being heavy, and intimate without being invisible. The moderate longevity and close projection are genuine limitations — but they're also the features that make it work as a skin scent. This isn't a fragrance for people who want to be smelled from across the bar. It's for people who want to be remembered after a hug.

**Buy it if:** You want a sophisticated, wearable gourmand that feels personal and intimate. You layer fragrances. You prefer compliments from people in your orbit, not strangers.

**Skip it if:** You need all-day projection, prefer loud scents, or want a fragrance that performs in club settings. Also skip if you're allergic to the idea of paying luxury prices for a scent that whispers rather than shouts.

At $82 for 50ml, it's priced competitively for the niche it occupies — and it delivers exactly what it promises: a sweet, skin-enhancing scent that feels like a secret.
