## What's Actually in the Box

You're getting three 15ml bottles: one base coat, one matte top coat, and one glossy top coat. That's the full structural foundation for any gel manicure — nothing more, nothing less. The packaging is clean and gift-worthy with a simple box that doesn't scream "cheap set," which matters if you're giving this as a present. ## Real-World Performance

The base coat deserves specific attention. It self-levels remarkably well, meaning it smooths out minor imperfections as it cures. I tested it under a dark red polish — the kind that typically stains natural nails — and removal showed zero discoloration. The glossy top coat delivers the high-shine finish you'd expect from a salon, and it's stayed clear for two weeks without yellowing.

The matte top coat is where this set gets interesting. Matte finishes are notoriously finicky — too thick and you get patchy, uneven dullness. Beetles' version applies best in thin, even layers. When I did that, the finish was consistently matte across the entire nail, no streaks or shiny spots. Apply it too generously and you'll lose that effect, but that's user error more than product failure.

## Where It Falls Short

Honest tradeoffs exist. The 15ml bottles are smaller than the 30ml bottles many gel brands offer for individual purchases. If you're doing weekly manicures for a household of people, you'll replace this set faster than you'd like. The matte top coat's sensitivity to application thickness is a real learning curve — it took me two tries to get the technique right. And while the set includes everything essential, there's no nail prep solution or cleanser, so if you're starting from scratch, you'll need to buy those separately.

## FAQ

**Q: Does this work with any gel polish brand?**  
Yes. Gel base and top coats are universally compatible across brands as long as you're using a UV/LED lamp. Beetles' polishes work fine with this set, as do brands like Gelish or Modelones.

**Q: How long does a manicure last with this set?**  
Expect 2–3 weeks with proper application — cap the free edge of your nails with top coat and avoid soaking your hands excessively. Several reviewers report hitting the three-week mark.

**Q: Is the matte top coat truly matte?**  
Yes, when applied in thin layers. The finish is a true matte with no shine, but thick application will compromise it. Two thin coats beat one thick one.
