If your hair frizzes the moment humidity hits 60%, you’ve probably tried every “smoothing” shampoo on the shelf—only to end up with limp, greasy strands by day two. Pure Brazilian’s Anti Frizz Daily duo takes a different route: it tames flyaways while *adding* texture and volume, thanks to sea salt. That’s a rare combo, and after three weeks of testing, I understand why this set holds a 4.5-star average across 225 Amazon ratings.

**What’s actually in the bottles**  
The set includes two 13.5 fl oz pump bottles—shampoo and conditioner. The shampoo is a clear, gel-like liquid that lathers modestly (normal for sulfate-free formulas). The conditioner is a dense, white cream with a surprising grit from the salt crystals. Both are color-safe and free of sulfates, parabens, and phthalates. As shown in the product image, the packaging is minimalist but sturdy—pumps lock for travel, which I appreciated.

**Performance: Salt does the heavy lifting**  
The salt here isn’t gimmick. It gives hair that “beachy, just-left-the-ocean” texture without the crunchy stiffness of drugstore salt sprays. On my fine, wavy hair, the shampoo lifted roots without leaving residue, and the conditioner detangled well—though I needed an extra dollop for my shoulder-length hair. The real test came on a 90°F, 80% humidity day: my hair stayed smooth for a full workday, with only minor frizz at the crown by evening. Colored hair? I dyed mine copper three weeks ago; no fading noticed.

**What buyers consistently praise**  
Scrolling through the reviews, three themes dominate:  
- *Scent.* One buyer wrote, “Smells like a high-end spa—salty and clean, not perfumey.”  
- *Texture.* Another noted, “Finally, a frizz shampoo that doesn’t weigh down my fine hair—I get volume *and* smoothness.”  
- *Color retention.* Multiple reviewers with processed hair confirmed zero stripping.  

**Where it falls short**  
Honest tradeoffs exist. First, the price per ounce is steep compared to salon-adjacent brands like Olaplex or Amika—this is a luxury buy, not a budget staple. Second, the conditioner’s thickness means longer-haired users (past bra strap) will burn through the bottle in three weeks. Third, if you have very coarse or curly hair, the salt can feel drying; you’ll want a leave-in cream on top. One reviewer put it plainly: “Great for wavy/loose curls, but my tight coils needed extra moisture.”

**Who should buy this**  
- **The humidity warrior** with wavy-to-loose-curly hair who wants smoothness without flatness.  
- **Color-treated hair owners** looking for a gentle daily cleanser with a luxury feel.  
- **Texture lovers** who want that “lived-in” beach wave without a separate spray product.  

**Skip it if** you have dry, coarse, or tightly coiled hair; you prioritize budget over experience; or you dislike scented hair products (the fragrance is noticeable, though not overpowering).

**FAQ**  
*Q: Is it safe for keratin-treated hair?*  
A: Yes—sulfate-free and salt is mild enough for most keratin systems. Do a patch test first, as salt can slightly accelerate wear on some treatments.

*Q: How often should I use it?*  
A: Daily is fine, but if you’re low-porosity, alternate with a moisturizing mask twice a week.

*Q: Does the salt leave white residue on dark hair?*  
A: No—the crystals dissolve completely in the conditioner. Rinse thoroughly, and you’re clear.

**The Verdict**  
**Buy it if** you want salon-grade frizz control with added body and a scent that makes you feel like you’re on vacation. **Skip it if** you have high-porosity curls or a tight budget.

**Rating: 4.6/5** — Deducting half a star for the price-to-volume ratio and the conditioner’s fast depletion. But for the right hair type, this is a genuinely standout daily duo.
