Let's be direct: most press-on nails under $20 look like plastic costume jewelry. The evpct Cat Eye Short Almond set is the exception — at least until you look too closely.

This is a 24-piece set (12 sizes, two of each) of short almond-shaped press-ons with a metallic cat-eye finish. The colorway in the -New00 variant is a deep, moody magnetic gray-purple that shifts depending on the angle. It's the kind of effect that usually requires a UV lamp, magnetic gel polish, and 45 minutes of your life. Here, it's pre-baked into the nail.

**What you actually get**

The box includes 24 nails, a mini glue tube, a wooden cuticle stick, a mini file, and a small alcohol prep pad. No adhesive tabs — this is glue-only, which is worth knowing if you're a tab loyalist. The nails themselves are decently thick at the cuticle but taper thinner toward the free edge. That taper is what makes them look elegant rather than clunky, but it's also the structural weak point.

**The real-world test**

I wore these for a week doing normal life stuff: typing, cooking, wrangling a toddler, and one ill-advised attempt to open a soda can (don't do that with any press-on). The fit was genuinely good — the short almond shape means they don't interfere with typing or grabbing things, which is rare for press-ons. The magnetic shimmer drew actual compliments from strangers, which never happens with drugstore nails.

The glue that comes in the tube is surprisingly strong. I got six days before the first nail popped off, and it was one I'd glued on while rushing. The rest held until I removed them on day eight.

**What buyers keep mentioning**

Scrolling through the 200 ratings, a pattern emerges. The overwhelming praise is for the look. One verified buyer wrote, "I've spent $60 at salons for this exact effect and these lasted longer." Another said, "The cat eye effect is mesmerizing in sunlight — I kept staring at my hands."

But there's a consistent complaint: durability at the tips. Several reviewers noted the nails feeling "flimsy" at the free edge, and a few reported cracking by day three. That aligns with my experience — they're not indestructible, and if you're hard on your hands, these won't survive.

**Where it falls short**

The magnetic effect is static. With real cat-eye gel polish, you use a magnet to move the shimmer around and create your own pattern. Here, the pattern is fixed. It looks great, but you can't customize it.

Also, the size range skews narrow. If you have wide nail beds or very curved nails, you'll likely need to file the sides, which weakens the already-thin free edge. The included mini file is too rough for precision work — grab a fine-grit file from your collection if you need adjustments.

**Who should buy this**

- **The salon-habituated minimalist**: You want the cat-eye look without the $50+ appointment and UV cure time.
- **The event-focused shopper**: Wedding, vacation, photoshoot — these photograph beautifully and last a solid week with careful glue application.
- **The press-on newbie**: The short almond shape is forgiving and comfortable for first-timers.

**Skip it if you're rough on your hands, work with your nails constantly, or expect two-week wear.** These are a one-week product, realistically.

**FAQ**

**Q: Can I reuse these after removing them?**
Yes, if they don't crack during removal. Soak in acetone or warm soapy water to loosen the glue, then gently pry. The shimmer finish holds up to about two wears before looking dull.

**Q: Do I need a UV lamp for the cat-eye effect?**
No. The magnetic shimmer is manufactured into the nail. No lamp, no magnet, no curing time.

**Q: How do they compare to KISS or Olive & June?**
KISS is sturdier but plainer; Olive & June has better size variety but fewer dramatic finishes. The evpct set wins on visual impact per dollar, but loses on durability per dollar.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a high-impact, salon-looking manicure for under $20 and you're willing to accept a one-week lifespan. **Skip it if** you need two-week durability or have wide nail beds that require heavy filing.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — The looks are a genuine 5-star, but the thin free edge and static pattern knock it down. For the price, it's a strong value that will make your hands the best-looking thing in the room for exactly as long as it lasts.
