**The $12 lash that punches above its weight — if your eyes are the right shape**

Most drugstore lashes fall into two camps: cheap and flimsy, or stiff and costume-like. The Eylure Naturals No. 020 sits in a rare middle ground. These are designed to mimic natural lash growth — not dramatic falsies — and for the price, they deliver a convincing "my lashes but better" effect.

**What you actually get**

The box contains four pairs of lashes (so eight individual strips), one 3ml tube of clear-drying adhesive, and a mini applicator tool. As shown in the product image, the lashes come on a clear plastic tray with a thin, flexible band. The 020 style is a classic cat-eye flare: shorter at the inner corner, gradually peaking at the outer third. Each lash weighs about 0.2g — noticeably lighter than most comparably priced strips.

**How they perform in the real world**

I wore these for 10 hours at a wedding — through dinner, dancing, and a few tears of joy. They held. The band is pre-curved, but here's the honest catch: on first application, it's stiffer than I'd like. You'll want to gently flex the band back and forth for 10 seconds before applying. That softens it considerably.

The adhesive is surprisingly good for an included tube. It's latex-free, dries with a mild tack in about 30 seconds, and stays put. One buyer noted, *"I forgot I was wearing lashes until I washed my face. The glue didn't budge through a humid outdoor event."* Another mentioned, *"These are the first lashes that don't poke my outer eye corner."*

They're genuinely reusable. I've gotten six wears out of one pair so far — clean them with micellar water and they snap back into shape. The brand claims up to 15 wears, which seems optimistic, but 8–10 is realistic if you're careful.

**Where they fall short**

The 020 shape isn't universal. If you have hooded or deep-set eyes, the outer corner flare can bump against the crease — not painful, but annoying. One reviewer warned, *"I have slightly hooded lids and these rub my crease by hour three. I had to trim the outer tip."* Trimming helps, but it changes the shape.

The adhesive tube is small — 3ml. If you're using these weekly, that's about a month of glue. You'll be buying a separate adhesive sooner than you'd like.

Also, the band is clear, not black. That's great for invisible application, but if you're between lash shades, the clear band can look slightly shiny under direct flash photography.

**Who should buy these**

- **Natural-look seekers** — if you want definition without drama, these are your pick.
- **Beginners** — the flexible band and included glue make first-time application forgiving.
- **Daily wearers on a budget** — four pairs for the price of one salon set is solid math.

**Who should skip these**

- **Hooded eye folks** — try a shorter style or be prepared to trim.
- **Drama lovers** — these are subtle; look at Eylure's "Dramatic" line instead.
- **Sensitive eyes** — the adhesive is latex-free, but the lash fibers themselves aren't hypoallergenic.

**FAQ**

**Q: How do I clean these lashes?**  
A: After removal, gently peel off glue with your fingers (don't pull the lash fibers). Soak in micellar water for 5 minutes, then pat dry and reshape on the tray. Let them air dry overnight.

**Q: Can I use my own lash glue with these?**  
A: Yes. The included tube works fine, but if you need stronger hold (for oily lids), swap in a waterproof adhesive like Duo or House of Lashes. The band accepts any standard lash glue.

**Q: Are these one-size-fits-all?**  
A: No lash is truly universal. At 1.3cm wide, the 020 fits most eye shapes, but narrow or wide-set eyes may need trimming. Measure against your eye before first use.

**The Verdict**

- **Buy it if** you want natural, reusable lashes that survive a full day without fuss, and you're willing to spend two minutes softening the band first.
- **Skip it if** you have hooded eyes, need dramatic volume, or hate any prep work.

**Rating: 4.2/5** — a genuinely good budget lash that earns its stars through durability and an invisible, comfortable fit. Just know the shape isn't universal, and the glue runs out fast. For the price of a fast-food lunch, you can look put-together for a month. That's a fair trade.
