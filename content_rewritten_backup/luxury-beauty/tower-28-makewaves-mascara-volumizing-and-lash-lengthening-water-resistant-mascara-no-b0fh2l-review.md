Let's cut to the chase: Tower 28's MakeWaves Mascara has earned its 4.2-star rating across 210 Amazon reviews, but it's not the universal miracle product the brand's marketing suggests. I've tested this against drugstore staples and luxury department store formulas — here's what you need to know before dropping your cash.

**What's Actually in the Tube**

The packaging is refreshingly minimal: a frosted glass-look tube with a weighted feel that doesn't scream "eco-friendly compromise." You get 0.24 ounces of product, which is standard for the category. The wand deserves special mention — it's a silicone bristle brush with densely packed, slightly curved spikes that grab every lash, including the tiny corner ones most applicators miss.

The formula itself is a hybrid: it's designed to build volume *and* add length without the dry, brittle finish many lengthening mascaras leave behind. It's infused with peptides and castor oil, which explains why lashes feel conditioned rather than stiff after removal.

**Real-World Performance**

Here's where MakeWaves separates itself from the pack. I applied two coats to straight, stick-straight Asian lashes (the hardest test for any mascara). The first coat separated and defined; the second added genuine volume without spider-leg clumping. The curl held for a full workday — roughly 10 hours — without drooping. That's impressive for a mascara that doesn't rely on heavy waxes.

The formula dries to a flexible, slightly waxy finish that resists flaking. I wore it through a sweaty commute and an evening run; zero raccoon eyes. One reviewer on Amazon put it well: *"Finally a clean mascara that doesn't crumble by lunch. I forget I'm wearing it."*

**Where It Falls Short**

Let's be honest about the limitations. Despite the "water-resistant" label, this is not swim-proof. A few tears at a wedding or unexpected rain will produce minor smudging under the lash line. If you need something for pool days or emotional events, look elsewhere — this is an everyday workhorse, not an ironclad formula.

The drying time is another quibble. At roughly 60-90 seconds, it's slower than most mascaras I've tested. If you're rushing to apply before curling your lashes (a common mistake), you'll end up with transfer marks on your brow bone. Wait the extra minute; it's worth it.

Removal requires effort. Micellar water alone leaves a gray residue. You'll need an oil-based cleanser or balm to break down the formula completely. For a product marketed as clean and gentle, this feels slightly counterintuitive.

**Who Should Buy This**

This is your mascara if you:
- Have sensitive eyes or wear contacts — the fragrance-free, paraben-free formula is genuinely gentle
- Prefer a natural-but-noticeable lash look over dramatic falsie effect
- Want a clean beauty product that actually performs like conventional mascaras
- Are willing to invest in a proper cleansing routine

Skip it if you need waterproof performance, prefer a crunchy, ultra-black dramatic finish, or are on a tight budget — you can find comparable drugstore options for half the price.

**Frequently Asked Questions**

*Does this mascara smudge on oily eyelids?*
The formula includes film-forming polymers that resist oil reasonably well. Most reviewers report minimal smudging through an 8-hour day. If you have very oily lids, use a primer underneath for extra insurance.

*Is this mascara truly clean?*
Yes, it's free of parabens, phthalates, sulfates, and synthetic fragrance. It's also vegan and cruelty-free. However, "clean" doesn't mean non-comedogenic — if you're acne-prone, check the ingredient list for the castor oil component.

*How does it compare to Ilia or Saie mascaras?*
MakeWaves offers better volume than Ilia's Limitless Lash but less dramatic length than Saie's Mascara 01. It sits comfortably in the middle — a jack-of-all-trades rather than a specialist.

**The Verdict: 4.2/5**

*Buy it if* you want a reliable, everyday clean mascara that separates, volumizes, and holds a curl without flaking — and you don't mind the slower dry time or oil-based removal.

*Skip it if* you need waterproof protection or want a $30 formula that outperforms a $10 drugstore tube. It's good, not transformative. But for clean beauty enthusiasts with sensitive eyes, this is one of the better options in the category. Just keep your oil cleanser handy.
