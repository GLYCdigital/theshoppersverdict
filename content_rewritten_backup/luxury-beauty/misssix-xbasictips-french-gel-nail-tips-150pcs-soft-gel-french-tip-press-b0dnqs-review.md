If you've burned money on cheap press-ons that pop off by day two, the Misssix XBASICTIPS French Gel Nail Tips are worth a serious look. These aren't the stiff acrylic stickers you find at drugstores — they're soft gel tips designed to flex with your natural nails. With 150 pieces across 12 sizes, this set is built for repeat use, not just a one-night experiment.

**What's actually in the box**

You get 150 soft gel tips in a clear, stackable case. The sizes range from 0 to 11, with roughly 12-13 nails per size — enough for multiple full manicures. Each tip has a pre-painted French white tip that's cleanly applied; no streaks or uneven edges. As shown in the product image, the tips have a natural almond shape that suits most fingers without aggressive filing.

One thing to note upfront: there's no glue or gel adhesive in the box. You'll need to purchase nail glue or a gel base separately. That's an extra $8-15 depending on what you pick, so factor that into the true cost.

**Performance and real-world wear**

I tested these with both brush-on nail glue and a gel base cured under a lamp. With proper preparation (buffing the natural nail, dehydrating, applying a thin glue layer), the tips held for a full 14 days without lifting. The soft gel material is the standout feature — it bends slightly under pressure rather than snapping, which is the main reason acrylic press-ons fail for most people.

The length is moderate — about half the length of stiletto tips — so they're practical for typing, cooking, and general daily wear. The French tip color is opaque and doesn't yellow or fade, a common complaint with cheaper sets.

**What buyers consistently praise**

Scrolling through the 220 ratings at 4.4 stars, three themes dominate. First, the sizing variety: "Finally a set where I don't have to file every single nail to find a match," one reviewer noted. Second, the durability: multiple reviews mention getting two full manicures from one box. Third, the natural look — several buyers said people couldn't tell they were press-ons.

**Where it falls short**

The biggest recurring complaint is the lack of adhesive. "Great nails, but I didn't realize I'd need to buy glue separately," one 3-star review reads. That's fair — at this price point, including a small glue tube seems like a no-brainer.

Second, the sizing runs narrow. If you have wider nail beds, you'll likely need to size up and file the edges down, which can leave rough spots. The 12 sizes help, but they don't fully accommodate very wide or very flat nail shapes.

Third, removal takes patience. Because the soft gel adheres so well, you can't just pop them off — you'll need to soak in acetone or use an electric file. That's a tradeoff for durability, but it's worth knowing before you buy.

**Who should buy this**

The Misssix XBASICTIPS are ideal for three specific buyers: DIY nail enthusiasts who want salon-quality French tips without the $50 appointment; people with naturally flexible nails who've had acrylic press-ons snap on them; and anyone who wants a reusable set — one box genuinely lasts 3-4 full manicures.

Skip them if you want a quick, one-night application with included adhesive, or if you have extremely wide nail beds that need custom shaping.

**FAQ**

*Do these work with regular nail glue?* Yes. Standard brush-on nail glue works fine — just prep your nails properly and hold each tip for 10-15 seconds.

*How long do they actually last?* With proper prep and a gel base, expect 10-14 days. With standard glue, most reviewers report 5-7 days.

*Can I reuse them?* Yes, if you remove them carefully with acetone and file off old glue residue. The soft gel material holds up well across multiple applications.

**The Verdict**

**Buy it if** you want a durable, natural-looking French manicure that lasts two weeks and comes from a box you'll use multiple times.

**Skip it if** you're looking for a quick, all-in-one set with glue included, or you have very wide nail beds that don't fit standard sizing.
