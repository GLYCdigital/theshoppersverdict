# Lorell Fortress Series Double Pedestal Desk Review: Solid Office Foundation Plagued by Delivery Issues

**Rating: 3.5/5 ⭐**
**Price: $739.99**
**Best for:** Budget-conscious offices needing a traditional double-pedestal desk who can handle receiving a heavy item

---

## Product Overview

The **Lorell Fortress Series Double Pedestal Desk** is a traditional office desk designed for professional workspaces. Measuring 60 inches wide by 30 inches deep by 29.5 inches high, it offers a large, uninterrupted work surface suitable for dual monitors, paperwork, and everyday office tasks. The desk combines a black steel frame with a walnut laminate top, giving it a classic executive look at a mid-range price point.

At roughly $740, it sits below premium brands like Steelcase or Herman Miller executive desks but above the budget flat-pack offerings from Amazon Basics and similar brands. The "double pedestal" design means two vertical cabinet sections support the desk — one typically holds file drawers, while the other offers box/storage drawers. A central locking mechanism secures all drawers, adding basic security for sensitive documents.

Key specs as listed: 60"W x 30"D x 29.5"H, black steel frame, walnut laminate top finish.

---

## What Customers Love

### Quality and Durability

The strongest thread across positive reviews is that the desk delivers on its core promise: it's sturdy, well-constructed furniture. One verified buyer describes it simply as "very good quality, durable and sturdy," while another echoes that it "looks good and appears to be sturdy and well-constructed." These aren't effusive raves, but they're consistent — the kind of measured praise that suggests the desk does its job reliably.

Another customer who already owned a matching Lorell desk noted that the new unit was a "great match to existing desk that we already had" — a useful detail for anyone expanding or replacing within an existing Lorell setup. The walnut top and black steel aesthetic appears to be consistent across units, which is reassuring for buyers trying to match existing furniture.

### Assembly Experience

Despite being a heavy, substantial piece of furniture, the desk drew no significant complaints about assembly difficulty. One reviewer said it was "reasonably easy to assemble" — decent feedback for a double-pedestal desk, which typically requires more effort than a single-pedestal or basic table-style desk. The 5-star reviewers seemed to get it set up without major frustration.

---

## What Customers Complain About

### Shipping Damage Is the Real Issue

This is where the Lorell Fortress Series desk stumbles. Two of the five sampled reviews report significant delivery damage, and both gave 1-star ratings as a result. This is a pattern worth taking seriously.

One buyer received their desk with "significant damage" — dents, scratches, and chips in the laminate, plus scratches and dents in the metal frame. To make matters worse, the key for the locking drawer was missing entirely, rendering the center lock drawer inoperable.

Another customer reported a "huge dent at the back" and described being "beyond disappointed" after paying for a brand new desk. Their frustration was compounded by the desk's weight — "the desk is very heavy and will be a hassle to return" — meaning they felt stuck with a damaged product because the return logistics were daunting.

This is the single most important takeaway for potential buyers: **the desk itself appears to be a solid product when it arrives in good condition, but delivery handling is a real vulnerability.** This is a heavy, 60-inch-long piece of furniture, and the packaging isn't reliably protecting it during transit. If you're in a location with less-than-careful shippers, or if your delivery address makes careful handling difficult, this is a genuine risk.

### Missing Parts

The missing lock key on one unit suggests quality control inconsistency. While this only appeared in one review, it's worth noting because a locking desk drawer is a specific feature people pay for — if it doesn't work out of the box, you're essentially paying for a feature you can't use.

## Who Is This Desk For?

The Lorell Fortress Series Double Pedestal Desk makes sense for:

- **Small to medium offices** furnishing multiple workstations on a budget — the $740 price point is competitive for a 60" double-pedestal desk
- **Home offices** that need traditional file storage (two pedestals of drawers) in a classic walnut/black finish
- **Anyone who already owns Lorell furniture** — the finish reportedly matches well across units
- **Buyers with a loading dock or ground-floor delivery** who can receive heavy furniture without carrying it up stairs

It's **less ideal** for:

- **Anyone without easy return logistics** — if returning a heavy desk would be a nightmare for you, the damage risk should give you pause
- **Those seeking premium materials** — the top is laminate, not solid wood, and while it looks decent, it won't match all-wood furniture
- **People who need the desk urgently** — delivery issues could mean sending it back and waiting for a replacement

---

## The Bottom Line

The Lorell Fortress Series Double Pedestal Desk has a clear split personality. On paper and in the hands of satisfied customers, it's a genuinely good value: traditional styling, sturdy construction, reasonable assembly, and a workable $740 price tag. The 5-star reviews describe exactly what you'd hope for from this purchase.

But the delivery damage reports are hard to ignore. At this weight (it's heavy enough that even assembling it with a dent isn't worth the return hassle), the packaging needs to be robust enough to survive standard parcel handling — and based on customer accounts, it sometimes isn't.

**Our advice:** If your delivery situation is favorable (ground floor, good shippers in your area, ability to inspect on arrival), this desk is likely a solid 4-star purchase. If you're in a tricky delivery zone or don't have the patience for a potential return-and-replace cycle, consider whether the risk is worth the savings over a premium brand — or look for sellers who offer white-glove delivery.
