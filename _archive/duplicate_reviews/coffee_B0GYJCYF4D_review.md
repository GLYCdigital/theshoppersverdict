**Buy this if:**
- You're currently using a blade grinder and wondering what you're missing

For $79.99, this grinder punches so far above its weight that you'd be forgiven for thinking there's a catch. ## The Not-So-Good Stuff

### 🍶 The Portafilter Holder Needs Work

This is the one consistent complaint, and it's a real one. The grinder comes with a portafilter fork/adapter, but it doesn't sit well with many portafilter designs. The angle and depth make it tricky to align properly, and several users reported that most of their espresso grounds ended up on the counter or in the tray rather than in the portafilter basket.

The solution: use the included container instead. It works perfectly, and you just transfer grounds to your portafilter. But if direct portafilter grinding is a non-negotiable part of your morning routine, this might bug you every single day. It's not a dealbreaker — but it's worth knowing going in.

### 📏 30 Steps (Not Stepless)

For most people, 30 grind settings is plenty. But true espresso enthusiasts who chase that last 2% of extraction might find the step increments a bit wide. You'll get in the ballpark for espresso, but you might not be able to dial in *perfectly* between steps with some beans. This is an $80 grinder, not a $500 commercial unit — reasonable, but worth mentioning for the perfectionists.
