# HP 67 Ink Cartridges Review: Genuine HP Quality for Your Printer

Printer ink is one of those things you don't think about until you run out mid-print job. The **HP 67 Black/Tri-Color Ink Cartridges 2-Pack** is one of the most popular ink options on Amazon, with a **4.6-star rating from over 62,000 reviews** — and it serves millions of HP Envy and DeskJet printers worldwide.

## What's in the Box

The 2-pack includes one black ink cartridge and one tri-color (cyan, magenta, yellow) cartridge. Both are genuine HP cartridges, meaning they're engineered specifically for HP printers with optimized ink formulations and chip recognition.

## Installation and Setup

Installation is genuinely painless. Both cartridges slide into their respective slots and are recognized by the printer immediately — no alignment issues, no error messages, no fiddling with protective tape.

One reviewer summed it up: "Installation was quick and hassle-free — my printer recognized both cartridges right away without any errors or alignment issues."

## Print Quality

Genuine HP ink delivers on quality. Text is crisp and dark black — no grayish tones or feathering on standard paper. Color prints are bright, vibrant, and accurate, making this a solid choice for school projects, presentations, and family photos.

For everyday document printing, the output is indistinguishable from what you'd get from a higher-end office printer. Photo quality on HP photo paper is surprisingly good for an all-in-one consumer printer.

## Page Yield and Value

The standard HP 67 black cartridge yields approximately 120 pages, with color at around 100 pages. For light to moderate users, this lasts a few months. For heavy printers, the 67XL high-yield option offers better value per page.

At $43.89 for the 2-pack, the per-page cost is moderate for genuine ink. Third-party alternatives are available for less, but users frequently report issues with firmware updates, color accuracy, and print quality on non-genuine cartridges.

## Instant Ink Compatibility

One major advantage of genuine HP cartridges is Instant Ink eligibility. The HP Instant Ink subscription service monitors your ink levels and sends replacement cartridges before you run out. For households that print regularly, this can reduce ink costs by up to 50% while eliminating the "I'm out of ink" emergency.

## Common Complaints

The feedback is overwhelmingly positive, but the main criticisms are predictable:

- **Page yield**: Standard cartridges don't last long for frequent printers
- **Cost**: Ink is expensive, and this is true for all OEM cartridges
- **Tariff impact**: Some recent buyers noted price increases due to tariffs

## Verdict

**4.6/5** — The HP 67 Ink Cartridges 2-Pack delivers reliable, high-quality prints with effortless installation. The cost of genuine ink is always a consideration, but the consistency and compatibility are worth it for most users. If you print heavily, look into XL cartridges or Instant Ink. If you print occasionally, this pack will serve you well.
