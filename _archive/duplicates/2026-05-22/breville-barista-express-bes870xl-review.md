# Breville Barista Express BES870XL Review: The Gold Standard for Home Espresso

If you're serious about espresso at home, the **Breville Barista Express BES870XL** has been a fixture of the "best home espresso machine" conversation for years — and for good reason. With a 4.5-star rating from over 27,000 reviews, it's one of the most popular semi-automatic machines on the market.

But after all these years and with newer competitors emerging, is it still worth your money in 2026? Let's find out.

## Design and Build Quality

The Breville Barista Express presents itself well with a brushed stainless steel finish that looks right at home in any modern kitchen. It's substantial without being overly bulky — at roughly 13 inches wide, it fits comfortably on most countertops.

The build quality is impressive for a machine at this price point. The die-cast metal construction gives it a solid, premium feel. Users consistently report this machine lasting 5-7 years with regular use and proper maintenance. One review even noted their unit ran daily for over six years before needing replacement.

## The Built-in Grinder: A Game-Changer

One of the biggest selling points is the integrated conical burr grinder. Rather than buying a separate grinder (which can easily cost $200+), you get a capable grinder built right into the machine. It offers 30 grind settings, allowing you to dial in everything from fine espresso to coarser French press grinds.

The direct-to-portafilter grinding keeps waste minimal and freshness maximal. You grind exactly what you need, when you need it. Most users find that starting with the recommended settings and adjusting from there works well.

## Espresso Quality

Here's where the Barista Express truly shines. With fresh beans and a proper dial-in, this machine produces espresso that genuinely rivals cafe-quality shots. The 15-bar Italian pump delivers consistent pressure, and the thermocoil heating system maintains stable water temperature throughout extraction.

The crema is thick and golden, the body is rich, and the flavor extraction is impressive for a machine in this class. Once you dial in your favorite beans, you'll be pulling shots that would cost $4-5 at your local coffee shop.

## Steam Wand Performance

The steam wand uses a single-hole tip and produces adequate power for home use. It's not quite commercial-grade, but it's more than capable of creating silky microfoam for lattes and cappuccinos. The wand is adjustable and swivels for easy positioning.

Pro tip: wipe the wand immediately after each use and purge it to prevent milk buildup. This simple habit extends the wand's life significantly.

## Learning Curve

Let's be honest — there is a learning curve. This isn't a super-automatic "push a button" machine. You'll need to learn about grind size, dose weight, tamp pressure, and extraction time. But that's precisely what makes it rewarding.

Most reviewers note that while the first few shots may be mediocre, you'll be pulling excellent espresso within a week or two of practice. The included instructional materials are solid, and there's an enormous online community of Breville users to help troubleshoot.

## Maintenance

Regular maintenance is non-negotiable. You'll need to:
- Clean the grinder burrs periodically
- Descale the machine every 2-3 months
- Replace the water filter as recommended
- Backflush with the cleaning disc

It sounds like a lot, but these routines become second nature quickly. Skipping maintenance is the number one cause of premature failure.

## Is It Worth It in 2026?

At around $685, the Breville Barista Express isn't cheap. But when you consider that a single latte costs $5-6 at a cafe, this machine pays for itself within 6-12 months for daily drinkers. The build quality, excellent espresso output, and integrated grinder make it arguably the best value in home espresso today.

## Verdict

**4.5/5** — The Breville Barista Express BES870XL remains the benchmark for entry-level prosumer espresso machines. It's not perfect — the learning curve is real, and maintenance requires commitment — but for the quality of espresso it produces at this price point, nothing else comes close.
